1
宗廟之禮。 每歲以四孟月及季冬，凡五享，朔、望則上食、薦新。 三年一祫，以孟冬； 五年一禘，以孟夏，唯親郊、封祀。 又有朝享、告謝及新主祔謁，皆大祀也。 二薦，則行一獻禮。 其祔祭，春祀司命及戶，夏祀灶，季夏祀中霤，秋祀門及厲，冬祀行，惟臘享、禘祫則遍祀焉。
Rites of the Imperial Ancestral Temple. Each year the court held five major offerings—in the first month of each season and in the last month of winter—and on the new and full moon presented food and seasonal first-fruits. Every three years a xiá rite was held in early winter; and every five years a dì rite in early summer, but only in years when the emperor personally performed the suburban or feng and shan sacrifices. Court offerings, thanksgiving rites, and ceremonies to install a new spirit tablet were likewise classified as great sacrifices. For the two seasonal presentations, only the single-offering ceremony was used. Sacrificial rites to household spirits followed a seasonal schedule—spring for the Director of Fate and Door, summer for the Stove, midsummer for the Middle Drain, autumn for the Gate and the Pestilence Spirit, winter for the Road—with all spirits included only at the winter offering and the great dì and xiá ceremonies.
2
禘祫之禮。 真宗咸平二年八月，太常禮院言：「今年冬祭畫日，以十月六日薦享太廟。 按《禮》，三年一祫，以孟冬。 又《疑義》云：三年喪畢，遭禘則禘，遭袷則袷。 宜改孟冬薦享為祫享。 」仁宗天聖元年，禮官言：「真宗神主祔廟，已行吉祭，三年之制，又從易月之文，自天禧二年四月禘享，至今已及五年，合行禘禮。 」遂以孟夏薦享為禘享。 八年九月，太常禮院言：「自天聖六年夏行禘享之禮，至此年十月，請以孟冬薦享為祫享。 」詔恭依。
Rites of the Dì and Xiá Sacrifices. In the eighth month of the second year of Xianping (999), the Court of Imperial Sacrifices reported: "The date for this year's winter sacrifice has been set: the Grand Ancestral Temple will receive offerings on the sixth day of the tenth month. According to the canonical Rites, a xiá sacrifice is held every three years in early winter. The Doubts on the Rites likewise states that once the three-year mourning is complete, if a dì sacrifice falls due, perform the dì; if a xiá falls due, perform the xiá. The early-winter offering should therefore be redesignated a xiá sacrifice. " In the first year of Tiansheng (1023), the ritual officials reported: "Emperor Zhenzong's spirit tablet has been installed in the temple and the auspicious sacrifice completed. Under the three-year rule, following the provision on shortened months, and counting from the dì offering in the fourth month of the second year of Tianxi (1018), five years have now elapsed; a dì sacrifice is due. " The early-summer offering was accordingly performed as a dì sacrifice. In the ninth month of the eighth year of Tiansheng (1030), the Court of Imperial Sacrifices reported: "Since the dì sacrifice was performed in early summer of the sixth year of Tiansheng (1028), and the tenth month of this year has now arrived, we request that the early-winter offering be performed as a xiá sacrifice. " The emperor approved the proposal.
3
嘉祐四年十月，仁宗親詣太廟行祫享禮，以宰臣富弼為祫享大禮使，韓琦為禮儀使，樞密使宋庠為儀仗使，參知政事曾公亮為橋道頓遞使，樞密副使程戡為鹵簿使。 同判宗正寺趙良規請正太祖東向位，禮官不敢決。 觀文殿學士王舉正等議曰：「大祫之禮所以合昭穆，辨尊卑，必以受命之祖居東向之位。 本朝以太祖為受命之君，然僖祖以降，四廟在上，故每遇大袷，止列昭穆而虛東向。 魏、晉以來，亦用此禮。 今親享之盛，宜如舊便。」
In the tenth month of the fourth year of Jiayou (1059), Emperor Renzong went in person to the Grand Ancestral Temple to perform the xiá sacrifice. Grand Councillor Fu Bi was appointed chief commissioner for the xiá rite, Han Qi ritual commissioner, Privy Councillor Song Qi insignia commissioner, Vice Grand Councillor Zeng Gongliang bridge and relay commissioner, and Vice Privy Councillor Cheng Zhan guard-of-honor commissioner. Associate Director of the Imperial Clan Court Zhao Lianggui petitioned to restore Emperor Taizu to the east-facing seat, but the ritual officials could not reach a decision. Academician of the Hall for Viewing Literature Wang Juzheng and others argued: "The great xiá sacrifice unites the zhao and mu lines and distinguishes rank and precedence; the ancestor who received the Mandate must occupy the east-facing seat. Our dynasty regards Emperor Taizu as the ruler who received the Mandate, yet from Emperor Xi onward four ancestral temples stand above him; at each great xiá only the zhao and mu tablets are arrayed, leaving the east-facing seat vacant. From the Wei and Jin dynasties onward the same practice has been followed. For this grand personal sacrifice, the established practice should be retained."
4
禮官張洞、韓維言：「國朝每遇禘祫，奉別廟四後之主合食太廟。 唐《郊祀志》載禘祫祝文，自獻祖至肅宗所配皆一後，惟睿宗二後，蓋昭成，明皇母也。 《續曲台禮》有別廟皇后合食之文，蓋未有本室，遇祫享即祔祖姑下。 所以大順中，三太后配列禘祭，議者議其非禮。 臣謂每室既有定配，則餘後不當參列，義當革正。」
Ritual officials Zhang Dong and Han Wei argued: "Whenever our dynasty performs the dì and xiá sacrifices, the spirit tablets of the four consorts from the separate shrines are brought to share the feast at the Grand Ancestral Temple. The Tang Records of Suburban Sacrifices records dì and xiá prayer texts: from Emperor Xian to Emperor Suzong each emperor was paired with a single consort, except Emperor Ruizong, who had two—Zhaocheng, mother of Emperor Xuanzong. The Continued Rites of the Qu Terrace contains provisions for consorts from separate shrines to share the feast—when they lacked a proper chamber of their own, at the xiá sacrifice they were placed below the principal consort of the founding ancestor. Hence in the Dashun era (890–891) three empress dowagers were included in the dì sacrifice, which critics held to be improper. We hold that since each chamber already has its fixed pairing, the other consorts should not participate; the practice ought to be reformed."
5
學士孫抃等議：「《春秋傳》曰：『大祫者何，合祭也。 未毀廟之主皆升合食於太祖。 』是以國朝事宗廟百有餘年，至祫之日，別廟後主皆升合食，非無典據。 大中祥符中已曾定議，禮官著酌中之論，先帝有『恭依』之詔。 他年有司攝事，四後皆預。 今甫欲親祫而四後見黜，不亦疑於以禮之煩故邪？ 宗廟之禮，至尊至重，苟未能盡祖宗之意，則莫若守舊禮。 臣等愚以謂如故便。」
Academicians Sun Bian and others argued: "The Commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals states: 'What is the great xiá? A combined sacrifice. All spirit tablets whose temples have not yet been dismantled ascend to share the feast with Emperor Taizu.' ' Our dynasty has tended the ancestral temple for more than a century; on the day of the xiá, consorts from separate shrines have always ascended to share the feast—hardly without canonical precedent. During the Dazhong Xiangfu era (1008–1016) this was already settled: ritual officials drafted a balanced judgment, and the former emperor issued an edict of approval. In subsequent years, when officials performed the rite by proxy, all four consorts still participated. Now, just as His Majesty is about to perform the xiá in person, the four consorts are to be excluded—does this not suggest that inconvenience, rather than ritual principle, is the motive? Ancestral temple rites are supremely exalted; if we cannot fully honor the ancestors' intent, it is better to preserve the established practice. We humbly submit that the established practice should be retained."
6
學士歐陽修等曰：「古者宗廟之制，皆一帝一後。 後世有以子貴者，始著並祔之文，其不當祔者，則有別廟之祭。 本朝禘祫，乃以別廟之後列於配後之下，非惟於古無文，於今又四不可。 淑德，太宗之元配，列於元德之下； 章懷，真宗之元配，列於章懿之下，一也。 升祔之後，統以帝樂； 別廟之後，則以本室樂章自隨，二也。 升祔之後，同牢而祭，牲器祝冊亦統於帝； 別廟諸後，乃從專享，三也。 升祔之後，聯席而坐； 別廟之後，位乃相絕，四也。 章獻、章懿在奉慈廟，每遇禘祫，本廟致享，最為得禮。 若四後各祭於廟，則其尊自申，是於禮無失。 以為行之已久，重於改作，則是失禮之舉，無復是正也。 請從禮官。」
Academicians Ouyang Xiu and others argued: "In antiquity the ancestral temple system paired one emperor with one consort. Later ages, when a mother was ennobled through her son, introduced provisions for joint enshrinement; those not eligible received sacrifices at separate shrines. In our dynasty's dì and xiá sacrifices, consorts from separate shrines are ranked below the principal paired consort—without ancient precedent and presenting four practical impossibilities today. Shude, Emperor Taizong's first consort, is ranked below Yuande— Zhanghuai, Emperor Zhenzong's first consort, is ranked below Zhangyi—the first objection. Consorts elevated to joint enshrinement share the emperor's music; consorts from separate shrines retain their own chamber hymns—the second objection. Elevated consorts sacrifice from a shared pen, with victims, vessels, and prayer texts unified under the emperor; consorts from separate shrines receive separate offerings—the third objection. Elevated consorts sit on linked mats; consorts from separate shrines are seated far apart—the fourth objection. Zhangxian and Zhangyi reside in the Fengci Shrine; at each dì and xiá their own shrine receives offerings—fully in accord with ritual propriety. If all four consorts sacrifice at their own shrines, their dignity is properly expressed without any breach of ritual. To argue that because a practice has long continued, reform is too burdensome, is to perpetuate a breach of ritual that can never be corrected. We request that the ritual officials' proposal be adopted."

十月二日，命樞密副使張望告昊天上帝、皇地祇。 帝齋大慶殿。 十一日，服通天冠、絳紗袍，執圭、乘輿，至大慶殿門外降輿，乘大輦，至天興殿，薦享畢，齋於太廟。 明日，帝常服至大次，改袞冕，行禮畢，質明，乘大輦還宮，更服靴袍，禦紫宸殿，宰臣、百官賀，升宣德門肆赦。 二十一日，詣諸觀寺行恭謝禮。 二十六日，御集英殿為飲福宴。
On the second day of the tenth month, Vice Privy Councillor Zhang Wang was commissioned to announce the sacrifice to the Supreme Lord of the Azure Heaven and the Imperial Earth Sovereign. The emperor observed abstention in the Hall of Great Celebration. On the eleventh day, wearing the Tongtian crown and crimson gauze robe, holding the gui tablet and riding the imperial carriage, he alighted outside the Hall of Great Celebration, boarded the great imperial litter, proceeded to the Hall of Heavenly Prosperity to complete the offering, then observed abstention at the Grand Ancestral Temple. The next day the emperor in ordinary dress proceeded to the great resting pavilion, changed into dark ceremonial robes, and completed the rites. At dawn he rode the great litter back to the palace, changed into boots and court robe, presided at the Hall of Purple Brilliance while grand councillors and all officials offered congratulations, then ascended Xuande Gate to proclaim a general amnesty. On the twenty-first day he proceeded to the various Daoist and Buddhist temples to perform the reverent thanksgiving rite. On the twenty-sixth day he presided at the Hall of Assembled Excellence for the feast of receiving blessing.

Emperor Zhenzong completed mourning in the sixth month of the second year of Xianping (999) but did not perform the xiá sacrifice until the tenth month. 11
二年二月，翰林學士王珪等上議曰：「同知太常禮院呂夏卿狀：古者新君踐阼之三年，先君之喪二十七月為禫祭，然後新主祔廟，特行禘祭，謂之始禘。 是冬十月行袷祭，明年又行禘祭，自此五年，再為禘祫。 喪除必有禘祫者，為再大祭之本也。 今當袷祭，緣陛下未終三年之制，納有司之說，十月依舊時享。 然享廟、袷祭，其禮不同。 故事，郊享之年遇祫未嘗權罷，唯罷臘祭。 是則孟享與享廟嘗並行於季冬矣。 其禘祫年數，乞一依太常禮院請，今年十月行祫祭，明年四月行禘祭。 仍如夏卿議。 」權罷今年臘享。
In the second month of the second year (1065), Hanlin Academician Wang Gui and others submitted a deliberation: "Associate Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices Lü Xiaqing reported that in antiquity, in the new ruler's third year after accession, once the former ruler's twenty-seven-month mourning ended with the end-of-mourning sacrifice, the new spirit tablet was enshrined and a special dì sacrifice—the inaugural dì—was performed. That winter, in the tenth month, the xiá sacrifice was performed; the following year the dì was performed again; thereafter dì and xiá alternated every five years. After mourning ends, dì and xiá are obligatory because they establish the cycle of the two great sacrifices. A xiá sacrifice is now due, but because Your Majesty has not yet completed the three-year mourning period, accepting the responsible offices' proposal, the regular seasonal offering will be performed in the tenth month. Yet the seasonal temple offering and the xiá sacrifice differ in ritual form. By precedent, in years when suburban sacrifice coincided with a scheduled xiá, the xiá was never suspended—only the winter sacrifice was omitted. Thus the first-month offering and the temple offering were sometimes performed together in late winter. As for the schedule of dì and xiá sacrifices, we beg to follow the Court of Imperial Sacrifices' proposal: perform the xiá this year in the tenth month and the dì next year in the fourth month. This accords with Xiaqing's proposal. " The winter sacrifice for this year was provisionally suspended.
12
熙寧八年，有司言：「已尊僖祖為太廟始祖，孟夏禘祭，當正東向之位。 」又言：「太廟禘祭神位，已尊始祖居東向之位，自順祖而下，昭、穆各以南北為序。 自今禘祫，著為定禮。」
In the eighth year of Xining (1075), the responsible offices reported: "Emperor Xi has been honored as founding ancestor of the Grand Ancestral Temple; at the early-summer dì sacrifice, his tablet should occupy the corrected east-facing seat. " They further reported: "At the Grand Ancestral Temple dì sacrifice, the founding ancestor's tablet already occupies the east-facing seat; from Emperor Shun downward, zhao and mu tablets are ordered north and south respectively. Henceforth this arrangement shall be fixed ritual for all dì and xiá sacrifices."
13
元豐四年，詳定郊廟禮文所言：「禘祫之義，存於《周禮》、《春秋》，而不著其名。 行禮之年，經皆無文，唯《公羊傳》曰：『五年而再盛祭。 』《禮緯》曰：『三年一祫，五年一禘。 』而又分為二說：鄭氏則曰：『前三後二』，謂禘後四十二月而祫，祫後十八月而禘。 徐邈則曰：『前二後三』，謂二祭相去各三十月。 以二說考之，惟鄭氏曰：『魯禮，三年喪畢，祫於太廟，明年禘於群廟，自後五年而再盛祭，一祫一禘。 』實為有據。 本朝慶曆初用徐邈說，每三十月一祭。 熙寧八年，既禘而祫，此有司之失也。 請今十八月而禘，禘四十二月而祫，庶幾舉禮不煩，事神不瀆。 」太常禮院言：「本朝自慶曆以來，皆三十月而一祭。 至熙寧五年後，始不通計，遂至八年禘祫並在一歲。 昨元豐三年四月已行禘禮，今年若依舊例，十月行祫享，即比年頻袷，復踵前失。 請依慶曆以來之制，通計年數，皆三十月而祭。 」詔如見行典禮。
In the fourth year of Yuanfeng (1081), the commission for suburban and temple ritual texts stated: "The meaning of dì and xiá is preserved in the Rites of Zhou and the Spring and Autumn Annals, though the terms themselves are not recorded there. The years in which these rites are performed are nowhere specified in the classics; only the Gongyang Commentary states: 'Every five years the great sacrifice is performed again. ' The Rites Apocrypha states: 'Once every three years a xiá, once every five years a dì. ' Two interpretations emerged: Master Zheng's 'three before, two after' holds that xiá follows dì after eighteen months and dì follows xiá after eighteen months. Xu Miao's 'two before, three after' holds that the two sacrifices are each thirty months apart. Comparing the two schools, only Master Zheng states: 'In Lu ritual, when three-year mourning ends, xiá at the Grand Temple; the next year dì at the group of temples; thereafter every five years the great sacrifice alternates—one xiá, one dì. ' This interpretation has solid textual support. Our dynasty initially adopted Xu Miao's interpretation at the beginning of Qingli (1041–1048), performing one sacrifice every thirty months. In the eighth year of Xining (1075), dì and xiá were performed in the wrong order—an error by the responsible offices. We propose that dì follow xiá after eighteen months and xiá follow dì after forty-two months, so that ritual is performed without excess and the spirits are served without irreverence. " The Court of Imperial Sacrifices replied: "Since Qingli our dynasty has performed one sacrifice every thirty months. From the fifth year of Xining onward the schedule was no longer maintained, until in the eighth year both dì and xiá fell in a single year. The dì rite was already performed in the fourth month of the third year of Yuanfeng (1080); if this year we follow precedent and perform the xiá in the tenth month, xiá sacrifices will come in successive years, repeating the earlier error. We request adherence to the system in place since Qingli, reckoning years comprehensively so that sacrifices fall every thirty months. " The emperor ordered that the currently practiced canonical ritual be followed.
14
詳定所又言：「古者稞獻、饋食，禴祠、烝、嘗，並為先王之享，未嘗廢一時之祭。 故孔氏《正義》以為：『天子夏為大祭之禘，不廢時祭之礿； 秋為大祭之祫，不廢時祭之嘗。 』則王禮三年一袷與禘享，更為時祭。 本朝沿襲故常，久未厘正，請每禘祫之月雖已大祭，仍行時享，以嚴天子備禮，所以丕崇祖宗之義。 其郊禮、親祠準此。」
The commission further stated: "In antiquity libation presentation, feeding offerings, spring sacrifice, winter offering, and autumn tasting were all offerings of the former kings; no seasonal sacrifice was ever abandoned. Therefore Kong Yingda's Correct Meaning holds: 'When the Son of Heaven in summer performs the great dì sacrifice, he does not abandon the seasonal yue offering; when in autumn he performs the great xiá sacrifice, he does not abandon the seasonal chang offering.' ' Thus in royal ritual the xiá every three years and dì every five years supplement, rather than replace, the seasonal sacrifices. Our dynasty has long followed established custom without correction; we request that in months when dì or xiá sacrifices are performed, seasonal offerings still be conducted, so that the Son of Heaven's full ritual observance may honor the ancestors as fully as possible. Suburban sacrifice and personal offerings shall follow the same standard."
15
又言：「《禮》：不王不禘。 虞、夏、商、周四代所禘，皆以帝有天下，其世係所出者明，故追祭所及者遠也。 太祖受命，祭四親廟，推僖祖而上所自出者，譜失其傳，有司因仍舊說，禘祫皆合群廟之主，綴食於始祖，失禮莫甚。 今國家世係與四代不同，既求其祖之所自出而不得，則禘禮當闕，必推見祖係乃可以行。 」神宗謂輔臣曰：「禘者，本以審禘祖之所自出，故禮，不王不禘。 秦、漢以後，譜牒不明，莫知其祖之所自出，由禘禮可廢也。」
They also stated: "The Rites declare: without attaining kingship there is no dì sacrifice. The dì sacrifices of the four dynasties Yu, Xia, Shang, and Zhou all presupposed that the emperor possessed the realm and that the lineage of origin was clear; hence retroactive sacrifices could reach far back in time. When Emperor Taizu received the Mandate, he sacrificed at the four parental temples; tracing above Emperor Xi to identify the lineage of origin, the genealogy had been lost; responsible offices followed the old account and at every dì and xiá combined all spirit tablets from the group of temples to share the feast with the founding ancestor—a breach of ritual that could hardly be greater. Our state's lineage now differs from those four dynasties; since the ancestor's origin cannot be determined, the dì rite ought to be omitted—it can be performed only when the ancestral lineage can be traced. " Emperor Shenzong told his chief ministers: "The dì sacrifice exists fundamentally to ascertain the origin of the ancestral line; hence the Rites: without attaining kingship, no dì. From Qin and Han onward genealogical records were unclear and no one knew the ancestor's origin; the dì rite may therefore be abolished."
16
已而詳定所言：「古者天子祭宗廟，有堂事焉，有室事焉。 按《禮》，祝延屍入奧，灌後乃出延牲，延屍主出於室，坐於堂上，始祖南面，昭在東，穆在西，乃行朝踐之禮，是堂事也。 設饌於堂，復延主入室，始祖東面，昭南穆北，徙常上之饌於室中，乃行饋食之禮，是室事也。 請每行大祫，堂上設南面之位，室中設東面之位。 」禮部言：「合食之禮，始祖東面、昭南穆北者，本室中之位也。 今設位戶外，祖宗昭、穆別為幄次，殆非合食之義。 請自今祫享，即前楹通設帳幕，以應室中之位。」
Subsequently the commission stated: "In antiquity when the Son of Heaven sacrificed at the ancestral temple, there were hall rites and chamber rites. According to the Rites, the invoker led the representative corpse into the inner chamber; after libation the sacrificial victim was led out; the corpse-host emerged from the chamber and was seated in the hall with the founding ancestor facing south, zhao to the east and mu to the west; then the court presentation rite was performed—this was the hall rite. Food was set in the hall, then the host was led back into the chamber with the founding ancestor facing east, zhao to the south and mu to the north; the food prepared above was moved into the chamber and the feeding rite performed—this was the chamber rite. We request that at each great xiá a south-facing seat be set in the hall and an east-facing seat in the chamber. " The Ministry of Rites stated: "In the combined feast rite, the founding ancestor facing east with zhao to the south and mu to the north is originally the chamber arrangement. Setting seats outside the doors with ancestors, zhao, and mu in separate pavilions scarcely accords with the meaning of combined feasting. We request that from now on at the xiá sacrifice, canopy curtains be set across the front pillars to correspond to the chamber positions."
17
大觀四年，議禮局請：「每大祫，堂上設南面之位，室中設東南之位，始祖南面則昭穆東西相向，始祖東面則昭穆南北相向，以應古義。 」又請：「陳瑞物及代國之寶與貢物可出而陳者，並令有司依嘉祐、元豐詔旨，凡親祠太廟準此。 」從之。
In the fourth year of Daguan (1110), the Bureau for Deliberating Ritual requested: "At each great xiá, set a south-facing seat in the hall and a southeast-facing seat in the chamber: when the founding ancestor faces south, zhao and mu face each other east and west; when the founding ancestor faces east, zhao and mu face each other north and south, in accord with ancient usage. " They also requested: "Auspicious objects, treasures of former dynasties, and displayable tribute items shall all be arranged by responsible offices according to the edicts of Jiayou and Yuanfeng; all personal sacrifices at the Grand Ancestral Temple shall follow this standard. " The request was approved.
18
南渡之後，有祫而無禘。 高宗建炎二年，祫享於洪州。 紹興二年，祫享於溫州。 時儀文草創，奉遷祖宗及祧廟神主、別廟神主，各設幄合食於太廟。 始祖東向，昭、穆以次南北相向。
After the court crossed south, xiá sacrifices were performed but dì sacrifices were not. In the second year of Jianyan (1128), Emperor Gaozong performed the xiá sacrifice at Hongzhou. In the second year of Shaoxing (1132), the xiá sacrifice was performed at Wenzhou. Ritual protocols were then newly devised: relocated ancestors and spirit tablets from the distant-lineage temple and separate shrines each had pavilions erected so that all could share the feast at the Grand Ancestral Temple. The founding ancestor faced east; zhao and mu tablets were arrayed in sequence facing each other north and south.
19
五年，吏部員外郎董弅言：「臣聞戎、祀，國之大事，而宗廟之祭，又祀之大者也。 大祀，禘祫為重，祫大禘小，則袷為莫大焉。 今戎事方殷，祭祀之禮未暇遍舉，然事有違經戾古，上不當天地神祇之意，下未合億兆黎庶之心，特出於一時大臣好勝之臆說，而行之六十年未有知其非者。 顧雖治兵禦戎之際，正厥違誤，宜不可緩。 仰惟太祖受天明命，混一區宇，即其功德所起，宜祇享以正東向之尊。 逮至仁宗，親行祫享，嘗議太祖東向，用昭正統之緒。 當時在廷之臣，僉謂自古必以受命之祖乃居東向之位，本朝太祖乃受命之君，若論七廟之次，有僖祖以降四廟在上，當時大祫，止列昭穆而虛東向，蓋終不敢以非受命之祖而居之也。 暨熙寧之初，僖祖以世次當祧，禮官韓維等據經有請，適王安石用事，奮其臆說，乃俾章衡建議，尊僖祖為始祖，肇居東向。 馮京奏謂士大夫以太祖不得東向為恨，安石肆言以折之。 已而又欲罷太祖郊配，神宗以太祖開基受命，不許，安石終不以為然。 元祐之初，翼祖既祧，正合典禮。 至於崇寧，宣祖當祧，適蔡京用事，一遵安石之術，乃建言請立九廟，自我作古，其已祧翼祖、宣祖並即依舊。 循沿至今，太祖尚居第四室，遇大祫處昭穆之列。 今若正太祖東向之尊，委合《禮經》。」
In the fifth year (1135), Vice Director of the Ministry of Personnel Dong Bian memorialized: "I have heard that war and sacrifice are great affairs of state, and sacrifice at the ancestral temple is the greatest of sacrifices. Among great sacrifices, dì and xiá are paramount; xiá is greater and dì lesser, making xiá the greatest of all. Military affairs are pressing and sacrificial rites cannot all be performed, yet certain practices violate the classics and contradict antiquity, failing above to satisfy Heaven, Earth, and the spirits and below to satisfy the people—arising solely from one minister's competitive private opinion, practiced for sixty years without anyone recognizing the error. Even amid training troops and resisting enemies, correcting these errors should not be delayed. Emperor Taizu received Heaven's clear Mandate and unified the realm; as the source of our dynasty's merit, he ought to receive sole honor with the corrected east-facing dignity. By Emperor Renzong's time, when he personally performed the xiá sacrifice, the court once debated restoring Emperor Taizu to the east-facing seat to clarify the orthodox succession. Ministers at court unanimously held that from antiquity only the ancestor who received the Mandate could occupy the east-facing seat; our Emperor Taizu was that ruler; yet with four temples above him from Emperor Xi downward, at the great xiá only zhao and mu were arrayed while the east-facing seat was left vacant—they ultimately dared not seat a non-Mandate-receiving ancestor there. At the beginning of Xining (1068), Emperor Xi by generational order should have been removed from the temple; ritual officials Han Wei and others petitioned on canonical grounds; Wang Anshi was then in power and advanced his private opinion, having Zhang Heng propose honoring Emperor Xi as founding ancestor with the east-facing seat. Feng Jing memorialized that scholar-officials resented Emperor Taizu's exclusion from the east-facing seat; Wang Anshi spoke freely to refute them. He then also sought to abolish Emperor Taizu's suburban pairing; Emperor Shenzong, holding that Taizu founded the state and received the Mandate, refused; Wang Anshi never accepted the decision. At the beginning of Yuanyou (1086), Emperor Yi was removed from the temple, in proper accord with canonical ritual. By Chongning (1102), Emperor Xuan should have been removed; Cai Jing was then in power and followed Wang Anshi's methods entirely, proposing nine temples and making antiquity anew; the already-removed Emperors Yi and Xuan were both restored. Down to the present, Emperor Taizu still occupies the fourth chamber and at the great xiá is placed among the zhao and mu tablets. If Emperor Taizu's east-facing dignity is now corrected, the arrangement will truly accord with the Ritual Canon."
20
太常寺丞王普又言：「弅所奏深得禮意，而其言尚有未盡。 臣竊以古者廟製異宮，則太祖居中，而群廟列其左右； 後世廟製同堂，則太祖居右，而諸室皆列其左。 古者祫享，朝踐於堂，則太祖南向，而昭穆位於東西； 饋食於室，則太祖東向，而昭穆位於南北。 後世祫享一於堂上，而用室中之位，故唯以東向為太祖之尊焉。 若夫群廟迭毀，而太祖不遷，則其禮尚矣。 臣故知太祖即廟之始祖，是為廟號，非諡號也。 惟我太宗嗣服之初，太祖廟號已定，雖更累朝，世次猶近，每於祫享，必虛東向之位，以其非太祖必不可居也。 迨至熙寧，又尊僖祖為廟之始祖，百世不遷，祫享東向，而太祖常居穆位，則名實舛矣。 倘以熙寧之禮為是，僖祖當稱太祖，而太祖當改廟號。 然則太祖之名不正，前日之失大矣。 今宜奉太祖神主居第一室，永為廟之始祖。 每歲五享、告朔、薦新，止於七廟。 三年一祫，則太祖正東向之位。 太宗、仁宗、神宗南向為昭，真宗、英宗、哲宗北向為穆。 五年一禘，則迎宣祖神主享於太廟，而以太祖配焉。 如是，則宗廟之事盡合《禮經》，無復前日之失矣。 」上曰：「太祖皇帝開基創業，始受天命，祫享宜居東向之位。 」宰相趙鼎等奏曰：「三昭三穆，與太祖之廟而七，載在《禮經》，無可疑者。」
Assistant Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices Wang Pu also stated: "Bian's memorial deeply grasps ritual intent, yet his argument is incomplete. I hold that in antiquity, when temples were separate structures, Emperor Taizu stood at the center with subsidiary temples arrayed to left and right; in later ages, when temples shared one hall, Emperor Taizu stood on the right with all chambers arrayed to his left. In antiquity at the xiá sacrifice, court presentation took place in the hall: Emperor Taizu faced south with zhao and mu to east and west; feeding in the chamber, Emperor Taizu faced east with zhao and mu to north and south. Later ages perform the xiá sacrifice entirely in the hall yet use chamber positions; hence only the east-facing seat expresses Emperor Taizu's dignity. When subsidiary temples are successively dismantled while Emperor Taizu is never moved, that principle still holds. I therefore know that Emperor Taizu is the temple's founding ancestor—this is a temple name, not a posthumous title. When Emperor Taizong first succeeded, Emperor Taizu's temple name was already fixed; though many reigns passed, generations were still near; at each xiá sacrifice the east-facing seat was left vacant, because none but Taizu could occupy it. By Xining, Emperor Xi was again honored as the temple's founding ancestor, never to be moved for a hundred generations, facing east at the xiá sacrifice while Taizu constantly occupied the mu position—name and reality were at odds. If the Xining ritual is held correct, Emperor Xi should be called Taizu and Taizu should receive a different temple name. Then Taizu's name would be incorrect and the earlier error would be grave indeed. Emperor Taizu's spirit tablet should now be placed in the first chamber as the temple's permanent founding ancestor. Each year's five offerings, new-moon reports, and seasonal first-fruits should be limited to the seven temples. Every three years at the xiá sacrifice, Taizu should occupy the corrected east-facing seat. Emperors Taizong, Renzong, and Shenzong should face south as zhao; Emperors Zhenzong, Yingzong, and Zhezong north as mu. Every five years at the dì sacrifice, Emperor Xuan's spirit tablet should be welcomed to the Grand Ancestral Temple with Taizu as his pairing. Thus ancestral temple affairs would fully accord with the Ritual Canon, with no repetition of the earlier error. " The emperor said: "Emperor Taizu founded the state and first received the Mandate; at the xiá sacrifice he should occupy the east-facing seat. " Grand Councillor Zhao Ding and others memorialized: "Three zhao and three mu, with Taizu's temple making seven, are recorded in the Ritual Canon—beyond dispute."
21
紹熙五年九月，太常少卿曾三復亦言：請祧宣祖，就正太祖東向之位，其言甚切。 既而吏部尚書鄭僑等亦乞因大行祔廟之際，定宗廟萬世之禮，慰太祖在天之靈，破熙寧不經之論。 今太祖為始祖，則太宗為昭，真宗為穆，自是而下以至孝宗，四昭四穆與太祖之廟而九。 上參古禮，而不廢崇寧九廟之制，於義為允。 又言：「治平四年，僖祖祧遷，藏在西夾室。 至熙寧五年，王安石以私意使章衡等議，乃復祔僖祖以為始祖，又將推以配天，欲罷太祖郊配。 韓維、司馬光等力爭，而安石主其說愈堅。 孫固慮其罷太祖配天，建議以僖祖權居東向之位。 既曰權居，則當厘正明矣。 」詔從之。
In the ninth month of the fifth year of Shaoxi (1194), Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices Zeng Sanfu likewise petitioned to remove Emperor Xuan and restore Emperor Taizu to the east-facing seat—his argument was very earnest. Subsequently Minister of Personnel Zheng Qiao and others petitioned that when the late emperor's spirit tablet was installed, the ancestral temple ritual for all generations be fixed, comforting Taizu's spirit in Heaven and refuting the heterodox Xining argument. With Taizu as founding ancestor, Taizong is zhao and Zhenzong mu; from there down to Emperor Xiaozong, four zhao and four mu with Taizu's temple make nine. The emperor consulted ancient ritual yet did not abolish the Chongning nine-temple system—a principled compromise. They also noted: "In the fourth year of Zhiping (1067), Emperor Xi was removed from the temple and stored in the west side chamber. By the fifth year of Xining (1072), Wang Anshi from private intent had Zhang Heng and others deliberate, restoring Emperor Xi as founding ancestor, further intending to pair him with Heaven and abolish Taizu's suburban pairing. Han Wei, Sima Guang, and others argued forcefully, while Wang Anshi held ever more firmly to his view. Sun Gu, fearing abolition of Taizu's heaven pairing, proposed that Emperor Xi provisionally occupy the east-facing seat. Since the arrangement was explicitly provisional, the time for correction is now clear. " An edict approved the proposal.
22
閏十月，權禮部侍郎許及之言：「僖、順、翼、宣四祖，為太祖之祖考，所遷之主，恐不得藏於子孫之廟。 今順、翼二祖藏於西夾室，實居太廟太祖之右。 遇祫享，則於夾室之前，設位以昭穆焉。 」於是詔有司集議，吏部尚書兼侍讀鄭僑等言：「僖祖當用唐興聖之制，立為別廟，順祖、翼祖、宣祖之主皆祔藏焉。 如此，則僖祖自居別廟之尊，三祖不祔子孫之廟。 自漢、魏以來，太祖而上，毀廟之主皆不合食，今遇祫，則即廟而享，於禮尤稱。 」諸儒如樓鑰、陳傅良皆以為可，詔從之。
In the intercalary tenth month, Acting Vice Minister of Rites Xu Jizhi stated: "The four ancestors Xi, Shun, Yi, and Xuan, as Taizu's forebears whose tablets were removed, probably ought not be stored in descendants' temples. Emperors Shun and Yi are now stored in the west side chamber, actually to the right of Taizu in the Grand Ancestral Temple. At the xiá sacrifice, seats are set before the side chambers according to zhao and mu. " An edict then ordered responsible offices to deliberate collectively; Minister of Personnel and Palace Reader Zheng Qiao and others argued: "Emperor Xi should follow the Tang Xingsheng precedent and be established in a separate shrine, with the tablets of Emperors Shun, Yi, and Xuan enshrined and stored there. Thus Emperor Xi would retain the dignity of a separate shrine, and the three ancestors would not be enshrined in descendants' temples. From Han and Wei onward, spirit tablets above Taizu from dismantled temples have never shared the feast; offering at their own shrines at xiá is especially fitting in ritual. " Confucian scholars such as Lou Yue and Chen Fuliang all approved; an edict adopted the proposal.
23
時朱熹在講筵，獨入議狀，條其不可者四，大略云：「準尚書吏部牒，集議四祖祧主宜有所歸。 今詳群議雖多，而皆有可疑。 若曰藏之夾室，則是以祖宗之主下藏於子孫之夾室。 至於祫祭，設幄於夾室之前，則亦不得謂之祫。 欲別立一廟，則喪事即遠，有毀無立。 欲藏之天興殿，則宗廟、原廟不可相雜。 議者皆知其不安，特以其心欲尊奉太祖三年一袷時暫東向之故，其實無益於太祖之尊，而徒使僖祖、太祖兩朝威靈，相與校強弱於冥冥之中。 今但以太祖當日追尊帝號之令而默推之，則知今日太祖在天之靈，必有所不忍而不敢當矣。 又況僖祖祧主遷於治平，不過數年，神宗復奉以為始祖，已為得禮之正而合於人心，所謂『有其舉之，莫敢廢者』。 」又言：「當以僖祖為始祖，如周之後稷，太祖如周之文王，太宗如周之武王，與仁宗之廟，皆萬世不祧； 昭穆而次，以至高宗之廟亦萬世不祧。 」又言：「元祐大儒程頤以為王安石言『僖祖不當祧』，復立廟為得禮。 竊詳頤之議論與安石不同，至論此事則深服之，足以見義理人心之所同，固有不約而合者。 特以司馬光、韓維之徒皆是大賢，人所敬信，其議偶不出此，而安石乃以變亂穿鑿得罪於公議，故欲堅守二賢之說，並安石所當取者而盡廢之。 今以程頤之說考之，則是非可判矣。」
Zhu Xi was then at the lecture mat and alone submitted a deliberation listing four objections, broadly stating: "According to the Ministry of Personnel dispatch, collective deliberation is required on where the four removed ancestors' tablets should be placed. Examining the many proposals, all raise doubts. Storing them in side chambers would place ancestors' tablets beneath descendants' side chambers. Setting pavilions before side chambers for the xiá sacrifice likewise cannot properly be called xiá. Establishing a separate temple is problematic because mourning is long past—there is precedent for dismantling but not for establishing anew. Storing them in the Hall of Heavenly Prosperity would improperly mix the ancestral temple with the distant-lineage temple. Deliberators all recognize the proposals are unsound, yet wish solely to honor Taizu by having him temporarily face east at each three-year xiá—in reality this does not enhance Taizu's dignity but only pits the spiritual authority of Xi and Taizu against each other in the unseen realm. If one silently infers from the order by which Taizu's imperial title was posthumously conferred, one knows that Taizu's spirit in Heaven today must find the proposal unbearable and dare not accept it. Moreover, Emperor Xi's tablet was removed in Zhiping; only a few years later Emperor Shenzong restored him as founding ancestor—already the ritually correct course and in accord with popular sentiment, as the saying goes: 'When something has been undertaken, none dare abolish it.' " He also argued: "Emperor Xi should remain founding ancestor, like Zhou's Hou Ji; Taizu like Zhou's King Wen; Taizong like Zhou's King Wu; together with Renzong's temple, all never to be removed for ten thousand generations; with zhao and mu in sequence down to Emperor Gaozong's temple, likewise never to be removed. " He also noted: "The great Yuanyou scholar Cheng Yi held that Wang Anshi was correct in saying 'Emperor Xi should not be removed' and that restoring his temple accorded with ritual. Yi and Wang Anshi differed on many matters, yet on this point Yi deeply approved—showing that principle and popular sentiment can converge without prior arrangement. Only because Sima Guang, Han Wei, and their peers are great worthies whom people respect, their deliberations happened not to reach this conclusion, while Wang Anshi offended public opinion through reckless alteration; opponents therefore wish to hold firmly to the two worthies' views and reject even what Wang Anshi got right. Examined through Cheng Yi's argument, right and wrong can now be judged."
24
時宰臣趙汝愚既以安石之論為非，異議者懼其軋己，藉以求勝，事竟不行。 熹時以得罪，遺汝愚書曰：「相公以宗子入輔王室，而無故輕納妄議，拆祖宗之廟以快其私，欲望神靈降歆，垂休錫羨，以永國祚於無窮，其可得乎？ 」時太廟殿已為十二室，故孝宗升祔，而東室尚虛。 熹以為非所以祝延壽康之意，深不然之，因自劾不堪言語侍從之選，乞追奪待制，不許。 及光宗祔廟，遂復為九世十二室。 蓋自昌陵祔廟，逾二百年而後正太祖之位。 慶元二年四月，禮部太常寺言：「已於太廟之西，別建僖祖廟，及告遷僖、順、翼、宣帝後神主詣僖祖廟奉安。 所有今年孟冬祫享，先詣四祖廟室行禮，次詣太廟，逐幄次行禮。」
Grand Councillor Zhao Ruyu already held Wang Anshi's view mistaken; dissenters feared he would suppress them and used the dispute to seek advantage; the proposal was ultimately not adopted. Zhu Xi, then under censure, wrote to Zhao Ruyu: "You entered government as a clansman to assist the royal house, yet without cause lightly accepted reckless deliberation and would dismantle the ancestors' temple to satisfy private desire—can you expect the spirits to descend with favor, bestow blessing, and extend the state's fortune endlessly? " The Grand Ancestral Temple hall already had twelve chambers; when Emperor Xiaozong was elevated to joint enshrinement, the east chamber remained vacant. Zhu Xi held that this was not the intent of praying for the emperor's long life and health, deeply disapproved, and impeached himself as unfit for attendance in discourse, begging revocation of his academician appointment—the request was denied. When Emperor Guangzong was enshrined in the temple, the arrangement again became nine generations and twelve chambers. From Emperor Taizu's enshrinement at Changling, more than two centuries passed before his seat was finally corrected. In the fourth month of the second year of Qingyuan (1196), the Ministry of Rites and Court of Imperial Sacrifices reported: "A separate shrine to Emperor Xi has been built west of the Grand Ancestral Temple, and the spirit tablets of Emperors Xi, Shun, Yi, and Xuan and their consorts have been relocated there for installation. For this year's early-winter xiá sacrifice, rites shall first be performed at the four ancestors' shrine chambers, then at the Grand Ancestral Temple, pavilion by pavilion in sequence."

理宗紹定四年九月丙戌，京師大火，延及太廟。 太常少卿度正言：「伏見近世大儒侍講朱熹詳考古禮，尚論宗廟之制，畫而為圖，其說甚備。 然其為製，務效於古而頗更本朝之制，故學士大夫皆有異論，遂不能行。 今天降災異，火發民家，延及宗廟，舉而行之，莫此時為宜。 臣於向來備聞其說，今備員禮寺，適當此變，若遂隱默，則為有負，謹為二說以獻。 其一，純用朱熹之說，謂本朝廟製未合於古，因畫為圖，謂僖祖如周後稷，當為本朝始祖。 夫尊僖祖以為始祖，是乃順太祖皇帝之孝心也。 始祖之廟居於中，左昭右穆各為一廟，門皆南向，位皆東向。 祧廟之主藏於始祖之廟夾室，昭常為昭，穆常為穆，自不相亂。 三年合食，則並出祧廟之主，合享於始祖之廟。 始祖東向，群昭之主皆位北而南向，群穆之主皆位南而北向。 昭穆既分，尊卑以定。 其說合於古而宜於今，盡美盡善。 舉而行之，祖宗在天之靈必歆享於此，而垂祐於無窮也。 其一說，則因本朝之制，而參以朱熹之說。 蓋本朝廟製，神宗嘗命禮官陸佃討論，欲復古制，未及施行。 渡江以來，稽古禮文之事，多所未暇。 今欲驟行更革，恐未足以成其事，而徒為紛紛。 或且仍遵本朝之制，自西徂東，並為一列。 惟於每室之後，量展一間，以藏祧廟之主。 每室之前，量展二間，遇三年袷享，則以帷幄幕之，通為一室，盡出諸廟主及祧廟主並為一列，合食其上。 前乎此廟為一室，凡遇袷享，合祭於其室，名為袷享，而實未嘗合。 今量展此三間，後有藏祧主之所，前有祖宗合食之地，於本朝之制，初無大段更革，而頗已得三年大袷之義。 今來朝廷若能舉行朱熹前議，固無以加； 如其不然，姑從後說，亦為允當，不失禮意。 然宗廟之禮，倘無其故，何敢妄議？ 今因大火之後，若加損益，亦惟其時，乞賜詳議。 」有旨，令侍從、禮部、太常集議，後竟不行。
On the bingxu day of the ninth month of the fourth year of Shaoding (1231), a great fire in the capital spread to the Grand Ancestral Temple. Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices Du Zheng memorialized: "I observe that the great scholar of recent times, Lecturer Zhu Xi, exhaustively examined ancient ritual, elevated discussion of the ancestral temple system, illustrated it in diagrams, and his exposition is remarkably complete. Yet his design, striving to emulate antiquity, would considerably alter our dynasty's established system; scholar-officials therefore disputed it, and it was never implemented. Heaven has now sent calamity: fire broke out among the people and spread to the ancestral temple—to implement his design, no moment is more fitting than this. I have long been fully informed of his doctrine; now serving on the ritual staff at this very juncture, silence would be a dereliction of duty; I respectfully submit two proposals. The first proposal purely adopts Zhu Xi's doctrine: our dynasty's temple system does not accord with antiquity; in his diagram Emperor Xi is like Zhou's Hou Ji and should be our dynasty's founding ancestor. Honoring Emperor Xi as founding ancestor accords with Emperor Taizu's filial intent. The founding ancestor's temple stands at the center; zhao to the left and mu to the right each have a separate temple; all doors face south and all seats face east. Spirit tablets from dismantled temples are stored in the founding ancestor's side chambers; zhao always remains zhao and mu always remains mu, never intermingled. Every three years at the combined feast, distant-lineage tablets are all brought out to share offerings at the founding ancestor's temple. The founding ancestor faces east; all zhao tablets stand to the north facing south; all mu tablets stand to the south facing north. With zhao and mu clearly divided, rank and precedence are thereby fixed. This doctrine accords with antiquity and suits the present—altogether excellent. Implemented in full, the ancestors' spirits in Heaven will surely delight in this and bestow protection without end. The second proposal follows our dynasty's established system while incorporating elements of Zhu Xi's doctrine. Regarding our dynasty's temple system, Emperor Shenzong once ordered ritual official Lu Dian to deliberate on restoring ancient practice, but it was never implemented. Since the court crossed south, investigation of ancient ritual texts has largely been neglected. Sudden comprehensive reform may not suffice to accomplish the task and would only provoke uproar. Perhaps for now we should retain our dynasty's system, arraying chambers from west to east in a single row. Only behind each chamber, extend one bay to store distant-lineage tablets. Before each chamber, extend two bays; at the three-year xiá sacrifice, enclose them with canopies and screens to form one chamber, bring out all temple tablets and distant-lineage tablets in a single row, and share the feast together. Previously the temple functioned as separate chambers: at each xiá sacrifice a combined rite was performed within one chamber—called a xiá offering, yet never truly combined. Extending these three bays provides storage for distant-lineage tablets behind and space for ancestors to share the feast before—without major alteration to our dynasty's system, yet largely realizing the meaning of the three-year great xiá. If the court can now implement Zhu Xi's former proposal, nothing could surpass it; if not, the latter proposal is also acceptable for the present and does not depart from ritual intent. Yet regarding ancestral temple ritual, without compelling cause, who would dare propose rash changes? Now, in the wake of the great fire, if reforms are to be made, the moment is opportune—we beg that detailed deliberation be granted. " The emperor ordered attendants-in-waiting, the Ministry of Rites, and the Court of Imperial Sacrifices to deliberate collectively; in the end the proposals were not adopted.
