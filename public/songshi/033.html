1
及元懿太子薨，高宗未有後，而昭慈聖獻皇后亦自江西還行在，後嘗感異夢，密爲高宗言之，高宗大寤。 會右僕射范宗尹亦造膝以請，高宗曰：「太祖以神武定天下，子孫不得享之，遭時多艱，零落可憫。 朕若不法仁宗，爲天下計，何以慰在天之靈！ 」於是詔選太祖之後。 同知樞密院事李回曰：「藝祖不以大位私其子，發于至誠。 陛下爲天下遠慮，合于藝祖，可以昭格天命。 」參知政事張守曰：「藝祖諸子，不聞失德，而傳位太宗，過堯、舜遠甚。 」高宗曰：「此事不難行，朕于'伯'字行中選擇，庶幾昭穆順序。 」而上虞丞婁寅亮亦上書言：「昌陵之後，寂寥無聞，僅同民庶。 藝祖在上，莫肯顧歆，此金人所以未悔禍也。 望陛下于'伯'字行內選太祖諸孫有賢德者。 」高宗讀之，大感歎。 紹興二年五月，選帝育於禁中。 三年二月，除和州防御使，賜名瑗。 壬寅，改貴州。 五年五月，用左僕射趙鼎議，立書院宮中教之，既成，遂以爲資善堂。 帝讀書強記，天資特異。 己亥，制授保慶軍節度使，封建國公。 六月己酉，聽讀資善堂，以徽猷閣待制範沖兼詡善，起居郎朱震兼贊讀，高宗命帝見沖、震皆拜。 十二年正月丁酉，加檢校少保，封普安郡王。
After Prince Yuanyi passed away, Gaozong still had no successor, and Empress Zhao Ci the Sagely and Sacrificing had also returned from Jiangxi to the court. The empress had had a strange dream and confided in Gaozong in secret; he was deeply stirred. About then Right Vice Director Fan Zongyin came forward to urge the same course, and Gaozong said: "Our Founder subdued the empire by martial genius, but his line could not hold the throne; scattered in hard times, they deserve pity. If I do not take Renzong as my model and plan for the empire, how shall I answer those above! " An edict was then promulgated to choose an heir from Taizu's line. Li Hui, Vice Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs, said: "Taizu refused to bequeath the throne to his son out of pure sincerity. Your Majesty's far-sighted care for the empire matches Taizu's and may win Heaven's approval. " Zhang Shou, Participating in Governance, said: "None of Taizu's sons was known for vice, yet he yielded to Taizong—a deed that outshines Yao and Shun. " Gaozong replied: "That is easy to do; I will pick from the 'bo' generation so the ancestral ranks stay in order. " Shangyu Assistant Magistrate Lou Yinliang also memorialized: "Taizu's descendants live in obscurity, no better than common folk. Heaven will not smile on us while Taizu's line languishes—that is why the Jin have not yet turned from disaster. I beg Your Majesty to choose a worthy grandson of Taizu from among the 'bo' generation. " Gaozong read the memorial and sighed with deep feeling. In the fifth month of Shaoxing 2, the chosen heir was brought up inside the palace. In the second month of year 3 he was made Defender of Hezhou and granted the name Yuan. On renyin day his post was changed to Guizhou. In the fifth month of year 5, on Zhao Ding's advice, a palace school was built to teach him; when finished it was called the Hall for Cultivating Goodness. He proved a voracious reader with a remarkable memory and unusual native talent. On jihai day he was formally made Military Commissioner of Baoji Army and created Duke of Jian. On the sixth month, day jiyou, he began study at the Hall for Cultivating Goodness with Fan Chong as instructor and Zhu Zhen as reader; Gaozong commanded him to bow to both. In the first month of year 12, on dingyou day, he was made Junior Guardian and Prince of Pu'an Commandery.
2
三月壬寅，出閣就外第。 十三年九月，秀王歿於秀州。 十四年正月庚辰，用廷臣議，聽解官行服。 十六年四月乙巳，免喪，還舊官。 十七年六月戊午，改常德軍節度使。 二十四年，衢州盜起，秦檜遣殿前司將官辛立將千人捕之，不以聞。 帝入侍言之，高宗大驚。 明日，以問檜，檜謂不足煩聖慮，故不敢聞，俟朝夕盜平則奏矣。 檜退，知爲帝言，忌之。 及檜疾篤，其家秘不以聞，謀以子熺代相，帝又密啟高宗破其奸。 三十年二月癸酉，立爲皇子，更名瑋。 甲戌，詔下。 丙子，制授甯國軍節度使、開府儀同三司，進封建王。 制出，中外大悅。 四月，賜字元瑰。 三十一年十月壬子，以明堂恩，改鎮南軍節度使。 先是，金人犯邊，高宗下詔親征，而兩淮失守，朝臣多陳退避之計，帝不勝其憤，請率師爲前驅。 直講史浩以疾在告，聞之亟入，爲帝言，太子不宜將兵，乃爲草奏，因中宮以進，請衛從以共子職。 高宗因亦欲帝遍識諸將，十二月，遂扈蹕如金陵。 三十二年五月甲子，立爲皇太子，改名昚。 初，高宗久有禪位之意，嘗以諭帝，帝流涕固辭，會有邊事不果。 及歸自金陵，陳康伯求去，高宗復以倦勤諭之。 中書舍人唐文若聞而請對，言不宜急遽，故先下建儲之詔，賜名燁。 監察御史周必大密與康伯言，與唐昭宗名同音，不可。 詔別擬進，乃定今名。 既又命學士承旨洪遵爲太子擇字，遵擬四字以進，皆不稱旨。
In the third month, on renyin day, he moved out of the inner palace to his own residence outside. In the ninth month of year 13 Prince Xiu died in Xiuzhou. On gengchen in the first month of year 14 the court allowed him to leave office and enter mourning dress. On yisi in the fourth month of year 16 he left mourning and returned to his former post. On wuwu in the sixth month of year 17 he was reassigned as Military Commissioner of Changde Army. In year 24 rebels broke out in Quzhou; Qin Hui sent Xin Li of the Palace Front Office with a thousand troops to suppress them without informing the throne. The heir told Gaozong during an audience; Gaozong was shocked. Next day Gaozong asked Qin Hui, who said the matter was too slight to trouble the emperor and that he would report when the rebels were crushed in a day or two. Qin Hui left knowing the heir had spoken against him and bore a grudge. When Qin Hui grew critically ill his household concealed it and schemed to install his son Qi as chief minister; the heir again secretly warned Gaozong and foiled the conspiracy. On guiyou in the second month of year 30 he was made imperial son and renamed Wei. On jiaxu day the edict was issued. On bingzi day he was made Military Commissioner of Ningguo Army and Commissioner with Protocol of the Three Excellencies, and raised to Prince of Jian. When the appointment was announced, the court and the realm rejoiced. In the fourth month he received the courtesy name Yuan Gui. On renzi in the tenth month of year 31, by Bright Hall grace, he was transferred to Military Commissioner of Zhennan Army. Earlier, when the Jin attacked, Gaozong had ordered a personal campaign; after the two Huai fell many ministers urged withdrawal, and the heir, unable to bear it, asked to lead the vanguard. Lecturer Shi Hao, though ill at home, rushed in and argued that a crown prince must not take the field; he drafted a memorial sent through the inner palace asking instead to serve as escort in filial duty. Gaozong also wanted him to meet the commanders; in the twelfth month he accompanied the court to Jinling. On jiazi in the fifth month of year 32 he was made crown prince and renamed Shen. Gaozong had long meant to abdicate and had told him so; he wept and refused, and border troubles prevented it. After returning from Jinling, Chen Kangbo sought retirement, and Gaozong again spoke of his wish to yield the throne. Drafting Academician Tang Wenruo urged caution against haste; an edict to establish the heir was issued first and the name Ye granted. Supervising Censor Zhou Bida told Kangbo in private that it echoed Tang Zhaozong's name and was unacceptable. The throne ordered a new name proposed, and the present name was settled. Hong Zun, Academician Expositor, was again ordered to propose a style; he offered four characters, none of which satisfied.
3
六月甲戌，御筆賜字元永。
On jiaxu in the sixth month the emperor personally wrote the style Yuan Yong.
4
乙亥，內降御劄：「皇太子可即皇帝位。 朕稱太上皇帝，退處德壽宮； 皇后稱太上皇后。 」丙子，遣中使召帝入禁中，面諭之，帝又推遜不受，即趨側殿門，欲還東宮，高宗勉諭再三，乃止。 於是高宗出御紫宸殿，輔臣奏事畢，高宗還宮。 百官移班殿門外，拜詔畢，復入班殿庭。 頃之，內侍掖帝至御榻前，側立不坐，內侍扶掖至七八，乃略就坐。 宰相率百僚稱賀，帝遽興。 輔臣升殿固請，帝愀然曰：「君父之命，出於獨斷。 然此大位，懼不克當。 」班退，太上皇帝即駕之德壽宮，帝服袍履，步出祥曦殿門，冒雨掖輦以行，及宮門弗止。 上皇麾謝再三，且令左右扶掖以還，顧曰：「吾付託得人，吾無憾矣。 」左右皆呼萬歲。 是日，詔有司議太上皇帝、太上皇后尊號以聞，在內諸司日輪官吏應奉德壽宮，增置，朝德壽宮提點、幹辦等官，德壽宮宿衛依皇城及宮門法。 戊寅，大赦。 詔宰相率百官月兩朝德壽宮。 己卯，以即位告於天地、宗廟、社稷。 庚辰，詔五日一朝德壽宮。 以左武大夫龍大淵爲樞密副都承旨，武翼郎曾覿帶禦器械。 癸未，始御後殿。 甲申，詔中外士庶陳時政闕失。 丙戌，詔進宰執官二等。 丁亥，詔以太上皇不許五日一朝，自今月四朝。 復除名勒停人胡銓官、知饒州。 己丑，詔有司月奉德壽宮緡錢十萬。 辛卯，詔罷四川市馬。 壬辰，詔百官日一人入對。 癸巳，蝗。 甲午，上太上皇帝尊號曰光堯壽聖太上皇帝，太上皇后曰壽聖太上皇后。 乙未晦，金人屠原州。
On yihai an inner edict came down: "The crown prince shall at once ascend the throne. I shall take the title Retired Emperor and retire to De'shou Palace; the empress shall be styled Retired Empress. " On bingzi an envoy summoned him; he declined again and made for the Eastern Palace until Gaozong persuaded him repeatedly to stay. Gaozong then appeared at the Purple Forbidden Hall; after the ministers reported affairs he withdrew. Officials moved their ranks outside the doors, bowed to the edict, and re-formed in the hall. Attendants led him to the throne; he stood aside and would not sit until they had steadied him seven or eight times. The chief minister led congratulations; he rose immediately. The ministers pressed him to stay seated; he said gravely: "Father and emperor have decided alone. Yet I fear I am not equal to so great a charge. When court ended the retired emperor went to De'shou Palace; the new emperor walked in robe and shoes through the rain, steadying the carriage, and would not turn back at the gate. The retired emperor waved him off repeatedly and had attendants lead him back, saying: "I leave it to the right man; I have no regret. " All around cried "Long live the emperor!" That day orders went out on retired honorifics, daily rotation of palace staff to serve De'shou, new posts for its administration, and guards under imperial-city law. On wuyin day a general amnesty was declared. The chief minister was ordered to lead officials to De'shou twice a month. On jimao the accession was reported to Heaven, Earth, ancestors, and the altars. On gengchen audiences at De'shou every five days were ordered. Long Dayuan was made Vice Commissioner-General of Military Affairs; Zeng Di received the Imperial Equipment grant. On guiwei he first held court in the rear hall. On jiashen all were invited to report governmental shortcomings. On bingxu chief ministers were promoted two ranks. On dinghai, because the retired emperor would not accept five-day audiences, four per month were set instead. Hu Quan, formerly dismissed and barred from office, was restored as prefect of Raozhou. On jichou De'shou was granted one hundred thousand strings of cash each month. On xinmao the Sichuan horse market was abolished. On renchen one official a day was to attend for audience. On guisi there was a locust plague. On jiawu the retired emperor received the title Guangyao Shousheng Retired Emperor and the retired empress Shousheng Retired Empress. At month's end, on yihai, the Jin massacred Yuanzhou.
5
秋七月戊戌，興州中軍統制吳挺復鞏州。 庚子，判建康府張浚入見。 以雨水、飛蝗，令侍從、台諫條上民間利害。 壬寅，詔戒飭諸郡守臣。 癸卯，以張浚爲少傅、江淮宣撫使，封魏國公。 甲辰，以參知政事汪澈視師湖北、京西。 遣劉珙等使金告即位。 戊申，以四川宣撫使吳璘兼陝西河東路宣撫、招討使。 追復岳飛元官，以禮改葬。 是夜，地震，大風拔木。 己酉，有事於太廟、別廟。 癸丑，馬軍司中軍統制趙撙、忠義軍統領皇甫倜復光州。 甲寅，朝獻景靈宮。 詔淮南諸州存恤淮北來歸之民，權免稅役。 丙辰，以少保、保康軍節度使吳益爲少傅，太尉、甯武軍節度使吳蓋爲開府儀同三司。 丁巳，罷李寶措置海道。 戊午，恩平郡王璩入見。 庚申，以御前軍器所仍隸工部。 辛酉，詔後省看詳中外上書，有可采者以聞。 壬戌，以黃祖舜兼權參知政事。 罷諸路聖節進奉。 詔李顯忠軍馬聽張浚節制。 癸亥，增將士戰傷死者推恩格。 詔蠲四川積年逋負。
In the seventh month, on wuxu, Wu Ting of Xingzhou's middle army recaptured Gongzhou. On gengzi Zhang Jun, acting prefect of Jiankang, was received in audience. Because of floods and locusts, attendants and censors were told to report popular grievances. On renyin an edict warned local magistrates. On guimao Zhang Jun became Junior Tutor and Jiang-Huai Pacification Commissioner, Duke of Wei. On jiachen Wang Che was dispatched to oversee troops in Hubei and Jingxi. Liu Qi and others were sent to the Jin to announce the new reign. On wushen Wu Lin of Sichuan was also made Pacification Commissioner of Shaanxi and Hedong. Yue Fei's former rank was posthumously restored and he was reinterred with honor. That night the earth quaked and a gale uprooted trees. On jiyou rites were held at the Great Temple and subsidiary shrines. On guichou Zhao Zun and Huangfu Tong recaptured Guangzhou. On jiayin he worshipped at the Jingling Palace. Huainan was ordered to care for refugees from north of the river and to suspend their taxes and labor service for a time. On bingchen Wu Yi was made Junior Tutor and Wu Gai Commissioner with Protocol of the Three Excellencies. On dingsi Li Bao's sea-route command was ended. On wuwu Prince of Enping Jun was received. On gengshen the Imperial Front Armory was returned to the Ministry of Works. On xinyou the Secretariat was told to review submitted memorials and report what was useful. On renxu Huang Zuxun was made acting Participating in Governance. Birthday tribute from the circuits was abolished. Li Xianzhong's troops were placed under Zhang Jun's command. On guihai rewards for soldiers wounded or killed in battle were enlarged. An edict canceled Sichuan's long-standing tax arrears.
6
八月乙丑朔，四川馬軍統制高師中與金人戰于摧沙，敗死。 丙寅，吳璘與金人戰於德順軍。 己巳，以翰林學士史浩爲參知政事。 戊寅，率群臣詣德壽宮，奉上太上皇帝、太上皇后尊號冊寶。 于亥，班寬恤事十八條。 起居舍人洪邁、知閣門事張掄坐奉使辱命罷。 甲申，吳璘敗金人於北山。 戊子，追復李光資政殿學土，趙鼎、範沖並還合得恩數。 庚寅，以生日爲會慶節。 追冊故妃郭氏爲皇后。
On the eighth month's new moon, yichou, Gao Shizhong of the Sichuan horse army fought the Jin at Cuisha, was defeated, and died. On bingyin Wu Lin engaged the Jin at Deshun. On jisi Hanlin academician Shi Hao became Participating in Governance. On wuyin he led the court to De'shou to present the retired emperor's and empress's honorific registers and seals. On yuhai eighteen measures of lenient relief were announced. Hong Mai and Zhang Lun were removed for dishonoring their embassy. On jiashen Wu Lin routed the Jin at North Mountain. On wuzi Li Guang was posthumously restored; Zhao Ding and Fan Chong also regained their proper benefits. On gengyin his birthday was declared the Festival of Congregated Celebration. Consort Guo was posthumously made empress.
7
九月甲午，以子愭爲少保、永興軍節度使，進封鄧王； 愷爲雄武軍節度使、開府儀同三司，進封慶王； 惇爲鎮洮軍節度使、開府儀同三司，進封恭王。 甲午，金人攻德順軍東山堡，中軍將李庠戰死。 丁酉，詔開講日召輔臣觀講。 川、陝宣諭使虞允文以論邊事不合罷。 己亥，詔侍從、台諫舉知四川利害可爲都轉運使者。 庚子，以金人來索舊禮，詔宰執、侍從、台諫各陳應敵定論以聞。 辛丑，詔吳璘審度措置，保全川蜀。 乙巳，詔纂錄勳臣名次。 丙午，轉補朱震、范衝子孫官。 庚戌，諡皇后郭氏曰恭懷。 辛亥，振淮東義兵及歸正人。 以總領四川財賦軍馬錢糧王之望爲戶部侍郎、川陝宣諭使，仍命將調兵同防守興州川口。 乙卯，詔虞允文赴吳璘軍議事。 辛酉，以吳璘爲少師。
In the ninth month, jiawu, his son Qi was made Junior Guardian and Yongxing military commissioner and created Prince of Deng; Kai was made Xiongwu commissioner and Three Excellencies protocol commissioner and created Prince of Qing; Dun was made Zhentao commissioner and Three Excellencies protocol commissioner and created Prince of Gong. On jiawu the Jin attacked Deshun's eastern fort; Li Xiang of the middle army fell in battle. On dingyou chief ministers were to attend lectures on lecture days. Yu Yunwen, Sichuan-Shaanxi proclamation commissioner, was dismissed over frontier policy disagreements. On jihai attendants and censors were told to nominate experts on Sichuan fit to be chief transport commissioner. On gengzi, when the Jin demanded the old treaty forms, ministers, attendants, and censors were each to submit a policy for dealing with them. On xinchou Wu Lin was ordered to plan measures to hold Chuan and Shu. On yisi an edict ordered the merit roll of distinguished ministers compiled. On bingwu the descendants of Zhu Zhen and Fan Chong were given office. On gengxu Empress Guo received the posthumous title Gonghuai. On xinhai loyal militia in eastern Huai and submitters from the north were given relief. Wang Zhiwang, head of Sichuan finances and supplies, became Vice Minister of Revenue and Chuan-Shaanxi proclamation commissioner and was to move troops to defend the Xingzhou crossing. On yimao Yu Yunwen was sent to Wu Lin's army for consultation. On xinyou Wu Lin was made Junior Master.
8
冬十月丙寅，詔朝臣舉堪監司、郡守者，戊辰，以岳陽軍節度使居廣開府儀同三司，史浩兼權知樞密院事。 己巳，葉義問罷。 詔登聞鼓院毋沮抑進狀。 庚午，以恩平郡王璩爲少保。 詔會慶節權免上壽。 戊寅，詔張浚、陳俊卿覆實諸將所陳功賞。 改諡皇后郭氏曰安穆。 壬午，官岳飛孫六人。 甲申，契丹招討蕭鷓巴來奔。 金人攻德順城，吳璘擊走之，復遣兵追襲，遂爲所敗。 乙酉，升建州爲建寧府。 戊子，以資政殿學士張燾同知樞密院事。 己丑，安南都護、南平王同李天祚、闍婆國王悉里地茶蘭固野、占城國王鄒時巴蘭並加食邑實封。
In the tenth month, bingyin, officials were to recommend intendant and prefect candidates; on wuchen Ju Guang received Three Excellencies protocol and Shi Hao acting military affairs commissioner. On jisi Ye Yiwen left office. The Court of Appeal was told not to block petitions. On gengwu Prince of Enping Jun was made Junior Guardian. Birthday congratulations at the Festival of Congregated Celebration were suspended for a time. On wuyin Zhang Jun and Chen Junqing were to verify generals' battle honors. Empress Guo's posthumous title was changed to Anmu. On renwu six of Yue Fei's grandsons received appointments. On jiashen Khitan recruit commissioner Xiao Zheba defected. The Jin attacked Deshun; Wu Lin repulsed them but was defeated when he pursued. On yiyou Jianzhou was elevated to Jianning Prefecture. On wuzi Zhang Tao became vice commissioner of military affairs. On jichou Li Tianzuo of Annan, the Java king, and the Champa king received enlarged substantive fiefs.

23
三月丙戌朔，詔張浚視師於淮。 又詔王之望等以幣還。 丁亥，詔荆襄、川陝帥臣嚴邊備，毋先事妄舉。 盧仲賢除名，械送郴州編管。 壬寅。 詔知光州皇甫倜毋招納歸正人。 丙午，王宣等降。 詔三衙戍兵歸司，建康、鎮江大軍更番歸砦。 庚戌，芝生德壽宮。 以戶部侍郎錢端禮爲淮東宣諭使，吏部侍郎王之望爲淮西宣諭使。 詔撫諭兩淮軍民。 壬子，以廣西賊平，詔減高、藤、雷、容四州雜犯死罪囚，釋杖以下，蠲夏秋稅賦。 以忠勇軍隸步軍司，神勁右軍隸鎮江都統司。 癸丑，以王彥爲建康諸軍都統制兼淮西招撫使。
On the third month's new moon Zhang Jun was ordered to inspect the Huai army. Wang Zhiwang's party was again ordered to return the tribute goods. On dinghai Jingxiang and Sichuan-Shaanxi commanders were warned not to provoke incidents. Lu Zhongxian was disbarred and exiled to Chenzhou. On renyin day. Guangzhou prefect Huangfu Tong was forbidden to recruit northern submitters. On bingwu Wang Xuan and others surrendered. Three Commands garrisons returned to barracks; Jiankang and Zhenjiang armies rotated home. On gengxu auspicious fungus appeared at De'shou. Qian Duanli became Huaidong proclamation commissioner and Wang Zhiwang Huaidong proclamation commissioner. The two Huai armies and people were comforted by edict. On renzi, after Guangxi was pacified, death sentences were eased in four prefectures and taxes remitted. The Loyal and Brave Army went to the Foot Soldiers Office; the Divine Valiant Right Army to Zhenjiang command. On guichou Wang Yan commanded Jiankang forces and pacified Huaidong.
24
夏四月庚申，召張浚還朝。 甲子，以李顯忠侵欺官錢給還諸軍。 丁卯，以建康歸正人爲忠毅軍，鎮江爲忠順軍，命蕭琦、蕭鷓巴分領之。 戊辰，罷江、淮都督府。 高麗入貢。 丁丑，張浚罷。 癸未，言者論宰相、執政徇欺之弊，命書寘政事堂。
In the fourth month Zhang Jun was recalled. On jiazi Li Xianzhong's embezzled funds were returned to the troops. On dingmao Jiankang and Zhenjiang submitters became two loyal armies under Xiao Qi and Xiao Zheba. On wuchen the Jiang-Huai headquarters was abolished. Koryo presented tribute. On dingchou Zhang Jun left office. On guiwei critics' charges of ministerial deceit were posted in the Hall of Administration.
25
五月壬辰，復置環衛官。 丙申，詔吳璘毋招納歸正人。 辛丑，詔劉寶量度泗州輕重取捨事宜以聞。 江西總管邵宏淵責授靖州團練副使、南安軍安置，仍征其盜用庫錢。 乙巳，率群臣詣德壽宮賀天申節，始用樂。 丁未，蝗。 詔內外贓私不法官吏，尚書省置籍檢勘。 庚戌，罷招神勁效用軍。 辛亥，鬻兩淮所招戶馬。
In the fifth month Imperial Guard posts were restored. On bingshen Wu Lin was forbidden to recruit northern submitters. On xinchou Liu Bao was to assess whether to hold or abandon Sizhou. Shao Hongyuan was demoted to Nan'an and ordered to repay stolen treasury funds. On yisi the court observed the Tian Shen festival at De'shou with music for the first time. On dingwei locusts appeared. The Department of State Affairs was to register and investigate corrupt officials. On gengxu recruitment of divine valiant effectives ended. On xinhai recruited household horses in the two Huai were sold off.
26
六月甲寅朔，日有食之。 辛酉，以淫雨，詔州縣理滯囚。 戊辰，太白晝見。 壬申，命虞允文棄唐、鄧，允文不奉詔。 丁丑，振江東、兩淮被水貧民。
On the sixth month's new moon there was an eclipse. On xinyou prolonged rain led to orders to clear backlog cases. On wuchen Venus appeared by day. On renshen Yu Yunwen was ordered to abandon Tang and Deng but refused. On dingchou flood victims in Jiangdong and the two Huai were relieved.
27
秋七月乙酉，召虞允文。 以戶部尚書韓仲通爲湖北、京西制置使。 丁亥，洪遵罷。 己丑，以周葵兼權知樞密院事。 遣主管馬軍司公事張守忠以兵詣淮西，措置邊備。 庚子，太白經天。 詔內外文武官年七十不請致仕者，遇郊毋得蔭補。 乙巳。 命海、泗州徹戍。 丁未，雨雹。 戊申，蠲淮東內庫坊場錢一年。 庚戌，洪遵落端明殿學士。 癸丑，以江東、浙西大水，詔侍從、台諫、卿監、郎官、館職陳闕失及當今急務。 是月，罷內侍押班梁珂爲在外宮觀。 移廣西提刑司于容州。
In the seventh month Yu Yunwen was recalled. Han Zhongtong became Hubei-Jingxi arrangement commissioner. On dinghai Hong Zun left office. On jichou Zhou Kui became acting military affairs commissioner. Zhang Shouzhong was sent with cavalry to arrange Huaixi defenses. On gengzi Venus crossed the heavens. Officials over seventy who had not retired could not secure sons' appointments at suburban rites. On yisi day. Garrisons at Hai and Si were withdrawn. On dingwei there was hail. On wushen Huaidong monopoly fees were remitted for a year. On gengxu Hong Zun lost his Hanlin academician title. On guichou floods led attendants, censors, and officials to report failures and urgent issues. That month eunuch Liang Ke was sent to an outer palace post. The Guangxi judicial commission was moved to Rongzhou.
28
九月甲申。 罷內侍李珂賜諡。 甲午，詔江東、浙西監司、守臣講明措置田事。 乙未，交阯入貢。 丁酉，嚴髒吏法。 辛丑，以王之望爲參知政事，權刑部侍郎吳芾爲給事中兼淮西宣諭使。 金人犯邊。 以久雨，出內庫白金四十萬兩，糴米賑貧民。 壬寅，王彥帥師濟江，軍昭關。 癸卯。 命湯思退都督江、淮東西路軍馬，辭不行。 乙巳，復命楊存中爲同都督，錢端禮、吳芾並爲都督府參贊軍事。 罷宣諭司。 仍易國書以付魏杞。 少保、崇信軍節度使趙密落致仕，權領殿前司職事。
In the ninth month, on jiashen day. Eunuch Li Ke was denied a posthumous title. On jiawu Jiangdong and Western Zhe officials were to clarify land policy. On yiwei Jiaozhi sent tribute. On dingyou the law on corrupt officials was tightened. On xinchou Wang Zhiwang became participating minister and Wu Bi drafting academician and Huaidong proclamation commissioner. The Jin invaded the frontier. Prolonged rain led to four hundred thousand taels from the inner treasury to buy rice for the poor. On renyin Wang Yan crossed the Yangzi and camped at Zhao Pass. On guimao day. Tang Situi was ordered to command Jiang-Huai forces but refused. On yisi Yang Cunzhong became co-commander with Qian Duanli and Wu Bi as staff officers. The proclamation offices were abolished. The state letter was revised and given to Wei Qi. Zhao Mi left retirement to head the Palace Front Office temporarily.
29
冬十月甲寅，魏杞至盱眙，金帥以國書未如式，弗受，欲得商、秦地及俘獲人，且邀歲幣三十萬，杞未得進。 丁卯，賀允中罷爲資政殿大學士致仕。 己巳，以周葵兼權知樞密院事，王之望兼同知樞密院事。 庚午，詔輔臣夕對便殿。 丙子，大風。 庚辰，蠲京西、湖北運糧所經州縣秋稅之半。 以靖海軍節度使李寶爲沿海駐紮御前水軍都統制。 辛巳，金人分道渡淮，劉寶棄楚州遁。
In the tenth month Wei Qi reached Xuyi; the Jin rejected the letter's form, demanded Shang, Qin, captives, and three hundred thousand strings of tribute, and blocked his advance. On dingmao He Yunzhong retired as palace academician. On jisi Zhou Kui and Wang Zhiwang became acting military commissioners. On gengwu chief ministers were to attend evening audience in the side hall. On bingzi a great gale blew. On gengchen half the autumn tax along Jingxi and Hubei supply routes was remitted. Li Bao became commander of the coastal imperial navy. On xinsi the Jin crossed the Huai; Liu Bao abandoned Chuzhou and fled.
30
十一月乙酉，知楚州魏勝與金人戰，死之，州遂陷，濠州亦陷。 王彥棄昭關遁，滁州又陷，丙戌，詔諭沿邊將士。 丁亥，詔魏祀等以所齎禮幣犒軍。 杞弗從，命留鎮江侯旨。 復命王之望督視江、淮軍馬。 戊子，以金人侵擾，詔郊杞改用明年。 又詔諭歸正官民軍士。 命王之望同都督江、淮軍馬。 湯思退罷都督。 召陳康伯。 己丑，王之望罷同都督。 庚寅，命楊存中都督江、淮軍馬。 辛卯，湯思退罷，尋以尹穡、晁公武論之，落觀文殿大學士、永州居住，未至而卒。 甲午，以黃榜禁太學生伏闕。 是日，太學生張觀等七十二人上書，請斬湯思退、王之望、尹穡，竄其党洪適、晁公武而用陳康伯、胡銓等，以濟大計。 丙申，遣國信所大通事王抃持周葵書如金帥府，請正皇帝號，爲叔侄之國； 易歲貢爲歲幣，減十萬； 割商、秦地； 歸被俘人，惟叛亡者不與； 誓目大略與紹興同。 以金人犯淮南，詔避殿減膳。 丁酉，詔擇日視師。 戊戌，以少保、觀文殿大學士陳康伯爲尚書左僕射、同中書門下平章事兼樞密使。 庚子，遣兵部侍郎胡銓、右諫議大夫尹穡分詣兩浙措置海道。 贈魏勝甯國軍節度使，諡忠壯。 辛丑，兵部尚書錢端禮賜出身，簽書樞密院事兼提領德壽宮。 壬寅，詔侍從、兩省官日一至都堂議事，有關台諫者亦聽會議。 以顯謨閣學士虞允文同簽書樞密院事。 癸卯，遣王之望勞師江上。 甲辰，金人犯六合縣，步軍司統制崔皋擊卻之。 乙巳，以錢端禮兼權參知政事。 丁未，以顯謨閣直學士沈介爲沿江制置使。 命沿江諸州調保甲分守渡口。 己酉，劉寶落節鉞，爲武泰軍承宣使，王彥落龍神衛四廂都指揮使。
In the eleventh month Wei Sheng of Chuzhou fell fighting the Jin; Chuzhou and Haozhou were lost. Wang Yan fled Zhao Pass; Chuzhou fell; on bingxu frontier troops were instructed by edict. On dinghai Wei Qi was ordered to reward the army with his tribute goods. Qi refused and was held at Zhenjiang awaiting orders. Wang Zhiwang was again ordered to oversee Jiang-Huai forces. On wuzi the suburban sacrifice was postponed to the next year because of Jin attacks. Submitters among officials, people, and soldiers were again comforted by edict. Wang Zhiwang was made co-commander of Jiang-Huai forces. Tang Situi left the command post. The court summoned Chen Kangbo. On jichou Wang Zhiwang was removed as co-commander. On gengyin Yang Cunzhong was put in charge of Jiang-Huai forces. On xinmao Tang Situi fell; Yin Ji and Chao Gongwu then denounced him, and he was stripped of his grand academician rank and sent to Yongzhou, but died en route. On jiawu a yellow notice banned university students from gate protests. That day seventy-two students including Zhang Guan petitioned for the execution of Tang Situi, Wang Zhiwang, and Yin Ji, the exile of Hong Shi and Chao Gongwu, and the appointment of Chen Kangbo and Hu Qian to carry out the national plan. On bingshen Wang Bian of the Credentials Bureau was sent with Zhou Kui's letter to the Jin command to secure imperial titles and an uncle-nephew relationship; to convert tribute into annual payments and cut one hundred thousand strings from the sum; to surrender Shang and Qin; to return prisoners of war but not defectors; The pledge largely followed the Shaoxing terms. Because the Jin attacked Huainan, the court withdrew from the main hall and reduced its meals. On dingyou the court chose a date to inspect the troops. On wuxu Chen Kangbo became left vice director, grand counselor, and military affairs commissioner. On gengzi Hu Qian and Yin Ji were sent to the two Zhe circuits to organize coastal shipping. Wei Sheng was posthumously titled commissioner of Ningguo Army with the name Loyal and Robust. On xinchou Qian Duanli received examination credentials and became drafting military commissioner while heading De'shou Palace. On renyin attendants and provincial officials were ordered to discuss policy daily at the Hall of Administration, with censors invited when relevant. Yu Yunwen was made co-drafting commissioner of military affairs. On guimao Wang Zhiwang was sent to buoy the Yangzi armies. On jiachen the Jin struck Luhe County and were repulsed by Cui Gao. On yisi Qian Duanli was also made acting participating minister. On dingwei Shen Jie became Yangzi coastal arrangement commissioner. Yangzi prefectures were ordered to deploy militia at the ferry crossings. On jiyou Liu Bao lost his command and was demoted to Martial Pacification commissioner, and Wang Yan was removed as Dragon Divine Guard commander.
31
閏月甲寅，陳康伯入見，詔康伯間日一朝，肩輿至殿門，給扶升殿。 丙辰，周葵罷。 王抃見金二帥，皆得其報書以歸。 戊午，蕭琦卒。 壬戌。 詔罷胡銓、尹穡。 丙寅，召韓仲通。 以沈介爲兵部尚書、湖北京西制置使。 戊辰，以金人且退，詔督府擇利擊之，王之望執不可。 乙亥。 之望罷。 丙子，以王抃爲奉使金國通問國信所參議官，持陳康伯報書以行。 丁丑。 金遣張恭愈來迓使者。 詔台諫、侍從、兩省官舉楚、廬、滁、濠四州守臣。 十二月甲申，罷陝西路轉運司。 戊子，魏杞始渡淮。 詔郊祀大禮遵至道典故，改用來年正月一日上辛。 辛卯，以錢端禮爲參知政事兼知樞密院事，虞允文同知樞密院事兼權參知政事，禮部尚書王剛中簽書樞密院事。 丙申，制曰：「比遣王抃，遠抵潁濱，得其要約。 尋澶淵盟誓之信，仿大遼書題之儀，正皇帝之稱，爲叔侄之國，歲幣減十萬之數，地界如紹興之時。 憐彼此之無辜，約叛亡之不遣，可使歸正之士咸起甯居之心。 重念數州之民，罹此一時之難，老稚有蕩析之災，丁壯有系累之苦，宜推蕩滌之宥，少慰凋殘之情。 應沿邊被兵州軍，除逃遁官吏不赦外，雜犯死罪情輕者減一等，餘並放遣。 」遣洪適等賀金主生辰。 詔吳挺市馬赴行在。 己亥，雨雹。 壬寅，罷三衙、江上、荆襄諸軍招軍。 甲辰，遣沿海水軍還屯。 己酉，朝獻景靈宮。 庚戌，朝饗太廟。
On intercalary jia-yin Chen Kangbo was received in audience and granted alternate-day attendance by sedan, with aides to help him into the hall. On bingchen Zhou Kui left office. Wang Bian met both Jin commanders and returned with their replies. On wuwu the court mourned the death of Xiao Qi. On renxu day. Hu Qian and Yin Ji were dismissed by edict. On bingyin Han Zhongtong was called to court. Shen Jie became minister of war and Hubei-Jingxi arrangement commissioner. On wuchen, with the Jin preparing to pull back, headquarters was ordered to strike when advantageous; Wang Zhiwang refused. On yihai day. Wang Zhiwang left office. On bingzi Wang Bian was made Jin envoy and departed with Chen Kangbo's reply. On dingchou day. The Jin sent Zhang Gongyu to receive the envoys. Censors, attendants, and provincial officials were ordered to recommend governors for Chu, Lu, Chuzhou, and Hao. In the twelfth month the Shaanxi transport office was abolished. On wuzi Wei Qi started crossing the Huai. The grand suburban rite was rescheduled to the first upper xin day of next year's first month, following Zhidao precedent. On xinmao Qian Duanli became participating minister and military commissioner; Yu Yunwen became co-commissioner and acting participating minister; Wang Gangzhong became drafting military commissioner. On bingshen an edict declared: "Wang Bian was recently sent to the Ying River and secured the essential terms. It restored Chanyuan pledge and Liao-style letter forms, set imperial titles and uncle-nephew relations, cut annual payments by one hundred thousand strings, and restored Shaoxing borders. Pitying the innocent on both sides and excluding returned defectors, loyalists may again settle in peace. Remembering how several prefectures suffered—old and young dispersed, men bound in toil—sweeping clemency should ease their ruin. In invaded frontier districts, fleeing officials excepted, lesser capital offenses were reduced one grade and others released. Hong Shi and others were sent to celebrate the Jin ruler's birthday. Wu Ting was ordered to buy horses for the court. On jihai there was hail. On renyin recruitment by the Three Commands and Yangzi and Jingxiang armies ended. On jiachen the coastal navy returned to base. On jiyou the emperor worshiped at Jingling Palace. On gengxu the emperor feasted the ancestors at the Imperial Temple.
32
三月甲寅，太白晝見。 己未，御殿復膳。 庚申，以虞允文爲參知政事兼同知樞密院事，王剛中同知樞密院事。 命淮西、湖北、荆襄帥臣措置屯田，復置榷場。 癸亥，黃祖舜薨。 戊辰，白氣亙天。 己巳，罷諸軍額外制領將佐。 乙亥，太白經天。 是春，湖南盜起，入廣東焚掠州縣，官軍討平之。 夏四月庚子，金報問使完顏仲等入見。 乙巳，吳璘入見。
In the third month Venus appeared by day. On jiwei the court resumed hall audiences and full rations. On gengshen Yu Yunwen became participating minister and co-military commissioner; Wang Gangzhong became co-military commissioner. Huai-West, Hubei, and Jingxiang commanders were told to set up colonies and restore trade posts. On guihai the official Huang Zushun died. On wuchen white vapor filled the sky. On jisi extra adjunct command posts in the armies were abolished. On yihai Venus crossed the heavens. That spring Hunan bandits raided into Guangdong; government forces crushed them. In the fourth month Wanyan Zhong and other Jin envoys were received. On yisi Wu Lin was received at court.
33
五月庚戌，以璘爲太傅，封新安郡王。 丙辰，詔有司治皇后家廟。 壬戌，詔監司、帥守講究弊事以聞。 合廣南東、西路監事爲一司。 癸亥，詔總領、帥、漕臣、諸軍都統制並兼提領措置屯田，沿邊守臣兼管屯田事。 丁卯，詔吳璘措置馬綱、水路。 壬申，蠲四川州縣虛額錢。 吳璘改判興元府。 乙亥，詔未銓試人毋得堂除。 丙子，遣李若川等使金賀上尊號。 增置諸路鈐轄、都監。 郴州盜李金等復作亂，遣兵討捕之。
In the fifth month Wu Lin became grand tutor and was made Prince of Xin'an. On bingchen offices were ordered to build the empress's family shrine. On renxu circuit and prefectural officials were told to report abuses. Eastern and Western Guangnan oversight was merged into one commission. On guihai intendants, commanders, transport officials, and army chiefs were all put in charge of colonies, with frontier governors managing local colonies. On dingmao Wu Lin was ordered to organize horse convoys and waterways. On renshen inflated quota taxes in Sichuan were canceled. Wu Lin was transferred to Xingyuan. On yihai direct court appointments without examination were banned. On bingzi Li Ruochuan's party was sent to the Jin to celebrate their honorific title. Circuit controllers and directors were added on the routes. Li Jin of Chenzhou rebelled again and troops were sent to suppress him.
34
六月癸未，王剛中薨。 乙酉，詔恭王府直講王淮傾邪不正，有違禮經，可與外任。 丙戌，以翰林學士洪適簽書樞密院事。 戊子，步軍司統制官崔皋坐奏功冒濫，奪所遷觀察使，止進橫行三官，令本軍自效。 辛卯，以武經郎令德爲安定郡王。 壬辰，以淮南轉運判官姚岳言境內飛蝗自死，奪一官罷之。 丙申，以兩淮守令勞徠安集無效，下詔戒飭之，仍以詔置守令治所。 壬寅，蠲廣東殘破郡縣稅賦。 甲辰，罷湖北、京西制置司。
In the sixth month Wang Gangzhong died. On yiyou lecturer Wang Huai of Gong's household was expelled for corruption and ritual violations. On bingxu Hong Shi became drafting military commissioner. On wuzi Cui Gao lost his observer rank for inflated battle reports and was limited to three lateral promotions, told to prove himself with his unit. On xinmao Ling De was enfeoffed as Prince of Anding. On renchen Yao Yue of Huainan transport was demoted for falsely reporting that locusts had died off. On bingshen ineffective Huai officials were admonished by edict and seats were established for their governance. On renyin taxes in ruined Guangdong districts were forgiven. On jiachen the Hubei-Jingxi headquarters was abolished.
35
秋七月辛亥，詔知州年七十以上者與宮觀。 癸丑。 輔臣晚對選德殿，御坐後有大屏，記注諸道監司、郡守姓名，因令都堂視此書之。 甲寅，借職田租二年，以裨經費。 己未，鑄當二錢。 己巳，蠲關外四州民今年租稅及湖南賊蹂郡縣夏稅。
In the seventh month prefects over seventy were granted palace directorships. On guichou day. At evening audience in Xuande Hall a screen behind the throne listed every intendant and governor, and the Hall of Administration was told to keep the same register. On jia-yin office-field rents were levied for two years to cover costs. On jiwei the two-cash coin was minted. On jisi rents in four frontier prefectures and Hunan summer taxes in bandit-ravaged counties were remitted.
36
八月己卯，以永豐圩田賜建康都統司。 癸未，獲李金。 乙酉，詔立子愭爲皇太子。 丁亥，虞允文罷。 戊子，大赦。 己丑，以洪適爲參知政事兼權知樞密院事，吏部侍郎葉顒簽書樞密院事兼權參知政事。 庚寅，立知州軍、諸路總管鈐轄都監辭見法。 癸巳，錢端禮以避東宮親嫌，罷爲資政殿大學士、提舉萬壽觀。 戊戌，吏部侍郎章服以論虞允文阿附罷，謫居汀州。
In the eighth month Yongfeng polder fields were granted to Jiankang command. On guiwei Li Jin was taken. On yiyou the son Qi was made crown prince. On dinghai Yu Yunwen left office. On wuzi there was a general amnesty. On jichou Hong Shi became participating minister and acting military commissioner, and Ye Yong became drafting military commissioner and acting participating minister. On gengyin day the court issued rules governing how prefectural governors, route commanders, supervisory commissioners, and chief inspectors took leave at audience. On guisi day Qian Duanli was relieved to avoid conflict over his ties to the crown prince, becoming Grand Academician of the Hall for Cherishing Governance with charge over the Longevity Abbey. On wuxu day Zhang Fu of the Ministry of Personnel was dismissed after accusing Yu Yunwen of sycophancy and was banished to live at Tingzhou.
37
九月乙卯，立廣國夫人錢氏爲皇太子妃。 丁巳，申嚴百司官出入局之制。 丁卯，升鼎州爲常德府。 甲戌，以端明殿學士汪澈知樞密院事，洪適兼同知樞密院事。 乙亥，置沿淮諸州都巡檢。
In the ninth month, on yimao day, Lady Qian of the Broad Domain was made crown prince's consort. On dingsi day the court tightened rules on when officials of the central offices might enter or leave their posts. On dingmao day Dingzhou was elevated to Changde Prefecture. On jiaxu day Wang Che was appointed to head the Privy Council while Hong Shi became his associate director. On yihai day chief patrol inspectors were posted to Huai River prefectures.
38
冬十月己卯，遣方滋等使金賀正旦。 戊子，增頭子錢。 歸正人右通直郎劉蘊古坐以軍器法式送北境，伏誅。 壬辰，御大慶殿，冊皇太子。 癸巳，詣德壽宮稱謝。 乙未，詔侍從各舉所知宗室一二人。 丁酉，金遣高衎等來賀會慶節。 乙巳，淮北紅巾賊逾淮劫掠，立賞討捕之，已而知楚州胡明遣巡尉擊殺其首蕭榮。
In the tenth month of winter, on jimao day, Fang Zi and others were dispatched to Jin with New Year's greetings. On wuzi day transaction surcharges were raised. Liu Wengu, a returned loyalist serving as Right Gentlemen for Direct Communication, was put to death for forwarding military-equipment regulations to Jin territory. On renchen day the emperor presided in the Hall of Great Celebration to invest the crown prince. On guisi day he visited Deshou Palace to express gratitude. On yiwei day attendants were commanded each to nominate one or two clansmen they knew for office. On dingyou day Jin dispatched Gao Kan and others with greetings for the Huiqing Festival. On yisi day Huai-north red-turban rebels raided across the river; rewards were offered for their capture, and word came that Chuzhou patrol officers under Hu Ming had slain the ringleader Xiao Rong.
39
十一月辛亥，招收兩淮流散忠義人。 丙寅，白氣亙天。 辛未，遣龍大淵撫諭兩淮，措置屯田，督捕盜賊。
In the eleventh month, on xinhai day, the court recruited loyal volunteers dispersed throughout the two Huai circuits. On bingyin day a white streak filled the sky. On xinwei day Long Dayuan was dispatched to reassure the two Huai circuits, organize military colonies, and hunt down bandits.
40
十二月戊寅，以洪适爲尚書右僕射、同中書門下平章事兼樞密使，汪澈爲樞密使。 命廣東提刑司招安李金餘黨。 癸未，遣王曮等賀金主生辰。 康寅，以葉顒爲參知政事兼同知樞密院事。 辛卯，詔侍從、臺諫、兩省舉堪監司、郡守者各一人，三衙、知閣舉材武可守邊者一人。 庚子，罷兩淮諸州權攝官。 壬寅，金遣烏古論忠弼等來賀明年正旦。 癸卯。 詔樞密院文書依三省式，經中書門下畫黃書讀。
In the twelfth month, on wuyin day, Hong Shi became Right Vice Director, Grand Councillor, and Military Affairs Commissioner, and Wang Che was named Military Affairs Commissioner. Guangdong's judicial commissioner was told to bring in the survivors of Li Jin's band. On guiwei day Wang Yan and others were dispatched with birthday greetings for the Jin emperor. On gengyin day Ye Yong was appointed Participating in Governance and associate director of the Privy Council. On xinmao day attendants, censors, and secretariat officials were each told to nominate one candidate for circuit or prefectural command, while the Three Yamaz and Palace Attendants were each to name one martial man suited to the border. On gengzi day temporary acting posts in the two Huai circuits were eliminated. On renyin day Jin dispatched Wugulun Zhongbi and others with greetings for the next New Year. On guimao day. The court commanded that Privy Council paperwork follow Three Department forms, with yellow endorsement and formal reading at the Secretariat-Chancellery.
41
二年春正月辛酉，省六合戍兵，以所墾田給還復業之民。 辛未，命湖南監司存恤寇盜殘破郡縣。
In spring of year 2, on xinyou in the first month, the Liuhe garrison was cut back and reclaimed fields were given back to returning farmers. On xinwei day Hunan circuit officials were told to succor counties wrecked by raiders.
42
二月丁丑，罷盱眙屯田，振兩浙、江東饑。 戊寅，幸玉津園宴射，遂幸龍井。
In the second month, on dingchou day, Xuyi military colonies were shut down and relief was extended to famine-stricken Zhejiang east and Jiangdong. On wuyin day the emperor feasted and shot at Jade Ford Garden before proceeding to Dragon Well.
43
三月乙巳，禁京西、利州路科役保勝義士。 壬子，詔戒飭刑獄官。 戊午，殿中侍御史王伯庠請裁定奏薦，詔三省、臺諫集議，具條式以聞。 詔：「縣令非兩任，毋除監察御史； 非任守臣，毋除郎官。 著爲令。 」丁卯，賜禮部進士蕭國梁以下四百九十有三人及第、出身。 戊辰，再增諸州軍離軍添差員闕。 辛未，罷洪适右僕射。 癸酉，以給事中、權吏部尚書魏𣏌同知樞密院事兼權參知政事。 丁丑，罷和糴。
In the third month, on yisi day, the Jingxi and Lizhou circuits were barred from levying Baosheng volunteer militia. On renzi day the court issued a warning to prison and judicial officials. On wuwu day Wang Boxiang asked that rules on recommended appointments be settled; the Three Departments and censors were ordered to draft regulations and report. The edict ran: "No county magistrate may be made censor without two full terms in office; and no one who has not held circuit or prefectural office may be appointed to a court gentleman post. The rule was written into law. " On dingmao day Xiao Guoliang and four hundred ninety-three other jinshi graduates received degrees and formal passage. On wuchen day additional slots for retired soldiers given nominal posts in prefectures and armies were expanded again. On xinwei day Hong Shi was dismissed from the Right Vice Directorship. On guiyou day Wei Qi, Supervising Secretary and Acting Minister of Personnel, became associate Privy Council director and acting Participating in Governance. On dingchou day the hedi grain-purchase system was ended.
44
夏四月戊寅，以久雨，命侍從、臺諫議刑政所宜以聞。 減大理、三衙、臨安府及浙西州縣雜犯死罪以下囚一等，釋杖以下。 庚辰，詔兩浙漕臣王炎開平江、湖、秀圍田。 辛巳，避殿減膳。 甲申，太白晝見。 癸巳，御殿復膳。 乙未，汪澈罷。 丁酉，以知荆南府李道憑恃戚里妄作，罷之。
In summer's fourth month, on wuyin day, after long rains attendants and censors were asked to advise on needed reforms in criminal justice. Death sentences and lesser penalties for mixed offenses in the Court of Judicial Review, the Three Yamaz, Lin'an, and western Zhejiang were commuted one degree, and those facing beating or lighter punishment were freed. On gengchen day Transport Commissioner Wang Yan of the two Zhe circuits was told to develop polder farmland in Pingjiang, Huzhou, and Xiuzhou. On xinsi day the emperor withdrew from the main hall and cut back court meals as a drought observance. On jiashen day Venus appeared in daylight. On guisi day the emperor returned to the main hall and restored full court meals. On yiwei day Wang Che was dismissed. On dingyou day Li Dao of Jingnan was ousted for presuming on family ties to the throne and misconduct.
45
五月戊申，張燾薨。 己酉，罷權借職田。 庚戌，葉顒罷。 以魏𣏌爲參知政事，右諫議大夫林安宅同知樞密院事兼權參知政事，中書舍人蔣芾簽書樞密院事。 癸丑，太白晝見，經天。 禁浙西修築圍田。 罷修建康行宮。 丁卯，命監司、守臣預備水旱。
In the fifth month, on wushen day, Zhang Tao died. On jiyou day the practice of borrowing official land allotments was ended. On gengxu day Ye Yong was dismissed. Wei Qi became Participating in Governance; Lin Anzhai, Right Remonstrance Officer, became associate Privy Council director and acting Participating in Governance; Jiang Fu, Drafting Official, was named signing secretary of the Privy Council. On guichou day Venus shone at noon and crossed the sky. Western Zhe was barred from building new polder embankments. Work on the Jiankang traveling palace was stopped. On dingmao day circuit commissioners and prefects were told to ready relief for flood and drought.
46
六月甲戌，罷兩浙路提舉市舶司。 詔諸路監司、帥臣各察守令臧否以聞。 丙子，刑部上《乾道新編特旨斷例》。 戊寅，詔制科權罷注疏出題，守臣、監司亦許解送。 庚辰，封孫挺爲福州觀察使、榮國公，攄爲左千牛衛大將軍。 癸未，詔使相毋奏補文資，七色補官人毋任子，堂吏遷朝議大夫以五員爲額。 乙酉，申嚴內外牒式法，裁其額。 丙戌，廢永豐圩。 戊戌，詔改官人實曆知縣一任，方許關升。 著爲定式。 秋七月己酉，調泉州左翼軍二千人屯許浦鎮。 甲寅，以鎮江都統制戚方爲武當軍節度使。
In the sixth month, on jiaxu day, the two Zhe maritime trade superintendencies were abolished. Each route's civil and military commissioners were commanded to review local prefects and magistrates and report their conduct. On bingzi day the Ministry of Justice presented the Qiandao Newly Compiled Special Edict Case Precedents. On wuyin day decree-exam commentary topics were suspended for the time being, and circuit officials were allowed to forward candidates. On gengchen day Sun Ting was created Military Commissioner of Fuzhou and Duke of the Glory State, and Sun Shu was appointed Grand General of the Left Thousand-Ox Guard. On guiwei day chief ministers were barred from recommending civil posts, seven-color appointees were denied hereditary privilege for sons, and promotions of chancellery clerks to Grand Master for Discussion at Court were capped at five. On yiyou day document formats for inner and outer offices were tightened and their quotas reduced. On bingxu day the Yongfeng polder was dismantled. On wuxu day the court ruled that rank advancement required one documented term as county magistrate. The requirement was fixed as permanent policy. In autumn's seventh month, on jiyou day, two thousand Quanzhou Left Wing troops were posted to Xupu garrison. On jiayin day Zhenjiang commander Qi Fang was appointed Military Commissioner of the Wudang Army.
47
八月辛未朔，詔兩淮行鐵錢，銅錢毋過江北。 癸酉，以武鋒軍隸步軍司。 甲戌。 罷任子年三十得免試參選之令。 丁丑，蠲淮南放歸萬弩手差役二年。 壬午，詔諸州守臣兼訓練禁軍。 癸未，降會子、交子於鎮江、建康務場，令江、淮之人對換。 丙戌，林安宅劾葉顒之子受金失實，罷之。 丁亥，詔安宅筠州居住，溫州大水。 戊子，以魏杞兼同知樞密院事，蔣芾權參知政事。 召葉顒。 庚寅，少保、新興郡王吳蓋薨。 甲午，立中興以來十三處戰功格目。 乙未，詔吳璘復判興州。 丙申，升宣州爲甯國府。 罷戶部諸路歲糴一年。
On the eighth month's new moon, xinwei day, iron cash was authorized in the two Huai circuits while copper cash was forbidden north of the Yangtze. On guiyou day the Wufeng Army was assigned to the Bureau of Foot Soldiers. On jiaxu day. The rule exempting officials' thirty-year-old sons from the qualifying exam was revoked. On dingchou day Huainan's returned crossbowmen were freed from corvée for two years. On renwu day prefects were commanded to oversee training of local imperial-guard units. On guiwei day huizi and jiaozi were released at Zhenjiang and Jiankang exchange offices for circulation among Jiang and Huai residents. On bingxu day Lin Anzhai accused Ye Yong's son of bribery; the charge failed, yet Ye Yong was dismissed. On dinghai day Lin Anzhai was ordered to live at Yunzhou, while Wenzhou was hit by severe floods. On wuzi day Wei Qi became associate Privy Council director and Jiang Fu acting Participating in Governance. Ye Yong was recalled to court. On gengyin day Junior Mentor Wu Gai, Prince of the Emerging Domain, died. On jiawu day the court fixed thirteen grades of battlefield merit dating from the Restoration. On yiwei day Wu Lin was commanded to resume concurrent command at Xingzhou. On bingshen day Xuanzhou was elevated to Ningguo Prefecture. The Ministry of Revenue halted circuit-wide annual grain purchases for a year.
48
九月甲辰，知上元縣李允升犯髒貸死，杖脊刺面，配惠州牢城，籍其貲。 丙午，建康守臣王佐坐縱允升去官，奪三官勒停、建昌軍居住。 余失按官吏及薦舉官奪官有差。 辛亥，遣官按視溫州水災，振貧民，決系囚。 乙卯，詔改造大曆。 辛酉，追封子恪爲邵王，諡曰悼肅。 甲子，詔監司各舉部內知縣、縣令二三人，守臣各舉屬縣一二人。 己巳，魏𣏌等上神宗、哲宗、徽宗三朝《帝紀》、《太上皇聖政》。 太白晝見。 是月，詔舉將帥，置章奏簿。
In the ninth month, on jiachen day, Shangyuan magistrate Li Yunzheng was convicted of capital corruption; his sentence was commuted to beating, facial tattoo, banishment to Huizhou's prison colony, and confiscation of his estate. On bingwu day Jiankang prefect Wang Zuo was ousted for releasing Yunzheng, stripped of three ranks, dismissed, and sent to live at Jianchang. Other officials under review and those who had recommended Yunzheng were demoted in differing measure. On xinhai day commissioners were dispatched to survey Wenzhou's flood damage, aid the needy, and review jailed cases. On yimao day the court commanded a revision of the official calendar. On xinyou day the deceased Prince Ke was created posthumous Prince of Shao with the temple name Daosu. On jiazi day circuit commissioners were told each to nominate two or three capable magistrates in their circuits, and prefects one or two from their counties. On jisi day Wei Qi and colleagues presented annals of Shenzong, Zhezong, and Huizong and the Retired Emperor's Sage Governance records. Venus appeared in daylight. That month the court called for nominations of commanders and set up a register of military memorials.
49
冬十月癸酉，上《太上皇聖政》于德壽宮。 乙亥，遣薛良朋等使金賀正旦。 己卯，減饒州歲貢金三之一，蠲諸路灑坊逋賦。 戊子，知峽州呂令問坐縱髒吏知夷陵縣韓贄胄去官，奪二官、鄂州居住。 辛卯，雨雹。 金遣魏子平等來賀會慶節。 十一月丙午，楊存中薨。 己酉，盡出內藏及南庫銀以易會子，官司並以錢銀支遣，民間從便。 兩淮總領所許自造會子。 鬻諸路營田。 壬子，詔修祥曦殿記注。 乙卯，密詔四川制置使汪應辰：如吳璘不起，收其宣撫使牌印，權行主管職事。 甲子，大閱。 戊辰，築郢州城。 是月，詔汰冗兵。 十二月庚午朔，白氣亙天。 癸酉，詔三省、侍從、台諫、兩淮漕臣、郡守，條具兩淮鐵錢、交子利害以聞。 乙亥，遣梁克家等賀金主生辰。 己卯，以資政殿學士葉顒知樞密院事。 辛巳，詔免進呈《欽宗日曆》，送國史院修纂實錄。 壬午，追封楊存中爲和王。 甲申，以葉顒爲尚書左僕射，魏杞右僕射，並同中書門下平章事兼樞密使。 蔣芾參知政事，吏部尚書陳俊卿同知樞密院事兼權參知政事。 庚寅，詔宰相領兼制國用使，參知政事同知國用事。 癸巳，詔監司、守臣舉廉吏。 丙申，金遣烏古論元忠等來賀明年正旦。 以江東兵馬鈐轄王抃爲帶禦器械。 是歲，裁定內外軍額。
In winter's tenth month, on guiyou day, the Retired Emperor's Sage Governance was submitted at Deshou Palace. On yihai day Xue Liangpeng and others were dispatched to Jin with New Year's greetings. On jimao day Raozhou's yearly gold levy was cut by a third and distillery tax arrears throughout the circuits were forgiven. On wuzi day Xiazhou prefect Lü Lingwen was dismissed for releasing the corrupt Yiling magistrate Han Zanzou, demoted two ranks, and sent to live at Ezhou. On xinmao day hail fell. Jin dispatched Wei Ziping and others with greetings for the Huiqing Festival. In the eleventh month, on bingwu day, Yang Cunzhong died. On jiyou day inner-treasury and southern-vault silver was poured out to back huizi notes; official payments would be in coin and silver, with private exchange left to convenience. The Two Huai headquarters were authorized to issue huizi on their own. Circuit military farms were put up for sale. On renzi day the court commanded an overhaul of the Hall of Auspicious Dawn annals. On yimao day a confidential order to Wang Yingchen in Sichuan instructed that if Wu Lin refused appointment, his commissioner's seal and tally were to be taken and his post managed provisionally. On jiazi day the court held a grand military review. On wuchen day construction of Yingzhou's fortifications was completed. That month the court commanded a purge of superfluous soldiers. On the twelfth month's new moon, gengwu day, a white streak filled the sky. On guiyou day the Three Departments, attendants, censors, Two Huai transport officials, and prefects were told to assess iron cash and jiaozi in the two Huai circuits and submit findings. On yihai day Liang Kejia and others were dispatched with birthday greetings for the Jin emperor. On jimao day Ye Yong, Academician of the Hall for Cherishing Governance, was appointed Privy Council director. On xinsi day the court waived formal presentation of the 《Chronicle Calendar of Emperor Qinzong》 and sent it to the State History Office to compile the Veritable Record. On renwu day the late Yang Cunzhong was created Prince of He. On jiashen day Ye Yong became Left Vice Director and Wei Qi Right Vice Director, each Grand Councillor and Military Affairs Commissioner. Jiang Fu became Participating in Governance, and Chen Junqing, Minister of Personnel, associate Privy Council director and acting Participating in Governance. On gengyin day chief councillors were told to lead the State Revenue Commission and Participating in Governance officials to serve as its deputies. On guisi day circuit commissioners and prefects were commanded to nominate honest local officials. On bingshen day Jin dispatched Wugulun Yuanzhong and others with greetings for the next New Year. Jiangdong horse commissioner Wang Bian was appointed Bearer of Imperial Armaments. That year the court settled troop establishments throughout the realm.
