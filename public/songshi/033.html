1
及元懿太子薨，高宗未有後，而昭慈聖獻皇后亦自江西還行在，後嘗感異夢，密爲高宗言之，高宗大寤。 會右僕射范宗尹亦造膝以請，高宗曰：「太祖以神武定天下，子孫不得享之，遭時多艱，零落可憫。 朕若不法仁宗，爲天下計，何以慰在天之靈！ 」於是詔選太祖之後。 同知樞密院事李回曰：「藝祖不以大位私其子，發于至誠。 陛下爲天下遠慮，合于藝祖，可以昭格天命。 」參知政事張守曰：「藝祖諸子，不聞失德，而傳位太宗，過堯、舜遠甚。 」高宗曰：「此事不難行，朕于'伯'字行中選擇，庶幾昭穆順序。 」而上虞丞婁寅亮亦上書言：「昌陵之後，寂寥無聞，僅同民庶。 藝祖在上，莫肯顧歆，此金人所以未悔禍也。 望陛下于'伯'字行內選太祖諸孫有賢德者。 」高宗讀之，大感歎。 紹興二年五月，選帝育於禁中。 三年二月，除和州防御使，賜名瑗。 壬寅，改貴州。 五年五月，用左僕射趙鼎議，立書院宮中教之，既成，遂以爲資善堂。 帝讀書強記，天資特異。 己亥，制授保慶軍節度使，封建國公。 六月己酉，聽讀資善堂，以徽猷閣待制範沖兼詡善，起居郎朱震兼贊讀，高宗命帝見沖、震皆拜。 十二年正月丁酉，加檢校少保，封普安郡王。
After Prince Yuanyi's death, Emperor Gaozong still had no successor, and Empress Dowager Meng had also come back from Jiangxi to the court at Lin'an. She had had a portentous dream and confided it to Gaozong in private; the emperor was deeply stirred. Right at that moment Vice Director Fan Zongyin pressed the matter in audience. Gaozong said, "Our Founder subdued the empire by martial genius, yet his line could not hold it; battered by hard times, their fall is heartbreaking. If I do not take Renzong as my model and choose for the good of the empire, how shall I answer those above? " He then ordered that an heir be chosen from Taizu's line. Li Hui, deputy head of the Bureau of Military Affairs, said, "Taizu refused to keep the throne for his son—that came from perfect sincerity. Your Majesty's far-sighted choice for the realm matches Taizu's act and will show Heaven that the mandate is honored. " Zhang Shou, a participating administrator, said, "None of Taizu's sons was known for vice, yet he gave the throne to Taizong—a deed that puts even Yao and Shun in the shade. " Gaozong replied, "That is easily done. I will pick from the 'bo' generation so the ancestral ranks stay in order. " Lou Yinliang, assistant magistrate of Shangyu, wrote in, "Since Changling the imperial branch has dwindled to obscurity, scarcely above the people. Taizu in heaven will not look kindly on us; that is why the Jin have not yet turned from their violence. Choose, sire, a worthy grandson of Taizu from the 'bo' generation. " Gaozong read the memorial and was deeply moved. In the fifth month of Shaoxing 2 (1132), the boy was taken into the palace to be reared. In the second month of year 3 he was made Defender of Hezhou and named Yuan. On renyin day his post was changed to Guizhou. In the fifth month of year 5, on Zhao Ding's advice, a school was set up inside the palace; when finished it was called the Hall of Cultivating Goodness. He was a prodigious reader with a remarkable memory and unusual talent. On jihai day he was appointed Military Commissioner of Baoqing and created Duke of the State. On jiyou day in the sixth month he began study at the Hall of Cultivating Goodness, with Fan Chong as tutor and Zhu Zhen as reader. Gaozong commanded that he bow to them on meeting. In the first month of year 12, on dingyou day, he was made Junior Guardian and created Prince of Pu'an.
2
三月壬寅，出閣就外第。 十三年九月，秀王歿於秀州。 十四年正月庚辰，用廷臣議，聽解官行服。 十六年四月乙巳，免喪，還舊官。 十七年六月戊午，改常德軍節度使。 二十四年，衢州盜起，秦檜遣殿前司將官辛立將千人捕之，不以聞。 帝入侍言之，高宗大驚。 明日，以問檜，檜謂不足煩聖慮，故不敢聞，俟朝夕盜平則奏矣。 檜退，知爲帝言，忌之。 及檜疾篤，其家秘不以聞，謀以子熺代相，帝又密啟高宗破其奸。 三十年二月癸酉，立爲皇子，更名瑋。 甲戌，詔下。 丙子，制授甯國軍節度使、開府儀同三司，進封建王。 制出，中外大悅。 四月，賜字元瑰。 三十一年十月壬子，以明堂恩，改鎮南軍節度使。 先是，金人犯邊，高宗下詔親征，而兩淮失守，朝臣多陳退避之計，帝不勝其憤，請率師爲前驅。 直講史浩以疾在告，聞之亟入，爲帝言，太子不宜將兵，乃爲草奏，因中宮以進，請衛從以共子職。 高宗因亦欲帝遍識諸將，十二月，遂扈蹕如金陵。 三十二年五月甲子，立爲皇太子，改名昚。 初，高宗久有禪位之意，嘗以諭帝，帝流涕固辭，會有邊事不果。 及歸自金陵，陳康伯求去，高宗復以倦勤諭之。 中書舍人唐文若聞而請對，言不宜急遽，故先下建儲之詔，賜名燁。 監察御史周必大密與康伯言，與唐昭宗名同音，不可。 詔別擬進，乃定今名。 既又命學士承旨洪遵爲太子擇字，遵擬四字以進，皆不稱旨。
In the third month, on renyin day, he moved out of the palace to his own residence. In the ninth month of year 13, Prince Xiu died at Xiuzhou. In the first month of year 14, on gengchen day, the court allowed him to leave office and mourn. In the fourth month of year 16, on yisi day, mourning ended and he returned to his post. On wuwu day in the sixth month of year 17 he was transferred to Military Commissioner of Changde. In year 24 rebels broke out in Quzhou. Qin Hui sent a thousand troops under Xin Li without informing the throne. The prince mentioned it while attending Gaozong, who was shocked. Next day Gaozong questioned Qin Hui, who said the matter was too slight to trouble the emperor and that he would report once the rebels were crushed. Qin Hui left knowing the prince had told, and bore a grudge. When Qin Hui fell seriously ill his household hid it and schemed to put his son Qi in power; the prince again warned Gaozong and foiled them. On guiyou day in the second month of year 30 he was made imperial son and renamed Wei. On jiaxu day the edict was issued. On bingzi day he was made Military Commissioner of Ningguo and Grand Master with honors equal to the Three Dukes, and advanced to Prince of Jian. Court and country rejoiced when the appointment was announced. In the fourth month he received the courtesy name Yuanrui. On renzi day in the tenth month of year 31, by Bright Hall grace, he was transferred to Military Commissioner of Zhennan. Earlier, when the Jin attacked, Gaozong had ordered a personal campaign; after the Huai line collapsed many ministers urged withdrawal. The prince, furious, volunteered to lead the van. Lecturer Shi Hao, though ill, rushed in and argued that a crown prince must not take the field. He drafted a memorial sent through the inner court, offering instead to guard the emperor as a son should. Gaozong also wanted him to meet the commanders; in the twelfth month he escorted the court to Jinling. On jiazi day in the fifth month of year 32 he was made crown prince and renamed Shen. Gaozong had long meant to abdicate and had told him so; the prince wept and refused, and border troubles postponed the plan. After the return from Jinling, Chen Kangbo sought to leave office, and Gaozong again spoke of abdication. Tang Wenruo, drafting academician, urged caution; the heir-establishment edict came first, with the name Ye. Censor Zhou Bida told Kangbo privately that Ye echoed Tang Zhaozong's name and must not stand. The court ordered a new name; the present one was chosen. Gaozong again had Hong Zun propose a courtesy name; four were offered and all rejected.
3
六月甲戌，御筆賜字元永。
On jiaxu day in the sixth month the emperor wrote the courtesy name Yuanyong.
4
乙亥，內降御劄：「皇太子可即皇帝位。 朕稱太上皇帝，退處德壽宮； 皇后稱太上皇后。 」丙子，遣中使召帝入禁中，面諭之，帝又推遜不受，即趨側殿門，欲還東宮，高宗勉諭再三，乃止。 於是高宗出御紫宸殿，輔臣奏事畢，高宗還宮。 百官移班殿門外，拜詔畢，復入班殿庭。 頃之，內侍掖帝至御榻前，側立不坐，內侍扶掖至七八，乃略就坐。 宰相率百僚稱賀，帝遽興。 輔臣升殿固請，帝愀然曰：「君父之命，出於獨斷。 然此大位，懼不克當。 」班退，太上皇帝即駕之德壽宮，帝服袍履，步出祥曦殿門，冒雨掖輦以行，及宮門弗止。 上皇麾謝再三，且令左右扶掖以還，顧曰：「吾付託得人，吾無憾矣。 」左右皆呼萬歲。 是日，詔有司議太上皇帝、太上皇后尊號以聞，在內諸司日輪官吏應奉德壽宮，增置，朝德壽宮提點、幹辦等官，德壽宮宿衛依皇城及宮門法。 戊寅，大赦。 詔宰相率百官月兩朝德壽宮。 己卯，以即位告於天地、宗廟、社稷。 庚辰，詔五日一朝德壽宮。 以左武大夫龍大淵爲樞密副都承旨，武翼郎曾覿帶禦器械。 癸未，始御後殿。 甲申，詔中外士庶陳時政闕失。 丙戌，詔進宰執官二等。 丁亥，詔以太上皇不許五日一朝，自今月四朝。 復除名勒停人胡銓官、知饒州。 己丑，詔有司月奉德壽宮緡錢十萬。 辛卯，詔罷四川市馬。 壬辰，詔百官日一人入對。 癸巳，蝗。 甲午，上太上皇帝尊號曰光堯壽聖太上皇帝，太上皇后曰壽聖太上皇后。 乙未晦，金人屠原州。
On yihai day an inner edict read, "The crown prince shall take the throne at once. I shall take the title Retired Emperor and live at De'shou Palace; the empress shall be Retired Empress. " On bingzi day envoys summoned him; Gaozong told him in person. He refused again and made for the Eastern Palace until Gaozong pressed him repeatedly to stay. Gaozong then appeared at Zichen Hall; when the ministers had reported, he withdrew. Officials moved outside the gate, bowed to the edict, and re-formed in the courtyard. Attendants brought him to the throne; he stood aside and would not sit until they had steadied him seven or eight times. The chief minister led felicitations; the new emperor rose immediately. The ministers pressed him to be seated; somberly he said, "My father and lord has decided this alone. Yet I fear I am not equal to so great a charge. " When court ended the retired emperor went to De'shou. The new emperor walked out in robe and shoes, held the carriage through rain, and would not turn back at the gate. The retired emperor waved him off, had attendants pull him back, and said, "I leave it to the right man; I have no regret. " All around cried "Long may he live!" That day orders went out to fix honorific titles and to staff and guard De'shou Palace like the inner city. On wuyin day there was a general amnesty. The chief minister was to lead officials to De'shou twice a month. On jimao day the accession was reported to Heaven, Earth, temples, and altars. On gengchen day court ordered visits to De'shou every five days. Long Dayuan became deputy commissioner of military affairs; Zeng Di received charge of imperial arms. On guiwei day he began holding court in the rear hall. On jiashen day all were invited to report policy failures. On bingxu day chief ministers were promoted two ranks. On dinghai day, because the retired emperor declined five-day visits, four monthly audiences were set. Hu Quan, long disgraced and dismissed, was restored as prefect of Raozhou. On jichou day De'shou was granted one hundred thousand strings of cash per month. On xinmao day Sichuan horse markets were abolished. On renchen day one official a day was to have audience. On guisi day locusts appeared. On jiawu day the retired emperor was titled Guangyao Shousheng Retired Emperor and the empress Shousheng Retired Empress. On the last day, yiwei, the Jin massacred Yuanzhou.
5
秋七月戊戌，興州中軍統制吳挺復鞏州。 庚子，判建康府張浚入見。 以雨水、飛蝗，令侍從、台諫條上民間利害。 壬寅，詔戒飭諸郡守臣。 癸卯，以張浚爲少傅、江淮宣撫使，封魏國公。 甲辰，以參知政事汪澈視師湖北、京西。 遣劉珙等使金告即位。 戊申，以四川宣撫使吳璘兼陝西河東路宣撫、招討使。 追復岳飛元官，以禮改葬。 是夜，地震，大風拔木。 己酉，有事於太廟、別廟。 癸丑，馬軍司中軍統制趙撙、忠義軍統領皇甫倜復光州。 甲寅，朝獻景靈宮。 詔淮南諸州存恤淮北來歸之民，權免稅役。 丙辰，以少保、保康軍節度使吳益爲少傅，太尉、甯武軍節度使吳蓋爲開府儀同三司。 丁巳，罷李寶措置海道。 戊午，恩平郡王璩入見。 庚申，以御前軍器所仍隸工部。 辛酉，詔後省看詳中外上書，有可采者以聞。 壬戌，以黃祖舜兼權參知政事。 罷諸路聖節進奉。 詔李顯忠軍馬聽張浚節制。 癸亥，增將士戰傷死者推恩格。 詔蠲四川積年逋負。
In the seventh month, on wuxu day, Wu Ting of Xingzhou recovered Gongzhou. On gengzi day Zhang Jun, acting prefect of Jiankang, had audience. Rain and locusts led to orders for attendants and censors to report popular grievances. On renyin day an edict warned prefects throughout the realm. On guimao day Zhang Jun became Junior Tutor and Jiang-Huai pacification commissioner, Duke of Wei. On jiachen day Wang Che was sent to oversee Hubei and Jingxi armies. Liu Qi and others were sent to the Jin to announce the enthronement. On wushen day Wu Lin of Sichuan was also made pacifier of Shaanxi and Hedong. Yue Fei's rank was posthumously restored and he was reburied with honor. That night the earth shook and a gale uprooted trees. On jiyou day rites were held at the Grand Temple and subsidiary shrines. On guichou day Zhao Zun and Huangfu Tong recovered Guangzhou. On jiayin day offerings were presented at Jingling Palace. Huainan prefectures were to aid refugees from north of the Huai and temporarily exempt them from tax and labor. On bingchen day Wu Yi was made Junior Tutor and Wu Gai Grand Master with Three-Duke honors. On dingsi day Li Bao's sea-route commission was ended. On wuwu day Prince of Enping Wang Shan had audience. On gengshen day the Imperial Armory returned to the Ministry of Works. On xinyou day the Secretariat was to review submitted memorials and report what merited adoption. On renxu day Huang Zushun became acting participating administrator. Birthday tribute from all circuits was abolished. Li Xianzhong's troops were placed under Zhang Jun's command. On guihai day benefits for soldiers wounded or killed in battle were enlarged. An edict canceled long-standing tax arrears in Sichuan.
6
八月乙丑朔，四川馬軍統制高師中與金人戰于摧沙，敗死。 丙寅，吳璘與金人戰於德順軍。 己巳，以翰林學士史浩爲參知政事。 戊寅，率群臣詣德壽宮，奉上太上皇帝、太上皇后尊號冊寶。 于亥，班寬恤事十八條。 起居舍人洪邁、知閣門事張掄坐奉使辱命罷。 甲申，吳璘敗金人於北山。 戊子，追復李光資政殿學土，趙鼎、範沖並還合得恩數。 庚寅，以生日爲會慶節。 追冊故妃郭氏爲皇后。
On the first day of the eighth month, Gao Shizhong of the Sichuan cavalry fought the Jin at Cuisha, was defeated, and died. On bingyin day Wu Lin engaged the Jin at Deshun. On jisi day Hanlin academician Shi Hao became participating administrator. On wuyin day he led the court to De'shou to present honorific seals to the retired emperor and empress. On yihai day eighteen measures of clemency were announced. Hong Mai and Zhang Lun were dismissed for failing their embassy. On jiashen day Wu Lin defeated the Jin at North Mountain. On wuzi day Li Guang's academician rank was restored; Zhao Ding and Fan Chong received their due honors. On gengyin day the emperor's birthday became the Festival of Congregant Celebration. The late Consort Guo was posthumously made empress.
7
九月甲午，以子愭爲少保、永興軍節度使，進封鄧王； 愷爲雄武軍節度使、開府儀同三司，進封慶王； 惇爲鎮洮軍節度使、開府儀同三司，進封恭王。 甲午，金人攻德順軍東山堡，中軍將李庠戰死。 丁酉，詔開講日召輔臣觀講。 川、陝宣諭使虞允文以論邊事不合罷。 己亥，詔侍從、台諫舉知四川利害可爲都轉運使者。 庚子，以金人來索舊禮，詔宰執、侍從、台諫各陳應敵定論以聞。 辛丑，詔吳璘審度措置，保全川蜀。 乙巳，詔纂錄勳臣名次。 丙午，轉補朱震、范衝子孫官。 庚戌，諡皇后郭氏曰恭懷。 辛亥，振淮東義兵及歸正人。 以總領四川財賦軍馬錢糧王之望爲戶部侍郎、川陝宣諭使，仍命將調兵同防守興州川口。 乙卯，詔虞允文赴吳璘軍議事。 辛酉，以吳璘爲少師。
In the ninth month, on jiawu day, Prince Qi was made Junior Guardian of Yongxing and advanced to Prince of Deng; Kai was made Military Commissioner of Xiongwu with Three-Duke honors and advanced to Prince of Qing; Dun was made Military Commissioner of Zhentao with Three-Duke honors and advanced to Prince of Gong. On jiawu day the Jin attacked Deshun's eastern fort; Commander Li Xiang was killed. On dingyou day lectures were opened to the chief ministers. Yu Yunwen, commissioner for Sichuan and Shaanxi, was dismissed over border policy. On jihai day attendants and censors were to recommend a Sichuan transport chief. On gengzi day, when the Jin demanded the old rites, ministers were ordered to submit policies for response. On xinchou day Wu Lin was ordered to secure Sichuan. On yisi day the court ordered a register of meritorious ministers. On bingwu day the sons of Zhu Zhen and Fan Chong received offices. On gengxu day Empress Guo was given the posthumous title Gonghuai. On xinhai day east-Huai volunteers and defectors were aided. Wang Zhiwang, who oversaw Sichuan supplies, became vice minister and Sichuan-Shaanxi commissioner and was to move troops to guard Xingzhou. On yimao day Yu Yunwen was sent to Wu Lin's headquarters. On xinyou day Wu Lin was made Junior Preceptor.
8
冬十月丙寅，詔朝臣舉堪監司、郡守者，戊辰，以岳陽軍節度使居廣開府儀同三司，史浩兼權知樞密院事。 己巳，葉義問罷。 詔登聞鼓院毋沮抑進狀。 庚午，以恩平郡王璩爲少保。 詔會慶節權免上壽。 戊寅，詔張浚、陳俊卿覆實諸將所陳功賞。 改諡皇后郭氏曰安穆。 壬午，官岳飛孫六人。 甲申，契丹招討蕭鷓巴來奔。 金人攻德順城，吳璘擊走之，復遣兵追襲，遂爲所敗。 乙酉，升建州爲建寧府。 戊子，以資政殿學士張燾同知樞密院事。 己丑，安南都護、南平王同李天祚、闍婆國王悉里地茶蘭固野、占城國王鄒時巴蘭並加食邑實封。
In the tenth month, on bingyin day officials were to recommend intendants and prefects; on wuchen Ju Guang received Three-Duke honors and Shi Hao acted as military commissioner. On jisi day Ye Yiwen left office. The drum court was forbidden to block petitions. On gengwu day Prince of Enping Wang Shan became Junior Guardian. Birthday congratulations at the festival were suspended. On wuyin day Zhang Jun and Chen Junqing were to verify generals' claims of merit. Empress Guo's posthumous title was changed to Anmu. On renwu day six of Yue Fei's grandsons received office. On jiashen day Khitan pacification commissioner Xiao Zheba defected. The Jin attacked Deshun; Wu Lin repulsed them but was defeated when he pursued. On yiyou day Jianzhou became Jianning Prefecture. On wuzi day Zhang Tao became vice commissioner of military affairs. On jichou day the rulers of Annan, Java, and Champa received enlarged fiefs.
9

10

11

The Anqing commissioner Shi Qian offered half his salary for the army. Thereafter similar requests from the imperial clan were all granted. On wuchen day the chief ministers halved their salaries by request. On jimao day Huai refugees and Shandong loyalists were aided. On guiwei day Huang Zushun left office. On gengyin day Qin Hui's faction was banished from the capital.
12
三月壬辰朔，金左副元帥紇石烈志寧以書取侵地。 癸巳，以張燾爲參知政事，御史中丞辛次膺同知樞密院事，葉義問落端明殿學士、饒州居住。 丙申，雨雹。 丁酉，詔戶部置局，議節浮費。 己亥，楊存中等乞減半奉如宰執例，許之。 庚子，以龍大淵知閣門事，曾覿同知閣門事。 壬寅，陳康伯上欽宗陵名曰永獻。 乙巳，詔求遺逸。 丁未，詔修《太上皇帝聖政》。 罷龍大淵，別與差遣。 曾覿復帶禦器械。 召張浚。 己酉，張燾罷。 立選人減舉主法。 甲寅，復龍大淵知閤門事。 曾覿同閣門事，給事中、中書舍人留黃不行。 乙卯，詔飭郡縣吏。 庚申，以久雨，命有司振災傷，察刑禁。
In the third month, on renchen day, the Jin commander Zhining demanded return of seized lands. On guisi day Zhang Tao and Xin Ciying were promoted; Ye Yiwen was demoted to Raozhou. On bingshen day hail fell. On dingyou day the Revenue Ministry was to cut waste. On jihai day Yang Cunzhong and others halved their salaries like the ministers. On gengzi day Long Dayuan and Zeng Di received gate commissions. On renyin day Chen Kangbo proposed Qinzong's temple name Yongxian. On yisi day the court sought hidden talent. On dingwei day compilation of the retired emperor's Sagely Governance was ordered. Long Dayuan was dismissed and reassigned. Zeng Di again received charge of imperial arms. Zhang Jun was summoned. On jiyou day Zhang Tao left office. Rules reducing sponsors for examination candidates were set. On jiayin day Long Dayuan returned to the gate commission. Zeng Di's appointment was blocked when drafting officials withheld the edict. On yimao day local clerks were admonished. On gengshen day prolonged rain led to disaster relief and review of prisons.
13

In the fourth month, on yichou day, annual promotion quotas were set. On wuchen day Zhang Jun proposed crossing the Huai without prior consultation of the ministries. On renshen day five hundred thirty-eight new Jinshi were graduated. On yihai day Wang Zhiwang left office. On renwu day revenue officials and censors were to cut waste. On guiwei day two hundred fifty thousand taels of silver were allotted to the Jiang-Huai command. On wuzi day Zhang Jun sent Shao Hongyuan to Xuyi. On jichou day Li Xianzhong was sent to Dingyuan. That month the Jin took Huanzhou; Qiang Ni and his brother Zhen died defending it.
14
五月壬辰，申嚴鋪翠銷金及神祠僭擬之禁。 丁酉，李顯忠復靈壁縣。 邵宏淵次虹縣，金人拒之。 戊戌，顯忠東趨虹縣。 庚子，復虹縣，金知泗州蒲察徒穆及同知泗州大周仁降。 辛丑，命左右史日更立前殿。 壬寅，張浚渡江視師。 癸卯，金右翼軍都統蕭琦降于李顯忠。 甲辰，顯忠及宏淵敗金人於宿州。 乙己，史浩罷。 追復司馬康右諫議大夫。 丙午，復宿州，戮金兵數千人。 建康前軍統領官王珙巷戰，死之。 丁未，以辛次膺爲參知政事，翰林學士承旨洪遵同知樞密院事。 督諸路開營田。 辛亥，詣德壽宮賀天申節。 金紇石烈志甯自睢陽引兵至宿州，李顯忠擊卻之。 壬子，欽宗大祥，帝服衰服詣几筵，易祥服行祥祭禮。 顯忠與金人戰於宿州，邵宏淵不援，顯忠失利。 是夜，建康中軍統制周巨集及邵巨集淵之子世雄、殿前司統制官左士淵逃歸。 癸丑，進李顯忠開府儀同三司、淮南京畿京東河北招討使，邵宏淵檢校少保、甯遠軍節度使、招討副使。 金人攻宿州城，顯忠大敗之。 殿前司統制官張訓通等七人、統領官十二人，以二將不葉而遁。 甲寅，李顯忠、邵宏淵軍大潰于符離。 乙卯，下詔親征。 丙辰，召汪澈。 以張浚兼都督荆、襄軍馬。 李顯忠、邵宏淵至濠州。 張浚以劉寶爲鎮江諸軍都統制。 丁巳，以蒲察徒穆、大周仁、蕭琦並爲節度使，徒穆大同軍、周仁彰國軍、琦威塞軍。 遣御前忠勇軍赴都督府。 是月，成都地震三。
In the fifth month, on renchen day, sumptuary laws on gold-inlaid furnishings and illicit shrines were tightened. On dingyou day Li Xianzhong retook Lingbi. Shao Hongyuan reached Hong county and met Jin resistance. On wuxu day Xianzhong advanced on Hong. On gengzi day Hong fell; the Jin prefects of Sizhou surrendered. On xinchou day the court recorders were posted daily in the front hall. On renyin day Zhang Jun crossed the Yangzi to oversee the armies. On guimao day the Jin commander Xiao Qi surrendered to Li Xianzhong. On jiachen day Xianzhong and Hongyuan beat the Jin at Suzhou. On yisi day Shi Hao left office. Sima Kang's censor rank was posthumously restored. On bingwu day Suzhou was taken and thousands of Jin troops were killed. Wang Gong of Jiankang's vanguard died fighting in the streets. On dingwei day Xin Ciying and Hong Zun were promoted. Military colonies were ordered opened in all circuits. On xinhai day the emperor celebrated at De'shou. Zhining marched from Suiyang to Suzhou; Li Xianzhong drove him back. On renzi day, Qinzong's great mourning, the emperor performed the full rite sequence. At Suzhou Xianzhong fought the Jin without Hongyuan's aid and lost. That night Zhou Ji, Shao Shixiong, and Zuo Shiyuan fled home. On guichou day Xianzhong and Hongyuan received grand promotions. The Jin assaulted Suzhou; Xianzhong repulsed them heavily. Seven commanders and twelve unit leaders fled over discord between the two generals. On jiayin day the Song army was routed at Fuli. On yimao day the emperor proclaimed a personal campaign. On bingchen day Wang Che was summoned. Zhang Jun also took command of Jing and Xiang forces. Xianzhong and Hongyuan withdrew to Haozhou. Zhang Jun appointed Liu Bao commander at Zhenjiang. On dingsi day the surrendered Jin generals were made military commissioners. The Imperial Loyal and Brave Army was sent to headquarters. That month Chengdu suffered three earthquakes.
15
六月庚申朔，日有食之。 遣內侍趣上淮東將士功賞。 癸亥，汪澈罷。 張浚乞致仕，且請通好，皆不許。 丁卯，以觀文殿大學士湯思退爲醴泉觀使兼侍讀。 戊辰，召虞允文。 以兵部侍郎周葵爲參知政事。 汪澈落資政殿學士、台州居住。 庚午，張浚自盱眙還揚州。 辛未，李顯忠罷軍職。 壬申，乙太傅、同安郡王楊存中爲御營使、節制殿前司軍馬。 癸酉，下詔罪己。 張浚降授特進，仍前樞密使、江淮東西路宣撫使，官屬各奪二官。 邵宏淵降武義大夫，職仍舊。 詔楊存中先詣建康措置營砦，檢視沿江守備。 戊寅，詔展巡幸之期。 辛次膺罷。 己卯，李顯忠責授清遠軍節度副使、筠州安置。 辛巳，命浙西副都總管李寶兼御營統制官、措置浙西海道。 甲申，右諫議大夫王大寶入封，論移蹕。 以敷文閣學士虞允文爲兵部尚書兼湖北、京西宣諭使。 戊子，放宮人三十人。 以蕭琦爲檢校少保、河北招撫使。
In the sixth month, on the new moon, there was an eclipse. Envoys were sent to expedite Huaidong battle rewards. On guihai day Wang Che left office. Zhang Jun sought retirement and peace; both were refused. On dingmao day Tang Situ received a court lecture post. On wuchen day Yu Yunwen was summoned. Zhou Kui became participating administrator. Wang Che was demoted to residence at Taizhou. On gengwu day Zhang Jun returned to Yangzhou. On xinwei day Li Xianzhong lost his command. On renshen day Yang Cunzhong took charge of the palace armies. On guiyou day the emperor issued a self-reproach edict. Zhang Jun was demoted but kept his pacification post; his staff were punished. Hongyuan was demoted one rank but kept his post. Yang Cunzhong was sent to fortify Jiankang and the river line. On wuyin day the planned imperial tour was postponed. Xin Ciying left office. On jimao day Li Xianzhong was banished to Yunzhou. On xinsi day Li Bao was put in charge of Zhexi sea defense. On jiashen day Wang Dabao memorialized on moving the capital. Yu Yunwen became war minister and Hubei-Jingxi commissioner. On wuzi day thirty palace women were freed. Xiao Qi was made Hebei pacification commissioner.
16
秋七月庚寅朔，以虞允文爲湖北、京西制置使。 癸巳，以湯思退爲尚書右僕射、同中書門下平章事兼樞密使。 李顯忠再責授果州團練副使、潭州安置。 乙未，詔宿州棄軍將佐奪官、貶竄有差。 丙申，太白晝見，經天。 罷江、淮宣撫司便宜行事。 乙巳，以旱蝗、星變，詔侍從、台諫、兩省官條上時政闕失。 丁未，詔征李顯忠侵欺官錢金銀，免籍其家。 乙卯，裁減省、部、寺、監官吏。 戊午，給還岳飛田宅。
In the seventh month, on gengyin day, Yu Yunwen became Hubei-Jingxi commissioner. On guisi day Tang Situ became chief minister. Li Xianzhong was further demoted to Tanzhou. On yiwei day Suzhou deserters were punished and exiled. On bingshen day Venus was visible crossing the sky at noon. The pacification commission lost discretionary powers. On yisi day drought, locusts, and omens led to calls for policy critique. On dingwei day Xianzhong's embezzlement was investigated without family confiscation. On yimao day central government staff were cut. On wuwu day Yue Fei's property was restored.
17
八月丙寅，張浚復都督江、淮軍馬。 庚午，以劉寶兼淮東招撫使。 丙子，以飛蝗、風水爲災，避殿減膳。 罷借諸路職田之令。 戊寅，金紇石烈志寧又以書求海、泗、唐、鄧四州地及歲幣。 癸未，復以龍大淵知閣門事，曾覿同知閣門事。 丙戌，遣淮西安撫司幹辦公事盧仲賢等齎書至金帥府，戒勿許四州，差減歲幣。 仍命諸將毋遣兵人出境。
In the eighth month, on bingyin day, Zhang Jun resumed Jiang-Huai command. On gengwu day Liu Bao also pacified Huaidong. On bingzi day locusts and floods led the emperor to reduce court and diet. The borrowing of circuit official fields was ended. On wuyin day the Jin again demanded four prefectures and tribute. On guiwei day Long Dayuan and Zeng Di returned to the gate offices. On bingxu day Lu Zhongxian was sent to the Jin with strict negotiating limits. Generals were forbidden to cross the border.
18
九月己酉，楊存中罷。

19

On jisi day the Imperial Guard was sent south. On bingzi day the retired empress's orders were styled sage instructions. Consort Xian was made empress. On dingchou day the earth shook. On xinsi day Hongzhou became Longxing Prefecture. An edict centralized troop dispatch under the command headquarters. In the eleventh month Lu Zhongxian returned from Suzhou with Pusan Zhongyi's letter. On gengzi day Wang Ziwang's embassy was sent to the Jin. On xinchou day the court debated peace terms and envoy lists. On bingwu day Lu Zhongxian was punished for conceding the four prefectures. Zhang Jun was summoned. On guichou day Hu Fang and Yang Youyi reviewed Jin credentials.
20

21

22

On bingzi day suburban gifts to officials were halved. Participating commissioners in several circuits were abolished. On dingchou day hail and snow fell. Li Yun was taken and his followers crushed. 23

24

25

26

27

On guichou day floods led to calls for policy memorials. That month Liang Ke was sent to an outside sinecure. The Guangxi judicial office moved to Rongzhou.
28
九月甲申。 罷內侍李珂賜諡。 甲午，詔江東、浙西監司、守臣講明措置田事。 乙未，交阯入貢。 丁酉，嚴髒吏法。 辛丑，以王之望爲參知政事，權刑部侍郎吳芾爲給事中兼淮西宣諭使。 金人犯邊。 以久雨，出內庫白金四十萬兩，糴米賑貧民。 壬寅，王彥帥師濟江，軍昭關。 癸卯。 命湯思退都督江、淮東西路軍馬，辭不行。 乙巳，復命楊存中爲同都督，錢端禮、吳芾並爲都督府參贊軍事。 罷宣諭司。 仍易國書以付魏杞。 少保、崇信軍節度使趙密落致仕，權領殿前司職事。
In the ninth month, on jiashen day. Li Ke was denied a posthumous title. On jiawu day Jiangdong and Zhe officials were to reform field policy. On yiwei day Jiaozhi sent tribute. On dingyou day anti-corruption laws were tightened. On xinchou day Wang Zhiwang and Wu Bi were promoted. The Jin invaded the border. Prolonged rain led to rice relief from the inner treasury. 29

In the tenth month Wei Qi reached Xuyi; the Jin rejected his letter and demanded more. On dingmao day He Yunzhong retired. On jisi day Zhou Kui and Wang Zhiwang took military posts. On gengwu day ministers were to evening audience. On bingzi day a great gale blew. On gengchen day transport-route taxes were halved. Li Bao was made coastal navy commander. On xinsi day the Jin crossed the Huai; Liu Bao fled Chuzhou.
30
十一月乙酉，知楚州魏勝與金人戰，死之，州遂陷，濠州亦陷。 王彥棄昭關遁，滁州又陷，丙戌，詔諭沿邊將士。 丁亥，詔魏祀等以所齎禮幣犒軍。 杞弗從，命留鎮江侯旨。 復命王之望督視江、淮軍馬。 戊子，以金人侵擾，詔郊杞改用明年。 又詔諭歸正官民軍士。 命王之望同都督江、淮軍馬。 湯思退罷都督。 召陳康伯。 己丑，王之望罷同都督。 庚寅，命楊存中都督江、淮軍馬。 辛卯，湯思退罷，尋以尹穡、晁公武論之，落觀文殿大學士、永州居住，未至而卒。 甲午，以黃榜禁太學生伏闕。 是日，太學生張觀等七十二人上書，請斬湯思退、王之望、尹穡，竄其党洪適、晁公武而用陳康伯、胡銓等，以濟大計。 丙申，遣國信所大通事王抃持周葵書如金帥府，請正皇帝號，爲叔侄之國； 易歲貢爲歲幣，減十萬； 割商、秦地； 歸被俘人，惟叛亡者不與； 誓目大略與紹興同。 以金人犯淮南，詔避殿減膳。 丁酉，詔擇日視師。 戊戌，以少保、觀文殿大學士陳康伯爲尚書左僕射、同中書門下平章事兼樞密使。 庚子，遣兵部侍郎胡銓、右諫議大夫尹穡分詣兩浙措置海道。 贈魏勝甯國軍節度使，諡忠壯。 辛丑，兵部尚書錢端禮賜出身，簽書樞密院事兼提領德壽宮。 壬寅，詔侍從、兩省官日一至都堂議事，有關台諫者亦聽會議。 以顯謨閣學士虞允文同簽書樞密院事。 癸卯，遣王之望勞師江上。 甲辰，金人犯六合縣，步軍司統制崔皋擊卻之。 乙巳，以錢端禮兼權參知政事。 丁未，以顯謨閣直學士沈介爲沿江制置使。 命沿江諸州調保甲分守渡口。 己酉，劉寶落節鉞，爲武泰軍承宣使，王彥落龍神衛四廂都指揮使。
In the eleventh month Wei Sheng died defending Chuzhou; Chuzhou and Haozhou fell. Wang Yan fled; Chuzhou fell; on bingxu day border troops were exhorted. On dinghai day Wei Qi was to use tribute to reward troops. Qi refused and was held at Zhenjiang. Wang Zhiwang was again sent to oversee the Huai armies. On wuzi day the suburban rite was postponed for the Jin invasion. Defectors and troops were again exhorted. Wang Zhiwang became co-commander on the Huai. Tang Situ left the command. Chen Kangbo was summoned to court. On jichou day Wang Zhiwang left the co-command. On gengyin day Yang Cunzhong took Jiang-Huai command. On xinmao day Tang Situ fell; Yin Ji and Chao Gongwu's attacks sent him to Yongzhou, where he died en route. On jiawu day students were forbidden to petition at the gate. That day seventy-three students demanded execution of peace ministers and restoration of war party leaders. On bingshen day Wang Bian carried Zhou Kui's letter seeking uncle-nephew relations; tribute became "payment," cut by one hundred thousand; Shang and Qin were to be ceded; captives would return, but not deserters; the oath largely followed Shaoxing terms. Jin invasion of Huainan led to reduced court and diet. On dingyou day a date was set for inspecting troops. On wuxu day Chen Kangbo became chief minister. On gengzi day Hu Quan and Yin Ji were sent to manage Zhe sea routes. Wei Sheng was posthumously honored as Zhongzhuang, commissioner of Ningguo. On xinchou day Qian Duanli entered the military commission and oversaw De'shou. On renyin day ministers were to meet daily at the Secretariat. Yu Yunwen became concurrent military signatory. On guimao day Wang Zhiwang was sent to reward river armies. On jiachen day Cui Gao repulsed the Jin at Liuhe. On yisi day Qian Duanli became acting participating administrator. On dingwei day Shen Jie became riverine commissioner. River prefectures were to rotate militia at crossings. On jiyou day Liu Bao and Wang Yan lost their commands.
31
閏月甲寅，陳康伯入見，詔康伯間日一朝，肩輿至殿門，給扶升殿。 丙辰，周葵罷。 王抃見金二帥，皆得其報書以歸。 戊午，蕭琦卒。 壬戌。 詔罷胡銓、尹穡。 丙寅，召韓仲通。 以沈介爲兵部尚書、湖北京西制置使。 戊辰，以金人且退，詔督府擇利擊之，王之望執不可。 乙亥。 之望罷。 丙子，以王抃爲奉使金國通問國信所參議官，持陳康伯報書以行。 丁丑。 金遣張恭愈來迓使者。 詔台諫、侍從、兩省官舉楚、廬、滁、濠四州守臣。 十二月甲申，罷陝西路轉運司。 戊子，魏杞始渡淮。 詔郊祀大禮遵至道典故，改用來年正月一日上辛。 辛卯，以錢端禮爲參知政事兼知樞密院事，虞允文同知樞密院事兼權參知政事，禮部尚書王剛中簽書樞密院事。 丙申，制曰：「比遣王抃，遠抵潁濱，得其要約。 尋澶淵盟誓之信，仿大遼書題之儀，正皇帝之稱，爲叔侄之國，歲幣減十萬之數，地界如紹興之時。 憐彼此之無辜，約叛亡之不遣，可使歸正之士咸起甯居之心。 重念數州之民，罹此一時之難，老稚有蕩析之災，丁壯有系累之苦，宜推蕩滌之宥，少慰凋殘之情。 應沿邊被兵州軍，除逃遁官吏不赦外，雜犯死罪情輕者減一等，餘並放遣。 」遣洪適等賀金主生辰。 詔吳挺市馬赴行在。 己亥，雨雹。 壬寅，罷三衙、江上、荆襄諸軍招軍。 甲辰，遣沿海水軍還屯。 己酉，朝獻景靈宮。 庚戌，朝饗太廟。
In the intercalary month Chen Kangbo was granted every-other-day audience with carriage support. On bingchen day Zhou Kui left office. Wang Bian met both Jin commanders and brought back their replies. On wuwu day Xiao Qi died. On renxu day. Hu Quan and Yin Ji were dismissed. On bingyin day Han Zhongtong was summoned. Shen Jie became war minister and Huguang-Jingxi commissioner. On wuchen day the command was ordered to strike the retreating Jin; Wang Zhiwang refused. On yihai day. Wang Zhiwang left office. On bingzi day Wang Bian was sent with Chen Kangbo's reply. On dingchou day. The Jin sent Zhang Gongyu to meet the envoy. Officials were to recommend prefects for four Huai prefectures. In the twelfth month the Shaanxi transport office was abolished. On wuzi day Wei Qi first crossed the Huai. The suburban rite was rescheduled to the coming year's first month. On xinmao day Qian, Yu, and Wang took top posts. On bingshen day an edict announced Wang Bian's treaty terms: Chanyuan-style faith, Liao-style titles, uncle-nephew relations, reduced payment, Shaoxing borders. Exempting deserters from return was meant to reassure loyalists. Border peoples who had suffered war were to receive clemency. Border amnesty excepted fleeing officials and lightened other sentences. Hong Shi's embassy congratulated the Jin ruler's birthday. Wu Ting was to buy horses for the court. On jihai day hail fell. On renyin day army recruitment was halted. On jiachen day the coastal navy returned to base. On jiyou day offerings were at Jingling Palace. On gengxu day the Grand Temple received offerings.
32

33

On renshen day Sichuan fictitious levies were forgiven. Wu Lin was made acting prefect of Xingyuan. On yihai day unexamined appointees were barred from hall appointment. On bingzi day Li Ruochuan's embassy congratulated the Jin honorific. Route controllers and commanders were increased. Li Jin rebelled again at Chenzhou and troops were sent against him.
34

In the sixth month, on guiwei day, Wang Gangzhong died. On yiyou day Wang Huai of Prince Gong's household was dismissed for violating ritual propriety. On bingxu day Hong Shi became military signatory. On wuzi day Cui Gao was punished for false merit claims and ordered to redeem himself in the ranks. On xinmao day Ling De was made Prince of Anding. On renchen day Yao Yue was dismissed for a false locust report. On bingwu day Huai prefects were admonished for failing to settle the people. On renyin day Guangdong war-torn districts were tax-exempt. On jiachen day the Hubei-Jingxi commission was abolished.
35

On jiwei day two-cash coins were minted. On jisi day border and Hunan taxes were remitted.
36

In the eighth month the Yongfeng polder went to Jiankang command. On guiwei day Li Jin was captured. On yiyou day Prince Qi was made crown prince. On dinghai day Yu Yunwen left office. On wuzi day there was a general amnesty. On jichou day Hong Shi and Ye Yong were promoted to top posts. On gengyin day farewell-audience rules for military officials were set. On guisi day Qian Duanli left office over Eastern Palace kinship. On wuxu day Zhang Fu was exiled to Tingzhou for attacking Yu Yunwen.
37
九月乙卯，立廣國夫人錢氏爲皇太子妃。 丁巳，申嚴百司官出入局之制。 丁卯，升鼎州爲常德府。 甲戌，以端明殿學士汪澈知樞密院事，洪適兼同知樞密院事。 乙亥，置沿淮諸州都巡檢。
In the ninth month Lady Qian became the crown prince's consort. On dingsi day bureau entry rules were tightened. On dingmao day Dingzhou became Changde Prefecture. On jiaxu day Wang Che and Hong Shi took military posts. On yihai day Huai regional inspectors were set.
38
冬十月己卯，遣方滋等使金賀正旦。 戊子，增頭子錢。 歸正人右通直郎劉蘊古坐以軍器法式送北境，伏誅。 壬辰，御大慶殿，冊皇太子。 癸巳，詣德壽宮稱謝。 乙未，詔侍從各舉所知宗室一二人。 丁酉，金遣高衎等來賀會慶節。 乙巳，淮北紅巾賊逾淮劫掠，立賞討捕之，已而知楚州胡明遣巡尉擊殺其首蕭榮。
In the tenth month Fang Zi's embassy congratulated the Jin new year. On wuzi day head-tax surcharges rose. Liu Yungu was executed for leaking arms regulations to the Jin. On renchen day the crown prince was enthroned at the Hall of Great Celebration. On guisi day the emperor thanked the retired emperor at De'shou. On yiwei day attendants were to recommend imperial clansmen. On dingyou day the Jin congratulated the festival. On yisi day Huai bandits were pursued; Xiao Rong was killed by Chuzhou patrols.
39
十一月辛亥，招收兩淮流散忠義人。 丙寅，白氣亙天。 辛未，遣龍大淵撫諭兩淮，措置屯田，督捕盜賊。
In the eleventh month scattered Huai loyalists were recruited. On bingyin day white vapor filled the sky. On xinwei day Long Dayuan was sent to the two Huai.
40
十二月戊寅，以洪适爲尚書右僕射、同中書門下平章事兼樞密使，汪澈爲樞密使。 命廣東提刑司招安李金餘黨。 癸未，遣王曮等賀金主生辰。 康寅，以葉顒爲參知政事兼同知樞密院事。 辛卯，詔侍從、臺諫、兩省舉堪監司、郡守者各一人，三衙、知閣舉材武可守邊者一人。 庚子，罷兩淮諸州權攝官。 壬寅，金遣烏古論忠弼等來賀明年正旦。 癸卯。 詔樞密院文書依三省式，經中書門下畫黃書讀。
In the twelfth month Hong Shi and Wang Che became chief ministers. Guangdong was to amnesty Li Jin's remnants. On guiwei day Wang Yan's embassy congratulated the Jin birthday. On gengyin day Ye Yong was promoted. On xinmao day officials were to recommend intendants, prefects, and border commanders. On gengzi day acting Huai prefects were ended. On renyin day the Jin sent new-year greetings. On guimao day. Military documents were brought under Secretariat procedure.
41
二年春正月辛酉，省六合戍兵，以所墾田給還復業之民。 辛未，命湖南監司存恤寇盜殘破郡縣。
In spring of year 2 Liuhe garrisons were cut and fields returned to civilians. On xinwei day Hunan officials were to aid bandit-ravaged counties.
42
二月丁丑，罷盱眙屯田，振兩浙、江東饑。 戊寅，幸玉津園宴射，遂幸龍井。
In the second month Xuyi colonies ended and Zhe-Jiangdong famine relief began. On wuyin day the emperor feasted at Jade Ford and visited Longjing.
43
三月乙巳，禁京西、利州路科役保勝義士。 壬子，詔戒飭刑獄官。 戊午，殿中侍御史王伯庠請裁定奏薦，詔三省、臺諫集議，具條式以聞。 詔：「縣令非兩任，毋除監察御史； 非任守臣，毋除郎官。 著爲令。 」丁卯，賜禮部進士蕭國梁以下四百九十有三人及第、出身。 戊辰，再增諸州軍離軍添差員闕。 辛未，罷洪适右僕射。 癸酉，以給事中、權吏部尚書魏𣏌同知樞密院事兼權參知政事。 丁丑，罷和糴。
In the third month corvée on Baosheng troops was forbidden. On renzi day prison officials were admonished. On wuwu day memorial-recommendation rules were ordered drafted. An edict barred unseasoned magistrates from the censorate; none without prefectural service could enter the central bureaus. The rule was codified. On dingmao day four hundred ninety-three new Jinshi graduated. On wuchen day detached army posts increased again. On xinwei day Hong Shi left the right vice directorship. On guiyou day Wei Qi became acting military vice commissioner. On dingchou day harmonized grain purchase ended.
44
夏四月戊寅，以久雨，命侍從、臺諫議刑政所宜以聞。 減大理、三衙、臨安府及浙西州縣雜犯死罪以下囚一等，釋杖以下。 庚辰，詔兩浙漕臣王炎開平江、湖、秀圍田。 辛巳，避殿減膳。 甲申，太白晝見。 癸巳，御殿復膳。 乙未，汪澈罷。 丁酉，以知荆南府李道憑恃戚里妄作，罷之。
In the fourth month prolonged rain led to criminal-policy review. Prisoners across the capital and western Zhe received sentence reductions. On gengchen day Wang Yan was to open polders in the lake districts. On xinsi day the emperor reduced court and diet. On jiashen day Venus appeared by day. On guisi day normal court diet resumed. On yiwei day Wang Che left office. On dingyou day Li Dao was dismissed for abusing kinship ties.
45
五月戊申，張燾薨。 己酉，罷權借職田。 庚戌，葉顒罷。 以魏𣏌爲參知政事，右諫議大夫林安宅同知樞密院事兼權參知政事，中書舍人蔣芾簽書樞密院事。 癸丑，太白晝見，經天。 禁浙西修築圍田。 罷修建康行宮。 丁卯，命監司、守臣預備水旱。
In the fifth month Zhang Tao died. On jiyou day borrowing of official fields ended. On gengxu day Ye Yong left office. Wei Qi, Lin Anzhai, and Jiang Fu were promoted. On guichou day Venus crossed the sky at noon. Western Zhe polder construction was banned. The Jiankang traveling palace project was canceled. On dingmao day officials were to prepare for flood and drought.
46
六月甲戌，罷兩浙路提舉市舶司。 詔諸路監司、帥臣各察守令臧否以聞。 丙子，刑部上《乾道新編特旨斷例》。 戊寅，詔制科權罷注疏出題，守臣、監司亦許解送。 庚辰，封孫挺爲福州觀察使、榮國公，攄爲左千牛衛大將軍。 癸未，詔使相毋奏補文資，七色補官人毋任子，堂吏遷朝議大夫以五員爲額。 乙酉，申嚴內外牒式法，裁其額。 丙戌，廢永豐圩。 戊戌，詔改官人實曆知縣一任，方許關升。 著爲定式。 秋七月己酉，調泉州左翼軍二千人屯許浦鎮。 甲寅，以鎮江都統制戚方爲武當軍節度使。
In the sixth month the Zhe maritime trade office was abolished. All routes were to report on prefect conduct. On bingzi day the Qiandao criminal precedents were submitted. On wuyin day special-examination rules were revised. On gengchen day Sun Ting and Shu received enfeoffments. On guiwei day appointment abuses by ministers and clerks were restricted. On yiyou day memorial-form quotas were cut. On bingxu day the Yongfeng polder was abandoned. On wuxu day county service was required before promotion. The rule was made permanent. In the seventh month Quanzhou troops garrisoned Xupu. On jiayin day Qi Fang became Wudang commissioner.
47
八月辛未朔，詔兩淮行鐵錢，銅錢毋過江北。 癸酉，以武鋒軍隸步軍司。 甲戌。 罷任子年三十得免試參選之令。 丁丑，蠲淮南放歸萬弩手差役二年。 壬午，詔諸州守臣兼訓練禁軍。 癸未，降會子、交子於鎮江、建康務場，令江、淮之人對換。 丙戌，林安宅劾葉顒之子受金失實，罷之。 丁亥，詔安宅筠州居住，溫州大水。 戊子，以魏杞兼同知樞密院事，蔣芾權參知政事。 召葉顒。 庚寅，少保、新興郡王吳蓋薨。 甲午，立中興以來十三處戰功格目。 乙未，詔吳璘復判興州。 丙申，升宣州爲甯國府。 罷戶部諸路歲糴一年。
In the eighth month the Huai iron-coin zone was set and copper barred north. On guiyou day the Martial Vanguard joined the infantry. On jiaxu day. The age-thirty son exemption from examination was ended. On dingchou day Huainan crossbowmen had two years' corvée forgiven. On renwu day prefects were to train local garrison troops. On guiwei day notes were issued at Zhenjiang and Jiankang for Huai exchange. On bingxu day Lin Anzhai ousted Ye Yong over a false bribery charge. On dinghai day Lin Anzhai was sent to Yunzhou as Wenzhou flooded. On wuzi day Wei Qi and Jiang Fu were promoted. Ye Yong was summoned. On gengyin day Prince of Xinxing Wu Gai died. On jiawu day thirteen Restoration battle-merit grades were set. On yiwei day Wu Lin returned to Xingzhou. On bingshen day Xuanzhou became Ningguo Prefecture. Annual grain purchase was suspended for one year.
48
九月甲辰，知上元縣李允升犯髒貸死，杖脊刺面，配惠州牢城，籍其貲。 丙午，建康守臣王佐坐縱允升去官，奪三官勒停、建昌軍居住。 余失按官吏及薦舉官奪官有差。 辛亥，遣官按視溫州水災，振貧民，決系囚。 乙卯，詔改造大曆。 辛酉，追封子恪爲邵王，諡曰悼肅。 甲子，詔監司各舉部內知縣、縣令二三人，守臣各舉屬縣一二人。 己巳，魏𣏌等上神宗、哲宗、徽宗三朝《帝紀》、《太上皇聖政》。 太白晝見。 是月，詔舉將帥，置章奏簿。
In the ninth month Li Yunsheng of Shangyuan was executed by exile for corruption. On bingwu day Wang Zuo was punished for letting the corrupt magistrate escape. Other negligent and recommending officials were punished in varying degrees. On xinhai day Wenzhou flood victims were aided and prisoners reviewed. On yimao day calendar reform was ordered. On xinyou day the late Prince Ke was posthumously honored. On jiazi day intendants and ministers were to recommend local officials. On jisi day Wei Qi presented the three dynastic annals and retired emperor's governance. Venus appeared by day. That month generals were to be recommended and a register kept.
49
冬十月癸酉，上《太上皇聖政》于德壽宮。 乙亥，遣薛良朋等使金賀正旦。 己卯，減饒州歲貢金三之一，蠲諸路灑坊逋賦。 戊子，知峽州呂令問坐縱髒吏知夷陵縣韓贄胄去官，奪二官、鄂州居住。 辛卯，雨雹。 金遣魏子平等來賀會慶節。 十一月丙午，楊存中薨。 己酉，盡出內藏及南庫銀以易會子，官司並以錢銀支遣，民間從便。 兩淮總領所許自造會子。 鬻諸路營田。 壬子，詔修祥曦殿記注。 乙卯，密詔四川制置使汪應辰：如吳璘不起，收其宣撫使牌印，權行主管職事。 甲子，大閱。 戊辰，築郢州城。 是月，詔汰冗兵。 十二月庚午朔，白氣亙天。 癸酉，詔三省、侍從、台諫、兩淮漕臣、郡守，條具兩淮鐵錢、交子利害以聞。 乙亥，遣梁克家等賀金主生辰。 己卯，以資政殿學士葉顒知樞密院事。 辛巳，詔免進呈《欽宗日曆》，送國史院修纂實錄。 壬午，追封楊存中爲和王。 甲申，以葉顒爲尚書左僕射，魏杞右僕射，並同中書門下平章事兼樞密使。 蔣芾參知政事，吏部尚書陳俊卿同知樞密院事兼權參知政事。 庚寅，詔宰相領兼制國用使，參知政事同知國用事。 癸巳，詔監司、守臣舉廉吏。 丙申，金遣烏古論元忠等來賀明年正旦。 以江東兵馬鈐轄王抃爲帶禦器械。 是歲，裁定內外軍額。
In the tenth month the retired emperor's Sagely Governance was presented at De'shou. On yihai day Xue Liangpeng's embassy congratulated the Jin new year. On jimao day Raozhou gold tribute was cut and wine-shop arrears forgiven. On wuzi day Lü Lingwen of Xiazhou was demoted to Ezhou for shielding a corrupt magistrate. On xinmao day hail fell. The Jin sent Wei Ziping to congratulate the festival. In the eleventh month Yang Cunzhong died. On jiyou day inner silver was used to back notes and offices paid in coin. The two Huai treasuries could issue notes locally. Route military colonies were sold. On renzi day the Xiangxi Hall record was ordered compiled. On yimao day Wang Yingchen was secretly ordered to take Wu Lin's seals if he refused office. On jiazi day a grand troop review was held. On wuchen day Yingzhou's walls were built. That month redundant troops were cut. In the twelfth month white vapor filled the sky on the new moon. On guiyou day officials were to report on Huai currency policy. On yihai day Liang Kejia's embassy congratulated the Jin birthday. On jimao day Ye Yong became military commissioner. On xinsi day Qinzong's calendar went to the Historiography Institute for the Veritable Record. On renwu day Yang Cunzhong was posthumously made Prince of He. On jiashen day Ye Yong and Wei Qi became chief ministers. Jiang Fu and Chen Junqing received top posts. On gengyin day ministers took charge of state revenue. On guisi day incorrupt officials were to be recommended. On bingshen day the Jin sent new-year greetings. Wang Bian received charge of imperial arms. That year inner and outer army quotas were fixed.
