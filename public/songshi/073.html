推天正十一月經朔加時入交：置天正十一月朔積分，以交終分秒去之，不盡，滿樞法為日，不滿為餘秒，即天正經朔加時入交泛日及餘秒。
To find the civil eleventh-month mean new-moon hour entry into convergence: set the civil eleventh-month new-moon accumulated parts, discard full convergence termination parts and seconds, divide the remainder by the pivot factor for days and remainder seconds, and obtain the civil mean new-moon hour general convergence day and remainder seconds.
19
求次朔及望入交：因天正經朔加時入交泛日及餘秒，求次朔，以朔差日及餘秒加之； 求望，以望策及餘秒加之：滿交終日及餘秒皆去之，即次朔及望加時所入。 若以經朔、望小餘減之，即各得朔、望夜半入交泛日及餘秒。
To find the next new moon and full moon entry into convergence: starting from the civil mean new-moon hour general convergence day and remainder seconds, add the new-moon difference day and remainder seconds to obtain the next new moon; to find the full moon, add the full-moon increment and remainder seconds; when the total fills a convergence termination day and remainder seconds, discard the full period — that yields the hour entries for the next new moon and full moon. Subtract the mean new- or full-moon minor remainders to obtain the midnight general convergence day and remainder seconds for each new moon and full moon.
20
求定朔夜半入交：因經朔、望夜半入交，若定朔、望大餘有進退者，亦進退交日，不則因經為定，各得所求。
To find the fixed new-moon midnight entry into convergence: follow the mean new- or full-moon midnight entry; if the fixed new- or full-moon major remainder advances or retreats, advance or retreat the convergence day accordingly; otherwise treat the mean value as fixed — each procedure yields the desired result.
21
求次定朔夜半入交：各因前定朔夜半入交，大月加日二，小月加日一，餘皆加八千三百四十二、秒五千七百二十三； 若求次日，累加一日：滿交終日及餘秒皆去之，即得次定朔及每日夜半入交泛日及餘秒。
To find the next fixed new-moon midnight entry into convergence: take the previous fixed new-moon midnight entry; for a long month add 2 days, for a short month add 1 day, and to every remainder add 8,342 parts and 5,723 seconds; to find the following day, add one day repeatedly; when the total fills a convergence termination day and remainder seconds, discard the full period — thereby obtaining the next fixed new moon and the general convergence day and remainder seconds for each day's midnight.
22
求朔望加時入交常日：置經朔、望入交泛日及餘秒，以其朔、望入氣朏朒定數，朏減朒加之，即朔、望入交常日及餘秒。
To find the new- or full-moon hour regular convergence day: set the mean new- or full-moon general convergence day and remainder seconds, apply that syzygy's qi-entry waxing-waning correction (subtract when waning, add when waxing), and obtain the regular convergence day and remainder seconds.
23
求朔望加時入交定日：置其朔、望入轉朏朒定數，以交率乘之，如交數而一，所得，以朏減朒加入交常日余，滿若不足，進退其日，即朔、望加時入交定日及餘秒。
To find the new- or full-moon hour fixed convergence day: take that syzygy's rotation waxing-waning fixed number, multiply by the convergence ratio and divide by the convergence number, apply the result to the regular convergence remainder (subtract when waning, add when waxing), and advance or retreat the day if the total overflows or falls short — thereby obtaining the fixed convergence day and remainder seconds.
24
求月行入陰陽曆：視其朔、望入交定日及餘秒，在中日及餘秒以下者為月在陽曆； 如中日及餘秒已上者，減去之，為月在陰曆。 凡入交定日，陽初陰末為交初，陰初陽末為交中。
To find the moon's entry into the yin and yang calendars: inspect the syzygy's fixed convergence day and remainder seconds; if it is at or below the middle day and remainder seconds, the moon is in the yang calendar; if it is at or above the middle day and remainder seconds, subtract the middle day and remainder — the moon is in the yin calendar. For any fixed convergence day, yang at the beginning and yin at the end marks convergence beginning; yin at the beginning and yang at the end marks convergence middle.
25
求朔望加時月入陰陽曆積度：置其月入陰陽曆日及余，其餘，先以一百乘之，樞法除為約分。
To find the syzygy hour accumulated degrees in the yin-yang calendars: set the moon's day and remainder in the yin or yang calendar; multiply the remainder by 100 and divide by the pivot factor to obtain contracted parts.
26
以九百九乘之，六十八除為度，不盡，退除為分，即朔、望加時月入陰陽曆積度及分。 其月在陽曆，即為入陽曆積度； 月在陰曆，即為入陰曆積度。
Multiply by 999, divide by 68 for degrees, and treat the remainder as parts — thereby obtaining the syzygy hour accumulated degrees and parts in the yin-yang calendars. If the moon is in the yang calendar, this is the accumulated degrees in the yang calendar; if the moon is in the yin calendar, this is the accumulated degrees in the yin calendar.
27
求朔望加時月去黃道度：置入陰陽曆積度及分，如交象以下為在少象； 已上，覆減半交，餘為入老象。 置所入老少象度及分，以五因之，用減一千一十，餘，以老少象度及分乘之，八十四而一，列於上位； 又置所入老少象度及分，如半象以下為在初限； 已上，減去半象，餘為入末限。 置初、末限底及分於上，列半象度及分於下，以上減下，餘以乘上，四十而一，所得，初限以減，末限以加，上位滿百為度，不滿為分，即朔、望加時月去黃道度數及分。
To find the syzygy hour lunar latitude from the ecliptic: set the yin-yang accumulated degrees and parts; if at or below the convergence image, the moon is in the lesser image; if above, subtract from half convergence — the remainder marks entry into the greater image. Set the entered lesser or greater image degrees and parts, multiply by 5, subtract from 1,010, multiply the remainder by those degrees and parts, divide by 84, and place the result in the upper position; again set the entered lesser or greater image degrees and parts; if at or below half the image, the moon is in the initial limit; if above, subtract half the image — the remainder marks entry into the final limit. Place the initial and final limit bases and parts above and half-image degrees and parts below, subtract the lower from the upper, multiply the remainder by the upper value and divide by 40; subtract for the initial limit and add for the final limit; when the upper total reaches 100 count it as degrees and otherwise as parts — thereby obtaining the syzygy hour lunar latitude in degrees and parts.
28
求食定餘：置定朔小餘，如半法以下覆加半法，餘為午前分； 已上，減去半法，餘為午後分。 置午前、後分於上，列半法於下，以上減下，以下乘上，午前以三萬一千七百七十除，午後以一萬三千八百八十五除之，各為時差。 午前以減、午後以加定朔小餘，各為食定小餘。 以時差加午前、後分，為午前、後定分。 其月食，直以定望小餘便為食定小餘。
To find the eclipse fixed remainder: set the fixed new-moon minor remainder; if at or below half the divisor, add half the divisor in overlay fashion — the result is the before-noon fraction; if above, subtract half the divisor — the remainder is the after-noon fraction. Place before- and after-noon fractions above and half the divisor below, subtract the lower from the upper and multiply, divide before-noon results by 31,770 and after-noon results by 13,885 — each quotient is the time difference. Subtract before noon and add after noon to the fixed new-moon minor remainder — each yields the eclipse fixed minor remainder. Add the time difference to the before- and after-noon fractions to obtain the fixed before- and after-noon parts. For lunar eclipses, the fixed full-moon minor remainder itself serves as the eclipse fixed minor remainder.
29
求日月食甚辰刻：置食定小餘，以辰法除之為辰數，不滿，進一位，刻法除之為刻，不滿為刻分。 其辰數命子正，算外，即食甚辰、刻及分。
To find the greatest eclipse moment in double-hours and quarters: set the eclipse fixed minor remainder, divide by the double-hour divisor for the double-hour count, advance one place for the remainder, divide by the quarter divisor for quarters, and treat any remainder as quarter parts. Count the double-hours from midnight (zi zheng), with the count exclusive, to obtain the greatest eclipse double-hour, quarter, and parts.
30
求氣差：置其朔中積，滿二至限去之，餘在一象以下為在初； 已上，覆減二至限，餘為在末。 皆自相乘，進二位，滿二百三十六除之，用減三千五百三十三，為氣差。 以乘距午定分，半晝分而一，所得以減氣差，為定數。 春分後，交初以減，交中以加； 秋分後，交初以加，交中以減。
To find the qi difference: set the new-moon mid-qi accumulated value, discard full solstice limits, and if the remainder is at or below one image the eclipse is in the initial phase; if above, subtract the solstice limit in overlay fashion — the remainder is in the final phase. Square each value, advance two decimal places, divide by 236, subtract from 3,533 — the result is the qi difference. Multiply by the fixed noon-distance parts, halve the day-half parts and divide, subtract the result from the qi difference — this is the fixed correction. After the spring equinox, subtract at convergence beginning and add at convergence middle; after the autumn equinox, add at convergence beginning and subtract at convergence middle.
31
求刻差：置其朔中積，滿二至限去之，餘，列二至限於下，以上減下，餘以乘上，進二位，滿二百三十六除之，為刻差以乘距午定分，四因之，樞法而一，為定數。 冬至後食甚在午前，夏至後食甚在午後。 交初以加，交中以減。 冬至後食甚在午後，夏至後食甚在午前。 交初以加，交中以減。
To find the quarter difference: set the new-moon mid-qi accumulated value, discard full solstice limits, place the solstice limit below the remainder, subtract and multiply as above, advance two places and divide by 236 for the quarter difference, multiply by the fixed noon-distance parts, multiply by 4, and divide by the pivot factor — yielding the fixed correction. After the winter solstice the greatest eclipse falls before noon; after the summer solstice it falls after noon. Add at convergence beginning; subtract at convergence middle. After the winter solstice the greatest eclipse falls after noon; after the summer solstice it falls before noon. Add at convergence beginning; subtract at convergence middle.
32
求日入食限：置入交定日及餘秒，以氣、刻、時三差定數各加減之，如中日及餘秒以下為不食； 已上者，減去中日及餘秒，如後限以下、前限已上為入食限； 後限以下為交後分； 前限以上覆減中日，餘為交前分。
To find solar entry into the eclipse limit: set the fixed convergence day and remainder seconds, apply the qi, quarter, and time fixed corrections, and if the result is at or below the middle day and remainder seconds there is no eclipse; if above, subtract the middle day and remainder seconds; if the result is at or below the posterior limit and at or above the anterior limit, the sun has entered the eclipse limit; at or below the posterior limit counts as post-convergence parts; at or above the anterior limit, subtract the middle day in overlay fashion — the remainder is pre-convergence parts.
33
求日食分：置入交前後分，如陽曆食限以下者為陽曆食定分； 已上者，覆減一萬一千二百，餘為陰曆食定分； 不足減者，不食。
To find the solar eclipse magnitude: set the pre- and post-convergence parts; if at or below the yang-calendar eclipse limit, that value is the yang-calendar fixed eclipse parts; if above, subtract 11,200 in overlay fashion — the remainder is the yin-calendar fixed eclipse parts; if the subtraction cannot be completed, there is no eclipse.
34
各如限陽曆定法而一，為食之大分，不盡，退除為小分，半已上為半強，半以下為半弱。 命大分以十為限，得日食之分。
Divide each by the appropriate yang- or yin-calendar fixed divisor to obtain major eclipse parts; treat the remainder as minor parts, calling half and above half-strong and half and below half-weak. Take ten as the limit for the major parts to obtain the solar eclipse magnitude.
35
求日食泛用分：置朔入陰陽曆食定分，一百約之，在陽曆者列入十四於下，在陰曆者列一百四十於下，各以上減下，餘以乘上，進二位，陽曆以一百八十五除，陰曆以五百一十四除，各為日食泛用分。
To find the solar eclipse general-use parts: set the new-moon fixed eclipse parts in the yin-yang calendars and contract by 100; for the yang calendar place 14 below, for the yin calendar place 140 below; subtract, multiply, advance two places, divide by 185 for yang and 514 for yin — each quotient is the solar eclipse general-use parts.
36
求月入食限：視月入陰陽曆日及餘，如後限以下為交後分； 前限已上覆減中日，為交前分。
To find lunar entry into the eclipse limit: inspect the moon's day and remainder in the yin-yang calendars; if at or below the posterior limit, that is post-convergence parts; at or above the anterior limit, subtract the middle day in overlay fashion — that is pre-convergence parts.
37
求月食分：置交前後分，如三千二百以下者，食既； 已上，用減一萬二百，不足減者不食； 餘以七百除之為大分，不盡，退除為小分，小分半已上為半強，半已下為半弱。 命大分以十為限，得月食之分。
To find the lunar eclipse magnitude: set the pre- and post-convergence parts; if at or below 3,200, the eclipse is total; if above, subtract 10,200; if the subtraction cannot be completed, there is no eclipse; divide the remainder by 700 for major parts and treat the remainder as minor parts, calling half and above half-strong and half and below half-weak. Take ten as the limit for the major parts to obtain the lunar eclipse magnitude.
38
求月食泛用分：置望入交前後分，退一等，自相乘，交初以九百三十五除，交中以一千一百五十六除之，得數用減刻率，交初以一千一百一十一為刻率，交中以九百為刻率。
To find the lunar eclipse general-use parts: set the full-moon pre- and post-convergence parts, reduce one grade and square them; at convergence beginning divide by 935 and at convergence middle by 1,156; subtract the result from the quarter rate — 1,111 at convergence beginning and 900 at convergence middle.

40
求日月食定用分：置日月食泛用分，以一千三百三十七乘之，以所食日轉定分除之，即得所求。
To find the fixed-use parts for a solar or lunar eclipse: set the general-use parts, multiply by 1,337, divide by that day's rotation fixed parts — thereby obtaining the desired value.
41
求日月食虧初復滿小餘：各以定用分減食甚小餘，為虧初； 加食甚小餘，為復滿：即各得虧初復滿小餘。 若求時刻者，依食甚術入之。
To find the minor remainders for first contact and full restoration: subtract the fixed-use parts from the greatest-eclipse minor remainder for first contact; add the fixed-use parts to the greatest-eclipse minor remainder for full restoration — thereby obtaining both minor remainders. To convert to clock time, apply the greatest-eclipse procedure.
42
求月食更籌定法：置其望晨分，四因之，退一等，為更法； 倍之，退一等，為籌法。
To find the lunar eclipse watch and tally fixed divisors: set the full-moon dawn parts, multiply by 4 and reduce one grade — that is the watch divisor; double it and reduce one grade — that is the tally divisor.
43
求月食入更籌：置虧初、食甚、復滿小餘，在晨分以下加晨分，昏分已上減去昏分，餘以更法除之為更數，不滿，以籌法除之為籌數。 其更數命初更，算外，即各得所入更、籌。
To find the lunar eclipse watch and tally entry: set the first-contact, greatest, and full-restoration minor remainders; add dawn parts if below dawn and subtract dusk parts if above dusk; divide the remainder by the watch divisor for watches and by the tally divisor for tallies. Count the watches from the first watch (exclusive) to obtain the watch and tally for each phase.
44
求朔、望食甚宿次：置其經朔、望入氣小餘，以入氣、入轉朏朒定數朏減朒加之，乘其日升降分，樞法而一，加減其日盈縮分，至後、分前以加，分後、至前以減。
To find the lodge of greatest eclipse at new or full moon: set the mean syzygy qi-entry minor remainder, apply qi-entry and rotation waxing-waning corrections, multiply the day's ascent-descent parts and divide by the pivot factor, then add or subtract the day's excess-deficit parts — adding after solstice and before equinox, subtracting after equinox and before solstice.
45
一百約之為分，分滿百為度，以盈加縮減其定朔、望加時中積，以天正冬至加時黃道日度及分加而命之，即定朔、望加時日躔宿次。 其望加半周天，命如前，即朔、望食甚宿次。
Contract by 100 for parts and promote every 100 parts to a degree; add excess and subtract deficit from the fixed syzygy hour mid-accumulation, then add the civil winter-solstice hour ecliptic solar longitude and count the lodges — thereby obtaining the fixed syzygy hour solar position. For full moon add half a celestial circuit and count as before — thereby obtaining the lodge of greatest eclipse.
46
求月食既內外刻分：置月食交前、後分，覆減三千二百，不及減者，為食下既。
To find the inner and outer quarter parts of lunar totality: set the pre- and post-convergence parts and subtract 3,200 in overlay fashion; if the subtraction cannot be completed, the eclipse is total throughout.
47
一百約之，列六十四於下，以上減下，餘以乘上，進二位，交初以二百九十三除，交中以三百六十五除，所得，以定用分乘之，如泛用分而一，為月食既內刻分； 覆減定用分，即既外刻分。
Contract by 100, place 64 below, subtract and multiply as above, advance two places and divide by 293 at convergence beginning or 365 at convergence middle; multiply by the fixed-use parts and divide by the general-use parts — yielding the inner quarter parts of lunar totality; subtract the fixed-use parts in overlay fashion — that is the outer quarter parts of totality.
48
求日月帶食出入分數：各以食定小餘與日出、入分相減，餘為帶食差； 其帶食差滿定用分已上者，不帶食出入也。
To find the grazing-eclipse rising and setting fractions: subtract the eclipse fixed minor remainder from the sunrise or sunset parts — the remainder is the grazing-eclipse difference; if the grazing-eclipse difference equals or exceeds the fixed-use parts, there is no grazing eclipse at rising or setting.
49
以帶食差乘所食分，滿定用分而一，若月食既者，以既內刻分減帶食差，餘所食分，以既外刻分而一，不及減者，為帶食既出入也。
Multiply the grazing-eclipse difference by the eclipse magnitude and divide by the fixed-use parts; for a total lunar eclipse subtract the inner totality quarter parts from the difference and divide the remainder by the outer totality quarter parts — if the subtraction cannot be completed, the grazing eclipse is total at rising or setting.
50
各以減所食分，即帶出、入所見之分。 其朔日食甚在晝者，晨為漸進之分，昏為已退之分； 若食甚在夜者，晨為已退之分，昏為漸進之分。 其月食者，見此可知也。
Subtract each result from the eclipse magnitude to obtain the visible grazing fraction at rising or setting. When the new-moon solar eclipse greatest moment falls in daylight, dawn marks the gradually advancing fraction and dusk the already receding fraction; if the greatest moment falls at night, dawn marks the already receding fraction and dusk the gradually advancing fraction. The same reasoning applies to lunar eclipses.
51
求日食所起：日在陰曆，初起西北，甚於正北，復于東北； 日在陽曆，初起西南，甚于正南，復于東南。 其食八分已上者，皆起正西，復於正東。 此據午地而論之，其餘方位，審黃道斜正、月行所向，可知方向。
To find the solar eclipse's starting direction: when the sun is in the yin calendar, contact begins in the northwest, reaches maximum due north, and ends in the northeast; when the sun is in the yang calendar, contact begins in the southwest, reaches maximum due south, and ends in the southeast. When the eclipse magnitude is eight-tenths or greater, contact always begins due west and ends due east. This assumes the noon meridian as reference; for other locations, inspect the ecliptic's obliquity and the moon's direction of motion to determine the bearing.
52
求月食所起：月在陰曆，初起東南，甚于正南，復於西南； 月在陽曆，初起東北，甚於正北，復於西北。 其食八分已上，皆起正東，復於正西。 此亦據午地而論之，其餘方位，依日食所向，即知既虧、復滿。
To find the lunar eclipse's starting direction: when the moon is in the yin calendar, contact begins in the southeast, reaches maximum due south, and ends in the southwest; when the moon is in the yang calendar, contact begins in the northeast, reaches maximum due north, and ends in the northwest. When the eclipse magnitude is eight-tenths or greater, contact always begins due east and ends due west. This likewise assumes the noon meridian; for other locations, follow the solar eclipse's directional rule to determine totality, first contact, and full restoration.

歲差：一百二十六、秒一十七。
Annual difference: 126 parts, 17 seconds.
119
求諸變總差：各以其段平行分與後段平行分相減，餘為泛差； 並前段泛差，四因之，退一等，為總差。 若前段無平行分相減為泛差，各因後段初日行分與其段平行分相減，為半總差； 倍之，為總差。
To find the total difference for each phase: subtract this segment's mean motion from the next segment's mean motion — the remainder is the general difference; combine with the previous segment's general difference, multiply by 4, and reduce one grade — yielding the total difference. If the previous segment lacks a mean motion subtraction for the general difference, subtract this segment's mean motion from the next segment's initial-day motion parts — yielding half the total difference; double it to obtain the total difference.
120
若後段無平行分相減為泛差者，各因前段末日行分與其段平行分相減，為半總差； 倍之，為總差。
If the following segment lacks a mean motion subtraction for the general difference, subtract this segment's mean motion from the previous segment's final-day motion parts — yielding half the total difference; double it to obtain the total difference.

其前後退行者，各置本段平行分，十四乘，十五除，為總差。 其金星夕退、夕伏、再合、晨退，各依順段術入之，即得所求。
For retrograde segments before and after, set the mean motion parts, multiply by 14, and divide by 15 — yielding the total difference. For Venus evening retrograde, evening occultation, second conjunction, and morning retrograde, apply the direct-segment procedure in each case to obtain the desired result.
122
求五星定合及見伏泛用積：其木、火、土三星，各以平合及前疾、後伏定積為泛用積，金、水二星平合及夕見、晨伏者，置其星其段盈縮差，金以倍之，水以三之，列於上位； 又置盈縮差，以其段初行率乘之，退二等，以減上位； 又置初行率，減去一百分，餘以除之為日，不滿，退除為分，乃盈減縮加中積，為其星其變泛用積。
To find the general-use accumulations for fixed conjunction and visibility-occultation: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn use the mean-conjunction and anterior-fast or posterior-occultation fixed accumulations; for Venus and Mercury at mean conjunction, evening visibility, or morning occultation, set the segment's excess-deficit difference — doubling for Venus and tripling for Mercury — and place it in the upper position; again set the excess-deficit difference, multiply by the segment's initial motion rate, reduce two grades, and subtract from the upper position; again set the initial motion rate, subtract 100 parts, divide for days and parts, then subtract when in excess and add when in deficit to the mid-accumulation — thereby obtaining the phase general-use accumulation.
123
金、水二星再合及夕伏、晨見者，其星其段盈縮差，金星直用，水以倍之，進二位，以其段初行率加一百分以除之，所得，並盈縮差，以盈加縮減中積，為其星其段泛用積。
For Venus and Mercury at second conjunction, evening occultation, or morning visibility, use the segment's excess-deficit difference directly for Venus and doubled for Mercury, advance two places, add 100 parts to the initial motion rate and divide, combine with the excess-deficit difference, and add or subtract from the mid-accumulation — yielding the segment general-use accumulation.
124
求五星定合定積定星：其木、火、土三星平合者，以平合初日行分減一百分，餘以約其日太陽盈縮分為分，滿百為日，不滿為分，命為距合差日； 以盈縮分減之，為距合差度； 以差日、差度縮加盈減其星平合泛用積，為其星定合日定積定星。 金、水二星平合者，以一百分減初日行分，餘以除其日太陽盈縮分，為距合差日； 以盈縮分加之，為距合差度； 以差日、差度盈加縮減平合泛用積，為其星定合日定積定星也。
To find the fixed conjunction accumulation and star: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn at mean conjunction, subtract 100 parts from the mean-conjunction initial-day motion, use the remainder to contract that day's solar excess-deficit parts, and promote every 100 parts to a day — this is the conjunction-distance difference day; subtract the excess-deficit parts to obtain the conjunction-distance difference in degrees. Apply the difference day and degrees, adding when in deficit and subtracting when in excess to the mean-conjunction general-use accumulation — thereby obtaining the fixed conjunction day, accumulation, and star. For Venus and Mercury at mean conjunction, subtract 100 parts from the initial-day motion and divide that day's solar excess-deficit parts by the remainder — yielding the conjunction-distance difference day; add the excess-deficit parts to obtain the conjunction-distance difference in degrees. Apply the difference day and degrees, adding when in excess and subtracting when in deficit to the mean-conjunction general-use accumulation — thereby obtaining the fixed conjunction day, accumulation, and star.
125
金、水二星退合者，以初日行分一百分，以除太陽盈縮分，為距合差日； 以距合差日減盈縮分，為距合差度； 以差日盈減縮加再合泛用積，為其星再合定日定積差度； 盈加縮減再合泛用積，為其星再合日定星； 各加冬至大、小余及黃道加時日躔宿次命之，即得其日日辰及宿次。
For Venus and Mercury in retrograde conjunction, take the initial-day motion as 100 parts and divide the solar excess-deficit parts — yielding the conjunction-distance difference day; subtract the conjunction-distance difference day from the excess-deficit parts to obtain the conjunction-distance difference in degrees. Apply the difference day, subtracting when in excess and adding when in deficit to the second-conjunction general-use accumulation — thereby obtaining the second-conjunction fixed day, accumulation, and difference degrees; add when in excess and subtract when in deficit to the second-conjunction general-use accumulation — thereby obtaining the second-conjunction day and fixed star; Add the winter-solstice major and minor remainders and the ecliptic hour solar position for each, count the lodges, and obtain the day, double-hour, and lodge for each.

求木火土星晨見夕伏定用積：各置其星其段泛用積，乃加減一象度，晨見加之，夕伏減之。
To find the fixed-use accumulations for morning visibility and evening occultation of Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn: set each segment's general-use accumulation, then add or subtract one image degree — adding for morning visibility and subtracting for evening occultation.

半周天已下自相乘，已上，覆減周天度，餘亦自相乘，各二因百約之，在一百六十七已上，以一百約其日太陽盈縮分減之，不滿一百六十七者即加之，以其星本伏見度乘之，十五除，為差； 乃置其段初日行分，覆減一百分，餘以除其差為日，不滿，退除為分所得，以加減泛用積，晨見加之，夕伏減之。
Below half a celestial circuit, square the value; above, subtract from the full circuit and square the remainder; double each and contract by 100; if at or above 167, subtract the day's solar excess-deficit parts contracted by 100, otherwise add; multiply by the planet's base occultation-visibility degrees and divide by 15 — yielding the correction; Set the segment's initial-day motion, subtract 100 parts in overlay fashion, divide the difference by the remainder for days and parts, and apply to the general-use accumulation — adding for morning visibility and subtracting for evening occultation.

Each thereby obtains the fixed-use accumulation for morning visibility and evening occultation; add the civil winter-solstice major remainder, count from jiazi exclusive, and obtain the day and double-hour.

To find the fixed-use accumulations for evening visibility and morning occultation of Venus and Mercury: set each segment's general-use accumulation, then add or subtract one image degree — subtracting for evening visibility and adding for morning occultation.
