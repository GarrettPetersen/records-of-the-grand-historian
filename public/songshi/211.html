公元紀年宰相進拜加官罷免執政進拜加官罷免
1033二年癸酉四月己未，張士遜自刑部尚書、平章事加門下侍郎、昭文館大學士、監修國史。李迪自資政殿大學士、工部尚書、判都省以本官平章事加集賢殿大學士。十月戊午，呂夷簡自武勝軍節度使、同平章事、判陳州加兼吏部尚書、同平章事、昭文館大學士、監修國史。 張士遜 呂夷簡 李迪四月己未，呂夷簡自門下侍郎、同平章事以使相判澶州。十月戊午，中書侍郎、平章事張士遜以過楊崇勳飲，稽留慰班，為中丞范諷所劾，以左僕射判河南府，己未，改授使相。四月己未，王隨自翰林侍讀學士、戶部侍郎除參加政事。李諮自樞密直學士、禮部侍郎，權三司使遷樞密副使。王德用自步軍副都指揮使、福州觀察使加檢校太保、簽書樞密院事。五月乙亥，李諮丁父憂起復。十月戊午，王曙自資政殿學士、吏部侍郎、知河南府加檢校太傅，遷樞密使。宋綬自端明殿學士兼翰林侍讀學士、刑部侍郎除參知政事。王德用自檢校太保、簽書樞密院事，蔡齊自龍圖閣學士、權三司使事，並遷樞密副使。四月己未，張耆自樞密使以左僕射、檢校太師兼侍中、護國軍節度使判許州。夏竦自樞密副使以禮部尚書知襄州。陳堯佐自參知政事以戶部尚書知永興軍。范雍自樞密副使以戶部侍郎知荊南府。趙稹自樞密副使以尚書左丞知河中府。晏殊自參加政事以禮部尚書知江寧府。十月戊午，楊崇勳自樞密使以河陽三城節度使、同平章事判許州。十一月癸亥朔，參知政事薛奎辭疾，以資政殿學士判都省免。
1034景祐元年甲戌李迪 呂夷簡七月庚子，王曙自樞密使加平章事。八月庚午，王曾自天平軍節度使、檢校太師、同平章事除樞密使。八月癸亥，樞密使王曙卒。
1035二年乙亥二月戊辰，王曾自樞密使加右僕射兼門下侍郎、同平章事、集賢殿大學士。呂夷簡自門下侍郎兼吏部尚書、平章事加右僕射。十一月乙巳，夷簡加申國公，曾加沂國公。 李迪 呂夷簡 王曾二月戊辰，李迪自集賢殿大學士、工部尚書、平章事以刑部尚書知亳州。二月戊辰，王隨自參知政事加吏部侍郎，李諮自樞密副使加戶部侍郎，並知樞密院事。王德用自簽書樞密事加奉國軍節度使留後，韓億自御史中丞加工部侍郎，並同知樞密院事。蔡齊自樞密副使加禮部侍郎，盛度自端明殿學士兼翰林侍讀學士加禮部侍郎，並除參知政事。
1036三年丙子呂夷簡王曾十二月丁卯，王德用自同知樞密院事進知樞密院事。章得象自翰林學士承旨兼侍讀學士、龍圖閣直學士、禮部侍郎遷同知樞密院事。十二月丙寅，樞密副使李諮卒。
1037四年丁丑四月甲子，王隨自知樞密院事加門下侍郎、同中書門下平章事、昭文館大學士、監修國史。陳堯佐自戶部侍郎、知鄭州加同中書門下平章事、集賢殿大學士。 呂夷簡 王曾 王隨 陳堯佐四月甲子，呂夷簡自右僕射、申國公以鎮安軍節度使、同平章事判許州。王曾自右僕射、沂國公以左僕射、資政殿大學士判鄆州。四月甲子，盛度自禮部侍郎、參知政事除知樞密院事。王鬷自樞密直學士、左司郎中、右諫議大夫除同知樞密院事。韓億自工部侍郎、同知樞密院事，遷三司使，程琳自吏部侍郎石中立自翰林學士承旨兼龍圖閣學士，並除參知政事。庚午，王德用自樞密副使加定武軍節度使。初，呂夷簡、王曾不協，曾言夷簡招權，交論於上前，參知政事宋綬善夷簡，樞密副使蔡齊頗附曾。四月甲子，綬以尚書左丞，齊以吏部侍郎並免。
1038寶元元年戊寅三月戊戌，張士遜自山南東道節度使、同平章事、判河南府加門下侍郎兼兵部尚書、平章事、昭文館大學士、監修國史。章得象自同知樞密院事守本官加平章事、集賢殿大學士。 王隨 陳堯佐 張士遜 章得象先是，右司諫韓琦屢言，宰執非才，三月戊戌，王隨自門下侍郎、同中書門下平章事，以彰信軍節度使守本官判河陽。陳堯佐自同中書門下平章事，以淮康軍節度使守本官判鄭州。十一月乙卯，王曾薨。三月戊戌，盛度自知樞密院事加武寧軍節度使、檢校太傅，王鬷自知樞密院事，加龍圖閣學士李若谷自工部侍郎權知開封府，並除參知政事。王博文自龍圖閣直學士、給事中、權三司使，加龍圖閣直學士陳執中自工部郎中、知永興軍加右諫議大夫，並除同知樞密院事。四月乙亥，張觀自給事中、權御史中丞除同知樞密院事。三月戊戌，韓億自參知政事罷守本官歸班，尋知應天府。石中立自參知政事以戶部侍郎、資政殿學士免。四月癸酉，同知樞密院事王博文卒。
1039二年己卯張士遜 章得象五月壬子，夏守贇自鎮海軍節度使、真定都部署除知樞密院事。十月壬寅，王鬷自參知政事加工部侍郎，除知樞密院事。宋庠自翰林學士、知制詰加諫議大夫，除參知政事。五月壬子，王德用自宣徽南院使、定國軍節度使、樞密副使以武寧軍節度使免。十月丁酉，盛度自武寧軍節度使、知樞密院事坐馮士元事，奪節貶秩，以尚書右丞知揚州。程琳自參知政事以光祿卿知潁州。
1040康定元年庚辰五月壬戌，呂夷簡自鎮安軍節度使、判許州，加右僕射、門下侍郎、同平章事、昭文館大學士、監修國史。 張士遜 章得象 呂夷簡五月壬戌，張士遜自門下侍郎兼兵部尚書、平章事罷守太傅，進封鄧國公。三月戊寅，晏殊自三司使、刑部尚書，宋綬自禮部尚書、知河南府，並除知樞密院事。王貽永自保安軍節度使、檢校太傅、駙馬都尉除同知樞密院事。七月丁亥，夏守贇自宣徽南院使、陝西都部署復除同知樞密院事。八月戊申，杜衍自龍圖閣學士、刑部侍郎、權知開封府除同知樞密院事。九月戊午，宋綬自守尚書左丞，起復加翰林學士兼龍圖閣學士晁宗慤自左司郎中、知制誥加右諫議大夫，並除參知政事。鄭戩自龍圖閣直學士、起居捨人、權三司使加右諫議大夫除同知樞密院事。戊辰，晏殊自知樞密院事加檢校太傅、樞密使。王貽永、杜衍、鄭戩並自同知樞密院事進樞密副使。二月丁亥，夏守贇自知樞密院事除宣徽南院使、陝西都部署兼經略安撫等使。三月戊寅，知樞密院事王鬷，同知樞密院事陳執中、張觀，並以西兵不利，又議鄉兵不決，鬷知河南府，執中知青州，觀知相州。九月戊午，李若谷自參知政事以疾授資政殿大學士、吏部侍郎、提舉會靈觀。十二月癸卯，參知政事宋綬卒。
1041慶曆元年辛巳十月壬午，呂夷簡自右僕射加司空，固辭。 章得象 呂夷簡五月辛未，王舉正自翰林學士、兵部侍郎、知制誥加右諫議大夫除參知政事。任中師自樞密直學士、右諫議大夫、知益州，任布自給事中、知河南府，並除樞密副使。五月辛未，宋庠自參知政事本官知揚州。鄭戩自樞密副使以資政殿學士知杭州。
1042二年壬午七月壬午，呂夷簡自右僕射、門下侍郎、同平章事判樞密院事。章得象自守同知樞密院事、平章事兼樞密使。晏殊自樞密使加同平章事。九月丙午，呂夷簡改兼樞密使。是年冬，夷簡以疾授司空平章軍國重事，固辭。 章得象 呂夷簡 晏殊三月辛酉，晁宗慤自參知政事以疾遷資政殿學士、給事中免。七月丙午，任布自樞密副使遷秩知河陽府。
1043三年癸未三月戊子，呂夷簡自司空、平章軍國重事以疾授司徒、監修國史，與議軍國大事。章得象自平章事加工部尚書、昭文館大學士，晏殊自檢校太尉、刑部尚書、同平章事加同中書門下平章事、集賢殿大學士，並兼樞密使。九月戊辰，章得象加監修國史。 章得象 呂夷簡 晏殊四月甲子，司徒呂夷簡罷與議軍國大事，九月戊辰，以太尉致仕。三月戊子，夏竦自宣徽南院使判蔡州遷戶部尚書，除樞密使。賈昌朝自右諫議大夫、權御史中丞除參知政事。富弼自右正言、知制誥、史館修撰除右諫議大夫、樞密副使，固辭。四月甲辰，韓琦自陝西四路馬步軍都部署、兼經略安撫招討等使、樞密直學士、右諫議大夫，范仲淹自安撫經略招討使，並樞密副使。乙巳，杜衍自吏部侍郎、樞密副使除樞密使。七月丁丑，范仲淹自樞密副使除參知政事，富弼自史館修撰除樞密副使，皆固辭，八月丁未，復命之。四月乙巳，樞密使夏竦既至，為臺諫所論免。七月丙子，王舉正自參知政事以資政殿學士知許州。九月乙亥，任中師自樞密副使以禮部侍郎、資政殿學士知永興軍。
1044四年甲申九月甲申，杜衍自檢校太傅依前行吏部侍郎加同平章事兼樞密使、集賢殿大學士。 晏殊 章得象 杜衍九月庚午，同中書門下平章事晏殊為孫甫、蔡襄所論，以工部尚書知潁州。六月壬子，范仲淹自參知政事出為陝西、河東宣撫使。八月甲午，富弼自樞密副使出為河北宣撫使。九月甲寅，賈昌朝自右諫議大夫、參知政事加檢校太傅、行工部侍郎、樞密使。陳執中自資政殿學士、工部侍郎、知青州除參知政事，蔡襄、孫甫等言其剛愎不可任，上不聽。
1045五年乙酉正月丙戌，賈昌朝自樞密使、檢校太傅依前工部侍郎加同平章事兼樞密使、集賢殿大學士。四月戊申，陳執中自參知政事依前行工部侍郎加同平章事、集賢殿大學士。昌朝尋加昭文館大學士、監修國史兼譯經潤文使。 杜衍 章得象 賈昌朝 陳執中正月丙戌，杜衍自行吏部侍郎、同平章事以行尚書左丞知兗州。四月戊申章得象自檢校太尉、工部尚書、同平章事以檢校太傅、同平章事、鎮安軍節度使知陳州。十月庚辰，詔宰臣罷兼樞密使。正月丙戌，王貽永自宣徽南院使、保寧軍節度使依前檢校太傅除樞密使。宋庠自資政殿學士、給事中、知鄆州除參知政事。 吳育自翰林學士、禮部郎中、知制誥、權知開封府加右諫議大夫，龍圖閣學士龐籍自右諫議大夫、知延州，並除樞密副使。四月庚戌，吳育自諫議大夫、樞密副使除參知政事。丁度自翰林學士承旨、端明殿學士除工部尚書、樞密副使。正月乙酉，范沖淹自右諫議大夫以資政殿學士出知邠州兼陝西四路沿邊安撫使。富弼自右諫議大夫以資政殿學士、京東西路安撫使兼知鄆州，用錢明逸疏也。先是，樞密副使韓琦上疏，論富弼不當輕罷，不報。董士廉又訟水洛城事，三月辛酉，琦以資政殿學士出知揚州。
1046六年丙戌賈昌朝 陳執中二月癸丑，王貽永自檢校太傅、樞密使加同平章事。八月癸酉，參知政事吳育以與賈昌朝論事不合，改樞密副使。丁度自工部侍郎、樞密副使除參知政事。
1047七年丁亥三月乙未，陳執中自工部侍郎、平章事加昭文館大學士、監修國史兼譯經潤文使。 賈昌朝 陳執中先是，賈昌朝與吳育爭論上前，三月乙未，昌朝以武勝軍節度使判大名府兼北京留守。初，上欲以河陽三城節度使、檢校太尉、同平章事、知大名府夏竦為相，臺諫言其與陳執中素不合，三月乙未，乃命竦為樞密使。文彥博自樞密直學士、戶部郎中、知益州加右諫議大夫，除樞密副使。丁酉，除參知政事。高若訥自右諫議大夫、御史中丞除樞密副使。三月乙未，吳育自右諫議大夫、樞密副使以給事中歸班。
1048八年戊子閏正月戊申，文彥博自諫議大夫、參知政事加行禮部侍郎、同平章事、集賢殿大學士。 陳執中 文彥博四月辛未，明鎬自端明殿學士、給事中、權三司使除參知政事。五月辛酉，宋庠自給事中、參知政事除樞密使。龐籍自樞密副使、左諫議大夫除參知政事。四月辛未，丁度自參知政事遷紫宸殿學士兼侍讀學士。五月辛酉，夏竦自樞密使以檢校太師依前同平章事、河陽三城節度使判河南府兼西京留守司，以何郯論其姦邪故也。六月甲午，參知政事明鎬卒。
1049皇祐元年己丑八月壬戌，文彥博自行禮部侍郎、同平章事加吏部侍郎、昭文館大學士、監修國史。宋庠自樞密使、檢校太傅、行工部侍郎加兵部侍郎、同平章事、集賢殿大學士。 陳執中 文彥博八月壬戌，陳執中自工部侍郎、平章事以兵部尚書出知陳州。是歲，賈昌朝自山南東道節度使、同平章事、安國公、判鄭州依前尚書右僕射、觀文殿大學士判尚書都省。八月壬戌，王貽永自樞密使、檢校太傅、同平章事加兼侍中。龐籍自左諫議大夫、參知政事加檢校太傅、行工部侍郎、樞密使。高若訥自樞密副使、右諫議大夫加工部侍郎，除參知政事。梁適自翰林學士、吏部郎中加右諫議大夫，除樞密副使。
1050二年庚寅文彥博 宋庠
1051三年辛卯十月庚子，龐籍自樞密使、檢校太傅依前戶部侍郎加同平章事、昭文館大學士、監修國史兼譯經使。 文彥博 宋庠龐籍先是，包拯、吳奎等言宋庠無所建明，三月庚申，庠以行刑部尚書、觀文殿大學士出知河南府。十一月庚子，文彥博自吏部侍郎、同平章事以行吏部尚書、觀文殿學士出知許州。三月庚申，劉沆自龍圖閣學士、工部侍郎、權知開封府除參知政事。十月庚子，高若訥自戶部侍郎、參知政事除檢校太傅、樞密使。辛丑，梁適自樞密副使、給事中除參知政事。王堯臣自給事中、翰林學士承旨除樞密副使。
1052四年壬辰龐籍先是，樞密使王貽永以疾求罷，五月丁未，聽解侍中，為同平章事，依前樞密使。六月丁亥，狄青自彰化軍節度使、知延州除檢校司空、樞密副使，王舉正、賈黯皆論其不可，不聽。九月庚午，青宣徽南院使、荊湖北路宣撫使、都大提舉廣南東西路經制盜賊事，辛未，詔宣撫回日，依前樞密副使。
1053五年癸巳七月壬申，陳執中自集慶軍節度使、判大名府加行吏部尚書、同平章事、昭文館大學士、監修國史兼譯經潤文使。梁適自參知政事加禮部侍郎、同平章事、集賢殿大學士。 龐籍 陳執中 梁適龐籍甥與堂吏受賕，諫官韓絳論之，七月壬申，籍依前戶部侍郎、同平章事以京東西路安撫使出知鄆州。二月癸未，狄青以宣徽南院使復為樞密副使。五月乙巳，狄青自樞密副使、護國軍節度使、檢校太尉、河中尹兼御史大夫仍守前官除樞密使。丁未，孫沔自樞密直學士、給事中、知杭州除樞密副使。五月乙巳，高若訥自戶部侍郎、樞密使以尚書左丞、觀文殿學士兼厩牧制置使免。
1054至和元年甲午八月丙午，劉沆自參知政事依前工部侍郎加同平章事、集賢殿大學士。 陳執中 梁適 劉沆七月丁卯，中丞孫抃等劾梁適，戊辰，適罷，守本官出知鄭州，尋加觀文殿大學士、知秦州。二月壬戌，田况自三司使、禮部侍郎除樞密副使。三月己巳，王德用自檢校太師、同平章事兼羣牧制置使、判鄭州、冀國公，仍守前官除樞密使兼河陽三城節度使。七月丁卯，程戡自端明殿學士、給事中、知益州除參知政事。二月壬戌，孫沔自樞密副使以資政殿學士出知杭州。三月己巳，王貽永自樞密使依前檢校太師，以行尚書右僕射兼侍中、鄧國公、景靈宮使領彰德軍節度使。
1055二年乙未六月戊戌，文彥博自忠武軍節度使、檢校太尉兼知永興軍加禮部尚書、同平章事、昭文館大學士兼譯經潤文使。富弼自宣徽南院使、檢校太保、判幷州加戶部侍郎、同平章事、集賢殿大學士。劉沆自工部侍郎、同平章事加兵部侍郎、監修國史。 陳執中 劉沆 文彥博 富弼六月戊戌，行吏部尚書、同平章事陳執中以檢校太尉、同平章事、鎮海軍節度判亳州。
1056嘉祐元年丙申十二月壬戌，文彥博自禮部尚書、同平章事加監修國史。 劉沆 文彥博 富弼中丞張昪等論劉沆疏十七上，十二月壬子，沆以行工部尚書、觀文殿大學士出知應天府。閏三月癸未，王堯臣自樞密副使、給事中除戶部侍郎、參知政事。程戡自給事中、參知政事除戶部侍郎、樞密副使。八月癸亥，韓琦自三司使加檢校少傅，依前行工部尚書、樞密使。十一月辛巳，賈昌朝自山南東道節度使、檢校太師兼侍中、判大名府兼北京留守、許國公，仍守前官除樞密使、襄州觀察使。十二月壬子，曾公亮自翰林學士、權知開封府除給事中、參知政事。八月癸亥，狄青自樞密使依前檢校太尉，以同平章事、護國軍節度使判陳州。十一月辛巳，王德用自樞密使依前檢校太師，以同平章事、景靈宮使領忠武軍節度使。十二月壬戌，樞密使賈昌朝辭兼侍中。

1058三年戊戌六月丙午，富弼自戶部侍郎、同平章事加禮部侍郎、昭文館大學士、監修國史兼譯經潤文使。韓琦自樞密使、工部尚書依前官加同平章事、集賢殿大學士。 文彥博 富弼 韓琦六月丙午，文彥博自檢校太師、同平章事以河陽三城節度使判河南府兼西京留守。六月丙午，宋庠自觀文殿大學士依前行兵部尚書、同平章事兼厩牧制置使、樞密使。田况自樞密副使、禮部侍郎、檢校太傅除樞密使。張昪自右諫議大夫、權御史中丞除樞密副使。六月丙午，賈昌朝自樞密使以行尚書左僕射、檢校太師兼侍中、景靈宮使領鎮安軍節度使。八月己未，參知政事王堯臣卒。
1059四年己亥富弼 韓琦五月丙辰，田况自樞密使以尚書右丞、觀文殿學士兼翰林侍讀學士提舉景靈宮事。
1060五年庚子富弼 韓琦四月癸未，孫抃自翰林學士承旨兼侍讀學士、禮部侍郎除樞密副使。十一月辛丑，曾公亮自參知政事、檢校太傅依前禮部侍郎除樞密副使。張昪自樞密副使、右諫議大夫，孫抃自禮部侍郎、樞密副使，並除參知政事。歐陽修自翰林學士兼侍讀學士、禮部侍郎、知制誥、史館修撰，陳旭自樞密直學士、右諫議大夫，趙槩自御史中丞、加禮部侍郎，並除樞密副使。旭後改名升之。四月癸未，程戡自樞密副使以禮部侍郎、觀文殿學士兼翰林侍讀學士、同厩牧制置使免。十一月辛丑，宋庠自檢校太尉、行兵部尚書、同平章事兼羣牧制置使、莒國公、樞密使以河陽三城節度使、同平章事判鄭州。
1061六年辛丑六月甲戌，富弼起復以前官同平章事，固辭。閏八月庚子，韓琦自工部尚書、同平章事加昭文館大學士、監修國史。曾公亮自樞密副使、檢校太傅兼羣牧制置使、行吏部侍郎加同平章事、集賢殿大學士。 韓琦 曾公亮三月己亥，禮部侍郎、同平章事富弼丁母憂。四月庚辰，包拯自三司使、給事中除樞密副使。閏八月庚子，張昪自右諫議大夫、參知政事加檢校太傅、行工部侍郎，除樞密使。歐陽修自禮部侍郎、樞密副使除參知政事。胡宿自翰林學士兼端明殿學士、翰林學士、左司郎中、知制誥、史館修撰除左諫議大夫、樞密副使。四月庚辰，樞密副使陳旭以資政殿學士知定州。
1062七年壬寅韓琦 曾公亮三月乙卯，趙槩自樞密副使、禮部侍郎除參知政事。吳奎自翰林學士、權知開封府除樞密副使。三月乙卯，孫抃自參知政事以觀文殿學士兼翰林侍讀學士、同厩牧制置使免。五月庚午，樞密副使包拯卒。
1063八年癸卯英宗四月壬申朔即位韓琦 曾公亮五月戊午，富弼既除喪，授樞密使、檢校太師、行禮部尚書、同平章事。
1064治平元年甲辰閏五月戊辰，韓琦自門下侍郎兼兵部尚書、同平章事、昭文館大學士、監修國史、魏國公加尚書右僕射。 韓琦 曾公亮十二月丙午，王疇自翰林學士、禮部侍郎除樞密副使。十二月戊子，樞密副使吳奎丁父憂。
1065二年乙巳韓琦 曾公亮三月己未，起復吳奎，奎固辭。五月癸亥，陳旭自資政殿學士、禮部侍郎除樞密副使。七月庚辰，文彥博自淮南節度使、檢校太師兼侍中、行揚州大都督長史、潞國公除樞密使。辛巳，呂公弼自工部侍郎、權三司使、樞密直學士除守本官、樞密副使。二月癸卯，樞密副使王疇卒。七月癸亥，富弼自行戶部尚書以檢校太師、同平章事、鎮海軍節度使判河陽。庚辰，張昪自樞密使辭疾，以檢校太尉、同平章事、彰信軍節度使判許州。
1066三年丙午韓琦 曾公亮四月庚戌，郭逵自殿前都虞候、容州觀察使加檢校太保，除同簽書樞密院事。四月庚戌，胡宿自樞密副使以觀文殿學士、吏部侍郎知杭州。十月丁亥，郭逵自同簽書樞密院事除陝西四路沿邊宣撫使兼判渭州。
1067四年丁未神宗正月丁巳即位正月戊辰，韓琦自尚書右僕射、同平章事、魏國公加守司空兼侍中。九月壬寅，曾公亮自集賢殿大學士、同中書門下平章事加尚書左僕射。 韓琦 曾公亮九月辛丑，韓琦自守司空兼侍中、同平章事、魏國公以守司徒兼侍中、檢校太師、鎮安武勝軍節度使判相州。正月丙寅，吳奎除喪，復樞密副使。三月癸酉，以禮部侍郎參知政事。九月辛丑，呂公弼自樞密副使、刑部侍郎除檢校太傅、樞密使。張方平自翰林學士承旨兼龍圖閣學士、端明殿學士、戶部尚書，趙抃自龍圖閣學士、右司郎中、知諫院，並除參知政事。抃遷右諫議大夫。韓絳自三司使、吏部侍郎，邵亢自樞密直學士、兵部員外郎、知開封府，並除樞密副使。亢遷右諫議大夫。三月壬申，歐陽修自尚書右丞、參知政事以觀文殿學士、刑部尚書知亳州。九月辛丑，吳奎自參知政事以資政殿學士、戶部侍郎知青州。陳旭自樞密副使、戶部侍郎以觀文殿學士、尚書右丞知越州。癸卯，郭逵自同簽書樞密院事以宣徽南院使判鄆州。十月己酉，參知政事張方平丁父憂。
1068熙寧元年戊申曾公亮正月丙申，唐介自龍圖閣學士、給事中、權三司使除參知政事。七月己卯，陳旭自觀文殿學士、尚書左丞、知越州除知樞密院事。正月丙申，趙槩自參知政事以吏部尚書、觀文殿學士知徐州。十二月辛酉，邵亢自樞密副使以資政殿學士、給事中知越州。
1069二年己酉二月己亥，富弼自觀文殿大學士、行尚書左僕射，鄭國公依前左僕射加兼門下侍郎、同平章事、昭文館大學士、監修國史。十月丙申，曾公亮自行吏部侍郎、同平章事、集賢殿大學士加昭文館大學士、監修國史兼譯經潤文使魯國公。陳旭自尚書右丞、知樞密院事加行禮部尚書、同平章事、集賢殿大學士。 富弼 曾公亮 陳旭十月丙申，富弼自左僕射兼門下侍郎、同平章事加檢校太師，依前行左僕射、同平章事以武寧軍節度使判亳州。二月庚子，王安石自翰林學士、工部侍郎兼侍講除右諫議大夫、參知政事。四月丁未，參知政事唐介卒。
1070三年庚戌十二月丁卯，韓絳自吏部尚書、參知政事加同平章事、昭文館大學士。王安石自右諫議大夫、參知政事加禮部侍郎、同平章事、監修國史。 曾公亮 陳旭 韓絳 王安石九月庚子，曾公亮自行吏部侍郎、同平章事、昭文館大學士以守司空、檢校太師兼侍中領河陽三城節度使、集禧觀使，五日一奉朝請。十月戊寅，陳旭自行禮部尚書、同平章事丁母憂。二月壬申，司馬光自翰林學士、兼侍讀學士、右諫議大夫、史館修撰除樞密副使，辭不拜。四月己卯，韓絳自樞密副使除兼參知政事。七月壬辰，馮京自□部侍郎、權御史中丞除右諫議大夫、樞密副使。九月辛丑，馮京自樞密副使除參知政事。吳充自翰林學士、右司郎中、權三司使除右諫議大夫、樞密副使。十二月丁卯，王珪自翰林學士承旨、端明殿學士、翰林侍讀學士、禮部侍郎仍守本官，除參知政事。四月己卯，趙抃自參知政事、右諫議大夫以資政殿學士知杭州。七月壬辰，呂公弼自樞密副使以吏部侍郎、觀文殿大學士知太原府。九月乙未，韓絳自樞密副使除陝西路宣撫使。
1071四年辛亥正月壬子，陳旭起復，仍禮部尚書加同平章事，辭不拜。 韓絳 王安石三月丁未，韓絳自同平章事守吏部侍郎，知鄧州。
1072五年壬子王安石二月丙寅，蔡挺自龍圖閣直學士、右諫議大夫、知渭州除樞密副使。十二月壬午，陳旭以檢校太傅、行禮部尚書、同平章事除樞密使。
1073六年癸丑王安石四月己亥，文彥博自劍南西川節度使、守司空兼侍中、樞密使以守司徒兼侍中、河東節度使判河陽。
1074七年甲寅四月丙戌，韓絳自觀文殿大學士、行吏部侍郎、知大名府守本官加同中書門下平章事、監修國史。 王安石 韓絳四月丙戌，王安石自禮部侍郎、同平章事以吏部尚書、觀文殿大學士知江寧府。四月丙戌，呂惠卿自翰林學士、右正言兼侍讀除右諫議大夫、參知政事。十二月丁卯，王韶自觀文殿學士兼端明殿學士、龍圖閣學士、禮部侍郎、知熙州除樞密副使。
1075八年乙卯二月癸酉，王安石自觀文殿大學士、吏部尚書、知江寧府依前官加同平章事、昭文館大學士。 韓絳 王安石八月庚戌，韓絳自觀文殿大學士、行吏部侍郎、同中書門下平章事以禮部尚書、觀文殿大學士知許州。四月戊寅，吳充自樞密副使、右諫議大夫除檢校太傅、行工部尚書、樞密使。十二月壬寅，元絳自翰林學士兼侍讀學士、判太常寺兼厩牧使、工部侍郎除參知政事。曾孝寬自龍圖閣直學士、起居舍人兼樞密都承旨、同羣牧使除樞密直學士、簽書樞密院事。正月庚子，蔡挺自右諫議大夫、樞密副使以資政殿學士判南京留司御史臺。馮京自右諫議大夫、參知政事以守本官知亳州。閏四月乙未，陳旭自禮部尚書、同平章事、樞密使以檢校太尉、鎮江軍節度使、同平章事判揚州。十月庚寅，呂惠卿以參知政事守本官知陳州。
1076九年丙辰十月丙午，吳充自樞密使、檢校太傅、行工部侍郎守前官加同平章事、監修國史。王珪自禮部侍郎、參知政事守前官加同平章事、集賢殿大學士。 王安石 吳充 王珪十月丙午，王安石自檢校太傅依前尚書左僕射，領鎮南軍節度使判江寧府。十月丙午，馮京自資政殿學士、右諫議大夫、知成都府除知樞密院事。
1077十年丁巳吳充 王珪二月己亥，王韶自樞密副使以戶部侍郎、觀文殿大學士知洪州。
1078元豐元年戊午吳充 王珪閏正月壬辰，孫固自權知開封府、樞密直學士、右諫議大夫除同知樞密院事。九月乙酉，呂公著自端明殿學士兼翰林侍讀學士、寶文閣學士、戶部侍郎，薛向自樞密直學士、工部侍郎，並除同知樞密院事。閏正月己亥，簽書樞密院事曾孝寬丁父憂，五月庚辰，起復，固辭。
1079二年己未吳充 王珪五月戊子，蔡確自右諫議大夫、權御史中丞、直學士院、判司農寺除參知政事。五月甲申，元絳自參知政事以工部侍郎知亳州。
1080三年庚申九月丙戌，王珪自同中書門下平章事加監修國史。 吳充 王珪三月己丑，吳充辭疾，以吏部尚書、觀文殿大學士、西太一宮使免。二月丙午，章惇自翰林學士、右正言、知審官東院除右諫議大夫、參知政事。九月癸未，薛向自工部侍郎、同知樞密院事、加正議大夫，孫固自右諫議大夫、同知樞密院事、加太中大夫，並除樞密副使。丙戌，馮京自通議大夫、知樞密院事除正議大夫、樞密使。丁亥，呂公著自戶部侍郎、同知樞密院除正議大夫、樞密副使。
1081四年辛酉王珪正月辛亥，孫固自樞密副使、太中大夫除兼厩牧制置使、知樞密院事。呂公著自樞密副使、正議大夫，加龍圖閣直學士韓縝自太中大夫、樞密都承旨兼厩牧使，並除同知樞密院事。三月甲辰，張璪自翰林學士除太中大夫、參知政事。正月辛亥，馮京自正議大夫兼羣牧制置使、樞密使以光祿大夫、觀文殿學士知河陽府。三月癸卯，章惇自參知政事坐父俞強占民田及為朱服所奏，而報上不實，以太中大夫知蔡州。
1082五年壬戌是年，改官制，以左、右僕射為宰相。四月癸酉，王珪自銀青光祿大夫兼門下侍郎、同中書門下平章事依前官加尚書左僕射兼門下侍郎。蔡確自太中大夫、參知政事依前官加右僕射兼中書侍郎。 王珪 蔡確四月甲戌，章惇自太中大夫、知定州加守門下侍郎。張璪自太中大夫、參知政事加守中書侍郎。蒲宗孟自翰林學士加中大夫、守尚書左丞。王安禮自翰林學士加中大夫、守尚書右丞。四月丁丑，呂公著自政議大夫、同知樞密院事以光祿大夫、資政殿大學士知定州。
1083六年癸亥王珪 蔡確七月丙辰，韓縝自太中大夫、同知樞密院進知樞密院事。安燾自太中大夫、試戶部尚書除同知樞密院事。八月辛卯，王安禮自守尚書右丞除尚書左丞。李清臣自吏部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。七月丙辰，孫固自知樞密院事以通議大夫、觀文殿學士知河陽。八月辛卯，蒲宗孟自中大夫以守尚書左丞知汝州。
1084七年甲子王珪 蔡確七月甲寅，王安禮自尚書左丞以端明殿學士知江寧府。
1085八年乙丑哲宗三月戊戌即位五月戊午，蔡確自通議大夫、右僕射兼中書侍郎加兼門下侍郎、左僕射。韓縝自通議大夫、知樞密院事加兼中書侍郎、右僕射。 王珪 蔡確 韓縝五月庚戌，左僕射兼門下侍郎王珪薨。五月戊午，章惇自通議大夫、門下侍郎除知樞密院事。司馬光自資政殿學士、通議大夫、知陳州加守門下侍郎。七月戊戌，呂公著自資政殿大學士、銀青光祿大夫兼侍讀加尚書左丞。

1033 CE.The second year of the reign, guiyou.In the fourth month on jiwei, Zhang Shisun rose from Minister of Justice and Vice Grand Councilor to Vice Director of the Secretariat, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and Supervisor of National History. Li Di, already Grand Academician of the Zizheng Hall, Minister of Works, and head of the Department of State Affairs, kept those posts as Vice Grand Councilor and was also made Jixian Grand Academician. In the tenth month on wuwu, Lü Yijian advanced from Wusheng military commissioner, Grand Councilor Associate, and Chenzhou administrator to add Minister of Personnel, Grand Councilor Associate, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and Supervisor of National History. Incumbents: Zhang Shisun, Lü Yijian, Li Di.On jiwei in the fourth month, Lü Yijian left the capital as Vice Director of the Secretariat and Grand Councilor Associate to serve as dispatched grand councilor at Duanzhou. On wuwu in the tenth month, Zhang Shisun, Vice Director of the Chancellery and Vice Grand Councilor, was censured by Censor-in-Chief Fan Feng for stopping at Yang Chongxun's feast and delaying in the condolence line; he was demoted to Left Vice Director administering Henan, then on jiwei reassigned as a dispatched grand councilor.On jiwei in the fourth month, Wang Sui was made Vice Grand Councilor from Hanlin Attendant-Reader and Vice Minister of Revenue. Li Zi moved from Direct Academician of Military Affairs and acting Three Departments commissioner to Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs. Wang Deyong was promoted from deputy foot-commander and Fuzhou observer to Honorary Grand Guardian and signing officer of the Bureau of Military Affairs. On yihai in the fifth month, Li Zi resumed office despite mourning his father. On wuwu in the tenth month, Wang Shu became Commissioner of Military Affairs with Honorary Grand Tutor rank after serving as Zizheng academician, Vice Minister of Personnel, and Henan administrator. Song Shou was appointed Vice Grand Councilor from Duanming and Hanlin reader and Vice Minister of Punishments. Wang Deyong and Cai Qi were both promoted to Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs.On jiwei in the fourth month, Zhang Qi left the Bureau of Military Affairs for Left Vice Director, Honorary Grand Preceptor with Palace Attendant, and Huguo commissioner at Xuzhou. Xia Song became Minister of Rites at Xiangzhou; Chen Yaozuo Minister of Revenue at Yongxing; Fan Yong Vice Minister of Revenue at Jingnan; Zhao Zhen Left Vice Director at Hezhong; Yan Shu Minister of Rites at Jiangning—all from military or vice-councilor posts. On wuwu in the tenth month, Yang Chongxun took Heyang Three Cities with Grand Councilor Associate at Xuzhou. On the first of the eleventh month, guihai, Vice Grand Councilor Xue Kui quit for illness and was relieved as Zizheng academician heading the Department of State Affairs.
1034 CE.Jingyou reign, year one, jiaxu.The incumbents were Li Di and Lü Yijian.In the seventh month on gengzi, Wang Shu added Vice Grand Councilor to his post as Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the eighth month on gengwu, Wang Zeng was made Commissioner of Military Affairs from Tianping commissioner, Honorary Grand Preceptor, and Grand Councilor Associate.In the eighth month on guihai, Commissioner of Military Affairs Wang Shu died.
1035 CE.The second year of the reign, yihai.In the second month on wuchen, Wang Zeng was promoted from Commissioner of Military Affairs to Right Vice Director, concurrently Vice Director of the Chancellery, Grand Councilor, and Jixian Grand Academician. Lü Yijian was promoted from Vice Director of the Chancellery concurrently Minister of Personnel and Vice Grand Councilor to Right Vice Director. In the eleventh month on yisi, Yijian was enfeoffed as Duke of Shen and Zeng as Duke of Yi. Incumbents: Li Di, Lü Yijian, and Wang Zeng.On wuchen in the second month, Li Di left the council for Minister of Punishments at Bozhou from Jixian Grand Academician, Minister of Works, and Vice Grand Councilor.In the second month on wuchen, Wang Sui was promoted from Vice Grand Councilor to Vice Minister of Personnel, and Li Zi from Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs to Vice Minister of Revenue; both were made administrators of the Bureau of Military Affairs. Wang Deyong was promoted from signing officer of the Bureau of Military Affairs to designated Fengguo military commissioner, and Han Yi from Vice Censor-in-Chief to Vice Minister of Works; both were made associate administrators of the Bureau of Military Affairs. Cai Qi was promoted from Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs to Vice Minister of Rites, and Sheng Du from Duanming academician concurrently Hanlin reader to Vice Minister of Rites; both were appointed Vice Grand Councilors.
1036 CE.The third year of the reign, bingzi.The incumbents were Lü Yijian and Wang Zeng.In the twelfth month on dingmao, Wang Deyong was advanced from associate administrator of the Bureau of Military Affairs to administrator. Zhang Dexiang was transferred from Hanlin expositor concurrently reader, Dragon Diagram direct academician, and Vice Minister of Rites to associate administrator of the Bureau of Military Affairs.In the twelfth month on bingyin, Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs Li Zi died.
1037 CE.The fourth year of the reign, dingchou.In the fourth month on jiazi, Wang Sui was promoted from administrator of the Bureau of Military Affairs to Vice Director of the Secretariat, Grand Councilor jointly with the Secretariat and Chancellery, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and Supervisor of National History. Chen Yaozuo was promoted from Vice Minister of Revenue and prefect of Zhengzhou to Grand Councilor jointly with the Secretariat and Chancellery and Jixian Grand Academician. Incumbents: Lü Yijian, Wang Zeng, Wang Sui, and Chen Yaozuo.In the fourth month on jiazi, Lü Yijian was made Zhen'an military commissioner, Grand Councilor Associate, and Xuzhou administrator, having held Right Vice Director and the title Duke of Shen. Wang Zeng was made Left Vice Director and Zizheng academician at Yanzhou, having held Right Vice Director and the title Duke of Yi.In the fourth month on jiazi, Sheng Du was appointed administrator of the Bureau of Military Affairs from Vice Minister of Rites and Vice Grand Councilor. Wang Jun was appointed associate administrator from direct military academician, Left Office director, and Right Remonstrance Counsellor. Han Yi was transferred from Vice Minister of Works and associate administrator to Three Departments commissioner; Cheng Lin and Shi Zhongli were both appointed Vice Grand Councilors. On gengwu, Wang Deyong was promoted from Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs to Dingwu military commissioner.At first Lü Yijian and Wang Zeng were at odds; Zeng accused Yijian of monopolizing power, and they traded charges before the emperor. Vice Grand Councilor Song Shou favored Yijian, while Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs Cai Qi largely sided with Zeng. In the fourth month on jiazi, Shou was dismissed from Left Vice Director of the Department of State and Qi from Vice Minister of Personnel.
1038 CE.Baoyuan reign, year one, wuyin.In the third month on wuxu, Zhang Shisun was promoted from Shannan East commissioner, Grand Councilor Associate, and Henan administrator to Vice Director of the Secretariat concurrently Minister of War, Vice Grand Councilor, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and Supervisor of National History. Zhang Dexiang, retaining associate administrator rank, was additionally made Vice Grand Councilor and Jixian Grand Academician. Incumbents: Wang Sui, Chen Yaozuo, Zhang Shisun, and Zhang Dexiang.Earlier, Han Qi of the Right Remonstrance Bureau had repeatedly declared the chief ministers lacked talent. In the third month on wuxu, Wang Sui was made Zhangxin commissioner at Heyang, retaining rank, from Vice Director of the Secretariat and joint Grand Councilor; Chen Yaozuo was made Huaikang commissioner at Zhengzhou, retaining rank, from joint Grand Councilor. In the eleventh month on yimao, Wang Zeng died.In the third month on wuxu, Sheng Du was promoted from military administrator to Wuning commissioner and Honorary Grand Preceptor; Wang Jun from military administrator to Dragon Diagram academician; Li Ruogu from Vice Minister of Works and acting Kaifeng prefect—all three with new posts, Wang Jun and Li Ruogu as Vice Grand Councilors. Wang Bowen and Chen Zhizhong were both appointed associate military administrators; in the fourth month on yihai, Zhang Guan joined them from Presentation Draft Attendant and acting Vice Censor-in-Chief.In the third month on wuxu, Han Yi was dismissed from Vice Grand Councilor, retaining rank and returning to court service; he soon became prefect of Yingtian. Shi Zhongli was removed from Vice Grand Councilor for Vice Minister of Revenue and Zizheng academician. In the fourth month on guiyou, associate military administrator Wang Bowen died.
1039 CE.The second year of the reign, jimao.The incumbents were Zhang Shisun and Zhang Dexiang.In the fifth month on renzi, Xia Shouyun was appointed military administrator from Zhenhai commissioner and Zhending supreme commander. In the tenth month on renyin, Wang Jun was promoted from Vice Grand Councilor to Vice Minister of Works and military administrator. Song Qi was promoted from Hanlin academician and drafting official to Remonstrance Counsellor and Vice Grand Councilor.In the fifth month on renzi, Wang Deyong was removed as Wuning commissioner, having served as Southern Palace Attendant commissioner, Dingguo commissioner, and Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the tenth month on dingyou, Sheng Du, implicated in the Feng Shiyuan affair, lost his commission and rank and was made Right Vice Director and Yangzhou prefect. Cheng Lin was made Palace Commissary director and Yingzhou prefect from Vice Grand Councilor.
1040 CE.Kangding reign, year one, gengchen.In the fifth month on renxu, Lü Yijian was promoted from Zhen'an commissioner and Xuzhou administrator to Right Vice Director, Vice Director of the Secretariat, Grand Councilor Associate, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and Supervisor of National History. Incumbents: Zhang Shisun, Zhang Dexiang, and Lü Yijian.In the fifth month on renxu, Zhang Shisun was dismissed from Vice Director of the Secretariat concurrently Minister of War and Vice Grand Councilor, made Grand Preceptor, and enfeoffed as Duke of Deng.In the third month on wuyin, Yan Shu and Song Shou were both appointed military administrators from Three Departments commissioner and Minister of Justice, and from Minister of Rites and Henan prefect respectively. Wang Yiyong became associate administrator from Bao'an commissioner, Honorary Grand Preceptor, and imperial sons-in-law commissioner. In the seventh month on dinghai, Xia Shouyun was again associate administrator from Southern Palace Attendant commissioner and Shaanxi supreme commander. In the eighth month on wushen, Du Yan became associate administrator from Dragon Diagram academician, Vice Minister of Justice, and acting Kaifeng prefect. In the ninth month on wuwu, Song Shou, recalled from mourning as Left Vice Director, was additionally Hanlin and Dragon Diagram academician, and Chao Zongdan was promoted to Right Remonstrance Counsellor; both became Vice Grand Councilors. Zheng Jian was promoted to Right Remonstrance Counsellor and associate administrator. On wuchen, Yan Shu became Commissioner of Military Affairs with Honorary Grand Preceptor rank; Wang Yiyong, Du Yan, and Zheng Jian were all promoted from associate administrator to Vice Commissioner.In the second month on dinghai, Xia Shouyun left the capital as Southern Palace Attendant commissioner, Shaanxi supreme commander, and concurrent military, pacification, and assault commissioner, from military administrator. In the third month on wuyin, Wang Jun, Chen Zhizhong, and Zhang Guan, after western defeats and deadlock over militia policy, were sent to Henan, Qingzhou, and Xiangzhou prefectures. In the ninth month on wuwu, Li Ruogu, ill, received Zizheng Grand Academician, Vice Minister of Personnel, and Huiling Temple commissioner from Vice Grand Councilor. In the twelfth month on guimao, Vice Grand Councilor Song Shou died.
1041 CE.Qingli reign, year one, xinsi.In the tenth month on renwu, Lü Yijian was promoted from Right Vice Director to Minister of Works; he firmly declined. Incumbents: Zhang Dexiang and Lü Yijian.In the fifth month on xinwei, Wang Juzheng was promoted from Hanlin academician, Vice Minister of War, and drafting official to Right Remonstrance Counsellor and Vice Grand Councilor. Ren Zhongshi and Ren Bu were both appointed Vice Commissioners of Military Affairs.In the fifth month on xinwei, Song Qi was made Yangzhou prefect while retaining Vice Grand Councilor rank. Zheng Jian was made Zizheng academician and Hangzhou prefect from Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs.
1042 CE.The second year of the reign, renwu.In the seventh month on renwu, Lü Yijian was made concurrent military administrator from Right Vice Director, Vice Director of the Secretariat, and Grand Councilor Associate. Zhang Dexiang, retaining associate administrator and Vice Grand Councilor rank, was made concurrent Commissioner of Military Affairs. Yan Shu added Grand Councilor Associate to Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the ninth month on bingwu, Lü Yijian became concurrent Commissioner of Military Affairs. That winter, ill, Yijian was offered Minister of Works and Grand Councilor for Important State Affairs; he declined. Incumbents: Zhang Dexiang, Lü Yijian, and Yan Shu.In the third month on xinyou, Chao Zongdan, ill, was transferred to Zizheng academician and Presentation Draft Attendant and left the Vice Grand Councilor post. In the seventh month on bingwu, Ren Bu was transferred and made Heyang prefect from Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs.
1043 CE.The third year of the reign, guiwei.In the third month on wuzi, ill Lü Yijian was granted Grand Preceptor and Supervisor of National History and a voice in great state affairs from Minister of Works and Grand Councilor for Important State Affairs. Zhang Dexiang was promoted to Vice Minister of Works and Zhaowen Grand Academician; Yan Shu to joint Grand Councilor and Jixian Grand Academician; both became concurrent Commissioners of Military Affairs. In the ninth month on wuchen, Zhang Dexiang also became Supervisor of National History. Incumbents: Zhang Dexiang, Lü Yijian, and Yan Shu.In the fourth month on jiazi, Grand Preceptor Lü Yijian was removed from deliberation on great state affairs. In the ninth month on wuchen, he retired as Grand Preceptor.In the third month on wuzi, Xia Song became Minister of Revenue and Commissioner of Military Affairs from Southern Palace Attendant commissioner at Caizhou. Jia Changchao became Vice Grand Councilor; Fu Bi declined Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the fourth month on jiachen, Han Qi and Fan Zhongyan became Vice Commissioners; on yisi, Du Yan became Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the seventh month on dingchou, Fan Zhongyan and Fu Bi declined Vice Grand Councilor and Vice Commissioner posts; in the eighth month on dingwei, the orders were reissued.In the fourth month on yisi, Commissioner of Military Affairs Xia Song was dismissed after censor-remonstrance criticism once he arrived. In the seventh month on bingzi, Wang Juzheng was made Zizheng academician and Xuzhou prefect from Vice Grand Councilor. In the ninth month on yihai, Ren Zhongshi was made Vice Minister of Rites, Zizheng academician, and Yongxing prefect from Vice Commissioner.
1044 CE.The fourth year of the reign, jiashen.In the ninth month on jiashen, Du Yan was additionally made Grand Councilor Associate concurrently Commissioner of Military Affairs and Jixian Grand Academician from Honorary Grand Preceptor while retaining acting Vice Minister of Personnel rank. Incumbents: Yan Shu, Zhang Dexiang, and Du Yan.In the ninth month on gengwu, Yan Shu, joint Grand Councilor, was criticized by Sun Fu and Cai Xiang and made Vice Minister of Works and Yingzhou prefect.In the sixth month on renzi, Fan Zhongyan was sent out as Shaanxi and Eastern Hedong pacification commissioner from Vice Grand Councilor. In the eighth month on jiawu, Fu Bi was sent to Hebei from Vice Commissioner. In the ninth month on jiayin, Jia Changchao became Honorary Grand Preceptor, acting Vice Minister of Works, and Commissioner of Military Affairs; Chen Zhizhong became Vice Grand Councilor despite remonstrance that he was too stubborn—the emperor ignored it.
1045 CE.The fifth year of the reign, yiyou.In the first month on bingxu, Jia Changchao added Grand Councilor Associate, concurrent Commissioner of Military Affairs, and Jixian Grand Academician to Honorary Grand Preceptor and acting Vice Minister of Works. In the fourth month on wushen, Chen Zhizhong added Grand Councilor Associate and Jixian Grand Academician. Changchao soon also became Zhaowen Grand Academician, Supervisor of National History, and scripture-polishing commissioner. Incumbents: Du Yan, Zhang Dexiang, Jia Changchao, and Chen Zhizhong.In the first month on bingxu, Du Yan was made acting Left Vice Director and Yanzhou prefect from acting Vice Minister of Personnel and Grand Councilor Associate. In the fourth month on wushen, Zhang Dexiang was made Honorary Grand Preceptor, Grand Councilor Associate, Zhen'an commissioner, and Chenzhou prefect. In the tenth month on gengchen, an edict ended chief ministers' concurrent military commissions.In the first month on bingxu, Wang Yiyong became Commissioner of Military Affairs; Song Qi became Vice Grand Councilor; Wu Yu and Pang Ji became Vice Commissioners. In the fourth month on gengxu, Wu Yu became Vice Grand Councilor and Ding Du became Vice Minister of Works and Vice Commissioner.In the first month on yiyou, Fan Chongyan was sent to Binzhou with border pacification duties; Fu Bi to Yanzhou with capital-circuit pacification on Qian Mingyi's recommendation—Han Qi's plea to keep Fu Bi went unanswered. In the third month on xinyou, after Dong Shilian's Shuiluo City suit, Han Qi was sent to Yangzhou.
1046 CE.The sixth year of the reign, bingxu.The incumbents were Jia Changchao and Chen Zhizhong.In the second month on guichou, Wang Yiyong added Grand Councilor Associate. In the eighth month on guiyou, Wu Yu, clashing with Jia Changchao, was demoted to Vice Commissioner; Ding Du became Vice Grand Councilor.
1047 CE.The seventh year of the reign, dinghai.In the third month on yimao, Chen Zhizhong was promoted to Zhaowen Grand Academician, Supervisor of National History, and scripture-polishing commissioner. Incumbents: Jia Changchao and Chen Zhizhong.Earlier, Jia Changchao and Wu Yu had debated before the emperor. In the third month on yimao, Changchao was made Wusheng commissioner at Daming with Northern Capital regency.The emperor first wanted Xia Song, Heyang Three Cities commissioner, Honorary Grand Marshal, Grand Councilor Associate, and Daming prefect, as chancellor, but remonstrance officials cited his feud with Chen Zhizhong. In the third month on yimao, Song became Commissioner of Military Affairs instead. Wen Yanbo became Vice Commissioner then Vice Grand Councilor on dingyou; Gao Ruonu became Vice Commissioner from Right Remonstrance Counsellor and Censor-in-Chief.In the third month on yimao, Wu Yu returned to court ranks as Presentation Draft Attendant from Right Remonstrance Counsellor and Vice Commissioner.
1048 CE.The eighth year of the reign, wuzi.In the intercalary first month on wushen, Wen Yanbo was promoted from Remonstrance Counsellor and Vice Grand Councilor to acting Vice Minister of Rites, Grand Councilor Associate, and Jixian Grand Academician. Incumbents: Chen Zhizhong and Wen Yanbo.In the fourth month on xinwei, Ming Hao became Vice Grand Councilor; in the fifth month on xinyou, Song Qi became Commissioner of Military Affairs and Pang Ji Vice Grand Councilor.In the fourth month on xinwei, Ding Du left the council for Purple Brightness academician and reader. In the fifth month on xinyou, Xia Song was sent to Heyang and Henan with Honorary Grand Preceptor and Grand Councilor Associate rank after He Yan's charge of treachery. In the sixth month on jiawu, Vice Grand Councilor Ming Hao died.
1049 CE.Huangyou reign, year one, jichou.In the eighth month on renxu, Wen Yanbo was promoted to Vice Minister of Personnel, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and Supervisor of National History; Song Qi to Vice Minister of War, Grand Councilor Associate, and Jixian Grand Academician. Incumbents: Chen Zhizhong and Wen Yanbo.In the eighth month on renxu, Chen Zhizhong was made Minister of War and Chenzhou prefect from Vice Minister of Works and Grand Councilor Associate.That year Jia Changchao became Right Vice Director and Observing Culture academician heading the Department of State. In the eighth month on renxu, Wang Yiyong added Palace Attendant; Pang Ji became Commissioner of Military Affairs; Gao Ruonu Vice Grand Councilor; Liang Shi Vice Commissioner.
1050 CE.The second year of the reign, gengyin.The incumbents were Wen Yanbo and Song Qi.
1051 CE.The third year of the reign, xinmao.In the tenth month on gengzi, Pang Ji added Grand Councilor Associate, Zhaowen Grand Academician, Supervisor of National History, and scripture-translation commissioner while keeping Commissioner of Military Affairs, Honorary Grand Preceptor, and Vice Minister of Revenue. Incumbents: Wen Yanbo, Song Qi, and Pang Ji.Earlier, Bao Zheng and Wu Kui charged that Song Qi had accomplished nothing in office. In the third month on gengshen, Qi left as acting Minister of Punishments and Observing Culture academician to serve as Henan prefect. In the eleventh month on gengzi, Wen Yanbo was sent to Xuzhou as acting Minister of Personnel and Observing Culture academician from Vice Minister of Personnel and Grand Councilor Associate.In the third month on gengshen, Liu Kang became Vice Grand Councilor from Dragon Diagram academician, Vice Minister of Works, and acting Kaifeng administrator. In the tenth month on gengzi, Gao Ruonu became Commissioner of Military Affairs with Honorary Grand Preceptor rank. On xinchou, Liang Shi became Vice Grand Councilor; Wang Yaochen became Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs.
1052 CE.The fourth year of the reign, renchen.The incumbent was Pang Ji.Earlier, Wang Yiyong, Commissioner of Military Affairs, sought relief on account of illness. In the fifth month on dingwei he surrendered Palace Attendant rank but kept Grand Councilor Associate and Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the sixth month on dinghai, Di Qing was made Vice Commissioner despite objections from Wang Juzheng and Jia Dan. In the ninth month on gengwu he was sent south as Pacification Commissioner and bandit-suppression intendant; on gengwei the court ruled he would return to Vice Commissioner when the campaign ended.
1053 CE.The fifth year of the reign, gisi.In the seventh month on renshen, Chen Zhizhong was promoted to acting Minister of Personnel, Grand Councilor Associate, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and Supervisor of National History with scripture-polishing duties; Liang Shi added Vice Minister of Rites, Grand Councilor Associate, and Jixian Grand Academician. Incumbents: Pang Ji, Chen Zhizhong, and Liang Shi.After Pang Ji's nephew and a hall clerk were caught taking bribes and Han Jiang censured him, Pang Ji left the capital on renshen in the seventh month as Jingdong-Jingxi pacification commissioner and Yanzhou prefect, keeping Vice Minister of Revenue and Grand Councilor Associate.In the second month on guiwei, Di Qing resumed Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs while serving as Southern Court commissioner. In the fifth month on yisi he became Commissioner of Military Affairs from Vice Commissioner, Huguo commissioner, Honorary Grand Marshal, and Hezhong prefect; on dingwei Sun Hao became Vice Commissioner from Direct Academician, Presentation Draft Attendant, and Hangzhou administrator.In the fifth month on yisi, Gao Ruonu was removed from the Bureau of Military Affairs and made Left Vice Director, Observing Culture academician, and pasturage commissioner, from Vice Minister of Revenue and Commissioner of Military Affairs.
1054 CE.Zhihe reign, year one, jiawu.In the eighth month on bingwu, Liu Kang added Grand Councilor Associate and Jixian Grand Academician while keeping Vice Minister of Works and Vice Grand Councilor. Incumbents: Chen Zhizhong, Liang Shi, and Liu Kang.In the seventh month on dingmao, Sun Bian and fellow censors impeached Liang Shi; on wuchen Shi was removed from council, kept his rank, and was sent to Zhengzhou, soon adding Observing Culture academician and Qinzhou administrator.In the second month on renxu, Tian Kuang became Vice Commissioner from Three Departments commissioner and Vice Minister of Rites. In the third month on jisi, Wang Deyong became Commissioner of Military Affairs and Heyang commissioner while keeping Honorary Grand Preceptor, Grand Councilor Associate, herd commissioner, and Zhengzhou administration. In the seventh month on dingmao, Cheng Zai became Vice Grand Councilor.In the second month on renxu, Sun Hao left Military Affairs for Zizheng academician and Hangzhou prefect. In the third month on jisi, Wang Yiyong became acting Right Vice Director with Palace Attendant, Duke of Deng, Jingling Palace commissioner, and Zhangde military commissioner while keeping Honorary Grand Preceptor and Commissioner of Military Affairs.
1055 CE.The second year of the reign, yiwei.In the sixth month on wuxu, Wen Yanbo was promoted to Minister of Rites, Grand Councilor Associate, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and scripture-polishing commissioner; Fu Bi to Vice Minister of Revenue, Grand Councilor Associate, and Jixian Grand Academician; Liu Kang to Vice Minister of War and Supervisor of National History. Incumbents: Chen Zhizhong, Liu Kang, Wen Yanbo, and Fu Bi.In the sixth month on wuxu, Chen Zhizhong left the capital as Honorary Grand Marshal, Grand Councilor Associate, and Zhenhai commissioner at Bozhou from acting Minister of Personnel and Grand Councilor Associate.
1056 CE.Jiayou reign, year one, bingshen.In the twelfth month on renxu, Wen Yanbo added Supervisor of National History to Minister of Rites and Grand Councilor Associate. Incumbents: Liu Kang, Wen Yanbo, and Fu Bi.After Zhang Fang and fellow censors memorialized seventeen times against Liu Kang, he was sent to Yingtian in the twelfth month on renzi as acting Minister of Works and Observing Culture academician.In the intercalary third month on guiwei, Wang Yaochen became Vice Grand Councilor and Cheng Zai Vice Commissioner, both as Vice Ministers of Revenue. In the eighth month on guihai, Han Qi added Honorary Junior Tutor while keeping acting Minister of Works and Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the eleventh month on xinsi, Jia Changchao became Commissioner of Military Affairs and Xiangzhou observer from his Shannan East and Daming posts. In the twelfth month on renzi, Zeng Gongliang became Vice Grand Councilor.In the eighth month on guihai, Di Qing was dispatched as Grand Councilor Associate and Huguo commissioner at Chenzhou while keeping Honorary Grand Marshal rank. In the eleventh month on xinsi, Wang Deyong likewise became Grand Councilor Associate and Zhongwu commissioner with Jingling Palace duties. In the twelfth month on renxu, Jia Changchao refused concurrent Palace Attendant rank.
1057 CE.The second year of the reign, dingyou.The incumbents were Wen Yanbo and Fu Bi.
1058 CE.The third year of the reign, wuxu.In the sixth month on bingwu, Fu Bi was promoted to Vice Minister of Rites, Zhaowen Grand Academician, Supervisor of National History, and scripture-polishing commissioner; Han Qi added Grand Councilor Associate and Jixian Grand Academician while keeping Commissioner of Military Affairs and Minister of Works. Incumbents: Wen Yanbo, Fu Bi, and Han Qi.In the sixth month on bingwu, Wen Yanbo left the capital as Heyang Three Cities commissioner with Henan and Western Capital regency from Honorary Grand Preceptor and Grand Councilor Associate.In the sixth month on bingwu, Song Qi became Commissioner of Military Affairs while keeping Observing Culture academician, acting Minister of War, Grand Councilor Associate, and pasturage commissioner; Tian Kuang succeeded him as Commissioner from Vice Commissioner; Zhang Fang became Vice Commissioner from Right Remonstrance Counsellor and acting Censor-in-Chief.In the sixth month on bingwu, Jia Changchao left Military Affairs for acting Left Vice Director, Honorary Grand Preceptor with Palace Attendant, Jingling Palace commissioner, and Zhen'an military commissioner. In the eighth month on jiwei, Vice Grand Councilor Wang Yaochen died.
1059 CE.The fourth year of the reign, jihai.The incumbents were Fu Bi and Han Qi.In the fifth month on bingchen, Tian Kuang left the Bureau of Military Affairs for Right Vice Director, Observing Culture academician, Hanlin reader, and superintendent of the Jingling Palace.
1060 CE.The fifth year of the reign, gengzi.The incumbents were Fu Bi and Han Qi.In the fourth month on guiwei, Sun Bian became Vice Commissioner; in the eleventh month on xinchou, Zeng Gongliang did likewise from Vice Grand Councilor. Zhang Fang and Sun Bian were both promoted to Vice Grand Councilor. Ouyang Xiu, Chen Xu, and Zhao Gai were appointed Vice Commissioners; Chen Xu later renamed himself Shengzhi.In the fourth month on guiwei, Cheng Zai was dismissed from Vice Commissioner and his academician and pasturage posts. In the eleventh month on xinchou, Song Qi was dispatched to Heyang and Zhengzhou as military commissioner and Grand Councilor Associate from Honorary Grand Marshal, acting Minister of War, Grand Councilor Associate, herd commissioner, and Commissioner of Military Affairs.
1061 CE.The sixth year of the reign, xinchou.In the sixth month on jiaxu, Fu Bi refused recall to Grand Councilor Associate while still in mourning. In the intercalary eighth month on gengzi, Han Qi added Zhaowen Grand Academician and Supervisor of National History; Zeng Gongliang became Grand Councilor Associate and Jixian Grand Academician from Vice Commissioner with herd commissioner duties. Incumbents: Han Qi and Zeng Gongliang.In the third month on jihai, Vice Minister of Rites and Grand Councilor Associate Fu Bi began mourning for his mother.In the fourth month on gengchen, Bao Zheng became Vice Commissioner from Three Departments commissioner. In the intercalary eighth month on gengzi, Zhang Fang became Commissioner of Military Affairs; Ouyang Xiu became Vice Grand Councilor; Hu Su became Left Remonstrance Counsellor and Vice Commissioner.In the fourth month on gengchen, Chen Xu left Military Affairs for Zizheng academician and Dingzhou administrator.
1062 CE.The seventh year of the reign, renyin.The incumbents were Han Qi and Zeng Gongliang.In the third month on yimao, Zhao Gai became Vice Grand Councilor and Wu Kui Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs.In the third month on yimao, Sun Bian was dismissed from council and his academician and pasturage posts. In the fifth month on gengwu, Vice Commissioner Bao Zheng died.
1063 CE.The eighth year of the reign, guimao; Emperor Yingzong ascended the throne on the new moon of the fourth month, renshen.The incumbents were Han Qi and Zeng Gongliang.In the fifth month on wuwu, once mourning ended, Fu Bi was made Commissioner of Military Affairs with Honorary Grand Preceptor, acting Minister of Rites, and Grand Councilor Associate.
1064 CE.Zhiping reign, year one, jiachen.In the intercalary fifth month on wuchen, Han Qi was promoted to Right Vice Director from Vice Director of the Secretariat with his council, Zhaowen, and history posts and ducal rank. Incumbents: Han Qi and Zeng Gongliang.In the twelfth month on bingwu, Wang Chou became Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs from Hanlin Academician and Vice Minister of Rites.In the twelfth month on wuzi, Vice Commissioner Wu Kui began mourning for his father.
1065 CE.The second year of the reign, yisi.The incumbents were Han Qi and Zeng Gongliang.In the third month on jiwei, Wu Kui refused recall from mourning. In the fifth month on guihai, Chen Xu became Vice Commissioner; in the seventh month on gengchen, Wen Yanbo became Commissioner from Huainan commissioner and ducal rank; on xinsi, Lü Gongbi became Vice Commissioner while keeping his existing posts.In the second month on guimao, Vice Commissioner Wang Chou died. In the seventh month on guihai, Fu Bi was dispatched as Honorary Grand Preceptor, Grand Councilor Associate, and Zhenhai commissioner at Heyang; on gengchen, Zhang Fang left Military Affairs for the same council rank at Zhangxin and Xuzhou, citing illness.
1066 CE.The third year of the reign, bingwu.The incumbents were Han Qi and Zeng Gongliang.In the fourth month on gengxu, Guo Kui was promoted to Honorary Grand Guardian and made co-signing officer of the Bureau of Military Affairs from chief front commandant and Rongzhou observer.In the fourth month on gengxu, Hu Su left for Observing Culture academician, Vice Minister of Personnel, and Hangzhou prefect. In the tenth month on dinghai, Guo Kui was sent to the Shaanxi frontier as pacification commissioner and Weizhou administrator from co-signing officer.
1067 CE.The fourth year of the reign, dingwei; Emperor Shenzong ascended the throne in the first month on dingsi.In the first month on wuchen, Han Qi added Defender Grand Marshal and Palace Attendant to Right Vice Director, Grand Councilor Associate, and ducal rank. In the ninth month on renyin, Zeng Gongliang became Left Vice Director from Jixian Grand Academician and Grand Councilor Associate. Incumbents: Han Qi and Zeng Gongliang.In the ninth month on xinchou, Han Qi left the capital as Defender Grand Preceptor with Palace Attendant, Honorary Grand Preceptor, and Zhen'an-Wusheng commissioner at Xiangzhou from Defender Grand Marshal, Grand Councilor Associate, and Duke of Wei.In the first month on bingyin, Wu Kui returned as Vice Commissioner after mourning; in the third month on guiyou he became Vice Grand Councilor as Vice Minister of Rites. In the ninth month on xinchou, Lü Gongbi became Commissioner; Zhang Fangping and Zhao Bian joined the council; Han Jiang and Shao Kang became Vice Commissioners, with Zhao Bian and Shao Kang promoted to Right Remonstrance Counsellor.In the third month on renshen, Ouyang Xiu was sent to Bozhou as Observing Culture academician and Minister of Punishments. In the ninth month on xinchou, Wu Kui went to Qingzhou and Chen Xu to Yuezhou; on guimao, Guo Kui became Southern Court commissioner at Yanzhou. In the tenth month on jiyou, Zhang Fangping began mourning for his father.
1068 CE.Xining reign, year one, wushen.The incumbent was Zeng Gongliang.In the first month on bingshen, Tang Jie became Vice Grand Councilor; in the seventh month on jimao, Chen Xu became Director of Military Affairs from Observing Culture academician, Left Vice Director, and Yuezhou administrator.In the first month on bingshen, Zhao Gai was dispatched to Xuzhou as Minister of Personnel and Observing Culture academician. In the twelfth month on xinyou, Shao Kang was sent to Yuezhou as Zizheng academician and Presentation Draft Attendant.
1069 CE.The second year of the reign, jiyou.In the second month on jihai, Fu Bi added Vice Director of the Secretariat, Grand Councilor Associate, Zhaowen Grand Academician, and Supervisor of National History while keeping Left Vice Director and Duke of Zheng. In the tenth month on bingshen, Zeng Gongliang gained Zhaowen, history, and scripture-polishing duties and ducal rank; Chen Xu became acting Minister of Rites, Grand Councilor Associate, and Jixian Grand Academician. Incumbents: Fu Bi, Zeng Gongliang, and Chen Xu.In the tenth month on bingshen, Fu Bi was dispatched as Honorary Grand Preceptor and Wuning commissioner at Bozhou while keeping Left Vice Director and Grand Councilor Associate.In the second month on gengzi, Wang Anshi became Vice Grand Councilor from Hanlin Academician, Vice Minister of Works, and lecturer-in-waiting.In the fourth month on dingwei, Vice Grand Councilor Tang Jie died.
1070 CE.The third year of the reign, gengxu.In the twelfth month on dingmao, Han Jiang added Grand Councilor Associate and Zhaowen Grand Academician; Wang Anshi was promoted to Vice Minister of Rites, Grand Councilor Associate, and Supervisor of National History. Incumbents: Zeng Gongliang, Chen Xu, Han Jiang, and Wang Anshi.In the ninth month on gengzi, Zeng Gongliang retired to Heyang as Defender Grand Marshal with Honorary Grand Preceptor, Palace Attendant, and Jixi Abbey commissioner, attending court every five days. In the tenth month on wuyin, Chen Xu began mourning for his mother while acting Minister of Rites and Grand Councilor Associate.In the second month on renshen, Sima Guang was offered Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs but declined. In the fourth month on jimao, Han Jiang added Vice Grand Councilor to Vice Commissioner. In the seventh month on renchen, Feng Jing became Vice Commissioner from acting Censor-in-Chief; in the ninth month on xinchou he joined the council. Wu Chong became Vice Commissioner; in the twelfth month on dingmao, Wang Gui became Vice Grand Councilor while keeping his Hanlin and academician posts.In the fourth month on jimao, Zhao Bian left the council for Zizheng Grand Academician and Hangzhou administrator. In the seventh month on renchen, Lü Gongbi was sent to Taiyuan as Vice Minister of Personnel and Observing Culture Grand Academician. In the ninth month on yiwei, Han Jiang left the Bureau of Military Affairs to become Shaanxi Circuit pacification commissioner.
1071 CE.The fourth year of the reign, xinhai.In the first month on renzi, Chen Xu returned from mourning, kept Minister of Rites, and was offered Grand Councilor Associate but declined. Incumbents: Han Jiang and Wang Anshi.In the third month on dingwei, Han Jiang left the Grand Councilor post to hold Vice Minister of Personnel and administer Dengzhou.
1072 CE.The fifth year of the reign, renzi.The incumbent was Wang Anshi.In the second month on bingyin, Cai Ting became Vice Commissioner from Direct Longtu Academician, Right Remonstrance Counsellor, and Weizhou administrator. In the twelfth month on renwu, Chen Xu became Commissioner from Honorary Grand Mentor, acting Minister of Rites, and Grand Councilor Associate.
1073 CE.The sixth year of the reign, guichou.The incumbent was Wang Anshi.In the fourth month on jihai, Wen Yanbo left the capital as Defender Grand Preceptor with Palace Attendant, Hedong commissioner, and Heyang administrator from Jiannan West commissioner, Defender Grand Marshal, and Military Affairs commissioner.
1074 CE.The seventh year of the reign, jiayin.In the fourth month on bingxu, Han Jiang added Grand Councilor and Supervisor of National History while keeping Observing Culture Grand Academician, acting Vice Minister of Personnel, and Daming prefect. Incumbents: Wang Anshi and Han Jiang.In the fourth month on bingxu, Wang Anshi left the council for Jiangning as Minister of Personnel and Observing Culture Grand Academician from Vice Minister of Rites and Grand Councilor Associate.In the fourth month on bingxu, Lü Huiqing became Vice Grand Councilor from Hanlin Academician and Right Rectifier lecturing-in-waiting. In the twelfth month on dingmao, Wang Shao became Vice Commissioner from his Observing Culture, Duanming, and Longtu posts, Vice Minister of Rites, and Xizhou administrator.
1075 CE.The eighth year of the reign, yimao.In the second month on guiyou, Wang Anshi was recalled to add Grand Councilor Associate and Zhaowen Grand Academician while keeping Observing Culture Grand Academician, Minister of Personnel, and Jiangning administrator. Incumbents: Han Jiang and Wang Anshi.In the eighth month on gengxu, Han Jiang was sent to Xuzhou as Minister of Rites and Observing Culture Grand Academician from Observing Culture Grand Academician, acting Vice Minister of Personnel, and Grand Councilor.In the fourth month on wuyin, Wu Chong became Commissioner as Honorary Grand Mentor and acting Vice Minister of Works from Vice Commissioner and Right Remonstrance Counsellor. In the twelfth month on renyin, Yuan Jiang became Vice Grand Councilor from Hanlin and lecturing academician, Court of Imperial Sacrifices judge, and Vice Minister of Works; Zeng Xiaokuan became Military Affairs direct academician and co-signing officer from Longtu direct academician, diarist, and chief military receivor.In the first month on gengzi, Cai Ting was sent to the Southern Capital Censorate as Zizheng Grand Academician; Feng Jing was dispatched to Bozhou while keeping his council rank. In the intercalary fourth month on yiwei, Chen Xu went to Yangzhou as Honorary Grand Commandant, Zhenjiang commissioner, and Grand Councilor Associate. In the tenth month on gengyin, Lü Huiqing left the council for Chenzhou while holding his former rank.
1076 CE.The ninth year of the reign, bingchen.In the tenth month on bingwu, Wu Chong added Grand Councilor Associate and Supervisor of National History while keeping Commissioner, Honorary Grand Mentor, and acting Vice Minister of Works; Wang Gui added Grand Councilor Associate and Jixian Grand Academician while keeping Vice Minister of Rites and Vice Grand Councilor. Incumbents: Wang Anshi, Wu Chong, and Wang Gui.In the tenth month on bingwu, Wang Anshi took the Zhennan commission and Jiangning administration while keeping Honorary Grand Mentor and Left Vice Director.In the tenth month on bingwu, Feng Jing became Director of Military Affairs from Zizheng Grand Academician, Right Remonstrance Counsellor, and Chengdu administrator.
1077 CE.The tenth year of the reign, dingsi.The incumbents were Wu Chong and Wang Gui.In the second month on jihai, Wang Shao was sent to Hongzhou as Vice Minister of Revenue and Observing Culture Grand Academician from Vice Commissioner.
1078 CE.Yuanfeng reign, year one, wuwu.The incumbents were Wu Chong and Wang Gui.In the intercalary first month on renchen, Sun Gu became co-director of Military Affairs from acting Kaifeng administrator, Military Affairs direct academician, and Right Remonstrance Counsellor. In the ninth month on yiyou, Lü Gongzhu and Xue Xiang both became co-directors from their Duanming, Hanlin, and Baowen posts and from Military Affairs direct academician and Vice Minister of Works.In the intercalary first month on jihai, co-signing officer Zeng Xiaokuan began mourning for his father; in the fifth month on gengchen he was recalled but declined.
1079 CE.The second year of the reign, jiwei.The incumbents were Wu Chong and Wang Gui.In the fifth month on wuzi, Cai Que became Vice Grand Councilor from Right Remonstrance Counsellor, acting Censor-in-Chief, Direct Academy academician, and Ministry of Agriculture administrator.In the fifth month on jiashen, Yuan Jiang left the council for Bozhou as Vice Minister of Works.
1080 CE.The third year of the reign, gengshen.In the ninth month on bingxu, Wang Gui added Supervisor of National History to Grand Councilor. Incumbents: Wu Chong and Wang Gui.In the third month on jichou, Wu Chong resigned citing illness and was relieved as Minister of Personnel, Observing Culture Grand Academician, and West Grand Unity Abbey commissioner.In the second month on bingwu, Zhang Dun became Vice Grand Councilor from Hanlin Academician, Right Rectifier, and Eastern Review of Appointments administrator. In the ninth month on guimao, Xue Xiang and Sun Gu were promoted to Proper Policy Grandee and Grandee of the Palace and both made Vice Commissioners; on bingxu Feng Jing became Commissioner from Proper Policy Grandee and Director; on dinghai Lü Gongzhu became Vice Commissioner from Vice Minister of Revenue and co-director.
1081 CE.The fourth year of the reign, xinyou.The incumbent was Wang Gui.In the first month on xinhai, Sun Gu became Director of Military Affairs and co herd commissioner from Vice Commissioner and Grandee of the Palace; Lü Gongzhu added Direct Longtu Academician and, with Han Zong from chief military receivor and herd commissioner, both became co-directors of Military Affairs. In the third month on jiachen, Zhang Song became Vice Grand Councilor from Hanlin Academician.In the first month on xinhai, Feng Jing was sent to Heyang as Glorious Grandee and Observing Culture Grand Academician from Proper Policy Grandee, co herd commissioner, and Commissioner. In the third month on guimao, Zhang Dun was demoted to Caizhou as Grandee of the Palace after his father Yu's land seizure and Zhu Fu's impeachment over a false report to the throne.
1082 CE.The fifth year of the reign, renxu.That year the bureaucracy was reorganized: Left and Right Vice Directors became the chief ministers. In the fourth month on guiyou, Wang Gui added Left Vice Director and Vice Director of the Chancellery while keeping Glorious Grandee and Grand Councilor; Cai Que added Right Vice Director and Vice Director of the Secretariat while keeping Grandee of the Palace and Vice Grand Councilor. Incumbents: Wang Gui and Cai Que.In the fourth month on jiaxu, Zhang Dun added Defender Vice Director of the Chancellery while administering Dingzhou; Zhang Song added Defender Vice Director of the Secretariat; Pu Zongmeng and Wang Anli, both Hanlin academicians, were promoted to Palace Grandee as Defender Left and Right Vice Directors.In the fourth month on dingchou, Lü Gongzhu was sent to Dingzhou as Glorious Grandee and Zizheng Grand Academician from Policy Grandee and co-director of Military Affairs.
1083 CE.The sixth year of the reign, guihai.The incumbents were Wang Gui and Cai Que.In the seventh month on bingchen, Han Zong became Director of Military Affairs from Grandee of the Palace and co-director; An Yan became co-director from Grandee of the Palace and provisional Vice Minister of Revenue. In the eighth month on xinmao, Wang Anli became Left Vice Director from Defender Right Vice Director; Li Qingchen became Palace Grandee and Right Vice Director from Minister of Personnel.In the seventh month on bingchen, Sun Gu was sent to Heyang as Proper Policy Grandee and Observing Culture Grand Academician from Director of Military Affairs. In the eighth month on xinmao, Pu Zongmeng left the Left Vice Director post for Ruzhou from Palace Grandee and Defender Left Vice Director.
1084 CE.The seventh year of the reign, jiazi.The incumbents were Wang Gui and Cai Que.In the seventh month on jiayin, Wang Anli left the Left Vice Director post for Jiangning as Duanming Academician.
1085 CE.The eighth year of the reign, yichou; Emperor Zhezong ascended the throne in the third month on wuxu.In the fifth month on wuwu, Cai Que added Vice Director of the Chancellery and Left Vice Director while keeping Right Vice Director and Vice Director of the Secretariat; Han Zong added Vice Director of the Secretariat and Right Vice Director from Director of Military Affairs. Incumbents: Wang Gui, Cai Que, and Han Zong.In the fifth month on gengxu, Left Vice Director and Vice Director of the Chancellery Wang Gui died.In the fifth month on wuwu, Zhang Dun became Director of Military Affairs from Proper Policy Grandee and Vice Director of the Chancellery; Sima Guang added Defender Vice Director of the Chancellery from Zizheng Grand Academician, Proper Policy Grandee, and Chenzhou administrator. In the seventh month on wuxu, Lü Gongzhu added Left Vice Director from Zizheng Grand Academician, Glorious Grandee, and lecturing academician.
