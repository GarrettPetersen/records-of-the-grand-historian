西南溪峒諸蠻下梅山峒誠徽州南丹州
Southwestern Stream-cave Tribes (continued): Meishan Dong, Chenghui Prefecture, and Nandan Prefecture.
2
西南溪峒諸蠻下
Southwestern Stream-cave Tribes (continued)
3
紹興三年，臣僚言：“武岡軍溪峒舊嚐集人戶為義保，蓋其風土、習俗、服食、器械悉同徭人。 故可為疆場捍蔽，雖曰籍之於官，然亦未嚐遠戍。 靖康間，調之以勤王，其後湖南盜起，征斂百出，義保無復舊制，困苦不勝，乃舉其世業，客依蠻峒，聽其繇役。 州縣猶驗舊籍催科，胥隸及門，則挈家遠徙，官失其稅，蠻獠日強。 兼武岡所屬三縣，悉為徭人所有，遠戍之實已無，而鄉戶弩手之名尚在，歲取其直，人戶谘怨。 乞擇本路監司詳議以聞。 ”詔從之。
In the third year of Shaoxing (1133), a memorialist reported: "The stream-cave communities of Wugang Prefecture once enrolled households as Righteous Guards—their environment, customs, food, clothing, and arms were indistinguishable from the Yao." They could thus shield the borderlands; though registered with the state, they were never posted to distant garrisons. During the Jingkang crisis they were called up to rescue the dynasty; later, when Hunan was overrun by bandits and exactions multiplied, the Righteous Guards lost their old organization. Overwhelmed by hardship, they gave up their ancestral estates, took refuge among tribal strongholds, and accepted corvée under tribal masters. Prefectures and counties still used old registers to dun them for taxes; when runners appeared at the door, families fled wholesale. The state lost revenue while the tribes grew stronger by the day. All three counties under Wugang had passed into Yao hands; distant garrison duty existed in name only, yet village crossbowmen were still listed and their annual pay still collected—households complained bitterly. He asked that circuit supervisors be directed to study the matter in detail and report. The throne approved.
4
四年，辰州言，歸明保靜、南渭、永順三州彭儒武等久欲奉表入貢。 詔以道路未通，俾荊湖北帥司慰諭，免赴闕。 遣人持表及方物赴行在，仍優賜以答之。 九月，詔荊湖南、北路溪峒頭首土人及主管年滿人合給恩賜，俾各路帥司會計覆實以聞。
In the fourth year (1134), Chen Prefecture reported that Peng Ruwu and other chiefs of the pacified prefectures Baojing, Nanwei, and Yongshun had long wished to submit memorials and tribute. Because routes were still unsafe, the Jinghu North commander was ordered to reassure them and exempt them from traveling to court. They might send envoys with memorials and local products to the mobile court, and would receive generous gifts in return. In the ninth month, headmen and native supervisors in Jinghu South and North whose terms had expired were ordered imperial rewards; route commanders were to verify accounts and report.
5
六年，知鼎州張觷言：“鼎、澧、辰、沅、靖州與溪峒接壤，祖宗時嚐置弓弩手，得其死力，比緣多故，遂皆廢闕。 萬一蠻夷生變，將誰與捍禦？ 今雖各出良田，募人以補其額，率皆豪強遣僮奴竄名籍中，乘時射利，無益公家，所宜汰去。 則募溪峒司兵得三百人，俾加習練，足為守禦，給田募人開墾，以供軍儲。 ”詔荊湖北路帥司相度以聞。 帥司言：“營田四州舊置弓弩手九千一百一十人，練習武事，散居邊境，鎮撫蠻夷，平居則事耕作，緩急以備戰守，深為利便。 靖康初，調發應援河東，全軍陷沒。 今辰、沅、澧、靖等州乏兵防守，竊慮蠻夷生變叵測。 若將四州弓弩手減元額，定為三千五百人，辰州置千人，沅州置千五百人，澧州、靖州各置五百人，分處要害，量給土田，訓練以時，耕戰合度，庶可備禦。 以所餘閑田募人耕作，歲收其租，其於邊防財賦，兩得其便，可為經久之計。 ”詔從之。
In the sixth year (1136), Zhang Jue, prefect of Ding, memorialized: "Ding, Li, Chen, Yuan, and Jing border the stream-caves. Former emperors posted crossbowmen who served with utter loyalty, but successive crises have abolished the system." If the tribes rebel, who will defend the frontier? Good land is offered to fill quotas, but powerful families usually plant bondsmen in the rolls to profit at opportune moments—this helps the state not at all and should be purged. Recruit three hundred stream-cave militiamen, drill them for defense, and grant land to settlers to open fields for military stores. The Jinghu North commander was ordered to study the proposal and report. The commander replied: "The four garrison-field prefectures once fielded 9,110 crossbowmen trained for war, scattered along the border to pacify tribes. They farmed in peace and fought in crisis—a deeply effective arrangement." At the start of the Jingkang crisis they were sent to reinforce Hedong and the entire force was destroyed. Chen, Yuan, Li, and Jing now lack troops for defense; I fear an unpredictable tribal revolt. Reduce the four-prefecture crossbowmen from the old quota to 3,500—1,000 at Chen, 1,500 at Yuan, 500 each at Li and Jing—posted at key points with measured land grants, timely training, and balanced farming and fighting, and defense should hold. Recruit farmers for remaining idle land, collect rent annually, and both frontier defense and revenue will benefit—a lasting plan. The throne approved.
6
七年六月，張觷言：“湖外自靖康以來，盜賊盤踞，鍾相、楊太山、雷德進等相繼叛，澧州所屬尤甚。 獨慈利縣向思勝等五人素號溪峒歸明，誓掌防拓，卒能保境息民，使德進賊黨無所剽掠，思勝後竟殺德進。 會官軍招撫劉智等，而彭永健、彭永政、彭永全、彭永勝及思勝共獻糧助官軍，招復諸山四十餘柵，宣力效忠功居多，宜加恩賞。 ”詔思勝等五人各轉兩資。 九月，詔荊湖、廣南路溪峒頭首土人內有子孫應襲職名差遣，及主管年滿合給恩賜之數，俾帥司取會核實以聞。
In the sixth month of the seventh year (1137), Zhang Jue reported: "Since Jingkang, bandits have held the lake country—Zhong Xiang, Yang Taishan, Lei Dejin, and others rebelled in turn, especially in Li Prefecture." Only Xiang Sisheng and four others of Cili, long known as pacified stream-cave leaders, vowed to guard the frontier. They kept the district secure, denied Lei Dejin's band any loot, and Sisheng eventually killed Dejin. When government troops pacified Liu Zhi and others, Peng Yongjian, Peng Yongzheng, Peng Yongquan, Peng Yongsheng, and Sisheng supplied grain, recovered more than forty mountain stockades, and served loyally—they deserved enhanced rewards. Sisheng and the other four were each advanced two salary grades. In the ninth month, Jinghu and Guangnan commanders were ordered to verify headmen and native supervisors due for hereditary succession or end-of-term rewards and report.
7
九年，宜章峒民駱科作亂，寇郴、道、連、桂陽諸州縣，詔發大兵往討之，獲駱科。 餘黨歐幼四等復叛，據藍山，寇平陽縣，遣江西兵馬都監程師回討平之。
In the ninth year (1139), Luo Ke of the Yizhang caves rebelled and raided Chen, Dao, Lian, and Guiyang. A large force was dispatched; Luo Ke was captured. Remnants led by Ou Yousi rebelled again, seized Lanshan, and raided Pingyang. Jiangxi military supervisor Cheng Shihui was sent and pacified them.
8
十年，承信郎琴州溪峒楊進顒等率族屬歸生界五百餘戶、疆土三百餘里，獻累世所造兵器及金爐、酒杯各一，求入覲，詔本路帥司敦遣以行。 十二年，詔以施州南砦路夷人向再健襲父思遷充銀青光祿大夫、檢校國子祭酒兼監察御史、武騎尉、知懿州事。
In the tenth year (1140), Yang Jinyang of the Qin Prefecture stream-caves led his clan to submit more than 500 households and 300 li of territory, presented heirloom weapons plus a gold censer and wine cup, and sought audience. The route commander was ordered to escort them promptly. In the twelfth year (1142), Xiang Zaijian of the Shi Prefecture Nanzhai Yi succeeded his father Sixian as Silver-Gleam Grand Master, acting Director of the Imperial Academy and Investigating Censor, Martial Cavalry Commandant, and prefect of Yi.
9
十四年十月，湖南安撫使劉昉奏，武岡軍徭人有父子相殺者，宜出兵助其父，俾還省地。 上以問輔臣秦檜，檜曰：“恐輕舉生事。 ”帝曰：“恩威不可偏廢，可懷則示之以恩，否則威之。 不侵省地則已，或有所侵，奈何不舉，俾知所畏哉。 ”十二月，成忠郎充武岡軍綏寧縣管界都巡檢兼溪峒首領楊進京，率其族三百人，備黃金、朱砂、方物求入貢，先遣其子孝友陳請。 詔本路帥司閱舊制以聞，給孝友錢三百貫，俾還聽進止。
In the tenth month of the fourteenth year (1144), Hunan pacification commissioner Liu Fang reported a father-son killing among Wugang Yao and urged troops to aid the father and recover provincial land. The emperor asked Grand Councilor Qin Hui, who said, "A rash move might stir trouble." The emperor replied, "Favor and awe must both be used—win them with kindness where possible, with force where not." "If they keep off provincial land, fine—but if they encroach, how can we stand idle? They must learn fear." In the twelfth month, Yang Jinjing—Loyal and Faithful Gentleman, Suining frontier inspector and stream-cave headman—led three hundred clansmen with gold, cinnabar, and tribute goods, sending his son Xiaoyou ahead to petition. The route commander was told to review old regulations and report; Xiaoyou received three hundred strings of cash and was sent home pending further orders.
10
十五年，楊進顒復求入貢，以武岡軍不時敦遣為言。 詔本路帥司閱實應襲人姓名來上，並促進顒入覲。 四月，廣南東路提刑黃應南言：“溪峒巡檢、尉、砦官不嚴守備，縱民與徭交通，恐啟邊釁，乞詔有司申嚴法令，俾帥臣、監司常加覺察。 ”宰臣以為沿邊互市，恐不宜禁絕。 帝曰：“往年禁西夏互市，遂至用兵，可令帥司裁決。 ”前知全州高楫言：“徭人今皆微弱，不敢先侵省地，砦官每縱人深入，略其財物，遂致乘間竊發。 宜詔與溪峒接壤州郡毋侵徭人，庶使邊民安業，以廣陛下柔遠好生之德。 ”帝從其言，詔守臣一遵成法，務在撫綏。
In the fifteenth year (1145), Yang Jinyang again sought tribute, complaining that Wugang had not escorted him promptly. The route commander was ordered to verify heirs due for succession and urge Jinyang to court. In the fourth month, Guangnan East judicial intendant Huang Yingnan warned that stream-cave inspectors and fort officers neglected defense and let civilians trade with Yao, risking border conflict. He asked for stricter laws and constant supervision by commanders. Councilors argued that frontier trade should not be entirely banned. The emperor said, "Banning trade with Western Xia once led to war—let commanders decide." Former Quan prefect Gao Ji said, "The Yao are now weak and dare not strike first—but fort officers let men raid deep into their lands, plunder them, and provoke opportunistic outbreaks." Prefectures bordering stream-caves should be forbidden to harass the Yao, so frontier people may live in peace and extend Your Majesty's virtue of cherishing the distant. The emperor agreed and ordered local officials to follow established law and prioritize pacification.
11
二十四年，禽楊正修及其弟正拱，送理寺獄鞫治，斬之。 初，正修侍其父再興入覲，獻還省民疆土，遂命以官。 建炎後，與弟正拱率九十團峒徭人出武岡軍，縱火殺掠民財為亂。 紹興間，潭州帥司嚐招徠之，後復作亂，屢抗官軍，至是伏誅。 二十八年七月，楊進京等復求入貢，詔以道遠慰諭之，優其賜與。
In the twenty-fourth year (1154), Yang Zhengxiu and his brother Zhenggong were captured, tried at the Court of Judicial Review, and executed. Earlier Zhengxiu had accompanied his father Zaixing to court, returned seized provincial land, and received an official post. After Jianyan he and Zhenggong led ninety regiment-caves of Yao out of Wugang, burning, killing, and looting in rebellion. During Shaoxing the Tan commander once won them over, but they rebelled again, repeatedly fought government troops, and were finally executed. In the seventh month of the twenty-eighth year (1158), Yang Jinjing again sought tribute; the court consoled them on the long journey and gave generous gifts.
12
隆興初，右正言尹穡言：“湖南州縣多鄰溪峒，省民往往交通徭人，擅自易田，豪猾大姓或詐匿其產徭人，以避科差。 內虧國賦，外滋邊患。 宜詔湖南安撫司表正經界，禁民毋質田徭人。 詐匿其產徭人者論如法，仍沒入其田，以賞告奸者。 田前賣入徭人，俾為別籍，毋遽奪，能還其田者，縣代給錢嚐之。 ”帝從其言。
Early in Longxing (1163), Right Remonstrator Yin Ji reported that Hunan counties bordering stream-caves saw civilians trading land with Yao and powerful families falsely registering property under Yao names to evade taxes. Revenue was lost within while border troubles grew without. The Hunan pacification office should fix boundaries and forbid mortgaging land to Yao. False registration under Yao names should be punished, fields confiscated, and informers rewarded. Land already sold to Yao should be separately registered without sudden seizure; counties should compensate those who recover their fields. The emperor approved.
13
乾道元年，宜章峒賊李金陷郴州，焚桂陽軍，州將棄城遁，衡州調常寧縣兵救之，弗克。 世忠峒李昂霄者，率壯丁禦賊，民恃以安。 湖南提舉常平鄭丙請發鄂渚軍討賊，平之。 昂霄以功補承節郎，管轄衡州常寧縣溪峒，及官其子當年，俾後得襲職。
In the first year of Qiandao (1165), the Yizhang bandit Li Jin seized Chen Prefecture and burned Guiyang Commandery. The prefectural commander fled; Heng's Changning troops failed to relieve the city. Li Angxiao of the Shizhong cave led stalwarts against the bandits, and the people found safety in him. Hunan Ever-Normal intendant Zheng Bing requested Ezhou troops, who pacified the rebels. Angxiao was made Bearer of Merit and placed over Heng's Changning stream-caves; his son Dangnian received office with right of succession.
14
三年，靖州界徭人姚明教等作亂，詔荊、鄂駐紮明椿選將率精銳千人，會屯戍官合擊之，能立功者有厚賞。 八月，詔平溪峒互市鹽米價，聽民便，毋相抑配，其徭人歲輸身丁米，務平收，無取羨餘及折輸錢，違者論罪。 十一月，南郊禮成，詔以緣邊溪峒，州縣失於拊循，致懷反側，或逃竄山谷，其在赦恩以前，並加寬宥，能復業者，罪一切置不問，互市如故，悉聽其便，守臣常加撫問。 以稱綏遠之意。
In the third year (1167), Yao Mingjiao and others on the Jing border rebelled. Ming Chun of the Jing-E garrison was ordered to lead a thousand elites with local garrison officers to attack them; achievers would be richly rewarded. In the eighth month, stream-cave market prices for salt and grain were ordered fair, trade free of forced allocation, and Yao head-tax rice collected levelly without surcharges—violators punished. After the southern suburban rites in the eleventh month, frontier stream-cave peoples neglected by local officials who had fled or wavered were pardoned for all pre-amnesty offenses if they returned to livelihood; markets continued freely under constant official care. This expressed the policy of pacifying the frontier.
15
四年二月，詔湖南北、四川、二廣州軍應有溪峒處，務先恩信綏懷，毋弛防閑，毋襲科擾，毋貪功而啟釁。 委各路帥臣、監司常加覺察。 是月，詔禁沿邊奸人毋越逸溪峒，誘致蠻獠侵內地，違者論如律，其不能防閑致越逸者亦罪之。 湖廣總領周嗣武言邊事，如二年四月之詔，帝嘉納之。 是歲，田彥古死，子忠佐襲職，授銀青光祿大夫、檢校散騎常侍、知溪峒安化州兼監察御史、飛龍騎尉。
In the second month of the fourth year (1168), all stream-cave regions in Hunan, Sichuan, and Guang were told to prioritize trust and pacification, maintain defense, avoid oppressive levies, and not provoke conflict for merit. Route commanders and supervisors were to monitor constantly. That month, frontier scoundrels were forbidden to cross into stream-caves and incite tribal raids—violators punished, and officials who failed to prevent crossings also guilty. Huguang chief steward Zhou Siwu spoke on border affairs echoing the second-year fourth-month edict; the emperor praised and accepted it. That year Tian Yanggu died; his son Zhongzuo succeeded as Silver-Gleam Grand Master, acting Master of Casual Cavaliers, prefect of stream-cave Anhua and Investigating Censor, and Soaring Dragon Cavalry Commandant.
16
六年，盧陽西據獠楊添朝寇邊，知沅州孫叔傑調兵數千討之，敗績，死者十七八。 初，徭人與省戶交爭，殺二人死，叔傑輒出兵破其十三柵，奪還所侵地，於是徭人相結為亂。 諸司請調常德府城兵三百人，益官兵三千人，合擊討之。 宰臣虞允文奏曰：“蠻夷為變，皆守臣貪功所致。 今徭人仇視守臣，若更去叔傑，量遣官軍，示以兵威，徐與盟誓，自可平定。 ”帝允其奏，俾葉行代叔傑，開示恩信，諭以禍福，遂招降之，邊境悉平。 前知武岡軍趙善穀言：“武岡與湖北、廣西鄰壤，為極邊之地，溪峒七百八十餘所，七峒隸綏寧縣，五溪峒隸臨岡縣。 紹興三十年，減冗員，改縣為臨口砦。 然五峒之徭俗尤獷悍，釁生毫髮，則操戈相仇，砦官不能為輕重。 況本軍巡防砦柵，惟真良、三門、兵溪、香平有土軍可備守禦，餘有官無兵，其關硤、武陽等砦設巡檢二員，徒費廩祿。 以臣所知，宜復臨口砦為縣，則徭蠻易於製服，汰去冗員，則官廩亦無虛費，實邊郡之利也。 ”
In the sixth year (1170), the Luyang tribal Yang Tianchao raided the border. Yuan prefect Sun Shujie led several thousand troops, was routed, and lost seven or eight in ten. Earlier Yao and provincial households had fought, killing two. Shujie promptly attacked thirteen stockades and recovered land—whereupon the Yao united in revolt. Offices requested three hundred Changde troops plus three thousand regulars for joint suppression. Councilor Yu Yunwen said, "Tribal revolts stem from officials seeking merit." "The Yao now hate the local commander. Remove Shujie, send a measured force to show strength, then slowly make oaths—order can be restored." The emperor agreed. Ye Xing replaced Shujie, showed favor and explained consequences, won their surrender, and pacified the border. Former Wugang prefect Zhao Shangu said, "Wugang borders Hubei and Guangxi at the extreme frontier—more than 780 stream-caves, seven under Suining and five under Lingang." In the thirtieth year of Shaoxing (1160) redundant posts were cut and the county became Linkou Fort. Yet the five-cave Yao were especially fierce—at the slightest provocation they took up arms against one another, and fort officers had no leverage. Of Wugang's patrol forts, only Zhenliang, Sanmen, Bingxi, and Xiangping had local troops; others had officers but no men. Guanxia and Wuyang each had two inspectors wasting salaries. Restore Linkou Fort as a county to control Yao and tribes more easily; cut redundant posts to end wasted salaries—a real frontier benefit. "
17
七年，前知辰州章才邵上言：“辰之諸蠻與羈縻保靜、南渭、永順三州接壤，其蠻酋歲貢溪布，利於回賜，頗覺馴伏。 盧溪諸蠻以靖康多故，縣無守禦，犵狑乘隙焚劫。 後徙縣治於沅陵縣之江口，蠻酋田仕羅、龔誌能等遂雄據其地。 沅陵之浦口，地平衍膏腴，多水田，頃為徭蠻侵掠，民皆轉徙而田野荒穢。 會守倅無遠慮，乃以其田給靖州犵狑楊姓者，俾佃作而課其租，所獲甚微。 楊氏專其地將二十年，其地當沅、靖二州水陸之衝，一有蠻隙，則為害不細，臣謂宜預為之備。 靖康前，辰州每歲蒙朝廷賜錢七萬貫，、絹、布共八千一百匹，綿一萬七千兩。 是時，本州廂禁軍一千四百餘人，沿邊一十六砦，土兵六百餘人，皆可贍給。 其後中外多故，今歲賜止得一萬二千緡，而本州財復匱乏，無以充召募之費。 禁軍止二百一十餘人，諸砦土兵止一百五人，甚至砦官有全無一兵而徒存虛名者，其於邊防豈可不為深慮？ 若歲增給民錢一萬，俾本州募強壯禁軍或效用二百人，分屯盧溪等處，以防諸蠻，庶使邊患永消，可免異時調遣之費。 ”書奏，詔湖北帥臣詳議以聞。 是年，申嚴邊民售田之禁，守令不能奉法者除名，部刺史常加糾察。
In the seventh year (1171), former Chen prefect Zhang Caishao reported that Chen tribes bordering pacified Baojing, Nanwei, and Yongshun submitted annual stream-cloth tribute, profited from return gifts, and were fairly tractable. Luxi tribes, undefended after Jingkang troubles, were raided by Gelao who burned and plundered. When the county seat moved to Yuanling's river mouth, chiefs Tian Shiluo and Gong Zhineng dominated the old territory. Yuanling's Pukou was level, fertile paddy land—recently raided by Yao and tribes until people fled and fields lay waste. Short-sighted officials gave the fields to Jing Gelao of the Yang clan to farm for rent, gaining very little. The Yang clan has held the land nearly twenty years at the Yuan-Jing land-and-water junction—any tribal incident would do great harm; preparations are needed. Before Jingkang, Chen received seventy thousand strings of cash, 8,100 bolts of silk and cloth, and seventeen thousand taels of cotton annually. Then the prefecture's 1,400 metropolitan troops, sixteen frontier forts, and 600 local troops were all adequately supplied. After successive crises, this year's grant is only twelve thousand strings while prefectural funds are depleted—nothing remains for recruitment. Metropolitan troops number barely 210, fort local troops only 105—some forts have officers but no soldiers. How can frontier defense not alarm us? Adding ten thousand strings yearly would let Chen recruit two hundred stalwart troops posted at Luxi and elsewhere to guard tribes, end border troubles, and avoid future mobilization costs. The Hubei commander was ordered to study the memorial and report. That year the ban on frontier land sales to tribes was strictly enforced—noncompliant officials removed, circuit commissioners to investigate.
18
八年，知貴州陳乂上疏言：“臣前知靖州時，居蠻夷腹心，民不服役，田不輸賦，其地似若可棄。 然為重湖、二廣保障，實南服之要區也。 或控製失宜，或金穀不繼，或兵甲少振，蠻獠則乘時竊發，勤勞王師，朝廷當重守臣之選。 崇寧初戍兵三千人，建炎以來，每於都統司或帥司摘兵二千人，以備屯戍。 其凶悍者，以州郡不能製，遂慢守臣，反通徭蠻以撓編民。 州郡非白主帥不敢治，比得報，已晚矣。 故戍兵敢肆其惡，一旦有警，復安能為用？ 臣以為宜聽守臣節製為便。 ”帝嘉其言，復問左右曰：“靖隸湖北，今聞仰給廣西，何也？ ”趙雄對曰：“靖州本溪峒，神宗時創為誠州，元祐間廢，尋復為軍，徽宗朝始改靖州，與桂府為鄰，故令廣西給其金穀之費。 近歲漕司匱乏，乃責辦諸州，以故不能如約。 宜復舊制，俾廣西漕臣如期饋運。 靖州屯戍官兵聽守臣節製，於事為便。 ”帝從之。
In the eighth year (1172), Gui prefect Chen Yi wrote: "When I governed Jing, I sat in the tribal heartland where people paid no corvée or tax—the place seemed abandonable." Yet it is the bulwark of the lake country and the two Guang circuits—a vital southern region. If control fails, funds run short, or arms weaken, tribes strike opportunistically and burden the royal army—the court must choose defending officials carefully. Chongning began with three thousand garrison troops; since Jianyan, two thousand are drawn yearly from overall or route commands for frontier duty. The fiercer troops, finding that prefectures and counties could not restrain them, grew contemptuous of local officials and colluded with Yao tribes to harass registered civilians. Prefectures and counties would not act without reporting to the overall commander first—by the time approval came, it was too late. Thus garrison troops dared their outrages; when crisis struck, how could they be relied upon? I believe it would be best to place them under the defending officials' command. The Emperor approved and asked his attendants: "Jing falls under Hubei circuit, yet I hear it now relies on Guangxi for supplies—why?" Zhao Xiong answered: "Jing was originally stream-cave country. Shenzong created Chen Prefecture there; it was abolished under Yuanyou, then restored as a military district and renamed Jing under Huizong. Because it neighbors Guangxi, that circuit was assigned to fund it." In recent years the transport commission ran short and pushed costs onto individual prefectures, so supplies no longer arrived on schedule. The old arrangement should be restored so Guangxi transport officials deliver on time. Jing's garrison troops should answer to the defending officials—that would serve best. The throne approved.
19
十年四月，全州上言：“本州密邇溪峒，邊民本非奸惡。 其始，朝廷禁法非不嚴密，監司、州郡非不奉行，特以平居失於防閑，故馴致其亂。 又兼溪穀山徑非止一途，如靜江、興安之大通虛，武岡軍之新寧、盆溪及八十里山，永州之東安，皆可以徑達溪峒。 其地綿亙郡邑，非一州得專約束，故遊民惡少之棄本者，商旅之避征稅者，盜賊之亡命者，往往由之以入。 萃為淵藪，交相鼓扇，深為邊患。 如武岡楊再興、桂陽陳峒相繼為亂，實原於此。 為今計者，宜徙閑地巡檢兵，及分遣士卒屯諸溪穀山徑間，俾湖南北、廣西帥憲總其役，庶幾事權有歸，號令可行也。 ”儒林郎李大性上言：“比年徭蠻為亂，邊吏慮妨賞格，往往匿不以聞，遂致猖獗，使一方民命寄於徭人之手，誠可哀憫。 近如梁牟等寇沅州，劫墟市，殺戮齊民，州縣告急於兩月之後，比調官軍討捕，俘降其賊，而人之被害已酷矣。 宜戒州縣或遇徭人竊發，畫時以聞，違者論罪。 仍命監司、帥臣常加覺察，庶幾先事備禦，俾徭人亦知畏懼，不敢侵軼，以傷吾民也。 ”
In the fourth month of the tenth year, Quan Prefecture reported: "We lie close to the stream-caves; our frontier people are not inherently wicked." At first the court's bans were strict and circuit and prefectural officials enforced them—but routine vigilance lapsed, and disorder followed by degrees. Moreover, mountain paths through the stream valleys are many: Datongxu in Jingjiang and Xing'an; Xinning, Penxi, and Eighty-li Mountain in Wugang; Dong'an in Yong—all lead straight into the stream-caves. The region spans many prefectures, so no single one can control it. Vagabonds who abandon honest work, merchants evading taxes, and fugitive bandits slip in by these routes. They gather into a deep lair, rousing one another—a grave border menace. The successive rebellions of Yang Zaixing at Wugang and Chen Dong at Guiyang originated here. The remedy is to redeploy idle patrol troops and station soldiers at the mountain passes, coordinated by the route and circuit officials of Hunan, northern Hunan, and Guangxi, so authority is unified and orders can be enforced. Ruilin Lang Li Daxing wrote: "In recent years Yao tribes have rebelled, yet frontier officials, fearing lost credit for rewards, often suppress reports—allowing violence to run wild until an entire region's people hang by a Yao thread. It is pitiable." Take the recent case of Liang Mou raiding Yuan Prefecture, looting markets, and killing civilians: prefectures and counties reported the emergency only two months later. By the time troops were mobilized and the bandits captured or surrendered, the damage was already grievous. Prefectures and counties should be ordered to report any Yao uprising immediately, on pain of punishment. Circuit and route officials should supervise constantly so defenses are ready in advance—and Yao people will learn fear and cease crossing boundaries to harm our people. "
20
十一年，詔給事中、中書舍人、戶部長貳同敕令所議，禁民毋質徭人田，以奪其業，俾能自養，以息邊釁。 從知沅州王鎮之請也。 沅州生界犵狑副峒官吳自由子三人，貨丹砂麻陽縣，巡檢唐人傑誣為盜，執之送獄，自由率峒官楊友祿等謀為亂。 帥司調神勁軍三百人及沅州民兵屯境上，聲言進討。 先遣歸明官田思忠往招撫之，以孔目官為質，世祿等既盟，自由取其三子以歸。
In the eleventh year an edict directed memorialists, drafters, and finance vice-ministers together with the drafting office to ban pledging Yao land—preventing dispossession so Yao people could sustain themselves and border unrest subside. This followed a petition from Yuan Prefecture prefect Wang Zhenzhi. Three sons of Wu Ziyou, a Gelao sub-cave official in Yuan's wild borderlands, were selling cinnabar at Mayang County when patrol officer Tang Renjie falsely accused them of theft, arrested them, and sent them to jail. Ziyou then led cave officials including Yang Youlu in plotting revolt. The route command mobilized three hundred Shenjin Army troops and Yuan militia to the border, announcing a punitive expedition. They first sent pacified official Tian Sizhong to negotiate, holding a clerical officer hostage. After Shilu and the others swore the oath, Ziyou reclaimed his three sons and returned home.
21
嘉泰三年，前知潭州、湖南安撫趙彥勵上言：“湖南九郡皆接溪峒，蠻夷叛服不常，深為邊患。 製馭之方，豈無其說？ 臣以為宜擇素有知勇為徭人所信服者，立為酋長，借補小官以鎮撫之。 況其習俗嗜欲悉同徭人，利害情偽莫不習知，故可坐而製服之也。 五年之間能立勞效，即與補正。 彼既榮顯其身，取重鄉曲，豈不自愛，盡忠公家哉？ 所謂捐虛名而收實利，安邊之上策也。 ”帝下其議。 既而諸司復上言：“往時溪峒設首領、峒主、頭角官及防遏、指揮等使，皆其長也。 比年往往行賄得之，為害滋甚。 今宜一新蠻夷耳目，如趙彥勵之請，所謂以蠻夷治蠻夷，策之上也。 ”帝從之。
In Jiatai 3 (1203), former Tan prefect and Hunan pacification commissioner Zhao Yanli wrote: "All nine Hunan prefectures border stream-caves where tribes submit and rebel by turns—a chronic border affliction." Surely there must be a way to control them? I believe we should select men of proven courage and wisdom whom the Yao trust, appoint them chieftains, and grant minor official titles to keep the peace. Their customs and appetites match the Yao's; they know tribal interests and deceptions intimately—so they can restrain them from their seats. Those who prove themselves within five years should receive regular appointments. Once honored in person and respected in their communities, would they not value themselves and serve the state faithfully? This is trading empty titles for real gain—the best frontier policy. The throne referred the proposal for discussion. Soon various offices replied: "Stream-caves once had chieftains, cave lords, horn officials, and frontier-defense and command envoys—all drawn from their own leaders." In recent years these posts are often bought by bribery, causing ever greater harm. Tribal eyes should be refreshed now—as Zhao Yanli proposed, using tribes to govern tribes is the finest policy. The throne approved.
22
嘉定元年，郴州黑風峒徭人羅世傳寇邊，飛虎統製邊寧戰沒。 江西、湖南驚擾，知隆興趙希懌、知潭州史彌堅共招降之。 二年，李元礪、羅孟二寇江西，攻破龍泉縣。 李再興戰敗，死之，江州駐紮都統製趙選亦戰死。 初，吉州獲賊長七人係獄，土豪黃從龍為賊畫策，賂吉守李絪，得縱還，賊遂無所忌。 有侯押隊者，領兵戍龍泉境上，元礪復用從龍計，椎牛釃酒以犒官軍。 賊至，官軍皆醉，狼狽散走。 寇之初起甚微，賊伺知議論不一，故玩侮官軍。 方江西力戰則求降湖南，湖南戰則求降江西，牽製王師，使不得相應援。 其後命工部侍郎王居安知豫章，擒獲之，溪峒略平。
In Jiading 1 (1208), Luo Shichuan of Heifeng Cave, Chen Prefecture, led Yao raiders against the border; Flying Tiger commander Bian Ning fell in battle. Jiangxi and Hunan were thrown into alarm; Longxing prefect Zhao Xichang and Tan prefect Shi Mijian jointly offered terms of surrender. In the second year Li Yuanli and Luo Meng'er raided Jiangxi and took Longquan County. Li Zaixing was defeated and killed; Jiangzhou garrison overall commander Zhao Xuan also died in battle. Earlier Ji Prefecture had captured seven bandit chiefs and jailed them. Local strongman Huang Conglong plotted for the bandits, bribed Ji prefect Li Yin, and won their release—after which the bandits feared nothing. Commander Hou was garrisoning the Longquan border when Yuanli again used Conglong's scheme—slaughtering cattle and pouring libations to feast the government troops. When the bandits struck, the troops were all drunk and fled in disarray. The rebellion began very small; the bandits saw official policy was divided and treated the army with contempt. While Jiangxi fought they sued for peace in Hunan; while Hunan fought they sued in Jiangxi—splitting the royal forces so they could not reinforce each other. Later Wang Ju'an of the Ministry of Works was appointed Yuzhang prefect and captured them; the stream-caves were largely pacified.
23
五年，臣僚上言：“辰、沅、靖等州舊嚐募民為弓弩手，給地以耕，俾為世業。 邊陲獲保障之安，州縣無轉輸之費。 比年多故，其製浸弛，徭蠻因之為亂，沿邊諸郡悉受其害。 比申朝廷調兵詔捕，曠日持久，蠻夷習玩，成其猖獗之勢。 其如楊晟台、李金、姚明教、羅孟二、李元礪、陳廷佐之徒，皆近事之明驗也。 為今計者，宜講舊制，可紓饋餉之勞而得備禦之實，其安邊息民之長策歟。 ”
In the fifth year a memorialist wrote: "Chen, Yuan, and Jing once recruited civilians as crossbowmen, granting farmland as hereditary livelihood." The frontier gained secure protection and prefectures avoided supply costs. In recent years many disruptions caused the system to decay; Yao tribes rebelled in its wake and every border prefecture suffered. Only when the court finally mobilized troops did months pass while tribes grew bold and their power ran rampant. Yang Shengtai, Li Jin, Yao Mingjiao, Luo Meng'er, Li Yuanli, Chen Tingzuo—these recent cases prove the point. The remedy is to restore the old system—easing supply burdens while gaining real defense. Is this not the lasting policy for securing the frontier and calming the people? "
24
七年，臣僚復上言：“辰、沅、靖三州之地，多接溪峒，其居內地者謂之省民，熟戶、山徭、峒丁乃居外為捍蔽。 其初，區處詳密，立法行事，悉有定制。 峒丁等皆計口給田，多寡闊狹，疆畔井井，擅鬻者有禁，私易者有罰。 一夫歲輸租三鬥，無他繇役，故皆樂為之用。 邊陲有警，眾庶雲集，爭負弩矢前驅，出萬死不顧。 比年防禁日弛，山徭、峒丁得私售田。 田之歸於民者，常賦外復輸稅，公家因資之以為利，故謾不加省。 而山徭、峒丁之常租仍虛掛版籍，責其償益急，往往不能聊生，反寄命徭人，或導其入寇，為害滋甚。 宜敕湖、廣監司檄諸郡，俾循舊制毋廢，庶邊境綏靖而遠人獲安也。 ”
In the seventh year officials submitted again: "Chen, Yuan, and Jing largely border stream-caves. Interior dwellers are called provincial people; settled households, mountain Yao, and cave militiamen live outside as a shield." At first arrangements were meticulous, with fixed regulations governing every practice. Cave militiamen received land per capita—boundaries marked square by square. Unauthorized sales were forbidden and private transfers punished. Each man paid three pecks rent a year with no other corvée, so all served willingly. When the frontier alarm sounded, crowds gathered, competing to carry crossbows and arrows in the vanguard, risking death without hesitation. In recent years prohibitions have slackened daily, and mountain Yao and cave militiamen have sold land privately. Land passing to common people paid extra tax beyond regular levies; the state profited and deliberately looked the other way. Yet the regular rent of mountain Yao and cave militiamen still hung on the registers while collection grew ever more urgent. Unable to live, they entrusted themselves to Yao people or guided raids—causing ever greater harm. Hunan and Guang circuit commissioners should order all prefectures to follow the old system without abolishing it, so the border is tranquil and distant peoples secure. "

梅山峒
Meishan Dong
26
梅山峒蠻，舊不與中國通。 其地東接潭，南接邵，其西則辰，其北則鼎、澧，而梅山居其中。 開寶八年，嚐寇邵之武岡、潭之長沙。 太平興國二年，左甲首領苞漢陽、右甲首領頓漢淩寇掠邊界，朝廷累遣使招諭，不聽，命客省使翟守素調潭州兵討平之。 自是，禁不得與漢民交通，其地不得耕牧。 後有蘇方者居之，數侵奪舒、向二族。
The Meishan cave tribes had no contact with China. Their land bordered Tan to the east, Shao to the south, Chen to the west, and Ding and Li to the north, with Meishan at the center. In Kaibao 8 (975) they raided Wugang in Shao and Changsha in Tan. In Taiping Xingguo 2 (977), left-wing chieftain Bao Hanyang and right-wing chieftain Dun Hanling raided the border. The court repeatedly sent envoys to summon them, but they refused; Guest Reception Commissioner Zhai Shousu was ordered to mobilize Tan troops and suppress them. Thereafter contact with Han commoners was forbidden and the land could not be farmed or grazed. Later a man named Su Fang settled there and repeatedly encroached on the Shu and Xiang clans.
27
嘉祐末，知益陽縣張頡收捕其桀黠符三等，遂經營開拓。 安撫使吳中復以聞，其議中格。 湖南轉運副使範子奇復奏，蠻恃險為邊患，宜臣屬而郡縣之。 子奇尋召還，又述前議。 熙寧五年，乃詔知潭州潘夙、湖南轉運副使蔡熚、判官喬執中同經製章惇招納之。 惇遣執中知全州，將行，而大田三砦蠻犯境。 又飛山之蠻近在全州之西，執中至全州，大田諸蠻納款，於是遂檄諭開梅山，蠻徭爭辟道路，以待得其地。 東起寧鄉縣司徒嶺，西抵邵陽白沙砦，北界益陽四里河，南止湘鄉佛子嶺。 籍其民，得主、客萬四千八百九戶，萬九千八十九丁。 田二十六萬四百三十六畝，均定其稅，使歲一輸。 乃築武陽、關硤二城，詔以山地置新化縣，並二城隸邵州。 自是，鼎、澧可以南至邵。
At the end of Jiayou, Yiyang magistrate Zhang Jie captured ringleaders including Fu San and began opening the region for settlement. Pacification Commissioner Wu Zhongfu reported the plan, but it was blocked midway. Hunan transport vice-commissioner Fan Ziqi memorialized again that the tribes, relying on terrain, were a border menace and should be brought under county administration. Ziqi was soon recalled to court and restated his earlier proposal. In Xining 5 (1072) an edict directed Tan prefect Pan Su, Hunan transport vice-commissioner Cai Bi, and aide Qiao Zhizhong to join Zhang Zhang in receiving their submission. Zhang sent Qiao Zhizhong to govern Quan Prefecture, but as he prepared to depart the Datian Three Stockade tribes raided the border. The Feishan tribes lay just west of Quan. When Zhizhong arrived, the Datian tribes submitted; he then proclaimed the opening of Meishan, and tribal Yao competed to clear roads in anticipation of receiving the land. The boundaries ran east from Situling in Ningxiang, west to Baisha Stockade in Shaoyang, north to Sili River in Yiyang, and south to Foziling in Xiangxiang. A census registered 14,809 registered and guest households with 19,089 adult males. There were 260,436 mu of fields; taxes were assessed uniformly for annual payment. Wuyang and Guanxia cities were built; an edict created Xinhua County in the mountain lands, with both cities under Shao Prefecture. Thereafter Ding and Li could reach Shao from the south.

誠、徽州
Chenghui and Huizhou Prefectures
29
誠、徽州，唐溪峒州。 宋初，楊氏居之，號十峒首領，以其族姓散掌州峒。
Chenghui and Huizhou were Tang-era stream-cave prefectures. Early in the Song the Yang clan held them, styled chieftains of ten caves, governing the prefectural caves through scattered clan branches.
30
太平興國四年，首領楊蘊始來內附。 五年，楊通寶始入貢，命為誠州刺史。 淳化二年，其刺史楊政岩復來貢。 是歲，政岩卒，以其子通盈繼知州事。
In Taiping Xingguo 4 (979) chieftain Yang Yun first submitted to the court. In the fifth year Yang Tongbao first presented tribute and was appointed prefect of Chen Prefecture. In Chunhua 2 (991) its prefect Yang Zhengyan again presented tribute. That year Zhengyan died, and his son Tong Ying succeeded as acting prefect.
31
熙寧八年，有楊光富者，率其族姓二十三州峒歸附，詔以光富為右班殿直，昌運五人補三班奉職，晟情等十六人補三司軍將。 繼有楊昌銜者，亦願罷進奉，出租賦為漢民，詔補為右班殿直，子弟侄十八人補授有差。 獨光僭頗負固不從命，詔湖南轉運使朱初平羈縻之，未幾亦降，乃與其子日儼請於其側建學舍，求名士教子孫。 詔潭州長史樸成為徽、誠等州教授； 光僭皇城使、誠州刺史致仕，官為建宅； 置飛山一帶道路巡檢。 光僭未及拜而卒，遂以贈之，錄其子六人。
In Xining 8 (1075) Yang Guangfu led twenty-three clan caves to submit. Guangfu was appointed Right Gate Guard Direct Attendant; Changyun and four others received Supplementary Third-Rank Attendants; Shengqing and fifteen others received Three-Directorate Military General appointments. Then Yang Changxian also wished to end tribute and pay rent and tax as Han subjects. He was appointed Right Gate Guard Direct Attendant; eighteen sons, brothers, and nephews received appointments at varying ranks. Only Guangjian remained stubborn and defied orders. Hunan transport commissioner Zhu Chuping was ordered to manage him by loose rein; soon he too surrendered. He and his son Riyan then requested a school built nearby and sought renowned scholars to educate their descendants. An edict appointed Tan chief recorder Pu Cheng as professor for Huizhou, Chen, and other prefectures; Guangjian retired as Imperial City Commissioner and Chen Prefecture prefect, with the state building his residence; a road patrol inspector was established for the Feishan region. Guangjian died before taking office; posthumous honors were granted and six sons enrolled in service.
32
元豐三年，知邵州關杞請於徽、誠州融嶺鎮擇要害地築城砦，以絕邊患。 詔湖南安撫謝景溫、轉運使朱初平、判官趙揚商度以聞，景溫等以為宜如杞言。 乃議誠州以沅州貫保砦為渠陽縣隸之，以徽州為蒔竹縣隸邵州。 趙揚言上江、多星、銅鼓、羊鎮、潭溪、上和、上誠、天村、大田等團並至誠州城下貿易，可漸招撫，並乞下湖南邵州蒔竹縣招諭芙蓉、萬驛諸團，從之，徒誠州治渠陽而貫保為砦如故。 上江等諸團果皆納土，於是增築多星等砦，還連徽、廣西融州王口砦焉。
In Yuanfeng 3 (1080) Shao prefect Guan Qi requested building fortified stockades at strategic points in Rongling town, Huizhou and Chen Prefecture, to eliminate border trouble. The court ordered Hunan pacification commissioner Xie Jingwen, transport commissioner Zhu Chuping, and judicial officer Zhao Yang to study the matter and report back. Jingwen and his colleagues agreed that Guan Qi’s plan should be adopted. They then proposed making Guanbao stockade in Yuan Prefecture into Qeyang County under Chen Prefecture, and converting Huizhou into Shizhu County under Shao Prefecture. Zhao Yang reported that the Shangjiang, Duoxing, Tonggu, Yangzhen, Tanxi, Shanghe, Shangcheng, Tiancun, Datian, and other tribal communities all came to trade at Chen Prefecture and could be gradually brought under control. He also asked that Shizhu County in Shao Prefecture be ordered to summon the Furong, Wanyi, and other communities. The court agreed. Chen Prefecture’s seat was moved to Qeyang, while Guanbao remained a stockade as before. The Shangjiang communities and the rest duly submitted. Duoxing and other stockades were then expanded and fortified, reestablishing links with Huizhou and Wangkou stockade in Rong Prefecture, Guangxi.
33
元祐二年，改誠州為渠陽軍，罷兩州兵馬及守禦民丁。 有楊晟台者，乘間寇文村堡，知渠陽軍胡田措置亡術，蠻結西融州蠻砦粟仁催，往來兩路為民患，調兵屯渠陽至萬人，湖南亦增屯兵應援，三路俱驚。 朝廷方務省事，議廢堡砦，徹戍守，而以其地予蠻，乃詔湖北轉運副使李茂直招撫，又遣唐乂同措置邊事討之。 後以渠陽為誠州，命光僭之子供備庫使昌達、供備庫副使楊昌等同知州事，而貫保、豐山、若水等砦皆罷戍，擇授土官，俾乂間毀樓櫓，撤官舍，護領居民入砦。 崇寧初，改誠州為靖州。
In Yuanyou 2 (1087) Chen Prefecture was redesignated Qeyang Army, and the troops and defensive civilian levies of both prefectures were disbanded. A man named Yang Shengtai seized the moment to raid Wencun fort. Qeyang Army commander Hu Tian handled matters poorly. Shengtai allied with Suren of a tribal stockade in western Rong Prefecture and raided back and forth along both routes, plaguing the populace. Ten thousand troops were stationed at Qeyang; Hunan also sent reinforcements, and all three frontier circuits were thrown into alarm. The court, eager to simplify administration, considered abandoning the forts and stockades, withdrawing the garrisons, and turning the territory over to the tribes. Li Maozhi, vice transport commissioner of Hubei, was ordered to win them over, and Tang Yi was sent to manage border affairs and campaign against them. Qeyang was later restored as Chen Prefecture. Guangjian’s son Changda, Storehouse Provision Commissioner, and Yang Chang, Deputy Storehouse Provision Commissioner, were appointed jointly as acting prefects. Garrisons at Guanbao, Fengshan, Ruoshui, and other stockades were withdrawn and native officials appointed in their place. Yi was ordered to dismantle towers and parapets, tear down official buildings, and escort residents into the stockades. At the start of the Chongning era (1102), Chen Prefecture was renamed Jing Prefecture.
34
南丹州
Nandan Prefecture
35
南丹州蠻，亦溪峒之別種也，地與宜州及西南夷接壤。 開寶七年，酋帥莫洪[B16D]遣使陳紹規奉表求內附。 九年，復來貢，求賜牌印，詔刻印以給之。 太平興國五年，洪[B16D]貢銀百兩，以賀太平。
The tribes of Nandan Prefecture were another branch of the stream-cave peoples, living along the borders of Yizhou and the southwestern tribes. In Kaibao 7 (974) chieftain Mo Hong sent envoy Chen Shaogui with a memorial requesting submission to the court. In the ninth year they returned with tribute and requested official plaques and seals. The court ordered seals carved and issued to them. In Taiping Xingguo 5 (980) Mo Hong presented one hundred taels of silver to celebrate the era of peace.
36
雍熙四年，洪[B16D]族人知寶隆鎮莫淮閬牛一頭，逐水草至金城州河池縣，宜州牙校周承鑒以其牛耕作，淮閬三遣人取牛，承鑒不還，凡耕十日，始釋牛逐水草去。 淮閬怒，領鄉兵六十人劫取承鑒家資財，驅縣民莫世家牛六頭以歸，誘群蠻為寇。 上遣供奉官王承緒乘傳劾承鑒，具伏占牛，詔棄市。 時知宜州、讚善大夫侯汀失於備禦，群蠻之擾，頗害及民庶，詔發諸州兵進討，兵未至，悉已遁歸，汀坐免官。 詔諭宜、融、柳州百姓及蠻界人戶曰：“朕托兆庶之上，處司牧之重，照臨所暨，撫養是均，矧於遐陬，尤所軫慮。 昨以知宜州事侯汀失於綏緝，恣其侵牟，致茲邊夷，起為寇鈔，侵騷閭裏，虔劉士庶。 及興師而討伐，乃畏威而竄伏。 朕以興戎召釁，職由於汀，爰舉國章，削其官秩。 汝等所宜體予含垢，革乃前非，安土厚生，保境延世，嬉我至化，是為永圖。 或尚恣於陸梁，當盡剿其族類。 ”自是不復為寇。
In Yongxi 4 (987) Mo Huailang, a kinsman of Mo Hong and magistrate of Baolong town, lost track of an ox that had wandered with the grazing to Hechi County in Jincheng Prefecture. Yizhou garrison officer Zhou Chengjian seized the animal and used it to plow. Huailang sent men three times to reclaim it, but Chengjian refused. Only after ten days of plowing did he finally release the ox to wander off again. Enraged, Huailang led sixty local militiamen to plunder Chengjian’s household, drove off six oxen belonging to a county commoner named Mo, and returned home, inciting the tribes to raid. The emperor sent palace attendant Wang Chengxu by express relay to investigate Chengjian. Chengjian confessed fully to seizing the ox, and the court ordered him executed in the marketplace. Yizhou prefect Hou Ting, Supporter of Goodness Grand Master, had failed to maintain defenses, and the tribal raids had done considerable harm to the people. Troops from several prefectures were ordered to campaign against them, but the raiders fled before the army arrived. Ting was dismissed from office. An edict was issued to the people of Yizhou, Rongzhou, and Liuzhou and to households on the tribal frontier: “I stand above the myriad people, charged with shepherding the realm. Wherever my authority extends, I nurture all alike—and distant borderlands I hold especially close to heart. Recently Hou Ting, acting prefect of Yizhou, failed to keep order and allowed encroachment and exaction to run unchecked. The frontier tribes therefore rose in raids, harassing villages and slaughtering officials and commoners alike. When troops were raised against them, they feared imperial might and fled into hiding. I hold that war was provoked through Ting’s fault. I have therefore invoked the law of the realm and stripped him of rank and office. You should accept my forbearance, amend your former wrongs, settle peacefully and prosper, guard your borders and preserve your lineages, and rejoice in my civilizing rule. That is the path to lasting peace. If any still persist in lawless violence, their entire clans will be exterminated. "
37
淳化元年，洪[B16D]卒，其弟洪皓襲稱刺史，遣其子淮通來貢銀碗二十，銅鼓三面，銅印一鈕，旗一帖，繡真珠紅羅襦一。 上降優詔，賜彩百匹，還其襦。 自洪[B16D]領州十餘年，歲輸白金百兩。 洪皓之襲兄位，專其地利，不修常貢。 其弟洪沅忿之，挈妻子來奔宜州。 洪皓怒其背己，數引兵攻洪沅。 洪沅與二男並牙將一人，乘傳詣闕訴其事，請發兵致討。 上以蠻夷之俗，羈縻而已，不欲為之興師報怨。 洪沅先自稱南丹州副使，以為邵州團練使，給田十頃，下詔戒敕洪皓。
In Chunhua 1 (990) Mo Hong died. His younger brother Hong Hao succeeded as prefect and sent his son Huaitong with tribute: twenty silver bowls, three bronze drums, one bronze seal, one banner, and one pearl-embroidered red silk jacket. The emperor issued a gracious edict, granting one hundred bolts of colored silk and returning the jacket. During Mo Hong’s more than ten years as prefect, he paid one hundred taels of silver in tribute each year. When Hong Hao succeeded his elder brother, he kept the local profits for himself and ceased paying regular tribute. His younger brother Hong Huai, resentful of this, fled with his wife and children to Yizhou. Hong Hao, enraged at this defection, repeatedly led troops against Hong Huai. Hong Huai traveled to court by express relay with two sons and a garrison officer to plead his case and request a punitive expedition. The emperor held that tribal peoples were to be managed by loose rein and did not wish to raise armies over private feuds. Hong Huai, who had styled himself vice commissioner of Nandan Prefecture, was appointed regimental training commissioner of Shao Prefecture and granted ten qing of land. An edict was issued warning Hong Hao.
38
景德二年，洪皓死，長子淮襲父任。 俄為弟淮辿攻南丹州，淮帥屬來奔，詔宜州賜閑田資給之。 大中詳符五年，宜州言淮辿頗集諸蠻，阻富仁監道路，上廉知淮辿無侵擾狀，遣使犒設撫勞之。 九年，撫水蠻叛，詔淮辿約勒溪峒，勿從誘脅。 明年，平撫水蠻，淮辿等並以勞進秩。 景祐三年，有淮戟者舉族來歸，命為湖南州團練副使，敕州縣拊存。 後淮辿老，自言願傳其子世漸。 至和元年，命世漸為檢校散騎常侍，權發遣州事。 明年，以淮辿為懷遠大將軍致仕，世漸為刺史、檢校工部尚書，賜袍帶，錢十萬，絹百匹。 又補其親黨數十人為檢校官，如故事也。 世漸死，嘉祐末，命其子公帳襲之。
In Jingde 2 (1005) Hong Hao died, and his eldest son Huai succeeded to his father’s post. Before long his younger brother Huai Huainai attacked Nandan Prefecture. Huai fled with his followers to the court, and Yizhou was ordered to grant them idle land and provide support. In Dazhong Xiangfu 5 (1012) Yizhou reported that Huai Huainai had gathered many tribes and blocked the road at Furen supervisory post. Learning that Huai Huainai had not harassed the frontier, the emperor sent envoys with rewards to soothe and encourage him. In the ninth year the Fushui tribes rebelled. Huai Huainai was ordered to restrain the stream-cave peoples and not join the rebellion under coercion. The following year, after the Fushui tribes were pacified, Huai Huainai and others were promoted for their service. In Jingyou 3 (1036) Huaiji led his entire clan to submit. He was appointed vice regimental training commissioner of Hunan Prefecture, and local officials were ordered to care for them. Later, as Huai Huainai grew old, he asked to pass his post to his son Shijian. In Zhihe 1 (1054) Shijian was appointed honorary Regular Attendant of the Crown Prince’s Palace and authorized to act as prefect. The following year Huai Huainai retired as Grand General for Cherishing the Distant. Shijian was appointed prefect and honorary Minister of Works, and was granted robes and belt, one hundred thousand cash, and one hundred bolts of silk. Several dozen of his kinsmen and followers were also given honorary appointments, as was customary. When Shijian died near the end of the Jiayou era (1056–1063), his son Gongzhang was appointed to succeed him.
39
有世忍者，亦淮辿之子也。 初率其屬人內附，治平初逃歸，攻殺公帳，奪其地自首，請於朝廷，願授刺史，補其親黨如故事，歲輸銀百兩。 三年，遂命為刺史，皆如其請。 熙寧二年，徭賊殺人，世忍執以獻，授檢校禮部尚書。 元豐三年入貢，其印以“西南諸道武盛軍德政官家明天國主”為文，詔以南丹州印賜之，令毀其舊印。 六年，大軍討安化，世忍獻弓矢，自言願世世為外臣，修貢不懈，遷檢校戶部尚書，給銅牌旗號，官其子侄九人。 世忍死，子公佞襲。
There was also Shiren, another son of Huai Huainai. He had first led his followers to submit to the court. At the start of the Zhiping era (1064) he fled back, killed Gongzhang, seized his territory, and surrendered himself. He asked the court to appoint him prefect, grant honorary offices to his kinsmen as customary, and pay one hundred taels of silver in annual tribute. In the third year he was appointed prefect, with all his requests granted. In Xining 2 (1069) Yao bandits killed people. Shiren captured them and turned them over to the authorities, and was appointed honorary Minister of Rites. In Yuanfeng 3 (1080) he presented tribute. His seal bore the inscription “Lord of the Bright Heaven Kingdom, Virtuous Ruler of the Martial Flourishing Army of the Southwestern Routes.” The court granted him the Nandan Prefecture seal and ordered his old one destroyed. In the sixth year, during the campaign against Anhua, Shiren presented bows and arrows and declared his wish to remain a loyal outer subject for generations, maintaining tribute without fail. He was promoted to honorary Minister of Revenue, granted bronze plaques and banner tokens, and nine sons and nephews received appointments. When Shiren died, his son Gongren succeeded him.
40
大觀元年，廣西經略使王祖道言公佞就擒。 進築平、允、從州，牧文、地、蘭、那、安、外、習、南丹八州之地，並為鎮庭孚觀州、延德軍，以其弟公晟襲刺史。 宣和四年，公晟乞以州事付其侄延豐，願與其子歸朝，詔從之，仍乘驛給券。
In Daguan 1 (1107) Guangxi frontier commissioner Wang Zudao reported that Gongren had been captured. The court advanced by building Ping, Yun, and Cong prefectures, bringing under control the eight prefectures of Wen, Di, Lan, Na, An, Wai, Xi, and Nandan, and establishing Zhenting Fu Guan Prefecture and Yande Army. Gongren’s younger brother Gong Sheng succeeded as prefect. In Xuanhe 4 (1122) Gong Sheng asked to entrust prefectural affairs to his nephew Yanfeng and requested to return to court with his son. The court agreed and provided post-horses and travel warrants.
41
紹興三年，公晟攻圍觀州，焚寶積監。 朱勝非奏：“崇、觀、宣和間所開新邊，比來往往棄而不守，帥臣、監司屢言觀州為控扼之地。 不宜棄。 ”帝曰：“前日用事之臣，貪功生事，公為欺罔，其實勞民費財，使遠俗不安也。 ”又用廣南經略安撫使劉彥適言，以公晟知南丹州兼溪峒都巡檢使、提舉盜賊公事，給以南丹州刺史舊印，公晟未受命。 二十四年，公晟始貢馬，率諸蠻來歸。 帝諭輔臣曰：“得南丹非為廣地也，但徭人不叛，百姓安業，為可喜耳。 ”遂以延沈襲公晟職，授銀青光祿大夫、檢校太子賓客、使持節南丹州諸軍事、南丹州刺史兼御史大夫、知南丹州公事、武騎尉。 廣西經略安撫使呂願中諭降諸蠻三十二種，得州二十七，縣一百三十五，砦四十，峒一百七十九及一鎮、三十二團，皆為羈縻州縣。 二十五年，延沈進補團練、防禦二使。 三十一年，延沈恣行慘酷，為諸蠻所逐，歸死省地，眾推延廩襲職。 隆興二年，延廩復為諸蠻所圖，攜家歸朝，經略司奏以延葚襲職。 淳熙元年，南丹為永樂州所攻，使來告急，廣西帥臣遣將領陳泰權、天河縣主簿徐彌高諭和之。 十四年，經略司奏以延陰襲職，詔從其請。 嘉定五年，延陰之子光熙襲職，知南丹州事。
In Shaoxing 3 (1133) Gong Sheng besieged Guan Prefecture and burned Baoji supervisory post. Zhu Shengfei memorialized: “The new frontier territories opened during the Chongning, Daguan, and Xuanhe eras have lately been abandoned one after another. Frontier commanders and supervisory officials have repeatedly argued that Guan Prefecture is a strategic choke point. It should not be abandoned. ” The emperor replied: “The officials in power back then, greedy for glory, manufactured incidents and deceived the court. In truth they exhausted the people, wasted resources, and left distant tribes unsettled. ” Following the recommendation of Guangnan frontier pacification commissioner Liu Yanshi, Gong Sheng was appointed prefect of Nandan Prefecture, concurrently overall stream-cave patrol inspector and commissioner for bandit suppression. He was given the old Nandan prefect’s seal, but Gong Sheng did not accept the appointment. In the twenty-fourth year (1154) Gong Sheng first presented horses in tribute and led the various tribes to submit. The emperor told his chief ministers: “Gaining Nandan is not about expanding territory. What matters is that the Yao do not rebel and the people live in peace—that is what is worth celebrating. ” Yan Shen was then appointed to succeed Gong Sheng, with the titles Silver-Gleaming Grand Master of Splendid Happiness, honorary Guest of the Crown Prince, commissioner with full credentials for all military affairs of Nandan Prefecture, prefect of Nandan and concurrent Grand Censor, acting prefect of Nandan, and Martial Rider Commandant. Guangxi frontier pacification commissioner Lü Yuanzhong announced the submission of thirty-two tribal groups, bringing under control twenty-seven prefectures, one hundred thirty-five counties, forty stockades, one hundred seventy-nine caves, one town, and thirty-two communities—all registered as loose-rein prefectures and counties. In the twenty-fifth year (1155) Yan Shen was further promoted to regimental training and defense commissioner. In the thirty-first year (1161) Yan Shen ruled with cruelty and was driven out by the tribes. He returned to provincial territory and died there, and the tribes installed Yan Lin in his place. In Longxing 2 (1164) Yan Lin was again targeted by the tribes. He fled to court with his family, and the frontier commission memorialized that Yan Shen should succeed to the post. In Chunxi 1 (1174) Nandan came under attack from Yongle Prefecture. Envoys arrived pleading for help, and the Guangxi commander sent generals Chen Taiquan and Tianhe County registrar Xu Migao to mediate. In the fourteenth year (1187) the frontier commission recommended Yan Yin to succeed to the post, and the court agreed. In Jiading 5 (1212) Yan Yin’s son Guangxi succeeded to the post as acting prefect of Nandan.
