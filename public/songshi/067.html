1
稼穡作甘，土之性也。 土失其性，則為災兇。 舊說以恆風、脂夜之妖，華孽、臝蟲之孽，牛禍、黃眚、黃祥，皆屬之土，今從之。
Grain crops yield sweetness—such is the nature of earth. When earth loses its proper nature, disaster and calamity follow. Earlier doctrine assigned constant winds, night-oil apparitions, floral anomalies, naked-insect portents, cattle calamities, yellow blights, and yellow omens to earth; this account follows that classification.
2
建隆元年，河南諸州乏食。
In the first year of Jianlong, prefectures throughout Henan suffered food shortages.
3
開寶四年，州府六水、一旱，諸州民乏食。 五年，大饑。 六年，水，民飢。 九年，州府十二飢。
In the fourth year of Kaibao, six circuits were flooded and one stricken by drought, and people across many prefectures lacked food. In the fifth year, a severe famine struck. In the sixth year, floods brought famine upon the people. In the ninth year, twelve circuits were stricken by famine.
4

In the fourth year of Taiping Xingguo, Taiping Prefecture was stricken by famine.
5
淳化元年，開封、河南等九州飢。 五年，京東西、淮南、陝西水潦，民飢。
In the first year of Chunhua, famine afflicted nine prefectures, including Kaifeng and Henan. In the fifth year, floods inundated the Eastern and Western Capital circuits, Huainan, and Shaanxi, and the people went hungry.
6
咸平五年，河北及鄭、曹、滑飢。
In the fifth year of Xianping, Hebei and the prefectures of Zheng, Cao, and Hua were stricken by famine.
7
景德元年，江南東、西路飢。 二年，淮南、兩浙、荊湖北路飢。 三年，京東西、河北、陝西飢。
In the first year of Jingde, famine struck the Eastern and Western Jiangnan circuits. In the second year, Huainan, the two Zhejiang circuits, and northern Jing suffered famine. In the third year, famine spread through the Eastern and Western Capital circuits, Hebei, and Shaanxi.
8
大中祥符三年，陝西飢。 四年，河北、陝西、劍南飢。 五年，河北、淮南飢。 七年，淮南、江、浙飢。 八年，陝西州府五飢。
In the third year of Dazhong Xiangfu, Shaanxi was stricken by famine. In the fourth year, famine afflicted Hebei, Shaanxi, and Jiannan. In the fifth year, Hebei and Huainan were stricken by famine. In the seventh year, famine struck Huainan, Jiangnan, and Zhejiang. In the eighth year, five Shaanxi circuits were stricken by famine.
9
天禧元年，飢。 三年，江、浙及利州路飢。
In the first year of Tianxi, famine struck. In the third year, famine afflicted Jiangnan, Zhejiang, and the Lizhou circuit.
10

In the third year of Tiansheng, famine struck Jin, Jiang, Shaan, and Xie.
11

In the first year of Mingdao, famine afflicted the Eastern Capital circuit, Huainan, and Jiangdong. In the second year, Huainan, Jiangdong, and western Sichuan were stricken by famine.
12
寶元二年，益、梓、利、夔路飢。
In the second year of Baoyuan, famine struck the Yi, Zi, Li, and Kui circuits.
13
嘉祐三年，夔州路旱，飢。
In the third year of Jiayou, drought brought famine to the Kuizhou circuit.
14
熙寧三年，河北、陝西旱。 四年，河北旱，飢。 六年，淮南、江東、劍南西川、潤州飢。 七年，京畿、河北、京東西、淮西、成都、利州、延、常、潤、府州、威勝、保安軍飢。 八年，兩河、陝西、江南、淮、浙飢。 九年，雄州飢。 十年，漳泉州、興化軍飢。 元豐元年，河北飢。 四年，鳳翔府、鳳階州飢。 七年，河東飢。
In the third year of Xining, Hebei and Shaanxi were stricken by drought. In the fourth year, drought in Hebei gave way to famine. In the sixth year, famine afflicted Huainan, Jiangdong, western Jiannan, and Run Prefecture. In the seventh year, famine spread across the capital region, Hebei, the Eastern and Western Capital circuits, western Huai, Chengdu, Lizhou, Yan, Chang, Run, Fuzhou, Weisheng, and Baojun. In the eighth year, famine struck the Yellow River circuits, Shaanxi, Jiangnan, Huai, and Zhejiang. In the ninth year, Xiong Prefecture was stricken by famine. In the tenth year, Zhang and Quan prefectures and Xinghua suffered famine. In the first year of Yuanfeng, Hebei was stricken by famine. In the fourth year, Fengxiang and the Feng and Jie prefectures suffered famine. In the seventh year, Hedong was stricken by famine.

In the second year of Yuanfu, famine struck.
16

In the first year of Chongning, famine afflicted Jiangnan, Zhejiang, and Xihe.

In the third year of Daguan, famine struck Qin, Feng, Jie, and Cheng.
18

In the first year of Zhonghe, the Western Capital circuit was stricken by famine. In the fifth year, famine afflicted Hebei, the Eastern Capital circuit, and Huainan.
19
建炎元年，汴京大饑，米升錢三百，一鼠直數百錢，人食水藻、椿槐葉，道殣，骼無餘胔。 三年，山東郡國大饑，人相食。 時金人陷京東諸郡，民聚為盜，至車載乾屍為糧。
In the first year of Jianyan, Bianjing was gripped by severe famine: rice sold for three hundred cash per sheng, a single rat fetched hundreds of cash, and people ate waterweeds and pagoda- and scholar-tree leaves until the roads were heaped with the dead and bones lay stripped of flesh. In the third year, Shandong was ravaged by famine so severe that people resorted to cannibalism. As the Jurchens overran the Eastern Capital commanderies, desperate people banded together as robbers, going so far as to haul dried corpses in carts for food.
20
紹興元年，行在、越州及東南諸路郡國飢。 淮南、京東西民流常州、平江府者多殍死。 二年春，兩浙、福建飢，米鬥千錢。 時餫餉繁急，民益艱食。 三年，吉、郴、道州、桂陽監飢。 五年，湖南大饑，殍死、流亡者眾。 夏，潼川路飢，米鬥二千，人食糟糠。 興元飢，民流於果、閬。 秋，溫、處州飢。 六年春，浙東、福建飢，湖南、江西大饑，殍死甚眾，民多流徙，郡邑盜起。 夏，蜀亦大饑，米鬥二千，利路倍之，道殣枕藉。 是歲，果州守臣宇文彬獻《禾粟九穗圖》，吏部侍郎晏敦復言：“果、遂饑民未蘇，不宜導諛。 ”坐黜爵。 七年夏，欽、廉、邕州飢。 九年，江東西、浙東飢，米鬥千錢，饒、信州尤甚。 十年，浙東、江南薦飢，人食草木。 十一年，京西、淮南飢。 十八年冬，浙東、江、淮郡國多飢，紹興尤甚。 民之仰哺於官者二十八萬六千人，不給，乃食糟糠、草木，殍死殆半。 十九年春、夏，紹興府大饑，明、婺州亦如之。 二十四年，衢州飢。 二十八年，平江府飢。 二十九年，紹興府薦飢。
In the first year of Shaoxing, the temporary capital, Yue Prefecture, and circuits across the southeast were stricken by famine. Refugees from Huainan and the Eastern and Western Capital circuits who streamed into Chang and Pingjiang mostly perished along the way. In the spring of the second year, famine struck the two Zhejiang circuits and Fujian, with rice selling at a thousand cash per dou. With grain levies urgently demanded for the war effort, the people found it ever harder to feed themselves. In the third year, Ji, Chen, and Dao prefectures and the Guiyang Directorate were stricken by famine. In the fifth year, Hunan was ravaged by famine, and vast numbers starved to death or fled their homes. That summer, famine in the Tongchuan circuit drove rice to two thousand cash per dou, and people subsisted on chaff and bran. Famine in Xingyuan sent the people fleeing toward Guo and Lang. In autumn, Wen and Chu prefectures were stricken by famine. In the spring of the sixth year, eastern Zhejiang and Fujian went hungry while Hunan and Jiangxi were devastated by famine: countless dead littered the roads, masses of people fled, and banditry broke out across the counties. That summer, Sichuan too was gripped by famine—rice at two thousand cash per dou, twice that on the Lizhou circuit—with the dead heaped along every road. That year, Yuwen Bin, prefect of Guo, presented a painting of grain bearing nine ears, but Yan Dunfu, vice minister of personnel, protested: "The starving people of Guo and Sui have not yet recovered; we must not encourage flattery." For this he was stripped of rank. In the summer of the seventh year, Qin, Lian, and Yong prefectures were stricken by famine. In the ninth year, Jiangdong, Jiangxi, and eastern Zhejiang suffered famine, with rice at a thousand cash per dou; Rao and Xin were hardest hit. In the tenth year, recurring famine in eastern Zhejiang and Jiangnan drove people to eat wild plants and tree leaves. In the eleventh year, the Western Capital circuit and Huainan were stricken by famine. In the winter of the eighteenth year, famine spread through eastern Zhejiang, Jiangnan, and the Huai region, with Shaoxing worst affected. Of the 286,000 people depending on official relief, supplies ran out; forced to eat chaff, bran, and wild plants, nearly half starved to death. In the spring and summer of the nineteenth year, Shaoxing Prefecture was devastated by famine, as were Ming and Wu. In the twenty-fourth year, Qu Prefecture was stricken by famine. In the twenty-eighth year, Pingjiang Prefecture suffered famine. In the twenty-ninth year, Shaoxing Prefecture again suffered famine.
21
隆興元年，紹興府大饑，四川尤甚。 平江、襄陽府、隨、泗州、棗陽、盱眙軍大饑，隨、棗間米鬥六七千。 二年，平江府、常、秀州飢，華亭縣人食粃糠。 行都及鎮江府、興化軍、台、徽州亦艱食。 淮民流徙江南者數十萬。
In the first year of Longxing, Shaoxing Prefecture was devastated by famine, and Sichuan was hit even harder. Pingjiang, Xiangyang, Sui and Si, Zaoyang, and Xuyi were ravaged by famine, with rice between Sui and Zaoyang selling for six or seven thousand cash per dou. In the second year, Pingjiang and the Chang and Xiu prefectures went hungry, and people in Huating County subsisted on bran and chaff. The temporary capital, Zhenjiang, Xinghua, and Tai and Hui prefectures also struggled to find food. Several hundred thousand refugees from the Huai region fled into Jiangnan.
22
乾道元年春，行都、平江、鎮江、紹興府、湖、常、秀州大饑，殍徙者不可勝計。 是歲，台、明州、江東諸郡皆飢。 夏，亡麥。 二年夏，亡麥。 三年九月，不雨，麥種不入。 四年春，蜀、邛、綿、劍、漢州、石泉軍大饑，邛為甚。 盜延八郡，漢饑民至九萬餘。 五年夏，饒、信州薦飢，民多流徙。 徽州大饑，人食蕨葛。 台、楚州、盱眙軍亦飢。 秋冬不雨，淮郡麥種不入。 六年冬，寧國府、廣德軍、太平、湖、秀、池、徽、和州皆飢。 七年秋，江東西、湖南十餘郡飢，江、筠州、隆興府為甚。 人食草實，流徙淮甸，詔出內帑收育棄孩。 淮郡亦薦飢，金人運麥於淮北岸易南岸銅鏹，鬥錢八千。 江西飢，民流光、濠、安豐間，皆效淮人私糴，錢為之耗。 荊南亦飢。 八年，江西亡麥。 隆興府薦飢，南昌、新建縣饑民仰給者二八千餘。 九年春，成都、永康、邛三州飢。 秋，台州飢，溫、婺州亦飢。
In the spring of the first year of Qiandao, the temporary capital, Pingjiang, Zhenjiang, Shaoxing, and the Hu, Chang, and Xiu prefectures were devastated by famine, with untold numbers dead and displaced. That year, Tai and Ming prefectures and commanderies throughout Jiangdong were stricken by famine. That summer, the wheat crop failed. In the summer of the second year, the wheat crop failed again. In the ninth month of the third year, drought prevented the sowing of winter wheat. In the spring of the fourth year, Sichuan, Qiong, Mian, Jian, Han, and Shiquan were devastated by famine, with Qiong hardest hit. Banditry spread across eight commanderies, and more than ninety thousand people in Han alone went hungry. In the summer of the fifth year, recurring famine in Rao and Xin drove masses of people to flee. Hu Prefecture was devastated by famine, and people ate bracken and kudzu roots. Tai and Chu prefectures and Xuyi also went hungry. Autumn and winter drought on the Huai prevented the sowing of winter wheat. In the winter of the sixth year, Ningguo, Guangde, and Taiping, Hu, Xiu, Chi, Hui, and He all suffered famine. In the autumn of the seventh year, more than ten commanderies in Jiangdong, Jiangxi, and Hunan went hungry, with Jiang, Jun, and Longxing worst affected. People ate wild seeds and fled to the Huai region; an edict ordered funds from the inner treasury to rescue abandoned children. The Huai commanderies again suffered famine; Jurchens ferried wheat to the north bank of the Huai to trade for southern copper cash at eight thousand per dou. Famine in Jiangxi sent refugees streaming to Guang, Hao, and Anfeng, where they followed the Huai people's example in buying grain on the black market, draining the currency supply. Jingnan was stricken by famine as well. In the eighth year, the wheat crop failed in Jiangxi. Recurring famine in Longxing Prefecture left more than twenty-eight thousand people in Nanchang and Xinjian dependent on official relief. In the spring of the ninth year, Chengdu, Yongkang, and Qiong were stricken by famine. In autumn, Tai Prefecture went hungry, as did Wen and Wu.
23
淳熙元年，浙東、湖南、廣西、江西、蜀關外皆飢，台、處、郴、桂、昭、賀尤甚。 二年，淮東西、江東飢，滁、真、揚州、盱眙軍、建康府為甚。 是歲，鎮江、寧國府、常州、廣德軍亦艱食。 詔獎建康留守劉珙振濟有方。 三年，淮甸飢。 夏，台州亡麥。 冬，复、施、隨、郢州、荊門軍、襄陽、江陵、德安府大饑； 四年春，尤飢。 六年冬，和州飢。 泰、通、楚州、高郵軍大饑，人食草木。 七年，鎮江府、台州、無為、廣德軍民大饑。 是歲，江、浙、荊、湘、淮郡皆飢。 八年春，江州飢，人采葛而食，詔罷守臣章騂。 冬，行都、寧國、建康府、嚴、婺、太平州、廣德軍飢，徽、饒州大饑，流淮郡者萬餘人，浙東常平使者朱熹進對論荒政，請蠲田賦、身丁錢，詔江、浙、淮、湖北三十八郡並免之。 九年春，大亡麥。 行都飢，於潛、昌化縣人食草木。 紹興府、衢、婺、嚴、明、台、湖州飢。 徽州大饑，穜稑亦絕。 湖北七郡薦飢。 蜀潼、利、夔三路郡國十八皆飢，流徙者數千人。 十年，合、昌州薦飢，民就振相蹂死者三千餘人。 十一年，泉、汀、漳州、興化軍亡禾。 邕、賓、象州飢。 十二年，福建飢，亡麥。 江西、廣東西飢。 金州飢，有流徙者。 十四年，金、洋、階、成、鳳、西和州人乏食。 七月，秀州飢，有流徙者。 臨安府九縣飢。 十六年夏，成州亡麥。 冬，際、成、鳳、西和州薦飢。
In the first year of Chunxi, famine struck eastern Zhejiang, Hunan, Guangxi, Jiangxi, and Sichuan beyond the passes, with Tai, Chu, Chen, Gui, Zhao, and He hardest hit. In the second year, the Eastern and Western Huai circuits and Jiangdong suffered famine, with Chu, Zhen, Yang, Xuyi, and Jianfu worst affected. That year, Zhenjiang, Ningguo, Chang, and Guangde also struggled to find food. An edict praised Liu Gong, defender of Jianfu, for his skillful famine relief. In the third year, the Huai region was stricken by famine. That summer, the wheat crop failed in Tai Prefecture. In winter, Fu, Shi, Sui, Ying, Jingmen, Xiangyang, Jiangling, and De'an were devastated by famine; In the spring of the fourth year, the famine grew especially severe. In the winter of the sixth year, He Prefecture was stricken by famine. Tai, Tong, Chu, and Gaoyou were ravaged by famine, and people ate wild plants and tree leaves. In the seventh year, the people of Zhenjiang, Tai, Wuwei, and Guangde were devastated by famine. That year, commanderies across Jiangnan, Zhejiang, Jing, Hunan, and the Huai region all went hungry. In the spring of the eighth year, Jiang Prefecture went hungry, and people gathered kudzu to eat; an edict dismissed the prefect Zhang Ping. In winter, the temporary capital, Ningguo, Jianfu, Yan, Wu, Taiping, and Guangde went hungry, while Hui and Rao were devastated by famine; more than ten thousand refugees streamed into the Huai region. Zhu Xi, eastern Zhe's Ever-Normal Granary envoy, addressed the throne on famine policy and urged remission of land and poll taxes; an edict exempted thirty-eight commanderies in Jiangnan, Zhejiang, the Huai region, and Hubei. In the spring of the ninth year, the wheat crop failed catastrophically. The temporary capital went hungry, and people in Yuqian and Changhua counties ate wild plants and tree leaves. Shaoxing and the Qu, Wu, Yan, Ming, Tai, and Hu prefectures were stricken by famine. Hu Prefecture was devastated by famine, and both early and late grain crops were exhausted. Seven commanderies in Hubei again suffered famine. Across eighteen commanderies in Shu's Tong, Li, and Kui circuits, famine struck, and several thousand people fled. In the tenth year, He and Chang again suffered famine, and more than three thousand people were trampled to death in the crush at relief stations. In the eleventh year, Quan, Ting, Zhang, and Xinghua lost their grain harvest. Yong, Bin, and Xiang prefectures were stricken by famine. In the twelfth year, Fujian went hungry and the wheat crop failed. Jiangxi and eastern and western Guangdong suffered famine. Jin Prefecture went hungry, and refugees took to the roads. In the fourteenth year, people in Jin, Yang, Jie, Cheng, Feng, and Xihe lacked food. In the seventh month, Xiu Prefecture went hungry, and refugees took to the roads. All nine counties of Lin'an Prefecture were stricken by famine. In the summer of the sixteenth year, the wheat crop failed in Cheng Prefecture. In winter, Jie, Cheng, Feng, and Xihe again suffered famine.
24
紹熙二年，蘄州飢。 夔路五郡飢，渝、涪為甚。 階、成、鳳、西和州亡麥。 三年，資、榮州亡麥，普、敘、簡、隆州、富順監皆大饑，亡麥，殍死者眾，民流成都府至千餘人，威遠縣棄兒且六百人。 揚州亦多飢。 四年，簡、資、普州飢，綿州亡麥。 夏，紹興府亡麥。 安豐軍大亡麥。 五年冬，亡麥苗。 行都、淮、浙西東、江東郡國皆飢，常、明州、寧國、鎮江府、廬滁、和州為甚，人食草木。
In the second year of Shaoxi, Qi Prefecture was stricken by famine. Five commanderies in the Kui circuit went hungry, with Yu and Fu hardest hit. The wheat crop failed in Jie, Cheng, Feng, and Xihe. In the third year, the wheat crop failed in Zi and Rong; Pu, Xu, Jian, Long, and Fushun were devastated by famine, the wheat crop failed, and countless people starved to death. More than a thousand refugees reached Chengdu, and nearly six hundred infants were abandoned in Weiyuan County. Yang Prefecture also suffered widespread famine. In the fourth year, Jian, Zi, and Pu went hungry, and the wheat crop failed in Mian. That summer, the wheat crop failed in Shaoxing Prefecture. The wheat crop failed catastrophically in Anfeng. In the winter of the fifth year, the winter wheat seedlings perished. The temporary capital, the Huai region, eastern and western Zhejiang, and Jiangdong all went hungry, with Chang, Ming, Ningguo, Zhenjiang, Lu, Chu, and He worst affected; people ate wild plants and tree leaves.
25
嘉泰元年，浙西郡國薦飢，常州、鎮江、嘉興府為甚。 二年，四川飢，廣安、懷安軍、潼川府大亡麥。 衡、郴州、武岡、桂陽軍乏食。 三年春，邵、永州大饑，死徙者眾，民多剽盜。 夏，行都艱食。 四年春，撫、袁州、隆興府、臨江軍大饑，殍死者不可勝瘞，有舉家二十七人同赴水死者。
In the first year of Jiatai, western Zhejiang again suffered famine, with Chang, Zhenjiang, and Jiaxing hardest hit. In the second year, Sichuan went hungry, and the wheat crop failed catastrophically in Guang'an, Huai'an, and Tongchuan. Heng, Chen, Wugang, and Guiyang lacked food. In the spring of the third year, Shao and Yong were devastated by famine; countless people died or fled, and banditry spread. That summer, the temporary capital struggled to find food. In the spring of the fourth year, Fu, Yuan, Longxing, and Linjiang were devastated by famine; the dead were too many to bury, and in one case twenty-seven members of a single household drowned themselves together.
26
開禧二年，紹興府、衢、婺州亡麥。 湖北、京西、淮東西郡國飢，民聚為剽盜。 南康軍、忠、涪州皆飢。
In the second year of Kaixi, the wheat crop failed in Shaoxing and the Qu and Wu prefectures. Famine struck Hubei, the Western Capital circuit, and the Eastern and Western Huai circuits, and people banded together as robbers. Nankang, Zhong, and Fu all went hungry.

元祐二年二月辛亥，代州地震有聲。 四年春，陝西、河北地震。 七年九月己酉，蘭州、鎮戎軍、永興軍地震，十月庚戌朔，環州地再震。
In the second month, second year of Yuanyou, on xinhai, Dai Prefecture was shaken by earthquake with a roaring sound. In the spring of the fourth year, Shaanxi and Hebei were shaken by earthquake. In the ninth month, seventh year, on jiyou, Lan Prefecture, Zhenrong Army, and Yongxing Army were shaken; on the new moon of the tenth month, gengxu, Huan Prefecture trembled again.
168
紹聖元年十一月丙戌，大原府地震。 二年十月、十一月，河南府地震。 是歲，蘇州自夏迄秋地震。 三年三月戊戌夜，劍南東川地震。 九月己酉，滁州、沂州地震。 四年六月己酉，太原府地震有聲。
In the eleventh month, first year of Shaosheng, on bingxu, Dayuan Prefecture was shaken by earthquake. In the tenth and eleventh months, second year, Henan Prefecture was shaken by earthquake. That year Su Prefecture trembled from summer through autumn. On the night of wuxu, third month, third year, Jiannan East Circuit was shaken by earthquake. In the ninth month, on jiyou, Chu Prefecture and Yi Prefecture were shaken by earthquake. In the sixth month, fourth year, on jiyou, Taiyuan Prefecture was shaken by earthquake with a thunderous sound.
169
元符元年七月壬申夜，雲陰蔽天，地震良久。 二年正月壬申，恩州地震。 八月甲戌，太原府地震； 三年五月己巳，太原府又震。
On the night of renshen, seventh month, first year of Yuanfu, cloud and darkness shrouded the sky as the earth shook for a long interval. In the first month, second year, on renshen, En Prefecture was shaken by earthquake. In the eighth month, on jiaxu, Taiyuan Prefecture was shaken by earthquake; In the fifth month, third year, on jisi, Taiyuan Prefecture trembled again.
170
政和七年六月，詔曰：“熙河、環慶、涇原路地震經旬，城砦、關堡、城壁、樓櫓、官私廬舍並皆摧塌，居民覆壓死傷甚眾，而有司不以聞，其遣官按視之。 ”
In the sixth month, seventh year of Zhenghe, an edict declared: "The Xihe, Huanqing, and Jingyuan circuits have trembled for ten days straight; forts, passes, walls, watchtowers, and public and private houses have all given way; countless inhabitants lie buried, dead or wounded, yet the authorities have not reported it—send officials to investigate on the spot. "
171
宣和四年，北方用兵，雄州地大震。 玄武見於州之正寢，有龜大如錢，蛇若朱漆箸，相逐而行，宣撫使焚香再拜，以銀奩貯二物。 俄俱死。 六年正月，京師連日地震，宮殿門皆動有聲。 七年七月己亥，熙河路地震，有裂數十丈者，蘭州尤甚。 陷數百家，倉庫俱沒。 河東諸郡或震裂。
In the fourth year of Xuanhe, as the north was mobilized for war, Xiong Prefecture was struck by a great earthquake. The Dark Warrior manifested in the prefecture's main hall—a turtle the size of a coin and a snake like a vermilion lacquer chopstick chasing one another across the floor; the Pacification Commissioner burned incense, bowed twice, and placed both creatures in a silver casket. Before long both were dead. In the first month, sixth year, the capital shook day after day, and the palace gates rattled audibly. In the seventh month, seventh year, on jihai, Xihe Circuit was shaken by earthquake, the ground opening in rents tens of zhang long; Lan Prefecture suffered worst. Several hundred households were engulfed and every warehouse was buried. Several prefectures in Hedong likewise split open in the tremors.
172
建炎二年正月戊戌，長安地大震，金將婁宿圍城，彌旬無外援，乘地震而入，城遂陷。
On wuxu, first month, second year of Jianyan, Chang'an was shaken by a great earthquake; the Jin general Lou Su had the city under siege, and for ten days no relief came from outside—his men rushed in amid the tremor, and the city was lost.
173
紹興三年八月甲申，地震，平江府、湖州尤甚。 是歲，劉豫陷鄧、隨等州，金人犯蜀。 四年，四川地震。 五年五月，行都地震。 六年六月乙巳夜，地震自西北，有聲如雷，餘杭縣為甚。 是冬，劉麟、猊犯順，寇濠、壽州。 七年，地震。 二十四年正月戊寅，地震。 二十五年三月壬申，地震。 二十八年八月甲寅夜，震。 三十一年三月壬辰，地震。 三十二年七月戊申，地震。
On jiashen, eighth month, third year of Shaoxing, the earth shook; Pingjiang Prefecture and Hu Prefecture were hardest hit. That year Liu Yu seized Deng, Sui, and other prefectures, and the Jurchens thrust into Shu. In the fourth year, Sichuan was shaken by earthquake. In the fifth month, fifth year, the traveling capital trembled. On the night of yisi, sixth month, sixth year, a quake rolled in from the northwest with thunderous roars; Yuhang County suffered worst. That winter Liu Lin and Ni rose against Shun and raided Hao and Shou prefectures. In the seventh year the earth shook. In the first month, twenty-fourth year, on wuyin, the earth shook. In the third month, twenty-fifth year, on renshen, there was an earthquake. On the night of jiayin, eighth month, twenty-eighth year, the ground trembled. In the third month, thirty-first year, on renchen, there was an earthquake. In the seventh month, thirty-second year, on wushen, the earth shook.
174
隆興元年十月丁丑，地震； 六月甲寅，又震。
In the tenth month, first year of Longxing, on dingchou, the earth shook; In the sixth month, on jiayin, it trembled again.
175
乾道二年九月丙午，地震自西北方。 四年十二月壬子，石泉軍地震三日，有聲如雷，屋瓦皆落，時綿竹有冤獄雲。
In the ninth month, second year of Qiandao, on bingwu, a quake rolled in from the northwest. In the twelfth month, fourth year, on renzi, Shiquan Army trembled for three days with thunderous roars and every roof tile came down; at the time Mianzhu was said to harbor a grievous miscarriage of justice.
176
淳熙元年十二月戊辰，地震自東北方。 九年十二月壬寅夜，地震。 十年十二月丙寅，地震。 十二年五月庚寅，地震。
In the twelfth month, first year of Chunxi, on wuchen, a quake rolled in from the northeast. On the night of renyin, twelfth month, ninth year, the earth shook. In the twelfth month, tenth year, on bingyin, there was an earthquake. In the fifth month, twelfth year, on gengyin, the earth shook.
177
慶元六年九月，東北地震。 十一月甲子，地震東北方。
In the ninth month, sixth year of Qingyuan, the northeast was shaken by earthquake. In the eleventh month, on jiazi, the northeast trembled.
178
嘉定六年四月，行都地震。 六月丙子，淳安縣地震。 九年二月辛亥，東、西川地大震四日。 十年二月庚申，地震自東南。 十二年五月，地震​​。 六月，西川地震。 十四年正月乙未夜，地震，大雷。 五月丙申，西川地震。
In the fourth month, sixth year of Jiading, the traveling capital was shaken. In the sixth month, on bingzi, Chun'an County was shaken by earthquake. In the second month, ninth year, on xinhai, eastern and western Sichuan were convulsed by a great earthquake for four days. In the second month, tenth year, on gengshen, a quake rolled in from the southeast. In the fifth month, twelfth year, the earth shook. In the sixth month, western Sichuan was shaken by earthquake. On the night of yiwei, first month, fourteenth year, the earth shook amid great thunder. In the fifth month, on bingshen, western Sichuan trembled again.
179
寶慶元年八月己酉，地震。
In the eighth month, first year of Baoqing, on jiyou, the earth shook.
180
嘉熙四年十二月丙辰，地震。
In the twelfth month, fourth year of Jiaxi, on bingchen, there was an earthquake.
181
淳祐元年十二月庚辰夜，地震。
On the night of gengchen, twelfth month, first year of Chunyou, the earth shook.
182
寶祐三年，蜀地震。
In the third year of Baoyou, Shu was shaken by earthquake.
183
咸淳七年，嘉定府城震者三。
In the seventh year of Xianchun, the seat of Jiading Prefecture trembled three times.
184
雍熙三年，階州福津縣常峽山圮，壅白江水，逆流高十許丈，壞民田數百里。
In the third year of Yongxi, Changxia Mountain in Fujin County of Jie Prefecture gave way, damming the Bai River; the reversed current rose more than ten zhang and destroyed farmland for hundreds of li.
185
淳化二年五月，名山縣大風雨，登遼山圮，壅江水逆流入民田，害稼。
In the fifth month, second year of Chunhua, Mingshan County was struck by violent wind and rain; Dengliao Mountain collapsed, choking the river so that water surged backward into the fields and ruined the harvest.
186
咸平元年七月庚午，寧化軍汾水漲，壞北水門，山石摧圮，軍士有壓死者。 二年七月庚寅，靈寶縣暴雨崖圮，壓居民，死者二十二戶。 三年三月辛丑夜，大澤縣三陽砦大雨崖摧，壓死者六十二人。 四年正月，成紀縣山摧，壓死者六十餘人。
In the seventh month, first year of Xianping, on gengwu, the Fen River at Ninghua Army swelled and broke the north water gate; cliffs gave way and some troops were buried alive. In the seventh month, second year, on gengyin, Lingbao County was hit by cloudburst and cliff-fall that crushed the inhabitants—twenty-two households perished. On the night of xinchou, third month, third year, at Sanyang Stockade in Daze County a downpour brought cliffs down on sixty-two people. In the first month, fourth year, mountains at Chengji County gave way and buried more than sixty people.
187
景德四年七月，成紀縣崖圮，壓死居民。
In the seventh month, fourth year of Jingde, cliffs at Chengji County fell and buried the inhabitants.
188
熙寧五年九月丙寅，華州少華山前阜頭峰越八盤領及谷，摧陷於石子坡。 東西五里，南北十里，潰散墳裂，湧起堆阜，各高數丈，長若堤岸。 至陷居民六社，凡數百戶，林木、廬舍亦無存者。 並山之民言：“數年以來，峰上常有云，每遇風雨，即隱隱有聲。 是夜初昏，略無風雨，山上忽霧起，有聲漸大，地遂震動，不及食頃而山摧。 ”
In the ninth month, fifth year of Xining, on bingyin, at Hua Prefecture on Lesser Hua Mountain the forward peak Foutou swept over Eight Plank Ridge and the ravine and plunged into Shizi Slope. For five li east and west and ten li north and south the hillside burst and split open, heaping up mounds several zhang high, long as dikes. Six village communities, several hundred households in all, were swallowed; not a tree or a house remained. Villagers along the mountain said: "For years clouds have clung to the peak, and whenever wind and rain came a faint rumble could be heard. That night, at first dusk, there was hardly wind or rain; then mist suddenly veiled the summit, the roar swelled, the earth heaved, and in less time than a meal the mountain was gone. "
189
元祐元年十二月，鄭縣界小敷谷山摧，傷居民。
In the twelfth month of the first year of Yuanyou, Little Fugu Valley Mountain collapsed on the border of Zheng County, injuring local residents.
190
紹興十二年十二月，陝西不雨，五穀焦枯，涇、渭、灞、滻皆竭。 時秦民以飢離散，壯者為北人所買，郡邑遂空。
In the twelfth month of the twelfth year of Shaoxing, drought gripped Shaanxi: the grain crops withered, and the Jing, Wei, Ba, and Chan rivers ran dry. The people of Qin scattered in famine; able-bodied men were sold to northerners, and commanderies and counties were left deserted.
191
紹熙四年秋，南嶽祝融峰山自摧。 劍門關山摧。 五年十二月，臨安府南高峰山自摧。
In the autumn of the fourth year of Shaoxi, Zhurong Peak on the Southern Marchmount collapsed on its own. A mountain at Jianmen Pass gave way. In the twelfth month of the fifth year, Southern Peak Mountain in Lin'an Prefecture collapsed on its own.
192
慶元二年六月辛未，台州黃岩縣大雨水，有山自徙五十餘里，其聲如雷，草木、塚墓皆不動，而故址潰為淵潭。 時臨海縣清潭山亦自移。
On xinwei in the sixth month of the second year of Qingyuan, torrential rain struck Huangyan County in Taizhou, and a mountain shifted more than fifty li with a thunderous roar; plants and graves were left unmoved, but its former site collapsed into a deep pool. At the same time, Qingtan Mountain in Linhai County also shifted on its own.
193
嘉泰二年七月丁未，閩建安縣山摧，民廬之壓者六十餘家。
On dingwei in the seventh month of the second year of Jiatai, a mountain collapsed in Jian'an County in Fujian, crushing more than sixty commoners' homes.
194
嘉定六年六月丙子，嚴州淳安縣長樂鄉山摧水湧。 九年，黎州山崩。
On bingzi in the sixth month of the sixth year of Jiading, a mountain collapsed in Changle Township, Chun'an County, Yan Prefecture, and floodwaters surged forth. In the ninth year, a mountain collapsed in Li Prefecture.
195
咸淳十年，天目山崩。
In the tenth year of Xianchun, Tianmu Mountain collapsed.
196
熙寧元年，荊、襄間天雨白犛如馬尾，長者尺餘，瀰漫山谷。 三月丁酉，潭州雨毛。 八年五月丁丑，雨黃毛。
In the first year of Xining, between Jing and Xiang, white yak hair fell from the sky like horse tails, some more than a foot long, filling the valleys. In the third month, on dingyou, hair rained down on Tan Prefecture. In the fifth month of the eighth year, on dingchou, yellow hair fell from the sky.
197
紹熙四年十一月癸酉，地生毛。
On guiyou in the eleventh month of the fourth year of Shaoxi, hair sprouted from the ground.
198
咸淳九年，江南平地產白毛，臨安尤多。
In the ninth year of Xianchun, white hair sprouted on the flatlands of Jiangnan, especially in Lin'an.
