1
何灌李熙靖王雲譚世勣梅執禮程振劉延慶
He Guan, Li Xijing, Wang Yun, Tan Shiji, Mei Zhili, Cheng Zhen, and Liu Yanqing.
2
何灌，字仲源，開封祥符人。 武選登第，為河東從事。 經略使韓縝雖數試其材，而常沮抑之，不假借。 久乃語之曰：「君奇士也，他日當據吾坐。 」為府州、火山軍巡檢。 盜蘇延福狡悍，為二邊患，灌親梟其首。 賈胡疃有泉，遼人常越境而汲，灌親申畫界堠，遏其來，忿而舉兵犯我。 灌迎高射之，發輒中，或著崖石皆沒鏃，敵驚以為神，逡巡斂去。 後三十年，契丹蕭太師與灌會，道曩事，數何巡檢神射，灌曰：「即灌是也。 」蕭矍然起拜。
He Guan, styled Zhongyuan, was a native of Xiangfu in Kaifeng. He passed the military selection examination and served as an aide in Hedong. Although the frontier commissioner Han Zong repeatedly tested his abilities, he regularly held him back and never gave him a fair chance. After a long time he finally told him, "You are an extraordinary man. One day you will sit in my place." He was appointed patrol inspector of Fuzhou and the Huoshan Army. The bandit Su Yanfu was cunning and fierce, a menace on both borders, and Guan personally cut off his head. At Jiahu Tuan there was a spring that Liao subjects often crossed the border to use. Guan personally marked out the boundary posts to stop them. Enraged, they raised an army and attacked our territory. Guan met them and shot from higher ground. Every arrow found its mark, and those that struck cliff faces buried their heads deep in the rock. The enemy were awestruck and took it for divine skill; after a pause they withdrew. Thirty years later the Khitan Grand Master Xiao met Guan and spoke of old times, repeatedly praising Patrol Inspector He's uncanny marksmanship. Guan said, "That man was I." Xiao started up in astonishment and bowed to him.
3
為河東將，與夏人遇，鐵騎來追，灌射皆徹甲，至洞胸出背，疊貫後騎，羌懼而引卻。 知甯化軍、豐州，徙熙河都監，見童貫不拜，貫憾焉。 張康國薦于徽宗，召對，問西北邊事，以笏畫禦榻，指坐衣花紋為形勢。 帝曰：「敵在吾目中矣。」
Serving as a Hedong general, he met the Xia in battle. Iron cavalry pressed the pursuit, but Guan's arrows pierced every coat of mail, some passing clean through a man's chest and out his back and striking several riders in a row. The Qiang were terrified and fell back. He governed Ninghua Army and Fengzhou, then was transferred to inspector-general of Xihe. When he met Tong Guan he did not bow, and Tong took offense. Zhang Kangguo recommended him to Emperor Huizong. Called to audience and questioned about northwest frontier affairs, he traced on the imperial couch with his tablet and used the flower patterns on the Emperor's robe to illustrate the terrain. The Emperor said, "The enemy is already plain before my eyes."
4
提點河東刑獄，遷西上閣門使、領威州刺史、知滄州。 以治城鄣功，轉引進使。 詔運粟三十萬石於並塞三州，灌言：「水淺不勝舟，陸當用車八千乘，沿邊方登麥，願以運費增價就糴之。 」奏上，報可。 安撫使忌之，劾雲板築未畢而冒賞，奪所遷官，仍再貶秩，罷去。
While serving as judicial intendant of Hedong, he was promoted to West Upper Gate envoy, given concurrent charge of Weizhou, and made administrator of Cangzhou. For his achievements in repairing walls and fortifications, he was transferred to the post of Introducing Envoy. An edict ordered three hundred thousand piculs of grain transported to the three border prefectures of Bing. Guan said, "The water is too shallow for boats, and overland transport would require eight thousand carts. The frontier is just entering the wheat harvest. I ask that the transport costs be used instead to buy grain locally at a higher price." His memorial was submitted and approved. The pacification commissioner envied him and impeached him for claiming reward before the cloud-plank fortifications were finished. He was stripped of his newly granted office, demoted two more ranks, and dismissed.
5
未幾，知岷州，引邈川水溉間田千頃，湟人號廣利渠。 徙河州，復守岷，提舉熙河蘭湟弓箭手。 入言：「漢金城、湟中谷斛八錢，今西寧、湟、廓即其地也，漢、唐故渠尚可考。 若先葺渠引水，使田不病旱，則人樂應募，而射士之額足矣。 」從之。 甫半歲，得善田二萬六千頃，募士七千四百人，為他路最。 童貫用兵西邊，灌取古骨龍馬進武軍，加吉州防禦使，改知蘭州。 又攻仁多泉城，炮傷足不顧，卒拔城，斬首五百級。 尋改廓州防禦使。
Before long he was made prefect of Minzhou. He diverted Miaochuan water to irrigate a thousand mu of scattered fields, and the people of Huang called the channel the Broad Benefit Canal. He was transferred to Hezhou, then returned to defend Min and was put in charge of the Xihe, Lan, and Huang archer corps. He went to court and said, "In Han times grain in Jincheng and Huangzhong sold for eight cash per picul. Present-day Xining, Huang, and Kuo occupy that same region, and the old Han and Tang canals can still be traced." If we first repair the canals and bring in water so the fields are spared drought, people will gladly enlist, and the quota of archer soldiers will be filled. The court accepted his proposal. In barely half a year he brought under cultivation twenty-six thousand mu of good land and recruited seventy-four hundred soldiers, the highest total of any circuit. When Tong Guan campaigned on the western frontier, Guan captured Gugulong and entered Jinwu Army. He was made defender of Jizhou and transferred to administer Lanzhou. He also attacked Rendequan city. Though wounded in the foot by cannon fire, he paid no attention and finally took the city, beheading five hundred men. Soon afterward he was made defender of Kuozhou.
6
宣和初，劉法陷於敵，震武危甚，熙帥劉仲武使灌往救。 灌以眾寡不敵，但張虛聲駭之，夏人宵遁。 灌恐覘其實，遽反兵，仲武猶奏其逗遛，罷為淮西鈐轄。 從平方臘，獲賊帥呂師囊，遷同州觀察使、浙東都鈐轄，改浙西。
Early in the Xuanhe era Liu Fa was lost to the enemy and Zhenwu was in grave danger, so the Xi commander Liu Zhongwu sent Guan to the rescue. Outnumbered, Guan merely put on a show of force to frighten them, and the Xia withdrew by night. Fearing the enemy would discover how weak he really was, Guan hurriedly turned his army back. Zhongwu still memorialized that he had dragged his feet, and he was removed from office and made Huai West commander. He took part in the pacification of Fang La and captured the rebel leader Lü Shichong. He was promoted to military commissioner of Tongzhou and commander of Eastern Zhejiang, then transferred to Western Zhejiang.
7
童貫北征，檄統制兵馬，涿、易平，以知易州，遷甯武軍承宣使、燕山路副都總管，又加龍、神衛都指揮使。 夔離不取景州，圍薊州。 貫諉以兵事，即復景城，釋薊圍。 郭藥師統蕃、漢兵，灌白：「頃年折氏歸朝，朝廷別置一司，專部漢兵，至於克行，乃許同營。 今但宜令藥師主常勝軍，而以漢兵委灌輩。 」貫不聽。 召還，管幹步軍司。
When Tong Guan marched north, Guan was ordered to take command of the troops. After Zhuo and Yi were pacified he was made administrator of Yizhou, promoted to Ningwu Army commissioner and deputy overall commander of Yan Road, and then also made commander-in-chief of the Dragon and Divine Guards. Aguda did not take Jingzhou and instead besieged Jizhou. Tong Guan blamed him for the military situation, whereupon he immediately recovered Jing city and lifted the siege of Jizhou. Guo Yaoshi commanded both Tangut and Han troops. Guan advised, "When the Zhe clan submitted years ago, the court set up a separate office to command Han troops alone. Only at Kexing were the two allowed to camp together." Now Yaoshi alone should command the Ever-Victorious Army, and the Han troops should be entrusted to men like myself. Tong Guan would not listen. He was recalled to manage the Bureau of Infantry.
8
陪遼使射玉津園，一發破的，再發則否。 客曰：「太尉不能耶？ 」曰：「非也，以禮讓客耳。 」整弓復中之，觀者誦歎，帝親賜酒勞之。 遷步軍都虞候。
While accompanying a Liao envoy in archery at Jade Ford Garden, his first shot hit the target, but the second did not. The guest said, "Can the Grand Marshal not do it?" He replied, "It is not that. I was simply yielding to our guest out of courtesy." He readied his bow and hit the target again. The onlookers broke into admiring cries, and the Emperor personally bestowed wine to reward him. He was promoted to vice commander of the infantry.
9
金師南下，悉出禁旅付梁方平守黎陽。 灌謂宰相白時中曰：「金人傾國遠至，其鋒不可當。 今方平掃精銳以北，萬有一不枝梧，何以善吾後，盍留以衛根本。 」不從，明日，又命灌行，辭以軍不堪戰，強之，拜武泰軍節度使、河東河北制置副使。 未及行而帝內禪，灌領兵入衛。 鄆王楷至門欲入，灌曰：「大事已定，王何所受命而來？ 」導者懼而退。 灌竟行，援兵二萬不能足，聽募民充數。
When the Jin armies marched south, all the palace troops were sent out under Liang Fangping to hold Liyang. Guan told Grand Councilor Bai Shizhong, "The Jurchens have come from afar with their whole nation behind them. Their onslaught cannot be withstood." Now Fangping is sweeping the elite troops north. If by any chance they cannot hold, how will we secure our rear? We ought to keep forces here to guard the capital itself." They did not listen. The next day Guan was ordered to march again. He declined on the grounds that the army was unfit for battle, but was forced to go and was appointed military commissioner of Wutai Army and deputy frontier commissioner of Hedong and Hebei. Before he could set out the Emperor abdicated, and Guan led troops in to guard the palace. Prince Yun Wang Kai came to the gate wishing to enter. Guan said, "The great matter is already settled. By what commission does Your Highness come?" The guide was frightened and withdrew. Guan set out all the same, but the relief force of twenty thousand could not be filled, and he was permitted to recruit civilians to make up the numbers.
10
靖康元年正月二日，次滑州，方平南奔，灌亦望風迎潰。 黃河南岸無一人禦敵，金師遂直叩京城。 灌至，乞入見，不許，而令控守西隅。 背城拒戰凡三日，被創，沒於陣，年六十二。 帳下韓綜、雷彥興，奇士也，各手殺數人，從以死。 欽宗哀悼，賜金帛，命官護葬。 已而言者論其不守河津，追削官秩。
On the second day of the first month of the first year of Jingkang he halted at Huazhou. Fangping fled south, and Guan too broke before the enemy's advance. South of the Yellow River not a single man stood to resist the enemy, and the Jin armies marched straight on the capital. When Guan arrived he begged for an audience but was refused, and was ordered to hold the western sector of the defenses. He fought with his back to the wall for three days, was wounded, and died in battle at the age of sixty-two. Under his command were Han Zong and Lei Yanxing, both extraordinary men. Each killed several enemies with his own hands and died with him. Emperor Qinzong mourned him, bestowed gold and silk, and ordered officials to escort the funeral. Later critics argued that he had failed to hold the river crossing, and his ranks were posthumously stripped.
11
長子薊，至閣門宣贊舍人。 從父戰，箭貫左臂，拔出之，病創死。 紹興四年，中子蘚以灌事泣訴於朝，詔復履正大夫、忠正軍承宣使。
His eldest son Ji rose to the post of Gate Proclamation Registrar of the Inner Gate. He followed his father into battle. An arrow pierced his left arm; he pulled it out and died of the wound. In the fourth year of Shaoxing his second son Xian wept before the court over Guan's case, and an edict restored his ranks of Lüzheng Daifu and Zhongzheng Army commissioner.
12
李熙靖
Li Xijing
13
李熙靖，字子安，常州晉陵人，唐衛公德裕九世孫也。 祖均、父公弼皆進士第。 公弼，崇甯初通判潞州，以議三舍法不便，使者劾其沮格詔令，坐削黜以死。 熙靖擢第，又中詞學兼茂，選為辟雍錄、太學正，升博士。 以父老丐外，除提舉淮東學事便養，命下，乃得河東； 而為淮東者，臧祐之也。 蓋省吏取祐之賂，輒易之。 或教使自言，熙靖曰：「事君不擇地，吾其可發人之私，求自便也？ 」宰相聞而賢之，留為兵部員外郎。 遭父憂去，還，為右司員外郎。
Li Xijing, styled Zi'an, was a native of Jinling in Changzhou and a ninth-generation descendant of Tang Duke of Wei Li Deyu. His grandfather Jun and his father Gongbi both passed the jinshi examination. Gongbi, early in the Chongning era while serving as deputy prefect of Lu, argued that the Three Hall system was inconvenient. Envoys impeached him for obstructing edicts, and he was demoted and died in disgrace. Xijing passed the examinations and also passed the Polished Literary Learning examination. He was selected as director of the Imperial College and senior examiner, then promoted to erudite. Citing his aged father, he sought a post outside the capital and was appointed education commissioner of Huai East so he could care for his parents nearby. When the order came down, however, he received Hedong instead. The man appointed to Huai East was Zang Youzhi. A provincial clerk had taken a bribe from Youzhi and switched the appointments. Someone urged him to speak up for himself. Xijing said, "In serving one's lord one does not choose the place of service. How could I expose another man's secret just to seek my own convenience?" The grand councilor heard of this and admired him, and he was kept at court as vice minister of war. After mourning his father's death he left office. When he returned he served as vice director of the Right Office.
14
王黼乙太宰領應奉司，又方事燕雲，立經撫房於中書獨專之，他執政皆不得預。 熙靖與言曰：「應奉之職，非宰相所當預。 尚書、樞密皆有兵房，足以治疆事，經撫何為者哉？ 」黼積不樂。 同列五人皆躐躋禁從，獨滯留四年。 都水丞失職，移過於熙靖，貶其兩秩，又將左轉為國子司業，執政交言不可，僅遷太常少卿。 黼罷，乃拜中書舍人，蔡攸又惡之，出知拱州。
Wang Fu, as Grand Chancellor, also headed the Tribute Service. With Yan-Yun affairs his main concern, he established a frontier pacification office in the Central Secretariat and kept sole control of it, while the other chief ministers were excluded. Xijing spoke to him and said, "The Tribute Service is not a duty a chancellor should oversee." The ministries and the Military Affairs Commission both have military offices sufficient to handle frontier affairs. What need is there for a frontier pacification office?" Fu grew increasingly displeased. Five colleagues all vaulted into high attendant posts, while he alone remained stuck in place for four years. When a director of waterways lost his post through negligence, the blame was shifted to Xijing. He was demoted two ranks and was about to be transferred down to vice director of the Imperial University, but the chief ministers protested, and he was only moved to vice director of rituals. When Fu fell from power, Xijing was appointed Secretariat drafter. Cai You also hated him and had him sent out to administer Gongzhou.
15
越兩月，復以故官召，入對言：「燕山雖定，宜益謹思患豫防之戒。 」徽宗曰：「《詩》所謂'迨天之未陰雨，徹彼桑土，綢繆牖戶'者是也。 」熙靖進曰：「孔子云：'為此詩者，其知道乎！ 能治其國家，誰敢侮之？ '願陛下為無疆之計。 」帝嘉之。
Two months later he was recalled to his former office. At audience he said, "Although the Yan Mountains are settled, we should be all the more careful to guard against trouble before it comes." Emperor Huizong said, "That is what the Odes mean when they say, 'Before heaven's rain clouds gather, gather mulberry bark and line window and door.'" Xijing advanced and said, "Confucius said, 'The composer of this poem—did he know the Way! Who can govern his state well and still be insulted?'" I wish Your Majesty would plan for lasting security without limit." The Emperor praised him.
16
靖康初，同譚世勣事龍德宮，改顯謨閣待制、提舉醴泉觀。 道君待之甚厚，常從容及內禪事，曰：「外人以為吳敏功，殊不知此自出吾意耳，吾苟不欲，人言且滅族，誰敢哉？ 或謂吾似唐睿宗上畏天戒，故為之，吾有此心久矣。 」熙靖再拜賀。 敏聞而忌之，以進對不時受罰。
Early in the Jingkang era he served Longde Palace together with Tan Shiji. He was made Xianmo Pavilion attendant and director of Liquan Abbey. The Retired Emperor treated him very generously and often spoke at ease of the abdication. He said, "Outsiders credit Wu Min, but they do not know this came from my own intent. If I had not wished it, any word against it would have meant the destruction of my clan. Who would have dared?" Some say I resemble Tang Ruizong yielding from fear of heaven's warning, but I had this in mind long ago." Xijing bowed twice in congratulation. Min heard of this and resented it, and Xijing was penalized for an untimely audience.
17
既拒張邦昌之命，憂憤廢食，家人進粥藥寬譬之，終無生意。 故人視其病，相持啜泣，索筆書唐王維所賦「百官何日再朝天」之句，明日遂卒，年五十三，與世勣同贈端明殿學士。
Having refused Zhang Bangchang's commission, he was so grief-stricken and angry that he stopped eating. His family brought porridge and medicine to comfort him, but in the end he had no will to live. An old friend visited him in his illness, and they wept holding each other. He asked for a brush and wrote the line from Wang Wei's poem, "When will the officials again attend court at dawn?" The next day he died at the age of fifty-three. Together with Shiji he was posthumously made an academician of the Hall of Endpointal Brightness.
18
王雲，字子飛，澤州人。 父獻可，仕至英州刺史、知瀘州。 黃庭堅謫於涪，獻可遇之甚厚，時人稱之。 雲舉進士，從使高麗，撰《雞林志》以進。 擢秘書省校書郎，出知簡州，遷陝西轉運副使。 宣和中，從童貫宣撫幕，入為兵部員外郎、起居中書舍人。
Wang Yun, styled Zifei, was a native of Zezhou. His father Xianke served to the posts of prefect of Yingzhou and administrator of Luzhou. When Huang Tingjian was exiled to Fu, Xianke treated him very generously, and people of the time praised him for it. Yun passed the jinshi examination, accompanied a mission to Goryeo, and composed Records of Goguryeo Chicken Forest, which he submitted to the throne. He was promoted to collator in the Secretariat, sent out to administer Jianzhou, and then transferred to vice commissioner of Shaanxi transport. During the Xuanhe era he served on Tong Guan's pacification staff, then entered court as vice minister of war and drafting attendant of the Secretariat.
19
靖康元年，以給事中使斡離不軍，議割三鎮以和。 使還，傳道斡離不之意，以為黏罕得朝廷所與餘睹蠟書，堅雲中國不可信，欲敗和約。 執政以為不然，罷為徽猷閣待制、知唐州。
In the first year of Jingkang he was sent as supervising censor to Wolibu's army to negotiate the cession of the three prefectures in exchange for peace. On his return he relayed Wolibu's message: Nianhan had obtained the wax-sealed letter the court had sent to Yutu and insisted that Song China could not be trusted, and now wished to break the peace treaty. The chief ministers did not believe him, and he was dismissed to serve as Huiyao Pavilion attendant and administrator of Tangzhou.
20
金人陷太原，召拜刑部尚書，再出使，許以三鎮賦入之數。 雲至真定，遣從吏李裕還言：「金人不復求地，但索五輅及上尊號，且須康王來，和好乃成。 」欽宗悉從之，且命王及馮澥往。 未行，而車輅至長垣，為所卻，雲亦還。 澥奏言雲誕妄誤國，雲言：「事勢中變，金人必欲得三鎮，不然，則進兵取汴都。 」中外震駭，詔集百官議，雲固言：「康王舊與斡離不結歡，宜將命。 」帝慮為所留，雲曰：「和議既成，必無留王之理，臣敢以百口保之。 」王遂受命，而雲以資政殿學士為之副。
When the Jurchens took Taiyuan he was summoned and appointed minister of justice, sent on another mission, and authorized to offer the tax revenue of the three prefectures. Yun reached Zhending and sent his attendant Li Yu back with word that the Jurchens no longer demanded territory, but only wanted the five imperial carriages and an honorific title for the Emperor, and that Prince Kang must come in person before peace could be concluded. Emperor Qinzong agreed to everything and ordered the Prince and Feng Xi to go. Before they could set out the imperial carriages reached Changyuan and were turned back, and Yun returned as well. Xi memorialized that Yun's reckless falsehoods had harmed the state. Yun said, "The situation has changed midway. The Jurchens will insist on having the three prefectures, or else they will advance and take the capital at Bian." Court and country were shaken, and an edict summoned all officials to deliberate. Yun insisted, "Prince Kang once formed friendly ties with Wolibu and should carry the commission." The Emperor feared the Prince would be detained. Yun said, "Once peace is concluded there can be no reason to detain the Prince. I stake the lives of my whole household on it." The Prince then accepted the commission, with Yun serving as his deputy in the capacity of Zizheng Hall academician.
21
頃雲奉使過磁、相，勸兩郡徹近城民舍，運粟入保，為清野之計，民怨之。 及是，次磁州，又與守臣宗澤有憾。 於是王出謁嘉應神祠，雲在後，民遮道諫曰：「肅王已為金人所留，王不宜北去。 」厲聲指雲曰：「清野之人，真奸賊也。 」王出廟行，或發雲笥，得烏絁短巾，蓋雲夙有風眩疾，寢則以護首者。 民益信其為奸，噪而殺之。 王見事勢洶洶，乃南還相州。 是役也，雲不死，王必北行，議者以是驗天命雲。 建炎初，贈觀文殿學士。
Earlier, when Yun went on mission through Ci and Xiang, he urged both prefectures to demolish dwellings near the cities, move grain into fortified places, and adopt a scorched-earth policy. The people resented him for it. On this occasion, when they halted at Cizhou, he also had a grudge against the defending official Zong Ze. Thereupon the Prince went out to pay respects at the Jiaying Spirit Shrine with Yun following behind. The people blocked the road and remonstrated, "Prince Su has already been detained by the Jurchens. Your Highness should not go north." They shouted and pointed at Yun, "This man of the scorched-earth policy is a true traitor." As the Prince left the shrine and set out, someone opened Yun's trunk and found a black silk headcloth. Yun had long suffered from vertigo and wore it to protect his head when he slept. The people were all the more convinced he was a traitor. They raised a clamor and killed him. Seeing how turbulent matters had become, the Prince turned south and returned to Xiangzhou. In this affair, had Yun not died the Prince would certainly have gone north. Commentators took this as proof that Heaven had ordained Yun's death. Early in the Jianyan era he was posthumously made Guanwen Hall academician.
22
雲兄霽，崇甯時，為謀議司詳議官，上書告蔡京罪，黥隸海島。 欽宗復其官，從種師中戰死。
Yun's elder brother Ji, during the Chongning era, served as detailed reviewer in the Policy Deliberation Office. He submitted a memorial accusing Cai Jing of crimes and was tattooed and exiled to an island. Emperor Qinzong restored his office. He followed Zhong Shizhong and died in battle.
23
譚世勣
Tan Shiji
24
譚世勣字彥成，潭州長沙人。 第進士，教授郴州。 時王氏學盛行，世勣雅不喜。 或問之，曰：「說多而屢變，無不易之論也。 」置其書不觀。 又中詞學兼茂科，除秘書省正字。 時相蔡京子攸領書局，同舍郎多翕附以取貴仕。 世勣獨坐直廬，繙書竟日。 梁師成之客與為鄰居，數致師成願交意，謝不答。
Tan Shiji, styled Yancheng, was a native of Changsha in Tanzhou. He passed the jinshi examination and served as instructor in Chenzhou. At the time Wang Anshi's learning was in full vogue, and Shiji had never cared for it. When someone asked him about it he said, "Its doctrines are many and constantly changing. There is no settled teaching that does not shift." He put their books aside and would not read them. He also passed the Polished Literary Learning examination and was appointed corrector in the Secretariat. At the time Chancellor Cai Jing's son You headed the book bureau, and many fellow secretaries fawned on him to obtain high office. Shiji alone sat in the duty hall and read books all day long. A client of Liang Shicheng lived nearby and repeatedly conveyed Liang's wish to become acquainted. Shiji declined and did not respond.
25
在館六年不遷，京罷，用久次為司門員外郎。 又三年，遷吏部。 京復相，嫌不附己，罷提點太平宮。 久之，復還吏部。 幸臣妄引恩澤任子，持不與。 吏白有某例，世勣曰：「豈當以暫例破成法！ 」已而取中旨行之。 進少府監，擢中書舍人，以謹命令、惜名器、廣言路、吝賜予、正上供、省浮費六事言於上，又為當路所嫉。 以徽猷閣待制知婺州，未行，復留之。
He spent six years in the bureau without promotion. When Jing fell from power Shiji was appointed vice director of the Gate Office by seniority. Three years later he was transferred to the Ministry of Personnel. When Jing returned as chancellor he resented that Shiji did not support him and dismissed him to the post of director of Taiping Palace. After a long while he returned to the Ministry of Personnel. A favored minister falsely invoked an imperial grace to appoint his son to office, and Shiji refused to approve it. The clerks reported that there was a precedent. Shiji said, "How can a temporary precedent be used to break established law!" Soon afterward an edict from the inner court was obtained and the appointment was carried out anyway. He was promoted to director of the Palace Workshop and then elevated to Secretariat drafter. He spoke to the throne on six matters: strict commands, cherishing offices, broadening the avenue of remonstrance, restraining grants, correcting tribute deliveries, and reducing wasteful spending. Once again he aroused the jealousy of those in power. He was appointed Huiyao Pavilion attendant and administrator of Wuzhou, but before he could set out he was kept at court again.
26
徽宗禪位東幸，且還，使與李熙靖副執政奉迎，遂同主管龍德宮。 請辨正宣仁國史之謗，述欽聖遺旨以復瑤華，大享神祖仍用富弼侑食，釋奠先聖不當以王安石配，後皆施行。
When Emperor Huizong abdicated and traveled east, and then returned, Shiji and Li Xijing were made deputies to the chief ministers to welcome him back, and together they took charge of Longde Palace. He requested that the slanders in Empress Xuanren's official history be corrected, set forth Empress Qinsheng's final instructions to restore Consort Yaohua, that the grand sacrifice to Emperor Shenzong still use Fu Bi as attendant spirit, and that Wang Anshi should not be enshrined with the Sage at the Confucian sacrifice. All of these proposals were later carried out.
27
秋七月，彗出東方，大臣或謂此四夷將衰之兆，世勣面奏：「垂象可畏，當修德以應天，不宜惑諛說。 」進給事中兼侍讀。 內侍喧爭殿門，詔以贖論，世勣駁其不恭，因言：「童貫輩初亦甚微，小惡不懲，將馴至大患。 」疏入，同類側目。 何栗建議分外郡為四道，置都總管，事得顓決。 世勣言：「裂天下以付四人，而王畿所治者才十六縣，獨無尾大不掉之慮乎？ 」栗不樂。 改禮部侍郎。
In the seventh month of autumn a comet appeared in the east. Some ministers said this was a sign that the four barbarians would decline. Shiji addressed the throne in person, "Heaven's signs are to be feared. We should cultivate virtue to respond to Heaven and must not be misled by flattery." He was promoted to supervising censor and concurrent reader-in-waiting. Inner attendants quarreled noisily at the palace gate, and an edict ordered that the matter be settled by payment of a fine. Shiji rebutted this as disrespectful and said, "Men like Tong Guan were at first very insignificant. If small wrongs are not punished, they will gradually grow into great calamities." When the memorial was submitted, his colleagues glared at him. He Li proposed dividing the outer prefectures into four circuits and appointing overall commanders with sole decision-making power. Shiji said, "To split the realm among four men while the imperial domain governs only sixteen counties—is there no fear of a tail too heavy to wag?" Li was displeased. He was transferred to vice minister of rites.
28
金騎駸駸南下，世勣言：「守邊為上策； 今邊不得守，守河則京畿自固，中策也； 巡幸江、淮，會東南兵以捍敵，下策也。 金人既渡河，又請遣大將秦元以所部京畿保甲，分護國門，使兵勢連屬，首尾相援，即金人不敢逼。 孫傅深然之，又格于栗議。 再扈車駕至金帥帳，以十害說其用事者，言講解之利，詞意忠激，金人聳聽。
As Jin cavalry pressed swiftly south, Shiji said, "Defending the frontier is the best strategy; now that the frontier cannot be held, defending the river will naturally secure the capital region. That is the middle strategy; touring the Yangzi and Huai and gathering southeastern troops to resist the enemy is the worst strategy. Once the Jurchens had crossed the river, he also requested that the great general Qin Yuan be sent with his capital-region militia to guard the city gates in sections, so that military strength would be linked from end to end and mutually support one another. Then the Jurchens would not dare press close. Sun Fu strongly agreed, but the proposal was blocked by Li's faction. He again accompanied the imperial carriage to the Jin commander's tent and set forth ten harms to those in power there, speaking of the benefits of negotiated settlement. His words were loyal and impassioned, and the Jurchens listened with rapt attention.
29
張邦昌僭國，令與李熙靖同直學士院，皆稱疾臥不起，以憂卒，年五十四。 建炎初，褒其守節，贈端明殿學士。
When Zhang Bangchang usurped the throne he was ordered to serve in the Hanlin Academy together with Li Xijing. Both claimed illness and would not rise. Shiji died of grief at the age of fifty-four. Early in the Jianyan era his steadfast integrity was praised, and he was posthumously made an academician of the Hall of Endpointal Brightness.
30
梅執禮
Mei Zhili
31
梅執禮，字和勝，婺州浦江人。 第進士，調常山尉未赴，以薦為敕令刪定官、武學博士。 大司成強淵明賢其人，為宰相言，相以未嘗識面為慊。 執禮聞之曰：「以人言而得，必以人言而失，吾求在我者而已。 」卒不往謁。
Mei Zhili, styled Hesheng, was a native of Pujiang in Wuzhou. He passed the jinshi examination and was assigned as magistrate of Changshan but did not take up the post. Through recommendation he became an editor of edicts and commands and military academy erudite. Grand director Qiang Yuanming admired him and spoke of him to the chancellor, who regretted that he had never met him in person. When Zhili heard this he said, "What is gained through others' words can be lost through others' words. I seek only what depends on myself." In the end he never went to pay his respects.
32
曆軍器、鴻臚丞，比部員外郎，比部職勾稽財貨，文牘山委，率不暇經目。 苑吏有持茶券至為錢三百萬者，以楊戩旨意迫取甚急。 執禮一閱，知其妄，欲白之，長貳疑不敢，乃獨列上，界詐也。 改度支、吏部，進國子司業兼資善堂翊善，遷左司員外郎，擢中書舍人、給事中。
He served as aide in the Armory and in the Office of Ceremonial, then as vice director in the Ministry of Revenue's comparison bureau, whose duty was to audit goods and funds. Documents piled up like mountains and generally no one had time to examine them. A garden official presented a tea certificate redeemable for three million cash and, acting on Yang Jian's orders, pressed for payment with great urgency. Zhili read it once and knew it was fraudulent. He wished to report it, but his superiors hesitated and dared not act, so he alone submitted a memorial declaring it a fraud. He was transferred to the revenue and personnel bureaus, promoted to vice director of the Imperial University and concurrent tutor in the Hall of Goodness, moved to vice director of the Left Office, and elevated to Secretariat drafter and supervising censor.
33
林攄以前執政赴闕宿留，冀復故職，執禮論去之。 孟昌齡居鄆質人屋，當贖不肯與，而請中旨奪之，外郡卒留役中都者萬數，肆不逞為奸，詔悉令還，楊戩占不遣； 內侍張佑董葺太廟，僭求賞：皆駁奏弗行。 遷禮部侍郎。
Lin Shu, a former chief minister, came to the capital and lingered, hoping to recover his old office. Zhili memorialized to have him removed. Meng Changling lived in a house in Yun pledged to another man and, when it came time to redeem it, refused to surrender it, yet requested an inner-court edict to seize it. Tens of thousands of conscripts from outer prefectures remained in service in the capital and ran wild committing crimes. An edict ordered them all sent home, but Yang Jian kept them and would not release them. The inner attendant Zhang You oversaw repairs to the Imperial Ancestral Temple and presumptuously sought reward. Zhili rebutted all of these in memorials and none were approved. He was transferred to vice minister of rites.
34
素與王黼善，黼嘗置酒其第，誇示園池妓妾之盛，有驕色。 執禮曰：「公為宰相，當與天下同憂樂。 今方臘流毒吳地，瘡痍未息，是豈歌舞宴樂時乎？ 」退又戒之以詩。 黼愧怒，會孟饗原廟後至，以顯謨閣待制知蘄州，又奪職。
He had long been on good terms with Wang Fu. Fu once held a banquet at his residence and displayed the splendor of his gardens, ponds, and courtesans with a proud air. Zhili said, "As chancellor you should share the worries and joys of the realm. Now Fang La's poison is spreading through Wu territory and the wounds have not yet healed. Is this a time for song, dance, and feasting?" On leaving he also admonished him with a poem. Fu was ashamed and angry. When Zhili arrived late for the sacrifice at the Original Temple, he was made Xianmo Pavilion attendant and administrator of Qizhou, and then stripped of office.
35
明年，徙滁州，復集英殿修撰。 時賦鹽虧額，滁亦苦抑配。 執禮曰：「郡不能當蘇、杭一邑，而食鹽乃倍粟數，民何以堪？ 」請於朝，詔損二十萬，滁人德之。
The next year he was transferred to Chuzhou and restored as compiler in the Hall for Assembling Excellence. At the time salt tax quotas fell short, and Chuzhou also suffered from forced apportionment. Zhili said, "This prefecture is not equal to a single district of Su or Hang, yet the salt levy is double the grain quota. How can the people bear it?" He petitioned the court, and an edict reduced the levy by two hundred thousand. The people of Chu were grateful.
36
欽宗立，徙知鎮江府，召為翰林學士，道除吏部尚書，旋改戶部。 方軍興，調度不足，執禮請以禁內錢隸有司，凡六宮廩給，皆由度支乃得下。 嘗有小黃門持中批詣部取錢，而封識不用璽，既悟其失，復取之。 執禮奏審，詔責典寶夫人而杖黃門。
When Emperor Qinzong ascended the throne Zhili was transferred to administer Zhenjiang Prefecture, summoned as Hanlin academician, appointed minister of personnel en route, and soon transferred to minister of revenue. With war underway supplies were insufficient. Zhili requested that inner-palace funds be placed under regular offices, so that all provisions for the six palaces had to pass through the revenue bureau before disbursement. Once a junior eunuch came to the ministry with an inner-court draft to withdraw funds, but the seal on the wrapper was not the imperial seal. Realizing the error, he took it back. Zhili memorialized for investigation. An edict rebuked the lady keeper of the seals and had the eunuch beaten.
37
金人圍京都，執禮勸帝親征，而請太上帝后、皇后、太子皆出避，用事者沮之。 洎失守，金人質天子，邀金帛以數百千萬計，曰：「和議已定，但所需滿數，則奉天子還闕。 」執禮與同列陳知質、程振、安扶皆主根索，四人哀民力已困，相與謀曰：「金人所欲無藝極，雖銅鐵亦不能給，盍以軍法結罪，儻窒其求。 」而宦者挾宿怨語金帥曰：「城中七百萬戶，所取未百一，但許民持金銀換粟麥，當有出者。 」已而果然。 酋怒，呼四人責之，對曰：「天子蒙塵，臣民皆願致死，雖肝腦不計，于金繒何有哉？ 顧比屋枵空，亡以塞命耳。 」酋問官長何在，振恐執禮獲罪，遂前曰：「皆官長也。 」酋益怒，先取其副胡舜陟、胡唐老、姚舜明、王俁，各杖之百。 執禮等猶為之請，俄遣還，將及門，呼下馬撾殺之，而梟其首，時靖康二年二月也。 是日，天宇晝冥，士庶皆隕涕憤歎。
When the Jurchens besieged the capital Zhili urged the Emperor to lead the campaign in person and requested that the Retired Emperor, Empress Dowager, Empress, and Crown Prince all leave to take refuge. Those in power blocked the proposal. When the city fell the Jurchens held the Son of Heaven hostage and demanded gold and silk in the tens of millions, saying, "Peace is already settled. Once the full amount is supplied the Son of Heaven will be returned to the palace." Zhili, together with his colleagues Chen Zhizhi, Cheng Zhen, and An Fu, all directed the search for funds. The four men grieved that the people's strength was already exhausted and consulted together, "The Jurchens' demands know no limit. Even copper and iron could not satisfy them. Why not use military law to fix culpability and perhaps block their demands?" But eunuchs bearing old grudges told the Jin commander, "There are seven million households in the city. What has been taken is not yet one in a hundred. Just allow the people to exchange gold and silver for grain and wheat, and contributions will come forth." Soon afterward it proved so. The chieftain was enraged and summoned the four men to rebuke them. They replied, "The Son of Heaven suffers in exile. Officials and people all wish to die for the cause. Even livers and brains are not counted. What of gold and silk?" Only every household is empty and hollow. There is nothing with which to fulfill the command. The chieftain asked where the chief officials were. Zhen, fearing Zhili would be punished, stepped forward and said, "We are all chief officials." The chieftain grew still angrier and first seized his deputies Hu Shunzhi, Hu Tanglao, Yao Shunming, and Wang Hou, beating each a hundred strokes. Zhili and the others still pleaded for them. Soon they were sent back, and just as they reached the gate they were called down from their horses, beaten to death, and their heads displayed. This was in the second month of the second year of Jingkang. That day the sky darkened at noon, and officials and commoners all wept tears of grief and indignation.
38
初，車駕再出，執禮與宗室子昉、諸將吳革等謀集兵奪萬勝門，夜搗金帥帳，迎二帝以歸。 而王時雍、徐秉哲使範瓊泄其謀，故不克。 死時，年四十九。 高宗即位，詔贈通奉大夫、端明殿學士。 議者以為薄，復加資政殿學士。
Earlier, when the imperial carriage went out again, Zhili, together with the imperial clansman Zifang and the generals Wu Ge and others, plotted to gather troops, seize Wansheng Gate, raid the Jin commander's tent by night, and bring the two emperors back. But Wang Shiyong and Xu Bingzhe had Fan Qiong reveal the plot, and so it failed. At his death he was forty-nine. When Emperor Gaozong ascended the throne, an edict posthumously granted him the ranks of Tongfeng Daifu and academician of the Hall of Endpointal Brightness. Commentators thought this too meager, and he was further made Zizheng Hall academician.
39
程振，字伯起，饒州樂平人。 少有軼材，入太學，一時名輩多從之遊。 徽宗幸學，以諸生右職除官，為辟雍錄，升博士，遷太常博士，提舉京東、西路學事。 請立廟于鄒祀孟軻，以公孫醜、萬章、樂正克等配食，從之。
Cheng Zhen, styled Boqi, was a native of Leping in Raozhou. In youth he had extraordinary talent. He entered the Imperial College, and many leading men of the time sought his company. When Emperor Huizong visited the college, Zhen, as a student of outstanding merit, was granted office. He served as director of the Imperial College, was promoted to erudite, transferred to erudite of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices, and directed education in the eastern and western circuits of the capital region. He requested that a temple be established at Zou to sacrifice to Mencius, with Gongsun Chou, Wan Zhang, Yuezheng Ke, and others as attendant spirits, and the court approved.
40
提舉京西常平，入為膳部員外郎、監察御史、辟雍國子司業、左司員外郎兼太子舍人。 始至，即言：「古者大祭禮登餕受爵，必以上嗣，既《禮經》所載，且元豐彝典具存。 昨天子展事明堂，而殿下不預，非所以尊宗廟、重社稷也。 」太子矍然曰：「宮僚初無及此者。 」由是特加獎異。
He directed the western capital-region granary reserve, then entered court as vice director in the Board of Provisions, investigating censor, vice director of the Imperial College and National University, and vice director of the Left Office concurrently attendant to the Crown Prince. As soon as he arrived he said, "In antiquity at the great sacrifice, when ascending to receive the sacrificial meat and accept the cup, the heir apparent had to be present. This is recorded in the Book of Rites, and the Yuanfeng canonical regulations still survive intact. Yesterday the Son of Heaven performed the rites at the Bright Hall, yet Your Highness did not participate. This is not the way to honor the ancestral temples and esteem the altars of soil and grain. The Crown Prince started and said, "None of the palace staff had ever raised this." Thereupon he was specially praised and favored.
41
方臘起，振謂王黼宜乘此時建革天下弊事，以上當天意，下順人心。 黼不懌，曰：「上且疑黼挾寇，奈何？ 」振知黼忌其言，趨而出，然太子薦之甚力，遂擢給事中。 黼白振資淺，且雅長書命，請以為中書舍人。 侍郎馮熙載出知亳州，黼怨熙載，欲振詆以醜語，振不肯。 黼使言者劾為黨，罷提舉沖佑觀。 居三年，復還故官。
When Fang La rose in rebellion Zhen told Wang Fu that he ought to seize this moment to reform the abuses of the realm, thereby answering Heaven above and winning hearts below. Fu was displeased and said, "The Emperor already suspects that I am exploiting the bandits. What can be done?" Zhen knew Fu resented his words and hurried out, but the Crown Prince recommended him vigorously, and he was elevated to supervising censor. Fu reported that Zhen's seniority was shallow and that he excelled at drafting documents, and requested that he be made Secretariat drafter instead. Vice minister Feng Xizai was sent out to administer Bozhou. Fu resented Xizai and wanted Zhen to slander him with ugly words, but Zhen refused. Fu had critics impeach him as a factional partisan, and he was dismissed to the post of director of Chongyou Abbey. After three years he returned to his former office.
42
靖康元年，進吏部侍郎，為欽宗言：「柄臣不和，論議多駁，詔令輕改，失於事幾。 金人交兵半歲，而至今不解者，以和戰之說未一故也。 裁抑濫賞，如白黑易分，而數月之間，三變其議，以私心不除，各蔽其黨故也。 今日一人言之，以為是而行； 明日一人言之，以為非而止。 或聖斷隃度而不暇疇咨，或大臣偏見而遂形播告，所以動未必善，處未必宜，乃輒為之反汗，其勢不得不爾也。」
In the first year of Jingkang he was promoted to vice minister of personnel and told Emperor Qinzong, "The chief ministers are not in harmony, deliberations are often overturned, and edicts are lightly changed. This misses the critical moment in affairs. The Jurchens have been at war for half a year, yet the conflict remains unresolved because the debate over peace and war has not been settled. Restraining excessive rewards should be as easy as distinguishing black from white, yet within a few months the policy changed three times, because private motives were not removed and each man shielded his own faction. Today one man speaks and it is deemed right and carried out; tomorrow another speaks and it is deemed wrong and halted. Sometimes the sacred decision is made in private without time to consult broadly. Sometimes a chief minister's bias is immediately proclaimed abroad. Thus actions are not necessarily good and measures not necessarily fitting, yet they are repeatedly reversed. Under such conditions it cannot be otherwise."
43
時金兵至河北，振請糾諸道兵掎角擊之，曰：「彼猖獗如此，陛下尚欲守和議，而不使之少有懲艾乎？ 」上嗟味其言，而牽於外廷，不能用。 拜開封尹。 故時，大辟有情可矜，多奏取原貸； 崇寧以來，議者謂輦轂先彈壓，率便文殺之。 振請復舊制。 詔捕亡命卒，得數千人，振請以隸步軍而除其罪。 步軍司欲論如法，振曰：「方多事之際，而一日殺數千人，必大駭觀聽。 」乃盡釋之。 改刑部侍郎。
At the time Jin troops had reached Hebei. Zhen requested that armies from all circuits be assembled to attack them from converging angles, saying, "They are so rampant. Does Your Majesty still wish to uphold the peace agreement and not make them suffer even a little punishment?" The Emperor sighed over his words but was constrained by the outer court and could not act on them. He was appointed prefect of Kaifeng. Formerly, in capital cases where there were extenuating circumstances, memorials often sought commutation or pardon; since the Chongning era critics said the capital must suppress crime first and generally used expedient documents to execute offenders. Zhen requested restoration of the old system. An edict ordered the capture of deserters, and several thousand were found. Zhen requested that they be assigned to the infantry and their crimes remitted. The Bureau of Infantry wished to punish them according to law. Zhen said, "At a time of many crises, to kill several thousand men in a single day would greatly shock public opinion." Thereupon all were released. He was transferred to vice minister of justice.
44
金騎在郊，邀車駕出城，振為何栗言：「宜思所以折之之策。 」栗不從。 未幾，及於難，年五十七。 金人去，從子庭訪得其首歸葬之。 初，王黼使其客沈積中圖燕，振戒以後禍，積中懼而言不可。 既而振乃用是死，聞者痛之。
Jin cavalry were in the suburbs and demanded that the imperial carriage leave the city. Zhen told He Li, "We should think of a strategy to rebuff them." Li did not listen. Before long he met disaster at the age of fifty-seven. After the Jurchens departed his nephew Ting recovered his head and returned it for burial. Earlier Wang Fu had his client Shen Jizhong plan the Yan campaign. Zhen warned of future calamity, and Jizhong, fearing, said it could not be done. In the end Zhen died for this very reason, and those who heard of it grieved.
45
初，宣和崇道家之說，振侍坐東宮，從容言：「孔子以《鴟鴞》之詩為知道，其詞不過曰'迨天之未陰雨，綢繆牖戶'而已。 老子亦云：'為之於未有，治之於未亂。 '今不固根本於無事之時，而事目前區區，非二聖人意。 」他日，太子為徽宗道之。 徽宗寤，頗欲去健羨，疏左右近習，而宦寺楊戩輩方大興宮室，懼不得肆，因讒家令楊馮，以為將輔太子幸非常。 徽宗震怒，執馮誅之，而太子之言亦廢。 振尹京時，兩宮方困於惎間，振極意彌縫，治龍德梁忻獄，寬其罪，不使有纖介可指。
Earlier, in the Xuanhe era when Daoist teachings were honored, Zhen sat in attendance in the Eastern Palace and said at ease, "Confucius held the poem 'Owl' to show knowledge of the Way. Its words amount to no more than 'Before heaven's rain clouds gather, gather mulberry bark and line window and door.'" Laozi also said, 'Act when nothing yet exists; govern before disorder arises. Now if we do not strengthen the root in times of peace but attend only to petty present concerns, this is not the intent of the Two Sages.'" Another day the Crown Prince reported this to Emperor Huizong. Emperor Huizong awoke to it and wished to remove excess and distance his close attendants. But eunuchs like Yang Jian were then greatly expanding the palace and feared they would not be free to indulge themselves. They therefore slandered the household steward Yang Feng, claiming he would assist the Crown Prince in an irregular coup. Emperor Huizong was greatly enraged, seized Feng and executed him, and the Crown Prince's advice was also abandoned. When Zhen served as prefect of the capital the two palaces were troubled by mutual suspicion. Zhen did his utmost to mend matters, handled the Longde Palace case of Liang Xin, lightened the offense, and left not the slightest point that could be seized upon.
46
劉延慶
Liu Yanqing
47
劉延慶，保安軍人。 世為將家，雄豪有勇，數從西伐，立戰功，積官至相州觀察使、龍神衛都指揮使、鄜延路總管。 遷泰寧軍節度觀察留後，改承宣使。 破夏人成德軍，擒其酋賞屈，降王子益麻党征。 拜保信軍節度使、馬軍副都指揮使。 從童貫平方臘，節度河陽三城。 又從北伐，以宣撫都統制督兵十萬，渡白溝。
Liu Yanqing was a native of Bao'an Army. His family had been military for generations. Bold and brave, he repeatedly joined western campaigns, achieved merit in battle, and rose through the ranks to military commissioner of Xiangzhou, commander-in-chief of the Dragon and Divine Guards, and overall commander of Fuyan Road. He was transferred to military commissioner and observation commissioner of Taining Army, then made commissioner. He defeated the Xia at Chengde Army, captured their chieftain Shangqu, and received the surrender of the princely scion Yima Dangzheng. He was appointed military commissioner of Baoxin Army and vice commander-in-chief of the cavalry. Following Tong Guan in the pacification of Fang La, he served as military commissioner of the Three Cities of Heyang. He again joined the northern campaign as overall pacification commander, directing one hundred thousand troops across the Bai Ditch.
48
延慶行軍無紀律，郭藥師扣馬諫曰：「今大軍拔隊行而不設備，若敵人置伏邀擊，首尾不相應，則望塵決潰矣。 」不聽。 至良鄉，遼將蕭幹帥眾來，延慶與戰，敗績，遂閉壘不出。 藥師曰：「幹兵不過萬人，今悉力拒伐，燕山必虛，願得奇兵五千，倍道襲取，令公之子三將軍簡師為後繼。 」延慶許之，遣大將高世宣與藥師先行，即入燕城，幹舉精甲三千巷戰。 三將軍者，光世也。
Yanqing marched without discipline. Guo Yaoshi seized his horse's bridle and remonstrated, "The main army is now moving out in column without taking precautions. If the enemy sets an ambush and attacks, with head and tail unable to support each other, the army will break at the mere sight of dust." He would not listen. At Liangxiang the Liao general Xiao Gan led his forces against him. Yanqing fought and was defeated, then shut himself in the fort and would not come out. Yaoshi said, "Gan's troops number no more than ten thousand. He is now exerting all his strength to resist us, so Yan Mountains must be empty. Give me five thousand picked troops to take a forced march and seize it, with Your Excellency's son the Third General leading a picked force as rear support." Yanqing agreed and sent the great general Gao Shixuan with Yaoshi ahead. They entered Yan city, and Gan rallied three thousand elite armored troops for street fighting. The Third General was Guangshi.
49
渝約不至，藥師失援敗走，世宣死之。 延慶營于盧溝南，幹分兵斷餉道，擒護糧將王淵，得漢軍二人，蔽其目，留帳中，夜半偽相語曰：「聞漢軍十萬壓吾境，吾師三倍，敵之有餘。 當分左右翼，以精兵沖其中，左右翼為應，殲之無遺。 」陰逸其一人歸報。 明旦，延慶見火起，以為敵至，燒營而奔，相蹂踐死者百余裏。 自熙、豐以來，所儲軍實殆盡。 退保雄州，燕人作賦及歌誚之。 朝議延慶喪師，不可不行法，坐貶率府率，安置筠州。 契丹知中國不能用兵，由是輕宋。
He broke the agreement and did not come. Yaoshi lost support, was defeated and fled, and Shixuan died in the battle. Yanqing camped south of the Lu Ditch. Gan divided his troops to cut the supply route, captured the grain-protecting general Wang Yuan, and took two Han soldiers. He covered their eyes and kept them in his tent. At midnight he feigned conversation, "I hear a hundred thousand Han troops are pressing our border. Our army is three times as large—more than enough to defeat the enemy. We should divide into left and right wings, strike the center with elite troops, and have the wings respond. Destroy them to the last man. Secretly he let one man escape to report back." At dawn Yanqing saw fires rise and thought the enemy had arrived. He burned his camp and fled, and men trampled one another to death for more than a hundred li. Since the Xining and Yuanfeng eras the stored military supplies were nearly exhausted. He withdrew to hold Xiongzhou, and the people of Yan composed rhapsodies and songs to mock him. The court debated that Yanqing had lost his army and the law could not go unenforced. He was demoted to director of the Rate Office and placed under restraint in Yunzhou. The Khitan knew that China could not use its armies effectively, and from this they came to despise Song.
50
未幾，復為鎮海軍節度使。 靖康之難，延慶分部守京城，城陷，引秦兵萬人奪開遠門以出，至龜兒寺，為追騎所殺。 光世自有傳。
Before long he was again made military commissioner of Zhenhai Army. During the Jingkang crisis Yanqing divided his forces to defend the capital. When the city fell he led ten thousand Qin troops to seize Kaiyuan Gate and break out. At Guier Temple he was killed by pursuing cavalry. Guangshi has his own biography.
51
論曰：靖康之變、執禮、振不忍都人塗炭，拒強敵無厭之欲，親逢其凶。 熙靖、世勣不肯以一身事二姓，悲不食以終。 灌、延慶戰敗而沒。 此數人者，其所遭不同，至於死國難則一而已。 雲之死，雖其有以取之，殆亦天未欲絕宋祀也； 不然，是行也，康王其危哉！
The commentary says: In the Jingkang crisis Zhili and Zhen could not bear to see the people of the capital suffer ruin. They resisted the insatiable demands of a powerful enemy and personally met disaster. Xijing and Shiji refused to serve two dynasties with one body and died from grief without eating. Guan and Yanqing were defeated in battle and perished. These several men encountered different circumstances, yet in dying for the state's crisis they were one. As for Yun's death, though he partly brought it on himself, perhaps Heaven also did not yet wish to cut off the sacrifices of Song; otherwise on that journey Prince Kang would have been in grave danger!
