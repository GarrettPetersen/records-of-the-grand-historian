外戚下
Part Two: Families of Imperial Consorts
2
○孟忠厚韋淵錢忱邢煥潘永思吳益 〈 （弟蓋）〉 李道鄭興裔楊次山
● Meng Zhonghou, Wei Yuan, Qian Chen, Xing Huan, Pan Yongsi, and Wu Yi Opening bracket marking a biographical aside. 〈Younger brother: Gai〉 Li Dao, Zheng Xingyi, and Yang Cishan
3
帝幸揚州，除顯謨閣直學士，台諫交章論列，帝以太后故，難之。 後聞，即命易武秩，遂授常德軍承宣使，幹辦皇城司。 未幾，奉太后幸杭州。 苗傅亂平，趙鼎謂張浚曰：“太后復辟，其功甚大，當推恩外家。 ”浚乃奏忠厚寧遠軍節度使。 尋奉太后幸南昌，歸至越，以母憂解職。
When the emperor went to Yangzhou, Meng Zhonghou was appointed Direct Academician of the Hall for Manifesting Strategies. Censors and remonstrators submitted memorial after memorial in protest, but because of the empress dowager the emperor found it hard to proceed. When she heard of this, the empress dowager at once had his appointment switched to a military rank. He was then made commissioner-in-chief of the Changde army and put in charge of the Imperial City Bureau. Before long he escorted the empress dowager on her journey to Hangzhou. After Miao Fu's rebellion was put down, Zhao Ding said to Zhang Jun, "The empress dowager's restoration was a tremendous achievement. We ought to extend favors to her family." " Zhang Jun then memorialized to appoint Zhonghou military commissioner of the Ningyuan army. Soon afterward he escorted the empress dowager to Nanchang. When they returned to the Yue region, he left office to observe mourning for his mother.
4
頃之，後崩，以祔廟恩，起復鎮潼軍節度使，開府儀同三司。 及後大祥，封信安郡王，充禮儀使，奉太后神禦幸溫州。 紹興九年，判鎮江府，改判明州兼安撫使，改判婺州。 既而帝以太后攢會稽，乃命忠厚判紹興府兼修奉攢宮事，加少保。 三梓宮歸，充迎護使。 及營佑陵，秦檜當為總護使，憚往，乃除忠厚樞密使以代其行。 檜與忠厚僚婿也，然心實忌之。 山陵事畢，忠厚欲歸樞密府，檜諷言路引故事論列，遂判福州。
Before long the empress dowager died. On the grace granted for her enshrinement in the ancestral temple, he was recalled from mourning and appointed military commissioner of the Zhentong army and grand preceptor with protocol equal to the three excellencies. When the grand mourning period ended, he was enfeoffed as Prince of Xin'an, made director of ritual, and sent to escort the empress dowager's spirit tablet to Wenzhou. In 1139 he was assigned to administer Zhenjiang prefecture, then reassigned to Ming prefecture as pacification commissioner, and later to Wu prefecture. Because the empress dowager's temporary resting place was at Kuaiji, the emperor then appointed Zhonghou to administer Shaoxing prefecture and to oversee maintenance of the temporary palace, and promoted him to junior guardian. When the three imperial coffins were brought back, he served as commissioner for their reception and escort. When work began on the You Mausoleum, Qin Hui was supposed to serve as overall supervisor but shrank from going, so Zhonghou was made commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs to take his place. Qin Hui and Zhonghou were brothers-in-law through their wives, yet Qin secretly resented him. After the mausoleum work was finished, Zhonghou wished to return to the Bureau of Military Affairs. Qin Hui prompted remonstrance officials to cite precedent and attack him, and Zhonghou was reassigned to administer Fuzhou.
5
時海寇猖獗，帝憂忠厚不能弭其患，改判建康府，又改判紹興府。 會郊赦加恩，謝表有“本無時才，出為世用”語。 中丞詹大方希檜意，論忠厚表辭輕侮，謂今日不足與有為，遂罷為醴泉觀使。 檜死，召還行在，授保寧軍節度使、判平江府，再改判紹興府，過闕入見，復詔充萬壽觀使，提舉秘書省。 二十七年，卒，贈太保。
Sea bandits were rampant at the time. Fearing Zhonghou could not suppress them, the emperor reassigned him first to Jiankang prefecture and then to Shaoxing prefecture. At a suburban sacrifice amnesty he submitted a memorial of thanks containing the words, "I never had talent suited to the age, yet was brought out for public service." Vice censor-in-chief Zhan Dafang, eager to please Qin Hui, charged that Zhonghou's memorial was disrespectful and that he was unfit for serious responsibility in the present age. Zhonghou was dismissed and made commissioner of the Liquan Abbey. After Qin Hui died, Zhonghou was recalled to the temporary capital, appointed military commissioner of the Baoning army and assigned to Pingjiang prefecture, then reassigned to Shaoxing prefecture. When he passed through the capital for an audience, he was again made commissioner of the Wanshou Abbey and supervisor of the Secretariat. In 1157 he died and was posthumously granted the title grand guardian.
6
忠厚奉昭聖太后訓，避遠權勢，不敢以私幹朝廷。 明受之變，太后垂簾，忠厚乞裁節本家恩澤，如有夤緣，令三省執奏。 御史劾秦檜當國，親姻扳援以進，忠厚獨與之忤。 自越入見，語所善王钅至曰：“忠厚與檜雖有親好，每懷疑心，今欲求一不傷時忌對劄。 ”銍教之，但言乞免提舉學事而已，然亦見廢。 帝以太后擁佑功，故眷忠厚特優。 後在瑤華三十年，恩澤未嚐陳請，詔賜忠厚田三十頃以賞之。 既奉內祠，金使至，特命押班，且令月過局，如宰執例。 及卒，三子皆除直秘閣，親屬六人各進以一官。
Zhonghou followed Empress Dowager Zhaosheng's teaching, kept his distance from power, and never dared to press private interests on the court. During the Mingshou Incident, when the empress dowager held court from behind the curtain, Zhonghou asked that favors to his own family be reduced. If anyone sought advancement through improper connections, he asked that the three departments be instructed to memorialize firmly against it. Censors impeached Qin Hui for dominating the government and advancing relatives and in-laws through pull. Zhonghou alone stood against him. When Qin Hui came from the Yue region for an audience, he told his confidant Wang Yin, "Although Zhonghou and I are related by marriage and on friendly terms, I am always suspicious of him. I need a memorial reply that will not give offense in the present climate." " Wang Yin advised him to ask only to be relieved of supervising academic affairs, yet even that request led to his dismissal. Because of the empress dowager's merit in supporting and protecting the throne, the emperor showed Zhonghou exceptional favor. The empress dowager lived at Yaohua for thirty years and never once petitioned for favors. An edict granted Zhonghou thirty qing of land as a reward. After he took up residence at an inner abbey, whenever Jin envoys arrived he was specially ordered to stand in the rear escort rank and to pass through the bureau monthly, following the precedent for chief councillors. When he died, all three sons were appointed to the Direct Secretariat Pavilion, and six relatives were each advanced one rank in office.
7
淵性暴橫，不循法度，帝慮其有過，難於行法，遂遷福建路副總管。 淵引疾丐祠，許之。 淵乃言，自宣和及今，十二年未嚐磨勘，乞遷秩。 吏部言，在法，橫行無以年勞磨勘者，帝遂不許。 久之，落階官，除德慶軍節度使。 召赴行在，除開府儀同三司。 會建康軍帥邊順疾篤，留守呂頤浩奏以淵代，帝不欲以戚裏管軍，不許。 淵陳乞恩數，帝詢太后家故例，賜田五十頃，房緡錢日二十千。 帝久不予淵官，聞太后將入境，乃封平樂郡王，令逆於境上。 既從後歸，即令致仕。 又詔奉朝請，遷少師。 淵在內不得逞，乞致仕，任便居住。 從之。
Wei Yuan was violent and overbearing by nature and did not observe the law. The emperor feared that if he committed an offense it would be difficult to punish him, and therefore transferred him to be deputy commander-in-chief of the Fujian circuit. Yuan pleaded illness and asked for an abbey appointment; his request was granted. Yuan then said that from the Xuanhe era until now, twelve years had passed without his ever undergoing merit review, and he asked for promotion in rank. The Ministry of Personnel replied that by law, those of overbearing conduct are not eligible for merit review based on years of service. The emperor therefore refused. After a long interval he was stripped of his graded rank and appointed military commissioner of the Deqing army. He was summoned to the temporary capital and appointed grand preceptor with protocol equal to the three excellencies. When Jiankang army commander Bian Shun fell gravely ill, acting prefect Lü Yihao memorialized to have Yuan replace him. The emperor did not wish to have a maternal relative command troops and refused. Yuan memorialized requesting largesse. The emperor looked up former precedents for the empress dowager's family and granted him fifty qing of land and twenty thousand strings per day for housing expenses. The emperor had long withheld office from Yuan. Hearing that the empress dowager was about to enter the realm, he enfeoffed him as Prince of Pingle and ordered him to go to the border to meet her. After he returned with the empress dowager, he was immediately ordered to retire. He was again ordered to attend court as a retainer and was promoted to junior preceptor. Unable to have his way in the capital, Yuan asked to retire and to live where he pleased. His request was granted.
8
未幾，帝恐其肆橫於外，復詔落致仕，還居賜第。 太后朝景靈宮，淵見後，出言詆毀，詔侍御史餘堯弼即其家鞫治，淵具伏誣罔，責授寧遠軍節度副使、袁州安置。 數年復故職，累遷太保、太傅。 卒，贈太師。 子三人：訊、謙、讜。
Before long, fearing that he would run riot outside the capital, the emperor again revoked his retirement and ordered him back to his granted mansion. When the empress dowager attended the Jingling Palace, Yuan saw her afterward and spoke slanderously. An edict ordered attending censor Yu Yaobi to investigate at his house. Yuan fully confessed to falsehood and calumny and was demoted to deputy military commissioner of the Ningyuan army and placed under restraint at Yuan prefecture. After several years he was restored to his former office and was repeatedly promoted to grand guardian and grand tutor. He died and was posthumously granted the title grand preceptor. He had three sons: Xun, Qian, and Tan.
9
訊，紹興中，官至達州刺史，坐過，用太后旨降武德郎，與嶺外監當。 謙，好學能詩，官至建康軍節度使。
Xun, during the Shaoxing era, rose to prefect of Dazhou. After an offense, by the empress dowager's instruction he was demoted to Wude Lang and assigned to a supervisory post beyond the Lingnan passes. Qian loved learning and was skilled at poetry; he rose to military commissioner of the Jiankang army.
10
謙子璞，淳熙末，仕至太府少卿。 高崇崩，擢司農少卿，為金國告哀使。 金主錫宴，其館使欲用樂，璞不可，自朝至夜漏下三十刻，金人不能奪。 及入見，其閣門令璞吉服入，璞又不可。 日將中，乃以凶服見。 紹熙初，除煥章閣，論者以為非祖宗舊制，遂換授明州觀察使，十年不遷。 寧宗嘉其恬退，授清遠軍節度使，致仕，卒，贈太尉。
Qian's son Pu, toward the end of the Chunxi reign, served as vice minister of the Court of the Imperial Treasury. When the Jin emperor Gaozong died, Pu was promoted to vice minister of the Court of the Imperial Granary and sent as envoy to announce mourning to Jin. The Jin ruler gave a banquet. The host envoy wished to use music, but Pu refused. From morning until the night watch had passed the thirtieth mark, the Jin side could not prevail over him. When he went in for an audience, the gatekeepers ordered Pu to enter in auspicious dress. He refused again. When the sun was nearly at the meridian, he was finally received in mourning dress. At the beginning of the Shaoxi reign he was appointed to the Huanzhang Pavilion. Critics held that this was not the ancestral institution, so he was transferred to observation commissioner of Ming prefecture and received no further promotion for ten years. Emperor Ningzong commended his quiet withdrawal and appointed him military commissioner of the Qingyuan army. He retired, then died, and was posthumously granted the title grand commandant.
11
錢忱，字伯誠，吳越王俶五世孫。 父景臻，尚仁宗第十女秦魯國大長公主，生忱，神宗命賜名，除莊宅副使、騎都尉。
Qian Chen, style name Bocheng, was a fifth-generation descendant of Qian Chu, king of Wuyue. His father Jingzhen married Emperor Renzong's tenth daughter, the Grand Princess of Qin and Lu, and fathered Chen. Emperor Shenzong ordered that a name be bestowed on him and appointed him deputy commissioner of the Estates Bureau and cavalry commandant.
12
帝嚐諭景臻曰：“主賢，宜有子，為擇嘉配。 ”娶唐介孫女，又晁迥外孫。 忱從二家遊，伯父勰在翰苑，因得識一時名卿。
The emperor once told Jingzhen, "The princess is worthy and ought to have a son. Choose a fine match for her." " He married a granddaughter of Tang Jie, who was also a granddaughter on her mother's side of Chao Kui. Chen moved in the circles of both families. His uncle Xie served in the Hanlin Academy, and through him Chen came to know the eminent ministers of the age.
13
哲宗愛之，常使侍左右。 徽宗覃八寶恩，為邕州觀察使，遷武寧軍觀察留後。 喜其靖共，除瀘州節度使。 欽宗加檢校少保，尋納節。 高宗立，復拜檢校少保、瀘川節度使、中太一宮使，禦書“忠孝之家”四字賜之，進開府儀同三司。 紹興十五年，以秦魯主終喪，除少保，封榮國公。 三十年，遷少師，仍舊節，致仕，給真奉。 明年卒，年八十餘，贈太師。 子端禮，自有傳。
Emperor Zhezong was fond of him and often had him attend at his side. Emperor Huizong extended the grace of the Eight Treasures and made him observation commissioner of Yong prefecture, then transferred him to acting observation commissioner of the Wuning army. Pleased with his respectful diligence, the court appointed him military commissioner of Luzhou. Emperor Qinzong added the honorary title of junior guardian. Soon afterward he surrendered his commissioner's seal. When Emperor Gaozong was enthroned, Chen was again appointed honorary junior guardian, military commissioner of the Luchuan army, and commissioner of the Central Taiyi Palace. The emperor's own hand wrote the four characters "House of Loyalty and Filial Piety" and bestowed them on him. He was advanced to grand preceptor with protocol equal to the three excellencies. In 1145, because the Princess of Qin and Lu had completed her mourning, he was appointed junior guardian and enfeoffed as Duke of Rong. In 1160 he was transferred to junior preceptor while retaining his former commission, retired, and was granted full salary in retirement. The following year he died at over eighty and was posthumously granted the title grand preceptor. His son Duanli has his own biography.
14
邢煥，字文仲，開封人。 以父任調孟州汜水縣主簿，監在京藥局、平準務、茶場，以勞改宣德郎、莫州司錄。 移知開封府陽武縣，都大提舉開德、大名府堤埽。 曆開封府士、工、儀曹。
Xing Huan, style name Wenzhong, was a native of Kaifeng. Through his father's privilege of office he was appointed registrar of Sishui county in Meng prefecture, supervised the capital pharmacy bureau, the equalization office, and the tea depot, and for meritorious service was promoted to attendant gentleman of propagating virtue and recorder of Mo prefecture. He was transferred to serve as magistrate of Yangwu county in Kaifeng prefecture and as overall supervisor for dikes and embankments in Kaide and Daming prefectures. He successively served in the scholar, works, and ritual sections of Kaifeng prefecture.
15
遷保靜軍承宣使。 苗、劉之變，煥自度不能爭，乃病免。 兼提舉萬壽觀，求去不已，改江州太平觀，遂徙居忠州。
He was transferred to commissioner-in-chief of the Baojing army. During the Miao and Liu incident, Huan judged that he could not contend and resigned on grounds of illness. He concurrently supervised the Wanshou Abbey and repeatedly asked to leave. He was reassigned to the Taiping Abbey in Jiang prefecture and then moved his residence to Zhong prefecture.
16
紹興二年，入對，首陳川、陝形勢利害，請幸荊南，分兵以圖恢復，凡數百言，帝甚嘉之。 復以為都承旨，引疾不拜。 擢慶遠軍節度使、提舉洞霄宮。
In 1132 he came for an audience and first set forth the strategic advantages and disadvantages of Sichuan and Shaanxi, asking the emperor to visit Jingnan and divide troops to plan recovery. His memorial ran to several hundred words, and the emperor greatly commended him. He was again appointed chief palace aide but pleaded illness and declined to accept. He was promoted to military commissioner of the Qingyuan army and supervisor of the Dongxiao Palace.
17
煥涉學有文，節儉自持，未嚐恃恩私請，識者取焉。 是年，卒，贈開府儀同三司，諡恭簡，加贈少師，追封嘉國公。
Huan was broadly learned and literary, frugal and self-restrained, and never relied on favor to make private requests. Men of discernment approved of him. That year he died and was posthumously granted grand preceptor with protocol equal to the three excellencies, given the posthumous title Gongjian, further granted junior preceptor, and enfeoffed posthumously as Duke of Jia.
18
潘永思，賢妃叔父也。 妃初進封，詔以梁師成第賜永思。 建炎初，為閣門宣讚舍人、帶禦器械。
Pan Yongsi was the paternal uncle of the Worthy Consort. When the consort was first advanced in rank, an edict granted Yongsi the mansion of Liang Shicheng. At the beginning of the Jianyan era, he served as attendant of the gate for proclamation and praise and as bearer of the imperial armory.
19
元祐太后在虔，帝遣永思迎歸，權三省、樞密事。 盧益頗與之交結，為諫官吳表臣所論，範宗尹請出永思，帝曰：“未可，姑罷祿以困之，庶知悔過。 ”遂奪職。 既而辛企宗言永思嚐捕魔賊有功，復為帶禦器械。
When Empress Dowager Yuanyou was at Qian, the emperor sent Yongsi to welcome her back and provisionally entrusted him with the affairs of the three departments and the Bureau of Military Affairs. Lu Yi associated with him rather freely and was criticized by remonstrance official Wu Biaochen. Fan Zongyin asked that Yongsi be removed, but the emperor said, "Not yet. For now suspend his salary to press him, so that he may know remorse." " His office was thereupon stripped. Soon afterward Xin Qizong said that Yongsi had once captured demon-bandits with merit, and he was again made bearer of the imperial armory.
20
未幾，大理推治偽告，事連永思，帝曰：“永思雖戚裏，既有過，安可廢法！ ”乃罷職就逮。 獄成，追一官。 尋復為閣門宣讚舍人，遷同知閣門事。 永思乞增給飧錢，戶部言其不應格法，乃止。 紹興八年，自右武郎擢右武大夫、知閣門事，尋卒。
Before long the Court of Judicial Review investigated a false accusation case that implicated Yongsi. The emperor said, "Although Yongsi is a maternal relative, since he has committed an offense, how can the law be set aside!" " He was therefore dismissed from office and taken into custody. When the case was concluded, one rank in office was stripped from him. Soon afterward he was again made attendant of the gate for proclamation and praise and was transferred to vice director of the gate. Yongsi requested an increase in his meal allowance. The Ministry of Revenue said this did not meet the regulations, and the request was denied. In 1138 he was promoted from right martial gentleman to right martial grandee and made director of the gate. Soon afterward he died.
21
吳益，字叔謙，蓋字叔平，俱憲聖皇后弟也。 益，建炎末，以恩補官，累遷幹辦禦輦院、帶禦器械。 蓋，紹興五年，以恩補官，累遷宣讚舍人。 帝與後皆喜翰墨，故益、蓋兄弟師法，亦有書名。 後受冊推恩，益加成州團練使，蓋加文州刺史。 帝為置皇后宅大小學教授，以王糸茲為之。 糸茲明經，善訓導，益、蓋折節事之。
Wu Yi, style name Shuqian, and Gai, style name Shuping, were both younger brothers of Empress Dowager Xiansheng. Yi, toward the end of the Jianyan era, received office through grace privilege and was repeatedly promoted to manager of the Imperial Carriage Bureau and bearer of the imperial armory. Gai, in 1135, received office through grace privilege and was repeatedly promoted to attendant for proclamation and praise. Both the emperor and empress loved calligraphy, so the brothers Yi and Gai studied their methods and also gained a reputation as calligraphers. When the empress received her investiture and favors were extended, Yi was made regiment commander of Cheng prefecture and Gai was made prefect of Wen prefecture. The emperor established professors for the primary and secondary schools of the empress's residence and appointed Wang Zi to the post. Wang Zi was a classicist and skilled at instruction; Yi and Gai humbled themselves to serve him.
22
益娶秦檜長孫女，又與王繼先交相薦引，故三家姻族皆躐美官。 益曆官至保康軍節度使，加太尉、開府儀同三司。 初，既建節，以檜故，授文資，直秘閣。 檜進徽宗御製，辭免加恩，帝乃特命賜益三品服，累加秘閣修撰，直徽猷閣。 以檜提舉編修寬恤詔令，又加益直寶文閣。 檜死，其子熺復請於帝，又升敷文閣待制。 中丞湯鵬舉言，益以庸瑣之才，恃親昵之勢，乞褫職名，以示至公，帝謂：“鵬舉所論甚切當，然朕於奠檜日，諭檜妻子，許以保全其家，今若遽出其婿則傷恩，臣僚無得更有論列。 ”自是不復遷。 顯仁太后葬，為攢宮總護使，始進少保。 孝宗嗣位，進少傅，又進太師，封太寧郡王。 乾道七年，卒，年四十八，諡莊簡，追封衛王。
Yi married Qin Hui's eldest granddaughter and also traded recommendations with Wang Jixian, so the three families by marriage all vaulted into high offices. Yi rose to military commissioner of the Baokang army and was further granted grand commandant and grand preceptor with protocol equal to the three excellencies. At first, after he received his commissioner's seal, on account of Qin Hui he was given a civil-service rank and appointed to the Direct Secretariat Pavilion. Qin Hui presented compositions in Emperor Huizong's own hand and declined added favors. The emperor thereupon specially ordered that Yi be granted third-rank robes and repeatedly added the titles compiler of the Secretariat Pavilion and attendant of the Huiyao Pavilion. Because Qin Hui supervised the compilation of edicts of leniency and relief, Yi was further made attendant of the Baowen Pavilion. When Qin Hui died, his son Xi again petitioned the emperor, and Yi was further promoted to awaiting draft of the Fuwen Pavilion. Vice censor-in-chief Tang Pengju said that Yi, with mediocre talent, relied on intimate favor and asked that his titles be stripped to show utmost fairness. The emperor said, "Pengju's argument is very apt, yet on the day I laid offerings for Qin Hui I told his wife and children that I would preserve their family. If I now suddenly remove his son-in-law, it would wound grace. Officials must not submit further criticisms." From then on he received no further promotions. At the burial of Empress Dowager Xianren, he served as overall supervisor of the temporary palace and was first promoted to junior guardian. When Emperor Xiaozong succeeded to the throne, Yi was promoted to junior tutor, then to grand preceptor, and enfeoffed as Prince of Taining. In 1171 he died at forty-eight, was given the posthumous title Zhuangjian, and was enfeoffed posthumously as Prince of Wei.
23
蓋官至寧武軍節度使，亦累升太尉、開府儀同三司、少保，封新興郡王。 乾道二年，卒，年四十二。 贈太傅，追封鄭王。
Gai rose to military commissioner of the Ningwu army and was also repeatedly promoted to grand commandant, grand preceptor with protocol equal to the three excellencies, and junior guardian, and was enfeoffed as Prince of Xinxing. In 1166 he died at forty-two. He was posthumously granted grand tutor and enfeoffed posthumously as Prince of Zheng.
24
益子琚，習吏事，乾道九年，特授添差臨安府通判，其後曆尚書郎、部使者，換資至鎮安軍節度使，復以才選，除知明州兼沿海製置使。 寧宗初，乃得祠，奉朝請。 尋知鄂州，再知慶元府，位至少師，判建康府兼留守，卒。 方孝宗崩，光宗以疾不能執喪，大臣請太后垂簾，冊立寧宗。 琚言於後曰：“垂簾可暫不可久。 ”後遂以翌日徹簾。 琚嚐使金，金人嘉其信義。 琚死後，宋遣使至金議和，屢不合，金人言南使中惟吳琚言為可信。
Yi's son Ju was versed in administrative affairs. In 1173 he was specially appointed additional vice prefect of Lin'an prefecture. He later served as a secretariat gentleman and circuit envoy, exchanged credentials to become military commissioner of the Zhen'an army, and was again selected for talent and appointed prefect of Ming prefecture concurrently as coastal pacification commissioner. At the beginning of Emperor Ningzong's reign, he received an abbey appointment and attended court as a retainer. Soon afterward he was made prefect of E prefecture, then again prefect of Qingyuan prefecture. He reached the rank of junior preceptor, was assigned to administer Jiankang prefecture concurrently as acting prefect, and died. When Emperor Xiaozong died, Emperor Guangzong was ill and unable to observe mourning. The chief ministers asked the empress dowager to hold the curtain audience and invest Ningzong as heir. Ju said to the empress dowager, "The curtain audience may be temporary but cannot be prolonged." The empress dowager thereupon withdrew the curtain audience the next day. Ju once served as envoy to Jin, and the Jin people commended his trustworthiness and integrity. After Ju died, Song sent envoys to Jin to negotiate peace, but agreement was repeatedly not reached. The Jin people said that among the southern envoys only Wu Ju's words were trustworthy.
25
琚弟璹，仕至保靜軍節度使。 蓋子瑰，亦至昭化軍節度使。
Ju's younger brother Jun served as military commissioner of the Baojing army. Gai's son Gui also rose to military commissioner of the Zhaohua army.
26
李道，字行之，相州人。 其中女為光宗後。 初，道與兄旺聚眾歸宗澤，澤因事斬旺，命道掌其軍。 澤薨，道引軍依襄陽鎮撫使桑仲，仲以為副都統製兼知隨州，奏於朝，授武義郎、閣門宣讚舍人。 仲為霍明所殺，道與統製李橫率兵縞素圍明於郢，明亡去。
Li Dao, style name Xingzhi, was a native of Xiang prefecture. His second daughter became Empress to Emperor Guangzong. At first Dao and his elder brother Wang gathered followers and submitted to Zong Ze. Ze executed Wang on account of an offense and ordered Dao to take command of his troops. When Zong Ze died, Dao led his troops to attach himself to Sang Zhong, pacification commissioner of Xiangyang. Zhong made him deputy overall commander concurrently prefect of Sui prefecture and memorialized the court. Dao was appointed martial righteousness gentleman and attendant of the gate for proclamation and praise. Sang Zhong was killed by Huo Ming. Dao and commander Li Heng led troops in white mourning dress and besieged Ming at Ying. Ming fled.
27
劉豫遣人持書招道，道不從，執其使以聞，詔嘉獎之。 豫怒，遣將穆楷攻道，道拒破之。 除鄧、隨州鎮撫使兼知鄧州。 時李橫已命別將守鄧，道憚橫，不敢受，遂命仍知隨州。 樞密院以道能察軍情，不受鎮撫之命，理宜褒賞。 詔領榮州團練使，進武義大夫。
Liu Yu sent someone with a letter to recruit Dao. Dao refused, seized the envoy and reported it, and an edict commended him. Liu Yu was enraged and sent the general Mu Kai to attack Dao. Dao resisted and defeated him. He was appointed pacification commissioner of Deng and Sui prefectures concurrently prefect of Deng prefecture. At that time Li Heng had already ordered another general to hold Deng. Dao feared Heng and did not dare accept, and was therefore ordered to continue as prefect of Sui prefecture. The Bureau of Military Affairs held that Dao could discern military conditions and had not accepted the pacification commissioner's order, and that he deserved commendation. An edict appointed him regiment commander of Rong prefecture and advanced him to martial righteousness grandee.
28
胡安中守唐州，勢孤不能自立，遂附豫。 道招之，安中復來歸。 會李成入寇，鎮撫使李橫棄襄陽去，道亦棄隨南歸，至江州。 詔道屬岳飛為選鋒軍統製，入唐州，擒偽將，除唐、鄧、郢州、襄陽都統製。 從飛收復襄陽等郡，授行營護軍。 累至復州防禦使、果州觀察使。 戍鄂州，加中侍大夫、武勝軍承宣使，又升御前諸軍統製。
Hu Anzhong held Tang prefecture. Isolated and unable to stand on his own, he submitted to Liu Yu. Dao recruited him, and Anzhong returned to allegiance. When Li Cheng invaded, pacification commissioner Li Heng abandoned Xiangyang and fled. Dao also abandoned Sui and returned south to Jiang prefecture. An edict placed Dao under Yue Fei as commander of the Vanguard Army. He entered Tang prefecture, captured a puppet general, and was appointed overall commander of Tang, Deng, Ying, and Xiangyang. Following Yue Fei in recovering Xiangyang and other commanderies, he was appointed protector of the army of the mobile headquarters. He rose to defense commissioner of Fu prefecture and observation commissioner of Guo prefecture. He garrisoned E prefecture, was further granted palace attendant grandee and commissioner-in-chief of the Wusheng army, and was again promoted to commander of the imperial front armies.
29
武興蠻楊再興連歲寇掠，道破其眾，擒再興及其二子，遷保寧軍承宣使。 群盜朱持等聚桂陽，詔道移軍衡州經理，道遣高仲等擊平之。 落階官，加龍神衛四廂都指揮使，遷鎮南軍承宣使。
The Wuxing barbarian Yang Zaixing raided year after year. Dao defeated his forces, captured Zaixing and his two sons, and was transferred to commissioner-in-chief of the Baoning army. The bandits Zhu Chi and others gathered at Guiyang. An edict ordered Dao to move his army to Heng prefecture to handle the matter. Dao sent Gao Zhong and others to attack and pacify them. He was stripped of his graded rank, made commander of the four divisions of the Dragon Spirit Guard, and transferred to commissioner-in-chief of the Zhennan army.
30
金將渝盟，命道以所部戍荊南府。 帥臣劉錡奏改為御前前軍、右軍，就命道統之。 錡召奏事，道代為御前諸軍都統製。 金將劉士萼屯光化境，道掩擊，焚其舟，萼遂遁去。 尋因大將言道與鄂帥不協，罷。 逾年，起授捧日、天武四廂都指揮使、知荊南府。
When the Jin generals violated the treaty, Dao was ordered to garrison Jingnan prefecture with his command. Commander Liu Qi memorialized to reorganize them as the imperial front forward army and right army and at once appointed Dao to command them. When Liu Qi was summoned to report on affairs, Dao acted in his stead as overall commander of the imperial front armies. The Jin general Liu Shie encamped on the Guanghua border. Dao made a surprise attack, burned his boats, and Shie fled. Soon afterward, because the great general Yan Dao was at odds with the E prefecture commander, Li Dao was dismissed. After more than a year he was recalled and appointed commander of the four divisions of the Sun-Bearing and Heavenly Martial guards and prefect of Jingnan prefecture.
31
隆興初，湖北諸司劾其過，帝曰：“道恃戚裏妄作，可罷。 ”久之，再為湖北副總管。 及卒，乃拜慶遠軍節度使，贈太尉，諡忠毅。 後既貴，進封楚王。 孫孝友、孝純，皆至節度使。
At the beginning of the Longxing era, the various offices of Hubei impeached him for offenses. The emperor said, "Dao relies on his status as a maternal relative and acts recklessly. He may be dismissed." After a long time he again served as deputy commander-in-chief of Hubei. When he died, he was posthumously appointed military commissioner of the Qingyuan army, granted grand commandant, and given the posthumous title Zhongyi. After he had become eminent, he was further enfeoffed as Prince of Chu. His grandsons Xiaoyou and Xiaochun both rose to military commissioner.
32
鄭興裔，字光錫，初名興宗，顯肅皇后外家三世孫也。 曾祖紳，封樂平郡王。 祖翼之，陸海軍節度使。 父蕃，和州防禦使。 興裔早孤，叔父藻以子字之，分以餘貲，興裔不受，請立義莊贍宗族。 及藻沒，遂解官致追報之義。 初以後恩授成忠郎，充幹辦祗候庫。 聖獻後葬，充攢宮內外巡檢，累至江東路鈐轄。
Zheng Xingyi, style name Guangxi, original name Xingzong, was a third-generation descendant of the maternal family of Empress Xiansu. His great-grandfather Shen was enfeoffed as Prince of Leping. His grandfather Yizhi was military commissioner of the land and sea forces. His father Fan was defense commissioner of He prefecture. Xingyi was orphaned early. His uncle Zao treated him as a son and offered him a share of surplus property, but Xingyi refused and asked instead to establish a charity estate to support the clan. When Zao died, he resigned office to fulfill the obligation of repaying his uncle's kindness. At first, through the empress's grace, he was appointed loyalty-in-office gentleman and made manager of the reception stores. At the burial of Empress Shengxian, he served as inspector inside and outside the temporary palace and rose to controller of the Jiangdong circuit.
33
乾道初，建康留司請治行宮備巡幸，興裔奏勞人費財，乞罷其役，且言都統及馬軍帥皆非其人。 徒福建路兵馬鈐轄，過闕入見，詢以守令臧否，興裔條析以對。 帝曰：“卿識時務，習吏事，行當用卿。 ”會復置武臣提刑，就命為之，加遙領高州刺史。 郡縣積玩，檢驗法廢，興裔創為格目，分畀屬縣，吏不得行其奸，因著為令。
At the beginning of Qiandao, the Jiankang acting prefecture requested repairs to the traveling palace in preparation for an imperial tour. Xingyi memorialized that it would weary the people and waste resources and asked that the work be stopped. He also said that neither the overall commander nor the cavalry commander were suitable men. He was transferred to controller of troops and horses of the Fujian circuit. Passing through the capital for an audience, he was asked about the merits and faults of prefects and magistrates, and Xingyi analyzed them item by item in reply. The emperor said, "You understand the needs of the times and are versed in administrative affairs. Before long I shall employ you." It happened that the post of military-service circuit intendant was restored, and he was appointed to it, with the additional nominal title of prefect of Gao prefecture. Prefectures and counties had long grown lax and the inspection law had fallen into disuse. Xingyi created standardized forms, distributed them to subordinate counties, and clerks could no longer carry out their frauds. He therefore codified this as a regulation.
34
建、劍、汀、邵鹽策屢更，漕臣請易綱運為鈔法，興裔極言其不可。 海寇倏去忽來，調兵常無及，興裔請置澳長，寇至徑率民兵禦之。 又言禁兵事藝不精，多充私役，乞行禁止，尉以捕盜改秩，多偽，當加審實。 帝善其數論事，詔加成州團練使。
The salt policies of Jian, Jian, Ting, and Shao were repeatedly changed. Transport officials asked to replace quota transport with the note system, and Xingyi strongly argued that this could not be done. Sea bandits came and went abruptly, and troop deployments often could not arrive in time. Xingyi asked to establish bay chiefs so that when bandits came they could directly lead local militia to resist them. He also said that palace troops' skills were not refined and that many served as private laborers, and he asked that this be prohibited. He said county captains who gained rank through capturing bandits often did so fraudulently and should be more carefully verified. The emperor approved his repeated discussions of affairs and ordered that he be made regiment commander of Cheng prefecture.
35
時傳聞金欲敗盟，召興裔為賀生辰副使以覘之，使還，言無他，卒如所料。 累差浙東、浙西、江東提刑，請祠以歸。 尋詔知閣門事兼幹辦皇城司，又兼樞密副都承旨。 軍婦楊殺鄰舍兒，取其臂釧而棄其屍，獄成，刑部以無證左，出之。 命興裔覆治得實，帝喜，賜居第。 丁母憂去官，服闋，復故職，除均州防禦使。
At that time it was rumored that Jin intended to break the treaty. Xingyi was summoned as deputy envoy to congratulate the emperor's birthday in order to observe them. When the mission returned, he said there was nothing else, and in the end matters were as he had judged. He was repeatedly assigned as intendant of Zhedong, Zhexi, and Jiangdong and requested an abbey appointment to return home. Soon an edict appointed him director of the gate concurrently manager of the Imperial City Bureau, and also deputy chief aide of the Bureau of Military Affairs. A soldier's wife named Yang killed a neighbor's child, took his arm bracelet, and abandoned the corpse. When the case was concluded, the Ministry of Justice released her for lack of evidence. Xingyi was ordered to reinvestigate the case and established the facts. The emperor was pleased and granted him a residence. He left office to mourn his mother. When the mourning period ended, he resumed his former post and was appointed defense commissioner of Jun prefecture.
36
再使金，還，遷潭州觀察使。 復請祠，起知廬州，移知揚州。 揚與廬為鄰。 初，興裔在廬嚐卻鄰道互送禮，至是按郡籍，見前所卻者有出無歸，遂奏嚴其禁，揚有重屯，糧乏，例糴他境，興裔搜括滲漏以補之，食遂足。 民舊皆茅舍，易焚，興裔貸之錢，命易以瓦，自是火患乃息。 又奏免其償，民甚德之。 修學宮，立義塚，定部轄民兵升差法，郡以大治。 楚州議改築城，有謂韓世忠遺基不可易者，命興裔往視，既至，闕地丈餘增築之。 帝閱奏，喜曰：“興裔不吾欺也。 ”
He again served as envoy to Jin. On his return he was transferred to observation commissioner of Tan prefecture. He again requested an abbey appointment, was recalled to serve as prefect of Lu prefecture, and was transferred to prefect of Yang prefecture. Yang and Lu were neighboring prefectures. At first, when Xingyi was in Lu, he had refused the neighboring circuit's mutual gift exchanges. When he arrived in Yang he checked the prefectural registers and saw that what had previously been refused went out but did not return, and he memorialized to tighten the prohibition. Yang had a large garrison and grain was scarce; it was customary to purchase grain from other regions. Xingyi searched out leaks and seepage to make up the shortfall, and provisions became sufficient. The people had formerly lived in thatched huts that burned easily. Xingyi lent them money and ordered that they be replaced with tile, and from then on fire disasters ceased. He also memorialized to exempt them from repayment, and the people were deeply grateful. He repaired the school palace, established a charity burial ground, and fixed the rules for promotion and demotion among the local militia under his jurisdiction. The prefecture was thereby greatly well governed. Chu prefecture debated rebuilding the city wall. Some said Han Shizhong's surviving foundations could not be changed. Xingyi was ordered to inspect the site. When he arrived, he left a gap of more than a zhang and built the wall higher. The emperor read the memorial and said with pleasure, "Xingyi has not deceived me." Closing quotation mark of the emperor's remark.
37
紹熙元年，遷保靜軍承宣使，召領內祠，充明堂大禮都大主管大內公事。 寧宗即位，除知明州兼沿海製置使。 告老，授武泰軍節度使。 卒，年七十四，贈太尉，諡忠肅。
In 1190 he was transferred to commissioner-in-chief of the Baojing army, summoned to head an inner abbey, and appointed overall supervisor of major inner palace affairs for the Bright Hall grand rite. When Emperor Ningzong ascended the throne, he was appointed prefect of Ming prefecture concurrently as coastal pacification commissioner. He reported old age and was granted military commissioner of the Wutai army. He died at seventy-four and was posthumously granted grand commandant and given the posthumous title Zhongsu.
38
興裔曆事四朝，以材名結主知，中興外族之賢，未有其比。 子三人：挺，以橫行團練使曆淮、襄兩道帥。 損，登進士甲科，與抗皆有位於朝。
Xingyi served through four reigns and won the ruler's trust through his talent and reputation. Among the worthy members of imperial consort families in the restoration era, none matched him. He had three sons: Ting, who as regiment commander through overbearing conduct successively served as commander of the Huai and Xiang circuits. Sun passed the jinshi examination in the first class, and both he and Kang held office at court.
39
楊次山，字仲甫，恭聖仁烈皇后兄也，其先開封人。 曾祖全，以材武奮，靖康末，捍京城死事。 祖漸，以遺澤補官，仕東南，家於越之上虞。
Yang Cishan, style name Zhongfu, was the elder brother of Empress Gongsheng Renlie. His ancestors were natives of Kaifeng. His great-grandfather Quan rose through martial talent and ability. At the end of the Jingkang era he died defending the capital in the line of duty. His grandfather Jian received office through inherited grace, served in the southeast, and made his home at Shangyu in Yue.
40
次山儀狀魁偉，少好學能文，補右學生。 後受職宮中，次山遂沾恩得官，積階至武德郎。 後為貴妃，累遷帶禦器械、知閣門事。 丐祠，除吉州刺史，提舉佑神觀。 後受冊，除福州觀察使，尋拜岳陽軍節度使。 後謁家廟，加太尉。 韓侂胄誅，加開府儀同三司。 尋進少保，封永陽郡王。 南郊恩加少傅，充萬壽觀使。 致仕，加太保，授安德軍、昭慶軍節度使，改封會稽郡王。
Cishan was imposing in bearing and stature. In youth he loved learning and was skilled at writing, and was appointed a student of the Right Study Hall. When his younger sister received a post in the palace, Cishan gained office through her grace and accumulated ranks up to Wude Lang. When she became noble consort, he was repeatedly promoted to bearer of the imperial armory and director of the gate. He requested an abbey appointment and was made prefect of Ji prefecture and supervisor of the Youshen Abbey. When she received her investiture, he was made observation commissioner of Fuzhou and soon afterward appointed military commissioner of the Yueyang army. When the empress visited the family temple, he was granted grand commandant. When Han Tuozhou was executed, Cishan was granted grand preceptor with protocol equal to the three excellencies. Soon afterward he was promoted to junior guardian and enfeoffed as Prince of Yongyang. At the suburban sacrifice grace he was further made junior tutor and appointed commissioner of the Wanshou Abbey. He retired and was granted grand guardian, appointed military commissioner of the Ande and Zhaoping armies, and enfeoffed as Prince of Kuaiji.
41
次山能避權勢，不預國事，時論賢之。 嘉定十二年，卒，年八十一，贈太師，追封冀王。 子二人。 穀，至太傅、保寧軍節度使，充萬壽觀使、永寧郡王。
Cishan kept his distance from power and did not participate in state affairs. Contemporary opinion deemed him worthy. In 1219 he died at eighty-one, was posthumously granted grand preceptor, and was enfeoffed posthumously as Prince of Ji. He had two sons. Gu rose to grand tutor and military commissioner of the Baoning army, served as commissioner of the Wanshou Abbey, and was enfeoffed as Prince of Yongning.
42
石，字介之，乾道間入武學，以恭聖仁烈後貴，賜第。 慶元中，補承信郎，差充閣門看班祗候，尋帶禦器械。 嘉泰四年，充賀正旦接伴使。 時金使頗驕倨，自矜其善射，石從容起，挽弦三發三中的，金使氣沮。 嘉定改元，除揚州觀察使、知閣門事，進保寧承宣使。 久之，授保寧節度使，提舉萬壽觀，奉朝請，進封信安郡侯。 十五年，以檢校少保進封開國公。
Shi, style name Jiezhi, entered the Military Academy during Qiandao. Through the eminence of Empress Gongsheng Renlie he was granted a mansion. During Qingyuan he was appointed trustworthy-in-office gentleman, assigned as attendant on duty at the gate, and soon made bearer of the imperial armory. In 1204 he served as reception envoy for congratulating the New Year's Day. At that time the Jin envoy was quite arrogant, boasting of his skill in archery. Shi rose calmly, drew the bow three times and hit the target three times, and the Jin envoy's spirit was deflated. At the change of reign title in Jiading, he was made observation commissioner of Yang prefecture and director of the gate and was promoted to commissioner-in-chief of Baoning. After a long time he was appointed military commissioner of the Baoning army, supervisor of the Wanshou Abbey, and retainer at court, and was advanced and enfeoffed as Marquis of Xin'an. In the fifteenth year, as honorary junior guardian he was advanced and enfeoffed as duke of the state.
43
寧宗崩，宰相史彌遠謀廢皇子竑而立成國公昀，命石與穀白後，後不可，曰：“皇子，先帝所立，豈敢擅變。 ”穀、石凡一夜七往反以告，後終不聽。 穀等拜泣曰：“內外軍民皆已歸心，苟不從，禍變必生，則楊氏且無噍類矣！ ”後默然良久，曰：“其人安在？ ”彌遠等召昀入，遂矯詔廢竑為濟王，立昀，是為理宗。 授開府儀同三司，充萬壽觀使。
When Emperor Ningzong died, chief councillor Shi Miyuan plotted to depose Prince Gong and establish the Duke of Chengguo Yun. He ordered Shi and Gu to inform the empress dowager. She would not agree and said, "The prince was established by the late emperor. How dare we change this on our own authority?" Gu and Shi went back and forth seven times in one night to report, but the empress dowager ultimately would not listen. Gu and the others bowed and wept, saying, "Soldiers and civilians inside and outside have all given their hearts. If you do not comply, calamity will surely arise, and then the Yang clan will have no one left alive!" The empress dowager was silent for a long while and then said, "Where is that man?" Miyuan and the others summoned Yun in. They then forged an edict deposing Gong as Prince of Ji and establishing Yun, who became Emperor Lizong. Shi was granted grand preceptor with protocol equal to the three excellencies and appointed commissioner of the Wanshou Abbey.
44
時寶慶垂簾，人多言本朝世有母後之聖。 石獨曰：“事豈容概言？ 昔仁宗、英宗、哲宗嗣位，或尚在幼衝，或素由撫育，軍國重事有所未諳，則母後臨朝，宜也。 今主上熟知民事，天下悅服，雖聖孝天通，然不早復政，得無基小人離間之嫌乎？ ”乃密疏章獻、慈聖、宣仁所以臨朝之由，遠及漢、唐母後臨朝稱製得失上之，後覽奏，即命擇日徹簾。 進石少保，封永寧郡王。 以壽明慈睿仁福三冊太后寶，進至太傅。
At that time, during the Baoning curtain audience, many people said that this dynasty had sagely empress dowagers generation after generation. Shi alone said, "Can such matters be spoken of in general terms?" In the past, when Emperor Renzong, Emperor Yingzong, and Emperor Zhezong succeeded to the throne, some were still in infancy and some had long been raised under care. If they were not yet versed in the weighty affairs of army and state, then it was fitting for an empress dowager to hold court. Now the sovereign is well versed in the affairs of the people and the realm is pleased and submissive. Although his sagely filial piety reaches heaven, if he does not soon restore government to himself, might this not give petty men grounds to suspect sowing division? He submitted a secret memorial on the reasons Zhangxian, Cisheng, and Xuanren held court, extending back to the gains and losses when empress dowagers of Han and Tang held court and assumed imperial style. When the empress dowager read it, she at once ordered a day chosen to withdraw the curtain audience. Shi was promoted to junior guardian and enfeoffed as Prince of Yongning. For the three investitures of the empress dowager's seals of Shouming, Cirui, and Renfu, he was advanced to grand tutor.
45
石性恬淡，每拜爵命必力辭。 恭聖祔廟，除太師。 兄穀疑於辭受，石力言曰：“吾家非有元勳盛德，徒以恭聖故致貴顯，曩吾父不居是官，吾兄弟今偃然受之，是將自速顛覆耳。 矧恭聖抑遠族屬，意慮深遠，言猶在耳，何可遽忘？ ”乃合疏懇辭，至再三，不受。 及屬疾，除彰德、集慶節度使，進封魏郡王。 卒，年七十一，贈太師。
Shi was tranquil by nature and whenever ennobled or appointed always strenuously declined. When Gongsheng was enshrined in the ancestral temple, he was appointed grand preceptor. His elder brother Gu hesitated over whether to decline or accept. Shi spoke forcefully, "Our family has no founding merit or great virtue. We have become eminent only because of Gongsheng. Formerly our father did not hold this office. If we brothers now accept it complacently, we will hasten our own downfall." Moreover, Gongsheng restrained distant kinsmen. Her thought was far-reaching, and her words are still in our ears. How can we forget them so soon? They submitted a joint memorial earnestly declining, again and again, and did not accept. When he fell ill, he was appointed military commissioner of the Zhangde and Jiqing armies and was advanced and enfeoffed as Prince of Wei. He died at seventy-one and was posthumously granted grand preceptor.
