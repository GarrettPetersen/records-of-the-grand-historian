1
王景，萊州掖人，家世力田。 景少倜儻，善騎射，不事生業，結里中惡少為群盜。 梁大將王檀鎮滑臺，以景隸麾下，與後唐莊宗戰河上，檀有功，景嘗左右之。 莊宗入汴，景來降，累遷奉聖都虞候。 清泰末，從張敬達圍晉陽，會契丹來援，景以所部歸晉祖。
Wang Jing came from Ye in Laizhou. For generations his family had been farmers. In his youth Wang Jing was bold and unrestrained, skilled in horsemanship and archery. He neglected honest work and banded with local rowdies to become a gang of brigands. The Later Liang general Wang Tan held Huatai and took Jing into his service. When Tan fought the Later Tang Emperor Zhuangzong along the Yellow River, Tan won credit in battle and Jing often fought at his side. After Zhuangzong entered Bian, Jing surrendered and rose through successive appointments to Chief Commandant of the Fengsheng Guard. Late in the Qingtai reign he followed Zhang Jingda in the siege of Jinyang. When the Khitans marched to relieve the city, Jing defected with his troops to the Later Jin founder.
2
天福初，授相州刺史。 范延光據鄴叛，屬郡多為所脅從，景獨分兵拒守，晉祖嘉之，遷耀州團練使。 及代，會晉祖幸鄴，留為京城巡檢使，改洺州團練使。 開運初，授侍衛馬軍左廂都校。 二年，契丹南侵，少帝幸澶淵，景與高行周等大破契丹眾於戚城，遷侍衛馬軍都指揮使、領鄭州防禦使，出為晉州巡檢使、知州事，拜橫海軍節度。 契丹至汴，以其黨代景。 景歸次常山，聞契丹主殂欒城，即間道歸鎮，斬關而入，契丹遁去。
Early in the Tianfu reign he was made Prefect of Xiangzhou. When Fan Yanguang rebelled and held Ye, most neighboring prefectures were forced to join him, but Jing alone sent detachments to resist and hold his ground. The Later Jin founder commended him and appointed him Militia Commissioner of Yaozhou. After the change of dynasty, while the Later Jin founder was visiting Ye, Jing was left in the capital as Patrol Commissioner and later transferred to Militia Commissioner of Mingzhou. Early in the Kaiyun reign he was appointed Commander of the Left Wing of the Palace Cavalry. In the second year of the reign the Khitans invaded from the north. The young emperor went to Chanyuan. Jing joined Gao Xingzhou and others in a great victory over the Khitans at Qi City. He was promoted to Commander-in-Chief of the Palace Cavalry and Defender of Zhengzhou, then sent out as Patrol Commissioner and acting prefect of Jinzhou, and finally enfeoffed as Military Governor of Henghai. When the Khitans reached Bian, they replaced him with one of their own men. Jing paused at Changshan. When he heard that the Khitan emperor had died at Luancheng, he took a hidden route back to his post, forced the passes open, and entered; the Khitans withdrew.
3
漢乾祐初，加同平章事。 會契丹饑，幽州民多度關求食，至滄州境者五千餘人，景善懷撫，詔給田處之。
Early in the Qianyou reign of Later Han he was made Co-Grand Councillor. Famine among the Khitans drove many people of Youzhou across the passes in search of food. More than five thousand reached Cangzhou. Jing received them with kindness, and the court ordered fields allotted to settle them.
4
宋初，加守太保，封太原郡王。 建隆二年春來朝，太祖宴賜加等，復以為鳳翔節度、西面緣邊都部署。 四年，卒，年七十五。 贈太傅，追封岐王，謚元靖。
Early in the Song dynasty he was made Acting Grand Guardian and enfeoffed as Prince of Taiyuan. In the spring of the second year of Jianlong he came to court. Taizu entertained him with a feast and added honors, then reappointed him Military Governor of Fengxiang and Overall Commander of the Western Frontier. In the fourth year he died at the age of seventy-five. He was posthumously honored as Grand Tutor, posthumously enfeoffed as Prince of Qi, and given the posthumous name Yuanjing.
5
初，景之奔晉也，妻坐戮，二子逃獲免。 晉祖待之厚，賞賜萬計，嘗問景所欲，對曰：「臣自歸國，受恩隆厚，誠無所欲。 」固問之，景稽顙再拜曰：「臣昔為卒，嘗負胡床從隊長出入，屢過官妓侯小師家，意甚慕之。 今妻被誅，誠得小師為妻足矣。 」晉祖大笑，即以小師賜景。 景甚寵嬖之，後累封楚國夫人。 侯氏嘗盜景金數百兩，私遺舊人，景知而不責。
When Jing had first defected to Later Jin, his wife was executed for his crime, but his two sons escaped and survived. The Later Jin founder treated him with great generosity, rewarding him lavishly on countless occasions. Once he asked what Jing wanted. Jing answered, "Since I came over to Your Majesty I have received such deep favor that I truly want nothing." " When the emperor pressed him again, Jing kowtowed and said, "When I was a common soldier I used to carry a camp stool for my squad leader and often passed the house of the official courtesan Hou Xiaoshi. I admired her greatly." Now that my wife has been put to death, if I could have Xiaoshi for my wife, that would be enough." " The Later Jin founder laughed heartily and at once gave him Hou Xiaoshi. Jing cherished her deeply, and she was later raised in rank to Lady of the State of Chu. Lady Hou once stole several hundred taels of his gold and secretly gave them to old friends. Jing knew of it but did not reproach her.
6
性謙退，折節下士，每朝廷使至，雖卑位必降階送迎，周旋盡禮。 左右或曰：「王位尊崇，無自謙抑。 」景曰：「人臣重君命，固當如是，我惟恐不謹耳。 」初封郡王，朝廷以吏部尚書張昭將命，景尤加禮重，以萬餘緡遺昭。 左右或言其過厚，景曰：「我在行伍間，即聞張尚書名，今使於我，是朝廷厚我也，豈可以往例為限耶？」
By nature he was modest and unassuming, treating men of learning with deference. Whenever an envoy from court arrived, no matter how low his rank, Jing would go down the steps to welcome and escort him, observing every point of ceremony. His attendants sometimes said, "Your rank as prince is exalted. You need not humble yourself so." " Jing replied, "A subject who honors the ruler's commission ought to act in just this way. I only fear that I am not careful enough." " When he was first enfeoffed as a commandery prince, the court sent Zhang Zhao, Minister of Personnel, to invest him. Jing treated him with exceptional respect and gave him more than ten thousand strings of cash. His attendants said the gift was too lavish. Jing said, "Even when I was in the ranks I had heard of Minister Zhang. That the court sends him to me is a mark of honor. How can I be limited by ordinary precedent?"
7
景子廷義、廷睿、廷訓。 廷訓至驍衛大將軍致仕。
Jing's sons were Tingyi, Tingrui, and Tingxun. Tingxun rose to Grand General of the Valiant Guard and then retired.
8
廷義起家供奉官，改如京副使，以善騎射，周世宗擢為虎捷都虞候，遷龍捷右第二軍都校、領珍州刺史。 宋初，改內外馬步軍副都軍頭。 乾德四年，與韓重贇率師護治滑州靈河新堤。 六年，增治京城，又命廷義董其役。 開寶二年，加領橫州團練使，從征太原。 廷義性勇敢，親鼓士乘城，獨免胄，矢中其腦而顛，經宿卒，年四十七。 太祖甚惜之，優詔贈建雄軍節度。
Tingyi began as a Palace Attendant and became Vice Commissioner for the Capital. Skilled in horsemanship and archery, he was promoted by Later Zhou Shizong to Chief Commandant of the Tiger Swift Guard, then made Commander of the Second Army of the Right Dragon Swift Guard and Prefect of Zhenzhou. Early in the Song he was appointed Deputy Commander-in-Chief of the Inner and Outer Horse and Foot Armies. In the fourth year of Qiande he joined Han Chongyun in leading troops to repair the new Ling River embankment at Huazhou. In the sixth year the capital was expanded and repaired, and Tingyi was again put in charge of the project. In the second year of Kaibao he was also made Militia Commissioner of Hengzhou and took part in the campaign against Taiyuan. Tingyi was naturally courageous. He personally rallied the men onto the walls, alone removed his helmet, was struck in the head by an arrow and fell, and died the next day at the age of forty-seven. Taizu deeply mourned his loss and by gracious edict posthumously made him Military Governor of Jianxiong.
9
廷睿性驕傲，好夸誕，每言：「我當代王景之子。 」聞者咸笑之，因目為『王當代』。
Tingrui was arrogant and fond of grandiose boasts. He often said, "I am the successor to Wang Jing's sons." " Everyone who heard him laughed, and people nicknamed him "Wang the Successor."
10
王晏，徐州滕人，家世力田。 晏少壯勇無賴，嘗率群寇行攻劫。 梁末，徐方大亂，屬邑皆為他盜所剽，惟晏鄉里恃晏獲全。 後唐同光中，應募隸禁軍，累遷奉國小校。
Wang Yan came from Teng in Xuzhou. For generations his family had been farmers. In his youth Wang Yan was strong, fierce, and lawless, and once led a band of raiders on attacks and plunder. Late in the Later Liang, the Xu region fell into chaos. Neighboring districts were plundered by other bandits, but Yan's home village alone was spared because it relied on him. During the Tongguang reign of Later Tang he enlisted in the Palace Guard and rose to Junior Officer of the Fengguo Guard.
11
晉開運末，與本軍都校趙暉、忠衛都校侯章等戍陜州。 會契丹至汴，遣其將劉愿據陜，恣行暴虐，晏與暉等謀曰：「今契丹南侵，天下洶洶，英雄豪傑固當乘時自奮。 且聞太原劉公威德遠被，人心歸服，若殺愿送款河東，為天下倡首，則取富貴如反掌耳。 」暉等然之。 晏乃率敢死士數人夜踰城，入府署，劫庫兵給其徒，遲明，斬愿首級府門外。 眾請暉為帥，章為本城副指揮使、內外巡檢使兼都虞候； 遣其子漢倫奉表晉陽。 時漢祖雖建號，威聲未振，得晏等來歸，甚喜，即日以暉為保平軍節度，章為鎮國軍節度，晏為絳州防禦使，仍領舊職。 既而暉等表晏始謀功為第一，遷建雄軍節度。 漢祖入汴，加同平章事。
Late in the Kaiyun reign of Later Jin he was posted to Shaanzhou with his army commander Zhao Hui, Loyal Guard commander Hou Zhang, and others. When the Khitans reached Bian they sent their general Liu Yuan to hold Shaan, where he ruled with violence and cruelty. Yan and Hui plotted together, saying, "The Khitans are invading from the north and the realm is in turmoil. Men of talent ought to seize the moment and rise." We hear that Lord Liu of Taiyuan's fame and virtue reach far and that the people look to him. If we kill Yuan and submit to Hedong, taking the lead for the realm, wealth and honor will be ours in an instant." " Hui and the others agreed. Yan led a handful of dare-to-die men over the wall by night, entered the government compound, seized arms from the arsenal for his followers, and at dawn cut off Yuan's head and displayed it outside the gate. The troops asked that Hui be commander and Zhang Deputy Commander of the garrison, Inner and Outer Patrol Commissioner, and Chief Commandant; and sent his son Hanlun to present a memorial at Jinyang. Though the Later Han founder had already proclaimed his reign, his authority was not yet secure. When Yan and the others submitted, he was delighted. That same day he made Hui Military Governor of Baoping, Zhang Military Governor of Zhenguo, and Yan Defender of Jiangzhou while keeping his former post. Soon afterward Hui and the others reported that Yan had conceived the plot and ranked his merit first, and he was transferred to Military Governor of Jianxiong. When the Later Han founder entered Bian, Yan was made Co-Grand Councillor.
12
初，晏至鎮，悉召故時同為盜者遺以金帛，從容置酒語之曰：「吾鄉素多盜，我與諸君昔嘗為之。 後來者固當出諸君之下，為我告諭，令不復為，若不能改，吾必盡滅其族。 」由是境內安靜，吏民詣闕舉留，請為晏立衣錦碑。 世宗初，復請立德政碑。 世宗命比部郎中、知制誥張正撰文賜之，詔改其鄉里為使相鄉勳德里，私門立戟。 未幾，改河南尹、西京留守。 顯德三年，移鳳翔節度。 六年，從世宗北征，為益津關一路馬軍都部署，韓令坤副焉，遂平三關。
When Yan first took up his post he summoned all his former fellow bandits, gave them gold and silks, and over wine said calmly, "Our district has always had many bandits, and I once did the same with you." Those who come after ought to defer to you. Tell them for me to stop. If they will not change, I shall exterminate their families to the last man." " From then on his territory was at peace. Officials and commoners went to court to praise him and ask that he be retained, and requested a stele commemorating his return in glory. Early in Shizong's reign they again asked for a stele commemorating his virtuous rule. Shizong ordered Zhang Zheng, Director in the Ministry of Revenue and Drafter of Imperial Edicts, to compose the inscription. An edict renamed his home village Merit Lane in Envoy-Minister Village and granted halberds at his private gate. Before long he was made Governor of Henan and Regent of the Western Capital. In the third year of Xiande he was transferred to Military Governor of Fengxiang. In the sixth year he followed Shizong on the northern campaign as Overall Cavalry Commander for the Yijin Pass route, with Han Lingkun as his deputy, and helped secure the Three Passes.
13
太祖即位，進封趙國公。 從征李筠，師還，改安遠軍節度。 乾德元年，進封韓國公，上章請老，拜太子太師致仕。 每朝會，令綴中書門下班。 俄歸洛陽別墅。 四年冬，卒，年七十七。 廢朝三日，贈中書令。
When Taizu took the throne, Yan was promoted and enfeoffed as Duke of Zhao. He took part in the campaign against Li Jun, and when the army returned he was made Military Governor of Anyuan. In the first year of Qiande he was promoted to Duke of Han, asked to retire, and was appointed Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent before leaving office. At each court assembly he was placed at the end of the Zhongshu Gate line. Before long he retired to his villa in Luoyang. In the winter of the fourth year he died at the age of seventy-seven. Court mourning lasted three days, and he was posthumously honored as Chief Councillor of the Central Secretariat.
14
初，晏為軍校，與平陸人王興善，其妻亦相為娣姒。 晏既貴，乃薄興，興不能平。 晏妻病，興語人曰：「吾能治之。 」晏遽訪興，興曰：「我非能醫，但以公在陜時止一妻，今妓妾甚眾，得非待糟糠之薄，致夫人怏怏成疾耶？ 若能斥去女侍，夫人之疾可立愈。 」晏以為謗己，乃誣以他事，悉案誅其夫妻。
When Yan was still an army officer he was close friends with Wang Xing of Pinglu, and their wives were sisters-in-law. After Yan rose to prominence he treated Xing coldly, and Xing could not accept it. When Yan's wife fell ill, Xing told people, "I can cure her." " Yan hurried to see him. Xing said, "I am no physician, but when you were at Shaan you had only one wife. Now you have many singing girls and concubines. Could it be that neglecting the wife who shared your hardships has made her ill with resentment?" If you dismiss your female attendants, your wife's illness will clear at once." " Yan took this as an insult, framed them on other charges, and had husband and wife executed after investigation.
15
守西洛日，白重贊鎮河陽，時世宗征淮南，重贊慮幷人乘間為寇，因葺城壘，且約晏為援。 晏意欲兼有三城，即與漢倫同率兵赴之。 重贊聞其來，拒不納，遣人語之曰：「公在陜州已立大功，河陽小城不煩枉駕。 」慚不能對，遂引兵還。
While Yan held the Western Capital, Bai Chongzan was posted at Heyang. Shizong was campaigning in Huainan, and Chongzan feared that the Bing people might raid while he was away, so he repaired the fortifications and asked Yan for support. Yan hoped to seize all three cities for himself and marched with Hanlun to Heyang. Chongzan heard he was coming, refused him entry, and sent word, "You have already won great merit at Shaanzhou. This little city of Heyang does not require your personal visit." " Ashamed, he had no reply and withdrew his troops.
16
郭從義
Guo Congyi
17
郭從義，其先沙陀部人。 父紹古，事後唐武皇忠謹，特見信任，賜姓李氏。 紹古卒，從義才丱角，莊宗畜於宮中，與諸子齒。 明宗與紹古同事武皇，情好款狎，即位，以從義補內職，累遷內園使。
Guo Congyi's ancestors were of the Shatuo tribe. His father Shaogu served the Later Tang Martial Emperor with loyal devotion, won exceptional trust, and was granted the surname Li. After Shaogu died, Congyi was still a small child. Zhuangzong raised him in the palace and treated him as one of his own sons. Mingzong and Shaogu had served the Martial Emperor together and were close friends. When Mingzong took the throne he gave Congyi an inner palace post and eventually made him Commissioner of the Inner Gardens.
18
晉天福初，始復姓郭氏。 坐事出為宿州團練副使。 丁內艱北歸，遂家太原。 漢祖在鎮，表為馬步軍都虞候，屢率師破破契丹於代北，及建大號，從義首贊其謀，擢鄭州防禦使，充東南道行營都虞候，領首軍自太行路渡河。
Early in the Tianfu reign of Later Jin he resumed the surname Guo. After an offense he was sent out as Deputy Militia Commissioner of Suzhou. After his mother's death he returned north and settled in Taiyuan. While the Later Han founder held his post, he recommended Congyi as Chief Commandant of the Horse and Foot Armies. Congyi repeatedly led troops to defeat the Khitans north of Dai. When the founder proclaimed his reign, Congyi was the first to support his plans. He was promoted to Defender of Zhengzhou, made Chief Commandant of the Southeastern Route Army, and led the vanguard across the Yellow River by the Taihang route.
19
漢祖入汴，以為河北都巡檢使。 杜重威據大名叛，以為行營諸軍都虞候，重威降，為鎮寧軍節度。 趙思綰之叛，為行營都部署，賜戎裝、器仗、金帶。 師至永興，圍其城，即以從義為永興軍節度。 思綰糧盡，城中人相食，從義繫書矢上，射入城中，說思綰令降，仍表於朝廷，許以華州節制。 隱帝從其計，即遣使諭思綰，思綰開門納款。 翌日，從義具軍容入城，憩候館中，思綰入謁，即令武士執之，並其黨三百餘人悉斬於市，以功加同平章事。 周廣順初，加兼侍中，移鎮許州。 顯德初，親郊，加檢校太師。 世宗將征劉崇，從義適來朝，因請扈從，世宗甚悅，改天平軍節度，即令從符彥卿破契丹於忻口。 師還，以功加兼中書令。 四年，從征淮南，移鎮徐州。 及世宗自迎鑾至泗州，見於行在。 恭帝即位，加開府階。
When the Later Han founder entered Bian, Congyi was appointed Overall Patrol Commissioner of Hebei. When Du Chongwei rebelled and held Daming, Congyi was made Chief Commandant of All Armies of the Field Headquarters. After Chongwei surrendered, Congyi was made Military Governor of Zhenning. During Zhao Sihuan's rebellion he was made Overall Commander of the Field Headquarters and granted military dress, arms, and a gold belt. When the army reached Yongxing and besieged the city, Congyi was at once made Military Governor of Yongxing. When Sihuan's supplies ran out the people in the city resorted to cannibalism. Congyi tied a letter to an arrow, shot it into the city, and urged him to surrender. He also memorialized the court, offering him the Huazhou command. Emperor Yindi accepted his plan and sent envoys to instruct Sihuan, who opened the gates and submitted. The next day Congyi entered the city in full military array and rested at the reception lodge. When Sihuan came to pay his respects, Congyi had warriors seize him. More than three hundred of his followers were beheaded in the market. For this achievement he was made Co-Grand Councillor. Early in the Guangshun reign of Later Zhou he was also made Palace Attendant and transferred to Xuzhou. Early in Xiande he performed the suburban sacrifice in person and was made Acting Grand Preceptor. When Shizong was about to campaign against Liu Chong, Congyi happened to be at court and asked to accompany him. Shizong was delighted, made him Military Governor of Tianping, and ordered him to join Fu Yanqing in defeating the Khitans at Xinkou. When the army returned, he was also made Chief Councillor of the Central Secretariat for his achievements. In the fourth year he joined the Huainan campaign and was transferred to Xuzhou. When Shizong traveled from Yingluan to Sizhou, Congyi met him at the field headquarters. When Emperor Gong took the throne, he was granted the rank of Opening the Government.
20
宋初，加守中書令。 太祖征揚州，從義迎謁於路，願扈從，不允。 乾德二年，又為河中尹、護國國節度。 六年，以疾歸京師。 開寶二年，改左金吾衛上將軍。 逾年，上章請老，拜太子太師致仕。 四年，卒，年六十三，贈中書令。
Early in the Song dynasty he was made Acting Chief Councillor of the Central Secretariat. When Taizu campaigned against Yangzhou, Congyi met him on the road and asked to accompany him, but was refused. In the second year of Qiande he was again made Governor of Hezhong and Military Governor of Huguo. In the sixth year he returned to the capital because of illness. In the second year of Kaibao he was made Grand General of the Left Golden Crow Guard. A year later he asked to retire and was appointed Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent before leaving office. In the fourth year he died at the age of sixty-three and was posthumously honored as Chief Councillor of the Central Secretariat.

侯章，幷州榆次人。 初在幷門事後唐莊宗為隊長，明宗朝遷小校。 晉開運末，為忠衛指揮使，屯兵陜州，為內外馬步軍都指揮使兼三城巡檢使。
Hou Zhang came from Yuci in Bingzhou. He first served at Bing Gate under Later Tang Zhuangzong as squad leader and was promoted to junior officer under Mingzong. Late in the Kaiyun reign of Later Jin he commanded the Loyal Guard, garrisoned Shaanzhou, and was made Commander-in-Chief of the Inner and Outer Horse and Foot Armies and Patrol Commissioner of the Three Cities.
47
會契丹入中原，與趙暉、王晏謀斬契丹將劉愿，送款於漢祖。 漢祖入汴，擢為鎮國軍節度。 乾祐初，加同平章事，尋移鎮邠州。 章居鎮無善政，傲上剝下，以貪猥聞，用見戶為逃，擅其租賦，乃矯奏貧民數千戶負稅租，久禁繫不能輸，願以己俸代。 時方姑息，詔褒之。 副使趙彥鐸有良馬，章欲之不與，誣彥鐸謀逆之，殺之，亦置而不問。 俄加檢校太師。
When the Khitans entered the Central Plains, he joined Zhao Hui and Wang Yan in plotting to kill the Khitan general Liu Yuan and submit to the Later Han founder. When the Later Han founder entered Bian, Zhang was promoted to Military Governor of Zhenguo. Early in Qianyou he was made Co-Grand Councillor, then soon transferred to Binzhou. In his commandery Zhang governed badly. He was arrogant toward superiors and exploited subordinates, and was known for greed and corruption. He treated registered households as fugitives and seized their taxes. He then falsely reported that several thousand poor households owed taxes, had long been imprisoned unable to pay, and offered to cover the debt from his own salary. The court was then in an indulgent mood and issued an edict praising him. Deputy Commissioner Zhao Yanduo had a fine horse. Zhang wanted it but Yanduo refused. Zhang falsely accused him of plotting rebellion and killed him, and this too went unpunished. Soon he was also made Acting Grand Preceptor.
48
論曰：王景輩微時，或至為盜、負薪，遭五代之亂，奮身戎功，重據邊要。 宋興，稽顙北嚮，太祖待以誠信，宜無不自安者。 景趨利改圖，乃至滅族。 王晏、郭從義遷怒肆忿，誣人以死。 侯章在藩邸有剝下之名，李洪義狃於肺腑之戚，而無外凜之志，咎孰甚焉！ 斯皆亂世之習，有不能盡去之者。 武行德守洛邑，辯究欺罔，民用畏服，顧不優於諸人耶？
Commentary: Men like Wang Jing in humble times sometimes became bandits or gathered firewood. In the chaos of the Five Dynasties they threw themselves into military service and repeatedly held important frontier posts. When the Song arose they bowed to the north. Taizu treated them with sincerity and trust, and they ought all to have felt secure. Yet when Jing turned for profit and shifted his allegiance, his clan was destroyed. Wang Yan and Guo Congyi vented their anger on others and framed men to their deaths. Hou Zhang was known in his commandery for exploiting subordinates. Li Hongyi relied on imperial kinship but lacked proper respect for authority — whose fault was worse! These were habits of a chaotic age that could not be entirely cast off. Wu Xingde held Luoyang, investigated fraud, and made the people fear and respect him — was he not superior to the rest?
