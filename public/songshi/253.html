1
折德扆
Zhe Deyi
2
折德扆，世居雲中，為大族。 父從阮，自晉、漢以來，獨據府州，控扼西北，中國賴之。 仕周至靜難軍節度使。 其鎮府州時，署德扆為馬步軍都校。 廣順間，周世宗建府州為永安軍，以德扆為節度使，時從阮鎮邠寧，父子俱領節鎮，時人榮之。
Zhe Deyi came from a great clan that had long lived in Yunzhong. His father Zhe Congruan had held Fuzhou alone since the Jin and Han periods, commanding the northwest frontier on which the Central Court depended. Under the Zhou he rose to become military commissioner of the Jingnan army. While governing Fuzhou, he appointed Deyi commander of the combined horse and foot forces. In the Guangshun period Emperor Shizong of Zhou made Fuzhou the Yong'an army and appointed Deyi its military commissioner, while Congruan held Binning—father and son both commanding frontier circuits, to the envy of their contemporaries.
3
顯德中，德扆率師攻下河市鎮，斬幷軍五百餘級。 入朝，以其弟德愿權總州事。 時世宗南征，還次通許橋，德扆迎謁，且請遷內地。 世宗以其素得蕃情，不許，厚加賜賚而遣之。 德扆未至，德愿又破幷軍五百餘於沙谷砦，斬其將郝章、張釗。
During the Xiande reign Deyi led an assault that captured Hexiahe town and killed more than five hundred soldiers of Bingzhou. He went to court and left his younger brother Deyuan in charge of the prefecture. The emperor was returning from his southern campaign and halted at Tongxu Bridge; Deyi came to pay his respects and asked to be moved inland. The emperor refused, saying Deyi had long understood the frontier tribes, but sent him home with generous gifts. Before Deyi returned, Deyuan routed another five hundred Bingzhou troops at Shagu fort and killed their generals Hao Zhang and Zhang Zhao.
4
子御勳
Son: Yuxun
5
御勳，字世隆，德扆鎮府州日，表為右職。 德扆卒，以御勳領汾州團練使、權知府州事。 開寶二年，太祖征太原，御勳詣行在謁見，以為永安軍留後。 四年，以郊祀來朝，禮畢歸鎮。 九年，郊祀西洛，復來朝，道病後期，改泰寧軍節度使，留京師。 太平興國二年，卒，年四十，贈侍中。
Yuxun, styled Shilong, was recommended for a senior staff post while his father governed Fuzhou. After Deyi's death Yuxun was made regimental commander of Fenzhou and acting prefect of Fuzhou. In 969, when Taizu marched on Taiyuan, Yuxun came to the imperial camp and was appointed acting commissioner of the Yong'an army. Four years later he came to court for the suburban sacrifice and returned to his post when the rites ended. In 976 he came again for the suburban sacrifice at Luoyang, fell ill on the road and arrived late, was made military commissioner of the Taining army, and was kept at the capital. He died in 977 at the age of forty and was posthumously made palace attendant.
6
子御卿
Son: Yuqing
7
御卿，幼補節院使，御勳知州事，署為兵馬都校。 御勳徙鎮，召為閑廄副使、知府州。 太宗征河東，命御卿與尹憲領屯兵同攻嵐州，又破岢嵐軍，擒其軍使折令圖以獻，遂下嵐州，又殺其憲州刺史霍翊，又擒其將馬延忠等七人。 遷崇儀使。
Yuqing entered service young as a military-commission envoy; when Yuxun governed the prefecture he was appointed commander of the garrison. When Yuxun was transferred elsewhere, Yuqing was summoned as deputy commissioner of the imperial stud and made prefect of Fuzhou. During Taizong's Hedong campaign Yuqing and Yin Xian jointly led garrison troops against Lanzhou, routed the Kelan army, captured its commander Zhe Lingtu and presented him, took the city, killed Xianzhou prefect Huo Yi, and seized seven enemy generals including Ma Yanzhong. He was promoted to commissioner of honored rites.
8
淳化三年，凡四遷而為府州觀察使。 五年，拜永安軍節度使。 既而契丹眾萬餘入寇，御卿大敗之於子河氵義，斬首五千級，獲馬千匹，契丹將號突厥太尉、司徒、舍利死者二十餘人，擒其吐渾一人，自是契丹知所畏。 太宗因遣使問御卿曰：「西北要害皆屯勁兵，戎人何自而至？ 」御卿對曰：「敵緣山峽小徑入，謀剽略。 臣諜知之，遣人邀其歸路，因縱兵大擊，敗走之，人馬墜厓谷死者相忱，其大將韓德威僅以身免。 皆聖靈所及，非臣之功也。 」上嘉之。
By the third year of Chunhua, after four promotions, he had become observation commissioner of Fuzhou. Five years later he was formally appointed military commissioner of the Yong'an army. Soon afterward more than ten thousand Khitan invaded; Yuqing crushed them at Ziheyi, taking five thousand heads and a thousand horses, killing more than twenty Khitan officers bearing titles such as Tujue grand commandant, minister, and sheli, and capturing a Tuhun warrior—from then on the Khitan learned to fear him. Taizong sent an envoy to ask Yuqing, "Every key point in the northwest is held by strong garrisons—how could the barbarians have gotten through on their own?" Yuqing answered, "They came in by narrow mountain paths intending to raid. I learned this through spies, blocked their retreat, and struck hard; men and horses fell into ravines in heaps, and their commander Han Dewei barely escaped alive. That was your sacred power at work, not mine." The emperor praised him warmly.
9
歲餘，御卿被病，德威諜知之，且為李繼遷所誘，率眾來侵，以報子河氵義之役。 御卿力疾出戰，德威聞其至，不敢進。 會疾甚，其母密遣人召歸，御卿曰：「世受國恩，邊寇未滅，御卿罪也。 今臨敵棄士卒自便，不可，死於軍中乃其分也。 為白太夫人，無念我，忠孝豈兩全！ 」言訖泣下。 翌日卒，年三十八。 上聞悼惜久之，贈侍中，以其子惟正為洛苑使、知州事。 惟正歸朝，以其弟惟昌繼之。
More than a year later Yuqing fell ill; Han Dewei learned of it through spies and, urged on by Li Jiqian, led a force to invade and avenge Ziheyi. Yuqing dragged himself into battle despite his illness; when Han Dewei heard he had come out, he did not dare advance. When his illness worsened his mother secretly sent for him; Yuqing said, "Our family has owed the state for generations; if the border enemy is not destroyed, that is my fault. To abandon the troops now for my own comfort is impossible; dying in the ranks is my proper duty." Tell my mother not to worry about me—loyalty and filial piety cannot both be satisfied!" He wept as he finished speaking. He died the next day, aged thirty-eight. The emperor mourned him at length, posthumously made him palace attendant, and appointed his son Weizheng commissioner of the Luoyang park and prefect of Fuzhou. Weizheng returned to court and his younger brother Weichang succeeded him.
10
大中祥符二年，表求赴闕。 真宗命近臣與射於苑中，宴賜甚厚。 上言：「先臣御卿蒙賜旗三十竿以壯軍容，請別給賜。 」許之。 七年，命河東民運糧赴麟州，當出兵為援，惟昌力疾領步騎屯寧遠砦，冒風沙而行。 時疾已亟，猶與賓佐宴飲，談笑自若焉。 明日卒，年三十七。 以其弟惟忠繼之。
In 1009 he petitioned to come to court. Zhenzong had his close ministers shoot with him in the imperial park and entertained him with lavish gifts. He said, "My late father Yuqing was granted thirty banner poles to dignify his troops; I ask for a grant of my own." The request was granted. Seven years later, when Hedong civilians were ordered to haul grain to Linzhou and troops were required as escort, Weichang, though seriously ill, led infantry and cavalry to Ningyuan fort through wind and sand. Though his illness was grave, he still feasted with his staff, talking and laughing as if nothing were wrong. He died the next day, aged thirty-seven. His younger brother Weizhong succeeded him.
11
惟忠，字藎臣，初以兄惟信戰沒，補西頭供奉官，擢閣門祗候。 及惟昌卒，以惟忠為六宅使、知府州兼麟府路都巡檢使，領普州刺史； 再遷左藏庫使，真拜嘉州刺史，改資州，進簡州團練使。 喪母，起復雲麾將軍，卒。
Weizhong, styled Jinchen, first entered service as a western-head palace attendant because his elder brother Weixin had died in battle, then was promoted to gate commissioner. When Weichang died, Weizhong was made commissioner of the six residences, prefect of Fuzhou and overall inspector of the Lin-Fu circuit, and also served as prefect of Puzhou; he was later made commissioner of the left treasury, formally appointed prefect of Jiazhou, transferred to Zizhou, and promoted to regimental commander of Jianzhou. After his mother's death he was recalled from mourning as cloud-banner general and soon died.
12
惟忠知兵事。 天聖中，契丹與夏國會兵境上，聲言嫁娶，惟忠覘得其實，率麾下往備之，戒士卒毋輕動。 一夕風霾，有騎走營中，以為寇至，惟忠堅臥不動，徐命擒之，得數誕馬，蓋虜所縱也。 既卒，錄其弟侄子孫七人，以其子繼宣嗣州事。 久之，特贈惟忠耀州觀察使。
Weizhong was skilled in military affairs. During the Tiansheng reign the Khitan and Xia massed on the border under the pretense of a marriage alliance; Weizhong discovered their real intent, led his men to prepare, and ordered the troops not to stir rashly. One night in a sandstorm riders dashed into camp and were taken for the enemy; Weizhong stayed in bed unmoved, then calmly ordered them caught—they proved to be stray horses the barbarians had turned loose. After his death seven younger kinsmen were enrolled in office and his son Jixuan succeeded him as prefect. Long afterward Weizhong was posthumously made observation commissioner of Yaozhou.
13
寶元中，繼宣坐苛虐掊刻，種落嗟怨，絀為左監門衛將軍、楚州都監，擢其弟右侍禁繼閔為西京作坊使，嗣州事。
In the Baoyuan period Jixuan was demoted for harsh extortion that angered the tribes; he was made left gate guard general and superintendent of Chuzhou, while his younger brother Jimin, a right palace guard, was promoted to commissioner of the western capital workshops and succeeded him at Fuzhou.
14
繼閔，字廣孝。 慶歷中，元昊兵攻麟州不克，進圍州城。 城險且堅，東南有水門，厓壁峭絕，阻河。 賊緣厓腹微徑魚貫而前，城中矢石亂下，賊轉攻城北，士卒復力戰，賊死傷甚眾，遂引去，圍豐州，豐州遂陷。 繼閔以城守勞，特遷宮苑使、普州刺史。 未幾，護送麟州戍卒冬服，賊伏兵邀擊之，盡掠所賫，繼閔脫身繇間道歸。 會赦，止奪宮苑使，後復官，領果州團練使。 自元昊反，繼閔招輯歸業者三千餘戶。 皇祐二年，卒，以其弟繼祖嗣州事。
Jimin, styled Guangxiao. During the Qingli reign Yuan Hao's army failed to take Linzhou and turned to besiege the city. The city was strong and perilous; on the southeast was a water gate where sheer cliffs dropped to the river. The enemy filed along a narrow cliff path; the defenders rained arrows and stones upon them; when the enemy shifted to the north wall the garrison fought them off again with heavy losses; the enemy withdrew, besieged Fengzhou, and Fengzhou fell. For his defense of the city Jimin was specially promoted to commissioner of the imperial park and prefect of Puzhou. Soon afterward, while escorting winter uniforms for the Linzhou garrison, he was ambushed and everything was seized; Jimin escaped by a back trail. An amnesty spared him further punishment beyond loss of the imperial-park commission; he was later restored and made regimental commander of Guozhou. After Yuan Hao's rebellion Jimin resettled more than three thousand households who had fled. He died in 1050 and his younger brother Jizu succeeded him.
15
繼祖，字應之，由右侍禁遷西染院使，累轉皇城使、成州團練使。 臨政二十餘年。 奏乞書籍，仁宗賜以《九經》。 韓絳發河東兵城囉兀，繼祖為先鋒，深入敵帳，降部落戶八百。 加解州防禦使，卒。 繼祖有子當襲州事，請以授兄之子克柔，詔從之，而進其三子官，錄二孫為借職。
Jizu, styled Yingzhi, rose from right palace guard to commissioner of the western dyeing bureau and through successive promotions became imperial city commissioner and regimental commander of Chengzhou. He governed the prefecture for more than twenty years. He petitioned for books and Renzong granted him the 《Nine Classics》. When Han Jiang sent Hedong troops to build Lawu fort, Jizu led the vanguard, penetrated deep into enemy territory, and brought eight hundred tribal households to submit. He was promoted to defense commissioner of Jiezhou and died. Jizu's son was heir to the prefecture, but he asked that it go to his elder brother's son Kerou; the court agreed, promoted his three sons, and enrolled two grandsons in office.
16
弟繼世，少從軍，為延州東路巡檢。 嵬名山之內附，繼世先知之，遣其子克懃報种諤，諤用是取綏州。 繼世以騎步萬軍於懷寧砦，入晉祠谷，往銀川，分名山之眾萬五千戶居於大理河。 夏人來攻，再戰皆捷。 諤抵罪逮繫獄，以兵付之而行，遂同名山守綏州，綠功領忠州刺史。 說韓絳城囉兀以撫橫山，因畫取河南之策，絳以為然。 以左騏驥使、果州團練使卒。 諸司使無賻禮，詔以繼世蕃官，捍邊有績，特給之。 從子克行。
His younger brother Jishi entered the army young and became eastern-route inspector of Yanzhou. When Wei Mingshan defected, Jishi learned of it first and sent his son Keqin to inform Zhong E, who thereby captured Suizhou. Jishi led ten thousand horse and foot from Huaining fort into Jinci valley and on to Yinchuan, resettling fifteen thousand of Mingshan's followers along the Dali River. When the Xia attacked he fought twice and won both engagements. When Zhong E was imprisoned for his crimes, Jishi was given his troops and marched out; he then held Suizhou with Mingshan and, for his service, was made prefect of Zhongzhou. He persuaded Han Jiang to build Lawu to pacify Hengshan and sketched a plan to seize the territory south of the Yellow River; Jiang agreed. He died while serving as left commissioner of fine horses and regimental commander of Guozhou. Bureau commissioners normally received no funeral gifts, but the court ruled that as a frontier tribal officer who had distinguished himself on the border, Jishi should receive them. Nephew: Kexing
17
曾孫克行
Great-grandson: Kexing
18
克行，字遵道，繼閔子也。 初仕軍府，無所知名。 夏人寇環慶，种諤拒之，詔河東出師為援，克行請往。 諤使以兵三千護餉道，戰於葭蘆川，先登，斬級四百，降戶千，馬畜萬計。 諸老將矍然曰：「真折太尉子也！ 」擢知府州。
Kexing, styled Zundao, was Jimin's son. He first served in the military prefecture without distinction. When the Xia raided Huanqing, Zhong E held them off; the court ordered Hedong to reinforce him, and Kexing volunteered. Zhong E sent him with three thousand men to guard the supply line; at Jialu River Kexing was first up the wall, took four hundred heads, brought a thousand households to submit, and captured tens of thousands of horses and livestock. The veteran generals exclaimed, "A true son of Grand Marshal Zhe!" He was promoted to prefect of Fuzhou.
19
秦兵討夏國，張世矩將河外軍民，克行與俱。 廷議謂守臣難自行，詔克行選兵隸世矩。 克行抗章，願率部落先驅，未報，即委管鑰而西。 大酋咩保吳良以萬騎來躡，克行為後拒，度賊半度隘，縱擊大破之，殺咩保吳良。 師還自劾，釋不問。 王中正出塞，克行先拔宥州，每出必勝，夏人畏之，益左廂兵，專以當折氏。
When Qin forces campaigned against Xia, Zhang Shiju led the troops and civilians beyond the river, and Kexing went with him. The court held that border commanders could not march on their own; Kexing was ordered to select troops and place them under Zhang Shiju. Kexing protested in a memorial that he wished to lead his tribes as vanguard; before a reply arrived he handed over his keys and marched west. The great chieftain Miebao Wuliang pursued with ten thousand horsemen; Kexing covered the rear, waited until half the enemy had passed the defile, then struck and routed them, killing Miebao Wuliang. On his return he submitted a self-impeachment, but the court took no action. When Wang Zhongzheng campaigned beyond the frontier, Kexing first captured Youzhou; he never marched without victory; the Xia feared him and reinforced their left wing specifically to face the Zhe family.
20
太原孫覽議城葭蘆，諸將論多不合，召克行問策，即頓兵吐渾河，約勒部伍，為深入窮討之狀，敵疑不敢動。 既訖役，又入津慶、龍橫川，斬級三千。
Sun Lan of Taiyuan proposed building a fort at Jialu; when the generals could not agree, Kexing was consulted; he halted at the Tuhun River, drilled his men, and feigned a deep pursuit so the enemy dared not stir. When the fort was finished he raided Jinqing and Longhengchuan and took three thousand heads.
21
詔河東進築八砦，通道鄜延。 延帥遣秦希甫來共議，克行請兩路併力，以遠者為先，希甫曰：「由近及遠，法也。 」克行曰：「不然，事有奇正。 今乘士氣之銳，所利在速，故先遠役，以出其不意，若徐圖之，士心且怠矣。 」希甫持不可，並上二議，卒用克行策。 城成，諜言寇至，軍中皆戒嚴，克行止之曰：「彼自擾耳。 」已而果然。
The court ordered Hedong to build eight advance forts and open a route to Fuyan. The Fuyan commander sent Qin Xifu to consult; Kexing urged both circuits to combine forces and begin with the farthest posts; Xifu said, "From near to far—that is the rule." Kexing replied, "Not so—there are orthodox and unorthodox ways to war." Morale is high and speed is everything—we must build the distant forts first and catch them unawares; delay will only dull the men's spirit." Xifu objected; both plans went up to the throne, and Kexing's was adopted. When the fort was finished, scouts reported the enemy was coming and the army went on alert; Kexing stopped them. "They are only frightening themselves," he said. Soon enough it proved true.
22
克行在邊三十年，善拊士卒，戰功最多，羌人呼為『折家父』。 官至秦州觀察使，卒，贈武安軍節度使。 子可大為榮州團練使、知府州。 從子可適。
Kexing served on the frontier for thirty years, won the soldiers' loyalty, and amassed more victories than any other; the Qiang called him "Father Zhe." He rose to observation commissioner of Qinzhou, died, and was posthumously made military commissioner of the Wu'an army. His son Kedai became regimental commander of Rongzhou and prefect of Fuzhou. Nephew: Keshi.
23
可適未冠有勇，馳射不習而能。 鄜延郭逵見之，歎曰：「真將種也！ 」薦試廷中，補殿侍，隸延州。 從种諤出塞，遇敵馬以少年易之，可適索與鬥，斬其首，取馬而還，益知名，米脂之役，與夏人戰三角嶺，得級多，又敗之於蒲桃谷東。 兵久不得食，千人成聚，籍籍於軍門，或欲掩殺以為功，可適曰：「此以饑而逃耳，非叛也。 」單馬出詰之曰：「爾輩何至是，不為父母妻子念而甘心為異域鬼耶？ 」皆回面聲喏，流涕謝再生，各遣歸。
Before he came of age Keshi was already brave and a natural horseman and archer. Guo Kui of Fuyan saw him and exclaimed, "A true soldier's breed!" He was recommended for court examination, made a palace attendant, and assigned to Yanzhou. Campaigning beyond the frontier with Zhong E, he met enemy riders who mocked his youth; Keshi insisted on a duel, cut off a head, seized the horse, and returned to greater fame; at Mizhi he fought the Xia at Sanjiao Ridge with many kills and routed them again east of Putao Valley. After long hunger a thousand men gathered murmuring at headquarters; some wanted to slaughter them for credit; Keshi said, "They are running from hunger, not rebelling." He rode out alone and demanded, "How can you do this—forget your parents, wives, and children and choose to die as ghosts in enemy country?" They turned, shouted assent, wept their thanks for new life, and were sent home one by one.
24
羌、夏人十萬入寇，可適先得其守烽卒姓名，詐為首領行視，呼出塞斬之，烽不傳，因卷甲疾趨，大破之於尾丁磑。 回次檉楊溝，正午駐營，分騎據西山，曰：「彼若躡吾後，腹背受敵，必敗。 」果舉軍來，可適所部𦆵八千，轉戰至高嶺，廼從間道趣洪德，設伏邀其歸路。 敵至，伏發衝之，其國母窬山而遁，焚棄輜重，雖帷賬首飾之屬亦不返，眾相蹈藉，赴厓澗死者如積。 論前後功，至皇城使、成州團練使、知岷蘭州鎮戎軍。
When a hundred thousand Qiang and Xia invaded, Keshi learned the beacon guards' names, posed as a chieftain on inspection, lured them beyond the pass and killed them before alarms could spread, then marched swiftly and crushed the enemy at Weiding'ao. On the march back he camped at Chenyang Gully at noon, posted cavalry on the western hills, and said, "If they follow us we will be caught between two fires and surely lose." The enemy came in full force; with only eight thousand men Keshi fought his way to Gaoling, then took a back trail to Hongde and laid an ambush on their retreat. When the enemy arrived the ambush struck; their queen mother fled over the mountains; they abandoned and burned their baggage, leaving even tents and ornaments behind; the rout trampled the dead in heaps in the ravines. For his cumulative service he was made imperial city commissioner, regimental commander of Chengzhou, and administrator of Minzhou, Lanzhou, and the Zhenrong army.

渭帥章楶合熙、秦、慶三道兵築好水川，命總管王文振統之，而可適將軍為副。 熙州兵千人失道盡死，文振歸罪於可適，楶即下之吏，宰相章惇欲按軍法，哲宗不許，猶削十三官而罷。 楶請留以責效，乃以權第十二將。

When a thousand Xi troops lost their way and perished, Wenzhen blamed Keshi; Jie had him arrested; Chancellor Zhang Dun wanted a court-martial; Zhezong refused but still stripped thirteen ranks and dismissed him. Jie asked to keep him on to redeem himself, and he was made acting commander of the twelfth division.

嵬名阿埋、昧勒都逋，皆夏人桀黠用事者，詔可適密圖之，會二酋以畜牧為名會境上，可適諜知之，遣兵夜往襲，並俘其族屬三千人，遂取天都山。 帝為御文德殿受賀，以其地為西安州，遷可適東上閤門使、洺州防禦使、涇原鈐轄、知州事，真拜和州防禦使，進明州觀察使，為副都總管。
Weiming Ami and Mile Dubo were powerful, cunning Xia leaders; the court ordered Keshi to eliminate them; when the two chiefs met on the border ostensibly to graze livestock, Keshi learned of it, struck by night, captured three thousand of their kin, and seized Tiandu Mountain. The emperor received congratulations in the Wende Hall; the territory became Xi'anzhou; Keshi was made eastern upper gate commissioner, defense commissioner of Mingzhou, Jingyuan commandant and prefect, then formally defense commissioner of Hezhou, observation commissioner of Mingzhou, and deputy overall commander.

When Commander Zhong Chuan was cut off on the border, Keshi broke through with light cavalry and brought him back. Chuan proposed taking Lingwu and Huanqing and asked for troops; Keshi was ordered to lead ten thousand cavalry and advanced to the Lingzhou River. The Xia, old and young together, entered the city at midnight; by morning the captures were immense, but Qing reinforcements never came, and he withdrew. Summoned to court, he was asked about Chuan's plan and answered, "It is easy to take but hard to hold—we should first raid and weaken their territory, secure our frontier defenses, and only then press further." The emperor said, "You are right." He was promoted to acting military and observation commissioner of the Wu'an army and overall adjutant of the foot army.

He fortified Xiaoguan on a large scale, quarreled with Chuan, and when several hundred men were lost at Tako, Chuan impeached him and he was demoted to observation commissioner of Zhengzhou. Soon he was made prefect of Weizhou and military commissioner of the Huaikang army. The transport commissioner wanted to build granaries at four frontier forts and stock five million units of fodder; Keshi protested the cost, sought carts and oxen to haul grain, and planned to move a hundred thousand hu to Xihe—all against the commissioner's wishes; he was accused of obstruction and recalled as commissioner of the Youshen Abbey. The next year he was again made prefect of Weizhou; his son Yanzhi was made a direct secretariat archives administrator; he died several months later at sixty-one. Yanzhi later signed documents at the Bureau of Military Affairs in the Shaoxing era; his biography is given elsewhere.

Feng Jiye
30
馮繼業，字嗣宗，大名人。 父暉，朔方節度，封衛王。 繼業幼敏慧，有度量，以父任補朔方軍節院使，隨父歷邠、孟，及再領朔方，皆補牙職。 周廣順初，暉疾，繼業圖殺其兄繼勳。 暉卒，遂代其父為朔方軍留後。 以郊祀恩，加靈州大都督府長史，遷朔方節度、靈環觀察、處置、度支、溫池榷稅等使。
Feng Jiye, styled Sizong, was from Daming. His father Hui was military commissioner of Shuofang and was enfeoffed as prince of Wei. Clever and broad-minded as a youth, Jiye entered service through his father as envoy of the Shuofang commission, followed him through Bin and Meng, and whenever his father again held Shuofang received a staff appointment. Early in the Guangshun period, when Hui fell ill, Jiye plotted to kill his elder brother Jixun. When Hui died he succeeded his father as acting commissioner of the Shuofang army. By suburban-sacrifice grace he was made chief secretary of the Lingzhou grand protectorate and military commissioner of Shuofang with concurrent posts over Ling and Huan, disposition, revenue, and the Wenchi salt monopoly.

恭帝時，繼業既殺兄代父領鎮，頗驕恣，時出兵劫略羌夷，羌夷不附，又撫士卒少恩，繼業慮其為變，以太祖居鎮日常得給事，乃豫徙其孥闕下。
Under Emperor Gong, Jiye had killed his brother and taken his father's command; arrogant and overbearing, he raided the Qiang and Yi until they would not submit and showed his troops little kindness; fearing mutiny, and remembering how Taizu had once served in the circuit, he moved his family to the capital in advance.
32
建隆初，來朝，連以駝馬、寶器為獻。 開寶二年，賜詔獎諭，拜靜難軍節度使。 三年，改鎮定國軍，吏民立碑頌其遺愛。 太平興國初來朝，封梁國公，留京師。 明年，卒，年五十一，贈侍中。
Early in Jianlong he came to court bearing camels, horses, and precious goods. In 969 an edict praised him and he was made military commissioner of the Jingnan army. The next year he was transferred to the Dingguo army, and the people erected a stele praising his benevolent rule. Early in Taiping Xingguo he came to court, was made duke of Liang, and was kept at the capital. He died the next year at fifty-one and was posthumously made palace attendant.

王承美
Wang Chengmei
34
王承美，豐州人，本河西藏才族都首領。 其父事契丹，為左千牛衛將軍，開寶二年，率眾來歸。 承美授豐州牙內指揮使，父卒，改天德軍蕃漢都指揮使、知州事，移豐州刺史。 遣軍校詣闕，言願誘退渾、突厥內附，上嘉其意。
Wang Chengmei of Fengzhou was originally chief of the Zangcai tribe of Hexi. His father had served the Khitan as left imperial guard general; in 969 he led his people to defect. Chengmei was made Fengzhou inner garrison commander; after his father's death he became overall tribal-and-Han commander of the Tiande army and prefect, then Fengzhou prefect. He sent an officer to court offering to lure the Hun and Turks to submit; the emperor approved.
35
太平興國七年，與契丹戰，斬獲以萬計，禽其天德軍節度使韋太以獻。 明年，契丹來寇，又擊敗其眾萬餘，追北至青冢百餘里，斬獲益眾。 以功授本州團練使。 以乞黨族次首領弗香克浪買為歸德郎將，沒細大首領越移為懷化大將軍，瓦窯為歸德大將軍。 淳化二年冬來朝，令歸所部，控子河�。 自是諸蕃歲修貢禮，頗效忠順。
In 982 he fought the Khitan, killing and capturing by the tens of thousands, and presented their Tiande army commander Wei Tai as a captive. The next year he repelled a Khitan invasion, routed more than ten thousand of them, and pursued north to Qingzhong for over a hundred li with still greater slaughter. For his service he was made regimental commander of his home prefecture. He made the Qidang secondary chief Fuxiangkelangmai a returning-virtue commandant, the Mosi great chief Yueyi a cherishing-transformation grand general, and Wayao a returning-virtue grand general. In the winter of 991 he came to court and was sent back to his command to hold Ziheyi. Thereafter the tribes paid annual tribute and showed marked loyalty.
36
景德初來朝，以其守邊歲久，遷本州防禦使以還。 自承美內屬，給奉同蕃官例，至是，特詔月增五萬。 尋請於州城置孔子廟，詔可之。 未幾被疾，遣中使挾醫視之。 大中祥符五年，卒，贈恩州觀察使。 六年，錄其子文寶、孫懷筠以官。
Early in Jingde he came to court and, for his long border service, was promoted to defense commissioner of his prefecture and sent back. Since his submission his pay had followed the tribal-officer scale; now an edict specially added fifty thousand per month. He soon asked to build a Confucius temple in the prefectural city, and the court agreed. He soon fell ill and the court sent envoys with physicians. He died in 1012 and was posthumously made observation commissioner of Enzhou. The next year his son Wenbao and grandson Huaijun were given offices.
37
初，承美養其長孫文玉為子，奏署殿直，及卒，其本族首領上言文玉曉達軍政，請令襲承美任。 下蕃漢議，議同，以為侍禁、知州事。 文玉父文恭時為侍禁，在沂州，表訴其事，詔改文恭為供奉宮。 九年，承美葬，詔以帛緡、米麴、羊酒賜其家。
Chengmei had adopted his eldest grandson Wenyu and made him a palace attendant; after his death the tribal chiefs petitioned that Wenyu, skilled in military affairs, succeed him. Tribal and Han councils agreed, and he was made a palace guard and acting prefect. Wenyu's father Wengong, a palace guard at Yizhou, petitioned about the matter, and the court made Wengong a palace attendant instead. Nine years later, at Chengmei's funeral, the court granted his family silk, grain, yeast, sheep, and wine.

李繼周
Li Jizhou
39
李繼周，延州金明人。 祖計都，父孝順，皆為金明鎮使，繼周嗣掌本族。
Li Jizhou was from Jinming in Yanzhou. His grandfather Jidu and father Xiaoshun had both commanded the Jinming garrison; Jizhou succeeded as head of the clan.
40
太平興國三年，東山蕃落集眾寇清化砦，繼周率眾敗之，殺三千餘人，補殿前承旨。 雍熙中，又與侯延廣敗未藏、未腋等族於渾州西山。 淳化四年，遷殿直，賜介冑、戎器、茶綵。 明年，討李繼遷，命開治塞門、鴉兒兩路，又招降族帳首領二十餘人，率所部入夏州，敗蕃兵數千於石堡砦。 以功轉供奉官，復加恩賞，仍賜官第。
In 978 eastern tribes massed against Qinghua fort; Jizhou routed them, killing more than three thousand, and was made a palace front bearer. During Yongxi he and Hou Yanguang defeated the Weicang, Weiye, and other tribes on the western hills of Hunzhou. In 993 he was made a palace attendant and given armor, weapons, tea, and silks. The next year, in the campaign against Li Jiqian, he opened the Saimen and Ya'er routes, brought more than twenty tribal chiefs to submit, marched into Xiazhou, and routed several thousand tribal troops at Shibao fort. For his service he was made a palace attendant, rewarded again, and given an official residence.
41
繼周以阿都關、塞門、盧關等砦最居邊要，遂規修築砦城。 在磨盧家、媚咩、拽藏等族居近盧關，未嘗內順。 繼周夜率所部往襲，焚之，斬首俘獲甚眾。 至道二年，授西京作坊副使，賜袍帶、銀綵、雕戈以寵之。 大軍討西夏，命為延州路踏白先鋒。 會繼遷邀戰於路，繼周戰卻之。 咸平初，改西京左藏庫副使。 三年，復為先鋒，人賊境，焚積聚，殺人畜，獲器甲凡六十餘萬。 授供備庫使，領金明縣兵馬都監、新砦解家河盧關路都巡檢。 五年，授西京作坊使。 蕃騎入鈔，繼周逐之出境。 景德元年，夏人圍麟州，繼周受詔率兵會李繼福掩擊之。 加領誠州刺史。
Seeing that Adu Pass, Saimen, Lumen, and other forts were the most critical border posts, Jizhou planned to rebuild their walls. The Molujia, Meiyang, Zhuaizang, and other tribes near Lumen Pass had never submitted. Jizhou raided them by night, burned their settlements, and took many heads and captives. In 996 he was made deputy commissioner of the western capital workshops and honored with robes, belts, silks, and a carved halberd. In the great campaign against Western Xia he was made scout vanguard of the Yanzhou route. When Jiqian ambushed him on the march, Jizhou fought him off. Early in Xianping he was made deputy commissioner of the western capital left treasury. Three years later he again led the vanguard into enemy country, burned stores, slaughtered people and livestock, and seized more than six hundred thousand pieces of armor and equipment. He was made commissioner of the supply reserve and overall inspector of Jinming county troops and the Xiejiahe-Lumen pass route. Five years later he was made commissioner of the western capital workshops. When tribal horsemen raided, Jizhou drove them back across the border. In 1004 the Xia besieged Linzhou; Jizhou was ordered to join Li Jifu in a surprise attack. He was also made prefect of Chengzhou.
42
大中祥符二年，卒，年六十七，詔邊臣擇其子可襲職者以名聞，邊臣言其子殿直士彬遜愞，從子士用樸忠練邊事，且為部落所伏。 乃詔士彬管勾部族事，士用為巡檢都監以左右之。
He died in 1009 at sixty-seven; the court asked frontier officials to name an heir; they said his son Shibin was mild but his nephew Shiyong was plain, loyal, skilled on the border, and respected by the tribes. The court put Shibin in charge of tribal affairs with Shiyong as inspection superintendent to assist him.
43
士彬後至供備庫副使、金明縣都監、新砦解家河盧關路巡檢。 康定元年，元昊反，攻保安軍，而潛兵襲金明，士彬父子俱被禽。 士彬兄士紹至內殿崇班，士用至供奉官、閣門祗候。
Shibin later became deputy commissioner of the supply reserve, Jinming county superintendent, and inspector of the Xiejiahe-Lumen route. In 1040 Yuan Hao rebelled, attacked Bao'an army, and secretly struck Jinming; Shibin and his son were both captured. Shibin's brother Shishao became an inner-palace honored-class officer; Shiyong became a palace attendant and gate commissioner.
44
李繼福者，亦與繼周同時歸順，授永平砦茭村軍主，以戰功歷歸德將軍，領順州刺史，至內殿崇班、新歸明諸族都巡檢。
Li Jifu, who had submitted with Jizhou, became chief of Jiaocun at Yongping fort, rose through battle to returning-virtue general and prefect of Shunzhou, and eventually became an inner-palace honored-class officer and overall inspector of newly submitted tribes.

孫行友
Sun Xingyou
46
孫行友，莫州清苑人，世業農。 初，定州西二百里有狼山者，當易州中路，舊有城堡，邊人賴之以避寇。 山中蘭若有尼，姓孫氏，名深意，有術惑眾。 行友兄子方諫名之為姑帥，事之甚謹。 及尼坐亡，行友益神其事，因以其術然香燈，聚民漸眾。 自晉少帝與契丹絕好，邊州困於轉輸，逋民往往依方諫，推以為帥。 方諫懼主帥捕逐，乃表歸朝，因署為東北面招收指揮使，且賜院額曰「勝福」。 每契丹軍來，必率其徒襲擊之，鎧仗、畜產所得漸多，人益依以避難焉。 易、定帥聞於朝，因以方諫為邊界遊奕使，行友副之。 自是捍禦侵軼，多所殺獲。 乘勝入祁溝關、平庸城，破飛狐砦，契丹頗畏之，邊民千餘家賴以無患。 然亦陰持兩端，以圖自固。 已而晉師失律，薊人道契丹陷中原，方諫之密構也。 契丹授方諫定州節度，行友易州刺史。 尋以蕃將耶律忠代方諫於雲州，方諫不受命，歸保狼山。 契丹北歸，焚劫山中，方諫自狼山率眾復保定州，歸命於漢，授行友易州刺史，行義泰州刺史。 弟兄掎角以居，寇每入，諸軍鎮閉壘坐視，一無所得。
Sun Xingyou of Qingyuan in Mozhou came from a farming family. Two hundred li west of Dingzhou stood Wolf Mountain on the main Yizhou route; an old fort there had long sheltered frontier people from raiders. At a mountain temple there lived a nun surnamed Sun, called Shenyi, whose occult arts beguiled the crowd. Xingyou's nephew Fang Jian called her "Aunt Commander" and waited on her with great devotion. When the nun died in meditation, Xingyou treated the matter as even more miraculous; he adopted her rites, lit incense lamps, and drew a steadily growing following. After Emperor Shao of Jin broke with the Khitan, border prefectures were strained by supply runs, and refugees often rallied to Fang Jian and chose him as their leader. Fearing pursuit by the local commander, Fang Jian submitted to the court and was made northeast reception commanding envoy; the abbey was granted the name Shengfu. Whenever Khitan forces appeared, he led his men against them; booty in arms and livestock grew, and more people looked to him for refuge. The Yi and Ding commanders reported to court, which appointed Fang Jian border patrol commissioner with Xingyou as his deputy. From then on they repelled incursions and took many kills and captives. They pressed their advantage into Qigou Pass and Pingyong City, took Feihu Fort, and so daunted the Khitan that more than a thousand frontier households lived secure under their protection. Yet they also hedged between rival powers to secure their own position. Soon the Jin armies collapsed; people of Ji blamed the Khitan conquest of the Central Plains on Fang Jian's secret collusion. The Khitan made Fang Jian military commissioner of Dingzhou and Xingyou prefect of Yizhou. Soon the Khitan general Yelü Zhong was sent to replace Fang Jian at Yunzhou; Fang Jian refused and withdrew to Wolf Mountain. As the Khitan withdrew north they burned the mountains; Fang Jian marched from Wolf Mountain, retook Dingzhou, and submitted to Han; Xingyou was made prefect of Yizhou and Xingyi prefect of Taizhou. The brothers held adjoining positions in mutual support; whenever raiders came, the frontier garrisons shut their walls and watched from afar without intercepting a single prize.
47
行友嘗遣都校王友遇巡警於石河，與契丹遇，殺百餘騎，又嘗獲其刺史蔡幅順、清苑令王璉。 乾祐中，契丹復犯塞，行友禦之，俘殺數百人。 周太祖北征，行友道獻俘馘人馬以求見，且請自效，廼厚加賜予，留之軍門。 及周祖受命，行友屢上言偵得契丹離合，願得勁兵三千，乘間平定幽州，乃移方諫鎮華州，以行友為定州留後。 顯德初，正授節鉞。 世宗自河東還，加檢校太傅。 六年，世宗北征，行友攻下契丹之易州，擒其刺史任欽以獻。
Xingyou once sent Commander Wang Youyu to patrol the Shi River, where he killed more than a hundred Khitan horsemen; he also captured the Khitan prefect Cai Fushun and Qingyuan magistrate Wang Lian. During Qianyou the Khitan again raided the frontier; Xingyou fought them off and killed or captured several hundred men. During Emperor Taizu of Zhou's northern campaign, Xingyou met the army with captives and severed heads, asked for an audience, and offered his service; the emperor richly rewarded him and kept him at headquarters. After Zhou Taizu took the throne, Xingyou repeatedly reported Khitan disunity and asked for three thousand elite troops to seize Youzhou; Fang Jian was transferred to Huazhou and Xingyou made acting governor of Dingzhou. Early in Xiande he received full military commission. When Emperor Shizong returned from Hedong, Xingyou was made honorary grand tutor. In the sixth year of Xiande, during Emperor Shizong's northern campaign, Xingyou took Yizhou from the Khitan and presented the captured prefect Ren Qin.
48

Early in the Song he was made associate director of the Department of State Affairs. The Wolf Mountain temple grew ever more febrile with cult activity; crowds flocked there beyond restraint; uneasy, Xingyou repeatedly asked to resign and retire to the hills, but the court refused. In 961 he moved his stores, mustered fighting men, and readied arms, intending to withdraw to Wolf Mountain and fortify himself there. Troop commander Yao Jineng reported secretly; Emperor Taizu sent Vice Commissioner Wu Huaijie at speed with forces from Zhen and Zhao, ostensibly on a border tour, straight into the city before Xingyou knew. An edict was then produced ordering his entire clan to the capital; Xingyou, taken aback, obeyed. Once he arrived, Censor Li Weiyue tried him at his residence; the charges were proved, a stern edict rebuked him, and his former ranks were stripped away. He was ordered confined to his private residence. Several of his followers were executed, and messengers were sent posthaste to Wolf Mountain to bring out the dead nun's body and burn it. Xingyou's brother Fangjin, prefect of Yizhou, and his nephew Quanhui, commissioner of Baosai army, came to court to answer for the affair and were pardoned.
49
四年秋，詔免行友禁錮。 未幾，以郊祀恩，起為右龍武軍將軍。 乾德二年，遷右監門衛大將軍，又改左龍武軍大將軍。 太平興國六年，卒，年八十，贈左衛上將軍。 方進至德州刺史。 子全照。
That autumn an edict lifted Xingyou's house arrest. Soon, by the grace of the suburban sacrifice, he was restored as general of the Right Dragon Martial Army. In 964 he became grand general of the Right Gate Guard corps, then grand general of the Left Dragon Martial Army. He died in 981 at eighty and was posthumously made senior general of the Left Guard. Fangjin rose to become prefect of Dezhou. His son was Quanzhao.
50
子全照
Quanzhao
51
全照，字繼明，以蔭補殿直，雍熙中授京南巡檢，俄隸幽州部署曹彬麾下，遷供奉官、閣門祗候歷靜戎、威虜二軍監軍。 從田重進擊賊有功，就加西京作坊使，兼知威虜軍，連為廣韶、鄜延二路都巡檢使。 淳化五年，率兵與李繼隆克綏州，因與張崇貴等同戍守之。 俄護屯兵於夏州，兼知州事。 召還，為登萊路都巡檢使，遷左藏庫使、延州監軍兼阿都關盧關路都巡檢事。
Quanzhao, courtesy name Jiming, entered service by privilege as a palace attendant; in Yongxi he became capital-south inspector, soon joined Cao Bin's Youzhou command, and rose to palace attendant and gate commissioner, supervising the Jingrong and Weilu armies in turn. Serving under Tian Chongjin against rebels, he earned merit and was made commissioner of the western capital workshops while commanding Weilu army; he then served in turn as overall inspector on the Guang-Shao and Fu-Yan routes. In 994 he joined Li Jilong in taking Suizhou and garrisoned it with Zhang Chonggui and others. He soon guarded troops at Xizhou and also administered the prefecture. Recalled to court, he became overall inspector of the Deng-Lai route, then commissioner of the left treasury, Yanzhou military supervisor, and overall inspector of the Adu and Lumen passes.
52
咸平初，人掌軍頭引見司。 二年，加如京使，為涇原路鈐轄兼安撫都監，是冬徙幷、汾等州都巡檢使。 三年，改知順安軍，代還，復為環慶路鈐轄，與李繼和規度靈州道路。 四年，加西上閣門使，復為環慶路鈐轄。 五年，將城綏州，以慕興為綏州路部署，全照為鈐轄。 既又慮全照素剛執，與興不協，乃以曹璨代之。 既調兵夫二萬餘，全照言其非便，乃罷。 又嘗命度地河北，全照言沿河高阜可分置城堡屯戍者，寧邊軍南、武強縣側凡二處，上重於興役，止命營安平南，徙置祁州。 俄知天雄軍府。 六年夏，上裁定防秋禦戎之要，命為寧邊軍部署，領兵八千扼要害之路。 以全照好陵人，取其嘗所保薦者王德鈞、裴自榮共事焉。
Early in Xianping he took charge of the army presentation office. Two years later he became envoy bearing credentials, Jingyuan route tongzhuan and pacification superintendent; that winter he was made overall inspector of Bing, Fen, and neighboring prefectures. Three years later he was put in charge of Shun'an army; after his replacement he returned as Huanqing route tongzhuan and with Li Jihe surveyed routes to Lingzhou. Four years later he became western upper inner-gate commissioner and again served as Huanqing route tongzhuan. Five years later, as the court prepared to rebuild Suizhou, Mu Xing was made route deployment commissioner and Quanzhao tongzhuan. Concerned that Quanzhao's stubborn temperament would clash with Mu Xing, the court replaced him with Cao Can. After more than twenty thousand laborers had been mobilized, Quanzhao argued the plan was impracticable and it was dropped. When ordered to survey Hebei, Quanzhao proposed two high riverbank sites near Ningbian army and Wuqiang county for fortified garrisons; the emperor, wary of the labor burden, approved only a camp south of Anping and moved Qizhou there. He soon became administrator of Tianxiong army headquarters. In the sixth year the emperor finalized autumn frontier defense and made Quanzhao deployment commissioner at Ningbian army with eight thousand men to hold the key approach. Because Quanzhao tended to bully subordinates, the court paired him with two men he had once recommended—Wang Dejun and Pei Zirong.
53
全照形短精悍，知兵，以嚴毅整眾，然性剛使氣，專任刑罰。 中書初進擬嚴州刺史，上曰：「全照深刻，常慮人以嚴察議己，今授此州，似涉譏誚。 」乃改焉。 三年，為邠寧環慶都部署。 趙德明納款，朝議減西鄙戍兵，令屯近地，全照以邊防不可無備，未即奉詔。 上曰：「全照是好勇多言者。 德明使已至闕，復何慮焉？ 」因徙全照知永興軍府，仍拜四方館使。 西師移屯者至府，命全照兼駐泊鈐轄。 全照許州有別墅，求典是州，可之。 大中祥符中，遷引進使。 逾歲表求歸朝，命掌閣門、客省、四方館事。 四年，車駕西幸，留為新城都巡檢，未幾，卒，年六十。
Quanzhao was short, wiry, and skilled in war; he kept his troops in order through stern discipline, but his stubborn, combative temperament made him lean heavily on punishment. When the Secretariat first proposed him for prefect of Yanzhou, the emperor said, "Quanzhao is harsh by nature and always worried that people would mock his severity; giving him Yanzhou would look like a taunt. " So the appointment was changed. Three years later he became overall deployment commissioner of Binning and Huanqing. When Zhao Deming submitted, the court debated thinning western garrisons and moving troops inland; Quanzhao argued the frontier could not be left undefended and did not obey at once. The emperor said, "Quanzhao is bold and talkative. Deming's envoy is already at court—what is there to fear? " He transferred Quanzhao to Yongxing army headquarters and made him commissioner of the Four Directions Hall. When relocated western troops reached the prefecture, Quanzhao was made their stationed garrison tongzhuan as well. Quanzhao owned a villa in Xuzhou and asked to govern that prefecture; the request was granted. During Dazhong Xiangfu he was made introducing envoy. A year later he asked to return to court and was put in charge of the inner gate, guest reception, and Four Directions Hall. In the fourth year, when the emperor traveled west, he was left as overall inspector of Xincheng; he died soon after, aged sixty.
54
論曰：五代之季，邊圉之不靖也久矣。 太祖之興，雖不勤遠略，而向之陸梁跋扈而不可制者，莫不竭忠效節，雖奔走僵仆而不避，豈人心之有異哉？ 良由威德之並用，控御之有道也。 折氏據有府谷，與李彜興之居夏州，初無以異。 太祖嘉其嚮化，許以世襲，雖不無世卿之嫌，自從阮而下，繼生名將，世篤忠貞，足為西北之捍，可謂無負於宋者矣。 承美、繼周，分蒞種落，亦能世其職者也。 繼業雖出賊叛之族，而有循良之風。 方諫、行友介遼、晉間，持兩端以取將相，終以首鼠獲咎，其諸異端之害歟？ 全照職親禁衛，素稱嚴果，而昧於弭兵之利，君子所不予也。
The historians remark: By the close of the Five Dynasties, the frontier had been unsettled for a long time. When Emperor Taizu rose, though he did not pursue grand distant campaigns, even the once unruly and uncontrollable border lords poured out loyalty and served to exhaustion—had human nature somehow changed? No—it was because he wielded both awe and kindness, and governed the frontier with skill. The Zhe clan held Fugu much as Li Yixing held Xizhou; at first the two cases were hardly different. Taizu rewarded their submission with hereditary office; though that smacked of feudal privilege, from Congruan onward the family produced one loyal frontier commander after another and became the northwest bulwark—the Zhe did not fail the Song. Chengmei and Jizhou likewise held tribal commands in succession across generations. Jiye came from a rebellious house yet governed with steady, humane discipline. Fang Jian and Xingyou, straddling Liao and Jin, played both sides to win high office and in the end paid for their vacillation—is this the cost of border cults and double-dealing? Quanzhao held a palace guard post and was known for stern efficiency, yet he failed to see the value of easing military tensions—a failing no gentleman would condone.
