公元紀年宰相進拜加官罷免執政進拜加官罷免
1086元祐元年丙寅閏二月庚寅，司馬光自正議大夫、守門下侍郎依前官加左僕射兼門下侍郎。壬辰，呂公著自金紫光祿大夫、尚書左丞加門下侍郎。四月壬寅，呂公著自金紫光祿大夫、門下侍郎依前官加右僕射兼中書侍郎。文彥博自河東節度使、守太師、開府儀同三司、潞國公落致仕加太師、平章軍國重事。 韓縝 蔡確 司馬光 呂公著 文彥博閏二月庚寅，左僕射蔡確累為劉摯、孫覺、蘇轍、朱光庭、王巖叟所論，守前官以觀文殿大學士知陳州。四月己丑，韓縝自右僕射以光祿大夫、觀文殿大學士知潁昌府、京西北路安撫使。九月丙辰朔，左僕射兼門下侍郎司馬光薨。閏二月丙午，李清臣自通議大夫、守尚書右丞除尚書左丞。 呂大防自試吏部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。乙卯，安燾自同知樞密院事進知樞密院事。范純仁自試吏部尚書兼侍讀除中大夫、同知樞密院事。五月丁巳朔，韓維自資政殿大學士、正議大夫兼侍讀除守門下侍郎。十一月戊午，劉摯自試御史中丞除中大夫、尚書右丞。呂大防自中大夫、尚書左丞除中書侍郎。閏二月辛亥，正議大夫、知樞密院事章惇為劉摯、王巖叟、朱光庭等所論，又於簾前爭論諠悖，罷守本官知汝州。九月己卯，張璪自中書侍郎以資政殿學士、光祿大夫知鄭州。
1087二年丁卯文彥博 呂公著五月丁卯，劉摯自中大夫、守尚書右丞除尚書左丞。王存自守兵部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。六月辛丑，安燾自正議大夫、同知樞密院事進知樞密院事。四月戊申，李清臣自尚書左丞以資政殿學士知河陽。七月辛未，韓維自正議大夫、門下侍郎以資政殿大學士知鄧州。
1088三年戊辰四月辛巳，呂公著自金紫光祿大夫、尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎加司空、平章軍國重事。呂大防自中書侍郎加太中大夫、左僕射兼門下侍郎。范純仁自同知樞密院加太中大夫、右僕射兼門下侍郎。 文彥博 呂公著 呂大防 范純仁四月壬午，孫固自觀文殿學士、正議大夫兼侍讀除門下侍郎。王存自中大夫、尚書右丞除尚書左丞。胡宗愈自試御史中丞除中大夫、尚書右丞。趙瞻自中散大夫、試戶部侍郎除簽書樞密院事。
1089四年己巳文彥博 呂公著 呂大防 范純仁二月甲辰，司空、同平章軍國事呂公著薨。六月甲辰，范純仁自太中大夫、右僕射守前官以觀文殿學士知潁昌府。六月丙午，韓忠彥自樞密直學士、戶部尚書除中大夫、尚書左丞。趙瞻自簽書樞密院事進同知樞密院事。十一月癸未，孫固自門下侍郎除光祿大夫、知樞密院事。劉摯自中大夫、守中書侍郎除門下侍郎。傅堯俞自試吏部尚書除中大夫、守中書侍郎。三月己卯，胡宗愈自尚書右丞以資政殿學士知陳州。六月甲辰，王存自中大夫、尚書左丞以端明殿學士知蔡州。七月乙亥，安燾自知樞密院事丁母憂。十月乙卯，詔起復，燾辭。
1090五年庚午文彥博 呂大防二月庚戌，文彥博自太師、平章軍國重事以守太師、儀同三司、河中興元尹、護國軍山南西道節度使致仕。三月壬申，韓忠彥自中大夫、尚書左丞遷同知樞密院事。蘇頌自翰林學士承旨、知制誥兼侍讀除右光祿大夫、尚書左丞。三月丙寅朔，中大夫、同知樞密院事趙瞻卒。四月甲辰，右光祿大夫、知樞密院事孫固卒。十二月辛卯朔，許將自尚書右丞以太中大夫、資政殿學士知定州。
1091六年辛未二月辛卯，劉摯自守門下侍郎、太中大夫加右僕射兼中書侍郎。 呂大防 劉摯十一月乙酉朔，劉摯自右僕射以觀文殿學士知鄆州。二月辛卯，王巖叟自龍圖閣待制、知開封府除簽書樞密院事。癸巳，蘇轍自龍圖閣學士、御史中丞除中大夫、尚書右丞。十一月辛丑，中大夫、守中書侍郎傅堯俞卒。
1092七年壬申六月辛酉，蘇頌自守尚書左僕射加左光祿大夫、守尚書左僕射兼中書侍郎。 呂大防 蘇頌六月辛酉，蘇轍自守尚書右丞除中大夫、門下侍郎。韓忠彥自同知樞密院事除中大夫知樞密院事。范百祿自翰林學士、太中大夫除中書侍郎。梁燾自翰林學士除中大夫、尚書左丞。鄭雍自太中大夫、御史中丞除尚書右丞。劉奉世自左朝請大夫、寶文閣待制、權戶部尚書除簽書樞密院事。五月丙午，王巖叟自簽書樞密院事以端明殿學士知鄭州。
1093八年癸酉七月丙子朔，范純仁自觀文殿大學士知潁昌府加通議大夫、尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎。 呂大防 蘇頌 范純仁三月癸未，蘇頌自右僕射依前左光祿大夫、觀文殿大學士、集禧觀使。三月辛卯，范百祿自中書侍郎以太中大夫、資政殿學士知河中府。六月戊午，梁燾自尚書左丞以資政殿學士領醴泉觀使。
1094紹聖元年甲戌四月壬戌，章惇自通議大夫、提舉洞霄宮加左正議大夫、守尚書左僕射兼門下侍郎。 呂大防 范純仁 章惇三月乙亥，呂大防自左僕射、中書侍郎]以觀文殿大學士、右光祿大夫知潁昌府，改知永興軍。四月壬戌，范純仁自尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎以右正議大夫、觀文殿大學士知潁昌府、京西北路安撫使。二月丁未，李清臣自資政殿學士、守戶部尚書除正議大夫、中書侍郎。鄧溫伯自端明殿學士、守兵部尚書除右光祿大夫、尚書左丞。閏四月甲申，安燾自觀文殿學士、右正議大夫除門下侍郎。六月癸未，曾布自翰林學士承旨、知制誥兼侍讀、除中大夫、同知樞密院事。三月丁酉，蘇轍自太中大夫、門下侍郎以守本官知汝州。五月辛亥，劉奉世自簽書樞密院事以端明殿學士、真定府路安撫使兼知成德軍。乙丑，尚書左丞鄧潤甫卒。潤甫舊名溫伯，復今名。
1095二年乙亥章惇十月甲戌，許將自守吏部尚書兼侍讀除守尚書左丞。蔡卞自翰林學士兼侍讀、修國史除守尚書右丞。十月甲子，鄭雍自太中大夫、尚書右丞以資政殿學士知陳州。十一月乙未，安燾自右正議大夫、門下侍郎以觀文殿學士知河南府。
1096三年丙子章惇正月庚子，韓忠彥自太中大夫、知樞密院事以觀文殿學士知真定府。
1097四年丁丑章惇二月己未，故司馬光自左僕射追貶清海軍節度副使。故呂公著自司空、平章軍國事追貶建武軍節度副使。癸未，前宰相呂大防責授舒州團練副使，循州安置。前宰相劉摯責授鼎州團練副使，新州安置。前宰相范純仁責授武安軍節度副使，永州安置。甲申，前太師致仕文彥博降授太子少保致仕。故左僕射王珪追貶萬安軍司戶。閏二月壬寅，曾布自同知樞密院事除太中大夫、知樞密院事。林希自翰林學士、知制誥除太中大夫、同知樞密院事。許將自守尚書左丞除正議大夫、中書侍郎。蔡卞自太中大夫、守尚書右丞除尚書左丞。黃履自試吏部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。正月庚戌，李清臣自正議大夫、中書侍郎以資政殿大學士知河南府。二月己未，故王巖叟自端明殿學士追貶雷州別駕。癸未，蘇轍自前門下侍郎責授化州別駕，雷州安置。梁燾自前資政殿學士責授雷州別駕，化州安置。劉奉世自前端明殿學士落職，分司南京，郴州居住。韓維自前資政殿大學士致仕落職，特授左朝議大夫致仕。
1098元符元年戊寅章惇四月壬辰，林希自同知樞密院事出知亳州。
1099二年己卯章惇閏九月辛巳，黃履自通議大夫、尚書右丞以守本官知亳州。
1100三年庚辰徽宗正月己卯即位四月甲辰，韓忠彥自門下侍郎加正議大夫、右僕射兼中書侍郎。十月丁酉，遷左僕射兼門下侍郎，進封儀國公。壬寅，曾布自知樞密院事加右銀青光祿大夫、守尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎。 章惇 韓忠彥 曾布先是，豐稷、陳次升、龔夬、陳瓘劾章惇為山陵使而喪轝陷濘暴露，九月辛未，以守本官知越州。二月戊午，韓忠彥自通議大夫、守吏部尚書仍前官加門下侍郎。黃履自資政殿學士、提舉中太乙宮兼侍讀仍前官加尚書右丞。四月甲辰，李清臣自左正議大夫、禮部尚書加門下侍郎。蔣之奇自翰林學士、通議大夫、知制誥除同知樞密院事。十一月戊寅，安燾自觀文殿學士、提舉太乙宮兼集禧觀公事兼侍讀除知樞密院事。辛卯，范純禮自禮部尚書除中大夫、守尚書右丞。先是，龔夬、陳瓘累疏劾蔡卞，五月乙酉，卞以資政殿學士知江寧府。十一月庚辰，尚書右丞黃履以資政殿大學士仍前正議大夫、提舉中太乙宮兼集禧觀公事。
1101建中靖國元年辛巳韓忠彥 曾布正月癸亥，前宰相觀文殿學士、中太乙宮使范純仁薨。七月丁亥，蔣之奇自正議大夫、同知樞密院事進知樞密院事。陸佃自試吏部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。章楶自端明殿學士、通議大夫、提舉中太乙宮兼集禧觀事除同知樞密院事。十一月庚申，陸佃自守尚書右丞除尚書左丞。溫益自試吏部尚書兼侍讀除中大夫、尚書右丞。六月戊午，范純禮自中大夫、尚書右丞以仍前官出知潁昌府。七月丙戌，安燾自左正議大夫、知樞密院事以觀文殿學士出知河南府兼西京留守。十月乙未，李清臣自右光祿大夫、門下侍郎以資政殿學士出知大名府兼北京留守。
1102崇寧元年壬午七月戊子，蔡京自守尚書左丞加通議大夫、守尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎。 韓忠彥 曾布 蔡京五月庚申、韓忠彥自左僕射兼門下侍郎以觀文殿大學士出知大名府兼北京留守。甲子落職。閏六月壬戌，曾布自右僕射以觀文殿大學士、右銀青光祿大夫出知潤州。六月丙申，許將自右銀青光祿大夫、守中書侍郎加門下侍郎。溫益自中大夫、守尚書右丞加中書侍郎。蔡京自翰林學士承旨中大夫、知制誥兼侍讀、修國史、實錄修撰除尚書左丞。趙挺之自試吏部尚書兼侍讀、修國史、編修國朝會要除中大夫、尚書右丞。八月己卯，遷尚書左丞。張商英自翰林學士、知制誥兼侍讀、修國史、實錄修撰除中大夫、尚書右丞。十月戊寅，蔡卞自資政殿學士、左正議大夫、中太乙宮使兼侍讀除知樞密院事。六月丙申，陸佃自尚書左丞依前太中大夫出知亳州。七月庚午，章楶自同知樞密院事以資政殿學士領中太乙宮使。十月癸亥，蔣之奇自知樞密院事依前右正議大夫，以觀文殿學士出知杭州。
1103二年癸未正月丁未，蔡京自右僕射加右光祿大夫、尚書左僕射兼門下侍郎。 蔡京四月戊寅，趙挺之自中大夫、尚書左丞除中書侍郎。張商英自中大夫、尚書右丞除尚書左丞。吳居厚自戶部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。安惇自兵部尚書兼侍讀除中大夫、同知樞密院事。正月壬辰，中書侍郎溫益卒。八月戊申，張商英自尚書左丞以通議大夫出知亳州，尋改蘄州。
1104三年甲申五月己卯，蔡京自尚書左僕射加司空。 蔡京九月乙亥，趙挺之自右光祿大夫、中書侍郎加門下侍郎。吳居厚自右光祿大夫、尚書右丞加中書侍郎。張康國自翰林學士承旨、知制誥除尚書左丞。鄧洵武自刑部尚書除尚書右丞。八月乙巳，許將自門下侍郎仍前官以資政殿學士出知河南府。十二月戊午，同知樞密院事安惇卒。
1105四年乙酉三月甲辰，趙挺之自門下侍郎加右銀青光祿大夫、尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎。 蔡京 趙挺之六月戊子，趙挺之自右僕射授金紫光祿大夫、觀文殿大學士，領中太乙宮使。二月甲寅，張康國自中大夫、守尚書右丞仍前官知樞密院事。劉逵自兵部尚書兼侍讀、修國史兼實錄修撰除中大夫、同知樞密院事。何執中自太中大夫、試吏部尚書兼侍讀除尚書左丞。正月丙申，蔡卞自金紫光祿大夫、知樞密院事仍前官以資政殿大學士出知河南府。
1106五年丙戌二月丙寅，趙挺之自尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎加特進、光祿大夫、尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎。 蔡京 趙挺之二月丙寅，蔡京自左僕射以守司空、安遠軍節度使、開府儀同三司領中太乙宮使。正月甲辰，吳居厚自右光祿大夫、中書侍郎加門下侍郎。劉逵自中大夫、同知樞密院事加中書侍郎。十二月戊午，劉逵自中大夫、中書侍郎守本官出知亳州。
1107大觀元年丁亥正月甲午，蔡京自安遠軍節度使依前司空、左僕射兼門下侍郎、魏國公。十二月庚寅，蔡京自司空、左僕射兼門下侍郎、魏國公加太尉。 趙挺之 蔡京三月丁酉，趙挺之自尚書右僕射兼中書侍郎以特進、觀文殿大學士領佑神觀致仕。正月壬子，何執中自太中大夫、尚書左丞加中書侍郎。鄧洵武自中大夫、守尚書右丞除尚書左丞。梁子美自中大夫、試戶部尚書除尚書右丞。三月丁酉，何執中自中書侍郎加門下侍郎。鄧洵武自尚書左丞加中書侍郎。梁子美自尚書右丞遷尚書左丞。朱諤自吏部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。六月乙未，梁子美加中書侍郎。八月庚申，徐處仁自試戶部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。林攄自太中大夫、試吏部尚書兼侍讀實錄修撰仍前官同知樞密院事。閏十月丙戌，林攄自中大夫、同知樞密院事除尚書左丞。鄭居中自資政殿學士、太中大夫、中太乙宮使兼侍讀、實錄修撰仍前官同知樞密院事。十二月壬寅，起復徐處仁，辭。正月壬寅，吳居厚自右光祿大夫、門下侍郎以資政殿學士仍前官領太一宮使。五月庚寅，鄧洵武自中書侍郎以守本官知隨州。六月己亥，尚書右丞朱諤卒。十月癸酉，尚書右丞徐處仁丁母憂。
1108二年戊子正月己未，蔡京自太尉、左僕射兼門下侍郎、魏國公加太師。 蔡京九月辛亥，林攄自右光祿大夫、尚書左丞加中書侍郎。余深自試吏部尚書除尚書左丞。八月丙申，梁子美自中書侍郎以資政殿學士、正奉大夫出知鄆州。

1123五年癸卯五月庚申，王黼自太宰兼門下侍郎加太傅，依蔡京昨任太師例。 王黼二月乙酉朔，李邦彥自太中大夫、尚書右丞遷尚書左丞。趙野自翰林學士、知制誥除中大夫、尚書右丞。五月庚申，鄭居中自少師、領樞密院事加太保。癸亥，童貫落節鉞，依前太師。六月乙巳，蔡攸以少師、安遠軍節度使領樞密院事。正月辛酉，王安中自中大夫、尚書左丞以慶遠軍節度使、河北河東燕山府路宣撫使知燕山府。六月丁未，鄭居中自領樞密院事、太保致仕，明日卒。七月己未，童貫自領陝西河東河北宣撫使致仕。

1126靖康元年丙午正月辛未，李邦彥自銀青光祿大夫、少宰兼中書侍郎起復，加特進、太宰兼門下侍郎。張邦昌自正奉大夫加特授依前官少宰兼中書侍郎、神霄玉清萬壽宮使。二月庚戌，加光祿大夫，太宰兼門下侍郎。吳敏自知樞密院事遷太中大夫、少宰兼中書侍郎。三月己巳，徐處仁自守中書侍郎加通奉大夫、太宰兼門下侍郎。八月己未，唐恪自正奉大夫加少宰兼中書侍郎。閏十一月壬辰，{上鹵下木}自守門下侍郎加通奉大夫、右僕射、中書侍郎。蔡京 白時中 李邦彥 張邦昌 吳敏 徐處仁 唐恪{上鹵下木}正月辛未，白時中自太宰以觀文殿大學士領中太乙宮使依前特進、慶國公。二月庚戌，李邦彥自太宰兼門下侍郎以觀文殿學士領中太乙宮使。甲寅蔡京自太師、魯國公致仕責授中奉大夫、祕書監、分司南京，河南居住。三月己巳，張邦昌自太宰、兼門下侍郎以觀文殿大學士領中太乙宮使。八月己未，徐處仁自太宰以觀文殿大學士領中太乙宮使。吳敏自少宰以觀文殿大學士領醴泉觀使。閏十一月壬辰朔，唐恪自少宰以觀文殿大學士領中太乙宮使兼侍讀依前光祿大夫。正月己巳，吳敏自中大夫、守門下侍郎除知樞密院事、親征行營副使。李梲自正奉大夫、守吏部尚書除同知樞密院事。庚午，李綱自試兵部侍郎、中大夫除尚書右丞。辛未，趙野自通議大夫，尚書左丞除門下侍郎。王孝迪自通議大夫、翰林學士承旨除中書侍郎。蔡懋自中大夫、同知樞密院事除尚書左丞。甲戌，唐恪自正議大夫、守吏部尚書除同知樞密院事。耿南仲自簽書樞密院事進同知樞密院事。辛巳，路允迪自試兵部尚書除資政殿學士、簽書樞密院事。丁亥，種師道自檢校少保、靜難軍節度使、河北河東制置使除同知樞密院事。二月戊戌，尚書右丞李綱罷，辛丑復職。癸卯，徐處仁自觀文殿大學士、大名尹除中書侍郎。宇文虛中自保和殿大學士除資政殿大學士、簽書樞密院事。庚戌，李綱自中大夫、尚書右丞除知樞密院事。耿南仲自同知樞密院事除尚書左丞。李梲自正奉大夫、同知樞密院事除尚書右丞。三月己巳，唐恪自正議大夫、同知樞密院事依前官加中書侍郎。{上鹵下木}自翰林學士、知制誥除中大夫、尚書右丞。許翰自御史中丞除中大夫、同知樞密院事。四月癸卯，耿南仲自太中大夫、尚書左丞依前官加門下侍郎。是月，種師道自檢校少傅、中太乙宮使復除同知樞密院事。六月戊戌，李綱宣撫河北、河東。八月丙申，種師道代李綱為宣撫。己未，{上鹵下木}自太中大夫、尚書右丞除中書侍郎。陳過庭自太中大夫、試禮部尚書兼侍讀除尚書右丞。聶昌自開封府尹除中大夫、同知樞密院事。李回自朝請大夫、御史中丞兼侍讀除延康殿學士、簽書樞密院事。九月丁丑，王{上宀下禹}自禮部尚書除尚書左丞。十月丁未，馮澥自禮部尚書、太子詹事兼侍讀除中大夫、知樞密院事。十一月丁丑，陳過庭自太中大夫、尚書右丞除中書侍郎。孫傅自試兵部尚書除中大夫、尚書右丞。甲申，除同知樞密院事。曹輔自御史中丞除延康殿學士、簽書樞密院事。庚寅，{上鹵下木}自資政殿學士、中大夫、提舉萬壽觀兼侍讀、領開封府事除門下侍郎。閏十一月丁酉，馮澥自資政殿學士、中大夫、太子賓客除尚書左丞。庚子，張叔夜自延康殿學士、南道都總管除簽書樞密院事。二月癸卯，蔡懋自尚書左丞以資攻殿學士、依前中大夫知大名府兼北京留守。王孝迪自中書侍郎以資政殿學士提舉醴泉觀。癸丑，種師道自同知樞密院事以檢校少傅領中太乙宮使。甲寅，童貫自太師、廣陽郡王、徐豫國公責授左衞上將軍致仕，池州居住。蔡攸自太保、領樞密院事、燕國公責授太中大夫、提舉亳州明道宮，任便居住。三月戊辰，李梲自尚書左丞以正奉大夫、資政殿學士提舉南京鴻慶宮。庚午，宇文虛中自簽書樞密院事以資政殿學士、仍中大夫出知青州。四月庚戌，趙野自門下侍郎以資政殿學士出知襄陽府。六月癸卯，路允迪自簽書樞密院事以資政殿學士提舉醴泉觀。八月戊午，許翰自同知樞密院事以延康殿學士出知亳州。九月丁丑，李綱自知樞密院事以觀文殿學士出知揚州。十月戊午，王{上宀下禹}自尚書左丞坐辭使軍前責授單州團練副使，新州安置。辛酉，種師道卒。十一月戊辰，馮澥自知樞密院除資政殿學士、太子賓客。丁丑，{上鹵下木}自中書侍郎依前官提舉醴泉觀兼侍讀。丁亥，李回自簽書樞密院事除提舉萬壽觀。

1086 CE.Yuanyou reign, year one, bingyin.In the intercalary second month on gengyin, Sima Guang rose from Right Remonstrance Grandee and Acting Vice Director of the Secretariat to Left Vice Director, retaining his former offices and concurrently Vice Director of the Secretariat. Incumbent: Cai Jing.In the ninth month on yihai, Zhao Tingzhi added Vice Director of the Secretariat from Right Glory Grandee and Vice Director of the Chancellery; Wu Juhou became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Right Glory Grandee and Right Vice Director; Zhang Kangguo became Left Vice Director from Hanlin expositor and drafting officer; Deng Xunwu became Right Vice Director from Minister of Justice.In the eighth month on yisi, Xu Jiang was sent to Henan as Assisting Governance Academician from Vice Director of the Secretariat at his former rank. In the twelfth month on wuwu, Associate Commissioner of Military Affairs An Dun died.
1105 CE.Chongning reign, year four, yiyou.In the third month on jiachen, Zhao Tingzhi rose from Vice Director of the Secretariat to Right Vice Director and Vice Director of the Chancellery as Right Silver Glory Grandee. The incumbents were Cai Jing and Zhao Tingzhi.In the sixth month on wuzi, Zhao Tingzhi left the council as Golden Purple Glory Grandee and Hall for Viewing Literature Grand Academician directing the Central Great One Abbey from Right Vice Director.In the second month on jiayin, Zhang Kangguo became Commissioner of Military Affairs from Acting Right Vice Director at his former rank; Liu Kui became Associate Commissioner from Minister of War, Reader-in-Waiting, and history compiler; He Zhizhong became Left Vice Director from Acting Minister of Personnel and Reader-in-Waiting.In the first month on bingshen, Cai Bian was sent to Henan as Assisting Governance Academician from Commissioner of Military Affairs at his former rank.
1106 CE.Chongning reign, year five, bingxu.In the second month on bingyin, Zhao Tingzhi added Specially Advanced and Glory Grandee to Right Vice Director and Vice Director of the Chancellery. The incumbents were Cai Jing and Zhao Tingzhi.In the second month on bingyin, Cai Jing left as Left Vice Director to serve as Acting Minister of Works, Anyuan commissioner, and Honorary Equal to Three Counsellors directing the Central Great One Abbey.In the first month on jiachen, Wu Juhou added Vice Director of the Secretariat from Right Glory Grandee and Vice Director of the Chancellery; Liu Kui became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Associate Commissioner.In the twelfth month on wuwu, Liu Kui was sent to Bozhou at his original rank from Vice Director of the Chancellery.
1107 CE.Daguan reign, year one, dinghai.In the first month on jiawu, Cai Jing returned from Anyuan commissioner as Minister of Works, Left Vice Director, Vice Director of the Secretariat, and Duke of Wei. In the twelfth month on gengyin, Cai Jing was advanced to Grand Commandant. The incumbents were Zhao Tingzhi and Cai Jing.In the third month on dingyou, Zhao Tingzhi retired from Right Vice Director and Vice Director of the Chancellery as Specially Advanced, Hall for Viewing Literature Grand Academician directing the Youshen Abbey.In the first month on renzi, He Zhizhong became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Left Vice Director; Deng Xunwu became Left Vice Director from Acting Right Vice Director; Liang Zimei became Right Vice Director from Acting Minister of Revenue. In the third month on dingyou, He Zhizhong added Vice Director of the Secretariat; Deng Xunwu became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Left Vice Director; Liang Zimei moved to Left Vice Director; Zhu E became Right Vice Director from Minister of Personnel. In the sixth month on yiwei, Liang Zimei was made Vice Director of the Chancellery. In the eighth month on gengshen, Xu Churen became Right Vice Director from Acting Minister of Revenue. Lin Shu became Associate Commissioner from Acting Minister of Personnel, Reader-in-Waiting, and Veritable Record compiler at his former rank. In the intercalary tenth month on bingxu, Lin Shu became Left Vice Director from Associate Commissioner; Zheng Juzhong became Associate Commissioner from Assisting Governance Academician and Central Great One Abbey director. In the twelfth month on renyin, Xu Churen was recalled from mourning and declined.In the first month on renyin, Wu Juhou became Great One Abbey director as Assisting Governance Academician from Vice Director of the Secretariat at his former rank. In the fifth month on gengyin, Deng Xunwu was sent to Suizhou at his original rank from Vice Director of the Chancellery. In the sixth month on jihai, Right Vice Director Zhu E died. In the tenth month on guiyou, Right Vice Director Xu Churen entered mourning for his mother.
1108 CE.Daguan reign, year two, wuzi.In the first month on jiwei, Cai Jing rose from Grand Commandant, Left Vice Director, and Duke of Wei to Grand Preceptor. Incumbent: Cai Jing.In the ninth month on xinhai, Lin Shu became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Left Vice Director as Right Glory Grandee; Yu Shen became Left Vice Director from Acting Minister of Personnel.In the eighth month on bingshen, Liang Zimei was sent to Yanzhou as Assisting Governance Academician and Right Supporter Grandee from Vice Director of the Chancellery.
1109 CE.Daguan reign, year three, jichou.In the sixth month on xinsi, He Zhizhong rose from concurrent Vice Director of the Chancellery and Secretariat to Left Vice Director and Vice Director of the Secretariat as Specially Advanced. The incumbents were Cai Jing and He Zhizhong.In the sixth month on xinsi, Cai Jing left as Left Vice Director to serve as Grand Preceptor directing the Central Great One Abbey.In the fourth month on guisi, Zheng Juzhong was promoted from Associate Commissioner to Commissioner of Military Affairs; Guan Shiren became Associate Commissioner from Acting Minister of Personnel. On guimao, Yu Shen became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Left Vice Director; Xue Ang became Left Vice Director from Acting Minister of War and Reader-in-Waiting; Liu Zhengfu became Right Vice Director from Acting Minister of Works and Veritable Record compiler.In the third month on wushen, Commissioner of Military Affairs Zhang Kangguo died suddenly. In the fourth month on wuyin, Lin Shu was sent to Chuzhou at his former rank from Vice Director of the Chancellery. In the sixth month on jiaxu, Guan Shiren left as Youshen Abbey director as Assisting Governance Academician from Associate Commissioner and soon died.
1110 CE.Daguan reign, year four, gengyin.In the sixth month on yihai, Zhang Shangying rose from Acting Vice Director of the Chancellery to Acting Right Vice Director and Vice Director of the Chancellery as General Policy Grandee. The incumbents were He Zhizhong and Zhang Shangying.In the second month on jichou, Yu Shen added Vice Director of the Secretariat; Zhang Shangying became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Assisting Governance Academician and Central Great One Abbey director; Hou Meng became Associate Commissioner from Acting Minister of Revenue. In the eighth month on yihai, Liu Zhengfu became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Right Vice Director; Hou Meng became Left Vice Director from Associate Commissioner; Deng Xunren became Right Vice Director from Hanlin expositor and Reader-in-Waiting. On gengchen, Wu Juhou was made Vice Director of the Secretariat from Youshen Abbey director. In the tenth month on gengshen, he was appointed Commissioner of Military Affairs.In the fifth month on bingyin, Yu Shen was sent to Qingzhou as Assisting Governance Academician from Vice Director of the Secretariat at his former rank. In the sixth month on bingshen, Xue Ang was sent to Jiangning as Assisting Governance Academician from Left Vice Director. In the tenth month on dingyou, Zheng Juzhong became Central Great One Abbey director as Hall for Viewing Literature Academician from Commissioner of Military Affairs at his former rank.
1111 CE.Zhenghe reign, year one, xinmao.The incumbents were He Zhizhong and Zhang Shangying.In the eighth month on dingsi, Zhang Shangying left as Right Vice Director for Henan as Hall for Viewing Literature Grand Academician and Western Capital regent.In the third month on guiyou, Wang Xiang became Associate Commissioner from Acting Minister of Personnel.In the ninth month on wuyin, Wang Xiang was sent to Bozhou from Associate Commissioner at the rank of Grandee of the Palace.
1112 CE.Zhenghe reign, year two, renchen.In the fourth month on gengxu, He Zhizhong was made Minister of Works for completing the Veritable Records of Emperor Zhezong. In the fifth month on jisi, Cai Jing came out of retirement as Grand Preceptor, attending the Secretariat every three days. In the eleventh month on xinsi, he was enfeoffed as Duke of Lu. He Zhizhong rose from Left Vice Director to Junior Tutor and Grand Preceptor, retaining Vice Director of the Secretariat. The incumbents were Cai Jing and He Zhizhong.In the sixth month on jichou, Yu Shen was restored to Vice Director of the Secretariat from his posting at Qingzhou.
1113 CE.Zhenghe reign, year three, guisi.In the eighth month on bingzi, He Zhizhong was advanced to Junior Preceptor from Left Vice Director. The incumbents were Cai Jing and He Zhizhong.In the first month on yihai, Zheng Juzhong was reappointed Commissioner of Military Affairs from Hall for Viewing Literature Academician and Central Great One Abbey director. In the fourth month on jiyou, Xue Ang became Right Vice Director from Assisting Governance Academician and General Policy Grandee.In the first month on yihai, Wu Juhou was sent to Hongzhou as Wukang commissioner from Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the fourth month on guisi, Deng Xunren was sent to Bozhou as Assisting Governance Academician and General Policy Grandee from Right Vice Director and was soon stripped of rank.
1114 CE.Zhenghe reign, year four, jiawu.The incumbents were Cai Jing and He Zhizhong.
1115 CE.Zhenghe reign, year five, yiwei.The incumbents were Cai Jing and He Zhizhong.
1116 CE.Zhenghe reign, year six, bingshen.In the fifth month on gengzi, Zheng Juzhong rose from Commissioner of Military Affairs to Junior Protector and Grand Preceptor with Vice Director of the Secretariat; Liu Zhengfu became Specially Advanced, Junior Preceptor, and Vice Director of the Chancellery from Vice Director of the Chancellery. The incumbents were Cai Jing, He Zhizhong, Zheng Juzhong, and Liu Zhengfu.In the fourth month on xinwei, He Zhizhong retired from Left Vice Director as Grand Tutor. In the twelfth month on yiyou, Liu Zhengfu retired from Junior Preceptor as Anhua commissioner and Honorary Equal to Three Counsellors.In the fifth month on renyin, Deng Xunwu became Commissioner of Military Affairs from Baoda commissioner and Youshen Abbey director. In the eighth month on yisi, Xue Ang became Left Vice Director from Right Vice Director; Hou Meng became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Left Vice Director. In the eleventh month on xinmao, Tong Guan was made Signing Officer of the Bureau of Military Affairs from pacification commissioner of Shaanxi, Hedong, and Hebei; on gengzi, Bai Shizhong became Right Vice Director from Minister of Rites and Reader-in-Waiting.
1117 CE.Zhenghe reign, year seven, dingyou.In the eleventh month on xinmao, Zheng Juzhong was recalled from mourning for his mother to Vice Director of the Secretariat from Grand Preceptor. Yu Shen was made Specially Advanced, Junior Preceptor, and Vice Director of the Chancellery. The incumbents were Cai Jing, Zheng Juzhong, and Yu Shen.In the eighth month on gengwu, Grand Preceptor Zheng Juzhong entered mourning for his mother.In the second month, Tong Guan was made pacification commissioner of Shaanxi, Hedong, and Hebei while retaining concurrent Co-Signing Officer of the Bureau of Military Affairs. In the third month on yiwei, his title was changed to Acting Director of Military Affairs. In the eleventh month on xinmao, Bai Shizhong became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Right Vice Director. In the twelfth month on dingsi, Xue Ang added Specially Advanced and Vice Director of the Secretariat from Left Vice Director. On gengwu, Tong Guan was made Director of Military Affairs.In the tenth month on wuyin, Hou Meng was sent to Bozhou as Assisting Governance Academician from Vice Director of the Chancellery.
1118 CE.Chonghe reign, year one, wuxu.In the seventh month on renwu, Zheng Juzhong was advanced to Junior Tutor from Grand Preceptor; Yu Shen was advanced to Junior Protector from Junior Preceptor. In the ninth month on xinchou, Zheng Juzhong was removed from recall-from-mourning status. The incumbents were Cai Jing, Zheng Juzhong, and Yu Shen.In the first month on gengxu, Wang Fu was recalled from mourning as Left Vice Director from Hanlin expositor. In the seventh month on renwu, Director of Military Affairs Tong Guan was advanced to Acting Grand Protector; in the eighth month on jiayin, to Grand Protector. In the ninth month on gengyin, Bai Shizhong moved to Vice Director of the Secretariat from Vice Director of the Chancellery; Wang Fu became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Left Vice Director; Feng Xizai became Left Vice Director from Hanlin expositor and Lecturer-in-Waiting; Fan Zhixu became Right Vice Director from Minister of Justice.In the ninth month on gengyin, Xue Ang left as Zhanghua commissioner and Youshen Abbey director from Vice Director of the Secretariat.
1119 CE.Xuanhe reign, year one, jihai.In the first month on wuwu, Yu Shen rose from Junior Preceptor and Vice Director of the Chancellery to Grand Preceptor and Vice Director of the Secretariat as Specially Advanced; Wang Fu became Specially Advanced, Junior Preceptor, and Vice Director of the Chancellery directing the Divine Empyrean Jade Clarity Longevity Abbey from General Policy Grandee. The incumbents were Cai Jing, Yu Shen, and Wang Fu.In the second month on wuxu, Deng Xunwu at Suizhou was advanced to Junior Protector from Acting Vice Director of the Chancellery. In the third month on jiwei, Feng Xizai became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Left Vice Director; Fan Zhixu moved to Left Vice Director from Right Vice Director; Zhang Bangchang became Right Vice Director from Hanlin Academician. In the seventh month on jiayin, Tong Guan was advanced to Grand Tutor from Commissioner of Military Affairs. In the eleventh month on wuchen, Zhang Bangchang moved to Left Vice Director from Right Vice Director; Wang Anzhong became Right Vice Director from Hanlin expositor and drafting officer.In the eighth month on dingyou, Left Vice Director Fan Zhixu entered mourning for his mother.
1120 CE.Xuanhe reign, year two, gengzi.In the ninth month on guihai, Grand Preceptor Yu Shen was advanced to Junior Tutor for completing the Precious Instructions of Emperor Zhezong. In the eleventh month on gengxu, Wang Fu rose from Junior Preceptor and Vice Director of the Chancellery to Junior Protector and Grand Preceptor with Vice Director of the Secretariat as Specially Advanced. The incumbents were Cai Jing, Yu Shen, and Wang Fu.In the sixth month on wuyin, Cai Jing retired from Grand Commandant, Left Vice Director, Duke of Wei, and Grand Preceptor as Grand Preceptor and Duke of Lu. In the eleventh month on jihai, Yu Shen left as Junior Tutor and Zhenjiang commissioner for Fuzhou from Grand Preceptor.In the twelfth month on jichou, Junior Tutor and Weiwu commissioner Zheng Juzhong was made Acting Director of Military Affairs.
1121 CE.Xuanhe reign, year three, xinchou.In the ninth month on bingyin, Wang Fu was advanced to Junior Tutor from Junior Protector and Grand Preceptor with Vice Director of the Secretariat. Incumbent: Wang Fu.In the first month on guimao, Tong Guan left as pacification commissioner of the Jiang-Zhe-Jiang-Huai circuits from Grand Protector and Director of Military Affairs. In the fifth month on wuxu, Zheng Juzhong had the "acting" designation removed and became Director of Military Affairs following Tong Guan's precedent. In the eighth month on yisi, Tong Guan was advanced to Grand Preceptor and Zheng Juzhong to Junior Preceptor. In the tenth month on gengshen, Tong Guan resumed pacification duties over Shaanxi, Hedong, and Hebei. In the eleventh month on dingchou, Zhang Bangchang became Vice Director of the Chancellery from Left Vice Director; Wang Anzhong moved to Left Vice Director from Right Vice Director; Li Bangyan became Right Vice Director from Hanlin expositor, Reader-in-Waiting, and national history compiler.In the first month on renyin, Junior Protector Deng Xunwu, administering Suizhou, died. In the eleventh month on dingchou, Feng Xizai was sent to Bozhou as Assisting Governance Academician from Vice Director of the Chancellery.
1122 CE.Xuanhe reign, year four, renyin.In the sixth month on bingwu, Wang Fu was advanced to Junior Preceptor. Incumbent: Wang Fu.
1123 CE.Xuanhe reign, year five, guimao.In the fifth month on gengshen, Wang Fu was advanced to Grand Tutor from Grand Preceptor and Vice Director of the Secretariat, following the precedent of Cai Jing's former Grand Preceptor tenure. Incumbent: Wang Fu.On the first of the second month, yiyou, Li Bangyan moved to Left Vice Director from Right Vice Director; Zhao Ye became Right Vice Director from Hanlin Academician and drafting officer. In the fifth month on gengshen, Zheng Juzhong was advanced to Grand Protector from Junior Preceptor and Director of Military Affairs; on guihai, Tong Guan was stripped of his command seal but kept his rank as Grand Preceptor. In the sixth month on yisi, Cai You became Director of Military Affairs from Junior Preceptor and Anyuan commissioner.In the first month on xinyou, Wang Anzhong was sent to Yanshan as Qingyuan commissioner and pacification commissioner of the Hebei-Hedong-Yanshan circuit from Left Vice Director. In the sixth month on dingwei, Zheng Juzhong retired as Director of Military Affairs and Grand Protector and died the next day. In the seventh month on jiwei, Tong Guan retired as pacification commissioner of Shaanxi, Hedong, and Hebei.
1124 CE.The sixth year of the reign, jiachen.In the ninth month on yihai, Li Bangyan rose from Tongfeng Grandee and acting Left Vice Director to Silver-Gleam Grandee, Junior Preceptor with the Secretariat, and commissioner of the Divine Empyrean Jade-Pure Longevity Palace. Bai Shizhong advanced from Purple-Gold Grandee and Vice Director of the Chancellery to specially advanced rank, Grand Preceptor with the Chancellery, and the same palace commission. In the twelfth month on guihai, Cai Jing was recalled from retirement as Duke of Lu and restored as Grand Preceptor, head of the Three Departments, and commissioner of the Divine Empyrean palace. Incumbents: Wang Fu, Cai Jing, Bai Shizhong, Li Bangyan.In the eleventh month on bingzi, Wang Fu, Duke of Chu, retired as Grand Tutor from his posts as Grand Preceptor and Vice Director of the Chancellery.On dingsi in the fourth month, Li Bangyan resumed office despite mourning a parent. On yimao in the eighth month, Tong Guan was recalled from retirement as Jiannan East Sichuan commissioner to resume as Grand Preceptor, head of Military Affairs, and pacification commissioner for Shaanxi, Hebei, Hedong, and Yanshan, and was enfeoffed Duke of Xu and Yu. On yihai in the ninth month, Zhao Ye moved from Right to Left Vice Director; Yu Wen Cuizhong became Right Vice Director from Hanlin expositor; Cai Mao became Vice Commissioner of Military Affairs from acting Kaifeng administrator; Cai You lost his military commission but kept the rank of Junior Preceptor.
1125 CE.The seventh year of the reign, yisi; Emperor Qinzong took the throne in the twelfth month on gengshen.The incumbents were Cai Jing, Bai Shizhong, and Li Bangyan.In the fourth month on gengshen, Cai Jing ceased overseeing the Three Departments and retired again as Grand Preceptor and Duke of Lu.On jiwei in the sixth month, Cai You added Grand Guardian to his posts as Junior Preceptor and head of Military Affairs. On gengshen in the twelfth month, Wu Min became Vice Director of the Chancellery from acting supervising secretary; on renxu, Geng Nanzhong was made signing officer of Military Affairs from Huayou academician and crown-prince tutor.
1126 CE.Jingkang reign, year one, bingwu.On xinwei in the first month, Li Bangyan resumed from mourning as Silver-Gleam Grandee and Junior Preceptor with the Secretariat and was advanced to specially advanced rank, Grand Preceptor with the Chancellery. Zhang Bangchang was specially promoted to Junior Preceptor with the Secretariat and commissioner of the Divine Empyrean palace. On gengxu in the second month he added Grand Preceptor with the Chancellery and Grandee of the Imperial Household. Wu Min moved from Commissioner of Military Affairs to Junior Preceptor with the Secretariat; on jisi in the third month Xu Churen became Grand Preceptor with the Chancellery; on jiwei in the eighth month Tang Que became Junior Preceptor with the Secretariat; on renchen in the intercalary eleventh month He Li became Right Vice Director and Vice Director of the Secretariat. Incumbents: Cai Jing, Bai Shizhong, Li Bangyan, Zhang Bangchang, Wu Min, Xu Churen, Tang Que, and He Li.On xinwei in the first month, Bai Shizhong left the Grand Preceptorate for Viewing Literature grand academician heading the Central Grand Unity Palace, keeping his specially advanced rank as Duke of Qing. On gengxu in the second month, Li Bangyan took the same palace post from Grand Preceptor with the Chancellery. On jiayin, retired Grand Preceptor Cai Jing, Duke of Lu, was punished with a post as Middle-Feng director of the Secretariat at the Southern Capital, residing in Henan. On jisi in the third month Zhang Bangchang received the Central Grand Unity commission; on jiwei in the eighth month Xu Churen did likewise; Wu Min took the Sweet Spring Abbey from Junior Preceptor; on the first of the intercalary eleventh month, renchen, Tang Que took the Central Grand Unity palace with reader duties, keeping Grandee of the Imperial Household rank.On jisi in the first month, Wu Min became Commissioner of Military Affairs and deputy commander of the imperial campaign headquarters; Li Zhuo became Vice Commissioner from acting Minister of Personnel. On gengwu, Li Gang was made Right Vice Director from acting Vice Minister of War; on xinwei Zhao Ye became Vice Director of the Chancellery; Wang Xiaodi Vice Director of the Secretariat; Cai Mao Left Vice Director; on jiaxu Tang Que Vice Commissioner; Geng Nanzhong was promoted to Vice Commissioner; on xinsi Lu Yundi became signing officer; on dinghai Zhong Shidao became Vice Commissioner from Jingnan commissioner. On wuxu in the second month Li Gang was dismissed as Right Vice Director but restored on xinchou; on guimao Xu Churen became Vice Director of the Secretariat; Yu Wen Xuzhong signing officer; on gengxu Li Gang became Commissioner, Geng Nanzhong Left Vice Director, and Li Zhuo Right Vice Director. On jisi in the third month Tang Que added the Secretariat, He Li became Right Vice Director, and Xu Han Vice Commissioner. On guimao in the fourth month Geng Nanzhong added the Chancellery; that month Zhong Shidao returned as Vice Commissioner. On wuxu in the sixth month Li Gang pacified Hebei and Hedong; on bingshen in the eighth month Zhong Shidao replaced him. On jiwei, He Li became Vice Director of the Secretariat; Chen Guoting Right Vice Director; Nie Chang Vice Commissioner; Li Hui signing officer; on dingchou in the ninth month Wang Yu became Left Vice Director; on dingwei in the tenth month Feng Xi Commissioner of Military Affairs; on dingchou in the eleventh month Chen Guoting Vice Director of the Secretariat; Sun Fu Right Vice Director and then Vice Commissioner on jiashen; Cao Fu signing officer; on gengyin He Li Vice Director of the Chancellery; on dingyou in the intercalary eleventh month Feng Xi Left Vice Director; on gengzi Zhang Shuye signing officer.On guimao in the second month, Cai Mao left the Left Vice Directorship for Assisting Governance academician at Daming with Northern Capital duties; Wang Xiaodi took the Sweet Spring Abbey from the Secretariat. On guichou, Zhong Shidao became Honorary Junior Tutor heading the Central Grand Unity Palace; on jiayin Tong Guan was stripped to Left Guard senior general in retirement at Chizhou, and Cai You was demoted to commissioner of the Mingdao Abbey at Bozhou. On wuchen in the third month Li Zhuo took the Hongqing Abbey at the Southern Capital; on gengwu Yu Wen Xuzhong went to Qingzhou; on gengxu in the fourth month Zhao Ye to Xiangyang; on guimao in the sixth month Lu Yundi to the Sweet Spring Abbey; on wuwu in the eighth month Xu Han to Bozhou; on dingchou in the ninth month Li Gang to Yangzhou. On wuwu in the tenth month Wang Yu was exiled to Xinzhou for refusing envoy duty before the army; on xinyou Zhong Shidao died. On wuchen in the eleventh month Feng Xi left Military Affairs for Assisting Governance academician and crown-prince tutor; on dingchou He Li took the Sweet Spring Abbey with reader duties; on dinghai Li Hui the Longevity Abbey.
