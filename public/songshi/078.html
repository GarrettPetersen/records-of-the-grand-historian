求天正十一月經朔加時入交泛日：置天正十一月經朔加時積分，以交終分及秒去之，不盡，滿統法為日，不滿為餘秒，即天正十一月經朔加時入交泛日及餘秒。
To find the civil eleventh-month mean new-moon hour general convergence day: set the civil eleventh-month mean new-moon hour accumulated parts, discard full convergence termination parts and seconds, convert the remainder by the unification factor into days and remainder seconds, and obtain the civil eleventh-month mean new-moon hour general convergence day and remainder seconds.
20
求次朔及望加時入交泛日：置天正經朔加時入交泛日及餘秒，求次朔，以朔差加之。 求望，以望策加之，滿交終日及餘秒去之。 即次朔及望加時入交泛日及餘秒。 若以經朔小餘減之，餘為夜半入交泛日。
To find the next new moon and full moon hour general convergence day: set the civil new-year mean new-moon hour general convergence day and remainder seconds; for the next new moon, add the new-moon difference. To seek the full moon, add the full-moon stride and discard full convergence termination days and remainder seconds. That yields the next new moon and full moon hour general convergence day and remainder seconds. If the mean new-moon small remainder is subtracted, the remainder is the midnight general convergence day.
21
求定朔望夜半入交泛日：置經朔、望夜半入交泛日，若定朔、望大餘有進退者，亦進退交日，否則因經為定，即定朔望夜半入交泛日及餘秒。
To find the fixed new and full moon midnight general convergence day: set the mean new and full moon midnight general convergence day; if the fixed new or full moon great remainder advances or retreats, advance or retreat the convergence day accordingly; otherwise keep the mean value as fixed, yielding the fixed new and full moon midnight general convergence day and remainder seconds.
22
求次朔夜半入交泛日：置定朔夜半入交泛日及餘秒，大月加二日，小月加一日，餘皆加九千四百七十八、秒五十六，求次日，累加一日，滿交終日及餘秒去之，即次定朔及每日夜半入交泛日及餘秒。
To find the next new-moon midnight general convergence day: set the fixed new-moon midnight general convergence day and remainder seconds; add 2 days in a long month and 1 day in a short month; always add 9,478 to the remainder and 56 to the seconds; for each following day accumulate one day and discard full convergence termination days and remainder seconds, obtaining the next fixed new moon and each day’s midnight general convergence day and remainder seconds.
23
求朔望加時入交常日：置經朔、望入交泛日及餘秒，以其朔、望入盈縮限朏朒定數朏減朒加之，即朔、望加時入交常日及餘秒。
To find the new and full moon hour regular convergence day: set the mean new and full moon general convergence day and remainder seconds; apply the waxing–waning fixed number from their expansion–contraction limit entry, subtracting waxing and adding waning, to obtain the new and full moon hour regular convergence day and remainder seconds.
24
求朔望加時入交定日：置其朔、望入轉朏朒定數，以交率乘之，交數而一，所得，以朏減朒加入交常日及余秒，滿與不足，進退其日，即朔、望加時入交定日及餘秒。
To find the new and full moon hour fixed convergence day: set the rotation waxing–waning fixed number for that new or full moon, multiply by the convergence rate and divide by the convergence number, then subtract waxing and add waning to the regular convergence day and remainder seconds, advancing or retreating the day as needed to obtain the new and full moon hour fixed convergence day and remainder seconds.
25
求月行入陰陽曆：置其朔、望入交定日及餘秒，在交中已下為月行陽曆； 已上去之，餘為月行陰曆。
To find the moon’s motion entry into yin and yang calendars: set the new or full moon fixed convergence day and remainder seconds; at convergence middle or below, the moon is in the yang calendar; if above, remove that amount; the remainder is motion in the yin calendar.
26
求朔望加時月行入陰陽曆積度：置月行入陰陽曆日及餘秒，以統法通日，內餘，九而一為分，分滿百為度，即朔望加時月行入陰陽曆積度及分。
To find the new and full moon hour accumulated lunar degree in yin and yang calendars: set the day and remainder seconds of lunar motion in the yin or yang calendar, convert days through the unification factor with the remainder included, divide by 9 for parts, and let parts filling 100 become degrees, yielding the new and full moon hour accumulated degree and parts.
27
求朔望加時月去黃道度：置入陰陽曆積度及分，如交象已下為入少象； 已上，覆減交中度，餘為入老象。 皆列於上，下列交中度，相減相乘，進位，如一百三十八而一，為泛差。 又視入老、少象度，如半交象已下為初； 已上去之，餘為末。 皆二因，退位，初減末加泛差，滿百為度，即朔、望加時月去黃道度及分。
To find the new and full moon hour lunar departure from the ecliptic in degrees: set the accumulated degree in yin and yang calendars and parts; at convergence image or below counts as entry into the lesser image; if above, subtract from convergence middle degree; the remainder is entry into the elder image. Place both values above with convergence middle degree below, subtract and multiply, carry, and divide by 138 to obtain the general difference. Again, for entry into elder or lesser image degrees, at half convergence image or below counts as initial; if above, remove that amount; the remainder is final. Double each, retreat one place, subtract the general difference at initial and add at final; when parts fill 100 they become degrees, yielding the new and full moon hour lunar departure from the ecliptic in degrees and parts.
28
求日月食甚定餘：置定朔小餘，如半統法已下，與半統法相減相乘，如三萬六千九十而一為時差，以減。 如半統法已上減去半統法，餘亦與半統法相減相乘，如一萬八千四十五而一為時差，午前以減，午後以加，皆加、減定朔小餘，為日食甚小餘。 與半法相減，餘為午前、後分。 其月食者，以定望小餘為月食甚小餘。
To find the fixed remainder at greatest solar and lunar eclipse: set the fixed new-moon small remainder; if at half the unification factor or below, subtract and multiply against half the unification factor, divide by 36,090 for the time difference, and subtract. If at half the unification factor or above, subtract half the unification factor first; with the remainder likewise compute the time difference using 18,045 as divisor; subtract before noon and add after noon, adjusting the fixed new-moon small remainder to obtain the solar eclipse greatest small remainder. Subtract from half the factor; the remainder is the before- and after-noon parts. For lunar eclipse, use the fixed full-moon small remainder as the lunar eclipse greatest small remainder.
29
求日月食甚辰刻：各置食甚小餘，倍之，以辰法除之為辰數，不滿，五因，滿刻法而一為刻，不滿為分。 其辰數命子正，算外，即食甚辰刻及分。 若加半辰，即命起子初。
To find the hour and quarter of greatest solar and lunar eclipse: set the eclipse greatest small remainder, double it, divide by the hour factor for hours; multiply the remainder by 5 and divide by the quarter factor for quarters; the remainder is parts. Name the hour count from zi zheng, counting outside the reckoning, to obtain the hour, quarter, and parts of greatest eclipse. If half an hour is added, the naming starts from zi chu.
30
求氣差：置其朔盈、縮限度及分，自相乘，進二位，盈初、縮末一百九十七而一，盈末、縮初二百一十九而一，皆用減四千一十，為氣泛差。 以乘午前、後分，如半晝分而一，所得，以減泛差，為定差。 春分後，交初以減，交中以加； 秋分後，交初以加，交中以減。 如食在夜，反用之。
To find the qi difference: set the new-moon expansion and contraction limit degree and parts, square it and advance two places; at expansion initial and contraction final divide by 197, at expansion final and contraction initial divide by 219; subtract each result from 4,010 to obtain the general qi difference. Multiply by the before- and after-noon parts, divide by half the daylight parts, and subtract the result from the general difference to obtain the fixed difference. After the spring equinox, subtract at convergence initial and add at convergence middle; after the autumn equinox, add at convergence initial and subtract at convergence middle. If the eclipse occurs at night, reverse the operations.
31
求刻差：置其朔盈、縮限度及分，與半周天相減相乘，進二位，二百九而一，為刻泛差。 以乘午前、後分，如三千七百半而一，為定差。 冬至後午前、夏至後午後，交初以加，交中以減。 冬至後午後、夏至後午前，交初以減，交中以加。
To find the quarter difference: set the new-moon expansion and contraction limit degree and parts, subtract and multiply against half the circuit of heaven, advance two places, and divide by 209 for the general quarter difference. Multiply by the before- and after-noon parts and divide by 3,750½ to obtain the fixed difference. After the winter solstice before noon and after the summer solstice after noon, add at convergence initial and subtract at convergence middle. After the winter solstice after noon and after the summer solstice before noon, subtract at convergence initial and add at convergence middle.
32
求日入食限交前後分：置朔入交定日及餘秒，以氣、刻、時三差各加減之，如交中日已下為不食； 已上去之，如後限已下為交後分； 前限已上覆減交中日，餘為交前分。
To find the day’s entry into the eclipse limit before and after convergence parts: set the new moon’s fixed convergence day and remainder seconds; apply the qi, quarter, and time differences; at convergence middle day or below there is no eclipse; if above, remove that; if at the later limit or below, it is the after-convergence part; if at the earlier limit or above, subtract from convergence middle day; the remainder is the before-convergence part.
33
求日食分：置交前後分，如陽曆食限已下為陽曆食定分； 已上，用減一萬二千八百，餘為陰曆食定分。 如不足減者，日不食。
To find the solar eclipse parts: set the before- and after-convergence parts; at the yang-calendar eclipse limit or below gives the yang-calendar eclipse fixed parts; if above, subtract from 12,800; the remainder is the yin-calendar eclipse fixed parts. If subtraction is impossible, the sun does not eclipse.
34
各如定法而一為大分，不盡，退除為小分。 小分半已上為半強，已下為半弱。 命大分以十為限，即得日食之分。
In each case divide by the fixed factor for great parts; divide the remainder down for small parts. Small parts at half or above count as half-strong; below half as half-weak. Name the great parts with ten as the limit to obtain the solar eclipse parts.
35
求日食泛用分：置日食定分，退二位，列於上，在陽曆列九十八於下，在陰曆列一百五十八於下，各相減相乘，陽以二百五十而一，陰以六百五十而一，各為日食泛用分。
To find the solar eclipse general usable parts: set the solar eclipse fixed parts, retreat two places above; place 98 below in the yang calendar and 158 in the yin calendar; subtract and multiply; divide by 250 for yang and 650 for yin to obtain the solar eclipse general usable parts.
36
求月入食限交前後分：置望月行入陰陽曆日及餘秒，如後限已下為交後分。 前限已上覆減交中日，餘為交前分。
To find the moon’s entry into the eclipse limit before and after convergence parts: set the full-moon day of lunar motion in yin and yang calendars and remainder seconds; at the later limit or below is the after-convergence part. If at the earlier limit or above, subtract from convergence middle day; the remainder is the before-convergence part.
37
求月食分：置交前後分，如三千七百已下，為食既； 已上，覆減一萬一千七百，不足減者為不食。
To find the lunar eclipse parts: set the before- and after-convergence parts; at 3,700 or below indicates totality; if above, subtract from 11,700; if subtraction fails, there is no eclipse.
38
餘以八百而一為大分，不盡，退除為小分。 小分半已上為半強，已下為半弱。 命大分以十為限，即得月食之分。
Divide the remainder by 800 for great parts; divide the remainder down for small parts. Small parts at half or above count as half-strong; below half as half-weak. Name the great parts with ten as the limit to obtain the lunar eclipse parts.
39
求月食泛用分：置望交前、後分，自相乘，退二位，交初以一千一百三十八而一，用減一千二百三，交中以一千二百六十四而一，用減一千八十三，各為月食泛用分。
To find the lunar eclipse general usable parts: set the full-moon before- and after-convergence parts, square and retreat two places; at convergence initial divide by 1,138 and subtract from 1,203; at convergence middle divide by 1,264 and subtract from 1,083, yielding the lunar eclipse general usable parts.
40
求日月食定用分：置日月食泛用分，以一千三百三十七乘之，以定朔、望入轉算外轉定分而一，所得，為日月食定用分。
To find the fixed usable parts of solar and lunar eclipse: set the general usable parts, multiply by 1,337, divide by the fixed rotation parts outside the reckoning for the fixed new or full moon, and obtain the fixed usable parts.
41
求日月食虧初復滿小餘：置日月食甚小餘，以定用分減之，為虧初； 加之，為復滿：即各得所求小餘。 若求辰刻，依食甚術入之。
To find the small remainders of first loss and final fullness: set the eclipse greatest small remainder; subtract the fixed usable parts for first loss; add the fixed usable parts for final fullness; each yields the sought small remainder. If hours and quarters are sought, apply the greatest-eclipse procedure.
42
求月食更籌法：置望辰分，四因，退位，為更法； 五除之，為籌法。
To find the lunar eclipse watch and tally method: set the full-moon hour parts, multiply by 4, and retreat one place for the watch method; divide by 5 for the tally method.
43
求月食入更籌：置虧初、食甚、復滿小餘，在晨分已下加晨分，昏分已上減去昏分，皆以更法除之為更數，不盡，以籌法除之為籌數。 其更、籌數命初更，算外，即各得所入更、籌。
To find lunar eclipse entry into watch and tally: set the first loss, greatest eclipse, and final fullness small remainders; add dawn parts if at or below dawn parts, subtract dusk parts if at or above dusk parts; divide by the watch method for watches and by the tally method for tallies on the remainder. Name watch and tally counts from the first watch, counting outside the reckoning, to obtain the watch and tally entered.
44
求日月食甚宿次：置朔、望之日晨前夜半黃道日度及分，以統法約日月食甚小餘，加之，內月食更加半周天，各依宿次去之，即日月食甚所在宿次。
To find the lodge of greatest solar and lunar eclipse: set the ecliptic solar degree and parts at dawn before midnight on the new- or full-moon day, add the reduced eclipse greatest small remainder, add half the circuit of heaven for lunar eclipse, and remove by lodge to obtain the lodge of greatest eclipse.
45
求月食既內外刻分：置月食交前、後分，覆減三千七百，如不足減者，為食不既。
To find inner and outer quarter-parts of lunar totality: set the lunar eclipse before- and after-convergence parts and subtract from 3,700; if subtraction fails, the eclipse is not total.
46
退二位，列於上，下列七十四，相減相乘，進位，如三十七而一，所得以定用分乘之，如泛用分而一，為既內分； 以減定用分，餘為既外分。
Retreat two places above, place 74 below, subtract and multiply, carry, divide by 37, multiply by fixed usable parts and divide by general usable parts for inner totality parts; subtract from fixed usable parts; the remainder is outer totality parts.
47
求日月帶食出入所見之分：各以食甚小餘與日出、入分相減，餘為帶食差。 其帶食差在定用分已上，為不帶食出入。
To find the parts seen when eclipse accompanies sunrise or sunset: subtract sunrise or sunset parts from the eclipse greatest small remainder; the remainder is the accompanying-eclipse difference. If the accompanying-eclipse difference equals or exceeds the fixed usable parts, there is no accompanying eclipse at exit or entry.
48
以乘所食之分，滿定用分而一，若月食既者，以既內分減帶食差，餘乘所食之分，如既外分而一，所得，以減既分，如不足減者，為帶食既出入。
Multiply by the parts eclipsed and divide by fixed usable parts; for total lunar eclipse subtract inner totality parts from the accompanying difference, multiply the remainder by parts eclipsed and divide by outer totality parts, subtract from totality parts, or if impossible, it is accompanying total eclipse at exit and entry.
49
以減所食之分，餘為帶食出、入所見之分。
Subtract from the parts eclipsed; the remainder is the parts seen at accompanying exit and entry.

求日食所起：日在陽曆，初起西南，甚于正南，復滿東南； 日在陰曆，初起西北，甚於正北，復滿東北。 其食八分已上者，皆起正西，復滿正東。 此據午地而論之，當審黃道斜正可知。
To find where the solar eclipse begins: with the sun in the yang calendar, it first appears in the southwest, reaches maximum due south, and ends in the southeast; with the sun in the yin calendar, it first appears in the northwest, reaches maximum due north, and ends in the northeast. If the eclipse is eight-tenths or greater, it begins due west and ends due east. This is discussed for the noon meridian; the ecliptic’s obliquity and orientation must be taken into account.
51
求月食所起：月在陽曆，初起東北，甚於正北，復滿西北； 月在陰曆，初起東南，甚于正南，復滿西南。 其食八分已上者，皆起正東，復滿正西。 此據午地而論之，當審黃道斜正可知。
To find where the lunar eclipse begins: with the moon in the yang calendar, it first appears in the northeast, reaches maximum due north, and ends in the northwest; with the moon in the yin calendar, it first appears in the southeast, reaches maximum due south, and ends in the southwest. If the eclipse is eight-tenths or greater, it begins due east and ends due west. This is discussed for the noon meridian; the ecliptic’s obliquity and orientation must be taken into account.

求五星天正冬至後平合中積中星：置天正冬至氣積分，各以其星周率去之，不盡，用減周率，余滿統法約之為度，不滿，退除為分秒，命之為平合中積。 因而重列之為平合中星，各以前段變日加平合中積，又以前段變度加平合中星，其經退行者即減之，各得五星諸變中積中星。
To find each planet's mean-conjunction central accumulation and central star after civil winter solstice: set the winter solstice qi accumulated parts, cast out each star's circuit rate, subtract the remainder from the circuit rate, convert the result through the unification factor into degrees and parts and seconds, and name it the mean-conjunction central accumulation. Arrange it again as the mean-conjunction central star; add each prior segment's change days to the central accumulation and change degrees to the central star, subtracting where retrograde motion applies, to obtain the central accumulation and central star for every planetary segment.
79
求五星入曆：各以其星歲差乘所求積年，滿周天分去之，不盡，以統法約之為度，不滿，退除為分秒，以減平合中星，為平合入曆度及分秒。 求諸變者，各以前段限度累加之，為五星諸變入曆度及分秒。
To find planetary entry into the calendar cycle: multiply each star's annual difference by the accumulated years sought, discard full circuit-of-heaven parts, convert the remainder through the unification factor to degrees and parts and seconds, subtract from the mean-conjunction central star, and obtain the mean-conjunction entry-into-calendar degree and parts and seconds. For the various changes, add each prior segment's limit degree cumulatively to obtain every planetary change's entry-into-calendar degree and parts and seconds.
80
求五星諸變盈縮定差：各置其星其變入曆度及分秒，如半周天已下為盈，已上去之為縮。 以五星曆策度除之為策數，不盡，為入策度及分秒。 以其策下損益率乘之，如曆策而一為分，分滿百為度，以損益其下盈縮積度，即五星諸段盈縮定差。
To find the fixed expansion-contraction difference for each change: set that star's change entry-into-calendar degree and parts and seconds; at half the circuit of heaven or below counts as expansion, above as contraction. Divide by the five-planet calendar-stride degree for the stride number; the remainder is entry-into-stride degree and parts and seconds. Multiply by the increase-decrease rate under that stride, divide by the calendar stride for parts, let parts filling 100 become degrees, and increase or decrease the expansion-contraction accumulated degree below to obtain each segment's fixed expansion-contraction difference.
81
求五星平合及諸變定積：各置其星其變中積，以其段盈縮定差盈加縮減之，即其段定積日及分。 以天正冬至大餘及約分加之，滿統法去之，不盡，命甲子，算外，即定日辰及分。
To find fixed accumulation for mean conjunction and every change: set each star's change central accumulation, add expansion and subtract contraction using that segment's fixed expansion-contraction difference, and obtain the segment's fixed accumulated days and parts. Add the civil winter solstice great remainder and approximate parts, discard full unification factors, name from jiazi counting outside the reckoning, and obtain the fixed day, hour, and parts.
82
求五星諸變入所在月日：各置其星其變定積，以天正閏日及約分加之，滿朔策及約分除之為月數，不盡，為入月已來日數。 命月數起天正十一月，算外，即其星其段入其月經朔日數及分。 乃以其朔日、辰相距，即所在月、日。
To find the month and day of each change: set that star's change fixed accumulation, add the civil new-year intercalary day and approximate parts, divide by the new-moon stride and approximate parts for months, and treat the remainder as days elapsed within the month. Name months from the civil eleventh month, counting outside the reckoning, to obtain that star's segment entry into that month's mean new-moon day count and parts. Then take the interval of new-moon day and hour to obtain the month and day where it lies.
83
求五星平合及諸變加時定星：各置其星其變中星，以盈縮定差盈加縮減之，內金倍之，水三之，然後加減，即五星諸段定星。 以天正冬至加時黃道日度加時命之，即其星其段加時所在宿度及分秒。 五星皆因留為後段初日定星，餘依術算。
To find the hour-fixed star for mean conjunction and every change: set each star's change central star, add expansion and subtract contraction with the fixed difference, double for Venus and triple for Mercury before adjusting, and obtain the fixed star for every segment. Add and name from the civil winter solstice hour ecliptic solar degree to obtain that star's segment hour-fixed lodge degree and parts and seconds. All five planets use station as the next segment's first-day fixed star; the remainder follows the procedure reckoning.
84
求五星諸變初日晨前夜半定星：各以其段初行率乘其段加時分，百約之，以順減退加其日加時定星，即為其星其段初日晨前夜半定星。 加命如前，即得所求。
To find the dawn-before-midnight fixed star on the first day of each change: multiply that segment's initial motion rate by its hour parts, reduce by 100, subtract for direct motion or add for retrograde to that day's hour-fixed star, and obtain the dawn-before-midnight fixed star. Add and name as before to obtain what is sought.
85
求諸變日率度率：各以其段日辰距至後段日辰為其段日率； 以其段夜半定星與後段夜半定星相減，余為其段度率。
To find day-rate and degree-rate for each change: take that segment's day-and-hour interval to the next segment as the day-rate; subtract that segment's midnight fixed star from the next segment's midnight fixed star; the remainder is the degree-rate.
86
求諸變平行分：各置其段度率，以其段日率除之，為其段平行度及分秒。
To find mean motion parts for each change: set the segment's degree-rate, divide by its day-rate, and obtain mean motion degree and parts and seconds.
87
求諸變總差：各以其段平行分與後段平行分相減，餘為泛差。 並前段泛差，四因，退一位，為總差。 若前段無平行分相減為泛差者，因後段初日行分與其段平行分相減，為半總差，倍之，為總差。 若後段無平行分相減為泛差者，因前段末日行分與其段平行分相減，為半總差，倍之，為總差。 其在再行者，以本段平行分十四乘之，十五而一，為總差。 內金星依順段術求之。
To find the total difference for each change: subtract the next segment's mean motion parts from that segment's mean motion parts; the remainder is the general difference. Combine with the prior segment's general difference, multiply by 4, and retreat one place for the total difference. If the prior segment lacks mean motion parts for subtraction, subtract the next segment's first-day motion parts from that segment's mean motion parts for half the total difference and double it. If the next segment lacks mean motion parts for subtraction, subtract that segment's mean motion parts from the prior segment's last-day motion parts for half the total difference and double it. For repeated motion, multiply that segment's mean motion parts by 14 and divide by 15 for the total difference. For Venus, apply the procedure for direct segments.
88
求初末日行分：各半其段總差，加減其段平行分，後行分少，加之為初，減之為末； 後行分多，減之為初，加之為末。 退行者，前段減之為初，加之為末； 後段加之為初，減之為末。
To find first- and last-day motion parts: halve the segment's total difference and add or subtract to mean motion parts; if later motion parts are fewer, add for the first and subtract for the last; if later motion parts are more, subtract for the first and add for the last. For retrograde motion, the prior segment subtracts for the first and adds for the last; the next segment adds for the first and subtracts for the last.
89
為其星其段初、末日行分。
That yields that star's segment first- and last-day motion parts.
90
求每日晨前夜半星行宿次：置其段總差，減日率一以除之，為日差； 累損益初日行分，後行分少，損之； 後行分多，益之。
To find each day's dawn-before-midnight star lodge: set the segment's total difference, subtract 1 from the day-rate and divide for the day difference; accumulate to adjust the first-day motion parts; if later motion parts are fewer, decrease; if later motion parts are more, increase.
91
為每日行度及分秒； 乃順加退減其星其段初日晨前夜半定星，命之，即每日夜半星行所在宿次。
That yields daily motion degree and parts and seconds; then add for direct motion and subtract for retrograde from that star's segment first-day dawn-before-midnight fixed star and name it to obtain the lodge at each day's midnight.
92
徑求其日宿次：置所求日，減一，半之，以日差乘而加減初日行分，後行分少，減之； 後行分多，加之算。
To find that day's lodge directly: set the sought day, subtract 1 and halve, multiply by the day difference and add or subtract to first-day motion parts; if later motion parts are fewer, subtract; if later motion parts are more, add in the reckoning.
93
以所求日乘之，為積度； 以順加退減其星其段初日夜半宿次，即所求日夜半宿次。
Multiply by the sought day for the accumulated degree; add for direct motion and subtract for retrograde from that star's segment first-day midnight lodge to obtain the sought day's midnight lodge.
94
求五星合見伏行差：木、火、土三星，以其段初日星行分減太陽行分，為行差。 金、水二星順行者，以其段初日太陽行分減星行分，為行差。 金、水二星退行者，以其段初日星行分並太陽行分，為行差。 內水星夕伏、晨見，直以太陽行分為行差。
To find motion difference for conjunction, appearance, and hiding: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn, subtract the sun's motion parts from that segment's first-day star motion parts. For Venus and Mercury in direct motion, subtract the star's motion parts from that segment's first-day sun motion parts for the motion difference. For Venus and Mercury in retrograde motion, combine that segment's first-day star motion parts and sun motion parts for the motion difference. For Mercury at evening hiding and morning appearance, use the sun's motion parts directly as the motion difference.
95
求五星定合見伏泛用積：木、火、土三星，各以平合晨疾、夕伏定積，便為定合見、伏泛用積。 金、水二星各置其段盈縮定差，內水星倍之，以其段行差除之為日，不滿，退除為分，在平合夕見、晨伏者，盈減縮加定積，為定合見、伏泛用積； 在退合夕伏、晨見者，盈加縮減定積，為定合見、伏泛用積。
To find general usable accumulation for fixed conjunction, appearance, and hiding: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn, the mean-conjunction morning swift and evening hiding fixed accumulation directly serves as the general usable accumulation. For Venus and Mercury, set that segment's fixed expansion-contraction difference, double for Mercury, divide by the segment's motion difference for days and reduce the remainder to parts; at mean-conjunction evening appearance and morning hiding, subtract expansion and add contraction to the fixed accumulation for the general usable accumulation; at retrograde conjunction evening hiding and morning appearance, add expansion and subtract contraction to the fixed accumulation for the general usable accumulation.
96
求五星定合積定星：木、火、土三星，以平合行差除其日盈縮分，為距合差日。 以盈縮分減之，為距合差度。 以差日、差度盈減縮加其星定合泛用積，為其星定合定積、定星。 金、水二星順合者，以平合行差除其日盈縮分，為距合差日。 以盈縮分加之，為距合差度； 以差日、差度盈加縮積其星定合泛用積，為其星定合定積、定星。 金、水二星退合者，以平合行差除其日盈縮分，為距合差日； 以減盈縮減之分，為距合差度； 以差日盈減縮加，以差度盈加縮減再定合泛用積，為其星再定合定積、定星。 各以天正冬至大餘及約分加定積，滿統法去之，命甲子，算外，即得定合日辰。 以天正冬至加時黃道日度加定星，依宿次去之，即得定合所在宿次。
To find fixed conjunction accumulation and fixed star: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn, divide the day's expansion-contraction parts by the mean-conjunction motion difference for the interval-conjunction difference day. Subtract the expansion-contraction parts for the interval-conjunction difference degree. Apply the difference day and difference degree, subtracting expansion and adding contraction to that star's fixed-conjunction general usable accumulation, for fixed accumulation and fixed star. For Venus and Mercury in direct conjunction, divide the day's expansion-contraction parts by the mean-conjunction motion difference for the interval-conjunction difference day. Add the expansion-contraction parts for the interval-conjunction difference degree; apply the difference day and difference degree, adding expansion and subtracting contraction from that star's fixed-conjunction general usable accumulation, for fixed accumulation and fixed star. For Venus and Mercury in retrograde conjunction, divide the day's expansion-contraction parts by the mean-conjunction motion difference for the interval-conjunction difference day; subtract the parts used to reduce expansion-contraction for the interval-conjunction difference degree; with the difference day subtract expansion and add contraction, with the difference degree add expansion and subtract contraction to the second fixed-conjunction general usable accumulation, for second fixed accumulation and fixed star. In each case add the civil winter solstice great remainder and approximate parts to the fixed accumulation, discard full unification factors, name from jiazi counting outside the reckoning, and obtain the fixed-conjunction day and hour. Add the fixed star to the civil winter solstice hour ecliptic solar degree and remove by lodge to obtain the fixed-conjunction lodge.
97
求五星定見伏定積：木、火、土三星以泛用積晨加、夕減一象，如半周天已下自相乘，已上，覆減一周天，餘亦自相乘，七十五而一，所得，以其星伏見度乘之，十五而一為差，如其段行差除之為日，不滿，退除為分，見加伏減泛用積，為其星定見、伏定積。 金、水二星以行差除其日盈縮分為日，在夕見、晨伏，盈加縮減泛用積，為常用積； 夕伏、晨見，盈減縮加泛用積，為常用積； 如常用積在半周天已下為冬至後； 已上去之，餘為夏至後。 各在一象已下自相乘，已上，覆減一周天，餘亦自相乘，冬至後晨、夏至後夕，以十八而一； 冬至後夕、夏至後晨，以七十五而一，所得，以其星伏見度乘之，十五而一為差，如其段行差除之為日，不滿，退除為分，冬至後晨見、夕伏，夏至後夕見、晨伏，以加常用積，為其星定見、伏定積； 冬至後夕見、晨伏，夏至後晨見、夕伏，以減常用積，為其星定見、伏定積。 加命如前，即得定見、伏日辰。
To find fixed appearance-and-hiding accumulation: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn, add at dawn and subtract at dusk by one image from the general usable accumulation; square values at or below half the circuit of heaven, overlay-subtract one circuit and square the remainder if above, divide by 75, multiply by heliacal appearance-and-hiding degree and divide by 15 for the difference, convert by motion difference to days and parts, add for appearance and subtract for hiding from the general usable accumulation. For Venus and Mercury, divide the day's expansion-contraction parts by the motion difference for days; at evening appearance and morning hiding, add expansion and subtract contraction from the general usable accumulation for the usual accumulation; at evening hiding and morning appearance, subtract expansion and add contraction from the general usable accumulation for the usual accumulation; if the usual accumulation is at half the circuit of heaven or below, it is after the winter solstice; if above, remove it; the remainder is after the summer solstice. Square values at one image or below, overlay-subtract one circuit and square the remainder if above; after winter solstice at dawn and after summer solstice at dusk, divide by 18; after winter solstice at dusk and after summer solstice at dawn, divide by 75, multiply by heliacal appearance-and-hiding degree and divide by 15, convert by motion difference to days and parts; add to the usual accumulation for winter-solstice dawn appearance and evening hiding and summer-solstice evening appearance and morning hiding; subtract from the usual accumulation for winter-solstice evening appearance and morning hiding and summer-solstice morning appearance and evening hiding. Add and name as before to obtain the fixed appearance-and-hiding day and hour.
