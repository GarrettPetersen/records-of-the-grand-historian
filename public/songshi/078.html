求天正十一月經朔加時入交泛日：置天正十一月經朔加時積分，以交終分及秒去之，不盡，滿統法為日，不滿為餘秒，即天正十一月經朔加時入交泛日及餘秒。
To find the eleventh civil month's mean new-moon hour of entry into general convergence: set the accumulated parts for that syzygy's hour, cast out full convergence termination parts and seconds, convert the remainder through the common divisor into days and remainder-seconds, and obtain the general convergence day and remainder-seconds for the eleventh month's mean new moon.
20
求次朔及望加時入交泛日：置天正經朔加時入交泛日及餘秒，求次朔，以朔差加之。 求望，以望策加之，滿交終日及餘秒去之。 即次朔及望加時入交泛日及餘秒。 若以經朔小餘減之，餘為夜半入交泛日。
To find the next new and full moon hour entry into general convergence: set the eleventh-month mean new-moon hour general convergence day and remainder-seconds; for the next new moon, add the new-moon difference. For the full moon, add the full-moon stride and cast out full convergence termination days and remainder-seconds. That yields each next new or full moon hour general convergence day and remainder-seconds. Subtracting the mean new moon's minor remainder yields the midnight general convergence day.
21
求定朔望夜半入交泛日：置經朔、望夜半入交泛日，若定朔、望大餘有進退者，亦進退交日，否則因經為定，即定朔望夜半入交泛日及餘秒。
To find fixed syzygy midnight general convergence entry: start from mean midnight general convergence day; if the fixed syzygy's major remainder advances or retreats, adjust the convergence day likewise, otherwise take the mean value as fixed, and obtain the fixed syzygy midnight general convergence day and remainder-seconds.
22
求次朔夜半入交泛日：置定朔夜半入交泛日及餘秒，大月加二日，小月加一日，餘皆加九千四百七十八、秒五十六，求次日，累加一日，滿交終日及餘秒去之，即次定朔及每日夜半入交泛日及餘秒。
For the next fixed new-moon midnight entry: from each fixed new-moon midnight general convergence day and remainder-seconds, add two days in a long month and one in a short month, always add 9,478 seconds 56 to the remainder, advance day by day adding one day cumulatively, cast out full convergence termination days and remainder-seconds, and obtain each next fixed new moon and every midnight general convergence day and remainder-seconds.
23
求朔望加時入交常日：置經朔、望入交泛日及餘秒，以其朔、望入盈縮限朏朒定數朏減朒加之，即朔、望加時入交常日及餘秒。
To find syzygy hour general convergence day: set mean syzygy general convergence day and remainder-seconds, apply entry-into-excess-deficit-limit tiao–chuo fixed number (tiao subtract, chuo add), and obtain syzygy hour general convergence day and remainder-seconds.
24
求朔望加時入交定日：置其朔、望入轉朏朒定數，以交率乘之，交數而一，所得，以朏減朒加入交常日及余秒，滿與不足，進退其日，即朔、望加時入交定日及餘秒。
To find syzygy hour fixed convergence entry: set entry-into-rotation tiao–chuo fixed number, multiply by convergence ratio and divide by convergence number, apply tiao subtract and chuo add to general convergence day and remainder-seconds, adjust the day as needed, and obtain syzygy hour fixed convergence day and remainder-seconds.
25
求月行入陰陽曆：置其朔、望入交定日及餘秒，在交中已下為月行陽曆； 已上去之，餘為月行陰曆。
To find the moon's yang or yin calendar: set that syzygy's fixed convergence day and remainder-seconds; at or below convergence middle counts as the moon's yang calendar; if above, subtract convergence middle; the remainder is the moon's yin calendar.
26
求朔望加時月行入陰陽曆積度：置月行入陰陽曆日及餘秒，以統法通日，內餘，九而一為分，分滿百為度，即朔望加時月行入陰陽曆積度及分。
To find syzygy hour yin- or yang-calendar accumulated degrees: convert the entered calendar day through the common divisor with the remainder, divide by nine for parts, convert parts filling 100 to degrees, and obtain the hour moon's yin- or yang-calendar accumulated degrees and parts.
27
求朔望加時月去黃道度：置入陰陽曆積度及分，如交象已下為入少象； 已上，覆減交中度，餘為入老象。 皆列於上，下列交中度，相減相乘，進位，如一百三十八而一，為泛差。 又視入老、少象度，如半交象已下為初； 已上去之，餘為末。 皆二因，退位，初減末加泛差，滿百為度，即朔、望加時月去黃道度及分。
To find syzygy hour lunar ecliptic latitude: set entered yin- or yang-calendar accumulated degrees and parts; at or below the convergence image counts as entry into the young image; if above, cover and subtract convergence middle; the remainder is entry into the old image. Array the upper value above and convergence middle below, subtract and multiply with carry, divide by 138, and obtain the general difference. Again inspect entered old or young image degrees; at or below half the convergence image counts as initial; if above, subtract half the convergence image; the remainder is final. Double each value and shift one place; subtract the general difference for initial and add for final, convert parts filling 100 to degrees, and obtain syzygy hour lunar ecliptic latitude in degrees and parts.
28
求日月食甚定餘：置定朔小餘，如半統法已下，與半統法相減相乘，如三萬六千九十而一為時差，以減。 如半統法已上減去半統法，餘亦與半統法相減相乘，如一萬八千四十五而一為時差，午前以減，午後以加，皆加、減定朔小餘，為日食甚小餘。 與半法相減，餘為午前、後分。 其月食者，以定望小餘為月食甚小餘。
To find eclipse-greatest fixed remainder: set the fixed new-moon minor remainder; if at or below half the common divisor, subtract and multiply against half the common divisor, divide by 36,090 for the time difference, and subtract. If at or above half the common divisor, subtract half the common divisor, then subtract and multiply the remainder against half the common divisor, divide by 18,045 for the time difference, subtract before noon and add after noon, and apply to the fixed new-moon minor remainder to obtain the solar eclipse-greatest minor remainder. Subtract the half method to obtain the before- and after-noon fraction. For lunar eclipse, use the fixed full-moon minor remainder as the lunar eclipse-greatest minor remainder.
29
求日月食甚辰刻：各置食甚小餘，倍之，以辰法除之為辰數，不滿，五因，滿刻法而一為刻，不滿為分。 其辰數命子正，算外，即食甚辰刻及分。 若加半辰，即命起子初。
To find eclipse-greatest watch and quarter: double the greatest minor remainder, divide by the watch divisor for watches, convert the remainder to quarters (multiply by five, divide by the quarter divisor), and obtain parts. Count watches from zi-zheng, outside the count, to obtain eclipse-greatest watch, quarter, and parts. If adding half a watch, count from zi-initial.
30
求氣差：置其朔盈、縮限度及分，自相乘，進二位，盈初、縮末一百九十七而一，盈末、縮初二百一十九而一，皆用減四千一十，為氣泛差。 以乘午前、後分，如半晝分而一，所得，以減泛差，為定差。 春分後，交初以減，交中以加； 秋分後，交初以加，交中以減。 如食在夜，反用之。
To find the qi difference: square that new moon's excess- or deficit-limit degree and parts and shift two places; divide by 197 for excess initial and deficit final or by 219 for excess final and deficit initial, subtract each from 4,010, and obtain the qi general difference. Multiply by the before- and after-noon fraction, divide by half the day-length fraction, subtract from the general difference, and obtain the fixed difference. After the spring equinox, subtract at convergence beginning and add at convergence middle; after the autumn equinox, add at convergence beginning and subtract at convergence middle. If the eclipse occurs at night, reverse the operation.
31
求刻差：置其朔盈、縮限度及分，與半周天相減相乘，進二位，二百九而一，為刻泛差。 以乘午前、後分，如三千七百半而一，為定差。 冬至後午前、夏至後午後，交初以加，交中以減。 冬至後午後、夏至後午前，交初以減，交中以加。
To find the quarter difference: subtract and multiply that new moon's excess- or deficit-limit degree and parts against half the circumpolar circuit, shift two places, divide by 209, and obtain the quarter general difference. Multiply by the before- and after-noon fraction, divide by 3,700½, and obtain the fixed difference. After winter solstice before noon and after summer solstice after noon, add at convergence beginning and subtract at convergence middle. After winter solstice after noon and after summer solstice before noon, subtract at convergence beginning and add at convergence middle.
32
求日入食限交前後分：置朔入交定日及餘秒，以氣、刻、時三差各加減之，如交中日已下為不食； 已上去之，如後限已下為交後分； 前限已上覆減交中日，餘為交前分。
To find the sun's eclipse-limit anterior and posterior parts: set the new moon's fixed convergence day and remainder-seconds, apply the qi, quarter, and time differences, and if at or below convergence middle there is no eclipse; if above, subtract convergence middle; if at or below the posterior limit, it is the posterior convergence part; if at or above the anterior limit, cover and subtract convergence middle; the remainder is the anterior convergence part.
33
求日食分：置交前後分，如陽曆食限已下為陽曆食定分； 已上，用減一萬二千八百，餘為陰曆食定分。 如不足減者，日不食。
To find the solar eclipse fraction: set anterior and posterior convergence parts; if at or below the yang-calendar eclipse limit, that is the yang-calendar eclipse fixed part; if above, subtract 12,800; the remainder is the yin-calendar eclipse fixed part. If the subtraction cannot be completed, there is no solar eclipse.
34
各如定法而一為大分，不盡，退除為小分。 小分半已上為半強，已下為半弱。 命大分以十為限，即得日食之分。
In each case divide by the fixing divisor for the major part and reduce the remainder to the minor part. Minor parts of half or more count as half-strong; below half count as half-weak. Take ten as the limit for the major part to obtain the solar eclipse fraction.
35
求日食泛用分：置日食定分，退二位，列於上，在陽曆列九十八於下，在陰曆列一百五十八於下，各相減相乘，陽以二百五十而一，陰以六百五十而一，各為日食泛用分。
To find the solar eclipse general-use part: set the fixed part and shift two places, array above with 98 below for yang calendar or 158 below for yin calendar, subtract and multiply, divide by 250 for yang or 650 for yin, and obtain the solar eclipse general-use part.
36
求月入食限交前後分：置望月行入陰陽曆日及餘秒，如後限已下為交後分。 前限已上覆減交中日，餘為交前分。
To find the moon's eclipse-limit anterior and posterior parts: set the full moon's entered yin- or yang-calendar day and remainder-seconds; if at or below the posterior limit, it is the posterior convergence part. If at or above the anterior limit, cover and subtract convergence middle; the remainder is the anterior convergence part.
37
求月食分：置交前後分，如三千七百已下，為食既； 已上，覆減一萬一千七百，不足減者為不食。
To find the lunar eclipse fraction: set anterior and posterior convergence parts; if at or below 3,700, the eclipse is total; if above, cover and subtract 11,700; if the subtraction cannot be completed, there is no lunar eclipse.
38
餘以八百而一為大分，不盡，退除為小分。 小分半已上為半強，已下為半弱。 命大分以十為限，即得月食之分。
Divide the remainder by 800 for the major part and reduce the remainder to the minor part. Minor parts of half or more count as half-strong; below half count as half-weak. Take ten as the limit for the major part to obtain the lunar eclipse fraction.
39
求月食泛用分：置望交前、後分，自相乘，退二位，交初以一千一百三十八而一，用減一千二百三，交中以一千二百六十四而一，用減一千八十三，各為月食泛用分。
To find the lunar eclipse general-use part: square the full moon's anterior and posterior convergence parts and shift two places; at convergence beginning divide by 1,138 and subtract 1,203, at convergence middle divide by 1,264 and subtract 1,083, and obtain the lunar eclipse general-use part.
40
求日月食定用分：置日月食泛用分，以一千三百三十七乘之，以定朔、望入轉算外轉定分而一，所得，為日月食定用分。
To find the eclipse fixed-use part: multiply the general-use part by 1,337, divide by the fixed syzygy entry-into-rotation count-outside rotation fixed part, and obtain the eclipse fixed-use part.
41
求日月食虧初復滿小餘：置日月食甚小餘，以定用分減之，為虧初； 加之，為復滿：即各得所求小餘。 若求辰刻，依食甚術入之。
To find eclipse first contact and full restoration minor remainders: set the eclipse-greatest minor remainder, subtract the fixed-use part for first contact, and add it for full restoration; each yields the sought minor remainder. To find watch and quarter, follow the eclipse-greatest procedure.
42
求月食更籌法：置望辰分，四因，退位，為更法； 五除之，為籌法。
To find the lunar eclipse watch and tally divisors: set the full-moon watch parts, multiply by four and shift one place for the watch divisor; divide by five to obtain the tally divisor.
43
求月食入更籌：置虧初、食甚、復滿小餘，在晨分已下加晨分，昏分已上減去昏分，皆以更法除之為更數，不盡，以籌法除之為籌數。 其更、籌數命初更，算外，即各得所入更、籌。
To find lunar eclipse entry into watch and tally: set first contact, greatest, and full restoration minor remainders, adjust for dawn or dusk parts as needed, divide by the watch divisor for watches and by the tally divisor for the tally count. Count watches from the first watch, outside the count, to obtain each entered watch and tally.
44
求日月食甚宿次：置朔、望之日晨前夜半黃道日度及分，以統法約日月食甚小餘，加之，內月食更加半周天，各依宿次去之，即日月食甚所在宿次。
To find eclipse-greatest lodge position: set the syzygy day's before-dawn midnight ecliptic solar degree and parts, add the reduced eclipse-greatest minor remainder (and for lunar eclipse add half the circumpolar circuit), remove by lodge sequence, and obtain the lodge of greatest eclipse.
45
求月食既內外刻分：置月食交前、後分，覆減三千七百，如不足減者，為食不既。
To find lunar eclipse total interior and exterior quarter parts: set anterior and posterior convergence parts, cover and subtract 3,700; if the subtraction cannot be completed, the eclipse is not total.
46
退二位，列於上，下列七十四，相減相乘，進位，如三十七而一，所得以定用分乘之，如泛用分而一，為既內分； 以減定用分，餘為既外分。
Shift two places, array above with 74 below, subtract and multiply with carry, divide by 37, multiply by fixed-use part and divide by general-use part, and obtain the total-interior part; subtract from the fixed-use part; the remainder is the total-exterior part.
47
求日月帶食出入所見之分：各以食甚小餘與日出、入分相減，餘為帶食差。 其帶食差在定用分已上，為不帶食出入。
To find the visible fraction of a grazing eclipse at rising or setting: subtract the sunrise or sunset part from the eclipse-greatest minor remainder; the remainder is the grazing-eclipse difference. If the grazing-eclipse difference is at or above the fixed-use part, there is no grazing eclipse at rising or setting.
48
以乘所食之分，滿定用分而一，若月食既者，以既內分減帶食差，餘乘所食之分，如既外分而一，所得，以減既分，如不足減者，為帶食既出入。
Multiply by the eclipsed fraction and divide by the fixed-use part; for a total lunar eclipse, subtract the total-interior part from the grazing difference, multiply the remainder by the eclipsed fraction and divide by the total-exterior part, subtract from the total fraction, and if insufficient to subtract, it is a grazing total eclipse at rising or setting.
49
以減所食之分，餘為帶食出、入所見之分。
Subtract from the eclipsed fraction; the remainder is the visible fraction of the grazing eclipse at rising or setting.

求日食所起：日在陽曆，初起西南，甚于正南，復滿東南； 日在陰曆，初起西北，甚於正北，復滿東北。 其食八分已上者，皆起正西，復滿正東。 此據午地而論之，當審黃道斜正可知。
To find where the solar eclipse begins: when the sun is in the yang calendar, first contact is in the southwest, greatest eclipse due south, and full restoration in the southeast; when the sun is in the yin calendar, first contact is in the northwest, greatest eclipse due north, and full restoration in the northeast. For eclipses of eight-tenths or more, first contact is always due west and full restoration due east. This is stated with reference to the meridian; the obliquity of the ecliptic must be taken into account.
51
求月食所起：月在陽曆，初起東北，甚於正北，復滿西北； 月在陰曆，初起東南，甚于正南，復滿西南。 其食八分已上者，皆起正東，復滿正西。 此據午地而論之，當審黃道斜正可知。
To find where the lunar eclipse begins: when the moon is in the yang calendar, first contact is in the northeast, greatest eclipse due north, and full restoration in the northwest; when the moon is in the yin calendar, first contact is in the southeast, greatest eclipse due south, and full restoration in the southwest. For eclipses of eight-tenths or more, first contact is always due east and full restoration due west. This is stated with reference to the meridian; the obliquity of the ecliptic must be taken into account.

求五星天正冬至後平合中積中星：置天正冬至氣積分，各以其星周率去之，不盡，用減周率，余滿統法約之為度，不滿，退除為分秒，命之為平合中積。 因而重列之為平合中星，各以前段變日加平合中積，又以前段變度加平合中星，其經退行者即減之，各得五星諸變中積中星。
To find each planet's mean-conjunction central accumulation and central star after civil new-year winter solstice: set winter solstice qi accumulated parts, cast out full circuit rates for each star, subtract the remainder from the circuit rate, convert through the common divisor to degrees and parts and seconds, and name the result the mean-conjunction central accumulation. Array it again as the mean-conjunction central star; for each segment add prior change days to central accumulation and prior change degrees to central star, subtract for retrograde motion, and obtain every planet's segment central accumulation and central star.
79
求五星入曆：各以其星歲差乘所求積年，滿周天分去之，不盡，以統法約之為度，不滿，退除為分秒，以減平合中星，為平合入曆度及分秒。 求諸變者，各以前段限度累加之，為五星諸變入曆度及分秒。
To find entry into the calendar cycle: multiply each star's annual precession by the accumulated years sought, cast out full circumpolar circuit parts, convert the remainder to degrees and parts and seconds, subtract from the mean-conjunction central star, and obtain mean-conjunction entry-into-calendar degree and parts and seconds. For each segment, add prior limit degrees cumulatively to obtain every planet's segment entry-into-calendar degree and parts and seconds.
80
求五星諸變盈縮定差：各置其星其變入曆度及分秒，如半周天已下為盈，已上去之為縮。 以五星曆策度除之為策數，不盡，為入策度及分秒。 以其策下損益率乘之，如曆策而一為分，分滿百為度，以損益其下盈縮積度，即五星諸段盈縮定差。
To find each segment's excess-and-deficit fixed difference: set each star and segment's entry-into-calendar degree and parts and seconds; at or below half the circumpolar circuit counts as excess, above as deficit. Divide by the five-planet calendar stride degree to obtain the stride count; the remainder is entry-into-stride degree and parts and seconds. Multiply the tabulated loss-and-gain rate for that stride, divide by the calendar stride for parts, convert parts filling 100 to degrees, apply to the underlying excess-and-deficit accumulation, and obtain each segment's excess-and-deficit fixed difference.
81
求五星平合及諸變定積：各置其星其變中積，以其段盈縮定差盈加縮減之，即其段定積日及分。 以天正冬至大餘及約分加之，滿統法去之，不盡，命甲子，算外，即定日辰及分。
To find mean conjunction and each segment's fixed accumulation: set each star and segment's central accumulation, add or subtract the segment's excess-and-deficit fixed difference according to excess or deficit, and obtain the segment's fixed-accumulation days and parts. Add civil new-year winter solstice greater remainder and approximate parts, cast out full common divisors, name from jiazi outside the count, and obtain the fixed day, double-hour, and parts.
82
求五星諸變入所在月日：各置其星其變定積，以天正閏日及約分加之，滿朔策及約分除之為月數，不盡，為入月已來日數。 命月數起天正十一月，算外，即其星其段入其月經朔日數及分。 乃以其朔日、辰相距，即所在月、日。
To find the month and day for each planet's segments: set each segment's fixed accumulation, add civil new-year intercalary days and approximate parts, divide by the new-moon policy and approximate parts for month count, and take the remainder as days elapsed within the month. Name the month count from civil new-year month eleven outside the count to obtain the segment's days and parts since mean new moon. Then take the interval of new-moon day and double-hour as the month and day where it falls.
83
求五星平合及諸變加時定星：各置其星其變中星，以盈縮定差盈加縮減之，內金倍之，水三之，然後加減，即五星諸段定星。 以天正冬至加時黃道日度加時命之，即其星其段加時所在宿度及分秒。 五星皆因留為後段初日定星，餘依術算。
To find mean conjunction and each segment's hour-of-addition fixed star: set each star and segment's central star, apply the excess-and-deficit fixed difference (double for Venus, triple for Mercury), then add or subtract according to excess or deficit, and obtain each segment's fixed star. Add to the winter solstice ecliptic solar degree at hour-of-addition, name the lodges, and obtain that star and segment's hour-of-addition lodge degree, parts, and seconds. For all five planets, the station fixes the first-day star of the posterior segment; compute the remainder by the procedure.
84
求五星諸變初日晨前夜半定星：各以其段初行率乘其段加時分，百約之，以順減退加其日加時定星，即為其星其段初日晨前夜半定星。 加命如前，即得所求。
To find each segment's first-day before-dawn midnight fixed star: multiply the segment's initial motion rate by its hour-of-addition parts and hundred-reduce, then subtract for direct motion or add for retrograde from that day's hour-of-addition fixed star to obtain the segment's first-day before-dawn midnight fixed star. Add and name as before to obtain the result.
85
求諸變日率度率：各以其段日辰距至後段日辰為其段日率； 以其段夜半定星與後段夜半定星相減，余為其段度率。
To find each segment's day rate and degree rate: for each take the day-and-double-hour interval from that segment to the next segment as the segment's day rate; subtract that segment's midnight fixed star from the next segment's midnight fixed star to obtain the segment's degree rate.
86
求諸變平行分：各置其段度率，以其段日率除之，為其段平行度及分秒。
To find each segment's parallel degree: set each segment's degree rate, divide by its day rate, and obtain the segment's parallel degree, parts, and seconds.
87
求諸變總差：各以其段平行分與後段平行分相減，餘為泛差。 並前段泛差，四因，退一位，為總差。 若前段無平行分相減為泛差者，因後段初日行分與其段平行分相減，為半總差，倍之，為總差。 若後段無平行分相減為泛差者，因前段末日行分與其段平行分相減，為半總差，倍之，為總差。 其在再行者，以本段平行分十四乘之，十五而一，為總差。 內金星依順段術求之。
To find each segment's total difference: subtract each segment's parallel parts from the next segment's parallel parts; the remainder is the general difference. Add the previous segment's general difference, multiply by four, and shift one decimal place to obtain the total difference. When the prior segment lacks parallel parts for subtraction, subtract that segment's parallel parts from the next segment's first-day motion parts; double the half-total difference to obtain the total difference. When the following segment lacks parallel parts for subtraction, subtract that segment's parallel parts from the previous segment's last-day motion parts; double the half-total difference to obtain the total difference. For second retrograde motion, multiply that segment's parallel parts by fourteen and divide by fifteen to obtain the total difference. For Venus, follow the direct-segment procedure.
88
求初末日行分：各半其段總差，加減其段平行分，後行分少，加之為初，減之為末； 後行分多，減之為初，加之為末。 退行者，前段減之為初，加之為末； 後段加之為初，減之為末。
To find first- and last-day motion parts: halve each segment's total difference and add or subtract to parallel parts; if posterior motion parts are fewer, add for the first day and subtract for the last; if posterior motion parts are more, subtract for the first day and add for the last. In retrograde motion, subtract for the first day and add for the last in the anterior segment; the posterior segment adds for the first day and subtracts for the last.
89
為其星其段初、末日行分。
That yields the star and segment's first- and last-day motion parts.
90
求每日晨前夜半星行宿次：置其段總差，減日率一以除之，為日差； 累損益初日行分，後行分少，損之； 後行分多，益之。
To find each day's before-dawn midnight star lodge position: set the segment's total difference, divide by day rate minus one, and obtain the day difference; adjust the first-day motion parts cumulatively; if posterior motion parts are fewer, reduce them; if posterior motion parts are more, increase them.
91
為每日行度及分秒； 乃順加退減其星其段初日晨前夜半定星，命之，即每日夜半星行所在宿次。
That yields each day's motion in degrees, parts, and seconds; then add for direct motion or subtract for retrograde from the segment's first-day before-dawn midnight fixed star, name the lodges, and obtain each midnight star lodge position.
92
徑求其日宿次：置所求日，減一，半之，以日差乘而加減初日行分，後行分少，減之； 後行分多，加之算。
To find that day's lodge position directly: set the sought day, subtract one and halve, multiply by the day difference and add or subtract to first-day motion parts; if posterior motion parts are fewer, subtract; if posterior motion parts are more, add and count.
93
以所求日乘之，為積度； 以順加退減其星其段初日夜半宿次，即所求日夜半宿次。
Multiply by the sought day to obtain the accumulated degree; add for direct motion or subtract for retrograde from the segment's first-day midnight lodge position to obtain the sought day's midnight lodge position.
94
求五星合見伏行差：木、火、土三星，以其段初日星行分減太陽行分，為行差。 金、水二星順行者，以其段初日太陽行分減星行分，為行差。 金、水二星退行者，以其段初日星行分並太陽行分，為行差。 內水星夕伏、晨見，直以太陽行分為行差。
To find conjunction visibility motion difference: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn, subtract the sun's motion from that segment's first-day planetary motion to obtain the motion difference. For Venus and Mercury in direct motion, subtract planetary motion from first-day solar motion to obtain the motion difference. For Venus and Mercury in retrograde motion, add first-day planetary motion to solar motion to obtain the motion difference. For Mercury at evening invisibility and morning visibility, use the solar motion directly as the motion difference.
95
求五星定合見伏泛用積：木、火、土三星，各以平合晨疾、夕伏定積，便為定合見、伏泛用積。 金、水二星各置其段盈縮定差，內水星倍之，以其段行差除之為日，不滿，退除為分，在平合夕見、晨伏者，盈減縮加定積，為定合見、伏泛用積； 在退合夕伏、晨見者，盈加縮減定積，為定合見、伏泛用積。
To find fixed conjunction visibility general-use accumulation: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn, use the mean-conjunction morning swift or evening hiding fixed accumulation directly as the fixed conjunction visibility general-use accumulation. For Venus and Mercury, set each segment's excess-and-deficit fixed difference (double for Mercury), divide by segment motion difference for days and reduce remainder to parts; at mean-conjunction evening visibility and morning hiding, subtract excess or add deficit to fixed accumulation to obtain fixed conjunction visibility general-use accumulation; at retrograde conjunction evening hiding and morning visibility, add excess or subtract deficit from fixed accumulation to obtain fixed conjunction visibility general-use accumulation.
96
求五星定合積定星：木、火、土三星，以平合行差除其日盈縮分，為距合差日。 以盈縮分減之，為距合差度。 以差日、差度盈減縮加其星定合泛用積，為其星定合定積、定星。 金、水二星順合者，以平合行差除其日盈縮分，為距合差日。 以盈縮分加之，為距合差度； 以差日、差度盈加縮積其星定合泛用積，為其星定合定積、定星。 金、水二星退合者，以平合行差除其日盈縮分，為距合差日； 以減盈縮減之分，為距合差度； 以差日盈減縮加，以差度盈加縮減再定合泛用積，為其星再定合定積、定星。 各以天正冬至大餘及約分加定積，滿統法去之，命甲子，算外，即得定合日辰。 以天正冬至加時黃道日度加定星，依宿次去之，即得定合所在宿次。
To find fixed conjunction accumulation and fixed star: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn, divide that day's excess-and-deficit parts by mean-conjunction motion difference to obtain the interval-to-conjunction difference in days. Subtract the excess-and-deficit parts to obtain the interval-to-conjunction difference in degrees. Apply the difference day and difference degree, subtracting excess and adding deficit to that star's fixed-conjunction general-use accumulation, to obtain fixed-conjunction fixed accumulation and fixed star. For Venus and Mercury in direct conjunction, divide that day's excess-and-deficit parts by mean-conjunction motion difference to obtain the interval-to-conjunction difference in days. Add the excess-and-deficit parts to obtain the interval-to-conjunction difference in degrees; apply the difference day and difference degree, adding excess and subtracting deficit from that star's fixed-conjunction general-use accumulation, to obtain fixed-conjunction fixed accumulation and fixed star. For Venus and Mercury in retrograde conjunction, divide that day's excess-and-deficit parts by mean-conjunction motion difference to obtain the interval-to-conjunction difference in days; subtract the excess-and-deficit parts to obtain the interval-to-conjunction difference in degrees; apply the difference day with excess subtract and deficit add, and the difference degree with excess add and deficit subtract to the fixed-conjunction general-use accumulation again, to obtain the star's second fixed-conjunction fixed accumulation and fixed star. For each add civil new-year winter solstice greater remainder and approximate parts to fixed accumulation, cast out full common divisors, name from jiazi outside the count, and obtain the fixed-conjunction day and double-hour. Add the fixed star to the winter solstice ecliptic solar degree at hour-of-addition, remove by lodge sequence, and obtain the lodge of the fixed conjunction.
97
求五星定見伏定積：木、火、土三星以泛用積晨加、夕減一象，如半周天已下自相乘，已上，覆減一周天，餘亦自相乘，七十五而一，所得，以其星伏見度乘之，十五而一為差，如其段行差除之為日，不滿，退除為分，見加伏減泛用積，為其星定見、伏定積。 金、水二星以行差除其日盈縮分為日，在夕見、晨伏，盈加縮減泛用積，為常用積； 夕伏、晨見，盈減縮加泛用積，為常用積； 如常用積在半周天已下為冬至後； 已上去之，餘為夏至後。 各在一象已下自相乘，已上，覆減一周天，餘亦自相乘，冬至後晨、夏至後夕，以十八而一； 冬至後夕、夏至後晨，以七十五而一，所得，以其星伏見度乘之，十五而一為差，如其段行差除之為日，不滿，退除為分，冬至後晨見、夕伏，夏至後夕見、晨伏，以加常用積，為其星定見、伏定積； 冬至後夕見、晨伏，夏至後晨見、夕伏，以減常用積，為其星定見、伏定積。 加命如前，即得定見、伏日辰。
To find fixed visibility and hiding fixed accumulation: for Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn, adjust the general-use accumulation by one image morning add and evening subtract, square within half the circumpolar circuit or cover-subtract and square above, divide by 75, multiply by heliacal visibility and by one-fifteenth for the difference, convert through segment motion difference to days and parts, add for visibility and subtract for hiding, and obtain fixed visibility and hiding fixed accumulation. For Venus and Mercury, divide that day's excess-and-deficit parts by motion difference for days; at evening visibility and morning hiding, add excess or subtract deficit from general-use accumulation to obtain the usual accumulation; at evening hiding and morning visibility, subtract excess or add deficit to general-use accumulation to obtain the usual accumulation; if the usual accumulation is at or below half the circumpolar circuit, it is after the winter solstice; if above, subtract half the circumpolar circuit; the remainder is after the summer solstice. In each case square if within one image, otherwise cover-subtract one circumpolar circuit and square; after winter solstice morning and after summer solstice evening, divide by eighteen; after winter solstice evening and after summer solstice morning, divide by seventy-five; multiply by heliacal visibility and one-fifteenth for the difference, convert through segment motion difference to days and parts; for winter-solstice morning visibility and evening hiding and summer-solstice evening visibility and morning hiding, add to usual accumulation to obtain fixed visibility and hiding fixed accumulation; for winter-solstice evening visibility and morning hiding and summer-solstice morning visibility and evening hiding, subtract from usual accumulation to obtain fixed visibility and hiding fixed accumulation. Add and name as before to obtain the fixed visibility and hiding day and double-hour.
