河北路河東路
Hebei Circuit and Hedong Circuit
2
河北路。 舊分東、西兩路，後並為一路。 熙寧六年，再分為兩路。
Hebei Circuit. It had formerly been split into eastern and western circuits, then united as a single circuit. In Xining 6 it was again divided into two circuits.
3
東路。 府三：大名，開德，河間。 州十一：滄，冀，博，棣，莫，雄，霸，德，濱，恩，清。 軍五：德清，保順，永靜，信安，保定。 縣五十七。
Eastern Circuit. Three prefectures: Daming, Kaide, and Hejian. Eleven prefectures: Cang, Ji, Bo, Di, Mo, Xiong, Ba, De, Bin, En, and Qing. Five army prefectures: Deqing, Baoshun, Yongjing, Xin'an, and Baoding. Fifty-seven counties.
4
大名府，魏郡。 慶曆二年，建為北京。 八年，始置大名府路安撫使，統北京、澶懷衛德博濱棣、通利保順軍。 熙寧以來並因之，六年，分屬河北東路。 崇甯戶一十五萬五千二百五十三，口五十六萬八千九百七十六。 貢花槹、綿槹、平槹、紫草。 縣十二：元城，赤。 熙寧六年，省大名縣為鎮入焉。
Daming Prefecture, Wei commandery. Qingli 2 made it the Northern Capital. In the eighth year the Daming Circuit pacification commissioner was first appointed, overseeing the Northern Capital, Chan, Huai, Wei, De, Bo, Bin, Di, and the Tongli and Baoshun army prefectures. From the Xining era onward the arrangement held; in the sixth year it was placed under Hebei East Circuit. Chongning register: 155,253 households and 568,976 persons. Tribute items: flowered poles, cotton poles, plain poles, and purple gromwell. Twelve counties: Yuancheng, red grade. Xining 6 abolished Daming County and reduced it to a town under Yuancheng.
5
莘，畿。
Shen, metropolitan vicinity grade.
6
大名，次赤。 熙寧六年，省入元城。 紹聖三年復。 政和六年，徙治南樂鎮。
Daming, secondary red grade. Xining 6 it was abolished and merged into Yuancheng. Shaosheng 3 restored it. Zhenghe 6 moved the seat to Nanle Town.

內黃，畿。
Neihuang, metropolitan vicinity grade.
8
成安，畿。 熙寧六年，省洹水縣為鎮入焉。
Cheng'an, metropolitan vicinity grade. Xining 6 abolished Huanshui County and reduced it to a town under Cheng'an.
9
魏，次畿。
Wei, secondary metropolitan vicinity grade.
10
館陶，畿。 熙寧五年，省永濟縣為鎮入焉，尋復舊。
Guantao, metropolitan vicinity grade. Xining 5 briefly abolished Yongji County and made it a town under Guantao, but the old arrangement was soon restored.
11
臨清，次畿。
Linqing, secondary metropolitan vicinity grade.
12
夏津，畿。
Xiajin, metropolitan vicinity grade.
13
清平，畿。 宋初，自博州來隸。 熙寧二年，又割博平縣明靈砦隸焉，本縣移置明靈。
Qingping, metropolitan vicinity grade. Early in the dynasty it was transferred from Bozhou. Xining 2 detached the Mingling Stockade from Boping County and attached it to Qingping, moving the county seat to Mingling.
14
冠氏，畿
Guanshi, metropolitan vicinity grade
15
宗城。 畿。 熙寧五年，省臨清縣為鎮入焉。 當年復舊，尋以永濟隸臨清。 熙寧六年，又省經城縣為鎮入焉。
Zongcheng. Metropolitan vicinity grade. Xining 5 abolished Linqing County and reduced it to a town under Zongcheng. The old arrangement was restored the same year, and Yongji was soon placed under Linqing. Xining 6 abolished Jingcheng County and reduced it to a town under Zongcheng.
16
開德府，上，澶淵郡，鎮寧軍節度。 本澶州。 崇寧四年，建為北輔。 五年，升為府。 宣和二年，罷輔郡，仍隸河北東路。 崇甯戶三萬一千八百七十八，口八萬二千八百二十六。 貢莨莠席、南粉。 縣七：濮陽，中。
Kaide Prefecture, upper grade, Chanyuan commandery, Zhenning Army jiedushi commission. It had been Chan Prefecture. Chongning 4 made it a northern auxiliary capital. The fifth year raised it to prefectural status. Xuanhe 2 ended its status as an auxiliary capital, but it remained under Hebei East Circuit. Chongning register: 31,878 households and 82,826 persons. Tribute items: digitata rush mats and southern starch powder. Seven counties: Puyang, middle grade.
17
觀城，望。 皇祐元年，省入濮陽、頓丘。 四年，復置。
Guancheng, illustrious grade. Huangyou 1 abolished it and merged it into Puyang and Dunqiu. The fourth year restored it.

臨河，緊。
Linhe, close grade.
19
清豐，中。 慶曆四年，徙清豐縣治德清軍，即縣置軍使，隸州。 熙寧六年，省頓丘縣入清豐。
Qingfeng, middle grade. Qingli 4 moved the Qingfeng seat to Deqing Army, where an army commissioner was placed at the county seat under the prefecture. Xining 6 abolished Dunqiu County and merged it into Qingfeng.

Weinan, middle grade.

Chaocheng, metropolitan vicinity grade. It had belonged to Daming Prefecture; Chongning 4 it was transferred here together with Nanle.
22
南樂。 畿。
Nanle. Metropolitan vicinity grade.
23

Deqing Army, as noted above.
24
滄州，上，景城郡，橫海軍節度。 崇甯戶六萬五千八百五十一，口一十一萬八千二百一十八。 貢大絹、大柳箱。 縣五：清池，望。 熙甯四年，省饒安縣為鎮入清池。 有乾符、巷姑、三女、泥姑、小南河五砦。 政和二年，改巷姑曰海清，三女曰三河，泥姑曰河平。
Cang Prefecture, upper grade, Jingcheng commandery, Henghai Army jiedushi commission. Chongning register: 65,851 households and 118,218 persons. Tribute items: large silk and large willow chests. Five counties: Qingchi, illustrious grade. Xining 4 abolished Rao'an County and reduced it to a town under Qingchi. It had five stockades: Qianfu, Xianggu, Sannü, Nigu, and Xiaonanhe. Zhenghe 2 renamed Xianggu to Haiqing, Sannü to Sanhe, and Nigu to Heping.
25
無棣，望。 治平中，徙無棣縣治保順軍，即縣治置軍使，隸州。
Wudi, illustrious grade. During Zhiping the Wudi seat was moved to Baoshun Army, where an army commissioner was placed at the county seat under the prefecture.
26
鹽山，緊。
Yanshan, close grade.
27
樂陵，緊。 熙寧二年，徙治咸平鎮。
Leling, close grade. Xining 2 moved the seat to Xianping Town.
28
南皮。 中。 熙寧六年，省臨津縣入焉。
Nanpi. Middle grade. Xining 6 abolished Linjin County and merged it into Nanpi.
29
保順軍。 周置軍於滄州無棣縣南三十里。 開寶三年，又以滄棣二州界保順、吳橋二鎮之地益焉，仍棣滄州。
Baoshun Army. The Zhou founded the army thirty li south of Wudi County in Cang Prefecture. Kaibao 3 added the border towns of Baoshun and Wuqiao from Cang and Di prefectures; it remained under Di and Cang.
30
冀州，上，信都郡，舊團練。 慶曆八年，升安武軍節度。 崇甯戶六萬六千二百四十四，口一十萬一千三十。 貢絹。 縣六：信都，望。
Ji Prefecture, upper grade, Xindu commandery, formerly a regiment-training commission. Qingli 8 elevated it to the Anwu Army jiedushi commission. Chongning register: 66,244 households and 101,030 persons. Tribute item: silk. Six counties: Xindu, illustrious grade.
31
蓨上。
Tiaoshang.
32
南宮，上。 皇祐四年，升新河鎮為縣，廢南宮。 六年，省新河為鎮入焉。 棗強，上。 熙甯元年，省為鎮入信都。 十年，復置。
Nangong, upper grade. Huangyou 4 raised Xinhe Town to county status and abolished Nangong. The sixth year abolished Xinhe as a county and reduced it to a town under Nangong. Zaoqiang, upper grade. Xining 1 abolished it and reduced it to a town under Xindu. The tenth year restored it.

武邑，上。
Wuyi, upper grade.
34
衡水。 中。
Hengshui. Middle grade.
35
河間府，上，河間郡，瀛海軍節度。 至道三年，以高陽隸順安軍。 舊名關南，太平興國元年，改名高陽關。 慶曆八年，始置高陽關路安撫使，統瀛莫雄貝冀滄、永靜保定乾甯信安一十州軍。 本瀛州，防禦。 大觀二年升為府，賜軍額。 崇甯戶三萬一千九百三十，口六萬二百六。 貢絹。 縣三：河間，望。 雍熙中，即縣西置平虜砦，景德二年，改為肅寧城。
Hejian Prefecture, upper grade, Hejian commandery, Yinghai Army jiedushi commission. Zhidao 3 placed Gaoyang under Shun'an Army. It had been called Guannan; Taiping Xingguo 1 renamed it Gaoyang Pass. Qingli 8 first appointed a Gaoyang Pass Circuit pacification commissioner overseeing ten prefectures and armies: Ying, Mo, Xiong, Bei, Ji, Cang, Yongjing, Baoding, Qianning, and Xin'an. It had been Ying Prefecture with a defense commission. Daguan 2 raised it to prefectural status and granted a military designation. Chongning register: 31,930 households and 60,206 persons. Tribute item: silk. Three counties: Hejian, illustrious grade. During Yongxi a Pingluo Stockade was built west of the county; Jingde 2 renamed it Suning Fort.
36
樂壽，望。 至道三年，自深州來隸。 熙甯六年，省景城為鎮入焉。
Leshou, illustrious grade. Zhidao 3 it was transferred from Shen Prefecture. Xining 6 abolished Jingcheng County and reduced it to a town under Leshou.
37
束城。 上。 熙寧六年，省為鎮入河間，元祐元年復。
Shucheng. Upper grade. Xining 6 abolished it and reduced it to a town under Hejian; Yuanyou 1 restored county status.
38
博州，上，博平郡，防禦。 淳化三年，河決，移治于孝武渡西。 崇甯戶四萬六千四百九十二，口九萬一千三百三十三。 貢平絹。 縣四：聊城，望。
Bo Prefecture, upper grade, Boping commandery, defense commission. Chunhua 3 the Yellow River broke through; the seat moved west of Xiaowu Ford. Chongning register: 46,492 households and 91,333 persons. Tribute item: plain silk. Four counties: Liaocheng, illustrious grade.

Boping. Close grade. Xining 2 detached Mingling Stockade and attached it to Qingping in the Northern Capital.
42
棣州，上，樂安郡，防禦。 建隆二年，升為團練，俄為防禦。 大中祥符八年，移治陽信縣界八方寺。 崇甯戶三萬九千一百三十七，口五萬七千二百三十四。 貢絹。 縣三：厭次，上。
Di Prefecture, upper grade, Le'an commandery, defense commission. Jianlong 2 raised it to a regiment-training commission, and soon to a defense commission. Dazhong Xiangfu 8 moved the seat to Bafang Temple on the Yangxin county line. Chongning register: 39,137 households and 57,234 persons. Tribute item: silk. Three counties: Yanci, upper grade.
43

Shanghe, middle grade.

陽信。 下。
Yangxin. Lower grade.
45
莫州，上，文安郡，防禦。 熙甯六年，省長豐縣為鎮，又省鄚縣入任丘。 元祐二年，復鄚縣，尋又罷為鎮。 崇甯戶一萬四千五百六十，口三萬一千九百九十二。 貢綿。 縣一：任丘。 上。 有馬村王家二砦。 政和三年改馬村砦曰定安，王家砦曰定平。
Mo Prefecture, upper grade, Wen'an commandery, defense commission. Xining 6 abolished Changfeng County and reduced it to a town, and merged Mao County into Renqiu. Yuanyou 2 restored Mao County, which was soon abolished again and reduced to a town. Chongning register: 14,560 households and 31,992 persons. Tribute item: cotton wadding. One county: Renqiu. Upper grade. It had two stockades: Macun and Wangjia. Zhenghe 3 renamed Macun Stockade to Ding'an and Wangjia Stockade to Dingping.
46
雄州，中，防禦。 本唐涿州瓦橋關。 政和三年，賜郡名曰易陽。 崇甯戶一萬三千一十三，口五萬二千九百六十七。 貢槹。 縣二：歸信，中。 有張家、木場、三橋、雙柳、大渦、七姑垣、紅城、新垣八砦。
Xiong Prefecture, middle grade, defense commission. It had been Waqiao Pass of Tang Zhuozhou. Zhenghe 3 granted the commandery name Yiyang. Chongning register: 13,013 households and 52,967 persons. Tribute item: pole. Two counties: Guixin, middle grade. It had eight stockades: Zhangjia, Muchang, Sanqiao, Shuangliu, Dawo, Qiguyuan, Hongcheng, and Xinyuan.
47
容城。 中。 建隆四年復置。
Rongcheng. Middle grade. Jianlong 4 restored it.
48
霸州，中，防禦。 本唐幽州永清縣地，後置益津關。 周置霸州，以莫之文安、瀛州之大城來屬。 政和三年，賜郡名曰永清。 崇甯戶一萬五千九百一十八，口二萬一千五百一十六。 貢絹。 縣二：文安，上。 景祐二年，廢永清縣入焉。 有劉家渦、刁魚、莫金口、阿翁、雁頭、黎陽、喜渦、鹿角八砦。 元豐四年，割鹿角砦隸信安軍。 政和三年，改劉家渦砦曰安平，阿翁曰仁孝，雁頭曰和寧，喜渦曰喜安。
Ba Prefecture, middle grade, defense commission. It had been Yongqing County of Tang Youzhou; Yijin Pass was later established there. The Zhou created Ba Prefecture, attaching Wen'an from Mo and Dacheng from Ying Prefecture. Zhenghe 3 granted the commandery name Yongqing. Chongning register: 15,918 households and 21,516 persons. Tribute item: silk. Two counties: Wen'an, upper grade. Jingyou 2 abolished Yongqing County and merged it into Wen'an. It had eight stockades: Liujiawo, Diaoyu, Mojinkou, A'weng, Yantou, Liyang, Xiwo, and Lujiao. the fourth year of Yuanfeng detached Lujiao Stockade and attached it to Xin'an Army. Zhenghe 3 renamed Liujiawo Stockade to Anping, A'weng to Renxiao, Yantou to Hening, and Xiwo to Xi'an.
49
大城。 上。
Dacheng. Upper grade.
50
德州，上，平原郡，軍事。 宋初，省歸化縣。 景祐二年，廢安陵縣入將陵，後割屬永靜軍。 熙寧六年，省德平縣為鎮，入安德。 崇甯戶四萬四千五百九十一，口八萬二千二十五。 貢絹。 縣二：安德，望。
De Prefecture, upper grade, Pingyuan commandery, military prefecture. Early in the Song, Guihua County was abolished. Jingyou 2 abolished Anling County and merged it into Jiangling; it was later transferred to Yongjing Army. Xining 6 abolished Deping County and reduced it to a town under Ande. Chongning register: 44,591 households and 82,025 persons. Tribute item: silk. Two counties: Ande, illustrious grade.
51
平原。 緊。
Pingyuan. Close grade.
52
濱州，上，軍事。 大觀二年，賜渤海郡名。 大中祥符五年，廢蒲台縣。 崇甯戶四萬九千九百九十一，口一十一萬四千九百八十四。 貢絹。 縣二：渤海，望
Bin Prefecture, upper grade, military prefecture. Daguan 2 granted the Bohai commandery name. Dazhong Xiangfu 5 abolished Putai County. Chongning register: 49,991 households and 114,984 persons. Tribute item: silk. Two counties: Bohai, illustrious grade.
53
招安。 上。 慶曆三年，升招安鎮為縣。 熙寧六年，省為鎮入渤海。 元豐二年復為縣。
Zhao'an. Upper grade. Qingli 3 raised Zhao'an Town to county status. Xining 6 abolished it and reduced it to a town under Bohai. the second year of Yuanfeng restored county status.
54
恩州，下，淸河郡，軍事。 唐貝州，晉永淸軍節度，周爲防禦。 宋初，復爲節度。 慶暦八年，改州名，罷節度。 崇甯戸五萬一千三百四十二，口八萬五千九百八十六。 貢絹、白毯。 縣三：淸河，望。 端拱元年，徙治永寧鎭。 淳化五年徙今治。 熙寧四年，省淸陽縣入焉。
En Prefecture, lower grade, Qinghe commandery, military prefecture. Under Tang it was Bei Prefecture; under Jin it held Yongqing Army jiedushi; under Zhou it was a defense commission. Early in the Song it was restored as a jiedushi commission. Qingli 8 changed the prefecture name and abolished the jiedushi commission. Chongning register: 51,342 households and 85,986 persons. Tribute items: silk and white blankets. Of its three counties, Qinghe ranked illustrious. Duangong 1 moved the seat to Yongning Town. Chunhua 5 moved the seat to its present location. Xining 4 abolished Qingyang County and merged it in.
55
武城，望。
Wucheng, illustrious grade.
56
歴亭。 緊。 至和元年，廢漳南縣入焉。
Liting. Close grade. Zhihe 1 abolished Zhangnan County and merged it into Liting.
57
永靜軍，同下州。 唐景州。 太平興國六年，以軍直屬京。 淳化元年，以冀州阜城來屬。 景德元年，改軍名。 崇甯戶三萬四千一百九十三，口三萬九千二十二。 貢簟、絹。 縣三：東光，緊。
Yongjing Army, equal to a lower prefecture. It had been Tang Jingzhou. Taiping Xingguo 6 placed the army directly under the capital. Chunhua 1 transferred Fucheng from Ji Prefecture to it. Jingde 1 changed the army's name. Chongning register: 34,193 households and 39,022 persons. Tribute items: mats and silk. Three counties: Dongguang, close grade.
58
將陵。 望。 景祐元年，移治于長河鎮。
Jiangling. Illustrious grade. Jingyou 1 moved the seat to Changhe Town.
59
阜城。 中。 嘉祐八年，省為鎮入東光。 熙寧十年復為縣。
Fucheng. Middle grade. Jiayou 8 abolished it and reduced it to a town under Dongguang. Xining 10 restored county status.
60
清州，下，本乾寧軍。 幽州蘆台軍之地，晉陷契丹。 周平三關，置永安縣，屬滄州。 太平興國七年置軍，改縣曰乾寧隸焉。 大觀二年，升為州。 政和三年，賜郡名曰乾寧。 崇寧戶六千六百一十九，口一萬二千七十八。 貢絹。 縣一：乾寧。 熙寧六年省為鎮。 元符二年復。 崇寧三年再省。 政和五年又復。
Qing Prefecture, lower grade, originally Qianning Army. It was the territory of Lutai Army in Youzhou, lost to the Khitan during Jin. Zhou's pacification of the Three Passes created Yong'an County under Cang Prefecture. Taiping Xinguo 7 established the army, renamed the county Qianning, and placed it under army jurisdiction. Daguan 2 raised it to prefectural status. Zhenghe 3 granted the commandery name Qianning. Chongning register: 6,619 households and 12,078 persons. Tribute item: silk. It had one county: Qianning. Xining 6 abolished it and reduced it to a town. Yuanfu 2 restored county status. Chongning 3 abolished it again. Zhenghe 5 restored it once more.
61
砦六。 釣台、獨流北、獨流東、當城、沙渦、百萬。
It had six stockades. They were Diaotai, Duliu North, Duliu East, Dangcheng, Shawo, and Baiwan.
62
信安軍，同下州。 太平興國六年，以霸州淤口砦建破虜軍。 景德二年，改為信安。 崇寧戶七百一十五，口一千四百三十七。 貢絹。 砦七。 周河、刁魚、田家、狼城、佛聖渦、鹿角、李詳。 元豐四年，霸州鹿角砦始隸軍。
Xin'an Army, equal to a lower prefecture. Taiping Xinguo 6 built Polu Army from Bazhou's Yukou Stockade. Jingde 2 renamed it Xin'an. Chongning register: 715 households and 1,437 persons. Tribute item: silk. It had seven stockades. They were Zhouhe, Diaoyu, Tianjia, Langcheng, Foshengwo, Lujiao, and Lixiang. the fourth year of Yuanfeng first attached Bazhou's Lujiao Stockade to the army.
63
保定軍，同下州。 太平興國六年，以涿州新鎮建平戎軍。 景德元年，改為保定軍。 景祐元年，析霸州文安、大城二縣五百戶隸軍。 宣和七年，廢保定軍為保定縣，隸莫州，知縣事仍兼軍使，尋依舊。 崇寧戶一千二十九，口二千四百八十四。 貢絁。 砦二。 桃花、父母。 政和三年，改父母砦曰安寧。
Baoding Army, equal to a lower prefecture. Taiping Xinguo 6 established Pingrong Army from Zhuozhou's Xinzhen. Jingde 1 renamed it Baoding Army. Hebei Circuit broadly lay within the Yugong domains of Yan, Ji, and Qing, though Ji and Yan predominated. It fell within the celestial divisions of Net, Pleiades, Encampment, eastern Wall, Tail, and Winnowing Basket. It bordered the Great River on the south, met You and Shuo in the north, faced the sea and Mount Dai in the east, and pressed upon Shangdang in the west. It was a source of cocoon silk and woven silk tribute. The people were plain and spare in refinement, often devoted to classical learning; on the whole they were spirited, brave, and honor-bound, and were known as fierce and resolute. The land was flat and close to the frontier, and fighting was a prevailing custom. River transport supplied the frontier, merchants moved goods in trade, and fodder and grain were stockpiled in abundance. At the founding of the dynasty, local militia were recruited and posted, defenses were greatly strengthened, three passes were established, and frontier fields were set aside to supply military granaries. The Khitan raided again and again, and many people abandoned their homes; once the court sought to restore peaceful relations and opened border markets more widely, drifting vagrants returned. The lands of Daming, Chanyuan, Anyang, Linming, and Ji commandery contained much saline soil and were suited to pasturage. Fuyang adjoined the sea, and salt trading brought great profit. In holding the northern territory, Zhen, Wei, and Zhongshan all served as mighty strongholds.

河東路，蓋《禹貢》冀、雍二州之域，而冀州為多。 當觜、參之分。 其地東際常山，西控党項，南盡晉、絳，北控雲、朔，當太行之險地，有鹽、鐵之饒。 其俗剛悍而樸直，勤農織之事業，寡桑柘而富麻苧。 善治生，多藏蓄，其靳嗇尤甚。 朔方、樓煩，馬之所出，歲增貿市以充監牧之用。 太宗平太原，慮其恃險，徙州治焉。 然猶為重鎮，屯精兵以控邊部雲。
Hedong Circuit broadly lay within the Yugong domains of Ji and Yong, though Ji predominated. It fell within the celestial divisions of Turtle Beak and Three Stars. Its lands bordered Mount Changshan in the east, held the Tangut in check to the west, reached Jin and Jiang in the south, and controlled Yun and Shuo in the north; it occupied the strategic terrain of the Taihang Mountains and enjoyed abundant salt and iron. Its people were hardy and plain-spoken, diligent in farming and weaving, with little mulberry or pagoda tree but abundant hemp and ramie. They were skilled at making a living and amassed stores; their stinginess was especially marked. Shuofang and Loufan were sources of horses; trade was increased yearly to supply the imperial horse pasturage. After Emperor Taizong pacified Taiyuan, fearing its reliance on natural defenses, he moved the prefectural seat away from it. Yet it remained a key stronghold, garrisoned with elite troops to control the Yun frontier sector.
