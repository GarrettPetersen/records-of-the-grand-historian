1
蔚昭敏
Yu Zhaomin
2
蔚昭敏，字仲明，開封祥符人。 父興，事周世宗，數戰伐有功，又從太宗平太原，終龍尉都虞候。 真宗為襄王，昭敏自東班殿侍選隸襄王府。 帝即位，授西頭供奉官，累遷崇儀使、冀貝行營兵馬都監。 契丹以五千騎突至冀州城南，昭敏帥部兵與戰，敗之，得其器甲，賊遁去，而師不失一人。
Yu Zhaomin, whose style name was Zhongming, came from Xiangfu in Kaifeng. His father Xing had served Emperor Shizong of Zhou and won distinction in repeated campaigns; he also followed Emperor Taizong in the conquest of Taiyuan and eventually rose to chief military adjutant of the Dragon Guard. While Zhenzong was still Prince of Xiang, Zhaomin was chosen from the Eastern Class palace attendants and assigned to the prince's household. After the emperor's accession he was made a Western Head Palace Attendant and was promoted in due course to Commissioner of Ceremonial Regalia and overall military supervisor of the Ji-Bei campaign headquarters. When five thousand Khitan horsemen suddenly appeared south of Jizhou, Zhaomin led his troops into battle, routed them, and captured their arms and armor. The enemy fled, and not one of his men was lost.
3
咸平四年，領順州刺史、定州行營鈐轄兼押大陣，又為鎮、定、高陽關三路先鋒。 契丹入寇，帝北巡至大名，契丹退趨莫州，昭敏與范廷召追至莫州東三十里，斬首萬餘級，擒生口甚眾，契丹委器甲遁去。 拜唐州團練使，累遷至殿前副都指揮使，遷都指揮使、保靜軍節度使。 以足疾，命入謁無拜。 卒，贈侍中。
In the fourth year of the Xianping era he served as prefect of Shunzhou, as campaign military commissioner at Dingzhou with command of the main army, and as vanguard for the Zhen, Ding, and Gaoyang Pass frontier commands. During a Khitan invasion the emperor advanced north as far as Daming. As the Khitan withdrew toward Mozhou, Zhaomin and Fan Tingzhao pursued them thirty li east of Mozhou, took more than ten thousand heads, and captured a great number of prisoners. The Khitan abandoned their arms and armor and fled. He was appointed regimental commissioner of Tangzhou, rose through successive promotions to vice overall commander of the Palace Front Division, and was then made overall commander and military governor of the Baojing army. Because of a foot ailment, he was excused from bowing when he came to court. He died and was posthumously enfeoffed as Palace Attendant.
4
高化，字仲熙，真定人。 少沉勇有力，不事耕稼，學擊劍，善射。 契丹犯河北，應募轉餉飛狐口。 楊業留戲下，使捕賊酋大鵬翼，獲之。 會契丹又犯真定，乃辭業還家，家屬盡為契丹所掠去。 從州將入京師，遂隸禁軍，選為襄王牽䪊官。 王尹京，命巡內外八廂，積獲奸盜甚眾。 盜有遺化金帛者，化弗受。 一日，王趨急召出府門，馬驚墮，化掖之而起。 王曰：「微爾。 吾幾殆。 」益親信之。
Gao Hua, whose style name was Zhongxi, came from Zhending. As a youth he was steady, brave, and powerful. He did not work the fields but trained in swordsmanship and became an expert archer. When the Khitan invaded Hebei, he volunteered to transport supplies to Feihukou. Yang Ye kept him in his service and sent him to capture the rebel chieftain Dapengyi, whom he took alive. When the Khitan invaded Zhending again, he left Yang Ye's service and went home, only to find that the Khitan had carried off his entire family. He followed a prefectural commander to the capital, entered the Palace Army, and was chosen as groom for the Prince of Xiang. When the prince served as mayor of the capital, he was ordered to patrol the eight inner and outer wards of the city and over time captured a great many criminals. Thieves sometimes offered him gold and silks, but he refused to take them. One day the prince rushed out at an urgent summons beyond the mansion gate. His horse bolted and threw him, but Hua caught him and helped him to his feet. The prince said, "But for you, I would have been killed. " After that he trusted him all the more.
5
發兵襲明珠族，不利，降滑州總管。 改興州防禦使、真定路副都總管，徙高陽關路。 修護章惠太后園陵，累拜殿前副都指揮使，歷建武軍節度使。 以老，辭管軍。 詔入朝，化又固請，改武安軍節度使、知滄州，未行，改相州。 部有大獄已具，皆當論死。 化疑之，遣移訊，果出無罪者三人。 逾年，復告老，以右屯衛上將軍致仕。 卒，年八十。 贈太尉，諡曰恭莊。
He led troops against the Mingzhu tribe, but the campaign went badly and he was transferred to serve as commandant of Huazhou. He was made defense commissioner of Xingzhou and vice overall commander of the Zhending circuit, then transferred to the Gaoyang Pass circuit. He oversaw repairs at the garden tomb of Empress Dowager Zhanghui, rose through successive promotions to vice overall commander of the Palace Front Division, and served as military governor of the Jianwu army. When he grew old he asked to be relieved of command of the army. He was summoned to court, but Hua again pressed his request to retire. He was appointed military governor of the Wu'an army and prefect of Cangzhou, but before he could take up the post he was reassigned to Xiangzhou. In his jurisdiction a major trial had already been concluded, and every defendant was to be sentenced to death. Hua had doubts about the case and ordered a reinvestigation, which revealed three men who were in fact innocent. A year later he again asked to retire and was granted retirement with the rank of senior general of the Right Unicorn Guard. He died at the age of eighty. He was posthumously enfeoffed as Grand Commandant with the posthumous title Respectful and Solemn.
6
化謹質少過，馭軍有法。 雖起身行伍，然頗知民事焉。
Hua was careful and upright, seldom at fault, and disciplined his troops by strict rules. Though he had risen from the ranks, he understood civilian affairs remarkably well.
7
周美，字之純，靈州回樂人。 少隸朔方軍，以材武稱。 趙保吉陷靈州，美棄其族，間走歸京師，天子召見，隸禁軍。 契丹犯邊，真宗幸澶州，禦城北門，美慷慨自陳，願假數騎縛契丹將至闕下，帝壯之，常令宿衛。
Zhou Mei, whose style name was Zhichun, came from Huile in Lingzhou. In his youth he served in the Shuofang army and won renown for his ability and fighting skill. When Zhao Baoji seized Lingzhou, Mei left his clan behind, made his way secretly back to the capital, was received in audience by the emperor, and entered the Palace Army. During a Khitan border raid, Emperor Zhenzong went to Chanyuan and took his stand at the city's north gate. Mei stepped forward boldly and declared that, given a few horsemen, he would bind a Khitan general and bring him to the palace gates. The emperor was stirred by his daring and often kept him on night guard.
8
天聖初，德明部落寇平涼方渠，美以軍候戍邊，與州將追戰，破之於九井原、烏侖河，斬首甚眾。 累遷天武都虞候。 元昊反，陝西用兵，經略使夏竦薦其材，擢供備庫使、延州兵馬都監。 夏人既破金明諸砦，美請於經略使范仲淹曰：「夏人新得志，其勢必復來。 金明當邊衝，我之蔽也，今不亟完，將遂失之。 」仲淹因屬美復城如故。 數日賊果來，其眾數萬薄金明，陣於延安城北三十里。 美領眾二千力戰，抵暮，援兵不至，乃徙軍山北，多設疑兵。 夏人望見，以為救至，即引去。 既而復出艾蒿砦，遂至郭北平，夜鬥不解。 美率眾使人持一炬從間道上山，益張旗幟，四面大噪，賊懼走。 獲牛羊、橐駝、鎧甲數千計，遂募兵築萬安城而還。 敵復寇金明，美引兵由虞家堡並北山而下，敵即引卻。 遷文思使，徙知保定軍。 經略使龐籍表留之，改東路都巡檢使。 敗敵於金湯城，焚其族部二十一。
Early in the Tiansheng era, Deming's tribes raided Pingliang and Fangqu. Mei served as an army scout on the frontier and, together with the prefectural commander, pursued the enemy and defeated them at Jiujing Plain and on the Wulun River, taking a great many heads. He rose through successive promotions to chief military adjutant of the Heavenly Martial Guard. When Yuanhao rebelled and war broke out in Shaanxi, frontier commissioner Xia Song recommended him for his ability. He was promoted to commissioner of the supply depot and overall military supervisor of Yanzhou. After the Xia had overrun the Jinming stockades and others, Mei urged frontier commissioner Fan Zhongyan, "The Xia are riding high on victory and will surely return. Jinming lies on a critical frontier pass and is our shield. If we do not repair it at once, we shall lose it for good." Zhongyan therefore put Mei in charge of rebuilding the city as it had been. Within days the enemy did come. Tens of thousands of them pressed Jinming and drew up in battle order thirty li north of Yan'an. Mei led two thousand men in a hard fight until evening, but no reinforcements came. He then shifted his army to the north side of the mountain and set up many decoy detachments. The Xia saw them, took them for arriving reinforcements, and withdrew at once. Soon they emerged again from Aigao Stockade and advanced to Guobei Plain, where fighting raged through the night without pause. Mei led his men up a hidden path, each carrying a torch, spread more banners, and raised a deafening shout on every side. The enemy fled in panic. They captured thousands of cattle, sheep, camels, and suits of armor. Mei then recruited troops, built Wan'an city, and returned. When the enemy raided Jinming again, Mei led his troops down from Yujia Fort along the northern hills, and the enemy withdrew at once. He was promoted to Literary Accomplishment Commissioner and appointed to govern Baoding Army. Frontier commissioner Pang Ji petitioned to keep him in the region, and he was made overall inspector of the eastern route. He defeated the enemy at Jintang city and burned twenty-one of their tribal settlements.
9
元昊大入，據承平砦。 諸將會兵議攻討，洛苑副使种世衡請齎三日糧直搗敵穴。 美曰：「彼知吾來，必設伏待我。 不如間道掩其不意。 」世衡不聽。 美獨以兵西出芙蓉谷，大破敵。 世衡等果無功。 未幾，敵復略土塠砦，美迎擊於野家店，追北至拓跋谷，大敗其眾。 以功遷右騏驥使。 軍還，築柵於蔥梅官道谷，以據敵路。 令士卒益種營田，而收穀六千斛。 復率眾繇廳子部西濟大理河，屠劄萬多移二百帳，焚其積聚以歸。 籍、仲淹交薦之，除鄜延路兵馬都監，遷賀州刺史。
Yuanhao launched a major invasion and seized Chenping Stockade. The generals assembled their forces to plan an attack. Luoyuan vice commissioner Zhong Shihang proposed taking three days' rations and striking straight at the enemy stronghold. Mei said, "They will know we are coming and are sure to lay ambushes for us. Better to take a hidden route and fall on them unawares." Shihang would not listen. Mei alone led his troops west through Furong Valley and routed the enemy. Shihang and the others, as it turned out, accomplished nothing. Before long the enemy raided Tudun Stockade again. Mei met them at Yejia Inn, pursued them north to Tuoba Valley, and crushed their force. For these achievements he was promoted to Right Qiji Commissioner. On his return he built a stockade in Congmei Official Road Valley to block the enemy's line of advance. He had his soldiers expand the garrison farms and harvested six thousand hu of grain. He again led his troops west from the Tingzi tribe, crossed the Dali River, destroyed more than two hundred tents of the Zha Wanduoyi tribe, burned their stores, and returned. Ji and Zhongyan both recommended him, and he was made overall military supervisor of the Fuyan circuit and then prefect of Hezhou.
10
初，美自靈武來，上其所服精甲，詔藏軍器庫。 至是，加飾黃金，遣使即軍中賜之。 又破敵於無定河，乘勝至綏州，殺其酋豪，焚廬帳，獲牛馬、羊駝、器械三百計，因城龍口平砦。 敵以精騎數千來襲，美從百餘騎馳擊破之。 加本路鈐轄，遂為副總管。 遷龍神衛四廂都指揮使、通州刺史； 進捧日、天武四廂都指揮使，陵州團練使。
When Mei first came from Lingwu he presented the fine armor he wore, and an edict ordered it stored in the imperial armory. Now it was trimmed with gold and an envoy was sent to present it to him in camp. He again defeated the enemy on the Wuding River, pressed the victory to Suizhou, killed their chieftains, burned their camps, and captured some three hundred head of cattle, horses, sheep, and camels along with weapons. He then fortified Longkou Ping Stockade. When several thousand elite enemy horsemen attacked, Mei with a little over a hundred horsemen charged and routed them. He was made military commissioner of the circuit and then vice overall commander. He was promoted to overall commander of the four wings of the Dragon Spirit Guard and made prefect of Tongzhou; then advanced to overall commander of the four wings of the Sun-Bearer and Heavenly Martial guards and regimental commissioner of Lingzhou.
11
慶曆中，又城清水、安定、黑水、佛堂、北橫山、乾谷、土明、柳谷、雕巢、盧兒、原安砦十一堡。 安定之役，諜報敵數萬將大至，經略使遣管勾機宜楚建中分諸將兵，趣城黑水以待。 諸將憚敵且至，不肯與兵。 美曰：「兵常以寡擊眾，何自怯也。 」卒以兵二千與建中，而敵亦引去。 每邊書至，諸將各擇便利，獨美未嘗辭難，然所向輒克，諸將以此服之。 歷侍衛親軍馬軍殿前都虞候、眉州防禦使、步軍副都指揮使、遂州觀察使、鄜延副都總管。 召還，授耀州觀察使，又進馬軍副都指揮使。 卒，贈忠武軍節度使，諡忠毅。
During the Qingli era he fortified eleven more stockades: Qingshui, Anding, Heishui, Fotang, North Hengshan, Qian'gu, Tuming, Liugu, Diaochao, Lu'er, and Yuan'an. During the Anding campaign, intelligence reported that tens of thousands of enemy troops were about to arrive. The frontier commissioner sent staff officer Chu Jianzhong to divide the generals' forces and hurry to Heishui to hold the city against them. The generals feared the enemy's imminent arrival and refused to detach troops. Mei said, "Soldiers are meant to strike many with few—why shrink back now? In the end he gave Chu two thousand men, and the enemy withdrew as well. Whenever frontier dispatches arrived, the other generals chose the easier assignments, but Mei never shirked a hard task. Wherever he went he prevailed, and for this the other generals came to respect him. He served in turn as chief military adjutant of the Palace Cavalry in the Imperial Guard, defense commissioner of Meizhou, vice overall commander of the Foot Soldiers, observation commissioner of Suizhou, and vice overall commander of Fuyan. He was recalled to court, appointed observation commissioner of Yaozhou, and promoted again to vice overall commander of the Cavalry. He died and was posthumously enfeoffed as military governor of the Zhongwu army with the posthumous title Loyal and Resolute.
12
自陝西用兵，諸將多不利，美前後十餘戰，平族帳二百，焚二十一，招種落內附者十一族，復城堡甚多。 在軍中所得祿賜，多分其戲下，有餘，悉饗勞之。 及死，家無餘貲。 子蚤卒，以孫永清為子，官至引進副使。
Since the Shaanxi campaigns began, most generals had fared poorly, but Mei fought more than a dozen engagements, subdued two hundred tribal encampments, burned twenty-one, brought eleven tribes in submission, and restored a great many forts and stockades. He gave most of his salary and rewards in the army to his men, and whatever remained he spent entirely on feasting and rewarding them. When he died, his family had nothing left. His son died young, so he adopted his grandson Yongqing, who rose to vice commissioner of presentation.
13
閻守恭
Yan Shougong
14
閻守恭，并州榆次人。 父榮，倜儻有志略，劉繼元欲召至帳下，辭以母老不就。 守恭生而體貌奇偉，榮曰：「是必當事太平天子，吾無恨矣。 」後十七年，劉氏平，徙太原民於大名府，因家焉。 往來負販於並、汾間，過西山，聞郭進為都巡檢使，太宗甚寵遇之。 乃慨然曰：「進不遇主，亦行伍爾，吾自度豈不及進邪？ 」遂應募，隸拱聖軍，擢殿前押班。
Yan Shougong came from Yuci in Bingzhou. His father Rong was bold and enterprising. Liu Jiyuan wished to summon him to his staff, but he declined on the ground that his mother was elderly. Shougong was born with an imposing physique. Rong said, "This boy is bound to serve the emperor of a peaceful age. I shall have no regrets." Seventeen years later the Liu regime fell. The people of Taiyuan were relocated to Daming Prefecture, and the family settled there. He traveled as a peddler between Bing and Fen. Passing the Western Hills, he heard that Guo Jin was overall inspector and that Emperor Taizong favored him greatly. He sighed and said, "If Jin had not found his sovereign, he too would be nothing but a common soldier. Why should I not measure up to Jin?" He enlisted, joined the Palatine Sacred Army, and was promoted to palace-front escort leader.
15
咸平中，從幸河北，以功為捧日副指揮使，歷拱聖、龍衛、捧日指揮使，累遷左第二軍指揮使、乾州刺史。 明道中，落軍職，以德州刺史為永興軍兵馬鈐轄，徙幷代路。
During the Xianping era he accompanied the emperor to Hebei. For his achievements he was made vice commander of the Sun-Bearer Guard and served in turn as commander of the Palatine Sacred, Dragon Guard, and Sun-Bearer guards before rising to commander of the Left Second Army and prefect of Qianzhou. During the Mingdao era he left active army command and, as prefect of Dezhou, was appointed military commissioner of Yongxing Army, then transferred to the Bing-Dai circuit.
16
守恭性沉勇，御軍嚴。 雖家居如對賓客。 常訪求士大夫，取郭進事而師法之。 所得奉祿悉散予人。 在并州，因春社會賓客曰：「守恭，太原一貧民爾。 徒步位刺史，老復官鄉里，逾分多矣。 今日與卿輩訣。 」後十日卒。
Shougong was by nature steady and brave and ruled his troops with strict discipline. Even at home he conducted himself as if he were receiving guests. He regularly sought out scholar-officials and took Guo Jin's career as his model to emulate. He gave away all his salary and stipends to others. While in Bingzhou, at the spring community sacrifice he entertained his guests and said, "Shougong is only a poor man from Taiyuan. I rose on foot to the rank of prefect, and now in old age I am returning to serve in my home district—already far more than I deserve. Today I bid you farewell. Ten days later he died.
17
孟元，字善長，洺州人。 性謹願少過，頗喜讀書。 少隸禁軍，以挽強選補殿侍，累遷散都頭班指揮使，擢如京使、幷代州兵馬都監，改鈐轄，徙高陽關路，又徙真定路。
Meng Yuan, whose style name was Shanchang, came from Mingzhou. He was by nature careful and upright, seldom at fault, and fond of reading. In his youth he entered the Palace Army. Selected for his skill at drawing the heavy bow, he became a palace attendant and rose through successive promotions to commander of the Scattered Chief Squad. He was then made Capital Envoy and overall military supervisor of Bing-Dai prefecture, appointed military commissioner, transferred to the Gaoyang Pass circuit, and then to the Zhending circuit.
18
王則據貝州反，元赴城下攻戰，被數十創，又中機石，墜濠中。 既出，戰愈力。 更募死士由永濟渠穴地以進。 賊平，改右騏驥使，徙大名府路鈐轄。 河朔饑，權知滄州。 民鬻鹽為生，歲荒鹽多不售，民無以自給。 元度軍食有餘，悉用易鹽，繇是民不轉徙。
When Wang Ze seized Beizhou and rebelled, Yuan fought at the foot of the city walls. He suffered dozens of wounds, was struck by a catapult stone, and fell into the moat. Once he climbed out, he fought all the harder. He then recruited dare-to-die troops to tunnel along the Yongji Canal and advance into the city. After the rebels were suppressed, he was made Right Qiji Commissioner and transferred to military commissioner of the Daming Prefecture circuit. When famine struck Heshuo, he served as acting prefect of Cangzhou. The people made their living by selling salt, but in years of famine salt often would not sell and they could not support themselves. Yuan found that army provisions were in surplus and used them all to buy the people's salt, so the people did not have to flee the region.
19
御史中丞郭勸言其貝州功而賞未當，乃擢普州刺史，遷宮苑使，專管勾麟府軍馬事。 護築永寧堡，敵不敢動。 為龍神衛四廂都指揮使、忠州團練使、高陽關馬步軍總管，遷天武、捧日四廂都指揮使，又遷步軍都虞候、眉州防禦使、幷代路副都總管。 判北京賈昌朝奏為大名府路副都總管，徙定州路，遷馬軍都虞候，徙鄜延路，行至鄭州卒，贈遂州觀察使。
Censor-in-chief Guo Quan argued that his achievements at Beizhou had not been properly rewarded. He was promoted to prefect of Puzhou, made Palace Park Commissioner, and put in charge of Linfu army and horse affairs. He oversaw the building of Yongning Fort, and the enemy did not dare move against it. He became overall commander of the four wings of the Dragon Spirit Guard, regimental commissioner of Zhongzhou, and overall commander of cavalry and infantry at Gaoyang Pass. He was then made overall commander of the four wings of the Heavenly Martial and Sun-Bearer guards, and later chief military adjutant of the Foot Soldiers, defense commissioner of Meizhou, and vice overall commander of the Bing-Dai circuit. Administrator of the Northern Capital Jia Changchao recommended him as vice overall commander of the Daming Prefecture circuit. He was transferred to the Dingzhou circuit, promoted to chief military adjutant of the Cavalry, then moved to the Fuyan circuit. He died on the road at Zhengzhou and was posthumously enfeoffed as observation commissioner of Suizhou.
20

Liu Qian, whose style name was Hanzong, came from Kaifeng. In his youth he joined the guard corps and rose through several promotions to overall commander of the Right Wing of the Sun-Bearer Guard, serving as regimental commissioner of Jiazhou and concurrently as capital patrol inspector. When Yuanhao rebelled, he was made regimental commissioner of Bozhou, overall commander of cavalry and infantry on the Huanqing circuit, and concurrently prefect of Binzhou. Qian was not a reader, yet in civil disputes he always judged cases fairly and according to reason. Earlier prefects had often forced the people to supply goods for their kitchens and travel expenses, but Qian did nothing of the kind, and the people of Bin came to esteem him. Xia Song recommended him as overall commander of the Jingyuan circuit and appointed him to govern Jingzhou. Before he could depart, bandits raided Zhenrong Army. Qian led his troops deep into enemy territory, destroyed their settlements, and returned. For this achievement he was promoted to overall commander of the four wings of the Dragon Spirit Guard and defense commissioner of Xiangzhou. He died suddenly of illness and was posthumously enfeoffed as military governor and observation regent of the Yongqing army.

Zhao Zhen, whose style name was Zhongwei, came from Guixin in Xiongzhou. During the Jingde era he served under Shi Pu at Shun'an Army. He captured a Khitan battle map and was granted a borrowed post in the Third Class. Several years later, as military supervisor of Xi prefecture, he hunted bandits on Qinghui Mountain and killed or captured a great many of them.

At the time the Jintang Li Qin, Baibao Shenmu Ma'er, and Gaoluo Bozang tribes were especially fierce and hard to control. Zhen set out wine for them and drank first. He took slender weapons, piled up several heaps of valuables a hundred paces away, and they shot at them together. Military commissioner Wang Huaixin assigned several thousand troops to Zhen for roaming patrol, and he won repeated victories. With only a few dozen horsemen he went to join Huaixin and met an enemy force ten times his size. He shot down dozens of them and the rest fled. Several months later tens of thousands of bandits besieged Pingyuan Stockade, and overall supervisor Zhao Shilong was killed in battle. Zhen took a separate route, fought his way to the stockade, seized its water supply, and led dare-to-die troops to break the siege. The enemy fled and he pursued them, taking several thousand heads. He was made overall salt commissioner of Jingyuan, served in turn as prefect of Shun'an, Bao'an, Guangxin Army, and Bazhou, and was appointed overall commissioner for apprehending bandits in the eastern capital circuit. Thereupon the crack troops of the Dongjiao, Jinming, Wanliu, and other tribes, numbering tens of thousands, all fell into the enemy's hands. Soon afterward he was transferred to command of the four wings of the Sun-Bearer and Heavenly Martial guards. Zhen told his officers, "The enemy will take advantage of our tribal losses and press their victory. The situation calls for holding firm. I still fear not every city can follow our plan. Before long the enemy raided Saimen Stockade. Zhen had nearly eight thousand troops but held them motionless. The stockade had only a thousand men and sent repeated urgent appeals. After five months under siege, Zhen sent only a little over a hundred men to their aid, and the stockade fell. Stockade commander Gao Yande and salt transport officer Wang Jiyuan both perished at the enemy's hands. Zhen was punished for holding his troops back and not rescuing the stockade. Overall transport commissioner Pang Ji memorialized against him, and he was demoted to regimental commissioner of Baizhou and prefect of Jiangzhou. Before he could depart, the families of Yande and Jiyuan appealed again at court. An edict ordered censor Fang Xie to go and investigate Zhen on the spot. By law he deserved execution. He was demoted again to colonel of the Left Pure Way of the Heir Apparent's Guard and exiled to Tanzhou. A year later he was restored to general of the Right Martial Guard, made regimental commissioner of Huizhou and military commissioner of the Bing-Dai circuit, and then promoted to vice overall commander and regimental commissioner of Qizhou.

He was also put in charge of military affairs in the six prefectures of Lan and Xian. When famine struck the region beyond the Yellow River, Zhen devised a way to open trade with merchants outside the stockades and obtained several hundred thousand hu of grain, sustaining both army and people. He was promoted to defense commissioner of Bozhou and then retired at Jiezhou. He was recalled as great general of the Left Divine Martial Army and died in office.

Zhen was tough and self-confident, physically powerful, skilled with bow and horse, fond of strategy, generous with money and valuing honor. Men gladly followed him. His sons Xun and Yu were both expert horsemen and archers.

During the Jingyou era it was reported that Xun's martial skills had advanced further and that he was also studying history and the classics. He was summoned again to demonstrate his martial skills and tested in strategy at the Secretariat, where he submitted answers running to several thousand words. He advanced from palace attendant direct to gatekeeper attendant and soon afterward was appointed overall military supervisor of Haozhou.

Lü Yijian and Song Xiu memorialized, "Since the war began, strategists have submitted tens of thousands of proposals, but none equal Xun's." Xun reported to headquarters, led twenty thousand troops from Jingbian through Kuiwu to Muning to strike the enemy, and captured several thousand. Jingbian commander Liu Hu brought up the rear and was ambushed by the enemy. Xun climbed a slope and saw what had happened. With a few hundred horsemen he charged back in, rescued Liu Hu's force, and all the soldiers marveled at his daring. Xiazhan lived in Kan Valley without allegiance to either side. Xun wrote to win him over, sent him silks and cotton, and Xiazhan submitted.

Headquarters ordered Xun to remain and join Ge Huaimin at Wating. Huaimin had already encamped from Wugu Pass west to Malan city. Hearing that the Xia had moved their army outside the new moat, his officers discussed a surprise attack at dawn. Xun told Huaimin, "The enemy has come from far away with numbers twice ours and their morale at its peak. Otherwise we shall be slaughtered by the enemy." Huaimin would not listen. The army pressed on to Zhenrong city, crossed the border moat, and reached Dingchuan. On another occasion, returning from the capture of Jiaogao Stockade, Ke alone brought up the rear and was attacked by several thousand horsemen. Glancing at his quiver, he found only two Plowshare arrows left. He drew his bow to the full as if to shoot, and the enemy withdrew at once. He was appointed a Third Class attendant and served as master of Nan'an Stockade at Yanzhou and eastern-route patrol inspector. He was promoted to Right Palace Guard.
40
以范仲淹、韓琦薦，授閣門祗候，延州龐籍入奏為東路都巡檢使。 夷黑神、厥保等十八砦，賊以四萬騎犯邊，趨僕射穀。 懷德以兵數千據穀旁高原待之，斬首二百級，得畜產、器械以千數。 遷內殿崇班。 又以兵修龍安城，虜不敢犯，遂為鄜延路都監。 又城綏平，破賊青化、押班、吃當三砦，殺獲甚眾。
On the recommendation of Fan Zhongyan and Han Qi he was made gatekeeper attendant. Pang Ji of Yanzhou memorialized at court to appoint him overall inspector of the eastern route. At the eighteen stockades including Yiheishen and Juebao, the enemy invaded the frontier with forty thousand horsemen and advanced on Pushe Valley. Huaide with several thousand men held the high ground beside the valley and awaited them, taking two hundred heads and capturing thousands of livestock and weapons. He was promoted to Inner Hall Honored Company. He again led troops to repair Long'an city, and the enemy did not dare attack. He was then made overall supervisor of the Fuyan circuit. He also fortified Suiping and captured the three enemy stockades of Qinghua, Yaban, and Chidang, killing and capturing a great many of the enemy.
41
元昊為夏國主，命國子博士高良夫與懷德會西人畫界。 龐籍具論其前後功，遷供備庫副使兼閣門通事舍人。 時用兵久，民多亡散，懷德招輯有方，經略使梁適奏請推其法諸路。 曆知保安軍、環州、環慶益利路鈐轄，累遷至四方館使、舒州團練使，徙鄜延路副都總管。
When Yuanhao became ruler of the Xia state, he ordered Erudite of the Imperial Academy Gao Liangfu to meet with Huaide and the western tribes to demarcate the border. Pang Ji detailed his achievements over the years, and he was promoted to vice commissioner of the supply depot and concurrently Communications Envoy of the gate. The war had dragged on and many people had fled or been scattered. Huaide had effective methods for resettling them, and frontier commissioner Liang Shi petitioned to extend his methods to all circuits. He served in turn as prefect of Bao'an Army and Huanzhou and as military commissioner of the Huanqing-Yili circuit, rose to commissioner of the Four Directions Hall and regimental commissioner of Shuzhou, and was transferred to vice overall commander of the Fuyan circuit.
42
坐違法賂宦官閻士良，為安撫呂景初奏，降四方館使、英州刺史。 大名府路總管，侍衛親軍步軍都虞候、象州防禦使、鄜延路副都總管，遷馬軍都虞候，徙環慶路。 環州蕃官蘇恩以其屬叛，往降之。 又遷殿前都虞候、步軍副指揮使、隨州觀察使。
He was punished for illegally bribing the eunuch Yan Shiliang. Pacification commissioner Lü Jingchu memorialized against him, and he was demoted to commissioner of the Four Directions Hall and prefect of Yingzhou. He served as overall commander of the Daming Prefecture circuit, chief military adjutant of the Imperial Guard Foot Soldiers, defense commissioner of Xiangzhou, and vice overall commander of the Fuyan circuit, was promoted to chief military adjutant of the Cavalry, and transferred to the Huanqing circuit. When the Qiang official Su En of Huanzhou rebelled with his followers, Huaide went to bring him back to submission. He was then made chief military adjutant of the Palace Front Division, vice commander of the Foot Soldiers, and observation commissioner of Suizhou.
43
英宗即位，遷靜難軍節度觀察留後，召還，卒，贈安遠軍節度使。 嘗因戰，流矢中其顙，鏃入於骨，以弩弦係鏃，發機而出之。
When Emperor Yingzong took the throne, he was made regent military governor of the Jingnan army, recalled to court, and died. He was posthumously enfeoffed as military governor of the Anyuan army. Once in battle a stray arrow struck his forehead and the head lodged in the bone. He looped a crossbow string around the head, drew the bow, and pulled the arrow out.
44
安俊，字智周，其先太原人。 祖贇，高州團練使。 仁宗為皇太子，俊以將家子謹厚，選為資善堂祗候。 及即位，補右班殿直，累遷東頭供奉官、閣門祗候，為環州都監。 破趙元昊吃江、井那等諸砦，安撫使韓琦上其功，遷內殿崇班、環慶路都監，徙涇原。 契丹欲渝盟，與狄青、范恪同召至京師，將使備北邊，擢內園副使。 翌日，改禮賓使。
An Jun, whose style name was Zhizhou, was descended from a Taiyuan family. His grandfather Yun had been regimental commissioner of Gaozhou. When Renzong was crown prince, Jun, the careful and upright son of a military family, was chosen as attendant of the Hall of Cultivating Goodness. After the emperor's accession he was made Right Class Palace Attendant Direct and rose through successive promotions to Eastern Head Palace Attendant and gatekeeper attendant, then served as overall supervisor of Huanzhou. He captured the stockades of Zhao Yuanhao at Chijiang, Jingna, and elsewhere. Pacification commissioner Han Qi reported his achievements, and he was promoted to Inner Hall Honored Company and overall supervisor of the Huanqing circuit, then transferred to Jingyuan. When the Khitan threatened to break the alliance, he was summoned to the capital together with Di Qing and Fan Ke to prepare the northern frontier and was promoted to vice commissioner of the Inner Garden. The following day he was made Communications Envoy.
45
會葛懷敏敗，命為秦鳳路鈐轄，復徙涇原。 因條上御戎十三事，改原州，徙麟州，遷六宅使、貴州刺史、知忻州，徙代州。 為帥臣誣奏，降京東路鈐轄。 富弼知青州，為之辨理，真除虢州刺史，徙高陽關路，又遷原州刺史，知滄、涇、冀三州。 秦州築古渭城，蕃部大擾，徙秦鳳路總管。 曆龍神衛、捧日、天武四廂都指揮使，果州團練使，環慶路副總管； 遷侍衛步軍都虞候、陵州防禦使。 卒，贈閬州觀察使。
When Ge Huaimin was defeated, he was appointed military commissioner of the Qinfeng circuit and then transferred again to Jingyuan. He submitted thirteen recommendations on frontier defense, was reassigned to Yuanzhou, transferred to Linzhou, promoted to commissioner of the Six Residences, prefect of Guizhou, and governor of Xinzhou, and then moved to Daizhou. A frontier commander falsely memorialized against him, and he was demoted to military commissioner of the eastern capital circuit. Fu Bi, then prefect of Qingzhou, argued his case, and he was formally appointed prefect of Guozhou. He was transferred to the Gaoyang Pass circuit, then made prefect of Yuanzhou and put in charge of Cang, Jing, and Ji prefectures. When Guwei city was built at Qinzhou and the tribal peoples were greatly disturbed, he was transferred to overall commander of the Qinfeng circuit. He served in turn as overall commander of the four wings of the Dragon Spirit, Sun-Bearer, and Heavenly Martial guards, as regimental commissioner of Guozhou, and as vice overall commander of the Huanqing circuit; then was made chief military adjutant of the Imperial Guard Foot Soldiers and defense commissioner of Lingzhou. He died and was posthumously enfeoffed as observation commissioner of Langzhou.
46
俊久在邊，羌人識之。 環州得俘虜，知州種世衡問之曰：「若屬於吾將孰畏？ 」曰：「畏安大保。 」指俊於坐曰：「此長髯將軍是也。」
Jun had long served on the frontier, and the Qiang knew him well. When Huanzhou took captives, prefect Zhong Shihang asked them, "Among our generals, whom do you fear? They answered, "We fear Grand Guardian An. " Pointing to Jun among those present, they said, "That long-bearded general is the one."
47
向寶，鎮戎軍人，為御前忠佐，換禮賓使，涇原、秦鳳鈐轄。 積勞，自皇城使帶禦器械，曆真定、鄜延副總管，遷龍神衛四廂都指揮使、嘉州團練使，卒。
Xiang Bao, a native of Zhenrong Army, served as a loyal aide before the throne, was transferred to Communications Envoy, and served as military commissioner of Jingyuan and Qinfeng. Through long service he rose from imperial city commissioner bearing imperial weapons to vice overall commander of Zhending and Fuyan, then to overall commander of the four wings of the Dragon Spirit Guard and regimental commissioner of Jiazhou, and died in office.
48
寶善騎射，年十四，與敵戰，斬首二級。 及壯，以勇聞。 有虎踞五原卑邪州，東西百里斷人跡，寶一矢殪之。 道過潼關，巨盜郭邈山多載關中金帛、子女，寶射走之，盡得其所掠。 嘗至太原，梁適射弩再中的，授寶矢射之，四發三中。 適曰：「今之飛將也。 」神宗稱其勇，以比薛仁貴。 及死，厚恤其家。
Bao was skilled in horsemanship and archery. At fourteen he fought the enemy and took two heads. When he grew to manhood he was famed for his courage. A tiger at Wuyuan in Beixie Prefecture had blocked travelers for a hundred li east and west. Bao brought it down with a single arrow. Passing Tong Pass, he encountered the great bandit Guo Miaoshan, who was carrying away gold, silks, and captives from within the pass. Bao shot at him and drove him off, recovering everything that had been plundered. Once at Taiyuan, Liang Shi shot a crossbow and hit the target twice. He handed Bao his arrows; Bao fired four times and hit three times. Shi said, "A flying general of our age! Emperor Shenzong praised his courage and compared him to Xue Rengui. When he died, his family received generous compensation.
49
論曰：蔚昭敏、高化、周美，蓋皆有功於邊鄙者。 化在蜀州，取軍中積材以塞水患，又能平反冤獄，脫人於死，蓋武人之知民事者。 美敗夏人，焚族部，城堡砦，未嘗擇便利，而所向輒勝； 所得祿賜，悉分與麾下，士亦樂為之用，推古良將，何以加此。 閻守恭慕郭進為人，而慷慨自效，起徒步至刺史，其志亦豈小哉。 孟元、劉謙、馬懷德、范恪皆經略西鄙，數戰有功。 其初起自卒伍，而能練習民事、招輯散亡，不獨一武夫而已。 趙振挽強命中，精曉兵機。 塞門之敗，振擁兵不救，何獨暗於此邪？ 」子珣年少習書史，閱武技，用兵以來，人以為無如珣者。 籠竿一戰，西人奔走不暇，從容而拔劉滬於死，英風義烈，何可少哉！ 葛懷敏以不用珣計而取敗，珣亦力戰而沒，惜哉！ 安俊、向寶無多戰功，夏人皆識其名而畏之。 張忠區區，較之諸人，未可同日語也。
The commentators say: Yu Zhaomin, Gao Hua, and Zhou Mei were all men who had served the frontier with distinction. At Shuzhou, Hua used accumulated army timber to stem flooding, and he also reversed wrongful convictions and saved men from execution—a warrior who understood civilian affairs. Mei defeated the Xia, burned tribal settlements and forts, never chose the easy assignment, yet prevailed wherever he went; he gave all his salary and rewards to his men, who gladly followed him. Compared with the great generals of antiquity, what more could be asked? Yan Shougong admired Guo Jin and devoted himself generously. Rising from the ranks to prefect, his ambition was surely no small thing. Meng Yuan, Liu Qian, Ma Huaide, and Fan Ke all served on the western frontier and won distinction in repeated battles. Though they rose from the ranks, they could handle civilian affairs and resettle the displaced—not mere fighting men alone. Zhao Zhen was unmatched with the heavy bow and finely versed in military strategy. Yet at the defeat at Saimen he held back his troops and did not rescue the garrison—why was he alone so blind in that matter? His son Xun studied history and the classics in his youth and trained in martial arts. Since the war began, people considered him without equal. At the battle of Longgan the westerners fled in disorder, yet he calmly rescued Liu Hu from death. His heroic spirit and righteous daring—how could one slight such a man! Ge Huaimin was defeated because he ignored Xun's counsel, and Xun too fought to the end and perished. What a pity! An Jun and Xiang Bao won few great victories, yet the Xia all knew their names and feared them. Zhang Zhong was a minor figure who cannot be mentioned in the same breath as these men.
