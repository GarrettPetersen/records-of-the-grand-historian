1
唐制，取士之科，多因隋舊，然其大要有三。 由學館者曰生徒，由州縣者曰鄉貢，皆升於有司而進退之。 其科之目，有秀才，有明經，有俊士，有進士，有明法，有明字，有明算，有一史，有三史，有開元禮，有道舉，有童子。 而明經之別，有五經，有三經，有二經，有學究一經，有三禮，有三傳，有史科。 此歲舉之常選也。 其天子自詔者曰制舉，所以待非常之才焉。
Tang rules for selecting officials drew mostly on Sui practice, but the system rested on three broad paths. Candidates from state schools were termed academy pupils; those nominated by prefectures and counties were village tribute entrants. Both were sent up to the responsible agencies, which decided who advanced and who was turned back. The examination subjects included Xiucai, Mingjing, Junshi, Jinshi, Mingfa (law), Mingzi (writing), Mingsuan (mathematics), single-history and triple-history tracks, Kaiyuan Rituals, Daoju (Daoist recommendation), and the child-prodigy Tongzi examination. The Mingjing track in turn subdivided into five classics, three classics, two classics, single-classic specialist study, the three rites, the three commentaries, and the history examination. These formed the routine annual recruitment cycle. Examinations proclaimed directly by the emperor were called zhiju (special summons), reserved for candidates of exceptional ability.
2
凡學六，皆隸於國子監：國子學，生三百人，以文武三品以上子孫若從二品以上曾孫及勳官二品、縣公、京官四品帶三品勳封之子為之； 太學，生五百人，以五品以上子孫、職事官五品期親若三品曾孫及勳官三品以上有封之子為之； 四門學，生千三百人，其五百人以勳官三品以上無封、四品有封及文武七品以上子為之，八百人以庶人之俊異者為之； 律學，生五十人，書學，生三十人，算學，生三十人，以八品以下子及庶人之通其學者為之。 京都學生八十人，大都督、中都督府、上州各六十人，下都督府、中州各五十人，下州四十人，京縣五十人，上縣四十人，中縣、中下縣各三十五人，下縣二十人。 國子監生，尚書省補，祭酒統焉。 州縣學生，州縣長官補，長史主焉。
Six schools in all fell under the Directorate of Education. The National University enrolled three hundred students: descendants of civil or military officials of third rank and above, great-grandsons in the collateral line of second rank and above, and sons of second-rank merit nobles, county dukes, and fourth-rank capital officials who carried third-rank merit titles and fiefs. The Imperial College took five hundred students from descendants of fifth-rank officials and above, close relatives of fifth-rank active-duty officials or great-grandsons of third rank, and sons of enfeoffed merit officials of third rank and higher. The Four Gates School enrolled thirteen hundred: five hundred were sons of unenfeoffed third-rank merit nobles, enfeoffed fourth-rank officials, or civil and military officials of seventh rank and above; eight hundred were exceptionally talented commoners. The Law School had fifty students; the Calligraphy and Mathematics Schools thirty each—sons of officials below eighth rank and commoners who had mastered those fields. Enrollment quotas ran: eighty in the capital district; sixty each in major and middle area commands and upper prefectures; fifty in lesser commands and middle prefectures; forty in lower prefectures; fifty in capital counties; forty in upper counties; thirty-five in middle and lower-middle counties; twenty in lower counties. Directorate students were appointed through the Department of State Affairs and placed under the Chancellor of the Directorate. Prefectural and county students were enrolled by local chiefs and supervised by their chief secretaries.
3
凡館二：門下省有弘文館，生三十人； 東宮有崇文館，生二十人。 以皇緦麻以上親，皇太后、皇后大功以上親，宰相及散官一品、功臣身食實封者、京官職事從三品、中書黃門侍郎之子為之。
Two academies stood apart: the Hongwen Academy under the Secretariat, with thirty students; and the Chongwen Academy in the Eastern Palace, with twenty. Students were sons of imperial relatives within the zimao mourning circle, relatives of the empress dowager and empress within the dagong circle, chancellors and first-rank honorary officials, merit nobles on substantive fiefs, active third-rank capital officials, and sons of vice directors of the Secretariat and Chancellery.
4
凡博士、助教，分經授諸生，未終經者無易業。 凡生，限年十四以上，十九以下； 律學十八以上，二十五以下。
Erudites and assistants taught the classics by division; students could not switch subjects before completing a classic. Ordinary students had to be between fourteen and nineteen; law students from eighteen through twenty-five.
5
凡《禮記》、《春秋左氏傳》為大經，《詩》、《周禮》、《儀禮》為中經，《易》、《尚書》、《春秋公羊傳》、《谷梁傳》為小經。 通二經者，大經、小經各一，若中經二。 通三經者，大經、中經、小經各一。 通五經者，大經皆通，餘經各一，《孝經》、《論語》皆兼通之。 凡治《孝經》、《論語》共限一歲，《尚書》、《公羊傳》、《谷梁傳》各一歲半，《易》、《詩》、《周禮》、《儀禮》各二歲，《禮記》、《左氏傳》各三歲。 學書，日紙一幅，間習時務策，讀《國語》、《說文》、《字林》、《三蒼》、《爾雅》。 凡書學，石經三體限三歲，《說文》二歲，《字林》一歲。 凡算學，《孫子》、《五曹》共限一歲，《九章》、《海島》共三歲，《張丘建》、《夏侯陽》各一歲，《周髀》、《五經算》共一歲，《綴術》四歲，《緝古》三歲，《記遺》、《三等數》皆兼習之。
The Record of Rites and Zuo's Spring and Autumn Commentary ranked as major classics; the Odes, Rites of Zhou, and Etiquette and Rites as middle classics; the Changes, Documents, and the Gongyang and Guliang commentaries as minor classics. Mastery of two classics meant one major and one minor, or two middle classics. Three classics required one major, one middle, and one minor. Five classics meant all major texts plus one from each remaining group, with both the Classic of Filial Piety and the Analects included. The Filial Piety Classic and Analects together took one year; Documents, Gongyang, and Guliang one and a half each; Changes, Odes, Rites of Zhou, and Etiquette two each; Record of Rites and Zuo Commentary three each. Calligraphy students copied one sheet a day, practiced policy essays on current affairs from time to time, and studied the Discourses of the States, Shuowen, Zilin, Sancang, and Erya. Calligraphy training allotted three years for the three stone-classic scripts, two for the Shuowen, and one for the Zilin. Mathematics curricula ran: Master Sun and Five Offices, one year together; Nine Chapters and Sea Island, three; Zhang Qiujian and Xiahou Yang, one each; Zhou Bi and Five Classics Mathematics, one together; Continuation Methods, four; Collation of Antiquity, three—with Memorial of Remainders and Third-Rank Numbers studied alongside throughout.
6
旬給假一日。 前假，博士考試，讀者千言試一帖，帖三言，講者二千言問大義一條，總三條通二為第，不及者有罰。 歲終，通一年之業，口問大義十條，通八為上，六為中，五為下。 並三下與在學九歲、律生六歲不堪貢者罷歸。 諸學生通二經、俊士通三經已及第而願留者，四門學生補太學，太學生補國子學。 每歲五月有田假，九月有授衣假，二百里外給程。 其不帥教及歲中違程滿三十日，事故百日，緣親病二百日，皆罷歸。 既罷，條其狀下之屬所，五品以上子孫送兵部，准廕配色。
Students received one day off every ten-day cycle. Before holidays, erudites tested students: readers faced one character-slip per thousand characters read (three characters per slip); lecturers one question on major principles per two thousand characters. Passing two of three questions counted as success; failure brought punishment. At year-end, after completing the annual curriculum, students answered ten oral questions on major principles: eight passes ranked upper, six middle, five lower. Students with three lower grades, or who after nine years (six for law students) still could not qualify for presentation, were dismissed. Students who had passed two classics, or Junshi who passed three and wished to stay on, advanced from Four Gates to Imperial College, and from Imperial College to the National University. Each year May brought field-work leave and September garment leave; students living more than two hundred li away received travel time. Students who defied instruction, or were absent thirty days without leave, one hundred for official duties, or two hundred for a parent's illness, were dismissed. On dismissal, their records went to the appropriate office; descendants of fifth rank and above were referred to the Ministry of War for hereditary privilege and uniform-color assignment.
7
每歲仲冬，州、縣、館、監舉其成者送之尚書省； 而舉選不繇館、學者，謂之鄉貢，皆懷牒自列於州、縣。 試已，長吏以鄉飲酒禮，會屬僚，設賓主，陳俎豆，備管弦，牲用少牢，歌《鹿鳴》之詩，因與耆艾敘長少焉。 既至省，皆疏名列到，結款通保及所居，始由戶部集閱，而關於考功員外郎試之。
Each midwinter, prefectures, counties, academies, and the Directorate presented their graduates to the Department of State Affairs; candidates who did not come through schools were village tribute entrants, each presenting credentials at their prefecture or county. After the tests, local chiefs held the village drinking rite with their staff—host and guest seated, offerings set out, music provided, a lesser pen sacrifice, the "Deer Cry" sung, and elders and juniors seated by age. At the capital, candidates registered by memorial, filed mutual guarantees and addresses, passed Revenue Ministry review, and were examined by the Vice Director of Personnel.
8
凡秀才，試方略策五道，以文理通粗為上上、上中、上下、中上，凡四等為及第。 凡明經，先帖文，然後口試，經問大義十條，答時務策三道，亦為四等。 凡《開元禮》，通大義百條、策三道者，超資與官； 義通七十、策通二者，及第。 散、試官能通者，依正員。 凡三傳科，《左氏傳》問大義五十條，《公羊》、《谷梁傳》三十條，策皆三道，義通七以上、策通二以上為第，白身視五經，有出身及前資官視學究一經。 凡史科，每史問大義百條、策三道，義通七、策通二以上為第。 能通一史者，白身視五經、三傳，有出身及前資官視學究一經； 三史皆通者，獎擢之。 凡童子科，十歲以下能通一經及《孝經》、《論語》，卷誦文十，通者予官； 通七，予出身。 凡進士，試時務策五道、帖一大經，經、策全通為甲第； 策通四、帖過四以上為乙第。 凡明法，試律七條、令三條，全通為甲第，通八為乙第。 凡書學，先口試，通，乃墨試《說文》、《字林》二十條，通十八為第。 凡算學，錄大義本條為問答，明數造術，詳明術理，然後為通。 試《九章》三條、《海島》《孫子》《五曹》《張丘建》《夏侯陽》《周髀》《五經算》各一條，十通六，《記遺》、《三等數》帖讀十得九，為第。 試《綴術》、《輯古》，錄大義為問答者，明數造術，詳明術理，無注者合數造術，不失義理，然後為通。 《綴術》七條、《輯古》三條，十通六，《記遺》、《三等數》帖讀十得九，為第。 落經者，雖通六，不第。
Xiucai candidates answered five policy-strategy questions; four grades from upper-upper through middle-upper, judged on literary clarity, counted as passing. Mingjing candidates first took text-slips, then oral exams: ten questions on classical principles and three policy essays, again graded in four ranks. In Kaiyuan Rituals, passing one hundred principle questions and three policy essays brought extraordinary promotion and appointment; seventy principles and two policy essays sufficed to pass. Honorary and probationary officials who passed were treated like regular appointees. The three-commentaries track tested fifty Zuo questions, thirty each on Gongyang and Guliang, plus three policy essays; seven-tenths on principles and two on policy passed. Commoners were ranked like five-classics graduates; those with credentials or prior office like single-classic specialists. The history track set one hundred principle questions and three policy essays per history; seven-tenths on principles and two on policy constituted a pass. Those who passed one history ranked commoners like five-classics or three-commentaries graduates, and credentialed candidates like single-classic specialists; those who passed all three histories received special commendation and advancement. The child-prodigy track admitted those under ten who mastered one classic plus the Filial Piety Classic and Analects, reciting ten passages—full passers received office; passing seven passages granted examination credentials. Jinshi candidates took five policy essays and one major-classic text-slip; passing both fully earned first class; four policy passes and more than four text-slip passes earned second class. Mingfa tested seven statute articles and three ordinance articles; all ten passed for first class, eight for second. Calligraphy candidates took an oral test first, then a written test on twenty Shuowen and Zilin items—eighteen passes constituted a grade. Mathematics exams recorded major principles as Q&A; candidates had to demonstrate numerical clarity, method construction, and thorough explanation of method—these together constituted a pass. Tests drew three Nine Chapters problems and one each from seven other texts; six of ten had to pass. Memorial of Remainders and Third-Rank Numbers required nine of ten text-slips correct for a grade. Continuation Methods and Collation of Antiquity required Q&A on major principles, clear numeracy and method-building, and sound reasoning—even without commentaries, methods had to be constructed correctly. Seven Continuation Methods and three Collation of Antiquity items were tested—six of ten to pass; nine of ten text-slips on the other two texts also sufficed. Candidates who failed the classics section did not pass even if they cleared six other items.
9
凡弘文、崇文生，試一大經、一小經，或二中經，或《史記》、《前後漢書》、《三國志》各一，或時務策五道。 經史皆試策十道。 經通六，史及時務策通三，皆帖《孝經》、《論語》共十條通六，為第。
Hongwen and Chongwen students took one major and one minor classic, two middle classics, one volume each from four standard histories, or five policy essays. Classic and history tracks both required ten policy questions. A pass required six classic questions, three history or policy questions, and six of ten combined Filial Piety and Analects text-slips.
10
凡貢舉非其人者、廢舉者、校試不以實者，皆有罰。
Officials who presented unfit candidates, failed to present qualified ones, or graded exams dishonestly faced punishment.
11
其教人取士著於令者，大略如此。 而士之進取之方，與上之好惡、所以育材養士、招來獎進之意，有司選士之法，因時增損不同。
Statutory rules for educating talent and selecting officials followed this general outline. Yet how scholars advanced, what emperors favored, how the court meant to cultivate and recruit talent, and how officials selected candidates—all shifted with the times.
12
自高祖初入長安，開大丞相府，下令置生員，自京師至於州縣皆有數。 既即位，又詔秘書外省別立小學，以教宗室子孫及功臣子弟。 其後又詔諸州明經、秀才、俊士、進士明于理體為鄉里稱者，縣考試，州長重覆，歲隨方物入貢； 吏民子弟學藝者，皆送於京學，為設考課之法。 州、縣、鄉皆置學焉。 及太宗即位，益崇儒術。 乃于門下別置弘文館，又增置書、律學，進士加讀經、史一部。 十三年，東宮置崇文館。 自天下初定，增築學舍至千二百區，雖七營飛騎，亦置生，遣博士為授經。 四夷若高麗、百濟、新羅、高昌、吐蕃，相繼遣子弟入學，遂至八千餘人。
When Gaozu first entered Chang'an, he opened the Grand Chancellor's Office and ordered student quotas for the capital and every prefecture and county. After his enthronement, he ordered a separate elementary school at the outer Secretariat for imperial clansmen and sons of meritorious officials. He later ordered prefectures to examine locally renowned Mingjing, Xiucai, Junshi, and Jinshi candidates at the county level, with prefects re-examining them and sending them to court each year with regional tribute; officials' sons and commoners studying the arts were sent to capital schools with formal examination rules. Schools were set up in prefectures, counties, and townships throughout the realm. When Taizong succeeded, he elevated Confucian learning still further. He established the Hongwen Academy under the Secretariat, added calligraphy and law schools, and required Jinshi candidates to study an extra classic or history. In his thirteenth year, the Eastern Palace gained the Chongwen Academy. After the empire was pacified, school buildings rose to twelve hundred; even the seven flying-cavalry camps enrolled students and received erudites to teach the classics. States such as Koguryŏ, Paekche, Silla, Gaochang, and Tibet sent princes and sons to study in succession, until enrollment exceeded eight thousand.
13
高宗永徽二年，始停秀才科。 龍朔二年，東都置國子監，明年以書學隸蘭台，算學隸秘閣，律學隸詳刑。 上元二年，加試貢士《老子》策，明經二條，進士三條。 國子監置大成二十人，取已及第而聰明者為之。 試書日誦千言，並日試策，所業十通七，然後補其祿俸，同直官。 通四經業成，上于尚書，吏部試之，登第者加一階放選。 其不第則習業如初，三歲而又試，三試而不中第，從常調。
In Yonghui year 2 of Gaozong, the Xiucai examination was abolished. In Longshuo year 2, a Directorate of Education was set up in the eastern capital; the next year calligraphy moved under the Orchid Terrace, mathematics under the Secret Archive, and law under Detailed Punishments. In Shangyuan year 2, tribute candidates faced added Laozi policy questions—two for Mingjing, three for Jinshi. The Directorate appointed twenty Senior Accomplished Scholars from examination graduates who showed exceptional ability. They recited one thousand characters daily in calligraphy tests and took daily policy exams; passing seven of ten subjects earned salary supplementation equal to duty officers. After mastering four classics, they were reported to the Department of State Affairs; the Ministry of Personnel examined them, and passers gained one rank and release for appointment. Failures resumed study; after three years they were retested; three failures sent them into regular bureaucratic rotation.
14
永隆二年，考功員外郎劉思立建言，明經多抄義條，進士唯誦舊策，皆亡實才，而有司以人數充第。 乃詔自今明經試帖粗十得六以上，進士試雜文二篇，通文律者然後試策。
In Yonglong year 2, Vice Director Liu Sili warned that Mingjing candidates copied textbook entries, Jinshi memorized old essays—neither showed real ability—yet examiners filled quotas by head count. The court ordered Mingjing candidates to pass six of ten text-slips, Jinshi to submit two belles-lettres pieces and take policy exams only after passing literary standards.
15
武后之亂，改易舊制頗多。 中宗反正，詔宗室三等以下、五等以上未出身，願宿衛及任國子生，聽之。 其家居業成而堪貢者，宗正寺試，送監舉如常法。 三衛番下日，願入學者，聽附國子學、太學及律館習業。 蕃王及可汗子孫願入學者，附國子學讀書。
Under Empress Wu, the old examination system was altered extensively. After Zhongzong's restoration, imperial clansmen of third rank and below or fifth rank and above without credentials could serve as palace guards or enroll as Directorate students if they wished. Clansmen who completed home study and qualified for presentation were examined by the Imperial Clan Court and sent to the Directorate by the usual process. When the Three Guards rotated off duty, guards who wished to study could attach to the National University, Imperial College, or law schools. Sons of foreign kings and khans who wished to study enrolled at the National University.
16
玄宗開元五年，始令鄉貢明經、進士見訖，國子監謁先師，學官開講問義，有司為具食，清資五品以上官及朝集使皆往閱禮焉。 七年，又令弘文、崇文、國子生季一朝參。 及注《老子道德經》成，詔天下家藏其書，貢舉人滅《尚書》、《論語》策，而加試《老子》。 又敕州縣學生年二十五以下、八品子若庶人二十一以下通一經及未通經而聰悟有文辭、史學者，入四門學為俊士。 即諸州貢舉省試不第，願入學者亦聽。
In Kaiyuan year 5, Xuanzong required village-tribute Mingjing and Jinshi, after their audience, to worship the sage at the Directorate while erudites lectured on principles; officials provided a feast, and fifth-rank pure-capital officials and assembly envoys attended the ceremony. In year 7, Hongwen, Chongwen, and Directorate students were required to attend court once per season. When Xuanzong finished his commentary on the Daodejing, he ordered every household to keep a copy, reduced Documents and Analects questions for tribute candidates, and added Laozi to the exams. He also ordered prefectural students under twenty-five, and eighth-rank sons or commoners under twenty-one who had mastered one classic—or showed literary and historical talent without a full classic—to enter Four Gates as Junshi. Candidates who failed the provincial examination but wished to study were also admitted.
17
二十四年，考功員外郎李昂為舉人詆訶，帝以員外郎望輕，遂移貢舉於禮部，以侍郎主之。 禮部選士自此始。
In year 24, candidates abused Vice Director Li Ang; deeming that post too low in rank, the emperor transferred examinations to the Ministry of Rites under a vice minister. From this point, the Ministry of Rites oversaw civil examinations.
18
二十九年，始置崇玄學，習《老子》、《莊子》、《文子》、《列子》，亦曰道舉。 其生，京、都各百人，諸州無常員。 官秩、廕第同國子，舉送、課試如明經。
In year 29, the Chongxuan School was founded for Laozi, Zhuangzi, Wenzi, and Liezi—the Daoist recommendation track. Enrollment was one hundred in each capital; prefectures had no fixed quota. Rank and hereditary privilege matched the National University; presentation and testing followed Mingjing rules.
19
天寶九載，置廣文館于國學，以領生徒為進士者。 舉人舊重兩監，後世祿者以京兆、同、華為榮，而不入學。 十二載，乃敕天下罷鄉貢，舉人不由國子及郡、縣學者，勿舉送。 是歲，道舉停《老子》，加《周易》。 十四載，復鄉貢。
In Tianbao year 9, the Guangwen Academy was set up within the state schools to supervise Jinshi candidates. Candidates once prized enrollment in the two directorates, but later office-seekers took prestige from Jingzhao, Tong, and Hua and skipped school entirely. In year 12, the court suspended village tribute nationwide and barred presentation of candidates who had not come through the Directorate or local schools. That year the Daoist track dropped Laozi and added the Book of Changes. In year 14, village tribute was restored.
20
代宗廣德二年，詔曰：「古者設太學，教胄子，雖年穀不登，兵革或動，而俎豆之事不廢。 頃年戎車屢駕，諸生輟講，宜追學生在館習業，度支給廚米。 」是歲，賈至為侍郎，建言歲方艱歉，舉人赴省者，兩都試之。 兩都試人自此始。
In Guangde year 2, Daizong decreed: "Antiquity established the Imperial College to educate imperial heirs; even when harvests failed or war broke out, sacrificial rites were not abandoned." In recent years repeated campaigns had halted instruction; students should return to the academies, with the Revenue Department supplying grain for their kitchens. " That year Vice Minister Jia Zhi proposed that, given famine conditions, candidates bound for the capital be examined in both capitals instead. Dual-capital examinations began at this point.
21
貞元二年，詔習《開元禮》者舉同一經例，明經習律以代《爾雅》。 是時弘文、崇文生未補者，務取員闕以補，速於登第，而用廕乖實，至有假市門資、變易昭穆及假人試藝者。 六年，詔宜據式考試，假代者論如法。 初，禮部侍郎親故移試考功，謂之別頭。 十六年，中書舍人高郢奏罷，議者是之。
In Zhenyuan year 2, Kaiyuan Rituals candidates followed single-classic presentation rules, and Mingjing students studied law codes instead of the Erya. Unplaced Hongwen and Chongwen students rushed to fill vacant slots and pass quickly; hereditary privilege was abused—some bought credentials, falsified lineage, or sent proxies to take exams. In year 6, the court required exams to follow regulations and punished proxy test-takers by law. Originally, the vice minister's kin and associates were examined separately by Personnel—called the "separate head" examination. In year 16, Secretariat Drafter Gao Ying petitioned to abolish it, and critics agreed.
22
元和二年，置東都監生一百員。 然自天寶後，學校益廢，生徒流散。 永泰中，雖置西監生，而館無定員。 於是始定生員：西京國子館生八十人，太學七十人，四門三百人，廣文六十人，律館二十人，書、算館各十人； 東都國子館十人，太學十五人，四門五十人，廣文十人，律館十人，書館三人，算館二人而已。 明經停口義，復試墨義十條。 五經取通五，明經通六。 其嘗坐法及為州縣小吏，雖藝文可采，勿舉。 十三年，權知禮部侍郎庾承宣奏復考功別頭試。
In Yuanhe year 2, the eastern capital received one hundred Directorate student slots. After Tianbao, however, schools fell into disuse and students dispersed. During Yongtai, western-capital Directorate students were added, but academies still lacked fixed quotas. Quotas were then fixed for the western capital: National University 80, Imperial College 70, Four Gates 300, Guangwen 60, Law 20, Calligraphy and Mathematics 10 each; the eastern capital had only 10, 15, 50, 10, 10, 3, and 2 respectively. Mingjing dropped oral interpretation and restored ten written principle questions. Five-classics candidates had to pass five; Mingjing candidates six. Candidates with criminal records or service as petty local officials were not presented, however talented their writing. In year 13, Acting Vice Minister Yu Chengxuan restored the Personnel separate-track exam.
23
初，開元中，禮部考試畢，送中書門下詳覆，其後中廢。 是歲，侍郎錢徽所舉送，覆試多不中選，由是貶官，而舉人雜文復送中書門下。 長慶三年，侍郎王起言：「故事，禮部已放榜，而中書門下始詳覆。 今請先詳覆，而後放榜。 」議者以起雖避嫌，然失貢職矣。 諫議大夫殷侑言：「《三史》為書，勸善懲惡，亞於《六經》。 比來史學都廢，至有身處班列，而朝廷舊章莫能知者。 」於是立史科及三傳科。 大和三年，高鍇為考功員外郎，取士有不當，監察御史姚中立又奏停考功別頭試。 六年，侍郎賈餗又奏復之。 八年，宰相王涯以為「禮部取士，乃先以榜示中書，非至公之道。 自今一委有司，以所試雜文、鄉貫、三代名諱送中書門下」。
Under Kaiyuan, after Rites exams finished, results went to the Secretariat and Chancellery for review—later discontinued. That year Vice Minister Qian Hui's nominees mostly failed re-examination; he was demoted, and candidates' essays again went to the Secretariat and Chancellery. In Changqing year 3, Vice Minister Wang Qi said: "By precedent, Rites posted results first, and only then did the Secretariat and Chancellery review them." "I ask that review come first and posting afterward." " Critics said Wang Qi avoided conflict of interest but neglected his duty to present talent. Remonstrance Grandee Yin You said: "The Three Histories encourage virtue and punish vice, ranking just below the Six Classics." Historical study has lapsed so far that officials in court ranks no longer know the dynasty's own precedents." " The history and three-commentaries examinations were then established. In Dahe year 3, Vice Director Gao Kai selected candidates improperly; Censor Yao Zhongli again petitioned to end the Personnel separate-track exam. In year 6, Vice Minister Jia Su restored it again. In year 8, Chancellor Wang Ya argued that showing Rites examination results to the Secretariat first was not fully fair. "Henceforth responsible offices alone should send examined essays, native place, and three generations of names and taboos to the Secretariat and Chancellery."
24
大抵眾科之目，進士尤為貴，其得人亦最為盛焉。 方其取以辭章，類若浮文而少實； 及其臨事設施，奮其事業，隱然為國名臣者，不可勝數，遂使時君篤意，以謂莫此之尚。 及其後世，俗益媮薄，上下交疑，因以謂按其聲病，可以為有司之責，舍是則汗漫而無所守，遂不復能易。 嗚呼，乃知三代鄉里德行之舉，非至治之隆莫能行也。 太宗時，冀州進士張昌齡、王公謹有名于當時，考功員外郎王師旦不署以第。 太宗問其故，對曰：「二人者，皆文采浮華，擢之將誘後生而弊風俗。 」其後，二人者卒不能有立。
Among all examination tracks, Jinshi was the most prestigious and produced the greatest number of outstanding officials. When selected by literary composition alone, candidates seemed skilled in surface rhetoric but thin on substance; yet in office countless Jinshi graduates became renowned statesmen, so emperors came to regard no track as superior. Later ages grew lax; rulers and candidates grew suspicious of one another. Examiners clung to tonal rules as their only standard, fearing that without them selection would lack all discipline—and the system could no longer be reformed. Alas—only under the loftiest age of perfect governance could the Three Dynasties' village selection by moral conduct have been sustained. Under Taizong, the celebrated Jizhou Jinshi Zhang Changling and Wang Gongjin were denied grades by Vice Director Wang Shidan. Taizong asked why. Wang replied: "Both men write florid, ornamental prose. Promoting them would mislead later candidates and corrupt public morals." " In the end, neither man achieved lasting distinction.
25
寶應二年，禮部侍郎楊綰上疏言：「進士科起于隋大業中，是時猶試策。 高宗朝，劉思立加進士雜文，明經填帖，故為進士者皆誦當代之文，而不通經史，明經者但記帖括。 又投牒自舉，非古先哲王側席待賢之道。 請依古察孝廉，其鄉閭孝友、信義、廉恥而通經者，縣薦之州，州試其所通之學，送於省。 自縣至省，皆勿自投牒，其到狀、保辨、識牒皆停。 而所習經，取大義，聽通諸家之學。 每問經十條，對策三道，皆通，為上第，吏部官之； 經義通八，策通二，為中第，與出身； 下第，罷歸。 《論語》、《孝經》、《孟子》兼為一經，其明經、進士及道舉並停。」
In Baoying year 2, Vice Minister Yang Guan memorialized: "The Jinshi track began in Sui's Daye era, when candidates still answered policy questions." Under Gaozong, Liu Sili added belles-lettres for Jinshi and fill-in slips for Mingjing, so Jinshi candidates memorized contemporary essays without mastering classics, and Mingjing candidates memorized slip formulas alone. Self-nomination by credential is not how ancient sage-kings vacated their thrones to await worthy men. Restore the ancient Filial and Incorrupt investigation: men known in their villages for filial piety, friendship, faithfulness, righteousness, integrity, and classical learning should be recommended by counties, tested by prefectures, and sent to the capital. From county to capital, self-presentation should end; arrival memorials, guarantees, and identification papers should all be abolished. Classics should be tested on major principles, allowing mastery across schools of interpretation. Ten classic questions and three policy answers: full passage earned upper grade and appointment by the Ministry of Personnel; eight classics and two policy passes earned middle grade and examination credentials; lower grade meant dismissal. The Analects, Filial Piety Classic, and Mencius together should count as one classic; Mingjing, Jinshi, and the Daoist track should all be abolished."
26
詔給事中李棲筠、李廙、尚書左丞賈至、京兆尹兼御史大夫嚴武議。 棲筠等議曰：
The emperor ordered Attendants Li Qiyun and Li Yi, Assistant Director Jia Zhi, and Jingzhao Intendant Yan Wu to deliberate. Li Qiyun and his colleagues replied:
27
「夏之政忠，商之政敬，周之政文，然則文與忠敬皆統人行。 且諡號述行，莫美于文，文興則忠敬存焉。 故前代以文取士，本文行也，由辭觀行，則及辭焉。 宣父稱顏子「不遷怒，不貳過」，謂之「好學」。 今試學者以帖字為精通，不窮旨義，豈能知遷怒、貳過之道乎？ 考文者以聲病為是非，豈能知移風易俗化天下乎？ 是以上失其源，下襲其流，先王之道莫能行也。 夫先王之道消，則小人之道長，亂臣賊子由是生焉！ 今取士試之小道，而不以遠大，是猶以蝸蚓之餌垂海，而望吞舟之魚，不亦難乎？ 所以食垂餌者皆小魚，就科目者皆小藝。 且夏有天下四百載，禹之道喪而商始興； 商有天下六百祀，湯之法棄而周始興； 周有天下八百年，文、武之政廢而秦始並焉。 三代之選士任賢，皆考實行，是以風俗淳一，運祚長遠。 漢興，監其然，尊儒術，尚名節，雖近戚竊位，強臣擅權，弱主外立，母后專政，而亦能終彼四百，豈非學行之效邪？ 魏、晉以來，專尚浮侈，德義不修，故子孫速顛，享國不永也。 今綰所請，實為正論。 然自晉室之亂，南北分裂，人多僑處，必欲復古鄉舉裏選，竊恐未盡。 請兼廣學校，以明訓誘。 雖京師州縣皆有小學，兵革之後，生徒流離，儒臣、師氏，祿廩無向。 請增博士員，厚其稟稍，選通儒碩生，間居其職。 十道大郡，置太學館，遣博士出外，兼領郡官，以教生徒。 保桑梓者，鄉里舉焉； 在流寓者，庠序推焉。 朝而行之，夕見其利。」
"Xia government embodied sincerity, Shang reverence, Zhou culture—culture, sincerity, and reverence together govern human conduct." Posthumous titles describe a life's conduct, and nothing surpasses culture; when culture flourishes, sincerity and reverence endure. Former dynasties selected officials through literary culture because culture reflects conduct; to read conduct through words is to value the words themselves. Confucius praised Yan Hui for "not transferring anger, not repeating the same fault," and called this true love of learning. Today students are tested on memorized text-slips without probing meaning—how can they grasp not transferring anger or repeating faults? Examiners judge essays by tonal rules—how can they identify those who would transform customs and civilize the realm? Superiors have lost the source, subordinates follow the drift, and the way of the ancient kings cannot be practiced. When the way of the ancient kings fades, petty men flourish—and rebellious ministers and unfilial sons arise! Testing petty skills instead of great capacity is like dangling worm bait over the ocean and expecting leviathans—is that not futile? Only small fish take dangling bait; only petty skills win examination categories. Xia ruled four hundred years; when Yu's way faded, Shang rose; Shang endured six hundred years; when Tang's methods were abandoned, Zhou rose; Zhou lasted eight hundred years; when the governments of Wen and Wu collapsed, Qin united the realm. The Three Dynasties selected talent by examining real conduct, so customs stayed pure and dynasties endured. Han observed this lesson, honored Confucian learning, and prized integrity. Though kin usurped power, strong ministers dominated, weak emperors were installed, and empress dowagers ruled, the dynasty still lasted four centuries—is that not the fruit of learning and moral conduct? From Wei and Jin onward, extravagance alone was prized and virtue neglected, so dynasties fell swiftly and ruled briefly. Yang Guan's proposal is fundamentally sound. Yet since Jin's collapse and the north-south split, many people live far from home; restoring ancient village and district selection may not be fully feasible. We ask instead to expand schools and strengthen instruction. Though elementary schools exist in capital and provinces alike, war has scattered students and left teachers without salaries or supplies. Increase erudite posts, enrich stipends, and appoint distinguished Confucian scholars to fill them in rotation. In major prefectures of the ten circuits, establish Imperial College halls, send erudites to teach while concurrently holding prefectural office. Those who remain in their home districts should be recommended locally; those living abroad should be nominated by their schools. Adopt this in the morning and see its benefit by evening."
28
而大臣以為舉人循習，難於速變，請自來歲始。 帝以問翰林學士，對曰：「舉進士久矣，廢之恐失其業。 」乃詔明經、進士與孝廉兼行。
Senior ministers argued that candidates were too entrenched in current practice for sudden change and asked to begin the following year. The emperor asked Hanlin academicians, who replied: "The Jinshi track has existed for generations; abolishing it would deprive many of their livelihood." " The court ordered Mingjing, Jinshi, and Filial and Incorrupt tracks to continue side by side.
29
先是，進士試詩、賦及時務策五道，明經策三道。 建中二年，中書舍人趙贊權知貢舉，乃以箴、論、表、贊代詩、賦，而皆試策三道。 大和八年，禮部復罷進士議論，而試詩、賦。 文宗從內出題以試進士，謂侍臣曰：「吾患文格浮薄，昨自出題，所試差勝。 」乃詔禮部歲取登第者三十人，苟無其人，不必充其數。 是時，文宗好學嗜古，鄭覃以經術位宰相，深嫉進士浮薄，屢請罷之。 文宗曰：「敦厚浮薄，色色有之，進士科取人二百年矣，不可遽廢。 」因得不罷。
Previously Jinshi took poetry, rhapsody, and five policy questions; Mingjing took three. In Jianzhong year 2, Acting Presentation Overseer Zhao Zan replaced poetry and rhapsody with admonitions, discourses, memorials, and encomia, while all candidates took three policy questions. In Dahe year 8, Rites again dropped Jinshi discourses and restored poetry and rhapsody. Wenzong set Jinshi topics himself from within the palace and told his ministers: "I worry that literary standards have grown shallow; when I set the topics yesterday, the results were somewhat better." " He ordered Rites to pass thirty Jinshi each year, without filling the quota if qualified candidates were lacking. Wenzong loved learning and antiquity; Chancellor Zheng Tan, a classicist, despised the Jinshi track's shallowness and repeatedly petitioned to abolish it. Wenzong replied: "Integrity and shallowness appear in every walk of life. The Jinshi track has selected talent for two centuries—it cannot be abolished overnight." " The track was therefore spared.
30
武宗即位，宰相李德裕尤惡進士。 初，舉人既及第，綴行通名，詣主司第謝。 其制，序立西階下，北上東向； 主人席東階下。 西向； 諸生拜，主司答拜； 乃敘齒，謝恩，遂升階，與公卿觀者皆坐； 酒數行，乃赴期集。 又有曲江會、題名席。 至是，德裕奏：「國家設科取士，而附黨背公，自為門生。 自今一見有司而止，其期集、參謁、曲江題名皆罷。 」德裕嘗論公卿子弟艱于科舉，武宗曰：「向聞楊虞卿兄弟朋比貴勢，妨平進之路。 昨黜楊知至、鄭樸等，抑其太甚耳。 有司不識朕意，不放子弟，即過矣，但取實藝可也。 」德裕曰：「鄭肅、封敖子弟皆有才，不敢應舉。 臣無名第，不當非進士。 然臣祖天寶末以仕進無他岐，勉強隨計，一舉登第。 自後家不置《文選》，蓋惡其不根藝實。 然朝廷顯官，須公卿子弟為之。 何者？ 少習其業，目熟朝廷事，台閣之儀，不教而自成。 寒士縱有出人之才，固不能閑習也。 則子弟未易可輕。 」德裕之論，偏異蓋如此。 然進士科當唐之晚節，尤為浮薄，世所共患也。
When Wuzong succeeded, Chancellor Li Deyu especially despised the Jinshi track. After passing, candidates formed a procession, announced their names, and visited the chief examiner's home to give thanks. By regulation they lined up below the western steps, facing north and east; the host sat below the eastern steps. Facing west; students bowed and the examiner returned the bow; they then took seats by seniority, expressed gratitude, ascended the steps, and grandees and onlookers sat down; after several rounds of wine they went on to the scheduled gathering. There were also the Qujiang gathering and the inscription banquet. Li Deyu then memorialized: "The state established examinations to recruit talent, yet graduates attach to factions, betray the public interest, and form private patronage circles." Henceforth candidates were to visit the responsible offices once only; scheduled gatherings, courtesy calls, and Qujiang inscription banquets were all abolished. " Li Deyu once argued that sons of high officials struggled in the examinations. Wuzong replied: "I have heard that the Yang Yuqing brothers formed factions with powerful families and blocked fair advancement." I dismissed Yang Zhizhi, Zheng Pu, and others only to restrain excess, not to exclude all high-born candidates. If examiners misunderstand my intent and bar all officials' sons, that goes too far—they need only select genuine ability. " Li Deyu said: "The sons of Zheng Su and Feng Ao are talented but dare not sit for the examinations." I hold no examination degree myself and should not disparage the Jinshi track. Yet at the end of Tianbao my grandfather, with no other path to office, reluctantly took the examination and passed on the first try. Afterward our family kept no copy of the Wenxuan, despising its lack of grounding in real learning. Yet prominent court offices must be filled by sons of grandees and ministers. Why? From youth they practice statecraft, grow familiar with court affairs and terrace etiquette, and master protocol without formal instruction. Poor scholars, however gifted, cannot acquire such familiarity in their spare time. Officials' sons therefore should not be lightly dismissed. " Li Deyu's argument was partial and idiosyncratic in roughly this way. Yet in late Tang the Jinshi track had grown especially shallow—an affliction universally lamented.
31
所謂制舉者，其來遠矣。 自漢以來，天子常稱制詔道其所欲問而親策之。 唐興，世崇儒學，雖其時君賢愚好惡不同，而樂善求賢之意未始少怠，故自京師外至州縣，有司常選之士，以時而舉。 而天子又自詔四方德行、才能、文學之士，或高蹈幽隱與其不能自達者，下至軍謀將略、翹關拔山、絕藝奇伎莫不兼取。 其為名目，隨其人主臨時所欲，而列為定科者，如賢良方正、直言極諫、博通墳典達於教化、軍謀宏遠堪任將率、詳明政術可以理人之類，其名最著。 而天子巡狩、行幸、封禪太山梁父，往往會見行在，其所以待之之禮甚優，而宏材偉論非常之人亦時出於其間，不為無得也。
The zhiju (special summons) examination dates back to antiquity. Since Han times, emperors often issued edicts in their own name stating what they wished to ask and personally examined candidates. When Tang rose, the age honored Confucian learning; though emperors differed in wisdom and taste, the will to seek worthy men never slackened, and offices from the capital to every prefecture presented talent on schedule. Emperors also summoned by edict men of virtue, talent, and letters from every direction—including recluses who could not advance themselves—along with strategists, strongmen, and masters of rare skills. Categories varied with each emperor's wishes; fixed tracks included Filial and Incorrupt, Direct Remonstrance, Broad Mastery of the Classics, Grand Military Strategy, and Clarity in Governmental Arts—the best-known designations. When emperors toured the realm or performed feng and shan rites on Mount Tai and Liangfu, candidates often met them at the traveling palace and were treated with great honor; outstanding men of exceptional ability sometimes emerged—so the system was not without merit.
32
其外，又有武舉，蓋其起于武后之時。 長安二年，始置武舉。 其制，有長垛、馬射、步射、平射、筒射，又有馬槍、翹關、負重、身材之選。 翹關，長丈七尺，徑三寸半，凡十舉後，手持關距，出處無過一尺； 負重者，負米五斛，行二十步：皆為中第，亦以鄉飲酒禮送兵部。 其選用之法不足道，故不復書。
There was also a military examination track, which arose under Empress Wu. In Chang'an year 2, the military examination was formally established. Tests included long-range archery, mounted and foot archery, level and tube shooting, mounted lance, gate-lifting, weight-bearing, and physique assessment. In gate-lifting, candidates raised a pole one zhang seven chi long and three and a half cun thick; after ten lifts, the pole's rise and fall at the grip could not exceed one chi; in weight-bearing, carrying five hu of grain twenty paces—all counted as a pass, and candidates were sent to the Ministry of War with the village drinking ceremony. Their appointment procedures are not worth recounting and are therefore omitted.
