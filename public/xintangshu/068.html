756至德元載置淮南節度使，領揚、楚、滁、和、壽、廬、舒、光、蘄、安、黃、申、沔十三州，治揚州。 尋以光州隸淮西。
In 756, the first year of Zhide, a Huainan circuit military commissioner was created, commanding thirteen prefectures—Yang, Chu, Chu, He, Shou, Lu, Shu, Guang, Qi, An, Huang, Shen, and Mian—from his seat at Yang Prefecture. Shortly afterward Guang Prefecture was assigned to the Huaixi circuit.
50
757二載置劍南東川節度使，領梓、遂、綿、劍、龍、閬、普、陵、瀘、榮、資、簡十二州，治梓州。 置江東防禦使，治杭州。
In 757, the second year of Zhide, a Jiannan East Chuan military commissioner was appointed, commanding twelve prefectures—Zi, Sui, Mian, Jian, Long, Lang, Pu, Ling, Lu, Rong, Zi, and Jian—from his seat at Zi Prefecture. A Jiangdong defense commissioner was installed, headquartered at Hang Prefecture.
51
758乾元元年置浙江西道節度兼江寧軍使，領昇、潤、宣、歙、饒、江、蘇、常、杭、湖十州，治昇州，尋徙治蘇州，未幾，罷領宣、歙、饒三州，副使兼餘杭軍使，治杭州。 置浙江東道節度使，領越、睦、衢、婺、臺、明、處、溫八州，治越州。 改福建經略使為都防禦使兼寧海軍使。 置洪吉都防禦團練觀察處置使兼莫傜軍使，領洪、吉、虔、撫、袁五州，治洪州。 置宣歙饒觀察使，治宣州。
In 758, the first year of Qianyuan, a Zhejiang West circuit commissioner was created who also served as Jiangning Army commissioner, commanding ten prefectures—Sheng, Run, Xuan, She, Rao, Jiang, Su, Chang, Hang, and Hu—from his seat at Sheng Prefecture. The seat was soon moved to Su Prefecture; before long Xuan, She, and Rao were removed from his command, while the deputy commissioner took the Yuhang Army commissioner post at Hang Prefecture. A Zhejiang East circuit military commissioner was appointed, commanding eight prefectures—Yue, Mu, Qu, Wu, Tai, Ming, Chu, and Wen—from his seat at Yue Prefecture. The Fujian frontier commissioner was redesignated chief defense commissioner, concurrently serving as Ninghai Army commissioner. A Hong–Ji chief defense, training, observation, and disposal commissioner was installed who also served as Mo Yao Army commissioner, commanding Hong, Ji, Qian, Fu, and Yuan from his seat at Hong Prefecture. An Xuan–She–Rao observation commissioner was appointed, headquartered at Xuan Prefecture.
52
759二年劍南東川增領昌、渝、合三州。 沔州隸鄂岳節度，壽州隸淮西節度。 廢浙江西道節度使，置觀察處置都團練守捉及本道營田使，更領丹陽軍使，治蘇州，復領宣、歙、饒三州。 廢宣歙饒觀察使。 置鄂、岳、沔三州都團練守捉使，治鄂州。
In 759, the second year of Qianyuan, Jiannan East Chuan gained jurisdiction over Chang, Yu, and He prefectures. Mian Prefecture was assigned to the E–Yue circuit, and Shou Prefecture to the Huaixi circuit. The Zhejiang West circuit military commissioner was abolished and replaced by an observation, disposal, chief training, and garrison commissioner who also supervised the circuit's farming operations and held the Danyang Army commissioner post at Su Prefecture; Xuan, She, and Rao were restored to his command. The Xuan–She–Rao observation commissioner was discontinued. A chief training and garrison commissioner for E, Yue, and Mian prefectures was installed, headquartered at E Prefecture.
53
760上元元年升福建都防禦使為節度使。 洪吉觀察使增領信州。 岳州隸荊南節度。
In 760, the first year of Shangyuan, the Fujian chief defense commissioner was raised to military commissioner. The Hong–Ji observation commissioner gained jurisdiction over Xin Prefecture. Yue Prefecture was transferred to the Jingnan circuit.

761二年浙江西道觀察使徙治宣州，罷領昇州。 杭州刺史領防禦使。
In 761, the Zhejiang West observation commissioner relocated to Xuan Prefecture and relinquished Sheng Prefecture. The Hangzhou prefect also served as defense commissioner.

In 764, the East Chuan circuit was abolished and its fifteen prefectures were assigned to the West Chuan circuit. The Hong–Ji chief defense, training, and observation commissioner was retitled the Jiangnan West circuit.

In 765, the first year of Yongtai, Qi and Huang prefectures were transferred to the E–Yue circuit. The E Prefecture chief training commissioner was raised to observation commissioner, gaining jurisdiction over Yue, Qi, and Huang.

The Xuan–She–Chi chief training, garrison, observation, and disposal commissioner, also serving as Caishi Army commissioner, was restored.

In 767, the Jiannan East Chuan circuit was abolished and replaced by a chief defense and observation commissioner who also served as Jingrong Army commissioner at Sui Prefecture. Shortly afterward the military commissioner was restored, headquartered at Zi Prefecture.

781二年淮南節度增領泗州。 合浙江東、西二道觀察置節度使，治潤州，尋賜號鎮海軍節度。 廢浙江東道都團練觀察使，以所管州隸浙江西道。 省沔州。
In 781, the Huainan circuit gained jurisdiction over Si Prefecture. The Zhejiang East and West observation circuits were merged under a military commissioner at Run Prefecture, soon titled the Zhenhai Army circuit. The Zhejiang East chief training and observation commissioner was abolished and its prefectures assigned to Zhejiang West. Mian Prefecture was abolished.

The Zhejiang West chief training and observation commissioner was raised to Zhenhai Army military commissioner.

In 892, the first year of Jingfu, a Long–Jian military commissioner was appointed, commanding Long, Jian, Li, and Lang prefectures. The Hangzhou defense commissioner was titled Wusheng Army defense commissioner. The Xuan–She training commissioner was raised to Ningguo Army military commissioner.

In 893, the Wusheng Army defense commissioner was raised to chief training and observation commissioner for Su, Hang, and related prefectures, but was soon abolished. The Zhenhai Army military commissioner relocated to Hang Prefecture.
