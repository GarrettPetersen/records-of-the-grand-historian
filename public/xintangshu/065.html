756至德元載置河南節度使，治汴州，領郡十三：陳留、睢陽、靈昌、淮陽、汝陰、譙、濟陰、濮陽、淄川、琅邪、彭城、臨淮、東海。
In 756, the first year of Zhide, a Henan military commissioner was created, headquartered at Bian Prefecture, commanding thirteen commanderies—Chenliu, Suiyang, Lingchang, Huaiyang, Ruyin, Qiao, Jiyin, Puyang, Zichuan, Langya, Pengcheng, Linhuai, and Donghai.

置青密節度使，領北海、高密、東牟、東萊四郡，治北海郡。

757二載
757 CE, second year.
55
758乾元元年廢河南節度使，置汴州都防禦使，領州十三如故；
In 758, the first year of Qianyuan, the Henan military commissioner was abolished and replaced by a Bian Prefecture chief defense commissioner, still commanding the same thirteen prefectures;
56
尋以滑、濮二州隸青密節度，亳州隸淮西節度。
Before long Hua and Pu were assigned to the Qing–Mi circuit, and Bo Prefecture to Huaixi.
57
淮南西道節度徙治鄭州，增領陳、潁、亳三州，別置豫許汝節度使，治豫州。
The Huainan West circuit military commissioner moved his seat to Zheng Prefecture, gained Chen, Ying, and Bo, and a separate Yu–Xu–Ru military commissioner was installed at Yu Prefecture.
58
青密節度增領滑、濮二州。
The Qing–Mi circuit gained jurisdiction over Hua and Pu prefectures.
59
759二年廢汴州都防禦使，置汴滑節度使，治滑州，領州五：滑、濮、汴、曹、宋。
In 759, the second year of Zhide, the Bianzhou chief defense commissioner was abolished and replaced by a Bian–Hua military commissioner at Hua Prefecture, commanding Hua, Pu, Bian, Cao, and Song.
60
又置河南節度使，治徐州，領州五：徐、泗、海、亳、潁。
Another Henan military commissioner was appointed, with his seat at Xu Prefecture, commanding Xu, Si, Hai, Bo, and Ying.
61
未幾，潁州隸鄭陳節度，尋復領潁州。
Before long Ying Prefecture was transferred to the Zheng–Chen circuit; soon it was restored to Henan's command.
62
是年，又以濮州隸兗鄆節度，潁、亳二州隸鄭陳節度。
That same year Pu Prefecture was assigned to the Yan–Yun circuit, while Ying and Bo went to the Zheng–Chen circuit.
63
置鄭陳節度使，領鄭、陳、亳、潁四州，治鄭州，尋增領申、光、壽三州；
A Zheng–Chen military commissioner was created, commanding Zheng, Chen, Bo, and Ying from Zheng Prefecture; before long Shen, Guang, and Shou were added to his charge;
64
未幾，以三州隸淮西。
Before long those three prefectures were transferred to the Huaixi circuit.
65
廢淮南西道節度使，以陳、潁、亳隸陳鄭。
The Huainan West circuit military commissioner was abolished, and Chen, Ying, and Bo were assigned to the Chen–Zheng circuit.
66
是年，復置淮南西道節度使，領申、光、壽、安、沔、蘄、黃七州，治壽州。
That same year the Huainan West circuit military commissioner was reinstated, commanding seven prefectures—Shen, Guang, Shou, An, Mian, Qi, and Huang—from his seat at Shou Prefecture.
67
青密節度使增領淄、沂、海三州。
The Qing–Mi military commissioner gained jurisdiction over Zi, Yi, and Hai prefectures.
68
滑州隸汴滑節度使，濮州隸鄆齊兗節度使。
Hua Prefecture was assigned to the Bian–Hua military commissioner, and Pu Prefecture to the Yun–Qi–Yan military commissioner.
69
是年以海州隸汴滑節度。
That year Hai Prefecture was transferred to the Bian–Hua circuit.
70
升鄆、齊、兗三州都防禦使為節度使，治兗州，增領濮州，尋以濮州隸河南節度。
The Yun–Qi–Yan chief defense commissioner was raised to military commissioner at Yan Prefecture and given Pu Prefecture; before long Pu was transferred to the Henan circuit.
71
760上元元年以海州隸青密節度。
In 760, the first year of Shangyuan, Hai Prefecture was assigned to the Qing–Mi circuit.
72
海州復隸青密節度。
Hai Prefecture was restored to the Qing–Mi circuit.
73
761二年置滑衛節度使，治滑州，領州六：滑、衛、相、魏、德、貝。
In 761, the second year of Shangyuan, a Hua–Wei military commissioner was created at Hua Prefecture, commanding Hua, Wei, Xiang, Wei, De, and Bei.
74
尋以德州隸淄沂節度而增領博州。
Before long De Prefecture went to the Zi–Yi circuit while Bo Prefecture was added to Hua–Wei's command.
75
廢汴滑、河南二節度，以徐、泗、汴、宋、曹五州隸淮西節度，滑州隸滑衛節度。
The Bian–Hua and Henan circuits were abolished; Xu, Si, Bian, Song, and Cao were assigned to Huaixi, and Hua Prefecture to Hua–Wei.
76
廢鄭陳節度，以鄭、陳、亳、潁四州隸淮西。
The Zheng–Chen circuit was abolished, and Zheng, Chen, Bo, and Ying were transferred to Huaixi.
77
淮南西道節度使增領陳、鄭、潁、亳、汴、曹、宋、徐、泗九州，徙治安州，號淮西十六州節度使。
The Huainan West circuit military commissioner took command of nine more prefectures—Chen, Zheng, Ying, Bo, Bian, Cao, Song, Xu, and Si—moved his seat to An Prefecture, and was styled commissioner of the Huaixi Sixteen Prefectures circuit.
78
尋以亳州隸滑衛節度，徐州隸兗鄆節度。
Before long Bo Prefecture was assigned to Hua–Wei, and Xu Prefecture to Yan–Yun.
79
置淄沂節度使，領淄、沂、滄、德、棣五州，治沂州。
A Zi–Yi military commissioner was appointed, commanding Zi, Yi, Cang, De, and Di from his seat at Yi Prefecture.
80
平盧軍節度使侯希逸引兵保青州，授青密節度使，遂廢淄沂節度，並所管五州，號淄青平盧節度，增領齊州。
Pinglu Army military commissioner Hou Xiy led his troops to hold Qingzhou and was appointed Qing–Mi military commissioner; the Zi–Yi circuit was then abolished and its five prefectures absorbed into the newly styled Zi–Qing–Pinglu circuit, which also gained Qi Prefecture.
81
以齊州隸青密，而兗鄆節度增領徐州。
Qi Prefecture was transferred to Qing–Mi, and the Yan–Yun circuit gained Xu Prefecture.
82
762寶應元年復置河南節度使，治汴州，領州八：汴、宋、曹、徐、潁、兗、鄆、濮。
In 762, the first year of Baoying, the Henan military commissioner was reinstated, headquartered at Bian Prefecture, commanding Bian, Song, Cao, Xu, Ying, Yan, Yun, and Pu.
83
淮西節度增領許、隋、唐三州，以鄭州隸澤潞節度，潁、汴、宋、曹四州隸河南節度，泗州隸兗鄆節度，申州隸蔡汝節度。
Huaixi gained Xu, Sui, and Tang prefectures; Zheng went to the Ze–Lu circuit, Ying, Bian, Song, and Cao to Henan, Si to Yan–Yun, and Shen to Cai–Ru.
84
登、萊、沂、海、泗五州隸兗鄆節度。
Deng, Lai, Yi, Hai, and Si were assigned to the Yan–Yun circuit.
85
是年，廢兗鄆節度，以鄆、兗、濮、徐四州隸河南節度，登、萊、沂、海、泗隸淄青平盧節度。
That same year the Yan–Yun circuit was abolished; Yun, Yan, Pu, and Xu went to Henan, and Deng, Lai, Yi, Hai, and Si to the Zi–Qing–Pinglu circuit.
86
763廣德元年滑衛節度增領亳州，更號滑亳節度使，增領德州。
In 763, the first year of Guangde, the Hua–Wei circuit gained Bo Prefecture, was redesignated the Hua–Bo military commissioner, and also gained De Prefecture.
87
以衛州隸澤潞，析相、貝別置節度，魏博別置防禦。
Wei Prefecture was assigned to Ze–Lu; Xiang and Bei were split off under a new military commissioner, and Weibo received a separate chief defense commissioner.
88

Cang and De prefectures were assigned to the Weibo circuit.
89

The Zi–Qing–Pinglu circuit gained Ying Prefecture;
90

Before long Ying Prefecture was transferred back to the Weibo circuit.

In 776, the eleventh year of Dali, the Yongping circuit gained jurisdiction over Song and Si prefectures.

The Zi–Qing–Pinglu circuit gained Yun, Cao, Pu, Xu, and Yan, while Si Prefecture was assigned to the Yongping Army circuit.

In 779, the fourteenth year of Dali, the Yongping circuit gained Bian and Ying and moved its seat to Bian Prefecture.
113

The Huaixi military commissioner again made Cai his seat; that year the command was granted the title Huaining Army circuit, then redesignated the Shen–Guang–Cai military commissioner; Ru Prefecture went to the Eastern Capital Intendant, and Bian Prefecture to the Yongping Army circuit.

780 CE, first year of the Jianzhong reign.
115
781二年永平節度增領鄭州，析宋、亳、潁別置節度使，以泗州隸淮南。
In 781, the second year of Jianzhong, Yongping gained Zheng Prefecture; Song, Bo, and Ying were split off under a new military commissioner, and Si Prefecture was assigned to Huainan.
116

That same year Zheng Prefecture was transferred to the Heyang Three Fortresses circuit, but the former arrangement was soon restored.
117

A Song–Bo–Ying military commissioner was installed at Song Prefecture; before long the post was styled the Xuanwu Army military commissioner.
118
782三年置徐海沂密都團練觀察使，治徐州。
In 782, the third year of Jianzhong, a Xu–Hai–Yi–Mi regimental training observation commissioner was installed, headquartered at Xu Prefecture.
119
廢淄青平盧節度使，置淄青都團練觀察使，領淄、青、登、萊、齊、兗、鄆七州，治青州；
The Zi–Qing–Pinglu military commissioner was abolished and replaced by a Zi–Qing regimental training observation commissioner commanding Zi, Qing, Deng, Lai, Qi, Yan, and Yun from Qing Prefecture;
120
置曹濮都團練觀察使，治濮州。
A Cao–Pu regimental training observation commissioner was installed, headquartered at Pu Prefecture.

In 784, the first year of Xingyuan, the Yongping Army circuit transferred Bian and Hua to the Xuanwu Army; before long it regained Hua Prefecture and moved its seat there.
123

The Xuanwu Army military commissioner moved his seat to Bian Prefecture.
124

A separate observation commissioner was installed for Shou Prefecture.
125

The Xu–Hai–Yi–Mi regimental training observation commissioner was abolished.
126
復置淄青平盧節度使，領青、淄、登、萊、齊、兗、鄆、徐、海、沂、密、曹、濮十三州，治青州，廢曹濮都團練觀察使。
The Zi–Qing–Pinglu military commissioner was reinstated, commanding thirteen prefectures—Qing, Zi, Deng, Lai, Qi, Yan, Yun, Xu, Hai, Yi, Mi, Cao, and Pu—from Qing Prefecture, and the Cao–Pu regimental training observation commissioner was abolished.
127

The Hedong circuit was granted the title Baoning Army circuit.
128
785貞元元年永平軍節度更號義成軍節度，增領許州。
In 785, the first year of Zhenyuan, the Yongping Army circuit was redesignated the Yicheng Army circuit and gained jurisdiction over Xu Prefecture.
129

Tang Prefecture was assigned to the Eastern Capital Intendant, and Xu Prefecture to the Yicheng Army circuit.

135
788四年置徐、泗、濠三州節度使，治徐州。
In 788, the fourth year of Zhenyuan, a military commissioner for Xu, Si, and Hao was installed, headquartered at Xu Prefecture.
136

The Zi–Qing–Pinglu military commissioner moved his seat to Yun Prefecture, and Xu Prefecture was assigned to the Xu–Si circuit.
