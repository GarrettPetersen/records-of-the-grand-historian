1
25%唐都長安，而關中號稱沃野，然其土地狹，所出不足以給京師，備水旱，故常轉漕東南之粟。 高祖、太宗之時，用物有節而易贍，水陸漕運，歲不過二十萬石，故漕事簡。 自高宗已後，歲益增多，而功利繁興，民亦罹其弊矣。
The Tang made Chang'an their capital, and although Guanzhong was renowned as rich farmland, the region was cramped and could not produce enough to feed the court or buffer against flood and drought; grain from the southeast therefore had to be shipped in by canal on a regular basis. Under Gaozu and Taizong, the court spent with restraint and provisions were readily met; combined water and land transport moved no more than two hundred thousand shi of grain a year, and canal logistics remained straightforward. After Gaozong, the annual volume grew year by year, ambitious public works multiplied, and the people bore the cost.
2
初，江淮漕租米至東都輸含嘉倉，以車或馱陸運至陝。 而水行來遠，多風波覆溺之患，其失嘗十七八，故其率一斛得八斗為成勞。 而陸運至陝，纔三百里，率兩斛計傭錢千。 民送租者，皆有水陸之直，而河有三門底柱之險。 顯慶元年，苑西監褚朗議鑿三門山為梁，可通陸運。 乃發卒六千鑿之，功不成。 其後，將作大匠楊務廉又鑿為棧，以輓漕舟。 輓夫繫二鈲於胸，而繩多絕，輓夫輒墜死，則以逃亡報，因繫其父母妻子，人以為苦。
Initially, Jianghuai tribute grain was shipped to Luoyang and stored in the Hanjia Granary, then hauled overland by cart or pack train to Shanzhou. The river leg was long and perilous; wind and waves often claimed seven or eight tenths of the cargo, so only eight dou delivered from each hu was counted as a full haul. Overland hauls to Shan covered barely three hundred li, yet hire for every two hu ran to a thousand cash. Rent-bearers paid both river and road charges, and the Yellow River gorge at Sanmen and the Dizhu reefs made the route deadly. In 656, Chu Lang, supervisor of the western park, proposed cutting through Sanmen Mountain for a beam bridge so grain could move by land. Six thousand conscripts were sent to cut the pass, but the project failed. Later, Yang Wulian, chief artisan of the Directorate of Works, cut plank roads along the cliffs to haul grain barges. Trackers wore two chest hooks on ropes that snapped constantly; when a man fell to his death, officials reported him as a deserter and imprisoned his family—an ordeal the people found unbearable.
3
開元十八年，宣州刺史裴耀卿朝集京師，玄宗訪以漕事，耀卿條上便宜曰：「江南戶口多，而無征防之役。 然送租、庸、調物，以歲二月至揚州入斗門，四月已後，始渡淮入汴，常苦水淺，六七月乃至河口，而河水方漲，須八九月水落始得上河入洛，而漕路多梗，船檣阻隘。 江南之人，不習河事，轉雇河師水手，重為勞費。 其得行日少，阻滯日多。 今漢、隋漕路，瀕河倉廩，遺迹可尋。 可於河口置武牢倉，鞏縣置洛口倉，使江南之舟不入黃河，黃河之舟不入洛口。 而河陽、柏崖、太原、永豐、渭南諸倉，節級轉運，水通則舟行，水淺則寓於倉以待，則舟無停留，而物不耗失。 此甚利也。 」玄宗初不省。 二十一年，耀卿為京兆尹，京師雨水，穀踊貴，玄宗將幸東都，復問耀卿漕事，耀卿因請「罷陝陸運，而置倉河口，使江南漕舟至河口者，輸粟於倉而去，縣官雇舟以分入河、洛。 置倉三門東西，漕舟輸其東倉，而陸運以輸西倉，復以舟漕，以避三門之水險。 」玄宗以為然。 乃於河陰置河陰倉，河清置柏崖倉； 三門東置集津倉，西置鹽倉； 鑿山十八里以陸運。 自江、淮漕者，皆輸河陰倉，自河陰西至太原倉，謂之北運，自太原倉浮渭以實關中。 玄宗大悅，拜耀卿為黃門侍郎、同中書門下平章事，兼江淮都轉運使，以鄭州刺史崔希逸、河南少尹蕭炅為副使，益漕晉、絳、魏、濮、邢、貝、濟、博之租輸諸倉，轉而入渭。 凡三歲，漕七百萬石，省陸運傭錢三十萬緡。
In 730, Pei Yaoqing of Xuanzhou came to Chang'an for the annual assembly; when Xuanzong asked about canal transport, he laid out a plan: "Jiangnan is populous yet free of frontier garrison duty. Yet rent, corvée cloth, and diao goods leave in the second month for Yangzhou and the Dou Gate, cross the Huai into the Bian only after the fourth, struggle through shallows, reach the river mouth in summer when the flood is rising, and cannot enter the Yellow River and Luoyang until the eighth or ninth month when the water falls—so the route is choked and convoys jam the channel. Southerners know little of river work and must hire pilots and crews at heavy cost. Sailing days are few and waiting days many. The Han and Sui canal system, with riverside granaries, still leaves traces we can follow. Build the Wulao Granary at the river mouth and the Luokou Granary at Gong County, so Jiangnan craft never enter the Yellow River and Yellow River craft never enter Luokou. Relay through Heyang, Boya, Taiyuan, Yongfeng, and Weinan: sail when the channel is deep, store in granaries when it is low, and boats need not linger while grain is spared spoilage. The savings would be enormous." Xuanzong at first did not take his meaning. In 733, as Jingzhao intendant, he faced flood-driven grain panic as the emperor prepared to move to Luoyang; asked again, he urged ending the Shan overland haul, building granaries at the river mouth where southern boats would unload and leave, and having the state hire craft to relay cargo up the Yellow River and into the Luo. Build granaries east and west of Sanmen: barges unload at the east store, porters relay to the west store, then boats resume the river leg and skirt Sanmen's rapids." The emperor agreed. They built the Heyin Granary at Heyin and the Boya Granary at Heqing; the Jijin Granary east of Sanmen and the Yan Granary to the west; and cut an eighteen-li mountain road for the overland relay. Jiang-Huai grain all went to Heyin; the leg west to Taiyuan was called the Northern Transport, and from Taiyuan barges went down the Wei to supply Guanzhong. Delighted, Xuanzong made him vice president of the Secretariat, chief councillor, and Jianghuai transport commissioner, with Cui Xiyi and Xiao Jiong as deputies, and added rent grain from eight north China prefectures into the relay system feeding the Wei. Within three years they moved seven million shi and saved three hundred thousand strings in overland wages.
4
是時，民久不罹兵革，物力豐富，朝廷用度亦廣，不計道里之費，而民之輸送所出水陸之直，增以「函脚」、「營窖」之名，民間傳言用斗錢運斗米，其糜耗如此。
Peace had left the realm prosperous and the court lavish; on top of legitimate transport fees, collectors added surcharges called "crate-foot" and "camp-pit," until people said it cost a peck of cash to ship a peck of grain.
5
及耀卿罷相，北運頗艱，米歲至京師纔百萬石。 二十五年，遂罷北運。 而崔希逸為河南陝運使，歲運百八十萬石。 其後以太倉積粟有餘，歲減漕數十萬石。
After Yaoqing left office the northern route faltered, and only a million shi a year reached the capital. In 737 the northern route was abandoned. Cui Xiyi, as Henan-Shan transport commissioner, still moved 1.8 million shi annually. Later, with the Ever-Normal Granary overflowing, annual quotas were trimmed by several hundred thousand shi.
6
二十九年，陝郡太守李齊物鑿砥柱為門以通漕，開其山顛為輓路，燒石沃醯而鑿之。 然棄石入河，激水益湍怒，舟不能入新門，候其水漲，以人輓舟而上。 天子疑之，遣宦者按視，齊物厚賂使者，還言便。
In 741, Shan prefect Li Qiwu cut a gate through the Dizhu reefs, opened a haul road along the cliff top, and used fire and vinegar to fracture the rock. Rubble dumped into the river made the current fiercer; boats could not use the new gate until the flood rose, when teams hauled them through. The emperor sent eunuchs to inspect; Qiwu bribed them lavishly, and they reported the project sound.
7
齊物入為鴻臚卿，以長安令韋堅代之，兼水陸運使。 堅治漢、隋運渠，起關門，抵長安，通山東租賦。 乃絕灞、滻，並渭而東，至永豐倉與渭合。 又於長樂坡瀕苑牆鑿潭於望春樓下，以聚漕舟。 堅因使諸舟各揭其郡名，陳其土地所產寶貨諸奇物於栿上。 先時民間唱俚歌曰「得体紇那邪」。 其後得寶符於桃林，於是陝縣尉崔成甫更得体歌為得寶弘農野。 堅命舟人為吳、楚服，大笠、廣袖、芒屩以歌之。 成甫又廣之為歌辭十闋，自衣缺後綠衣、錦半臂、紅抹額，立第一船為號頭以唱，集兩縣婦女百餘人，鮮服靚粧，鳴鼓吹笛以和之。 眾艘以次輳樓下，天子望見大悅，賜其潭名曰廣運潭。 是歲，漕山東粟四百萬石。 自裴耀卿言漕事，進用者常兼轉運之職，而韋堅為最。
Li Qiwu became Minister of Ceremonies; Wei Jian, magistrate of Chang'an, replaced him as combined water-land transport commissioner. Wei Jian restored the Han and Sui canals from Tong Pass to Chang'an to bring Shandong tribute to the capital. He diverted the Ba and Chan rivers into the Wei eastward to the Yongfeng Granary. Below Wangchun Tower on Changle Slope he excavated a basin along the imperial park wall to berth the grain fleet. Each barge bore its prefecture's name and displayed local treasures on its deck beams. A folk song had long run, "Deti he na ye." When a treasure talisman was found at Taolin, assistant magistrate Cui Chengfu rewrote the refrain as "Treasure found in the Hongnong wilds." Wei Jian dressed the boatmen in Wu and Chu costume—broad hats, wide sleeves, straw sandals—to perform the song. Chengfu expanded it to ten stanzas, stood on the lead boat in green robes, brocade half-sleeves, and a red forehead cloth to lead the chorus, and mustered more than a hundred brightly dressed women from two counties with drums, pipes, and flutes. The flotilla filed past the tower; the delighted emperor named the basin Broad Transport Pool. That year four million shi of eastern grain reached the capital by canal. After Pei Yaoqing's reforms, favored officials often doubled as transport commissioners, and none outdid Wei Jian.
8
初，耀卿興漕路，請罷陸運，而不果廢。 自景雲中，陸運北路分八遞，雇民車牛以載。 開元初，河南尹李傑為水陸運使，運米歲二百五十萬石，而八遞用車千八百乘。 耀卿罷久之，河南尹裴迥以八遞傷牛，乃為交場兩遞，濱水處為宿場，分官總之，自龍門東山抵天津橋為石堰以遏水。 其後大盜起，而天下匱矣。
Yaoqing had sought to end overland hauls when he opened the new route, but the road was never fully abandoned. From the Jingyun era the northern road used eight relay stages with hired civilian carts and oxen. Early Kaiyuan, Henan intendant Li Jie moved 2.5 million shi a year while the eight relays required 1,800 carts. Years later, Pei Jiong reduced the eight relays to two exchange yards and riverside depots, posted officials along the route, and built stone weirs from Longmen to Tianjin Bridge to tame the current. Then the great rebellions broke out and the empire was drained.
9
肅宗末年，史朝義兵分出宋州，淮運於是阻絕，租庸鹽鐵泝漢江而上。 河南尹劉晏為戶部侍郎，兼句當度支、轉運、鹽鐵、鑄錢使，江淮粟帛，繇襄、漢越商於以輸京師。
Late in Suzong's reign, Shi Chaoyi's armies cut the Huai route, and rent, corvée goods, salt, and iron had to go up the Han. Liu Yan of Henan, as vice minister of revenue and fiscal, transport, salt-iron, and coinage commissioner, routed Jianghuai grain and cloth through Xiangyang, the Han, and the Shangyu road to the capital.
10
及代宗出陝州，關中空窘，於是盛轉輸以給用。 廣德二年，廢句當度支使，以劉晏顓領東都、河南、淮西、江南東西轉運、租庸、鑄錢、鹽鐵，轉輸至上都，度支所領諸道租庸觀察使，凡漕事亦皆決於晏。 晏即鹽利顧傭分吏督之，隨江、汴、河、渭所宜。 故時轉運船繇潤州陸運至揚子，斗米費錢十九，晏命囊米而載以舟，減錢十五； 繇揚州距河陰，斗米費錢百二十，晏為歇艎支江船二千艘，每船受千斛，十船為綱，每綱三百人，篙工五十，自揚州遣將部送至河陰，上三門，號「上門填闕船」，米斗減錢九十。 調巴、蜀、襄、漢麻枲竹篠為綯挽舟，以朽索腐材代薪，物無棄者。 未十年，人人習河險。 江船不入汴，汴船不入河，河船不入渭； 江南之運積揚州，汴河之運積河陰，河船之運積渭口，渭船之運入太倉。 歲轉粟百一十萬石，無升斗溺者。 輕貨自揚子至汴州，每馱費錢二千二百，減九百，歲省十餘萬緡。 又分官吏主丹楊湖，禁引溉，自是河漕不涸。 大曆八年，以關內豐穰，減漕十萬石，度支和糴以優農。 晏自天寶末掌出納，監歲運，知左右藏，主財穀三十餘年矣。 及楊炎為相，以舊惡罷晏，轉運使復歸度支，凡江淮漕米，以庫部郎中崔河圖主之。
When Daizong marched out of Shanzhou, starving Guanzhong forced a vast expansion of transport to keep the court fed. In 764 the fiscal commissioner post was abolished and Liu Yan took sole charge of transport, rent-yong, coinage, and salt-iron from Luoyang through Henan, Huaixi, and both Jiangnans to the capital; every canal decision passed through him. He used salt revenue to hire crews and post supervisors suited to each leg on the Yangtze, Bian, Yellow River, and Wei. The old Runzhou-to-Yangzi leg cost nineteen cash per dou by land; Yan switched to barges and cut fifteen cash; the Yangzhou-Heyin leg had cost 120 cash per dou; he fielded two thousand river barges of a thousand hu each in convoys of ten with three hundred men and fifty polemen, escorted to Heyin and through Sanmen as "gap-fillers," saving ninety cash per dou. He sourced hemp, bamboo, and splints from the southwest and Han basin for tow ropes, burned scrap cord and rotten timber for fuel, and wasted nothing. Within a decade crews mastered the river's dangers. Yangtze craft never entered the Bian, Bian craft never the Yellow River, Yellow River craft never the Wei; Jiangnan grain piled up at Yangzhou, Bian grain at Heyin, Yellow River grain at the Wei mouth, and Wei barges fed the Ever-Normal Granary. He moved 1.1 million shi a year without losing a single peck to the water. Light freight from Yangzi to Bianzhou had cost 2,200 cash per load; he cut 900 and saved more than 100,000 strings a year. Officials guarded Danyang Lake against irrigation diversions, and the canal stayed navigable. In 773, with Guanzhong abundant, he cut transport by 100,000 shi and the fiscal office bought grain at fair prices to help farmers. From late Tianbao onward Liu Yan had run receipts, annual transport, and both treasuries for more than thirty years. When Yang Yan became chancellor, old grudges ended Liu Yan's career; transport reverted to the fiscal office and Jianghuai grain passed to Cui Hetu of the repository section.
11
及田悅、李惟岳、李納、梁崇義拒命，舉天下兵討之，諸軍仰給京師。 而李納、田悅兵守渦口，梁崇義搤襄、鄧，南北漕引皆絕，京師大恐。 江淮水陸轉運使杜佑以秦、漢運路出浚儀十里入琵琶溝，絕蔡河，至陳州而合，自隋鑿汴河，官漕不通，若導流培岸，功用甚寡； 疏雞鳴岡首尾，可以通舟，陸行纔四十里，則江、湖、黔中、嶺南、蜀、漢之粟可方舟而下，繇白沙趣東關，歷潁、蔡，涉汴抵東都，無濁河泝淮之阻，減故道二千餘里。 會李納將李洧以徐州歸命，淮路通而止。 戶部侍郎趙贊又以錢貨出淮迂緩，分置汴州東西水陸運兩稅鹽鐵使，以度支總大綱。
When Tian Yue, Li Weiyue, Li Na, and Liang Chongyi rebelled, imperial armies looked to the capital for every ration. Li Na and Tian Yue held Wokou, Liang Chongyi choked Xiangyang and Nanyang, and with both canal arteries severed the capital panicked. Du You, Jianghuai transport commissioner, proposed reviving the Qin-Han route from Junyi through Pipa Ditch to Chenzhou; since Sui times the Bian had blocked official transport, but dredging and banking would be light work; clearing Jiming Hill would need only forty li of portage, letting grain from the Yangtze basin, southwest, and Han reach Luoyang via Baisha, Ying, Cai, and the Bian without fighting the Huai current—more than two thousand li saved. But Li Wei's surrender of Xuzhou reopened the Huai route and the project was dropped. Zhao Zan, vice minister of revenue, split eastern and western Bianzhou transport and two-tax salt-iron commissions because Huai coin shipments were too slow, leaving overall control with the fiscal office.
12
貞元初，關輔宿兵，米斗千錢，太倉供天子六宮之膳不及十日，禁中不能釀酒，以飛龍駝負永豐倉米給禁軍，陸運牛死殆盡。 德宗以給事中崔造敢言，為能立事，用為相。 造以江、吳素嫉錢穀諸使顓利罔上，乃奏諸道觀察使、刺史選官部送兩稅至京師，廢諸道水陸轉運使及度支巡院、江淮轉運使，以度支、鹽鐵歸尚書省，宰相分判六尚書事。 以戶部侍郎元琇判諸道鹽鐵、榷酒，侍郎吉中孚判度支諸道兩稅。 增江淮之運，浙江東、西歲運米七十五萬石，復以兩稅易米百萬石，江西、湖南、鄂岳、福建、嶺南米亦百二十萬石，詔浙江東、西節度使韓滉，淮南節度使杜亞運至東、西渭橋倉。 諸道有鹽鐵處，復置巡院。 歲終宰相計課最。 崔造厚元琇，而韓滉方領轉運，奏國漕不可改。 帝亦雅器滉，復以為江淮轉運使。 元琇嫉其剛，不可共事，因有隙。 琇稱疾罷，而滉為度支、諸道鹽鐵、轉運使，於是崔造亦罷。 滉遂劾琇常餫米淄青、河中，而李納、懷光倚以構叛，貶琇雷州司戶參軍，尋賜死。
Early Zhenyuan, with armies camped in Guanzhong, rice hit a thousand cash per dou; the Ever-Normal Granary could feed court and harem less than ten days; the palace stopped brewing wine; Flying Dragon camels hauled Yongfeng grain to the guards while overland oxen died almost to a beast. Dezong made outspoken supervising secretary Cui Zao chancellor for his reputation as a doer. Cui Zao, reflecting Jiangnan resentment of profit-grubbing fiscal commissioners, had circuit commissioners and prefects send the two taxes directly, abolished transport commissioners and fiscal inspection offices, restored fiscal and salt-iron affairs to the Department of State Affairs, and split the six ministries among the chancellors. Yuan Xiu took salt-iron and liquor monopoly; Ji Zhongfu took fiscal two-tax affairs. He raised Jianghuai quotas: 750,000 shi from both Zhejiangs, a million from tax swaps, 1.2 million from Jiangxi, Hunan, Eyue, Fujian, and Lingnan, ordering Han Huang and Du Ya to deliver to the Wei Bridge granaries. Salt-iron circuits regained inspection offices. At year-end the chancellors graded officials' performance. Cui Zao favored Yuan Xiu, but Han Huang, still running transport, insisted the canal system could not be dismantled. The emperor valued Huang and restored him as Jianghuai transport commissioner. Yuan Xiu despised his harshness; they could not cooperate and a feud opened. Yuan Xiu quit citing illness; Huang took fiscal, salt-iron, and transport power, and Cui Zao fell soon after. Huang accused Yuan Xiu of feeding Ziqing and Hezhong, fueling Li Na and Zhu Ci's rebellions; he was demoted to Leizhou and then ordered to die.
13
是時，汴宋節度使春夏遣官監汴水，察盜灌溉者。 歲漕經底柱，覆者幾半。 河中有山號「米堆」，運舟入三門，雇平陸人為門匠，執標指麾，一舟百日乃能上。 諺曰：「古無門匠墓。 」謂皆溺死也。 陝虢觀察使李泌益鑿集津倉山西逕為運道，屬于三門倉，治上路以回空車，費錢五萬緡，下路減半； 又為入渭船，方五板，輸東渭橋太倉米至凡百三十萬石，遂罷南路陸運。 其後諸道鹽鐵、轉運使張滂復置江淮巡院。 及浙西觀察使李錡領使，江淮堰埭隸浙西者，增私路小堰之稅，以副使潘孟陽主上都留後。 李巽為諸道轉運、鹽鐵使，以堰埭歸鹽鐵使，罷其增置者。 自劉晏後，江淮米至渭橋寖減矣，至巽乃復如晏之多。
Each spring and summer the Bian-Song commissioner posted officials to guard the Bian Canal against illegal irrigation. Nearly half the annual fleet was lost at the Dizhu reefs. A reef called Rice Mound stood in the gorge; barges entering Sanmen hired Pinglu pilots called gate craftsmen to pole them through—a single vessel could take a hundred days. A proverb ran: "Of old there were no gate craftsmen's graves." —meaning they all drowned. Li Bi of Shan-Guo cut a western bypass from Jijin Granary to Sanmen, built an upper haul road for empty carts costing fifty thousand strings and a lower road half as much; built five-plank Wei barges that moved 1.3 million shi from the Ever-Normal Granary at East Wei Bridge, and ended the southern overland route. Later Zhang Pang restored the Jianghuai inspection office. When Li Qian of Zhexi took the commission, he taxed private weirs on Zhexi-controlled Jianghuai dams while deputy Pan Mengyang ran the capital rear office. Li Xun returned the dams to the salt-iron office and abolished the extra taxes. After Liu Yan, Jianghuai grain to Wei Bridge had dwindled; only under Li Xun did volumes match Liu Yan's again.
14
初，揚州疏太子港、陳登塘，凡三十四陂，以益漕河，輒復堙塞。 淮南節度使杜亞乃濬渠蜀岡，疏句城湖、愛敬陂，起隄貫城，以通大舟。 河益庳，水下走淮，夏則舟不得前。 節度使李吉甫築平津堰，以洩有餘，防不足，漕流遂通。 然漕益少，江淮米至渭橋者纔二十萬斛。 諸道鹽鐵、轉運使盧坦糴以備一歲之費，省冗職八十員。 自江以南，補署皆剸屬院監，而漕米亡耗於路頗多。 刑部侍郎王播代坦，建議米至渭橋五百石亡五十石者死。 其後判度支皇甫鎛議萬斛亡三百斛者償之，千七百斛者流塞下，過者死； 盜十斛者流，三十斛者死。 而覆船敗輓，至者不得十之四五。 部吏舟人相挾為姦，榜笞號苦之聲聞于道路，禁錮連歲，赦下而獄死者不可勝數。 其後貸死刑，流天德五城，人不畏法，運米至者十亡七八。 鹽鐵、轉運使柳公綽請如王播議加重刑。 大和初，歲旱河涸，掊沙而進，米多耗，抵死甚眾，不待覆奏。
Yangzhou had dredged Taizi Harbor, Chendeng Pond, and thirty-four reservoirs to feed the canal, but they kept silting shut again. Du Ya of Huainan dredged the Shugang canal, opened Jucheng Lake and Aijing Embankment, and built city dikes so large vessels could pass. The channel grew shallower, water drained toward the Huai, and summer left boats stranded. Li Jifu built the Pingjin Weir to spill excess water and hold back shortages, and the canal ran clear again. Yet transport kept shrinking, and only two hundred thousand hu of Jianghuai grain reached Wei Bridge. Lu Tan bought grain for a year's supply and cut eighty redundant posts. South of the Yangtze every post answered to inspection-office supervisors, and grain losses in transit were heavy. Wang Bo of the Ministry of Justice replaced him and proposed death for losing fifty shi out of every five hundred delivered to Wei Bridge. Later Huangfu Bo of the fiscal office required compensation for three hundred hu lost per ten thousand, exile beyond the passes for 1,700 hu lost, and death beyond that; theft of ten hu meant exile and thirty hu meant death. Yet capsizes and wrecked hauls meant less than half the cargo arrived. Clerks and boatmen colluded in fraud; flogging and cries of pain echoed along the roads; prisoners languished for years, and even after amnesties the dead in custody were beyond counting. When death sentences were commuted to exile in the Tiande Five Cities, the law lost its terror and seven or eight tenths of grain never arrived. Liu Gongchuo asked to restore Wang Bo's harsh penalties. Early in Dahe a drought bared the riverbed; crews dug through sand, grain spoiled, and many were executed without awaiting imperial review.
15
奏、漢時故漕興成堰，東達永豐倉，咸陽縣令韓遼請疏之，自咸陽抵潼關三百里，可以罷車輓之勞。 宰相李固言以為非時，文宗曰：「苟利於人，陰陽拘忌，非朕所顧也。 」議遂決。 堰成，罷輓車之牛以供農耕，關中賴其利。
Han Liao, magistrate of Xianyang, asked to dredge the Qin-Han Xingcheng Weir reaching Yongfeng Granary—a three-hundred-li waterway from Xianyang to Tong Pass that could end cart haulage. Chancellor Li Guyan called the timing wrong; Wenzong said, "If it helps the people, I will not be bound by yin-yang taboos. " The project went ahead. When the weir was finished, haul oxen returned to the fields and Guanzhong prospered.
16
故事，州縣官充綱，送輕貨四萬，書上考。 開成初，為長定綱，州擇清彊官送兩稅，至十萬遷一官，往來十年者授縣令。 江淮錢積河陰，轉輸歲費十七萬餘緡，行綱多以盜抵死。 判度支王彥威置縣遞羣畜萬三千三百乘，使路傍民養以取傭，日役一驛，省費甚博。 而宰相亦以長定綱命官不以材，江淮大州，歲授官者十餘人，乃罷長定綱，送五萬者書上考，七萬者減一選，五十萬減三選而已。 及戶部侍郎裴休為使，以河瀕縣令董漕事，自江達渭，運米四十萬石。 居三歲，米至渭橋百二十萬石。
By precedent, county officials led convoys; delivering forty thousand in light goods earned top marks. Early Kaicheng introduced long-fixed convoys: prefectures chose capable men to deliver the two taxes; one hundred thousand earned a promotion, ten years of service earned a county magistracy. Jianghuai coin piled up at Heyin; relay costs exceeded 170,000 strings a year, and many convoy leaders were executed for theft. Wang Yanwei of the fiscal office fielded 13,300 relay animals raised by roadside households for hire, one post a day, at great savings. Chancellors also appointed convoy leaders without regard to talent; large Jianghuai prefectures posted more than ten men a year, so the long-fixed convoy was scrapped—fifty thousand earned top marks, seventy thousand one fewer selection, five hundred thousand three fewer. When Pei Xiu became commissioner, river-bank magistrates ran canal affairs and moved four hundred thousand shi from the Yangtze to the Wei. Within three years deliveries to Wei Bridge reached 1.2 million shi.
17
凡漕達于京師而足國用者，大略如此。 其他州、縣、方鎮，漕以自資，或兵所征行，轉運以給一時之用者，皆不足紀。
Such was the broad outline of canal transport that fed the capital and the state. Local canal hauls for prefectures, counties, and garrisons, or wartime relays, are not recorded here.
18
唐開軍府以扞要衝，因隙地置營田，天下屯總九百九十二。 司農寺每屯三十頃，州、鎮諸軍每屯五十頃。 水陸腴瘠、播殖地宜與其功庸煩省、收率之多少，皆決於尚書省。 苑內屯以善農者為屯官、屯副，御史巡行莅輸。 上地五十畝，瘠地二十畝，稻田八十畝，則給牛一。 諸屯以地良薄與歲之豐凶為三等，具民田歲穫多少，取中熟為率。 有警，則以兵若夫千人助收。 隸司農者，歲三月，卿、少卿循行，治不法者。 凡屯田收多者，褒進之。 歲以仲春籍來歲頃畝、州府軍鎮之遠近，上兵部，度便宜遣之。 開元二十五年，詔屯官敍功以歲豐凶為上下。 鎮戍地可耕者，人給十畝以供糧。 方春，屯官巡行，讁作不時者。 天下屯田收穀百九十餘萬斛。
The Tang opened military prefectures on key frontiers and established garrison farms on spare land; nationwide there were 992 garrisons. Imperial Granary garrisons farmed thirty qing each; prefectural and frontier armies fifty qing each. Soil quality, crop suitability, labor costs, and expected yields were all set by the Department of State Affairs. Park garrisons named skilled farmers as chiefs and deputies, with censors touring to inspect deliveries. One ox was allotted per fifty mu of good land, twenty mu of poor land, or eighty mu of paddy. Garrisons graded plots and harvests in three tiers against civilian yields, using an average year as the benchmark. In emergencies a thousand troops or laborers were sent to help harvest. Granary garrisons were inspected every third month by the director and vice director, who punished violations. High-yield garrisons were commended and promoted. Each mid-spring they registered next year's acreage and relay distances, reported to the Ministry of War, and deployed forces as needed. In 737 an edict ranked garrison officers by harvest quality. Where garrison land could be tilled, each man received ten mu for his rations. Each spring garrison chiefs toured and punished untimely planting. Nationwide garrison farms harvested more than 1.9 million hu.
19
初，度支歲市糧於北都，以贍振武、天德、靈武、鹽、夏之軍，費錢五六十萬緡，泝河舟溺甚眾。 建中初，宰相楊炎請置屯田於豐州，發關輔民鑿陵陽渠以增溉。 京兆尹嚴郢嘗從事朔方，知其利害，以為不便，疏奏不報。 郢又奏：「五城舊屯，其數至廣，以開渠之糧貸諸城，約以冬輸； 又以開渠功直布帛先給田者，據估轉穀。 如此則關輔免調發，五城田闢，比之浚渠利十倍也。 」時楊炎方用事，郢議不用，而陵陽渠亦不成。 然振武、天德良田，廣袤千里。
Initially the fiscal office bought grain yearly at Taiyuan to feed the Zhenwu, Tiande, Lingwu, Yan, and Xia armies at five or six hundred thousand strings, with heavy losses on the river route. Early in Jianzhong, Chancellor Yang Yan proposed garrison farms at Fengzhou and conscripted Guanfu labor to cut the Lingyang Canal. Yan Ying of Jingzhao, who had served in Shuofang, saw the drawbacks and memorialized against it without response. Yan Ying argued again: "The old Five Cities garrisons are vast—lend canal grain to the garrisons for winter delivery; pay canal workers in cloth and silk upfront, then convert to grain at appraised rates. Guanfu would avoid levies, the Five Cities fields would open, and the gain would be ten times dredging the canal." Yang Yan was in power, Yan Ying's plan was ignored, and the Lingyang Canal was never finished. Yet Zhenwu and Tiande still had fine fields stretching a thousand li.
20
元和中，振武軍饑，宰相李絳請開營田，可省度支漕運及絕和糴欺隱。 憲宗稱善，乃以韓重華為振武、京西營田、和糴、水運使，起代北，墾田三百頃，出贓罪吏九百餘人，給以耒耜、耕牛，假種糧，使償所負粟，二歲大熟。 因募人為十五屯，每屯百三十人，人耕百畝，就高為堡，東起振武，西逾雲州，極於中受降城，凡六百餘里，列柵二十，墾田三千八百餘頃，歲收粟二十萬石，省度支錢二千餘萬緡。 重華入朝，奏請益開田五千頃，法用人七千，可以盡給五城。 會李絳已罷，後宰相持其議而止。 憲宗末，天下營田皆雇民或借庸以耕，又以瘠地易上地，民間苦之。 穆宗即位，詔還所易地，而耕以官兵。 耕官地者，給三之一以終身。
In Yuanhe, starving Zhenwu troops led Chancellor Li Jiang to propose garrison farms to cut fiscal transport costs and fair-purchase fraud. Xianzong approved and made Han Chonghua commissioner; north of Dai he opened three hundred qing, paroled nine hundred corrupt officials with plows, oxen, and seed on condition they repay grain debts, and reaped a great harvest in two years. He raised fifteen garrisons of 130 men each farming a hundred mu, building hill forts from Zhenwu to Central Accept-Submission City—six hundred li, twenty palisades, 3,800 qing opened, 200,000 shi a year, saving over twenty million strings. At court he asked to open another five thousand qing with seven thousand men to fully supply the Five Cities. Li Jiang had fallen; later chancellors blocked the plan. Late in Xianzong, garrison farms hired civilians or corvée labor and swapped poor plots for good ones at the people's expense. Muzong ordered the exchanged land returned and put farming back in soldiers' hands. Those who farmed government land kept one-third of the yield for life.
21
靈武、邠寧，土廣肥而民不知耕。 大和末，王起奏立營田。 後党項大擾河西，邠寧節度使畢諴亦募士開營田，歲收三十萬斛，省度支錢數百萬緡。
Lingwu and Binning had broad fertile soil, yet the people scarcely farmed it. Late in Dahe, Wang Qi proposed establishing garrison farms. When Tangut raids shook Hexi, Bi Dan of Binning recruited colonists, harvested 300,000 hu a year, and saved millions of strings.
22
貞觀、開元後，邊土西舉高昌、龜茲、焉耆、小勃律，北抵薛延陀故地，緣邊數十州戍重兵，營田及地租不足以供軍，於是初有和糴。 牛仙客為相，有彭果者獻策廣關輔之糴，京師糧稟益羨，自是玄宗不復幸東都。 天寶中，歲以錢六十萬緡賦諸道和糴，斗增三錢，每歲短遞輸京倉者百餘萬斛。 米賤則少府加估而糴，貴則賤價而糶。
After Zhenguan and Kaiyuan the frontier stretched west to Gaochang and Kucha and north to the old Xueyantuo lands; dozens of border prefectures held heavy garrisons that garrison farms and land tax could not feed—so fair purchase began. Under Chancellor Niu Xianke, Peng Guo proposed expanding Guanfu fair purchase; capital granaries overflowed, and Xuanzong never again visited Luoyang. In Tianbao, circuits paid six hundred thousand strings yearly for fair purchase with three cash per dou added; more than a million hu reached the capital granary each year. When rice was cheap the treasury bought at a premium; when dear, it sold at a discount.
23
貞元初，吐蕃劫盟，召諸道兵十七萬戍邊。 關中為吐蕃蹂躪者二十年矣，北至河曲，人戶無幾，諸道戍兵月給粟十七萬斛，皆糴於關中。 宰相陸贄以「關中穀賤，請和糴，可至百餘萬斛。 計諸縣船車至太倉，穀價四十有餘，米價七十，則一年和糴之數當轉運之二年，一斗轉運之資當和糴之五斗。 減轉運以實邊，存轉運以備時要。 江淮米至河陰者罷八十萬斛，河陰米至太原倉者罷五十萬，太原米至東渭橋者罷二十萬。 以所減米糶江淮水菑州縣，斗減時五十以救乏。 京城東渭橋之糴，斗增時三十以利農。 以江淮糶米及減運直市絹帛送上都。 」帝乃命度支增估糴粟三十三萬斛，然不能盡用贄議。 憲宗即位之初，有司以歲豐熟，請畿內和糴。 當時府、縣配戶督限，有稽違則迫蹙鞭撻，甚於稅賦，號為和糴，其實害民。
Early in Zhenyuan, after the Tibetans broke the peace treaty, 170,000 troops from every circuit were posted to the frontier. Guanzhong had been ravaged by Tibetans for twenty years; north to Hequ almost no households remained; border garrisons needed 170,000 hu a month, all bought in Guanzhong. Chancellor Lu Zhi argued: "Guanzhong grain is cheap—fair purchase could reach more than a million hu. Boats and carts from the counties to the Ever-Normal Granary cost over forty for grain and seventy for rice—one year of fair purchase equals two years of transport, and one dou of transport cost equals five dou of fair purchase. Cut transport to stock the frontier, keep enough for emergencies. Cut Jianghuai-to-Heyin by 800,000 hu, Heyin-to-Taiyuan by 500,000, and Taiyuan-to-East Wei Bridge by 200,000. Sell the saved grain in flood-hit Jianghuai counties at fifty cash below market to relieve distress. Fair purchase at East Wei Bridge in the capital would add thirty cash per dou to help farmers. Use Jianghuai grain sales and transport savings to buy silk for the capital." The emperor ordered the fiscal office to buy 330,000 hu at premium prices, but did not fully adopt Lu Zhi's plan. Early in Xianzong's reign, officials requested capital-region fair purchase because the harvest was abundant. Prefectures and counties assigned household quotas; delays brought coercion and flogging worse than taxation—called fair purchase, it truly harmed the people.
