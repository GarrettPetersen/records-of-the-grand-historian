楊戴陽二李韓杜邢
Yang, Dai, Yang, the two Lis, Han, Du, and Xing
2
楊朝晟
Yang Zhaosheng
3
楊朝晟，字叔明，夏州朔方人。 興行間，以先鋒功授甘泉府果毅。 建中初，從李懷光討劉文喜涇州，斬獲多，加驃騎大將軍。 李納寇徐州，從唐朝臣往討，常冠軍。 懷光赴難奉天，屬朝晟兵千人下咸陽，賜實封百五十戶。
Yang Zhaosheng, courtesy name Shuming, came from Shuofang in Xia Prefecture. In the Xingyuan period he earned appointment as fruiti-corps commander of Ganquan Prefecture for distinguished service in the vanguard. Early in the Jianzhong reign he followed Li Huaiguang against Liu Wenxi at Jing Prefecture, took many heads and captives, and was promoted to General of the Indomitable Cavalry. When Li Na invaded Xuzhou, he marched with Tang Chaochen to suppress him and repeatedly led the van. When Huaiguang rushed to the emperor's aid at Fengtian, he assigned Zhaosheng a thousand men to advance on Xianyang, and the court granted him a substantive fief of one hundred fifty households.
4
懷光反，韓遊瑰退保邠、寧，賊黨張昕守邠州，大索軍實，多募士，欲潛歸之。 朝晟父懷賓為遊瑰將，夜以數十騎斬昕及同謀者。 遊瑰遣懷賓告行在，德宗勞問，授兼御史中丞。 朝晟泣見懷光曰：「父立功於國，子當誅，不可以主兵。 」懷光縶之。 及諸軍圍河中，遊瑰營長春宮，而懷賓戰甚力。 懷光平，帝原朝晟，因為遊瑰都虞候。 父子皆開府、賓客、御史中丞，軍中以為榮。
After Huaiguang turned rebel, Han Yougui fell back to defend Bin and Ning; the rebel agent Zhang Xin held Bin Prefecture, requisitioned military supplies on a wide scale, enlisted many troops, and planned to defect to Huaiguang in secret. Zhaosheng's father Huaibin served under Yougui; one night he rode out with several dozen men and killed Xin and his co-conspirators. Yougui sent Huaibin to announce the deed to the emperor's camp; Dezong received him with praise and inquiry and appointed him acting Vice Censor-in-Chief. Zhaosheng went to Huaiguang in tears and said, "My father has won glory for the realm; I, his son, deserve execution and must not be allowed to lead soldiers. " Huaiguang had him arrested. When the allied armies besieged Hezhong, Yougui encamped at Changchun Palace while Huaibin fought with exceptional vigor. After Huaiguang's defeat the emperor pardoned Zhaosheng and appointed him chief adjutant under Yougui. Father and son alike held grand preceptorate titles, served as household guests, and bore the rank of Vice Censor-in-Chief—a distinction the army took as a signal honor.
5
吐蕃犯邊，遊瑰自將守寧州，而禦士寬，軍驕。 及張獻甫來代，軍遂亂，朝晟逃於郊。 眾脅監軍，請以范希朝為節度使。 希朝時已在京師。 明日，朝晟出，紿眾曰：「予來賀所請之當也。 」眾稍定。 朝晟結諸將謀誅首惡者。 居三日，紿遣人自邠來，曰：「前請報罷，張公已舍邠矣，反者皆當死，吾不願盡誅也，第取首惡者。 」眾所讙指，斬二百餘人，獻甫遂入於軍。 帝以希朝為節度副使，而朝晟加御史大夫。
When Tibetans struck the border, Yougui took command in person to hold Ning Prefecture; his discipline was lax, and the troops grew insubordinate. When Zhang Xianfu arrived to replace him, the army mutinied at once, and Zhaosheng escaped into the outskirts. The mutineers seized the army supervisor and demanded that Fan Xichao be made military commissioner. Xichao was already in the capital at the time. The next day Zhaosheng appeared and told the troops, "I have come to congratulate you on asking for the right man. " The men calmed somewhat. Zhaosheng rallied the commanders to plot the execution of the ringleaders. Three days later he sent a messenger from Bin with a fabricated report: "Your petition has been refused; Lord Zhang has already quit Bin. Every rebel deserves death, but I do not mean to slaughter you all—only the ringleaders. " The men the crowd denounced were beheaded—more than two hundred—and Xianfu was able to take command. The emperor appointed Xichao deputy commissioner and promoted Zhaosheng to Censor-in-Chief.
6
貞元九年，城鹽州，發卒護境，朝晟屯木波堡。 會獻甫卒，有詔代為邠寧節度使。 朝晟請城方渠，合道，木波以遏吐蕃路。 詔問：「須兵幾何？ 」報曰：「部兵可辦。 」帝問：「前日城五原，興師七萬，今何易邪？ 」對曰：「鹽州之役，虜先知之。 今薄戎而城，虜料王師不十萬，勢難輕入。 若發部兵，十日至塞下，未三旬城畢，積芻聚糧，留卒守之，寇至不可拔，萊野翦夷，虜且走，此萬全計也。 若大發兵，閱月乃至，虜亦來，來必戰，戰則不暇城矣。 」帝納其策。 師次方渠，水乏。 有青蛇降險下走，視其跡，水從而流，朝晟使築防環之，遂為渟淵，士飲仰足，圖其事以聞。 有詔置祠，命泉曰應聖。 已城，吐蕃悉眾至，度不能害，乃引去。 復城馬嶺而歸，開地三百里。 十七年，卒於屯。
In the ninth year of Zhenyuan the court fortified Salt Prefecture and sent troops to guard the frontier; Zhaosheng garrisoned Mubo Fort. When Xianfu died, an edict named Zhaosheng military commissioner of Bin-Ning in his place. Zhaosheng proposed building fortresses at Fangqu, Hedao, and Mubo to choke off Tibetan advances. The emperor asked by edict, "How many troops will you need? " He replied, "The forces of my command can handle it. " The emperor said, "When we fortified Wuyuan we mobilized seventy thousand men—why is this so easy now? " He answered, "At Salt Prefecture the barbarians learned of our plans in advance. This time we will build close to their territory; they will assume the imperial host is under one hundred thousand and will not dare strike lightly. Send only my command's troops: in ten days they reach the frontier, in less than thirty the walls are finished, fodder and grain stockpiled, and a garrison left behind. When raiders come they cannot take the forts; we clear the countryside, the enemy retreats, and the plan is foolproof. Mobilize a huge army and a month passes before it arrives; the enemy comes too, battle is inevitable, and there will be no time to build. " The emperor accepted his plan. The army halted at Fangqu, where water was scarce. A green serpent was seen slithering down the cliff; where it passed, water followed. Zhaosheng had a retaining wall built around the spring, forming a deep pool from which the men drank their fill; he recorded the event and reported it to court. An edict ordered a shrine erected and named the spring "Responsive to the Sage." When the fortifications were finished the Tibetans marched in full force, saw they could do no harm, and withdrew. He fortified Maling as well and returned, opening three hundred li of new territory. In the seventeenth year he died in camp.
7
戴休顏弟戴休璿戴休晏
Dai Xiuyan; his younger brothers Dai Xiuxuan and Dai Xiuyan
8
戴休顏，字休顏，夏州人。 家世尚武，誌膽不常。 郭子儀引為大將，諭平党項羌，以安河曲。 試太常卿，封濟陰郡公，進封咸寧郡王，兼朔方節度副使。 城邠州功最，遷鹽州刺史。 朱泚反，率兵三千晝夜馳，奔問行在，德宗嘉之，賜實戶二百。 與渾瑊、杜希全、韓遊瑰等扞禦有勞。 帝進狩梁、洋，留守奉天。 李懷光屯咸陽，使人誘之，休顏斬其使，勒兵自守。 懷光眙駭，自涇陽夜走。 遷檢校工部尚書、奉天行營節度使。 合渾瑊兵破泚偏師，斬首三千級，追至中渭橋。 京師平，又與瑊率兵趨岐陽，邀泚殘黨。 加檢校尚書右僕射，進戶四百。 從乘輿至京師，賜女樂、甲第，拜左龍武軍統軍。 卒，贈揚州大都督。
Dai Xiuyan, courtesy name Xiuyan, was a native of Xia Prefecture. His family had long honored the martial arts, and his resolve and daring were out of the ordinary. Guo Ziyi took him on as a senior commander and charged him with pacifying the Tangut and Qiang to secure the Yellow River bend. He served provisionally as Minister of Ceremonies, was enfeoffed Duke of Jiyin, advanced to Prince of Xianning, and named deputy commissioner of Shuofang. His contribution to the fortification of Bin Prefecture ranked first, and he was transferred to prefect of Salt Prefecture. When Zhu Ci rebelled he raced day and night with three thousand men to the emperor's camp; Dezong praised him and granted two hundred substantive households. With Hun Jian, Du Xiquan, Han Yougui, and others he earned distinction in the defense of the capital. When the emperor withdrew toward Liang and Yang, he stayed behind to hold Fengtian. Li Huaiguang camped at Xianyang and sent envoys to win him over; Xiuyan executed the messenger and rallied his men to stand firm. Huaiguang was stunned and fled Jingyang under cover of night. He was promoted to acting Minister of Works and commissioner of the Fengtian field command. Joining Hun Jian he routed Zhu Ci's flank force, took three thousand heads, and pursued to the Middle Wei Bridge. After the capital was recovered he marched again with Jian toward Qiyang to cut off Zhu Ci's remnants. He was made acting Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs and granted four hundred additional households. He escorted the emperor back to the capital, received female musicians and a grand residence, and was named commander of the Left Dragon Martial Army. He died and was posthumously enfeoffed Grand Governor of Yang Prefecture.
9
弟休璿，歷開府儀同三司，封東陽郡王； 休晏，歷輔國大將軍，封彭城郡公。 俱以將略稱。
His younger brother Xiuxuan rose to Grand Preceptor with equipage equal to the Three Excellencies and was enfeoffed Prince of Dongyang; Xiuyan served as General Who Assists the State and was enfeoffed Duke of Pengcheng. Both were renowned for generalship.
10
陽惠元
Yang Huiyuan
11
陽惠元，平州人。 以趫勇奮，事平盧軍。 從田神功、李忠臣浮海入青州。 詔以兵隸神策，為京西兵馬使，鎮奉天。
Yang Huiyuan was a native of Ping Prefecture. Renowned for swift daring, he served in the Pinglu Army. He followed Tian Shenggong and Li Zhongchen by sea into Qing Prefecture. By edict his troops were attached to the Shence Army; he became commissioner of capital-west forces and garrisoned Fengtian.
12
德宗初立，稍繩諸節度跋扈者。 於是李正己屯曹州，田悅增河上兵，河南大擾。 詔移兵萬二千戍關東，帝御望春樓誓師，因勞遣諸將。 酒至神策，將士不敢飲。 帝問故，惠元曰：「初發奉天，臣之帥張巨濟與眾約：『是役也，不立功，毋飲酒！ 』臣不敢食其言。 」既行，有饋於道，惟惠元軍瓶罍不發。 帝咨嘆不已，璽書慰勞。 俄以兵三千會諸將擊田悅，戰御河，奪三橋，惠元功多。 以兵屬李懷光。
Soon after Dezong's accession he began reining in overbearing regional commanders. Li Zhengji then massed troops at Cao Prefecture, Tian Yue reinforced his forces on the Yellow River, and Henan fell into turmoil. Twelve thousand troops were ordered east of the pass; the emperor reviewed them at Wangchun Tower, administered the oath of campaign, and sent the commanders off with words of encouragement. When the toast reached the Shence contingent, the men refused to drink. The emperor asked why; Huiyuan said, "When we left Fengtian, my commander Zhang Juji pledged to the men: 'In this campaign, whoever wins no merit shall not touch wine! ' I dare not break that vow. " On the march, gifts of wine were offered along the road, yet only Huiyuan's men kept their flasks sealed. The emperor marveled aloud and sent an imperial letter of praise. Soon afterward he led three thousand men to join the attack on Tian Yue, fought at the Imperial River, captured three bridges, and won the greatest share of credit. His command was placed under Li Huaiguang.
13
及朱泚反，自河朔赴難，解奉天圍，加檢校工部尚書，攝貝州刺史。 詔惠元與神策行營節度使李晟、鄜坊節度使李建徽及懷光聯營便橋。 晟知懷光且叛，移屯東渭橋。 翰林學士陸贄諫帝曰：「四將接壘，晟等兵寡位下，為懷光所易，勢不兩完。 晟既慮變，請與惠元東徙，則建徽孤立。 宜因晟行，合兩軍皆往，以備賊為解，趣裝進道，則懷光計無所施。 」帝不從，使神策將李升往伺。 還奏：「懷光反明甚。 」是夕，奪二軍，惠元、建徽走奉天，懷光遣將冉宗馳騎追及於好畤。 惠元被髮呼天，血流出眥，袒裼戰而死。 二子晟、暠匿井中，皆及害。 建徽獨免。 詔贈惠元尚書左僕射，晟殿中監，暠邠州刺史。
When Zhu Ci rebelled he marched from the north to the emperor's aid, broke the siege of Fengtian, was made acting Minister of Works, and named provisional prefect of Bei. An edict ordered Huiyuan, Li Sheng of the Shence field army, Li Jianhui of Fufang, and Huaiguang to encamp together at Bian Bridge. Sheng, knowing Huaiguang was on the verge of rebellion, moved his camp to the Eastern Wei Bridge. Hanlin Academician Lu Zhi warned the emperor: "With all four camps adjacent, Sheng's force is small and his rank subordinate—Huaiguang can crush him at will. Both sides cannot survive this arrangement. If Sheng, fearing treachery, asks to move east with Huiyuan, Jianhui will be left alone. Use Sheng's move as the occasion to send both armies east together, citing defense against the rebel as the reason; if they pack and march at once, Huaiguang's plot will fail. " The emperor refused and sent the Shence officer Li Sheng to investigate. He returned and reported, "Huaiguang's rebellion is unmistakable. " That night he seized both armies. Huiyuan and Jianhui fled toward Fengtian; Huaiguang sent Ran Zong in pursuit and overtook them at Haozhi. Huiyuan tore loose his hair and cried to heaven, blood streaming from his eyes; stripped to the waist he fought until he was killed. His sons Sheng and Gao hid in a well and were both slain. Only Jianhui escaped. By edict Huiyuan was posthumously made Left Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs, Sheng Palace Supervisor, and Gao prefect of Bin.
14
少子旻，字公素。 惠元之死，被八創，墮別井，或救得免。 歷邢州刺史。 盧從史既縛，潞軍潰，有驍卒五千，從史嘗以子視者，奔於旻，旻閉城不內。 眾皆哭曰：「奴失帥，今公有完城，又度支錢百萬在府，少賜之，為表天子求旌節。 」旻開諭禍福遣之，眾感悟，遂還軍。 憲宗嘉之，遷易州刺史。
His youngest son Min, courtesy name Gongsu. At his father's death Min suffered eight wounds, fell into another well, and was pulled out alive. He later served as prefect of Xing Prefecture. After Lu Congshi was captured the Lu army collapsed; five thousand elite troops whom Congshi had treated like sons fled to Min, and Min shut the gates against them. They wept and said, "We have lost our commander; you hold an intact city, and a million strings of revenue funds lie in the treasury—give us a share and petition the Son of Heaven for a commander's commission. " Min opened the gates, explained the consequences, and dismissed them; the men came to their senses and marched away. Emperor Xianzong praised him and transferred him to prefect of Yi.
15
王師討吳元濟，以唐州刺史提兵深入二百里，薄申州，拔外郛，殘其垣。 以功加御史中丞。 容州西原蠻反，授本州經略招討使，擊定之。 進御史大夫，合邕、容兩管為一道。 卒，贈左散騎常侍。
During the campaign against Wu Yuanji, as prefect of Tang he led a deep thrust two hundred li, pressed Shen Prefecture, took its outer defenses, and battered its walls. For this he was promoted to Vice Censor-in-Chief. When the Xiyuan tribes of Rong Prefecture rebelled, he was named commissioner for pacification and suppression and crushed the uprising. He was promoted to Censor-in-Chief and the Yong and Rong circuits were merged into a single command. He died and was posthumously made Left Regular Attendant of the Cavalry.
16
李元諒
Li Yuanliang
17
李元諒，安息人。 本安氏，少為宦官駱奉先養息，冒姓駱，名元光。 美須髯，鷙敢有謀。 以宿衛積勞試太子詹事。 李懷讓節度鎮國，署奏以自副。 居軍十年，士心憚服。
Li Yuanliang was of Parthian origin. Born of the An clan, he was raised in youth by the eunuch Luo Fengxian, took Luo as his surname, and was called Yuanguang. Handsome of beard, he was fierce, bold, and resourceful. For long service in the palace guard he was appointed provisional Grand Mentor of the Heir Apparent. When Li Huairang commanded Zhenguo, he named Yuanliang his deputy in a memorial to court. He remained with the army ten years, and the troops feared and obeyed him.
18
德宗出奉天，賊遣將何望之襲華州，於是刺史董晉棄城走。 望之欲聚兵以絕東道，元諒自潼關引兵徑薄其城，拔之。 時兵興倉卒，裹罽為鎧，剡蒿為矢，募兵數日至萬餘，軍氣乃振。 賊來攻，輒卻。 時尚可孤守藍田，元諒屯昭應，王權壁中渭橋，賊兵不能逾渭南。 未幾，遷鎮國軍節度使，封武康郡王。 先是，詔發豳、隴兵東討李希烈。 師方出關，泚使劉忠孝召還； 至華陰，華陰尉李夷簡說驛官捕之，追及關，元諒斬以徇，所召兵不得入，由是華州獨完。 俄詔元諒與李晟收京師，次浐西。 元諒先奮鏖賊，敗之，進屯苑東，晟使壞苑垣入。 泚連戰皆北，遂大潰，京師平。 讓功於晟，退壁近郊。 加檢校尚書左僕射，實封戶五百，賜甲第、女樂、一子六品官。
When Dezong withdrew to Fengtian, the rebels sent He Wangzhi against Hua Prefecture; the prefect Dong Jin abandoned the city and fled. Wangzhi meant to mass troops and sever the eastern route; Yuanliang marched from Tong Pass, struck the city directly, and captured it. With war raised in haste, they wrapped felt for armor and carved artemisia for arrows; within days recruits topped ten thousand and the army's spirit revived. Whenever rebels attacked, they were driven back. Shang Keji held Lantian, Yuanliang camped at Zhaoying, and Wang Quan walled the Middle Wei Bridge, so rebel forces could not cross south of the Wei. Soon he was made military commissioner of the Zhenguo Army and enfeoffed Prince of Wukang. Earlier an edict had sent Bin and Long troops east against Li Xilie. The column had barely cleared the pass when Ci sent Liu Zhongxiao to recall them; At Huayin, assistant Li Yijian persuaded the courier station to arrest him; pursued to the pass, Yuanliang executed him as a warning, the recalled troops could not enter, and Hua Prefecture alone stayed intact. Soon an edict ordered Yuanliang and Li Sheng to retake the capital; they encamped west of Chan. Yuanliang led the fierce assault, routed the rebels, and encamped east of the imperial park while Sheng breached the park wall to enter. Ci lost battle after battle, broke completely, and the capital was recovered. He ceded the credit to Sheng and withdrew his camp to the suburbs. He was made acting Left Vice Director, granted five hundred substantive households, a mansion, female musicians, and a sixth-rank post for one son.
19
李懷光反，與馬燧、渾瑊討之。 其將徐廷光素易元諒，數嫚罵，為優胡戲，斥侮其祖。 又使約降，曰：「我降漢將耳。 」及馬燧至，降於燧。 元諒見韓遊瑰曰：「彼詬吾祖，今日斬之，子助我乎？ 」許諾。 既而遇諸道，即數其罪，叱左右斬之，詣燧謝。 燧大怒，將殺元諒，遊瑰見曰：「殺一偏裨尚爾，即殺一節度，法宜如何？ 」燧默然。 元諒請輸錢百萬勞軍自贖，瑊亦為請，燧赦之。 帝以專殺，恐有司劾治，前詔勿論。
When Li Huaiguang rebelled, he joined Ma Sui and Hun Jian against him. His general Xu Tingguang had long despised Yuanliang, repeatedly abused him, mocked him in comic skits, and insulted his forebears. He also sent to negotiate surrender, saying, "I will yield only to a Han general. " When Ma Sui arrived, he surrendered to Sui. Yuanliang told Han Yougui, "He cursed my forebears—I mean to kill him today; will you aid me? " Yougui agreed. Meeting him soon after among the allied commands, he listed his crimes, ordered his men to execute him, and went to apologize to Sui. Sui was furious and would have killed Yuanliang; Yougui intervened: "You would kill a subordinate for this—what of killing a commissioner by law? " Sui said nothing. Yuanliang offered a million strings to reward the troops in ransom; Jian pleaded too, and Sui pardoned him. Fearing prosecution for summary execution, the emperor issued a prior edict that the matter should not be pursued.
20
貞元三年，吐蕃請盟，詔以軍從瑊會平涼，元諒軍潘原、遊瑰軍洛口以為援。 元諒曰：「潘原去平涼七十里，虜詐不情，如有急，何以赴？ 請與公連屯。 」瑊以違詔，不聽。 瑊壁盟所二十里，元諒密徙營次之。 既會，元諒望雲物曰：「不詳，虜必有變！ 」傳令約部伍出陣。 俄而虜劫盟，瑊奔還，元諒兵成列出，而涇原節度使李觀亦以精兵五千伏險，與元諒相表裏，虜騎乃解。 元諒遣車重先，而與瑊振旅徐還，時以為有古良將風。 是會也，微元諒、觀二人，瑊且不免。 帝嘉嘆，賜善馬金幣良厚，因賜姓及名。

Han Yougui of Lingwu in Ling Prefecture began as a lieutenant under Guo Ziyi. When An Lushan rebelled, he sent Ashina Congli with five thousand Tongluo and Turk horsemen in false surrender to Shuofang, lured the Nine Fan Offices and Six Hu of the river bend to revolt—five hundred thousand tribesmen in all. Ziyi sent Yougui with Xin Jinggao to rout them, and the Nine Fan Offices returned to the fold. He rose repeatedly to acting commissioner of Bin-Ning.

Du Xiquan
35
杜希全，京兆醴泉人。 以裨將隸郭子儀，積功勞至朔方節度使。 軍令整嚴，士畏其威。 奉天之狩，希全與鄜坊節度使李建徽、鹽州刺史戴休顏、夏州刺史時常春引兵赴難。 次漠谷，為賊邀擊，乘高縱石下之，強弩雜發。 德宗使援之，不克，還保邠州。 賊平，遷檢校尚書左僕射、靈鹽豐夏節度使，封餘姚郡王。 將即屯，獻《體要》八章，砭切政病。 帝嘉納，賜《君臣箴》一篇。
Du Xiquan came from Liquan in Jingzhao. He served as lieutenant under Guo Ziyi and rose through merit to commissioner of Shuofang. His discipline was severe and the troops feared him. At Dezong's withdrawal to Fengtian, Xiquan marched with Li Jianhui of Fufang, Dai Xiuyan of Salt, and Shi Changchun of Xia to the emperor's aid. At Mogu Valley rebels ambushed them, rolling stones from the heights and loosing powerful crossbows. Dezong sent relief but failed; they fell back to Bin Prefecture. After the rebellion he was made acting Left Vice Director and commissioner of Ling, Salt, Feng, and Xia, enfeoffed Prince of Yuyao. On taking command he submitted eight chapters of Essentials sharply criticizing government failings. The emperor praised and accepted them and granted a chapter of Admonitions for Ruler and Minister.
36
尋兼夏綏銀節度都統，建言：「鹽州據要會，為塞保鄣。 自平涼背盟，城陷於虜，於是靈武勢縣，鄜坊單逼，為邊深患，請復城鹽州。 」乃詔希全及朔方、邠寧、銀夏、鄜坊、振武及神策行營諸節度合選士三萬五千屯鹽州，又敕涇原、劍南、山南軍深入吐蕃，牽撓其力，使不得犯塞。 執築凡六千人，閱二旬畢。 由是虜憚，不輕入。
Soon he also held overall command of Xia-Sui-Yin and memorialized that Salt Prefecture held a vital junction and was the frontier's bulwark. Since Pingliang's betrayal the city had fallen; Lingwu hung exposed and Fufang stood alone in peril—he asked to rebuild Salt Prefecture. An edict ordered Xiquan and commissioners of Shuofang, Bin-Ning, Yin-Xia, Fufang, Zhenwu, and the Shence field army to mass thirty-five thousand men at Salt, and ordered Jingyuan, Sword South, and southern Shan to raid Tibet and pin down their strength. Six thousand laborers finished the work in twenty days. The enemy thereafter feared to strike lightly.
37
希全居河西久，頗越法橫肆，帝數容掩其短。 豐州刺史李景略名出希全上，疑逼己，遂排劾之。 帝為斥以答其意。 素苦風眩，稍劇，益忌忍，遂誣殺判官李起，吏下累息。 卒，贈司空。
Long stationed on the western frontier, Xiquan often broke the law with arrogant license; the emperor repeatedly overlooked his faults. Fengzhou prefect Li Jinglue outshone him; fearing rivalry, Xiquan impeached and drove him out. The emperor removed Jinglue to satisfy him. Plagued by vertigo, he grew crueler, falsely killed judge Li Qi, and his staff lived in terror. He died and was posthumously made Minister of Works.
38
邢君牙
Xing Junya
39
邢君牙，瀛州樂壽人。 少從幽薊、平盧軍，以戰功歷果毅、折沖郎將。 安祿山反，從侯希逸涉海入青州。 田神功為兗鄆節度使，使君牙將兵屯好畤防盛秋。 吐蕃犯京師，代宗出陜，以扈從功，累封河間郡公。
Xing Junya came from Leshou in Ying Prefecture. In youth he served the Youji, Jishan, and Pinglu armies and rose through battle to fruiti-corps and vanguard commandant. When An Lushan rebelled he followed Hou Xiyi by sea into Qing Prefecture. Tian Shenggong of Yan-He sent him to garrison Haozhi against autumn raids. When Tibetans struck the capital Daizong withdrew to Shan; for escort merit he was repeatedly enfeoffed Duke of Hejian.
40
建中初，李晟從馬燧討田悅，以君牙為都將，在武安、襄國間凡五戰，斬馘功最。 德宗出奉天，晟率君牙倍道赴難，徙屯渭橋，軍中便宜，惟君牙得豫。 晟在鳳翔，數行邊，常以君牙守。 晟入朝，代為鳳翔觀察使。 俄領節度，檢校尚書右僕射。 吐蕃歲犯邊，君牙劭耕講戰以為備，戎不能侵。 又城隴州平戎川，號永信城。 卒官，贈司空。
Early in Jianzhong Li Sheng followed Ma Sui against Tian Yue and made Junya his chief commander; fighting between Wu'an and Xiangguo in five battles he took the greatest share of kills and captures. When Dezong withdrew to Fengtian, Sheng led Junya in forced march to the rescue and shifted to the Wei Bridge; on every expedient within the army only Junya was consulted. When Sheng was at Fengxiang he often patrolled the frontier and left Junya in command. When Sheng went to court Junya replaced him as observation commissioner of Fengxiang. Soon he also held the commission and was acting Right Vice Director. Tibetans raided yearly; Junya urged farming and drilled the troops for war, and the enemy could not break through. He also fortified Pingrong River in Long Prefecture and named the stronghold Yongxin City. He died in office and was posthumously made Minister of Works.
41
初，布衣張汾者，無紹而干君牙，軒然坐客上。 會吏擿簿書，以盜沒宴錢五萬，君牙怒其欺，汾不謝去，曰：「吾在京師，聞邢君牙一時豪俊，今乃與設吏論錢，云何？ 」君牙慚，遽釋吏，引為上客，留月餘，以五百縑為謝。 其屈己好士類此。
Early on a commoner named Zhang Fen came unintroduced to Junya and boldly took the seat above the other guests. When clerks audited the books and found fifty thousand strings of banquet funds stolen, Junya raged at the fraud; Fen left without apology and said, "In the capital I heard Xing Junya was a man of the age—now you haggle over money with a petty clerk? " Junya was ashamed, released the clerk at once, received Fen as an honored guest, kept him over a month, and sent five hundred bolts of silk in thanks. He humbled himself and honored men of talent in this fashion.
