劉鍾崔二王
Liu, Zhong, Cui, and the Two Wangs
2
◎劉鐘崔二王
Liu, Zhong, Cui, and the Two Wangs
3
劉幽求，冀州武強人。 聖歷中，舉制科中第。 調閬中尉，刺史不禮，棄官去。 久之，授朝邑尉。 桓彥範等誅張易之、昌宗，而不殺武三思，幽求謂彥範曰：「公等無葬地矣。 不早計，後且噬臍。 」不從。 既，五王皆為三思構死。
Liu Youqiu was a native of Wuqiang in Jizhou. During the Shengli reign period, he passed the special imperial examination. He was posted as assistant magistrate of Langzhong, but the prefect slighted him, so he resigned and left office. Some time later he was appointed assistant magistrate of Chaoyi. When Huan Yanfan and his allies killed Zhang Yizhi and Zhang Changzong but spared Wu Sansi, Youqiu told Yanfan, "You have doomed yourselves — you will have no graves to lie in." If you do not act now, you will regret it when it is too late." They did not heed him. Before long, all five princes were framed and put to death by Sansi.
4
臨淄王入誅韋庶人，預參大策，是夜號令詔敕一出其手。 以功授中書舍人，參知機務，爵中山縣男，實封二百戶，授二子五品官，二代俱贈刺史。 睿宗立，進尚書右丞、徐國公，增封戶至五百，賜物千段、奴婢二十人、第一區、良田千畝、金銀雜物稱是。
When the Prince of Linzi moved in to kill Empress Wei, Youqiu had helped shape the grand strategy; that night every order and edict came from his hand. For his service he was made Secretariat drafter with a voice in policy, ennobled as Baron of Zhongshan with an income of two hundred households, and both his sons were given fifth-rank posts; two generations of his forebears were posthumously honored as prefects. After Emperor Ruizong took the throne, Youqiu was promoted to Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs and created Duke of Xu; his fief was raised to five hundred households, and he received a thousand bolts of silk, twenty servants, a first-class mansion, a thousand mu of fertile land, and comparable gifts in gold and silver.
5
景雲二年，以戶部尚書罷政事。 不旬月，遷吏部，拜侍中。 璽詔曰：「頃王室不造，中宗厭代，戚孽專亂，將隕社稷，朕與王公皆幾於難。 幽求處危思奮，翊贊聖儲，協和義士，震殄元惡。 國家之復存，繄幽求是賴，厥庸茂焉，朕用嘉之。 雖胙以土宇，而賦入未廣。 昔西漢行封，更擇多戶； 東京定賞，復增大邑。 宜加賜實封二百戶，子子孫孫傳國無絕，特免十死，銘諸鐵券，以傳其功。 」先天元年，為尚書右仆射、同中書門下三品，監修國史。
In 711 he left the chief council as Minister of Revenue. Within the month he was moved to the Ministry of Personnel and appointed Palace Attendant. An imperial edict read: "Not long ago the house of Tang was in peril. Emperor Zhongzong passed away, wicked relatives seized power and threw the realm into chaos, and the throne itself was nearly lost — I and the princes were all brought to the brink of ruin." Youqiu rose to the crisis with resolve, supported the heir apparent, rallied loyal men, and crushed the arch-villain." The dynasty's survival rested on Youqiu alone; his achievement was outstanding, and I hold him in the highest regard." Though he has been granted a fief, its revenue remains modest." In the Western Han, enfeoffment was often enlarged by adding households;" under the Eastern Han, meritorious ministers likewise received expanded fiefs." He shall receive an additional two hundred taxable households, his line shall hold the fief in perpetuity, he is granted ten exemptions from capital punishment, and his merit shall be recorded on an iron certificate to pass down to posterity." In 712 he became Vice Director of the Right Secretariat, a Grand Councilor of the second rank, and supervisor of the compilation of the national history.
6
幽求自謂有勞於國，在諸臣右，意望未滿，而竇懷貞為左仆射，崔湜為中書令，殊不平，見於言面。 已而湜等附太平公主，有逆計。 幽求與右羽林將軍張暐定計，使暐說玄宗曰：「湜等皆太平黨與，日夜陰計，若不早圖，且產大害，太上不得高枕矣。 臣請督羽林兵除之。 」帝許之。 未發也，而暐漏言於侍御史鄧光賓，帝懼，即列其狀。 睿宗以幽求等屬吏，劾奏以疏間親，罪應死。 帝密申右之，乃流幽求於封州、暐於峰州、光賓於繡州。 明年，太平公主誅，即日召復舊官，知軍國事，還封戶，賜錦衣一襲。
Youqiu believed his service to the state placed him above the other ministers, and he was far from content when Dou Huaizhen became Left Vice Director and Cui Shi Secretariat Director; his resentment showed plainly in word and countenance. Before long Cui Shi and his faction aligned with Princess Taiping and plotted treason. Youqiu and Zhang Wei of the Right Imperial Guard devised a plan whereby Zhang would urge Xuanzong: "Cui Shi and the rest are Princess Taiping's partisans; they scheme day and night. Unless you strike first, great harm will follow — the Retired Emperor will not sleep easy." I ask leave to lead the Imperial Guard and eliminate them." The emperor agreed. Before the plan could be carried out, Zhang leaked word to Attending Censor Deng Guangbin; alarmed, the emperor reported the whole affair at once. Ruizong referred Youqiu and the others to the courts, which charged them with driving a wedge between father and son — a capital offense. Xuanzong secretly interceded on their behalf, and the sentence was commuted to exile: Youqiu to Fengzhou, Zhang Wei to Fengzhou, and Deng Guangbin to Xiuzhou. The following year, when Princess Taiping was put to death, he was recalled the same day to his former posts, placed in charge of military and civil affairs, had his fief households restored, and received a suit of brocade robes.
7
開元初，進尚書左丞相，兼黃門監，俄以太子少保罷。 姚崇素忌之，奏幽求郁怏散職，有怨言。 詔有司鞫治，宰相盧懷慎等奏言：「幽求輕肆不恭，失大臣禮，乖崖分之節。 」翌日，貶睦州刺史，削實封戶六百。 遷杭、郴二州，恚憤卒於道，年六十一。 贈禮部尚書，謚曰文獻。 六年，詔與蘇瑰配享睿宗廟廷。 建中中，追贈司徒。
Early in the Kaiyuan era he was promoted to Left Director of the Department of State Affairs and concurrently Director of the Palace Secretariat, but soon resigned as Junior Mentor to the Heir Apparent. Yao Chong, who had long disliked him, reported that Youqiu, bitter over his idle appointment, was voicing grievances. The emperor ordered an inquiry; Chancellor Lu Huaishen and others reported, "Youqiu has been insolent and disrespectful, failing in the deportment owed a chief minister and overstepping the bounds proper to his rank." The next day he was demoted to Prefect of Muzhou and stripped of six hundred fief households. Reassigned through Hang and Chen prefectures, he died on the road in bitterness at the age of sixty-one. He was posthumously honored as Minister of Rites with the posthumous name Literary Offering. In 718 an edict ordered him and Su Gui to share offerings in Emperor Ruizong's ancestral temple. During the Jianzhong reign he was posthumously created Minister of Education.
8
鐘紹京，虔州贛人。 初為司農錄事，以善書直鳳閣。 武後時署諸宮殿、明堂及銘九鼎，皆其筆也。 景龍中，為苑總監，會討韋氏難，紹京帥戶奴、丁夫從。 事平，夜拜中書侍郎，參知機務。 明日，進中書令、越國公，實封五百戶，賚賜與劉幽求等。 既當路，以賞罰自肆，當時惡之。 因上疏讓官，睿宗用薛稷謀，進戶部尚書，出為彭州刺史。
Zhong Shaojing was a native of Gan in Qianzhou. He began as a clerk in the Ministry of Agriculture and, for his excellence in calligraphy, was posted to the Phoenix Pavilion. Under Empress Wu he wrote the inscriptions for the palaces, the Bright Hall, and the Nine Cauldrons. During the Jinglong era he was Director of the Imperial Parks; when the coup against the Wei faction came, Shaojing led household retainers and laborers to join the fight. When the affair was settled, he was appointed that same night Vice Director of the Secretariat with a voice in policy. The next day he was promoted to Secretariat Director and created Duke of Yue with a fief of five hundred households, receiving rewards equal to those given Liu Youqiu and his fellows. Once in power he dispensed rewards and punishments at whim, and the court detested him for it. He then memorialized to resign his post; following Xue Ji's counsel, Ruizong promoted him to Minister of Revenue and sent him out as Prefect of Pengzhou.
9
玄宗即位，復拜戶部尚書，增實封，改太子詹事。 不為姚祟所喜，與幽求並以怨望得罪，貶果州刺史，賜封邑百戶。 後坐它事，貶懷恩尉，悉奪階封，再遷溫州別駕。 十五年入朝，見帝泣曰：「陛下忘疇日事邪，忍使棄死草莽！ 且同時立功者，今骨已朽，而獨臣在，陛下不垂湣乎？ 」帝惻然，即日授太子右諭德。 久之，遷少詹事。 年逾八十，以官壽卒。 紹京嗜書畫，如王義之、獻之、褚遂良真跡，藏家者至數十百卷。 建中中，追贈太子太傅。
When Xuanzong came to the throne, Shaojing was again made Minister of Revenue, his fief was increased, and he was transferred to Administrator of the Heir Apparent's Household. Disliked by Yao Chong, he and Youqiu alike were punished for their discontent; he was demoted to Prefect of Guozhou with a reduced fief of one hundred households. Later, convicted on another charge, he was demoted to assistant magistrate of Huai'en, stripped of all rank and fief, and eventually reassigned as Vice Prefect of Wenzhou. In the fifteenth year of Kaiyuan he came to court, wept before the emperor, and said, "Has Your Majesty forgotten what we did in those days? How can you leave me to die forgotten in the wilds?" Those who won merit beside me — their bones are dust, yet I alone still live. Will Your Majesty show no mercy?" The emperor was moved and that same day appointed him Right Moral Instructor to the Heir Apparent. Some time later he was made Junior Administrator of the Heir Apparent's Household. He passed eighty and died in office. Shaojing was a passionate collector of calligraphy and painting; families holding authentic works by Wang Xizhi, Wang Xianzhi, and Chu Suiliang might own scores or even hundreds of scrolls. During the Jianzhong reign he was posthumously created Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent.
10
帝崩，韋後專制，畏禍及，更因僧普潤、道士王曄私謁臨淄王以自托，且密贊大計。 王曰：「謀非計身，直紓親難爾。 」日用曰：「至孝動天，舉無不克。 然利先發，不則有後憂。 」及韋氏平，夜詔權雍州長史，以功授黃門侍郎，參知機務，封齊國公，賜實戶二百。 坐與薛稷相忿競，罷政事，為婺州長史。 歷揚、汴、允三州刺史。
After the emperor's death, when Empress Wei seized power, Riyong feared he would be implicated; through the monk Purun and the Daoist Wang Ye he paid a secret visit to the Prince of Linzi to place himself under the prince's protection and quietly endorsed the coup. The prince said, "This plot is not for my own gain — it is only to rescue my father from peril." Riyong replied, "Filial devotion so perfect moves Heaven itself — nothing undertaken in such a spirit can fail." Yet you must strike first — otherwise regret will follow." When the Wei faction was overthrown, he was named that night acting chief administrator of Yongzhou; for his service he was made Vice Director of the Palace Secretariat with a voice in policy, created Duke of Qi, and granted two hundred fief households. After a bitter feud with Xue Ji he left the council and was sent out as chief administrator of Wuzhou. He served in turn as prefect of Yang, Bian, and Yun prefectures.
11
由荊州長史入奏計，因言：「太平公主逆節有萌，陛下往以宮府討有罪，臣、子勢須謀與力，今據大位，一下制書定矣。 」帝曰：「畏驚太上皇，奈何？ 」日用曰：「庶人之孝，承順顏色； 天子之孝，惟安國家，定社稷。 若令奸宄竊發，以亡大業，可為孝乎？ 請先安北軍而後捕逆黨，於太上皇固無所驚。 」帝納之。 及討逆，詔權檢校雍州長史，以功益封二百戶，進吏部尚書。
Returning from Jingzhou as chief administrator to present his accounts, he said, "Princess Taiping's treason is already showing itself. When Your Majesty once used the palace guard to punish criminals, father and son had to combine counsel and force; now that you hold the throne, a single edict will settle the matter." The emperor said, "I fear frightening the Retired Emperor — what then?" Riyong said, "For a common man, filial piety means pleasing his parents' countenance;" for a Son of Heaven, filial piety means securing the realm and settling the altars of state." If traitors are allowed to strike and the dynasty is lost, how is that filial piety?" Secure the Northern Army first, then arrest the rebels — the Retired Emperor need never be alarmed." The emperor accepted his counsel. When the coup was carried out, he was named acting chief administrator of Yongzhou; for his merit his fief was increased by two hundred households and he was promoted to Minister of Personnel.
12
會帝誕日，日用采《詩》《大》、《小雅》二十篇及司馬相如《封禪書》獻之，借以諷諭，且勸告成事。 有詔賜衣一副、物五十段，以示無言不酬之義。
On the emperor's birthday, Riyong presented twenty pieces from the Major and Minor Odes of the Book of Songs together with Sima Xiangru's "Memorial on the Feng and Shan Sacrifices," using them as oblique counsel and urging him to complete the great enterprise. An edict rewarded him with a suit of robes and fifty bolts of silk, signifying that good counsel never goes unrewarded.
13
久之，坐兄累，出為常州刺史。 後以例減封戶三百，徙汝州。 開元七年，詔曰：「唐元之際，日用實贊大謀，功多不宜減封，復食二百戶。 」徙並州長史，卒年五十。 並人懷其惠，吏民數百皆縞服送喪。 贈吏部尚書，謚曰昭。 再贈荊州大都督。
Some time later, implicated through his elder brother's offense, he was sent out as Prefect of Changzhou. Later, under the usual rule, three hundred fief households were cut and he was transferred to Ruzhou. In 719 an edict read: "At the restoration of the Tang house, Riyong truly aided the grand design; his merit was too great for a reduced fief — restore two hundred households to his income." He was transferred to chief administrator of Bingzhou and died there at the age of fifty. The people of Bingzhou cherished his kindness; several hundred officials and commoners wore mourning white to escort his funeral. He was posthumously honored as Minister of Personnel with the posthumous name Illustrious. He was later posthumously created Area Commander of Jingzhou.
14
日用才辯絕人，而敏於事，能乘機反禍取富貴。 先天後，求復相，然亦不獲也。 嘗謂人曰「吾平生所事，皆適時制變，不專始謀。 然每一反思，若芒刺在背」雲。
Riyong's wit and eloquence were unmatched, and he was quick in action — able to turn danger to his advantage and win wealth and rank. After the Xiantian coup he sought to return to the chancellorship but never succeeded. He once told others, "Everything I have done in life was adapted to the moment — I never clung to the plan I started with." Yet every time I look back, it feels like a thorn in my flesh," he said.
15
子宗之，襲封。 亦好學，寬博有風檢，與李白、杜甫以文相知者。
His son Zongzhi inherited the title. He too was studious, generous in spirit and upright in conduct, and was known in literary friendship with Li Bai and Du Fu.
16
日用從父兄日知，字子駿，少孤貧，力學，以明經進至兵部員外郎。 與張說同為魏元忠朔方判官，以健吏稱。 遷洛州司馬，會譙王重福之變，官司逃，日知獨率吏卒助屯營擊賊，以功加銀青光祿大夫。 遷殿中少監，建言「廄馬多，請分牧隴右，省關畿芻調」。 授荊州長史，四遷京兆尹，封安平縣侯。 坐贓，為御史李如璧所劾，貶歙縣丞。 後歷殿中監，進中山郡公。 說執政，薦為御史大夫，帝不許，遂為左羽林大將軍，而自用崔隱甫。 隱甫繇是怨說。 日知俄授太常卿。 自以處朝廷久，每入謁，必與尚書齒，時謂「尚書裏行」。 終潞州長史，謚曰襄。
Riyong's cousin Rizhi, courtesy name Zijun, was orphaned young and poor; he studied diligently and, having passed the Classicist examination, rose to Vice Director in the Ministry of War. He served with Zhang Yue as judicial officer under Wei Yuanzhong in Shuofang and was known as an able administrator. Promoted to Vice Prefect of Luozhou, he was present during Prince Qiao Chongfu's rebellion; when officials fled, Rizhi alone led clerks and troops to aid the garrison in crushing the rebels and was rewarded with the title Silver Radiance Grand Master of Palace Service. Made Junior Supervisor of the Palace, he proposed, "The imperial stables hold too many horses — pasture them in Longyou to ease fodder levies on the capital region." He was appointed chief administrator of Jingzhou, was transferred four times to Intendant of Jingzhao, and was created Marquis of Anping. Convicted of corruption and impeached by Censor Li Rubi, he was demoted to assistant magistrate of She County. He later served as Supervisor of the Palace and was advanced to Duke of Zhongshan. When Zhang Yue was chief minister he recommended Rizhi for Censor-in-Chief, but the emperor refused and instead made Rizhi Left General of the Imperial Guard while appointing Cui Yinpu himself. Yinpu therefore bore a grudge against Zhang Yue. Rizhi was soon appointed Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices. Having long served at court, he insisted on taking rank with the Directors whenever he attended audience, and contemporaries called him "Director in practice." He ended his career as chief administrator of Luzhou with the posthumous name Accomplished.
17
王琚，懷州河內人。 少孤，敏悟有才略，明天文象緯。 以從父隱客嘗為鳳閣侍郎，故數與貴近交。 時年甫冠，見駙馬都尉王同皎，同皎器之。 會謀刺武三思，琚義其為，即與周璟、張仲之等共計。 事泄亡命，自傭於揚州富商家，識非庸人，以女嫁之，厚給以貲，琚亦賴以濟。 睿宗立，琚自言本末，主人厚賫使還長安。 玄宗為太子，閑遊獵韋、杜間，怠休樹下，琚以儒服見，且請過家，太子許之。 至所廬，乃蕭然窶陋。 坐久，殺牛進酒殊豐厚，太子駭異。 自是每到韋、杜，輒止其廬。
Wang Ju was a native of Henei in Huaizhou. Orphaned in youth, he was quick-witted and strategically gifted, versed in astronomy and the patterns of the heavens. His uncle Yinke had once served as Vice Director of the Phoenix Pavilion, so Ju often mingled with the court elite. Still in his teens, he met Chief Commandant of the Horse Wang Tongjiao, who took a liking to him. When a plot arose to assassinate Wu Sansi, Ju admired the cause and joined Zhou Jing, Zhang Zhongzhi, and others in planning it. When the plot was exposed he fled, hiring himself out to a wealthy Yangzhou merchant who saw he was no ordinary man, gave him his daughter in marriage, and supported him generously — Ju lived on that patronage. After Emperor Ruizong took the throne, Ju told his whole story; his patron gave him a rich send-off and sent him back to Chang'an. While Xuanzong was still heir apparent, he was hunting between Wei and Du when, resting beneath a tree, he met Ju dressed as a scholar who invited him home — the prince agreed. At Ju's house he found only a bare, shabby cottage. After they had sat awhile, Ju served beef and wine in lavish abundance, to the prince's astonishment. From then on, whenever he hunted in Wei and Du, he stopped at Ju's house.
18
初，太子在潞州，襄城張暐為銅鞮令，性豪殖，喜賓客弋獵事，厚奉太子，數集其家。 山東倡人趙元禮有女，善歌舞，得幸太子，止暐第，其後生子瑛者也。 太子已平內難，召暐，拜宮門郎，與姜交、崔滌、李令問、王守一、薛伯陽等並侍左右。 令問累擢殿中少監，守一太仆少卿。 此數人以東宮皆勢重天下。
Earlier, when the heir was posted in Luzhou, Zhang Hui of Xiangcheng was magistrate of Tongdi — a man of lavish tastes who loved guests, hunting, and sport, he entertained the heir generously and often hosted him at home. A Shandong entertainer named Zhao Yuanli had a daughter skilled in song and dance who won the heir's favor and lived at Hui's house — she later bore Prince Ying. After the heir quelled the inner crisis, he summoned Hui and made him Palace Gate Gentleman; Jiang Jiao, Cui Di, Li Lingwen, Wang Shouyi, Xue Boyang, and others likewise attended him. Lingwen rose to Junior Supervisor of the Palace; Shouyi to Vice Director of the Imperial Stud. Through their ties to the Eastern Palace, these men all wielded power across the realm.
19
琚是時方補諸暨縣主簿，過謝東宮，至廷中，徐行高視，侍衛何止曰：「太子在！ 」琚怒曰：「在外惟聞太平公主，不聞有太子。 太子本有功於社稷，孝於君親，安得此聲？ 」太子遽召見，琚曰：「韋氏躬行弒逆，天下動搖，人思李氏，故殿下取之易也。 今天下已定，太平專思立功，左右大臣多為其用，天子以元妹，能忍其過，臣竊為殿下寒心。 」太子命坐，且泣曰：「計將安便？ 」琚曰：「昔漢蓋主供養昭帝，其後與上官桀謀殺霍光，不及天子，而帝猶以大義去之。 今太子功定天下，公主乃敢妄圖，大臣樹黨，有廢立意。 太子誠召張說、劉幽求、郭元振等計之，憂可紓也。 」太子曰：「先生何以自隱而日與寡人遊？ 」琚曰：「臣善丹沙，且工諧隱，願比優人。 」太子喜，恨相知晚。 翌日授詹事府司直、內供奉，兼崇文學士。 日以諸王及姜皎等入侍，獨琚常豫秘謀。 不逾月，遷太子舍人，兼諫議大夫。 太子受內禪，擢中書侍郎。
Ju had just been appointed chief clerk of Zhuji County and came to thank the Eastern Palace; in the courtyard he walked slowly with head held high until guard He Zhi called out, "The heir is here!" Ju snapped, "Outside these walls one hears only of Princess Taiping — not of a crown prince." The crown prince has served the realm and honored his father — how has he come to such a reputation?" The prince summoned him at once. Ju said, "The Wei clan committed regicide and threw the realm into turmoil; the people yearned for the house of Li — that is why Your Highness won the throne so easily." Now the realm is settled, yet Princess Taiping schemes for power, most ministers at court serve her, and the emperor indulges her as his sister — I tremble for Your Highness." The prince bade him sit and wept, "What is to be done?" Ju said, "In Han times Princess Gai raised Emperor Zhao, then joined Shangguan Jie in plotting against Huo Guang — the plot never reached the emperor, yet Zhao still cast her off on principle." Your Highness has settled the realm, yet the princess dares plot rebellion and her faction among the ministers already contemplates deposing you." Summon Zhang Yue, Liu Youqiu, Guo Yuanzhen, and the like to counsel you, and your fears may be eased." The prince asked, "How can you hide yourself yet visit me every day?" Ju replied, "I am skilled in alchemy and in witty disguise — let me pass as a court entertainer." The prince was delighted and wished they had met sooner. The next day Ju was made Rectifier in the Heir Apparent's Household, Inner Attendant, and Scholar of the Chongwen Institute. Day after day princes, Jiang Jiao, and others attended the heir, but only Ju was regularly privy to secret counsel. Within the month he was made Heir Apparent Attendant and Remonstrance Grand Master. When the heir accepted the inner abdication, Ju was promoted to Vice Director of the Secretariat.
20
公主謀益甚，幽求、暐謀先事誅之，侍御史鄧光賓漏謀，不克，皆得罪。 久之，琚見事迫，請帝決策。 先天二年七月，乃與岐王、薛王、姜皎、李令問、王毛仲、王守一以鐵騎至承天門。 太上皇聞外嘩噪，召郭元振升承天樓，閉關以拒，俄而侍御史任知古召募數百人於朝堂，不得入。 少選，琚從帝至樓下，誅蕭至忠、岑義、竇懷貞，斬常元楷李慈北闕下、賈膺福李猷於內客省。 事平，琚進戶部尚書、封趙國公，皎工部尚書、楚國公，毛仲輔國大將軍、霍國公，守一太常卿、晉國公，各食實戶五百； 令問殿中監、宋國公，實戶三百。 琚、皎、令問辭不就，以舊官增戶二百。 於是帝召燕內殿，賜金銀雜皿皆一床、帛二千、第一區。
As the princess's plotting intensified, Youqiu and Zhang Wei planned a preemptive strike, but Attending Censor Deng Guangbin leaked the plot; it failed and all were punished. When the crisis pressed, Ju urged the emperor to act. In the seventh month of 713 he marched with the Princes of Qi and Xue, Jiang Jiao, Li Lingwen, Wang Maozhong, and Wang Shouyi at the head of armored cavalry to Chengtian Gate. Hearing the uproar outside, the Retired Emperor summoned Guo Yuanzhen to Chengtian Tower and shut the gates; Attending Censor Ren Zhigu soon rallied several hundred men in the audience hall but could not break in. Shortly after, Ju followed the emperor below the tower and executed Xiao Zhizhong, Cen Yi, and Dou Huaizhen; Chang Yuankai and Li Ci were beheaded below the northern gate, and Jia Yingfu and Li You in the Inner Guests Office. When the coup succeeded, Ju was made Minister of Revenue and Duke of Zhao; Jiao Minister of Works and Duke of Chu; Maozhong Assistant State General and Duke of Huo; Shouyi Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices and Duke of Jin — each with five hundred fief households; Lingwen was made Supervisor of the Palace and Duke of Song with three hundred households. Ju, Jiao, and Lingwen declined the new posts and kept their former offices with two hundred additional households each. The emperor then feasted them in the inner hall and gave each a couch-load of gold and silver vessels, two thousand bolts of silk, and a first-class mansion.
21
帝於琚眷委特異，豫大政事，時號「內宰相」。 每見閤中，視日薄乃得出。 遇休日，使者至第召之，而皇后亦使尚宮勞琚母，賜賚接足，群臣不能無望。 或說帝曰：「王琚、麻嗣宗皆譎詭縱橫，可與履危，不可與共安。 方天下已定，宜益求純樸經術士以自輔。 」帝悟，稍疏之。 俄拜御史大夫，持節巡天兵以北諸軍。 改紫微侍郎，道未至，拜澤州刺史，削封戶百。 歷九刺史，復封戶。 又改六州、二郡。
The emperor favored Ju above all others; he took part in major policy and was called the "inner chancellor." Each audience in the privy chamber lasted until sunset. On his days off envoys still came to fetch him, and the empress sent palace ladies to console Ju's mother with gifts in endless stream — ministers could not help but envy him. Someone told the emperor, "Wang Ju and Ma Zong are cunning schemers — useful in crisis, dangerous in peace." Now that the realm is settled, you should surround yourself with upright scholars versed in the classics." The emperor took the point and gradually distanced himself from Ju. Soon he was made Censor-in-Chief and sent with imperial authority to inspect the armies north of Tianbing. He was named Vice Director of the Palace Secretariat, but before he arrived he was made Prefect of Zezhou and stripped of one hundred fief households. He served as prefect of nine prefectures and eventually had his fief households restored. He was reassigned through six more prefectures and two commanderies.
22
琚自以立勛，至天寶時為舊臣，性豪侈，其處方面，去故就新，受饋遺至數百萬，侍兒數十，寶帳備具，闔門三百口。 既失誌，稍自放，不能遵法度。 在州與官屬小史酋豪飲謔、摴博、藏鉤為樂。 每徙官，車馬數里不絕。 從賓客女伎馳弋，凡四十年。 李邕故與琚善，皆華首外遷，書疏往復，以譴謫留落為慊。 右相李林甫恨琚恃功使氣，欲除之，使人劾發琚宿贓，削封階，貶江華員外司馬。 又使羅希奭深按其罪，琚懼，仰藥，未及死，希奭縊之。 時人哀其無罪。 始，琚為中書侍郎，母居洛陽，來京師，讓琚曰：「爾家上世皆州縣職，今汝無攻城野戰勞，以謅佞取容，海內切齒，吾恐汝家墳墓無人復掃除也。 」琚卒不免。 寶應元年，贈太子少保。
Ju counted himself among the founders of the dynasty; by the Tianbao era he was an elder statesman of lavish tastes. In every regional post he moved from place to place, accepting gifts in the millions, keeping dozens of concubines, sumptuous quarters, and a household of three hundred. Once his ambitions were thwarted, he gave himself over to excess and ignored the law. In office he caroused with clerks and local magnates, gambling and playing drinking games. Whenever he changed posts, his train of horses and carriages stretched for miles. For forty years he ranged with guests, courtesans, and hunting parties. Li Yong had long been his friend; both were white-haired exiles who exchanged letters and found bitter comfort in their shared disgrace. Right Chancellor Li Linfu hated Ju's arrogance and sought to destroy him; he had Ju impeached for long-standing corruption, stripped of rank and fief, and demoted to acting prefect of Jianghua. He sent Luo Xiyi to press the case further; Ju took poison in fear, and before it could kill him Luo strangled him. Contemporaries mourned him as innocent. When Ju first became Vice Director of the Secretariat, his mother came from Luoyang to the capital and rebuked him: "Our forebears held only county and prefectural posts. You have won no merit in battle yet curried favor through flattery — the realm despises you, and I fear no one will tend our family graves." Ju could not escape her prophecy in the end. In 762 he was posthumously created Junior Mentor to the Heir Apparent.
23
太平之誅，張暐召還為大理卿，封鄧國公，實封戶三百，進京兆尹，入侍宴樂，出主京邑，時人以為寵，然自以幹治稱。 累遷太子詹事，判尚書左右丞，再為羽林大將軍，三至左金吾大將軍，以年高加特進。 子履冰、季良，弟晤，仕皆清近。 暐嘗還鄉上冢，帝賜詩及錦袍繒彩。 乘驲就道，子弟車馬聯咽。 使者賜賚，敕州縣供擬，居處尊顯。 天寶五載卒，年九十，贈開府儀同三司。 履冰，歷金吾將軍，季良，殿中監，俱列棨戟。
After Princess Taiping's execution, Zhang Hui was recalled as Director of the Court of Judicial Review, created Duke of Deng with three hundred households, and made Intendant of Jingzhao — attending banquets within the palace and governing the capital without; contemporaries saw this as favor, though he was known for able administration. He rose to Administrator of the Heir Apparent's Household, served as acting Left and Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs, twice as General of the Imperial Guard, thrice as Left General of the Golden Crow Guard, and received special advancement in rank for his great age. His sons Lübing and Jiliang and his younger brother Wu all held posts close to the throne. When Hui once returned home to tend his ancestors' graves, the emperor gave him a poem and brocade robes. He traveled by imperial post relay; sons and brothers followed in an endless train of carriages. Imperial envoys brought gifts; edicts ordered local officials to provide for him; he lived in conspicuous honor. He died in 746 at the age of ninety and was posthumously created Palace Attendant of the First Rank. Lübing served as General of the Golden Crow Guard; Jiliang as Supervisor of the Palace — both bore the halberd insignia of high rank.
24
王毛仲，高麗人。 父坐事，沒為官奴，生毛仲，故長事臨淄王。 王出潞州，有李守德者，為人奴，善騎射，王市得之，並侍左右，而毛仲為明悟。 景龍中，王還長安，二人常負房箙以從。 王數引萬騎帥長及豪俊，賜飲食金帛，得其歡心。 毛仲曉旨，亦布誠結納，王嘉之。
Wang Maozhong was a native of Goryeo. His father was convicted and enslaved to the government; Maozhong was born in bondage and from childhood served the Prince of Linzi. When the prince left Luzhou he bought Li Shoude, a slave skilled in riding and archery, and both attended him — Maozhong being the sharper of the two. During the Jinglong era the prince returned to Chang'an, and the two often carried quivers as they followed him. The prince often entertained commanders of the Ten Thousand Riders and bold spirits with food, drink, gold, and silk, winning their loyalty. Maozhong understood his master's aims and won men over with sincerity — the prince valued him highly.
25
韋後稱制，令韋播、高嵩為羽林將軍，押萬騎，以苛峭樹威。 果毅葛福順、陳玄禮訴於王，王方與劉幽求、薛宗簡及利仁府折沖麻嗣宗謀舉大計，幽求諷之，皆願效死，遂入討韋氏。 守德從帝止苑中，而毛仲匿不出，事定數日，乃還，不之責，例擢將軍。
When Empress Wei seized power she appointed Wei Bo and Gao Song as generals of the Imperial Guard over the Ten Thousand Riders, ruling them with harsh severity. Company commanders Ge Fushun and Chen Xuanli appealed to the prince, who was then plotting with Liu Youqiu, Xue Zongjian, and defense commissioner Ma Zong — Youqiu rallied them, and all pledged their lives; they marched in to overthrow the Wei faction. Shoude followed the emperor into the park while Maozhong hid and did not appear; several days after the coup he returned unblamed and was promoted to general by precedent.
26
王為皇太子，以毛仲知東宮馬駝鷹狗等坊。 不旬歲，至大將軍，階三品。 與誅蕭至忠等，以功進輔國大將軍，檢校內外閑廄，知監牧使，進封霍國公，實封戶五百。 與諸王及姜皎等侍禁中，至連榻而坐。 帝暫不見，惘惘若有失，見則釋然。 開元九年，詔持節為朔方道防禦討擊大使，與左領軍大總管王晙、天兵軍節度使張說、幽州節度使裴伷先等數計事。
When the prince became heir apparent, Maozhong was placed in charge of the Eastern Palace stables and hunting yards. Within the year he rose to Grand General of the third rank. For his part in executing Xiao Zhizhong and others he was made Assistant State General, placed in charge of all imperial stud parks and pasturage, and created Duke of Huo with five hundred households. He attended the emperor in the inner palace with the princes and Jiang Jiao and others, even sitting on shared couches with them. When the emperor did not see him for a time he grew restless; when Maozhong appeared he was at ease. In 721 he was commissioned with imperial authority as Defense and Strike Commissioner of the Shuofang Circuit and met several times with Grand Commander Wang Jun, Tianbing Military Governor Zhang Yue, Youzhou Military Governor Pei Youxian, and others to plan strategy.
27
毛仲始見飾擢，頗持法，不避權貴為可喜事。 兩營萬騎及閑廄官吏憚之無敢犯，雖官田草萊，樵斂不敢欺。 於牧事尤力，娩息不訾。 初監馬二十四萬，後乃至四十三萬，牛羊皆數倍。 蒔茼麥、苜蓿千九百頃以禦冬。 市死畜，售絹八萬。 募嚴道僰僮千口為牧圉。 檢勒芻菽無漏隱，歲贏數萬石。 從帝東封，取牧馬數萬匹，每色一隊，相間如錦繡，天子才之。 還，加開府儀同三司，自開元後，唯王仁皎、姚崇、宋璟及毛仲得之。
At first, newly honored with high office, he enforced the law without fear of the powerful — a welcome change. Officers of both Ten Thousand Rider camps and the stud parks feared him and dared not transgress; even on government land no one dared cheat the timber or fodder levies. He threw himself especially into herd management, and foals multiplied beyond counting. Horses under his charge rose from two hundred forty thousand to four hundred thirty thousand, and cattle and sheep multiplied several times over. He planted gongmai and alfalfa on nineteen hundred qing to feed the herds through winter. He sold off dead stock for eighty thousand bolts' worth of silk. He recruited a thousand Bo retainers from Yandao as herders. He tightened control over fodder so that nothing was concealed, yielding tens of thousands of shi in surplus each year. At the emperor's eastern feng sacrifice he paraded tens of thousands of horses in companies by color, alternating like brocade — the emperor was delighted. On his return he was given Palace Attendant of the First Rank — since the Kaiyuan era only Wang Renjiao, Yao Chong, Song Jing, and Maozhong had received it.
28
然資小人，誌既滿，不能無驕，遂求為兵部尚書，帝不悅，毛仲鞅鞅。 及與葛福順為姻家，而守德及左監門將軍盧龍子唐地文、左右威衛將軍王景耀高廣濟數十人與毛仲相倚杖為奸。 毛仲恃舊，最不法。 中使至其家稱詔，毛仲不甚恭，位卑者，或踞見，迕意即侮誶，以氣淩之，直出其上。 高力士、楊思勖等銜之。 毛仲有兩妻，其一上所賜，皆有國色。 嘗生子，帝命力士就賜，仍授子五品官，還，問曰：「毛仲喜乎？ 」力士奏：「毛仲熟視臣曰：『是子亦何辱三品官？ 』」帝怒曰：「前毛仲負我，未嘗為意，今以嬰兒顧云云。 」力士等知帝怒，它日，從容曰：「北門奴官皆毛仲所與，不除之，必起大患。 」後毛仲移書太原索甲仗，少尹嚴挺之以聞，帝恐毛仲遂亂，匿其狀。 十九年，有詔貶州，福順壁州，守德嚴州，盧龍子唐地文振州，王景耀黨州，高廣濟道州，並為別駕員外置。 毛仲四子悉奪官，貶惡地，緣坐數十人。 有詔縊毛仲於零陵。
Yet he was a man of low birth; once his ambitions were satisfied he could not restrain his pride and sought the post of Minister of War — the emperor was displeased, and Maozhong grew resentful. He allied by marriage with Ge Fushun, and Shoude, Left Gate Guard General Lu Longzi Tang Diwen, Majestic Guard Generals Wang Jingyao and Gao Guangji, and dozens of others leaned on Maozhong to commit abuses together. Maozhong, relying on his long service, was the worst lawbreaker of them all. When palace envoys came to his house with imperial orders, Maozhong showed little respect; toward men of lower rank he sometimes received them sprawled on his seat, and if they displeased him he abused them outright, browbeating them as though he stood above the throne itself. Gao Lishi, Yang Sixun, and others bore grudges against him. Maozhong had two wives, one of them a gift from the emperor; both were renowned beauties. When his son was born, the emperor sent Lishi with gifts and made the boy a fifth-rank official; on his return he asked, "Was Maozhong pleased?" Lishi reported, "Maozhong stared at me and said, 'Why should this child stoop to a mere third-rank post?'" The emperor raged, "Maozhong has wronged me before and I overlooked it — now he scorns even my gift to his infant son." Lishi and the others, seeing the emperor's anger, said another day in passing, "The officers of the Northern Gate were all appointed by Maozhong — unless they are removed, great trouble will follow." Later Maozhong wrote to Taiyuan demanding arms and armor; Junior Administrator Yan Tingzhi reported it, but the emperor, fearing Maozhong might rebel, suppressed the report. In 731 an edict demoted Maozhong to a distant prefecture; Fushun to Bizhou, Shoude to Yanzhou, Lu Longzi Tang Diwen to Zhenzhou, Wang Jingyao to Dangzhou, and Gao Guangji to Daozhou — all as supernumerary vice prefects. All four of Maozhong's sons were stripped of rank and exiled to harsh regions, and dozens were punished by association. By edict Maozhong was strangled at Lingling.
29
守德本名宜得，立功乃改今名，位武衛將軍。 嘗遇故主於道，主走避，守德命左右迎之至第，親上食奉酒，主流汗不敢當。 數日，入奏曰：「臣蒙國恩過分，而故主無寸祿，請解官授之。 」帝嘉其誌，擢為郎將。
Shoude's original name was Yide; after his meritorious service he received his present name and rose to Majestic Guard General. Once he met his former master on the road; the man fled in fear. Shoude had his attendants bring him to his mansion, served him food and wine in person — the master sweated and dared not accept. Days later he memorialized, "I have received more grace from the state than I deserve, while my former master has no livelihood — I beg to resign my post and give it to him." The emperor admired his loyalty and promoted the former master to Commandant.
30
陳玄禮宿衛宮禁，以淳篤自檢。 帝嘗欲幸虢國夫人第，諫曰：「未宣敕，不可輕去就。 」帝為止。 後在華清宮，正月望夜，帝將出遊，復諫曰：「宮外曠野無備豫，陛下必出遊，願歸城闕。 」帝不能奪。 安祿山反，謀誅楊國忠闕下，不克，至馬嵬，卒誅之。 從入蜀。 還，封祭國公。 及李輔國遷帝西內，玄禮以老卒。
Chen Xuanli long guarded the inner palace and held himself to strict integrity. When the emperor wished to visit the Lady of Guo's mansion, he remonstrated, "No edict has been issued — Your Majesty must not go on impulse." The emperor desisted. Later at Huaqing Palace, on the full-moon night of the first month, when the emperor meant to go out, he remonstrated again, "Beyond the palace lies open country with no guard — if Your Majesty must go out, I beg you return to the capital." The emperor could not refuse him. When An Lushan rebelled, he plotted to kill Yang Guozhong at the palace gates but failed; at Mawei he finally put Guozhong to death. He followed the emperor into Shu. On the return he was created Duke of Ji. After Li Fuguo moved the emperor to the Western Inner Palace, Xuanli died of old age.
31
贊曰：幽求之謀，紹京之果，日用之智，琚之辯，皆足濟危紓難，方多故時，必資以成功者也。 雄邁之才，不用其奇則厭然不滿，誠不可與共治平哉！ 姚崇勸不用功臣，宜矣。 然待幽求等恨太薄雲。 毛仲小人，誌得而驕，不足論已。
The commentator writes: Youqiu's strategy, Shaojing's decisiveness, Riyong's wit, and Ju's eloquence were all equal to saving the dynasty in crisis — in turbulent times such men are indispensable to success. Men of towering talent, if their gifts are not employed, grow restless and discontent — they truly cannot share in governing a settled age! Yao Chong was right to urge that meritorious ministers not be kept in power. Yet his treatment of Youqiu and the others was perhaps too cold. Maozhong was a petty man who grew arrogant once his ambitions were met — hardly worth mention.
