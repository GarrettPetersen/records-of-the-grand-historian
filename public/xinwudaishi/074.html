1
奚吐渾達靼党項突厥吐蕃回鶻于闐高麗渤海新羅黑水靺鞨南詔蠻牂牁昆明占城
Xi, Tuyuhun, Dada, Tangut, Turks, Tibetans, Uyghurs, Khotan, Goryeo, Bohai, Silla, Heishui Mohe, Nanzhao, Zhangke, Kunming, and Champa.
2
奚，本匈奴之別種。 當唐之末，居陰涼川，在營府之西，幽州之西南，皆數百里。 有人馬二萬騎。 分爲五部：一曰阿薈部，二曰啜米部，三曰粵質部，四曰奴皆部，五曰黑訖支部。 後徙居琵琶川，在幽州東北數百里。 地多黑羊，馬嵒前蹄堅善走，其登山逐獸，下上如飛。
The Xi were originally an offshoot of the Xiongnu. By the late Tang they lived along the Yinliang River, several hundred li west of the frontier camp and southwest of Youzhou. They could field twenty thousand mounted warriors. They were split into five divisions: the Ahui, Chuomi, Yuezhi, Nujie, and Heiqizhi. Later they relocated to the Pipa River, several hundred li northeast of Youzhou. Black sheep were plentiful there, and their horses had exceptionally tough forehooves and great speed; hunting in the mountains, they seemed to fly up and down the slopes.
3
契丹阿保機強盛，室韋、奚、霫皆服屬之。 奚人常爲契丹守界上，而苦其苛虐，奚王去諸怨叛，以別部西徙媯州，依北山射獵，常采北山麝香、仁參賂劉守光以自托。 其族至數千帳，始分爲東、西奚。 去諸之族，頗知耕種，歲借邊民荒地種穄，秋熟則來獲，窖之山下，人莫知其處。 爨以平底瓦鼎，煮穄爲粥，以寒水解之而飲。
As the Khitan leader Abaoji rose to power, the Shiwei, the Xi, and the Wuhuan Xi all came under his sway. The Xi were often made to guard the Khitan borderlands and groaned under their cruelty. King Quzhu of the Xi rebelled in anger, led a splinter group west to Gui Prefecture, and lived by hunting in the northern hills, regularly trading musk and ginseng from those slopes to Liu Shouguang in exchange for his patronage. His following grew to several thousand tents, and the Xi were first split into eastern and western branches. Quzhu's band had learned something of agriculture. Each year they sowed millet on abandoned plots borrowed from frontier settlers, returned in autumn to reap the crop, and cached the grain in hidden pits in the hills, whose locations outsiders could not find. They cooked in flat-bottomed clay pots, boiling millet into gruel that they thinned with cold water before drinking.
4
去諸卒，子掃剌立。 莊宗破劉守光，賜掃剌姓李，更其名紹威。 紹威卒，子拽剌立。 同光以後，紹威父子數遣使朝貢。 初，紹威娶契丹女舍利逐不魯之姊爲妻，後逐不魯叛亡入西奚，紹威納之。 晉高祖入立，割幽州雁門以北入於契丹，是時紹威與逐不魯皆已死，耶律德光已立晉北歸，拽剌迎謁馬前，德光曰：「非爾罪也。 負我者，掃剌與逐不魯爾。 」乃發其墓，粉其骨而揚之。 後德光滅晉，拽剌常以兵從。 其後不復見於中國。
After Quzhu died, his son Saoci became chief. After Emperor Zhuangzong overthrew Liu Shouguang, he gave Saoci the surname Li and the new name Shaowei. Shaowei was succeeded by his son Zhuaila. From the Tongguang reign onward, father and son sent tribute missions again and again. Shaowei had first married the elder sister of the Khitan woman Shelizhubulu. When Zhubulu later defected to the Western Xi, Shaowei took her in as well. When the Later Jin founder took the throne, he ceded everything north of Youzhou and Yanmen Pass to the Khitan. Shaowei and Zhubulu were already dead. Yelü Deguang, having placed Jin on the throne, was riding north when Zhuaila greeted him at his stirrup. Deguang said, "The fault is not yours." Those who betrayed me are Saoci and Zhubulu." He had their graves opened, their bones ground to dust, and the dust cast to the winds. When Deguang later destroyed Jin, Zhuaila regularly marched at the head of his warriors in support. After that they disappear from the Chinese record.
5
自去諸徙媯州，自別爲西奚，而東奚在琵琶川者，亦爲契丹所並，不復能自見云。
Once Quzhu resettled at Gui and the Western Xi stood apart, the Eastern Xi on the Pipa River were swallowed up by the Khitan as well, and neither branch could any longer assert itself independently.
6
吐渾，本號吐谷渾，或曰乞伏乾歸之苗裔。 自後魏以來，名見中國，居於青海之上。 當唐至德中，爲吐蕃所攻，部族分散，其內附者，唐處之河西。 其大姓有慕容、拓拔、赫連等族。 懿宗時，首領赫連鐸爲陰山府都督，與討龐勳，以功拜大同軍節度使。 爲晉王所破，其部族益微，散處蔚州界中。 莊宗時，有首領白承福者，依中山北石門爲柵，莊宗爲置寧朔、奉化兩府，以承福爲都督，賜其姓名爲李紹魯。 終唐時，常遣使朝貢中國。
The Tuyuhun, originally known as the Tuyuhun in full, were said to descend from Qifu Gangui. Their name had been known in China since the Northern Wei, when they lived beyond Qinghai. During the Tang Zhide reign they were overrun by Tibet, their clans scattered, and those who submitted were resettled by the Tang in Hexi. Prominent lineages among them included the Murong, Tuoba, and Helian. Under Emperor Yizong, the chief Helian Duo served as commander of Yinshan Prefecture, helped suppress Pang Xun's rebellion, and was rewarded with the post of military commissioner of Datong. Defeated by the Prince of Jin, the people grew weaker still and were scattered along the Yuzhou frontier. During Zhuangzong's reign a leader named Bai Chengfu fortified Shimen north of Zhongshan. Zhuangzong created the Ningshuo and Fenghua prefectures for his people, appointed Chengfu their commander, and bestowed the Chinese name Li Shaolu. Throughout the Tang they regularly sent tribute missions to the court.
7
晉高祖立，割雁門以北入於契丹，於是吐渾爲契丹役屬，而苦其苛暴。 是時，安重榮鎮成德，有異志，陰遣人招吐渾入塞，承福等乃自五台入處中國。 契丹耶律德光大怒，遣使者責誚高祖，高祖恐懼，遣供奉官張澄率兵搜索並、鎮、忻、代等州山谷中吐渾驅出之。 然晉亦苦契丹，思得吐渾爲緩急之用，陰遣劉知遠鎮太原慰撫之。 終高祖時，承福數遣使者朝貢。 後出帝與契丹絕盟，召承福入朝，拜大同軍節度使，待之甚厚。 契丹與晉相距於河，承福以其兵從出帝禦虜。 是歲大熱，吐渾多疾死，乃遣承福歸太原，居之嵐、石之間。 劉知遠稍侵辱之，承福謀復亡出塞，知遠以兵圍其族，殺承福及其大姓赫連海龍、白可久、白鐵匱等，其羊馬貲財巨萬計，皆籍沒之，其餘衆以其別部王義宗主之。 吐渾遂微，不復見。
After the Jin founder took power and ceded everything north of Yanmen to the Khitan, the Tuyuhun fell under Khitan domination and endured their brutal rule. An Chongrong, then military governor of Chengde, was plotting treason and secretly invited the Tuyuhun across the border. Chengfu and his followers came in from Wutai and settled within China. Yelü Deguang flew into a rage and sent envoys to upbraid the Jin emperor, who in fear dispatched the palace attendant Zhang Cheng to scour the hills of Bing, Zhen, Xin, and Dai and expel every Tuyuhun he could find. Jin itself was harried by the Khitan and wanted the Tuyuhun as allies in a crisis, so it quietly posted Liu Zhiyuan at Taiyuan to win them over. For the rest of Gaozu's reign Chengfu sent tribute missions again and again. When Emperor Chudi later renounced the Khitan alliance, he summoned Chengfu to court, made him military commissioner of Datong, and lavished favors on him. As Khitan and Jin armies confronted each other along the Yellow River, Chengfu marched with his warriors to aid Chudi against the enemy. That summer was punishingly hot, and disease carried off many Tuyuhun. Chudi sent Chengfu back to Taiyuan to settle his people between Lan and Shi prefectures. Liu Zhiyuan began to bully them. When Chengfu planned another escape beyond the passes, Zhiyuan surrounded the tribe, executed Chengfu and leading clansmen such as Helian Hailong, Bai Kejiu, and Bai Tiegui, seized their vast herds and wealth for the state treasury, and left the survivors under Wang Yizong of a collateral branch. The Tuyuhun thereafter faded from view.
8
初，唐以承福之族爲熟吐渾。 長興中，又有生吐渾杜每兒來朝貢。 每兒，不知其國地、部族。 至漢乾祐二年，又有吐渾何戛剌來朝貢，不知爲生、熟吐渾，蓋皆微，不足考錄。
The Tang had earlier classified Chengfu's band as the acculturated, or "ripe," Tuyuhun. During the Changxing reign a leader of the wild, or "raw," Tuyuhun named Du Meier also came to pay tribute. Nothing is known of Meier's homeland or clan affiliation. In the second year of Later Han's Qianyou reign yet another Tuyuhun envoy, He Gela, arrived with tribute. Whether these visitors were raw or ripe Tuyuhun cannot be told; all were too insignificant to warrant fuller notice.
9
達靼，靺鞨之遺種，本在奚、契丹之東北，後爲契丹所攻，而部族分散，或屬契丹，或屬渤海，別部散居陰山者，自號達靼。 當唐末，以名見中國。 有每相溫、于越相溫，咸通中，從硃邪赤心討龐勳。 其後李國昌、克用父子爲赫連鐸等所敗，嘗亡入達靼。 後從克用入關破黃巢，由是居雲、代之間。 其俗善騎射，畜多駝、馬。 其君長、部族名字，不可究見，惟其嘗通於中國者可見云。
The Dada were descendants of the Mohe who once lived northeast of the Xi and Khitan. After Khitan attacks broke them apart, some bands joined the Khitan and others Bohai, while splinter groups along the Yinshan Mountains took the name Dada for themselves. By the late Tang their name was on record in China. Leaders named Meixiangwen and Yuyuexiangwen joined Zhuye Chixin's campaign against Pang Xun in the Xiantong period. Later, when Li Guochang and his son Keyong were routed by Helian Duo and his allies, they took refuge among the Dada. They later marched with Keyong through the passes to crush Huang Chao and thereafter settled between Yun and Dai prefectures. They excelled at mounted archery and raised great numbers of camels and horses. Most of their chiefs and tribal names are lost to us; only those who had contact with China appear in the record.
10
同光中，都督折文逋數自河西來貢駝、馬。 明宗討王都於定州，都誘契丹入寇，明宗詔達靼入契丹界，以張軍勢，遣宿州刺史薛敬忠以所獲契丹團牌二百五十及弓箭數百賜雲州生界達靼，蓋唐常役屬之。 長興三年，首領頡哥率其族四百餘人來附。 訖于顯德，常來不絕。
During the Tongguang reign the commander Zhe Wenyu made repeated missions from Hexi bearing camels and horses as tribute. When Mingzong attacked Wang Du at Dingzhou, Du drew Khitan raiders into the war. Mingzong ordered Dada bands into Khitan territory to swell his front, and sent the prefect of Suzhou, Xue Jingzhong, to reward the frontier Dada of Yunzhou with two hundred fifty captured Khitan round shields and hundreds of bows and arrows—Tang had long treated them as auxiliaries. In the third year of Changxing the chief Xiege brought more than four hundred followers to submit. Tribute missions continued without interruption through the Xiande era.
11
党項，西羌之遺種。 其國在《禹貢》析支之地，東至松州，西接葉護，南界春桑，北鄰吐渾，有地三千餘里。 無城邑而有室屋，以毛罽覆之。 其人喜盜竊而多壽，往往百五六十歲。 其大姓有細封氏、費聽氏、折氏、野利氏，拓拔氏爲最強。 唐德宗時，党項諸部相率內附，居慶州者號東山部落，居夏州者號平夏部落。 部有大姓而無君長，不相統一，散處邠寧、鄜延、靈武、河西，東至麟、府之間。 自同光以後，大姓之強者各自來朝貢。
The Tangut were descendants of the Western Qiang. Their homeland lay in the Qizhi country described in the Tribute of Yu, reaching east to Songzhou, west to Yehu, south to Chunsang, and north to the Tuyuhun, spanning more than three thousand li. They built no walled towns, only felt-covered dwellings. They were prone to raiding yet remarkably long-lived, often reaching one hundred fifty or sixty years of age. Major lineages included the Xifeng, Feiting, Zhe, and Yeli, with the Tuoba the most powerful. Under Dezong the Tangut tribes submitted in succession. Those around Qingzhou were called the Eastern Mountain tribes; those around Xiazhou, the Pingxia tribes. They recognized great clan names but no paramount ruler, remained divided, and were scattered from Binning and Fuyan through Lingwu and Hexi as far east as Lin and Fu prefectures. After the Tongguang reign each powerful clan sent its own tribute missions.
12
明宗時，詔沿邊置場市馬，諸夷皆入市中國，而回鶻、党項馬最多。 明宗招懷遠人，馬來無駑壯皆售，而所售常過直，往來館給，道路倍費。 其每至京師，明宗爲御殿見之，勞以酒食，既醉，連袂歌呼，道其土風以爲樂，去又厚以賜賚，歲耗百萬計。 唐大臣皆患之，數以爲言。 乃詔吏就邊場售馬給直，止其來朝，而党項利其所得，來不可止。 其在靈、慶之間者，數犯邊爲盜。 自河西回鶻朝貢中國，道其部落，輒邀劫之，執其使者，賣之他族，以易牛馬。 明宗遣靈武康福、邠州藥彥稠等出兵討之。 福等擊破阿埋韋悉褒勒強賴埋廝骨尾及其大首領連香李八薩王、都統悉那埋摩、侍御乞埋嵬悉逋等族，殺數千人，獲其牛羊巨萬計，及其所劫外國寶玉等，悉以賜軍士。 由是党項之患稍息。
Mingzong ordered horse markets set up along the frontier. Tribal traders flocked to China, with Uyghur and Tangut horses the most common. Mingzong courted distant peoples by buying every horse offered, sound or sorry, often above market price, while lodging and travel costs along the route doubled the burden. Whenever they reached the capital, Mingzong received them in the palace, feasted them with food and wine, and watched them, arm in arm and roaring songs, perform their homeland customs for sport. Lavish parting gifts followed, draining more than a million strings of cash a year. Court ministers deplored the expense and remonstrated again and again. An edict then directed officials to buy horses at fair prices on the frontier and barred further court visits. The Tangut, however, prized their profits and kept coming anyway. Bands between Ling and Qing prefectures raided the border again and again. Whenever Hexi Uyghur tribute missions passed through Tangut lands, the Tangut ambushed them, seized the envoys, and sold them to other tribes for cattle and horses. Mingzong dispatched Kang Fu of Lingwu, Yao Yanchou of Binzhou, and others to punish them. Fu's forces crushed the Amai, Weixi, Baole, Qianglaimai, and Siguwei clans along with chiefs such as Lianxiang, Li Basawang, Commander-in-Chief Xinamaimo, and Attendant Qimai Weixibo, killing thousands. Vast herds and the foreign gems they had looted were distributed among the troops. Tangut raids thereafter subsided for a time.
13
至周太祖時，府州党項尼也六泥香王子、拓拔山等皆來朝貢。 廣順三年，慶州刺史郭彥欽貪其羊馬，侵擾諸部，獨野雞族強不可近，乃誣其族犯邊。 太祖遣使招慰之。 野雞族苦彥欽，不肯聽命，太祖遣邠州折從阮、甯州刺史張建武等討之。 建武勇於立功，不能通夷情，馳軍擊野雞族，殺數百人。 而喜玉、折思、殺牛三族聞建武擊破野雞族，各以牛酒犒軍，軍士利其物，反劫掠之。 三族共誘建武軍至包山，度險，三族共擊之，軍投崖谷，死傷甚衆。 太祖怒，罪建武等，選良吏爲慶州刺史以招撫之。
Under the Zhou founder, Fuzhou Tangut leaders such as Niyeliuni Xiangwangzi and Tuobashan came to court with tribute. In the third year of Guangshun the prefect of Qingzhou, Guo Yanqin, coveting their herds, bullied the tribes. Only the Yaji clan remained too formidable to bully, so he falsely accused them of border raids. Taizu sent envoys to reassure them. Harried by Yanqin, the Yaji refused to submit. Taizu dispatched Zhe Congruan of Binzhou and Zhang Jianwu of Ningzhou to attack them. Eager for glory but ignorant of tribal ways, Jianwu charged the Yaji and killed several hundred. The Xiyu, Zhesi, and Shaniu clans, hearing of the victory, brought cattle and wine to feast the troops. The soldiers coveted the gifts and looted the donors instead. The three clans lured Jianwu's force to Baoshan, ambushed them in the defiles, and drove many soldiers over the cliffs to their deaths. Taizu punished Jianwu and his officers and appointed able men as prefect of Qingzhou to pacify the tribes.
14
其他諸族，散處尚邊界上者甚衆，然其無國地、君長，故莫得而紀次云。
Countless other tribes still dotted the frontier, but without fixed territories or rulers they cannot be set down in any orderly account.
15
突厥，國地、君世、部族、名號、物俗，見於唐著矣。 至唐之末。 爲諸夷所侵，部族微散。 五代之際，嘗來朝貢。 同光三年，渾解樓來。 天成二年，首領張慕晉來。 長興二年，首領杜阿熟來。 天福六年，遣使者薛同海等來。 凡四至，其後不復來。 然突厥于時最微，又來不數，故其君長史皆失不能紀。
The Turks' lands, rulers, tribes, titles, and customs were already set forth in the Tang histories. By the end of the Tang they had been harried by neighboring peoples until their clans were broken and scattered. During the Five Dynasties period they still sent occasional tribute missions. In the third year of Tongguang, Hun Jielou arrived. In the second year of Tiancheng the chief Zhang Mujin came. In the second year of Changxing the chief Du Ashu came. In the sixth year of Tianfu they dispatched envoys led by Xue Tonghai. They came four times in all, then ceased entirely. By then the Turks were the weakest of peoples and visited but seldom, so the names and deeds of their chiefs have been lost.
16
吐蕃，國地、君世、部族、名號、物俗，見於唐著矣。 當唐之盛時，河西、隴右三十三州，涼州最大，土沃物繁而人富樂。 其地宜馬，唐置八監，牧馬三十萬匹。 以安西都護府羈縻西域三十六國。 唐之軍、鎮、監、務，三百餘城，常以中國兵更戍，而涼州置使節度之。 安祿山之亂，肅宗起靈武，悉召河西兵赴難，而吐蕃乘虛攻陷河西、隴右，華人百萬皆陷於虜。 文宗時，嘗遣使者至西域，見甘、涼、瓜、沙等州城邑如故，而陷虜之人見唐使者，夾道迎呼，涕泣曰：「皇帝猶念陷蕃人民否？ 」其人皆天寶時陷虜者子孫，其語言稍變，而衣服猶不改。
Tibet's lands, rulers, tribes, titles, and customs were already set forth in the Tang histories. At the height of Tang power, Hexi and Longyou comprised thirty-three prefectures. Liangzhou was the greatest: fertile soil, rich produce, and a prosperous, contented population. The region was ideal for horses; the Tang maintained eight stud farms pasturing three hundred thousand mounts. Through the Protectorate General of Anxi the court held the thirty-six states of the Western Regions in loose allegiance. More than three hundred Tang garrisons, stud farms, and supply posts dotted the region, manned by rotating Chinese troops under a commissioner stationed at Liangzhou. When An Lushan rebelled, Suzong rallied at Lingwu and called every Hexi soldier east to the rescue. Tibet struck the undefended west, overran Hexi and Longyou, and a million Chinese subjects fell into Tibetan hands. During Wenzong's reign envoys reached the western lands and found Gan, Liang, Guazhou, Shazhou, and the other prefectures still standing as before. Subjects trapped in Tibetan hands lined the roads to greet the Tang emissaries, weeping as they cried, "Does the Son of Heaven still remember those of us lost to Tibet?" They were all descendants of subjects taken captive in the Tianbao reign. Their speech had shifted, but they still dressed as Chinese.
17
至五代時，吐蕃已微弱，回鶻、党項諸羌夷分侵其地，而不有其人民。 值中國衰亂，不能撫有，惟甘、涼、瓜、沙四州常自通於中國。 甘州爲回鶻牙，而涼、瓜、沙三州將吏，猶稱唐官，數來請命。 自梁太祖時，嘗以靈武節度使兼領河西節度，而觀察甘、肅、威等州。 然雖有其名，而涼州自立守將。 唐長興四年，涼州留後孫超遣大將拓拔承謙及僧、道士、耆老楊通信等至京師求旌節，明宗問孫超等世家，承謙曰：「吐蕃陷涼州，張掖人張義朝募兵擊走吐蕃，唐因以義朝爲節度使，發鄆州兵二千五百人戍之。 唐亡，天下亂，涼州以東爲突厥、党項所隔，鄆兵遂留不得返。 今涼州漢人，皆其戍人子孫也。 」明宗乃拜孫超節度使。 清泰元年，留後李文謙來請命。 後數年，涼州人逐出文謙，靈武馮暉遣牙將吳繼勳代文謙爲留後，是時天福七年。 明年，晉高祖遣涇州押牙陳延暉齎詔書安撫涼州，涼州人共劫留延暉，立以爲刺史。 至漢隱帝時，涼州留後折逋嘉施來請命，漢即以爲節度使。 嘉施，土豪也。 周廣順二年，嘉施遣人市馬京師，因來請命帥。 是時，樞密使王峻用事。 峻故人申師厚者，少起盜賊，爲兗州牙將，與峻相友善，後峻貴，師厚敝衣蓬首，日候峻出，拜馬前，訴以饑寒，峻未有以發。 而嘉施等來請帥，峻即建言：「涼州深入夷狄，中國未嘗命吏，請募率府率、供奉官能往者。 」月餘，無應募者，乃奏起師厚爲左衛將軍，已而拜河西節度使。 師厚至涼州，奏薦押衙副使崔虎心、陽妃谷首領沈念般等及中國留人子孫王廷翰、溫崇樂、劉少英爲將吏。 又自安國鎮至涼州，立三州以控扼諸羌，用其酋豪爲刺史。 然涼州夷夏雜處，師厚小人，不能撫有。 至世宗時，師厚留其子而逃歸，涼州遂絕於中國。 獨瓜、沙二州，終五代常來。 沙州，梁開平中有節度使張奉，自號「金山白衣天子」。 至唐莊宗時，回鶻來朝，沙州留後曹義金亦遣使附回鶻以來，莊宗拜義金爲歸義軍節度使、瓜沙等州觀察處置等使。 晉天福五年，義金卒，子元德立。 至七年，沙州曹元忠、瓜州曹元深皆遣使來。 周世宗時，又以元忠爲歸義軍節度使，元恭爲瓜州團練使。 其所貢：硇砂、羚羊角、波斯錦、安西白灊、金星礬、胡桐律、大鵬砂、毦褐、玉團。 皆因其來者以名見，而其卒立、世次，史皆失其紀。
By the Five Dynasties period Tibet had weakened. Uyghurs, Tangut, and other Qiang peoples carved up its territory but could not rule its population. With China in turmoil the court could no longer govern the region, yet Gan, Liang, Guazhou, and Shazhou still kept contact with the capital on their own. Ganzhou served as the Uyghur headquarters, while officers in Liang, Guazhou, and Shazhou still styled themselves Tang officials and repeatedly petitioned for imperial commissions. From the Liang founder's reign onward the Lingwu military governor sometimes also held the Hexi command and oversaw Gan, Su, Wei, and neighboring prefectures. In name the office existed, but Liangzhou kept its own local defenders. In the fourth year of Changxing, acting prefect Sun Chao of Liangzhou sent General Tuoba Chengqian, monks, Daoist priests, and elder Yang Tongxin to the capital to request an imperial commission. When Mingzong asked about Sun Chao's background, Chengqian replied, "After Tibet seized Liangzhou, Zhang Yichao of Zhangye raised troops and drove them out. The Tang appointed Yichao military commissioner and sent twenty-five hundred men from Yanzhou to garrison the city." When the Tang fell and the realm dissolved into chaos, Turks and Tangut blocked the route east, and the Yanzhou garrison was stranded. The Chinese population of Liangzhou today are all descendants of those garrison troops. Mingzong then appointed Sun Chao military commissioner of Liangzhou. In the first year of Qingtai, acting prefect Li Wenqian petitioned for a commission. A few years later the people of Liangzhou expelled Wenqian. Feng Hui of Lingwu sent his officer Wu Jixun to replace him as acting prefect in the seventh year of Tianfu. The following year Jin Gaozu sent Jingzhou adjutant Chen Yanhui with an edict to pacify Liangzhou. The locals seized him and made him their prefect. Under Later Han's Emperor Yindi, acting prefect Zhebo Jiashi of Liangzhou petitioned for a commission and was appointed military commissioner. Jiashi was a local magnate. In the second year of Guangshun, Jiashi sent agents to sell horses in the capital and used the occasion to request formal command of the region. At the time Commissioner Wang Jun of the Bureau of Military Affairs dominated the court. Jun's old friend Shen Shihou had begun life as a bandit and became an officer in Yanzhou. When Jun rose to power, Shihou would wait for him each day in ragged clothes and matted hair, bow before his horse, and plead for relief from hunger and cold, yet Jun had nothing to give him. When Jiashi's petition arrived, Jun proposed, "Liangzhou lies deep in barbarian country and China has never stationed officials there. Let us recruit a guard commander or palace attendant willing to take the post." After a month without volunteers, he had Shihou recalled as general of the Left Guard and soon appointed him military commissioner of Hexi. On reaching Liangzhou, Shihou recommended adjutant Cui Huxin, Yangfeigu chief Shen Nianban, and descendants of stranded Chinese such as Wang Tinghan, Wen Chongle, and Liu Shaoying for military posts. Between Anguo and Liangzhou he established three prefectures to restrain the Qiang tribes, appointing tribal chiefs as prefects. But Liangzhou was a mixed frontier of Chinese and tribesmen, and the petty Shihou could not hold it together. Under Emperor Shizong, Shihou abandoned his son and fled home. Liangzhou was lost to China thereafter. Only Guazhou and Shazhou maintained regular contact throughout the Five Dynasties. At Shazhou during Liang's Kaiping era, military commissioner Zhang Feng styled himself the White-Robed Son of Heaven of Golden Mountain. When the Uyghurs visited Zhuangzong's court, acting prefect Cao Yijin of Shazhou sent envoys in their train. Zhuangzong appointed Yijin commissioner of the Army of Returning Allegiance and overseer of Guazhou and Shazhou. Yijin died in the fifth year of Tianfu and was succeeded by his son Yuande. In the seventh year Cao Yuanzhong of Shazhou and Cao Yuanshen of Guazhou both sent missions. Under Zhou's Emperor Shizong, Yuanzhong was again made commissioner of the Army of Returning Allegiance and Yuangong regimental commissioner of Guazhou. Their tribute included sal ammoniac, antelope horn, Persian brocade, Anxi white felt, golden-star alum, tamarisk resin, great-peng sand, felt coats, and jade spheres. We know them only through the names of visiting envoys; the records of their deaths, successions, and lineages have been lost.

而吐蕃不見於梁世。 唐天成三年，回鶻王仁喻來朝，吐蕃亦遣使附以來，自此數至中國。 明宗嘗禦端明殿見其使者，問其牙帳所居，曰：「西去涇州二千里。 」明宗賜以虎皮，人一張，皆披以拜，委身宛轉，落其氈帽，亂髮如蓬，明宗及左右皆大笑。 至漢隱帝時猶來朝，後遂不復至，史亦失其君世云。
Tibet does not appear in the records of the Liang. In the third year of Tiancheng the Uyghur king Renyu came to court, and Tibet sent envoys in his train. Thereafter Tibetan missions reached China repeatedly. Mingzong once received their envoys in the Hall of Illustrious Clarity and asked where their headquarters stood. They replied, "Two thousand li west of Jingzhou." Mingzong gave each man a tiger skin. They draped the pelts to bow, writhing so violently that their felt caps fell off and their tangled hair flew like rushes, to the great amusement of the emperor and his attendants. They still came under Later Han's Emperor Yindi, then ceased entirely, and the line of their rulers has been lost.

回鶻，爲唐患尤甚。 其國地、君世、物俗，見於唐著矣。 唐嘗以女妻之，故其世以中國爲舅。 其國本在娑陵水上、後爲黠戛斯所侵，徙天德、振武之間，又爲石雄、張仲武所破，其餘衆西徙，役屬吐蕃。 是時吐蕃已陷河西、隴右，乃以回鶻散處之。
Of all the peoples who troubled the Tang, the Uyghurs were the greatest menace. Their lands, rulers, and customs were already set forth in the Tang histories. The Tang had given them imperial brides, so they called China their uncle-by-marriage. They had originally lived along the Seling River. After the Kirghiz drove them out, they shifted between Tiande and Zhenwu. Defeated by Shi Xiong and Zhang Zhongwu, the survivors moved west and submitted to Tibet. Tibet had already seized Hexi and Longyou and settled the Uyghur remnants across that region.

當五代之際，有居甘州、西州者嘗見中國，而甘州回鶻數至，猶呼中國爲舅，中國答以詔書亦呼爲甥。 梁乾化元年，遣都督周易言等來，而史不見其君長名號，梁拜易言等官爵，遣左監門衛上將軍楊沼押領還蕃。 至唐莊宗時，王仁美遣使者來，貢玉、馬，自稱「權知可汗」，莊宗遣司農卿鄭續持節冊仁美爲英義可汗。 是歲，仁美卒，其弟狄銀立，遣都督安千想等來。 同光四年，狄銀卒，阿咄欲立。 天成二年，權知國事王仁裕遣李阿山等來朝，明宗遣使者冊仁裕爲順化可汗。 晉高祖時又冊爲奉化可汗。 阿咄欲，不知其爲狄銀親疏，亦不知其立卒； 而仁裕訖五代常來朝貢，史亦失其紀。 其地出玉、犛、綠野馬、獨峰駝、白貂鼠、羚羊角、硇砂、膃肭臍、金剛鑽、紅鹽、罽氎、騊駼之革。 其地宜白麥、青騑麥、黃麻、蔥韭、胡荽，以橐駝耕而種。 其可汗常樓居，妻號天公主，其國相號媚祿都督。 見可汗，則去帽被髮而入以爲禮。 婦人總發爲髻，高五六寸，以紅絹囊之； 既嫁，則加氈帽。 又有別族號龍家，其俗與回紇小異。 長興四年，回鶻來獻白鶻一聯，明宗命解緤放之。 自明宗時，常以馬市中國，其所齎寶玉皆屬縣官，而民犯禁爲市者輒罪之。 周太祖時除其禁，民得與回鶻私市，玉價由此倍賤。 顯德中，來獻玉，世宗曰：「玉雖寶而無益。 」卻之。
During the Five Dynasties the Uyghurs of Ganzhou and Xizhou still had contact with China. The Ganzhou Uyghurs came often, still calling China their uncle, while Chinese edicts addressed them as nephews. In the first year of Qianhua they sent Commander Zhou Yiyan and others, though the histories do not record their ruler's name. Liang granted Yiyan titles and sent Yang Zhao of the Left Gate Guard to escort the mission home. Under Zhuangzong, Wang Renmei sent envoys with jade and horses, styling himself acting khan. Zhuangzong dispatched Minister of Agriculture Zheng Xu to invest him as the Yingyi Kaghan. Renmei died that year and was succeeded by his brother Diyin, who sent Commander An Qianxiang and others. Diyin died in the fourth year of Tongguang and was succeeded by Aduyu. In the second year of Tiancheng, Wang Renyu, acting ruler, sent Li Ashan and others to court. Mingzong invested him as the Shunhua Kaghan. Jin Gaozu later invested him as the Fenghua Kaghan. Nothing is known of Aduyu's kinship to Diyin or the dates of his rise and death; yet Renyu sent tribute throughout the Five Dynasties, and his line too has been lost from the record. Their country yielded jade, yak, green wild horses, Bactrian camels, white sable, antelope horn, sal ammoniac, narwhal tusk, diamond drills, red salt, felt cloth, and taotu leather. They grew white wheat, bluish wheat, yellow hemp, scallions, leeks, and coriander, sowing the fields with camel-drawn plows. The khan lived in a tower dwelling; his consort was called the Heavenly Princess, and his chief minister bore the title Meilu Commander. To approach the khan one removed one's cap, unbound one's hair, and entered bareheaded—that was the proper rite. Women bound their hair into a topknot five or six inches high and wrapped it in red silk; after marriage they added a felt cap. A separate people called the Dragon Clan lived among them, with customs slightly different from the Uyghurs. In the fourth year of Changxing the Uyghurs presented a pair of white swans, which Mingzong ordered released. From Mingzong's reign they regularly sold horses in China. The jade they brought was reserved for the state, and private trade was punished. Zhou's Taizu lifted the ban, allowing private trade with the Uyghurs, and the price of jade fell sharply. During the Xiande era they presented jade. Shizong said, "Jade may be precious, but it serves no practical purpose." He refused the gift.

于闐，國地、君世、物俗見於唐。 五代亂世，中國多故，不能撫來四夷。 其嘗自通於中國者僅以名見，其君世、終始，皆不可知。 而于闐尤遠，去京師萬里外。 其國西南近蔥嶺，與婆羅門爲鄰國，而相去猶三千餘里，南接吐蕃，西北至疏勒二千餘里。
Khotan's lands, rulers, and customs were recorded in the Tang histories. During the chaotic Five Dynasties China was consumed by internal troubles and could not win over the frontier peoples. Those who maintained contact appear only as names; the lineages and histories of their rulers are unknown. Khotan lay farthest of all, more than ten thousand li from the capital. Southwest it approached the Onion Mountains and lay three thousand li from Brahman lands. It bordered Tibet to the south and stood more than two thousand li northwest of Shule.

晉天福三年，于闐國王李聖天遣使者馬繼榮來貢紅鹽、郁金、氂牛尾、玉灊等，晉遣供奉官張匡鄴假鴻臚卿，彰武軍節度判官高居誨爲判官，冊聖天爲大寶于闐國王。 是歲冬十二月，匡鄴等自靈州行二歲至于闐，至七年冬乃還。 而居誨頗記其往復所見山川諸國，而不能道聖天世次也。
In the third year of Tianfu, King Li Shengtian of Khotan sent Ma Jirong with tribute of red salt, turmeric, yak tails, and jade felt. Jin dispatched palace attendant Zhang Kuangye as acting director of diplomatic reception, with Zhangwu judge Gao Juhui as vice-commissioner, to invest Shengtian as king of Great Treasure Khotan. They departed Lingzhou in the twelfth month of that winter, reached Khotan after two years on the road, and returned in the winter of the seventh year. Gao Juhui recorded the mountains, rivers, and kingdoms seen along the route, but could not trace Shengtian's lineage.

居誨記曰：「自靈州過黃河，行三十里，始涉沙入党項界，曰細腰沙、神點沙。 至三公沙，宿月支都督帳。 自此沙行四百餘里，至黑堡沙，沙尤廣，遂登沙嶺。 沙嶺，党項牙也，其酋曰撚崖天子。 渡白亭河至涼州，自涼州西行五百里至甘州。 甘州，回鶻牙也。 其南，山百餘里，漢小月支之故地也，有別族號鹿角山沙陀，雲硃耶氏之遺族也。 自甘州西，始涉磧。 磧無水，載水以行。 甘州人教晉使者作馬蹄木澀，木澀四竅，馬蹄亦鑿四竅而綴之，駝蹄則包以氂皮乃可行。 西北五百里至肅州，渡金河，西百里出天門關，又西百里出玉門關，經吐蕃界。 吐蕃男子冠中國帽，婦人辮髪，戴瑟瑟珠，雲珠之好者，一珠易一良馬。 西至瓜州、沙州，二州多中國人，聞晉使者來，其刺史曹元深等郊迎，問使者天子起居。 瓜州南十里鳴沙山，雲冬夏殷殷有聲如雷，雲《禹貢》流沙也。 又東南十里三危山，雲三苗之所竄也。 其西，渡都鄉河曰陽關。 沙州西曰仲雲，其牙帳居胡盧磧。 雲仲雲者，小月支之遺種也，其人勇而好戰，瓜、沙之人皆憚之。 胡盧磧，漢明帝時征匈奴，屯田于吾盧，蓋其地也。 地無水而嘗寒多雪，每天暖雪銷，乃得水。 匡鄴等西行入仲雲界，至大屯城，仲雲遣宰相四人、都督三十七人候晉使者，匡鄴等以詔書慰諭之，皆東向拜。 自仲雲界西，始涉𨢑磧，無水，掘地得濕沙，人置之胸以止渴。 又西，渡陷河，伐檉置水中乃渡，不然則陷。 又西，至紺州。 紺州，于闐所置也，在沙州西南，雲去京師九千五百里矣。 又行二日至安軍州，遂至于闐。 聖天衣冠如中國，其殿皆東向，曰金冊殿，有樓曰七鳳樓。 以蒲桃爲酒，又有紫酒、青酒，不知其所釀，而味尤美。 其食，粳沃以蜜，粟沃以酪。 其衣布帛。 有園圃花木。 俗喜鬼神而好佛。 聖天居處，嘗以紫衣僧五十人列侍，其年號同慶二十九年。 其國東南曰銀州、盧州、湄州，其南千三百里曰玉州，雲漢張騫所窮河源出于闐，而山多玉者此山也。 」其河源所出，至于闐分爲三：東曰白玉河，西曰綠玉河，又西曰烏玉河。 三河皆有玉而色異，每歲秋水涸，國王撈玉于河，然後國人得撈玉。
Gao Juhui wrote: "From Lingzhou we crossed the Yellow River and, after thirty li, entered the sands of Tangut territory at Narrow-Waist Sand and Spirit-Point Sand." At Three-Lord Sand they lodged in the tent of the Yuezhi commander. They crossed four hundred li of sand to Black Fortress Sand, where the desert widened still further, then climbed Sand Ridge. Sand Ridge was the Tangut headquarters, ruled by a chief styled the Nianya Son of Heaven. They crossed the Baiting River to Liangzhou, then traveled five hundred li west to Ganzhou. Ganzhou was the Uyghur headquarters. A hundred li to the south lay the old homeland of the Lesser Yuezhi. A separate people called the Antler Mountain Shatuo lived there, said to be descendants of the Zhuye clan. West of Ganzhou the gravel desert began. The desert held no water, so they carried supplies to survive. Locals taught the Jin envoys to fit wooden hoof grips with four holes, bored to match four holes drilled in each horse's hooves. Camel hooves had to be wrapped in yak hide before they could cross the desert. Five hundred li northwest lay Suzhou. They crossed the Golden River, passed Heaven's Gate a hundred li west, then Jade Gate another hundred li west, traveling through Tibetan lands. Tibetan men wore Chinese-style caps. Women braided their hair and wore sese beads; the finest beads were said to trade for a good horse apiece. Farther west lay Guazhou and Shazhou, where many Chinese still lived. Prefect Cao Yuanshen and others rode out to greet the Jin envoys and inquire after the emperor's health. Ten li south of Guazhou stood Echoing Sand Mountain, which rumbled like thunder year-round and was identified with the drifting sands of the Tribute of Yu. Ten li farther southeast lay Three Perils Mountain, said to be where the Three Miao had fled. West of there, crossing the Duxiang River, lay Yang Pass. West of Shazhou lay Zhongyun, whose headquarters stood in the Hulu Desert. The Zhongyun were said to be remnants of the Lesser Yuezhi, fierce warriors whom the people of Guazhou and Shazhou feared. The Hulu Desert was likely the site where Emperor Ming of Han had garrisoned troops at Wulu during his campaigns against the Xiongnu. The land had no standing water and was bitterly cold and snowy. Water appeared only when the sun melted the snow. Kuangye's party entered Zhongyun territory as far as Great Garrison City. Four Zhongyun chancellors and thirty-seven commanders came to meet them. Kuangye read the imperial edict of consolation, and all bowed toward the east. West of Zhongyun lay the salt desert without water. Travelers dug up damp sand and pressed it to their chests to slake their thirst. Farther west they crossed the Sinking River by laying cut tamarisk branches in the water; without that precaution travelers sank in. Still farther west they reached Indigo Prefecture. Indigo Prefecture, founded by Khotan southwest of Shazhou, was said to lie nine thousand five hundred li from the capital. Two days later they reached Anjun Prefecture, and then Khotan itself. Shengtian dressed in Chinese style. His halls faced east, including the Golden Book Hall and the Seven Phoenix Tower. They made wine from grapes, along with purple and green wines of unknown ingredients whose flavor was especially fine. They ate glutinous rice softened with honey and millet softened with curds. They dressed in cloth and silk. They kept gardens and flowering trees. They venerated spirits and were devoted to Buddhism. Fifty purple-robed monks stood in attendance at Shengtian's court. His reign era was Tongqing, in its twenty-ninth year. Southeast lay Yinzhou, Luzhou, and Meizhou. Thirteen hundred li south stood Yuzhou, on the mountain said to be the jade-rich source of the river whose upper reaches Zhang Qian of Han had traced to its end in Khotan. At its source the river split into three branches in Khotan: the White Jade River to the east, the Green Jade River to the west, and the Black Jade River farther west. Each river yielded jade of a different color. Each autumn, when the waters fell, the king gathered jade first; only afterward could the people do the same.

自靈州渡黃河至于闐，往往見吐蕃族帳，而于闐常與吐蕃相攻劫。 匡鄴等至于闐，聖天頗責誚之，以邀誓約。 匡鄴等還，聖天又遣都督劉再升獻玉千斤及玉印、降魔杵等。 漢乾祐元年，又遣使者王知鐸來。
From Lingzhou to Khotan along the Yellow River route, Tibetan encampments were common, and Khotan and Tibet raided each other repeatedly. When Kuangye's party arrived, Shengtian rebuked them harshly to extract a sworn alliance. On their return Shengtian sent Commander Liu Zaisheng with a thousand jin of jade, a jade seal, a demon-subduing staff, and other gifts. In the first year of Later Han's Qianyou reign he sent another envoy, Wang Zhiduo.
25
高麗，本扶餘人之別種也。 其國地、君世見於唐，比他夷狄有姓氏，而其官號略可曉其義。 當唐之末，其王姓高氏。 同光元年，遣使廣評侍郎韓申一、副使春部少卿樸岩來，而其國王姓名，史失不紀。 至長興三年，權知國事王建遣使者來，明宗乃拜建玄菟州都督，充大義軍使，封高麗國王。 建，高麗大族也。 開運二年，建卒，子武立。 乾祐四年，武卒，子昭立。 王氏三世，終五代常來朝貢，其立也必請命中國，中國常優答之。 其地產銅、銀，周世宗時，遣尚書水部員外郎韓彥卿以帛數千匹市銅於高麗以鑄鐵。 六年，昭遣使者貢黃銅五萬斤。 高麗俗知文字，喜讀書，昭進《別敘孝經》一卷、《越王新義》八卷、《皇靈孝經》一卷、《孝經雌圖》一卷。 《別敘》，敘孔子所生及弟子事蹟； 《越王新義》，以「越王」爲問目，若今「正義」； 《皇靈》，述延年辟穀； 《雌圖》，載日食、星變。 皆不經之說。
Goryeo was originally an offshoot of the Fuyu people. Their lands and rulers appear in the Tang histories. Unlike other frontier peoples they used surnames, and their official titles are largely intelligible. By the late Tang their royal house bore the surname Gao. In the first year of Tongguang they sent envoy Han Shenyi and vice-envoy Piao Yan, but the king's name was not recorded. In the third year of Changxing, Wang Jian, acting ruler, sent envoys. Mingzong appointed him commander of Xuantu, commissioner of the Dayi Army, and king of Goryeo. Jian came from a leading Goryeo clan. Jian died in the second year of Kaiyun and was succeeded by his son Wu. Wu died in the fourth year of Qianyou and was succeeded by his son Zhao. Three generations of the Wang house sent tribute throughout the Five Dynasties, always seeking Chinese investiture on succession, which the court generously granted. Their country produced copper and silver. Shizong sent Han Yanqing of the Ministry of Works with thousands of bolts of silk to buy Goryeo copper for iron casting. In the sixth year Zhao sent fifty thousand jin of brass as tribute. The Goryeo were literate and fond of learning. Zhao presented Separate Narrative to the Classic of Filial Piety, New Meaning of the King of Yue in eight scrolls, Imperial Spirit Classic of Filial Piety, and Yin Chart of the Classic of Filial Piety. Separate Narrative recounted Confucius's birthplace and the deeds of his disciples; New Meaning of the King of Yue used "King of Yue" as its interpretive frame, like modern commentaries; Imperial Spirit described Yan Nian's fasting regimen; Yin Chart recorded solar eclipses and unusual stars. All were unorthodox works.
26
渤海，本號靺鞨，高麗之別種也。 唐高宗滅高麗，徙其人散處中國，置安東都護府於平壤以統治之。 武后時，契丹攻北邊，高麗別種大乞乞仲象與靺鞨酋長乞四比羽走遼東，分王高麗故地，武后遣將擊殺乞四比羽，而乞乞仲象亦病死。 仲象子祚榮立，因並有比羽之衆，其衆四十萬人，據挹婁，臣于唐。 至中宗時，置忽汗州，以祚榮爲都督，封渤海郡王，其後世遂號渤海。 其貴族姓大氏，開平元年，國王大諲撰遣使者來，訖顯德常來朝貢。 其國土物產，與高麗同。 諲撰世次、立卒，史失其紀。
Bohai, originally called Mohe, was an offshoot of Goryeo. Gaozong of Tang destroyed Goryeo, resettled its people across China, and placed the Protectorate General of Andong at Pyongyang to rule the region. Under Empress Wu the Khitan raided the north. The Goryeo splinter leader Daqiqizhongxiang and Mohe chief Qisibiyu fled to Liaodong and divided the old Goryeo lands between them. Wu's generals killed Qisibiyu, and Zhongxiang soon died as well. Zhongxiang's son Zuorong succeeded him, absorbed Biyu's followers, and commanded four hundred thousand people at Yilou before submitting to Tang. Under Zhongzong the court established Hohan Prefecture, made Zuorong its commander, and enfeoffed him as Prince of Bohai. Later generations took Bohai as their name. Their nobility bore the surname Da. King Da Yinzuan sent envoys in the first year of Kaiping, and tribute missions continued through the Xiande era. Their products were much the same as Goryeo's. Yinzuan's lineage and reign dates have been lost.
27
黑水靺鞨
Heishui Mohe.
28
黑水靺鞨，本號勿吉。 當後魏時見中國。 其國，東至海，南界高麗，西接突厥，北鄰室韋，蓋肅慎氏之地也。 其衆分爲數十部，而黑水靺鞨最處其北，尤勁悍，無文字之記。 其兵，角弓、楛矢。 同光二年，黑水兀兒遣使者來，其後常來朝貢，自登州泛海出青州。 明年，黑水胡獨鹿亦遣使來。 兀兒、胡獨鹿若其兩部酋長，各以使來。 而其部族、世次、立卒，史皆失其紀。 至長興三年，胡獨鹿卒，子桃李花立，嘗請命中國，後遂不復見云。
The Heishui Mohe were originally known as the Wuji. They appear in Chinese records from the Northern Wei period. Their country reached the sea in the east, bordered Goryeo in the south, met the Turks in the west, and neighbored the Shiwei in the north—the ancient land of the Sushen. They were divided into dozens of tribes. The Heishui Mohe lived farthest north, were especially fierce, and left no written records. They fought with horn bows and wooden-shafted arrows. In the second year of Tongguang the Heishui chief Wuer sent envoys. Thereafter tribute missions sailed from Dengzhou across the sea to Qingzhou. The following year the Heishui chief Hudulu sent envoys as well. Wuer and Hudulu were apparently chiefs of two divisions, each sending his own mission. Their tribal affiliations, genealogies, and reign dates have all been lost. Hudulu died in the third year of Changxing and was succeeded by his son Taolihua, who once sought Chinese investiture but thereafter disappears from the record.
29
南詔蠻
Nanzhao.
30
南詔蠻，見於唐。 其國在漢故永昌郡之東、姚州之西。 僖宗幸蜀，募能使南詔者，得宗室子李龜年及徐虎、虎侄藹，乃以龜年爲使，虎爲副，藹爲判官，使南詔。 南詔所居曰苴絺城，龜年等不至苴絺，至善闡，得其要約與唐爲甥舅。 僖宗許以安化公主妻之，南詔大喜，遣人隨龜年求公主。 已而黃巢敗，收復長安，僖宗東還，乃止。
Nanzhao appears in the Tang histories. Their kingdom lay east of the old Han commandery of Yongchang and west of Yaozhou. When Xizong fled to Shu he sought men who could treat with Nanzhao and found the clansman Li Guinian, Xu Hu, and Hu's nephew Ai. Guinian was made envoy, Hu vice-envoy, and Ai judge of the mission. Nanzhao's capital was Juzhi City. Guinian's party did not reach it but stopped at Shanchan and secured a pact of uncle-nephew kinship with Tang. Xizong promised the Princess of Anhua in marriage. Nanzhao rejoiced and sent men with Guinian to claim her. When Huang Chao fell, Chang'an was recovered, and Xizong returned east, the marriage plan was abandoned.
31
同光三年，魏王繼岌及郭崇韜等破蜀，得王衍時所俘南詔蠻數十人，又得徐藹，自言嘗使南詔，乃矯詔還其所俘，遣藹等持金帛招撫南詔，諭以威德，南詔不納。 至明宗時，巂州山后兩林百蠻都鬼主、右武衛大將軍李卑晚，遣大鬼主傅能何華來朝貢，明宗拜卑晚甯遠將軍，又以大渡河南山前邛州六姓都鬼主懷安郡王勿定摽莎爲定遠將軍。 明年遣左金吾衛將軍烏昭遠爲入蠻國信使，昭遠不能達而還。
In the third year of Tongguang, Prince Jiji and Guo Chongtao conquered Shu and recovered dozens of Nanzhao captives taken under Wang Yan, along with Xu Ai, who claimed prior service as envoy. They forged an edict returning the captives and sent Ai with gold and silk to win Nanzhao over, but Nanzhao refused. Under Mingzong, Li Beiwan, chief of the Two Forests Hundred Barbarians of Xizhou and general of the Right Martial Guard, sent Great Ghost-Lord Fu Nenghua with tribute. Mingzong made Beiwan General of Pacifying the Distance and Wuding Biaosha, Six-Clans ghost-lord south of the Dadu River, General of Fixing the Distance. The following year Wu Zhaoyuan of the Left Golden Crow Guard was sent as credentialed envoy to the Man lands but could not get through and returned.
32
牂牁蠻，在辰州西千五百里，以耕植爲生，而無城郭聚落，有所攻擊，則相屯聚。 刻木爲契。 其首領姓謝氏，其名見於唐。 至天成二年嘗一至，其使者曰清州八郡刺史宋朝化，冠帶如中國，貢草豆蔻二萬個、硃砂五百兩、蠟二百斤。
The Zhangke Man lived fifteen hundred li west of Chenzhou by agriculture, without walled towns. When threatened they gathered for defense. They recorded agreements by carving wood. Their chiefs bore the surname Xie, recorded in the Tang histories. In the second year of Tiancheng they came once. Their envoy, styled Prefect Song Chaohua of Qingzhou's eight commanderies, dressed in Chinese cap and belt and offered twenty thousand cardamom pods, five hundred liang of cinnabar, and two hundred jin of wax.
33
昆明，在黔州西南三千里外，地產羊馬。 其人椎髻、跣足、披氈，其首領披虎皮。 天成二年，嘗一至，其首領號昆明大鬼主，羅殿王、普露靜王九部落，各遣使者來，使者號若土，附牂牁以來。
Kunming lay three thousand li southwest of Qianzhou and produced sheep and horses. They wore topknots, went barefoot, and wrapped themselves in felt; their chiefs wore tiger pelts. In the second year of Tiancheng they came once. Their chief styled himself Great Ghost-Lord of Kunming. The kings of Luodian and Pulujing and nine tribes each sent envoys called Ruotu, arriving with the Zhangke mission.
34
占城，在西南海上。 其地方千里，東至海，西至雲南，南鄰真腦，北抵驩州。 其人，俗與大食同。 其乘，象、馬； 其食，稻米、水兕、山羊。 鳥獸之奇，犀、孔雀。 自前世未嘗通中國。 顯德五年，其國王因德漫遣使者莆訶散來，貢猛火油八十四瓶、薔薇水十五瓶，其表以貝多葉書之，以香木爲函。 猛火油以灑物，得水則出火。 薔薇水，雲得自西域，以灑衣，雖敝而香不滅。
Champa lay on the southwestern seas. Their territory spanned a thousand li, reaching the sea in the east, Yunnan in the west, Zhennao in the south, and Huanzhou in the north. Their customs resembled those of the Arabs. They rode elephants and horses; their food was rice, river rhinoceros, and mountain goats. Notable among their fauna were rhinoceroses and peacocks. No earlier dynasty had had contact with them. In the fifth year of Xiande King Yindeman sent envoy Puhesan with eighty-four bottles of Greek fire and fifteen of rose water. The memorial was written on pattra leaves in a case of fragrant wood. Greek fire burst into flame when sprinkled on objects and touched by water. Rose water, said to come from the Western Regions, perfumed clothing so that the scent lingered even when the cloth was worn threadbare.
35
五代，四夷見中國者，遠不過于闐、占城。 史之所紀，其西北頗詳，而東南尤略，蓋其遠而罕至，且不爲中國利害云。
Among the frontier peoples who reached China during the Five Dynasties, none came from farther than Khotan and Champa. The histories are fairly detailed on the northwest but especially thin on the southeast, no doubt because those lands were remote, seldom visited, and of little consequence to China.
