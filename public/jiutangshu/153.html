=姚南仲=姚南仲，華州下邽人。 乾元初，制科登第，授太子校書，歷高陵、昭應、萬年三縣尉。 遷右拾遺，轉右補闕。 大歷十三年，貞懿皇后獨孤氏崩，代宗悼惜不已，令於近城為陵墓，冀朝夕臨望於目前。 南仲上疏諫曰：
Yao Nanzhong was from Xiaji in Huazhou. In the early Qianyuan period he passed the institutional examination and was made collator to the crown prince, then served in succession as magistrate of Gaoling, Zhaoying, and Wannian. He was promoted to Right Reminder and later transferred to Right Supplementation-eradicator. In Dali 13 the Zhenyi Empress, Lady Dugu, died. Daizong grieved deeply and ordered a mausoleum built just outside the city so he could visit it at every hour. Nanzhong submitted a memorial of remonstrance, saying:

疏奏，帝甚嘉之，賜緋魚袋，特加五品階，宣付史館。
When the memorial reached the throne, the emperor was greatly pleased, awarded him the crimson fish tally, promoted him five grades in rank, and had the document sent to the Historiography Office.
3
與宰相常袞善，袞貶官，南仲坐出為海鹽縣令。 浙江東、西道觀察使韓滉辟為推官，奏授殿中侍御史、內供奉，充支使。 尋征還，歷左司兵部員外，轉郎中，遷御史中丞、給事中、同州刺史、陜虢觀察使。
He was close to the chief minister Chang Gun; when Gun was demoted, Nanzhong was caught up in the affair and posted out as magistrate of Haiyan. Han Huang, regional commissioner for eastern and western Zhejiang, took him on as investigating clerk and had him appointed attending censor and inner attendant, with responsibility for disbursements. He was soon recalled, served in the left secretariat and the War Ministry, rose to bureau director, and was appointed chief censor, recipient of edicts, prefect of Tongzhou, and commissioner for Shaan and Guo.
4
貞元十五年，代李復為鄭滑節度使。 監軍薛盈珍恃勢奪軍政，南仲數為盈珍讒毀，德宗頗疑之。 十六年，盈珍遣小使程務盈馳驛奉表，誣奏南仲陰事。 南仲裨將曹文洽亦入奏事京師，伺知盈珍表中語。 文洽私懷憤怒，遂晨夜兼道追務盈，至長樂驛及之，與同舍宿； 中夜殺務盈，沈盈珍表於廁中，乃自殺。 日旰，驛吏辟門，見血流塗地，旁得文洽二緘，一告於南仲，一表理南仲之冤，且陳首殺務盈。 上聞其事，頗駭異之。 南仲慮釁深，遂乞入朝。 德宗曰：「盈珍擾軍政耶？ 」南仲對曰：「勇珍不擾軍政，臣自隳陛下法耳。 如盈珍輩所在有之，雖羊、杜復生，撫百姓，禦三軍，必不能成愷悌父母之政，師律善陣之制矣。 」上默然久之。 授尚書右僕射。 貞元十九年七月，終於位，年七十四，贈太子太保，謚曰貞。
In Zhenyuan 15 he replaced Li Fu as military commissioner of Zheng and Hua. The army supervisor Xue Yingzhen abused his position to seize control of the command; Yingzhen slandered Nanzhong repeatedly, and Dezong grew suspicious of him. In year sixteen Yingzhen sent a minor aide, Cheng Wuying, by courier with a memorial falsely charging Nanzhong with covert misconduct. Nanzhong's deputy Cao Wenzha had also gone to the capital on business and learned what Yingzhen's memorial said. Fuming in private, Wenzha rode day and night after Wuying, overtook him at Changle post station, and lodged with him in the same inn. At midnight he killed Wuying, dropped Yingzhen's memorial into the latrine, and then took his own life. Toward evening the station attendant opened the door, found blood everywhere, and discovered two letters from Wenzha—one for Nanzhong and one clearing Nanzhong's name while confessing that he had killed Wuying. When the emperor learned of the incident, he was profoundly astonished. Fearing the scandal would deepen, Nanzhong asked permission to come to court. Dezong asked, "Has Yingzhen been meddling in army affairs?" Nanzhong replied, "Yingzhen does not disturb military matters; I am the one who has broken Your Majesty's laws." Wherever men like Yingzhen are found, even if Yang Xiong and Du Fu were brought back to life to nurture the people and lead the armies, they could not establish benevolent rule or sound military discipline. The emperor fell silent for a long while. He was appointed Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs. In the seventh month of Zhenyuan 19 he died in office at seventy-four; he was posthumously made Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent with the temple name Zhen.
5
=劉迺=劉迺，字永夷，洺州廣平人。 高祖武幹，武德初拜侍中，即中書侍郎林甫從祖兄子也。 父如璠，昫山丞，以迺貴，贈民部郎中。 迺少聰穎誌學，暗記《六經》，日數千言。 及長，文章清雅，為當時推重。 天寶中，舉進士，尋丁父艱，居喪以孝聞。 既終制，從調選曹。 迺常以文部選才未為盡善，遂致書於知銓舍人宋昱曰：
Liu Nai, courtesy name Yongyi, was from Guangping in Mozhou. His great-grandfather Wu Gan was made Attendant-in-ordinary at the start of Wude; he was a cousin once removed of Vice Director of the Secretariat Linpu. His father Ru Fan had been assistant magistrate of Xushan; when Nai rose in rank, Ru Fan was posthumously made bureau director in the Ministry of Revenue. As a youth Nai was bright and eager to study; he knew the Six Classics by heart and could recite several thousand characters a day. When he came of age, his prose was refined and elegant, and his contemporaries held him in high regard. During Tianbao he passed the jinshi examination; when his father died he observed mourning and became known for his filial devotion. After mourning ended, he reported to the Selection Bureau for appointment. Nai often felt that the Ministry of Rites did not select talent as well as it might, and wrote to Selection Commissioner Song Yu, saying:
6
《虞書》稱：「知人則哲，能官人則惠。 」巍巍唐、虞，舉以為難。 今夫文部，既始之以掄材，終之以授位，是則知人官人，斯為重任。 昔在禹、稷、臯陶之眾聖，猶曰載采有九德，考績以九載。 近代主司，獨委一二小冢宰，察言於一幅之判，觀行於一揖之內，古今遲速，何不侔之甚哉！ 夫判者，以狹詞短韻，語有定規為體，亦猶以一小冶而鼓眾金，雖欲為鼎為鏞，不可得也。 故曰：判之在文，至局促者。 夫銓者，必以崇衣冠，自媒耀為賢，斯又士之醜行，君子所病。 若引文公、尼父登之於銓廷，則雖圖書《易象》之大訓，以判體挫之，曾不及徐、庾。 雖有至德，以喋喋取之，曾不若嗇夫。 嗚呼！ 彼幹霄蔽日，誠巨樹也，當求尺寸之材，必後於椓杙。 龍吟武嘯，誠希聲也，若尚頰舌之感，必下於蛙黽。 觀察之際，猶不悲夫！ 執事慮過龜策，文合雅誥，豈拘以瑣瑣故事，曲折因循哉？ 誠能先資以政事，次征以文學，退觀其理家，進察其臨節，則厖鴻深沈之事，亦可以窺其門戶矣！
The Documents of Yu says, "To know men is wisdom; to place men in office is benevolence." Even in the lofty age of Tang and Yu, all regarded this as difficult. Today the Ministry of Rites begins by selecting talent and ends by conferring office—so knowing men and placing them in office is a weighty charge. Even among the sages Yu, Ji, and Gao Yao, it was said that one must gather and test the nine virtues and examine achievement over nine years. In recent times chief examiners leave everything to one or two junior stewards, judging talent from a single examination essay and character from a single bow—how vast the gap between antiquity and the present! Judgment essays use cramped phrasing and short rhymes within fixed forms—like trying to cast tripods and bells in a small furnace: the grand result is impossible. Hence the saying that judgment essays are the most cramped form of writing. At selection time candidates parade fine dress and self-promotion as proof of merit—an ugly habit among scholars that gentlemen deplore. If Duke Wen and Confucius appeared at the selection hall, their mastery of the classics and the Changes would be cut down by the judgment format and they would rank below Xu Ling and Yu Xin. Though possessed of supreme virtue, if chosen for glib chatter they would rank below a petty miser. Alas! Trees that pierce the clouds and shade the sun are truly great timber, yet when boards are measured by the foot they rank behind stakes and pegs. The dragon's cry and the tiger's roar are truly rare voices; if glib talk is prized, one must rank below frogs and toads. When one considers this, is it not lamentable! Your deliberations surpass divination, your prose matches the classical decrees—surely you need not be bound by petty precedent and roundabout compliance? If you first weigh administrative ability, then literary accomplishment, observe how candidates manage their households in private life and how they meet crises in public office, even the weightiest matters may be judged through their doorway.
7
其載，補剡縣尉，改會稽尉。 宣州觀察使殷日用奏為判官，宣慰使李季卿又以表薦，連授大理評事、兼監察御史。 轉運使劉晏奏令巡覆江西，多所蠲免。 改殿中侍御史、檢校倉部員外、民部郎中，並充浙西留後。 佐晏征賦，頗有裨益，晏甚任之。
He was then appointed defender of Shan County and later transferred to defender of Kuaiji. Yin Riyong, commissioner of Xuanzhou, had him appointed as aide; pacification commissioner Li Jiqing also recommended him, and he was successively made evaluator in the Court of Judicial Review and concurrent investigating censor. Transport commissioner Liu Yan had him tour and review Jiangxi, and many taxes were remitted. He was made attending censor within the palace, acting assistant in the Granaries Bureau, and bureau director in the Ministry of Revenue, and concurrently served as acting commissioner of western Zhejiang. In assisting Yan with tax collection he proved highly useful, and Yan relied on him greatly.
8
大歷十二年，元載既誅，以迺久在職，召拜司門員外郎。 十四年，崔祐甫秉政，素與迺友善。 會加郭子儀尚父，以冊禮久廢，至是復行之。 祐甫令兩省官撰冊文，未稱旨； 召迺至閣草之，立就。 詞義典裁，祐甫嘆賞久之。 數日，擢為給事中，尋遷權知兵部侍郎。 及楊炎、盧杞為相，意多醜正，以故五歲不遷。 建中四年夏，但真拜而已。
In Dali 12, after Yuan Zai was executed, Nai was summoned to court and appointed assistant director in the Bureau of Gates because of his long service. In year fourteen Cui Youfu held power; he had long been friendly with Nai. Guo Ziyi was then given the title Sublime Preceptor; the enfeoffment rites had long fallen into disuse and were now revived. Youfu had officials of the two departments draft the enfeoffment document, but it did not please the throne; Nai was summoned to the pavilion to draft it and finished immediately. The wording was classical and measured; Youfu admired it at length. Within days he was promoted to recipient of edicts and soon made acting vice minister of war. When Yang Yan and Lu Qi became chief ministers, they disliked upright men, and for this reason he went five years without promotion. In the summer of Jianzhong 4 he received only a formal appointment and nothing more.
9
其冬，涇師作亂，駕幸奉天。 迺臥疾在私第，賊泚遣使以甘言誘之，乃稱疾篤。 又令其偽宰相蔣鎮自來招誘，迺托喑疾，灸灼遍身。 鎮再至，知不可劫脅，乃嘆息曰：「鎮亦嘗忝列曹郎，茍不能死，以至於斯，寧以自辱膻腥，復欲汙穢賢哲乎？ 」歔欷而退。 及聞輿駕再幸梁州，乃自投於床，搏膺呼天，因是危惙，絕食數日而卒，時年六十。 德宗還京，聞迺之忠烈，追贈禮部尚書。 子伯芻。
That winter the Jing army mutinied and the emperor fled to Fengtian. Nai lay ill at home; the rebel Zhu Ci sent envoys with flattering words to entice him, and he claimed his illness was grave. Ci also sent his puppet chief minister Jiang Zhen to recruit him in person; Nai feigned muteness and had moxibustion burns applied all over his body. When Zhen came again and saw he could not be coerced, he sighed and said, "I too once served among the bureau directors. If one cannot die when one should, one comes to this—how could I further disgrace myself and then try to defile a worthy man?" He sobbed and withdrew. When he heard the emperor had again gone to Liangzhou, he threw himself on his bed, beat his breast, and cried to Heaven; he then fell critically ill, refused food for several days, and died at sixty. When Dezong returned to the capital and heard of Nai's loyal death, he posthumously made him Minister of Rites. His son was Bo Chu.
10
伯芻，字素芝，登進士第，誌行修謹。 淮南杜佑辟為從事，府罷，屏居吳中。 久之，征拜右補闕，遷主客員外郎。 以過從友人飲噱，為韋執誼密奏，貶虔州掾曹，復為考功員外郎裴垍善其應對機捷，遷考功郎中、集賢院學士，轉給事中。 裴垍罷相，為太子賓客，未幾而卒。 李吉甫復入相，與垍宿嫌，不加贈官； 伯芻上疏論之，贈垍太子少傅。 伯芻妻，垍從姨也。 或讒於吉甫，此以論奏。 伯芻懼，亟請散地，因出為虢州刺史。 吉甫卒，裴度擢為刑部侍郎，俄知吏部選事。 元和十年，以左常侍致仕，卒，年六十一，贈工部尚書。 伯芻風姿古雅，涉學，善談笑，而動與時適，論者稍薄之。
Bo Chu, courtesy name Suzhi, passed the jinshi examination; his conduct was cultivated and careful. Du You of Huainan took him on as staff officer; when the office closed he lived in seclusion in the Wu region. After a long interval he was summoned as Right Supplementation-eradicator and later promoted to assistant in the Ministry of Guests. Because he went out drinking and jesting with friends, Wei Zhiyi secretly memorialized against him and he was demoted to staff officer in Ganzhou. Later Pei Ji, assistant in the Bureau of Merit, admired his quick wit in audience; he was promoted to bureau director in Merit, academician of the Hall of Assembled Worthies, and then recipient of edicts. Pei Ji was removed as chief minister and made mentor to the heir apparent; he died soon afterward. Li Jifu again became chief minister; he had an old grudge against Ji and did not grant him a posthumous title; Bo Chu submitted a memorial on the matter, and Ji was posthumously made Junior Tutor to the Heir Apparent. Bo Chu's wife was Ji's younger cousin on the maternal side. Someone slandered him to Jifu; that was why he submitted the memorial. Bo Chu was afraid and urgently asked for a lighter post; he was sent out as prefect of Guo. When Jifu died, Pei Du promoted him to vice minister of justice, and soon he was in charge of selection affairs in the Ministry of Personnel. In Yuanhe 10 he retired as Left Regular Attendant, died at sixty-one, and was posthumously made Minister of Works. Bo Chu had an ancient and elegant bearing, was learned, and was skilled at conversation, yet his actions often suited the moment, and critics held him somewhat lightly.
11
子寬夫，登進士第，歷諸府從事。 寶歷中，入為監察御史。 嘗上言曰：「近日攝祭多差王府官僚，位望既輕，有乖嚴敬。 伏請今後攝太尉，差尚書省三品已上及保傅賓詹等官； 如人少，即令丞郎通攝之。 」俄轉左補闕。 少列陳岵進註《維摩經》，得濠州刺史。 寬夫與同列，因對論之，言岵因供奉僧進經以圖郡牧。 敬宗怒謂宰相曰：「陳岵不因僧得郡，諫官安得此言，須推排頭首來。 」寬夫奏曰：「昨論陳岵之時，不記發言前後，唯握筆草狀，即是微臣。 今論事不當，臣合當罪。 若尋究推排，恐傷事體。 」帝嘉其引過，欣然釋之。
His son Kuanfu passed the jinshi examination and served as staff officer in various offices. During Baoli he entered office as investigating censor. He once submitted a statement, saying, "Recently acting sacrifices have often assigned princely establishment officials; their rank is too low, which violates solemn reverence." I humbly request that hereafter for acting Grand Marshal, officials of third rank and above from the Department of State Affairs and tutors, mentors, guests, and aides be assigned; if there are too few men, let vice directors and bureau directors jointly act in their place. Soon he was transferred to Left Supplementation-eradicator. Junior Attendant Chen Hu submitted a commentary on the Vimalakirti Sutra and was made prefect of Hao. Kuanfu and his colleagues, during an audience discussion, said that Hu had presented the sutra through a court monk in order to obtain a prefecture. Jingzong angrily told the chief ministers, "Chen Hu did not obtain his prefecture through a monk—how can the remonstrance officials say such a thing? The ringleader must be identified and brought forward." Kuanfu submitted, "When Chen Hu was discussed yesterday, I do not remember who spoke before or after; I alone held the brush and drafted the memorial—that was I." If the discussion was improper, I ought to bear the guilt. If you investigate and identify the ringleader, I fear it will harm the proper conduct of affairs. The emperor praised him for taking blame upon himself and gladly released the matter.
12
寬夫弟端夫，為太常博士，駁韋綬謚議知名。 寬夫子允章、煥章。
Kuanfu's younger brother Duanfu was an erudite of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices and became famous for refuting the posthumous-title deliberation on Wei Shou. Kuanfu's sons were Yunzhang and Huanzhang.
13
允章登進士第，累官至翰林學士承旨、禮部侍郎。 咸通九年，知貢舉，出為鄂州觀察使、檢校工部尚書，後遷東都留守。 黃巢犯洛陽，允章不能拒，賊不之害，坐是廢於家。 以疾卒。
Yunzhang passed the jinshi examination and rose to expositor of the Hanlin Academy and vice minister of rites. In Xiantong 9 he supervised the examinations, was sent out as commissioner of Ezhou and acting minister of works, and later became defender of the eastern capital. Huang Chao struck Luoyang, and Yunzhang was unable to hold the city; the rebels spared his life, but he was stripped of office and sent home for it. He died of an illness.
14
=袁高=袁高，字公頤，恕己之孫。 少慷慨，慕名節。 登進士第，累辟使府，有贊佐裨益之譽。 代宗登極，征入朝，累官至給事中、御史中丞。 建中二年，擢為京畿觀察使。 以論事失旨，貶韶州長史，復拜為給事中。
Yuan Gao, courtesy name Gongyi, was a grandson of Yuan Suji. Even as a young man he was bold and high-minded, and he revered men of honor and principle. He passed the jinshi examination, served repeatedly in regional headquarters, and won praise for the help he gave his superiors. After Emperor Daizong took the throne, he was called to the capital and advanced step by step to supervising censor and attendant censor. In Jianzhong 2 he was promoted to metropolitan observation commissioner. Because his policy remonstrances displeased the throne, he was demoted to chief administrator of Shaozhou, then appointed supervising censor again.
15
貞元元年，德宗復用吉州長史盧杞為饒州刺史，令高草詔書。 高執詞頭以謁宰相盧翰、劉從一曰：「盧杞作相三年，矯詐陰賊，退斥忠良。 朋附者咳唾立至青雲、睚眥者顧盼已擠溝壑。 傲很明德，反易天常，播越鑾輿，瘡痍天下，皆杞之為也。 爰免族戮，雖示貶黜，尋已稍遷近地，若更授大郡，恐失天下之望。 惟相公執奏之，事尚可救。 」翰、從一不悅，改命舍人草之。 詔出，執之不下，仍上奏曰：「盧杞為政，窮極兇惡。 三軍將校，願食其肉； 百辟卿士，嫉之若讎。 」遺補陳京、趙需、裴佶、宇文炫、盧景亮、張薦等上疏論奏。 次日，又上疏。 高又於正殿奏云：「陛下用盧杞獨秉鈞軸，前後三年，棄斥忠良，附下罔上，使陛下越在草莽，皆杞之過。 且漢時三光失序，雨旱不時，皆宰相請罪，小者免官，大者刑戮。 杞罪合至死，陛下好生惡殺，赦杞萬死，唯貶新州司馬，旋復遷移。 今除刺史，是失天下之望。 伏惟聖意裁擇。 」上謂曰：「盧杞有不逮，是朕之過。 」復奏曰； 「盧杞奸臣，常懷詭詐，非是不逮。 」上曰：「朕已有赦。 高曰：「赦乃赦其罪，不宜授刺史。 且赦文至優黎民，今饒州大郡，若命奸臣作牧，是一州蒼生，獨受其弊。 望引常參官顧問，並擇謹厚中官，令采聽於眾。 若億兆之人異臣之言，臣當萬死。 」於是，諫官爭論於上前，上良久謂曰：「若與盧杞刺史太優，與上佐可乎？ 」曰：「可矣！ 」遂追饒州制。 翌日，遣使宣慰高云：「朕思卿言深理切，當依卿所奏。 」太子少保韋倫、太府卿張獻恭等奏：「袁高所奏至當，高是陛下一良臣，望加優異。 」
In Zhenyuan 1 Emperor Dezong again appointed Lu Qi, former chief administrator of Jizhou, prefect of Raozhou, and told Gao to draft the appointment edict. Gao took the draft edict in hand and went to see chancellors Lu Han and Liu Congyi. "Lu Qi was chancellor for three years," he said, "a man of twisted fraud and hidden villainy who drove out the loyal and worthy." His followers rose to the highest offices on his slightest favor; anyone who crossed him was, with a look, cast down into ruin. In the ninth month of Zhenyuan 12 he died at sixty-nine; he was posthumously made minister of rites.

=史臣曰=史臣曰：古之諍臣，有死於言者。 其次，引裾折檻，不改其操，亦難矣哉！ 袁高之執盧杞，存誠之戮鑒虛，有古人之遺風焉！ 平仲觸鱗之氣，糾其謬歟？ 文洽奪章，以攄府憤； 永夷絕食，不飲盜泉，節義之士也。 南仲非葬之言，盧坦西城之議，量之深也。 如數子，道為時無君子，乃是厚誣。
The historiographer says: Among remonstrance ministers of antiquity, some died for their words. Next, those who seized the robe and broke the balustrade without changing their integrity—that too was difficult!" Yuan Gao's holding fast against Lu Qi and Cuncheng's executing Jianxu have the lingering style of antiquity!" Did Pingzhong's spirit of touching the dragon scales correct their errors?" Wenzha seized the memorial to vent the commandery's anger; Yongyi starved himself and would not drink the thief's spring—men of integrity and righteousness." Nanzhong's words against the burial and Lu Tan's argument for the western city show deep judgment." To say that when such men existed the age had no gentlemen is gross slander."

贊曰：靈草指佞，諫臣匡失。 惟袁與薛，人中屈軼。 寬夫雀躍，廷老鴻軒。 姚、盧啟奏，君子之言。
The encomium says: The spirit herb points at flatterers; remonstrance ministers correct errors." Only Yuan and Xue are bending axes among men." Kuanfu leapt like a sparrow; Tinglao soared like a wild goose." The memorials of Yao and Lu are words of gentlemen.
