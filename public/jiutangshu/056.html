1
玄宗元獻皇后楊氏，弘農華陰人。 曾祖士達，隋納言，天授中，以則天母族，追封士達為鄭王，贈太尉。 父知慶，左千牛將軍，贈太尉、鄭國公。 后景雲元年八月選入太子宮。 時太平公主用事，尤忌東宮。 宮中左右持兩端，而潛附太平者，必陰伺察，事雖纖芥，皆聞于上，太子心不自安。 后時方娠，太子密謂張說曰：「用事者不欲吾多息胤，恐禍及此婦人，其如之何？」 密令說懷去胎藥而入。 太子于曲室躬自煮藥，醺然似寐，夢神人覆鼎。 既寤如夢，如是者三。 太子異之，告說。 說曰：「天命也，無宜他慮。」 既而太平誅，後果生肅宗。 太子妃王氏無子，后班在下，后不敢母肅宗。 王妃撫鞠，慈甚所生。 開元中，肅宗為忠王，后為妃，又生甯親公主。 張說以舊恩特承寵異，說亦奇忠王儀錶，心知運曆所鐘，故甯親公主降說子垍。
Empress Yuanxian of Emperor Xuanzong, the Yang clan, came from Huayin in Hongnong Commandery. Her great-grandfather Shida had served the Sui as Director of the Secretariat. In the Tianshou period, as a kinsman on Empress Wu's mother's side, he was posthumously created Prince of Zheng and honored as Grand Commandant. Her father Zhiqing, Left Colonel of the Thousand-Ox Guard, was posthumously honored as Grand Commandant and Duke of Zheng. In the eighth month of Jingyun 1, she was chosen for the crown prince's household. Princess Taiping was then in power and bore special enmity toward the crown prince. Palace attendants hedged their bets, and those who secretly favored Taiping watched in the shadows. Even the smallest matters reached the emperor's ears, and the crown prince lived in constant unease. The empress was pregnant. The crown prince confided to Zhang Yue, "Those in power do not want me to have many sons. I fear harm will reach this woman. What can we do?" He secretly told Zhang Yue to carry abortifacient medicine inside his robes and bring it in. In a side room the crown prince brewed the medicine himself. Growing drowsy as if asleep, he dreamed that a divine being covered the cauldron. When he woke, it was exactly as in the dream—and so three times. The crown prince found it strange and told Zhang Yue. Zhang Yue said, "This is Heaven's decree. Give no thought to anything else." Not long after, Taiping was put to death, and the empress did bear Emperor Suzong. Crown Princess Wang had no son. The empress ranked below her and did not dare claim Suzong as her own child. The princess consort raised him with a tenderness equal to a birth mother's. During Kaiyuan, when Suzong was Prince of Zhong, she became his consort and later bore Princess Ningqin. Zhang Yue, owing to old ties, enjoyed exceptional favor. He also admired Prince Zhong's bearing and sensed that destiny favored him, so Princess Ningqin was married to his son Qi.
2
開元十七年，后薨，葬細柳原，玄宗命說為志文，其銘云：「石獸澀兮綠苔黏，宿草殘兮白露霑。 園寢閉兮脂粉膩，不知何年開鏡奩。」 二十四年，忠王立為皇太子。 至德元年，肅宗即位于靈武。 二載五月，玄宗在蜀，誥曰：「聖人垂范，是推顧復之恩； 王者建極，抑有追尊之禮。 蓋母以子貴，德以諡尊。 故妃弘農楊氏，特稟坤靈，久厘陰教。 往以續塗山之慶，降華渚之祥。 誕發異圖，載光帝業。 而冊命猶闕，幽靈尚牴。 夏王繼統，方軫陽城之恩； 漢後褒榮，庶協昭靈之稱。 宜於彼追冊為元獻太后。」 寶應二年正月，祔葬泰陵。
In Kaiyuan 17 the empress died and was buried at Xiliuyuan. Xuanzong had Zhang Yue write the epitaph, which reads, "Stone beasts roughen; green moss clings. The garden tomb is sealed; rouge and powder thicken—who knows what year the mirror case will open again." In the twenty-fourth year, Prince Zhong was made crown prince. In Zhide 1, Suzong took the throne at Lingwu. In the fifth month of the second year, with Xuanzong in Shu, an edict declared, "The sage sets the pattern and extends the grace of nurturing; the ruler who establishes the supreme norm likewise observes rites of posthumous elevation. A mother is honored through her son, and virtue is exalted through the posthumous title. Consort Yang of Hongnong was endowed with Earth's spirit and long upheld the feminine teachings of the inner court. Once she continued the blessing of Tushan and descended with the auspice of Huazhu. She bore an extraordinary heir and brought glory to the imperial enterprise. Yet formal investiture was still lacking, and her spirit remained unreconciled. When the Xia king succeeded to the throne, he mourned the kindness owed to Yangcheng; when the Han empress was honored, it was hoped to accord with the title Zhaoling. Let her there be posthumously enfeoffed as Empress Dowager Yuanxian." In the first month of Baoying 2, she was enshrined and buried at Tailing.
3
肅宗張皇后，本南陽西鄂人，後徙家昭應。 祖母竇氏，玄宗母昭成皇太后之妹也。 昭成為天后所殺，玄宗幼失所恃，為竇姨鞠養。 景雲中，封鄧國夫人，恩渥甚隆。 其子去惑、去疑、去奢、去逸，皇姨弟也，皆至大官。 去盈尚玄宗女常芬公主。 去逸生后，天寶中，選入太子宮為良娣。 后弟清，又尚大寧郡主。
Empress Zhang of Suzong was originally from Xi'e in Nanyang; her family later moved to Zhaoying. Her grandmother Lady Dou was the younger sister of Empress Zhaocheng, Xuanzong's mother. Zhaocheng was killed by Empress Wu. Xuanzong, bereft of his mother in youth, was raised by Aunt Dou. In the Jingyun period she was created Lady of Deng and received lavish favor. Her sons Quhuo, Quyi, Qushe, and Quyi were the emperor's maternal cousins and all rose to high office. Quyi married Xuanzong's daughter, Princess Changfen. Quyi's daughter became the empress. In the Tianbao era she was chosen for the crown prince's palace as Worthy Consort. The empress's younger brother Qing also married Princess Daning.
4
后辯惠豐碩，巧中上旨。 祿山之亂，玄宗幸蜀，太子與良娣俱從，車駕渡渭，百姓遮道請留太子收復長安。 肅宗性仁孝，以上皇播越，不欲違離左右。 宦者李靖忠啟太子請留，良娣贊成之，白于玄宗。 太子如靈武，時賊已陷京師，從官單寮，道路多虞。 每太子次舍宿止，良娣必居其前。 太子曰：「捍禦非婦人之事，何以居前？」 良娣曰：「今大家跋履險難，兵衛非多，恐有倉卒，妾自當之，大家可由後而出，庶幾無患。」 及至靈武，產子，三日起，縫戰士衣。 太子勞之曰：「產忌作勞，安可容易？」 後曰：「此非妾自養之時，須辦大家事。」 肅宗即位，冊為淑妃。 贈父太僕卿去逸左僕射，母竇氏封義章縣主，姊李曇妻封清河郡夫人，妹師師封郕國夫人。 乾元元年四月，冊為皇后。 弟駙馬都尉清加特進、太常卿，同正，封范陽郡公。 皇后寵遇專房，與中官李輔國持權禁中，干預政事，請謁過當，帝頗不悅，無如之何。 後於光順門受外命婦朝，親蠶苑中，內外命婦相見，儀注甚盛。 先在靈武時，太子弟建甯王倓為后誣譖而死。 自是太子憂懼，常恐后之構禍，乃以恭遜取容，后以建寧之隙，常欲危之。 張后生二子：興王佋、定王侗。 興王早薨，侗又孩幼，故儲位獲安。
The empress was eloquent, clever, and full-figured, and skillfully matched the sovereign's wishes. When An Lushan's rebellion broke out, Xuanzong fled to Shu. The crown prince and Worthy Consort followed. At the Wei crossing the people blocked the road and begged the crown prince to stay and recover Chang'an. Suzong was naturally filial. With the retired emperor cast into exile, he could not bear to leave his side. The eunuch Li Jingzhong urged the crown prince to stay. Worthy Consort supported him and reported it to Xuanzong. The crown prince went on to Lingwu. The rebels had already taken the capital; his retinue was thin and the roads perilous. Whenever the crown prince stopped to lodge, Worthy Consort always took the place ahead of him. The crown prince said, "Defense is no woman's work. Why go ahead?" Worthy Consort said, "Your Majesty now treads danger with few guards. I fear a sudden attack. Let me meet it first; Your Majesty can leave by the rear. Then we may escape harm." At Lingwu she gave birth. On the third day she rose and sewed clothing for the soldiers. The crown prince urged her, "Childbirth forbids exertion. How can you take this so lightly?" She replied, "This is no time to tend myself. I must see to Your Majesty's affairs." When Suzong took the throne, she was invested as Cultivated Consort. Her father Quyi was posthumously made Vice Director of the Imperial Stud and Left Vice Director; her mother Lady Dou was created Lady of Yizhang; her elder sister, Li Yan's wife, Lady of Qinghe; her younger sister Shishi, Lady of Zheng. In the fourth month of Qianyuan 1 she was invested as empress. Her brother Qing, Chief Commandant of the Empress's Household, was specially advanced to Grand Master of Imperial Entertainments with concurrent rank and enfeoffed Duke of Fanyang. The empress enjoyed exclusive favor. With the eunuch Li Fuguo she wielded power inside the palace, meddled in affairs of state, and petitions and audiences exceeded all bounds. The emperor was deeply displeased but powerless. Later she received outer-court ladies in audience at Guangshun Gate and personally performed the mulberry rite in the garden. Inner and outer ladies met in ceremony of great splendor. Earlier at Lingwu, the crown prince's younger brother Prince Chen of Jianning had died after the empress slandered him. From then on the crown prince lived in fear that the empress would contrive his ruin. He won favor through humble deference, while she, because of the Jianning affair, constantly sought to destroy him. Empress Zhang bore two sons: Prince Zhou of Xing and Prince Tong of Ding. Prince of Xing died young and Tong was still an infant, so the heir's position held firm.
5
寶應元年四月，肅宗大漸，后與內官朱輝光、馬英俊、啖廷瑤、陳仙甫等謀立越王係，矯詔召太子入侍疾。 中官程元振、李輔國知其謀，及太子入，二人以難告，請太子在飛龍廄。 元振率禁軍收越王，捕硃輝光等。 俄而肅宗崩，太子監國，遂移后於別殿，幽崩。 誅馬英俊，女道士許靈素配流，山人申大芝賜死，駙馬都尉清貶硤州司馬，弟延和郡主婿鴻臚卿潛貶郴州司馬，舅鴻臚卿竇履信貶道州刺史。
In the fourth month of Baoying 1, as Suzong lay dying, the empress plotted with the inner officials Zhu Huiguang, Ma Yingjun, Dan Tingyao, and Chen Xianfu to install Prince Xi of Yue. A forged edict summoned the crown prince to the bedside. The eunuchs Cheng Yuanzhen and Li Fuguo learned of the plot. When the crown prince arrived, they warned him and asked him to remain at Feilong Stable. Yuanzhen led the imperial guard to seize Prince of Yue and arrest Zhu Huiguang and the rest. Soon Suzong died. The crown prince took charge of the state, moved the empress to a separate hall, and she died in confinement. Ma Yingjun was executed; the Daoist Xu Lingsu was banished; the recluse Shen Dazhi was put to death; Chief Commandant Qing was demoted to aide in Xia Prefecture; his brother Qian, Director of Imperial Sacrifices and Princess Yanhe's son-in-law, to aide in Chen Prefecture; and their uncle Dou Lüxin, also Director of Imperial Sacrifices, to prefect of Dao Prefecture.
6
肅宗韋妃。 父元珪，兗州都督。 肅宗為忠王時，納為孺人，及升儲位，為太子妃，生兗王僴、絳王佺、永和公主、永穆公主。 天寶中，宰相李林甫不利於太子，妃兄堅為刑部尚書，林甫羅織，起柳勣之獄，堅連坐得罪，兄弟並賜死。 太子懼，上表自理，言與妃情義不睦，請離婚，玄宗慰撫之，聽離。 妃遂削髮被尼服，居禁中佛舍。 西京失守，妃亦陷賊。 至德二年，薨於京城。
Consort Wei of Emperor Suzong. Her father Yuanqi was military governor of Yan Prefecture. When Suzong was Prince of Zhong he took her as a lady; when he became heir apparent she became crown princess and bore Prince Xian of Yan, Prince Quan of Jiang, Princess Yonghe, and Princess Yongmu. In Tianbao, Chancellor Li Linfu was hostile to the crown prince. The consort's brother Jian, Minister of Justice, was framed in the Liu Ji affair; Jian was implicated and the brothers were all put to death. The crown prince, in fear, submitted a memorial clearing himself, saying he and the consort were not close and asking for divorce. Xuanzong comforted him and granted it. The consort shaved her head, put on nun's robes, and lived in a Buddhist hall within the palace. When the Western Capital fell, she too was captured by the rebels. In Zhide 2 she died in the capital.
7
肅宗章敬皇后吳氏，坐父事沒入掖庭。 開元二十三年，玄宗幸忠王邸，見王服御蕭然，傍無媵侍，命將軍高力士選掖庭宮人以賜之，而吳后在籍中。 容止端麗，性多謙抑，寵遇益隆。 明年，生代宗皇帝。 二十八年薨，葬於春明門外。
Empress Zhangjing of Suzong, née Wu, entered the palace women because of her father's offense. In Kaiyuan 23 Xuanzong visited Prince Zhong's residence, found his furnishings sparse and no attendant ladies beside him, and ordered General Gao Lishi to choose palace women from the inner quarters as gifts. Wu was on the register. Her bearing was graceful and her nature deeply modest; favor toward her steadily grew. The next year she bore Emperor Daizong. In the twenty-eighth year she died and was buried outside Chunming Gate.
8
代宗即位之年十二月，群臣以肅宗山陵有期，准禮以先太后祔陵廟。 宰臣郭子儀等上表曰：
In the twelfth month of Daizong's accession year, the ministers, with Suzong's tomb date set, enshrined the former empress dowager at tomb and temple according to rite. Chancellor Guo Ziyi and others submitted a memorial:
9
「儷宸極者，允歸於淑德； 諡徽號者，必副於鴻名。 當履運而承天，則因心而追往，此先王之明訓，聖人之茂典也。 伏惟先太后圓精挺質，方祗稟秀。 禎符協於四星，典禮敦于萬國，得元和之正氣，韞霄漢之清英。 顧史求箴，道先於壺則； 捴謙率禮，教備於中闈。 太陰無昃朓之征，丙殿有祝延之慶。 尊敬師傅，佩服禮經，勤於蘋藻之薦，罔貴珩璜之飾。 徽音允穆，嘉慶聿彰，憲度輔佐之勞，緝熙玄默之化，足以光昭宗祀，作配紫微。 豈《騶虞》之風，行于江、漢之域； 《葛覃》之詠，起自岐陽之下。 爰膺歷數，作啟聖明，大拯艱難，永清夷夏。 雖復文母成周王之業，慶都誕帝堯之聖，異代同符，彼多慚德。 昊蒼不吊，聖善長違。 當圓魄之成，玉英早落； 有坤儀之美，象服未加。 悲懷於先遠之辰，感慟于易名之日。 伏以山陵貞兆，良吉有期，虞祔之儀，式資配享。 率由故實，敬奉嘉名。 謹按諡法：『敬慎高明曰章，法度明大曰章，夙興夜寐曰敬，齊莊中正曰敬。』 敢遵先典，仰圖懿德，謹上尊諡曰章敬皇后。」
"She who shares the throne belongs by right to virtuous conduct; she who receives a posthumous title must bear a name worthy of it. When one inherits the mandate and receives Heaven, one honors the past from the heart—this is the bright instruction of former kings and the abundant norm of sages. We respectfully consider that the former empress dowager, pure in essence and upright in substance, received Earth's excellence. Auspicious signs matched the four stars; ceremonial norms were honored among all states; she possessed harmony's correct breath and stored the Milky Way's clear brilliance. Looking to history for admonition, the Way came first in inner-court rule; holding humility and leading by rite, her teaching was complete within the inner palace. The great yin showed no sign of waning; in the Bing Hall there were prayers for long life. She revered her teachers, wore the rites upon her person, diligently offered water-cress and duckweed, and did not prize pendant and ring. Her fine reputation was true and solemn, her good fortune brightly manifest; her labor in assisting with statutes and her silent, radiant transformation were enough to glorify the ancestral sacrifices and serve as consort to the Purple Palace. Could the wind of the 'Zou Yu' run in the Yangtze and Han; the song of the 'Ge Tan' rise from below Mount Qi? She received the succession and opened sage clarity, greatly rescued hardship, and forever cleared barbarian and Chinese alike. Though Wen Mu completed the Zhou king's enterprise and Qingdu bore Emperor Yao's sagehood, different ages bore the same sign—yet they would mostly blush before her virtue. Great Heaven did not pity; sage goodness was long lost. When the full orb was complete, the jade blossom fell early; though she had the beauty of the feminine norm, ceremonial robes were not yet conferred. Grief filled the time of the distant past; anguish moved the day of changing the name. We respectfully note that the tomb's auspicious site has a fixed date; the rite of enshrinement requires a consort for joint sacrifice. Following former precedent, we respectfully offer this fine name. We respectfully consult the posthumous-name law: 'Reverent, cautious, and lofty is called Zhang; clear in law and great in measure is called Zhang; rising early and sleeping late is called Jing; even, solemn, and centered is called Jing.' We dare follow the former canon, look up to her excellent virtue, and respectfully submit the honored posthumous title Empress Zhangjing."
10
二年三月，祔葬建陵。 啟春明門外舊壟，后容狀如生，粉黛如故，而衣皆赭黃色，見者駭異，以為聖子符兆之先。
In the third month of the second year she was enshrined and buried at Jianling. They opened the old mound outside Chunming Gate. The empress looked as in life, powder and rouge unchanged, and her garments were all ochre-yellow. Viewers were astonished and took it as an omen of the sage son's mandate.
11
后父令珪，寶應初贈太尉； 母李氏，贈秦國夫人。 叔令瑤，拜太子家令，封馮翊郡公； 令瑜，太子右諭德，封濟陰郡公。 后兄漵，鴻臚少卿，封鄄城縣公； 澄，太子賓客，濮陽縣公； 湊，太子詹事，臨濮縣公； 並加開府儀同三司。 漵位終金吾大將軍，湊位終京兆尹，見《外戚傳》。
At the beginning of Baoying her father Linggui was posthumously honored as Grand Commandant; her mother Lady Li as Lady of Qin. Her uncle Lingyao was appointed Tutor to the Crown Prince and enfeoffed Duke of Fenyang; Lingyu was Right Mentor to the Crown Prince and enfeoffed Duke of Jiyin. Her elder brother Su was Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices and enfeoffed Duke of Juancheng; Cheng was Companion to the Crown Prince and Duke of Puyang; Cou was Chief Administrator to the Crown Prince and Duke of Linpu; all were given the added title of Grand Master with rank equal to the Three Excellencies. Su ended as General of the Gold Crow Guard; Cou as Intendant of Jingzhao—see the Biographies of Outer Relatives.
12
代宗睿真皇后沈氏，吳興人，世為冠族。 父易直，秘書監。 開元末，以良家子選入東宮，賜太子男廣平王。 天寶元年，生德宗皇帝。 祿山之亂，玄宗幸蜀，諸王、妃、主從幸不及者，多陷於賊，后被拘於東都掖庭。 及代宗破賊，收東都，見之，留于宮中，方經略北征，未暇迎歸長安。 俄而史思明再陷河洛。 及朝義敗，復收東都，失后所在，莫測存亡。 代宗遣使求訪，十餘年寂無所聞。 德宗即位，下詔曰：「王者事父孝，故事天明； 事母孝，故事地察。 則事天莫先于嚴父，事地莫盛於尊親。 朕恭承天命，以主社稷，執珪璧以事上帝，祖宗克配，園寢永終。 而內朝虛位，闕問安之禮，銜悲內惻，憂戀終歲。 思欲曆舟車之路，以聽求音問，而主茲重器，莫匪深哀。 是用仰稽舊儀，敬崇大號，舉茲禮命，式遵前典。 宜令公卿大夫稽度前訓，上皇太后尊號。」
Empress Ruizhen of Daizong, née Shen, came from Wuxing; her family for generations were leading clans. Her father Yizhi was Director of the Secretariat. At the end of Kaiyuan, as a woman of good family she was chosen for the Eastern Palace and given to the crown prince's son, Prince of Guangping. In Tianbao 1 she bore Emperor Dezong. When An Lushan's rebellion broke out, Xuanzong fled to Shu. Princes, consorts, and princesses who could not follow mostly fell to the rebels. The empress was detained in the Eastern Capital's inner quarters. When Daizong defeated the rebels and recovered the Eastern Capital, he saw her and kept her in the palace. He was then planning the northern campaign and had no leisure to bring her back to Chang'an. Soon Shi Siming again took Henei and Luoyang. When Chaoyi was defeated and the Eastern Capital was recovered again, the empress's whereabouts were lost and none could tell whether she lived or died. Daizong sent envoys to seek her; for more than ten years there was no word. When Dezong ascended the throne, an edict proclaimed, "When a king serves his father with filial piety, he thereby illuminates Heaven; when he serves his mother with filial piety, he thereby illuminates Earth. Thus in serving Heaven nothing comes before honoring the father; in serving Earth nothing surpasses honoring the parent. We respectfully receive Heaven's mandate and preside over the altars of soil and grain; we hold jade scepter and disk in serving the Supreme Lord; the ancestors are properly matched, and the garden tomb is forever complete. Yet the inner court's place stands empty, the rite of inquiring after well-being is lacking, grief fills the heart, and longing endures through the year. We wish to traverse roads by boat and carriage to seek word of her, yet in holding this heavy vessel none is free of deep sorrow. Therefore we respectfully look to former rites, reverently raise a great title, offer this ceremonial command, and follow the former canon. Let the high ministers and grandees examine former instruction and submit the honored title of empress dowager."

The Office of Rites memorialized, "We respectfully consult the Zengzi Questions: 'The base do not eulogize the noble; the young do not eulogize the elder—this is rite.' In antiquity the Son of Heaven proclaimed Heaven to eulogize; the empress's posthumous title was then read in the temple. The Collected Rites of Jiangdu cites the White Tiger Discourse: 'Where is the empress eulogized? It is considered to be in the temple.' 贊曰：坤德既軌，彤管有煒。 韋、武喪邦，毒侔蛇虺。 陰教斯僻，嬪風浸毀。 賢哉長孫，母儀何偉。
The eulogist says: Once the feminine virtue is aligned, the red record shines bright. Wei and Wu ruined the state; their poison matched serpents. The feminine teaching turned perverse; consortly wind gradually decayed. How worthy was Lady Changsun; what a grand motherly model!
