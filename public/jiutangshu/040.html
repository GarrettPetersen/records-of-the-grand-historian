天文之為十二次，所以辨析天體，紀綱辰象，上以考七曜之宿度，下以配萬方之分野，仰觀變謫，而驗之于郡國也。 《傳》曰：「歲在星紀，而淫于玄枵。」 「姜氏、任氏，實守其地。」 及七國交爭，善星者有甘德、石申，更配十二分野，故有周、秦、齊、楚、韓、趙、燕、魏、宋、衛、魯、鄭、吳、越等國。 張衡、蔡邕，又以漢郡配焉。 自此因循，但守其舊文，無所變革。 且懸象在上，終天不易，而郡國沿革，名稱屢遷，遂令後學難為憑准。 貞觀中，李淳風撰《法象志》，始以唐之州縣配焉。 至開元初，沙門一行又增損其書，更為詳密。 既事包今古，與舊有異同，頗裨後學，故錄其文著於篇。 並配武德以來交蝕淺深及注蝕不虧，以紀日月之變云爾。
Astronomy divides heaven into twelve celestial stations to distinguish celestial bodies and organize stellar phenomena—above, to verify the lodge-degrees of the seven luminaries; below, to match the territorial fields of the myriad regions; gazing upward at transformations and anomalies and testing them against the commanderies and kingdoms. The Commentary says: "The year stood in Xingji, yet strayed into Xuanxiao." "The Jiang and Ren clans in truth held those lands." When the Seven States contended, those skilled in stars included Gan De and Shi Shen, who reassigned the twelve territorial fields, hence there were the states of Zhou, Qin, Qi, Chu, Han, Zhao, Yan, Wei, Song, Wei, Lu, Zheng, Wu, Yue, and others. Zhang Heng and Cai Yong further matched them to Han commanderies. From this point they followed precedent, merely preserving the old texts without any reform. Moreover, the suspended signs above remain unchanged throughout heaven, while commanderies and kingdoms underwent successive changes and names were repeatedly altered, so that later students found it difficult to rely on them as standards. During the Zhenguan era, Li Chunfeng compiled the Record of Celestial Forms, first matching them to the prefectures and counties of Tang. At the beginning of the Kaiyuan era, the monk Yixing further revised and supplemented his book, making it still more detailed. Since it embraced matters from antiquity to the present and differed in places from earlier accounts, it greatly aided later students; therefore its text is recorded here in full. It also records the depth of conjunctions and eclipses since the Wude era, and eclipses in which the disk was not fully obscured, to chronicle the transformations of sun and moon.

須女、虛、危，玄枵之次。 子初起女五度，二千三百七十四分，秒四少。 中虛九度，終危十二度。 其分野：自濟北郡東逾濟水，涉平陰至於山茌，漢太山郡山茌縣，屬齊州西南之界。 東南及高密，漢高密國，今在密州北界。 自此以上，玄枵之分。 東盡東萊之地，漢之東萊郡及膠東國，今為萊州、登州也。 又得漢之北海、千乘、淄川、濟南、齊郡，今為淄、青、齊等州，及濟州東界。 及平原、渤海，盡九河故道之南，濱于碣石。 今為德州、棣州，滄州其北界。 自九河故道之北，屬析木分也。
Nü, Xu, and Wei—the station of Xuanxiao. At the beginning of zi it starts at Nü, five degrees, two thousand three hundred seventy-four minutes, four seconds slightly deficient. The midpoint is at Xu, nine degrees; it ends at Wei, twelve degrees. Its territorial field: from Jibei Commandery eastward across the Ji River, crossing Pingyin to Shanzhi—Shanzhi County of Han Mount Tai Commandery, on the southwestern border of Qi Prefecture. Southeastward to Gaomi—the Han Kingdom of Gaomi, now on the northern border of Mi Prefecture. From here upward belongs to the field of Xuanxiao. Eastward to the lands of Donglai—Han's Donglai Commandery and the Kingdom of Jiaodong, now Laizhou and Dengzhou. It also includes Han's Beihai, Qiancheng, Zichuan, Jinan, and Qi Commanderies, now Zi, Qing, and Qi prefectures, and the eastern border of Ji Prefecture. And Pingyuan and Bohai, to the south of the old course of the Nine Rivers, bordering Jieshi. Now Dezhou and Di Prefecture; Cang Prefecture forms its northern border. North of the old course of the Nine Rivers belongs to the field of Ximu.
3
營室、東壁，陬訾之次。 亥初起危十三度，二千九百二十六分太。 中室十二度，五百五十分，秒二十一半。 終奎一度。 其分野：自王屋、太行而東，盡漢河內之地，今為懷州、洺、衛州之西境。 北負漳、鄴，東及館陶、聊城，漢地自黎陽、內黃及鄴、魏、武安，東至館陶、元城，皆屬魏郡； 自頓邱、三城、武陽，東至聊城，皆屬東郡。 今為相、魏、衛州。 東盡漢東郡之地，漢東郡、清河，西南至白馬、濮陽，東至東河、須昌，濱濟，至於鄆城。 今為滑州、濮州、鄆州。 其須昌、濟東之地，屬降婁，非豕韋也。
Yingshi and Dongbi—the station of Zouzi. At the beginning of hai it starts at Wei, thirteen degrees, two thousand nine hundred twenty-six minutes slightly excess. The midpoint is at Shi, twelve degrees, five hundred fifty minutes, twenty-one and a half seconds. It ends at Kui, one degree. Its territorial field: from Wangwu and the Taihang eastward, encompassing all of Han's Henei lands, now the western borders of Huaizhou, Ming, and Wei prefectures. Northward it bears upon Zhang and Ye, eastward to Guantao and Liaocheng—Han lands from Liyang, Neihuang, Ye, Wei, and Wu'an east to Guantao and Yuancheng all belonged to Wei Commandery; from Dunqiu, Sancheng, and Wuyang east to Liaocheng all belonged to Dong Commandery. Now Xiang, Wei, and Wei prefectures. Eastward to the lands of Han's Dong Commandery—Han's Dong and Qinghe commanderies, southwest to Baima and Puyang, east to Donghe and Xuchang, bordering the Ji, reaching Yuncheng. Now Hua, Pu, and Yun prefectures. The lands of Xuchang and Jidong belong to Jianglü, not to Zhuwei.
4
奎、婁及胃，降婁之次。 戌初起奎二度，一千二百一十七分，秒十七少。 中婁一度，一千八百八十三。 終胃三度。 其分野：南屆钜野，東達梁父，以負東海。 又東至於呂梁，乃東南抵淮水，而東盡于徐夷之地。 東為降婁之次。 得漢東平、魯國。 漢東平國在任城、平陸，今在兗州。 奎為大澤，在陬訾之下流，濱於淮、泗，東北負山，為婁、胃之墟。 蓋中國膏腴之地，百穀之所阜也。 胃星得馬牧之氣，與冀之北土同占。
Kui, Lou, and Wei—the station of Jianglü. At the beginning of xu it starts at Kui, two degrees, one thousand two hundred seventeen minutes, seventeen seconds slightly deficient. The midpoint is at Lou, one degree, one thousand eight hundred eighty-three. It ends at Wei, three degrees. Its territorial field: southward to Juye, eastward to Liangfu, bearing upon the Eastern Sea. Also eastward to Lüliang, then southeast to the Huai River, and eastward to the lands of the Xu Yi. Eastward is the station of Jianglü. It includes Han's Dongping and Lu. Han's Dongping was at Rencheng and Pinglu, now in Yanzhou. Kui is the Great Marsh, below the downstream of Zouzi, bordering the Huai and Si, bearing mountains to the northeast—the domain of Lou and Wei. It is in general the fertile heartland of China, where the hundred grains flourish. The Wei star receives the qi of horse pasture, sharing the same astrological correspondence as the northern lands of Ji.

昴、畢，大梁之次。 畢酉初起胃四度，二千五百四十九分，秒八太。 中昴六度，一百七十四分半。 終畢九度。 其分野：自魏郡濁漳之北，得漢之趙國、廣平、钜鹿、常山，東及清河、信都，北據中山、真定。 今為洺、趙、邢、恆、定、冀、貝、深八州。 又分相、魏、博之北界，與瀛州之西，全趙之分。 又北盡漢代郡、雁門、雲中、定襄之地，與北方群狄之國，皆大梁分也。
Mao and Bi—the station of Daliang. At the beginning of you it starts at Wei, four degrees, two thousand five hundred forty-nine minutes, eight seconds slightly excess. The midpoint is at Mao, six degrees, one hundred seventy-four and a half minutes. It ends at Bi, nine degrees. Its territorial field: from the north of the muddy Zhang in Wei Commandery, it includes Han's Zhao, Guangping, Julu, and Changshan, east to Qinghe and Xindu, northward occupying Zhongshan and Zhending. Now Ming, Zhao, Xing, Heng, Ding, Ji, Bei, and Shen—the eight prefectures. Also dividing the northern borders of Xiang, Wei, and Bo and the west of Ying Prefecture—all belong to the field of Zhao. Also northward to the limits of Han's Dai, Yanmen, Yunzhong, and Dingxiang, and the northern states of the various Di—all belong to the field of Daliang.

觜觿、參伐，實沈之次也。 申初起畢十度，八百四十一分，十五太。 中參七度，一千五百二十六，終井十一度。 其分野：得漢河東郡，今為蒲、絳、晉州，又得澤州及慈州界也。 及上黨、今為澤、潞、儀、沁也。 太原，今為並、汾州。 盡西河之地。 今為隰州、石州、嵐州，西涉河，得銀州以北也。 又西河戎狄之國，皆實沈分也。 今河東郡永樂、芮城、河北縣及河曲豐、勝、夏州，皆為實沈之次，東井之分也。 參伐為戎索，為武政，故殷河東，盡大夏之墟。 上党次居下流，與趙、魏相接，為觜觿之分。
Zixī and Canfa—the station of Sishen. At the beginning of shen it starts at Bi, ten degrees, eight hundred forty-one minutes, fifteen seconds slightly excess. The midpoint is at Shen, seven degrees, one thousand five hundred twenty-six; it ends at Jing, eleven degrees. Its territorial field: it includes Han's Hedong Commandery, now Pu, Jiang, and Jin prefectures, and also the borders of Ze and Ci prefectures. And Shangdang—now Ze, Lu, Yi, and Qin prefectures. Taiyuan—now Bing and Fen prefectures. To the limits of Xihe lands. Now Xi, Shi, and Lan prefectures; westward across the river, reaching north of Yin Prefecture. Also the Rong and Di states of Xihe—all belong to the field of Sishen. Now Hedong Commandery's Yongle, Ruicheng, Hebei County, and the Hequ region of Feng, Sheng, and Xia prefectures all belong to the station of Sishen and the field of Dongjing. Canfa is the Rong cord, the domain of martial governance; therefore the Yin held Hedong and encompassed the ruins of Great Xia. Shangdang lies in the lower reaches, adjoining Zhao and Wei—the field of Zixī.

東井、輿鬼，鶉首之次也。 未初起井十二度，二千一百七十二秒，十五太。 中井二十七度，二千八百二十八分，秒一半。 終柳六度。 其分野：自漢之三輔及北地、上郡、安定，西自隴坻至河西，西南盡巴、蜀、漢中之地，及西南夷犍為、越巂、益州郡，極南河之表，東至牂柯，皆鶉首分也。 鶉首之分，得《禹貢》雍、梁二州，其郡縣易知，故不詳載。 狼星分野在江、河上源之西，孤矢、犬、雞，皆徼外之象。 今之西羌、吐蕃、蕃渾，及西南徼外夷，皆狼星之象。
Dongjing and Yuji—the station of Zhenshou. At the beginning of wei it starts at Jing, twelve degrees, two thousand one hundred seventy-two seconds, fifteen seconds slightly excess. The midpoint is at Jing, twenty-seven degrees, two thousand eight hundred twenty-eight minutes, one and a half seconds. It ends at Liu, six degrees. Its territorial field: from Han's Three Metropolises and Beidi, Shang, and Anding, west from Longqi to Hexi, southwest to the lands of Ba, Shu, and Hanzhong, and the southwestern Yi of Jianwei, Yuexi, and Yizhou commanderies, to the southern limit beyond the Yellow River, east to Zangke—all belong to the field of Zhenshou. The field of Zhenshou includes Yong and Liang of the Documents of Yu; its commanderies and counties are easy to identify, and therefore are not recorded in detail. The territorial field of the Wolf star lies west of the upper reaches of the Yangtze and Han rivers; the Lonely Arrow, Dog, and Rooster—all are signs beyond the frontier. Today's Western Qiang, Tibet, and the Fan and Hun, and the southwestern frontier Yi beyond the passes—all are signs of the Wolf star.

柳、星、張，鶉火之次。 午初起柳七度，四百六十四，秒七少。 中七星七度，一千一百三。 終張十四度。 其分野：北自滎澤、滎陽，並京、索，暨山南，得新鄭、密縣，至於方陽。 方陽之南得漢之潁川郡陽翟、崇高、郟城、襄城，南盡鄴縣。 今為鄧、汝、唐、仙四州界。 又漢南陽郡，北自宛、葉，南盡漢東申、隨之地，大抵以淮源桐柏、東陽為限。 今之唐州、隨州屬鶉火，申州屬壽星。 又自洛邑負河之南，西及函谷南紀，達武當漢水之陰，盡弘農郡。 漢弘農盧氏、陝縣，今為虢、陝二州。 上洛、商洛為商州。 丹水為均州。 宜陽、沔池、新安、陸渾，今屬洛州。 古成周、虢、鄭、管、鄶、東虢、密、滑、焦、唐、申、鄧，皆鶉火分也，及祝融氏之都。 新鄭為祝融氏之墟，屬鶉火。 其東鄙則入壽星。 舊說皆在函谷，非也。 柳、星、輿鬼之東，又接漢源，故殷商、洛之陽，接南河之上流。 七星上系軒轅，得土行之正位，中嶽象也，故為河南之分。 張星直河南漢東，與鶉尾同占。
Liu, Xing, and Zhang—the station of Zhenhuo. At the beginning of wu it starts at Liu, seven degrees, four hundred sixty-four, seven seconds slightly deficient. The midpoint is at the Seven Stars, seven degrees, one thousand one hundred three. It ends at Zhang, fourteen degrees. Its territorial field: north from Xingze and Xingyang, including Jing and Suo, and the southern mountains, reaching Xinzheng and Mi County to Fangyang. South of Fangyang it includes Han's Yingchuan Commandery—Yangzhai, Chonggao, Jiacheng, and Xiangcheng, south to Ye County. Now the borders of Deng, Ru, Tang, and Xian—the four prefectures. Also Han's Nanyang Commandery, north from Wan and Ye, south to Shen and Sui of eastern Han—generally bounded by the Tongbai source of the Huai, and Dongyang. Now Tang and Sui prefectures belong to Zhenhuo; Shen Prefecture belongs to Shouxing. Also from Luoyi south of the Yellow River, west to the southern limit at Hangu Pass, reaching the northern bank of the Wudang Han River, to the limits of Hongnong Commandery. Han's Hongnong—Lushi and Shan County, now Guo and Shan—the two prefectures. Shangluo and Shangluo are Shang Prefecture. The Danshui is Jun Prefecture. Yiyang, Mianchi, Xin'an, and Luhun—now belonging to Luo Prefecture. Ancient Chengzhou, Guo, Zheng, Guan, Kuai, Eastern Guo, Mi, Hua, Jiao, Tang, Shen, and Deng—all belong to the field of Zhenhuo, and to the capital of Zhurong. Xinzheng is the domain of Zhurong, belonging to Zhenhuo. Its eastern border enters Shouxing. Older accounts place them all at Hangu Pass—this is incorrect. East of Liu, Xing, and Yuji again adjoins the Han source; therefore the Yin held Shang and the south of Luo, adjoining the upper reaches of the southern Yellow River. The Seven Stars are tied to Xuanyuan, receiving the orthodox position of the Earth element—the image of the Central Peak; therefore they are the field of Henan. The Zhang star stands directly south of Henan and east of Han, sharing the same astrological correspondence as Zhenwei.

翼、軫，鶉尾之次。 巳初起張十五度，一千七百九十五，秒二十二少。 中翼十二度，二千四百六十一，秒八半。 終軫九度。 其分野：自房陵、白帝而東，盡漢之南郡、南郡：巫縣，今在夔州。 秭歸在西，夷陵在峽州。 襄、夔、郢、申在襄、郢界，餘為荊州。 江夏，江夏：竟陵今為復州，安、鄂、蘄、沔、黃五州，皆漢江夏界。 東達廬江南郡。 漢廬江之尋陽，今在江州，于山河之像，宜屬鶉尾也。 濱彭蠡之西，得漢長沙、武陵、桂陽、零陵郡。 零陵今為道州、永州。 桂陽今為郴州。 大抵自沅、湘上流，西通黔安之左，皆楚之分也。 又逾南紀，盡郁林、合浦之地。 郁林縣今在貴州。 定林縣今在廉州。 合浦縣今為桂州。 今自富、昭、蒙、龔、繡、容、白、罕八州以西，皆屬鶉尾之墟也。 荊、楚、鄖、鄀、羅、權、巴、夔與南方蠻貊，殷河南之南。 其中一星主長沙國，逾嶺徼而南，皆甌東、青丘之分。 今安南諸州，在雲漢上源之東，宜屬鶉火。
Yi and Zhen—the station of Zhenwei. At the beginning of si it starts at Zhang, fifteen degrees, one thousand seven hundred ninety-five, twenty-two seconds slightly deficient. The midpoint is at Yi, twelve degrees, two thousand four hundred sixty-one, eight and a half seconds. It ends at Zhen, nine degrees. Its territorial field: from Fangling and Baidi eastward, to the limits of Han's Nan, Nan Commandery—Wu County, now in Kui Prefecture. Zigui is to the west; Yiling is in Xia Prefecture. Xiang, Kui, Ying, and Shen lie on the Xiang and Ying border; the remainder is Jing Prefecture. Jiangxia—Jiangxia: Jingling is now Fu Prefecture; An, E, Qi, Mian, and Huang—the five prefectures—are all within Han's Jiangxia border. Eastward to Lujiang and Nan Commandery. Han's Xunyang of Lujiang is now in Jiang Prefecture; in the image of mountains and rivers it ought to belong to Zhenwei. Bordering the west of Poyang, it includes Han's Changsha, Wuling, Guiyang, and Lingling commanderies. Lingling is now Dao and Yong prefectures. Guiyang is now Chen Prefecture. Generally from the upper reaches of the Yuan and Xiang, west connecting to the left of Qian'an—all belong to the field of Chu. Also crossing the southern limit, to the lands of Yulin and Hepu. Yulin County is now in Gui Prefecture. Dinglin County is now in Lian Prefecture. Hepu County is now Gui Prefecture. Now from Fu, Zhao, Meng, Gong, Xiu, Rong, Bai, and Han—the eight prefectures westward—all belong to the domain of Zhenwei. Jing, Chu, Yun, E, Luo, Quan, Ba, and Kui, and the southern Man and Mo—the Yin south of Henan. Among them one star governs the Kingdom of Changsha; crossing the ranges southward—all belong to the fields of Ou East and Green Hill. Now the various prefectures of Annan, east of the upper source of the Cloudy Han, ought to belong to Zhenhuo.

角、亢，壽星之次。 辰初起軫十度，八十七，秒十四半。 中角八度，七百五十，秒三十。 終氐一度。 其分野：自原武、管城，濱河、濟之南，東至封邱、陳留，盡陳、蔡、汝南之地，逾淮源至於弋陽。 漢陳留郡，自封邱、陳留已東，皆入大火之分。 漢汝南，今為豫州。 西華、南頓、項城縣今為陳州。 汝陰縣今在潁州。 弋陽縣在光州。 西涉南陽郡，至於桐柏，又東北抵嵩之東陽。 漢南陽郡舂陵、湖陽，蔡陽後分為舂陵郡，後魏以為南荊州，今有舊義陽郡，在申國之東界，今為申州。 按中國地絡，在南北河之間，故申、隨、光三州，皆屬《禹貢》豫州之分，宜屬鶉火、壽星。 非南方負海之地。 古陳、蔡、隨、許，皆屬壽星分也。 氐星涉壽星之次，故其分野殷雒邑眾山之東，與亳土相接。
Jiao and Kang—the station of Shouxing. At the beginning of chen it starts at Zhen, ten degrees, eighty-seven, fourteen and a half seconds. The midpoint is at Jiao, eight degrees, seven hundred fifty, thirty seconds. It ends at Di, one degree. Its territorial field: from Yuanwu and Guancheng, south of the Yellow and Ji rivers, east to Fengqiu and Chenliu, to the lands of Chen, Cai, and Runan, crossing the Huai source to Yiyang. Han's Chenliu Commandery—from Fengqiu and Chenliu eastward—all enter the field of Great Fire. Han's Runan is now Yu Prefecture. Xihua, Nandun, and Xiangcheng counties are now Chen Prefecture. Ruyin County is now in Ying Prefecture. Yiyang County is in Guang Prefecture. Westward crossing Nanyang Commandery to Tongbai, and northeast reaching the Dongyang of Song. Han's Nanyang Commandery—Chunling and Huyang; Yangcai was later divided into Chunling Commandery; Later Wei made it Southern Jing Prefecture; now there is the old Yiyang Commandery on the eastern border of Shen—now Shen Prefecture. According to the terrestrial network of China, between the northern and southern Yellow Rivers, therefore Shen, Sui, and Guang—the three prefectures—all belong to the field of Yu in the Documents of Yu, and ought to belong to Zhenhuo and Shouxing. They are not lands of the south bearing upon the sea. Ancient Chen, Cai, Sui, and Xu—all belong to the field of Shouxing. Former regulations: the Astronomy Bureau was subordinate to the Secretariat, supervising observation of heaven, the calendar, and celestial signs. In the reign of Empress Wu, the technician Shang Xianfu was skilled in calendar calculation and was summoned and appointed Director of the Astronomy Bureau. Xianfu declined: "I am a man of the mountains and wilds, by nature free and ungoverned; I cannot bow to serving official superiors." The Empress regretted losing his talent; on the nineteenth day of the fifth month she decreed that the Astronomy Bureau would no longer be subordinate to the Secretariat, becoming an independent office, and was renamed the Bureau of the Armillary Heaven. By the sixth day of the seventh month it was again renamed the Bureau of the Armillary Sphere. In the eighth month Xianfu died; it reverted to the Astronomy Bureau, subordinate to the Secretariat, and the officials he had advanced were all abolished. Sixth month—it was changed to the Astronomy Directorate, not subordinate to the Secretariat. Seventh month—it reverted to the Astronomy Bureau, subordinate to the Secretariat. Eighth month—it was again changed to the Astronomy Directorate. Eleventh month—it was again changed to the Astronomy Bureau. Second year, intercalary ninth month—it was changed to the Bureau of the Armillary Sphere. Second month—it was changed to the Astronomy Directorate. Fifteenth year, first month, it was changed to the Astronomy Bureau, subordinate to the Secretariat, and again changed to the Astronomy Directorate.
