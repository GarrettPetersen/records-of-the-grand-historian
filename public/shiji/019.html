太史公讀列封至便侯，曰：有以也夫！ 長沙王者，著令甲，稱其忠焉。 昔高祖定天下，功臣非同姓疆土而王者八國。 至孝惠時，唯獨長江全，禪五世，禪五世，以無嗣絕，意無過，為藩守職，信矣。 故其澤流枝庶，毋功而侯者數人。 及孝惠訖孝景閒五十載，追修高祖時遣功臣，及從代來，吳楚之勞，諸侯子弟若肺腑，外國歸義，封者九十有餘。 咸表始終，當世仁義成功之著者也。
Reading through the register of enfeoffments down to the Marquis of Bian, the Grand Historian said, "There is reason in this indeed!" In Ordinance A, the King of Changsha is held up as a model of loyalty. When Gaozu settled the realm, eight non-Liu kingdoms were granted to meritorious ministers. By Emperor Hui's time, only Changsha still stood intact, having passed through five generations; when the succession line ended for lack of heirs, they had been without fault; as frontier vassals they faithfully kept their posts—and that is credible. Thus, their bounty flowed to collateral branches, and several men were enfeoffed as marquises though they had rendered no merit. From Emperor Hui through Emperor Jing, over fifty years, the court repeatedly honored Gaozu's surviving meritorious ministers, those who had come from Dai, the men who had toiled in the Wu and Chu campaigns, sons of feudal lords as close as kin, and outsiders who submitted in allegiance. More than ninety were enfeoffed. All show their courses from start to finish—they are the era's outstanding exemplars of humane rule and successful merit.

平陸。楚元王子，侯，三千二百六十七戶。二。元年四月乙巳，侯劉禮元年。三年，侯禮為楚王，國除。
休楚元王子，侯。二。元年四月乙巳，侯富元年。三年，侯富以兄子戎為楚王反，富與家屬至長安北闕自歸，不能相教，上印綬。詔復王。後以平陸侯為楚王，更封富為紅侯。

紅。楚元王子，侯，千七百五十戶。四。三年四月乙巳，莊侯富元年。一。前七年，悼侯澄元年。九。中元年，敬侯發元年。十五。元朔四年，侯章元年。一。元朔五年，侯章薨，無後，國除。

俞。以將軍吳楚反擊齊有功。布故彭越舍人，越反時布使齊，還已梟越，布祭哭之，當亨，出忠言，高祖舍之。黥布反，布為都尉，侯，戶千八百。六。六年四月丁卯，侯欒布元年。中五年，侯布薨。十。元狩六年，侯賁坐為太常廟犧牲不如令，有罪，國除。

It was the year 99 BC, and a grim tension hung over the Han dynasty court, as word reached Chang'an of General Li Ling's defeat at the hands of the Xiongnu. When word reached Emperor Wu that Li Ling had not died in battle, but instead had surrendered to the Xiongnu, the emperor was furious. The one man in the Han court willing to defend Li Ling was his friend Sima Qian, a court historian. Emperor Wu had Sima Qian arrested, and he was given a choice: execution or castration.
