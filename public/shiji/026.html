1
昔自在古，歷建正作於孟春。 於時冰泮發蟄，百草奮興，秭鳺先滜。 物乃歲具，生於東，次順四時，卒于冬分。 時雞三號，卒明。 撫十二［月］節，卒于丑。 日月成，故明也。 明者孟也，幽者幼也，幽明者雌雄也。 雌雄代興，而順至正之統也。 日歸于西，起明於東； 月歸於東，起明于西。 正不率天，又不由人，則凡事易壞而難成矣。
In ancient times, the calendar was set to begin in the first month of spring. At that time, the ice melts and the hibernating creatures stir; the hundred grasses flourish, and the cuckoo calls first. Things then complete the year, springing from the east, moving in order through the four seasons, and ending at the winter solstice. When the rooster crows three times, dawn arrives. It runs through the twelve monthly divisions and ends in Chou. When the sun and moon are in order, brightness results. Bright means the elder; dark means the younger. Together, dark and bright mean female and male. Female and male alternate in succession, thus following the right order. The sun returns to the west and rises in the east. The moon returns to the east and rises in the west. If the calendar does not follow heaven or derive from human ordering, then all affairs are easy to ruin and hard to complete.
2
王者易姓受命，必慎始初，改正朔，易服色，推本天元，順承厥意。
When rulers change their surname and receive the mandate, they must be cautious at the beginning, reform the calendar’s starting point, change the colors of dress, trace back to the root of the heavenly origin, and follow its intention.
3
太史公曰：神農以前尚矣。 蓋黃帝考定星歷，建立五行，起消息，正閏餘，於是有天地神祇物類之官，是謂五官。 各司其序，不相亂也。 民是以能有信，神是以能有明德。 民神異業，敬而不瀆，故神降之嘉生，民以物享，災禍不生，所求不匱。
The Grand Historian says: the age before Shennong lies far back in antiquity. The Yellow Emperor examined and fixed the star calendar, established the five phases, initiated the waxing and waning, and corrected intercalary remainders. Thereupon there were officials for heaven, earth, spirits, and things and categories; these are called the five officials. Each administered its own sequence and did not disturb the others. Thus the people were able to trust, and the spirits were able to have bright virtue. People and spirits had different occupations; when respectful and not profane, the spirits bestowed auspicious births, the people enjoyed the things they offered, disasters did not arise, and what they sought was not lacking.
4
少暤氏之衰也，九黎亂德，民神雜擾，不可放物，禍菑薦至，莫盡其氣。 顓頊受之，乃命南正重司天以屬神，命火正黎司地以屬民，使復舊常，無相侵瀆。
When the Shaohao clan declined, the Nine Li brought virtue into disorder. People and spirits were thrown into confusion; things could not be properly released; disasters and calamities came repeatedly, and none could exhaust their qi. Zhuanxu received it and ordered Chong, the Southern Corrector, to govern heaven and be attached to the spirits, and ordered Li, the Fire Corrector, to govern earth and be attached to the people, so that he restored the old constant and there was no mutual invasion or profanation.
5
其後三苗服九黎之德，故二官咸廢所職，而閏餘乖次，孟陬殄滅，攝提無紀，歷數失序。 堯復遂重黎之後，不忘舊者，使復典之，而立羲和之官。 明時正度，則陰陽調，風雨節，茂氣至，民無夭疫。 年耆禪舜，申戒文祖，云「天之歷數在爾躬」。 舜亦以命禹。 由是觀之，王者所重也。
Afterward, the Three Miao followed the virtue of the Nine Li, so the two officials both abandoned their duties, and the intercalary remainder was out of sequence, the first month was extinguished, Sheti was unrecorded, and the calendar numbers lost their order. Yao in turn continued the descendants of Chong and Li, not forgetting the old order, and again made them administer it, establishing the office of Xi and He. When the bright measure is established in due time, yin and yang are harmonized, wind and rain are in season, luxuriant qi arrives, and the people suffer no untimely epidemics. When old in years, he ceded the throne to Shun and again admonished the ancestral line, saying, “The calendar numbers of Heaven are in your person.” Shun likewise passed this command to Yu. From this we see how much kings valued it.
6
夏正以正月，殷正以十二月，周正以十一月。 蓋三王之正若循環，窮則反本。 天下有道，則不失紀序； 無道，則正朔不行於諸侯。
The Xia calendar took the first month as its beginning, the Yin calendar the twelfth month, and the Zhou calendar the eleventh month. Indeed, the three kings’ calendars revolved in a cycle, returning to their root when the cycle was exhausted. When the realm has the Way, it does not lose its sequence and order. Without the Way, the correct calendar does not prevail among the feudal lords.
7
幽、厲之後，周室微，陪臣執政，史不記時，君不告朔，故疇人子弟分散，或在諸夏，或在夷狄，是以其禨祥廢而不統。 周襄王二十六年閏三月，而春秋非之。 先王之正時也，履端於始，舉正於中，歸邪於終。 履端於始，序則不愆； 舉正於中，民則不惑； 歸邪於終，事則不悖。
After the reigns of You and Li, the Zhou house declined; subordinate ministers seized power; historians did not record the seasons; lords did not report the new moon. Thus the descendants of the calendar experts scattered, some among the various Xia states and some among the Yi and Di, so their auspicious observations fell into disuse and lost their system. In the twenty-sixth year of King Xiang of Zhou, there was an intercalary third month, which the Spring and Autumn Annals condemned. The former kings’ regulation of time was to set the beginning at the start, establish the correct point in the middle, and return what was askew at the end. If the beginning is properly set at the start, the sequence does not err; if the correct point is established in the middle, the people are not confused; if what is askew is returned at the end, affairs are not opposed.
8
其後戰國并爭，在於彊國禽敵，救急解紛而已，豈遑念斯哉！ 是時獨有鄒衍，明於五德之傳，而散消息之分，以顯諸侯。 而亦因秦滅六國，兵戎極煩，又升至尊之日淺，未暇遑也。 而亦頗推五勝，而自以為獲水德之瑞，更名河曰「德水」，而正以十月，色上黑。 然歷度閏餘，未能睹其真也。
Afterward the Warring States contended with one another; they were occupied only with strong states capturing enemies and with emergency relief and untangling disputes. How could they have leisure for this? At that time, only Zou Yan clearly understood the transmission of the Five Virtues and explained the divisions of waxing and waning, so as to display them to the feudal lords. And because Qin had destroyed the six states, military affairs were incessant and the days since his rise to supreme power were still few, he had no leisure for it. He also pushed the Five Conquests quite far, taking himself to have obtained the auspicious sign of water virtue, renamed the river “Virtue Water,” adopted the tenth month as the calendar’s beginning, and made black the principal color. Yet in matters of calendrical measures and intercalary remainders, he did not reach the truth.
9
漢興，高祖曰「北畤待我而起」，亦自以為獲水德之瑞。 雖明習歷及張蒼等，咸以為然。 是時天下初定，方綱紀大基，高后女主，皆未遑，故襲秦正朔服色。
When Han rose, Gaozu said, “The northern altar awaited me and rose with me,” and he also took himself to have received the auspicious sign of water virtue. Even those who were skilled in the calendar, including Zhang Cang and others, all agreed. At that time the realm had only just been settled and the great foundation of order was being laid. Empress Gao, as a female ruler, had no leisure for it, so the Qin calendar, new moon, and clothing colors were retained.
10
至孝文時，魯人公孫臣以終始五德上書，言「漢得土德，宜更元，改正朔，易服色。 當有瑞，瑞黃龍見」。 事下丞相張蒼，張蒼亦學律歷，以為非是，罷之。 其後黃龍見成紀，張蒼自黜，所欲論著不成。 而新垣平以望氣見，頗言正歷服色事，貴幸，後作亂，故孝文帝廢不復問。
When it came to Emperor Xiaowen, Lu Gongsun Chen memorialized on the succession of the Five Virtues, saying, “Han has obtained earth virtue. It should change the era, reform the calendar, and alter the clothing colors. There should be an auspicious omen; the auspicious omen would be the appearance of a yellow dragon.” The matter was referred to Chancellor Zhang Cang, who had also studied the pitch-pipes and the calendar and judged it incorrect, so it was dropped. Later, a yellow dragon appeared at Chengji. Zhang Cang was dismissed by himself, and what he wished to set down and discuss did not come to fruition. Xinyuan Ping, who was known through his observations of qi, spoke extensively on reforming the calendar and clothing colors, and he was favored and honored. Later he caused disorder, so Emperor Wen discarded the matter and did not pursue it again.
11
至今上即位，招致方士唐都，分其天部； 而巴落下閎運算轉歷，然後日辰之度與夏正同。 乃改元，更官號，封泰山。 因詔御史曰：「乃者，有司言星度之未定也，廣延宣問，以理星度，未能詹也。 蓋聞昔者黃帝合而不死，名察度驗，定清濁，起五部，建氣物分數。 然蓋尚矣。 書缺樂弛，朕甚閔焉。 朕唯未能循明也，紬績日分，率應水德之勝。 今日順夏至，黃鐘為宮，林鐘為徵，太蔟為商，南呂為羽，姑洗為角。 自是以後，氣復正，羽聲復清，名復正變，以至子日當冬至，則陰陽離合之道行焉。 十一月甲子朔旦冬至已詹，其更以七年為太初元年。 年名『焉逢攝提格』，月名『畢聚』，日得甲子，夜半朔旦冬至。」
When the current emperor ascended the throne, he summoned the fangshi Tang Du and had him apportion the heavenly divisions; and when Luoxia Hong of Ba calculated the revolving calendar, the degrees of the sun and the days then matched the Xia calendar. He then changed the era name, altered the office titles, and sacrificed at Mount Tai. He therefore issued an edict to the Censor, saying: “Before now, the relevant officials said the stellar degrees had not yet been fixed. I widely opened inquiry to consider the stellar degrees, but have not yet fully reached them. I have heard that in antiquity the Yellow Emperor attained unity and did not die; he named, examined, and verified the degrees, fixed the clear and turbid, established the five divisions, and set up the measures and proportions of qi and things. But this is still very remote indeed. The records are incomplete and music has fallen into decay; I am deeply distressed by this. I myself have not yet been able to follow the clearly established path, but have only threaded and carried forward the daily divisions, broadly conforming to the victory of water virtue. Today, in accordance with the summer solstice, Huangzhong is gong, Linzhong is zhi, Taicu is shang, Nanlü is yu, and Guxi is jue. From this point on, the qi again became correct, the yu pitch again became clear, and the names again returned to proper alteration. By the time the zi day coincides with the winter solstice, the way of yin and yang separating and joining is put into practice. By the time the eleventh month, Jiazi, new moon, and winter solstice were all reached, they changed it so that the seventh year became the first year of Taichu. The year was named “Yanfeng Shetige”; the month was named “Biju”; the day was Jiazi; and at midnight came the new moon and winter solstice.

The calendar method of the Jiazi chapter for the first year of Taichu: the year was named “Yanfeng Shetige”; the month was named “Biju”; the day obtained Jiazi; at midnight, the new moon dawn and winter solstice.

右歷書：大餘者，日也。 小餘者，月也。 端（旃）蒙者，年名也。 支：丑名赤奮若，寅名攝提格。 干：丙名游兆。 正北，冬至加子時； 正西，加酉時； 正南，加午時； 正東，加卯時。
The small remainder refers to months. Duan (Zhan) Meng are year names. The earthly branches: Chou is called Chi Fen Ruo, and Yin is called Shetige. The heavenly stems: Bing is called You Zhao. Due north: add the zi hour at the winter solstice; Due west: add the you hour; Due south: add the wu hour; Due east: add the mao hour. Due east: the mao hour is added.
