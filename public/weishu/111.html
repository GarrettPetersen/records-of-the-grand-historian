1
夫在天成象，聖人是觀，日月五星，象之著者，變常舛度，徵咎隨焉。 然則明晦暈蝕，疾餘犯守，飛流欻起，彗孛不恒，或皇靈降臨，示譴以戒下，或王化有虧，感達於天路。 易稱「天垂象，見吉凶」，「觀乎天文，以察時變」； 書曰「曆象日月星辰，敬授民時」。 是故有國有家者之所祗畏也。 百王興廢之驗，萬國禍福之來，兆勤雖微，罔不必至，著於前載，不可得而備舉也。 班史以日暈五星之屬列天文志，薄蝕彗孛之比入五行說。 七曜一也，而分為二志，故陸機云學者所疑也。 今以在天諸異咸入天象，其應徵符合，隨而條載，無所顯驗則闕之云。
Heaven forms images for men to read, and the sage watches them. The sun, moon, and five planets are the clearest of these signs; when their courses stray from the norm, omens of disaster follow in turn. Brightness and dimness, halos and eclipses, swift passages and lingering stations, planetary conjunctions, shooting stars that flare up without warning, and comets that appear at no fixed season—all of this may mean that the imperial spirit has descended to show reproof and warn the realm below, or that the kingly transformation has faltered and Heaven has answered along the celestial roads. The Changes says that Heaven displays images by which fortune and misfortune may be seen, and that one should observe the patterns of Heaven to discern how the times are changing; the Documents says that one must calculate and chart the sun, moon, stars, and constellations and respectfully hand down the seasons to the people. That is why every lord of state and house holds these matters in reverent awe. The proof of how the hundred kings rose and fell, and how fortune and disaster came to the myriad states, shows that even the slightest omen, however faint, was sure to be fulfilled in the end. Earlier records preserve these events, though they cannot all be listed here. In Ban Gu's history, solar halos and the five planets were set out in the Treatise on Astronomy, whereas eclipses, comets, and related phenomena were folded into the discussion of the Five Phases. The seven luminaries are really one subject, yet they were split across two treatises, which is why Lu Ji said scholars have long found this arrangement doubtful. Here all anomalies in the heavens are gathered into the Treatise on Celestial Phenomena. Where a corresponding event can be confirmed, it is recorded in sequence; where no clear verification exists, the entry is left blank.

太祖天興五年八月，天鳴。
In the eighth month of Tianxing 5, under Emperor Daozu, the sky roared.

六年九月，天鳴。
In the ninth month of the sixth year, the sky roared again.
4
皇始二年十月壬辰，日暈，有佩璚。 占曰「兵起」。 天興元年九月，烏丸張超收合亡命，聚黨三千餘家，據勃海之南皮，自號征東大將軍、烏丸王，鈔掠諸郡。 詔將軍庾岳討之。
On renchen in the tenth month of Huangshi 2, the sun was ringed by a halo with pendant rings. The prognostication read: "Warfare will arise." In the ninth month of Tianxing 1, the Wuhuan leader Zhang Chao rallied fugitives into a force of more than three thousand households, seized Nanpi in Bohai, proclaimed himself Grand General Who Conquers the East and King of the Wuhuan, and raided the surrounding commanderies. The court ordered General Yu Yue to campaign against him.
5
天興三年六月庚辰朔，日有蝕之。 占曰「外國侵，土地分」。 五年五月，姚興遣其弟義陽公平率眾四萬來侵平陽，乾壁為平所陷。
On the gengchen new moon in the sixth month of Tianxing 3, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "Foreign powers will invade, and territory will be divided." In the fifth month of the fifth year, Yao Xing sent his brother Yao Ping, Prince of Yiyang, at the head of forty thousand men to invade Pingyang, and Ganbi fell to Ping.
6
六年四月癸巳朔，日有蝕之。 占曰「兵稍出」。 十月，太祖詔將軍伊謂率騎二萬北襲高車，大破之。
On the guisi new moon in the fourth month of the sixth year, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "Armies will gradually take the field." In the tenth month, Emperor Daozu ordered General Yi Wei to lead twenty thousand cavalry in a northern raid against the Gaoche and routed them decisively.

On the wuxu new moon in the seventh month of Tianci 5, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "The empress will die." In the seventh month of the sixth year, Lady Liu died and was later posthumously titled Empress Xuanmu.

On the guimao new moon in the sixth month of Shiguang 4, under Emperor Taiwu, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "The feudal lords are not the right men for their posts." In the second month of Shenlu 1, Minister of Works Xi Jin and Supervisory Army Attendant Censor An Ji campaigned against Helian Chang and captured him at Anding. The survivors enthroned Chang's younger brother Ding and fled to Pingliang. Jin pursued them and was captured by Ding. General Qiu Dui abandoned his armor and fled east to Puban with the defending commander, Prince of Gaoliang Tuoba Li. Emperor Taiwu was furious and had Dui executed.

On the yisi new moon in the ninth month, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "The eastern regions will raise armies." On dingwei in the tenth month of the fourth year, Huan Fu of Lanling killed his county magistrate. Together with Huan He of Changlu and the Mount Tai bandits Zhang He, Yan, and others, he gathered a force, held Wugu, and set up Sima Langzhi as their leader. The court ordered Prince of Huaiyang Yu Yuan and others to campaign against them.
39
三年春正月癸丑，日暈，東西有珥，有佩戟一重，北有偃戟四重，後有白氣貫日珥，狀如車輪。 京師不見，雍州以聞。
On guichou in the first month of spring in the third year, the sun was ringed by a halo with luminous appendages to the east and west, one layer of pendant halberds, four layers of reclining halberds to the north, and then white vapor pierced the halo and appendages in the shape of a carriage wheel. The capital did not see it; Yong Province reported the phenomenon.

五年正月庚辰，日暈，東西有珥； 南北並白氣，長一丈，廣二尺許； 北有連環暈。 又貫珥內，復有直氣，長三丈許，內黃，中青，外白。 暈乍成，散，乃滅。
On gengchen in the first month of the fifth year, the sun was ringed by a halo with luminous appendages to the east and west; to the north and south white vapors appeared as well, one zhang long and about two chi wide; to the north there was a chain of linked halos. It also pierced within the appendages, and a straight vapor about three zhang long appeared, yellow within, green in the middle, and white without. The halo formed suddenly, then dispersed and vanished.

74
延昌元年二月甲戌至于辛巳，日初出及將沒，赤白無光明。
From jiaxu through xinsi in the second month of Yanchang 1, at sunrise and sunset the sun appeared red and white without brightness.
75
五月己未晦，日十五分蝕九。 占曰「大旱，民流千里」。 二年春，京師民饑，死者數萬口。
On the last day of the fifth month, the sun was eclipsed by nine-fifteenths of its disk. The prognostication read: "Great drought; the people will flee a thousand li." In the spring of the second year, famine struck the capital, and tens of thousands died.
76
二年閏月辛亥，日中有黑氣。 占曰「內有逆謀」。 三年十一月丁巳，幽州沙門劉僧紹聚眾反，自號淨居國明法王，州郡捕斬之。
On xinhai in the intercalary month of the second year, black vapor appeared within the sun. The prognostication read: "Treason is plotting within." On dingsi in the eleventh month of the third year, the monk Liu Sengshao of You Province rallied rebels, proclaimed himself Bright Dharma King of the Pure Dwelling State, and was captured and beheaded by local authorities.
77
五月甲寅朔，日有蝕之，京師不見，恒州以聞。
On the jiayin new moon in the fifth month, a solar eclipse occurred. The capital did not see it; Heng Province reported the phenomenon.
78
三年三月庚申，日交暈，其色內赤黃，外青白； 南北有佩，可長二丈許，內赤黃，外青白； 西有白暈貫日。 又日東有一抱，長二丈許，內赤黃，外青。
On gengshen in the third month of the third year, intersecting solar halos appeared, red-yellow within and green-white without; to the north and south pendant halos about two zhang long appeared, red-yellow within and green-white without; to the west a white halo pierced the sun. East of the sun an embracing halo about two zhang long also appeared, red-yellow within and green without.
79
四月甲辰卯時，日暈帀； 西有一背，內赤外黃； 南北有珥，內赤外黃； 漸滅。
At the mao hour on jiachen in the fourth month, a solar halo completed its ring; to the west a contrary halo appeared, red within and yellow without; to the north and south luminous appendages appeared, red within and yellow without; then gradually faded away.
80
十二月己酉，日暈，北有一抱，內赤外白，兩傍有珥，北有白虹貫日。
On jiyou in the twelfth month, the sun was ringed by a halo. To the north an embracing halo appeared, red within and white without, with luminous appendages on both sides, and a white rainbow pierced the sun from the north.
81
神龜元年三月丁丑，白虹貫日。 占曰「天下有來臣之眾，不三年」。 十一月乙酉，[5]蠕蠕莫緣梁賀侯豆率男女七百口來降。
On dingchou in the third month of Shengui 1, a white rainbow pierced the sun. The prognostication read: "Subjects will come in from across the realm, within three years." On yiyou in the eleventh month, [5] Moyuanliang Hehoudou of the Rouran led seven hundred men and women in submission.
82
二年正月辛巳朔，日有蝕之。
On the xinsi new moon in the first month of the second year, a solar eclipse occurred.
83
正光元年正月乙亥朔，日有蝕之。 占曰「有大臣亡」。 七月丙子，殺太傅、領太尉、清河王懌。
On the yihai new moon in the first month of Zhengguang 1, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "A great minister will perish." On bingzi in the seventh month, Yuan Yi, Prince of Qinghe, Grand Tutor and concurrent Grand Commandant, was executed.
84
二年五月丁酉，日有蝕之，夏州以聞。
On dingyou in the fifth month of the second year, a solar eclipse occurred. Xia Province reported the phenomenon.
85
三年正月甲寅，日交暈，內赤外青，有白虹貫暈； 外有直氣，長二丈許，內赤外青。
On jiayin in the first month of the third year, intersecting solar halos appeared, red within and green without, and a white rainbow pierced the halo; outside was a straight vapor about two zhang long, red within and green without.
86
五月壬辰朔，日有蝕之。 占曰「秦邦不臣」。 五年六月，秦州城人莫折大提據城反，自稱秦王。
On the renchen new moon in the fifth month, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "The Qin region will rebel." In the sixth month of the fifth year, Mozhe Dati of Qinzhou seized the city in rebellion and proclaimed himself King of Qin.
87
十月己巳，太史奏自八月已來，黃埃掩日，日出三丈，色赤如赭，無光曜。
On jisi in the tenth month, the Grand Astrologer reported that since the eighth month yellow dust had veiled the sun; when it rose three zhang high, it was red as ochre and without radiance.
88
十一月己丑朔，日有蝕之。 占曰「有小兵，在西北」。 四年二月己卯，蠕蠕主阿那瓌率眾犯塞。
On the jichou new moon in the eleventh month, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "Minor warfare will arise in the northwest." On jimao in the second month of the fourth year, the Rouran ruler Anagui led his forces in an attack on the frontier.
89
四年十一月癸未朔，日有蝕之。
On the guiwei new moon in the eleventh month of the fourth year, a solar eclipse occurred.
90
五年閏月乙酉，日暈，內赤外青； 南有珥，上有一抱兩背，內赤外青。
On yiyou in the intercalary month of the fifth year, the sun was ringed by a halo, red within and green without; to the south luminous appendages appeared; above were one embracing halo and two contrary halos, red within and green without.
91
十二月丙申，日暈，南北有珥，上有兩抱一背。
On bingshen in the twelfth month, the sun was ringed by a halo with luminous appendages to the north and south; above were two embracing halos and one contrary halo.
92
孝昌元年十二月丙戌，白虹刺日不過，虹中有一背。 占曰「有臣背其主」，一曰「有反城」。 三年九月己卯，[6]東豫州刺史元慶和據城南叛。
On bingxu in the twelfth month of Xiaochang 1, a white rainbow struck at the sun but did not pass through; within the rainbow was one contrary halo. The prognostication read: "A minister will turn against his lord," or alternatively, "A city will rebel." On jimao in the ninth month of the third year, [6] Yuan Qinghe, governor of Eastern Yuzhou, seized the city and rebelled southward.
93
三年十一月戊寅辰時，日暈，東面不合，其色內赤外黃； 東西有珥，內赤外黃； 西北去暈一尺餘，有一背，長二丈餘，廣三尺許，內赤外黃。
At the chen hour on wuyin in the eleventh month of the third year, a solar halo appeared, incomplete on the eastern side, red within and yellow without; to the east and west luminous appendages appeared, red within and yellow without; more than one chi northwest of the halo, a contrary halo more than two zhang long and about three chi wide appeared, red within and yellow without.
94
莊帝永安二年三月甲戌未時，日暈三重，內黃赤，外青白，暈東西兩處不合，其狀如抱。
At the wei hour on jiaxu in the third month of Yong'an 2, under Emperor Xiaozhuang, a triple solar halo appeared, yellow-red within and green-white without; the halo was incomplete in two places to the east and west, shaped like an embrace.
95
五月辛酉，日暈，東西兩處不合。 辛未申時，日南有珥； 去一尺餘有一背，長三丈許，廣五尺餘，內赤外青。
On xinyou in the fifth month, the sun was ringed by a halo incomplete in two places to the east and west. At the shen hour on xinwei, luminous appendages appeared to the south of the sun; more than one chi away a contrary halo about three zhang long and more than five chi wide appeared, red within and green without.
96
七月丙寅，直東去日三尺許有一背，長二丈餘，內赤外青。 半食頃，從北頭漸滅至半，須臾還如初見，內赤外青，其色分炳。
On bingyin in the seventh month, due east about three chi from the sun, a contrary halo more than two zhang long appeared, red within and green without. After half a meal's time it faded from the northern end to the midpoint; in a moment it returned as at first, red within and green without, its colors clearly distinct.
97
十月己酉朔，日從地下蝕出，十五分蝕七，虧從西南角起。 占曰「西夷欲殺，後有大兵，必西行」。 三年四月丁卯，雍州刺史尒朱天光討擒万俟醜奴、蕭寶夤於安定，送京師斬之。
On the jiyou new moon in the tenth month, the sun emerged from eclipse below the horizon, eclipsed by seven-fifteenths of its disk, with the shadow beginning from the southwestern corner. The prognostication read: "Western barbarians seek bloodshed; afterward great armies will march, and they will surely advance west." On dingmao in the fourth month of the third year, Erzhu Tianguang, governor of Yong Province, captured Mozhe Chounu and Xiao Baoyin at Anding, sent them to the capital, and had them beheaded.
98
三年五月戊戌辰時，日暈帀，內赤外白，暈內有兩珥； 西有白虹貫日； 東北有一背，內赤外青； 南有一背，內赤外青； 東有一抱，內青外赤。 京師不見，青州表聞。 六月辛丑，日暈，白虹貫日。
At the chen hour on wuxu in the fifth month of the third year, a solar halo completed its ring, red within and white without, with two luminous appendages within the halo; to the west a white rainbow pierced the sun; to the northeast one contrary halo appeared, red within and green without; to the south one contrary halo appeared, red within and green without; to the east one embracing halo appeared, green within and red without. The capital did not see it; Qing Province reported the phenomenon by memorial. On xinchou in the sixth month, the sun was ringed by a halo, and a white rainbow pierced it.
99
前廢帝普泰元年三月丁亥，日月並赤赭色，天地溷濁。
On dinghai in the third month of Putai 1, under the Deposed Emperor Jiemin, the sun and moon both turned red-ochre, and heaven and earth grew murky and dim.
100
六月己亥朔，日蝕從西南角起，雲陰不見，定相二州表聞。 占曰「主弱，小人持政」。 時尒朱世隆兄弟專擅威福。
On the jihai new moon in the sixth month, the eclipse began from the southwestern corner. Clouds hid it from view; Ding and Xiang Provinces reported the phenomenon by memorial. The prognostication read: "The ruler is weak, and petty men hold power." At that time Erzhu Shilong and his brothers monopolized power and favor.
101
後廢帝中興二年二月辛丑辰時，日暈，東西不合，其色內赤外青； 南北有珥； 西北去暈一尺餘有一背，長二丈許，可廣三尺，內赤外青。
At the chen hour on xinchou in the second month of Zhongxing 2, under the Deposed Emperor Xiaojing, a solar halo appeared, incomplete to the east and west, red within and green without; to the north and south luminous appendages appeared; more than one chi northwest of the halo, a contrary halo about two zhang long and about three chi wide appeared, red within and green without.
102
十一月，日暈再重； 上有背，長三丈餘，內青外赤。
In the eleventh month, a double solar halo appeared; above it a contrary halo more than three zhang long appeared, green within and red without.
103
出帝太昌元年五月，日暈再重； 上有兩背，一尺許。 癸丑午時，日南有珥； 去日一尺餘有一背，長三丈許，廣五尺，內赤外青。
In the fifth month of Taichang 1, under Emperor Xiaowu, a double solar halo appeared; above were two contrary halos about one chi in size. At the wu hour on guichou, luminous appendages appeared to the south of the sun; more than one chi from the sun, a contrary halo about three zhang long and five chi wide appeared, red within and green without.
104
十月辛酉朔，日從地下蝕出，虧從西南角起。 占曰「有兵大行」。 永熙二年正月甲午，齊獻武王自晉陽出討尒朱兆。 丁酉，大破之於赤洪嶺，兆遁走自殺。
On the xinyou new moon in the tenth month, the sun emerged from eclipse below the horizon, with the shadow beginning from the southwestern corner. The prognostication read: "Armies will march on a great scale." On jiawu in the first month of Yongxi 2, Prince Xianwu of Qi marched out from Jinyang to campaign against Erzhu Zhao. On dingyou he routed Zhao decisively at Chihong Ridge. Zhao fled and took his own life.
105
永熙二年四月己未朔，日有蝕之，在丙，虧從正南起。 占曰「君陰謀」。 三年五月辛卯，出帝為斛斯椿等諸佞關構，猜於齊獻武王，託討蕭衍，盛暑徵發河南諸州之兵，天下怪惡之。 語在斛斯椿傳。
On the jiwei new moon in the fourth month of Yongxi 2, a solar eclipse occurred at the bing position, with the shadow beginning from due south. The prognostication read: "The ruler plots in secret." On xinmao in the fifth month of the third year, flatterers led by Husu Chun turned Emperor Xiaowu against Prince Xianwu of Qi. He used a campaign against Xiao Yan as a pretext and, in the height of summer, mobilized the armies of the Henan commanderies. The realm regarded this with horror and loathing. The full account appears in the biography of Husu Chun.
106
三年四月癸丑，日有蝕之。 占曰「有亂殺天子者」。 七月丁未，出帝為斛斯椿等迫脅，遂出於長安。
On guichou in the fourth month of the third year, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "Rebellion will arise, and the Son of Heaven will be killed." On dingwei in the seventh month, Husu Chun and his faction forced Emperor Xiaowu to flee to Chang'an.
107
二年二月己丑巳時，日暈帀，白虹貫日不徹。
At the si hour on jichou in the second month of the second year, a solar halo completed its ring, and a white rainbow pierced the sun but did not pass through.
108
興和二年閏月丁丑朔，日有蝕之。 占曰「有小兵」。 七月癸巳，元寶炬廣豫二州行臺趙繼宗、南青州刺史崔康寇陽翟，鎮將擊走之。
On the dingchou new moon in the intercalary month of Xinghe 2, a solar eclipse occurred. The prognostication read: "Minor warfare will arise." On guisi in the seventh month, Zhao Jizong of the Guang-Yu garrison under Yuan Baoju and Cui Kang, governor of Southern Qing, raided Yangdi. The garrison commander drove them off.
109
武定三年冬十一月壬申，日暈兩重，東南角不合； 西南、東北有珥； 西北有兩重背； 東北、西北有白氣，并有兩珥； 中間有一白氣，東西撗至珥。
On renshen in the eleventh month of winter in Wuding 3, a double solar halo appeared, incomplete at the southeastern corner; to the southwest and northeast luminous appendages appeared; to the northwest two layers of contrary halos appeared; to the northeast and northwest white vapors appeared, with two luminous appendages as well; in the middle one white vapor stretched east and west to the appendages.
110
十二月乙酉，竟天微有白雲，日暈，東南角不合； 西南、東北有珥； 西北有一背，去日一尺。
On yiyou in the twelfth month, faint white clouds covered the sky. A solar halo appeared, incomplete at the southeastern corner; to the southwest and northeast luminous appendages appeared; to the northwest one contrary halo appeared one chi from the sun.
111
三月辛丑，日暈帀，西北交暈貫日，并有一珥一抱。
On xinchou in the third month, a solar halo completed its ring. Intersecting halos to the northwest pierced the sun, with one luminous appendage and one embracing halo as well.
112
六年七月庚寅朔，日有蝕之，虧從西北角起。
On the gengyin new moon in the seventh month of the sixth year, a solar eclipse occurred, with the shadow beginning from the northwestern corner.

Collation Notes
114
高祖延興元年十二月癸卯日有蝕之按是年十二月乙酋朔，癸卯是十九日，不應有日蝕。
The entry for a solar eclipse on guimao in the twelfth month of Yanxing 1 is doubtful. That month began on yiyou; guimao was the nineteenth day, when no solar eclipse should have occurred.
115
三年十二月癸卯朔日有蝕之四年正月癸酋朔日有蝕之按無連續兩月日蝕之理，必有誤。
Entries for solar eclipses on the guimao new moon in the twelfth month of the third year and the guiyou new moon in the first month of the fourth year cannot both be correct, since two eclipses in successive months are impossible. The dates must be wrong.
116
二月乙酋晦日有蝕之按太和二年二月己卯朔，乙酋乃七日，晦日值丁未。 必有誤。
The entry for a solar eclipse on the last day of the second month, yiyou, is doubtful. In Taihe 2 the second month began on jimao; yiyou was only the seventh day, and the last day of the month was dingwei. The date must be wrong.
117
延昌二年二月庚辰諸本「二月」作「正月」。 按卷八世宗紀，事在是年二月庚辰。 是年正月丙戊朔，無庚辰，二月丙辰朔，庚辰乃二十五日。 這裏「正月」乃「二月」之訛。 今據紀改。
Yanchang 2, second month, gengchen: various editions read "first month" for "second month." The Basic Annals of Emperor Xuanwu in fascicle 8 place the event on gengchen in the second month of that year. That year's first month began on bingxu, so there was no gengchen in the first month. The second month began on bingchen, and gengchen was its twenty-fifth day. Here "first month" is a scribal error for "second month." The text is corrected here according to the annals.
118
十一月乙酉按卷九肅宗紀，事在神龜二年十一月乙酋。 這裏「十一月」上脫「二年」二字。
Eleventh month, yiyou: the Basic Annals of Emperor Xiaozong in fascicle 9 place the event in the eleventh month of Shengui 2, on yiyou. Here the words "second year" are missing before "eleventh month."
119
三年九月己卯諸本「三年」作「二年」。 按卷九肅宗紀，事在孝昌三年九月辛卯，梁書卷五武帝紀三，在梁大通元年 〈即魏孝昌三年〉 十月。 記月不同，當是各據奏報，至元慶和降梁，必在孝昌三年無疑。 這裏「二」字乃「三」之訛。 今據紀改。 是年九月有「己卯」，無「辛卯」，則是紀誤。
Third year, ninth month, jimao: various editions read "second year" for "third year." The Basic Annals of Emperor Xiaozong in fascicle 9 place the event in the ninth month of Xiaochang 3, on xinmao, while the Basic Annals of Emperor Wu in fascicle 5 of the History of Liang place it in Liang Datong 1 (that is, Wei Xiaochang 3) in the tenth month. The months differ because each source relied on its own memorial report. Yuan Qinghe's surrender to Liang must in any case have occurred in Xiaochang 3. Here "two" is a scribal error for "three." The text is corrected here according to the annals. That year's ninth month had a jimao day but no xinmao day, so the annals are wrong.
