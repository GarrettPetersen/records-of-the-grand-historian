1
高句麗百濟勿吉失韋豆莫婁地豆于庫莫奚契丹烏洛侯
Goguryeo, Baekje, Wuji, Shiwei, Doumolou, Didouyu, Kumo Xi, Khitan, and Wuluohou
2
高句麗者，出於夫餘，自言先祖朱蒙。 朱蒙母河伯女，為夫餘王閉於室中，為日所照，引身避之，日影又逐。 既而有孕，生一卵，大如五升。 夫餘王棄之與犬，犬不食； 棄之與豕，豕又不食； 棄之於路，牛馬避之； 後棄之野，眾鳥以毛茹之。 夫餘王割剖之，不能破，遂還其母。 其母以物裹之，置於暖處，有一男破殼而出。 及其長也，字之曰朱蒙，其俗言「朱蒙」者，善射也。 夫餘人以朱蒙非人所生，將有異志，請除之，王不聽，命之養馬。 朱蒙每私試，知有善惡，駿者減食令瘦，駑者善養令肥。 夫餘王以肥者自乘，以瘦者給朱蒙。 後狩于田，以朱蒙善射，限之一矢。 朱蒙雖矢少，殪獸甚多。 夫餘之臣又謀殺之。 朱蒙母陰知，告朱蒙曰：「國將害汝，以汝才略，宜遠適四方。 」朱蒙乃與烏引、烏違等二人，棄夫餘，東南走。 中道遇一大水，欲濟無梁，夫餘人追之甚急。 朱蒙告水曰：「我是日子，河伯外孫，今日逃走，追兵垂及，如何得濟？ 」於是魚鼈並浮，為之成橋，朱蒙得渡，魚鼈乃解，追騎不得渡。 朱蒙遂至普述水，遇見三人，其一人著麻衣，一人著納衣，一人著水藻衣，與朱蒙至紇升骨城，遂居焉，號曰高句麗，因以為氏焉。
Goguryeo arose from Buyeo; its people claim Jumong as their founding ancestor. Jumong's mother was a daughter of the River Lord. The king of Buyeo had her shut in a chamber; when sunlight fell upon her, she shrank away to avoid it, yet the sun's shadow followed her still. She soon conceived and laid an egg the size of a five-sheng measure. The king of Buyeo threw it to the dogs, but they would not touch it. He gave it to the pigs, and they too refused to eat it. He left it on the road, and cattle and horses turned aside from it. Finally he abandoned it in the open country, where flocks of birds covered it with their down to warm it. The king of Buyeo tried to cut the egg open but could not break the shell, and so gave it back to the mother. She wrapped the egg in cloth and set it in a warm place, until a boy broke through the shell and was born. When he grew up they styled him Jumong, which in their tongue means 'skilled archer.' The Buyeo people, believing Jumong was not human-born and would prove disloyal, urged the king to kill him; the king refused and set him to tend the royal horses. Jumong secretly tested each horse to learn its quality: he cut the rations of the swift ones until they grew lean, and he fed the slow ones generously until they grew fat. The king of Buyeo kept the fat horses for himself and gave Jumong only the lean ones. Later, on a hunt, they allowed Jumong only a single arrow because of his renown as an archer. With that one arrow Jumong nevertheless brought down a great number of game. The ministers of Buyeo again conspired to have him killed. Jumong's mother learned of the plot in secret and warned him: 'The realm means to destroy you. With your gifts, you should flee far beyond its borders.' Jumong then left Buyeo with Wuyin, Wuwei, and two companions and fled toward the southeast. On the way they reached a great river with no bridge to cross it, while the Buyeo pursuers closed in fast behind them. Jumong called to the river: 'I am a son of the sun, grandson of the River Lord. I flee for my life and the pursuers are almost upon me—how can I cross?' At once fish and turtles rose to the surface and formed a bridge. Jumong crossed safely; when the creatures dispersed, the pursuers could not follow. Jumong came to the Pusu River and met three men, one in hemp, one in coarse cloth, and one in garments of waterweed. Together they went to Kesunggol City and made it their seat. They took the name Goguryeo, which became their royal clan.
3
初，朱蒙在夫餘時，妻懷孕，朱蒙逃後生一子，字始閭諧。 及長，知朱蒙為國主，即與母亡而歸之，名之曰閭達，委之國事。 朱蒙死，閭達代立。 閭達死，子如栗代立。 如栗死，子莫來代立，乃征夫餘，夫餘大敗，遂統屬焉。 莫來子孫相傳，至裔孫宮，生而開目能視，國人惡之。 及長凶虐，國以殘破。 宮曾孫位宮亦生而視，人以其似曾祖宮，故名為位宮，高句麗呼相似為「位」。 位宮亦有勇力，便弓馬。 魏正始中，入寇遼西安平，[1]為幽州刺史毋丘儉所破。 其玄孫乙弗利，利子釗，烈帝時與慕容氏相攻擊。 建國四年，慕容元真率眾伐之，入自南陝，戰於木底，大破釗軍，乘勝長驅，遂入丸都，釗單馬奔竄。 元真掘釗父墓，載其屍，并掠其母妻、珍寶、男女五萬餘口，焚其宮室，毀丸都城而還。 自後釗遣使來朝，阻隔寇讎，不能自達。 釗後為百濟所殺。
While Jumong was still in Buyeo his wife had been pregnant; after he fled she gave birth to a son styled Shilüxie. When the boy grew up and learned that Jumong had become king, he fled with his mother to join him. Jumong named him Lüda and placed the affairs of state in his hands. After Jumong's death, Lüda succeeded to the throne. When Lüda died, his son Yuli took the throne. When Yuli died, his son Molai became king, marched against Buyeo, routed it utterly, and brought the kingdom under Goguryeo's dominion. Molai's line passed from heir to heir until a distant descendant, Gong, was born with his eyes already open. The people regarded this as an evil omen. As he grew to manhood he proved cruel and tyrannical, and the kingdom fell into ruin. Gong's great-great-grandson Weigong was likewise born with his eyes open. Because he resembled his ancestor Gong, he was named Weigong—the Goguryeo word wei meaning 'likeness.' Weigong too was brave and strong and excelled at archery and horsemanship. During the Wei reign-period Zhengshi he invaded Anping in western Liaodong and was defeated by Guanqiu Jian, Inspector of Youzhou. His descendant in the fifth generation was Yifuli; Li was Yifuli's son and Chao was Li's son. In the reign of Emperor Lie, Chao waged war against the Murong. In the fourth year of Jian'guo, Murong Yuanzhen marched against him by the southern pass, defeated Chao's army at Mudi, drove deep into Goguryeo territory, and took Wandu while Chao fled alone on horseback. Yuanzhen opened the tomb of Chao's father and carried off the corpse, together with Chao's mother and wife, the royal treasures, and more than fifty thousand captives. He burned the palaces, razed the walls of Wandu, and withdrew. Afterward Chao sent tribute missions to the court, but enemies along the route barred the way and none could arrive. Chao was later killed by Baekje.
4
世祖時，釗曾孫璉始遣使者安東奉表貢方物，并請國諱。 世祖嘉其誠款，詔下帝系名諱於其國，遣員外散騎侍郎李敖拜璉為都督遼海諸軍事、征東將軍、領護東夷中郎將、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王。 敖至其所居平壤城，訪其方事，云：遼東南一千餘里，東至柵城，南至小海，北至舊夫餘，民戶參倍於前。 魏時，其地東西二千里，南北一千餘里。 民皆土著，隨山谷而居，衣布帛及皮。 土田薄塉，蠶農不足以自供，故其人節飲食。 其俗淫，好歌舞，夜則男女羣聚而戲，無貴賤之節，然潔淨自喜。 其王好治宮室。 其官名有謁奢、太奢、大兄、小兄之號。 頭著折風，其形如弁，旁插鳥羽，貴賤有差。 立則反拱，跪拜曳一脚，行步如走。 常以十月祭天，國中大會。 其公會，衣服皆錦繡，金銀以為飾。 好蹲踞。 食用俎几。 出三尺馬，云本朱蒙所乘，馬種即果下也。 後貢使相尋，歲致黃金二百斤，白銀四百斤。
Under Emperor Shizu, Chao's descendant Lian in the fifth generation first sent the envoy Andong with a memorial, local products as tribute, and a request for the imperial taboo names. Emperor Shizu praised his sincerity, sent down the imperial genealogy and taboo names to his kingdom, and dispatched Li Ao to invest Lian as commander-in-chief of all military affairs in Liaohai, General Who Conquers the East, Protector of the Eastern Yi, Duke of Liaodong with a state-founding fief, and King of Goguryeo. When Li Ao reached Lian's capital at Pyongyang and inquired into the realm, he was told that its lands stretched more than a thousand li south of Liaodong, east to Seong Fortress, south to the Lesser Sea, and north to old Buyeo, with registered households now more than triple their former number. In Wei times its domain measured two thousand li from east to west and more than a thousand li from north to south. The people were sedentary, living along valleys and hillsides, and dressed in cloth, silk, and furs. The land was thin and unproductive, and farming and sericulture could not fully sustain them, so the people were frugal in their eating and drinking. Their customs were licentious and they loved song and dance; at night men and women gathered without regard to rank, yet they prided themselves on cleanliness. Their kings delighted in constructing palaces. Their official titles included Yeshe, Taishe, Elder Brother, and Younger Brother. They wore on the head a zhefeng cap shaped like a formal bonnet, with bird feathers at the sides in ranks that marked noble and common status. When standing they clasped their hands behind their backs; when bowing they dragged one foot; and they walked with a quick, almost running step. Each year in the tenth month they sacrificed to Heaven in a great national assembly. At these assemblies the nobles wore brocade and embroidery adorned with gold and silver. They were fond of squatting. They ate from stands and low tables. They bred horses only three feet tall, said to be the same breed Jumong once rode—the guoxia pony. Thereafter tribute missions came in steady succession, each year bringing two hundred jin of gold and four hundred jin of silver.
5
時馮文通率眾奔之，世祖遣散騎常侍封撥詔璉令送文通，璉上書稱當與文通俱奉王化，竟不送。 世祖怒，欲往討之，樂平王丕等議待後舉，世祖乃止，而文通亦尋為璉所殺。
At that time Feng Wentong fled to Goguryeo with his followers. Emperor Shizu sent Feng Bo with an edict ordering Lian to extradite him. Lian replied that he and Wentong would together accept imperial rule, but in the end he refused to hand him over. Emperor Shizu was enraged and prepared to march against him, but the Prince of Leping, Pi, and others urged delay, and the emperor held his hand. Before long Lian had Wentong killed as well.
6
後文明太后以顯祖六宮未備，敕璉令薦其女。 璉奉表，云女已出嫁，求以弟女應旨，朝廷許焉，乃遣安樂王真、尚書李敷等至境送幣。 璉惑其左右之說，云朝廷昔與馮氏婚姻，未幾而滅其國，殷鑒不遠，宜以方便辭之。 璉遂上書妄稱女死。 朝廷疑其矯詐，又遣假散騎常侍程駿切責之，若女審死者，聽更選宗淑。 璉云：「若天子恕其前愆，謹當奉詔。 」會顯祖崩，乃止。
Later, because Emperor Xianzu's inner palaces were not yet filled, Empress Dowager Wenming ordered Lian to present his daughter. Lian memorialized that his daughter was already married and asked that his younger brother's daughter be accepted instead. The court agreed and sent the Prince of Anle, Zhen, Minister Li Fu, and others to the border with betrothal gifts. Lian was swayed by his advisers, who reminded him that when the court had once married a woman of the Feng clan, it had soon destroyed their kingdom—a warning too recent to ignore—and urged him to find some polite excuse. Lian then sent a false memorial claiming the girl had died. The court suspected fraud and sent Cheng Jun, Acting Regular Attendant Cavalier, to rebuke him sternly, adding that if the girl were truly dead he might choose another suitable woman of his clan. Lian replied, 'If the Son of Heaven will forgive my earlier offense, I shall respectfully obey.' But before anything further could be done, Emperor Xianzu died, and the matter was dropped.
7
至高祖時，璉貢獻倍前，其報賜亦稍加焉。 時光州於海中得璉所遣詣蕭道成使餘奴等送闕，高祖詔責璉曰：「道成親殺其君，竊號江左，朕方欲興滅國於舊邦，繼絕世於劉氏，而卿越境外交，遠通篡賊，豈是藩臣守節之義！ 今不以一過掩卿舊款，即送還藩，其感恕思愆，祗承明憲，輯寧所部，動靜以聞。」
Under Emperor Gaozu, Lian's tribute doubled, and the court's rewards in return grew somewhat as well. Guangzhou then captured at sea Lian's envoys to Xiao Daocheng, including Yu Nu, and sent them to court. Emperor Gaozu rebuked Lian: 'Daocheng murdered his own sovereign and seized the title south of the Yangzi. We mean to restore a fallen kingdom and continue the Liu line, yet you send envoys across your border to treat with a distant usurper—is this how a loyal frontier vassal should behave?' We will not let this single fault efface your long record of loyalty. Return to your domain at once, reflect on our mercy, accept the imperial statutes, keep your lands in order, and report all you do.'
8
太和十五年，璉死，年百餘歲。 高祖舉哀於東郊，遣謁者僕射李安上策贈車騎大將軍、太傅、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王，諡曰康。 又遣大鴻臚拜璉孫雲使持節、都督遼海諸軍事、征東將軍、領護東夷中郎將、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王，賜衣冠服物車旗之飾，又詔雲遣世子入朝，令及郊丘之禮。 雲上書辭疾，惟遣其從叔升于隨使詣闕，嚴責之。 自此歲常貢獻。 正始中，世宗於東堂引見其使芮悉弗，[2]悉弗進曰：「高麗係誠天極，累葉純誠，地產土毛，無愆王貢。 但黃金出自夫餘，珂則涉羅所產。 今夫餘為勿吉所逐，涉羅為百濟所并，國王臣雲惟繼絕之義，悉遷于境內。 二品所以不登王府，實兩賊是為。 」世宗曰：「高麗世荷上將，專制海外，九夷黠虜，實得征之。 瓶罄罍耻，誰之咎也？ 昔方貢之愆，責在連率。 卿宜宣朕旨於卿主，務盡威懷之略，揃披害羣，輯寧東裔，使二邑還復舊墟，土毛無失常貢也。」
In the fifteenth year of Taihe, Lian died at the age of more than a hundred. Emperor Gaozu mourned him at the eastern suburb and sent Li An with a posthumous patent investing him as General of Chariots and Cavalry, Grand Tutor, Duke of Liaodong, and King of Goguryeo, with the posthumous title Kang. He also sent the Grand Herald to invest Lian's grandson Yun with the same titles and to grant him robes, regalia, chariots, and banners. Yun was further ordered to send his heir to court in time for the suburban sacrifices. Yun pleaded illness in a memorial and sent only his cousin Shengyu with the embassy. The court rebuked him sharply. From then on tribute was sent every year without fail. During Zhengshi, Emperor Shizong received the envoy Ruixifu in the Eastern Hall. Ruixifu said, 'Goguryeo has bound its loyalty to Heaven through generations of sincere devotion. The land yields its native products, and we have never failed in tribute.' But our gold came from Buyeo, and our ke-shells from Shero. Buyeo has now been driven out by Wuji and Shero annexed by Baekje. Our king Yun, fulfilling the duty to preserve fallen states, has taken all their people within our borders. The reason the second-grade tribute has not reached the imperial treasury is solely these two aggressors. Emperor Shizong replied, 'Goguryeo for generations has borne the imperial commission and ruled the eastern seas alone. These crafty tribes among the nine Yi are precisely what you are empowered to subdue.' 'When the bottles are empty, the jars are shamed—whose fault is that?' In former times, when tribute from the regions failed, the regional commander was held responsible. Proclaim Our intent to your king: employ both force and kindness, destroy these harmful peoples, pacify the eastern lands, restore the two domains to their former seats, and see that tribute flows again without interruption.'
9
神龜中，雲死，靈太后為舉哀於東堂，遣使策贈車騎大將軍、領護東夷校尉、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王。 又拜其世子安為安東將軍、領護東夷校尉、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王。 正光初，光州又於海中執得蕭衍所授安寧東將軍衣冠劍佩，及使人江法盛等，送於京師。 安死，子延立。 出帝初，詔加延使持節、散騎常侍、車騎大將軍、領護東夷校尉、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王，賜衣冠服物車旗之飾。 天平中，詔加延侍中、驃騎大將軍，餘悉如故。 延死，子成立。 訖於武定末，其貢使無歲不至。
In the Shengui era Yun died. Empress Dowager Ling mourned him at the Eastern Hall and sent a posthumous patent investing him as General of Chariots and Cavalry, Protector of the Eastern Yi, Duke of Liaodong, and King of Goguryeo. His heir An was invested as General of the Pacified East, Protector of the Eastern Yi, Duke of Liaodong, and King of Goguryeo. Early in Zhengguang, Guangzhou again captured at sea the regalia of General of the Pacified East that Xiao Yan had granted to An, together with the envoy Jiang Fasheng and his party, and sent them to the capital. When An died, his son Yan succeeded him. Early in the reign of Emperor Chu, Yan was further invested as bearer of the credentials of authority, Regular Attendant Cavalier, General of Chariots and Cavalry, Protector of the Eastern Yi, Duke of Liaodong, and King of Goguryeo, with robes, regalia, chariots, and banners. During Tianping he was further made Attendant-in-Chief and General of Agile Cavalry; his other titles remained unchanged. When Yan died, his son Cheng took the throne. Down to the end of Wuding, tribute missions arrived every year without exception.
10
百濟國，其先出自夫餘。 其國北去高句麗千餘里，處小海之南。 其民土著，地多下濕，率皆山居。 有五穀，其衣服飲食與高句麗同。
The kingdom of Baekje traced its origins to Buyeo. Its realm lay more than a thousand li south of Goguryeo, on the southern shore of the Lesser Sea. Its people were sedentary; the land was low and marshy, and they mostly lived in the hills. They grew the five grains, and their dress and diet were the same as in Goguryeo.
11
延興二年，其王餘慶始遣使上表曰：「臣建國東極，豺狼隔路，雖世承靈化，莫由奉藩，瞻望雲闕，馳情罔極。 涼風微應，伏惟皇帝陛下協和天休，不勝係仰之情，謹遣私署冠軍將軍、駙馬都尉弗斯侯，長史餘禮，龍驤將軍、帶方太守、司馬張茂等投舫波阻，搜徑玄津，託命自然之運，遣進萬一之誠。 冀神祇垂感，皇靈洪覆，克達天庭，宣暢臣志，雖旦聞夕沒，永無餘恨。 」又云：「臣與高句麗源出夫餘，先世之時，篤崇舊款。 其祖釗輕廢隣好，親率士眾，陵踐臣境。 臣祖須整旅電邁，應機馳擊，矢石暫交，梟斬釗首。 自爾已來，莫敢南顧。 自馮氏數終，餘燼奔竄，醜類漸盛，遂見陵逼，構怨連禍，三十餘載，財殫力竭，轉自孱踧。 若天慈曲矜，遠及無外，速遣一將，來救臣國，當奉送鄙女，執掃後宮，并遣子弟，牧圉外廐。 尺壤匹夫不敢自有。 」又云：「今璉有罪，國自魚肉，大臣強族，戮殺無已，罪盈惡積，民庶崩離。 是滅亡之期，假手之秋也。 且馮族士馬，有鳥畜之戀； 樂浪諸郡，懷首丘之心。 天威一舉，有征無戰。 臣雖不敏，志效畢力，當率所統，承風響應。 且高麗不義，逆詐非一，外慕隗囂藩卑之辭，內懷兇禍豕突之行。 或南通劉氏，或北約蠕蠕，共相脣齒，謀陵王略。 昔唐堯至聖，致罰丹水； 孟常稱仁，不捨塗詈。 涓流之水，宜早壅塞，今若不取，將貽後悔。 去庚辰年後，臣西界小石山北國海中見屍十餘，并得衣器鞍勒，視之非高麗之物，後聞乃是王人來降臣國。 長蛇隔路，以沉于海，雖未委當，深懷憤恚。 昔宋戮申舟，楚莊徒跣； 鷂撮放鳩，信陵不食。 克敵建名，美隆無已。 夫以區區偏鄙，猶慕萬代之信，況陛下合氣天地，勢傾山海，豈令小竪，跨塞天逵。 今上所得鞍一，以為實驗。」
In the second year of Yanxing, King Yuqing of Baekje sent his first embassy with a memorial: 'Your subject's kingdom stands at the eastern edge of the world, and wolves block the road between us. Though we have long received the court's grace, we have had no way to offer our submission. Gazing toward the imperial palace, my longing knows no limit.' As the cool wind rises, I humbly reflect that Your Majesty is in harmony with Heaven's favor. Unable to contain my devotion, I send my General of the Champions and Commandant of the Horse-Attendants Fusi Hou, Chief Clerk Yu Li, General of the Dragon Courser and Administrator of Daifang Zhang Mao, and others—they have braved the waves, found a path across the dark sea, and trust to Heaven's will to bear this faint token of our loyalty. May the spirits be moved and the imperial majesty extend over us, that we may reach the heavenly court and make our purpose known—even if we hear the reply at dawn and die at dusk, we shall have no regret. The memorial continued: 'Your subject and Goguryeo both spring from Buyeo, and in former ages we held each other in close friendship.' But their ancestor Chao lightly broke that friendship, led his army in person, and ravaged our lands. Your subject's ancestor Xu mustered his troops and struck like lightning; after only a brief clash of arms he took Chao's head. From that day forward none of them dared turn south again. When the house of Feng fell, its remnants fled to us, but their enemies grew stronger still and pressed us hard. For more than thirty years grievance followed disaster, until our wealth was spent and our strength exhausted and we grew ever weaker. If Your Majesty in Heaven's mercy extends his care even to the farthest lands and quickly sends a general to save my kingdom, I shall offer my humble daughter to serve in the inner palace and send my sons to tend your outer stables. Not a foot of my soil nor a single subject would I keep for myself. The memorial continued: 'King Lian is guilty; his realm is being carved up like fish on a cutting board. Powerful ministers and great clans slaughter without end; crime and wickedness have piled high, and the people are fleeing in disorder.' This is the hour of their destruction—the moment to strike with another's hand. Moreover, the Feng warriors and horses still yearn for their homeland as birds yearn for their nests. The people of the Lelang commanderies still turn their hearts toward their ancestral hills. When Your Majesty's majesty moves once, you will conquer without fighting. Though I am unworthy, I will give all my strength and lead my forces to answer your call. Goguryeo is faithless; its treacheries are many. Outwardly it flatters like a humble frontier vassal; inwardly it schemes like a wild boar on the rampage. It allies now with the Liu in the south, now with the Rouran in the north, acting as mutual support in a plot to threaten the imperial domain. Even the sage Emperor Yao of antiquity punished the tribes of the Dan River. Lord Mengchang was famed for humanity, yet he did not spare those who reviled him in the streets. A trickling stream should be stopped early; if you do not act now, you will regret it later. After the gengchen year, on the northern sea beyond Small Stone Mountain on my western frontier, more than ten corpses were found with clothing, gear, and saddlery that were plainly not Goguryeo goods. I later learned they were men of your court fleeing to my kingdom. Goguryeo, like a great serpent, barred their way and they perished at sea. Though the facts are not yet fully known, my anger runs deep. When Song killed Shen Zhou, King Zhuang of Chu went barefoot in mourning for him. When the hawk seized the dove, Lord Xinling would not eat. To defeat the enemy and win renown—such glory is everlasting. If even our small, remote kingdom still honors eternal faith, how much more should Your Majesty, who unites with Heaven and earth and whose power shakes mountains and seas, allow a petty chieftain to block the imperial highway! I present the saddle found above as proof.'

顯祖以其僻遠，冒險朝獻，禮遇優厚，遣使者邵安與其使俱還。 詔曰：「得表聞之，無恙甚善。 卿在東隅，處五服之外，不遠山海，歸誠魏闕，欣嘉至意，用戢于懷。 朕承萬世之業，君臨四海，統御羣生。 今宇內清一，八表歸義，襁負而至者不可稱數，風俗之和，士馬之盛，皆餘禮等親所聞見。 卿與高麗不穆，屢致陵犯，苟能順義，守之以仁，亦何憂於寇讎也。 前所遣使，浮海以撫荒外之國，從來積年，往而不返，存亡達否，未能審悉。 卿所送鞍，比校舊乘，非中國之物。 不可以疑似之事，以生必然之過。 經略權要，已具別旨。 」又詔曰：「知高麗阻強，侵軼卿土，修先君之舊怨，棄息民之大德，兵交累載，難結荒邊。 使兼申胥之誠，國有楚越之急，乃應展義扶微，乘機電舉。 但以高麗稱藩先朝，供職日久，於彼雖有自昔之釁，於國未有犯令之愆。 卿使命始通，便求致伐，尋討事會，理亦未周。 故往年遣禮等至平壤，欲驗其由狀。 然高麗奏請頻煩，辭理俱詣，行人不能抑其請，司法無以成其責，故聽其所啟，詔禮等還。 若今復違旨，則過咎益露，後雖自陳，無所逃罪，然後興師討之，於義為得。 九夷之國，世居海外，道暢則奉藩，惠戢則保境，故羈縻著於前典，楛貢曠於歲時。 卿備陳強弱之形，具列往代之迹，俗殊事異，擬貺乖衷，洪規大略，其致猶在。 今中夏平一，宇內無虞，每欲陵威東極，懸旌域表，拯荒黎於偏方，舒皇風於遠服。 良由高麗即敍，未及卜征。 今若不從詔旨，則卿之來謀，載協朕意，元戎啟行，將不云遠。 便可豫率同興，具以待事，時遣報使，速究彼情。 師舉之日，卿為鄉導之首，大捷之後，又受元功之賞，不亦善乎。 所獻錦布海物雖不悉達，明卿至心。 今賜雜物如別。 」又詔璉護送安等。
Emperor Xianzu, impressed that they had braved the distant seas to offer tribute, received them with exceptional honor and sent Shao An to escort their embassy home. An edict declared: 'We have received your memorial and are pleased to learn that you are well.' You stand at the eastern edge of the world, beyond the five domains, yet despite mountains and seas you have turned your hearts to the Wei court. We rejoice in your sincerity and hold it close. We inherit the enterprise of ten thousand generations, rule the four seas, and govern all living beings. Within the realm there is peace; the eight directions submit. Countless peoples arrive bearing children on their backs. The harmony of customs and the strength of armies are things your envoys have themselves witnessed. You and Goguryeo are at odds and have suffered repeated invasions. If you uphold righteousness and govern with humanity, what enemy need you fear? The envoys We once sent across the sea to the outer lands have for years gone forth and not returned, and We have been unable to learn whether they live or die. The saddle you sent, compared with Our own mounts, is plainly not of Chinese make. You must not turn doubtful matters into certain accusations. The essentials of strategy have been set forth in separate instructions. Another edict said: 'We know Goguryeo, relying on its strength, encroaches on your lands, nurses old royal grievances, and neglects the welfare of its people. War has dragged on for years and the frontier remains unsettled.' You would combine the loyalty of Shen Xu with the urgency of Chu and Yue; you should aid the weak in righteousness and strike when opportunity comes like lightning. Yet Goguryeo has long been a vassal of former courts and performed its duties faithfully; though it has old offenses, it has not yet violated Our commands. Your embassy has only just arrived, yet you already ask for war; considered carefully, the matter is not yet ripe. Therefore We sent Li and others to Pyongyang last year to investigate the facts. But Goguryeo's petitions were frequent and its arguments persuasive; Our envoy could not refuse its requests, and the courts could not establish its guilt, so We allowed its pleas and recalled Li and his party. If it now disobeys Our command again, its guilt will be plain for all to see; even if it pleads later, it cannot escape punishment—then to march against it will be just. The peoples of the nine Yi have always lived beyond the seas; when the Way prevails they submit, when kindness rests they guard their borders. Thus former canons record their tethering, though tribute has sometimes lapsed. You have fully described their strength and weakness and recounted former ages; customs differ and your comparisons miss the mark, yet the broad design you propose still holds. Now the central realm is united and the realm is at peace. We long to display Our majesty in the far east, plant Our banners beyond the borders, rescue distant peoples, and spread imperial civilization to the outer lands. Only because Goguryeo is for the moment submissive have We not yet chosen a day for campaign. If you now follow Our edict, your counsel accords with Our mind; the main army will march—and not long from now. Prepare your allies to rise together and stand ready; send envoys in good time to report on their situation. When the army marches you shall lead the way; after the great victory you shall receive the highest reward—is that not well? Though not all the brocade, cloth, and sea products you sent arrived, your utmost sincerity is clear. Miscellaneous gifts are now granted as listed separately. Another edict ordered Lian to escort An and his party.

安等至高句麗，璉稱昔與餘慶有讎，不令東過，安等於是皆還。 乃下詔切責之。 五年，使安等從東萊浮海，賜餘慶璽書，褒其誠節。 安等至海濱，遇風飄蕩，竟不達而還。
When An and his party reached Goguryeo, Lian claimed an old feud with Yuqing and refused to let them pass eastward, so they all turned back. The court then issued a stern rebuke. In the fifth year, An and his party set out from Donglai by sea, bearing an imperial letter with the seal praising Yuqing's loyalty. At the seashore they were scattered by storms and never reached Baekje, returning empty-handed.

勿吉國，在高句麗北，舊肅慎國也。 邑落各自有長，不相總一。 其人勁悍，於東夷最強。 言語獨異。 常輕豆莫婁等國，諸國亦患之。 去洛五千里。 自和龍北二百餘里有善玉山，山北行十三日至祁黎山，又北行七日至如洛瓌水，水廣里餘，又北行十五日至太魯水，又東北行十八日到其國。 國有大水，闊三里餘，名速末水。 其地下濕，築城穴居，屋形似塚，開口於上，以梯出入。 其國無牛，有車馬，佃則偶耕，車則步推。 有粟及麥穄，菜則有葵。 水氣醎凝，鹽生樹上，亦有鹽池。 多猪無羊。 嚼米醞酒，飲能至醉。 婦人則布裙，男子猪犬皮裘。 初婚之夕，男就女家執女乳而罷，便以為定，仍為夫婦。 俗以人溺洗手面。 頭插虎豹尾。 善射獵，弓長三尺，箭長尺二寸，以石為鏃。 其父母春夏死，立埋之，冢上作屋，不令雨濕； 若秋冬，以其屍捕貂，貂食其肉，多得之。 常七八月造毒藥傅箭鏃，射禽獸，中者便死，煮藥毒氣亦能殺人。 國南有徒太山，魏言「大白」，有虎豹羆狼害人，人不得山上溲汙，行逕山者，皆以物盛。
The kingdom of Wuji lay north of Goguryeo; it was the old land of the Sushen. Each settlement had its own chief and they were not united under one ruler. Its people were fierce and strong—the most powerful among the Eastern Yi. Their language was unique. They regularly looked down on Doumolou and neighboring states, who in turn feared them. It lay five thousand li from Luoyang. Two hundred-odd li north of Helong stood Mount Shanyu; thirteen days' march north brought one to Mount Qili, seven days more to the Ruluohui River, more than a li across, fifteen days more to the Tailu River, and eighteen days northeast to their capital. Their land had a great river more than three li wide called the Sumo River. The ground was low and wet; they built walled settlements and lived in pit-dwellings shaped like mounds, entering and leaving by ladder through an opening in the roof. They had no cattle but kept horses and chariots; they plowed in pairs for farming and pushed carts on foot. They grew millet and wheat, and mallow among vegetables. Salt formed on trees from congealed brine, and there were salt pools as well. They kept many pigs but no sheep. They chewed rice to brew wine strong enough to bring on deep drunkenness. Women wore cloth skirts; men wore furs of pig and dog skin. On the wedding night the groom went to the bride's house, touched her breast, and the match was sealed; they then became husband and wife. By custom they washed their hands and faces with human urine. They wore tiger and leopard tails in their hair. They excelled at hunting; their bows were three feet long, their arrows a foot and two inches, tipped with stone. When parents died in spring or summer they were buried at once and a shelter built over the mound to keep off the rain. If death came in autumn or winter, they used the corpse to bait sable traps; the sable ate the flesh and many were caught. Each seventh or eighth month they made poison for arrow points; beasts struck died at once, and even the vapor from boiling the poison could kill a man. South of their land stood Mount Taitai—'Great White' in Wei speech—with tigers, leopards, bears, and wolves. No one might relieve himself on the mountain; travelers carried vessels for that purpose.

去延興中，遣使乙力支朝獻。 太和初，又貢馬五百匹。 乙力支稱：初發其國，乘船泝難河西上，至太𣳅河，沉船於水，南出陸行，渡洛孤水，從契丹西界達和龍。 自云其國先破高句麗十落，密共百濟謀從水道并力取高句麗，遣乙力支奉使大國，請其可否。 詔敕三國同是藩附，宜共和順，勿相侵擾。 乙力支乃還。 從其來道，取得本船，汎達其國。 九年，復遣使侯尼支朝獻。 明年復入貢。
During Yanxing they sent the envoy Yilizhi to court with tribute. Early in Taihe they again presented five hundred horses as tribute. Yilizhi reported that they had sailed up the Nanshui River, scuttled their boat at the Tai River, continued overland south, crossed the Luogu River, and reached Helong by the Khitan western border. They said their state had already overrun ten Goguryeo settlements and were secretly plotting with Baekje to attack Goguryeo by sea; Yilizhi was sent to ask the great state's permission. An edict commanded that all three states were frontier vassals and should live in harmony without encroaching on one another. Yilizhi then returned. By the route he had come he recovered his original boat and sailed home. In the ninth year they again sent the envoy Hounizhi to court. The following year they sent tribute again.

太和十二年，勿吉復遣使貢楛矢方物於京師。 十七年，又遣使人婆非等五百餘人朝獻。 景明四年，復遣使俟力歸等朝貢。 自此迄于正光，貢使相尋。 爾後，中國紛擾，頗或不至。 興和二年六月，遣使石久云等貢方物，至於武定不絕。
In the twelfth year of Taihe, Wuji again sent envoys with arrow-shafts and local products to the capital. In the seventeenth year they sent the envoy Renpo Fei and more than five hundred others to court. In the fourth year of Jingming they again sent the envoy Houligui and others to court. From then until Zhengguang, tribute missions came in steady succession. Afterward, when China fell into turmoil, they sometimes failed to come. In the sixth month of the second year of Xinghe they sent Shi Jiuyun and others with local products, and missions continued without break down to Wuding.

失韋國，在勿吉北千里，去洛六千里。 路出和龍北千餘里，入契丹國，又北行十日至啜水，又北行三日有蓋水，又北行三日有犢了山，其山高大，周回三百餘里，又北行三日有大水名屈利，又北行三日至刃水，又北行五日到其國。 有大水從北而來，廣四里餘，名㮈水。 國土下濕。 語與庫莫奚、契丹、豆莫婁國同。 頗有粟麥及穄，唯食猪魚，養牛馬，俗又無羊。 夏則城居，冬逐水草。 亦多貂皮。 丈夫索髮。 用角弓，其箭尤長。 女婦束髮，作叉手髻。 其國少竊盜，盜一徵三，殺人者責馬三百匹。 男女悉衣白鹿皮襦袴。 有麴釀酒。 俗愛赤珠，為婦人飾，穿挂於頸，以多為貴，女不得此，乃至不嫁。 父母死，男女眾哭三年，屍則置於林樹之上。 武定二年四月，始遣使張焉豆伐等獻其方物，迄武定末，貢使相尋。
The Shiwei lay a thousand li north of Wuji and six thousand li from Luoyang. The route ran more than a thousand li north of Helong through Khitan territory: ten days north to the Chuo River, three days to the Gai River, three days to great Mount Duliao, three hundred li around, three days to the Quli River, three days to the Ren River, and five days north to their capital. A great river more than four li wide flowed from the north, called the Nu River. The land was low and marshy. Their language was the same as that of the Kumo Xi, Khitan, and Doumolou. They grew millet, wheat, and hulled grain; they ate pork and fish, raised cattle and horses, and had no sheep. In summer they lived in walled settlements; in winter they followed pasture and water. They also had many sable pelts. The men wore their hair loose. They used horn bows and especially long arrows. Women bound their hair in a forked-hand bun. Theft was rare; one thief paid for three, and a murderer was fined three hundred horses. Men and women alike dressed in white deerskin jackets and trousers. They brewed wine from grain mash. They prized red pearls as women's ornaments, hung about the neck; the more pearls, the higher the status, and a girl without them might not marry. When parents died, the whole family mourned for three years and placed the body in the forest treetops. In the fourth month of the second year of Wuding they first sent Zhang Yandoufa and others with local products; tribute missions continued without break until the end of Wuding.
19
豆莫婁國，在勿吉國北千里，去洛六千里，舊北扶餘也。 在失韋之東，東至於海，方二千里。 其人土著，有宮室倉庫。 多山陵廣澤，於東夷之域最為平敞。 地宜五穀，不生五果。 其人長大，性強勇，謹厚，不寇抄。 其君長皆以六畜名官，邑落有豪帥。 飲食亦用俎豆。 有麻布衣，制類高麗而幅大，其國大人，以金銀飾之。 用刑嚴急，殺人者死，沒其家人為奴婢。 俗淫，尤惡妬婦，妬者殺之，尸其國南山上至腐。 女家欲得，輸牛馬乃與之。 或言本穢貊之地也。
Doumolou lay a thousand li north of Wuji and six thousand li from Luoyang; it was the old territory of Northern Buyeo. It lay east of Shiwei, extending to the sea, covering two thousand square li. Its people were sedentary and had palaces and storehouses. Its land had many hills and broad marshes and was the most level and open among the Eastern Yi. The soil grew the five grains but not orchard fruits. Its people were tall, brave, prudent, and honest, and did not raid or plunder. Their chiefs named their offices after the six domestic animals, and each settlement had powerful leaders. They ate from stands and dishes as well. They wore hemp cloth like Goguryeo's but broader in weave; nobles adorned their garments with gold and silver. Punishment was harsh: murderers were executed and their families enslaved. Their customs were licentious and they hated jealous wives, whom they killed and left on the southern mountain until the bodies rotted. The bride's family received cattle and horses before giving her in marriage. Some hold that it was originally Mo territory.
20
地豆于國，[5]在失韋西千餘里。 多牛羊，出名馬，皮為衣服，無五穀，惟食肉酪。 延興二年八月，遣使朝貢，至于太和六年，貢使不絕。 十四年，頻來犯塞，高祖詔征西大將軍、陽平王頤擊走之。 自後時朝京師，迄武定末，貢使不絕。
Didouyu lay more than a thousand li west of Shiwei. It had many cattle and sheep and famed horses; people dressed in hides and ate only meat and dairy, with no grain. In the eighth month of Yanxing's second year they sent tribute envoys, and missions continued without break until Taihe's sixth year. In the fourteenth year they repeatedly raided the frontier; Emperor Gaozu ordered the Prince of Yangping, Yi, to drive them off. Thereafter they sometimes visited the capital, and tribute missions continued until the end of Wuding.
21
庫莫奚國之先，東部宇文之別種也。 初為慕容元真所破，遺落者竄匿松漠之間。 其民不潔淨，而善射獵，好為寇鈔。 登國三年，太祖親自出討，至弱洛水南，大破之，獲其四部落，馬牛羊豕十餘萬。 帝曰：「此羣狄諸種不識德義，互相侵盜，有犯王略，故往征之。 且鼠竊狗盜，何足為患。 今中州大亂，吾先平之，然後張其威懷，則無所不服矣。 」既而車駕南還雲中，懷服燕趙。 十數年間，諸種與庫莫奚亦皆滋盛。 及開遼海，置戍和龍，諸夷震懼，各獻方物。 高宗、顯祖世，庫莫奚歲致名馬文皮。 高祖初，遣使朝貢。 太和四年，輒入塞內，辭以畏地豆于鈔掠，詔書切責之。 二十二年，入寇安州，營燕幽三州兵數千人擊走之。 後復款附，每求入塞，與民交易。 世宗詔曰：「庫莫奚去太和二十一年以前，與安營二州邊民參居，交易往來，並無疑貳。 至二十二年叛逆以來，遂爾遠竄。 今雖款附，猶在塞表，每請入塞與民交易。 若抑而不許，乖其歸向之心； 聽而不虞，或有萬一之警。 不容依先任其交易，事宜限節，交市之日，州遣上佐監之。 」自是已後，歲常朝獻，至於武定末不絕。
The Kumo Xi were a branch of the Eastern Yuwen. Murong Yuanzhen first defeated them, and the survivors fled into the pine deserts. They were unclean in their habits but skilled hunters who loved to raid and plunder. In Dengguo's third year Taizu campaigned in person to the Ruoluo River, routed them utterly, took four tribes, and seized more than a hundred thousand head of livestock. The Emperor said, 'These tribes know neither virtue nor duty; they rob one another, and when they offend the realm We chastise them.' Rats and dogs stealing in the night—what harm can they do?' The central realm is in chaos; We shall pacify it first, then extend Our majesty and kindness, and none will refuse to submit.' The imperial carriage then returned south to Yunzhong, and Yan and Zhao submitted. Within a dozen years the various tribes and the Kumo Xi all grew stronger. When Liaohai was opened and garrisons placed at Helong, the frontier peoples were awed and each sent tribute. Under Emperors Gaozong and Xianzu, the Kumo Xi yearly sent famed horses and patterned furs. Early in Emperor Gaozu's reign they sent tribute envoys to court. In Taihe's fourth year they repeatedly entered the frontier passes, claiming fear of Didouyu raids; the court rebuked them sharply. In the twenty-second year they invaded Anzhou and were driven off by several thousand troops from the Yan and You garrisons. Later they submitted again and repeatedly asked to enter the passes to trade with frontier peoples. Emperor Shizong decreed: 'Before Taihe's twenty-first year, the Kumo Xi mingled with the people of An and Ying and traded without suspicion.' Since their rebellion in the twenty-second year they have fled far away. Though they have now submitted, they remain beyond the passes and repeatedly ask to trade with our people. If We refuse, We thwart their desire to submit. If We permit without precaution, there may yet be trouble. They may no longer trade as freely as before; markets shall be regulated, and on trading days the province shall send an official to supervise.' From then on they sent annual tribute until the end of Wuding without interruption.
22
契丹國，在庫莫奚東，異種同類，俱竄於松漠之間。 登國中，國軍大破之，遂逃迸，與庫莫奚分背。 經數十年，稍滋蔓，有部落，於和龍之北數百里，多為寇盜。 真君以來，求朝獻，歲貢名馬。 顯祖時，使莫弗紇何辰奉獻，得班饗於諸國之末。 歸而相謂，言國家之美，心皆忻慕，於是東北羣狄聞之，莫不思服。 悉萬丹部、何大何部、伏弗郁部、羽陵部、[6]日連部、匹絜部、黎部、[7]吐六于部等，[8]各以其名馬文皮入獻天府，遂求為常。 皆得交市於和龍、密雲之間，貢獻不絕。 太和三年，高句麗竊與蠕蠕謀，欲取地豆于以分之。 契丹懼其侵軼，其莫弗賀勿于率其部落車三千乘、眾萬餘口，驅徙雜畜，求入內附，止於白狼水東。 自此歲常朝貢。 後告饑，高祖矜之，聽其入關市糴。 及世宗、肅宗時，恒遣使貢方物。 熙平中，契丹使人祖真等三十人還，靈太后以其俗嫁娶之際，以青氊為上服，人給青氊兩匹，賞其誠款之心，餘依舊式。 朝貢至齊受禪常不絕。
The Khitan lay east of the Kumo Xi; a related but distinct people, they too hid in the pine deserts. During Dengguo the imperial army routed them, and they fled apart from the Kumo Xi. After several decades they spread until their tribes lay several hundred li north of Helong, mostly as raiders and robbers. From the Zhenjun era they sought audience at court and yearly sent famed horses. Under Emperor Xianzu the envoy Mofohe Hechen came with tribute and was seated at the feast among the lowest-ranked states. On returning they spoke of the empire's splendor with admiration, and when the northeastern tribes heard of it, all wished to submit. The Xiwandan, Hedahé, Fufuyu, Yuling, Rilian, Pijie, Li, and Tuliuyu divisions and others each sent famed horses and patterned furs to the imperial treasury and asked to make the practice regular. They all traded at markets between Helong and Miyun, and tribute flowed without cease. In Taihe's third year Goguryeo secretly plotted with the Rouran to seize Didouyu and divide it. Fearing invasion, the Khitan chief Hewuyu led three thousand carts and more than ten thousand people with their herds to submit, settling east of the White Wolf River. From then on they sent tribute every year. When they later reported famine, Emperor Gaozu allowed them to enter the passes to buy grain. Under Emperors Shizong and Suzong they regularly sent envoys with local products. When the Khitan envoy Zuzhen and thirty others returned in Xiping, Empress Dowager Ling gave each two bolts of blue felt—their ceremonial garment at weddings—to reward their sincerity. Tribute continued without break down to the Qi dynasty's founding.
23
烏洛侯國，在地豆于之北，去代都四千五百餘里。 其土下濕，多霧氣而寒，民冬則穿地為室，夏則隨原阜畜牧。 多豕，有穀麥。 無大君長，部落莫弗皆世為之。 其俗繩髮，皮服，以珠為飾。 民尚勇，不為姦竊，故慢藏野積而無寇盜。 好獵射。 樂有箜篌，木槽革面而施九弦。 其國西北有完水，東北流合于難水，其地小水皆注於難，東入于海。 又西北二十日行有于巳尼大水，所謂北海也。 世祖真君四年來朝，稱其國西北有國家先帝舊墟，石室南北九十步，東西四十步，高七十尺，室有神靈，民多祈請。 世祖遣中書侍郎李敞告祭焉，刊祝文於室之壁而還。
Wuluohou lay north of Didouyu, more than four thousand five hundred li from the capital. The land was low, wet, foggy, and cold; in winter they lived in pit-dwellings, in summer they pastured on the hills. They kept many pigs and grew grain and wheat. They had no great king; tribal chiefs held office by hereditary succession. They plaited their hair, wore skins, and adorned themselves with pearls. They were brave and honest; grain was left in the open without fear of thieves. They loved hunting and archery. Their music included the konghou, a wooden trough with a leather face and nine strings. Northwest lay the Wanshui, flowing northeast into the Nanshui; lesser streams all joined the Nan and reached the sea in the east. Twenty days northwest lay the great Yisini River, the so-called Northern Sea. In Zhenjun's fourth year they came to court, reporting that northwest of their land stood a stone chamber of the Former Emperor's age—ninety paces by forty, seventy feet high—with a numinous presence to which the people prayed. Emperor Shizu sent Li Chang of the Secretariat to perform the sacrifice and carved a prayer on the chamber wall before returning.
24
史臣曰：夷狄之於中國，羈縻而已。 高麗歲修貢職，東藩之冠，榮哀之禮，致自天朝，亦為優矣。 其他碌碌，咸知款貢，豈牛馬內向，東風入律者也。
The historian writes: Toward China the Yi and Di are only tethered and bridled. Goguryeo performed yearly tribute and stood first among the eastern frontier; the mourning honors sent from the court were exceptional as well. The others, humbler still, all knew to offer tribute—was this the turning of cattle and horses inward, the eastern wind entering the pitch-pipes?
25
魏正始中入寇遼西安平三國魏志卷三０東夷傳、隋書卷八一高麗傳「遼西安平」作「西安平」。 按後漢書郡國志二安平國屬冀州，志五，西安平屬幽州遼東郡。 遼西郡沒有安平或西安平縣。 這裏「遼」字衍，或「遼」下脫「東」字。
Wei Zhengshi-era invasion of Liaoxi Anping: the Records of the Three Kingdoms, Eastern Yi biography (juan 30), and the Book of Sui, Goguryeo account (juan 81), read 'Liaoxi Anping' as 'Xi'anping.' The Book of the Later Han, Treatise on Commanderies (juan 2), places Anping Kingdom in Jizhou; juan 5 places Xi'anping in Liaodong Commandery, Youzhou. Liaoxi Commandery had neither Anping nor Xi'anping county. Here the character for 'Liao' is superfluous, or 'east' is missing after 'Liao.'
26
正始中世宗於東堂引見其使芮悉弗諸本「宗」作「祖」，北史卷九四高麗傳作「宣武」。 按上稱「正始」年號，自當作「世宗」，北史例稱帝號。 今改正。
Zhengshi-era audience with Ruixifu at the Eastern Hall: various editions read 'Shizong' as 'Shizu'; Beishi, Goguryeo account (juan 94), has 'Xuanwu.' Since the reign-period Zhengshi is named above, the text should read 'Shizong'; Beishi regularly uses reign titles. Corrected accordingly.
27
具弗伏國按卷六顯祖紀皇興元年二月、二年四月兩見此部，都作「具伏弗」。 下契丹傳有「伏弗郁部」，上脫「具」字，又「郁」「部」二字倒誤，但也可證這裏「弗伏」當作「伏弗」。
Jufufu state: the Basic Annals (juan 6) twice records this tribe in Huangxing 1.2 and 2.4 as 'Jufufu.' The Khitan account below has the 'Fufuyu Division'; 'ju' is missing above and 'yu' and 'bu' are transposed, confirming that 'fufu' here should be 'fufu.'
28
拔大何國按下契丹傳有「何大何部」，卷六顯祖紀皇興二年四月作「阿大何」。 這裏「拔」字當是「何」或「阿」之訛。
Badahé state: the Khitan account below has the 'Hedahé Division'; the Basic Annals (juan 6, Huangxing 2.4) has 'Adahé.' Here 'ba' is probably a corruption of 'he' or 'a.'
29
地豆于國北史卷九四「于」作「干」。 按本書他處也多作「地豆于」，間亦作「干」，北史則多作「干」，間作「于」，今仍之。
Didouyu state: Beishi (juan 94) writes 'yu' as 'gan.' Elsewhere in this book the name appears mostly as Didouyu, sometimes Didougan; Beishi mostly uses gan. The present text is retained.
30
伏弗郁部羽陵部按上勿吉傳見「具弗伏國」、「郁羽陵國」，卷六顯祖紀皇興元年二月、二年四月並見「具伏弗、郁羽陵」，這裏「伏弗」上當脫「具」字，「郁」「部」誤倒，當作「具伏弗部、郁羽陵部」。
Fufuyu and Yuling divisions: the Wuji account above has 'Jufufu state' and 'Yuyuling state'; the Basic Annals (juan 6, Huangxing 1.2 and 2.4) has 'Jufufu, Yuyuling.' 'Ju' is missing before 'fufu' and 'yu' and 'bu' are transposed; it should read 'Jufufu Division, Yuyuling Division.'
31
匹絜部黎部按上勿吉傳及卷六顯祖紀皇興元年、二年並見「匹黎尒部」，通典卷二００契丹條作「匹黎部」。 疑這裏「匹絜」下衍「部」字，本作「匹絜黎部」，誤分二部。
Pijie and Li divisions: the Wuji account and Basic Annals (juan 6, Huangxing 1–2) have 'Pilier Division'; the Tongdian (juan 200, Khitan entry) has 'Pili Division.' Probably 'bu' after 'Pijie' is superfluous and the original was 'Pijieli Division,' wrongly split into two.
32
吐六于部北史卷九四契丹傳「于」作「干」，卷六顯祖紀皇興二年四月作「叱六手」，通典卷二００作「比六干」。 疑「吐」「比」皆「叱」字之訛。 「手」顯訛，「于」「干」不知孰是。
Tuliuyu Division: Beishi, Khitan account (juan 94), writes 'yu' as 'gan'; the Basic Annals (juan 6, Huangxing 2.4) has 'Chiliushou'; the Tongdian (juan 200) has 'Biliugan.' Both 'tu' and 'bi' are probably corruptions of 'chi.' 'Shou' is clearly wrong; whether 'yu' or 'gan' is correct remains uncertain.
