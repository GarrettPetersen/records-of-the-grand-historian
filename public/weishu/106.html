高句麗百濟勿吉失韋豆莫婁地豆于庫莫奚契丹烏洛侯
Goguryeo, Baekje, Wuji, Shiwei, Doumolou, Didouyu, Kumo Xi, Khitan, and Wuluohou
2
高句麗者，出於夫餘，自言先祖朱蒙。 朱蒙母河伯女，為夫餘王閉於室中，為日所照，引身避之，日影又逐。 既而有孕，生一卵，大如五升。 夫餘王棄之與犬，犬不食； 棄之與豕，豕又不食； 棄之於路，牛馬避之； 後棄之野，眾鳥以毛茹之。 夫餘王割剖之，不能破，遂還其母。 其母以物裹之，置於暖處，有一男破殼而出。 及其長也，字之曰朱蒙，其俗言「朱蒙」者，善射也。 夫餘人以朱蒙非人所生，將有異志，請除之，王不聽，命之養馬。 朱蒙每私試，知有善惡，駿者減食令瘦，駑者善養令肥。 夫餘王以肥者自乘，以瘦者給朱蒙。 後狩于田，以朱蒙善射，限之一矢。 朱蒙雖矢少，殪獸甚多。 夫餘之臣又謀殺之。 朱蒙母陰知，告朱蒙曰：「國將害汝，以汝才略，宜遠適四方。 」朱蒙乃與烏引、烏違等二人，棄夫餘，東南走。 中道遇一大水，欲濟無梁，夫餘人追之甚急。 朱蒙告水曰：「我是日子，河伯外孫，今日逃走，追兵垂及，如何得濟？ 」於是魚鼈並浮，為之成橋，朱蒙得渡，魚鼈乃解，追騎不得渡。 朱蒙遂至普述水，遇見三人，其一人著麻衣，一人著納衣，一人著水藻衣，與朱蒙至紇升骨城，遂居焉，號曰高句麗，因以為氏焉。
The people of Goguryeo came forth from Buyeo and claim Jumong as their founding ancestor. Jumong's mother was a daughter of the River Lord. The king of Buyeo had shut her in a chamber; when sunlight fell upon her, she drew back to avoid it, yet the sun's shadow followed her still. She soon conceived and gave birth to an egg as large as a five-sheng measure. The king of Buyeo cast it out to a dog, but the dog would not touch it. He cast it out to a pig, and the pig likewise refused it. He left it on the road, and cattle and horses turned aside from it. At last he abandoned it in the wild, where flock upon flock of birds covered it with their feathers to warm it. The king of Buyeo tried to cut it open but could not break the shell, and so returned the egg to its mother. His mother wrapped it in cloth and set it in a warm place, until a boy broke the shell and emerged. When he had grown to manhood they gave him the style Jumong, which in their tongue means one skilled in archery. The men of Buyeo held that Jumong, not being born of mortal seed, would harbor rebellious designs, and asked that he be killed. The king refused and set him to raising horses instead. Jumong would secretly test the horses and judge which were sound and which were not: he cut the rations of the swift so they grew lean, while he fed and cared for the slow until they grew fat. The king of Buyeo kept the fat horses for himself and gave Jumong only the lean ones. Later, on a hunt in the countryside, they allowed Jumong only a single arrow because of his renown as an archer. Though his arrows were few, Jumong brought down a great many beasts. The ministers of Buyeo again conspired to kill him. Jumong's mother learned of the plot in secret and warned him, "The realm is about to destroy you. With your talent and resourcefulness, you should flee far beyond its borders. " Jumong then left Buyeo with Wuyin, Wuwei, and two companions and fled southeast. On the way they came to a great river. They wished to cross but found no bridge, while the men of Buyeo pressed close behind. Jumong cried to the river, "I am a son of the sun and grandson of the River Lord. Today I flee for my life, and the pursuers are almost upon me. How am I to cross? " At once fish and turtles rose together and formed a bridge. Jumong crossed safely; when the creatures dispersed, the pursuing horsemen could not follow. Jumong came at last to the Pusu River, where he met three men—one clad in hemp, one in coarse cloth, one in garments of waterweed. Together they went to Kesunggu City and made their home there, taking the name Goguryeo as their state and clan.
3
初，朱蒙在夫餘時，妻懷孕，朱蒙逃後生一子，字始閭諧。 及長，知朱蒙為國主，即與母亡而歸之，名之曰閭達，委之國事。 朱蒙死，閭達代立。 閭達死，子如栗代立。 如栗死，子莫來代立，乃征夫餘，夫餘大敗，遂統屬焉。 莫來子孫相傳，至裔孫宮，生而開目能視，國人惡之。 及長凶虐，國以殘破。 宮曾孫位宮亦生而視，人以其似曾祖宮，故名為位宮，高句麗呼相似為「位」。 位宮亦有勇力，便弓馬。 魏正始中，入寇遼西安平，[1]為幽州刺史毋丘儉所破。 其玄孫乙弗利，利子釗，烈帝時與慕容氏相攻擊。 建國四年，慕容元真率眾伐之，入自南陝，戰於木底，大破釗軍，乘勝長驅，遂入丸都，釗單馬奔竄。 元真掘釗父墓，載其屍，并掠其母妻、珍寶、男女五萬餘口，焚其宮室，毀丸都城而還。 自後釗遣使來朝，阻隔寇讎，不能自達。 釗後為百濟所殺。
While Jumong was still in Buyeo, his wife had been with child. After he fled she gave birth to a son, whom she styled Shilüxie. When the boy had grown, he learned that Jumong had become king and fled with his mother to join him. Jumong named him Lüda and entrusted him with the affairs of state. When Jumong died, Lüda succeeded to the throne. When Lüda died, his son Yuli took the throne. When Yuli died, his son Molai succeeded and marched against Buyeo. Buyeo was routed, and Molai brought it under his rule. Molai's line held the throne in succession until a distant descendant named Gong was born with his eyes already open. The people regarded this as an ill omen. When he came of age he proved fierce and cruel, and the realm fell into ruin. Gong's great-great-grandson Weigong was likewise born with his eyes open. Because he resembled his forebear Gong, he was named Weigong—the Goguryeo word wei meaning "to resemble." Weigong too was brave and strong, and adept with bow and horse. During Wei's Zhengshi era he raided Anping in Liaoxi[1] and was defeated by Guanqiu Jian, Inspector of Youzhou. His great-great-grandson Yifuli was succeeded by his son Chao, who in the reign of Emperor Lie waged war with the Murong. In the fourth year of Jianguo, Murong Yuanzhen led an army against him, entering by the southern pass. At Mudi he routed Chao's forces, drove deep in pursuit, and entered Wandu while Chao fled alone on horseback. Yuanzhen opened Chao's father's tomb and carried off the corpse, together with Chao's mother and wife, his treasures, and more than fifty thousand captives. He burned the palaces, razed Wandu, and withdrew. Thereafter Chao sent envoys to court, but enemies and warfare barred the way and they could not arrive. Chao was later killed by Baekje.
4
世祖時，釗曾孫璉始遣使者安東奉表貢方物，并請國諱。 世祖嘉其誠款，詔下帝系名諱於其國，遣員外散騎侍郎李敖拜璉為都督遼海諸軍事、征東將軍、領護東夷中郎將、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王。 敖至其所居平壤城，訪其方事，云：遼東南一千餘里，東至柵城，南至小海，北至舊夫餘，民戶參倍於前。 魏時，其地東西二千里，南北一千餘里。 民皆土著，隨山谷而居，衣布帛及皮。 土田薄塉，蠶農不足以自供，故其人節飲食。 其俗淫，好歌舞，夜則男女羣聚而戲，無貴賤之節，然潔淨自喜。 其王好治宮室。 其官名有謁奢、太奢、大兄、小兄之號。 頭著折風，其形如弁，旁插鳥羽，貴賤有差。 立則反拱，跪拜曳一脚，行步如走。 常以十月祭天，國中大會。 其公會，衣服皆錦繡，金銀以為飾。 好蹲踞。 食用俎几。 出三尺馬，云本朱蒙所乘，馬種即果下也。 後貢使相尋，歲致黃金二百斤，白銀四百斤。
In the reign of Emperor Shizu, Chao's great-grandson Lian first sent the envoy Andong with a memorial and local tribute, and asked to receive the imperial taboo names. The Emperor praised his sincerity, sent down the imperial lineage and taboo names, and dispatched Supernumerary Cavalier Attendant Li Ao to invest Lian as Commander-in-Chief of All Military Affairs in Liaohai, General Who Conquers the East, Colonel Protector of the Eastern Yi, Duke Who Founded Liaodong Commandery, and King of Goguryeo. When Li Ao reached Lian's capital at Pyongyang and inquired into local conditions, he learned that the realm stretched more than a thousand li south of Liaodong, east to Zhacheng, south to the Lesser Sea, and north to old Buyeo, with registered households several times greater than before. In Wei times its territory measured two thousand li from east to west and more than a thousand li from north to south. The people were all native settlers who lived along valleys and hills and dressed in cloth, silk, and hides. The land was thin and poor, and sericulture and farming could not fully sustain them, so the people were sparing in what they ate and drank. Their customs were loose: they loved song and dance, and at night men and women would gather in groups to revel without regard to rank, though they took pride in cleanliness. Their king delighted in building palaces. Among their offices were the titles Yeshe, Taishe, Da Xiong, and Xiao Xiong. They wore on the head a zhefeng cap shaped like a formal crown, with bird feathers set in at the sides in grades according to rank. When they stood they folded their arms behind their backs; when they bowed and knelt they dragged one foot; when they walked their gait was almost a run. Each year in the tenth month they sacrificed to Heaven, and the whole realm gathered in celebration. At their public assemblies all wore brocade and embroidery adorned with gold and silver. They were fond of squatting. They ate from platters set on low tables. They bred horses only three feet tall, said to be the same breed Jumong had ridden—the guoxia pony. Thereafter tribute envoys came in steady succession, presenting two hundred jin of gold and four hundred jin of silver each year.
5
時馮文通率眾奔之，世祖遣散騎常侍封撥詔璉令送文通，璉上書稱當與文通俱奉王化，竟不送。 世祖怒，欲往討之，樂平王丕等議待後舉，世祖乃止，而文通亦尋為璉所殺。
At that time Feng Wentong fled to Goguryeo with his followers. The Emperor sent Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary Feng Bo with an edict ordering Lian to surrender him. Lian replied that he and Wentong would together accept the royal transform, but in the end he did not hand him over. The Emperor was enraged and wished to march against them, but the Prince of Leping, Pi, and others urged delay, and he desisted. Before long Wentong was killed by Lian as well.
6
後文明太后以顯祖六宮未備，敕璉令薦其女。 璉奉表，云女已出嫁，求以弟女應旨，朝廷許焉，乃遣安樂王真、尚書李敷等至境送幣。 璉惑其左右之說，云朝廷昔與馮氏婚姻，未幾而滅其國，殷鑒不遠，宜以方便辭之。 璉遂上書妄稱女死。 朝廷疑其矯詐，又遣假散騎常侍程駿切責之，若女審死者，聽更選宗淑。 璉云：「若天子恕其前愆，謹當奉詔。 」會顯祖崩，乃止。
Later, because Emperor Xianzu's inner palaces were not yet filled, Empress Dowager Wenming ordered Lian to present his daughter. Lian memorialized that his daughter was already married and asked to send his younger brother's daughter instead. The court agreed and dispatched the Prince of Anle, Zhen, Minister Li Fu, and others to the border with betrothal gifts. Lian was swayed by his advisers, who said that when the court had once married a woman of the Feng clan, it had soon destroyed their state—a lesson not far in the past—and that he should find some polite excuse to refuse. Lian then sent a false memorial claiming that the girl had died. The court suspected fraud and sent Acting Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary Cheng Jun to rebuke him sharply, adding that if the girl were truly dead he might select another suitable woman of the royal clan. Lian replied, "If the Son of Heaven will pardon my former offense, I shall respectfully obey. " But before anything further could be done, Emperor Xianzu died, and the matter was dropped.
7
至高祖時，璉貢獻倍前，其報賜亦稍加焉。 時光州於海中得璉所遣詣蕭道成使餘奴等送闕，高祖詔責璉曰：「道成親殺其君，竊號江左，朕方欲興滅國於舊邦，繼絕世於劉氏，而卿越境外交，遠通篡賊，豈是藩臣守節之義！ 今不以一過掩卿舊款，即送還藩，其感恕思愆，祗承明憲，輯寧所部，動靜以聞。」
Under Emperor Gaozu, Lian's tribute doubled what it had been, and the court's rewards in return grew somewhat as well. At that time officials of Guangzhou seized at sea envoys Lian had sent to Xiao Daocheng, including Yunu, and brought them to court. Emperor Gaozu rebuked Lian: "Daocheng murdered his own sovereign and usurped rule south of the Yangtze. We mean to restore extinguished states and continue the house of Liu, yet you reach across the border to treat with a usurper. Is this how a frontier subject keeps faith? We do not let a single fault efface your long loyalty. Return now to your realm, reflect on Our pardon, uphold the imperial statutes, keep your lands in order, and report all you do."
8
太和十五年，璉死，年百餘歲。 高祖舉哀於東郊，遣謁者僕射李安上策贈車騎大將軍、太傅、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王，諡曰康。 又遣大鴻臚拜璉孫雲使持節、都督遼海諸軍事、征東將軍、領護東夷中郎將、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王，賜衣冠服物車旗之飾，又詔雲遣世子入朝，令及郊丘之禮。 雲上書辭疾，惟遣其從叔升于隨使詣闕，嚴責之。 自此歲常貢獻。 正始中，世宗於東堂引見其使芮悉弗，[2]悉弗進曰：「高麗係誠天極，累葉純誠，地產土毛，無愆王貢。 但黃金出自夫餘，珂則涉羅所產。 今夫餘為勿吉所逐，涉羅為百濟所并，國王臣雲惟繼絕之義，悉遷于境內。 二品所以不登王府，實兩賊是為。 」世宗曰：「高麗世荷上將，專制海外，九夷黠虜，實得征之。 瓶罄罍耻，誰之咎也？ 昔方貢之愆，責在連率。 卿宜宣朕旨於卿主，務盡威懷之略，揃披害羣，輯寧東裔，使二邑還復舊墟，土毛無失常貢也。」
In the fifteenth year of Taihe, Lian died at more than a hundred years of age. The Emperor performed mourning rites at the eastern suburb and sent Gentlemen-attendant-in-Ordinary Li An with a patent posthumously investing him as General of Chariots and Cavalry, Grand Tutor, Duke Who Founded Liaodong Commandery, and King of Goguryeo, with the posthumous title Kang. He also sent the Grand Herald to invest Lian's grandson Yun with the same titles and regalia, and ordered Yun to send his heir to court in time for the suburban sacrifices. Yun pleaded illness in a memorial and sent only his cousin Shengyu with the embassy. The court rebuked him sharply. From that time tribute came every year without fail. During the Zhengshi era, Emperor Shizong received the envoy Ruixifu in the Eastern Hall.[2] Ruixifu said, "Goryeo has bound itself in loyalty to Heaven, generation after generation, and the land yields its tribute without fail. Yet our gold came from Buyeo and our ke-shell from Silla's lands. Buyeo has now been driven out by the Wuji, and Silla's territory has been swallowed by Baekje. Our king Yun, upholding the duty to preserve severed lines, has taken all their people within our borders. It is solely because of these two enemies that the two grades of tribute no longer reach your treasury. " The Emperor replied, "Goryeo has long enjoyed imperial favor and rules the seas beyond. The crafty tribes of the nine Yi are yours to subdue. When the bottle is empty the jar is shamed—whose fault is that? In former times, when tribute failed, the fault lay with the regional commander. You should convey Our intent to your king: employ both force and kindness, sweep away these enemies, restore peace to the eastern lands, and bring the two territories back to their old domains so that tribute may flow as before."
9
神龜中，雲死，靈太后為舉哀於東堂，遣使策贈車騎大將軍、領護東夷校尉、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王。 又拜其世子安為安東將軍、領護東夷校尉、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王。 正光初，光州又於海中執得蕭衍所授安寧東將軍衣冠劍佩，及使人江法盛等，送於京師。 安死，子延立。 出帝初，詔加延使持節、散騎常侍、車騎大將軍、領護東夷校尉、遼東郡開國公、高句麗王，賜衣冠服物車旗之飾。 天平中，詔加延侍中、驃騎大將軍，餘悉如故。 延死，子成立。 訖於武定末，其貢使無歲不至。
During the Shengui era Yun died. Empress Dowager Ling performed mourning rites at the Eastern Hall and sent envoys posthumously investing him as General of Chariots and Cavalry, Colonel Protector of the Eastern Yi, Duke Who Founded Liaodong Commandery, and King of Goguryeo. His heir An was invested as General of Pacification of the East, Colonel Protector of the Eastern Yi, Duke Who Founded Liaodong Commandery, and King of Goguryeo. Early in Zhengguang, Guangzhou again seized at sea the regalia of General of Pacification and Tranquility of the East that Xiao Yan had granted, together with his envoy Jiang Fasheng and others, and sent them to the capital. When An died, his son Yan succeeded him. Early in the reign of Emperor Chu, Yan was further invested as Bearer of the Staff of Authority, Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary, General of Chariots and Cavalry, Colonel Protector of the Eastern Yi, Duke Who Founded Liaodong Commandery, and King of Goguryeo, with full regalia. During the Tianping era he was further named Palace Attendant and General of Agile Cavalry, his other titles remaining unchanged. When Yan died, his son Cheng took the throne. Down to the end of the Wuding era, tribute envoys came every year without exception.
10
百濟國，其先出自夫餘。 其國北去高句麗千餘里，處小海之南。 其民土著，地多下濕，率皆山居。 有五穀，其衣服飲食與高句麗同。
The kingdom of Baekje traced its origins to Buyeo. Its realm lay more than a thousand li south of Goguryeo, on the southern shore of the Lesser Sea. The people were native settlers on low, damp ground and for the most part lived in the hills. They grew the five grains, and their dress, food, and customs matched those of Goguryeo.
11
延興二年，其王餘慶始遣使上表曰：「臣建國東極，豺狼隔路，雖世承靈化，莫由奉藩，瞻望雲闕，馳情罔極。 涼風微應，伏惟皇帝陛下協和天休，不勝係仰之情，謹遣私署冠軍將軍、駙馬都尉弗斯侯，長史餘禮，龍驤將軍、帶方太守、司馬張茂等投舫波阻，搜徑玄津，託命自然之運，遣進萬一之誠。 冀神祇垂感，皇靈洪覆，克達天庭，宣暢臣志，雖旦聞夕沒，永無餘恨。 」又云：「臣與高句麗源出夫餘，先世之時，篤崇舊款。 其祖釗輕廢隣好，親率士眾，陵踐臣境。 臣祖須整旅電邁，應機馳擊，矢石暫交，梟斬釗首。 自爾已來，莫敢南顧。 自馮氏數終，餘燼奔竄，醜類漸盛，遂見陵逼，構怨連禍，三十餘載，財殫力竭，轉自孱踧。 若天慈曲矜，遠及無外，速遣一將，來救臣國，當奉送鄙女，執掃後宮，并遣子弟，牧圉外廐。 尺壤匹夫不敢自有。 」又云：「今璉有罪，國自魚肉，大臣強族，戮殺無已，罪盈惡積，民庶崩離。 是滅亡之期，假手之秋也。 且馮族士馬，有鳥畜之戀； 樂浪諸郡，懷首丘之心。 天威一舉，有征無戰。 臣雖不敏，志效畢力，當率所統，承風響應。 且高麗不義，逆詐非一，外慕隗囂藩卑之辭，內懷兇禍豕突之行。 或南通劉氏，或北約蠕蠕，共相脣齒，謀陵王略。 昔唐堯至聖，致罰丹水； 孟常稱仁，不捨塗詈。 涓流之水，宜早壅塞，今若不取，將貽後悔。 去庚辰年後，臣西界小石山北國海中見屍十餘，并得衣器鞍勒，視之非高麗之物，後聞乃是王人來降臣國。 長蛇隔路，以沉于海，雖未委當，深懷憤恚。 昔宋戮申舟，楚莊徒跣； 鷂撮放鳩，信陵不食。 克敵建名，美隆無已。 夫以區區偏鄙，猶慕萬代之信，況陛下合氣天地，勢傾山海，豈令小竪，跨塞天逵。 今上所得鞍一，以為實驗。」
In the second year of Yanxing, King Yuqing first sent envoys with a memorial: "Your subject founded his realm at the eastern edge of the world. Wolves block the road, and though we have received the imperial transform for generations, we have had no way to present ourselves at court. Gazing toward the celestial towers, our longing knows no bounds. As the cool wind stirs, we bow before Your Majesty, who harmonizes with Heaven's blessings. Unable to contain our devotion, we send our General of Champions and Commandant of the Majordomo's Stables, the Marquis Fusi, Chief Clerk Yuli, General of the Dragon Courser and Administrator of Daifang Zhang Mao, and others—casting their boats upon storm-tossed seas and searching out hidden routes—to bring, by Heaven's grace, the smallest measure of our loyalty. May the spirits incline to us and the imperial numen spread its shelter, that we may reach the celestial court and fulfill our purpose—even should we hear Your reply at dawn and die at dusk, we would have no regret remaining. " He added, "Your subject and Goguryeo alike spring from Buyeo, and in former ages we held each other in deep friendship. Their ancestor Chao lightly cast aside our friendship, led his army in person, and trampled our borders. Your subject's ancestor Xu mustered his troops and struck like lightning. After a brief clash of arms he took Chao's head. From that day none of them dared turn south again. Since the fall of the Feng clan and the flight of their remnants, vile enemies have grown strong and pressed upon us. Grievance has piled upon calamity for more than thirty years until our wealth is spent and our strength exhausted, and we grow ever weaker. If Heaven shows mercy and extends its care to the farthest reaches, send a general at once to rescue my realm. I shall offer my humble daughter to serve in the inner palace and send my sons and younger kin to tend your outer stables. Not a foot of land nor a single subject would I dare hold as my own. " He added, "Lian is guilty now. His realm tears itself apart like meat on a chopping block; powerful ministers and clans slaughter without end; crimes and wickedness pile high, and the people are scattering. The hour of their destruction has come—the season to strike by another's hand. The Feng clan's warriors and horses still yearn for their old homeland, like birds and beasts for their nests. The people of the Lelang commanderies still turn their hearts toward home. One display of Heaven's majesty, and they will submit without a fight. Though I am not clever, I am resolved to give all my strength and will lead my followers to answer your call. Goryeo is faithless, and their treachery knows many forms: outwardly they mouth the humble phrases of a frontier vassal like Wei Xiao, while inwardly they plot violence like a charging boar. Now they treat with the Liu house in the south, now they pact with the Rouran in the north, standing lip to teeth with each ally as they plot against the imperial domain. Even the sage Emperor Yao of old punished the people of the Dan River. Meng Chang was famed for benevolence, yet he did not spare those who reviled him in the streets. A trickling stream should be dammed early. If you do not act now, you will regret it later. After the gengchen year, more than ten corpses were found in the sea north of Little Stone Mountain on my western border, together with clothing, gear, saddles, and bridles that were plainly not Goryeo goods. I later learned they were men of Your Majesty's realm seeking refuge in my country. A long serpent blocked their path and they perished in the sea. Though the facts are not yet clear, I am deeply aggrieved. When Song killed Shen Zhou, King Zhuang of Chu went barefoot in mourning. The hawk seized the dove and then released it; Lord Xinling would not touch his meal. To defeat one's foes and win renown is glory without end. If even our small and remote realm still cherishes loyalty lasting ten thousand generations, how much more should Your Majesty, who shares the breath of Heaven and Earth and whose power overawes mountains and seas, permit a petty chieftain to block the road to Heaven? The saddle I now present is offered as proof."

顯祖以其僻遠，冒險朝獻，禮遇優厚，遣使者邵安與其使俱還。 詔曰：「得表聞之，無恙甚善。 卿在東隅，處五服之外，不遠山海，歸誠魏闕，欣嘉至意，用戢于懷。 朕承萬世之業，君臨四海，統御羣生。 今宇內清一，八表歸義，襁負而至者不可稱數，風俗之和，士馬之盛，皆餘禮等親所聞見。 卿與高麗不穆，屢致陵犯，苟能順義，守之以仁，亦何憂於寇讎也。 前所遣使，浮海以撫荒外之國，從來積年，往而不返，存亡達否，未能審悉。 卿所送鞍，比校舊乘，非中國之物。 不可以疑似之事，以生必然之過。 經略權要，已具別旨。 」又詔曰：「知高麗阻強，侵軼卿土，修先君之舊怨，棄息民之大德，兵交累載，難結荒邊。 使兼申胥之誠，國有楚越之急，乃應展義扶微，乘機電舉。 但以高麗稱藩先朝，供職日久，於彼雖有自昔之釁，於國未有犯令之愆。 卿使命始通，便求致伐，尋討事會，理亦未周。 故往年遣禮等至平壤，欲驗其由狀。 然高麗奏請頻煩，辭理俱詣，行人不能抑其請，司法無以成其責，故聽其所啟，詔禮等還。 若今復違旨，則過咎益露，後雖自陳，無所逃罪，然後興師討之，於義為得。 九夷之國，世居海外，道暢則奉藩，惠戢則保境，故羈縻著於前典，楛貢曠於歲時。 卿備陳強弱之形，具列往代之迹，俗殊事異，擬貺乖衷，洪規大略，其致猶在。 今中夏平一，宇內無虞，每欲陵威東極，懸旌域表，拯荒黎於偏方，舒皇風於遠服。 良由高麗即敍，未及卜征。 今若不從詔旨，則卿之來謀，載協朕意，元戎啟行，將不云遠。 便可豫率同興，具以待事，時遣報使，速究彼情。 師舉之日，卿為鄉導之首，大捷之後，又受元功之賞，不亦善乎。 所獻錦布海物雖不悉達，明卿至心。 今賜雜物如別。 」又詔璉護送安等。
Emperor Xianzu, moved that they had braved such distances to present tribute, received them with generous courtesy and sent the envoy Shao An to return with their embassy. An edict said, "We have received your memorial and are pleased to learn you are well. You dwell in the far east, beyond the five domains, yet not so far from mountains and sea that you cannot turn in loyalty to the Wei court. We rejoice in your devotion and hold it close in Our heart. We inherit the enterprise of ten thousand generations, rule the four seas, and govern all living beings. Within the four seas all is at peace, and from the eight directions subjects come in numbers beyond count. The harmony of customs and the strength of armies—your envoy Yuli and his companions have seen this with their own eyes. You and Goryeo are at odds and have suffered repeated encroachments. If you uphold righteousness and govern with benevolence, what enemy need you fear? Envoys We sent before crossed the sea to reach distant lands, and for years none have returned. Whether they live or die, succeed or fail, We have been unable to learn. The saddle you sent, compared with Our own mounts, is plainly not of the Middle Kingdom. One must not turn doubtful evidence into certain guilt. The essentials of strategy have been set forth in separate instructions. " Another edict said, "We know that Goryeo, relying on its strength, encroaches on your lands, nurses old grievances, abandons the great virtue of giving the people peace, and that war has dragged on for years along the frontier. Your envoy speaks with the sincerity of Wu Zixu, and your state faces the urgency of Chu and Yue. It is right that you should uphold righteousness, aid the weak, and strike like lightning when the moment comes. Yet Goryeo has long called itself a vassal of the former court and performed its duties. Though old grievances exist between you, it has not yet violated imperial orders. Your embassy has only just opened relations, yet you already ask for a campaign. When the matter is examined, the case is not yet complete. Therefore in former years We sent Li and others to Pyongyang to verify the facts. But Goryeo's petitions came again and again with reasoned pleas. Our envoys could not silence their appeals, and the judicial offices could not establish guilt. We therefore heard their case and ordered Li and the others to return. If they now disobey Our edict again, their guilt will stand revealed. Even if they plead later, they cannot escape punishment, and only then will it be right to raise armies against them. The states of the nine Yi have long dwelt beyond the sea: when the Way prevails they submit as vassals, when favor rests they keep their borders. Loose reins are recorded in the ancient canons, yet tribute has sometimes lapsed. You have fully described the balance of strength and weakness and traced the record of former ages. Customs differ and affairs vary, and some of your proposals miss the mark, yet the broad intent of your plan still stands. Now the central lands are unified and the realm is at peace. We constantly wish to extend Our majesty to the eastern edge, plant banners beyond the borders, rescue distant peoples, and spread the imperial wind to far domains. It is only because Goryeo has just renewed relations that We have not yet undertaken a campaign. If they now disobey Our edict, your counsel will match Our intent, and the great army will march—not far off. Prepare your forces to rise with Us, make ready for what is to come, and send envoys from time to time to report swiftly on their situation. When the army marches, you shall lead the way as chief guide; after a great victory you shall receive the foremost reward. Would that not be fitting? The brocade, cloth, and sea products you presented, though not all arrived, show your utmost sincerity. Miscellaneous gifts are granted as listed separately. " Another edict ordered Lian to escort An and his party.

安等至高句麗，璉稱昔與餘慶有讎，不令東過，安等於是皆還。 乃下詔切責之。 五年，使安等從東萊浮海，賜餘慶璽書，褒其誠節。 安等至海濱，遇風飄蕩，竟不達而還。
When An and his party reached Goguryeo, Lian claimed an old feud with Yuqing and refused to let them pass eastward. They therefore all returned. An edict was then issued sternly rebuking him. In the fifth year, An and his party were sent from Donglai by sea with a sealed letter for Yuqing praising his sincerity. When they reached the coast, storms tossed them about, and in the end they could not arrive and returned.

勿吉國，在高句麗北，舊肅慎國也。 邑落各自有長，不相總一。 其人勁悍，於東夷最強。 言語獨異。 常輕豆莫婁等國，諸國亦患之。 去洛五千里。 自和龍北二百餘里有善玉山，山北行十三日至祁黎山，又北行七日至如洛瓌水，水廣里餘，又北行十五日至太魯水，又東北行十八日到其國。 國有大水，闊三里餘，名速末水。 其地下濕，築城穴居，屋形似塚，開口於上，以梯出入。 其國無牛，有車馬，佃則偶耕，車則步推。 有粟及麥穄，菜則有葵。 水氣醎凝，鹽生樹上，亦有鹽池。 多猪無羊。 嚼米醞酒，飲能至醉。 婦人則布裙，男子猪犬皮裘。 初婚之夕，男就女家執女乳而罷，便以為定，仍為夫婦。 俗以人溺洗手面。 頭插虎豹尾。 善射獵，弓長三尺，箭長尺二寸，以石為鏃。 其父母春夏死，立埋之，冢上作屋，不令雨濕； 若秋冬，以其屍捕貂，貂食其肉，多得之。 常七八月造毒藥傅箭鏃，射禽獸，中者便死，煮藥毒氣亦能殺人。 國南有徒太山，魏言「大白」，有虎豹羆狼害人，人不得山上溲汙，行逕山者，皆以物盛。
The state of Wuji lies north of Goguryeo and is the old land of Sushen. Each settlement has its own chief, and they are not united under one ruler. Their people are fierce and strong—the most powerful among the Eastern Yi. Their language is wholly distinct. They constantly look down on Doumolou and the other states, which likewise suffer from their raids. It lies five thousand li from Luoyang. Some two hundred li north of Helong stands Mount Shanyu. Thirteen days' travel north of the mountain brings one to Mount Qili; seven days farther north to the Ruluohuan River, more than a li across; fifteen days farther north to the Tailu River; and eighteen days northeast to their country. Their country has a great river more than three li wide called the Sumo River. The land is low and damp. They build walled settlements and live in pit dwellings shaped like burial mounds, entering and leaving by ladder through an opening above. They have no cattle but do have horses and carts. They plow in pairs, and push their carts on foot. They grow millet and wheat, and among vegetables they cultivate mallow. Moisture turns brackish and congeals; salt forms on trees, and there are salt ponds as well. They keep many pigs but have no sheep. They chew rice to brew wine and can drink themselves drunk. Women wear cloth skirts; men wear furs of pig and dog skin. On the wedding night the groom goes to the bride's house, touches her breast, and the match is sealed; they then become husband and wife. By custom they wash their hands and face with human urine. They wear tiger and leopard tails in their hair. They are skilled archers and hunters. Their bows are three feet long, their arrows a foot and two inches, with stone heads. When parents die in spring or summer they bury them at once, build a house over the mound, and keep the rain off. If death comes in autumn or winter, they use the corpse to trap sable, which feed on the flesh and are caught in great numbers. Each year in the seventh or eighth month they brew poison, smear it on arrowheads, and shoot game. Whatever is struck dies at once, and even the fumes from boiling the poison can kill a man. South of their country stands Mount Taitai, called in Wei speech Great White. Tigers, leopards, bears, and wolves harm people. No one may relieve himself on the mountain; travelers carry vessels for that purpose.

去延興中，遣使乙力支朝獻。 太和初，又貢馬五百匹。 乙力支稱：初發其國，乘船泝難河西上，至太𣳅河，沉船於水，南出陸行，渡洛孤水，從契丹西界達和龍。 自云其國先破高句麗十落，密共百濟謀從水道并力取高句麗，遣乙力支奉使大國，請其可否。 詔敕三國同是藩附，宜共和順，勿相侵擾。 乙力支乃還。 從其來道，取得本船，汎達其國。 九年，復遣使侯尼支朝獻。 明年復入貢。
During the Yanxing era they sent the envoy Yilizhi to court with tribute. Early in the Taihe era they presented five hundred horses in tribute. Yilizhi said that when they first set out they sailed up the Nahan River westward to the Taiyu River, sank their boats, continued south overland, crossed the Luogu River, and reached Helong by way of the Khitan western border. He said his people had first overrun ten Goguryeo settlements and were secretly plotting with Baekje to attack Goguryeo by water. Yilizhi was sent to the great state to ask whether this was permitted. An edict replied that all three states were imperial vassals and should live in harmony and not harass one another. Yilizhi then returned home. By the route they had taken, they recovered their original boats and sailed home. In the ninth year they again sent the envoy Hounizhi to court. The following year they presented tribute again.

太和十二年，勿吉復遣使貢楛矢方物於京師。 十七年，又遣使人婆非等五百餘人朝獻。 景明四年，復遣使俟力歸等朝貢。 自此迄于正光，貢使相尋。 爾後，中國紛擾，頗或不至。 興和二年六月，遣使石久云等貢方物，至於武定不絕。
In the twelfth year of Taihe, Wuji again sent envoys to the capital with bundled arrows and local products. In the seventeenth year they sent the envoy Renpofei and more than five hundred followers to court. In the fourth year of Jingming they again sent the envoy Houligui and others to court. From then until Zhengguang, tribute envoys came in steady succession. Thereafter, as the central realm fell into turmoil, they often failed to come. In the sixth month of the second year of Xinghe they sent the envoy Shijiuyun and others with local tribute, and envoys continued without break down to the Wuding era.

失韋國，在勿吉北千里，去洛六千里。 路出和龍北千餘里，入契丹國，又北行十日至啜水，又北行三日有蓋水，又北行三日有犢了山，其山高大，周回三百餘里，又北行三日有大水名屈利，又北行三日至刃水，又北行五日到其國。 有大水從北而來，廣四里餘，名㮈水。 國土下濕。 語與庫莫奚、契丹、豆莫婁國同。 頗有粟麥及穄，唯食猪魚，養牛馬，俗又無羊。 夏則城居，冬逐水草。 亦多貂皮。 丈夫索髮。 用角弓，其箭尤長。 女婦束髮，作叉手髻。 其國少竊盜，盜一徵三，殺人者責馬三百匹。 男女悉衣白鹿皮襦袴。 有麴釀酒。 俗愛赤珠，為婦人飾，穿挂於頸，以多為貴，女不得此，乃至不嫁。 父母死，男女眾哭三年，屍則置於林樹之上。 武定二年四月，始遣使張焉豆伐等獻其方物，迄武定末，貢使相尋。
The state of Shiwei lies a thousand li north of Wuji and six thousand li from Luoyang. The route runs more than a thousand li north of Helong into Khitan territory, then ten days north to the Chuo River, three days north to the Gai River, three days north to lofty Mount Duliao, which circles more than three hundred li, three days north to the great Quli River, three days north to the Ren River, and five days north to their country. A great river flows from the north, more than four li wide, called the Nu River. Their land is low and damp. Their language is the same as that of the Kumo Xi, Khitan, and Doumolou. They grow millet, wheat, and broomcorn millet, eat chiefly pork and fish, raise cattle and horses, and by custom keep no sheep. In summer they live in walled settlements; in winter they follow the pastures. They also produce much sable fur. Men wear their hair in braids. They use horn bows, and their arrows are especially long. Women bind their hair into a crossed-hands bun. Theft is rare among them: one theft draws a threefold penalty, and murder is punished with a fine of three hundred horses. Men and women alike dress in jackets and trousers of white deerskin. They brew wine with yeast. They prize red pearls as women's ornaments, strung about the neck—the more the better. A woman without them might not marry at all. When parents die, the whole community mourns for three years, and the corpse is placed in the forest treetops. In the fourth month of the second year of Wuding they first sent the envoy Zhang Yandoufa and others with local tribute, and envoys continued in succession down to the end of the Wuding era.
19
豆莫婁國，在勿吉國北千里，去洛六千里，舊北扶餘也。 在失韋之東，東至於海，方二千里。 其人土著，有宮室倉庫。 多山陵廣澤，於東夷之域最為平敞。 地宜五穀，不生五果。 其人長大，性強勇，謹厚，不寇抄。 其君長皆以六畜名官，邑落有豪帥。 飲食亦用俎豆。 有麻布衣，制類高麗而幅大，其國大人，以金銀飾之。 用刑嚴急，殺人者死，沒其家人為奴婢。 俗淫，尤惡妬婦，妬者殺之，尸其國南山上至腐。 女家欲得，輸牛馬乃與之。 或言本穢貊之地也。
The state of Doumolou lies a thousand li north of Wuji and six thousand li from Luoyang, in the territory of old northern Buyeo. It lies east of Shiwei, reaching east to the sea, and measures two thousand li square. The people are native settlers with palaces and storehouses. The land has many hills and broad marshes and is the most level and open country in the Eastern Yi region. The soil is good for the five grains but does not bear the five fruits. The people are tall and sturdy, brave by nature, sober and honest, and do not raid or plunder. Their rulers name their offices after the six domestic animals, and each settlement has its powerful headmen. They also eat from platters and stands. They weave hemp cloth garments similar to Goryeo's but broader in cut, and their great men adorn them with gold and silver. Their punishments are harsh: murderers are put to death and their families enslaved. Their customs are loose, and they especially despise jealous wives, whom they kill and leave exposed on the southern mountain until the bodies rot. The bride's family must deliver cattle and horses before she is given in marriage. Some say the land was originally that of the Huimo.
20
地豆于國，[5]在失韋西千餘里。 多牛羊，出名馬，皮為衣服，無五穀，惟食肉酪。 延興二年八月，遣使朝貢，至于太和六年，貢使不絕。 十四年，頻來犯塞，高祖詔征西大將軍、陽平王頤擊走之。 自後時朝京師，迄武定末，貢使不絕。
The state of Didouyu[5] lies more than a thousand li west of Shiwei. They keep many cattle and sheep and breed famed horses, dress in hides, grow no grain, and live on meat and dairy alone. In the eighth month of the second year of Yanxing they sent envoys to court, and tribute missions continued without break down to the sixth year of Taihe. In the fourteenth year they raided the frontier repeatedly. Emperor Gaozu ordered the General Who Conquers the West, the Prince of Yangping, Yi, to drive them off. Thereafter they came periodically to the capital, and tribute envoys continued without break down to the end of Wuding.
21
庫莫奚國之先，東部宇文之別種也。 初為慕容元真所破，遺落者竄匿松漠之間。 其民不潔淨，而善射獵，好為寇鈔。 登國三年，太祖親自出討，至弱洛水南，大破之，獲其四部落，馬牛羊豕十餘萬。 帝曰：「此羣狄諸種不識德義，互相侵盜，有犯王略，故往征之。 且鼠竊狗盜，何足為患。 今中州大亂，吾先平之，然後張其威懷，則無所不服矣。 」既而車駕南還雲中，懷服燕趙。 十數年間，諸種與庫莫奚亦皆滋盛。 及開遼海，置戍和龍，諸夷震懼，各獻方物。 高宗、顯祖世，庫莫奚歲致名馬文皮。 高祖初，遣使朝貢。 太和四年，輒入塞內，辭以畏地豆于鈔掠，詔書切責之。 二十二年，入寇安州，營燕幽三州兵數千人擊走之。 後復款附，每求入塞，與民交易。 世宗詔曰：「庫莫奚去太和二十一年以前，與安營二州邊民參居，交易往來，並無疑貳。 至二十二年叛逆以來，遂爾遠竄。 今雖款附，猶在塞表，每請入塞與民交易。 若抑而不許，乖其歸向之心； 聽而不虞，或有萬一之警。 不容依先任其交易，事宜限節，交市之日，州遣上佐監之。 」自是已後，歲常朝獻，至於武定末不絕。
The Kumo Xi were originally a branch of the Eastern Yuwen. Murong Yuanzhen first defeated them, and the survivors fled into the pine deserts. They are not fastidious in their habits, but are skilled hunters and fond of raiding. In the third year of Dengguo, Taizu campaigned against them in person, routed them south of the Ruoluo River, and captured four tribes together with more than a hundred thousand head of livestock. The Emperor said, "These tribes know neither virtue nor righteousness. They plunder one another and violate the imperial domain, and that is why We marched against them. Mere rat-thieves and dog-bandits are hardly worth Our concern. The central provinces are in turmoil. We shall pacify them first, and then extend Our majesty and kindness, and none will fail to submit. " Thereafter the imperial carriage returned south to Yunzhong, and Yan and Zhao submitted in their hearts. Within a dozen years the various tribes, Kumo Xi included, all grew strong again. When Liaohai was opened and garrisons placed at Helong, the frontier peoples were awed into submission and each presented local tribute. Under Emperors Gaozong and Xianzu, Kumo Xi presented famed horses and patterned furs every year. Early in the reign of Emperor Gaozu they sent envoys to court. In the fourth year of Taihe they repeatedly entered the frontier passes, claiming fear of raids by Didouyu. An edict sternly rebuked them. In the twenty-second year they raided Anzhou and were driven off by several thousand troops from the Yan and You garrisons. Later they submitted again and repeatedly asked to enter the passes to trade with the people. Emperor Shizong edicted, "Before the twenty-first year of Taihe, Kumo Xi lived among the frontier people of An and Ying, traded freely, and gave no cause for suspicion. Since their rebellion in the twenty-second year, they have fled far away. Though they have submitted again, they remain beyond the passes and repeatedly ask to enter and trade with the people. To refuse them would thwart their desire to submit. Yet to permit them without precaution may invite unforeseen trouble. They may no longer trade as freely as before. The exchange must be regulated, and on market days the province shall send a senior official to supervise. " Thereafter they presented tribute every year without break down to the end of Wuding.
22
契丹國，在庫莫奚東，異種同類，俱竄於松漠之間。 登國中，國軍大破之，遂逃迸，與庫莫奚分背。 經數十年，稍滋蔓，有部落，於和龍之北數百里，多為寇盜。 真君以來，求朝獻，歲貢名馬。 顯祖時，使莫弗紇何辰奉獻，得班饗於諸國之末。 歸而相謂，言國家之美，心皆忻慕，於是東北羣狄聞之，莫不思服。 悉萬丹部、何大何部、伏弗郁部、羽陵部、[6]日連部、匹絜部、黎部、[7]吐六于部等，[8]各以其名馬文皮入獻天府，遂求為常。 皆得交市於和龍、密雲之間，貢獻不絕。 太和三年，高句麗竊與蠕蠕謀，欲取地豆于以分之。 契丹懼其侵軼，其莫弗賀勿于率其部落車三千乘、眾萬餘口，驅徙雜畜，求入內附，止於白狼水東。 自此歲常朝貢。 後告饑，高祖矜之，聽其入關市糴。 及世宗、肅宗時，恒遣使貢方物。 熙平中，契丹使人祖真等三十人還，靈太后以其俗嫁娶之際，以青氊為上服，人給青氊兩匹，賞其誠款之心，餘依舊式。 朝貢至齊受禪常不絕。
The Khitan state lies east of Kumo Xi. Though a distinct lineage, they are kin, and both took refuge in the pine deserts. During the Dengguo era the imperial army routed them, and they fled in disorder, separating from the Kumo Xi. After several decades they gradually spread, forming tribes several hundred li north of Helong that mostly raided and plundered. From the Zhenjun era onward they sought audience at court and presented famed horses each year. Under Emperor Xianzu the envoy Mofuhe Hechen presented tribute and was granted a place at the feast, though last among the states. On returning they spoke to one another of the splendor of the state, and all were filled with admiration. When the tribes of the northeast heard of it, none failed to think of submission. The Xiwandan, Hedahé, Fufuyu, Yuling,[6] Rilian, Pijie, Li,[7] Tuliuyu,[8] and other divisions each presented famed horses and patterned furs to the imperial treasury and asked to make the practice regular. They were all permitted to trade between Helong and Miyun, and tribute continued without break. In the third year of Taihe, Goguryeo secretly plotted with the Rouran to seize Didouyu and divide its territory. The Khitan, fearing invasion, were led by their chief Hewuyu with three thousand carts and more than ten thousand people, driving their herds, to seek refuge within the empire. They halted east of the White Wolf River. From that time they presented tribute every year. Later they reported famine. Emperor Gaozu took pity on them and allowed them to enter the passes to buy grain. Under Emperors Shizong and Suzong they regularly sent envoys with local tribute. During the Xiping era, when the Khitan envoy Zuzhen and thirty others returned home, Empress Dowager Ling, knowing that blue felt was their ceremonial dress at weddings, gave each man two bolts of blue felt to reward their sincerity. The rest followed the usual forms. Their tribute missions continued without break down to the Qi succession.
23
烏洛侯國，在地豆于之北，去代都四千五百餘里。 其土下濕，多霧氣而寒，民冬則穿地為室，夏則隨原阜畜牧。 多豕，有穀麥。 無大君長，部落莫弗皆世為之。 其俗繩髮，皮服，以珠為飾。 民尚勇，不為姦竊，故慢藏野積而無寇盜。 好獵射。 樂有箜篌，木槽革面而施九弦。 其國西北有完水，東北流合于難水，其地小水皆注於難，東入于海。 又西北二十日行有于巳尼大水，所謂北海也。 世祖真君四年來朝，稱其國西北有國家先帝舊墟，石室南北九十步，東西四十步，高七十尺，室有神靈，民多祈請。 世祖遣中書侍郎李敞告祭焉，刊祝文於室之壁而還。
The state of Wuluohou lies north of Didouyu, more than four thousand five hundred li from the capital at Dai. The land is low, damp, foggy, and cold. In winter the people dig pit dwellings; in summer they pasture their herds on the hills. They keep many pigs and grow grain and wheat. They have no paramount ruler; tribal chiefs hold office by hereditary succession. By custom they bind their hair, dress in hides, and adorn themselves with pearls. The people value courage and do not steal, so goods are left openly in the fields without fear of thieves. They love hunting and archery. Their music includes the konghou, a wooden trough covered with leather and strung with nine strings. Northwest of their country is the Wan River, which flows northeast to join the Nan River. The lesser streams of their land all feed the Nan and reach the sea to the east. Twenty days' travel northwest lies the great Yusini Sea, called the Northern Sea. In the fourth year of Zhenjun they came to court and reported that northwest of their country stood an ancient site of the imperial ancestors: a stone chamber ninety paces north to south, forty east to west, and seventy feet high, inhabited by a numinous presence to whom the people prayed. Emperor Shizu sent Palace Gentleman Li Chang to perform the sacrifice, inscribed a prayer on the chamber wall, and returned.
24
史臣曰：夷狄之於中國，羈縻而已。 高麗歲修貢職，東藩之冠，榮哀之禮，致自天朝，亦為優矣。 其他碌碌，咸知款貢，豈牛馬內向，東風入律者也。
The historiographer remarks: Toward the Middle Kingdom, the frontier peoples were held only by loose reins. Goryeo performed annual tribute and stood foremost among the eastern frontier states. That mourning honors should come from the celestial court was generosity indeed. The others, numerous though they were, all knew how to offer sincere tribute. Was this truly a case of cattle and horses turning homeward and the eastern wind falling into tune with the pitch pipes?
25
魏正始中入寇遼西安平三國魏志卷三０東夷傳、隋書卷八一高麗傳「遼西安平」作「西安平」。 按後漢書郡國志二安平國屬冀州，志五，西安平屬幽州遼東郡。 遼西郡沒有安平或西安平縣。 這裏「遼」字衍，或「遼」下脫「東」字。
On the Zhengshi-era raid on Liaoxi Anping: the Eastern Yi account in Wei Annals 30 of the Records of the Three Kingdoms and Goryeo account in History of the Sui 81 read "Xi'anping" rather than "Liaoxi Anping." According to the Treatise on Commanderies and States in the History of the Later Han, Anping State belonged to Jizhou, while Xi'anping belonged to Liaodong Commandery in Youzhou. Liaoxi Commandery had neither an Anping nor an Xi'anping county. Here the character for "Liao" appears to be superfluous, or the character for "east" has been lost after "Liao."
26
正始中世宗於東堂引見其使芮悉弗諸本「宗」作「祖」，北史卷九四高麗傳作「宣武」。 按上稱「正始」年號，自當作「世宗」，北史例稱帝號。 今改正。
On Emperor Shizong receiving the envoy Ruixifu at the Eastern Hall in Zhengshi: various editions read "Zong" as "Zu," and the Goryeo account in History of the North 94 has "Xuanwu." Since the era title Zhengshi is used above, the reference should be to Emperor Shizong; the History of the North regularly uses reign titles rather than temple names. The text is corrected accordingly.
27
具弗伏國按卷六顯祖紀皇興元年二月、二年四月兩見此部，都作「具伏弗」。 下契丹傳有「伏弗郁部」，上脫「具」字，又「郁」「部」二字倒誤，但也可證這裏「弗伏」當作「伏弗」。
On the state of Jufufu: Basic Annals 6 records this tribe in the second month of Huangxing 1 and the fourth month of Huangxing 2, both times as "Jufufu." The Khitan account below has "Fufuyu Division," showing that "Ju" is missing above and that "yu" and "bu" are reversed. This also confirms that "fufu" here should read "fufu."
28
拔大何國按下契丹傳有「何大何部」，卷六顯祖紀皇興二年四月作「阿大何」。 這裏「拔」字當是「何」或「阿」之訛。
On the state of Badahé: the Khitan account below has "Hedahé Division," and Basic Annals 6, fourth month of Huangxing 2, reads "Adahé." Here the character "ba" is likely a corruption of "he" or "a."
29
地豆于國北史卷九四「于」作「干」。 按本書他處也多作「地豆于」，間亦作「干」，北史則多作「干」，間作「于」，今仍之。
On the state of Didouyu: History of the North 94 writes the final character as "gan" rather than "yu." Elsewhere in this book the name appears mostly as Didouyu and occasionally as Didougan; the History of the North mostly has Didougan and occasionally Didouyu. The present text is retained as it stands.
30
伏弗郁部羽陵部按上勿吉傳見「具弗伏國」、「郁羽陵國」，卷六顯祖紀皇興元年二月、二年四月並見「具伏弗、郁羽陵」，這裏「伏弗」上當脫「具」字，「郁」「部」誤倒，當作「具伏弗部、郁羽陵部」。
On Fufuyu Division and Yuling Division: the Wuji account above has "Jufufu State" and "Yuyuling State," and Basic Annals 6 records "Jufufu" and "Yuyuling" in Huangxing 1.2 and 2.4. Here "ju" is missing before "fufu," and "yu" and "bu" are reversed; the text should read "Jufufu Division, Yuyuling Division."
31
匹絜部黎部按上勿吉傳及卷六顯祖紀皇興元年、二年並見「匹黎尒部」，通典卷二００契丹條作「匹黎部」。 疑這裏「匹絜」下衍「部」字，本作「匹絜黎部」，誤分二部。
On Pijie Division and Li Division: the Wuji account above and Basic Annals 6 for Huangxing 1 and 2 have "Pilier Division," while Tongdian 200, Khitan entry, has "Pili Division." It is likely that the character for "division" after "Pijie" is superfluous and that the original reading was "Pijieli Division," wrongly split into two divisions.
32
吐六于部北史卷九四契丹傳「于」作「干」，卷六顯祖紀皇興二年四月作「叱六手」，通典卷二００作「比六干」。 疑「吐」「比」皆「叱」字之訛。 「手」顯訛，「于」「干」不知孰是。
On Tuliuyu Division: History of the North 94, Khitan account, has "gan" for "yu"; Basic Annals 6, Huangxing 2.4, has "Chiliushou"; and Tongdian 200 has "Biliugan." Both "tu" and "bi" are likely corruptions of "chi." "Shou" is clearly erroneous; whether "yu" or "gan" is correct remains uncertain.
