于什門段進石文德汲固王玄威婁提劉渴侯朱長生于提馬八龍門文愛晁清劉侯仁石祖興邵洪哲王榮世胡小虎孫道登李几張安祖王閭
Yu Shenmen, Duan Jin, Shi Wende, Ji Gu, Wang Xuanwei, Lou Ti, Liu Kehou, Zhu Changsheng, Yu Ti, Ma Balong, Men Wen'ai, Chao Qing, Liu Hourén, Shi Zuxing, Shao Hongzhe, Wang Rongshi, Hu Xiaohu, Sun Daodeng, Li Ji, Zhang Anzu, and Wang Lü.
2
大義重於至聞自日人。 [2]慕之者蓋希，行之者實寡。 至於輕生蹈節，臨難如歸，殺身成仁，死而無悔，自非耿介苦心之人，鬱怏激氣之士，亦何能若斯。 僉列之傳，名節義云。
From antiquity, people have held that great righteousness outweighs the highest fame—the opening line of our text is damaged beyond recovery in the received version. Many may admire such conduct, but few truly practice it. To hold life lightly yet honor duty, to meet danger as if going home, to give one's body for humane virtue and die without regret—only a man of unyielding integrity, nursing deep resolve and burning spirit, could act thus. Here we gather their lives together under the rubric of righteous fidelity.
3
于簡，字什門，代人也。 太宗時為謁者，使喻馮跋。 及至和龍，住外舍不入，使人謂跋曰：「大魏皇帝有詔，須馮主出受，然後敢入。 」跋使人牽逼令入，見跋不拜，跋令人按其項。 什門曰：「馮主拜受詔，吾自以賓主致敬，何須苦見逼也！ 」與跋往復，聲氣厲然，初不撓屈。 既而跋止什門。 什門於羣眾之中，回身背跋，被袴後襠以辱之。 既見拘留，隨身衣裳敗壞略盡，蟣虱被體。 跋遺以衣服，什門拒而不受。 和龍人皆歎曰：「雖古烈士，無以過也！ 」歷二十四年，[3]後馮文通上表稱臣，乃送什門歸。 拜治書侍御史。 世祖下詔曰：「什門奉使和龍，值狂豎肆虐，勇志壯厲，不為屈節，雖昔蘇武何以加之。 」賜羊千口、帛千匹，進為上大夫，策告宗廟，頒示天下，咸使聞也。
Yu Jian, styled Shenmen, came from Dai. Under Emperor Taizong he served as Herald and was dispatched to deliver the imperial message to Feng Ba. On reaching Helong he stayed in an outer lodge and refused to enter, sending word to Feng Ba: "The Great Wei emperor has issued an edict. Lord Feng must come out to receive it; only then will I dare enter." " Feng Ba had men drag him in by force. He faced Feng Ba but would not bow, so Feng Ba ordered someone to press his head down. Shenmen said, "When Lord Feng bows to receive the edict, I shall pay respect as between guest and host. Why torment me like this?" " He and Feng Ba argued back and forth, his voice fierce and unwavering; he never bent from the start. Before long Feng Ba held Shenmen captive. Before the assembled crowd Shenmen turned his back on Feng Ba, dropped his trousers, and bared his rear to shame him. Once held prisoner, his clothes were worn to rags and lice covered his body. Feng Ba offered him clothes, but Shenmen refused them. The people of Helong all marveled: "Even the heroes of old could not equal this!" " He remained there twenty-four years; only after Feng Wentong submitted a memorial acknowledging subjection was Shenmen sent home. He was appointed Attendant Censor of the Imperial Library. Emperor Shizu issued an edict: "Shenmen served as envoy at Helong when a violent usurper ran riot. His courage was fierce and he would not bend his integrity—even Su Wu of old could hardly surpass him." " He received a thousand sheep and a thousand bolts of silk, was promoted to Grand Master of the First Order, and the court reported his deed to the ancestral temple and proclaimed it throughout the realm.
4
段進，不知何許人也。 世祖初，為白道守將。 蠕蠕大檀入塞，圍之，力屈被執。 進抗聲大罵，遂為賊殺。 世祖愍之，追贈安北將軍，賜爵顯美侯，諡曰莊。
Duan Jin was a man of unknown origin. Early in Emperor Shizu's reign he served as garrison commander at Baidao. The Rouran khan Datan raided across the border, besieged the post, and when resistance failed Duan was taken prisoner. Duan shouted his defiance and cursed them until the enemy slew him. Emperor Shizu mourned him and posthumously made him General Who Pacifies the North, with the title Marquis of Xianmei and the posthumous name Zhuang.
5
石文德，河中蒲坂人也，[4]有行義。 真君初，縣令黃宣在任喪亡，宣單貧無期親，文德祖父苗以家財殯葬，持服三年，奉養宣妻二十餘載。 及亡，又衰絰斂祔，率禮無闕。 自苗逮文德，刺史守令卒官者，制服送之。 五世同居，閨門雍睦。
Shi Wende came from Puban in Hedong and was known for his integrity. Early in the Zhenjun era the county magistrate Huang Xuan died in office. Xuan was poor and without kin, so Wende's grandfather Miao buried him at family expense, observed three years of mourning, and supported Xuan's wife for more than twenty years. When she died in turn, he again wore full mourning, laid her out, and enshrined her beside her husband, observing every rite without lapse. From Miao through Wende, whenever a provincial inspector, prefect, or magistrate died in office, the family wore mourning and saw the funeral through. Five generations lived under one roof, and the household was harmonious throughout.
6
又梁州上言天水白石縣人趙令安、孟蘭彊等，四世同居，行著州里。 詔並標牓門閭。
Liang Province also reported that Zhao Ling'an, Meng Lanqiang, and others of Baishi County in Tianshui had lived together four generations, their virtue known throughout the region. An edict ordered commemorative plaques posted at all their gates.
7
汲固，東郡梁城人也。 為兗州從事。 刺史李式坐事被收，吏民皆送至河上。 時式子憲生始滿月，式大言於眾曰：「程嬰、杵臼何如人也！ 」固曰：「今古豈殊。 」遂便潛還，不復回顧，徑來入城，於式婦閨抱憲歸藏之。 及捕者收憲屬，有一婢產男，母以婢兒授之。 事尋泄，固乃攜憲逃遁，遇赦始歸。 憲即為固長育至十餘歲，恒呼固夫婦為郎婆。 後高祐為兗州刺史，嘉固節義，以為主簿。
Ji Gu came from Liangcheng in Dong Commandery. He served as Registrar of Yan Province. Provincial Inspector Li Shi was arrested for an offense, and officials and commoners escorted him as far as the river. Shi's infant son Xian was only a month old. Shi cried out to the crowd, "What manner of men were Cheng Ying and Gongsun Chujiu!" " Ji Gu replied, "Past and present are not so different." " He slipped away without a backward glance, went straight into the city, entered Shi's wife's inner quarters, took the infant Xian, and hid him away. When officers came to seize the boy, a maid had just given birth; Shi's wife handed over the maid's infant in his place. The ruse was soon discovered, and Ji fled with Xian until an amnesty allowed them to return. Ji raised Xian to his teens, and the boy always addressed Ji and his wife as Father and Mother. Later, when Gao You became Inspector of Yan Province, he admired Ji's fidelity and made him Chief Clerk.
8
王玄威，恒農北陝人也。 顯祖崩，玄威立草廬於州城門外，衰裳蔬粥，哭踴無時。 刺史苟頹以事表聞。 詔令問狀，玄威稱：「先帝統御萬國，慈澤被於蒼生，含氣之類莫不仰賴，玄威不勝悲慕，中心知此，不知禮式。 」詔問玄威欲有所訴，聽為表列。 玄威云：「聞諱悲號，竊謂臣子同例，無所求謁。 」及至百日，乃自竭家財，設四百人齋會。 忌日，又設百僧供。 至大除日，詔送白紬袴褶一具，與玄威釋服，下州令表異焉。 婁提，代人也。 顯祖時為內三郎。 顯祖暴崩，提謂人曰：「聖主升遐，安用活為！ 」遂引佩刀自刺，幾至於死。 文明太后詔賜帛二百匹。
Wang Xuanwei came from Beishan in Hengnong. When Emperor Xianzu died, Xuanwei built a thatched hut outside the provincial capital, wore hemp mourning, lived on vegetables and gruel, and wept and stamped at all hours. Provincial Inspector Gou Tui reported the matter to court. The court ordered an inquiry. Xuanwei said, "The late emperor ruled the realm; his mercy embraced all living things, and every creature under heaven relied on him. I cannot contain my grief; I feel it in my heart, though I do not know the proper forms of mourning." " The edict asked whether he wished to submit a petition and allowed him to do so in a memorial. Xuanwei replied, "Hearing the late emperor's name I weep aloud. I believe all subjects share this grief and have nothing to ask of the throne." " On the hundredth day he spent his entire family fortune to host a vegetarian feast for four hundred people. On the anniversary he again provided offerings for a hundred monks. When the period of mourning ended, the court sent a set of white silk garments so Xuanwei could leave mourning, and ordered the province to note his exceptional conduct. Lou Ti came from Dai. Under Emperor Xianzu he served as an Inner Third-Rank Guard. When Emperor Xianzu died suddenly, Lou said to those around him, "The sage lord has departed—what use is life?" " He drew his belt dagger and stabbed himself, nearly dying. Empress Dowager Wenming ordered two hundred bolts of silk bestowed on him.
9
時有敕勒部人蛭拔寅兄地于，坐盜食官馬，依制命死。 拔寅自誣己殺，兄又云實非弟殺，兄弟爭死，辭不能定。 高祖詔原之。
At that time two Chile tribesmen, Zhibayin and his elder brother Diyu, were sentenced to death for stealing government horses. Bayin confessed to the crime himself, while his brother insisted the younger man was innocent; the two brothers competed to accept death, and the court could not decide between their accounts. Emperor Gaozu pardoned them both by edict.
10
劉渴侯，不知何許人也。 禀性剛烈。 太和中，為徐州後軍，以力死戰，眾寡不敵，遂禽。 瞋目大罵，終不降屈，為賊所殺。 高祖贈立忠將軍、平州刺史、上庸侯，賜絹千匹、穀千斛。
Liu Kehou was a man of unknown origin. He was fierce and unyielding by nature. During the Taihe era he served in the rear guard at Xuzhou, fought to the death, was overwhelmed by superior numbers, and was taken prisoner. He glared and cursed them until the enemy slew him, never yielding to the end. Emperor Gaozu posthumously made him General Who Establishes Loyalty, Governor of Ping Province, and Marquis of Shangyong, with a thousand bolts of silk and a thousand hu of grain.
11
有嚴季者，亦為軍校尉，與渴侯同殿，勢窮被執，終不降屈，後得逃還。 除立節將軍，賜爵五等男。
A company commander named Yan Ji fought in the same battle; when defeat was certain he too was captured but never yielded, and later escaped back to Wei territory. He was made General Who Establishes Integrity and ennobled as a fifth-rank baron.
12
朱長生及于提，並代人也。 高祖時，以長生為員外散騎常侍，與提俱使高車。 至其庭，高車主阿伏至羅責長生等拜，長生拒之曰：「我天子使，安肯拜下土諸侯！ 」阿伏至羅乃不以禮待。 長生以金銀寶器奉之，至羅既受獻，長生曰：「為臣內附，宜盡臣禮，何得口云再拜而實不拜！ 」呼出帳，命眾中拜。 阿伏至羅慚其臣下，大怒曰：「帳中何不教我拜，而辱我於大眾！ 」奪長生等獻物，囚之叢石之中，兵脅之曰：「汝能為我臣則活，如其不降，殺汝！ 」長生與于提瞋目厲聲責之曰：「豈有天子使人拜汝夷，我寧為魏鬼，不為汝臣！ 」至羅彌怒，絕其飲食。 從行者三十人皆降，至羅乃給以肉酪，惟長生與提不從，乃各分徙之。 積三歲，乃得還。 高祖以長生等守節遠同蘇武，甚嘉之，拜長生河內太守，于提隴西太守，並賜爵五等男。 從者皆為令長。
Zhu Changsheng and Yu Ti both came from Dai. Under Emperor Gaozu, Changsheng was appointed Extraordinary Attendant Cavalier and sent with Ti as envoys to the Gaoche. At the Gaoche court their ruler Avuzhiluo demanded that the envoys bow. Changsheng refused: "I am the Son of Heaven's envoy—how could I bow to a frontier chieftain?" " Avuzhiluo then treated them without proper ceremony. Changsheng presented gold, silver, and precious vessels as tribute. Once Avuzhiluo had accepted them, Changsheng said, "You have submitted as a subject—perform the full rites of submission. How can you promise to bow twice and then not bow at all?" " He summoned Avuzhiluo from the tent and ordered him to bow before the assembled crowd. " Avuzhiluo, shamed before his followers, raged: "Why did you not tell me to bow in private, yet humiliate me before the whole assembly!" " He seized their gifts, imprisoned them in the stony hills, and soldiers threatened them: "Become my subjects and live; refuse and die!" " Changsheng and Ti glared and shouted their rebuke: "How could the Son of Heaven's envoy bow to you barbarians! I would rather be a ghost of Wei than your subject!" " Avuzhiluo flew into a greater rage and cut off their food and drink. All thirty of their attendants submitted; Avuzhiluo gave them meat and curds, but Changsheng and Ti alone refused, so he had them exiled to separate places. After three years they were finally able to return. Emperor Gaozu praised them for holding their integrity in a manner rivaling Su Wu of old, appointed Changsheng Governor of Henei and Ti Governor of Longxi, and ennobled both as fifth-rank barons. Their attendants were all appointed district magistrates.
13
馬八龍，武邑武強人也。 輕財重義。 友人武遂縣尹靈哲在軍喪亡，八龍聞即奔赴，負屍而歸，以家財殯葬，為制緦服。 撫其孤遺，恩如所生。 州郡表列，詔表門閭。
Ma Balong came from Wuqiang in Wuyi. He held wealth lightly and prized righteousness. When his friend Yin Lingzhe of Wusui County died on campaign, Balong rushed to the field, carried the body home on his back, buried him at his own expense, and wore the full mourning of a close kinsman. He raised the orphan left behind with the love of a father. The province reported his conduct, and the court ordered a commemorative plaque at his gate.
14
門文愛，汲郡山陽人也。 早孤，供養伯父母以孝謹聞。 伯父亡，服未終，伯母又亡。 文愛居喪，持服六年，哀毀骨立。 鄉人魏中賢等相與標其孝義。
Men Wen'ai came from Shanyang in Ji Commandery. Orphaned young, he supported his uncle and aunt and was renowned for filial devotion. His uncle died, and before that mourning was finished his aunt died as well. Wen'ai mourned six years in all, his grief so severe that he was wasted to bone. His neighbors Wei Zhongxian and others jointly commended his filial conduct.
15
晁清，遼東人也。 祖暉，濟州刺史、潁川公。 清襲祖爵，例降為伯。 為梁城戍將。 蕭衍攻圍，糧盡城陷，清抗節不屈，為賊所殺。 世宗褒美，贈樂陵太守，諡曰忠。 子榮賓襲。 劉侯仁，豫州人也。 城人白早生殺刺史司馬悅，據城南叛。 悅息朏，走投侯仁。 賊雖重加購募，又嚴其捶撻，侯仁終無漏泄，朏遂免禍。 事寧，有司奏其操行，請免府籍，敍一小縣，詔可。
Chao Qing came from Liaodong. His grandfather Hui had been Governor of Ji Province and Duke of Yingchuan. Qing inherited his grandfather's title, which by regulation was reduced to marquis. He served as garrison commander at Liangcheng. When Xiao Yan besieged the city and supplies ran out, Liangcheng fell; Qing held firm and refused to yield, and the enemy killed him. Emperor Shizong commended him and posthumously made him Governor of Leling, with the posthumous name Loyal. His son Rongbin inherited the title. Liu Hourén came from Yu Province. A local man named Bai Zaosheng killed Provincial Inspector Sima Yue, seized the southern quarter of the city, and rebelled. Yue's son Fei fled to Hourén for protection. Though the rebels raised the bounty on his head and beat him savagely, Hourén never revealed Fei's hiding place, and the boy was spared. After order was restored, officials reported his conduct and asked that he be struck from the prefectural rolls and given a small county post; the court approved.
16
石祖興，常山九門人也。 太守田文彪、縣令和真等喪亡，祖興自出家絹二百餘匹，營護喪事。 州郡表列，高祖嘉之，賜爵二級，為上造。 後拜寧陵令，卒。 吏部尚書李韶奏其節義，請加贈諡，以奬來者，靈太后令如所奏。 有司諡曰恭。
Shi Zuxing came from Jiumen in Changshan. When Prefect Tian Wenbiao, Magistrate He Zhen, and others died, Zuxing supplied more than two hundred bolts of silk from his own household to manage their funerals. The province reported his deeds; Emperor Gaozu praised him, raised his noble rank by two steps, and enfeoffed him as Shangzao. He was later appointed magistrate of Ningling and died in office. Li Shao, Minister of Personnel, praised his fidelity and asked for a posthumous title to encourage others; Empress Dowager Lingming approved. The court granted him the posthumous name Respectful.
17
邵洪哲，上谷沮陽人也。 縣令范道榮先自朐城歸款以除縣令，[5]道榮鄉人徐孔明，妄經公府，訟道榮非勳，道榮坐除名。 羇旅孤貧，不能自理。 洪哲不勝義憤，遂代道榮詣京師，明申曲直。 經歷寒暑，不憚劬勞，道榮卒得復雪。 又北鎮反亂，道榮孤單，無所歸附。 洪哲兄伯川復率鄉人來相迎接，送達幽州。 道榮感其誠節，訴省申聞。 詔下州郡，標其里閭。
Shao Hongzhe came from Juyang in Shanggu. Magistrate Fan Daorong had earlier surrendered from Qucheng and been appointed magistrate;[5] a townsman named Xu Kongming falsely petitioned the authorities that Daorong had no merit, and Daorong was dismissed. Destitute and alone in a strange place, he could not fend for himself. Hongzhe, overcome by righteous anger, went to the capital on Daorong's behalf and laid out the full truth of the case. Through winter and summer he labored without complaint until Daorong's name was cleared. When the Northern Garrison rose in revolt, Daorong was left alone with nowhere to go. Hongzhe's elder brother Bochuan again led townsfolk to fetch him and escorted him safely to You Province. Moved by their loyalty, Daorong petitioned the provincial authorities to report their conduct. The court ordered plaques posted at their gates throughout the province.
18
王榮世，陽平館陶人也。 為三城戍主、方城縣子。 蕭衍攻圍，力窮知不可全，乃先焚府庫，後殺妻妾。 及賊陷城，與戍副鄧元興等俱以不屈被害。 肅宗下詔褒美忠節，進榮世爵為伯，贈齊州刺史； 元興開國子，贈洛州刺史。
Wang Rongshi came from Guantao in Yangping. He was garrison commander of Sancheng and Viscount of Fangcheng. When Xiao Yan besieged the city and resistance failed, knowing the post could not be saved, he burned the government stores and then killed his wife and concubines. When the city fell, he and Deputy Commander Deng Yuanxing and others were killed for refusing to yield. Emperor Suzong commended their loyalty, promoted Rongshi's title to earl, and posthumously made him Governor of Qi Province; Yuanxing was made Founding Viscount and posthumously appointed Governor of Luo Province.
19
胡小虎，河南河陰人也。 少有武氣。 正光末，為統軍於晉壽。 孝昌中，蕭衍將樊文熾等寇邊，益州刺史邴虬遣長史和安固守小劍，文熾圍之。 虬命小虎與統軍崔珍寶同往防拒。 文熾掩襲小虎、珍寶，並擒之。 文熾攻小劍未陷，乃將珍寶至城下，使謂和安曰：「南軍強盛，北救不來，豈若歸款，取其富貴。 」和安命射之，乃退。 復逼小虎與和安交言，小虎乃慷慨謂安曰：「我柵不防，為賊所虜。 觀其兵士，勢不足言，努力堅守。 魏行臺傳梁州遣將已至。 」[6]賊以刀毆擊，言不得終，遂害之。 三軍無不歎其壯節，哀其死亡。 賊尋奔敗，禽其次將蕭世澄、陳文緒等一十一人。 行臺魏子建壯其氣概，啟以世澄購其屍柩，乃獲骸骨歸葬。
Hu Xiaohu came from Heyin in Henan. Even as a youth he had a warrior's spirit. Late in the Zhenguang era he served as army commander at Jinshou. During the Xiaochang era, Liang generals led by Fan Wenchì raided the border. Yi Province Governor Bing Qiu sent Chief Clerk He An to hold Xiaojian, and Wenchì besieged the fortress. Bing ordered Xiaohu and Army Commander Cui Zhenbao to reinforce the defense together. Wenchì ambushed them and took both Xiaohu and Zhenbao prisoner. Xiaojian had not yet fallen. Wenchì brought Zhenbao to the wall and had him call to He An: "The southern army is strong and northern relief will not come. Why not surrender and win wealth and rank?" " He An ordered his archers to shoot, and the enemy withdrew. They forced Xiaohu to speak with He An again. Xiaohu cried out to him, "Our stockade was poorly defended and I was taken prisoner. Look at their troops—their strength is nothing. Hold the fortress with all your might. The Wei field headquarters reports that reinforcements from Liang Province have already arrived. "[6] The enemy beat him with blades before he could finish, and killed him. The whole army marveled at his courage and mourned his death. The enemy soon fled in defeat, and eleven officers including deputy commanders Xiao Shicheng and Chen Wenxu were taken. Field headquarters chief Wei Zijian admired his spirit, petitioned to exchange the captive Xiao Shicheng for his body, and brought his remains home for burial.
20
孫道登，彭城呂縣人也。 永安初，為蕭衍將韋休等所虜，面縛臨刃，巡遠村塢，令其招降鄉曲。 道登厲聲唱呼：「但當努力，賊無所能。 」賊遂屠戮之。
Sun Daodeng came from Lü County in Pengcheng. Early in the Yong'an era he was captured by Liang general Wei Xiu. Bound and facing execution, he was marched through distant hamlets and ordered to urge his neighbors to surrender. Daodeng shouted, "Stand firm—the enemy can do nothing against you!" " The enemy then killed him.
21
又荊州被圍，行臺宗靈恩遣使宗女等四人入城曉喻，為賊將所獲，執女等巡城，令其改辭。 女等大言：「天軍垂至，堅守莫降。 」賊忿，各刳其腹，然後斬首。 二州表其節義，道登等並賜五品郡、五等子爵，聽子弟承襲。 遣使詣所在弔祭。
When Jing Province was besieged, field headquarters chief Zong Ling'en sent four envoys including Zong Nü into the city to rally the defenders. A rebel general captured them, paraded them along the wall, and ordered them to change their message. The women cried out, "Imperial troops are near—hold fast and do not surrender!" " Enraged, the enemy disemboweled each of them and then cut off their heads. Both provinces reported their fidelity. Daodeng and the others were granted fifth-rank commandery titles and viscountcies, heritable by their sons. The court sent envoys to their home districts to perform mourning rites.
22
李几，博陵安平人也。 七世共居同財，家有二十二房，一百九十八口，長幼濟濟，風禮著聞，至於作役，卑幼競進。 鄉里嗟美，標其門閭。
Li Ji came from Anping in Boling. Seven generations lived together under one roof with shared property—twenty-two branches and 198 mouths. Young and old alike were orderly and renowned for their propriety; even the youngest hurried to take up any task. Neighbors marveled at their household, and the court ordered a commemorative plaque at their gate.
23
張安祖，河陽人也。 襲世爵山北侯。 時有元承貴，曾為河陽令，家貧，且赴尚書求選，逢天寒甚，遂凍死路側。 一子年幼，停屍門巷，棺斂無託。 安祖悲哭盡禮，買木為棺，手自營作，斂殯周給。 朝野嘉歎。 尚書聞奏，標其門閭。 王閭，北海密人也。 數世同居，有百口。 又太山劉業興四世同居，魯郡蓋儁六世同居，並共財產，家門雍睦。 鄉里敬異。 有司申奏，皆標門閭。
Zhang Anzu came from Heyang. He inherited the hereditary title Marquis of Shanbei. At the time Yuan Chenggui, a former magistrate of Heyang, was traveling to the Ministry of Personnel to seek an appointment. The weather turned bitterly cold and he froze to death by the road. He left a young son; the body lay in the street with no one to bury it. Anzu mourned him with full rites, bought wood and built the coffin with his own hands, and saw to the entire funeral. Court and country alike praised his conduct. The Ministry reported his deed, and the court ordered a plaque at his gate. Wang Lü came from Mi in Beihai. Several generations lived together under one roof, a household of a hundred mouths. Liu Yexing of Mount Tai lived together four generations, and Gai Jun of Lu Commandery six—all sharing property in harmonious households. Their neighbors held them in awe and respect. Officials reported their conduct, and commemorative plaques were posted at all their gates.
24
史臣曰：于什門等或臨危不撓，視死如歸； 或赴險如夷，惟義所在。 其大則光國隆家，其小則損己利物。 故其盛烈所著，與河海而爭流； 峻節所標，共松栢而俱茂。 並蹈履之所致，身歿名立，豈徒然哉。
The historian writes: Men such as Yu Shenmen faced danger without flinching and met death as if going home; others entered peril as calmly as level ground, wherever duty called. At their grandest they brought honor to state and clan; at their humblest they sacrificed themselves for others. Their renown races the rivers and seas in its reach; the integrity they embodied endures with pine and cypress. All this came of living what they preached: bodies may perish, but names endure. Their deaths were not in vain.

26
魏書卷八十七諸本目錄此卷注「闕」，卷末附宋人校語 〈殿本入考證〉 云：「魏收書節義傳亡。 」按傳序與北史卷八五節義傳序不同，當自原序刪節。 諸傳中只于什門傳稍詳於北史，王玄威傳所載詔書有溢出北史所載的文句，其他諸傳偶有一二字溢出，大抵全同北史。 諸人事迹本無可述，原文也必甚簡略。 此卷多數傳文全同北史，或由於所據的史鈔和北史都是直錄魏書，非必以北史補。
Various editions of the Wei Shu mark juan 87 as deficient in their tables of contents; Song-dynasty collation notes are appended at the end of the scroll. (Palace Edition, Textual Verification) which states: "Wei Shou's original chapter on righteous fidelity is lost." " The chapter preface differs from that in Beishi juan 85 and was evidently abridged from the original. Only Yu Shenmen's biography is somewhat fuller than in the Beishi; Wang Xuanwei's biography preserves edict language not found there; other biographies differ by a word or two at most—overall the text matches the Beishi nearly completely. These figures had little to recount in any case; the original text was surely very brief. Most of this juan matches the Beishi verbatim, perhaps because both drew on excerpts copied directly from the Wei Shu—not necessarily because the Beishi was used to fill gaps.

大義重於至聞自日人殿本考證云：「首九字訛舛，不可推尋。」
On the corrupt opening line "大義重於至聞自日人": the Palace Edition notes that the first nine characters are damaged beyond recovery.
28
歷二十四年通鑑卷一二二 〈三八五三頁〉 記于什門還魏於元嘉十一年 〈魏延和三年 (四三四)〉 ，「二十四」作「二十一」。 考異云：「後魏本紀 〈卷三〉 神瑞元年 〈四一四〉 八月遣于什門招諭馮跋，至此年二十一年矣。 若二十四年，乃在太延三年，而太延二年馮氏亡矣。 」按傳稱「馮文通上表稱臣，乃送什門歸」，卷四上世祖紀上延和三年三月記「文通遣尚書高顒上表稱藩」，故通鑑置於是年。 疑「二十四」當作「二十一」。
On "twenty-four years": Zizhi Tongjian, juan 122 (p. 3853) records Yu Shenmen's return to Wei in Yuanjia 11 (Northern Wei Yanhe 3 , 434) , reading "twenty-one" instead of "twenty-four." Examination of Discrepancies notes: "The Later Wei annals (juan 3) Shenrui 1 (414) sent Yu Shenmen in the eighth month to negotiate with Feng Ba—twenty-one years by this date. Twenty-four years would place the return in Taiyan 3, but the Feng state fell in Taiyan 2. " The biography says Feng Wentong submitted and Shenmen was released; the annals for Yanhe 3, month 3 record Feng Wentong's submission—hence the Tongjian dates the return to that year. "Twenty-four" is probably a corruption for "twenty-one."

河中蒲坂人也北史卷八五石文德傳「河中蒲坂」作「中山蒲陰」。 按蒲坂自來屬河東郡，魏也無「河中」郡名。 卷一０六上地形志上定州北平郡有蒲陰縣，北平郡乃孝昌二年分中山置。 若非「中」為「東」字之訛，則「河中蒲坂」為「中山蒲陰」之訛。
On "Puban in Hedong": Beishi juan 85 reads "Puyin in Zhongshan" instead. Puban belonged to Hedong Commandery; Northern Wei had no "Hedong Central" commandery. Geography juan 106A lists Puyin County in Beiping Commandery, Ding Province; Beiping was carved from Zhongshan in Xiaochang 2. Unless zhong (central) is a corruption of dong (east), "Puban in Hedong" should read "Puyin in Zhongshan."
30
縣令范道榮先自朐城歸款以除縣令諸本「朐」作「眴」，北史卷八五邵洪哲傳作「眗」。 冊府卷一三八 〈一六六六頁〉 、卷八０三 〈九五四八頁〉 並作「朐」。 按「朐城歸款」事見卷八世宗紀永平四年四月、卷四七盧淵附盧昶傳、卷九八蕭衍傳，「朐城」亦作「朐山」。 「眴」「眗」都是「朐」之訛，今據改。
On "submitted from Qucheng": other editions read xuan or ju; Beishi juan 85 agrees the place name is Qucheng. Cefu yuan gui, juan 138 (p. 1666) and juan 803 (p. 9548) Both passages read "Qucheng." The "Qucheng submission" is recorded in the annals for Yongping 4, month 4, in the biography of Lu Chao (appended to Lu Yuan), and in the biography of Xiao Yan; the place is also called Qushan. The variants xuan and ju are both corruptions of qu; the text is emended to Qucheng accordingly.
