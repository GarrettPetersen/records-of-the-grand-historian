1
高麗自有國以來，傳次久近，人民土田，歷代各有其志，然高麗與遼，相為終始二百餘年。 自太祖皇帝神冊間，高麗遣使進寶劍。 天贊三年，來貢。 太宗天顯二年，來貢。 會同二年，受晉上尊號冊，遣使往報。 聖宗統和三年秋七月，詔諸道各完戎器，以備東征高麗。 八月，以遼澤沮洳，罷師。 十年，以東京留守蕭恒德伐高麗。 十一年，王治遣樸良柔奉表請罪，詔取女直國鴨淥江東數百里地賜之。 十二年，入貢。 三月，王治遣使請所俘生口，詔贖還之，仍遣使撫諭。 十二月，王治進妓樂，詔卻之。 十三年，治遣李周楨來貢，又進鷹。 十月，遣李知白奉貢。 十一月，遣使冊治為王。 遣童子十人來學本國語。 十四年，王治表乞為婚姻，以東京留守駙馬蕭恒德女下嫁之。 六月，遣使來問起居。 自是至者無時。
From the time Goryeo became a kingdom, its royal succession, population, and territory each generation followed its own course; nevertheless Goryeo and the Liao were locked in relations that ran their full course for over two hundred years. During the Shence reign of the Taizu Emperor, Goryeo dispatched envoys bearing a treasured sword as tribute. In the third year of Tianzan, they presented tribute. In the second year of Tianxian of Emperor Taizong, they came to offer tribute. In the second year of Huitong, after receiving the honorific-title document from Jin, the court sent envoys to notify Goryeo. In the seventh month of autumn in the third year of Tonghe, Emperor Shenzong ordered every circuit to ready its arms for an eastern expedition against Goryeo. In the eighth month the campaign was called off, the Liao marshes being too boggy. In the tenth year, Xiao Hengde as Military Governor of the Eastern Capital led a campaign against Goryeo. In the eleventh year, King Wang Zhi sent Pak Yang-nyeo with a memorial pleading for pardon; the court decreed that several hundred li of territory east of the Yalu in Jurchen lands be ceded to Goryeo. In the twelfth year they presented tribute. In the third month, Wang Zhi asked for the return of captives; the court ordered them redeemed and sent envoys to reassure him. In the twelfth month Wang Zhi offered female entertainers and musicians; the court declined them. In the thirteenth year, Zhi sent Li Zhouzhen with tribute and also presented hunting hawks. In the tenth month, Li Zhibai was dispatched with tribute. In the eleventh month, envoys were sent to invest Wang Zhi as king of Goryeo. Goryeo sent ten youths to learn the Khitan language. In the fourteenth year, Wang Zhi petitioned for a marital alliance; the court married him to the daughter of Xiao Hengde, Military Governor of the Eastern Capital and imperial son-in-law. In the sixth month, envoys came to inquire after the emperor's well-being. From then on, tribute missions arrived at irregular intervals.
2
五月，高麗西京留守康肇弒其主誦，擅立誦從兄詢。 八月，聖宗自將伐高麗，報宋，遣引進使韓杞宣問詢。 詢奉表乞罷師，不許。 十一月，大軍渡鴨淥江，康肇拒戰於銅州，敗之。 肇復出，右皮室詳穩耶律敵魯擒肇等，追奔數十里，獲所棄糧餉、鎧仗，銅、霍、貴、寧等州皆降。 詢上表請朝，許之，禁軍士俘掠。 以政事舍人馬保祐為開京留守，安州團練使王八為副留守。 太子太師乙凜將騎兵一千，送保祐等赴京。 守將卓思正殺我使者韓喜孫等十人，領兵出拒，保祐等復還。 乙凜領兵擊之，思正遂奔西京。 圍之五日，不克，駐蹕於城西佛寺。 高麗禮部郎中渤海陀失來降。 遣排押、盆奴攻開京，遇敵於京西，敗之。 詢棄城遁走，遂焚開京，至清江而還。 二十九年正月，班師，所降諸城復叛。 至貴州南嶺谷，大雨連日，霽乃得渡，馬駝皆疲乏，甲仗多遺棄。 次鴨淥江，以所俘人分置諸陵廟，余賜內戚、大臣。
In the fifth month, Kang Zhao, Western Capital Military Governor of Goryeo, assassinated King Song and installed Song's cousin Xun without authorization. In the eighth month, Shenzong led the campaign in person, informed the Song court, and sent Introducing Commissioner Han Qi to proclaim the charges against Xun. Xun sent a memorial pleading for withdrawal of the army; the request was denied. In the eleventh month the main force crossed the Yalu; Kang Zhao gave battle at Tong Prefecture and was routed. Zhao sallied forth again; Yelü Dilu of the Right Pishi Army captured Zhao and his party, pursued them for dozens of li, and recovered abandoned provisions and arms; Tong, Huo, Gui, Ning, and other prefectures submitted. Xun petitioned to come to court; permission was granted and the soldiers were forbidden to take captives by force. Ma Baoyou, Administrative Associate, was appointed Military Governor of Kaesong, with Wang Ba, Militia Training Commissioner of An Prefecture, as deputy. Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent Yiling led a thousand horsemen to escort Baoyou and his party to the capital. The garrison commander Zhuo Sizheng killed ten of our envoys including Han Xisun, marched out to resist, and Baoyou and his party fell back. Yiling attacked him; Sizheng fled to the Western Capital. After a five-day siege failed, the emperor encamped at a Buddhist temple west of the city. Tuoshi, a Goryeo Bureau of Rites official of Bohai descent, defected. Paiya and Pannu attacked Kaesong, met the enemy west of the city, and routed them. Xun abandoned Kaesong and fled; the army burned the city and withdrew to the Qing River. In the first month of the twenty-ninth year the army withdrew, and the surrendered cities rose again. At the Southern Ridge Valley south of Gui Prefecture, rain fell for days; they crossed only after it cleared, with horses and camels spent and much armor cast away. At the Yalu, captives were assigned to the imperial mausoleums and temples; the rest were granted to imperial kin and senior ministers.
3
開泰元年，詢遣蔡忠順來乞稱臣如舊，詔詢親朝。 八月，遣田拱之奉表，稱病不能朝。 詔復取六州之地。 二年，耶律資忠使高麗取地，未幾還。 三年，資忠復使，如前索地。 五月，詔國舅詳穩蕭敵烈、東京留守耶律團石等造浮梁於鴨淥江，城保、宣義、定遠等州。 四年，命北府宰相劉慎行為都統，樞密使耶律世良為副，殿前都點檢蕭虛烈為都監。 慎行挈家邊上，致緩師期，追還之，以世良、虛烈總兵伐高麗。 五年，世良等與高麗戰於郭州西，破之。 六年，樞密使蕭合卓為都統，漢人行宮都部署王繼忠為副，殿前都點檢蕭虛烈為都監進討。 蕭合卓攻興化軍不克，師還。 七年，詔東平郡王蕭排押為都統，蕭虛烈為副統，東京留守耶律八哥為都監，復伐高麗。 十二月，蕭排押與戰於茶、陀二河之間，我軍不利，天雲、右皮室二軍沒溺者眾，天雲軍詳穩海裏、遙輦帳詳穩阿果達、客省使酌古、渤海詳穩高清明等皆沒於陣。 八年，詔數排押討高麗罪，釋之。 加有功將校，益封戰沒將校之妻，錄其子弟。 以南皮室軍校有功，賜衣物銀絹有差，出金帛賜肴裏、涅哥二奚軍。 八月，遣郎君曷不呂等率諸部兵，會大軍同討高麗。 詢遣使來乞貢方物。 九年，資忠還，以詢降表進，釋詢罪。
In the first year of Kaitai, Xun sent Cai Zhongshun to plead for restoration of vassal status; the court commanded Xun to attend in person. In the eighth month, Tian Gongzhi presented a memorial on Xun's behalf, citing illness that prevented him from coming to court. An edict was issued to retake the six prefectures. In the second year, Yelü Zizhong was sent to Goryeo to seize territory and soon returned. In the third year, Zizhong was dispatched again with the same demand for territory. In the fifth month, the court ordered State Uncle Xiao Dilie, Eastern Capital Military Governor Yelü Tuanshi, and others to erect a pontoon bridge on the Yalu and garrison Bao, Xuanyi, Dingyuan, and other prefectures. In the fourth year, Northern Chancellery Chancellor Liu Shenxing was named supreme commander, Bureau of Military Affairs Commissioner Yelü Shiliang as deputy, and Chief Palace Inspector Xiao Xulie as chief overseer. Shenxing moved his household to the border and missed the campaign deadline; recalled, he was replaced by Shiliang and Xulie, who led the army against Goryeo. In the fifth year, Shiliang and his forces defeated the Goryeo army west of Guo Prefecture. In the sixth year, Military Affairs Commissioner Xiao Hezhuo became supreme commander, Han Mobile Camp Chief Deployer Wang Jizhong deputy, and Chief Palace Inspector Xiao Xulie chief overseer of the advance. Xiao Hezhuo besieged Xinghua Army unsuccessfully and withdrew. In the seventh year, Eastern Peace Prince Xiao Paiya was appointed supreme commander, Xiao Xulie deputy, and Eastern Capital Military Governor Yelü Bage chief overseer for another Goryeo campaign. In the twelfth month, Xiao Paiya fought between the Cha and Tuo rivers; our forces fared badly, and many men of the Tianyun and Right Pishi armies drowned; detailed commanders Hai Li and Aguoda, Guest Reception Commissioner Zhuogu, Bohai detailed commander Gao Qingming, and others fell in battle. In the eighth year, the court repeatedly arraigned Paiya for failures in the Goryeo war, then pardoned him. Officers who had distinguished themselves were promoted; widows of the fallen received added fiefs, and their sons and younger brothers were entered on the rolls. Officers of the Southern Pishi Army were rewarded with clothing, silver, and silk according to merit; gold and silk were granted to the Xiao and Niege Jurchen forces. In the eighth month, Langjun Yebu and others led tribal levies to unite with the main force against Goryeo. Xun sent envoys asking permission to resume tribute offerings. In the ninth year, Zizhong returned with Xun's submission memorial, and Xun was absolved.
4
太平元年，詢薨，遣使來報嗣位，即遣使冊王欽為王。 九年，賜欽物。 十一年，聖宗崩，遣使告哀。 七月，使來慰奠。 興宗重熙七年，來貢。 十二年三月，以加上尊號，來賀。 十三年，遣使來貢。 十四年三月，又來貢。 十五年，入貢。 八月，王欽薨，遣使來告。 十六年，來貢。 明年，又來貢。 十九年，復貢。 六月，遣使來賀伐夏之捷。 二十二年，入貢。 二十三年四月，王徽請官其子，詔加檢校太尉。 興宗崩，道宗即位，清寧元年八月，遣使報國哀，以先帝遺留物賜之。 十一月，使來會葬。 二年、三年，皆來貢。 四年春，遣使報太皇太后哀。 五月，使來會葬。 咸雍七年、八年，來貢。 十二月，以佛經一藏賜徽。 九年、十年，來貢。 大康二年三月，皇太后崩，遣使報哀。 六月，使來吊祭。 四年，王徽乞賜鴨淥江以東地，不許。 九年八月，王徽薨，以徽子三韓國公勛權知國事。 十二月，勛薨。 大安元年，冊勛子運為國王。 二年，遣使來謝封冊。 三年，來貢。 四年三月，免歲貢。 五年、六年，連貢。 九年，賜王運羊。 十年，運薨，子昱遣使來告，即賻贈。 壽隆元年，來貢。 十一月，王昱病，命其子颙權知國事。 二年，來貢。 三年三月，王昱薨。 五年，王颙乞封冊。 六年，封颙為三韓國公。 七年，道宗崩，天祚即位，改為乾統元年，報道宗哀，使來慰奠。 十二月，遣使來賀。 五年，三韓國公颙薨，子俁遣使來告。 八年，封俁為三韓國公，贈其父颙為國王。 十二月，遣使來謝。 九年，來貢。 天慶二年，王俁母薨，來告，遣使致祭，起復。 三年，遣使來謝致祭，又來謝起復。 十年，乞兵於高麗以禦金，而金人責之。 至是遼國亡矣。
In the first year of Taiping, Xun died; Goryeo reported the succession, and the court at once invested Wang Qin as king. In the ninth year, presents were granted to Qin. In the eleventh year, upon Shenzong's death, Goryeo sent envoys to announce the bereavement. In the seventh month, envoys arrived to offer condolences. In the seventh year of Chongxi of Emperor Xingzong, they presented tribute. In the third month of the twelfth year, they came to congratulate the bestowal of an honorific title. In the thirteenth year, tribute envoys arrived. In the third month of the fourteenth year, they presented tribute again. In the fifteenth year they offered tribute. In the eighth month, Wang Qin died; envoys reported his death. In the sixteenth year they presented tribute. The following year they came with tribute again. In the nineteenth year they offered tribute once more. In the sixth month, envoys came to congratulate the defeat of Western Xia. In the twenty-second year they presented tribute. In the fourth month of the twenty-third year, Wang Hui asked that his son be given an office; the court granted him Honorary Grand Marshal. After Xingzong's death and Daozong's accession, in the eighth month of the first year of Qingning, Goryeo was notified of the national mourning and given gifts from the late emperor's estate. In the eleventh month, envoys came to take part in the funeral rites. In the second and third years they presented tribute each year. In the spring of the fourth year, envoys announced the Grand Empress Dowager's death. In the fifth month, envoys came for the funeral. In the seventh and eighth years of Xianyong they presented tribute. In the twelfth month, a complete Buddhist canon was granted to Wang Hui. In the ninth and tenth years they offered tribute. In the third month of the second year of Dakang, upon the Empress Dowager's death, envoys announced the mourning. In the sixth month, envoys came to perform mourning rites. In the fourth year, Wang Hui petitioned for territory east of the Yalu; the request was denied. In the eighth month of the ninth year, Wang Hui died; his son Xun, Duke of the Three Han States, was authorized to govern provisionally. In the twelfth month, Xun died. In the first year of Da'an, Xun's son Yun was invested as king. In the second year, envoys came to express gratitude for the investiture. In the third year they presented tribute. In the fourth month of the fourth year, annual tribute was waived. In the fifth and sixth years they offered tribute in consecutive years. In the ninth year, the court granted sheep to King Yun. In the tenth year, Yun died; his son Yu reported the death, and funeral gifts were sent at once. In the first year of Shoulong they presented tribute. In the eleventh month, Yu fell ill and appointed his son Yong to govern provisionally. In the second year they offered tribute. In the third month of the third year, Wang Yu died. In the fifth year, Wang Yong petitioned for formal investiture. In the sixth year, Yong was enfeoffed as Duke of the Three Han States. In the seventh year, after Daozong's death and Tianzuo's accession, the era became Qiantong year one; Goryeo was notified of the mourning and sent envoys to console the court. In the twelfth month, envoys came with congratulations. In the fifth year, Duke Yong died; his son Yu sent envoys to report the death. In the eighth year, Yu was enfeoffed Duke of the Three Han States and his father Yong was posthumously made king. In the twelfth month, envoys came to offer thanks. In the ninth year they presented tribute. In the second year of Tianqing, upon the death of Wang Yu's mother, Goryeo reported the bereavement; the court sent envoys to sacrifice and ordered Yu's return to duty after mourning. In the third year, envoys thanked the court for the sacrificial rites and again thanked for permission to leave mourning. In the tenth year, Liao asked Goryeo for troops against the Jin; the Jin held Goryeo responsible. By then the Liao dynasty had fallen.

○西夏
○ Western Xia
6
西夏，本魏拓跋氏後，其地則赫連國也。 遠祖思恭，唐季受賜姓曰李，涉五代至宋，世有其地。 至李繼遷始大，據夏、銀、綏、宥、靜五州，緣境七鎮，其東西二十五驛，南北十餘驛。 子德明，曉佛書，通法律，嘗觀《太一金鑒訣》、《野戰歌》，制番書十二卷，又制字若符篆。
Western Xia traced its line to the Tuoba of Northern Wei; its lands had once formed the Helian kingdom. Their distant forebear Sigong received the surname Li in late Tang times; through the Five Dynasties into Song, the clan kept its domain generation after generation. Under Li Jiqian the realm first expanded, holding Xia, Yin, Sui, You, and Jing prefectures, seven frontier commands, twenty-five relay posts east and west, and more than ten north and south. His son Deming knew Buddhist writings and legal codes, studied the 《Taiyi Golden Mirror Formula》 and 《Song of Field Battle》, compiled twelve volumes in the Tangut script, and devised characters resembling seal script.
7
其俗，衣白窄衫，氈冠，冠後垂紅結綬。 自號嵬名，設官分文武。 其冠用金縷貼，間起雲，銀紙帖，緋衣，金塗銀帶，佩蹀躞、解錐、短刀、弓矢，穿靴，禿發，耳重環，紫旋襕六襲。 出入乘馬，張青蓋，以二旗前引，從者百餘騎。 民庶衣青綠。 革樂之五音為一音，裁禮之九拜為三拜。 凡出兵先卜，有四：一炙勃焦，以艾灼羊胛骨； 二擗算，擗竹於地以求數，若揲蓍然； 三咒羊，其夜牽羊，焚香禱之，又焚谷火於野，次晨屠羊，腸胃通則吉，羊心有血則敗； 四矢擊弦，聽其聲，知勝負及敵至之期。 病者不用醫藥，召巫者送鬼，西夏語以巫為「廝」也； 或遷他室，謂之「閃病」。 喜報仇，有喪則不伐人，負甲葉於背識之。 仇解，用雞豬犬血和酒，貯於髑髏中飲之，乃誓曰：「若復報仇，穀麥不收，男女禿癩，六畜死，蛇入帳。 」有力小不能復仇者，集壯婦，享以牛羊酒食，趨讎家縱火，焚其廬舍。 俗曰敵女兵不祥，輒避去。 訴於官，官擇舌辯氣直之人為和斷，官聽其屈直。 殺人者，納命價錢百二十千。
In dress they wore narrow white shirts and felt caps with a red knotted cord hanging behind. They took the style Weiming and organized government into civil and military ranks. Official crowns bore gold filigree clouds and silver appliqué; scarlet robes and gold-plated silver belts; accoutrements included gaiters, knife pendants, short blades, bows and arrows, boots, shaved heads, heavy ear-rings, and six layers of purple twisted-lapel garments. They traveled on horseback beneath a blue canopy, preceded by two banners and escorted by more than a hundred riders. Commoners dressed in blue and green. They compressed the five tones of hide drums into a single tone and reduced the nine ritual prostrations to three. Before campaigning they always divined by four methods: first, scapulimancy—burning moxa on a sheep shoulder blade; second, tally-casting—splitting bamboo on the ground for numbers, as with yarrow stalks; third, the sheep curse—at night a sheep was led out, incense burned and prayers offered, and grain fires lit in the open; at dawn the sheep was slaughtered; clear entrails meant good fortune, blood in the heart meant defeat; fourth, shooting an arrow at the bowstring and reading victory, defeat, and the enemy's approach from the sound. The ill relied not on medicine but on shamans to drive off spirits—the Xia word for shaman was "si"; or they moved to another house, called "flashing the illness." They prized blood vengeance; while in mourning they refrained from raiding and marked themselves by wearing armor-leaves on the back. To end a feud they mixed chicken, pig, and dog blood with wine, drank from a skull, and swore: "Should vengeance be renewed, grain will fail, men and women go bald and leprous, livestock perish, and snakes enter the tent." If too weak to revenge, they assembled strong women, feasted them with cattle, sheep, and wine, and rushed the foe's home to burn it down. They held enemy women warriors ill-omened and always avoided them. Litigants appealed to officials, who chose eloquent, upright men to arbitrate and judged the merits of each side. Murder was settled by a life-price of one hundred and twenty thousand cash.
8
土產大麥、蓽豆、青稞、皞子、古子蔓、堿地蓬實、蓯蓉苗、小蕪荑、席雞草子、地黃葉、登廂草、沙蔥、野韭、拒灰、白蒿、堿地松實。
Local produce included barley, pi beans, highland barley, haozi, guzi vines, alkali thistle, cistanche sprouts, small fennel, mat-rush seed, rehmannia leaves, dengxiang grass, wild scallion, wild leek, juhui, white artemisia, and alkali pine nuts.
9
民年十五為丁。 有二丁者，取一為正軍。 負擔雜使一人為抄，四丁為兩抄。 餘人得射它丁，皆習戰鬥。 正軍馬駝各一，每家自置一帳。 團練使上，帳、弓、矢各一，馬五百匹，橐駝一，旗鼓五，槍、劍、棍棓、少袋、雨氈、渾脫、鍬、钁、箭牌、鐵笊籬各一； 刺史以下，人各一駝，箭三百，毛幕一； 餘兵三人共一幕。 有炮手二百人，號「潑喜」。 勇健者號「撞令郎」。 賫糧不過一旬。 晝則舉煙、揚塵，夜則篝火為候。 若獲人馬，射之，號曰殺鬼招魂。 或射草縛人。 出軍用單日，避晦日。 多立虛寨，設伏兵。 衣重甲，乘善馬，以鐵騎為前鋒，用鉤索絞聯，雖死馬上不落。 其民俗勇悍，衣冠、騎乘、土產品物、子姓傳國，亦略知其大概耳。
Men were counted as ding at age fifteen. Households with two ding furnished one regular soldier. Each regular soldier had one porter-attendant; four ding supplied two attendants. The rest could be drafted from other ding; all were trained for war. Regular soldiers each had a horse and camel; every household kept one tent. Militia Training Commissioners and above received one tent, bow, and quiver each, five hundred horses, one camel, five drums and banners, and one each of spear, sword, club, small bag, rain felt, felt coat, shovel, hoe, arrow shield, and iron rake; Prefects and below: one camel, three hundred arrows, and one felt tent per man; other troops shared one tent among three. Two hundred artillerymen were called "Poxi." Brave fighters were styled "Zhuanglinglang." Marching rations did not exceed ten days. By day they signaled with smoke and dust; by night with beacon fires. Captives of men or horses were shot in a rite called "shooting ghosts to summon souls." Sometimes they shot straw effigies bound like men. Armies marched on odd days and avoided even days. They erected many decoy camps and laid ambushes. They wore heavy armor on fine horses, led with iron cavalry linked by hooked ropes so that even in death they did not fall from the saddle. Their folk were by nature fierce; their dress, horses, products, royal surnames, and succession may be known in broad outline.

At first, while long subject to Song, Xia received the surname Zhao; In the fourth year of Tonghe, Jiqian defected from Song to Liao, was made Special Advancement and Honorary Grand Preceptor with command of Xia forces, and took back the surname Li. In the tenth month they presented tribute. In the sixth year they offered tribute. In the seventh year they came with tribute; Yelü Xiang's daughter was made Princess Yicheng and married to Jiqian. In the first month of the eighth year they came to give thanks. In the third month they presented tribute again. In the ninth month, Jiqian sent envoys with captives taken from Song. In the tenth month they reported a victory over Song. In the twelfth month, after capturing Lin, Fu, and other Song prefectures, they reported the feat; envoys invested Jiqian as King of Xia. In the second month of the ninth year, envoys announced victory over Song. In the fourth month, Li Zhibai came to thank the court for the investiture. In the seventh month, after retaking Sui and Yin, they reported to the court. In the tenth month, Jiqian sent up the patents Song had granted him. That month, Dingnan Military Commissioner Li Jibeng submitted; he was made Grand Master for Splendid Happiness, Honorary Grand Preceptor, Palace Attendant, Western Peace Prince, with the merit title Loyal and Obedient, Enlightening and Sagely, Stabilizing Dingnan. In the twelfth month, Jiqian secretly leaned toward Song; Han Dewei was dispatched with an edict to admonish him. In the thirteenth year, after defeating Song, they sent envoys with the news. In the fourteenth year they came with tribute again. In the third month of the twenty-second year, Dezhao sent Jiqian's remaining possessions. In the seventh month, Dezhao was enfeoffed Western Peace Prince. In the tenth month, envoys thanked the court for the investiture. In the twenty-third year, they reported capturing Song's Qingcheng. In the twenty-fifth year, upon Dezhao's mother's death, envoys performed sacrifices and ordered his return from mourning. In the twenty-seventh year, Empress Dowager Chengtian died; envoys announced the mourning to Xia. In the twenty-eighth year, Dezhao was invested King of Xia. In the first year of Kaitai, Dezhao sent fine horses. In the second year, Introducing Commissioner Li Yanhong granted carriages and horses to King Li Dezhao and Princess Yicheng. In the first year of Taiping they presented tribute. In the eleventh year, upon Shenzong's death, Xia was notified; Dezhao sent funeral gifts.

興宗即位，以興平公主下嫁李元昊，以元昊為駙馬都尉。 重熙元年，夏國遣使來賀。 李德昭薨，冊其子夏國公元昊為王。 二年，來貢。 十二月，禁夏國使沿路私市金鐵。 七年，來貢。 李元昊與平公主不諧，公主薨，遣北院承旨耶律庶成持詔問之。 九年，宋遣郭禎以伐夏來報。 十年，夏國獻所俘宋將及生口。 十一年，遣使問宋興師伐夏之由。 十二月，禁吐渾鬻馬於夏，沿邊築障塞以防之。 十二年正月，遣同知析津府事耶律敵烈、樞密都承旨王惟吉諭夏國與宋和。 二月，元昊以加上尊號，遣使來賀。 耶律敵烈等使夏國還，奏元昊罷兵，遣使報宋。 四月，夏國遣使進馬、駝。 七月，元昊上表請伐宋，不從。 十月，夏人侵党項，遣延昌宮使高家奴讓之。 十三年四月，党項及山西部族節度使屈烈以五部叛入西夏，詔征諸道兵討之。 六月，阻卜酋長烏八遣其子執元昊所遣求援使窊邑改來。 八月，夏使對不以情，羈之。 使復來，詢事宜不實對，笞之。 十月，元昊上表謝罪，欲收集叛黨以獻，從之。 進方物，命北院樞密副使蕭革迓之。 元昊親率党項三部來降，詰其納叛背盟，元昊伏罪。 初，夏人執蕭胡睹，至是，請以被執者來歸。 詔所留夏使亦還其國。 十二月，胡睹來歸，又遣使來貢。
At Xingzong's accession, Princess Xingping married Li Yuanhao, who was made Commandant of the Imperial Son-in-law. In the first year of Chongxi, Xia envoys came to congratulate. Li Dezhao died; his son Li Yuanhao was invested King of Xia. In the second year they offered tribute. In the twelfth month, private trade in gold and iron by Xia envoys along the road was banned. In the seventh year they presented tribute. Li Yuanhao quarreled with Princess Ping; when she died, Northern Chancellery Attendant Yelü Shucheng was sent to investigate. In the ninth year, Song dispatched Guo Zhen to announce their campaign against Xia. In the tenth year, Xia offered captured Song commanders and prisoners. In the eleventh year, envoys inquired of Song why it had mobilized against Xia. In the twelfth month, Tuyuhun horse sales to Xia were banned and border barriers were erected. In the first month of the twelfth year, Yelü Dilie and Wang Weiji were dispatched to urge Xia and Song to reconcile. In the second month, Yuanhao sent envoys to congratulate the bestowal of an honorific title. Yelü Dilie and his party returned from Xia reporting that Yuanhao had halted fighting and notified Song. In the fourth month, Xia envoys brought horses and camels. In the seventh month, Yuanhao petitioned to attack Song; the request was refused. In the tenth month, Xia raided the Dangxiang; Gao Jianu of the Yanchang Palace was sent to rebuke them. In the fourth month of the thirteenth year, the Dangxiang and Western Mountain Commissioner Qu Lie led five tribes into Xia; troops from all circuits were ordered to suppress them. In the sixth month, Zubu chief Wuba sent his son with the captured Xia relief envoy Woyigai. In the eighth month, a Xia envoy answered insincerely and was held. When another envoy came and gave false answers, he was beaten. In the tenth month, Yuanhao apologized and offered to round up the rebels; the court agreed. In the twelfth month, Huzhao came back and Xia again sent tribute.

十七年，元昊薨，其子諒祚遣使來告，上其父遺留物。 鐵不得國乞以本部軍助攻夏國，不許。 十八年，復議伐夏，留其賀正使不遣，遣北院樞密副使蕭惟信以伐夏告宋。 六月，夏國遣使來貢，留之。 七月，親征。 八月，渡河，夏人遁。 九月，蕭惠為夏人所敗。 十月，招討使耶律敵古率阻卜軍至賀蘭山，獲元昊妻及其官屬。 遇其軍三千來拒，殪之； 詳穩蕭慈氏奴、南克耶律斡裏歿於陣。 十九年正月，遣使問罪於夏。 夏將窪普等攻金肅城，耶律高家奴等破之，窪普被創遁去，殺猥貨乙靈紀。 三月，殿前都點檢蕭叠裏得與夏軍戰於三角川，敗之。 招討使蕭蒲奴、北院大王宜新等帥師伐夏，都部署別古得為監戰。 五月，蕭蒲奴等入夏境，不遇敵，縱軍俘掠而還。 夏國窪普來降。 十月，李諒祚母遣使乞依舊稱臣。 十二月，諒祚上表如母訓。 二十年二月，遣使索党項叛戶。 五月，蕭爻括使夏回，進諒祚母表，乞代党項權進馬駝牛羊等物； 又求唐隆鎮，仍乞罷所建城邑。 以詔答之。 六月，獲元昊妻，及俘到夏人置於蘇州。 二十一年十月，諒祚遣使乞弛邊備，遣爻括賫詔諭之。 二十二年七月，諒祚進降表，遣林牙高家奴賫詔撫諭。 二十三年正月，貢方物。 五月，乞進馬駝，詔歲貢之。 七月，諒祚遣使求婚。 十月，進誓表。 二十四年，興宗崩，遣使報哀於夏。

論曰：高麗、西夏之事遼，雖嘗請婚下嫁，烏足以得其固誌哉？ 三韓接壤，反覆易知； 涼州負遠，納叛侵疆。 乘隙輒動，貢使方往，事釁隨生。 興師問罪，屢煩親征，取勝固多，敗亦貽悔。 昔吳趙咨對魏之言曰：「大國有征伐之兵，小國有備禦之固。 」豈其然乎！ 先王柔遠，以德而不以力，尚矣。 遼亡，求援二國，雖能出師，豈金敵哉！
Commentary: Though Goryeo and Western Xia sometimes sought Liao brides, marriage alone could not bind their true loyalties? Goryeo lay adjacent to Liao, its reversals plain to see; Xia held distant Liangzhou, sheltering defectors and raiding the border. They struck at every chance; no sooner had tribute envoys departed than trouble broke out. Campaigns to punish them drew the emperors repeatedly to the field; wins were frequent, but defeats left lasting regret. Long ago Zhao Zi of Wu told Wei: "Great powers wage war; small states hold fast in defense." Was he not right! The ancient kings won the far frontier through virtue, not force—how exalted a policy. At Liao's fall they sought help from Goryeo and Xia; armies could march, but none could stand against the Jin!
