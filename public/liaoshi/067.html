◎外戚表漢外戚有新室之患，晉宗室有八王之難。 遼史耶律、蕭氏十居八九，宗室、外戚，勢分力敵，相為唇齒，以翰邦家。 是或一道。 然以是而興，亦以是而亡，又其法之弊也。 契丹外戚，其先曰二審密氏、曰拔里、曰乙室已。 至遼太祖，娶述律氏。 述律，本回鶻糯思之後。 大同元年，太宗自汴將還，留外戚小漢為汴州節度使，賜姓名曰蕭翰，以從中國之俗，由是拔裏、乙室已、述律三族皆為蕭姓。 拔裏二房，曰大父、小父； 乙室已亦二房，曰大翁、小翁； 世宗以舅氏塔列葛為國舅別部。 三族世預北宰相之選，自太祖神冊二年命阿骨只始也。 聖宗合拔裏、乙室已二國舅帳為一，與別部為二。 此遼外戚之始末也。 作《外戚表》。 外戚表
◎Families of Imperial Consorts Table. Han history warns of the danger consort families posed when Wang Mang seized power; Jin history records the catastrophe of the Rebellion of the Eight Princes. In the Liao annals the Yelü and Xiao families account for nearly all high officeholders; the imperial lineage and the empresses' kin were evenly matched in strength and acted as mutual safeguards of the dynasty. This may have been one workable approach to statecraft. Yet what raised them also destroyed them—a defect inherent in the system. The Khitan empresses' kin traced their origins to the Two Shimi clans, the Bali, and the Yishi. Taizu of Liao took a wife from the Shulü clan. The Shulü were originally descended from the Uyghur chieftain Nusi. In the first year of Datong (947), as Emperor Taizong prepared to withdraw from Kaifeng, he left the consort kinsman Xiaohan as military governor of Bianzhou and bestowed on him the Chinese-style name Xiao Han; thereafter the Bali, Yishi, and Shulü lineages all adopted the surname Xiao in keeping with Chinese custom. The Bali were divided into two houses, the Great Father and Little Father lines; the Yishi likewise into the Great Elder and Little Elder lines; Emperor Shizong established his uncle Taliege as head of a separate Imperial Uncle division. All three clans held hereditary claim to the office of Northern Chancellor from Shence 2 (917), when Taizu first appointed Agu Zhi. Emperor Shengzong consolidated the Bali and Yishi imperial-uncle households into a single registry, leaving two divisions when the Separate Division is counted. Such is the full account of how the Liao empresses' kin took shape. Hence the compilation of the Families of Imperial Consorts Table. Families of Imperial Consorts Table
