道宗四
Daozong, Part Four
2
五年春正月壬申，如混同江。 癸酉，賜宰相耶律孝傑名仁傑。 乙亥，如山榆澱。 三月辛未，以宰相仁傑獲頭鵝，加侍中。 壬辰，以北院樞密使魏王耶律乙辛知南院大王事，加於越，知北院樞密使事耶律霂為北院樞密使，北院樞密副使耶律特裏底知北院樞密使事，左夷離畢耶律世遷同知北院樞密使事。 夏四月己未，如納葛濼。 五月丁亥，謁慶陵。 以契丹行宮都部署耶律燕哥為南府宰相，北面林牙耶律永寧為夷離畢，同知南院樞密使事蕭撻不也及殿前副點檢、駙馬都尉蕭酬斡並封蘭陵郡王。 六月辛亥，阻卜來貢。 丁巳，以北府宰相、遼西郡王蕭餘里也為西北路招討使。 己未，遣使隸囚。 是月，放進士劉瓘等百一十三人。 秋七月己卯，獵夾山。 八月庚申，命有司撰《太宗神功碑》，立於南京。 九月己卯，詔諸路毋禁僧徒開壇。 壬午，禁扈從擾民。 冬十月戊戌，夏國遣使來貢。 己亥，駐蹕獨盧金。 壬子，詔惟皇子仍一字王，餘並削降。 丁巳，振平州貧民。 己未，以趙王楊績為遼西郡王，魏王耶律乙辛降封混同郡王，吳王蕭韓家奴蘭陵郡王，致仕。 十一月丁丑，召沙門守道開壇於內殿。 癸未，復南京流民差役三年，被火之家免租稅一年。 十二月丙午，彗星犯尾。 乙卯，幸西京。 戊午，行再生禮，赦雜犯死罪以下。
In the fifth year, on renshen day of the first spring month the court went to the Huntong River. On guiyou day chancellor Yelü Xiaojie was granted the name Renjie. On yihai day the court went to Shanyu Marsh. On xinwei day of the third month, because Chancellor Renjie obtained a swan's head, he was promoted Palace Attendant. On renchen day Northern Court privy commissioner Prince of Wei Yelü Yixin was made to administer Southern Court great king affairs and promoted yuyue; Yelü Li, who administered Northern Court privy affairs, was made Northern Court privy commissioner; Northern Court associate privy commissioner Yelü Telidi was made administrator of Northern Court privy affairs; Left yilibi Yelü Shiqian was made associate Northern Court privy commissioner. On jiwei day of the fourth summer month the court went to Nage Marsh. On dinghai day of the fifth month the court paid respects at Qing Mausoleum. Khitan mobile palace deployment director Yelü Yange was made Southern Court chancellor; Northern linya Yelü Yongning was made yilibi; associate Southern Court privy commissioner Xiao Tabuye and Palace Front deputy commander and imperial son-in-law Xiao Chouwo were all enfeoffed Prince of Lanling Commandery. On xinhai day of the sixth month Zubu came with tribute. On dingsi day Northern Court chancellor and Prince of Liaoxi Xiao Yuliye was made Northwest Route pacification commissioner. On jiwei day envoys were dispatched to review prisoners. That month the jinshi Liu Jin and one hundred thirteen others were passed. On jimao day of the seventh autumn month the court hunted at Jia Mountain. On gengshen day of the eighth month the relevant offices were ordered to compose the 《Stele of Taizong's Divine Merit》 and erect it at Nanjing. On jimao day of the ninth month an edict ordered all routes not to forbid monks from opening altars. On renwu day escorts were forbidden to harass the people. On wuxu day of the tenth winter month Xia State sent envoys with tribute. On jihai day the court halted at Dulujin. On renzi day an edict ordered that only imperial sons might remain single-character princes; the rest were all demoted. On dingsi day poor people in Ping Prefecture were relieved. On jiwei day Prince of Zhao Yang Ji was made Prince of Liaoxi; Prince of Wei Yelü Yixin was demoted to Prince of Huntong Commandery; Prince of Wu Xiao Hanjianu Prince of Lanling Commandery, retired. On dingchou day of the eleventh month monk Shoudao was summoned to open an altar in the inner hall. On guimao day Nanjing refugees' corvée was remitted for three years; households that had suffered fire were exempt from rent and tax for one year. On bingwu day of the twelfth month a comet violated the Tail constellation. On yimao day the court visited the Western Capital. On wuwu day the rebirth ritual was performed; amnesty was granted for mixed offenses from capital crime downward.
3
六年春正月癸酉，如鴛鴦濼。 辛卯，耶律乙辛出知興中府事。 三月庚寅，封皇孫延禧為梁王，忠順軍節度使耶律頗德南院大王，耶律仲禧南院樞密使，戶部使陳毅參知政事。 夏四月乙卯，獵炭山。 五月壬申，免平州復業民租賦一年。 庚寅，以旱，禱雨，命左右以水相沃，俄而雨降。 六月戊戌，駐蹕納葛濼。 戊申，以度支使王績參知政事。 庚戌，女直遣使來貢。 秋七月戊辰，觀市。 癸未，為皇孫梁王延禧設旗鼓拽剌六人衛護之。 甲申，獵沙嶺。 九月壬寅，祠木葉山。 己酉，駐蹕藕絲澱。 冬十月己未朔，省同知廣德軍節度使事，命奉先軍節度使兼巡警乾、顯二州。 丁卯，耶律仁傑出為武定軍節度使。 庚午，參知政事劉詵致仕。 癸酉，以陳毅為漢人行宮都部署，王績同知樞密院事。 辛巳，回鶻遣使來貢。 十一月己丑朔，日有食之。 癸卯，召群臣議政。 十二月甲子，以耶律特裏底為孟父敞穩。 乙丑，以蕭撻不也為北府宰相，耶律世遷知北院樞密使事，耶律慎思同知北院樞密使事。 庚午，免西京流民租賦一年。 甲戌，減民賦。 丁亥，豫行正旦禮。 戊子，如混同江。
In the sixth year, on guiyou day of the first spring month the court went to Yuanyang Marsh. On xinmao day Yelü Yixin was dismissed as administrator of Xingzhong Prefecture. On gengyin day of the third month imperial grandson Yanxi was enfeoffed Prince of Liang; Zhongshun Army military commissioner Yelü Pode was made Southern Court great king; Yelü Zhongxi Southern Court privy commissioner; Household Commissioner Chen Yi Associate Administrator of Affairs. On yimao day of the fourth summer month the court hunted at Tanshan. On renshen day of the fifth month one year's rent and tax for Ping Prefecture households resuming cultivation was remitted. On gengyin day, because of drought, rain was prayed for; attendants were ordered to douse one another with water, and soon rain fell. On wuxu day of the sixth month the court halted at Nage Marsh. On wushen day Revenue Commissioner Wang Ji was made Associate Administrator of Affairs. On gengxu day Jurchen sent envoys with tribute. On wuchen day of the seventh autumn month the court viewed the market. On guimao day six drum-and-banner zhala guards were established to protect imperial grandson Prince of Liang Yanxi. On jiashen day the court hunted at Shaling. On renyin day of the ninth month sacrifice was made at Muye Mountain. On jiyou day the court halted at Ousi Marsh. On the jiwei new moon of the tenth winter month the post of associate Guangde Army military commissioner was abolished; Fengxian Army military commissioner was ordered also to patrol Qian and Xian prefectures. On dingmao day Yelü Renjie was dismissed as military commissioner of Wuding Army. On gengwu day Associate Administrator of Affairs Liu Shen retired. On guiyou day Chen Yi was made Han mobile palace deployment director; Wang Ji associate administrator of Privy Council affairs. On xinsi day Uighur sent envoys with tribute. On the jichou new moon of the eleventh month there was a solar eclipse. On guimao day ministers were summoned to discuss government. On jiazi day of the twelfth month Yelü Telidi was made Mengfu detail-stable. On yichou day Xiao Tabuye was made Northern Court chancellor; Yelü Shiqian was made administrator of Northern Court privy affairs; Yelü Shensi associate Northern Court privy commissioner. On gengwu day Western Capital refugees' rent and tax were remitted for one year. On jiaxu day the people's tax levies were reduced. On dinghai day New Year's rites were performed in advance. On wuzi day the court went to the Huntong River.
4
七年春正月戊申，五國部長來貢。 甲寅，女直貢良馬。 二月甲子，如魚兒濼。 夏五月壬子，駐蹕嶺西。 癸丑，有司奏永清、武清、固安三縣蝗。 甲寅，以蕭撻不也兼殿前都點檢，蕭酬斡為漢人行宮都部署兼知樞密院事。 六月甲子，詔月祭觀德殿，歲寒食，諸帝在時生辰及忌日，詣景宗御容殿致奠。 丙寅，阻卜余古赧來貢。 丁卯，以翰林學士王言敷參知政事，封北院宣徽使石篤漆水郡王。 秋七月戊子，如秋山。 丙申，謁慶陵。 八月丁卯，射鹿赤山，加圍場使涅葛為靜江軍節度使。 九月戊子，次懷州，命皇謁懷陵。 辛卯，次祖州，命皇后謁祖陵。 乙巳，駐蹕藕絲澱。 冬十月戊辰，以惕隱王九為南院大王，夷離畢奚抄只為彰國軍節度使。 十一月乙酉，詔歲出官錢，振諸宮分及邊戍貧戶。 丁亥，幸駙馬都尉蕭酬斡第，方飲，宰相梁穎諫曰：「天子不可飲人臣家。 」上即還宮。 己亥，高麗遣使來貢。 辛亥，除絹帛尺度狹短之令。 十二月丁卯，武定軍節度使耶律仁傑以罪削爵為民。 辛未，知興中府事耶律乙辛以罪囚於來州。
In the seventh year, on wushen day of the first spring month chieftains of the Five States came with tribute. On jiayin day Jurchen presented fine horses. On jiazi day of the second month the court went to Yuer Marsh. On renzi day of the fifth summer month the court halted west of the ridge. On guichou day the relevant offices memorialized locusts in Yongqing, Wuqing, and Gu'an counties. On jiayin day Xiao Tabuye was also Palace Front commander; Xiao Chouwo was made Han mobile palace deployment director and associate administrator of Privy Council affairs. On jiazi day of the sixth month an edict ordered monthly sacrifice at Guande Hall; on Cold Food each year, on birthdays and death days while emperors lived, proceed to Jingzong's imperial portrait hall to offer sacrifice. On bingyin day Zubu Yugunian came with tribute. On dingmao day Hanlin academician Wang Yanfu was made Associate Administrator of Affairs; Northern Court palace commissioner Shi Du was enfeoffed Prince of Qishui Commandery. On wuzi day of the seventh autumn month the court went to autumn mountains. On bingshen day the court paid respects at Qing Mausoleum. On dingmao day of the eighth month deer were shot at Red Mountain; hunting-grounds commissioner Niege was promoted military commissioner of Jingjiang Army. On wuzi day of the ninth month the court halted at Huai Prefecture; the crown prince was ordered to pay respects at Huai Mausoleum. On xinmao day the court halted at Zu Prefecture; the Empress was ordered to pay respects at the ancestral mausoleum. On yisi day the court halted at Ousi Marsh. On wuchen day of the tenth winter month tiyin Wangjiu was made Southern Court great king; yilibi Xi Chaozhi military commissioner of Zhangguo Army. On yiyou day of the eleventh month an edict ordered yearly disbursement of official funds to relieve poor households of various palace divisions and border garrisons. On dinghai day the court visited imperial son-in-law Xiao Chouwo's residence; while drinking, Chancellor Liang Ying admonished: "The Son of Heaven must not drink in a subject's house. " The Emperor immediately returned to the palace. On jihai day Goryeo sent envoys with tribute. On xinhai day the regulation on narrow silk and cloth measures was abolished. On dingmao day of the twelfth month Wuding Army military commissioner Yelü Renjie, for an offense, was stripped of rank and made a commoner. On xinwei day Xingzhong Prefecture administrator Yelü Yixin, for an offense, was imprisoned at Laizhou.
5
八年春正月甲申，如混同江。 丁酉，鐵驪、五國諸長各貢方物。 二月戊午，如山榆澱。 辛酉，詔北、南院官，凡給驛者，必先奏聞。 貢新及奏獄訟，方許馳驛，餘並禁之。 己巳，夏國獲宋將張天一，遣使來獻。 壬申，以耶律頗德為南府宰相兼知北院樞密使，燕哥為惕隱，蕭撻不也兼知契丹行宮都部署事。 三月庚戌，黃龍府女直部長術乃率部民內附，予官，賜印綬。 是月，詔行黍所定升斗。 夏四月壬戌，以耶律世遷為上京留守。 六月辛亥朔，駐蹕納葛濼。 丙辰，夏國遣使來貢。 丁巳，以耶律頗德為北院樞密使，耶律巢哥南府宰相，劉筠南院樞密使，蕭撻不也兼知北院樞密使事，王績漢人行宮都部署，蕭酬斡國舅詳穩。 乙丑，阻卜長來貢。 丙子，以耶律慎思知右夷離畢事。 秋七月甲午，如秋山。 南京霖雨，沙河溢永清、歸義、新城、安次、武清、香河六縣，傷稼。 九月庚寅，謁慶陵。 丁未，駐蹕藕絲澱。 大風雪，牛馬多死，賜扈從官以下衣馬有差。 冬十月乙卯，詔化哥傅導梁王延禧，加金吾衛大將軍。 丙子，謁乾陵。 十一月壬午，以乙室大王蕭何葛為南院宣徽使。 權知奚六部大王事圖趕為本部大王。 十二月癸丑，烏古敵烈統軍使耶律馬五為北院大王。 庚申，降皇后為惠妃，出居乾陵。
In the eighth year, on jiashen day of the first spring month the court went to the Huntong River. On dingyou day chiefs of Tieli and the Five States each presented local products. On wuwu day of the second month the court went to Shanyu Marsh. On xinyou day an edict ordered Northern and Southern Court officials: whoever used post stations must first memorialize. Only when presenting tribute of new products or memorializing criminal cases might they ride post relays; the rest were all forbidden. On jisi day Xia State captured Song general Zhang Tianyi and sent envoys to present him. On renshen day Yelü Pode was made Southern Court chancellor and also administrator of Northern Court privy affairs; Yange tiyin; Xiao Tabuye also administrator of Khitan mobile palace deployment affairs. On gengxu day of the third month Huanglong Prefecture Jurchen tribal chief Sunai led his tribesmen to submit; office was granted and seals were bestowed. That month an edict ordered use of the bushel and peck Taixing had fixed. On renxu day of the fourth summer month Yelü Shiqian was made supreme capital regent. On the xinhai new moon of the sixth month the court halted at Nage Marsh. On bingchen day Xia State sent envoys with tribute. On dingsi day Yelü Pode was made Northern Court privy commissioner; Yelü Chaoge Southern Court chancellor; Liu Yun Southern Court privy commissioner; Xiao Tabuye also administrator of Northern Court privy affairs; Wang Ji Han mobile palace deployment director; Xiao Chouwo imperial maternal uncle detail-stable. On yichou day Zubu chiefs came with tribute. On bingzi day Yelü Shensi was made administrator of Right yilibi affairs. On jiawu day of the seventh autumn month the court went to autumn mountains. Nanjing had continuous rain; the Sha River overflowed Yongqing, Guiyi, Xincheng, Anci, Wuqing, and Xianghe six counties and injured the crops. On gengyin day of the ninth month the court paid respects at Qing Mausoleum. On dingwei day the court halted at Ousi Marsh. Great wind and snow fell; many cattle and horses died; clothing and horses were bestowed on escort officials and below by degree. On yimao day of the tenth winter month an edict ordered Huage to tutor Prince of Liang Yanxi; Defender of the Golden Crow Commandant was promoted grand general. On bingzi day the court paid respects at Qian Mausoleum. On renwu day of the eleventh month Yishi great king Xiao Hege was made Southern Court palace commissioner. Acting administrator of Xi Six Tribes great king affairs Tugan was made great king of that tribe. On guichou day of the twelfth month Wugu Dilie supreme commander Yelü Mawu was made Northern Court great king. On gengshen day the Empress was demoted to Consort Hui and sent to dwell at Qian Mausoleum.
6
九年春正月辛巳，如春水。 夏四月丙午朔，大雪，平地丈餘，馬死者十六七。 五月，如黑嶺。 六月己未，駐蹕散水原。 甲子，以耶律阿思為契丹行宮都部署，耶律慎思北院樞密副使。 庚午，詔諸路檢括脫戶，罪至死者，原之。 閏月丁丑，以漢人行宮副部署可汗奴為南院大王。 戊寅，追謚庶人浚為昭懷太子。 丁亥，阻卜來貢。 己丑，以知興中府事邢熙年為漢人行宮都部署，漢人行宮都部署王績為南院樞密副使。 秋七月乙巳，獵馬尾山。 丁巳，謁慶陵。 癸亥，禁外官部內貸錢取息及使者館於民家。 八月，高麗王徽薨。 九月癸卯朔，日有食之。 己酉，射熊於白石山，加圍場使涅葛左金吾衛大將軍。 己巳，以高麗王徽子三韓國公勛權知國事。 辛未，五國部長來貢。 壬申，召北、南樞密院官議政事。 冬十月丁丑，謁觀德黔。 己卯，南院樞密使劉筠薨。 壬辰，混同郡王耶律乙辛謀亡入宋，伏誅。 十一月丙午，進封梁王延禧為燕國王，大赦。 以南院宣徽使蕭何葛為南府宰相，三司使王經參知政事兼知樞密事。 甲寅，詔僧善知讎校高麗所進佛經，頒行之。 己未，定諸令史、譯史遷敘等級。 十二月丁亥，以邢熙年知南院樞密使事。 辛卯，以王言敷為漢人行宮都部署。 高麗三韓國公王勛薨。 是年，御前放進士李君裕等五十一人。
In the ninth year, on xinsi day of the first spring month the court went to spring waters. On the bingwu new moon of the fourth summer month heavy snow fell more than a zhang deep on level ground; sixty or seventy percent of horses died. In the fifth month the court went to Heiling. On jiwei day of the sixth month the court halted at Sanshui Plain. On jiazi day Yelü Asi was made Khitan mobile palace deployment director; Yelü Shensi Northern Court associate privy commissioner. On gengwu day an edict ordered all routes to register escaped households; offenses reaching death were pardoned. In the intercalary month, on dingchou day, Han mobile palace deputy deployment director Kehannu was made Southern Court great king. On wuyin day the commoner Jun was posthumously honored as Crown Prince Zhaohuai. On dinghai day Zubu came with tribute. On jichou day Xingzhong Prefecture administrator Xing Xinian was made Han mobile palace deployment director; Han mobile palace deployment director Wang Ji Southern Court associate privy commissioner. On yisi day of the seventh autumn month the court hunted at Mawei Mountain. On dingsi day the court paid respects at Qing Mausoleum. On guihai day outer officials were forbidden to lend money at interest within their jurisdictions and envoys were forbidden to lodge in commoners' houses. In the eighth month Goryeo king Hui died. On the guimao new moon of the ninth month there was a solar eclipse. On jiyou day a bear was shot at Baishi Mountain; hunting-grounds commissioner Niege was promoted Left Defender of the Golden Crow Commandant grand general. On jisi day Goryeo king Hui's son Duke of Three Han Xun was ordered to administer state affairs provisionally. On xinwei day chieftains of the Five States came with tribute. On renshen day Northern and Southern Privy Council officials were summoned to discuss government. On dingchou day of the tenth winter month the court paid respects at Guande Hall. On jimao day Southern Court privy commissioner Liu Yun died. On renchen day Prince of Huntong Commandery Yelü Yixin plotted to flee into Song and was executed. On bingwu day of the eleventh month Prince of Liang Yanxi was advanced to Prince of Yan and a general amnesty was proclaimed. Southern Court palace commissioner Xiao Hege was made Southern Court chancellor; Three Departments commissioner Wang Jing Associate Administrator of Affairs and administrator of privy affairs. On jiayin day an edict ordered monk Shanzhi to collate Buddhist scriptures presented by Goryeo and promulgate them. On jiwei day promotion grades were fixed for various clerks and translators. On dinghai day of the twelfth month Xing Xinian was made to administer Southern Court privy affairs. On xinmao day Wang Yanfu was made Han mobile palace deployment director. Goryeo Duke of Three Han Wang Xun died. That year before the throne the jinshi Li Junyu and fifty-one others were passed.
7
十年春正月辛丑朔，如春水。 丙午，復建南京奉福寺浮圖。 戊辰，如山榆澱。 二月庚午朔，萌古國遣使來聘。 三月戊申，遠萌古國遣使來聘。 丁巳，命知制誥王師儒、牌印郎君耶律固傅導燕國王延禧。 夏四月丁丑，女直貢良馬。 五月壬戌，駐蹕散水原。 乙丑，阻卜來貢。 丙寅，降國舅詳穩班位在敞穩之下。 六月壬辰，禁毀銅錢為器。 秋七月甲辰，如黑嶺。 九月癸亥，駐蹕藕絲澱。 冬十二月乙未，改慶州大安軍曰興平。 是月，改明年為大安，赦雜犯死罪以下。
In the tenth year, on the xinchou new moon of the first spring month the court went to spring waters. On bingwu day the pagoda of Nanjing's Fengfu Temple was rebuilt. On wuchen day the court went to Shanyu Marsh. On the gengwu new moon of the second month Menggu State sent envoys with a friendly mission. On wushen day of the third month distant Menggu State sent envoys with a friendly mission. On dingsi day drafting edict commissioner Wang Shiru and seal-and-tally langjun Yelü Gu were ordered to tutor Prince of Yan Yanxi. On dingchou day of the fourth summer month Jurchen presented fine horses. On renxu day of the fifth month the court halted at Sanshui Plain. On yichou day Zubu came with tribute. On bingyin day imperial maternal uncle detail-stable rank was demoted to below detail-stable. On renchen day of the sixth month destroying copper cash to make vessels was forbidden. On jiachen day of the seventh autumn month the court went to Heiling. On guihai day of the ninth month the court halted at Ousi Marsh. On yiwei day of the twelfth winter month Qing Prefecture's Da'an Army was changed to Xingping. That month the coming year was changed to Da'an and amnesty was granted for mixed offenses from capital crime downward.
8
大安元年春正月丁酉，如混同江。 癸卯，王績知南院樞密使事，邢熙年為中京留守。 戊申，以樞密直學士杜公謂參知政事。 庚戌，五國酋長來貢良馬。 二月辛未，如山榆澱。 夏四月乙酉，宋主頊殂，子煦嗣位，使來告哀。 辛卯，西幸。 六月戊辰，駐蹕拖古烈。 壬申，以王績為南府宰相，蕭撻不也兼知南院樞密使事。 丁丑，遣使吊祭於宋。 戊寅，宋遣王真、甄佑等饋其先帝遺物。 秋七月乙巳，遣使賀宋主即位。 戊午，獵於赤山。 八月丁卯，幸慶州。 戊辰，謁慶陵。 冬十月癸亥，駐蹕好草澱。 戊辰，夏國王李秉常遣使報其母梁氏哀。 甲申，以蕭撻不也為南院樞密使。 十一月乙未，詔「比者外官因譽進秩，久而不調，民被其害。 今後皆以資給遷轉。 」丁酉，以南女直詳穩蕭袍裏為北府宰相。 辛亥，史臣進太祖以下七帝《實錄》。 丙辰，遣使冊三韓國公王勛弟運為高麗國王。 己未，詔僧尼無故不得赴闕。 十二月甲戌，宋遣蔡卞來謝吊祭。
In the first year of Da'an, on dingyou day of the first spring month the court went to the Huntong River. On guimao day Wang Ji was made administrator of Southern Court privy affairs; Xing Xinian regent of the Central Capital. On wushen day Privy Council academician Du Gongyu was made Associate Administrator of Affairs. On gengxu day chieftains of the Five States came with tribute of fine horses. On xinwei day of the second month the court went to Shanyu Marsh. On yiyou day of the fourth summer month Song ruler Xiang died; his son Xu succeeded; envoys came to announce the mourning. On xinmao day the court traveled west. On wuchen day of the sixth month the court halted at Tuogulie. On renshen day Wang Ji was made Southern Court chancellor; Xiao Tabuye also administrator of Southern Court privy affairs. On dingchou day envoys were dispatched to offer condolences in Song. On wuyin day Song sent Wang Zhen, Zhen You, and others presenting the late emperor's bequeathed goods. On yisi day of the seventh autumn month envoys were dispatched to congratulate the Song ruler on his accession. On wuwu day the court hunted at Red Mountain. On dingmao day of the eighth month the court visited Qing Prefecture. On wuchen day the court paid respects at Qing Mausoleum. On guihai day of the tenth winter month the court halted at Haocao Marsh. On wuchen day Xia State king Li Bingchang sent envoys to announce his mother Lady Liang's mourning. On jiashen day Xiao Tabuye was made Southern Court privy commissioner. On yiwei day of the eleventh month an edict said: "Lately outer officials have advanced in rank through praise and long go without transfer, harming the people. Henceforth all are to be transferred according to seniority of service." On dingyou day Southern Jurchen detail-stable Xiao Paoli was made Northern Court chancellor. On xinhai day historians presented the 《Veritable Records》 of the seven emperors from Taizu downward. On bingchen day envoys were dispatched to invest Duke of Three Han Wang Xun's younger brother Yun as king of Goryeo. On jiwei day an edict ordered monks and nuns not to come to court without cause. On jiaxu day of the twelfth month Song sent Cai Bian to thank for the condolence mission.
9
二年春正月辛卯，如混同江。 己酉，五國諸部長來貢。 癸丑，召權翰林學士趙孝嚴、知制誥王師儒等講《五經》大義。 二月癸酉，駐蹕山榆澱。 是月，太白犯歲星。 三月乙酉，女直貢良馬。 夏四月戊戌，北幸。 癸丑，遣使加統軍使蕭訛都斡太子太保，裨將老古金吾衛大將軍，蕭雅哥靜江軍節度使，耶律燕奴右監門衛大將軍，仍賜賚諸軍士。 五月丁巳朔，以牧馬蕃息多至百萬，賞群牧官，以次進階。 乙亥，駐蹕納葛濼。 戊寅，宰相梁穎出知興中府事。 是月，放進士張轂等二十六人。 六月丁亥朔，以左夷離畢耶律坦為惕隱，知樞密院事耶律斡特剌兼知左夷離畢事。 丙申，阻卜來朝。 癸卯，遣使按諸路獄。 甲辰，以同知南京留守事耶律那也知右夷離畢事。 乙巳，阻卜酋長余古赧及愛的來朝，詔燕國王延禧相結為友。 戊申，以契丹行宮都部署耶律阿思兼知北院大王事。 壬子，高墩以下、縣令、錄事兄弟及子，悉許敘用。 秋七月丁巳，惠妃母燕國夫人削古以厭魅梁王事覺，伏誅，子蘭陵郡王蕭酬斡除名，置邊郡，仍隸興聖宮。 戊午，獵沙嶺。 甲子，賜興聖、積慶二宮貧民錢。 乙酉，出粟振遼州貧民。 八月戊子，以雪罷獵。 九月庚午，還上京。 壬申，發粟振上京、中京貧民。 丙子，謁二儀、五鸞二殿。 己卯，出太祖、太宗所禦鎧仗示燕國王延禧，諭以創業征伐之難。 辛巳，召南府宰相議國政。 冬十月乙酉朔，以樞密副使竇景庸知樞密院事。 丙戌，五國部長來貢。 丁亥，以夏國王李秉常薨，遣使詔其子乾順知國事。 十一月甲戌，為燕國王延禧行再生禮，曲赦上京囚。 戊寅，高麗遣使謝封冊。 癸未，出粟振乾、顯、成、懿四州貧民。 十二月辛卯，以蘭陵郡王蕭撻不也為南院樞密使。 己亥，夏國王李乾順遣使上其父遺物。
In the second year, on xinmao day of the first spring month the court went to the Huntong River. On jiyou day chieftains of various Five States tribes came with tribute. On guichou day acting Hanlin academician Zhao Xiaoyan, drafting edict commissioner Wang Shiru, and others were summoned to lecture on the great meaning of the Five Classics. On guiyou day of the second month the court halted at Shanyu Marsh. That month Venus violated Jupiter. On yiyou day of the third month Jurchen presented fine horses. On wuxu day of the fourth summer month the court traveled north. On guichou day envoys were dispatched to promote supreme commander Xiao Eduwo to Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, deputy general Laogu Defender of the Golden Crow Commandant grand general, Xiao Yage military commissioner of Jingjiang Army, Yelü Yannu Right Defender of the Gate Commandant grand general, and still bestow rewards on the troops. On the dingsi new moon of the fifth month, because herds had multiplied to as many as a million, herd officials were rewarded and advanced in rank by degree. On yihai day the court halted at Nage Marsh. On wuyin day Chancellor Liang Ying was dismissed as administrator of Xingzhong Prefecture. That month the jinshi Zhang Gu and twenty-six others were passed. On the dinghai new moon of the sixth month Left yilibi Yelü Tan was made tiyin; Yelü Wotela, who administered Privy Council affairs, also administrator of Left yilibi affairs. On bingshen day Zubu came to court. On guimao day envoys were dispatched to investigate prisons in all routes. On jiachen day associate regent of Nanjing Yelü Naye was made administrator of Right yilibi affairs. On yisi day Zubu chiefs Yugunian and Aide came to court; an edict ordered Prince of Yan Yanxi to bond with them as friends. On wushen day Khitan mobile palace deployment director Yelü Asi was also made administrator of Northern Court great king affairs. On renzi day Gaodun and below, county magistrates, and record clerks' brothers and sons were all permitted to enter service by seniority. On dingsi day of the seventh autumn month Consort Hui's mother Lady Yan of Yan State Xiaogu, because sorcery against Prince of Liang was exposed, was executed; her son Prince of Lanling Commandery Xiao Chouwo was struck from the register and placed in a border commandery, still attached to Xing Sheng Palace. On wuwu day the court hunted at Shaling. On jiazi day money was bestowed on poor people of Xing Sheng and Jiqing palaces. On yiyou day grain was issued to relieve poor people in Liao Prefecture. On wuzi day of the eighth month hunting was stopped because of snow. On gengwu day of the ninth month the court returned to the Supreme Capital. On renshen day grain was issued to relieve poor people at the Supreme and Central capitals. On bingzi day the court paid respects at the Two Principles and Five Phoenix halls. On jimao day armor and weapons used by Taizu and Taizong were displayed to Prince of Yan Yanxi, instructing him on the hardship of founding and campaigning. On xinsi day the Southern Court chancellor was summoned to discuss state affairs. On the yiyou new moon of the tenth winter month associate privy commissioner Dou Jingyong was made administrator of Privy Council affairs. On bingxu day chieftains of the Five States came with tribute. On dinghai day, because Xia State king Li Bingchang died, envoys were dispatched to inform his son Qianshun to administer state affairs. On jiaxu day of the eleventh month the rebirth ritual was performed for Prince of Yan Yanxi; partial amnesty for prisoners at the Supreme Capital. On wuyin day Goryeo sent envoys to thank for the investiture. On guimao day grain was issued to relieve poor people in Qian, Xian, Cheng, and Yi prefectures. On xinmao day of the twelfth month Prince of Lanling Commandery Xiao Tabuye was made Southern Court privy commissioner. On jihai day Xia State king Li Qianshun sent envoys presenting his father's bequeathed goods.
