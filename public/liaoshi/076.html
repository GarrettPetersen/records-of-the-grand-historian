Yelu Jieli, Yelu Balide, Yelu Shuogu, Yelu Lubugu, Zhao Yanshou, Gao Mohan, Zhao Siwen, Yelu Oulisi, and Zhang Li
2
耶律解裏，字潑單，突呂不部人，世為小吏。 解裏早隸太宗麾下，擢為軍校。 天顯間，唐攻定州，既陷，解裏為唐兵所獲。 晉高祖立，始歸國。 太宗貰其罪，拜御史大夫。 會同九年伐晉，師次滹沱河，奪中渡橋，降其將杜重威。 上命解裏與降將張彥澤率騎兵三千疾趨河南，所至無敢當其鋒。 既入汴，解裏等遷晉主重貴於開封府。 彥澤恣殺掠，亂宮掖，解裏不能禁，百姓騷然，莫不怨憤。 車駕至京，數彥澤罪，斬於市，汴人大悅。 解裏亦被詰責，尋釋之。 天祿間，加守太子太傅。 應歷初，置本部令穩，解裏世其職，卒。
Yelu Jieli, whose courtesy name was Podan, came from the Tulubu tribe; for generations his family had held low clerical posts. Jieli entered Taizong's service while still young and was eventually promoted to the rank of military instructor. In the Tianxian period, when Tang forces attacked and captured Dingzhou, Jieli was taken prisoner. Only after Emperor Gaozu of Jin came to the throne did he return to Liao. Taizong forgave his lapse and appointed him Censor-in-Chief. In the ninth year of Huitong, on the campaign against Jin, the army encamped at the Hutuo River, took the Zhongdu Bridge, and compelled the surrender of the Jin commander Du Chongwei. The emperor ordered Jieli and the surrendered general Zhang Yanze to ride south into Henan at the head of three thousand cavalry; nowhere along their route did anyone dare stand against them. Once they had entered Bian, Jieli and his colleagues transferred the Jin emperor Chonggui to the Kaifeng prefectural residence. Yanze ran wild with slaughter and looting and threw the inner palace into chaos; Jieli was unable to stop him, and the populace seethed with universal outrage. When the emperor arrived in the capital, he recited Yanze's crimes and had him beheaded in the market; the people of Bian rejoiced. Jieli was rebuked as well, though he was released shortly afterward. In the Tianlu period he was additionally appointed Guardian Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent. Early in the Yingli reign the post of tribal commander Wen was created; Jieli inherited the office in succession and died while serving.
3
耶律拔裏得，字孩鄰，太祖弟剌葛之子。 太宗即位，以親愛見任。 會同七年，討石重貴，拔裏得進圍德州，下之，擒刺史師居璠等二十七人。 九年，再舉兵，次滹沱河，降杜重威，戰功居多。 太宗入汴，以功授安國軍節度使，總領河北道事。 師還，州郡往往叛，以應劉知遠，拔裏得不能守而歸。 世宗即位，遷中京留守，卒。
Yelu Balide, whose courtesy name was Hailin, was the son of Taizu's younger brother Yelu Dilie. When Taizong came to the throne, Balide won his trust through their close family bond. In the seventh year of Huitong, on the expedition against Shi Chonggui, Balide pressed the siege of Dezhou, captured the city, and took the prefect Shi Jufan and twenty-seven others prisoner. In the ninth year he marched again, encamped at the Hutuo River, and helped bring about Du Chongwei's surrender; among the generals his share of the fighting was the greatest. After Taizong entered Bian, Balide was appointed Military Governor of Anguo in recognition of his service and placed in overall charge of the Hebei circuit. On the army's withdrawal, one prefecture after another rose in revolt to answer Liu Zhiyuan; Balide could not maintain his position and withdrew. After Shizong's accession he was transferred to serve as garrison commander of the Central Capital, where he died.
4
耶律魯不古，字信寧，太祖從侄也。 初，太祖制契丹國字，魯不古以贊成功，授林牙、監修國史。 後率偏師，為西南邊大詳穩，從伐党項有功。 會河東節度使石敬瑭為其主所討，遣人求援，魯不古導送於朝，如其請。 帝親率師往援，魯不古從擊唐將張敬達於太原北，敗之。 會同初，從討党項，俘獲最諸將，師還。 天祿中，拜於越。 六年，為北院大王。 終年五十五。
Yelu Lubugu, whose courtesy name was Xinning, was a younger cousin of Taizu on the paternal line. When Taizu first created the Khitan national script, Lubugu helped bring the project to completion and was appointed chancellor and supervisor of the compilation of the national history. He later commanded a detached force as grand commander of the southwestern frontier and distinguished himself in campaigns against the Tanguts. When the Hedong military governor Shi Jingtang was attacked by his own ruler and sent envoys to plead for help, Lubugu escorted them to court and presented their request, which the throne approved. The emperor marched in person to relieve him; Lubugu took part in the attack on the Tang commander Zhang Jingda north of Taiyuan and routed his army. Early in the Huitong era he joined the expedition against the Tanguts and took more prisoners and booty than any other commander before the army withdrew. In the Tianlu period he received the title of Yuyue. In the sixth year he was made Great King of the Northern Court. He died at the age of fifty-five.

趙延壽，本姓劉，恒山人。 父邟，令蓚。 梁開平初，滄州節度使劉守文陷蓚，其稗將趙德鈞獲延壽，養以為子。 少美容貌，好書史。 唐明宗先以女妻之，及即位，封其女為興平公主，拜延壽駙馬都尉、樞密使。 明宗子從榮恃權跋扈，內外莫不震懾，延壽求補外避之，出為宣武軍節度使。 清泰初，加魯國公，復為樞密使，鎮許州。 石敬瑭發兵太原，唐遣張敬達往討。 會敬達敗保晉安寨，延壽與德鈞往救，聞晉安已破，走團柏峪。 太宗追及，延壽與其父俱降。 明年，德鈞卒，以延壽為幽州節度使，封燕王。 及改幽州為南京，遷留守，總山南事。 天顯末，以延壽妻在晉，詔取之以歸。 自是益自激昂圖報。 會同初，帝幸其第，加政事令。 六年冬，晉人背盟，帝親征，延壽為先鋒，下貝州，授魏、博等州節度使，封魏王。 敗晉軍於南樂，獲其將賽項羽。 軍元城，晉將李守貞、高行周率兵來逆，破之。 至頓丘，會大霖雨，帝欲班師，延壽諫曰：「晉軍屯河濱，不敢出戰，若徑入澶州，奪其橋，則晉不足平。 」上然之。 適晉軍先歸澶州，高行周至析城，延壽將輕兵逆戰； 上親督騎士突其陣，敵遂潰。 師還，留延壽徇貝、冀、深三州。 八年，再伐晉，晉主遣延壽族人趙行實以書來招。 時晉人堅壁不出，延壽紿曰：「我陷虜久，寧忘父母之邦？ 若以軍逆，我即歸。 」晉人以為然，遣杜重威率兵迎之。 延壽至滹沱河，據中渡橋，與晉軍力戰，手殺其將王清，兩軍相拒。 太宗潛由他渡濟，留延壽與耶律朔古據橋，敵不能奪，屢敗之，杜重威掃厥眾降。 上喜，賜延壽龍鳳赭袍，且曰：「漢兵皆爾所有，爾宜親往撫慰。 」延壽至營，杜重威、李守貞迎謁馬首。
Zhao Yanshou, whose original surname was Liu, came from Hengshan. His father Kuang had served as magistrate of Shuo. Early in the Liang Kaiping era, when the military governor of Cangzhou, Liu Shouwen, overran Shuo, his lieutenant Zhao Dejun took Yanshou prisoner and adopted him as a son. As a youth he was striking in appearance and devoted to books and history. Emperor Mingzong of Tang had already given him a daughter in marriage; once on the throne Mingzong ennobled the princess as Xingping and appointed Yanshou Imperial Son-in-Law Commandant and Commissioner of Military Affairs. Mingzong's son Congrong abused his power and terrorized the court; Yanshou sought a provincial post to escape him and was sent out as military governor of Xuanwu. Early in the Qingtai reign he was ennobled as Duke of Lu, returned to the post of Commissioner of Military Affairs, and took up his command at Xuzhou. When Shi Jingtang raised an army at Taiyuan, Tang dispatched Zhang Jingda to suppress him. When Zhang Jingda was beaten back and bottled up at Jin'an Stockade, Yanshou and Dejun marched to relieve him; learning that Jin'an had already fallen, they retreated toward Tuanbai Valley. Taizong caught up with them, and Yanshou surrendered together with his father. The following year, after Dejun's death, Yanshou was appointed military governor of Youzhou and enfeoffed as Prince of Yan. When Youzhou was redesignated the Southern Capital, he was made its garrison commander with authority over all affairs south of the mountains. Late in the Tianxian era, because Yanshou's wife remained in Jin territory, an edict was issued to have her brought back to him. From that time on he burned with still greater zeal to repay the favor shown him. Early in Huitong the emperor visited his home and additionally appointed him Chief of Government Affairs. In the winter of the sixth year, when Jin broke the treaty, the emperor marched in person with Yanshou in the vanguard; Yanshou took Beizhou, was made military governor of Wei, Bo, and neighboring prefectures, and was enfeoffed as Prince of Wei. He routed the Jin army at Nanle and took their general Sai Xiangyu prisoner. Encamped at Yuancheng, he met and broke the Jin forces led by Li Shouzhen and Gao Xingzhou. At Dunqiu a torrential rain fell, and the emperor wanted to turn the army back. Yanshou urged him: "The Jin forces are camped along the river and dare not offer battle; if we strike straight for Chunzhou and seize its bridge, Jin will not be difficult to subdue." " The emperor agreed. By then the Jin army had already withdrawn to Chunzhou; when Gao Xingzhou reached Xicheng, Yanshou led light troops forward to give battle; the emperor himself directed the cavalry in a charge on their line, and the enemy broke and fled. On the army's withdrawal, Yanshou was left behind to secure the prefectures of Bei, Ji, and Shen. In the eighth year, on the second expedition against Jin, the Jin emperor sent Yanshou's kinsman Zhao Xingshi with a letter inviting him to defect. The Jin forces were holding their defenses and refusing battle; Yanshou feigned to them: "I have been captive among the Khitan so long—would I forget the land of my fathers? Send an army to receive me, and I will come back at once." " The Jin took him at his word and dispatched Du Chongwei with an army to welcome him. Yanshou reached the Hutuo River, seized the Zhongdu Bridge, and fought the Jin army head on; he personally killed their commander Wang Qing, and the two sides remained locked in combat. Taizong forded the river by another crossing in secret, leaving Yanshou and Yelu Shuogu to hold the bridge; the enemy could not take it, and after repeated defeats Du Chongwei surrendered with his entire force. Delighted, the emperor granted Yanshou a dragon-and-phoenix scarlet robe and said: "All these Han soldiers are now under your command; you should go in person to reassure them." " When Yanshou arrived at the camp, Du Chongwei and Li Shouzhen came out to pay their respects at his stirrup.

高模翰，一名松，渤海人。 有膂力，善騎射，好談兵。 初，太祖平渤海，模翰避地高麗，王妻以女。 因罪亡歸。 坐使酒殺人下獄，太祖知其才，貰之。 天顯十一年七月，唐遣張敬達、楊光遠帥師五十萬攻太原，勢銳甚。 石敬瑭遣人求救，太宗許之。 九月，征兵出雁門，模翰與敬達軍接戰，敗之，太原圍解。 敬瑭夜出謁帝，約為父子。 帝召模翰等賜以酒饌，親饗士卒，士氣益振。 翌日，復戰，又敗之。 敬達鼠竄晉安寨，模翰獻俘於帝。 會敬瑭自立為晉帝，光遠斬敬達以降，諸州悉下。 上諭模翰曰：「朕自起兵，百餘戰，卿功第一，雖古名將無以加。 」乃授上將軍。 會同元年，冊禮告成，宴百官及諸國使於二儀殿。 帝指模翰曰：「此國之勇將，朕統一天下，斯人之力也。 」群臣皆稱萬歲。 及晉叛盟，出師南伐。 模翰為統軍副使，與僧遏前驅，拔赤城，破德、貝諸寨。 是冬，兼總左右鐵鷂子軍，下關南城邑數十。 三月，敕虎官楊覃赴乾寧軍，為滄州節度使田武名所圍，模翰與趙延壽聚議往救。 俄有光自模翰目中出，縈繞旗矛，焰焰如流星久之。 模翰喜曰：「此天贊之祥！ 」遂進兵，殺獲甚眾。 以功加侍中。 略地鹽山，破饒安，晉人震怖，不敢接戰。 加太傅。 晉以魏府節度使杜重威領兵三十萬來拒，模翰謂左右曰：「軍法在正不在多。 以多陵少，不義必敗。 其晉之謂乎！ 」詰旦，以麾下三百人逆戰，殺其先鋒梁漢璋，餘兵敗走。 手詔褒美，比漢之李陵。 頃之，杜重威等復至滹沱河，帝召模翰問計。 上善其言曰：「諸將莫及此。 」乃令模翰守中渡橋。 及戰，復敗之，上曰：「朕憑高觀兩軍之勢，顧卿英銳無敵，如鷹逐雉兔。 當圖形麟閣，爵貤後裔。 」已而杜重威等降。 車駕入汴，加特進檢校太師，封悊郡開國公，賜璽書、劍器。 為汴州巡檢使，平汜水諸山土賊，遷鎮中京。 天祿二年，加開府儀同三司，賜對衣、鞍勒、名馬。 應歷初，召為中臺省右相。 至東京，父老歡迎曰：「公起戎行，致身富貴，為鄉里榮，相如、買臣輩不足過也。 」九年正月，遷左相，卒。
Gao Mohan, who also went by the name Song, was a native of Bohai. Powerfully built, he was a superb horseman and archer and loved to talk of war. When Taizu first conquered Bohai, Mohan fled to Goryeo, where the king gave him a princess in marriage. After committing an offense he fled home again. He was thrown into prison for killing a man while drunk; Taizu, recognizing his ability, pardoned him. In the seventh month of the eleventh year of Tianxian, Tang dispatched Zhang Jingda and Yang Guangyuan with an army said to number five hundred thousand against Taiyuan; their advance was ferocious. Shi Jingtang sent envoys to plead for aid, and Taizong agreed to march. In the ninth month the army marched out through Yanmen Pass; Mohan met Zhang Jingda in battle, routed him, and lifted the siege of Taiyuan. That night Jingtang came out to pay homage to the emperor and agreed to enter a father-and-son relationship with him. The emperor summoned Mohan and the other commanders, treated them to food and wine, and personally entertained the troops, whose spirits soared. The next day they fought again and routed the Tang army a second time. Zhang Jingda fled in disarray to Jin'an Stockade, and Mohan presented prisoners to the emperor. When Jingtang proclaimed himself emperor of Jin, Yang Guangyuan killed Zhang Jingda and surrendered, and the prefectures fell one after another. The emperor told Mohan: "Since I first took the field, in more than a hundred battles your merit has stood first; not even the great commanders of old could surpass it." " He then appointed him General-in-Chief. In the first year of Huitong, when the investiture ceremonies were complete, he held a banquet for the officials and the envoys of foreign states in the Hall of Two Principles. The emperor pointed to Mohan and said: "Here is the bravest general of the realm; in uniting the empire under my rule, it is to this man that I owe the most." " The assembled ministers all cried "Long live the emperor!" When Jin violated the treaty, the army marched south on campaign. Mohan served as deputy army commander; advancing with Senge as vanguard, he captured Chicheng and overran the fortified camps of De and Bei. That winter he also took command of the left and right Iron Hawk corps and reduced several dozen towns south of the pass. In the third month the Tiger Guard officer Yang Tan was posted to Qianning Army, where he was besieged by the military governor of Cangzhou, Tian Wuming; Mohan and Zhao Yanshou held council and agreed to march to his relief. Suddenly a light shot from Mohan's eyes, wound about the banners and spears, and flared like a meteor for some time. Mohan exclaimed with joy: "This is Heaven's blessing upon us!" " He then pressed forward, killing and capturing a great many of the enemy. For this service he was additionally appointed Palace Attendant. He swept through Yanshan, took Rao'an, and the Jin were so terrified that they dared not offer battle. He was promoted to Grand Tutor. Jin sent the military governor of Wei, Du Chongwei, with an army said to number three hundred thousand to oppose him; Mohan told his attendants: "The art of war rests on justice, not on numbers. To overwhelm the few with the many is unjust and bound to end in defeat. Is that not precisely Jin's situation?" " At dawn the next day he met them with three hundred men of his own command, killed their vanguard Liang Hanzhang, and put the rest to flight. The emperor wrote him a personal commendation comparing him to Li Ling of Han. Before long Du Chongwei and his forces again reached the Hutuo River, and the emperor called Mohan in to ask his counsel. The emperor approved his plan and said: "None of the other commanders can equal this." " He then ordered Mohan to defend the Zhongdu Bridge. In the fighting that followed he routed them again; the emperor said: "From the heights I watched the two armies contend; your fierce brilliance was unmatched—it was like a hawk stooping on pheasant and hare. Your portrait should hang in the Qilin Pavilion, and ennoblement should pass to your descendants." " Soon afterward Du Chongwei and his command surrendered. When the emperor entered Bian, Mohan was promoted to Special Advancement and Acting Grand Preceptor, enfeoffed as Duke of Te with state-founding rank, and granted an imperial letter under seal together with a ceremonial sword. He was made inspector of Bianzhou, suppressed the mountain bandits around Sishui, and was then transferred to command the garrison at the Central Capital. In the second year of Tianlu he was additionally appointed Grand General with Imperial Scion Ceremonies and presented with matching robes, saddle and bridle, and a fine horse. Early in the Yingli reign he was recalled to serve as Right Chancellor of the Central Secretariat. On reaching the Eastern Capital, the local elders came out to welcome him, saying: "You rose from the ranks to wealth and high office and have brought glory to our home district—even figures like Sima Xiangru and Zhu Maichen could hardly outdo you." " In the first month of the ninth year he was moved to Left Chancellor and died in office.

趙思溫，字文美，盧龍人。 少果銳，膂力兼人，隸燕帥劉仁恭幕。 李存勖問罪於燕，思溫統偏師拒之。 流矢中目，裂裳漬血，戰猶不已。 為存勖將周德威所擒，存勖壯而釋其縛。 久之，日見信用。 與梁戰於莘縣，以驍勇聞，授平州刺史，兼平、營、薊三州都指揮使。 神冊二年，太祖遣大將經略燕地，思溫來降。 及伐渤海，以思溫為漢軍都團練使，力戰，拔扶餘城。 身被數創，太祖親為調藥。 太宗即位，以功擢檢校太保、保靜軍節度使。 天顯十一年，唐兵攻太原，石敬瑭遣使求救，上命思溫自嵐、憲間出兵援之。 既罷兵，改南京留守、盧龍軍節度使、管內觀察處置等使、開府儀同三司，兼侍中，賜協謀靜亂翊聖功臣，尋改臨海軍節度使。 會同初，從耶律牒摐使晉行冊禮，還，加檢校太師。 二年，有星隕於庭，卒。 上遣使賻祭，贈太師、魏國公。 子延照、延靖，官至使相。
Zhao Siwen, whose courtesy name was Wenmei, came from Lulong. As a youth he was bold and decisive, with strength beyond that of ordinary men, and entered the staff of the Yan commander Liu Rengong. When Li Cunxu marched against Yan, Siwen led a detached force to oppose him. A stray arrow struck his eye; he ripped his garment, blood soaking through, yet he would not stop fighting. Zhou Dewei, a commander under Cunxu, took him prisoner; impressed by his courage, Cunxu had his bonds cut. In time he won increasing trust. In battle with Liang at Shen county he won fame for fierce courage and was made prefect of Pingzhou and overall commander of Ping, Ying, and Ji. In the second year of Shence, when Taizu dispatched a senior commander to secure the Yan region, Siwen defected to Liao. On the expedition against Bohai he was appointed commander-in-chief of the Han forces; after hard fighting he took Fuyu. He suffered several wounds, and Taizu personally prepared medicine for him. After Taizong's accession he was promoted for his service to Acting Grand Guardian and military governor of Baojing. In the eleventh year of Tianxian, when Tang forces besieged Taiyuan, Shi Jingtang sent envoys to plead for aid; the emperor ordered Siwen to march from the Lan and Xian region to relieve the city. When the campaign ended he was appointed garrison commander of the Southern Capital, military governor of Lulong, and commissioner for observation and disposition within the circuit, with the additional titles of Grand General with Imperial Scion Ceremonies and Palace Attendant, and was granted the honorific of meritorious minister who assists in planning to pacify disorder; he was soon transferred to military governor of Linhai. Early in Huitong he accompanied Yelu Dieceng on the mission to Jin to perform the enthronement ceremonies; on his return he was additionally appointed Acting Grand Preceptor. In the second year a meteor fell in his courtyard, and he died. The emperor sent envoys with funeral offerings and posthumously enfeoffed him as Grand Preceptor and Duke of Wei. His sons Yanzhao and Yanjing both rose to commissioner-chancellor rank.

耶律漚裏思，六院夷離堇蒲古只之後。 負勇略，每戰被重鎧，揮鐵槊，所向披靡。 會同間，伐晉，上至河而獵，適海東青鶻搏雉，晉人隔水以鴿引去。 上顧左右曰：「誰為我得此人？ 」漚裏思請內廄馬，濟河擒之，並殺救者數人還。 上大悅，優加賞賚。 既而晉將杜重威逆於望都，據水勒戰。 漚裏思介馬突陣，余軍繼之。 被圍，眾言陣薄處可出，漚裏思曰：「恐彼有他備。 」竟引兵沖堅而出； 回視眾所指，皆大塹也。 其料敵多此類。 是年，總領敵烈皮室軍，坐私免部曲，奪官，卒。
Yelu Oulisi was descended from Pugu, the Yelü chieftain of the Six Courts. Endowed with courage and tactical skill, he went into battle in heavy armor, swinging an iron spear, and swept aside all who stood before him. During the Huitong campaigns against Jin, the emperor came to the river on a hunting excursion; a gyrfalcon from the eastern sea stooped on a pheasant, and men of Jin across the water lured the bird away with a carrier pigeon. The emperor turned to his attendants and asked: "Who will bring that man to me?" " Oulisi asked for a horse from the imperial stables, forded the river, seized the man, killed several of those who tried to rescue him, and returned. The emperor was delighted and heaped rewards upon him. Soon afterward the Jin commander Du Chongwei met them at Wangdu, taking position along the water and forcing a battle. Oulisi, in full armor on horseback, smashed into the enemy line, and the rest of the army followed. When they were surrounded, his men pointed to a thin spot in the enemy line as a way out, but Oulisi said: "They may have another trap laid there." " In the end he drove his men straight through the strongest part of the line and broke out; and when he looked back at the spot his men had indicated, he saw that it was lined with deep trenches. His judgment of the enemy was often of this kind. That year, while commanding the Dilie Pishi army, he was deprived of his post for privately dismissing men from his command and died shortly afterward.

張礪，磁州人，初仕唐為掌書記，遷翰林學士。 會石敬瑭起兵，唐主以礪為招討判官，從趙德鈞援張敬達於河東。 及敬達敗，礪入契丹。 後太宗見礪剛直有文彩，擢翰林學士。 礪臨事必盡言，無所避，上益重之。 未幾，謀亡歸，為追騎所獲。 上責曰：「汝何故亡？ 」礪對曰：「臣不習北方土俗，飲食居處，意常郁郁，以是亡耳。 」上顧通事高彥英曰：「朕嘗戒汝善遇此人，何乃使失所而亡？ 礪去，可再得耶？ 」遂杖彥英而謝礪。 會同初，升翰林承旨，兼吏部尚書，從太宗伐晉。 入汴，諸將蕭翰、耶律郎五、麻答輩肆殺掠，礪奏曰：「今大遼始得中國，宜以中國人治之，不可專用國人及左右近習。 茍政令乖失，則人心不服，雖得之亦將失之。 」上不聽。 改右僕射，兼門下侍郎、平章事。 頃之，車駕北還，至欒城崩。 時礪在恒州，蕭翰與麻答以兵圍其第。 礪方臥病，出見之。 翰數之曰：「汝何故於先帝言國人不可為節度使？ 我以國舅之親，有征伐功，先帝留我守汴，以為宣武軍節度使，汝獨以為不可。 又譖我與解裏好掠人財物子女。 今必殺汝！ 」趣令鎖之。 礪抗聲曰：「此國家大體，安危所系，吾實言之。 欲殺即殺，奚以鎖為？ 」麻答以礪大臣，不可專殺，乃救止之。 是夕，礪恚憤卒。
Zhang Li, a native of Cizhou, first served Tang as chief secretary and was later promoted to Hanlin academician. When Shi Jingtang rebelled, the Tang emperor appointed Li staff judge on the punitive campaign, and he accompanied Zhao Dejun to relieve Zhang Jingda in Hedong. After Jingda's defeat, Li went over to the Khitan. Later Taizong, seeing that Li was upright and accomplished in letters, appointed him Hanlin academician. In office Li spoke his mind fully and held nothing back, and the emperor came to esteem him still more. Before long he plotted to escape and return home, but was caught by pursuing horsemen. The emperor reproached him: "Why did you run away?" " Li answered: "I am unused to northern ways of eating, drinking, and living; I was constantly depressed, and that is why I fled." " The emperor turned to the interpreter Gao Yan'ying and said: "I once told you to treat this man well—how did you let him become so miserable that he fled? If Li were lost, could we ever find his like again?" " He then had Yan'ying beaten and apologized to Li. Early in Huitong he was promoted to chief Hanlin academician and concurrent minister of personnel, and accompanied Taizong on the campaign against Jin. After entering Bian, generals such as Xiao Han, Yelu Langwu, and Mada ran wild with killing and looting; Li submitted a memorial saying: "Now that Great Liao has only just taken possession of China, China should be governed by Chinese; the court cannot rely solely on Khitan and on those close at hand." If government and orders go wrong, the people will not submit; even what has been won will soon be lost again. " The emperor would not listen. He was appointed Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs, with concurrent posts as Vice Director of the Chancellery and Grand Councilor. Before long the emperor turned north; at Luancheng he died. Li was then at Hengzhou; Xiao Han and Mada surrounded his house with soldiers. Though Li was ill in bed, he went out to face them. Han denounced him: "Why did you tell the late emperor that Khitan could not be made military governors?" I am the emperor's uncle by marriage and have earned merit in war; the late emperor left me to guard Bian and made me military governor of Xuanwu—you alone said that was wrong. You also accused me and Jieli of plundering people's goods and taking their women. Today I will kill you!" " He ordered him bound at once. Li answered boldly: "This touches the fundamental interests of the state and its safety—I spoke only the truth. If you mean to kill me, then kill me—why bother to bind me?" " Mada, holding that a senior minister could not be killed on a private grudge, intervened and stopped him. That night Li died of grief and anger.

論曰：初，晉因遼之兵而得天下，故兼臣禮而父事之，割地以為壽，輸帛以為貢。 未久也，而會同之師次滹沱矣。 豈群帥貪功黷武而致然歟？ 抑所謂信不由衷也哉？ 模翰以功名自終，可謂良將。 若延壽之勛雖著，至於覬覦儲位，謬矣，利令智昏，固無足議。 若乃成末釁以虧俊功，如解裏者，何譏焉！
The commentators say: At first Jin had won the empire with Liao's armies; it therefore observed the rites of a subject while treating Liao as a father, ceding territory as a gift and sending silk as tribute. Before long, the armies of Huitong were encamped at the Hutuo River. Was this because the commanders, greedy for glory, abused military force? Or was it, as the saying goes, that their pledge of faith never came from the heart? Mohan ended his career in honor and may truly be called a fine general. Yanshou's achievements were great, but in coveting the throne he was deluded; gain had blinded his judgment, and he is hardly worth further discussion. As for letting a late misstep tarnish a fine record, as with Jieli—what is there to blame?!
