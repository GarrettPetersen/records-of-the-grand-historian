遼有五京。 上京為皇都，凡朝官、京官皆有之； 余四京隨宜設官，為制不一。 大抵西京多邊防官，南京、中京多財賦官。 五京並置者，列陳之； 特置者，分列於後。
Liao maintained five capitals. Shangjing served as the imperial capital, with the full range of court and capital posts; The other four capitals appointed offices as needed, under varying schemes. Generally Xijing emphasized frontier posts, while Nanjing and Zhongjing emphasized fiscal posts. Posts found in all five capitals are listed together; Capital-specific posts appear in separate sections below.

遼東西、燕，秦、漢、唐已置郡縣，設官職矣。 高麗、渤海因之。 至遼，五京列峙、包括燕、代，悉為畿甸。 二百餘年，城郭相望，田野益辟。 冠以節度，承以觀察、防禦、團練等使，分以刺史、縣令，大略采用唐制。 其間宗室、外戚、大臣之家，築城賜額，謂之「頭下州軍」； 唯節度使朝廷命之，後往往皆歸王府。 不能州者謂之軍，不能縣者謂之城，不能城者謂之堡。 其設官則末詳雲。
Liaodong, Yan, and the Qin, Han, and Tang realms had long had prefectures, counties, and officials. Goryeo and Bohai followed suit. Under Liao the five capitals stood in a row, embracing Yan and Dai as the metropolitan heartland. For over two centuries walled towns lined the landscape and farmland spread. Military commissioners headed circuits, observation, defense, and training commissioners followed, and cishi and magistrates filled the counties—largely on the Tang model. Imperial kin, in-laws, and great ministers also founded walled estates with granted names—the "head-below prefectures and armies"; only military commissioners were court-appointed, but in time most posts fell to princely households. Units too small for a prefecture became armies; too small for a county became cities; too small for a city became forts. Their specific offices are not recorded here.

三皇、五帝寬柔之化，澤及漢、唐。 好生惡殺，習與性成。 雖五代極亂，習於戰鬥者才幾人耳。 宋以文勝，然遼之邊防猶重於南面，直以其地大民眾故耳。 卒之親仁善鄰，桴鼓不鳴幾二百年。 此遼之所以為美也歟。
The ancient sage-kings were gentle; their grace extended through Han and Tang. Love of life and hatred of killing were second nature. Even Five Dynasties chaos produced few habitual warriors. Song won by civil culture, but Liao still stressed the southern frontier because of its size and population. Finally, benevolence and good neighbors kept the drums quiet for nearly two centuries. That was Liao's glory.
