二年春正月己卯，御製《奎章閣記》。 行樞密臣言：「十一月，仁德府權達魯花赤曲術，糾集兵眾以討雲南，首敗伯忽賊兵於馬龍州，以是月十一日殺伯忽弟拜延，獻馘於豫王。 十三日。 戰於馬金山，獲伯忽及其弟伯顏察兒、其黨拜不花、卜顏帖木兒等十餘人，誅之，余兵皆潰，獨祿余猶據金沙江。 」有旨趣進兵討之。 庚辰，住持大承天護聖寺僧寶峰加司徒。 辛巳，大名魏縣民曹革輸粟賑陜西饑，旌其門。 癸未，立侍正府以總近侍，秩從二品。 乙酉，時享太廟。 丙戌，伯顏、月魯帖木兒、月璐不花、阿卜海牙等十四人，並以本官兼侍正。 旌大都大興縣郭仲安妻李氏貞節。 丁亥，以壽安山英宗所建寺未成，詔中書省給鈔十萬錠供其費，仍命燕鐵木兒、撒迪等總督其工役。 命後衛指揮使史塤往四川行省調軍官選。 戊子，命奴都赤阿裏火者按行北邊牧地。 以晉邸部民劉元良等二萬四千餘戶隸壽安山大昭孝寺為永業戶。 中書省臣言：「四川省臣塔出、脫帖木兒等討雲南，以十一月九日領兵至烏撒周泥驛。 明日，祿余、阿奴、阿答等賊兵萬餘，自山後間道潛出。 塔出、脫帖木兒等進擊，屢戰敗之。 十五日，又戰七星關，六日凡十七戰，賊大敗潰去。 」詔遣使以銀、幣賞塔出、脫帖木兒等。 造歲額鈔本至元鈔八十九萬五十錠，中統鈔五千錠。 給鈔五千錠，賑寧海州饑民。 罷益都等處廣農提舉司，改立田賦總管府，秩從三品，仍令隆祥總管府統之。 命興和路建燕鐵木兒鷹棚。 樞密院臣言：「四川行省地鄰烏撒，而雲南未平，今戍卒單少，宜增兵防遏。 請調夔路怯憐口戶丁七百、重慶河東五路兩營兵三百，同往戍之。 俟征進軍還日，悉罷遣。 」從之。 庚寅，改東路蒙古軍萬戶府為東路蒙古侍衛親軍指揮使司。 諸王哈兒蠻遣使來貢蒲萄酒。 國制，累朝行帳設衛士、給事如在位時。 近嘗汰其冗濫，武宗、仁宗兩朝，各定為八百人，英宗七百人。 中書省臣言，舊給事人有失職者，詔復其百人。 辛卯，皇太子阿剌忒納答剌薨。 壬辰，命宮相法裏及給事者五十八人護靈輿北祔葬於山陵，仍令法裏等守之。 御史臺臣劾奏：「福建宣慰副使哈只，前為廣東廉訪副使，貪汙狼籍，宜罷黜。 」從之。 己亥，遣吏部尚書撒裏瓦，佩虎符，禮部郎中趙期頤，佩金符，賫即位詔告安南國，且賜以《授時曆》。 賜武寧王徹徹禿金百兩、銀五百兩，以淮安路之海寧州為其食邑。 癸卯，以皇子古納答剌疹疾愈，賜燕鐵木兒及公主察吉兒各金百兩、銀五百兩、鈔二千錠，撒敦等金、銀、鈔各有差； 又賜醫巫、乳媼、宦官、衛士六百人金三百五十兩、銀三千四百兩、鈔五千三百四十錠。 甲辰，敕每歲四祭五福太一星。 建孔子廟於後衛。 至元末，討諸王乃顏之叛，獲其部蒙古軍，分置河南、江浙、湖廣、江西諸省，命樞密院遣使括其數，得二千六百人。 乙巳，封蒙古巫者所奉神為靈感昭應護國忠順王，號其廟曰靈祐。 給衛士萬人歲例鈔，人八十錠，內以他物及粟折五之一。 鎮西武靖王搠思班、豫王阿剌忒納失裏及行省、行院官同討雲南，兵十餘萬，以去年十一月十一日，搠思班師次羅羅斯，期躍裏鐵木兒俱至三泊郎，仍趣小雲失會於曲靖馬龍等州，同進兵。 躍裏鐵木兒倍道兼進，奪金沙江。 十二月十七日，大兵與阿禾蒙古軍相值，戰敗之，阿禾偽降，明日，率其兵三千為三隊來襲我營，搠思班、躍裏鐵木兒等分十三隊又擊敗之，阿禾竄走，大兵直趨中慶。 二十六日，遇賊黨蒙古軍於安寧州，與再戰，又大敗之。 二十八日，阿禾來逆戰，遂就禽，斬於軍前。 三十日，將抵中慶，賊兵七千猶拒戰於伽橋、古壁口，兵交，躍裏鐵木兒左頰中流矢，洞耳後，拔矢復與戰，大捷，遂復行省治。 諸軍皆會，駐於城中，分兵追捕殘賊於嵩明州。 樞密院臣以捷聞，詔總兵官量度緩急，從宜區處。 新添安撫司甕河寨主，訴他部徭、獠蹂其禾，民饑，命湖廣行省發鈔二千錠，市米賑之。
Second year of Zhishun, spring, first month, on jimao day, the Emperor personally composed the 《Record of the Kui Zhang Pavilion》. Mobile privy council officials said: "In the 11th month, acting Rendeh Prefecture darughachi Qushu gathered troops to campaign against Yunnan; he first defeated the rebel troops of Boqu at Malong Prefecture; on the 11th day of that month he killed Boqu's younger brother Bairan and presented heads to the Prince of Yu. On the 13th day. They fought at Majin Mountain, captured Boqu and his younger brother Boyan Tsaghur and more than 10 of his followers including Baibuhua and Boyan Temür, executed them; the remaining troops all fled; only Luchu still held the Jinsha River. " An edict ordered urgent advance of troops to suppress him. On gengchen day, Baofeng, abbot of the Great Cheng Tian Protecting the Sagely Monastery, was promoted to Grand Mentor. On xinsi day, Cao Ge of Wei County in Daming Prefecture contributed grain to relieve famine in Shaanxi; his household gate was honored. On guiwei day, the Directorate of Palace Attendance was established to oversee inner attendants, rank 2b. On yiyou day, the seasonal offering was performed at the Imperial Ancestral Temple. On bingxu day, Bayan, Yolu Temür, Yolu Buhua, Abu Haiya, and 14 others were all made to concurrently hold palace attendance posts with their existing offices. Li, wife of Guo Zhong'an of Daxing County in Dadu, was honored for chastity. On dinghai day, because the monastery Yingzong had built on Shou'an Mountain was unfinished, the central secretariat was ordered to supply 100,000 ingots of notes for its expenses, and Yan Temür, Sadi, and others were still ordered to oversee the labor. Rear Guard commander Shi Kan was ordered to go to the Sichuan Branch Secretariat to select military officers. On wuzi day, Nuduchi Aliqa was ordered to inspect grazing lands along the northern frontier. More than 24,000 households including Liu Yuanliang among the Jin estate's subject people were made perpetual-tenure households subordinate to the Shou'an Mountain Great Zhaoxiao Monastery. Central secretariat officials said: "Sichuan provincial officials Toghan and Toqtemür and others campaigned against Yunnan; on the 9th day of the 11th month they led troops to Wusa Zhouni Post Station. The next day, more than 10,000 rebel troops under Luchu, Anu, and Ada emerged by a hidden mountain path from behind the range. Toghan, Toqtemür, and others advanced to attack and repeatedly defeated them. On the 15th day they fought again at Qixing Pass; in 6 days there were 17 battles in all; the rebels were greatly defeated and fled. " An edict dispatched envoys bearing silver and silks to reward Toghan, Toqtemür, and the others. The year's quota of note reserves was minted: 890,050 ingots of Zhiyuan notes and 5,000 ingots of Zhongtong notes. 5,000 ingots of notes were granted to relieve the famished people of Ninghai Prefecture. The Yidu and elsewhere Broad Agriculture Intendancy was abolished and replaced with the Land-Tax Directorate-General, rank 3b, still placed under the Longxiang Directorate-General. Xinghe Route was ordered to build Yan Temür's hawking mews. Privy council officials said: "The Sichuan Branch Secretariat borders Wusa, yet Yunnan is not yet pacified; garrison troops are few and thin; troops should be increased for defense. We request transferring 700 qielian household service men from Kuizhou Route and 300 soldiers of the 2 battalions of Chongqing Hedong's 5 circuits to garrison there together. When the campaign troops return, all shall be dismissed and sent home. " It was approved. On gengyin day, the Eastern Route Mongol Army Wanhu Office was renamed the Eastern Route Mongol Palace Guard Command. Prince Kharmang dispatched envoys to present grape wine as tribute. By national regulation, successive reigns' traveling encampments had guards and attendants as when the sovereign was on the throne. Recently the redundant and excessive were cut; for the Wuzong and Renzong reigns each was fixed at 800 men, and for Yingzong 700 men. Central secretariat officials said that among the former attendants some had lost their posts; an edict restored 100 of them. On xinmao day, Crown Prince Alaqa Nadala died. On renchen day, 58 persons including palace steward Fali and attendants were ordered to escort the spirit carriage north for joint burial at the imperial tombs, and Fali and the others were still ordered to guard it. Surveillance censors memorialized: "Fujian Branch Secretariat associate commissioner Haji, formerly Guangdong surveillance vice commissioner, was greedy and corrupt beyond reckoning — he should be dismissed. " It was approved. On jihai day, Minister of Personnel Sarva, wearing a tiger tally, and Ministry of Rites bureau director Zhao Qiyi, wearing a gold tally, were dispatched bearing the accession proclamation to announce to Annan, and also granted the 《Season-Granting Calendar》. Prince of Wu Ning Cheche Tu was granted 100 taels of gold and 500 taels of silver, and Haining Prefecture in Huai'an Route was made his fief. On guimao day, because the rash illness of Prince Guna Nadala had healed, Yan Temür and Princess Chaghijir were each granted 100 taels of gold, 500 taels of silver, and 2,000 ingots of notes; Sadun and others received gold, silver, and notes in varying amounts; physicians, shamans, wet nurses, eunuchs, and guards — 600 persons in all — were also granted 350 taels of gold, 3,400 taels of silver, and 5,340 ingots of notes. On jiachen day, an edict ordered the 4 annual sacrifices to the Great Taiyi Star of the Five Blessings. A Confucius temple was built at the Rear Guard. At the end of the Zhiyuan era, campaigning against Prince Nayan's rebellion, Mongol troops of his following were captured and distributed among Henan, Jiang-Zhe, Huguang, and Jiangxi provinces; the privy council was ordered to dispatch envoys to register their numbers and found 2,600 men. On yisi day, the spirit worshipped by Mongol shamans was enfeoffed as King Linggan Zhaoying Huguo Zhongshun, and its temple was titled Lingyou. The guards' annual allotment of notes was granted — 80 ingots per man, one-fifth of which was taken in other goods and grain. Western Pacification Martial Tranquility King Chaghan Temür, Prince of Yu Alaqa Nasirli, and branch secretariat and branch privy council officials jointly campaigned against Yunnan with more than 100,000 troops; on the 11th day of the 11th month last year Chaghan Temür's army halted at Luoluo; they expected Yolqi Temür to arrive together at Sanbolang, and still urged Xiao Yunshi to meet at Qujing, Malong, and other prefectures to advance together. Yolqi Temür pressed forward by forced marches and seized the Jinsha River. On the 17th day of the 12th month the main army encountered Ahe's Mongol troops, defeated them, and Ahe feigned surrender; the next day he led 3,000 of his troops in 3 columns to raid Our camp; Chaghan Temür, Yolqi Temür, and others divided into 13 columns and again defeated them; Ahe fled in disorder; the main army pressed straight for Zhongqing. On the 26th day they met rebel Mongol troops at Anning Prefecture and fought again, again defeating them greatly. On the 28th day Ahe came to give battle and was thereupon captured and beheaded before the army. On the 30th day, as they were about to reach Zhongqing, 7,000 rebel troops still resisted at Qiqiao and Gubikou; when the armies clashed, Yolqi Temür was struck by an arrow in the left cheek that pierced behind the ear; he pulled out the arrow and fought again; there was a great victory, and the branch secretariat administration was restored. All armies assembled and encamped within the city; troops were divided to pursue remnant rebels at Songming Prefecture. Privy council officials reported the victory; an edict ordered the overall commanders to gauge urgency and ease and dispose as circumstances required. The chief of Wenghe Stockade in the Xintian Pacification Commission complained that other departments' corvée laborers and Liao people trampled his grain and the people were famished; Huguang Branch Secretariat was ordered to issue 2,000 ingots of notes to buy rice for relief.

二月丙戌，以上都留守乃馬臺行嶺北行樞密院事，太禧宗禋使謹只兒、答鄰答裏、篤烈捏四人並知院事，遙授平章政事。 戊申，立廣教總管府，以掌僧尼之政，凡十六所：曰京畿山後道，曰河東山右道，曰遼東山北道，曰河南荊北道，曰兩淮江北道，曰湖北湖南道，曰浙西江東道，曰浙東福建道，曰江西廣東道，曰廣西兩海道，曰燕南諸路，曰山東諸路，曰陜西諸路，曰甘肅諸路，曰四川諸路，曰雲南諸路。 秩正三品，府設達魯花赤、總管、同知府事、判官各一員，宣政院選流內官擬註以聞，總管則僧為之。 四川行省招諭懷德府驢谷什用等四洞及生蠻十二洞，皆內附，詔升懷德府為宣撫司以鎮之。 諸洞各設長官司及巡檢司，且命各還所掠生口。 湖廣參政徹裏帖木兒與速速、班丹俱坐出怨言，鞫問得實，刑部議當徹裏帖木兒、班丹杖一百七，速速處死，會赦，徹裏帖木兒流廣東，班丹廣西，速速徙海南，皆置荒僻州郡。 有旨：「此輩怨望於朕，向非赦原，俱當置之極刑，可俱籍其家，速速禁錮終身。 」己酉，白虹貫日。 旌鞏昌金州民杜祖隆妻張氏誌節。 樞密院臣言：「徹裏鐵木兒、孛羅以正月戊寅敗烏撒蠻兵，射中祿余，降其民，烏蒙、東川、易良州蠻兵、夷獠等俱款附。 鎮西武靖王搠思班等駐中慶，復行省事； 豫王阿剌忒納失裏等至當當驛，安輯其人民。 」又言：「澂江路蠻官郡容報賊古剌忽及禿堅之弟必剌都迷失等偽降於豫王而反圍之，至易龍驛，古剌忽等兵掩襲官軍。 四川行省平章塔出頓兵不進，平章乞住妻子孳畜為賊所掠。 諜知禿堅方修城堡，布兵拒守，無出降意。 」詔速進兵討之。 敕探馬赤軍士歲以五月十日遷處山後諸州。 辛亥，建燕鐵木兒居第於興聖宮之西南，詔撒迪及留守司董其役。 壬子，太白晝見。 中書平章政事亦列赤兼沈陽等路安撫使，燕王宮相伯撒裏為中書平章政事，陜西行臺中丞朵兒只班為中書參知政事，戶部尚書高履亨、兩淮都轉運鹽使許有壬並參議中書省事。 甲寅，燕鐵木兒言：「賽因怯列木丁，英宗時嘗獻寶貨於昭獻元聖太后，議給價鈔十二萬錠，故相拜住奏酬七萬錠，未給，泰定間以鹽引萬六百六十道折鈔給之，今有司以詔書奪之還官。 臣等議，以為寶貨太后既已用之，以鹽引還之為宜。 」從之。 燕鐵木兒又言：「安慶萬戶鎖住，坐令家人殺人系獄，久未款伏，宜若無罪，乞釋之。 」制曰：「可。 」乙卯，太白犯昴。 祀太祖、太宗、睿宗御容。 雲南統兵官來報捷，諸蠻悉降，唯祿余追捕未獲。 命番休各衛漢軍，十之二以三月一日放遣。 丁巳，駙馬不顏帖木兒自北邊從武寧王徹徹禿來朝。 己未，命西僧為皇子古納答剌作佛事一周歲。 壬戌，改封武寧王徹徹禿為郯王，賜以金印。 甲子，中書省臣言：「國家錢谷，歲入有額，而所費浩繁，是以不足。 天歷二年，嘗以鹽賦十分之一折銀納之，凡得銀二千餘錠。 今請以銀易官帑鈔本，給宿衛士卒。 」又言：「陛下不用經費，不勞人民，創建大承天護聖寺。 臣等願上向所易鈔本十萬錠、銀六百錠助建寺之需。 」從之。 丙寅，以太祖四大行帳世留朔方不遷者，其馬駝孳畜多死損，發鈔萬錠，命內史府市以給之。 行樞密院都事阿裏火者來報雲南之捷。 庚午，給宿衛士歲例鈔，詔毋出定額萬人之外。 占城國遣其臣高暗都剌來朝貢。 創建五福太一宮於京城乾隅，修上都洪禧、崇壽等殿。 諸王徹徹禿、沙哥坐妄言不道，詔安置徹徹禿廣州，沙哥雷州。 壬申，命遼陽行省發粟賑國王朵兒只及納忽答兒等六部蒙古軍民萬五千戶。 旌大都民劉德仁妻王氏貞節。 甲戌，給宣讓王王傅印。 荊王也速也不幹貢犛牛。 命田賦總管府稅礦銀輸大承天護聖寺。 命興和路為月璐不花作鷹棚。 雲南景東甸蠻官阿只弄遣子罕旺來朝，獻馴象，乞升甸為景東軍民府，阿只弄知府事，罕旺為千戶，常賦外歲增輸金五千兩、銀七百兩，許之。 以山東鹽課鈔萬錠，賑膠州饑。 命龍翊衛以屯田歲入粟贍衛卒孤貧者。 是月，深、冀二州有蟲食桑為災。
Second month, on bingxu day, Shangdu garrison commander Naimatai was made administrator of the Northern Pacification Mobile Privy Council; Taixi Zongyin commissioner Jinjir, Dalindari, and Doliniq were all made privy council administrators by remote appointment as associate grand councillors. On wushen day, the Broad Teaching Directorate-General was established to govern Buddhist and Daoist clergy; there were 16 jurisdictions in all: the Capital Region and Shanhou Circuit; Hedong and Shanxi Circuit; Liaodong and Shanbei Circuit; Henan and Jingbei Circuit; Liang-Huai and Jiangbei Circuit; Hubei and Hunan Circuit; Zhexi and Jiangdong Circuit; Zhedong and Fujian Circuit; Jiangxi and Guangdong Circuit; Guangxi and Lianghai Circuit; the various routes of Yan South; the various routes of Shandong; the various routes of Shaanxi; the various routes of Gansu; the various routes of Sichuan; and the various routes of Yunnan. Rank 3a; the directorate-general had 1 darughachi, director-general, associate prefect, and judge each; the Commission for Buddhist and Secular Affairs selected inner-circuit officials for nomination and report; the director-general was a monk. The Sichuan Branch Secretariat induced the submission of the 4 caves of Ligu Shiyong and elsewhere in Huaide Prefecture and 12 caves of raw barbarians; all came within the allegiance; an edict promoted Huaide Prefecture to a Pacification Commission to garrison them. Each cave was given a native-official prefecture and patrol office, and they were also ordered each to return persons they had seized. Huguang associate administrator Cheku Temür, Susu, and Bandan were all punished for uttering complaints; interrogation established the facts; the Ministry of Justice judged that Cheku Temür and Bandan should receive 107 blows of the staff and Susu should be executed; an amnesty intervened — Cheku Temür was exiled to Guangdong, Bandan to Guangxi, and Susu to Hainan — all placed in remote frontier prefectures. An edict said: "These men harbored resentment toward Us; had it not been for the amnesty, all should have been put to the extreme penalty; their households may all be registered and confiscated; Susu is to be imprisoned for life. " On jiyou day, a white rainbow crossed the sun. Zhang, wife of Du Zulong of Jin Prefecture in Gongchang, was honored for steadfast integrity. Privy council officials said: "Cheku Temür and Boluo on the first month, wuyin day, defeated Wusa barbarian troops, shot Luchu, and reduced his people; Wumeng, Dongchuan, Yiliang Prefecture barbarian troops, Yi, and Liao all came to submit. On xinhai day, the grand carriage returned south to Dadu. On renzi day, western-region prince Darimishi succeeded Dorjie Temür's position; Princes Borjijin Tai and others came to court with tribute. On jiayin day, Yolqi Temür and Mielun Yisun, grandson of Xuebietai, presented falcons; they were granted 100 taels of gold, 1,500 taels of silver, 500 ingots of notes, and 1 gold belt. The Tax Exaction Office was ordered not to collect commodity tax on commercial goods at Yan Temür's residence. The Ordos region had suffered disaster years; much livestock died; 17,160 households — the Inner Secretariat was ordered to give 20,000 ingots for relief. On yimao day, Venus infringed the great star of Xuanyuan. On bingchen day, inner scribe Qolaiqai was enfeoffed Duke of Feng State. Because of stellar anomalies, ministers were ordered to deliberate amnesty. On dingsi day, Prince of Bin Buyan Temür was ordered to hunt at Fuzhou. On jiwei day, the Pacification Prince chief directorate was established at Fuzhou. Princess Tuotuohui came to court. Weishi County, Bianliang Route was granted to Bayan as enfeoffment fief. An edict ordered the Ministry of Justice to try inner attendant Saribuhua for witchcraft; all who merited death received 107 strokes and exile to Guangdong and Guangxi. Central Secretariat officials said: "Next year's sea-transport grain is 2,400,000 shi — Jiang-Zhe has already been ordered to transport 2,200,000 and Henan 200,000. Now we request that Jiang-Zhe again increase by 200,000 — associate administrator Du Zhen is to supervise." It was approved. Relief-sale of 50,000 shi of rice was again ordered to aid the capital's poor. Wei Duo of Jining Route was honored for filial conduct; Lady Wei, wife of Lu Tianlin of Yangzhou Route, was honored for chastity. On gengshen day, Venus infringed the left horn of Xuanyuan. Central Secretariat and Privy Council officials said: "Western-region prince Busayin — his minister Qolaimuding forged the prince's command to court; Busayin dispatched an envoy requesting he be arrested and returned. We deliberated: in a feudatory state's affairs, seizing envoys traveling back and forth and handing them over is impermissible; they should take the relay post home to justify themselves." Imperial rescript approved. On renshen day, the Palace Attendance Directorate rank was raised to 2a. This month, Jiang-Zhe routes had floodwater that harmed grain — 188,738 qing of fields were counted. Jing Prefecture from the 6th month to this month had no rain. Li and Si prefectures and other counties — last year's flood — this year's rent was exempted. Yuan Prefecture had famine; 2,000 shi of relief-sale rice was distributed. Jin and Xihe prefectures — successive years of drought disaster; the people were starving — they were relieved with 5,000 ingots of Shaanxi salt-duty notes. Ninth month, on guiyou new moon, Arluqun Sari's residence was purchased; Yan Temür was ordered to lodge Prince Guna Nadala there. Central Secretariat officials said: "This year 148,400 horses and camels should be fed; the capital feeds 60,000; the remainder are assigned to outer prefectures for divided feeding — each is given 4 ingots as fodder-and-grain price." It was approved. On yihai day, the garrison command was ordered to mobilize soldiers to build a halting platform east of the Great Chengtian Huguo Temple. Censorate officials impeached: "Sichuan Branch Secretariat associate administrator Ma Yong — he issued 6,000 shi of grain to supply the Yunnan army but turned back midway; he borrowed 19 salary ingots in advance to take a concubine and also reviled grand councillor Wang Shouchang; though pardoned, he can hardly bear a chief ministerial post. " The Emperor said: "The norms of propriety and distinctions of noble and base — knowing nothing of them — how can one dwell above and oversee below! Dismiss him at once." " On bingzi day, Venus infringed Saturn. Privy Council officials said: "Dongchuan Route darughachi Puzhe'ernaju, joining Luchu's troops, killed Wusa Pacification Commissioner Yolqi and more than 20 of Dongchuan Route assistant prefect Jiaohuadu and others; again joining Boqu's nephew Afu, he led Mongol troops intending to strike Luoluo. We deliberated with Yan Temür and dispatched Western Regions commander Suozhu and others to mobilize Shaanxi Metropolitan ten-thousand-household troops straight to Luoluo, mobilized Diaomen Pacification Commission troops, crossed the Great River, and went straight to Qiongbo Prefecture to patrol passes. " An edict also ordered the Xuanzheng Court to dispatch envoys jointly to supervise. Hainan bandit Wang Zhou enticed 19 cave Li barbarians, more than 20,000 persons, into rebellion; Guangdong and Fujian troops were ordered transferred under Huguang Branch Secretariat left associate administrator Yila Sinu to command suppression and capture. New frontier garrisoners of Ashiq and Oros — Liaoyang Branch Secretariat was ordered to supply ox implements and provisions. On jimao day, 5,000 shi of grain was distributed to relieve the eagle factory of Xinghe Route. On gengchen day, privy council officials said: "In the 6th month, mobile privy council officials fought the Wusa rebel troops 5 times with troops and defeated them; only Luchu remains in hiding and has not been captured. The Sichuan Branch Secretariat was ordered to supply their military provisions. 2,000 shi of grain were distributed to relieve the famine people of Xinghe Baochang Prefecture. Censorate officials said: "Grand Sacrificial Director Yan Jixiang of the Great Sage Longevity Ten Thousand Peace Monastery, stealing government goods and keeping wife and children — he should be removed from his posts as Grand Mentor and Grand Sacrificial Director. " Approved. All relay stations were forbidden to keep stolen traveling horses; miscellaneous corvée of Crane-Control households was remitted. In Anji County of Huzhou, long rains overflowed Tai Lake, floating away 2,890 households' dwellings, drowning 157 men and women; Jiang-Zhe Branch Secretariat was ordered to relieve and comfort them. On dinghai day, censorate officials said: "Jiangxi Branch Secretariat associate administrator Li Yunzhong is the adopted son of the former inner attendant Li Bangning; his substance is mediocre and low — mistakenly receiving heavy selection; he should be dismissed. " Approved. Luchu raided Shunyuan Route. 8
冬十月甲辰，遣秘書太監王珪等代祀嶽鎮、海瀆、后土。 乙巳，召行樞密院徹裏鐵木兒、小雲失還朝。 以前東川路總管普折子安樂襲其父職。 己酉，時享於太廟。 為皇子古納答剌作佛事，釋在京囚，死罪者二人，杖罪者四十七人。 辛亥，召江南行臺御史大夫阿兒思蘭海牙赴闕。 癸丑，幸大承天護聖寺。 蒙古都元帥怯烈引兵擊阿禾賊黨於澂江路海中山，為雲梯登山，破其柵，殺賊五百餘人。 禿堅之弟必剌都古彖失舉家赴海死。 又獲禿堅弟二人、子三人，誅之。 甲寅，杭州火，命江浙行省賑其不能自存者。 丁巳，中書省臣言：「江浙平江、湖州等路水傷稼，明年海漕米二百六十萬石，恐不足，若令運百九十萬，而命河南發三十萬，江西發十萬為宜。 又，遣官賫鈔十萬錠、鹽引三萬五千道，於通、漷、陵、滄四州，優價和糴米三十萬石。 又，以鈔二萬五千錠、鹽引萬五千道，於通、漷二州，和糴粟豆十五萬石； 以鈔三十萬錠，往遼陽懿、錦二州，和糴粟豆十萬石。 」並從之。 燒在京積年還倒昏鈔二百七十餘萬錠。 戊午，詔還平江路大玉清昭應宮田百頃，官勿征其租。 己未，給宿衛士有官者芻豆。 諸王卜賽因使者還西域，詔酬其所貢藥物價直。 辛酉，命西僧作佛事於興聖宮，十有五日乃罷。 吳江州大風雨，太湖溢，漂沒廬舍資畜千九百七十家，命江浙行省給鈔千五百錠賑之。 乙丑，立昭功萬戶都總使府，伯顏、鐵木兒補化並兼昭功萬戶都總使。 丙寅，命大都路定時估，每月朔望送廣誼司，以酬物價。 燕鐵木兒取犛牛五千於西域來獻。
Winter, 10th month, on jiachen day, secretary supervisor Wang Gui and others were dispatched to perform sacrifices at the mountain peaks, sea deities, and Hou Tu. 十一月壬申朔，日有食之。 雲南行省言：「亦乞不薛之地所牧國馬，歲給鹽，以每月上寅日啖之，則馬健無病。 比因伯忽叛亂，去南鹽不可到，馬多病死。 」詔令四川行省以鹽給之。 乙亥，李彥通、蕭不蘭奚等謀反，伏誅。 丙子，封諸王斡即為保寧王，賜以印，以其先所受印賜諸王渾禿帖木兒之子庚兀臺。 詔給移剌四奴分行省印。 丁丑，興和路鷹坊及蒙古民萬一千一百餘戶，大雪畜牧凍死，賑米五千石。 戊寅，樞密院臣言：「天歷兵興，以揚州重鎮，嘗假淮東宣慰司以兵權，今事已寧，宜以所部兵復隸河南行省。 又，征西元帥府自泰定初調兵四千一百人戍龍剌、亦集乃，期以五年為代，今已十年，逃亡者眾，宜加優恤，期以來歲五月代還。 」並從之。 己卯，封蘸班為豳國公。 庚辰，左、右欽察衛軍士千四百九十戶饑，命上都留守司賑之。 辛巳，以戶部尚書耿煥為中書參知政事。 癸未，詔養燕鐵木兒之子塔剌海為子，賜居第及所籍李彥通貲產。 荊王也速也不幹獻犛牛四百。 詔：「每歲樞密院、宗正府遣官，與遼陽行省官巡歷諸郡，毋令諸王所部擾民。 」隆祥司使晃忽兒不花言：「海南所建大興龍普明寺，工費浩穰，黎人不勝其擾，以故為亂。 」詔湖廣行省臣月璐不花及宣慰、宣撫二司領其役，仍命廉訪司蒞之。 辛卯，諸王撒兒蠻遣使者七十四人來。 賑左欽察衛撒敦等翼頂也兒古駐冬軍千五百八十戶。 諸鹽課鈔以十分之一折收銀，銀每錠五十兩，折鈔二十五錠。 乙未，敕宮相都總管府勿隸昭功都總使府。 丁酉，以南陽府之嵩州，更賜伯顏為食邑。
11th month, on renshen new moon, there was a solar eclipse. On yihai day, Li Yantong, Xiao Bulanxi, and others plotted rebellion and were executed. On bingzi day, Prince Woqi was enfeoffed Prince of Baoning and granted a seal; the seal he had formerly received was granted to Prince Huntoutemür's son Gengutai. Also, the Western Campaign Martial Prince's headquarters from Taiding's beginning deployed 4,100 men to garrison Longla and Yijinay — rotation was set at 5 years; now 10 years have passed and deserters are many; preferential treatment should be increased, with rotation to return next year's 5th month." All approved. On jimao day, Zhanban was enfeoffed Duke of Bin State. On gengchen day, 1,490 households of Left and Right Qinch'a Guard soldiers suffered famine; Shangdu garrison command was ordered to relieve them. Longxiang commissioner Huanggur Buqa said: "The Great Prospering Dragon Universal Bright Monastery built in Hainan — its labor costs vast and heavy; the Li people could not bear the oppression and thus rose in revolt." 10
十二月戊申，陜西行臺御史捏古伯、高坦等劾奏：「本臺監察御史陳良，恃勢肆毒，徇私破法，請罷職籍贓，還歸田里。 」有旨：「雖會赦，其準風憲例，追奪敕命，余如所奏。 」以黃金符鐫文曰「翊忠徇義迪節同勛」，賜西域親軍副都指揮使欽察，以旌其天歷初紅橋戰功。 壬子，復命諸王忽剌出還鎮雲南。 癸丑，撒敦獻斡羅思十六戶，酬以銀百七錠、鈔五千錠。 以河間路清池、南皮縣牧地賜斡羅思駐冬，仍以忽裏所牧官羊給之。 河南河北道廉訪副使僧家奴言：「自古求忠臣必於孝子之門。 今官於朝者，十年不省覲者有之，非無思親之心，實由朝廷無給假省親之制，而有擅離官次之禁。 古律，諸職官父母在三百里，於三年聽一給定省假二十日； 無父母者，五年聽一給拜墓假十日。 以此推之，父母在三百里以至萬里，宜計道里遠近，定立假期，其應省覲匿而不省覲者，坐以罪。 若詐冒假期，規避以掩其罪，與詐奔喪者同科。 」御史臺臣以聞，命中書省、禮部、刑部及翰林、集賢、奎章閣議之。 丁巳，雨木冰。 戊午，西域諸王禿列帖木兒遣使獻西馬及蒲萄酒。 預給四宿衛及諸潛邸衛士歲賜鈔，人二十錠。 庚申，遣集賢直學士答失蠻詣真定玉華宮，祀睿宗及顯懿莊聖皇后神御殿。 辛酉，遣兵部尚書也速不花、同僉通政院事忽納不花迎帝師。 詔中書省、御史臺遣官詣各道，同廉訪司錄囚。 癸亥，雨木冰。 給征東元帥府兵仗。 丁卯，御史臺臣言：「甘肅行省平章月魯帖木兒，既非蒙古族姓，且暗於事機，使總兵柄，恐非所宜。 」詔樞密院勿令提調軍馬。 己巳，御史臺臣言：「河東道廉訪副使忽哥兒不花，僉燕南道廉訪司事不顏忽都、王士元、郝誌善，憲綱不振，宜免官。 」從之。 旌寧海州崔惟孝孝行。 是歲，真定路屬州水，冀寧、河南二路旱，大饑。
12th month, on wushen day, Shaanxi Branch Censorate censors Niegu Bo and Gao Tan memorialized in accusation: "This office's surveillance censor Chen Liang, relying on power and rampant with poison, favoritism and law-breaking — we request removal from office, confiscation of corrupt goods, and return to home district. " An instruction said: "Though an amnesty has occurred, by the wind-and-banners precedent pursue and seize the patent; the remainder as memorialized." A gold tally was inscribed with text reading "Assisting loyalty and pursuing righteousness, upholding integrity and sharing achievement," granted to Western Regions Imperial Guard deputy commander-in-chief Qincha to honor his Hongqiao achievement at the beginning of Tiansli. On renzi day, Prince Hulachu was again ordered to return and guard Yunnan. On guichou day, Sadun presented 16 households of Rus'; they were compensated with 107 ingots of silver and 5,000 ingots of notes. Pasture lands of Qingchi and Nanpi counties of Hejian Route were granted for Rus' winter quarters, and official sheep pastured by Huli were still supplied to them. Henan-Hebei Surveillance associate commissioner Sengianu said: "Since antiquity seeking loyal ministers must be at the gate of filial sons. Now those serving at court — some 10 years without visiting parents — it is not that they lack thought for kin; truly the court has no system for granting leave to visit parents, yet there is prohibition against leaving office on one's own. Ancient statutes: for officeholders whose parents are within 300 li, once in 3 years a fixed provincial leave of 20 days was granted; for those without parents, once in 5 years tomb-visiting leave of 10 days was granted. By this reasoning, for parents from 300 li to 10,000 li, leave periods should be fixed according to distance; those who should visit parents but conceal and do not visit shall be punished. If falsely claiming leave to evade and cover crimes, the same penalty as falsely rushing to mourning." Censorate officials reported it; the central secretariat, Ministry of Rites, Ministry of Justice, and Hanlin, Academy of Scholarly Worthies, and Kuizhang Pavilion were ordered to deliberate. Advance grants of yearly allowance notes to the 4 night guards and various hidden-residence guard soldiers — 20 ingots per man. On gengshen day, Academy of Scholarly Worthies direct academician Dasi Man was dispatched to Zhending Yuhua Palace to sacrifice at the spirit halls of Ruizong and the Manifest Virtue and Sagely Empress Dowager. On xinyou day, Minister of War Yesubuhua and transmission court associate commissioner Hunabuhua were dispatched to welcome the Imperial Preceptor. An edict ordered central secretariat and censorate to dispatch officials to each circuit together with surveillance offices to record prisoners. On guihai day, rain froze on trees. Weapons were issued to the Eastern Campaign Martial Prince's headquarters. On dingmao day, censorate officials said: "Gansu Branch Secretariat associate grand councillor Yolu Temür is neither of Mongol ethnicity nor surname, and is dim regarding affairs; to let him hold overall military authority — we fear it is not fitting. " An edict commanded the privy council not to let him command and transfer troops. On jisi day, censorate officials said: "Hedong Route surveillance associate commissioner Huqur Buqa; convening Yan-Nan Route surveillance commissioner Buyanqudu, Wang Shiyuan, and Hao Zhishan — the banners of law do not stand firm; they should be removed from office. " Approved. Cui Weixiao of Ninghai Prefecture was honored for filial conduct. This year, prefectures subordinate to Zhending Route suffered flood; Jining and Henan 2 routes drought; great famine.
