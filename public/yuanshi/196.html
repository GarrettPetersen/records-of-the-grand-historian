普顏不花，字希古，蒙古氏。 倜儻有大志。 至正五年，由國子生登右榜進士第一人，授翰林修撰，調河南行省員外郎。 十一年，遷江西行省左右司郎中。 蘄黃徐壽輝來寇，普顏不花戰守之功為多，語在《道童傳》。 十六年，除江西廉訪副使。 頃之，召還，授益都路達魯花赤，遷山東廉訪使，再轉為中書參知政事。 十八年，詔與治書侍御史李國鳳同經略江南。 至建寧，江西陳友諒遣鄧克明來寇，而平章政事阿魯溫沙等皆夜遁。 國鳳時分鎮延平，城陷，遁去。 普顏不花曰：「我承制來此，去將何之？ 誓與此城同存亡耳。 」命築各門甕城，前後拒戰六十四日，既而大敗賊眾。 明年，召還，授山東宣慰使，再轉知樞密院事、平章山東行省，守禦益都。 大明兵壓境，普顏不花捍城力戰。 城陷，而平章政事保保出降。 普顏不花還告其母曰：「兒忠孝不能兩全，有二弟，當為終養。 」拜母，趨官舍，坐堂上。 主將素聞其賢，召之再三，不往。 既而面縛之，普顏不花曰：「我元朝進士，官至極品，臣各為其主。 」不屈，死之。
Puyan Buhua, whose courtesy name was Xigu, was a Mongol. He was free-spirited and ambitious. In 1345, as an Imperial Academy student he topped the right roster of jinshi graduates, was made Hanlin Academician Compiler, and was posted as Assistant Department Director on the Henan Branch Secretariat. In 1351 he was moved to Director of the Left and Right Bureaus on the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat. When Xu Shouhui of Qihuang invaded, Puyan Buhua accounted for most of the fighting and defense; that story is told in the biography of Daotong. In 1356 he was made Deputy Surveillance Commissioner of Jiangxi. Soon after he was recalled, named Darughachi of Yidu Circuit, promoted to Shandong Surveillance Commissioner, and later made Vice Councilor of the Central Secretariat. In 1358 an edict sent him with Investigating Censor Li Guofeng to direct operations in Jiangnan. At Jianning, Chen Youliang of Jiangxi sent Deng Keming against the city, while Grand Councilors such as Aruwinsha fled overnight. Guofeng was holding Yanping separately; when that city fell he escaped. Puyan Buhua said, "I was sent here on imperial orders. If I leave, where shall I go? I swear to stand or fall with this city." He had barbicans raised at each gate and held the enemy off for sixty-four days before routing them in a major victory. The following year he was recalled, made Shandong Pacification Commissioner, then Vice Director of the Privy Council and Grand Councilor of the Shandong Branch Secretariat, charged with defending Yidu. As Ming forces pressed the frontier, Puyan Buhua threw himself into the defense. When the city fell, Grand Councilor Baobao went over to the enemy. Puyan Buhua went home and told his mother, "Loyalty and filial piety cannot both be kept—I have two younger brothers who must see to your final years. " He bowed to her, went straight to the yamen, and took his seat in the main hall. The Ming commander, who had long admired him, summoned him repeatedly, but he refused to come. They soon bound him before their ranks. Puyan Buhua said, "I was a Yuan jinshi who rose to the highest offices; every subject serves his own sovereign. " He would not yield and was executed.
2
先是，其妻阿魯真，歷呼家人告之曰：「我夫受國恩，我亦封齊國夫人，今事至此，唯有死耳。 」家人莫不嘆息泣下。 已而普顏不花二弟之妻，各抱幼子，及婢妾，溺舍南井死。 比阿魯真欲下，而井填咽不可容，遂抱子投舍北井。 其女及妾女、孫女，皆隨溺焉。
Before this, his wife Aruzhen had gathered the household and said, "My husband owes the dynasty his life, and I was enfeoffed Lady of Qi; now there is nothing left but to die. " Everyone wept and lamented. Soon the wives of his two younger brothers, each clutching a small son, with maids and concubines, drowned themselves in the south well. When Aruzhen tried to follow, the well was already blocked and would not take her; she embraced her child and leapt into the north well instead. Her daughter, the concubines' daughters, and granddaughters drowned with them.
3
是時有申榮者，平章山東行省，守東昌，榮見列郡皆降，告其父曰：「人生世間，不能全忠孝者，兒也。 」父曰：「何為？ 」榮曰：「城中兵少不敵，戰則萬人之命由兒而廢，但有一死報國耳。 」遂自經。
About then Shen Rong, Grand Councilor of the Shandong Branch Secretariat, was holding Dongchang. Seeing every district surrender, he told his father, "In this life, the son who cannot keep both loyalty and filial piety whole is me. " His father asked, "What do you mean?" " Rong said, "We have too few men to stand against them; if I fight, I destroy ten thousand lives—only one death repays the state. " He then hanged himself.
4
閔本，字宗先，河內人。 性剛正敏給，而刻志於學。 早歲得推擇為禮部令史，御史大夫不花奇本之才，辟以為掾，平反冤獄，甚有聲。 擢御史臺照磨。 頃之，遷樞密院都事，拜監察御史，遷中書左司都事，五轉為吏部尚書，移刑、戶二部，皆以能見稱。 本素貧，且有目疾，嘗上章乞謝事，不允，詔授集賢侍講學士。 大明兵薄京師，本謂其妻程氏曰：「國事至此，吾知之久矣。 愧不能立功補報，敢愛六尺軀苟活哉！ 」程氏曰：「君能死忠，我尚有愛於君乎！ 」本乃朝服，與程氏北向再拜，大​​書於屋壁曰：「元中奉大夫、集賢侍講學士閔本死。 」遂各縊焉。 二女：長真真，次女女，見本死，呼天號泣，亦自縊於其傍。
Min Ben, whose courtesy name was Zongxian, came from Henei. Upright, sharp, and quick by nature, he devoted himself single-mindedly to study. Early on he was chosen as a Ministry of Rites clerk; Censor-in-Chief Buhua prized his talent and took him on staff, where he overturned wrongful convictions and won wide praise. He rose to Registrar of the Censorate. Soon he was Privy Council Director, then Investigating Censor, then Director of the Central Secretariat's Left Bureau; after five moves he became Minister of Personnel and later headed Punishments and Revenue, earning renown for ability at each post. Ben had long been poor and afflicted with eye trouble; he once asked to retire but was refused and instead made Academician Lecturer of the Hall of Gathered Worthies. As Ming troops closed on the capital, Ben told his wife Lady Cheng, "The dynasty has come to this—I have seen it coming for a long time. I am ashamed I could do nothing to repay it—how can I cling to this body and live on in shame? " Lady Cheng said, "If you will die for the dynasty, what hold do I still have on you? " Ben donned court dress; he and Lady Cheng bowed twice to the north and he wrote large characters on the wall: "Min Ben, Recipient of Grace of the Middle Rank, Academician Lecturer of the Hall of Gathered Worthies, dies here. " Then they hanged themselves. His daughters Zhenzhen and Nü, seeing him dead, wailed to heaven and hanged themselves at his side.
5
有拜住者，康里人也，字聞善。 以材累官至翰林國史院都事，為太子司經。 兵至，拜住謂家人曰：「吾始祖海藍伯封河東公者，與太祖同事王可汗，太祖取王可汗，收諸部落，吾祖引數十騎馳西北方，太祖使人追問之，曰：'昔者與皇帝同事王可汗，王可汗今已滅，欲為之報仇，則帝乃天命； 欲改事帝，則吾心有所不忍，故避之於遠地，以沒吾生耳。 '此吾祖之言也。 且吾祖生朔漠，其言尚如此，今吾生長中原，讀書國學，而可不知大義乎！ 況吾上世受國厚恩，至吾又食祿，今其國破，尚忍見之！ 與其苟生，不如死。 」遂赴井死。 其家人瘞之舍東，悉以其書籍焚之為殉云。
There was Baizhu of the Kangli people, whose courtesy name was Wenshan. Talent carried him step by step to Director of the Hanlin National History Academy and Tutor in the Classics for the Heir Apparent. When the armies came, Baizhu told his family, "Our forebear Hailanbo, enfeoffed Duke of Hedong, had served Prince Wang Khan alongside the Founder; when the Founder overthrew Wang Khan and gathered the tribes, our ancestor rode off northwest with a few dozen men. The Founder sent pursuers to ask why; he answered, 'I once served the Prince with the Emperor; the Prince is gone—if I avenged him I would fight Heaven's chosen; if I turned to serve the Emperor my heart could not bear it—so I withdrew to a distant land to end my days.' That was my ancestor's word. Born on the northern steppe, he still spoke so; I was raised in the Central Plains and schooled at the Imperial Academy—how could I not know what righteousness demands? My forebears received the dynasty's deep favor, and I have lived on its stipend; now the state is broken—how can I still face the world! Better to die than linger on in shame. " He threw himself into a well. His family buried him east of the house and burned all his books to accompany him. Zhao Hongyi, whose courtesy name was Renqing, came from Jin Prefecture in Zhending.
6
趙弘毅，字仁卿，真定晉州人。 少好學，家貧無書，傭於巨室，晝則為役，夜則藉書讀之。 或閔其志，但使總其事而不役焉。 嘗受經於臨川吳澄。 始辟翰林書寫，再轉為國史院編修官，調大樂署令。 大明兵入京城，弘毅嘆息曰； 「忠臣不二君，烈女不二夫，此古語也。 我今力不能救社稷，但有一死報國耳。 」乃與妻解氏皆自縊。
As a youth he loved learning; too poor to own books, he hired himself to a wealthy house, laboring by day and reading borrowed books by night. Moved by his resolve, his employer sometimes let him supervise the work instead of toiling himself. He studied the classics under Wu Cheng of Linchuan. He studied the classics under Wu Cheng of Linchuan. He began as a Hanlin copyist, became National History Compiler, and was appointed Director of the Grand Music Office. When Ming forces entered the capital, Hongyi sighed and said, "A loyal minister does not serve two masters, a steadfast woman does not follow two husbands—that is the old saying. I lack the strength to save the realm—only death can repay it. " He and his wife Lady Xie hanged themselves.
7
其子恭，中書管勾，與妻子訣曰：「今乘輿北奔，我父子食祿，不能效尺寸力，吾父母已死，尚何敢愛死乎！ 」或止之曰：「我曹官卑，何自苦如此。 」恭叱曰：「爾非我徒也。 古者，忠義人各儘自心，豈問職之崇卑乎！ 」遂公服北向再拜，亦縊死。
His son Gong, a Secretariat clerk, bade his wife and children farewell: "The Son of Heaven flees north; we have eaten the state's grain yet cannot lift a finger; my parents are dead—how dare I spare my life? Some urged him, "We are only minor officials—why suffer so? " Gong snapped, "You are not my sort! In antiquity loyal men followed their own hearts—who asked whether rank was high or low? " He donned official robes, bowed twice to the north, and hanged himself.
8
恭女官奴，年十七，見恭死，方大泣，適鄰嫗數輩來，相率出避，曰：「我未適人，避將何之？ 」不聽，嫗欲力挽之，女曰：「人生在世，便百歲亦須一死。 」乃潛入中堂，解衣帶自經。
Gong's daughter Guannu, seventeen, was wailing over his body when neighboring women came to flee with her. They pressed her; she said, "In this life, even at a hundred one must die once. " She slipped into the central hall and hanged herself with her belt.
9
鄭玉，字子美，徽州歙縣人。 幼敏悟嗜學，既長，覃思《六經》，尤邃於《春秋》，絕意仕進，而勤於教。 學者門人受業者眾，所居至不能容。 學者相與即其地構師山書院以處焉。 玉為文章，不事雕刻煅煉，流傳京師，揭徯斯、歐陽玄咸加稱賞。 至正十四年，朝廷除玉翰林待制、奉議大夫，遣使者賜以御酒名幣，浮海征之。 玉辭疾不起，而為表以進曰：「名爵者，祖宗之所以遺陛下，​​使與天下賢者共之者，陛下不得私予人。 待制之職，臣非其才，不敢受。 酒與幣，天下所以奉陛下，陛下得以私與人，酒與幣，臣不敢辭也。 」玉既不仕，則家居，日以著書為事，所著有《周易纂注》。 十七年，大明兵入徽州，守將將要致之，玉曰：「吾豈事二姓者耶！ 」因被拘囚。 久之，親戚朋友攜具餉之，則從容為之盡歡，且告以必死狀。 其妻聞之，使語之曰：「君苟死，吾其相從地下矣。 」玉使謂之曰：「若果從吾死，吾其無憾矣。 」明日，具衣冠，北向再拜，自縊而死。
Zheng Yu, whose courtesy name was Zimei, came from She County in Huizhou. Clever and studious as a boy, he later immersed himself in the Six Classics, especially the Spring and Autumn Annals, renounced office entirely, and threw himself into teaching. Pupils and disciples flocked to him until his house could hold no more. Scholars built the Shishan Academy on that site to house them all. His essays avoided ornament; when they reached the capital, Jie Xisi and Ouyang Xuan praised them warmly. In 1354 the court named him Hanlin Attendant-in-Waiting and Recipient of Grace, Grand Master of Discussion, and sent envoys with imperial wine and fine silks across the sea to fetch him. Yu pleaded illness and stayed home, submitting a memorial: "Rank and stipend are what your forebears left you to share with the empire's worthies—Your Majesty may not grant them privately. I am unfit for Attendant-in-Waiting and dare not accept it. Wine and silks are what the realm offers Your Majesty, which you may give privately—I dare not refuse those. " Unwilling to serve, he stayed home writing; among his works is Collected Commentary on the Book of Changes. In 1357, when Ming troops entered Huizhou, the garrison commander meant to present him; Yu said, "How could I serve two dynasties?" " He was detained. After a long while kinsmen brought food; he feasted with them calmly and told them he would die. His wife sent word, "If you die, I will follow you to the grave." Yu answered, "If you truly follow me, I shall die without regret." " The next day he dressed in full regalia, bowed twice to the north, and hanged himself.
10
黃冔，字殷士，撫州金溪人。 博學明經，善屬文，尤長於詩。 至正十七年，用左丞相太平奏，授淮南行省照磨，未行，除國子助教，遷太常博士，轉國子博士，升監丞，擢翰林待制，兼國史院編修官。 二十八年，京城既破，冔歎曰：「我以儒致身，累蒙國恩，為冑子師，代言禁林。 今縱無我戮，何面目見天下士乎！ 」遂赴井而死，年六十一。 有詩文傳於世。
Huang Yi, whose courtesy name was Yinshi, came from Jinxi in Fuzhou. Learned in the classics, skilled at prose, and especially gifted in poetry. In 1357, on Left Chancellor Taiping's recommendation, he was named Huainan Branch Registrar; before departing he became Directorate Assistant Instructor, Grand Sacrificial Office Doctor, then Directorate Doctor, Vice Director, and Hanlin Attendant-in-Waiting with a concurrent National History compile. In 1368, when the capital fell, Yi sighed, "Confucian study raised me; the dynasty heaped favor on me as tutor to its elite and voice of the inner court. Even if spared, how could I face the scholars of the empire? " He drowned himself in a well at sixty-one. His poetry and prose survive.
11
柏帖穆爾，字君壽，蒙古人。 家世歷履無所考。 居官所至，以廉能著聲。 至正中，累遷為福建行省左右司郎中。 行省治福州。 二十七年，大明以騎兵出杉關，取邵武，以舟師由海道趣閩，奄至城下。 柏帖穆爾知城不可守，引妻妾坐樓上，慷慨謂曰：「丈夫死國，婦人死夫，義也。 今城且陷，吾必死於是，若等能吾從乎？ 」皆泣曰：「有死而已，無他志也。 」縊而死者六人。
Baitiemuer, whose courtesy name was Junshou, was a Mongol. His family's official record is unknown. Every post he held won renown for integrity and competence. Under Zhizheng he rose to Director of the Left and Right Bureaus on the Fujian Branch Secretariat. The secretariat was based at Fuzhou. In 1367 Ming cavalry came through Shan Pass and seized Shaowu while a fleet advanced along the coast on Fujian, appearing suddenly beneath the walls. Seeing the city could not be held, Baitiemuer brought his wife and concubines upstairs and said firmly, "A man dies for his country, a woman for her husband—that is righteousness. The city will fall; I shall die here—will you follow me?" " They wept and said, "Death alone will do—we have no other wish. " Six died by hanging.
12
有十歲女，度其不能自死，則紿之曰：「汝稽顙拜佛，庶保我無恙也。 」甫拜，即挈米囊壓之死。 乳媼抱其幼子，旁立以泣，柏帖穆爾熟視之，歎曰：「父死國，母死夫，妾與女，從父者也，皆當死。 汝三歲兒，於義何所從乎？ 為宗祀計可也。 」乃命媼抱匿旁近民舍，而斂金珠畀之曰：「即有緩急，可以此贖兒命。 」有頃，兵入城，即舉燈自燃，四圍窗火大發，遂自焚死。
He had a ten-year-old daughter; knowing she could not take her own life, he tricked her: "Kowtow to the Buddha—it may keep me safe. " The moment she bowed, he crushed her beneath a rice sack. A nurse held his infant son, weeping nearby; Baitiemuer gazed at them and sighed, "A father dies for his country, a mother for her husband; concubines and daughters follow the father—all should die. Your three-year-old—what does duty require of him? For the lineage he may live. " He had the nurse hide the boy in a neighbor's house, gave her gold and pearls, and said, "If trouble comes, use these to buy his life. " Soon soldiers entered; he lit a lamp and set himself ablaze; fire roared from every window and he perished in the flames.
13
迭裡彌實，字子初，回回人。 性剛介，事母至孝。 年四十，猶不仕，或問之，曰：「吾不忍舍吾母以去也。 」以宿衛年勞，授行宣政院崇教，三遷為漳州路達魯花赤，居三年，居甚安之。 時陳有定據全閩，八郡之政，皆用其私人以總制之。 朝廷命官，不得有所與。 大明兵既取福州，興化、泉州皆納款。 或以告，迭裡彌實仰天歎曰：「吾不材，位三品，國恩厚矣，其何以報乎！ 報國恩者，有死而已。 」亡何，吏走白招諭使者至，請出城迓之，迭裡彌實從容語之曰：「爾第往，吾行出矣。 」乃詣廳事，具公服，北面再拜畢，引斧斫其印文，又大書手版曰「大元臣子」。 即入位端坐，拔所佩刀，剚喉中以死。 既死，猶手執刀按膝坐，儼然如生時。 郡民相聚哭庭中，斂其屍，葬東門外。
Dielimishi, whose courtesy name was Zichu, was a Hui Muslim. Stern and upright by nature, he was devoted to his mother. At forty he still refused office; asked why, he said, "I cannot abandon my mother. " Long palace-guard service won him Xuanzheng Court Chongjiao; three promotions later he was Darughachi of Zhangzhou, where he lived three peaceful years. Chen Youding then held all Fujian; his men controlled every prefecture. Court appointees could do nothing. Once Ming forces took Fuzhou, Xinghua and Quanzhou surrendered. Told of this, Dielimishi looked heavenward and sighed, "I am unworthy of third rank, yet the dynasty favored me deeply—how repay it? Only death repays such grace. " Soon a clerk announced the pacification envoy and urged him to greet them outside the walls; Dielimishi said calmly, "You go—I am coming out now. " He entered the hall in full dress, bowed twice north, chopped his seal with an axe, and wrote on his tablet: "Subject of the Great Yuan." He sat upright, drew his belt knife, and stabbed his throat. Dead, he still gripped the knife on his knee, looking alive. County folk wept in the courtyard, buried him outside the east gate.
14
時又有獲獨步丁者，回回人，舊進士，累官僉廣東廉訪司事； 有呂复者，為江西行省左右司都事。 皆閒居，寓福州。 而復以行省命，攝長樂縣尹。 福州既下，獲獨步丁曰：「吾兄弟三人，皆忝進士，受國恩四十年，今雖無官守，然大節所在，其可辱乎！ 」以石自係其腰，投井死。 复亦曰：「吾世食君祿，今雖攝官，若不以死報國，則無以見先人於地下。 」引繩自經死。 獲獨步丁兄曰穆魯丁者，官建康； 曰海魯丁者，官信州。 先是，亦皆死國難云。
About then Huo Dubuding, a Hui Muslim and former jinshi, had been Vice Commissioner of Guangdong Surveillance; Lu Fu was Director of the Left and Right Bureaus on the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat. Both lived in retirement at Fuzhou. Fu was acting magistrate of Changle on secretariat orders. When Fuzhou fell, Huo Dubuding said, "We three brothers are jinshi who have eaten the state's grain forty years; though without office now, how bear such shame? " He weighted his waist with stone and drowned himself in a well. Fu said, "My line has fed on royal salary for generations; though only acting magistrate, without dying for the state I cannot face my ancestors. " He hanged himself. Huo Dubuding's elder brother Muruding served at Jiankang; Hailuding served at Xinzhou. They too had earlier died for the dynasty.
15
樸賽因不花，字德中，肅良合台人。 有膂力，善騎射。 由速古兒赤授利器庫提點，再轉為資正院判官，累遷同知樞密院事，遷翰林學士，尋升承旨，賜虎符，兼巡軍合浦全羅等處軍民萬戶都元帥，除大司農，出為嶺北行省右丞，昇平章政事。 至正二十四年，甘肅行省以孛羅帖木兒矯弒皇后、皇孫，遣人白事，平章政事也速答兒即欲署諭眾榜，樸賽因不花持不可曰：「此大事，何得輕​​信，況非符驗公文。 」卒不署榜。 既而果妄傳。 會皇太子撫軍冀寧，承製拜樸賽因不花翰林學士承旨，遷集賢大學士，又為宣政院使，遂拜中書平章政事。 大明兵逼京師，詔樸賽因不花以兵守順承門，其所領兵僅數百羸卒而已。 乃嘆息謂左右曰：「國事至此，吾但知與此門同存亡也。 」城陷被執，以見主將，唯請速死，不少屈。 主將命留營中，終不屈，殺之。
Pusaiyinbuhua, whose courtesy name was Dezhong, was a Sulianghetai tribesman. Powerful and skilled in mounted archery. From palace guard he became Arsenal Director, then Zizheng Court Vice Director, Privy Council Vice Director, Hanlin Academician, then Expositor with a tiger tally, concurrently commander in Hepu and Jeolla, Grand Minister of Agriculture, Lingbei Right Councilor, and Grand Councilor. In 1364 Gansu reported Boluotiemuer had murdered empress and imperial grandson; Grand Councilor Yesudaer wanted a public notice; Pusaiyinbuhua refused: "This is grave—how believe it without authenticated documents? " He never signed the notice. It proved a false rumor. When the Crown Prince campaigned at Jining, he was made Hanlin Expositor, Grand Academician, Xuanzheng Commissioner, then Central Secretariat Grand Councilor. As Ming troops closed on the capital, he was ordered to hold Shuncheng Gate with a few hundred exhausted men. He sighed to his attendants, "The realm has come to this—I live or die with this gate. " Captured when the city fell, he asked only for a swift death and would not bend. The commander kept him in camp; he never yielded and was killed.
16
是時有張庸者，字存中，溫州人。 性豪爽，精太乙數，會世亂，以策幹經略使李國鳳，承製授庸福建行省員外郎，治兵杉關。 頃之，計事赴京師，因進《太乙數圖》，順帝喜之，擢秘書少監。 皇太子立大撫軍院，命庸團結房山，遷同僉將作院事，又除刑部尚書，仍領團結。 會諸寨既降，庸守駱駝谷，遣從事段禎請援於擴廓帖木兒，不報。 庸獨堅守拒戰，眾將潰，庸無去志。 已而寨民李世傑執庸出降，以見主將，庸不屈，與禎同被殺。
About then Zhang Yong of Wenzhou, courtesy name Cunzhong, was bold, versed in Taiyi numerology, and in the turmoil offered plans to Li Guofeng, winning appointment as Fujian Branch Assistant Director to drill troops at Shan Pass. Soon he went to the capital with business and presented his Taiyi chart; the emperor made him Secretariat Vice Director. When the Crown Prince formed the Great Pacification Army Bureau, Yong organized Fangshan militia, became Associate Palace Buildings Director, then Minister of Punishments while still leading militia. When other strongholds surrendered, Yong held Luotuo Valley and sent Duan Zhen to Kökö Temür for aid without reply. Yong alone held out; his officers fled but he would not leave. Soon Li Shijie of the stockade seized him and surrendered him; before the commander he would not yield and was killed with Zhen.
17
丁好禮，字敬可，真定蠡州人。 精律算，初試吏於戶部，辟中書掾，授戶部主事，擢江南行台監察御史，復入戶部為員外郎，拜監察御史，又入戶部為郎中，升侍郎。 除京畿漕運使，建議置司於通州，重講究漕運利病，著為成法，人皆便之。 除戶部尚書。 時國家多故，財用空乏，好禮能撙節浮費，國家用度，賴之以給。 拜參議中書省事，遷治書侍御史，出為遼陽行省左丞，未行，留為樞密副使。 至正二十年，遂拜中書參知政事。 時京師大饑，天壽節，廟堂欲用故事大宴會，好禮言：「今民父子有相食者，君臣當修省，以弭大患，燕會宜減常度。 」不聽，乞謝事，乃以集賢大學士致仕，給全俸家居。 擴廓帖木兒扈從皇太子還京，輸山東粟以遺朝貴，饋好禮麥百石，好禮不受。 二十七年，復起為中書平章政事，尋以論議不合，謝政去，特封趙國公。 大明兵入京城，或勉其謁大將，好禮叱之曰：「我以小吏致位極品，爵上公，今老矣，恨無以報國，所欠惟一死耳。 」後數日，大將召好禮，不肯行，舁至齊化門，抗辭不屈而死，年七十五。
Ding Haoli, courtesy name Jingke, came from Li County in Zhending. Skilled in law and reckoning, he began in Revenue, became Secretariat staff, Revenue Main Clerk, Jiangnan Investigating Censor, Revenue Assistant Director, Investigating Censor again, Revenue Director, then Vice Minister. Named Capital Region Transport Commissioner, he proposed a Tongzhou office, studied canal transport thoroughly, and set lasting rules everyone welcomed. He became Minister of Revenue. The realm was in turmoil and coffers bare; Haoli cut waste and kept the treasury supplied. He was Secretariat Councilor, Investigating Censor, then Liaoyang Left Councilor; before leaving he was kept as Privy Council Vice Director. In 1360 he became Central Secretariat Vice Councilor. Famine gripped the capital; on the emperor's birthday the court wanted the usual feast; Haoli said, "Families are eating one another—ruler and ministers should repent, not feast as usual. " Ignored, he asked to retire as Grand Academician with full pay at home. Kökö Temür, escorting the Crown Prince home, sent Shandong grain to court nobles and offered Haoli a hundred piculs of wheat; he refused. In 1367 he was again Grand Councilor; soon he resigned over policy disputes and was enfeoffed Duke of Zhao. When Ming troops entered the capital, some urged him to visit the commander; Haoli snapped, "I rose from a clerk to the highest rank and a grand duke; I am old and owe the state only one death. " Days later the commander summoned him; he refused, was carried to Qihua Gate, defied them, and died at seventy-five.
18
是日，中書參知政事郭庸亦舁至齊化門，眾叱之拜，庸曰：「臣各為其主，死自吾分，何拜之有！ 」語不少屈而死。 庸字允中，蒙古氏，由國學生釋褐出身，累遷為陝西行台監察御史，與同列劾知樞密院事也先帖木兒喪師，左遷中興總管府判官。 其後也先帖木兒以罪黜，召拜監察御史，累轉參政中書，其節義與好禮並云。
That day Vice Councilor Guo Yong was also brought to Qihua Gate; the crowd ordered him to bow; Yong said, "Each serves his sovereign—death is mine; why bow? " He would not yield and died. Yong, courtesy name Yunzhong, was Mongol, entered from the Imperial Academy, became Shaanxi Investigating Censor, and with colleagues impeached Privy Council Director Yesentiemuer for losing armies, demoted to Zhongxing Vice Magistrate. Later Yesentiemuer was dismissed and Yong was recalled as Investigating Censor, rising to Secretariat Vice Councilor; his loyalty matches Haoli's.
