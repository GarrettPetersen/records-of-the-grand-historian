1
兵者，先王所以威天下，而折奪奸宄、戡定禍亂者也。 三代之制遠矣，漢、唐而下，其法變更不一。 大抵用得其道，則兵力富，而國勢強； 用失其宜，則兵力耗，而國勢弱。 故兵制之得失，國勢之盛衰系焉。
War is what the ancient kings employed to overawe the realm, to crush treachery and villainy, and to quell rebellion and chaos. The military systems of the Three Dynasties lie far in the past; from the Han and Tang onward, the regulations have shifted again and again. As a rule, when armies are used as they ought to be, military power grows ample and the state grows strong; when they are misused, military strength is drained away and the state grows weak. Thus the success or failure of military institutions is tied directly to the rise and fall of national power.
2
考之國初，典兵之官，視兵數多寡，為爵秩崇卑，長萬夫者為萬戶，千夫者為千戶，百夫者為百戶。 世祖時，頗修官制，內立五衛，以總宿衛諸軍，衛設親軍都指揮使； 外則萬戶之下置總管，千戶之下置總把，百戶之下置彈壓，立樞密院以總之。 遇方面有警，則置行樞密院，事已則廢，而移都鎮撫司屬行省。 萬戶、千戶、百戶分上中下。 萬戶佩金虎符，符趺為伏虎形，首為明珠，而有三珠、二珠、一珠之別。 千戶金符，百戶銀符。 萬戶、千戶死陣者，子孫襲爵，死病則降一等。 總把、百戶老死，萬戶遷他官，皆不得襲。 是法尋廢，後無大小，皆世其官，獨以罪去者則否。
At the founding of the dynasty, military officers were ranked by the size of the forces they commanded: the commander of ten thousand men held the title Commander of Ten Thousand Households, of a thousand men Commander of a Thousand Households, and of a hundred men Commander of a Hundred Households. Under Emperor Shizu the bureaucracy was substantially reorganized: within the capital five Guards were established to command all palace-guard units, each headed by a Commander-in-Chief of the Imperial Guard; in the field, general supervisors were placed under commanders of ten thousand households, chief commanders under commanders of a thousand, and suppressors under commanders of a hundred, while a Bureau of Military Affairs was created to supervise the whole system. When trouble arose on a regional frontier, a traveling Bureau of Military Affairs was set up; once the crisis passed it was dissolved, and the Metropolitan Pacification Commission was placed under the regional secretariat. Commanders of ten thousand, a thousand, and a hundred households were each ranked upper, middle, or lower. Commanders of ten thousand households carried golden tiger tallies shaped like crouching tigers with pearl finials, distinguished as bearing three pearls, two pearls, or one pearl. Commanders of a thousand households held golden tallies; commanders of a hundred held silver ones. If a commander of ten thousand or a thousand households fell in battle, his descendants inherited his rank; if he died of illness, the rank passed down one grade lower. Chief commanders and commanders of a hundred households who died of old age, and commanders of ten thousand who were transferred to other posts, could not pass their offices to heirs. This rule was soon set aside; thereafter offices of every rank became hereditary, except when an incumbent was removed for a crime.
3
若夫軍士，則初有蒙古軍、探馬赤軍。 蒙古軍皆國人，探馬赤軍則諸部族也。 其法，家有男子，十五以上、七十以下，無眾寡盡簽為兵。 十人為一牌，設牌頭，上馬則備戰鬥，下馬則屯聚牧養。 孩幼稍長，又籍之，曰漸丁軍。 既平中原，發民為卒，是為漢軍。 或以貧富為甲乙，戶出一人，曰獨戶軍，合二三而出一人，則為正軍戶，余為貼軍戶。 或以男丁論，嘗以二十丁出一卒，至元七年十丁出一卒。 或以戶論，二十戶出一卒，而限年二十以上者充。 士卒之家，為富商大賈，則又取一人，曰余丁軍，至十五年免。 或取匠為軍，曰匠軍。 或取諸侯將校之子弟充軍，曰質子軍，又曰禿魯華軍。 是皆多事之際，一時之制。
Among the soldiery, the earliest categories were the Mongol Army and the Tamma Army. The Mongol Army consisted entirely of Mongols; the Tamma Army drew from the various subject tribes. Under their system, every household with males between fifteen and seventy was conscripted in full, regardless of how many sons it had. Ten men formed a squad under a squad leader: mounted, they stood ready for combat; dismounted, they camped together to herd and graze their animals. As boys grew older they were registered again in what was called the Gradually Mature Army. Once the Central Plains had been conquered, Chinese subjects were drafted as soldiers—the Han Army. Sometimes households were graded by wealth: a single household supplying one soldier was called a sole-household levy; when two or three households together supplied one man, one became the principal military household and the others supplementary military households. Sometimes the levy was calculated by adult males: originally one soldier per twenty males, reduced in Zhiyuan 7 to one per ten. Sometimes it was reckoned by households: one soldier from every twenty households, limited to men aged twenty or above. If a soldier's household became wealthy through trade, an additional man was levied as a surplus-male soldier, exempted after fifteen years of service. Sometimes artisans were drafted as soldiers—the Artisan Army. Sometimes the sons of princes and officers were taken into service as the Hostage Army, also known as the Turghul Army. All of these were expedients of a turbulent age, measures adopted for the moment.
4
天下既平，嘗為軍者，定入尺籍伍符，不可更易。 詐增損丁產者，覺則更籍其實，而以印印之。 病死戍所者，百日外役次丁； 死陣者，復一年。 貧不能役，則聚而一之，曰合並； 貧甚者、老無子者，落其籍。 戶絕者，別以民補之。 奴得縱自便者，俾為其主貼軍。 其戶逃而還者，復三年，又逃者杖之，投他役者還籍。 其繼得宋兵，號新附軍。 又有遼東之糺軍、契丹軍、女直軍、高麗軍，雲南之寸白軍，福建之畬軍，則皆不出戍他方者，蓋鄉兵也。 又有以技名者，曰炮軍、弩軍、水手軍。 應募而集者，曰答剌罕軍。
Once the empire was pacified, former soldiers were entered permanently in the household register and squad roster, never to be reassigned. Anyone caught falsifying household size or property was re-registered to reflect the truth and marked with an official seal. If a soldier died of illness at his garrison, after a hundred days the next adult male in the household took his place; if he fell in battle, the household received a one-year exemption. Households too poor to serve alone were grouped together in what was called consolidation; the desperately poor and the elderly without sons were removed from the rolls. When a military household died out, ordinary civilians were assigned to replace it. Slaves granted personal freedom were required to serve as supplementary soldiers for their former masters. Deserters who returned received a three-year exemption; repeat deserters were flogged; those who had taken other employment were restored to the military rolls. Song troops who subsequently submitted were called the Newly Attached Army. In Liaodong there were the Jiu, Khitan, Jurchen, and Korean armies; in Yunnan the Cunbai Army; in Fujian the She Army—all local forces that did not garrison distant provinces. Others were named for specialized skills: the Artillery Army, Crossbow Army, and Sailor Army. Volunteers who answered the call were called the Darqan Army.
5
其名數，則有憲宗二年之籍、世祖至元八年之籍、十一年之籍，而新附軍有二十七年之籍。 以兵籍系軍機重務，漢人不閱其數。 雖樞密近臣職專軍旅者，惟長官一二人知之。 故有國百年，而內外兵數之多寡，人莫有知之者。
Their rolls included the census of Xianzong 2, Shizu's registers of Zhiyuan 8 and 11, and for the Newly Attached Army a register of Zhiyuan 27. Because military registers touched vital state secrets, Han officials were not permitted to inspect the troop counts. Even among the Bureau of Military Affairs' inner circle, charged solely with military affairs, only one or two senior officers knew the figures. Thus for a century of rule, no one knew how many troops the dynasty fielded at home or abroad.
6
今其典籍可考者，曰兵制，曰宿衛，曰鎮戍，而馬政、屯田、站赤、弓手、急遞鋪兵、鷹房捕獵，非兵而兵者，亦以類附焉，作《兵志》。 ○兵制
What survives in the records covers military institutions, palace guards, and frontier garrisons; horse policy, military colonies, relay stations, archer levies, courier-post soldiers, and falconry hunting—matters that are not strictly military yet function like armies—are appended by category in this Treatise on Military Affairs. ○ Military Institutions
7
七年七月，簽宣德、西京、平陽、太原、陜西五路人匠充軍，命各處管匠頭目，除織匠及和林建宮殿一切合幹人等外，應有回回、河西、漢兒匠人，並劄魯花赤及劄也、種田人等，通驗丁數，每二十人出軍一名。
In the seventh month of the seventh year, artisans from the circuits of Xuande, Xijing, Pingyang, Taiyuan, and Shaanxi were conscripted. Local craft supervisors were ordered that, apart from weavers and all workers needed for palace construction at Karakorum, Muslim, Hexi, and Han artisans, together with darughachi, jalanay, and farmers, should be tallied jointly and one soldier levied from every twenty men.
8
八年七月，詔：「燕京路保州等處，每二十戶簽軍一名，令答不葉兒統領出軍。 真定、河間、邢州、大名、太原等路，除先簽軍人外，於斷事官忽都虎新籍民戶三十七萬二千九百七十二人數內，每二十丁起軍一名，亦令屬答不葉兒領之。」
In the seventh month of the eighth year an edict declared: "In Baozhou and other districts of the Yanjing circuit, levy one soldier from every twenty households and place them under Tabuyir's command for campaign." "In the circuits of Zhending, Hejian, Xingzhou, Daming, Taiyuan, and elsewhere, apart from men already conscripted, levy one soldier from every twenty adult males among the 372,972 civilian households newly registered by the judicial officer Qutuq, and assign them likewise to Tabuyir's command."
9
十三年八月，諭總管萬戶劉黑馬，據斜烈奏，忽都虎等元籍諸路民戶一百萬四千六百五十六戶，除逃戶外，有七十二萬三千九百一十戶，隨路總簽軍一十萬五千四百七十一名，點數過九萬七千五百七十五人，余因近年蝗旱，民力艱難，往往在逃。 有旨，今後止驗見在民戶簽軍，仍命逃戶復業者免三年軍役。
In the eighth month of the thirteenth year Liu Heima, commander of ten thousand households, was instructed that according to Xielie's memorial, Qutuq had originally registered 1,046,556 civilian households across the circuits; excluding fugitives, 723,910 remained, with 105,471 soldiers levied circuit by circuit. When mustered, only 97,575 appeared—the rest, worn down by recent locusts and drought, had fled. The throne ordered that henceforth conscription should count only households actually present, and that fugitives who returned to farming be exempt from military service for three years.
10
三年三月，詔：「真定、彰德、邢州、洺磁、東平、大名、平陽、太原、衛輝、懷孟等路各處，有舊屬按劄兒、孛羅、笑乃<角┦>、闊闊不花、不裏合拔都兒等官所管探馬赤軍人，乙卯歲籍為民戶，亦有簽充軍者。 若壬寅、甲寅兩次簽定軍，已入籍冊者，令隨各萬戶依舊出征； 其或未嘗為軍，及蒙古、漢人民戶內作數者，悉簽為軍。 」六月，以軍士訴貧乏者眾，命貧富相兼應役，實有不能自存者優恤三年。 十月，諭山東東路經略司：「益都路匠軍已前曾經簽把者，可遵別路之例，俾令從軍。 」以鳳翔府屯田軍人準充平陽軍數，仍於鳳翔屯田，勿遣從軍。 刁國器所管重簽軍九百一十五人，即日放罷為民。 陜西行省言：「士卒戍金州者，諸奧魯已嘗服役，今重勞苦。 」詔罷之。 並罷山東、大名、河南諸路新簽防城戍卒。
In the third month of the third year an edict declared: "Throughout the circuits of Zhending, Zhangde, Xingzhou, Luoci, Dongping, Daming, Pingyang, Taiyuan, Weihui, and Huaimeng, Tamma soldiers formerly under officials such as Ajjachi, Boluo, Xiaonaijiao, Kuokuobuhua, and Bulihebaduer had been registered as civilians in the yimao year, though some had also been conscripted." "Men registered in the renyin and jiayin levies who already appear on the rolls shall campaign under their commanders of ten thousand households as before;" "those who have never served, and any counted within Mongol or Han civilian households, shall all be conscripted." In the sixth month, because many soldiers complained of poverty, the court ordered that rich and poor households share the burden of service, with a three-year relief for those genuinely unable to support themselves. In the tenth month the Shandong Eastern Circuit Pacification Commission was told: "Artisan soldiers of Yidu previously registered for levy should follow other circuits' precedent and be sent to campaign." Fengxiang's garrison-colony soldiers were counted toward Pingyang's troop quota but ordered to remain farming at Fengxiang rather than join campaigns. The 915 men doubly conscripted under Diao Guoqi were immediately discharged and restored to civilian status. The Shaanxi Branch Secretariat reported: "The soldiers garrisoning Jinzhou come from ordo that have already served once and are now doubly burdened." An edict ordered the arrangement discontinued. The newly conscripted city-defense garrisons in Shandong, Daming, and Henan were discontinued as well.
11
四年二月，詔：「統軍司及管軍萬戶、千戶等，可遵太祖之制，令各官以子弟入朝充禿魯花。 」其制：萬戶，禿魯花一名，馬一十匹，牛二具，種田人四名。 千戶見管軍五百或五百已上者，禿魯花一名，馬六匹，牛一具，種田人二名。 雖所管軍不及五百，其家富強子弟健壯者，亦出禿魯花一名，馬匹、牛具、種田人同。 萬戶、千戶子弟充禿魯花者，挈其妻子同至，從人不拘定數，馬匹、牛具，除定去數目已上，復增余者聽。 若有貧乏不能自備者，於本萬戶內不該出禿魯花之人，通行津濟起發，不得因而科及眾軍。 萬戶、千戶或無親子、或親子幼弱未及成人者，以弟侄充，候親子年及十五，卻行交換。 若委有親子，不得隱匿代替，委有氣力，不得妄稱貧乏，及雖到來，氣力卻有不完者，並罪之。 是月，帝以太宗舊制，設官分職，軍民之事，各有所司。 後多故之際，不暇分別，命阿海充都元帥，專於北京、東京、平灤、懿州、蓋州路管領見管軍人，凡民間之事毋得預焉。 五月，立樞密院，凡蒙古、漢軍並聽樞密節制。 統軍司、都元帥府，除遇邊面緊急事務就便調度外，其軍情一切大小公事，並須申覆。 合設奧魯官，並從樞密院設置。 七月，詔免河南保甲丁壯、射生軍三千四百四十一戶雜泛科差，專令守把巡哨。 八月，諭成都路行樞密院：「近年軍人多逃亡事故者，可於各奧魯內盡實簽補，自乙卯年定入軍籍之數，悉簽起赴軍。 」十一月，女直、水達達及乞烈賓地合簽鎮守軍，命亦裏不花簽三千人，付塔匣來領之； 並達魯花赤官之子及其餘近上戶內，亦令簽軍，聽亦裏不花節制。
In the second month of the fourth year an edict declared: "The Army Command and all commanders of ten thousand and a thousand households shall follow the Grand Ancestor's institution and send sons or younger brothers to court as turghul hostages." The regulation required of each commander of ten thousand households: one turghul, ten horses, two ox-teams, and four farm laborers. A commander of a thousand households commanding five hundred men or more supplied one turghul, six horses, one ox-team, and two farm laborers. Even commanders of fewer than five hundred men, if their families were wealthy and their sons robust, still furnished one turghul with the same horses, oxen, and farm laborers. Turghul sons of commanders of ten thousand and a thousand households were to bring their wives and children; attendants were unlimited; horses and oxen beyond the fixed quota were permitted. Officers too poor to equip themselves were to be assisted by others in the same command not liable for turghul duty, without levying the cost on ordinary soldiers. Commanders without sons, or whose sons were still minors, might send younger brothers or nephews instead, exchanging them when a son reached fifteen. Those who truly had sons must not hide them and send substitutes; those who had the means must not falsely claim poverty; and those who arrived physically unfit were all subject to punishment. That month the Emperor, reviving Taizong's institutions, reestablished separate offices for military and civilian affairs. Later, amid constant emergencies, the court had no time to maintain the distinction and appointed Ahai Grand Marshal to command troops in the Northern Capital, Eastern Capital, Pingluan, Yizhou, and Gaizhou circuits alone, with no authority over civilian affairs. In the fifth month the Bureau of Military Affairs was established, placing all Mongol and Han armies under its command. The Army Command and Grand Marshal's headquarters, except for urgent frontier matters requiring immediate action, had to report all military business for approval. All ordo administrative offices were to be established through the Bureau of Military Affairs. In the seventh month an edict exempted 3,441 Henan baojia and hunting-soldier households from miscellaneous levies, assigning them solely to guard duty and patrol. In the eighth month the traveling Bureau of Military Affairs at Chengdu was told: "Because many soldiers have deserted or died in recent years, fully register replacements in each ordo and send all men entered on the military rolls since the yimao year to the army." In the eleventh month garrison troops were levied jointly in Jurchen, Shuidada, and Qiliebin territory; Yilibuhua was ordered to conscript three thousand men for Taqilai to command; sons of darughachi officials and men from leading households were also conscripted, all under Yilibuhua's command.
12
至元二年八月，陜西五路西蜀四川行省言：「新簽軍七千人，若發民戶，恐致擾亂。 今鞏昌已有舊軍三千，諸路軍二千，余二千人亦不必發民戶，當以便宜起補。 」從之。 十一月，省院官議，收到私走間道、盜販馬匹、曾過南界人三千八百四戶，悉令充軍，以一千九百七十八人與山東路統軍司，一千人與蔡州萬戶，余八百二十六戶，有旨留之軍中。
In the eighth month of Zhiyuan 2 the Branch Secretariat for Shaanxi and Sichuan reported: "Levying 7,000 new soldiers from civilian households risks causing unrest." "Gongchang already has 3,000 veteran soldiers and the circuits 2,000 more; the remaining 2,000 need not come from civilians but should be filled as circumstances allow." The proposal was approved. In the eleventh month officials deliberated over 3,804 households of men who had smuggled horses across secret routes into Song territory; all were conscripted—1,978 to the Shandong Army Command, 1,000 to the Caizhou commander of ten thousand households, and the remaining 826 retained in the army by imperial order.
13
三年七月，添內外巡軍，外路每百戶選中產者一人充之，其賦令余戶代輸，在都增武衛軍四百。
In the seventh month of the third year patrol forces were expanded: one man of middling means from every hundred households in the provinces, with taxes paid by the rest; four hundred Martial Guard soldiers were added in the capital.
14
四年正月，簽蒙古軍，每戶二丁、三丁者一人，四丁、五丁者二人，六丁、七丁者三人。 二月，詔遣官簽平陽、太原人戶為軍，除軍、站、僧、道、也裏可溫、答失蠻、儒人等戶外，於系官、投下民戶、運司戶、人匠、打捕鷹房、金銀鐵冶、丹粉錫碌等，不以是何戶計，驗酌中戶內丁多堪當人戶，簽軍二千人，定立百戶、牌子頭，前赴陜西五路西蜀四川行中書省所轄東川出征。 復於京兆、延安兩路簽軍一千人，如平陽、太原例。 五月，詔：「河南路驗酌中戶內丁多堪當軍人戶，簽軍四百二十名，歸之樞密院，俾從軍，復其徭役。 南京路，除邳州、南宿州外，依中書省分間定應簽軍人戶，驗丁數，簽軍二千五百八十名，管領出征。 」十二月，簽女直、水達達軍三千人。
In the first month of the fourth year the Mongol Army was levied: one soldier from households with two or three adult males, two from those with four or five, three from those with six or seven. In the second month officials were dispatched to conscript households in Pingyang and Taiyuan. Exempting military, relay, monastic, Nestorian, Muslim, and Confucian households, they levied 2,000 soldiers from middle households with many able-bodied men across state, appanage, transport, artisan, mining, and hunting households, organized them under commanders of a hundred and squad leaders, and sent them to campaign in Eastern Sichuan. Another 1,000 soldiers were levied in the Jingzhao and Yan'an circuits on the same terms. In the fifth month an edict ordered 420 soldiers levied from Henan's middle households with the most able-bodied men, assigned to the Bureau of Military Affairs for campaign with corvée duties restored. "In the Nanjing circuit, apart from Pizhou and Nansuzhou, levy 2,580 soldiers according to the Central Secretariat's quota, verified by adult male count, and send them to campaign." In the twelfth month 3,000 Jurchen and Shuidada soldiers were conscripted.
15
五年閏正月，詔益都李亶元簽軍，仍依舊數充役。 二月，詔諸路奧魯毋隸總管府，別設總押所官，聽樞密院節制。 六月，省臣議：「簽起禿魯花官員，皆已遷轉，或物故黜退者，於內復有貧難蒙古人氏，除隨路總管府達魯花赤、總管及掌兵萬戶，合令應當，其次官員禿魯花，宜放罷，其自願留質者聽之。 」十月，禁長軍之官不得侵漁士卒，違者論罪。 十一月，簽山東、河南沿邊州城民戶為軍，遇征進，則選有力之家同元守邊城漢軍一體出征，其無力之家代守邊城及屯田勾當。
In the intercalary first month of the fifth year an edict restored Li Tan's formerly conscripted Yidu soldiers to their original quota of service. In the second month an edict removed ordo administration from circuit superintendencies and placed it under separate chief-warden offices answerable to the Bureau of Military Affairs. In the sixth month provincial ministers recommended releasing turghul obligations for officials who had been transferred, died, or been dismissed, except for circuit darughachi, supervisors, and commanders of ten thousand households, who must still comply; those who wished to remain as hostages voluntarily might do so. In the tenth month officers were forbidden to exploit their soldiers, with violators subject to prosecution. In the eleventh month border households in Shandong and Henan were conscripted: on campaign wealthy households marched with the original Han border garrisons, while poor households remained to guard the cities and manage garrison farms.
16
六年二月，簽懷孟、衛輝路丁多人戶充軍，益都、淄萊所轄登、萊州李鋋舊軍內，起簽一萬人，差官部領出征。 其淄萊路所轄淄、萊等處有非李鋋舊管者，簽五百二十六人，其餘諸色人戶，亦令酌驗丁數，簽軍起遣，至軍前赴役。 十月，從山東路統軍司言，應系逃軍未獲者，令其次親丁代役，身死軍人亦令親丁代補，無親丁則以少壯驅丁代之。
In the second month of the sixth year households with many adult males in Huaimeng and Weihui were conscripted, and 10,000 men were raised from Li Shen's old Deng and Lai garrisons under Yidu and Zilai, with officials dispatched to lead them to campaign. In Zibo, Lai, and elsewhere outside Li Shen's former command 526 men were levied; other households of every category were also examined and soldiers dispatched to report for duty at the front. In the tenth month, following the Shandong Army Command's recommendation, deserters not yet captured were replaced by the nearest adult male relative; dead soldiers likewise, or by young able serfs if no relatives were available.
17
七年三月，定軍官等級，萬戶、千戶、百戶、總把以軍士為差。 六月，成都府括民三萬一千七十五戶，簽義士軍八千六十七人。 七月，分揀隨路炮手軍。 始太祖、太宗征討之際，於隨路取發，並攻破州縣，招收鐵木金火等人匠充炮手，管領出征，壬子年俱作炮手附籍。 中統四年揀定，除正軍當役外，其餘戶與民一體當差。 後為出軍正戶煩難，至元四年取元充炮手民戶津貼，其間有能與不能者，影占不便，至是分揀之。
In the third month of the seventh year military ranks were fixed, with commanders of ten thousand, a thousand, and a hundred households and chief commanders graded by the number of troops they commanded. In the sixth month Chengdu Prefecture registered 31,075 households and conscripted 8,067 volunteer soldiers. In the seventh month artillery troops from the various circuits were sorted and reassigned. During the Grand Ancestor's and Taizong's campaigns, artillerymen were levied circuit by circuit; as territories were conquered, artisans skilled in iron, wood, metal, and fire were recruited as gunners and led on campaign; in the renzi year they were all registered as artillery households. In Zhongtong 4 the rolls were fixed: apart from principal military households on active duty, the rest bore corvée like ordinary civilians. Later, because campaign households found the burden onerous, in Zhiyuan 4 subsidies were levied from former artillery households; since some were capable and others not, and false registration caused trouble, they were now sorted.
18
八年二月，以瓜州、沙州鷹房三百人充軍。
In the second month of the eighth year three hundred falconers from Guazhou and Shazhou were conscripted as soldiers.
19
九年正月，河南省請益兵，敕諸路簽軍三萬，詔元帥府、統軍司、總管萬戶府閱實軍籍。 二月，命阿術典行省蒙古軍，劉整、阿裏海牙典漢軍。 四月，詔：「諸路軍戶驅丁，除至元六年前從良入民籍者當差。 七年後，凡從良文書寫從便為民者，亦如之。 余雖從良，並令津助本戶軍役。 」七月，閱大都、京兆等處探馬赤戶名籍。 九月，詔樞密：「諸路正軍貼戶及同籍親戚僮奴，丁年堪役，依諸王權要以避役者，並還之軍，惟匠藝精巧者以名聞。 」十二月，命府州司縣達魯花赤及治民長官，不妨本職，兼管諸軍奧魯。 各路總管府達魯花赤、總管，別給宣命印信，府州司縣達魯花赤長官止給印信，任滿則別具解由，申樞密院。
In the first month of the ninth year Henan requested reinforcements; the circuits were ordered to levy 30,000 soldiers, and marshal headquarters, army commands, and commanders of ten thousand households were told to verify the military rolls. In the second month Aju was placed in command of the branch secretariat's Mongol forces and Liu Zheng and Alihaiya in command of the Han forces. In the fourth month an edict declared: "Serfs of military households in the various circuits, except those who obtained freedom and entered civilian registers before Zhiyuan 6, shall bear corvée." "After the seventh year, those whose manumission papers allowed them to become civilians are treated the same." "All others, even if manumitted, must still assist their household's military obligations." In the seventh month the household registers of Tamma troops in the capital, Jingzhao, and elsewhere were reviewed. In the ninth month the Bureau of Military Affairs was ordered: "Able-bodied men of military age among principal and supplementary military households, kinsmen, and bondservants on the same register who evade service through princely privilege must return to the army; only those of exceptional artisan skill may be reported by name." In the twelfth month prefectural, district, and county darughachi and civil magistrates were ordered to administer military ordo concurrently without neglecting their regular duties. Circuit darughachi and supervisors received separate commissions and seals; prefectural and county darughachi received seals only and submitted credentials to the Bureau of Military Affairs at the end of their terms.
20
十年正月，合剌請於渠江之北雲門山及嘉陵西岸虎頭山立二戍，以其圖來上，仍乞益兵二萬，敕給京兆新簽軍五千人益之。 陜西京兆、延安、鳳翔三路諸色人戶，約六萬戶內，簽軍六千。 五月，禁乾討虜人，其願充軍者，於萬戶、千戶內結成牌甲，與大軍一體征進。 八月，禁軍吏之長舉債，不得重取其息，以損軍力，違者罪之。 九月，襄陽生券軍至都釋械系免死，聽自立部伍，俾征日本，仍於蒙古、漢人內選官率領之。
In the first month of the tenth year Hela requested garrisons at Yunmen Mountain north of the Qu River and Hutou Mountain on the Jialing's west bank, submitted his map, and asked for 20,000 reinforcements; the court assigned 5,000 newly levied soldiers from Jingzhao. From roughly 60,000 households of every category in the Jingzhao, Yan'an, and Fengxiang circuits, 6,000 soldiers were levied. In the fifth month freebooting was banned; volunteers were organized into squads under commanders of ten thousand and a thousand households and marched with the main army. In the eighth month officers were forbidden to lend money at usurious rates that weakened their troops; violators were punished. In the ninth month Xiangyang life-bond soldiers reached the capital, were disarmed and granted exemption from execution, allowed to form their own units for the Japan campaign, with Mongol and Han officers chosen to lead them.
21
十一年正月，初立軍官以功升散官格。 五月，便宜總帥府言：「本路軍經今四十年間，或死或逃，無丁不能起補，見在軍少，乞選擇堪與不堪丁力，放罷貧乏無丁者，於民站內別選充役。 」從之。 詔延安府、沙井、凈州等處種田白達達戶，選其可充軍者，簽起出征。 六月，潁州屯田總管李珣言：「近為簽軍事，乞依徐、邳州屯田例，每三丁內，一丁防城，二丁納糧，可簽丁壯七百餘人，並元撥保甲丁壯，令珣通領，鎮守潁州，代見屯納合監戰軍馬別用。 」從之。
In the first month of the eleventh year regulations were first established for promoting military officers to honorary ranks by merit. In the fifth month the expedient grand marshal reported: "Over forty years our circuit's troops have died or deserted; without adult males we cannot fill the ranks. We ask to assess fitness, discharge poor households without sons, and select replacements from civilian relay households." The proposal was approved. An edict ordered that able-bodied men among farming White Tatar households in Yan'an, Shajing, Jingzhou, and elsewhere be conscripted for campaign. In the sixth month Li Xun, garrison-colony supervisor at Yingzhou, reported: "For recent conscription, we ask to follow the Xu and Pi precedent: one of every three adult males for city defense, two to deliver grain—over 700 able-bodied men, together with the originally assigned baojia militia, under my command to garrison Yingzhou and replace the current colony supervisor's war horses." The proposal was approved.
22
十二年三月，遣官往遼東，簽揀蒙古達魯花赤、千戶、百戶等官子弟出軍。 詔隨處所置襄陽生券軍之為農者，或自願充軍，具數以聞。 五月，正陽萬戶劉復亨言：「新下江南三十餘城，俱守以兵，及江北、淮南、潤、揚等處未降，軍力分散，調度不給，以致鎮巢軍、滁州兩處復叛。 乞簽河西等戶為軍，並力剿除，庶無後患。 」有旨，命肅州達魯花赤，並遣使同往驗各色戶計物力富強者簽起之。 六月，簽平陽、西京、延安等路達魯花赤弟男為軍。 萊州酒稅官王貞等上言：「國家討平殘宋，吊伐為事，何嘗以賄利為心。 彼不紹事業小人，貪圖貨利，作乾討虜名目，侵掠彼地，所得人口，悉皆貨賣，以充酒食之費，勝則無益朝廷，敗則實為辱國。 其招討司所收乾討虜人，可悉罷之，第其高下，籍為正軍，命各萬戶管領征進，一則得其實用，二則正王師吊伐之名，實為便益。 」從之。
In the third month of the twelfth year officials were sent to Liaodong to conscript sons of Mongol darughachi and commanders of a thousand and a hundred households. An edict ordered that Xiangyang life-bond soldiers farming in various places who wished to volunteer for military service report their numbers. In the fifth month Liu Fuheng, commander at Zhengyang, reported: "Over thirty newly conquered Jiangnan cities all require garrisons, while north of the river Huainan, Runzhou, and Yangzhou remain unsubmitted. Forces are overstretched and supplies inadequate, causing rebellions at Zhenchao and Chuzhou." "We ask to conscript Hexi households as soldiers and suppress the rebels jointly, to prevent further trouble." The throne ordered the Suzhou darughachi and envoys to inspect households of every category and conscript the wealthy and strong. In the sixth month sons and younger brothers of darughachi in Pingyang, Xijing, Yan'an, and other circuits were conscripted. Wang Zhen and other Laizhou wine-tax officials memorialized: "In conquering the remnant Song the state acted to succor the afflicted and punish the wicked—never for profit or plunder." "Petty men who betray the enterprise, greedy for gain, raid under the name of freebooters, sell captives for wine and food—victory brings the court no benefit, defeat brings national shame." "Freebooters under the Pacification Commission should all be discharged, graded, registered as regular soldiers, and placed under commanders of ten thousand households for campaign—both to gain their real utility and to restore the royal army's righteous name." The proposal was approved.
23
十四年正月，詔：「上都、隆興、西京、北京四路編民捕獵等戶，簽選丁壯軍二千人，防守上都。 」中書省議：「從各路搭配，二十五戶內取軍一名，選善騎射者充，官給行資中統鈔一錠，仍自備鞍馬衣裝器仗，編立牌甲，差官部領，前來赴役。 」十二月，樞密院臣言：「收附亡宋州城，新附請糧官軍，並通事馬軍人等，軍官不肯存恤，多逃散者，乞招誘之。 」命左丞陳巖等，分揀堪當軍役者，收系充軍，依舊例月支錢糧。 其生券不堪當軍者，官給牛具糧食，屯田種養。
In the first month of the fourteenth year an edict ordered 2,000 able-bodied soldiers levied from registered civilians and hunters in the Shangdu, Longxing, Xijing, and Beijing circuits to defend the upper capital. The Central Secretariat decided: one soldier from every twenty-five households across the circuits, skilled in riding and archery, given one ingot of Zhongtong paper money for travel but providing their own mounts, arms, and equipment, organized into squads under officers sent to serve. In the twelfth month the Bureau of Military Affairs reported: "Newly attached Song soldiers and interpreter cavalry are poorly treated by their officers and many have deserted; we ask permission to recruit them back." Left Chancellor Chen Yan and others were ordered to select those fit for service, enroll them as soldiers, and pay monthly rations according to precedent. Life-bond soldiers unfit for duty were given oxen, grain, and land for garrison-colony farming.
24
十五年正月，定軍官承襲之制。 凡軍官之有功者升其秩，元受之職，令他有功者居之，不得令子侄復代。 陣亡者始得承襲，病死者降一等。 總把、百戶老病死，不在承襲之例。 凡將校臨陣中傷、還營病創者，亦令與陣亡之人一體承襲。 禁長軍之官不恤士卒，及士卒亡命避役，侵擾初附百姓者，俱有罪。 雲南行省言：「雲南舊屯駐蒙古軍甚少，遂取漸長成丁怯困都等軍，以備出征。 雲南闊遠，多未降之地，必須用兵，已簽爨、僰人一萬為軍，續取新降落落、和泥等人，亦令充軍。 然其人與中原不同，若赴別地出征，必致逃匿，宜令就各所居一方未降處用之。 」九月，並軍士。 初，至元九年簽軍三萬，止擇精銳年壯者，不復問其貲產，且無貼戶之助，歲久多貧乏不堪。 樞密院臣奏，宜縱為民，遂並為一萬五千。 諸軍戶投充諸侯王怯憐口、人匠，或托為別戶以避其役者，復令為軍，有良匠則別而出之。 樞密臣又言：「至元八年，於各路軍之為富商大賈者一百四十三戶，各增一軍，號余丁軍。 今東平等路諸奧魯總管府言，往往人死產乏，不能充二軍，乞免余丁充役者。 」制可。 十二月，樞密院官議：「諸軍官在軍籍者，除百戶、總把權準軍役，其元帥、招討、萬戶、總管、千戶或首領官，俱合再當正軍一名。」
In the first month of the fifteenth year regulations for hereditary succession of military offices were established. Meritorious officers were promoted in rank; their original posts went to other meritorious men, not to sons or nephews. Only those who fell in battle could pass their rank to heirs; those who died of illness passed down one grade lower. Chief commanders and commanders of a hundred households who died of old age or illness could not pass their offices to heirs. Officers wounded in battle or dying of wounds after returning to camp inherited on the same terms as those killed in action. Officers who failed to care for their men, and soldiers who deserted or harassed newly submitted civilians, were all subject to punishment. The Yunnan Branch Secretariat reported: "Few Mongol troops were formerly garrisoned in Yunnan, so Gradually Mature and Qiedundu troops were levied for campaigns." "Yunnan is vast and remote with many unsubmitted regions requiring troops. Ten thousand Cuan and Bo have been conscripted; newly submitted tribes and Heni people are also ordered to serve." "These peoples differ from the Central Plains and will desert if sent elsewhere; they should campaign only in unsubmitted regions near their homes." In the ninth month troop rolls were consolidated. Originally in Zhiyuan 9 thirty thousand elite young soldiers were levied without regard to wealth and without supplementary households; over the years many became destitute. The Bureau of Military Affairs recommended releasing the poorest as civilians, reducing the force to fifteen thousand. Military households who had entered princely service or claimed other status to evade duty were restored to the rolls; skilled artisans were separately exempted. The Bureau again reported: "In Zhiyuan 8, 143 wealthy military households in the circuits each supplied an additional soldier called surplus-male soldiers." "The Dongping ordo offices report that many households, depleted by death and poverty, cannot supply two soldiers and ask exemption from surplus-male service." Approved. In the twelfth month the Bureau ruled that officers on the military rolls—apart from commanders of a hundred and chief commanders provisionally exempt—marshals, pacification commissioners, commanders of ten thousand and a thousand households, and chief officers must each additionally serve as one principal soldier.
25
十六年正月，罷五翼探馬赤重役軍。 三月，括兩淮造回回炮新附軍匠六百人，及蒙古、回回、漢人、新附人能造炮者，至京師。 五月，淮西道宣慰司官昂吉兒請招諭亡宋通事軍，俾屬之麾下。 初，亡宋多招納北地蒙古人為通事軍，遇之甚厚，每戰皆列於前行，願效死力。 及宋亡，無所歸。 朝議欲編入版籍未暇也，人人疑懼，皆不自安。 至是，昂吉兒請招集，列之行伍，以備征戍。 從之。 九月，詔河西地未簽軍之官，及富強戶有物力者，簽軍六百人。 十月，壽州等處招討使李鐵哥，請召募有罪亡命之人充軍，其言：「使功不如使過。 始南宋未平時，蒙古、諸色人等，因得罪皆亡命往依焉，今已平定，尚逃匿林藪。 若釋其罪而用之，必能效力，無不一當十者矣。 」十一月，罷太原、平陽、西京、延安路新簽軍還籍。
In the first month of the sixteenth year duplicate-service Tamma troops of the five wings were abolished. In the third month six hundred Muslim-artillery artisans from the two Huai and all Mongols, Muslims, Han, and newly attached men skilled in gunnery were summoned to the capital. In the fifth month Angur of the Huaixi Pacification Commission asked to recruit the former Song interpreter army under his command. The Song had recruited many northern Mongols as interpreter troops, treated them generously, placed them in the front ranks, and they fought with desperate loyalty. When the Song fell they had nowhere to go. The court had intended to register them but had not yet done so; all were anxious and uneasy. Now Angur asked to gather them into ranks for campaign and garrison duty. Approved. In the ninth month an edict ordered 600 soldiers levied from Hexi officials not yet conscripted and wealthy households. In the tenth month Li Tiege, pacification commissioner of Shouzhou, asked to recruit fugitive criminals as soldiers, saying: "It is better to employ the guilty than the meritorious." "Before the Southern Song was conquered, Mongols and others who had committed crimes fled to join them; now that the realm is pacified they still hide in the wilderness." "If their crimes are pardoned and they are employed, each will fight as ten men." In the eleventh month newly conscripted soldiers in Taiyuan, Pingyang, Xijing, and Yan'an were discharged and restored to the rolls.
26
十七年七月，詔江淮諸路招集答剌罕軍。 初平江南，募死士願從軍者，號答剌罕，屬之劉萬戶麾下。 南北既混一，復散之，其人皆無所歸，率群聚剽掠。 至是，命諸路招集之，令萬奴部領如故，聽範左丞、李拔都二人節制。
In the seventh month of the seventeenth year an edict ordered the Jianghuai circuits to gather the Darqan Army. When Jiangnan was first conquered, volunteers were recruited as the Darqan Army under Commander Liu of ten thousand households. After unification they were dispersed again; with nowhere to go they banded together for plunder. Now the circuits were ordered to gather them under Wannu's command, subject to Left Chancellor Fan and Li Badu.
27
十八年二月，並貧乏軍人三萬戶為一萬五千，取帖戶津帖正軍充役。 四月，置蒙古、漢人、新附軍總管。 六月，樞密院議：「正軍貧乏無丁者，令富強丁多帖戶權充正軍應役，驗正軍物力，卻令津濟貼戶，其正軍仍為軍頭如故。 或正軍實系單丁者，許傭雇練習之人應役，丁多者不得傭雇，軍官亦不得以親從人代之。」
In the second month of the eighteenth year thirty thousand impoverished military households were consolidated to fifteen thousand, with supplementary households subsidizing principal soldiers. In the fourth month general supervisors were established for Mongol, Han, and Newly Attached armies. In the sixth month the Bureau ruled that poor principal soldiers without adult males might have wealthy supplementary households serve in their place, with principals subsidizing supplements while remaining squad leaders. "Principal soldiers with only one adult male may hire trained substitutes; those with many sons may not; officers may not substitute personal attendants."
28
十九年二月，諸侯王阿只吉遣使言：「探馬赤軍凡九處出征，各奧魯內復征雜泛徭役，不便。 」詔免之，並詔有司毋重役軍戶。 六月，禁長軍之官，毋得占役士卒。 散定海答剌罕軍還各營，及歸戍城邑。 十月，簽發漸丁軍士。 遵舊制，家止一丁者不作數，凡二丁至五丁、六丁之家，止存一人，餘皆充軍。
In the second month of the nineteenth year Prince Ajiji reported: "Tamma troops have campaigned in nine regions while their ordo still bear miscellaneous corvée—this is burdensome." An edict granted exemption and forbade officials from imposing heavy burdens on military households. In the sixth month officers were forbidden to use their soldiers for private labor. The Dinghai Darqan Army was dispersed to their camps and garrison cities. In the tenth month Gradually Mature soldiers were conscripted. Following old regulations, single-son households were exempt; households with two to six sons kept one and conscripted the rest.
29
二十年二月，命各處行樞密院造新附軍籍冊。 六月，從丞相伯顏議，所括宋手號軍八萬三千六百人，立牌甲，設官以統之。 十月，定出征軍人亡命之罪，為首者斬，余令減死一等。
In the second month of the twentieth year traveling Bureaus of Military Affairs were ordered to compile Newly Attached Army registers. In the sixth month, following Chancellor Bayan's recommendation, 83,600 Song tattoo-mark soldiers were organized into squads under appointed officers. In the tenth month penalties for deserters on campaign were fixed: ringleaders were beheaded, the rest received one grade below death.
30
二十一年八月，江東道僉事馬奉訓言：「劉萬奴乾討虜軍，私相糾合，結為徒黨，張弓挾矢，或詐稱使臣，莫若散之各翼萬戶、千戶、百戶、牌甲內管領為便。 」省院官以聞，有旨，可令問此軍：「欲從脫歡出征虜掠耶？ 欲且放散還家耶？ 」回奏：「眾軍皆言，自圍襄樊渡江以來，與國效力，願令還家少息。 」遂從之。 籍亡宋手記軍。 宋時有是軍，死則以兄弟若子承代。 有旨，依漢軍例籍之，毋涅其手。
In the eighth month of the twenty-first year Ma Fengxun of Jiangdong reported: "Liu Wannu's freebooters band together, bear arms, and impersonate envoys—it would be better to disperse them among commanders of ten thousand, a thousand, and a hundred households and squads." Provincial and bureau officials reported to the throne, which ordered the army asked: "Do you wish to follow Prince Toqan on campaign for plunder?" "Or do you wish to be discharged and return home for now?" They replied: "All the soldiers say that since the siege of Xiangyang and Fan and the crossing of the Yangzi they have served the state and wish to return home for rest." The request was approved. The former Song hand-mark army was registered. Under the Song this army existed; when a soldier died a brother or son took his place. The throne ordered them registered as Han army without tattooing their hands.
31
二十二年正月，立行樞密院於江南三省，其各處行省見管軍馬悉以付焉。 九月，詔福建黃華畬軍，有恒產者放為民，無恒產與妻子者編為守城軍。 征交趾蒙古軍五百人、漢軍二千人，除留蒙古軍百人、漢軍四百人，為鎮南王脫歡宿衛，余悉遣還，別以江淮行樞密院蒙古軍戍江西。 十月，從月的迷失言，以乾討虜軍七百人，籍名數，立牌甲，命將官之無軍者領之。 十一月，御史臺臣言：「昔宋以無室家壯士為鹽軍，內附之初，有五千人，除征占城運糧死亡者，今存一千一百二十二人。 此徒皆性習凶暴，民患苦之，宜給以衣糧，使屯田自贍，庶絕其擾。 」從之。 十二月，從樞密院請，嚴立軍籍條例，選壯士及有力之家充軍。 舊例，丁力強者充軍，弱者出錢，故有正軍、貼戶之籍。 行之既久，而強者弱，弱者強，籍亦如故。 其同戶異居者，私立年期，以相更代，故有老稚不免從軍，而強壯家居者，至是革焉。 江浙省募鹽徒為軍，得四千七百六十六人，選軍官麾下無士卒者，相參統之，以備各處鎮守。
In the first month of the twenty-second year traveling Bureaus of Military Affairs were established in the three Jiangnan provinces, with all troops under regional secretariats placed under their command. In the ninth month an edict ordered that She soldiers of Huang Hua in Fujian with property be released as civilians, and those without property, with their families, be enrolled as city-garrison troops. Of the five hundred Mongol and two thousand Han soldiers sent against Jiaozhi, one hundred Mongols and four hundred Han were retained as palace guard for Prince Toqan of Zhennan and the rest sent home; Mongol troops of the Jianghuai traveling Bureau garrisoned Jiangxi. In the tenth month, following Yedishire's recommendation, seven hundred freebooters were registered, organized into squads, and placed under officers without commands. In the eleventh month censors reported: "The Song formerly conscripted unmarried men as salt soldiers; five thousand submitted initially; apart from those who died transporting grain to Champa, 1,122 remain." "These men are habitually violent and the people suffer from them. They should be given clothing and grain for garrison farming so they can support themselves and cease their harassment." The proposal was approved. In the twelfth month, at the Bureau's request, military registration rules were tightened and able-bodied men from powerful households were selected for service. Under the old rule the strongest adult males served as soldiers while weaker households paid money, hence the distinction between principal and supplementary military households. Over time the strong grew weak and the weak grew strong, yet the registers were never updated. Households living apart privately arranged rotations by term of years, so the elderly and young were conscripted while the strong stayed home—this abuse was now abolished. Jiangzhe recruited 4,766 salt convicts as soldiers and assigned them to officers without troops for garrison duty throughout the province.
32
二十四年閏二月，樞密院臣言：「諸軍貼戶，有正軍已死者，有充工匠者，放為民者，有元系各投下戶回付者，似此歇閑一千三百四十戶，乞差人分揀貧富，定貼戶、正軍。 」制可。
In the intercalary second month of the twenty-fourth year the Bureau reported: "Among supplementary military households, 1,340 are idle because principal soldiers have died, become artisans, been released as civilians, or returned to appanages. We ask that officials sort them by wealth and reassign principal and supplementary status." Approved.
33
二十六年八月，樞密院議：「諸管軍官萬戶、千戶、百戶等，或治軍有法、鎮守無虞、鎧仗精完、差役均平、軍無逃竄者，許所司薦舉以聞，不次擢用。 諸軍吏之長，非有上司之命，毋擅離職。 諸長軍者，及蒙古、漢軍，毋得妄言邊事。」
In the eighth month of the twenty-sixth year the Bureau ruled that commanders of ten thousand, a thousand, and a hundred households who governed troops well, kept garrisons secure, maintained arms in good order, distributed duties fairly, and prevented desertion might be recommended for exceptional promotion. "Chief military clerks must not leave their posts without orders from superiors." "Officers and Mongol and Han troops must not speak rashly of frontier affairs."
34
成宗大德二年十二月，定各省提調軍馬官員。 凡用隨從軍士，蒙古長官三十名，次官二十名，漢人一十名； 萬戶、千戶、百戶人等，俱不得占役。 行省鎮撫止用聽探外，亦不得多余役占。
In the twelfth month of Dade 2 under Emperor Chengzong, regulations were set for provincial officials authorized to command troops. Attendant soldiers were limited to thirty for Mongol senior officials, twenty for junior officials, and ten for Han officials; commanders of ten thousand, a thousand, and a hundred households were all forbidden to use soldiers for private labor. Branch secretariat pacification commissioners, apart from scouts, could not take additional soldiers for private use.
35
十一年四月，詔禮店軍還屬土番宣慰司。 初，西川也速叠兒、按住奴、帖木兒等所統探馬赤軍，自壬子年屬籍禮店，隸王相府，後王相府罷，屬之陜西省，桑哥奏屬土番宣慰司，咸以為不便，大德十年命依壬子之籍，至是復改屬焉。
In the fourth month of the eleventh year an edict returned the Lidian army to the Tubo Pacification Commission. The Tamma army of Yesuder, Anzhunu, and Temür in Sichuan had been registered at Lidian since the renzi year under the Prince's Secretariat; after that office was abolished it passed to Shaanxi; Sangge placed it under the Tubo Pacification Commission, which proved inconvenient; in Dade 10 it reverted to the renzi arrangement; now jurisdiction was changed again.
36
武宗至大元年正月，以通惠河千戶劉粲所領運糧軍九百二十人，屬萬戶赤因帖木爾兵籍。 十二月，丞相三寶奴等言：「國制，行省佐貳及宣慰使不得提調軍馬，若遙授平章、揚州宣慰使阿憐帖木兒者，嘗與成宗同乳母，故得行之，非常憲也。 今命沙的代之，宜遵國制，勿令提調。 」制可。
In the first month of Zhida 1 under Emperor Wuzong, 920 grain-transport soldiers under Liu Can of the Tonghui Canal were transferred to the register of Commander Chiyintemür of ten thousand households. In the twelfth month Chancellor Sanbaonu reported: "By state law branch secretariat deputies and pacification commissioners may not command troops. Alintemür of Yangzhou, who shared a wet nurse with Chengzong, was an exception, not the rule." "Now that Shadi replaces him, he should follow state law and not command troops." Approved.
37
仁宗皇慶元年三月，中書省臣奏李馬哥等四百戶為民。 初，李馬哥等四百戶屬諸侯王脫脫，乙未年定籍為民，高麗林衍及乃顏叛，皆嘗簽為軍。 至元八年置軍籍，以李馬哥等非七十二萬戶內軍數，復改為民。 至大四年，樞密院復奏為軍。 至是，省官以為言，命遵乙未年已定之籍。 後樞密復奏，竟以為軍戶。 十二月，省臣言：「先是樞密院奏準，雲南省宜遵各省制，其省官居長者二員，得佩虎符，提調軍馬，余佐貳者不得預，已受虎符者悉收之。 今雲南省言，本省籍軍士之力，以辦集錢谷，遇有調遣，則省官親率眾上馬，故舊制雖牧民官亦得佩虎符，領軍務，視他省為不同。 臣等議，已受虎符者依故事，未受者宜頒賜之。 」制可。
In the third month of Huangqing 1 under Emperor Renzong the Central Secretariat memorialized that the 400 households of Li Mage and others be restored to civilian status. Originally the 400 households of Li Mage belonged to Prince Toto and were registered as civilians in the yimao year; during the rebellions of Lin Yan of Goryeo and Nayan they were temporarily conscripted. In Zhiyuan 8 they were entered on military rolls but restored to civilian status because they were not part of the 720,000-household quota. In Zhida 4 the Bureau of Military Affairs again recommended conscripting them. Now provincial officials reported accordingly and the court ordered adherence to the yimao registers. Later the Bureau memorialized again and they were ultimately made military households. In the twelfth month provincial ministers reported: "The Bureau had approved that Yunnan follow other provinces' rules: only the two senior officials might wear tiger tallies and command troops; deputies might not; existing tallies were to be collected." "Yunnan reports that it relies on registered soldiers to collect revenue and that provincial officials personally lead troops on campaign. Hence even civil administrators traditionally wore tiger tallies and commanded troops, unlike other provinces." "We recommend that those who already hold tallies keep them by precedent, and that others be granted them." Approved.
38
二年正月，詔：「雲南省鎮遠方，掌邊務，凡事涉軍旅者，自平章至僚佐須同署押，其長官二員，復與哈必赤。」
In the first month of the second year an edict declared: "Yunnan guards a distant frontier; all military matters, from associate administrators to aides, require joint signature, and the two senior officials additionally use the qabichi seal."
39
延祐元年二月，四川省軍官闕員，詔：「依民官遷調之制，差人與本省提調官及監察御史同銓註。」
In the second month of Yanyou 1, with military officer vacancies in Sichuan, an edict ordered selections made jointly with provincial troop commanders and censors according to the civil transfer system.
40
三年三月，命伯顏都萬戶府及紅胖襖總帥府各調軍九千五百人，往諸侯王所，更代守邊士卒。 其屬都萬戶府者，軍一名，馬三匹； 屬總帥府者，軍一名，馬二匹。 令人自為計，其貧不能自備者，則命行伍之長及百戶、千戶等助之。 悉遣精銳練習騎射之士。 每軍一百名，百戶一員； 五百名，千戶一員。 復命買住、囊加<角┦>二人分左右部領之。
In the third month of the third year Bayan's command of ten thousand households and the Red Coats grand marshal's headquarters were each ordered to send 9,500 soldiers to princes' domains to relieve border garrisons. Those from the command of ten thousand households supplied one soldier and three horses each; those from the grand marshal's headquarters one soldier and two horses each. Men were to equip themselves; the poor were to be assisted by squad leaders and commanders of a hundred and a thousand households. Only elite troops trained in riding and archery were sent. Every hundred soldiers had one commander of a hundred households; every five hundred had one commander of a thousand households. Maizhu and Nangajiao were appointed to command the left and right divisions respectively.
