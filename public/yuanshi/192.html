耶律伯堅，字壽之，桓州人。 氣豪俠，喜與名士遊。 用薦舉入官，為工部主事。 至元九年，轉保定路清苑縣尹。 初，安肅州苦徐水之害，訴於大司農司，大司農司欲奪水故道，導水使東。 東則清苑境也，地勢不利，果導之，則清苑被其害，而水亦必反故道為災。 伯堅陳其形勢，圖其利害，要大司農司官及郡守行視可否，事遂得已。 縣西有塘水，溉民田甚廣，勢家據以為磑，民以失利來訴。 伯堅命毀磑，決其水而注之田，許以溉田之餘月，乃得堰水置磑。 仍以其事聞於省部，著為定制。 縣居南北之衝，歲為親王大官治供帳於縣西，限以十月成，至明年復撤而新之，吏得併緣侵漁，其費不貲。 伯堅命築公館，以代供帳，其弊遂絕。 凡郡府賦役，於縣有重於他縣者，輒曰：「寧得罪於上，不可得罪於下。 」必詣府力爭之。 在清苑四年，民親戴之如父母，比去而猶思之，立石頌其德焉。 擢為恩州同知。
Ye Lü Bojian, courtesy name Shouzhi, came from Huanzhou. He had a bold, chivalrous temperament and loved to keep company with distinguished men of letters. On recommendation he joined the bureaucracy and was appointed secretary in the Ministry of Works. In 1272 he was reassigned as magistrate of Qingyuan County on the Baoding circuit. Earlier, Ansuzhou had been plagued by flooding from the Xu River and petitioned the Grand Secretariat of Agriculture, which planned to seize the river's former channel and divert its waters eastward. East lay Qingyuan territory, where the land lay low and ill-suited to such a change; if the diversion went ahead, Qingyuan would bear the damage, and the water would almost certainly revert to its old bed and bring disaster again. Bojian laid out the terrain and spelled out the pros and cons, insisting that Grand Secretariat officials and the prefect come see for themselves whether the scheme would work; the project was dropped. West of the county lay a reservoir that watered a broad swath of farmland; powerful families had seized it to run mills, and peasants came forward complaining of lost livelihoods. He ordered the mills torn down, broke their embankments to restore flow to the fields, and allowed mills only in the slack months when irrigation did not require the water. He also reported the case to provincial headquarters, and it was codified as standing policy. The county stood on the main north-south route, and each year the court had supply encampments built west of town, due by the tenth month—only to be torn down the following year and rebuilt from scratch. Clerks milked the cycle for graft, and the expense was incalculable. Bojian had a public lodge erected in place of the seasonal camps, and the abuse ceased. Whenever prefectural taxes or labor fell on his county more heavily than elsewhere, he would declare, "I would rather anger my superiors than wrong the people." He would go to the prefecture and fight the assessment tooth and nail. For four years in Qingyuan the people loved him as they would their own parents; long after he left they still missed him and set up a stone praising his virtue. He was promoted to deputy prefect of Enzhou.

段直，字正卿，澤州晉城人。 至元十一年，河北、河東、山東盜賊充斥，直聚其鄉黨族屬，結壘自保。 世祖命大將略地晉城，直以其眾歸之，幕府承製，署直潞州元帥府右監軍。 其後論功行賞，分土世守，命直佩金符，為澤州長官。 澤民多避兵未還者，直命籍其田廬於親戚鄰人之戶，且約曰：「俟業主至，當析而歸之。 」逃民聞之，多來還者，命歸其田廬如約，民得安業。 素無產者，則出粟賑之； 為他郡所俘掠者，出財購之； 以兵死而暴露者，收而瘞之。 未幾，澤為樂土。 大修孔子廟，割田千畝，置書萬卷，迎儒士李俊民為師，以招延四方來學者。 不五六年，學之士子，以通經被選者，百二十有二人。 在官二十年，多有惠政。 朝廷特命提舉本州學校事，未拜而卒。
Duan Zhi, courtesy name Zhengqing, was from Jincheng in Zezhou. In 1274 banditry engulfed Hebei, Hedong, and Shandong; Duan rallied his neighbors and kinsmen and fortified a stronghold for their protection. Kublai sent a senior commander to secure Jincheng; Duan brought his followers over and the campaign headquarters, under emergency powers, named him deputy overseer on the Luzhou marshal's staff. Later, when rewards were apportioned and hereditary fiefs granted, Duan was given a gold tally and made chief administrator of Zezhou. Many Ze residents had fled the fighting and not returned; Duan had their land and homes registered to relatives and neighbors on the promise that "when the owners come back, every plot and roof will be returned to them." Refugees heard of this and streamed home; he restored fields and houses as promised, and the people could live in peace again. Families with nothing to their name received grain relief; those seized as captives in other districts were bought back at his expense; the war dead left unburied on the fields were gathered in and laid to rest. Before long Ze had become a haven of peace. He refurbished the Confucian temple on a grand scale, endowed a thousand mu of land, stocked ten thousand books, and brought in Li Junmin as master-teacher to draw students from far and wide. Within five or six years a hundred twenty-two of his students had passed selection on the strength of classical learning. Over twenty years in office he earned a reputation for benevolent rule. The court named him to oversee the prefectural schools, but he died before he could take up the appointment.

諳都剌，字瑞芝，凱烈氏。 祖阿思蘭，嘗從大將阿術伐宋，仕至冀寧路達魯花赤，子孫因其名蘭，遂以蘭為氏。 諳都剌通經史，兼習諸國語。 成宗時，為翰林院札爾里赤，職書制誥。 會有旨命書籓王添力聖旨，諳都剌曰：「此旨非惟有虧國體，行且為民殃矣。 」帝聞之，謂近臣曰：「小吏如此，真難得也。 」事乃止。 尋授應奉翰林文字，凡蒙古傳記，多所校正。 升待制。 時方選守令，除遼州達魯花赤，以最聞，賜上尊名幣，除集賢直學士。 至順元年，遷襄陽路達魯花赤。 山西大饑，河南行省恐流民入境為變，檄守武關，諳都剌驗其良民，輒聽其度關。 吏曰：「得無違上命乎？ 」諳都剌曰：「吾防奸耳，非仇良民也，可不開其生路耶！ 」既又煮粥以食之，所活數万人。 又城臨漢水，歲有水患，為築堤城外，遂以無虞。 元統二年，除益都路總管。 俗頗悍黠，而諳都剌務興學校，以平易治之。 有上馬賊白晝劫人，久不能捕，諳都剌生擒之，其黨賂宣慰使羅鍋，誣以枉勘，縱其賊。 已而賊劫河間，復被獲，乃盡輸其情，而諳都剌之誣始白，俾再任一考。 親王買奴鎮益都，其府屬病民，諳都剌裁抑之，民以無擾。 至正六年卒，年七十。
Oqatël, courtesy name Ruizhi, was a Qarluq by clan. His grandfather Aslan had followed the general Aju against the Song and served as darughachi of Jining; later generations took Lan from his name as their family name. Oqatël was steeped in the classics and histories and fluent in several court languages besides. Under Temür (Chengzong) he served as the Hanlin Academy's jarlichi, responsible for drafting patents and edicts. Once the court ordered him to draft an edict for the frontier prince Tianli; Oqatël objected: "Such a patent would disgrace the realm and bring ruin on the people besides." The emperor, hearing of it, told his intimates, "For a junior clerk to show such spirit—that is truly exceptional." The order was withdrawn. He was soon made Hanlin editorial drafter and corrected many errors in Mongolian historical texts. He rose to Hanlin academician. During a round of provincial appointments he was named darughachi of Liaozhou; his excellence won imperial wine and honors, and he was made direct scholar of the Jixian Academy. In 1330 he was transferred to serve as darughachi of Xiangyang. When Shanxi was struck by famine the Henan secretariat feared unrest from refugees and sealed Wuguan Pass; Oqatël screened peaceful families and let them through. A clerk asked, "Are you not defying headquarters?" Oqatël replied, "I am guarding against rogues, not hounding honest folk—should I not leave them a way to survive?" He also had gruel cooked for them, saving tens of thousands of lives. The city fronted the Han, which flooded yearly; he built outer embankments and the town was thereafter secure. In 1334 he was made chief administrator of Yidu circuit. The region was rough and shrewd, but Oqatël promoted schools and governed with plain, gentle fairness. Mounted robbers had terrorized the roads by day for years without capture; Oqatël took them alive. Their associates bribed Pacification Commissioner Luo Guo to accuse him of wrongful prosecution and the thieves walked free. Later the same band struck Hejian and were caught again; they confessed everything, clearing Oqatël's name and earning him another term in office. Prince Mainu was posted to Yidu with a household staff that harassed the people; Oqatël curbed them and peace returned. He died in 1346 at seventy.

His son Seyiqi, deputy prefect of Xinyu, was renowned for filial devotion.

楊景行，字賢可，吉安太和州人。 登延祐二年進士第，授贛州路會昌州判官。 會昌民素不知井飲，汲於河流，故多疾癘； 不知陶瓦，以茅覆屋，故多火災。 景行教民穿井以飲，陶瓦以代茅茨，民始免於疾癘火災。 豪民十人，號十虎，干政害民，悉捕置之法。 乃創學舍，禮師儒，勸民斥腴田以膳士，弦誦之聲遂盛。 調永新州判官，奉郡府命，核民田租，除剷宿弊，姦欺不容，細民賴焉。 改江西行省照磨，轉撫州路宜黃縣尹，理白冤獄之不決者數十事。 升撫州路總管府推官，發擿姦伏，郡無冤獄。 金溪縣民陶甲，厚積而凶險，嘗屢誣陷其縣長吏罷去之，由是官吏畏其人，不敢詰治，陶遂暴橫於一郡。 景行至，以法痛繩之，徙五百里外。 金溪豪僧云住，發人塚墓取財物，事覺，官吏受賄，緩其獄，景行急按之，僧以賄動之，不聽，乃賂當道者，以危語撼之，一不顧，卒治之如法。 由是豪猾屏跡，良民獲安。 轉湖州路歸安縣尹，奉行省命，理荒田租，民無欺弊。 景行所歷州縣，皆有惠政； 所去，民皆立石頌之。 以翰林待制、朝列大夫致仕，年七十四卒。
Yang Jingxing, courtesy name Xiankai, was from Taihe in Ji'an. He took his jinshi degree in 1315 and was appointed assistant prefect of Huichang in Ganzhou circuit. Huichang folk had never drawn drinking water from wells and took it from the river instead, so epidemics were common; they knew nothing of tile and thatched their roofs, so fires were frequent. Jingxing taught them to sink wells for drinking water and to roof with tile instead of thatch, and the district was finally spared epidemics and conflagrations. Ten local strongmen known as the Ten Tigers had meddled in government and preyed on the people; he arrested them all and punished them to the full extent of the law. He founded a school, honored Confucian masters, and urged families to set aside fertile land to support students; soon the district rang with study. Transferred to Yongxin as assistant prefect, he audited land taxes on prefectural orders, uprooted entrenched abuses, brooked no fraud, and ordinary people came to depend on him. He became archival reviewer for the Jiangxi secretariat, then magistrate of Yihuang in Fuzhou circuit, and cleared dozens of long-stalled wrongful convictions. Promoted to investigating officer for Fuzhou circuit, he exposed hidden crimes until the prefecture held no wrongful cases. Tao Jia of Jinxi was rich, brutal, and had repeatedly framed county magistrates until they were removed; officials thereafter feared him and dared not prosecute, and Tao tyrannized the whole prefecture. When Jingxing took office he punished Tao to the full letter of the law and exiled him five hundred li distant. A powerful Jinxi monk named Yunzhu had been robbing graves; when the crime surfaced, officials took bribes and stalled the case. Jingxing pressed the prosecution; the monk tried bribery, then bribed higher authorities and threatened him—Jingxing ignored it all and punished him by law. The bullies vanished from sight and honest folk lived in peace. As magistrate of Gui'an in Huzhou he carried out provincial orders to settle tax on abandoned land, and the people were spared fraud and abuse. In every post he held Jingxing left benevolent policies behind; and wherever he moved on, the people set up stones in his praise. He retired as Hanlin academician and Grand Master of the Court and died at seventy-four.

林興祖，字宗起，福州羅源人。 至治二年，登進士第，授承事郎、同知黃岩州事，三遷而知鉛山州。 鉛山素多造偽鈔者，豪民吳友文為之魁，遠至江淮、燕薊，莫不行使。 友文姦黠悍鷙，因偽造致富，乃分遣惡少四五十人，為吏於有司，伺有欲告之者，輒先事戕之，前後殺人甚眾，奪人妻女十一人為妾，民罹其害，銜冤不敢訴者十餘年。 興祖至官，曰：「此害不除，何以牧民！ 」即張榜禁偽造者，且立賞募民首告。 俄有告者至，佯以不實斥去； 又有告獲偽造二人並贓者，乃鞫之，款成。 友文自至官，為之營救，興祖命並執之。 須臾，來訴友文者百餘人，擇其重罪一二事鞫之，獄立具，逮捕其黨二百餘人，悉置之法。 民害既去，政聲籍甚。 江浙行省丞相別兒怯不花薦諸朝，升南陽知府，改建德路同知，俱未任。 至正八年，特旨遷為道州路總管，行至城外，撞賊已迫其後，相去僅二十里。 時湖南副使哈剌帖木兒屯兵城外，聞賊至，以乏軍需，欲退兵，興祖聞，即夜詣說留之。 哈剌帖木兒曰：「明日得鈔五千錠、桐盾五百，乃可破賊。 」興祖許之。 明日甫入城視事，即以恩信勸諭鹽商，貸鈔五千錠，且取郡樓舊桐板為盾，日中皆備。 哈剌帖木兒得鈔、盾，大喜，遂留，為御賊計。 賊聞新總管至，一日具五百盾，以為大軍且至，中夕遁去。 永明縣洞徭屢竊發為民害，興祖以手榜諭之。 皆曰：「林總管廉而愛民，不可犯也。 」三年不入境。 春旱，蟲食麥苗，興祖為文禱之，大雨三日，蟲死而麥稔。 已而罷興作，賑貧乏，輕徭薄斂，郡中大治，憲司考課，以道州為最。 以年老致仕，終於家。
Lin Xingzu, courtesy name Zongqi, was from Luoyuan in Fuzhou. He took his jinshi in 1322, was appointed deputy prefect of Huangyan, and after three promotions became prefect of Qianshan. Qianshan had long been notorious for counterfeit banknotes, with the magnate Wu Youwen as ringleader; his notes circulated as far as the Jiang-Huai and Yan-Ji regions. Youwen was cunning, fierce, and brutal; counterfeiting made him rich, and he planted forty or fifty thugs as clerks in government offices to murder anyone who tried to inform before they could act—killing many over the years and seizing eleven women as concubines. For more than a decade the people suffered in silence, too afraid to sue. On taking office Xingzu declared, "If this scourge is not uprooted, how can I govern the people!" He posted notices forbidding counterfeiting and offered rewards for informants. When an informant soon appeared, he pretended to dismiss the charge as false; then came word that two counterfeiters had been seized with the evidence; he interrogated them until they confessed fully. Youwen came to the yamen in person to intervene; Xingzu ordered him arrested too. Within moments more than a hundred accusers came forward; he tried one or two of the gravest charges, the case was complete at once, and over two hundred accomplices were arrested and punished. With the scourge gone, his reputation for good government spread far and wide. Jiang-Zhe chancellor Bayiqinbuqa recommended him to court; he was promoted to prefect of Nanyang and then deputy prefect of Jiande, but never took either post. In 1348 he was specially appointed chief administrator of Daozhou; as he reached the suburbs, rebel forces were only twenty li behind. Hunan vice commissioner Qaratemür was camped outside the walls; hearing the rebels approach and lacking supplies, he meant to withdraw. Xingzu went to him that night and persuaded him to stand firm. Qaratemür said, "Give me five thousand ingots of paper money and five hundred paulownia shields by tomorrow, and I can break the rebels." Xingzu agreed. The next morning, as soon as he entered the city to take office, he persuaded salt merchants on trust to lend five thousand ingots and had old paulownia boards from the city tower fashioned into shields; everything was ready by noon. Qaratemür received the money and shields, was delighted, and stayed to plan the defense. The rebels heard a new chief administrator had arrived and that five hundred shields had been readied in a day; believing a large army was coming, they fled at midnight. The cave Yao of Yongming had repeatedly raided the countryside; Xingzu addressed them with a handwritten proclamation. They all said, "Prefect Lin is honest and loves the people—we must not offend him." For three years they did not cross the border. A spring drought brought locusts on the wheat; Xingzu wrote a prayer, rain fell for three days, the pests died, and the crop ripened. He halted wasteful public works, relieved the poor, lightened labor and tax burdens, and brought the prefecture to good order; in the surveillance commission's review Daozhou ranked first. He retired in old age and died at home.

觀音奴，字志能，唐兀人氏，居新州。 登泰定四年進士第。 由戶部主事，再轉而知歸德府。 廉明剛斷，發擿如神。 民有銜冤不直者，雖數十年前事，皆千里奔走來訴，觀音奴立為剖決，旬日悉清。 彰德富商任甲，抵睢陽，驢斃，令郄乙剖之，任以怒毆郄，經宿而死。 郄有妻王氏、妾孫氏，孫訴於官，官吏納任賄，謂郄非傷死，反抵孫罪，置之獄。 王來訴冤，觀音奴立破械出孫於獄，呼府胥語之曰：「吾為文具香幣，若為吾以郄事禱諸城隍神，令神顯於吾。 」有睢陽小吏，亦預郄事，畏觀音奴嚴明，且懼神顯其事，乃以任所賂鈔陳首曰：「郄實傷死，任賂上下匿其實，吾亦得賂，敢以首。 」於是罪任商而釋孫妾。 寧陵豪民楊甲，夙嗜王乙田三頃，不能得。 值王以飢攜其妻就食淮南，而王得疾死，其妻還，則田為楊據矣。 王妻訴之官，楊行賄，偽作文憑，曰：「王在時已售我。 」觀音奴令王妻挽楊，同就崔府君神祠質之。 楊懼神之靈，先期以羊酒浼巫囑神勿洩其事，及王與楊詣祠質之，果無所顯明。 觀音奴疑之，召巫詰問，巫吐其實曰：「楊以羊酒浼我囑神曰：'我實據王田，幸神勿洩也。 '」觀音奴因訊得其實，坐楊罪，歸其田王氏，責神而撤其祠。 亳州有蝗食民禾，觀音奴以事至亳，民以蝗訴，立取蝗向天祝之，以水研碎而飲，是歲蝗不為災。 後升為都水監官。
Guanyin Nu, courtesy name Zhineng, was a Tangut who lived in Xinzhou. He took his jinshi degree in 1327. From a post as secretary in the Ministry of Revenue he was twice promoted until he became prefect of Guide. Honest, clear-sighted, and firm in judgment, he uncovered wrongdoing with uncanny precision. People with old wrongs unresolved—even cases from decades past—would travel a thousand li to seek justice; Guanyin Nu heard them at once, and within ten days every backlog was cleared. Ren Jia, a rich merchant from Zhangde, reached Suiyang; when his donkey died he ordered Qie Yi to butcher it, then beat Qie in a rage until he died overnight. Qie's wife Wang and concubine Sun sued; officials took Ren's bribes, ruled Qie had not died from beating, charged Sun instead, and jailed her. Wang came to plead her case; Guanyin Nu freed Sun from prison at once, called a clerk, and said, "Prepare the papers, incense, and offerings—you will pray to the City God about Qie's death and have the god reveal the truth to me." A Suiyang clerk involved in the case, fearing Guanyin Nu's rigor and divine exposure, produced Ren's bribe money and confessed: "Qie truly died from the beating; Ren bribed officials to cover it up, and I took a bribe too—I come to inform." Ren was punished and Sun was freed. Yang Jia, a Ningling magnate, had long coveted three qing of Wang Yi's land but could not get them. When famine drove Wang and his wife to Huainan for food, Wang fell ill and died; when his wife returned, Yang already held the fields. Wang's wife sued; Yang bribed officials and forged a deed claiming Wang had sold him the land while alive. Guanyin Nu had Wang's wife take Yang to the shrine of Lord Cui to settle the matter before the god. Fearing the god, Yang bribed the shaman with sheep and wine to ask the deity to stay silent; when Wang and Yang went to the shrine, nothing was revealed. Guanyin Nu grew suspicious and questioned the shaman, who confessed: "Yang gave me sheep and wine and asked the god not to reveal that he had seized Wang's land." Guanyin Nu got the truth, punished Yang, restored the land to the Wangs, rebuked the shrine, and had it torn down. When locusts devoured the crops in Bozhou, Guanyin Nu on official business took locusts, prayed heavenward, ground them in water and drank the mixture—and that year the plague did no harm. He was later promoted to an office in the Directorate of Waterways.

周自強，字剛善，臨江路新喻州人。 好學能文，練於吏事，以文法推擇為吏。 泰定間，廣西洞徭反，自強往見徭酋，說以禍福，中其要害，徭酋立為罷兵，貢方物，納款請命。 事聞於朝，特旨超授廣西兩江道宣慰司都事。 轉饒州路經歷，遷婺州路義烏縣尹。 周知民情，而性度寬厚，不為刻深。 民有以爭訟訴於庭者，一見即能知其曲直，然未遽加以刑責，必取經典中語，反覆開譬之，令其誦讀講解。 若能悔悟首實，則原其罪； 若迷謬怙惡不悛，然後繩之以法不少貸。 民畏且愛，獄訟頓息。 民間田稅之籍多失實，以故差徭不平，自強出令履畝核之，民不能欺，文簿井井可考，於是賦役平均，貧富樂業。 其聽訟決獄，物無遁情，黠吏欲以片言欺惑之不可得。 由是政治大行，聲譽籍甚。 部使者數以廉能舉於朝，選授撫州路金溪縣尹，階奉議大夫，政績愈著。 以亞中大夫、江州路總管致仕。
Zhou Ziqiang, courtesy name Gangshan, was from Xinyu in Linjiang circuit. A devoted student and capable writer, skilled in administrative work, he was selected as a clerk through competitive examination. During the Taiding era the Guangxi cave Yao rebelled; Ziqiang went to their chiefs, spoke of reward and ruin, and hit their weak point; they at once laid down arms, sent tribute, and sued for peace. The court heard of it and specially promoted him to chief secretary of the Guangxi Liangjiang Pacification Commission. He became intendant of Raozhou circuit and then magistrate of Yiwu in Wuzhou circuit. He knew the people's hearts well, yet governed with generous temper and without harshness. When litigants came to court he could see right and wrong at a glance, yet he never rushed to punish; he quoted the classics, explained them patiently, and made the parties recite and discuss them. If they repented and confessed, he pardoned them; only if they persisted in error and clung to evil did he apply the law without mercy. The people feared and loved him alike, and lawsuits dwindled away. Land-tax registers were often false, skewing labor and levies; Ziqiang ordered fields measured plot by plot so no one could cheat; the records became reliable, taxes and corvée were fair, and rich and poor alike prospered. In court nothing escaped him; no clever clerk could fool him with a word. Good government flourished and his fame spread far. Department inspectors repeatedly recommended him to court; he was appointed magistrate of Jinxi in Fuzhou with the rank of Grand Master of Discussion, and his achievements grew still greater. He retired as Subordinate Grand Master and former chief administrator of Jiangzhou circuit.
9
白景亮，字明甫，南陽人。 明法律，善書算。 由征東行省譯史有勞，超遷南恩知州，升沔陽府尹，奏最於朝，特授衢州路總管。 先是，為郡者於民間徭役，不盡校​​田畝以為則，吏得併緣高下其手，富民或優有餘力，而貧弱不能勝者，多至破產失業。 景亮深知其弊，乃始核驗田畝以均之，役之輕重，一視田之多寡，大小家各使得宜，咸便安之，由是民不勞而事易集，他郡邑皆取以為法。 郡學之政久弛，從祀諸賢無塑像，諸生無廩膳，祭服樂器有缺，景亮皆為備之，儒風大振，縉紳稱頌焉。 景亮性廉介勤苦，自奉甚薄，妻尤儉約，惟以脫粟對飯而已。 部使者嘗上其事，特詔褒美，賜以宮錦，改授台州路總管。 卒於官。
Bai Jingliang, courtesy name Mingfu, was from Nanyang. He mastered law and excelled at accounts and documents. For service as a translator-clerk on the eastern campaign he was promoted to prefect of Nan'en, then Mianyang; after reporting top marks to court he was specially made chief administrator of Quzhou. Earlier prefects had not fully verified acreage when assigning corvée, so clerks manipulated the figures; the rich often had spare capacity while the poor were ruined and driven from their livelihoods. Jingliang saw the abuse clearly and began verifying acreage to spread the burden fairly; labor was scaled strictly to landholding, large and small households alike were treated justly, the people were not overtaxed, work was easily done, and other districts took his method as their model. The prefectural school had long declined—sage statues were missing, students had no stipends, ritual robes and instruments were lacking; Jingliang supplied them all, learning revived, and the gentry praised him. Jingliang was honest, upright, and frugal; he lived very simply, and his wife was still more austere—they ate only plain grain with rice. A department inspector reported his conduct to court; the emperor specially commended him, granted palace brocade, and transferred him to chief administrator of Taizhou. He died in office.
10
王艮，字止善，紹興諸暨人。 尚氣節，讀書務明理以致用，不苟事言說。 淮東廉訪司辟為書吏，遷淮西。 會例革南士，就為吏於兩淮都轉運鹽使司，以歲月及格，授廬州錄事判官。 淮東宣慰司辟為令史，以廉能稱。 再調峽州總管府知事，又辟江浙行省掾史。 會朝廷復立諸市舶司，艮從省官至泉州，建言：「若買舊有之船以付舶商，則費省而工易集，且可絕官吏侵欺掊克之弊。 」中書省報如艮言。 凡為船六宗，省官錢五十餘萬緡。
Wang Gen, courtesy name Zhishan, was from Zhuji in Shaoxing. He prized integrity; he read to grasp principle for practical use and disdained empty talk. The Huaidong surveillance commission recruited him as a clerk; he was transferred to Huaixi. When southern scholars were barred from office by regulation, he served as a clerk in the Lianghuai Salt Transport Commission and, when seniority qualified him, was appointed recording secretary and assistant judge of Luzhou. The Huaidong Pacification Commission recruited him as chief clerk, and he was known for integrity and ability. He was transferred to administrative secretary in Xizhou and then recruited as a clerk for the Jiang-Zhe secretariat. When the court restored the maritime trade offices, Gen went with provincial officials to Quanzhou and proposed buying existing vessels for merchant fleets—saving expense, easing assembly, and ending official graft and extortion. The Central Secretariat approved Gen's proposal. Six ships were built in all, saving more than five hundred thousand strings of official funds.
11
歷建德縣尹，除兩浙都轉運鹽使司經歷。 紹興路總管王克敬，以計口食鹽不便，嘗言於，行省，未報，而克敬為轉運使，集議欲稍損其額，以紓民力。 沮之者以為有成籍不可改，艮毅然曰：「民實寡而強賦多民之錢，今死、徙已眾矣，顧重改成籍而輕棄民命乎！ 且浙右之郡，商賈輻輳，未嘗以口計也。 移其所賦，散於商旅之所聚，實為良法。 」於是議歲減紹興食鹽五千六百引。 尋有復排前議者，艮欲辭職去，丞相聞之，亟遣留艮，而議遂定。
He served as magistrate of Jiande and was appointed intendant of the Liang-Zhe Salt Transport Commission. Wang Kejing, chief administrator of Shaoxing, had found the per-capita salt levy burdensome and petitioned the secretariat without reply; when he became salt commissioner he convened a meeting to reduce the quota and ease the people's burden. Opponents said established registers could not be changed. Gen declared firmly, "The population is shrinking while we force heavy levies; deaths and flight are already numerous—shall we cling to old registers and abandon the people's lives?" Besides, western Zhejiang is crowded with merchants and has never been taxed by head count. Shifting the levy to where merchants and travelers congregate is truly the better method. The assembly agreed to cut Shaoxing's annual salt quota by five thousand six hundred yin. When opponents revived the old plan, Gen threatened to resign; the chancellor heard and urgently ordered him retained, and the reform stood.
12
遷海道漕運都萬戶府經歷。 紹興之官糧入海運者十萬石，城距海十八里，歲令有司拘民船以備短送，吏胥得併緣以虐民。 及至海次，主運者又不即受，有折缺之患。 艮執言曰：「運戶既有官賦之直，何復為是紛紛也！ 」乃責運戶自載糧入運船。 運船為風所敗者，當核實除其數，移文往返，連數歲不絕，艮取吏牘披閱，即除其糧五萬二千八百石、鈔二百五十萬緡，運戶乃免於破家。
He was transferred to intendant of the maritime grain transport office. Shaoxing sent one hundred thousand shi of official grain by sea; the city lay eighteen li inland, and each year officials requisitioned civilian boats for the short haul—giving clerks a chance to abuse the people. At the coast the transport officers did not accept delivery promptly, inviting shortages and losses. Gen insisted, "Transport households already receive official payment for the grain—why all this turmoil?" He required transport households to load the grain onto the ships themselves. Ships wrecked by wind should have their quotas reduced, but documents shuttled back and forth for years; Gen reviewed the files and at once remitted fifty-two thousand eight hundred shi and two and a half million strings in paper money, sparing transport households from ruin.
13
遷江浙行省檢校官。 有詣中書訴松江富民包隱田土，為糧一百七十餘萬石； 沙盪，為鈔五百餘萬緡； 宜立官府糾察收追之。 中書移行省議，遣官驗視，而鬆江獨當十九。 艮至松江，條陳曲折，以破其誑妄，言其「不過欲竦朝廷之聽而報宿怨，且冀創立衙門，為徼名爵計耳。 萬一民心動搖，患生不測，豈國家培養根本之策哉！ 」艮言上，事遂寢。
He was transferred to auditing officer of the Jiang-Zhe secretariat. Someone petitioned the Central Secretariat claiming Songjiang's wealthy had concealed land worth more than 1.7 million shi in grain tax; and sand flats worth more than five million strings in paper money; and urged establishing an office to investigate and recover them. The Central Secretariat referred the matter to the secretariat for inspection; Songjiang alone bore nine-tenths of the burden. Gen reached Songjiang, laid out the facts in detail, and exposed the fraud: "This is only meant to alarm the court, settle old scores, and win a new office for fame and promotion." If popular sentiment is shaken and unforeseen trouble follows, how does that serve the state's policy of nurturing its foundation?" Gen's memorial went up and the matter was dropped.
14
除江西行省左右司員外郎。 吉之安福有小吏，誣民欺隱詭寄田租​​九千餘石，初止八家，前後數十年，株連至千家，行省數遣官按問，吏已伏其虛誑，而有司喜功生事者，复勒其民報合徵糧六百餘石，憲司援詔條革去，終莫能止。 艮到官，首言：「是州之糧，比元經理已增一千一百餘石，豈復有欺隱詭寄者乎？ 準憲司所擬可也。 」行省用艮言，悉蠲之。 艮在任歲餘，以中憲大夫、淮東道宣慰副使致仕。 卒年七十一。
He was appointed vice director in the Jiangxi secretariat's left and right bureaus. In Anfu, Ji, a clerk falsely accused people of concealing more than nine thousand shi in land tax; what began with eight households spread over decades to a thousand. The secretariat sent investigators repeatedly; the clerk had confessed the fraud, yet ambitious officials still pressed for six hundred more shi; the surveillance commission cited an edict to cancel it but could not stop the demand. On taking office Gen said at once, "This prefecture's grain quota has already risen more than eleven hundred shi since the Yuan land survey—how can there still be concealed land?" The surveillance commission's proposal should stand." The secretariat followed Gen's advice and remitted the levies entirely. After a year in office he retired as Grand Master of the Palace and vice commissioner of the Huaidong Pacification Commission. He died at seventy-one.
15
盧琦，字希韓，惠安人，登至正二年進士第。 十二年，稍遷至永春縣尹。 始至，賑饑饉，止橫斂，均賦役，減口鹽一百餘引，蠲包銀榷鐵之無徵者。 已而訟息民安，乃新學宮，延師儒課子弟，月書季考，文風翕然。 鄰邑仙遊盜發，琦適在邑境，盜遙見之，迎拜曰：「此永春大夫也。 為大夫百姓者，何幸之大乎！ 吾邑長乃以暴毒驅我，故至此耳。 」琦因立馬喻以禍福，眾皆投刃槊，請縛其酋以自新，琦許之。 酋至，琦械送帥府，自是威惠行於境外。 十三年，泉郡大饑，死者相枕籍。 其能行者，皆老幼扶攜，就食永春。 琦命分諸浮屠及大家使食之，所存活不可勝計。 十四年，安溪寇數万人來襲永春。 琦聞，召邑民喻之曰：「汝等能戰則與之戰，不能，則我當獨死之爾。 」眾皆感憤，曰：「使君何言也！ 使君父母，我民赤子，其忍以父母畀賊邪！ 且彼寇方將虜掠我妻子，焚毀我室廬，乃一邑深仇也。 今日之事，有進無退，使君其勿以為憂。 」因踴躍爭奮。 琦率以攻賊，大破之。 明日，賊复傾巢而至，又破之。 大小三十餘戰，斬獲一千二百餘人，而邑民無死傷者。 賊大衄，遂遁去。 時兵革四起，列郡皆洶洶不寧，獨永春晏然，無異承平時。 十六年，改調寧德縣尹而去。
Lu Qi, courtesy name Xihan, was from Hui'an and took his jinshi degree in 1342. In the twelfth year he was gradually promoted to magistrate of Yongchun. On arrival he relieved famine, stopped illegal exactions, equalized taxes and labor, cut the per-capita salt quota by more than a hundred yin, and remitted uncollected package silver and iron monopoly dues. Soon lawsuits ceased and the people were at peace; he rebuilt the school, brought in Confucian teachers, held monthly essays and quarterly exams, and learning flourished. Bandits rose in neighboring Xianyou; Qi happened to be at the border. Seeing him from afar they came forward and bowed, saying, "This is the magistrate of Yongchun." What fortune to be subjects of such a magistrate! Our county chief drove us here with cruelty—that is all." Qi reined in his horse and spoke of reward and ruin; they all cast down their weapons and asked to bind their leader and submit; Qi agreed. When the chief came, Qi sent him in chains to the marshal's headquarters; thereafter his authority and kindness extended beyond the border. In the thirteenth year Quan prefecture suffered famine; the dead lay piled one upon another. Those who could still walk, old and young together, came to Yongchun for food. Qi ordered monasteries and wealthy households to feed them; the lives saved were beyond counting. In the fourteenth year tens of thousands of bandits from Anxi attacked Yongchun. Hearing this, Qi summoned the people and said, "If you can fight, fight with me; if not, I alone will die for you." All were stirred and indignant, crying, "My lord, what words are these! You are our parent and we your children—how could we hand our parent over to bandits! Besides, those bandits would seize our wives and children and burn our homes—they are the county's mortal enemies. Today we advance and do not retreat—my lord, do not worry." They leaped up, each striving to outdo the other in zeal. Qi led them against the bandits and routed them completely. The next day the bandits returned in full force and were defeated again. In more than thirty engagements they killed or captured over twelve hundred bandits, yet not one townsman was killed or wounded. The bandits suffered a crushing defeat and fled. War flared everywhere and every prefecture was in turmoil; Yongchun alone remained peaceful, as calm as in the best years of the dynasty. In the sixteenth year he was transferred to magistrate of Ningde and left Yongchun.
16
鄒伯顏，字從吉，高唐人。 為建寧崇安縣尹。 崇安之為邑，區別其土田，名之曰都者五十，五十都之田上送官者，為糧六千石。 其大家以五十餘家，而兼五千石； 細民以四百餘家，而合一千石。 大家之田，連跨數都，而細民之糧，或僅升合。 有司常以四百之細民，配五十大家之役，故貧者受役旬日，而家已破。 伯顏曰：「貧弱之受困，一至此乎！ 」乃取其糧籍而分計從，有糧一石者，受一石之役，有糧升斗者，受升斗之役。 田多者受數都之役而不可辭，田少者稱其所出而無倖免。 貧因無告之民，始得以休息。 崇安賦役之均，遂為四方最。 邑有宋趙抃所鑿溝，溉民田數千畝。 歲久，溝湮而田廢。 伯顏修長溝十里，繞楓樹陂，累石以為固，溝悉复抃遺跡，而田為常稔，民賴其利。 安慶路嘗得造偽鈔者，遣卒械其囚至崇安，求其黨而執之，囚與卒結謀，望風入良民家肆虐。 伯顏捕訊得其狀，即執而歸諸安慶，自是偽造之連逮無濫及崇安者。 於是行省帥府、御史憲府咸舉其能。 選調漳州路判官。
Zou Boyan, courtesy name Congji, was from Gaotang. He served as magistrate of Chong'an in Jianning. Chong'an divided its land into fifty units called du; grain tax from all fifty du totaled six thousand shi. More than fifty great households together bore five thousand shi; more than four hundred humble households combined for one thousand shi. Great families' fields spanned several du, while humble folk might owe only a sheng or two. Officials routinely assigned corvée from four hundred poor households to cover fifty wealthy ones' obligations, so the poor labored ten days and their families were ruined. Boyan said, "The poor have been driven to this pass!" He took the tax registers and apportioned labor by grain owed: one shi of grain meant one shi's labor, a sheng or dou meant a sheng or dou's labor. Those with much land bore corvée across several du and could not refuse; those with little paid what they owed and could not evade it. The poor, who had nowhere to turn, were at last able to breathe. Chong'an's fair taxes and labor became the model for the entire region. The county had a canal dug by Zhao Bian of the Song, which irrigated several thousand mu of farmland. Years passed, the canal silted up, and the fields lay fallow. Boyan rebuilt a ten-li canal around Maple Tree Embankment, reinforcing it with stone; the canal restored Zhao Bian's work, the fields bore reliably, and the people prospered from it. Anqing once caught counterfeiters and sent soldiers to escort the prisoners in chains to Chong'an to round up their gang; prisoners and soldiers conspired to raid peaceful households whenever they could. Boyan arrested and interrogated them, learned the truth, sent them back to Anqing, and thereafter counterfeiting cases no longer swept up innocent Chong'an people. The secretariat, marshal's headquarters, and surveillance commission all recommended him for his ability. He was selected and transferred to assistant prefect of Zhangzhou circuit.
17
劉秉直，字清臣，大都武清人。 至正八年，來為衛輝路總管​​，平徭役，興教化，敦四民之業，崇五土之利，養鰥寡，恤孤獨。 賊劫汲縣民張聚鈔一千二百錠而殺之，賊不獲，秉直具詞致禱城隍祠，而使人伺於死所，忽有村民阿蓮者，戰怖僕地，具言賊之姓名及所在，乃命尉襲之，果得賊於汴，遂正其罪。 秋七月，蟲螟生，民患之，秉直禱於八蜡祠，蟲皆自死。 歲大饑，人相食，死者過半，秉直出俸米，倡富民分粟，餒者食之，病者與藥，死者與棺以葬。 天不雨，禾且槁，秉直詣城北太行之蒼峪神祠，具詞祈祝，有青蛇蜿蜒而出，觀者異之。 辭神而還，行及數里，雷雨大至。 秩滿，以親老，去官侍養。
Liu Bingzhi, courtesy name Qingchen, was from Wuqing in Dadu. In 1348 he became chief administrator of Weihui, equalized corvée, promoted education, encouraged the four occupations, honored agriculture, and cared for widows, orphans, and the destitute. Bandits robbed and killed Zhang Ju of Jixi, taking twelve hundred ingots; the culprits escaped. Bingzhi prayed at the City God shrine and posted men at the murder site; a villager named Alian suddenly collapsed in terror and named the bandits and their hideout; a constable raided them and caught them at Bian, and they were punished by law. In the seventh month locusts and borers appeared; Bingzhi prayed at the Eight Sacrifices shrine and the pests died off. Famine struck; people ate one another and more than half perished. Bingzhi gave his salary grain, urged the wealthy to share theirs, fed the hungry, medicated the sick, and coffined the dead. Rain failed and the crops were withering; Bingzhi prayed at the Cangyu shrine on Mount Taihang north of the city; a green serpent emerged, to the wonder of all who saw it. He left the shrine; within a few li a great thunderstorm broke. When his term ended he left office to care for his aging parents.
18
許義夫，碭山人。 為夏邑縣尹，每親詣鄉社，教民稼穡。 見民勤謹者，出己俸賞之，怠惰者罰之。 三年之間，境內豐足。 後為封丘縣尹，值至正四年大饑，盜賊群起，抄掠州縣。 義夫聞賊至近境，乃單馬出郊十里外迎之，見賊數百人，義夫力言：「封丘縣小民貧，皆已驚惶逃竄，幸無入吾境也。 」言辭願款，賊遂他往。 封丘之民，得免於難。
Xu Yifu was from Dangshan. As magistrate of Xiayi he personally visited village altars each year to teach farming. He rewarded the diligent from his own salary and punished the idle. Within three years the district was abundantly supplied. Later, as magistrate of Fengqiu during the great famine of 1344, bandits rose in bands and raided the districts. Hearing bandits had neared the border, Yifu rode out alone ten li beyond the suburbs to meet them; facing several hundred men he pleaded, "Fengqiu is small and poor; the people have fled in terror—please do not enter our territory." His words were earnest and sincere, and the bandits went elsewhere. Fengqiu's people were spared disaster.
