1
太不花
Taibuhua
2
太不花，弘吉剌氏。 世為外戚，官最貴顯。 太不花沉厚有大度，以世冑入官，累遷雲南行省右丞，歷通政使、上都留守、遼陽行省平章政事。 至正八年，太平為丞相，力荐太不花可大用，召入，為中書平章政事。 明年，太平既罷，脫脫復為相。 太不花因黨於脫脫謀欲害太平，眾由是不​​平之。
Taibuhua belonged to the Hongjila clan. His family had long served as imperial affines by marriage and held the highest offices at court. Taibuhua was grave, steady, and magnanimous. Entering government by virtue of his noble birth, he rose through the ranks to become Right Vice Minister of the Yunnan Branch Secretariat and later served as Commissioner of the Bureau of Transmission, Keeper of the Upper Capital, and Grand Councillor of the Liaoyang Branch Secretariat. In the eighth year of Zhizheng (1348), when Taiping became chancellor, he strongly urged that Taibuhua was fit for high office. Taibuhua was summoned to the capital and appointed Grand Councillor of the Central Secretariat. The following year Taiping was removed from office and Toghto returned as chancellor. Taibuhua aligned himself with Toghto and plotted against Taiping, and public opinion turned against him.
3
十二年，盜起河南，知樞密院事老章出師久無功，詔拜太不花河南行省平章政事，加太尉，將兵往代之。 未期月，平南陽、汝寧、唐、隨，又下安陸、德安等路，招降服叛，動合事宜，軍聲大振。 十四年，脫脫以太師、右丞相總大兵徵高郵，尋詔奪其兵柄，而升太不花本省左丞相，與太尉月闊察兒、樞密知院雪雪代總其兵。 山東、河北諸軍悉令太不花節制。 而太不花乃以軍士乏糧之故，頗驕傲不遵朝廷命令，軍士又往往剽掠為民患。 十五年，監察御史也裡忽都等劾其慢功虐民之罪，於是天子下詔盡奪其職，俾率領火赤溫，從平章政事答失八都魯徵進。
In the twelfth year (1352), rebels broke out in Henan. Lao Zhang, Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs, had campaigned for a long time without success. The court appointed Taibuhua Grand Councillor of the Henan Branch Secretariat with the additional rank of Grand Marshal and sent him with an army to relieve him. Within less than a month he pacified Nanyang, Runing, Tang, and Sui, then took Anlu, De'an, and other prefectures. His efforts to win surrenders and crush defections were well judged at every turn, and his army's reputation soared. In the fourteenth year (1354), Toghto, as Grand Preceptor and Right Chancellor, took command of a large force to attack Gaoyou. Shortly afterward an edict stripped him of military authority. Taibuhua was promoted to Left Chancellor of the Henan Branch Secretariat and, together with Grand Marshal Yuekuocher and Vice Commissioner Xuexue, assumed overall command of the campaign in his place. All forces in Shandong and Hebei were placed under his command. But Taibuhua, citing shortages of rations for his troops, grew arrogant and defied court orders, while his soldiers frequently plundered the countryside and terrorized the populace. In the fifteenth year (1355), Investigating Censor Yelihudu and others charged him with dragging out the campaign and oppressing the people. The emperor then issued an edict removing him from all his posts and ordered him to lead the Huochiwen guard troops and follow Grand Councillor Däshibatu'er on the advance.
4
頃之，復拜湖廣行省左丞相，節制湖廣、荊襄諸軍，招捕沔陽、湖廣等處水陸賊徒。 會朝廷復拜太平為中書左丞相，太不花聞之，意不能平，歎曰：「我不負朝廷，朝廷負我矣。 太平漢人，今乃復居中用事，安受逸樂，我反在外勤苦邪！ 」及擊賊，賊且退，諸將皆欲乘勝渡江，而太不花乃反勒兵而退，以養銳為名。 其後賊犯汴梁，守臣請援兵，至十往反，太不花乃始率兵援汴梁，而猶按甲不進。 時睢、亳、太康俱已陷，邊警日急。 或諫之曰：「賊旦夕且至，丞相兵不進何也？ 」太不花顧左右大言曰：「我在，何物小寇敢犯境邪？ 若等毋多言，我自有神算也。 」既而縱軍出掠，百里之內，蕩然無遺。 繼又渡師河北，聲取曹、濮，遂駐於彰德、衛輝。 俄而曹、濮之賊奪竄晉、冀，大同亦相繼不守，遂蔓延不可製。 朝廷以為憂，兩遣重臣諭以密旨，授之成算，而太不花恬不為意。 是時，其子壽童以同知樞密院事將兵分討山東，久無功，嘗以事入奏，語言有驕慢意，帝由是惡之。
Before long he was reappointed Left Chancellor of the Huguang Branch Secretariat, placed in command of the armies of Huguang and Jingxiang, and charged with suppressing the rebel forces on land and water in Mianyang, Huguang, and neighboring regions. Just then the court reappointed Taiping as Left Chancellor of the Central Secretariat. When Taibuhua heard the news he could not contain his resentment and exclaimed, "I have not failed the court—the court has failed me. Taiping is a Han Chinese, yet he is back at the center wielding power, enjoying ease and comfort, while I am left out here toiling in the field! " When they engaged the rebels and the enemy was on the verge of retreat, every general wanted to press the advantage and cross the river. Taibuhua, however, ordered a withdrawal, claiming he was resting his troops to restore their fighting edge. Later, when the rebels threatened Bianliang, the local defenders pleaded for reinforcements again and again. Only after ten appeals did Taibuhua finally march to their aid—and even then he kept his army idle and refused to engage. By then Sui, Bo, and Taikang had all fallen, and frontier alarms grew more urgent by the day. Someone urged him, saying, "The rebels will be here any day—why will the Chancellor's army not advance? " Taibuhua turned to his attendants and declared loudly, "As long as I am here, what trifling rebel would dare cross our border? Say no more—I have my own infallible plan. " He then allowed his troops to rampage through the countryside, and within a hundred li nothing was left untouched. He next moved his army north of the Yellow River, announcing that he would seize Cao and Pu, and encamped at Zhangde and Weihui. Before long the rebels of Cao and Pu broke into Shanxi and Hebei. Datong fell in turn, and the uprising spread until it could no longer be contained. The court grew alarmed and twice dispatched senior ministers with secret instructions and a settled strategy, but Taibuhua remained utterly indifferent. At this time his son Shoutong, serving as Vice Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs, led a separate force to suppress Shandong but achieved nothing for a long while. On one occasion he reported to court in person, and his tone betrayed arrogance. The emperor took a dislike to him from that moment.
5
十八年，山東賊愈充斥，且逼近京畿，於是詔拜太不花中書右丞相，總其兵討山東。 既渡河，即上疏以謂：「賊勢張甚，軍行宜以糧餉為先。 昔漢韓信行軍，蕭何饋糧，方今措畫，無如丞相太平者，如令太平至軍中供給，事乃可濟，不然兵不能進矣。 」其意實銜太平，欲其至軍中即害之也。 時參知政事卜顏帖木兒、張晉等分省山東，二人者嘗劾壽童不進兵，太不花至，則以其饋運不前斷遣之。 又以知樞密院事完者帖木兒為右丞之日嘗劾其非，亦加以失誤專制之罪，擅改其官，徵至軍欲害之。 事聞，廷議喧然。 而太平與太不花久有隙，會其疏來上，以其欲害己也，遂諷監察御史迷只兒海等劾其緩師拒命之罪，而於帝前力譖之。 於是乃下詔削其官爵，奪其兵柄，安置於蓋州，以知樞密院事悟良哈台總其兵。
In the eighteenth year (1358), rebels in Shandong grew ever more rampant and even threatened the capital region. The court appointed Taibuhua Right Chancellor of the Central Secretariat and placed him in overall command of the campaign against Shandong. As soon as he crossed the river he submitted a memorial saying, "The rebel forces are overwhelming. An army on the march must put supplies first. In Han times, when Han Xin took the field, Xiao He fed his army. For planning today there is no one like Chancellor Taiping. If Taiping were sent to the front to manage supplies, the campaign could succeed; otherwise the army cannot move forward. " His real aim was to settle a score with Taiping: he wanted Taiping brought to the army so he could be killed on the spot. At the time Vice Ministers Buyan Temur and Zhang Jin were administering Shandong on separate commissions. Both had previously impeached Shoutong for failing to advance. When Taibuhua arrived he had them dismissed on the charge that they had failed to keep supplies moving. He likewise punished Commissioner Qanisher Temur, who as Right Vice Minister had once impeached him, charging him with misconduct and usurping authority, demoting him without authorization, summoning him to headquarters, and intending to have him killed. When word of this reached the capital, the court was in an uproar. Taiping and Taibuhua had long been at odds. When Taibuhua's memorial arrived, Taiping, believing it was a plot against his life, induced Investigating Censor Mizhihai and others to charge him with delaying the campaign and defying orders, and pressed the case vigorously before the emperor. An edict was then issued stripping him of rank and office, removing his command, and confining him at Gaizhou. Commissioner Ulangqatai was appointed to take over his troops.
6
太不花聞有詔，夜馳詣劉哈剌不花求救解。 劉哈剌不花者，太不花故部將也，以破賊累有功，拜淮南行省平章政事，時駐兵保定。 見太不花來，因張樂大宴，舉酒慷慨言曰：「丞相國家柱石，有大勳勞如此，天子終不害丞相，是必讒言間之耳。 我當自往見上言之，丞相毋憂也。 」哈剌不花即走至京，首見太平。 太平問其來何故，哈剌不花具以其故告之。 太平曰：「太不花大逆不道，今詔已下，爾乃敢輒妄言邪？ 不審處，禍將及爾矣。 」哈剌不花聞太平言，畏懼，噤不能發。 太平度太不花必在哈剌不花所，即語之曰：「爾能致太不花以來，吾以爾見上，爾功不細矣。 」哈剌不花因許之。 太平乃引入見帝，賜賚良渥。 初，劉哈剌不花之為部將於太不花也，與倪晦者同在幕下，太不花每委任晦，而哈剌不花計多阻不行，哈剌不花心嘗以為怨。 及是，知事已不可解，還，縛太不花父子送京師，未至，皆殺之於路。
When Taibuhua learned of the edict, he rode through the night to Liu Qara Buqa to beg for help. Liu Qara Buqa had once been Taibuhua's subordinate and had won repeated distinction against the rebels. He had been appointed Grand Councillor of the Huainan Branch Secretariat and was then stationed at Baoding. When Taibuhua arrived, Liu Qara Buqa set out a feast with music and, raising his cup, spoke with feeling: "Chancellor, you are a pillar of the state. With merit such as yours, the Son of Heaven would never harm you—this can only be the work of slanderers driving a wedge between you and the throne. I will go to the emperor myself and plead your case. Have no fear, Chancellor. " Qara Buqa set off at once for the capital and went first to see Taiping. Taiping asked why he had come, and Qara Buqa told him the whole story. Taiping said, "Taibuhua is guilty of treason. The edict has already been issued—how dare you speak up for him? If you do not watch your step, disaster will fall on you as well. " Qara Buqa was terrified by Taiping's words and fell silent. Taiping guessed that Taibuhua must be with Qara Buqa and said to him, "If you can deliver Taibuhua to me, I will present you to the emperor. Your reward will be no small thing. " Qara Buqa agreed. Taiping then presented him to the emperor, who lavished generous gifts upon him. Earlier, when Liu Qara Buqa had served under Taibuhua, he and a staff officer named Ni Hui had shared the same command. Taibuhua always favored Hui with responsibility, while Qara Buqa's plans were repeatedly blocked. Qara Buqa had long nursed a grudge over this. Now, seeing that the situation could not be salvaged, he returned, bound Taibuhua and his son, and set out to deliver them to the capital. Before they arrived, he killed them both on the road.
7
察罕帖木兒擴廓帖木兒
Chaghan Temur; Köke Temur
8
察罕帖木兒，字廷瑞，係出北庭。 曾祖闊闊台，元初隨大軍收河南。 至祖乃蠻台、父阿魯溫，皆家河南，為潁州沈丘人。 察罕帖木兒幼篤學，嘗應進士舉，有時名。 身長七尺，修眉覆目，左頰有三毫，或怒則毫皆直指。 居常慨然有當世之志。 至正十一年，盜發汝、潁，焚城邑，殺長吏，所過殘破，不數月，江淮諸郡皆陷。 朝廷徵兵致討，卒無成功。 十二年，察罕帖木兒乃奮義起兵，沈丘之子弟從者數百人。 與信陽之羅山人李思齊合兵，同設奇計襲破羅山。 事聞，朝廷授察罕帖木兒中順大夫、汝寧府達魯花赤。 於是所在義士俱將兵來會，得萬人，自成一軍，屯沈丘，數與賊戰，輒克捷。
Chaghan Temur, whose style name was Tingrui, was descended from the Beiting line. His great-grandfather Köködei had followed the main armies in the reconquest of Henan at the founding of the dynasty. His grandfather Naimantai and his father Arun had all settled in Henan, and the family were natives of Shenqiu in Ying Prefecture. Chaghan Temur was a serious student in his youth. He once sat for the jinshi examinations and enjoyed a measure of literary renown. He stood seven chi tall, with long brows that shaded his eyes. Three hairs grew on his left cheek, and when he grew angry they all stood straight up. In daily life he often sighed with the ambition to make his mark on the age. In the eleventh year of Zhizheng (1351), rebels rose in the Ru and Ying region, burning towns, killing local officials, and laying waste wherever they marched. Within a few months the prefectures of the Jianghuai region had all fallen. The court mobilized armies to suppress them but achieved nothing in the end. In the twelfth year (1352), Chaghan Temur took up arms in a righteous cause, and several hundred young men of Shenqiu rallied to him. He joined forces with Li Siqi of Luoshan in Xinyang, and together they devised a stratagem that took Luoshan by surprise. When word reached the court, Chaghan Temur was appointed Junior Grand Master of Honor and darughachi of Runing Prefecture. Righteous volunteers from all around then rallied to him with their forces. He mustered ten thousand men, formed an independent army, and encamped at Shenqiu. In battle after battle with the rebels he carried the day.
9
十五年，賊勢滋蔓，由汴以南陷鄧、許、嵩、洛。 察罕帖木兒兵日益盛，轉戰而北，遂戍虎牢，以遏賊鋒。 賊乃北渡盟津，焚掠至覃懷，河北震動。 察罕帖木兒進戰，大敗之，餘黨柵河洲，殲之無遺類，河北遂定。 朝廷奇其功，除中書刑部侍郎，階中議大夫。 苗軍以滎陽叛，察罕帖木兒夜襲之，虜其眾幾盡，乃結營屯中牟。 已而淮右賊眾三十萬掠汴以西，來搗中牟營。 察罕帖木兒結陳待之，以死生利害諭士卒。 士卒賈勇決死戰，無不一當百。 會大風揚沙，自率猛士鼓譟從中起，奮擊賊中堅，賊勢遂披靡不能支，棄旗鼓遁走，追殺十餘里，斬首無算。 軍聲益大振。
In the fifteenth year (1355), the rebel tide swelled. South of Bianliang they overran Deng, Xu, Song, and Luo. Chaghan Temur's forces grew stronger by the day. Fighting his way north, he garrisoned Hulao to block the rebel advance. The rebels then crossed the Yellow River at Meng Ford, burning and looting as far as Tanhuai, and all of Hebei was thrown into alarm. Chaghan Temur pressed the attack and routed them utterly. The survivors fortified an islet in the river; he wiped them out to the last man, and Hebei was pacified. The court was astonished by his achievement and appointed him Vice Minister of Punishments in the Central Secretariat with the rank of Grand Master of Discussion. When the Miao army rebelled at Xingyang, Chaghan Temur attacked by night and captured nearly their entire force. He then established a camp at Zhongmou. Before long three hundred thousand Huaiyou rebels swept west of Bianliang and fell upon the camp at Zhongmou. Chaghan Temur drew up his battle line to meet them and addressed his troops on what was at stake between life and death. His men steeled themselves for a fight to the death, and each fought as if he were worth a hundred. A fierce wind whipped up the sand. He personally led his elite troops, drums beating and voices roaring, in a charge straight at the enemy center. The rebel line broke and could not hold. They abandoned their banners and drums and fled. He pursued them for more than ten li and cut down an untold number. His army's fame rose still higher.
10
十六年，升中書兵部尚書，階嘉議大夫。 繼而賊西陷陝州，斷殽、函，勢欲趨秦、晉。 知樞密院事答失八都魯方節制河南軍，調察罕帖木兒與李思齊往攻之。 察罕帖木兒即鼓行而西，夜拔殽陵，立柵交口。 陝為城，阻山帶河，險且固，而賊轉南山粟給食以堅守，攻之猝不可拔。 察罕帖木兒乃焚馬矢營中，如炊煙狀，以疑賊，而夜提兵拔靈寶城。 守既備，賊始覺，不敢動，即渡河陷平陸，掠安邑，蹂晉南鄙。 察罕帖木兒追襲之，蹙之以鐵騎。 賊回扼下陽津，赴水死者甚眾。 相持數月，賊勢窮，皆遁潰。 以功加中奉大夫、僉河北行樞密院​​事。
In the sixteenth year (1356) he was promoted to Minister of War in the Central Secretariat with the rank of Grand Master of Court Discussion. The rebels then pushed west, took Shanzhou, and severed the Xiao and Han passes, threatening to drive into Qin and Jin. Commissioner Däshibatu'er, who was then in overall command of the Henan armies, ordered Chaghan Temur and Li Siqi to attack them. Chaghan Temur marched west at once, took Xiaoling by night, and threw up fortifications at Jiaokou. Shanzhou was a walled city hemmed in by mountains and rivers, steep and strong. The rebels brought grain from the southern hills to sustain a stubborn defense, and a direct assault could not break them. Chaghan Temur burned horse dung in his camp until it looked like cooking fires, to deceive the enemy, then led his troops by night and seized Lingbao. By the time the garrison was ready, the rebels had only just realized what had happened and dared not stir. They crossed the river at once, took Pinglu, plundered Anyi, and ravaged the southern borderlands of Shanxi. Chaghan Temur pursued and harried them with his armored cavalry. The rebels turned to hold Xiayang Ford, and a great many drowned trying to cross the water. After several months of stalemate the rebels were spent and broke and fled in disorder. For this achievement he was promoted to Grand Master of Palace Attendance and appointed Associate Commissioner of the Hebei Branch Bureau of Military Affairs.
11
十七年，賊尋出襄樊，陷商州，攻武關，官軍敗走，遂直趨長安，至灞上，分道掠同、華諸州，三輔震恐。 陝西省台來告急。 察罕帖木兒即領大眾入潼關，長驅而前，與賊遇，戰輒勝，殺獲以億萬計。 賊餘黨皆散潰，走南山，入興元。 朝廷嘉其復關陝有大功，授資善大夫、陝西行省左丞。 未幾，賊出自巴蜀，陷秦、隴，據鞏昌，遂窺鳳翔。 察罕帖木兒即先分兵入守鳳翔城，而遣諜者誘賊圍鳳翔。 賊果來圍之，厚凡數十重。 察罕帖木兒自將鐵騎，晝夜馳二百里往赴。 比去城裡所，分軍張左右翼掩擊之。 城中軍亦開門鼓譟而出，內外合擊，呼聲動天地。 賊大潰，自相踐蹂，斬首數万級，伏尸百餘里，餘黨皆遁還。 關中悉定。
In the seventeenth year (1357) the rebels broke out from Xiang and Fan, took Shangzhou, and assaulted Wuguan. The government troops were routed and the enemy drove straight for Chang'an, reaching Bashang. They fanned out to loot Tong, Hua, and neighboring prefectures, and the Three Metropolises were gripped with terror. The Shaanxi provincial administration sent urgent appeals for help. Chaghan Temur marched at once with a large force through Tong Pass and pressed forward without halt. In every encounter he prevailed, and the enemy killed or captured ran into untold numbers. The surviving rebels broke and fled into the southern mountains and on into Xingyuan. The court commended his great service in recovering the Guan-Shaan region and appointed him Grand Master for the Good of the State and Left Vice Minister of the Shaanxi Branch Secretariat. Before long rebels emerged from Ba and Shu, overran Qin and Long, seized Gongchang, and turned their eyes toward Fengxiang. Chaghan Temur at once detached a force to hold Fengxiang and sent agents to lure the enemy into besieging the city. The rebels came as expected and invested the city in layer upon layer of siege lines. Chaghan Temur himself led his armored cavalry and rode two hundred li day and night to the relief. When he was still a li from the city, he split his force and sent the wings in a converging attack. The garrison threw open the gates, drums beating and voices roaring as they sallied forth. The two forces struck together from within and without, and their shouts shook heaven and earth. The rebels broke in utter rout, trampling one another underfoot. Tens of thousands were beheaded, and corpses lay along the road for more than a hundred li. The survivors fled back the way they had come. The Guanzhong region was fully pacified.
12
十八年，山東賊分道犯京畿。 朝廷征四方兵入衛，詔察罕帖木兒以兵屯涿州。 察罕帖木兒即留兵戍清湫、義谷，屯潼關，塞南山口，以備他盜。 而自將銳卒往赴召。 而曹、濮賊方分道逾太行，焚上黨，掠晉、冀，陷雲中、雁門、代郡，烽火數千里，復大掠南且還。 察罕帖木兒先遣兵伏南山阻隘，而自勒重兵屯聞喜、絳​​陽。 賊果走南山，縱伏兵橫擊之，賊皆棄輜重走山谷，其得南還者無幾。 乃分兵屯澤州，塞碗子城，屯上黨，塞吾兒谷，屯并州，塞井陘口，以杜太行諸道。 賊屢至，守將數血戰擊卻之，河東悉定。 進陝西行省右丞，兼陝西行台侍御史、同知河南行樞密院事。 於是天子乃詔察罕帖木兒守禦關陝、晉、冀，撫鎮漢、沔、荊、襄，便宜行閫外事。 察罕帖木兒益務練兵訓農，以平定四方為己責。
In the eighteenth year (1358), Shandong rebels advanced on the capital by separate routes. The court called in armies from every quarter to defend the capital and ordered Chaghan Temur to station his troops at Zhuozhou. Chaghan Temur left troops to garrison Qingqiu and Yigu, stationed his force at Tong Pass, and sealed the southern mountain passes against other rebel bands. He then led his elite troops south in answer to the imperial summons. Meanwhile the Cao and Pu rebels were advancing by separate routes over the Taihang range, burning Shangdang, ravaging Shanxi and Hebei, and seizing Yunzhong, Yanmen, and Daizhou. Beacon fires blazed along a thousand li of frontier as they plundered their way south on the return march. Chaghan Temur first posted troops in ambush along the mountain passes to the south, while he personally led his main force to Wenxi and Jiangyang. The rebels took the southern route as expected. His ambush force struck across their line of march. They abandoned their wagons and fled into the hills, and only a handful made it back south. He then garrisoned Zezhou and sealed Wanzicheng, held Shangdang and blocked the Wuer Valley, stationed troops at Bingzhou and closed Jingxing Pass, cutting off every route through the Taihang. The rebels attacked again and again, but the garrison commanders beat them back in repeated bloody engagements until the whole of Hedong was pacified. He was promoted to Right Vice Minister of the Shaanxi Branch Secretariat and concurrently appointed Attending Censor on the Shaanxi surveillance commission and Associate Commissioner of the Henan Branch Bureau of Military Affairs. The emperor then ordered Chaghan Temur to defend the Guan-Shaan, Shanxi, and Hebei regions, pacify Han, Mian, Jing, and Xiang, and exercise discretionary authority over military affairs in the field. Chaghan Temur redoubled his efforts to drill troops and promote farming, making the pacification of the realm his personal charge.
13
是年，安豐賊劉福通等陷汴梁，造宮闕，易正朔，號召群盜。 巴蜀、荊楚、江淮、齊魯、遼海，西至甘肅，所在兵起，勢相聯結。 察罕帖木兒乃北塞太行，南守鞏、洛，而自將中軍軍沔池。 會叛將周全棄覃懷，入汴城，合兵攻洛陽。 察罕帖木兒下令嚴守備，別以奇兵出宜陽，而自將精騎發新安來援。 賊至城下，見堅壁不可犯，退引去，因追至虎牢，塞成皋諸險而還。 拜陝西行省平章政事，仍兼同知行樞密院事，便宜行事。
That year Liu Futong and the other Anfeng rebels took Bianliang, erected palaces, proclaimed a new calendar, and summoned rebel bands from every quarter. From Ba-Shu, Jing-Chu, and the Jianghuai region to Qi-Lu, Liaohai, and westward into Gansu, rebellions broke out everywhere and their forces linked up across the map. Chaghan Temur sealed the Taihang to the north, held Gong and Luo to the south, and personally led his central army to Mianchi. Meanwhile the turncoat Zhou Quan abandoned Tanhuai, entered Bianliang, and combined with other rebel forces to attack Luoyang. Chaghan Temur ordered the defenses tightened, sent a flanking force out through Yiyang, and marched from Xin'an at the head of his cavalry to relieve the city. The rebels reached the walls, found the defenses impregnable, and withdrew. He pursued them to Hulao, secured the passes at Chenggao and other strong points, and returned. He was appointed Grand Councillor of the Shaanxi Branch Secretariat, retaining his post as Associate Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs with discretionary authority.
14
十九年，察罕帖木兒圖复汴梁。 五月，以大軍次虎牢。 先發遊騎，南道出汴南，略歸、亳、陳、察，北道出汴東，戰船浮於河，水陸並下，略曹南，據黃陵渡。 乃大發秦兵，出函關，過虎牢； 晉兵出太行，逾黃河，俱會汴城下，首奪其外城。 察罕帖木兒自將鐵騎屯杏花營，諸將環城而壘。 賊屢出戰，戰輒敗，遂嬰城以守。 乃夜伏兵城南，旦日，遣苗軍跳梁者略城而東。 賊傾城出追，伏兵鼓譟起，邀擊敗之。 又令弱卒立柵外城以餌賊。 賊出爭之，弱卒佯走，薄城西，因突鐵騎縱擊，悉擒其眾。 賊自是益不敢出。 八月，諜知城中計窮，食且盡，乃與諸將閆思孝、李克彝、虎林赤、賽因赤、答忽、脫因不花、呂文、完哲、賀宗哲、安童、張守禮、伯顏、孫翥、姚守德、魏賽因不花、楊履信、關關等議，各分門而攻。 至夜，將士鼓勇登城，斬關而入，遂拔之。 劉福通奉其偽主從數百騎出東門遁走。 獲偽後及賊妻子數万、偽官五千、符璽印章寶貨無算。 全居民二十萬。 軍不敢私，市不易肆，不旬日河南悉定。 獻捷京師，歡聲動中外，以功拜河南行省平章政事，兼知河南行樞密院事、陝西行台御史中丞，仍便宜行事。 詔告天下。
In the nineteenth year Chaghan Temur set out to recover Bianliang. In the fifth month his main army encamped at Hulao. He first sent out scouting parties: one column marched south of Bianliang, sweeping Gui, Bo, Chen, and Cha; another marched east of the city while warships moved down the river. Land and water forces advanced together, overran southern Cao, and seized Huangling Ford. He then mobilized the Qin armies, marching out through Hangu Pass and through Hulao; Shanxi troops came through the Taihang, crossed the Yellow River, and joined the siege below Bianliang, where they first took the outer city. Chaghan Temur personally led his armored cavalry to Xinghua Camp while his generals ringed the city with siege works. The rebels sallied out again and again and were beaten each time. They then shut themselves inside the walls. He posted an ambush south of the city by night, and at dawn sent Miao skirmishers to feint along the eastern wall. The rebels poured out of the city in pursuit. The ambush force rose with drums and shouts, cut them off, and routed them. He also had a weak detachment throw up palisades outside the outer wall as bait. The rebels came out to seize the palisades. The decoy force feigned flight toward the west wall, then his armored cavalry burst out and overwhelmed them, capturing the entire party. After that the rebels scarcely dared to show themselves outside the walls. In the eighth month his agents reported that the city was at the end of its resources and food was almost gone. He conferred with his generals Yan Sixiao, Li Keyi, Hulinchi, Sayinchi, Dahu, Toyin Buqa, Lü Wen, Wanzhe, He Zongzhe, Antong, Zhang Shouli, Bayan, Sun Gao, Yao Shoude, Wei Sayin Buqa, Yang Lüxin, Guan Guan, and others, and assigned each a gate for the assault. That night his men scaled the walls with drums beating, broke through the gates, and took the city. Liu Futong fled through the east gate with his puppet ruler and a few hundred horsemen. They captured the rebel empress, tens of thousands of wives and children, five thousand rebel officials, and countless seals, tallies, and treasure. Two hundred thousand civilians were spared. His troops took no private plunder, the markets never closed, and within ten days all of Henan was pacified. When news of victory reached the capital, cheers rang inside and outside the palace. For this achievement he was appointed Grand Councillor of the Henan Branch Secretariat, Commissioner of the Henan Branch Bureau of Military Affairs, and Censor-in-Chief of the Shaanxi surveillance commission, retaining discretionary authority. An edict proclaimed the victory throughout the realm.
15
先是，中原亂，江南海漕不復通，京師屢苦飢。 至是，河南既定，檄書達江浙，海漕乃復至。 察罕帖木兒既定河南，乃以兵分鎮關陝、荊襄、河洛、江淮，而重兵屯太行，營壘旌旗相望數千里。 乃日修車船，繕兵甲，務農積穀，訓練士卒，謀大舉以復山東。
Earlier, with the Central Plains in chaos, grain shipments by sea from Jiangnan had ceased and the capital had suffered repeated famines. Now that Henan was secure, his dispatches reached Jiangsu and Zhejiang, and the sea transport of grain resumed. After pacifying Henan, Chaghan Temur posted garrisons across Guan-Shaan, Jingxiang, the Yellow River basin, and the Jianghuai region, while concentrating his main force on the Taihang, where camps and banners stretched in an unbroken line for thousands of li. He set to repairing chariots and ships, maintaining arms and armor, promoting agriculture and stockpiling grain, drilling his troops, and planning a major campaign to recover Shandong.
16
先是，山西晉、冀之地皆察罕帖木兒所平定。 而答失八都魯之子曰孛羅帖木兒，以兵駐大同，因欲並據晉、冀，遂至兵爭，天子屢下詔和解之，終不聽，事見《本紀》及《答失八都魯傳》中。
By then the Jin-Ji region of Shanxi had all been brought under Chaghan Temur's control. But Bolod Temur, son of Däshibatu'er, was stationed at Datong and sought to seize Shanxi and Hebei as well, leading to open warfare. The emperor repeatedly ordered them to make peace, but neither would yield. The affair is recorded in the *Basic Annals* and in the biography of Däshibatu'er.
17
二十一年，諜知山東群賊自相攻殺，而濟寧田豐降於賊。 六月，察罕帖木兒乃輿疾自陝抵洛，大會諸將，與議師期。 發并州軍出井陘，遼、沁軍出邯鄲，澤、潞軍出磁州，懷、衛軍出白馬，及汴、洛軍，水陸俱下，分道並進。 而自率鐵騎，建大將旗鼓，渡孟津，逾覃懷，鼓行而東，复冠州、東昌。 八月，師至鹽河。 遣其子擴廓帖木兒及諸將等，以精卒五萬搗東平。 與東平賊兵遇，兩戰皆敗之，斬首萬餘級，直抵其城下。 察罕帖木兒以田豐據山東久，軍民服之，乃遣書諭以逆順之理。 豐及王士誠皆降。 遂復東平、濟寧。 時大軍猶未渡，群賊皆聚於濟南，而出兵齊河、禹城以相抗。 察罕帖木兒分遣奇兵，取間道出賊後，南略泰安，逼益都，北徇濟陽、章丘，中循瀕海郡邑。 乃自將大軍渡河，與賊將戰於分齊，大敗之，進逼濟南城，而齊河、禹城俱來降，南道諸將亦報捷。 再敗益都兵於好石橋，東至海濱，郡邑聞風皆送款。 攻圍濟南三月，城乃下。 詔拜中書平章政事、知河南山東行樞密院事，陝西行台中丞如故。 察罕帖木兒遂移兵圍益都，環城列營凡數十，大治攻具，百道並進。 賊悉力拒守。 复掘重塹，築長圍，遏南洋河以灌城中。 仍分守要害，收輯流亡，郡縣戶口再歸職方，號令煥然矣。
In the twenty-first year his agents reported that Shandong rebel bands were fighting among themselves, while Tian Feng of Jining had gone over to the enemy. In the sixth month Chaghan Temur, though gravely ill, was carried in a litter from Shaanxi to Luoyang, summoned his generals, and set the date for the campaign. Bingzhou forces marched out through Jingxing, Liaoxi and Qinzhou troops through Handan, Ze and Lu troops through Cizhou, Huai and Wei troops through Baima, together with the Bianliang and Luoyang armies—all advancing by land and water on separate routes at once. He personally led his armored cavalry under the banners of a field marshal, crossed at Meng Ford, passed Tanhuai, and marched east with drums beating, recovering Guanzhou and Dongchang. In the eighth month the army reached the Salt River. He sent his son Köke Temur and other commanders with fifty thousand elite troops to strike Dongping. They met the rebel force at Dongping, defeated it twice, beheaded more than ten thousand men, and pressed to the city walls. Because Tian Feng had held Shandong for a long time and commanded the loyalty of soldiers and civilians alike, Chaghan Temur wrote urging him on the logic of loyalty and rebellion. Tian Feng and Wang Shicheng both surrendered. He then recovered Dongping and Jining. The main army had not yet crossed the river. The rebel hosts were massed at Jinan while detachments held Qihe and Yucheng in resistance. Chaghan Temur sent flanking columns by hidden routes behind the enemy lines, overran Tai'an to the south and pressed Yidu, swept north through Jiyang and Zhangqiu, and in the center subdued the coastal prefectures. He then crossed the river at the head of the main army, routed the rebel commanders at Fenqi, and advanced on Jinan. Qihe and Yucheng surrendered, and the southern columns reported victories as well. He defeated the Yidu force again at Haoshi Bridge. Eastward to the sea, prefectures and counties submitted as his fame spread. The siege of Jinan lasted three months before the city fell. An edict appointed him Grand Councillor of the Central Secretariat and Commissioner of the Henan-Shandong Branch Bureau of Military Affairs, while he retained his post as Shaanxi Censor-in-Chief. Chaghan Temur then shifted his army to besiege Yidu, ringing the city with dozens of camps, building siege engines on a grand scale, and assaulting from every direction at once. The defenders threw everything they had into the defense. He dug deep trenches, built a continuous encirclement, and dammed the Nanyang River to flood the city. He posted troops at key points, gathered in the displaced population, and brought prefectural registers back under official control until his authority shone clear across the region.
18
二十二年，時山東俱平，獨益都孤城猶未下。 六月，田豐、王士誠陰結賊，复圖叛。 田豐之降也，察罕帖木兒推誠待之不疑，數獨入其帳中。 及豐既謀變，乃請察罕帖木兒行觀營壘。 眾以為不可往，察罕帖木兒曰：「吾推心待人，安得人人而防之？ 」左右請以力士從，又不許，乃從輕騎十有一人行。 至王信營，又至豐營，遂為王士誠所刺。 訃聞，帝震悼，朝廷公卿及京師四方之人，不問男女老幼，無不慟哭者。
In the twenty-second year Shandong was largely pacified, and Yidu alone still held out. In the sixth month Tian Feng and Wang Shicheng secretly contacted rebel forces and plotted a new revolt. When Tian Feng surrendered, Chaghan Temur had treated him with open trust and often visited his camp alone. Once the plot was laid, Tian Feng invited Chaghan Temur to inspect the camps. His officers urged him not to go. Chaghan Temur said, "I deal with men in good faith—how can I suspect everyone? His attendants asked to send bodyguards along, but he refused and rode out with only eleven light horsemen. He visited Wang Xin's camp, then Tian Feng's camp, where Wang Shicheng stabbed him to death. When word of his death reached the court, the emperor was stricken with grief. Nobles and commoners alike throughout the capital and the realm, young and old, men and women, wept without exception.
19
先是，有白氣如索，長五百餘丈，起危宿，掃太微垣。 太史奏山東當大水。 帝曰：「不然，山東必失一良將。 」即馳詔戒察罕帖木兒勿輕舉，未至而已及於難。 詔贈推誠定遠宣忠亮節功臣、開府儀同三司、上柱國、河南行省左丞相，追封忠襄王，諡獻武。 及葬，賜賻有加，改贈宣忠興運弘仁效節功臣，追封潁川王，改諡忠襄，食邑沈丘縣，所在立祠，歲時致祭。 封其父阿魯溫汝陽王，後又進封梁王。
Earlier a white vapor like a cord more than five hundred zhang long had risen from the lodge of Wei and swept across the Supreme Palace enclosure. The court astrologer reported that Shandong would suffer catastrophic flooding. The emperor said, "No—Shandong is about to lose a great general. He immediately sent an urgent edict warning Chaghan Temur not to take risks, but it arrived too late. An edict posthumously ennobled him Meritous Minister of Sincere Trust, Settling the Distance, Proclaiming Loyalty, and Bright Integrity, appointed him Grand Master of the Palace with Golden Seal and Ceremonial equal to the Three Excellencies and Supreme Pillar of the State and Left Chancellor of the Henan Branch Secretariat, and enfeoffed him posthumously as Prince of Zhongxiang with the temple name Xianwu. At his burial the court added to the funeral gifts. He was later granted the title Meritous Minister of Proclaiming Loyalty, Promoting the Dynasty, and Manifesting Benevolence and Effecting Integrity, posthumously enfeoffed as Prince of Yingchuan with the temple name Zhongxiang and a fief at Shenqiu County. Temples were erected wherever he had served, with sacrifices at the proper seasons. His father Arun was enfeoffed as Prince of Ruyang and later advanced to Prince of Liang.
20
於是復起擴廓帖木兒，拜銀青榮祿大夫、太尉、中書平章政事、知樞密院事、皇太子詹事，仍便宜行事，襲總其父兵。 擴廓帖木兒既領兵柄，銜哀以討賊，攻城益急，而城守益固，乃穴地通道以入。 十一月，拔其城，執其渠魁陳猱頭二百餘人獻闕下，而取田豐、王士誠之心以祭其父，餘黨皆就誅。 即遣關保以兵取莒州，於是山東悉平。 擴廓帖木兒本察罕帖木兒之甥，自幼養以為子。 當是時，東至淄、沂，西逾關陝，皆晏然無事。 擴廓帖木兒乃駐兵於汴、洛。 朝廷方倚之以為安。
Köke Temur was then restored to office as Silver-Gleaming Grand Master of Splendid Happiness, Grand Marshal, Grand Councillor of the Central Secretariat, Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs, and Tutor to the Heir Apparent, with discretionary authority and command of his father's armies. Taking command in grief, Köke Temur pressed the siege harder as the defenders held firm. He then drove tunnels under the walls to break in. In the eleventh month he took the city, sent more than two hundred ringleaders including Chen Yaotou to the capital, cut out the hearts of Tian Feng and Wang Shicheng to sacrifice to his father, and executed the remaining rebels. He then sent Guan Bao to take Juzhou, and Shandong was fully pacified. Köke Temur was in fact Chaghan Temur's nephew, whom he had raised from childhood as his own son. At that time, from Zibo and Yishui in the east to the Guan-Shaan region in the west, the land was tranquil. Köke Temur stationed his army at Bianliang and Luoyang. The court was counting on him to keep the realm secure.
21
孛羅帖木兒自察罕帖木兒既沒，復數以兵爭晉、冀。 帝雖屢解諭之，而仇隙日深。 二十三年，御史大夫老的沙與知樞密院事禿堅帖木兒得罪於皇太子，皇太子欲誅之，皆奔於大同，為孛羅帖木兒所匿。 老的沙者，帝母舅，以故帝數為皇太子寢其事，而皇太子不從，帝無如之何，則傳旨密令孛羅帖木兒隱其跡。 而丞相搠思監、宦者樸不花皆附皇太子，必窮竟其事。 皇太子又方倚重於擴廓帖木兒。 時擴廓帖木兒駐太原，與孛羅帖木兒構兵，勢相持不可解。 二十四年，搠思監、樸不花因誣孛羅帖木兒、老的沙謀為不軌，而皇太子亦怒不已。 三月，天子以故下詔數孛羅帖木兒罪，削其官職而奪其兵。 孛羅帖木兒不受詔，遂遣兵逼京師，必欲得搠思監、樸不花乃已。 天子不得已，縛兩人與之。 語在搠思監、孛羅帖木兒傳。 七月，孛羅帖木兒又與老的沙合禿堅帖木兒兵同犯闕。 時擴廓帖木兒遣部將白鎖住以萬騎衛京師，駐於龍虎台，與戰不利，遂奉皇太子奔於太原。 孛羅帖木兒既入朝，據相位。 白鎖住又將二萬騎屯漁陽，為朝廷聲援。 二十五年，擴廓帖木兒以兵搗大同取之。 皇太子乃趣擴廓帖木兒大舉以討逆，發丞相也速兵屯東鄙，魏、遼、齊、吳、豫、豳諸王兵駐西邊，而自率擴廓帖木兒兵取中道，抵京師。 亡何，孛羅帖木兒既伏誅，帝詔白鎖住兵守京城，遂詔皇太子還京，而擴廓帖木兒亦扈從入朝。 九月，詔拜伯撒里右丞相，擴廓帖木兒左丞相。 伯撒里累朝舊臣，而擴廓帖木兒以後生晚出，乃與並相。 居兩月，即請南還視師。
After Chaghan Temur's death, Bolod Temur again and again fought for control of Shanxi and Hebei. Though the emperor repeatedly ordered them to make peace, their hatred only deepened. In the twenty-third year Censor-in-Chief Laodisha and Commissioner Toghan Temur offended the heir apparent, who sought to have them executed. Both fled to Datong, where Bolod Temur sheltered them. Laodisha was the emperor's uncle on his mother's side. The emperor repeatedly asked the heir apparent to drop the matter, but the heir would not relent. Unable to resolve it openly, the emperor secretly ordered Bolod Temur to conceal them. But Chancellor Toghtum and the eunuch Puhua, both aligned with the heir apparent, were determined to pursue the case to the end. The heir apparent was also placing great reliance on Köke Temur. Köke Temur was then stationed at Taiyuan and clashed with Bolod Temur; their armies faced each other in a stalemate that would not break. In the twenty-fourth year Toghtum and Puhua accused Bolod Temur and Laodisha of plotting sedition, and the heir apparent's anger knew no end. In the third month the emperor issued an edict listing Bolod Temur's crimes, stripped him of rank and office, and removed his command. Bolod Temur refused the edict and marched on the capital, vowing not to stop until Toghtum and Puhua were delivered to him. The emperor had no choice but to bind the two men and hand them over. The full account appears in the biographies of Toghtum and Bolod Temur. In the seventh month Bolod Temur again joined Laodisha and Toghan Temur in an assault on the palace. Köke Temur had sent his subordinate Bai Suozhu with ten thousand cavalry to guard the capital at Longhutai. They were defeated and escorted the heir apparent in flight to Taiyuan. Once Bolod Temur entered the capital, he took control of the government. Bai Suozhu meanwhile stationed twenty thousand cavalry at Yuyang to support the court from the northeast. In the twenty-fifth year (1365) Köke Temur attacked and seized Datong. The heir apparent then pressed Köke Temur to mount a major campaign against the rebel, deployed Chancellor Yesü's force on the eastern frontier, posted the armies of the Wei, Liao, Qi, Wu, Yu, and Bin princes on the west, and himself led Köke Temur's troops along the central route toward the capital. Before long Bolod Temur was executed. The emperor ordered Bai Suozhu to hold the capital, summoned the heir apparent back to Beijing, and Köke Temur escorted him into court. In the ninth month an edict appointed Barsari Right Chancellor and Köke Temur Left Chancellor. Barsari was a veteran minister of many reigns, yet Köke Temur, a much younger man who had risen late, was made his equal as chancellor. After two months he asked leave to return south and take command of the armies in the field.
22
是時，中原雖無事，而江淮、川蜀皆非我所有。 皇太子累請出督師，而帝難之，乃詔封擴廓帖木兒河南王，俾總天下兵而代之行。 擴廓帖木兒於是分省以自隨，官屬之盛，幾與朝廷等，而用孫翥、趙恆等為謀主。 二十六年二月，自京師還河南，欲廬墓以終喪。 左右咸以謂受命出師不可中止，乃復北渡，居懷慶，又移居彰德。
The Central Plains were quiet for the moment, but Jianghuai and Sichuan were no longer under court control. The heir apparent repeatedly asked to take the field in person, but the emperor hesitated. He then enfeoffed Köke Temur as Prince of Henan and charged him to command all armies in the heir's stead. Köke Temur took a full provincial staff with him, his entourage nearly rivaling the court in scale, with Sun Gao and Zhao Heng as his chief advisers. In the second month of the twenty-sixth year (1366) he left the capital for Henan, intending to build a mourning lodge at his father's tomb and observe the full mourning rites. His advisers urged that a campaign order could not be abandoned halfway. He crossed the river again, encamped at Huaiqing, and later moved to Zhangde.
23
初，李思齊與察罕帖木兒同起義師，齒位相等。 及是擴廓帖木兒總其兵，思齊心不能平。 而張良弼首拒命，孔興、脫列伯等​​亦皆以功自恃，各懷異見，請別為一軍，莫肯統屬。 釁隙既開，遂成仇敵。 擴廓帖木兒乃遣關保、虎林赤以兵西攻良弼於鹿台，而思齊亦與良弼合，兵連不能罷。 擴廓帖木兒始受命南征，而顧乃退居彰德，又惟務用兵陝西，天子之命置而不問，朝廷因疑其有異志。 皇太子之奔太原也，欲用唐肅宗靈武故事，因而自立。 擴廓帖木兒與孛蘭奚等不從。 及還京師，皇后奇氏傳旨，令擴廓帖木兒以重兵擁太子入城，欲脅帝禪之位。 擴廓帖木兒知其意，比至京城三十里，即散遣其軍。 由是皇​​太子心銜之。 及是，屢趣其出師江淮。 擴廓帖木兒第遣弟脫因帖木兒及部將完哲、貊高以兵往山東。 而西兵互相勝負，終不解。 帝又下詔和解之，顧乃戕殺詔使天下奴等，而跋扈之跡成矣。
Li Siqi had risen alongside Chaghan Temur in the righteous armies, and their standing had long been equal. Now that Köke Temur commanded his forces, Li Siqi could not accept the arrangement. Zhang Liangbi was the first to defy him. Kong Xing, Töle Buqa, and others, each proud of his own achievements, refused unified command and demanded separate armies. The breach widened until they were outright enemies. Köke Temur sent Guan Bao and Hulinchi west to attack Liangbi at Lutai. Li Siqi joined Liangbi, and the fighting dragged on without end. Though ordered south, Köke Temur lingered at Zhangde and busied himself with warfare in Shaanxi, ignoring imperial commands until the court suspected disloyalty. When the heir apparent fled to Taiyuan, he wished to follow Tang Suzong's precedent at Lingwu and proclaim himself independently. Köke Temur, Berlanxi, and others refused to go along. On his return to the capital, Empress Qi sent word instructing Köke Temur to enter the city at the head of a large army escorting the heir apparent, intending to force the emperor to abdicate. Köke Temur understood their aim. When he was still thirty li from the capital, he dismissed his army. The heir apparent never forgave him for this. He now pressed him again and again to march on Jianghuai. Köke Temur sent only his brother Toyin Temur and the generals Wanzhe and Mo Gao with troops into Shandong. The western armies traded victories without resolution. The emperor issued another edict ordering reconciliation, but Köke Temur killed the envoys including Tianxia Nu, and his defiance was complete.
24
二十七年八月，帝乃下詔命皇太子親出總天下兵馬，而分命擴廓帖木兒以其兵自潼關以東，肅清江淮； 李思齊以其兵自鳳翔以西，進取川蜀； 禿魯以其兵與張良弼、孔興、脫列伯等​​取襄樊； 王信以其兵固守山東信地。 然詔書雖下，皇太子亦竟止不行，而分兵之命，擴廓帖木兒終扞拒不肯受。 於是貊高、關保等皆叛擴廓帖木兒。 關保自察罕帖木兒起兵以來即為將，勇冠諸軍，功最高。 而貊高善論兵，尤為察罕帖木兒所信任。 及是，兩人見擴廓帖木兒有不臣之心，故皆叛之，列其罪狀聞於朝，舉兵共攻之。 而皇太子用沙藍答兒、帖林沙、伯顏帖木兒、李國鳳等計，立撫軍院，總制天下軍馬，專備擴廓帖木兒。 以貊高等能倡大義，賜號忠義功臣。
In the eighth month of the twenty-seventh year (1367) the emperor ordered the heir apparent to take personal command of all armies, while Köke Temur was to lead his forces east of Tong Pass to recover Jianghuai; Li Siqi was to advance from west of Fengxiang into Sichuan; Tulu, together with Zhang Liangbi, Kong Xing, and Töle Buqa, was to recover Xiangyang and Fancheng; Wang Xin was to hold the loyal territories of Shandong. The edict was issued, but the heir apparent never marched. As for the divided commands, Köke Temur refused them outright. Mo Gao, Guan Bao, and others then rebelled against Köke Temur. Guan Bao had been a commander since Chaghan Temur first took the field, renowned as the bravest of the army and highest in achievement. Mo Gao was an expert in military affairs and had been especially trusted by Chaghan Temur. Seeing that Köke Temur harbored disloyal ambitions, both men rebelled, reported his crimes to court, and joined forces against him. The heir apparent, following the counsel of Shalandari, Tielinsha, Bayan Temur, Li Guofeng, and others, established the Pacification Army Commission to control all military forces and focus them against Köke Temur. Mo Gao and his allies were honored as Meritous Loyalty Ministers for taking the lead in righteous resistance.
25
十月，詔落擴廓帖木兒太傅、中書左丞相，依前河南王，以汝州為食邑，與弟脫因帖木兒同居河南府，而以河南府為梁王食邑，從行官屬悉令還朝。 凡擴廓帖木兒所總諸軍，在帳前者白鎖住、虎林赤領之，在河南者李克彝領之，在山東者也速領之，在山西者沙藍答兒領之，在河北者貊高領之。 擴廓帖木兒既受詔，即退軍屯澤州。 詔又命禿魯與李思齊、張良弼、孔興、脫列伯率兵東向，以正天討。 二十八年，朝廷命左丞孫景益分省太原，關保以兵為之守。 擴廓帖木兒即遣兵據太原，而盡殺朝廷所置官。 皇太子乃命魏賽因不花及關保皆以兵與思齊、良弼諸軍夾攻澤州，而天子又下詔削奪擴廓帖木兒爵邑，令諸軍共誅之，其將士官吏效順者與免本罪，惟孫翥、趙恆罪在所不赦。 二月，擴廓帖木兒退守於平陽，而關保遂據澤、潞二州，以與貊高合。 時李思齊、張良弼、孔興、脫列伯與擴廓帖木兒相持既久，大明兵時已及河南，思齊、良弼皆遣使詣擴廓帖木兒，告以出師非本心，乃解兵大掠西歸。 七月，貊高、關保進攻平陽。 當是時，擴廓帖木兒氣稍沮，而關保、貊高勢甚振，數請戰，擴廓帖木兒不應，或師出即復退。 一日，諜知貊高分軍掠祁縣，即夜出師薄其營掩擊之，大敗其眾，貊高、關保皆就擒。 朝廷聞之，遽罷撫軍院，而帖林沙、伯顏帖木兒、李國鳳等以誤國皆受黜。 既而擴廓帖木兒上疏自陳其情悃，帝尋亦悔悟，下詔滌其前非。
In the tenth month an edict stripped Köke Temur of the posts of Grand Preceptor and Left Chancellor while confirming him as Prince of Henan with Ruzhou as his fief. He and his brother Toyin Temur were confined to Henan Prefecture, which was reassigned as the Liang prince's fief, and all his staff were ordered back to court. His former armies were reassigned: Bai Suozhu and Hulinchi took those in his personal command; Li Keyi those in Henan; Yesü those in Shandong; Shalandari those in Shanxi; and Mo Gao those in Hebei. On receiving the edict Köke Temur withdrew his army to Zezhou. Another edict ordered Tulu, Li Siqi, Zhang Liangbi, Kong Xing, and Töle Buqa to march east and execute Heaven's punishment. In the twenty-eighth year (1368) the court appointed Left Vice Minister Sun Jingyi to administer Taiyuan on separate commission, with Guan Bao's troops as garrison. Köke Temur at once sent troops to seize Taiyuan and killed every official the court had installed there. The heir apparent ordered Wei Sayin Buqa and Guan Bao to join Li Siqi and Liangbi in attacking Zezhou, while the emperor stripped Köke Temur of rank and fief and ordered all armies to destroy him. Subordinates who submitted would be pardoned—only Sun Gao and Zhao Heng were excepted. In the second month Köke Temur fell back to Pingyang. Guan Bao seized Ze and Lu prefectures and joined Mo Gao. Li Siqi, Liangbi, Kong Xing, and Töle Buqa had long faced Köke Temur in stalemate while Ming forces were already in Henan. Siqi and Liangbi sent envoys to Köke Temur saying they had never meant to fight him, then broke camp and plundered their way west. In the seventh month Mo Gao and Guan Bao advanced on Pingyang. Köke Temur's morale was flagging while Guan Bao and Mo Gao were at their peak. They repeatedly offered battle, but Köke Temur refused or marched out only to retreat again. When spies reported that Mo Gao had split his force to plunder Qi County, Köke Temur marched by night, fell on the camp, and routed them. Mo Gao and Guan Bao were captured. On hearing the news the court abolished the Pacification Army Commission and dismissed Tielinsha, Bayan Temur, Li Guofeng, and others for misleading the state. Köke Temur then submitted a memorial pleading his case in his own words. The emperor soon regretted the breach and issued an edict clearing his earlier faults.
26
於是大明兵已定山東及河、洛，中原俱不守。 閏七月，帝乃下詔，復命擴廓帖木兒仍前河南王、太傅、中書左丞相，孫翥、趙恆並復舊職，以兵從河北南討，也速以兵趨山東，禿魯兵出潼關，李思齊兵出七盤、金、商，以圖复汴、洛。 未幾，也速兵遂潰，禿魯、思齊兵亦未嘗出，而擴廓帖木兒又自平陽退守太原，不復敢南向，事已不可為矣。 已而大明兵迫京城，帝北奔，國遂以亡。 及大明兵至太原，擴廓帖木兒即棄城遁，領其餘眾西奔於甘肅。
By then Ming forces had taken Shandong and the Yellow River basin, and the Central Plains were lost. In the intercalary seventh month the emperor restored Köke Temur as Prince of Henan, Grand Preceptor, and Left Chancellor, reinstated Sun Gao and Zhao Heng, and ordered a multi-front counteroffensive: Köke Temur south from Hebei, Yesü into Shandong, Tulu through Tong Pass, and Li Siqi through the Qipan and Jin-Shang routes to recover Bianliang and Luoyang. Yesü's army was soon routed. Tulu and Li Siqi never marched. Köke Temur fell back from Pingyang to Taiyuan and dared not advance south. The cause was already lost. Before long Ming forces reached the capital. The emperor fled north, and the dynasty fell. When Ming troops reached Taiyuan, Köke Temur abandoned the city and fled west into Gansu with what remained of his force.
