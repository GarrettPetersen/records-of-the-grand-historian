1
烏古孫良楨
Wugusun Liangzhen
2
烏古孫良楨字幹卿，世次見父澤傳。 資器絕人，好讀書。 至治二年，蔭補江陰州判官，尋丁內艱，服除，調婺州武義縣尹，有惠政。 改漳州路推官，獄有疑者，悉平反之。 上言：「律，徒者不杖，今杖而又徒，非恤刑意，宜加徒減杖。 」遂定為令。 移泉州，益以能稱。 轉延平判官，拜陝西行臺監察御史，劾遼陽行省左丞相達識帖睦邇賣國不忠，援漢高帝斬丁公故事，以明人臣大義。 并劾御史中丞胡居祐奸邪，皆罷之，中外震懾。 陞都事，猶以言不盡行，解去。
Wugusun Liangzhen, style name Ganqing, is treated in his father Ze's biography for his family line. He was gifted beyond ordinary men and devoted to books. In Zhizhi 2 (1322) he entered office by hereditary privilege as Jiangyin assistant prefect, then withdrew for his mother's mourning. After the mourning period he became magistrate of Wuyi in Wuzhou and won a reputation for humane rule. Made judicial reviewer on the Zhangzhou circuit, he overturned every doubtful case in the prisons. He submitted a memorial: "The law exempts those sentenced to penal servitude from the cane, yet today they are caned and then given servitude as well. That is not the spirit of lenient punishment; additional servitude should replace beating. " The rule was then enacted in law. Transferred to Quanzhou, he became still better known for competence. Promoted to Yanping judge and then Shaanxi investigating censor, he impeached Tash Temür, left chancellor of the Liaoyang secretariat, for treasonous collusion with the enemy, invoking Gaozu's execution of Ding Gong to state the paramount duty of a minister. He also impeached Vice Censor-in-Chief Hu Juyou for corruption; both men were removed, and the realm took notice. Raised to secretariat supervisor, he resigned when his recommendations were not fully implemented.
3
復起為監察御史，良楨以帝方覽萬幾，不可不求賢自輔，於是連疏：「天曆數年間紀綱大壞，元氣傷夷。 天祐聖明，入膺大統，而西宮秉政，奸臣弄權，畜憾十有餘年。 天威一怒，陰晦開明，以正大名，以章大孝，此誠兢兢業業祈天永命之秋，其術在乎敬身修德而已。 今經筵多領以職事臣，數日一進講，不渝數刻已罷，而御小臣，恒侍左右，何益於盛德哉。 臣願招延儒臣若許衡者數人，置於禁密，常以唐、虞、三代之道，啟沃宸衷，日新其德，實萬世無疆之福也。 」又以國俗父死則妻其從母，兄弟死則收其妻，父母死無憂制，遂言：「綱常皆出於天而不可變，議法之吏，乃言國人不拘此例，諸國人各從本俗。 是漢、南人當守綱常，國人、諸國人不必守綱常也。 名曰優之，實則陷之，外若尊之，內實侮之，推其本心所以待國人者，不若漢、南人之厚也。 請下禮官有司及右科進士在朝者會議，自天子至於庶人，皆從禮制，以成列聖未遑之典，明萬世不易之道。 」又言：「隱士劉因，道學經術可比許文正公衡，從祀孔子廟庭。 」皆不報。 御史臺作新風憲，復疏其所當行者，以舉賢才為綱，而以厚風俗、均賦役、重審理、汰冗官、選守令、出奉使、均公田為目，指擿剴切，雖觸忌諱，亦不顧也。 宦者罕失嬖妾殺其妻，糜其肉飼犬，上疏乞正重刑，并論宦寺結廷臣撓政為害，可汰黜之。 憸佞側目。
Reappointed investigating censor, Liangzhen held that with the emperor now personally handling state affairs he must seek worthy assistants; he therefore submitted repeated memorials: "Through the Tiansli era statutes collapsed and the polity's vital force was drained. Heaven guarded his sacred wisdom and he took the throne, yet the western palace faction held power and scheming ministers played with authority, hoarding grievances for more than ten years. When imperial wrath broke once, darkness lifted, the rightful name was restored, and supreme filial piety was proclaimed. This is the moment to tremble before Heaven and pray for an enduring mandate; the means is nothing but self-restraint and moral cultivation. The Classics Colloquium is now staffed mainly by busy officials who lecture every few days for no more than a few quarters of an hour, while petty attendants always hover at the emperor's side. How does that advance his virtue? I beg that several scholars such as Xu Heng be invited into the inner palace to instruct Your Majesty daily in the ways of Tang, Yu, and the Three Dynasties, renewing your virtue—an immeasurable blessing for ages to come. " He also noted that Mongol custom allowed marrying a stepmother or a brother's widow and imposed no mourning for parents, and argued: "Ethical constants come from Heaven and cannot change, yet drafters of law say Mongols need not follow these rules and that each people may keep its own customs. So Han and southerners must uphold moral law, while Mongols and other peoples need not. What is called favor is really a snare: honored in appearance, despised in fact. At bottom Mongols are treated less generously than Han and southerners. He asked ritual officials, responsible agencies, and right-branch jinshi at court to deliberate so that from the Son of Heaven to commoners all follow ritual law, completing what earlier sages had not had time to enact and fixing the eternal Way. " He also urged that the recluse Liu Yin, whose learning matched Xu Heng's, be enshrined in Confucius' temple. " None of these proposals was answered. When the Censorate revised its charter he memorialized again, making recommendation of talent the theme and listing sound customs, fair taxes, careful trials, cutting redundant posts, choosing magistrates, sending envoys, and equalizing public fields as headings—sharp and fearless even of taboo. The eunuch Han killed his wife and fed her flesh to his dogs after losing a concubine; Liangzhen demanded the heaviest penalty and argued that eunuch establishments in league with courtiers should be abolished. Flatterers and villains glared at him.
4
至正四年，召為刑部員外郎，轉御史臺都事。 五年，改中書左司都事，出為江東道肅政廉訪司副使。 上官一日，辭歸。 六年，授平江路緫管，不拜。 八年，復召為右司員外郎。 九年，陞郎中，尋遷廣東道肅政廉訪使，未行，還為郎中，遷福建道肅政廉訪使，中道召還，參議中書省事，兼經筵官。 十一年，拜治書侍御史，陞中書參知政事、同知經筵事。
In Zhizheng 4 (1344) he was called up as vice minister of punishments and made Censorate secretariat supervisor. In the fifth year he became left secretariat supervisor and was posted as deputy surveillance commissioner on the Jiangdong integrity circuit. He took office for a single day and resigned. In the sixth year he was named prefect of Pingjiang but declined the appointment. In the eighth year he was recalled as vice director of the right secretariat department. In the ninth year he rose to director, was named Guangdong surveillance commissioner, recalled before leaving, made Fujian commissioner, summoned back en route, appointed secretariat participant, and given charge of the Classics Colloquium. In the eleventh year he became associate censor, then secretariat vice minister and concurrent vice head of the Classics Colloquium.
5
十三年，陞左丞，兼大司農卿，仍同知經筵事。 時中書參用非人，事多異同，不得一一如志。 會軍餉不給，請與右丞悟良哈台主屯田，歲入二十萬石。 東宮久未建，懇懇為言，車駕幸上都，始冊皇太子。 立詹事院，驛召為副詹事，每直端本堂，則進正心誠意之說、親君子遠小人之道，皇太子嘉納焉。 當時盜賊蜂起，帝聞，惡之，下詔分討，必盡誅而後已。 良楨言：「平賊在收人心，以回天意，多殺非道也。 」乃赦以安之。
In the thirteenth year he was made left chancellor and grand minister of agriculture while retaining his Classics Colloquium post. The Secretariat then relied on unfit men; business often went against his wishes. When army rations ran short he joined Right Chancellor Wulianghatai in supervising garrison farming, yielding two hundred thousand piculs that year. The heir apparent had long been uninstalled; he pleaded repeatedly until, on the emperor's journey to the Upper Capital, the crown prince was finally invested. When the heir's household was founded he was urgently summoned as deputy household master. At the Hall of the Upright Root he taught sincerity and the choice of worthy friends over flatterers, and the crown prince welcomed his counsel. Bandits swarmed the realm; the emperor, enraged, ordered pursuit everywhere and extermination to the last man. Liangzhen argued: "Suppressing rebels means winning hearts and restoring Heaven's favor, not slaughter. " An amnesty followed to calm the people.
6
十四年，遷淮南行省左丞。 初，泰州賊張士誠既降復叛，殺淮南行省參知政事趙璉，進據高郵、六合，太師脫脫奉詔，緫諸王軍南征，而良楨洎參議龔伯璲、刑部主事廬山等從之。 既平六合，垂克高郵，會詔罷脫脫兵柄，遂有上變告伯璲等勸脫脫勒兵北向者，下其事逮問，詞連良楨，簿對無所驗。 即日還中書左丞，命分省彰德，主調軍食，居半歲，還中書。 十六年，進階榮祿大夫，賜玉帶一。
In the fourteenth year he became left chancellor of the Huainan secretariat. Zhang Shicheng of Taizhou had submitted, then rebelled again, killing Huainan vice minister Zhao Lian and seizing Gaoyou and Liuhe. Grand Preceptor Toqto'a led the princes' armies south, with Liangzhen, Gong Bojue, Lushan, and others in his train. Liuhe fell and Gaoyou was nearly taken when Toqto'a was stripped of command. Someone then accused Gong Bojue of urging him to march north with the army; Liangzhen was caught in the inquiry but the ledger showed no proof. The same day he was restored as left chancellor, sent to Zhangde to provision troops, and after six months returned to the central secretariat. In the sixteenth year he was promoted to grand master for glorious happiness and granted a jade belt.
7
十七年，除大司農。 明年，陞右丞，兼大司農，辭，不允。 論罷陷賊延坐之令。 有惡少年誣知宜興州張復通賊之罪，中書將籍其孥，吏抱案請署。 良楨曰； 「手可斷，案不可署。 」同列變色，卒不署。
In the seventeenth year he became grand minister of agriculture. The following year he was made right chancellor while keeping agriculture; he declined but was refused. He persuaded the court to repeal collective punishment for families of those who joined bandits. Ruffians accused Yixing prefect Zhang Fu of colluding with rebels; the secretariat was set to confiscate his family when a clerk brought the file for signature. Liangzhen said: "My hand may be severed, but I will not sign this file. " His colleagues blanched, yet he never signed.
8
良楨自左曹登政府，多所建白。 罷福建、山東食鹽，浙東、西長生牛租，瀕海被災圍田稅，民皆德之。 嘗論至正格輕重不倫，吏得並緣為奸，舉明律者數人，參酌古今，重定律書，書成而罷。 家居輒訓諸子曰：「吾無過人者，惟待人以誠，人亦以誠遇我，汝宜志之。 」晚歲病瘠，數謁告，病益侵，遂卒。 自號約齋。 有詩文奏議凡若干卷，藏于家。
After rising from the left secretariat into government, Liangzhen offered many reforms. He ended Fujian and Shandong salt taxes, Zhejiang ox rents, and coastal disaster-field levies, winning popular gratitude. He showed that the Zhizheng code's penalties were incoherent and invited abuse; experts in the Ming code helped him redraft the statutes, but the project ended when he left office. At home he told his sons, "I surpass no one except in sincerity toward others, which others return—remember that. " In old age he wasted away, took repeated leave, worsened, and died. He took the sobriquet Master of Restraint. His writings and memorials, several scrolls in all, remain with his family.
9
賈魯字友恒，河東高平人。 幼負志節，既長，謀略過人。 延祐、至治間，兩以明經領鄉貢。 泰定初，恩授東平路儒學教授，辟憲史，歷行省掾，除潞城縣尹，選丞相東曹掾，擢戶部主事，未上。 一日，覺心悸，尋得父書，筆勢顫縮，即辭歸，比至家，父已有風疾，未幾卒。
Jia Lu, style name Youheng, came from Gaoping in Hedong. As a boy he showed firm purpose; grown, his plans outmatched others. In the Yanyou and Zhizhi periods he twice headed his circuit in the classics examination. Early in Taiding he became Dongping Confucian professor, then clerk, branch aide, Lucheng magistrate, chancellor's eastern aide, and revenue director—though he never assumed the last post. One day his heart fluttered; a letter arrived with his father's shaking hand—he resigned at once. He reached home to find his father stricken and soon dead.
10
魯居喪服闋，起為太醫院都事。 會詔修遼、金、宋三史，召魯為宋史局官。 書成，選魯燕南山東道奉使宣撫幕官，考績居最，遷中書省檢校官。 上言：「十八河倉，近歲淪沒官糧百三十萬斛，其弊由富民兼并，貧民流亡，宜合先正經界，然事體重大，非處置盡善，不可輕發。 」書累數萬言，切中其弊。 俄拜監察御史，首言御史有封事，宜專達聖聰，不宜臺臣先有所可否。 陞臺都事，遷山北廉訪副使，復召為工部郎中，言考工一十九事。
After mourning Lu became supervisor of the Imperial Medical Academy. When the court ordered histories of Liao, Jin, and Song compiled, Lu joined the Song History bureau. After the history was done he served on the Yanshan-Shandong east pacification mission, topped the review, and became secretariat inspector. He reported that eighteen river granaries had lost 1.3 million piculs to flood because the rich seized land and the poor fled; boundaries must be restored, but the task was too large to rush. " His memorial ran to tens of thousands of words and hit the abuses squarely. Soon made investigating censor, he argued sealed memorials should reach the emperor directly without prior censorate debate. He rose to Censorate supervisor, became Shanbei deputy commissioner, was recalled as works director, and submitted nineteen proposals on public works.
11
至正四年，河決白茅堤，又決金堤，並河郡邑，民居昏墊，壯者流離。 帝甚患之，遣使體驗，仍督大臣訪求治河方略，特命魯行都水監。 魯循行河道，考察地形，往復數千里，備得要害，為圖上進二策：其一，議修築北堤，以制橫潰，則用工省； 其一，議疏塞並舉，挽河東行，使復故道，其功數倍。 會遷右司郎中，議未及竟。 其在右司，言時政二十一事，皆見舉行。 調都漕運使，復以漕事二十事言之，朝廷取其八事：一曰京畿和糴，二曰優恤漕司舊領漕戶，三曰接連委官，四曰通州緫治豫定委官，五曰船戶困於壩夫，海運壞於壩戶，六曰疏濬運河，七曰臨清運糧萬戶府當隸漕司，八曰宣忠船戶付本司節制。 事未盡行。 既而河水北侵安山，淪入運河，延袤濟南、河間，將隳兩漕司鹽場，實妨國計。
In Zhizheng 4 the Yellow River burst the Baimao and Jin dikes; river towns were drowned and the able-bodied fled. The emperor sent inspectors and ordered ministers to seek river remedies, naming Lu acting waterways director. Lu surveyed thousands of li, mapped the river, and offered two plans: repair the north dike to check lateral floods at lower cost; or dredge and dam together to force the river east back to its old channel at several times the labor. Transferred to the right secretariat, he left the debate unfinished. In the right department he proposed twenty-one policies, all enacted. As transport director he listed twenty transport reforms; eight were accepted: capital-region harmonized purchase; relief for old transport households; chained appointments; Tongzhou oversight; easing boat families' dam labor; canal dredging; subordinating Linqing transport to the main office; and placing Xuanzhong boat households under its control. Not all were implemented. Soon the river drove north into the canal through Jinan and Hejian, threatening both transport offices' salt fields and the state revenue.
12
九年，太傅、右丞相脫脫復相，論及河決，思拯民艱，以塞詔旨，乃集廷臣群議，言人人殊。 魯昌言：「河必當治。 」復以前二策進，丞相取其後策，與魯定議，且以其事屬魯。 魯固辭，丞相曰：「此事非子不可。 」乃入奏，大稱帝旨。 十一年四月，命魯以工部尚書、緫治河防使，進秩二品，授以銀章，領河南、北諸路軍民，發汴梁、大名十有三路民一十五萬，廬州等戍十有八翼軍二萬供役，一切從事大小軍民官，咸稟節度，便宜興繕。 是月鳩工，七月鑿河成，八月決水故河，九月舟楫通，十一月諸埽諸堤成，水土工畢，河復故道。 事見河渠志。 帝遣使報祭河伯，召魯還京師，魯以河平圖獻。 帝適覽臺臣奏疏，請褒脫脫治河之績，次論魯功，超拜榮祿大夫、集賢大學士，賞賚金帛，敕翰林丞旨歐陽玄製河平碑，以旌脫脫勞績，具載魯功，且宣付史館，并贈魯先臣三世。
In the ninth year Toqto'a returned as right chancellor, gathered ministers on the breach to ease popular suffering and meet the edict, but opinions diverged. Lu declared flatly, "The river must be tamed. " He resubmitted his two plans; Toqto'a chose the larger and entrusted Lu with it. Lu refused, but Toqto'a said, "Only you can do this. " He memorialized and won full imperial approval. In the eleventh year, fourth month, he became works minister and river commissioner, raised two ranks, given a silver seal, commanding Henan-north armies and civilians—150,000 laborers from thirteen circuits and 20,000 troops from eighteen wings—with full authority over the project. Work began that month; the channel was cut in the seventh month, water released in the eighth, shipping restored in the ninth, and all dikes finished by the eleventh, returning the river to its old bed. Details appear in the Treatise on Rivers and Canals. The emperor sent sacrifice to the River Lord and recalled Lu, who presented a map of the pacified river. Reading censors' praise of Toqto'a and Lu's service, the emperor promoted Lu, rewarded him, ordered Ouyang Xuan's River Pacified stele honoring Toqto'a while recording Lu, sent it to the History Office, and ennobled three generations of his ancestors.
13
尋拜中書左丞，從脫脫平徐州，脫脫既旋師，命魯追餘黨，分攻濠州，同緫兵官平章月可察兒督戰，魯誓師曰：「吾奉旨統八衛漢軍，頓兵于濠七日矣。 爾諸將同心協力，必以今日巳、午時取城池，然後食。 」魯上馬麾進，抵城下，忽頭眩下馬，且戒兵馬弗散，病愈亟，却藥不肯汗，竟卒于軍中，年五十七。 十三年五月壬午也。 月可察兒躬為治喪，選士護柩還高平，有旨賜交鈔五百錠以給葬事。 子稹。
Soon left chancellor, he followed Toqto'a against Xuzhou, then was told to hunt remnants and besiege Haozhou with Yue Kechuer. He swore to the troops: "I lead eight Han guard units and have sat before Haozhou seven days. You must take the city between si and wu today—then we eat. " He charged the wall, grew dizzy, dismounted, yet kept the army formed. He refused sweat-inducing medicine and died in camp at fifty-seven. This was renwu, the fifth month of the thirteenth year. Yue Kechuer buried him, sent an escort to Gaoping, and the court granted five hundred ingots for the funeral. His son was Zhen.
14
逯魯曾
Lu Luceng
15
逯魯曾字善止，修武人。 性剛介，通經術，中天曆二年進士第，授翰林國史院編修官，辟御史臺掾，掌機密。 監察御史劾中丞史顯夫簡傲，魯曾開實封於大夫前曰：「中丞素持重，不能與人周旋，御史以人情劾之，非公論。 」由是皆知其直。
Lu Luceng, style name Shanzhi, was from Xiuwu. Stern and upright, versed in classics, he passed the jinshi in Tiansli 2, became Hanlin history compiler, then confidential Censorate clerk. When a censor impeached Shi Xianfu for arrogance, Luceng opened the sealed report before the minister and said, "The vice censor is naturally reserved; to impeach him on personal grounds is not justice. " Everyone then knew how upright he was.
16
除太常博士。 武宗一廟，未立后主配享，集群臣廷議之。 魯曾抗言：「先朝以武宗皇后真哥無子，不立其主。 」時伯顏為右丞相，以為明宗之母亦乞列氏，可以配享。 徽政院傳太后旨，以文宗之母唐兀氏可以配享。 伯顏問魯曾曰：「先朝既以真哥皇后無子，不為立主，今所立者，明宗母乎？ 文宗母乎？ 」對曰：「真哥皇后在武宗朝，已膺玉冊，則為武宗皇后，明宗、文宗二母后，固為妾也。 今以無子之故，不為立主，以妾后為正宮，是為臣而廢先君之后，為子而追封先父之妾，於禮不可。 且燕王垂即位，追廢其母后，而立其生母為后，以配享先王，為萬世笑，豈可復蹈其失乎？ 」集賢大學士陳顥，素嫉魯曾，出曰：「唐太宗冊曹王明之母為后，是亦二后也，豈不可乎？ 」魯曾曰：「堯之母為帝嚳庶妃，堯立為帝，未聞冊以為后而配嚳。 皇上為大元天子，不法堯、舜，而法唐太宗邪？ 」衆服其議，而伯顏韙之，遂以真哥皇后配焉。
He became an erudite of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices. Wuzong's spirit temple lacked a principal consort for joint sacrifice, so ministers were summoned to debate the matter. Luceng objected: "The prior court refused Empress Zhenge because she bore no son. " Bayan, then right chancellor, argued that Mingzong's mother of the Yelü clan could share sacrifice. The palace bureau relayed the dowager's wish that Wenzong's Tangut mother be enshrined. Bayan asked Luceng, "Since Zhenge had no son and was not made principal, are we now to install Mingzong's mother? Or Wenzong's? " He replied, "Zhenge already held the jade book under Wuzong and was his empress; Mingzong's and Wenzong's mothers were concubines. To reject a childless empress yet elevate a concubine violates ritual: a subject abolishing his lord's wife, a son ennobling his father's concubine. Murong Chui once deposed his empress for his birth mother—a folly for the ages; why repeat it? " Chen Hao, who envied him, cited Tang Taizong ennobling Prince Cao's mother: "Were there not two empresses then?" " Luceng answered, "Yao's mother was Ku's concubine; Yao became emperor without making her Ku's sacrificial consort. You are Son of Heaven of Great Yuan—will you model Yao and Shun, or Tang Taizong? " The court agreed; Bayan assented; Zhenge was installed after all.
17
復拜監察御史，劾荅失海牙、阿吉剌太尉，鞏卜班右丞，兀突蠻刑部尚書，吉當普監察御史，哈剌完者、月魯不花院使，呂思誠郎中，皆黜之。 八人之中，惟思誠少過，亦變祖宗選法，餘皆伯顏之黨，朝廷肅然。
Reappointed investigating censor, he impeached Quesihaiya, Ajila, Gongbuban, Utuman, Jidangpu, Halawanzhe, Yuelubuhua, and Lü Sicheng, who were all removed. Of the eight only Sicheng's offense was minor, though he too had bent selection rules; the rest were Bayan's men and the court grew stern.
18
除樞密院都事，上言：「前伯顏專殺大臣，其黨利其妻女，巧誣以罪。 今大小官及諸人有罪，止坐其身，不得籍其妻女。 郯王為伯顏搆陷，妻女流離，當雪其無辜，給復子孫。 」從之。 除刑部員外郎，悉辨正橫罹伯顏所誣者。 遷宗正府郎中，出為遼陽行省左右司郎中，除僉山北道肅政廉訪司事，入為禮部郎中。
Made Bureau of Military Affairs supervisor, he wrote: "Bayan once killed ministers at will; his faction seized their families with false charges. " Henceforth offenders should answer only for themselves—wives and daughters must not be seized. Prince of Tan was framed and his family scattered; their innocence should be cleared and his line restored. " The court agreed. As vice minister of punishments he cleared all Bayan's victims. He became imperial clan director, Liaoyang secretariat director, Shanbei integrity participant, then rites director.
19
至正十二年，丞相脫脫討徐州賊，以官軍不習水土，募瀕海鹽丁為軍，乃超遷魯曾資善大夫、淮南宣慰使，領征討事，遣其募鹽丁五千人從征。 徐州平，繼使領所部軍討淮東，卒於軍。
In Zhizheng 12 Toqto'a fought Xuzhou rebels; finding regular troops ill-suited to the climate he raised coastal salt workers as an army and made Luceng pacification commissioner at grand master rank, sending five thousand salt recruits. After Xuzhou fell he led his men east along the Huai and died in service.
20
貢師泰
Gong Shitai
21
貢師泰字泰甫，寧國之宣城人。 父奎，以文學名家，延祐、至治間，官京師，為集賢直學士，卒，諡文靖。
Gong Shitai, style name Taifu, came from Xuancheng in Ningguo. His father Kui, a noted scholar, served at court in the Yanyou and Zhizhi eras as a Hall for Collecting Worthies academician and died with the posthumous title Wenjing.
22
師泰早肄業國子學為諸生。 泰定四年，釋褐出身，授從仕郎、太和州判官。 丁外艱，改徽州路歙縣丞，江浙行省辟為掾，尋以土著，自劾去。 大臣有以其名聞者，擢應奉翰林文字。 丁內艱，服闋，除紹興路緫管府推官，郡有疑獄，悉為詳讞而剖決之。
Shitai studied early at the National University. In Taiding 4 he entered service as attendant gentleman and Taihe assistant prefect. After his father's mourning he became She County assistant magistrate, then a Jiangzhe secretariat aide, but resigned as a local native. Ministers who heard of him made him Hanlin attendant for literary composition. After his mother's mourning he became Shaoxing judicial reviewer and decided every doubtful case in the prefecture.
23
山陰白洋港有大船飄近岸，史甲二十人，適取鹵海濱，見其無主，因取其篙櫓，而船中有二死人。 有徐乙者，怪其無物而有死人，稱為史等所劫。 史傭作富民高丙家，事遂連高。 史既誣服，高亦就逮。 師泰密詢之，則里中沈丁載物抵杭而回，漁者張網海中，因盜網中魚，為漁者所殺，史實未嘗殺人奪物，高亦弗知情，其冤皆白。
At Shanyin's Baiyang Harbor a large ship drifted ashore; twenty men named Shi, gathering salt on the beach, took its oars from the seemingly abandoned vessel, which held two corpses. Xu Yi, finding goods missing but bodies present, accused them of robbery. Shi worked for the rich man Gao Bing, so Gao was drawn in. After Shi confessed under torture Gao was seized too. Shitai learned privately that Shen Ding had shipped goods to Hangzhou; fishermen killed net-thieves at sea—Shi had murdered no one and Gao knew nothing; both were cleared.
24
游徼徐裕，以巡鹽為名，肆暴村落間。 一日，遇諸暨商，奪其所齎錢，撲殺之，投尸於水，走告縣曰：「我獲私鹽犯人，畏罪赴水死矣。 」官驗視，以有傷，疑之。 遂以疑獄釋。 師泰追詢覆按之，具得裕所以殺人狀，復俾待報。
Patrol inspector Xu Yu used salt inspection as a cover for village violence. One day he robbed and killed a Zhuji merchant, threw the body in the water, and reported, "A salt smuggler drowned himself in fear. " Officials found wounds and grew suspicious. The case was shelved as doubtful. Shitai reinvestigated, proved the murder, and held Yu for sentencing.
25
餘姚孫國賓，以求盜，獲姚甲造偽鈔，受賕而釋之，執高乙、魯丙赴有司，誣以同造偽。 高嘗為姚行用，實非自造，孫既舍姚，因加罪於高，而魯與孫有隙，故并連之，魯與高未嘗相識也。 師泰疑高等覆造不合，以孫詰之，辭屈而情見。 即釋魯而加高以本罪，姚遂處死，孫亦就法。 其於冤獄詳讞之明多類此。 以故郡民自以不冤，治行為諸郡第一。
In Yuyao, Sun Guobin caught Yao Jia counterfeiting, took a bribe to free him, then framed Gao Yi and Lu Bing as accomplices. Gao had served Yao but never counterfeited; Sun, sparing Yao, blamed Gao, and implicated Lu over an old feud though the two were strangers. Shitai doubted repeated forgery by Gao; questioning Sun exposed the fraud. He freed Lu, punished Gao only for his real offense, executed Yao, and punished Sun. His handling of unjust cases was often like this. The prefecture's people felt fairly treated and his governance ranked first in the circuit.
26
考滿，復入翰林為應奉，預修后妃、功臣列傳，事畢，遷宣文閣授經郎，歷翰林待制、國子司業，擢禮部郎中，再遷吏部，拜監察御史。 自世祖以後，省臺之職，南人斥不用，及是，始復舊制，於是南士復得居省臺，自師泰始，時論以為得人。
After his term he returned to Hanlin, helped compile empress and minister biographies, became Promoting Culture lecturer, Hanlin attendant, National University vice director, rites and personnel director, then investigating censor. Since Shizu southerners had been barred from secretariat and censor posts; now the old rule returned and Shitai was the first southern appointee—hailed as a good choice.
27
至正十四年，除吏部侍郎。 時江淮兵起，京師食不足，師泰奉命和糴于浙右，得糧百萬石，以給京師。 遷兵部侍郎。 朝廷以京師至上都，驛戶凋弊，命師泰巡視整飭之。 至則歷究其病原，驗其富貧，而均其徭役，數十郡之民，賴以稍蘇。 豪貴以其不利於己，深嫉之，然莫能有所中傷也。 會朝廷欲仍和糴浙西，因除師泰都水庸田使。
In Zhizheng 14 he became vice minister of personnel. With Jiang-Huai in turmoil and the capital short of grain, he harmoniously purchased a million piculs in western Zhejiang for the capital. He was made vice minister of war. The court ordered him to reform relay households between the capital and Shangdu. He traced abuses, assessed wealth, equalized corvée, and relieved dozens of prefectures. Great families resented him but could not touch him. When the court resumed harmonized purchase in western Zhejiang he was named director of water control and public fields.
28
十五年，庸田司罷，擢江西廉訪副使，未行，遷福建廉訪使，居亡何，除禮部尚書。 時平江缺守，廷議難其人，師泰又以選為平江路緫管。 其年冬，甫視事，張士誠自高郵率衆渡江，直抵城下，攻圍甚急。 明年春，守將弗能支，斬關遁去，師泰領義兵出戰，力不敵，亦懷印綬棄城遁，匿海濱者久之。
In the fifteenth year, after the public-fields office closed, he was named Jiangxi deputy commissioner, then Fujian commissioner, and soon minister of rites. Pingjiang lacked a prefect; unable to find another man, the court named Shitai prefect. That winter, newly in office, Zhang Shicheng crossed from Gaoyou and besieged the city fiercely. Next spring the garrison fled; Shitai led militia out, was defeated, abandoned the city with his seal, and hid long on the coast.
29
士誠既納降，江浙行省丞相達識帖睦邇，以便宜授師泰兩浙都轉運鹽使。 至則剔其積蠹，通其利源，大課以集，國用資之。 丞相復承制除師泰江浙行省參知政事。
After Shicheng surrendered, Jiangzhe chancellor Tash Temür made him Two-Zhe salt transport director by expedient authority. He purged corruption, opened revenue, and greatly swelled state receipts. The chancellor then made him Jiangzhe secretariat participant.
30
二十年，朝廷除戶部尚書，俾分部閩中，以閩鹽易糧，由海道轉運給京師，凡為糧數十萬石，朝廷賴焉。 二十二年，召為祕書卿，行至杭之海寧，得疾而卒。
In the twentieth year he became revenue minister, stationed in Fujian to trade salt for grain and ship hundreds of thousands of piculs to the capital by sea. In the twenty-second year he was summoned as secretary director but died of illness at Haining near Hangzhou.
31
師泰性倜儻，狀貌偉然，既以文字知名，而於政事尤長，所至績效輒暴著。 尤喜接引後進，士之賢，不問識不識，即加推轂，以故士譽翕然咸歸之。 有詩文若干卷行于世。
Unconventional and imposing, famed for letters, he excelled in government and left striking results everywhere. He eagerly promoted young talent, known or not, and scholars flocked to his reputation. His collected writings circulate in several scrolls.
32
周伯琦
Zhou Boqi
33
周伯琦字伯溫，饒州人。 父應極，至大間，仁宗為皇太子，召見，獻皇元頌，為言于武宗，以為翰林待制。 後為皇太子說書，日侍英邸。 仁宗即位，遷集賢待制，終池州路同知緫管府事。 伯琦自幼從宦，游京師，入國學，為上舍生，積分及高等。 去，以蔭授將仕郎、南海縣主簿，三轉為翰林修撰。
Zhou Boqi, style name Bowen, was from Raozhou. His father Yingji, in the Zhida era when Renzong was heir apparent, presented the Ode on the Primordial Yuan and, through Wuzong, became Hanlin attendant. He later lectured the crown prince daily in the heir's quarters. When Renzong reigned he rose to Hanlin attendant and ended as Chizhou associate prefect. Boqi grew up in official life, studied at the National University as a top dormitory student, and earned the highest credits. By yin privilege he became Nanhai chief clerk, then after three steps Hanlin compiler.
34
至正元年，改奎章閣為宣文閣、藝文監為崇文監，伯琦為宣文閣授經郎，教戚里大臣子弟，每進講，輒稱旨，且日被顧問。 帝以伯琦工書法，命篆「宣文閣寶」，仍題扁宣文閣； 及摹王羲之所書蘭亭序、智永所書千文，刻石閣中。 自是累轉官，皆宣文、崇文之間，而眷遇益隆矣。 帝嘗呼其字伯溫而不名。 會御史奏風憲宜用近臣，特命僉廣東廉訪司事。 八年，召入為翰林待制，預修后妃、功臣列傳，累陞直學士。
In Zhizheng 1 the Kuizhang Pavilion became the Hall of Promoting Culture; Boqi lectured imperial kin and ministers' sons, pleased the emperor each session, and was consulted daily. The emperor, valuing his calligraphy, had him carve the Promoting Culture seal and write the hall plaque; and copy the Orchid Pavilion Preface and Thousand-Character Essay in stone for the hall. Thereafter his posts stayed within those bureaus while favor deepened. The emperor called him Bowen rather than by personal name. When censors urged near ministers for integrity posts, he was named Guangdong commissioner. In the eighth year he returned to Hanlin, helped compile empress and minister biographies, and rose to academician.
35
十二年，有旨令南士皆得居省臺。 除伯琦兵部侍郎，遂與貢師泰同擢監察御史。 兩人皆南士之望，一時榮之。 時御史大夫也先帖木兒以大軍南討，而失律喪師，陝西行臺監察御史劉希曾等十人共劾奏之。 伯琦乃劾希曾等越分干譽，希曾等皆坐左遷，補郡判官，由是不為公論所與。
In the twelfth year an edict restored southerners to secretariat and censor posts. Boqi became war vice minister and, with Gong Shitai, investigating censor. Both were leading southern scholars—an honor of the age. Censor-in-chief Yexian Timur led a southern campaign but lost his army; ten Shaanxi censors including Liu Xizeng impeached him. Boqi impeached them for overstepping; they were demoted to assistant prefects, and public opinion turned against him.
36
十三年，遷崇文太監，兼經筵官，代祀天妃。 丁內艱。 十四年，起復為江東肅政廉訪使。 長槍鎖南班陷寧國，伯琦與僚佐倉皇出見之，尋遁走至杭州除兵部尚書，未行，改浙西肅政廉訪使。 江南行臺監察御史余觀，糾言伯琦失陷寧國，宜正其罪。
In the thirteenth year he became Honoring Culture superintendent and Classics Colloquium officer and sacrificed to the sea goddess. He mourned his mother. In the fourteenth year he left mourning as Jiangdong surveillance commissioner. When Suonan's Long Spear band took Ningguo, Boqi met them, fled to Hangzhou, was named war minister, then Zhexi commissioner before taking office. Jiangnan censor Yu Guan demanded punishment for losing Ningguo.
37
十七年，江浙行省丞相達識帖睦爾承制假伯琦參知政事，招諭平江張士誠。
In the seventeenth year Tash Temur provisionally made Boqi participant to treat with Zhang Shicheng at Pingjiang.
38
士誠既降，江南行臺監察御史亦辯釋伯琦罪，除同知太常禮儀院事，士誠留之，未行，拜資政大夫、江浙行省左丞。 於是留平江者十餘年。 士誠既滅，伯琦乃得歸鄱陽，尋卒。
After surrender censors cleared him; he was named associate sacrifices director but Shicheng detained him and then made him Jiangzhe left chancellor. He remained at Pingjiang more than ten years. When Shicheng fell he returned to Poyang and soon died.
39
伯琦儀觀溫雅，粹然如玉，雖遭時多艱，而善於自保。 博學工文章，而尤以篆、隸、真、草擅名當時。 嘗著六書正訛、說文字原二書，又有詩文稿若干卷。
Mild, elegant, and jade-pure, he survived troubled times by prudent self-preservation. Learned and literary, he was above all famed for seal, clerical, regular, and cursive calligraphy. He wrote Correcting Errors in the Six Scripts and Origins of the Explanation of Characters, besides collected poems and essays.
40
吳當字伯尚，澄之孫也。 當幼承祖訓，以穎悟篤實稱。 長精通經史百家言，侍其祖至京，補國子生。 久之，澄既捐館，四方學子從澄遊者，悉就當卒業焉。
Wu Dang, style name Boshang, was the grandson of Wu Cheng. As a boy he followed his grandfather's teaching and was known for quick wit and steadfast character. Grown, he mastered classics, histories, and philosophers, accompanied his grandfather to court, and entered the National University. After Cheng died, students from across the realm who had studied under him finished their training with Dang.
41
至正五年，以父文蔭，授萬億四庫照磨，未上，用薦者改國子助教。 勤講解，嚴肄習，諸生皆樂從之。 會詔修遼、金、宋三史，當預編纂。 書成，除翰林修撰。 七年，遷國子博士。 明年，陞監丞。 十年，陞司業。 明年，遷翰林待制。 又明年，改禮部員外郎。 十三年，擢監察御史，尋復為國子司業。 明年，遷禮部郎中。 又明年，除翰林直學士。
In Zhizheng 5 he was named verifier of the Four Repositories by his father's yin privilege, then made National University assistant instructor on recommendation before taking the first post. He lectured carefully and disciplined study strictly; students gladly followed him. When the Liao, Jin, and Song histories were ordered compiled, Dang joined the project. After completion he became Hanlin compiler. In the seventh year he became National University erudite. The following year he rose to supervisor. In the tenth year he became vice director. The next year he was made Hanlin attendant. Another year on he became vice minister of rites. In the thirteenth year he was investigating censor, then again National University vice director. The next year he became rites director. Another year on he was Hanlin academician.
42
時江南兵起且五年，大臣有薦當世居江西，習知江西民俗，且其才可任政事者，詔特授江西肅政廉訪使，偕江西行省參政火你赤、兵部尚書黃昭，招捕江西諸郡，便宜行事。 當以朝廷兵力不給，既受命至江南，即召募民兵，由浙入閩。 至江西境建昌界，招安新城孫塔，擒殄李三。 道路既通，乃進攻南豐，渠凶鄭天瑞遁，鄭原自刎死。 十六年，調檢校章迪率本部兵，與黃昭夾攻撫州，勦殺首寇胡志學，進兵復崇仁、宜黃。 於是建、撫兩郡悉定。
After five years of southern warfare ministers urged that Dang, a Jiangxi native who knew local ways, could govern; he was specially named Jiangxi surveillance commissioner with Huonizhi and Huang Zhao to pacify Jiangxi by expedient authority. Finding court armies too weak, he reached the south, raised militia, and entered Fujian from Zhejiang. At Jianchang in Jiangxi he pacified Sun Ta of Xincheng and destroyed Li San. Roads secured, he attacked Nanfeng; ringleader Zheng Tianrui fled and Zheng Yuan committed suicide. In the sixteenth year he sent Inspector Zhang Di with Huang Zhao to crush Fuzhou bandit Hu Zhixue and recover Chongren and Yihuang. Jian and Fu circuits were then fully pacified.
43
是時，參知政事朶歹緫兵撫、建，積年無功。 因忌當屢捷，功在己上，又以為南人不宜緫兵，則搆為飛語，謂當與黃昭皆與寇通。 有旨解二人兵柄，除當撫州路緫管，昭臨江路緫管，並供億平章火你赤軍。 火你赤殺當從事官范淳及章迪，將士皆憤怒不平，當諭之曰：「上命不可違也。 」而火你赤又上章言：「二人者，難任牧民。 」尋有旨當與昭皆罷緫管，除名。
Participant Duodai had commanded in Fu and Jian for years without result. Jealous of Dang's victories and believing southerners should not command armies, Duodai slandered Dang and Huang Zhao as colluding with rebels. An edict stripped their commands; Dang became Fuzhou prefect and Zhao Linjiang prefect, both provisioning Huonizhi's army. Huonizhi killed staff officers Fan Chun and Zhang Di; troops raged, but Dang said, "We cannot disobey the throne. " Huonizhi then memorialized that both were unfit to govern the people. 十八年，火你赤自瑞州還龍興，當、昭皆隨軍不敢去。 先是，當與昭平賊功狀，自廣東由海道未達京師，而朶歹、火你赤等公牘乃先至，故朝廷責當、昭，皆左遷。 及得當、昭功狀，乃始知其誣，詔拜當中奉大夫、江西行省參知政事，昭湖廣行省參知政事。 命未下，而陳友諒已陷江西諸郡。 火你赤棄城遁，當乃戴黃冠，著道士服，杜門不出，日以著書為事。 友諒遣人辟之，當卧床不食，以死自誓，乃舁床載之舟，送江州，拘留一年，終不為屈。 遂隱居廬陵吉水之谷坪。 逾年，以疾卒，年六十五。 所著書，有周禮纂言及學言稿。
In the eighteenth year Huonizhi returned from Ruizhou to Longxing; Dang and Zhao followed, afraid to leave. Their victory reports sent by sea from Guangdong had not reached the capital before Duodai and Huonizhi's documents arrived, so the court blamed and demoted them. When their reports finally arrived the court saw the slander; Dang was named Jiangxi participant and Zhao Huguang participant. Before the orders issued Chen Youliang overran Jiangxi. Huonizhi fled; Dang donned Taoist dress, shut his door, and wrote. Youliang tried to recruit him; Dang fasted in bed, was carried to Jiangzhou and held a year, yet never submitted. He retired to Guping in Jishui, Jianling. A year later he died of illness at sixty-five. His works include Expository Notes on the Rites of Zhou and Drafts of Learning.
