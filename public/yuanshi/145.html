亦憐真班
Yilianzhenban
2
亦憐真班，西夏人。 父俺伯，以忠勤事世祖，為知樞密院事。 亦憐真班性剛正，動有禮法。 仁宗召見，令入宿衛。 延祐六年，超拜翰林侍講學士、中奉大夫。 至治二年，調同知通政院事，擢虎符唐兀親軍都指揮使。 泰定初，遷資善大夫、典瑞院使。 天歷二年，以選為太子家令，尋升資政大夫、同知樞密院事，擢侍御史，仍兼指揮使。 至順初，拜翰林學士承旨、榮祿大夫，遷功德使，指揮使如故。 尋出為陝西行省平章政事，未行，復為翰林學士承旨。 元統、至元之間，伯顏為丞相，專權擅政，嫉其論事不阿，出為江南行台御史大夫。 尋殺其子答裡麻，而謫置海南。 及伯顏敗，乃得召還朝。 至正六年，拜光祿大夫、御史大夫，盡遷中外廉能之官置諸風憲，一時號稱得人。 遷宣政院使，出為甘肅行省平章政事，設法弭西羌之寇，民賴以安，立石頌之。 召還，為銀青榮祿大夫、知樞密院事，提調太醫院，尋加金紫光祿大夫，復為御史大夫、知經筵事，兼宣忠斡羅思扈衛親軍指揮使。 嘗奏言：「風俗人心日趨於薄，請禁故吏不許彈劾所事官長。」
Yilianzhenban was a native of Western Xia. His father Anbo served Emperor Shizu with loyal diligence and rose to be Director of the Bureau of Military Affairs. Yilianzhenban was stern and upright by nature, and in all his conduct he upheld ritual propriety and the law. Emperor Renzong summoned him to an audience and appointed him to the palace guard. In the sixth year of the Yanyou era (1319), he was promoted ahead of schedule to Hanlin Attendant Lecturer and Senior Grand Master of the Palace. In the second year of the Zhizhi era (1322), he was transferred to Vice Director of the Bureau of Transmission and promoted to Commander-in-Chief of the Tiger-Tally Tangut Imperial Guard. At the opening of the Taiding reign, he was appointed Grand Master for the Heir Apparent and Commissioner of the Court of Imperial Regalia. In the second year of the Tianli era (1329), he was selected as Household Steward of the Heir Apparent. Soon afterward he was promoted to Grand Master of Counsel and Vice Director of the Bureau of Military Affairs, made Attending Censor, and retained his command as well. At the start of the Zhishun era, he was appointed Hanlin Academician-in-Ordinary and Grand Master for Glorious Happiness, then transferred to Commissioner of Meritorious Achievements while keeping his military command. He was soon posted as Pacification Commissioner of the Shaanxi Branch Secretariat, but before he could take up the post he was recalled to serve again as Hanlin Academician-in-Ordinary. During the Yuantong and Zhiyuan periods, Chancellor Bayan monopolized power and ruled arbitrarily. He resented Yilianzhenban's refusal to flatter him in debate and had him transferred to Grand Censor of the Jiangnan Branch Surveillance Commission. Bayan soon had Yilianzhenban's son Dalima put to death and banished Yilianzhenban himself to Hainan. After Bayan's downfall, he was summoned back to the capital. In the sixth year of the Zhizheng era (1346), he was appointed Grand Master for Splendid Happiness and Censor-in-Chief. He filled the surveillance offices with every upright and capable official he could find at home and abroad, and for a time the appointments were hailed as having brought in the right men. He was transferred to Commissioner of the Xuanzheng Court and then posted as Pacification Commissioner of the Gansu Branch Secretariat. There he devised measures to suppress raids by the Western Qiang, and the people lived in peace because of him; a commemorative stone was raised in his honor. Recalled to court, he was made Silver-Blue Grand Master for Glorious Happiness and Director of the Bureau of Military Affairs, with charge over the Imperial Medical Academy. Soon he was advanced to Gold-and-Purple Grand Master for Splendid Happiness, restored as Censor-in-Chief and Director of the Classics Colloquium, and concurrently appointed Commander of the Xuanzhong Oros Guard Imperial Army. He once submitted a memorial: "Popular morals and men's hearts grow more shallow by the day. I ask that former subordinates be forbidden to impeach the officials they once served under."
3
太師馬扎兒台與子丞相脫脫既謫居在外，時相欲傾之，嗾人告變，且扳台臣同上奏。 亦憐真班曰：「凡為相者，孰無閑退之日？ 況脫脫父子在官無大咎過，奈何迫之於險？ 」終不從。 經筵進講必詳必慎，故每讀譯文，必被嘉納。 監察御史劾奏時相，帝不聽，亦憐真班反覆論奏不已，由是忤上意，出為江浙行省平章政事，遷拜湖廣行省左丞相。 復召知樞密院事。 十一年，潁、亳兵起，朝廷命將出師，多失律致敗，數進言於時相，不見聽，復出為江浙行省左丞相。 十二年，移江西行省左丞相。 於是妖寇由蘄、黃陷饒州，饒之屬邑安仁與龍興相接境，其民皆相挺為亂。 亦憐真班道出安仁，因駐兵招之，來者厚加賞賚，不從者命子哈藍朵兒只與江西左丞火你赤等乘高縱火攻散之。 餘乾久為盜區，亦聞風順服。 先是，江西行省平章政事道童以寬容為政，軍民懈馳。 亦憐真班既至，風采一新，威聲大振，所在群盜咸謀歸款矣。 十四年八月，以疾卒於官，所部為之喪氣。 事聞，贈推忠佐運正憲秉義同德功臣，追封齊王，謚忠獻。
Grand Preceptor Ma Zh'iertai and his son Chancellor Toghto had already been demoted and were living in exile. The reigning chancellor sought to destroy them, incited men to accuse them of sedition, and pressed other court ministers to join in a joint memorial. Yilianzhenban said, "Whoever has served as chancellor has not had his day of retirement? Besides, Toghto and his father committed no grave offense in office. Why drive them into such danger? " In the end the court would not heed him. Whenever he lectured at the Classics Colloquium he was thorough and careful, and each time he read the translated text the emperor commended him warmly. When a supervising censor impeached the reigning chancellor, the emperor refused to act. Yilianzhenban argued the matter again and again in memorial after memorial. By this he fell from imperial favor and was posted as Pacification Commissioner of the Jiang-Zhe Branch Secretariat, then promoted to Left Chancellor of the Huguang Branch Secretariat. He was soon recalled to serve again as Director of the Bureau of Military Affairs. In the eleventh year of Zhizheng (1351), rebellion broke out in Ying and Bo prefectures. The court sent generals against the rebels, but many lost discipline and were defeated. Yilianzhenban repeatedly urged counsel on the chancellor in power, yet none of it was heeded, and he was again transferred out as Left Chancellor of the Jiang-Zhe Branch Secretariat. In the twelfth year (1352) he was transferred to Left Chancellor of the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat. Rebel forces then advanced from Qi and Huang prefectures and took Raozhou. Anren, a county under Raozhou that bordered Longxing, saw its people rise together in revolt. Yilianzhenban's route took him through Anren, where he halted his troops to offer surrender. Those who came in were richly rewarded; those who refused he ordered his son Qarandorji, together with the Jiangxi Left Chancellor Huonichi and others, to take the high ground, set fires, and break up their resistance. Yugan, long a haunt of bandits, likewise submitted at the news of his approach. Earlier, Pacification Commissioner Daotong of the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat had governed with excessive leniency, and both soldiers and civilians had grown slack. Once Yilianzhenban arrived, discipline was restored and his authority resounded far and wide; bandit bands everywhere began to plan their surrender. In the eighth month of the fourteenth year (1354) he died of illness in office, and the regions under his command were cast into despair. When word reached the throne, he was posthumously granted the title Meritorious Subject Who Pushed Loyalty, Assisted Fortune, Upheld Rectitude, Held to Righteousness, and Shared in Virtue; enfeoffed posthumously as Prince of Qi; and given the posthumous name Zhongxian, "Loyal and Offering."

子九人：長答裡麻； 次普達失理，翰林學士承旨、知制誥兼修國史； 桑哥八剌，同知青海宣慰司事； 哈藍朵兒只，宣政院使； 桑哥答思，嶺北行省平章； 沙嘉室理，嶺北行省參政； 易納室理，大宗正也可扎魯火赤； 馬的室理，僉書樞密院事； 馬剌室理，內八府宰相。
He had nine sons. The eldest was Dalima; the second, Pudashili, Hanlin Academician-in-Ordinary, Controller of Imperial Documents, and Compiler of the National History; Sangge Bala, Vice Commissioner of the Qinghai Pacification Commission; Qarandorji, Commissioner of the Xuanzheng Court; Sangge Das, Pacification Commissioner of the Lingbei Branch Secretariat; Shajia Shili, Vice Chancellor of the Lingbei Branch Secretariat; Yina Shili, Yeke Jarquchi of the Grand Council for the Imperial Clan; Madi Shili, Secretary of the Bureau of Military Affairs; and Mala Shili, Chancellor of the Inner Eight Offices.

○廉惠山海牙
○ Lianhuishanhaiya
6
廉惠山海牙，字公亮，布魯海牙之孫，希憲之從子也。 父阿魯渾海牙，廣德路達魯花赤。 惠山海牙幼孤，言及父，輒泣下。 獨養母而家日不給，垢衣糲食，不以為恥。 母喪，哀毀逾禮，負喪渡江而風濤作，舟人以神龍忌屍為言，即仰天大呼曰：「吾將附母于先人，神奈何厄我也！ 」風遂止。 年弱冠，大臣欲俾入宿衛。 辭曰：「吾大父事世祖，以通經號廉孟子。 今方設科取士，願讀書以科第進。 」乃入國學積分。 至治元年，登進士第，授承事郎、同知順州事。 有弓匠提舉馬都剌者，怙勢奪州民田，同列畏之。 惠山海牙至，即治其事。 在官期年，用薦者召入史館，預修英宗、仁宗《實錄》，尋拜監察御史。 時中書省有大臣貪猥狼籍，即抗章劾之，語同列曰：「儻以言責獲罪，吾之職也。 」既又劾奏明裡董阿不當攝祭太廟。 遷都水監，疏會通河，堤灤、漆二水，又修京東閘。 歷秘書丞、會福總管府治中，上疏言二月迎佛費財蠹俗，時論韙之。 出僉淮東廉訪司事，遷江浙行省左右司員外郎，既而歷僉河東、河南、江西廉訪司事，升江南行御史臺經歷。 時山東鹽法大壞，以選除都轉運使，曾未期月，用課最，賞賚金幣、上尊。 至正三年初，行郊禮，召拜侍儀使。 明年，預修遼、金、宋三史，遷崇文太監。 自是累遷為河南行省右丞。 時有詔發民治決河，遍騷屬郡，亟以不便上言，而時宰不用。 遷湖廣行省右丞，以武昌失守連坐，既而事白，遷江西行省右丞。 時所隸郡縣多陷於賊，乃與平章政事、司徒道童協謀殫力，以定守禦招捕之策，就除本道廉訪使。 未幾，江西省治亦陷，惠山海牙遁往福建。 久之，除僉江浙行樞密院事，改拜福建行省右丞，以兵鎮延平、邵武，境內以寧。 居歲餘，奉詔還治省事，總備禦事，且督賦稅由海道供京師，朝廷賴焉。 遷行宣政院使。 明年，拜翰林學士承旨、知制誥兼修國史。 卒，年七十有一。
Lianhuishanhaiya, whose style was Gongliang, was the grandson of Buluhaia and a nephew of Xian. His father Aruqun Haiya served as Darughachi of Guangde Circuit. Huishanhaiya lost his father while still young, and whenever he spoke of him tears would fall. He supported his mother alone while the household grew poorer by the day, yet he wore threadbare clothes and ate coarse food without shame. When his mother died, his grief exceeded what ritual required. As he carried her coffin across the river, a storm rose. The boatmen said the river dragon forbade corpses on the water. He looked up to heaven and cried, "I am taking my mother to lie beside our forebears — how can the spirits thwart me like this! " The wind fell still at once. Before he had come of age, senior ministers wished to place him in the palace guard. He declined, saying, "My great-grandfather served Emperor Shizu and, for his mastery of the classics, was known as Master Lian the Confucian. The civil service examinations have now been restored. I wish to study and advance by earning a degree. " He entered the National University and worked through its curriculum. In the first year of the Zhizhi era (1321) he passed the jinshi examinations and was appointed Gentleman for Managing Affairs and Vice Prefect of Shunzhou. A bowyers' intendant named Madula used his influence to seize land from the people of the prefecture, and his colleagues were afraid of him. As soon as Huishanhaiya took office, he prosecuted the case. After a year in office he was recommended into the History Office to help compile the Veritable Records of Emperors Yingzong and Renzong, and soon afterward was appointed Supervising Censor. A senior minister at the Central Secretariat was notoriously greedy and corrupt. Huishanhaiya at once submitted a formal impeachment, telling his colleagues, "If I am punished for speaking as a censor should, that is the risk of my office. " He soon impeached Mingli Dong'a as well, charging that he should not have conducted the proxy sacrifice at the Imperial Ancestral Temple. He was transferred to the Directorate of Waterways, where he dredged the Huitong Canal, built embankments on the Luan and Qi rivers, and repaired the Jingdong Sluice. He served as Secretary of the Palace Library and Administrative Assistant of the Huifu Directorate, and submitted a memorial arguing that the February Buddha-welcoming ceremony wasted public funds and corrupted popular morals. Contemporary opinion sided with him. He was posted as an associate of the Huaidong Surveillance Commission, then promoted to Outside Director in the left and right secretariats of the Jiang-Zhe Branch Secretariat. He later served as associate in the Hedong, Henan, and Jiangxi surveillance commissions before rising to Administrator of the Jiangnan Branch Censorate. The salt monopoly in Shandong was then in ruins. Selected for the post of Salt Transport Commissioner, he restored order in less than a month and, for achieving the highest revenue yield, was rewarded with gold coins and imperial wine. Early in the third year of the Zhizheng era (1343), when the suburban sacrifices were performed, he was summoned and appointed Master of Ceremonies. The following year he took part in compiling the histories of the Liao, Jin, and Song dynasties and was promoted to Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices. Thereafter he rose by stages to Right Chancellor of the Henan Branch Secretariat. An edict then went out to conscript the people for Yellow River flood works, throwing every subordinate prefecture into turmoil. He urgently memorialized that the policy was unworkable, but the chancellor in power ignored him. He was transferred to Right Chancellor of the Huguang Branch Secretariat and was punished by association when Wuchang fell. After the matter was cleared, he was appointed Right Chancellor of the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat. Many of the prefectures and counties under his jurisdiction had fallen to rebels. He joined Pacification Commissioner and Situ Daotong in concerted planning, devoting all their strength to devising policies for defense and for pacification, and was appointed Surveillance Commissioner of the circuit on the spot. Before long the Jiangxi provincial capital fell as well, and Huishanhaiya fled to Fujian. After some time he was appointed Associate of the Jiang-Zhe Branch Bureau of Military Affairs, then made Right Chancellor of the Fujian Branch Secretariat. With troops he secured Yanping and Shaowu, and peace returned within his jurisdiction. After more than a year he was ordered back to manage provincial affairs, took overall charge of defense, and supervised the shipment of taxes by sea to supply the capital. The court came to rely on him. He was promoted to Acting Commissioner of the Xuanzheng Court. The following year he was appointed Hanlin Academician-in-Ordinary, Controller of Imperial Documents, and Compiler of the National History. He died at the age of seventy-one.

○月魯不花
○ Yuelubuhua
8
月魯不花，字彥明，蒙古遜都思氏。 生而容貌魁偉，咸以令器期之。 未冠，父脫帖穆耳以千戶職戍越，因受業於韓性先生，為文下筆立就，粲然成章。 就試江浙鄉闈，中其選，居右榜第一。 方揭曉，試官夢月中有花象，已而果符其名，人以為異。 遂登元統元年進士第，授將仕郎、台州路錄事司達魯花赤。 縣未有學，乃首建孔子廟，既又延儒士為之師，以教後進。 丁外艱。 至正元年，朝廷立行都水監，以選為其監經歷。 尋擢廣東廉訪司經歷。 會廷議將治河決，以行都水監丞召之，比至，改集賢待制，除吏部員外郎。 奉命至江浙糴粟二十四萬石，至則第戶產之高下，以為糴之多寡，不擾而事集。 既而軍餉不給，又奉命出糴於江浙，召父老諭曰：「今天子宵衣旰食，惟恐澤不下民而民不得其所耳，然奈盜賊何。 夫討賊者必先糧餉，以我不汝擾，故命我復來，蓋討賊即所以安民耳。 父老其謂何？ 」眾咸應曰：「公言是也。 」不逾月，糧事以畢。 丁母憂，中書遣賻且起復，不應。 未幾，太師、右丞相脫脫南征，辟從軍事，督饋餉，饋餉用舒。 升吏部郎中，尋拜監察御史。 首上疏言：「郊廟禮甚缺，天子宜躬祀南郊，殷祭太室。 」繼又上疏言：「皇太子天下之本，當簡老成重臣為輔導，以成其德。 」帝皆嘉納之。 升吏部侍郎，銓選於江浙，時稱其公允。 適朝廷有建議欲於河間、長蘆置局造海船三百艘者，月魯不花即為書具言其非便。 言入中書，忤議者，遷工部侍郎。 後分部彰德，道過河間，民遮擁拜謝曰：「微公言，吾民其斃矣。」
Yuelubuhua, whose style was Yanming, belonged to the Mongol Xundusi clan. From childhood he was tall and imposing in bearing, and everyone expected great things of him. Before he came of age, his father Tuotiemuer was posted to garrison Yue as a chiliarch. He studied under Master Han Xing, and whenever he wrote, the essay flowed complete from his brush in polished paragraphs. He sat for the Jiang-Zhe provincial examination, passed, and placed first on the Mongolian list. When the results were posted, one examiner had dreamed of flowers and an elephant in the moon—omens that matched Yuelubuhua's name—and people took it as a marvel. He passed the jinshi examinations in the first year of the Yuantong era (1333) and was appointed Gentleman for Initial Appointment and Darughachi of the Taizhou Circuit Records Office. The county had no school, so he first built a Temple of Confucius and then invited Confucian scholars to teach the younger generation. He entered mourning upon his father's death. In the first year of the Zhizheng era (1341), when the court established the Branch Directorate of Waterways, he was selected as its Administrator. He was soon promoted to Administrator of the Guangdong Surveillance Commission. When the court debated repairing the Yellow River breach, he was summoned as Vice Director of the Branch Directorate of Waterways. By the time he arrived he had been made Attendant Gentleman of the Hall of Gathered Worthies and appointed Outside Director of the Ministry of Personnel. Ordered to Jiang-Zhe to purchase 240,000 shi of grain, he ranked households by wealth to set each family's quota and completed the levy without disturbing the people. Soon army provisions ran short and he was again ordered to purchase grain in Jiang-Zhe. He summoned the village elders and said, "The Son of Heaven rises before dawn and eats after dark, fearing only that his grace does not reach the people and that they cannot live in peace—yet what can be done about the bandits? To fight bandits one must first secure provisions. Because I did not trouble you harshly before, I have been sent back. Suppressing bandits is precisely how the people are to be kept safe. What do you elders say to that? " The crowd answered as one, "Your Excellency speaks truly. " In less than a month the grain levy was complete. When his mother died he entered mourning. The Central Secretariat sent funeral gifts and ordered him back to office, but he refused. Before long Grand Preceptor and Right Chancellor Toghto marched south. Yuelubuhua was recruited for the campaign, supervised provisions, and kept the supply lines flowing smoothly. He was promoted to Director in the Ministry of Personnel and soon appointed Supervising Censor. His first memorial read, "The suburban and temple rites are gravely neglected. The Son of Heaven should personally sacrifice at the Southern Suburb and perform the Yin sacrifice in the Grand Chamber. " He soon submitted another memorial: "The heir apparent is the foundation of the realm. Seasoned and weighty ministers should be chosen to guide him and complete his moral formation. " The emperor commended and accepted both memorials. Promoted to Vice Director of the Ministry of Personnel, he conducted civil appointments in Jiang-Zhe and was widely praised for his fairness. When the court proposed establishing shipyards at Hejian and Changlu to build three hundred seagoing vessels, Yuelubuhua wrote a detailed memorial explaining why the plan was unwise. His memorial reached the Central Secretariat and offended the plan's advocates, and he was transferred to Vice Director of the Ministry of Works. Later, while on circuit duty at Zhangde, he passed through Hejian. The people crowded around him, bowed, and said, "But for your memorial, our people would have been ruined."

會方重選守令，以保定密邇京畿，除保定路達魯花赤。 陛辭，詔諭諄切。 保定歲輸糧數十萬石於新鄉，苦弗便。 月魯不花請輸京倉以便之。 俄除吏部尚書。 保定父老百數詣闕，言乞留監郡以撫吾民，遂以尚書仍知郡事。 會賊北渡河，日修城浚濠為戰守具。 廷議發五省八衛軍出戍外鎮，月魯不花疏願留其兵護本郡，遂兼統黑軍數千人及團結西山八十二寨民義軍，勢大張。 賊再侵境，皆不利，遁去。 升中奉大夫，錫上尊四、馬百匹，僚佐增秩有差，別降宣敕俾賞有功者。 召還為詳定使。 保定民不忍其去，繪像以祀之。 去保定一月而城陷矣。 朝廷以月魯不花夙負民望，令入城招諭之，抵城，賊堅壁不出，民多竊出謁拜者。 改大都路達魯花赤。 有執政以故中書令耶律楚材先塋地冒奏與蕃僧為業者，月魯不花格之，卒弗與。 轉吏部尚書。 會劇賊程思忠據永平，其佐雷帖木兒不花偽降，事覺被擒，殺之，思忠壁守遂益堅。 詔令月魯不花招撫之，眾悉難其行，月魯不花毅然曰：「臣死君命，分也，奈何先計禍福哉！ 」竟入城諭賊，賊皆感泣羅拜納降。
When the court renewed its emphasis on choosing capable prefects and magistrates, Yuelubuhua was appointed Darughachi of Baoding Circuit because Baoding lay close to the capital region. At his farewell audience the emperor gave him earnest and detailed instructions. Baoding was required each year to ship several hundred thousand shi of grain to Xinxiang, a burden that caused great hardship. Yuelubuhua petitioned that the grain be delivered instead to the capital granaries to ease the burden. He was soon appointed Director of the Ministry of Personnel. Several hundred elders of Baoding went to the capital and begged that he be kept to govern and comfort their people. He was therefore allowed to remain as Director of Personnel while continuing to manage the prefecture. When rebels crossed the Yellow River from the north, he worked daily to repair the walls and deepen the moats in preparation for defense. The court debated sending troops from five provinces and eight guards to garrison distant posts. Yuelubuhua memorialized asking to keep those forces to defend his prefecture, and he was allowed to command several thousand Black Army troops as well as militia from the eighty-two stockaded villages of the Western Hills. His strength grew greatly. When the rebels invaded again, they met defeat each time and withdrew. He was promoted to Senior Grand Master of the Palace and rewarded with four vessels of imperial wine and a hundred horses. His subordinates received promotions of varying rank, and a separate rescript was issued to reward those who had distinguished themselves. He was recalled to serve as Commissioner for Detailed Deliberation. The people of Baoding could not bear to see him leave and painted his portrait for veneration. One month after he left Baoding, the city fell to the rebels. Because Yuelubuhua had long enjoyed the people's trust, the court ordered him to enter the fallen city and win the people back. When he arrived, the rebels held fast behind their walls and refused to come out, but many townspeople slipped out secretly to bow before him. He was appointed Darughachi of the Dadu Circuit. A powerful minister falsely memorialized that the ancestral grave land of the former Central Secretariat Director Yelü Chucai should be granted to Tibetan monks. Yuelubuhua blocked the proposal and in the end refused to allow it. He was transferred back to Director of the Ministry of Personnel. The fierce rebel Cheng Sizhong held Yongping. His lieutenant Lei Temür Buhua feigned surrender, but when the plot was discovered he was seized and executed, and Cheng Sizhong's defense grew only stronger. The court ordered Yuelubuhua to go and win Cheng Sizhong over. Everyone thought the mission perilous, but Yuelubuhua said firmly, "A subject dies in carrying out his lord's command—that is his duty. How can one first weigh one's own safety! " He entered the city at last, addressed the rebels, and they wept, bowed before him in ranks, and surrendered.
10
還，遷翰林侍講學士，俄復為大都路達魯花赤。 入見帝宣文閣，有旨若曰：「朕以畿甸之民疲敝，特選爾撫吾民。 爾毋峻威，毋弛法，或挾權以乾汝於非法，其即以聞。 」視事之初，帝及皇后、皇太子皆遣使賜之酒。 有權臣以免役事來謁，月魯不花面斥曰：「聖訓在耳，不敢違。 」轉資善大夫，拜江南行御史臺中丞。 陛辭之日，帝御嘉禧殿慰勞之，且賜以上尊、金幣； 皇太子亦書「成德誠明」四大字賜之。 月魯不花乃由海道趨紹興，為政寬猛不頗。 詔進階一品為榮祿大夫。 既而除浙西肅政廉訪使。 會張士誠據浙西，僭王號，度弗可與並處，謂侄同壽曰：「吾家世受國恩，恨不能刺賊以報國，矧乃與賊同處邪！ 」令同壽具舟載妻子，而匿身木櫃中，蔽以藳秸，脫走，至慶元。 士誠部下察知之，遣鐵騎百餘追至曹娥江，不及而返。
On his return he was promoted to Hanlin Attendant Lecturer, and soon afterward was again appointed Darughachi of the Dadu Circuit. When he was received at the Xuanwen Pavilion, the emperor said, "The people of the capital region are worn down and impoverished. I have chosen you specially to govern and comfort them. Do not rule by harsh intimidation, and do not relax the law. If anyone abuses power to pressure you into wrongdoing, report it to me at once. " At the start of his tenure the emperor, empress, and heir apparent all sent envoys to present him with wine. A powerful minister came to seek exemption from corvée labor. Yuelubuhua rebuked him to his face: "The emperor's words are still in my ears. I dare not disobey them. " He was promoted to Grand Master for the Heir Apparent and appointed Vice Censor-in-Chief of the Jiangnan Branch Surveillance Commission. On the day of his farewell audience the emperor received him in the Jiaxi Hall, comforted him, and bestowed fine wine and gold coins; and the heir apparent wrote for him the four characters "Accomplished in Virtue, Sincere and Clear." Yuelubuhua traveled by sea to Shaoxing and governed with a balanced mixture of leniency and severity. An edict advanced him to first rank as Grand Master for Glorious Happiness. He was soon appointed Surveillance Commissioner of Western Zhe. Zhang Shicheng had seized Western Zhe and taken a royal title. Judging that he could not remain under such a regime, Yuelubuhua said to his nephew Tongshou, "Our family has received the state's grace for generations. I regret that I cannot assassinate this rebel to repay my country—how could I live alongside him! " He had Tongshou prepare a boat for his wife and children while he hid inside a wooden chest covered with straw and fled to Qingyuan. Zhang Shicheng's men discovered the escape and sent more than a hundred armored horsemen in pursuit as far as the Cao'e River, but they failed to overtake him and turned back.
11
俄改山南道廉訪使，浮海北而往，道阻，還抵鐵山，遇倭賊船甚眾，乃挾同舟人力戰拒之，倭賊紿言投降，弗納。 於是賊即登舟攫月魯不花，令拜伏，月魯不花罵曰：「吾朝廷重臣，寧為賊拜邪！ 」遂遇害。 當遇害時，麾家奴那海刺殺首賊。 次子樞密院判官老安、侄百家奴捍敵，亦死之。 同舟死事者八十餘人。 事聞，朝廷贈攄忠宣武正憲徇義功臣、銀青榮祿大夫、遼陽等處行中書省平章政事、上柱國，謚忠肅。
He was soon appointed Surveillance Commissioner of the Shannan Circuit and set out north by sea, but the route was blocked and he returned to Tieshan. There he encountered a large fleet of Japanese pirates. He rallied his fellow passengers to fight them off. When the pirates feigned surrender, he refused to accept it. The pirates then boarded the vessel, seized Yuelubuhua, and ordered him to bow prostrate. He cursed them, saying, "I am a high minister of the court—would I bow to bandits! " He was killed on the spot. As he was being killed, he ordered his household slave Naha to stab the pirate leader to death. His second son Lao'an, an Assessor of the Bureau of Military Affairs, and his nephew Baijianu fought the enemy and were also killed. More than eighty people aboard the vessel died with him. When word reached the throne, the court posthumously granted him the title Meritorious Subject Who Expelled Loyalty, Proclaimed Martial Rectitude, Upheld Law, and Followed Righteousness; made him Silver-Blue Grand Master for Glorious Happiness and Pacification Commissioner of the Liaoyang Branch Secretariat; ennobled him as Upper Pillar of the State; and gave him the posthumous name Zhongsu, "Loyal and Solemn."
12
○達禮麻識理
○ Dalimashili
13
達禮麻識理，字遵道，怯烈台氏。 其先北方大族，六世祖始居開平。 父曰阿剌不花，江西行省參知政事，追封趙國公，謚襄惠。 達禮麻識理幼穎敏，從師授經史，過目輒領解。 至正五年，經筵選充譯史，益自砥礪於學，搢紳先生皆以遠大期之。 轉補御史臺譯史，遂除御史臺照磨。 十五年，拜監察御史，出僉山北道肅政廉訪司事，未行，留為詹事院長史。 俄遷工部員外郎，復留為長史。 明年，除中議，尋升參議詹事院事。 十七年，為太子家令。 十八年，歷秘書太監、吏部侍郎、御史臺經歷、中書右司郎中。 十九年，除刑部尚書，提調南北兵馬司巡綽事。 盜逼畿甸，人心大恐。 達禮麻識理能鎮之以靜，民恃以為安。 二十一年，由中書參議升中書參知政事、同知經筵事。 二十三年冬，遷上都留守，兼開平府尹，加榮祿大夫，分司土嶺，東鎮三州，以督轉輸。
Dalimashili, whose style was Zundao, belonged to the Qielietai clan. His ancestors were a great northern clan; his sixth-generation forebear was the first to settle at Kaiping. His father Alabuhua had served as Vice Chancellor of the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat and was posthumously enfeoffed as Duke of Zhao with the posthumous name Xianghui, "Assisting and Kind." Dalimashili was clever from childhood. Under his teachers he studied the classics and histories, and whatever he read he understood at once. In the fifth year of the Zhizheng era he was selected by the Classics Colloquium to serve as translation clerk. He redoubled his studies, and scholars of standing all looked to him for great things. He was transferred to the Censorate as translation clerk and then appointed its Registrar. In the fifteenth year he was appointed Supervising Censor and was to go out as associate of the Shanbei Circuit Surveillance Commission, but before he could leave he was retained as Senior Clerk of the Household of the Heir Apparent. He was soon promoted to Outside Director of the Ministry of Works but was again kept on as Senior Clerk. The following year he was appointed Central Deliberator and soon promoted to Deliberator of the Household of the Heir Apparent. In the seventeenth year he became Household Steward of the Heir Apparent. In the eighteenth year he served in succession as Director of the Palace Library, Vice Director of the Ministry of Personnel, Administrator of the Censorate, and Director in the Right Secretariat of the Central Secretariat. In the nineteenth year he was appointed Director of the Ministry of Justice and given charge of patrol and pursuit affairs for the Northern and Southern Military Horse Offices. Rebels pressed close to the capital region, and fear spread among the people. Dalimashili kept order through calm authority, and the people took comfort in him. In the twenty-first year he rose from Central Secretariat Deliberator to Vice Chancellor of the Central Secretariat and Associate Director of the Classics Colloquium. In the winter of the twenty-third year he was appointed Governor of Shangdu and concurrently Prefect of Kaiping, advanced to Grand Master for Glorious Happiness, and charged with overseeing Tuling, garrisoning the three eastern prefectures, and supervising transport.
14
二十四年，朝廷以前中書平章政事塔失帖木兒來為留守。 時孛羅帖木兒擁兵京師，而皇太子出居於外，達禮麻識理與塔世帖木兒皆以忠義許國，相與結人心以觀時變。 未幾，改授塔世帖木兒為大司農。 塔世帖木兒謂達禮麻識理曰：「我至京師則制於強臣，未易圖也。 」因留不行。 適脫吉兒以孛羅帖木兒命屯兵蓋裡泊，托腹心於宗王也速也不堅，授以金印，俾駐上都之東郊，而以留守善安集兵於瓦吉剌部落。 達禮麻識理遇之有禮，善安辭去。 孛羅帖木兒復調帖木兒、托忽速哥至上都，以守禦為名，事益矛盾。 達禮麻識理與之周旋，略無幾微見於外，而密遣前宗正扎魯忽赤月魯帖木兒潛通音問於罕哈哈剌海行樞密知院益老答兒，請亟調兵南行。 又遣留守司照磨陳恭取兵興州，訪求在閒官吏之有才者，約束東西手八剌哈赤、虎賁司、糾集丁壯苗軍，火銃什伍相聯，一旦，布列鐵幡竿山下，揚言四方勤王之師皆至，帖木兒等大駭，一夕東走，其所將兵盡潰。 由是達禮麻識理增修武備，城守益嚴。
In the twenty-fourth year the court sent the former Pacification Commissioner Tash Temür to serve as governor of Shangdu. Boluo Timür then held the capital with his army while the heir apparent lived outside the city. Dalimashili and Tashi Temür both pledged themselves to the dynasty in loyalty and worked together to rally popular support while watching how events would turn. Before long Tashi Temür was reassigned as Minister of Agriculture. Tashi Temür said to Dalimashili, "If I go to the capital I shall be controlled by powerful ministers and will not be able to act freely. " He therefore stayed and did not go. Tuojier, acting on Boluo Timür's orders, garrisoned troops at Gaili Po. He entrusted his plans to the imperial prince Yasutubujian, gave him a gold seal, and stationed him east of Shangdu, while Governor Shan'an assembled troops among the Wajila tribes. Dalimashili received Shan'an courteously, and Shan'an then withdrew. Boluo Timür then transferred Temür and Tohusuge to Shangdu on the pretext of defense, and tensions grew sharper. Dalimashili dealt with them outwardly without showing the slightest sign of his plans, while secretly sending the former Jarquchi of the Imperial Clan Council Yuelu Temür to communicate with Yilaoda'er, Director of the Branch Bureau of Military Affairs at Hanhahalahai, urging him to march troops south at once. He also sent Registrar Chen Gong of the Shangdu Governorate to raise troops at Xingzhou, sought out capable officials who were not then in office, and organized the Eastern and Western Hand Balagachi, the Tiger Guard Office, and stalwart Miao troops armed with fire-lances in linked squads. One day he drew them up below Iron Banner Pole Mountain and spread word that loyalist armies were arriving from every quarter. Temür and his men were terrified and fled east overnight, and their forces collapsed in rout. Thereafter Dalimashili strengthened the defenses and made the city's guard still more rigorous.
15
二十五年，皇太子在冀寧，命立上都分省，達世帖木兒為平章政事，達禮麻識理為右丞，便宜行事，以固護根本。 七月，禿堅帖木兒用孛羅帖木兒命，以兵犯上都，先遣利用少監帖裡哥赤至上都，令廣備糧餼，遠迓大軍。 達禮麻識理開陳大義，戮之於市，民情乃定。 已而禿堅帖木兒帥鐵甲馬步軍蔽野而至，呼聲動天。 達禮麻識理飭軍士城守，申明逆順之理以安人心，巡視城壁，晝夜不少息。 夜遣死士縋城而下，焚其攻具，而調副留守禿魯迷失海牙引兵由小東門出，與之大戰臥龍岡，敗之。 未幾，孛羅帖木兒伏誅，禿堅帖木兒皆奔潰，而上都以安。 拜中書右丞，兼上都留守，提調虎賁司，加光祿大夫，賜黃金系腰，仍命提調東西手八剌哈赤。 既而上都分省罷，遙授中書平章政事、上都留守，位居第一，力辭不允。 明年，召為大宗正府也可扎魯忽赤。 又明年，拜太子詹事。 奉詔至軍中，宣明大義，籓將感悅。 遷翰林學士承旨。 秋，除知樞密院事、大撫軍院事。 初，大撫軍院之立，皇太子用完者帖木兒、答爾麻、帖林沙、伯顏帖木兒、李國鳳等計，專以備禦擴廓帖木兒，既而政權不一，事務益乖，各復引去，而達禮麻識理之至，事且無可為者。
In the twenty-fifth year, while the heir apparent was at Jining, he ordered a branch secretariat established at Shangdu. Tashi Temür was made Pacification Commissioner and Dalimashili Right Chancellor, with discretionary authority to secure the dynasty's base at Shangdu. In the seventh month Tujian Temür, acting on Boluo Timür's orders, marched against Shangdu with an army. He first sent Junior Supervisor Telige Chi of the Utilization Office to Shangdu to demand large stores of provisions and preparations to receive the main force. Dalimashili stated the right cause plainly and had him executed in the marketplace, and the people's minds were calmed. Soon Tujian Temür arrived leading armored cavalry and infantry that covered the plain, and their battle cries shook the heavens. Dalimashili ordered the troops to hold the city, explained the difference between loyalty and rebellion to steady the people's hearts, and inspected the walls day and night without rest. By night he sent dare-to-die troops lowered over the wall to burn the enemy siege engines, and ordered Vice Governor Tulushihaiya to lead troops out through the Small East Gate to fight a great battle with them on Wolong Ridge and defeat them. Before long Boluo Timür was executed, Tujian Temür and his forces fled in rout, and Shangdu was secure. He was appointed Right Chancellor of the Central Secretariat and concurrently Governor of Shangdu, with charge over the Tiger Guard Office. He was advanced to Grand Master for Splendid Happiness, granted a gold waist-belt, and given continued charge of the Eastern and Western Hand Balagachi. When the Shangdu branch secretariat was abolished, he was appointed from afar as Pacification Commissioner of the Central Secretariat and Governor of Shangdu with first rank in precedence. He tried hard to decline but was not allowed. The following year he was summoned to serve as Yeke Jarquchi of the Grand Council for the Imperial Clan. The year after that he was appointed Grand Mentor of the Heir Apparent. Ordered to the army, he proclaimed the dynasty's cause, and the frontier generals were moved and won over. He was promoted to Hanlin Academician-in-Ordinary. That autumn he was appointed Director of the Bureau of Military Affairs and Commissioner of the Great Pacification Army Office. When the Great Pacification Army Office was first established, the heir apparent had followed the plans of Wanze Temür, Da'erma, Tielinsha, Bayan Temür, Li Guofeng, and others to guard specifically against Koço Temür. Soon governmental authority was divided, affairs fell further into disorder, and the original planners withdrew one by one. By the time Dalimashili arrived, there was little left for him to accomplish.
16
達禮麻識理之卒也，先一夕，怯薛官哈剌章者，阿兒剌氏阿魯圖孫也，夜夢太祖召見，語之曰：「我以勤勞取天下，以傳於妥歡帖睦爾。 而愛猷識理達臘不克肖似，廢壞我家法，苟不即收圖，天命不可保矣。 爾吾功臣之後，且誠實，故召汝語，汝明旦亟以吾言告而主及愛猷識理達臘。 汝不以告，吾即殛汝，告而不改，則吾它有處之。 達禮麻識理其人庶幾識事宜者，然知而不言，將焉用之？ 吾其先殛之矣。 」明旦，哈剌章入見帝，具以夢告，帝令以告皇太子。 比出，則達禮麻識理已無疾而卒矣。
On the night before Dalimashili died, the keshig officer Harajang, a grandson of Arutu of the Arlat clan, dreamed that Taizu summoned him and said, "I won the realm through toil and passed it to Toqon Temür. But Ayushiridara does not resemble him and has ruined our house law. Unless he recovers his purpose at once, the Mandate of Heaven cannot be preserved. You are the descendant of my meritorious subject and are honest besides, so I summon you to speak. Tomorrow morning tell your master and Ayushiridara my words at once. If you do not report them, I shall execute you at once. If you report them and nothing changes, I shall deal with the matter in another way. Dalimashili is perhaps a man who understands affairs, yet knowing and not speaking—of what use is he? I shall execute him first. " The next morning Harajang had an audience with the emperor and reported the dream in full. The emperor ordered that it be told to the heir apparent. By the time he came out, Dalimashili had already died suddenly without prior illness.
