董摶霄弟昂霄
Dong Tuanxiao's Younger Brother Angxiao
2
董摶霄，字孟起，磁州人。 由國子生辟陝西行台掾。 時天大旱，從侍御史郭貞讞獄華陰縣，有李謀兒累殺商賈於道，為賊十五年，至百餘事。 事覺，獄已具，賄賂有司，謂徒黨未盡獲，五年不決，人皆以為憤。 摶霄知之，以言於貞，即以屍諸市中，天乃大雨。 授四川肅政廉訪司知事，除涇陽縣尹。 入為戶部主事，升員外郎，拜監察御史。 又出僉遼東肅政廉訪司事，歷江西行省左右司郎中，遷浙東宣慰副使。 其歷官所至，往往理冤獄，革弊政，才譽益著稱於時。
Dong Tuanxiao, courtesy name Mengqi, was a native of Cizhou. Recruited from among the Imperial Academy students, he was appointed a clerk of the Shaanxi Branch Secretariat. At the time a severe drought afflicted the land. While accompanying Supervising Censor Guo Zhen in hearing cases at Huayin County, he encountered the case of Li Mou'er, who had spent fifteen years as a highway robber, repeatedly murdering merchants in more than a hundred incidents. Once the crime was exposed the case was fully prepared for judgment, yet Li Mou'er bribed the officials, claiming that not all his accomplices had been caught. For five years no verdict was rendered, and public outrage mounted. Tuanxiao learned of the situation and reported it to Guo Zhen, who immediately had the corpse displayed in the marketplace. Heaven then sent down torrential rain. He was appointed a clerk of the Sichuan Commission for Purifying Administration and Investigating Censors, and later served as magistrate of Jingyang County. He was recalled to the capital as a principal clerk in the Ministry of Revenue, promoted to vice director, and appointed supervising censor. He was again dispatched as associate commissioner of the Liaodong Commission for Purifying Administration and Investigating Censors, served as a director in the left and right secretariats of the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat, and was later transferred to vice commissioner of the Zhedong Pacification Commission. Wherever he served, he regularly cleared wrongful convictions and abolished corrupt practices, and his talent and reputation steadily grew until he was widely acclaimed.
3
至正十一年，除濟寧路總管，奉旨從江浙平章教化徵進安豐，兵至合肥定林站，遇賊，大破之。 時硃皋、固始賊复猖獗，軍少不足以分討。 有大山民寨及芍陂屯田軍，摶霄皆獎勞而約束之，遂得障蔽硃皋。 我軍屯硃家寺，賊至，追殺之。 乃遣進士程明仲往諭賊中，招徠者千二百家，因悉知其虛實。 夜縛浮橋於淝水，既渡，賊始覺。 賊眾數万據磵南，我軍渡者，輒為其所敗。 摶霄乃麾騎士，別渡淺灘襲賊後。 賊回東南向，與騎士迎敵，摶霄忽躍馬渡磵，揚言於眾曰：「賊已敗。 」諸軍皆渡，一鼓而擊之。 賊大敗，亟追殺之，相藉以死者二十五里，遂復安豐。
In the eleventh year of Zhizheng (1351), he was appointed prefect of Jining Circuit. By imperial order he joined Jiang-Zhe Branch Secretariat Pingzhang Jiaohua on a campaign to relieve Anfeng. When the army reached Dinglin Station near Hefei, it encountered rebel forces and routed them decisively. At that time rebel activity at Zhugao and Gushi flared up again, and the army was too small to divide for simultaneous operations. Tuanxiao rewarded, encouraged, and put under discipline both the Dashan civilian stockade and the Shaopi garrison-farm troops, thereby securing a defensive screen before Zhugao. The army encamped at Zhujia Temple, and when rebels appeared they were pursued and cut down. He then sent the jinshi Cheng Mingzhong to proclaim terms among the rebels, winning over more than twelve hundred households and thereby learning their full disposition. Under cover of night they built a pontoon bridge across the Fei River and crossed before the rebels even noticed. Tens of thousands of rebels held the south bank of the ravine, and every unit that crossed was beaten back. Tuanxiao then ordered the cavalry to ford at a shallow crossing elsewhere and attack the rebels from behind. The rebels wheeled southeast to engage the cavalry. Tuanxiao suddenly spurred his horse across the ravine and shouted to the troops: "The enemy is already beaten!" All the units crossed over and, at a single roll of the drums, charged the enemy. The rebels suffered a crushing defeat. Tuanxiao pressed the pursuit, and corpses lay stacked along the route for twenty-five li. Anfeng was thus restored.
4
十二年，有旨命摶霄攻濠州，又命移軍援江南。 遂渡江，至湖州德清縣，而徽、饒賊已陷杭州。 教化問摶霄計，摶霄曰：「賊皆野人，見杭城子女玉帛，非平日所有，必縱慾，不暇為備，宜急攻之。 今欲退保湖州，設使賊乘銳直趨京口，則江南不可為矣。 」教化猶豫未決，而諸將亦難其行。 摶霄正色曰：「江浙相君方面既陷於賊，今可取而不取，誰任其咎！ 」复拔劍顧諸將曰：「諸君荷國厚恩，而臨難苟免，今相君在是，敢有慢令者斬。 」計乃決。 遂進兵杭城。 賊迎敵，至鹽橋，摶霄麾壯士突前，斬殺數級，而諸軍相繼夾擊之，凡七戰，追殺​​至清河坊。 賊奔接待寺，塞其門而焚之，賊皆死，遂復杭州。 已而餘杭、武康、德清次第以平，摶霄亦受代去。
In the twelfth year (1352), an imperial edict ordered Tuanxiao to attack Haozhou and then to shift his army south to relieve Jiangnan. He crossed the Yangzi and reached Deqing County in Huzhou, only to learn that rebels from Huizhou and Raozhou had already captured Hangzhou. Jiaohua asked Tuanxiao for counsel. Tuanxiao replied: "These rebels are country people. Confronted with Hangzhou's women, children, and riches—luxuries they have never known—they will surely abandon themselves to plunder and neglect their defenses. We should strike at once." If we now fall back to defend Huzhou, and the rebels, emboldened by victory, drive straight for Jingkou, all of Jiangnan will be lost. Jiaohua hesitated, unable to decide, and the other generals balked at the advance. Tuanxiao said sternly: "The Jiang-Zhe regional commander has already lost his command to the rebels. If we can retake the city now and fail to do so, who will answer for it?" He drew his sword and turned to the generals: "You have all received great favor from the state, yet in the face of danger you would shirk your duty. The regional commander is present. Anyone who dares defy orders will be executed." The plan was settled. The army then marched on Hangzhou. The rebels came out to fight. At Salt Bridge, Tuanxiao sent picked warriors charging ahead and cut down several enemy officers. The other units then closed in from both flanks. After seven engagements the pursuit carried as far as Qinghe Ward. The rebels fled into the Reception Temple, barricaded the doors, and set the building ablaze, perishing in the flames. Hangzhou was thus restored. Soon afterward Yuhang, Wukang, and Deqing were pacified in turn, and Tuanxiao completed his tour of duty and departed.
5
徽、饒賊復自昱嶺關寇於潛，行省乃假摶霄為參知政事，俾复提兵討之。 摶霄曰：「必欲除殘去暴，所不敢辭。 若假以重爵，則不​​敢受。 」即日引兵至臨安新溪，是為入杭要路，既分兵守之而始進兵至叫口及虎檻，遇賊，皆大破之，追殺至於潛，遂復其縣治。 既又克復昌化縣及昱嶺關，降賊將潘大ＣＯ二千人。 賊又有犯千秋關者，摶霄還軍守於潛，而賊兵大至，焚倚郭廬舍。 摶霄按軍不動，左右請出兵，摶霄曰：「未也。 」遣人執白旗登山望賊，約曰：「賊以我為怯，必少懈； 伺其有間，則麾所執旗。 」又伏兵城外，皆授以火砲，复約曰：「見旗動，砲即發。 」已而旗動，砲發，兵乃盡出，斬首數知級，遂復千秋關。 未幾，賊復攻獨松、百丈、幽嶺三關，摶霄乃先以兵守多溪。 多溪，三關要路也。 既又分為三軍：一出獨松，一出百丈，一出幽嶺。 然後會兵搗賊巢，遂乘勝復安吉，七戰而克之，賊將以其徒來降者數百人。 既數日，賊復來窺獨松。 摶霄即以兵守苦嶺及黃沙嶺。 賊帥梅元來降，且言復有帥十一人欲降者，即遣偏將餘思忠至賊寨諭之。 賊皆入暗室潛議，思忠持火投入室內，拔劍語眾曰：「元帥命我來活汝，汝復何議！ 」已而火起，焚其寨，叱賊黨散去，而引賊帥來降。 明日，進兵廣德，克之。 有蘄賊與饒、池諸賊复犯徽州。 賊中有道士，能作十二里霧。 摶霄以兵擊之，已而妖霧開豁，諸伏兵皆起，襲賊兵後，賊大潰亂，斬首數万級，擒千餘人。 獲道士，焚其妖書而斬之。 遂平徽州。
Rebels from Huizhou and Raozhou again invaded Yuqian through Yuling Pass. The Branch Secretariat temporarily appointed Tuanxiao vice administrator of government affairs and ordered him to take command once more and suppress them. Tuanxiao said: "If the task is to destroy the cruel and suppress the violent, I shall not refuse." But if you offer me a lofty title merely for show, I cannot accept it." That same day he led his troops to Xinxi in Lin'an, the vital approach to Hangzhou. After detaching units to hold that route, he advanced to Jiaokou and Hujian, routed the rebels in both places, pursued them to Yuqian, and restored the county seat. He next recovered Changhua County and Yuling Pass, and the rebel general Pan Da surrendered with two thousand men. Rebels again threatened Qianqiu Pass. Tuanxiao withdrew to defend Yuqian, where a large enemy force arrived and burned the suburbs. Tuanxiao held his army in place. His officers urged him to engage, but he said: "Not yet." He sent men with white flags up the heights to watch the enemy, instructing them: "The rebels will think us timid and let their guard down;" when you see an opening, wave your flags." He also placed ambush troops outside the walls and armed them with fire-lances, with the same signal: "When the flags move, open fire." Soon the flags waved, the fire-lances roared, and the whole army surged out, taking several thousand heads. Qianqiu Pass was restored. Before long the rebels again assaulted the three passes at Dusong, Baizhang, and Youling. Tuanxiao first deployed troops to hold Duoxi. Duoxi was the key route linking the three passes. He then divided his force into three columns, one advancing through each pass. The columns then converged on the rebel stronghold, and riding the momentum they recovered Anji after seven battles. Several hundred rebel officers and their followers surrendered. A few days later the rebels again probed Dusong Pass. Tuanxiao immediately posted troops at Kul Ridge and Huangsha Ridge. The rebel leader Mei Yuan came over and reported that eleven other chiefs also wished to surrender. Tuanxiao sent Deputy General Yu Sizhong to the rebel camp to accept their submission. The rebels withdrew into a dark room to confer in secret. Sizhong tossed a torch inside, drew his sword, and shouted: "The commander sent me to spare your lives—what is there left to discuss!" Flames soon engulfed the camp. He drove the rebel bands away and escorted the rebel chiefs back to surrender. The next day he marched on Guangde and captured it. Rebels from Qi, together with forces from Raozhou, Chizhou, and elsewhere, again invaded Huizhou. Among the rebels was a Daoist priest who could summon a fog twelve li wide. Tuanxiao attacked with his army. Soon the sorcerous fog lifted, ambush troops sprang up behind the rebel line, and the enemy collapsed in chaos. Tens of thousands were slain and more than a thousand taken prisoner. They captured the priest, burned his occult texts, and executed him. Huizhou was thus pacified.
6
十四年，除水軍都萬戶。 俄升樞密院判官，從丞相脫脫徵高郵，分戍鹽城、興化。 賊巢在大縱、德勝兩湖間，凡十有二，悉剿平之。 即其地築芙蓉寨，賊入，輒迷故道，盡殺之，自是不復敢犯。 賊恃習水，渡淮北，據安東州。 摶霄招善水戰者五百人，與賊戰安東之大湖，大敗之，遂復安東。 十六年，剿平北沙、廟灣、沙浦等寨。 尋進兵泗州，不利。 賊乘勝東下，斷我軍糧道，乃回軍屯北沙，糧且絕，與賊死戰，凡七晝夜。 賊敗走，奪賊船七十餘，乃得渡淮，保泗州。 時方暑雨，湖水溢，諸營皆避去，而摶霄獨守孤城，賊環繞數十里攻之。 摶霄坐城上，遣偏將以騎士由四門突出賊後，約曰：「旗一麾即還。 」既而旗動，騎士還，步卒自城中出，夾擊之，賊大敗。 然賊寨猶阻西行之路，乃結陣而往，翊以奇兵，轉戰數十合，軍始得至海寧。 朝廷嘉其功，升同僉淮南行樞密院事。 摶霄建議於朝曰：
In the fourteenth year (1354) he was appointed commander-in-chief of the naval forces. He was soon promoted to vice director of the Bureau of Military Affairs and joined Chancellor Toqtoghan's campaign against Gaoyou, detaching units to garrison Yancheng and Xinghua. Twelve rebel strongholds lay between the Dazong and Desheng lakes, and he wiped them all out. On that ground he built Furong Stockade. Rebels who entered lost their way and were slaughtered to the last man, and from then on none dared approach. Confident in their mastery of water warfare, the rebels crossed north of the Huai and seized Andong Prefecture. Tuanxiao recruited five hundred skilled naval fighters and met the rebels on the Great Lake at Andong, routing them and recovering the prefecture. In the sixteenth year (1356) he suppressed the rebel stockades at Beisha, Miaowan, Shapu, and elsewhere. He soon advanced on Sizhou but met with defeat. The rebels pressed their advantage eastward and severed the army's supply lines. Tuanxiao withdrew to Beisha, where provisions ran out and he fought the enemy to the death for seven days and nights. The rebels broke and fled. After seizing more than seventy of their boats, the army crossed the Huai and held Sizhou. It was the height of summer, with heavy rains and flooded lakes. The other camps withdrew, but Tuanxiao alone held the isolated city while rebels surrounded it for miles and pressed the attack. Tuanxiao remained on the wall and sent a deputy general with cavalry to burst through all four gates and strike the enemy rear, with orders to return at a single wave of the flag. When the flag waved the cavalry wheeled back, infantry poured from the gates, and the army closed in from both sides. The rebels were routed. Rebel camps still blocked the road west, so he advanced in battle formation with flanking detachments, fighting through dozens of engagements before the army finally reached Haining. The court commended his achievements and promoted him to associate director of the Huainan Branch Bureau of Military Affairs. Tuanxiao submitted the following proposal to the court:
7
淮安為南北襟喉、江淮要沖之地，其地一失，兩淮皆未易復也。 則救援淮安，誠為急務。 為今日計，莫若於黃河上下，並瀕淮海之地，及南自沭陽，北抵沂、莒、贛榆諸州縣，布連珠營，每三十里設一總寨，就三十里中又設一小寨，使斥堠烽燧相望，而巡邏往來，遇賊則並力野戰，無事則屯種而食。 然後進有援，退有守，此善戰者所以常為不可勝，以待敵之可勝也。
Huai'an is the strategic hinge between north and south and the vital crossing of the Yangzi and Huai. Once it falls, recovering the two Huai regions will be exceedingly difficult. Relieving Huai'an must therefore be treated as the most urgent priority. For the present crisis, I propose establishing a chain of fortified camps along the Yellow River, along the Huai and sea coasts, and from Shuyang in the south to Yi, Ju, Ganyu, and neighboring counties in the north. Every thirty li should have a main stockade, with a smaller outpost midway between. Scouts and beacon towers should line the route, patrols should circulate constantly, and when rebels appear the garrisons should unite for battle; in peacetime they should farm for their own subsistence. Thus advance will always have support and retreat will always have a defense. This is how a skilled commander keeps himself invincible while waiting for the enemy to become vulnerable.
8
又海寧一境，不通舟楫，軍糧惟可陸運，而凡瀕淮海之地，人民屢經盜賊，宜加存撫，權令軍人搬運。 其陸運之方，每人行十步，三十六人可行一里，三百六十人可行一十里，三千六百人可行一百里。 每人負米四斗，以夾布囊盛之，用印封識，人不息肩，米不著地，排列成行，日行五百回，計路二十八里，輕行一十四里，重行一十四里，日可運米二百石。 每運給米一升，可供二萬人。 此百里一日運糧之術也。 又江淮流移之民，並安東、海寧、沭陽、贛榆等州縣俱廢，其民壯者既為軍，老弱無所依歸者，宜設置軍民防禦司，擇軍官材堪牧守者，使居其職，而籍其民，以屯故地。 於是練兵積穀，且耕且戰，內全山東完固之邦，外禦淮海出沒之寇，而後恢復可圖也。
Furthermore, the Haining region is inaccessible by boat, so army grain must go by land. The people along the Huai and sea coasts have suffered repeatedly from bandit raids and deserve relief and protection. For the present, soldiers should be assigned to carry the grain. The method of overland transport is as follows: each man walks ten paces. Thirty-six men span one li, three hundred sixty span ten li, and three thousand six hundred span one hundred li. Each man carries four dou of rice in paired cloth sacks, sealed with an official stamp. Shoulders never rest and grain never touches the ground. Lined up in ranks, the carriers make five hundred relays per day over a route of twenty-eight li—fourteen li outbound empty and fourteen li returning loaded—moving two hundred shi of grain daily. Each shi of grain transported yields one sheng per man, enough to feed twenty thousand troops. This is the method for moving grain one hundred li in a single day. The displaced people of the Jiang and Huai regions, together with the abandoned prefectures and counties of Andong, Haining, Shuyang, and Ganyu, also require attention. Able-bodied men have already been drafted into the army, while the old and weak have nowhere to turn. A military-civilian defense bureau should be established, staffed with officers capable of governing, to register the people and resettle them on their former lands. In this way the army could train and stockpile grain, fighting while farming, securing Shandong within and holding off the raiders of the Huai and sea without. Only then would recovery become possible.
9
十七年，毛貴陷益都、般陽等路，有旨命摶霄從知樞密院事卜蘭奚討之。 而濟南又告急，摶霄乃提兵援濟南。 賊眾自南山來攻濟南，望之兩山皆赤。 摶霄按兵城中，先以數十騎挑之，賊眾悉來鬥，騎兵少卻，至磵上，伏兵起，遂合戰，城中兵又大出，大破之。 而般陽賊复約泰安之黨，逾南山來襲濟南。 摶霄列兵城上，弗為動。 賊夜攻南門，獨以矢石御之。 黎明，乃默開東門，放兵出賊後。 既旦，城上兵皆下，大開南門合擊之，賊敗走。 复追殺之，賊眾悉無遺者。 於是濟南始寧。 詔就升淮南行樞密院副使，兼山東宣慰使都元帥，仍賜上尊、金帶、楮幣、名馬以勞之。 有疾其功者，譖於總兵太尉紐的該，令摶霄依前詔，從卜蘭奚同徵益都。 摶霄即出濟南城，屬老且病，請以其弟昂霄代領其眾，朝廷從之。 授昂霄淮南行樞密院判官。 未幾，有旨命摶霄守河間之長蘆。
In the seventeenth year (1357), Mao Gui captured Yidu, Banyang, and other circuits. An imperial edict ordered Tuanxiao to join Bureau of Military Affairs Director Bulanxi in suppressing him. Jinan then sent urgent appeals for help, and Tuanxiao marched to its relief. Rebel forces descended from the southern mountains to attack Jinan, and from a distance both hillsides seemed awash in red. Tuanxiao kept his army in the city and sent several dozen horsemen to provoke the enemy. The whole rebel host surged forward in response. The cavalry feigned retreat to a ravine, where hidden troops sprang up and joined the fight. Troops then poured from the city gates, and the rebels were routed. The Banyang rebels then joined forces with allies from Tai'an, crossed the southern mountains, and struck at Jinan again. Tuanxiao lined his troops along the wall and held his position. The rebels attacked the south gate by night, and he repelled them with arrows and stones alone. At daybreak he quietly opened the east gate and sent troops out behind the enemy. When morning came the troops descended from the walls, the south gate swung wide, and the army closed in from both sides. The rebels broke and fled. He pursued and cut them down until not one rebel remained. Jinan was at last restored to peace. An edict immediately promoted him to vice director of the Huainan Branch Bureau of Military Affairs and commander-in-chief of the Shandong Pacification Commission, and rewarded him with fine wine, a gold belt, paper currency, and a prized horse. Envious of his success, some officials slandered him to Grand Marshal Niudigai, commander-in-chief of the army, who ordered Tuanxiao to obey the earlier edict and join Bulanxi on the campaign against Yidu. Tuanxiao then left Jinan. Being old and ill, he asked that his younger brother Angxiao take command of his troops in his stead, and the court approved. Angxiao was appointed judge of the Huainan Branch Bureau of Military Affairs. Shortly afterward, an imperial edict ordered Tuanxiao to hold Changlu in Hejian.
10
十八年，摶霄以兵北行，且曰：「我去，濟南必不可保。 」既而濟南果陷。 摶霄方駐兵南皮縣之魏家莊，適有使者奉詔拜摶霄河南行省右丞，甫拜命，毛貴兵已至，而營壘猶未完。 諸將謂摶霄曰：「賊至當如何？ 」摶霄曰：「我受命至此，當以死報國耳。 」因拔劍督兵以戰。 而賊眾突至摶霄前，捽而問曰：「汝為誰？ 」摶霄曰：「我董老爺也！ 」眾刺殺之，無血，惟見其有白氣沖天。 是日，昂霄亦死之。 事聞，贈宣忠守正保節功臣、榮祿大夫、河南行省平章政事、柱國，追封魏國公，諡忠定。 昂霄贈推誠孝節功臣、嘉議大夫、禮部尚書、上輕車都尉，追封隴西郡侯，諡忠毅。
In the eighteenth year (1358), Tuanxiao marched north with his army, saying, "Once I am gone, Jinan will surely fall. Jinan did indeed fall soon after. Tuanxiao was encamped at Weijiazhuang in Nanpi County when an envoy arrived bearing an edict appointing him Right Vice Chancellor of the Henan Branch Secretariat. He had barely accepted the commission when Mao Gui's forces appeared, and the camp defenses were not yet complete. The generals asked Tuanxiao, "What are we to do when the rebels arrive? Tuanxiao replied, "I was sent here on imperial commission. I can only repay the dynasty with my life. He then drew his sword and led his men into battle. Rebel troops suddenly surged up to Tuanxiao, seized him, and demanded, "Who are you? Tuanxiao answered, "I am Lord Dong! The mob stabbed him to death. No blood flowed; only a white vapor was seen rising into the sky. That same day Angxiao was killed as well. When word reached the court, he was posthumously honored as Meritorious Subject Who Proclaims Loyalty, Upholds Integrity, and Preserves Righteousness, given the rank of Grand Master for Glorious Happiness, Chief Administrator of the Henan Branch Secretariat, and Pillar of the State, enfeoffed posthumously as Duke of Wei, and granted the posthumous name Zhongding. Angxiao was posthumously honored as Meritorious Subject of Sincere Devotion and Filial Righteousness, given the rank of Grand Master for Deliberation, Minister of Rites, and Superior Commandant of Light Chariots, enfeoffed posthumously as Marquis of Longxi Commandery, and granted the posthumous name Zhongyi.
11
摶霄早以儒生起家，輒為能吏，會天下大亂，乃復以武功自奮，其才略有大過人者，而當時用之不能盡其才，君子惜之。
Tuanxiao had begun his career as a Confucian scholar and quickly proved himself a capable administrator. When the empire descended into chaos, he distinguished himself anew through military service. His talent and strategic ability far exceeded those of ordinary men, yet the court of his day never fully employed his gifts. Reflective observers have lamented the waste.

○劉哈剌不花
Liu Halabuhua
13
劉哈剌不花，其先江西人。 倜儻好義，不事家產，有古俠士風。 居燕趙有年，遂為探馬赤軍戶。 至正十二年，潁、亳盜起，朝遷以泰不花為河南行省平章政事，總兵討之。 哈剌不花上書陳十事，其七言兵機及攻守方略。 泰不花大喜，即辟為掾史。 未幾，奏除左右司都事。 泰不花以哈剌不花嘗為探馬赤，有膂力，善騎射，俾統前八翼軍，為先鋒將。 明號令，信賞罰，士皆樂為之用，而料敵成敗，所向無失。 是時，答失八都魯軍潰於長葛，收集散卒，复屯中牟。 哈剌不花軍於汴梁南彭子岡。 有自長葛來者言，總兵官已為賊所敗，次中牟。 哈剌不花曰：「賊既捷，兵必再至，我不可不往援。 」遂整兵而前。 既而有使馳報：夜四鼓，賊從洧川渡河，未知其所向。 哈剌不花曰：「是必襲答失八都魯營耳。 我行已緩，不及事，不若以精銳斷賊歸路，覆之必矣。 」於是領軍徐行。 天未明，伏軍其歸路。 賊果襲答失八都魯營，大掠輜重而回。 哈剌不花伏軍四起，賊大敗，盡俘獲之。 當是時，答失八都魯雖以平章政事總大兵，而哈剌不花功名與之相埒。
Liu Halabuhua's family originally came from Jiangxi. Free-spirited and devoted to righteousness, he cared little for family wealth and carried himself in the manner of the knights-errant of old. After living in the Yan and Zhao region for many years, he became registered as a tamachi soldier. In the twelfth year of Zhizheng (1352), rebels broke out in Ying and Bo. The court appointed Taibuhua Chief Administrator of the Henan Branch Secretariat and put him in command of the campaign against them. Halabuhua submitted a memorial outlining ten proposals, seven of them devoted to military timing and offensive and defensive strategy. Taibuhua was greatly impressed and immediately took him on as a clerk. Before long he was promoted by memorial to director of the Left and Right Secretariats. Because Halabuhua had served as a tamachi soldier, possessed exceptional strength, and excelled at horsemanship and archery, Taibuhua put him in command of the front eight wings of the army as vanguard general. He enforced clear orders and kept his rewards and punishments reliable, so his men served him willingly. In reading the enemy's strengths and weaknesses, he never failed to prevail. At this time Chaghan Temur's army was routed at Changge. He gathered his scattered troops and reestablished camp at Zhongmou. Halabuhua encamped his army at Pengzigang, south of Bianliang. A messenger from Changge reported that the commander-in-chief had already been defeated by the rebels and was now at Zhongmou. Halabuhua said, "The rebels have won once; their troops will surely strike again. We must go to his aid. He then marshaled his troops and marched forward. Then a courier arrived with urgent news: at the fourth watch of the night, rebels had crossed the river from Weichuan, and their destination was still unknown. Halabuhua said, "They must be striking at Chaghan Temur's camp. We are already too late to intervene in time. Better to deploy elite troops to cut off the rebels' retreat; we can surely destroy them that way. He therefore led his army forward at a deliberate pace. Before dawn he placed ambush troops along their line of retreat. The rebels did indeed raid Chaghan Temur's camp, plundered his baggage train, and withdrew. Halabuhua's ambush forces rose on every side. The rebels were routed and all were captured. At that time, although Chaghan Temur held overall command of the army as chief administrator, Halabuhua's achievements and reputation matched his own.
14
十七年，山東毛貴率其賊眾，由河間趨直沽，遂犯漷州，至棗林。 已而略柳林，逼畿甸，樞密副使達國珍戰死，京師人心大駭。 在廷之臣，或勸乘輿北巡以避之，或勸遷都關陝，眾議紛然，獨左丞相太平執不可。 哈剌不花時為同知樞密院事，奉詔以兵拒之，與之戰於柳林，大捷。 貴眾悉潰退，走據濟南，京師遂安，哈剌不花之功居多。 哈剌不花後遷河南行省平章政事以卒。
In the seventeenth year (1357), Mao Gui of Shandong led his rebel forces from Hejian toward Zhigu, then attacked Huo Prefecture and reached Zaolin. He then plundered Liulin and pressed toward the capital region. Daguozhen, associate commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs, was killed in battle, and panic seized the capital. At court some officials urged the emperor to tour north to escape the rebels, others urged relocating the capital to the Guanzhong region, and debate raged on all sides, but Left Chancellor Taiping alone insisted that neither course must be taken. Halabuhua was then associate commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs. By imperial order he led troops to meet the enemy and fought them at Liulin, winning a great victory. Mao Gui's forces broke and fled, retreating to hold Jinan. The capital was thus secured, and Halabuhua's contribution was the greatest. Halabuhua was later promoted to Chief Administrator of the Henan Branch Secretariat, in which post he died.
15
初，哈剌不花與信州人倪晦字孟晰同事泰不花為掾史。 晦涉書史，精文墨，機識警敏，泰不花深委任之，言無不從； 而哈剌不花或有所論白，多沮不行，由是心銜泰不花。 及泰不花事敗，走詣哈剌不花求援，而哈剌不花不能曲為保全，乃縛泰不花送京師，致之死地，君子以是少之。
Earlier, Halabuhua and Ni Hui of Xin Prefecture, courtesy name Mengxi, had served together as clerks under Taibuhua. Ni Hui was well read in history and skilled in writing, quick-witted and perceptive. Taibuhua relied on him deeply and never refused his counsel. When Halabuhua offered proposals of his own, however, most were blocked and never adopted, and he came to bear a grudge against Taibuhua. When Taibuhua's cause collapsed, he fled to Halabuhua seeking help. Halabuhua could not bring himself to protect him, however, and instead bound Taibuhua and sent him to the capital, ensuring his death. Reflective observers have held this against him.

○王英
Wang Ying
17
王英，字邦傑，益都人。 性剛果，有大節，膂力絕人，善騎射。 襲父職，為莒州翼千戶。 父子皆善用雙刀，人號之曰刀王。 至元二十九年，江西行樞密院命帥師南雄，討賊丘大老。 賊六百餘人突至，英與戰，殺其渠帥劉把東，獲九十餘人。 元貞元年，從左丞董士選討大山賊劉貴，擒之。 二年，討永新、安福二州賊，餘黨皆息。 延祐二年，寧都賊起，行省命英率各萬戶軍討之。 賊勢甚張，英屢戰皆勝，斬獲不可勝數，積尸盈野，水為不流。 行省平章李世安，遣英迓江浙平章張閭所領軍於閩境，至木麻坑，擒賊蔡五九。 又追賊至上虎嶂，遇賊三千餘人，盡殲之。 至治元年，以大臣薦，授忠武校尉、益都淄萊萬戶府副千戶。 天曆元年，授宣武將軍。 至順二年，行省命英招捕桂陽州賊張思進等二千人。 英至，布以威信，皆相率請降。 元統元年，授懷遠大將軍、同知海北海南道宣慰使司事。
Wang Ying, courtesy name Bangjie, was a native of Yidu. By nature he was resolute and decisive, possessed of great integrity, with exceptional strength and skill in horsemanship and archery. He inherited his father's post and became centurion of the Juzhou wing. Father and son were both masters of the paired saber, and people called them the Saber Kings. In the twenty-ninth year of Zhiyuan (1292), the Jiangxi Branch Bureau of Military Affairs ordered him to lead troops at Nanxiong against the rebel Qiu Dalao. More than six hundred rebels suddenly appeared. Ying engaged them, killed their leader Liu Badong, and captured more than ninety men. In the first year of Yuanzheng (1295), he followed Left Vice Chancellor Dong Shixuan in suppressing the Dashan rebel Liu Gui and captured him. In the second year he suppressed rebels in Yongxin and Anfu prefectures, and the remaining bands were pacified. In the second year of Yanyou (1315), rebels broke out in Ningdu. The branch secretariat ordered Ying to lead the armies of the various ten-thousand-household officers against them. The rebel force was formidable, but Ying fought repeatedly and always prevailed. The slain and captured were beyond counting; corpses piled across the fields until the water ceased to flow. Li Shi'an, chief administrator of the branch secretariat, sent Ying to meet the army led by Zhang Lu, chief administrator of the Jiang and Zhe Branch Secretariat, at the Fujian border. At Mumakeng he captured the rebel Cai Wujiu. He then pursued rebels to Shanghuzhang, encountered more than three thousand of them, and annihilated them all. In the first year of Zhizhi (1321), on the recommendation of a senior official, he was appointed Colonel Who Maintains Martial Integrity and deputy centurion of the Yidu Zilai Ten-Thousand Households Office. In the first year of Tianli (1328), he was appointed General Who Proclaims Military Might. In the second year of Zhishun (1331), the branch secretariat ordered Ying to summon and capture the two thousand rebels of Guiyang Prefecture led by Zhang Sijin and others. When Ying arrived, he won them over with authority and good faith, and they all came forward to surrender. In the first year of Yuantong (1333), he was appointed Grand General of Expansive Far-reaching and associate commissioner of the Haibei and Hainan Circuit Pacification Commission.
18
至元三年，萬安軍賊吳汝期等作亂，聚眾三千人。 英至，賊皆就擒。 未幾，李志甫起漳州，劉虎仔起潮州，詔命江西行省右丞燕帖木兒討之。 方賊起時，英已致仕，平章政事伯撒里謂僚佐曰：「是雖鼠竊狗偷，非刀王行不可。 其人雖投老，必可以義激。 」乃使迎致之。 英曰：「國家有事，吾雖老，其可坐視乎！ 」據鞍橫槊，精神飛動，馳赴焉。 及賊平，英功居多。
In the third year of Zhiyuan, the Wan'an Army rebels Wu Ruqi and others rose in revolt and gathered a force of three thousand men. When Ying arrived, the rebels were all captured. Before long Li Zhipu rebelled in Zhang Prefecture and Liu Huzai in Chao Prefecture. An edict ordered El Temur, Right Vice Chancellor of the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat, to suppress them. When the rebels broke out, Ying had already retired. Chief Administrator Barsuri told his staff, "Petty as these raids may be, nothing will succeed unless the Saber King goes. Though he is old, he can surely be moved by a call to duty. He therefore sent envoys to invite him back. Ying said, "When the state is in need, how can I, though old, sit idle! He mounted his horse, spear in hand, his spirit afire, and galloped to the front. When the rebels were pacified, Ying's contribution was the greatest.
19
至正中，毛貴陷益都，英時年九十有六，乃謂其子弘曰：「我世受國恩，美官厚祿，備嘗享之。 今老矣，縱不能事戎馬以報天子，尚忍食異姓之粟以求生乎！ 」水漿不入口者數日，遂卒。 毛貴聞之，使具棺衾以葬。 將斂，舉其屍不動，焚香祝曰：「公子弘請公歸葬先塋。 」祝畢，屍遂起，觀者莫不驚異。 山東宣慰使普顏不花及憲司請卹典於朝，有曰：「不食寇粟，餓死芹泉，有夷、齊之風，為臣之清者也。 」芹泉，谷名，英所居也。
During the Zhizheng period, Mao Gui captured Yidu. Ying was then ninety-six years old. He said to his son Hong, "Our family has enjoyed the state's favor for generations. We have held fine offices and generous salaries and tasted every comfort they bring. I am old now. Even if I can no longer serve on horseback to repay the emperor, how can I still eat a rebel's grain merely to stay alive! For several days he took neither food nor water, and then he died. When Mao Gui heard of it, he had a coffin and burial shroud prepared for the funeral. As they prepared to lay him in the coffin, they lifted the body but it would not move. They burned incense and prayed, "Your son Hong asks that you return to be buried in the ancestral grave. When the prayer was finished, the body rose. All who witnessed it were astonished. Puyan Buhua, pacification commissioner of Shandong, and the censorate petitioned the court for mourning honors. One memorialist wrote, "Refusing the rebels' grain and starving to death at Qinquan, he showed the spirit of Bo Yi and Shu Qi—the integrity of a loyal minister. Qinquan was the name of the valley where Ying had lived.

○石抹宜孫
Shimoyisun
21
石抹宜孫，字申之。 其先遼之迪烈糺人。 五世祖曰也先，事太祖為御史大夫，自有傳。 也先之曾孫曰繼祖，字伯善，襲父職，為沿海上副萬戶。 初以沿海軍分鎮台州，皇慶元年，又移鎮婺、處兩州。 馭軍嚴肅，平寧都寇，有戰功； 且明達政事，講究鹽策，多合時宜。 為學本於經術，而兼通名法、縱橫、天文、地理、術數、方技、釋老之說，見稱薦紳間。 宜孫其子也。
Shimoyisun, courtesy name Shenzhi. His ancestors were Diliejiu people of the Liao dynasty. His fifth-generation ancestor Yexian served Taizu as Censor-in-Chief and has his own biography. Yexian's great-grandson Jizu, courtesy name Boshan, inherited his father's post and became deputy commander of the Coastal Guard. At first he garrisoned Taizhou with detachments of the coastal army. In the first year of Huangqing (1312), he was transferred to garrison Wu and Chu prefectures. He commanded his troops with stern discipline, pacified the Ningdu rebels, and won distinction in battle. He was also clear-sighted in administration, studied salt policy closely, and devised measures well suited to the times. His scholarship was rooted in the classics, yet he was also versed in law, statecraft, astronomy, geography, divination, medicine, and Buddhist and Daoist learning, and was praised among the gentry. Yisun was his son.
22
宜孫性警敏，嗜學問，於書務博覽，而長於詩歌。 嘗借嫡弟厚孫廕，襲父職，為沿海上副萬戶，守處州。 及弟長，即讓其職還之，退居台州。 至正十一年，方國珍起海上，江浙行省檄宜孫守溫州，宜孫即起任其事。 其年閩寇犯處州，复檄宜孫以兵平之。 以功升浙東宣慰副使，分府於台州。 頃之，處之屬縣山寇並起，宜孫復奉省檄往討之。 至則築處州城，為御敵計。 十七年，江浙行省左丞相達識鐵睦邇承製升宜孫行樞密院判官，總制處州，分院治於處。 又以江浙儒學副提舉劉基為其院經歷，蕭山縣尹蘇友龍為照磨，而宜孫又辟郡人胡深、葉琛、章溢參謀其軍事。 處為郡，山谷聯絡，盜賊憑據險阻，輒竊發，不易平治。 宜孫用基等謀，或搗以兵，或誘以計，未幾，皆殲殄無遺類。 尋升同僉行樞密院事。 當是之時，天下已多故，所在守將各自為計相保守。 於是浙東則宜孫在處州，邁里古思在紹興為稱首。
Yisun was quick-witted by nature, devoted to learning, widely read, and accomplished in poetry. He once used his younger brother Housun's hereditary privilege to inherit his father's post as deputy commander of the Coastal Guard and took charge of defending Chuzhou. When his brother came of age, he immediately returned the post to him and retired to Taizhou. In the eleventh year of Zhizheng (1351), Fang Guozhen rose on the sea. The Jiang and Zhe Branch Secretariat ordered Yisun to defend Wenzhou, and he immediately took up the assignment. That same year Fujian rebels invaded Chuzhou, and he was again ordered to pacify them by force. For his achievements he was promoted to vice commissioner of the Eastern Zhe Pacification Commission, with his headquarters at Taizhou. Before long mountain rebels rose simultaneously in the counties subordinate to Chu Prefecture, and Yisun again received orders from the branch secretariat to go suppress them. Upon arrival he fortified the walls of Chuzhou to prepare for enemy attack. In the seventeenth year (1357), Jiang-Zhe Branch Secretariat Left Chancellor Dash Timur, acting on provisional authority, promoted Yisun to vice director of the Branch Bureau of Military Affairs, placed him in overall command of Chuzhou, and established a sub-office there. He appointed Liu Ji, associate commissioner for Confucian studies in Jiang-Zhe, as director of affairs for the bureau; Su Youlong, magistrate of Xiaoshan County, as registrar; and Yisun further recruited his countrymen Hu Shen, Ye Chen, and Zhang Yi as military advisers. Chuzhou was a mountainous prefecture where valleys and ridges interlocked. Bandits exploited the rugged terrain to launch raids and were difficult to suppress. Following the counsel of Liu Ji and the others, Yisun alternately attacked with troops and enticed with stratagems. Before long the bandits were wiped out to the last man. He was soon promoted to associate director of the Branch Bureau of Military Affairs. By then the empire was in turmoil, and local commanders everywhere looked to their own survival. In eastern Zhejiang, Yisun at Chuzhou and Mailigusi at Shaoxing stood foremost among the defenders.
23
十八年十二月，大明兵取蘭溪，且逼婺，而宜孫母實在婺城。 宜孫泣曰：「義莫重於君親，食祿而不事其事，是無君也； 母在難而不赴，是無親也。 無君無親，尚可立天地間哉！ 」即遣胡深等將民兵數万往赴援，而親率精銳為之殿。 兵至婺，與大明兵甫接，即敗績而還。 時經略使李國鳳至浙東，承製拜宜孫江浙行省參知政事，階中奉大夫。 明年，大明兵入處州，宜孫將數十騎走福建境上，欲圖報復，而所至人心已散，事不可復為。 歎曰：「處州，吾所守者也。 今吾勢已窮，無所於往，不如還處州境，死亦為處州鬼耳！ 」既還，至處之慶元縣，為亂兵所害。 事聞，朝廷贈推誠宣力效節功臣、集賢大學士、榮祿大夫、上柱國，追封越國公，諡忠愍。
In the twelfth month of the eighteenth year (1358), the Ming army captured Lanxi and advanced on Wu Prefecture, where Yisun's mother was living. Yisun wept and said: "No obligation outweighs loyalty to sovereign and filial duty to parent. To draw a salary yet fail in one's duty is to betray one's sovereign;" to leave one's mother in danger and not go to her is to abandon one's parent. Without loyalty or filial piety, how can one still face the world! He immediately sent Hu Shen and others with tens of thousands of militia to the rescue, while he personally led an elite force to cover the rear. When the army reached Wu Prefecture, it barely engaged the Ming forces before suffering defeat and withdrawing. Pacification Commissioner Li Guofeng had just arrived in eastern Zhejiang and, acting on provisional authority, appointed Yisun vice administrator of the Jiang-Zhe Branch Secretariat with the rank of Grand Master for Attendant Service. The following year the Ming army entered Chuzhou. Yisun fled to the Fujian border with a few dozen horsemen, hoping to regroup for revenge, but morale had collapsed everywhere and the cause was lost. He sighed and said: "Chuzhou is the land I have defended. My strength is spent and I have nowhere left to go. Better to return to Chuzhou—even in death I shall be a ghost of that land!" He turned back, but upon reaching Qingyuan County in Chuzhou he was killed by mutinous soldiers. When word reached the court, Yisun was posthumously honored as Meritorious Minister for Sincerity, Proclaiming Strength, and Devoted Integrity; Grand Academician of the Hall of Worthies; Grand Master for Glorious Emolument; and Superior Pillar of the State. He was posthumously enfeoffed as Duke of Yue, with the posthumous name Zhongmin.

邁里古思
Mailigusi
25
邁里古思者，寧夏人也，字善卿。 至正十四年進士，授紹興路錄事司達魯花赤。 苗軍主將楊完者在杭，縱其軍鈔掠，莫敢誰何，民甚苦之。 俄有至紹興城中強奪人馬者，邁里古思擒斬數人，苗軍乃懼，不敢復至其境。 邁里古思名聲遂大振。 會江南行台移治紹興，檄邁里古思為行台鎮撫，乃大募民兵，為守禦計。 處州山賊焚掠婺之永康、東陽，邁里古思提兵往擊之，與石抹宜孫約期夾攻其巢穴，山賊以平。 擢江東廉訪司經歷，仍留紹興，以兵衛台治。 時浙東、西郡縣多殘破，獨邁里古思保障紹興，境內晏然，民愛之如父母。 江浙省臣乃承製授行樞密院判官，分院治紹興。
Mailigusi was a native of Ningxia, courtesy name Shanqing. A jinshi of the fourteenth year of Zhizheng (1354), he was appointed darughachi of the Shaoxing Circuit Records Office. Miao Army commander Yang Waner was stationed at Hangzhou, where his troops looted at will. No one dared resist, and the people suffered terribly. When Miao soldiers entered Shaoxing and began seizing people and horses by force, Mailigusi captured and executed several of them. The Miao Army grew fearful and never again entered his jurisdiction. Mailigusi's reputation soared. When the Jiangnan Branch Censorate relocated to Shaoxing, it appointed Mailigusi as its pacification commissioner. He raised a large militia force to prepare the city's defense. Mountain bandits from Chuzhou raided Yongkang and Dongyang in Wu Prefecture. Mailigusi marched against them and, coordinating with Shimoyisun for a pincer attack on their stronghold, pacified the mountain rebels. He was promoted to director of affairs in the Jiangdong Commission for Investigating Censors while remaining at Shaoxing to garrison the censorate with troops. While prefectures and counties across Zhejiang lay in ruins, Mailigusi alone kept Shaoxing secure and at peace. The people loved him as they would their own parents. The Jiang-Zhe provincial officials then, acting on provisional authority, appointed him vice director of the Branch Bureau of Military Affairs with a sub-office at Shaoxing.

會方國珍遣兵侵紹興屬縣，邁里古思曰：「國珍本海賊，今既降，為大官，而復來害吾民，可乎！ 」欲率兵往問罪。 先遣部將黃中取上虞，中還，請益兵。 是時朝廷方倚重國珍，資其舟以運糧，而御史大夫拜住哥，與國珍素通賄賂，情好甚厚，憤邁里古思擅舉兵，恐且生事，即使人召邁里古思至其私第，與計事，至則命左右以鐵槌撾死之，斷其頭，擲廁溷中。 城中民聞之，不問男女老幼，無不慟哭者。 黃中乃率其眾復仇，盡殺拜住哥家人及台府官員掾史，獨留拜住哥不殺，以告於張士誠，士誠乃遣其將以兵守紹興。 拜住哥尋遷行宣政院使，監察御史真童糾言：「拜住哥陰害帥臣，幾致激變，不法不忠，莫斯為甚。 宜稽諸彝典，置於嚴刑。 」於是詔削拜住哥官職，安置潮州，而邁里古思之冤始白。
When Fang Guozhen sent troops to raid counties under Shaoxing's jurisdiction, Mailigusi said: "Fang Guozhen was once a pirate. Now that he has surrendered and holds high office, how can he come again to harm our people!" He resolved to march against Fang Guozhen and demand an accounting. He first sent his deputy Huang Zhong to capture Shangyu. Zhong returned and requested reinforcements. The court was then relying heavily on Fang Guozhen, using his fleet to transport grain. Censor-in-Chief Baizhuge, who had long exchanged bribes with Fang and enjoyed close ties with him, was enraged that Mailigusi had mobilized troops without authorization and feared he would cause further trouble. He summoned Mailigusi to his private residence on the pretext of consultation, then ordered his attendants to beat him to death with iron mallets, sever his head, and throw it into a latrine. When the people of the city learned of it, men and women, old and young alike, wept without exception. Huang Zhong then led his troops in revenge, slaughtering Baizhuge's entire family along with censorate and prefectural officials and clerks. He spared Baizhuge alone and reported the matter to Zhang Shicheng, who dispatched generals to garrison Shaoxing. Baizhuge was soon transferred to commissioner of the Branch Commission for the Spread of Governance. Supervising Censor Zhentong impeached him, declaring: "Baizhuge secretly murdered a commanding general, nearly provoking a full-scale revolt. In lawlessness and disloyalty, his crime knows no equal." He should be judged by the statutes and punished with the utmost severity. An edict then stripped Baizhuge of his rank and exiled him to Chaozhou, and Mailigusi's wrongful death was at last vindicated.
