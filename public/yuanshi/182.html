1
張起岩
Zhang Qiyan
2
張起岩，字夢臣。 其先章丘人，五季避地禹城。 高祖迪，以元帥右監軍權濟南府事，徙家濟南。 當金之季，張榮據有章丘、鄒平、濟陽、長山、辛市、蒲台、新城、淄州之地，歲丙戌，歸於太祖，始終能效忠節，迪與其子福，實先後羽翼之。 福仕為濟南路軍民鎮撫兵鈐轄，權府事，生東昌錄事判官鐸，鐸生四川行省儒學副提舉範，範生起岩。 初，其母丘氏有娠，見長蛇數丈入榻下，已忽不見，乃驚而誕起岩。
Zhang Qiyan, whose style name was Mengchen. His forebears were from Zhangqiu; in the Five Dynasties period the family took refuge in Yucheng. His great-grandfather Di served as right supervisor of the marshalate with acting charge of Jinan prefecture and resettled the family there. Late in the Jin, Zhang Rong controlled Zhangqiu, Zouping, Jiyang, Changshan, Xinshi, Putai, Xincheng, and Zizhou; in the bingxu year he submitted to Taizu and proved loyal to the end. Di and his son Fu were among those who aided him from the first. Fu was Jinan circuit military and civil pacification commissioner and military controller with acting prefectural duties; his son Duo became recorder and judge at Dongchang, whose son Fan was associate commissioner for Confucian learning on the Sichuan branch secretariat; Fan was Qiyan's father. Before his birth, his mother Lady Qiu, while pregnant, saw a serpent many zhang long slip under the couch and vanish; startled, she then bore Qiyan.
3
幼從其父學，年弱冠，以察舉為福山縣學教諭，值縣官捕蝗，移攝縣事。 久之，聽斷明允，其民相率曰：「若得張教諭為真縣尹，吾屬何患焉。 」政成，遷安丘。 中延祐乙卯進士，首選，除同知登州事，特旨改集賢修撰，轉國子博士，升國子監丞，進翰林待制，兼國史院編修官。 丁內艱，服除，選為監察御史。 中書參政楊廷玉以墨敗，台臣奉旨就廟堂逮之下吏。 丞相倒剌沙疾其摧辱同列，悉誣台臣罔上，欲置之重辟。 起岩以新除留台，抗章論曰：「台臣按劾百官，論列朝政，職使然也。 今以奉職獲戾，風紀解體，正直結舌，忠良寒心，殊非盛世事。 且世皇建臺閣，廣言路，維持治體，陛下即位詔旨，動法祖宗。 今台臣坐譴，公論杜塞，何謂法祖宗耶！ 」章三上，不報。 起岩廷爭愈急，帝感悟，事乃得釋，猶皆坐罷免還鄉里。 遷中書右司員外郎，進左司郎中，兼經筵官，拜太子右贊善。 丁外艱，服除，改燕王府司馬，拜禮部尚書。
He studied with his father as a boy; at twenty he was nominated as instructor at the Fushan county school. When the magistrate went out to catch locusts, Qiyan was left to administer the county. Before long his judgments were clear and fair, and the people said among themselves, "If Instructor Zhang were our real magistrate, we would have nothing to fear." " When he had finished his term, he was moved to Anqiu. He took the jinshi in Yanyou's yimao year as first in the class, became associate administrator of Dengzhou, then by special order was made compiler at the Hall of Gathered Talents, erudite of the National University, vice director of the directorate of education, Hanlin attendant draftsman, and concurrent historiography compiler. After mourning his mother he was chosen investigating censor. Yang Tingyu, a central secretariat administrator, fell for corruption; the censorate, by imperial order, seized him in the council hall and handed him to the judicial officers. Chancellor Toghto hated their humiliation of fellow officials, accused the whole censorate of deceiving the throne, and sought to punish them severely. Qiyan, newly appointed and still at the censorate, memorialized: "Censors investigate officials and discuss court affairs—that is their office. To punish them for performing it destroys discipline, silences the upright, and chills the loyal—hardly what a flourishing age should see. Moreover Kublai founded the censorate and widened free speech to uphold government; Your Majesty's accession edicts constantly follow the ancestors. Now the censors are punished and public debate is stifled—how is that following the ancestors?" " He submitted it three times without answer. Qiyan pressed his case in court; the emperor relented and the matter was dropped, though all were still dismissed and sent home. He became right-section vice director of the central secretariat, then left-section director, classics mat lecturer, and right tutor to the heir apparent. After mourning his father he was made chief of staff to the Prince of Yan and minister of rites.
4
文宗親郊，起岩充大禮使，導帝陟降，步武有節，衣前後襜如，陪位百官，望之如古圖畫中所睹。 帝甚嘉之，賜賚優渥。 轉參議中書省事。 寧宗崩，燕南俄起大獄，有妄男子上變，言部使者謀不軌，按問皆虛，法司謂：「《唐律》，告叛者不反坐。 」起岩奮謂同列曰：「方今嗣君未立，人情危疑，不亟誅此人，以杜奸謀，慮妨大計。 」趣有司具獄，都人肅然，大事尋定。 中書方列坐銓選，起岩荐一士可用，丞相不悅，起岩即攝衣而起，丞相以為忤己。 遷翰林侍講學士、知制誥兼修國史，修三朝實錄，加同知經筵事。 御史臺奏除浙西廉訪使，不允。 已而擢陝西行台侍御史。 將行，复留為侍講學士。 拜江南行台侍御史，召入中台，為侍御史。 轉燕南廉訪使。 搏擊豪強，不少容貸，貧民賴以吐氣。 滹沱河水為真定害，起岩論封河神為侯爵，而移文責之，復修其提防，瀹其湮鬱，水患遂息。 升江南行台御史中丞，拜翰林學士承旨、知制誥兼修國史、知經筵事。 右丞相別裡怯不花為台臣所糾，去位。 未幾再入相，諷詞臣言台章之非，起岩執不可，聞者壯之。 俄拜御史中丞，論事剴直，無所顧忌，與上官多不合。 詔修遼、金、宋三史，復命入翰林為承旨，充總裁官，積階至榮祿大夫。 起岩熟於金源典故，宋儒道學源委，尤多究心，史官有露才自是者，每立言未當，起岩據理竄定，深厚醇雅，理致自足。 史成，年始六十有五，遂上疏乞骸骨以歸，後四年卒。 諡曰文穆。
When Emperor Wenzong sacrificed at the suburbs, Qiyan was grand master of rites, guiding the emperor's ascent and descent with measured steps and even hanging robes; officials in attendance thought him a figure stepped from an old painting. The emperor praised him highly and gave him generous gifts. He was made secretariat counselor. After Emperor Ningzong's death a great case erupted in Yan South: a madman reported that a branch commissioner plotted treason; inquiry found it false, and the law officers cited the Tang Code: informers on rebellion are not punished in return. " Qiyan urged his colleagues: "The heir is not yet enthroned and minds are uneasy; unless we execute this man at once to stop conspiracy, the great design may suffer." " He pressed for a completed verdict; the capital grew quiet and the crisis passed. During a secretariat session on appointments Qiyan recommended a capable man; the chancellor took offense and Qiyan rose and left; the chancellor deemed it defiance. He became Hanlin expositor, draftsman, and national history compiler, edited the veritable records of three reigns, and was made associate classics mat lecturer. The censorate proposed him surveillance commissioner of Western Zhe, but the court refused. Soon he was promoted to attending censor on the Shaanxi branch secretariat. About to leave, he was kept on again as expositor. He was made attending censor on the Jiangnan branch, summoned to the central censorate, and confirmed as attending censor. He became surveillance commissioner of Yan South. He prosecuted the powerful without mercy, and the poor at last had voice. The Hutuo flooded Zhending; Qiyan opposed ennobling the river god as marquis, sent a rebuking dispatch, repaired the dikes and dredged the silt, and the floods ceased. He rose to vice censor-in-chief on the Jiangnan branch, Hanlin recipient of edicts, draftsman, national history compiler, and classics mat lecturer. Right chancellor Bayan Qubilad was impeached by the censors and left office. Soon he returned as chancellor and urged the draftsmen to say the censorial charge was wrong; Qiyan refused—listeners admired his stand. He was soon made vice censor-in-chief, spoke sharply and without fear, and often clashed with superiors. Ordered to compile the Liao, Jin, and Song histories, he re-entered the Hanlin as recipient of edicts and chief compiler, rising to grand master for glorious happiness. He knew Jin precedents and Song Neo-Confucian lineage deeply; when compilers vaunted talent and missed the mark, he revised by reason—writing thick, mellow, and self-sufficient. When the histories were finished he was sixty-five; he asked to retire and died four years later. He was posthumously titled Wenmu.
5
起岩面如紫瓊，美髯方頤，而眉目清揚可觀，望而知為雅量君子。 及其臨政決議，意所背鄉，屹若泰山，不可回奪。 或時面折人，面頸發赤，不少恕，廟堂憚之。 識者謂其外和中剛，不受人籠絡，如歐陽修，名聞四裔。 安南修貢，其陪臣致其世子之辭，必候起岩起居。 性孝友，少處窮約，下帷教授，躬致米百里外，以養父母； 撫弟如石，教之宦學，無不備至。 舉親族弗克葬者二十餘喪，且買田以給其祭。 凡獲俸賜，必與故人賓客共之。 卒之日，廩無餘粟，家無餘財。
His face was like purple jade, with a fine beard and square jaw, yet bright brows and eyes—at a glance a man of large spirit. In council, whatever he opposed stood like Mount Tai and could not be swayed. Sometimes he rebuked men to their faces until he flushed crimson, never sparing; the court stood in awe. Observers said he was mild outside and firm within, not to be coerced—like Ouyang Xiu—and his fame reached the four quarters. When Annam sent tribute, its envoys presenting the crown prince's message always inquired after Qiyan's health first. Filial and brotherly by nature, he lived poor in youth, taught behind closed curtains, and walked a hundred li to bring rice for his parents; he raised his younger brother steadfastly, schooling him for office and learning without stint. He buried more than twenty kinsmen who could not afford funerals and bought fields for their sacrifices. Whatever salary or gifts he received he shared with old friends and guests. When he died the granary held no surplus grain and the house no surplus wealth.
6
先是，至元乙酉三月乙亥，太史奏文昌星明，文運將興。 時世祖行幸上京，明日丙子，皇孫降生於儒州。 是夜，起岩亦生。 其後皇孫踐祚，是為仁宗，始詔設科取士，及廷試，起岩遂為第一人，論者以為非偶然也。 起岩博學有文，善篆、隸，有《華峰漫稿》、《華峰類稿》、《金陵集》各若干卷，藏於家。 子二人：琳，琛。
Earlier, on the third month's yihai day of Zhiyuan's yiyou year, the grand astrologer reported Wenchang bright and literary fortune rising. Shizu was then at Shangdu; next day, bingzi, the imperial grandson was born at Ruzhou. That same night Qiyan was born. The grandson later reigned as Emperor Renzong, restored examinations, and at the palace test Qiyan ranked first—commentators called it no accident. He was learned and literary, skilled in seal and clerical script, and left Huafeng Miscellany, Huafeng Classified Drafts, and Jinling Collection in several volumes at home. He had two sons: Lin and Chen.

○歐陽玄
○ Ouyang Xuan
8
歐陽玄，字原功，其先家廬陵，與文忠公修同所自出。 至曾大父新，始遷居瀏陽，故玄為瀏陽人。 幼岐嶷，母李氏，親授《孝經》、《論語》、小學諸書，八歲能成誦，始從鄉先生張貫之學，日記數千言，即知屬文。 十歲，有黃冠師注目視玄，謂貫之曰：「是兒神氣凝遠，目光射人，異日當以文章冠世，廊廟之器也。 」言訖而去，亟追與語，已失所之。 部使者行縣，玄以諸生見，命賦梅花詩，立成十首，晚歸，增至百首，見者駭異之。 年十四，益從宋故老習為詞章，下筆輒成章，每試庠序，輒佔高等。 弱冠，下帷數年，人莫見其面。 經史百家，靡不研究，伊、洛諸儒源委，尤為淹貫。
Ouyang Xuan, styled Yuangong, came from Luling, the same line as Ouyang Xiu the Duke of Letters. His great-great-grandfather Xin first moved the family to Liuyang, so Xuan was counted a Liuyang man. Precocious as a boy, his mother Lady Li taught him the Filial Classic, Analects, Elementary Learning, and more; at eight he recited them by heart; under Zhang Guan he wrote thousands of words a day and soon composed essays. At ten a Daoist priest stared at Xuan and told Guan, "The boy's spirit is far and keen, his gaze piercing; one day his writing will crown the age—he is hall-and-temple timber." " He left at once; they ran to speak with him but he was gone. When the branch commissioner visited the county, Xuan as a student was told to write plum-blossom poems and finished ten at once; that evening he raised them to a hundred and astonished onlookers. At fourteen he studied composition with Song veterans; his brush flowed into chapters; in every school examination he took top place. At twenty he shut his door for years and scarcely showed his face. Classics and histories of every school he studied; the Yi and Luo masters he knew through and through.
9
召為國子博士，升國子監丞。 致和元年，遷翰林待制，兼國史院編修官。 時當兵興，玄領印攝院事，日直內廷，參決機務，凡遠近調發，制詔書檄。 既而改元天歷，郊廟、建後、立儲、肆赦之文，皆經撰述。 复條時政數十事，實封以聞，多推行之。 明年，初置奎章閣學士院，又置藝文監隸焉，皆選清望官居之，文宗親署玄為藝文少監。 奉詔纂修《經世大典》，升太監、檢校書籍事。 元統元年，改僉太常禮儀院事，拜翰林直學士，編修四朝實錄，俄兼國子祭酒，召赴中都議事，升侍講學士，复兼國子祭酒。 重紀至元五年，足患風痺，乞南歸以便醫藥，帝不允。 拜翰林學士，未幾，懇辭去位，帝復不允，免其行朝賀禮。 至正改元，更張朝政，事有不便者，集議廷中，玄極言無隱，科目之复，沮者尤眾，玄尤力爭之。 未幾南歸，復起為翰林學士，以疾未行。
He was summoned as national university erudite and promoted vice director of the directorate of education. In Zhihe 1 he became Hanlin attendant draftsman and historiography compiler. War had broken out; Xuan took charge of the historiography institute, attended court daily, and decided urgent business—all dispatches, edicts, and proclamations. When the era became Tianli, he drafted the suburban rites, empress installation, heir establishment, and amnesty. He listed dozens of policy reforms, sealed and submitted them; many were adopted. Next year the Kui Zhang academy was founded with an arts bureau under it, staffed by men of standing; Wenzong personally appointed Xuan vice director of arts. Ordered to compile the Comprehensive Institutions, he rose to grand director and proofreader of books. In Yuantong 1 he became vice director of imperial sacrifices, Hanlin direct academician, compiler of four reigns' veritable records, then also chancellor of the national university, was summoned to the central capital, made expositor, and again chancellor of the university. In Chongji Zhizheng 5 his legs were paralyzed by wind; he asked to go south for treatment and the emperor refused. Made Hanlin academician, he soon begged to resign; the emperor again refused but excused him from congratulatory audiences. At the Zhengzheng renewal, matters disputed in court drew his blunt speech; on restoring civil examinations opposition was fierce and he argued hardest. Soon he went south, was recalled as Hanlin academician, but illness kept him from traveling.
10
詔修遼、金、宋三史，召為總裁官，發凡舉例，俾論撰者有所據依。 史官中有悻悻露才、論議不公者，玄不以口舌爭，俟其呈稿，援筆竄定之，統係自正。 至於論、贊、表、奏，皆玄屬筆。 五年，帝以玄歷仕累朝，且有修三史功，諭旨丞相，超授爵秩，遂擬拜翰林學士承旨。 及入奏，上稱快者再三。 已而乞致仕，帝復不允。 御史臺奏除福建廉訪使，行次浙西，疾復作，乃上休致之請，作南山隱居，優游山水之間，有終焉之志。 復拜翰林學士承旨，玄屢力辭，不獲命。 奉敕定國律，尋乞致仕，陳情懇切，乃特授湖廣行中書省右丞致仕，賜白玉束帶，給俸賜以終其身。 將行，帝复降旨不允，仍前翰林學士承旨，進階光祿大夫。 十四年，汝潁盜起，蔓延南北，州縣幾無完城。 玄獻招捕之策千餘言，鑿鑿可行，當時不能用。 十七年春，乞致仕，以中原道梗，欲由蜀還鄉，帝復不允。 時將大赦天下，宣赴內府。 玄久病，不能步履，丞相傳旨，肩輿至延春閣下，實異數也。 是歲十二月戊戌，卒於崇教里之寓舍，年八十五。 中書以聞，帝賜賻甚厚，贈崇仁昭德推忠守正功臣、大司徒、柱國，追封楚國公，諡曰文。
Ordered to compile the Liao, Jin, and Song histories, he was chief compiler, laid down principles, and gave the writers a standard. Some compilers were resentful and unfair; Xuan did not argue but revised their drafts until the lineage stood straight. Discussions, eulogies, tables, and memorials were all his pen. In year 5 the emperor, noting his service through many reigns and compiling the three histories, told the chancellor to advance him beyond rule and proposed him recipient of edicts. When he reported in audience, the emperor expressed delight again and again. He soon asked to retire and the emperor again refused. The censorate proposed him Fujian surveillance commissioner; in Western Zhe his illness returned; he asked to retire, built South Mountain Hermitage, and meant to end his days among hills and streams. Again made recipient of edicts, Xuan repeatedly declined but could not refuse. Ordered to fix the national code, he then begged retirement in earnest terms and was specially made right vice administrator of the Huguang branch in retirement, with white jade belt and salary for life. As he was leaving the emperor again refused, kept him recipient of edicts, and advanced him to grand master for imperial brightness. In year 14 bandits rose in Ru and Ying and spread; few prefectures and counties kept intact walls. Xuan submitted a thousand-word plan to recruit and suppress rebels, sound and feasible, but it went unused. In spring of year 17 he asked to retire; central roads were blocked and he wished to go home through Shu; the emperor again refused. A great amnesty was imminent and he was ordered to the inner palace. Long ill and unable to walk, he was carried by sedan to the Yanchun Pavilion by the chancellor's order—an exceptional honor. On the twelfth month's wuxu day he died at his Chongjiao Lane lodging, aged eighty-five. The secretariat reported his death; the emperor gave lavish funeral gifts, enfeoffed him grand preceptor and pillar of the state, duke of Chu with posthumous title Wen.
11
玄性度雍容，含弘縝密，處己儉約，為政廉平。 歷官四十餘年，在朝之日，殆四之三。 三任成均，而兩為祭酒，六入翰林，而三拜承旨。 修實錄、《大典》、三史，皆大製作。 屢主文衡，兩知貢舉及讀卷官，凡宗廟朝廷雄文大冊、播告萬方制誥，多出玄手。 金繒上尊之賜，幾無虛歲。 海內名山大川，釋、老之宮，王公貴人墓隧之碑，得玄文辭以為榮。 片言隻字，流傳人間，咸知寶重。 文章道德，卓然名世。 羽儀斯文，贊衛治具，與有功焉。 玄無子，以從子達老後，复先玄卒。 有《圭齋文集》若干卷，傳於世。
Xuan was dignified and magnanimous, meticulous in counsel, frugal in private life, and impartial in office. He served over forty years, spending nearly three-quarters of that time at court. Thrice he led the directorate of education and twice was its chancellor; six times he entered the Hanlin and thrice became recipient of edicts. Editing veritable records, the Comprehensive Institutions, and the three histories were all major works. He often chaired examinations, twice oversaw recruitment and reading papers; state liturgies and edicts to the realm largely came from his pen. Imperial gifts of gold and silk scarcely missed a year. Mountains, rivers, Buddhist and Daoist shrines, and noble tomb inscriptions prized his writing as an honor. Even a line or a character of his, once abroad, was treasured. In literature and conduct he stood eminent in his age. He adorned letters, upheld governance, and shared in its achievement. Childless, he adopted his nephew Dala as heir, who predeceased him. His Guizhai Collected Works in several volumes circulated in the world.

○許有壬
○ Ouyang Xuan
13
許有壬，字可用，其先世居潁，後徙湯陰。 有壬幼穎悟，讀書一目五行，嘗閱衡州《淨居院碑》，文近千言，一覽輒背誦無遺。 年二十，暢師文薦入翰林，不報，授開寧路學正，升教授，未上，辟山北廉訪司書吏。 擢延祐二年進士第，授同知遼州事。 會關中有警，鄰州聽民出避，棄孩嬰滿道上，有壬獨率弓箭手，閉城門以守，卒獲無虞。 州有追逮，不許胥隸足跡至村疃，唯給信牌，令執裡役者呼之，民安而事集。 右族貪虐者懲之，冤獄雖有成案，皆平翻而釋其罪，州遂大治。 六年己未，除山北​​廉訪司經歷。 至治元年，遷吏部主事。 二年，轉江南行台監察御史，行部廣東，以貪墨劾罷廉訪副使哈只蔡衍。 至江西，會廉訪使苗好謙監焚昏鈔，檢視鈔者日至百餘人，好謙恐其有弊，痛鞭之。 人畏罪，率剔真為偽，以迎其意。 筦庫吏而下，榜掠無全膚，迄莫能償。 有壬覆視之，率真物也，遂釋之。 凡勢官豪民，人畏之如虎狼者，有壬悉擒治以法，部內肅然。 召拜監察御史。
Xu Youren, styled Keyong, came from Ying and later Tangyin. As a boy he was brilliant, reading five lines at a glance; he once read the thousand-word Hengzhou Jingju Monastery Stele and recited it entire after a single pass. At twenty Chang Shiwen recommended him to the Hanlin without result; he became Kaining circuit instructor, then professor, but before reporting was recruited as Shanbei surveillance clerk. He passed the jinshi in Yanyou 2 and became associate administrator of Liaozhou. When Guanzhong alarmed, neighbors let people flee and abandoned infants littered the roads; Youren alone led archers, shut the gates, and kept the city safe. For arrests he barred clerks from villages, issued tokens, and had village officers summon suspects—keeping the people calm and business orderly. He punished predatory clans, reversed settled wrongful cases, and brought the prefecture to good order. In year 6, jiwei, he became Shanbei surveillance commissioner. In Zhizhi 1 he became director of personnel. In year 2 he became Jiangnan investigating censor, toured Guangdong, and impeached vice surveillance commissioner Haizhi Caiyan for corruption. In Jiangxi he found surveillance commissioner Miao Haoqian burning worn notes; over a hundred inspectors came daily, and fearing fraud Miao flogged them harshly. Terrified of punishment, they often stripped genuine notes to forge false ones to please him. Treasury clerks down were beaten until raw and still could not make restitution. Youren re-examined the notes, found them genuine, and released the men. Powerful bullies he seized and punished by law until the circuit was quiet. He was summoned as investigating censor.
14
三年八月，英宗暴崩於南坡，賊臣鐵失遣使者自上京至，封府庫，收百官印，有壬知事急，即往告御史中丞董守庸，守庸謂宮禁事，非子所當問。 有壬即疏守庸及經歷朵爾只班、監察御史郭也先忽都阿附鐵失之罪以俟。 十月，鐵失伏誅。 泰定帝發上都，御史大夫紐澤先還京師，有壬即袖疏上之。 及帝至，复上章言：「帖木迭兒之子瑣南，與聞大逆，乞賜典刑。 其兄弟勿令出入宮禁。 中書平章政事王毅、右丞高昉，橫罹奪爵，而四川行省平章政事趙世延，受禍尤慘，皆請雪冤復職。 」繼上正始十事：一曰輔翼太子，宜先訓導； 二曰遴選長官，宜先培養； 三曰通籍宮禁，宜別貴賤； 四曰欲謹兵權，宜削兼領； 五曰武備廢弛，宜加修飭； 六曰賊臣妻妾，宜禁勢官徵索； 七曰前赦權以止變，宜再詔以正名； 八曰帖木迭兒諸子，宜籍沒以懲惡； 九曰考驗經費，以減民賦； 十曰撙節浮蠹，以紓國用。 帝多從之。
In month 8 of year 3 Emperor Yingzong died suddenly at Nanpo; traitor Teshi sent envoys from Shangdu, sealed treasuries, and seized official seals. Youren warned vice censor-in-chief Dong Shouyong, who said palace affairs were none of his business. Youren memorialized Shouyong, commissioner Duoerzhiban, and censor Guo Yexian Hudou for siding with Teshi, to await judgment. In the tenth month Teshi was executed. Emperor Taiding left Shangdu; censor-in-chief Niuzhe returned first to the capital and Youren submitted a memorial from his sleeve. When the emperor arrived he memorialized again: "Temuder's son Suonan shared the great treason—grant him statutory punishment. His brothers must not enter or leave the palace precincts. Administrator Wang Yi and right administrator Gao Fang were wrongly disgraced, while Sichuan administrator Zhao Shiyan suffered worse—all sought rehabilitation. " He then submitted ten reform items: first, instruct the heir apparent before all else; second, cultivate chief officials before appointing them; third, distinguish ranks for palace registry; fourth, to tighten military authority, end concurrent commands; fifth, restore neglected military preparations; sixth, forbid powerful men from seizing traitors' wives; seventh, follow the expedient amnesty with a new edict to set names right; eighth, confiscate Temuder's sons' property to punish wickedness; ninth, audit expenditures to lighten taxes; tenth, cut waste to ease state finances. The emperor largely accepted them.
15
泰定元年，初立詹事院，選為中議，改中書左司員外郎。 京畿飢，有壬請賑之。 同列讓曰：「子言固善，其如虧國何！ 」有壬曰：「不然。 民，本也，不虧民，顧豈虧國邪！ 」卒白於丞相，發糧四十萬斛濟之，民賴以活者甚眾。 國學舊法，每以積分次第貢以出官，執政用監丞張起岩議，欲廢之，而以推擇德行為務。 有壬折之曰：「積分雖未盡善，然可得博學能文之士，若曰惟德行之擇，其名固佳，恐皆厚貌深情，專意外飾，或懵不能識丁矣。 」議久不決。 三年六月，升右司郎中，其事遂行，已而復寢。 獲盜例有賞，論者多疑其偽，有淹四十餘年者，群訴於馬首，有壬曰：「盜賊方熾，求疵太甚，緩急何以使人！ 但經部使者覆核者，皆予官。 」俄移左司郎中，每遇公議，有壬屢爭事得失，汛掃積滯，幾無留牘。 都事宋本退語人曰：「此貞觀、開元間議事也。 」明年，丁父憂。
In Taiding 1, when the heir's household was founded, he became middle counselor, then left-section vice director of the secretariat. When the capital region starved, Youren sought relief. Colleagues objected: "Your plan is good, but what of the state's loss!" " Youren said, "Not so. The people are the root; harm not the people and the state cannot be harmed!" " He persuaded the chancellor to issue four hundred thousand hu of grain; multitudes lived by it. The university advanced students by accumulated points for office; the administration, following Zhang Qiyan, wished to abolish points and select only by moral conduct. Youren objected: "Points are imperfect yet yield learned writers; moral selection sounds fine but may produce hypocrites or men who cannot read." " Debate dragged on. In month 6 of year 3 he became right-section director; the reform briefly took effect, then lapsed. Rewards for captured bandits were often doubted as fraud; some cases lingered forty years. Youren said, "Bandits rage now—nitpicking defeats urgency!" Those rechecked by branch commissioners he rewarded with office. " Soon left-section director; in council he argued every point, cleared backlog, and left almost no pending papers. Director Song Ben said afterward, "This is council like Zhenguan and Kaiyuan." " The next year he mourned his father.
16
天曆三年，擢兩淮都轉運鹽司使。 先是，鹽法壞，廷議非有壬不能集事，故有是命。 有壬詢究弊端，立法而通融之，國課遂登。 至順二年二月，召參議中書省事，未幾，以丁母憂去。 元統元年，復以參議召。 明年甲戌，拜治書侍御史，轉奎章閣學士院侍書學士，仍治台事。 會福達魯花赤完卜，藉丞相勢，宿衛東宮，其行頗淫穢，御史劾之，完卜藏御史大夫家，有壬捕而遣之。 九月，拜中書參知政事、知經筵事。 帝詔群臣議上皇太后尊號為太皇太后，有壬曰：「皇上於皇太后，母子也，若加太皇太后，則為孫矣，非禮也。 」眾弗之從，有壬曰：「今制，封贈祖父母，降於父母一等，蓋推恩之法，近重而遠輕，今尊皇太后為太皇太后，是推而遠之，乃反輕矣，豈所謂尊之者邪！ 」弗之聽。 中書平章政事徹理帖木兒挾私憾，奏罷進士科，有壬廷爭甚苦不能奪，遂稱疾在告，帝強起之，拜侍御史。 會汝寧棒胡反，大臣有忌漢官者，取賊所造旗幟及偽宣敕，班地上，問曰：「此欲何為耶？ 」意漢官諱言反，將以罪中之。 有壬曰：「此曹建年號，稱李老君太子，部署士卒，以敵官軍，其反狀甚明，尚何言！ 」其語遂塞。 廷議欲行古劓法，立行樞密院，禁漢人、南人勿學蒙古、畏吾兒字書，有壬皆爭止之。
In Tianli 3 he became Lianghuai salt transport commissioner. The salt law had collapsed; court opinion held only Youren could fix it, hence the appointment. He investigated abuses, legislated flexibly, and state salt revenue rose. In Zhishun 2, month 2, he was summoned as secretariat counselor, then left to mourn his mother. In Yuantong 1 he was again summoned as counselor. Next year, jiaxu, he became investigating censor-in-chief, Kui Zhang expositor, and still ran censorate business. Darughachi Wandu, backed by the chancellor, guarded the eastern palace licentiously; censors impeached him; he hid with the censor-in-chief; Youren seized and expelled him. In month 9 he became secretariat administrator and classics mat lecturer. Ordered to raise the empress dowager to grand empress dowager, Youren said, "Mother and son would become grandson and elder—against ritual." " Overruled, he said, "Enfeoffing grandparents one rank below parents extends grace outward; making her grand empress dowager pushes honor away and lightens it—how is that honoring?" " They would not listen. Administrator Cherig Temur abolished the jinshi; Youren argued bitterly in vain, feigned illness, and was forced up as attending censor. When Runing's Bang Hu rebelled, ministers hostile to Han officials spread rebel banners and forged edicts on the floor and asked, "What do you make of this?" " They meant to trap Han officials who shrank from saying "rebellion." Youren said, "They proclaimed a reign title, called themselves Lao's crown prince, and fought the army—the rebellion is obvious!" " Their trap failed. Council proposed nose-cutting, a mobile privy council, and barring Han and southerners from Mongol and Uighur script; Youren stopped all.
17
重紀至元初，長蘆韓公溥因家藏兵器，遂起大獄，株連台若省，多以贓敗，獨無有壬名，由是忌者益甚。 有壬度不可留，遂歸彰德，已而南游湘、漢間。 至元六年，召入中書，仍為參知政事。 明年，改元至正，有壬極論帝當親祠太廟，母后虛位，徽政院當罷，改元命相當合為上詔，冗職當沙汰，錢糧當裁節，如此之類，不一而足。 人皆韙之。 轉中書左丞。 二年，囊加慶善八及孛羅帖木兒獻議，開西山金口導渾河，逾京城，達通州，以通漕運。 丞相脫脫主之甚力，有壬曰：「渾河之水，湍悍易決，而足以為害，淤淺易塞，而不可行舟； 況地勢高下，甚有不同，徒勞民費財耳。 」不聽，後卒如有壬言。
Early in Chongji Zhizheng, Han Gongpu of Changlu raised a weapons case implicating censorate and secretariat; many fell for graft, but not Youren—resentment deepened. Seeing he could not stay, he returned to Zhangde, then traveled through Xiang and Han. In Zhiyuan 6 he was summoned to the secretariat again as administrator. Next year, at the Zhengzheng change, he urged personal temple sacrifice, filling the empress's seat, abolishing the Huizheng Court, merging edicts, purging offices, and cutting revenue—among many reforms. All approved. He became left secretariat administrator. In year 2 Nanggasanba and Bolu Temur proposed opening West Mountain's Jinkou to divert the Hun River through the capital to Tongzhou for grain transport. Chancellor Toqtoq backed it fiercely. Youren said, "The Hun is violent and prone to burst, shallow and prone to silt, unfit for shipping; and the terrain varies—this only wastes labor and treasure." " They refused; events proved him right.
18
先是，有壬之父熙載仕長沙日，設義學，訓諸生。 既歿，而諸生思之，為立東岡書院，朝廷賜額設官，以為育才之地。 南台監察御史木八剌沙，緣睚眥怨，言書院不當立，並構浮辭，誣衊有壬，併其二弟有儀、有孚，有壬遂稱病歸。 四年，改江浙行省左丞，辭。 六年，召為翰林學士，既上，又辭。 監察御史累章辨其誣。 俄拜浙西廉訪使，未上，復以翰林學士承旨召，仍知經筵事。 明年夏，授御史中丞，賜白玉束帶及御衣一襲，未幾，復以病歸。 監察御史答蘭不花銜有壬，時短長之，奏劾甚力，事尋白。
Earlier, his father Xizai at Changsha founded a charity school. After his death students built Donggang Academy; the court granted a plaque and officials to nurture talent. Southern censor Mubalasha, grudging him, attacked the academy and slandered Youren and brothers Youyi and Youfu; Youren retired ill. In year 4 he was offered Jiangzhe left administrator and declined. In year 6 he was summoned Hanlin academician, reported, and declined again. Censors repeatedly cleared the slander. Soon made Western Zhe surveillance commissioner, but before reporting was recalled as Hanlin recipient of edicts and classics mat lecturer. Next summer he became vice censor-in-chief with white jade belt and imperial robes, then soon retired ill again. Censor Dalan Buhua impeached him fiercely from resentment; the charge soon failed.
19
十二年，盜起河南，聲撼河朔間，有壬畫備御之策十五條，以授郡將，民藉以安。 十三年，起拜河南行省左丞。 朝廷遣將出征，環河南境，連營以百數，一切芻餉，皆仰給之，有壬從容集事，若平時然。 十五年，遷集賢大學士，尋改樞密副使，復拜中書左丞。 時以言為諱，有壬力言朝廷務行姑息之政，賞重罰輕，故將士貪掠子女玉帛而無鬥志，遂倡招降之策，言多不載。 有僧名開，自高郵來，言張士誠乞降，眾幸事且成，皆大喜，有壬獨疑其妄，呼僧詰之，果語塞不能對。 轉集賢大學士，兼太子左諭德，階至光祿大夫。 有壬前朝舊德，太子頗敬禮之。 一日入見，方臂鷙禽以為樂，遽呼左右屏去。 十七年，以老病，力乞致其事，久之始得請，給俸賜以終其身。 二十四年九月二十一日卒，年七十八。
In year 12 Henan rebels shook Hebei; Youren drafted fifteen defense measures for commanders, and the people were secured. In year 13 he was raised left administrator of the Henan branch secretariat. The court sent armies around Henan in hundreds of camps; all fodder depended on him, yet he managed affairs as calmly as in peacetime. In year 15 he became grand academician of the Hall of Gathered Talents, then vice privy council minister, then again left secretariat administrator. Speech was dangerous; he argued the court's indulgence—heavy rewards, light punishments—left troops plundering without fight, and urged surrender policies, much unrecorded. A monk named Kai from Gaoyou claimed Zhang Shicheng would surrender; all rejoiced, but Youren doubted him, questioned him, and the monk could not answer. He became grand academician, left tutor to the heir apparent, and grand master for imperial brightness. An elder of the prior reign, he was greatly respected by the heir apparent. Once when he came to audience, the heir was sporting a hawk on his arm and hastily had it taken away. In year 17, aged and ill, he begged hard to retire; after long delay he was granted salary for life. On the twenty-first day of the ninth month of year 24 he died, aged seventy-eight.
20
有壬歷事七朝，垂五十年，遇國家大事，無不盡言，皆一根至理，而曲盡人情。 當權臣恣睢之時，稍忤意，輒誅竄隨之，有壬絕不為巧避計，事有不便，明辨力諍，不知有死生利害，君子多之。 有壬善筆札，工辭章，歐陽玄序其文，謂其雄渾閎雋，湧如層瀾，迫而求之，則淵靚深實，蓋深許之也。 所著有《至正集》若干卷。 諡曰文忠。 子一人，曰禎。
Serving seven reigns for nearly fifty years, on every great state matter he spoke fully—grounded in principle yet mindful of human feeling. When ministers were overbearing, slight offense meant death or exile; he never dodged craftily, argued bluntly heedless of life or profit, and gentlemen praised him. Skilled in letters and composition, Ouyang Xuan praised his writing as vast and surging yet deep and solid when examined closely. He left the "Zhengzheng Collection" in several volumes. He was posthumously titled Wenzhong. He had one son, Zhen.

22
宋本，字誠夫，大都人。 自幼穎拔異群兒，既成童，聚經史窮日夜讀之，句探字索，必通貫乃已。 嘗從父禎官江陵，​​江陵王奎文，明性命義理之學，本往質所得，造詣日深。 善為古文，辭必己出，峻潔刻厲，多微辭。 年四十，始還燕。 至治元年，策天下士於廷，本為第一人，賜進士及第，授翰林修撰。 泰定元年春，除監察御史，首言：「逆賊鐵失等雖伏誅，其黨樞密副使阿散，身親弒逆，以告變得不死，竄嶺南，乞早正天討。 」國製，範黃金為太廟神主，仁宗室盜竟竊去，本言：「在法，民間失盜，捕之違期不獲猶治罪，太常失典守，及在京應捕官，皆當罷去。 」又言：「中書宰執，日趨禁中，固寵苟安，兼旬不至中堂，壅滯機務，乞戒飭臣僚，自非入宿衛日，必詣所署治事。 」皆不報。
Song Ben, styled Chengfu, was from Dadu. From boyhood he outshone other children; as a youth he read classics and histories day and night until he mastered every line. Following his father Zhen to Jiangling, he studied nature and moral principle under Wang Kuiwen and deepened daily. He wrote ancient-style prose in his own voice—severe, clean, incisive, often subtly allusive. At forty he first returned to Yan. In Zhizhi 1 he ranked first in the palace examination, received jinshi with honors, and became Hanlin compiler. In Taiding 1 spring he became investigating censor and first urged: "Though Teshi was executed, his partisan Asan joined the regicide yet escaped death by informing—punish him at once." " National law cast golden spirit tablets for the Grand Temple; Renzong's tablet was stolen. Ben said arrest officers and Court of Imperial Sacrifices custodians must be dismissed, as with overdue theft cases." " He also said secretariat chiefs lingered in the palace half a month without reaching the central hall, blocking business—officials except on guard duty must attend their offices." " All went unanswered.
23
踰月，調國子監丞。 夏，風烈地震，有旨集百官雜議弭災之道。 時宿衛士自北方來者，復遣歸，乃百十為群，剽劫殺人桓州道中。 既逮捕，旭滅傑奏釋之。 蒙古千戶使京師​​，宿邸中，適民間硃甲妻女車過邸門，千戶悅之，並從者奪以入，硃泣訴於中書，旭滅傑庇不問。 本適與議，本復抗言：「鐵失餘黨未誅，仁廟神主盜未得，桓州盜未治，硃甲冤未伸，刑政失度，民憤天怨，災異之見，職此之由。 」辭氣激奮，眾皆聳聽。 冬，移兵部員外郎。 二年，轉中書左司都事。 會議招撫溪洞民，故將李牢山之子嘗假兵部尚書，從諸王帥兵徵鬱林州徭民，李在道納妾，留不進，兵敗歸，樞密副使王卜鄰吉台言：「李平徭有功，當遷官。 」本言； 「李棄軍娶妾，逗撓軍期，宜亟置諸法，況可官邪！ 」王色沮，乃不敢言。
A month later he became vice director of the directorate of education. That summer fierce wind and earthquake struck; the court gathered officials to discuss quelling disaster. Palace guards returning from the north were sent back and banded in hundreds, plundering and killing on the Huanzhou road. After arrest, Xumiejie memorialized to release them. A Mongol chiliarch at an inn seized commoner Zhu Jia's wife and daughter from a passing cart; Zhu appealed to the secretariat but Xumiejie shielded him. Ben argued: "Teshi's partisans remain, the tablet thief is uncaught, Huanzhou bandits unpunished, Zhu's wrong unredressed—hence prodigies and popular wrath." " All listened in awe. In winter he became vice director of war. In year 2 he became left-section director of the secretariat. Discussing pacifying stream-cave peoples, Li Laoshan's son had borrowed the war minister title, dallied with a concubine, lost the Yao campaign, yet Wang Bulingjitai urged promoting him for pacifying the Yao. " Ben said: "Li abandoned the army for a concubine and delayed the schedule—execute him by law, not promote him!" " Wang fell silent.
24
旭滅傑死，左丞相倒剌沙當國得君，與平章政事烏伯都剌，皆西域人，西域富賈以其國異石名曰瓓者來獻，其估巨萬，或未酬其直； 諸嘗有過，為司憲褫官，或有出其門下者。 三年冬，烏伯都剌自禁中出，至政事堂，集宰執僚佐，命左司員外郎胡彝以詔稿示本，乃以星孛地震赦天下，仍命中書酬累朝所獻諸物之直，擢用自英廟至今為憲台奪官者。 本讀竟，白曰：「今警災異，而畏獻物未酬直者憤怨，此有司細故，形諸王言，必貽笑天下。 司憲褫有罪者官，世祖成憲也，今上即位，累詔法世祖，今擢用之，是廢成憲而反汗前詔也，後復有邪佞贓穢者，將治之邪？ 置不問邪？ 」宰執聞本言，相視嘆息罷去。 明日，宣詔竟，本遂稱疾不出。
After Xumiejie's death, Toghto and Ubaidula, both Western Region men, ruled; a Western merchant offered a stone called lan worth tens of thousands, often unpaid; some stripped by the surveillance commission were their clients. Winter of year 3 Ubaidula showed Ben a draft amnesty for comet and earthquake, paying tribute debts and promoting men the censorate had stripped since Yingzong. Ben said fearing unpaid tribute donors in a royal amnesty would make the realm laugh. Stripping corrupt officers was Kublai's law; promoting them now voided that law—would future villains be punished? Or ignored? " The administrators sighed and left. Next day the edict was proclaimed; Ben feigned illness and stayed away.

四年春，遷禮部郎中。 天曆元年冬，升吏部侍郎。 二年，改禮部侍郎。 是年，文宗開奎章閣，置藝文監，檢校書籍，超大監。 至順元年，進奎章閣學士院供奉學士。 二年冬，出為河東廉訪副使，將行，擢禮部尚書。 三年冬，寧宗崩，順帝未至，皇太后在興聖宮，正旦，議循故事，行朝賀禮，本言：「宜上表興聖宮，廢大明殿朝賀。 」眾是而從之。 元統元年，兼經筵官，冬，拜陝西行台治書侍御史，不拜，复留為奎章閣學士院承製學士，仍兼經筵官。 二年夏，轉集賢直學士，兼國子祭酒，兼經筵如故。 是年冬十一月二十五日卒，年五十四。 階官自承務郎十轉至太中大夫。
In spring of year 4 he became director of rites. In Tianli 1 winter he became vice minister of personnel. In year 2 he became vice minister of rites. That year Wenzong opened the Kui Zhang Hall and arts bureau and made him proofreader of books above grand director. In Zhishun 1 he became Kui Zhang attendant academician. In Zhishun 2 winter he was sent as Hedong vice surveillance commissioner but before leaving was made minister of rites. In year 3 winter Ningzong died before Shundi arrived; on New Year's Day Ben urged memorializing Xingsheng Palace and abolishing Daming Hall congratulations. " All agreed. In Yuantong 1 he was classics mat lecturer, offered Shaanxi investigating censor-in-chief but declined, and stayed Kui Zhang drafting academician. In Yuantong 2 summer he became direct academician, national university chancellor, and kept the classics mat. That winter, on the eleventh month's twenty-fifth day, he died at fifty-four. His rank rose from junior service gentleman through ten steps to grand master for court discussion.
26
本性高抗不屈，持論堅正，制行純白，不可干以私，而篤朋友之義，堅若金鐵，人有片善，稱道不少置，尤以植立斯文自任。 知貢舉，取進士滿百人額； 為讀卷官，增第一甲為三人。 父官南中，貧，賣宅以去。 ，居官清慎自持，饘粥至不給。 本未弱冠，聚徒以養親，殆二十年，歷仕通顯，猶僦屋以居。 及卒，非賻贈幾不能給棺斂，執紼者近二千人，皆縉紳大夫、門生故吏及國子諸生，未嘗有一雜賓，時人榮之。 本所著有《至治集》四十卷，行於世。 諡正獻。
Lofty and unyielding, upright and pure, unmoved by private interest, yet devoted in friendship; he praised every slight merit and took establishing letters as his charge. As examiner he filled the hundred jinshi quota; as reader he increased first class to three. When his father served in the south they were poor and sold their house to depart. , in office he lived with pure caution until thin gruel scarcely sufficed. Before twenty he taught to support his parents nearly twenty years; though eminent he still rented his home. At death he scarcely afforded burial without gifts; nearly two thousand gentry, students, and officials bore the bier—no stray guests—and the age honored it. His forty-volume "Zhizhi Collection" circulated in the world. Posthumous title Zhengxian.

○謝端
○ Xie Duan
28
謝端，字敬德，蜀之遂寧人。 宋末，蜀士多避兵江陵，因家焉。 端幼穎異，五六歲能吟詩，十歲能作賦。 弱冠，與尚書宋本同師，明性理，為古文，又同教授江陵城中，以文學齊名，時號「謝宋」。 史槓宣慰荊南，數加延禮，薦之姚燧，燧方以文章大名自負，少所許可，以所為文眎端，端一讀，即能指擿其用意所在，燧嘆獎不已，語人：「後二十年，若謝端者，豈易得哉！ 」用薦者署校官，不報。 科舉法行，就試河南行省，中其舉，以內艱不會試。 延祐五年，乃擢進士乙科。 授承事郎、潭州路同知湘陰州事。 歲滿，入為國子博士，遷太常博士。 盜入太廟，失第八室黃金主，坐罷去。 端禮官，非典守，不當坐，亦不辨。 尋除翰林修撰，升待制，以選為國子司業，遂為翰林直學士，階太中大夫。
Xie Duan, styled Jingde, was from Suining in Shu. Late Song Shu scholars fled war to Jiangling and settled there. Precocious, he recited poetry at five or six and wrote fu at ten. At twenty he studied with Song Ben, mastered Neo-Confucian learning and prose, taught with him in Jiangling, and was famed as "Xie and Song." Shi Geng of Jingnan honored him and recommended him to Yao Sui, who rarely praised anyone; Duan read Sui's essay once and pinpointed its intent; Sui marveled and said another like Duan would be rare in twenty years. " Recommended as school official, he received no appointment. When examinations began he passed the Henan nomination but missed the exam mourning his mother. In Yanyou 5 he finally took jinshi second class. He became junior service gentleman and associate administrator of Xiangyin on Tanzhou circuit. After his term he became national university erudite, then court of imperial sacrifices erudite. Bandits stole the eighth shrine's golden tablet from the Grand Temple; he was dismissed. A ritual officer, not a custodian, he should not have been punished, yet he did not argue. Soon Hanlin compiler, attendant draftsman, national university vice chancellor, then direct academician at grand master for court discussion.
29
端善為政，筮仕湘陰，猾吏束手，不敢舞文法，豪民無賴者遠避去。 部使者行部，旁郡滯訟，皆諉端讞，端剖決如流，績譽籍然。 其文章嚴謹有法，寧約近瘠，無奢滋駁。 居翰林久，至順、元統以來，國家崇號，慈極升祔先朝，加封宣聖考妣，制冊多出其手。 預修文宗、明宗、寧宗三朝實錄，及累朝功臣列傳，時稱其有史才。 初，文宗建奎章閣，搜羅中外才俊置其中，嘗語阿榮曰：「當今文學之士，朕惟未識謝端。 」亡何，文宗崩，竟不及用端。 端又與趙郡蘇天爵同著《正統論》，辨金、宋正統甚悉，世多傳之。 至元六年卒，年六十二。 元世蜀士以文名者，曰虞集，而謝端其次云。
In office at Xiangyin crafty clerks dared not twist law and ruffians fled. Neighboring prefectures referred stalled suits to him; he decided them swiftly and won renown. His writing was strict and lean, without excess. Long in the Hanlin, he drafted state honorifics, empress installations, and Confucius enfeoffments from Zhishun onward. He helped compile veritable records of Wenzong, Mingzong, and Ningzong and meritorious biographies, praised for historiographical talent. When Wenzong founded the Kui Zhang Hall he told Arong he had not yet met Xie Duan among literary men. " Soon Wenzong died and never employed him. With Su Tianjue he wrote the "Discourse on Legitimate Succession" on Jin and Song legitimacy, widely transmitted. In Zhiyuan 6 he died at sixty-two. Among Yuan Shu men famed for letters, Yu Ji was first and Xie Duan second.
