1
鐵哥朮
Tiegezhu
2
鐵哥朮，高昌人。 世居五城，後徙京師。 曾祖父達釋，有謀略，為國人所信服。 太祖西征，高昌國主懼，以錦衣、白貂帽召達釋與謀。 達釋知天命有歸，勸其主執贄稱臣，以安其國，由是號為尚書。 太祖班師，諸王言於帝曰：「達釋之子野里朮驍勇善戰，所將部落又強大。 聞其人每思率衆效順而未有機便，盍致之乎？ 」太祖是其議，即詔給驛馬五百，迎與俱來。 既至，引見，甚器重之。 丙午，太祖西征，野里朮別從親王按只台與敵戰有功，甚見親遇。 王方以絳蓋障日而坐，及聞野里朮議事，喜見顏色，稱善久之，既退，撒其蓋送之十里。 遂得兼長四環衛之必闍赤。 壬辰，從國兵討金，以戰功最多，賞賚優渥。 甲午，副忽都虎籍漢戶口，籌其賦役，分諸功臣以地，人服其敏。
Tiegezhu was a native of Gaochang. His family had long lived at Wucheng before relocating to the capital. His great-grandfather Dashi was a man of strategic counsel whom the people of the realm trusted. When Taizu marched west, the king of Gaochang grew fearful and summoned Dashi for counsel, presenting him with brocade robes and a white sable cap. Seeing that the mandate of Heaven had turned toward the Mongols, Dashi urged his lord to submit with tribute and become a vassal so the kingdom might be spared, and for this he was given the title Shangshu. As Taizu withdrew his forces, the princes told the emperor, "Dashi's son Yelizhu is a fierce warrior, and the tribes under his command are strong as well. We hear he has long wished to lead his people in submission but lacked the chance—should we not summon him?" " Taizu agreed, and at once ordered five hundred relay horses sent to escort Yelizhu to court. Once he arrived and was presented at court, the emperor held him in high regard. In 1246, during Taizu's western campaign, Yelizhu served under Prince Ajinai, distinguished himself in battle, and won exceptional favor. The prince had been sitting beneath a crimson canopy to block the sun; when Yelizhu joined the council, his face brightened and he praised him at length. When Yelizhu took his leave, the prince cast off the canopy and escorted him ten li. He was then appointed to head the imperial kitchen staff of all four ring guards. In 1232 he joined the campaign against Jin, earned the highest distinction in battle, and received generous rewards. In 1234 he assisted Huduhu in registering Han households, assessing taxes and labor obligations, and distributing land among the meritorious officials—a work people admired for its swift competence.
3
鐵哥朮，野里朮長子也，尤沉鷙有才。 嘗有擁兵叛者，鐵哥朮率族人與戰于魚兒濼。 時軍興，簿檄繁急，鐵哥朮一以其國書識之，無遺失者，帝甚嘉焉。 至元中，擢為棣州達魯花赤，遷德安府達魯花赤。 適土人蔡知府者以衆叛，鐵哥朮率衆先登，冒矢石，身被數槍，猶戰不已，遂討平之。 主將怒，將屠其城。 鐵哥朮請曰：「叛者蔡知府數人而已，城中之人何預焉。 盍誅其黨與而止，毋令濫及非辜。 」主將嘉其誠懇，城遂得全。 累官至嘉議大夫、婺州路達魯花赤，所在咸著政蹟。 大德己亥卒，成宗敕其孫海壽載其柩歸葬京師，贈榮祿大夫、江浙行省平章政事、柱國，封雲國公，諡簡肅。
Tiegezhu, Yelizhu's eldest son, was a man of exceptional reserve, force of character, and ability. When a rebel raised armed followers, Tiegezhu led his kinsmen against him at Fish Lake Marsh. During the mobilization, when orders and registers poured in, Tiegezhu could read every document in his native script without a single loss, and the emperor praised him highly. During the Zhiyuan reign he was promoted to darughachi of Di Prefecture and later transferred to De'an Prefecture. When a local official, Prefect Cai, rose in rebellion with a mob, Tiegezhu led the assault over the walls, braving arrows and stones though pierced by several spears, and fought on until the revolt was crushed. The commander, enraged, was about to slaughter the entire city. Tiegezhu pleaded, "Only Prefect Cai and a handful of men rebelled—what crime have the townspeople committed? Execute the ringleaders and their accomplices, but do not let punishment spill onto the innocent." " The commander was moved by his earnest plea, and the city was spared. He rose to Jiayi dafu and darughachi of Wuzhou Circuit, leaving a record of distinguished administration wherever he served. He died in 1299. Chengzong ordered his grandson Haishou to bring his coffin home for burial in the capital and posthumously honored him as Ronglu dafu, Pingzhang of the Jiang-Zhe Branch Secretariat, and Zhuguo, enfeoffing him as Duke of Yun with the posthumous name Jiansu.
4
子四人：義堅亞禮，幼給事裕宗宮。 至元十五年，為中書省宣使。 嘗使河南，適汴、鄭大疫，義堅亞禮命所在村郭搆室廬，備醫藥，以畜病者，由是軍民全活者衆。 遷直省舍人。 承中書檄徵考上都儲偫，及還，帝賜錦衣貂裘一襲，以旌其能。 出為湖州路達魯花赤，卒于官。 月連朮，同知安陸府事。 八扎，同知宣政院事。 孫九人，海壽，義堅亞禮子也。 由宿衛世祖朝累官至太中大夫、杭州路達魯花赤，招復流民有恩惠。 卒，贈翰林直學士，封范陽郡侯，諡惠敏。
He had four sons. Yijianyali served in the household of Heir Apparent Yuzong from childhood. In 1278 he was appointed an envoy of the Central Secretariat. On a mission to Henan, when Bian and Zheng were struck by plague, he ordered villages to erect shelters, stock medicines, and care for the sick, saving a great many soldiers and civilians. He was promoted to attendant of the Direct Secretariat. Dispatched by the Central Secretariat to audit the reserves at Shangdu, he returned to receive brocade robes and a sable coat from the emperor in recognition of his competence. He was appointed darughachi of Huzhou Circuit and died in office. Yuelianzhu served as associate administrator of Anlu Prefecture. Baza served as associate administrator of the Bureau of Buddhist and Tibetan Affairs. He had nine grandsons. Haishou was Yijianyali's son. Rising from palace guard in Kublai's reign to Taizhong dafu and darughachi of Hangzhou, he won gratitude for bringing back displaced people with humane policies. After his death he was posthumously made Hanlin Academician, enfeoffed as Marquis of Fanyang, with the posthumous name Huimin.
5
十年，改僉淮西等處行樞密院事，城正陽以扼淮海諸州兵。 宋陳奕率安豐、廬、壽等州兵數撓其役，塔出選精銳日十數戰，奕遁去，卒城正陽。 宋人復造戰艦於六安，欲攻正陽，塔出詢知之，率騎兵焚其艦。 餽饟久不繼，出兵據險，潛取安豐麥以餉軍，宋兵壁橫河口，塔出將奇兵大破之。
In the tenth year (1273) he was made commissioner of the Huai-Xi Branch Bureau of Military Affairs and built up Zhengyang to block Song forces along the Huai-Hai line. Chen Yi of Song led troops from Anfeng, Lu, Shou, and neighboring prefectures to harry the works repeatedly. Tachu picked elite troops and fought a dozen battles a day until Yi withdrew and Zhengyang was completed. When the Song built warships at Lu'an to strike Zhengyang, Tachu learned of it and led cavalry to burn them. When supplies ran low, he seized strong points, secretly harvested Anfeng wheat to feed his men, and when Song forces camped at Henghekou, led a flanking force that routed them completely.
6
十一年，朝議：「淮上諸郡，宋之北藩，城堅兵精，攻之不可猝下，徒老我師。 宜先渡江翦其根本，留兵淮甸絕其救援，則長江可乘虛而渡也。 」於是以塔出為鎮國上將軍、淮西行省參知政事，帥師攻安豐、廬、壽等州，俘生口萬餘來獻，賜蒲萄酒二壺，仍以曹州官園為第宅，給城南閑田為牧地。
In the eleventh year (1274) the court debated: "The Huai prefectures are Song's northern rampart—strong walls and seasoned troops. They cannot be taken at a stroke, and a frontal assault would only exhaust our forces. We should cross the Yangzi first and strike at the root, leaving forces in the Huai basin to block relief—then the great river can be crossed while the enemy is divided." " Tachu was appointed Zhenguo shangjiangjun and Vice Minister of the Huai-Xi Branch Secretariat and led attacks on Anfeng, Lu, Shou, and other prefectures. He sent more than ten thousand captives to court, received two jars of grape wine, was given the official garden at Cao Prefecture as his residence, and idle fields south of the city as pastureland.
7
宋夏貴帥舟師十萬圍正陽，決淮水灌城，幾陷，帝遣塔出往救之。 道出潁州，遇宋兵攻潁，戍卒僅數百人，盛暑，塔出即發公庫弓矢，驅市人出戰，預度潁之北關攻易破，乃急徙民入城伏兵以待。 是夜，宋人果焚北關，火光屬天，塔出率衆從暗中射之，矢下如雨，宋軍退走至沙河，大破之，溺死者不可勝計。 明日，長驅直走正陽，時方霖雨，突圍入城，遂堅壁不出。 俄復開霽，與右丞阿塔海分帥銳師以出，渡淮至中流，皆殊死戰，宋軍大潰，追數十里，斬首數千級，奪戰艦五百餘艘，遂解正陽之圍。 塔出乃上奏：「方事之殷，宜明賞罰，俾將士有所懲勸。 」帝納其言，頒賞有差。 秋八月，淮西行省復為行院。 塔出引兵渡淮，屯廬、揚間。
Xia Gui of Song led a fleet of one hundred thousand to besiege Zhengyang, breached the Huai to flood the city, and nearly took it. The emperor dispatched Tachu to the relief. Passing through Ying Prefecture, he found Song forces attacking the city with only a few hundred defenders on hand. In the midsummer heat he issued arms from the public stores, pressed townspeople into battle, judged the north gate the weak point, evacuated civilians into the city, and laid an ambush. That night the Song burned the north gate as he had foreseen, flames lighting the sky. Tachu rained arrows from ambush until the enemy fled to Shahe, where they were shattered; the drowned were beyond counting. The next day he pressed on to Zhengyang through pouring rain, broke through the siege lines into the city, and held the walls without sallying forth. When the weather cleared, he and Right Chancellor Atahai led elite troops out, crossed the Huai to midstream, and fought with desperate valor. The Song army collapsed; they pursued for miles, took thousands of heads and more than five hundred warships, and lifted the siege of Zhengyang. Tachu then memorialized: "With fighting at its height, rewards and punishments should be made explicit so officers and men know what to strive for and what to fear." " The emperor accepted his advice and distributed rewards accordingly. In the eighth month the Huai-Xi Branch Secretariat was reorganized as a Branch Bureau of Military Affairs. Tachu crossed the Huai with his army and encamped between Luzhou and Yangzhou.
8
十二年，從丞相伯顏以舟師與宋軍戰，宋軍大潰，其臣賈似道奔揚州，遂分兵四出，克池州，取太平，順流東下，至建康、丹徒、江陰、常州，皆望風迎降。 時揚州未附，諜告揚州人將夜襲丹徒，守將乞援，塔出設伏以待，揚州軍果夜至，塔出扼西津邀擊之，殺獲溺死者甚衆。 入朝，帝賜玉帶旌其功，授淮東左副都元帥，仍佩金虎符。 十三年，加通奉大夫、參知政事，領淮西行中書省事。 時沿淮諸州新附，塔出禁侵掠，撫瘡痍，練士卒，備姦宄，境內帖然。 俄遷江西都元帥，征廣東，塔出宣布恩信，所至溪峒納款，廣東遂平。
In 1275 he followed Chancellor Bayan's fleet against the Song. The Song forces collapsed, minister Jia Sidao fled to Yangzhou, and Mongol columns fanned out to take Chizhou and Taiping, then swept downstream through Jiankang, Dantu, Jiangyin, and Changzhou, each city surrendering at their approach. Yangzhou had not yet submitted when intelligence warned of a night attack on Dantu. The garrison commander begged for help; Tachu laid an ambush, and when Yangzhou troops came by night he blocked them at Xijin Ferry, killing and drowning a great many. At court the emperor awarded him a jade belt for his achievements, appointed him Left Vice Commander-in-Chief of Huaidong, and confirmed his golden tiger tally. In 1276 he was promoted to Tongfeng dafu and Vice Minister, directing the Huai-Xi Branch Secretariat. With the Huai prefectures newly submitted, Tachu forbade looting, tended the war-ravaged populace, drilled his troops, and guarded against unrest until the region lay quiet. Soon he was made Commander-in-Chief of Jiangxi and marched on Guangdong. Proclaiming mercy and good faith, he won over every mountain pass and ravine until Guangdong was pacified.
9
十四年，加賜雙虎符，為江西宣慰使。 宋益王昰、廣王昺走保嶺海，復改江西宣慰司為行中書省，遷治贛州，授塔出資政大夫、中書右丞，行中書省事。
In 1277 he received the double tiger tally and was appointed Pacification Commissioner of Jiangxi. When the Song princes Yi Zhao and Guang Zhao fled to the southern coast, Jiangxi was elevated to a Branch Secretariat with its seat at Ganzhou. Tachu was made Zizheng dafu and Right Chancellor, directing provincial affairs.
10
十五年，以二王事入議。 帝命張弘範、李恒緫兵進討，塔出留後，以供軍費。 初江西甫定，帝命隳其城，塔出即表言：「豫章諸郡皆瀕江為城，霖潦泛溢，無城必至墊溺，隳之不便。 」帝從之。 降附之初，有謀畔者，既敗獲矣，塔出謂同僚曰：「撫治乖方之所致也，中間豈無詿誤。 」止誅其渠魁，盡釋餘黨。 瑞州張公明愬左丞呂師夔謀為不軌，塔出廉知其誣，曰：「狂夫欲脅求貨耳，若以曚昧言遽聞之朝廷，則大獄茲興，連及無辜。 且師夔既居相職，詎肯為狂妄之事！ 若遲疑不決，恐彼驚疑，反生異謀。 」乃斬公明而後聞，帝是之。 十七年，入覲，賜勞有加，復命行省於江西，尋以疾卒於京師，時年三十七。 妻明理氏，以貞節稱，旌其門閭。
In 1278 he went to court to discuss the campaign against the two Song princes. The emperor ordered Zhang Hongfan and Li Heng to lead the expedition while Tachu stayed behind to provision the army. When Jiangxi was first pacified, the court ordered its walls torn down. Tachu memorialized at once: "The Yuzhang prefectures all stand on the riverbanks. In flood season, without walls the people will be drowned—demolition would do more harm than good." " The emperor agreed. Early after the surrender, a rebel plot was crushed and the ringleaders captured. Tachu told his colleagues, "This stems from failures in our governance—surely some were misled rather than malicious." " He executed only the leaders and released the rest. Zhang Gongming of Ruizhou accused Vice Minister Lü Shiqiao of treason. Tachu investigated and found the charge false. "This madman only wants to extort money," he said. "If we report vague accusations to court, a great purge will follow and sweep up the innocent. Besides, Shiqiao holds a chancellorship—would he commit such folly? If we hesitate, he may grow alarmed and turn to desperate measures." " He executed Gongming and then reported to court; the emperor approved. In 1280 he attended court, received special honors, and was sent back to Jiangxi. He soon died of illness in the capital at thirty-seven. His wife of the Mingli clan was renowned for her fidelity, and the court honored her household.
11
二子：長宰牙，襲爵中奉大夫、江西宣慰使； 次必宰牙，仕至征東行中書省左丞，妻伯牙倫，泰安郡武穆王孛魯歡之女，亦守義有賢行。
He had two sons. The elder, Zaia, inherited the title of Zhongfeng dafu and Pacification Commissioner of Jiangxi; the younger Bizaiya rose to Left Chancellor of the Eastern Campaign Branch Secretariat. His wife Boyalun, daughter of Prince Wumu Bolughuan of Tai'an, was likewise noted for fidelity and virtue.
12
塔里赤
Talichi
13
塔里赤，康里人。 其父也里里白，太祖時以武功授帳前緫校，奉旨南徵至洛陽，得唐白樂天故址，遂家焉。
Talichi was a Kangli tribesman. His father Yelilibai earned appointment as commander of the imperial guard in Taizu's reign for military merit. Campaigning south to Luoyang on imperial orders, he settled on the former estate of the Tang poet Bai Juyi and made it his home.
14
塔里赤幼穎異，好讀書，尤善騎射。 襲父職，參佐戎幕，調度軍馬，動合事宜。 行省奏充斷事官。 時南北民戶主客良賤雜糅，蒙古軍牧馬草地互相占據，命塔里赤至其地理之，軍民各得其所，由是世祖知其能。 俾領蒙古軍圍樊襄，塔里赤躬冒矢石，所向摧陷，樊城破，襄陽降。 從丞相伯顏渡江，駐臨安，尋命平章奧魯赤等分為六路，追襲宋二王。 塔里赤領軍至福建，所過秋毫無犯，降者如歸，宋都統陳宗榮率衆來降。 以功遷福建招討使。
Talichi was precocious as a boy, loved books, and excelled at horsemanship and archery. Inheriting his father's post, he served on campaign staffs, deploying cavalry and infantry with unfailing judgment. The Branch Secretariat recommended him as a judicial officer. With northern and southern households intermingled and Mongol grazing lands overlapping civilian holdings, Talichi was sent to survey the boundaries so soldiers and civilians each had their proper place. Kublai took note of his ability. He led Mongol forces in the siege of Fan and Xiangyang, braving arrows and stones in person until Fancheng fell and Xiangyang surrendered. He followed Chancellor Bayan across the Yangzi and encamped at Lin'an. Soon he and Pingzhang Aoluchi were ordered to divide into six columns in pursuit of the two Song princes. Talichi marched into Fujian without harming a soul; those who surrendered came as if returning home. Song Commander Chen Zongrong led his forces in submission. For his achievements he was made Pacification Commissioner of Fujian.
15
時諸郡盜起，其最盛者陳吊眼，擁衆五萬，陷漳州。 行省承制命塔里赤為閩廣大都督、征南都元帥，緫四省軍，復漳州，生擒陳吊眼戮于市，餘黨悉伏誅。 繼從征交趾，擊敗黃聖許等，積功加鎮國上將軍、三珠虎符、廣西兩江道宣慰使都元帥。 賀州盜起，塔里赤討平之。 改福建宣慰使，又改浙東。 金瘡發卒，贈輔國上將軍、浙東道宣慰使都元帥、護軍，追封臨安郡公。
Banditry broke out across the prefectures. The worst was Chen Diaoyan, who mustered fifty thousand men and seized Zhangzhou. The Branch Secretariat appointed him Grand Commander of Fujian-Guangdong and Commander-in-Chief of the Southern Campaign with authority over four provinces. He retook Zhangzhou, captured Chen Diaoyan and executed him publicly, and put down the remaining rebels. He then campaigned in Annam, defeated Huang Shengxu and others, and for accumulated merit was promoted to Zhenguo shangjiangjun with the three-pearl tiger tally and made Commander-in-Chief and Pacification Commissioner of the Guangxi Liangjiang Circuit. When bandits rose in He Prefecture, Talichi suppressed them. He was transferred to Pacification Commissioner of Fujian, then of eastern Zhejiang. An old battle wound reopened and he died. He was posthumously made Fuguo shangjiangjun, Commander-in-Chief and Pacification Commissioner of Zhedong, and Hugun, and enfeoffed as Duke of Lin'an.
16
子二人：脫脫木兒，邵武汀州新軍萬戶府達魯花赤； 萬奴，廣西宣慰使都元帥。
He had two sons: Tuotuomu'er, darughachi of the Shaowu Tingzhou New Army Ten-thousand Household Office; and Wannu, Commander-in-Chief and Pacification Commissioner of Guangxi.
17
塔海帖木兒
Tahai Temur
18
塔海帖木兒，荅荅里帶人。 其先在太祖時事國王木華黎，將左手大萬戶下蒙古軍，鎮太原以西八州。 破金將王公佐軍，斬公佐。 從攻陝右，征河西，滅金，皆有功，賜種田戶二百七十。 曾祖忒木勒哥嗣，從都元帥塔海紺卜征蜀，死於興元。 祖扎剌帶嗣。 扎剌帶卒，父拜荅兒尚幼，從祖扎里、荅朮相繼襲其職。 扎里從都元帥大荅征蜀，以所統軍二百人破宋軍于巴州，斬首三百級，生擒五十餘人。 荅朮以西川行樞密院檄領兵三千人救碉門，大敗宋軍，斬首三百餘級，俘百餘人以歸。 拜荅兒既長，始以父官從行省也速帶兒征建都，死軍中。
Tahai Temur was of the Dadalidai people. His forebears served Prince Muqali in Taizu's reign, commanding the Mongol forces of the Left Hand Great Ten-thousand Household and garrisoning the eight prefectures west of Taiyuan. They defeated the Jin general Wang Gongzuo's army and beheaded him. In campaigns in western Shaanxi, Hexi, and the final conquest of Jin, they distinguished themselves and were granted two hundred and seventy farming households. His great-grandfather Temulege succeeded to the post and followed Commander-in-Chief Tahai Ganbu in the Sichuan campaign, dying at Xingyuan. His grandfather Zhaladai succeeded to the post. When Zhaladai died, his son Baicha'er was still a child, so his uncles Zhali and Dashu succeeded to the post in turn. Zhali followed Commander-in-Chief Dada in the Sichuan campaign. With two hundred men he routed the Song army at Bazhou, took three hundred heads, and captured more than fifty alive. On orders from the Western Sichuan Branch Bureau, Dashu led three thousand men to relieve Diaomen, routed the Song army, took over three hundred heads, and returned with more than a hundred prisoners. When Baicha'er came of age he inherited his father's post and followed Yesudai'er of the Branch Secretariat in the campaign against Jiandu, dying in the field.
19
塔海帖木兒襲父職，初從行院忽敦圍嘉定，嘉定降。 進圍重慶，守將張珏出師迎敵，塔海帖木兒力戰陷陣，功最多。 十五年，又以都魯軍二百人破宋軍於白水江，奪戰船一，俘其衆十三人。 陞宣武將軍、管軍緫管。 從也速荅兒征亦奚不薛，又從征都掌蠻，皆以為前鋒，殺獲甚衆。
Tahai Temur inherited his father's post. He first followed the Branch Bureau under Hudun in the siege of Jiading until the city surrendered. Advancing to besiege Chongqing, he met defender Zhang Jue in battle, charged into the enemy lines, and won the greatest distinction. In the fifteenth year he again led two hundred Dulu troops to defeat the Song at Baishui River, capturing a warship and thirteen men. He was promoted to Xuanwu General and Commander-in-Chief of Troops. He campaigned under Yesuda'er against Yixi Buxue and the Duzhang tribes, serving as vanguard in both and taking a great toll of enemy lives and captives.
20
九溪蠻、散貓、大盤蠻尚木的世用等叛，從行省曲立吉思帥師往討，皆擒之，及殺其酋長頭狗等。 也速荅兒、藥剌罕率兵萬人會雲南兵討烏蒙蠻，至鬧竈，其酋長阿蒙率五百餘衆奔麻布蠻地，塔海帖木兒以四百人追至山箐中，大敗之，擒阿蒙以歸。 二十六年，又從也速荅兒西征，不知所終。
When the Jiuxi, Sanmao, Dapan, and other tribes rebelled under leaders such as Shangmu and Shiyong, he followed Qulijisi of the Branch Secretariat to suppress them, capturing the rebels and executing chieftains including Tougou. Yesuda'er and Yalahkan led ten thousand men with Yunnan forces against the Wumeng tribes. At Naozao, chieftain Ameng fled with five hundred followers into Mabu territory. Tahai Temur pursued with four hundred men into the ravines, routed them, and brought Ameng back captive. In the twenty-sixth year he again followed Yesuda'er on the western campaign, after which nothing more is recorded of him.
21
口兒吉
Kou'erji
22
忽都，蒙古兀羅帶氏。 父孛罕，事太祖，備宿衛。 至太宗時為鎮西行省，領蒙古、漢軍從攻河中、潼關、河南，與拜只思、扎忽歹、阿思蘭攻秦鞏及仁和諸堡，又與拜只思守京兆。 歲乙未，授左手萬戶，從都元帥荅海鉗卜出征，卒軍中。
Hudu was of the Mongol Ulodai clan. His father Bohon served Taizu as a palace guardsman. Under Taizong he served as Western Pacification Commissioner, leading Mongol and Han forces against Hezhong, Tongguan, and Henan, and with Baizisi, Zhahudai, and Asilan captured the Qin-Gong and Renhe forts before joining Baizisi in the defense of Jingzhao. In the yiwei year he was made Left Hand Ten-thousand Household and followed Commander-in-Chief Dahai Qianbu on campaign, dying in the field.
23
子扎忽帶，時在宿衛，弟忽都荅立襲其職。 忽都荅立卒，札忽帶嗣，為千戶，從行樞密院圍重慶。 重慶守張珏遣勁兵數千出挑戰，札忽帶力戰大破之。 回軍圍瀘州，未下，行樞密院遣入朝計事，授宣武將軍、管軍緫管。 復還攻瀘，登城，與瀘兵搏戰而死。 子阿都赤嗣。
His son Zhahudai was then on palace guard, and his younger brother Hududali inherited the post. When Hududali died, Zhahudai succeeded as thousand-household and joined the Branch Bureau in the siege of Chongqing. Defender Zhang Jue sent several thousand elite troops to challenge him; Zhahudai fought fiercely and routed them. The army then besieged Luzhou without success. The Branch Bureau sent him to court to report, and he was appointed Xuanwu General and Commander-in-Chief of Troops. He returned to assault Luzhou, scaled the walls, and died fighting the garrison hand to hand. His son Aduchi succeeded to the post.
24
孛兒速
Bei'ersu
25
孛兒速，脫脫忒氏。 世祖時直宿衛，扈駕征哈剌章還，帝駐蹕高阜，見河北有駕舟而來者，顧謂左右曰：「是賊也，奈何？ 」孛兒速進曰：「臣請禦之。 」即解衣徑渡，揮戈刺死舟尾二人，拏其舟就岸，舟中之人倉惶失措，帝命左右悉擒之。 哈剌章平，以功論賞。
Bei'ersu was of the Tuotuote clan. In Kublai's reign he served as a palace guardsman. Returning from the Hala Zhang campaign, the emperor halted on a high mound and saw someone rowing across the river northward. He turned to his attendants and said, "That is an enemy—what shall we do? " Bei'ersu stepped forward. "Let your subject meet them," he said. " Stripping off his coat, he swam across, killed the two men at the stern with his spear, dragged the boat to shore, and left its occupants in panic. The emperor ordered them all seized. When Hala Zhang was pacified, he received rewards according to his merit.
26
子荅荅呵兒，從征孛可有功，由宿衛陞武德將軍、揭只揭烈溫千戶所達魯花赤。 從征叛王乃顏、也不干等，奮戈擊死數人，擒也不干，收其所管欽察之民。 武宗時，進懷遠大將軍、元帥，卒。
His son Dada Ha'er distinguished himself in the campaign against Boke and rose from palace guard to Wude General and darughachi of the Jiezhijieliewen Ten-thousand Household Office. Campaigning against the rebel princes Nayan and Yebuqan, he killed several enemies in fierce combat, captured Yebuqan, and took charge of the Qipchaq people under his command. Under Wuzong he was promoted to Huaiyuan General and Marshal, and died.
27
月舉連赤海牙
Yuejulianchihaiya
28
月舉連赤海牙，畏兀兒人。 從憲宗征釣魚山，奉命修麴藥以療師疫，賞白金五十兩。 繼從太子滿哥都征雲南，戰數勝。 中統三年，火都暨荅離叛，領兵與討平之。 至元十二年，佩虎符，為隴右河西道提刑按察使。 兀朗孫火石顏謀亂，從皇太子安西王往鎮之，皇太子賜以白金五十兩。
Yuejulianchihaiya was a Uighur. He followed Möngke in the campaign at Diaoyu Mountain, prepared fermented medicines to treat the army's epidemic, and was rewarded with fifty taels of silver. He then followed Crown Prince Mangudai in the Yunnan campaign and won several victories. In 1262, when Hodu and Dali rebelled, he led troops to suppress them. In 1275 he received the tiger tally and was appointed Surveillance Commissioner of the Longyou Hexi Circuit. When Wulangsun Huoshiyan plotted rebellion, he followed Crown Prince Anxi to suppress it and received fifty taels of silver from the prince.
29
十五年，與伯速帶平土魯，皇子復賜金衣腰帶金碗，且以其功聞。 十七年，進官嘉議大夫，仍居舊職。 二十年，進中奉大夫、四川等處行中書省參知政事，尋以疾歸秦州。 大德八年卒。 至順中，贈推忠宣力定遠功臣、資善大夫、陝西行省左丞、護軍，追封威寧郡公，諡襄靖。
In the fifteenth year he and Bosudai pacified the Tulu tribes. The prince again rewarded him with golden robes, a belt, and a golden bowl and reported his achievements to court. In the seventeenth year he was promoted to Jiayi dafu while retaining his former duties. In the twentieth year he was promoted to Zhongfeng dafu and Vice Minister of the Sichuan Branch Secretariat, but soon retired to Qin Prefecture due to illness. He died in 1304. During the Zhishun era he was posthumously honored as a merit subject, Zishan dafu, Left Chancellor of the Shaanxi Branch Secretariat, and Hugun, enfeoffed as Duke of Weining with the posthumous name Xiangjing.
30
阿荅赤
Adachi
31
阿荅赤，阿速氏。 父昂和思，憲宗時佩虎符為萬戶。
Adachi was of the Alan clan. His father Anghesi held the tiger tally as a ten-thousand household under Möngke.
32
阿荅赤扈從憲宗南征，與敵兵戰于劍州，以功賞白銀。 阿里不哥叛，從也兒怯等征之，有功。 世祖中統三年，從征李璮，身二十餘戰，累功授金符千戶。 丞相伯顏、平章阿朮之平江南也，阿荅赤皆在行中，著戰功，歿于陣，帝憐之，特賜鈔七十錠、白金五百兩，為葬具，仍賜鎮巢之民一千五百三十九戶，命其子伯荅兒襲職。
Adachi accompanied Möngke on the southern campaign, fought at Jian Prefecture, and was rewarded with silver for his merit. When Ariq Böke rebelled, he followed Yeerqie and others against him with distinction. In 1262 he joined the campaign against Li Tan, fought in more than twenty battles in person, and for accumulated merit was made a golden-tally thousand-household. When Chancellor Bayan and Pingzhang Azhu pacified the south, Adachi was with them throughout, distinguished himself in battle, and fell in the field. The emperor pitied him, granted seventy ingots of paper money and five hundred taels of silver for his funeral, allotted him 1,539 households at Zhenchao, and ordered his son Bocha'er to succeed him.
33
伯荅兒從別急列迷失北征，與瓮吉剌只兒瓦台戰于牙里伴朶之地，以功受上賞。 尋進定遠大將軍、後衛都指揮使，兼右阿速衛事，將阿速軍往征別失八里，與敵兵累戰累捷。 樞密臣以其功聞，賞白金、貂裘、弓矢、鞍轡等，尋復以銀坐椅賜之。
Bocha'er followed Bieji Lieimishi on the northern campaign, defeated Onggirad Jirgatai at Yalibanduo, and received the highest reward. Soon he was promoted to Dingyuan General and Commander of the Rear Guard, also directing the Right Alan Guard. Leading Alan troops against Beshbalik, he won victory after victory. The Bureau of Military Affairs reported his achievements. He received silver, sable coats, bows, arrows, saddles, and bridles, and soon afterward a silver armchair as well.
34
明安，康里氏。 至元十三年，世祖詔民之蕩析離居及僧道、漏籍諸色人不當差徭者萬餘人充貴赤，令明安領之。 明安歲扈駕出入，克勤于事。 二十年，授定遠大將軍、中衛親軍都指揮使。 明年，賜佩虎符，領貴赤軍北征。 又明年，立貴赤親軍都指揮使司，命為本衛達魯花赤。 尋奉旨領蒙古軍八千北征，明年，至別失八剌哈思之地，與海都軍戰有功。
Ming An was of the Kangli clan. In 1276 Kublai decreed that more than ten thousand displaced persons, monks, Daoists, and others not liable for corvée should be enrolled as Keshi guards, with Ming An placed in command. Year after year Ming An attended the emperor on campaign and at court, diligent in every duty. In the twentieth year he was appointed Dingyuan General and Commander of the Central Guard. The following year he received the tiger tally and led the Keshi on the northern campaign. The year after that the Keshi Imperial Guard Command was established, and he was made its darughachi. Soon he was ordered to lead eight thousand Mongol troops north. The next year, reaching Beshbalik, he fought Haidu's forces with distinction.
35
二十六年冬十二月，別乞憐叛，劫取官站脫脫火孫塔剌海等，明安率衆追擊之，五戰五捷，悉還之。 至杭海，強民闊闊台、撒兒塔台等率衆作亂，奪三站地，劫脫脫火孫，明安引兵又追擊之，却其軍。 二十七年秋七月，布四麻、當先別乞失、出春伯駙馬、兀者台、朶羅台、兀兒荅兒、塔里雅赤等掠四怯薛牛馬畜牧，及劫滅烈太子昔博赤并斡脫、布伯各投下民殆盡。 明安將兵追擊于汪吉昔博赤之城，賊軍敗走，還所掠之民并獲其牛馬畜牧等以歸。 時出伯、伯都所領軍乏食，奉旨以明安所獲畜牧濟之。 二十九年，以功陞定遠大將軍、貴赤親軍都指揮使司達魯花赤。 時別失八剌哈孫盜起，詔以兵討之，戰于別失八里禿兒古闍，有功，賊軍再合四千人於忽蘭兀孫，明安設方略與戰，大敗之。 大德二年，復將兵北征，與海都戰。 七年，歿于軍。 子曰帖哥台，曰孛蘭奚。
In the twelfth month of the twenty-sixth year, Bieqiling rebelled and raided the relay station of Tuotuohuosun Talahai. Ming An pursued him, won five battles in succession, and recovered everything taken. At Hanghai, strongmen Kuokuotai and Sa'ertatai rose in revolt, seized three relay stations, and robbed Tuotuohuosun. Ming An pursued again and drove them off. In the seventh month of the twenty-seventh year, Busima, Dangxian Bieqishi, imperial son-in-law Chuchunbo, Wuzhetai, Duoluotai, Wu'erdai'er, Taliyachi, and others raided the kheshig herds and nearly wiped out the households of Prince Mielie's son Xibochi and the appanages of Otuo and Bubo. Ming An pursued them to Wangjixibochi's fort, routed the rebels, restored the people they had seized, and recovered their herds. When the armies of Chubo and Baidu ran short of provisions, the court ordered Ming An's captured herds used to feed them. In the twenty-ninth year he was promoted to Dingyuan General and darughachi of the Keshi Imperial Guard Command for his achievements. When bandits rose in Beshbalik, he was ordered to suppress them and distinguished himself at Turguchan. When four thousand rebels regrouped at Hulanwusun, Ming An devised a plan and routed them. In 1298 he again led troops north and fought Haidu. In the seventh year he died in the field. His sons were Tiegetai and Bolanxi.
36
孛蘭奚，昭武大將軍、中衛親軍都指揮使，積官銀青榮祿大夫、太尉。 子桑兀孫，中衛親軍都指揮使。 桑兀孫卒，弟乞荅海襲職。
Bolanxi was Zhaowu General and Commander of the Central Guard, rising through accumulated offices to Silver-Green Ronglu dafu and Grand Preceptor. His son Sangwusun became Commander of the Central Guard. When Sangwusun died, his younger brother Qidahai succeeded to the post.
37
忽林失
Hulinshi
38
忽林失，八魯剌氏。 曾祖不魯罕罕劄，事太祖，從平諸國，充八魯剌思千戶，以其軍與太赤溫等戰，重傷墜馬，帝親勒兵救之，以功陞萬戶，賜黃金五十兩、白金五百兩，俾直宿衛。 祖許兒台，年十五能馳射賊，以勇略稱。 從定宗征欽察，為千戶。 領兵下西番。 從世祖伐宋，至亳州，與宋人迎敵，敗之。 父瓮吉剌帶，初為軍器監官，從世祖親征阿里不哥，以功受上賞。 俄奉旨使西域，籍地產，悉得其實。 帝方欲大用之而卒。
Hulinshi was of the Barulas clan. His great-grandfather Bulughan Hanja served Taizu in the conquest of the realm as a Barulas thousand-household. Fighting Taichiwen, he was gravely wounded and fell from his horse; the emperor personally led troops to rescue him. For this he was promoted to ten-thousand household, granted fifty taels of gold and five hundred of silver, and assigned to palace guard duty. His grandfather Xu'ertai could ride and shoot down bandits at fifteen and was famed for courage and resourcefulness. He followed Ögedei in the Qipchaq campaign and became a thousand-household. He led troops into the western frontier. He followed Kublai against the Song, met the enemy at Bozhou, and defeated them. His father Onggiradai first served in the Armory Directorate, accompanied Kublai's personal campaign against Ariq Böke, and received the highest reward. Soon he was sent to the western regions on imperial orders to register land and produce, and reported with complete accuracy. The emperor was about to give him greater responsibilities when he died.
39
忽林失初直宿衛。 後以千戶從征乃顏，馳馬奮戈，衝擊敵營，矢下如雨，身被三十三創。 成宗親督左右出其鏃，命醫療之，以其功聞。 世祖以克宋所得銀甕及金酒器等賜之，命領太府監。 後以千戶從皇子闊闊出出征，還，留鎮軍中。
Hulinshi began as a palace guardsman. Later, as a thousand-household, he campaigned against Nayan, charging the enemy camp at full gallop through a rain of arrows and sustaining thirty-three wounds. Chengzong personally supervised the removal of the arrowheads, ordered him treated, and reported his achievements to court. Kublai rewarded him with silver urns and gold wine vessels taken in the conquest of Song and appointed him head of the Imperial Storehouse Directorate. Later he followed Prince Kuokuochu on campaign as a thousand-household and remained to garrison the army on his return.
40
後從成宗與海都、都瓦等戰有功，成宗嘉之，特命為翰林承旨，俄改萬戶。 與叛王斡羅思、察八兒等戰，以功授榮祿大夫、司徒，賜銀印。 武宗嘗曰：「群臣中能為國宣力如忽林失者實鮮，其厚賚之。 」於是遣使召見。 未幾武宗崩，仁宗即位，念其舊勳，賞賚特厚。
He later fought under Chengzong against Haidu and Duwa with distinction. Chengzong praised him, appointed him Hanlin Academician, and soon made him a ten-thousand household. Fighting the rebel princes Oljei and Chabar, he was made Ronglu dafu and Situ and granted a silver seal. Wuzong once said, "Few among my ministers serve the state with the force Hulinshi does—reward him generously." " Envoys were sent to summon him to court. Before he could be received, Wuzong died and Renzong succeeded, remembering his old service and rewarding him with exceptional generosity.
41
失剌拔都兒
Shilabadouer
42
失剌拔都兒，阿速氏。 父月魯達某，憲宗時領阿速十人入覲，充阿塔赤，從世祖至哈剌章之地，戰數勝，兀里羊哈台以其功聞，賜所俘人一口以賞之，後以金瘡發卒。
Shilabadouer was of the Alan clan. His father Yueludamou led ten Alans to court under Möngke, served as an imperial attendant, followed Kublai to Hala Zhang, won several battles, and was rewarded with a captive. He later died when an old wound reopened.
43
失剌拔都兒至自脫別之地，帝特賜白金、楮幣、牛馬等物。 至元二十一年，從丞相伯顏南征有功，仍充阿塔赤。 帝嘗命放海青，曰：「能獲新者賞之。 」失剌拔都兒即援弓射一兔二禽以獻，賞沙魚皮雜帶及貂裘，且命於尚乘寺為少卿、於阿速為千戶。 二十四年，授武略將軍，管阿速軍千戶，賜金符。 乃顏叛，從諸王和元魯往征之，力戰有功。 乃顏平，帝賞以金腰帶及銀交床等。 二十五年，進武德將軍、尚乘寺少卿，兼阿速千戶。 征哈荅安等，敗之，獲其駝馬等物。 成宗嘉其功，以軍二千益之。 討叛王脫脫，擒之，以功受賞。 大德六年卒。
When Shilabadouer returned from Tuobie, the emperor specially rewarded him with silver, paper currency, cattle, and horses. In 1284 he distinguished himself in Chancellor Bayan's southern campaign and continued to serve as an imperial attendant. The emperor once ordered his sea hawks flown and said, "Whoever brings back a fresh catch shall be rewarded." " Shilabadouer immediately shot a rabbit and two birds and presented them. He received a shark-skin belt and a sable coat, and was appointed Vice Director of the Imperial Transport Directorate and Alan thousand-household. In the twenty-fourth year he was made Wulue General, placed in command of an Alan thousand-household, and granted the golden tally. When Nayan rebelled, he followed Prince Heyuanlu against him and distinguished himself in fierce fighting. After Nayan's defeat, the emperor rewarded him with a golden belt, a silver folding bed, and other gifts. In the twenty-fifth year he was promoted to Wude General and Vice Director of the Imperial Transport Directorate while retaining his Alan thousand-household post. Campaigning against Hada'an and others, he defeated them and seized their camels, horses, and other goods. Chengzong praised his achievements and added two thousand men to his command. In the campaign against the rebel prince Tuotuo he captured him and was rewarded for his merit. He died in 1302.
44
子那海產，襲其職。 至大二年，進宣武將軍、右衛阿速親軍都指揮使，賜三珠虎符。 泰定二年，覃加明威將軍。
His son Nahai Chan succeeded to his post. In 1309 he was promoted to Xuanwu General and Commander of the Right Guard Alan Imperial Guard and received the three-pearl tiger tally. In 1325 he was promoted to Mingwei General.
45
徹里，阿速氏。 父別吉八，在憲宗時從攻釣魚山，以功受賞。 徹里事世祖，充火兒赤。 從征海都，奮戈擊其前鋒，官軍二人陷陣，掖而出之，以功受賞。 後從征杭海，獲其牛馬畜牧，悉以給軍食。 帝嘉之，賞鈔三千五百錠，仍以分賚士卒。
Cheli was of the Alan clan. His father Biejiba followed Möngke in the attack on Diaoyu Mountain and was rewarded for his merit. Cheli served Kublai as a kitchen attendant. Campaigning against Haidu, he charged the enemy vanguard, rescued two soldiers who had fallen in the melee, and was rewarded for his merit. Later, campaigning at Hanghai, he seized enemy herds and used them entirely to feed the army. The emperor praised him, granted 3,500 ingots of paper money, and shared the reward among his soldiers.
46
成宗時，盜據博落脫兒之地，命將兵討之，獲三千餘人，誅其酋長還。 奉命同客省使拔都兒等往八兒胡之地，以前所獲人口畜牧悉給其主。 軍還，帝特賜鈔一百錠。 武宗居潛邸，亦以銀酒器賞之。 至大二年，立左阿速衛，授本衛僉事，賜金符。 皇慶二年，從湘寧王北征，以功賜一珠虎符。
Under Chengzong, when bandits seized Boluotuo'er, he was ordered to suppress them, took more than three thousand captives, executed their chieftain, and returned. On orders he went with Guest Reception Commissioner Badu'er and others to Ba'erhu and restored all the people and livestock previously captured to their rightful owners. On the army's return, the emperor specially granted him one hundred ingots of paper money. While still heir apparent, Wuzong also rewarded him with silver wine vessels. In 1309 the Left Alan Guard was established; he was made its commissioner and granted the golden tally. In 1313 he followed the Prince of Xiangning on the northern campaign and received the one-pearl tiger tally for his merit.
47
乞台，察台氏。 至元二十四年為欽察衛百戶，從土土哈征叛王失烈吉及乃顏有功，賜金符，陞千戶。 從征忽剌出，戰于阿里台之地。 元貞二年，以疾卒。
Qitai was of the Chatai clan. In 1287 he was a hundred-household of the Qipchaq Guard. Campaigning under Totoq against the rebel princes Shilieji and Nayan, he distinguished himself, received the golden tally, and was promoted to thousand-household. He campaigned against Hulachu and fought at Alitai. He died of illness in 1296.
48
子哈贊赤襲職，從創兀兒於魁烈兒之地，與哈荅安戰有功。 大德五年，從戰杭海。 從武宗親征哈剌阿荅。 復從創兀兒征不別、八憐，為前鋒，以功受賞賚。 皇慶二年，授金符，為千戶。 明宗居潛邸，延祐四年命從西征，與禿滿帖木兒戰于失剌塔兒馬失之地，以功復受厚賞，居其地十五年。 天曆二年，賜金符，授昭勇大將軍、同知大都督府事。 卒。
His son Hazanchi succeeded him. Following Chuangwu'er at Kuilier, he distinguished himself in battle against Hada'an. In 1301 he fought at Hanghai. He accompanied Wuzong's personal campaign against Hala A'da. He again followed Chuangwu'er against Bubie and Baling as vanguard and was rewarded for his achievements. In 1313 he received the golden tally and was made a thousand-household. While Mingzong was still heir apparent, he was ordered west in 1317, defeated Tudie Temur at Shilata'er Mashi, received rich rewards, and remained in that region for fifteen years. In 1329 he received the golden tally and was appointed Zhaoyong General and Associate Administrator of the Metropolitan Commandery. He died.

脫因納
Tuoyinna
50
有子曰定童、只兒哈朗。 定童襲父職，阿兒魯萬戶府襄陽萬戶府漢軍達魯花赤，佩金虎符，明威將軍。 只兒哈朗，初授欽察親軍千戶所達魯花赤，佩金符，武略將軍。 改授朝列大夫、通政院副使，歷同知，陞院使，積官中奉大夫。
He had two sons, Dingtong and Zhierhalang. Dingtong inherited his father's post as Mingwei General and darughachi of the Han forces of the Xiangyang Ten-thousand Household under the Arlu Ten-thousand Household Office, bearing the golden tiger tally. Zhierhalang was first made Wulue General and darughachi of the Qipchaq Imperial Guard thousand-household office with the golden tally. He was transferred to Chaolie dafu and Vice Minister of the Communications Office, served as associate administrator, rose to Director, and eventually reached Zhongfeng dafu through accumulated promotions.
51
和尚，蒙古乃蠻台氏。 祖海速，充昔烈木千戶所蒙古軍百戶。 伯父兀魯不花，初充蒙古軍五十戶。 至元七年，從昔烈木千戶南征，以功命權百戶，從僉省阿剌海牙攻樊城。 十一年，從攻新城，又從攻鄂東門，攻處州，屢立戰功。 二十五年，賜銀符，授敦武校尉、後衛親軍百戶。 是年秋卒。 父怯烈吉襲。 怯烈吉卒，和尚襲。
Heshang was of the Mongol Naimantai clan. His grandfather Haisu was a hundred-household in the Mongol forces of the Xiliemu thousand-household office. His uncle Ulubuhua first served as a fifty-household in the Mongol army. In 1270 he followed the Xiliemu thousand-household south, was made acting hundred-household for his merit, and joined Vice Secretariat Alahaiya in the assault on Fancheng. In 1274 he took part in the assaults on Xincheng, the east gate of Ezhou, and Chuzhou, distinguishing himself repeatedly in battle. In 1288 he received the silver tally and was appointed Dunwu Weiwei and hundred-household of the Rear Guard. He died that autumn. His father Qielieji succeeded to the post. When Qielieji died, Heshang succeeded.
