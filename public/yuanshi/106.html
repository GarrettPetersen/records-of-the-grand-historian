后妃之制，厥有等威，其來尚矣。 元初，因其國俗，不娶庶姓，非此族也，不居嫡選。 當時史臣以為舅甥之貴，蓋有周姬、齊姜之遺意，歷世守之，固可嘉也。 然其居則有曰斡耳朵之分，沒復有繼承守宮之法。 位號之淆，名分之瀆，則亦甚矣。 累朝嘗詔有司修后妃傳，而未見成書。 內廷事祕，今莫之考，則其氏名之僅見簡牘者，尚可遺而不錄乎？ 且一代之制存焉，闕疑而慎言，斯可矣。 作后妃表。
The ranks and authority governing empresses and consorts are ancient institutions. Early in the dynasty the Mongols followed their own custom of not marrying outside certain clans; only women of the designated lineage could be chosen as principal consorts. Contemporary historians held that the esteem accorded maternal-kin marriage preserved something of the Zhou custom of taking brides from the Ji of Zhou and the Jiang of Qi—a practice maintained for generations and rightly praised. Yet their households were divided into separate ordas, and after a consort's death there arose the practice of heirs inheriting and maintaining her palace establishment. The confusion of titles and the breach of proper status were extreme as well. Dynasty after dynasty the court ordered officials to compile biographies of empresses and consorts, yet no finished book ever appeared. Inner-palace affairs are secret and cannot now be fully verified; yet the clan names that survive on scattered documents surely ought not to be omitted. What remains here still preserves the institutions of an age; to note uncertainties and speak with caution is enough. Hence this table of empresses and consorts.

Shunzong (Darmabala)
Consort Daji (Kongirat; honored as Empress Dowager in 1307; in 1315 given the lengthy honorific Empress Dowager Yitian Xingsheng Ciren Zhaoyi Shouyuan Quande Taining Fuqing; raised to Grand Empress Dowager in 1320 with the added epithet Weiwen Chongyou; died in 1322 and was posthumously titled Empress Zhaoxian Yuansheng). She was clever but unrestrained; once established in the Eastern Palace her excesses deepened. Within the palace the "Black Donkey Mother" Yilieshiba dominated affairs; outside, favorites such as Shiliemen and Niulin, together with chief minister Tiemudier, abused imperial favor and corrupted government. After Gegeen acceded, this whole clique was executed and her influence waned.
