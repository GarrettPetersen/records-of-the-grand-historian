1
察罕，西域板勒紇城人也。 父伯德那，歲庚辰，國兵下西域，舉族來歸。 事親王旭烈，授河東民賦副緫管，因居河中猗氏縣，後徙解州。 贈榮祿大夫、宣徽使、柱國、芮國公。
Chahan came from Banlehe city in the Western Regions. His father Boridana: in a gengchen year, when the imperial armies reached the Western Regions, the entire clan submitted and settled. He served Prince Xulie and was made deputy commissioner for civilian levies in Hedong; the family settled at Yishi in Hezhong and later moved to Xiezhou. He was posthumously honored as Grand Master of Splendid Happiness, Palatial Intendant, Pillar of the State, and Duke of Rui.
2
察罕魁偉穎悟，博覽強記，通諸國字書，為行軍府奧魯千戶。 奧魯赤參政湖廣，辟為蒙古都萬戶府知事。 奧魯赤進平章，復辟為理問，政事悉委裁決，且令諸子受學焉。 至元二十四年，從鎮南王征安南，師次瀘江。 安南世子遣其叔父詣軍門自陳無罪，王命察罕數其罪而責之，使者辭屈，世子舉衆逃去。 二十八年，授樞密院經歷。 未幾從奧魯赤移治江西。 寧都民言：「某鄉石上雲氣五色，有物焉，視之玉璽也。 不以兵取，恐為居人所有。 」衆惑之。 察罕曰：「妄也，是必搆害仇家者。 」覈問之果然。 前後從奧魯赤出入湖廣、江西兩省，凡二十一年，多著勳績。
Chahan was tall and brilliant, widely read and retentive, fluent in the scripts of many countries, and held the post of thousand-household in the campaign headquarters' auru. When Aoluchi became administrative commissioner of Huguang, he recruited Chahan as secretary of the Mongol Chief Ten-Thousand Households Office. When Aoluchi rose to Grand Councillor, he again appointed Chahan judicial inquirer, entrusted him with full authority over affairs, and had his sons study under him. In 1287 he followed the Prince of Zhennan against Annam; the army encamped on the Lu River. The Annamese crown prince sent his uncle to the camp to plead innocence; the prince had Chahan list his crimes and rebuke him. When the envoy could not answer, the crown prince fled with his troops. In 1291 he was appointed secretary of the Bureau of Military Affairs. Soon afterward he accompanied Aoluchi when his administration was transferred to Jiangxi. The people of Ningdu reported, "On a stone in a certain village there are five-colored clouds; what lies there looks like a jade imperial seal. Unless troops are sent to seize it, the locals may claim it for themselves." Many were taken in by the story. Chahan said, "This is nonsense—it must be someone framing a personal enemy." A thorough investigation proved him right. Over twenty-one years he served Aoluchi in Huguang and Jiangxi and won many distinctions.
3
成宗大德四年，御史臺奏僉湖南憲司事，中書省奏為武昌路治中。 丞相哈剌哈孫曰：「察罕廉潔，固宜居風憲。 然武昌大郡，非斯人不可治。 」竟除武昌。 廣西妖賊高仙道以左道惑衆，平民詿誤者以數千計。 既敗，湖廣行省命察罕與憲司雜治之，鞫得其情，議誅首惡數人，餘悉縱遣，且焚其籍。 衆難之。 察罕曰：「吾獨當其責，諸君無累也。 」以治最聞，擢河南省郎中。
In 1300 the Censorate recommended him for concurrent service on the Hunan surveillance commission, while the Central Secretariat proposed him as assistant prefect of Wuchang. Chancellor Haraqasun said, "Chahan is incorrupt and well suited to surveillance work. But Wuchang is a major prefecture—no one but he can govern it." In the end he was appointed to Wuchang. In Guangxi the sorcerer-rebel Gao Xiandao misled the people with heterodox teachings, and thousands of commoners were led astray. After their defeat, the Huguang Branch Secretariat ordered Chahan and the surveillance officials to try the cases together. When the facts were established, they proposed executing a few ringleaders, releasing everyone else, and burning the registers. The others objected. Chahan said, "I alone will bear the responsibility—you need not be implicated." His administration won the highest praise, and he was promoted to director in the Henan Secretariat.
4
成宗崩，仁宗自藩邸入誅群臣之為異謀者，迎武宗于邊。 河南平章囊加台薦察罕，即驛召至上都，賜廄馬二匹、鈔一千貫、銀五十兩，曰：「卿少留，行用卿矣。 」武宗即位，立仁宗為皇太子，授察罕詹事院判，進僉詹事院事，賜銀百兩、錦二匹。 遣先還大都立院事。 仁宗至，謂曰：「上以故安西王地賜我，置都緫管府，卿其領之，慎揀僚屬，勿以詹事位高不屑此也。 進卿秩資德大夫。 」察罕叩頭謝曰：「都府之職，敢不恭命，進秩非所敢當。 」固辭，改正奉大夫，授以銀印。
When Chengzong died, Renzong left his princely estate, executed ministers who had plotted treason, and went to the frontier to welcome Wuzong. Nangatai, Grand Councillor of Henan, recommended Chahan, who was summoned at once by relay to Shangdu. He received two stable horses, a thousand strings of paper money, and fifty taels of silver, and was told, "Stay a little longer—you will soon be needed." When Wuzong ascended the throne he made Renzong crown prince, appointed Chahan vice-director of the Heir Apparent's household, then promoted him to concurrent director, and gave him a hundred taels of silver and two lengths of brocade. Chahan was sent ahead to Dadu to set up the household administration. When Renzong arrived he said, "The emperor has granted me the former Anxi princely domain and set up a chief commissionerate—you shall head it. Choose your staff carefully, and do not look down on the post because your rank as Household Director is high. I am raising your rank to Grand Master of Supporting Virtue." Chahan kowtowed and replied, "I dare not refuse the chief commissionerate, but the promotion is more than I deserve." He firmly declined; his rank was set at Grand Master for Proper Service, and he received a silver seal.
5
仁宗即位，拜中書參知政事，但緫持綱維，不屑細務，識者謂得大臣體。 帝嘗賜枸杞酒，曰：「以益卿壽。 」又語宰相曰：「察罕清素，可賜金束帶、鈔萬貫。 」前後賞賚不可勝計。 皇慶元年，進榮祿大夫、平章政事、商議中書省事。 乞歸解州立碑先塋，許之。 暮年，居德安白雲山別墅，以白雲自號。 嘗入見，帝望見曰：「白雲先生來也。 」其被寵遇如此。 帝嘗問張良何如人，對曰：「佐高帝，興漢，功成身退，賢者也。 」又問狄仁傑，對曰：「當唐室中衰，能卒保社稷，亦賢相也。 」因誦范仲淹所撰碑詞甚熟。 帝歎息良久曰：「察罕博學如此邪。 」嘗譯貞觀政要以獻。 帝大悅，詔繕寫遍賜左右。 且詔譯帝範。 又命譯脫必赤顏名曰聖武開天紀，及紀年纂要、太宗平金始末等書，俱付史館。 嘗以病請告，暨還朝，帝御萬歲山圓殿，與平章李孟入謝。 帝曰：「白雲病愈邪？ 」頓首對曰：「老臣衰病，無補聖明，荷陛下哀矜，放歸田里，幸甚，不覺沉痾去體爾。 」命賜茵以坐。 顧李孟曰：「知止不辱，今見其人。 朕始以荅剌罕、不憐吉台、囊加台等言用之，誠多裨益。 有言察罕不善者，其人即非善人也。 」又語及科舉并前古帝王賜姓命氏之事，因賜察罕姓白氏。
When Renzong ascended the throne, Chahan was made Vice Director of the Central Secretariat. He held only the broad lines of policy and left petty affairs aside; observers said he had the bearing of a true statesman. The emperor once gave him wolfberry wine, saying, "To add years to your life." He also told the chancellors, "Chahan lives plainly—give him a gold belt and ten thousand strings of paper money." Rewards before and after were beyond counting. In 1312 he was promoted to Grand Master of Splendid Happiness, Grand Councillor, and consultant on Central Secretariat affairs. He asked leave to return to Xiezhou and set up a stele at his family's graves; the request was granted. In old age he lived at a villa on White Cloud Mountain in De'an and styled himself Master White Cloud. Once when he came to court, the emperor saw him from afar and said, "Master White Cloud has arrived." Such was the favor he enjoyed. The emperor once asked what sort of man Zhang Liang was. Chahan replied, "He aided the founding emperor, helped raise Han, and withdrew once his work was done—a true worthy." Asked about Di Renjie, he said, "When the Tang was in decline he still preserved the realm in the end—another worthy chancellor." He then recited from memory the stele inscription Fan Zhongyan had written, word for word. The emperor sighed and said, "Chahan is this learned!" He once translated the Essentials of Government from the Zhenguan era and presented it to the throne. The emperor was delighted and ordered copies made for all his close attendants. He also ordered a translation of the Models of the Emperor. He also ordered translated the Tuobichiyan work called Record of the Sacred Martial Opening of Heaven, along with Essentials of Chronology, the Complete Account of Taizong's Pacification of Jin, and other works, all of which were sent to the Historiography Office. Once he took sick leave; when he returned to court the emperor received him in the round hall on Longevity Mountain, and he entered with Grand Councillor Li Meng to give thanks. The emperor asked, "Has Master White Cloud recovered?" He kowtowed and said, "This old servant's weakness is of no use to your wisdom. Thanks to your compassion in letting me return home, I am most fortunate—my grave illness has left me without my noticing." The emperor had a cushion brought for him to sit on. Turning to Li Meng he said, "Knowing when to stop and keep one's honor—today I see such a man." I first employed him on the recommendation of Darqan, Bulinjutai, Nangatai, and others, and he has truly been of great help. Whoever speaks ill of Chahan is not a good man himself. He also spoke of the civil examinations and of how ancient emperors granted surnames and clans, and bestowed on Chahan the surname Bai.
6
初，察罕生於河中，其夜，天氣清肅，月白如晝。 相者賀曰：「是兒必貴。 」國人謂白為察罕，故名察罕。 察罕天性孝友，田宅之在河中者，悉分與諸昆弟。 昆弟貧來歸者，復分與田宅奴婢，縱奴為民者甚衆。 故人多稱長者。 既致仕，優游八年，以壽終。
When Chahan was born in Hezhong, the night was clear and still, and the moon shone white as day. A physiognomist congratulated the family, saying, "This boy will surely rise high." In the realm white was called chahan, and so he was given the name Chahan. By nature Chahan was filial and brotherly; he divided all his land and houses in Hezhong among his brothers. When poor brothers came to him, he again gave them land, houses, and servants, and freed many slaves to become commoners. For this many called him a man of true generosity. After retirement he lived at ease for eight years and died at a full age.
7
子外家奴，太中大夫、武岡路緫管； 李家奴，早卒； 忽都篤，承直郎、高郵府判官。 孫九人，仕者二人：闊闊不花、哈撒。
His son Waijianu was Grand Master of the Palace and circuit intendant of Wugang; Lijianu, who died young; and Hutudu, Gentleman for Supporting Uprightness and assistant prefect of Gaoyou. He had nine grandsons; two held office: Kuokuobuhua and Hasa.
8
曲樞，西土人。 曾祖達不台，祖阿達台，父質理花台，世贈功臣，追封王爵。
Qushu was a native of the western regions. His great-grandfather was Dabutai, his grandfather Adatai, and his father Zhilihuatai; for generations the family received posthumous honors as meritorious ministers and princely titles.
9
曲樞七歲失怙恃。 既壯，沉密靜專，為徽仁裕聖皇太后宮臣。 仁宗幼時，以曲樞可任保傅，左右擁翼。 曲樞入則佐視食飲，出則抱負游衍，鞠躬盡力，夙夜匪懈。 大德三年，武宗緫戎北邊。 九年，讒人亂國。 仁宗侍皇太后之國于懷，未幾，復之雲中，連年奔走不暇。 曲樞櫛風沐雨，跋涉艱險，無倦色。
Qushu lost both parents when he was seven. When he came of age he was grave, discreet, and steady, and became an attendant in the palace of the Empress Dowager of Sagely Benevolence and Abundant Grace. In Renzong's youth Qushu was judged fit to be his guardian and tutor and was kept at his side to support and protect him. Within the palace Qushu helped watch over his meals; outside he carried him on outings. He bent every effort to the task and never slackened, day or night. In 1299 Wuzong took command of the northern armies. In 1305 slanderers threw the court into turmoil. Renzong accompanied the empress dowager when she withdrew to Huai; soon they were back in Yunzhong, and for years they were constantly on the move. Qushu braved wind and rain and hard roads without ever showing weariness.
10
成宗崩，仁宗奉太后入朝，殲姦黨，迎武宗即皇帝位，仁宗為皇太子，天下以安。 拜曲樞榮祿大夫、平章政事，行大司農。 未幾，進光祿大夫，領詹事院事，加特進，封應國公。 至大元年，拜開府儀同三司、太子詹事、平章軍國重事、上柱國，依前大司農、應國公。 進太子太保，領典醫監事。 四年，授太保、錄軍國重事、集賢大學士，兼大司農，領崇祥院、司天臺事，官爵勳封如故。 後以疾薨于位。
When Chengzong died, Renzong brought the empress dowager to court, destroyed the traitorous faction, and welcomed Wuzong to the throne; Renzong was made crown prince and the realm was at peace. Qushu was appointed Grand Master of Splendid Happiness and Grand Councillor, with concurrent charge of the chief ministry of agriculture. Soon he was promoted to Grand Master of the Palace, put in charge of the Heir Apparent's household, granted special advancement, and enfeoffed as Duke of Ying. In 1308 he was made Grand Preceptor with ceremonial equal to the Three Excellencies, Household Director of the Heir Apparent, Grand Councillor for Military and State Affairs of the First Rank, and Pillar of the State, while retaining his posts as chief minister of agriculture and Duke of Ying. He was promoted to Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent and put in charge of the Directorate of Imperial Physicians. In 1311 he was made Grand Mentor, Recorder of Military and State Affairs of the First Rank, and Grand Academician of the Hall of Assembled Worthies, while retaining the chief ministry of agriculture and taking charge of the Chongxiang Court and the Directorate of Astronomy; his other titles and honors were unchanged. He later died in office of illness.
11
子二人。 長伯都，大德十一年特授翰林學士、嘉議大夫，遷中奉大夫、典寶監卿，加資德大夫、治書侍御史。 至大元年，陞榮祿大夫，遙授中書平章政事，改侍御史。 明年，拜中書參知政事，進右丞，年三十二而卒。 子咬住。
He had two sons. The elder son Bodu: in 1307 he was specially made Hanlin Academician and Grand Master of Court Discussion, then promoted to Grand Master for Supporting Service and Director of the Directorate of Imperial Treasures, and later to Grand Master of Supporting Virtue and Attending Censor-in-Chief. In 1308 he was promoted to Grand Master of Splendid Happiness, given the honorary title of Grand Councillor of the Central Secretariat, and made Attending Censor. The following year he was made Vice Director of the Central Secretariat and promoted to Right Chancellor; he died at thirty-two. His son was Yaozhu.
12
阿禮海牙
Alihaiya
13
阿禮海牙，畏吾氏，集賢大學士脫列之子也。 兄野訥，事仁宗於潛邸。 大德九年，仁宗奉興聖太后出居懷州。 從者單弱，多懷去計。 野訥獨無所畏難。 成宗崩，權臣阿附中宮，不遣使告哀宗藩。 仁宗有聞，將自懷州入京，宮臣或持不可。 野訥屏人密啟曰：「天子晏駕而皇子已早卒，天下無主，邪謀方興。 懷寧王及殿下，世祖、裕皇賢孫，人心所屬久矣。 宜急奉太母入定大計，邪謀必止。 迎立懷寧王以正神器，在此行矣。 」仁宗即白太后，以二月至京師，遂誅柄臣二人，遣使迎武宗。 武宗即位，召野訥，賜玉帶，授嘉議大夫、祕書監。 仁宗居東宮，兼太子右庶子，遷侍御史、崇祥院使，兼將作院使。 閩有綉工，工官大集民間子女居肆督責，吏因為奸利，野訥奏罷之，閩人感悅。 尋兼太醫院使。 仁宗即位，請召文武老臣，咨以朝政。 又請以中都苑囿還諸民。 拜樞密院副使，進同知樞密院事。 命為中書平章政事，辭不拜。 野訥之在臺及侍禁中，於國家事有不便，輒言之，言無不納。 然韜晦惡盈，不泄於外。 延祐四年卒，年四十。 贈推誠保節翊運功臣、金紫光祿大夫、行中書省左丞相、上柱國、趙國公，諡忠靖。
Alihaiya was a Uyghur and the son of Tuolie, Grand Academician of the Hall of Assembled Worthies. His elder brother Yene had served Renzong when he was still in his princely household. In 1305 Renzong accompanied the Empress Dowager of Sagely Birth when she withdrew to Huai Prefecture. The entourage was small and weak, and many thought of leaving. Yene alone showed no fear or hesitation. When Chengzong died, the powerful minister Azhong controlled the inner palace and sent no envoys to announce the mourning to the princes. When Renzong heard of it he prepared to enter the capital from Huai Prefecture, but some palace attendants argued against it. Yene sent everyone away and secretly urged, "The emperor is dead and the crown prince died young. The realm has no master, and treacherous plots are rising. The Prince of Huaining and Your Highness are worthy grandsons of Shizu and Yuzong, and the people's hearts have long been with you. You should hurry to bring the Grand Empress Dowager in and settle the succession—the plots will collapse. Welcoming the Prince of Huaining to the throne and securing the regalia depends on this move alone." Renzong at once told the empress dowager; in the second month they reached the capital, executed two powerful ministers, and sent envoys to welcome Wuzong. When Wuzong ascended the throne he summoned Yene, gave him a jade belt, and appointed him Grand Master of Court Discussion and Director of the Secretariat. While Renzong was crown prince, Yene also served as Right Mentor of the Heir Apparent, then became Attending Censor, Director of the Chongxiang Court, and concurrently Director of the Directorate of Palace Buildings. In Fujian, craft officials herded commoners' children into workshops to drive embroidery labor while clerks profited corruptly. Yene memorialized to end the practice, and the people of Min were grateful. Soon he was also made Director of the Imperial Medical Service. When Renzong ascended the throne, Yene asked that veteran civil and military officials be summoned to advise on state affairs. He also asked that the imperial parks around the central capital be returned to the people. He was appointed Vice Director of the Bureau of Military Affairs and promoted to Concurrent Director. He was offered the post of Grand Councillor of the Central Secretariat but declined. Whether at the Censorate or in attendance within the palace, whenever he saw something harmful to the state he spoke up, and his advice was always accepted. Yet he kept a low profile and hated excess, and never let grievances spill outside. He died in 1317 at the age of forty. He was posthumously honored as Meritorious Minister Who Supports Sincerity, Preserves Integrity, and Aids the Dynasty, Grand Master of the Golden Purple and Golden Seal, Acting Left Chancellor of the Central Secretariat, Pillar of the State, and Duke of Zhao, with the posthumous name Loyal and Tranquil.
14
阿禮海牙亦早事武宗、仁宗，為宿衛，以清慎通敏與父兄並見信任。 十餘年間，歷華近，入侍帷幄，出踐省闥，廷無間言。 至治初，出為平章政事，歷鎮江浙、湖廣、河南、陝西四省，皆有惠政，汴人尤懷思之。 歸朝拜翰林學士承旨。 丁父憂，解官家居。
Alihaiya had also served Wuzong and Renzong early on as a palace guard; for his integrity, caution, and sharp intelligence he was trusted along with his father and brother. For more than ten years he rose through the highest posts—serving within the imperial curtains and governing in the provinces—and at court no one spoke against him. At the start of the Zhizhi reign he was sent out as Grand Councillor and governed Jiang-Zhe, Huguang, Henan, and Shaanxi in turn; everywhere his rule brought benefit, and the people of Bian cherished him most of all. On returning to court he was made Chief Academician of the Hanlin Academy. When his father died he left office and lived at home in mourning.
15
天曆元年秋，文宗入承大統。 阿禮海牙即易服南迎，至於汴郊見焉。 帝命復鎮汴省。 時當艱難之際，阿禮海牙高價糴粟，以峙糧儲，命近郡分治戎器，閱士卒，括馬民間，以備不虞。 先是，文宗即位之詔已播告天下，而陝西官府結連靖安王等起兵，東擊潼關。 阿禮海牙開府庫，量出鈔二十五萬緡，屬諸行省參政河南淮北蒙古軍都萬戶朶列圖、廉訪副使萬家閭犒軍河南以禦之。 令都鎮撫卜伯率軍吏巡行南陽、高門、武關、荊子諸隘，南至襄、川二江之口，督以嚴備。 萬戶博羅守潼關，不能軍。 是月二十五日，只兒哈率小汪緫帥、脫帖木兒萬戶等之兵，突出潼關，東掠閿鄉，披靈寶，盪陝州、新安諸郡邑，放兵四劫，迤邐前進。 河南告急之使狎至，而朶列圖亦以兵寡為言。
In the autumn of 1328 Wenzong succeeded to the throne. Alihaiya at once changed his dress and went south to welcome him, meeting him on the outskirts of Bian. The emperor ordered him back to govern the Henan Branch Secretariat at Bian. In those hard times Alihaiya bought grain at high prices to build reserves, ordered nearby prefectures to ready weapons, inspected troops, and requisitioned horses from the populace against emergencies. Wenzong's accession edict had already been proclaimed, yet officials in Shaanxi joined the Prince of Jing'an and others in rebellion and marched east against Tong Pass. Alihaiya opened the treasury and issued two hundred fifty thousand strings of paper money, charging Duolietu, administrative commissioner of the branch secretariat and chief ten-thousand household of the Henan and Huaibei Mongol army, and Wanjialu, vice surveillance commissioner, to reward troops in Henan for the defense. He ordered Metropolitan Pacification Commissioner Bobo to patrol the passes at Nanyang, Gaomen, Wuguan, and Jingzi, south to the mouths of the Xiang and Chuan rivers, and enforce strict readiness. The ten-thousand household Boluo held Tong Pass but could not keep his troops in order. On the twenty-fifth of that month Zhi'erha led the forces of Commander-in-Chief Xiaowang, Ten-Thousand Household Tuotiemuer, and others in a sudden breakout from Tong Pass. They raided east through Minxiang and Lingbao, overran Shaanzhou, Xin'an, and neighboring counties, let their troops plunder everywhere, and pressed forward. Urgent dispatches from Henan arrived one after another, and Duolietu too reported that his forces were too few.
16
十月一日，阿禮海牙集省憲官屬，問以長策，無有言者。 阿禮海牙曰：「汴在南北之交，使西人得至此，則江南三省之道不通于畿甸，軍旅應接何日息乎。 夫事有緩急輕重，今重莫如足兵，急莫如足食。 吾徵湖廣之平陽、保定兩翼軍，與吾省之鄧新翼、廬州、沂、郯砲弩手諸軍，以備虎牢； 裕州哈剌魯、鄧州孫萬戶兩軍，以備武關、荊子口。 以屬郡之兵及蒙古兩都萬戶、左右兩衛、諸部丁壯之可入軍者，給馬乘貲裝，立行伍，以次備諸隘。 芍陂等屯兵本自襄、鄧諸軍來田者，還其軍，益以民之丁壯，使守襄陽、白土、峽州諸隘。 別遣塔海以備自蜀至者，以汴、汝、荊、襄、兩淮之馬以給之，府庫不足，則命郡縣假諸殷富之家。 安豐等郡之粟，溯黃河運至于陝，糴諸汴、汝，近郡者，則運諸滎陽以達於虎牢。 吾與諸軍各奮忠義以從王事，宜無不濟者。 」衆曰：「唯。 」命即日部分行事。 自伯顏不花王以下省都事李元德等，凡省之屬吏與有官而家居者，各授以事而出。 廉訪使董守中、僉事沙沙在南陽，右丞脫帖木兒、廉訪使卜顏在虎牢，分遣兵馬以聽其調用。 餽餉之行，千車相望，阿禮海牙親閱實之，必豐必良，信以期會。 自虎牢之南至於襄、漢，無不畢給。 蓋為粟二十萬石，豆如之，兵甲五十五萬，芻萬萬。 是時，朝廷置行樞密院以緫西事。 襄、漢、荊湖、河南郡縣皆缺官，阿禮海牙便宜擇材以處之，朝廷皆從其請。
On the first day of the tenth month Alihaiya assembled secretariat and surveillance officials and asked for a plan; no one had an answer. Alihaiya said, "Bian stands at the crossroads of north and south. If the western rebels reach here, the three southern provinces will be cut off from the capital—when would the fighting ever end?" Every crisis has its priorities: nothing matters more now than enough troops, nothing is more urgent than enough grain. I will summon the Pingyang and Baoding wing armies from Huguang, together with our province's Dengxin wing, Luzhou, Yi, and Tan catapult and crossbow units, to hold Hulao; the Halalu army of Yuzhou and Ten-Thousand Household Sun's army of Dengzhou to guard Wuguan and Jingzikou. With troops from subordinate prefectures, the two metropolitan Mongol ten-thousand households, the left and right guards, and every able-bodied man who can serve, I will supply horses, funds, and gear, form ranks, and man the passes in turn. The garrison farmers at Shaopi and elsewhere originally came from the Xiang and Deng armies; send them back to their units and add civilian recruits to hold Xiangyang, Baitu, and Xiazhou. Send Tahai separately to block any advance from Shu, supply him with horses from Bian, Ru, Jing, Xiang, and the two Huai regions, and if the treasury falls short, have prefectures and counties borrow from wealthy families. Ship grain from Anfeng and other prefectures up the Yellow River toward Shaan; buy grain at Bian and Ru, and from nearby prefectures move it to Xingyang and on to Hulao. If we and every army give our loyal best to the throne's cause, we should succeed." Everyone answered, "Yes." He ordered the plan put into action that same day. From Prince Bayanbuhua down to Secretariat Director Li Yuande and the rest, every clerk and official at home on leave was given a task and sent out. Surveillance Commissioner Dong Shouzhong and Administrative Aide Shasha were at Nanyang; Right Chancellor Tuotiemuer and Surveillance Commissioner Buyan at Hulao; troops were sent to each to follow their orders. Supply trains rolled out by the thousand; Alihaiya personally inspected every load to ensure it was ample, sound, and on time. From south of Hulao to the Xiang and Han regions, nothing was left unsupplied. In all they gathered two hundred thousand piculs of grain, as many of beans, five hundred fifty thousand sets of arms and armor, and a vast quantity of fodder. At this time the court set up a mobile Bureau of Military Affairs to direct the western campaign. Prefectures and counties across Xiang, Han, Jinghu, and Henan lacked officials; Alihaiya appointed capable men on his own authority, and the court approved every request.
17
是月，西兵逼河南，行院使來報，曰：「西人北行者度河中以趨懷、孟、磁； 南行者帖木哥，過武關，掠鄧州而殘之，直趨襄陽，攻破郡邑三十餘，橫絕數千里，所過殺官吏，焚廬舍，虜民人婦女財物，賊虐殄盡，西結囊家469f以蜀兵至矣。 」阿禮海牙益督餉西行，遣行院官塔海領兵攻帖木哥，而又設備於江、黃，置鐵繩於峽口，作舟艦以待戰。 十九日，師與西兵遇於鞏縣之石渡，而湖廣所徵太原之兵最為可用。 甫至，未及食，或趣之倍道以進，轉戰及暮，兩軍殺傷與墮澗谷死者相等，而虎牢遂為敵有。 兵儲巨萬，阿禮海牙盡其心、民殫其力者，一旦悉亡焉。 行省院與諸軍斂兵退。 二十二日至汴，民大恐。 阿禮海牙前後遣使告于朝，輒為也先捏留不遣，不得朝廷音問已二十日，阿禮海牙亦憂之，親出行撫其民。 乃修城闕以備衝突，立四門以通往來，戒卒伍以嚴守衛。 時雖甚危急，阿禮海牙朝夕出入，聲色不動，怡然如平時，衆賴以安。
That month, as western troops pressed Henan, the mobile bureau commissioner reported, "The northern column is crossing the Yellow River toward Huai, Meng, and Ci; the southern column under Temuge has passed Wuguan, ravaged Dengzhou, and driven straight on Xiangyang, taking more than thirty prefectures and counties and laying waste for thousands of li—killing officials, burning homes, and seizing people, women, and goods wherever they pass. In the west they have joined Niejiasai, and Shu troops are on the way." Alihaiya pressed supply lines westward harder, sent mobile bureau officer Tahai against Temuge, and prepared on the Yangzi and Yellow rivers—setting iron chains at the gorges and building warships for battle. On the nineteenth our army met the western forces at Shidu in Gong County; the Taiyuan troops summoned from Huguang proved the most reliable. They had just arrived and had not even eaten when someone urged a forced march. Fighting lasted until evening; casualties on both sides, including men who fell into ravines, were about equal—but Hulao fell to the enemy. The vast stores that Alihaiya and the people had poured their hearts and strength into were lost in a single day. The branch secretariat, military bureau, and allied armies drew back. On the twenty-second they reached Bian, and the populace was terrified. Alihaiya had sent repeated reports to court, but Yexianie detained every messenger; for twenty days he heard nothing from the capital. Anxious himself, he went among the people to reassure them. He repaired the walls and towers against assault, kept the four gates open for traffic, and ordered the garrison to tighten its guard. Though the crisis was extreme, Alihaiya came and went morning and evening without a change in voice or expression, as calm as in peacetime, and the people took heart from him.
18
十一月六日，西師逼城將百里而近，阿禮海牙召行院將帥、憲司與凡在官者，而告之曰：「吾荷國厚恩，唯有一死以報上耳。 行院之出，唯敵是圖，而退保吾城，不亦怯乎？ 然敵亦烏合之衆，何所受命而敢犯我乎？ 且吾甲兵非不堅勁，芻峙非不豐給，而弗利者，太平日久，將校不知兵，吏士不練習，彼所以得披猖至此。 彼誠知我聖天子之命，則衆沮而散爾，何足慮乎。 吾今遣使告于朝，請降詔大赦脅從詿誤。 比詔下，先募士，以即位詔及朝廷招諭之文入其軍，明示利害。 吾整大軍西嚮以征之，別遣驍將率精騎數千上龍門，繞出其後，使之進無所投，退無所歸，成擒於鞏、洛之間必矣。 而我軍所獲陝西官吏，命有司羈而食之，一無所戮。 」衆曰：「諾，唯命。 」即日與行院整兵南薰門外以行。
On the sixth of the eleventh month, with western troops within a hundred li of the city, Alihaiya summoned the mobile bureau commanders, surveillance officials, and every officer on duty and said, "I owe the state a deep debt; I have only my life to repay the throne. The mobile bureau was sent out to seek the enemy—yet we withdraw and hide behind our walls. Is that not cowardice? Yet the enemy are only a mob—whose orders do they follow that they dare attack us? Our arms are strong and our fodder ample—the trouble is that after long peace our officers do not know war and our men are untrained. That is why the enemy could run wild this far. If they truly knew the command of our sage emperor, their host would lose heart and scatter. What is there to fear? I will send envoys to court and ask for an edict of general amnesty for those coerced and misled. Before the edict arrives, recruit men to carry the accession proclamation and the court's summons into their camps and make the stakes plain. I will march a great army west to attack them and send fierce generals with several thousand elite horsemen up Longmen to take them from behind, so that advancing they have nowhere to go and retreating nowhere to flee—they will surely be taken between Gong and Luo. As for Shaanxi officials we capture, order the authorities to detain and feed them, and kill none. Everyone answered, "Yes—we await your orders." That same day he and the mobile bureau marshaled troops outside the Nanxun Gate and marched out.
19
會有使者自京師還，言齊王已克上都，奉天子寶璽來歸，刻日至京矣。 阿禮海牙乃置酒高會於省堂以賀，發書告屬郡，報諸江南三省，而募士得蘭住者齎書諭之。 西人猶搒掠蘭住，訊以其實，而朝廷亦遣都護月魯帖木兒從十餘人奉詔放散西軍之在虎牢者。 西人殺其從者之半，械都護以送諸荊王所。 荊王時在河南之白馬寺，以是西人雖未解散，各已駭悟。 又聞行省院以兵至，猶豫不敢進。 朝廷又使參政馮不花親諭之，乃信服。 靖安王遣使四輩與蘭住來請命，逡巡而去，難平。 阿禮海牙乃解嚴報捷，斂餘財以還民，從陝西求民人之被俘掠者歸其家，凡數千人。 陝西官吏被獲者，皆遣還其所。
Just then an envoy returned from the capital with word that the Prince of Qi had taken Shangdu and was bringing the imperial seals home, due in the capital on the appointed day. Alihaiya held a grand feast in the secretariat hall to celebrate, wrote to subordinate prefectures, notified the three southern provinces, and recruited a man named Lanzhe to carry the proclamation to the enemy. The western troops still beat and robbed Lanzhe and questioned him for the truth, while the court also sent Protector Yelutiemuer with a dozen men bearing an edict to disband the western army at Hulao. The western troops killed half his escort, bound the protector, and sent him to the Prince of Jing. The Prince of Jing was then at White Horse Temple in Henan; though the western army had not yet dispersed, each man was already shaken into doubt. Hearing that the branch secretariat and military bureau were marching against them, they hesitated and dared not advance. The court then sent Administrative Commissioner Fengbuhua in person to reason with them, and they submitted. The Prince of Jing'an sent four missions with Lanzhe to seek terms; they lingered and withdrew, and peace was still hard to secure. Alihaiya then stood down the alert and reported victory, returned surplus funds to the people, and from Shaanxi recovered several thousand civilians who had been seized and sent them home. Captured Shaanxi officials were all sent back to their posts.
20
阿禮海牙自始至鎮，迨乎告功，居汴省者數月。 後以功遷陝西行御史大夫，復拜中書省平章政事。
From his first arrival in office until he reported victory, Alihaiya spent several months at the Henan secretariat in Bian. Later, for his merit he was made Grand Censor of the Shaanxi Branch Secretariat and again appointed Grand Councillor of the Central Secretariat.
21
奕赫抵雅爾丁
Yihediyaerding
22
奕赫抵雅爾丁字太初，回回氏。 父亦速馬因，仕至大都南北兩城兵馬都指揮使。
Yihediyaerding, styled Taichu, was a Muslim. His father Yisumayin rose to metropolitan commander of military forces for the northern and southern sectors of Dadu.
23
奕赫抵雅爾丁幼穎悟嗜學，所讀書一過目即終身不忘。 尤工其國字語。 初為中書掾，以年勞授江西行省員外郎，入為吏部主事，不再閱月，固辭。 擢刑部員外郎，四方所上獄，反復披閱成牘，多所平反。 遷陝西漢中道肅政廉訪司僉事，不赴。 改中書右司員外郎，尋陞郎中。
From boyhood Yihediyaerding was brilliant and eager to learn; whatever he read once he remembered for life. He was especially skilled in his people's script and language. He began as a clerk in the Central Secretariat, was made outer director of the Jiangxi Branch Secretariat for long service, then entered the Ministry of Personnel as director—but within a month he firmly declined the post. He was promoted to outer director of the Ministry of Justice, repeatedly reviewed trial dossiers sent from every province, and reversed many wrongful convictions. He was transferred to administrative aide on the Shaanxi-Hanzhong surveillance commission but did not take up the post. He was made outer director of the Right Department of the Central Secretariat and soon promoted to director.
24
一日，與同列共議獄，有異其說者，奕赫抵雅爾丁曰：「公等讀律，苟不能變通以適事宜，譬之醫者，雖熟於方論，而不能切脈用藥，則於疾痛奚益哉。 」同列雖不平，識者服其為名言。 大德八年肆赦，廷議惟官吏因事受賕者不預。 奕赫抵雅爾丁曰：「不可。 恩如雨露，萬物均被，贓吏固可嫉，比之盜賊則有間矣。 宥盜而不宥吏，何耶？」
One day, while discussing a case with colleagues, someone disagreed with him. Yihediyaerding said, "You study the law, but if you cannot adapt it to the circumstances, you are like physicians who know formulas by heart yet cannot feel the pulse or prescribe medicine—what good does that do the patient?" His colleagues were displeased, but men of judgment hailed it as a memorable saying. In 1304 a general amnesty was proclaimed; the court debated excluding only officials who had taken bribes in office. Yihediyaerding said, "That will not do." Grace is like rain and dew—it should fall on all alike. Corrupt officials are detestable, but they are not the same as robbers and thieves. Why pardon thieves but not officials?"
25
刑部嘗有獄事，上讞既論決，已而丞相知其失，以譴右司主者。 奕赫抵雅爾丁初未嘗署其案，因取成案閱之，竊署其名於下。 或訝之曰：「茲獄之失，公實不與，丞相方譴怒而公反追署其案，何也？ 」奕赫抵雅爾丁曰：「吾偶不署此案耳，豈有與諸君同事而獨幸免哉。 」丞相聞而賢之，同列因以獲免。
The Ministry of Justice once had a case in which the superior memorial had already ruled, but the chancellor later learned of the error and rebuked the chief of the Right Department. Yihediyaerding had never signed that dossier, so he took the completed file, read it through, and quietly signed his name at the bottom. Someone asked in surprise, "You had nothing to do with this case's error, and the chancellor is angry—why add your name to the file now?" Yihediyaerding replied, "I simply failed to sign this case—how could I work with all of you and escape blame alone?" When the chancellor heard of it he admired him, and his colleagues were spared punishment.
26
遷左司郎中。 時左司闕一都事，平章梁暗都剌謂奕赫抵雅爾丁曰：「人之材幹固嘗有之，惟篤實不欺為難得，公當以所知舉。 」奕赫抵雅爾丁遂以王毅、李迪為言，一時輿論莫不稱允。 又嘗論朝士，如王仁卿、賈元播、高彥敬、敬威卿、李清臣輩可大用，時諸公處下僚，後皆如其言。 遷翰林侍講學士、知制誥兼修國史，轉中奉大夫、集賢大學士。
He was promoted to director of the Left Department. The Left Department then lacked a director of affairs. Grand Councillor Liangandula told Yihediyaerding, "Talent is common enough, but steadfast honesty is rare—you should recommend men you know." Yihediyaerding named Wang Yi and Li Di, and public opinion at once approved. He also once said that men such as Wang Renqing, Jia Yuanbo, Gao Yanjing, Jing Weiqing, and Li Qingchen were fit for high office; they were then in junior posts, and later events proved him right. He was made Hanlin Attending Lecturer, Drafter of Imperial Edicts, and concurrent compiler of the national history, then promoted to Grand Master for Proper Service and Grand Academician of the Hall of Assembled Worthies.
27
未幾，除江東建康道肅政廉訪使。 始視事，見以獄具陳列庭下甚備，問之，乃前官創製以待有罪者。 奕赫抵雅爾丁蹙然曰：「凡逮至臬司，皆命官及有出身之吏，廉得其情，則將服罪，獄具毋庸施也。 」即屏去之。 監憲一年，贓吏削跡。
Soon he was appointed surveillance commissioner of the Jiangdong Jiankang circuit. On first taking office he found torture instruments laid out in full in the courtyard and asked about them; a predecessor had prepared them for the guilty. Yihediyaerding frowned and said, "Everyone brought before this commission is a commissioned official or qualified clerk. Once the facts are fairly established they will confess—there is no need for torture." He had them removed at once. After a year in office, corrupt officials had disappeared.
28
至大初元，立尚書省，拜參議尚書省事，召至京師，懇辭不就。 改立中書省，復拜參議中書省事，亦以疾辭。 延祐元年卒，年四十有七。
At the start of the Zhida reign the Ministry of Revenue was established and he was offered the post of consultant; summoned to the capital, he firmly declined. When the Central Secretariat was restored he was again offered the post of consultant and again declined on grounds of illness. He died in 1314 at the age of forty-seven.
29
脫烈海牙
Tuoliehaiya
30
脫烈海牙，畏吾氏。 世居別失拔里之地。 曾祖闊華八撒朮，當太祖西征，導其主亦都護迎降。 帝嘉其有識，欲官之，辭以不敏。 祖八剌朮，始徙真定，仕至帥府鎮撫。 富而樂施，或貸不償，則火其券，人稱為長者。 父闍里赤，性純正，知讀書。
Tuoliehaiya was a Uyghur. His family had long lived in Bieshibali. His great-grandfather Kuohuabasazhu, when Taizu marched west, guided his lord the Idiqut to submit. The emperor praised his judgment and wished to give him office, but he declined as unworthy. His grandfather Balazhu first settled at Zhending and rose to pacification commissioner of the marshal's headquarters. He was wealthy and generous; when loans went unpaid he burned the bonds, and people called him a true elder. His father Chanlizhi was upright by nature and literate.
31
脫烈海牙幼嗜學，警敏絕人。 性整暇，雖居倉卒，未嘗見其急遽。 喜從文士游，犬馬聲色之娛，一無所好。 由中書宣使，出為寧晉主簿。 改隆平縣達魯花赤，均賦興學，劭農平訟，橋梁、水防、備荒之政，無一不舉。 及滿去，民勒石以紀其政。 拜監察御史。 時江西胡參政殺其弟，訟久不決，脫烈海牙一訊竟伏其辜。 出僉燕南道肅政廉訪司事，務存大體，不事苛察。 在任六年，黜污吏百四十有奇。 召為戶部郎中，轉右司員外郎，陞右司郎中。 贊畫之力居多。 仁宗在東宮，知其嗜學，出祕府經籍及聖賢圖像以賜，時人榮之。 母霍氏卒，哀毀骨立，事聞，賜鈔五萬貫，給葬事。 起為吏部尚書，量能敘爵，以平允稱。 改禮部尚書，領會同館事。 進中奉大夫、荊湖北道宣慰使。 適峽人艱食，脫烈海牙先發廩賑之，而後以聞。 朝議韙之。
From youth Tuoliehaiya loved learning and was exceptionally quick-witted. He was orderly and unhurried by nature; even in sudden crises he was never seen flustered. He enjoyed the company of scholars and cared nothing for horses, hounds, music, or women. Starting as a Central Secretariat envoy, he was sent out as registrar of Ningjin. As darughachi of Longping he equalized taxes, promoted schools, encouraged farming and settled lawsuits, and carried out every policy for bridges, flood control, and famine relief. When his term ended the people set up a stone to commemorate his rule. He was appointed Investigating Censor. When Administrative Commissioner Hu of Jiangxi killed his younger brother, the case dragged on until Tuoliehaiya tried it once and brought out a confession. He served as concurrent administrative aide on the Yannan surveillance commission, keeping to broad principles and avoiding petty scrutiny. In six years he dismissed more than a hundred and forty corrupt officials. He was recalled as director of the Ministry of Revenue, made outer director of the Right Department, and promoted to director. His contribution in counsel was the greatest. While Renzong was crown prince, knowing his love of learning, he gave him classics from the imperial library and portraits of the sages—a mark of honor at the time. When his mother Lady Huo died he mourned until he was wasted to skin and bone; the court heard of it and granted fifty thousand strings of paper money for the funeral. Recalled as Minister of Personnel, he assigned ranks according to merit and was known for fairness. He was made Minister of Rites and put in charge of the Hall of United Submission. He was promoted to Grand Master for Proper Service and Pacification Commissioner of the northern Jinghu circuit. When the people of the gorges were short of food, Tuoliehaiya opened the granaries to relieve them first and reported afterward. The court approved his action.

至治三年，遷淮東宣慰使。 七月，以疾卒于廣陵，年六十有七，贈通奉大夫、河南江北等處行中書省參知政事、護軍，追封恒山郡公。 弟觀音奴，廉明材幹，亦仕至清顯云。
In 1323 he was transferred to Pacification Commissioner of Huaidong. In the seventh month he died of illness at Guangling at sixty-seven. He was posthumously honored as Grand Master for Imperial Service, Vice Director of the Henan and Jiangbei Branch Secretariat, and Guardian of the Army, and enfeoffed as Duke of Hengshan. His younger brother Guanyinnu was incorrupt and capable and likewise rose to high and honorable office.
