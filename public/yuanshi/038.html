1
順帝一
Emperor Shundi, Part One
2
順帝名妥歡帖睦爾，明宗之長子。 母罕祿魯氏，名邁來迪，郡王阿兒廝蘭之裔孫也。 初，太祖取西北諸國，阿兒廝蘭率其眾來降，乃封為郡王，俾領其部族。 及明宗北狩，過其地，納罕祿魯氏。 延祐七年四月丙寅，生帝於北方。
Emperor Shundi, whose taboo name was Toghon Temür, was the eldest son of Emperor Mingzong. His mother belonged to the Qarlukh clan and was named Mailaiti, a descendant of Prince Arslan. In the beginning, when Taizu conquered the northwestern states, Arslan led his people in submission and was enfeoffed as a commandery prince to govern his tribe. When Mingzong marched north and passed through that territory, he took a woman of the Qarlukh clan. In the fourth month of the seventh year of Yanyou, on the day bingyin, the Emperor was born in the north.
3
三年八月己酉，文宗崩，燕鐵木兒請文宗後立太子燕帖古思，後不從，而命立明宗次子懿璘只班，是為寧宗。 十一月壬辰，寧宗崩，燕鐵木兒復請立燕帖古思，文宗後曰：「吾子尚幼，妥歡貼睦爾在廣西，今年十三矣，且明宗之長子，禮當立之。 」乃命中書左丞闊裏吉思迎帝於靜江。 至良鄉，具鹵簿以迓之。 燕鐵木兒既見帝，並馬徐行，具陳迎立之意。 帝幼且畏之，一無所答。 於是燕鐵木兒疑之，故帝至京，久不得立。 適太史亦言帝不可立，立則天下亂，以故議未決。 遷延者數月，國事皆決於燕鐵木兒，奏文宗後而行之。 俄而燕鐵木兒死，後乃與大臣定議立帝，且曰：「萬歲之後，其傳位於燕帖古思，若武宗、仁宗故事。 」諸王宗戚奉上璽綬勸進。
On the day jiyou in the eighth month of the third year, Wenzong died. Yan Temür asked Wenzong's empress to enthrone the crown prince Arig Kabak, but she refused and instead had Mingzong's second son Irinjibal installed as Emperor Ningzong. In the eleventh month, on the day renchen, Ningzong died. Yan Temür again pressed for Arig Kabak's enthronement. Wenzong's empress said, "My son is still young. Toghon Temür is in Guangxi; he is thirteen this year, and as Mingzong's eldest son he is the one who should succeed by right." " She then ordered Korghuz, left vice director of the Secretariat, to escort the Emperor from Jingjiang. At Liangxiang a full imperial escort was prepared to receive him. When Yan Temür met the Emperor, they rode abreast at an easy pace while he explained at length why he had come to install him on the throne. The Emperor was still young and afraid of him, and made no reply at all. Yan Temür thereupon grew suspicious of him, and so even after the Emperor reached the capital his enthronement was long delayed. At the same time the court astronomers declared that the Emperor should not be enthroned, warning that his installation would throw the realm into chaos, and for this reason the matter remained undecided. Months passed in delay. State affairs were all decided by Yan Temür, who submitted memorials to Wenzong's empress and then acted on her approval. Before long Yan Temür died. The empress then joined the senior ministers in deciding to enthrone the Emperor, adding, "After my death the throne shall pass to Arig Kabak, following the precedent of Wuzong and Renzong." " The princes and imperial kinsmen presented the imperial seal and cord and urged him to ascend the throne.
4
四年六月己巳，帝即位於上都，詔曰：
On the day jisi in the sixth month of the fourth year, the Emperor ascended the throne at Shangdu and issued an edict that read:
5
洪惟我太祖皇帝，受命於天，肇造區夏； 世祖皇帝，奄有四海，治功大備； 列聖相傳，丕承前烈。 我皇祖武宗皇帝入纂大統，及致和之季，皇考明宗皇帝遠居朔漠，劄牙篤皇帝戡定內難，讓以天下。 我皇考賓天，劄牙篤皇帝復正宸極。 治化方隆，奄棄臣庶。 今皇太后召大臣燕鐵木兒、伯顏等曰：「昔者闊徹伯、脫脫木兒、只兒哈郎等謀逆，以明宗太子為名，又先為八不沙始以妒忌，妄構誣言，疏離骨肉。 逆臣等既正其罪，太子遂遷於外。 劄牙篤皇帝後知其妄。 尋至大漸，顧命有曰：『朕之大位，其以朕兄子繼之。 』」時以朕遠征南服，以朕弟懿璘只班登大位，以安百姓，乃遽至大故。 皇太后體承劄牙篤皇帝遺意，以武宗皇帝之元孫，明宗皇帝之世嫡，以賢以長，在予一人，遣使迎還。 征集宗室諸王來會，合辭推戴。 今奉皇太后勉進之篤，宗親大臣懇請之至，以至順四年六月初八日，即皇帝位於上都。 於戲！ 惟天、惟祖宗全付予有家，栗栗危懼，若涉淵冰，罔知攸濟。 尚賴宗親臣鄰，交修不逮，以底隆平。 其赦天下。
Greatly do We reflect that Our Taizu Emperor received Heaven's mandate and first founded the Middle Kingdom; Shizu Emperor came to hold all within the four seas, and his governing achievements were brought to full completion; the successive sage emperors transmitted the throne, greatly inheriting the achievements of those who came before. Our imperial ancestor Emperor Wuzong assumed the Great Foundation; at the close of the Zhihe era Our father Emperor Mingzong dwelt far in the northern steppe, while Emperor Jayaatu quelled the internal strife and yielded the realm. When Our father departed this life, Emperor Jayaatu again restored the imperial throne. His rule was just reaching its height when he suddenly left his ministers and people. Now the Empress Dowager summoned the senior ministers Yan Temür, Bayan, and others, saying, "Formerly Kochebo, Toghtogha, Jir Qalan, and others plotted rebellion in the name of Mingzong's heir; and Budashiri, moved by jealousy, had earlier fabricated false charges to drive a wedge between close kin." Once the traitors had been punished, the heir was sent away to the provinces. Emperor Jayaatu later learned that this was false. Soon he fell gravely ill and left a deathbed command: 'Let my elder brother's son succeed to the great throne.' '" Because We were then on campaign in the south, Our younger brother Irinjibal was enthroned to reassure the people, but he too suddenly died." The Empress Dowager, honoring Emperor Jayaatu's dying wish, recognized that We are Wuzong's eldest grandson and Mingzong's legitimate heir — worthy and senior, residing in Us alone — and dispatched envoys to bring Us back. She summoned the princes of the imperial clan to assemble, and with one voice they urged Our enthronement. Now, yielding to the Empress Dowager's earnest urging and the impassioned pleas of the imperial kin and senior ministers, on the eighth day of the sixth month of the fourth year of Zhishun We have ascended the imperial throne at Shangdu. Alas! Heaven and the ancestors have wholly entrusted the realm to Us; trembling with fear, as if walking over deep water and thin ice, We know not how We shall succeed. We still rely on the imperial kin, Our ministers, and Our allies to help Us remedy Our failings and bring the realm to lasting peace. Let there be a general amnesty throughout the realm.
6
時有阿魯輝帖木兒者，明宗親臣也，言於帝曰：「天下事重，宜委宰相決之，庶可責其成功； 若躬自聽斷，則必負惡名。 」帝信之，由是深居宮中，每事無所專焉。 辛未，命伯顏為太師、中書右丞相、上柱國、監修國史，兼奎章閣大學士，領學士院、太史院、回回、漢人司天監事； 撒敦為太傅、左丞相。 是月，大霖雨，京畿水平地丈餘，饑民四十餘萬，詔以鈔四萬錠賑之。 涇河溢，關中水災。 黃河大溢，河南水災。 兩淮旱，民大饑。
At that time a certain Aruq Temür, a close minister of Mingzong, said to the Emperor, "The affairs of the realm are weighty and ought to be left to the chief ministers to decide, so that they may be held accountable for results;" "if Your Majesty personally hears and decides every matter, you will surely earn a bad name." " The Emperor believed him and thereafter kept to the inner palace, taking personal charge of nothing. On the day xinwei, Bayan was appointed Grand Preceptor, right chancellor of the Secretariat, supreme pillar of state, and supervisor of the national history, while also serving as grand academician of the Kuizhang Pavilion and directing the Hanlin Academy, the Astronomical Bureau, and the Muslim and Han astronomical directorates; Satu was appointed Grand Tutor and left chancellor. That month torrential rains flooded the capital region to more than a zhang on level ground. More than four hundred thousand people faced famine, and an edict ordered relief with forty thousand ingots of paper money. The Jing River burst its banks, and Guanzhong suffered flooding. The Yellow River flooded severely, bringing disaster to Henan. Drought struck the two Huai regions, and the people suffered severe famine.
7
秋七月，霖雨。 潮州路水。 己亥，太陰犯房宿。
In the seventh month of autumn there was continuous rain. Chaozhou circuit was flooded. On the day jihai, the moon transited the lodge Fang.
8
八月壬申，鞏昌徽州山崩。 是月，立燕鐵木兒女伯牙吾氏為皇后。
In the eighth month, on the day renshen, mountains collapsed in Gongchang and Huizhou. That month Yan Temür's daughter of the Bayiju clan was installed as empress.
9
九月甲午，太陰犯填星。 乙未，太陰犯天江。 甲寅，中書省臣言：「官員遞升，窒礙選法。 今請自省、院、臺官外，其餘不許遞升。 」從之。 丁巳，太陰犯填星。 己未，太陰犯氐宿。 庚申，詔太師、右丞相伯顏，太傅、左丞相撒敦，專理國家大事，其餘官不得兼領三職。 秦州山崩。 賑恤寧夏饑民五萬三千人一月。 詔免儒人役。
In the ninth month, on the day jiawu, the moon transited Saturn. On the day yiwei, the moon transited Tianjiang. On the day jiayin, Secretariat officials reported, "Successive promotion of officials obstructs the selection regulations." "We now request that, apart from officials of the Secretariat, the Censorate, and the various courts, no others be permitted successive promotion." " The request was approved. On the day dingsi, the moon transited Saturn. On the day jiwei, the moon transited the lodge Di. On the day gengshen, an edict directed Grand Preceptor and right chancellor Bayan and Grand Tutor and left chancellor Satu to manage the great affairs of state exclusively, and forbade other officials from concurrently holding three offices. Mountains collapsed in Qinzhou. Fifty-three thousand famine victims in Ningxia were given relief for one month. An edict exempted scholars from corvée labor.
10
冬十月甲子，太陰犯斗宿。 丙寅，鳳州山崩。 戊辰，改元，詔曰：「在昔世祖皇帝，紹開丕圖，稽古建元，立經陳紀，列聖相承，恪遵成憲。 肆予沖人，嗣大歷服，茲圖治之雲初，嘉與民而更始。 乃新紀號，誕告多方，其以至順四年為元統元年。 於戲！ 一元運於四時，惟裁成之有道； 大統綿於萬世，思保佑於無疆。 」中書省臣言：「凡朝賀遇雨，請便服行禮。 」從之。 己巳，加知樞密院事、答剌罕答裏金紫光祿大夫。 庚午，詔以察罕腦兒宣慰司人民，止令應當徽政院差發。 癸酉，雲南亻嵬羅土官渾鄧馬弄來貢方物，詔以其地升立散府。 丁丑，依皇太后行年之數，釋放罪囚二十七人。 庚辰，奉文宗皇帝及太皇太后御容於大承天護聖寺，命左丞相撒敦為隆祥使，奉其祭祀。 乙酉，詔以高郵府為伯顏食邑。 戊子，封撒敦為榮王，食邑廬州。 唐其勢襲父封為太平王，進階金紫光祿大夫。 庚寅，中書省臣請集議武宗、英宗、明宗三朝皇后升祔。
In the tenth month of winter, on the day jiazi, the moon transited the lodge Dou. On the day bingyin, mountains collapsed in Fengzhou. On the day wuchen the era name was changed. An edict read, "In the past Emperor Shizu continued the great design, looked to antiquity to establish era names, and set forth institutions and regulations; the successive sage emperors transmitted the throne, reverently following the established statutes." "Now We, still young, have succeeded to the great foundation; at this first dawn of Our governance We gladly join the people in making a fresh start." "We therefore establish a new era name and proclaim it throughout the realm: the fourth year of Zhishun shall be the first year of Yuantong." Alas! The one origin moves through the four seasons — only through proper shaping and completion is there the Way; the great foundation extends through ten thousand generations — We pray for boundless blessing. " Secretariat officials reported, "Whenever court congratulations are held in rain, we request that informal dress be permitted for the rites." " The request was approved. On the day jisi, Darqan Dari, associate manager of the Bureau of Military Affairs, was promoted to Grand Master of Splendid Happiness with golden seal and purple robe. On the day gengwu, an edict directed that the people of the Chaghan Nao'er Pacification Commission answer only to labor requisitions from the Bureau of Palace Provisions. On the day guiyou, the native official Hun Dengmanong of the Wailuo tribe in Yunnan presented tribute; an edict ordered his territory elevated to a loose-administration prefecture. On the day dingchou, in accordance with the Empress Dowager's age, twenty-seven prisoners were released. On the day gengchen, the imperial portraits of Emperor Wenzong and the Grand Empress Dowager were installed at the Great Chengtian Protecting Sage Temple, and left chancellor Satu was appointed superintendent of auspicious rites to conduct their sacrifices. On the day yiyou, an edict made Gaoyou Prefecture Bayan's sustenance fief. On the day wuzi, Satu was enfeoffed as Prince Rong with Luzhou as his sustenance fief. Tangqishi inherited his father's title as Prince of Taiping and was promoted to Grand Master of Splendid Happiness with golden seal and purple robe. On the day gengyin, Secretariat officials requested a collective deliberation on elevating the empresses of the Wuzong, Yingzong, and Mingzong reigns to joint enshrinement.
11
十一月辛卯朔，罷富州金課。 甲午，太陰犯壘壁陣。 丙申，鞏昌成紀縣地裂山崩，令有司賑被災人民。 丁酉，享於太廟。 辛丑，起棕毛殿。 丙午，申飭鹽運司。 辛亥，江西、湖廣、江浙、河南復立榷茶運司。 追謚劄牙篤皇帝為聖明元孝皇帝，廟號文宗。 時寢廟未建，於英宗室次權結彩殿，以奉安神主。 封伯顏為秦王，錫金印。 是日，秦州山崩地裂。 夜，太陰犯太微東垣上相。 壬子，太陰犯填星。 癸丑，太陰犯亢宿。 乙卯，以燕鐵木兒平江所賜田五百頃，復賜其子唐其勢。 罷河間大報恩寺諸色人匠總管府。 江浙旱饑，發義倉糧、募富人入粟以賑之。 詔秦王、右丞相伯顏，榮王、左丞相撒敦，統百官，總庶政。
On the first day of the eleventh month, xinmao, the gold tax at Fuzhou was abolished. On the day jiawu, the moon transited the Leibi formation. On the day bingshen, the ground split and mountains collapsed in Chengji County, Gongchang; the responsible offices were ordered to relieve the afflicted population. On the day dingyou, offerings were presented at the Imperial Ancestral Temple. On the day xinchou, construction began on the Palm-Fiber Hall. On the day bingwu, the Salt Transport Directorate was admonished and rectified. On the day xinhai, the Tea Monopoly Transport Directorates in Jiangxi, Huguang, Jiangzhe, and Henan were re-established. Emperor Jayaatu was posthumously titled Emperor Shengming Yuanxiao, with the temple name Wenzong. As the resting temple had not yet been built, a temporary colored hall was erected at Yingzong's shrine position to install the spirit tablets. Bayan was enfeoffed as Prince of Qin and granted a golden seal. That same day mountains collapsed and the ground split open in Qinzhou. That night the moon transited the Upper Minister star on the eastern wall of Taiwei. On the day renzi, the moon transited Saturn. On the day guichou, the moon transited the lodge Kang. On the day yimao, the five hundred qing of fields Yan Temür had been granted at Pingjiang were granted again to his son Tangqishi. The General Directorate of Artisans of All Categories at the Great Repaying-Kindness Temple in Hejian was abolished. Jiangzhe suffered drought and famine; charity-granary grain was distributed and wealthy households were urged to contribute grain for relief. An edict directed Prince of Qin and right chancellor Bayan and Prince Rong and left chancellor Satu to command all offices and oversee civil government.
12
十二月庚申，命伯顏提調彰德威武衛。 乙丑，廣西徭寇湖南，陷道州，千戶郭震戰死，寇焚掠而去。 壬申，遣省、臺官分理天下囚，罪狀明者處決，冤者辨之，疑者讞之，淹滯者罪其有司。 以奴列你他代其父塔剌赤為耽羅國軍民安撫使司達魯花赤，錫三珠虎符。 癸酉，太陰犯鬼宿。 甲戌，禿堅帖木兒致仕，錫太尉印，置僚屬。 乙亥，為皇太后置徽政院，設官屬三百六十有六員。 太白犯壘壁陣，太陰犯軒轅。 己卯，太陰犯進賢。 癸未，太陰犯東咸。
In the twelfth month, on the day gengshen, Bayan was ordered to take charge of the Zhangde Weiwu Guard. On the day yichou, Yao bandits from Guangxi raided Hunan and captured Daozhou. Chiliarch Guo Zhen was killed in battle, and the bandits burned and plundered the region before withdrawing. On the day renshen, Secretariat and Censorate officials were dispatched throughout the realm to review prisoners: clear cases were executed, wrongful convictions were overturned, doubtful cases were adjudicated, and officials responsible for delays were punished. Nulienita succeeded his father Talaqi as darughachi of the Tamna Military-Civilian Pacification Commission and was granted a three-pearl tiger tally. On the day guiyou, the moon transited the lodge Gui. On the day jiaxu, Tuq Temür retired and was granted the seal of Grand Commandant, with a staff appointed for him. On the day yihai, a Bureau of Palace Provisions was established for the Empress Dowager with three hundred sixty-six officials. Venus transited the Leibi formation; the moon transited Xuanyuan. On the day jimao, the moon transited Jinxian. On the day guiwei, the moon transited Dongxian.
13
元統二年春正月庚寅朔，雨血於汴梁，著衣皆赤。 辛卯，東平須城縣、濟寧濟州、曹州濟陰縣水災，民饑，詔以鈔六萬錠賑之。 以御史大夫脫別臺為中書平章政事，阿裏海牙為河南行省左丞相。 丁酉，享於太廟。 戊戌，四川大盤洞蠻謀谷什用遣男謀者什用來貢方物，即其地立盤順府，命謀谷什用為知府。 遣吏部尚書帖住、禮部郎中智熙善使交趾，以《授時曆》賜之。 太陰犯軒轅。 癸卯，敕僧道與民一體充役。 己酉，以上文宗皇帝謚號，遣官告祭於南郊。 庚戌，太陰犯房宿。 甲寅，罷廣教總管府，立行宣政院。 乙卯，雲南土酋姚安路總管高明來獻方物，錫符印遣之。
On the first day of the first month of spring in the second year of Yuantong, the day gengyin, blood rained at Bianliang and stained clothing red. On the day xinmao, floods struck Xucheng County in Dongping, Jizhou in Jining, and Jiyin County in Caozhou. The people faced famine, and an edict ordered relief with sixty thousand ingots of paper money. Censor-in-chief Tobedai was appointed associate director of the Secretariat, and Aliqaya was appointed left chancellor of the Henan Branch Secretariat. On the day dingyou, offerings were presented at the Imperial Ancestral Temple. On the day wuxu, Mogushiyong of the great Pan Cave Miao in Sichuan sent his son Moguzheshiyong to present tribute; a Pan Shun Prefecture was established on his territory and Mogushiyong was appointed prefect. Minister of Personnel Tiezhu and director of rites Zhixi Shan were dispatched as envoys to Annam, presenting the Shoushi Calendar as a gift. The moon transited Xuanyuan. On the day guimao, an edict ordered that monks and Daoists perform corvée labor on the same basis as commoners. On the day jiyou, in accordance with Emperor Wenzong's posthumous title, officials were dispatched to announce sacrifices at the southern suburb. On the day gengxu, the moon transited the lodge Fang. On the day jiayin, the General Directorate of Broad Teaching was abolished and a Branch Commission for the Regulation of Buddhist Affairs was established. On the day yimao, the native chieftain Gao Ming, route commander of Yao'an in Yunnan, came to present tribute and was granted seals and credentials before being sent home.
14
二月己未朔，詔內外興舉學校。 癸亥，廣西徭寇邊，殺官吏。 廣海官已除而未上者罪之。 甲子，塞北東涼亭雹，民饑，詔上都留守發倉廩賑之。 乙丑，命有司以時給宿衛冬衣。 以燕不鄰為太保，置僚屬。 戊辰，封也真也不幹為昌寧王，錫金印。 癸酉，太陰犯太微上相。 丁丑，封皇姑妥妥輝為英壽大長公主。 癸未，安豐路旱饑，敕有司賑糶麥萬六千七百石。 甲申，太廟木陛壞，遣官告祭。 丁亥，太白經天。 是月，灤河、漆河溢，永平諸縣水災，賑鈔五千錠。 瑞州路水，賑米一萬石。
On the first day of the second month, jiwei, an edict ordered schools to be established throughout the realm. On the day guihai, Yao bandits in Guangxi raided the frontier and killed officials. Guangdong and maritime officials who had been appointed but had not reported for duty were punished. On the day jiazi, hail fell at Dongliangting in the northern frontier. The people faced famine, and an edict ordered the Shangdu garrison commander to open granaries for relief. On the day yichou, the responsible offices were ordered to supply winter clothing to the palace guards on schedule. Yanbulin was appointed Grand Guardian and given a staff. On the day wuchen, Yazhenyebugan was enfeoffed as Prince of Changning and granted a golden seal. On the day guiyou, the moon transited the Upper Minister star of Taiwei. On the day dingchou, the Emperor's paternal aunt Totoqei was enfeoffed as Grand Princess of Yingshou. 33
朕祗紹天明，入纂丕緒，於今三年，夙夜寅畏，罔敢怠荒。 茲者年谷順成，海宇清謐，朕方增修厥德，日以敬天恤民為務，屬太史上言，星文示儆。 將朕德菲薄，有所未逮歟？ 天心仁愛，俾予以治，有所告戒歟？ 弭災有道，善政為先。 更號紀年，實惟舊典。 惟世祖皇帝在位長久，天人協和，諸福咸至，祖述之志，良切朕懷。 今特改元統三年仍為至元元年。 遹遵成憲，誕布寬條，庶格禎祥，永綏景祚。 赦天下。
We reverently received Heaven's mandate and entered to inherit the great succession; for three years now We have been vigilant from dawn till dusk and have not dared to be idle or negligent. Now the year's grain ripened in season and the realm was tranquil; We were just then improving Our virtue and taking reverence for Heaven and care for the people as Our daily task, when the court astronomer reported that the stars showed a warning. Is it that Our virtue is insufficient and something has been left undone? Is Heaven's heart benevolent, enabling Us to govern, and offering some admonition? There is a way to quell calamity — good government comes first. Changing era names and counting years is indeed the ancient precedent. Only Emperor Shizu reigned long, Heaven and humanity were in harmony, and every blessing arrived — the aspiration to follow his example is truly close to Our heart. Now We specially change the third year of Yuantong back to the first year of Zhiyuan. Far following the established statutes, We broadly promulgate lenient measures, hoping to draw down auspicious signs and forever secure the throne's fortune. A general amnesty was declared throughout the realm.

35
