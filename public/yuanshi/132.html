1
杭忽思
Hanghusi
2
杭忽思，阿速氏，主阿速國。 太宗兵至其境，杭忽思率衆來降，賜名拔都兒，錫以金符，命領其土民。 尋奉旨選阿速軍千人，及其長子阿塔赤扈駕親征。 既還，阿塔赤入直宿衛。 杭忽思還國，道遇敵人，戰歿，敕其妻外麻思領兵守其國。 外麻思躬擐甲冑，平叛亂，後以次子按法普代之。
Hanghusi belonged to the Asud people and held sway over their kingdom. When Taizong's forces reached his lands, Hanghusi led his people in surrender. He received the name Badou'er, a gold tally, and command over his own subjects. He was soon ordered to muster a thousand Asud soldiers, and his eldest son Atachi joined the emperor's personal expedition. When the campaign ended, Atachi was posted to the palace guard on regular rotation. Hanghusi was homeward bound when he met foes on the road and fell in battle. The court commanded his wife Waimasi to take the army and hold the kingdom. Waimasi herself buckled on armor and put down revolts; later her second son Anfapu took her place.
3
阿塔赤從憲宗征西川軍于釣魚山，與宋兵戰有功，帝親飲以酒，賞以白金。 阿里不哥叛，從也里可征之，至寧夏，與阿藍荅兒、渾都海戰，率先赴敵，矢中其腹，不懼，世祖聞而嘉之，賞以白金，召入宿衛。 中統二年，扈駕親征阿里不哥，追至失木里禿之地，以功復賞白金。 三年，從征李璮，平之。 至元五年，奉旨同不荅台領兵南征，攻破金剛臺。 六年，從攻安慶府，戰有功。 七年，從下五河口。 十一年，從下沿江諸郡，戍鎮巢，民不堪命，宋降將洪福以計乘醉而殺之。 世祖憫其死，賜其家白金五百兩、鈔三千五百貫，併鎮巢降民一千五百三十九戶，且命其子伯荅兒襲千戶，佩金符。
Atachi marched with Xianzong against western Sichuan and fought at Diaoyushan, where he distinguished himself against the Song. The emperor drank with him in person and gave him white gold. During Ariq Böke's revolt he campaigned under Yelikhe as far as Ningxia, where he battled Arigh Böke and Qutuqai, charging ahead though an arrow pierced his belly. Kublai praised his courage, gave him white gold, and brought him into the palace guard. In Zhongtong 2 he joined the emperor's personal campaign against Ariq Böke, pursuing him to Shimulitu, and received another grant of white gold for his service. The next year he took part in the campaign against Li Tan and helped bring it to an end. In Zhiyuan 5 he was ordered south with Badutai and helped storm the Jingang Platform. In the sixth year he fought at Anqing Prefecture and again distinguished himself. In the seventh year he helped take the Wuhekou. In the eleventh year he helped subdue the Yangtze prefectures and was posted to garrison Zhenchao, where the people groaned under harsh rule. The surrendered Song officer Hong Fu tricked him into a feast and killed him while he was drunk. Kublai mourned his death and granted his household five hundred taels of white gold, three thousand five hundred strings of paper money, and the 1,539 surrendered households of Zhenchao. He also had his son Bada'er inherit the chiliarchy with a gold tally.
4
時失烈吉叛，詔伯荅兒領阿速軍一千往征之，與甕吉剌只兒瓦台軍戰于押里，復與藥木忽兒軍戰于禿剌及斡魯歡之地。 十五年春，至伯牙之地，與赤憐軍合戰。 五月，駐兵呵剌牙，與外剌台、寬赤哥思等軍合戰。 其大將塔思不花樹木為柵，積石為城，以拒大軍。 伯荅兒督勇士先登，拔之，伯荅兒矢中右股，別吉里迷失以其功聞，賞白金。 二十年，授虎符、定遠大將軍、後衛親軍都指揮使，兼領阿速軍，充阿速拔都達魯花赤。
When Shilieji rose in revolt, Bada'er was ordered to take a thousand Asud troops against him. He fought Jirwatai of the Onggirad at Yali, then clashed with Yeshmutu'er's forces at Tula and Olughun. In the spring of the fifteenth year he reached Boya and fought the Chiling forces. In the fifth month he camped at Halaya and fought Walatai, Kuanchiges, and their allies. The enemy general Tasibuhua built wooden palisades and stone walls to hold off the main force. Bada'er drove his men to scale the works first and took them, though an arrow struck his right thigh. Begirimishi reported his feat and he was given white gold. In the twentieth year he received a tiger tally and the posts of general for pacifying the distant and commander of the rear guard personal army, while continuing to lead the Asud troops as their badu darughachi.
5
二十二年，征別失八里，軍于亦里渾察罕兒之地，與禿呵、不早麻軍戰，有功。 二十六年，征杭海，敵勢甚盛，大軍乏食，其母乃咬真輸己帑及畜牧等給軍食，世祖聞而嘉之，賜予甚厚。 大德四年，伯荅兒卒。
In the twenty-second year he marched on Beshbaligh, camped at Yilihun Chaghan'er, and distinguished himself against the forces of Tuqa and Buzama. In the twenty-sixth year, campaigning against Hanghai, the enemy was strong and supplies ran short. His mother Naiyaozhen gave her own treasury goods and herds to feed the troops. Kublai praised her and rewarded them lavishly. Bada'er died in Dade 4.
6
長子斡羅思，由宿衛仕至隆鎮衛都指揮使； 次子福定，襲職，官懷遠大將軍，尋改右阿速衛達魯花赤，兼管後衛軍。 至大四年，兄都丹充右阿速衛都指揮使； 福定復職後衛，陞樞密同僉，命領軍一千守遷民鎮，尋授定遠大將軍、僉樞密院事、後衛親軍都指揮使，提調右衛阿速達魯花赤。 二年，進資善大夫、同知樞密院事。 後至元間，進知樞密院事。
His eldest son Woluosi rose from the palace guard to commander of the Longzhen Guard; the second son Fuding inherited the post as general for cherishing the distant, soon became darughachi of the Right Asud Guard, and also oversaw the rear guard. In Zhide 4 his elder brother Dudan became commander of the Right Asud Guard; Fuding then returned to the rear guard, rose to associate commissioner of military affairs, and was sent with a thousand men to hold Qianmin Town. He was soon made general for pacifying the distant, associate commissioner, commander of the rear guard personal army, and overseer of the Right Guard Asud darughachi. Two years later he was promoted to grand master for fostering goodness and vice commissioner of military affairs. Under Hou Yuan he became commissioner of military affairs.
7
步魯合荅
Buluhecha
8
至元二年，車里以老疾，不任事，諸王阿只吉命步魯合荅代領其軍。 至元八年，制授管軍千戶，佩金符。 宋將昝萬壽攻成都，僉省嚴忠範遣步魯合荅將兵七百人禦之于沙坎，流矢中右頰，拔矢，戰愈力，大敗其軍。 十一年，行院汪田哥以兵圍嘉定，步魯合荅即率其衆攻九頂山，破之，嘉定降。 進攻重慶，宋軍突圍出走銅鑼峽，行院忽敦遣步魯合荅追之，至廣羊埧，斬首二百級。 瀘州叛，還軍討之，步魯合荅以所部兵攻寶子寨，歲餘不下，乃造雲梯先登，急擊，遂破之，殺虜殆盡。 十六年，取重慶，以功遷武略將軍、征行元帥。
In Zhiyuan 2 Cheli was too old and ill to serve, and Prince Azhi put Buluhecha in command of his troops. In Zhiyuan 8 he was formally made a troop chiliarch with a gold tally. When the Song general Zan Wanshou threatened Chengdu, Yan Zhongfan sent Buluhecha with seven hundred men to hold Shakan. A stray arrow hit his right cheek; he pulled it out, fought harder, and routed the enemy. In the eleventh year Wang Tiange of the mobile secretariat besieged Jiading. Buluhecha stormed Jiuding Mountain, took it, and Jiading surrendered. During the assault on Chongqing the Song broke out toward Tongluo Gorge. Hudun sent Buluhecha in pursuit to Guangyangba, where he took two hundred heads. When Luzhou rebelled he marched back to crush it. His men besieged Baozi Stockade for over a year until he built siege ladders, led the climb, stormed the place, and slaughtered nearly everyone inside. In the sixteenth year Chongqing fell, and he was promoted to general of military stratagem and campaign marshal.
9
二十一年，命統蒙古探馬赤軍千人從征金齒蠻，平之。 都元帥蒙古歹征羅必甸，步魯合荅率游兵先行，江水暴溢，率衆泅水而渡，去城三百步而營。 居七日，諸軍會城下，乃進攻之，步魯合荅先登，拔其城，遂屠之。 又從征八百媳婦國，至車厘，車厘者，其酋長所居也。 諸王闊闊命步魯合荅將游騎三百往招之降，不聽，進兵攻之，都鎮撫侯正死焉。 步魯合荅毀其北門木，遂入其寨，其地悉平。 賜金虎符，授懷遠大將軍、雲南萬戶府達魯花赤，卒。 子忙古不花，襲管軍千戶。
In the twenty-first year he led a thousand Mongol tamachi in the campaign against the Jinchi tribes and subdued them. When Grand Marshal Mengudai marched on Luobidian, Buluhecha took the vanguard ahead. The river surged, but he swam his men across and pitched camp three hundred paces from the town. Seven days later the armies gathered under the walls and assaulted the town. Buluhecha was first over the wall, took the city, and put it to the sword. He also joined the campaign against Babai Xifu and reached Cheli, the seat of their chieftain. Prince Kuoku sent Buluhecha with three hundred light horsemen to demand surrender, but they refused. When the attack began, Regional Commander Hou Zheng was killed in action. Buluhecha smashed the north gate timbers, stormed the stockade, and pacified the whole district. He received a gold tiger tally and the posts of general for cherishing the distant and Yunnan myriarchate darughachi, then died. His son Mangubuhua inherited the troop chiliarchy.
10
初，按主奴三子：長車里，次黑子，次帖木兒。 黑子別賜金符，為奧魯元帥，兼文州吐蕃達魯花赤，卒。 其子那懷幼，以帖木兒攝其官。 那懷長，解職授之，遂改授帖木兒隨路拔都萬戶，後移鎮重慶，卒。
Anzhunu had three sons: Cheli the eldest, Heizi next, and Timur the youngest. Heizi received a separate gold tally as Ordu marshal and Wenzhou Tibetan darughachi before his death. While his son Nahuan was still a child, Timur held the post for him. When Nahuan came of age the office passed to him, and Timur was made route-wide Badu myriarch, later posted to Chongqing, where he died.
11
玉哇失
Yuwashi
12
玉哇失，阿速人。 父也烈拔都兒，從其國主來歸，太宗命充宿衛。 歲戊午，從憲宗征蜀，為游兵，前行至重慶，戰數有功。 嘗出獵遇虎於隘，下馬搏虎，虎張吻欲噬之，以手探虎口，抉其舌，拔所佩刀刺而殺之。 帝壯其勇，賞黃金五十兩，別立阿速一軍，使領其衆。 從世祖征阿里不哥，又從親王哈必失征李璮，俱有功，賜金符，授本軍千戶。 從下襄陽，又從下沿江諸城，宋洪安撫既降復叛，誘其入城宴，乘醉殺之。 長子也速歹兒代領其軍，從攻揚州，中流矢卒。
Yuwashi was of the Asud people. His father Yeliebadou'er came with their king to submit, and Taizong enrolled him in the palace guard. In the wuwu year he marched with Xianzong against Shu as a vanguard, reaching Chongqing ahead of the main force and winning repeated credit in battle. Once on a hunt he met a tiger in a narrow defile, dismounted, and fought it hand to hand. When it opened its jaws to bite him he thrust his hand into its mouth, seized its tongue, drew his belt knife, and killed it. The emperor admired his courage, gave him fifty taels of gold, and formed a separate Asud corps under his command. He served Kublai against Ariq Böke and Prince Habishi against Li Tan, earning rewards each time, a gold tally, and the chiliarchy of his own unit. He helped take Xiangyang and the Yangtze towns. A surrendered Song pacification commissioner of the Hong clan feigned loyalty, invited him to a feast in the city, and killed him while he was drunk. His eldest son Yesudai'er took command and joined the assault on Yangzhou, where a stray arrow killed him.
13
玉哇失襲父職，為阿速軍千戶。 從丞相伯顏平宋，賜巢縣二千五十二戶。 只兒瓦歹叛，率所部兵擊之，至懷魯哈都，擒其將失剌察兒，斬于軍，其衆悉平。 諸王和林及失剌等叛，從皇子北安王討之，至斡耳罕河，無舟，躍馬涉流而渡，俘獲甚衆。 時北安王方戰失利，陷敵陣中，玉哇失從諸王藥木忽兒追至金山，王乃得脫歸。 賞白金五十兩、鈔二千五百貫，改賜金虎符，進定遠大將軍、前衛親軍都指揮使。
Yuwashi inherited his father's post as chiliarch of the Asud army. He marched with Chancellor Bayan to conquer the Song and was granted 2,552 households in Chaoxian County. When Jirwadai rebelled he led his men to Huailuhadu, captured the rebel general Shilacha'er, executed him before the troops, and pacified the rest. When Princes Helin, Shila, and others rose in revolt he campaigned with the Northern Pacification Prince to the Orkhon. With no boats he spurred his horse through the current and took many prisoners. The Northern Pacification Prince was then losing ground and trapped in the enemy lines. Yuwashi rode with Prince Yeshmutu'er to Jinshan and brought the prince safely out. He received fifty taels of white gold and 2,500 strings of paper money, a gold tiger tally, and promotion to general for pacifying the distant and commander of the forward guard personal army.
14
諸王乃顏叛，世祖親征，玉哇失為前鋒。 乃顏遣哈丹領兵萬人來拒，擊敗之。 追至不里古都伯塔哈之地，乃顏兵號十萬，玉哇失陷陣力戰，又敗之，追至失列門林，遂擒乃顏。 帝嘉其功，賜金帶、只孫、錢幣甚厚。 乃顏餘黨塔不歹、金家奴聚兵滅捏該，從大軍討平之。 既而哈丹復叛於曲連江，追擊其軍，渡河而遁。 又與海都將八憐、帖里哥歹、必里察等戰於亦必兒失必兒之地，戰屢捷。
When Prince Nayiyan rebelled, Kublai marched in person and Yuwashi led the vanguard. Nayiyan sent Hadan with ten thousand men to block him, and he routed them. He pursued to Buligud Botaha, where Nayiyan reportedly had a hundred thousand men. Yuwashi charged into the enemy ranks, broke them again, chased them to Shiliemenlin, and captured Nayiyan. The emperor praised his feat and gave him a gold belt, zhisun robes, and a lavish purse. Nayiyan's lieutenants Tabudai and Jinjianu rallied at Mieniegai, and he joined the main force in crushing them. Hadan rebelled again at Qulian River; Yuwashi pursued his army until it fled across the river. He also fought Kaidu's generals Balin, Tiligedai, and Bilicha at Yibiershibi'er and won repeated victories.
15
成宗時在潛邸，帝以海都連年犯邊，命出鎮金山，玉哇失率所部在行。 從皇子闊闊出、丞相朶兒朶懷擊海都軍，突陣而入，大破之。 復從諸王藥木忽兒、丞相朶兒朶懷擊海都將八憐，八憐敗。 海都復以禿苦馬領精兵三萬人直趨撒剌思河，欲據險以襲我師。 玉哇失率善射者三百人守其隘，注矢以射，竟全軍而歸。 帝嘉之，賜鈔萬五千緡、金織段三十匹。 海都、朶哇以兵來襲，擊走之。
While Chengzong was still heir apparent, Kublai sent him to garrison Jinshan against Kaidu's yearly raids, and Yuwashi marched with his troops. With Prince Kuokuchu and Chancellor Duoduhuai he smashed through Kaidu's lines and routed the enemy. He again joined Prince Yeshmutu'er and Duoduhuai against Kaidu's general Balin and put him to flight. Kaidu sent Tuquma with thirty thousand picked men toward the Salasi River to seize the defiles and ambush the imperial force. Yuwashi held the pass with three hundred archers, rained arrows on the enemy, and brought the whole army back intact. The emperor praised him and gave him fifteen thousand strings of paper money and thirty bolts of gold brocade. When Kaidu and Duowa attacked, he beat them back.
16
武宗鎮北邊，海都復入寇，至兀兒禿，玉哇失敗之，獲其駝馬器仗以獻。 時扎魯花赤孛羅帖木兒所將兵為海都困於小谷，帝命玉哇失援出之。 帝喜，謂諸將曰：「今日大丈夫之事，舍玉哇失其誰能之，縱以黃金包其身，猶未足以厭朕志。 」武宗南還，命玉哇失後從，敵懼莫敢近，因留之戍邊。 賜以金察剌二，玉束帶、渾金段各一，仍賜秫米七十石，使為酒以犒其軍。 後海都子察八兒等遣人詣闕請和，朝廷許之，遂撤邊備，玉哇失乃還。 帝錄其功，賜鈔五萬貫，進鎮國上將軍，仍舊職。
While Wuzong held the northern frontier, Kaidu raided again as far as Wuerdu. Yuwashi defeated him and presented the captured camels, horses, and arms. Kaidu had trapped the darughachi Boluotiemur's men in a narrow valley, and the emperor sent Yuwashi to relieve them. Delighted, the emperor told his generals, "Who but Yuwashi could do what a true man must do today? Even if I wrapped him in gold it would not repay what I owe him." When Wuzong marched south he put Yuwashi in the rearguard; the enemy dared not come near, and Yuwashi was left to hold the frontier. He received two gold chasala ornaments, a jade belt set, a bolt of pure gold brocade, and seventy shi of grain to brew wine for his troops. Later Kaidu's son Chabar sued for peace, the court agreed, the frontier garrisons were drawn down, and Yuwashi came home. The emperor recorded his service, gave him fifty thousand strings of paper money, promoted him to general for stabilizing the state, and kept him in his old post.
17
大德十年五月，晝寢于衛舍，不疾而卒。 子亦乞里歹襲。 亦乞里歹卒，子拜住襲。
In the fifth month of Dade 10 he fell asleep in the guard quarters at midday and died without warning. His son Yiqilidai succeeded him. When Yiqilidai died, his son Baizhu took the post.
18
麥里，徹兀臺氏。 祖雪里堅那顏，從太祖與王罕戰，同飲班真河水，以功授千戶，領徹里臺部，征討諸國，卒于河西。 父麥吉襲職，從太宗定中原，以疾卒。 麥里襲職，從定宗略定欽察、阿速、斡魯思諸國。 從憲宗伐宋，有功。
Maili belonged to the Che'utai clan. His grandfather Sherijian Nayen fought Wang Han with Genghis Khan and drank from the Banzhen River with him. For his service he became a chiliarch leading the Cheritai and campaigned across many lands before dying in Hexi. His father Maiji inherited the post, helped Taizong pacify the Central Plains, and died of illness. Maili inherited in turn and followed Dingzong in subduing Qincha, the Asud lands, and Oros. He also served Xianzong against the Song with distinction.
19
探馬赤
Tanmachi
20
探馬赤，禿立不帶人。 從諸王沒赤征蜀，後以兵從塔海紺卜、火魯赤、紐璘諸大帥。 歲戊午，紐璘攻涪州，還至馬湖江，宋兵連艦絕江不得進，探馬赤率精兵二千擊之，奪其舟以濟。 又於橫江、嘉定、宣化三縣造浮橋，以達成都，紐璘以為能，命將千人，從萬戶昔力荅略地碉門、黎、雅、土蕃。 昔力荅死，行院帖赤以探馬赤為萬戶，領其軍。 中統四年，授蒙古漢軍萬戶。
Tanmachi was of the Tulibudai people. He campaigned in Shu under Prince Mechi, then served under the great marshals Tahai Ganbu, Huoluchi, and Niulian. In the wuwu year Niulian attacked Fuzhou. On the return march at the Mahu River, Song warships blocked the crossing. Tanmachi led two thousand picked men, seized their boats, and forced a passage. He also built pontoon bridges at Hengjiang, Jiading, and Xuanhua to open the route to Chengdu. Niulian judged him capable and gave him a thousand men under Myriarch Xilida to seize Diaomen, Li, Ya, and the Tibetan marches. After Xilida died, the mobile secretariat Tiechi made Tanmachi a myriarch and gave him command of the force. In Zhongtong 4 he was made myriarch of the Mongol and Han army.
21
至元九年，從行省也速帶兒征建都，獨以銳卒千五百人，與建都兵戰于梅子嶺，大敗之，夜馳與速哥會，直擣其營，斬首數十級，生擒百餘人，獲其輜重以歸。 復益兵三千人，與左丞曲立吉思乘勝進擊，建都勢蹙，請降。 又從行院汪田哥、忽敦等，攻嘉定、重慶、瀘、敘諸州，以功兼崇慶府達魯花赤。 十九年卒。 子拜延，襲蒙古軍萬戶，戍甘州。
In Zhiyuan 9 he joined Provincial Commissioner Yesudai'er against Jiandu with fifteen hundred picked men, routed the enemy at Meizi Ridge, rode through the night to join Suge, stormed their camp, took dozens of heads and more than a hundred prisoners, and returned with their baggage. Reinforced by three thousand men, he and Left Chancellor Qulijisi pressed the victory until Jiandu, hard pressed, sued for surrender. He also fought under Wang Tiange, Hudun, and others at Jiading, Chongqing, Lu, and Xu, and for his service was made concurrent darughachi of Chongqing Prefecture. He died in the nineteenth year. His son Baiyan inherited the Mongol myriarchy and was posted to Ganzhou.
22
拔都兒
Badou'er
23
拔都兒，阿速氏，世居上都宜興。 憲宗在潛邸，與兄兀作兒不罕及馬塔兒沙帥衆來歸。 馬塔兒沙從憲宗征麥各思城，為前鋒將，身中二矢，奮戰拔其城。 又從征蜀，至釣魚山，歿于軍。
Badou'er was an Asud whose family had long lived at Yixing in Shangdu. While Xianzong was still heir apparent, he came in with his elder brother Wuzuo'erbohan and Mata'ersha at the head of their people. Mata'ersha marched with Xianzong against Maigesicheng as vanguard, took two arrows, and still stormed the city. He later campaigned in Shu as far as Diaoyushan and died on campaign.
24
拔都兒從征李璮，圍濟南，身二十餘戰，世祖嘉其能，賞納失思段九，命領阿速軍一千，常居左右。 尋於阿塔赤內充怯薛百戶。 後從塔不台南征，與敵軍戰于金剛臺，又以功受賞。 師還，言於帝曰：「臣願從軍，為國效死。 」世祖留之，仍命充孛可孫，兼領阿速軍，御馬必令鞚引。
Badou'er joined the campaign against Li Tan and fought more than twenty times at the siege of Jinan. Kublai admired his skill, gave him nine bolts of nashisi brocade, put a thousand Asud troops under him, and kept him close at hand. He was soon made a keshig centurion in Atachi's guard. He later marched south with Tabutai, fought at the Jingang Platform, and was rewarded again. On the army's return he told the emperor, "I wish to stay with the troops and give my life for the realm." Kublai kept him at court as bekson, still in command of the Asud troops, and always had him lead the imperial horse by the bridle.
25
昂吉兒
Angji'er
26
昂吉兒，張掖人，姓野蒲氏，世為西夏將家。 歲辛巳，父甘卜率所部歸太祖，以其軍隸蒙古軍籍，仍以甘卜為千戶主之。 從木華黎出征，病卒。
Angji'er came from Zhangye, bore the surname Yepu, and his family had been generals of Western Xia for generations. In the xinsi year his father Ganbu brought his men to Genghis Khan, enrolled them in the Mongol army rolls, and remained their chiliarch. He campaigned under Muqali and died of illness.
27
昂吉兒領其父軍，從征諸國有功。 至元六年，授本軍千戶，佩金符。 俄略地淮南，所向無前。 時國兵初南，塞馬當暑，往往疥癘，昂吉兒以所部馬入太行療之，所病良已。 由是軍中馬病者，率以屬焉，歲療馬以萬數。 宋輸糧金剛臺，意將深入，昂吉兒將兵馳往，斷其輸道，因上言：「河南邊郡與宋對境，宋兵時為邊患，唐州東南皆大山，信陽在蔡州南，南直九里、武陽、平靖、五水等關，宋兵必經諸關以入，信陽實其咽喉，守禦莫急焉。 往年金亡，朝廷得壽、泗、襄、郢，而不留兵守，卒使宋得之，請城信陽，以扼宋。 」得旨，令率河西軍一千三百人城之，城成。
Angji'er took over his father's troops and won credit on campaigns across many lands. In Zhiyuan 6 he became chiliarch of his own unit with a gold tally. He soon swept south of the Huai and met no equal. As the army first marched south, northern horses sickened in the summer heat with mange and scab. Angji'er sent his horses into the Taihang to recover, and they healed. After that, sick army horses were routinely sent to him, and he treated tens of thousands each year. When the Song sent grain to the Jingang Platform for a deeper push, Angji'er raced to cut their supply line and memorialized the throne: "Henan's border counties face Song territory and suffer constant raids. Southeast of Tangzhou lies rugged mountain country. Xinyang sits south of Caizhou on the road to the passes of Jiuli, Wuyang, Pingjing, and Wushui—any Song advance must come that way. Xinyang is the choke point and must be held first." When Jin fell we took Shou, Si, Xiang, and Ying but left no garrison, and the Song seized them. I ask that Xinyang be fortified to block the Song advance. The emperor agreed and ordered him to build the city with thirteen hundred Hexi troops; the work was finished.
28
九年，加明威將軍、信陽軍萬戶，佩虎符，分木華黎及阿朮所將河西兵俾將之。 加懷遠大將軍。 丞相伯顏渡江，留阿朮定淮南東道，其西道則屬之昂吉兒，駐兵和州。 宋淮西制置夏貴遣侯都統將兵四萬來攻，有謀內應者悉誅之，潛兵出千秋澗，塞其歸路，因出城奮擊，大敗之，獲人馬千計。 鎮巢軍降，阿速軍戍之，人不堪其橫，都統洪福盡殺戍者以叛。 昂吉兒攻拔其城，擒福及董統制、譚正將。 遂攻廬州，夏貴使人來言曰：「公毋吾攻為也，吾主降，吾即降矣。 」宋亡，貴舉所部納款。 昂吉兒入廬州，民按堵無所犯，遷鎮國上將軍、淮西宣慰使。
In the ninth year he became general of illustrious might and myriarch of Xinyang Army with a tiger tally, commanding the Hexi troops once led by Muqali and Azhu. He was promoted to general for cherishing the distant. When Bayan crossed the Yangtze, Azhu held eastern Huainan while Angji'er took the western route and camped at Hezhou. Song Huaixi commissioner Xia Gui sent Commander Hou with forty thousand men. Angji'er executed would-be collaborators, sent a hidden force through Qianqiu Ravine to cut the enemy's retreat, sallied from the city, and routed them, taking thousands of men and horses. After Zhenchao surrendered, Asud troops garrisoned it until the people could bear their abuses no longer. Commander Hong Fu slaughtered the garrison and rebelled. Angji'er stormed the city and captured Hong Fu along with Commissioners Dong and Tan Zheng. He then moved on Luzhou. Xia Gui sent word: "Do not press me—once my sovereign surrenders, I shall surrender at once." When the Song fell, Xia Gui brought his whole command over. Angji'er entered Luzhou without harming the people and was promoted to general for stabilizing the state and Huaixi pacification commissioner.
29
宋丞相文天祥復起兵海道，舒民張德興應之，襲破興國、德安諸郡，還據司空山。 詔昂吉兒攻之，一戰而定，殺張德興，執其三子以獻。
Song chancellor Wen Tianxiang rallied forces along the coast; Zhang Dexing of Shuzhou joined him, seized Xingguo and De'an, and held Sikong Mountain. The court ordered Angji'er against them; one battle ended the revolt. He killed Zhang Dexing and sent his three sons to the capital.
30
江左初平，官制草創，權臣阿合馬納賂鬻爵，江南官僚冗濫為甚，郡守而下佩金符者多至三四人，由行省官舉薦超授宣慰使者甚衆，民不堪命。 昂吉兒入朝，具為帝言之，且枚舉不循資歷而驟陞者數人。 帝驚曰：「有是哉！ 」因謂姚樞等曰：「此卿輩所知，而不為朕言，昂吉兒顧言之邪。 」即命偕平章哈伯、左丞崔斌、翰林承旨和魯火孫、符寶奉御董文忠減汰之，選曹以清。 仍詔諭江淮軍民，俾通知之。
After the lower Yangtze was pacified, offices were still being invented and Ahmad sold posts for bribes. Jiangnan was awash in redundant officials—some counties had three or four men with gold tallies—and provincial patrons pushed unqualified men into pacification posts until the people groaned under the burden. Angji'er came to court, laid the whole matter before the emperor, and named several men promoted far beyond their rank. The emperor exclaimed, "Can this be true?" He turned to Yao Shu and the others and said, "You knew this and never told me—only Angji'er would speak up?" He at once ordered Habo, Cui Bin, Heluohuosun, and Dong Wenzong to weed out the excess, and the personnel offices were cleared. An edict also announced the purge to the armies and people of the Jianghuai region.
31
時兩淮兵革之餘，荊榛蔽野，昂吉兒請立屯田，以給軍餉，帝從之。 既而阿塔海言：「屯田所用人牛農具甚衆，今方有事日本，若復調發民兵，將不勝動搖矣。 」議遂寢。 未幾，宣慰使燕公楠復以為言，帝乃遣數千人，即芍陂、洪澤試之，果如昂吉兒所言，乃以二萬兵屯之，歲得米數十萬斛。 加輔國上將軍、河南行省參知政事、淮西宣慰使都元帥，進驃騎衛上將軍、行中書省左丞，加龍虎衛上將軍、行尚書省右丞，兩官皆兼淮西使、帥。
After the wars on the two Huai, the fields lay waste under thorn and bramble. Angji'er asked to establish military colonies to feed the troops, and the emperor agreed. Atahai then objected: "Colonies need vast numbers of men, oxen, and tools. We are already committed against Japan; another levy would shake the realm." The plan was dropped. Soon Pacification Commissioner Yan Gongnan raised it again. The emperor sent several thousand men to trial plots at Shaopi and Hongze, and the yield matched Angji'er's prediction. Twenty thousand colonists were posted there, producing hundreds of thousands of hu of rice each year. He rose to general for assisting the state, Henan associate commissioner, and Huaixi grand marshal, then became biaoqi guard general and branch secretariat left chancellor, and also longhu guard general and branch ministry right chancellor, retaining Huaixi civil and military command throughout.
32
日本不庭，帝命阿塔海等領卒十萬征之。 昂吉兒上疏，其略曰：「臣聞兵以氣為主，而上下同欲者勝。 比者連事外夷，三軍屢衂，不可以言氣，海內騷然，一遇調發，上下愁怨，非所謂同欲也，請罷兵息民。 」不從。 既而師果無功。
When Japan refused tribute, the emperor ordered Atahai and others to take a hundred thousand men against it. Angji'er memorialized the throne in substance as follows: "I have heard that armies live on morale and win only when ruler and troops want the same thing." Lately we have fought abroad again and again while the armies suffer defeat after defeat—there is no morale left. The realm is unsettled; every levy breeds resentment above and below. That is not shared purpose. I beg you to stop the campaigns and let the people rest." The emperor refused. The expedition failed, as he had warned.
33
昂吉兒屢為直言，雖帝怒甚，其辭不少屈。 臺臣慮昂吉兒難制，以牙以迷失不畏強禦，奏為本道按察使以察之。 牙以迷失時捃摭昂吉兒細故以聞，及廷辨，帝察其無他，輒遷其官，後竟以微過罪之。 元貞元年卒。
Angji'er spoke blunt truth again and again, and even when the emperor flew into a rage he would not soften his words. The censorate feared Angji'er was unmanageable and, because Yayishimishi was known not to flinch before the powerful, had him made circuit surveillance commissioner to watch him. Yayishimishi kept reporting petty complaints against Angji'er. At court the emperor saw no malice and kept shifting Yayishimishi's post, but in the end punished him for a trifling offense. He died in Yuanzhen 1.
34
子五人，其顯者曰昂阿禿，廬州蒙古漢軍萬戶府達魯花赤； 曰暗普，海北海南道肅政廉訪使。 孫教化的，世襲千戶。
He had five sons. The notable ones were Ang'atu, darughachi of the Luzhou Mongol and Han myriarchate; and Anpu, surveillance commissioner of the Haibei and Hainan circuit. His grandson Jiaohuade inherited the chiliarchy in the family line.
35
哈剌，哈魯氏。 初從軍攻襄樊，蒙古四萬戶府辟為水軍鎮撫。 至元十二年，從丞相伯顏渡江，改管軍百戶，賞甲冑、銀鞘刀。 十二年秋，從丞相阿朮與宋兵戰焦山，敗之，獲海舟二。 阿朮與王世強招討造白鷂海船百艘，就四十一萬戶翼摘遣漢軍三千五百、新附軍一千五百，俾哈剌、王世強并統之。 攻宋江陰、許浦、金山、上海、崇明、金浦皆下之，獲海船三百餘艘，遂戍澉浦海口。
Hala belonged to the Halu clan. He first campaigned at Xiangyang and Fancheng and was recruited by the Fourth Mongol Myriarchate as naval regional commander. In Zhiyuan 12 he crossed the Yangtze with Bayan, became a troop centurion, and received armor and a silver-sheathed knife. That autumn he fought the Song with Azhu at Jiaoshan, routed them, and took two seagoing vessels. Azhu and Pacification Commissioner Wang Shiqiang built a hundred white-hawk warships, drew thirty-five hundred Han and fifteen hundred newly attached troops from the Forty-first Myriarchate wing, and put Hala and Wang Shiqiang in joint command. He took Jiangyin, Xupu, Jinshan, Shanghai, Chongming, and Jinpu, seized more than three hundred warships, and garrisoned the Ganpu estuary.
36
十三年春，行省檄充沿海招討副使。 宋將張世傑舟師至慶元朐山東門海界，哈剌追之，獲船四艘，上其功，行省增撥軍七百并舊所領士卒，守定海港口。 秋七月，宋昌國州、朐山、秀山戍兵舟師千餘艘，攻奪定海港口，哈剌迎擊，虜其裨將并海船三艘。 八月，宋兵復攻定海港口，哈剌擊退之，行省檄充蒙古漢軍招討使。 十月，哈剌引兵至溫州青門，遇宋兵，奪船五艘，遣使諭溫州守臣家之柄以城降。 十一月，至福州，奪宋海船二十艘，擒毛監丞等。
In the spring of the thirteenth year the provincial administration named him associate coastal pacification commissioner. When Zhang Shijie's fleet reached the sea off eastern Qushan in Qingyuan, Hala pursued, took four ships, reported his success, and was reinforced with seven hundred men to hold Dinghai Harbor. In the seventh month more than a thousand Song ships from Changguo, Qushan, and Xiushan seized Dinghai Harbor. Hala counterattacked and captured a deputy commander and three warships. In the eighth month the Song attacked Dinghai again and Hala drove them off. The provincial administration then named him Mongol and Han pacification commissioner. In the tenth month Hala reached Qing'ao Gate in Wenzhou, met Song forces, took five ships, and sent word to the defender Jia Zhibing to surrender the city. In the eleventh month he reached Fuzhou, took twenty Song warships, and captured Supervisor Mao and others.
37
十四年，賜金符，宣武將軍、沿海招討副使，行省檄充沿海經略副使，俾與劉萬戶行元帥府事於慶元，鎮守沿海上下，南至福建，北趾許浦。 六月，行省檄充沿海經略使，兼左副都元帥，督造海船千艘。 八月，有旨：江西省右丞塔出等進兵攻廣南，哈剌以兵從。 十月，進昭勇大將軍、沿海招討使。 時宋處州兵復溫州，哈剌歹率兵復取之。 進至潮陽縣，宋都統陳懿等兄弟五人以畬兵七千人降。 塔出兵攻廣州，踰月未下，哈剌引兵繼至，諭宋安撫張鎮孫、侍郎譚應斗以城降。 從攻張世傑于大洋，獲其軍資器械不可勝計。 諭南恩州，宋閤門宣贊、舍人梁國傑以畬軍萬人降。
In the fourteenth year he received a gold tally as general of martial proclamation and associate coastal pacification commissioner, then became associate coastal commissioner-in-chief. With Myriarch Liu he ran the marshal's headquarters at Qingyuan, guarding the coast from Fujian south to Xupu north. In the sixth month he was named coastal commissioner-in-chief and left associate grand marshal and ordered to build a thousand warships. In the eighth month an edict ordered Jiangxi right chancellor Tachu and others against Guangnan, and Hala marched with them. In the tenth month he was promoted to general of martial valor and coastal pacification commissioner. Song forces from Chuzhou had retaken Wenzhou, and Haladai marched in and recovered it. At Chaoyang County, Song commander Chen Yi and his five brothers surrendered with seven thousand She troops. Tachu besieged Guangzhou for more than a month without success. Hala came up behind and persuaded pacification commissioner Zhang Zhensun and vice minister Tan Yingdou to surrender. He joined the attack on Zhang Shijie at sea and captured stores and arms beyond numbering. At Nan'en Prefecture, Song gate proclaimer Liang Guojie surrendered with ten thousand She troops.
38
十五年，還軍慶元。 秋八月，入覲，帝問曰：「汝何氏族？ 」對曰：「臣哈魯人。 」賜金織文衣、鞍勒，擢昭武大將軍、沿海左副都元帥、慶元路緫管府達魯花赤，將所部軍戍海口。 十六年，日本商船四艘，篙師二千餘人至慶元港口，哈剌歹諜知其無他，言于行省，與交易而遣之。 海賊賀文達、顧潤等寇掠海島，哈剌歹諭降之，得舟六十餘艘。 十八年，擢輔國上將軍、都元帥，從國兵征日本，值颶風，舟回。 明年二月，還戍慶元。 二十二年，罷都元帥，改沿海上萬戶府達魯花赤。
In the fifteenth year the army returned to Qingyuan. In the eighth month he presented himself at court. The emperor asked, "What is your clan?" He answered, "I am a Halu man, Your Majesty." He received gold-woven robes and saddle gear, was made general of martial solemnity, left associate coastal grand marshal, and Qingyuan circuit darughachi, and garrisoned the seaport with his troops. In the sixteenth year four Japanese merchant ships with more than two thousand crew reached Qingyuan. Haladai found they meant no harm, reported to the provincial administration, traded with them, and sent them on. Sea bandits He Wenda, Gu Run, and others raided the islands until Haladai talked them into surrender and took more than sixty ships. In the eighteenth year he became general for assisting the state and grand marshal, joined the invasion of Japan, and turned back when a typhoon struck the fleet. The following second month he returned to garrison Qingyuan. In the twenty-second year the grand marshal post was abolished and he became darughachi of the coastal superior myriarchate.
39
二十四年，入朝，帝問日本事宜，哈剌歹應對甚悉，令還戍海道。 授浙東宣慰使，賜金織文段、玉束帶、鞍勒、弓矢有差。 二十五年，樞密以水軍乏帥，奏兼前職。 冬，徵入見。 明年，拜金吾衛上將軍、中書左丞，行浙東道宣慰使，領軍職如故。
In the twenty-fourth year he came to court, answered the emperor's questions about Japan in detail, and was sent back to guard the sea routes. He was made Zhedong pacification commissioner and given gold brocade, a jade belt set, saddle gear, and bows and arrows. In the twenty-fifth year the Bureau of Military Affairs, citing a shortage of naval commanders, had him resume his former military post as well. That winter he was summoned to court. The following year he became jinyu guard general and secretariat left chancellor, acting Zhedong pacification commissioner, while keeping his military command.
40
沙全，哈剌魯氏。 父沙的，世居沙漠，從太祖平金，戍河南柳泉，家焉。 全初名抄兒赤，甫五歲，為宋軍所虜，年十八，留劉整幕下，宋人以其父名沙的，使以沙為姓，而名曰全。 全久居宋，險固備知之。
Sha Quan belonged to the Qarluq clan. His father Shadi had long lived in the steppe, followed Genghis Khan in conquering Jin, and settled at Liuchuan in Henan on garrison duty. Quan was born Chao'erchi. The Song captured him at five, and at eighteen he served in Liu Zheng's staff. The Song took his father's name Shadi, gave him the surname Sha, and called him Quan. Having lived long under the Song, he knew every pass and strongpoint.
41
中統二年，整以瀘州來歸，全與之同行，宋軍追之，全力戰得脫，授管軍百戶。 至元三年，整出兵雲頂山，與宋將夏貴兵遇，全擊殺甚衆。 五年，命整領都元帥事，出師圍襄樊，以全為鎮撫。 整遣全率軍攻仙人山、陳家洞諸寨，破之，陞千戶，賜銀符。 敗宋將張貴，拔樊城，與劉整軍會。 修正陽城，引兵渡淮，與宋將陳安撫戰，敗之。 十二年，從丞相阿朮與宋將張世傑、孫虎臣大戰于焦山，水陸並進，宋人不能支，盡棄鼓旗走，獲其將士三十三人。 從攻常州，克之，乘勝下沿海諸城。 至華亭，戒士卒毋殺掠，遂傾城出降，以功授華亭軍民達魯花赤。
In Zhongtong 2 Zheng defected with Luzhou and Quan went with him. Pursued by Song forces, Quan fought his way clear and was made a troop centurion. In Zhiyuan 3 Zheng marched from Yunding Mountain, met Song general Xia Gui, and Quan killed a great many of the enemy. In the fifth year Zheng was put in charge of the grand marshal's command, besieged Xiangyang and Fancheng, and made Quan regional commander. Zheng sent Quan against the stockades at Xianren Mountain and Chenjiadong. After they fell he was promoted to chiliarch and given a silver tally. He defeated Song general Zhang Gui, took Fancheng, and rejoined Liu Zheng. He repaired Zhengyang, crossed the Huai, and defeated Song pacification commissioner Chen. In the twelfth year he fought with Azhu against Zhang Shijie and Sun Huchen at Jiaoshan. The combined land and sea assault broke the Song line; they fled abandoning drums and banners, and thirty-three officers and men were taken. He helped take Changzhou and then swept down the coastal cities in pursuit. At Huating he forbade killing and looting, and the whole city surrendered. For this he was made darughachi of Huating.
42
時民心未定，有未附鹽徒聚衆數萬掠華亭，全擊破之，籍其名得六千人，請于行省，遣屯田于淮之芍陂。 行省以邑人新附，時有叛側，委萬戶忽都忽等體察，欲屠其城，全言：「鹽卒多非其土人，若屠之，枉死者衆。 」以死保其不叛，遂止。 賜金符，加武略將軍，兼領鹽場，職如舊。 尋陞華亭為府，以全為達魯花赤，賜虎符。 時盜賊蜂起，其最盛者有衆數千人，全悉招來之，境內得安。 改松江萬戶府達魯花赤，始專領軍政。
The people were still unsettled when unaffiliated salt gangs of tens of thousands raided Huating. Quan defeated them, registered six thousand men, and asked the provincial administration to settle them at Shaopi on the Huai. Because the town had only lately submitted and still wavered, the provincial administration sent Myriarch Huduhu to investigate and planned a massacre. Quan argued, "Most of the salt rebels were outsiders—a massacre would kill many innocents." He pledged his life that they would not rebel, and the massacre was called off. He received a gold tally and promotion to general of military stratagem, still overseeing the salt works. Huating was soon raised to a prefecture, and Quan became its darughachi with a tiger tally. Bandits rose everywhere, some with thousands of men. Quan talked them all into submission and restored order in the district. He was transferred to darughachi of the Songjiang myriarchate and took sole charge of military government.
43
二十二年，召見，遷隆興萬戶府達魯花赤，得請，復舊名曰抄兒赤。 未幾，帝以為松江瀕海重地，復命鎮之，賜三珠虎符，卒于官。
In the twenty-second year he was called to court, moved to Longxing myriarchate darughachi, and with permission resumed his birth name Chao'erchi. Soon the emperor sent him back to Songjiang as a vital coastal post, gave him a three-pearl tiger tally, and he died in office.
44
帖木兒不花
Tiemu'erbuhua
45
帖木兒不花，中統初入備宿衛。 至元七年，授虎符，代張馬哥為淄萊水軍萬戶，將其衆赴襄陽，與宋將范文虎戰于灌子灘，手殺四十餘人，奪其戰艦，追至雲勝洲，大敗之。 行省上其功，賜白金五十兩、衣一襲、鞍轡一副。 九年，授益都新軍萬戶。 十一年，改益都、淄萊新軍萬戶。
Tiemu'erbuhua entered the palace guard reserve at the start of Zhongtong. In Zhiyuan 7 he received a tiger tally, replaced Zhang Mage as Zilai naval myriarch, and marched to Xiangyang. At Guanzitan he fought Fan Wenhu, killed more than forty men with his own hand, seized enemy warships, pursued to Yunshengzhou, and routed the Song fleet. The provincial administration reported his feat and he received fifty taels of white gold, a suit of clothes, and saddle gear. In the ninth year he became myriarch of the Yidu new army. In the eleventh year he was made myriarch of the combined Yidu and Zilai new army.
46
從丞相伯顏伐宋，敗其大將夏貴於陽羅堡。 大軍渡江，論其功最多，賜白金五百兩。 又從下鄂、蘄、黃、江、建康、常、秀、蘇、杭諸郡，累加昭武大將軍。 從參知政事阿剌罕略定紹興、溫、台、福建諸郡，授台州路緫管府達魯花赤，遷廣東宣慰使。
He marched with Bayan against the Song and defeated Xia Gui at Yangluo Fort. When the main force crossed the Yangtze his service was judged foremost and he received five hundred taels of white gold. He helped reduce E, Qi, Huang, Jiang, Jiankang, Chang, Xiu, Suzhou, Hangzhou, and other prefectures, rising step by step to general of martial solemnity. He campaigned with Associate Commissioner Alahan through Shaoxing, Wenzhou, Taizhou, and Fujian, became Taizhou circuit darughachi, and was moved to Guangdong pacification commissioner.
47
十六年，加都元帥。 追宋將張世傑於香山島，世傑死，降其衆數千人。 廣東諸郡及海島盡平，領諸降臣及將校之有功者，入見於大安閣，命太府監視其身，製銀鼠裘成，親賜予之，授中書左丞，行省江西，其餘爵賞有差。 二十五年，拜四川等處行尚書省平章政事，兼緫軍務，改行中書省平章政事。
In the sixteenth year he was made grand marshal. He pursued Zhang Shijie to Xiangshan Island; Shijie died and several thousand of his men surrendered. When Guangdong and its islands were pacified he led the surrendered officials and meritorious commanders to audience at the Da'an Pavilion. The emperor had the Imperial Storehouse measure him for a silver-marten coat, gave it with his own hand, and appointed him secretariat left chancellor and Jiangxi commissioner; others received lesser honors. In the twenty-fifth year he became pacification commissioner of the Sichuan branch ministry with overall military command, then pacification commissioner of the branch secretariat.
48
其兄帖木脫斡，初以蒙古軍千戶從伐蜀有功，行樞密院承制授萬戶。 并將列別朮、塔海帖木兒、也速帶兒、匣剌撒兒四千戶軍，從大軍攻重慶。 重慶降，收其衆，徇下流諸城，留鎮夔門，兼本路安撫司達魯花赤。 進懷遠大將軍、蒙古軍萬戶。 遷定遠大將軍，兼嘉定守鎮萬戶、本路緫管府達魯花赤。 尋陞鎮國上將軍、諸蠻夷部宣慰使，加都元帥。 亦奚不薛蠻畔，與岳剌海會雲南兵討平之。 改征緬都元帥，死于軍。 子忽都荅兒嗣。
His elder brother Tiemutuwo first distinguished himself as a Mongol chiliarch in the Shu campaign and was made myriarch by provisional order of the mobile Bureau of Military Affairs. He also led the four-thousand-household forces of Liebiezhu, Tahai Timur, Yesudai'er, and Xialasa'er in the assault on Chongqing. After Chongqing surrendered he took its troops downstream, garrisoned Kuimen, and became circuit pacification darughachi. He was promoted to general for cherishing the distant and Mongol army myriarch. He became general for pacifying the distant, garrison myriarch of Jiading, and circuit darughachi. He was soon made general for stabilizing the state and pacification commissioner over the frontier tribes, with the added rank of grand marshal. When the Yixi Buxie tribes rebelled he joined Yuelahai and Yunnan forces to crush them. He was made grand marshal for the Burma campaign and died on service. His son Huducha'er succeeded him.
