宰相者，上承天子，下統百司，治體繫焉。 元初，將相大臣，年月疏闊，簡牘未詳者則闕之。 中統建元以來，宰執之官，其拜罷歲月之可考者，列而書之。 作宰相年表。
The chancellor bears up the Son of Heaven above and commands the hundred ministries below; the order of government depends on it. In the early Yuan, dates for generals and ministers were often incomplete; where records are unclear, entries are omitted. From the founding of the Zhongtong era onward, for each chancellor post, appointees and the dates of their appointment and dismissal are listed wherever records allow. The Annals Table of Chancellors is compiled.
