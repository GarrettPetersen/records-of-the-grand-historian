1
姚興 (上)
Yao Xing (Part One)
2
姚興，字子略，萇之長子也。 苻堅時為太子舍人。 萇之在馬牧，興自長安冒難奔萇，萇立為皇太子。 萇出征討，常留統後事。 及鎮長安，甚有威惠。 與其中舍人梁喜、洗馬範勖等講論經籍，不以兵難廢業，時人咸化之。
Yao Xing, whose courtesy name was Zilüe, was the eldest son of Yao Chang. During Fu Jian's reign he held the office of Gentleman Attendant to the Crown Prince. While Yao Chang was stationed at the Horse Pastures, Yao Xing braved peril to flee from Chang'an and join him, and Yao Chang installed him as crown prince. Whenever Yao Chang marched on campaign, he routinely left Yao Xing behind to manage affairs in the rear. When he held Chang'an, he ruled with both stern authority and kindness. He debated the classics with Liang Xi, Gentleman of the Palace Within, Fan Xu, Groom of the Heir Apparent, and others, refusing to abandon scholarship because of the hardships of war; his contemporaries took inspiration from him.
3
萇死，興秘不發喪，以其叔父緒鎮安定，碩德鎮陰密，弟崇守長安。 碩德將佐言於碩德曰：「公威名宿重，部曲最強，今喪代之際，朝廷必相猜忌，非永安之道也。 宜奔秦州，觀望事勢。 」碩德曰：「太子志度寬明，必無疑阻。 今苻登未滅而自尋干戈，所謂追二袁之蹤，授首與人。 吾死而已，終不若斯。 」及至，興優禮而遣之。
When Yao Chang died, Yao Xing concealed the death and withheld the obsequies; he assigned his uncle Yao Xu to hold Anding, Yao Shuode to hold Yinmi, and his younger brother Yao Chong to defend Chang'an. Yao Shuode's subordinates said to him: "Your prestige has stood for years and your command is the strongest force at hand; at a moment of imperial succession the court will inevitably grow wary of you; that is no path to lasting peace. You should withdraw to Qinzhou and see how the situation unfolds. Yao Shuode replied: "The crown prince is broad-minded and discerning; he will not harbor groundless doubts or block our way. Fu Deng still lives; if we now turn our blades on one another, we shall only repeat the folly of the two Yuans and hand victory to someone else. I would sooner die than follow such counsel. When Yao Shuode arrived, Yao Xing received him with exceptional courtesy and let him return to his post.
4
興自稱大將軍，以尹緯為長史，狄伯支為司馬，率眾伐苻登。 咸陽太守劉忌奴據避世堡以叛，興襲忌奴，擒之。 苻登自六陌向廢橋，始平太守姚詳據馬嵬堡以距登。 登眾甚盛，興慮詳不能遏，乃自將精騎以迫登，遣尹緯領步卒赴詳。 緯用詳計，據廢橋以抗登。 登因急攻緯，緯將出戰，興馳遣狄伯支謂緯曰：「兵法不戰而制人者，蓋為此也。 苻登窮寇，宜持重，不可輕戰。 」緯曰：「先帝登遐，人情擾懼，今不因思奮之力，梟殄逆豎，大事去矣。 緯敢以死爭。 」遂與登戰，大破之，登眾渴死者十二三，其夜大潰，登奔雍。 興乃發喪行服。 太元十九年，僭即帝位於槐里，大赦境內，改元曰皇初，遂如安定。
Yao Xing took the title of Grand General, appointed Yin Wei as Chief Clerk and Di Bozhi as Marshal, and led the army against Fu Deng. Liu Jinu, Administrator of Xianyang, seized Bishibao and rose in rebellion; Yao Xing struck him, took him prisoner, and subdued the revolt. Fu Deng marched from Liumo toward Feiqiao; Yao Xiang, Administrator of Shiping, entrenched himself at Maweibao to hold Fu Deng at bay. Fu Deng's army was formidable; Yao Xing feared Yao Xiang could not contain it, so he personally led elite horsemen to pressure Fu Deng while dispatching Yin Wei with foot soldiers to reinforce Yao Xiang. Yin Wei followed Yao Xiang's plan and secured Feiqiao to block Fu Deng. Fu Deng redoubled his assault on Yin Wei; as Yin Wei was about to give battle, Yao Xing urgently sent Di Bozhi with orders: "The maxim that the adept warrior wins without fighting is meant for moments such as this. Fu Deng is a desperate foe; stand firm and do not hazard a rash engagement. Yin Wei answered: "The late sovereign has just passed away; fear and uncertainty grip the army. If we do not seize this surge of loyal resolve to strike down the traitor Fu Deng, the whole enterprise is lost. I am prepared to stake my life on this battle. He engaged Fu Deng and won a crushing victory; two or three men in ten of Fu Deng's army perished of thirst, and that night the enemy ranks collapsed in panic as Fu Deng fled toward Yong. Only then did Yao Xing publicly announce his father's death and don mourning garb. In the nineteenth year of the Jin Taiyuan era he presumptuously assumed the throne at Huaili, proclaimed a general amnesty throughout his realm, adopted the reign title Huangchu, and then proceeded to Anding.
5
先是，苻登使弟廣守雍，子崇屯胡空堡，聞登敗，各棄守走。 登無所投據，遂奔平涼，率其餘眾入馬毛山。 興自安定如涇陽，與登戰于山南，斬登。 散其部眾，歸復農業。 徙陰密三萬戶于長安，分大營戶為四，置四軍以領之。
Earlier, Fu Deng had stationed his brother Fu Guang at Yong and his son Fu Chong at Hukongbao; when they heard of his defeat, both abandoned their positions and fled. With no refuge left, Fu Deng rushed to Pingliang and led his surviving troops into the Maomao Hills. Yao Xing marched from Anding to Jingyang, fought Fu Deng south of the mountains, and slew him in the field. He disbanded Fu Deng's soldiers and sent them back to their fields. He relocated thirty thousand households from Yinmi to Chang'an, split the former great camp population into four divisions, and created four armies to command them.
6
安南強熙、鎮遠楊多叛，推竇沖為盟主，所在擾亂。 興率諸將討之，軍次武功，多兄子良國殺多而降。 沖弟彰武與沖離貳，沖奔強熙。 熙聞興將至，率戶二千奔秦州。 竇沖走汧川，汧川氐仇高執送之。 沖從弟統率其眾降於興。
Qiang Xi, General Who Pacifies the South, and Yang Duo, General Who Stabilizes the Distance, rose in revolt and proclaimed Dou Chong their leader; unrest spread across the region. Yao Xing led his commanders against them and camped at Wugong, where Yang Liangguo, Yang Duo's nephew, assassinated Yang Duo and came over to Yao Xing's side. Dou Chong's younger brother Dou Zhangwu broke with him, so Dou Chong fled to join Qiang Xi. When Qiang Xi learned that Yao Xing was approaching, he took two thousand households and fled toward Qinzhou. Dou Chong fled to Qianchuan, where Qiu Gao of the local Di people seized him and delivered him to the court. Dou Chong's cousin Dou Tong led the remainder of the rebel forces to surrender to Yao Xing.
7
封征虜緒為晉王，征西碩德為隴西王，征南靖等及功臣尹緯、齊難、楊佛嵩等並為公侯，其餘封爵各有差。
He invested Yao Xu, General Who Subdues Invaders, as Prince of Jin; Yao Shuode, General Who Campaigns West, as Prince of Longxi; Yao Jing, General Who Campaigns South, together with such merit holders as Yin Wei, Qi Nan, and Yang Fosong, received ducal or marquisal rank; other officers were ennobled according to their deeds.
8
鮮卑薛勃於貳城為魏軍所伐，遣使請救，使姚崇赴救。 魏師既還，薛勃復叛，崇伐而執之，大收其士馬而還。
The Xianbei leader Xue Bo, holding Ercheng, was attacked by Wei troops; he appealed for rescue, and Yao Xing sent Yao Chong to relieve him. After the Wei army withdrew, Xue Bo rebelled again; Yao Chong campaigned against him, took him captive, seized large numbers of men and mounts, and withdrew.
9
興追尊其庶母孫氏為皇太后，配饗太廟。
Yao Xing posthumously elevated his father's concubine, Lady Sun, to Empress Dowager and granted her a place beside the royal ancestors in the Grand Temple.
10
楊盛保仇池，遣使請命，拜使持節、鎮南將軍、仇池公。 鮮卑越質詰歸率戶二萬叛乞伏乾歸，降於興，興處之于成紀，拜使持節、鎮西將軍、平襄公。
Yang Sheng, who held Qiuchi, sent envoys suing for recognition; Yao Xing appointed him Bearer of the Imperial Staff, General Who Guards the South, and Duke of Qiuchi. The Xianbei chief Yuezhi Jiegui led twenty thousand households in breaking away from Qifu Gangui and submitting to Yao Xing; Yao Xing resettled them at Chengji and named him Bearer of the Imperial Staff, General Who Guards the West, and Duke of Pingxiang.
11
姚碩德討平涼胡金豹於洛城，克之。 初，上邽薑乳據本縣以叛，自稱秦州刺史。 碩德進討之，乳率眾降。 以碩德為秦州牧，領護東羌校尉，鎮上邽。 征乳為尚書。 強熙及略陽豪族權幹城率眾三萬圍上邽，碩德擊破之。 熙南奔仇池，遂假道歸晉。 碩德西討幹城，幹城降。
Yao Shuode campaigned against the Pingliang Hu leader Jin Bao at Luocheng and captured the town. Earlier, Jiang Ru of Shanggui had seized his home county, rebelled, and declared himself Inspector of Qinzhou. Yao Shuode advanced against him; Jiang Ru led his forces in surrender. He named Yao Shuode Governor of Qinzhou, with the additional post of Colonel Who Protects the Eastern Qiang, and stationed him at Shanggui. He summoned Jiang Ru to the capital to serve as a Minister of State. Qiang Xi and Quan Gancheng, a powerful clan leader from Lueyang, brought thirty thousand men to besiege Shanggui; Yao Shuode broke their army. Qiang Xi fled south to Qiuchi and from there obtained safe conduct into Eastern Jin territory. Yao Shuode then marched west against Quan Gancheng, who capitulated.
12
興令郡國各歲貢清行孝廉一人。
Yao Xing decreed that every commandery and princedom should each year nominate one candidate for the Filial and Incorrupt examination who excelled in moral conduct.
13
慕容永既為慕容垂所滅，河東太守柳恭等各阻兵自守，興遣姚緒討之。 恭等依河距守，緒不得濟。 鎮東薛強先據楊氏壁，引緒從龍門濟河，遂入蒲阪。 恭勢屈，請降。 徙新平、安定新戶六千于蒲阪。
After Murong Chui had destroyed Murong Yong, regional administrators such as Liu Gong of Hedong dug in with their own troops; Yao Xing dispatched Yao Xu to reduce them. Liu Gong and his allies held the Yellow River line, and Yao Xu could not force a crossing. Xue Qiang, the general holding the east, had already occupied Yangshi Stockade; he guided Yao Xu across the river at Longmen, and the army entered Puban. Seeing his position collapse, Liu Gong offered his surrender. Yao Xing resettled six thousand newly registered households from Xinping and Anding in Puban.
14
興母虵氏死，興哀毀過禮，不親庶政。 群臣議請依漢、魏故事，既葬即吉。 興尚書郎李嵩上疏曰「三王異制，五帝殊禮。 孝治天下，先王之高事也，宜遵聖性，以光道訓。 既葬之後，應素服臨朝，率先天下，仁孝之舉也。 」尹緯駁曰：「帝王喪制，漢、魏為准。 嵩矯常越禮，愆於軌度，請付有司，以專擅論。 既葬即吉，乞依前議。 」興曰：「嵩忠臣孝子，有何咎乎？ 尹僕射棄先王之典，而欲遵漢、魏之權制，豈所望於朝賢哉！ 其一依嵩議。」
Yao Xing's mother, Lady She, died; he mourned so fiercely that he exceeded prescribed ritual and ceased to handle daily affairs of state. His ministers debated the matter and urged him to follow Han and Wei precedent by ending mourning as soon as the burial was complete. Li Song, a Gentleman at the Masters of Writing, presented a memorial: "The Three Ages knew variant institutions; the Five Emperors observed differing rites. Governing the world through filial devotion was the highest priority of the ancient sages; Your Majesty should heed your own humane nature and thereby illuminate moral teaching. After the interment you should hold court in plain hemp, setting an example for the realm—such is the conduct of humane filial piety. Yin Wei objected: "Imperial mourning law takes the Han and Wei codes as its touchstone. Li Song twists convention and oversteps ritual propriety; he departs from proper bounds. Hand him to the judicial officials and try him for overstepping authority. End mourning when the burial is finished; we respectfully ask that you adhere to the ministers' original proposal. Yao Xing said: "Li Song speaks as a loyal minister and a devoted son. Where lies his offense? Yet Director Yin Wei would discard the models of the ancient kings and cling to the expedients of Han and Wei—is that what we expect of worthy ministers? Let everything be decided according to Li Song's memorial.
15
鮮卑薛勃叛奔嶺北，上郡、貳川雜胡皆應之，遂圍安遠將軍姚詳于金城。 遣姚崇、尹緯討之。 勃自三交趣金城，崇列營掎之，而租運不繼，三軍大饑。 緯言於崇曰：「輔國彌姐高地、建節杜成等皆諸部之豪，位班三品，督運稽留，令三軍乏絕，宜明置刑書，以懲不肅。 」遂斬之。 諸部大震，租入者五十餘萬。 興率步騎二萬親討之，勃懼，棄其眾奔于高平公沒奕于，於執而送之。
The Xianbei Xue Bo rebelled and fled beyond the northern ranges; mixed Hu peoples of Shang Commandery and the Er River basin rose with him, and together they besieged Yao Xiang, General Who Pacifies the Distance, at Jincheng. Yao Xing dispatched Yao Chong and Yin Wei to suppress the revolt. Xue Bo drove from Sanjiao toward Jincheng; Yao Chong drew up camps to contain him, yet supplies failed to arrive and the entire army faced famine. Yin Wei told Yao Chong: "Mijie Gaodi, the general who supports the state, Du Cheng, who holds the banner of integrity, and others are tribal chiefs of third-rank standing. They were charged with convoys yet stalled them until the army ran dry. Apply the penal code openly to restore discipline. They executed the negligent officers. The tribal levies were terrified into compliance, and more than five hundred thousand measures of grain poured in. Yao Xing personally led twenty thousand foot and horse against him; Xue Bo panicked, abandoned his troops, and fled to Moyuyu, Duke of Gaoping, who seized him and handed him over to Yao Xing.
16
泫氏男姚買得欲因興葬母虵氏殺興，會有告之者，興未之信，遣李嵩詐往。 買得具以告嵩，嵩還，以聞，興乃賜買得死，誅其黨與。
Yao Maide, a baron of Xuanshi county, plotted to murder Yao Xing during the burial of Lady She. When an informer brought word, Yao Xing doubted the tale and sent Li Song on a pretended errand to probe the matter. Maide confessed everything to Li Song, who reported back; Yao Xing then forced Maide to take his own life and executed his accomplices.
17
興下書禁百姓造錦繡及淫祀。
Yao Xing promulgated an edict forbidding commoners to weave brocades and to practice illicit, excessive worship.
18
興率眾寇湖城，晉弘農太守陶仲山、華山太守董邁皆降於興。 遂如陝城，進寇上洛，陷之。 遣姚崇寇洛陽，晉河南太守夏侯宗之固守金墉，崇攻之不克，乃陷柏穀，徙流人西河嚴彥、河東裴岐、韓襲等二萬餘戶而還。
Yao Xing led his army against Hucheng; Tao Zhongshan, Eastern Jin's Administrator of Hongnong, and Dong Mai, Administrator of Huashan, both capitulated. He then advanced to Shancheng, struck Shangluo, and captured the city. He sent Yao Chong against Luoyang; Xiahou Zongzhi, Jin's Administrator of Henan, held the Jinyong fortress and repelled him, so Yao Chong turned aside, seized Bogu, and marched away more than twenty thousand displaced households led by Yan Yan of Xihe and Pei Qi and Han Xi of Hedong.
19
興下書，令士卒戰亡者守宰所在埋藏之，求其近親為之立後。
Yao Xing issued an edict requiring local magistrates to bury soldiers fallen in battle within their jurisdictions and to find close kin to continue each man's line.
20
武都氐屠飛、啖鐵等殺隴東太守姚回，略三千餘家，據方山以叛。 興遣姚紹等討之，斬飛、鐵。 遣狄伯支迎流人曹會、牛壽萬餘戶於漢中。
Tu Fei, Dan Tie, and other Di leaders from Wudu murdered Yao Hui, Administrator of Longdong, ravaged more than three thousand households, and entrenched themselves on Fangshan in rebellion. Yao Xing sent Yao Shao and others against them and executed Tu Fei and Dan Tie. He dispatched Di Bozhi to escort more than ten thousand displaced households led by Cao Hui and Niu Shou out of Hanzhong.
21
興留心政事，苞容廣納，一言之善，咸見禮異。 京兆杜瑾、馮翊吉默、始平周寶等上陳時事，皆擢處美官。 天水姜龕、東平淳于岐、馮翊郭高等皆耆儒碩德，經明行修，各門徒數百，教授長安，諸生自遠而至者萬數千人。 興每於聽政之暇，引龕等於東堂，講論道藝，錯綜名理。 涼州胡辯，苻堅之末，東徙洛陽，講授弟子千有餘人，關中後進多赴之請業。 興敕關尉曰：「諸生諮訪道藝，修己厲身，往來出入，勿拘常限。 」於是學者咸勸，儒風盛焉。 給事黃門侍郎古成詵、中書侍郎王尚、尚書郎馬岱等，以文章雅正，參管機密。 詵風韻秀舉，確然不群，每以天下是非為己任。 時京兆韋高慕阮籍之為人，居母喪，彈琴飲酒。 詵聞而泣曰：「吾當私刃斬之，以崇風教。 」遂持劍求高。 高懼，逃匿，終身不敢見詵。
Yao Xing devoted himself to governance, generous in tolerance and eager for counsel; even a single sound suggestion earned distinctive honor. Du Jin of Jingzhao, Ji Mo of Fengyi, Zhou Bao of Shiping, and others memorialized on policy; each was promoted to a prestigious post. Jiang Kan of Tianshui, Chunyu Qi of Dongping, Guo Gao of Fengyi, and others were eminent elder scholars, masters of the classics and models of conduct; each commanded hundreds of disciples and lectured in Chang'an, while learners arrived from distant regions by the tens of thousands. Whenever court business allowed, Yao Xing invited Jiang Kan and his peers to the eastern hall to debate learning and sort through philosophical distinctions. Hu Bian of Liangzhou had relocated east to Luoyang near the end of Fu Jian's rule; he instructed over a thousand pupils, and ambitious young scholars from Guanzhong flocked to study under him. Yao Xing ordered the frontier captains: "Students who pursue learning and cultivate their characters may pass the checkpoints without the usual curfew restrictions. Scholars took fresh heart, and Confucian learning flourished as never before. Gucheng Shen, Supernumerary Gentleman of the Yellow Gates; Wang Shang, Gentleman of the Palace Secretariat; Ma Dai, Gentleman at the Masters of Writing; and others—writers of refined, principled prose—were admitted to confidential deliberations. Gucheng Shen carried himself with poised distinction; he stood apart from the crowd and habitually treated the moral welfare of the empire as his personal charge. At that time Wei Gao of Jingzhao, emulating Ruan Ji, played the zither and drank wine while mourning his mother. When Gucheng Shen heard of it, he wept and swore, "I should run him through with my own sword for the sake of public morality. He seized a blade and went looking for Wei Gao. Terrified, Wei Gao fled into hiding and never again dared face Gucheng Shen.
22
興遣將鎮東楊佛嵩攻陷洛陽。
Yao Xing dispatched Yang Fosong, General Who Stabilizes the East, who captured Luoyang.
23
班命郡國，百姓因荒自賣為奴婢者，悉免為良人。 興以日月薄蝕，災眚屢見，降號稱王，下書令群公卿士將牧守宰各降一等。 於是其太尉趙公旻等五十三人上疏諫曰：「伏惟陛下勳格皇天，功濟四海，威靈振於殊域，聲教暨于遐方，雖成湯之隆殷基，武王之崇周業，未足比喻。 方當廓靖江、吳，告成中嶽，豈宜過垂沖損，違皇天之眷命乎！ 」興曰：「殷湯、夏禹德冠百王，然猶順守謙沖，未居崇極，況朕寡昧，安可以處之哉！ 」乃遣旻告於社稷宗廟，大赦，改元弘始。 賜孤獨鰥寡栗帛有差，年七十已上加衣杖。 始平太守周班、槐里令李青彡皆以黷貨誅，於是郡國肅然矣。 洛陽既陷，自淮、漢已北諸城，多請降送任。
He ordered every commandery and kingdom that all commoners who had sold themselves into slavery because of famine should be freed and restored to free commoner status. Eclipses of the sun and moon and repeated omens of disaster led Yao Xing to lower his style and call himself king; he issued an edict ordering every noble, minister, official, general, governor, prefect, and county magistrate to step down one rank. Grand Commandant Duke Zhao Min and fifty-two others jointly memorialized: "We humbly submit: Your Majesty's merit rivals Heaven, your work delivers the four seas, your majesty awakens distant lands, and your civilizing influence reaches the farthest regions; even the grandeur with which Tang founded Yin and King Wu exalted Zhou cannot serve as a fair comparison. You are on the verge of pacifying the Jiang and Wu regions and completing your achievement at the sacred central peak—how can you so diminish yourself and turn away from Heaven's favoring mandate!" Yao Xing replied: "Tang of Shang and Yu of Xia surpassed all kings in virtue, yet they still held to humility and refused the highest throne. How much less may I, dull and unworthy, presume to occupy it!" He then had Min report the decision at the altars of soil and grain and the imperial ancestral temple, proclaimed a general amnesty, and adopted the era name Hongshi. He distributed grain and silk in set amounts to orphans, the isolated elderly, widows, and widowers; anyone seventy or older also received a robe and a staff. Prefect Zhou Ban of Shiping and Magistrate Li Qingshan of Huaili were put to death for taking bribes, after which discipline prevailed across the commanderies and kingdoms. Once Luoyang had fallen, most of the cities north of the Huai and Han rivers offered submission and sent hostages.
24
興下書聽祖父母昆弟得相容隱。 姚緒、姚碩德以興降號，固讓王爵，興弗許。
Yao Xing decreed that grandparents and full siblings might lawfully shield one another from prosecution. Because Yao Xing had lowered his own rank, Yao Xu and Yao Shuode repeatedly refused their princely titles; Yao Xing would not allow them to decline.
25
京兆韋華、譙郡夏侯軌、始平龐眺等率襄陽流人一萬叛晉，奔於興。 興引見東堂，謂華曰：「晉自南遷，承平已久，今政化風俗何如？ 」華曰：「晉主雖有南面之尊，無總禦之實，宰輔執政，政出多門，權去公家，遂成習俗，刑網峻急，風俗奢宕。 自桓溫、謝安已後，未見寬猛之中。 」興大悅，拜華中書令。
Wei Hua of Jingzhao, Xiahou Gui from Qiao commandery, Pang Tiao of Shiping, and others led ten thousand displaced people from Xiangyang in revolt against Jin and defected to Yao Xing. Yao Xing received them in the Eastern Hall and asked Wei Hua: "Since the Jin court moved south, there has been a long spell of peace—what are governance and popular customs like now?" Wei Hua replied: "The Jin sovereign enjoys nominal supremacy but exercises no unified control; chief ministers hold power, edicts issue from competing factions, and authority has drained from the throne until it is habitual—the laws are harsh and society extravagant and dissolute. Since Huan Wen and Xie An, no ruler has struck a sound balance between severity and leniency." Yao Xing was delighted and appointed Wei Hua Palace Secretary.
26
興如河東。 時姚緒鎮河東，興待以家人之禮。 下書封其先朝舊臣姚驢磑、趙惡地、王平、馬萬載、黃世等子為五等子男。 命百僚舉殊才異行之士，刑政有不便於時者，皆除之。 兵部郎金城邊熙上陳軍令煩苛，宜遵簡約。 興覽而善之，乃依孫吳誓眾之法以損益之。 興立律學于長安，召郡縣散吏以授之。 其通明者還之郡縣，論決刑獄。 若州郡縣所不能決者，讞之廷尉。 興常臨諮議堂聽斷疑獄，于時號無冤滯。
Yao Xing traveled to Hedong. Yao Xu was stationed in Hedong; Yao Xing treated him with the intimacy reserved for close kin. He decreed enfeoffment for the sons of his dynasty's senior ministers—Yao Lü'ai, Zhao Ede, Wang Ping, Ma Wanzai, Huang Shi, and others—as viscounts and barons of the fifth rank. He directed all officials to recommend men of outstanding ability and integrity, and abolished every statute or policy ill suited to the times. Bian Xi of Jincheng, an officer in the Ministry of War, argued that military regulations were excessively harsh and should be simplified. Yao Xing approved the memorial and revised the military code along the lines of the oath-taking assemblies described in the Sunzi and Wuzi traditions. He founded a law school in Chang'an and summoned clerks from across the commanderies and counties for instruction. Those who mastered the curriculum returned to try criminal cases at their posts. Cases local authorities could not settle were forwarded to the Commandant of Justice. Yao Xing regularly heard doubtful lawsuits in the Consultation Hall; people said at the time that innocent verdicts did not languish in the courts.
27
姚緒、姚碩德固讓王爵，許之。 緒、碩德威權日盛，興恐奸佞小人沮惑之，乃簡清正君子為之輔佐。
When Yao Xu and Yao Shuode again pressed to surrender their princely titles, Yao Xing granted the request. As Yao Xu and Yao Shuode's influence grew, Yao Xing worried that sycophants might mislead them, so he appointed principled scholars as their advisers.
28
興以司隸校尉郭撫、扶風太守強超、長安令魚佩、槐里令彭明、倉部郎王年等清勤貞白，下書褒美，增撫邑一百戶，賜超爵關內侯，佩等進位一級。
Metropolitan Governor Guo Fu, Fufeng Prefect Qiang Chao, Chang'an Magistrate Yu Pei, Huaili Magistrate Peng Ming, Granaries Officer Wang Nian, and others were diligent, honest, and upright, so Yao Xing commended them in an edict: Guo Fu's fief gained a hundred households, Qiang Chao received the title of secondary marquis within the passes, and Yu Pei and the rest were promoted one rank.
29
使碩德率隴右諸軍伐乞伏乾歸，興潛軍赴之，乾歸敗走，降其部眾三萬六千，收鎧馬六萬匹。 軍無私掠，百姓懷之。 興進如枹罕，班賜王公以下，遍于卒伍。
He sent Yao Shuode to lead the Longyou armies against Qifu Gangui while Yao Xing moved troops secretly to support him. Gangui was routed and fled; Yao Xing accepted the surrender of thirty-six thousand of his followers and seized sixty thousand armored horses. The troops took nothing for themselves, and the common people were grateful. Yao Xing proceeded to Fuhan and distributed gifts from the nobility down to the common soldiers.
30
興之西也，沒奕於密欲乘虛襲安定，長史皇甫序切諫乃止。 于自恨失言，陰欲殺序。
While Yao Xing marched west, Moyi Yu planned to strike Anding while it was exposed; Chief Clerk Huangfu Xu argued fiercely against it, and he abandoned the idea. Moyi Yu bitterly regretted having spoken and secretly plotted to murder Huangfu Xu.
31
乞伏乾歸以窮蹙來降，拜鎮遠將軍、河州刺史、歸義侯，復以其部眾配之。
Qifu Gangui, driven to desperation, submitted; he was named General Who Extends Afar, Inspector of Hezhou, and Marquis Who Returns to Righteousness, and his troops were restored to his command.
32
興下書，將帥遭大喪，非在疆埸險要之所，皆聽奔赴，及期，乃從王役。 臨戎遭喪，聽假百日。 若身為邊將，家有大變，交代未至，敢輒去者，以擅去官罪罪之。 遣晉將軍劉嵩等二百三十七人歸於建鄴。
Yao Xing decreed that generals mourning a parent might leave duty for the funeral unless stationed at a vital frontier pass; when mourning ended they were to resume service under the king. Those on campaign when bereavement occurred were granted leave for a hundred days. Frontier commanders whose families suffered a major loss could not depart until a replacement arrived; anyone who left without authorization was punished as for deserting one's post. He repatriated two hundred thirty-seven Jin officers, including Liu Song, to Jianye.
33
魏人襲沒奕于，於棄其部眾，率數千騎與赫連勃勃奔于秦州。 魏軍進次瓦亭，長安大震，諸城閉門固守。 魏平陽太守貳塵入侵河東。 興於是練兵講武，大閱於城西，於勇壯異者召入殿中。 引見群臣於東堂，大議伐魏。 群臣咸諫以為不可，興不從。 司隸姚顯進曰：「陛下天下之鎮，不宜親行，可使諸將分討，授以廟勝之策。 」興曰：「王者正以廓土靖亂為務，吾焉得而辭之！」
Northern Wei forces attacked Moyi Yu; he deserted his army and fled to Qinzhou with Helian Bobo at the head of several thousand cavalry. The Wei army advanced to Wating, throwing Chang'an into panic; every city barred its gates and held fast. Erchen, prefect of Pingyang under Wei, invaded Hedong. Yao Xing then trained his troops, rehearsed tactics, and held a major parade west of the city; men of exceptional valor were summoned into the palace. In the Eastern Hall he received his ministers and held a full debate on attacking Wei. Every minister urged against the campaign; Yao Xing would not listen. Metropolitan Governor Yao Xian said: "Your Majesty anchors the empire; you should not take the field yourself. Send the generals on separate expeditions and supply them with strategies approved at court." Yao Xing replied: "A true king exists to expand the realm and put down disorder—how could I shrink from that!"
34
興立其子泓為皇太子，大赦境內，賜男子為父後者爵一級。
Yao Xing invested his son Yao Hong as crown prince, proclaimed a general amnesty throughout his realm, and awarded one noble rank to each adult son who was his father's heir.
35
遣姚平、狄伯支等率步騎四萬伐魏，姚碩德、姚穆率步騎六萬伐呂隆。 平等軍次河東，興遣其光遠黨娥、立節雷星、建忠王多等率杏城及嶺北突騎自和寧赴援，越騎校尉唐小方、積弩姚良國率關中勁卒為平後繼，姚緒統河東見兵為前軍節度，姚紹率洛東之兵，姚詳率朔方見騎，並集平望，以會於興。 使沒奕于權鎮上邽，中軍、廣陵公斂權鎮洛陽，姚顯及尚書令姚晃輔其太子泓，入直西宮。
He dispatched Yao Ping and Di Bozhi with forty thousand foot and horse against Wei, and Yao Shuode and Yao Mu with sixty thousand against Lü Long. When Yao Ping's army stopped in Hedong, Yao Xing sent Guangyuan Dang'e, Lijie Leixing, Jianzhong Wang Duo, and others with cavalry from Xingcheng and north of the ridges from Hening as reinforcements; Colonel Tang Xiaofang of the Rapid Cavalry and Strong Crossbow General Yao Liangguo with elite Guanzhong troops as Yao Ping's rear; Yao Xu to coordinate the forward contingent from existing Hedong forces; Yao Shao with troops east of the Luo River; and Shiping prefect Yao Xiang with Shuofang's mounted levies (the sitting prefect, not the late warrior who bore the same name). All were to rally at Pingwang to unite with Yao Xing. He left Moyi Yu as acting governor of Shanggui, appointed Central Army Commander and Duke of Guangling Lian Quan to hold Luoyang, and assigned Yao Xian and Palace Secretary Yao Huang to assist Crown Prince Yao Hong on duty in the Western Palace.
36
碩德至姑臧，大敗呂隆之眾，俘斬一萬。 隆將呂他等率眾二萬五千，以東苑來降。 先是，禿髮利鹿孤據西平，沮渠蒙遜據張掖，李玄盛據敦煌，與呂隆相持。 至是，皆遣使降。
Yao Shuode reached Guzang and routed Lü Long's army, killing or capturing ten thousand men. Lü Long's general Lü Ta and others brought twenty-five thousand men from the Eastern Enclosure to surrender. Earlier Tufa Ligugu had occupied Xiping, Juqu Mengxun Zhangye, and Li Xuansheng Dunhuang, while they contended with Lü Long. They now all sent envoys to submit.
37
興率戎卒四萬七千，自長安赴姚平。 平攻魏乾城，陷之，逐據柴壁。 魏軍大至，攻平，截汾水以守之。 興至蒲阪，憚而不進。
Yao Xing marched forty-seven thousand troops from Chang'an to reinforce Yao Ping. Yao Ping took the Wei city of Qian, then advanced to hold Chaiyu. Massive Wei forces besieged Yao Ping and dammed the Fen River to trap him. Yao Xing reached Puban but halted out of fear.
38
時碩德攻呂隆，撫納夷夏，分置守宰，節糧積粟，為持久之計。 隆懼，遂降。 碩德軍令齊整，秋毫無犯，祭先賢，禮儒哲，西土悅之。
Meanwhile Yao Shuode was pressing Lü Long, winning over both tribal peoples and Han Chinese, appointing garrison commanders and magistrates, and stockpiling grain for a long campaign. Lü Long, terrified, capitulated. Yao Shuode's discipline was strict—his men hardly harmed a hair on the people; he sacrificed to past worthies and honored learned men, and the western regions admired him.
39
姚平糧竭矢盡，將麾下三十騎赴汾水而死，狄伯支等十將四萬餘人，皆為魏所擒。 興下書，軍士戰沒者，皆厚加褒贈。 魏軍乘勝進攻蒲阪，姚緒固守不戰，魏乃引還。
When Yao Ping ran out of food and arrows, he drowned in the Fen River with thirty horsemen; Di Bozhi and ten other generals with more than forty thousand soldiers were taken captive by Wei. Yao Xing decreed generous posthumous honors for every soldier killed in action. The Wei army pressed on to Puban, but Yao Xu held the defenses and refused battle, so Wei withdrew.
40
興徙河西豪右萬餘戶于長安。
Yao Xing resettled over ten thousand wealthy households from west of the Yellow River in Chang'an.
41
晉輔國將軍袁虔之、甯朔將軍劉壽、冠軍將軍高長慶、龍驤將軍郭恭等貳于桓玄，懼而奔興。 興臨東堂引見，謂虔之等曰：「桓玄雖名晉臣，其實晉賊，其才度定何如父也？ 能辦成大事以不？ 」虔之曰：「玄籍世資，雄據荊、楚，屬晉朝失政，遂偷竊宰衡。 安忍無親，多忌好殺，位不才授，爵以愛加，無公平之度，不如其父遠矣。 今既握朝權，必行篡奪，既非命世之才，正可為他人驅除耳。 此天以機便授之陛下，願速加經略，廓清吳、楚。 」興大悅，以虔之為大司農，餘皆有拜授。 虔之固讓，請疆埸自效，改授假節、甯南將軍、廣州刺史。
Jin generals Yuan Qianzhi, Liu Shou, Gao Changqing, Guo Gong, and others who had sided against Huan Xuan fled in alarm to Yao Xing. Yao Xing received them in the Eastern Hall and asked Yuan Qianzhi and the rest: "Huan Xuan may call himself a Jin minister, but he is really a traitor—how do his abilities compare with his father's? Can he accomplish a great undertaking or not?" Yuan Qianzhi replied: "Huan Xuan rests on his family's prestige and controls Jingzhou and Chu; with the Jin court in disorder he has usurped the chief minister's authority. He is ruthless toward his own kin, suspicious and murderous, hands out offices and titles by whim rather than merit—nothing like fair rule—and falls far short of his father. Now that he controls the court he will surely seize the throne; lacking the stature to rule an era, he will only pave the way for someone else. Heaven is handing Your Majesty the opening—move quickly to plan the conquest and pacify Wu and Chu." Yao Xing was delighted, named Yuan Qianzhi Grand Minister of Agriculture, and gave the others posts as well. Yuan Qianzhi insisted on declining and asked to serve on the frontier; he was instead given the credential-bearing title of General Who Calms the South and Inspector of Guangzhou.
42
興立其昭儀張氏為皇后，封子懿、弼、洸、宣、諶、愔、璞、質、逵、裕、國兒皆為公。 遣其兼大鴻臚梁斐，以新平張構為副，拜禿髮傉檀車騎將軍、廣武公，沮渠蒙遜鎮西將軍、沙州刺史、西海侯，李玄盛安西將軍、高昌侯。
Yao Xing elevated his Zhaoyi consort, Lady Zhang, to empress and enfeoffed his sons Yao Yi, Yao Bi, Yao Guang, Yao Xuan, Yao Chen, Yao Yin, Yao Pu, Yao Zhi, Yao Kui, Yao Yu, and Yao Guo'er as dukes. He dispatched Concurrent Grand Herald Liang Fei with Shiping's Zhang Gou as deputy to invest Tufa Nutan as General of Chariots and Cavalry and Duke of Guangwu, Juqu Mengxun as General Who Guards the West and Inspector of Shazhou with the marquisate of Xihai, and Li Xuansheng as General Who Pacifies the West and Marquis of Gaochang.
43
興遣鎮遠趙曜率眾二萬西屯金城，建節王松忿率騎助呂隆等守姑臧。 松忿至魏安，為傉檀弟文真所圍，眾潰，執松忿，送于傉檀。 傉檀大怒，送松忿還長安，歸罪文真，深自陳謝。
He sent General Who Extends Afar Zhao Yao with twenty thousand men to encamp west of Jincheng and Jianjie officer Wang Songfen with cavalry to help Lü Long hold Guzang. Wang Songfen reached Wei'an, where Tufa Nutan's brother Wenzhen surrounded him; his force broke and Wenzhen captured him and sent him to Nutan. Nutan was furious but sent Wang Songfen back to Chang'an, blamed Wenzhen, and offered a contrite apology.
44
興下書，錄馬嵬戰時將吏，盡擢敘之，其堡戶給復二十年。
Yao Xing ordered a roster of everyone who had served at the Battle of Mawei and promoted them all; households in those fortresses received twenty years of tax and labor exemption.
45
興性儉約，車馬無金玉之飾，自下化之，莫不敦尚清素。 然好游田，頗損農要。 京兆杜挻以僕射齊難無匡輔之益，著《豐草詩》以箴之，馮翊相雲作《德獵賦》以諷焉。 興皆覽而善之，賜以金帛，然終弗能改。
Yao Xing lived simply—his carriages bore no gold or jade—and his example spread downward until everyone favored austerity. Yet he loved the chase, which somewhat harmed farming. Du Ting of Jingzhao wrote "The Luxuriant Grass" to reproach Vice Director Qi Nan for failing to counsel the throne; Xiang Yun of Fengyi composed "The Virtuous Hunt" as a hunting satire. Yao Xing read both pieces with approval and rewarded the authors with gold and silk, yet he never reformed his habits.
46
晉順陽太守彭泉以郡降興，興遣楊佛嵩率騎五千，與其荊州刺史趙曜迎之，遂寇陷南鄉，擒建威將軍劉嵩，略地至於梁國而歸。 又遣其兼散騎常侍席確詣涼州，征呂隆弟超入侍，隆遣之。 呂隆懼禿髮傉檀之逼，表請內徙。 興遣齊難及鎮西姚詰、鎮遠乞伏乾歸、鎮遠趙曜等步騎四萬，迎隆於河西。 難至姑臧，以其司馬王尚行涼州刺史，配兵三千鎮姑臧，以將軍閻松為倉松太守，郭將為番禾太守，分戍二城，徙隆及其宗室僚屬于長安。 沮渠蒙遜遣弟如子貢其方物。 王尚綏撫遺黎，導以信義，百姓懷其惠化，翕然歸之。 北部鮮卑並遣使貢款。
Peng Quan, Jin prefect of Shunyang, yielded his commandery; Yao Xing sent Yang Fosong with five thousand horsemen and his Inspector of Jingzhou, Zhao Yao, to receive him. They then stormed Nanxiang, captured General Who Establishes Might Liu Song, raided as far as Liang commandery, and withdrew. He also dispatched Concurrent Palace Attendant Xi Que to Liangzhou to demand that Lü Long send his brother Lü Chao to the capital as a hostage; Lü Long complied. Fearful of Tufa Nutan, Lü Long petitioned to be resettled inland. Yao Xing sent Qi Nan with General Who Guards the West Yao Jie, General Who Extends Afar Qifu Gangui, General Who Extends Afar Zhao Yao, and forty thousand foot and horse to escort Lü Long from west of the Yellow River. When Qi Nan reached Guzang, he put his chief clerk Wang Shang in charge as Inspector of Liangzhou with three thousand men to hold the city, appointed General Yan Song prefect of Cangsong and Guo Jiang prefect of Fanhe to garrison those two towns, and relocated Lü Long with his kinsmen and staff to Chang'an. Juqu Mengxun sent his younger brother with tribute of regional goods. Wang Shang soothed the survivors, ruling through good faith, and the people welcomed his kindness and rallied to him. Northern Xianbei tribes all sent envoys with tribute and pledges of loyalty.
47
桓玄遣使來聘，請辛恭靖、何澹之。 興留恭靖而遣澹之，謂曰：「桓玄不推計曆運，將圖篡逆，天未忘晉，必將有義舉，以吾觀之，終當傾覆。 卿今馳往，必逢其敗，相見之期，遲不雲遠。 」初，恭靖至長安，引見興而不拜，興曰：「朕將任卿以東南之事。 」靖曰：「我寧為國家鬼，不為羌賊臣。 」興怒，幽之別室。 至是，恭靖亦逾牆遁歸。
Huan Xuan dispatched a diplomatic mission asking for Xin Gongjing and He Danzhi. Yao Xing kept Xin Gongjing but released He Danzhi, telling him: "Huan Xuan ignores the turning of Heaven's mandate and plots usurpation. Heaven has not abandoned Jin—a righteous movement will arise. In my view he is doomed to fall; Ride south now and you will arrive in time for his ruin; we shall meet again before long." Earlier, when Xin Gongjing had reached Chang'an, Yao Xing summoned him but he refused to bow. Yao Xing said, "I mean to put you in charge of affairs in the southeast." Xin Gongjing replied, "I would sooner be a loyal ghost of Jin than a minister to Qiang brigands." Yao Xing, enraged, had him confined in a separate cell. By then Xin Gongjing had also scaled a wall and escaped homeward.
48
興遣其將姚碩德、姚斂成、姚壽都等率眾三萬，伐楊盛于仇池。 壽都等入自宕昌，斂成從下辯而進。 盛遣其弟壽距成，從子斌距都。 都逆擊擒之，盡俘其眾。 楊壽等懼，率眾請降。 碩德還師。
Yao Xing sent Generals Yao Shuode, Yao Liancheng, and Yao Shoudu at the head of thirty thousand men to campaign against Yang Sheng in Chouchi. Shoudu’s column came in through Dangchang; Liancheng drove up from Xiabian. Yang Sheng sent his brother Yang Shou to hold Liancheng at bay and his nephew Yang Bin to face Shoudu. Shoudu counterattacked, took them captive, and rounded up their entire force. Yang Shou and his men, stricken with fear, brought their troops forward and offered submission. Yao Shuode withdrew his army.
49
晉汝南太守趙策委守奔於興。
Zhao Ce, Jin’s prefect of Runan, threw over his post and defected to Yao Xing.
50
興如趙逍園，引諸沙門于澄玄堂聽鳩摩羅什演說佛經。 羅什通辯夏言，尋覽舊經，多有乘謬，不與胡本相應。 興與羅什及沙門僧略、僧遷、道樹、僧睿、道坦、僧肇、曇順等八百餘人，更出大品，羅什持胡本，興執舊經，以相考校，其新文異舊者皆會於理義。 續出諸經並諸論三百餘卷。 今之新經皆羅什所譯。 興既托意於佛道，公卿已下莫不欽附，沙門自遠而至者五千餘人。 起浮圖於永貴裏，立波若台于中宮，沙門坐禪者恆有千數。 州郡化之，事佛者十室而九矣。
Yao Xing visited the Zhao Xiao Garden and gathered śramaṇas in the Chengxuan Hall to hear Kumārajīva lecture on the sutras. Kumārajīva commanded Chinese with fluency and precision; when he reviewed older translations, he found them often wrong and out of step with the Indic originals. Yao Xing joined Kumārajīva and more than eight hundred monks—among them Seng Lüe, Seng Qian, Dao Shu, Seng Rui, Dao Tan, Seng Zhao, and Tan Shun—in issuing a fresh translation of the Great Perfection of Wisdom Sutra: Kumārajīva read from the Indic manuscript while Yao Xing followed the old Chinese text, line by line, so that wherever the new wording departed from the former, it aligned with sound doctrine. They went on to issue over three hundred fascicles of additional sutras and śāstras. The “new” scriptures in circulation today are Kumārajīva’s work. Once Yao Xing committed himself to the Dharma, every minister and noble under him rushed to follow suit, and more than five thousand monks converged from distant regions. He built a stūpa in Yonggui Ward and a Prajñā terrace inside the palace; monks meditating in hall numbered in the thousands day after day. The practice spread through every prefecture and commandery until nine households in ten took up devotion to the Buddha.
51
使姚碩德及冠軍徐洛生等伐仇池，又遣建武趙琨自宕昌而進，遣其將斂俱寇漢中。
He ordered Yao Shuode and Champion General Xu Luosheng to strike Chouchi, sent General Who Establishes Martial Might Zhao Kun in from Dangchang, and tasked his officer Lian Ju with raiding Hanzhong.
52
時劉裕誅桓玄，迎復安帝，玄衛將軍、新安王桓謙，臨原王桓怡，雍州刺史桓蔚，左衛將軍桓謐，中書令桓胤，將軍何澹之等奔於興。 劉裕遣大參軍衡凱之詣姚顯，請通和，顯遣吉默報之，自是聘使不絕。 晉求南鄉諸郡，興許之。 群臣咸諫以為不可，興曰：「天下之善一也，劉裕拔萃起微，匡輔晉室，吾何惜數郡而不成其美乎！ 」遂割南鄉、順陽、新野、舞陰等十二郡歸於晉。
While Liu Yu put Huan Xuan to death and restored Emperor An to the throne, Huan Qian—Huan Xuan’s Guard General and Prince of Xin’an—along with Prince of Linyuan Huan Yi, Yongzhou inspector Huan Wei, Left Guard General Huan Mi, Palace Secretary Huan Yin, General He Danzhi, and others took refuge with Yao Xing. Liu Yu dispatched Senior Army Advisor Heng Kaizhi to Yao Xian to negotiate peace; Xian answered through Ji Mo, and formal embassies thereafter crossed without interruption. Jin asked for the Nanxiang cluster of commanderies, and Yao Xing agreed. The whole court protested that he must not yield the territory. Yao Xing replied: “Excellence under Heaven is not a zero-sum prize. Liu Yu rose from nowhere to shore up the Jin throne—why should I stint over a few commanderies and deny him this noble deed?” He therefore handed twelve commanderies—including Nanxiang, Shunyang, Xinye, and Wuyin—back to Jin.
53
姚碩德等頻敗楊盛，盛懼，請降，遣子難當及僚佐子弟數十人為質，碩德等引還。 署盛為使持節、散騎常侍、都督益、甯州諸軍事、征南大將軍、開府、益州牧、武都侯。 斂俱陷城固，徙漢中流人郭陶等三千餘家於關中。
Yao Shuode dealt Yang Sheng one defeat after another until Yang Sheng, desperate, sued for peace: he sent his son Nan Dang and dozens of officials’ sons as hostages, whereupon Shuode marched home. He invested Yang Sheng with the credential-bearing commission, the title of Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary, regional command over Yi and Ning, the rank of General Who Conquers the South with independent headquarters, the governorship of Yi Province, and the marquisate of Wudu. Lian Ju took Chenggu and resettled upward of three thousand displaced Hanzhong households—Guo Tao among them—in Guanzhong.
54
興班告境內及在朝文武，立名不得犯叔父緒及碩德之名，以彰殊禮。 興謙恭孝友，每見緒及碩德，如家人之禮，整服傾悚，言則稱字，車馬服玩，必先二叔，然後服其次者，朝廷大政，必諮之而後行。
Yao Xing issued an edict to the realm and the capital bureaucracy: no one might adopt characters tabooed by his uncles’ personal names—Yao Xu and Yao Shuode—so as to mark their singular honor. Yao Xing comported himself with humility, filial piety, and brotherly regard: whenever he met Yao Xu or Yao Shuode he behaved as though at a family gathering—adjusting his dress, bowing low, and addressing them by their courtesy names. Fine mounts, gear, and curios went first to his two uncles; only the surplus reached his own household. Weighty decisions of state he never carried out until he had sought their counsel.
55
太史令郭黁言於興曰：「戌亥之歲，當有孤寇起於西北，宜慎其鋒。 起兵如流沙，死者如亂麻，戎馬悠悠會隴頭，鮮卑、烏丸居不安，國朝疲於奔命矣。 」時所在有泉水湧出，傳云飲則愈病，後多無驗。 屢有妖人自稱神女，戮之乃止。
Court Astrologer Guo Fen warned Yao Xing: “When the calendar reaches the Xu–Hai cycle, a solitary foe will stir in the northwest—do not underestimate its bite.” Armies will surge like desert sand, corpses tangle like tossed hemp; cavalry will mass beneath Long Mountain until Xianbei and Wuhuan have nowhere to rest easy, and the throne will exhaust itself answering every alarm.” About then springs suddenly welled up across the land; people claimed the water healed sickness, though most such tales soon failed the test. Charlatans kept declaring themselves heavenly maidens until the sword silenced them.
56
興大閱，自杜郵至於羊牧。 興以姚碩德來朝，大赦其境內。 及碩德歸於秦州，興送之，及雍乃還。
Yao Xing staged a grand military review along the stretch from Duyou to Yangmu. When Yao Shuode arrived for an audience, Yao Xing proclaimed a general amnesty throughout his domain. As Shuode headed back to Qinzhou, Yao Xing rode with him all the way to Yong before turning home.
57
禿髮傉檀獻興馬三千匹，羊三萬頭。 興以為忠於己，乃署傉檀為涼州刺史，征涼州刺史王尚還長安。 涼州人申屠英等二百餘人，遣主簿胡威詣興，請留尚，興弗許。 引威見之，威流涕謂興曰：「臣州奉國五年，王威不接，銜膽棲冰、孤城獨守者，仰恃陛下威靈，俯杖良牧惠化。 忽違天人之心，以華土資狄。 若傉檀才望應代，臣豈敢言。 竊聞乃以臣等貿馬三千匹，羊三萬口，如所傳實者，是為棄人貴畜。 苟以馬供軍國，直煩尚書一符，三千餘家戶輸一匹，朝下夕辦，何故以一方委此奸胡！ 昔漢武傾天下之資，開建河西，隔絕諸戎，斷匈奴右臂，所以終能屠大宛王毋寡。 今陛下方布政玉門，流化西域，奈何以五郡之地資之犬嚴狁，忠誠華族棄之虐虜！ 非但臣州裏塗炭，懼方為聖朝旰食之憂。 」興乃遣西平人車普馳止王尚，又遣使喻傉檀。 會傉檀已至姑臧，普以狀先告之。 傉檀懼，脅遣王尚，遂入姑臧。
Tufa Nunan sent Yao Xing three thousand horses and thirty thousand sheep. Convinced of Nunan’s loyalty, Yao Xing named him inspector of Liangzhou and recalled Inspector Wang Shang to Chang’an. Over two hundred Liangzhou notables, Shen Tu Ying among them, dispatched Chief Clerk Hu Wei to beg Yao Xing to let Wang Shang stay; the emperor refused. Yao Xing received Hu Wei, who said through tears: “For five years Liangzhou has flown your banner though no imperial majesty has touched our border. Those of us who slept on frost and swallowed bitterness, holding ruined towns alone, clung to Your Majesty’s distant prestige above and to our governor’s humane rule below.” Now you would turn Heaven and men against you by handing Chinese soil to barbarian chieftains.” Had Nunan’s merit truly suited him to replace Wang Shang, I would hold my tongue.” Whisperers say we were sold for three thousand horses and thirty thousand sheep—if that is true, you prize livestock above human lives.” If horses alone were wanted for army and state, a single writ from the Ministry could levy one mount from each of three thousand households—done by nightfall. Why surrender an entire province to that treacherous Hu!” Emperor Wu of Han once poured the empire’s wealth into the Hexi corridor, walling off the western tribes and severing the Xiongnu right wing—only thus could he eventually behead the king of Dayuan, Wu-gua.” Your Majesty is only now extending edicts beyond the Jade Gate and spreading civilization westward—how can you barter five commanderies to Xianyun wolves and cast loyal Han houses to cruel nomads!” Not only will my province burn; I fear it will become the sort of crisis that keeps Your Majesty from his evening meal.” Thereupon Yao Xing sent Che Pu of Xiping racing ahead to halt Wang Shang and dispatched another envoy to warn Nunan. But Nunan had already entered Guzang; Che Pu reached him first with the news. Alarmed, Nunan bullied Wang Shang out of the city and marched in.
58
尚既至長安，坐匿呂氏宮人，擅殺逃人薄禾等，禁止南台。 涼州別駕宗敞、治中張穆、主簿邊憲、胡威等上疏理尚曰：
Once Wang Shang arrived in Chang’an, authorities charged him with harboring ladies of the former Lü court and executing fugitives such as Bo He without warrant; he was locked up at the Southern Terrace. Zong Chang, Liangzhou’s chief clerk, together with Zhang Mu, Bian Xian, Hu Wei, and others petitioned in Wang Shang’s defense:
59
臣州荒裔，鄰帶寇仇，居泰無垂拱之安，運否離傾覆之難。 自張氏頹基，德風絕而莫扇； 呂數將終，梟鶚以之翻翔。 群生嬰罔極之痛，西夏有焚如之禍。 幸皇鑒降眷，純風遠被。 刺史王尚受任垂滅之州，策成難全之際，輕身率下，躬儉節用，勞逸豐約，與眾同之，勸課農桑，時無廢業。 然後振王威以掃不庭，回天波以蕩氛穢。 則群逆冰摧，不俟硃陽之曜； 若秋霜隕籜，豈待勁風之威。 何定遠之足高，營平之獨美！ 經始甫爾，會朝算改授，使希世之功不終於必成，易失之機踐之而莫展。 當其時而明其事者，誰不慨然！
“Our province lies on a barbarous march, ringed by enemies. Even in calm years we enjoy no effortless peace; when fate turns cruel, we face utter ruin. Since the Zhang dynasty’s foundations crumbled, no kindly breeze has stirred to revive them; when the Lü regime neared its term, owls and kites wheeled in its dying sky. The people tasted measureless grief; the western regions knew the scourge of fire.” Heaven looked down with mercy; your civilizing wind reached even us. Inspector Wang Shang took charge when our land was almost lost and survival hung by a thread. He shared every hardship with the people—labors and leisure, feast and famine—lived modestly, and pushed farming and silk work until no season lay idle. Only then did he unfurl imperial majesty to subdue rebels and turn your cleansing tide upon corruption.” The traitors shattered like thaw ice—no need to wait for midsummer sun; they fell like bamboo struck by autumn frost—no gust required.” Why exalt only Ban Chao of Dingyuan or praise Zhao Chongguo of Yingping alone!” His work had scarcely begun when the court reassigned him, cutting short a deed that might have rivaled history’s finest and leaving a fleeting chance trampled, unrealized. Who, knowing the truth of that hour, could withhold a bitter sigh!”
60
既遠役遐方，劬勞於外，雖效未酬恩，而在公無闕。 自至京師，二旬於今，出車之命莫逮，萋斐之責惟深。 以取呂氏宮人裴氏及殺逃人薄禾等為南台所禁，天鑒玄鏡，暫免圇圄，譏繩之文，未離簡墨。 裴氏年垂知命，首發二毛，嫠居本家，不在尚室，年邁姿陋，何用送為！ 邊籓耍捍，眾力是寄，禾等私逃，罪應憲墨，以殺止殺，安邊之義也。 假若以不送裴氏為罪者，正闕奚官之一女子耳。 論勳則功重，言瑕則過微。 而執憲吹毛求疵，忘勞記過，斯先哲所以泣血于當年，微臣所以仰天而灑淚。
Though service on that distant frontier earned him no fitting reward, he never failed the public trust.” Since he reached the capital twenty days ago, no summons to duty has come—only slander piles deeper.” He stands accused of sheltering a Lü-clan concubine surnamed Pei and executing fugitives such as Bo He—charges that led the Southern Terrace to jail him. Heaven’s judgment may have paused his fetters, yet the indictment’s ink has not dried.” That Lady Pei nears fifty, her hair streaked with grey, widowed in her own kin’s house—never Wang Shang’s consort—and plain with age: why pretend she was ‘presented’ to him!” Frontiers depend on collective strength: Bo He and his fellows were deserters felons—death was statutory. To kill lawbreakers to still the frontier is precisely how peace is kept.” If omitting to hand over one palace maid is crime, the tally is short by a single missing servant girl.” Weighed for merit, his deeds tower; weighed for fault, the blemish is slight.” Yet the law officers hunt for the slightest flaw, blind to his sweat—precisely why worthies of old wept blood, and why your servants lift tear-streaked faces to the sky.”
61
且尚之奉國，曆事二朝，能否效於既往，優劣簡在聖心，就有微過，功足相補，宜弘罔極之施，以彰覆載之恩。
Wang Shang has served two reigns; his record speaks plainly to your sage discernment. Even if a small fault attaches, his deeds outweigh it—grant him Heaven-wide mercy and show the breadth of your favor.”
62
臣等生自西州，無翰飛之翼，久沈偽政，絕進趣之途。 及皇化既沾，投竿之心冥發，遂策名委質，位忝吏端。 主辱臣憂，故重繭披款，惟陛下亮之。
We hail from the western marches without wings to fly to court, long trapped under usurpers and barred from honorable office.” Once your civilization touched us, our hearts pledged loyalty unbidden; we inscribed our names and took humble posts.” A lord’s shame is his servants’ grief—hence we blister our feet again to lay bare our plea; may Your Majesty understand.”
63
興覽之大悅，謂其黃門侍郎姚文祖曰：「卿知宗敞乎？ 」文祖曰：「與臣州裏，西方之英雋。 」興曰：「有表理王尚，文義甚佳，當王尚研思耳。 」文祖曰：「尚在南台，禁止不與賓客交通，敞寓於楊桓，非尚明矣。 」興曰：「若爾，桓為措思乎？ 」文祖曰：「西方評敞甚重，優於楊桓。 敞昔與呂超周旋，陛下試可問之。 」興因謂超曰：「宗敞文才何如？ 可是誰輩？ 」超曰：「敞在西土，時論甚美，方敞魏之陳、徐，晉之潘、陸。 」即以表示超曰：「涼州小地，寧有此才乎？ 」超曰：「臣以敞余文比之，未足稱多。 琳琅出於昆嶺，明珠生於海濱，若必以地求人，則文命大夏之棄夫，姬昌東夷之擯士。 但當問其文彩何如，不可以區宇格物。 」興悅，赦尚之罪，以為尚書。
Yao Xing read the memorial with delight and asked his Palace Gate Gentleman Yao Wenzhu, “Do you know Zong Chang?” Wenzhu replied, “He is a countryman of mine—a brilliant man of the western frontier.” Yao Xing said, “This defense of Wang Shang is superbly written—it must be Wang Shang’s own careful prose.” Wenzhu answered, “Wang Shang is confined on the Southern Terrace, forbidden all visitors. Zong Chang is staying with Yang Huan—the author cannot be Wang Shang.” Yao Xing asked, “Then did Yang Huan set brush to it?” “Western scholars rank Zong Chang far above Yang Huan.” He once moved in Lü Chao’s circle—Your Majesty might ask him.” Yao Xing turned to Lü Chao. “What do you make of Zong Chang’s literary gifts?” To whom would you compare him?” Lü Chao said, “Among western scholars he is celebrated—the sort of writer Wei paired with Chen Lin and Xu Gan, Jin with Pan Yue and Lu Ji.” Yao Xing handed him the memorial. “Liangzhou is a petty corner—could it breed such a talent?” Lü Chao replied, “Set beside his other compositions, this piece is hardly remarkable.” Treasured jade comes from Kunlun’s ridges; lustrous pearls wash ashore—yet if birthplaces alone qualified men, Yu the Great would remain a Xia outcast and King Wen a barbarian reject.” Judge the prose on its merits—do not fence genius inside provincial borders.” Pleased, Yao Xing absolved Wang Shang and appointed him Minister of the Masters of Writing.
