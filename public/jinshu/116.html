1
姚弋仲姚襄姚萇
Biographies of Yao Yizhong, Yao Xiang, and Yao Chang
2
姚弋仲，南安赤亭羌人也。 其先有虞氏之苗裔。 禹封舜少子於西戎，世為羌酋。 其後燒當雄於洮、罕之間，七世孫填虞，漢中元末寇擾西州，為楊虛侯馬武所敗，徙出塞。 虞九世孫遷那率種人內附，漢朝嘉之，假冠軍將軍、西羌校尉、歸順王，處之于南安之赤亭。 那玄孫柯回為魏鎮西將軍、綏戎校尉、西羌都督。 回生弋仲，少英毅，不營產業，唯以收恤為務，眾皆畏而親之。 永嘉之亂，東徙榆眉，戎夏繈負隨之者數萬，自稱護西羌校尉、雍州刺史、扶風公。
Yao Yizhong was a Qiang from Chiting in Nan'an commandery. His line traced back to the ancient Youyu people. When Yu the Great enfeoffed a younger son of Shun among the western tribes, the family became hereditary Qiang chieftains. Later the Shao Dang branch dominated the Tao and Han riverlands. Seven generations later Tian Yu raided the northwest at the end of the Han Zhongyuan era, was crushed by Ma Wu, Marquis of Yangxu, and was driven beyond the frontier. Nine generations on, Qian Na led his clansmen to submit to the Han, which honored him with the titles General Who Crowns the Army, Colonel of the Western Qiang, and King of Allegiance, and resettled the tribe at Chiting in Nan'an. His descendant Ke Hui served Wei as General Who Garrisons the West, Colonel Who Pacifies the Rong, and commander of the Western Qiang. Ke Hui's son Yao Yizhong was bold and resolute from boyhood. He spurned private gain and devoted himself to succoring the needy; men feared his sternness yet loved him. When disaster struck in the Yongjia era he led his people east to Yumei, and tens of thousands of tribesmen and Chinese followed in his train. He declared himself Colonel Who Protects the Western Qiang, Inspector of Yongzhou, and Duke of Fufeng.
3
劉曜之平陳安也，以弋仲為平西將軍，封平襄公，邑之於隴上。 及石季龍克上邽，弋仲說之曰：「明公握兵十萬，功高一時，正是行權立策之日。 隴上多豪，秦風猛勁，道隆後服，道洿先叛，宜徙隴上豪強，虛其心腹，以實畿甸。 」季龍納之，啟勒以弋仲行安西將軍、六夷左都督。 後晉豫州刺史祖約奔於勒，勒禮待之，弋仲上疏曰：「祖約殘賊晉朝，逼殺太后，不忠於主，而陛下寵之，臣恐奸亂之萌，此其始矣。 」勒善之，後竟誅約。
After Liu Yao destroyed Chen An he named Yao Yizhong General Who Pacifies the West, invested him as Duke of Pingxiang, and settled his fief on the Long plateau. After Shi Hu captured Shanggui, Yao Yizhong urged him, "You command a hundred thousand veterans and stand above every rival—now is the moment to seize the initiative and lay long-term plans. The Long plateau teems with strongmen; Qin folk are fierce. They bow late when power waxes and rebel first when it wanes. Relocate those magnates from the heartland and settle them around the capital to strengthen the core. Shi Hu agreed and petitioned Shi Le to name Yao Yizhong acting General Who Pacifies the West and left commander over the six barbarian corps. When Zu Yue of Jin defected, Shi Le honored him. Yao Yizhong memorialized: "Zu Yue ravaged the Jin court and hounded the empress dowager to her death. Rewarding such infidelity plants the seed of rebellion. Shi Le accepted the advice and later executed Zu Yue.
4
勒既死，季龍執權，思弋仲之言，遂徙秦、雍豪傑于關東。 弋仲率部眾數萬遷於清河，拜奮武將軍、西羌大都督，封襄平縣公。 及季龍廢石弘自立，弋仲稱疾不賀。 季龍累召之，乃赴，正色謂季龍曰：「奈何把臂受託而反奪之乎！ 」季龍憚其強正而不之責。 遷持節、十郡六夷大都督、冠軍大將軍。 性清儉鯁直，不修威儀，屢獻讜言，無所回避，季龍甚重之。 朝之大議，靡不參決，公卿亦憚而推下之。 武城左尉，季龍寵姬之弟也，曾擾其部，弋仲執尉，數以迫脅之狀，命左右斬之。 尉叩頭流血，左右諫，乃止。 其剛直不回，皆此類也。
After Shi Le's death Shi Hu took power, recalled Yao Yizhong's counsel, and resettled the great clans of Qin and Yong beyond the eastern passes. Yao Yizhong brought tens of thousands of followers to Qinghe, received the titles General Who Rouses Might and commander of the Western Qiang, and was ennobled as Duke of Xiangping county. When Shi Hu deposed Shi Hong and seized the throne, Yao Yizhong feigned illness and offered no congratulations. After repeated summons he appeared and rebuked Shi Hu: "How can you clasp a friend's arm in trust and then steal his throne? Shi Hu, respecting his blunt integrity, let the words pass. He was promoted to Bearer of the Staff, commander over ten commanderies and six barbarian corps, and Grand General Who Crowns the Army. Austere, blunt, and indifferent to pomp, he spoke his mind without flinching, and Shi Hu held him in high regard. He sat in on every weighty policy debate, and even the great ministers deferred to him. The left commandant of Wucheng was the brother of Shi Hu's favorite concubine. When he molested Yao Yizhong's men, Yizhong seized him, listed his crimes, and ordered his immediate execution. The man begged until his brow bled; aides interceded, and Yizhong stayed the blade. Such unbending integrity was typical of him.
5
季龍末，梁犢敗李農于滎陽，季龍大懼，馳召弋仲。 弋仲率其部眾八千餘人屯於南郊，輕騎至鄴。 時季龍病，不時見弋仲，引入領軍省，賜其所食之食。 弋仲怒不食，曰：「召我擊賊，豈來覓食邪！ 我不知上存亡，若一見，雖死無恨。 」左右言之，乃引見。 弋仲數季龍曰：「兒死來愁邪？ 乃至於疾！ 兒小時不能使好人輔相，至令相殺。 兒自有過，責其下人太甚，故反耳。 汝病久，所立兒小，若不差，天下必亂。 當宜憂此，不煩憂賊也。 犢等因思歸之心，共為奸盜，所行殘賊，此成擒耳。 老羌請效死前鋒，使一舉而了。 」弋仲性狷直，俗無尊卑皆汝之，季龍恕而不責，於坐授使持節、侍中、征西大將軍，賜以鎧馬。 弋仲曰：「汝看老羌堪破賊以不？ 」於是貫鉀跨馬於庭中，策馬南馳，不辭而出，遂滅梁犢。 以功加劍履上殿，入朝不趨，進封西平郡公。
Late in Shi Hu's reign Liang Du routed Li Nong at Xingyang. Terrified, Shi Hu urgently summoned Yao Yizhong. Yao Yizhong brought eight thousand warriors to camp south of the capital and rode ahead to Ye. Shi Hu was too ill for an audience and had him wait in the guard headquarters with a meal from the imperial table. Yao Yizhong refused the food: "You called me to fight rebels, not to dine. I do not know whether our lord still lives. Let me see him once and I can die content. When his escort relayed this, Shi Hu received him. Yao Yizhong scolded Shi Hu: "Have you sickened yourself mourning your sons? Enough to fall ill? You never gave your boys worthy tutors, and now they have murdered one another. Your sons were at fault, yet you punished their followers too harshly—that is why they turned on you. You have been ill a long time and the heir is a child. If you do not recover, the realm will tear apart. Worry about that, not about Liang Du. Liang Du's men are mutineers driven by homesickness; their cruelty makes them easy prey. Let this old Qiang die in your vanguard and finish them in one blow. Blunt to a fault, he addressed everyone as "you," high or low. Shi Hu only smiled and at table invested him as Bearer of the Staff, Palace Attendant, and Grand General Who Campaigns West, with armor and a charger. Yao Yizhong asked, "Tell me, can this old Qiang still break your rebels? He donned mail, leaped into the saddle in the courtyard, and galloped south without a word of leave, and annihilated Liang Du. For this feat he received the right to wear sword and shoes in court, enter without hurrying, and was promoted to Duke of Xiping.
6
冉閔之亂，弋仲率眾討閔，次於混橋。 石祗僭號于襄國，以弋仲為右丞相，待以殊禮。 祗與閔相攻，弋仲遣其子襄救祗，戒襄曰：「汝才十倍于閔，若不梟擒，不須復見我也。 」襄擊閔于常盧澤，大破之而歸。 弋仲怒襄之不擒閔也，杖之一百。
During Ran Min's coup Yao Yizhong marched against him and camped at Hun Bridge. Shi Zhi, who had declared himself emperor at Xiangguo, named Yao Yizhong Right Chancellor and honored him with exceptional ceremony. When Shi Zhi and Ran Min clashed, Yao Yizhong sent Yao Xiang to the rescue with a warning: "You outmatch Ran Min tenfold. Bring me his head or do not return. Yao Xiang met Ran Min at Changlu Marsh, routed him, and withdrew. Furious that Yao Xiang had not taken Ran Min prisoner, Yao Yizhong had him flogged one hundred strokes.
7
弋仲部曲馬何羅博學有文才，張豺之輔石世也，背弋仲歸豺，豺以為尚書郎。 豺敗，復歸，咸勸殺之。 弋仲曰：「今正是招才納奇之日，當收其力用，不足害也。 」以為參軍。 其寬恕如此。
Ma Heluo, a client of Yao Yizhong, was a scholar. When Zhang Cai dominated the boy emperor Shi Shi, Ma deserted Yizhong for Zhang, who named him a secretary at the Masters of Writing. When Zhang Cai fell Ma came back; everyone urged his execution. Yao Yizhong replied, "These are times to gather talent. Use his abilities; he is no threat. He made him a staff adviser. Such was his magnanimity.
8
弋仲有子四十二人，常戒諸子曰：「吾本以晉室大亂，石氏待吾厚，故欲討其賊臣以報其德。 今石氏已滅，中原無主，自古以來未有戎狄作天子者。 我死，汝便歸晉，當竭盡臣節，無為不義之事。 」乃遣使請降。 永和七年，拜弋仲使持節、六夷大都督、都督江、淮諸軍事、車騎大將軍、儀同三司、大單于，封高陵郡公。 八年，卒，時年七十三。
Yao Yizhong fathered forty-two sons and often told them, "I served the Shi because they honored me while Jin collapsed, and I meant to purge their traitors in return. The Shi are gone and the heartland has no ruler, yet no barbarian has ever ruled all China as Son of Heaven. When I die, submit to Jin and serve with full loyalty. Do nothing dishonorable. He then sent envoys to offer submission to Jin. In the seventh Yonghe year Jin confirmed him as Bearer of the Staff, commander of the six barbarian hosts, director of Jiang and Huai operations, Grand General of Chariots and Cavalry with honors matching the Three Excellencies, Great Chanyu, and Duke of Gaoling. He died the next year at seventy-three.
9
子襄之入關也，為苻生所敗，弋仲之柩為生所得，生以王禮葬之于天水冀縣。 萇僭位，追諡曰景元皇帝，廟號始祖，墓曰高陵，置園邑五百家。
When Yao Xiang invaded Guanzhong Fu Sheng defeated him and seized Yao Yizhong's coffin, burying it with princely honors at Ji in Tianshui. After Yao Chang took the throne he posthumously named his father Emperor Jingyuan, temple name Progenitor, with a mausoleum called Gaoling and a guard colony of five hundred households.
10
襄字景國，弋仲之第五子也。 年十七，身長八尺五寸，臂垂過膝，雄武多才藝，明察善撫納，士眾愛敬之，咸請為嗣。 弋仲弗許，百姓固請者日有千數，乃授之以兵。 石祗僭號，以襄為使持節、驃騎將軍、護烏丸校尉、豫州刺史、新昌公。 晉遣使拜襄持節、平北將軍、并州刺史、即丘縣公。
Yao Xiang, courtesy name Jingguo, was the fifth son of Yao Yizhong. At seventeen he stood eight and a half chi, his arms reached past his knees, and he combined martial prowess with many skills. Keen-eyed and winning in manner, soldiers loved him and urged that he succeed his father. Yao Yizhong refused until thousands petitioned daily; then he gave Yao Xiang a command. Shi Zhi named him Bearer of the Staff, General of Agile Cavalry, Wuhuan colonel, Inspector of Yuzhou, and Duke of Xinchang. Jin invested him with the staff, the title General Who Pacifies the North, Inspector of Bingzhou, and Duke of Jiuqiu county.
11
弋仲死，襄秘不發喪，率戶六萬南攻陽平、元城、發幹，皆破之，殺掠三千餘家，屯於碻磝津。 以太原王亮為長史，天水尹赤為司馬，略陽伏子成為左部帥，南安斂岐為右部帥，略陽黑那為前部帥，強白為後部帥，太原薛贊、略陽王權翼為參軍。 南至滎陽，始發喪行服。 與高昌、李曆戰于麻田，馬中流矢死，賴其弟萇以免。 晉處襄於譙城，遣五弟為任，單騎度淮，見豫州刺史謝尚于壽春。 尚命去仗衛，幅巾以待之，一面交款，便若平生。
When Yao Yizhong died Yao Xiang concealed the death, took sixty thousand households south, stormed Yangping, Yuancheng, and Fagan, slaughtered more than three thousand families, and camped at the Qiao'ao crossing. He named Wang Liang of Taiyuan chief clerk, Yin Chi of Tianshui army major, Fuzicheng of Lueyang commander of the left wing, Lianqi of Nan'an of the right, Heina of Lueyang of the vanguard, Qiang Bai of the rear, and Xue Zan and Quan Yi as staff advisers. Only at Xingyang did he announce his father's death and don mourning. Against Gao Chang and Li Li at Matian his mount was killed by an arrow; only his brother Yao Chang saved him. Jin quartered him at Qiao, took his fifth brother as hostage, and Yao Xiang rode alone across the Huai to meet Xie Shang, Inspector of Yuzhou, at Shouchun. Xie Shang dismissed arms and escort, received him in simple dress, and at first meeting they spoke as old friends.
12
襄少有高名，雄武冠世，好學博通，雅善談論，英濟之稱著于南夏。 中軍將軍、揚州刺史殷浩憚其威名，乃因襄諸弟，頻遣刺客殺襄，刺客皆推誠告實，襄待之若舊。 浩潛遣將軍魏憬率五千餘人襲襄，襄乃斬憬而並其眾。 浩愈惡之，乃使將軍劉啟守譙，遷襄于梁國蠡台，表授梁國內史。 襄遣權翼詣浩，浩曰：「姚平北每舉動自由，豈所望也。 」翼曰：「將軍輕納奸言，自生疑貳，愚謂猜嫌之由，不在於彼。 」浩曰：「姚君縱放小人，盜竊吾馬，王臣之體固若是乎？ 」翼曰：「將軍謂姚平北以威武自強，終為難保，校兵練眾，將懲不恪，取馬者欲以自衛耳。 」浩曰：「何至是也。 」浩遣謝萬討襄，襄逆擊破之。 浩甚怒，會聞關中有變，浩率眾北伐，襄乃要擊浩于山桑，大敗之，斬獲萬計，收其資仗。 使兄益守山桑壘，復如淮南。 浩遣劉啟、王彬之伐山桑，襄自淮南擊滅之，鼓行濟淮，屯於盱眙，招掠流人，眾至七萬，分置守宰，勸課農桑，遣使建鄴，罪狀殷浩，並自陳謝。
Renowned while young, unmatched in arms, learned and eloquent, Yao Xiang was hailed as a champion of the south. Yin Hao, general of the central army and governor of Yangzhou, envied his fame and repeatedly sent killers through Yao Xiang's brothers; each assassin confessed and Yao Xiang treated him as a guest. Yin Hao secretly sent Wei Jing with five thousand men; Yao Xiang slew Wei Jing and absorbed his troops. Yin Hao then garrisoned Qiao under Liu Qi, moved Yao Xiang to Litai in Liang, and had him named interior clerk of Liang. Yao Xiang sent Quan Yi to Yin Hao, who complained, "Your General Who Pacifies the North does as he pleases—hardly what we expect. Quan Yi replied, "You credit malicious gossip and breed your own suspicions. The fault is not his. Yin Hao said, "He lets his men steal my horses—is that fitting for a servant of the throne? Quan Yi said, "You fear Yao Xiang will rely on force and prove untrustworthy. He drills his troops to punish slack discipline; the horses were taken for self-defense. Yin Hao said, "Surely not. Yin Hao sent Xie Wan against him; Yao Xiang met him and won. Yin Hao was furious. When unrest drew him north on campaign, Yao Xiang ambushed him at Shansang, shattered his army, took ten thousand heads, and captured his baggage and weapons. He left his brother Yao Yi to hold Shansang and returned toward Huainan. Yin Hao sent Liu Qi and Wang Binzhi against Shansang; Yao Xiang marched from Huainan, destroyed them, crossed the Huai with drums beating, and camped at Xuyi. He rallied refugees until his host reached seventy thousand, appointed local officials, promoted farming, and sent envoys to the capital accusing Yin Hao while offering his own apology.
13
流人郭斁等千餘人執晉堂邑內史劉仕降於襄，朝延大震，以吏部尚書周閔為中軍將軍，緣江備守。 襄將佐部眾皆北人，咸勸襄北還。 襄方軌北引，自稱大將軍、大單于，進攻外黃，為晉邊將所敗。 襄收散卒而勤撫恤之，於是復振。 乃據許昌，將如河東以圖關右，自許遂攻洛陽，逾月不克。 其長史王亮諫襄曰：「公英略蓋天下，士眾思效力命，不可損威勞眾，守此孤城。 宜還河北，以弘遠略。 」襄曰：「洛陽雖小，山河四塞之固，亦是用武之地。 吾欲先據洛陽，然後開建大業。 」俄而亮卒，襄哭之甚慟，曰：「天將不欲成吾事乎？ 王亮舍我去也！」
More than a thousand refugees led by Guo Yi seized Liu Shi, interior clerk of Tangyi, and defected to Yao Xiang. The court was shaken and named Zhou Min of the Ministry of Personnel general of the central army to guard the Yangzi line. His officers and troops were northerners and urged him to march home. He turned north as Grand General and Great Chanyu, struck Waihuang, and was beaten by Jin frontier troops. He rallied the fugitives, tended them kindly, and his strength returned. He seized Xuchang, meant to cross into Hedong and win the northwest, but from Xuchang attacked Luoyang and failed for more than a month. Wang Liang urged him, "Your genius spans the realm and your men yearn to serve. Do not waste prestige and troops on this isolated city. Withdraw north of the Yellow River and pursue the larger design. Yao Xiang replied, "Luoyang is small but ringed by natural barriers—a true seat of war. I mean to take Luoyang first, then build the great enterprise. Soon Wang Liang died. Yao Xiang wailed, "Does Heaven forbid my success? Wang Liang has abandoned me!"
14
晉征西大將軍桓溫自江陵伐襄，戰于伊水北，為溫所敗，率麾下數千騎奔於北山。 其夜，百姓棄妻子隨襄者五千餘人，屯據陽鄉，赴者又四千餘戶。 襄前後敗喪數矣，眾知襄所在，輒扶老攜幼賓士而赴之。 時或傳襄創重不濟，溫軍所得士女莫不北望揮涕。 其得物情如此。 先是，弘農楊亮歸襄，襄待以客禮。 後奔桓溫，溫問襄於亮，亮曰：「神明器宇，孫策之儔，而雄武過之。 」其見重如是。
Huan Wen marched from Jiangling, met Yao Xiang north of the Yi River, broke him, and Yao Xiang fled into the northern hills with a few thousand horsemen. That night over five thousand civilians left families to follow him at Yangxiang, and four thousand more households soon joined. Though Yao Xiang had suffered many defeats, whenever people learned where he was they hurried old and young alike to his camp. From time to time word ran that Yao Xiang's wounds were mortal and he would not survive; every soldier's wife and captive commoner in Huan Wen's host gazed northward and wept. Such was the devotion he commanded among the people. Earlier, Yang Liang of Hongnong had defected to Yao Xiang, who received him with the rites due an honored guest. He later fled to Huan Wen, who asked Yang Liang what manner of man Yao Xiang was. Liang replied, "He has the aura of a born leader—Sun Ce's peer, and even more formidable in arms." Such was the esteem he inspired.
15
萇字景茂，弋仲第二十四子也。 少聰哲，多權略，廓落任率，不修行業，諸兄皆奇之。 隨襄征伐，每參大謀。 襄之寇洛陽也，夢萇服袞衣，升御坐，諸酋長皆侍立，旦謂將佐曰：「吾夢如此，此兒志度不恆，或能大起吾族。 」襄之敗于麻田也，馬中流矢死，萇下馬以授襄，襄曰：「汝何以自免？ 」萇曰：「但令兄濟，豎子安敢害萇！ 」會救至，俱免。
Yao Chang, whose courtesy name was Jingmao, was the twenty-fourth son of Yao Yizhong. Even as a boy he was sharp and resourceful, fond of schemes yet openhanded and free in his ways, careless of formal self-cultivation; each of his older brothers regarded him as extraordinary. He rode with Yao Xiang on every campaign and was consulted on the weightiest decisions. While Yao Xiang was striking toward Luoyang he dreamed that Yao Chang stood robed as an emperor on the throne while tribal chiefs waited on him in ranks. At daybreak he told his staff, "I have seen such a dream. The lad's ambition is no common thing; he may yet exalt our whole line." After the rout at Madian, Yao Xiang's horse was killed by a stray shaft; Yao Chang sprang down and pressed the reins into his brother's hand. Yao Xiang cried, "And how will you get away?" Yao Chang answered, "If you ride clear, no pack of nobodies will touch me." Relief came in time, and both men survived.
16
及襄死，萇率諸弟降于苻生。 苻堅以萇為揚武將軍。 曆左衛將軍，隴東、汲郡、河東、武都、武威、巴西、扶風太守，甯、幽、兗三州刺史，復為揚武將軍，步兵校尉，封益都侯。 為堅將，累有大功。
After Yao Xiang fell, Yao Chang brought their brothers to submit to Fu Sheng. Fu Jian named him General Who Raises Might. He rose through General of the Left Guard, magistracies across Longdong, Ji, Hedong, Wudu, Wuwei, Baxi, and Fufeng, and inspectorships of Ning, You, and Yan, then returned to the rank of General Who Raises Might and Colonel of Foot Soldiers, with a marquisate at Yidu. As Fu Jian's commander he won distinction again and again.
17
初，萇隨楊安伐蜀，嘗晝寢水旁，上有神光煥然，左右咸異之。 及苻堅寇晉，以萇為龍驤將軍、督益、梁州諸軍事，謂萇曰：「朕本以龍驤建業，龍驤之號未曾假人，今特以相授，山南之事一以委卿。 」堅左將軍竇沖進曰：「王者無戲言，此將不祥之征也，惟陛下察之。 」堅默然。
Long before, while serving under Yang An in the conquest of Shu, Yao Chang dozed by a stream at noon; a supernatural radiance played above him, and every man in his escort knew it for an omen. When Fu Jian marched against Jin he invested Yao Chang as Dragon-Soaring General and gave him command of all forces in Yi and Liang. "The Dragon-Soaring banner built my throne," he said. "I have never given that name away; now it is yours alone. South of the Qinling ridges, your word is law." His general Dou Chong stepped forward. "Sovereigns do not speak in sport," he warned. "That commission is a dark portent. I urge you to reconsider." Fu Jian said nothing.
18
堅既敗於淮南，歸長安，慕容泓起兵叛堅。 堅遣子叡討之，以萇為司馬。 為泓所敗，叡死之。 萇遣龍驤長史趙都詣堅謝罪，堅怒，殺之。 萇懼，奔於渭北，遂如馬牧。 西州豪族尹詳、趙曜、王欽盧、王欽盧、牛雙、狄廣、張乾等率五萬餘家，咸推萇為盟主。 萇將距之，天水尹緯說萇曰：「今百六之數既臻，秦亡之兆已見，以將軍威靈命世，必能匡濟時艱，故豪傑驅馳，咸同推仰。 明公宜降心從議，以副群望，不可坐觀沈溺而不拯救之。 」萇乃從緯謀，乙太元九年自稱大將軍、大單于、萬年秦王，大赦境內，年號白雀，稱制行事。 以天水尹詳、南安龐演為左右長史，南安姚晃、尹緯為左右司馬，天水狄伯支、焦虔、梁希、龐魏、任謙為從事中郎，姜訓、閻遵為掾屬，王據、焦世、蔣秀、尹延年、牛雙、張乾為參軍，王欽盧、姚方成、王破虜、楊難、尹嵩、裴騎、趙曜、狄廣、黨刪等為帥。
After Fu Jian's defeat in Huainan, he withdrew to Chang'an; Murong Hong then rose in revolt. Fu Jian sent his son Fu Rui against the rebels and named Yao Chang his army marshal. Murong Hong shattered the expedition, and Fu Rui died on the field. Yao Chang sent his chief clerk Zhao Du to apologize; Fu Jian, furious, had Zhao executed. Terror-stricken, Yao Chang bolted across the Wei to the northern pastures. Western magnates—Yin Xiang, Zhao Yao, Wang Qinlu, Wang Qinlu (named twice in the text), Niu Shuang, Di Guang, Zhang Qian, and their following—mobilized over fifty thousand households and hailed Yao Chang as their league chief. Yao Chang meant to decline, but Yin Wei of Tianshui urged him on: "The great reckoning is upon us and Former Qin is doomed. Your prestige commands the hour; every bold spirit rallies to you because they believe you alone can save the realm." Humble your pride, accept their mandate, and answer the hope of the world. You must not watch them founder and do nothing. Yao Chang took Yin Wei's counsel. In the ninth year of the Eastern Jin Taiyuan era he declared himself Grand General, Great Chanyu, and Prince of Wannian of Qin, ordered a general amnesty, chose the reign title White Sparrow, and ruled as regent. He named Yin Xiang of Tianshui and Pang Yan of Nan'an his senior clerks, Yao Huang and Yin Wei his majors, Di Bozhi, Jiao Qian, Liang Xi, Pang Wei, and Ren Qian attendant clerks, Jiang Xun and Yan Zun administrative aides, Wang Ju, Jiao Shi, Jiang Xiu, Yin Yannian, Niu Shuang, and Zhang Qian staff advisers, and Wang Qinlu, Yao Fangcheng, Wang Polu, Yang Nan, Yin Song, Pei Qi, Zhao Yao, Di Guang, Dang Shan, and the rest brigade commanders.
19
時慕容沖與苻堅相攻，眾甚盛。 萇將西上，恐沖遏之，乃遣使通和，以子崇為質於沖，進屯北地，厲兵積粟，以觀時變。 苻堅先徙晉人李祥等數千戶于敷陸，至是，降於萇，北地、新平、安定羌胡降者十餘萬戶。 堅率諸將攻之，不能克。
Murong Chong and Fu Jian were locked in war, and Chong's army had swollen to vast numbers. Planning a drive westward, Yao Chang feared Murong Chong might bar his path. He sued for truce, left his son Yao Chong as hostage, moved his camp to Beidi, drilled his troops and filled his granaries, and waited for the political weather to break. Fu Jian had once resettled several thousand Jin households led by Li Xiang at Fulu; they now came over to Yao Chang, and more than a hundred thousand Qiang and Hu households in Beidi, Xinping, and Anding followed suit. Fu Jian threw his generals against him but could not break his lines.
20
萇聞容慕沖攻長安，議進趨之計，群下咸曰：「宜先據咸陽以制天下。 」萇曰：「燕因懷舊之士而起兵，若功成事捷，咸有東歸之思，安能久固秦川！ 吾欲移兵嶺北，廣收資實，須秦弊燕回，然後垂拱取之。 兵不血刃，坐定天下，此卞莊得二之義也。 」堅甯朔將軍宋方率騎三千從雲中將赴長安，萇自貳縣要破之，方單馬奔免，其司馬田晃率眾降萇。 萇遣諸將攻新平，克之，因略地至安定，嶺北諸城盡降之。
Learning that Murong Chong was storming Chang'an, Yao Chang called council. His officers urged, "Seize Xianyang first and the empire is yours to steer." Yao Chang objected. "Yan feeds on nostalgia. Once they win, every soldier will dream of home in the east. They cannot hold the Guanzhong basin for long." I will shift my host beyond the northern ranges, gather strength and stores, and let Former Qin bleed while Yan marches away—then I can take Guanzhong without striking a blow. To settle the empire from a chair, blades unstained—that is Bian Zhuang's trick of letting two tigers tear each other. Fu Jian's General Who Calms the North, Song Fang, was racing from Yunzhong toward Chang'an with three thousand riders when Yao Chang ambushed him at Er County. Song Fang escaped alone; his major Tian Huang surrendered the column. Yao Chang's generals stormed Xinping, swept on to Anding, and every fortress north of the ranges opened its gates.
21
時苻堅為慕容沖所逼，走入五將山。 沖入長安。 堅司隸校尉權翼、尚書趙遷、大鴻臚皇甫覆、光祿大夫薛贊、扶風太守段鏗等文武數百人奔於萇。 萇遣驍騎將軍吳忠率騎圍堅，萇如新平。 俄而忠執堅，送之。
Fu Jian, harried by Murong Chong, fled into the Wujiang hills. Murong Chong took Chang'an. Several hundred of Fu Jian's officials—among them Metropolitan Governor Quan Yi, Minister Zhao Qian, Grand Herald Huangfu Fu, Palace Grandee Xue Zan, and Fufeng prefect Duan Keng—rode to Yao Chang's camp. Yao Chang sent Fierce Cavalry General Wu Zhong to ring Fu Jian with cavalry while he himself rode to Xinping. Wu Zhong soon seized Fu Jian and brought him in.
22
慕容沖遣其車騎大將軍高蓋率眾五萬來伐，戰于新平南，大破之，蓋率麾下數千人來降，拜散騎常侍。
Murong Chong ordered Gao Gai, his Cavalry and Chariots commander, against Yao Chang with fifty thousand men. South of Xinping Yao Chang shattered the army; Gao Gai brought a few thousand survivors over and was named Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary.
23
沖既率眾東下，長安空虛。 盧水郝奴稱帝于長安，渭北盡應之。 扶風王驎有眾數千，保據馬嵬。 奴遣弟多攻驎。 萇伐驎，破之，驎走漢中。 執多而進攻奴，降之。
Once Murong Chong marched east, Chang'an stood hollowed out. Hao Nu of the Lushui tribes proclaimed himself emperor in Chang'an, and the north bank of the Wei rose at his call. Wang Lin of Fufeng held Macang with a few thousand followers. Hao Nu sent his brother Duo against him. Yao Chang struck Wang Lin, broke his line, and drove him into Hanzhong. He captured Duo, then wheeled on Hao Nu and forced his submission.
24
乙太元十一年萇僭即皇帝位於長安，大赦，改元曰建初，國號大秦，改長安曰常安。 立妻虵氏為皇后，子興為皇太子，置百官。 自謂以火德承苻氏木行，服色如漢氏承周故事。 徙安定五千餘戶于長安。 以弟征虜緒為司隸校尉，鎮長安。
In the eleventh year of the Taiyuan era Yao Chang seized the throne at Chang'an, ordered a general amnesty, adopted the reign name Jianchu, styled his state Great Qin, and renamed the capital Chang'an to Changan ("Constant Peace"). He raised his consort of the She clan to empress, invested his son Yao Xing as crown prince, and filled out the imperial bureaucracy. He claimed the Mandate of Fire to follow Fu's Wood, and chose court colors by the Han precedent that had succeeded Zhou. He moved over five thousand Anding households into the capital. He gave his brother Yao Xu, General Who Conquers the Enemy, the metropolitan post and left him to hold Changan.
25
萇如安定，擊平涼胡金熙、鮮卑沒奕於，大破之。 遂如秦州，與苻堅秦州刺史王統相持，天水屠各、略陽羌胡應萇者二萬餘戶，統懼，乃降。 因饗將士於上邽，南安人古成詵進曰：「臣州人殷地險，俊傑如林，用武之國也。 王秦州不能收拔賢才，三分鼎足，而坐玩珠玉，以至於此。 陛下宜散秦州金帛以施六軍，旌賢表善以副鄙州之望。 」萇善之，擢為尚書郎。 拜弟碩德都督隴右諸軍事、征西將軍、秦州刺史，領護東羌校尉，鎮上邽。
Yao Chang marched to Anding, smashed the Pingliang Di leader Jin Xi and the Xianbei chief Moboyi Yu, and scattered their hosts. He pressed into Qinzhou against Fu Jian's inspector Wang Tong until Tianshui Tuge and Lüeyang Qiang and Hu—more than twenty thousand households—rallied to Yao Chang. Wang Tong, terrified, capitulated. At a victory banquet in Shanggui, Gucheng Shen of Nan'an spoke up: "My home commandery is populous, its terrain cruel, its heroes thick as trees—a land born for war." Yet Inspector Wang never gathered men fit to divide the realm three ways; he sat fondling gems until ruin found him. Scatter Qinzhou's gold and silk among the hosts, honor the able and the loyal, and you will answer what my people pray for. Yao Chang agreed and raised him to a secretariat post. He named his brother Yao Shuode commander of Longyou, General Who Conquers the West, Qinzhou inspector, and Colonel of the Eastern Qiang, with headquarters at Shanggui.
26
萇還安定，修德政，布惠化，省非急之費，以救時弊，閭閻之士有豪介之善者，皆顯異之。
Back in Anding he pursued humane rule, spread relief, slashed needless spending, and singled out commoners of stubborn integrity for honor.
27
萇復如秦州，為苻登所敗，語在《登傳》。 以其太子興鎮長安，而與登相距。 登馮翊太守蘭犢與苻師奴離貳，慕容永攻之，犢遣使請救。 萇將赴救，尚書令姚旻、左僕射尹緯等言於萇曰：「苻登近在瓦亭，陛下未宜輕舉。 」萇曰：「登遲重少決，每失時機，聞吾自行，正當廣集兵資，必不能輕軍深入。 兩月之間，足可克此三豎，吾事必矣。 」遂師次於渥源。 師奴率眾來距，大戰，敗之，盡俘其眾。 又擒蘭犢，收其士馬。 萇乃掘苻堅屍，鞭撻無數，裸剝衣裳，薦之以棘，坎土而埋之。 慕容永征西將軍王宣率眾降萇。
Yao Chang returned to Qinzhou and lost to Fu Deng, as told in Deng's memoir. He left crown prince Yao Xing to hold Changan while he faced Fu Deng in the field. Fu Deng's Fengyi prefect Lan Du had broken with Fu Shinu; Murong Yong fell upon Lan Du, who begged Yao Chang for aid. As Yao Chang prepared to march, Yao Min and Yin Wei warned him, "Fu Deng sits close at Wating; this is no moment for a rash advance." Yao Chang answered, "Fu Deng is slow and hesitant; he always misses his moment. If he hears I have taken the field he will hoard supplies and dare not strike deep with a light column." Two months will suffice to break those three upstarts; then the way is clear for me. He encamped the army at Woyuan. Fu Shinu marched to meet him; Yao Chang shattered his line and took every survivor. He seized Lan Du as well and absorbed his cavalry. Yao Chang had Fu Jian's corpse dug up, flogged it beyond counting, stripped it bare, rolled it on brambles, and heaped earth over the ruin. Wang Xuan, Murong Yong's western commander, brought his army over to Yao Chang.
28
初，關西雄傑以苻氏既終，萇雄略命世，天下之事可一旦而定。 萇既與苻登相持積年，數為登所敗，遠近咸懷去就之計，唯征虜齊難、冠軍徐洛生、輔國劉郭單、冠威彌姐婆觸、龍驤趙惡地、鎮北梁國兒等守忠不貳，並留子弟守營，供繼軍糧，身將精卒，隨萇征伐。 時諸營既多，故號萇軍為大營，大營之號自此始也。 時天大雪，萇下書深自責罰，散後宮文綺珍寶以供戎事，身食一味，妻不重彩。 將帥死王事者，加秩二等，士卒戰沒，皆有褒贈。 立太學，禮先賢之後。
Once, every champion beyond the Pass believed Fu was finished and Yao Chang's genius owned the hour—that the empire could be settled in a single dawn. Years of stalemate with Fu Deng, and repeated defeat, made allies far and near weigh betrayal—yet Qi Nan, Xu Luosheng, Liu Guodan, Mijie Potu, Zhao Wudi, Liang Guo'er, and a handful of others never wavered, left sons to guard their camps, fed Yao Chang's army, and rode with him in person. So many satellite camps sprang up that men called Yao Chang's host the Great Camp—a name born in those days. When blizzards struck he published a self-rebuke, auctioned the harem's silks and jewels for the war chest, ate a single plain dish, and forbade his consorts rich dress. Generals who died in service gained two extra ranks of pension; every common soldier killed in action received a stipend for his kin. He founded an imperial academy and endowed the heirs of ancient sages.
29
敦煌索盧曜請刺苻登，萇曰：「卿以身徇難，將為誰乎？ 」曜曰：「臣死之後，深以友人隴西辛暹仰托。 」萇遣之。 事發，為登所殺，萇以暹為騎都尉。
Suolu Yao of Dunhuang volunteered to murder Fu Deng. Yao Chang asked, "You mean to throw your life away—but for whose gain?" Suolu Yao replied, "When I am gone, let my friend Xin Xian of Longxi take my place in your service." Yao Chang let him go. The plot surfaced; Fu Deng executed him, and Yao Chang named Xin Xian a cavalry commandant.
30
登進逼安定，諸將勸萇決戰，萇曰：「與窮寇競勝，兵家之下。 吾將以計取之。 」於是留其尚書令姚旻守安定，夜襲登輜重於大界，克之。 諸將或欲因登駭亂擊之，萇曰：「登眾雖亂，怒氣猶盛，未可輕也。 」遂止。 萇以安定地狹，且逼苻登，使姚碩德鎮安定，徙安定千餘家安于陰密，遣弟征南靖鎮之。
As Fu Deng closed on Anding his generals demanded a pitched battle. Yao Chang refused. "To wrestle a desperate enemy for glory is the meanest stratagem in the canon." I mean to break him by craft. He left Yao Min to hold Anding, slipped by night against Fu Deng's baggage column at Dajie, and overran it. Some urged a follow-up blow while Fu Deng reeled. Yao Chang said no: "His ranks may waver, but their rage still runs high—do not despise them." He held his hand. Anding was cramped under Fu Deng's shadow, so Yao Chang stationed Yao Shuode there, moved a thousand Anding families into Yinmi, and sent his brother Yao Jing, General Who Conquers the South, to hold the new line.
31
立社稷于長安。 百姓年七十有德行者，拜為中大夫，歲賜牛酒。
He dedicated the state altars at Changan. Commoners past seventy known for virtue were named grand counselors of the palace and given oxen and wine each year.
32
尹緯、姚晃謂古成詵曰：「苻登窮寇，歷年未滅，奸雄鴟峙，所在糾扇，夷夏皆貳，將若之何？ 」詵曰：「主上權略無方，信賞必罰，賢能之士，咸懷樂推，豈慮大業不成，氐賊不滅乎！ 」緯曰：「登窮寇未滅，奸雄所在扇合，吾等寧無懼乎？ 」詵曰：「三秦天府之國，主上十分已有其八。 今所在可慮者，苻登、楊定、雷惡地耳，自餘瑣瑣，焉足論哉！ 然惡地地狹眾寡，不足為憂。 苻登藉烏合犬羊，偷存假息，料其智勇，非至尊之匹。 霸王之起，必有驅除，然後克定大業。 昔漢、魏之興也，皆十有餘年，乃能一同於海內，五六年間未為久也。 主上神略內明，英武外發，可謂無敵於天下耳，取登有餘力。 願布德行仁，招賢納士，厲兵秣馬，以候天機。 如其鴻業不成者，詵請腰斬以謝明公。 」緯言之於萇，萇大悅，賜詵爵關內侯。
Yin Wei and Yao Huang asked Gucheng Shen, "Fu Deng still survives after all these years; every strongman preens like a hawk and fans revolt; tribesmen and Chinese alike waver—what now?" Gucheng Shen answered, "Our lord's designs know no limit; he rewards faith and punishes without mercy; every able man gladly lifts him up. Why doubt the great work—or that the Di bandit will fall?" Yin Wei pressed him: "While Fu Deng lives and rogues flock everywhere, should we not be afraid?" Gucheng Shen said, "The three Qin are heaven's own granary; our lord already holds eight parts in ten." Only Fu Deng, Yang Ding, and Lei Wudi still matter; the rest are gnats unworthy of talk. Lei Wudi holds thin ground and few spears—hardly worth a second thought. Fu Deng clings to a rabble no better than sheep, stealing breath on borrowed time; set his mind and mettle against yours, and he cannot rival the throne. The rise of every conqueror clears the field of rivals; only then can the great work stand complete. The Han and the Wei each needed more than a decade to knit the realm into one. A span of five or six years is scarcely long to wait. Our lord reads the field in silence and strikes with open might; none under Heaven can stand before him. Fu Deng would fall and still leave force in reserve. Spread humanity, gather the able, hone the blades, rest the steeds, and watch for the hour fate tilts your way. If that high design fails, I, Shen, beg to be halved at the waist to answer to you. Wei relayed it to Yao Chang, who was delighted and enfeoffed Shen as a marquis within the passes.
33
雷惡地率眾降萇，拜為鎮東將軍。 魏褐飛自稱大將軍、沖天王，率氐胡數萬人攻安北姚當城於杏城，雷惡地應之，攻鎮東姚漢得于李潤。 萇議將討之，群臣咸曰：「陛下不憂六十里苻登，乃憂六百里褐飛？ 」萇曰：「登非可卒殄，吾城亦非登所能卒圖。 惡地多智，非常人也。 南引褐飛，東結董成，甘言美說以成奸謀，若得杏城、李潤，惡地據之，控制遠近，相為羽翼，長安東北非復吾有。 」於是潛軍赴之。 萇時眾不滿二千，褐飛、惡地眾至數萬，氐胡赴之者首尾不絕。 萇每見一軍至，輒有喜色。 群下怪而問之，萇曰：「今同惡相濟，皆來會集，吾得乘勝席捲，一舉而覆其巢穴，東北無復餘也。 」褐飛等以萇兵少，盡眾來攻。 萇固壘不戰，示之以弱，潛遣子崇率騎數百，出其不意，以乘其後。 褐飛兵擾亂，萇遣鎮遠王超、平遠譚亮率步騎擊之，褐飛眾大潰，斬褐飛及首級萬餘。 惡地請降，萇待之如初。 惡地每謂人曰：「吾自言智勇所施，足為一時之傑。 校數諸雄，如吾之徒，皆應跨據一方，獸嘯千里。 遇姚公智力摧屈，是吾分也。 」惡地猛毅清肅，不可幹以非義，嶺北諸豪皆敬憚之。
Lei Ede brought his troops over to Yao Chang, who named him General Who Guards the East. The rebel Wei Hefei declared himself Grand General and King Who Charges Heaven, then drove tens of thousands of Di and Hu against Yao Dangcheng at Xingcheng. Lei Ede joined the move and hit Yao Hande at Lirun. Yao Chang weighed a punitive march. His ministers said: "You lose no sleep over Fu Deng on your doorstep, but lose it over Hefei far down the road?" " Yao Chang replied: "Fu Deng will not vanish overnight, and my walls will not fall to him overnight either." Lei Ede is cunning—no common soldier of fortune. He pulls Hefei from the south, ropes in Dong Cheng from the east, and sweet-talks his way through treason. Seize Xingcheng and Lirun and Lei Ede commands the approaches; wings locked, the northeast of Chang'an slips from our grasp. So he stole a march with hidden troops. Yao Chang had fewer than two thousand men; Hefei and Lei Ede mustered tens of thousands, with Di and Hu pouring in without end. Each time fresh rebels appeared, Yao Chang looked glad. His officers wondered aloud. Yao Chang said: "They flock together—traitors feeding traitors. I can sweep them in one rush, tear out the nest, and clear the whole northeast." Thinking Yao Chang weak, Hefei threw every man forward. Yao Chang hugged his walls and feigned frailty, while his son Chong looped behind with a few hundred riders and hit them where they did not look. Hefei's lines crumbled. Yao Chang sent Wang Chao and Tan Liang with mixed arms; the rebel army shattered. Hefei lost his head, and the tally passed ten thousand. Lei Ede sued for peace, and Yao Chang welcomed him as if nothing had changed. Lei Ede used to say: "Where I bring mind and nerve, I am fit to stand among the heroes of the hour." Stack me against the lords of the age: my kind should hold a quarter of the map and make the wilds echo for a thousand li. Then I met Lord Yao, and mind and might alike were broken—that was simply my lot. Lei Ede was grim, upright, and austere—no appeal to wrong could sway him. North of the passes every strongman respected and dreaded him.
34
萇命其將當城于營處一柵孔中蒔樹一根，以旌戰功。 歲餘，問之，城曰：「營所至小，已廣之矣。 」萇曰：「少來鬥戰無如此快，以千六百人破三萬眾，國之事業，由此克舉。 小乃為奇，大何足貴！」
Yao Chang told General Yao Dangcheng to set a single sapling in a hole in the stockade where they had camped, a living marker of the fight. A year later he inquired. Dangcheng said: "The plot was tiny—I have widened it." " Yao Chang said: "I have never had a quicker fight. Sixteen hundred broke thirty thousand; the kingdom's fate turned on that stroke." Small made the wonder—size would have cheapened it!"
35
貳城胡曹寅、王達獻馬三千匹。 以寅為鎮北將軍、并州刺史，達鎮遠將軍、金城太守。
Cao Yin and Wang Da, Hu leaders of Ercheng, brought three thousand horses as tribute. Cao Yin became General Who Guards the North and inspector of Bingzhou; Wang Da became General Who Distantly Pacifies and prefect of Jincheng.
36
萇性簡率，群下有過，或面加罵辱。 太常權翼言於萇曰：「陛下弘達自任，不修小節，駕馭群雄，苞羅俊異，棄嫌錄善，有高祖之量。 然輕慢之風，所宜除也。 」萇曰：「吾之性也。 吾於舜之美，未有片焉； 漢祖之短，已收其一。 若不聞讜言，安知過也！」
Yao Chang was rough and direct; when a follower slipped, he might curse him to his face. Quan Yi, Minister of Ceremonies, told Yao Chang: "You are broad enough to trust your own judgment, above petty nicety; you harness warlords, gather odd talents, forgive old grudges and honor merit—you have the span of Han Gaozu. Still, the habit of contempt is a thing you should shed. " Yao Chang answered: "That is simply how I am." I own no shred of Shun's grace; but I have already pocketed one of the Han founder's vices. Without blunt advice, how would I ever recognize my own errors?"
37
南羌竇鴦率戶五千來降，拜安西將軍。
Dou Yang of the southern Qiang came in with five thousand households; Yao Chang named him General Who Pacifies the West.
38
萇下書，有復私仇者，皆誅之。 將吏亡滅者，各隨所親以立後，振給長育之。
Yao Chang proclaimed: private vengeance would cost a man's life. Fallen officers and clerks left heirs named by kin, and the state fed and reared them.
39
鎮東苟曜據逆萬堡，密引苻登。 萇與登戰，敗于馬頭原，收眾復戰。 姚碩德謂諸將曰：「上慎于輕戰，每欲以計取之。 今戰既失利，而更逼賊者，必有由也。 」萇聞而謂碩德曰：「登用兵遲緩，不識虛實，今輕兵直進，逕據吾東，必苟曜豎子與之連結也。 事久變成，其禍難測。 所以速戰者，欲使豎子謀之未就，好之未深，散敗其事耳。 」進戰，大敗之，登退屯於郿。 登將金槌以新平降萇，萇輕將數百騎入槌營。 群下諫之，萇曰：「槌既去苻登，復欲圖我，將安所歸！ 且懷德初附，推款委質，吾復以不信待之，何以禦物乎！ 」群氐果有異謀，槌不從而止。
Gou Yao, General Who Guards the East, held Niwang Fort and secretly opened the door to Fu Deng. Yao Chang met Fu Deng, lost on Matou Plain, regrouped, and struck again. Yao Shuo De told his captains: "Our lord hates careless fighting; he prefers to win by craft." We have just tasted defeat, yet he drives harder at the foe—something drives him. " Yao Chang heard and said to Shuo De: "Fu Deng is slow and blind to ruse; light horse now spear our eastern flank—that pup Gou Yao has tied his fate to Deng's." Let the knot tighten and the harm becomes unreadable. I hit fast so the whelp's scheme never ripens, his commitment never deepens—I break the thing apart. He pressed the attack, shattered them, and Fu Deng fell back to entrench at Mei. Fu Deng's general Jin Chui yielded Xinping; Yao Chang rode in with only a few hundred horse to Chui's lines. His men protested. Yao Chang said: "If Jin Chui has quit Fu Deng only to knife me, where would he run?" A new friend offers open heart and hostage; answer with suspicion and nothing in the realm will hold!" The Di clique did plot treason, yet Chui refused—and the plot died.
40
萇如陰密攻登，敕其太子興曰：「苟曜好奸變，將為國害，聞吾還北，必來見汝，汝便執之。 」苟曜果見興于長安，興遣尹緯讓而誅之。
Yao Chang marched on Yinmi against Fu Deng and told the crown prince Xing: "Gou Yao thrives on double dealing and will poison the realm. When he learns I have swung north he will seek you out—take him then." Gou Yao called on Yao Xing in Chang'an; Xing sent Yin Wei to shame him, then executed him.
41
萇大敗登于安定東，置酒高會，諸將咸曰：「若值魏武王，不令此賊至今，陛下將牢太過耳。 」萇笑曰：「吾不如亡兄有四：身長八尺五寸，臂垂過膝，人望而畏之，一也； 當十萬之眾，與天下爭衡，望麾而進，前無橫陣，二也； 溫古知今，講論道藝，駕馭英雄，收羅雋異，三也； 董率大眾，履險若夷，上下咸允，人盡死力，四也。 所以得建立功業，策任群賢者，正望算略中一片耳。 」群臣咸稱萬歲。
East of Anding Yao Chang crushed Fu Deng, then feasted his captains. They said: "Under Wei Wuwang this cur would never have lived so long; you keep too tight a hand, Majesty." " Yao Chang smiled: "I lack four gifts my dead brother had: eight chi five of height, arms past the knee—men quailed at the sight; one; before a hundred thousand spears he matched the world, flag dipped and line moved—no shield could hold him; two; he knew old texts and the hour, discoursed on craft and principle, mastered captains and netted rare men; three; he marched great armies through razor country as on a road, won every heart, and drew the last breath from every soldier; four. If I raised an empire and set able men to work, it was because I still had one thin edge of cunning left." The court cried "May you live ten thousand years!"
42
萇下書令留台諸鎮各置學官，勿有所廢，考試優劣，隨才擢敘。 苻登驃騎將軍沒奕于率戶六千降，拜使持節、車騎將軍、高平公。
Yao Chang ordered every rear command to keep schools and masters, test for merit, and advance men by gift. Moyiyu, Fu Deng's chief of horse, brought six thousand households over; Yao Chang gave him the staff, the chariots-and-horse command, and the ducal title of Gaoping.
43
萇寢疾，遣姚碩德鎮李潤，尹緯守長安，召其太子興詣行營。 征南姚方成言於興曰：「今寇賊未滅，上復寢疾，王統、苻胤等皆有部曲，終為人害，宜盡除之。 」興於是誅苻胤、王統、王廣、徐成、毛盛，乃赴召。 興至，萇怒曰：「王統兄弟是吾州裏，無他遠志，徐成等昔在秦朝，並為名將。 天下小定，吾方任之，奈何輒便誅害，令人喪氣！」
Yao Chang fell ill; he posted Yao Shuo De at Lirun, left Yin Wei in Chang'an, and called Crown Prince Xing to camp. Yao Fangcheng, who campaigned south, warned Xing: "Rebels still live; your father sickens again; Wang Tong and Fu Yin keep private hosts—they will turn venomous. Cut them clean." Xing killed Fu Yin, Wang Tong, Wang Guang, Xu Cheng, and Mao Sheng, then rode to his father. When Xing came, Yao Chang blazed: "Wang Tong and his brothers are home-country men with no larger design. Xu Cheng and his fellows were named generals of Qin. Peace is near and I meant to use them—why cut them down and drain the courage from every officer?"
44
萇下書，兵吏從征伐，戶在大營者，世世復其家，無所豫。
Yao Chang decreed perpetual exemption for families of soldiers and scribes who had marched with the host and were tallied to the grand camp—no corvee would touch their line.
45
苻登與竇沖相持，萇議擊之，尹緯言於萇曰：「太子純厚之稱，著于遐邇，將領英略，未為遠近所知。 宜遣太子親行，可以漸廣威武，防窺窬之原。 」萇從之，戎興曰：「賊徒知汝轉近，必相驅入堡，聚而掩之，無不克矣。 」比至胡空堡，沖圍自解。 登聞興向胡空堡，引還，興因襲平涼，大獲而歸，咸如萇策。 使興還鎮長安。
Fu Deng and Dou Chong were deadlocked; Yao Chang thought to strike. Yin Wei said: "The crown prince is famed for honest depth, but his steel as a commander is still a secret beyond the court. Send him in person—let awe spread step by step and choke off any thought of usurpation. " Yao Chang assented and briefed Xing: "Once they know you come, they will herd into their forts—ring and swallow them, and none escape." By the time he reached Hukong Fort, Dou Chong's ring had fallen away on its own. Fu Deng heard Xing aimed at Hukong Fort and pulled back; Xing swept Pingliang for a rich prize and rode home—just as Yao Chang had drawn it. He sent Xing back to hold Chang'an.
46
萇下書除妖謗之言及赦前奸穢，有相劾舉者，皆以其罪罪之。
Yao Chang voided charges of witch-slander and old filth pardoned by amnesty—any accuser who dredged them up wore the guilt he tried to pin.
47
晉平遠將軍、護氐校尉楊佛嵩率胡蜀三千餘戶降于萇，晉將楊佺期、趙睦追之。 遣姚崇赴救，大敗晉師，斬趙睦。 以佛嵩為鎮東將軍。
Yang Fosong, Jin's far pacifier and Di warden, led three thousand-odd Hu and Ba households to Yao Chang; Yang Quanqi and Zhao Mu of Jin gave chase. Yao Chang sent Yao Chong; Jin's host shattered and Zhao Mu lost his head. Yang Fosong became General Who Guards the East.
48
萇如長安，至於新支堡，疾篤，輿疾而進。 夢苻堅將天官使者、鬼兵數百突入營中，萇懼，走入宮，宮人迎萇刺鬼，誤中萇陰，鬼相謂曰：「正中死處。 」拔矛，出血石餘。 寤而驚悸，遂患陰腫，醫刺之，出血如夢。 萇遂狂言，或稱「臣萇，殺陛下者兄襄，非臣之罪，願不枉臣。 」至長安，召太尉姚旻、尚書左僕射尹緯、右僕射姚晃、尚書狄伯支等入，受遺輔政。 萇謂興曰：「有毀此諸人者，慎勿受之。 汝撫骨肉以仁，接大臣以禮，待物以信，遇黔首以恩，四者既備，吾無憂矣。 」乙太元十八年死，時年六十四，在位八年。 偽諡武昭皇帝，廟號太祖，墓稱原陵。
Yao Chang set out for Chang'an; at Xinzhai Fort his sickness bit deep, and they bore him onward on a litter. He dreamed Fu Jian at the head of heaven's clerks and hundreds of wraith-troops storming the camp. Yao Chang ran to the inner hall; women struck at spirits and gashed his groin by mistake. The shades murmured: "Dead center. They pulled the lance free; blood ran more than a peck. He woke shaking; the flesh swelled; the leech lanced it and the flood matched the vision. Then Yao Chang raved, crying: "Lord, I am Chang—your killer was my brother Xiang, not I; do not call it my guilt. In Chang'an he called Yao Min, Yin Wei, Yao Huang, Di Bozhi, and the rest to take the deathbed regency. He told Xing: "Whoever blackens these men—do not lend your ear. Rule kin with kindness, greet high ministers with rite, handle the realm with trust, meet the people with mercy—set those four and I rest easy." He died in the eighteenth year of the Taiyuan era, aged sixty-four, after eight years on the throne. They gave him the posthumous name Martial Luminous Emperor, the temple title Grand Founder, and buried him at the tomb called Yuanling.
