1
姚弋仲姚襄姚萇
Yao Yizhong; Yao Xiang; Yao Chang.
2
姚弋仲，南安赤亭羌人也。 其先有虞氏之苗裔。 禹封舜少子於西戎，世為羌酋。 其後燒當雄於洮、罕之間，七世孫填虞，漢中元末寇擾西州，為楊虛侯馬武所敗，徙出塞。 虞九世孫遷那率種人內附，漢朝嘉之，假冠軍將軍、西羌校尉、歸順王，處之于南安之赤亭。 那玄孫柯回為魏鎮西將軍、綏戎校尉、西羌都督。 回生弋仲，少英毅，不營產業，唯以收恤為務，眾皆畏而親之。 永嘉之亂，東徙榆眉，戎夏繈負隨之者數萬，自稱護西羌校尉、雍州刺史、扶風公。
Yao Yizhong was a Qiang from Chiting in Nan'an commandery. His line traced back to the house of Youyu. Yu enfeoffed Shun’s youngest son among the western tribes, and his descendants became hereditary Qiang chiefs. Later the Shaodang branch dominated the Tao and Han rivers; seven generations on, Dian Yu raided Liangzhou in the late Eastern Han until Ma Wu drove the clan beyond the frontier. Nine generations later Qianna led his people to submit; the Han court named him champion general, colonel of the western Qiang, king of submission, and settled them at Chiting in Nan'an. His descendant Kehui served Wei as general who guards the west, colonel who pacifies the Rong, and overseer of the western Qiang. Kehui’s son Yao Yizhong was bold in youth, cared nothing for property, and devoted himself to succoring his people, who feared and loved him. When turmoil struck in the Yongjia era he moved east to Yumei; tens of thousands of tribesmen and Chinese followed, and he styled himself colonel protecting the western Qiang, Yongzhou inspector, and Duke of Fufeng.
3
劉曜之平陳安也，以弋仲為平西將軍，封平襄公，邑之於隴上。 及石季龍克上邽，弋仲說之曰：「明公握兵十萬，功高一時，正是行權立策之日。 隴上多豪，秦風猛勁，道隆後服，道洿先叛，宜徙隴上豪強，虛其心腹，以實畿甸。 」季龍納之，啟勒以弋仲行安西將軍、六夷左都督。 後晉豫州刺史祖約奔於勒，勒禮待之，弋仲上疏曰：「祖約殘賊晉朝，逼殺太后，不忠於主，而陛下寵之，臣恐奸亂之萌，此其始矣。 」勒善之，後竟誅約。
After Liu Yao crushed Chen An he named Yao Yizhong general who pacifies the west and Duke of Pingxiang with a fief on the Long plateau. After Shi Hu took Shanggui, Yao Yizhong urged him, “You command a hundred thousand soldiers and stand supreme—now is the moment to seize initiative.” Longxi teems with strongmen whose Qin temperament turns volatile—relocate them from your heartland and settle them around the capital to strengthen the core.” ” Shi Hu agreed and had Shi Le appoint Yao Yizhong acting general who pacifies the west and left supervisor of the six tribes. When Jin’s Yu governor Zu Yue fled to Shi Le, Yao Yizhong memorialized: “Zu Yue ravaged the court and slew the empress dowager—why honor him and sow sedition?” ” Shi Le agreed and later executed Zu Yue.
4
勒既死，季龍執權，思弋仲之言，遂徙秦、雍豪傑于關東。 弋仲率部眾數萬遷於清河，拜奮武將軍、西羌大都督，封襄平縣公。 及季龍廢石弘自立，弋仲稱疾不賀。 季龍累召之，乃赴，正色謂季龍曰：「奈何把臂受託而反奪之乎！ 」季龍憚其強正而不之責。 遷持節、十郡六夷大都督、冠軍大將軍。 性清儉鯁直，不修威儀，屢獻讜言，無所回避，季龍甚重之。 朝之大議，靡不參決，公卿亦憚而推下之。 武城左尉，季龍寵姬之弟也，曾擾其部，弋仲執尉，數以迫脅之狀，命左右斬之。 尉叩頭流血，左右諫，乃止。 其剛直不回，皆此類也。
After Shi Le died Shi Hu recalled Yao Yizhong’s counsel and resettled Qin and Yong magnates east of the passes. Yao Yizhong led tens of thousands to Qinghe and received the titles general who rouses might, grand supervisor of the western Qiang, and Duke of Xiangping. When Shi Hu deposed Shi Hong and seized the throne Yao Yizhong feigned illness and skipped the court congratulation. Shi Hu summoned him repeatedly; Yao Yizhong came and demanded, “How can you clasp arms in trust and then steal the throne?” ” Shi Hu respected his blunt integrity and did not punish him. He was promoted to staff-bearing grand supervisor over ten commanderies and six tribes and champion grand general. Frugal and blunt, heedless of pomp, he spoke plainly without flinching, and Shi Hu valued him deeply. He sat in on every great policy debate, and even senior ministers deferred to him. The left captain of Wucheng, brother of Shi Hu’s favorite concubine, molested Yao Yizhong’s men; Yizhong seized him, listed his offenses, and had him decapitated. The captain begged until blood ran; attendants pleaded and Yao Yizhong stayed the execution. Such unbending integrity marked every incident.
5
季龍末，梁犢敗李農于滎陽，季龍大懼，馳召弋仲。 弋仲率其部眾八千餘人屯於南郊，輕騎至鄴。 時季龍病，不時見弋仲，引入領軍省，賜其所食之食。 弋仲怒不食，曰：「召我擊賊，豈來覓食邪！ 我不知上存亡，若一見，雖死無恨。 」左右言之，乃引見。 弋仲數季龍曰：「兒死來愁邪？ 乃至於疾！ 兒小時不能使好人輔相，至令相殺。 兒自有過，責其下人太甚，故反耳。 汝病久，所立兒小，若不差，天下必亂。 當宜憂此，不煩憂賊也。 犢等因思歸之心，共為奸盜，所行殘賊，此成擒耳。 老羌請效死前鋒，使一舉而了。 」弋仲性狷直，俗無尊卑皆汝之，季龍恕而不責，於坐授使持節、侍中、征西大將軍，賜以鎧馬。 弋仲曰：「汝看老羌堪破賊以不？ 」於是貫鉀跨馬於庭中，策馬南馳，不辭而出，遂滅梁犢。 以功加劍履上殿，入朝不趨，進封西平郡公。
Late in Shi Hu’s reign Liang Du routed Li Nong at Xingyang; terrified, Shi Hu summoned Yao Yizhong in haste. Yao Yizhong camped eight thousand men south of Ye and rode lightly into the city. Shi Hu was ill and delayed the audience, led him to the bureau of the commander of the guard, and offered him dinner. Yao Yizhong refused food and snapped, “You called me to fight rebels—not to lunch!” I know not whether our lord lives—grant one audience and I die content.” ” His attendants relayed the plea and Shi Hu received him. Yao Yizhong scolded Shi Hu: “Do you sulk because your sons died?” Has grief brought you to this sickbed?” You never gave your boys worthy tutors, so they turned blades on each other.” Your sons erred, yet you scourged their followers until revolt flared.” You have lingered ill while the heir is a child—if you die the realm will boil.” Worry that, not Liang Du’s raiders.” They are hungry bandits riding nostalgia—I will cage them in one blow.” Let this old Qiang die in your van and finish this.” ” Blunt to a fault, Yao Yizhong addressed everyone as “you”; Shi Hu laughed it off and at table invested him as staff-bearing palace attendant and general who conquers the west with armored horse. He asked, “Does this old Qiang look fit to break your rebels?” ” He armored up, vaulted his horse in the courtyard, galloped south without leave, and destroyed Liang Du. For merit he earned sword-and-shoes audience, leisure pace at court, and promotion to Duke of Xiping.
6
冉閔之亂，弋仲率眾討閔，次於混橋。 石祗僭號于襄國，以弋仲為右丞相，待以殊禮。 祗與閔相攻，弋仲遣其子襄救祗，戒襄曰：「汝才十倍于閔，若不梟擒，不須復見我也。 」襄擊閔于常盧澤，大破之而歸。 弋仲怒襄之不擒閔也，杖之一百。
During Ran Min’s rising Yao Yizhong marched against him and camped at Hun Bridge. Shi Zhi declared himself emperor at Xiangguo, named Yao Yizhong right chancellor, and honored him lavishly. When Shi Zhi and Ran Min clashed Yao Yizhong sent Yao Xiang to aid Shi Zhi with the vow: “You outmatch Ran Min tenfold—bring his head or do not return.” ” Yao Xiang smashed Ran Min at Changlu Marsh but withdrew without capturing him. Yao Yizhong flogged his son a hundred strokes for failing to seize Ran Min.
7
弋仲部曲馬何羅博學有文才，張豺之輔石世也，背弋仲歸豺，豺以為尚書郎。 豺敗，復歸，咸勸殺之。 弋仲曰：「今正是招才納奇之日，當收其力用，不足害也。 」以為參軍。 其寬恕如此。
Ma Heluo, Yao Yizhong’s follower, was learned and literary; when Zhang Chai sponsored Shi Shi he defected and Zhang made him a masters-of-writing gentleman. After Zhang Chai fell Ma returned; everyone urged execution. Yao Yizhong said, “These are times to hoard talent—use his gifts; killing gains nothing.” ” He named Ma army adjutant. Such was his magnanimity.
8
弋仲有子四十二人，常戒諸子曰：「吾本以晉室大亂，石氏待吾厚，故欲討其賊臣以報其德。 今石氏已滅，中原無主，自古以來未有戎狄作天子者。 我死，汝便歸晉，當竭盡臣節，無為不義之事。 」乃遣使請降。 永和七年，拜弋仲使持節、六夷大都督、都督江、淮諸軍事、車騎大將軍、儀同三司、大單于，封高陵郡公。 八年，卒，時年七十三。
Yao Yizhong had forty-two sons and often warned them: “When Jin collapsed the Shi treated us well, so I meant to punish their traitors in return.” Shi is gone and the heartland has no ruler—never has a tribesman sat as Son of Heaven.” When I die submit to Jin and serve with full loyalty—commit no injustice.” ” He sent envoys to offer submission. In Yonghe seven (351) he received staff authority as grand supervisor of six tribes and Jiang-Huai armies, grand general of chariots and cavalry with three-duke honors, great chanyu, and Duke of Gaoling. He died the next year at seventy-three.
9
子襄之入關也，為苻生所敗，弋仲之柩為生所得，生以王禮葬之于天水冀縣。 萇僭位，追諡曰景元皇帝，廟號始祖，墓曰高陵，置園邑五百家。
When Yao Xiang invaded Guanzhong Fu Sheng defeated him and seized Yao Yizhong’s coffin, burying it with princely honors at Ji in Tianshui. When Yao Chang took the throne he posthumously honored him as Emperor Jingyuan, temple name First Ancestor, tomb Gaoling, with five hundred households to tend it.
10
襄字景國，弋仲之第五子也。 年十七，身長八尺五寸，臂垂過膝，雄武多才藝，明察善撫納，士眾愛敬之，咸請為嗣。 弋仲弗許，百姓固請者日有千數，乃授之以兵。 石祗僭號，以襄為使持節、驃騎將軍、護烏丸校尉、豫州刺史、新昌公。 晉遣使拜襄持節、平北將軍、并州刺史、即丘縣公。
Yao Xiang, courtesy name Jingguo, was Yao Yizhong’s fifth son. At seventeen he stood eight chi five inches tall with arms past his knees, heroic and skilled, perceptive and winning loyalty; the army adored him and pressed him as heir. Yao Yizhong refused until thousands petitioned daily, then gave him command. When Shi Zhi claimed the throne he named Yao Xiang staff-bearing general of agile cavalry, colonel protecting the Wuhuan, Yu inspector, and Duke of Xinchang. Jin invested him as general who pacifies the north, Bingzhou inspector, and Duke of Jiuqiu.
11
弋仲死，襄秘不發喪，率戶六萬南攻陽平、元城、發幹，皆破之，殺掠三千餘家，屯於碻磝津。 以太原王亮為長史，天水尹赤為司馬，略陽伏子成為左部帥，南安斂岐為右部帥，略陽黑那為前部帥，強白為後部帥，太原薛贊、略陽王權翼為參軍。 南至滎陽，始發喪行服。 與高昌、李曆戰于麻田，馬中流矢死，賴其弟萇以免。 晉處襄於譙城，遣五弟為任，單騎度淮，見豫州刺史謝尚于壽春。 尚命去仗衛，幅巾以待之，一面交款，便若平生。
When Yao Yizhong died Yao Xiang hid the news, marched sixty thousand households south to storm Yangping, Yuancheng, and Facan, sacked three thousand families, and camped at Qiaoao ford. He named Wang Liang chief clerk, Yin Chi major, Fuzicheng and Lianqi left and right division leaders, Heina and Qiangbai van and rear leaders, and Xue Zan and Wang Quanyi adjutants. South of Xingyang he announced his father’s death and donned mourning. Against Gaochang and Li Li at Matian his horse was shot; his brother Yao Chang saved him. Jin quartered him at Qiao with five brothers as hostages; he crossed the Huai alone to meet Yu governor Xie Shang at Shouchun. Xie Shang dismissed guards and met him in casual dress; one meeting bred trust like old friends.
12
襄少有高名，雄武冠世，好學博通，雅善談論，英濟之稱著于南夏。 中軍將軍、揚州刺史殷浩憚其威名，乃因襄諸弟，頻遣刺客殺襄，刺客皆推誠告實，襄待之若舊。 浩潛遣將軍魏憬率五千餘人襲襄，襄乃斬憬而並其眾。 浩愈惡之，乃使將軍劉啟守譙，遷襄于梁國蠡台，表授梁國內史。 襄遣權翼詣浩，浩曰：「姚平北每舉動自由，豈所望也。 」翼曰：「將軍輕納奸言，自生疑貳，愚謂猜嫌之由，不在於彼。 」浩曰：「姚君縱放小人，盜竊吾馬，王臣之體固若是乎？ 」翼曰：「將軍謂姚平北以威武自強，終為難保，校兵練眾，將懲不恪，取馬者欲以自衛耳。 」浩曰：「何至是也。 」浩遣謝萬討襄，襄逆擊破之。 浩甚怒，會聞關中有變，浩率眾北伐，襄乃要擊浩于山桑，大敗之，斬獲萬計，收其資仗。 使兄益守山桑壘，復如淮南。 浩遣劉啟、王彬之伐山桑，襄自淮南擊滅之，鼓行濟淮，屯於盱眙，招掠流人，眾至七萬，分置守宰，勸課農桑，遣使建鄴，罪狀殷浩，並自陳謝。
Renowned young for valor and arms, learned and eloquent, Yao Xiang was hailed across the south as a champion. Yin Hao feared him and sent killers through Yao Xiang’s brothers; each assassin confessed and Yao Xiang welcomed them like kin. Yin Hao secretly sent Wei Jing with five thousand men; Yao Xiang slew Wei Jing and absorbed his troops. Yin Hao then garrisoned Liu Qi at Qiao, moved Yao Xiang to Litai in Liang, and named him interior steward of Liang. Wang Quanyi called on Yin Hao, who said, “General Yao who pacifies the north acts as he pleases—hardly what we wished.” ” Quanyi replied, “You credit slander and breed mistrust—the fault lies not with Yao Xiang.” ” Yin Hao said, “He lets underlings steal my horses—is that ministerial duty?” ” Quanyi answered, “You fear his army and drill your own; he took horses only to guard himself.” ” Yin Hao said, “Surely not.” ” Yin Hao sent Xie Wan; Yao Xiang routed him. Furious, Yin Hao marched north on Guanzhong news; Yao Xiang ambushed him at Shansang, slaughtered thousands, and seized baggage and arms. He left his brother Yi at Shansang and returned to Huainan. When Yin Hao sent Liu Qi and Wang Binzhi, Yao Xiang destroyed them from Huainan, crossed the Huai to Xuyi with seventy thousand followers, organized officials, promoted farming, and wrote to Jianye denouncing Yin Hao while apologizing for himself.
13
流人郭斁等千餘人執晉堂邑內史劉仕降於襄，朝延大震，以吏部尚書周閔為中軍將軍，緣江備守。 襄將佐部眾皆北人，咸勸襄北還。 襄方軌北引，自稱大將軍、大單于，進攻外黃，為晉邊將所敗。 襄收散卒而勤撫恤之，於是復振。 乃據許昌，將如河東以圖關右，自許遂攻洛陽，逾月不克。 其長史王亮諫襄曰：「公英略蓋天下，士眾思效力命，不可損威勞眾，守此孤城。 宜還河北，以弘遠略。 」襄曰：「洛陽雖小，山河四塞之固，亦是用武之地。 吾欲先據洛陽，然後開建大業。 」俄而亮卒，襄哭之甚慟，曰：「天將不欲成吾事乎？ 王亮舍我去也！」
Over a thousand refugees led by Guo Yi seized Liu Shi and yielded to Yao Xiang; the court trembled and named Zhou Min central army general to guard the Yangzi. His northern officers urged him to march north. He drove north as grand general and great chanyu, attacked Waihuang, and lost to Jin frontier forces. He rallied the fugitives with care and restored morale. He held Xuchang, planned Hedong and the northwest, then struck Luoyang from Xu without taking it in a month. Wang Liang urged, “Your genius fills the realm and men yearn to serve—do not waste strength on this lonely city.” Return north of the Yellow River and pursue the larger design.” ” Yao Xiang replied, “Luoyang is small yet fortified by mountains and rivers—a worthy base.” I mean to take Luoyang first, then raise the great enterprise.” ” When Wang Liang died soon after, Yao Xiang wailed, “Does heaven forbid my success?” Wang Liang has left me!”
14
晉征西大將軍桓溫自江陵伐襄，戰于伊水北，為溫所敗，率麾下數千騎奔於北山。 其夜，百姓棄妻子隨襄者五千餘人，屯據陽鄉，赴者又四千餘戶。 襄前後敗喪數矣，眾知襄所在，輒扶老攜幼賓士而赴之。 時或傳襄創重不濟，溫軍所得士女莫不北望揮涕。 其得物情如此。 先是，弘農楊亮歸襄，襄待以客禮。 後奔桓溫，溫問襄於亮，亮曰：「神明器宇，孫策之儔，而雄武過之。 」其見重如是。
Huan Wen marched from Jianglang, defeated Yao Xiang north of the Yi, who fled into the northern hills with thousands of horse. That night over five thousand civilians forsook families to follow him at Yangxiang; four thousand more households joined. Though often beaten, wherever Yao Xiang went throngs carried elders and children to join him. Rumors flew that Yao Xiang's wounds were mortal; every captive Huan Wen's army had seized turned north and shed tears. Such was the devotion he commanded among the people. Earlier, Yang Liang of Hongnong had come over to Yao Xiang, who received him with full guest honors. Liang later crossed to Huan Wen, who pressed him about Yao Xiang. Liang answered: "He carries himself like a god among men—Sun Ce's equal—and exceeds him in bold warcraft. Such was the esteem in which others held him.
15
萇字景茂，弋仲第二十四子也。 少聰哲，多權略，廓落任率，不修行業，諸兄皆奇之。 隨襄征伐，每參大謀。 襄之寇洛陽也，夢萇服袞衣，升御坐，諸酋長皆侍立，旦謂將佐曰：「吾夢如此，此兒志度不恆，或能大起吾族。 」襄之敗于麻田也，馬中流矢死，萇下馬以授襄，襄曰：「汝何以自免？ 」萇曰：「但令兄濟，豎子安敢害萇！ 」會救至，俱免。
Yao Chang, courtesy name Jingmao, was Yao Yizhong's twenty-fourth son. Clever and resourceful from boyhood, openhanded and blunt, he spurned petty propriety, and his brothers wondered at him. He rode with Yao Xiang on campaign and shared in each great decision. During Yao Xiang's thrust on Luoyang he dreamed Yao Chang robed as emperor on the throne while tribal headmen stood attendance. At dawn he told his staff: "That dream tells me this son's aims are no common thing—maybe he will lift our house high. When Yao Xiang lost at Matian, his horse fell to an arrow; Yao Chang sprang down and handed him his own. Xiang asked, "How will you escape? Yao Chang replied: "Once you are safe, what fool would dare touch me! Help rode up and both men survived.
16
及襄死，萇率諸弟降于苻生。 苻堅以萇為揚武將軍。 曆左衛將軍，隴東、汲郡、河東、武都、武威、巴西、扶風太守，甯、幽、兗三州刺史，復為揚武將軍，步兵校尉，封益都侯。 為堅將，累有大功。
After Yao Xiang fell, Yao Chang brought his brothers to submit to Fu Sheng. Fu Jian named him General Who Raises Might. He rose through Left Guard general; governed Longdong, Ji, Hedong, Wudu, Wuwei, Ba-xi, and Fufeng; held Ning, You, and Yanzhou; returned to General Who Raises Might and colonel of foot; and received the marquisate of Yidu. Under Fu Jian he won distinction again and again.
17
初，萇隨楊安伐蜀，嘗晝寢水旁，上有神光煥然，左右咸異之。 及苻堅寇晉，以萇為龍驤將軍、督益、梁州諸軍事，謂萇曰：「朕本以龍驤建業，龍驤之號未曾假人，今特以相授，山南之事一以委卿。 」堅左將軍竇沖進曰：「王者無戲言，此將不祥之征也，惟陛下察之。 」堅默然。
Once, while serving under Yang An in the Shu campaign, he slept by a stream at noon; a supernatural radiance played overhead, and his companions took it for an omen. On the Jin invasion Fu Jian made Yao Chang Dragon-General and area commander for Yi and Liang. He told him: "I began my rise under that banner and have never given the title away—now it is yours. Everything south of the Qinling is in your hands. Left general Dou Chong objected: "Rulers do not speak in sport—that commission is a dark portent; please reconsider, Your Majesty. Fu Jian said nothing.
18
堅既敗於淮南，歸長安，慕容泓起兵叛堅。 堅遣子叡討之，以萇為司馬。 為泓所敗，叡死之。 萇遣龍驤長史趙都詣堅謝罪，堅怒，殺之。 萇懼，奔於渭北，遂如馬牧。 西州豪族尹詳、趙曜、王欽盧、王欽盧、牛雙、狄廣、張乾等率五萬餘家，咸推萇為盟主。 萇將距之，天水尹緯說萇曰：「今百六之數既臻，秦亡之兆已見，以將軍威靈命世，必能匡濟時艱，故豪傑驅馳，咸同推仰。 明公宜降心從議，以副群望，不可坐觀沈溺而不拯救之。 」萇乃從緯謀，乙太元九年自稱大將軍、大單于、萬年秦王，大赦境內，年號白雀，稱制行事。 以天水尹詳、南安龐演為左右長史，南安姚晃、尹緯為左右司馬，天水狄伯支、焦虔、梁希、龐魏、任謙為從事中郎，姜訓、閻遵為掾屬，王據、焦世、蔣秀、尹延年、牛雙、張乾為參軍，王欽盧、姚方成、王破虜、楊難、尹嵩、裴騎、趙曜、狄廣、黨刪等為帥。
Beaten at Huainan, Fu Jian withdrew to Chang'an as Murong Hong rose in revolt. Fu Jian dispatched his son Fu Rui against Hong and named Yao Chang his chief of staff. Hong broke them and Fu Rui was killed. Yao Chang sent his Dragon-General chief clerk Zhao Du to apologize; Fu Jian in fury executed him. Terrified, Yao Chang bolted across the Wei to the northern pasturelands. Western magnates Yin Xiang, Zhao Yao, Wang Qinlu, Wang Qinlu, Niu Shuang, Di Guang, Zhang Qian, and others—over fifty thousand households—united to make Yao Chang their league chief. Yao Chang meant to decline until Yin Wei of Tianshui urged him: "The fatal cycle has turned; Qin's end is written. Your prestige fits the hour—you alone can right this ruin—so every bold spirit rallies and lifts you. Humble yourself to their counsel and answer what the realm expects—do not watch the world drown from the shore. Yao Chang took Wei's advice. In Taiyuan 384 he proclaimed himself grand general, great chanyu, and prince of Qin at Wannian; ordered a realm-wide amnesty, adopted the reign title White Sparrow, and issued orders as sovereign. He named Yin Xiang and Pang Yan senior clerks left and right; Yao Huang and Yin Wei masters of records; Di Bozhi, Jiao Qian, Liang Xi, Pang Wei, and Ren Qian consultant gentlemen; Jiang Xun and Yan Zun staff officers; Wang Ju, Jiao Shi, Jiang Xiu, Yin Yannian, Niu Shuang, and Zhang Qian adjutants; and Wang Qinlu, Yao Fangcheng, Wang Polu, Yang Nan, Yin Song, Pei Qi, Zhao Yao, Di Guang, Dang Shan, and others as column commanders.
19
時慕容沖與苻堅相攻，眾甚盛。 萇將西上，恐沖遏之，乃遣使通和，以子崇為質於沖，進屯北地，厲兵積粟，以觀時變。 苻堅先徙晉人李祥等數千戶于敷陸，至是，降於萇，北地、新平、安定羌胡降者十餘萬戶。 堅率諸將攻之，不能克。
Murong Chong and Fu Jian were locked in battle, and Chong's army swelled. Before marching west Yao Chang feared Murong Chong might bar his path, so he sued for peace and left his son Yao Chong as hostage; he camped at Beidi, drilled troops, stockpiled grain, and waited on events. Fu Jian had moved thousands of Jin households such as Li Xiang's to Fulu; they now came over to Yao Chang, and more than a hundred thousand Qiang and Hu households in Beidi, Xinping, and Anding followed suit. Fu Jian threw his generals at Yao Chang but could not break him.
20
萇聞容慕沖攻長安，議進趨之計，群下咸曰：「宜先據咸陽以制天下。 」萇曰：「燕因懷舊之士而起兵，若功成事捷，咸有東歸之思，安能久固秦川！ 吾欲移兵嶺北，廣收資實，須秦弊燕回，然後垂拱取之。 兵不血刃，坐定天下，此卞莊得二之義也。 」堅甯朔將軍宋方率騎三千從雲中將赴長安，萇自貳縣要破之，方單馬奔免，其司馬田晃率眾降萇。 萇遣諸將攻新平，克之，因略地至安定，嶺北諸城盡降之。
Word came that Murong Chong was closing on Chang'an; Yao Chang's council urged him: "Take Xianyang first and you command the realm. Yao Chang disagreed: "The Yan host fights for old homes—once they win they will stream eastward; they cannot hold the Guanzhong basin for long. I will shift north of the mountains, hoard grain and gear, and wait until Qin is spent and Yan retreats—then take the prize without lifting a blade. No blood on the sword yet the empire settled—the stratagem of Bian Zhuang letting two tigers wound each other. Fu Jian's northern general Song Fang rode three thousand horsemen from Yunzhong toward Chang'an; Yao Chang ambushed him at Er county and broke his column. Fang escaped alone; his chief of staff Tian Huang brought the remainder to Yao Chang. Yao Chang stormed Xinping, swept on toward Anding, and every walled town north of the Qinling opened its gates.
21
時苻堅為慕容沖所逼，走入五將山。 沖入長安。 堅司隸校尉權翼、尚書趙遷、大鴻臚皇甫覆、光祿大夫薛贊、扶風太守段鏗等文武數百人奔於萇。 萇遣驍騎將軍吳忠率騎圍堅，萇如新平。 俄而忠執堅，送之。
Murong Chong drove Fu Jian from the field; the emperor fled into the Wujiang hills. Murong Chong seized Chang'an. Several hundred of Fu Jian's officials—among them Metropolitan Governor Quan Yi, Secretary Zhao Qian, Grand Herald Huangfu Fu, Privy Counselor Xue Zan, and Fufeng governor Duan Keng—defected to Yao Chang. Yao Chang ordered Valiant Cavalry general Wu Zhong to pin Fu Jian with cavalry while he himself struck toward Xinping. Shortly afterward Wu Zhong took Fu Jian captive and brought him in.
22
慕容沖遣其車騎大將軍高蓋率眾五萬來伐，戰于新平南，大破之，蓋率麾下數千人來降，拜散騎常侍。
Murong Chong sent chariot-and-cavalry commander Gao Gai at Yao Chang with fifty thousand men; south of Xinping Yao Chang shattered the column. Gai crossed over with thousands of survivors and received appointment as regular attendant.
23
沖既率眾東下，長安空虛。 盧水郝奴稱帝于長安，渭北盡應之。 扶風王驎有眾數千，保據馬嵬。 奴遣弟多攻驎。 萇伐驎，破之，驎走漢中。 執多而進攻奴，降之。
When Murong Chong marched east, Chang'an lay open. Hao Nu of the Lu-shui tribes proclaimed himself emperor at Chang'an, and the north bank of the Wei rose with him. Wang Lin of Fufeng mustered a few thousand men and held the Mawei defile. Hao Nu sent his brother Hao Duo against Wang Lin. Yao Chang struck Wang Lin, broke his force, and Lin fled to Hanzhong. He took Duo captive, pressed on against Hao Nu, and forced his surrender.
24
乙太元十一年萇僭即皇帝位於長安，大赦，改元曰建初，國號大秦，改長安曰常安。 立妻虵氏為皇后，子興為皇太子，置百官。 自謂以火德承苻氏木行，服色如漢氏承周故事。 徙安定五千餘戶于長安。 以弟征虜緒為司隸校尉，鎮長安。
In Taiyuan 386, Yao Chang seized the throne at Chang'an in open usurpation: he declared an amnesty, adopted the reign title Jianchu, named his state Great Qin, and rechristened the capital Chang'an as Everlasting Peace. He raised Lady She to empress, named his son Yao Xing crown prince, and filled out the imperial bureaucracy. He declared that Fire succeeded the Fu house's Wood in the cycle of virtues, and dressed his court after the Han model's succession from Zhou. He resettled over five thousand Anding households to the capital. He gave his brother Yao Xu—General Who Subdues the Enemy—the post of Metropolitan Governor and left him to hold Chang'an.
25
萇如安定，擊平涼胡金熙、鮮卑沒奕於，大破之。 遂如秦州，與苻堅秦州刺史王統相持，天水屠各、略陽羌胡應萇者二萬餘戶，統懼，乃降。 因饗將士於上邽，南安人古成詵進曰：「臣州人殷地險，俊傑如林，用武之國也。 王秦州不能收拔賢才，三分鼎足，而坐玩珠玉，以至於此。 陛下宜散秦州金帛以施六軍，旌賢表善以副鄙州之望。 」萇善之，擢為尚書郎。 拜弟碩德都督隴右諸軍事、征西將軍、秦州刺史，領護東羌校尉，鎮上邽。
Yao Chang marched to Anding, fell upon Jin Xi of the Pingliang Hu and Moyuyu of the Xianbei, and shattered both hosts. He pressed into Qinzhou, where he and Wang Tong—Fu Jian's inspector—glared at each other across the lines. When more than twenty thousand Tianshui Tuge and Lüeyang Qiang and Hu households rallied to Yao Chang, Tong lost heart and yielded. At Shanggui he feasted his troops. Gu Cheng Shen of Nan'an came forward: "This land is crowded with people and folded in rough terrain; talent clusters here like trees in a forest—it was made for war." Your predecessor could neither gather good men nor hold his third of the realm; he sat fondling gems until everything collapsed. Spend Qinzhou's gold and silk on the armies; honor the worthy and lift up the virtuous—give this province what it has been waiting for. Yao Chang approved and raised Shen to Gentleman of the Secretariat. He named his brother Yao Shuode commander on the Longyou front, General Who Conquers the West and Inspector of Qinzhou, with the Eastern Qiang commission, and stationed him at Shanggui.
26
萇還安定，修德政，布惠化，省非急之費，以救時弊，閭閻之士有豪介之善者，皆顯異之。
Back in Anding, Yao Chang pursued humane rule and generous policies, cut every expense that could wait, and tried to heal a broken age; commoners of stubborn integrity he singled out for honor.
27
萇復如秦州，為苻登所敗，語在《登傳》。 以其太子興鎮長安，而與登相距。 登馮翊太守蘭犢與苻師奴離貳，慕容永攻之，犢遣使請救。 萇將赴救，尚書令姚旻、左僕射尹緯等言於萇曰：「苻登近在瓦亭，陛下未宜輕舉。 」萇曰：「登遲重少決，每失時機，聞吾自行，正當廣集兵資，必不能輕軍深入。 兩月之間，足可克此三豎，吾事必矣。 」遂師次於渥源。 師奴率眾來距，大戰，敗之，盡俘其眾。 又擒蘭犢，收其士馬。 萇乃掘苻堅屍，鞭撻無數，裸剝衣裳，薦之以棘，坎土而埋之。 慕容永征西將軍王宣率眾降萇。
Yao Chang returned to Qinzhou and lost to Fu Deng; the full account is in Deng's memoir. He left Crown Prince Yao Xing to hold Chang'an while he kept Fu Deng at bay. Fu Deng's Fengyi governor Lan Du had broken with Fu Shinu; Murong Yong struck Du, who sent envoys pleading for aid. Yao Chang was riding to the rescue when Grand Secretary Yao Min, Yin Wei the Left Vice Director, and others warned him: "Fu Deng sits at Wuting—too close for you to march rashly." Yao Chang replied: "Deng is slow and seldom commits; he habitually misses the hour. News that I lead in person will send him scrambling to hoard men and grain—he will not dare a fast, shallow thrust." Two months will suffice to break those three whelps—and then my work is done. The host halted at Woyuan. Fu Shinu advanced to meet him. After a pitched battle Yao Chang broke him and took every man captive. He seized Lan Du as well and rounded up his troops and mounts. Yao Chang had Fu Jian's body exhumed, lashed it beyond counting, stripped it bare, heaped brambles beneath the corpse, and shoveled it into a pit. Wang Xuan—Murong Yong's general on the western front—brought his command over to Yao Chang.
28
初，關西雄傑以苻氏既終，萇雄略命世，天下之事可一旦而定。 萇既與苻登相持積年，數為登所敗，遠近咸懷去就之計，唯征虜齊難、冠軍徐洛生、輔國劉郭單、冠威彌姐婆觸、龍驤趙惡地、鎮北梁國兒等守忠不貳，並留子弟守營，供繼軍糧，身將精卒，隨萇征伐。 時諸營既多，故號萇軍為大營，大營之號自此始也。 時天大雪，萇下書深自責罰，散後宮文綺珍寶以供戎事，身食一味，妻不重彩。 將帥死王事者，加秩二等，士卒戰沒，皆有褒贈。 立太學，禮先賢之後。
Once, the strongmen beyond the Pass believed the Fu house finished and Yao Chang unrivaled in daring—surely the empire could be settled before noon. Years of deadlock with Fu Deng—and repeated defeats—left allies everywhere weighing whether to stay or bolt. Only Qi Nan, Xu Luosheng, Liu Guoshan, Mizijie Pochu, Zhao Edui, Liang Guo'er, and a handful like them stayed true: they left kin to guard the camps, kept grain flowing to the army, and rode with picked troops at Yao Chang's side. So many camps had sprung up that men began calling Yao Chang's host the Great Camp—the name starts here. Snow poured from the sky. Yao Chang issued a self-rebuke in writing, poured the harem's silks and jewels into the war effort, ate a single dish at each meal, and forbade his consorts bright robes. Generals who died in service rose two ranks posthumously; every soldier fallen in battle received honors and stipends for his kin. He founded the Imperial Academy and received the descendants of ancient sages with full courtesy.
29
敦煌索盧曜請刺苻登，萇曰：「卿以身徇難，將為誰乎？ 」曜曰：「臣死之後，深以友人隴西辛暹仰托。 」萇遣之。 事發，為登所殺，萇以暹為騎都尉。
Suolu Yao of Dunhuang volunteered to slip a blade into Fu Deng. Yao Chang asked, "You mean to die for this—whose victory would that buy?" Yao answered, "When I am gone, look after my friend Xin Xian of Longxi—that is all I ask." Yao Chang let him go. The plot surfaced; Deng executed Suolu Yao; Yao Chang named Xin Xian Commandant of Cavalry.
30
登進逼安定，諸將勸萇決戰，萇曰：「與窮寇競勝，兵家之下。 吾將以計取之。 」於是留其尚書令姚旻守安定，夜襲登輜重於大界，克之。 諸將或欲因登駭亂擊之，萇曰：「登眾雖亂，怒氣猶盛，未可輕也。 」遂止。 萇以安定地狹，且逼苻登，使姚碩德鎮安定，徙安定千餘家安于陰密，遣弟征南靖鎮之。
Fu Deng pressed toward Anding; his captains begged for a pitched battle. Yao Chang refused to oblige them: trading blows with a foe who has nothing left to lose is the meanest tactic in the canon. He would take Fu Deng by stratagem instead. He left Grand Secretary Yao Min to hold Anding and struck Deng's baggage park at Dajie by night, taking it whole. Some urged a strike while Deng reeled. Yao Chang held them back: "His ranks may waver, but their rage still burns—do not underestimate them." He held his hand. Anding was too tight against Fu Deng, so Yao Chang stationed Yao Shuode there, moved thirteen hundred families into Yinmi for safety, and sent his brother Yao Jing—the southern commander—to hold the new line.
31
立社稷于長安。 百姓年七十有德行者，拜為中大夫，歲賜牛酒。
He raised the altars of soil and grain at Chang'an. Commoners seventy years of age who were known for virtue received appointment as Palace Gentlemen, with an annual gift of ox and wine.
32
尹緯、姚晃謂古成詵曰：「苻登窮寇，歷年未滅，奸雄鴟峙，所在糾扇，夷夏皆貳，將若之何？ 」詵曰：「主上權略無方，信賞必罰，賢能之士，咸懷樂推，豈慮大業不成，氐賊不滅乎！ 」緯曰：「登窮寇未滅，奸雄所在扇合，吾等寧無懼乎？ 」詵曰：「三秦天府之國，主上十分已有其八。 今所在可慮者，苻登、楊定、雷惡地耳，自餘瑣瑣，焉足論哉！ 然惡地地狹眾寡，不足為憂。 苻登藉烏合犬羊，偷存假息，料其智勇，非至尊之匹。 霸王之起，必有驅除，然後克定大業。 昔漢、魏之興也，皆十有餘年，乃能一同於海內，五六年間未為久也。 主上神略內明，英武外發，可謂無敵於天下耳，取登有餘力。 願布德行仁，招賢納士，厲兵秣馬，以候天機。 如其鴻業不成者，詵請腰斬以謝明公。 」緯言之於萇，萇大悅，賜詵爵關內侯。
Yin Wei and Yao Huang asked Gu Cheng Shen: "Fu Deng still staggers on year after year; predators perch on every height; rebellion sparks wherever you look; barbarian and Han alike hedge their bets—what now?" Shen answered: "Our lord's designs know no limit; he rewards the true word and punishes without fail; every able man gladly lifts him up—why doubt the great work will finish or the Di traitors vanish!" Wei pressed him: "Deng still lives; conspirators flock in every quarter—are we not afraid?" Shen replied: The Three Qins are heaven's granary; our lord already holds eight shares in ten. Those worth fearing now are only Fu Deng, Yang Ding, and Lei Edui; the rest are gnats beneath notice. Yet Edui holds narrow ground and few followers—not enough to fret over. Fu Deng clings to a mongrel horde and buys time by theft and guile; measured against him, he cannot rival your Majesty. Every founding conqueror sweeps rivals aside before the realm can be settled. Han and Wei each took well over a decade to knit the empire together—half a dozen years is no great span. Your Majesty reads situations inwardly and strikes outwardly with unmatched force; Fu Deng is well within your grasp. Spread virtue, recruit talent, drill the army and provision the horses, and watch for the turning tide. Should the great cause miscarry, I ask to die halved at the waist as amends to you. When Yin Wei reported Gu Cheng Shen's words to Yao Chang, he was delighted and invested Shen as Marquis Within the Passes.
33
雷惡地率眾降萇，拜為鎮東將軍。 魏褐飛自稱大將軍、沖天王，率氐胡數萬人攻安北姚當城於杏城，雷惡地應之，攻鎮東姚漢得于李潤。 萇議將討之，群臣咸曰：「陛下不憂六十里苻登，乃憂六百里褐飛？ 」萇曰：「登非可卒殄，吾城亦非登所能卒圖。 惡地多智，非常人也。 南引褐飛，東結董成，甘言美說以成奸謀，若得杏城、李潤，惡地據之，控制遠近，相為羽翼，長安東北非復吾有。 」於是潛軍赴之。 萇時眾不滿二千，褐飛、惡地眾至數萬，氐胡赴之者首尾不絕。 萇每見一軍至，輒有喜色。 群下怪而問之，萇曰：「今同惡相濟，皆來會集，吾得乘勝席捲，一舉而覆其巢穴，東北無復餘也。 」褐飛等以萇兵少，盡眾來攻。 萇固壘不戰，示之以弱，潛遣子崇率騎數百，出其不意，以乘其後。 褐飛兵擾亂，萇遣鎮遠王超、平遠譚亮率步騎擊之，褐飛眾大潰，斬褐飛及首級萬餘。 惡地請降，萇待之如初。 惡地每謂人曰：「吾自言智勇所施，足為一時之傑。 校數諸雄，如吾之徒，皆應跨據一方，獸嘯千里。 遇姚公智力摧屈，是吾分也。 」惡地猛毅清肅，不可幹以非義，嶺北諸豪皆敬憚之。
Lei E'di brought his host over and received the title General Who Guards the East. Wei Hefei proclaimed himself Grand General and Celestial Invader King, marched tens of thousands of Di and Hu against Yao Dangcheng at Xingcheng while Lei E'di joined him and struck Yao Hande at Li Run. When Yao Chang proposed to march, his officials cried: "Fu Deng lies sixty li off—why worry about Wei Hefei six hundred li away?" Yao Chang replied: "Fu Deng will not fall in one blow, and he cannot suddenly seize my walled towns either." Lei E'di is cunning—he is no common foe. He pulls Wei Hefei from the south, joins Dong Cheng in the east, and flatters his way through a conspiracy—should he seize Xingcheng and Li Run and Lei E'di fortify them, swaying the countryside and backing one another, we lose everything northeast of Chang'an. He slipped an army toward them unseen. Yao Chang had under two thousand soldiers; Wei Hefei and Lei E'di mustered tens of thousands, and bands of Di and Hu kept arriving. Each time fresh enemy troops appeared, Yao Chang looked pleased. His staff asked why he smiled; he said: "They are flocking to aid one another—I can sweep them in one stroke and tear out their lair; the northeast will hold no remnant." Thinking Yao Chang weak in numbers, Wei Hefei threw every man forward. Yao Chang stayed behind his walls to seem weak, then secretly sent Yao Chong with a few hundred riders to slash their rear by surprise. Wei Hefei's ranks broke; Yao Chang sent Wang Chao and Tan Liang with horse and foot to charge—Wei Hefei fell and over ten thousand heads were taken. Lei E'di sued for peace and Yao Chang received him as he had earlier. Lei E'di used to say: "My wit and nerve fit me to tower above my generation." Stack me against the warlords and I should rule a quarter of the realm and thunder across a thousand miles. Lord Yao broke my schemes—that was simply my fate. Lei E'di was grim, upright, and incorruptible—nothing unjust could move him; every strongman beyond the ranges feared and respected him.
34
萇命其將當城于營處一柵孔中蒔樹一根，以旌戰功。 歲餘，問之，城曰：「營所至小，已廣之矣。 」萇曰：「少來鬥戰無如此快，以千六百人破三萬眾，國之事業，由此克舉。 小乃為奇，大何足貴！」
Yao Chang told General Yao Dangcheng to set one sapling in a picket hole at the battlefield camp as a trophy. When asked a year later, Dangcheng said: "The plot was cramped—I have widened it." Yao Chang answered: "No fight ever pleased me more—sixteen hundred broke thirty thousand; our dynasty turns on blows like that." Small numbers becoming supreme—that is the marvel; sheer bulk counts for nothing!"
35
貳城胡曹寅、王達獻馬三千匹。 以寅為鎮北將軍、并州刺史，達鎮遠將軍、金城太守。
Cao Yin and Wang Da of the Hu in Ercheng offered three thousand horses. Cao Yin took office as General Who Guards the North and inspector of Bing; Wang Da as General Who Guards the Distance and prefect of Jincheng.
36
萇性簡率，群下有過，或面加罵辱。 太常權翼言於萇曰：「陛下弘達自任，不修小節，駕馭群雄，苞羅俊異，棄嫌錄善，有高祖之量。 然輕慢之風，所宜除也。 」萇曰：「吾之性也。 吾於舜之美，未有片焉； 漢祖之短，已收其一。 若不聞讜言，安知過也！」
Yao Chang was plainspoken—when followers slipped, he often cursed them publicly. Quan Yi told him: "You trust yourself broadly and scorn small etiquette; you harness champions and gather odd talents, forgiving old slights—that is Gaozu's breadth." But the habit of casual contempt must go. Yao Chang said: "That is simply how I am." I own no shred of Shun's virtues; of Han Gaozu's faults I have already taken one. Had I no frank words, how would I see my errors?"
37
南羌竇鴦率戶五千來降，拜安西將軍。
Dou Yuan of the southern Qiang brought five thousand households over and became General Who Pacifies the West.
38
萇下書，有復私仇者，皆誅之。 將吏亡滅者，各隨所親以立後，振給長育之。
Yao Chang decreed death for anyone settling private blood-feuds. Where officers or clerks had perished, heirs were named by nearest kin and supported to maturity.
39
鎮東苟曜據逆萬堡，密引苻登。 萇與登戰，敗于馬頭原，收眾復戰。 姚碩德謂諸將曰：「上慎于輕戰，每欲以計取之。 今戰既失利，而更逼賊者，必有由也。 」萇聞而謂碩德曰：「登用兵遲緩，不識虛實，今輕兵直進，逕據吾東，必苟曜豎子與之連結也。 事久變成，其禍難測。 所以速戰者，欲使豎子謀之未就，好之未深，散敗其事耳。 」進戰，大敗之，登退屯於郿。 登將金槌以新平降萇，萇輕將數百騎入槌營。 群下諫之，萇曰：「槌既去苻登，復欲圖我，將安所歸！ 且懷德初附，推款委質，吾復以不信待之，何以禦物乎！ 」群氐果有異謀，槌不從而止。
Gou Yao, General Who Guards the East, occupied Niwan Fort and secretly welcomed Fu Deng. Yao Chang clashed with Fu Deng, lost at Matou Plain, regrouped, and renewed the battle. Yao Shuode told the commanders: "Our lord hates reckless fights and prefers to win by craft." Now he charges again after a setback—there has to be a motive. Yao Chang heard and explained to Shuode: "Fu Deng moves slowly and misreads the field; light forces racing east mean that pup Gou Yao has joined him." Delay turns peril unknowable. I strike fast so the wretch's scheme barely forms and his ties stay shallow—I mean to break his plan outright. He pressed the attack, shattered Fu Deng, and Deng fell back to Mei. Fu Deng's general Jin Chui yielded Xiping; Yao Chang entered Chui's camp with only a few hundred riders. Courtiers protested; Yao Chang said: "If Jin Chui has quit Fu Deng yet schemes against me, where can he run?" Men first joining lay down good faith—if I meet them with suspicion, how do I lead anyone?" The Di did plot treason, yet Jin Chui refused and the plot died.
40
萇如陰密攻登，敕其太子興曰：「苟曜好奸變，將為國害，聞吾還北，必來見汝，汝便執之。 」苟曜果見興于長安，興遣尹緯讓而誅之。
Yao Chang marched on Yinmi against Fu Deng and warned Crown Prince Yao Xing: "Gou Yao is a shifty traitor—when he hears I am riding north he will call on you; arrest him." Gou Yao came to Yao Xing at Chang'an; Xing had Yin Wei denounce him and put him to death.
41
萇大敗登于安定東，置酒高會，諸將咸曰：「若值魏武王，不令此賊至今，陛下將牢太過耳。 」萇笑曰：「吾不如亡兄有四：身長八尺五寸，臂垂過膝，人望而畏之，一也； 當十萬之眾，與天下爭衡，望麾而進，前無橫陣，二也； 溫古知今，講論道藝，駕馭英雄，收羅雋異，三也； 董率大眾，履險若夷，上下咸允，人盡死力，四也。 所以得建立功業，策任群賢者，正望算略中一片耳。 」群臣咸稱萬歲。
After crushing Fu Deng east of Anding, Yao Chang feasted his officers; they said: "Had Wei Wu lived this rebel would not endure—you have hedged too much." Yao Chang laughed: "I trail my dead brother in four ways: eight feet five inches tall, arms past the knees—men quail—that is one; with a hundred thousand under him he wrestled the empire—his standard moved and no formation barred him—two; mastering past and present, discussing doctrine and craft, yoking heroes and rare talents—three; leading vast armies through danger as on level ground—every rank trusted him and men died willingly—four. What lets me build merit and seat wise men is only a sliver of their planning. The court cried "Long live your Majesty!"
42
萇下書令留台諸鎮各置學官，勿有所廢，考試優劣，隨才擢敘。 苻登驃騎將軍沒奕于率戶六千降，拜使持節、車騎將軍、高平公。
He ordered each rear-base command to keep schools staffed, test pupils, and promote by ability. Moyijian, Fu Deng's Grand Marshal of Agile Cavalry, brought six thousand households over and received credentials as General of Chariots and Cavalry and Duke of Gaoping.
43
萇寢疾，遣姚碩德鎮李潤，尹緯守長安，召其太子興詣行營。 征南姚方成言於興曰：「今寇賊未滅，上復寢疾，王統、苻胤等皆有部曲，終為人害，宜盡除之。 」興於是誅苻胤、王統、王廣、徐成、毛盛，乃赴召。 興至，萇怒曰：「王統兄弟是吾州裏，無他遠志，徐成等昔在秦朝，並為名將。 天下小定，吾方任之，奈何輒便誅害，令人喪氣！」
Gravely ill, Yao Chang stationed Yao Shuode at Li Run, Yin Wei at Chang'an, and called Crown Prince Yao Xing to headquarters. Yao Fangcheng warned Yao Xing: "Enemies remain and the emperor sickens—Wang Tong and Fu Yin still command retinues and will become dangers; purge them." Yao Xing slew Fu Yin, Wang Tong, Wang Guang, Xu Cheng, and Mao Sheng, then rode to camp. When Yao Xing came, Yao Chang thundered: "The Wang brothers are fellow townsmen without wider aims; Xu Cheng and company were Former Qin's renowned captains. Peace was almost within reach—I meant to use them—why kill them and chill every heart?"
44
萇下書，兵吏從征伐，戶在大營者，世世復其家，無所豫。
His edict exempted forever every soldier's household registered in the main camp from service.
45
苻登與竇沖相持，萇議擊之，尹緯言於萇曰：「太子純厚之稱，著于遐邇，將領英略，未為遠近所知。 宜遣太子親行，可以漸廣威武，防窺窬之原。 」萇從之，戎興曰：「賊徒知汝轉近，必相驅入堡，聚而掩之，無不克矣。 」比至胡空堡，沖圍自解。 登聞興向胡空堡，引還，興因襲平涼，大獲而歸，咸如萇策。 使興還鎮長安。
While Fu Deng faced Dou Chong, Yao Chang weighed an attack. Yin Wei said: "The heir is famed for kindness far and wide but his generalship is still unknown." Dispatch the heir himself—that spreads martial awe and blocks plots early. Yao Chang agreed and told Yao Xing: "When bandits sense you closing they will cram into stockades—surround and swallow them—none survive. When he neared Konghu Fort, Dou Chong's siege collapsed on its own. Fu Deng pulled back on news of Yao Xing; Yao Xing struck Pingliang and returned heavy with booty—just as Yao Chang predicted. He sent Yao Xing back to hold Chang'an.
46
萇下書除妖謗之言及赦前奸穢，有相劾舉者，皆以其罪罪之。
He banned accusations founded on tales of witchcraft or old unpardoned crimes—the accuser suffers the accused's penalty.
47
晉平遠將軍、護氐校尉楊佛嵩率胡蜀三千餘戶降于萇，晉將楊佺期、趙睦追之。 遣姚崇赴救，大敗晉師，斬趙睦。 以佛嵩為鎮東將軍。
Yang Fosong, Jin's distant-pacifying general and Di colonel, brought three thousand Shu and Hu households to Yao Chang; Yang Quanqi and Zhao Mu gave chase. Yao Chang sent Yao Chong, shattered the Jin force, and killed Zhao Mu. Yang Fosong became General Who Guards the East.
48
萇如長安，至於新支堡，疾篤，輿疾而進。 夢苻堅將天官使者、鬼兵數百突入營中，萇懼，走入宮，宮人迎萇刺鬼，誤中萇陰，鬼相謂曰：「正中死處。 」拔矛，出血石餘。 寤而驚悸，遂患陰腫，醫刺之，出血如夢。 萇遂狂言，或稱「臣萇，殺陛下者兄襄，非臣之罪，願不枉臣。 」至長安，召太尉姚旻、尚書左僕射尹緯、右僕射姚晃、尚書狄伯支等入，受遺輔政。 萇謂興曰：「有毀此諸人者，慎勿受之。 汝撫骨肉以仁，接大臣以禮，待物以信，遇黔首以恩，四者既備，吾無憂矣。 」乙太元十八年死，時年六十四，在位八年。 偽諡武昭皇帝，廟號太祖，墓稱原陵。
Bound for Chang'an, he fell desperately ill at Xinchi Fort and was borne onward in his carriage. He dreamed Fu Jian sent celestial envoys and hundreds of ghost warriors storming his camp; fleeing into the harem, women stabbed at ghosts but pierced his groin; the spirits murmured: "Dead center. They pulled the spear free and blood gushed over a peck. He woke in panic; his groin swelled; doctors lanced it and blood flowed as in the nightmare. Then Yao Chang raved, crying that brother Yao Xiang had slain Fu Jian, not he—begging the throne not to blame him. At Chang'an he called in Yao Min, Yin Wei, Yao Huang, Di Bozhi, and others to accept charge as regents. He told Yao Xing: "If anyone defames these ministers, do not listen. Cherish kin with kindness, greet ministers with courtesy, handle affairs with trust, and rule commoners with grace—once those four stand firm I need fear nothing. He died in the eighteenth year of Emperor Xiaowu's Taiyuan era at the age of sixty-four after eight years on the throne. They gave him the Later Qin posthumous title Emperor Wuzhao, temple name Taizu, tomb name Yuanling.
