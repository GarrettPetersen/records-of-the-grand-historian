禿髮烏孤
Tufa Wugu
2
禿髮烏孤，河西鮮卑人也。 其先與後魏同出。 八世祖匹孤率其部自塞北遷於河西，其地東至麥田、牽屯，西至濕羅，南至澆河，北接大漠。 匹孤卒，子壽闐立。 初，壽闐之在孕，母胡掖氏因寢而產於被中，鮮卑謂被為「禿髮」，因而氏焉。 壽闐卒，孫樹機能立，壯果多謀略。 泰始中，殺秦州刺史胡烈于萬斛堆，敗涼州刺史蘇愉于金山，盡有涼州之地，武帝為之旰食。 後為馬隆所敗，部下殺之以降。 從弟務丸立。 死，孫推斤立。 死，子思復鞬立，部眾稍盛。 烏孤即思復鞬之子也。 及嗣位，務農桑，修鄰好。 呂光遣使署為假節、冠軍大將軍、河西鮮卑大都統、廣武縣侯。 烏孤謂諸將曰：「呂氏遠來假授，當可受不？ 」眾咸曰：「吾士眾不少，何故屬人！ 」烏孤將從之，其將石真若留曰：「今本根未固，理宜隨時。 光德刑修明，境內無虞，若致死於我者，大小不敵，後雖悔之，無所及也。 不如受而遵養之，又待其釁耳。 」烏孤乃受之。
Tufa Wugu was a Xianbei chieftain of the Hexi corridor. His line had sprung from the same stock as Later Wei. Eight generations back, Pigu had led the tribe from the northern marches into Hexi, holding a domain that ran east to Maitian and Qiantun, west to Shiruo, south to the Jiao River, and north to the edge of the great desert. Pigu died and was succeeded by his son Shoutian. Shoutian's mother, Lady Hu She, had borne him in the bedding while he was still in the womb; in Xianbei a quilt was called tufa, and from that word the clan took its name. After Shoutian's death his grandson Shunengji came to power—a man of force, daring, and many stratagems. In the Taishi period he killed Qinzhou inspector Hu Lie at Wandui Mound, routed Liangzhou inspector Su Yu at Jinshan, and seized all of Liangzhou—so that Emperor Wu of Jin could scarcely eat for worry. Ma Long later defeated him, and his own men slew him and submitted. His cousin Wuwan succeeded him. When he died, his grandson Tuojin took the leadership. He died in turn, and his son Sifujian succeeded, by which time the tribe had grown somewhat stronger. Wugu was Sifujian's son. On succeeding, he encouraged farming and sericulture and kept friendly ties with neighboring powers. Lü Guang dispatched envoys to invest him as acting commissioner, Grand General Who Conquers the Champions, supreme commander of the Hexi Xianbei, and Marquis of Guangwu. Wugu asked his commanders, "The Lü have sent titles from far away—should we take them?" They all cried, "Our warriors are numerous enough—why bow to anyone else!" Wugu was on the point of agreeing when his officer Shizhen Ruoliu said, "Our foundations are still weak; it is only sensible to bend with the moment." "Guang's laws are clear and his realm is at peace. If we defy him to our ruin, we cannot match his strength; when regret comes, nothing can undo it." "Better to accept, keep ourselves in his good graces, and watch for his slip." Wugu accepted.
3
烏孤討乙弗、折掘二部，大破之，遣其將石亦幹築廉川堡以都之。 烏孤登廉川大山，泣而不言。 石亦幹進曰：「臣聞主憂臣辱，主辱臣死，大王所為不樂者，將非呂光乎！ 光年已衰老，師徒屢敗。 今我以士馬之盛，保據大川，乃可以一擊百，光何足懼也。 」烏孤曰：「光之衰老，亦吾所知。 但我祖宗以德懷遠，殊俗憚威，盧陵、契汗萬里委順。 及吾承業，諸部背叛，邇既乖違，遠何以附，所以泣耳。 」其將苻渾曰：「大王何不振旅誓眾，以討其罪。 」烏孤從之，大破諸部。 呂光封烏孤廣武郡公。 又討意雲鮮卑，大破之。
Wugu attacked the Yifu and Zhejue tribes and broke them, then sent his general Shiyi Gan to build Lianchuan Fort and make it his seat. Wugu climbed the great hill at Lianchuan and wept in silence. Shiyi Gan stepped forward and said, "When the ruler grieves, the servant is disgraced; when the ruler is disgraced, the servant should die. Your grief, great king—is it not Lü Guang?" Guang is aging, and his armies have been beaten again and again. "With our strength in men and horses, holding the great rivers, we can meet a hundred with one—what is Guang to fear?" Wugu said, "I know Guang is old." "Yet my ancestors won distant peoples through virtue; barbarian tribes stood in awe of them, and Luling and Qihan submitted from a thousand li away." "Since I took over, the tribes have rebelled. With the near in defiance, how can the far be won? That is why I weep." His general Fu Hun said, "Great king, why not raise the host and swear the army to punish their treason?" Wugu took his advice and routed the rebel tribes. Lü Guang made Wugu Duke of Guangwu commandery. He also attacked the Yiyun Xianbei and crushed them.
4
光又遣使署烏孤征南大將軍、益州牧、左賢王。 烏孤謂使者曰：「呂王昔以專征之威，遂有此州，不能以德柔遠，惠安黎庶。 諸子貪淫，三甥肆暴，郡縣土崩，下無生賴。 吾安可違天下之心，受不義之爵！ 帝王之起，豈有常哉！ 無道則滅，有德則昌，吾將順天人之望，為天下主。 」留其鼓吹羽儀，謝其使而遣之。
Guang sent envoys again to name Wugu Grand General Who Conquers the South, governor of Yizhou, and Left Worthy King. Wugu told the envoys, "King Lü won this province by the sword alone, yet he never won the distant by kindness or eased the lot of the people." His sons are greedy and dissolute; his three nephews by marriage run wild; the districts are crumbling and the people have nowhere to turn. How can I defy the will of the realm and take a title built on injustice!" The rise of emperors and kings—is there ever a fixed rule?" The lawless fall and the virtuous prevail. I will follow Heaven and the people and become master of the realm." He kept their drums, pipes, banners, and regalia, thanked the envoys, and dismissed them.
5
烏孤從容謂其群下曰：「隴右區區數郡地耳！ 因其兵亂，分裂遂至十餘。 乾歸擅命河南，段業阻兵張掖，虐氐假息，偷據姑臧。 吾藉父兄遣烈。 思郭清西夏。 兼弱攻昧，三者何先？ 」楊統進曰：「乾歸本我所部，終必歸服。 段業儒生，才非經世，權臣擅命，制不由已，千里伐人，糧運懸絕，且與我鄰好，許以分災共患，乘其危弊，非義舉也。 呂光衰老，嗣紹沖暗，二子纂、弘，雖頗有文武，而內相猜忌。 若天威臨之，必應鋒瓦解。 宜遣車騎鎮浩亹，鎮北據廉川，乘虛迭出，多方以誤之，救右則擊其左，救左則擊其右，使纂疲於奔命，人不得安其農業。 兼弱攻昧，於是乎在，不出二年，可以坐定姑臧。 姑臧既拔，二寇不待兵戈，自然服矣。 」烏孤然之，遂陰有吞併之志。
Wugu said calmly to his followers, "Longyou is only a handful of commanderies!" Their wars have splintered them into more than a dozen states. Qifu Gangui rules Henan on his own authority; Duan Ye holds Zhangye by force; the cruel Di pretender Lü Guang has stolen Guzang. I have my father and brothers' heroic deeds to build on. I mean to bring order to the western lands. To overwhelm the weak and strike down the blind—which of these three should we take first?" Yang Tong stepped forward and said, "Gangui was once our vassal and will submit in the end." "Duan Ye is a bookish man, unfit to govern an age; his strongmen do as they please and he cannot control them. A thousand-li campaign with supply lines severed—and he is our neighbor, pledged to share hardship—would be an unrighteous blow at a weakened friend." "Lü Guang is old; his heir Shao is young and dim; his sons Zuan and Hong have talent in arms and statecraft, yet they mistrust one another." "If our power falls on them, they will break at a touch." "Post the chariot-and-cavalry commander at Haoqian and the north-pacifying commander on the Lianchuan line; raid in turn wherever they are thin, feint on many fronts—relieve their right and hit their left, relieve their left and hit their right—until Zuan is worn out running about and the people cannot farm in peace." "Attack the weak and strike the blind—that is the way. Within two years Guzang can be taken without your leaving the seat." "Once Guzang falls, the other two enemies will yield without a fight." Wugu agreed and secretly resolved on conquest.
6
段業為呂纂所侵，遣利鹿孤救之。 纂懼，燒氐池、張掖穀麥而還。 以利鹿孤為涼州牧，鎮西平，追傉檀入錄府國事。
Duan Ye, pressed by Lü Zuan, asked Lilugu for relief. Zuan, alarmed, burned the grain stores of Dichí and Zhangye and retreated. He made Lilugu governor of Liangzhou at Xiping and called Rutan back to handle affairs at court.
7
是歲，烏孤因酒墜馬傷脅，笑曰：「幾使呂光父子大喜。 」俄而患甚，顧謂群下曰：「方難未靜，宜立長君。 」言終而死，在王位三年，偽諡武王，廟號烈祖。 弟利鹿孤立。
That year Wugu, drunk, fell from his horse and hurt his ribs. He laughed and said, "I nearly made Lü Guang and his sons very happy." Soon he worsened. He told his followers, "The realm is still unsettled; you must raise a seasoned ruler." He died as he finished speaking. He had ruled three years; his posthumous title was King Wu and his temple name Founder Lie. His younger brother Lilugu succeeded him.
8
禿髮利鹿孤
Tufa Lilugu
9
利鹿孤以隆安三年即偽位，赦其境內殊死已下，又徙居於西平。 使記室監麹梁明聘于段業。 業曰：「貴主先王創業啟運，功高先世，宜為國之太祖，有子何以不立？ 」梁明曰：「有子羌奴，先王之命也。 」業曰：「昔成王弱齡，周召作宰； 漢昭八歲，金、霍夾輔。 雖嗣子沖幼，而二叔休明，左提右挈，不亦可乎？ 」明曰：「宋宣能以國讓，《春秋》美之； 孫伯符委事仲謀，終開有吳之業。 且兄終弟及，殷湯之制也，亦聖人之格言，萬代之通式，何必胤已為是，紹兄為非。 」業曰：「美哉！ 使乎之義也。」
In the third year of Long'an Lilugu took the throne, pardoned all crimes short of capital offense within his realm, and moved his capital to Xiping. He sent his registrar Qu Liangming as envoy to Duan Ye. Ye said, "Your late king opened the enterprise; his merit outshines the past. He should be dynastic founder. You have a son—why not set him on the throne?" Liangming said, "There is a son, Qiangnu, as the late king commanded." Ye said, "King Cheng of Zhou was young, yet the Duke of Zhou and Duke of Shao ruled as regents;" Han Emperor Zhao was eight, and Jin Ri and Huo Guang flanked him in support. "Though the heir is young, two able uncles could guide him—would that not serve?" Ming said, "Duke Xuan of Song gave up the state, and the Spring and Autumn Annals honor him;" Sun Ce handed power to Sun Quan and in the end founded Wu. "Elder yielding to younger was Tang of Yin's rule—a sage's maxim and the pattern of ages. Why must a son succeed and an elder brother be wrong?" Ye said, "Well spoken!" That is the duty of an envoy."
10
利鹿孤聞呂光死，遣其將金樹、蘇翹率騎五千屯於昌松漠口。
Hearing of Lü Guang's death, Lilugu sent his generals Jin Shu and Su Qiao with five thousand horse to camp at the mouth of the Changsong desert.
11
既逾年，赦其境內，改元曰建和。 二千石長吏清高有惠化者，皆封亭侯、關內侯。
After another year he proclaimed a general amnesty and changed the era name to Jianhe. Every prefect or magistrate of two thousand piculs and above who had governed uprightly with kindness was enfeoffed as a ting marquis or a marquis within the passes.
12
呂纂來伐，使傉檀距之。 纂士卒精銳，進度三堆，三軍擾懼。 傉檀下馬據胡床而坐，士眾心乃始安。 與纂戰，敗之，斬二千餘級。 纂西擊段業，傉檀率騎一萬，乘虛襲姑臧。 纂弟緯守南北城以自固。 傉檀置酒於硃明門上，鳴鐘鼓以饗將士，耀兵于青陽門，虜八千餘戶而歸。
Lü Zuan marched against him; Lilugu sent Rutan to meet him. Zuan's soldiers were crack troops; crossing the Three Mounds they threw the army into fear. Rutan dismounted and sat on a camp stool, and the troops steadied. He gave battle, routed Zuan, and took more than two thousand heads. Zuan marched west against Duan Ye; Rutan led ten thousand horse and raided Guzang while he was away. Zuan's brother Wei held the inner and outer cities of Guzang. Rutan feasted his officers with wine at the Vermilion Bright Gate, beat drums at the Green Yang Gate to display his strength, and withdrew with more than eight thousand households.
13
乞伏乾歸為姚興所敗，率騎數百來奔，處之晉興，待以上賓之禮。 乾歸遣子謙等質于西平。 鎮北將軍俱延言于利鹿孤曰：「乾歸本我之屬國，妄自尊立，理窮歸命，非有款誠； 若奔東秦，必引師西侵，非我利也。 宜徙於乙弗之間，防其越逸之路。 」利鹿孤曰：「吾方弘信義以收天下之心，乾歸投誠而徙之，四海將謂我不可以誠信託也。 」俄而乾歸果奔于姚興。 利鹿孤謂延曰：「不用卿言，乾歸果叛，卿為吾行也。 」延追乾歸至河，不及而還。
Qifu Gangui, beaten by Yao Xing, fled with a few hundred horse; Lilugu lodged him at Jinxing and honored him as a chief guest. Gangui sent his son Qian and others as hostages to Xiping. The north-pacifying commander Ju Yan told Lilugu, "Gangui was our vassal who proclaimed himself without warrant; driven to the wall he submits, not from true loyalty;" "if he escapes to Eastern Qin he will bring their armies west, which cannot profit us." "Move him among the Yifu and cut off his path of flight." Lilugu said, "I am winning the realm by faith; if Gangui submits in trust and I uproot him, all will say I cannot be trusted." Before long Gangui did flee to Yao Xing. Lilugu told Yan, "Had I listened to you, Gangui would have betrayed us. Go after him for me." Yan chased Gangui to the river, failed to catch him, and returned.
14
利鹿孤立二年，龍見於長寧，麒麟游於綏羌，於是群臣勸進，以隆安五年僭稱河西王。 其將鍮勿侖進曰：「昔我先君肇自幽、朔，被髮左衽，無冠冕之義，遷徙不常，無城邑之制，用能中分天下，威振殊境。 今建大號，誠順天心。 然甯居樂士，非貽厥之規； 倉府粟帛，生敵人之志。 且首兵始號，事必無成，陳勝、項籍，前鑒不遠。 宜置晉人于諸城，勸課農桑，以供軍國之用，我則習戰法以誅未賓。 若東西有變，長算以縻之； 如其敵強於我，徙而以避其鋒，不亦善乎！ 」利鹿孤然其言。
In Lilugu's second year a dragon was seen at Changning and a qilin at Suiqiang; his ministers urged elevation, and in the fifth year of Long'an he took the title King of Hexi. His general Chouwu Lun said, "Our first lords rose from You and Shu with hair unbound and garments to the left, knowing neither caps nor courts nor fixed cities—yet they split the realm and made their power felt beyond the border." "To raise a great title now truly follows Heaven's will." "Yet ease on rich land is no legacy for sons and grandsons;" "full granaries and treasuries only whet the enemy's appetite." "He who first takes up arms and first proclaims a title seldom succeeds—Chen Sheng and Xiang Yu are warnings near at hand." "Settle the Jin people in the towns to farm and feed army and state; we ourselves should train for war and subdue those not yet bowed." If trouble flares east or west, distant strategy can hold them in check; and if they outmatch us, shift ground and avoid their edge—would that not serve as well!" Lilugu agreed with him.
15
於是率師伐呂隆，大敗之，獲其右僕射楊桓。 傉檀謂之曰：「安寢危邦，不思擇木，老為囚虜，豈曰智也！ 」桓曰：「受呂氏厚恩，位忝端貳，雖洪水滔天，猶欲濟彼俱溺，實恥為叛臣以見明主。 」傉檀曰：「卿忠臣也！ 」以為左司馬。
He then marched against Lü Long, broke him, and took his right vice director Yang Huan. Rutan told him, "You rested easy in a doomed realm and never chose a safer master; now, old and a prisoner, can you call that wisdom?" Huan said, "The Lü treated me generously and I held the second rank; even if floods covered heaven I would still try to drown with them—I could not face a worthy ruler as a turncoat." Rutan said, "You are a loyal man!" He appointed him Left Marshal.
16
利鹿孤謂其群下曰：「吾無經濟之才，忝承業統，自負乘在位，三載於茲。 雖夙夜惟寅，思弘道化，而刑政未能允中，風俗尚多凋弊； 戎車屢駕，無辟境之功； 務進賢彥，而下猶蓄滯。 豈所任非才，將吾不明所致也？ 二三君子其極言無諱，吾將覽焉。 」祠部郎中史暠對曰：「古之王者，行師以全軍為上，破國次之，拯溺救焚，東征西怨。 今不以綏寧為先，惟以徙戶為務，安土重遷，故有離叛，所以斬將克城，土不加廣。 今取士拔才，必先弓馬，文章學藝為無用之條，非所以來遠人，垂不朽也。 孔子曰：'不學禮，無以立。 '宜建學校，開庠序，選耆德碩儒以訓胄子。 」利鹿孤善之，於是以田玄沖、趙誕為博士祭酒，以教胄子。
Lilugu told his ministers, "I lack the talent to govern yet hold the throne; three years have passed since I succeeded." "Though I labor day and night to spread the Way, justice is not yet even and customs still decay;" "armies march again and again, yet we win no new ground;" "I seek worthy men, yet talent still languishes below." "Have I chosen the wrong men, or is my own blindness to blame?" "Speak plainly, all of you—I will hear it." Palace officer Shi Hao answered, "Ancient kings, when they took the field, prized sparing the army above all, conquering states second, rescuing the drowning and quenching fire; whether they marched east or west, the people had grievances." "Today you do not put peace first but uproot the people; they love their homes and resist removal, so they rebel—thus you may take cities yet your domain does not widen." "You choose men by bow and horse alone and treat learning as useless—that will not draw the distant or win lasting fame." Confucius said, 'Without ritual, a man cannot stand.' 'Build schools, open halls of learning, and set venerable scholars to teach the sons of the realm.' Lilugu agreed and appointed Tian Xuanchong and Zhao Yan libationers to instruct the royal youths.
17
時利鹿孤雖僭位，尚臣姚興。 楊桓兄經佐命姚萇，早死，興聞桓有德望，征之。 利鹿孤餞桓於城東，謂之曰：「本期與卿共成大業，事乖本圖，分歧之感，實情深古人。 但鯤非溟海，無以運其軀； 鳳非修梧，無以晞其翼。 卿有佐時之器，夜光之寶，當振纓雲閣，耀價連城，區區河右，未足以逞卿才力。 善勖日新，以成大美。 」桓泣曰：「臣往事呂氏，情節不建。 陛下宥臣于俘虜之中，顯同賢舊，每希攀龍附風，立尺寸之功，龍門既開，而臣違離，公衡之戀，豈曰忘之！ 」利鹿孤為之流涕。
Though Lilugu had taken a royal title, he still acknowledged Yao Xing as suzerain. Yang Huan's elder brother Jing had served Yao Chang and died young; when Xing heard of Huan's reputation he summoned him. Lilugu feasted Huan east of the city and said, "I meant to build the great enterprise with you; fate has turned—this parting cuts as deep as any in the histories." "The great fish is not the ocean deep and cannot swim there; the phoenix is not the sacred tree and cannot spread its wings." "You have talent for the times and jewels that shine in the dark; you belong in the halls of empire, worth cities in ransom—Hexi alone cannot hold you." "Press on daily and fulfill your promise." Huan wept and said, "I served the Lü and achieved nothing for them." "Your Majesty raised me from captivity and treated me as an old friend; I hoped to rise with you and do some small service. The gate is open yet I must go—the tie to a true lord—how could I forget it!" Lilugu wept with him.
18
遣傉檀又攻呂隆昌松太守孟禕于顯美，克之。 傉檀執禕而數之曰：「見機而作，賞之所先； 守迷不變，刑之所及。 吾方耀威玉門，掃平秦、隴，卿固守窮城，稽淹王憲，國有常刑，于分甘乎？ 」禕曰：「明公開翦河右，聲播宇內，文德以綏遠人，威武以懲不恪，況禕蔑爾，敢距天命！ 釁鼓之刑，禕之分也。 但忠於彼者，亦忠於此。 荷呂氏厚恩，受籓屏之任，明公至而歸命，恐獲罪于執事，惟公圖之。 」傉檀大悅，釋其縛，待之客禮。 徙顯美、麗靬二千餘戶而歸。 嘉禕忠烈，拜左司馬。 禕請曰：「呂氏將亡，聖朝之並河右，昭然已定。 但為人守而不全，復忝顯任，竊所未安。 明公之恩，聽禕就戮于姑臧，死且不朽。 」傉檀義而許之。
He sent Rutan to attack Lü Long's Changsong prefect Meng Yi at Xianmei and took the place. Rutan seized Yi and said, "To seize the moment wins reward;" "to cling to error invites punishment." "I will show my might at the Jade Gate and sweep Qin and Long; you held a doomed town against the royal command—do you accept the law's due?" Yi said, "Your lordship has conquered Hexi and your name fills the realm; civil virtue wins the distant and martial might punishes the proud—how could I, a nobody, resist Heaven?" "The drum of war is my due." "Loyalty to one master is loyalty to another." "The Lü favored me with a frontier post; when you came I submitted, fearing I might seem a traitor—only you can judge." Rutan was delighted, freed him, and honored him as a guest. He resettled more than two thousand households from Xianmei and Ligan and withdrew. Praising Yi's loyalty, he made him Left Marshal. Yi said, "The Lü are finished; your taking of Hexi is already plain." "I held a post I could not keep; to take honor again shames me." "By your grace let me die at Guzang—that death would be immortal." Rutan, respecting his honor, agreed.
19
呂隆為沮渠蒙遜所伐，遣使乞師，利鹿孤引群下議之。 尚書左丞婆衍侖曰：「今姑臧饑荒殘弊，谷石萬錢，野無青草，資食無取。 蒙遜千里行師，糧運不屬，使二寇相殘，以乘其釁。 若蒙遜拔姑臧，亦不能守，適可為吾取之，不宜救也。 」傉檀曰：「侖知其一，未知其二。 姑臧今雖虛弊，地居形勝，可西一都之會，不可使蒙遜據之，宜在速救。 」利鹿孤曰：「車騎之言，吾之心也。 」遂遣傉檀率騎一萬救之。 至昌松而蒙遜已退，傉檀徙涼澤、段塚五百餘家而歸。
Juqu Mengxun attacked Lü Long, who begged for aid; Lilugu called his ministers to counsel. Director Poyan Lun said, "Guzang is starving; grain costs ten thousand cash a picul and the fields are bare—there is nothing to feed an army." "Mengxun marches a thousand li without supplies—let the two enemies weaken each other and strike when they falter." "If Mengxun takes Guzang he cannot hold it—we should take it ourselves, not rescue Long." Rutan said, "Lun sees one side only." "Guzang is weak but its ground is strong—the western hub of realms. Mengxun must not hold it; we must rescue at once." Lilugu said, "The chariot commander's words are my own mind." He sent Rutan with ten thousand horse to relieve Long. At Changsong Mengxun had already withdrawn; Rutan resettled five hundred households from Liangze and Duandui and returned.
20
利鹿孤寢疾，令曰：「內外多虞，國機務廣，其令車騎嗣業，以成先王之志。 」在位三年而死，葬于西平之東南，偽諡曰康王。 弟傉檀嗣。
Lilugu fell ill and commanded, "Trouble presses within and without; let the chariot commander succeed and fulfill our father's will." He had ruled three years and died; he was buried southeast of Xiping with the posthumous name King Kang. His younger brother Rutan succeeded him.

禿髮傉檀
Biography of Tufa Rutan
22
傉檀少機警，有才略。 其父奇之，謂諸子曰：「傉檀明識幹藝，非汝等輩也。 」是以諸兄不以授子，欲傳之于傉檀。 及利鹿孤即位，垂拱而已，軍國大事皆以委之。 以元興元年僭號涼王，遷于樂都，改元曰弘昌。
Rutan was quick and resourceful from youth. His father said to his sons, "Rutan's vision and ability are not yours." So the elder brothers meant the realm for Rutan, not their own sons. When Lilugu reigned he held court in name only; Rutan ran army and state. In the first year of Yuanxing he took the title King of Liang, moved the capital to Ledu, and proclaimed the era Hongchang.
23
初，乞伏乾歸之在晉興也，以世子熾磐為質。 後熾磐逃歸，為追騎所執，利鹿孤命殺之。 傉檀曰：「臣子逃歸君父，振古通義，故魏武善關羽之奔，秦昭恕頃襄之逝。 熾磐雖逃叛，孝心可嘉，宜垂全宥，以弘海嶽之量。 」乃赦之。 至是，熾磐又奔允街，傉檀歸其妻子。
When Qifu Gangui had been at Jinxing, he left his heir Chibpan as hostage. Chibpan later fled home and was caught by pursuers; Lilugu ordered his death. Rutan said, "A son who flees to his father follows the oldest law; Cao Cao honored Guan Yu's return and King Zhao of Qin spared King Xiang of Chu." "Chibpan fled from loyalty to kinship—that deserves mercy, not death." He was pardoned. When Chibpan fled again to Yunjie, Rutan sent back his family.
24
姚興遣使拜傉檀車騎將軍、廣武公。 傉檀大城樂都。 姚興遣將齊難率眾迎呂隆于姑臧，傉檀攝昌松、魏安二戍以避之。
Yao Xing sent envoys naming Rutan General of Chariots and Cavalry and Duke of Guangwu. Rutan greatly expanded the walls of Ledu. Xing sent General Qi Nan to install Lü Long at Guzang; Rutan seized the garrisons of Changsong and Wei'an to give way.
25
興涼州刺史王尚遣主薄宗敞來聘。 敞父燮，呂光時自湟河太守入為尚書郎，見傉檀于廣武，執其手曰：「君神爽宏拔，逸氣陵雲，命世之傑也，必當克清世難。 恨吾年老不及見耳，以敞兄弟托君。 」至是，傉檀謂敞曰：「孤以常才，謬為尊先君所見稱，每自恐有累大人水鏡之明。 及忝家業，竊有懷君子。 《詩》云：'中心藏之，何日忘之。 '不圖今日得見卿也。 」敞曰：「大王仁侔魏祖，存念先人，雖硃暉眄張堪之孤，叔向撫汝齊之子，無以加也。 」酒酣，語及平生。 傉檀曰：「卿魯子敬之儔，恨不與卿共成大業耳。」
Wang Shang, Xing's inspector of Liangzhou, sent his registrar Zong Chang as envoy. Chang's father Xie had been governor of Huanghe under Lü Guang and later master of writing; meeting Rutan at Guangwu he took his hand and said, "You have the air of a hero of the age—you will clear the world's troubles." "I am too old to see it; I leave my sons to you." Rutan told Chang, "Your father praised my small talent; I feared I would shame his judgment." "When I took the realm I still held his kindness in my heart." The Odes say, 'Kept in the heart—when shall I forget?' "I never thought to see you today." Chang said, "Your kindness matches Cao Cao's; in honoring my father you surpass even the famous kindnesses of old." Over wine they spoke of their lives. Rutan said, "You are Lu Su's peer—I regret we did not build the realm together."
26
傉檀以姚興之盛，又密圖姑臧，乃去其年號，罷尚書丞郎官，遣參軍關尚聘於興。 興謂尚曰：「車騎投誠獻款，為國籓屏，擅興兵眾，輒造大城，為臣之道固若是乎？ 」尚曰：「王侯設險以自固，先王之制也，所以安人衛眾，預備不虞。 車騎僻在遐籓，密邇勍寇，南則逆羌未賓，西則蒙遜跋扈，蓋為國家重門之防，不圖陛下忽以為嫌。 」興笑曰：「卿言是也。」
Seeing Yao Xing's power and plotting Guzang, Rutan dropped his era title, cut court offices, and sent Guan Shang to Xing. Xing asked Shang, "Your lord submits as a frontier shield, yet raises troops and builds a great city—is that how a vassal behaves?" Shang said, "Princes fortify themselves by ancient rule—to guard their people and meet sudden danger." "Your servant sits on the frontier beside strong enemies—Qiang unrest in the south, Mengxun proud in the west. The walls are the realm's outer gate; we did not think you would take offense." Xing laughed and said, "Well spoken."
27
傉檀遣其將文支討南羌、西虜，大破之。 上表姚興，求涼州，不許，加傉檀散騎常侍，增邑二千戶。 傉檀於是率師伐沮渠蒙遜，次於氐池。 蒙遜嬰城固守，芟其禾苗，至於赤泉而還。 獻興馬三千匹，羊三萬頭。 興乃署傉檀為使持節、都督河右諸軍事、車騎大將軍、領護匈奴中郎將、涼州刺史，常侍、公如故，鎮姑臧。 傉檀率步騎三萬次於五澗，興涼州刺史王尚遣辛晁、孟禕、彭敏出迎。 尚出自清陽門，鎮南文支入自涼風門。 宗敞以別駕送尚還長安，傉檀曰：「吾得涼州三千餘家，情之所寄，唯卿一人，奈何舍我去乎？ 」敞曰：「今送舊君，所以忠於殿下。 」傉檀曰：「吾今新牧貴州，懷遠安邇之略，為之若何？ 」敞曰：「涼土雖弊，形勝之地，道由人弘，實在殿下。 段懿、孟禕、武威之宿望； 辛晁、彭敏，秦、隴之冠冕； 斐敏、馬輔，中州之令族； 張昶，涼國之舊胤； 張穆、邊憲、文齊、楊班、梁崧、趙昌，武同飛、羽。 以大王之神略，撫之以威信，農戰並修，文教兼設，可以從橫於天下，河右豈足定乎！ 」傉檀大悅，賜敞馬二十匹。 於是大饗文武于謙光殿，班賜金馬各有差。
Rutan sent Wenzhi against the southern Qiang and western tribes and routed them. He asked Xing for Liangzhou and was refused, but was made Regular Attendant with two thousand added households. Rutan then marched on Juqu Mengxun and camped at Dichi. Mengxun held his walls; they ravaged his crops and withdrew to Chiquan. He sent Xing three thousand horses and thirty thousand sheep. Xing then named Rutan commander of Hexi, Grand General of Chariots and Cavalry, protector of the Xiongnu, governor of Liangzhou, and duke as before, with his seat at Guzang. Rutan led thirty thousand men to Wujian; Wang Shang sent Xin Chao, Meng Yi, and Peng Min to welcome him. Shang went out by the Clear Yang Gate; Wenzhi entered by the Cool Wind Gate. Zong Chang escorted Shang to Chang'an; Rutan said, "I have won three thousand Liang households I lean on—you above all—how can you leave?" Chang said, "I serve the old master—that is loyalty to you." Rutan asked, "I am new to this province—how shall I win the distant and settle the near?" Chang said, "Liang is weary but strong in position; men make the Way—it rests with you." Duan Yi and Meng Yi are the old pillars of Wuwei; Xin Chao and Peng Min stand foremost among the gentry of Qin and Longyou. Fei Min and Ma Fu head eminent families of the heartland. Zhang Chang carries the blood of the former Liang kings. Zhang Mu, Bian Xian, Wen Qi, Yang Ban, Liang Song, and Zhao Chang fight with the renown of Li Guang and Huo Qubing. Under your genius, rule them with awe and good faith; train the fields and the ranks alike, and pair arms with letters—you could play the great powers against one another under heaven. Why should the Hexi corridor be the end of your ambition? Rutan was delighted and gave Zong Chang twenty horses. He then feasted his civil and military officers in the Hall of Modest Radiance, awarding gold and horses in graded shares.
28
遣西曹從事史暠聘于姚興。 興謂暠曰：「車騎坐定涼州，衣錦本國，其德我乎？ 」暠曰：「車騎積德河西，少播英問，王威未接，投誠萬里，陛下官方任才，量功授職，彝倫之常，何德之有！ 」興曰：「朕不以州授車騎者，車騎何從得之。 」暠曰：「使河西雲擾、呂氏顛狽者，實由車騎兄弟傾其根本。 陛下雖鴻羅遐被，涼州猶在天網之外。 故征西以周、召之重，力屈姑臧； 齊難以王旅之盛，勢挫張掖。 王尚孤城獨守，外逼群狄，陛下不連兵十年，殫竭中國，涼州未易取也。 今以虛名假人，內收大利，乃知妙算自天，聖與道合，雖云遷授，蓋亦時宜。 」興悅其言，拜騎都尉。
He dispatched Shi Hao of the Western Bureau as envoy to Yao Xing. Yao Xing asked Hao, "The Chariots-and-Cavalry General has secured Liangzhou and come home in glory—does he feel beholden to me?" Hao answered, "The General built his name in Hexi and offered allegiance from afar before your majesty's writ had reached him. You place men by merit and office by measure—that is the way of kings. What debt is there?" Xing said, "If I had not given him the province, how would he have gained it?" Hao said, "The storms in Hexi and the fall of the Lü were wrought by the General and his brothers uprooting their rule." Though your reach extends far, Liangzhou still lay beyond your net. So the Campaigning West General, burdened like the Duke of Zhou and Duke of Shao, was spent before Guzang; and Qi Nan, at the head of the imperial host, was turned back at Zhangye. Wang Shang held one city alone against surrounding tribes. Without ten years of war and the draining of the realm, Liangzhou would not have been easily won. Now you grant a hollow title and reap a rich gain within—proof that Heaven aids your reckoning and the sage-king moves with the Way. Though men call it a transfer of office, it is simply what the hour demands. Pleased with his reply, Xing made him Cavalry Commandant.
29
傉檀宴群僚於宣德堂，仰視而歎曰：「古人言作者不居，居者不作，信矣。 」孟禕進曰：「張文王築城苑，繕宗廟，為貽厥之資，萬世之業，秦師濟河，漼然瓦解。 梁熙據全州之地，擁十萬之眾，軍敗於酒泉，身死于彭濟。 呂氏以排山之勢，王有西夏，率土崩離，銜璧秦、雍。 寬饒有言：'富貴無常，忽輒易人。 '此堂之建，年垂百載，十有二主，唯信順可以久安，仁義可以永固，願大王勉之。 」傉檀曰：「非君無以聞讜言也。 」傉檀雖受制于姚興，然車服禮章一如王者。 以宗敞為太府主簿、錄記室事。
Rutan entertained his ministers in the Hall of Illustrious Virtue, gazed upward, and sighed, "The ancients were right: those who build do not long enjoy; those who enjoy did not build." Meng Yi spoke up: "Prince Zhang Wen raised walls and gardens and restored the ancestral shrines for heirs yet unborn—yet when Qin armies crossed the river, his house melted away like ice in sun." Liang Xi held the whole province and a hundred thousand men, yet lost his army at Jiuquan and his life at Pengji. The Lü, who seemed able to overturn mountains and ruled Western Xia, saw their lands crumble until they surrendered with jade seals to Qin and Yong. Kuang Rao once said, "Fortune and office never stay fixed; in a blink they change hands." This hall is nearly a century old and has seen twelve masters. Only loyalty and compliance keep a house long; only benevolence and right keep it firm. I urge Your Highness to live by that. Rutan said, "Without you I would never have heard such honest counsel." Though he owed allegiance to Yao Xing, Rutan's carriages, dress, and court ritual matched a king's. He made Zong Chang Registrar of the Grand Treasury and chief recorder of the secretariat.
30
傉檀偽遊澆河，襲徙西平、湟河諸羌三萬餘戶于武興、番禾、武威、昌松四郡。 徵集戎夏之兵五萬餘人，大閱于方亭，遂伐沮渠蒙遜，入西陝。 蒙遜率眾來距，戰于均石，為蒙遜所敗。 傉檀率騎二萬，運谷四萬石以給西郡。 蒙遜攻西郡，陷之。 其後傉檀又與赫連勃勃戰于陽武，為勃勃所敗，將佐死者十餘人，傉檀與數騎奔南山，幾為追騎所得。 傉檀懼東西寇至，徙三百里內百姓入于姑臧，國中駭怨。 屠各成七兒因百姓之擾也，率其屬三百人，叛傉檀於北城。 推梁貴為盟主，貴閉門不應。 一夜眾至數千。 殿中都尉張猛大言於眾曰：「主上陽武之敗，蓋恃眾故也。 責躬悔過，明君之義，諸君何故從此小人作不義之事！ 殿內武旅正爾相尋，目前之危，悔將無及。 」眾聞之，咸散。 七兒奔晏然，殿中騎將白路等追斬之。 軍諮祭酒梁裒、輔國司馬邊憲等七人謀反，傉檀悉誅之。
Pretending a pleasure tour along the Jiao River, Rutan secretly relocated more than thirty thousand Qiang households from Xiping and Huangshui into Wuxing, Fanhe, Wuwei, and Changsong. He gathered over fifty thousand barbarian and Chinese troops, reviewed them at Fangting, and marched against Juqu Mengxun into western Shaan. Mengxun came out to meet him; at Junshi Rutan was beaten. Rutan led twenty thousand cavalry and hauled forty thousand piculs of grain to provision Western Commandery. Mengxun assaulted Western Commandery and captured it. Later Rutan fought Helian Bobo at Yangwu and lost again; a dozen officers fell. He escaped into the southern hills with only a few riders and was almost overtaken. Fearing attack from both quarters, Rutan moved everyone within three hundred li into Guzang, and the whole state groaned in fear and anger. Cheng Qi'er of the Tufa, exploiting the people's turmoil, led three hundred men in revolt at the North City. They pressed Liang Gui to lead them, but he barred his gate and refused. By morning their numbers had swelled to several thousand. Palace Commandant Zhang Meng cried to the throng, "Our lord's loss at Yangwu came of trusting too many men." A wise ruler blames himself and mends his ways—why follow this nobody into treason? The palace guard is already on you. Disaster is at hand; repent now or never. At his words the mob melted away. Qi'er fled to Yanran; Bai Lu, palace cavalry commander, overtook and killed him. Liang Pou, military adviser, Bian Xian, assisting-state major, and five others plotted revolt; Rutan put them all to death.
31
姚興以傉檀外有陽武之敗，內有邊、梁之亂，遣其尚書郎韋宗來觀釁。 傉檀與宗論六國從橫之規，三家戰爭之略，遠言天命廢興，近陳人事成敗，機變無窮，辭致清辯。 宗出而歎曰：「命世大才、經綸名教者，不必華宗夏士； 撥煩理亂、澄氣濟世者，亦未必《八索》、《九丘》。 五經之外，冠冕之表，復自有人。 車騎神機秀髮，信一代之偉人，由余、日磾豈足為多也！ 」宗還長安，言於興曰：「涼州雖殘弊之後，風化未頹，傉檀權詐多方，憑山河之固，未可圖也。 」興曰：「勃勃以烏合之眾尚能破之，吾以天下之兵，何足克也！ 」宗曰：「形移勢變，終始殊途，陵人者易敗，自守者難攻。 陽武之役，傉檀以輕勃勃致敗。 今以大軍臨之，必自固求全，臣竊料群臣無傉檀匹也。 雖以天威臨之，未見其利。 」興不從，乃遣其將姚弼及斂成等率步騎三萬來伐，又使其將姚顯為弼等後繼，遺傉檀書云「遣尚書左僕射齊難討勃勃，懼其西逸，故令弼等於河西邀之。 」傉檀以為然，遂不設備。 弼眾至漠口，昌松太守蘇霸嬰城固守，弼喻霸令降，霸曰：「汝違負盟誓，伐委順之籓，天地有靈，將不祐汝！ 吾甯為涼鬼，何降之有！ 」城陷，斬霸。 弼至姑臧，屯於西苑。 州人王鐘、宋鐘、王娥等密為內應，候人執其使送之。 傉檀欲誅其元首，前軍伊力延侯曰：「今強敵在外，內有奸豎，兵交勢踧，禍難不輕，宜悉坑之以安內外。 」傉檀從之，殺五千餘人，以婦女為軍賞。 命諸郡縣悉驅牛羊於野，斂成縱兵虜掠。 傉檀遣其鎮北俱延、鎮軍敬歸等十將率騎分擊，大敗之，斬首七千餘級。 姚弼固壘不出，傉檀攻之未克，乃斷水上流，欲以持久斃之。 會雨甚，堰壞，弼軍乃振。 姚顯聞弼敗，兼道赴之，軍勢甚盛。 遣射將孟欽等五人挑戰於涼風門，弦未及發，材官將軍宋益等馳擊斬之。 顯乃委罪斂成。 遣使謝傉檀，引師而歸。
With Yangwu lost without and the Bian and Liang plots within, Yao Xing sent Master of Writing Wei Zong to probe for weakness. Rutan discoursed with him on Warring States alliances and Three Kingdoms stratagems, on Heaven's mandate in the distance and men's fortunes close at hand—endlessly inventive, his speech bright and sharp. Leaving, Zong sighed, "A man who shapes an age and orders the rites need not spring from a noble house or Han stock;" nor need one who cuts through disorder and steadies the realm have read the Eight Canons or Nine Mounds. Outside the Five Classics, among the foremost of the land, others still arise. The Chariots-and-Cavalry General is keen-witted and brilliant—a true giant of his time. You Yu and Jinti are nothing beside him! Back in Chang'an, Zong told Xing, "Liangzhou is battered but not broken. Rutan is cunning and holds the mountains and rivers—do not think him easy prey." Xing said, "Bobo with a rabble beat him. With the armies of the realm, how can I fail?" Zong said, "Situations turn; what worked once fails next. The aggressor breaks easily; the defender is hard to crack." At Yangwu Rutan slighted Bobo and lost. If a great host comes now, he will dig in and save himself—and I doubt any of his ministers equal Rutan. Even with imperial might against him, I see little gain. Xing refused and sent Yao Bi and Qincheng with thirty thousand foot and horse to invade, with Yao Xian following as reserve. He wrote Rutan: "I have sent Qi Nan, Minister of the Left, against Bobo and fear he may slip west—Bi is posted in Hexi to block him." Rutan took the letter at face value and left his guard down. Bi reached Mokou; Su Ba, Changsong's governor, shut the walls. Bi called on him to yield. Ba replied, "You broke your oath and strike a loyal frontier—Heaven and Earth see; they will not aid you!" I would sooner be a ghost of Liang than submit—never! The city fell; Su Ba was beheaded. Bi came to Guzang and camped in the Western Park. Wang Zhong, Song Zhong, Wang E, and others of the province plotted treason within; scouts caught their courier and brought him in. Rutan meant to kill the leaders; vanguard general Yiliyanhou said, "With the enemy outside and traitors inside, battle pressing and peril near, the danger is grave. Exterminate them all to steady within and without." Rutan agreed, slaughtered more than five thousand, and gave the women to the troops as booty. He ordered every district to drive herds into the open; Qincheng's men swept the countryside for plunder. Rutan sent Juyan, Pacifier of the North, Jing Gui, Pacifier of the Army, and ten other generals with cavalry in separate strikes, crushing them and taking more than seven thousand heads. Yao Bi held his walls and would not fight; Rutan could not break him and cut the river upstream, planning to starve him out. Torrential rain broke the dam; Bi's army rallied. Hearing Bi had lost, Yao Xian rushed up by forced marches with overwhelming force. He sent archer Meng Qin and five others to duel at Liangfeng Gate; before they could loose, weapons officer Song Yi and others charged out and killed them. Xian then blamed Qincheng for the defeat. He sent envoys to make peace with Rutan and pulled his troops back.
32
傉檀於是僭即涼王位，赦其境內，改年為嘉平，置百官。 立夫人折掘氏為五後，世子武台為太子、錄尚書事，左長史趙晁、右長史郭倖為尚書左右僕射，鎮北俱延為太尉，鎮軍敬歸為司隸校尉，自余封署各有差。
Rutan then took the title King of Liang, proclaimed an amnesty, adopted the era name Jiaping, and installed a full court. He made Lady Sheju chief among five consorts, heir Wu Tai crown prince and overseer of the Masters of Writing, Zhao Chao and Guo Xing left and right masters, Juyan Grand Commandant, Jing Gui metropolitan colonel, and enfeoffed the rest in graded ranks.
33
遣其左將軍枯木、駙馬都尉胡康伐沮渠蒙遜，掠臨松人千餘戶而還。 蒙遜大怒，率騎五千至於顯美方亭，破車蓋鮮卑而還。 俱延又伐蒙遜，大敗而歸。 傉檀將親率眾伐蒙遜，趙晁及太史令景保諫曰：「今太白未出，歲星在西，宜以自守，難以伐人。 比年天文錯亂，風霧不時，唯修德責躬可以甯吉。 」傉檀曰：「蒙遜往年無狀，入我封畿，掠我邊疆，殘我禾稼。 吾蓄力待時，將報東門之恥，今大軍已集，卿欲沮眾邪？ 」保曰：「陛下不以臣不肖，使臣主察乾象，若見事不言，非為臣之體。 天文顯然，動必無利。 」傉檀曰：「吾以輕騎五萬伐之，蒙遜若以騎兵距我，則眾寡不敵； 兼步而來，則舒疾不同； 救右則擊其左，赴前則攻其後，終不與之交兵接戰，卿何懼乎？ 」保曰：「天文不虛，必將有變。 」傉檀怒，鎖保而行，曰：「有功當殺汝以徇，無功封汝百戶侯，」既而蒙遜率眾來距，戰於窮泉，傉檀大敗，單馬奔還。 景保為蒙遜所擒，讓之曰：「卿明于天文，為彼國所任，違天犯順，智安在乎？ 」保曰：「臣匪為無智，但言而不從。 」蒙遜曰：「昔漢祖困于平城，以婁敬為功； 袁紹敗於官渡，而田豐為戮。 卿策同二子，貴主未可量也。 卿必有婁敬之賞者，吾今放卿，但恐有田豐之禍耳。 」保曰：「寡君雖才非漢祖，猶不同本初，正可不得封侯，豈慮禍也。 」蒙遜乃免之。 至姑臧，傉檀謝之曰：「卿，孤之蓍龜也，而不能從之，孤之深罪。 」封保安亭侯。
He sent Left General Kumu and Horse-Master Hu Kang against Mengxun, seized more than a thousand households at Linsong, and withdrew. Mengxun, furious, led five thousand horse to Fangting in Xianmei, broke the Chegai Xianbei, and turned back. Juyan attacked Mengxun again and came home routed. Rutan prepared to lead the army against Mengxun in person; Zhao Chao and grand astrologer Jing Bao urged, "Venus has not yet risen and Jupiter stands in the west—defend, do not strike." Heaven's signs have been errant these years, winds and mists out of season. Only virtue and self-reproach can restore fortune. Rutan said, "Mengxun has long trampled our borders, looted our marches, and destroyed our crops." I have waited to wipe away the shame of the eastern gate. The host is ready—will you damp their spirit? Bao said, "Your Majesty set me to read the sky. If I see peril and stay silent, I fail my office." The stars are plain: march now and we cannot win. Rutan said, "I take fifty thousand light horse. If Mengxun meets me with horse alone, he cannot match my numbers;" if he mixes foot soldiers, fast and slow will not keep step; relieve his right and I hit his left; rush his van and I take his rear. I will never give him a stand-up fight—what is there to fear? Bao said, "Heaven does not lie. Disaster will come." Rutan, furious, had Bao chained and marched, saying, "If you are right I will kill you before the army; if wrong I will make you marquis of a hundred households." Mengxun met him at Qiongquan; Rutan was shattered and fled home alone. Mengxun captured Jing Bao and rebuked him: "You read the heavens for that court yet marched against Heaven's will—where was your wisdom?" Bao said, "I was not without wisdom; my lord would not listen." Mengxun said, "When Gaozu of Han was trapped at Pingcheng, he honored Lou Jing's advice;" when Yuan Shao lost at Guandu, he killed Tian Feng. Your counsel matches theirs; your lord's mind cannot yet be read. You may win Lou Jing's reward; I spare you now—but you may yet suffer Tian Feng's end." Bao said, "My lord is no Gaozu, but he is no Yuan Shao either. I may miss a fief; I do not fear the axe." Mengxun let him go. Back in Guzang, Rutan said to him, "You were my oracle, and I would not heed you—that is my grave fault." He enfeoffed him as Marquis of Bao'anting.
34
蒙遜進圖姑臧，百姓懲東苑之戮，悉皆驚散。 壘掘、麥田、車蓋諸部盡降于蒙遜。 傉檀遣使請和，蒙遜許之，乃遣司隸校尉敬歸及子他為質，歸至胡坑，逃還，他為追兵所執。 蒙遜徙其眾八千餘戶而歸。 右衛折掘奇鎮據石驢山以叛。 傉檀懼為蒙遜所滅，又慮奇鎮克嶺南，乃遷于樂都，留大司農成公緒守姑臧。 傉檀始出城，焦諶、王侯等閉門作難，收合三千餘家，保據南城。 諶推焦朗為大都督、龍驤大將軍，諶為涼州刺史，降于蒙遜。 鎮軍敬歸討奇鎮于石驢山，戰敗，死之。
Mengxun pressed on Guzang; remembering the massacre at the Eastern Park, the people scattered in terror. The Leiju, Maitian, Chegai, and other tribal groups all surrendered to Mengxun. Rutan sued for peace; Mengxun accepted and sent Jing Gui, Censor-in-Chief, with his son Ta as hostages. At Hukeng Jing Gui escaped; pursuers captured Ta. Mengxun resettled eight thousand-odd households and withdrew. Qi Jizhen of the Right Guard Zhejue rebelled and held Shilu Mountain. Fearing Mengxun would destroy him and Qi would seize the southern passes, Rutan moved to Ledu and left Cheng Gongxu, Grand Minister of Agriculture, to hold Guzang. As Rutan left the city, Jiao Chen and Wang Hou shut the gates in revolt, rallying three thousand families behind the south wall. Chen made Jiao Lang Grand Commander and Dragon Cavalry Grand General, took the governorship of Liang himself, and surrendered to Mengxun. Jing Gui of the Pacifying Army attacked Qi at Shilu Mountain, was routed, and killed.
35
蒙遜因克姑臧之威來伐，傉檀遣其安北段苟、左將軍雲連乘虛出番禾以襲其後，徙三千餘家于西平。 蒙遜圍樂都，三旬不克，遣使謂傉檀曰：「若以寵子為質，我當還師。 」傉檀曰：「去否任卿兵勢。 卿違盟無信，何質以供！ 」蒙遜怒，築室返耕，為持久之計。 群臣固請，乃以子安周為質。 蒙遜引歸。
Mengxun marched on the strength of Guzang; Rutan sent Duan Gou and Yun Lian to raid Fanhe from behind and resettled three thousand families in Xiping. Mengxun besieged Ledu for thirty days in vain, then sent word: "Give me a beloved son as hostage and I will withdraw." Rutan replied: "Stay or go—as your armies please." You broke faith—what hostage could I offer you!" Mengxun in fury built houses and turned the soil, settling in for a long siege. His ministers pressed him hard; at last he gave his son Anzhou as hostage. Mengxun broke the siege and marched away.
36
吐谷渾樹洛幹率眾來伐，傉檀遣其太子武台距之，為洛幹所敗。
Shuluogan of the Tuyuhun attacked; Rutan sent his heir Wutai to meet him and was defeated.
37
傉檀又將伐蒙遜，邯川護軍孟愷諫曰：「蒙遜初並姑臧，凶勢甚盛，宜固守伺隙，不可妄動。 」不從。 五道俱進，至番禾、苕藋，掠五千餘戶。 其將屈右進曰：「陛下轉戰千里，前元完陣，徙戶資財，盈溢衢路，宜倍道旋師，早度峻險。 蒙遜善於用兵，士眾習戰，若輕軍卒至，出吾慮表，大敵外逼，徙戶內攻，危之道也。 」衛尉伊力延曰：「我軍勢方盛，將士勇氣自倍，彼徒我騎，勢不相及，若倍道旋師，必捐棄資財，示人以弱，非計也。 」屈右出而告其諸弟曰：「吾言不用，天命也。 此吾兄弟死地。 」俄而昏霧風雨，蒙遜軍大至，傉檀敗績而還。 蒙遜進圍樂都，傉檀嬰城固守，以子染幹為質，蒙遜乃歸，久之，遣安西紇勃耀兵西境。 蒙遜侵西平，徙戶掠牛馬而還。
Rutan prepared to strike Mengxun again; Meng Kai, Han River Protector, warned: "Mengxun has just taken Guzang and his power is at its peak—hold fast and wait; do not move rashly." Rutan would not listen. Five columns marched to Fanhe and Tiaodiao and seized more than five thousand households. General Qu You urged: "You have marched a thousand li with the van unbroken and booty choking the roads—double the pace and cross the passes while you can." Mengxun is a master of war and his men are hardened; if light troops strike where we do not expect, the foe without and the settlers within will crush us—that is the road to ruin." Yiliyan, Commandant of the Guard, said: "Our army is at flood tide and every man fights twice as hard; infantry cannot match our horse. To turn back is to abandon plunder and show fear—that is no plan." Qu You left and told his brothers: "They will not hear me—this is fate." Here we brothers will die." Soon fog, wind, and rain closed in; Mengxun's host fell upon them; Rutan was routed and fled home. Mengxun besieged Ledu again; Rutan held the walls and gave his son Ran Gan as hostage; Mengxun withdrew. Later he sent Hebo, Pacifier of the West, to parade troops on the frontier. Mengxun raided Xiping, carried off families and livestock, and returned.
38
邯川護軍孟愷表鎮南、湟河太守文支荒酒愎諫，不血阝政事。 傉檀謂伊力延曰：「今州土傾覆，所杖者文支而已，將若之何？ 」延曰：「宜召而訓之，使改往修來。 」傉檀乃召文支，既到，讓之曰：「二兄英姿早世，吾以不才嗣統，不能負荷大業，顛狽如是，胡顏視世，雖存若隕。 庶憑子鮮存衛，藉文種復吳，卿之謂也。 聞卿唯酒是耽，荒廢庶事。 吾年已老，卿復若斯，祖宗之業將誰寄也。 」文支頓首陳謝。
Meng Kai memorialized that Wen Zhi, Defender of the South and Governor of Huang River, drank without measure, spurned good counsel, and neglected his duties. Rutan asked Yiliyan: "The province is ruined; Wen Zhi is all I have left—what can I do?" Yiliyan said: "Summon him, rebuke him, and make him mend his ways." Rutan summoned Wen Zhi and rebuked him: "My brilliant elder brothers died young; I, unfit, inherited and have brought the realm to ruin—how can I show my face! I live as one already dead." I counted on you as Zixian to guard the state and as Wen Zhong to restore Wu." They say you drink your days away and abandon every duty. I am old, and you are still like this—who will carry our fathers' work!" Wen Zhi kowtowed and begged forgiveness.
39
邯川人衛章等謀殺孟愷，南啟乞伏熾磐。 郭越止之曰：「孟尹寬以惠下，何罪而殺之！ 吾寧違眾而死，不負君以生。 」乃密告之愷，誘章等飲酒，殺四十餘人。 愷懼熾磐軍之至，馳告文支，文支遣將軍匹珍赴之。 熾磐軍到城，聞珍將至，引歸。
Wei Zhang of Han River plotted to kill Meng Kai and sent word south to Qifu Chifen. Guo Yue stopped them: "Minister Meng is generous to his men—what crime could justify his murder!" I would rather die with the crowd against me than live by betraying my lord." He warned Meng Kai in secret, lured Zhang and his fellows to drink, and killed more than forty. Fearing Chifen's approach, Meng Kai rode to Wen Zhi; Wen Zhi sent General Pi Zhen to meet the enemy. Chifen reached the city, heard Pi Zhen was marching, and withdrew.
40
蒙遜又攻樂都，二旬不克而還。 鎮南文支以湟河降蒙遜，徙五千餘戶于姑臧。 蒙遜又來伐，傉檀以太尉俱延為質，蒙遜乃引還。
Mengxun besieged Ledu again, failed in twenty days, and left. Wen Zhi surrendered Huang River to Mengxun, who resettled five thousand households at Guzang. Mengxun attacked again; Rutan gave Grand Marshal Juyan as hostage, and Mengxun withdrew.
41
傉檀議欲西征乙弗，孟愷諫曰：「連年不收，上下饑弊，南逼熾磐，北迫蒙遜，百姓騷動，下不安業。 今遠征雖克，後患必深，不如結盟熾磐，通糴濟難，慰喻雜部，以廣軍資，畜力繕兵，相時而動。 《易》曰：'其亡其亡，系于苞桑。 '惟陛下圖之。 」傉檀曰：「孤將略地，卿無沮眾。 」謂其太子武台曰：「今不種多年，內外俱窘，事宜西行，以拯此弊。 蒙遜近去，不能卒來，旦夕所慮，唯在熾盤。 彼名微眾寡，易以討禦，吾不過一月，自足周旋。 汝謹守樂都，無使失墮。 」傉檀乃率騎七千襲乙弗，大破之，獲牛馬羊四十餘萬。
Rutan planned a western campaign against the Yifu; Meng Kai warned: "Famine has drained us; Chifen presses from the south, Mengxun from the north—the people are in turmoil and cannot live in peace." Even victory abroad will breed disaster at home; ally with Chifen, buy grain, win over the tribes, hoard strength and sharpen arms, and strike only when the moment is ripe." The Changes says: 'Ruin is near, ruin is near—tie yourself to the mulberry while it is still in bud.' Your Majesty, weigh this well." Rutan said: "I am going to take land—do not dampen the army's spirit." He told his heir Wutai: "Years without sowing have beggared us inside and out—a western march is our only rescue." Mengxun has just withdrawn and cannot strike at once; only Chifen troubles me day and night." His fame is small and his host few—he is easy to beat; within a month I shall be free to turn back." Hold Ledu fast and do not let it fall." Rutan led seven thousand horse against the Yifu, routed them, and took four hundred thousand head of livestock.
42
熾磐乘虛來襲，撫軍從事中郎尉肅言于武台曰：「今外城廣大，難以固守，宜聚國人於內城，肅等率諸晉人距戰於外，如或不捷，猶有萬全。 」武台曰：「小賊蕞爾，旦夕當走，卿何慮之過也。 」武台懼晉人有二心也，乃召豪望有勇謀者閉之於內。 孟愷泣曰：「熾磐不道，人神同憤，愷等進則荷恩重遷，退顧妻子之累，豈有二乎！ 今事已急矣，人思自效，有何猜邪？ 」武台曰：「吾豈不知子忠，實懼餘人脫生慮表，以君等安之耳。 」一旬而城潰。
Chifen struck while Rutan was away; Wei Su, adjunct to the Pacifying Army, urged Wutai: "The outer city is too wide to hold—gather our people within the inner wall; we Jin will fight outside, and even if we lose, the core remains safe." Wutai said: "A petty thief—he will run by tomorrow; you worry too much." Fearing the Jin would turn traitor, Wutai locked the brave and wise within the inner city. Meng Kai wept: "Chifen is a villain; heaven and earth rage with us. Advance, and we repay your grace for moving us here; retreat, and we think of our wives and children—how could we be divided!" The crisis is upon us; every man longs to die fighting—why doubt us!" Wutai said: "I know you are loyal; I fear others may slip away in panic—that is why I confine you, to keep you safe." Within ten days the city fell.
43
安西樊尼自西平奔告傉檀，傉檀謂眾曰：「今樂都為熾磐所陷，男夫盡殺，婦女賞軍，雖欲歸還，無所赴也。 卿等能與吾藉乙弗之資，取契汗以贖妻子者，是所望也。 不爾，歸熾磐便為奴僕矣，豈忍見妻子在他人懷抱中！ 」遂引師而西，眾多逃返，遣鎮北段苟追之，苟亦不還。 於是將士皆散，惟中軍紇勃、後軍洛肱、安西樊尼、散騎侍郎陰利鹿在焉。 傉檀曰：「蒙遜、熾磐昔皆委質於吾，今而歸之，不亦鄙哉！ 四海之廣，匹夫無所容其身，何其痛也！ 蒙遜與吾名齊年比，熾磐姻好少年，俱其所忌，勢皆不濟。 與其聚而同死，不如分而或全。 樊尼長兄之子，宗部所寄，吾眾在北者戶垂二萬，蒙遜方招懷遐邇，存亡繼絕，汝其西也。 紇勃、洛肱亦與尼俱。 吾年老矣，所適不容，甯見妻子而死！ 」遂歸熾磐，唯陰利鹿隨之。 傉檀謂利鹿曰：「去危就安，人之常也。 吾親屬皆散，卿何獨留？ 」利鹿曰：「臣老母在家，方寸實亂。 但忠孝之義，勢不俱全。 雖不能西哭沮渠，申包胥之誠； 東感秦援，展毛遂之操，負羈靮而侍陛下者，臣之分也。 惟願開弘遠猷，審進止之算。 」傉檀歎曰：「知人固未易，人亦未易知。 大臣親戚皆棄我去，終紿不虧者，唯卿一人。 歲寒不凋，見之於卿。 」傉檀至西平，熾磐遣使郊迎，待以上賓之禮。
Fanni fled from Xiping with the news; Rutan told his men: "Chifen has taken Ledu; the men are dead, the women given to his soldiers—even if we turn back, we have nowhere left." If you will follow me west, use the Yifu to seize Qihan, and buy back your wives and children—that is my hope." Otherwise we submit to Chifen as slaves—how can I bear to see our wives in another's arms!" He marched west; many deserted on the road; he sent Duan Gou to bring them back, but Gou never returned. Officers and men melted away; only Hebo, Luogong, Fanni, and Yin Lili, Gentleman Attendant of Scattered Cavalry, stayed. Rutan said: "Mengxun and Chifen once pledged themselves to me—now to crawl to them, how shameful!" All beneath heaven, and not one corner for a single man—how bitter!" Mengxun is my peer in rank and age; Chifen is a young kinsman by marriage—each fears me; neither road can save me." Better to split and live in part than die together in a heap." Fanni is my brother's son and the tribe's hope; twenty thousand of our people remain in the north; Mengxun is winning the distant and restoring the broken line—go west." Hebo and Luogong, go with Fanni." I am old; no land will have me; I would rather see my wife and children and die!" He went to Chifen; only Yin Lili followed. Rutan told Lili: "To flee danger for safety is only human." My kin have all fled—why do you alone stay!" Lili said: "My old mother is at home; my heart is torn." Yet loyalty and filial piety cannot both be whole in such a day." I cannot weep west of the Qu for Juqu as Shen Baoxu did for Chu; nor win Qin from the east and show Mao Sui's courage, yet to take bridle and serve at your side—that is my duty." Only grant me this: think far, and weigh every move." Rutan sighed: "To know a man is hard; to be known is harder still." Ministers and kin abandoned me; in the end only you did not fail me." The pine does not fade in winter—that is you." At Xiping, Chifen's envoys met him beyond the walls and received him as a guest of the first rank.
44
初，樂都之潰也，諸城皆降於熾磐，傉檀將尉賢政固守浩亹不下。 熾磐呼之曰：「樂都已潰，卿妻子皆在吾間，孤城獨守，何所為也！ 」賢政曰：「受涼王厚恩，為國家籓屏，雖知樂都已陷，妻子為擒，先歸獲賞，後順受誅，然不知主上存亡，未敢歸命。 妻子小事，豈足動懷！ 昔羅憲待命，晉文亮之； 文聘後來，魏武不責。 邀一時之榮，忘委付之重，竊用恥焉，大王亦安用之哉！ 」熾磐乃遣武台手書喻政，政曰：「汝為國儲，不能盡節，面縛於人，棄父負君，虧萬世之業，賢政義士，豈如汝乎！ 」既而聞傉檀至左南，乃降。
When Ledu fell, every city surrendered to Chifen; only Wei Xianzheng held Hao'e and would not yield. Chifen called: "Ledu is lost; your wife and children are mine—why hold a lone city!" Xianzheng said: "The King of Liang favored me; I was his border shield. I know Ledu has fallen and my family is captive—the first to submit is rewarded, the last is punished—but I do not know whether my lord lives or dies, and I dare not yield yet." Wife and children are a small thing—they cannot sway me!" Luo Xian waited for his orders and Jin Wen honored him; Wen Pi came late and Cao Wei did not reproach him." To grasp a moment's gain and forget the charge given me—I would be ashamed; why would you want such a man!" Chifen sent Wutai's own letter; Xianzheng answered: "You were heir of the realm yet could not die with honor—you bound yourself to the enemy, forsook your father, betrayed your lord, and ruined a thousand years of work. I, Xianzheng, am not such a man!" When he heard Rutan had reached Zuonan, he surrendered.
45
熾磐以傉檀為驃騎大將軍，封左南公。 歲餘，為熾磐所鴆。 左右勸傉檀解藥，傉檀曰：「吾病豈宜療邪！ 」遂死，時年五十一，在位十三年，偽諡景王。 武台後亦為熾磐所殺。 傉檀少子保周、臘於破羌、俱延子覆龍、鹿孤孫副周、烏孤孫承缽皆奔沮渠蒙遜。 久之，歸魏，魏以保周為張掖王，覆龍酒泉公，破羌西平公，副周永平公，承缽昌松公。
Chifen made Rutan Grand General of Agile Cavalry and Duke of Zuonan. A year later Chifen poisoned him. Attendants pressed antidote on him; Rutan said: "This sickness—why cure it!" He died at fifty-one after thirteen years' reign, posthumously titled King Jing. Wutai was later killed by Chifen as well. Rutan's sons Baozhou and La, Juyan's son Fulong, Lilugu's grandson Fuzhou, and Wugu's grandson Chengbo fled to Juqu Mengxun. Years later they went to Wei; Wei made Baozhou Prince of Zhangye, Fulong Duke of Jiuquan, La Duke of Xiping, Fuzhou Duke of Yongping, and Chengbo Duke of Changsong.
46
烏孤以安帝隆安元年僭立，至傉檀三世，凡十九年，以安帝義熙十年滅。
Wugu claimed kingship in Long'an 1 of Emperor An; three generations later, under Rutan, the line had lasted nineteen years when it fell in Yixi 10 of Emperor An.
47
【評贊】
Historian's commentary and eulogy
48
史臣曰：禿髮累葉酋豪，擅強邊服，控弦玉塞，躍馬金山，候滿月而窺兵，乘折膠而縱鏑，禮容弗被，聲教斯阻。 烏孤納苻渾之策，治兵以討不賓； 鹿孤從史暠之言，建學而延胄子。 遂能開疆河右，抗衡強國。 道由人弘，抑此之謂！
The historian writes: For generations the Tufa were frontier chiefs who ruled the marches. They strung bows at Jade Pass and rode at Golden Mountain, raided under the full moon and shot when the cold made bow-glue snap—court ritual never touched them, and the throne's teaching never reached them. Wugu took Fu Hun's counsel, drilled his armies, and punished rebels; Lilugu heeded Shi Gao, founded schools, and brought up the sons of the nobility. So they opened lands west of the Yellow River and stood as equals against great powers. The Way advances through men—here was the proof.
49
傉檀承累捷之銳，藉二昆之資，摧呂氏算無遣策，取姑臧兵不血刃，武略雄圖，比蹤前烈。 既而叨竊重位，盈滿易期，窮兵以逞其心，縱慝自貽其弊，地奪于蒙遜，勢衄于赫連，覆國喪身，猶為幸也。 昔宋殤好戰，致災于華督； 楚靈黷武，取殺於乾溪。 異代同亡，其于傉檀見之矣。
Rutan rode the sharp edge of repeated victories and his brothers' legacy, outschemed the house of Lü, and took Guzang without drawing a blade—his martial vision matched the great kings of old. Then he clung to a throne too heavy for him; fullness invites ruin. He spent armies to feed his pride and wickedness to destroy himself—Mengxun seized his lands, Helian broke his power. To lose realm and life was still a mercy. Long ago Duke Shang of Song loved war and brought ruin on Hua Du; King Ling of Chu doted on arms and died at Ganxi. Different ages, the same end—so it was with Rutan.
50
贊曰：禿髮弟兄，擅雄群虜。 開疆河外，清氛西土。 傉檀傑出，騰駕時英。 窮兵黷武，喪國頹聲。
The eulogy says: The Tufa brothers ruled the frontier tribes. They carved a realm beyond the river and cleared the western sky. Rutan rose above the heroes of his age. He spent his armies on war—realm lost, name in ruins.
