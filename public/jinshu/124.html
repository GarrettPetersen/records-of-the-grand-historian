Murong Bao; Murong Sheng; Murong Xi; Murong Yun
2
慕容寶，字道祐，垂之第四子也。 少輕果無志操，好人佞己。 苻堅時為太子洗馬、萬年令。 堅淮肥之役，以寶為陵江將軍。 及為太子，砥礪自修，敦崇儒學，工談論，善屬文，曲事垂左右小臣，以求美譽。 垂之朝士翕然稱之，垂亦以為克保家業，甚賢之。
Murong Bao, courtesy name Daoyou, was Murong Chui's fourth son. As a young man he was impulsive and lacked firm principles, and he liked people who flattered him. During Fu Jian's reign he held the posts of Grand Mentor to the crown prince and magistrate of Wannian. When Fu Jian fought at the Fei River, Bao was appointed General Who Crosses the River. After he became heir apparent, he disciplined himself, promoted Confucian studies, excelled at discussion and literary composition, and cultivated Chui's attendants and lesser officials to win praise. Chui's courtiers united in praising him, and Chui too believed he could safeguard the family legacy and held him in high esteem.
3
垂死，其年寶嗣偽位，大赦境內，改元為永康。 以其太尉庫辱官偉為太師、左光祿大夫，段崇為太保，其餘拜授各有差。 遵垂遺令，校閱戶口，罷諸軍營分屬郡縣，定士族舊籍，明其官儀，而法峻政嚴，上下離德，百姓思亂者十室而九焉。
After Chui's death, Bao took the throne that same year, declared a general amnesty, and adopted the era name Yongkang. He appointed Grand Commandant Kuru Guan Wei as Grand Preceptor and Left Grand Master of Splendid Virtue, Duan Chong as Grand Guardian, and assigned the others offices according to their stations. He carried out Chui's deathbed orders by reviewing household registers, disbanding separate military camps and assigning the men to local counties, restoring gentry registers and clarifying official ritual—but his laws were harsh and his rule severe; court and people fell out, and nine households in ten among the populace yearned for rebellion.
4
初，垂以寶塚嗣未建，每憂之。 寶庶子清河公會多材藝，有雄略，垂深奇之。 及寶之北伐，使會代攝宮事，總錄、禮遇一同太子，所以見定旨也。 垂之伐魏，以龍城舊都，宗廟所在，復使會鎮幽州，委以東北之重，高選僚屬以崇威望。 臨死顧命，以會為寶嗣，而寶寵愛少子濮陽公策，意不在會。 寶庶長子長樂公盛自以同生年長，恥會先之，乃盛稱策宜為儲貳，而非毀會焉。 寶大悅，乃訪其趙王麟、高陽王隆，麟等咸希旨贊成之。 寶遂與麟等定計，立策母段氏為皇后，策為皇太子，盛、會進爵為王。 策字道符，年十一，美姿貌，而蠢弱不慧。
Earlier, because Bao had not yet prepared a proper burial site for the succession, Chui had worried about it repeatedly. Bao's illegitimate son Hui, Duke of Qinghe, was gifted in many skills and possessed bold strategic vision; Chui admired him greatly. On Bao's northern campaign he left Hui to manage palace affairs with authority and honors equal to the crown prince's, signaling that Hui was the intended successor. When Chui campaigned against Wei, he again stationed Hui in You Province to guard Longcheng, the old capital and seat of the ancestral shrines, entrusting him with the northeast and staffing his command with eminent officials to bolster his authority. On his deathbed Chui had named Hui as Bao's successor, but Bao favored his younger son Ce, Duke of Puyang, and had no intention of installing Hui. Sheng, Duke of Changle, Bao's eldest son by a concubine, resented that Hui, though younger, had been placed ahead of him; he loudly advocated making Ce crown prince and spoke ill of Hui. Bao was delighted and consulted Princes Lin of Zhao and Long of Gaoyang, who all sensed his wishes and agreed. Bao then acted with Lin and the others: he made Ce's mother of the Duan clan empress, named Ce crown prince, and ennobled Sheng and Hui as princes. Ce, courtesy name Daofu, was eleven, handsome in face, but dull, feeble, and witless.
5
魏伐并州，驃騎農逆戰，敗績，還于晉陽，司馬慕輿嵩閉門距之。 農率騎數千奔歸中山，行及潞川，為魏追軍所及，餘騎盡沒，單馬遁還。 寶引群臣於東堂議之。 中山尹苻謨曰：「魏軍強盛，千里轉鬥，乘勝而來，勇氣兼倍，若逸騎平原，形勢彌盛，殆難為敵，宜度險距之。 」中書令晆邃曰：「魏軍多騎，師行剽銳，馬上齎糧，不過旬日。 宜令郡縣聚千家為一堡，深溝高壘，清野待之。 至無所掠，資食無出，不過六旬，自然窮退。 」尚書封懿曰：「今魏師十萬，天下之勍敵也。 百姓雖欲營聚，不足自固，是則聚糧集兵以資強寇，且動眾心，示之以弱，阻關距戰，計之上也。 」慕容麟曰：「魏今乘勝氣銳，其鋒不可當，宜自完守設備，待其弊而乘之。 」於是修城積粟，為持久之備。
When Wei invaded Bing Province, Flying Cavalry General Nong fought them, was routed, and withdrew to Jinyang, where Chief Administrator Murong Song shut the gates against him. Nong fled with a few thousand cavalry toward Zhongshan; at Lu River Wei pursuers caught him, his escort was wiped out, and he rode back alone. Bao convened his ministers in the Eastern Hall for counsel. Fu Mo, governor of Zhongshan, said: "The Wei forces are strong; marching a thousand li in pursuit of victory, their morale is doubled. If their light cavalry sweep the open country their advantage will only grow—we should hold difficult ground and keep them out." Palace Secretary Fei Sui said: "The Wei host is mostly cavalry; they march fast and carry provisions on horseback—they cannot sustain themselves for more than ten days." "Let every commandery and county group a thousand households into one fortified settlement, dig deep moats, raise high walls, and strip the countryside bare while we wait." "With nothing to seize and no food to draw on, within sixty days they will withdraw of their own accord, spent and broken." Minister of Works Feng Yi said: "A hundred thousand Wei troops are upon us—the strongest foe in the realm." "Even if the people try to cluster for defense, they cannot hold; gathering grain and men would only feed a stronger foe, unsettle morale, advertise weakness, and forfeit the best course—holding the passes and fighting." Murong Lin said: "Wei comes flushed with victory; their charge cannot be faced head-on. We should fortify, stock supplies, and wait until they weaken, then strike." They then repaired the walls, hoarded grain, and prepared for a long siege.
6
魏攻中山不克，進據博陵魯口，諸將望風奔退，郡縣悉降于魏，寶聞魏有內難，乃盡眾出距，步卒十二萬，騎三萬七千，次於曲陽柏肆。 魏軍進至新梁。 寶憚魏師之銳，乃遣征北隆夜襲魏軍，敗績而還。 魏軍方軌而至，對營相持，上下兇懼，三軍奪氣。 農、麟勸寶還中山，乃引歸。 魏軍追擊之，寶、農等棄大軍，率騎二萬奔還。 時大風雪，凍死者相枕於道。 寶恐為魏軍所及，命去袍杖戎器，寸刃無返。
Wei failed to take Zhongshan and moved on Boling Lukou; commanders fled and districts surrendered. Learning of turmoil in Wei, Bao marched out his whole army—120,000 infantry and 37,000 cavalry—and camped at Quyang Baixi. The Wei forces advanced to Xinliang. Fearing Wei's edge, Bao sent Long on a night raid; Long was beaten and withdrew. Wei advanced in tight order and pitched camp opposite; panic spread through the ranks and the army's spirit collapsed. Nong and Lin persuaded Bao to withdraw to Zhongshan, and he turned back. Wei pursued; Bao and Nong abandoned the main body and galloped back with twenty thousand horse. A blizzard struck; corpses of men frozen on the march lay heaped along the road. Fearing capture, Bao ordered cloaks, staves, and arms discarded; not a weapon was carried back.
7
魏軍進攻中山，屯于芳林園。 其夜尚書慕容皓謀殺寶，立慕容麟。 皓妻兄蘇泥告之，寶使慕容隆收皓，皓與同謀數十人斬關奔魏。 麟懼不自安，以兵劫左衛將軍、北地王精，謀率禁旅弑寶。 精以義距之，麟怒，殺精，出奔丁零。
Wei pressed the attack on Zhongshan and camped in Fanglin Garden. That night Minister Murong Hao plotted to kill Bao and enthrone Murong Lin. Su Ni, brother of Hao's wife, betrayed the plot; Bao sent Long to arrest Hao, but Hao and dozens of conspirators broke out and fled to Wei. Lin, terrified, seized Left Guard General Jing, Prince of Beidi, and plotted to use the palace troops to murder Bao. Jing refused on grounds of loyalty; Lin killed him in rage and fled to the Dingling.
8
初，寶聞魏之來伐也，使慕容會率幽、并之眾赴中山，麟既叛，寶恐其逆奪會軍，將遣兵迎之。 麟侍郎段平子自丁零奔還，說麟招集丁零，軍眾甚盛，謀襲會軍，東據龍城。 寶與其太子策及農、隆等萬餘騎迎會於薊，以開封公慕容詳守中山。 會傾身誘納，繕甲厲兵，步騎二萬，列陣而進，迎寶薊南。 寶分其兵給農，隆，遣西河公庫辱官驥率眾三千助守中山。 會以策為太子，有恨色。 寶以告農、隆，俱曰：「會一年少，專任方事，習驕所致，豈有他也。 臣當以禮責之。 」幽平之士皆懷會威德，不樂去之，咸請曰：「清河王天資神武，權略過人，臣等與之誓同生死，感王恩澤，皆勇氣自倍。 願陛下與皇太子、諸王止駕薊宮，使王統臣等進解京師之圍，然後奉迎車駕。 」寶左右皆害其勇略，譖而不許，眾咸有怨言。 左右勸寶殺會，侍御史仇尼歸聞而告會曰：「左右密謀如是，主上將從之。 大王所恃唯父母也，父已異圖； 所杖者兵也，兵已去手，進退路窮，恐無自全之理。 盍誅二王，廢太子，大王自處東宮，兼領將相，以匡社稷。 」會不從。 寶謂農、隆曰：「觀會為變，事當必然，宜早殺之。 不爾，恐成大禍。 」農曰：「寇賊內侮，中州紛亂，會鎮撫舊都，安眾寧境，及京師有難，萬里星赴，威名之重，可以振服戎狄。 又逆跡未彰，宜且隱忍。 今社稷之危若綴旒然，復內相誅戮，有損威望。 」寶曰：「會逆心已成，而王等仁慈，不欲去之，恐一旦釁發，必先害諸父，然後及吾。 事敗之後，當思朕言。 」農等固諫，乃止。 會聞之彌懼，奔于廣都黃榆穀。 會遣仇尼歸等率壯士二千餘人分襲農、隆，隆是夜見殺，農中重創。 既而會歸於寶，寶意在誅會，誘而安之，潛使左衛慕輿騰斬會，不能傷。 會復奔其眾，於是勒兵攻寶。 寶率數百騎馳如龍城，會率眾追之，遣使請誅左右佞臣，並求太子，寶弗許。 會圍龍城，侍御郎高雲夜率敢死士百餘人襲會，敗之，眾悉逃散，單馬奔還中山，乃逾圍而入，為慕容詳所殺。
Earlier Bao had summoned Hui with the armies of You and Bing; after Lin's revolt he feared Hui would turn those troops against him and prepared to go meet him. Pingzi, an attendant of Lin's, escaped from the Dingling and reported that Lin was rallying a huge Dingling force to attack Hui and seize Longcheng. Bao rode out with Ce, Nong, Long, and over ten thousand cavalry to meet Hui at Ji, leaving Xiang, Duke of Kaifeng, to defend Zhongshan. Hui worked to win loyalty, drilled his troops, and marched south of Ji with twenty thousand men in battle order to receive Bao. Bao split his force between Nong and Long and sent Ji, Duke of Xihe, with three thousand men to reinforce Zhongshan. Learning that Ce had been named crown prince, Hui's face darkened with resentment. Bao confided in Nong and Long, who said: "Hui is young and commands the frontier alone—pride in power explains it; there is nothing more." "We shall admonish him properly." The warriors of You and Ping revered Hui and refused to leave him; they pleaded: "The Prince of Qinghe has Heaven's gift of martial genius and surpassing strategy; we have sworn to live and die with him, and under his favor our courage is doubled." "We ask that Your Majesty, the crown prince, and the princes remain at Ji while the prince leads us to lift the siege of the capital; then we will escort you home." Bao's courtiers feared Hui's prowess and slandered him until Bao refused; discontent spread through the ranks. Courtiers urged Bao to execute Hui; Attendant Censor Qiu Nigui warned Hui: "The inner circle plots your death, and the emperor will heed them." "You trust only your parents, but your father already turns against you;" "your strength is your army, and that army is slipping away; with neither advance nor retreat left, I fear you cannot save yourself." "Slay the two princes, depose the crown prince, take the heir's place yourself, and hold civil and military power—that is how to save the realm." Hui refused. Bao told Nong and Long: "Hui's rebellion is certain; we must kill him soon." "Otherwise disaster will follow." Nong said: "Enemies press within and the heartland is chaotic; Hui holds the old capital, keeps order on the frontier, and when the capital is threatened he races a thousand li—his name alone can subdue the tribes." "His guilt is not yet open; we should bear with him for now." "The state hangs by a thread; fratricide now would shatter our authority." Bao said: "Hui's treason is set, yet you are too merciful to act; when he strikes he will kill your fathers first, then me." "When it is too late you will remember what I said." Nong and the others pressed him hard, and he relented. Hui, hearing this, grew more afraid and fled to Huangyu Valley in Guangdu. Hui sent Qiu Nigui with over two thousand men to strike Nong and Long; Long was killed that night and Nong badly wounded. Hui then came back to Bao, who meant to kill him, soothed him with false promises, and secretly ordered Teng of the Left Guard to strike him down—but failed. Hui fled to his men and marched against Bao. Bao galloped toward Longcheng with a few hundred horse; Hui pursued, demanding the heads of corrupt advisers and the surrender of the crown prince; Bao refused both. Hui besieged Longcheng; Attendant Lang Gao Yun led a hundred daredevils in a night assault, routed him, and his army scattered; Hui rode alone to Zhongshan, broke through the lines, and was slain by Xiang.
9
詳僭稱尊號，置百官，改年號。 荒酒奢淫，殺戮無度，誅其王公以下五百餘人，內外震局，莫敢忤視。 城中大饑，公卿餓死者數十人。 麟率丁零之眾入中山，斬詳及其親党三百餘人，復僭稱尊號。 中山饑甚，麟出據新市，與魏師戰於義台，麟軍敗績。 魏師遂人中山，麟乃奔鄴。
Xiang seized the imperial title, set up the full bureaucracy, and proclaimed a new era name. He drowned himself in wine and debauch, slaughtered without limit, and killed more than five hundred princes and officials; the court and city shook, and none dared meet his gaze. Famine gripped the city; dozens of high officials died of hunger. Lin entered Zhongshan at the head of the Dingling, executed Xiang and over three hundred of his kin and followers, and again declared himself emperor. Starvation wracked Zhongshan; Lin moved to Xinshi and fought Wei at Yitai, where he was beaten. Wei entered Zhongshan; Lin fled to Ye.
10
慕容德遣侍郎李延勸寶南伐，寶大悅，慕容盛切諫，以為兵疲師老，魏新平中原，宜養兵觀釁，更俟他年。 寶將從之。 撫軍慕輿騰進曰：「今眾旅已集，宜乘新定之機以成進取之功。 人可使由之，而難與圖始，惟當獨決聖慮，不足廣采異同，以沮亂軍議也。 」寶曰：「吾計決矣，敢諫者斬！ 」寶發龍城，以慕輿騰為前軍大司馬，慕容農為中軍，寶為後軍，步騎三萬，次於乙連。 長上段速骨、宋赤眉因眾軍之憚役也，殺司空、樂浪王宙，逼立高陽王崇。 寶單騎奔農，仍引軍討速骨。 眾咸憚征幸亂，投杖奔之。 騰眾亦潰，寶、農馳還龍城。 蘭汗潛與速骨通謀，速骨進師攻城，農為蘭汗所譎，潛出赴賊，為速骨所殺。 眾皆奔散，寶與慕容盛、慕輿騰等南奔。 蘭汗奉太子策承制，遣使迎寶，及於薊城。 寶欲還北，盛等咸以汗之忠款虛實未明，今單馬而還，汗有貳志者，悔之無及。 寶從之，乃自薊而南。 至黎陽，聞慕容德稱制，懼而退。 遣慕輿騰招集散兵于钜鹿，慕容盛結豪桀于冀州，段儀、段溫收部曲于內黃，眾皆響會，克期將集。 會蘭汗遣左將軍蘇超迎寶，寶以汗垂之季舅，盛又汗之壻也，必謂忠款無貳，乃還至龍城。 汗引寶入于外邸，弑之，時年四十四，在位三年，即隆安三年也。 汗又殺其太子策及王公卿士百餘人。 汗自稱大都督、大將軍、大單于、昌黎王。 盛僭位，偽諡寶惠湣皇帝，廟號烈宗。
Murong De's envoy Li Yan urged a southern campaign; Bao was delighted, but Sheng argued fiercely that the army was exhausted, Wei had just secured the north, and they should rest, watch for weakness, and wait another year. Bao was inclined to agree. Commissioner Murong Teng pressed him: "The army is assembled; we must strike now while the enemy is still settling and win our victory." "Soldiers can be led but not easily persuaded at the outset; only your own resolve should decide—do not canvass every opinion and muddy the council of war." Bao said: "My mind is made up; anyone who objects dies!" Bao marched from Longcheng: Teng commanded the vanguard as Grand Marshal, Nong the center, Bao the rear—thirty thousand men camped at Yilian. Senior troops Duan Sugui and Song Chimei, resenting forced service, murdered Minister Zhou, Prince of Lelang, and forced Chong, Prince of Gaoyang, onto the throne. Bao rode alone to Nong and turned to crush Sugui. The soldiers, dreading another march into chaos, threw down their arms and joined the mutineers. Teng's division broke as well; Bao and Nong raced back to Longcheng. Lan Han plotted with Sugui; Sugui besieged the city; Nong, tricked by Lan Han, slipped out to the rebels and was killed by Sugui. The army dissolved; Bao fled south with Sheng, Teng, and their party. Lan Han installed Ce as regent and sent envoys to receive Bao; they met at Ji. Bao wanted to go north; Sheng and the rest argued that Han's loyalty was unproved and that returning alone would be fatal if Han turned traitor. Bao took their advice and marched south from Ji. At Liyang he learned that Murong De had taken power and withdrew in alarm. He sent Teng to rally stragglers at Julu, Sheng to raise champions in Ji Province, and Duan Yi and Duan Wen to muster their bands at Neihuang; supporters gathered and set a date to rise. Lan Han then sent General Su Chao to escort him; trusting Han as Chui's brother-in-law and Sheng as Han's son-in-law, Bao believed him loyal and returned to Longcheng. Han received Bao at an outer lodge and murdered him; he was forty-four, had ruled three years—in the third year of Long'an. Han also slew Crown Prince Ce and more than a hundred princes and officials. Han proclaimed himself Grand Commander, Grand General, Grand Chanyu, and Prince of Changli. After seizing power, Sheng posthumously honored Bao as Emperor Hui Min, temple name Liezong.
11
皝之遷于龍城也，植松為社主。 及秦滅燕，大風吹拔之。 後數年，社處忽有桑二根生焉。 先是，遼川無桑，及廆通于晉，求種江南，平州桑悉由吳來。 廆終而垂以吳王中興，寶之將敗，大風又拔其一。
When Murong Huang moved the capital to Longcheng, he planted pines as the altar's sacred trees. When Qin conquered Yan, a violent wind tore them up. Years later, two mulberry shoots appeared where the altar had stood. Liaochuan had once had no mulberry; when Huang allied with Jin he imported seed from the south, and Ping Province's mulberry all came from Wu. Huang's death coincided with Chui's revival of Wu; as Bao's fall approached, another great wind uprooted one of the trees.

李旱自遼西還，聞盛殺其將衛雙，懼，棄軍奔走。 既而歸罪，復其爵位。 盛謂侍中孫勍曰：「旱總三軍之任，荷專征之重，不能杖節死綏，無故逃亡，考之軍正，不赦之罪也。 然當先帝之避難，眾情離貳，骨肉忘其親，股肱失忠節，旱以刑餘之體，效力盡命，忠款之至，精貫白日。 朕故錄其忘身之功，免其丘山之罪耳。」
Returning from Liaoxi, Li Han learned Sheng had executed his officer Wei Shuang; terrified, he deserted his command. He later confessed guilt and was restored to rank. Sheng told Sun Qin: "Han commanded three armies with full authority yet fled instead of dying at his post—by military law that is unpardonable." "Yet in our father's exile, when kin turned traitor and loyalty failed, Han—a man once condemned—fought to the death with loyalty bright as the sun." "I pardon his desertion for the sake of that selfless service."
20
盛去皇帝之號，稱庶人大王。
Sheng laid aside the imperial title and called himself Great King of the Common People.
21
魏襲幽州，執刺史盧溥而去。 遣孟廣平援之，無及。
Wei raided You Province, captured Governor Lu Pu, and carried him off. He sent Meng Guangping to the rescue, but too late.
22
盛率眾三萬伐高句驪，襲其新城、南蘇，皆克之，散其積聚，徙其五千餘戶於遼西。
Sheng marched thirty thousand men against Koguryŏ, took Xincheng and Nansu, burned their stores, and relocated five thousand households to Liaoxi.
23
盛引見百遼於東堂，考詳器藝，超拔者十有二人。 命百司舉文武之士才堪佐世者各一人。 立其子遼西公定為太子，大赦殊死已下。 宴其群臣於新昌殿，盛曰：「諸卿各言其志，朕將覽之。 」七兵尚書丁信年十五，盛之舅子也，進曰：「在上不驕，高而不危，臣之願也。 」盛笑曰：「丁尚書年少，安得長者之言乎！ 」盛以威嚴馭下，驕暴少親，多所猜忌，故信言及之。
In the Eastern Hall Sheng examined Liaodong officials and promoted twelve outstanding men. He ordered every ministry to recommend one civil or military man fit to serve the state. He named his son Ding, Duke of Liaoxi, crown prince and granted amnesty short of capital crimes. Feasting at Xinchang Hall, Sheng said: "Tell me your ambitions; I will hear them." Ding Xin, fifteen, Sheng's nephew, said: "To stand high without pride and avoid danger—that is my wish." Sheng laughed: "Young Minister Ding, where did you learn an old man's counsel?" Sheng ruled with harsh majesty, trusted few, and suspected many—hence Xin's careful words.
24
盛討庫莫奚，大虜獲而還。 左將軍慕容國與殿中將軍秦輿、段贊等謀率禁兵襲盛，事覺，誅之，死者五百餘人。 前將軍、思悔侯段璣、輿子興、贊子泰等，因眾心動搖，夜於禁中鼓躁大呼。 盛聞變，率左右出戰，眾皆披潰。 俄而有一賊從暗中擊傷盛，遂輦升前殿，申約禁衛，召叔父河間公熙屬以後事。 熙未至而盛死，時年二十九，在位三年。 偽諡昭武皇帝，墓號興平陵，廟號中宗。
Sheng campaigned against the Kumo Xi and returned laden with captives. General Guo, Qin Yu, Duan Zan, and others plotted a palace coup; discovered, they were executed along with over five hundred men. Duan Ji, Xing, and Tai, sensing unrest, drummed and shouted inside the palace at night. Hearing the uproar, Sheng rushed out with his guard; the rebels scattered. A hidden assailant wounded him; bearers carried him to the front hall, where he secured the guard and summoned his uncle Xi, Duke of Hejian, to entrust the succession. Xi had not yet arrived when Sheng died, aged twenty-nine, in the third year of his reign. He was posthumously titled Emperor Zhaowu, buried at Xingping Mausoleum, temple name Zhongzong.
25
盛幼而羈賤流漂，長則遭家多難，夷險安危，備嘗之矣。 懲寶暗而不斷，遂峻機威刑，織芥之嫌，莫不裁之於未萌，防之於未兆。 於是上下振局，人不自安，雖忠誠親戚亦皆離貳，舊臣靡不夷滅，安忍無親，所以卒於不免。 是歲隆安五年也。
Sheng knew exile in youth and endless family calamity in manhood—he had tasted every fortune and misfortune. Correcting Bao's blindness and hesitation, he tightened law and punishment, crushing every slight before it could grow. Court and country shook; even loyal kin turned away and old servants perished; his cruelty estranged all, and he could not escape his fate. This occurred in the fifth year of Long'an.
26
熙字道文，垂之少子也。 初封河間王。 段速骨之難，諸王多被其害，熙素為高陽王崇所親愛，故得免焉。 蘭汗之篡也，以熙為遼東公，備宗祀之義。 盛初即位，降爵為公，拜都督中外諸軍事、驃騎大將軍、尚書左僕射，領中領軍。 從征高句驪、契丹，皆勇冠諸將。 盛曰：「叔父雄果英壯，有世祖之風，但弘略不如耳。」
Xi, courtesy name Daowen, was Chui's youngest son. He was first made Prince of Hejian. When Sugui rebelled many princes died; Xi survived because Chong of Gaoyang favored him. Lan Han's usurpation made Xi Duke of Liaodong to keep the ancestral rites alive. At Sheng's accession Xi was reduced to duke yet made supreme commander, Flying Cavalry Grand General, Left Vice Director, and Central Commandant. In campaigns against Koguryŏ and the Khitan his bravery outshone every general. Sheng said: "Uncle is bold and fierce like our founder, though less gifted in grand strategy."
27
及盛死，其太后丁氏以國多難，宜立長君。 群望皆在平原西元，而丁氏意在於熙，遂廢太子定，迎熙入宮。 群臣勸進，熙以讓元，元固以讓熙，熙遂僭即尊位。 誅其大臣段璣、秦興等，並夷三族。 元以嫌疑賜死。 元字道光，寶之第四子也。 赦殊死已下，改元曰光始，改北燕台為大單于台，置左右輔，位次尚書。
After Sheng's death Empress Dowager Ding argued that troubled times required an elder ruler. The people favored Yuan of Pingyuan, but Lady Ding wanted Xi; they deposed Crown Prince Ding and brought Xi to the throne. Ministers pressed Xi to reign; he and Yuan exchanged refusals until Xi took the throne. He executed Duan Ji, Qin Xing, and others and wiped out three generations of their kin. Yuan was forced to suicide on suspicion. Yuan, courtesy name Daoguang, was Bao's fourth son. He granted amnesty, proclaimed the era Guangshi, renamed the Northern Yan Terrace the Grand Chanyu Terrace, and created Left and Right Assistants ranking below the ministers.
28
初，熙烝于丁氏，故為所立。 及寵倖苻貴人，丁氏怨恚呪詛，與兄子七兵尚書信謀廢熙。 熙聞之，大怒，逼丁氏令自殺，葬以後禮，誅丁信。
Xi had lain with Lady Ding, who therefore raised him to the throne. When he doted on Lady Fu, Lady Ding cursed him and plotted with her nephew Xin to depose him. Xi forced Lady Ding to suicide, buried her as an empress, and executed Xin.
29
熙狩于北原，石城令高和殺司隸校尉張顯，閉門距熙。 熙率騎馳返，和眾皆投杖，熙入誅之。 於是引見州郡及單于八部耆舊於東宮，問以疾苦。
Hunting on the northern plain, Xi found Magistrate Gao He had killed Director Zhang Xian and shut Shicheng against him. Xi galloped back; He's men dropped their weapons; Xi entered and killed him. He then received provincial elders and tribal chiefs in the Eastern Palace and asked after their hardships.
30
大築龍騰苑，廣袤十餘里，役徒二萬人。 起景雲山于苑內，基廣五百步，峰高十七丈。 又起逍遙宮、甘露殿，連房數百，觀閣相交。 鑿天河渠，引水入宮。 又為其昭儀苻氏鑿曲光海、清涼池。 季夏盛暑，士卒不得休息，暍死者太半。 熙游于城南，止大柳樹下，若有人呼曰：「大王且止。 。 」熙惡之，伐其樹，乃有蛇長丈餘，從樹中而出。
He built Longteng Garden, more than ten li across, with twenty thousand laborers. Inside the garden he raised Jingyun Mountain, five hundred paces at the base and seventeen zhang high. He built Xiaoyao Palace and Ganlu Hall—hundreds of linked chambers and tiered towers. He dug the Tianhe Canal to bring water into the palace. For Lady Fu he excavated Quguang Sea and Qingliang Pool. In midsummer heat the laborers could not rest; more than half died of sunstroke. Touring south of the city, Xi halted under a great willow; a voice seemed to call: "Great King, stop here." The voice fell silent. Enraged, Xi felled the tree; a serpent over ten feet long slithered out.
31
立其貴嬪苻氏為皇后，赦殊死已下。
He made Lady Fu empress and granted amnesty short of capital crimes.
32
熙北襲契丹，大破之。
Xi raided the Khitan northward and crushed them.
33
昭儀苻氏死，偽諡湣皇后。 贈苻謨太宰，諡文獻公。 二苻並美而豔，好微行遊宴，熙弗之禁也。 請謁必從，刑賞大政無不由之。 初，昭儀有疾，龍城人王溫稱能療之，未幾而卒，熙忿其妄也，立於公車門支解溫而焚之。 其後好游田，熙從之，北登白鹿山，東過青嶺，南臨滄海，百姓苦之，士卒為豺狼所害及凍死者五千餘人矣。 會高句驪寇燕郡，殺略百餘人。 熙伐高句驪，以苻氏從，為沖車地道以攻遼東。 熙曰：「待剗平寇城，朕當與後乘輦而入，不聽將士先登。 」於是城內嚴備，攻之不能下。 會大雨雪，士卒多死，乃引歸。
When Lady Fu died she was posthumously titled Empress Min. He posthumously honored Fu Mo as Grand Preceptor, Duke of Literature and Offerings. Both Fu ladies were beautiful and loved secret outings; Xi never restrained them. He accompanied them everywhere; punishments, rewards, and major policy all passed through them. When Lady Fu fell ill, Wang Wen of Longcheng claimed to heal her; she died; Xi had him dismembered at the public gate and burned. They then hunted endlessly—north to Bailu Mountain, east through Qingling, south to the sea—five thousand soldiers died of wolves or cold while the people groaned. Koguryŏ raided Yan commandery and killed or captured over a hundred people. Xi marched on Koguryŏ with the Fu ladies, using siege towers and tunnels against Liaodong. Xi said: "After the city falls I will enter in the rear carriage—no soldier may climb the wall before me." The city stood firm; the assault failed. Blizzards killed many soldiers; Xi withdrew.
34
擬鄴之鳳陽門，作弘光門，累級三層。
Modeled on Ye's Fengyang Gate, he built the Hongguang Gate in three tiers.
35
熙與苻氏襲契丹，憚其眾盛，將還，苻氏弗聽，遂棄輜重，輕襲高句驪，周行三千餘里，士馬疲凍，死者屬路。 攻木底城，不克而還。
Xi and the Fu ladies raided the Khitan; when he wished to turn back they refused; abandoning supplies he struck Koguryŏ in a three-thousand-li circuit—men and horses froze dead along the road. He failed to take Mudi and withdrew.
36
盡殺寶諸子。 大城肥如及宿軍，以仇尼倪為鎮東大將軍、營州刺史，鎮宿軍，上庸公懿為鎮西將軍、幽州刺史，鎮令支； 尚書劉木為鎮南大將軍、冀州刺史，鎮肥如。
He slaughtered all of Bao's sons. He fortified Feiru and Sugun, stationed Qiu Ni Ni at Sugun as Grand General Who Pacifies the East, and Yi of Shangyong at Lingzhi as Grand General Who Pacifies the West; Minister Liu Mu guarded Feiru as Grand General Who Pacifies the South and Governor of Ji.
37
為苻氏起承華殿，高承光一倍，負土於北門，土與穀同價。 典軍杜靜載棺詣闕，上書極諫。 熙大怒，斬之。 苻氏嘗季夏思凍魚膾，仲冬須生地黃，皆下有司切責，不得，加以大辟，其虐也如此。 苻氏死，熙悲號躃踴，若喪考妣，擁其屍而撫之曰：「體已就冷，命遂斷矣！ 」於是僵僕氣絕，久而乃蘇。 大斂既訖，復啟其棺而與交接。 服斬縗，食粥。 制百僚于宮內哭臨，令沙門素服。 使有司案檢哭者，有淚以為忠孝，無則罪之，於是群臣震懼，莫不含辛以為淚焉。 慕容隆妻張氏，熙之嫂也，美姿容，有巧思。 熙將以為苻氏之殉，欲以罪殺之，乃毀其禭靴，中有弊氈，遂賜死。 三女叩頭求哀，熙不許。 制公卿已下至於百姓，率戶營墓，費殫府藏。 下錮三泉，周輸數里，內則圖畫尚書八坐之象。 熙曰：「善為之，朕將隨後入此陵。 」識者以為不祥。 其右僕射韋璆等並懼為殉，沐浴而待死焉。 號苻氏墓曰征平陵。 熙被髮徒跣，步從苻氏喪。 轜車高大，毀北門而出。 長老竊相謂曰：「慕容氏自毀其門，將不久也。」
For Lady Fu he built Chenghua Hall, twice as tall as Chengguang, hauling earth at the north gate until soil cost as much as grain. Director of Troops Du Jing brought a coffin to court and memorialized in desperate protest. Xi had him beheaded in rage. Lady Fu once demanded chilled fish in summer and fresh rehmannia in winter; officials who failed were executed—such was her cruelty. When Lady Fu died Xi wailed like a bereaved son, clutched her corpse, and cried: "Your body is cold—your life is gone!" He collapsed breathless and long afterward revived. After the funeral he opened the coffin and lay with her corpse. He wore deepest mourning and ate only gruel. He forced officials to mourn in the palace and monks to wear white. Inspectors punished mourners without tears; terrified, the court faked grief with stinging eyes. Zhang, wife of Murong Long and Xi's sister-in-law, was beautiful and clever. Xi meant to bury Zhang with Lady Fu; he ruined her funeral boots, found worn felt inside, and condemned her to death. Her three daughters begged on their knees; Xi refused mercy. From princes to commoners he ordered every household to build tombs until the treasury was drained. He sank three springs in a tomb several li around, painting the eight ministers of state within. Xi said: "Build it well—I will enter this tomb after you." Wise men took this as an ill omen. Vice Director Wei Qiu and others, fearing burial sacrifice, bathed and awaited death. He named Lady Fu's tomb Zhengping Mausoleum. Xi followed Lady Fu's bier barefoot with hair unbound. The towering funeral carriage smashed the north gate on its way out. Elders whispered: "The Murong house destroys its own gate—the end is near."
38
中衛將軍馮跋、左衛將軍張興，先皆坐事亡奔，以熙政之虐也，與跋從兄萬泥等二十二人結盟，推慕容雲為主，發尚方徒五千餘人閉門距守。 中黃門趙洛生奔告之，熙曰：「此鼠盜耳，朕還當誅之。 」乃收發貫甲，馳還赴難。 夜至龍城，攻北門不克，遂敗，走入龍騰宛，微服隱于林中，為人所執，雲得而弑之，及其諸子同殯城北。 時年二十三，在位六年。 雲葬之於苻氏墓，偽諡昭文皇帝。
Feng Ba and Zhang Xing, once punished and fled, with Wan Ni and twenty-two others made Murong Yun their leader and shut the gates with five thousand imperial convicts. Attendant Zhao Luosheng brought word; Xi said: "Mere rats—I'll kill them when I return." He armed and galloped back to the crisis. By night he reached Longcheng, failed at the north gate, fled to Longteng Garden, hid in the woods in disguise, was seized, and Yun killed him; he and his sons were buried north of the city. He was twenty-three and had ruled six years. Yun buried him beside Lady Fu and posthumously titled him Emperor Zhaowen.
39
垂以孝武帝太元八年僭立，至熙四世，凡二十四年，以安帝義熙三年滅。 初，童謠曰：「一束槁，兩頭然，禿頭小兒來滅燕。 」槁字上有草，下有禾，兩頭然則禾草俱盡而成高字。 雲父名拔，小字禿頭，三子，而雲季也。 熙竟為雲所滅，如謠言焉。
Chui seized power in Taiyuan year 8 of Emperor Xiaowu; four generations later, twenty-four years on, the state fell in Yixi year 3 of Emperor An. A children's rhyme had said: "One bundle of straw, both ends burning—the bald-headed boy will destroy Yan." "The character gao has grass atop and grain below; burn both ends and grass and grain vanish, leaving gao." Yun's father Ba, nicknamed Bald Head, had three sons; Yun was the youngest. Xi was destroyed by Yun, just as the rhyme foretold.
40
慕容雲，字子雨，寶之養子也。 祖父和，高句驪之支庶，自雲高陽氏之苗裔，故以高為氏焉。 雲沈深有局量，厚重希言，時人咸以為愚，唯馮跋奇其志度而友之。 寶之為太子，雲以武藝給事侍東宮，拜侍御郎，襲敗慕容會軍。 寶子之，賜姓慕容氏，封夕陽公。
Murong Yun, courtesy name Ziyu, was Bao's adopted son. His grandfather He came from a Koguryŏ collateral line; Yun claimed Gaoyang descent and took Gao as his surname. Yun was deep, reserved, and sparing of words; most thought him dull—only Feng Ba saw his ambition and befriended him. As Bao's heir, Yun served in the Eastern Palace, became Attendant Lang, and defeated Murong Hui's army. Bao adopted him, gave him the Murong surname, and enfeoffed him Duke of Xiyang.
41
熙之葬苻氏也，馮跋詣雲，告之以謀。 雲懼曰：「吾嬰疾歷年，卿等所知，願更圖之。 」跋逼曰：「慕容氏世衰，河間虐暴，惑妖淫之女而逆亂天常，百姓不堪其害，思亂者十室九焉，此天亡之時也。 公自高氏名家，何能為他養子！ 機運難邀，千歲一時，公焉得辭也！ 」扶之而出。 雲曰：「吾疾苦日久，廢絕世務。 卿今興建大事，謬見推逼。 所以徘徊，非為身也，實惟否德不足以濟元元故耳。 」跋等強之，雲遂即天王位，復姓高氏，大赦境內殊死以下，改元曰正始，國號大燕。 署馮跋侍中、都督中外諸軍事、征北大將軍、開府儀同三司、錄尚書事、武邑公，封伯、子、男，鄉、亭侯者五十餘人，士卒賜穀帛有差。 熙之群官，復其爵位。 立妻李氏為天王後，子彭為太子。 越騎校尉慕輿良謀叛，雲誅之。
At Lady Fu's funeral Feng Ba revealed the plot to Yun. Yun pleaded: "I have been ill for years, as you know—choose another leader." Ba pressed him: "The Murong house decays; Xi is cruel and led astray by a witch—nine households in ten crave rebellion; heaven itself destroys them." "You are of the noble Gao clan—why remain another man's adopted son!" "Fortune comes once in a thousand years—you cannot refuse!" They lifted him and led him out. Yun said: "My illness is long; I have left the world behind." "You raise a great cause and wrongly force me forward." "I hesitate not for myself but because my virtue cannot save the people." Forced by Ba and the rest, Yun took the title Heavenly King, resumed the Gao surname, proclaimed amnesty, adopted the era Zhengshi, and named the state Great Yan. He made Feng Ba Attendant-in-Ordinary, supreme commander, Grand General Who Pacifies the North, Director of the Masters of Writing, and Duke of Wuyi; he enfeoffed over fifty nobles and rewarded the soldiers with grain and cloth. He restored the ranks of Xi's former officials. He made Lady Li Heavenly Queen and his son Peng crown prince. Colonel Murong Liang plotted treason; Yun executed him.
42
雲臨東堂，幸臣離班、桃仁懷劍執紙而入，稱有所啟，拔劍擊雲，雲以幾距班，桃仁進而弑之。 馮跋遷雲屍於東宮，偽諡惠懿皇帝。 雲自以無功德而為豪桀所推，常內懷懼，故寵養壯士以為腹心。 離班、桃仁等並專典禁衛，委之以爪牙之任，賞賜月至數千萬，衣食臥起皆與之同，終以此致敗雲。
At the Eastern Hall favored ministers Li Ban and Tao Ren entered with swords, struck Yun—he blocked Ban with a table, but Tao Ren stabbed him dead. Feng Ba moved Yun's body to the Eastern Palace and posthumously titled him Emperor Huiyi. Yun, knowing he lacked merit yet was thrust forward by heroes, lived in fear and pampered strongmen as his personal guard. Li Ban, Tao Ren, and others controlled the palace guard, received millions monthly, shared Yun's table and bed—and in the end destroyed him.
43
史臣曰：四星東聚，金陵之氣已分； 五馬南浮，玉塞之雄方擾。 市朝屢改，艱虞靡息。 慕容垂天資英傑，威震本朝，以雄略見猜而庇身寬政，永固受之而以禮，道明事之而畢力。 然而隼質難羈，狼心自野。 淮南失律，三甥之謀已構； 河朔分麾，五木之祥雲啟。 斬飛龍而遐舉，逾石門而長邁，遂使翟氏景從，鄴師宵逸，收羅趙、魏，驅駕英雄。 叩囊餘奇，摧五萬于河曲； 浮船秘策，招七郡于黎陽。 返遼陰之舊物，創中山之新社，類帝禋宗，僭擬斯備。 夫以重耳歸晉，賴五臣之功； 句踐紿吳，資五千之卒。 惡有業殊二霸，眾微一旅，掎拔而傾山嶽，騰嘯而禦風雲！ 雖衛人忘亡復傳于東國，任好余裕伊愧於西鄰，信苻氏之奸回，非晉室之鯨鯢矣。
The historian writes: Four stars gathered in the east; Jin's mandate was already divided; five horses drifted south; the north's champions were in turmoil. Thrones shifted endlessly; hardship never ceased. Murong Chui was heaven-born, awe-inspiring at court; suspected for his genius, he survived under lenient rule while Yonggu honored him and Daoming served him fully. Yet hawk nature cannot be tethered; wolf heart runs wild. Huainan lost order; the three nephews' plot took shape; north of the Yellow River armies split; the omen of the Five Mulberries rose. He struck down danger and marched far, crossed Shimen in long advance; the Di followed, Ye's armies fled by night; he gathered Zhao and Wei and drove heroes before him. With hidden stratagems he shattered fifty thousand at the river bend; with secret fleets he rallied seven commanderies at Liyang. He restored Liaoyin's lands, founded Zhongshan, mimicked imperial rites—usurpation was complete. Chong'er regained Jin through five loyal ministers; Goujian fooled Wu with five thousand men. Yet with a force unlike the two hegemons—one small host—he shook mountains and rode the wind! Though Wey survived in the east and Duke Mu shamed the west, Fu's treachery was no whale devouring Jin.
44
寶以浮譽獲升，峻文禦俗，蕭牆內憤，勍敵外陵，雖毒不被物而惡足自剿。 盛則孝友冥符，文武不墜，韜光而夷仇賊，罪己而遜高危，翩翩然濁世之佳虜矣。 熙乃地非奧主，舉因淫德。 驪戎之態，取悅于匡床； 玄妻之姿，見奇於鬒發。 蕩輕舟于曲光之海，望朝涉于景雲之山，飾土木於驕心，窮怨嗟於蕞壤，宗祀夷滅，為馮氏之驅除焉。
Bao rose on empty fame and ruled with harsh law; palace fury and foreign invasion destroyed him though his cruelty spared nothing else. Sheng united filial piety and friendship, preserved civil and martial order, hid his light to crush foes, blamed himself in peril—the finest man of a dark age. Xi was no destined ruler; he rose wholly through debauchery. Like Li Rong he pleased on the couch; like Xuan's wife he was charmed by dark hair. He sailed Quguang's sea, gazed from Jingyun's heights, built palaces to feed pride and drained the land with grief—the ancestral line died, swept away for the Feng clan.
45
贊曰：戎狄憑陵，山川沸騰。 天未悔禍，人非與能。 疾走而捷，先鳴則興。 道明烈烈，鞭笞豪傑。 掃燕夷魏，釗屠永滅。 大盜潛移，鴻名遂竊。 寶心生亂，盛清家難。 熙極驕淫，人懷憤惋。 孽貽身咎，災無以逭。
The encomium says: Barbarians leaned on arms; rivers and mountains seethed. Heaven had not yet ended the scourge; no man could master fate. Run fast, strike first—who moves first, thrives. Daoming blazed forth, lashing the heroes. He swept Yan, subdued Wei, cut down foes to extinction. The great thief shifted in secret; the great name was stolen. Bao's heart bred chaos; Sheng cleared the house of danger. Xi reached the height of arrogant lust; men burned with resentment. Sin clung to his person; disaster could not be fled.
