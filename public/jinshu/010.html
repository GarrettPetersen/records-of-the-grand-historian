1
安皇帝諱德宗，字德宗，孝武帝長子也。 太元十二年八月辛巳，立爲皇太子。 二十一年九月庚申，孝武帝崩。 辛酉，太子卽皇帝位，大赦。 癸亥，以司徒、會稽王道子爲太傅，攝政。 冬十月甲申。 葬孝武皇帝于隆平陵。 大雪。
Emperor An, whose personal name was Dezong and whose courtesy name was likewise Dezong, was the eldest son of Emperor Xiaowu. In the twelfth year of Taiyuan, on xinsi day in the eighth month, he was invested as heir apparent. On gengshen day in the ninth month of the twenty-first year of Taiyuan, Emperor Xiaowu passed away. On xinyou day the crown prince ascended the throne, and a general amnesty was proclaimed. On guihai day Sima Daozi—who held the posts of Minister of Education and Prince of Kuaiji—was named Grand Tutor and assumed the regency. That winter, in the tenth month, on jiashen day, Emperor Xiaowu was interred at the Longping imperial tomb. There was heavy snowfall.
2
隆安元年春正月己亥朔，帝加元服，改元，增文武位一等。 太傅、會稽王道子稽首歸政。 以尚書右僕射王珣爲尚書令，領軍將軍王國寶爲尚書左僕射。
In the first month of spring in the first year of Long'an, on new year's day jihai, the emperor came of age, the reign title was changed, and every civil and military rank was advanced one step. Grand Tutor Sima Daozi, Prince of Kuaiji, formally surrendered the reins of government. Wang Xun advanced from Right Vice Director of the Secretariat to Director of the Secretariat, while Wang Guobao—General Who Guards the Army—was appointed Left Vice Director of the Secretariat.
3
二月，呂光將禿髮烏孤自稱大都督、大單于，國號南涼。 擊光將竇苟于金昌，大破之。 甲寅，尊皇太后李氏爲皇太后。 戊午，立皇后王氏。
In the second month Tufa Wugu, who served under Lü Guang, proclaimed himself Grand Commander-in-Chief and Grand Chanyu and adopted the dynastic title Southern Liang. He struck Dou Gou, one of Lü Guang's commanders, at Jinchang and routed him. On jiayin day Lady Li was formally recognized as Empress Dowager. On wuwu day Lady Wang was enthroned as empress.
4
三月，呂光子纂爲乞伏乾歸所敗。 光建康太守段業自號涼州牧。 慕容寶敗魏師于薊。
In the third month Lü Zuan, Lü Guang's son, suffered defeat at the hands of Qifu Gangui. Duan Ye, whom Lü Guang had appointed prefect of Jiankang, proclaimed himself Governor of Liangzhou. Murong Bao turned back the Wei army at Ji.
5
夏四月甲戌，兗州刺史王恭，豫州刺史庾楷舉兵，以討尚書左僕射王國寶、建威將軍王緒爲名。 甲申，殺國寶及緒以悅于恭，恭乃罷兵。 戊子，大赦。
In summer, the fourth month, on jiaxu day, Wang Gong of Yanzhou and Yu Kai of Yuzhou took up arms on the pretext of punishing Wang Guobao, Left Vice Director of the Secretariat, and Wang Xu, General Who Establishes Might. On jiashen day Wang Guobao and Wang Xu were put to death to placate Wang Gong, who thereupon stood his army down. On wuzi day a general amnesty was proclaimed.
6
五月，前司徒長史王廞以吳郡反，王恭討平之。 慕容寶將慕容詳僭卽皇帝位于中山，寶奔黃龍。
In the fifth month Wang Yin, erstwhile chief clerk to the Minister of Education, rose in Wu commandery; Wang Gong marched against him and crushed the revolt. Murong Xiang, a commander under Murong Bao, seized the throne at Zhongshan in defiance of legitimate succession, while Murong Bao withdrew toward Huanglong.
7
秋八月，呂光爲其僕射楊軌、散騎常侍郭黁所攻，光子纂擊走之。
In autumn, the eighth month, Vice Director Yang Gui and Attendant Cavalry Gentleman Guo Nen turned against Lü Guang; Lü Zuan, Lü Guang's son, counterattacked and put them to flight.
8
九月，慕容寶將慕容麟斬慕容祥于中山，因僭卽皇帝位。
In the ninth month Murong Lin, serving Murong Bao, executed Murong Xiang at Zhongshan and then proclaimed himself emperor.
9
冬十月，慕容麟爲魏師所敗。
That winter, in the tenth month, Wei troops defeated Murong Lin.
10
二年春三月，龍舟二災。
In the third month of spring in the second year of Long'an, fire or mishap struck two imperial dragon boats.
11
夏五月，蘭汗弑慕容寶而自稱大將軍、昌黎王。
In summer, the fifth month, Lan Han assassinated Murong Bao and assumed the titles Grand General and Prince of Changli.
12
秋七月，慕容寶子盛斬蘭汗，僭稱長樂王，攝天子位。 兗州刺史王恭、豫州刺史庾楷、荊州刺史殷仲堪、廣州刺史桓玄、南蠻校尉楊佺期等舉兵反。
In autumn, the seventh month, Murong Sheng, Murong Bao's son, killed Lan Han, took the title Prince of Changle without imperial sanction, and exercised imperial authority. Wang Gong of Yanzhou, Yu Kai of Yuzhou, Yin Zhongkan of Jingzhou, Huan Xuan of Guangzhou, Yang Quanqi as Colonel of the Southern Barbarians, and others rose in arms.
13
八月，江州刺史王愉奔于臨川。 丙子，寧朔將軍鄧啓方及慕容德將慕容法戰于管城，王師敗績。 丙戌，慕容盛僭卽皇帝位于黃龍。 桓玄大敗王師于白石。
In the eighth month Wang Yu, governor of Jiangzhou, escaped to Linchuan. On bingzi day Deng Qifang, General Who Pacifies the North, and Murong Fa, under Murong De, clashed at Guancheng; the court army was routed. On bingxu day Murong Sheng declared himself emperor at Huanglong. Huan Xuan shattered the imperial forces at Baishi.
14
九月辛卯，加太傅、會稽王道子黃鉞。 遣征虜將軍會稽王世子元顯、前將軍王珣、右將軍謝琰討桓玄等。 己亥，破庾楷于牛渚。 丙午，會稽王道子屯中堂，元顯守石頭。 己酉，前將軍王珣守北郊，右將軍謝琰備宣陽門。 輔國將軍劉牢之次新亭，使子敬宣擊敗恭，恭奔曲阿長塘湖，湖尉收送京師，斬之。 於是遣太常殷茂喻仲堪及玄，玄等走于尋陽。
On xinmao day in the ninth month Grand Tutor Sima Daozi, Prince of Kuaiji, was invested with the ceremonial yellow axe—full authority to execute. The court sent Sima Yuanxian—the Prince of Kuaiji's heir and General Who Subdues the Caitiffs—along with Former General Wang Xun and Right General Xie Yan to campaign against Huan Xuan and his allies. On jihai day government troops defeated Yu Kai at Niuzhu. On bingwu day Sima Daozi, Prince of Kuaiji, took station in the Central Hall while Sima Yuanxian held Stone Fortress. On jiyou day Former General Wang Xun covered the northern approaches while Right General Xie Yan manned Xuanyang Gate. General Who Supports the State Liu Laozhi camped at Xinting and dispatched his son Liu Jingxuan against Wang Gong. Gong fled to Changtang Lake near Qu'e; the local lake officer captured him and shipped him to the capital, where he was executed. The court then sent Yin Mao, Minister of Ceremonies, to reason with Yin Zhongkan and Huan Xuan; Huan Xuan and his allies withdrew toward Xunyang.
15
冬十月，新野言騶虞見。 丙子，大赦。 壬午，仲堪等盟于尋陽，推桓玄爲盟主。
That winter, in the tenth month, Xinye reported the sighting of a zouyu—a mythical beast taken as an auspicious omen. On bingzi day the throne proclaimed a general amnesty. On renwu day Yin Zhongkan and his confederates swore a pact at Xunyang and named Huan Xuan leader of the alliance.
16
十一月，以琅邪王德文爲衛將軍、開府儀同三司，領軍將軍王雅爲尚書左僕射。
In the eleventh month Sima Dewen, Prince of Langye, became General Who Guards the Army with his own administrative establishment and Three Elders-level honors; Wang Ya, General Who Leads the Army, was named Left Vice Director of the Secretariat.
17
十二月己丑，魏王珪卽尊位，年號天興。 京兆人韋禮帥襄陽流人叛，降于姚興。 己酉，前新安太守杜炯反于京口，會稽王世子元顯討斬之。 禿髮烏孤自稱武威王。
On jichou day in the twelfth month Tuoba Gui of Wei assumed supreme authority and adopted the reign title Tianxing. Wei Li of Jingzhao rallied displaced settlers from Xiangyang in revolt and went over to Yao Xing. On jiyou day Du Jiong, formerly prefect of Xin'an, mutinied at Jingkou; Sima Yuanxian, heir of the Prince of Kuaiji, crushed the rising and executed him. Tufa Wugu proclaimed himself Prince of Wuwei.
18
三年春正月辛酉，封宗室蘊爲淮陵王。
In the first month of spring in the third year of Long'an, on xinyou day, Sima Yun of the imperial clan was invested as Prince of Huailing.
19
二月甲辰，河間王國鎮薨。 林邑范胡達陷日南、九真，遂寇交阯，太守杜瑗討破之。 段業自稱涼王。 仇池公楊盛遣使稱籓，獻方物。
On jiachen day in the second month Sima Guozhen, Prince of Hejian, passed away. Fan Huda of Linyi overran Rinan and Jiuzhen and pressed on toward Jiaozhi; Prefect Du Yuan struck back and broke his army. Duan Ye declared himself King of Liang. Yang Sheng, lord of Chouchi, dispatched ambassadors to acknowledge tributary status and offer regional tribute.
20
三月己卯，追尊所生陳夫人爲德皇太后。
On jimao day in the third month the emperor's birth mother, Lady Chen, was posthumously titled Virtuous Empress Dowager.
21
夏四月乙未，加尚書令王珣衛將軍，以會稽王世子元顯爲揚州刺史。
On yiwei day in summer's fourth month Wang Xun added the title General Who Guards the Army to his role as Director of the Secretariat, and Sima Yuanxian, heir of the Prince of Kuaiji, was appointed inspector of Yangzhou.
22
六月戊子，以琅邪王德文爲司徒。 慕容德陷青州，害龍驤將軍辟閭渾，遂僭卽皇帝位于廣固。
On wuzi day in the sixth month Sima Dewen, Prince of Langye, became Minister of Education. Murong De overran Qingzhou and slew Pil Hun, General Who Quickens as a Dragon, then proclaimed himself emperor at Guanggu.
23
秋八月，禿髮烏孤死，其弟利鹿孤嗣偽位。
In autumn, the eighth month, Tufa Wugu died and his brother Tufa Lilugu inherited his unauthorized throne.
24
冬十月，姚興陷洛陽，執河南太守辛恭靖。
That winter, in the tenth month, Yao Xing captured Luoyang and took Xin Gongjing, prefect of Henan, prisoner.
25
十一月甲寅，妖賊孫恩陷會稽，內史王凝之死之，吳國內史桓謙、臨海太守新蔡王崇、義興太守魏隱並委官而遁，吳興太守謝邈、永嘉太守司馬逸皆遇害。 遣衛將軍謝琰、輔國將軍劉牢之逆擊，走之。
On jiayin day in the eleventh month the rebel Sun En overran Kuaiji; Wang Ningzhi, the interior secretary, perished. Huan Qian of Wu commandery, Prince Chong of Xin Cai at Linhai, and Wei Yin at Yixing abandoned their posts and fled, while Xie Miao of Wuxing and Sima Yi of Yongjia were slain. The court sent General Who Guards the Army Xie Yan and General Who Supports the State Liu Laozhi to meet Sun En in battle and forced him to retreat.
26
十二月，桓玄襲江陵，荊州刺史殷仲堪、南蠻校尉楊佺期並遇害。 呂光立其太子紹爲天王，自號太上皇。 是日，光死，呂纂弑紹而自立。
In the twelfth month Huan Xuan struck Jiangling; Yin Zhongkan, governor of Jingzhou, and Yang Quanqi, Colonel of the Southern Barbarians, were both killed. Lü Guang installed his heir Lü Shao as Heavenly King while claiming for himself the title Grand Supreme Emperor. The same day Lü Guang died; Lü Zuan assassinated Lü Shao and seized power.
27
是歲，荊州大水，平地三丈。
That year catastrophic floods struck Jingzhou, with standing water three zhang deep even on the flats.
28
四年春正月乙亥，大赦。
In the first month of spring in the fourth year of Long'an, on yihai day, the court issued a general amnesty.
29
二月己丑，有星孛于奎婁，進至紫微。
On jichou day in the second month a cometary apparition appeared between the Kui and Lou lunar lodges and swept onward into the Ziwei enclosure.
30
三月，彗星見于太微。
In the third month observers sighted a comet within the Taiwei asterism.
31
夏四月，地震。 孫恩寇浹口。
In summer, the fourth month, the earth shook. Sun En attacked the Jiakou narrows.
32
五月丙寅，散騎常侍、衛將軍、東亭侯王珣卒。 己卯，會稽內史謝琰爲孫恩所敗，死之。 恩轉寇臨海。
On bingyin day in the fifth month Wang Xun—Attendant Cavalry Gentleman, General Who Guards the Army, and Marquis of Dongting—died. On jimao day Xie Yan, interior secretary of Kuaiji, fell to Sun En and was killed. Sun En redirected his forces and ravaged Linhai.
33
六月庚辰朔，日有蝕之。 旱。 輔國司馬劉裕破恩於南山。 恩將盧循陷廣陵，死者三千餘人。 以琅邪王師何澄爲尚書左僕射。
On new moon gengchen in the sixth month a solar eclipse occurred. A severe drought set in. Liu Yu, army major on the staff of the general who supports the state, defeated Sun En at Nanshan. Lu Xun, a commander under Sun En, took Guangling at a cost of more than three thousand lives. He Cheng, tutor to the Prince of Langye, was appointed Left Vice Director of the Secretariat.
34
秋七月壬子，太皇太后李氏崩。 丁卯，大赦。 是月，姚興伐乞伏乾歸，降之。
On renzi day in autumn's seventh month Grand Empress Dowager Lady Li died. On dingmao day the throne proclaimed a general amnesty. That same month Yao Xing campaigned against Qifu Gangui, who submitted.
35
八月丁亥，尚書右僕射王雅卒。 壬寅，葬文太后于脩平陵。
On dinghai day in the eighth month Wang Ya, Right Vice Director of the Secretariat, died. On renyin day Dowager Wen was interred at the Xiuping tomb.
36
九月癸丑，地震。
On guichou day in the ninth month another earthquake struck.
37
冬十一月，寧朔將軍高雅之及孫恩戰於餘姚，王師敗績。 以揚州刺史元顯爲後將軍、開府儀同三司、都督揚豫徐兗青幽冀幷荊江司雍梁益交廣十六州諸軍事，前將軍劉牢之爲鎮北將軍，封元顯子彥璋爲東海王。
That winter, in the eleventh month, Gao Yazhi, General Who Pacifies the North, fought Sun En at Yuyao and the imperial troops were routed. Sima Yuanxian, inspector of Yangzhou, was promoted to Rear General with Three Elders honors and an independent command covering sixteen provinces; Former General Liu Laozhi became General Who Guards the North; Yuanxian's son Yanzhang received the title Prince of Donghai.
38
十二月戊寅，有星孛于天市。
On wuyin day in the twelfth month a cometary tail appeared in the Celestial Market asterism.
39
是歲，河右諸郡奉涼武昭王李玄盛爲秦涼二州牧、涼公，年號庚子。
That year the Hexi region rallied behind Li Xuansheng—honored posthumously as Martial Illustrious King of Liang—as governor of Qin and Liang and Duke of Liang, inaugurating the reign title Gengzi.
40
五年春二月丙子，孫恩復寇浹口。 呂超殺呂纂，以其兄隆僭卽偽位。
In the second month of spring in the fifth year of Long'an, on bingzi day, Sun En struck Jiakou once more. Lü Chao assassinated Lü Zuan and elevated his brother Lü Long to the illegitimate throne.
41
三月甲寅，衆星西流，歷太微。
On jiayin day in the third month a meteor shower swept westward across the Taiwei asterism.
42
夏五月，孫恩寇𣺣瀆，吳國內史袁山松死之。 沮渠蒙遜殺段業，自號大都督、北涼州牧。
In summer's fifth month Sun En assaulted the Hudu narrows on the coast near modern Shanghai; Yuan Shansong, interior secretary of Wu commandery, died defending them. Juqu Mengxun slew Duan Ye and proclaimed himself Grand Commander-in-Chief and governor of northern Liangzhou.
43
六月甲戌，孫恩至丹徒。 乙亥，內外戒嚴，百官入居于省。 冠軍將軍高素、右衛將軍張崇之守石頭，輔國將軍劉襲柵斷淮口，丹陽尹司馬恢之戍南岸，冠軍將軍桓謙、輔國將軍司馬允之、遊擊將軍毛邃備白石，左衛將軍王嘏、領軍將軍孔安國屯中皇堂。 徵豫州刺史、譙王尚之衛京師。 寧朔將軍高雅之擊孫恩于廣陵之郁洲，爲賊所執。
On jiaxu day in the sixth month Sun En's fleet reached Dantu. On yihai day the capital region went on full alert and civil officials billeted themselves inside the palace compounds. Gao Su, General Who Champions Might, and Zhang Chongzhi, Right Guards general, held Stone Fortress; Liu Xi, General Who Supports the State, threw up barriers athwart the Huai ford; Sima Huizhi, governor of Danyang, watched the southern shore; Huan Qian, Sima Yunzhi, and Mao Sui covered Baishi; Wang Jia and Kong Anguo—Left Guards and leader of the army—deployed at Zhonghuang Hall. Prince Sima Shangzhi of Qiao, inspector of Yuzhou, was recalled to defend the capital. Gao Yazhi, General Who Pacifies the North, fought Sun En off the Yuzhou islands near Guangling but was captured.
44
秋七月，段璣殺慕容盛，盛叔父熙盡誅段氏，因僭稱尊號。
In autumn's seventh month Duan Ji assassinated Murong Sheng; Murong Xi, Murong Sheng's uncle, extirpated the Duan house and seized the throne.
45
九月，呂隆降于姚興。
In the ninth month Lü Long submitted to Yao Xing.
46
冬十月，姚興帥師侵魏，大敗而旋。
That winter, in the tenth month, Yao Xing invaded Wei but was routed and withdrew.
47
是歲，饑，禁酒。
Famine struck that year, and the court banned the sale and consumption of wine.
48
元興元年春正月庚午朔，大赦，改元。 以後將軍元顯爲驃騎大將軍、征討大都督，鎮北將軍劉牢之爲元顯前鋒，前將軍、譙王尚之爲後部，以討桓玄。
On new year's day gengwu in the first month of spring of the first Yuanxing year the throne proclaimed a general amnesty and adopted the new reign title. Sima Yuanxian was promoted from Rear General to General Who Inspires Awe and supreme commander of the punitive expedition; Liu Laozhi, General Who Guards the North, led his van; Prince Sima Shangzhi of Qiao, Former General, covered the rear—all aimed at crushing Huan Xuan.
49
二月丙午，帝戎服餞元顯于西池。 丁巳，遣兼侍中、齊王柔之以騶虞幡宣告荊、江二州。 丁卯，桓玄敗王師于姑孰，譙王尚之、齊王柔之並死之。 以右將軍吳隱之爲都督交廣二州諸軍事、廣州刺史。
On bingwu day in the second month the emperor, dressed for campaign, held a farewell banquet for Sima Yuanxian at West Pond. On dingsi day Prince Sima Rouzhi of Qi—also holding Palace Attendant rank—was sent with the peace-bearing zouyu banner to publish the imperial summons in Jingzhou and Jiangzhou. On dingmao day Huan Xuan shattered the imperial army at Gushu; Prince Sima Shangzhi of Qiao and Prince Sima Rouzhi of Qi fell in the fighting. Wu Yinzhi, Right General, received joint command of Guangzhou and Jiaozhou as well as the Guangzhou governorship.
50
三月己巳，劉牢之叛降于桓玄。 辛未，王師敗績于新亭，驃騎大將軍、會稽王世子元顯，東海王彥璋，冠軍將軍毛泰，遊擊將軍毛邃並遇害。 壬申，桓玄自爲侍中、丞相、錄尚書事，以桓謙爲尚書僕射，遷太傅、會稽王道子于安城。 玄俄又自稱太尉、揚州牧，總百揆，以琅邪王德文爲太宰。
On jisi day in the third month Liu Laozhi defected and went over to Huan Xuan. On xinwei day government troops were routed at Xinting; Sima Yuanxian—General Who Inspires Awe and heir of the Prince of Kuaiji—Prince Yanzhang of Donghai, Mao Tai (General Who Champions Might), and Mao Sui (Roaming Strike) were all killed. On renshen day Huan Xuan seized the offices of Palace Attendant, Chancellor, and overseer of the Secretariat; he installed Huan Qian as Vice Director of the Secretariat and banished Grand Tutor Sima Daozi, Prince of Kuaiji, to Ancheng. Huan Xuan shortly assumed the titles Grand Commandant and governor of Yangzhou, taking charge of every branch of government, while naming Sima Dewen, Prince of Langye, Grand Minister.
51
臨海太守辛景擊孫恩，斬之。 是月，禿髮利鹿孤死，弟辱檀嗣偽位。
Xin Jing, prefect of Linhai, caught Sun En and executed him. That same month Tufa Lilugu died and his brother Tufa Rutan inherited his self-proclaimed throne.
52
秋七月乙亥，新蔡王崇爲其奴所害。
On yihai day in autumn's seventh month Prince Sima Chong of Xin Cai was murdered by a household slave.
53
八月庚子，尚書下舍災。
On gengzi day in the eighth month fire destroyed the lower Secretariat offices.
54
冬十月，冀州刺史劉軌叛奔于慕容德。
That winter, in the tenth month, Liu Gui of Jizhou defected and took refuge with Murong De.
55
十二月庚申，會稽王道子爲桓玄所害。 曲赦廣陵、彭城大逆以下。
On gengshen day in the twelfth month Huan Xuan had Sima Daozi, Prince of Kuaiji, murdered. The throne issued a limited amnesty for Guangling and Pengcheng, pardoning offenses short of the gravest treason.
56
二年春二月辛丑，建威將軍劉裕破徐道覆于東陽。 乙卯，桓玄自稱大將軍。 丁巳，冀州刺史孫無終爲桓玄所害。
On xinchou day in the second month of spring in the second year of Yuanxing, Liu Yu, General Who Establishes Might, routed Xu Daofu at Dongyang. On yimao day Huan Xuan assumed the title Grand General. On dingsi day Huan Xuan executed Sun Wuzhong, governor of Jizhou.
57
夏四月癸巳朔，日有蝕之。
On new moon guisi in the fourth month of summer a solar eclipse occurred.
58
秋八月，玄又自號相國、楚王。
In autumn, the eighth month, Huan Xuan added the titles Chancellor of State and King of Chu.
59
九月，南陽太守庾仄起義兵，爲玄所敗。
In the ninth month Yu Ze, prefect of Nanyang, mustered loyalist forces but Huan Xuan defeated them.
60
冬十一月壬午，玄遷帝于永安宮。 癸未，移太廟神主于琅邪國。
On renwu day in winter's eleventh month Huan Xuan removed the emperor to confinement in Yong'an Palace. On guiwei day the ancestral tablets were transferred to the princedom of Langye.
61
十二月壬辰，玄篡位，以帝爲平固王。 辛亥，帝蒙塵于尋陽。
On renchen day in the twelfth month Huan Xuan seized the throne and demoted the sovereign to Prince of Pinggu. On xinhai day the dethroned emperor was taken in humiliation to Xunyang.
62
三年春二月，帝在尋陽。 庚寅夜，濤水入石頭，漂殺人戶。 乙卯，建武將軍劉裕帥沛國劉毅、東海何無忌等舉義兵。 丙辰，斬桓玄所署徐州刺史桓修于京口，青州刺史桓弘于廣陵。 丁巳，義師濟江。
In the second month of spring in the third year of Huanxuan's usurpation the emperor remained at Xunyang. On the night of gengyin a storm surge poured into Stone Fortress, sweeping away lives and homes. On yimao day Liu Yu, General Who Establishes Martial Might, together with Liu Yi of Pei, He Wuji of Donghai, and others launched the loyalist uprising. On bingchen day the loyalists executed Huan Xiu—Huan Xuan's appointee as governor of Xuzhou—at Jingkou and slew Huan Hong, governor of Qingzhou, at Guangling. On dingsi day the loyalist host crossed the Great River.
63
三月戊午，劉裕斬玄將吳甫之于江乘，斬皇甫敷於羅落。 己未，玄衆潰而逃。 庚申，劉裕置留臺，具百官。 壬戌，桓玄司徒王謐推劉裕行鎮軍將軍、徐州刺史、都督揚徐兗豫青冀幽幷八州諸軍事、假節。 劉裕以謐領揚州刺史、錄尚書事。 辛酉，劉裕誅尚書左僕射王愉、愉子荊州刺史綏、司州刺史溫詳。 辛未，桓玄逼帝西上。 丙戌，密詔以幽逼於玄，萬機虛曠，令武陵王遵依舊典，承制總百官行事，加侍中，餘如故。 幷大赦謀反大逆己下，惟桓玄一祖之後不宥。
On wuwu day in the third month Liu Yu cut down Huan Xuan's officer Wu Fuzhi at Jiangcheng and killed Huangfu Fu at Luoluo. On jiwei day Huan Xuan's army collapsed in flight. On gengshen day Liu Yu set up a caretaker government at Jiankang with a full slate of ministers. On renxu day Wang Mi—still formally Minister of Education under Huan Xuan's regime—nominated Liu Yu as acting General Who Garrisons the Army, governor of Xuzhou, and commander of eight provinces with plenipotentiary credentials. Liu Yu left Wang Mi in charge of Yangzhou and of daily Secretariat business. On xinyou day Liu Yu put to death Wang Yu, Left Vice Director of the Secretariat, together with Wang Sui—his son and governor of Jingzhou—and Wen Xiang, governor of Sizhou. On xinwei day Huan Xuan marched the captive emperor upriver toward the west. On bingxu day a clandestine edict explained that the emperor, isolated under Huan Xuan's grip, could not rule; it authorized Prince Sima Zun of Wuling to exercise imperial authority over the bureaucracy according to precedent, added him as Palace Attendant, and left his other titles unchanged. A sweeping amnesty covered every offense short of the gravest rebellion—except for Huan Xuan's immediate patriline, who received no pardon.
64
夏四月己丑，大將軍、武陵王遵稱制，總萬機。 庚寅，帝至江陵。 庚戌，輔國將軍何無忌、振武將軍劉道規及桓玄將庾稚、何澹之戰于湓口，大破之。 玄復逼帝東下。
On jichou day in summer's fourth month Prince Sima Zun of Wuling—already Grand General—began ruling in the emperor's name and directed all branches of government. On gengyin day the emperor reached Jiangling. On gengxu day He Wuji (General Who Supports the State) and Liu Daogui (General Who Shakes Might) met Yu Zhi and He Danzhi—Huan Xuan's commanders—at Penkou and crushed them. Huan Xuan once more compelled the emperor to sail downriver toward the east.
65
五月癸酉，冠軍將軍劉毅及桓玄戰于崢嶸洲，又破之。 己卯，帝復幸江陵。 辛巳，荊州別駕王康產、南郡太守王騰之奉帝居于南郡。 壬午，督護馮遷斬桓玄於貊盤洲。 乘輿反正于江陵。 甲申，詔曰：「姦凶篡逆，自古有之。 朕不能式遏杜漸，以致播越。 賴鎮軍將軍裕英略奮發，忠勇絕世，冠軍將軍毅等誠心宿著，協助同嘉謀。 義聲旣振，士庶效節，社稷載安，四海齊慶。 其大赦，凡諸畏逼事屈逆命者，一無所問。 」戊寅，奉神主人于太廟。
On guiyou day in the fifth month Liu Yi, General Who Champions Might, fought Huan Xuan at Zhengrong Isle and defeated him again. On jimao day the emperor once more reached Jiangling. On xinsi day Wang Kangchan, assistant governor of Jingzhou, and Wang Tengzhi, prefect of Nan commandery, sheltered the emperor within Nan commandery. On renwu day Feng Qian, army supervisor, slew Huan Xuan on Mopan Isle. The sovereign's party was restored at Jiangling. On jiashen day an edict proclaimed: "Treason and usurpation by villains are as old as government itself. We failed to stamp out the evil while it was small, and so disaster swept the throne into exile. Liu Yu, General Who Garrisons the Army, struck with brilliant strategy and devotion beyond compare; Liu Yi, General Who Champions Might, together with others whose loyalty had long been proven, shared in every wise counsel. When their righteous cause resounded, gentlemen and commoners rallied as one, the altars stood secure again, and all within the four seas rejoiced. Let there be a general amnesty: none who acted under duress or obeyed the usurpers' orders shall be prosecuted. " On wuyin day the ancestral tablets were carried back into the Grand Temple.
66
閏月己丑，桓玄故將揚武將軍桓振陷江陵，劉毅、何無忌退守尋陽，帝復蒙塵于賊營。
On jichou day of the intercalary month Huan Zhen—once Huan Xuan's officer, now General Who Displays Might—took Jiangling; Liu Yi and He Wuji fell back to Xunyang, and the emperor fell once more into rebel hands.
67
六月，益州刺史毛璩討偽梁州刺史桓希，斬之。
In the sixth month Mao Qu of Yizhou campaigned against Huan Xi—the pretender's governor of Liangzhou—and executed him.
68
秋七月戊申，永安皇后何氏崩。
On wushen day in autumn's seventh month Empress He of Yong'an passed away.
69
八月癸酉，祔葬穆帝章皇后于永平陵。
On guiyou day in the eighth month Empress Zhang—consort of Emperor Mu—was laid beside him in the Yongping imperial tomb.
70
九月，前給事中刁騁、秘書丞王邁之謀反，伏誅。
In the ninth month Diao Cheng, erstwhile Gentleman for Discussion, and Wang Maizhi, Assistant Secretary, were executed for plotting rebellion.
71
冬十月，盧循寇廣州，刺史吳隱之爲循所敗。 執始興相阮腆之而還。
That winter, in the tenth month, Lu Xun attacked Guangzhou and routed Wu Yinzhi, its governor. They captured Ruan Tianzhi, prefect of Shixing, and withdrew.
72
慕容德死，兄子超嗣偽位。
Murong De died, and his nephew Murong Chao inherited his self-declared throne.
73
義熙元年春正月，帝在江陵。 南陽太守魯宗之起義兵，襲破襄陽。 己丑，劉毅次于馬頭。 桓振以帝屯于江津。 辛卯，宗之破振將溫楷于柞溪，進次紀南，爲振所敗。 振武將軍劉道規擊桓謙，走之。 乘輿反正，帝與琅邪王幸道規舟。 戊戌，詔曰：「朕以寡德，夙纂洪緒。 不能緝熙遐邇，式遏姦宄。 逆臣桓玄乘釁肆亂，乃誣罔天人，篡據極位。 朕躬播越，淪胥荒裔，宣皇之基，眇焉以墜。 賴鎮軍將軍裕忠武英斷，誠冠終古。 運謀機始，貞賢協其契； 抆淚誓衆，義士感其心。 故霜戈一揮。 巨猾奔迸，三率棱威，大憝授首。 而孽振倡狂，嗣凶荊郢。 幸天祚社稷，義旗載捷，狡徒沮潰，朕獲反正。 斯實宗廟之靈，勤王之勳。 豈朕一人，獨享伊祜，思與億兆，幸茲更始。 其大赦，改元，唯玄振一祖及同黨不在原例。 賜百官爵二級，鰥寡孤獨穀人五斛，大酺五日。」
In the first month of spring in the first year of Yixi the emperor remained at Jiangling. Lu Zongzhi, prefect of Nanyang, raised loyalist forces and stormed Xiangyang. On jichou day Liu Yi encamped at Matou. Huan Zhen held the emperor under guard at the Jiangjin ford. On xinmao day Lu Zongzhi routed Wen Kai—one of Huan Zhen's officers—at Zuoxi and pressed south to Jinan, south of the old Chu capital, where Huan Zhen defeated him. Liu Daogui, General Who Shakes Might, struck Huan Qian and put him to flight. The emperor's escort was restored; the sovereign and Sima Dewen, Prince of Langye, took refuge aboard Liu Daogui's ship. On wuxu day an edict read: "We possess slender virtue yet inherited the great mandate young. We failed to harmonize the realm or choke off treason before it spread. The traitor Huan Xuan seized on unrest to wreak havoc, deceiving both heaven and men while stealing the throne. We were driven into exile at the empire's edge; the patrimony of Emperor Xuan seemed ready to collapse. Through Liu Yu—General Who Garrisons the Army—whose loyalty, courage, and resolve outshine every age, who framed strategy from the first stirrings of rebellion while steadfast allies answered his summons, who wiped away tears as he pledged his army and stirred every loyal heart, therefore his icy blades swept forth. Great villains scattered in panic; allied hosts drove terror home; the arch traitor lost his head. Yet Huan Zhen—a bastard pretender—raged anew and prolonged havoc across Jingzhou. Fortune favored the altars: the loyalists routed every crafty foe, and We were restored. This owed everything to the ancestral shades and to those who marched for the throne. How could We hoard heaven's blessing? We mean to share this fresh dawn with every household. Let there be a general amnesty and a new reign title—except for Huan Xuan's and Huan Zhen's kin and their accomplices, who remain outside the pardon. Advance every official two noble ranks, grant widowers, widows, orphans, and the destitute five hu of grain per head, and authorize five days of public feasting."
74
二月丁巳，留臺備乘輿法駕，迎帝于江陵。 弘農太守戴寧之、建威主簿徐惠子等謀反，伏誅。 平西參軍譙縱害平西將軍、益州刺史毛璩，以蜀叛。
On dingsi day in the second month the Jiankang caretaker court dispatched the full imperial train to escort the emperor home from Jiangling. Dai Ningzhi of Hongnong and Xu Huizi—chief clerk to the general who establishes might—were executed for plotting rebellion. Qiao Zong, adjutant on the western pacification staff, assassinated Mao Qu—General Who Pacifies the West and governor of Yizhou—and rose in rebellion from Shu.
75
三月，桓振復襲江陵，荊州刺史司馬休之奔于襄陽。 建威將軍劉懷肅討振，斬之。 帝至自江陵。 乙未，百官詣闕請罪。 詔曰：「此非諸卿之過，其還率職。 」戊戌，舉章皇后哀三日，臨于西堂。 劉裕及何無忌等抗表遜位，不許。 庚子，以琅邪王德文爲大司馬，武陵王遵爲太保，加鎮軍將軍劉裕爲侍中、車騎將軍、都督中外諸軍事。 甲辰，詔曰：「自頃國難之後，人物凋殘，常所供奉，猶不改舊，豈所以視人如傷，禹湯歸過之誡哉！ 可籌量減省。」
In the third month Huan Zhen struck Jiangling again; Sima Xiuzhi, governor of Jingzhou, fled toward Xiangyang. Liu Huaisu, General Who Establishes Might, brought Huan Zhen to bay and executed him. The emperor returned from Jiangling. On yiwei day the civil service gathered at the palace gate to humble themselves before the throne. An edict assured them: "You bear no blame; resume your offices." On wuxu day the court observed three days of mourning for Empress Zhang, with vigil held in the western hall. Liu Yu, He Wuji, and others memorialized to resign their posts; the emperor refused. On gengzi day Sima Dewen, Prince of Langye, became Grand Marshal; Prince Sima Zun of Wuling became Grand Guardian; and Liu Yu added Palace Attendant, General of Chariots and Cavalry, and supreme command over every army at court and in the field. On jiachen day an edict declared: "After the realm's recent calamity the people lie in ruins, yet court expenditure remains unchanged—hardly the compassion Yu and Tang showed when they blamed themselves for every hurt in the land." Review every budget and cut what can be cut."
76
夏四月，劉裕旋鎮京口。 戊辰，餞于東堂。
In summer, the fourth month, Liu Yu withdrew to his headquarters at Jingkou. On wuchen day the court held a farewell banquet for him in the Eastern Hall.
77
五月癸未，禁絹扇及摴蒲。 遊擊將軍、章武王秀，益州刺史司馬軌之謀反，伏誅。 桓玄故將桓亮、苻宏、刁預寇湘州，守將擊走之。
On guiwei day in the fifth month the throne banned silk fans and the game of chupu. Sima Xiu—Roaming Strike general and Prince of Zhangwu—and Sima Guizhi, governor of Yizhou, were executed for plotting rebellion. Huan Liang, Fu Hong, and Diao Yu—former officers of Huan Xuan—invaded Xiangzhou until local commanders beat them back.
78
秋八月甲子，封臨川王子脩之爲會稽王。
On jiazi day in autumn's eighth month Sima Xiuzhi, Prince of Linchuan's son, received the title Prince of Kuaiji.
79
冬十一月，乞付乾歸伐仇池，仇池公楊盛大破之。
That winter, in the eleventh month, Qifu Gangui struck Chouchi, but Yang Sheng, lord of Chouchi, shattered his army.
80
是歲，涼武昭王玄盛遣使奉表稱籓。
That year Li Xuansheng—honored as Martial Illustrious King of Liang—sent tribute and acknowledged Jin as suzerain.
81
二年春正月，益州刺史司馬榮期擊譙縱將譙子明于白帝，破之。
In the first month of spring in the second Yixi year Sima Rongqi, governor of Yizhou, defeated Qiao Ziming—one of Qiao Zong's generals—at Baidi.
82
夏五月，封高密王子法蓮爲高陽王。
In summer's fifth month Sima Falian, Prince of Gaomi's son, became Prince of Gaoyang.
83
秋七月，梁州刺史楊孜敬有罪，伏誅。
In autumn, the seventh month, Yang Zijing, governor of Liangzhou, was executed for his crimes.
84
冬十月，論匡復之功，封車騎將軍劉裕爲豫章郡公，撫軍將軍劉毅南平郡公，右將軍何無忌安成郡公，自餘封賞各有差。 乙亥，以左將軍孔安國爲尚書左僕射。
That winter, in the tenth month, the court rewarded those who had restored the dynasty: Liu Yu became Duke of Yuzhang, Liu Yi Duke of Nanping, He Wuji Duke of Ancheng, while lesser honors followed merit. On yihai day Kong Anguo, Left General, was appointed Left Vice Director of the Secretariat.
85
十二月，盜殺零陵太守阮野。
In the twelfth month bandits murdered Ruan Ye, prefect of Lingling.
86
三年春二月己酉，車騎將軍劉裕來朝。 誅東陽太守殷仲文、南蠻校尉殷叔文、晉陵太守殷道叔、永嘉太守駱球。 己丑，大赦，除酒禁。
On jiyou day in the second month of spring in the third Yixi year Liu Yu arrived at court from his command. The court executed Yin Zhongwen of Dongyang, Yin Shuwen as Colonel of the Southern Barbarians, Yin Daoshu of the Jinling commandery, and Luo Qiu of Yongjia. On jichou day the throne proclaimed a general amnesty and lifted the ban on wine.
87
夏五月，大水。
In summer, the fifth month, catastrophic floods struck.
88
六月，姚興將赫連勃勃僭稱天王于朔方，國號夏。
In the sixth month Helian Bobo, serving under Yao Xing, proclaimed himself Heavenly King at Shuofang and founded the Xia state.
89
秋七月戊戌朔，日有蝕之。 汝南王遵之有罪，伏誅。
On new moon wuxu in autumn's seventh month a solar eclipse occurred. Prince Sima Youzhi of Runan was executed for his offenses.
90
八月，遣冠軍將軍劉敬宣持節監征蜀諸軍事。
In the eighth month Liu Jingxuan, General Who Champions Might, received the commission and tally to oversee the western campaign against Shu.
91
冬十一月，赫連勃勃大敗禿髮傉檀，傉檀奔于南山。
That winter Helian Bobo shattered Tufa Nutan's army, driving him into the southern hills.
92
是歲，高雲、馮跋殺慕容熙，雲僭卽帝位。
That year Gao Yun and Feng Ba assassinated Murong Xi; Gao Yun then seized the throne of Yan.
93
四年春正月甲辰，以琅邪王德文領司徒，車騎將軍劉裕爲揚州刺史、錄尚書事。 庚申，侍中、太保、武陵王遵薨。
On jiachen day in the first month of spring Sima Dewen, Prince of Langye, added the Ministry of Education to his duties; Liu Yu became governor of Yangzhou and overseer of the Secretariat. On gengshen day Prince Sima Zun of Wuling—Palace Attendant and Grand Guardian—passed away.
94
夏四月，散騎常侍、尚書左僕射孔安國卒。 甲午，加吏部尚書孟昶尚書左僕射。
In summer's fourth month Kong Anguo—Attendant Cavalry and Left Vice Director of the Secretariat—died. On jiawu day Meng Chang, head of the Ministry of Personnel, became Left Vice Director of the Secretariat.
95
冬十一月辛卯，雷。 梁州刺史楊思平有罪，棄市。 癸丑，大風拔樹。 是月，禿髮傉檀僭卽涼王位。
On xinmao day in winter's eleventh month thunder rolled. Yang Siping, governor of Liangzhou, was executed in the marketplace for his crimes. On guichou day a gale uprooted trees. That same month Tufa Nutan proclaimed himself King of Liang.
96
五年春正月辛卯，大赦。 庚戌，以撫軍將軍劉毅爲衛將軍、開府儀同三司，加輔國將軍何無忌鎮南將軍。 庚戌，尋陽地震。
On xinmao day in the first month of spring in the fifth Yixi year the court proclaimed a general amnesty. On gengxu day Liu Yi advanced to General Who Guards the Army with Three Elders honors and his own establishment; He Wuji added the title General Who Garrisons the South. On gengxu day the earth shook at Xunyang.
97
二月，慕容超將慕容興宗寇宿豫，陽平太守劉千載、南陽太守趙元並爲賊所執。
In the second month Murong Xingzong—under Murong Chao—struck Suyu and captured Liu Qianzai of Yangping and Zhao Yuan of Nanyang.
98
三月己亥，大雪，平地數尺。 車騎將軍劉裕帥師伐慕容超。 夏六月丙寅，震于太廟。 劉裕大破慕容超于臨朐。
On jihai day in the third month snow piled several feet deep on the flats. Liu Yu, General of Chariots and Cavalry, took the field against Murong Chao. On bingyin day in summer's sixth month lightning struck the ancestral temple. Liu Yu shattered Murong Chao's army at Linqu.
99
秋七月，姚興將乞伏乾歸僭稱西秦王于苑川。
In autumn, the seventh month, Qifu Gangui—under Yao Xing—proclaimed himself King of Western Qin at Yuanchuan.
100
九月戊辰，離班弑高雲，雲將馮跋攻班，殺之。 跋僭卽王位，仍號燕。
On wuchen day in the ninth month Li Ban assassinated Gao Yun; Feng Ba, Yun's general, turned on Li Ban and slew him. Feng Ba seized the throne of Yan and kept the dynastic name.
101
冬十月，魏清河王紹弑其主珪。
That winter Tuoba Shao, Prince of Qinghe, murdered Emperor Gui of Wei.
102
六年春正月丁亥，劉裕攻慕容超，克之，齊地悉平。 是月，廣州刺史盧循反，寇江州。
On dinghai day in the first month of spring in the sixth Yixi year Liu Yu took Murong Chao's last stronghold and brought the entire Qi region under control. That same month Lu Xun, governor of Guangzhou, rose in revolt and struck Jiangzhou.
103
三月，禿髮傉檀及沮渠蒙遜戰于窮泉，傉檀敗績。 壬申，鎮南將軍、江州刺史何無忌及循戰于豫章，王師敗績，無忌死之。
In the third month Tufa Nutan met Juqu Mengxun at Qiongquan and was routed. On renshen day He Wuji—General Who Garrisons the South and governor of Jiangzhou—fought Lu Xun at Yuzhang; imperial troops were shattered and He Wuji fell.
104
夏四月，青州刺史諸葛長民、兗州刺史劉籓、幷州刺史劉道憐乃入衛京師。
In summer's fourth month Zhuge Changmin of Qingzhou, Liu Fan of Yanzhou, and Liu Daolian of Bingzhou marched to defend the capital.

On yiyou day in winter's tenth month Liu Yu installed his son Liu Yizhen—Duke of Guiyang—as governor of Yangzhou.

十一月丁亥朔，日有蝕之。
On new moon dinghai in the eleventh month a solar eclipse occurred.
169
十二月辛卯，裕加殊禮。 己卯，太史奏，黑龍四見于東方。
On xinmao day in the twelfth month Liu Yu received extraordinary honors. On jimao day the astronomers reported four sightings of black dragons in the eastern sky.
170
二年夏六月壬戌，劉裕至于京師。 傅亮承裕密旨，諷帝禪位，草詔，請帝書之。 帝欣然謂左右曰：「晉氏久已失之，今復何恨。 」乃書赤紙爲詔。 甲子，遂遜于琅邪第。 劉裕以帝爲零陵王，居于秣陵，行晉正朔，車旗服色一如其舊，有其文而不備其禮。 帝自是之後，深慮禍機，褚後常在帝側，飲食所資，皆出褚后，故宋人莫得伺其隟，宋永初二年九月丁丑，裕使後兄叔度請后； 有間，兵人逾垣而入，弑帝于內房。 時年三十六。 諡恭皇帝，葬沖平陵。
On renxu day in summer's sixth month of Yuanxi 2 Liu Yu reached Jiankang. Fu Liang, acting on Liu Yu's orders, urged abdication, drafted the edict, and asked the emperor to copy it in his own hand. The emperor told his attendants willingly: "House Jin forfeited the mandate long ago—why resent yielding it now? " He copied the abdication onto red paper. On jiazi day he abdicated and withdrew to the Langye princely mansion. Liu Yu named him Prince of Lingling and housed him at Moling; Jin calendar and regalia remained in form, but ceremony was hollow. Afterward Emperor Gong lived in dread of assassination; Empress Chu never left his side, and every meal passed through her hands so Liu Yu's agents found no opening. In Yongchu 2 of Song, on dingchou day in the ninth month, Liu Yu had Empress Chu's brother Chu Shudu summon her away; Moments later soldiers scaled the wall and stabbed him to death in his private rooms. He was thirty-six years old. He received the posthumous title Emperor Gong and was buried at Chongping Mausoleum.
171
帝幼時性頗忍急，及在籓國，曾令善射者射馬爲戲。 旣而有人云：「馬者國姓，而自殺之，不祥之甚。 」帝亦悟，甚悔之。 其後復深信浮屠道，鑄貨千萬，造丈六金像，親于瓦官寺迎之，步從十許里。 安帝旣不惠，帝每侍左右，消息溫涼寢食之節，以恭謹聞，時人稱焉。
As a youth he was harsh and hasty; while ruling his princedom he once staged archery contests that targeted horses. Someone warned him: "Horse echoes Sima—the imperial surname—and shooting those beasts yourself is a dreadful omen. " He took the point and bitterly regretted the sport. Thereafter he embraced Buddhism fervently, poured millions of cash into a sixteen-foot gilded statue, and welcomed it in person at Waguan Temple, walking in procession for more than ten li. Because Emperor An was helpless, Emperor Gong stayed constantly at his side, tending every comfort of clothing, food, and sleep—his devotion won praise across the court.
172
始，元帝以丁丑歲稱晉王，置宗廟，使郭璞筮之，云「享二百年。 」自丁丑至禪代之歲，年在庚申，爲一百四歲。 然丁丑始係西晉，庚申終入宋年，所餘惟一百有二歲耳。 璞蓋以百二之期促，故婉而倒之爲二百也。
When Yuan of Jin took the princely title in the dingchou year he founded the temples and had Guo Pu cast the oracle: "The house shall endure two hundred years. " From dingchou to the abdication in gengshen is only one hundred four years. Yet dingchou still belonged to Western Jin, while gengshen fell under Liu Song—the Eastern Jin segment spans only one hundred two years. Guo Pu seems to have rounded up—finding one hundred two years too cramped he politely inverted the digits to "two hundred."
173
史臣曰：安帝卽位之辰，鐘無妄之日，道子、元顯並傾朝政，主昏臣亂，未有如斯不亡者也。 雖有手握戎麾，心存舊國，迴首無良，忽焉蕭散。 於是桓玄乘釁，勢逾飆指，六師咸泯，隻馬徂遷。 是以宋高非典午之臣，孫恩豈金行之寇。 若乃世遇顛覆，則恭皇斯甚。 於越之民，詎熏丹穴，會稽之侶，寧歎入臣。 去皇屋而歸來，灑丹書而不恨。 夫五運攸革，三微數盡，猶高秋彫候，理之自然。 觀其搖落，人有爲之流漣者也。
The historians write: Emperor An took the throne under baleful omens while Sima Daozi and Sima Yuanxian hijacked the government—a witless sovereign and treacherous ministers; never had a dynasty looked less likely to survive. Even commanders who still clutched their batons and cherished Jin looked round for allies and found none; their efforts dissolved overnight. Huan Xuan seized the moment and swept forward faster than a gale; imperial armies melted away until lone riders fled the field. Small wonder Liu Yu—future Emperor Wu of Song—was never truly a servant of House Sima, or that Sun En was scarcely a mere rebel against the Jin element. Among rulers caught in dynastic collapse, none suffered more than Emperor Gong. The folk of Wu and Yue were never destined for barbarian exile; the gentry of Kuaiji hardly regretted submitting when Heaven had shifted the mandate. He stepped down from the purple halls yet kept his honor; though branded by abdication edicts he bore no bitterness. When each cosmic phase ends and the calendar runs out, it is like late autumn stripping the trees—the way of nature. Watching those leaves fall still draws tears from men of feeling.
174
贊曰：安承流湎，大盜斯張。 恭乃寓命，他人是綱。 猶存周赧，始立懷王。 虛尊假號，異術同亡。
The verdict reads: Emperor An drifted in dissipation while mighty usurpers rose unchecked. Emperor Gong held only a borrowed throne; every rein lay in another's hands. Like late Zhou keeping King Nan as figurehead while enthroning King Huai of Chu. Empty titles and borrowed sovereignty—different paths to the same doom.
