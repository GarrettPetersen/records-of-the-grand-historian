1
石季龍[石虎] （上）
Shi Jilong (known as Shi Hu) (Part One)
2
石季龍，勒之從子也，名犯太祖廟諱 〈 （今注：名石虎，与唐高祖李渊之父李虎同名）〉 ，故稱字焉。 祖曰邪，父曰寇覓。 勒父硃幼而子季龍，故或稱勒弟焉。 年六七歲，有善相者曰：「此兒貌奇有壯骨，貴不可言。 」永興中，與勒相失。 後劉琨送勒母王及季龍于葛陂，時年十七矣。 性殘忍，好馳獵，遊蕩無度，尤善彈，數彈人，軍中以為毒患。 勒白王將殺之，王曰：「快牛為犢子時，多能破車，汝當小忍之。 」年十八，稍折節。 身長七尺五寸，趫捷便弓馬，勇冠當時，將佐親戚莫不敬憚，勒深嘉之，拜征虜將軍。 為娉將軍郭榮妹為妻。 季龍寵惑優僮鄭櫻桃而殺郭氏，更納清河崔氏女，櫻桃又譖而殺之。 所為酷虐。 軍中有勇幹策略與己俟者，輒方便害之，前後所殺甚眾。 至於降城陷壘，不復斷別善惡，坑斬士女，鮮有遺類。 勒雖屢加責誘，而行意自若。 然禦眾嚴而不煩，莫敢犯者，指授攻討，所向無前，故勒寵之，信任彌隆，仗以專征之任。
Shi Jilong was a nephew of Shi Le, but his personal name infringed the taboo on the founding emperor's name (Editorial opening bracket preceding an interpolated gloss.) (Editor's note: He was named Shi Hu—the graph matches that in Li Hu, father of Tang founder Li Yuan.)> , so he was commonly known by his courtesy name instead. His grandfather bore the name Ye; his father was Koumi. Because Shi Le's father Zhu took him in as a son in childhood, people sometimes spoke of him as Shi Le's younger brother. When he was six or seven, a fortune-teller declared, "The boy's face is strange and his frame powerful—eminence beyond words awaits him." " In the Yongxing years he was separated from Shi Le. Later Liu Kun escorted Shi Le's mother Lady Wang and Jilong to Gebei; Jilong was seventeen by then. He was vicious by temperament, addicted to the chase and idle roaming, and uncannily good with a sling—he shot men for sport, and the troops deemed him a lethal menace. Shi Le asked Lady Wang's leave to put him to death; she replied, "A mettlesome ox always kicks up trouble as a calf—you must bear with him awhile." " By eighteen he began to curb his ways. He stood seven chi five cun tall, fleet on horseback and deadly with the bow, and no warrior of the day surpassed him; officers and kin alike dreaded him. Shi Le delighted in him, naming him General Who Subjugates the Caitiffs. He married General Guo Rong's sister. Spellbound by the boy actor Zheng Yingtao, he murdered Lady Guo, took a Boling Cui bride, then let Yingtao poison her reputation until she too was executed. His deeds were wanton cruelty. Any officer whose grit or guile rivaled his own soon met with a convenient accident; the tally of his victims ran high. Captured towns brought no mercy: he made no distinction between the innocent and the guilty, slaughtering or entombing civilians until almost none remained. Shi Le scolded and cajoled him repeatedly, yet he would not change. Yet he ruled troops with stern clarity that none dared breach, and every campaign he led swept all before him—so Shi Le cherished him, leaned on him ever more, and gave him independent command in the field.
3
季龍徐州從事硃縱殺刺史郭祥，以彭城歸順。 季龍遣將王朗擊之，縱奔淮南。
Zhu Zong, a clerk in Xuzhou, slew Inspector Guo Xiang and handed Pengcheng over to the Jin. Jilong dispatched Wang Lang against him; Zhu bolted for Huainan.
4
季龍荒遊廢政，多所營繕，使邃省可尚書奏事，選牧守，祀郊廟； 惟征伐刑斷乃親覽之。 觀雀台崩，殺典匠少府任汪。 復使修之，倍于常度。
While Jilong roamed in dissipation and left state affairs idle, he piled up building works and ordered Shi Sui to screen petitions for the Masters of Writing, appoint provincial governors, and conduct suburban and temple rites— reserving for himself alone decisions on war, criminal law, and verdicts. When the Sparrow-Watching Terrace fell, he put Ren Wang, chief of the palace workshops, to death. He commanded its reconstruction at twice the ordinary dimensions.
5
季龍自率眾南寇曆陽，臨江而旋，京師大震。 遣其征虜石遇寇中廬，遂圍平北將軍桓宣于襄陽。 輔國將軍毛寶、南中郎將王國、征西司馬王愆期等率荊州之眾救之，屯于章山。 遇攻守二旬，軍中饑疫而還。
Jilong marched south at the head of an army to pillage Liyang, drew up along the Yangzi, and withdrew—leaving the Jin court trembling. He sent Shi Yu, General Who Subdues the Caitiffs, against Zhonglu and penned Huan Xuan, General Who Pacifies the North, inside Xiangyang. Mao Bao, Wang Guo, Wang Yanqi, and other Jin commanders marched Jingzhou troops to the rescue and camped at Zhang Mountain. Twenty days of fighting left Yu's army starving and plague-stricken, and they fell back.
6
季龍以租入殷廣，轉輸勞煩，令中倉歲入百萬斛，餘皆儲之水次。
With rents pouring in and haulage a headache, Jilong capped central intake at a million hu per year and stockpiled the rest along the waterways.
7
晉將軍淳于安攻其琅邪費縣，俘獲而歸。
The Jin general Chunyu An struck Fei county in Langye, carrying off prisoners on his return.
8
石邃保母劉芝初以巫術進，既養邃，遂有深寵，通賄賂，豫言論，權傾朝廷，親貴多出其門，遂封芝為宜城君。
Liu Zhi, Shi Sui's nurse, had wormed her way in by witchcraft; once she raised the prince she grew irresistible, peddling influence and prophecies until kin and magnates queued at her door—whereupon Sui invested her as Lady of Yicheng.
9
季龍下書令刑贖之家得以錢代財帛，無錢聽以穀麥，皆隨時價輸水次倉。 冀州八郡雨雹，大傷秋稼，下書深自咎責。 遣御史所在發水次倉麥，以給秋種，尤甚之處差復一年。
Jilong ruled that families buying off punishment could pay coin instead of silk, or grain where coin failed, valued at market rates into the waterfront granaries. Eight Jizhou prefectures were hail-struck and the harvest ruined; he published an edict blaming himself. He ordered inspectors to disburse riverside wheat for planting and to waive a year's taxes where damage was severest.
10
季龍將遷于鄴，尚書請太常告廟，季龍曰：「古者將有大事，必告宗廟，而不列社稷。 尚書可詳議以聞。 」公卿乃請使太尉告社稷，從之。 及入鄴宮，澍雨周洽，季龍大悅，赦殊死已下。 尚方令解飛作司南車成，季龍以其構思精微，賜爵關內侯，賞賜甚厚。 始制散騎常侍已上得乘軺軒，王公郊祀乘副車，駕四馬，龍旗八旒，塑望朝會即乘軺軒。
Planning to shift the seat to Ye, the Masters of Writing sought permission for the ritual minister to report at the imperial shrine; Jilong answered, "The old rule was to notify the lineage temple ahead of great undertakings, not the altars of soil and grain." " Have the Masters of Writing debate this thoroughly and report." " The high ministers then asked the Grand Commandant to proclaim the move at those altars, and Jilong agreed. As he took possession of the Ye palace, timely rains soaked the realm; overjoyed, he proclaimed amnesty for all but capital offenses. When Palace Workshop chief Xie Fei delivered a south-pointing chariot, Jilong admired its craftsmanship and made him Marquis Within the Passes with rich gifts. He first decreed that officials from Regular Palace Attendant upward could ride covered gigs; kings and dukes used secondary four-horse cars with eight pennons at suburban rites, but switched to light gigs for dawn audiences.
11
時羌薄句大猶保險未賓，遣其子章武王斌帥精騎二萬，並秦、雍二州兵以討之。
While Qiang leader Bo Gou-da clung to redoubts and withheld allegiance, Jilong dispatched Prince Zhangwu Shi Bin at the head of twenty thousand elite horse and forces from Bing and Yong to reduce him.
12
季龍如長樂、衛國，有田疇不辟、桑業不修者，貶其守宰而還。
Touring Changle and Weiguo, he demoted magistrates whose fields lay fallow or whose silk districts languished, then headed home.
13
咸康二年，使牙門將張彌徙洛陽鐘虡、九龍、翁仲、銅駝、飛廉於鄴。 鐘一沒於河，募浮沒三百人入河，系以竹絙，牛百頭，鹿櫨引之乃出。 造萬斛舟以渡之，以四輪纏輞車，轍廣四尺，深二尺，運至鄴。 季龍大悅，赦二歲刑，賚百官谷帛，百姓爵一級。
In Xiankang 2 he assigned Zhang Mi, colonel of the gates, to haul Luoyang's bell-stands, Nine Dragons, colossi, bronze camels, and whirlwind spirits to Ye. One bell slipped into the Yellow River; three hundred salvagers with bamboo cables and a hundred oxen on capstans wrestled it ashore. Ten-thousand-hu barges carried the freight; four-wheeled, tire-shod carts—four chi between ruts, two chi deep—rolled the treasure into Ye. Elated, he waived two years of convict labor, paid officials in grain and cloth, and promoted every commoner one rank.
14
下書曰：「三載考績，黜陟幽明，斯則先王之令典，政道之通塞。 魏始建九品之制，三年一清定之，雖未盡弘美，亦縉紳之清律，人倫之明鏡。 從爾以來，遵用無改。 先帝創臨天下，黃紙再定。 至於選舉，銓為首格。 自不清定，三載於茲。 主者其更銓論，務揚清激濁，使九流咸允也。 吏部選舉，可依晉氏九班選制，永為揆法。 選畢，經中書、門下宣示三省，然後行之。 其著此詔書於令。 銓衡不奉行者，御史彈坐以聞。」
His decree read: "The three-year review that elevates the worthy and casts down the dull was the ancient kings' statute and the touchstone of orderly rule." The Wei had instituted nine grades and purified them triennially—not flawless, yet the standard that kept the silk-gowned elite honest and reflected moral caliber. Ever since, the realm had accepted it unchanged. The late emperor began the enterprise and twice certified ranks on yellow edicts. In appointments, evaluation stood first. Yet three years had slipped by without a new ranking. Responsible ministers must reappraise, championing integrity and purging corruption until every class is satisfied. Personnel selection should adhere to the Jin nine-grade rotation as standing law. Once rosters are settled, the Secretariat and Chancellery shall publish them to the three bureaus before enactment. Engrave this decree in the codified regulations. Any registrar who flouts this faces censorial impeachment.
15
索頭郁鞠率眾三萬降于季龍，署鞠等一十三人親通趙王，皆封列侯，散其部眾于冀、青等六州。
The Suotou chief Yuju brought thirty thousand followers to capitulate; Jilong named thirteen men, Ju among them, as household companions to the Prince of Zhao with columnar marquisates, then scattered their people across Ji, Qing, and four other provinces.
16
時眾役煩興，軍旅不息，加以久旱谷貴，金一斤直米二斗，百姓嗷然無生賴矣。 又納解飛之說，于鄴正南投石於河，以起飛橋，功費數千億萬，橋竟不成，役夫饑甚，乃止。 使令長率丁壯隨山津采橡捕魚以濟老弱，而復為權豪所奪，人無所得焉。 又料殷富之家，配饑人以食之，公卿已下出穀以助振給，奸吏因之侵割無已，雖有貸贍之名而無其實。
Corvées multiplied, armies never rested, and drought drove grain prices until a pound of gold bought two dou of rice; the populace howled for want of a livelihood. Heeding Xie Fei, he tried to sink stone pylons in the river south of Ye for a soaring bridge; billions vanished and still no span emerged—famished workers made him abandon it. He told prefects to march young men into hills and shallows for acorns and fish to feed elders, but grandees seized every catch, leaving none for the needy. He assessed rich homes and assigned starvelings to eat them out; ministers donated grain, yet venal clerks skimmed without end—relief existed on paper alone.
17
改直蕩為龍騰，冠以絳幘。
He retitled the Straight Assault guard "Soaring Dragon" and dressed them in scarlet headcloths.
18
于襄國起太武殿，於鄴造東西宮，至是皆就。 太武殿基高二丈八尺，以文石碎之，下穿伏室，置衛士五百人於其中。 東西七十五步，南北六十五步。 皆漆瓦、金鐺、銀楹、金柱、珠簾、玉壁，窮極枝巧。 又起靈風台九殿于顯陽殿后，選士庶之女以充之。 後庭服綺縠、玩珍奇者萬餘人，內置女官十有八等，教宮人星占及馬步射。 置女太史於靈台，仰觀災祥，以考外太史之虛實。 又置女鼓吹羽儀，雜伎工巧，皆與外侔。 禁郡國不得私學星讖，敢有犯者誅。
The Grand Martial Hall at Xiangguo and twin palaces east and west at Ye were finished at last. The Grand Martial podium stood twenty-eight chi high, paved with patterned marble, with vaults below sheltering five hundred sentries. It measured seventy-five paces east to west and sixty-five north to south. Lacquered eaves-tiles, gilt chimes, silver jambs, gilded columns, pearl drapes, and jade screens strained every craftsman's wit. Behind Manifest Yang he erected nine Spirit-Wind terraces and pressed daughters of gentry and commoners to people them. Over ten thousand harem women dripped figured silk and rare jewels; eighteen ranks of female administrators taught star lore and horseback shooting. Women astronomers watched from the Spirit Terrace, charting portents to test the accuracy of the official sky-watchers. Female musicians, plumed processions, and variety acts rivaled anything beyond the walls. Provinces could no longer teach star lore or prognostication in secret—violators died.
19
左校令成公段造庭燎於崇杠之末，高十餘丈，上盤置燎，下盤置人，絙繳上下。 季龍試而悅之。 其太保夔安等文武五百九人勸季龍稱尊號，安等方入而庭燎油灌下盤，死者七人。 季龍惡之，大怒，斬成公段於閶闔門。
Left Works chief Cheng Gongduan mounted giant torches on masts exceeding ten zhang: fuel burned in the upper basin, men stood in the lower, hoisted on cables. Jilong tried the rig and crowed with pleasure. When Grand Guardian Kui An led 509 ministers to urge an exalted style upon him, oil cascaded into the lower basin as they approached—seven perished. Reading ill omen in the mishap, Jilong flew into a rage and beheaded Cheng Gongduan at Changhe Gate.
20
於是依殷周之制，以咸康三年僭稱大趙天王，即位于南郊，大赦殊死已下。 追尊祖㔨邪為武皇帝，父寇覓為太宗孝皇帝。 立其鄭氏為天王皇后，以子邃為天王皇太子。 親王皆貶封郡公，籓王為縣侯，百官封署各有差。
So, aping Yin and Zhou forms, in Xiankang 3 he arrogated the title Heavenly King of Great Zhao, took the throne south of the capital, and freed all but capital criminals. He canonized grandfather Bei Ye as Martial Emperor and Koumi as Filial Emperor Xiaozong. He raised Lady Zheng to Heavenly Queen and named Prince Sui Heavenly Heir. Imperial princes dropped to commandery dukes, feudal kings to county marquises; the bureaucracy saw reshuffled ranks.
21
太原徙人五百餘戶叛入黑羌。
Over five hundred Taiyuan deportees bolted to join the Black Qiang.
22
武鄉長城徙人韓強獲玄玉璽，方四寸七分，龜紐金文，詣鄴獻之。 拜強騎都尉，復其一門。 夔安等又勸進曰：「臣等謹案大趙水德，玄龜者，水之精也； 玉者，石之寶也； 分之數以象七政，寸之紀以准四極。 昊天成命，不可久違。 輒下史官擇吉日，具禮儀，謹昧死上皇帝尊號。 」季龍下書曰：「過相褒美，猥見推逼，覽增恧然，非所望也，其亟止茲議。 今東作告始，自非京城內外，皆不得表慶。 」中書令王波上《玄璽頌》以美之。 季龍以石弘時造此璽，強遇而獻之。
Han Qiang, deportee from Changcheng in Wuxiang, seized a black jade seal four cun seven fen on a side—tortoise knob, gilt inscription—and carried it to Ye. Qiang was named Colonel of Agile Cavalry with tax immunity for his clan. Kui An urged again: Great Zhao holds to Water phase; the black tortoise is Water's quintessence; jade is stone's treasure; its proportions echo the Seven Directors; its cun tally mirrors the Four Extremes. Heaven's sealed mandate brooks no delay. Charge the archives to fix an auspicious day, ready the ceremonies, and memorialize the imperial title though it cost our lives. Jilong answered, "Your flattery pushes me past bearing; the proposal shames rather than honors me—drop it immediately. With spring planting underway, felicitations are forbidden outside the capital." Palace Secretariat chief Wang Bo offered an "Ode to the Black Seal" in praise. Jilong reckoned Shi Hong had forged the seal and Han Qiang stumbled upon it.
23
邃自總百揆之後，荒酒淫色，驕恣無道，或盤游于田，懸管而入，或夜出於宮臣家，淫其妻妾。 妝飾宮人美淑者，斬首洗血，置於盤上，傳共視之。 又內諸比丘尼有姿色者，與其交褻而殺之，合牛羊肉煮而食之，亦賜左右，欲以識其味也。 河間公宣、樂安公韜有寵于季龍，邃疾之如仇。 季龍荒耽內游，威刑失度，邃以事為可呈呈之，季龍恚曰：「此小事，何足呈也。 」時有所不聞，復怒曰：「何以不呈？ 」誚責杖捶，月至再三。 邃甚恨，私謂常從無窮、長生、中庶子李顏等曰：「官家難稱，吾欲行冒頓之事，卿從我乎？ 」顏等伏不敢對。 邃稱疾不省事，率宮臣文武五百餘騎宴于李顏別舍，謂顏等曰：「我欲至冀州殺石宣，有不從者斬！ 」行數里，騎皆逃散，李顏叩頭固諫，邃亦昏醉而歸。 邃母鄭氏聞之，私遣中人責邃。 邃怒，殺其使。 季龍聞邃有疾，遣所親任女尚書察之。 邃呼前與語，抽劍擊之。 季龍大怒，收李顏等詰問，顏具言始末，誅顏等三十餘人。 幽邃於東宮，既而赦之，引見太武東堂。 邃朝而不謝，俄而便出。 季龍遣使謂邃曰：「太子應入朝中宮，何以便去？ 」邃逕出不顧。 季龍大怒，廢邃為庶人。 其夜，殺邃及妻張氏並男女二十六人，同埋於一棺之中。 誅其宮臣支党二百餘人。 廢鄭氏為東海太妃。 立其子宣為天王皇太子，宣母杜昭儀為天王皇后。
Once Shi Sui took the reins, he vanished into wine and women, swaggering beyond law—he hunted till dusk with flutes slung on his back or broke into ministers' houses after dark to rape their wives. Pretty maids he decapitated, rinsed the gore, arranged the heads on trays, and circulated them for sport. He dragged attractive nuns into assignations, murdered them, stewed their flesh with beef and mutton, served it to favorites, and demanded they guess the meat. His brothers Prince Xuan of Hejian and Prince Tao of Le'an basked in Jilong's love—Sui loathed them as bitter enemies. Lost in harem excess and capricious cruelty, Jilong blew up whenever Shi Sui submitted a report he thought trivial: "Why clutter my desk with this?" "If news had not reached him, he would rage instead: "Then why didn't you report it?" "He would jeer, scold, and cane him—sometimes several beatings a month." Burning with resentment, Shi Sui whispered to Wuqiong, Changsheng, and Li Yan, "Serving this lord is unbearable—I mean to repeat Modu's coup. Are you with me?" They kowtowed and could not bring themselves to reply. Shi Sui feigned sickness, skipped his duties, and rode off with five hundred courtiers to Li Yan's villa, declaring over wine, "I'm marching on Jizhou to murder Shi Xuan—anyone who balks loses his head!" Within a few miles his escort had melted away; Li Yan begged on his knees until the drunken prince staggered home. Lady Zheng learned of it and dispatched a confidential eunuch to rebuke her son. Shi Sui slew the messenger in a fury. Believing Shi Sui sick, Jilong sent the trusted female secretary Ren to inspect him. Shi Sui beckoned her close as if to talk, then drew steel and cut her down. Jilong had Li Yan and his circle arrested; under questioning Yan confessed the plot, and thirty-odd men went to the execution ground. He locked Shi Sui in the Eastern Palace, then unexpectedly pardoned him and received him at the east hall of the Grand Martial complex. Shi Sui appeared but offered no thanks and walked straight out. Jilong sent word: "The crown prince should attend the empress—why bolt away?" Shi Sui strode off without a backward glance. Jilong stripped him of rank and made him a commoner. That night he murdered Shi Sui, Lady Zhang, and twenty-six children, stuffing every corpse into a single coffin. Over two hundred of Shi Sui's household faction died with him. Lady Zheng was demoted to grand consort of Donghai. He named Shi Xuan heavenly heir and raised Consort Du to heavenly queen.
24
安定人侯子光，弱冠美姿儀，自稱佛太子，從大秦國來，當王小秦國。 易姓名為李子楊，游於鄠縣爰赤眉家，頗見其妖狀，事微有驗。 赤眉信敬之，妻以二女，轉相扇惑。 京兆樊經、竺龍、嚴諶、謝樂子等聚眾數千人于杜南山，子楊稱大黃帝，建元曰龍興。 赤眉與經為左右丞相，龍、諶為左右大司馬，樂子為大將軍。 鎮西石廣擊斬之。 子楊頸無血，十餘日而面色無異於生。
In Anding, the beardless youth Hou Ziguang—striking looks—proclaimed himself the Buddha's heir from Daqin, destined to king Little Qin. Renaming himself Li Ziyang, he boarded with Yuan Chimei in Hu; omens accumulated until even skeptics admitted something uncanny was afoot. Chimei worshipped him, married him both daughters, and the fever spread. Fan Jing, Zhu Long, Yan Chen, Xie Lezi, and others raised thousands on Du South Mountain; Li Ziyang declared himself Grand Yellow Emperor with era name Longxing. Chimei and Fan Jing took the left and right chancellor seals; Zhu Long and Yan Chen the grand marshal posts; Xie Lezi became commander-in-chief. Regional inspector Shi Guang rode out and took his head. No blood pumped from his neck, yet ten days later his face still looked alive—a wonder whispered through the camps.
25
季龍將伐遼西鮮卑段遼，募有勇力者三萬人，皆拜龍騰中郎。 遼遣從弟屈雲襲幽州，刺史李孟退奔易京。 季龍以桃豹為橫海將軍，王華為渡遼將軍，統舟師十萬出漂渝津，支雄為龍驤大將軍，姚弋仲為冠軍將軍，統步騎十萬為前鋒，以伐段遼。 季龍眾次金台，支雄長驅入薊，遼漁陽太守馬鮑、代相張牧、北平相陽裕、上谷相侯龕等四十餘城並率眾降于季龍。 支雄攻安次，斬其部大夫那樓奇。 遼懼，棄令支，奔於密雲山。 遼右左長史劉群、盧諶、司馬崔悅等封其府庫，遣使請降。 季龍遣將軍郭太、麻秋等輕騎二萬追遼，及之，戰於密雲，獲其母妻，斬級三千。 遼單馬竄險，遣子乞特真送表及名馬，季龍納之。 乃遷其戶二萬餘于雍、司、兗、豫四州之地，諸有才行者皆擢敘之。 先是，北單于乙回為鮮卑敦那所逐，既平遼西，遣其將李穆擊那破之，復立乙回而還。 季龍入遼宮，論功封賞各有差。
Mobilizing against Duan Liao of western Liaoxi, Jilong drafted thirty thousand stalwarts as Soaring Dragon household guards. Duan Liao sent Qu Yun against Youzhou; Inspector Li Meng fled to the refuge at Yijing. Tao Bao and Wang Hua led a hundred thousand sailors from Piaoyu; Zhi Xiong and Yao Yizhong marched another hundred thousand overland—the pincer aimed at Duan Liao. Halting at Golden Terrace, Zhi Xiong plunged into Ji; forty-some cities—Yuyang, Dai, Beiping, Shanggu—opened their gates to Zhao. Zhi Xiong stormed Anci and struck down minister Nalou Qi. Panicking, Duan Liao abandoned Lingzhi for Miyun Mountain. His chief clerks Liu Qun and Lu Chen, with Cui Yue, sealed the vaults and sued for peace. Guo Tai and Ma Qiu raced after him with twenty thousand light horse, fought at Miyun, seized his womenfolk, and piled three thousand skulls. Duan Liao bolted alone into the ravines while his son Qitezhen carried tribute horses and a surrender memorial—Jilong accepted. More than twenty thousand households went to Yong, Si, Yan, and Yu; anyone with merit earned promotion. Years before, Northern Chanyu Yihui had fled Xianbei Dunduo; once Liaoxi fell, Li Mu smashed Dunduo and reinstated Yihui. Jilong toured Duan Liao's halls and handed out fiefs by merit.
26
初，慕容皝與段遼有隙，遣使稱籓于季龍，陳遼宜伐，請盡眾來會。 及軍至令支，皝師不出，季龍將伐之。 天竺佛圖澄進曰：「燕福德之國，未可加兵。 」季龍作色曰：「以此攻城，何城不克？ 以此眾戰，誰能禦之？ 區區小豎，何所逃也？ 」太史令趙攬固諫曰：「燕城歲星所守，行師無功，必受其禍。 」季龍怒，鞭之，黜為肥如長。 進師攻棘城，旬餘不克。 皝遣子恪帥胡騎二千，晨出挑戰，諸門皆若有師出者，四面如雲，季龍大驚，棄甲而遁。 於是召趙攬復為太史令。 季龍旋自令支，過易京，惡其固而毀之。 還謁石勒墓，朝其群臣于襄國建德前殷，復從征文武有差。 至鄴，設飲至之禮，賜俘偏于丞郎。
Murong Huang, long estranged from Duan Liao, had pledged allegiance to Zhao and urged a joint strike. At Lingzhi the Yan army never showed; Jilong ready his wrath against Murong Huang. The monk Fotucheng warned, "Yan enjoys heaven's favor—do not send blades there." Jilong flushed: "With such hosts I could crack any wall." Who could stand against this army in the field? That stripling cannot slip away. Grand astrologer Zhao Lan insisted: "The Year Star watches Yan—strike there and heaven punishes you." Jilong had him whipped and busted down to magistrate of Feiru. The siege of Ji dragged past ten days without breach. Murong Ke sortied at dawn with two thousand Hu horse; phantom banners sprouted at every gate until Zhao's host broke and Jilong threw away harness and ran. Chastened, he recalled Zhao Lan as court astrologer. Marching home via Lingzhi, he razed Yijing out of spite for its stubborn walls. He paused at Shi Le's tomb, convened his ministers before Xiangguo's Jiande Front Hall, and graded rewards for the expedition. At Ye he staged the victors' banquet and parceled captives among secretariat aides.
27
季龍謀伐昌黎，遣渡遼曹伏將青州之眾渡海，戍蹋頓城，無水而還，因戍於海島，運谷三百萬斛以給之。 又以船三百艘運穀三十萬斛詣高句麗，使典農中郎將王典率眾萬餘屯田于海濱。 又令青州造船千艘。 使石宣率步騎二萬擊朔方鮮卑斛摩頭破之，斬首四萬餘級。
For Changli he ordered Cao Fu to ferry Qingzhou troops to Tadun; wells failed, so they held sea islands while three million hu of grain sailed out to feed them. Three hundred ships carried another three hundred thousand hu toward Goguryeo while Wang Dian planted ten thousand colonists along the coast. Qingzhou's yards laid keels for a thousand hulls. Shi Xuan shattered Shuofang Xianbei Humotou with twenty thousand men and stacked forty thousand heads.
28
冀州八郡大蝗，司隸請坐守宰，季龍曰：「此政之失和，朕之不德，而欲委咎守宰，豈禹、湯罪己之義邪！ 司隸不進讜言，佐朕不逮，而歸咎無辜，所以重吾之責，可白衣領司隸。」
Locusts devoured eight Jizhou circuits; when the capital governor demanded magistrates' heads, Jilong answered, "Policy failed—the fault is mine. Would Yu and Tang have blamed their prefects?" You offer no loyal counsel yet punish innocents—doubling my shame. Serve as metropolitan governor in plain cloth.
29
加其子司徒韜金鉦黃鉞，鑾輅九旒。
His son Shi Tao, minister of education, received golden cymbals, yellow axe, and a nine-banner imperial carriage.
30
先是，使襄城公涉歸、上庸公日歸率眾戍長安，二歸告鎮西石廣私樹恩澤，潛謀不軌。 季龍大怒，追廣至鄴，殺之。
Princes Shegui and Rigui, holding Chang'an, accused Shi Guang of buying loyalty and plotting sedition. Jilong recalled Guang to Ye and executed him.
31
段遼於密雲山遣使詐降，季龍信之，使征東麻秋百里郊迎，敕秋曰：「受降如待敵，將軍慎之。 」遼又遣使降于慕容皝曰：「胡貪而無謀，吾今請降求迎，彼終不疑也。 若伏重軍以要之，可以得志。 」皝遣子恪伏兵於密雲。 麻秋統眾三萬迎遼，為恪所襲，死者十六七，秋步遁而歸。 季龍聞之驚怒，方食吐餔，乃削秋官爵。
Duan Liao's sham surrender duped Jilong into ordering Ma Qiu to escort him—a hundred li out—with the warning, "Treat a capitulation like an ambush." Meanwhile Duan Liao messaged Murong Huang: "Those barbarians are greedy and dull; if I beg escort they will never smell the trap." Lay a cordon of veterans and you will break them." Murong Huang hid Murong Ke at Miyun. Ma Qiu marched thirty thousand into Murong Ke's teeth; six or seven parts died; Qiu limped home alone. The news hit Jilong mid-meal—he vomited and stripped Ma Qiu of every rank.
32
下書令諸郡國立五經博士。 初，勒置大小學博士，至是復置國子博士、助教。 季龍以吏部選舉斥外耆德，而勢門童幼多為美官，免郎中魏ＫＪ為庶人。 以其太子宣為大單于，建天子旌旗。
He commanded each province to appoint professors for the Five Classics. Shi Le's old minor and major academy chairs now gained imperial-academy assistants under Jilong. Angered that personnel posts went to boys from great houses while graybeards languished, he cashiered Palace Gentleman Wei—whose name is corrupted in the text—to commoner rank. Crown Prince Shi Xuan became Grand Chanyu with banners reserved for emperors.
33
以夔安為征討大都督，統五將步騎七萬寇荊揚北鄙。 石閔敗王師于沔陰，將軍蔡懷死之。 宣將硃保又敗王師于白石，將軍鄭豹、談玄、郝莊、隨相、蔡熊皆遇害。 季龍將張賀度攻陷邾城，敗晉將毛寶於邾西，死者萬餘人。 夔安進據胡亭，晉將軍黃沖、曆陽太守鄭進皆降之。 安於是掠七萬戶而還。
Kui An led five generals and seventy thousand men against Jin's northern Jing-Yang frontier. Shi Min shattered the Jin host at Yinyin; Cai Huai fell. Zhu Bao, under Shi Xuan, crushed another imperial corps at Baishi; Zheng Bao, Tan Xuan, Hao Zhuang, Sui Xiang, and Cai Xiong died. Zhang Hedu seized Zhu city and broke Mao Bao west of the walls—ten thousand Jin dead. Kui An took Huting; Huang Chong and Liyang's Zheng Jin capitulated. Seventy thousand households went north as booty.
34
時豪戚侵恣，賄托公行，季龍患之，擢殿中御史李矩為御史中丞，特親任之。 自此百僚震懾，州郡肅然。 季龍曰：「朕聞良臣如猛獸，高步通衢而豺狼避路，信矣哉！」
Kin of the great houses seized fields and sold offices until Jilong elevated palace censor Li Ju to palace vice-censor with carte blanche. Officials froze; provinces snapped to attention. Jilong mused, "They say a righteous minister is a tiger on the highway—wolves scatter ahead of him. True enough!"
35
鎮遠王擢表雍、秦二州望族，自東徙已來，遂在戍役之例，既衣冠華胄，宜蒙優免，從之。 自是皇甫、胡、梁、韋、杜、牛、辛等十有七姓蠲其兵貫，一同舊族，隨才銓敘，思欲分還桑梓者聽之； 其非此等，不得為例。
Wang Zhuo argued that Yong and Qin's old elite, dragged east, deserved exemption from soldier rolls as cultivated houses—Jilong agreed. Seventeen lineages—Huangfu, Hu, Liang, Wei, Du, Niu, Xin, and the rest—left the conscription lists like ancient nobility; talent decided appointments; anyone wishing to go home could. Others could cite no such privilege.
36
以其撫軍李農為使持節、監遼西北平諸軍事、征東將軍、營州牧，鎮令支。
Li Nong became commissioner with baton, overseer of Liaoxi-Beiping armies, eastern conqueror, Yingzhou shepherd, stationed at Lingzhi.
37
于時大旱，白虹經天，季龍下書曰：「朕在位六載，不能上和乾象，下濟黎元，以致星虹之變。 其令百僚各上封事，解西山之禁，蒲葦魚鹽除歲供之外，皆無所固。 公侯卿牧不得規占山澤，奪百姓之利。 」又下書曰：「前以豐國、澠池二冶初建，徙刑徒配之，權救時務。 而主者循為恆法，致起怨聲。 自今罪犯流徒，皆當申奏，不得輒配也。 京獄見囚，非手殺人，一皆原遣。 」其日澍雨。
Drought seared the realm and a white rainbow arched the sky; Jilong confessed six fruitless years that offended heaven and starved the people. He invited sealed criticism, opened western hills to fuel gathering, and forbade monopolies on reed, fish, or salt beyond statutory tribute. No duke, minister, or governor might fence off marshes or hills against commoners. A second edict admitted that assigning convicts to new Fengguo and Mianchi smelters had been an emergency measure. Bureaucrats froze it into permanent labor, sparking outrage. Henceforth every exile requires imperial approval. Capital inmates who killed no one directly go free. Rain soaked the capital that same day.
38
季龍將討慕容皝，令司、冀、青、徐、幽、、並、雍兼復之家五丁取三。 四丁取二，合鄴城舊軍滿五十萬，具船萬艘，自河通海，運穀豆千一百萬斛于安樂城，以備征軍之調。 徙遼西、北平、漁陽萬戶於兗、豫、雍、洛四州之地。
Mobilizing against Murong Huang, he took three of every five fit men from Si, Ji, Qing, Xu, You, Bing, Yong, and families liable for double service. Elsewhere two of four were pressed; with Ye's existing troops the roll reached half a million; ten thousand hulls moved eleven million hu of grain and beans by river and sea to Anle for the campaign. Ten thousand households from western Liaoxi, Beiping, and Yuyang resettled in Yan, Yu, Yong, and Luo.
39
季龍僭位之後，有所調用，皆選司擬官，經令僕而後奏行。 不得其人，案以為令僕之負，尚書及郎不坐。 至是，吏部尚書劉真以為失銓考之體而言之，季龍責怒主者，加真光祿大夫，金章紫綬。
Since claiming the throne, appointments flowed Selection Bureau → commandant → supervisor → throne. Bad picks blamed left/right directors—not the ministry rank-and-file. Personnel chief Liu Zhen protested that this gutted evaluation rites; Jilong punished the overseers and ennobled Zhen as household grandee with gold seal and purple ribbon.
40
季龍如宛陽，大閱於曜武場。
At Wanyang Jilong staged a full-dress military review on the Field of Glorious Arms.
41
慕容皝襲幽、冀，略三萬餘家而去。 幽州刺史石光坐懦弱征還。
Murong Huang swept through You and Ji, uprooting thirty thousand families before riding away. Youzhou inspector Shi Guang lost his post for spinelessness and was summoned home.
42
賜征士辛謐几杖衣服，穀五百斛，敕平原為起甲第。
The hermit Xin Mi received cane, armrest, clothes, and five hundred hu of grain; Jilong told Pingyuan to build him a noble compound.
43
先是，李壽將李宏自晉奔于季龍，壽致書請之，題曰趙王石君。 季龍不悅，付外議之，多有異同。 中書監王波議曰：「今李宏以死自誓，若得反魂蜀漢，當鳩率宗族，混同王化。 若遣而果也，則不煩一旅之師而坐定梁、益，就有進退，豈在逃命一夫。 壽既號並日月，跨僭一方，今若制詔，或敢酬反，則取誚戎裔。 宜書答之，並贈以楛矢，使壽知我遐荒必臻也。 」於是遣宏，備物以酬之。
Li Hong of Cheng Han had defected from Jin to Zhao; Li Shou wrote asking him back, superscribing the envelope to "Prince of Zhao, Lord Shi." The superscription irked Jilong; court debate split. Wang Bo urged: "Li Hong swears himself to death—yet if he lives to stir Shu-Han, his clan might submit to your sway. Should he prove loyal once returned, Liang and Yi fall without a campaign; gain or loss thereafter hardly rests on one refugee. Li Shou already pairs himself with heaven—bold usurpation—so an imperial tone might earn a sneering answer and shame among border peoples. Better reply in kind and send hawthorn arrows—the classical tribute of far tribes—so Shou sees Zhao's reach. He released Li Hong laden with return gifts.
44
以石韜為太尉，與太子宣迭日省可尚書奏事。 自幽州東至白狼，大興屯田。
Shi Tao became grand commandant, sharing alternate days with Shi Xuan screening petitions for the ministry. East from Youzhou to Bailang he poured labor into frontier farms.
45
張駿憚季龍之盛，遣其別駕馬詵朝之。 季龍初大悅，及覽其表，辭頗蹇傲，季龍大怒，將斬詵。 侍中石璞進曰：「為陛下之患者，丹陽也。 區區河右，焉能為有無！ 今斬馬詵，必征張駿，則南討之師勢分為二，建鄴君臣延其數年之命矣。 勝之不為武，弗克為四夷所笑，不如因而厚之。 彼若改圖謝罪，率其臣職者，則我又何求！ 迷而不悟，討之未後也。 」季龍乃止。
Zhang Jun of Former Liang, dreading Zhao's strength, dispatched aide Ma Shen to pay homage. Delight turned to rage when Ma Shen's memorial read arrogant—Jilong reached for the headsman's sword. Attendant Shi Pu murmured, "Your real foe sits in Danyang. The Hexi corridor is a sideshow. Kill Ma Shen and you march on Liang—splitting the army meant for the south and gifting Jianye extra seasons. Victory proves little; defeat makes barbarians mock—better pamper him. Should he mend his tone and resume vassalage, we need nothing more. If he stays blind, we can strike later. Jilong stayed his hand.
46
李宏既至蜀漢，李壽欲誇其境內，下令云：「羯使來庭，獻其楛矢。 」季龍聞之怒甚，黜王波以白衣守中書監。
Once Li Hong arrived, Li Shou bragged that "barbarian envoys" had brought classic hawthorn shafts. The boast enraged Jilong; Wang Bo kept his portfolio only as a humiliated officer in undyed robes.
47
季龍志在窮兵，以其國內少馬，乃禁畜私馬，匿者腰斬，收百姓馬四萬餘匹以入於公。 兼盛興宮室於鄴，起台觀四十餘所，營長安、洛陽二宮，作者四十餘萬人。 又敕河南四州具南師之備，並、朔、秦、雍嚴四討之資，青、冀、幽州三五發卒，諸州造甲者五十萬人。 兼公侯牧宰競興私利，百姓失業，十室而七。 船夫十七萬人為水所沒、猛獸所害，三分而一。 貝丘人李弘因眾心之怨，自言姓名應讖，遂連結奸黨，署置百僚。 事發，誅之，連坐者數千家。
Determined to fight forever and short of mounts, he banned private horses—hidden stock meant waist execution—and seized forty thousand civilian animals for the state. Ye sprouted forty-plus towers; Chang'an and Luoyang gained new palaces—four hundred thousand builders. Henan's south rim readied anti-Jin defenses; Bing, Shuo, Qin, Yong stockpiled northern campaigns; Qing, Ji, You drafted men by the three-in-five rule; fifty thousand smiths forged armor. Magnates skimmed contracts until seven tenths of homes collapsed. One third of a hundred seventy thousand boatmen drowned or fed river monsters. In Beiqu, Li Hong rode popular hate, claimed destiny chose his name, knotted a conspiracy, and named ministers. Exposure brought mass execution—thousands of families caught in the net.
48
季龍畋獵無度，晨出夜歸，又多微行，躬察作役之所。 侍中韋謏諫曰：「臣聞千金之子坐不垂堂，萬乘之主行不履危。 陛下雖天生神武，雄據四海，乾坤冥贊，萬無所慮。 然白龍魚服，有豫且之禍； 海若潛游，罹葛陂之酷，深願陛下清宮蹕路，思二神為元鑒，不可忽天下之重，輕行斤斧之間。 一旦有狂夫之變，龍騰之勇不暇施也，智士之計豈及設哉！ 又自古聖王之營建宮室，未始不于三農之隙，所以不奪農時也。 今或盛功於耘藝之辰，或煩役於收穫之月，頓斃屬途，怨聲塞路，誠非聖君仁後所忍為也。 昔漢明賢君也，鐘離一言而德陽役止。 臣誠識慚昔士，言無可采，陛下道越前王，所宜哀覽。 」季龍省而善之，賜以穀帛，而興繕滋繁，游察自若。
Jilong hunted from dawn to dusk, slipped out disguised, and spied on work gangs. Wei Su warned, "Princes of gold avoid risky ledges—so should an emperor avoid peril. Your star is martial and the realm kneels—heaven seconds you. Yet incognito—the white dragon dressed as fish—invites fishermen's knives. Even sea gods hidden in shallows meet cruelty—clear the roads, heed those omens, never gamble the empire on stealth. One assassin ends Soaring Dragon guards before they draw—no time for clever schemes. Ancient builders worked only between planting seasons. Now you draft men at hoeing and harvest—corpses litter roads and curses fill lanes—no humane sovereign tolerates this. Han Mingdi stopped Deyang palace on one loyal sentence. I am no ancient worthy, yet your virtue exceeds theirs—deign to read me. Jilong praised Wei Su and paid him grain—then built faster and prowled harder.
49
右僕射張離領五兵尚書，專總兵要，而欲求媚于石宣，因說之曰：「今諸公侯吏兵過限，宜漸削弱，以盛儲威。 」宣素疾石韜之寵，甚說其言，乃使離奏奪諸公府吏，秦、燕、義陽、樂平四公聽置吏一百九十七人，帳下兵二百人，自此以下，三分置一，余兵五萬，悉配東宮。 於是諸公咸怨，為大釁之漸矣。
Zhang Li, running the five-bureau army ministry, flattered Shi Xuan: "Princes hog clerks and soldiers—trim them to swell your prestige. Shi Xuan, loathing Shi Tao's clout, had Zhang Li strip rival households: four great princes kept lean staffs while fifty thousand soldiers flowed to the heir's compound. Every prince nursed a grudge—the civil war's fuse lit.
50
遣征北張舉自雁門討索頭郁鞠，克之。
Zhang Ju marched from Yanmen against Suotou Yuju and broke him.
51
制：「征士五人車一乘，牛二頭，米各十五斛，絹十匹，調不辦者以斬論。 」將以圖江表。 於是百姓窮窘，鬻子以充軍制，猶不能赴，自經于道路死者相望，而求發無已。 會青州言濟南平陵城北石獸，一夜中忽移在城東南善石溝，上有狼狐千餘跡隨之，跡皆成路。 季龍大悅曰：「獸者，朕也。 自平陵城北而東南者，天意將使朕平蕩江南之征也。 天命不可違，其敕諸州兵明年悉集。 朕當親董六軍，以副成路之祥。 」群臣皆賀，上《皇德頌》者一百七人。 時妖怪尤多，石然于泰山，八日而滅。 東海有大石自立，旁有血流。 鄴西山石間血流出，長十餘步，廣二尺餘。 太武殿畫古賢悉變為胡，旬餘，頭悉縮入肩中。 季龍大惡之，佛圖澄對之流涕。
Each quintet owed a wagon, two oxen, fifteen hu rice, ten bolts silk—default meant death. The quota fed the planned sweep south of the river. Families sold children yet missed quotas; suicides lined highways while requisitions never stopped. Qingzhou claimed a stone beast walked overnight from north of Jinan's Pingling to a southeastern ravine, tracked by a thousand wolf prints forming roads. Jilong crowed, "The beast is me. Its march southeast means heaven backs my southern war. Order every province to mobilize next year. I will command the hosts myself to fulfill the omen. Courtiers cheered; 107 scholars offered odes to his virtue. Omens multiplied—Taishan stone flamed eight days. Donghai saw a megalith rise overnight, blood pooling at its foot. West of Ye, stone seams bled a rivulet ten paces long and two chi across. Grand Martial murals of sages morphed into Hu faces; heads sank into shoulders within a fortnight. Jilong shuddered; Fotucheng wept before the portraits.
52
甯遠劉甯攻武都狄道，陷之。 使石宣討鮮卑斛穀提，大破之，斬首三萬級。
Liu Ning from Ningyuan seized Didao in Wudu. Shi Xuan shattered Huguti's Xianbei, claiming thirty thousand skulls.
53
中謁者令申扁有寵于季龍，而宣亦昵之。 扁聰辯明斷，專綜機密之任。 季龍既不省奏案，宣荒酒內游，石韜沈湎好獵，生殺除拜皆扁所決。 於是權傾內外，刺史二千石多出其門，九卿已下望塵而拜，唯侍中鄭系、王謨、常侍盧諶、崔約等十餘人與之抗禮。
Eunuch chief Shen Bian charmed both throne and heir. Witty Shen Bian seized confidential authority. With Jilong ignoring paperwork, drunken Xuan and hunt-mad Tao left promotions and executions to Shen Bian. Power hugged Shen Bian—governors kowtowed in his courtyard—only a dozen senior advisers stood upright.
54
季龍又取州郡吏馬一萬四千餘匹，以配曜武關將，馬主皆復一年。
Another fourteen thousand regional mounts went to Yaowu passes; owners earned a year's tax holiday.
55
鎮北宇文歸執送段遼之子蘭降于季龍，獻駿馬萬匹。
Yuwen Gui shipped Duan Liao's son Lan to Ye with ten thousand steppes horses.
56
季龍以平西張伏都為使持節、都督征討諸軍事，帥步騎三萬擊涼州。 既濟河，與張駿將謝艾大戰於河西，伏都敗績。
Zhang Fudu took thirty thousand west against Liangzhou with full banners. Across the Yellow River Xie Ai shattered him on Liang's banks.
57
季龍雖昏虐無道，而頗慕經學，遣國子博士詣洛陽寫石經，校中經于秘書。 國子祭酒聶熊注《谷梁春秋》，列於學官。
For all his tyranny Jilong loved books—doctors copied Luoyang stone classics into the palace library. Academy libationer Nie Xiong lectured on Guliang Spring and Autumn as official curriculum.
58
燕公石斌淫酒荒獵，常懸管而入。 征北張賀度以邊防宜警，每裁諫之。 斌怒，辱賀度。 季龍聞之大怒，杖斌一百，遣主書禮儀持節監之。 斌行意自若，儀持法呵禁，斌怒殺之。 欲殺賀度，賀度嚴衛馳白之，季龍遣尚書張離持節帥騎追斌，鞭之三百，免官歸第，誅其親任十餘人。
Prince Shi Bin of Yan drank and hunted endlessly, flutes slung like a wastrel. Zhang Hedu nagged him—border vigilance mattered. Bin publicly humiliated Hedu. Jilong flogged him a hundred blows and posted clerk Li Yi as monitor. Bin shrugged; Li Yi barked regulations—Bin murdered him. Bin next aimed at Hedu; Hedu bolted to warn Jilong, who sent Zhang Li to haul Bin home, whip him three hundred times, cashier him, and kill ten favorites.
59
建元初，季龍饗群臣于太武前殿，有白雁百餘集于馬道南。 季龍命射之，無所獲。 既將討三方，諸州兵至者百餘萬。 太史令趙攬私于季龍曰：「白雁集殿庭，宮室將空，不宜行也。 」季龍納之，臨宣武觀大閱而解嚴。
Early Jianyuan, feasting before Grand Martial Hall, a hundred white geese settled south of the avenue. Archers bagged none. As three-front war loomed, gathered troops passed a million. Zhao Lan whispered that white geese meant hollow palaces—stay home. Jilong listened, reviewed troops at Mystic Martial tower, and stood down mobilization.
60
以燕公斌為使持節、侍中、大司馬、錄尚書事。 置左右戎昭、曜武將軍，位在左右衛上。 東宮置左右統將軍，位在四率上。 置上、中光祿大夫，在左右光祿上。 置鎮衛將軍，在車騎將軍上。
Shi Bin became baton commissioner, attendant-in-chief, grand marshal, and overseer of policy. New ranks—Martial Glory left/right—sat above household guards. The heir's compound gained commander-generals senior to the four escorts. Senior and middle household grandees outranked ordinary grandees. A new garrison guard outranked chariot commanders.
61
時石宣淫虐日甚，而莫敢以告。 領軍王朗言之于季龍曰：「今隆冬雪寒，而皇太子使人斫伐宮材，引于漳水，功役數萬，士眾籲嗟。 陛下宜因游觀而罷之也。 」季龍如其言。 既而宣知朗所為，怒欲殺之而無因。 會熒惑守房，趙攬承宣旨言于季龍曰：「昴者，趙之分也，熒惑所在，其主惡之。 房為天子，此殃不小。 宜貴臣姓王者當之。 」季龍曰：「誰可當者？ 」攬久而對曰：「無復貴于王領軍也。 」季龍既惜朗，且猜之，曰：「更言其次。 」攬曰：「其次唯中書監王波耳。 」季龍乃下書追波前議遣李宏及答楛矢之愆，腰斬之，及其四子投于漳水，以厭熒惑之變。 尋湣波之無罪，追贈司空，封其孫為侯。
Shi Xuan's atrocities mounted—no one dared tell. General Wang Lang reported that the crown prince drafted tens of thousands to drag palace timber to the Zhang River in blizzard cold. Cancel it on inspection tour. Jilong complied. Shi Xuan learned Wang Lang meddled and burned to kill him—without pretext. When Mars parked in Fang, Zhao Lan—doing Xuan's bidding—said Mao marked Zhao's fate and Mars brought doom. Fang houses the sovereign—ominous indeed. A minister surnamed Wang must absorb it. Who counts? asked Jilong. After silence Zhao Lan named Wang Lang. Reluctant to lose Wang Lang yet wary, Jilong demanded an alternate. Next choice: Wang Bo, palace secretariat chief. He retroactively condemned Wang Bo for the Li Hong affair, halved him at the waist, drowned his four sons in the Zhang, and called it penance for Mars. Remorse came quickly: Wang Bo was canonized minister of works and a grandson received a fief.
62
平北尹農攻慕容皝凡城，不克而還。 黜農為庶人。
Yin Nong, general who pacifies the north, struck Murong Huang at Fancheng, failed, and withdrew. Yin Nong was stripped to commoner.
63
時白虹出自太社，經鳳陽門，東南連天，十餘刻乃滅。 季龍下書曰：「蓋古明王之理天下也，政以均平為首，化以仁惠為本，故能允協人和，絹熙神物。 朕以眇薄，君臨萬邦，夕惕乾乾，思遵古烈，是以每下書蠲除徭賦，休息黎元，庶俯懷百姓，仰稟三光。 而中年已來變眚彌顯，天文錯亂，時氣不應，斯由人怨於下，譴感皇天。 雖朕之不明，亦群後不能翼獎之所致也。 昔楚相修政，洪災旋弭； 鄭卿厲道，氛祲自消，皆服肱之良，用康群變，而群公卿士各懷道迷邦，拱默成敗，豈所望於台輔百司哉！ 其各上封事，極言無隱。 」於是閉鳳陽門，唯元日乃開。 立二畤於靈昌津，祠天及五郊。
A white rainbow shot from the soil altar through Fengyang Gate to the horizon—visible many minutes. His edict preached balance and mercy as the ancient kings had. He claimed humility and cited tax breaks meant to comfort subjects and please heaven. Mid-reign omens—skewed stars, broken seasons—he blamed on popular hatred alarming heaven. Petty kings failed him too, he admitted. Chu ministers once ended floods by reform. Zheng's stern ministers cleared foul air—today's cowards betray every hope. Demand blunt sealed advice. Fengyang Gate stayed shut except New Year. Twin altars rose at Lingchang Ford for heaven and the five-direction rites.
64
李壽以建寧、上庸、漢固、巴征、梓潼五郡降于季龍。
Li Shou handed over five Shu commanderies to Zhao.
65
先是，季龍起河橋於靈昌津，採石為中濟，石無大小，下輒隨流，用功五百餘萬而不成。 季龍遣使致祭，沈璧於河。 俄而所沈譬流於渚上，地震，水波騰上，津所殿觀莫不傾壞，壓死者百餘人。 季龍恚甚，斬工匠而止作焉。
Five hundred thousand labor-days sank stone that the current swept away—no bridge. He sacrificed and drowned a ritual jade in the Yellow River. The jade resurfaced; quake and surge flattened the ford buildings—over a hundred died. He beheaded the builders and abandoned the bridge.
66
命石宣、石韜，生殺拜除皆迭日省決，不復啟也。 司徒申鐘諫曰：「度賞刑威，後皇攸執，名器至重，不可以假人，皆以防奸杜漸，以示軌儀。 太子國之儲貳，朝夕視膳而不及政也。 庶人邃往以聞政致敗，殷鑒不遠，宜革而弗遵。 且二政分權，鮮不及禍。 周有子頹之釁，鄭有叔段之難，此皆由寵之不道，所以亂國害親，惟陛下覽之。 」季龍不從。 太子詹事孫珍問侍中崔約曰：「吾患目疾，何方療之？ 」約素狎珍，戲之曰：「溺中則愈。 」珍曰：「目何可溺？ 」約曰：「卿目睕々，正耐溺中。 」珍恨之，以白宣。 宣諸子中最胡狀，目深，聞之大怒，誅約父子。 珍有寵于宣，頗預朝政，自誅約之後，公卿已下憚之側目。
Shi Xuan and Shi Tao rotated daily power over appointments and executions—no need to ask Ye. Shen Zhong warned that rewards and terror belong on the throne alone. Heirs attend parents at meals, not policy. Shi Sui's ruin proved heirs must not rule. Split power invites disaster. Zhou's Zitui and Zheng's Shuduan warn what indulgence costs. Jilong ignored him. Sun Zhen asked Cui Yue for an eye cure. Cui Yue joked: bathe them in urine. Sun Zhen asked how eyes could steep in piss. Cui answered that deep-set sockets were perfect for the treatment. Sun Zhen told Shi Xuan. Shi Xuan—deepest eyes, most barbarian face—murdered the Cui clan. Sun Zhen dominated court business until officials flinched at his shadow.
67
季龍子義陽公鑒時鎮關中，役煩賦重，失關右之和。 其友李松勸鑒，文武有長髮者，拔為冠纓，余以給宮人。 長史取發白之，季龍大怒，以其右僕射張離為征西左長史、龍驤將軍、雍州刺史以察之，信然，征鑒還鄴，收松下廷尉，以石苞代鎮長安。 發雍、洛、秦、并州十六萬人城長安未央宮。
Prince Shi Jian of Yiyang held Guanzhong while crushing taxes bred hatred. Li Song proposed shaving officials' long hair for hat cords and sending locks to harem weavers. Memorial exposure infuriated Jilong; Zhang Li verified the hair scheme, recalled Shi Jian, jailed Li Song, and posted Shi Bao to Chang'an. A hundred sixty thousand builders raised walls around Weiyang.
68
季龍性既好獵，其後體重，不能跨鞍，乃造獵車千乘，轅長三丈，高一丈八尺，罝高一丈七尺，格獸車四十乘，立三級行樓二層於其上，克期將校獵。 自靈昌津南至滎陽，東極陽都，使御史監察，其中禽獸有犯者罪至大辟。 御史因之擅作威福，百姓有美女好牛馬者，求之不得，便誣以犯獸論，死者百餘家，海岱、河濟間人無寧志矣。
Too fat to ride, he commissioned a thousand hunting chariots—three-zhang poles, spring nets, paired towers—for staged battues. Censors patrolled a vast game preserve from Lingchang to Xingyang—touching royal quarry meant death. Censors framed hundreds for "poaching" to seize wives and herds—Huai to sea knew no rest.
69
又發諸州二十六萬人修洛陽宮。 發百姓牛二萬餘頭配朔州牧官。
Two hundred sixty thousand more rebuilt Luoyang. Twenty thousand private oxen went to Shuzhou herds.
70
增置女官二十四等，東宮十有二等，諸公侯七十餘國皆為置女官九等。 先是，大發百姓女二十已下十三已上三萬餘人，為三等之第以分配之。 郡縣要媚其旨，務於美淑，奪人婦者九千餘人。 百姓妻有美色，豪勢因而脅之，率多自殺。 石宣及諸公又私令采發者，亦垂一萬。 總會鄴宮。 季龍臨軒簡第諸女，大悅，封使者十二人皆為列侯。 自初發至鄴，諸殺其夫及奪而遣之縊死者三千餘人。 荊、楚、揚、徐間流叛略盡，宰守坐不能綏懷，下獄誅者五十餘人。 金紫光祿大夫逯明因侍切諫，季龍大怒，遣龍騰拉而殺之。 自是朝臣杜口，相招為祿仕而已。 季龍常以女騎一千為鹵簿，皆著紫綸巾、熟錦褲、金銀鏤帶、五文織成靴，游于戲馬觀。 觀上安詔書五色紙，在木鳳之口，鹿盧回轉，狀若飛翔焉。
The harem grew twenty-four female ranks; twelve for the heir; seventy feudal courts gained nine ranks each. He had pressed thirty thousand teen girls into three beauty tiers. Magistrates stole over nine thousand wives to curry favor. Many beautiful wives killed themselves rather than enter barracks. Princes' private impress gangs took another ten thousand. The harvest of women packed Ye. Jilong graded concubines from a balcony and made twelve recruiters full marquises. Three thousand women died—murdered husbands or suicide—before reaching Ye. Fifty southern magistrates died for failing to calm refugee waves. Lu Ming's blunt lecture earned dismemberment by Soaring Dragon guards. Officials clammed up—office became a paycheck. A thousand horsewomen in purple headcloths and brocade filled his processions to Playhorse Watch. Edicts unfurled from a mechanical phoenix beak on colored paper.
71
遣涼州刺史麻秋等伐張重華。
Ma Qiu marched on Zhang Chonghua of Former Liang.
72
尚書硃軌與中黃門嚴生不協，會大雨霖，道路陷滯不通，生因而譖軌不修道，又訕謗朝政，季龍遂殺之。 於是立私論之條，偶語之律，聽吏告其君，奴告其主，威刑日濫，公卿已下，朝會以目，吉凶之問，自此而絕。 軌之囚也，冠軍苻洪諫曰：「臣聞聖主之馭天下也，土階三尺，茅茨不翦，食不累味，刑措而不用。 亡君之馭海內也，傾宮瓊榭，象箸玉杯，截脛剖心，脯賢刳孕，故其亡也忽焉。 今襄國、鄴宮足康帝宇，長安、洛陽何為者哉？ 盤于游田，耽於女德，三代之亡恆必由此。 而忽為獵車千乘，養獸萬里，奪人妻女，十萬盈宮。 尚書硃軌，納言大臣，以道路不修，將加酷法，此自陛下政之失和，陰陽災沴，暴降霖雨七旬，霽方二日，縱有鬼兵百萬，尚未及修之，而況人乎！ 刑政如此，其如史筆何！ 其如四海何！ 特願止作徒，休宮女，赦硃軌，允眾望。 」季龍省之不悅，憚其強，但寢而不納，弗之罪也。 乃停二京作役焉。
Eunuch Yan Sheng blamed minister Zhu Gui for rain-ruined roads and "slander"—Jilong executed Gui. He legalized informers—clerks on lords, slaves on masters—until colleagues communicated only with glances. Fu Hong, as Zhu Gui sat in chains, praised frugal sage-kings. Tyrants built crystal towers and carved up counselors—speeding collapse. Xiangguo and Ye already palace enough—why twin western capitals? Hunts and harems ended every dynasty. Now you pen game across a continent and pack palaces with stolen women. Blaming Zhu Gui for rains that stalled roadworks ignores seventy days of storms clearing barely two days—not legions could pave that fast. How will histories judge this? What will the realm say? Stop corvée, free girls, spare Zhu Gui—that is what people crave. Jilong scowled, feared Fu Hong too much to punish, and tabled the memo. Yet he did halt building at the twin capitals.
