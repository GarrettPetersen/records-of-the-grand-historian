1
慕容廆
Murong Hui
2
慕容廆，字弈洛瑰，昌黎棘城鮮卑人也。 其先有熊氏之苗裔，世居北夷，邑于紫蒙之野，號曰東胡。 其後與匈奴並盛，控弦之士二十餘萬，風俗官號與匈奴略同。 秦漢之際為匈奴所敗，分保鮮卑山，因以為號。 曾祖莫護跋，魏初率其諸部入居遼西，從宣帝伐公孫氏有功，拜率義王，始建國於棘城之北。 時燕代多冠步搖冠，莫護跋見而好之，乃斂發襲冠，諸部因呼之為步搖，其後音訛，遂為慕容焉。 或云慕二儀之德，繼三光之容，遂以慕容為氏。 祖木延，左賢王。 父涉歸，以全柳城之功，進拜鮮卑單于，遷邑於遼東北，於是漸慕諸夏之風矣。
Murong Hui, courtesy Yiluogui, was a Xianbei from Jicheng in Changli. They traced their line to the Youxiong and lived beyond the frontier in Zimeng, a people known as the Eastern Hu. They grew as strong as the Xiongnu, fielding more than two hundred thousand archers, with similar customs and titles. Beaten by the Xiongnu at the fall of Qin, they withdrew to Mount Xianbei and took that name. His great-grandfather Mohuba brought the tribes into Liaoxi under Wei, fought the Gongsuns for Emperor Xuan, received the title Prince of Shuaiyi, and founded their seat north of Jicheng. Mohuba admired the Han-style swaying crown and wore it; tribes called him “Buyao,” which later slurred into “Murong.” Another etymology links the name to reverence for Heaven and Earth and the sun, moon, and stars. His grandfather Muyan held the Xiongnu-style title of Left Worthy King. His father Shegui earned promotion to Xianbei Shanyu for saving Liucheng, moved the capital toward eastern Liaodong, and began adopting Chinese ways.
3
廆幼而魁岸，美姿貌，身長八尺，雄傑有大度。 安北將軍張華雅有知人之鑒，廆童丱時往謁之，華甚歎異，謂曰：「君至長必為命世之器，匡難濟時者也。 」因以所服簪幘遺廆，結殷勤而別。 涉歸死，其弟耐篡位，將謀殺廆，廆亡潛以避禍。 後國人殺耐，迎廆立之。
From boyhood he was tall and striking—eight feet of frame—with the bearing of a born leader. Zhang Hua, famed for reading character, met him as a boy and said he would grow into the man who could save an era in crisis. He pressed his own cap on the boy as a pledge and sent him off with rare warmth. After Shegui died, his brother Nai seized power and plotted murder; Hui fled into hiding. The tribe killed Nai and brought Hui back as chief.
4
初，涉歸有憾于宇文鮮卑，廆將修先君之怨，表請討之。 武帝弗許。 廆怒，入寇遼西，殺略甚眾。 帝遣幽州諸軍討廆，戰於肥如，廆眾大敗。 自後復掠昌黎，每歲不絕。 又率眾東伐扶余，扶余王依慮自殺，廆夷其國城，驅萬餘人而歸。 東夷校尉何龕遣督護賈沈將迎立依慮之子為王，廆遣其將孫丁率騎邀之。 沈力戰斬丁，遂復扶余之國。 廆謀於其眾曰：「吾先公以來世奉中國，且華裔理殊，強弱固別，豈能與晉競乎？ 何為不和以害吾百姓邪！ 」乃遣使來降。 帝嘉之，拜為鮮卑都督。 廆致敬於東夷府，巾衣詣門，抗士大夫之禮。 何龕嚴兵引見，廆乃改服戎衣而入。 人問其故，廆曰：「主人不以禮，賓復何為哉！ 」龕聞而慚之，彌加敬憚。 時東胡宇文鮮卑段部以廆威德日廣，懼有吞併之計，因為寇掠，往來不絕。 廆卑辭厚幣以撫之。
Hui wanted to avenge his father’s feud with the Yuwen and asked the court for a campaign. Emperor Wu refused. Enraged, he swept into Liaoxi, slaughtering and plundering widely. Imperial troops from Youzhou met him at Feiru and broke his army. Year after year he raided Changli. He marched east against Puyŏ; its king killed himself; Murong sacked the capital and dragged off ten thousand captives. He Kan sent Jia Shen to restore the Puyŏ royal line; Murong countered with Sun Ding’s horsemen. Jia Shen killed Sun Ding in battle and put Puyŏ back on its throne. He told his chiefs: “Our house has long served China; steppe and heartland are not equals—we cannot stand against Jin forever.” Why keep war and ruin our own people?” He sent envoys to offer submission. The court commended him and named him commander over the Xianbei. He called at He Kan’s yamen in civil dress, observing Chinese courtesy. He Kan received him under arms; Murong switched to military dress before entering. Asked why, he said, “If the host offers no courtesy, neither will the guest.” He Kan was abashed and treated him with greater respect. The Yuwen and Duan, fearing Murong’s growing power, raided him without pause. He soothed them with humble letters and rich gifts.
5
太康十年，廆又遷於徒河之青山。 廆以大棘城即帝顓頊之墟也，元康四年乃移居之。 教以農桑，法制同于上國。 永甯中，燕垂大水，廆開倉振給，幽方獲濟。 天子聞而嘉之，褒賜命服。
In Taikang 10, Murong Hui relocated his seat to the Qing Mountains by Tuhe. Treating Great Jicheng as the ancient ground of Zhuanxu, he transferred his capital there in Yuankang 4. He promoted tillage and mulberry culture and patterned his statutes on the Jin heartland. When Yongning brought devastating floods to the Yan lands, Murong opened his granaries and kept the northeast from starving. The emperor lauded him with honors and presented insignia robes.
6
太安初，宇文莫圭遣弟屈雲寇邊城，雲別帥大素延攻掠諸部，廆親擊敗之。 素延怒，率眾十萬圍棘城，眾咸懼，人無距志。 廆曰：「素延雖犬羊蟻聚，然軍無法制，已在吾計中矣。 諸君但為力戰，無所憂也。 」乃躬貫甲胄，馳出擊之，素延大敗，追奔百里，俘斬萬餘人。
Early in Taian, Yuwen Mogui’s brother Quyun raided the frontier; Hui personally crushed Da Suyan’s marauding columns. Suyan threw a hundred thousand men around Jicheng; the garrison panicked and lost the will to fight. Murong said, “Suyan’s host is a rabble without discipline—he is already playing my game. Stand fast and hit hard; leave the rest to me.” He armored himself, rode out, broke Suyan, and chased the rout a hundred li, killing or capturing over ten thousand.
7
永嘉初，廆自稱鮮卑大單于。 遼東太守龐本以私憾殺東夷校尉李臻，附塞鮮卑素連、木津等托為臻報仇，實欲因而為亂，遂攻陷諸縣，殺掠士庶。 太守袁謙頻戰失利，校尉封釋懼而請和。 連歲寇掠，百姓失業，流亡歸附者日月相繼。 廆子翰言於廆曰：「求諸侯莫如勤王，自古有為之君靡不杖此以成事業者也。 今連、津跋扈，王師覆敗，蒼生屠膾，豈甚此乎！ 豎子外以龐本為名，內實幸而為寇。 封使君以誅本請和，而毒害滋深。 遼東傾沒，垂已二周，中原兵亂，州師屢敗，勤王杖義，今其時也。 單于宜明九伐之威，救倒懸之命，數連、津之罪，合義兵以誅之。 上則興復遼邦，下則併吞二部，忠義彰於本朝，私利歸於我國，此則吾鴻漸之始也，終可以得志于諸侯。 」廆從之。 是日，率騎討連、津，大敗斬之，二部悉降，徙之棘城，立遼東郡而歸。
At the beginning of Yongjia he proclaimed himself Great Shanyu of the Xianbei. Pang Ben murdered Colonel Li Zhen in a private feud; frontier bands Sulian and Mujin claimed vengeance for Li but used it to rebel, storming counties and slaughtering townspeople. Yuan Qian kept losing battles, and Feng Shi, frightened, sued for peace. Raiding went on for years, livelihoods collapsed, and displaced families arrived daily to Murong’s protection. His son Han urged: “Nothing secures standing like marching to rescue the throne—every founder who mattered leaned on that cause. With those two running wild, imperial forces routed, and commoners carved like meat, can ruin cut deeper? They wave Pang Ben’s name abroad yet relish brigandage at heart. Feng Shi’s bargain—punishing Pang Ben for peace—only deepened the harm. Liaodong has been lost for two years, the heartland is torn by war, provincial hosts keep losing—now is the moment to answer the emperor’s call and seize the moral high ground. You should wield the great powers of chastisement, rescue people from mortal peril, publish Sulian and Mujin’s crimes, and rally loyal arms to destroy them. You recover Liaodong above, absorb the two bands below, win gratitude from Jin and strength for us—this is the first beat of the goose’s flight toward leadership among the northern lords.” Murong agreed. That day he rode against Sulian and Mujin, slew both chiefs, resettled their people at Jicheng, founded Liaodong commandery, and came home.
8
懷帝蒙塵于平陽，王浚承制以廆為散騎常侍、冠軍將軍、前鋒大都督、大單于，廆不受。 建興中，湣帝遣使拜廆鎮軍將軍、昌黎、遼東二國公。 建武初，元帝承制拜廆假節、散騎常侍、都督遼左雜夷流人諸軍事、龍驤將軍、大單于、昌黎公，廆讓而不受。 征虜將軍魯昌說廆曰：「今兩京傾沒，天子蒙塵，琅邪承制江東，實人命所系。 明公雄據海朔，跨總一方，而諸部猶怙眾稱兵，未遵道化者，蓋以官非王命，又自以為強。 今宜通使琅邪，勸承大統，然後敷宣帝命，以伐有罪，誰敢不從！ 」廆善之，乃遣其長史王濟浮海勸進。 及帝即尊位，遣謁者陶遼重申前命，授廆將軍、單于，廆固辭公封。
While Emperor Huai languished in Pingyang, Wang Jun offered Murong a cluster of honors culminating in Great Shanyu; Murong declined them all. Under Jianxing, Emperor Min named him General Who Garrisons the Army and duke over both Changli and Liaodong. Early in Jianwu, Emperor Yuan vested him with plenary frontier command, the Dragon-Soaring generalship, the Grand Shanyu title, and the Changli dukedom; Murong again demurred. Lu Chang argued: “Luoyang and Chang’an are gone and the sovereign is a captive; Prince Langya’s court on the lower Yangtze is where fate now turns. You dominate the northern coast, yet tribes still defy you because your posts lack formal Jin sanction and they trust their own strength. Send envoys to Langya, urge him to take the throne, then march with genuine imperial writ—who would refuse!” Murong approved and sent Chief Clerk Wang Ji across the sea to urge Sima Rui to ascend. After Sima Rui took the throne, Herald Tao Liao renewed the grant of general and Shanyu; Murong still refused the ducal patent.
9
時二京傾覆，幽、冀淪陷，廆刑政修明，虛懷引納，流亡士庶多繈負歸之。 廆乃立郡以統流人，冀州人為冀陽郡，豫州人為成周郡，青州人為營丘郡，并州人為唐國郡。 於是推舉賢才，委以庶政，以河東裴嶷、代郡魯昌、北平陽耽為謀主，北海逢羨、廣平遊邃、北平西方虔、渤海封抽、西河宋奭、河東裴開為股肱，渤海封弈、平原宋該、安定皇甫岌、蘭陵繆愷以文章才俊任居樞要，會稽硃左車、太山胡毋翼、魯國孔纂以舊德清重引為賓友，平原劉贊儒學該通，引為東庠祭酒，其世子皝率國胄束修受業焉。 廆覽政之暇，親臨聽之，於是路有頌聲，禮讓興矣。
With both capitals lost and Hebei overrun, Murong’s clean government and open door drew exiles—infants bundled on backs and all. He organized refugees by homeland—Jiyang for Jizhou people, Chengzhou for Yuzhou, Yingqiu for Qingzhou, Tangguo for Bingzhou. He elevated talent across the board: Pei Yi, Lu Chang, and Yang Dan directed strategy; Feng Xian, You Sui, Xifang Qian, Feng Chou, Song Shi, and Pei Kai ran the administration; Feng Yi, Song Gai, Huangfu Ji, and Miao Kai held key literary posts; Zhu Zuoche, Hu Wuyi, and Kong Zuan were honored as mentors; Liu Zan, a thorough Confucian scholar, became dean of the eastern academy, where heir Murong Huang and young nobles studied. When duties allowed, he attended the lectures himself, and the domain gained a name for courtesy and deference.
10
時平州刺史、東夷校尉崔毖自以為南州士望，意存懷集，而流亡者莫有赴之。 毖意廆拘留，乃陰結高句麗及宇文、段國等，謀滅廆以分其地。 太興初，三國伐廆，廆曰：「彼信崔毖虛說，邀一時之利，烏合而來耳。 既無統一，莫相歸伏，吾今破之必矣。 然彼軍初合，其鋒甚銳，幸我速戰。 若逆擊之，落其計矣。 靖以待之，必懷疑貳，迭相猜防。 一則疑吾與毖譎而覆之，二則自疑三國之中與吾有韓魏之謀者，待其人情沮惑，然後取之必矣。 」於是三國攻棘城，廆閉門不戰，遣使送牛酒以犒宇文，大言於眾曰：「崔毖昨有使至。 」於是二國果疑宇文同於廆也，引兵而歸。 宇文悉獨官曰：「二國雖歸，吾當獨兼其國，何用人為！ 」盡眾逼城，連營三十里。 廆簡銳士配皝，推鋒於前； 翰領精騎為奇兵，從旁出，直沖其營； 廆方陣而進。 悉獨官自恃其眾，不設備，見廆軍之至，方率兵距之。 前鋒始交，翰已入其營，縱火焚之，其眾皆震擾，不知所為，遂大敗，悉獨官僅以身免，盡俘其眾。 於是營候獲皇帝玉璽三紐，遣長史裴嶷送於建鄴。 崔毖懼廆之仇己也，使兄子燾偽賀廆。 會三國使亦至請和，曰：「非我本意也，崔平州教我耳。 」廆將燾示以攻圍之處，臨之以兵，曰：「汝叔父教三國滅我，何以詐來賀我乎？ 」廆懼，首服。 廆乃遣燾歸說毖曰：「降者上策，走者下策也。 」以兵隨之。 毖與數十騎棄家室奔於高句麗，廆悉降其眾，徙燾及高瞻等於棘城，待以賓禮。 明年，高句麗寇遼東，廆遣眾擊敗之。
Cui Bi, confident in his southern pedigree, meant to rally refugees, yet none joined him. He blamed Murong for poaching his following and secretly enlisted Goguryeo, the Yuwen, and the Duan to carve up Murong’s territory. When the triple alliance struck in Taixing, Murong said, “They chase Cui Bi’s lies and a quick gain—nothing but a mob. With no single hand on the reins, they will not trust one another—I can crush them. Their first rush is fierce—they want me to meet them at once. A hasty counterblow would walk straight into their trap. Hold still and they will grow wary, eyeing each other. They will wonder whether I collude with Cui Bi or which partner is secretly dealing with me like Han and Wei against the rest; once doubt gnaws them hollow, we win.” The allies surrounded Jicheng; Murong barred the gates and sent beef and wine to the Yuwen camp, proclaiming, “Cui Bi’s messenger came only yesterday.” Goguryeo and the Duan decided the Yuwen had sided with Murong and marched off. Xiduguan growled, “Let them go—I will take Murong’s realm by myself. He threw every man against the walls, camps stretching thirty li. Murong gave picked troops to Murong Huang to spearhead the attack; Murong Han swept in with elite horse as a flanking column straight at the Yuwen camp; Murong himself advanced in ordered squares. Trusting his host, Xiduguan posted no pickets and only scrambled to meet Murong when the lines closed. The first clash barely started when Han torched the camp; the Yuwen melted in panic, Xiduguan fled alone, and his army was taken whole. Patrols recovered three imperial jade seals; Chief Clerk Pei Yi carried them to Jiankang. Fearing Murong’s vengeance, Cui Bi sent his nephew Cui Tao with hollow congratulations. The allied envoys came suing for peace, pleading, “We never meant it—Inspector Cui told us to.” Murong marched Tao to the siege lines under arms: “Your uncle set three powers on me—why this sham goodwill?” Tao was terrified and confessed. Murong sent Tao back with an ultimatum: surrender was the high road, flight the ruinous course. Troops trailed the mission. Cui Bi bolted to Goguryeo with a few dozen riders; Murong absorbed his followers and resettled Tao and Gao Zhan at Jicheng with guest courtesy. The following year he turned back a Goguryeo raid on Liaodong.
11
裴嶷至自建鄴，帝遣使者拜廆監平州諸軍事、安北將軍、平州刺史，增邑二千戶。 尋加使持節、都督幽州東夷諸軍事、車騎將軍、平州牧，進封遼東郡公，邑一萬戶，常侍、單于並如故； 丹書鐵券，承制海東，命備官司，置平州守宰。
When Pei Yi returned from the capital, the court named Murong governor-general of Pingzhou, General Who Pacifies the North, and added two thousand households to his fief. Soon he also received the staff of authority, command over Youzhou and the eastern tribes, the chariot-and-cavalry generalship, the governorship of Pingzhou, and elevation to Duke of Liaodong at ten thousand households, keeping his palace appointment and Shanyu title. He was also given the vermilion warrant and iron covenant, delegated full discretion on the Liaodong shore, and charged with staffing Pingzhou government.
12
段末波初統其國，而不修備，廆遣皝襲之，入令支，收其名馬寶物而還。
Duan Mobo, newly in power, left his borders slack; Murong Huang struck Lingzhi and came away with prized horses and treasure.
13
石勒遣使通和，廆距之。 送其使於建鄴。 勒怒，遣宇文乞得龜擊廆，廆遣皝距之。 以裴嶷為右部都督，率索頭為右翼，命其少子仁自平郭趣柏林為左翼，攻乞得龜，克之，悉虜其眾。 乘勝拔其國城，收其資用億計，徙其人數萬戶以歸。
Shi Le sent envoys seeking friendship; Murong turned them away. He forwarded Shi Le’s envoys to Jiankang. Shi Le flew into a rage and sent Yuwen Qidegui against Murong Hui; Hui sent Murong Huang to hold the line. He named Pei Yi commander of the right wing, led the Suotou tribesmen on the right, and sent his youngest son Murong Ren from Pingguo toward Bailin on the left; they crushed Qidegui and carried off his entire army. They pressed the victory, seized the enemy capital, gathered booty counted in the hundreds of millions, and resettled tens of thousands of households under their rule.
14
成帝即位，加廆侍中，位特進。 咸和五年，又加開府儀同三司，固辭不受。
Upon Emperor Cheng's accession, Murong Hui received the title of palace attendant and the specially advanced precedence. In Xianhe 5 he was again offered an independent establishment with parity to the three dukes, but he steadfastly refused the honor.
15
廆嘗從容言曰：「獄者，人命之所懸也，不可以不慎。 賢人君子，國家之基也，不可以不敬。 稼穡者，國之本也，不可以不急。 酒色便佞，亂德之甚也，不可以不戒。 」乃著《家令》數千言以申其旨。
Murong Hui once remarked calmly, "The jail holds men's lives in the balance; nothing demands greater care. Men of virtue are the bedrock of the realm; they must be treated with respect. Agriculture is the state's root; it must be pressed without delay. Wine, lust, and sycophancy are the worst spoilers of moral conduct; they call for unsparing vigilance. He therefore drafted the Family Instructions at length to spell out these maxims.
16
遣使與太尉陶侃箋曰：
He dispatched a messenger bearing a letter to Grand Commandant Tao Kan, which read:
17
明公使君轂下：振德曜威，撫甯方夏，勞心文武，士馬無恙，欽高仰止，注情彌久。 王途險遠，隔以燕越，每瞻江湄，延首遐外。
To you, my lord, whose wheels we attend: you spread moral sway and show your power, bringing peace across the heartland; you exhaust yourself for civil and military matters, yet your men and mounts remain sound. Long have I looked up to your eminence with ever-deepening respect. The path to the throne is long and dangerous, with Yan and Yue standing between us; each time I scan the riverbank my thoughts fly to what lies far away.
18
天降艱難，禍害屢臻，舊都不守，奄為虜庭，使皇輿遷幸，假勢吳、楚。 大晉啟基、祚流萬節，天命未改，玄象著明，是以義烈之士深懷憤踴。 猥以功薄，受國殊寵，上不能掃除群羯，下不能身赴國難，仍縱賊臣，屢逼京輦。 王敦唱禍于前，蘇峻肆毒於後，凶暴過於董卓，惡逆甚於傕、汜，普天率土，誰不同忿！ 深怪文武之士，過荷朝榮，不能滅中原之寇，刷天下之恥。
Heaven has sent affliction; calamities struck again and again. The former capital could not be held and overnight became barbarian ground, forcing the Son of Heaven to withdraw and lean on the power of Wu and Chu. Great Jin laid its foundation and its fortune should run through countless reigns; the Mandate stands unaltered and the stars proclaim it plainly. Men of loyal fire therefore nurse deep outrage. Humble though my deeds are slight, I have received uncommon grace from the throne; yet I could not above extirpate the Jie hosts nor below throw myself into the national crisis, and I allowed traitors again and again to threaten the capital. Wang Dun began the disaster and Su Jun followed with poison; their savagery outdid Dong Zhuo and their treason surpassed Li Jue and Guo Si. Who in the realm did not burn with the same anger? I am baffled that our civil and military officers, laden though they are with court honors, cannot crush the invaders of the heartland or wipe away the empire's disgrace.
19
君侯植根江陽，發曜荊、衡，杖葉公之權，有包胥之志，而令白公、伍員殆得極其暴，竊為丘明恥之。 區區楚國子重之徒，猶恥君弱、群臣不及先大夫，厲己戒眾，以服陳、鄭； 越之種蠡尚能弼佐句踐，取威黃池； 況今吳土英賢比肩，而不輔翼聖主，陵江北伐。 以義聲之直，討逆暴之羯，檄命舊邦之士，招懷存本之人，豈不若因風振落，頓阪走輪哉！ 且孫氏之初，以長沙之眾摧破董卓，志匡漢室。 雖中遇寇害，雅志不遂，原其心誠，乃忽身命。 及權據揚、越，外杖周、張，內馮顧、陸，距魏亦壁，克取襄陽。 自茲以降，世主相襲，咸能侵逼徐、豫，令魏朝旰食。 不知今之江表為賢俊匿智，藏其勇略邪？ 將呂蒙、淩統高蹤曠世哉？ 況今凶羯虐暴，中州人士逼迫勢促，其顛沛之危，甚於累卵。 假號之強，眾心所去，敵有釁矣，易可震盪。 王郎、袁術雖自詐偽，皆基淺根微，禍不旋踵，此皆君侯之所聞見者矣。
You, my lord, took root along the Yangzi and your brilliance reaches Jing and Heng; you wield the power of Duke Ye and the purpose of Shen Baoxu, yet you have allowed men in the mold of the White Duke and Wu Yuan to run to extremes of violence—I would be ashamed before Zuo Qiu Ming. Even minor figures such as Zizhong of Chu were shamed when their lord seemed feeble and the court fell short of the great ministers of old; they hardened themselves, rallied the army, and brought Chen and Zheng to heel. Even Wen Zhong and Fan Li of Yue could steady Gou Jian and win standing at the Pool of Huang. How much more may one expect now that the south of the Yangzi teems with talent, yet none will uphold the sage Son of Heaven or strike north across the great river. Under the banner of justice you could chastise the lawless Jie, call the gentry of the old domains to arms, and win over those who still cling to their allegiance—would it not be like wind stripping the trees or a chariot flying downhill? When the house of Sun first rose, they shattered Dong Zhuo with the army of Changsha, set on restoring the Han. Though violence cut them short and their high purpose went unfulfilled, the earnestness of their intent shows they thought nothing of their lives. When Sun Quan held Yang and Yue, he used Zhou and Zhang abroad and Gu and Lu at home; he held Wei at bay along the Red Cliff line and took Xiangyang. Thereafter each ruler in turn could harry Xu and Yu and keep the Wei court from dining at ease. I cannot tell whether the Yangzi region now hides its able men and muffles their courage and design. Or have we simply lost sight of towering exemplars such as Lü Meng and Ling Tong? Today the savage Jie ravage the land; the gentry of the Central Plain are trapped in an ever-tightening vise, and their plight is more fragile than a tower of eggs. Their counterfeit majesty is hollow, popular feeling has deserted them, and the foe shows a fissure—they could be toppled with a shove. Wang Lang and Yuan Shu were impostors, yet their power rested on sand; ruin overtook them before they could turn—all of which you yourself have witnessed.
20
王司徒清虛寡欲，善於全己，昔曹參亦綜此道，著畫一之稱也。 庾公居元舅之尊，處申伯之任，超然高蹈，明智之權。 廆於寇難之際，受大晉累世之恩，自恨絕域，無益聖朝，徒系心萬里，望風懷憤。 今海內之望，足為楚、漢輕重者，惟在君侯。 若戮力盡心，悉五州之眾，據兗、豫之郊，使向義之士倒戈釋甲，則羯寇必滅，國恥必除。 廆在一方，敢不竭命。 孤軍輕進，不足使勒畏首畏尾，則懷舊之士欲為內應，無由自發故也。 故遠陳寫，言不宣盡。
Director Wang Dao is spare in appetite and adept at keeping himself whole; Cao Can of old trod the same path and won praise for ruling with a single, steady stroke. Yu Liang stands as the emperor's uncle in the mold of the Earl of Shen—aloof, principled, and judicious in the scales of power. In these years of invasion I have enjoyed the Jin throne's favor for generations; I chafe at my remoteness and my inability to aid the court, and can only fix my thoughts a thousand leagues away, nursing righteous anger. Today the expectations that can tip the balance, as between Chu and Han, rest on you alone, my lord. If you muster the five provinces, hold the approaches to Yan and Yu, and turn loyal men to lay down their arms in your cause, the Jie foe will fall and the dynasty's humiliation can be wiped away. On my distant frontier I shall spare no effort in your service. A single column pushing forward lightly will not make Shi Le look to front and rear; that is why loyalists in the north who would rise within cannot strike without a coordinated blow. I therefore set down these lines from afar, knowing they cannot say everything I feel.
21
廆使者遭風沒海。 其後廆更寫前箋，並齎其東夷校尉封抽、行遼東相韓矯等三十餘人疏上侃府曰：
Murong Hui's messenger was lost at sea in a gale. Murong Hui then redrafted his earlier letter and, together with Eastern Yi Colonel Feng Chou, acting Liaodong administrator Han Jiao, and more than thirty others, submitted a joint memorial to Tao Kan's headquarters:
22
自古有國有家，鮮不極盛而衰。 自大晉龍興，克平㟭、會，神武之略，邁蹤前史。 惠皇之末，後黨構難，禍結京畿，釁成公族，遂使羯寇乘虛，傾覆諸夏，舊都淪滅，山陵毀掘，人神悲悼，幽明發憤。 昔獫狁之強，匈奴之盛，未有如今日羯寇之暴，跨躡華裔，盜稱尊號者也。
Since ancient times few realms have not, after reaching their height, begun to wane. When Great Jin rose like a dragon it subdued Min and Kuai; its military genius stands with the annals of old. Under Emperor Hui's late reign the empress's faction sowed strife; calamity gripped the capital and blood feuds split the imperial kin. The Jie then poured through the breach, overturned the heartland, took the old capital, and violated the imperial tombs, drawing grief from men and gods alike. Neither the Xianyun at their strongest nor the Xiongnu at their height matched the brutality of today's Jie, who stride across Huaxia and the borderlands and usurp the imperial style.
23
天祚有晉，挺授英傑。 車騎將軍慕容廆自弱冠蒞國，忠於王室，明允恭肅，志在立勳。 屬海內分崩，皇輿遷幸，元皇中興，初唱大業，肅祖繼統，蕩平江外。 廆雖限以山海，隔以羯寇，翹首引領，系心京師，常假寤寐，欲憂國忘身。 貢篚相尋，連舟載路，戎不稅駕，動成義舉。 今羯寇滔天，怙其丑類，樹基趙、魏，跨略燕、齊。 廆雖率義眾，誅討大逆，然管仲相齊，猶曰寵不足以禦下，況廆輔翼王室，有匡霸之功，而位卑爵輕，九命未加，非所以寵異籓翰，敦獎殊勳者也。
Heaven favors Jin and has furnished it with heroes. General of Chariots and Cavalry Murong Hui took the realm in his youth, served the dynasty with loyalty, and showed judgment, good faith, and grave decorum, intent on winning honor for the throne. As the realm shattered and the court withdrew south, Emperor Yuan revived the house and began the great restoration; Emperor Ming carried the work forward and cleared the lands beyond the Yangzi. Though mountains and seas hemmed him in and the Jie stood between him and the throne, Murong Hui strained toward the capital, waking and sleeping with the court on his mind, ready to sacrifice himself for the realm. Tribute trains followed one another, barges crowded the waterways, and his hosts never stood down—each move served the loyal cause. The Jie marauders now swell like a flood; they lean on their own kind, root themselves in Zhao and Wei, and span Yan and Qi. Though Murong Hui leads loyal armies against the arch-rebel, even Guan Zhong, minister to Qi, warned that indulgence alone cannot command subordinates—how much less can Hui, who props the throne and has the work of a hegemon behind him, be left in low office and light rank without the highest honors? Such treatment neither marks a bulwark marquis nor rewards uncommon service.
24
方今詔命隔絕，王路險遠，貢使往來，動彌年載。 今燕之舊壤，北周沙漠，東盡樂浪，西暨代山，南極冀方，而悉為虜庭，非復國家之域。 將佐等以為宜遠遵周室，近准漢初，進封廆為燕王，行大將軍事，上以總統諸部，下以割損賊境。 使冀州之人望風向化。 廆得祗承詔命，率合諸國，奉辭夷逆，以成桓文之功，苟利社稷，專之可也。 而廆固執謙光，守節彌高，每詔所加，讓動積年，非將佐等所能敦逼。 今區區所陳，不欲苟相崇重，而愚情至心，實為國計。
Imperial orders no longer reach us easily; the road to the throne is long and dangerous; envoys shuttling tribute may be a year on the way. The ancient land of Yan—ringed on the north by the desert, reaching Lelang in the east, Mount Dai in the west, and Jizhou in the south—has become barbarian ground and is lost to the dynasty. His officers held that, following the Zhou model in spirit and the early Han in practice, Murong Hui should be advanced as king of Yan with acting powers of grand general—uniting the tribes above and biting into enemy ground below. Let the folk of Jizhou feel the moral breeze and come over to the rightful cause. If Murong Hui may take up the imperial charge, rally the states, and move against the barbarian usurpers in the manner of Duke Huan and Duke Wen, he may act on his own authority whenever the altars of state stand to gain. Yet Murong Hui clings to modesty; the longer he holds his integrity, the loftier it seems, and each new honor draws refusals that drag on for years—no pressure from his staff can move him. We do not offer this out of mere flattery; our plain loyalty is truly bent on the realm's interest.
25
侃報抽等書，其略曰：「車騎將軍憂國忘身，貢篚載路，羯賊求和，執使送之，西討段國，北伐塞外，遠綏索頭，荒服以獻。 惟北部未賓，屢遣征伐。 又知東方官號，高下齊班，進無統攝之權，退無等差之降，欲進車騎為燕王，一二具之。 夫功成進爵，古之成制也。 車騎雖未能為官摧勒，然忠義竭誠。 今騰箋上聽，可不遲速，當任天臺也。 」朝議未定。 八年，廆卒，乃止。 時年六十五，在位四十九年。 帝遣使者策贈大將軍、開府儀同三司，諡曰襄。 及俊僭號，偽諡武宣皇帝。
Tao Kan wrote back to Feng Chou and his colleagues, in substance: 'The general of chariots and cavalry thinks only of the state; tribute fills the roads. When the Jie sued for peace he seized their envoys and forwarded them; he struck the Duan domain in the west, campaigned beyond the frontier in the north, pacified the Suotou in the distance, and brought wild tribes to submit.' Only the northern tribes still hold out; he has repeatedly sent forces against them. I also hear that eastern offices are ranked without clear hierarchy—neither unified command above nor graded demotion below—and you wish to raise the general of chariots and cavalry to king of Yan; lay out each point in order. Promoting rank after deeds are done is an ancient and settled rule. Though the general has not yet broken Shi Le for the court, his good faith is beyond question. I shall forward your memorial to the throne; timing is not for me to decide—the central government must rule on it. Court opinion remained divided. In the eighth year Murong Hui died, and the matter was dropped. He was sixty-five and had held power forty-nine years. The emperor sent envoys with a patent naming him grand general with an independent establishment equal to the three dukes and the posthumous title Xiang. When Murong Jun seized the imperial style he gave him the spurious posthumous name Emperor Wu Xuan.
26
裴嶷，字文冀，河東聞喜人也。 父昶，司隸校尉。 嶷清方有幹略，累遷至中書侍郎，轉給事黃門郎、滎陽太守。 屬天下亂，嶷兄武先為玄菟太守，嶷遂求為昌黎太守。 至郡，久之，武卒，嶷被征，乃將武子開送喪俱南。 既達遼西，道路梗塞，乃與開投廆。 時諸流寓之士見廆草創，並懷去就。 嶷首定名分，為群士啟行。 廆甚悅，以嶷為長史，委以軍國之謀。
Pei Yi, courtesy name Wenji, was a native of Wenxi in Hedong. His father Pei Chang had served as metropolitan commandant. Pei Yi was principled and able; he rose to vice director of the palace secretariat, then became a palace attendant and gentleman at the yellow gate, and finally prefect of Xingyang. When the realm fell into chaos, his elder brother Pei Wu was already prefect of Xuandu; Pei Yi therefore asked for the post at Changli. He had been in office some time when Pei Wu died; summoned to court, he escorted his brother's son Pei Kai south to bury him. When they reached Liaoxi the roads were impassable, so he and Pei Kai took refuge with Murong Hui. The refugees who saw Murong Hui founding his state hesitated whether to stay or leave. Pei Yi was first to settle ranks and duties and set the example that rallied the scholars. Murong Hui was delighted, named him chief clerk, and gave him charge of civil and military planning.
27
及悉獨官寇逼城下，外內騷動，廆問策於嶷，嶷曰：「悉獨官雖擁大眾，軍無號令，眾無部陣，若簡精兵，乘其無備，則成擒耳。 」廆從之，遂陷寇營。 廆威德於此甚振，將遣使獻捷於建鄴，妙簡行人，令嶷將命。
When Xiduguan drove up to the walls and panic spread inside and out, Murong Hui asked Pei Yi for a plan. Pei Yi replied, 'Xiduguan's horde is huge but undisciplined and without formation; elite troops catching him off guard will bring him in chains.' Murong Hui took the advice and stormed the enemy camp. Murong Hui's prestige soared; he meant to report his victory to Jianye and, choosing his envoy with care, sent Pei Yi on the mission.
28
初，朝廷以廆僻在荒遠，猶以邊裔之豪處之。 嶷既使至，盛言廆威略，又知四海英賢並為其用，舉朝改觀焉。 嶷將還，帝試留嶷以觀之，嶷辭曰：「臣世荷朝恩，濯纓華省，因事遠寄，投跡荒遐。 今遭開泰，得睹朝廷，復賜恩詔，即留京輦，於臣之私，誠為厚幸。 顧以皇居播遷，山陵幽辱，慕容龍驤將軍越在遐表，乃心王室，慷慨之誠，義感天地，方掃平中壤，奉迎皇輿，故遣使臣，萬里表誠。 今若留臣，必謂國家遺其僻陋，孤其丹心，使懷義懈怠。 是以微臣區區忘身為國，貪還反命耳。 」帝曰：「卿言是也。 」乃遣嶷還。 廆後謂群僚曰：「裴長史名重中朝，而降屈於此，豈非天以授孤也。 」出為遼東相，轉樂浪太守。
At first the court, seeing Murong Hui far off in the wilderness, still handled him as a border magnate. Once Pei Yi reached the capital he expounded Murong Hui's power and design and showed how men of talent from every quarter flocked to his banner; the whole court revised its opinion. As Pei Yi prepared to leave, the emperor tested him by offering a post at court. Pei Yi refused: 'My family has long enjoyed imperial grace; I served in the high ministries, then was driven far afield and took refuge in the frontier.' Now, in this calmer age, I have seen the throne again and received a gracious summons to remain—personally that would be a great blessing. Yet the court is in exile and the tombs lie shamed; General Murong Hui, though far away, keeps the dynasty in his heart with a passion that moves heaven and earth. He means to clear the heartland and escort the imperial train home, and he sent me across ten thousand li to prove his loyalty. If you keep me here, men will say the court scorns his distant realm and chills his loyal heart, sapping the zeal of those who would serve the throne. That is why I set my own safety aside for the realm and am eager only to return with my answer. The emperor said, "You speak rightly. He therefore let Pei Yi depart. Murong Hui later told his officers, 'Chief Clerk Pei was famed at court yet stooped to serve me—can this be anything but Heaven's gift?' Pei Yi was sent out as administrator of Liaodong, then moved to prefect of Lelang.
29
高瞻，字子前，渤海蓚人也。 少而英爽有俊才，身長八尺二寸。 光熙中，調補尚書郎。 屬永嘉之亂，還鄉里，乃與父老議曰：「今皇綱不振，兵革雲擾，此郡沃壤，憑固河海，若兵荒歲儉，必為寇庭，非謂圖安之所。 王彭祖先在幽、薊，據燕、代之資，兵強國富，可以托也。 諸君以為何如？ 」眾咸善之。 乃與叔父隱率數千家北徙幽州。 既而以王浚政令無恆，乃依崔毖，隨毖如遼東。
Gao Zhan, courtesy name Ziqian, was a native of Tiao in Bohai. In his youth he was dashing and gifted and stood eight chi two cun tall. During the Guangxi reign he was appointed gentleman of the secretariat. When the Yongjia upheaval came he went home and said to the elders, 'The dynasty's grip is failing and war spreads like clouds. Our county is rich land defended by river and sea; in famine and turmoil it will become raiders' ground—not a haven for quiet life.' Wang Jun has long held You and Ji, commands the wealth of Yan and Dai, and fields a strong, prosperous host—we can lean on him. What say you all? The company approved the plan. He and his uncle Gao Yin led thousands of families north into Youzhou. Wang Jun’s rule proved erratic, so they attached themselves to Cui Bi and followed him into Liaodong.
30
毖之與三國謀伐廆也，瞻固諫以為不可，毖不從。 及毖奔敗，瞻隨眾降於廆。 廆署為將軍，瞻稱疾不起。 廆敬其姿器，數臨候之，撫其心曰：「君之疾在此，不在餘也。 今天子播越，四海分崩，蒼生紛擾，莫知所系，孤思與諸君匡復帝室，翦鯨豕於二京，迎天子于吳、會，廓清八表，侔勳古烈，此孤之心也，孤之願也。 君中州大族，冠冕之餘，宜痛心疾首，枕戈待旦，柰何以華夷之異，有懷介然。 且大禹出於西羌，文王生於東夷，但問志略何如耳，豈以殊俗不可降心乎！ 」瞻仍辭疾篤，廆深不平之。 瞻又與宋該有隙，該陰勸廆除之。 瞻聞其言，彌不自安，遂以憂死。
When Cui Bi allied with the three powers against Murong, Gao Zhan urged him to stop; Cui ignored him. After Cui Bi’s rout, Gao Zhan surrendered with the rest to Murong Hui. Murong named him a general, but he pleaded sickness and stayed in bed. Murong admired him and visited often, laying a hand on his chest and saying, “Your sickness is here—in your heart—not in your limbs.” The emperor is a refugee, the realm shattered, and the people adrift—I mean to work with you to restore the dynasty, cleanse the two capitals of brutes, bring the throne back from the southeast, and pacify the world as the heroes of old did. That is my purpose and my prayer.” You come from the great families of the heartland; you should burn to avenge the throne and sleep with your spear—why let the steppe-versus-Chinese divide harden your heart?” Yu rose from the Qiang and King Wen from the eastern tribes—character matters, not birth. Surely you need not scorn a foreign court on principle!” Gao Zhan still pleaded grave illness; Murong took deep offense. He had quarreled with Song Gai, who quietly urged Murong to kill him. When word of that reached him, his fear deepened until worry killed him.
