志第十九郡国一
Treatise 19: Commanderies and Principalities, Part I
2
《汉书·地理志》记天下郡县本末及山川奇异、风俗所由，至矣。 今但录中兴以来郡县改异及《春秋》、三史会同征伐地名，以为《郡国志》。 凡《前志》有县名今所不载者，皆世祖所并省也。 前无今有者，后所置也。 凡县名先书者，郡所治也。
The Geography Treatise in the Book of Han exhaustively traces every commandery and county in the empire, notes remarkable mountains and rivers, and explains how regional customs arose. This chapter records only post-restoration changes among commanderies and counties, plus battle-related placenames where the Spring and Autumn and the three standard histories agree—hence the title Commanderies and States. Any county listed in the earlier Geography but omitted here was folded into another unit or struck from the rolls under Emperor Guangwu. Entries that had no Western Han predecessor were added in subsequent reigns. The county named first in each list is the commandery seat.

◎河南尹秦三川郡，高帝更名。 世祖都雒阳，建武十五年改曰河南尹。 二十一城，永和五年户二十万八千四百八十六，口百一万八百二十七。
◎ Henan (Administrator's jurisdiction): Qin's Three Rivers Commandery, renamed under Emperor Gaozu. After Guangwu fixed his capital at Luoyang, Jianwu 15 elevated the office to Administrator of Henan. Twenty-one county seats; the Yonghe 5 census recorded 208,486 households and 1,012,027 people.

〖Luoyang〗 Zhou documents knew it as Chengzhou. Di Spring lies within the city. It includes the settlement of Tang. It includes the settlement of Shangcheng. It includes the settlement of Shixiang. It includes the Chu surname settlement. It includes the Rongqi Gorge. It includes Front Pavilion. It includes Yu township. It includes Great Xie city.

〖Henan County〗 This is the Luoyi fortified by the Western Zhou dukes; Spring and Autumn sources call it the royal capital. Its east gate was Ding Gate; its north gate, Ganji. It also holds Gan city and Kuai township.

〖Yingyang〗 The Honggou Canal runs here. It includes Guangwu city. Guo pavilion marks the domain associated with Guo Shu. It includes Long city. It includes Bo pavilion. It includes Ao pavilion. It includes Ying Marsh.

〖Juan〗 The Long Wall runs through here, from Yangwu toward Mi. Yuanyong city—identified by some as ancient Hengyong—stands here. It includes Hucheng pavilion.
