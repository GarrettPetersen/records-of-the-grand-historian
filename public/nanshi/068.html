2
趙知禮蔡景曆宗元饒韓子高華皎劉師知謝岐毛喜沈君理陸山才
Zhao Zhili, Cai Jingli, Zong Yuanrao, Han Zigao, Hua Jiao, Liu Shizhi, Xie Qi, Mao Xi, Shen Junli, and Lu Shancai
3
趙知禮字齊旦，天水隴西人也。 父孝穆，梁候官令。 知禮涉獵文史，善書翰。 陳武帝之討元景仲也，或薦之，引為書記。 知禮為文贍速，每占授軍書，下筆便就，率皆稱旨。 由是恒侍左右，深被委任，當時計畫，莫不預焉。 武帝征侯景，至白茅灣，上表于梁元帝及與王僧辯論軍事，其文並知禮所制。 及景平，授中書侍郎，封始平縣子。 陳受命，位散騎常侍、太府卿，權知領軍事。
Zhao Zhili, whose style name was Qidan, came from Longxi in Tianshui. His father Xiaomu had served as magistrate of Houguan under the Liang. Zhili read widely in literature and history and was accomplished at writing. When Emperor Wu of Chen marched against Yuan Jingzhong, someone recommended Zhili, and the emperor took him on as a secretary. Zhili wrote with fluency and speed. Whenever he was tasked with military documents, he would finish them at a stroke, and they almost always pleased the emperor. He was henceforth always at the emperor's side and deeply trusted; he took part in every major plan of the day. When Emperor Wu campaigned against Hou Jing and reached Baimao Bay, the memorials to Emperor Yuan of Liang and the exchanges on military affairs with Wang Sengbian were all drafted by Zhili. After Hou Jing was defeated, he was appointed vice director of the Secretariat and enfeoffed as Viscount of Shiping. When Chen took the throne, he served as regular attendant and grand steward and acted in charge of the guards.
4
天嘉元年，進爵為伯。 王琳平，授吳州刺史。 知禮沈靜有謀謨，每軍國大事，文帝輒令璽書問之。 再遷右將軍，領前軍將軍。 卒，贈侍中，諡曰忠。 子元恭嗣。
In the first year of Tianjia (560), his title was raised to marquis. After Wang Lin was defeated, he was appointed governor of Wuzhou. Zhili was reserved and resourceful. On every major military or civil affair, Emperor Wen would send sealed letters to ask his counsel. He was later promoted to general of the right and also served as general of the van. He died and was posthumously honored as attendant-in-chief, with the posthumous name Loyal. His son Yuangong succeeded him.
5
蔡景曆字茂世，濟陽考城人也。 祖點，梁尚書左戶侍郎。 父大同，輕車岳陽王記室參軍。 景曆少俊爽，有孝行，家貧好學，善尺牘，工草隸。 為海陽令，政有能名。 在侯景中，與南康嗣王會理通，謀匡復，事泄被執，賊党王偉保護之，獲免，因客遊京口。
Cai Jingli, whose style name was Maoshi, came from Kaocheng in Jiyang. His grandfather Dian had been vice director of the left household under the Liang. His father Datong had served as secretary to the Prince of Yueyang of the Light Carriage. Jingli was in his youth handsome and quick-witted, known for filial devotion. Though his family was poor, he loved learning, wrote excellent letters, and was skilled in cursive and clerical script. As magistrate of Haiyang, he earned a reputation for capable government. During Hou Jing's rebellion he contacted Huili, heir of the Prince of Nankang, to plot restoration. When the plot was exposed he was arrested, but the rebel Wang Wei protected him and he escaped execution. He then went to Jingkou as a guest.
6
侯景平，陳武帝鎮朱方，素聞其名，以書要之。 景曆對使人答書，筆不停綴，文無所改。 帝得書，甚加欽賞，即日授征北府中記室參軍，仍領記室。
After Hou Jing was defeated, Emperor Wu of Chen, then stationed at Zhufang, had long heard of him and summoned him by letter. Jingli wrote his reply to the envoy without pausing his brush, and the text needed no revision. The emperor was greatly impressed and that same day appointed him secretary in the northern campaign headquarters, with charge of the secretariat.
7
衡陽獻王昌為吳興太守，帝以鄉里父老，尊卑有數，恐昌年少接對乖禮，乃遣景曆輔之。 承聖中，還掌記室。 武帝將討王僧辯，獨與侯安都等數人謀之，景曆弗之知。 部分既畢，召令草檄，景曆援筆立成，辭義感激，事皆稱旨。 及受禪，遷秘書監、中書通事舍人，掌詔誥。
When Prince Xian of Hengyang, Chang, became governor of Wuxing, the emperor feared that the young prince might breach protocol with the elders of their homeland and sent Jingli to assist him. During the Chengsheng reign he again headed the secretariat. When Emperor Wu prepared to move against Wang Sengbian, he plotted with only Hou Andu and a few others; Jingli was not told. When the dispositions were complete, he was summoned to draft the proclamation. Jingli wrote it at once in stirring language, and the emperor was fully satisfied. After the emperor took the throne, Jingli was promoted to director of the secretariat and secretariat communicator, with charge of imperial edicts.
8
永定二年，坐妻弟受周寶安餉馬，為御史中丞沈炯所劾，降為中書侍郎，舍人如故。
In the second year of Yongding (558), he was impeached by censor-in-chief Shen Jiong because his wife's younger brother had accepted horses from Zhou Bao'an. He was demoted to vice director of the Secretariat but kept his post as communicator.
9
三年，武帝崩。 時外有強寇，文帝鎮南皖，朝無重臣，宣後呼景曆及江大權、杜棱定議，秘不發喪，疾召文帝。 景曆躬共宦者及內人密營斂服，時既暑熱，須營梓宮，恐斤斧之聲聞外，乃以蠟為秘器，文詔依舊宣行。
In the third year (559), Emperor Wu died. Strong enemies threatened from without, and Emperor Wen was stationed at Nanwan while the court lacked senior ministers. Empress Xuan summoned Jingli, Jiang Daquan, and Du Leng to decide policy. They concealed the death and urgently summoned Wen. Jingli personally worked with eunuchs and palace women to prepare the burial rites in secret. It was already midsummer and a wooden coffin had to be built; fearing that the sound of axes would be heard outside, they used a wax coffin instead, while edicts continued to be issued as usual.
10
華皎反，以景曆為武勝將軍、吳明徹軍司。 皎平，明徹於軍中輒戮安成內史楊文通，又受降人馬仗有不分明，景曆又坐不能匡正被收。 久之獲宥。
When Hua Jiao rebelled, Jingli was appointed general who overcomes Wu and army adviser to Wu Mingche. After Jiao was defeated, Mingche summarily executed Yang Wentong, interior minister of Ancheng, in camp and accepted horses and arms from surrendering troops under unclear terms. Jingli was again arrested for failing to correct these abuses. After some time he was pardoned.
11
宣帝即位，累遷通直散騎常侍、中書通事舍人，掌詔誥，仍復封邑。
When Emperor Xuan took the throne, Jingli rose through the ranks to regular attendant and secretariat communicator, again in charge of edicts, and his fief was restored.
12
太建五年，都督吳明徹北侵，所向克捷，大破周梁士彥于呂梁，方進圍彭城。 時宣帝銳意河南，以為指麾可定，景曆稱師老將驕，不宜過窮遠略。 帝惡其沮眾，大怒，猶以朝廷舊臣，不加深罪，出為豫章內史。 未行，為飛章所劾，以在省之日，贓汙狼籍，帝令有司案問，景曆但承其半。 於是御史中丞宗元饒奏免景曆所居官，徙居會稽。
In the fifth year of Taijian (573), the commander Wu Mingche invaded the north. His forces swept all before them and routed Liang Shiyan of Northern Zhou at Lüliang, then advanced to besiege Pengcheng. Emperor Xuan was then bent on recovering the lands south of the Yellow River and believed victory was at hand. Jingli warned that the army was weary and the generals overconfident, and that they should not press a distant campaign too hard. The emperor hated his discouraging the troops and was furious, but because Jingli was a veteran minister he did not punish him severely and merely sent him out as interior minister of Yuzhang. Before he could leave, an urgent memorial accused him of rampant corruption during his years at court. The emperor ordered an inquiry, and Jingli admitted only half the charges. Censor-in-chief Zong Yuanrao then memorialized to strip Jingli of his offices and exile him to Kuaiji.
13
及吳明徹敗，帝追憶景曆前言，即日追還，以為征南鄱陽王諮議。 數日，遷員外散騎常侍，兼御史中丞，復本爵封，入守度支尚書。 舊式拜官在午後，景曆拜日，適逢輿駕幸玄武觀，在位皆侍宴，帝恐景曆不預，特令早拜，其見重如此。
When Wu Mingche was defeated, the emperor remembered Jingli's earlier warning and summoned him back that same day as adviser to the Prince of Poyang on the southern campaign. Within days he was promoted to extraordinary attendant and concurrent censor-in-chief, his original title and fief restored, and he entered court as minister of revenue. By custom, appointments were made in the afternoon. On the day of Jingli's appointment the emperor happened to visit the Xuanwu Observatory, and all officials were at the feast. Fearing Jingli would miss his ceremony, the emperor ordered an early investiture. Such was the esteem in which he was held.
14
卒官，贈太常卿，諡曰敬。 十三年，改葬，重贈中領軍。 禎明元年，配享武帝廟庭。 二年，車駕親幸其宅，重贈景曆侍中、中撫將軍，諡曰忠敬，給鼓吹一部，於墓所立碑。
He died in office and was posthumously honored as minister of ceremonies, with the posthumous name Respectful. In the thirteenth year (581), when his burial was moved, he was again honored as commander of the guards. In the first year of Zhenming (587), he was granted a place in Emperor Wu's temple. In the second year (588), the emperor personally visited his home. Jingli was again honored as attendant-in-chief and general of the central pacification, with the posthumous name Loyal and Respectful, granted a set of martial music, and a stele was erected at his tomb.
15
景曆屬文，不尚雕靡，而長於敘事，應機敏速，為當時所稱。 有文集三十卷。 子征嗣。
Jingli's writing shunned ornament, but he excelled at narrative and responded swiftly to the moment; his contemporaries praised him highly. He left a collected works in thirty juan. His son Zheng succeeded him.
16
江大權字伯謀，濟陽考城人，位少府，封四會縣伯。 太建二年，卒於通直散騎常侍。
Jiang Daquan, whose style name was Bomou, came from Kaocheng in Jiyang. He served as minister of the palace storehouses and was enfeoffed as Marquis of Sihui. In the second year of Taijian (570) he died while serving as regular attendant.
17
征字希祥，幼聰敏，精識強記。 年六歲，詣梁吏部尚書河南褚翔，嗟其穎悟。 七歲丁母憂，居喪如成人禮。 繼母劉氏，性悍忌，視之不以道，征供侍益謹，初無怨色。 征本名覽，景曆以其有王祥之性，更名字焉。
Zheng, whose style name was Xixiang, was clever and quick-witted as a child, with a sharp mind and an excellent memory. At the age of six he visited Chu Xiang of Henan, director of personnel under the Liang, who marveled at his brilliance. At seven he entered mourning for his mother and observed the rites with the propriety of an adult. His stepmother, Lady Liu, was fierce and jealous and treated him harshly. Zheng served her all the more carefully and never showed resentment. Zheng's original name was Lan. Jingli, seeing in him the filial devotion of Wang Xiang, gave him the name Zheng.
18
陳武帝為南徐州，召補迎主簿，尋授太學博士。 太建中，累遷太子中舍人，兼東宮領直，襲封新豐侯。 至德中，位太子中庶子、中書舍人，掌詔誥。 尋授左戶尚書，與僕射江總知撰五禮事。 後主器其才幹，任寄日重。 遷吏部尚書，每十日一往東宮，于皇太子前論述古今得喪及當時政務。 又敕以廷尉寺獄，事無大小，取征議決。 俄敕遣徵收募兵士，自為部曲，征善撫恤，得物情，旬月之間，眾近一萬。 位望既重，兼聲位熏灼，物議咸忌憚之。 尋徙中書令。 中書清簡無事，或云征有怨言，後主聞之大怒，收奪人馬，將誅之，左右致諫，獲免。
When Emperor Wu of Chen was governor of South Xuzhou, he summoned Zheng as reception secretary and soon appointed him erudite of the imperial academy. During the Taijian era he rose to attendant of the heir apparent and concurrently head of the eastern palace guard, and inherited the title Marquis of Xinfeng. During the Zhide era he served as senior tutor of the heir apparent and secretariat attendant, in charge of edicts. He was soon appointed minister of the left household and, with vice director Jiang Zong, oversaw the drafting of the Five Rites. Emperor Houzhu valued his talent, and his responsibilities grew heavier by the day. He was promoted to minister of personnel. Every ten days he went to the eastern palace to lecture the crown prince on the rise and fall of past dynasties and current affairs. He was also ordered that all cases in the court of justice, great or small, were to be decided on Zheng's advice. Soon he was ordered to send Zheng to recruit troops and form his own command. Zheng was skilled at winning men's loyalty, and within a month his force neared ten thousand. His position and prestige were already great, and his fame and influence blazed so brightly that public opinion feared and resented him. He was soon transferred to director of the secretariat. The secretariat was a light post with little to do, and some said Zheng had complained. Houzhu heard this and was furious. He seized Zheng's men and horses and was about to execute him, but attendants dissuaded him and Zheng was spared.
19
禎明三年，隋軍濟江，後主以征有幹用，令權知中領軍事。 征日夜勤苦，備盡心力，後主嘉焉，謂曰：「事寧有以相報」。 及決戰于鍾山南岡，敕征守宮城西北大營，尋令督眾軍戰事。 陳亡，隨例入長安。
In the third year of Zhenming (589), as Sui armies crossed the Yangzi, Houzhu, trusting Zheng's ability, put him in acting charge of the guards. Zheng labored day and night with all his strength. Houzhu praised him and said, "When this is over, I shall reward you." At the decisive battle on the southern ridge of Zhong Mountain, he was ordered to hold the great camp northwest of the palace. Soon he was put in overall command of the fighting. When Chen fell, he went to Chang'an like the other captives.
20
征美容儀，有口辯，多所詳究。 至於士流官宦，陳宗戚屬，及當朝制度，憲章儀軌，戶口風俗，山川土地，問無不對。 然性頗便佞進取，不能以退素自業。 初拜吏部尚書，啟後主借鼓吹，後主謂所司曰：「鼓吹軍樂，有功乃授，蔡征不自量揆，紊我朝章。 然其父景曆既有締構之功，宜且如啟，拜訖即追還。 」征不修廉隅，皆此類也。
Zheng was handsome and eloquent and had mastered a great many subjects in detail. Whether the question concerned the gentry and officials, Chen imperial kin, current institutions, statutes and ritual, household registers and customs, or mountains, rivers, and lands, he could answer without fail. Yet he was inclined to flattery and ambition and could not content himself with a modest, retiring life. When he was first appointed minister of personnel, he asked Houzhu to lend him the imperial martial music. Houzhu told the officials, "Martial music is military music, granted only for merit. Cai Zheng does not know his place and would disorder our court regulations. Yet his father Jingli helped found the state. For now grant his request, but recall the music as soon as the appointment ceremony is over." Zheng lacked integrity in matters of this kind throughout.
21
隋文帝聞其敏贍，召見顧問，言輒會旨。 然累年不調，久之，除太常丞。 曆尚書戶部儀曹郎，轉給事郎，卒。 子翼，位司徒屬。 入隋，為東宮學士。
Emperor Wen of Sui heard of his quick wit and summoned him for consultation. His answers always pleased the emperor. Yet for many years he received no promotion. After a long wait he was appointed vice director of the court of sacrifices. He served as attendant in the household department of the ministry of revenue, was transferred to supervising attendant, and died. His son Yi served as retainer of the minister of education. Under the Sui he became a scholar of the eastern palace.
22
宗元饒，南郡江陵人也。 少好學，以孝聞。 仕梁為征南府外兵參軍。 及司徒王僧辯幕府初建，元饒與沛國劉師知同為主簿。 陳武帝受禪，稍遷廷尉卿、尚書左丞。 宣帝初，軍國務廣，事無巨細，一以咨之，台省號為稱職。
Zong Yuanrao came from Jiangling in Nan commandery. In his youth he loved learning and was known for filial devotion. Under the Liang he served as external military staff of the southern campaign headquarters. When Wang Sengbian first established his secretariat, Yuanrao and Liu Shizhi of Pei both served as chief clerks. When Emperor Wu of Chen took the throne, he was gradually promoted to minister of justice and left vice director of the secretariat. Early in Emperor Xuan's reign, civil and military affairs were pressing, and matters great and small were all referred to him. The ministries called him thoroughly competent.
23
遷御史中丞，知五禮事。 時合州刺史陳褒贓汙狼籍，遣使就渚斂魚，又令人於六郡乞米，百姓甚苦之，元饒劾奏免之。 吳興太守武陵王伯禮、豫章內史南康嗣王方泰等，驕蹇放橫，元饒案奏，皆見削黜。 元饒性公平，善持法，諳曉故事，明練政體，吏有犯法，政不便時，及於名教不足者，隨事糾正，多所裨益。 遷南康內史，以秩米三千餘斛助人租課，存問高年，拯救乏絕，百姓甚賴焉。 以課最入朝，詔加散騎常侍。 後為吏部尚書，卒。
He was promoted to censor-in-chief and oversaw the Five Rites. At the time the governor of Hezhou, Chen Bao, was flagrantly corrupt. He sent agents to collect fish at the river islets and had men beg rice across six commanderies, greatly burdening the people. Yuanrao impeached him and had him removed. The governor of Wuxing, Prince Boli of Wuling, the interior minister of Yuzhang, the heir of Nankang Fangtai, and others were arrogant and overbearing. Yuanrao investigated and memorialized against them, and all were demoted. Yuanrao was fair by nature, skilled at upholding the law, versed in precedent, and clear in the workings of government. Whenever officials broke the law, policy was ill timed, or ritual propriety was neglected, he corrected matters as needed and brought great benefit. He was appointed interior minister of Nankang. He used more than three thousand hu of his salary rice to help people pay rent and taxes, inquired after the elderly, and relieved the destitute. The people relied on him greatly. Because his administration ranked highest, he was summoned to court and promoted to regular attendant. He later became minister of personnel and died.
24
韓子高，會稽山陰人也。 家本微賤。 侯景之亂，寓都下。 景平，陳文帝出守吳興，子高年十六，為總角，容貌美麗，狀似婦人，於淮渚附部伍寄載欲還鄉里，文帝見而問曰：「能事我乎？ 」子高許諾。 子高本名蠻子，帝改名之。 性恭謹，恒執備身刀及傳酒炙。 帝性急，子高恒會意旨。 稍長，習騎射，頗有膽決，願為將帥。 及平杜龕，配以士卒。 文帝甚愛之，未嘗離左右。
Han Zigao came from Shanyin in Kuaiji. His family was of humble origin. During Hou Jing's rebellion he lodged in the capital. After Hou Jing was defeated, Emperor Wen of Chen went out to govern Wuxing. Zigao was sixteen, still wearing his childhood topknot, with a beautiful face that resembled a woman's. At a ford on the Huai he joined a transport unit to return home. Wen saw him and asked, "Will you serve me?" Zigao agreed. Zigao's original name was Manzi; the emperor gave him a new name. He was respectful and careful, always carrying the emperor's guard sword and serving wine and food. The emperor was impatient by nature, but Zigao always understood his wishes. As he grew older he learned riding and archery, showed considerable courage, and wished to become a general. When Du Kan was defeated, troops were assigned to his command. Emperor Wen loved him dearly and never let him leave his side.
25
帝嘗夢騎馬登山，路危欲墮，子高推捧而升。
The emperor once dreamed he was riding up a mountain on a dangerous path about to give way; Zigao pushed and supported him to the summit.
26
文帝之討張彪也，沈泰等先降，帝據有州城，周文育鎮北郭香岩寺，張彪自剡縣夜還襲城，文帝自北門出，倉卒闇夕，軍人擾亂，唯子高在側。 文帝乃遣子高自亂兵中往見文育，反命酬答，於闇中又往慰勞眾軍。 文帝散兵稍集，子高引入文育營，因共立柵。 明日敗彪，彪奔松山，浙東平。 文帝乃分麾下多配子高，子高亦輕財禮士，歸之者甚眾。
When Emperor Wen campaigned against Zhang Biao, Shen Tai and others surrendered first and Wen held the prefectural city. Zhou Wenyü garrisoned Xiangyan Temple in the northern suburb. Zhang Biao returned by night from Shan county to raid the city. Wen fled through the north gate at dusk in haste while the army fell into chaos; only Zigao remained at his side. Wen sent Zigao through the disordered troops to see Wenyü and bring back his reply, and Zigao went again in the darkness to comfort the armies. Wen's scattered troops gradually reassembled. Zigao led him into Wenyü's camp, and together they erected palisades. The next day Biao was defeated and fled to Song Mountain, and eastern Zhe was pacified. Wen then assigned many of his followers to Zigao. Zigao was generous with wealth and courteous to scholars, and many men rallied to him.
27
文帝嗣位，除右軍將軍，封文招縣子。 及王琳平，子高所統益多，將士依附之，其有所論進，帝皆任使焉。 天嘉六年，為右衛將軍。 文帝不豫，入侍醫藥。
When Wen succeeded to the throne, Zigao was appointed general of the right army and enfeoffed as Viscount of Wenzhao. After Wang Lin was defeated, Zigao's command grew ever larger. Officers and soldiers attached themselves to him, and the emperor employed whomever he recommended. In the sixth year of Tianjia he became general of the right guard. When Emperor Wen fell ill, he attended him with medicine.
28
廢帝即位，加散騎常侍。 宣帝入輔，子高兵權過重，深不自安，好參訪台閣，又求出為衡、廣諸鎮。 光大元年八月，前上虞縣令陸昉及子高軍主告其謀反，宣帝在尚書省，因召文武在位議立皇太子，子高預焉，執送廷尉。 其夕與到仲舉同賜死。 父延慶及子弟並原宥。
When the deposed emperor took the throne, Zigao was given the additional title of regular attendant. When Emperor Xuan entered as regent, Zigao's military power was excessive and he felt deeply uneasy. He frequented the ministries and sought appointment as commander of the Heng and Guang garrisons. In the eighth month of the first year of Guangda, the former magistrate of Shangyu Lu Fang and Zigao's army commander accused him of plotting rebellion. Emperor Xuan was at the secretariat and summoned the officials in attendance to discuss establishing the crown prince. Zigao was present, seized, and sent to the court of justice. That evening he and Dao Zhongju were executed together. His father Yanqing and his sons and younger brothers were all pardoned.
29
王琳東下，皎隨侯瑱拒之。 琳平，知江州事。 後隨都督吳明徹征周迪，迪平，以功進爵為侯，仍授都督、湘州刺史。 皎起自下吏，善營產業，又征川洞，多致銅鼓及生口，並送都下。 廢帝即位，改封重安縣公。
When Wang Lin marched east, Jiao followed Hou Jin in resisting him. After Lin was defeated, he oversaw the affairs of Jiangzhou. He later followed the commander Wu Mingche against Zhou Di. When Di was defeated, his merit earned promotion to marquis, and he was appointed commander and governor of Xiangzhou. Jiao had risen from a low clerk, was skilled at building wealth, and campaigned in the river valleys, sending many bronze drums and captives to the capital. When the deposed emperor took the throne, Jiao was re-enfeoffed as Duke of Chong'an.
30
韓子高誅後，皎內不自安，光大元年，密啟求廣州，以觀時主意。 宣帝偽許之，而詔書未出。 皎亦遣使引周兵，又崇奉梁明帝，士馬甚盛。 詔乃以吳明徹為湘州刺史，實欲以輕兵襲之。 慮皎先發，乃前遣明徹率眾三萬，乘金翅直趣郢州，又遣撫軍大將軍淳于量率眾五萬，乘大艦繼之。
After Han Zigao was executed, Jiao felt inwardly uneasy. In the first year of Guangda he secretly memorialized requesting Guangzhou to test the court's intentions. Emperor Xuan pretended to agree, but the edict had not yet been issued. Jiao also sent envoys to bring in Northern Zhou troops and honored Liang's Emperor Ming. His forces were very strong. An edict then appointed Wu Mingche governor of Xiangzhou, intending in fact to strike Jiao with light troops. Fearing Jiao would strike first, they sent Mingche ahead with thirty thousand men aboard the Golden Wings straight for Yingzhou, and also sent pacification general Chunyu Liang with fifty thousand men in great ships to follow.
31
時梁明帝遣水軍為皎聲援，周武帝遣衛公宇文直頓魯山，又遣柱國長湖西元定攻圍郢州。 梁明帝授皎司空，巴州刺史戴僧朔、衡陽內史任蠻奴、巴陵內史潘智虔、岳陽太守章昭裕、桂陽太守曹宣、湘東太守錢明，並隸於皎。 又長沙太守曹慶等本隸皎下，因為之用。 帝恐上流宰守並為皎扇惑，乃下詔曲赦湘、巴二州，其賊主帥節將，並許開恩出首。
Liang's Emperor Ming sent a naval force to support Jiao. Northern Zhou's Emperor Wu sent Duke of Wei Yuwen Zhi to encamp at Lushan and pillar of state Changhuxi Yuanding to besiege Yingzhou. Liang's Emperor Ming appointed Jiao minister of works. The governor of Bazhou Dai Sengshuo, interior minister of Hengyang Ren Mannu, interior minister of Baling Pan Zhiqian, governor of Yueyang Zhang Zhaoyu, governor of Guiyang Cao Xuan, and governor of Xiangdong Qian Ming all served under him. The governor of Changsha Cao Qing and others had originally been under Jiao and now served him. The emperor feared the upstream administrators would all be swayed by Jiao and issued a special pardon for Xiang and Ba. Rebel chiefs and commanders were permitted to surrender with clemency.
32
皎以大艦載薪，因風放火，俄而風轉自焚，皎大敗，乃與戴僧朔奔江陵。 元定等無復船渡，步趣巴陵，巴陵城已為陳軍所據，乃降，送於建鄴。 皎遂終於江陵，其黨並誅，唯任蠻奴、章昭裕、曹宣、劉廣業獲免。
Jiao loaded fuel on great ships and set them afire with the wind, but soon the wind turned and burned his own fleet. He was utterly defeated and fled with Dai Sengshuo to Jiangling. Yuanding and the others had no ships left to cross and marched on foot toward Baling, but Baling was already held by Chen troops. They surrendered and were sent to Jianye. Jiao died at Jiangling. His followers were all executed; only Ren Mannu, Zhang Zhaoyu, Cao Xuan, and Liu Guangye were spared.
33
劉師知，沛國相人也。 家本素族。 祖奚之，齊淮南太守，以善政聞。 父景彥，梁司農卿。
Liu Shizhi came from Xiang in Pei. His family was of plain lineage. His grandfather Xizhi had been governor of Huainan under Qi and was known for good government. His father Jingyan had served as minister of agriculture under the Liang.
34
師知本名師智，以與敬帝諱同，改焉。 好學，有當務才，博涉書傳，工文筆，善儀體，台閣故事，多所詳悉。 紹泰初，陳武帝入輔，以師知為中書舍人，掌詔誥。 時兵亂後，朝儀多闕，武帝為丞相及加九錫並受禪，其儀注多師知所定。
Shizhi's original name was Shizhi; because it matched the taboo name of Emperor Jing, it was changed. He loved learning and had a talent for practical affairs. He read widely in books and records, wrote well, understood ritual forms, and knew the precedents of the ministries in detail. At the beginning of Shaotai, when Emperor Wu of Chen entered as regent, Shizhi was made secretariat attendant in charge of edicts. After the military chaos, court ritual was largely in disarray. When Emperor Wu was chancellor, received the Nine Bestowals, and took the throne, Shizhi set most of the ritual regulations.
35
梁敬帝在內殿，師知常侍左右。 及將加害，師知詐帝令出，帝覺，遶床走曰：「師知賣我，陳霸先反。 我本不須作天子，何意見殺。 」師知執帝衣，行事者加刃焉。 既而報陳武帝曰：「事已了。 」武帝曰：「卿乃忠於我，後莫復爾。 」師知不對。 武帝受命，仍兼舍人。 性疏簡，與物多忤，雖位宦不遷，而任遇甚重，其所獻替，皆有弘益。
When Emperor Jing of Liang was in the inner hall, Shizhi constantly attended him. When they were about to kill him, Shizhi tricked the emperor into leaving the hall. The emperor sensed it and ran around the bed crying, "Shizhi has betrayed me! Chen Baxian has rebelled! I never wanted to be emperor—why do you want to kill me?" Shizhi seized the emperor's robe, and the executioners struck. He then reported to Chen Wudi, "It is done." Wudi said, "You have been loyal to me. Do not do this again." Shizhi did not answer. When Emperor Wu took the throne, Shizhi still served as attendant. He was plain and unceremonious and often clashed with others. Though his rank did not advance, his trust and responsibilities were great, and his counsel brought broad benefit.
36
及武帝崩，六日成服，時朝臣共議大行皇帝靈座俠御人衣服吉凶之制，博士沈文阿議宜服吉，師知議云：「既稱成服，本備喪禮。 案梁昭明太子薨，成服，俠侍之官，悉著衰斬，唯著鎧不異，此即可擬。 愚謂六日成服，俠靈座須服衰絰。 」中書舍人蔡景曆、江德藻、謝岐等同師知議。 時以二議不同，乃啟取左丞徐陵決斷。 陵云：「案山陵鹵簿吉部伍中，公卿以下導引者，爰及武賁、鼓吹、執蓋、奉車，並是吉服，豈容俠禦獨為衰絰？ 若言公卿胥吏並服衰絰，此與梓宮部伍有何差別？ 若言文物並吉，司事者凶，豈容衽絰而奉華蓋，衰衣而升玉路邪？ 同博士議。 」謝岐議曰：「靈筵祔宗廟，梓宮祔山陵，實如左丞議。 但山陵鹵簿，備有吉凶，從靈輿者儀服無變，從梓宮者皆服苴衰，爰至士禮，悉同此制。 此自是山陵之儀，非關成服。 今謂梓宮靈扆，共在西階，稱為成服，亦無鹵簿，直是爰自胥吏，上至王公，四海之內，必備衰絰。 案梁昭明太子薨，略是成例，豈容凡百士庶，悉皆服重，而侍中至於武衛，最是近官，反鳴玉紆青，與平吉不異？ 左丞既推以山陵事，愚意或謂與成服有殊。 」陵重答云：「老病屬纊，不能多說。 古人爭議，多成怨府，傅玄見尤于晉代，王商取陷於漢朝。 謹自三緘，敬同高命。 若萬一不死，猶得展言，庶與群賢，更申揚榷。 」文阿猶執所見，眾議不能決，乃具錄二議奏聞，上從師知議。
When Emperor Wu died, mourning garments were completed on the sixth day. The court debated whether attendants at the late emperor's spirit seat should wear auspicious or mourning dress. Erudite Shen Wen'a argued for auspicious dress. Shizhi argued, "Since this is called completing mourning garments, it is fundamentally a funeral rite. When Liang's Crown Prince Zhaoming died, the attendants all wore hemp mourning when mourning garments were completed, differing only in wearing armor. This precedent applies." I hold that on the sixth day, attendants at the spirit seat must wear hemp mourning." Secretariat attendants Cai Jingli, Jiang Dezao, Xie Qi, and others agreed with Shizhi. Because the two views differed, they memorialized to have left vice director Xu Ling decide. Ling said, "In the auspicious section of the funeral procession for the imperial tomb, from dukes and ministers down through martial guards, martial music, canopy bearers, and carriage attendants, all wear auspicious dress. How can the attendants alone wear hemp mourning? If dukes, ministers, clerks, and officers all wore hemp mourning, what difference would there be from the catafalque procession? If the ritual objects are auspicious while those who perform the rites are inauspicious, how can one wear hemp sashes while holding the flowered canopy, or mourning robes while mounting the jade carriage?" He agreed with the erudite. Xie Qi argued, "The spirit seat is enshrined in the ancestral temple and the catafalque in the imperial tomb, as the left vice director says. But the funeral procession has both auspicious and inauspicious elements. Those following the spirit carriage keep their dress unchanged, while those following the catafalque all wear coarse hemp mourning. Even commoner rites follow this rule. This belongs to the tomb rites and is not connected with completing mourning garments. Now the catafalque and spirit seat stand together on the western steps. Called completing mourning garments, there is no procession—simply from clerks up to princes and dukes, all within the realm must wear hemp mourning. When Crown Prince Zhaoming of Liang died, this was roughly the precedent. How can common scholars and commoners all wear heavy mourning while attendants-in-chief down to the martial guard—the closest officials—wear jade and green robes no different from ordinary auspicious days? Since the left vice director argued from the tomb rites, in my view this may differ from completing mourning garments." Ling replied again, "Old and ill, I am near death and cannot say much. When men of old disputed ritual, they often made lasting enemies. Fu Xuan was blamed in Jin times, Wang Shang was ruined in Han times. I respectfully hold my tongue and defer to your command. If by chance I do not die, I may yet speak further and discuss the matter again with the assembled worthies." Wen'a still held to his view. The assembly could not decide, so both views were recorded and memorialized. The emperor followed Shizhi.
37
遷鴻臚卿，舍人如故。 天嘉元年，坐事免。 尋起為中書舍人，復掌詔誥。 天康元年，文帝不豫，師知與尚書僕射到仲舉等入侍醫藥。 帝崩，豫顧命。 宣帝入輔，師知與仲舉等遣舍人殷不佞矯詔令宣帝還東府，事覺，於北獄賜死。
He was promoted to minister of guests while retaining his post as attendant. In the first year of Tianjia he was dismissed for an offense. He was soon reappointed secretariat attendant and again put in charge of edicts. In the first year of Tiankang, when Emperor Wen fell ill, Shizhi with vice director Dao Zhongju and others attended him with medicine. When the emperor died, Shizhi was named in the final testament. When Emperor Xuan entered as regent, Shizhi with Zhongju and others sent attendant Yin Buren with a forged edict ordering Xuan back to the eastern residence. When the plot was exposed, he was executed in the northern prison.
38
初，文帝敕師知撰起居注，自永定二年秋至天嘉元年為十卷。
Earlier, Emperor Wen ordered Shizhi to compile the diary of activity and repose from autumn of the second year of Yongding through the first year of Tianjia, ten juan in all.
39
謝岐，會稽山陰人也。 父達，梁太學博士。
Xie Qi came from Shanyin in Kuaiji. His father Da had been erudite of the imperial academy under the Liang.
40
弟嶠，篤學，為通儒。
His younger brother Qiao was devoted to learning and became a comprehensive scholar.
41
毛喜字伯武，滎陽陽武人也。 祖稱，梁散騎侍郎。 父棲忠，中權司馬。
Mao Xi, whose style name was Bowu, came from Yangwu in Xingyang. His grandfather Cheng had been regular attendant under the Liang. His father Qizhong had been marshal of the central guard.
42
文帝嘗謂宣帝曰：「我諸子皆以'伯'為名，汝諸子宜用'叔'為稱。 」宣帝以訪喜，喜即條自古名賢杜叔英、虞叔卿等二十餘人以啟之，文帝稱善。
Emperor Wen once told Emperor Xuan, "All my sons take bo in their names; your sons should use shu in theirs." Emperor Xuan consulted Xi. Xi promptly listed more than twenty famous men of antiquity such as Du Shuying and Yu Shuqing and presented the list. Emperor Wen approved.
43
文帝崩，廢帝沖昧，宣帝錄尚書輔政，僕射到仲舉等矯太后令，遣宣帝還東府，當時疑懼，無敢厝言。 喜即馳入，謂宣帝曰：「今日之言，必非太后之意，宗社至重，願加三思。 」竟如其策。
When Emperor Wen died, the deposed emperor was young and inexperienced. Emperor Xuan took charge of the secretariat as regent. Vice director Dao Zhongju and others forged the empress dowager's order sending Xuan back to the eastern residence. All were fearful and none dared speak. Xi rushed in at once and told Emperor Xuan, "Today's order surely does not come from the empress dowager. The fate of the state is at stake—I beg you to think again." In the end events unfolded as he had advised.
44
右衛將軍韓子高始與仲舉通謀，其事未發，喜謂宣帝曰：「宜簡人馬配與子高，並賜鐵炭，使修器甲。 」宣帝曰：「子高即欲收執，何更如是？ 」喜曰：「山陵始畢，邊寇尚多，而子高受委前朝，名為杖順，宜推心安誘，使不自疑，圖之一壯士之力耳。 」宣帝卒行其計。
General of the right guard Han Zigao had at first plotted with Zhongju, though the plot had not yet broken out. Xi told Emperor Xuan, "You should assign men and horses to Zigao and grant him iron and charcoal to repair weapons and armor. Emperor Xuan said, "I am about to arrest Zigao—why do this?" Xi said, "The imperial tomb rites have only just ended and border enemies are still numerous. Zigao was entrusted by the former court and is known as loyal. You should reassure and win him over so he does not grow suspicious. Arresting him would take only one brave man's effort." Emperor Xuan carried out his plan.
45
及得淮南之地，喜陳安邊之術，宣帝納之，即日施行。 帝又欲進兵彭、汴，以問喜，喜以為「淮左新平，邊人未輯，周氏始吞齊國，難與爭鋒，未若安人保境，斯久長之術也」。 上不從。 吳明徹卒俘于周。
When they gained the Huainan region, Xi presented methods for securing the borders. Emperor Xuan accepted them and implemented them that same day. The emperor also wished to advance on Peng and Bian and consulted Xi. Xi argued, "The Huai region is newly pacified and the border people are not yet settled. Northern Zhou has just swallowed Qi and would be hard to fight. It is better to settle the people and guard the borders—that is the strategy for long survival." The emperor did not follow his advice. Wu Mingche was eventually captured by Northern Zhou.
46
喜後曆丹陽尹，吏部尚書。 及宣帝崩，叔陵構逆，敕中庶子陸瓊宣旨，令南北諸軍皆取喜處分。 賊平，加侍中。
Xi later served as governor of Danyang and minister of personnel. When Emperor Xuan died, Shuling plotted rebellion. An edict ordered senior tutor Lu Qiong to proclaim that all armies north and south were to follow Xi's orders. When the rebels were defeated, he was given the additional title of attendant-in-chief.
47
初，宣帝委政於喜，喜數有諫爭，事並見從。 自明徹敗後，帝深悔不用其言，謂袁憲曰：「一不用喜計，遂令至此。 」由是益見親重，喜乃言無回避。 時皇太子好酒德，每共親幸人為長夜之宴，喜嘗言之宣帝，太子遂銜之，即位後稍見疏遠。 及被始興王傷，創愈，置酒引江總以下，展樂賦詩，醉酣而命喜。 于時山陵初畢，未及踰年，喜見之不懌，欲諫而後主已醉。 喜言心疾，僕於階下，移出省中。 後主醒，乃謂江總曰：「我悔召毛喜，知其無病，但欲阻我歡宴，非我所為耳。 」乃與司馬申謀曰：「此人負氣，吾欲將乞鄱陽兄弟，聽其報讎，可乎？ 「對曰：「終不為官用，願如聖旨。 」傅縡爭之曰：「若許報讎，欲置先皇何地？ 」後主曰：「當與一小郡，勿令見人事耳。」
At first Emperor Xuan entrusted government to Xi. Xi repeatedly remonstrated, and his advice was followed. After Mingche's defeat, the emperor deeply regretted ignoring Xi's advice and told Yuan Xian, "If I had followed Xi's plan even once, we would not be in this plight." From then on Xi was all the more trusted and spoke without reserve. The crown prince was fond of wine and often held all-night feasts with his favorites. Xi once reported this to Emperor Xuan, and the prince bore a grudge. After he took the throne, Xi was gradually distanced. After he was wounded by the Prince of Shixing and recovered, he held a feast for Jiang Zong and others, with music and poetry. Drunk, he summoned Xi. The imperial tomb rites had only just ended and a year had not yet passed. Xi was displeased and wished to remonstrate, but Houzhu was already drunk. Xi claimed heart trouble, collapsed on the steps, and was carried out of the secretariat. When Houzhu awoke, he told Jiang Zong, "I regret summoning Mao Xi. I know he is not ill—he only wanted to spoil my feast. That is not what I would do." He then plotted with Sima Shen and said, "This man is proud. I wish to send him to the brothers of Poyang and let him take revenge on the Prince of Shixing—would that do?" Shen replied, "He will never serve in office again. Let it be as Your Majesty decrees." Fu Yi objected, "If you permit revenge, where does that leave the late emperor?" Houzhu said, "Give him a small commandery and keep him from court affairs."
48
至德元年，授永嘉內史。 喜至郡，不受奉秩，政弘清靜，人吏安之。 遇豐州刺史章大寶舉兵反，郡與豐州接，而素無備，喜乃修城隍器械，又遣兵援建安。 賊平，授南安內史。 禎明元年，徵為光祿大夫，領左驍騎將軍，道卒。 有集十卷。 子處沖嗣。
In the first year of Zhide he was appointed interior minister of Yongjia. When Xi reached his post he refused his salary. His government was clear and quiet, and the people and officials were content. When the governor of Fengzhou Zhang Dabao rebelled, Yongjia bordered Fengzhou and was unprepared. Xi repaired the walls and weapons and sent troops to aid Jian'an. When the rebels were defeated, he was appointed interior minister of Nan'an. In the first year of Zhenming he was summoned as grand master of splendid happiness and general of the left valiant cavalry, and died on the journey. He left a collected works in ten juan. His son Chuchong succeeded him.
49
沈君理字仲倫，吳興人也。 祖僧畟，梁左戶尚書。 父巡，元帝時位少府卿。 魏平荊州，梁宣帝署金紫光祿大夫。
Shen Junli, whose style name was Zhonglun, came from Wuxing. His grandfather Sengbi had been minister of the left household under the Liang. His father Xun had served as minister of the palace storehouses under Emperor Yuan. When Wei pacified Jingzhou, Emperor Xuan of Liang appointed him grand master of splendid happiness with golden seal and purple ribbon.
50
君理美風儀，博涉有識鑒。 陳武帝鎮南徐州，巡遣君理致謁，深見器重，命尚會稽長公主。 及帝受禪，拜駙馬都尉，封永安亭侯，為吳郡太守。 時兵革未甯，百姓荒弊，君理總集士卒，修飾器械，深以幹理見稱。
Junli had fine bearing and manners, read widely, and showed keen discernment. When Emperor Wu of Chen was stationed at South Xuzhou, Xun sent Junli to pay his respects. Wu greatly valued him and had him marry the elder princess of Kuaiji. When the emperor took the throne, he was appointed commandant of the horse guards, enfeoffed as Marquis of Yong'an Pavilion, and made governor of Wu commandery. War had not yet ended and the people were destitute. Junli gathered troops, repaired weapons, and was praised for capable administration.
51
文帝嗣位，累遷左戶尚書。 天嘉六年，為東陽太守。 天康元年，以父憂去職，自請往荊州迎柩。 朝議以在位重臣，難令出境，乃遣長兄君嚴往焉。 及還，將葬，詔贈巡侍中、領軍將軍，諡曰敬子。
When Emperor Wen succeeded to the throne, he rose to minister of the left household. In the sixth year of Tianjia he became governor of Dongyang. In the first year of Tiankang he left office to mourn his father and asked to go to Jingzhou to meet the coffin. The court held that as a senior minister in office he could not leave the realm, and sent his elder brother Junyan instead. When they returned for the burial, an edict posthumously honored Xun as attendant-in-chief and general of the guards, with the posthumous name Respectful.
52
太建中，歷位太子詹事，吏部尚書。 宣帝以君理女為皇太子妃，賜爵望蔡縣侯，位侍中、尚書右僕射。 卒，贈翊左將軍、開府儀同三司，諡曰貞憲。 君理弟君高、君公。
During the Taijian era he served as tutor of the heir apparent and minister of personnel. Emperor Xuan made Junli's daughter the crown prince's consort, enfeoffed him as Marquis of Wangcai, and appointed him attendant-in-chief and right vice director of the secretariat. He died and was posthumously honored as general of the left assistants and grand master with golden seal and office equal to the three excellencies, with the posthumous name Upright and Lawful. Junli's younger brothers were Jungao and Jungong.
53
君高字季高，少知名，性剛直，有吏能。 位衛尉卿，平越中郎將、都督、廣州刺史，甚得人和。 卒，諡祁子。
Jungao, whose style name was Jigao, was known from youth. He was upright by nature and skilled in administration. He served as minister of the guards, general who pacifies the Yue, commander, and governor of Guangzhou, and won great goodwill. He died and was given the posthumous name Qizi.
54
君公自梁元帝敗後，常在江陵。 禎明中，與蕭瓛、蕭岩叛隋歸陳，後主擢為太子詹事。 君公博學有才辯，善談論，後主深器之。 陳亡入隋，文帝以其叛亡，命斬于建康。
After Emperor Yuan of Liang was defeated, Jungong remained at Jiangling. In Zhenming he rebelled against Sui with Xiao Huan and Xiao Yan and returned to Chen. Houzhu promoted him to tutor of the heir apparent. Jungong was learned and eloquent, skilled at discussion, and Houzhu valued him deeply. When Chen fell he entered Sui. Emperor Wen of Sui, because of his defection, ordered him beheaded at Jianye.
55
君理第五叔邁，亦方正有幹局，位通直散騎常侍，侍東宮。
Junli's fifth uncle Mai was also upright and capable. He served as regular attendant and attended the eastern palace.
56
陸山才字孔章，吳郡吳人也。 祖翁寶，梁尚書水部郎。 父泛，中散大夫。
Lu Shancai, whose style name was Kongzhang, came from Wu in Wu commandery. His grandfather Wengbao had been director of the water bureau in the secretariat under the Liang. His father Fan had been grand master of leisure.
57
山才倜儻，好尚文史，范陽張纘、纘弟綰並欽重之。
Shancai was bold and unconventional and loved literature and history. Zhang Zuan of Fanyang and his younger brother Wan both esteemed him highly.
58
紹泰中，都督周文育出鎮南豫州，不知書疏，以山才為長史，政事悉以委之。 文育南討，克蕭勃，禽歐陽頠，計畫多出山才。 後文育重鎮豫章金口，山才復為鎮南長史、豫章太守。
In Shaotai the commander Zhou Wenyü went out to garrison South Yuzhou. Unable to handle documents himself, he made Shancai his chief secretary and entrusted all affairs to him. Wenyü campaigned south, defeated Xiao Bo, and captured Ouyang Yi. Most of the plans came from Shancai. Later Wenyü again garrisoned Jinkou in Yuzhang. Shancai again served as chief secretary of the southern garrison and governor of Yuzhang.
59
文育為熊曇朗所害，曇朗囚山才等，送于王琳。 未至，而侯安都敗琳將常眾愛，由是山才獲反。 累遷度支尚書，坐侍宴與蔡景曆言語過差，為有司所奏，免官。 尋授散騎常侍，遷西陽、武昌二郡太守。 卒，諡曰簡子。
Wenyü was killed by Xiong Tanlang. Tanlang imprisoned Shancai and the others and sent them to Wang Lin. Before they arrived, Hou Andu defeated Lin's general Chang Zhong'ai, and Shancai was able to return. He rose to minister of revenue but was dismissed after speaking improperly with Cai Jingli at a feast. He was soon appointed regular attendant and governor of Xiyang and Wuchang. He died and was given the posthumous name Jianzi.
60
論曰：趙知禮、蔡景曆屬陳武經綸之日，居文房書記之任，此乃宋、齊之初傅亮、王儉之職。 若乃校其才用，理不同年，而卒能膺務濟時，蓋其遇也。 希祥勞臣之子，才名自致，跡涉便佞，貞介所羞。 元饒始終任遇，無虧公道，名位自卒，其殆優乎。 子高權重為戮，亦其宜也。 華皎經綸雲始，既蹈元功，殷憂之辰，自同勁草，雖致奔敗，未足為非。 師知送往多闕，見忌新主，謀人之義，可無慎哉； 然晚遇誅夷，非其過也。 毛喜逢時遇主，好謀而成，見廢昏朝，不致公輔，惜矣。 沈、陸所以見重，固亦雅望之所致焉。
Appraisal: Zhao Zhili and Cai Jingli served during the days when Chen Wu was building the state, holding the posts of literary secretaries—the same offices Fu Liang and Wang Jian held at the beginning of Song and Qi. Compared in talent and employment, they were not equals, yet both could serve the times effectively—this was their good fortune. Xixiang, son of a meritorious minister, won fame by his own talent, but his conduct touched on flattery, which the upright despise. Yuanrao enjoyed trust from beginning to end without compromising justice and died in honor—was he not perhaps the superior man? Zigao's power was excessive and his execution was fitting. Hua Jiao helped build the state from the beginning and shared in its founding merit. In days of deep trouble he stood firm as tough grass. Though he met defeat, that was not enough to condemn him. Shizhi had many lapses in the matter of the deposed emperor and was resented by the new ruler. In plotting against others, could one not be more cautious? Yet his late execution was not his fault. Mao Xi met the right time and the right lord, planned well and succeeded, yet was cast aside by a benighted court and never reached the highest offices—a pity. The esteem in which Shen and Lu were held was indeed due to their refined reputations.
