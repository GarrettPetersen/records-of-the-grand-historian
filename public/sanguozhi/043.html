黃權，字公衡，巴西閬中人也。 少為郡吏，州牧劉璋召為主簿。 時別駕張鬆建議，宜迎先主，使伐張魯。 權諫曰：「左將軍有驍名，今請到，欲以部曲遇之，則不滿其心，欲以賓客禮待，則一國不容二君。 若容有泰山之安，則主有累卵之危。 可但閉境，以待河清。 」璋不聽，竟遣使迎先生，出權為廣漢長，及先主襲取益州，將帥分下郡縣。 郡縣風景附，權閉城堅守，須劉璋稽服，乃詣降先主。 先主假權偏將軍。 〈徐眾評曰：權既忠諫於主，又閉城拒守，得事君之禮。 武王下車，封比干之墓，表商容之閭，所以大顯忠賢之士，而明示所貴之旨。 先主假權將軍，善矣，然猶薄少，未足彰忠義之高節，而大勸為善者之心。〉 及曹公破張魯，魯走入巴中，權進曰：「若失漢中，則三巴不振，此為割蜀之股臂也。 」於是先主以權為護軍，率諸將迎魯，魯已還南鄭，北降曹公。 然卒破杜濩、樸胡，殺夏候淵，據漢中，皆權本謀也。
Huang Quan, courtesy name Gongheng, came from Langzhong in Ba Commandery. As a young man he served in the commandery administration; Provincial Governor Liu Zhang then appointed him chief clerk. At the time, Deputy Governor Zhang Song urged Liu Zhang to invite Liu Bei westward and use him against Zhang Lu. Huang Quan objected: "The General of the Left is famous as a fighting man. Having invited him in, if you treat him only as a subordinate army he will not be content; if you receive him as an honored guest, one realm cannot hold two masters. You may imagine that keeps you as secure as Mount Tai, but it will leave your master in peril stacked as high as eggs. Better seal the passes and bide your time until Heaven sorts matters out. " Liu Zhang refused to heed him and sent messengers to welcome Liu Bei, sidelining Huang Quan with an appointment as magistrate of Guanghan. When Liu Bei struck and took Yi Province, his commanders spread through the towns and districts. County after county went over to Liu Bei; Huang Quan shut his gates and held out until Liu Zhang had formally yielded, then he went in person to surrender to Liu Bei. Liu Bei named Huang Quan acting General of the Left Flank. < Xu Zhong comments: Huang Quan had spoken frankly for his master and then defended his walls—conduct fit for a faithful minister. When King Wu of Zhou entered the conquered Shang capital, he honored Bi Gan's grave and marked Shang Rong's lane—making plain whom he esteemed and giving loyal worthies their due. Liu Bei's provisional appointment of Huang Quan as general was decent as far as it went, yet it fell short of honoring such steadfast loyalty or firing others to virtue. After Cao Cao defeated Zhang Lu and Lu fled into Ba, Huang Quan urged Liu Bei: "Lose Hanzhong and the Three Ba lose heart—you would be severing Shu’s arm and leg. " Liu Bei then made Huang Quan chief of the Protectorate of the Army and marched to meet Zhang Lu, but Lu had already withdrawn to Nan Zheng and gone over to Cao Cao. The victories over Du Hu and Pu Hu, the slaying of Xiahou Yuan, and the seizure of Hanzhong all stemmed from Huang Quan's strategy.
2
後領益州刺史，徙佔河南。 大將軍司馬宣王深器之，問權曰：「蜀中有卿輩幾人？ 」權笑而答曰：「不圖明公見顧之重也！ 」宣王與諸葛亮書曰：「黃公衡，快士也，每坐起歎述足下，不去口實。 」景初三年，蜀延熙二年，權遷車騎將軍、儀同三司。 〈《蜀記》曰：魏明帝問權：「天下鼎立，當以何地為正？ 」權對曰：「當以天文為正。 往者熒惑守心而文皇帝崩，吳、蜀二主平安，此其徵也。」〉 明年卒，謚曰景侯。 子邕嗣、邕無子，絕。 權留蜀子祟，為尚書郎，隨衛將軍諸葛瞻拒鄧艾。 到涪縣，瞻盤桓未近，祟屢勸瞻宜速行據險，無令敵得入平地。 瞻猶與未納，祟至於流涕。 會艾長驅而前，瞻卻戰綿竹，祟帥厲軍士，期於必死，臨陣見殺。
He later governed Yi Province and resettled his family south of the Yellow River; standard editions use wording meaning ‘moved residence to Henan,’ i.e. south of the river. Grand Commander Sima Yi thought highly of him and asked, "How many men like you has Shu to offer? " Huang Quan laughed and said, "I never dreamed Your Grace would think so well of me! " Sima Yi wrote to Zhuge Liang: "Huang Gongheng is a blunt, candid man—rise or sit, he never stops speaking well of you. " In Wei’s third year of Jingchu (239), Shu’s second year of Yanxi, Huang Quan was promoted to General of Chariots and Cavalry with honors matching the Three Ducal Ministers. < According to the Shu Ji, Wei Mingdi asked Huang Quan which of the three realms held legitimate mandate. " Huang Quan answered that legitimacy rests with what Heaven shows in the stars. When Mars stayed in the “heart” asterism, Emperor Wen of Wei died, yet the rulers of Wu and Shu were unharmed—a sign, he said, of where Heaven’s favor lay." > He died the following year and received the posthumous title Marquis Jing. His son Huang Yong inherited the marquisate; Yong left no heir, so the title lapsed. A son he had left in Shu, Huang Chong, served as a court secretary under General of the Guard Zhuge Zhan during the campaign against Deng Ai. At Fu, Zhuge Zhan hesitated and advanced slowly; Huang Chong urged him repeatedly to seize the mountain passes before the enemy could reach open ground. Zhuge Zhan still would not heed him, and Chong wept in frustration. Deng Ai then advanced in force; Zhuge Zhan fell back to fight at Mianzhu. Huang Chong rallied the troops for a last stand and died in the fighting.
3
先主薨，高定恣睢於越巂，雍闓跋扈於建寧，朱褒反叛於牂牁。 丞相亮南征，先由越俊，而恢案道向建寧。 諸縣大相糾合，圍恢軍於昆明。 時恢眾少敵倍，又未得亮聲息，繪謂南人曰：「官軍糧盡，欲規退還，吾中間久斥鄉里，乃今得旋，不能復北，欲還與汝等同計謀，故以誠相告。 」南人信之，故圍守怠緩。 於是恢出擊，大破之，追奔逐北，南至槃江，東接牂牁，與亮聲勢相連。
After Liu Bei’s death, Gao Ding bullied the tribes in Yuexi, Yong Kai defied the court from Jianning, and Zhu Bao rose in rebellion in Zangke. Zhuge Liang marched south through Yuexi while Li Hui advanced toward Jianning; this chapter writes ‘Yuejun’ where other editions read ‘Yuexi,’ the modern Liangshan region. County militias combined and pinned Li Hui’s force at Kunming. Li Hui was outnumbered and cut off from Zhuge Liang’s word; feigning desertion, he told the southerners that government troops had run out of supplies and would retreat—that after years away from home he could finally turn south instead of north and wished to join their councils. " They believed him, and their siege slackened. Li Hui then sallied forth, routed them, and drove the fugitives south to the Pan River and east toward Zangke, linking up with Zhuge Liang’s advance.
4
南土平定，恢軍功居多。 封漢興亭侯，加安漢將軍。 後軍還，南夷復叛，殺害守將。 恢身往撲討，鋤盡惡類，徙其豪帥於成都，賦出叟、濮耕牛戰馬金銀犀革，充繼軍資，於時費用不乏。 建興七年，以交州屬吳，解恢刺史。 更領建寧太守，以還居本郡。 徙居漢中，九年卒。 子遺嗣，恢弟子球，羽林右部督，隨諸葛瞻拒鄧艾，臨陣授命，死於綿竹。
With the south subdued, most of the credit went to Li Hui’s column. He was made Village Marquis of Hanxing and promoted to General Who Pacifies Han. After the army withdrew, southern tribes rebelled again and slew the garrison commanders. Li Hui campaigned in person, extirpated the ringleaders, relocated their headmen to Chengdu, and levied cattle, horses, precious metals, and hides from the Sou and Pu peoples—keeping the army supplied without shortage. In Jianxing 7 (229), when Shu ceded suzerainty over Jiao Province to Wu, Li Hui was recalled from the governorship. He was given concurrent appointment as Administrator of Jianning and so returned to govern his home region. He later moved to Hanzhong and died in the ninth year (of Jianxing, 231). His son Li Yi inherited the title; his nephew Li Qiu commanded the right wing of the imperial guard and died beside Zhuge Zhan fighting Deng Ai at Mianzhu.

天降喪亂，奸雄乘釁，天下切齒，萬國悲悼，臣妾大小，莫不思竭筋力，肝腦塗地，以除國難。 伏惟將軍世受漢思，以為當躬聚黨眾，率先啟行，上以報國家，下不負先人，書功竹帛，遺名千載。 何期臣僕吳越，背本就末乎？ 昔舜勤民事，隕於蒼梧，書籍嘉之，流聲無窮。 崩於江浦，何足可悲！ 文、武受命，成王乃平。 先帝龍興，海內望風，宰臣聰睿，自天降康。 而將軍不睹盛衰之紀，成敗之符。 譬如野火在原，蹈覆河冰，火滅水泮，將何所依附？ 囊者將軍先君雍候，造怨而封，竇融知興，歸志世祖，皆流名後葉，世歌其美。 今諸葛丞相英才挺出，深睹未萌，受遺託孤，翊贊季興，與眾無忌，錄功忘瑕。 將軍若能翻然改圖，易跡更步，古人不難追，鄙土何足宰哉！ 蓋聞楚國不恭，齊桓是責，夫差僭號，晉人不長。 況臣與非主，誰肯歸之邪？ 竊惟古義，臣無越境之交，是以前後有來無往。 重承告示，發憤忘食，故略陳所懷，惟將軍察焉。
Heaven sent disaster and rebels seized their chance; every loyal heart raged, every household mourned, and high or low we longed to spend our strength—our lives if need be—to lift the realm from peril. Your family has enjoyed Han favor for generations—you should rally your followers, march in the vanguard, repay the dynasty above and your ancestors below, and earn a place in the histories. How could you choose instead to play vassal to the Wu and Yue regimes—abandoning the trunk for a twig? Ancient Shun wore himself out for the people and died at Cangwu—the classics honor him and his name still endures. Let lesser men perish on nameless riverbanks — beside Emperor Shun’s sacrifice that counts for nothing worth mourning. Kings Wen and Wu received Heaven’s charge, and King Cheng finished securing the realm. Our late emperor rose like a dragon; the realm turned to him; wise ministers appeared and Heaven sent peace. Yet you refuse to read the signs of rise and fall, victory and defeat. You are like someone crossing spring ice while grass fires rage—when the flames die and the ice breaks, what footing will you have? Your father the Marquis of Yong once turned hostility into enfeoffment; Dou Rong read the times and pledged to Emperor Guangwu—both won lasting fame because they chose the rising house. Chancellor Zhuge Liang is a genius who reads trouble before it shows; he holds the late emperor’s mandate, steadies the restoration, shares credit openly, and rewards merit while overlooking fault. If you reverse course now, you may still match the ancients in loyalty—this poor soil of Yongchang is hardly worth clinging to as a petty king. When Chu defied the league, Duke Huan of Qi called them to account; when King Fuchai usurped royal style, the lords of Jin did not let it pass for long. How much less should we serve a lord who lacks legitimacy—who would follow such a man? By ancient protocol ministers do not negotiate across borders without leave—hence your messengers came here with no reply from us. Your letter stirred me so deeply I forgot my meal; I lay out my thoughts plainly for your judgment.

Lü Kai’s authority and kindness were felt at home, and the commandery trusted him, so he could keep his honor intact.
8
及丞相亮南征討闓，既發在道，而闓已為高定部曲所殺。 亮至南，上表曰：「永昌郡吏呂凱、府丞王伉等，執忠絕域，十有餘年，雍闓、高定逼其東北，而凱等守義不與交通。 臣不意永昌風俗敦直乃爾！ 」以凱為雲南太守，封陽遷亭侯，會為叛夷所害，子祥嗣。 而王伉亦封亭侯，為永昌太守。 〈《蜀世譜》曰：呂祥後為晉南夷校尉，祥子及孫世為永昌太守。 李雄破寧州，諸呂不肯附，舉郡固守。 王伉等亦守正節。〉
Zhuge Liang had barely marched south against Yong Kai when word came that Gao Ding’s men had already killed him; this chapter writes Yong Kai’s name with an alternate graph. When Zhuge Liang arrived he memorialized: "Clerk Lü Kai of Yongchang, Assistant Wang Kang, and their comrades have kept faith in a remote border for more than a decade, cut off to the northeast by Yong Kai and Gao Ding, yet they never dealt with the rebels. I never imagined Yongchang still harbored such steadfast integrity! " He appointed Lü Kai Administrator of Yunnan and enfeoffed him as Village Marquis of Yangqian; when tribal rebels later murdered him, his son Lü Xiang inherited the title. Wang Kang too received a village marquisate and was appointed Administrator of Yongchang. < According to the Shu genealogical tables, Lü Xiang later served Jin as Colonel of the Southern Yi; his descendants held Yongchang for generations. When Li Xiong overran Ningzhou, the Lü clan refused submission and defended the whole commandery. Wang Kang and his fellows likewise died loyal to the end.>
9
王平字子均，巴西宕渠人也。 本養外家何氏。 後復姓王。 隨杜濩、樸胡詣洛陽，假校尉，從曹公征漢中，因降先主，拜牙門將、裨將軍。 建興六年，屬參軍馬謖先鋒。 謖捨水上山，舉措煩擾，平連規諫謖，謖不能用，大敗於街亭。 眾盡星散，惟平所領千人鳴鼓自持，魏將張郃疑其伏兵，不往逼也。 於是平徐徐收合諸營遺迸，率將士而還。 丞相亮既誅馬謖及將軍張休、李盛，奪將軍黃襲等兵。 平特見崇顯，加拜參軍，統五部兼當營事，進位討寇將軍，封亭侯。 九年，亮圍祁山，平別守南圍。 魏大將軍司馬宣王攻亮，張郃攻平，平堅守不動，郃不能克。
Wang Ping, courtesy name Zijun, came from Dangqu in Ba Commandery. He had been raised in his maternal He family. He later took back the surname Wang. He accompanied Du Hu and Pu Hu to Luoyang, received a brevet colonel’s rank, and joined Cao Cao’s Hanzhong campaign, then went over to Liu Bei and was made a camp-gate general and subordinate general. In Jianxing 6 (228) he served under Army Adviser Ma Su as an advance officer. Ma Su camped on the hills and gave up the water, throwing the army into confusion. Wang Ping warned him again and again, but Ma Su ignored the advice and was crushed at Jieting. The army broke and ran, but Wang Ping’s thousand men kept up a steady drumbeat; Zhang He suspected a trap and dared not close in. Wang Ping then regrouped the stragglers from every camp and marched the men home in good order. After Zhuge Liang executed Ma Su, Zhang Xiu, and Li Sheng, he stripped other generals such as Huang Xi of their commands. Wang Ping alone was singled out for praise—promoted to army adviser, given five brigades with charge of camp affairs, raised to General Who Punishes Raiders, and made a village marquis. In Jianxing 9 (231), while Zhuge Liang besieged Mount Qi, Wang Ping held the southern perimeter. When Sima Yi struck Zhuge Liang’s main force, Zhang He assaulted Wang Ping’s sector; Wang Ping held firm and Zhang He could not break him.
10
十二年，亮卒於武功，軍退還，魏延作亂，一戰而敗，平之功也。 遷後典軍、安漢將軍，副車騎將軍吳壹住漢中，又領軍漢中太守。 十五年，進封安漢侯，代壹督漢中。 延熙元年，大將軍蔣琬住沔陽，平更為前護軍，署琬府事。 六年，琬還住涪，拜平前監軍、鎮北大將軍，統漢中。
In Jianxing 12 (234), after Zhuge Liang died at Wugong and the army withdrew, Wei Yan mutinied and was crushed in a single engagement—credit belonged to Wang Ping. He was promoted to Assistant Director of the Army and General Who Pacifies Han, second to General of Chariots and Cavalry Wu Yi in the Hanzhong garrison, while holding concurrent authority as Administrator of Hanzhong. In Jianxing 15 (237) he was elevated to Marquis Who Pacifies Han and succeeded Wu Yi as commander at Hanzhong. In Yanxi 1 (238), with Grand General Jiang Wan posted at Mianyang, Wang Ping became Forward Protector of the Army and managed affairs in Jiang Wan’s headquarters. In Yanxi 6 (243), when Jiang Wan moved his headquarters to Fu, Wang Ping was named Forward Army Inspector and Grand General Who Guards the North, with overall command of Hanzhong.
11
七年春，魏大將軍曹爽率步騎十餘萬向漢川，前鋒已在駱谷。 時漢中守兵不滿三萬，諸將大驚。 或曰：「今力不足以拒敵，聽當固守漢、樂二城，遇賊令入，比爾間，涪軍足得救關。 」平曰：「不然。 漢中去涪垂千里。 賊若得關，便為禍也。 今宜先遣劉護軍、杜參軍據興勢，平為後拒。 若賊分向黃金，平率千人下自臨之，比爾間，涪軍行至，此計之上也。 」惟護軍劉敏與平意同，即便施行。 涪諸軍及費禕自成都相繼而至，魏軍退還，如平本策。 是時，鄧芝在東，馬忠在南，平在北境，咸著名跡。
In the spring of Yanxi 7 (244), Wei Grand General Cao Shuang advanced on Hanzhong with well over a hundred thousand foot and horse; his van had already entered Lu Valley. Fewer than thirty thousand men held Hanzhong, and the commanders panicked. Some urged: "We cannot meet them in the field—hold Hanxing and Lecheng and let the enemy through the mountains; reinforcements from Fu can relieve the passes before it is too late. " Wang Ping replied, "That would be a mistake. Hanzhong lies almost a thousand li from Fu. If they seize the passes, disaster follows. Send Protector of the Army Liu and Army Adviser Du to seize Xingshi first—I will cover the rear. If they split toward Huangjin, I will take a thousand men downhill to hold them; by then Fu’s relief column will arrive—the soundest plan. " Only Protector of the Army Liu Min agreed with Wang Ping, and they carried it out at once. Reinforcements from Fu and Fei Yi’s troops from Chengdu arrived in succession; the Wei army withdrew exactly as Wang Ping had predicted. Deng Zhi held the east, Ma Zhong the south, Wang Ping the northern frontier—each won renown in his theater.
12
平生長戎旅，手不能書，其所識不過十字，而口授作書，皆有意理。 使人讀《史》、《漢》諸紀傳，聽之，備知其大義，往往論說不失其指。 遵履法度，言不戲諺，從朝至夕，端坐徹日，懷無武將之體。 然性狹侵疑，為人自輕，以此為損焉。 十一年卒，子訓嗣。
Wang Ping grew up in the ranks; he could barely write a handful of characters, yet dictating letters to scribes he always produced clear, sensible prose. He had aides read him the Shiji and Hanshu; he grasped their main arguments and could discuss them without missing the point. He observed regulations strictly, never indulged in coarse soldier’s humor, and sat bolt upright from dawn to dusk—yet carried himself without the swagger usual in fighting men. Yet he was narrow-minded and quick to take offense, prone to sell himself short—a flaw in an otherwise formidable commander. He died in the eleventh year of Yanxi (248); his son Wang Xun inherited his rank.

張嶷字伯岐，巴郡南充國人也。 〈《益部耆舊傳》曰：嶷出自孤微，而少有通壯之節。〉 弱冠為縣功曹。 先主定蜀之際，山寇攻縣，縣長捐家逃亡，嶷冒白刃，攜負夫人，夫人得免。 由是顯名，州召為從事。 時郡內士人龔祿、姚冑位二千石，當世有聲名，皆與嶷友善。 建興五年，丞相亮北住漢中，廣漢綿竹山賊張慕等鈔盜軍資，劫略吏民，嶷以都尉將兵討之。 嶷度其鳥散，難以戰禽。 乃詐與和親，剋期置酒。 酒酣，嶷身率左右，因斬慕等五十餘級，渠帥悉珍。 尋其餘類，旬日清泰。 後得疾病困篤，家素貧匱。 廣漢太守蜀郡何祗，名為通厚，嶷宿與疏闊，乃自輿詣祗，託以治疾。 祗傾托醫療，數年除愈。 其黨道信義皆此類也。 拜為牙門將，屬馬忠，北討汶山叛羌，南平四郡蠻夷，輒有籌畫戰克之功。 〈《益部耆舊傳》曰：嶷受兵馬三百人，隨馬忠討叛羌。 嶷別督數營在先，至他裡。 邑所在高峻，嶷隨山立上四五里。 羌於要厄作石門，於門上施床，積石於其上，過者下石槌擊之，無不糜爛。 嶷度不可得攻，乃使譯告曉之曰：「汝汶山諸種反叛，傷害良善，天子命將討滅惡類。 汝等若稽顙過軍，資給糧費，福祿永隆，其報百倍。 若終不從，大兵致誅，雷擊電下，雖追悔之，亦無益也。 」耆帥得命，即出詣嶷，給糧過軍。 軍前討餘種，餘種聞他裡已下，悉恐怖失所，或迎軍出降，或奔竄山谷，放兵攻擊，軍以克捷。 後南夷劉冑又反，以馬忠為督庲降討冑，嶷复屬焉，戰鬥常冠軍首，遂斬冑。 平南事訖，牂牁興古獠種復反，忠令嶷領諸營往討，嶷內招降得二千人，悉傳詣漢中。〉
Zhang Ni, courtesy name Boqi, came from Nanchong in Ba Commandery. < The Yi-region elders’ tradition calls Zhang Ni a man of humble birth who showed stalwart resolve even as a youth.> When he came of age he served as merit clerk of his county. While Liu Bei was securing Shu, hill bandits stormed the county; the magistrate fled and abandoned his household. Zhang Ni ran through blade strokes and bore his superior’s wife to safety. He won fame for it and the provincial administration appointed him attendant clerk. County notables such as Gong Lu and Yao Zhou, senior officials with eminent reputations, all befriended him. In Jianxing 5 (227), while Zhuge Liang held Hanzhong, the Mianzhu bandits led by Zhang Mu raided army stores and civilians. Zhang Ni, as a colonel, took troops against them. Zhang Ni knew they would splinter like a flock of birds and could not be run down in open battle. He feigned a marriage alliance, set a date, and invited them to a feast. When the wine was flowing, Zhang Ni took his men in and cut down Zhang Mu and more than fifty others, wiping out the ringleaders (the text has a graph that should read ‘exterminate’). He hunted down the rest; within ten days the district was quiet. Later he fell gravely ill while his family remained desperately poor. He Zhi of Guanghan had a reputation for generosity though Zhang Ni barely knew him; Zhang Ni had himself carried to He Zhi’s door and begged him for treatment. He Zhi threw every resource into his care, and after several years Zhang Ni recovered. His circle honored loyalty and good faith in just this way. He was promoted to camp-gate general under Ma Zhong, campaigned north against the Wenshan Qiang and south through the four southern commanderies, winning every engagement he planned. < The Yi elders’ tradition records that Zhang Ni took three hundred horse and foot under Ma Zhong against the rebel Qiang. Zhang Ni led several camps on the advance guard and reached the settlement of Tali. The town sat on a high ridge; Zhang Ni climbed four or five li of mountain trail. The Qiang walled a stone gate across the defile, piled rocks on ledges above the portal, and smashed every traveler beneath with stone hammers—no one survived. Seeing no way to storm the pass, Zhang Ni sent interpreters: "Your Wenshan tribes have rebelled and preyed on the innocent; the emperor has sent armies to destroy such evil. Bow before our host, furnish grain and supplies, and your rewards will multiply a hundredfold. Refuse, and the full army will fall on you like thunder—too late then for regret. " The headman accepted the terms, came down to Zhang Ni, and fed the column through the pass. The army then rolled up the other bands: hearing Tali had fallen, they panicked—some surrendered to the columns, some scattered into the hills; Zhang Ni’s men pursued and crushed them. When the southern leader Liu Zhou rose again, Ma Zhong took command of the Zhangxi campaign and Zhang Ni fought under him, always leading the van until Zhou was beheaded. After the south was quiet, tribes in Zangke and Xinggu rebelled again; Ma Zhong sent Zhang Ni at the head of several camps. Zhang Ni won two thousand surrendering tribesmen and sent them all to Hanzhong.>
15
十四年，武都氐王苻健請降，遣將軍張尉往迎，過期不到，大將軍蔣琬深以為念。 嶷平之曰：「苻健求附款至，必無他變。 素聞健弟狡黠，又夷狄不能同功，將有乖離，是以稽留耳。 」數日，問至，健弟果將四百戶就魏，獨健來從。 初，越巂郡自丞相亮討高定之後，叟夷數反，殺太守龔祿、焦璜，是後太守不敢之郡，只住安定縣，去郡八百餘里，其郡徒有名而已。 時論欲復舊郡，除嶷為越巂太守，嶷將所領往之郡，誘以恩情，蠻夷皆服，頗來降附。 北徼捉馬最驍勁，不承節度，嶷乃往討，生縛其帥魏狼。 又解縱告喻，使招懷餘類。 表拜狼為邑侯，種落三千餘戶皆安土供職。 諸種聞之，多漸降服。 嶷以功賜爵關內侯。
In Yanxi 14 (251) the Wudu Di chieftain Fu Jian offered to defect; Shu dispatched General Zhang Wei to escort him. When the escort failed to appear on time, Jiang Wan grew deeply anxious. Zhang Ni reassured him: "Fu Jian’s offer of allegiance is sincere; nothing else is afoot. His younger brother is treacherous, and frontier peoples seldom pull together—some split was inevitable, hence the delay. " A few days later word arrived: the brother had taken four hundred households to Wei, while Fu Jian alone came over to Shu. After Zhuge Liang’s campaign against Gao Ding, the Sou tribes of Yuexi rose repeatedly, killing Administrators Gong Lu and Jiao Huang. Later appointees never dared enter the seat, camping eight hundred li away at Anding—the commandery existed on paper only. The court resolved to restore the old seat and named Zhang Ni Administrator. He marched his command into the territory, winning people with kindness until many tribes submitted. The Zhuoma horsemen on the northern marches were violent and defied orders; Zhang Ni attacked, took their chief Wei Lang alive, and bound him. He then freed Wei Lang and used him to bring the rest of the tribe to terms. He memorialized to enfeoff Wei Lang as a village marquis; more than three thousand households settled down and paid their dues. Other clans heard of it and steadily came over. For this merit Zhang Ni received the rank of Marquis Within the Passes.
16
蘇祁邑君冬逢、逢弟魄渠等，已降復反。 嶷誅逢。 逢妻，旄牛王女，嶷以計原之。 而渠逃入西徼。 渠剛猛捷悍，為諸種深所畏憚，遣所親二人詐降嶷，實取消息。 嶷覺之，許以重賞，使為反間，二人遂合謀殺渠。 渠死，諸種皆安。 又斯都耆帥李求承昔手殺龔祿，嶷求募捕得，數其宿惡而誅之。 始嶷以郡郛宇頹壞，更築小塢。 在官三年，徙還故郡，繕治城郭，夷種男女莫不致力。』
The Suqi headmen Dong Feng and his brother Po Qu had submitted once, then rose again. Zhang Ni executed Dong Feng. Dong Feng’s wife was a princess of the Maoniu king; Zhang Ni spared her by design. Po Qu fled into the western hills. Po Qu was fierce and feared by every tribe; he sent two trusted men under false surrender to spy on Zhang Ni. Zhang Ni saw through it, bought them with rich promises, and turned them as double agents—they murdered Po Qu together. With Po Qu dead the tribes quieted down. The Sidu leader Li Qiucheng had once murdered Gong Lu with his own hand; Zhang Ni had him hunted down, listed his crimes, and executed him. At first, finding the commandery walls ruined, Zhang Ni built a smaller fortified compound. After three years he returned to his former seat, rebuilt the walls, and men and women of every tribe labored beside him.』
17
定莋、台登、卑水三縣去郡三百餘里，舊出鹽鐵及漆，而夷徼久自固食。 嶷率所領奪取，署長吏焉。 嶷之到定莋，定莋率豪狼岑，盤木王舅，甚為蠻夷所信任，忿嶷自侵，不自來詣。 嶷使壯士數十直往收致，撻而殺之，持屍還種，厚加賞賜。 喻以狼岑之惡，且曰：「無得妄動，動即殄矣！ 」種類咸面縛謝過。 嶷殺午饗宴，重申恩信。 遂獲鹽鐵，器用周贍。
Dingzuo, Taideng, and Beishui lay three hundred li from the seat, rich in salt, iron, and lacquer that frontier tribes had kept for themselves for generations. Zhang Ni seized them by force and installed his own magistrates. At Dingzuo the magnate Langcen—uncle of the Panmu king—was influential among the tribes and refused to answer Zhang Ni’s summons, resenting what he saw as intrusion. Zhang Ni sent dozens of picked men to seize Langcen, executed him after a flogging, returned the body to his people, and lavished gifts on them. He explained Langcen’s crimes and warned: "Stay still—any move will mean your annihilation! " The clans came bound and begged forgiveness. Zhang Ni then gave them a midday feast and renewed his promises of goodwill. Salt and iron flowed again, and supplies grew plentiful.
18
漢嘉郡界旄牛夷種類四千餘戶，其率狼路，欲為姑婿冬逢報怨。 遣叔父離將，逢眾相度形勢。 嶷逆遣親近繼牛酒勞賜，又令離姊逆逢妻宣暢意旨。 離既受賜，並見其姊，姊弟歡悅，悉率所領將詣嶷，嶷厚加賞待，遣還。 旄牛由是輒不為患。
Four thousand Maoniu households in Hanjia followed chief Langlu, who meant to avenge his brother-in-law Dong Feng. He sent his uncle Li at the head of Dong Feng’s followers to scout the ground. Zhang Ni preempted him with cattle and wine sent through trusted agents and had Li’s sister—Dong Feng’s widow—carry his terms. Li accepted the gifts, met his sister, and the siblings persuaded their followers to visit Zhang Ni, who rewarded them generously and sent them home. The Maoniu tribes ceased to be a threat.
19
郡有舊道，經旄牛中至成都，既平且近。 自旄牛絕道，已百餘年，更由安上，既險且遠。 疑遣左右資貨幣賜路，重令路姑喻意，路乃率兄弟妻子悉詣疑，疑與盟誓，開通舊道，千里肅清，復古亭繹。 奏封路為旋牛毗王，遣使將路朝貢。 後主於是加疑撫戎將軍，領郡如故。 嶷初見費禕為大將軍，恣性泛愛，待信新附不過，嶷書戒之曰：「昔岑彭率師，來歙杖節，咸見害於刺客，今明將軍位尊權重，宜鑒前事，少以為警。 」後禕果為魏降人郭脩所害。
An old road once ran through Maoniu to Chengdu—level and short. For a century the Maoniu stretch had been closed, forcing travelers onto the long, dangerous Anshang detour. Zhang Ni sent gifts to Langlu through his aunt until Langlu brought his whole household in to swear allegiance. They reopened the old road, cleared a thousand li of trouble, and restored the postal stations. He obtained an edict naming Langlu king of the Maoniu people and escorted him to Chengdu to present tribute. The emperor then added Zhang Ni the title General Who Pacifies the Rong while he continued as administrator. When Fei Yi became grand general, Zhang Ni saw him trusting new allies too freely and wrote: "Cen Peng and Lai She died by assassins’ blades; your station is still higher—learn from them and stay guarded. " Fei Yi was indeed murdered soon after by the Wei defector Guo Xiu.

吳太傅諸葛恪以初破魏軍，大興兵眾以圖攻取。 侍中諸葛瞻，丞相亮之子，恪從弟也。 嶷與書曰：
Elated by his first victory over Wei, Wu’s Grand Tutor Zhuge Ke mobilized a massive host for further conquests. Palace Attendant Zhuge Zhan—Zhuge Liang’s son and Zhuge Ke’s cousin—was the man to receive the letter. Zhang Ni wrote to him:
21
東主初崩，帝實幼弱，太傅受寄託之重，亦何容易！ 親以周公之才，猶有管、蔡流言之變，霍光受任，亦有燕、蓋、上官逆亂之謀，賴成、昭之明，以免斯難耳。 昔每聞東主殺生賞罰，不任下人，又今以垂沒之命，卒召太傅，屬以後事，誠實可慮。 加吳、楚剽急，乃昔所記，而太傅離少主，履敵庭，恐非良計長算之術也。 雖云東家綱紀肅然，上下輯睦，百有一失，非明者之慮邪？ 取古則今，今則古也，自非郎君進忠言於太傅，誰復有盡言者也！ 旋軍廣農，務行德惠，數年之中，東西並舉，實為不晚，願深採察。
Your sovereign has just died, the boy emperor is frail, and the Grand Tutor shoulders the whole regency—nothing about this moment is safe. Even the Duke of Zhou faced the rebellion of his brothers; Huo Guang endured plots from princes and ministers until sober rulers restored order. Your late ruler trusted no one with life-and-death authority; on his deathbed he summoned the Grand Tutory alone—that legacy invites peril. Wu men are famously rash; for the regent to march deep into enemy lands while the orphan sovereign stays home is no sound strategy. They tell you eastern Wu is orderly—yet even perfect discipline admits one slip in a hundred; surely a thoughtful man guards against it? History judges present failures by ancient precedent—if you do not speak plainly to your cousin, who will? Withdraw the army, enrich the farms, and win hearts with kindness—within a few years you might strike east and west together; it would not be too late. Please weigh this carefully.

恪竟以此夷族。 嶷識見多如是類。
Zhuge Ke ignored the warning and his whole clan was slaughtered for it. Zhang Ni’s foresight often ran this true.

在郡十五年，邦域安穆。 屢乞求還，乃徵詣成都。 夷民戀慕，扶轂泣涕，過旄牛邑，邑君襁負來迎，及追尋至蜀郡界，其督相率隨嶷朝貢者百餘人。 嶷至，拜蕩寇將軍，慷慨壯烈，士人咸多貴之。 然放蕩少禮，人亦以此譏焉， 〈《益部耆舊傳》曰：時車騎將軍夏侯霸謂嶷曰：「雖與足下疏闊，然託心如舊，宜明此意。 」嶷答曰：「僕未知子，子未知我，大道在彼，何云託心乎！ 原三年之後徐陳斯言。 」有識之士以為美談。〉 是歲延熙十七年也。 魏狄道長李簡密書請降，衛將軍姜維率嶷等因簡之資以出隴西。 〈《益部耆舊傳》曰：嶷風濕固疾，至都浸篤，扶杖然後能起。 李簡請降，眾議狐疑，而嶷曰必然。 姜維之出，時論以嶷初還，股疾不能在行中，由是嶷自乞肆力中原，致身敵庭。 臨發，辭後主曰：「臣當值聖明，受恩過量，加以疾病在身，常恐一朝隕沒，辜負榮遇。 天不違原，得豫戎事。 若涼州克定，臣為籓表守將； 若有未捷，殺身以報。 」後主慨然為之流涕。〉 既到狄道，簡悉率城中吏民出迎軍。 軍前與魏將徐質交鋒，嶷臨陣隕身，然其所殺傷亦過倍。 既亡，封長子瑛西鄉侯，次子護雄襲爵。 南土越崔巂民夷聞嶷死，無不悲泣，為嶷立廟，四時水旱輒祀之。 〈《益部耆舊傳》曰：余觀張嶷儀貌辭令，不能駭人，而其策略足以入算，果烈足以立威，為臣有忠誠之節，處類有亮直之風，而動必顧典，後主深崇之。 雖古之英士，何以遠逾哉！ 《蜀世譜》曰：嶷孫奕，晉梁州刺史。〉
For fifteen years he governed Yuexi in quiet order. He begged repeatedly to come home until the court recalled him to Chengdu. Tribesmen clung to his wheels in tears; the Maoniu headman brought infants on his back to greet him; more than a hundred chiefs followed him to the Shu border to bid farewell. At court he was named General Who Sweeps the Raiders—fiery and outspoken, and the Ru scholars admired him for it. Others mocked him for loose manners and blunt ceremony. < The Yi elders relate that General of Chariots and Cavalry Xiahou Ba told Zhang Ni: "We seldom meet, yet I trust you as an old friend—let there be no misunderstanding between us. " Zhang Ni answered: "You do not know me, nor I you—how can we speak of sworn hearts while higher duty still stands between us? Ask me again in three years and we may speak of friendship. " Thoughtful men praised the exchange as exemplary.> That was the seventeenth year of Yanxi (254). When Li Jian, magistrate of Wei’s Didao, secretly offered to defect, Jiang Wei marched west with Zhang Ni and used Li’s stores to open a route into Longxi. < The tradition adds that chronic rheumatism left Zhang Ni barely able to rise without a staff once he reached the capital. Court opinion doubted Li Jian’s offer; Zhang Ni alone insisted it was genuine. When Jiang Wei marched, others argued Zhang Ni was too lame from his recent return to ride with the army—so Zhang Ni volunteered to serve in the interior, striking deep into enemy territory despite his legs. Before the army marched he bade the emperor farewell: "I have served a wise master and received more favor than I deserve, yet I am ill and fear I may die overnight and fail that trust. Heaven has granted my prayer to share in this campaign (the clause reads ‘does not oppose yuan,’ parallel to editions that say Heaven did not refuse me). If we conquer Liangzhou, let me hold its march as your frontier warden. If we fall short, I will lay down my life to repay you. " The emperor wept at his words.> When Jiang Wei reached Didao, Li Jian brought officials and townsfolk out to greet the Shu army. In the clash with Wei’s Xu Zhi, Zhang Ni died on the field yet killed more than twice his own losses. After his death his eldest son Zhang Ying was made Marquis of Xixiang; the younger son Zhang Huxiong inherited the marquisate. Across the southern frontier—in Yuexi and neighboring valleys—settlers and tribes alike mourned Zhang Ni, raised shrines to him, and prayed to him whenever drought or flood threatened. < The compiler writes: Zhang Ni cut an undistinguished figure, yet his schemes were sound, his blows decisive, his loyalty absolute, his manner bluntly honest, and every deed weighed against precedent—the emperor esteemed him above many flashier men. The heroes of antiquity scarcely surpassed him! The Shu genealogical tables add that his grandson Zhang Yi governed Liangzhou under Western Jin.>

Historian’s appraisal
25
評曰：黃權弘雅思量，李恢公亮志業，呂凱守節不回，馬忠擾而能毅， 〈《尚書》曰：擾而毅。 鄭玄注曰：擾，馴也。 致果曰毅。〉 王平忠勇而嚴整，張嶷識斷明果，咸以所長，顯名發跡，遇其時也。
The historian’s verdict: Huang Quan combined breadth with judgment; Li Hui pursued an honorable career; Lü Kai held firm without wavering; Ma Zhong was supple yet resolute, < as the Shangshu puts it: gentle in manner, firm in deed. Zheng Xuan glosses ‘compliant’ as tractable. ‘Resolute’ means carrying an attack through to victory.> Wang Ping married loyalty and courage to iron discipline; Zhang Ni paired sharp insight with bold execution. Each rose to fame through his strengths when fate gave him the opening.
