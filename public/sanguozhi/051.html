Sun Jing, styled You'ai, was Sun Jian's youngest brother. When Sun Jian first rebelled, Sun Jing raised five or six hundred local clansmen to shield him—and everyone rallied. Sun Ce defeated Liu Yao and brought the counties to heel. Marching on Kuaiji, Sun Ce summoned Sun Jing; Sun Jing brought his family and joined him at Qiantang. Wang Lang barred Guling; Sun Ce assailed the crossing again and again without success. Sun Jing counseled, "Wang Lang is entrenched; a frontal storm will not break him quickly. Zha Ford lies dozens of li to the south— Pronunciation note: the place-name graph Zha reads zu-jia fan. —and the path is the back door. Seize it and you hit him unawares—exactly "attack where there is no readiness." I will spearhead the assault myself—we will crack him open. Sun Ce agreed. He spread a false order: the river water was foul from rain and sickened the men—every unit must fetch hundreds of jars to strain it clear. At dusk decoy fires fooled Wang Lang; Sun Ce split his force and stole up Zha Ford by night to storm Gaoqian camp. Pei Songzhi notes that Gaoqian Bridge still stands in Yongxing county. Wang Lang panicked. He sent the former Danyang grand administrator Zhou Xin and others to meet the attack. Sun Ce routed them, struck off Zhou Xin's head, and secured Kuaiji. The Kuaiji Records gives Zhou Xin the style Daming. He studied in Luoyang under Chen Fan, read widely, and mastered wind-angle lore and portents. Called to the Grand Commandant's staff, advanced by recommendation, he rose to grand administrator of Danyang. When Cao Cao raised his army, Zhou Xin sent him well over ten thousand men across several levies. Zhou Xin loathed Yuan Shu's cruelty in Huainan and cut ties. The Annals of Emperor Xian says Yuan Shu sent Wu Jing against Zhou Xin; failing, Wu Jing threatened death for anyone siding with Zhou. Zhou Xin replied, "If I am unworthy, what fault lies with the people? He disbanded his army and went home. Sun Ce recommended Sun Jing as Colonel Who Rouses Might for greater responsibility; Sun Jing preferred to stay among tombs and kin, declined wider office, and asked to hold the homeland. Sun Ce agreed. Under Sun Quan he rose to General of the Household for Manifesting Righteousness and died at home. His sons were Hao, Yu, Jiao, Huan, and Qian. Sun Hao fathered Chuo, Chao, and Gong. Sun Chao became a lieutenant general. Sun Gong was father to Sun Jun. Sun Chuo was father to Sun Lin.

瑜字仲異，以恭義校尉始領兵眾。 是時賓客諸將多江西人，瑜虛心綏撫，得其歡心。 建安九年，領丹楊太守，為眾所附，至萬餘人。 加綏遠將軍。 十一年，與周瑜共討麻、保二屯，破之。 後從權拒曹公於濡須，權欲交戰，瑜說權持重，權不從，軍果無功。 遷奮威將軍，領郡如故，自溧陽徙屯牛渚。 瑜以永安人饒助為襄安長，無錫人顏連為居巢長，使招納廬江二郡，各得降附。 濟陰人馬普篤學好古，瑜厚禮之，使二府將吏子弟數百人就受業，遂立學官，臨饗講肄。 是時諸將皆以軍務為事，而瑜好樂墳典，雖在戎旅，誦聲不絕。 年三十九，建安二十年卒。 瑜五子：彌、熙、耀、曼、紘。 曼至將軍，封侯。
Sun Yu, styled Zhongyi, first took command as Colonel Who Honors Righteousness. Most of his officers came from Jiangxi; Sun Yu treated them with open patience and won their loyalty. In Jian'an 204 he became grand administrator of Danyang and drew more than ten thousand followers. He was promoted to General Who Pacifies the Distance. In Jian'an 206 he joined Zhou Yu against the Ma and Bao camps and broke them. Later at Ruxu against Cao Cao, Sun Quan wanted to fight; Sun Yu urged caution—Sun Quan ignored him and won nothing. Promoted to General Who Displays Might, he kept Danyang but moved his base from Liyang to Niuzhu. He named Rao Zhu of Yong'an over Xiang'an and Yan Lian of Wuxi over Juchao to draw Lujiang's two commanderies—both yielded. The scholar Ma Pu of Jiyin won Sun Yu's patronage; hundreds of officers' sons studied under him until Sun Yu founded a school and lectured in person. While other generals lived for war, Sun Yu buried himself in the Classics—even on campaign he chanted texts without cease. He died at thirty-nine in Jian'an 215. His sons were Mi, Xi, Yao, Man, and Hong. Sun Man rose to general and received a marquisate.
4
孫皎字叔朗，始拜護軍校尉，領眾二千餘人。 是時曹公數出濡須，皎每赴拒，號為精銳。 遷都護征虜將軍，代程普督夏口。 黃蓋及兄瑜卒，又並其軍。 賜沙羨、雲杜、南新市、竟陵為奉邑。 自置長吏。 輕財能施，善於交結，與諸葛瑾至厚。 委廬江劉靖以得失，江夏李允以眾事，廣陵吳碩、河南張梁以軍旅，而傾心親待，莫不自盡。 皎嘗遣兵候獲魏邊將吏美女以進皎，皎更其衣服送還之，下令曰："今所誅者曹氏，其百姓何罪?自今以往，不得擊其老弱。 "由是江淮間多歸附者。
Sun Jiao, styled Shulang, began as Colonel Who Guards the Army with over two thousand men. Whenever Cao Cao probed Ruxu, Sun Jiao countered—his corps was reckoned elite. He rose to Chief Controller and General Who Subdues Captives, succeeding Cheng Pu at Xiakou. After Huang Gai and Sun Yu died, he absorbed their commands. Sun Quan granted him Shaxian, Yundu, Nanxinshi, and Jingling as fiefs. He chose his own magistrates for those lands. Open-handed and sociable, he was closest to Zhuge Jin. He delegated strategy to Liu Jing of Lujiang, administration to Li Yun of Jiangxia, and operations to Wu Shuo and Zhang Liang—each repaid his trust fully. When scouts sent him captive Wei women, Sun Jiao reclothed them and returned them, decreeing that only Cao Cao's men were targets—not civilians, young or old. Hence many north of the river rallied to him.

自吾與北方為敵，中間十年，初時相持年小，今者且三十矣。 孔子言‘三十而立’，非但謂五經也。 授卿以精兵，委卿以大任，都護諸將於千里之外，欲使如楚任昭奚恤，揚威於北境。 非徒相使逞私志而已。 近聞卿與甘興霸飲，因酒發作，侵陵其人，其人求屬呂蒙督中。 此人雖粗豪，有不如人意時，然其較略大丈夫也。 吾親之者，非私之也。 我親愛之，卿疏憎之。 卿所為每與吾違，其可久乎？ 夫居敬而行簡，可以臨民； 愛人多容，可以得眾。 二者尚不能知，安可董督在遠，禦寇濟難乎？ 卿行長大，特受重任，上有遠方瞻望之視，下有部曲朝夕從事，何可恣意有盛怒邪？ 人誰無過，貴其能改，宜追前愆，深自咎責。 今故煩諸葛子瑜重宣吾意。 臨書摧愴，心悲淚下。
We have faced the north ten years; we were boys when this began—now some of us near thirty. Confucius said one stands firm at thirty—meaning maturity of character, not mere book learning. I gave you elite troops and far-reaching command—like Chu's Zhao Xixu—to awe the northern border. Not so you could indulge private tempers. I hear wine unleashed you on Gan Ning—who then asked to serve under Lu Meng. Gan Ning is crude yet he is a man in full. My warmth toward him is not favoritism. I favor him while you spurn him. You defy me at every turn—how long can this last? Revere duty and keep actions plain—that rules people. Love broadly and forgive—that wins the host. If you miss both lessons, how will you command on a distant front? You are grown, heavily tasked—every eye watches—your men attend you daily—how dare you rage at will? Everyone errs—the virtue is to mend—own your fault fully. I send Zhuge Jin again to repeat my meaning. My hand shakes as I write—I weep over this.

Sun Jiao apologized by memorial and sealed firm friendship with Gan Ning. When Lu Meng struck Nan Commandery, Sun Quan meant to pair him with Sun Jiao as dual commanders. Lu Meng argued: "If you trust Sun Jiao—use him. If you trust me—use me alone. Zhou Yu and Cheng Pu once split command at Jiangling—Yu decided matters yet Cheng Pu, the veteran, quarreled—and nearly wrecked the campaign. Remember that lesson. Sun Quan saw the point, thanked Lu Meng, and named him sole commander with Sun Jiao in reserve. Sun Jiao bore his share in taking Guan Yu and securing Jing. He died in Jian'an 219. Sun Quan ennobled his son Sun Yin marquis of Danyang for his father's deeds. Sun Yin died without heir. His brother Sun Xi inherited the title. Sun Xi led troops but disgraced himself and killed himself—the marquisate was struck. Younger brothers Sun Zi and Sun Mi. Sun Zi and Sun Mi both rose to general and received marquisates. Sun Zi led the Feathered Forest guard; Sun Mi commanded the Unmatched Difficulty corps. Teng Yin killed Sun Zi; Sun Jun murdered Sun Mi.

Sun Huan, styled Jiming. After Sun Jiao's death Sun Huan took his army as General of the Household Who Displays Might and grand administrator of Jiangxia. For a year he kept Sun Jiao's pattern—honoring Liu Jing, Li Yun, Wu Shuo, Zhang Liang, and Lü Ju—and heeded their advice. Slow to rush yet sharp in office, he won soldiers and commoners alike. In Huangwu 226 Sun Quan struck Shiyang; Sun Huan, the local commander, sent General Xianyu Dan with five thousand men to sever the Huai route first. He led Wu Shuo and Zhang Liang's five thousand as vanguard, took Gaocheng, and captured three enemy generals. When the army pulled back Sun Quan rode past Sun Huan's ranks—saw perfect order—and sighed, "I feared him slow—yet few command so well—I need not worry now. Sun Quan named him General Who Displays Might and marquis of Shaxian. Wu Shuo and Zhang Liang each became lieutenant general with marquis-within-the-passes rank. The Jiangbiao zhuan says Sun Quan at Wuchang hesitated to return the court to Jianye—a two-thousand-li upstream haul might isolate him if crisis struck. At Xiakou he convened his officers in the naval yard and ordered every rank to speak freely for the realm. Some proposed palisades at Xiakou or iron boom chains—Sun Quan rejected both. Minor officer Zhang Liang stepped forward: lure fighters with clear rewards, thrust into the Han River to seize the initiative, and the enemy will keep clear. Station ten thousand veterans at Wuchang under a proven strategist—always combat-ready. Then reinforcements can answer any alarm at once. Build Ganshui fortress, ready thousands of light craft—every contingency covered. Show strength openly and the foe will not dare approach. Sun Quan adopted Zhang Liang's plan and jumped him in rank. He rose by merit to command the Middle Han defenses.

Sun Huan too cherished scholars—sent his officers' sons to school—and dozens later served at court. He died at forty in Jiahe 234. His son Sun Cheng inherited his command as General of the Household Who Displays Might and kept Jiangxia. Sun Cheng died childless in Chiwu 243; Sun Yi, a younger half-brother, inherited to continue Sun Huan's line. When Sun Jun slew Zhuge Ke, Sun Yi joined Quan Xi and Shi Ji against Zhuge Rong at Gongan—Rong killed himself. Sun Yi rose from Defends the South general to Stabilizes the Army with ceremonial axe and Xiakou command. When Sun Lin killed Teng Yin and Lu Ju—both men had married Sun Yi's sisters—his brother Feng, privy to their plot, killed himself. Sun Lin sent Zhu Yi to ambush Sun Yi. Sun Yi learned at Wuchang that Sun Lin meant to strike—he fled north with over a thousand dependents and Teng Yin's wife. Wei made him chariot-and-cavalry general with Three Excellencies honors, marquis of Wu, and married him to Lady Xing—once honored consort of Emperor Cao Fang—along with her household. Lady Xing's jealousy drove her servants past endurance—they murdered Sun Yi and her. Sun Yi defected to Wei. The incident fell in the Wei Huangchu period. He died in the third year.

Sun Fen, styled Boyang— his father Sun Qiang, styled Sheng (variant reading Yi) —was Sun Jian's full brother. Orphaned young with infant brother Sun Fu, Sun Fen raised him—bonded in deepest affection. He served as recording officer and acting county chief. When Sun Jian rose at Changsha, Sun Fen resigned to join him. When Sun Jian died Sun Fen led the remnant army and bore the coffin. Later Sun Fen followed Yuan Shu to Shouchun. Yuan Shao had named Zhou Ang of Kuaiji over Jiujiang; at odds with Yuan Shu, Yuan Shu sent Sun Fen to crush Zhou Ang at Yinling. Yuan Shu memorialized Sun Fen as inspector of Yu Province, then Danyang commandant and acting General Who Subdues Captives against the Yue hills. Harried by Liu Yao, he withdrew to Liyang. Soon Yuan Shu paired him with Wu Jing against Fan Neng and Zhang Ying—without success. Sun Ce crossed the river and with Sun Fen and Wu Jing broke Fan Neng and Zhang Ying, then turned on Liu Yao. Liu Yao fled toward Yuzhang. Sun Ce sent them back to Shouchun—into Yuan Shu's sham court. Yuan Shu named Sun Fen grand administrator of Jiujiang. Sun Fen refused the post and slipped home to the south shore without his family. The Jiangbiao zhuan adds that Yuan Shu stationed Wu Jing at Guangling and employed cousin Sun Xiang as Runan administrator while Sun Fen led troops at Shouchun. Sun Ce wrote Wu Jing: "I march east—will you stand with me? Wu Jing came over at once; Sun Fen escaped Yuan Shu's grip; Sun Xiang stayed stranded far away. The Wu Book styles Sun Xiang Wenyang. His father Sun Ru, styled Zhongru—Sun Jian's second cousin—served as chief clerk and merit assessor. Sun Xiang campaigned with Sun Jian and became a Gentleman of the Palace. He later ran errands for Yuan Shu as General Who Defends the South and died at Shouchun. Once Wu and Kuaiji were his, Sun Ce took Sun Fen against Liu Xun and Huang Zu; returning after Liu Yao's death, he secured Yuzhang and named Sun Fen acting grand administrator The Jiangbiao zhuan quotes Sun Ce: with Tong Zhi holding Luling illegally, Sun Fu garrisoned Nanchang—"You throttle Tong Zhi's throat from Yuzhang. 及翊遇害，河馳赴宛陵，責怒覽、員，以不能全權，令使奸變得施。 二人議曰："伯海與將軍疏遠，而責我乃耳。 討虜若來，吾屬無遺矣。 "遂殺河，使人北迎揚州刺史劉馥，令住歷陽，以丹陽應之。 會翊帳下徐元、孫高、傅嬰等殺覽、員。 〈吴历曰：妫览、戴员亲近边洪等，数为翊所困，常欲叛逆，因吴主出征，遂其奸计。 时诸县令长并会见翊，翊以妻徐氏颇晓卜，翊入语徐：“吾明日欲为长吏作主人，卿试卜之。 ”徐言：“卦不能佳，可须异日。 ”翊以长吏来久，宜速遣，乃大请宾客。 翊出入常持刀，尔时有酒色，空手送客，洪从后斫翊，郡中扰乱，无救翊者，遂为洪所杀，迸走入山。 徐氏购募追捕，中宿乃得，览、员归罪杀洪。 诸将皆知览、员所为，而力不能讨。 览入居军府中，悉取翊嫔妾及左右侍御，欲复取徐。 恐逆之见害，乃绐之曰：“乞须晦日设祭除服。 ”时月垂竟，览听须祭毕。 徐潜使所亲信语翊亲近旧将孙高、傅婴等，说：“览已虏略婢妾，今又欲见偪，所以外许之者，欲安其意以免祸耳。 欲立微计，原二君哀救。 ”高、婴涕泣答言：“受府君恩遇，所以不即死难者，以死无益，欲思惟事计，事计未立，未敢启夫人耳。 今日之事，实夙夜所怀也。 ”乃密呼翊时侍养者二十餘人，以徐意语之，共盟誓，合谋。 到晦日，设祭，徐氏哭泣尽哀毕，乃除服，薰香沐浴，更於他室，安施帏帐，言笑欢悦，示无戚容。 大小凄怆，怪其如此。 览密觇视，无复疑意。 徐呼高、婴与诸婢罗住户内，使人报览，说已除凶即吉，惟府君敕命。 览盛意入，徐出户拜。 览適得一拜，徐便大呼：“二君可起！ ”高、婴俱出，共得杀览，餘人即就外杀员。 夫人乃还缞绖，奉览、员首以祭翊墓。 举军震骇，以为神异。 吴主续至，悉族诛览、员餘党，擢高、婴为牙门，其餘皆加赐金帛，殊其门户。〉
After Sun Yi's murder Yu He raced to Wanling and rebuked Gui Lan and Dai Yuan for failing Sun Quan. They muttered: Yu He is no kin to us—yet blames us harshly." "When Sun Quan comes we are dead men." They slew Yu He, summoned Liu Fu toward Liyang, and meant to yield Danyang. Sun Yi's guards Xu Yuan, Sun Gao, and Fu Ying slew Gui Lan and Dai Yuan. The Wu Calendar adds Gui Lan and Dai Yuan had plotted with Bian Hong while Sun Quan was away." As magistrates gathered, Sun Yi asked Lady Xu to cast yarrow sticks for his banquet because she knew divination." "The omens are bad—pick another day." Sun Yi refused delay—his guests had waited—and threw a great banquet anyway. Usually armed, Sun Yi saw guests off drunk and unarmed; Bian Hong struck from behind. No one saved him—the assassin fled to the hills. Lady Xu offered bounties and caught Bian Hong by midnight; Gui Lan and Dai Yuan blamed the killing on him. Every officer knew Gui Lan's guilt yet dared not move. Gui Lan seized the commandery, took Sun Yi's women, and meant to take Lady Xu. She feigned compliance: she must wait until the month's last day to end mourning." The month nearly done, Gui Lan agreed to wait until after the rites. Secretly she told Sun Gao and Fu Ying that her outward consent only bought time." She begged them for a hidden rescue." They wept: they had delayed only to plot—death alone would have helped nothing." "This is what we have burned for night and day." They secretly swore twenty of Sun Yi's old guards to the plot. On the final day she mourned, shed mourning clothes, bathed and perfumed in another room, then appeared smiling—no trace of grief. Kin thought her conduct chilling and strange. Gui Lan spied on her and dropped his guard. She stationed Sun Gao, Fu Ying, and maids inside and summoned Gui Lan: mourning was done—she awaited him." Gui Lan strode in as Lady Xu bowed him forward. At his bow she screamed, "Now, my lords!" Sun Gao and Fu Ying struck down Gui Lan; others slew Dai Yuan outside. Lady Xu put mourning back on and offered their heads at Sun Yi's tomb. The army stood awestruck as if heaven had acted. Sun Quan extirpated the conspirators, raised Sun Gao and Fu Ying to yamen rank, and rewarded every ally."

韶年十七，收河餘眾，繕治京城。 起樓櫓，修器備議禦敵。 權聞亂，從椒丘還，過定丹楊，引軍歸吳。 夜至京城下營，試攻驚之，兵皆乘城傳檄備警，歡聲動地，頗射外人，權使曉喻乃止。 明日見韶，甚器之。 即拜承烈校尉，統河部曲，食曲阿、丹徒二縣，自置長吏，一如河舊。 後為廣陵太守、偏將軍。 權為吳王，遷揚威將軍，封建德侯。 權稱尊號，為鎮北將軍。 韶為邊將數十年，善養士卒，得其死力。 常以警疆場遠斥候為務，先知動靜而為之備，故鮮有負敗。 青、徐、汝、沛頗來歸附，淮南濱江屯候皆徹兵遠徙，徐、泗、江、淮之地，不居者各數百里。 自權西征，還都武昌，韶不進見者十餘年。 權還建業，乃得朝覲。 權問青、徐諸屯要害，遠近人馬眾寡，魏將帥姓名，盡具識之，有問鹹對。 身長八尺，儀貌都雅。 權歡悅曰："吾久不見公禮，不圖進益乃爾。 "加領幽州牧、假節。 赤烏四年卒。 子越嗣，至右將軍，越兄楷武衛大將軍、臨成侯，代越為京下督。 楷弟異至領軍將軍，奕宗正卿，恢武陵太守。 天璽元年，征楷為宮下鎮驃騎將軍。 初永安賊施但等劫皓弟謙，襲建業，或白楷二端不即赴討者，皓數遣詰楷。 楷常惶怖，而卒被召，遂將妻子親兵數百人歸晉，晉以為車騎將軍，封丹楊侯。 〈晋诸公赞曰：吴平，降为渡辽将军，永安元年卒。 吴录曰：楷处事严整不如孙秀，而人间知名，过也。〉
At seventeen Sun Shao rallied Yu He's troops and rebuilt Jingcheng. He raised towers, stockpiled arms, and planned defense. Sun Quan raced back from Jiaoqiu through Danyang to Wu. He probed Jingcheng by night; defenders manned the walls, cheered, and shot outward until Sun Quan identified himself. Next day he valued Sun Shao highly. He named Sun Shao Colonel Who Inherits Vigor over Yu He's band with Qu'e and Dantu revenue and his father's privilege to pick magistrates. Later he governed Guangling as lieutenant general. When Sun Quan became king of Wu, Sun Shao rose to General Who Displays Might and Jiande marquis. At Sun Quan's accession he became General Who Guards the North. For decades on the frontier he fed his men well and won their last full measure. He lived by distant scouting—always ready—so he rarely lost. Northerners flocked to him; Wei stripped the Huai line until empty belts stretched hundreds of li. While Sun Quan ruled from Wuchang, Sun Shao went unseen at court for ten years. When the court returned to Jianye he finally attended. Sun Quan quizzed him on every Wei post—strengths, distances, commanders—and he answered from memory. Eight feet tall, he cut a graceful figure. Sun Quan exclaimed he had not seen how Sun Shao had grown." Sun Quan added Youzhou shepherd with ceremonial axe. He died in Chiwu 251. Son Sun Yue inherited—rose to General of the Right; elder brother Sun Kai was Grand Guardian and Lincheng marquis, succeeding as Jingxia commander. Among Sun Kai's younger kin, one rose to general of the central army, another became Director of the Imperial Clan, and Sun Hui governed Wuling. In Tianxi 276 Sun Kai was recalled as Agile Cavalry general under the palace. When Shi Dan seized Sun Qian and struck Jianye, rumor said Sun Kai hesitated—Sun Hao repeatedly questioned him. Terrified by Sun Hao's summons, Sun Kai fled to Jin with family and guards—Jin made him chariot cavalry general and marquis of Danyang. After Wu fell he became General Who Crosses Liao and died in Yongan 304. The Wu lu judges Sun Kai sterner than Sun Xiu yet better known."

Sun Huan, styled Shuwu, was Yu He's son. The Wu Book lists four sons. Eldest Zhu—magistrate of Qu'e. Next Yi—magistrate of Haiyan. Both died young. Sun Huan looked the scholar Sun Quan dubbed the clan's Yan Hui and made Guard Martial commandant. At Huarong he bagged five thousand of Guan Yu's stragglers with rich loot." At twenty-five he joined Lu Xun against Liu Bei as General of the Household Who Pacifies the East. Liu Bei blanketed the hills; Sun Huan threw in his whole strength. With Lu Xun he broke Liu Bei's host. Sun Huan cut off— (the place-name graph dou) (variant graph kui) —the Shangdou road and blocked the passes. Liu Bei barely escaped the narrows, groaning that the boy who held Jingcheng now doomed him; Sun Huan earned General Who Establishes Might, marquis of Dantu, Niugang command, and died building the cross-river fort." Younger brother Sun Jun (styled Shuying)—General of the Household Who Establishes Might—died guarding Boluo in Chiwu 250. Eldest Sun Jian inherited the title as General Who Pacifies Captives. Younger Sun Shen became General Who Guards the South. Sun Shen's son Sun Cheng, styled Xianshi. The Wenshi zhuan praises his Firefly Rhapsody. He served as palace attendant beside Gu Rong. Most inner courtiers fell to Sun Hao's wrath—only Gu Rong and Sun Cheng survived. Sun Hao decreed future attendants must match Sun Cheng and Gu Rong." After Wu fell he governed Zhuo county in Fanyang to wide praise. In Yongan Lu Ji took him as major on Sima Ying's staff—both died in the coup."
