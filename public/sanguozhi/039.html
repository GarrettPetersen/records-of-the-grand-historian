董和字幼宰，南郡枝江人也，其先本巴郡江州人。 漢末，和率宗族西遷，益州牧劉璋以為牛鞞、 〈音髀。〉 江原長、成都令。 蜀土富實，時俗奢侈，貨殖之家，侯服玉食，婚姻葬送，傾家竭產。 和躬率以儉，惡衣蔬食，防遏逾僭，為之軌制，所在皆移風變善，畏而不犯。 然縣界豪強憚和嚴法，說璋轉和為巴東屬國都尉。 吏民老弱相攜乞留和者數千人，璋聽留二年，還遷益州太守，其清約如前。 與蠻夷從事，務推誠心，南土愛而信之。
Dong He, styled Youzai, came from Zhijiang in Nan Commandery; his family had originally hailed from Jiangzhou in Ba Commandery. When the Han dynasty neared its end, Dong He moved west with his kinsmen, and Liu Zhang, governor of Yi Province, named him magistrate of Niupi and 〈The place name is read like the word for “thigh.”〉 magistrate of Jiangyuan and prefect of Chengdu. Shu was prosperous, yet people lived lavishly: merchant families dressed like nobles and dined on delicacies, and weddings and funerals could ruin a household overnight. Dong He set the tone himself with plain clothes and simple fare, curbing extravagance and overstepping by fixed rules, so that wherever he served manners improved and people stayed within bounds out of respect. Local magnates, chafing under his strict enforcement, talked Liu Zhang into moving him to commandant of the dependent state attached to Badong. Thousands of officials and townsfolk, young and old, pleaded together that he stay; Liu Zhang let him remain two years more, then restored him as governor of Yi Province, and he was as frugal and upright as ever. In his work with the southern tribes he relied on good faith, and the region came to love and trust him.
2
先主定蜀，徵和為掌軍中郎將，與軍師將軍諸葛亮並署左將軍大司馬府事，獻可替否，共為歡交。 自和居官食祿，外牧殊域，內幹機衡，二十馀年，死之日家無儋石之財。 亮後為丞相，教與群下曰：「夫參署者，集眾思廣忠益也。 若遠小嫌，難相違覆，曠闕損矣。 違覆而得中，猶棄弊蹻而獲珠玉。 然人心苦不能盡，惟徐元直處茲不惑，又董幼宰參署七年，事有不至，至於十反，來相啟告。 苟能慕元直之十一，幼宰之殷勤，有忠於國，則亮可少過矣。 」又曰：「昔初交州平，屢聞得失，後交元直，勤見啟誨，前參事於幼宰，每言則盡，後從事於偉度，數有諫止； 雖姿性鄙暗，不能悉納，然與此四子終始好合，亦足以明其不疑於直言也。 」其追思和如此。 〈偉度者，姓胡，名濟，義陽人。 為亮主簿，有忠藎之效，故見褒述。 亮卒，為中典軍，統諸軍，封成陽亭侯，遷中監軍前將軍，督漢中，假節領兗州刺史，至右驃騎將軍。 濟弟博，歷長水校尉尚書。〉
After Liu Bei took Shu, Dong He was called up as a general of the household for army affairs; he and Military Adviser General Zhuge Liang jointly ran the Left General–Grand Marshal headquarters, candidly offering counsel and enjoying genuine rapport. For over twenty years he served abroad on the frontier and at home at the center of government, yet when he died his family still owned scarcely a sack of grain. When Zhuge Liang later became chancellor, he taught his subordinates that shared office was meant to collect many views and broaden loyal, beneficial advice. If people nurse petty slights and shy away from challenging one another’s views, things fall through the cracks and the realm suffers. Frank exchange that lands on the right course is like trading tattered sandals for pearls and jade. Few can bring themselves to go that far—only Xu Shu stayed clearheaded here; Dong Youzai served seven years in joint administration and, whenever something was overlooked, came back as many as ten times to set the record straight. If others could show even a fraction of Xu Shu’s steadiness and Dong Youzai’s persistence in serving the state, I would make far fewer mistakes of my own. He added that when he first knew Zhouping he constantly learned what worked and what failed; Xu Shu then instructed him tirelessly; whenever he worked with Dong Youzai the man held nothing back; and Hu Weidu later checked him whenever he went astray; Dull and stubborn as I was, I could not embrace every proposal, yet I kept faith with all four to the end—enough to show I never shrank from blunt counsel.” Such was the regard in which Zhuge Liang held Dong He. 〈Hu Ji, styled Weidu, was from Yiyang. He served as Zhuge Liang’s chief clerk with conspicuous loyalty, which is why the text singles him out for praise. After Zhuge Liang’s death he rose through central army posts, took command of the forces, was made village marquis of Chengyang, advanced to front general and army supervisor for Hanzhong, carried imperial credentials with titular authority over Yan Province, and ended as senior general of the right wing. His younger brother Hu Bo became colonel of the Chang River regiment and a minister of state.〉
3
劉巴字子初，零陵烝陽人也。 少知名， 〈零陵先賢傳曰：巴祖父曜，蒼梧太守。 父祥，江夏太守、蕩寇將軍。 時孫堅舉兵討董卓，以南陽太守張諮不給軍糧，殺之。 祥與同心，南陽士民由此怨祥，舉兵攻之，與戰，敗亡。 劉表亦素不善祥，拘巴，欲殺之，數遣祥故所親信人密詐謂巴曰：「劉牧欲相危害，可相隨逃之。 」如此再三，巴輒不應。 具以報表，表乃不殺巴。 年十八，郡署戶曹史主記主簿。 劉先 （主） 欲遣週不疑就巴學，巴答曰：「昔遊荊北，時涉師門，記問之學，不足紀名，內無楊硃守靜之術，外無墨翟務時之風，猶天之南箕，虛而不用。 賜書乃欲令賢甥摧鸞鳳之艷，遊燕雀之宇，將何以啟明之哉？ 愧於‘有若無，實若虛’，何以堪之！」〉 荊州牧劉表連闢，及舉茂才，皆不就。 表卒，曹公徵荊州。 先主奔江南，荊、楚群士從之如雲，而巴北詣曹公。 曹公闢為掾，使招納長沙、零陵、桂陽。 〈零陵先賢傳曰：曹公敗於烏林，還北時，欲遣桓階，階辭不如巴。 巴謂曹公曰：「劉備據荊州，不可也。 」公曰：「備如相圖，孤以六軍繼之也。」〉 會先主略有三郡，巴不得反使，遂遠適交阯， 〈零陵先賢傳云：巴往零陵，事不成，欲遊交州，道還京師。 時諸葛亮在臨烝，巴與亮書曰：「乘危歷險，到值思義之民，自與之眾，承天之心，順物之性，非餘身謀所能勸動。 若道窮數盡，將託命於滄海，不復顧荊州矣。 」亮追謂曰：「劉公雄才蓋世，據有荊土，莫不歸德，天人去就，已可知矣。 足下欲何之？ 」巴曰：「受命而來，不成當還，此其宜也。 足下何言邪！」〉 先主深以為恨。
Liu Ba, styled Zichu, came from Zhengyang in Lingling commandery. Even as a youth he was widely known. 〈According to the Traditions of Worthies of Lingling, his grandfather Liu Yao had been governor of Cangwu. His father Liu Xiang held Jiangxia and the title General Who Sweeps Away Brigands. When Sun Jian marched against Dong Zhuo, he executed Nanyang governor Zhang Zi for withholding supplies. Liu Xiang had sided with Sun Jian, so the people of Nanyang turned on him, took up arms, and drove him out in defeat. Liu Biao had long distrusted Liu Xiang and held Liu Ba hostage, intending to execute him; he repeatedly sent men Liu Xiang had trusted to lure the boy with false warnings that “the governor means you harm—come away with us.” They tried several times; Liu Ba refused each overture. Liu Ba reported the whole ruse to Liu Biao, who then spared him. At eighteen the commandery put him on staff as household registrar, master of records, and chief clerk. Liu Xian 〈manuscript note: “host”〉 Liu Xian wished to send the prodigy Zhou Buyi to study under him. Liu Ba declined: “My schooling in the north of Jing was mere cramming for exams—I have neither Yang Zhu’s inward discipline nor Mozi’s practical drive; I would be the hollow Southern Dipper, bright in the sky but useless on earth.” You would have your brilliant nephew clip phoenix wings to flutter among sparrows—how could that enlighten him?” I fall short of the Master’s ‘to seem empty though full’—how could I face such a pupil!”〉 Liu Biao summoned him repeatedly and nominated him as a “flourishing talent,” but Liu Ba never accepted. When Liu Biao died, Cao Cao marched on Jing Province. Liu Bei fled south while talent from Jing and Chu flocked to him—yet Liu Ba went north to join Cao Cao. Cao Cao appointed him an aide and sent him to win over Changsha, Lingling, and Guiyang. 〈After Cao Cao’s defeat at Wulin he planned to send Huan Jie north; Huan Jie said Liu Ba was the better man. Liu Ba warned Cao Cao, “You cannot leave Liu Bei holding Jing Province.” Cao Cao replied, “If he turns on me, I will bring the host down on him.”〉 Liu Bei then seized three commanderies, blocking Liu Ba’s return to Cao Cao’s camp, so he fled far south toward Jiaozhi, 〈Another note says he tried Lingling first, then meant to cross into Jiao Province and make his way back to the capital. From Linzheng, Zhuge Liang received a letter: “I have crossed danger to reach people moved by duty; their gathering follows heaven’s intent and human nature—no private scheme of mine could sway them.” If every road closes, I will trust my life to the open sea and never trouble Jing Province again.” Zhuge Liang wrote back: “Liu Bei’s gifts tower above his age; he holds the Jing heartland and commands loyalty—Heaven’s choice is plain.” Where else could you possibly go?” Liu Ba answered, “I came under orders; if I fail I should report back—that is only proper.” What are you talking about?”〉 Liu Bei nursed a deep grudge over it.
4
巴復從交阯至蜀。 〈零陵先賢傳曰：巴入交阯，更姓為張。 與交阯太守士谿計議不合，乃由牂牁道去。 為益州郡所拘留，太守欲殺之。 主簿曰：「此非常人，不可殺也。 」主簿請自送至州，見益州牧劉璋，璋父焉昔為巴父祥所舉孝廉，見巴驚喜，每大事輒以諮訪。 臣松之案：劉焉在漢靈帝時已經宗正太常，出為益州牧，祥始以孫堅作長沙時為江夏太守，不得舉焉為孝廉，明也。〉 俄而先主定益州，巴辭謝罪負，先主不責。 〈零陵先賢傳曰：璋遣法正迎劉備，巴諫曰：「備，雄人也，入必為害，不可內也。 」既入，巴复諫曰：「若使備討張魯，是放虎於山林也。 」璋不聽。 巴閉門稱疾。 備攻成都，令軍中曰：「其有害巴者，誅及三族。 」及得巴，甚喜。〉 而諸葛孔明數稱薦之，先主闢為左將軍西曹掾。 〈零陵先賢傳曰：張飛嘗就巴宿，巴不與語，飛遂忿恚。 諸葛亮謂巴曰：「張飛雖實武人，敬慕足下。 主公今方收合文武，以定大事； 足下雖天素高亮，宜少降意也。 」巴曰：「大丈夫處世，當交四海英雄，如何與兵子共語乎？ 」備聞之，怒曰：「孤欲定天下，而子初專亂之。 其欲還北，假道於此，豈欲成孤事邪？ 」備又曰：「子初才智絕人，如孤，可任用之，非孤者難獨任也。 」亮亦曰：「運籌策於帷幄之中，吾不如子初遠矣！ 若提枹鼓，會軍門，使百姓喜勇，當與人議之耳。 」初攻劉璋，備與士眾約：「若事定，府庫百物，孤無預焉。 」及拔成都，士眾皆舍干戈，赴諸藏競取寶物。 軍用不足，備甚憂之。 巴曰：「易耳，但當鑄直百錢，平諸物賈，令吏為官巿。 」備從之，數月之間，府庫充實。〉
Liu Ba eventually traveled from Jiaozhi into Shu. 〈To hide in the south he entered Jiaozhi under the surname Zhang. He quarreled with Jiaozhi governor Shi Xi and slipped away along the Zangke route. Yi Province commandery seized him and the local governor meant to execute him. The chief clerk protested, “This man is no ordinary captive.” He persuaded the governor to send him up to Liu Zhang: Liu Yan had once received his “filial and incorrupt” nomination from Liu Ba’s father, so Zhang welcomed him eagerly and sought his counsel on weighty affairs. Pei Songzhi notes that Liu Yan was already grand master of ceremonies under Emperor Ling before he became governor of Yi, while Liu Xiang did not become governor of Jiangxia until Sun Jian’s Changsha days—so Xiang could not have nominated Liu Yan as filial and incorrupt; the anecdote is impossible.〉 When Liu Bei took Yi Province, Liu Ba offered a humble apology for his past disloyalty; Liu Bei did not punish him. 〈Earlier, when Liu Zhang sent Fa Zheng to invite Liu Bei, Liu Ba warned, “Liu Bei is a conqueror; admit him and he will devour you.” After Liu Bei crossed the border he urged again, “Sending him against Zhang Lu is like releasing a tiger into the hills.” Liu Zhang ignored him. Liu Ba shut his gates and feigned illness. When Liu Bei besieged Chengdu he ordered that anyone who harmed Liu Ba would die with his whole clan. When Liu Ba finally came in, Liu Bei was overjoyed.〉 Zhuge Liang kept recommending him, and Liu Bei appointed him to the Left General’s western bureau. 〈Zhang Fei once tried to spend the night under Liu Ba’s roof; Liu Ba refused to speak to him, and Zhang Fei boiled over. Zhuge Liang urged Liu Ba, “Zhang Fei is a soldier, but he admires you.” Our lord is pulling scholars and generals together for the great enterprise; however proud your nature, bend a little toward him.” Liu Ba replied, “A true man befriends heroes of the realm—why should he banter with some rough camp follower?” Liu Bei heard and thundered, “I mean to settle the realm, and Zichu alone stirs chaos.” Does he still mean to bolt north and only pass through my land—does he think that helps me?” Then he added, “Zichu’s genius stands apart—only someone with my stature can use him; lesser men could never manage him alone.” Zhuge Liang added, “For counsel within the tent I am nowhere near Zichu!” If the task is to drum at the gate and stir the people to valor, that is for someone else to handle.” Before the campaign against Liu Zhang, Liu Bei had promised his army, “When Chengdu falls, the treasury is yours—I claim nothing.” When the city fell, the men threw down their weapons and looted every warehouse. The army soon ran short of funds, and Liu Bei despaired. Liu Ba said, “That is simple: mint hundred-cash coins, stabilize prices, and let officials run public markets.” Liu Bei agreed; within months the coffers were full again.〉
5
建安二十四年，先主為漢中王，巴為尚書，後代法正為尚書令。 躬履清儉，不治產業，又自以歸附非素，懼見猜嫌，恭默守靜，退無私交，非公事不言。 〈零陵先賢傳曰：是時中夏人情未一，聞備在蜀，四方延頸。 而備銳意欲即真，巴以為如此示天下不廣，且欲緩之。 與主簿雍茂諫備，備以他事殺茂，由是遠人不復至矣。〉 先主稱尊號，昭告於皇天上帝后土神祇，凡諸文誥策命，皆巴所作也。 章武二年卒。 卒後，魏尚書僕射陳群與丞相諸葛亮書，問巴消息，稱曰劉君子初，甚敬重焉。 〈零陵先賢傳曰：輔吳將軍張昭嘗對孫權論巴褊□厄，不當拒張飛太甚。 權曰：「若令子初隨世沈浮，容悅玄德，交非其人，何足稱為高士乎？」〉
In 219 Liu Bei became king of Hanzhong; Liu Ba served as minister of state and later succeeded Fa Zheng as director of the secretariat. He lived plainly and amassed no estate; because he had joined Liu Bei late he feared suspicion, kept his silence, avoided private friendships, and spoke only on official business. 〈The heartland was still divided; when word spread that Liu Bei held Shu, hopeful men everywhere watched him. Liu Bei was impatient to take the throne; Liu Ba argued that haste would look small-minded to the empire and urged delay. He and chief clerk Yong Mao spoke against it; Liu Bei later executed Yong on another charge, and talent from afar stopped coming.〉 When Liu Bei took the imperial title and proclaimed it to Heaven and Earth, Liu Ba drafted every proclamation and patent of appointment. He died in the second year of Zhangwu (222). After his death, Wei’s Chen Qun wrote Zhuge Liang asking news of “Lord Liu Zichu,” with evident esteem. 〈Zhang Zhao once told Sun Quan that Liu Ba’s rigid temper had brought him grief and that he had slighted Zhang Fei too harshly. Sun Quan replied, “If Liu Ba had flattered Liu Bei and truckled to every faction, could we still call him a gentleman?”〉
6
馬良字季常，襄陽宜城人也。 兄弟五人，並有才名，鄉里為之諺曰：「馬氏五常，白眉最良。 」良眉中有白毛，故以稱之。 先主領荊州，闢為從事。 及先主入蜀，諸葛亮亦從後往，良留荊州，與亮書曰：「聞雒城已拔，此天祚也。 尊兄應期贊世，配業光國，魄兆見矣。 〈臣松之以為良蓋與亮結為兄弟，或相與有親； 亮年長，良故呼亮為尊兄耳。〉 夫變用雅慮，審貴垂明，於以簡才，宜適其時。 若乃和光悅遠，邁德天壤，使時閒於聽，世服於道，齊高妙之音，正鄭、衛之聲，並利於事，無相奪倫，此乃管弦之至，牙、曠之調也。 雖非鍾期，敢不擊節！ 」先主闢良為左將軍掾。
Ma Liang, styled Jichang, was a native of Yicheng in Xiangyang. Five brothers, all celebrated, inspired a local rhyme: “Among the Ma band of five, the white-browed one thrives.” Ma Liang had white hairs in his brows, hence the nickname. When Liu Bei governed Jing Province he appointed Ma Liang as an attendant clerk. When Liu Bei marched into Shu, Zhuge Liang followed later while Ma Liang stayed behind in Jing; he wrote to Zhuge Liang, “Luocheng has fallen—that is Heaven’s blessing.” You have risen with the times to aid the age; your service magnifies the state—and every auspicious sign is already plain.” 〈Pei Songzhi believed that Ma Liang had likely sworn brotherhood with Zhuge Liang, or that the two families were connected by marriage; Zhuge Liang was the elder, so Ma Liang addressed him as “honoured elder brother.”〉 To shift policy with careful judgment, weigh what truly matters, and bring it to light is how one chooses men for the hour. When ruler and minister move in concert, virtue spans heaven and earth, the age hears clearly and the world trusts the Way; sublime music harmonizes with the reform of vulgar song, every part serves the whole without breaking rank—that is the perfection Boya and Shi Kuang pursued. I am no Zhong Ziqi, yet how could I withhold my applause!” The Former Lord then appointed Ma Liang as an aide to the Left General.
7
後遣使吳，良謂亮曰：「今銜國命，協穆二家，幸為良介於孫將軍。 」亮曰：「君試自為文。 」良即為草曰：「寡君遣掾馬良通聘繼好，以紹昆吾、豕韋之勳。 其人吉士，荊楚之令，鮮于造次之華，而有克終之美，原降心存納，以慰將命。 」權敬待之。
Later Liu Bei sent Ma Liang as envoy to Wu. Ma Liang told Zhuge Liang, “I carry an imperial mandate to reconcile our two courts; please speak well of me to General Sun.” Zhuge Liang replied, “Draft the memorial yourself.” Ma Liang wrote at once: “My lord dispatches his aide Ma Liang to renew friendship and envoys, echoing the ancient deeds of Kunwu and Shiwei.” He is an honourable man and the pick of Jing and Chu—little given to empty sparkle, yet steadfast to the end; I pray you take him to heart and satisfy the trust of his mission.” Sun Quan received him with due honour.
8
先主稱尊號，以良為侍中。 及東征吳，遣良入武陵招納五溪蠻夷，蠻夷渠帥皆受印號，咸如意指。 會先主敗績於夷陵，良亦遇害。 先主拜良子秉為騎都尉。
When Liu Bei took the imperial title, he named Ma Liang palace attendant. During the eastern campaign against Wu, Liu Bei sent Ma Liang into Wuling to win over the Five Streams tribes; every chieftain accepted a seal and obeyed his instructions. When Liu Bei was routed at Yiling, Ma Liang fell with him. Liu Bei enfeoffed Ma Liang’s son Ma Bing as commandant of cavalry.
9
良弟謖，字幼常，以荊州從事隨先主入蜀，除綿竹成都令、越雋太守。 才器過人，好論軍計，丞相諸葛亮深加器異。 先主臨薨謂亮曰：「馬謖言過其實，不可大用，君其察之！ 」亮猶謂不然，以謖為參軍，每引見談論，自晝達夜。 〈《襄陽記》曰：建興三年，亮征南中，謖送之數十里。 亮曰：「雖共謀之歷年，今可更惠良規。 」謖對曰：「南中恃其險遠，不服久矣，雖今日破之，明日復反耳。 今公方傾國北伐以事強賊。 彼知官勢內虛，其叛亦速。 若殄盡遺類以除後患，既非仁者之情，且又不可倉卒也。 夫用兵之道，攻心為上，攻城為下，心戰為上，兵戰為下，原公服其心而已。 」亮納其策，赦孟獲以服南方。 故終亮之世，南方不敢復反。〉
Ma Liang’s younger brother Ma Su, styled Youchang, had entered Shu as a Jing Province clerk and became prefect of Mianzhu and Chengdu and governor of Yuexi. His abilities outshone most men’s, he delighted in military debate, and Chancellor Zhuge Liang prized him highly. On his deathbed Liu Bei warned Zhuge Liang, “Ma Su’s reputation outruns his talent—do not give him weighty command; mark my words!” Zhuge Liang disagreed: he kept Ma Su on staff and talked with him from dawn till midnight. 〈The Xiangyang Record notes that in 225 Zhuge Liang marched south and Ma Su accompanied him for miles.” Zhuge Liang said, “We have schemed together for years—give me one more round of sound advice.” Ma Su answered, “The south trusts its mountains and distance—it has resisted for ages; crush it today and it will rise again tomorrow.” You are about to stake the realm on a northern drive against a formidable foe. Once they sense the capital is drained, they will revolt at once. Exterminating every clan to forestall trouble is unworthy of a humane ruler and cannot be rushed besides.” In war, winning hearts comes first and taking cities second; battles of will rank above battles of arms—win their hearts and the south will yield.” Zhuge Liang took the counsel, spared Meng Huo, and brought the south to heel. For the rest of Zhuge Liang’s life the southern tribes did not rise again.〉
10
建興六年，亮出軍向祁山，時有宿將魏延、吳壹等，論者皆言以為宜令為先鋒，而亮違眾拔謖，統大眾在前，與魏將張郃戰於街亭，為郃所破，士卒離散。 亮進無所據，退軍還漢中。 謖下獄物故，亮為之流涕。 良死時年三十六，謖年三十九。 〈襄陽記曰：謖臨終與亮書曰：「明公視謖猶子，謖視明公猶父，原深惟殛鯀興禹之義，使平生之交不虧於此，謖雖死無恨於黃壤也。 」於時十萬之眾為之垂涕。 亮自臨祭，待其遺孤若平生。 蔣琬後詣漢中，謂亮曰：「昔楚殺得臣，然後文公喜可知也。 天下未定而戮智計之士，豈不惜乎！ 」亮流涕曰：「孫武所以能製勝於天下者，用法明也。 是以楊乾亂法，魏絳戮其僕。 四海分裂，兵交方始，若復廢法，何用討賊邪！ 」習鑿齒曰：諸葛亮之不能兼上國也，豈不宜哉！ 夫晉人規林父之後濟，故廢法而收功； 楚成闇得臣之益己，故殺之以重敗。 今蜀僻陋一方，才少上國，而殺其俊傑，退收駑下之用，明法勝才，不師三敗之道，將以成業，不亦難乎！ 且先主誡謖之不可大用，豈不謂其非才也？ 亮受誡而不獲奉承，明謖之難廢也。 為天下宰匠，欲大收物之力，而不量才節任，隨器付業； 知之大過，則違明主之誡，裁之失中，即殺有益之人，難乎其可與言智者也。〉
In 228 Zhuge Liang marched on Qishan. Veteran commanders such as Wei Yan and Wu Yi were available, and opinion favoured them for the van; Zhuge Liang overruled everyone and picked Ma Su to lead the main force. Ma Su met Zhang He at Jieting, was shattered, and his army broke. With no foothold left, Zhuge Liang pulled back to Hanzhong. Ma Su died in prison; Zhuge Liang wept for him. Ma Liang had died at thirty-six; Ma Su was thirty-nine when he perished. 〈The Xiangyang Record preserves Ma Su’s last letter: “You have treated me as a son and I have honoured you as a father; recall how Gun was punished yet Yu rose—let our bond survive this shame so that, though I die, I rest easy beneath the yellow springs.” A hundred thousand men wept for him. Zhuge Liang conducted the rites himself and cared for the orphans as though Ma Su still lived. Jiang Wan came to Hanzhong and said, “When Chu executed Dechen, Duke Wen of Jin could rejoice at last.” The empire is still unsettled—how can you spare a strategist?” Zhuge Liang answered through tears, “Sun Wu conquered because his law was inexorable.” When Yang Gan broke discipline, Wei Jiang executed his own servant to uphold it.” The realm is torn and war has barely begun—if we discard the law, how can we defeat Wei?” Xi Zuochi commented: Small wonder Zhuge Liang never mastered the Central Plain!” The Jin court watched Xun Lin Fu redeem himself later, bent the statute book, and still won; King Cheng of Chu misread how Dechen helped him, executed him, and doubled his disaster. Shu is a remote corner with fewer talents than the north; to execute its brightest men and rely on mediocrity—prizing statute over genius while ignoring the lesson of repeated defeat—yet expecting success is folly!” The Former Lord warned that Ma Su must not hold high command—was that not a verdict on his talent? Zhuge Liang heard the warning yet could not bring himself to set Ma Su aside—proof of how hard that was. To steward the realm and summon every gift while refusing to match office to ability or assign tasks by measure; to misread men that badly defies a dying sovereign’s counsel; to judge wrongly is to destroy the useful—how can we call that wisdom?〉

Chen Zhen, styled Xiaoqi, came from Nanyang. While Liu Bei governed Jing Province he employed Chen Zhen as an attendant clerk over several commanderies and brought him into Shu. After the conquest he served as northern commandant of Shu commandery, then—when the jurisdiction was renamed—as governor of Wenshan, and finally transferred to Qianwei. In 225 he entered court as a minister, rose to director of the secretariat, and was sent on embassy to Wu. In 229 Sun Quan declared himself emperor and named Chen Zhen minister of the guards to honour his accession. Zhuge Liang wrote to Zhuge Jin: “Xiaoqi’s loyalty has only deepened with age; his work knitting east and west is something to treasure.” When Chen Zhen crossed into Wu he notified the border officers:

東之與西，驛使往來，冠蓋相望，申盟初好，日新其事。 東尊應保聖祚，告燎受符，剖判土宇，天下響應，各有所歸。 於此時也，以同心討賊，則何寇不滅哉！ 西朝君臣，引領欣賴。 震以不才，得充下使，奉聘敘好，踐界踴躍，入則如歸。 獻子適魯，犯其山諱，春秋譏之。 望必啟告，使行人睦焉。 即日張旍誥眾，各自約誓。 順流漂疾，國典異制，懼或有違，幸必斟誨，示其所宜。
East and west exchange couriers without cease; chariots crowd the roads; the alliance renews itself daily. The eastern sovereign guards the mandate, performs the great rituals, and maps the realm; the world answers and each side knows its place. United now against Cao Wei, what enemy could survive?” The Shu court watches and waits with hope. I am unworthy of this mission, yet I bear goodwill across your border with joy and enter as I would my own hall.” The Spring and Autumn censures Xianzi of Jin for breaking Lu’s taboo on mountain names when he visited. Please instruct your officers so that envoys may pass without offense.” This very day we publish the mandate and bind ourselves anew by oath.” The river runs fast and our statutes differ—I fear some breach; I beg your guidance on what is proper.”
13
震到武昌，孫權與震升壇歃盟，交分天下：以徐、豫、幽、青屬吳，並、涼、冀、兗屬蜀，其司州之土，以函谷關為界。 震還，封城陽亭侯。
In Wuchang, Sun Quan joined Chen Zhen on the altar for a blood oath and partitioned the realm: Xu, Yu, You, and Qing went to Wu; Bing, Liang, Ji, and Yan to Shu; the Central Plain itself was split at Hangu Pass. Chen Zhen returned to Shu enfeoffed as village marquis of Chengyang.

九年，都護李平坐誣罔廢； 諸葛亮與長史蔣琬、侍中董允書曰：「孝起前臨至吳，為吾說正方腹中有鱗甲，鄉黨以為不可近。 吾以為鱗甲者但不當犯之耳，不圖復有蘇、張之事出於不意。 可使孝起知之。 」十三年，震卒。 子濟嗣。
In 231 Director Li Ping was stripped of office for slander; Zhuge Liang wrote to Jiang Wan and Dong Yun: “When Chen Zhen last visited Wu he warned me that Li Yan was ‘scaled’—impossible to get close to, say his neighbours.” I thought that only meant not to provoke him—I never expected another Su Qin or Zhang Yi.” Tell Chen Zhen what has happened.” Chen Zhen died in the thirteenth year of Jianxing (235). His son Chen Ji inherited the title.
15
董允字休昭，掌軍中郎將和之子也。 先主立太子，允以選為捨人，徙洗馬。 後主襲位，遷黃門侍郎。 丞相亮將北征，住漢中，慮後主富於春秋，硃紫難別，以允秉心公亮，欲任以宮省之事。 上疏曰：「侍中郭攸之、費禕、侍郎董允等，先帝簡拔以遺陛下，至於斟酌規益，進盡忠言，則其任也。 愚以為宮中之事，事無大小，悉以諮之，必能裨補闕漏，有所廣益。 若無興德之言，則戮允等以彰其慢。 」亮尋請禕為參軍，允遷為侍中，領虎賁中郎將，統宿衛親兵。 攸之性素和順，備員而已。 〈楚國先賢傳曰：攸之，南陽人，以器業知名於時。〉 獻納之任，允皆專之矣。 允處事為防制，甚盡匡救之理。 後主常欲採擇以充后宮，允以為古者天子后妃之數不過十二，今嬪嬙已具，不宜增益，終執不聽。 後主益嚴憚之。 尚書令蔣琬領益州刺史，上疏以讓費禕及允，又表「允內侍歷年，翼贊王室，宜賜爵土以褒勳勞。 」允固辭不受。 後主漸長大，愛宦人黃皓。 皓便闢佞慧，欲自容入。 允常上則正色匡主，下則數責於皓。 皓畏允，不敢為非。 終允之世，皓位不過黃門丞。
Dong Yun, styled Xiuzhao, was the son of Dong He, general of the household for army affairs. When Liu Bei named the heir apparent, Dong Yun was chosen as an attendant and soon promoted to junior mentor. When Liu Shan succeeded, Dong Yun rose to gentleman of the Yellow Gates. When Zhuge Liang marched north and stayed in Hanzhong, he feared the young emperor could not tell flattery from counsel; trusting Dong Yun’s fairness, he meant to leave palace affairs in his hands. His memorial named palace attendants Guo Youzhi, Fei Yi, and Dong Yun among others—“men Your Late Father singled out to advise you honestly.” Consult them on every palace matter, great or small; they will shore up every gap.” If they fail to speak for virtue, punish them to expose their negligence.” Zhuge Liang soon pulled Fei Yi onto campaign staff; Dong Yun became palace attendant, led the Rapid-as-Tigers guard, and commanded the imperial bodyguard.” Guo Youzhi was mild by nature and little more than a placeholder. 〈The Chu Worthies Traditions records Guo Youzhi of Nanyang as a man of recognised talent.〉 Dong Yun alone handled frank counsel to the throne. He ran court business with tight discipline and laboured to right every wrong. Liu Shan often wanted to draft girls for the harem; Dong Yun cited the classical limit of twelve consorts and noted the quarters were already full—he refused every request. The emperor learned to fear him even more. Director Jiang Wan, who also governed Yi Province, memorialised to praise Fei Yi and Dong Yun, arguing that Dong Yun’s years of palace service merited a fief. Dong Yun steadfastly declined.” As Liu Shan matured he grew fond of the eunuch Huang Hao. Huang Hao flattered cleverly and sought every opening at court. Dong Yun corrected the emperor to his face and scolded Huang Hao behind the screen. Huang Hao feared him too much to make trouble. While Dong Yun lived, Huang Hao never rose above assistant to the Yellow Gates.
16
允嘗與尚書令費禕、中典軍胡濟等共期遊宴，嚴駕已辦，而郎中襄陽董恢詣允脩敬。 恢年少官微，見允停出，逡巡求去，允不許，曰：「本所以出者，欲與同好遊談也，今君已自屈，方展闊積，捨此之談，就彼之宴，非所謂也。 」乃命解驂，禕等罷駕不行。 其守正下士，凡此類也。 〈《襄陽記》曰：董恢字休緒，襄陽人。 入蜀，以宣信中郎副費禕使吳。 孫權嘗大醉問禕曰：「楊儀、魏延，牧豎小人也。 雖嘗有鳴吠之益於時務，然既已任之，勢不得輕，若一朝無諸葛亮，必為禍亂矣。 諸君憒憒，曾不知防慮於此，豈所謂貽厥孫謀乎？ 」禕愕然四顧視，不能即答。 恢目禕曰：「可速言儀、延之不協起於私忿耳，而無黥、韓難御之心也。 今方掃除強賊，混一區夏，功以才成，業由才廣，若捨此不任，防其後患，是猶備有風波而逆廢舟楫，非長計也。 」權大笑樂。 諸葛亮聞之，以為知言。 還未滿三日，闢為丞相府屬，遷巴郡太守。 臣松之案：《漢晉春秋》亦載此語，不云董恢所教，辭亦小異，此二書俱出習氏而不同若此。 本傳云「恢年少官微」，若已為丞相府屬，出作巴郡，則官不微矣。 以此疑習氏之言為不審的也。〉 延熙六年，加輔國將軍。 七年，以侍中守尚書令，為大將軍費禕副貳。 九年，卒。 〈《華陽國志》曰：時蜀人以諸葛亮、蔣琬、費禕及允為四相，一號四英也。〉
Dong Yun once planned an excursion with Fei Yi, Hu Ji, and others; the carriages stood ready when Dong Hui of Xiangyang, a junior gentleman, arrived to pay his respects. Young and low in rank, Dong Hui tried to withdraw when Dong Yun delayed the party. Dong Yun refused: “We came out to swap ideas with friends; you have humbled yourself to join us—this is the conversation worth having; skipping it for a banquet misses the point.” He had the teams unhitched; Fei Yi and the rest cancelled the outing. His integrity and respect for humble talent showed in countless such gestures. 〈The Xiangyang Record adds that Dong Hui, styled Xiuxu, came from Xiangyang.” After entering Shu he served as deputy to Fei Yi on embassy to Wu under the title faithful gentleman of the household. Once, drunk, Sun Quan said to Fei Yi, “Yang Yi and Wei Yan are nothing but rustics.” They may yap usefully for now, yet once raised to office they cannot be cast aside lightly—remove Zhuge Liang one morning and those two will tear Shu apart.” You gentlefolk stumble along blind to this danger—is this what it means to secure your children’s future?” Fei Yi stared about, speechless. Dong Hui caught Fei Yi’s eye: “Say plainly that Yang Yi and Wei Yan feud out of private spite—they are no Ying Bu or Han Xin waiting to betray you.” You are crushing Wei and reuniting the realm; achievement rides on talent—discard capable men for fear of tomorrow and you might as well junk your boats while the seas still rage; that is no strategy.” Sun Quan roared with laughter. Zhuge Liang heard the story and pronounced it shrewd counsel. Within three days of his return he entered the chancellor’s bureau and was named governor of Ba commandery. Pei Songzhi notes that the Han–Jin Annals quotes the same exchange without crediting Dong Hui’s coaching and with slightly different wording—both stem from Xi Zuochi yet disagree. The main text calls Dong Hui young and lowly—yet if he had already served in the chancellor’s bureau before becoming governor of Ba, his rank was hardly humble. On these grounds Pei Songzhi doubts Xi Zuochi’s account.〉 In 243 he received the additional title general who supports the state. In 244, while palace attendant, he acted as director of the secretariat and deputy to Grand General Fei Yi. He died in the ninth year of Yanxi (246). 〈The 《Huayang Guozhi》 records that Shu folk ranked Zhuge Liang, Jiang Wan, Fei Yi, and Dong Yun as the “four chancellors,” or the Four Paragons.〉

陳祗代允為侍中，與黃皓互相表裡，皓始預政事。 祗死後，皓從黃門令為中常侍、奉車都尉，操弄威柄，終至覆國。 蜀人無不追思允。 及鄧艾至蜀，聞皓奸險，收閉，將殺之，而皓厚賂艾左右，得免。
Chen Zhi succeeded Dong Yun as palace attendant and colluded openly with Huang Hao, who now entered politics. After Chen Zhi died, Huang Hao rose from prefect of the Yellow Gates to regular attendant and chariot commandant, seized the levers of power, and helped bring down the kingdom. Everyone in Shu looked back on Dong Yun with longing. When Deng Ai entered Shu he jailed Huang Hao as a traitor and meant to execute him, but Huang Hao bought off Deng Ai’s staff and walked free.

呂乂字季陽，南陽人也。 父常，送故將 （軍） 劉焉入蜀，值王路隔塞，遂不得還。 乂少孤，好讀書鼓琴。 初，先主定益州，置鹽府校尉，較鹽鐵之利，後校尉王連請乂及南陽杜祺、南鄉劉幹等並為典曹都尉。 乂遷新都、綿竹令，乃心隱卹，百姓稱之，為一州諸城之首。 遷巴西太守。 丞相諸葛亮連年出軍，調發諸郡，多不相救，乂募取兵五千人詣亮，慰喻檢制，無逃竄者。 徙為漢中太守，兼領督農，供繼軍糧。 亮卒，累遷廣漢、蜀郡太守。 蜀郡一都之會，戶口眾多，又亮卒之後，士伍亡命，更相重冒，姦巧非一。 乂到官，為之防禁，開喻勸導，數年之中，漏脫自出者萬馀口。 後入為尚書，代董允為尚書令，眾事無留，門無停賓。 乂歷職內外，治身儉約，謙靖少言，為政簡而不煩，號為清能； 然持法刻深，好用文俗吏，故居大官，名聲損於郡縣。 延熙十四年卒。 子辰，景耀中為成都令。 辰弟雅，謁者。 雅清厲有文才，著格論十五篇。
Lü Yi, styled Jiyang, came from Nanyang. His father Lü Chang had gone as escort for a retiring general 〈manuscript gloss: “army”〉 when Liu Yan marched into Shu; strife severed the highways home, and Lü Chang never returned. Lü Yi lost his father early and devoted himself to books and the qin. After Liu Bei took Yi Province he set up a salt monopoly; Colonel Wang Lian later named Lü Yi, Du Qi of Nanyang, Liu Gan of Nanxiang, and others commandants of the canon bureau. As prefect of Xindu and Mianzhu he ruled with compassion and ranked first among the province’s districts. He advanced to governor of Brazil. Zhuge Liang’s yearly campaigns left commanderies unable to cover one another’s levies; Lü Yi raised five thousand men for him, kept order by encouragement and discipline, and lost no draftees to desertion. Transferred to Hanzhong, he supervised farming and kept the army fed. After Zhuge Liang’s death he rose through the governorships of Guanghan and Shu commandery. Shu commandery, thick with households, saw mass desertion and fraudulent census fraud after Zhuge Liang’s death. Lü Yi barred further abuse, explained the law, and within a few years more than ten thousand people came back onto the rolls. He entered court as minister and succeeded Dong Yun as director of the secretariat; paperwork never stalled and petitioners never waited at his gate. In civil and military posts he lived plainly, spoke little, ran government with lean efficiency, and earned a reputation for integrity and competence; yet he enforced the code harshly and favoured petty clerks, so his prestige at the capital never matched his fame as a local governor. He died in the fourteenth year of Yanxi (251). His son Lü Chen became prefect of Chengdu during the Jingyao era. Lü Chen’s brother Lü Ya served as a herald. Lü Ya was austere and literate; he wrote fifteen chapters of the Model Discourses.

杜祺歷郡守監軍大將軍司馬，劉幹官至巴西太守，皆與乂親善，亦有當時之稱，而儉素守法，不及於乂。
Du Qi rose through commandery posts to grand general’s chief of staff; Liu Gan reached Brazil—both were Lü Yi’s friends and noted men of their day, yet neither matched his frugal fidelity to the law.

評曰：董和蹈羔羊之素，劉巴履清尚之節，馬良貞實，稱為令士，陳震忠恪，老而益篤，董允匡主，義形於色，皆蜀臣之良矣。 呂乂臨郡則垂稱，處朝則被損，亦黃、薛之流亞矣。
The historian concludes: Dong He lived the simplicity praised in the Odes; Liu Ba kept an austere standard; Ma Liang was steadfast and rightly called a gentleman; Chen Zhen grew only more loyal with age; Dong Yun rebuked his sovereign with righteousness written on his face—these were Shu’s great servants.
