賀齊字公苗，會稽山陰人也。 〈《虞预晋书》曰：贺氏本姓庆氏。 齐伯父纯，儒学有重名，汉安帝时为侍中、江夏太守，去官，与江夏黄琼、 （汉中） 〔广汉〕杨厚俱公车徵。 避安帝父孝德皇 （帝） 讳，改为贺氏。 齐父辅，永宁长。〉 少為郡吏，守剡長。 縣吏斯從輕俠為奸，齊欲治之。 主簿諫曰：“從，縣大族，山越所附，今日治之，明日寇至。 ”齊聞大怒，便立斬眾。 從族黨遂相糾合，眾千餘人，舉兵攻縣。 齊率吏民，開城門突擊，大破之，威震山越。 後太末、豐浦民反，轉守太末長，誅惡養善，期用盡平。 建安元年，孫策臨郡，察齊孝廉。 時王朗奔東冶，侯官長商升為朗起兵。 策遣永寧長韓晏領南部都尉，將兵討升，以齊為永寧長。 晏為升所敗，齊又代晏領都尉事。 升畏齊威名，遣使乞盟。 齊因告喻，為陳禍福，升遂送上印綬，出捨求降。 賊帥張雅、詹強等不願升降，反共殺升，雅稱無上將軍，強稱會稽太守。 賊盛兵少，未足以討，齊住軍息兵。 雅與女婿何雄爭勢兩乖，齊令越人因事交構。 遂至疑隙，阻兵相圖。 齊乃進討，一戰大破雅，強黨震懼，率眾出降。
He Qi, whose courtesy name was Gongmiao, came from Shanyin in Kuaiji commandery. 〈Yu Yu’s Jin shu notes that the He family was originally surnamed Qing. His uncle He Chun was a noted Confucian who served as Palace Attendant and prefect of Jiangxia under Han Emperor An; after retiring he was summoned with Huang Qiong of Jiangxia (text reads “Hanzhong,” an editorial gloss) and Yang Hou of Guanghan—by imperial carriage. To avoid the taboo name of Emperor An’s father, the posthumously titled Virtuous Emperor, (the gloss marks the taboo referent as “the emperor”) the clan changed its surname to He. He Qi’s father He Fu was magistrate of Yongning.〉 He began as a commandery clerk and acting magistrate of Shan county. The county clerk Si Cong swaggered and broke the law; He Qi meant to bring him to justice. The chief clerk warned him: “Si Cong heads a powerful lineage the hill Yue rally to—move against him today and bandits will be at the walls tomorrow.” He Qi flew into a rage and had Si Cong executed on the spot. Si Cong’s kinsmen mustered over a thousand fighters and stormed the county seat. He Qi led militia out the gates in a sally and shattered them, and his name terrified the hill Yue. When Taimo and Fengpu rose, he was shifted to Taimo as magistrate, executed ringleaders, shielded the innocent, and pacified the district within his term. In Jian’an 1 Sun Ce took Kuaiji and nominated He Qi as filial and incorrupt. Wang Lang had fled to Dongye; Shang Sheng, magistrate of Houguan, mustered troops for him. Sun Ce sent Han Yan of Yongning south as area commandant against Shang Sheng and named He Qi magistrate of Yongning. Han Yan lost to Shang Sheng; He Qi then took his place as southern commandant. Shang Sheng, awed by He Qi’s reputation, sued for peace. He Qi parleyed, sketching survival and ruin, until Shang Sheng handed over his seal, left his camp, and offered submission. Bandit leaders Zhang Ya and Zhan Qiang refused to submit with Shang Sheng, murdered him, and Zhang Ya declared himself “general without superior” while Zhan Qiang called himself prefect of Kuaiji. The rebels outnumbered him, so He Qi held his troops and waited. Zhang Ya feuded with his son-in-law He Xiong; He Qi had Yue agents widen the crack. Suspicion hardened until they pointed spears at each other. He Qi then attacked, routed Zhang Ya in a single fight, and Zhan Qiang’s followers surrendered in panic.
2
侯官既平。 而建安、漢興、南平復亂，齊進兵建安，立都尉府，是歲八年也。 郡發屬縣五千兵，各使本縣長將之，皆受齊節度。 賊洪明、洪進、苑御、吳免、華當等五人，率各萬戶，連屯漢興，吳五六千戶別屯大潭。 鄒臨六千戶別屯蓋竹，大潭同出餘汗。 〈音干。〉 軍討漢興，經餘汗。 齊以為賊眾兵少，深入無繼，恐為所斷，令松陽長丁蕃留備余汗。 蕃本與齊鄰城，恥見部伍，辭不肯留。 齊乃斬蕃，於是軍中震慄。 無不用命。 遂分兵留備，進討明等，連大破之。 臨陳斬明，其免、當、進、御皆降。 轉擊蓋竹，軍向大潭，三將又降。 凡討治斬首六千級，名帥盡擒。 復立縣邑，料出兵萬人，拜為平東校尉。 十年，轉討上饒，分以為建平縣。
Houguan county was quiet again. Then Jian’an, Hanxing, and Nanping exploded; He Qi marched on Jian’an, set up a command post—it was the eighth Jian’an year. The commandery drafted five thousand men from its counties, each led by its own magistrate, all under He Qi’s orders. Rebel chiefs Hong Ming, Hong Jin, Yuan Yu, Wu Mian, and Hua Dang—five bands of some ten thousand households each—linked camps at Hanxing; another five or six thousand households under Wu camped at Datan. Zou Lin’s six thousand households held Gaizhu; Datan’s force also debouched toward Yukan. 〈The commentary notes the place name is read like “gan.”〉 He Qi’s column marched on Hanxing through Yukan. Fearing encirclement—many rebels, few men—he left Ding Fan of Songyang to hold Yukan. Ding Fan, a neighboring magistrate, resented serving under He Qi’s command and refused the posting. He Qi executed him on the spot, and the army shook with dread. After that, every officer obeyed without question. He split off detachments to guard the rear, then stormed Hong Ming’s positions and broke them repeatedly. Hong Ming died on the field; Wu Mian, Hua Dang, Hong Jin, and Yuan Yu capitulated. He swung against Gaizhu, then Datan, and three more chiefs yielded. The campaign took six thousand heads and every noted leader alive or dead. He refounded cantons, levied ten thousand picked soldiers, and was named Colonel Who Pacifies the East. In the tenth year he attacked Shangrao and carved out Jianping county.
3
十三年，遷威武中郎將，討丹陽黟、歙。 時武強、葉鄉、東陽、豐浦四鄉先降，齊表言以葉鄉為始新縣。 而歙賊帥金奇萬戶屯安勒山，毛甘萬戶屯烏聊山，黟帥陳僕，祖山等二萬戶屯林歷山。 林歷山四面壁立，高數十丈，逕路危狹，不容刀楯，賊臨高下石，不可得攻。 軍住經日，將吏患之。 齊身出周行，觀視形便，陰募輕捷士，為作鐵弋，密於隱險賊所不備處，以戈拓斬山為緣道，夜令潛上，乃多縣布以援下人，得上百數人，四面流布，俱鳴鼓角，齊勒兵待之。 賊夜聞鼓聲四合，謂大軍悉已得上，驚懼惑亂，不知所為，守路備險者，皆走還依眾。 大軍因是得上，大破僕等，其餘皆降，凡斬首七千。 〈《抱朴子》曰：昔吴遣贺将军讨山贼，贼中有善禁者，每当交战，官军刀剑不得拔，弓弩射矢皆还自向，辄致不利。 贺将军长情有思，乃曰：“吾闻金有刃者可禁，虫有毒者可禁，其无刃之物，无毒之虫，则不可禁。 彼必是能禁吾兵者也，必不能禁无刃物矣。 ”乃多作劲木白棓，选有力精卒五千人为先登，尽捉棓。 彼山贼恃其有善禁者，了不严备。 於是官军以白棓击之，彼禁者果不复行，所击杀者万计。〉 齊復表分歙為新定； 黎陽、休陽。 並黟、歙凡六縣。 權遂割為新都郡，齊為太守，立府於始新，加偏將軍。
Jian’an 13 brought promotion to General of the Household Gentlemen of Martial Might for the Danyang campaign against Yi and She. Four townships—Wujiang, Yexiang, Dongyang, and Fengpu—had already submitted; He Qi asked to make Yexiang into Shixin county. In She, Jin Qi camped ten thousand households on Mount Anle; Mao Gan did the same on Mount Wuliao; in Yi, Chen Pu and Zu Shan held twenty thousand on Mount Linli. Linli rose like a sheer wall scores of feet high, its trails too narrow for shield or blade, while defenders rained rock from above. The siege stalled and the staff grew desperate. He Qi reconnoitered in person, forged iron crowbars, and under cover of night cut secret stairways up the cliff, hauled picked climbers on cloth ropes, then had them drum and shout from every side while his main force waited below. The defenders thought the whole army had gained the heights and fled toward the main camp in confusion. He Qi’s men swarmed the summit, shattered Chen Pu’s host, and took seven thousand heads; the rest surrendered. 〈The Baopuzi tells how Wu once sent General He against mountain rebels whose shamans “bound” metal so blades would not leave the scabbard and bowstrings drove arrows backward. General He reasoned: “Spells can stop edged metal and venom, not plain wood or toothless vermin.” “They can hex our steel; they cannot hex a club.” He armed five thousand storm troops with heavy white-oak clubs. The rebels, trusting their wizardry, left their guard down. Government troops clubbed them until the magic failed and tens of thousands fell.〉 He Qi petitioned to split She commandery again—first Xinding; then Liyang and Xiuyang; with Yi and She that made six counties in all. Sun Quan formed Xindu commandery from them, made He Qi its prefect with seat at Shixin, and added the title General of the Gentlemen of the Side.
4
十六年，吳郡餘杭民郎稚合宗起賊，復數千人，齊出討之。 即復破稚，表言分餘杭為臨水縣。 被命詣所在，及當還郡，權出祖道，作樂舞象。 〈吴书曰：权谓齐曰：“今定天下，都中国，使殊俗贡珍，狡兽卒舞，非君谁与？ ”齐曰：“殿下以神武应期，廓开王业，臣幸遭际会，得驱驰风尘之下，佐助末行，效鹰犬之用，臣之原也。 若殊俗贡珍，狡兽率舞，宜在圣德，非臣所能。”〉 賜齊軿車駿馬，罷坐住駕，使齊就車。 齊辭不敢，權使左右扶齊上車，令導吏卒兵騎，如在郡儀。 權望之笑曰："人當努力，非積行累勤，此不可得。 "去百餘步乃旋。
In year 16 Lang Zhi of Yuhang rallied clans in revolt; He Qi crushed them. He broke Lang Zhi immediately and asked to carve Yuhang into Linshui county. When orders took him abroad and he was due home, Sun Quan held a farewell on the highway with music and elephant dance. 〈The Wu shu records Sun Quan saying, “When the realm is settled at Luoyang, who but you will bring tribute beasts and foreign gifts?” He Qi answered: “Your Highness’s divine arms opened the royal work; I am lucky to run before your dust like a hunting hound—that is all I wish.” “As for tribute and dancing beasts—that belongs to sagely virtue, not to me.”〉 Sun Quan gave him a light chariot and fine horses and made him ride in place of the heavier state carriage. He Qi protested; Sun Quan had attendants lift him aboard and sent him off with the full prefect’s escort. Sun Quan watched him go and laughed: “Rank like that is bought only with long toil.” He drove a hundred paces before turning back.
5
十八年，豫章東部民彭材、李玉、王海等起為賊亂，眾萬餘人。 齊討平之，誅其首惡，餘皆降服。 揀其精健為兵，次為縣戶。 遷奮武將軍。
In year 18 Peng Cai, Li Yu, and Wang Hai raised ten thousand rebels in eastern Yuzhang. He Qi crushed them, executed the ringleaders, and accepted the rest. The fittest he drafted into the army; the rest registered as county taxpayers. He was promoted to General Who Displays Might.
6
二十年，從權征合肥。 時城中出戰，徐盛被創失矛，齊引兵拒擊，得盛所失。 〈江表传曰：权征合肥还，为张辽所掩袭於津北，几至危殆。 齐时率三千兵在津南迎权。 权既入大船，会诸将饮宴，齐下席涕泣而言曰：“至尊人主，常当持重。 今日之事，几至祸败，群下震怖，若无天地，原以此为终身诫。 ”权自前收其泪曰：“大惭！ 谨以克心，非但书诸绅也。”〉
In year 20 he followed Sun Quan against Hefei. When the Wei garrison sallied, Xu Sheng was wounded and dropped his spear; He Qi counterattacked and recovered it. 〈The Jiang biao zhuan says Zhang Liao ambushed Sun Quan north of the crossing on the return from Hefei and nearly destroyed him. He Qi waited south of the ford with three thousand men. When Sun Quan gave a victory banquet aboard ship, He Qi left his cushion weeping: “A ruler must always weigh risk.” “Today we brushed annihilation; the officers were blind with panic—I beg you take this as a lifelong lesson.” Sun Quan stepped down, wiped his tears, and said, “I am mortified.” “I will burn this into my heart, not merely tie it on my girdle.”〉
7
二十一年，鄱陽民尤突受曹公印綬，化民為賊，陵陽、始安、涇縣皆與突相應。 齊與陸遜討破突，斬首數千，餘黨震服，丹楊三縣皆降，料得精兵八千人。 拜安東將軍，封山陰侯，出鎮江上，督扶州以上至皖。
In year 21 You Tu of Poyang took a seal from Cao Cao and raised the countryside; Linyang, Shi’an, and Jing joined him. He Qi and Lu Xun shattered the revolt, took thousands of heads, pacified Danyang’s three counties, and enrolled eight thousand veterans. He was named General Who Pacifies the East, marquis of Shanyin, and given the upper Yangzi from Fuzhou to Wan.
8
黃武初，魏使曹休來伐。 齊以道遠後至，因住新市為拒。 會洞口諸軍遭風流溺，所亡中分，將士失色，賴齊未濟，偏軍獨全，諸將倚以為勢。
Early in Huangwu, Wei sent Cao Xiu against Wu. He Qi arrived late and anchored the defense at Xinshi. A storm sank half the fleet at Dongkou; only He Qi’s detachment, still on the way, remained intact, and the survivors drew courage from it.
9
齊性奢綺，尤好軍事，兵甲器械極為精好，所乘船雕刻丹鏤，青蓋絳襜，干櫓戈矛，葩爪文畫，弓弩矢箭，咸取上材，蒙衝鬥艦之屬，望之若山。 休等憚之，遂引軍還。 遷後將軍，假節領徐州牧。
He Qi loved splendor: lacquered warships, vermilion carving, jade-green canopies, crimson blinds, gilded arms—his squadron looked like a floating mountain range. Cao Xiu’s officers took one look and withdrew. He rose to General of the Rear with credential-axe and nominal shepherd of Xu province.
10
初，晉宗為戲口將，以眾叛如魏，還為蘄春太守，圖襲安樂，取其保質。 權以為恥忿，因軍初罷，六月盛夏、出其不意，詔齊督麋芳、鮮于丹等襲蘄春，遂生虜宗。 後四年卒，子達及弟景皆有令名，為佳將。 〈会稽典录曰：景为灭贼校尉，御众严而有恩，兵器精饰，为当时冠绝，早卒。 达颇任气，多所犯迕，故虽有征战之劳，而爵位不至，然轻财贵义，胆烈过人。 子质，位至虎牙将军。 景子邵，别有传。〉
Jin Zong had commanded Xikou, defected to Wei, become prefect of Qichun, and plotted to raid Anle for hostages. Sun Quan, furious and shamed, struck in the sixth-month heat while Wei’s guard was slack; He Qi, Mi Fang, and Xianyu Dan took Qichun and seized Jin Zong alive. He died four years later; his sons He Da and He Jing were noted commanders. 〈The Kuaiji dian lu says He Jing was Colonel Who Exterminates Bandits—strict but kind, unmatched in equipping troops—yet died young. He Da was quick-tempered and blunt, so rank never matched his battles, yet he despised wealth and valued honor and was bolder than most. His son He Zhi became General of the Tiger’s Fang. He Jing’s son He Shao has his own biography.〉
11
全琮字子璜，吳郡錢唐人也。 父柔，漢靈帝時舉孝廉。 補尚書郎右丞，董卓之亂，棄官歸。 州辟別駕從事，詔書就拜會稽東部都尉。 孫策到吳，柔舉兵先附，策表柔為丹楊都尉。 孫權為車騎將軍，以柔為長史，徙桂陽太守。 柔嘗使琮齎米數千斛到吳，有所市易。 琮至，皆散用，空船而還。 柔大怒，琮頓首曰：“愚以所市非急，而士大夫方有倒縣之患，故便振贍，不及啟報。 ”柔更以奇之。 〈徐众评曰：礼，子事父无私财，又不敢私施，所以避尊上也。 弃命专财而以邀名，未尽父子之礼。 臣松之以为子路问“闻斯行诸？ ”子曰“有父兄在”。 琮辄散父财，诚非子道，然士类县命，忧在朝夕，权其轻重，以先人急，斯亦冯暖市义、汲黯振救之类，全谓邀名，或负其心。〉 是時中州士人避亂而南，依琮居者以百數，琮傾家給濟，與共有無，遂顯名遠近。 後權以為奮威校尉，授兵數千人，使討山越。 因開募召，得精兵萬餘人，出屯牛渚，稍遷偏將軍。
Quan Zong, whose courtesy name was Zihuang, came from Qiantang in Wu commandery. His father Quan Rou earned nomination as filial and incorrupt under Han Emperor Ling. He served in the secretariat until Dong Zhuo’s coup, then resigned and went home. The province named him aide-de-camp; an imperial edict made him commandant of eastern Kuaiji. When Sun Ce entered Wu, Quan Rou was first to rally to him; Sun Ce had him named commandant of Danyang. When Sun Quan held the chariot-and-cavalry generalship he took Quan Rou as chief clerk, then sent him south as prefect of Guiyang. Quan Rou once dispatched Quan Zong to Wu with several thousand hu of rice to sell. Quan Zong gave every bushel away and sailed home with empty holds. Quan Rou raged; Quan Zong kowtowed and said, “The goods were not urgent, but gentlemen were starving—I fed them first and failed to ask leave.” Quan Rou, reconsidering, marveled at his son. 〈Xu Zhong cites ritual: a son keeps no private purse from his father. To spend a father’s grain for fame strains filial duty. Pei Songzhi notes Zilu’s question: “Hear a thing—act at once?” Confucius answered, “While father and elder brothers live.” Quan Zong’s charity was rash by Confucian reckoning, yet it matched Feng Xuan “buying righteousness” or Ji An’s relief—calling it mere fame-seeking may miss his intent.〉 Scores of refugee scholars lodged with Quan Zong; he emptied his granaries to keep them and won fame across the southeast. Sun Quan later named him Colonel Who Rouses Might and sent him against the hill Yue with a few thousand men. He recruited ten thousand veterans, camped at Niuzhu, and rose to side general.
12
建安二十四年，劉備將關羽圍樊、襄陽，琮上疏陳羽可討之計，權時已與呂蒙陰議襲之，恐事洩，故寢琮表不答。 及禽羽，權置酒公安。 顧謂琮曰：“君前陳此，孤雖不相答，今日之捷，抑亦君之功也。 ”於是封陽華亭侯。
In Jian’an 24 Quan Zong urged a strike on Guan Yu at Fan; Sun Quan, already plotting with Lü Meng, shelved the memorial to avoid leaks. After Guan Yu fell, Sun Quan feasted the army at Gong’an. Sun Quan told him, “You warned me first; I kept silent, but this victory is yours too.” He ennobled Quan Zong as marquis of Yanghua village.
13

When three commanderies’ hill folk rose, he went as army overseer and crushed them. Chiefs Huang Luan and Chang offered their bands for the register. They ennobled him marquis of Qinting and named him colonel of the swift-cavalry guard.

永安六年，蜀併於魏，武陵五谿夷與蜀接界。 時論懼叛亂，乃以牧為平魏將軍，領武陵太守，往之郡。 魏遣漢葭縣長郭純試守武陵太守，率涪陵民入蜀遷陵界，屯於赤沙，誘致諸夷邑君，或起應純，又進攻酉陽縣，郡中震懼。 牧問朝吏曰：「西蜀傾覆，邊境見侵，何以禦之？ 」皆對曰：「今二縣山險，諸夷阻兵，不可以軍驚擾，驚擾則諸夷盤結。 宜以漸安，可遣恩信吏宣教慰勞。 」牧曰：「不然。 外境內侵，誑誘人民，當及其根柢未深而撲取之，此救火貴速之勢也。 」敕外趣嚴，掾史沮儀者便行軍法。 撫夷將軍高尚說牧曰：「昔潘太常督兵五萬，然後以討五谿夷耳。 是時劉氏連和，諸夷率化，今既無往日之援，而郭純已據遷陵，而明府以三千兵深入，尚未見其利也。 」牧曰：「非常之事，何得循舊？ 」即率所領，晨夜進道，緣山險行，垂二千里，從塞上，斬惡民懷異心者魁帥百餘人及其支黨凡於余級，純等散，五谿平。 遷公安督、揚武將軍，封都鄉侯，徙濡須督。 〈会稽典录曰：牧之在濡须，深以进取可图，而不敢陈其策，与侍中东观令硃育宴，慨然叹息。 育谓牧恨於策爵未副，因谓牧曰：「朝廷诸君，以际会坐取高官，亭侯功无与比，不肯在人下，见顾者犹以於邑，况於侯也！ 」牧笑而答曰：「卿之所言，未获我心也。 马援有言，人当功多而赏薄。 吾功不足录，而见宠已过当，岂以为恨？ 国家不深相知，而见害朝人，是以默默不敢有所陈。 若其不然，当建进取之计，以报所受之恩，不徒自守而已，愤叹以此也。 」育复曰：「国家已自知侯，以侯之才，无为不成。 愚谓自可陈所怀。 」牧曰：「武安君谓秦王云：『非成业难，得贤难； 非得贤难，用之难； 非用之难，任之难。 』武安君欲为秦王并兼六国，恐授事而不见任，故先陈此言。 秦王既许而不能，卒陨将成之业，赐剑杜邮。 今国家知吾，不如秦王之知武安，而害吾者有过范睢。 大皇帝时，陆丞相讨鄱阳，以二千人授吾，潘太常讨武陵，吾又有三千人，而朝廷下议，弃吾於彼，使江渚诸督，不复发兵相继。 蒙国威灵自济，今日何为常。 向使吾不料时度宜，苟有所陈，至见委以事，不足兵势，终有败绩之患，何无不成之有？」〉 復以前將軍假節，領武陵太守。
Yongan 6: Shu fell to Wei, and Wuling’s five-streams tribes touched the old Shu frontier. Court rumor foretold revolt, so Wu made Zhongli Mu General Who Pacifies Wei and sent him to Wuling. Wei’s Guo Chun crossed from Fuling, camped at Chisha, suborned tribal headmen, struck Youyang, and terrified the prefecture. Zhongli Mu asked his staff how to answer Wei’s probe. They urged patience: “The hills will explode if you march.” “Send envoys and soothe them bit by bit.” Mu said, “No.” “Hit the infection before it spreads—firefighting rewards speed.” He ordered war footing and threatened mutinous staff with martial discipline. Gao Shang reminded him: “Pan Jun needed fifty thousand for these hills.” “Today there is no Shu buffer, Guo Chun sits on Qianling—three thousand men buys nothing.” Mu answered, “Emergencies break precedent.” He forced the passes, took a thousand heads, broke Guo Chun, and quieted the five streams. He moved up to Ruxu command as General Who Rouses Might and marquis of Duxiang. 〈The Kuaiji dian lu says he burned to strike north but stayed silent at Ruxu, only sighing over wine with Zhu Yu. Zhu Yu thought he brooded on rank and flattered him. Mu smiled, “You miss my point.” Ma Yuan said the worthy deserve thin pay for heavy work. “My record is slight; the throne has already overpaid me—what grudge is there?” “Court gossip pins me down, so I hold my tongue.” “If I were trusted I would attack, not just garrison—that is what grieves me.” Zhu Yu pressed him to speak. “Say what you think.” Mu quoted Lord Bai Qi: “Finishing the work is easy—finding talent is hard.” “Finding him is easy—using him is hard.” “Using him is easy—trusting him is hard.” Bai Qi spoke first because he feared empty mandate. The king broke his word and killed Bai Qi at Duyou. “Wu trusts me less than Qin trusted Bai Qi, and my enviers surpass Fan Ju.” “Sun Quan gave me five thousand once, then court debate stranded me while river commands refused reinforcements.” “I saved myself by luck—why expect better now?” “Had I misjudged and begged for troops I lacked, I would have failed—silence was wisdom.”〉 He returned as General of the Front with credential-axe over Wuling.

卒官。 家無餘財，士民思之。 子褘嗣，代領兵。 〈会稽典录曰：牧次子盛，亦履恭让，为尚书郎。 弟徇领兵为将，拜偏将军，戍西陵，与监军使者唐盛论地形势，谓宜城、信陵为建平援，若不先城，敌将先入。 盛以施绩、留平，智略名将，屡经於彼，無云當城之者，不然徇计。 后半年，晋果遣将脩信陵城。 晋军平吴，徇领水军督，临陈战死。〉
He died in harness. He left no hoard, yet people mourned him. His son Zhongli Hui succeeded him in the army. 〈Second son Sheng was a courteous gentleman of the secretariat. Brother Zhongli Xun, a side general on the Xiling line, told Tang Sheng that Yicheng and Xinling must be walled for Jianping’s sake. Tang Sheng cited Shi Ji and Liu Ping—veterans who had never demanded those walls—and brushed the plan aside. Six months on Jin fortified Xinling as Xun had warned. When Jin conquered Wu, Zhongli Xun led the river fleet and died in the fighting.〉

47
評曰：山越好為叛亂，難安易動，是以孫權不遑外御，卑詞魏氏。 凡此諸臣，皆克寧內難，綏靜邦域者也。 呂岱清恪在公； 周魴譎略多奇； 鐘離牧蹈長者之規； 全琮有當世之才，貴重於時，然不檢姦子，獲譏毀名云。
The verdict: hill Yue were Wu’s chronic ulcer, which is why Sun Quan bowed low to Wei while sorting the interior. These men stamped out civil strife and steadied the state. Lü Dai served with icy integrity; Zhou Fang’s ruses were brilliant; Zhongli Mu lived the old-school gentleman’s code; Quan Zong was the age’s ablest yet lost his reputation by indulging corrupt sons.
