1
高祖睿文聖武昭肅孝皇帝，姓劉氏，諱暠，本名知遠，及即位改今諱。 其先本沙陀部人也。 四代祖諱湍，帝有天下，追尊為明元皇帝，廟號文祖，陵曰懿陵； 〈 （《五代會要》：湍為東漢顯宗第八子淮陽王丙之後。）〉 高祖母隴西李氏，追謚明貞皇后。 曾祖諱昂，晉贈太保，追尊為恭僖皇帝，朝號德祖，陵曰沛陵； 〈 （《五代會要》：懿陵、沛陵皆無陵所，遙申朝拜。）〉 曾祖母虢國太夫人楊氏，追謚恭惠皇后。 祖諱僎，晉贈太傅，追尊為昭獻皇帝，廟號翼祖，陵曰威陵； 祖母魯國太夫人李氏，追謚為昭穆皇后。 皇考諱琠，事後唐武皇帝為列校，晉贈太師，追尊為章聖皇帝，廟號顯祖，陵曰肅陵； 皇妣吳國太夫人安氏，追謚章懿皇后。 後以唐乾寧二年，歲在乙卯，二月四日生帝於太原。
Emperor Gaozu, styled Sagacious in Culture, Sacred in Martial prowess, Illustrious in Solemnity, and Filial in Piety, was of the Liu clan. His taboo name was Gao; he had been born Zhiyuan and took his present taboo name when he ascended the throne. His forebears were originally of the Shatuo tribe. His fourth-generation ancestor, whose taboo name was Tuan, was posthumously honored as Emperor Mingyuan when Gaozu won the empire; his temple name was Wenzu and his mausoleum Yiling; [ (Institutions of the Five Dynasties: Tuan descended from Bing, Prince of Huaiyang, eighth son of Emperor Xianzong of the Eastern Han.)] Gaozu's grandmother, Lady Li of Longxi, was posthumously honored as Empress Mingzhen. His great-grandfather, taboo name Ang, received a posthumous grant of Grand Guardian from Jin and was honored as Emperor Gongxi, with temple name Dezu and mausoleum Peiling; [ (Institutions of the Five Dynasties: neither Yiling nor Peiling had a known burial site; court worship was offered from afar.)] His great-grandmother, Grand Lady Yang of Guo, was posthumously honored as Empress Gonghui. His grandfather, taboo name Zhuan, received a posthumous grant of Grand Tutor from Jin and was honored as Emperor Zhaoxian, with temple name Yizu and mausoleum Weiling; His grandmother, Grand Lady Li of Lu, was posthumously honored as Empress Zhaomu. His father, taboo name Chuan, had served Emperor Wu of Later Tang as a company commander; Jin posthumously made him Grand Preceptor and honored him as Emperor Zhangsheng, with temple name Xianzu and mausoleum Suling; His mother, Grand Lady An of Wu, was posthumously honored as Empress Zhangyi. He was born at Taiyuan on the fourth day of the second month in the second year of Qianning of Tang (899), the cyclical year yimao.
2
帝弱不好弄，嚴重寡言，及長，面紫色，目睛多白。 初事唐明宗，列於麾下。 明宗與梁人對柵於德勝，時晉高祖為梁人所襲，馬甲連革斷，帝輟騎以授之，取斷革者自跨之，徐殿其後，晉高祖感而壯之。 明宗踐阼，晉高祖為北京留守，以帝前有護援之力，奏移麾下，署為牙門都校。 應順初，晉高祖鎮常山，唐明宗召赴闕，會閔帝出奔，與晉高祖相遇於途，遂俱入衛州，泊於郵舍。 閔帝左右謀害晉高祖，帝密遣禦士石敢袖鎚立於晉高祖後，及有變，敢擁晉高祖入一室，以巨木塞門，敢尋死焉。 帝率眾盡殺閔帝左右，遂免晉高祖於難。 〈 （《通鑒考異》引《漢高祖實錄》云：是夜，偵知少帝伏甲，欲與從臣謀害晉高祖，詐屏人對語，方坐庭廡。 帝密遣禦士石敢袖鎚立於後，俄頃伏甲者起，敢有勇力，擁晉高祖入一室，以巨木塞門，敢力當其鋒，死之。 帝解佩刀，遇夜晦，以在地葦炬未然者奮擊之，眾謂短兵也，遂散走。 帝乃匿身長垣下，聞帝親將李洪信謂人曰：「石太尉死矣。 」帝隔垣呼洪信曰：「太尉無恙。 」乃逾垣出就洪信兵，共護晉高祖，殺建謀者，以少主授王宏贄。）〉
As a boy he was frail and indifferent to play, grave and sparing of words. In manhood his complexion was purplish and the whites of his eyes unusually prominent. He first entered service under Emperor Mingzong of Tang and was enrolled in his personal command. At Desheng, where Mingzong faced the Liang behind palisades, the future Jin Emperor Gaozu came under Liang attack and the leather ties of his horse armor snapped in succession. Gaozu gave up his own mount for him, took a horse whose ties had already broken, and brought up the rear at an easy pace. The Jin emperor was deeply moved and impressed by his steadfastness. After Mingzong took the throne, the Jin emperor served as military governor of the Northern Capital. Remembering Gaozu's earlier rescue, he had him transferred to his own command and appointed him commander of the gate guards. Early in Yingshun, while the Jin emperor held Changshan, Mingzong summoned him to court. Emperor Min was then in flight, and the two met on the road, entered Weizhou together, and lodged at a postal station. Men close to Emperor Min plotted to kill the Jin emperor. Gaozu secretly posted his guardsman Shi Gan, hammer concealed in his sleeve, behind him. When the attack came, Gan hustled the Jin emperor into a room and barred the door with a heavy timber beam, and died in the fighting. Gaozu then led his men and killed every one of Emperor Min's attendants, thus saving the Jin emperor from the plot. [ (Comprehensive Mirror, Examination of Discrepancies, citing the Veritable Record of Han Gaozu: That night he learned the young emperor had hidden armed men and meant, with his followers, to murder the Jin emperor; they pretended to dismiss attendants for a private talk and were seated in the courtyard portico. Gaozu secretly posted Shi Gan behind him, hammer in his sleeve. Moments later the ambush rose. Gan was powerful; he pushed the Jin emperor into a room, barred the door with a heavy beam, met the rush head-on, and was killed. Gaozu drew his belt dagger. In the dark he snatched up an unlit reed torch from the ground and struck out wildly. The assailants took it for a drawn blade and fled. He hid beneath a long wall and heard the emperor's intimate general Li Hongxin tell someone, "Commander Shi is dead. " Gaozu called over the wall, "The commander is alive and well. " He climbed over the wall, joined Hongxin's troops, protected the Jin emperor, killed the plotters, and delivered the young sovereign to Wang Hongzan.)]
3
天福二年夏四月，加檢校太保。 八月，改許州節度使，典軍如故。 三年夏四月，加檢校太傅。 冬十月，授侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使。 十一月，移授宋州，加檢校太尉。 十二月，加同平章事。 時帝與杜重威同制加恩，帝憤然不樂，懇讓不受，杜門不出者數日。 〈 （《通鑒》：知遠自以有佐命功，重威起於外戚，無大功，恥與之同制。 制下數日，杜門四表辭不受。）〉 晉高祖怒，召宰相趙瑩等議落帝兵權，任歸私第。 瑩等以為不可，乃遣端明殿學士和凝就第宣諭，帝乃承命。 五年三月，改鄴都留守兼侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使。 九月，奉詔赴闕，晉高祖幸其第。 六年七月，授北京留守、河東節度使。 七年正月，加侍中。 時天下大蝗，惟不入河東界。 六月，晉高祖崩於鄴宮，少帝即位，加帝檢校太師。 八年三月，進位中書令。
In the fourth month of summer, Tianfu 2, he received the additional title of acting Grand Guardian. In the eighth month he became military commissioner of Xuzhou while retaining command of his troops. In the fourth month of summer, year 3, he was made acting Grand Tutor. In the tenth winter month he was appointed director-in-chief of the palace horse and foot guards. In the eleventh month he was reassigned to Songzhou with the additional title of acting Grand Marshal. In the twelfth month he was made concurrent grand councilor. He and Du Chongwei had been honored by the same edict. Gaozu was openly displeased, repeatedly declined the honors, and stayed behind closed doors for days. [ (Comprehensive Mirror: Zhiyuan believed he had earned founding merit, whereas Chongwei had risen only as an imperial in-law without comparable service, and he was ashamed to be honored in the same edict. For days after the edict he kept his doors shut and memorialized from every quarter that he would not accept.)] The Jin emperor was furious and summoned Chief Minister Zhao Ying and others to discuss stripping Gaozu of command and confining him to private life. Ying and his colleagues argued against it, so the court sent Academician He Ning of the Hall of Illuminated Clarity to his house with the imperial message, and Gaozu then accepted. In the third month of year 5 he became garrison commander of the Ye capital while retaining direction of the palace horse and foot guards. In the ninth month he answered a summons to court, and the Jin emperor paid a visit to his residence. In the seventh month of year 6 he was made garrison commander of the Northern Capital and military commissioner of Hedong. In the first month of year 7 he was made palace attendant. Locusts plagued the empire, yet they never crossed into Hedong. In the sixth month the Jin emperor died at Ye; the young emperor succeeded, and Gaozu was made acting grand preceptor. In the third month of year 8 he was promoted to director of the Secretariat.
4
開運元年正月，契丹南下，契丹主以大軍直抵澶州，遣蕃將偉王率兵入雁門。 朝廷以帝為幽州道行營招討使，帝大破偉王於忻口。 尋奉詔起兵至土門，軍至樂平，會契丹退，乃還。 三月，封太原王。 七月，兼北面行營都統。 二年四月，封北平王。 三年五月，加守太尉。 是月，帝誅吐渾白承福等五族凡四百人，以別部王義宗統其餘眾。 九月，契丹犯塞，帝親率牙兵至朔州南陽武谷，大破之。 〈 （《東都事略·郭進傳》：契丹屠安陽，高祖遣進拒戰，契丹敗走，以功除刺史。）〉 十一月，契丹主率蕃漢大軍由易、定抵鎮州，杜重威等駐軍於中渡橋以禦之。 十二月十日，杜重威等以全軍降於契丹。 十七日，相州節度使張彥澤受契丹命，陷京城，遷少帝於開封府。 帝聞之大駭，分兵守境，以備寇患。
In the first month of Kaiyun 1 the Khitan marched south. Their ruler drove a great army straight to Chunzhou and sent the tribal general Prince Wei through Yanmen Pass. The court named him pacification commissioner for the Youzhou campaign, and he routed Prince Wei at Xinkou. He soon marched under edict as far as Tumen Pass and reached Leping, but turned back when the Khitan withdrew. In the third month he was enfeoffed as Prince of Taiyuan. In the seventh month he was also made supreme commander of the northern front. In the fourth month of year 2 he was enfeoffed as Prince of Beiping. In the fifth month of year 3 he was made guardian grand marshal. That month he executed four hundred people of five Tujue clans led by Bai Chengfu and placed the survivors under Wang Yizong of another division. In the ninth month, when the Khitan raided the frontier, he led his guard in person to Nanyangwu Valley south of Shuozhou and crushed them. [ (Brief Account of the Eastern Capital, Biography of Guo Jin: after the Khitan sacked Anyang, Gaozu sent Jin to meet them; the Khitan were routed, and he was made prefect for his service.)] In the eleventh month the Khitan ruler led a mixed tribal and Chinese army from Yi and Ding to Zhenzhou, while Du Chongwei and others held the Zhongdu Bridge against him. On the tenth day of the twelfth month Du Chongwei surrendered his entire army to the Khitan. On the seventeenth Zhang Yanze of Xiangzhou, acting on Khitan orders, took the capital and removed the young emperor to Kaifeng. Gaozu was deeply alarmed and posted troops along his borders to guard against attack.
5
天福十二年春正月丁亥朔，契丹主入東京。 癸巳，晉少帝蒙塵於封禪寺。 癸卯，少帝北遷。 二月丁巳朔，契丹主具漢法服，禦崇元殿受朝，制改晉國為大遼國，大赦天下，號會同十年。 是月，帝遣牙將王峻奉表於契丹，契丹主賜詔褒美，呼帝為兒，又賜木拐一。 蕃法，貴重大臣方得此賜，亦猶漢儀賜几杖之比也。 王峻持持而歸，契丹望之皆避路。 及峻至太原，帝知契丹政亂，乃議建號焉。 是月，秦州節度使何建以其地入於蜀。 戊辰，河東行軍司馬張彥威與文武將吏等，以中原無主，帝威望日隆，群情所屬，上箋勸進，帝謙讓不允。 自是群官三上箋，諸軍將吏、緇黃耆耋，相次迫請，教答允之。 庚午，陜府屯駐奉國指揮使趙暉、侯章、都頭王晏殺契丹監軍及副使劉願，暉自稱留後。 契丹因授暉陜州兵馬留後，侯章為本州馬步軍都指揮使，王晏為副都指揮使，暉等不受命。 〈 （《宋史·王晏傳》：開運末，與本軍都校趙暉、忠衛都校侯章等戍陜州。 會契丹至汴，遣其將劉願據陜，恣行暴虐。 晏與暉等謀曰：「今契丹南侵，天下洶洶，英雄豪傑固當乘時自奮。 且聞太原劉公，威德遠被，人心歸服，若殺願送款河東，為天下倡首，則取富貴如反掌耳！ 」暉等然之。 晏乃率敢死士數人，夜逾城入府署，劫庫兵給其徒。 遲明，斬願首懸府門外。 眾請暉為帥，章為本城副指揮使、內外巡檢使兼都虞候，乃遣其子漢倫奉表晉陽。）〉
On the new year's day dinghai of the first spring month, Tianfu 12, the Khitan ruler entered the Eastern Capital. On guisi the young Jin emperor was held captive at Fengchan Temple. On guimao the young emperor was sent north. On the new moon dingsi in the second month the Khitan ruler donned Chinese court robes, held court in the Hall of Veneration of the Origin, changed Jin to Great Liao by edict, proclaimed a general amnesty, and dated the reign the tenth year of Huitong. That month Gaozu sent his guard officer Wang Jun with a memorial to the Khitan court. The Khitan ruler replied with a commendatory edict, addressed him as "son," and gave him a wooden staff. Under Khitan custom only the most honored ministers received such a gift, comparable to the Han court's bestowal of armrests and staffs. Wang Jun carried it home, and Khitan onlookers cleared the road before him. When Jun reached Taiyuan, Gaozu saw that Khitan rule was falling into disorder and began to plan proclaiming a new dynasty. That month He Jian of Qinzhou surrendered his territory to Shu. On wuchen Zhang Yanwei, Hedong campaigning marshal, with civil and military officers, urged him to take the throne because the Central Plains had no ruler and all eyes were turning to his rising prestige. Gaozu declined. Officials memorialized three times; officers, clergy, and elders pressed him in turn until he assented by edict. On gengwu Zhao Hui of the Fengguo Army at Shaan, Hou Zhang, and company chief Wang Yan killed the Khitan supervisor Liu Yuan and his deputy; Hui declared himself acting commissioner. The Khitan then appointed Hui military commissioner of Shaan, Zhang director of local horse and foot forces, and Yan his deputy, but they refused. [ (History of Song, Biography of Wang Yan: at the end of Kaiyun he was posted to Shaan with his unit commander Zhao Hui and Zhongwei commander Hou Zhang. When the Khitan reached Bian they sent Liu Yuan to hold Shaan, where he ruled with savage cruelty. Yan said to Hui and the others, "The Khitan are invading south and the realm is in uproar. Men of spirit ought to seize the moment. We hear that Lord Liu of Taiyuan's prestige reaches far and men gladly follow him. Kill Yuan, submit to Hedong, and lead the realm—and wealth and rank will come as easily as turning one's hand! " Hui and the others agreed. Yan led a handful of dare-to-die men over the wall by night into the government compound and seized arms from the arsenal for his men. At dawn he beheaded Yuan and displayed his head outside the government gate. The troops made Hui commander, Zhang deputy commander and patrol commissioner, and sent his son Hanlun with a memorial to Jinyang.)]
6
辛未，帝於太原宮受冊，即皇帝位，制改晉開運四年為天福十二年。 〈 （《契丹國志》云：漢祖仍稱天福年號，曰：「予未忍忘晉也。」）〉 甲戌，帝以晉帝舉族北遷，憤惋久之。 是日，率親兵趨土門路，邀迎晉帝至壽陽，聞其已過，乃還。 契丹聞帝建號，偽制削奪帝官爵。 以通事耿崇美為潞州節度使，高唐英為相州節度使，崔廷勛為河陽節度使，以扼要害之地。 丁丑，磁州賊帥梁暉據相州。 己卯，帝遣都將史宏肇率兵討代州，平之。 初，代州刺史王暉叛歸契丹，宏肇一鼓而拔之，斬暉以徇。 庚辰，權晉州兵馬留後張晏洪奏，軍亂，殺知州副使駱從朗及括錢使、諫議大夫趙熙，以城歸順。 時晉州留後劉在明赴東京，朝於契丹，從朗知軍州事，帝方遣使張晏洪、辛處明等告諭登極，從朗囚之本城。 大將藥可儔殺從朗於理所，州民相率害趙熙，三軍請晏洪為留後，處明為都監。 辛巳，權陜州留後趙暉、權潞州留後王守恩，並上表歸順。 癸未，澶州賊帥王瓊與其眾斷本州浮橋，瓊敗，死之。 時契丹以族人朗鄂為澶州節度使，朗鄂性貪虐，吏民苦之。 瓊為水運什長，乃構夏津賊帥張乙，得千餘人，沿河而上，中夜竊發，自南城殺守將，絕浮航，入北城，朗悟據牙城以拒之。 數日，會契丹救至，瓊敗死焉。 契丹主初聞其變也，懼甚，由是大河之南無久留之意，尋遣天雄軍節度使杜重威歸鎮。
On xinwei he was invested at the Taiyuan palace and took the throne, changing Jin's Kaiyun 4 to Tianfu 12 by edict. [ (Records of the Khitan State: Han Gaozu kept the Tianfu reign title, saying, "I am not yet ready to forget Jin.")] On jiaxu, learning that the Jin emperor and his whole clan had been sent north, Gaozu brooded in anger and grief for a long time. That same day he led his personal guard toward Tumen Pass to intercept the Jin emperor and welcome him as far as Shouyang, but turned back on hearing he had already passed. When the Khitan learned he had proclaimed a dynasty, they issued a counterfeit edict stripping him of rank and titles. They installed the interpreter Geng Chongmei at Luzhou, Gao Tangying at Xiangzhou, and Cui Tingxun at Heyang to hold the strategic passes. On dingchou the Cizhou rebel Liang Hui seized Xiangzhou. On jimao he sent Commander Shi Hongzhao against Daizhou and pacified it. Daizhou prefect Wang Hui had earlier defected to the Khitan; Hongzhao stormed the city at the first blow and executed Hui as a warning. On gengchen acting Jinzhou commissioner Zhang Yanhong reported that the garrison had mutinied, killed vice prefect Luo Conglang, tax commissioner Remonstrance Grandee Zhao Xi, and submitted the city. Jinzhou acting commissioner Liu Zaiming was then at the Eastern Capital paying court to the Khitan, while Conglang ran local affairs. Gaozu had just sent Zhang Yanhong and Xin Chuming to announce his accession, and Conglang imprisoned them in the city. General Yao Keyou killed Conglang in the government hall; townspeople together killed Zhao Xi; the armies asked for Yanhong as acting commissioner and Chuming as chief supervisor. On xinsi acting commissioners Zhao Hui of Shaan and Wang Shouen of Lu both submitted memorials of submission. On guiwei the Chunzhou rebel Wang Qiong and his men cut the prefecture's pontoon bridge; Qiong was defeated and killed. The Khitan had appointed their kinsman Lange military commissioner of Chunzhou; greedy and brutal by nature, he made life miserable for officials and commoners alike. Qiong, a water-transport squad chief, allied with the Xiajin bandit Zhang Yi and raised more than a thousand men. They moved upriver, struck at midnight from the south city, killed the garrison commander, severed the pontoon bridge, and entered the north city, while Langwu barricaded himself in the headquarters compound. Within days Khitan reinforcements arrived; Qiong was defeated and killed. When the Khitan ruler first heard of the uprising he was deeply alarmed and no longer wished to linger south of the Yellow River; he soon sent Tianxiong commissioner Du Chongwei back to his command.
7
三月丙戌朔，詔河東管內，自前稅外，雜色征配一切除放。 是日，契丹主坐崇元殿行入閣之禮，契丹主以舅蕭翰為宣武軍節度使。 辛卯，權延州留後高允權遣判官李彬奏：本道節度使周密為三軍所逐，以允權知留後事，上表歸順。 未幾，帝召密赴行在。 壬辰，丹州都指揮使高彥珣殺偽命刺史，據城歸命。 壬寅，契丹主發自東京還本國。 是日，宿於赤崗，至晡，有大聲如雷，起於敵帳之下。 契丹自黎陽濟河，遂趨相州。 庚戌，帝以北京馬步軍都指揮使、泗州防禦使、檢校太保劉崇為太原尹、檢校太尉，以北京馬步軍都虞候郭從義為鄭州防禦使、檢校太保，以北京興捷左廂都指揮使李洪信為陳州刺史、檢校司徒，以興捷右廂都指揮使尚洪遷為單州刺史、檢校司徒，以北京武節左廂都指揮使蓋萬為蔡州刺史，以武節右廂都指揮使周暉為濮州刺史，以保寧都指揮使朱奉千為隨州刺史。 辛亥，吐渾節度使王義宗加檢校太尉，以前忻州刺史秦習為耀州團練使。 癸丑，以北京副留守、檢校司徒白文珂為河中節度使、檢校太尉。
On the new moon bingxu in the third month an edict remitted every miscellaneous levy in the Hedong circuit beyond standing taxes. That day the Khitan ruler held court in the Hall of Veneration of the Origin and performed the inner-court ceremony; he also appointed his uncle Xiao Han military commissioner of the Xuanwu Army. On xinmao acting Yanzhou commissioner Gao Yunquan reported through aide Li Bin that the circuit commissioner Zhou Mi had been expelled by the armies, that Yunquan was acting in his place, and that they submitted to the throne. Soon afterward the emperor summoned Zhou Mi to the mobile court. On renchen Danzhou director Gao Yancong killed the Khitan-appointed prefect, held the city, and submitted. On renyin the Khitan ruler left the Eastern Capital for home. That day he camped at Chigang; toward evening a thunderous roar rose from beneath the enemy camp. The Khitan crossed the river at Liyang and marched on Xiangzhou. On gengxu the emperor made Liu Chong, Beijing horse-and-foot commander and Sizhou defense commissioner, prefect of Taiyuan with acting grand marshal; Guo Congyi Beijing chief military judge, Zhengzhou defense commissioner and acting grand guardian; Li Hongxin of the Beijing Xingjie left wing, Chenzhou prefect and acting minister of works; Shang Hongqian of the Xingjie right wing, Shanzhou prefect and acting minister of works; Gai Wan of the Wujie left wing, Caizhou prefect; Zhou Hui of the Wujie right wing, Puzhou prefect; and Zhu Fengqian of the Baoning Army, Suizhou prefect. On xinhai Tujue commissioner Wang Yizong was made acting grand marshal, and former Xinzhou prefect Qin Xi became regimental commissioner of Yaozhou. On guichou Beijing deputy garrison commander Bai Wenke was made Hezhong military commissioner and acting grand marshal.
8
夏四月己未，以北京馬軍都指揮使、集州刺史劉信為滑州節度使，充侍衛馬軍都指揮使、檢校太傅； 以北京隨使、右都押衙楊邠為權樞密使、檢校太保； 以北京武節都指揮使、雷州刺史宏肇為許州節度使，充待衛步軍都指揮使、檢校太傅； 以北京牢城都指揮使、壁州刺史常思為鄧州節度使、檢校太傅兼權北京馬步軍都指揮使、三城巡檢使； 以河東行軍司馬張彥威為同州節度使、檢校太保； 以蕃漢兵馬都孔目官郭威為權樞密使、檢校司徒； 以河東左都押衙扈彥珂為宣徽南院使、檢校司徒； 以右都押衙王浩為宣徽北院使、檢校司徒； 以兩使都孔目官王章為權三司使、檢校太保。 是日，契丹主取相州，殺留後梁暉。 〈 （《宋史·李穀傳》：潛遣河朔酋豪暉入據安陽，契丹主患之，即謀北旋，會有告契丹以城中虛弱者，契丹還攻安陽，陷其城。）〉 暉，磁州滏陽人，少為盜，會契丹入汴，暉收集徒黨，先入磁州，無所侵犯，遣使送款於帝。 暉偵知相州頗積兵仗，且無守備，遂以三月二十一日夜與其徒逾垣而入，殺契丹數十人，奪器甲數萬計，遂據其城。 契丹主先遣偽命相州節度使高唐英率兵討之。 未幾，契丹主至城下，是月四日攻拔之，遂屠其城。 翌日，契丹主北去，命高唐英鎮之，唐英閱城中遺民，得男女七百人而已。 乾祐中，王繼宏鎮相州，奏於城中得髑髏十餘萬，殺人之數，從可知也。 庚申，以石州刺史易全章為洺州團練使，以前遼州刺史安真為宿州團練使，以嵐州刺史孟行超為潁州團練使，以汾州刺史武彥宏為曹州防禦使，以前憲州刺史慕容信為齊州防禦使，以遼州刺史薛瓊為亳州防禦使，以沁州刺史李漢韜為汝州防禦使。 癸亥，冊魏國夫人李氏為皇后。 甲子，以皇長子承訓為左衛上將軍，第二子承祐為左衛大將軍，第三子承勛為右衛大將軍，皇女彭城郡君宋氏封永寧公主，皇侄承赟為右衛上將軍。 以河東節度判官蘇逢吉為中書侍郎、同平章事、集賢殿大學士，以河東觀察判官蘇禹珪為中書侍郎、同平章事。 升府州為節鎮，加永安軍額。 以振武節度使、府州團練使折從阮為永安軍節度使，行府州刺史、檢校太尉； 以北京隨使、左都押衙劉銖為河陽節度使； 以河東支使韓祚為左諫議大夫，充樞密直學士。 乙丑，遣史宏肇率兵一萬人趨潞州。 丙寅，以權知潞州軍州事、左驍衛大將軍王守恩為潞州節度使、檢校太保； 以權點檢延州軍州事高允權為延州節度使、檢校太保； 以岢嵐軍使鄭謙為忻州刺史，遙領應州節度使，充忻、代二州義軍都部署。 丁卯，以河東都巡館驛、沿河巡檢使閻萬進為嵐州刺史，領朔州節度使，充嵐、憲二州義軍都制置。 戊辰，權河陽留後武行德以城來歸。 初，契丹主將發東京，船載武庫兵仗，自汴浮河，欲置之於北地，遣奉國都虞候武行德部送，與軍士千餘人並家屬俱行。 至河陰，軍亂，奪兵仗，殺契丹監吏，眾推行德為帥，與河陰屯駐軍士合，乃自汜水抵河陽。 河陽偽命節度使崔廷勛率兵拒之，兵敗，行德等追躡之，廷勛棄城而遁，行德因據其城。 〈 （《東都事略武行德傳》：行德陷於契丹，偽請自效，因遣送將校數十人護所取尚方鎧甲還北方。 至河陰，行德謂眾曰：「我與若等能為邊地鬼耶？ 」眾素伏其威名，皆曰：「惟命。 」遂攻孟州，走其節度使崔廷勛，悉以府庫分諸校，而權領州事。 遣其弟行友詣太原勸進。）〉 偽命西京留守劉晞棄洛城，南走許州，遂奔東京，洛京巡檢使方太自署知留守事。 未幾，太為武行德所害。 是日，蕃將耿崇美屯澤州，史宏肇遣先鋒將馬誨率兵擊之，崇美退保懷州。 崔廷勛以契丹眾攻武行德於河陽，行德出戰，為廷勛所敗。 汴州蕭翰遣蕃將高牟翰將兵援送劉晞復歸於洛。 牟翰至，殺前澶州節度使潘環於洛陽。 辛未，以河陽都部署武行德為河陽節度使、檢校太尉，充一行馬步軍都部署。 甲戌，潞州節度使王守恩加檢校太尉，以前棣州刺史慕容彥超為澶州節度使、檢校太保。 丙子，契丹主耶律德光卒於鎮之欒城。 趙延壽於鎮州自稱權知國事。 辛巳，陜州節度使趙暉加檢校太尉，華州節度使兼陜州馬步軍都指揮使侯章加檢校太傅，以陜府馬步軍副都指揮使兼絳州防禦使王晏為晉州節度使、檢校太傅，以丹州都指揮使、權知軍州事高彥珣為丹州刺史。
In the fourth summer month, on jiwei, Beijing horse commander and Jizhou prefect Liu Xin became Huazhou military commissioner, palace horse commander, and acting grand tutor; Beijing attendant Yang Bin became acting commissioner of military affairs and acting grand guardian; Beijing Wujie commander and Leizhou prefect Hongzhao became Xuzhou military commissioner, palace foot commander, and acting grand tutor; Beijing Laocheng commander and Bizhou prefect Chang Si became Dengzhou military commissioner and acting grand tutor, concurrently acting Beijing horse-and-foot commander and inspector of the Three Cities; Hedong campaigning marshal Zhang Yanwei became Tongzhou military commissioner and acting grand guardian; Guo Wei, chief clerk of the mixed tribal and Chinese forces, became acting commissioner of military affairs and acting minister of works; Hedong left chief escort Hu Yanke became commissioner of the southern palace secretariat bureau and acting minister of works; Right chief escort Wang Hao became commissioner of the northern palace secretariat bureau and acting minister of works; Wang Zhang, chief clerk of the two bureaus, became acting commissioner of the three departments and acting grand guardian. That day the Khitan ruler took Xiangzhou and killed acting commissioner Liang Hui. [ (History of Song, Biography of Li Gu: he secretly sent the Hebei chieftain Hui to hold Anyang. The Khitan ruler, alarmed, planned to withdraw north; when someone reported the city was weak, he turned back, attacked Anyang, and captured it.)] Hui, a native of Fuyang in Cizhou, had been a bandit in his youth. When the Khitan entered Bian he gathered followers, took Cizhou without looting it, and sent envoys offering allegiance to the throne. Learning that Xiangzhou held large stores of weapons but few defenders, he and his men scaled the walls on the night of the twenty-first of the third month, killed dozens of Khitan, seized tens of thousands of arms and armor, and seized the city. The Khitan ruler first sent the Khitan-appointed Xiangzhou commissioner Gao Tangying against him with an army. Soon the Khitan ruler arrived beneath the walls; on the fourth of that month he stormed the city and then massacred its people. The next day the Khitan ruler marched north and left Gao Tangying to garrison the ruins; Tangying found only seven hundred survivors, men and women together. During Qianyou, when Wang Jihong governed Xiangzhou, he reported finding more than a hundred thousand skulls in the city—a measure of the slaughter. On gengshen the emperor appointed Yi Quanzhang of Shizhou regimental commissioner of Mingzhou; former Liaozhou prefect An Zhen, Suzhou; Lanzhou prefect Meng Xingchao, Yingzhou; Fenzhou prefect Wu Yanhong, Caozhou; former Xianzhou prefect Murong Xin, Qizhou; Liaozhou prefect Xue Qiong, Bozhou; and Qinzhou prefect Li Hantao, Ruzhou. On guihai Lady Li of Wei was invested as empress. On jiazi the eldest son Chengxun became general-in-chief of the Left Guard; the second son Chengyou, great general of the Left Guard; the third son Chengxun, great general of the Right Guard; the emperor's daughter, Lady Song of Pengcheng, was enfeoffed as Princess Yongning; and his nephew Chengyun became general-in-chief of the Right Guard. Hedong commission adjutant Su Fengji became vice director of the Secretariat, concurrent grand councilor, and academician of the Hall of Assembled Worthies; Hedong observation adjutant Su Yugui became vice director and concurrent grand councilor. Fuzhou was elevated to a full military commission under the designation Yong'an Army. Zhenwu commissioner and Fuzhou regimental commissioner Zhe Congruan became Yong'an military commissioner, acting Fuzhou prefect, and acting grand marshal; Beijing attendant and left chief escort Liu Zhu became Heyang military commissioner; Hedong branch commissioner Han Zuo became left remonstrance grandee and direct academician of the bureau of military affairs. On yichou Shi Hongzhao marched toward Luzhou with ten thousand men. On bingyin acting Luzhou administrator and left valiant-cavalry great general Wang Shouen became Luzhou military commissioner and acting grand guardian; Acting Yanzhou inspector Gao Yunquan became Yanzhou military commissioner and acting grand guardian; Kelan Army commander Zheng Qian became Xinzhou prefect, held Yingzhou in absentia, and commanded the loyal armies of Xin and Dai. On dingmao Hedong post-station inspector and river patrol commissioner Yan Wanjin became Lanzhou prefect, held Shuozhou, and commanded the loyal armies of Lan and Xian. On wuchen acting Heyang commissioner Wu Xingde submitted his city. Earlier, as the Khitan ruler prepared to leave the Eastern Capital, he loaded arsenal weapons onto boats at Bian to float north and sent Fengguo chief military judge Wu Xingde to escort them with more than a thousand soldiers and their families. At Heyin the escort mutinied, seized the weapons, killed the Khitan supervisors, and made Xingde their commander. Joining the Heyin garrison, they marched from Sishui to Heyang. Khitan-appointed Heyang commissioner Cui Tingxun marched out to resist but was routed; Xingde pursued him, Tingxun fled, and Xingde seized the city. [ (Brief Account of the Eastern Capital, Biography of Wu Xingde: captured by the Khitan, he pretended to serve them and was sent north with several dozen officers to escort seized imperial armor. At Heyin he told the men, "Are we to become ghosts on the frontier? " They had long respected his reputation and answered, "We follow your lead. " They attacked Mengzhou, drove out commissioner Cui Tingxun, divided the treasury among the officers, and Xingde acted as prefect. He sent his brother Xingyou to Taiyuan to urge the throne.)] Khitan-appointed western capital governor Liu Xi abandoned Luoyang, fled south to Xuzhou, then to the Eastern Capital. Luoyang patrol commissioner Fang Tai declared himself acting governor. Soon Fang Tai was killed by Wu Xingde. That day tribal general Geng Chongmei held Zezhou; Shi Hongzhao sent vanguard Ma Hui against him, and Chongmei fell back to Huaizhou. Cui Tingxun attacked Wu Xingde at Heyang with Khitan troops; Xingde marched out and was defeated. Xiao Han of Bian sent tribal general Gao Mouhan with troops to escort Liu Xi back to Luoyang. When Mouhan arrived he killed former Chunzhou commissioner Pan Huan at Luoyang. On xinwei Heyang supreme commander Wu Xingde became Heyang military commissioner and acting grand marshal, and supreme commander of the campaign forces. On jiaxu Luzhou commissioner Wang Shouen was made acting grand marshal, and former Dizhou prefect Murong Yanchao became Chunzhou military commissioner and acting grand guardian. On bingzi the Khitan ruler Yelü Deguang died at Luancheng in Zhen prefecture. At Zhenzhou Zhao Yanshou declared himself acting administrator of state affairs. On xinsi Shaan commissioner Zhao Hui was made acting grand marshal; Huazhou commissioner and Shaan horse-and-foot commander Hou Zhang, acting grand tutor; Shaan deputy commander and Jiangzhou defense commissioner Wang Yan, Jinzhou military commissioner and acting grand tutor; and acting Danzhou administrator Gao Yancong, Danzhou prefect.
