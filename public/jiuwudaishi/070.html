元行欽，本幽州劉守光之愛將。 守光之奪父位也，令行欽攻大恩山，又令殺諸兄弟。 天祐九年，周德威攻圍山州，守光困蹙，令行欽於山北募兵，以應契丹。 時明宗為將，攻行欽於山北，與之接戰，矢及明宗馬鞍，既而以勢迫來降。 明宗憐其有勇，奏隸為假子，後因從征討，恩禮特隆。 常臨敵擒生，必有所獲，名聞軍中。 莊宗東定趙、魏，選驍健置之麾下，因索行欽，莊宗不得已而遣之。 時有散指揮都頭，名為散員，命行欽為都部署，賜姓，名紹榮。 莊宗好戰，勇於大敵，或臨陣有急兵，行欽必橫身解鬥翼衛之。 莊宗營於德勝也，與汴軍戰於潘張，王師不利，諸軍奔亂。 莊宗得三四騎而旋，中野為汴軍數百騎攢槊攻之，事將不測，行欽識其幟，急馳一騎，奮劍斷二矛，斬一級，汴軍乃解圍，翼莊宗還宮。 莊宗因流涕言曰：「富貴與卿共之。 」自是寵冠諸將，官至檢校太傅、忻州刺史。 及莊宗平梁，授武寧軍節度使。 嘗因內宴群臣，使相預會，行欽官為保傅，當地褥下坐。 酒酣樂作，莊宗敘生平戰陣之事，因左右顧視，曰：「紹榮安在？ 」所司奏云：「有敕，使相預會，紹榮散官，殿上無位。 」莊宗徹會不懌。 翌日，以行欽為同平章事，由是不宴百官於內殿，但宴武臣而已，
Yuan Xingqin had originally been a favorite general of Liu Shouguang of Youzhou. When Shouguang seized power from his father, he sent Xingqin to attack Mount Da'en and had him put his brothers to death. In the ninth year of Tianyou, Zhou Dewei laid siege to Shan Prefecture. With Shouguang besieged and desperate, he ordered Xingqin to raise troops north of the mountains to join forces with the Khitan. The future Mingzong was then serving as a general. He attacked Xingqin in the northern hills and fought him in pitched battle—an arrow struck the Mingzong's saddle—until finally, overcome by the situation, Xingqin submitted. The Mingzong admired his valor and petitioned to take him as an adopted son. Thereafter, as Xingqin campaigned beside him, he received exceptional favor and respect. In battle he habitually took living captives and never came away empty-handed; his reputation rang through the ranks. When the Zhuangzong conquered Zhao and Wei in the east, he drafted the finest fighters into his personal following and specifically asked for Xingqin. The Zhuangzong had no choice but to release him. There was then a corps of unattached commanders known as the "scattered members"; the Zhuangzong made Xingqin their chief deployer, granted him the imperial surname, and gave him the name Shaorong. The Zhuangzong was eager for combat and fearless before formidable foes. Whenever danger closed in on the field, Xingqin would throw himself into the fray to fight off attackers and shield him. While the Zhuangzong was encamped at Desheng, his forces met the Bian army at Panzhang. The imperial troops were routed and the whole army broke in panic. The Zhuangzong escaped with only three or four riders. In the open country several hundred Bian cavalry closed in with leveled spears, and the situation was on the verge of disaster. Xingqin recognized their banners, spurred forward alone, swung his sword to snap two spears and cut down one man, and the Bian troops broke the encirclement. He then escorted the Zhuangzong safely back to the palace. The Zhuangzong wept and said, "Whatever wealth and honor are mine, I will share them with you. " From that day his favor outstripped every other general's, and he rose to honorary Grand Tutor and prefect of Xin Prefecture. After the Zhuangzong conquered the Liang, he was made military governor of Wuning. On one occasion the Zhuangzong held an inner banquet for his ministers, with chief commissioners present. Xingqin held the rank of guardian-tutor and was assigned a seat on the floor below the mat. As the wine flowed and music began, the Zhuangzong recounted his battles through the years, then glanced about and asked, "Where is Shaorong? " The master of ceremonies replied, "By imperial order, when chief commissioners attend, Shaorong holds only a nominal rank and has no place in the hall. " The Zhuangzong broke up the banquet in displeasure. The next day he appointed Xingqin co-equal chief councilor. Thereafter he no longer entertained the civil officials in the inner hall, but held banquets only for military men,

三年，行欽喪婦。 莊宗有所愛宮人生皇子者，劉皇后心忌之，會行欽入侍，莊宗勞之曰：「紹榮喪婦復娶耶！ 吾給爾婚財。 」皇后指所忌宮人謂莊宗曰：「皇帝憐紹榮，可使為婦。 」莊宗難違所請，微許之。 皇后即命紹榮謝之，未退，肩輿已出。 莊宗心不懌，佯不豫者累日，業已遣去，無如之何。 及貝州軍亂，趙在禮入魏州，莊宗方擇將，皇后曰：「小事不勞大將，促紹榮指揮可也。 」乃以行欽為鄴都行營招撫使，領騎二千進討。 洎至鄴城，攻之不能下，退保於澶州。 未幾，諸道之師稍集，復進軍於鄴城之南。 及明宗為帥，領軍至鄴，行欽來謁於軍中，拜起之際，誤呼萬歲者再，明宗驚駭，遏之方止。 既而明宗營於城西，行欽營於城南。 三月八日夜，明宗為亂軍所迫，惟行欽之軍不動，按甲以自固。 明宗密令張虔釗至行欽營，戒之曰：「且堅壁勿動，計會同殺亂軍，莫錯疑誤。 」行欽不聽，將步騎萬人棄甲而退。 自知失策，且保衛州，因誣奏明宗曰：「鎮師已入賊軍，終不為國使。 」明宗既劫出鄴城，令人走馬上章，申理其事，言：「臣且於近郡聽進止。 」莊宗覽奏釋然曰：「吾知紹榮妄矣。 」因令白從訓與明宗子繼璟至軍前，欲令見明宗，行欽縶繼璟於路。 明宗凡奏軍機，拘留不達，故旬日之間，音驛斷絕。 及莊宗出成皋，知明宗在黎陽，復令繼璟渡河召明宗，行欽即殺之，仍勸班師。 四月一日，莊宗既崩，行欽引皇后、存渥，得七百騎出師子門，將之河中就存霸，沿路部下解散，從者數騎而已。 四日，至平陸縣界，為百姓所擒，縣令裴進折其足，檻車以獻。 明宗即位，詔削奪行欽在位官爵，斬於洛陽。
In the third year of his reign, Xingqin lost his wife. The Zhuangzong favored a palace woman who had borne him a son, and Empress Liu resented her. When Xingqin came to attend him, the Zhuangzong said kindly, "Shaorong, now that your wife is dead, will you take another!" I will supply your wedding gifts. " The empress pointed at the woman she envied and said to the Zhuangzong, "Your Majesty pities Shaorong—let her become his wife. " The Zhuangzong could hardly refuse and gave a reluctant assent. The empress at once ordered Shaorong to give thanks. Before he could withdraw, a sedan chair had already been dispatched. The Zhuangzong was inwardly displeased and feigned illness for days, but the woman had already been sent off and nothing could be undone. When the Beizhou garrison mutinied and Zhao Zaili seized Weizhou, the Zhuangzong was choosing a commander. The empress said, "A trifling affair need not tie up a great general—send Shaorong to take command at once. " Xingqin was appointed commissioner for pacification of the Ye capital field headquarters and led two thousand cavalry against the rebels. When he reached Ye, he besieged the city but could not capture it and fell back to hold Chuan Prefecture. Before long troops from the various circuits gathered, and he advanced again to the south of Ye. When the Mingzong took command and marched to Ye, Xingqin came to pay his respects in camp. As he rose from his bow he twice cried "Long live the Emperor!" by mistake. The Mingzong was shocked and had to silence him before he stopped. The Mingzong then encamped west of the city and Xingqin south of it. On the night of the eighth day of the third month the Mingzong was driven by the mutineers, but only Xingqin's troops held their ground, keeping their armor on and standing firm. The Mingzong secretly sent Zhang Qianzhao to Xingqin's camp with this warning: "Hold your position and do not move. We mean to join forces and destroy the mutineers—do not misread the situation. " Xingqin refused to listen. He led ten thousand foot and horse, threw off their armor, and retreated. Realizing his mistake, he fell back to hold Weizhou and sent a false report accusing the Mingzong: "The circuit commander has gone over to the rebels and will never again serve the throne. " After the Mingzong had been seized and carried out of Ye, he sent a rider with a memorial explaining the affair: "Your servant will await further orders in a nearby prefecture. " When the Zhuangzong read the memorial he said with relief, "I knew Shaorong's report was false. " He ordered Bai Congxun and the Mingzong's son Jijing to the front, intending that they should see the Mingzong, but Xingqin seized Jijing on the way. Every memorial the Mingzong sent on military affairs was intercepted, and for ten days no word reached the capital. When the Zhuangzong marched out of Chenggao and learned the Mingzong was at Liyang, he again ordered Jijing to cross the river and summon him. Xingqin killed Jijing at once and pressed for a general withdrawal. On the first day of the fourth month, after the Zhuangzong's death, Xingqin escorted the empress and Cunwo with seven hundred horsemen out the Shizi Gate, intending to flee to Hezhong and join Cunba. Along the way his men deserted until only a handful of riders remained. On the fourth day he reached Pinglu County, where the local people seized him. Magistrate Pei Jin broke his legs and sent him to the capital in a cage cart. After the Mingzong took the throne, an edict stripped Xingqin of all his offices and titles, and he was executed at Luoyang.

夏魯奇，字邦傑，青州人也。 初事宣武軍為軍校，與主將不協，遂歸於莊宗，以為護衛指揮使。 從周德威攻幽州，燕將有單廷珪、元行欽，時稱驍勇，魯奇與之鬥，兩不能解，將士皆釋兵縱觀。 幽州平，魯奇功居多。 梁將劉鄩在洹水，莊宗深入致師，鄩設伏於魏縣西南葭蘆中。 莊宗不滿千騎，汴人伏兵萬餘，大噪而起，圍莊宗數重。 魯奇與王門關、烏德兒等奮命決戰，自午至申，俄而李存審兵至方解。 魯奇持槍攜劍，獨衛莊宗，手殺百餘人。 烏德兒等被擒，魯奇傷痍遍體，自是莊宗尤憐之，曆磁州刺史。 中都之戰，汴人大敗，魯奇見王彥章，識之，單馬追及，槍擬其頸； 彥章顧曰：「爾非餘故人乎？ 」即擒之以獻。 莊宗壯之，賞絹千匹。 〈 （《九國誌·趙庭隱傳》：王彥章守中都，庭隱在其軍中。 及彥章敗，庭隱為莊宗所獲，將以就戮，大將夏魯奇奏曰：「此矬也，其材可用。 」遂釋之。）〉 梁平，授鄭州防禦使。 四年，授河陽節度使。 天成初，移鎮許州，加同平章事。
Xia Luqi, whose courtesy name was Bangjie, was a native of Qingzhou. He first served the Xuanwu army as a junior officer. At odds with his commander, he defected to the Zhuangzong, who appointed him commander of the imperial guard. Campaigning with Zhou Dewei against Youzhou, he fought the Yan generals Shan Tinggui and Yuan Xingqin, both renowned for valor. Luqi grappled with them until neither side could break away, while soldiers on both sides laid down their arms to watch. When Youzhou fell, Luqi's contribution was the greatest of all. The Liang general Liu Yan held the Huan River. The Zhuangzong advanced deep into enemy territory to provoke battle, and Yan laid an ambush in the reed marshes southwest of Wei County. The Zhuangzong had fewer than a thousand riders when more than ten thousand Bian troops sprang from ambush with a great shout and surrounded him in layer after layer. Luqi, Wang Menguan, Wu De'er, and others fought for their lives from noon until mid-afternoon before Li Cunshen's reinforcements arrived and broke the encirclement. Luqi fought with spear in one hand and sword in the other, shielding the Zhuangzong alone and killing more than a hundred men with his own hand. Wu De'er and others were taken prisoner. Luqi was covered with wounds, and from then on the Zhuangzong favored him all the more. He later served as prefect of Ci Prefecture. At the battle of Zhongdu the Bian army was routed. Luqi spotted Wang Yanzhang, recognized him, chased him down alone, and pressed his spear to Yanzhang's neck; Yanzhang looked back and said, "Aren't you an old friend of mine? " He then seized him and presented him as a captive. The Zhuangzong was impressed and rewarded him with a thousand bolts of silk. [ (From the 《Records of the Nine States · Biography of Zhao Tingyin》: Wang Yanzhang held Zhongdu, and Tingyin served in his army. When Yanzhang was defeated, Tingyin was captured by the Zhuangzong and was to be executed. The great general Xia Luqi memorialized, "He is a dwarf, but his abilities are useful. " He was then released.)〉 After the Liang was conquered, he was appointed defender of Zheng Prefecture. In the fourth year he was made military governor of Heyang. At the beginning of the Tiancheng era he was transferred to Xuzhou as military governor and made co-equal chief councilor.

魯奇性忠義，尤通吏道，撫民有術。 及移鎮許田，孟州之民，萬眾遮道，斷鐙臥轍，五日不發。 父老詣闕請留，明宗令中使諭之，方得離州。 明宗討荊南，魯奇為副招討使，頃之，移鎮遂州。 〈 （《九國誌·李仁罕傳》：夏魯奇稟朝廷之命，繕治甲兵，將圖蜀，孟知祥與董璋謀先取魯奇，令仁罕攻遂州。）〉 董璋之叛，與孟知祥攻遂州，援路斷絕，兵盡食窮， 〈 （《九國誌·李肇傳》：蜀師圍夏魯奇於遂州，唐師來援，劍門不守，肇領兵赴普安以拒之，唐師不得進。）〉 魯奇自刎而卒，時年四十九。 帝聞其死也，慟哭之，厚給其家，贈太師、齊國公。
Luqi was loyal and upright by nature, especially skilled in administration, and knew how to govern the people well. When he was transferred to Xutian, tens of thousands of people from Meng Prefecture blocked his path, broke his stirrups, and lay across his carriage wheels so that for five days he could not leave. The elders petitioned the court to keep him. The Mingzong sent a palace envoy to reason with them, and only then could Luqi leave the prefecture. When the Mingzong campaigned against Jingnan, Luqi served as deputy pacification commissioner. Soon afterward he was transferred to Suizhou. [ (From the 《Records of the Nine States · Biography of Li Renhan》: Xia Luqi, acting on court orders, repaired armor and weapons and prepared to move against Shu. Meng Zhixiang and Dong Zhang plotted to strike Luqi first and sent Renhan to attack Suizhou.)〉 When Dong Zhang rebelled, he and Meng Zhixiang attacked Suizhou. Relief routes were cut, supplies ran out, and the garrison was starving, [ (From the 《Records of the Nine States · Biography of Li Zhao》: Shu forces besieged Xia Luqi at Suizhou. When Tang relief troops came, Jianmen had fallen, and Zhao led troops to Pu'an to block them so that the Tang army could not advance.)〉 Luqi took his own life by cutting his throat. He was forty-nine. When the emperor heard of his death, he wept bitterly, gave his family generous support, and posthumously enfeoffed him as Grand Preceptor and Duke of Qi.

姚洪，本梁之小校也。 在梁時，經事董璋，長興初，率兵千人戍閬州。 璋叛，領眾攻閬州，璋密令人誘洪，洪以大義拒之。 及璋攻城，洪悉力拒守者三日，禦備既竭，城陷被擒。 璋謂洪曰：「爾頃為健兒，由吾獎拔至此； 吾書誘諭，投之於側，何相負耶？ 」洪大罵曰：「老賊，爾為天子鎮帥，何苦反耶！ 爾既辜恩背主，吾與爾何恩，而雲相負！ 爾為李七郎奴，掃馬糞，得一臠殘炙，感恩無盡。 今明天子付與茅土，貴為諸侯，而驅徒結黨，圖為反噬。 爾本奴才，則無恥； 吾忠義之士，不忍為也。 吾可為天子死，不能與人奴苟生！ 」璋怒，令軍士十人，持刀刲割其膚，燃鑊於前，自取啖食，洪至死大罵不已。 明宗聞之泣下，置洪二子於近衛，給賜甚厚。
Yao Hong had originally been a junior officer in the Liang army. In Liang service he had once served under Dong Zhang. At the beginning of the Changxing era he led a thousand men to garrison Lang Prefecture. When Zhang rebelled, he marched on Lang Prefecture and secretly sent envoys to win Hong over, but Hong refused on grounds of loyalty. When Zhang besieged the city, Hong defended with all his strength for three days until supplies were exhausted, the city fell, and he was taken. Zhang said to Hong, "You were once a common soldier whom I raised to your present rank; I sent you letters urging you to join me. Why have you betrayed me? " Hong shouted back, "You old traitor! You were a frontier commander appointed by the Son of Heaven—why turn rebel! You have betrayed your sovereign—what debt do I owe you that you speak of betrayal! You were once Li the Seventh's servant, mucking out stables, and you were endlessly grateful for a scrap of leftover meat. Now the Son of Heaven has granted you a fief and made you a feudal lord, yet you rally followers and plot rebellion against your benefactor. You were born a slave, so you have no shame. I am a man of loyalty and righteousness and cannot stoop to that. I can die for the Son of Heaven, but I will not live on as another man's lackey! " Zhang in fury ordered ten soldiers to carve slices from Hong's flesh, heated a cauldron before him, and ate the flesh himself. Hong cursed him without cease until he died. When the Mingzong heard of it he wept, enrolled Hong's two sons in the inner guard, and gave them generous rewards.

李嚴，幽州人，本名讓坤。 初仕燕，為刺史，涉獵書傳，便弓馬，有口辯，多遊藝，以功名自許。 同光中，為客省使。 奉使於蜀，及與王衍相見，陳使者之禮，因於笏記中具述莊宗興復之功，其警句云：「才過汶水，縛王彥章於馬前； 旋及夷門，斬朱友貞於樓上。 」嚴復聲韻清亮，蜀人聽之愕然。 時蜀偽樞密使宋光嗣召嚴曲宴，因以近事訊於嚴。 嚴對曰：「吾皇前年四月即位於鄴宮，當月下鄆州。 十月四日，親統萬騎破賊中都，乘勝鼓行，遂誅汴孽，偽梁尚有兵三十萬，謀臣猛將，解甲倒戈。 西盡甘、涼，東漸海外，南逾閩、浙，北極幽陵。 牧伯侯王，稱藩不暇，家財入貢，府實上供。 吳國本朝舊臣，岐下先皇元老，遣子入侍，述職稱藩。 淮、海之君，卑辭厚貢，湖湘、荊楚，杭越、甌閩，異貨奇珍，府無虛月。 吾皇以德懷來，以威款附。 順則涵之以恩澤，逆則問之以干戈，四海車書，大同非晚。 」光嗣曰：「餘所未知，惟岐下宋公，我之姻好，洞見其心，反覆多端，專謀跋扈，大不足信也。 似聞契丹部族，近日稍強，大國可無慮乎？ 」嚴曰：「子言契丹之強盛，孰若偽梁？ 」曰：「比梁差劣也。 」嚴曰：「吾國視契丹如蚤虱耳，以其無害，不足爬搔。 吾良將勁兵布天下，彼不勞一郡之兵，一校之眾，則懸首槀街，盡為奴擄。 但以天生四夷，當置度外，不在九州之本，未欲窮兵黷武也。 」光嗣聞辯對，畏而奇之。 時王衍失政，嚴知其可取，使還具奏，故平蜀之謀，始於嚴。
Li Yan, a native of Youzhou, was originally named Rangkun. He first served Yan as a prefect, read widely in the classics, was skilled in archery and horsemanship, eloquent in speech, and accomplished in many arts, and he aspired to merit and fame. During the Tongguang era he served as commissioner of the reception office. On a mission to Shu, when he met Wang Yan he observed the protocol of an envoy and, in notes on his tablet, recounted the Zhuangzong's restoration of the Tang. A striking passage ran: "Hardly past the Wen River, he bound Wang Yanzhang before his horse; then at Yimen he beheaded Zhu Youzhen on the tower." Yan recited it again in a clear, resonant voice, and the Shu listeners were stunned. The Shu pretender's commissioner of military affairs Song Guangsi then invited Yan to a private banquet and questioned him about recent events. Yan replied, "Our emperor took the throne at Ye in the fourth month two years ago and that same month marched on Yun Prefecture. On the fourth day of the tenth month he personally led ten thousand horsemen and crushed the rebels at Zhongdu. Pressing the victory, he marched on and destroyed the Bian regime. Though the false Liang still had three hundred thousand troops, their strategists and fierce generals laid down arms and turned their coats. His realm reached west to Gan and Liang, east to the sea, south beyond Min and Zhe, and north to Youling. Regional lords and feudal kings vied to declare themselves vassals; they sent family wealth as tribute and filled the imperial storehouses with supplies. Wu, a former subject of our court, and the veteran minister below Qi sent their sons to serve at court and paid homage as vassals. Lords along the Huai and the coast sent humble embassies with rich tribute. From Huguang, Jingchu, Hangzhou, Yue, and the Min coast came exotic goods and curios so that the imperial storehouses were never empty for a month. Our emperor wins men by virtue and binds them by might. Those who submit he showers with grace; those who resist he meets with arms. When chariots and writing unite the four seas, universal peace will not be long delayed. " Guangsi said, "Of the rest I cannot speak, but Lord Song below Qi is my kinsman by marriage and I know his heart well. He is treacherous and schemes only for his own power—he is utterly untrustworthy. I hear the Khitan tribes have grown stronger lately. Surely your great state has no worries on that account? " Yan said, "You speak of Khitan strength—how does it compare with the false Liang? " He replied, "They are rather weaker than the Liang. " Yan said, "Our state regards the Khitan as fleas and lice—harmless vermin not worth troubling over. Our finest generals and strongest troops are posted across the realm. We would not need even one commandery's army or one colonel's men to hang their heads on the execution ground and make slaves of them all. But the four barbarians are Heaven's creation and lie outside our core realm. We have no wish to exhaust the army in reckless war. " Guangsi, hearing this reply, was both intimidated and impressed. Wang Yan's rule was failing, and Yan saw that Shu could be taken. On his return he memorialized in full, and the plan to conquer Shu began with him.
7
郭崇韜起軍之日，以嚴為三川招撫使，嚴與先鋒使康延孝將兵五千，先驅閣道，或馳以詞說，或威以兵鋒，大軍未及，所在降下。 延孝在漢州，王衍與書曰：「可請李司空先來，餘即舉城納款。 」眾咸以討蜀之謀始於嚴，衍以甘言，將誘而殺之，欲不令往。 嚴聞之喜，即馳騎入益州，衍見嚴於母前，以母、妻為托。 即日，引蜀使歐陽彬迎謁魏王繼岌。 蜀平班師，會明宗即位，遷泗州防禦使兼客省使。 長興初，安重誨謀欲控製兩川，嚴乃求為西川兵馬都監，庶效方略。 孟知祥覺之，既至，執而害之。 〈 （《九國誌·王彥銖傳》：李嚴之為監軍也，密懷異謀，知祥數其過，命彥銖擒斬之，嚴之左右無敢動者。）〉 贈太保。
When Guo Chongtao marched, he appointed Yan commissioner for pacification of the Three Rivers. Yan and the vanguard commander Kang Yanxiao led five thousand men ahead along the plank roads, sometimes winning surrender by persuasion, sometimes by the threat of arms. Before the main army arrived, city after city submitted. Yanxiao was at Han Prefecture when Wang Yan wrote to him: "Ask Minister Li to come first, and I will surrender the whole city. " Everyone said the Shu campaign had been Yan's idea and that Wang Yan's kind words were a trap to lure and kill him, and they tried to keep Yan from going. Yan was delighted and rode at once into Yizhou. Wang Yan received him before his mother and entrusted his mother and wife to Yan's care. That same day he brought the Shu envoy Ouyang Bin to pay respects to Prince of Wei Jiji. After Shu was pacified the army withdrew. When the Mingzong took the throne, Yan was made defender of Si Prefecture and commissioner of the reception office. At the beginning of the Changxing era An Chonghui sought to control the Two Rivers, and Yan asked to be made overall military inspector of western Shu so that he might put his plans into effect. Meng Zhixiang saw through him, and when Yan arrived he seized and killed him. [ (From the 《Records of the Nine States · Biography of Wang Yanzhu》: When Li Yan served as army inspector he secretly plotted treachery. Zhixiang listed his offenses and ordered Yanzhu to seize and behead him; none of Yan's attendants dared stir.)〉 He was posthumously enfeoffed as Grand Guardian.
8
嚴之母，賢明婦人。 初，嚴將赴蜀，母曰：「汝前啟破蜀之謀，今又入蜀，將死報蜀人矣！ 與汝永訣。 」既而果如其言。
Yan's mother was a wise and virtuous woman. When Yan was about to go to Shu, his mother said, "You were the one who proposed destroying Shu. Now you go into Shu again—you will die at the hands of the Shu people! This is our final parting. " In the end it happened exactly as she had foretold.
9
李仁矩，本明宗在藩鎮時客將也。 明宗即位，錄其趨走之勞，擢居內職，復為安重誨所庇，故數年之間，遷為客省使、左衛大將軍。 天成中，因奉使東川，董璋張筵以召之，仁矩貪於館舍，與倡妓酣飲，日既中而不至，大為璋所詬辱，自是深銜之。 長興初，璋既跋扈於東川，重誨奏以仁矩為閬州節度使，俾伺璋之反狀，時物議以為不可。 及仁矩至鎮，偵璋所為，曲形奏報，地裏遐僻，朝廷莫知事實，激成璋之逆節，由仁矩也。 長興元年冬十月，璋自率凶黨，以攻其城。 仁矩召軍校謀守戰利害，皆曰：「璋久圖反計，以賂誘士心，凶氣方盛，未可與戰，宜堅壁以守之。 儻旬浹之間，大軍東至，即賊必退。 」仁矩曰：「蜀兵懦，安能當我精甲！ 」即驅之出戰，兵未交，為賊所敗。 既而城陷，仁矩被擒，舉族為璋所害。
Li Renju had originally been a guest officer when the Mingzong held a frontier command. When the Mingzong took the throne he rewarded Renju's service in attendance and brought him into the inner court. Protected by An Chonghui, within a few years he rose to commissioner of the reception office and great general of the Left Guard. During the Tiancheng era, on a mission to eastern Shu, Dong Zhang held a banquet for him, but Renju lingered at his lodgings drinking with courtesans until past noon and never appeared. Zhang reviled him harshly, and from then on bore a deep grudge. At the beginning of the Changxing era Zhang was already overbearing in eastern Shu. Chonghui petitioned to make Renju military governor of Lang Prefecture to watch for signs of rebellion, though public opinion held this unwise. When Renju reached his post he spied on Zhang and sent distorted reports to the court. The region was remote and the court knew no better, and Zhang's rebellion was largely provoked by Renju. In the tenth month of winter in the first year of Changxing, Zhang personally led his followers to attack the city. Renju called his officers to discuss defense. All said, "Zhang has long plotted rebellion and bought his soldiers' loyalty. His momentum is at its peak—we should not fight but hold the walls. Within ten days the main army will arrive from the east and the rebels will withdraw. " Renju said, "Shu soldiers are cowards—how can they stand against our elite troops! " He drove them out to fight at once. Before battle was joined they were routed. Soon the city fell. Renju was captured and Zhang killed his entire clan.
10
康思立，晉陽人也。 少善騎射，事武皇為爪牙，署河東親騎軍使。 莊宗嗣位，從解圍於上黨，敗梁人於柏鄉，及平薊兵，後戰於河上，皆有功，累承製加檢校戶部尚書，右突騎指揮使。 莊宗即位，繼改軍帥，賜忠勇拱衛功臣，加檢校尚書右僕射。 天成元年，授應州刺史，尋移嵐州，充北面諸蕃部族都監。 三年，遷宿州團練使。 四年，領昭武軍節度、利巴集等州觀察處置等使。 改賜耀忠保節功臣。 長興初，朝廷舉兵討東川董璋，詔監西面行營軍馬都指揮使。 二年，移鎮陝州。 〈 （《通鑒》：潞王至靈寶，思立謀固守陝城以俟康義誠。 先是，捧聖五百騎戍陝，為潞王前鋒，至城下，呼城上人曰：「禁軍十萬已奉新帝，爾輩數人奚為！ 徒累一城人塗地耳！ 」於是捧聖卒爭出迎，思立不能禁，不得已，亦出迎。）〉 清泰初，改授邢台，累官至檢校太傅，封會稽郡開國侯。 二年，入為右神武統軍。 三年，充北面行營馬軍都指揮使。 是歲閏十一月，卒於軍，年六十三。
Kang Sili was a native of Jinyang. In youth he excelled at mounted archery. He served the Martial Emperor as a trusted agent and was appointed commander of the Hedong personal cavalry. When the Zhuangzong succeeded he fought at Shangdang, routed the Liang at Baixiang, helped pacify Ji, and later fought on the Yellow River, earning merit in each campaign. He was repeatedly promoted to honorary Minister of Revenue and commander of the Right Shock Cavalry. When the Zhuangzong took the throne he was made army commander, granted the title Meritorious Minister of Loyal Valor and Imperial Guard, and promoted to honorary Right Vice Director of the Masters of Writing. In the first year of Tiancheng he was appointed prefect of Ying Prefecture, then transferred to Lan Prefecture as overall inspector of the northern frontier tribes. In the third year he was made regimental commissioner of Suzhou. In the fourth year he became military governor of Zhaowu and commissioner overseeing Li, Ba, Ji, and the other prefectures. He was granted the new title Meritorious Minister of Glorious Loyalty and Preservation of Integrity. At the beginning of the Changxing era the court marched against Dong Zhang of eastern Shu and appointed him to supervise the western field headquarters as overall commander of army and horse. In the second year he was transferred to Shan Prefecture as military governor. [ (From the 《Comprehensive Mirror》: When the Prince of Lu reached Lingbao, Sili planned to hold Shanzhou firmly and await Kang Yicheng. Earlier, five hundred Pingsheng cavalry garrisoned Shan as the Prince of Lu's vanguard. At the foot of the wall they shouted, "A hundred thousand imperial guards have already acclaimed the new emperor—what are you few doing here! You will only bring ruin on the whole city! " The Pingsheng troops rushed out to welcome them. Sili could not stop them and, with no choice, went out to welcome them as well.)〉 At the beginning of the Qingtai era he was reassigned to Xing Prefecture, rose to honorary Grand Tutor, and was enfeoffed as Marquis of Kuaiji. In the second year he entered court as commander-in-chief of the Right Divine Martial Guard. In the third year he served as overall commander of cavalry for the northern field headquarters. In the intercalary eleventh month of that year he died in camp at the age of sixty-three.
11
張敬達，字志通，代州人，小字生鐵。 父審，素有勇，事武皇為列校，曆廳直軍使，同光初，卒於軍。 敬達少以騎射著名，莊宗知之，召令繼父職； 平河南有功，繼加檢校工部尚書。 明宗即位，曆捧聖指揮使、檢校尚書左僕射。 長興中，改河東馬步軍都指揮使，超授檢校司徒，領欽州刺史。 三年，加檢校太保、應州節度使。 四年，遷雲州。 時以契丹率族帳自黑榆林挎剌泊至沒越泊，雲借漢界水草，敬達每聚兵塞下，以遏其衝。 契丹竟不敢南牧，邊人賴之。 清泰中，自彭門移鎮平陽，加檢校太傅，從石敬瑭為北面兵馬副總管，仍屯兵雁門。 未幾，晉高祖建義，末帝詔以敬達為北面行營都招討使，仍使悉引部下兵圍太原，以定州節度使楊光遠副焉。 尋統兵三萬，營於晉安鄉。 末帝自六月繼有詔促令攻取，敬達設長城連柵、雲梯飛炮，使工者運其巧思，窮土木之力。 時督事者每有所構，則暴風大雨，平地水深數尺，而城柵崩墮，竟不能合其圍。 九月，契丹至，敬達大敗，尋為所圍。 晉高祖及蕃眾自晉安寨南門外，長百餘里，闊五十里，布以氈帳，用毛索掛鈴，而部伍多犬，以備警急。 營中嘗有夜遁者，出則犬吠鈴動，跬步不能行焉。 自是敬達與麾下部曲五萬人，馬萬匹，無由四奔，但見穹廬如崗阜相屬，諸軍相顧失色。 始則削木篩糞，以飼其馬，日望朝廷救軍，及漸羸死，則與將士分食之，馬盡食殫。 副將楊光遠、次將安審琦知不濟，勸敬達宜早降以求自安。 敬達曰：「吾受恩於明宗，位曆方鎮，主上授我大柄，而失律如此，已有愧於心也。 今救軍在近，旦暮雪恥有期，諸公何相迫耶！ 待勢窮，則請殺吾，攜首以降，亦未為晚。 」光遠、審琦知敬達意未決，恐坐成魚肉，遂斬敬達以降。 〈 （《契丹國志》：楊光遠謀害張敬達，諸將高行周陰為之備，敬達疏於防禦，推遠行周等。 清晨，光遠上謁，見敬達左右無人，遂殺之。）〉
Zhang Jingda, whose courtesy name was Zhitong, was a native of Daizhou; his childhood name was Raw Iron. His father Shen was known for courage. He served the Martial Emperor as a column commander and later as commissioner of the hall-direct guard, and died in service early in the Tongguang era. Jingda was famed in youth for mounted archery. The Zhuangzong knew of him and summoned him to succeed his father's post. He earned merit pacifying Henan and was promoted to honorary Minister of Works. When the Mingzong took the throne he served as commander of the Pingsheng guard and honorary Left Vice Director of the Masters of Writing. During the Changxing era he was made overall commander of the Hedong horse and foot armies, exceptionally promoted to honorary Defender-in-Chief, and given Qin Prefecture. In the third year he was made honorary Grand Guardian and military governor of Ying Prefecture. In the fourth year he was transferred to Yun Prefecture. The Khitan then led their tribes from Heiyulin and Kuolapo to Meiyuepo, grazing their herds on Han pasture. Jingda repeatedly massed troops at the passes to block them. The Khitan never dared drive their herds south again, and the border people owed him their security. During the Qingtai era he was transferred from Pengmen to Pingyang, made honorary Grand Tutor, and served under Shi Jingtang as deputy overall commander of the northern armies while garrisoning Yanmen. Before long the Jin Gaozu rebelled. The Last Emperor appointed Jingda overall commissioner for pacification of the northern field headquarters and ordered him to besiege Taiyuan with all his troops, with Yang Guangyuan, military governor of Ding Prefecture, as his deputy. Soon he commanded thirty thousand men and encamped at Jin'an. From the sixth month on the Last Emperor repeatedly ordered him to attack. Jingda built linked palisades, siege ladders, and catapults, employing every device of engineering. Whenever they built siege works, violent storms followed, flooding the plain several feet deep and collapsing walls and palisades until they could not complete the encirclement. In the ninth month the Khitan arrived. Jingda was routed and soon surrounded. The Jin Gaozu and the barbarian hosts spread south of the Jin'an camp for more than a hundred li in length and fifty li in width, their felt tents linked by wool ropes hung with bells, and their ranks full of dogs to give warning. When anyone in the camp tried to flee by night, the dogs barked and the bells rang, and he could not take a single step. Jingda and his fifty thousand men with ten thousand horses could flee nowhere. They saw only yurts stretching like hills, and the armies looked at one another in dismay. At first they fed their horses on shaved wood and sifted dung while awaiting relief from the court. As the horses weakened and died, officers and men shared the meat until every horse was eaten. Deputy Yang Guangyuan and second commander An Shenqi, seeing defeat was certain, urged Jingda to surrender early to save himself. Jingda said, "I owe my advancement to the Mingzong and have held frontier commands. The sovereign entrusted me with great authority, and to fail so badly already shames me. Relief is near, and we may yet redeem ourselves—why do you press me so! When all is lost, you may kill me and carry my head to surrender—that will be time enough. " Guangyuan and Shenqi saw that Jingda would not decide and feared they would be slaughtered like fish on a block, so they beheaded Jingda and surrendered. [ (From the 《Annals of the Khitan State》: Yang Guangyuan plotted to kill Zhang Jingda. General Gao Xingzhou secretly prepared against it, while Jingda was lax in defense and trusted Xingzhou and the others. At dawn Guangyuan came to pay his respects. Finding no one beside Jingda, he killed him.)〉
