萇從簡，陳州人也。 世以屠羊為業，力敵數人，善用槊。 初事後唐莊宗為小校，每遇攻城，召人為梯頭，從簡多應募焉，莊宗為其勇，擢領帳前親衛兼步軍都指揮使。 一日，莊宗領大軍與梁軍對陣，登高丘而坐，敵人有執大幟揚其武者，莊宗指之謂左右曰：「猛士也。 」從簡曰：「臣為大王取之。 」莊宗慮其不捷，不許。 從簡退，乃潛領十數騎挺身而入，奪幟以歸，萬眾鼓噪，莊宗壯之，錫賚甚厚。 又嘗中箭而鏃入於骨，使醫工出之，以刃鑿骨，恐其痛也，良久未能搖動。 從簡嗔目謂曰：「何不沈鑿？ 」洎出之，左右無不惻然，從簡顏色自若，其勇壯皆此類也。 從簡所為多不法，莊宗以其戰鬥多捷，常屈法赦之。 賜姓，名曰紹瓊。 後加竭誠匡國功臣，累官至金紫光祿大夫、檢校太保、景州刺史，歷洺州團練使。 及梁平，典蔡州。 同光四年，授許州節度使，會莊宗晏駕，未及赴鎮而止。 明宗登極，例復本姓，歷麟、汝、汾、金四州刺史。 〈 （《北夢瑣言》云：明宗尤惡貪貨，面戒汝州刺史萇從簡，為其貪暴。）〉 應順初，舉軍伐鳳翔，從簡亦預其行，會軍變，乃東還。 道遇張廷蘊，為廷蘊所執，送於末帝。 末帝數之曰：「人皆歸我，爾何背我而去也？ 」從簡曰：「事主不敢二心，今日死生惟命。 」末帝釋之。 清泰二年，授潁州團練使。 高祖舉義，末帝將議親征，詔赴闕，充副招討使，隨駕至孟津，除河陽節度使。 及趙延壽軍敗，斷浮橋歸洛，留從簡守河陽。 高祖自北而至，從簡察軍情離散，遂渡河迎謁高祖。 天福元年十二月，授許州節度使，改賜推忠佐運保國功臣。 二年秋，移鎮徐州。 三年，加開府儀同三司、檢校太尉，進封開國公，食邑至一千五百戶。 受代歸闕，授左金吾衛上將軍。
Chang Congjian was from Chenzhou. His family had butchered sheep for generations. He could match several men in strength and wielded the spear expertly. He first served Zhuangzong of Later Tang as a junior officer. In every siege the army called for ladder-team leaders, and Congjian often volunteered. Zhuangzong prized his courage and made him commander of the throne-guard escort and of all foot troops. One day Zhuangzong drew up his main force against Liang and sat on a high knoll. An enemy soldier was flourishing a great banner to show off his prowess. Zhuangzong pointed him out and told his attendants, "There's a fierce warrior. " Congjian said, "I will fetch him for you, Great King." " Zhuangzong feared he would fail and refused. Congjian withdrew, then slipped away with a dozen horsemen, charged in alone, seized the banner, and rode back. The army roared approval. Zhuangzong was thrilled and heaped rewards on him. He was once struck by an arrow whose head lodged in the bone. A surgeon was told to extract it by chiseling the bone with a blade. Fearing his pain, the man hesitated a long time and could not get it moving. Congjian glared and said, "Why not chisel hard? " When the head came out, everyone nearby winced in sympathy, but Congjian's face stayed calm. His bravery was always of this sort. Congjian broke the law often. Zhuangzong bent the rules and pardoned him because he won so many battles. He was granted the imperial surname and given the name Shaojiong. He was later made Meritorious Subject of Utmost Loyalty Who Upholds the State and rose through Grand Master of Splendid Honor with Golden Seal, Grand Guardian by inspection, prefect of Jing, and regimental commissioner of Mo. After Liang fell, he governed Cai. In Tongguang 4 he was made military governor of Xu, but Zhuangzong died before he could take up the post. When Mingzong took the throne, Congjian recovered his original surname and served as prefect of Lin, Ru, Fen, and Jin in turn. ( (Beimeng Suoyan says: Mingzong especially hated greed and personally warned Xu prefect Chang Congjian for his rapacity.) Early in Yingshun the army marched on Fengxiang. Congjian went along, but when the troops mutinied the force turned back east. On the march he met Zhang Tingyun, who seized him and sent him to the Last Emperor. The Last Emperor scolded him: "Everyone has come to me. Why did you abandon me? " Congjian said, "I serve my lord with undivided loyalty. My life or death is yours to decide." " The Last Emperor let him go. In Qingtai 2 he was made regimental commissioner of Ying. When Gaozu rose in revolt, the Last Emperor planned to campaign in person and summoned Congjian to court as deputy pacification commissioner. He accompanied the emperor to Mengjin and was made military governor of Heyang. When Zhao Yanshou's army was beaten, the floating bridge was cut and the force withdrew to Luoyang, leaving Congjian to hold Heyang. Gaozu arrived from the north. Seeing the army disintegrating, Congjian crossed the river to welcome him. In Tianfu 1, month 12, he was made military governor of Xu and granted the title Meritorious Subject Who Supports Loyalty and Aids Fortune in Protecting the State. In the second year, autumn, he was transferred to Xuzhou. In the third year he was made Grand Master of the Palace with independent office, Grand Preceptor by inspection, Duke Who Opens the State, with a fief of 1,500 households. Relieved of command, he returned to court and was made general-in-chief of the Left Golden Spear Guard.

從簡性忌刻而多疑，歷州鎮凡十餘，所在豎棘於公署，才通人行，左右稍違忤，即加鞭笞，或至殺害，其意不可測，吏人皆側行。 其煩苛暴虐，為武臣之最。 六年秋，隨駕幸鄴都，遇疾請告，尋卒於鄉里，年六十五。 贈太傅。
Congjian was jealous and suspicious. In more than ten prefectures and garrisons he planted thorn hedges in government offices barely wide enough to pass. The slightest offense from an attendant brought a flogging, sometimes death. No one could guess his mood; staff walked sideways. Among military governors his petty cruelty was unmatched. In the sixth year, autumn, he accompanied the emperor to the northern capital, fell ill, asked leave, and soon died at home at sixty-five. He was posthumously made Grand Tutor.
3
潘環，字楚奇，洛陽人也。 父景厚，以環貴，授左監門上將軍致仕。 環少以負販為業，始事梁邢州節度使閻寶，為帳中親校。 及莊宗定魏博，移兵攻邢，寶遣環間道馳奏於梁，梁末帝用為左堅銳夾馬都虞候，累遷左雄威指揮使。 時梁人與莊宗對壘於河上，環每預戰，先登陷敵，金瘡遍體。 〈 （《玉堂閑話》云：潘環常中流矢於面，骨銜其鏃，故負重傷。 醫療至經年，其鏃自出，其瘡成漏，終身不痊。）〉 莊宗知其名，及平梁，命典禁軍。 同光中，從明宗北禦契丹，鄴軍之亂，從明宗入洛。 天成初，授棣州刺史。 會定州王都反，朝廷攻之，以環為行營右廂步軍都指揮使。 賊平，改易州刺史、北面沿邊都部署，後移刺慶州。 受代歸闕，明宗召對，顧侍臣曰：「此人勇敢，少能偕者。 」尋除宿州團練使。 清泰中，移耀州。 天福中，預平範延光，授齊州防禦使。 四年，升金州為節鎮，以環為節度使，久之，入為左神武統軍。 開運初，契丹入寇，王師北征，以環為北面行營步軍左廂排陣使，預破契丹於陽城。 軍回，授澶州節度使，累官至檢校太傅。 三年，罷鎮歸闕，俄受詔洛京巡檢。 其年冬，戎王犯闕，偽署劉晞為西京留守，環乞罷巡警，閑居洛陽。 遇河陽軍亂，晞出奔，未幾，蕃將高牟翰以兵援晞入於洛，慮環有變，乃害之，盡取其家財。 〈 （《通鑒》云：晞疑環構其眾逐己，使牟翰殺之。）〉 漢高祖至京，贈太尉。
Pan Huan, courtesy name Chuji, was from Luoyang. His father Jinghou, owing to Huan's rank, was made Left Gate Guard general-in-chief and retired. As a youth Huan peddled for a living. He first served Liang's Xingzhou military governor Yan Bao as a personal attendant in headquarters. When Zhuangzong took Weibo and marched on Xing, Bao sent Huan by a secret route to urge Liang. The Last Emperor of Liang made him commander of the Left Firm Sharp Horse-Flanking Detachment and later commander of the Left Majestic Might. Liang and Zhuangzong faced off on the Yellow River. Huan fought in every clash, leading the charge; metal wounds covered his body. ( (Yutang Xianhua says: Pan Huan once took an arrow in the face; the bone gripped the head, leaving a grave wound. Treatment took nearly a year before the head worked free. The wound became a fistula and never fully healed.) Zhuangzong knew his name. When Liang fell, he put him in charge of the forbidden army. Under Tongguang he followed Mingzong north against the Khitan. In the Ye mutiny he followed Mingzong into Luoyang. Early in Tiancheng he was made prefect of Di. When Ding's Wang Du rebelled, Huan was made commander of the campaign headquarters' right-wing foot army. After the rebels fell he became Yi prefect and northern frontier deployment commander, then military governor of Qing. Recalled to court, Mingzong received him and told his ministers, "This man is brave. Few can equal him. " He was soon made regimental commissioner of Su. Under Qingtai he was moved to Yao. In Tianfu he helped crush Fan Yanguang and was made defender of Qi. In year 4 Jin was elevated to a military governorship and Huan was made its governor. Later he entered court as commander of the Left Divine Martial Army. Early in Kaiyun the Khitan invaded. Huan was made left-wing foot array marshal of the northern campaign and helped rout them at Yangcheng. On return he was made military governor of Cao and rose to Grand Preceptor by inspection. In year 3 he left his post and returned to court, then was ordered to patrol Luoyang. That winter the Khitan took the capital. Liu Xi was installed as western capital regent. Huan asked off patrol duty and lived quietly in Luoyang. Heyang mutinied and Xi fled. Soon the Khitan general Gao Mouhan entered Luoyang to support Xi. Fearing Huan might turn on them, they killed him and seized his family wealth. ( (Zizhi Tongjian says: Xi suspected Huan had stirred his troops to expel him and had Mouhan kill him.) When Gaozu of Han reached the capital, Huan was posthumously made Grand Preceptor.

環歷六部兩鎮，所至以聚斂為務。 在宿州時，有牙將因微過見怒，環紿言笞之，牙校因托一尼嘗熟於環者，獻白金兩鋌。 尼詣環白牙校餉𨫼腳兩枚，求免其責，環曰：「𨫼本幾腳？ 」尼曰：「三腳。 」環復曰：「今兩腳能成𨫼乎？ 」尼則以三數致之，當時號環為「潘𨫼腳」。
Huan held six prefectures and two military governorships and extorted wherever he went. At Su a junior officer angered him over a small matter. Huan pretended he would flog him. The man's deputy asked a nun who knew Huan well to offer two ingots of white gold. The nun said the deputy was offering two ingots of "foot-money" and begged mercy. Huan asked, "How many feet is foot-money? " The nun said, "Three feet." " Huan said, "Can two feet make foot-money? " She brought three. People nicknamed him "Pan Foot-Money."

方太，字伯宗，青州千乘人也。 少隸本軍為小校，嘗戍登州，劫海客，事泄，刺史淳於晏匿之，遇赦免。 事定州節度使楊光遠，光遠領兵赴晉陽。 本州軍亂，太與馬萬、盧順密等擒之，使太縛送至闕。 尋從杜重威破張從賓於汜水，以功除趙州刺史。 從楊光遠平範延光於鄴，移刺萊州，遷安州防禦使。 從少帝幸澶州，與契丹戰於戚城，中數創，改鳳州防禦使，行至中途，遷河陽留後，移邢州留後。 契丹犯闕，偽命遙領洋州節度使，充洛京巡檢，與前洺州團練使李瓊俱至鄭州，其屯駐兵士迫請太在城巡檢，以備外盜，號為「鄭王」。 時有嵩山賊帥張遇，領眾萬餘，於僧眾得梁朝故嗣密王朱乙，遂推為天子，取嵩山神冠冕之服以衣之。 張遇以其眾攻鄭州，太與李瓊擊之，賊眾敗走，瓊中流矢而死。 太乃括率郡中財物以賞軍士，因誘之欲同西去，其眾不從，太乃潛奔於洛陽。 〈 （《通鑒》云：戍兵既失太，反譖太於契丹，雲脅我為亂。 太遣子師朗自訴於契丹，契丹殺之。）〉 及劉晞南走許州，太殺晞牙校李暉，入河南府行留守事。 既而嵩山賊帥張遇殺嗣密王，傳首於太，懸於洛市。 又有伊闕賊帥自稱天子，領眾萬餘，將入洛城，集郊壇之上，太率兵數百人逆擊，破之，賊眾遂潰。 〈 （《通鑒考異》引實錄《方太傳》云：劉禧走許田，復有潁陽妖巫，姓朱，號嗣密王，誓眾於洛南天壇，號萬餘人。 太帥部曲與朝士輩虛張旗幟，一舉而逐之，洛師遂安。）〉 河陽武行德遣使召太，詐言欲推之為帥，尋為行德所害。
Fang Tai, courtesy name Bozong, was from Qiancheng in Qing. As a youth he served his home army as a junior officer. Garrisoned at Deng, he robbed seagoing merchants. When exposed, Prefect Chunyu Yan sheltered him until an amnesty. He served Ding military governor Yang Guangyuan, who marched troops to Jinyang. The prefecture mutinied. Tai with Ma Wan and Lu Shunmi seized Guangyuan and had Tai bind him for the capital. He followed Du Chongwei in defeating Zhang Congbin at Sishui and was made Zhao prefect for his merit. He followed Yang Guangyuan against Fan Yanguang at Ye, governed Lai, then became defender of An. He followed the Young Emperor to Cao, fought the Khitan at Weicheng, took several wounds, was made Feng defender, then Heyang regent en route, then Xing regent. When the Khitan took the capital they named him absentee Yang military governor and Luoyang patrol inspector. He and former Mo regimental commissioner Li Qiong reached Zheng. The garrison forced him to patrol against bandits and called him "King of Zheng." Song bandit chief Zhang Yu led ten thousand men. Among monks they found Zhu Yi, Liang's late heir Prince of Secret Succession, made him emperor, and dressed him in Mount Song ritual regalia. Zhang Yu attacked Zheng. Tai and Li Qiong repulsed him. The bandits fled; Qiong died of an arrow wound. Tai levied prefectural wealth to reward the troops and tried to lure them west. They refused. He slipped away to Luoyang. ( (Zizhi Tongjian says: Losing Tai, the garrison slandered him to the Khitan, claiming he had forced them into revolt. Tai sent his son Shilang to plead. The Khitan killed him.) When Liu Xi fled south to Xu, Tai killed his officer Li Hui and entered Henan to act as regent. Zhang Yu killed the false emperor and sent his head to Tai, who displayed it in the Luoyang market. Another Yique bandit styled himself emperor with ten thousand men and massed at the suburban altar to enter Luoyang. Tai led hundreds against them, routed them, and they scattered. ( (Zizhi Tongjian Kaoyi, citing the Veritable Records biography of Fang Tai, says: Liu Xi fled toward Xu. A Yingyang sorcerer surnamed Zhu styled himself heir Prince of Secret Succession and mustered ten thousand at Luoyang's southern altar. Tai led his men and courtiers with sham banners and drove them off in one stroke, securing Luoyang.) Heyang's Wu Xingde summoned Tai, pretending he would make him commander, then killed him.

何建，其先回鶻人也，代居雲、朔間。 祖慶，父懷福，俱事後唐武皇為小校。 建少以謹厚隸於高祖帳下，以掌廄為役，及即位，累典禁軍， 〈 （《九國志》云：重建初事晉祖為奉德馬軍都指揮使。）〉 遙領歡、睦二郡。 天福中，自曹州刺史遷延州兵馬留後，尋正授旄鉞。 〈 （《九國志》云：延州節度使丁審琪殘暴貪冒，蕃部苦之。 重建以所部兵攻其城，審琪遁去，晉祖即以重建權節度兵馬留後，下車諭以威福，邊民安堵，就加彰武軍節度使。）〉 數年之間，歷涇、鄧、貝、澶、孟五鎮節度使， 〈 （《九國志》云：皆以廉儉簡易稱。）〉 累官至檢校太傅。 開運三年，移鎮秦州。 是冬，契丹入汴，其主遣人賫詔以賜建，建憤然謂將吏曰：「吾事石氏二主，累擁戎旃，人臣之榮，亦已極矣。 今日不能率兵赴難，豈可受制於契丹乎！ 」即遣使賫表與其地送款於蜀，孟昶待之甚厚，偽加同平章事，依前秦州節度使。 〈 （《九國志》云：時固鎮與鳳州未平，重建悉經略討平之。）〉 歲餘，移閬州保寧軍節度使， 〈 （《九國志》云：昶大舉兵北伐，遣張虔釗出大散關，以重建為招討使，由隴州路以進師，無功而還。）〉 加偽官至中書令，後卒於蜀。
He Jian was of Uyghur descent; his family had lived between Yun and Shuo for generations. His grandfather Qing and father Huai Fu both served Later Tang's Martial Emperor as junior officers. In youth Jian's steady character won him a place in Gaozu's stables. After Gaozu took the throne he repeatedly commanded the forbidden army, ( (Jiuguo Zhi says: Early on he served the Jin founder as commander of the Fengde Horse Army.) He held Huan and Mu in absentia. In Tianfu he rose from Cao prefect to Yanzhou regent, then full military governor. ( (Jiuguo Zhi says: Yan military governor Ding Shenqi was brutal and greedy; frontier tribes suffered. Jian attacked his city; Shenqi fled. The Jin founder made Jian acting Yan regent. On taking office he lectured on authority and favor; the frontier settled, and he was made Zhangwu military governor.) Within a few years he governed Jing, Deng, Bei, Cao, and Meng in succession, ( (Jiuguo Zhi says: All were known for integrity, thrift, and simplicity.) He rose to Grand Preceptor by inspection. In Kaiyun 3 he was transferred to Qin. That winter the Khitan took Bian and sent an edict to Jian. He told his officers angrily, "I served both Shi emperors and held command banners many times—the honor of a subject could go no higher. Today I cannot march to save the dynasty. How can I submit to the Khitan? " He sent envoys with a memorial and his territory to Shu. Meng Chang received him generously, made him co-equal director of the Secretariat in name, and left him Qin military governor. ( (Jiuguo Zhi says: Guzhen and Feng were not yet pacified; Jian campaigned and pacified them.) After a year he was made Baoning Army military governor of Lang, ( (Jiuguo Zhi says: Chang launched a northern campaign and sent Zhang Qianzhao through Dasanguan, with Jian as pacification commissioner advancing by the Long route; they returned without success.) He was given false rank through Director of the Secretariat and later died in Shu.

When Du Chongwei surrendered to the enemy, Qiong was made Wei prefect. Reaching Zheng he met bandits attacking the city. With Fang Tai he fought them off but died of an arrow wound at sixty-five.

高漢筠，字時英，齊州歷山人也。 曾祖詣，嘗為是邑令，故家焉。 漢筠少好書傳，嘗詣長白山講肄，會唐末齊、魯交兵，梁氏方霸，乃擲筆謁焉，尋納於軍門。 未幾，出為衛州牙校。 唐天祐中，莊宗入魏，分兵諭其屬郡，時漢筠以利病說衛之牧守，俾送款於莊宗，以漢筠為功，尋移洺州都校。 其後改常山為北京，以漢筠為皇城使，加檢校兵部尚書、左驍衛將軍同正。 明宗即位，除成德軍節度副使，俄以荊門用軍，促詔漢筠移倅襄州，權知軍州事。 長興中，歷曹、亳二州刺史。 秩滿，加檢校司徒，行左金吾衛大將軍。 清泰末，高祖建義於河東，唐末帝遣晉昌節度使張敬達率師圍太原，委漢筠巡撫其郡。 及敬達遇害，節度副使田承肇率部兵攻漢筠於府署，漢筠乃啟關延承肇，謂曰：「仆與子俱承朝寄，而相迫何甚？ 」承肇曰：「我欲扶公為節度使。 」澤筠曰：「老夫耄矣，不敢首為亂階，死生系子籌之。 」承肇目左右令前，諸軍投刃於地，曰：「高金吾累朝宿德，不可枉殺。 」承肇以眾意難拒，遂謝云：「與公戲耳！ 」漢筠促騎以還。 高祖入洛，飛詔征之，遇諸途，乃入覲，尋遷左驍衛大將軍、內客省使。 天福三年正月，遘疾，終東京之私第，時年六十六。
Gao Hanjun, courtesy name Shiying, was from Lishan in Qi. His great-grandfather Yi had been magistrate of the district, so the family settled there. As a youth Hanjun loved learning and studied on Changbai Mountain. When Qi and Lu warred at the end of Tang he joined Liang's army. Soon he was a Weizhou junior officer. In Tang Tianyou Zhuangzong entered Wei. Hanjun persuaded Wei's governor to submit and was moved to chief officer of Mo. Changshan became the Northern Capital; Hanjun was imperial city commissioner, Minister of War by inspection, and Left Valiant Guard general. Mingzong made him Chengde deputy military governor, then acting Xiangzhou administrator when Jingmen needed troops. In Changxing he was Cao and Bo prefect in turn. When his term ended he was Grand Master of Ceremonies by inspection and acting Left Golden Spear general-in-chief. Late in Qingtai Gaozu rose in Hedong. The Last Emperor sent Zhang Jingda to besiege Taiyuan and left Hanjun to pacify the city. When Jingda was killed, deputy Tian Chengzhao attacked Hanjun at headquarters. Hanjun opened the gate and said, "We both serve the court—why press so hard?" " Chengzhao said, "I want to make you military governor." " Hanjun said, "I am old and will not start a rebellion—my life is in your hands." " Chengzhao signaled his men forward; they threw down their weapons and said, "Lord Gao is too venerable to kill." " Chengzhao apologized, "Only a jest!" " Hanjun rode back. Gaozu entered Luoyang and summoned him by express. He met Gaozu on the road, attended court, and became Left Valiant Guard general-in-chief and Inner Reception commissioner. In Tianfu 3, month 1, he fell ill and died at his home in the eastern capital at sixty-six.
15
漢筠性寬厚，儀容偉如也。 雖歷戎職，未嘗有非法之言出於口吻，多慕士大夫所為，復以清白自負。 在襄陽，有孽吏常課外獻白金二十鎰，漢筠曰：「非多納枿泬，則刻削阛鋏，吾有正俸，此何用焉！ 」因戒其主者不復然，其白金皆以狀上進，有詔嘉之。 及蒞濟陰，部民安之，四邑飯僧凡有萬八千人。 在亳州三年，歲以己俸百千代納逋租，斯亦近代之良二千石也。
Hanjun was generous and imposing in bearing. Though a soldier, he spoke no impropriety, admired scholars, and prized integrity. At Xiangyang a corrupt clerk offered twenty ingots of extra silver. Hanjun said, "I have my salary—what need of this?" " He forbade it and sent all the silver to court with a memorial; the emperor praised him. At Jiyin the people were at peace; he fed eighteen thousand monks in four districts. At Bo for three years he paid others' overdue rents from his salary—a model prefect of his age.

長子貞文，仕皇朝，為開封少尹，卒。
His eldest son Zhenwen served as Kaifeng vice magistrate and died.
17
彥韜出於軍旅，植性和厚，理綿州日，甚著綏懷之譽，故有賞典旌焉。 在濮陽，屬清泰末，群寇入郡，郡人大擾，彥韜率帳下百人，一呼破之，人皆感之。 但不能守廉養正，以終令譽。 長興中，罷密州赴闕，苞苴甚厚。 起甲第於洛陽，逾月而成，華堂廣廡，亞王公之家，見者嗤之。 故淹翔五郡，位不及廉察，抑有由也。
Yantao came from the army, was good-natured, and won fame soothing Mian—hence court rewards. At Puyang late in Qingtai, when bandits raided the prefecture he routed them with a hundred men at one shout. He could not keep his integrity to the end. In Changxing he left Mi for court laden with gifts. He built a Luoyang mansion in a month grand as a prince's house—and was mocked for it. He held five posts but never rose to inspector—for good reason.

李彥珣，邢州人也。 少為郡之牙吏。 唐天祐中，明宗鎮其地，彥珣素無檢節，因洽於左右，明宗即位，以為通事舍人。 嘗遣使東川，行至其境，其仆從為董璋所收，彥珣竄還，以失敬故也。 朝廷攻璋，詔授行營步軍都監。 彥珣素不孝於父母，在鄉絕其供饋，同列惡其鄙惡，旋出為外任。 清泰中，遷河陽行軍司馬，遇張從賓為亂，因朋助之，從賓敗，奔於魏州。 範延光既叛，署為步軍都監，委以守陴。 招討使楊光遠以彥珣見用，欲撓延光而誘彥珣，乃遣人就邢臺訪得其母，令於城下以招之。 彥珣識其母，發矢以斃之，見者傷之。 及隨延光出降，授坊州刺史。 近臣以彥珣之惡逆奏於高祖，高祖曰：「赦命已行，不可改也。 」遂令赴郡，後不知其所終焉。 〈 （《歐陽史》云：彥珣後以坐贓誅。）〉
Li Yanxun was from Xing. As a youth he was a prefectural clerk. When Mingzong governed Xing in Tang Tianyou, the unrestrained Yanxun curried favor and became Grand Secretariat courier when Mingzong took the throne. Sent to eastern Shu, his attendants were seized by Dong Zhang and he fled home in disgrace. When the court attacked Zhang he was made campaign foot army superintendent. He starved his parents and colleagues despised him; he was soon sent to posts outside court. Under Qingtai he was Heyang campaign secretary, aided Zhang Congbin's revolt, and fled to Wei when Congbin fell. When Fan Yanguang rebelled he was made foot army superintendent and entrusted with the walls. Yang Guangyuan, to undermine Fan Yanguang, found Yanxun's mother at Xing and had her call to him from below the wall. Yanxun recognized her and shot her dead—to the horror of onlookers. When Fan surrendered, Yanxun was made Fang prefect. Courtiers denounced Yanxun to Gaozu, who said, "The amnesty is already issued and cannot be revoked." " He was ordered to his post; his end is unknown. ( (Ouyang's History says: Yanxun was later executed for corruption.)
19
史臣曰：昔從簡從莊宗戰於河上，可謂勇矣，及其為末帝守於孟津，豈得為忠乎？ 忠既無聞，勇何足貴！ 潘環、方太，雖鹹負雄幹，而俱歿亂世，蓋方略不足以衛其身故也。 何建舉秦、隴之封，附巴、邛之俗，守方之寄，其若是乎！ 其餘皆儋珪析爵之流也，亦可以垂名於是矣。 秘瓊既覆董氏之族，旋為鄴帥所屠，何報應之速也！ 惟彥珣忍射其親，殆非人類，晉祖宥之不戮，蓋失刑之甚也。
The historian writes: Congjian was brave on the river under Zhuangzong, but holding Mengjin for the Last Emperor was not loyalty. Without loyalty, what is bravery worth? Pan Huan and Fang Tai were strong men but died in chaos—their strategy could not save them. He Jian took Qin and Long and went over to Ba and Qiong ways—is that how a frontier guardian should act? The rest were men who traded rank for favor, yet names remain. Mi Qiong destroyed the Dong clan and was slaughtered by the Ye commander—swift retribution! Only Yanxun shot his own mother—barely human. That Gaozu spared him was a grave failure of justice.
