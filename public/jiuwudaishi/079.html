1
天福五年春正月丁卯朔，帝御崇元殿受朝賀，仗衛如式。 降德音：「應天福三年終，公私債欠，一切除放。 」壬申，蜀人寇西鄙，群盜張達、任康等劫清水德鐵之城以應之。 癸酉，湖南奏，閩人殺王昶，夷其族，王延羲因民之欲而定之。 甲戌，遣宣徽使楊彥詢使於契丹。 辛巳，皇子開封尹、鄭王重貴加檢校太尉。 己丑，回鶻可汗仁美遣使貢良馬白玉，謝冊命也。 庚寅，以二王後前右贊善大夫、襲酅國公楊延壽為太子左諭德，三恪汝州襄城縣令、襲介國公宇文頡加食邑三千戶。 辛卯，升絳州為防禦州。 癸巳，以左神武統軍陸思鐸為右羽林統軍，以隴州防禦使何福進為右神武統軍。 甲午，太常少卿裴羽奏：「請追謚唐莊宗皇后劉氏為神閔敬皇后，明宗皇后曹氏請追謚為和武憲皇后，閔帝魯國夫人孔氏請追謚為閔哀皇后。 」從之。 丙申，河中節度使安審信奏：「軍校康從受、李崇、孫大裕、張崇、於千等以所部兵為亂，尋平之，死者五百人。」
In spring, the first month of Tianfu 5, on the first day of the month (day dingmao), the Emperor appeared at Chongyuan Hall to receive the New Year court congratulations, with guards and ceremonial escort as prescribed. An edict of grace declared: "As of the end of Tianfu 3, all public and private debts are hereby cancelled in full. " On day renshen, Shu forces raided the western frontier; the bandits Zhang Da and Ren Kang and others seized the fortress of Qingshui Detie in support of the raid. On day guiyou, Hunan reported that men of Min had killed Wang Chang and wiped out his clan, and that Wang Yanxi had taken power in response to popular demand. On day jiaxu, Palace Secretariat Commissioner Yang Yanxun was dispatched as envoy to the Khitan. On day xinsi, the imperial prince Chonggui, Mayor of Kaifeng and Prince of Zheng, was appointed additional Grand Guardian. On day jichou, the Uyghur qaghan Renmei sent tribute of fine horses and white jade to thank the court for his investiture. On day gengyin, Yang Yanshou, a scion of the two fallen dynasties who had served as Right Mentor and held the title Duke of Xi, was appointed Left Mentor of the Heir Apparent; Yuwen Hao, a third-generation descendant serving as magistrate of Xiangcheng in Ruzhou and holding the title Duke of Jie, received an additional fief of three thousand households. On day xinmao, Jiangzhou was elevated to defense-prefecture status. On day guisi, Lu Siduo, commander of the Left Divine Martial Army, was transferred to command the Right Forest of Feathers Army, and He Fujin, defense commissioner of Longzhou, was appointed commander of the Right Divine Martial Army. On day jiawu, Vice Minister of Rites Pei Yu memorialized: "We ask that Empress Liu, consort of Tang Zhuangzong, be given the posthumous title Shenmin Jing Empress; that Empress Cao, consort of Mingzong, be titled Hewu Xian Empress; and that Lady Kong of Lu, consort of Emperor Min, be titled Min'ai Empress." " The court approved. On day bingshen, Hedong military governor An Shenxin reported: "Officers Kang Congshou, Li Chong, Sun Dayu, Zhang Chong, Yu Qian, and others rose in mutiny with the troops under their command; the revolt was soon put down, leaving five hundred dead."
2
二月丁酉朔，沙州歸義軍節度使曹義金卒，贈太師，以其子元德襲其位。 乙巳，御史中丞竇貞固奏：「國忌日，宰臣跪爐焚香，文武百僚列坐。 竊惟禮例，有所未安。 今欲請宰臣仍舊跪爐，百僚依班序立。 」詔可之，仍令行香之後飯僧百人，永為定制。 庚戌，北京留守安彥威來朝，帝慰接甚厚，賜上樽酒。 壬子，升中書門下平章事為正二品。 丁巳，青州節度使、東平王王建立來朝。 己未，以中書門下侍郎為清望正三品，諫議大夫、御史中丞為清望正四品。
In the second month, on the first day (day dingyou), Cao Yijin, military governor of the Guiyi Army at Shazhou, died; he was posthumously honored as Grand Preceptor, and his son Yuande inherited the post. On day yisi, censor-in-chief Dou Zhengu memorialized: "On the state's day of mourning, chief ministers kneel at the censer to burn incense while all civil and military officials sit in rows. We believe this arrangement is at odds with proper ritual precedent. We ask that chief ministers continue to kneel at the censer as before, while the rest of the officials stand in rank order. " The edict approved the change and further ordered that after the incense ceremony a meal be provided for one hundred monks, establishing this as a permanent regulation. On day gengxu, Bejing garrison commander An Yanwei came to court; the Emperor received him with exceptional warmth and granted him wine from the imperial cup. On day renzi, the office of Grand Councilor of the Secretariat-Chancellery was raised to upper second rank. On day dingsi, Wang Jianli, military governor of Qingzhou and Prince of Dongping, came to court. On day jiwei, the vice minister of the Secretariat-Chancellery was classified as a prestigious upper third rank, and remonstrance officials and the censor-in-chief as prestigious upper fourth rank.
3
三月丁卯朔，右散騎常侍張允改禮部侍郎。 辛未，宋州歸德軍節度使、侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使劉知遠加特進，改鄴都留守、廣晉尹，典軍如故。 以兗州節度使李從溫為徐州節度使，以北京留守安彥威為宋州節度使。 壬申，詔朝臣覲省父母，依天成例頒賜茶藥。 癸酉，以青州節度使王建立為昭義軍節度使，進封韓王，仍割遼、沁二州為昭義屬郡，以建立本遼州人，用成其衣錦之美也。 以晉州節度使李德充為北京留守，以潞州節度使皇甫遇為晉州節度使。 是日，容州節度使馬存卒。 甲戌，以給事中李光廷為左散騎常侍，亳州團練使李式為給事中。 乙亥，相州節度使桑維翰加檢校司徒，改兗州節度使。 許州節度使杜重威改鄆州節度使，河中節度使安審信改許州節度使。 丁丑，長安公主出降駙馬都尉楊承祚。 戊寅，詔：「中書門下五品已上官於兩省上事，宰臣押角之禮； 及第舉人與主司選勝筵宴，及中書舍人靸鞋接見舉人； 兼兵部、禮部引人過堂之日幕次酒食會客，悉宜廢之。 」己卯，以前樞密使劉處讓為相州節度使。 辛巳，湖南遣牙將劉領兵大破溪峒群蠻，收溪、錦、獎三州。 丁亥，以秦州節度使康福為河中節度使，以徐州節度使侯益為秦州節度使。 庚寅，御明德樓，餞送昭義軍節度使王建立，賜玉斧、蜀馬。 甲午，詔吏部三銓，聽四時選擬官旋奏，不在團甲之限。
In the third month, on the first day (day dingmao), Right Regular Attendant Zhang Yun was appointed vice minister of rites. On day xinwei, Liu Zhiyuan, military governor of the Guide Army at Songzhou and commander-in-chief of the palace guard horse and foot forces, received the special advancement rank, was made garrison commander of Yedu and mayor of Guangjin, and retained command of the armies as before. Li Congwen, military governor of Yanzhou, was transferred to Xuzhou; An Yanwei, garrison commander of Bejing, was appointed military governor of Songzhou. On day renshen, an edict ordered that officials visiting their parents be granted tea and medicine according to the precedent of the Tiancheng era. On day guiyou, Wang Jianli, military governor of Qingzhou, was appointed military governor of the Zhaoyi Army and advanced to Prince of Han; the prefectures of Liao and Qin were detached as subordinate districts of Zhaoyi, since Jianli was a native of Liaozhou—a gesture to let him return home in glory. Li Dechong, military governor of Jinzhou, was made garrison commander of Bejing; Huangfu Yu, military governor of Luzhou, was transferred to Jinzhou. That same day, Ma Cun, military governor of Rongzhou, died. On day jiaxu, supervising secretary Li Guangting was appointed left regular attendant, and Li Shi, training commissioner of Bozhou, was made supervising secretary. On day yihai, Sang Weihan, military governor of Xiangzhou, was appointed additional Minister of Education and transferred to Yanzhou. Du Chongwei, military governor of Xuzhou, was transferred to Yanzhou; An Shenxin, military governor of Hedong, was transferred to Xuzhou. On day dingchou, Princess Chang'an was married to Commandant of the Horse Yang Chengzuo. On day wuyin, an edict declared: "The ceremony in which chief ministers press the corner when Secretariat-Chancellery officials of the fifth rank and above report business to the two departments; victory banquets held by successful candidates and the chief examiner, and secretariat drafters receiving candidates in informal dress; as well as tent-side feasts when the Ministries of War and Rites lead candidates through the examination hall—all of these are to be abolished." " On day jimao, former commissioner of military affairs Liu Churang was appointed military governor of Xiangzhou. On day xinsi, Hunan sent adjutant Liu at the head of troops to defeat the stream-and-cave tribes and recover the prefectures of Xi, Jin, and Jiang. On day dinghai, Kang Fu, military governor of Qinzhou, was transferred to Hedong; Hou Yi, military governor of Xuzhou, was appointed to Qinzhou. On day gengyin, the Emperor appeared at Mingde Tower to give a farewell feast for Wang Jianli, military governor of the Zhaoyi Army, and granted him a jade axe and a Shu horse. On day jiawu, an edict allowed the three selection boards of the Ministry of Personnel to nominate and report appointments throughout the year, without being bound by the group-and-cycle schedule.
4
夏四月丙申朔，宴群臣於永福殿。 戊戌，曹州防禦使石暉卒，帝之從弟也。 禮官奏：「天子為五服之內親本服周者，三哭而止。 」從之。 己亥，罷洛陽、京兆進苑囿瓜果，憫勞人也。 壬寅，右僕射致仕裴皞卒，贈太子太保。 丙午，詔曰：「承旨者，承時君之旨。 非近侍重臣，無以稟朕旨，宣予言。 是以大朝會宰臣承旨，草制詔學士承旨，若無區別，何表等威？ 除翰林承旨外，殿前承旨宜改為殿直； 密院承旨宜改為承宣； 御史臺、三司、閣門、客省所有承旨，並令別定其名。 」庚戌，以滄州節度使馬全節為安州節度使。 禮部侍郎張允奏，請廢明經、童子科。 從之。 因詔宏詞、拔萃、明算、道舉、百篇等科並停之。
In summer, the fourth month, on the first day (day bingshen), the Emperor feasted the ministers at Yongfu Hall. On day wuxu, Shi Hui, defense commissioner of Caozhou, died; he was a younger cousin of the Emperor. Ritual officials memorialized: "When the Son of Heaven mourns a relative within the five degrees of relationship who observes the one-year mourning garment for a full cycle, he should wail three times and stop. " The court approved. On day jihai, the tribute of garden fruits from Luoyang and Jingzhao was abolished out of compassion for the laborers. On day renyin, Pei Hao, retired right vice director, died; he was posthumously honored as Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent. On day bingwu, an edict declared: "The title chengzhi means to carry out the ruler's intent of the moment. Only close attendants and senior ministers can receive Our intent and proclaim Our words. At great court assemblies chief ministers carry the imperial intent, and Hanlin drafting academicians carry it for edicts and proclamations; without distinction, how can rank and authority be displayed? Apart from the Hanlin chengzhi, the Palace Front chengzhi should be renamed dianshi; the Privy Council chengzhi should be renamed chengxuan; and all chengzhi posts in the Censorate, the Three Departments, the Gate Office, and the Reception Office are to be given distinct new titles. " On day gengxu, Ma Quanjie, military governor of Cangzhou, was appointed military governor of Anzhou. Vice Minister of Rites Zhang Yun memorialized requesting abolition of the Classicist and Child Prodigy examination categories. The court approved. An edict thereupon also suspended the Hongci, Bacui, Ming suan, Dao ju, Hundred Pieces, and related categories.
5
五月癸酉，宋州貢瑞麥兩歧。 甲申，以前徐州節度使萇從簡為右金吾衛上將軍。 丙戌，安州節度使李金全叛，詔新授安州節度使馬全節以洛、汴、汝、鄭、單、宋、陳、蔡、曹、濮十州之兵討之。 以前鄜州節度使安審暉為副，以內客省使李守貞為都監，仍遣供奉官劉彥瑤奉詔以諭金全。 命麾下齊謙以詔送於淮夷，雲夢人齊峴斬謙，歸其詔於闕。 辛卯，昭義節度使韓王王建立薨，輟朝二日，贈尚書令。 壬寅， 〈 （壬寅上疑脫「六月」二字。）〉 少府監致仕尹玉羽卒。 癸卯，淮南使李承裕代李金全，金全南走，承裕以淮兵二千守其城。 甲辰，馬全節自應山縣進軍於大化鎮。 戊申，與鄂州賊軍陣於安陸之南，三戰而後克之，斬首三千級，生擒千餘人。 供奉官安友謙登鋒力戰，奮不顧身。 全節賞其忠勇，使馳獻捷書，暍死於路。 是日，削奪李金全官爵。 丁巳，淮夷偽校李承裕率眾掠城中資貨而遁，馬全節入城撫其遺民，遣安審暉率兵以逐承裕，擒而斬之。 執其偽都監杜光鄴，及淮南軍五百餘人，露布獻於闕下。 帝曰：此輩何罪，皆厚給放還。 癸亥，道士崇真大師張薦明賜號通元先生。 是時帝好《道德經》，嘗召薦明講說其義，帝悅，故有是命。 尋令薦明以《道》、《德》二經雕上印版，命學士和凝別撰新序，冠於卷首，俾頒行天下。
In the fifth month, on day guiyou, Songzhou presented auspicious double-eared wheat. On day jiashen, Chang Congjian, former military governor of Xuzhou, was appointed senior general of the Right Golden Crow Guard. On day bingxu, Li Jinquan, military governor of Anzhou, rebelled; an edict ordered the newly appointed Ma Quanjie to campaign against him with forces from the ten prefectures of Luo, Bian, Ru, Zheng, Shan, Song, Chen, Cai, Cao, and Pu. Former Fuzhou military governor An Shenhui was named deputy; palace reception commissioner Li Shouzhen was made overall supervisor; and palace service officer Liu Yanyao was sent with an edict to summon Jinquan to obedience. Jinquan ordered his officer Qi Qian to deliver the edict to the Huai forces; Qi Xian of Yunmeng killed Qian and sent the edict back to the court. On day xinmao, Wang Jianli, Prince of Han and military governor of the Zhaoyi Army, died; court mourning lasted two days, and he was posthumously honored as Director of the Imperial Secretariat. On day renyin, ( (The two characters "sixth month" are suspected to be missing before the entry for day renyin.)) Yin Yuyu, retired director of the Palace Workshops, died. On day guimao, Huainan envoy Li Chengyu replaced Li Jinquan; Jinquan fled south, and Chengyu garrisoned the city with two thousand Huai troops. On day jiachen, Ma Quanjie advanced from Yingshan County to Dahuazhen. On day wushen, he engaged the Ezhou rebel army south of Anlu; after three battles he took the position, beheading three thousand and capturing more than a thousand alive. Palace service officer An Youqian charged the front line and fought with reckless courage. Quanjie rewarded his loyalty and valor and sent him racing to deliver the victory report; he died of heatstroke on the way. That day, Li Jinquan's offices and titles were stripped. On day dingsi, the Huainan officer Li Chengyu led his men to plunder the city's goods and flee; Ma Quanjie entered the city to reassure the remaining inhabitants and sent An Shenhui in pursuit; Chengyu was captured and executed. His deputy supervisor Du Guangye and more than five hundred Huainan troops were taken prisoner, and a victory bulletin was presented at court. The Emperor said, "What crime have these men committed?" They were all generously provided for and sent home. On day guihai, the Daoist master Chongzhen Zhang Jianming was granted the honorific Tongyuan Xiansheng. The Emperor had taken a keen interest in the Daodejing, had summoned Jianming to expound it, was pleased with his teaching, and therefore issued this honor. Soon Jianming was ordered to carve printing blocks of the Dao and De sections; academician He Ning was commanded to write a new preface to stand at the head of the work, and the edition was promulgated throughout the realm.
6
秋七月甲子朔，降安州為防禦使額，以申州隸許州。 丙寅，安州節度使馬全節加檢校太尉，改昭義軍節度使。 前鄜州節度使安審暉加檢校太傅，為威勝軍節度使。 丁卯，湖南奏：遣天策府步騎將張少敵領兵五萬，樓船百艘，次於嶽陽，將進討淮夷也。 甲戌，宣徽使楊彥詢加檢校太傅，充安國軍節度使。 乙亥，戶部尚書致仕鄭韜光卒，贈右僕射。 戊寅，福州王延羲遣商人間路貢表自述。 戊子，宿州奏，淮東鎮移牒云：本國奏書於上國皇帝，曰：「久增景慕，莫會光塵，但循戰國之規，敢預睦鄰之道。 一昨安州有故，脫難相歸，邊校貪功，乘便據壘。 矧機宜之孰在，顧茫昧以難申。 否臧皆兇，乃大《易》之明義； 進取不止，亦聖人之厚顏。 適屬暑雨稍頻，江波甚漲，指揮未到，事實已違。 今者猥沐睿咨，曲形宸旨，歸其俘獲，示以英仁。 其如軍法朝章，彼此不可； 揚名建德，曲直相懸。 雖認好生，匪敢聞命。 其杜光鄴等五百七人，已令卻過淮北。 」帝復書曰：「昨者災生安陸，釁接漢陽，當三伏之炎蒸，動兩朝之師旅。 豈期邊帥，不稟上謀，洎復城池，備知本末。 尋已舍諸俘執，還彼鄉閭，不惟念效命之人，兼亦敦善鄰之道。 今承來旨，將正朝章，希循宥罪之文，用廣崇仁之美。 其杜光鄴等再令歸復。 」尋遣使押光鄴等於桐墟渡淮。 淮中有棹船，甲士拒之，南去不果。 詔光鄴等歸京師，授以職秩，其戎士五百人，立為顯義都。
In autumn, the seventh month, on the first day (day jiazi), Anzhou was reduced to defense-commissioner status, and Shenzhou was placed under Xuzhou. On day bingyin, Ma Quanjie, military governor of Anzhou, was appointed additional Grand Guardian and transferred to the Zhaoyi Army. Former Fuzhou military governor An Shenhui was appointed additional Grand Tutor and made military governor of the Weisheng Army. On day dingmao, Hunan reported that it had dispatched Tian Ce Fu general Zhang Shaodi with fifty thousand troops and one hundred tower ships, encamped at Yueyang, preparing to campaign against Huainan. On day jiaxu, palace secretariat commissioner Yang Yanxun was appointed additional Grand Tutor and military governor of the Anguo Army. On day yihai, Zheng Taoguang, retired minister of revenue, died; he was posthumously honored as right vice director. On day wuyin, Wang Yanxi of Fuzhou sent merchants by a secret route to present a memorial accounting for himself. On day wuzi, Suzhou reported that Huai East had sent a dispatch stating: "Our state presents a memorial to the emperor of the superior state, saying: 'We have long admired you from afar but have never met face to face; we follow only the customs of the Warring States era, yet dare to seek the path of good neighborly relations. Recently, when trouble arose at Anzhou, one of our men escaped hardship and returned home; a border officer, greedy for merit, seized the opportunity to occupy the fortress. Moreover, who held authority over the proper course of action remains unclear; we look back in confusion and find it hard to explain. Whether right or wrong, all is ill-omened—this is the clear teaching of the Changes; and to press forward without cease is also the shamelessness of which the sages speak. It happened that summer rains were frequent and the river ran high; orders had not yet arrived, but events had already gone awry. Now we have undeservedly received your wise inquiry; your gracious intent has been made clear, and captives have been returned, displaying heroic benevolence. Yet military law and court regulations differ on either side, and this cannot be done; to make a name by establishing virtue when right and wrong stand so far apart. Although we acknowledge your love of life, we dare not accept the command. The five hundred seven men including Du Guangye have already been ordered to cross back north of the Huai. " The Emperor replied: "Recently disaster arose at Anlu and trouble touched Hanyang; in the sweltering heat of midsummer, the armies of both courts were set in motion. Who could have expected border commanders to act without awaiting superior strategy? When the city was recovered, the full sequence of events became clear. Captives were soon released and sent back to their home villages—not only out of regard for those who had served faithfully, but also to uphold the way of good neighborly relations. Now that we have received your instructions, we shall rectify court regulations; we hope to follow the text on pardoning guilt and extend the beauty of exalted benevolence. Du Guangye and the others are again ordered to return home. " Envoys were soon sent to escort Guangye and the others across the Huai at Tongxu. Paddle-boats on the Huai barred their passage; armored soldiers turned them back, and they could not proceed south. An edict ordered Guangye and the others back to the capital and granted them offices and ranks; their five hundred soldiers were organized as the Xianyi Command.

On day xinmao, Zhao Ying, chief minister and supervising compiler of the national history, memorialized: "In accordance with the edict appointing Zhang Zhaoyuan and five others to compile the History of Tang, diarist Jia Wei has left office in mourning; we request that Vice Minister of Punishments Lu Qi and attending censor Yin Zhuo join the compilation." " He also memorialized: "The Tang Veritable Records missing from the History Office should be sought by imperial order." " Both requests were approved. ( (Wudai Huiyao: Supervising compiler Zhao Ying memorialized: "Since the disorders of the Tang, nearly fifty years have passed; the realm has been in turmoil and both capitals have fallen; of the libraries of today, scarcely two or three parts in a hundred remain. We have recently received the imperial command to compile and write. Praise and blame may follow new judgments, but compilation must rely on old records; with the source texts missing, we first fear omissions. We therefore request an edict ordering the purchase and search of the Tang Veritable Records missing from the History Office. Moreover, in the Xiantong era chief ministers Wei Baoheng, Jiang Shen, and Huangfu Huan compiled the Veritable Records of the reigns of Wuzong and Xuanzong; all encountered turmoil and displacement; although compilation was reported, none has circulated. Wei Baoheng and Pei Zan surely have descendants now in office, or former students and subordinates who took part in the compilation; hearing of this inquiry, they will surely be pleased. We request that an edict be sent to the three capitals, all circuits, and officials within and without: whoever presents these Veritable Records at court shall, according to civil or military merit and without regard to rank or background, be granted an office. If the scrolls are incomplete, rewards proportional to the number presented are also requested, to encourage others to come forward. From Huichang to Tianyou spans nearly sixty years; at the beginning Li Deyu pacified Shangdang and wrote the account of Wuzong's campaign against rebellion; Later Kang Chengxun pacified Xufang, and there is a full chronicle of the Wuning campaign. Accounts of this kind are quite numerous. We request that civil and military officials within and without, and renowned scholars and veteran learned men, be notified: whoever within these sixty years has compiled biographies, secretariat, Silver Terrace, or History Office calendars, edicts, investiture documents, or the like, without limit on years covered, may present them at court. If the years covered are extensive and the records are detailed and complete, we request special promotion without regard to rank or seniority. The History of Tang that I am compiling with Zhang Zhaoyuan and others uses basic annals to outline the imperial enterprise, biographies to narrate meritorious ministers, and ten treatises to record law and government. The regulations presented are requested to be transmitted to the relevant offices. " The requests were approved.)) On day renyin, Lu Jia, vice director of revenue and drafter of edicts, was appointed director of the parks bureau and drafter of edicts; Chen Yuan, deputy military governor of the Zhaoyi Army, was made retired director of the imperial banquets. On day yisi, the people of Qi and Lu suffered famine; an edict ordered Yan, Yun, and Qing to open the granaries for relief.
15
五月庚申朔，以前邢州節度使丁審琪為延州節度使，延州節度使劉景嚴為邠州節度使。 故皇子杲冊贈太尉，進封陳王。 庚午，涇州奏，雨雹，川水大溢，壞州郡鎮戍二十四城。 甲戌，北京遣牙將劉從以吐渾大首領白承福、念龐裏、赫連功德來朝。 邢州上言，吐渾移族帳於鎮州封部。
In the fifth month, on the first day (day gengshen), Ding Shenqi, former military governor of Xingzhou, was appointed military governor of Yanzhou; Liu Jingyan, military governor of Yanzhou, was transferred to Binzhou. The late imperial prince Gao was posthumously honored as Grand Guardian by investiture document and advanced to Prince of Chen. On day gengwu, Jingzhou reported rain and hail; rivers overflowed and destroyed twenty-four cities, commanderies, garrisons, and outposts. On day jiaxu, Bejing sent adjutant Liu Cong to court with the great Tuyuhun chieftains Bai Chengfu, Nian Pangli, and Helian Gongde. Xingzhou reported that the Tuyuhun had moved their tribal encampments into the jurisdiction of Zhenzhou.
16
六月丙申，以前衛尉卿趙延乂為司天監。 丁酉，詔：「今後藩侯郡守，凡有善政，委倅貳官條件聞奏，百姓官吏等不得遠詣京闕。 」壬寅，右領衛上將軍李頃卒，贈太師。 甲辰，迦葉彌陀國僧唵室哩以佛牙泛海而至。 丙午，高麗國王王建加開府儀同三司、檢校太師，食邑一萬戶。 戊午，鎮州節度使安重榮執契丹使拽剌，遣輕騎掠幽州南境之民，處於博野。 仍貢表及馳書天下，述契丹援天子父事之禮，貪傲無厭，困耗中國，已繕治甲兵，將與決戰。 帝發諭而止之。 重榮跋扈愈甚，由是與襄州節度使安從進潛相構謀為不軌。
In the sixth month on day bingshen, Zhao Yanyi, former director of the palace guard, was appointed director of the Imperial Astronomy Bureau. On day dingyou, an edict declared: "From now on, when frontier lords and prefects have good policies, deputy officials are to report the particulars by memorial; common people and officials may not travel to the capital to petition in person. " On day renyin, Li Qing, senior general of the Right Collar Guard, died; he was posthumously honored as Grand Preceptor. On day jiachen, the monk Anshili of Kasyapa Amitabha came across the sea bearing a Buddha tooth. On day bingwu, Wang Jian, king of Goryeo, was appointed opening-the-government with equipage equal to the three departments and acting grand preceptor, with a fief of ten thousand households. On day wuwu, An Chongrong, military governor of Zhenzhou, seized the Khitan envoy Zhuaila, sent light cavalry to plunder the people of southern Youzhou, and settled them at Boye. He also presented a memorial and dispatched letters throughout the realm, describing how the Khitan, while claiming to support the Son of Heaven as a father, were greedy, arrogant, and insatiable, draining China; he had already repaired armor and weapons and was preparing for decisive battle. The Emperor issued instructions ordering him to stop. Chongrong grew ever more overbearing; he and An Congjin, military governor of Xiangzhou, thereupon secretly plotted treason together.
