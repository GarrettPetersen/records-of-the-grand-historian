1
天福五年春正月丁卯朔，帝御崇元殿受朝賀，仗衛如式。 降德音：「應天福三年終，公私債欠，一切除放。 」壬申，蜀人寇西鄙，群盜張達、任康等劫清水德鐵之城以應之。 癸酉，湖南奏，閩人殺王昶，夷其族，王延羲因民之欲而定之。 甲戌，遣宣徽使楊彥詢使於契丹。 辛巳，皇子開封尹、鄭王重貴加檢校太尉。 己丑，回鶻可汗仁美遣使貢良馬白玉，謝冊命也。 庚寅，以二王後前右贊善大夫、襲酅國公楊延壽為太子左諭德，三恪汝州襄城縣令、襲介國公宇文頡加食邑三千戶。 辛卯，升絳州為防禦州。 癸巳，以左神武統軍陸思鐸為右羽林統軍，以隴州防禦使何福進為右神武統軍。 甲午，太常少卿裴羽奏：「請追謚唐莊宗皇后劉氏為神閔敬皇后，明宗皇后曹氏請追謚為和武憲皇后，閔帝魯國夫人孔氏請追謚為閔哀皇后。 」從之。 丙申，河中節度使安審信奏：「軍校康從受、李崇、孫大裕、張崇、於千等以所部兵為亂，尋平之，死者五百人。」
On the first day of the first month of spring in the fifth year of Tianfu, the Emperor took audience in the Chongyuan Hall to receive congratulations of the court; guards and escorts were arrayed according to ritual. The Emperor issued a beneficent edict: "As of the close of the third year of Tianfu, all public and private debts are hereby remitted in full." On renshen, Shu forces raided the western marches; the bandit leaders Zhang Da, Ren Kang, and others seized Qingshui and the fortified city of Detie in support of the invasion. On guiyou, Hunan reported that the people of Min had killed Wang Chang and wiped out his clan, and that Wang Yanxi had secured the throne in response to popular demand. On jiaxu, Yang Yanxun, Commissioner of the Privy Commissionerate, was sent as envoy to the Khitan. On xinsi, the imperial prince Chonggui, Mayor of Kaifeng and Prince of Zheng, was appointed honorary Grand Guardian. On jichou, the Uyghur qaghan Renmei sent tribute of fine horses and white jade to thank the court for his investiture. On gengyin, Yang Yanshou, former Right Mentor to the Heir Apparent of the two former dynasties and hereditary Duke of Xi, was appointed Left Mentor to the Heir Apparent; Yuwen Hao, Magistrate of Xiangcheng in Ruzhou and hereditary Duke of Jie of the third rank of reverence, received an additional three thousand households in his fief. On xinmao, Jiang Prefecture was elevated to defense-command status. On guisi, Lu Siduo, commander of the Left Divine Martial Army, was appointed commander of the Right Forest Army; He Fujin, defense commissioner of Long Prefecture, was appointed commander of the Right Divine Martial Army. On jiawu, Pei Yu, Vice Minister of the Imperial Ancestral Temple, memorialized: "We ask that Tang Zhuangzong's empress Liu be given the posthumous title Reverent and Respectful Divine Empress, Mingzong's empress Cao the title Harmonious Martial and Exemplary Empress, and Min Emperor's Lady of Lu the title Lamented Min Empress." The court approved the memorial. On bingshen, An Shenxin, military governor of Hezhong, reported: "The army officers Kang Congshou, Li Chong, Sun Dayu, Zhang Chong, Yu Qian, and others mutinied with the troops under their command; the revolt was soon put down, with five hundred dead."
2
二月丁酉朔，沙州歸義軍節度使曹義金卒，贈太師，以其子元德襲其位。 乙巳，御史中丞竇貞固奏：「國忌日，宰臣跪爐焚香，文武百僚列坐。 竊惟禮例，有所未安。 今欲請宰臣仍舊跪爐，百僚依班序立。 」詔可之，仍令行香之後飯僧百人，永為定制。 庚戌，北京留守安彥威來朝，帝慰接甚厚，賜上樽酒。 壬子，升中書門下平章事為正二品。 丁巳，青州節度使、東平王王建立來朝。 己未，以中書門下侍郎為清望正三品，諫議大夫、御史中丞為清望正四品。
On the first day of the second month, Cao Yijin, military governor of the Guiyi Army in Shazhou, died; he was posthumously honored as Grand Preceptor, and his son Yuande succeeded to his position. On yisi, Dou Zhengu, Censor-in-Chief, memorialized: "On the national day of mourning, the chief ministers kneel at the censer to burn incense while all civil and military officials sit in rows." I believe the established ritual in this regard is unsatisfactory. I propose that the chief ministers continue to kneel at the censer as before, while the other officials stand in rank order. The Emperor approved the proposal and ordered that after the incense ceremony a hundred monks be fed—a rule made permanent. On gengxu, An Yanwei, military commissioner of the Northern Capital, came to court; the Emperor received him with exceptional warmth and granted wine from the imperial cup. On renzi, the office of Grand Councilor of the Secretariat-Chancellery was elevated to the second positive rank. On dingsi, Wang Jianli, military governor of Qingzhou and Prince of Dongping, came to court. On jiwei, the Vice Minister of the Secretariat-Chancellery was classified as a prestigious third positive rank, and the Remonstrance Counselor and Censor-in-Chief as prestigious fourth positive ranks.
3
三月丁卯朔，右散騎常侍張允改禮部侍郎。 辛未，宋州歸德軍節度使、侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使劉知遠加特進，改鄴都留守、廣晉尹，典軍如故。 以兗州節度使李從溫為徐州節度使，以北京留守安彥威為宋州節度使。 壬申，詔朝臣覲省父母，依天成例頒賜茶藥。 癸酉，以青州節度使王建立為昭義軍節度使，進封韓王，仍割遼、沁二州為昭義屬郡，以建立本遼州人，用成其衣錦之美也。 以晉州節度使李德充為北京留守，以潞州節度使皇甫遇為晉州節度使。 是日，容州節度使馬存卒。 甲戌，以給事中李光廷為左散騎常侍，亳州團練使李式為給事中。 乙亥，相州節度使桑維翰加檢校司徒，改兗州節度使。 許州節度使杜重威改鄆州節度使，河中節度使安審信改許州節度使。 丁丑，長安公主出降駙馬都尉楊承祚。 戊寅，詔：「中書門下五品已上官於兩省上事，宰臣押角之禮； 及第舉人與主司選勝筵宴，及中書舍人靸鞋接見舉人； 兼兵部、禮部引人過堂之日幕次酒食會客，悉宜廢之。 」己卯，以前樞密使劉處讓為相州節度使。 辛巳，湖南遣牙將劉領兵大破溪峒群蠻，收溪、錦、獎三州。 丁亥，以秦州節度使康福為河中節度使，以徐州節度使侯益為秦州節度使。 庚寅，御明德樓，餞送昭義軍節度使王建立，賜玉斧、蜀馬。 甲午，詔吏部三銓，聽四時選擬官旋奏，不在團甲之限。
On the first day of the third month, Zhang Yun, Regular Attendant of the Right Cavalier, was appointed Vice Minister of Rites. On xinwei, Liu Zhiyuan, military governor of the Guide Army in Songzhou and commander-in-chief of the Palace Guard, received the honorary title Exceptional Promotion, was appointed military commissioner of Yedu and mayor of Guangjin, and retained command of the armies. Li Congwen, military governor of Yan Prefecture, was appointed military governor of Xu Prefecture; An Yanwei, military commissioner of the Northern Capital, was appointed military governor of Song Prefecture. On renshen, an edict directed that court officials visiting their parents be granted tea and medicine according to Tiancheng-era precedent. On guiyou, Wang Jianli, military governor of Qing Prefecture, was appointed military governor of the Zhaoyi Army and promoted to Prince of Han; Liao and Qin prefectures were assigned to Zhaoyi, since Jianli was a native of Liao—thus honoring him with the glory of returning home in splendor. Li Dechong, military governor of Jin Prefecture, was appointed military commissioner of the Northern Capital; Huangfu Yu, military governor of Lu Prefecture, was appointed military governor of Jin Prefecture. That same day, Ma Cun, military governor of Rong Prefecture, died. On jiaxu, Li Guangting, Supervising Secretary, was appointed Regular Attendant of the Left Cavalier; Li Shi, training commissioner of Bo Prefecture, was appointed Supervising Secretary. On yihai, Sang Weihan, military governor of Xiang Prefecture, was appointed honorary Minister of Education and transferred to military governor of Yan Prefecture. Du Chongwei, military governor of Xu Prefecture, was transferred to Yun Prefecture; An Shenxin, military governor of Hezhong, was transferred to Xu Prefecture. On dingchou, the Princess of Chang'an was married to Yang Chengzuo, Commander of the Imperial Sons-in-Law. On wuyin, an edict declared: "For Secretariat-Chancellery officials of the fifth rank and above presenting business to the two departments, the ritual of chief ministers countersigning at the corner; victory banquets of successful candidates with the chief examiner; Secretariat drafters receiving candidates in informal dress; and tent-side feasts on the days when the Ministries of War and Rites lead candidates through the examination hall—all are to be abolished." On jimao, Liu Churang, former Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs, was appointed military governor of Xiang Prefecture. On xinsi, Hunan sent the military adjutant Liu at the head of troops to rout the stream-gorge tribes and recover Xi, Jin, and Jiang prefectures. On dinghai, Kang Fu, military governor of Qin Prefecture, was appointed military governor of Hezhong; Hou Yi, military governor of Xu Prefecture, was appointed military governor of Qin Prefecture. On gengyin, the Emperor ascended the Mingde Tower to give a farewell feast for Wang Jianli, military governor of the Zhaoyi Army, and granted him a jade axe and a Shu horse. On jiawu, an edict directed the Ministry of Personnel's three selection boards to accept nominations throughout the year and report promptly, without restriction to the group-rotation schedule.
4
夏四月丙申朔，宴群臣於永福殿。 戊戌，曹州防禦使石暉卒，帝之從弟也。 禮官奏：「天子為五服之內親本服周者，三哭而止。 」從之。 己亥，罷洛陽、京兆進苑囿瓜果，憫勞人也。 壬寅，右僕射致仕裴皞卒，贈太子太保。 丙午，詔曰：「承旨者，承時君之旨。 非近侍重臣，無以稟朕旨，宣予言。 是以大朝會宰臣承旨，草制詔學士承旨，若無區別，何表等威？ 除翰林承旨外，殿前承旨宜改為殿直； 密院承旨宜改為承宣； 御史臺、三司、閣門、客省所有承旨，並令別定其名。 」庚戌，以滄州節度使馬全節為安州節度使。 禮部侍郎張允奏，請廢明經、童子科。 從之。 因詔宏詞、拔萃、明算、道舉、百篇等科並停之。
On the first day of the fourth month of summer, the Emperor gave a feast for the officials in the Yongfu Hall. On wuxu, Shi Hui, defense commissioner of Cao Prefecture, died; he was the Emperor's younger cousin. The ritual officials memorialized: "When the Son of Heaven mourns a relative within the five degrees of relationship who wears the one-year mourning garment, he should weep three times and stop." The court approved the memorial. On jihai, the court abolished the tribute of garden fruits from Luoyang and Jingzhao, moved by concern for the people's labor. On renyin, Pei Hao, retired Right Vice Director of the Imperial Secretariat, died; he was posthumously honored as Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent. On bingwu, an edict declared: "The title 'Bearer of the Imperial Will' means to convey the ruler's intent. Only a close attendant and senior minister can receive My will and proclaim My words. At great court assemblies the chief ministers bear the imperial will, and drafting academicians bear the imperial will—without distinction, how can rank and dignity be shown? Apart from the Hanlin Bearer of the Imperial Will, the Palace Directorate's Hall Bearer of the Imperial Will should be renamed Hall Direct Attendant; the Bureau of Military Affairs' Bearer of the Imperial Will should be renamed Proclaimer; and all Bearers of the Imperial Will in the Censorate, Three Bureaus, Gate Office, and Reception Office are to be given distinct titles." On gengxu, Ma Quanjie, military governor of Cang Prefecture, was appointed military governor of An Prefecture. Zhang Yun, Vice Minister of Rites, memorialized to abolish the Classics and Youth examination categories. The court approved the memorial. An edict thereupon suspended the Hongci, Bacui, Mingsuan, Daoju, Bai pian, and other examination categories as well.
5
五月癸酉，宋州貢瑞麥兩歧。 甲申，以前徐州節度使萇從簡為右金吾衛上將軍。 丙戌，安州節度使李金全叛，詔新授安州節度使馬全節以洛、汴、汝、鄭、單、宋、陳、蔡、曹、濮十州之兵討之。 以前鄜州節度使安審暉為副，以內客省使李守貞為都監，仍遣供奉官劉彥瑤奉詔以諭金全。 命麾下齊謙以詔送於淮夷，雲夢人齊峴斬謙，歸其詔於闕。 辛卯，昭義節度使韓王王建立薨，輟朝二日，贈尚書令。 壬寅， 〈 （壬寅上疑脫「六月」二字。）〉 少府監致仕尹玉羽卒。 癸卯，淮南使李承裕代李金全，金全南走，承裕以淮兵二千守其城。 甲辰，馬全節自應山縣進軍於大化鎮。 戊申，與鄂州賊軍陣於安陸之南，三戰而後克之，斬首三千級，生擒千餘人。 供奉官安友謙登鋒力戰，奮不顧身。 全節賞其忠勇，使馳獻捷書，暍死於路。 是日，削奪李金全官爵。 丁巳，淮夷偽校李承裕率眾掠城中資貨而遁，馬全節入城撫其遺民，遣安審暉率兵以逐承裕，擒而斬之。 執其偽都監杜光鄴，及淮南軍五百餘人，露布獻於闕下。 帝曰：此輩何罪，皆厚給放還。 癸亥，道士崇真大師張薦明賜號通元先生。 是時帝好《道德經》，嘗召薦明講說其義，帝悅，故有是命。 尋令薦明以《道》、《德》二經雕上印版，命學士和凝別撰新序，冠於卷首，俾頒行天下。
In the fifth month, on guiyou, Song Prefecture presented auspicious double-stalk wheat. On jiashen, Chang Congjian, former military governor of Xu Prefecture, was appointed senior general of the Right Golden Crow Guard. On bingxu, Li Jinquan, military governor of An Prefecture, rebelled; an edict ordered the newly appointed Ma Quanjie to campaign against him with troops from Luo, Bian, Ru, Zheng, Shan, Song, Chen, Cai, Cao, and Pu prefectures. An Shenhui, former military governor of Fu Prefecture, served as deputy; Li Shouzhen, commissioner of the Inner Reception Office, was overall supervisor; and Palace Attendant Liu Yanyao was sent with an edict to reason with Jinquan. Jinquan ordered his officer Qi Qian to deliver the edict to the Huai; Qi Xian of Yunmeng killed Qian and returned the edict to the capital. On xinmao, Wang Jianli, military governor of the Zhaoyi Army and Prince of Han, died; court was suspended for two days, and he was posthumously honored as Director of the Imperial Secretariat. On renyin, Editorial note begins. (Editor's note: the two characters sixth month are probably missing before renyin.) Editorial note closes. Yin Yuyu, retired Director of the Imperial Workshops, died. On guimao, Li Chengyu of Huainan replaced Li Jinquan; Jinquan fled south, and Chengyu held the city with two thousand Huai troops. On jiachen, Ma Quanjie advanced from Yingshan County to Dahua Fort. On wushen, he engaged the Ezhou rebel army south of Anlu; after three battles he prevailed, beheading three thousand and taking more than a thousand prisoners. Palace Attendant An Youqian charged the front and fought with reckless courage. Quanjie rewarded his loyalty and valor and sent him to deliver the victory report; he died of heatstroke on the way. That day, Li Jinquan's offices and titles were stripped. On dingsi, the Huai rebel officer Li Chengyu looted the city and fled; Ma Quanjie entered to comfort the survivors and sent An Shenhui in pursuit; Chengyu was captured and executed. His false overall supervisor Du Guangye and more than five hundred Huainan troops were taken, and a victory bulletin was presented at court. The Emperor said: "What crime have these men committed? Grant them generous gifts and send them home." On guihai, Zhang Jianming, Daoist master of Chongzhen, was granted the title Master Who Penetrates the Origin. The Emperor then favored the 《Daodejing》; he had summoned Jianming to expound it, was pleased, and therefore granted this title. Soon Jianming was ordered to carve printing blocks of the 《Dao》 and 《De》 classics; the academician He Ning was commanded to write a new preface for the front of the volume, to be distributed throughout the realm.
6
秋七月甲子朔，降安州為防禦使額，以申州隸許州。 丙寅，安州節度使馬全節加檢校太尉，改昭義軍節度使。 前鄜州節度使安審暉加檢校太傅，為威勝軍節度使。 丁卯，湖南奏：遣天策府步騎將張少敵領兵五萬，樓船百艘，次於嶽陽，將進討淮夷也。 甲戌，宣徽使楊彥詢加檢校太傅，充安國軍節度使。 乙亥，戶部尚書致仕鄭韜光卒，贈右僕射。 戊寅，福州王延羲遣商人間路貢表自述。 戊子，宿州奏，淮東鎮移牒云：本國奏書於上國皇帝，曰：「久增景慕，莫會光塵，但循戰國之規，敢預睦鄰之道。 一昨安州有故，脫難相歸，邊校貪功，乘便據壘。 矧機宜之孰在，顧茫昧以難申。 否臧皆兇，乃大《易》之明義； 進取不止，亦聖人之厚顏。 適屬暑雨稍頻，江波甚漲，指揮未到，事實已違。 今者猥沐睿咨，曲形宸旨，歸其俘獲，示以英仁。 其如軍法朝章，彼此不可； 揚名建德，曲直相懸。 雖認好生，匪敢聞命。 其杜光鄴等五百七人，已令卻過淮北。 」帝復書曰：「昨者災生安陸，釁接漢陽，當三伏之炎蒸，動兩朝之師旅。 豈期邊帥，不稟上謀，洎復城池，備知本末。 尋已舍諸俘執，還彼鄉閭，不惟念效命之人，兼亦敦善鄰之道。 今承來旨，將正朝章，希循宥罪之文，用廣崇仁之美。 其杜光鄴等再令歸復。 」尋遣使押光鄴等於桐墟渡淮。 淮中有棹船，甲士拒之，南去不果。 詔光鄴等歸京師，授以職秩，其戎士五百人，立為顯義都。
On the first day of the seventh month of autumn, An Prefecture was reduced to defense-command status, and Shen Prefecture was placed under Xu Prefecture. On bingyin, Ma Quanjie, military governor of An Prefecture, was appointed honorary Grand Guardian and transferred to military governor of the Zhaoyi Army. An Shenhui, former military governor of Fu Prefecture, was appointed honorary Grand Tutor and military governor of the Weisheng Army. On dingmao, Hunan reported dispatching Zhang Shaodi, infantry and cavalry general of the Tiance Bureau, with fifty thousand troops and a hundred tower ships encamped at Yueyang, poised to advance against the Huai. On jiaxu, Yang Yanxun, Commissioner of the Privy Commissionerate, was appointed honorary Grand Tutor and military governor of the Anguo Army. On yihai, Zheng Taoguang, retired Minister of Revenue, died; he was posthumously honored as Right Vice Director of the Imperial Secretariat. On wuyin, Wang Yanxi of Fuzhou sent merchants by a secret route to present a memorial explaining his position. On wuzi, Su Prefecture reported that Huaidong commandery had sent a dispatch: their state addressed a memorial to the emperor of the superior state, saying: "We have long admired you from afar yet never met in person; following only the customs of rival states, we venture to seek the path of good neighborliness. Recently when trouble arose at An Prefecture, men fled hardship to return home; frontier officers, greedy for merit, seized the moment to occupy the fort. Moreover, who held authority over events? Looking back in confusion, the matter is hard to explain. Whether good or ill, all turns inauspicious—so the great 《Changes》 plainly teach; and to press on without cease is likewise shamelessness before the sage's teaching. Summer rains came often and the river swelled; orders had not yet arrived, yet events had already gone awry. Now we have undeservedly received your sagely inquiry, your gracious intent made plain, the return of our captives, and a display of heroic benevolence. Yet military law and court regulations allow no compromise on either side; to proclaim one's name and establish merit—right and wrong stand far apart. Though we acknowledge your love of life, we dare not accept your command. Du Guangye and the five hundred and seven others have already been sent back north of the Huai." The Emperor replied: "Recently calamity arose at Anlu and trouble spread to Hanyang; in the swelter of midsummer, the armies of both courts were set in motion. Who could have expected frontier commanders to disregard the court's plan? Once the city was recovered, the whole story became clear. We soon released all captives and sent them home—not only in regard for those who had risked their lives, but also to uphold good neighborliness." Now we receive your message and will set court regulations right; we hope to follow the text that pardons guilt and extend exalted benevolence. Du Guangye and the others are again ordered to return. Soon envoys were sent to escort Guangye and the others across the Huai at Tongxu. On the Huai, oared boats met armored soldiers who barred them, and the party could not proceed south. An edict ordered Guangye and the others back to the capital with offices and ranks; their five hundred soldiers were organized as the Xianyi Command.

On yisi, Qi and Lu suffered famine; an edict ordered Yan, Yun, and Qing to open granaries for relief.
15
五月庚申朔，以前邢州節度使丁審琪為延州節度使，延州節度使劉景嚴為邠州節度使。 故皇子杲冊贈太尉，進封陳王。 庚午，涇州奏，雨雹，川水大溢，壞州郡鎮戍二十四城。 甲戌，北京遣牙將劉從以吐渾大首領白承福、念龐裏、赫連功德來朝。 邢州上言，吐渾移族帳於鎮州封部。
On the first day of the fifth month, Ding Shenqi, former military governor of Xing, was appointed military governor of Yan; Liu Jingyan was transferred to Bin. The late imperial prince Gao was posthumously honored as Grand Guardian and advanced to Prince of Chen. On gengwu, Jing Prefecture reported hail and severe flooding that destroyed twenty-four cities and garrisons. On jiaxu, the Northern Capital sent adjutant Liu Cong with Tuhun chieftains Bai Chengfu, Nian Pangli, and Helian Gongde to court. Xing Prefecture reported the Tuhun had moved their encampments into Zhen Prefecture's jurisdiction.
16
六月丙申，以前衛尉卿趙延乂為司天監。 丁酉，詔：「今後藩侯郡守，凡有善政，委倅貳官條件聞奏，百姓官吏等不得遠詣京闕。 」壬寅，右領衛上將軍李頃卒，贈太師。 甲辰，迦葉彌陀國僧唵室哩以佛牙泛海而至。 丙午，高麗國王王建加開府儀同三司、檢校太師，食邑一萬戶。 戊午，鎮州節度使安重榮執契丹使拽剌，遣輕騎掠幽州南境之民，處於博野。 仍貢表及馳書天下，述契丹援天子父事之禮，貪傲無厭，困耗中國，已繕治甲兵，將與決戰。 帝發諭而止之。 重榮跋扈愈甚，由是與襄州節度使安從進潛相構謀為不軌。
On bingshen in the sixth month, Zhao Yanyi, former Director of the Imperial Stables, was appointed Director of the Astronomical Bureau. On dingyou, an edict declared that when frontier lords and governors enact good policies, deputies must report them; the people must not travel to the capital. On renyin, Li Qing, senior general of the Right Lead Guard, died; he was posthumously honored as Grand Preceptor. On jiachen, the Kasyamitra monk Anshili brought a Buddha tooth across the sea. On bingwu, Wang Jian, King of Goryeo, was appointed Grand Master of Splendid Happiness with Golden Seal and Ribbon and honorary Grand Preceptor, with a fief of ten thousand households. On wuwu, An Chongrong, military governor of Zhen, seized the Khitan envoy Zhuaila and sent light cavalry to raid Youzhou's southern marches, resettling captives at Boye. He also memorialized and sent letters empire-wide denouncing Khitan greed and arrogance despite their pretense of supporting the Son of Heaven as a father, their drain on China, and his readiness for decisive battle. The Emperor issued an edict ordering him to stop. Chongrong grew ever more overbearing and secretly plotted rebellion with An Congjin, military governor of Xiang Prefecture.
