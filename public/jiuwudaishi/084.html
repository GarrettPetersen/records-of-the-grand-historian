1
開運二年夏五月丙申朔，帝御崇元殿受朝，大赦天下。 丁酉，以右衛上將軍馬萬為左金吾上將軍致仕。 戊戌，陜州節度使宋彥筠移鎮鄧州，澶州節度使何建移鎮河陽。 以左神武統軍潘環為澶州節度使，以宣徽北院使李彥韜遙領壽州節度使兼侍衛馬軍都指揮使，以滄州節度使田武遙領夔州節度使兼侍衛步軍都指揮使。 辛亥，白虹貫日。 壬子，宰臣桑維翰、劉句、李崧、和凝並加階爵。 禮部尚書竇貞固改刑部尚書，太常寺卿王松改工部尚書。 以尚書左丞龍敏為太常卿； 以翰林學士承旨、兵部侍郎李慎儀為尚書左丞； 以御史中丞張允為兵部侍郎、知制誥，充翰林學士承旨； 以左諫議大夫顏衎為御史中丞； 〈 （《宋史·顏衎傳》：喪亂之後，朝綱不振，衎執憲頗有風采，嘗上言：「才除御史者旋授外藩賓佐，復有以私故細事求假外拜，州郡無參謁之儀，出入失風憲之體，漸恐四方得以輕易，百辟無所準繩。 請自今藩鎮幕僚，勿得任臺官； 雖親王宰相出鎮，亦不得奏充賓佐； 非奉制勘事，勿得出京； 自余不令釐雜務。 」詔惟辟召入幕，余從其請。）〉 以兵部侍郎、宏文館學士、判館事田敏為國子祭酒； 以戶部侍郎段希堯為兵部侍郎； 以工部侍郎邊蔚為戶部侍郎，依前權知開封府事； 以左散騎常侍李式為工部侍郎； 以給事中王仁裕為左散騎常侍。 甲寅，以華州節度使趙瑩為開封尹，以皇弟開封尹重睿為秦州節度使，以宣徽南院使劉繼勛為華州節度使，以前鄆州節度使張從恩為晉州節度使。 丙辰，杜威來朝。 定州奏，大風雹，北嶽廟殿宇樹木悉摧拔之。
On the first day (bingchen) of the fifth month in summer of the second Kaiyun year, the Emperor took audience at Chongyuan Hall and proclaimed a general amnesty. On dingyou, Ma Wan, commanding general of the Right Guard, was appointed general-in-chief of the Left Golden Guard and retired from active duty. On wuxu, Song Yanyun was moved from Shaanzhou to command Dengzhou, and He Jian from Chanzhou to Heyang. Pan Huan, commander of the Left Divine Martial Army, became military governor of Chanzhou; Li Yantao of the northern Secretariat bureau held Shouzhou in absentia while retaining command of the palace cavalry; and Tian Wu of Cangzhou held Kuizhou in absentia while retaining command of the palace infantry. On xinhai, a white rainbow was seen crossing the sun. On renzi, chief ministers Sang Weihan, Liu Xu, Li Song, and He Ning all received promotions in rank and enfeoffment. Dou Zhengu moved from the Ministry of Rites to the Ministry of Justice, and Wang Song from the Court of Imperial Sacrifices to the Ministry of Works. Long Min, left vice director of the Department of State Affairs, was appointed director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices; Li Shenyi, expositor-in-chief of the Hanlin Academy and vice minister of war, became left vice director of the Department of State Affairs; Zhang Yun, censor-in-chief, was made vice minister of war with charge of drafting edicts and appointed expositor-in-chief of the Hanlin Academy; Yan Yan, left remonstrance grand master, was appointed censor-in-chief; [ (From the Biography of Yan Yan in the History of Song: After the upheavals, court discipline had slackened, yet Yan enforced the law with real authority. He once submitted: "Censors are appointed one day and sent the next as staff to provincial governors; others use petty private pretexts to seek outside posts on leave. The prefectures have no proper protocol for receiving them, and their movements no longer match censorial dignity. Before long the provinces will hold the office cheap, and the bureaucracy will lack a standard." Henceforth, let no staff member of a military governor hold a censorial post; even when princes or chief ministers take provincial commands, they must not be recommended as staff; and unless commissioned by imperial order to investigate a case, censors may not leave the capital; Beyond that, do not let them take on miscellaneous duties. " An edict allowed only direct summons into a governor's staff; the rest of his request was granted.)] Tian Min, vice minister of war, Hongwen academician, and institute administrator, was appointed chancellor of the Directorate of Education; Duan Xiyao, vice minister of revenue, became vice minister of war; Bian Wei, vice minister of works, became vice minister of revenue while continuing as acting administrator of Kaifeng; Li Shi, left attendant of the cavalry, was made vice minister of works; Wang Renyu, attendant-in-ordinary, was appointed left attendant of the cavalry. On jiayin, Zhao Ying moved from Huazhou to administer Kaifeng; the Emperor's younger brother Chongrui left Kaifeng for Qinzhou; Liu Jixun of the southern Secretariat bureau took Huazhou; and Zhang Cong'en, former governor of Yanzhou, took Jinzhou. On bingchen, Du Wei presented himself at court. Dingzhou reported that a violent hailstorm had uprooted the halls and trees of the Northern Sacred Peak temple.
2
六月乙丑朔，帝御崇元殿，百官入閣。 監修國史劉句、史官張昭遠等以新修《唐書》紀、誌、列傳並目錄凡二百三卷上之。 賜器帛有差。 癸酉，以恒州節度使杜威為天雄軍節度使，充鄴都留守； 以鄴都留守馬全節為恒州節度使； 以翰林學士、金部郎中、知制誥徐臺符為中書舍人； 以翰林學士、禮部郎中、知制誥李瀚為中書舍人； 翰林學士、都官郎中劉溫叟加知制誥； 翰林學士、主客員外郎範質改比部郎中、知制誥，並依舊充職； 祠部員外郎、知制誥張沆本官充學士； 以太常少卿陶穀為中書舍人。 〈 （《宋史·陶穀傳》：穀性急率，嘗與兗帥安審信集會，杯酒相失，為審信所奏，時方姑息武臣，穀坐責授太常少卿。 嘗上言：「頃蒞西臺，每見臺司詳斷刑獄，少有即時決者。 至於閭閻夫婦，小有爭訟，淹滯即時； 坊市死亡喪葬，必候臺司判狀； 奴婢病亡，亦須檢驗。 吏因緣為奸，而邀求不已，經旬不獲埋瘞。 望申條約，以革其弊。 」從之。 俄拜中書舍人。）〉 己亥，以邠州節度使劉景巖為陜州節度使。 己卯，新授恒州節度使馬全節卒，輟朝，贈中書令。 壬午，大理卿張仁願卒，贈秘書監。 遣刑部尚書竇貞固等分詣寺觀禱雨。 己丑，以定州節度使王周為恒州節度使，以前易州刺史安審約為定州留後。 是月，兩京及州郡十五並奏旱。
On the first day (yichou) of the sixth month, the Emperor attended Chongyuan Hall while the officials entered the inner court. Liu Xu, supervisor of the national history, the historiographer Zhang Zhaoyuan, and others presented the newly compiled Tang History—annals, treatises, biographies, and catalogue—in 203 juan. Rewards of vessels and silks were granted in graded amounts. On guiyou, Du Wei was transferred from Hengzhou to command the Tianxiong army and serve as garrison commander of the Ye capital; Ma Quanjie, garrison commander of the Ye capital, was made military governor of Hengzhou; Xu Taifu, Hanlin academician, director of the Bureau of Metals, and edict drafter, was appointed secretariat drafter; Li Han, Hanlin academician, director of the Bureau of Rites, and edict drafter, was appointed secretariat drafter; Liu Wensou, Hanlin academician and director of the Bureau of Justice, was additionally charged with drafting edicts; Fan Zhi, Hanlin academician and vice director of the Bureau of Guests, was made director of the Bureau of Audits with charge of drafting edicts while retaining his other duties; Zhang Hong, vice director of the Bureau of Sacrifices with charge of drafting edicts, was appointed academician at his existing rank; Tao Gu, vice director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices, was appointed secretariat drafter. [ (From the Biography of Tao Gu in the History of Song: Gu was brusque by nature. At a gathering with Yanzhou governor An Shenxin, a quarrel over wine led to Shenxin's memorial; the court was then indulgent toward military men, and Gu was demoted to vice director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices. He once submitted: "When I recently served at the Western Terrace, I saw the censorate review criminal cases, yet few were decided promptly. Ordinary couples in the lanes with petty disputes were delayed at once; deaths and burials in the wards and markets had to await a written ruling from the censorate; and when slaves or maidservants died of illness, examination was required as well. Clerks seized the chance for extortion and endless demands, so burials could be delayed ten days or more. I ask that clear regulations be issued to end these abuses. " The request was approved. Soon afterward he was appointed secretariat drafter.)] On jihai, Liu Jingyan was transferred from Binzhou to Shaanzhou. On jimao, Ma Quanjie, newly appointed to Hengzhou, died; court audiences were suspended, and he was posthumously made grand councilor. On renwu, Zhang Renyuan, director of the Court of Judicial Review, died and was posthumously made director of the Secretariat. Dou Zhengu, minister of justice, and others were sent to temples and monasteries throughout the realm to pray for rain. On jichou, Wang Zhou moved from Dingzhou to Hengzhou, and An Shenyue, former prefect of Yizhou, became garrison deputy of Dingzhou. That month, both capitals and fifteen prefectures reported drought.
3
秋七月乙未朔，以侍衛步軍都指揮使、領夔州節度使田武為昭義軍節度使。 甲寅，左諫議大夫李元龜奏，請禁止天下僧尼典買院舍，從之。 丙辰，前少府監李楷貶坊州司戶，坐冒請逃死吏人衣糧入己故也。 庚申，以前齊州防禦使薛可言為延州兵馬留後。
On the first day (yiwei) of the seventh month in autumn, Tian Wu, commander of the palace infantry who also held Kuizhou, was appointed military governor of the Zhaoyi army. On jiayin, Li Yuan'gui, left remonstrance grand master, asked that monks and nuns be forbidden to mortgage or buy monastic compounds nationwide; the court agreed. On bingchen, Li Kai, former director of the palace workshops, was demoted to registrar of Fangzhou for falsely claiming rations for clerks who had fled or died and keeping them for himself. On gengshen, Xue Keyan, former defense commissioner of Qizhou, was appointed garrison deputy for troops and horses at Yanzhou.
4
八月甲子朔，日有蝕之。 中書舍人陶穀奏，請權廢太常寺二舞郎。 從之。 丙寅，宰臣和凝罷相，守右僕射。 以樞密使馮玉為中書侍郎、平章事，使如故。 乙亥，詔：「諸御史今後除準式請假外，不得以細故小事請假離京； 除奉制命差推事及按察外，不得以諸雜細務差出。 」丙子，以靈州節度使馮暉為邠州節度使，加檢校太尉； 以前鄜州節度使丁審琪為左羽林統軍； 以前鄜州節度使郭謹為左神武統軍。 西京留司御史臺奏：「新授鄧州節度使宋彥筠於銀沙灘斬廳頭鄭溫。 」詔鞫之，款云：「彥筠出身軍旅，不知事體，不合專擅行法。 」詔釋其罪。 以工部尚書王松權知貢舉。 丁丑，以前晉州節度使安叔千為右金吾上將軍； 以三司副使、給事中李穀為磁州刺史，充北面水陸轉運使。 分遣使臣於諸道率馬。 戊寅，以左金吾上將軍皇甫立為左衛上將軍，以右羽林統軍李懷忠為左武衛上將軍。 庚辰，新授潞州節度使田武卒，輟朝，贈太尉。 戊子，湖南奏，靜江軍節度使馬希杲卒。
On the first day (jiazi) of the eighth month, there was a solar eclipse. Tao Gu, secretariat drafter, asked that the two dance officers of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices be provisionally abolished. The request was approved. On bingyin, chief minister He Ning left the chancellorship and remained as right vice director of the Department of State Affairs. Feng Yu, commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs, was made vice director of the Secretariat and grand councilor while retaining his existing commission. On yihai, an edict declared: "Henceforth, except for leave under the standard regulations, censors may not leave the capital on leave for petty private matters; nor may they be dispatched on miscellaneous minor duties except when commissioned by imperial order to investigate or inspect." On bingzi, Feng Hui was transferred from Lingzhou to Binzhou and given the additional title of grand preceptor; Ding Shenqi, former governor of Fuzhou, was made commander of the Left Forest of Feathers; Guo Jin, former governor of Fuzhou, was made commander of the Left Divine Martial Army. The Western Capital's resident censorate reported: "Song Yanyun, newly appointed to Dengzhou, executed his chief clerk Zheng Wen at Yinsha Beach." An edict ordered an inquiry; the confession read: "Yanyun came from the military ranks and did not understand proper procedure; he should not have executed the law on his own authority." An edict pardoned him. Wang Song, minister of works, was appointed acting superintendent of the civil service examinations. On dingchou, An Shuqian, former governor of Jinzhou, was made general-in-chief of the Right Golden Guard; Li Gu, deputy commissioner of the Three Departments and attendant-in-ordinary, was made prefect of Cizhou and commissioner for water and land transport on the northern frontier. Envoys were dispatched to the various circuits to levy horses. On wuyin, Huangfu Li moved from the Left Golden Guard to command the Left Guard, and Li Huaizhong from the Right Forest of Feathers to the Left Martial Guard. On gengchen, Tian Wu, newly appointed to Luzhou, died; court audiences were suspended, and he was posthumously made grand preceptor. On wuzi, Hunan reported the death of Ma Xigao, military governor of the Jingjiang army.
5
九月丙申，以西京留守、北面馬步軍都排陣使景延廣為北面行營副招討使。 丁酉，以刑部侍郎趙遠為戶部侍郎，以工部侍郎李式為刑部侍郎，以中書舍人盧價為工部侍郎。 價久次綸闈，舊例合轉禮部侍郎或御史中丞，宰臣馮玉擬此官，桑維翰以為資望淺，不署狀。 無何，維翰休沐數日，玉獨奏行之，維翰由是不樂，與玉有間矣。 己亥，幸繁臺觀馬，遂幸李守貞第。 庚子，以晉州節度使張從恩為潞州節度使。 吏部侍郎張昭遠加階爵，酬修《唐史》之勞也。 〈 （《宋史·張昭遠傳》：加金紫階，進爵邑。）〉 戊申，升曹州為節鎮，以威信軍為軍額。 詔李守貞率兵屯澶州。 己酉，月掩昴宿。 以宣徽北院使焦繼勛為宣徽南院使，以內客省使孟承誨為宣徽北院使。 壬子，以前太子詹事王居敏為鴻臚卿，李專美為大理卿，以太子賓客致仕馬裔孫為太子詹事。 甲寅，移泰州理所於滿城縣。 乙卯，詔相州節度使張彥澤率兵屯恒州。
On bingshen in the ninth month, Jing Yanguang, Western Capital garrison commander and northern frontier array commander for horse and foot, was made deputy pacification commissioner of the northern frontier headquarters. On dingyou, Zhao Yuan moved from justice to revenue, Li Shi from works to justice, and Lu Jia from the secretariat to works. Jia had long served in the drafting office and by precedent should have risen to vice minister of rites or censor-in-chief. Feng Yu proposed those posts, but Sang Weihan judged his seniority insufficient and refused to sign the appointment. Soon afterward, while Weihan was on leave for several days, Yu memorialized alone and had the appointment carried through. Weihan was displeased, and a rift opened between him and Yu. On jihai, the Emperor visited Fantai to watch horses, then called at Li Shouzhen's residence. On gengzi, Zhang Cong'en was transferred from Jinzhou to Luzhou. Zhang Zhaoyuan, vice minister of personnel, was advanced in rank and title as reward for compiling the Tang History. [ (From the Biography of Zhang Zhaoyuan in the History of Song: He was advanced to the gold-and-purple rank and promoted in enfeoffment.)] On wushen, Caozhou was elevated to a military governorship under the army name Weixin. An edict ordered Li Shouzhen to lead troops and encamp at Chanzhou. On jiyou, the moon occulted the Mao constellation. Jiao Jixun moved from the northern to the southern Secretariat bureau, and Meng Chenghui from the Inner Service Directorate to the northern bureau. On renzi, Wang Jumin, former grand mentor of the heir apparent, became director of the Court of State Ceremonial; Li Zhuanmei became director of the Court of Judicial Review; and Ma Yisun, retired grand mentor of the heir apparent, was reappointed grand mentor. On jiayin, the administrative seat of Taizhou was moved to Mancheng County. On yimao, an edict ordered Zhang Yanzhe of Xiangzhou to lead troops and encamp at Hengzhou.
6
冬十月戊寅，以河陽節度使何建為涇州節度使，以許州節度使李從溫為河陽節度使，以前鄭州節度使石赟為曹州節度使。 庚午，遣使太子賓客羅周嶽、使副太子右庶子王延濟冊兩浙節度使錢宏佐為守太尉。 辛未，右金吾衛上將軍楊彥詢卒，贈太子太師。 丁丑，高麗遣使貢方物。 庚辰，以前延州節度使王令溫為靈州節度使。 庚寅，以邢州兵馬留後劉在明為晉州兵馬留後，以前河陽留後方太為邢州留後。 癸巳，升陳州為節鎮，以鎮安軍為軍額。
On wuyin in the tenth month of winter, He Jian moved from Heyang to Jingzhou, Li Congwen from Xuzhou to Heyang, and Shi Yun, former governor of Zhengzhou, took Caozhou. On gengwu, Luo Zhouyue, grand mentor of the heir apparent, and Wang Yanji, right vice director of the heir apparent's household, were dispatched to invest Qian Hongzuo, military governor of the Two Zhes, as acting grand preceptor. On xinwei, Yang Yanxun, general-in-chief of the Right Golden Guard, died and was posthumously made grand preceptor of the heir apparent. On dingchou, Goryeo sent envoys bearing tribute goods. On gengchen, Wang Lingwen, former governor of Yanzhou, was appointed military governor of Lingzhou. On gengyin, Liu Zaiming moved from Xingzhou to serve as garrison deputy for troops and horses at Jinzhou, and Fang Tai, former Heyang deputy, took Xingzhou. On guisi, Chenzhou was elevated to a military governorship under the army name Zhen'an.
7
十一月戊戌，以邠州節度使馮暉兼侍衛步軍都指揮使，充北面行營先鋒馬步軍都指揮使； 以權知高麗國事王武為檢校太保、使持節、玄菟州都督，充大義軍使，封高麗國王。 癸卯，日南至，帝御崇元殿受朝賀。 戊申，兩浙奏，順化軍節度使錢鐸卒。 甲寅，以壽州節度使、侍衛馬軍都指揮使李彥韜為陳州節度使，典軍如故。 丙申，前商州刺史李俊除名，坐受財枉法也。
In the eleventh month, on wuxu, Feng Hui, military governor of Binzhou, was additionally made commander-in-chief of the palace infantry and appointed commander-in-chief of the vanguard combined cavalry and infantry of the northern expeditionary command; Wang Wu, who held provisional charge of Goryeo affairs, was made honorary grand guardian, commissioner with full credentials, and military commissioner of Xuanyuan Prefecture, appointed commissioner of the Dayi Army, and enfeoffed as king of Goryeo. On guimao, the winter solstice, the Emperor attended Chongyuan Hall to receive court congratulations. On wushen, Liangzhe reported that Qian Duo, military governor of the Shunhua Army, had died. On jiayin, Li Yantao, military governor of Shouzhou and commander-in-chief of the palace cavalry, was made military governor of Chenzhou while retaining his military command. On bingshen, Li Jun, former prefect of Shangzhou, was struck from the rolls for accepting bribes and perverting the law.
8

That year, whenever the Emperor received vessels presented from the four directions, he often had silver exchanged for gold at outer treasuries and brought the gold in. He said to those around him: "Gold is precious yet light, convenient for human labor. " Those with insight regarded this as an omen of northward relocation. [ (From the Biography of Liu Tao in the History of Song: The Young Emperor was extravagant and often exchanged silver for gold to enlarge his vessels. Li Song, overseeing the Three Offices, ordered a count of gold in the upper treasury. When Song checked against the original ledger, several thousand ingots were short. Song rebuked him, saying: "Treasury regulations—never for a single day accept false counts; even a hair's breadth brings severe penalty. 10
開運三年春正月癸巳朔，帝御崇元殿受朝賀，仗衛如式。 詔改鑄天下合同印、書詔印、御前印，並以黃金為之。 己亥，貝州梁漢璋奏，蕃寇屯聚，將謀入寇。 詔符彥卿屯荊州口。 〈 （《宋史·符彥卿傳》：再出河朔，彥卿不與，易其行伍，配以羸師數千戍荊州口。）〉 癸卯，以前華州節度使劉繼勛為同州節度使，以陜州節度使劉景巖為鄧州節度使。 丙午，以宣徽南院使、知陜州事焦繼勛為陜州留後。 丁未，刑部員外王洧賜自家自盡，坐私用宮錢經營求利故也。 右司郎中李知損貶均州司戶，員外置，馳驛發遣，坐前任度支判官日與解縣榷鹽使王景遇交遊借貸故也。 己酉，詔侍衛親軍副都指揮使李守貞率師巡撫北邊。 辛亥，以皇弟秦州節度使重睿為許州節度使，以許州節度使安審琦為兗州節度使，以兗州節度使趙在禮為晉昌軍節度使。 癸丑，以涇州節度使何建為秦州節度使，以前貝州節度使史威為涇州節度使。 乙卯，定州奏，契丹入寇。 己未，二王後守太僕少卿、襲酅國公楊延壽除名配流威州，終身勿齒。 延壽奉命於磁州檢苗，受贓二百餘匹，準律當絞，有司以二王後入議，故貸其死。
In spring, on the first day (guisi) of the first month of the third year of Kaiyun, the Emperor attended Chongyuan Hall to receive court congratulations; guards and escort were as prescribed. An edict ordered recasting the realm's contract seals, edict-writing seals, and imperial-front seals, all to be made of gold. On jihai, Liang Hanzhang of Beizhou reported that barbarian raiders were massing and planning an invasion. An edict ordered Fu Yanqing to encamp at Jingzhoukou. Li Zhisun, director of the right bureau, was demoted to registrar of Jun Prefecture, appointed outside the regular quota, and dispatched by express relay for, during his tenure as revenue adjudicator, associating and borrowing from Wang Jingyu, salt monopoly commissioner of Jie County. On xinhai, the Emperor's younger brother Chongrui, military governor of Qinzhou, was made military governor of Xuzhou; An Shenqi, military governor of Xuzhou, was made military governor of Yanzhou; and Zhao Zaili, military governor of Yanzhou, was made military governor of the Jinchang Army. In summer, on the first day (xinyou) of the fourth month, Li Shouzhen withdrew his troops from the north and arrived at court. Taiyuan reported that the Tuyuhun Bai Kejiu had fled to submit to the Khitan, and that the various lords all harbored contrary designs. On yihai, the chief ministers went to temples and monasteries to pray for rain. Caozhou reported that in succession three thousand local people had died of famine. At the time Henan and Hebei suffered great famine; corpses of the starved were very numerous; in Yi, Mi, Yan, and Yun bandits rose in groups, gathering everywhere, plundering counties and towns, and officials could not restrain them. An Shenqi, military governor of Yanzhou, sent troops to pursue and capture them but was defeated by the bandits. On wuyin, the Emperor visited Xiangguo Temple to pray for rain. The imperial prince Yanshu married the daughter of Zhao Zaili, military governor of the Jinchang Army; Shi Guangzan, director of the Imperial Clan, was ordered to preside.

On jiayin, Liang Hanzhang, garrison deputy of Beizhou, was made military governor of Beizhou; Guo Jin, commander of the Left Divine Martial Army, was made military governor of Yongzhou.

From early summer until now, tens of thousands had starved to death in Henan and Hebei; bandits swarmed and plundered counties and towns; unending rains flooded rivers and marshes and ruined the autumn harvest.

八月己未朔，以左諫議大夫裴羽為給事中。 庚申，李守貞、皇甫遇駐軍定州。 辛酉，幸南莊，召從臣宴樂，至暮還宮。 詔潞州運糧十三萬赴恒州。 癸亥，以右散騎常侍張煦為青州刺史。 李守貞奏，大軍至望都縣，相次至長城北，遇敵千餘騎，轉鬥四十里，斬蕃將解裏相公。 丁卯，詔班師。 庚午，以前亳州防禦使邊蔚為戶部侍郎； 以刑部侍郎李式為戶部侍郎，充三司副使； 以禮部侍郎盧價為刑部侍郎； 以樞密直學士、左散騎常侍邊光範為禮部侍郎充職。 〈 （《宋史·邊光範傳》：少帝以光範藩邸舊僚，待遇尤厚。 因遊宴，見光範位翰林學士下，即日拜尚書禮部侍郎、知制誥，充翰林學士，仍直樞密使院。）〉 辛未，以右龍武統軍周密為延州節度使。 癸酉，河東節度使劉知遠奏，誅吐渾大首領白承福、白鐵匱、赫連海龍等，並夷其族凡四百口，蓋利其孳畜財寶也，人皆冤之。 甲戌，以大理少卿劇可久為大理卿。 棣州刺史慕容彥超削奪在身官爵，房州安置，坐前任濮州擅出省倉麥及私賣官面，準法處死，太原節度使劉知遠上表救之，故貸其死。 丙戌，靈州馮暉奏，與威州刺史藥元福於威州土橋西一百里遇吐蕃七千餘人，大破之，斬首千餘級。 是月，秦州雨，兩旬不止，鄴都雨水一丈，洛京、鄭州、貝州大水，鄴都、夏津臨清兩縣，餓死民凡三千三百。 盜入臨濮、費縣。
On the first day (jiwei) of the eighth month, Pei Yu, left remonstrance grand master, was appointed attendant-in-ordinary. On gengshen, Li Shouzhen and Huangfu Yu encamped their armies at Dingzhou. On xinyou, the Emperor visited the Southern Villa, summoned his ministers for feasting and music, and returned to the palace at dusk. An edict ordered Luzhou to transport 130,000 units of grain to Hengzhou. On guihai, Zhang Xu, right attendant of the cavalry, was appointed prefect of Qingzhou. Li Shouzhen reported that the main army reached Wangdu County and then the northern Long Wall, where it met more than a thousand enemy horsemen; after fighting for forty li, it beheaded the barbarian general Xieli Xianggong. On dingmao, an edict ordered the army to withdraw. On gengwu, Bian Wei, former defense commissioner of Bozhou, was made vice minister of revenue; Li Shi, vice minister of justice, became vice minister of revenue and deputy commissioner of the Three Departments; Lu Jia, vice minister of rites, became vice minister of justice; Bian Guangfan, academician of the Bureau of Military Affairs and left attendant of the cavalry, was made vice minister of rites while retaining his duties. [ (From the Biography of Bian Guangfan in the History of Song: Because Guangfan had been an old associate in the princely residence, the Young Emperor treated him with especial favor. At a pleasure outing, seeing Guangfan seated below the Hanlin academicians, the Emperor that same day appointed him vice minister of rites with charge of drafting edicts, made him a Hanlin academician, and kept him on duty at the Bureau of Military Affairs.)] On xinwei, Zhou Mi, commander of the Right Dragon Martial Army, was appointed military governor of Yanzhou. On guiyou, Liu Zhiyuan of Hedong reported that he had executed the great Tuyuhun leaders Bai Chengfu, Bai Tiegui, Helian Hailong, and others and exterminated their clans—four hundred people in all—coveting their livestock and treasure; the act was widely regarded as unjust. On jiaxu, Ju Kejiu, vice director of the Court of Judicial Review, was appointed director. Murong Yanchao, prefect of Dizhou, was stripped of his offices and settled in Fangzhou for having, while previously in Puzhou, unauthorizedly taken wheat from the provincial granary and privately sold official flour; by law he deserved death, but Liu Zhiyuan of Taiyuan memorialized on his behalf, and his life was spared. On bingxu, Feng Hui of Lingzhou reported that with Wei Prefect Yao Yuanfu, one hundred li west of the earthen bridge at Weizhou, he had routed more than seven thousand Tibetans and taken more than a thousand heads. That month Qinzhou rained for twenty days without cease; at the Ye capital rainwater stood one zhang deep; Luoyang, Zhengzhou, and Beizhou were inundated; and in Xiajin and Linqing counties under the Ye capital, 3,300 people starved to death. Bandits raided Linpu and Fei counties.
17
秋九月壬辰，鄆州節度使、侍衛親軍都指揮使李守貞加兼侍中，滑州節度使皇甫遇進封邠國公，相州節度使張彥澤加檢校太尉。 甲午，以權知威武軍節度使李宏達為檢校太尉、同平章事，充福建節度使，知閩國事。 乙未，前商州刺史李俊賜自盡，坐與親妹奸及行劍斫殺女使，又殺部曲孫漢榮，強奸其妻，準法棄市，詔賜死於家。 己亥，張彥澤奏，破蕃賊於定州界，斬首二十餘級，追襲百餘里，生擒蕃將四人，摘得金耳環二副進呈。 癸卯，太原奏，破契丹於楊武谷，殺七千餘人。 甲辰，以天策上將軍、江南諸道都統、楚王馬希範兼諸道兵馬都元帥。 詔開封府，以霖雨不止，應京城公私僦舍錢放一月。 乙巳，詔安審琦率兵赴鄴都，皇甫遇赴相州。 丙午，以太子少保楊凝式為太子少傅，以刑部尚書王延為太子少保，前潁州團練使竇貞固為刑部尚書。 是月，河南、河北、關西諸州奏，大水霖雨不止，溝河泛濫，水入城郭及損害秋稼。 是月，契丹瀛州刺史詐為書與樂壽將軍王巒，願以本城歸順，且言城中蕃軍不滿千人，請朝廷發軍襲取之，己為內應。 又云：「今秋苦雨，川澤漲溢，自瓦橋已北，水勢無際。 契丹已歸本國，若聞南夏有變，地遠阻水，雖欲奔命，無能及也。 」又，巒繼有密奏，苦言瀛、鄚可取之狀。 先是，前歲中車駕駐於河上，曾遣邊將遺書於幽州趙延壽，勸令歸國，延壽尋有報命，依違而已。 是歲三月，復遣鄴都杜威致書於延壽，且述朝旨，啖以厚利，仍遣洺州軍將趙行實賫書而往，潛申款密。 行實曾事延壽，故遣之。 七月，行實自燕回，得延壽書，且言：「久陷邊庭，願歸中國，乞發大軍應接，即拔身南去。 」敘致懇切，辭旨綿密，時朝廷欣然從之，復遣趙行實計會延壽大軍應接之所。 有瀛州大將遣所親賫蠟書至闕下，告雲欲謀翻變，以本城歸命。 未幾，會彼有告變者，事不果就。 至是，瀛州守將劉延祚受契丹之命，詐輸誠款，以誘我軍，國家深以為信，遂有出師之議。
On renchen in the ninth month of autumn, Li Shouzhen of Yanzhou, commander of the palace guard, was additionally made palace attendant; Huangfu Yu of Huazhou was advanced to Duke of Bin; and Zhang Yanzhe of Xiangzhou was given the additional title of grand preceptor. On jiawu, Li Hongda, provisional governor of the Weiwu army, was made honorary grand preceptor and associate grand councilor, appointed military governor of Fujian, and charged with affairs of Min. On yiwei, Li Jun, former prefect of Shangzhou, was ordered to take his own life for incest with his full sister, hacking a maidservant to death with his sword, killing his retainer Sun Hanrong, and raping the man's wife; though the law called for public execution, an edict allowed him to die at home. On jihai, Zhang Yanzhe reported defeating barbarian raiders on the Dingzhou border, taking more than twenty heads, pursuing them over a hundred li, capturing four barbarian generals alive, and presenting two pairs of gold earrings as trophies. On guimao, Taiyuan reported a victory over the Khitan at Yangwu Valley with more than seven thousand killed. On jiachen, Ma Xifan, general-in-chief of the Heavenly Stratagem, overall commander of the Jiangnan circuits, and king of Chu, was additionally made overall commander of troops and horses for all circuits. An edict to Kaifeng, because the rains would not cease, remitted public and private rental payments in the capital for one month. On yisi, an edict ordered An Shenqi to lead troops to the Ye capital and Huangfu Yu to Xiangzhou. On bingwu, Yang Ningshi moved from junior mentor to junior tutor of the heir apparent; Wang Yan from minister of justice to junior mentor; and Dou Zhengu, former training commissioner of Yingzhou, became minister of justice. That month Henan, Hebei, and Guanxi reported great floods and unending rain, overflowing ditches and rivers, inundation of cities and towns, and damage to the autumn harvest. That month the Khitan prefect of Yingzhou forged a letter to Wang Luan, general of Leshou, offering to surrender the city, claiming fewer than a thousand barbarian troops inside, and asking the court to send an army to seize it while he acted as insider. It also said: "This autumn the rains have been relentless; rivers and marshes have overflowed; from Waqiao northward the waters know no bound. The Khitan have already returned home; if they hear of trouble in the south, distance and floodwaters will keep them from arriving in time even if they wished to hurry to the rescue." Wang Luan also sent secret memorials urgently arguing that Yingzhou and Mozhou could be taken. Earlier, when the imperial carriage had been stationed on the river the year before, a frontier general was sent with a letter to Zhao Yanshou in Youzhou urging his return; Yanshou replied, but evasively and without commitment. In the third month of this year Du Wei of the Ye capital was again sent with a letter to Yanshou, stating the court's intent and tempting him with rich rewards; the Luozhou officer Zhao Xingshi carried the letter and conveyed secret pledges of goodwill. Xingshi had once served under Yanshou, and for that reason was chosen. In the seventh month Xingshi returned from Yan with Yanshou's letter, which said: "Long trapped on the frontier, I wish to return to China; send a great army to meet me, and I will withdraw south at once." The tone was earnest and the wording careful; the court gladly assented and again sent Zhao Xingshi to arrange with Yanshou where the great army should meet him. A senior general of Yingzhou sent a close associate with a sealed wax letter to court, reporting his intent to defect and surrender the city. Before long someone there exposed the plot, and the affair came to nothing. Now Liu Yanzuo, garrison commander of Yingzhou, on Khitan orders feigned submission to lure our army; the court trusted him deeply, and the debate over dispatching troops began.
