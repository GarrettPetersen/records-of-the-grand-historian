1
開運二年夏五月丙申朔，帝御崇元殿受朝，大赦天下。 丁酉，以右衛上將軍馬萬為左金吾上將軍致仕。 戊戌，陜州節度使宋彥筠移鎮鄧州，澶州節度使何建移鎮河陽。 以左神武統軍潘環為澶州節度使，以宣徽北院使李彥韜遙領壽州節度使兼侍衛馬軍都指揮使，以滄州節度使田武遙領夔州節度使兼侍衛步軍都指揮使。 辛亥，白虹貫日。 壬子，宰臣桑維翰、劉句、李崧、和凝並加階爵。 禮部尚書竇貞固改刑部尚書，太常寺卿王松改工部尚書。 以尚書左丞龍敏為太常卿； 以翰林學士承旨、兵部侍郎李慎儀為尚書左丞； 以御史中丞張允為兵部侍郎、知制誥，充翰林學士承旨； 以左諫議大夫顏衎為御史中丞； 〈 （《宋史·顏衎傳》：喪亂之後，朝綱不振，衎執憲頗有風采，嘗上言：「才除御史者旋授外藩賓佐，復有以私故細事求假外拜，州郡無參謁之儀，出入失風憲之體，漸恐四方得以輕易，百辟無所準繩。 請自今藩鎮幕僚，勿得任臺官； 雖親王宰相出鎮，亦不得奏充賓佐； 非奉制勘事，勿得出京； 自余不令釐雜務。 」詔惟辟召入幕，余從其請。）〉 以兵部侍郎、宏文館學士、判館事田敏為國子祭酒； 以戶部侍郎段希堯為兵部侍郎； 以工部侍郎邊蔚為戶部侍郎，依前權知開封府事； 以左散騎常侍李式為工部侍郎； 以給事中王仁裕為左散騎常侍。 甲寅，以華州節度使趙瑩為開封尹，以皇弟開封尹重睿為秦州節度使，以宣徽南院使劉繼勛為華州節度使，以前鄆州節度使張從恩為晉州節度使。 丙辰，杜威來朝。 定州奏，大風雹，北嶽廟殿宇樹木悉摧拔之。
In summer of Kaiyun 2, on the first day of the fifth month (the day bingchen), the emperor received court in Chongyuan Hall and proclaimed a general amnesty. On dingyou, Ma Wan, formerly General-in-Chief of the Right Guards, was appointed General-in-Chief of the Left Imperial Guards and retired from active service. On wuxu, Song Yanyun was moved from Shaan to Deng Prefecture, and He Jian from Chan to Heyang. Pan Huan was appointed Military Commissioner of Chan; Li Yantao was given Shou in absentia while retaining command of the palace cavalry; and Tian Wu was given Kui in absentia while retaining command of the palace infantry. On xinhai, a white rainbow was seen across the sun. On renzi, chief ministers Sang Weihan, Liu Gou, Li Song, and He Ning all received promotions in rank and title. Dou Zhengu moved from Minister of Rites to Minister of Justice, and Wang Song from Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices to Minister of Works. Long Min was appointed Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices; Li Shenyi, Academician Expositor-in-Chief and Vice Minister of War, was made Left Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs; Zhang Yun, Censor-in-Chief, was made Vice Minister of War and drafter of edicts, and appointed Academician Expositor-in-Chief; Yan Yan was appointed Censor-in-Chief; ( (History of Song, Biography of Yan Yan: After the chaos of war, court discipline had grown lax; in office Yan enforced the law with real authority. He once submitted: "Censors are appointed one day and the next sent out as staff to frontier commands; others take petty leave on private grounds. Without proper audience protocol in the provinces, the censorate loses its dignity, the realm may come to hold it cheap, and officials will have no standard to follow." "Henceforth let no staff member of a military commission hold a censorate post; even when princes or chancellors take provincial commands, they must not be named to staff those posts; and unless commissioned by edict to investigate, censors may not leave the capital; nor handle miscellaneous duties beyond their charge." The edict allowed only personally invited staff appointments; the rest of his request was granted.)) Tian Min was appointed Rector of the Directorate of Education; Duan Xiyao was made Vice Minister of War; Bian Wei was made Vice Minister of Revenue while continuing to administer Kaifeng in an acting capacity; Li Shi was made Vice Minister of Works; Wang Renyu was appointed Left Regular Attendant of the Cavalry. On jiayin, Zhao Ying became Prefect of Kaifeng; the emperor's brother Chongrui, who had held that post, was sent to Qin; Liu Jixun took Hua; and Zhang Cong'en was appointed to Jin. On bingchen, Du Wei presented himself at court. Ding Prefecture reported that a violent hailstorm had uprooted the halls and trees of the Northern Peak temple.
2
六月乙丑朔，帝御崇元殿，百官入閣。 監修國史劉句、史官張昭遠等以新修《唐書》紀、誌、列傳並目錄凡二百三卷上之。 賜器帛有差。 癸酉，以恒州節度使杜威為天雄軍節度使，充鄴都留守； 以鄴都留守馬全節為恒州節度使； 以翰林學士、金部郎中、知制誥徐臺符為中書舍人； 以翰林學士、禮部郎中、知制誥李瀚為中書舍人； 翰林學士、都官郎中劉溫叟加知制誥； 翰林學士、主客員外郎範質改比部郎中、知制誥，並依舊充職； 祠部員外郎、知制誥張沆本官充學士； 以太常少卿陶穀為中書舍人。 〈 （《宋史·陶穀傳》：穀性急率，嘗與兗帥安審信集會，杯酒相失，為審信所奏，時方姑息武臣，穀坐責授太常少卿。 嘗上言：「頃蒞西臺，每見臺司詳斷刑獄，少有即時決者。 至於閭閻夫婦，小有爭訟，淹滯即時； 坊市死亡喪葬，必候臺司判狀； 奴婢病亡，亦須檢驗。 吏因緣為奸，而邀求不已，經旬不獲埋瘞。 望申條約，以革其弊。 」從之。 俄拜中書舍人。）〉 己亥，以邠州節度使劉景巖為陜州節度使。 己卯，新授恒州節度使馬全節卒，輟朝，贈中書令。 壬午，大理卿張仁願卒，贈秘書監。 遣刑部尚書竇貞固等分詣寺觀禱雨。 己丑，以定州節度使王周為恒州節度使，以前易州刺史安審約為定州留後。 是月，兩京及州郡十五並奏旱。
On the first day of the sixth month (yichou), the emperor held court in Chongyuan Hall and the officials entered the inner audience. Liu Gou, who supervised the national history, and Zhang Zhaoyuan and other historiographers presented the newly compiled History of Tang—annals, treatises, biographies, and catalogue—in 203 juan. Rewards of vessels and silk were granted according to rank. On guiyou, Du Wei was moved from Heng to command the Tianxiong Army and serve as defender of the Ye capital; Ma Quanjie, defender of Ye, was appointed to Heng; Xu Taifu was appointed Secretariat Drafter; Li Han was appointed Secretariat Drafter; Liu Wensou was additionally made drafter of edicts; Fan Zhi was transferred to the Comparison Bureau, made drafter of edicts, and kept his existing duties; Zhang Hong was appointed Hanlin academician while retaining his existing posts; Tao Gu was appointed Secretariat Drafter. ( (History of Song, Biography of Tao Gu: Impetuous by nature, he once quarreled over wine with Yan commander An Shenxin, who memorialized against him; as the court was then indulgent toward generals, Gu was demoted to Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices. He once submitted: "When I lately served on the Western Censorate, I saw case after case reviewed in detail, yet few decided at once. Ordinary neighborhood disputes, however small, were delayed without prompt settlement; burials in the wards and markets required a censorate writ; even the death of a sick slave or maid required inspection. Clerks exploited the rules for graft and endless demands, so bodies might lie unburied for ten days. I ask that clear rules be issued to end these abuses." The court approved. Soon after he was appointed Secretariat Drafter.)) On jihai, Liu Jingyan was transferred from Bin to Shaan. On jimao, Ma Quanjie died shortly after his appointment to Heng; court mourning was observed, and he was posthumously made Grand Counselor. On renwu, Zhang Renyuan, Director of the Court of Judicial Review, died and was posthumously made Director of the Palace Library. Dou Zhengu, Minister of Justice, and others were sent to temples and monasteries throughout the realm to pray for rain. On jichou, Wang Zhou was moved from Ding to Heng, and An Shenyue was left in charge at Ding. That month, both capitals and fifteen prefectures reported drought.
3
秋七月乙未朔，以侍衛步軍都指揮使、領夔州節度使田武為昭義軍節度使。 甲寅，左諫議大夫李元龜奏，請禁止天下僧尼典買院舍，從之。 丙辰，前少府監李楷貶坊州司戶，坐冒請逃死吏人衣糧入己故也。 庚申，以前齊州防禦使薛可言為延州兵馬留後。
On the first day of the seventh month (yiwei), Tian Wu, commander of the palace infantry who also held Kui, was appointed Military Commissioner of the Zhaoyi Army. On jiayin, Li Yuangui memorialized to forbid monks and nuns empire-wide from buying or selling monastic property; the court agreed. On bingchen, former Director of the Palace Workshops Li Kai was demoted to registrar of Fang Prefecture for embezzling rations issued for deserters and deceased clerks. On gengshen, Xue Keyan, former defense commissioner of Qi, was left in charge of Yan's forces.
4
八月甲子朔，日有蝕之。 中書舍人陶穀奏，請權廢太常寺二舞郎。 從之。 丙寅，宰臣和凝罷相，守右僕射。 以樞密使馮玉為中書侍郎、平章事，使如故。 乙亥，詔：「諸御史今後除準式請假外，不得以細故小事請假離京； 除奉制命差推事及按察外，不得以諸雜細務差出。 」丙子，以靈州節度使馮暉為邠州節度使，加檢校太尉； 以前鄜州節度使丁審琪為左羽林統軍； 以前鄜州節度使郭謹為左神武統軍。 西京留司御史臺奏：「新授鄧州節度使宋彥筠於銀沙灘斬廳頭鄭溫。 」詔鞫之，款云：「彥筠出身軍旅，不知事體，不合專擅行法。 」詔釋其罪。 以工部尚書王松權知貢舉。 丁丑，以前晉州節度使安叔千為右金吾上將軍； 以三司副使、給事中李穀為磁州刺史，充北面水陸轉運使。 分遣使臣於諸道率馬。 戊寅，以左金吾上將軍皇甫立為左衛上將軍，以右羽林統軍李懷忠為左武衛上將軍。 庚辰，新授潞州節度使田武卒，輟朝，贈太尉。 戊子，湖南奏，靜江軍節度使馬希杲卒。
On the first day of the eighth month (jiazi), the sun was eclipsed. Secretariat Drafter Tao Gu asked that the Court of Imperial Sacrifices' two dance officers be suspended for the time being. The court approved. On bingyin, chief minister He Ning left the chancellorship and remained as Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs. Feng Yu was made Vice Director of the Secretariat and Grand Counselor while retaining his post at the Bureau of Military Affairs. On yihai, an edict declared: "Henceforth censors may leave the capital only on regulated leave, not on petty pretexts; nor be sent out on miscellaneous duties except when commissioned by edict to investigate or inspect." On bingzi, Feng Hui was transferred from Ling to Bin and made Acting Grand Preceptor; Ding Shenqi, former commissioner of Fu, was made commander of the Left Forest of Feathers Army; Guo Jin, also formerly of Fu, was made commander of the Left Divine Martial Army. The Western Capital censorate reported that Song Yanyun, newly appointed to Deng, had executed his bureau chief Zheng Wen at Silver Sand Beach. The court ordered an inquiry; the confession read: "Yanyun came up through the army and did not understand proper forms; he had no right to execute justice on his own." An edict pardoned him. Wang Song, Minister of Works, was appointed acting supervisor of the civil examinations. On dingchou, An Shuqian, formerly of Jin, was made General-in-Chief of the Right Imperial Guards; Li Gu was made prefect of Ci and commissioner for northern land and water transport. Envoys were sent to the circuits to requisition horses. On wuyin, Huangfu Li became General-in-Chief of the Left Guards, and Li Huaizhong General-in-Chief of the Left Martial Guards. On gengchen, Tian Wu died shortly after his appointment to Lu; court mourning was observed, and he was posthumously made Grand Preceptor. On wuzi, Hunan reported the death of Jingjiang commander Ma Xigao.
5
九月丙申，以西京留守、北面馬步軍都排陣使景延廣為北面行營副招討使。 丁酉，以刑部侍郎趙遠為戶部侍郎，以工部侍郎李式為刑部侍郎，以中書舍人盧價為工部侍郎。 價久次綸闈，舊例合轉禮部侍郎或御史中丞，宰臣馮玉擬此官，桑維翰以為資望淺，不署狀。 無何，維翰休沐數日，玉獨奏行之，維翰由是不樂，與玉有間矣。 己亥，幸繁臺觀馬，遂幸李守貞第。 庚子，以晉州節度使張從恩為潞州節度使。 吏部侍郎張昭遠加階爵，酬修《唐史》之勞也。 〈 （《宋史·張昭遠傳》：加金紫階，進爵邑。）〉 戊申，升曹州為節鎮，以威信軍為軍額。 詔李守貞率兵屯澶州。 己酉，月掩昴宿。 以宣徽北院使焦繼勛為宣徽南院使，以內客省使孟承誨為宣徽北院使。 壬子，以前太子詹事王居敏為鴻臚卿，李專美為大理卿，以太子賓客致仕馬裔孫為太子詹事。 甲寅，移泰州理所於滿城縣。 乙卯，詔相州節度使張彥澤率兵屯恒州。
On bingshen in the ninth month, Jing Yanguang, defender of the Western Capital and northern array-master, was made deputy commander-in-chief of the northern campaign. On dingyou, Zhao Yuan moved to Revenue, Li Shi to Justice, and Secretariat Drafter Lu Jia to Works. Jia had long served in the drafting office and by precedent should have risen to Vice Minister of Rites or Censor-in-Chief; Feng Yu proposed the Works post, but Sang Weihan judged his standing too slight and refused to sign. Soon afterward, while Weihan was on leave, Yu had the appointment carried through on his own; Weihan took offense, and a breach opened between the two. On jihai, the emperor watched horses at Fanta Terrace, then called at Li Shouzhen's house. On gengzi, Zhang Cong'en was transferred from Jin to Lu. Zhang Zhaoyuan, Vice Minister of Personnel, received a promotion in rank and title as reward for compiling the History of Tang. ( (History of Song, Biography of Zhang Zhaoyuan: He was advanced to the gold-and-purple rank and given a higher title and fief.)) On wushen, Cao was elevated to a full military commission under the name Weixin Army. Li Shouzhen was ordered to encamp at Chan with his troops. On jiyou, the moon occulted the Mao asterism. Jiao Jixun moved from the northern to the southern palace secretariat bureau, and Meng Chenghui took the northern post. On renzi, Wang Jumin became Director of the Court of State Ceremonial, Li Zhuanmei Director of Judicial Review, and the retired palace companion Ma Yisun Grand Mentor of the Heir Apparent. On jiayin, Tai Prefecture's administrative seat was moved to Mancheng County. On yimao, Zhang Yanzhe of Xiang was ordered to encamp at Heng with his troops.
6
冬十月戊寅，以河陽節度使何建為涇州節度使，以許州節度使李從溫為河陽節度使，以前鄭州節度使石赟為曹州節度使。 庚午，遣使太子賓客羅周嶽、使副太子右庶子王延濟冊兩浙節度使錢宏佐為守太尉。 辛未，右金吾衛上將軍楊彥詢卒，贈太子太師。 丁丑，高麗遣使貢方物。 庚辰，以前延州節度使王令溫為靈州節度使。 庚寅，以邢州兵馬留後劉在明為晉州兵馬留後，以前河陽留後方太為邢州留後。 癸巳，升陳州為節鎮，以鎮安軍為軍額。
On wuyin in the tenth month, He Jian was sent to Jing, Li Congwen to Heyang, and Shi Yun, formerly of Zheng, to Cao. On gengwu, Luo Zhouyue and his deputy Wang Yanji were sent to invest Qian Hongzuo of the Two Zhes as Acting Grand Preceptor. On xinwei, Yang Yanxun died and was posthumously made Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent. On dingchou, Goryeo sent tribute envoys. On gengchen, Wang Lingwen was appointed from Yan to Ling. On gengyin, Liu Zaiming was left in charge at Jin, and Fang Tai at Xing. On guisi, Chen was elevated to a full military commission under the name Zhen'an Army.
7
十一月戊戌，以邠州節度使馮暉兼侍衛步軍都指揮使，充北面行營先鋒馬步軍都指揮使； 以權知高麗國事王武為檢校太保、使持節、玄菟州都督，充大義軍使，封高麗國王。 癸卯，日南至，帝御崇元殿受朝賀。 戊申，兩浙奏，順化軍節度使錢鐸卒。 甲寅，以壽州節度使、侍衛馬軍都指揮使李彥韜為陳州節度使，典軍如故。 丙申，前商州刺史李俊除名，坐受財枉法也。
In the eleventh month, on wuxu, Feng Hui, Military Commissioner of Bin Prefecture, was also appointed Commander of the Palace Foot Guard and placed in command of the vanguard horse-and-foot forces of the northern expeditionary army. Wang Wu, who was acting administrator of Goryeo affairs, was appointed Acting Grand Mentor, given a staff of authority as Commissioner, named Governor of Xuantu Commandery and envoy of the Dayi Army, and enfeoffed as King of Goryeo. On guimao, the winter solstice, the emperor received the New Year congratulations in the Chongyuan Hall. On wushen, the Two Zhe circuit reported that Qian Duo, Military Commissioner of the Shunhua Army, had died. On jiayin, Li Yan'ao, Military Commissioner of Shou and Commander of the Palace Horse Guard, was appointed Military Commissioner of Chen Prefecture while retaining his military command. On bingshen, the former prefect of Shangzhou, Li Jun, was expelled from office and stripped of rank for accepting bribes and perverting justice.
8
十二月乙丑，以兩浙節度使、吳越國王錢宏佐兼東南面兵馬都元帥。 丙寅，以吳越國金馬左廂都指揮使、湖州刺史胡思進遙領虔州昭信軍節度使，以吳越國金馬右廂都指揮使、明州刺史闞璠遙領宣州寧國軍節度使，並典軍如故。 左羽林統軍丁審琪卒，贈太尉。 辛未，以工部侍郎盧價為禮部侍郎，以右散騎常侍、集賢殿學士、判院事司徒詡為工部侍郎，依前充職； 以前中書舍人殷鵬為給事中，充樞密直學士； 以給事中劉知新為右散騎常侍。 乙亥，陜府節度使劉景巖來朝。 丁丑，狩於近郊，獵也。 己卯，光祿卿致仕陳元卒於太原。 庚辰，命使冊高麗國王王武。 癸未，以前兗州節度使安審信為華州節度使。 丁亥，以樞密使、中書令桑維翰為開封尹； 以司空、門下侍郎、平章事劉句判三司； 以左僕射、門下侍郎、平章事李崧為守侍中，充樞密使； 以開封尹趙瑩為中書令、宏文館大學士； 以宣徽南院使焦繼勛知陜州軍州事。 〈 （《宋史·焦繼勛傳》：西人寇邊，朝議發師致討，繼勛抗疏請行，拜秦州觀察使兼諸蕃水陸轉運使。 既至，推恩信，設方略，招誘諸部，相率奉玉帛牛酒乞盟，邊境以安。 俄徙知陜州。）〉 己丑，邠州節度使馮暉準詔來朝。
In the twelfth month, on yichou, Qian Hongzuo, Military Commissioner of Two Zhe and King of Wuyue, was also appointed Grand Marshal of southeastern forces. On bingyin, Hu Sijin, commander of the Wuyue Golden Horse Guard's left wing and prefect of Huzhou, was given the honorary title of Military Commissioner of Qianzhou's Zhaoxin Army, and Kan Fan, commander of the right wing and prefect of Ming, was given the honorary title of Military Commissioner of Xuanzhou's Ningguo Army; both retained their existing commands. Ding Shenqi, commander of the Left Forest of Feathers Army, died and was posthumously promoted to Grand Preceptor. On xinwei, Lu Jia, Vice Minister of Works, was appointed Vice Minister of Rites, and Situxun, Right Regular Attendant, Jixian Hall academician, and judge of academy affairs, was appointed Vice Minister of Works while retaining his other posts. The former Secretariat Gentleman Yin Peng was appointed Supervising Secretariat Gentleman and direct academician of the Bureau of Military Affairs. Liu Zhixin, Supervising Secretariat Gentleman, was appointed Right Regular Attendant of Cavalry. On yihai, Liu Jingyan, Military Commissioner of Shan Prefecture, came to court. On dingchou, the emperor went hunting in the suburbs. On jimao, Chen Yuan, retired Grand Master of Splendor, died at Taiyuan. On gengchen, the emperor dispatched envoys to invest Wang Wu as King of Goryeo. On guiwei, the former Military Commissioner of Yan, An Shenxin, was appointed Military Commissioner of Hua Prefecture. On dinghai, Sang Weihan, Bureau Commissioner and Director of the Secretariat, was appointed Acting Mayor of Kaifeng. That year, whenever tribute vessels arrived from the provinces, the emperor often had silver exchanged for gold at outer treasuries and brought the gold in, telling his attendants, "Gold is precious yet light — easier for men to carry. " Observers took this as an omen of the court's removal to the north. ( (History of Song, Biography of Liu Tao: The young emperor was extravagant and often exchanged silver for gold to enlarge his tableware. Song rebuked him: "Treasury rules forbid false entries even for a single day; the slightest discrepancy brings severe punishment. " Tao replied, "The treasury often underreports its holdings to reserve stock for imperial requisitions. In spring of the third year of Kaiyun, on guisi, the first day of the first month, the emperor received New Year congratulations in the Chongyuan Hall with the full guard array. An edict ordered the empire's contract seals, proclamation seals, and imperial seals recast in yellow gold. On jihai, Liang Hanzhang of Bei Prefecture reported that barbarian forces had gathered and were preparing to invade. An edict ordered Fu Yanqing to encamp at Jingzhou Ford. On dingwei, Wang Si, Vice Minister of Punishments, was permitted to commit suicide at home for misappropriating palace funds for private gain. On yiwei, Yan Yan, Chief Imperial Censor, was appointed Vice Minister of Revenue, and Zhao Yuan, Vice Minister of Revenue, was appointed Chief Imperial Censor. Taiyuan reported that the Toghuz chieftain Bai Kejiu had defected to the Khitan and that regional commanders were showing signs of disloyalty. On yihai, the chief ministers went to temples and monasteries to pray for rain. Caozhou reported that three thousand people in the prefecture had died of starvation in succession. At that time Henan and Hebei were stricken by severe famine and corpses lay everywhere; in Yi, Mi, Yan, and Yun bandit bands rose up, gathering to raid counties and towns, and local officials could not stop them. An Shenqi, Military Commissioner of Yan, led troops to pursue the bandits and was defeated. On wuyin, the emperor visited the Xiangguo Temple to pray for rain. The prince Yanxu married the daughter of Zhao Zaili, Military Commissioner of the Jinchang Army, with Shi Guangzan, Director of the Imperial Clan, presiding over the ceremony.

On xinhai, an edict appointed Huangfu Yu overall commander of the northern expeditionary army, Zhang Yanze as his deputy, and Li Yin as troop supervisor to lead forces to Yi, Ding, and other prefectures; the march was soon halted. On jiayin, Liang Hanzhang, provisional military commissioner of Bei, was appointed Military Commissioner of Bei, and Guo Jin, commander of the Left Divine Martial Army, was appointed Military Commissioner of Yun.

15

On xinwei, Zhou Mi, Commander of the Right Dragon Martial Army, was appointed Military Commissioner of Yan. On guiyou, Liu Zhiyuan, Military Commissioner of Hedong, reported that he had put to death the great Tuguhun chiefs Bai Chengfu, Bai Tiegui, Helian Hailong, and others, and exterminated their clans—four hundred persons in all—chiefly to seize their livestock and treasure; all regarded it as a grievous injustice. On jiaxu, Ju Kejiu, Vice Director of the Court of Law, was appointed Director of the Court of Law. Murong Yanchao, Prefect of Di, was stripped of his current rank and office and resettled in Fang Prefecture. That month Qin Prefecture rained twenty days without cease; at Yedu the floodwaters stood one zhang deep; Luoyang, Zheng, and Bei suffered great floods; and in Yedu and the counties of Xiajin and Linqing, three thousand three hundred people starved to death. Bandits raided Linpu and Fei counties.
17
秋九月壬辰，鄆州節度使、侍衛親軍都指揮使李守貞加兼侍中，滑州節度使皇甫遇進封邠國公，相州節度使張彥澤加檢校太尉。 甲午，以權知威武軍節度使李宏達為檢校太尉、同平章事，充福建節度使，知閩國事。 乙未，前商州刺史李俊賜自盡，坐與親妹奸及行劍斫殺女使，又殺部曲孫漢榮，強奸其妻，準法棄市，詔賜死於家。 己亥，張彥澤奏，破蕃賊於定州界，斬首二十餘級，追襲百餘里，生擒蕃將四人，摘得金耳環二副進呈。 癸卯，太原奏，破契丹於楊武谷，殺七千餘人。 甲辰，以天策上將軍、江南諸道都統、楚王馬希範兼諸道兵馬都元帥。 詔開封府，以霖雨不止，應京城公私僦舍錢放一月。 乙巳，詔安審琦率兵赴鄴都，皇甫遇赴相州。 丙午，以太子少保楊凝式為太子少傅，以刑部尚書王延為太子少保，前潁州團練使竇貞固為刑部尚書。 是月，河南、河北、關西諸州奏，大水霖雨不止，溝河泛濫，水入城郭及損害秋稼。 是月，契丹瀛州刺史詐為書與樂壽將軍王巒，願以本城歸順，且言城中蕃軍不滿千人，請朝廷發軍襲取之，己為內應。 又云：「今秋苦雨，川澤漲溢，自瓦橋已北，水勢無際。 契丹已歸本國，若聞南夏有變，地遠阻水，雖欲奔命，無能及也。 」又，巒繼有密奏，苦言瀛、鄚可取之狀。 先是，前歲中車駕駐於河上，曾遣邊將遺書於幽州趙延壽，勸令歸國，延壽尋有報命，依違而已。 是歲三月，復遣鄴都杜威致書於延壽，且述朝旨，啖以厚利，仍遣洺州軍將趙行實賫書而往，潛申款密。 行實曾事延壽，故遣之。 七月，行實自燕回，得延壽書，且言：「久陷邊庭，願歸中國，乞發大軍應接，即拔身南去。 」敘致懇切，辭旨綿密，時朝廷欣然從之，復遣趙行實計會延壽大軍應接之所。 有瀛州大將遣所親賫蠟書至闕下，告雲欲謀翻變，以本城歸命。 未幾，會彼有告變者，事不果就。 至是，瀛州守將劉延祚受契丹之命，詐輸誠款，以誘我軍，國家深以為信，遂有出師之議。
In autumn, on renchen of the ninth month, Li Shouzhen, Military Commissioner of Yan and Commander of the Palace Guard, was additionally made Palace Attendant; Huangfu Yu of Hua was enfeoffed as Duke of Bin; and Zhang Yanze of Xiang was made Acting Grand Preceptor. On jiawu, Li Hongda, acting Military Commissioner of the Weiwu Army, was made Acting Grand Preceptor and Co-Chancellor, appointed Military Commissioner of Fujian, and charged with governing Min. On yiwei, Li Jun, former Prefect of Shang, was granted suicide. He had lain with his own sister, hacked a maid with a sword, murdered his retainer Sun Hanrong, and raped Sun's wife; by law he merited public execution, but the edict allowed him to die at home. On jihai, Zhang Yanze reported that he had routed Khitan forces on the Ding border, taken more than twenty heads, pursued them over a hundred li, captured four Khitan generals alive, and presented two pairs of gold earrings taken from the enemy. On guimao, Taiyuan reported a victory over the Khitan at Yangwu Valley, with more than seven thousand killed. On jiachen, Ma Xifan, Grand General of the Heaven's Strategy, Overall Commander of the Jiangnan circuits, and King of Chu, was additionally made Supreme Commander of Armies and Horses of All Circuits. An edict to the Kaifeng prefecture ordered that, because the rains would not cease, rents on public and private leased dwellings in the capital be remitted for one month. On yisi, an edict ordered An Shenqi to lead troops to Yedu and Huangfu Yu to Xiang Prefecture. On bingwu, Yang Ningshi was moved from Vice Protector to Junior Tutor of the Crown Prince; Wang Yan, Minister of Punishments, became Vice Protector; and Dou Zhengu, former Military Training Commissioner of Ying, was made Minister of Punishments. That month the provinces of Henan, Hebei, and Guanxi reported ceaseless rains and great floods; ditches and rivers burst their banks, water entered the cities, and the autumn harvest was ruined. That month the Khitan prefect of Ying forged a letter to Wang Luan, general of Yueshou, offering to surrender the city and claiming that fewer than a thousand Khitan troops remained inside; he asked the court to send an army to seize it while he would act as insider. The letter also said: "This autumn the rains have been relentless; rivers and marshes have swollen. From Waqiao northward the floodwaters stretch without end." "The Khitan have already returned home. If they hear of trouble in the south, the distance is great and the waters block the way; even if they wished to hurry to the rescue, they could not arrive in time." Luan then sent further secret memorials urgently arguing that Ying and Mo could be seized. Earlier, when the year before the imperial carriage had been stationed on the Yellow River, border generals were sent with a letter to Zhao Yanshou at Youzhou urging him to return to the dynasty; Yanshou soon replied, but remained noncommittal. That year in the third month, Du Wei of Yedu was again sent to write Yanshou, setting forth the court's intent and tempting him with rich rewards; the Ming army officer Zhao Xingshi carried the letter and conveyed secret pledges of goodwill. Xingshi had once served under Yanshou, and was sent for that reason. In the seventh month Xingshi returned from Yan with Yanshou's letter, which said: "Long trapped on the frontier, I wish to return to China; send a great army to meet me, and I will flee south at once." The tone was earnest and the arguments meticulous; the court gladly assented and again sent Zhao Xingshi to arrange where Yanshou's main force would rendezvous. A senior officer of Ying sent a close associate with a sealed letter to the court, reporting a planned defection to surrender the city. Before long someone there exposed the plot, and the affair came to nothing. Now Liu Yanzuo, the Ying garrison commander, acting on Khitan orders, feigned submission to lure our army; the court placed deep faith in him, and thus arose the debate to march out.
