天福四年春正月癸卯，帝御崇元殿受朝賀，仗衛如式。 丙午，召太子太師致仕範延光宴於便殿。 以延光歸命之後，慮懷疑懼，故休假之內，錫以款密。 帝謂之曰：「無忿疾以傷厥神，無憂思以勞厥衷。 朕方示信於四方，豈食言於汝也。 」延光俯伏拜謝，其心遂安。 丁未，以西京副留守龍敏為吏部侍郎。 戊申，盜發唐閔帝陵。 己酉，朔方軍節度使張希崇卒，贈太師。 以澶州防禦使張從恩為樞密副使。 甲寅，以侍衛步軍都指揮使、寧江軍節度使景延廣為義成軍節度使，以義成軍節度使馮暉為朔方軍節度使。 乙卯，左諫議大夫曹國珍上言：「請於內外臣僚之中，選才略之士，聚《唐六典》、前後《會要》、《禮閣新儀》、《大中統類》、律令格式等，精詳纂集，俾無漏落，別為書一部，目為《大晉政統》。 」從之。 其詳議官，宜差太子少師梁文矩、左散騎常侍張允、大理卿張澄、國子祭酒唐汭、大理少卿高鴻漸、國子司業田敏、禮部郎中呂咸休、司勛員外郎劉濤、刑部員外郎李知損、監察御史郭延升等一十九人充。 文矩等咸曰：「改前代禮樂刑憲為《大晉政統》，則《堯典》、《舜典》當以《晉典》革名。 」列狀駁之曰：
In the spring of Tianfu 4, on the first day of the first month (day guimao), the emperor held audience in Chongyuan Hall to receive New Year congratulations, with guards and ceremonial regalia in full form. On bingwu, the emperor summoned Fan Yanguang, retired Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent, to a banquet in the Privy Hall. After Yanguang had surrendered, he still feared suspicion and anxiety; during his leave of absence the emperor therefore treated him with warm, confidential kindness. The emperor told him, "Do not let resentment harm your spirit, and do not let worry and brooding weary your heart. I am now showing good faith to the realm—how could I go back on my word to you." Yanguang prostrated himself and bowed in thanks, and his mind was finally at ease. On dingwei, Long Min, deputy military commissioner of the Western Capital, was appointed vice minister of personnel. On wushen, robbers broke into the tomb of Emperor Min of Tang. On jiyou, Zhang Xichong, military commissioner of Shuofang, died and was posthumously granted the title Grand Preceptor. Zhang Cong'en, defense commissioner of Chanzou, was appointed deputy commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs. On jiayin, Jing Yanguang, commander of the palace guard foot soldiers and military commissioner of Ningjiang, was transferred to Yicheng; Feng Hui, military commissioner of Yicheng, was transferred to Shuofang. On yimao, Left Remonstrance Grandee Cao Guozhen memorialized: "From among officials inside and outside the court, select men of ability and strategy to gather the Tang Six Canons, the earlier and later Essentials, the New Rites of the Ritual Pavilion, the Taizhong Compendia, statutes, ordinances, and formats, and compile them meticulously so that nothing is left out, producing a separate work to be titled the Great Jin Administrative Canon." The emperor approved the proposal. For the detailed deliberation, nineteen officials were appointed, including Junior Preceptor of the Heir Apparent Liang Wenju, Left Regular Attendant of the Cavalry Zhang Yun, Grand Judge Zhang Cheng, Chancellor of the Directorate of Education Tang Rui, Vice Grand Judge Gao Hongjian, Vice Chancellor Tian Min, Bureau Director of Rites Lü Xianxiu, Outer Office Director of Merits Liu Tao, Outer Office Director of Punishments Li Zhishun, and Investigating Censor Guo Yansheng. Wenju and the others all said, "To recast the rites, music, punishments, and statutes of former dynasties as the Great Jin Administrative Canon would mean that the Canon of Yao and the Canon of Shun would have to be renamed the Jin Canon." They submitted a joint memorial in rebuttal, stating:
2
作者之謂聖，述者之謂明，茍非聖明，焉能述作。 若運因革故，則事乃維新，或改正朔而變犧牲，或易服色而殊徽號。 是以五帝殊時，不相沿樂； 三王異世，不相襲禮。 至於近代，率由舊章； 比及前朝，日滋條目。 多因行事之失，改為立制之初，或臣奏條章，君行可否，皆表其年月，紀以姓名，聚類分門，成文作則。 莫不悉稽前典，垂範後昆，述自聖賢，歷於朝代，得金科玉條之號，設亂言破律之防，守而行之，其來尚矣。 皇帝陛下，運齊七政，歷契千年，爰從創業開基，莫不積功累德。 所宜直筆，具載鴻猷，若備錄前代之編年，目作聖朝之政統，此則是名不正也。 夫名不正則言不順，而媚時掠美，非其實矣。 若翦截其辭，此則是文不備也。 夫文不備則啟爭端，而禮樂刑政，於斯亂矣。 若改舊條而為新制，則未審何門可以刊削，何事可以編聯。 既當革故從新，又須廢彼行此，則未知國朝能守不能守乎？ 臣等同共參詳，未見其可。
The creator is called a sage; the expounder is called enlightened—without sage wisdom and enlightenment, how could one compose and compile. When the times demand reform, affairs are renewed—sometimes the calendar is corrected and sacrifices changed, sometimes dress colors and insignia are altered. Thus the Five Emperors, living in different ages, did not inherit one another's music; and the Three Kings, in different generations, did not inherit one another's rites. In recent times, however, governments have generally followed established regulations; and by the previous dynasty the number of provisions grew day by day. Most arose from administrative failures and were revised when new institutions were established; sometimes ministers submitted regulations and the ruler approved or rejected them—all dated by year and month, attributed by name, classified by category, and set down as written standards. All drew fully on earlier canons to set examples for posterity, handed down from sages through successive dynasties, earning the name of golden statutes and jade provisions and erecting barriers against reckless speech that would break the law—preserving and enforcing them has been the practice for a very long time. Your Majesty's fortune aligns with the seven luminaries and your reign spans a thousand years; from the founding of the state, every step has accumulated merit and virtue. What is fitting is straightforward writing that fully records your great designs; to compile the annals of former dynasties and title the work the holy dynasty's administrative canon would be a case of an improper name. When the name is improper, words do not accord with reality; to flatter the present age and claim undeserved credit would not reflect the truth. If their wording were cut and trimmed, the text would be incomplete. An incomplete text invites dispute, and rites, music, punishments, and administration would fall into disorder. If old provisions were changed into new institutions, it is unclear which sections could be cut and which matters could be linked together. Since one must reform the old and follow the new, and abolish the former to implement the latter, it is unknown whether the court could maintain consistency or not? We your subjects deliberated together and did not see how it could be done.
3
疏奏，嘉之，其事遂寢。 辛酉，以前晉昌軍節度使李周為靜難軍節度使。 是日，封皇第十一妹安定郡主為延慶長公主，皇第十二妹廣平郡主為清平長公主。
When the memorial was submitted, the emperor commended it, and the matter was dropped. On xinyou, Li Zhou, former military commissioner of Jinchang, was appointed military commissioner of Jingnan. That same day, the emperor's eleventh younger sister, Princess of Anding Commandery, was enfeoffed as Princess of Yanqing, and his twelfth younger sister, Princess of Guangping Commandery, as Princess of Qingping.
4
二月辛卯，改東京玉華殿為永福殿。 中書上言：「太原潛龍莊望建為慶長宮，使相鄉望改為龍飛鄉，都尉裏望改為神光裏。 」從之。 丁酉，宰臣馮道、左散騎常侍韋勛、禮部員外郎楊昭儉自契丹使回。 帝慰勞備至，錫賫豐厚。 庚子，以天和節宴群官於廣政殿，賜物有差。
In the second month, on xinmao, the Yuhua Hall in the Eastern Capital was renamed Yongfu Hall. The Secretariat memorialized: "The Qianlong Estate at Taiyuan should have a ward established as Qingchang Palace; the Xiangwang of the envoy's district should be renamed Longfei Township, and the Liwang of the commandant's district renamed Shenguang Lane." The emperor approved. On dingyou, Chief Minister Feng Dao, Left Regular Attendant of the Cavalry Wei Xun, and Outer Office Director of Rites Yang Zhaojian returned from their embassy to the Khitan. The emperor comforted them with the utmost care and bestowed lavish rewards. On gengzi, the emperor held a Tianhe Festival banquet for the officials in Guangzheng Hall and distributed gifts in graded amounts.
5
三月癸卯朔，左僕射劉句、給事中盧重自契丹使回，頒賜器幣如馮道等。 乙巳，回鶻可汗仁美遣使貢方物，中有玉狻猊，實奇貨也。 丙午，涇州節度使張萬進卒，贈太師。 己未，皇子開封尹鄭王重貴、歸德軍節度使兼侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使劉知遠、忠武軍節度使杜重威，並加同中書門下平章事。 天平軍節度使趙在禮封衛國公。 庚申，遣內臣趙處比以版詔征華山隱者前右拾遺鄭雲叟、玉笥山道士羅隱之。 靈州戍將王彥忠據懷遠城作叛，帝遣供奉官齊延祚乘驛而往，彥忠率眾出降，延祚矯制殺之。 詔：「齊延祚辜我誓言，擅行屠戮。 彰殺降之罪，隳示信之文。 宜除名決重杖一頓配流。 王彥忠贈官收葬。 辛酉，封回鶻可汗仁美為奉化可汗。 癸亥，以左龍武統軍皇甫遇為鎮國軍節度使，張彥澤為彰義軍節度使。
On the first day of the third month (guimao), Left Vice Director Liu Gou and Supervising Secretary Lu Chong returned from their Khitan embassy and received vessels and gifts of the same kind as Feng Dao and his party. On yisi, Kaghan Renmei of the Uyghurs sent envoys with tribute, including a jade suanni—a truly rare treasure. On bingwu, Zhang Wanjin, military commissioner of Jingzhou, died and was posthumously granted the title Grand Preceptor. On jiwei, Prince Chonggui of Zheng, heir apparent and Prince of Kaifeng, Liu Zhiyuan, military commissioner of Guide and commander of the palace guard horse and foot, and Du Chongwei, military commissioner of Zhongwu, were all appointed Grand Councilors. Zhao Zaili, military commissioner of Tianping, was enfeoffed as Duke of Wei. On gengshen, the inner attendant Zhao Chubi was dispatched with an edict on boards to summon the Huashan recluse, former Right Reminder Zheng Yunsou, and the Yujian Mountain Daoist Luo Yinshi. Wang Yanzhong, garrison commander at Lingzhou, seized Huaiyuan City and rebelled; the emperor sent palace attendant Qi Yanzuo by express relay. Yanzhong led his men out to surrender, but Yanzuo forged an order and had him killed. An edict declared: "Qi Yanzuo has violated my sworn pledge and on his own authority carried out a massacre. He has made manifest the crime of killing those who surrendered and has undermined our pledge of good faith. He is to be stripped of rank and office, sentenced to one heavy beating, and exiled by assignment. Wang Yanzhong was posthumously granted an office and given a proper burial. On xinyou, Uyghur Kaghan Renmei was enfeoffed as Kaghan of Fenghua. On guihai, Huangfu Yu, commander of the Left Dragon Martial Army, was appointed military commissioner of Zhenguo, and Zhang Yanze military commissioner of Zhangyi.
6
夏四月壬申朔，以河中節度副使薛仁謙為衛尉卿。 丙子，以汝州防禦使宋彥筠為同州節度使； 以護聖左右軍都指揮使李懷忠為侍衛親軍馬軍都指揮使，領壽州忠正軍節度使； 以奉國左右廂都指揮使郭謹為侍衛親軍步軍都指揮使、夔州寧江軍節度使。 戊寅，詔廢長春宮使額。 〈 （《五代會要》載原敕云：同州長春宮使額宜停，沿宮職務，委州司制置。）〉 己卯，改明德殿為滋德殿，宮城南門同名故也。 以華州節度使劉遂凝為右龍武統軍，以右龍武統軍張廷蘊為絳州刺史。 庚辰，征前右拾遺鄭雲叟為右諫議大夫，玉笥山道士羅隱之賜號希夷先生。 甲申，以翰林學士承旨、兵部侍郎崔棁權判太常卿，以端明殿學士、戶部侍郎和凝為翰林學士承旨。 樞密院學士、尚書倉部郎中司徒詡，樞密院學士、尚書工部郎中顏衎並落職守本官，樞密副使張從恩改宣徽使：初廢樞密院故也。 先是，桑維翰免樞密之務，以劉處讓代之，奏議多不稱旨，及處讓丁母憂，遂以密院印付中書，故密院廢焉。 丙戌，以韓昭裔為兵部尚書致仕，馬裔孫為太子賓客致仕，房暠為右驍衛大將軍致仕，皆唐末帝之舊臣也。 戊子，升永、嶽二州為團練使額，改湘川縣為全州，從馬希範之奏也。
On the first day of the fourth month (renshen), Xue Renqian, deputy military commissioner of Hezhong, was appointed Minister of the Court of Imperial Entertainments. On bingzi, Song Yanyun, defense commissioner of Ruzhou, was appointed military commissioner of Tongzhou; Li Huaizhong, commander of the left and right armies protecting the sage, was appointed commander of the palace guard cavalry and concurrently military commissioner of Zhongzheng at Shouzhou; and Guo Jin, commander of the left and right wings serving the state, was appointed commander of the palace guard foot soldiers and military commissioner of Ningjiang at Kuizhou. On wuyin, an edict abolished the Changchun Palace commissioner title. [ (The Five Dynasties Essentials records the original edict: "The Changchun Palace commissioner title at Tongzhou is to be discontinued; routine palace duties are to be arranged by the prefectural office.")〉 On jimao, Mingde Hall was renamed Zide Hall because it shared a name with the south gate of the palace city. Liu Sunning, military commissioner of Huazhou, was appointed commander of the Right Dragon Martial Army, and Zhang Tingyun, commander of the Right Dragon Martial Army, was made prefect of Jiangzhou. On gengchen, former Right Reminder Zheng Yunsou was summoned as Right Remonstrance Grandee, and the Yujian Mountain Daoist Luo Yinshi was granted the style Master Xiyi. On jiashen, Cui Zhuo, expositor-in-chief of the Hanlin Academy and vice minister of war, was appointed acting Minister of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices, and He Ning, academician of the Duanming Hall and vice minister of revenue, was made expositor-in-chief of the Hanlin Academy. Situ Xu and Yan Kan, academicians of the Bureau of Military Affairs, were both removed from their concurrent posts and retained their original offices; Deputy Commissioner Zhang Cong'en was transferred to Commissioner of the Palace Secretariat—because the Bureau of Military Affairs had just been abolished. Earlier, Sang Weihan had been relieved of military affairs duties and Liu Churang replaced him, but his memorials often failed to please the emperor; when Churang entered mourning for his mother, the bureau's seal was handed to the Secretariat, and the bureau was therefore abolished. On bingxu, Han Zhaoyi, Ma Yisun, and Fang Hao were granted retirement as minister of war, guest of the heir apparent, and general of the Right Martial Tiger Guard respectively—all former ministers of the last Tang emperor. On wuzi, Yong and Yue prefectures were promoted to regimental training commissioner status, and Xiangchuan County was renamed Quanzhou, following Ma Xifan's memorial.
7
五月壬寅朔，帝御崇元殿受朝，仗衛如式。 癸卯，以左僕射劉句兼太子太保，封譙國公。 乙巳，昭順軍節度使姚彥章卒。 升靈州方渠鎮為威州，隸於靈武。 改舊威州為清邊軍。 戊申，湖南節度使馬希範加天策上將軍。 以前邠州節度使安叔千為滄州節度使。 庚戌，虞部郎中楊昭儉可本官知制誥。 辛亥，置靜海軍於溫州，從錢元瓘之請也。 壬子，以侍御史盧價為戶部員外郎、知制誥。 戶部尚書崔居儉卒。 甲寅，詔止絕朝臣，不得外州府求覓表狀，奏薦交親。 乙卯，升金州為節鎮，以懷德軍為使額。 以齊州防禦使潘環為懷德軍節度使。 右諫議大夫致仕鄭雲叟賜號逍遙先生，仍給致仕官俸。 丁巳，以刑部尚書姚顗為戶部尚書，以兵部侍郎、權判太常卿事崔棁為尚書左丞，以工部侍郎任贊為兵部侍郎，以禮部尚書李懌為刑部尚書，以左丞盧詹為禮部尚書，以左散騎常侍韋勛為工部侍郎。 庚申，廢華清宮為靈泉觀。 辛酉，御史臺奏：「省郎知雜之時，赴臺禮上，軍巡邸吏之輩，咸集公參，赤縣府司，悉呈杖印。 今後年深御史判雜上事，欲依前例。 」從之。 丙寅，以鎮海軍衙內統軍、上直馬步軍都監、檢校太傅、睦州刺史陸仁章為同平章事，遙領遂州武信軍節度使； 以鎮海軍興武左右開道都指揮使、明州刺史仰仁銓為檢校太傅、同平章事，領宣州寧國軍節度使，從錢元瓘之請也。
On the first day of the fifth month (renyin), the emperor held audience in Chongyuan Hall, with guards and ceremonial regalia in full form. On guimao, Left Vice Director Liu Gou was appointed concurrent Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent and enfeoffed as Duke of Qiao. On yisi, Yao Yanzhang, military commissioner of Zhaoshun, died. Fangqu Town in Lingzhou was promoted to Weizhou, subordinate to Lingwu. The former Weizhou was renamed Qingbian Army. On wushen, Ma Xifan, military commissioner of Hunan, was given the additional title Supreme General of the Heavenly Strategies. An Shuqian, former military commissioner of Binzhou, was appointed military commissioner of Cangzhou. On gengxu, Yang Zhaojian, bureau director of the parks, was approved to retain his original office and serve as drafter of edicts. On xinhai, the Jinghai Army was established at Wenzhou at Qian Yuanguan's request. On renzi, Lu Jia, attending censor, was appointed outer office director of revenue and drafter of edicts. Cui Jujian, minister of revenue, died. On jiayin, an edict forbade court officials from soliciting recommendation forms from outer prefectures to nominate relatives by marriage. On yimao, Jinzhou was promoted to military commissioner status under the title Huaide Army. Pan Huan, defense commissioner of Qizhou, was appointed military commissioner of the Huaide Army. Zheng Yunsou, retired Right Remonstrance Grandee, was granted the style Master Xiaoyao and continued to receive retired official salary. On dingsi, Yao Yi was transferred from minister of punishments to minister of revenue; Cui Zhuo from vice minister of war and acting minister of sacrifices to left vice director of the Secretariat; Ren Zan from vice minister of works to vice minister of war; Li Yin from minister of rites to minister of punishments; Lu Zhan from left vice director to minister of rites; and Wei Xun from left regular attendant of the cavalry to vice minister of works. On gengshen, Huaqing Palace was abolished and converted to Lingquan Abbey. On xinyou, the Censorate memorialized: "When a provincial bureau director served as acting chief, on attending the Censorate for ceremonial reception, military patrol station clerks and the like all gathered for public audience, and metropolitan county prefectural officials all presented staffs and seals. Henceforth, when a senior censor acts as chief on affairs, we wish to follow the former precedent." The emperor approved. On bingyin, Lu Renzhang, commander within the Zhenhai Army headquarters, director of the upper direct horse and foot armies, acting grand mentor, and prefect of Muzhou, was appointed associate of the Secretariat and remotely held the military commission of Wuxin at Suizhou; and Yang Renquan, commander of the left and right armies opening the way for Xingwu of the Zhenhai Army and prefect of Mingzhou, was appointed acting grand mentor and associate of the Secretariat, holding the military commission of Ningguo at Xuanzhou, at Qian Yuanguan's request.
8
六月辛未朔，陳郡民王武穿地得黃金數餅，州牧取而貢之。 帝曰：「宿藏之物，既非符寶，不合入官。 」命付所獲之家。 庚辰，西京大風雨，應天福門屋瓦皆飛，鴟吻俱折。 辛卯，詔禮部貢舉宜權停一年。
On the first day of the sixth month (xinwei), Wang Wu of Chen Commandery unearthed several ingots of gold while digging; the prefect took them and presented them as tribute. The emperor said, "Objects long buried in the earth are not imperial talismans or treasures and should not enter the public treasury. He ordered them returned to the household that found them. On gengchen, a great storm struck the Western Capital; at Yingtianfu Gate every roof tile was torn away and every ridge ornament broken. On xinmao, an edict suspended the Ministry of Rites civil service examinations for one year.
9
秋七月庚子朔，日有食之。 西京大水，伊、洛、、澗盡溢，壞天津橋。 癸卯，以華清宮使李頃為右領軍衛上將軍。 甲辰，以定州節度使皇甫遇為潞州節度使、檢校太尉，以潞州節度使侯益為徐州節度使。 戊申，御史中丞薛融等上詳定編敕三百六十八道，分為三十一卷。 是日，詔：「先令天下州郡公私鑄錢，近多鉛錫相兼，缺薄小弱，有違條制。 今後私鑄錢下禁依舊法。 」壬戌，以太子少師梁文矩為太子太保致仕。
On the first day of the seventh month (gengzi), there was a solar eclipse. The Western Capital suffered severe flooding; the Yi, Luo, and Jian rivers all overflowed and destroyed the Tianjin Bridge. On guimao, Li Qing, commissioner of Huaqing Palace, was appointed general of the upper army of the Right Leading Army. On jiachen, Huangfu Yu, military commissioner of Dingzhou, was transferred to Luzhou as acting grand preceptor, and Hou Yi, military commissioner of Luzhou, was transferred to Xuzhou. On wushen, Vice Censor-in-Chief Xue Rong and others submitted the fully revised compiled statutes—368 provisions in 31 fascicles. That same day an edict declared: "An earlier order allowed public and private coin casting throughout the realm, but recently much lead and tin has been mixed in, producing thin, small, weak pieces that violate the regulations. Henceforth private coin casting is forbidden under the old law." On renxu, Liang Wenju, junior preceptor of the heir apparent, was granted retirement as grand guardian of the heir apparent.
10
閏七月庚午朔，百官不入閣，雨沾服故也。 壬申，以中書侍郎平章事、集賢殿大學士桑維翰為檢校司空、兼侍中、相州彰德軍節度使，以彰德軍節度使王庭允為義武軍節度使。 尚書戶部奏：「李自倫義居七世，準敕旌表門閭。 先有鄧州義門王仲昭六代同居，其旌表有廳事步欄，前列屏樹烏頭。 正門閥閱一丈二尺，二柱相去一丈，柱端安瓦桷墨染，號為烏頭。 築雙闕一丈，在烏頭之南三丈七尺。 夾街十有五步，槐柳成列。 今舉此為例，則令式不該。 」詔：「王仲昭正廳烏頭門等制，不載令文，又無敕命，既非故事，難黷大倫，宜從令式，只表門閭。 於李自倫所居之前，量地之宜，高其外門，門外安綽楔。 門外左右各建一臺，高一丈二尺，廣狹方正，稱臺之形，圬以白泥，四隅漆赤。 其行列樹植，隨其事力。 其同籍課役，一準令文。 」壬午，濮州刺史武從諫勒歸私第，受贓十五萬故也。 丁酉，故皇子河南尹重乂妻虢國夫人李氏落發為尼，賜名悟因，仍錫紫衣、法號及夏臘二十。
On the first day of the intercalary seventh month (gengwu), the hundred officials did not enter the Hall of Administration because rain had soaked their court robes. On renshen, Sang Weihan, vice director of the Secretariat, associate of the Secretariat, and grand academician of the Jixian Hall, was appointed acting minister of works and concurrent vice director of the Secretariat, with the military commission of Zhangde at Xiangzhou; Wang Tingyun, military commissioner of Zhangde, was transferred to Yiwu. The Ministry of Revenue memorialized: "Li Zilun has maintained a household of righteousness for seven generations; per the edict his gate and lane are to be marked with commendation. Previously Wang Zhongzhao of Dengzhou, a six-generation communal household, had been commended with a hall, steps, railings, and in front a screen of trees with black-headed gateposts. The main gate bore a commemorative tablet one zhang two chi high; two pillars stood one zhang apart, with tile eaves at the tops painted black—called "black-headed." A paired gate-tower one zhang high was built three zhang seven chi south of the black-headed gate. Locust and willow trees were planted in rows fifteen paces along either side of the street. If this is taken as precedent, the regulations do not cover it." An edict stated: "Wang Zhongzhao's main hall, black-headed gate, and similar features are not recorded in the statutes and lack an edictal command; since they are not precedent, they would profane great propriety—follow the regulations and mark only the gate and lane. Before Li Zilun's dwelling, the ground should be measured appropriately to raise the outer gate, with a commemorative frame installed outside it. On the left and right outside the gate, build one platform each, one zhang two chi high, square in proportion, plastered with white clay with the four corners painted red. Rows of planted trees should follow the household's means. Corvée and tax obligations of the same register follow the statutes entirely." On renwu, Wu Congjian, prefect of Pu, was compelled to return to private life for accepting bribes of 150,000 cash. On dingyou, Lady Li, wife of the late Prince Chongyi of Henan and Princess of Guo, took the tonsure as a nun; she was granted the name Wuyin along with purple robes, a dharma name, and twenty summer years of ordination.
11
八月己亥朔，河決博平，甘陵大水。 辛丑，以守司空兼門下侍郎、平章事、宏文館大學士馮道為守司徒、兼侍中，封魯國公。 壬寅，詔曰：「皇圖革故，庶政惟新，宜設規程，以諧公共。 其中書印只委上位宰臣一人知當。 」戊申，前兵部尚書王權授太子少傅致仕。 己酉，以天下兵馬副元帥、鎮海鎮東等軍節度使、檢校大師、行中書令、吳越王錢元瓘為天下兵馬元帥。 壬子，升亳州為防禦使額，依舊隸宋州。 丙辰，司天監馬重績等進所撰新歷。 降詔褒之，詔翰林學士承旨和凝制序，命之曰《調元曆》。
On the first day of the eighth month (jihai), the Yellow River broke its banks at Boping, and Ganling suffered severe flooding. On xinchou, Feng Dao, acting minister of works and concurrent vice director of the Secretariat, associate of the Secretariat, and grand academician of the Hongwen Hall, was appointed acting minister of education and concurrent vice director of the Secretariat and enfeoffed as Duke of Lu. On renyin, an edict declared: "The imperial design reforms the old and all government must be renewed—regulations should be established to harmonize public affairs. The Secretariat seal is entrusted to one senior minister alone to manage." On wushen, Wang Quan, former minister of war, was granted retirement as junior tutor of the heir apparent. On jiyou, Qian Yuanguan, vice commander-in-chief of all forces, military commissioner of Zhenhai and Zhendong and other armies, acting grand mentor, acting director of the Secretariat, and King of Wu and Yue, was appointed commander-in-chief of all forces under heaven. On renzi, Bozhou was promoted to defense commissioner status, remaining subordinate to Songzhou as before. On bingchen, Ma Chongji of the Directorate of Astronomy and others presented the new calendar they had compiled. An edict commended them; Hanlin expositor-in-chief He Ning was ordered to compose a preface, and the work was titled the Calendar for Harmonizing the Origin.
12
九月辛未，以右羽林統軍周密為鄜州節度使。 癸酉，升婺州為武勝軍額。 丁丑，宴群臣於永福殿。 契丹使訥默庫來聘， 〈 （《遼史》：會同二年正月戊申，晉遣金吾衛大將軍馬從斌、考功郎中劉知新來貢珍幣。 丙辰，晉遣使謝免沿邊四州錢幣。 七月戊申，晉遣使進犀帶。 閏月乙酉，遣使賜晉良馬。 八月己丑，晉遣使貢歲幣，奏輸戌、亥二歲金幣於燕京。）〉 致牛馬等物。 己卯，遙領洮州保順軍節度使鮑君福加檢校太師、兼侍中，判湖州諸軍事。 辛巳，相州節度使桑維翰上言：「管內所獲賊人，從來籍沒財產，請止之。 」詔：「今後凡有賊人，準格律定罪，不得沒納家資，天下諸州準此。 」癸未，封唐許王李從益為郇國公，奉唐之祀，服色旌旗一依舊制。 仍以西京至德宮為廟，牲幣器服悉從官給。 〈 （《五代會要》：九月，敕：「周受龍圖，立夏、殷之祀； 唐膺鳳歷，開酅、介之封。 乃眷前朝，載稽舊典，宜封土宇，俾奉宗祧。 宜以郇國三千戶封唐許王李從益為郇國公」云。）〉 丙戌，高麗王王建遣使貢方物。 己丑，以中書侍郎、平章事李崧權判集賢殿事。 庚寅，詔停寒食、七夕、重陽及十月暖帳內外群官貢獻。 丙申，以威勝軍節度副使羅周嶽為給事中，中書舍人李詳改禮部侍郎，禮部侍郎呂琦改刑部侍郎，刑部侍郎王松改戶部侍郎，戶部侍郎閻至改兵部侍郎，中書舍人王易簡充史館修撰，判館事。
On xinwei, Zhou Mi, commander of the Right Forest Feathered Army, was appointed military commissioner of Binzhou. On guiyou, Wuzhou was promoted to the Wusheng Army commissioner title. On dingchou, the emperor banqueted the officials in Yongfu Hall. The Khitan envoy Nemoku came on a friendly mission, [ (The 《History of Liao》 records: In the first month of Huitong 2, on wushen, Jin sent Grand General of the Golden Crow Guard Ma Congbin and Director of Merit Evaluation Liu Zhixin to present precious tribute. On bingchen, Jin sent envoys to thank for exemption from border tribute coin from four prefectures. On wushen in the seventh month, Jin sent envoys presenting rhinoceros-hide belts. On yiyou of the intercalary month, envoys were sent to bestow fine horses on Jin. On jichou in the eighth month, Jin sent envoys with the annual tribute, reporting delivery of gold and goods for the xu and hai years to Yanjing.)〉 presenting cattle, horses, and other goods. On jimao, Bao Junfu, who remotely held the military commission of Baoshun at Taozhou, was given the additional titles acting grand mentor and concurrent vice director of the Secretariat and assigned to judge military affairs at Huzhou. On xinsi, Sang Weihan, military commissioner of Xiangzhou, memorialized: "Within my jurisdiction, property of captured bandits has heretofore been confiscated—please stop this." An edict stated: "Henceforth whenever there are bandits, sentence according to the statutes and regulations; household assets must not be confiscated—all prefectures throughout the realm follow this." On guimao, Li Congyi, Prince of Xu of Tang, was enfeoffed as Duke of Xun to maintain Tang sacrifices, with dress colors and banners according to the old system. The Zhide Palace in the Western Capital was made his temple; sacrificial animals, vessels, and robes were all supplied by the government. [ In the ninth month an edict in the 《Five Dynasties Essentials》 reads: "Zhou received the dragon chart and established the sacrifices of Xia and Yin; Tang received the phoenix mandate and opened the fiefs of Zhe and Jie. Cherishing the former dynasty and examining old canons, it is fitting to enfeoff territory and enable them to maintain the ancestral temple. Li Congyi, Prince of Xu of Tang, with three thousand households of Xun, is fittingly enfeoffed as Duke of Xun.")〉 On bingxu, Wang Jian, king of Goryeo, sent envoys presenting local products. On jichou, Li Song, vice director of the Secretariat and associate of the Secretariat, was appointed acting director of the Jixian Hall. On gengyin, an edict suspended Cold Food, Qixi, Double Ninth, and tenth-month warm-pavilion tribute offerings by internal and external officials. On bingshen, Luo Zhouyue, deputy military commissioner of Wusheng, was appointed supervising secretary; Li Xiang, secretariat drafter, was transferred to vice minister of rites; Lü Qi from rites to punishments; Wang Song from punishments to revenue; Yan Zhi from revenue to war; and Wang Yijian, secretariat drafter, was made compiler of the Historiography Office and acting director.
13
冬十月戊戌朔，故昭信軍節度使白奉進贈太尉。 丙午，以太常卿程遜沒於海，廢朝一日，贈右僕射。 庚戌，閩王王昶、威武軍節度使王繼恭遣僚佐林思、鄭元弼等朝貢，致書於宰執，無人臣之禮。 帝怒，詔令不受所貢，應諸州綱運，並令林思、鄭元弼等押歸本道。 既而兵部員外郎李知損上疏，請禁錮使人，籍沒綱運。 可之，收林思等下獄。 丙辰，谿州刺史彭士愁，以錦、獎之兵與蠻部萬人掠辰、澧二境，湖南節度使馬希範遣牙兵拒之而退。 金州山賊度從讜等寇洵陽，遣兵討平之。
On the first day of the tenth month (wuxu), Bai Fengjin, former military commissioner of Zhaoxin, was posthumously granted the title grand marshal. On bingwu, Cheng Xun, minister of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices, perished at sea; the court suspended audiences for one day and posthumously granted him right vice director. On gengxu, Min King Wang Chang and Wang Jigong, military commissioner of Weiwu, sent subordinates Lin Si and Zheng Yuankui and others with tribute; their letter to the chief ministers lacked subject etiquette. The emperor was angry and ordered their tribute refused; all transport quotas from the relevant prefectures were to have Lin Si, Zheng Yuankui, and the others escort the goods back to their circuit. Thereafter Li Zhishun, outer office director of war, memorialized requesting that the envoys be confined and transport goods confiscated. The emperor approved; Lin Si and the others were arrested and imprisoned. On bingchen, Peng Shichou, prefect of Xi, with Jin and Jiang troops and ten thousand men of barbarian tribes raided the borders of Chen and Li; Ma Xifan, military commissioner of Hunan, dispatched troops to resist and they withdrew. Mountain bandits at Jinzhou led by Du Congzan and others raided Xunyang; troops were sent to suppress and pacify them.
14
十一月甲戌，以太子賓客李延範為司農卿。 乙亥，詔立唐高祖、太宗及莊宗、明宗、閔帝五廟於洛陽。 丁丑，祠部郎中、知制誥吳承範改中書舍人，充翰林學士； 翰林學士、中書舍人竇貞固改御史中丞； 御史中丞薛融改尚書左丞； 尚書右丞王延改吏部侍郎； 尚書左丞崔棁改太常卿。 戊寅，史館奏：「請令宰臣一人撰錄時政記，逐時以備撰述。 」從之。 〈 （《五代會要》：史館奏：「唐長壽二年，右丞姚璹奏，帝王謨訓，不可闕文。 其仗下所言軍國政事，令宰臣一人撰錄，號『時政記』。 唐明宗朝，又委端明殿學士撰錄，逐季送付史館，伏乞遵行者。 宜令宰臣一員撰述。」）〉 己卯，吏部侍郎龍敏改尚書左丞。 己丑，以太子賓客楊凝式為禮部尚書致仕。 詔建錢爐於欒川。 丙申，諫議大夫致仕逍遙先生鄭雲叟卒。
On jiaxu of the eleventh month, Li Yanfan, guest of the heir apparent, was appointed minister of the Court of the Imperial Granaries. On yihai, an edict established temples to Tang Gaozu, Taizong, Zhuangzong, Mingzong, and Emperor Min at Luoyang. On dingchou, Wu Chengfang, bureau director of rites and drafter of edicts, was appointed secretariat drafter and Hanlin academician; Dou Zhengu, Hanlin academician and secretariat drafter, was transferred to vice censor-in-chief; Xue Rong, vice censor-in-chief, was transferred to left vice director of the Imperial Secretariat; Wang Yan, right vice director, was transferred to vice minister of personnel; Cui Zhuo, left vice director, was appointed minister of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices. On wuyin, the Historiography Office memorialized: "We request that one chief minister compile the Current Affairs Record season by season to supply materials for compilation." The emperor approved. [ (The 《Five Dynasties Essentials》 records: The Historiography Office memorialized: "In Changshou 2 of Tang, Right Vice Director Yao Shanzhi memorialized that the emperor's counsels and instructions must not lack written record. Military and state affairs spoken below the halberds should have one chief minister compile them, titled 'Current Affairs Record.' In the Mingzong reign, a Duanming Hall academician was again entrusted to compile and deliver each season to the Historiography Office; we humbly request that this be followed. One chief minister should be ordered to compile.")〉 On jimao, Long Min, vice minister of personnel, was transferred to left vice director of the Imperial Secretariat. On jichou, Yang Ningshi, guest of the heir apparent, was granted retirement as minister of rites. An edict ordered coin furnaces established at Luanchuan. On bingshen, Zheng Yunsou, retired remonstrance grandee and Master Xiaoyao, died.
15
十二月丁酉朔，百官不入閣，大雪故也。 己亥，故皇子重英妻張氏落發為尼，賜名悟慎，並夏臘二十。 庚戌，禮官奏：「來歲正旦，王公上壽，皇帝舉酒，奏《元同之樂》； 再飲，奏《文同之樂》； 三飲，奏同前。 」從之。 歌辭不錄。 丙辰，詔今後城郭村坊，不得創造僧尼院舍。 丁巳，帝謂宰臣曰：「大雪害民，五旬未止。 京城祠廟，悉令祈禱，了無其驗。 豈非涼德不儲，神休未洽者乎？ 」因令出薪炭米粟給軍士貧民等。 壬戌，禮官奏：「正旦上壽，宮懸歌舞未全，且請雜用九部雅樂，歌教坊法曲。 」從之。
On the first day of the twelfth month (dingyou), the hundred officials did not enter the Hall of Administration because of heavy snow. On jihai, Lady Zhang, wife of the late Prince Chongying, took the tonsure as a nun; she was granted the name Wushen and twenty summer years of ordination as well. On gengxu, the ritual officials memorialized: "For next year's New Year's Day, when princes and dukes offer longevity wishes and the emperor raises his cup, play the music 'Perfect Unity'; at the second cup, play 'Cultured Unity'; at the third cup, play the same as before." The emperor approved. The song texts are not recorded. On bingchen, an edict forbade the creation of new Buddhist and Daoist monastic compounds in walled cities, villages, and wards. On dingsi, the emperor said to the chief ministers: "The great snow harms the people and has not stopped for fifty days. Shrines and temples in the capital have all been ordered to pray, yet there is utterly no effect. Is it not that my modest virtue is insufficient and the divine blessing has not been attained? He therefore ordered fuel, charcoal, rice, and grain distributed to soldiers and the poor. On renxu, the ritual officials memorialized: "For the New Year's longevity rites, the palace suspended music and dance are incomplete—please provisionally mix the nine departments of elegant music with court music teaching-house standard tunes." The emperor approved.
