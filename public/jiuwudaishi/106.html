王周，魏州人。 少勇健，從軍事唐莊宗、明宗，稍遷裨校，以戰功累歷郡守。 晉天福初，範延光叛於魏州，周從楊光遠攻降之，安重榮以鎮州叛，從杜重威討平之，以功授貝州節度使。 歲餘，移鎮涇州。 先是，前帥張彥澤在任苛虐，部民逃者五千餘戶，及下車，革前弊二十餘事，逃民歸復，賜詔褒美。 後歷鄧、陜二鎮。 陽城之役，周時為定州節度使，大軍往來，供饋無闕，未幾，遷鎮州節度使。 周稟性寬惠，人庶便之。 開運末，杜重威降於契丹，引契丹主臨城諭之。 周泣曰：「受國重恩，不能死戰，而以兵降，何面南行見人主與士大夫乎？ 」乃痛飲欲引決，家人止之。 事不獲已，乃見契丹主，授鄧州節度使、檢校太師。 高祖定天下，移鎮徐州，加同平章事。 乾祐元年二月，以疾卒於鎮，輟視朝二日，贈中書令。 周性寬恕，不忤物情。 初刺信都，州城西橋敗，覆民租車，周曰：「橋梁不飭，刺史之過也。 」乃還其所沈粟，出私財以修之，民庶悅焉。
Wang Zhou came from Weizhou. As a young man he was bold and sturdy. He soldiered under Tang Zhuangzong and Mingzong, rose step by step through the officer ranks, and earned a series of prefectural posts on the strength of his battlefield record. Early in the Jin Tianfu reign, Fan Yanguang rebelled at Weizhou. Zhou marched with Yang Guangyuan to crush him, then joined Du Chongwei against An Chongrong's revolt at Zhenzhou. For these services he was made military governor of Beizhou. A little over a year later he was reassigned to Jingzhou. His predecessor Zhang Yanzhe had ruled with harsh cruelty, driving more than five thousand households to flee. When Zhou arrived he abolished more than twenty abuses of the old regime; the refugees came back, and the court issued an edict of praise. He later held governorships in Deng and Shan in turn. During the Yangcheng campaign Zhou was military governor of Dingzhou. He kept the main armies supplied without fail and was soon moved to Zhenzhou. Zhou was by nature mild and generous, and the common people benefited from his rule. Late in the Kaiyun reign Du Chongwei surrendered to the Khitan and brought the Khitan ruler before the walls to address the garrison. Zhou wept and said, "The state loaded me with favor, yet I could not die in battle and instead surrender my troops—how can I face south to meet the emperor and the scholar-officials?" " He drank himself into a stupor and tried to kill himself, but his household stopped him. Left with no choice, he went before the Khitan ruler and was given Dengzhou and the honorary title of Grand Preceptor. After Gaozu secured the empire, Zhou was moved to Xuzhou and made associate director of the Secretariat-Chancellery. In the second month of Qianyou 1 he died of illness in office. The court halted audiences for two days and posthumously made him Director of the Secretariat. Zhou was forgiving by nature and never needlessly offended others. On his first appointment at Xindu the city bridge on the west side collapsed and capsized carts carrying the people's tax grain. Zhou said, "A bridge in disrepair is the prefect's fault." He returned the sunk grain to the owners, paid for repairs out of his own purse, and won the people's approval.

劉審交，字求益，幽州文安人也。 祖海，父師遂。 審交少讀書，尤精吏道，起家署北平主簿，轉興唐令，本府召補牙職。 劉守光之僭號，偽署兵部尚書，燕亡，歸於太原。 莊宗知之，用為諸府從事。 同光初，趙德鈞鎮幽州，朝廷以內官馬紹宏為北面轉運使，辟審交為判官。 王都據定州叛，朝廷命王晏球進討，以審交為轉運供軍使，王都平，以勞授遼州刺史。 明年，復為北面供軍轉運使，改磁州刺史，以母年高，去官就養。 及丁內艱，毀瘠過禮，服闋，不出累年。 晉高祖踐阼，範延光以魏州叛，命楊光遠以總兵討之，復召審交為供饋使。 鄴中平，命審交為三司使，授右衛大將軍。 六年夏，出為陳州防禦使，歲餘，移襄州防禦使。 審交治襄、漢，撫綏有術，民庶懷之。 青州楊光遠平，降平盧軍為防禦州，復用審交為防禦使，累官至檢校太傅。 時用軍之後，審交矜恤撫理，雕弊復蘇。 契丹破晉，審交以代歸，蕭翰在都，復用為三司使。 翰歸蕃，李從益在汴州，召高行周、武行德將委以軍事，皆不受命。 尋聞高祖起義於太原，史宏肇在澤潞，都人大懼。 時有燕軍千人守捉諸門，李從益母王淑妃詢於文武臣僚曰：「予子母在洛，孤危自處，一旦為蕭翰所逼，致令及此。 但遣人迎請太原，勿以予子母為事。 」或曰：「收拾諸處守營兵士與燕軍，足以把城，以俟河北救應可也。 」妃曰：「非謀也，我子母亡國之餘，安敢與人爭天下！ 」眾議籍籍，猶以把城為詞。 審交曰：「余燕人也，今城有燕軍，固合為燕謀，然事機有所不可。 此城經敵軍破除之後，民力空匱，餘眾幸存，若更謀之不臧，閉門拒守，一月之內，無復遺類。 諸君勿言，宜從太妃處分。 」繇是從益遣使往太原貢奉。 高祖至汴，罷使歸班。 隱帝嗣位，用為汝州防禦使。 汝為近輔，號為難治，審交盡去煩弊，無擾於民，百姓歌之。
Liu Shenjiao, courtesy name Qiuyi, came from Wen'an in Youzhou. His grandfather was Liu Hai and his father Liu Shisui. Shenjiao read widely in his youth and excelled at official routine. He began as secretary at Beiping, became magistrate of Xingtang, and was then called to a staff post at the circuit headquarters. When Liu Shouguang declared himself emperor, Shenjiao was given the false title of Minister of War. After Yan fell he went back to Taiyuan. Zhuangzong knew his ability and used him on the staffs of several bureaus. Early in Tongguang, Zhao Dejun governed Youzhou. The court sent the eunuch Ma Shaohong as north-front transport commissioner and recruited Shenjiao as his deputy. When Wang Du rebelled at Dingzhou the court sent Wang Yanqiu to suppress him, with Shenjiao as army transport and supply commissioner. After Dingzhou was pacified he was rewarded with the prefecture of Lingzhou. The following year he again served as north-front supply commissioner, then became prefect of Ci. When his mother grew old he resigned to care for her at home. During mourning for his mother he grieved beyond what ritual required. When the mourning ended he stayed secluded for years. When Jin Gaozu took the throne, Fan Yanguang rebelled at Weizhou. Yang Guangyuan was put in overall command to crush the revolt, and Shenjiao was again called up as supply commissioner. After Yezhong fell he was made Commissioner of the Three Departments and General of the Right Guard. In the summer of the sixth year he left the capital to defend Chenzhou. A year later he was transferred to Xiangzhou. In Xiang and Han he governed with a gift for reassurance and settlement, and the people held him in affection. After Yang Guangyuan's defeat at Qingzhou, Pinglu was reduced to a defense command. Shenjiao was again made defender there and eventually rose to honorary Grand Tutor. In the aftermath of war he governed with compassionate care, and ruined districts began to recover. When the Khitan overthrew Jin, Shenjiao came back from the north. Xiao Han held the capital and again made him Commissioner of the Three Departments. When Xiao Han went back to his people, Li Congyi was at Bian. He tried to put Gao Xingzhou and Wu Xingde in charge of the army, but neither would accept. Word soon came that Gaozu had risen at Taiyuan while Shi Hongzhao held Ze and Lu. The capital was gripped by fear. A thousand Yan troops then held the gates. Li Congyi's mother, Consort Wang Shufei, asked the civil and military officials, "My son and I are stranded in Luoyang, helpless and exposed. Xiao Han has forced us into this plight. Send envoys to welcome Taiyuan and do not make my son and me your concern." One man said, "Rally the garrison troops everywhere with the Yan force—we can hold the city and wait for help from Hebei." The consort replied, "That is no plan. My son and I are all that is left of a ruined house—how dare we fight others for the empire!" The assembly buzzed with argument, many still insisting on defending the walls. Shenjiao said, "I am a Yan man myself, and with Yan troops in the city it would seem right to plot for Yan. Yet circumstances forbid it. The enemy has already smashed this city and drained its people. Only a remnant survives. Another ill-judged plan to bar the gates and resist will, within a month, leave no one alive. Say no more, gentlemen. Do as the consort directs." On this advice Congyi sent envoys to Taiyuan with tribute. When Gaozu reached Bian, Shenjiao was relieved of his special commission and returned to ordinary service. Under Emperor Yin he was appointed defender of Ruzhou. Ru lay close to the capital and had a reputation for being unruly. Shenjiao swept away vexatious abuses, left the people unmolested, and they sang his praises.

乾祐二年春，卒，年七十四。 郡人聚哭柩前所，列狀乞留葬本州界，立碑起祠，以時致祭。 本州以聞，詔曰：「朝廷之制，皆有舊章，牧守之官，比無贈典。 其或政能殊異，惠及蒸黎，生有令名，沒留遺愛，褒賢獎善，豈限彜章。 可特贈太尉，吏民所請宜依。 」故相國、太師、秦國公馮道聞之曰：「予嘗為劉汝州僚佐，知其為人廉平慈善，無害之良吏也。 刺遼、磁，治陳、襄、青，皆稱平允，不顯殊尤，其理汝也，又安有異哉！ 民之租賦不能減也，徭役不能息也，寒者不能衣也，餒者不能食也，百姓自汲汲然，而使君何有於我哉！ 然身死之日，致黎民懷感如此者，誠以不行鞭樸，不行刻剝，不因公而循私，不害物以利己，確然行良吏之事，薄罰宥過，謹身節用，安俸祿、守禮分而已。 凡從事於斯者，孰不能乎！ 但前之守土者不能如是，是以汝民咨嗟愛慕。 今天下戎馬之後，四方兇盜之餘，杼柚空而賦斂繁，人民稀而倉稟匱，謂之康泰，未易輕言。 侯伯牧宰，若能哀矜之，不至聚斂，不殺無辜之民，民為邦本，政為民本，和平寬易，即劉君之政安足稱耶！ 復河患不至於令名哉！ 」道仍為著哀詞六章，鐫於墓碑之陰焉。
In the spring of Qianyou 2 he died at seventy-four. The commandery's people wept before his coffin and petitioned to bury him within their borders, erect a stele, build a shrine, and offer seasonal sacrifices. The prefecture reported upward. An edict said, "The court's rules all rest on ancient precedent, and prefects and governors are not usually honored after death. Yet when a man's rule is truly exceptional, his kindness reaches the people, his name in life is bright and his love survives him, to praise worth and reward goodness cannot be bound by ordinary statute." Let him be specially posthumously made Grand Marshal, and grant what the officials and people ask." The former chief minister and Grand Preceptor Feng Dao, Duke of Qin, said on hearing the news, "I once served under Liu of Ruzhou and knew him as an honest, fair, and kindly man—a harmless magistrate of the best sort. As prefect of Ling and Ci and as defender of Chen, Xiang, and Qing he was always called even-handed, without flashy distinction. His rule at Ru was no different. Peasant rents could not be cut, labor levies could not cease, the cold went unclothed and the hungry unfed. The people hurried about their own affairs—what did the magistrate do for us!" Yet at his death the people mourned him so warmly because he never wielded the lash or practiced harsh levies, never bent public duty to private gain or harmed others for his own profit, but simply did what a good official does: light penalties, mercy for faults, restraint, thrift, living on his salary and keeping to his station. Whoever holds such an office cannot do the same?" Only earlier governors had failed to do so, and that is why the people of Ru sighed and cherished his memory. Now, after armies have trampled the empire and robbers still haunt the four quarters, looms stand idle while taxes press hard, people are few and storehouses bare—to speak lightly of peace and plenty is impossible. If regional lords and governors can pity the people, refrain from crushing levies, and spare the innocent—making the people the root of the state and government the root of the people, with peace, leniency, and ease—then is Liu Jun's rule even worth mentioning!" Would he not win a great name simply by keeping the Yellow River from harming the people!" Feng Dao also wrote six stanzas of mourning verse and had them cut into the back of the tomb stele.

武漢球，澤州人也。 少拳勇，潞帥李嗣昭倚為親信，事唐莊宗、明宗，繼為禁軍裨校。 清泰中，會晉高祖引契丹為援，與朝廷隔絕，遂歸晉祖。 天福初，授趙州刺史，入為奉國軍都指揮使，出刺曹州。 開運初，遷耀州團練使。 高祖至東京，授洺州刺史，漢球以目疾年高辭郡，帝曰：「廣平小郡，卿臥理有餘，無以疾辭。 」至郡未期，復以目疾請代而免。 乾祐二年秋，卒於京師。 漢球雖出自行伍，然長於撫理，常以掊斂為戒，民懷其惠，身死之日，家無餘財。 有管回者，漢球守郡日，辟為判官。 及漢球卒於汴，回在洺州未之知，一日，忽謂所親曰：「太保遣人召我。 」遂沐浴，新衣冠，無疾瞑目而終。 家人不知其故，後數日，方聞漢球卒。
Wu Hanqiu came from Zezhou. As a youth he was strong in boxing and boldness. Li Sizhao of Lu made him a trusted confidant. He served Zhuangzong and Mingzong and rose through the ranks of the palace guard. During Qingtai, when Jin Gaozu allied with the Khitan and was cut off from the Later Tang court, Hanqiu went over to him. Early in Tianfu he became prefect of Zhao, then commander of the Fengguo Army in the capital, then governor of Cao. At the start of Kaiyun he was transferred to defender of Yao. When Gaozu reached the Eastern Capital he made him prefect of Luo. Hanqiu pleaded eye trouble and old age. The emperor said, "Guangping is a small prefecture—you can govern it from your couch. Do not refuse because of illness." Before his term was out he again asked to be relieved for eye trouble and was excused. In the autumn of Qianyou 2 he died in the capital. Though he came from the ranks, Hanqiu excelled at humane governance and shunned harsh levies. The people loved him, and when he died his house held no spare wealth. One Guan Hui had served as Hanqiu's deputy while he held the prefecture. When Hanqiu died at Bian, Hui was still at Luo and knew nothing. One day he suddenly told his intimates, "The Grand Guardian has sent for me." He bathed, dressed in fresh robes, and died peacefully without illness. His family did not understand why until, days later, they heard that Hanqiu was dead.

張瓘，同州車渡村人，故太原監軍使承業之猶子也。 承業，《唐書》有傳。 唐天祐中，承業佐唐武皇、莊宗有功，甚見委遇，瓘聞之，與昆仲五人，自故裏奔於太原，莊宗皆任用之。 瓘天祐十三年補麟州刺史。 承業治家嚴毅，小過無所容恕，一侄為磁州副使，以其殺河西賣羊客，承業立捕斬之。 常誡瓘等曰：「汝車渡村百姓劉開道下賊，慣作非為，今須改行，若故態不除，死無日矣。 」故瓘所至不敢誅求。 晉天福中，為密州刺史，秩滿入居環衛。 乾祐三年夏，卒於官，輟視朝一日。
Zhang Guan came from Chedu village in Tongzhou. He was a nephew by marriage of the former Taiyuan military supervisor Zhang Chengye. Chengye has a biography in the Tang History. Under Tianyou, Chengye aided Tang Wuhuang and Zhuangzong with great merit and won deep trust. When Guan heard of this, he and five brothers left home together for Taiyuan, and Zhuangzong gave them all posts. In Tianyou 13 Guan was appointed prefect of Lin. Chengye ran his household with iron discipline and forgave no small fault. A nephew serving as deputy at Ci killed a Hexi sheep trader; Chengye had him seized and beheaded at once. He often warned Guan and his brothers, "You Chedu people followed the bandit Liu Kaidao in wickedness and are used to wrongdoing. Reform now—if you do not drop your old ways, death is near." For that reason Guan never dared to squeeze the districts he governed. Under Jin Tianfu he governed Mi. When his term ended he entered the palace guard. In the summer of Qianyou 3 he died in office, and audiences were halted for a day.

李殷，薊州人也。 自後唐莊宗、明宗、晉高祖朝，以偏校遞遷，歷官至檢校司徒，累為郡守。 性沈厚，所蒞無苛暴之名。 晉少帝御契丹於澶淵，殷典禁旅，駕還，授鄜州留後，俄加檢校太保。 開運中，授定州節度使，將行，啟少帝曰：「臣之此行，破敵必矣。 」眾皆壯其言，及至郡，威略無聞，敵再至，首納降款。 後隨契丹至常山，其將解裏遣殷與契丹首領楊安，同拒我師於洺水，俄而安退，殷以橐裝駝馬遺安。 安既北走，殷匿於丘墓獲免，馳以歸我。 高祖嘉其首赴朝闕，及魏州平，以甘陵乏帥，乃命殷為貝州節度使，加檢校太傅。 乾祐初，卒於鎮。 詔贈太師。
Li Yin came from Jizhou. From Zhuangzong and Mingzong of Later Tang through Jin Gaozu he rose from junior officer to honorary Minister of Works, holding one prefecture after another. He was sober and steady by nature, and none of his postings earned a reputation for cruelty. When the Jin emperor faced the Khitan at Chanyuan, Yin commanded the palace guard. After the emperor returned he was made acting governor of Yan and soon honorary Grand Guardian. In Kaiyun he was made military governor of Dingzhou. Before leaving he told Shaodi, "On this campaign I am sure to break the enemy." Everyone cheered his boast. At his post he showed neither prestige nor strategy. When the enemy returned he was the first to offer surrender. Later he followed the Khitan to Changshan. The general Jieli sent Yin with the Khitan leader Yang An to block our army at the Luo River. Soon An retreated, and Yin gave him baggage camels and horses as gifts. When An fled north, Yin hid among graves and survived, then rode back to our side. Gaozu praised him as the first to reach the court. After Weizhou was pacified, with Ganling lacking a commander he made Yin military governor of Beizhou and honorary Grand Tutor. Early in Qianyou he died in office. The court posthumously made him Grand Preceptor.

劉在明，幽州人。 少有膽氣，本州節度使劉守光用為親信，出為平塞軍使。 守光敗，歸於太原，唐莊宗收為列校。 明宗時，為捧聖左廂都指揮使，領和州刺史。 從幸汴州，至滎陽，聞朱守殷叛，用為前鋒。 至汴城，率先登城，賊平，授汴州馬步軍都指揮使。 應順初，為貝州刺史。 明年，移趙州，兼北面行營馬軍都指揮使，以軍戍易州。 清泰末，幽州節度使趙德鈞引軍赴團柏谷，路由易州，取在明軍從。 及德鈞兵敗，在明奔歸懷州，唐末帝令與萇從簡同守河陽。 晉祖至，乃迎之，京都事定，出為單州刺史。 天福中，李金全以安州叛，在明從李守貞攻之，大破淮賊，以功授安州防禦使，明年，移絳州。 楊光遠據青州叛，召為行營馬步軍都指揮使，領齊州防禦使。 青州平，遷相州留後，歷邢州、晉州留後。 〈 （《通鑒》：契丹犯闕，建雄留後劉在明朝於契丹，以節度副使駱知朗知州事。）〉 高祖踐阼，授幽州道行營都部署。 時契丹守中山，在明出師經略，契丹乃棄城而去，遂授鎮州留後。 乾祐元年五月，正授鎮州節度使。 六月，以疾卒於鎮。 贈侍中。
Liu Zaiming came from Youzhou. As a youth he was bold. Liu Shouguang of the circuit made him a confidant and sent him out to command the Pingsai army. When Shouguang fell, Zaiming went to Taiyuan and Zhuangzong enrolled him among the commanders. Under Mingzong he commanded the left wing of the Palsheng Guard and also served as prefect of He. On an imperial visit to Bian, at Xingyang he heard that Zhu Shouyin had rebelled and was made vanguard. At Bian he was first over the wall. After the rebels were crushed he became commander of Bian's horse and foot forces. Early in Yingshun he was appointed prefect of Bei. The following year he was transferred to Zhao, made north-front horse-army commander, and stationed his troops at Yi. Late in Qingtai, Zhao Dejun of Youzhou marched to Tuanbogu and, passing Yi, took Zaiming's force with him. When Dejun was beaten, Zaiming fled to Huai. The last Tang emperor ordered him and Chang Congjian to hold Heyang. When the Jin founder came they welcomed him in. After the capital was secure he was sent out as prefect of Shan. During Tianfu, Li Jinquan rebelled at An. Zaiming followed Li Shouzhen, routed the Huai rebels, and was made defender of An, then moved to Jiang the next year. When Yang Guangyuan rebelled at Qingzhou, Zaiming was called up as camp horse-and-foot commander and defender of Qi. (Zizhi Tongjian: When the Khitan invaded the capital, Liu Zaiming of Jianxiong submitted to them and put Deputy Military Governor Luo Zhilang in charge of the prefecture.) When Gaozu took the throne he made Zaiming overall commander on the Youzhou front. The Khitan then held Zhongshan. Zaiming marched against them, the Khitan abandoned the city, and he was made acting governor of Zhen. In the fifth month of Qianyou 1 he was formally made military governor of Zhen. In the sixth month he died of illness in office. He was posthumously made Palace Attendant.

馬萬，澶州人也。 少從軍，善水遊。 唐莊宗與梁軍對壘於河上，莊宗於德勝渡夾河立南北寨。 會梁軍急攻南寨，於中流聯戰艦以絕援路，晝夜攻城者三日，寨將氏延賞告急於莊宗。 莊宗隔河望敵，無如之何，乃召人能水遊破賊者。 時萬兄弟皆應募，遂潛行入南寨，往來者三，又助燒船艦，汴軍遂退。 由此升為水軍小校，漸典禁軍，遙領刺史，累遷奉國左廂都指揮使、泗州防禦使。 晉天福二年夏，範延光叛於鄴，牙將孫銳率兵至黎陽，朝廷遣侍衛馬軍都指揮使白奉進領兵渡滑州，萬亦預其行。 時滑州節度使符彥饒潛通鄴下，殺白奉進於牙署。 萬領本軍兵士將助亂，會奉國右廂都指揮使盧順密亦以兵至，諭以逆順，萬不得已，與順密急趨公府，執彥饒生送闕下。 朝廷即以萬為滑州節度使，而盧順密酬之甚淺。 居無何，晉高祖稍知其事，即以順密為涇州兵馬留後，漸薄於萬。 萬鎮鄧州，未幾罷鎮，授上將軍，以目疾致仕。 乾祐三年四月卒。 輟視朝一日。
Ma Wan came from Cangzhou. As a youth he joined the army and was an expert swimmer. Zhuangzong faced the Liang army on the Yellow River and built paired north and south camps at Desheng Ford. The Liang pressed the south camp hard, chained warships in the stream to block relief, and assaulted it day and night for three days. Commander Shi Yanshang sent word to Zhuangzong. Zhuangzong watched the enemy from the far bank, helpless, and called for anyone who could swim through and break them. Wan and his brothers volunteered. They swam into the south camp three times, helped burn the Liang ships, and the Bian army withdrew. For this he rose in the water forces, entered the palace guard, held titular prefectures, and eventually commanded the left wing of the Fengguo Guard while defending Si. In the summer of Jin Tianfu 2, Fan Yanguang rebelled at Ye. Sun Rui marched to Liyang. The court sent Bai Fengjin across Huazhou, and Wan went with him. The Huazhou governor Fu Yanrao was secretly in league with Ye and murdered Bai Fengjin in headquarters. Wan was ready to join the mutiny when Lu Shunmi of the Fengguo right wing arrived, argued duty and treason, and forced Wan to rush with him to the government compound, seize Yanrao alive, and send him to court. The court made Wan military governor of Hua, but rewarded Shunmi meagerly. Soon Gaozu learned what had happened, gave Shunmi the Jingzhou army post, and began to slight Wan. Wan governed Deng, was soon removed, made Senior General, and retired for eye trouble. In the fourth month of Qianyou 3 he died. Audiences were halted for a day.

李彥從，字士元，汾州孝義人。 父德，麟州司馬。 彥從少習武藝，出行伍間，高祖典禁軍，以鄉里之舊，任為親信。 國初，用為左飛龍使、檢校司空。 鎮州逐敵之際，請兵於朝廷，高祖令彥從率軍赴之。 乾祐初，領恩州刺史。 趙暉討王景崇於岐下，彥從為兵馬都監，破川軍有功，賊平，授濮州刺史，治有政能，百姓悅之。 乾祐三年冬，卒於郡。
Li Yancong, courtesy name Shiyuan, came from Xiaoyi in Fen. His father Li De was Sima of Lin. He trained in arms young and served in the ranks. When Gaozu commanded the guard, their hometown tie made Yancong a trusted confidant. At the dynasty's founding he became Left Flying Dragon envoy and honorary Minister of Works. When Zhenzhou needed troops to drive off the enemy, Gaozu sent Yancong at the head of an army. Early in Qianyou he was prefect of En. Zhao Hui besieged Wang Jingchong below Qi. Yancong served as army supervisor, defeated the Chuan force, and after the rebels fell was made prefect of Pu, where his rule pleased the people. In the winter of Qianyou 3 he died in office.

白再榮，本蕃部人也。 少從軍，累遷護聖左廂指揮使。 晉末，契丹入汴，明年，契丹主北去，再榮從部帳至真定。 其年閏七月晦，李筠、何福晉相率殺北帥滿達勒，據甲仗庫，敵勢未退，筠等使人召再榮。 再榮端坐本營，遲疑久之。 為軍吏所迫，乃行。 翊日，逐出床答勒，諸軍以再榮名次在諸校之右，乃請權知留後事。 〈 （《東都事略·李筠傳》：筠請馮道領節度，道曰：「予主奏事而已，留後事當議功臣為之。 」以諸將之甲者為留後。）〉 再榮貪昧無決，舉止多疑，出入騎從，露刃註矢，諸校不相統攝，互有猜貳。 奉國廂主王饒懼為再榮所並，乃據東門樓，以兵自衛，偽稱足疾，不敢見再榮。 司天監趙延乂俱與之善，乃來往解釋，遂無相忌之意。 再榮以李崧、和凝攜家在彼，令軍士數百人環迫崧、凝，以求賞給，崧、凝各出家財與之。 再榮欲害崧以利其財，前磁州刺史李穀謂再榮曰：「公與諸將為契丹所擄，淩辱萬端，旦夕憂死。 今日眾力逐出蕃戎，鎮民死者不下三千人，豈獨公等之功！ 才得生路，便擬殺一宰相，他日到闕，儻有所問，何以為辭？ 」再榮默然。 再榮又欲括率在城居民家財，以給軍士，李穀又譬解之，乃止。 其漢人曾事滿達勒者盡拘之，以取其財。 高祖以再榮為鎮州留後，為政貪虐難狀，鎮人呼為「白麻答」。 未幾，移授滑州節度使，箕斂誅求，民不聊生，乃征還京師。 周太祖入京城，軍士攻再榮之第，迫脅再榮，盡取財貨既，軍士前啟曰：「某等軍健，常趨事麾下，一旦無禮至此，今後何顏謁見？ 」即奮刃擊之，挈其首而去，後家人以帛贖葬之。
Bai Zairong was originally from the frontier tribes. He entered the army young and rose to command the left wing of the Husheng Guard. Late in Jin the Khitan took Bian. The next year, when the Khitan ruler withdrew north, Zairong followed the tribal camp to Zhending. On the last day of the intercalary seventh month, Li Jun and He Fujin killed the northern commander Mandale and seized the armory. The enemy had not yet retreated, and they sent for Zairong. Zairong sat stiffly in camp and hesitated a long while. Pressed by his officers, he finally went. The next day they expelled Chuangdale. Because Zairong outranked the other commanders, the troops asked him to act as temporary governor. Editorial bracket opening a comment block. (Dongdu shilue, Biography of Li Jun: Jun asked Feng Dao to take the governorship. Dao said, "I only handle memorials to the throne; the acting post should go to a meritorious officer after discussion." " The commander with the finest armor was made acting governor.) Zairong was greedy, indecisive, and suspicious. His escort rode with naked blades and strung arrows. The commanders would not obey one another and eyed each other with distrust. Wang Rao of the Fengguo wing feared Zairong would swallow his command, held the east gate tower with troops, and pretended foot trouble to avoid meeting him. Zhao Yanyi of the Astronomy Bureau, friendly with both men, shuttled between them until suspicion eased. Because Li Song and He Ning had families in the city, Zairong ringed them with hundreds of soldiers demanding payment. Both men paid from their private wealth. Zairong wanted to kill Song for his money. Li Gu, former prefect of Ci, said, "You and the other officers were Khitan captives, humiliated beyond measure, expecting death at any hour. Today you drove the barbarians out by mass effort. No fewer than three thousand townspeople died—is that yours alone!" You have barely won your lives and already mean to kill a chief minister. At court, if anyone asks, what will you say?" Zairong said nothing. Zairong next wanted to strip the townspeople's wealth for the troops. Li Gu talked him out of it again. Every Han who had served Mandale was seized and robbed. Gaozu made Zairong acting governor of Zhen. His rule was greedily brutal beyond words, and the people called him "White Mandale." Soon he was moved to Hua as military governor, squeezed the people mercilessly, and was recalled to the capital. When Zhou Taizu entered the capital, troops stormed Zairong's house and robbed him. When everything was taken, one soldier said, "We always served under you, sir. After this disgrace, how can we face you again?" They hacked off his head and carried it away. Later his family bought the head back with silk for burial.

史臣曰：晉、漢之際，有以懋軍功、勤王事、取旌旄符竹者多矣，其間有及民之惠者無幾焉。 如王周之閫政、審交之民譽，蓋其優者也； 漢球、張瓘抑又次焉。 是宜紀之篇以示來者，其餘皆不足觀也已。 張鵬以一言之失，遽滅其身，亦足誡後代多言橫議之徒歟！
The historian writes: Between Jin and Han many men won merit in war, served the throne loyally, and took commissions—but few brought real kindness to the people. Wang Zhou's circuit rule and Shenjiao's popular praise were among the best cases. Hanqiu and Zhang Guan stood a rank below even those. Such men deserve record in these pages for posterity; the rest are hardly worth notice. Zhang Peng lost his life over a single ill-chosen word—enough warning for later generations of men who talk too much and argue rashly!
