1
長興四年春正月戊寅朔，帝御明堂殿受朝賀，仗衛如式。 是日雪盈尺。 戊子，秦王從榮加守尚書令、兼侍中，依前河南尹，判六軍諸衛事。 庚寅，以端明殿學士、尚書兵部侍郎劉煦為中書侍郎、平章事。 甲午，正衙命使冊故福慶長公主孟氏為晉國雍順長公主，遣太常卿崔居儉赴西川行冊禮。 突厥內附。 庚子，以前河東節度使李從溫為鄆州節度使。
On the wuyin new moon of the first month in spring of Changxing 4 Mingzong received New Year congratulations at the Bright Hall with full ceremonial guard. That day snow fell to a depth of a full foot. On wuzi the Prince of Qin, Congrong, was named acting Director of the Imperial Secretariat and concurrent Palace Attendant, continued as Henan prefect, and given charge of the Six Armies and the palace guards. On gengyin Liu Xu, academician of the Duanming Hall and Vice Minister of War, became Vice Director of the Secretariat and Grand Councilor. On jiawu the court commissioned an envoy to invest the late Princess of Fuqing, Lady Meng, as Princess of Yongshun of Jin, and sent Director of Ceremonies Cui Jujian to the west to conduct the ceremony. The Turks submitted to the court. On gengzi former Hedong commissioner Li Congwen was appointed to Yanzhou.
2
二月癸丑朔，帝於便殿問範延光內外見管馬數，對曰：「三萬五千匹。 」帝歎曰：「太祖在太願，騎軍不過七千，先皇自始至終馬才及萬。 今有鐵馬如是，而不能使九州混一，是吾養士練將之不至也。 吾老矣，馬將奈何！ 」延光奏曰：「臣每思之，國家養馬太多，試計一騎士之費，可贍步軍五人，三萬五千騎抵十五萬步軍，既無所施，虛耗國力，臣恐日久難繼。 」帝曰：「誠如卿言，肥騎士而瘠吾民，何益哉！」 〈 （《五代會要》：上問見管馬數，樞密使範延光奏：「天下常支草粟者近五萬匹。 見今西北諸道蕃賣馬者往來如市，其郵傳之費、中估之直，日以四十五貫，以臣計之，國力十耗其七，馬無所使，財賦漸消，朝廷甚非所利。 」上善之。 十月，敕沿邊藩鎮，或有蕃部賣馬，可擇其良壯給券，具數以聞。）〉 丁巳，以虔州節度使、檢校太尉、兼侍中馬希振為洪州節度使； 以鄂州節度使馬希廣為檢校太尉、同平章事，充桂州節度使； 以盧州節度使兼武安軍副使姚彥章為檢校太尉、同平章事； 以靜江節度副使馬希範為鄂州節度使。 故潞州節度使、檢校太保康君立贈太傅。 己未，宋州節度使安元信加兼侍中。 濮州進重修河堤圖，沿河地名，曆曆可數。 帝覽之，愀然曰：「吾佐先朝定天下，於此堤塢間小大數百戰。 」又指一邱曰：「此吾擐甲台也。 時事如昨，奄忽一紀，令人悲歎耳！ 」癸亥，以西川節度使孟知祥為劍南東、西兩川節度使，封蜀王。 三司奏：「當省有諸道鹽鐵轉運使衙職員都押衙、正押衙、同押衙、通引、衙前虞候、子弟，今欲列為三司職名。 」從之。 庚午，以御史中丞崔衍為兵部侍郎，以右諫議大夫龍敏為御史中丞。
On the guichou new moon of the second month the emperor asked Fan Yanguang in the side hall how many horses the court maintained; Fan answered, "Thirty-five thousand." The emperor sighed: "The Founding Emperor at Taiyuan had fewer than seven thousand cavalry; my predecessor never had more than ten thousand horses in all. Now we have so many war-horses and still cannot reunite the realm—it means I have not nurtured officers and trained generals well enough. I am old—what are we to do with all these horses?!" Fan Yanguang replied, "I have often thought the state keeps too many horses. One cavalryman's upkeep could feed five foot soldiers; thirty-five thousand riders equal a hundred fifty thousand infantry, yet we have no use for them and drain the treasury—I fear this cannot last." The emperor said, "You are right: fattening cavalry while the people starve—what good does that do?!" [Supplement begins] (The 《Five Dynasties Institutes》 records that when the emperor asked about managed horses, Fan Yanguang reported, "Nearly fifty thousand are on regular fodder and grain pay nationwide." Nowadays frontier tribes sell horses in a steady market; courier and broker costs alone run forty-five strings a day. I reckon the state loses seven-tenths of its strength, the horses serve no purpose, revenues dwindle, and the court gains little." The emperor approved. In the tenth month frontier commands were told to select sturdy tribal horses sold locally, issue vouchers, and report the numbers.)〉 On disi Ma Xizhen of Qianzhou, acting Grand Preceptor and Palace Attendant, was transferred to Hongzhou; Ma Xiguang of Ezhou became acting Grand Preceptor, Associate Grand Councilor, and military commissioner of Guilin; Yao Yanzhang of Luzhou and deputy of the Wu'an army was named acting Grand Preceptor and Associate Grand Councilor; Ma Xifan, deputy commissioner of Jingjiang, became commissioner of Ezhou. The late Luzhou commissioner Kang Junli was posthumously made Grand Tutor. On jiwei An Yuanxin of Songzhou was also named Palace Attendant. Pu prefecture submitted a map of rebuilt river dikes with every place name along the bank clearly marked. Reading it, the emperor grew somber: "I helped the previous reign conquer the realm and fought hundreds of battles along these dikes and forts. He pointed to a mound: "That is where I once buckled on my armor. It feels like yesterday, yet twelve years have slipped by in a breath—how sad!" On guihai Meng Zhixiang of Xichuan was made commissioner of both Jiannan circuits and enfeoffed as Prince of Shu. The Three Departments reported that posts such as chief escort, associate escort, guide, and yamen retainers from circuit salt-and-iron transport offices should be entered as Three Departments titles. The request was approved. On gengwu Cui Yan moved from Censor-in-Chief to Vice Minister of War, and Long Min from Right Remonstrance Grandee to Censor-in-Chief.
3
三月己卯，幸龍門。 延州節度使安從進奏，夏州節度使李仁福卒，其子彝超自稱留後。 甲申，鎮州奏，行軍司馬趙瑰、節度判官陸浣、元從押衙高知柔等並棄市，坐受賂枉法殺人也。 節度使李從敏罰一季俸。 乙酉，以西川節度副使、知武泰軍節度兵馬留後趙季良為檢校太保、黔南節度使； 以西川諸軍馬步都指揮使、知武信軍節度兵馬留後李仁罕為檢校太傅、遂州節度使； 以西川左廂馬步指揮使、知保寧軍節度兵馬留後趙廷隱為檢校太保、閬州節度使； 以西川右廂馬步都指揮使、知寧江軍兵馬留後張知業為檢校司徒、夔州節度使； 以西川衙內馬步都指揮使、知昭武軍兵馬留後李肇為檢校太保、利州節度使，從孟知祥之請也。 丙戌，賜宰相李愚絹百匹、錢十萬、鋪陳物一十三件。 時愚病，帝令近臣翟光鄴宣問，所居寢室，蕭然四壁，病榻弊氈而已。 光鄴具言其事，故有是賜。 戊子，以延州節度使安從進為夏州留後，以夏州左都押衙、四州防遏使李彝超為延州留後，仍命邠州節度使藥彥稠、宮苑使安重益帥師援送從進赴鎮。 以左衛上將軍盧文進為潞州節度使，以右龍武統軍張溫為雲州節度使。 庚寅，以鳳翔行軍司馬李彥琮為鹽州防禦使。 時範延光等奏，請因夏州之師製置鹽州，故有是命。 癸巳，以右威衛上將軍安重霸為同州節度使。 己亥，以左龍武統軍符彥超為安州節度使。 詔除放京兆、秦、岐、邠、涇、延、慶、同、華、興元十州長興元年二年係欠夏秋稅物，及營田莊宅務課利，以其曾輦運供軍糧料也。 甲辰，故晉國夫人夏氏追冊皇后，有司上諡曰昭懿，從之。
On jimao in the third month the emperor visited Longmen. An Congjin of Yanzhou reported that Li Renfu of Xiazhou had died and his son Yichao had proclaimed himself acting commissioner. On jiashen Zhenzhou reported that Zhao Gui, Lu Huan, Gao Zhirou, and others were executed for bribery, abuse of law, and murder. Commissioner Li Congmin was fined a quarter-year's salary. On yiyou Zhao Jiliang, Xichuan deputy and acting Wutai commissioner, became acting Grand Guardian and military commissioner of Qiannan; Li Renhan, Xichuan commander of all forces and acting Wuxin commissioner, was named acting Grand Tutor and commissioner of Suizhou; Zhao Tingyin, left-wing commander and acting Baoning commissioner, became acting Grand Guardian and commissioner of Langzhou; Zhang Zhiye, right-wing commander and acting Ningjiang commissioner, was made acting Minister of Works and commissioner of Kuizhou; Li Zhao, inner-palace commander and acting Zhaowu commissioner, became acting Grand Guardian and commissioner of Lizhou at Meng Zhixiang's request. On bingxu Grand Councilor Li Yu received a hundred bolts of silk, a hundred thousand cash, and thirteen sets of furnishings. Li Yu was then ill; the emperor sent Zhai Guangye to inquire after him and found his room utterly bare, with only a sickbed and a threadbare rug. Guangye reported this, which led to the grant. On wuzi An Congjin of Yanzhou was named acting Xiazhou commissioner, Li Yichao of Xiazhou acting Yanzhou commissioner, and Yao Yanchou of Bin and An Chongyi were ordered to escort Congjin to his post with troops. Lu Wenjin of the Left Guard became Luzhou commissioner, and Zhang Wen of the Right Dragon Martial became Yunzhou commissioner. On gengyin Li Yancong of Fengxiang became defense commissioner of Yanzhou. Fan Yanguang and others had asked to establish Yanzhou while the Xiazhou campaign was underway, which led to this appointment. On guisi An Chongba of the Right Awesome Guard became Tongzhou commissioner. On jihai Fu Yanchao of the Left Dragon Martial became Anzhou commissioner. An edict canceled summer and autumn tax arrears from Changxing 1–2 in Jingzhao, Qin, Qi, Bin, Jing, Yan, Qing, Tong, Hua, and Xingyuan, plus farm and manor levies, because those regions had once hauled grain for the army. On jiachen the late Lady of Jin, Lady Xia, was posthumously made empress with the temple name Zhaoyi.
4
夏四月戊申，李彝超奏：「奉詔除延州留後，已受恩命訖，三軍百姓擁隔，未遂赴任。 」帝遣閣門使蘇繼顏齎詔促彝超赴任。 癸丑，以刑部侍郎劉讚為秘書監、秦王傅。 〈 （《五代會要》：長興四年四月，以秘書監劉讚為秦王傅，前忠武軍節度判官蘇瓚為秦王友，前襄州觀察使魚崇遠為秦王府記室參軍。 時言事者請為秦王置師傅，上顧問近臣，皆以秦王名勢隆盛，不敢置議，請自選擇，乃降是命。）〉 甲寅，前鄧州節度使梁漢顒以太子少師致仕，太子賓客裴皞以兵部尚書致仕。 戊午，追冊昭宗皇后何氏為宣穆皇后，祔饗太廟，百僚進名奉慰，廢朝三日。 己巳，以左散騎常侍任讚為戶部侍郎，以吏部侍郎藥縱之為曹州刺史。 癸酉，延州奏，蕃部劫掠餉運及攻城之具，守蘆關兵士退守金明鎮。
On wushen in the fourth month of summer Li Yichao reported that although he had accepted appointment as acting Yanzhou commissioner, the troops and people were blocking him from taking office. The emperor sent Su Jiyan of the Palace Gate office with an edict ordering Yichao to proceed to his post. On guichou Liu Zan, Vice Minister of Justice, became Director of the Secretariat and tutor to the Prince of Qin. [Supplement begins] (The 《Five Dynasties Institutes》 says in the fourth month of Changxing 4 Liu Zan became tutor to the Prince of Qin, Su Zan companion, and Yu Chongyuan recorder of the prince's household. Memorialists had asked for a tutor and mentor for the prince; the emperor consulted close ministers, who said the prince's stature was too great for them to nominate anyone and asked the emperor to choose—hence these appointments.)〉 On jiayin former Dengzhou commissioner Liang Hanyong retired as Junior Tutor to the Heir Apparent, and Pei Hao as Minister of War. On wuwu Empress He of Zhaozong was posthumously honored as Empress Xuanmu and enshrined in the ancestral temple; officials offered condolences and court was suspended for three days. On jisi Ren Zan became Vice Minister of Revenue, and Yao Zongzhi of Personnel became prefect of Cao. On guiyou Yanzhou reported tribal raids on supply trains and siege gear, and that Luguan garrison troops had fallen back to Jinming.
5
五月丙子朔，帝御文明殿受朝。 戊寅，皇子鳳翔節度使從珂封潞王。 新授戶部侍郎任讚改刑部侍郎，讚訴以所授官是丁憂闕，故改正。 皇子從益封許王，鄆州節度使李從溫封兗王，河中節度使李從璋封洋王，鎮州節度使李從敏封涇王。 甲申，帝避暑於九曲池，既而登樓，風毒暴作，聖體不豫，翌日而愈。 〈 （《北夢瑣言》云：上聖體乖和，馮道對寢膳之間，動思調衛，因指御前果實曰：「如食桃不康，翌日見李而思戒可也。 」初，上因禦李，暴得風虛之疾，馮道不敢斥言，因奏事諷悟上意。）〉 丙戌，契丹遣使朝貢。 丁酉，安從進奏，大軍已至夏州，攻城，以其不受命也。 庚子，以靈武留後張希崇為本州節度使。 辛丑，故夏州節度使、朔方郡王李仁福追封虢王。 壬寅，以前晉州留後薄文為本州節度使。
On the bingzi new moon of the fifth month the emperor held court at the Hall of Civilization. On wuyin the prince Congke of Fengxiang was enfeoffed as Prince of Lu. Ren Zan, newly named Vice Minister of Revenue, was reassigned to Vice Minister of Justice after explaining that the Revenue post had been a mourning vacancy. Prince Congyi became Prince of Xu; Li Congwen of Yanzhou Prince of Yan; Li Congzhang of Hezhong Prince of Yang; and Li Congmin of Zhenzhou Prince of Jing. On jiashen the emperor cooled himself at the Nine-Bend Pool, then climbed a tower; a sudden wind chill left him ill, but he recovered the next day. [Supplement begins] (The 《Miscellaneous Tales of Northern Dreams》 says that when the emperor was unwell, Feng Dao at his bedside thought constantly of his health and pointed to fruit before the throne: "If peaches disagree with you, seeing plums the next day should remind you to be careful." The emperor had fallen ill after eating plums; Feng Dao dared not say so directly and used this roundabout remark while reporting on state business.)〉 On bingxu Khitan envoys presented tribute. On dingyou An Congjin reported that the main force had reached Xiazhou and was besieging the city for refusing orders. On gengzi Zhang Xichong, acting commissioner of Lingwu, became its military commissioner. On xinchou the late Xiazhou commissioner and Prince of Shuofang Li Renfu was posthumously made Prince of Guo. On renyin former acting Jinzhou commissioner Bo Wen became its military commissioner.
6
六月丙午朔，文武百僚、宰臣馮道等拜章，請於尊號內加「廣運法天」四字，凡拜三章，詔允之。 詔宮西新園宜名永芳園，其間新殿宜名和慶殿。 丙辰，秦王從榮加食邑至萬戶，實封二千戶。 丁巳，以右驍衛上將軍李從昶為左龍武統軍，以前邢州節度使高允韜為右龍武統軍，以右驍衛上將軍羅周敬為左羽林統軍，以右監門上將軍婁繼英為金州刺史。 戊午，宋王從厚加食邑至萬戶，實封一千戶。 壬戌，以前涇州節度使李金全為滄州節度使。 癸亥，詔御史中丞龍敏等詳定《大中統類》。 甲子，第十四女封壽安公主，第十五女封永樂公主。 戊辰，以前利州節度使孫漢韶為洋州節度使。 壬申，永寧軍節度使、容州管內觀察使、檢校太尉、兼侍中馬存加食邑實封。 甲戌，帝復不豫。
On the bingwu new moon of the sixth month officials and Grand Councilor Feng Dao thrice asked to add "Broad Fortune in Accord with Heaven" to the emperor's honorific title, and the request was granted. The new garden west of the palace was named Ever-Fragrant Garden and its new hall the Hall of Harmonious Celebration. On bingchen the Prince of Qin Congrong's fief was raised to ten thousand households, with two thousand actual enfeoffments. On dingsi Li Congchang of the Right Valiant Cavalry became Left Dragon Martial commander; Gao Yuntao of Xingzhou Right Dragon Martial commander; Luo Zhoujing of the Right Valiant Cavalry Left Forest of Feathers commander; and Lou Jiying of the Right Gatewatch prefect of Jin. On wuwu the Prince of Song Conghou's fief was raised to ten thousand households, with one thousand actual enfeoffments. On renxu former Jingzhou commissioner Li Jinquan became Cangzhou commissioner. On guihai Long Min and others were ordered to revise the 《Dazhong Comprehensive Categories》. On jiazi the fourteenth daughter became Princess of Shou'an and the fifteenth Princess of Yongle. On wuchen former Lizhou commissioner Sun Hanshao became Yangzhou commissioner. On renshen Ma Cun of Yongning, observation commissioner of Rong, acting Grand Preceptor and Palace Attendant, received an increase in fief income and actual enfeoffments. On jiaxu the emperor fell ill again.
7
秋七月丁丑，以著作佐郎尹拙為左拾遺，直史館。 國朝舊制，皆以畿赤尉直史館，今用諫官自拙始，從監修李愚奏也。 己卯，東嶽三郎神贈威雄大將軍。 初，帝不豫，前淄州刺史劉遂清薦泰山僧一人，雲善醫，及召見，乃庸僧耳。 問方藥，僧曰：「不工醫，嘗於泰山中親睹嶽神，謂僧曰：『吾第三子威靈可愛，而未有爵秩，師為我請之。 』」宮中神其事，故有是命，識者嫉遂清之妖佞焉。 詔應台官出行，須令人訶引，使軍巡職掌等規避。 壬午，詔安從進班師，時王師攻夏州無功故也。 乙酉，以許州節度使孟鵠卒廢朝，贈太傅。 詔賜在京諸軍將校優給有差。 時帝疾未痊，軍士有流言故也。 丁亥，兩浙節度使、檢校太傅、守中書令錢元瓘封吳王。
On dingchou in the seventh month of autumn Yin Zhuo, editorial assistant, became Left Reminder and was assigned to the Historiography Office. By old custom only capital-district magistrates served in the Historiography Office; Zhuo was the first remonstrance official appointed there, at supervising compiler Li Yu's request. On jimao the Third Son Spirit of Mount Tai was posthumously named General of Majestic Might. When the emperor had first fallen ill, former Zi prefect Liu Suiqing recommended a Mount Tai monk said to be a fine physician, but on meeting him he proved an ordinary monk. Asked for remedies, the monk said he was no physician but had seen the god of Mount Tai, who asked him to seek a title for his third son, whose spirit was worthy but had no rank. The palace took the story as miraculous and issued the appointment; the knowing blamed Suiqing for superstition and flattery. Censorial officials traveling abroad were to have heralds clear the road so military patrols would give way. On renwu An Congjin was ordered to withdraw because the assault on Xiazhou had failed. On yiyou court was suspended for the death of Xuzhou commissioner Meng Hu, who was posthumously made Grand Tutor. Officers of the capital armies received graded bonus allowances. This was because the emperor was still ill and rumors were spreading among the troops. On dinghai Qian Yuanguan of Liangzhe, acting Grand Tutor and Director of the Secretariat, was enfeoffed as Prince of Wu.
8
八月戊申，帝被袞冕，禦明堂殿受冊，徽號曰聖明神武廣運法天文德恭孝皇帝。 禮畢，製大赦天下，常赦所不原者咸赦除之。 己酉，賜侍衛諸軍優給有差。 時月內再有頒給，自茲府藏無餘積矣。 辛亥，以晉州節度使薄文卒廢朝。 丁巳，以右龍武統軍李從昶為許州節度使。 戊午，以秘書監高輅卒廢朝。 辛酉，以太子太師致仕符習卒廢朝，贈太師。 辛未，秦王從榮以本官充天下兵馬大元帥，加食邑萬戶，實封三千戶； 以右羽林統軍翟璋為晉州節度使； 以太子賓客馬鎬為戶部侍郎。 壬申，幸至德宮。
On wushen in the eighth month the emperor, in full regalia, received his investiture at the Bright Hall as Sagely, Bright, Divinely Martial, of Broad Fortune in Accord with Heaven, Cultured in Virtue, and Reverently Filial Emperor. When the ceremony ended he proclaimed a great amnesty, pardoning even offenses normally excluded from regular amnesties. On jiyou the palace guards received graded bonus allowances. There had already been another round of payouts that month, and the treasury was now empty. On xinhai court was suspended for the death of Jinzhou commissioner Bo Wen. On dingsi Li Congchang, commander of the Right Dragon Martial Guard, became commissioner of Xuzhou. On wuwu court was suspended for the death of Secretariat director Gao Luo. On xinyou court was suspended for the death of retired Heir Apparent tutor Fu Xi, who was posthumously named Grand Tutor. On xinwei the Prince of Qin Congrong was made Grand Marshal of All Armies while keeping his existing titles, with fief income raised by ten thousand households and actual enfeoffment by three thousand; Zhai Zhang of the Right Feathered Forest Guard became Jinzhou commissioner; Heir Apparent's guest Ma Hao was appointed Vice Minister of Revenue. On renshen the emperor visited the Zhide Palace.
9
九月甲戌，以戶部尚書李鈴為兵部尚書，以前戶部尚書韓彥惲為戶部尚書。 丙子，幸至德宮。 戊寅，樞密使範延光、趙延壽並加兼侍中，依前充使。 中書奏：「元帥儀注，諸道節度使以下帶兵權者，階下具軍禮參見； 其帶使相者，初見亦展一度公禮。 天下軍務公事，元帥府行帖指揮，其判六軍諸衛事則公牒往來，其官屬軍職，委元帥府奏請。 」從之。 癸未，以兵部侍郎盧詹為吏部侍郎。 丙戌，宰臣馮道加左僕射，李愚加吏部尚書，劉煦加刑部尚書。 戊子，河陽節度使兼侍衛親軍都指揮使康義誠、山南西道節度使檢校太傅張虔釗並加同平章事。 宣徽南院使、判三司馮贇依前檢校太傅、同中書門下二品，充三司使。 贇亡父名章，故改平章事為同二品。 壬戌，永寧公主石氏進封魏國公主，興平公主趙氏進封齊國公主； 皇孫重光、重哲並授銀青光祿大夫、檢校工部尚書，秦王、宋王子也。 前洋州節度使梁漢顒以太子少傅致仕。 丁酉，以右龍武統軍高允韜為滑州節度使，以韶州刺史、檢校司空王萬榮為華州節度使。 萬榮，王妃之父也。 戊戌，以樞密使趙延壽為汴州節度使，以襄州節度使朱宏昭為檢校太尉、同平章事，充樞密使。 時範延光、趙延壽相繼辭退樞密務，及朱宏昭有樞密之命，又麵辭訴，帝叱之曰：「爾輩皆欲離朕左右，怕在眼前，素養爾輩，將何用也！ 」宏昭退謝，不復敢言。 吏部侍郎張文寶卒。 庚子，清海軍節度使錢元璹加檢校太傅、同平章事； 中吳、建武等軍節度使錢元尞加檢校太師、兼中書令。 以前滑州節度使李讚華遙領虔州節度使。 辛丑，詔天下兵馬大元帥、秦王從榮班宜在宰臣之上。 〈 （《五代會要》：秦王從榮加兼中書令，與宰臣分班左右定位，及為天下兵馬元帥。 敕曰：「秦王位隆將相，望重磐維，委任既崇，等威合異，班位宜在宰臣之上。」）〉 壬寅，以北面行營都指揮使、易州刺史楊檀為振武軍節度使。
On jiaxu in the ninth month Li Ling moved from Revenue to War minister, and former revenue minister Han Yanyun took Revenue. On bingzi the emperor again visited the Zhide Palace. On wuyin Fan Yanguang and Zhao Yanshou were both made concurrent Palace Attendants while remaining privy commissioners. The Secretariat reported, "Under marshal protocol, circuit commissioners and other commanders with military authority should pay audience below the steps in full military ceremony; those who also hold commissioner-minister rank should perform the public rites once at the first audience. Military affairs nationwide would be directed by posted orders from the marshal's office; business concerning the Six Armies and guards would pass by official correspondence; staff and military appointments would be submitted through the marshal's office. " The proposal was approved. On guiwei Lu Zhan moved from War to Personnel vice minister. On bingxu Feng Dao became Left Vice Director, Li Yu Minister of Personnel, and Liu Xu Minister of Justice. On wuzi Heyang commissioner and palace guard commander Kang Yicheng and Shannan West commissioner Zhang Qianzhao were both made associate grand councilors. Feng Yun of the southern palace secretariat bureau, still directing the Three Offices, kept acting Grand Tutor and Second Rank of the Secretariat and Chancellery as Three Offices commissioner. Because Yun's late father had been named Zhang, the title associate grand councilor was changed to second rank of the secretariat and chancellery to avoid the taboo. On renxu Princess Yongning Lady Shi was raised to Princess of Wei and Princess Xingping Lady Zhao to Princess of Qi; Imperial grandsons Chongguang and Chongzhe, sons of the Princes of Qin and Song, were both made Silver-Gleam Grand Master for Splendid Happiness and acting Minister of Works. Former Yangzhou commissioner Liang Hanyong retired as junior tutor to the heir apparent. On dingyou Gao Yuntao of the Right Dragon Martial Guard became commissioner of Huazhou, and Shaozhou prefect Wang Wanrong acting Minister of Works became commissioner of Huazhou. Wanrong was the consort's father. On wuxu Zhao Yanshou left the privy council for Bianzhou, and Xiangzhou commissioner Zhu Hongzhao became acting grand commandant, associate grand councilor, and privy commissioner. Fan Yanguang and Zhao Yanshou had already tried to resign from the privy council in turn, and when Zhu Hongzhao received the appointment he too pleaded face-to-face to be excused. The emperor scolded them: "You all want to get away from me—you cannot bear having me before your eyes. After all I've done for you, what good are you!" " Hongzhao withdrew in apology and did not dare speak again. Personnel vice minister Zhang Wenbao died. On gengzi Qinghai commissioner Qian Yuanshao was made acting grand tutor and associate grand councilor; Zhongwu and Jianwu commissioner Qian Yuanliao was made acting grand preceptor and concurrent director of the secretariat. Former Huazhou commissioner Li Zanhua was given titular command of Ganzhou. On xinchou an edict placed Grand Marshal Congrong, Prince of Qin, above the grand councilors in court rank. [Supplement begins] (The Five Dynasties Institutes says the Prince of Qin Congrong was also made concurrent director of the secretariat, stood in separate ranks to the left and right of the councilors, and became grand marshal of all armies. An edict read, "The Prince of Qin stands above generals and ministers, his weight anchors the realm; with so lofty a commission his ceremonial rank should differ, and his place in court should be above the councilors.")〉 On renyin northern camp commander and Yizhou prefect Yang Tan became Zhenwu army commissioner.
10
冬十月丙午，以前同州節度使趙在禮為襄州節度使。 丁未，以前滑州節度使張敬詢卒廢朝。 以刑部侍郎任讚為兵部侍郎，充元帥府判官。 戊午，以前鳳翔節度使孫嶽為三司使。 庚申，以樞密使範延光為鎮州節度使，以三司使馮贇為樞密使。 辛酉，以前潞州節度使李承約為左龍武統軍； 以前威寨軍節度使王景戡為右龍武統軍； 以左驍衛上將軍安崇阮為左神武統軍； 以右監門上將軍高允貞為右神武統軍。 壬戌，以權知夏州事、檢校司空李彝超為夏州節度使、檢校司徒。 丙寅，詔在朝文武臣僚並與加恩，以受冊尊號也。 戊辰，以前安州節度使楊漢章為兗州節度使，以前雲州節度使張敬達為徐州節度使。 庚午，以前兗州節度使張延朗為秦州節度使。 壬申，秦州節度使劉仲殷移鎮宋州。
On bingwu in the tenth month former Tongzhou commissioner Zhao Zaili became Xiangzhou commissioner. On dingwei court was suspended for the death of former Huazhou commissioner Zhang Jingxun. Justice vice minister Ren Zan became war vice minister and commission judge of the marshal's office. On wuwu former Fengxiang commissioner Sun Yue became Three Offices commissioner. On gengshen Fan Yanguang left the privy council for Zhenzhou and Feng Yun moved from the Three Offices to privy commissioner. On xinyou former Luzhou commissioner Li Chengyue became commander of the Left Dragon Martial Guard; former Weizhai commissioner Wang Jingzhan became commander of the Right Dragon Martial Guard; Left Valiant Guard senior general An Chongruan became commander of the Left Divine Martial Guard; Right Gate Guard senior general Gao Yunzhen became commander of the Right Divine Martial Guard. On renxu Li Yichao, acting Xiazhou commissioner and acting Minister of Works, was confirmed as Xiazhou commissioner and acting Minister of Education. On bingyin all officials at court received bonus honors in connection with the emperor's investiture. On wuchen former Anzhou commissioner Yang Hanzhang became Yanzhou commissioner and former Yunzhou commissioner Zhang Jingda became Xuzhou commissioner. On gengwu former Yanzhou commissioner Zhang Yanlang became Qinzhou commissioner. On renshen Qinzhou commissioner Liu Zhongyin was transferred to Songzhou.
11
十一月丙子，以前滄州節度使盧質為右僕射。 庚辰，改慎州懷化軍為昭化軍，升洮州為保順軍。 辛巳，以保大軍節度使、檢校太尉鮑君福為保順軍節度、洮鄯等州觀察等使； 以彰義軍節度使、檢校太尉、同平章事杜建徽為昭化軍節度、慎瑞司等州觀察使。 乙酉，以前汴州節度使李從嚴為鄆州節度使，以鄆州節度使李從溫為定州節度使。 丙戌，新授右僕射盧質奏：「臣忝除官，合赴省上事，若準舊例，左右僕射上事儀注所費極多，欲從權務簡，隻取尚書丞、郎上事例，止集南省屬僚及兩省官送上，亦不敢輒援往例，有費官用，自量力排比； 兼不敢自臣隳廢前規，他時任行舊制。 」從之。
On bingzi in the eleventh month former Cangzhou commissioner Lu Zhi became Right Vice Director. On gengchen the Huaihua army in Shenzhou was renamed Zhaohua, and Taozhou was elevated to the Baoshun army. On xinsi Baoda commissioner and acting grand commandant Bao Junfu became Baoshun commissioner and observation commissioner of Tao, Shan, and neighboring prefectures; Zhangyi commissioner Du Jianhui, acting grand commandant and associate grand councilor, became Zhaohua commissioner and observation commissioner of Shen, Rui, Si, and neighboring prefectures. On yiyou former Bianzhou commissioner Li Congyan became Yanzhou commissioner and Yanzhou commissioner Li Congwen became Dingzhou commissioner. On bingxu the newly appointed Right Vice Director Lu Zhi memorialized, "Having received this appointment I should report to the ministry to assume office, but the old ceremony for a vice director's inauguration is enormously costly. I ask to simplify matters and follow only the precedent used for vice ministers and bureau directors, gathering just the southern ministry staff and officials of the two secretariats to escort me. I dare not invoke the full old rite and waste public funds, and will arrange what I can afford myself; nor do I mean by this to abolish the old rule for good—it may be restored when circumstances allow. " The request was approved.
12
戊子，帝不豫。 己丑，大漸，自廣壽殿移居雍和殿。 是夜四鼓後，帝自禦榻蹶然而興，顧謂知漏宮女曰：「今夜漏幾何？ 」對曰：「四更。 」因奏曰：「官家省事否？ 」帝曰：「省。 」因唾出肉片如肺者數片，便溺升餘。 六宮皆至，慶躍而奏曰：「官家今日實還魂也。 」已進食一器，侍醫進湯膳。 至曙，帝小康。 壬辰，天下大元帥、守尚書令、兼侍中、秦王從榮領兵陣於天津橋，內出禁軍拒之。 從榮敗奔河南府，遇害。 帝聞之，悲駭，幾落禦榻，氣絕而蘇者再，由是不豫有加。 癸巳，馮道率百僚見帝於雍和殿，帝雨泣哽噎，曰：「吾家事若此，慚見卿等！ 」百僚皆泣下沾襟。 甲午，賜宰臣、樞密使禦衣玉帶，康義誠已下錦帛鞍馬有差。 遣宣徽使孟漢瓊召宋王於鄴都。 乙未，以三司使孫嶽為亂兵所害廢朝。 丁酉，敕秦王府官屬，除諮議參軍高輦已處斬外，元帥府判官、兵部侍郎任讚配武州，秘書監兼秦王傅劉讚配嵐州，河南少尹劉陟配均州，並為長流百姓，縱逢恩赦，不在放還。 河南少尹李蕘配石州，河南府判官司徒詡配寧州，秦王友蘇瓚配萊州，記室參軍魚崇遠配慶州，河南府推官王說配隨州，並為長流百姓。 河南府推官尹諲，六軍巡官董裔、張九思，河南府巡官張沆、李潮、江文蔚並勒歸田里。 應長流人並除名。 六軍判官、殿中監王居敏責授復州司馬，六軍推官郭晙責授坊州司戶，並員外置，所在馳驛發遣。 時宰相、樞密使共議任讚等已下罪，馮道等曰：「任讚前在班行，比與從榮無舊，除官未及月餘，便逢此禍。 王居敏、司徒詡疾病請假，將近半年，近日之事，計不同謀。 從榮所款昵者高輦、劉陟、王說三人，昨從榮稱兵指闕之際，沿路隻與劉陟、高輦並轡耳語，至天津橋南，指日影謂諸判官曰：『明日如今，已誅王居敏矣。 』則知其冗泛之徒，不可一例從坐。 」朱宏昭意欲盡誅任讚已下，馮贇力爭之乃已。 戊戌，帝崩於大內之雍和殿，壽六十七。
On wuzi the emperor fell ill. On jichou, gravely ill, he was moved from the Guangshou Hall to the Yonghe Hall. That night after the fourth watch the emperor suddenly sat up on his couch and asked the palace woman keeping the water clock, "What watch is it tonight?" " She answered, "The fourth watch." She then asked, "Is Your Majesty feeling better?" " The emperor said, "Yes." He then spat out several lung-like pieces of flesh and passed more than a sheng of urine. The women of the inner palace all came and exclaimed in joy, "Your Majesty has truly come back from death today!" A bowl of food was brought at once, and the attending physicians served broth and a meal. By dawn the emperor had improved slightly. On renchen Grand Marshal Congrong, Prince of Qin, acting director of the secretariat and palace attendant, drew up troops at the Tianjin Bridge, and the palace sent the imperial guard to oppose him. Congrong was defeated, fled to the Henan prefecture seat, and was killed. When Mingzong heard the news he was stricken with grief and shock, nearly falling from his couch; twice he stopped breathing and came back, and from then on his illness worsened. On guisi Feng Dao led the officials to see the emperor in the Yonghe Hall. Mingzong wept aloud and said through sobs, "My family's affairs have come to this—I am ashamed to face you!" All the officials wept until their robes were wet with tears. On jiawu the grand councilors and privy commissioners received imperial robes and jade belts, and from Kang Yicheng down others received graded gifts of brocade, silk, saddles, and horses. Palace secretariat commissioner Meng Hanqiong was sent to summon the Prince of Song from Yedu. On yiwei court was suspended because Three Offices commissioner Sun Yue had been killed by mutinous troops. On dingyou an edict punished the Prince of Qin's staff: aside from advisory aide Gao Nian, already executed, marshal's commission judge and war vice minister Ren Zan was exiled to Wuzhou, secretariat director and Qin tutor Liu Zan to Lanzhou, and Henan vice prefect Liu Sheng to Junzhou—all as permanent exiles never to be released even by amnesty. Henan vice prefect Li Rao was exiled to Shizhou, Henan commission judge Sikong Xu to Ningzhou, Qin companion Su Zan to Laizhou, recorder Yu Chongyuan to Qingzhou, and Henan investigating officer Wang Yue to Suizhou—all as permanent exiles. Henan investigating officer Yin Shen, Six Armies patrol officers Dong Yi and Zhang Jiusi, and Henan patrol officers Zhang Hang, Li Chao, and Jiang Wenwei were all sent home. All those exiled were also struck from the registers. Six Armies commission judge and palace receptions director Wang Jumin was demoted to acting military aide of Fuzhou and Six Armies investigating officer Guo Jun to acting registrar of Fangzhou, both supernumerary posts, and both were sent off at once by courier. The grand councilors and privy commissioners then debated the guilt of Ren Zan and the others. Feng Dao said, "Ren Zan had been in court service and had no old ties with Congrong; he had held office barely a month when this disaster struck. Wang Jumin and Sikong Xu had been on sick leave for nearly half a year and can hardly have joined in the recent plot. Congrong's intimates were Gao Nian, Liu Sheng, and Wang Yue. When he marched on the palace yesterday he rode bridle to bridle whispering only with Liu Sheng and Gao Nian, and at the south end of the Tianjin Bridge he pointed at the sun's shadow and told the commission judges, "By this hour tomorrow Wang Jumin will already be dead." ' That shows the rest were hangers-on who should not all be punished alike. " Zhu Hongzhao wanted to execute Ren Zan and all the rest, but Feng Yun argued hard against it and stopped him. On wuxu the emperor died in the Yonghe Hall of the inner palace at the age of sixty-seven.
13
十二月癸卯朔，遷梓宮於二儀殿，宋王從厚自鄴都至。 是日發哀，百僚縞素於位，中書侍郎、平章事劉煦宣遺製，宋王從厚於柩前即皇帝位，服紀以日易月，一如舊制雲。 明年四月，太常卿盧文紀上諡議曰聖智仁德欽孝皇帝，廟號明宗，宰臣馮道議請改「聖智仁德」四字，為聖德和武欽孝皇帝。 宰臣劉煦撰諡冊文，宰臣李愚撰哀冊文，是月二十七日葬於徽陵。 〈 （《五代史補》：明宗之在位也，一日幸倉場觀納，時主者以車駕親臨，懼得罪，其較量甚輕。 明宗因謂之曰：「且朕自省事以來，倉場給散，動經一二十年未畢，今輕量如此，其後銷折將何以償之？ 」對曰：「竭盡家產，不足則繼之以身命。 」明宗愴然曰：「隻聞百姓養一家，未聞一家養百姓。 今後每石加二斗耗，以備鼠雀侵蠹，謂之鼠雀耗。 」倉糧加耗，自此始也。 《五代史闕文》：明宗出自邊地，老於戰陳，即位之歲，年已六旬，純厚仁慈，本乎天性。 每夕宮中焚香仰天禱祝云「某蕃人也，遇世亂為眾推戴，事不獲已，願上天早生聖人，與百姓為主。 」故天成、長興間，比歲豐登，中原無事，言於五代，粗為小康。）〉
On the guimao new moon of the twelfth month the coffin was moved to the Eryi Hall, and the Prince of Song Conghou arrived from Yedu. That day mourning was proclaimed. Officials stood at their posts in white hemp, councilor Liu Xu read the testamentary edict, and the Prince of Song Conghou took the throne before the coffin, observing mourning counted as days for months according to old custom. The following fourth month sacrifice director Lu Wenji proposed the posthumous title Sagely, Wise, Benevolent, and Cultured, Reverently Filial Emperor and the temple name Mingzong; Feng Dao proposed changing the four words "Sagely, Wise, Benevolent, and Cultured" to Sagely, Cultured, Harmonious, and Martial, Reverently Filial Emperor. Grand councilor Liu Xu wrote the posthumous-title patent and Li Yu the lament patent; on the twenty-seventh of that month Mingzong was buried at Huiling. [Supplement begins] (Supplement to the History of the Five Dynasties says that while Mingzong reigned he once visited a granary to watch grain intake; the manager, fearing punishment because the emperor had come in person, weighed the grain very lightly. Mingzong said to him, "Since I began taking an active hand in government, granary distributions have often taken ten or twenty years to finish. If you weigh this lightly now, how will later losses ever be made up?" " He answered, "I would exhaust my family's property, and if that were not enough I would pay with my life." Mingzong said sadly, "I have only ever heard of the people supporting one household—not of one household supporting the people." From then on two dou were added to every shi of grain to cover loss to rats, sparrows, and pests—the "rat-and-sparrow allowance." Thus began the practice of adding allowance to stored grain. Lost passages of the History of the Five Dynasties: Mingzong was a frontier man, hardened by war; when he took the throne he was already past sixty, and his purity, generosity, and kindness were inborn. Every night in the palace he burned incense and prayed to Heaven: "I am a barbarian. In a time of chaos the multitude thrust me forward, and I could not refuse. May Heaven soon send a sage to rule the people." Hence between Tiancheng and Changxing the harvests were good year after year, the Central Plain was at peace, and among the Five Dynasties this was reckoned a modest age of ease.)〉
14
史臣曰：明宗戰伐之勳，雖高佐命，潛躍之事，本不經心。 會王室之多艱，屬神器之自至，諒由天讚，匪出人謀。 及應運以君臨，能力行於王化，政皆中道，時亦小康，近代已來，亦可宗也。 倘使重誨得房、杜之術，從榮有啟、誦之賢，則宗祧未至於危亡，載祀或期於綿遠矣。 惜乎！ 君親可輔，臣子非才，遽泯烝嘗，良可深歎矣！
The historian writes: Mingzong's military achievements, though great in service to the founding, were not what he had ever sought; the hidden path to the throne had never been his aim. When the royal house fell into hardship the regalia came to him unbidden—surely Heaven approved, not human design. Once he answered the age and ruled, he was able to carry out true kingship; his policies held to the middle way and the times knew modest peace. In recent memory he may be counted among the exemplary rulers. Had Chonghui possessed the skill of Fang Xuanling and Du Rong, and Congrong the virtue of Qi Que and Song Shen, the ancestral line might not have fallen into peril, and the dynasty might have endured far longer. Alas! A ruler worthy of support had ministers unworthy of the task; the ancestral rites were cut short in a moment—how deeply this deserves our grief!
