毛璋，本滄州小校。 梁將戴思遠帥滄州，時莊宗已定魏博，思遠勢蹙，棄州遁去，璋據城歸莊宗， 〈 （《玉堂閑話》：戴思遠任浮陽日，有部曲毛璋，為性輕悍。 嘗與數十卒追捕盜賊，還宿於逆旅，毛枕劍而寢。 夜分，其劍忽大吼，躍出鞘外，從卒聞者愕然驚異，毛亦神之。 乃持劍祝曰：「某若異日有此山河，爾當更鳴躍，否則已。 」毛復寢，未熟，劍吼躍如初，毛深自負。 其後戴離鎮，毛請留，戴從之。 未幾，毛以州歸命於唐莊宗，莊宗以毛為其州刺史，後竟帥滄海。）〉 曆貝州、遼州刺史。 璋性凶悖，有膽略，從征河上，屢有戰功。 梁平，授滄州節度使。 王師討蜀，以璋為行營右廂馬軍都指揮使； 蜀平，璋功居多。 明年，蕭牆禍起，繼岌自西川至渭南，部下散亡，其川貨妓樂，為璋所掠。 明宗嗣位，錄平蜀功，授邠州節度使。
Mao Zhang began as a low-ranking soldier in Cangzhou. When the Liang general Dai Siyuan held Cangzhou, Zhuangzong had already pacified Weibo. Dai found himself cornered, abandoned the prefecture and fled, and Zhang seized the city and submitted to Zhuangzong, [ (From Idle Talk of the Jade Hall: While Dai Siyuan was stationed at Fuyang, one of his troops was Mao Zhang, a man of reckless and violent temperament. On one occasion he pursued bandits with several dozen men; on the way back they stayed at a roadside inn, and Mao slept with his sword beneath his head. Around midnight the sword suddenly gave a great roar and sprang from its scabbard. The men with him were dumbfounded, and Mao himself took it as an omen. He raised the sword and prayed: "If I am one day to hold these mountains and rivers, you shall roar and leap again; if not, be still." " He lay down once more; before sleep had deepened the sword roared and leaped as before, and Mao was filled with self-assurance. When Dai later left his command, Mao asked to stay behind and Dai agreed. Before long Mao turned the prefecture over to Tang Zhuangzong, who appointed him prefect of Qizhou; in the end he came to command Canghai as military commissioner.)〉 He later served as prefect of Beizhou and Liaozhou in turn. Zhang was savage and defiant, with courage and stratagem; on the river campaigns he won merit again and again. After the Liang was overthrown, he was made military commissioner of Cangzhou. When the court marched against Shu, he was appointed commander of the campaigning army's right-wing cavalry; After Shu was pacified, his contribution ranked among the foremost. The following year palace intrigue broke out. Li Jiji came down from western Shu to Weinan while his followers scattered; the Sichuan goods and entertainers were seized by Zhang. When Mingzong took the throne, his service in pacifying Shu was recorded and he was appointed military commissioner of Binzhou.

璋既家富於財，有蜀之妓樂，驕僭自大，動多不法，招致部下，繕理兵仗。 朝廷移授昭義節度使，璋謀欲不奉詔，判官邊蔚密言規責，乃僶勉承命。 洎至潞州，狂妄不悛，每擁川妓於山亭院，服赭黃，縱酒，令為王衍在蜀之戲。 事聞於朝，征為金吾上將軍。 其年秋，東川節度使董璋上言：「毛璋男廷贇齎父書往西川，慮有陰事。 」因追廷贇及同行人趙延祚，與璋俱下御史台獄。 廷贇乃璋之假侄，稱有叔在蜀，欲往省之，亦無私書，詔停任，令歸私第。 初，延祚在獄，多言璋陰事，璋許重賂，以塞其口。 及免，延祚征其賂，璋拒而不與，以至延祚詣台訴璋翻覆，復下御史台訊鞫。 中丞呂夢奇以璋前蒙昭雪，今延祚以責賂之故，復加織羅，故稍佑璋。 及款狀上聞，或云夢奇受璋賂，所以獄不盡情，執之，移於軍巡。 璋具狀曾許延祚賂未與，又云曾借馬與夢奇，別無行賂之事。 朝廷懲其宿惡，長流儒州，賜死於路。
Once his household was rich with plunder and he possessed Shu entertainers, he grew arrogant and overbearing, broke the law at every turn, gathered followers, and readied arms. The court transferred him to Zhaoyi as military commissioner. Zhang plotted to defy the edict, until his aide Bian Wei secretly rebuked him and he grudgingly obeyed. When he reached Luzhou he did not reform. Day after day he kept Sichuan entertainers in the mountain-pavilion courtyard, dressed in imperial yellow, drank without restraint, and had them perform the same plays Wang Yan had put on in Shu. Word reached the court, and he was recalled to serve as General of the Golden Insignia. That autumn the eastern Sichuan commissioner Dong Zhang memorialized: "Mao Zhang's son Yan Yun is taking his father's letter to western Shu, and one must fear some hidden plot." Yan Yun and his companion Zhao Yanzuo were pursued and seized, and Zhang was also sent down to the Censorate prison. Yan Yun was Zhang's adopted nephew and said only that he had an uncle in Shu whom he wished to visit; there was no secret letter. An edict suspended the case and let him return home. Earlier, while Zhao Yanzuo was in prison, he had disclosed much of Zhang's hidden conduct. Zhang promised him a large bribe to keep silent. After Yanzuo was released he demanded the payment; Zhang refused, and Yanzuo went to the censorate to accuse him of going back on his word. Zhang was interrogated once more. Vice censor Lü Mengqi, seeing that Zhang had already been cleared once and that Yanzuo now pressed a claim over an unpaid bribe, was piling on new charges and therefore leaned somewhat in Zhang's favor. When the written confession reached the throne, some said Mengqi had accepted a bribe from Zhang and therefore had not pressed the case to the full. Mengqi was arrested and the matter moved to Military Circuit. Zhang's full statement admitted that he had promised Yanzuo a bribe but never paid it, and that he had once lent Mengqi a horse, but denied any other bribery. The court punished his long accumulation of evil by banishing him to Ruzhou and ordering him put to death on the way.
3
聶嶼，鄴中人。 少為僧，漸學吟詠。 鄭玨之再主禮闈也，與鄉人趙都俱赴鄉薦，都納賄於玨，人報翌日登第，嶼聞不捷，詬來人以嚇之，玨懼，俾俱成名。 漸為拾遺，依郭崇韜為鎮州書記。 明宗時，為起居舍人。 雙眸若懸，性氣乖僻，人多忌之。 天成初，除鄴都留守判官，與趙敬怡、呂夢奇不足。 又改河東節度，及至，常鄙其土風，薄其人士。 或達於重誨，會敬怡入為樞密使，與夢奇同構殺之。 嶼早依郭崇韜門庭，致身朱紫，名登兩史，浙江使回，生涯巨萬。 嶼內河東節判時，郭氏次子之婦，孀居於家，嶼喪偶未久，復忍而納幣，人皆罪之。 明宗在藩邸時，素聞其醜聲。 天成中，與溫韜等同詔賜死。
Nie Yu came from Ye. As a youth he became a monk and gradually learned to compose verse. When Zheng Jue again ran the metropolitan examinations, Yu and his fellow townsman Zhao Du both sat for the provincial test. Du paid Jue a bribe, and word went about that Du would place the next day. Hearing that he himself had failed, Yu cursed the messenger to scare them. Jue was alarmed and saw to it that both men passed. He rose by degrees to Reminder of the Left and served Guo Chongtao as secretary at Zhenzhou. Under Mingzong he became Drafting Secretary. His eyes bulged as if hanging forward, and his disposition was contrary; many came to dislike him. At the beginning of Tiancheng he was made aide to the Ye garrison commandant and fell out with Zhao Jingyi and Lü Mengqi. He was reassigned to Hedong. Once there, he constantly looked down on local customs and slighted local men of standing. Word of this reached Zhu Hongli. When Jingyi entered office as Military Affairs Commissioner, he joined Mengqi in a plot to kill Yu. Yu had long clung to Guo Chongtao's patronage, rose to the highest court ranks, and had his name entered in both histories. After returning from service as envoy to Zhejiang, his household wealth was enormous. While Yu was judge at Hedong, the younger son of the Guo household had a widowed daughter-in-law living at home. Yu had not long lost his own wife, yet he still sent betrothal gifts to her, and all judged him harshly. Even when Mingzong was still in his princely residence, he had long heard of Yu's foul repute. In the Tiancheng era he was ordered to die by edict, together with Wen Tao and others.

溫韜，華原人。 少為盜，據華原，事李茂貞，名彥韜，後降於梁，更名昭圖。 為耀州節度，唐諸陵在境者悉發之，取所藏金寶：而昭陵最固，悉藏前世圖書，鍾、王紙墨，筆跡如新。 〈 （案：以下有闕文。）〉 移許州節度使，累官至檢校太尉、平章事。 韜素善趙岩，每依附之。 莊宗入汴，岩恃韜與己素厚，遂奔許州。 韜延之於第，斬首傳送闕下。 同光初，韜來朝，郭崇韜曰：「此劫陵賊，罪不可赦。 」韜納賂劉後，賜姓，名紹衝，遽遣還鎮。 明宗即位，流於德州，俄賜死。
Wen Tao came from Huayuan. In his youth he was a bandit who seized Huayuan and served Li Maozhen under the name Yan Tao. Later he surrendered to Liang and was renamed Zhaotu. As commissioner of Yaozhou he opened every Tang imperial tomb within his circuit and took the buried treasure. Zhaoling was the most tightly built; it held the books of former ages and the ink of Zhong You and Wang Xizhi, the writing still fresh. [ (Note: the passage below is missing.)〉 He was moved to Xuzhou as military commissioner and eventually reached Grand General and Chancellor. Tao had long been on good terms with Zhao Yan and constantly leaned on him. When Zhuangzong entered Bian, Yan trusted their old ties and fled to Xuzhou. Tao received him in his residence, then beheaded him and sent the head to the court. Early in Tongguang, Tao came to court. Guo Chongtao said, "This man who plundered the imperial tombs—his crime cannot be forgiven." Tao bribed Empress Liu, was granted the imperial surname and the name Shaochong, and was quickly sent back to his command. When Mingzong succeeded, Tao was exiled to Dezhou and soon ordered to die by edict.

長子延浚，清泰中為泥水關使； 次延招，為父牙帳都校； 次延袤，鄧州指揮使； 鹹聚居許下。 晉天福初，聞張從賓作亂於河陽，鹹往依之。 從賓慮其難製，悉斬於帳下。
His eldest son Yan Jun, in the Qingtai era, was officer at Nishui Pass; the second, Yan Zhao, was commandant of his father's headquarters guard; the third, Yan Mao, was commandant at Dengzhou; and all of them dwelt together in Xuzhou. Early in Jin Tianfu, when they heard that Zhang Congbin had risen at Heyang, they all went to join him. Congbin feared they would be difficult to control and had them all beheaded in his tent.

段凝，開封人也。 本名明遠，少穎悟，多智數。 初為澠池簿，脫荷衣以事梁祖，梁祖漸器之。 開平三年十月，自東頭供奉官授右威衛大將軍，充左軍巡使兼水北巡檢使。 凝妹為梁祖美人，故稍委心腹。 四年五月，授懷州刺史。 乾化元年十二月，梁祖北征回，過郡，凝貢獻加等，梁祖大悅。 梁祖復北，凝迎奉進貢，有加於前。 梁祖次相州，刺史李思安迎奉疏怠，梁祖怒，貶思安。 製云：「懷州刺史段明遠，少年治郡，庶事惟公，兩度隻奉行鑾，數程宿食本界，動無遺闕，舉必周豐，蓋能罄竭於家財，務在顯酬夫明獎。 觀明遠之忠勤若此，見思安之悖慢何如！ 」其見賞如此。 其後，遷鄭州刺史，監大軍於河上。 梁末帝以戴思遠為北面招討使。 行師不利，用王彥章代之，受任之翌日，取德勝之南城，軍聲大振。 張漢倫等推功於凝，凝掎摭彥章之失以間之。 〈 （《通鑒》：彥章棄鄒家口，復趨楊劉。 遊奕將李紹興敗梁遊兵於清邱縣南，段凝以為唐兵已自上流渡，驚駭失色，麵數彥章，尤其深入。）〉 梁末帝怒，罷彥章兵權。 凝納賂於趙、張二族，求為招討使，敬翔、李振極言不可，竟不能止。 凝以眾五萬營於高陵津，裨將康延孝叛歸莊宗，延孝具陳梁軍虛實，莊宗遂決長驅之計。 未幾，莊宗入汴，凝自滑率兵而南，前鋒杜晏球至封丘，解甲聽命。 翌日，凝率大軍乞降於汴郊，莊宗釋之，復以凝為滑州兵馬留後，賜姓，名紹欽。 有頃，正授節度，改兗州節度使。 凝初見莊宗，因伶人景進通貨於宮掖。 凝天性奸佞，巧言飾智，善候人意。 其年，契丹寇幽州，命宣徽使李紹宏監護諸軍，以禦契丹，凝與董璋戍瓦橋關。 凝巧事紹宏，紹宏嘗乘間奏凝蓋世奇才，可以大任，屢請以兵柄委之。 郭崇韜曰：「段凝亡國敗軍之將，奸諂難狀，不要信也。 」凝在藩鎮，私用庫物數萬計，有司促償，中旨貰其負。 同光三年四月，移授鄧州節度使。 四年二月，趙在禮據鄴城，李紹宏請用凝為大將，莊宗許之，令具方略條奏。 凝所請偏裨皆取其己黨，莊宗疑之，乃止。 明宗至洛陽，霍彥威怒其前事，與溫韜同收下獄，詔釋之，放歸田里。 明年，竄於遼州，竟與溫韜同製賜死。
Duan Ning came from Kaifeng. His original name was Mingyuan. Even as a youth he was quick-witted and full of schemes. He began as a clerk at Mianchi, then cast off his scholar's robes to serve the Liang Founder, who gradually came to rely on him. In the tenth month of Kaiping year 3 he rose from eastern-head attendant to Right Guard General, and was made left-army patrol officer and northern-water inspector. Ning's younger sister became one of the Liang Founder's palace beauties, and for that reason he won the Founder's confidential trust. In the fifth month of year 4 he was appointed prefect of Huaizhou. In the twelfth month of Qianhua year 1 the Liang Founder returned north from campaign and passed through his prefecture. Ning's tribute was richer than ever, and the Founder was greatly pleased. When the Founder marched north again, Ning met him with gifts still more lavish than before. The Founder halted at Xiangzhou. Prefect Li Si'an's reception was slack, and the Founder in anger demoted him. The edict declared: "Huaizhou prefect Duan Mingyuan, though young in office, governs with equity in all matters. Twice he alone attended the imperial carriage; on many stages he lodged and supplied the court within his own circuit without a single lapse, every provision complete—draining his family wealth to earn plain recognition of bright merit." When one sees Mingyuan's loyalty and diligence on this scale, what of Si'an's insolence and neglect?" Such was the esteem in which he was held. Later he was made prefect of Zhengzhou and placed in charge of the great army on the Yellow River. The Liang Last Emperor appointed Dai Siyuan northern campaign commander. The campaign fared badly, and Wang Yanzhang replaced him. The day after Yanzhang took command he seized the south city of Desheng, and the army's spirit rose sharply. Zhang Hanlun and others pushed the merit onto Ning, while Ning picked at Yanzhang's faults to drive a wedge between them. [ (Zizhi Tongjian: Yanzhang abandoned Zoujiakou and hurried to Yangliu. Patrol officer Li Shaoxing defeated Liang scouts south of Qingqiu county. Duan Ning, believing Tang troops had already crossed upstream, turned pale and rebuked Yanzhang to his face, blaming him above all for pushing too far forward.)〉 The Liang Last Emperor was enraged and removed Yanzhang's military authority. Ning bribed the Zhao and Zhang factions to win the post of campaign commander. Jing Xiang and Li Zhen argued strenuously against it but could not prevail. Ning encamped fifty thousand men at Gaoling Ford. His deputy Kang Yanxiao defected to Zhuangzong and described the Liang forces in detail; Zhuangzong then resolved on a long thrust forward. Soon Zhuangzong entered Bian. Ning marched south from Hua, while his vanguard Du Yanqiu at Fengqiu cast off his armor and submitted. The following day Ning surrendered with the main force outside Bian. Zhuangzong spared him, made him military defender at Hua, and granted him the imperial surname with the name Shaoqin. Before long he received a regular commission as military commissioner and was moved to Yanzhou. When he first met Zhuangzong, he used the actor Jing Jin to pass gifts into the inner palace. By nature Ning was treacherous, clever with words and false in counsel, and adept at guessing what others wished to hear. That year the Khitan attacked Youzhou. Li Shaohong was sent as palace commissioner to oversee the armies against them, while Ning and Dong Zhang guarded Waqiao Pass. Ning fawned on Shaohong, who in turn several times memorialized that Ning was a talent without peer and fit for heavy responsibility, repeatedly asking that the command of troops be given to him. Guo Chongtao said, "Duan Ning is the commander who lost the state and the army. His flattery is beyond description—do not trust him." While holding a command he privately drew on treasury goods worth tens of thousands. The relevant offices demanded repayment, but an imperial edict canceled what he owed. In the fourth month of Tongguang year 3 he was transferred to Dengzhou as military commissioner. In the second month of year 4 Zhao Zaili seized Yecheng. Li Shaohong asked that Ning be made supreme commander. Zhuangzong agreed and ordered him to submit a detailed plan. Every deputy Ning named was from his own clique. Zhuangzong grew suspicious and dropped the matter. When Mingzong came to Luoyang, Huo Yanwei was incensed over his earlier conduct. He and Wen Tao were seized and imprisoned together, then an edict released them and sent them home. The following year he fled to Liaozhou and was finally ordered to die under the same edict as Wen Tao.

孔謙， 〈 （《通鑒》作魏州人。）〉 莊宗同光初，為租庸副使。 謙本州之幹吏，上自天祐十二年，帝平定魏博，會計皆委製置。 謙能曲事權要，效其才力，帝委以泉貨之務，設法箕斂，七八年間，軍儲獲濟。 及帝即位於鄴城，謙已當為租庸使。 物議以謙雖有經營濟贍之勞，然人地尚卑，不欲驟總重任。 樞密使郭崇韜舉魏博觀察判官張憲為租庸使，以謙為副，謙悒然不樂者久之。
Kong Qian, [ (The Comprehensive Mirror says he was a man of Weizhou.)〉 Early in Tongguang, Zhuangzong appointed him deputy commissioner for tax and corvée. Qian had been a capable clerk in the prefecture. From Tianyou year 12, when the Emperor pacified Weibo, all fiscal accounts were placed in his hands. Qian knew how to curry favor with the powerful and put his talents to use. The Emperor entrusted him with fiscal affairs; he devised schemes of squeezing levies, and within seven or eight years the army's stores were kept full. By the time the Emperor took the throne at Yecheng, Qian was already the natural choice for commissioner of tax and corvée. Opinion held that although Qian had merit in managing and supplying the treasury, his rank and standing were still low, and courtiers did not want to thrust the highest office on him at once. Guo Chongtao, Military Affairs Commissioner, recommended Zhang Xian, observation judge at Weibo, as commissioner, with Qian as deputy. Qian brooded over this for a long time.

帝既平梁汴，謙徑自魏州馳之行在，因謂崇韜曰：「魏都重地，須大臣彈壓，以謙籌之，非張憲不可。 」崇韜以為忠告，即奏憲為鄴都副留守，乃命宰臣豆盧革專判租庸。 謙彌失望，乃尋革過失。 時革以手書便省庫錢數十萬，謙以手書示崇韜，亦辭避。 帝問：「當委何人為可？ 」崇韜曰：「孔謙雖久掌貨泉，然物議未當居大任，以臣所見，當委張憲為便。 」帝促征之。 憲性精辨，為趨時者所忌，人不祐之。 謙乘間訴於豆盧革曰：「租庸錢穀，悉在眼前，委一小吏可辦。 鄴都本根之地，不可輕付於人。 興唐尹王正言無裨益之才，徒有獨行，詔書既征張憲，復以何人為代？ 」豆盧革言於崇韜，崇韜曰：「鄴都分司列職，皆主上舊人，委王正言何慮不辦？ 」革曰：「俱是失也，設不獲已，以正言掌租庸，取書於大臣，或可辦矣； 若付之方面，必敗人事。 」謙以正言非德非勳，懦而易製，曰：「此議為便。 」然非己誌。 尋掎正言之失，泣訴於崇韜，厚賂閹伶，以求進用，人知奸諂，沮之，乃上章請退。 帝怒其規避，將置於法，樂人景進於帝前解喻而止。 王正言風病恍惚，不能綜三司事，景進屢言於帝，乃以正言守禮部尚書，以謙為租庸使。 謙以國用不足，奏：「諸道判官員數過多，請隻置節度、觀察、判官、書記、支使、推官各一員。 留守置判官各一員。 三京府置判官、推官，餘並罷俸錢。 」又奏：「百官俸錢雖多，折支非實，請減半數，皆支實錢。 」並從之。 未幾，半年俸復從虛折。 〈 （案：以下原闕。 《北夢瑣言》：明宗即位，誅租庸使孔謙等。 孔謙者，魏州孔目。 莊宗圖霸，以供饋兵食，謙有力焉。 既為租庸使，曲事嬖幸，奪宰相權，專以聚斂為意，剝削為端。 以犯眾怒，伏誅。）〉
Once the Emperor had pacified the Liang capital at Bian, Qian galloped from Weizhou to the imperial camp and told Chongtao: "Yecheng is a vital post and needs a senior minister to keep it firm. By my judgment Zhang Xian is not the man." " Chongtao took this as honest advice and immediately memorialized Zhang Xian as deputy garrison commandant of Yecheng, while chief minister Dou Lüge alone was put in charge of tax and corvée. Qian grew still more bitter and began hunting for Lüge's mistakes. At the time Lüge had taken several hundred thousand from the convenience treasury by personal letter. Qian showed the letter to Chongtao, who likewise refused the assignment. The Emperor asked, "Who should take the post?" " Chongtao replied, "Qian has long handled money affairs, but opinion does not yet deem him fit for the top post. In my view Zhang Xian would be the better choice." " The Emperor hurriedly summoned Zhang Xian. Zhang was sharp and discriminating by nature. Men who chased fashion resented him, and no one spoke up for him. Qian seized a chance to complain to Dou Lüge: "Tax and corvée funds are plain to see; a minor clerk could manage them." Yecheng is the foundation of the realm and must not be lightly handed to anyone. Xingtang prefect Wang Zhengyan has no talent that truly helps the state, only a reputation for solitary integrity. The edict has already summoned Zhang Xian—who should take his place?" " Lüge spoke to Chongtao, who said, "The branch offices at Yecheng are staffed with the sovereign's old men. If Wang Zhengyan is entrusted with it, what is there to fear?" " Lüge said, "Both courses are mistakes. If we must choose, let Zhengyan handle tax and corvée and take written instructions from the senior ministers—perhaps that could work; but if it is given to a frontier commissioner, affairs will surely be ruined." " Qian, seeing that Zhengyan lacked virtue and merit and was timid and easy to manipulate, said, "That plan will do." " Yet it was not what he truly wanted. Soon he seized on Zhengyan's faults, wept before Chongtao, and lavished bribes on eunuch actors to win promotion. When men saw his flattery and blocked him, he submitted a memorial asking to withdraw. The Emperor was angry at his shirking and was about to punish him. The musician Jing Jin pleaded before the throne and stayed the matter. Wang Zhengyan was afflicted with wind sickness and mental confusion and could not manage the Three Departments. Jing Jin spoke to the Emperor again and again, until Zhengyan was made Minister of Rites and Qian became commissioner for tax and corvée. Because state revenue was insufficient, Qian memorialized: "There are too many circuit judges in the provinces. Please allow only one each for military commissioner, observation commissioner, judge, secretary, branch commissioner, and investigating officer." For garrison commandants, one judge each. For the three capital prefectures, judges and investigating officers; all other salary payments should be cut off. " He also memorialized: "Although officials' salaries are large on paper, the discounted payments are not real money. Please cut them by half and pay entirely in cash." " Both proposals were approved. Before long, within half a year salaries again followed fictitious discount payments. [ (Note: the original passage below is missing. 李鄴，魏州人也。 幼事楊師厚，及莊宗入魏，漸轉裨將，曆數郡刺史，後遷亳州。 為政貪穢，有奴為人持金以賂鄴，奴隱其金，鄴殺之。 其家上訴，因訐其陰事，詔貶郴州司戶參軍，又貶崖州長流百姓，所在賜自盡。
Li Ye came from Weizhou. As a youth he served Yang Shihou. When Zhuangzong entered Wei he rose by degrees to deputy general, held several prefectures in succession, and was later moved to Bozhou. In office he was greedy and corrupt. A slave was carrying gold for someone else to bribe Ye; the slave concealed the gold, and Ye killed him. The family appealed. Because they exposed his hidden conduct, an edict demoted him to registrar at Chenzhou, then exiled him as a commoner to Yazhou with orders that wherever he was found he should be allowed to take his own life.

史臣曰：《易》云：「積不善之家，必有餘殃。 」又曰：「惡不積不足以滅身。 」如毛璋之儔，可謂積惡而滅其身矣，況溫韜之發陵寢，段凝之敗國家，罪不容誅，死猶差晚。 餘皆瑣瑣，何足議焉。
The historiographer remarks: The Book of Changes says, "A house that accumulates evil will surely have calamity left over." " It also says, "Evil that is not accumulated is not enough to destroy the person." " Men like Mao Zhang may be said to have piled up evil until it destroyed them. How much more Wen Tao, who opened imperial tombs, and Duan Ning, who ruined the state—their crimes could not be forgiven, and even death came late. The rest were trifles and hardly merit discussion.
