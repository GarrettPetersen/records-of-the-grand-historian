1
開運三年冬十月甲子，正衙命使冊皇太妃安氏。 己丑，以樞密直學士、禮部侍郎邊光範為翰林學士，以給事中邊歸讜為左散騎常侍，以翰林學士、祠部員外郎、知制誥張沆為右諫議大夫。 辛未，以鄴都留守杜威為北面行營都招討使，以侍衛親軍都指揮使、鄆州節度使李守貞為兵馬都監，兗州安審琦為左右廂都指揮使，徐州符彥卿為馬軍左廂都指揮使，滑州皇甫遇為馬軍右廂都指揮使，貝州梁漢璋為馬軍都排陣使，前鄧州宋彥筠為步軍左廂都指揮使，奉國左廂都指揮使王饒為步軍右廂都指揮使，洺州團練使薛懷讓為先鋒都指揮使。 〈 （案：《通鑒》載，當時敕榜曰：「先取瀛、鄚，安定關南； 次復幽、燕，蕩平塞北。 」蓋狃於陽城之役而驟驕也。）〉 癸酉，冊吳國夫人馮氏為皇后。 乙亥，以侍衛馬軍都指揮使李彥韜權知侍衛司事。 丙戌，鳳翔節度使秦王李從嚴薨，輟朝，冊贈尚書令。 丁亥，邠州節度使李德充卒，輟朝，贈太尉。
On the jiazi day in the tenth month of winter, Kaiyun year 3, an envoy commissioned at the main court invested Lady An as Grand Imperial Consort. On jichou, Bian Guangfan—Direct Academician of the Palace Secretariat and Vice Minister of Rites—was appointed Hanlin Academician; Bian Guichan, Supervising Secretary, became Left Regular Attendant of the Palace Horse; and Zhang Hong, Hanlin Academician, Clerk of the Ministry of Rites (extra rank), and Drafter of Imperial Edicts, was made Right Remonstrating Censor. On xinwei, Du Wei, who held Ye as his base, was appointed overall commander of the northern campaign; Li Shouzhen, commander of the imperial guard and military governor of Yanzhou, became supervisor of all troops and horses; An Shenqi of Yanzhou took command of both wings; Fu Yanqing of Xuzhou headed the left wing of the cavalry; Huangfu Yu of Huazhou the right wing of the cavalry; Liang Hanzhang of Beizhou marshaled the cavalry formation; the former governor of Dengzhou, Song Yanjun, led the left wing of the infantry; Wang Rao, commander of the Fengguo left wing, the right wing of the infantry; and Xue Huairang, militia commissioner of Mingzhou, served as vanguard commander. ( (Zizhi Tongjian: The edict placard of the time proclaimed, "First seize Ying and Mo and secure the territory south of the pass; then recover You and Yan and pacify the entire northern frontier." They had grown overconfident after Yangcheng and were suddenly boastful.)] On guiyou, Lady Feng of Wu was enthroned as empress. On yihai, Li Yantao, commander of the imperial guard cavalry, was placed in temporary charge of the imperial guard bureau. On bingxu, Li Congyan, Prince of Qin and military governor of Fengxiang, died; the court suspended audiences and posthumously invested him as Grand Master of the Secretariat. On dinghai, Li Dechong, military governor of Binzhou, died; audiences were suspended and he was posthumously made Grand Marshal.
2
十一月戊子朔，以給事中盧撰為右散騎常侍，以尚書兵部郎中兼侍御史、知雜事陳觀為左諫議大夫。 觀以祖諱「義」，乞改官，尋授給事中。 庚寅，樞密使、中書侍郎兼戶部尚書、平章事馮玉加尚書右僕射，以皇子鎮寧軍節度使延煦為陜州節度使，以陜州留後焦繼勛為鳳翔留後，以前定州留後安審琦為邠州留後，以右僕射和凝為左僕射。 甲午，兩浙節度使吳越國王錢宏佐起復舊任。 丁酉，詔李守貞知幽州行府事。 戊申，日南至，禦崇元殿受朝賀。 是月，北面行營招討使杜威率諸將領大軍自鄴北征，師次瀛州城下，貝州節度使梁漢璋戰死。 杜威等以漢璋之敗，遂收軍而退。 行次武強，聞契丹入寇，欲取直路，自冀、貝而南。 會張彥澤領騎自鎮定至，且言契丹可破之狀，於是大軍西趨鎮州。
On the first day of the eleventh month, wuzi, Supervising Secretary Lu Zhuan became Right Regular Attendant of the Palace Horse, and Chen Guan, director in the Bureau of Military Affairs who also served as censor in charge of miscellaneous affairs, was appointed Left Remonstrating Censor. Because his grandfather's taboo name was Yi, Guan asked to be reassigned and was soon made Supervising Secretary. On gengyin, Feng Yu, palace commissioner, vice director of the secretariat, concurrent minister of revenue, and grand councilor, was promoted to Right Vice Director of the Imperial Secretariat; Prince Yanshu, military governor of Zhenning, was sent to Shanzhou; Jiao Jixun, acting governor of Shanzhou, to Fengxiang; the former acting governor of Dingzhou, An Shenqi, to Binzhou; and He Ning moved from right to left vice director. On jiawu, Qian Hongzuo, King of Wuyue and military governor of the Two Zhes, returned from mourning to his former duties. On dingyou, Li Shouzhen was ordered to take charge of the campaign headquarters at Youzhou. On wushen, the winter solstice, the emperor held court at Chongyuan Hall to receive the seasonal congratulations. That month, Du Wei, commander of the northern campaign, marched north from Ye at the head of the main army; when the force halted beneath Yingzhou, Liang Hanzhang, military governor of Beizhou, was killed in action. After Hanzhang's defeat, Du Wei and his commanders pulled the army back. When the column reached Wuqiang and learned that the Khitan had invaded, they intended to march south by the direct route through Ji and Bei. Zhang Yanzhe then arrived from Zhen and Ding with cavalry, assuring them that the Khitan could be beaten, and the main army wheeled west toward Zhenzhou.
3
十二月丁巳朔， 〈 （案：以下有闕文。 據《通鑒》云：丁巳朔，李穀自書密奏，且言大軍危急之勢，請車駕幸滑州，遣高行周、符彥卿扈從，及發兵守澶州、河陽，以備敵之奔沖。 遣軍將關勛走馬上之。）〉 己未，杜威奏，駐軍於中渡橋。 庚申，以前司農卿儲延英為太子賓客。 詔徐州符彥卿屯澶州。 辛酉，詔澤潞、鄴都、邢洺、河陽運糧赴中渡，杜威遣人口奏軍前事宜，勢迫故也。 壬戌，又遣高行周屯澶州，景延廣守河陽。 博野縣都監張鵬入奏蕃軍事勢。 丙寅，定州李殷奏，前月二十八日夜，領捉生四百人往曲陽嘉山下，逢敵軍車帳，殺千餘人，獲馬二百匹。 詔宋州高行周充北面行營都部署，符彥卿充副，邢州方太充都虞候，領後軍駐於河上，以備敵騎之奔沖也。 時契丹遊騎涉滹水而南，至欒城縣。 自是中渡寨為蕃軍隔絕，探報不通，朝廷大恐，故委行周等繼領兵師守扼津要，且以張其勢也。 己巳，邢州方太奏，此月六日，契丹與王師戰於中渡，王師不利，奉國都指揮使王清戰死。 庚午，幸沙臺射兔。 壬申，始聞杜威、李守貞等以此月十日率諸軍降於契丹。 是夜，相州節度使張彥澤受契丹命，率先鋒二千人，自封丘門斬關而入。 癸酉旦，張彥澤頓兵於明德門外，京城大擾。 前曹州節度使石赟死，帝之堂叔也。 時自中渡寨隔絕之後，帝與大臣端坐憂危，國之衛兵，悉在北面，計無所出。 十六日聞滹水之降。 是夜，偵知張彥澤已至滑州，召李崧、馮玉、李彥韜入內計事，方議詔河東劉知遠起兵赴難，至五鼓初，張彥澤引蕃騎入京。 宮中相次火起，帝自攜劍驅擁后妃已下十數人，將同赴火，為親校薛超所持。 俄自寬仁門遞入契丹主與皇太后書，帝乃止，旋令撲滅煙火。 大內都點檢康福全在寬仁門宿衛，登樓覘賊，彥澤呼而下之。 癸酉，帝奉表於戎主曰：
On the first day of the twelfth month, dingsi, ( (Note: Text is missing below. Zizhi Tongjian reports that on dingsi, Li Gu wrote a secret memorial in his own hand describing how desperate the army's position had become. He urged the emperor to move to Huazhou with Gao Xingzhou and Fu Yanqing as escorts, and to dispatch troops to hold Chanzhou and Heyang against a sudden enemy thrust. He sent the officer Guan Xun to deliver it at a gallop.)] On jiwei, Du Wei reported that his army was encamped at Zhongdu Bridge. On gengshen, the former Minister of Agriculture, Chu Yanying, was appointed Mentor of the Heir Apparent. Fu Yanqing of Xuzhou was ordered to garrison Chanzhou. On xinyou, Ze-Lu, Ye, Xing-Ming, and Heyang were ordered to ship grain to Zhongdu; Du Wei sent a courier to report orally from the front because the crisis left no time for written memorials. On renxu, Gao Xingzhou was again posted to Chanzhou and Jing Yanguang assigned to defend Heyang. Zhang Peng, commandant of Boye County, came to court with a report on the Khitan forces. On bingyin, Li Yin of Dingzhou reported that on the night of the twenty-eighth of the previous month he had led four hundred scouts to Jia Mountain below Quyang, surprised an enemy encampment, killed more than a thousand men, and seized two hundred horses. Gao Xingzhou of Songzhou was made overall commander of the northern campaign, with Fu Yanqing as his deputy and Fang Tai of Xingzhou as chief controller; they were to hold the rear army along the river against a sudden Khitan cavalry thrust. Khitan patrols had crossed the Hutuo south as far as Luancheng County. The Zhongdu camp was now cut off from the capital, and no scouts could get through. The court was terrified and therefore sent Xingzhou and the others in turn to hold the key crossings—and to make a show of strength. On jisi, Fang Tai of Xingzhou reported that on the sixth the Khitan had fought the imperial army at Zhongdu; the imperial forces were beaten, and Wang Qing, commander of Fengguo, was killed. On gengwu, the emperor went to Shatai to hunt hares. On renshen, the court first learned that on the tenth Du Wei, Li Shouzhen, and the rest had surrendered the entire army to the Khitan. That night, Zhang Yanzhe of Xiangzhou, acting on Khitan orders, led two thousand vanguard troops through Fengqiu Gate after cutting down the guards. At dawn on guiyou, Zhang Yanzhe halted his troops outside Mingde Gate, and the capital erupted in chaos. Shi Yun, former military governor of Caozhou and the emperor's paternal uncle, died. After the Zhongdu camp was cut off, the emperor and his ministers sat helpless in dread; every soldier of the imperial guard was in the north, and no plan offered itself. On the sixteenth came word of the surrender at the Hutuo. That night scouts reported Zhang Yanzhe at Huazhou. Li Song, Feng Yu, and Li Yantao were called in to counsel the throne; they were debating an edict to Liu Zhiyuan in Hedong to march to the rescue when, at the fifth watch, Zhang Yanzhe rode into the capital at the head of Khitan cavalry. Fires broke out throughout the palace. The emperor drew his sword and drove the empress, his consorts, and more than a dozen attendants toward the flames, intending to die with them, but his personal guard Xue Chao seized and restrained him. Soon a letter from the Khitan emperor and the empress dowager was delivered through Kuanren Gate. The emperor desisted and ordered the fires put out. Kang Fuquan, chief inspector of the inner palace, was on guard at Kuanren Gate. He climbed a tower to watch the enemy, and Yanzhe summoned him down. On guiyou, the emperor submitted this memorial to the Khitan lord:
4
孫臣某言：今月十七日寅時，相州節度使張彥澤、都監傅住兒部領大軍入京，賫到翁皇帝賜太后書示，於滹沱河降下杜重威一行馬步兵士，見領蕃漢步騎來幸汴州者。
Your grandson humbly reports: At the yin hour on the seventeenth of this month, Zhang Yanzhe of Xiangzhou and Commandant Fu Zhu'er entered the capital at the head of a great army bearing the letter Grandfather Emperor had sent the empress dowager. It showed that Du Chongwei's entire army had surrendered at the Hutuo and that Khitan and Han infantry and cavalry were now marching on Bianzhou.
5
往者，唐運告終，中原失馭，數窮否極，天缺地傾。 先人有田一成，有眾一旅，兵連禍結，力屈勢孤。 翁皇帝救患摧鋒，興利除害，躬擐甲胄，深入寇場。 犯露蒙霜，度雁門之險； 馳風掣電，行中冀之誅。 黃鉞一麾，天下大定。 勢淩宇宙，義感神明，功成不居，遂興晉祚，則翁皇帝有大造於石氏也。
In former days the mandate of Tang expired; the Central Plains lost their ruler; fate had run its course and ruin was complete, as if heaven and earth themselves were breaking apart. My forebear held barely a single district and a single brigade; war piled upon disaster until his strength was spent and he stood alone. Grandfather Emperor came to our rescue, shattered the enemy's van, and worked for the people's good; he himself buckled on armor and plunged deep into the field of battle. He endured dew and frost and crossed the peril of Yanmen Pass; he swept like wind and lightning to execute justice in central Ji. At a single wave of the battle-axe, the realm was pacified. His might overawed heaven and earth; his righteousness moved the spirits. He achieved victory yet claimed no glory, and in doing so raised the Jin throne. Grandfather Emperor thus bestowed a great founding gift upon the House of Shi.
6
旋屬天降鞠兇，先君即世，臣遵承遺旨，纘紹前基。 諒暗之初，荒迷失次，凡有軍國重事，皆委將相大臣。 至於擅繼宗祧，既非稟命； 輕發文字，輒敢抗尊。 自啟釁端，果貽赫怒，禍至神惑，運盡天亡。 十萬師徒，皆望風而束手； 億兆黎庶，悉延頸以歸心。 臣負義包羞，貪生忍恥，自貽顛覆，上累祖宗，偷度晨昏，茍存食息。 翁皇帝若惠顧疇昔，稍霽雷霆，未賜靈誅，不絕先祀，則百口荷更生之德，一門銜無報之恩，雖所願焉，非敢望也。 臣與太后並妻馮氏及舉家戚屬，見於郊野面縛俟罪次。 所有國寶一面、金印三面，今遣長子陜府節度使延煦、次子曹州節度使延寶管押進納，並奉表請罪，陳謝以聞。
Soon heaven sent fresh calamity: my late father died, and I, obeying his final charge, took up the foundation he had laid. In the first days of mourning I was lost and disordered; every weighty matter of army and state I left to my generals and ministers. As for seizing the succession on my own authority, I had received no command to do so; and in issuing edicts lightly I repeatedly defied your exalted will. I myself provoked this war and truly earned your thunderous wrath. Calamity fell, the spirits themselves were confounded, my fortune ran out, and heaven decreed my end. A hundred thousand soldiers laid down their arms at the first rumor of your approach; and countless common people craned their necks in longing for your rule. I have betrayed my duty and wrapped myself in shame, clinging to life and enduring disgrace. I brought ruin on myself and shamed my ancestors, stealing through each day merely to keep breathing. If Grandfather Emperor would look back on former ties, temper his wrath, withhold the death I deserve, and let our ancestral rites continue, a hundred mouths would owe him the gift of new life and our whole house a debt we could never repay. That is my deepest wish, though I dare not expect it. The empress dowager, my wife Lady Feng, our kinsmen, and I wait in the suburbs with bound hands, ready to receive judgment. I send one imperial seal and three golden seals, escorted by my eldest son Yanshu, military governor of Shan, and my second son Yanbao, military governor of Cao, together with this memorial begging your pardon.
7
甲戌，張彥澤遷帝與太后及諸宮屬於開封府，遣控鶴指揮使李榮將兵監守。 是夜，開封尹桑維翰、宣徽使孟承誨皆遇害。 帝以契丹主將至，欲與太后出迎，彥澤先表之，稟契丹主之旨報云：「比欲許爾朝覲上國，臣僚奏言，豈有兩個天子道路相見！ 今賜所佩刀子，以慰爾心。 」己卯，皇子延煦、延寶自帳中回，得敵詔慰撫，帝表謝之。 時契丹主以所送傳國寶制造非工，與載籍所述者異，使人來問。 帝進狀曰：「頃以偽主王從珂於洛京大內自焚之後，其真傳國寶不知所在，必是當時焚之。 先帝受命，旋制此寶，在位臣僚，備知其事。 臣至今日，敢有隱藏」云。 時移內庫至府，帝使人取帛數段，主者不與，謂使者曰：「此非我所有也。 」又使人詣李崧求酒，崧曰：「臣有酒非敢愛惜，慮陛下杯酌之後憂躁，所作別有不測之事，臣以此不敢奉進。 」丙戌晦，百官宿封禪寺。
On jiaxu, Zhang Yanzhe transferred the emperor, the empress dowager, and the palace household to the Kaifeng prefectural yamen and posted Li Rong of the Crane-Control Guard with troops to keep watch. That night Sang Weihan, prefect of Kaifeng, and Meng Chenghui, palace commissioner, were murdered. When the Khitan emperor was expected, the emperor wished to go out with the empress dowager to welcome him. Yanzhe reported the Khitan reply in advance: "We had meant to let you pay court in our realm, but our ministers objected: how can two Sons of Heaven meet on the road? We now give you the knife at your belt to comfort you. On jimao, Yanshu and Yanbao returned from the Khitan camp with a message of reassurance, and the emperor sent a memorial of thanks. The Khitan emperor found the seal they had sent poorly made and unlike the descriptions in the classics, and sent an envoy to question them. The emperor replied: "After the usurper Wang Congke burned himself in the Luoyang palace, the true imperial seal vanished and was surely destroyed in that fire. When my late father took the throne he had this seal made at once; every minister then in office knows it. To this day I would not dare conceal anything," he said. When the inner treasury was moved to the prefectural yamen, the emperor sent for several lengths of silk; the keeper refused, telling the messenger, "These are no longer mine. He also sent to Li Song for wine. Song said, "I have wine and do not begrudge it, but I fear that after you drink, grief may drive you to some desperate act. For that reason I dare not send it. On bingxu, the last day of the month, the officials lodged at Fengchan Temple.

明年正月朔，契丹主次東京城北。 百官列班，遙辭帝於寺，詣北郊以迎契丹主。 帝舉族出封丘門，肩轝至野，契丹主不與之見，遣泊封禪寺。 文武百官素服紗帽，迎謁契丹主於郊次，俯伏俟罪，契丹主命起之，親自慰撫。 契丹主遂入大內，至昏出宮，是夜宿於赤堈。 偽詔應晉朝臣僚一切仍舊，朝廷儀制並用漢禮。 戊子，殺鄭州防禦使楊承勛，責以背父之罪，令左右臠割而死。 〈 （《遼史》：以其弟承信為平盧軍節度使，襲父爵。）〉 己丑，斬張彥澤於市，以其剽劫京城，恣行屠害也。 〈 （《遼史》云：以張彥澤擅徙重貴開封，殺桑維翰，縱兵大掠，不道，斬於市。）〉 庚寅，洛京留守景延廣自扼吭而死。 辛卯，契丹制，降帝為光祿大夫、檢校太尉，封負義侯，黃龍府安置。 其地在渤海國界。 癸巳，遷帝於封禪寺，遣蕃大將崔廷勛將兵守之。 癸卯，帝與皇太后李氏、皇太妃安氏、皇后馮氏、皇弟重睿、皇子延煦延寶俱北行，以宮嬪五十人、內官三十人、東西班五十人、醫官一人、控鶴官四人、禦廚七人、茶酒三人、儀鸞司三人、軍健二十人從行。 宰臣趙瑩、樞密使馮玉、侍衛馬軍都指揮使李彥韜隨帝入蕃，契丹主遣三百騎援送而去。 所經州郡，長吏迎奉，皆為契丹主阻絕，有所供饋亦不通。 〈 （《宋史·李穀傳》：少帝蒙塵而北，舊臣無敢候謁者，穀獨拜迎於路，君臣相對泣下。 穀曰：「臣無狀，負陛下。 」因傾囊以獻。）〉 嘗一日，帝與太后不能得食，乃殺畜而啖之。 帝過中渡橋，閱前杜威營寨之跡，慨然憤嘆，謂左右曰：「我家何負，為此賊所破，天乎！ 天乎！ 」於是號慟而去。 至幽州，傾城士庶迎看於路，見帝慘沮，無不嗟嘆。 〈 （《宣政雜錄》：徽宗北狩，經薊縣梁魚務，有還鄉橋，石少帝所命名也，里人至今呼之。）〉 駐留旬餘，州將承契丹命，犒帝於府署，趙延壽母以食饌來獻。 自范陽行數十程，過薊州、平州，至榆關沙塞之地，略無供給，每至宿頓，無非路次，一行乏食，宮女、從官但采木實野蔬，以救饑弊。 又行七八日至錦州，契丹迫帝與妃後往拜安巴堅遺像，帝不勝屈辱，泣曰：「薛超誤我，不令我死，以至今日也。 」又行數十程，渡遼水，至黃龍府，即此所命安置之地也。
On New Year's Day of the following year, the Khitan emperor made camp north of the Eastern Capital. The officials formed ranks at the temple, bade the emperor farewell from afar, and went to the northern suburbs to welcome the Khitan emperor. The emperor led his whole clan out through Fengqiu Gate and was carried in a litter to the open field, but the Khitan emperor refused to see him and had him lodged at Fengchan Temple. Civil and military officials in plain dress and gauze caps met the Khitan emperor at the suburban station, prostrating themselves to await judgment. He ordered them raised and personally reassured them. The Khitan emperor then entered the inner palace, left at dusk, and that night lodged at Chijin. An edict declared that Jin officials would remain in their posts and that court ritual would follow Han custom. On wuzi, Yang Chengxun, defender of Zhengzhou, was executed for betraying his father; his attendants hacked him to pieces until he died. ( (Liao History: His younger brother Chengxin was appointed military governor of Pinglu and inherited their father's title.)] On jichou, Zhang Yanzhe was beheaded in the market for plundering the capital and slaughtering its people without restraint. ( (Liao History: Zhang Yanzhe was beheaded in the market for unlawfully transferring Chonggui to Kaifeng, killing Sang Weihan, and letting his troops loot the city.)] On gengyin, Jing Yanguang, military governor of Luoyang, took his own life by strangling. On xinmao, the Khitan emperor demoted him to Grand Master for Splendid Happiness and Grand Marshal by Inspection, enfeoffed him as Marquis Who Bears Guilt, and sent him to live at Huanglong. That region lay within what had been Bohai territory. On guisi, the emperor was moved to Fengchan Temple under guard by the Khitan general Cui Tingxun. On guimao, the emperor set out north with Empress Dowager Li, Grand Imperial Consort An, Empress Feng, his brother Chongrui, and Princes Yanshu and Yanbao, attended by fifty palace women, thirty inner eunuchs, fifty court officials, one physician, four Crane-Control officers, seven imperial cooks, three tea and wine servants, three from the Ritual Phoenix Bureau, and twenty guards. Chief Minister Zhao Ying, Commissioner Feng Yu, and Li Yantao, commander of the imperial guard cavalry, accompanied the emperor into Khitan territory while three hundred Khitan horsemen escorted the party north. Wherever they passed, local officials tried to welcome and provision them, but the Khitan emperor blocked every approach, and no supplies could reach the exiled court. ( (Song History, Biography of Li Gu: When the Young Emperor was driven north into exile, none of his former ministers dared pay their respects; Li Gu alone bowed to him on the road, and emperor and subject wept face to face. Gu said, "I have been worthless and have failed Your Majesty. Then he emptied his purse and offered everything he had.)] Once the emperor and the empress dowager could get no food at all and had to slaughter their livestock to eat. Crossing Zhongdu Bridge, the emperor looked over the ruins of Du Wei's camp and cried out in bitter anger to his attendants, "What have we done to deserve ruin at the hands of this traitor? O Heaven! O Heaven! Then he wailed aloud and moved on. At Youzhou the whole city turned out along the road to watch; when they saw the emperor's wretched state, everyone sighed in grief. ( (Miscellaneous Records of Xuanzheng's Reign: When Huizong was marched north, he passed Liangyu Post in Ji County, where there is a Bridge of Returning Home, named by the Later Jin Young Emperor; locals still use that name today.)] He stayed more than ten days while the prefect, on Khitan orders, entertained him at the yamen; Zhao Yanshou's mother brought food as a gift. From Fanyang they marched dozens of stages through Jizhou and Pingzhou to the sandy frontier at Yuguan, with almost nothing to eat. At every halt they camped by the roadside; palace women and attendants gathered wild fruit and vegetables to stave off starvation. Seven or eight days later they reached Jinzhou, where the Khitan forced the emperor and his consorts to bow before the portrait of the late Ambaqian. Overwhelmed with shame, he wept, "Xue Chao was wrong to keep me from dying—I have lived to see this day. After many more stages they crossed the Liao River and reached Huanglong—the very place to which he had been exiled.
9
六月，契丹國母召帝一行往懷密州，州在黃龍府西北千戎王裏。 行至遼陽，皇后馮氏以帝陷蕃，過受艱苦，令內官潛求毒藥，將自飲之，並以進帝，不果而止。 又行二百里，會國母為永康王所執，永康王請帝卻往遼陽城駐泊，帝遣使奉表於永康，且賀克捷，自是帝一行稍得供給。
In the sixth month the Khitan empress dowager summoned the exiled court to Huaimi Prefecture, more than a thousand li northwest of Huanglong. At Liaoyang, Empress Feng, seeing how the emperor had suffered in Khitan captivity, secretly sent a eunuch to obtain poison so that she might kill herself and offer it to him as well; the plan failed and was abandoned. Two hundred li farther on they learned that the Khitan empress dowager had been seized by Prince Yongkang, who invited the emperor to lodge again at Liaoyang. The emperor sent a memorial congratulating him on his victory, and from then on the exiled court began to receive supplies again.
10
漢乾祐二年二月，帝自遼陽城發赴建州。 行至中路，太妃安氏得疾而薨，乃焚之，載其燼骨而行。 帝自遼陽行十數日，過儀州、灞州，遂至建州。 節度使趙延暉盡禮奉迎，館帝於衙署中。 其後割寨地五千餘頃，其地至建州數十里。 帝乃令一行人員於寨地內築室分耕，給食於帝。 是歲，述律王子遣契丹數騎詣帝，取內人趙氏、聶氏疾馳而去。 趙、聶者，帝之寵姬也，及其被奪，不勝悲憤。
In the second month of Han Qianyou year 2, the emperor left Liaoyang for Jianzhou. Midway on the journey Grand Imperial Consort An fell ill and died; they cremated her and carried her ashes with them. From Liaoyang he traveled more than ten days through Yizhou and Bazhou before reaching Jianzhou. Military Governor Zhao Yanhui received him with full ceremony and lodged him in the prefectural yamen. Later they were granted more than five thousand qing of frontier land, reaching to within several tens of li of Jianzhou. The emperor then had his party build houses on the allotted land, divide the fields, and farm them to feed the exiled court. That year a prince of the Shulü clan sent Khitan horsemen who seized the emperor's favorites Lady Zhao and Lady Nie and rode off with them. Zhao and Nie were his favorite consorts; when they were taken from him, his grief and anger were beyond bearing.
11
漢乾祐三年八月，太后薨。 周顯德初，有漢人自塞北而至者，言帝與後及諸子俱無恙，猶在建州，其隨從職官役使人輩，自蕃中亡歸，物故者大半矣。 〈 （《郡齋讀書志》云：《晉朝陷蕃記》，範質撰。 質，石晉末在翰林，為出帝草降表，知其事為詳。 記少帝初遷於黃龍府，後居於建州，凡十八年而卒。 案：契丹丙午歲入汴，順數至甲子歲為十八年，實太祖乾德二年也。 《五代史補》：少主之嗣位也，契丹以不俟命而擅立； 又，景延廣辱其使。 契丹怒，舉國南侵。 以駙馬都尉杜重威等領駕下精兵甲禦之於中流渡橋。 既而契丹之眾已深入，而重威等奏報未到朝廷。 時桑維翰罷相，為開封府尹，謂僚佐曰：「事急矣，非大臣鉗口之時。 」乃叩內閣求見，欲請車駕親征，以固將士之心。 而少主方在後苑調鷹，至暮竟不召。 維翰退而嘆曰：「國家阽危如此，草澤逋客亦宜下問，況大臣求見而不召耶！ 事亦可知矣。 」未幾，杜重威之徒降於契丹，少主遂北遷。）〉
In the eighth month of Han Qianyou year 3, the empress dowager died. Early in Zhou Xiande, a man returned from beyond the frontier reporting that the emperor, empress, and princes were still alive at Jianzhou; of the officials and servants who had accompanied them, most of those who made it back had died in captivity. ( (Junzhai Dushu Zhi: Record of the Jin Dynasty's Fall among the Barbarians, by Fan Zhi. Fan Zhi had served in the Hanlin at the end of Later Jin and drafted the Outgoing Emperor's surrender memorial, so he knew these events in detail. It records that the Young Emperor was first sent to Huanglong, later lived at Jianzhou, and died after eighteen years in all. Note: The Khitan entered Bian in bingwu year; counting forward eighteen years to jiazi brings us to Taizu Qiande year 2. Supplement to the History of the Five Dynasties: When the Young Lord took the throne, the Khitan were angered that he had been enthroned without their leave; and Jing Yanguang had insulted their envoy. Enraged, the Khitan invaded the south with their whole nation. Du Chongwei, Chief Commandant of the Imperial Son-in-Law's Guard, and others led the emperor's elite troops to meet them at Zhongliu Crossing Bridge. The Khitan army had already penetrated deep inland, yet no reports from Chongwei and the others had reached the court. Sang Weihan had been dismissed as chief minister and was serving as prefect of Kaifeng. He told his staff, "The crisis is upon us; this is no time for ministers to hold their tongues. He went to the inner gate and asked for an audience, urging the emperor to take the field in person to steady the troops. But the Young Lord was in the rear park training hawks and never summoned him, even by evening. Weihan withdrew and sighed, "The state stands on the brink of ruin. Even a fugitive in the marshes ought to be consulted—how much more a chief minister who asks for audience and is refused! The outcome is already plain. Before long Du Chongwei and his men surrendered to the Khitan, and the Young Lord was driven north into exile.)]
12
史臣曰：少帝以中人之才，嗣將墜之業，屬上天不祐，仍歲大饑，尚或絕強敵之歡盟，鄙輔臣之謀略。 奢淫自縱，謂有泰山之安； 委托非人，坐受平陽之辱。 族行萬里，身老窮荒。 自古亡國之醜者，無如帝之甚也。 千載之後，其如恥何，傷哉！
The historiographer writes: The Young Emperor had only ordinary ability yet inherited a dynasty on the verge of collapse. Heaven withheld its favor, famine struck year after year, yet he still broke the alliance with a powerful enemy and scorned the counsel of his ministers. He gave himself to luxury and dissipation, believing himself as secure as Mount Tai; he entrusted power to the wrong men and sat idle until he suffered the disgrace of Pingyang. His clan was driven ten thousand li; he grew old in a desolate land. Among the disgraces of fallen rulers since antiquity, none has equaled his. A thousand years from now, what shame can compare? How lamentable!
