1
開運三年冬十月甲子，正衙命使冊皇太妃安氏。 己丑，以樞密直學士、禮部侍郎邊光範為翰林學士，以給事中邊歸讜為左散騎常侍，以翰林學士、祠部員外郎、知制誥張沆為右諫議大夫。 辛未，以鄴都留守杜威為北面行營都招討使，以侍衛親軍都指揮使、鄆州節度使李守貞為兵馬都監，兗州安審琦為左右廂都指揮使，徐州符彥卿為馬軍左廂都指揮使，滑州皇甫遇為馬軍右廂都指揮使，貝州梁漢璋為馬軍都排陣使，前鄧州宋彥筠為步軍左廂都指揮使，奉國左廂都指揮使王饒為步軍右廂都指揮使，洺州團練使薛懷讓為先鋒都指揮使。 〈 （案：《通鑒》載，當時敕榜曰：「先取瀛、鄚，安定關南； 次復幽、燕，蕩平塞北。 」蓋狃於陽城之役而驟驕也。）〉 癸酉，冊吳國夫人馮氏為皇后。 乙亥，以侍衛馬軍都指揮使李彥韜權知侍衛司事。 丙戌，鳳翔節度使秦王李從嚴薨，輟朝，冊贈尚書令。 丁亥，邠州節度使李德充卒，輟朝，贈太尉。
On jiazi in the tenth month of winter in Kaiyun's third year, envoys from the main court were sent to invest Grand Consort Lady An. On jichou, Bian Guangfan—direct academician of the Bureau of Military Affairs and vice minister of rites—was appointed Hanlin academician; supervising secretary Bian Guichan became left regular attendant of the cavalry; and Hanlin academician Zhang Hong, who also served as vice director of sacrificial ceremonies and drafter of edicts, became right remonstrating grand master. On xinwei, Ye's military governor Du Wei was named overall commander of the northern expedition; Li Shouzhen, commander of the imperial guard and governor of Yanzhou, became overseer of troops and horses; An Shenqi of Yanzhou took the left and right wings; Fu Yanqing of Xuzhou led the horse army's left wing; Huangfu Yu of Huazhou the right wing; Liang Hanzhang of Beizhou marshaled the horse array; former Dengzhou prefect Song Yanjun the foot army's left wing; Fengguo left-wing commander Wang Rao the foot right wing; and Mingzhou training commissioner Xue Huairang the vanguard. 〈Editorial note: (The Zizhi Tongjian records that the campaign edict proclaimed: "First seize Ying and Mo and pacify the territory south of the pass; then recover You and Yan and pacify the northern marches." They had grown arrogant overnight on the strength of Yangcheng.)〉 On guiyou, Wu State consort Lady Feng was invested as empress. On yihai, imperial horse-guard commander Li Yantao was placed in acting charge of the imperial guard bureau. On bingxu, Prince of Qin Li Congyan, military governor of Fengxiang, died; the court mourned and posthumously enfeoffed him minister of works. On dinghai, Binzhou governor Li Dechong died; audiences were suspended and he was posthumously made grand preceptor.
2
十一月戊子朔，以給事中盧撰為右散騎常侍，以尚書兵部郎中兼侍御史、知雜事陳觀為左諫議大夫。 觀以祖諱「義」，乞改官，尋授給事中。 庚寅，樞密使、中書侍郎兼戶部尚書、平章事馮玉加尚書右僕射，以皇子鎮寧軍節度使延煦為陜州節度使，以陜州留後焦繼勛為鳳翔留後，以前定州留後安審琦為邠州留後，以右僕射和凝為左僕射。 甲午，兩浙節度使吳越國王錢宏佐起復舊任。 丁酉，詔李守貞知幽州行府事。 戊申，日南至，禦崇元殿受朝賀。 是月，北面行營招討使杜威率諸將領大軍自鄴北征，師次瀛州城下，貝州節度使梁漢璋戰死。 杜威等以漢璋之敗，遂收軍而退。 行次武強，聞契丹入寇，欲取直路，自冀、貝而南。 會張彥澤領騎自鎮定至，且言契丹可破之狀，於是大軍西趨鎮州。
On wuzi, the first of the eleventh month, supervising secretary Lu Zhuan became right regular cavalry attendant, and Chen Guan, director in the war ministry with concurrent censor duties, became left remonstrating grand master. Guan asked for a new post because his grandfather's name was Yi; he was soon made supervising secretary. On gengyin, grand councilor Feng Yu, bureau commissioner and vice minister of the secretariat with concurrent revenue portfolio, was promoted right vice minister; Prince Yanshu, governor of Zhenning, took Shanzhou; Shan acting governor Jiao Jixun became Fengxiang acting governor; former Ding acting governor An Shenqi took Bin; and right vice minister He Ning moved to the left. On jiawu, Wuyue king and Two-Zhe governor Qian Hongzuo returned from mourning to his old post. On dingyou, Li Shouzhen was ordered to administer the Youzhou field headquarters. On wushen, the winter solstice, the emperor received New Year congratulations in the Chongyuan Hall. That month Du Wei led the main northern army out of Ye; below Yingzhou city, Beizhou governor Liang Hanzhang was killed in action. After Hanzhang's defeat, Du Wei pulled the army back. At Wuqiang they learned the Khitan had invaded and meant to strike south through Ji and Bei by the shortest road. Zhang Yanzhe came from Zhending with cavalry, insisting the Khitan could be crushed, and the main force turned west for Zhenzhou.
3
十二月丁巳朔， 〈 （案：以下有闕文。 據《通鑒》云：丁巳朔，李穀自書密奏，且言大軍危急之勢，請車駕幸滑州，遣高行周、符彥卿扈從，及發兵守澶州、河陽，以備敵之奔沖。 遣軍將關勛走馬上之。）〉 己未，杜威奏，駐軍於中渡橋。 庚申，以前司農卿儲延英為太子賓客。 詔徐州符彥卿屯澶州。 辛酉，詔澤潞、鄴都、邢洺、河陽運糧赴中渡，杜威遣人口奏軍前事宜，勢迫故也。 壬戌，又遣高行周屯澶州，景延廣守河陽。 博野縣都監張鵬入奏蕃軍事勢。 丙寅，定州李殷奏，前月二十八日夜，領捉生四百人往曲陽嘉山下，逢敵軍車帳，殺千餘人，獲馬二百匹。 詔宋州高行周充北面行營都部署，符彥卿充副，邢州方太充都虞候，領後軍駐於河上，以備敵騎之奔沖也。 時契丹遊騎涉滹水而南，至欒城縣。 自是中渡寨為蕃軍隔絕，探報不通，朝廷大恐，故委行周等繼領兵師守扼津要，且以張其勢也。 己巳，邢州方太奏，此月六日，契丹與王師戰於中渡，王師不利，奉國都指揮使王清戰死。 庚午，幸沙臺射兔。 壬申，始聞杜威、李守貞等以此月十日率諸軍降於契丹。 是夜，相州節度使張彥澤受契丹命，率先鋒二千人，自封丘門斬關而入。 癸酉旦，張彥澤頓兵於明德門外，京城大擾。 前曹州節度使石赟死，帝之堂叔也。 時自中渡寨隔絕之後，帝與大臣端坐憂危，國之衛兵，悉在北面，計無所出。 十六日聞滹水之降。 是夜，偵知張彥澤已至滑州，召李崧、馮玉、李彥韜入內計事，方議詔河東劉知遠起兵赴難，至五鼓初，張彥澤引蕃騎入京。 宮中相次火起，帝自攜劍驅擁后妃已下十數人，將同赴火，為親校薛超所持。 俄自寬仁門遞入契丹主與皇太后書，帝乃止，旋令撲滅煙火。 大內都點檢康福全在寬仁門宿衛，登樓覘賊，彥澤呼而下之。 癸酉，帝奉表於戎主曰：
On dingsi, the first day of the twelfth month, 〈Editorial note: (The Zizhi Tongjian notes lacunae below. It adds that on that dingsi new moon Li Gu penned a secret memorial describing the army's peril, urging the emperor to move to Huazhou with Gao Xingzhou and Fu Yanqing as escort, and to garrison Cangzhou and Heyang against a Khitan dash south. He sent officer Guan Xun to ride ahead with the dispatch.)〉 On jiwei, Du Wei reported the army encamped at Zhongdu Bridge. On gengshen, former agriculture director Chu Yanying became mentor of the heir apparent. Fu Yanqing of Xuzhou was ordered to hold Cangzhou. On xinyou, Ze-Lu, Ye, Xing-Ming, and Heyang were told to ship grain to Zhongdu; Du Wei sent oral reports from the front under pressure. On renxu, Gao Xingzhou was again posted to Cangzhou and Jing Yanguang to Heyang. Boye county inspector Zhang Peng came in to report Khitan strength. On bingyin, Dingzhou's Li Yin reported that on the previous month's twenty-eighth he led four hundred scouts to Jia Mountain below Quyang, struck enemy camp trains, killed over a thousand, and took two hundred horses. Gao Xingzhou of Songzhou was named northern commander, Fu Yanqing his deputy, and Xingzhou's Fang Tai chief of staff, to hold the rear on the river against Khitan cavalry breaking south. Khitan raiders then forded the Hutuo south to Luancheng. Zhongdu camp was now cut off and no scouts got through; the court in panic sent Xingzhou and others to hold the crossings and show force. On jisi, Xingzhou's Fang Tai reported that on the sixth the Khitan beat the imperial force at Zhongdu; Fengguo commander Wang Qing fell. On gengwu the emperor hunted hares at Shatai. On renshen came first word that on the tenth Du Wei and Li Shouzhen had surrendered the army to the Khitan. That night Xiangzhou governor Zhang Yanzhe, under Khitan orders, led two thousand vanguard troops through Fengqiu Gate. At dawn on guiyou, Zhang Yanzhe halted outside Virtue Gate and the capital erupted. Former Caozhou governor Shi Yun died—the emperor's uncle. Since Zhongdu was isolated the emperor and ministers sat trapped while every guardsman was north and no plan availed. On the sixteenth came news of surrender at the Hutuo. That night, told Zhang Yanzhe had reached Huazhou, Li Song, Feng Yu, and Li Yantao were called in; as they drafted orders for Hedong's Liu Zhiyuan to march, Zhang Yanzhe entered the capital with Khitan horse at fifth watch. Palace fires broke out in succession; the emperor drew his sword to drive the empress and a dozen kin toward the flames, but intimate guard Xue Chao seized him. Soon a letter from the Khitan ruler and empress dowager came through Broad Benevolence Gate; the emperor halted and ordered the fires put out. Inner-palace inspector Kang Quanquan guarded Broad Benevolence Gate, climbed the tower to watch the raiders, and Yanzhe summoned him down. On guiyou the emperor submitted this memorial to the Khitan ruler:
4
孫臣某言：今月十七日寅時，相州節度使張彥澤、都監傅住兒部領大軍入京，賫到翁皇帝賜太后書示，於滹沱河降下杜重威一行馬步兵士，見領蕃漢步騎來幸汴州者。
Your grandson and subject writes: at yin on the seventeenth, Xiangzhou governor Zhang Yanzhe and overseer Fu Zhu'er entered the capital with the main force, bearing the Khitan emperor's letter to the empress dowager, announcing Du Chongwei's surrender at the Hutuo and that the mixed Khitan-Han host was marching on Bianzhou.
5
往者，唐運告終，中原失馭，數窮否極，天缺地傾。 先人有田一成，有眾一旅，兵連禍結，力屈勢孤。 翁皇帝救患摧鋒，興利除害，躬擐甲胄，深入寇場。 犯露蒙霜，度雁門之險； 馳風掣電，行中冀之誅。 黃鉞一麾，天下大定。 勢淩宇宙，義感神明，功成不居，遂興晉祚，則翁皇帝有大造於石氏也。
When Tang's mandate ended the heartland was left masterless—fortune spent, heaven cracked, earth askew. My forebear held a single field and a single company; war chained to disaster until strength failed and he stood alone. The Khitan emperor relieved the suffering, crushed our foes, bore armor himself, and drove deep into enemy country. Through dew and frost he crossed Yanmen's passes; With wind and lightning at his heels, he punished central Ji. One wave of the battle-axe and the realm was settled. His might overawed heaven; his righteousness moved the gods; refusing merit he enthroned Jin—so the Khitan emperor was founder to our house.
6
旋屬天降鞠兇，先君即世，臣遵承遺旨，纘紹前基。 諒暗之初，荒迷失次，凡有軍國重事，皆委將相大臣。 至於擅繼宗祧，既非稟命； 輕發文字，輒敢抗尊。 自啟釁端，果貽赫怒，禍至神惑，運盡天亡。 十萬師徒，皆望風而束手； 億兆黎庶，悉延頸以歸心。 臣負義包羞，貪生忍恥，自貽顛覆，上累祖宗，偷度晨昏，茍存食息。 翁皇帝若惠顧疇昔，稍霽雷霆，未賜靈誅，不絕先祀，則百口荷更生之德，一門銜無報之恩，雖所願焉，非敢望也。 臣與太后並妻馮氏及舉家戚屬，見於郊野面縛俟罪次。 所有國寶一面、金印三面，今遣長子陜府節度使延煦、次子曹州節度使延寶管押進納，並奉表請罪，陳謝以聞。
Soon heaven sent calamity; my father died; I followed his will and kept the throne. In first mourning I was lost; every weight of army and state went to generals and ministers. Seizing the temple succession was never commanded; I lightly issued edicts and dared defy your majesty. I opened the quarrel myself; wrath followed—spirits confused, fortune spent, heaven's favor gone. A hundred thousand soldiers waited for the wind and bound their hands; millions of people craned their necks in submission. I bore shame, clung to life, ruined myself and shamed my ancestors, stealing breath from dawn to dusk. If you would recall old days, stay your thunder, spare my life and my ancestors' rites, a hundred mouths would owe rebirth and one house a debt beyond payment—this is my wish, though I dare not expect it. I, the empress dowager, consort Feng, and all our kin wait bound in the suburbs for judgment. One imperial treasure set and three gold seals go forward under my eldest son Yanshu, Shanzhou governor, and second son Yanbao, Caozhou governor, with this plea for mercy.
7
甲戌，張彥澤遷帝與太后及諸宮屬於開封府，遣控鶴指揮使李榮將兵監守。 是夜，開封尹桑維翰、宣徽使孟承誨皆遇害。 帝以契丹主將至，欲與太后出迎，彥澤先表之，稟契丹主之旨報云：「比欲許爾朝覲上國，臣僚奏言，豈有兩個天子道路相見！ 今賜所佩刀子，以慰爾心。 」己卯，皇子延煦、延寶自帳中回，得敵詔慰撫，帝表謝之。 時契丹主以所送傳國寶制造非工，與載籍所述者異，使人來問。 帝進狀曰：「頃以偽主王從珂於洛京大內自焚之後，其真傳國寶不知所在，必是當時焚之。 先帝受命，旋制此寶，在位臣僚，備知其事。 臣至今日，敢有隱藏」云。 時移內庫至府，帝使人取帛數段，主者不與，謂使者曰：「此非我所有也。 」又使人詣李崧求酒，崧曰：「臣有酒非敢愛惜，慮陛下杯酌之後憂躁，所作別有不測之事，臣以此不敢奉進。 」丙戌晦，百官宿封禪寺。
On jiaxu, Zhang Yanzhe moved the emperor, empress dowager, and palace kin to Kaifeng prefecture under Control-Crane commander Li Rong's guard. That night prefect Sang Weihan and palace commissioner Meng Chenghui were killed. As the Khitan ruler approached, the emperor wished to go out with the empress dowager to welcome him; Yanzhe reported the Khitan reply: "We meant to let you attend court in our land, but ministers said two sons of heaven cannot meet on the road! We now give the knife at your belt to comfort you." On jimao, princes Yanshu and Yanbao returned from camp with the enemy's comforting edict; the emperor thanked them in memorial. The Khitan ruler, finding the jade seal sent him poorly made and unlike the records, sent to inquire. The emperor answered: "After the false ruler Wang Congke burned himself in Luoyang's inner palace, the true seal vanished—surely it perished in that fire. My father, taking the mandate, had this seal made; every minister in office knows it. To this day I dare not hide it," he wrote. When the inner treasury moved to the prefecture, the emperor sent for silk; the keeper refused, saying, "These are not mine." He also asked Li Song for wine; Song replied, "I have wine and do not begrudge it, but fear that after you drink, agitation may drive you to the unforeseen—I dare not offer it." On the last day of bingxu the officials lodged at Fengchan Temple.
8
明年正月朔，契丹主次東京城北。 百官列班，遙辭帝於寺，詣北郊以迎契丹主。 帝舉族出封丘門，肩轝至野，契丹主不與之見，遣泊封禪寺。 文武百官素服紗帽，迎謁契丹主於郊次，俯伏俟罪，契丹主命起之，親自慰撫。 契丹主遂入大內，至昏出宮，是夜宿於赤堈。 偽詔應晉朝臣僚一切仍舊，朝廷儀制並用漢禮。 戊子，殺鄭州防禦使楊承勛，責以背父之罪，令左右臠割而死。 〈 （《遼史》：以其弟承信為平盧軍節度使，襲父爵。）〉 己丑，斬張彥澤於市，以其剽劫京城，恣行屠害也。 〈 （《遼史》云：以張彥澤擅徙重貴開封，殺桑維翰，縱兵大掠，不道，斬於市。）〉 庚寅，洛京留守景延廣自扼吭而死。 辛卯，契丹制，降帝為光祿大夫、檢校太尉，封負義侯，黃龍府安置。 其地在渤海國界。 癸巳，遷帝於封禪寺，遣蕃大將崔廷勛將兵守之。 癸卯，帝與皇太后李氏、皇太妃安氏、皇后馮氏、皇弟重睿、皇子延煦延寶俱北行，以宮嬪五十人、內官三十人、東西班五十人、醫官一人、控鶴官四人、禦廚七人、茶酒三人、儀鸞司三人、軍健二十人從行。 宰臣趙瑩、樞密使馮玉、侍衛馬軍都指揮使李彥韜隨帝入蕃，契丹主遣三百騎援送而去。 所經州郡，長吏迎奉，皆為契丹主阻絕，有所供饋亦不通。 〈 （《宋史·李穀傳》：少帝蒙塵而北，舊臣無敢候謁者，穀獨拜迎於路，君臣相對泣下。 穀曰：「臣無狀，負陛下。 」因傾囊以獻。）〉 嘗一日，帝與太后不能得食，乃殺畜而啖之。 帝過中渡橋，閱前杜威營寨之跡，慨然憤嘆，謂左右曰：「我家何負，為此賊所破，天乎！ 天乎！ 」於是號慟而去。 至幽州，傾城士庶迎看於路，見帝慘沮，無不嗟嘆。 〈 （《宣政雜錄》：徽宗北狩，經薊縣梁魚務，有還鄉橋，石少帝所命名也，里人至今呼之。）〉 駐留旬餘，州將承契丹命，犒帝於府署，趙延壽母以食饌來獻。 自范陽行數十程，過薊州、平州，至榆關沙塞之地，略無供給，每至宿頓，無非路次，一行乏食，宮女、從官但采木實野蔬，以救饑弊。 又行七八日至錦州，契丹迫帝與妃後往拜安巴堅遺像，帝不勝屈辱，泣曰：「薛超誤我，不令我死，以至今日也。 」又行數十程，渡遼水，至黃龍府，即此所命安置之地也。
On the new year's first day the Khitan ruler halted north of the Eastern Capital. Officials lined up, bade the emperor farewell at the temple from afar, and went to the northern suburbs to welcome the Khitan ruler. The emperor's clan went out Fengqiu Gate on litters to the open country; the Khitan ruler would not see them and sent them back to Fengchan Temple. Civil and military officials in white gauze caps met the Khitan ruler at the suburban station, prostrated themselves, and were raised and comforted by him. The Khitan ruler entered the inner palace and at dusk departed, lodging that night at Chijin. A puppet edict kept all Jin officials in post and retained Han court ritual. On wuzi, Zhengzhou defender Yang Chengxun was executed for betraying his father, hacked apart on the spot. 〈Editorial note: (History of Liao: his brother Chenxin became Pinglu governor and inherited the father's rank.)〉 On jichou, Zhang Yanzhe was beheaded in the market for plundering the capital and massacring at will. 〈Editorial note: (History of Liao says Yanzhe was executed for moving Chonggui to Kaifeng, killing Sang Weihan, and letting troops loot the city.)〉 On gengyin, Luoyang acting governor Jing Yanguang strangled himself. On xinmao the Khitan demoted the emperor to grand master of splendid happiness and inspecting grand preceptor, enfeoffed him marquis who bears injustice, and sent him to Huanglong prefecture. The place lay on Bohai's frontier. On guisi the emperor was moved to Fengchan Temple under Khitan general Cui Tingxun's guard. On guimao the emperor, Empress Dowager Li, Grand Consort An, Empress Feng, Prince Chongrui, and Yanshu and Yanbao marched north with fifty palace women, thirty eunuchs, fifty eastern and western attendants, and the usual train of doctors, cooks, and guards. Chief minister Zhao Ying, commissioner Feng Yu, and horse-guard commander Li Yantao went into captivity with him; three hundred Khitan horsemen escorted them off. At each prefecture local officials tried to receive them, but the Khitan ruler blocked every approach and even gifts could not pass. 〈Editorial note: (History of Song, Biography of Li Gu: As the deposed emperor went north in disgrace, no former minister dared meet him—only Li Gu bowed to him on the road, and the two wept face to face.) Gu said, "I am unworthy and have failed you." Then he emptied his purse and offered what he had.)〉 On one occasion the emperor and empress dowager could get no food and slaughtered livestock to eat. Crossing Zhongdu Bridge and seeing where Du Wei had camped, the emperor sighed in rage and told his attendants, "What did our house do to deserve ruin by this traitor? Heaven! O Heaven! He broke into loud wailing and went on. At Youzhou the whole city turned out along the road; seeing the emperor's misery, everyone sighed. 〈Editorial note: (Miscellaneous Records of Xuanzheng: When Huizong was taken north, he passed Liangyu Post in Jixian, where the Later Jin minor emperor had named the Bridge of Return to the Homeland, which locals still use.)〉 After a stay of ten-odd days, the prefect, on Khitan orders, feasted the emperor in his yamen, and Zhao Yanshou's mother brought food and drink. From Fanyang they marched dozens of stages through Jizhou and Pingzhou to Yuguan's desert barrier, with almost no supplies; at each halt they camped by the road, and the party starved while palace women and attendants gathered wild fruit and greens to survive. Seven or eight days later at Jinzhou the Khitan forced the emperor and his consorts to bow before Anbajian's portrait; unable to bear the humiliation, he wept, "Xue Chao wronged me by keeping me alive until today." After many more stages they crossed the Liao River to Huanglong prefecture, the place of their exile.
9
六月，契丹國母召帝一行往懷密州，州在黃龍府西北千戎王裏。 行至遼陽，皇后馮氏以帝陷蕃，過受艱苦，令內官潛求毒藥，將自飲之，並以進帝，不果而止。 又行二百里，會國母為永康王所執，永康王請帝卻往遼陽城駐泊，帝遣使奉表於永康，且賀克捷，自是帝一行稍得供給。
In the sixth month the Khitan empress dowager summoned the party to Huaimi prefecture, a thousand li northwest of Huanglong in the Thousand Rong Kings' lands. At Liaoyang, seeing the emperor's suffering in captivity, Empress Feng had a eunuch secretly seek poison to drink herself and offer the emperor; the attempt failed. Two hundred li farther on they found the Khitan empress dowager held by Prince Yongkang, who asked the emperor to stay at Liaoyang; the emperor sent memorials congratulating his victory, and thereafter the party received some supplies.
10
漢乾祐二年二月，帝自遼陽城發赴建州。 行至中路，太妃安氏得疾而薨，乃焚之，載其燼骨而行。 帝自遼陽行十數日，過儀州、灞州，遂至建州。 節度使趙延暉盡禮奉迎，館帝於衙署中。 其後割寨地五千餘頃，其地至建州數十里。 帝乃令一行人員於寨地內築室分耕，給食於帝。 是歲，述律王子遣契丹數騎詣帝，取內人趙氏、聶氏疾馳而去。 趙、聶者，帝之寵姬也，及其被奪，不勝悲憤。
In the second month of Han Qianyou's second year the emperor left Liaoyang for Jianzhou. En route Grand Consort An fell ill and died; they cremated her and carried her ashes onward. Ten-odd days out of Liaoyang, passing Yizhou and Bazhou, he reached Jianzhou. Governor Zhao Yanhui received him with full ceremony and lodged him in the prefectural yamen. They then allotted more than five thousand qing of frontier land a few tens of li from Jianzhou. The emperor had his followers build houses on the allotment, farm in common, and supply his food. That year a son of Prince Shulü sent Khitan riders to seize the inner women Zhao and Nie, the emperor's favorites, and ride off with them. Zhao and Nie were his beloved consorts; their seizure overwhelmed him with grief and rage.
11
漢乾祐三年八月，太后薨。 周顯德初，有漢人自塞北而至者，言帝與後及諸子俱無恙，猶在建州，其隨從職官役使人輩，自蕃中亡歸，物故者大半矣。 〈 （《郡齋讀書志》云：《晉朝陷蕃記》，範質撰。 質，石晉末在翰林，為出帝草降表，知其事為詳。 記少帝初遷於黃龍府，後居於建州，凡十八年而卒。 案：契丹丙午歲入汴，順數至甲子歲為十八年，實太祖乾德二年也。 《五代史補》：少主之嗣位也，契丹以不俟命而擅立； 又，景延廣辱其使。 契丹怒，舉國南侵。 以駙馬都尉杜重威等領駕下精兵甲禦之於中流渡橋。 既而契丹之眾已深入，而重威等奏報未到朝廷。 時桑維翰罷相，為開封府尹，謂僚佐曰：「事急矣，非大臣鉗口之時。 」乃叩內閣求見，欲請車駕親征，以固將士之心。 而少主方在後苑調鷹，至暮竟不召。 維翰退而嘆曰：「國家阽危如此，草澤逋客亦宜下問，況大臣求見而不召耶！ 事亦可知矣。 」未幾，杜重威之徒降於契丹，少主遂北遷。）〉
In the eighth month of Han Qianyou's third year the empress dowager died. Early in Zhou Xiande a man from beyond the frontier reported that the emperor, empress, and sons were still alive at Jianzhou; of the officials and servants who had escaped the Khitan, more than half were dead. 〈Editorial note: (Notes on Books from the Prefect's Studio: Record of the Jin Dynasty's Fall into the Khitan Lands, by Fan Zhi. Zhi served in the Hanlin at the end of Jin and drafted Emperor Chu's surrender; he knew the events in detail. He records that the minor emperor was first sent to Huanglong, later to Jianzhou, and died after eighteen years. The Khitan entered Bian in bingwu; counting forward to jiazi is eighteen years—the second year of Taizu's Qiande. Supplement to the History of the Five Dynasties: When the minor emperor succeeded, the Khitan resented his taking the throne without leave; and Jing Yanguang had insulted their envoy. Enraged, they invaded south with the whole nation. Du Chongwei the imperial son-in-law and others led the palace guard to hold them at Zhongliu Crossing. The Khitan were already deep in the country while Chongwei's reports had not reached court. Sang Weihan, now out of office and prefect of Kaifeng, told his staff, "This is urgent—not a time for ministers to hold their tongues." He knocked at the inner gates, asking the emperor to take the field in person to steady the troops. But the minor emperor was in the rear park flying hawks and never summoned him before nightfall. Weihan withdrew sighing, "The state totters on the brink—even a fugitive in the wilds should be consulted, let alone a minister who is refused audience! The outcome was already plain." Soon Du Chongwei surrendered and the minor emperor went north.)〉
12
史臣曰：少帝以中人之才，嗣將墜之業，屬上天不祐，仍歲大饑，尚或絕強敵之歡盟，鄙輔臣之謀略。 奢淫自縱，謂有泰山之安； 委托非人，坐受平陽之辱。 族行萬里，身老窮荒。 自古亡國之醜者，無如帝之甚也。 千載之後，其如恥何，傷哉！
The historian writes: The minor emperor, a man of middling talent, inherited a failing house; heaven withheld favor and famine ran year on year; he still spurned alliance with a strong foe and scorned his ministers' counsel. He gave himself to luxury, believing he had Mount Tai's security; he entrusted power to the wrong men and sat to suffer Pingyang's shame. His clan marched ten thousand li; he grew old in the wilderness. Of ruined rulers since antiquity, none was shamed as deeply. A thousand years on, what shame can compare—how grievous!
