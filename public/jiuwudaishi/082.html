1
天福八年秋七月丁丑朔，京師雨水深三尺。 辛巳，許州節度使李從溫來朝，進封楚國公。 壬午，以前河陽節度使皇甫遇為右龍武統軍。 丁亥，以宣徽南院使李承福為同州節度使。 癸巳，改陜州甘棠驛為通津驛，避廟諱也。 甲午，正衙命冊皇太后，以宰臣李崧充使，右散騎常侍李慎儀為副。 丁酉，幸南莊，召從駕臣僚習射，路左農人各賜布衫麻屨。
On the dingchou new moon in the seventh month of autumn, Tianfu 8, floodwaters in the capital stood three chi deep. On xinsi Li Congwen, military governor of Xuzhou, presented himself at court and was promoted to Duke of Chu. On renwu former Heyang governor Huangfu Yu was appointed Right Dragon Martial commander-in-chief. On dinghai Li Chengfu of the Southern Bureau of the Palace Attendants was made military governor of Tongzhou. On guisi Gantang Post in Shaanzhou was renamed Tongjin Post to avoid a title taboo. On jiawu the main court ordered the enfeoffment rite for the empress dowager, with chief minister Li Song as envoy and Right Regular Cavalry Attendant Li Shenyi as deputy. On dingyou he visited South Manor and had his escort officials practice archery; farmers along the route each received a cloth shirt and hemp sandals.
2
八月戊申，右衛上將軍楊思權卒，贈太傅。 辛亥，分命朝臣一十三人分檢諸州旱苗。 涇、青、磁、鄴都共奏逃戶凡五千八百九十。 諸縣令佐以天災民餓，攜牌印納者五。 癸酉，以前昭義節度使李從敏為左龍武統軍。
In the eighth month on wushen Right Guard senior general Yang Siquan died and was posthumously honored as Grand Mentor. On xinhai thirteen officials were dispatched to inspect drought damage in the prefectures. Jing, Qing, Ci, and Yedu jointly reported 5,890 absconding households. Five county magistrates or assistants surrendered their seals, citing famine and disaster. On guiyou former Zhaoyi governor Li Congmin was appointed Left Dragon Martial commander-in-chief.
3
九月戊寅，尊秦國夫人安氏為皇太妃，帝所生母也。 丁亥，追冊故魏國夫人張氏為皇后，帝之元妃也。 丙子，以金部郎中、知制誥馮玉為檢校尚書右僕射，充潁州團練使。 戊子，前潁州團練使田令方追奪在身官爵，勒歸私第，坐前任耀州日，額外配民曲錢納歸私室故也。 延州奏，綏州刺史李彜敏拋棄城郡，與弟彜俊等五人將骨肉二百七十口來投，當州押送赴闕，稱與兄夏州節度使彜殷偶起猜嫌，互相攻伐故也。 辛卯，夏州奏，差宥州刺史李仁立權知綏州。 癸巳，故絳州刺史張從訓贈太尉，追冊皇后之父也。 甲午，夏州李彜殷奏：「衙內都指揮使拓拔崇斌等五人作亂，當時收擒處斬訖。 相次綏州刺史李彜敏擅將兵士，直抵城門，尋差人掩殺，彜敏知事不濟，與弟五人將家南走。 」詔：「李彜敏潛結兇黨，顯恣逆謀。 骨肉之間，尚興屠害； 照臨之內，難以含容。 送夏州處斬。 」丙申，幸大年莊，遂幸侍衛使景延廣第。 延廣進金玉器玩，賜延廣玉帶名馬，母妻、賓佐、部曲、僮仆錫賫鹹及之。 庚子，以右諫議大夫邊光範為給事中，以吏部郎中劉知新為右諫議大夫。 是月，諸州郡括借到軍食，以籍來上，吏民有隱落者，並處極法。 州郡二十七蝗，餓死者數十萬。
In the ninth month on wuyin Lady An of Qin State was elevated to imperial grand consort, the emperor's biological mother. On dinghai the late Lady Zhang of Wei State was posthumously enfeoffed as empress, his first wife. On bingzi Feng Yu of the Gold Department, drafter of edicts, was made acting vice director of the Department of State Affairs and training commissioner of Yingzhou. On wuzi former Yingzhou training commissioner Tian Lingfang was stripped of rank and ordered home, having extorted extra levies from the populace during his Yaozhou tenure for private gain. Yanzhou reported that Suizhou prefect Li Yimin had abandoned his post and fled with his brother Yijun and five others, bringing 270 family members to defect; they were escorted to court, having fallen out with his brother Yiyin, military governor of Xiazhou, in mutual attacks. On xinmao Xiazhou reported Li Renli of Youzhou provisionally administering Suizhou. On guisi late Jiangzhou prefect Zhang Congxun was posthumously made Grand Commandant as father of the posthumously enfeoffed empress. On jiawu Li Yiyin of Xiazhou reported: "Yamen inner director Tuoba Chongbin and four others had rebelled and were captured and executed. Then Suizhou prefect Li Yimin led troops unauthorized to the city gate; an ambush was sent, and seeing failure, Yimin fled south with five brothers and their families. " An edict declared: "Li Yimin had secretly allied with traitors and openly pursued rebellion. Even among his own kin he brought slaughter; within the emperor's grace he could not be spared. He was to be sent to Xiazhou for execution." " On bingshen he visited Great Year Manor, then the home of palace guard commissioner Jing Yanguang. Yanguang presented gold, jade, and curios; the emperor gave him a jade belt and fine horses, with rewards extending to his mother, wife, staff, retainers, and servants. On gengzi Bian Guangfan was promoted to Attendant within the Yellow Gate and Liu Zhixin of the Ministry of Personnel to Right Remonstrance Grandee. That month prefectures requisitioned army grain and reported the rolls; any official or subject who concealed shortfalls faced capital punishment. Locusts struck twenty-seven prefectures; starvation deaths ran into the hundreds of thousands.
4
冬十月戊申，制以吳國夫人馮氏為皇后，仍令所司擇日備禮冊命。 庚戌，封皇第十一妹為嘉興長公主，第十二妹為永泰長公主。 是夕五更，有彗見於東方，在角，旬日而滅。 壬子，以權知延州軍州事、前鳳州防禦使杜威為延州留後。 甲寅，以國子祭酒兼戶部侍郎田敏充宏文館學士，判館事； 以吏部侍郎張昭遠充史館修撰，判館事； 以給事中司徒詡充集賢殿學士，判院事。 西京奏，百姓馬知饒殺男吳九不死，以其侵母食也。 詔赦之。 甲子，以前延州節度使何健為涇州節度使。 丙寅，以涇州節度使王周為陜府節度使。 己巳，以左散騎常侍、權知開封府事邊蔚為工部侍郎，依前知府事。 壬申，以前兵部侍郎李玘為吏部侍郎。 癸酉，命使攝太尉、右僕射平章事和凝，使副攝司徒、給事中邊光範追冊故魏國夫人張氏為皇后，奉寶冊至西莊影殿行禮，鹵簿儀仗如式。
In the tenth month on wushen Lady Feng of Wu State was made empress, with orders to schedule the full enfeoffment ceremony. On gengxu his eleventh sister was made Princess Chang of Jiaxing and his twelfth Princess Chang of Yongtai. That night at the fifth watch a comet appeared in the east at the Horn mansion and faded within ten days. On renzi Du Wei, acting Yanzhou administrator and former Fengzhou defense commissioner, was made Yanzhou surveillance commissioner. On jiayin Tian Min, grand master of the National University and vice minister of revenue, was made academician of the Hall of Illustrious Culture and put in charge of it; Zhang Zhaoyuan of the Ministry of Personnel was made history institute compiler and put in charge; Situ Xu, Attendant within the Yellow Gate, was made academician of the Hall for Gathering Worthies and put in charge of it. Western Capital reported that Ma Zhirao struck his son Wu Jiu without killing him, the son having taken his mother's food. He was pardoned by edict. On jiazi former Yanzhou governor He Jian was made military governor of Jingzhou. On bingyin Jingzhou governor Wang Zhou was made military governor of Shaanfu. On jisi Bian Wei, Left Regular Cavalry Attendant and acting Kaifeng prefect, was made vice minister of works while retaining the prefecture. On renshen former vice minister of war Li Qi was made vice minister of personnel. On guiyou He Ning as acting Grand Commandant and Bian Guangfan as acting Minister of Education were sent to posthumously enfeoff the late Lady Zhang of Wei State as empress, bearing the registers to the Shadow Hall at West Manor with full ceremonial escort.
5
十一月丁丑，以鄧州節度使宋彥筠為晉州節度使，以涇州節度使何建為鄧州節度使。 己卯，以前鄴都留守、廣晉尹李德充為涇州節度使。 丙申，所司奏議，故天下兵馬都元帥、吳越國王錢元瓘謚曰莊穆，詔改為文穆。 戊戌，遣前復州防禦使吳巒權知貝州軍事，詔節度使王令溫赴闕。 庚戌，單州軍事判官趙嶽奏，刺史楊承祚初夜開門出城，稱為母病，往青州寧親，於孔目官齊琪處留下牌印，臣已行用權知州事。 辛丑，高麗遣使朝貢。 昭化軍節度使、瑞慎等州觀察等使杜建徽進封鄖國公。 遣侍衛步軍都指揮使郭謹領兵赴鄆州。
In the eleventh month on dingchou Song Yanyun of Dengzhou was transferred to Jinzhou and He Jian of Jingzhou to Dengzhou. On jimao former Yedu garrison commander and Guangjin prefect Li Dechong was made military governor of Jingzhou. On bingshen the offices proposed the posthumous title Solemn and Reverent for late commander-in-chief and King of Wuyue Qian Yuanguan; the emperor changed it to Cultured and Reverent. On wuxu former Fuzhou defense commissioner Wu Luan was assigned to administer Beizhou, and military governor Wang Lingwen was summoned to court. On gengxu Shanzhou judge Zhao Yue reported that prefect Yang Chengzuo left the city at night, citing his mother's illness and a visit to kin in Qingzhou, leaving his seal with clerk Qi Qi; Zhao was provisionally administering the prefecture. On xinchou Goryeo sent tribute envoys to court. Du Jianhui, military governor of Zhaohua Army and surveillance commissioner of Ruizhou and Shenzhou, was promoted to Duke of Yun State. Palace guard infantry commander Guo Jin was sent with troops to Yunzhou.
6
十二月乙巳朔，遣左領軍衛將軍蔡行遇押兵士屯於鄆州，仍遣供奉官殿直二十六人，自河陰至海口，分擘地分巡檢，以青州節度使楊光遠謀叛故也。 庚戌，前左禦正齊國夫人吳氏巳降二十一人，並封郡國夫人，太后宮、皇后宮知客夫人等亦如之。 太子太保致仕梁文矩卒，贈太子太傅。 癸丑，詔河陽節度使符彥卿、宋州節度使高行周、貝州節度使王令溫、同州節度使李承福、陳州梁漢璋、亳州李萼、懷州薛懷讓並赴闕，分命使臣諸州郡巡檢，以契丹入寇故也。 遣給事中邊光範、前登州刺史郭彥威使於契丹，行至恒州，敵已犯境，不能進，留於公館數月，不達其命而回。 〈 （《遼史》：天福八年二月乙卯，晉遣使進先帝遺物。 辛酉，晉遣使請居汴，從之。 三月丁未，晉主至汴，遣使來謝。 五月己亥，遣使如晉，致生辰禮。 六月辛酉，晉遣使貢金。 秋八月丁未朔，晉復貢金。 己未，如奉聖州，晉遣其子延煦來朝。）〉 甲寅，以單州刺史楊承祚為登州刺史，從其便也。 華州陜府奏，逃戶凡一萬二千三百。 乙丑，臘，車駕不出。 詔前陜州節度使石赟率諸節度使畋於郡郊。 太子賓客聶延祚卒。 丁卯，詔宣徽使劉繼勛就杜威園亭會節度使統軍等習射。 淄州奏，青州節度使楊光遠反，遣兵士取淄州，劫刺史翟進宗入青州。 是冬大饑，河南諸州餓死者二萬六千餘口。
On the yisi new moon in the twelfth month Cai Xingyu was sent to garrison Yunzhou with troops, and twenty-six palace officers were posted from Heyin to Haikou to patrol districts, as Qingzhou governor Yang Guangyuan was plotting rebellion. On gengxu former Left Imperial Rectifier Lady Wu of Qi State and twenty-one others who had submitted were all made ladies of commandery or state, as were guest-receiving ladies of the empress dowager's and empress's palaces. Retired crown prince guardian Liang Wenju died and was posthumously honored as crown prince grand tutor. On guichou Fu Yanqing of Heyang, Gao Xingzhou of Songzhou, Wang Lingwen of Beizhou, Li Chengfu of Tongzhou, Liang Hanzhang of Chenzhou, Li E of Bozhou, and Xue Huairang of Huaizhou were all summoned to court while envoys were dispatched to inspect the prefectures, as the Khitan had invaded. Bian Guangfan and former Dengzhou prefect Guo Yanwei were sent to the Khitan but halted at Hengzhou when the enemy had crossed the border; they lingered at the guesthouse for months and returned without completing their mission. 〈Note (History of Liao: In the second month, Tianfu 8, on yimao Jin sent envoys with the late emperor's relics. On xinyou Jin requested residence at Bian and was granted it. In the third month on dingwei the Jin ruler reached Bian and sent envoys in thanks. In the fifth month on jihai envoys were sent to Jin with birthday gifts. In the sixth month on xinyou Jin sent envoys with gold tribute. On the dingwei new moon in the eighth month of autumn Jin again sent gold tribute. On jiwei he went to Fengsheng Prefecture; Jin sent his son Yanshu to court.)〉 On jiayin Shanzhou prefect Yang Chengzuo was transferred to Dengzhou at his request. Huazhou and Shaanfu reported 12,300 absconding households. On yichou, at the La festival, the emperor did not go abroad. Former Shaanzhou governor Shi Yun was ordered to lead the military governors in a hunt outside the capital. Crown prince guest Nie Yanzuo died. On dingmao palace attendant Liu Jixun was ordered to gather military governors and commanders at Du Wei's garden for archery practice. Zizhou reported that Qingzhou governor Yang Guangyuan had rebelled, seized Zizhou by force, and carried off prefect Zhai Jinzong to Qingzhou. That winter famine struck; south of the Yellow River more than 26,000 people starved to death.
7
開運元年春正月甲戌朔，是夕陣雲掩北斗之魁星。 乙亥，滄、恒、貝、鄴馳告，契丹前鋒趙延壽、趙延昭引五萬騎入寇，將及甘陵，青州楊光遠召之也。 己卯，契丹陷貝州，知州吳巒死之。 庚辰，以宋州節度使高行周為北面行營都部署，以河陽節度使符彥卿為馬軍左廂排陣使，以右神武統軍皇甫遇為馬軍右廂排陣使，以陜州節度使王周為步軍左廂排陣使，以左羽林統軍潘環為步軍右廂排陣使。 太原奏，契丹入雁門，圍忻、代二州。 恒、滄、邢三州上言，契丹大至。 是歲，天下餓死者數十萬人，詔逐處長吏瘞之。 壬午，詔取此月十三日車駕北征，以前邠州節度使李周為權東京留守。 乙酉，車駕發東京。 丁亥，敵騎至黎陽。 以侍衛馬軍都指揮使李守貞為前軍都虞候。 河北危蹙，諸州求救者人使相望。 戊子，車駕至澶州。 以貝州節度使王令溫為鄧州節度使，時令溫弟令崇自契丹至，訴以舉族陷於甘陵，故有是命。 辛卯，鄴都留守張從恩遣人夜縋城間行，奏契丹主以鐵騎三四萬建牙帳於元城，以趙延壽為魏博節度使，改封魏王，延壽日率騎軍摩壘而退。 甲午，以北京留守劉知遠為幽州道行營招討使，以恒州節度使杜威副之，定州節度使馬全節為都虞候，其職員將校委招討使便宜署置。 乙未，大霧中有白虹相偶，占者曰：「斯為海淫，其下必將有戰。 」詔率天下公私之馬以資騎軍。 丙申，契丹攻黎陽。 遣右武衛上將軍張彥澤等率勁騎三千以禦之。 己亥，遣譯詔官孟守忠致書於契丹主，求修舊好。 守忠自敵帳回，契丹主復書曰：「已成之勢，不可改也。 」辛丑，太原奏，與契丹戰於秀谷，斬首三千級，生擒五百人，獲敵將一十七人，賊軍散入鴉鳴谷，已進軍追襲。
On the jiaxu new moon in spring of Kaiyun 1, battle clouds that night veiled the Chief Star of the Northern Dipper. On yihai Cang, Heng, Bei, and Ye urgently reported that Khitan vanguards Zhao Yanshou and Zhao Yanzhao were leading 50,000 horsemen toward Ganling, summoned by Qingzhou's Yang Guangyuan. On jimao the Khitan took Beizhou; prefect Wu Luan was killed. On gengchen Gao Xingzhou of Songzhou was made overall commander of the northern campaign; Fu Yanqing of Heyang, Huangfu Yu of the Right Divine Martial Army, Wang Zhou of Shaanzhou, and Pan Huan of the Left Forest Army were made array marshals of the horse and foot wings. Taiyuan reported the Khitan had entered Yanmen Pass and were besieging Xin and Dai. Heng, Cang, and Xing reported a massive Khitan advance. That year starvation deaths nationwide ran into the hundreds of thousands; local officials were ordered to bury the dead. On renwu the northern expedition was set for the thirteenth of the month, with former Binzhou governor Li Zhou left as acting eastern capital garrison commander. On yiyou the emperor departed the Eastern Capital. On dinghai enemy cavalry reached Liyang. Palace guard cavalry commander Li Shouzhen was made chief controller of the vanguard. Hebei was in crisis; plea envoys from the prefectures came one after another. On wuzi the emperor reached Chunzhou. Beizhou governor Wang Lingwen was transferred to Dengzhou after his brother Lingchong arrived from the Khitan reporting that their entire clan was trapped at Ganling. On xinmao Zhang Congen of Yedu sent men over the wall at night to report that the Khitan ruler had pitched camp at Yuancheng with 30,000–40,000 cavalry, made Zhao Yanshou Weibo governor and Prince of Wei, and was daily pressing the ramparts before withdrawing. On jiawu Liu Zhiyuan of the northern capital was made pacification commissioner for the Youzhou campaign, with Du Wei of Hengzhou as deputy and Ma Quanjie of Dingzhou as chief controller, with staffing left to the commissioner's discretion. On yiwei a white rainbow appeared paired in heavy fog; diviners said, "This betokens maritime excess—battle is sure to follow. " An edict ordered all public and private horses nationwide levied for the cavalry. On bingshen the Khitan attacked Liyang. Zhang Yanzhe and others were sent with 3,000 elite horsemen to resist them. On jihai interpreter Meng Shouzhong was sent to the Khitan ruler seeking to restore friendly relations. Shouzhong returned from the enemy camp with a reply: "What is already settled cannot be undone. " On xinchou Taiyuan reported victory at Xiugu: 3,000 heads, 500 captives, and 17 enemy generals taken; the enemy scattered into Yamming Valley and pursuit was underway.

二月甲辰朔，遣石赟守麻家口，何建守楊劉鎮，白再榮守馬家渡，安彥威守河陽。 鄆州奏，博州刺史周儒以城降契丹，又與楊光遠潛約，光遠引契丹於馬家渡濟河。 時郭謹在汶陽，遣左武衛將軍蔡行遇率數百騎赴之。 遇伏兵於葭葦中，突然而出，轉鬥數合，部下皆遁，行遇為賊所執，鋒鏑重傷，不能乘馬，坐畚中舁至幕帳。 遣李守貞等水陸進兵而下，以救汶陽。 丙午，先鋒指揮使石公霸與契丹遇於戚城之北，為契丹所圍。 高行周、符彥卿方息於林下，聞賊至駭愕，督軍而進。 契丹眾甚盛，被圍數重，遣人馳告景延廣，請益師。 延廣遲留，候帝進止。 行周等大噪，瞋目奮擊賊眾，傷死者甚多，帝自禦親兵救之方解。 〈 （《宋史·符彥卿傳》：契丹騎兵數萬，圍高行周於鐵丘，諸將莫敢當其鋒，彥卿獨引數百騎擊之，遼人遁去，行周得免。 《高懷德傳》：至戚城，被圍數重，援兵不至，危甚，懷德左右射，縱橫馳突，眾皆披靡，挾父而出。）〉 登戚城古臺，置酒以勞三將，鹹咎延廣不遣兵赴難，相對泣下。 戊申，契丹築壘於馬家渡東岸，以騎軍列於外，以禦王師。 李守貞以師搏之，遂破其眾。 敵騎散走，赴河溺死者數千，遂拔其壘。 初，西岸敵軍數萬，鼓噪揚旗以助其勢，及見東岸兵敗，號哭而去。 獲馬八百匹，生擒賊將七十八人，部眾五百人，送行在，悉斬之。 辛亥，夏州節度使李彜殷合蕃漢之兵四萬抵麟州，濟河，侵契丹之境，以牽脅之。 壬子，以彜殷為契丹西南面招討使。 易州刺史安審約奏，戰契丹於北平，賊退保祁溝關，斷其橋梁而還。 癸丑，博州殘兵至自敵中。 周儒之降也，賊執其軍士，將獻於幕帳，行次中途，守者夜寢，其中軍士一人自解桎梏，為諸兵釋縛，取敵戈矛，盡殺援者二百餘人，南走而歸，至河無舟，浮水而過，溺死之餘，所存者六十七人。 是日，日有黃白暈，二白虹夾日而行。 己未，滄州奏，賊眾三千人援送所掠人口寶貨等，由長蘆入蕃，以輕騎邀之，斬獲千餘人，人口輜重悉委之而走。 庚申，宰臣馮道等再上表請聽樂，皆不允。 時帝自期年之後，於宮中間舉細聲女樂，及親征以來，日於左右召淺蕃軍校，奏三弦胡琴，和以羌笛，擊節鳴鼓，更舞叠歌，以為娛樂。 常謂侍臣曰：「此非音樂也。 」故馮道等奏請舉樂，詔旨未允而止。 壬戌，楊光遠率兵圍冀州，刺史李瓊以州兵擊之，棄營而遁。 冀州奏，敗賊軍於城下，見舁棺者，訊其降者，曰「戚城之戰，上將金頭王中流矢而死，此其櫬也。 」癸亥，以前鄧州節度使何建為東南面馬步軍都部署，率師屯汶陽。 甲子，蜀人寇我階州。
On the jiachen new moon in the second month Shi Yun was posted at Majiakou, He Jian at Yangliu Fort, Bai Zairong at Majia Ford, and An Yanwei at Heyang. Yunzhou reported that Bozhou prefect Zhou Ru had surrendered to the Khitan and secretly coordinated with Yang Guangyuan to bring them across at Majia Ford. Guo Jin, then at Wenyang, sent Left Martial Guard general Cai Xingyu with several hundred horsemen to reinforce them. Ambushers burst from the reeds; after several clashes his men fled. Xingyu was captured, badly wounded, unable to ride, and was carried in a basket to the enemy camp. Li Shouzhen and others were ordered downstream by land and water to relieve Wenyang. On bingwu vanguard commander Shi Gongba met the Khitan north of Qicheng and was surrounded. Gao Xingzhou and Fu Yanqing were resting in a grove when news of the enemy startled them into advancing their troops. The Khitan were overwhelming; encircled in multiple rings, they sent urgent word to Jing Yanguang for reinforcements. Yanguang held back, awaiting the emperor's orders. Xingzhou's men shouted and fought fiercely, with heavy casualties; only when the emperor led the personal guard did the encirclement break. 〈Note (History of Song, Biography of Fu Yanqing: Tens of thousands of Khitan cavalry surrounded Gao Xingzhou at Tieqiu; no general dared meet them until Yanqing alone led several hundred horsemen in attack and the Liao fled, freeing Xingzhou. On wushen the Khitan fortified the east bank of Majia Ford with cavalry deployed outside against the imperial army. Li Shouzhen attacked and routed them. Enemy cavalry fled; thousands drowned in the river, and the ramparts were captured. Earlier tens of thousands on the west bank had cheered and raised banners; seeing the east-bank force defeated, they wailed and withdrew. Eight hundred horses were taken; 78 rebel officers and 500 soldiers were captured, brought to the emperor, and executed. On xinhai Li Yiyin of Xiazhou led 40,000 combined Tangut and Han troops to Linzhou, crossed the river, and raided Khitan territory to draw them off. On renzi Yiyin was appointed pacification commissioner against the Khitan southwest. Yizhou prefect An Shenyao reported defeating the Khitan at Beiping; the enemy fell back to Qigou Pass, and he destroyed their bridges and withdrew. On guichou remnant troops from Bozhou returned from behind enemy lines. After Zhou Ru's surrender his men were seized for presentation at the enemy camp. Midway, guards slept; one soldier freed himself and his comrades, seized enemy weapons, killed 200 escorts, and fled south. Without boats they swam the river; only 67 survived the crossing. That day the sun showed a yellow-white halo with two white rainbows flanking it. On jiwei Cangzhou reported intercepting 3,000 bandits escorting plunder toward Changlu: over 1,000 killed or captured, captives and baggage abandoned in flight. On gengshen chief minister Feng Dao and others again petitioned for court music; all were refused. Since the mourning year ended he had occasionally had soft female musicians in the palace; on campaign he daily summoned frontier officers to play huqin and Qiang flutes with drums, dancing and singing for amusement. He often told his attendants, "This is not music. " Hence Feng Dao's petitions for formal music were left unapproved. On renxu Yang Guangyuan besieged Jizhou; prefect Li Qiong counterattacked and drove him from camp. Jizhou reported victory below the walls; bearers with a coffin were questioned, and captives said, "At Qicheng the commander King Golden Head died of an arrow wound—this is his coffin. " On guihai former Dengzhou governor He Jian was made overall commander of southeastern forces and encamped at Wenyang. On jiazi Shu raided Jiezhou.

三月癸酉朔，契丹主領兵十餘萬來戰。 時契丹偽棄元城寨已旬日矣，伏精騎於頓丘故城，以待王師。 〈 （《通鑒》：鄴都留守張從恩屢奏敵已遁去，大軍欲進追之，會霖雨而止。）〉 設伏累日，人馬饑頓，趙延壽謀曰：「晉軍悉在河上，畏我鋒銳，不敢前進，不如徑造城下，四面而進，攻奪其橋梁，天下定矣。 」契丹主然之。 是日，前軍高行周在戚城之南，賊將趙延壽、趙延昭以數萬騎出王師之西，契丹主自擁精騎出王師之東，兩軍接戰，交相勝負。 至晡時，契丹主以勁兵中央出而來，帝御親軍列為後陣，東西濟河，為偃月之勢，旗幟鮮盛，士馬嚴整。 契丹主望之，謂左右曰：「楊光遠言晉朝兵馬半已餓死，今日觀之，何其壯耶！ 」敵騎往來馳突，王師植立不動，萬弩齊彀，飛矢蔽空，賊軍稍卻。 會有亡者告契丹主曰：「南軍東面人少，沿河城柵不固，可以攻之。 」契丹乃率精騎以攻東邊，王師敗走，敵騎追之。 時有夾馬軍士千餘人在堤間治水寨，旗幟之末出於堰埭，敵望見之，以為伏兵所起，追騎乃止。 久之復戰，王師又退，李守超以數百騎短兵直起擊之，敵稍卻。 戰場之地，人馬死者無算，斷箭殘鏃，橫厚數寸。 遇夜，賊擊鉦抽軍而退，夜行三十里而舍焉。 護聖指揮使協霸 〈 （協霸」二字上疑有脫文。）〉 亡入賊中，夷其族。 護聖第二軍都指揮使安重懷、指揮使烏韓七、監軍何彥超等臨陣畏怯，手失兵仗，悉斬之。 乙亥，契丹主帳內小校竊其主所乘馬來奔，云：「契丹已傳木書，收軍北去。」 〈 （《契丹國志》云：景延廣疑有詐，閉壁不敢追。 遼帝北歸，所過焚掠民物殆盡。）〉 齊州奏，青州賊軍寇明水鎮。 壬午，禮部尚書盧詹卒，贈太子太保。 甲申，契丹車帳已過貝州，以趙延昭守貝州。 〈 （《遼史》：三月壬午，留趙延昭守貝州，徙所俘戶於內地。）〉 辛卯，定州馬全節攻泰州，拔之，俘其兵士二千人，雜畜戎仗稱是。 癸巳，北京留守、兼中書令劉知遠封太原王，餘如故。 是日，詔天下抽點鄉兵，凡七戶出一士，六戶資之，仍自具兵仗，以「武定」為軍號。 太常丞王緒棄市。 緒家於青州，常致書於楊光遠，緒有妾之兄慊緒不為赒給，遂告與光遠連謀，密書述朝廷機事，遂收捕斬之。
On the guiyou new moon in the third month the Khitan ruler brought more than 100,000 men to fight. For ten days the Khitan had feigned abandoning Yuancheng camp while hiding elite cavalry at old Dunqiu to ambush the imperial army. 〈Note (Comprehensive Mirror: Zhang Congen of Yedu repeatedly reported the enemy had fled; pursuit was halted by heavy rain.)〉 After days in ambush, men and horses were exhausted. Zhao Yanshou urged: "Jin forces on the river fear our edge—strike straight for the walls on four sides and seize the bridges, and the realm is ours. " The Khitan ruler agreed. That day Gao Xingzhou's vanguard was south of Qicheng; Zhao Yanshou and Zhao Yanzhao led tens of thousands west of the imperial line while the Khitan ruler led elite cavalry east; battle seesawed. By afternoon the Khitan ruler committed his center; the emperor formed the personal guard as rear reserve, crossing east and west in a crescent with banners bright and ranks tight. The Khitan ruler looked and told his attendants, "Yang Guangyuan said half the Jin army had starved—yet see how strong they are today! " Enemy cavalry charged repeatedly; the imperial line held firm as ten thousand crossbows darkened the sky and the enemy slowly withdrew. A deserter told the Khitan ruler, "The southern eastern wing is thin and the riverside palisades weak—they can be attacked. " The Khitan struck the eastern wing with elite cavalry; the imperial army broke and fled under pursuit. More than a thousand horse-guard troops building a water camp on the dike had banner tips visible above the weir; the enemy mistook them for ambush and halted pursuit. Fighting resumed; the imperial line retreated again until Li Shouchao led several hundred horsemen in a direct charge and the enemy gave ground. The dead were beyond count; broken arrows and bolt-heads lay inches deep on the field. At nightfall the enemy sounded gongs and withdrew, marching thirty li before camping. Guardian Sage commander Xieba 〈Note (Text is likely missing above "Xieba.")〉 fled to the enemy, and his clan was exterminated. On renwu minister of rites Lu Zhan died and was posthumously honored as crown prince grand guardian. On jiashen the Khitan ruler's camp had passed Beizhou, leaving Zhao Yanzhao to hold it. 〈Note (History of Liao: Third month, renwu, Zhao Yanzhao held Beizhou and captives were resettled inland.)〉 On xinmao Ma Quanjie of Dingzhou took Taizhou, capturing 2,000 soldiers plus livestock and arms in quantity. Xu, whose family was in Qingzhou, had corresponded with Yang Guangyuan; his concubine's brother, resenting lack of support, denounced him for plotting with Guangyuan and revealing court secrets—he was arrested and executed.

On bingchen the Yellow River broke at Hua prefecture, flooding Cao, Shan, Pu, Yun, and other prefectures; waters around Liangshan merged with the Wen and Ji. 〈Note (History of Song, Biography of Yang Zhaojian: The river broke across several commanderies; corvée was levied en masse under the circuit commander until the breach was sealed. The Later Jin young emperor was pleased and ordered a commemorative stele. Zhaojian remonstrated: "Your Majesty, a stele recording merit is less fitting than an edict of grief and repentance; praise in prose is less fitting than publishing a confession of fault. " His words were earnest; the young emperor admired them and abandoned the stele.)〉 On wuwu Fuzhou was elevated to training commissioner status. On gengshen Xiangzhou presented a white magpie. On jiazi Hanlin academicians were restored. On yichou the chief ministers petitioned three times for court music and were granted permission. On wuchen Wang Song of the Chancellery was made left vice director; Wang Yijian, right vice director, was made vice minister of personnel; Xiao Yuan, Right Regular Cavalry Attendant, was made director of the Secretariat; Wang Renyu was promoted to Attendant within the Yellow Gate; Li Shi was made Left Regular Cavalry Attendant; Xu Taifu of the Gold Department, drafter of edicts, was made Hanlin academician; Li Huan of the Rites Department was made drafter of edicts and Hanlin academician; Liu Wensou of the Justice Department was transferred to the Capital Office and made Hanlin academician; Fan Zhi of the Bureau of Receptions was made Hanlin academician; Censor Zhang Yi was made outer assistant of the Granary Department and drafter of edicts. On gengwu former Jinzhou governor Zhou Mi was appointed Left Dragon Martial commander-in-chief and Li Huaizhong of Tongzhou Left Forest Army commander-in-chief.
