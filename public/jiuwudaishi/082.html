1
天福八年秋七月丁丑朔，京師雨水深三尺。 辛巳，許州節度使李從溫來朝，進封楚國公。 壬午，以前河陽節度使皇甫遇為右龍武統軍。 丁亥，以宣徽南院使李承福為同州節度使。 癸巳，改陜州甘棠驛為通津驛，避廟諱也。 甲午，正衙命冊皇太后，以宰臣李崧充使，右散騎常侍李慎儀為副。 丁酉，幸南莊，召從駕臣僚習射，路左農人各賜布衫麻屨。
On the first day of the seventh month of autumn in the eighth year of Tianfu, floodwaters in the capital stood three feet deep. On xinsi, Li Congwen, prefect of Xuzhou, arrived at court and was promoted to Duke of Chu. On renwu, Huangfu Yu, former prefect of Heyang, was appointed commander of the Right Dragon Martial Army. On dinghai, Li Chenfu, commissioner of the Southern Office of the Palace Secretariat, was appointed prefect of Tongzhou. On guisi, Gantang Post Station in Shanzhou was renamed Tongjin Post Station to observe the imperial temple taboo. On jiawu, the court ordered the formal investiture of the Empress Dowager, appointing Chief Minister Li Song as envoy and Right Regular Attendant Li Shenyi as his deputy. On dingyou, the emperor visited South Manor and summoned his accompanying officials for archery practice; to farmers along the roadside he gave each a cloth shirt and hemp sandals.
2
八月戊申，右衛上將軍楊思權卒，贈太傅。 辛亥，分命朝臣一十三人分檢諸州旱苗。 涇、青、磁、鄴都共奏逃戶凡五千八百九十。 諸縣令佐以天災民餓，攜牌印納者五。 癸酉，以前昭義節度使李從敏為左龍武統軍。
In the eighth month, on wushen, Right Guard General Yang Siquan died and was posthumously honored as Grand Preceptor. On xinhai, thirteen court officials were dispatched separately to inspect drought damage in the various prefectures. Jingzhou, Qingzhou, Cizhou, and Yedu jointly reported a total of 5,890 absconding households. Five county magistrates and their assistants surrendered their seals and credentials, citing natural disaster and famine among the people. On guiyou, Li Congmin, former prefect of Zhaoyi, was appointed commander of the Left Dragon Martial Army.
3
九月戊寅，尊秦國夫人安氏為皇太妃，帝所生母也。 丁亥，追冊故魏國夫人張氏為皇后，帝之元妃也。 丙子，以金部郎中、知制誥馮玉為檢校尚書右僕射，充潁州團練使。 戊子，前潁州團練使田令方追奪在身官爵，勒歸私第，坐前任耀州日，額外配民曲錢納歸私室故也。 延州奏，綏州刺史李彜敏拋棄城郡，與弟彜俊等五人將骨肉二百七十口來投，當州押送赴闕，稱與兄夏州節度使彜殷偶起猜嫌，互相攻伐故也。 辛卯，夏州奏，差宥州刺史李仁立權知綏州。 癸巳，故絳州刺史張從訓贈太尉，追冊皇后之父也。 甲午，夏州李彜殷奏：「衙內都指揮使拓拔崇斌等五人作亂，當時收擒處斬訖。 相次綏州刺史李彜敏擅將兵士，直抵城門，尋差人掩殺，彜敏知事不濟，與弟五人將家南走。 」詔：「李彜敏潛結兇黨，顯恣逆謀。 骨肉之間，尚興屠害； 照臨之內，難以含容。 送夏州處斬。 」丙申，幸大年莊，遂幸侍衛使景延廣第。 延廣進金玉器玩，賜延廣玉帶名馬，母妻、賓佐、部曲、僮仆錫賫鹹及之。 庚子，以右諫議大夫邊光範為給事中，以吏部郎中劉知新為右諫議大夫。 是月，諸州郡括借到軍食，以籍來上，吏民有隱落者，並處極法。 州郡二十七蝗，餓死者數十萬。
In the ninth month, on wuyin, Lady An, Madame of the State of Qin, was honored as Imperial Grand Consort—the emperor's own birth mother. On dinghai, the late Lady Zhang, Madame of the State of Wei, was posthumously invested as empress—she had been the emperor's original principal consort. On bingzi, Feng Yu, director in the Ministry of Revenue and drafter of edicts, was appointed honorary Right Vice Minister of the Ministry of Revenue and training commissioner of Yingzhou. On wuzi, Tian Lingfang, former training commissioner of Yingzhou, was stripped of his ranks and titles and sent home, because during his earlier tenure in Yaozhou he had levied extra taxes from the people and kept the proceeds for himself. Yanzhou reported that Suizhou Prefect Li Yimin had abandoned his post and fled the city with his brothers Yijun and four others, bringing two hundred seventy dependents to defect; the prefecture escorted them to court, explaining that Yimin and his elder brother Yi Yin, Military Governor of Xiazhou, had grown mutually suspicious and gone to war. On xinmao, Xiazhou reported that Li Renli, prefect of Youzhou, had been appointed acting prefect of Suizhou. On guisi, the late Jiang Prefect Zhang Congxun was posthumously honored as Grand Commandant—the father of the posthumously invested empress. On jiawu, Li Yiyin of Xiazhou reported: "Five intramural commanders, including Tubo Chongbin, had risen in revolt; they were captured and executed at once. Later, Suizhou Prefect Li Yimin led troops without authorization straight to the city gate; troops were sent at once to capture and kill them. When Yimin saw the plot could not succeed, he fled south with five brothers and their households. An edict declared: "Li Yimin secretly enlisted violent accomplices and openly pursued treasonous designs. Even among his own kin he brought violence; under imperial protection he could no longer be indulged. He shall be sent to Xiazhou for execution." On bingshen, the emperor visited Danian Manor, then called at the home of Jing Yanguang, commissioner of the palace guard. Yanguang presented gifts of gold, jade, vessels, and curios; the emperor gave him a jade belt and a fine horse, and lavished rewards on his mother, wife, staff, household troops, and servants as well. On gengzi, Right Remonstrance Grandee Bian Guangfan was appointed Attendant Gentleman, and Liu Zhixin, director in the Ministry of Personnel, was appointed Right Remonstrance Grandee. That month, prefectures and commanderies forcibly requisitioned military rations and submitted registers to the throne; any official or commoner who concealed shortfalls was punished by death. Locusts ravaged twenty-seven prefectures and commanderies, and hundreds of thousands died of starvation.
4
冬十月戊申，制以吳國夫人馮氏為皇后，仍令所司擇日備禮冊命。 庚戌，封皇第十一妹為嘉興長公主，第十二妹為永泰長公主。 是夕五更，有彗見於東方，在角，旬日而滅。 壬子，以權知延州軍州事、前鳳州防禦使杜威為延州留後。 甲寅，以國子祭酒兼戶部侍郎田敏充宏文館學士，判館事； 以吏部侍郎張昭遠充史館修撰，判館事； 以給事中司徒詡充集賢殿學士，判院事。 西京奏，百姓馬知饒殺男吳九不死，以其侵母食也。 詔赦之。 甲子，以前延州節度使何健為涇州節度使。 丙寅，以涇州節度使王周為陜府節度使。 己巳，以左散騎常侍、權知開封府事邊蔚為工部侍郎，依前知府事。 壬申，以前兵部侍郎李玘為吏部侍郎。 癸酉，命使攝太尉、右僕射平章事和凝，使副攝司徒、給事中邊光範追冊故魏國夫人張氏為皇后，奉寶冊至西莊影殿行禮，鹵簿儀仗如式。
In the tenth month of winter, on wushen, an edict made Lady Feng, Madame of the State of Wu, empress and ordered the appropriate offices to choose a day and prepare the formal investiture ceremony. On gengxu, the emperor's eleventh younger sister was enfeoffed as Princess Chang of Jiaxing and his twelfth as Princess Chang of Yongtai. That night, during the fifth watch, a comet appeared in the east in the Horn constellation and faded after ten days. On renzi, Du Wei, acting administrator of Yanzhou and former defender of Fengzhou, was appointed surveillance commissioner of Yanzhou. On jiayin, Tian Min, chancellor of the Directorate of Education and concurrent Vice Minister of Revenue, was appointed academician of the Hongwen Hall with charge of its affairs; Zhang Zhaoyuan, Vice Minister of Personnel, was appointed compiler of the Historiography Institute with charge of its affairs; Attendant Gentleman Situ Xu was appointed academician of the Hall of Assembled Worthies with charge of its affairs. The Western Capital reported that a commoner named Ma Zhirao killed his son Wu Jiu, who survived because the boy had taken food meant for his mother. An edict pardoned him. On jiazi, He Jian, former prefect of Yanzhou, was appointed prefect of Jingzhou. On bingyin, Wang Zhou, prefect of Jingzhou, was appointed prefect of Shan Prefecture. On jisi, Bian Wei, Left Regular Attendant and acting administrator of Kaifeng Prefecture, was appointed Vice Minister of Works while continuing to administer the prefecture. On renshen, Li Qi, former Vice Minister of War, was appointed Vice Minister of Personnel. On guiyou, He Ning, acting as Grand Commandant and Right Vice Minister with the title of Grand Councilor, and his deputy Bian Guangfan, acting as Minister of Education and Attendant Gentleman, were ordered to posthumously invest the late Lady Zhang, Madame of the State of Wei, as empress; they were to carry the seal and investiture document to the Shadow Hall at West Manor and perform the rites with the full guard of honor.
5
十一月丁丑，以鄧州節度使宋彥筠為晉州節度使，以涇州節度使何建為鄧州節度使。 己卯，以前鄴都留守、廣晉尹李德充為涇州節度使。 丙申，所司奏議，故天下兵馬都元帥、吳越國王錢元瓘謚曰莊穆，詔改為文穆。 戊戌，遣前復州防禦使吳巒權知貝州軍事，詔節度使王令溫赴闕。 庚戌，單州軍事判官趙嶽奏，刺史楊承祚初夜開門出城，稱為母病，往青州寧親，於孔目官齊琪處留下牌印，臣已行用權知州事。 辛丑，高麗遣使朝貢。 昭化軍節度使、瑞慎等州觀察等使杜建徽進封鄖國公。 遣侍衛步軍都指揮使郭謹領兵赴鄆州。
In the eleventh month, on dingchou, Song Yanyun, prefect of Dengzhou, was appointed prefect of Jinzhou, and He Jian, prefect of Jingzhou, was appointed prefect of Dengzhou. On jimao, Li Dechong, former surveillance commissioner of Yedu and prefect of Guangjin, was appointed prefect of Jingzhou. On bingshen, the relevant offices proposed the posthumous title Zhuangmu for the late Commander-in-Chief of All Armies and King of Wuyue, Qian Yuanguan; an edict changed it to Wenmu. On wuxu, Wu Luan, former defender of Fuzhou, was dispatched to act as administrator of Beizhou's military affairs, and Wang Lingwen, the prefect, was ordered to come to court. On gengxu, Zhao Yue, military affairs judge of Danzhou, reported that Prefect Yang Chengzuo had opened the city gate at nightfall and left, saying his mother was ill and that he was going to Qingzhou to visit his family; he left his seal and credentials with the clerk Qi Qi, and Zhao Yue had already assumed acting administration of the prefecture. On xinchou, Goryeo sent envoys to court with tribute. Du Jianhui, prefect of Zhaohua Army and surveillance commissioner of Ruizhou, Shenzhou, and other prefectures, was promoted to Duke of Yun. Palace Guard Infantry Commander Guo Jin was dispatched to lead troops to Yanzhou.
6
十二月乙巳朔，遣左領軍衛將軍蔡行遇押兵士屯於鄆州，仍遣供奉官殿直二十六人，自河陰至海口，分擘地分巡檢，以青州節度使楊光遠謀叛故也。 庚戌，前左禦正齊國夫人吳氏巳降二十一人，並封郡國夫人，太后宮、皇后宮知客夫人等亦如之。 太子太保致仕梁文矩卒，贈太子太傅。 癸丑，詔河陽節度使符彥卿、宋州節度使高行周、貝州節度使王令溫、同州節度使李承福、陳州梁漢璋、亳州李萼、懷州薛懷讓並赴闕，分命使臣諸州郡巡檢，以契丹入寇故也。 遣給事中邊光範、前登州刺史郭彥威使於契丹，行至恒州，敵已犯境，不能進，留於公館數月，不達其命而回。 〈 （《遼史》：天福八年二月乙卯，晉遣使進先帝遺物。 辛酉，晉遣使請居汴，從之。 三月丁未，晉主至汴，遣使來謝。 五月己亥，遣使如晉，致生辰禮。 六月辛酉，晉遣使貢金。 秋八月丁未朔，晉復貢金。 己未，如奉聖州，晉遣其子延煦來朝。）〉 甲寅，以單州刺史楊承祚為登州刺史，從其便也。 華州陜府奏，逃戶凡一萬二千三百。 乙丑，臘，車駕不出。 詔前陜州節度使石赟率諸節度使畋於郡郊。 太子賓客聶延祚卒。 丁卯，詔宣徽使劉繼勛就杜威園亭會節度使統軍等習射。 淄州奏，青州節度使楊光遠反，遣兵士取淄州，劫刺史翟進宗入青州。 是冬大饑，河南諸州餓死者二萬六千餘口。
On the first day of the twelfth month, Left Army Guard General Cai Xingyu was dispatched to escort troops to garrison at Yanzhou; twenty-six palace attendants were also sent from Heyin to Haikou to divide the territory and conduct patrols, because Yang Guangyuan, prefect of Qingzhou, was plotting rebellion. On gengxu, the former Left Imperial Rectifier Lady Wu and twenty-one others who had already submitted were all enfeoffed as Madame of a commandery or state; the guest-receiving ladies of the Empress Dowager's and Empress's palaces received the same honors. Liang Wenju, retired Grand Mentor of the Heir Apparent, died and was posthumously honored as Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent. On guichou, an edict ordered Fu Yanqing of Heyang, Gao Xingzhou of Songzhou, Wang Lingwen of Beizhou, Li Chenfu of Tongzhou, Liang Hanzhang of Chenzhou, Li E of Bozhou, and Xue Huairang of Huaizhou all to come to court; separate envoys were dispatched to inspect the prefectures and commanderies because the Khitan had invaded. Attendant Gentleman Bian Guangfan and the former Deng Prefect Guo Yanwei were dispatched as envoys to the Khitan; when they reached Hengzhou the enemy had already crossed the border and they could not proceed; they remained at the guesthouse for several months and returned without delivering their mission. Opening editorial annotation. (The 《History of Liao》 records that in the second month of the eighth year of Tianfu, on yimao, Jin sent envoys presenting the late emperor's personal effects. On xinyou, Jin sent envoys requesting to reside at Bian, and the request was granted. In the third month, on dingwei, the Jin ruler arrived at Bian and sent envoys to express thanks. In the fifth month, on jihai, envoys were dispatched to Jin to present birthday gifts. In the sixth month, on xinyou, Jin sent envoys presenting tribute of gold. On the first day of the eighth month of autumn, Jin again presented tribute of gold. On jiwei, he went to Fengsheng Prefecture; Jin sent his son Yanshu to court.) Closing editorial annotation. On jiayin, Yang Chengzuo, prefect of Danzhou, was appointed prefect of Dengzhou, as he had requested. Hua Prefecture and Shan Prefecture jointly reported a total of 12,300 absconding households. On yichou, during the La festival, the emperor did not leave the palace. An edict ordered Shi Yun, former prefect of Shan Prefecture, to lead the various military governors in hunting in the outskirts of the commandery. Nie Yanzuo, Mentor of the Heir Apparent, died. On dingmao, an edict ordered Palace Commissioner Liu Jixun to gather military governors, army commanders, and others at Du Wei's garden pavilion for archery practice. Zizhou reported that Yang Guangyuan, prefect of Qingzhou, had rebelled, sent troops to seize Zizhou, and abducted Prefect Zhai Jinzong into Qingzhou. That winter brought severe famine; in the prefectures south of the Yellow River more than 26,000 people died of starvation.
7
開運元年春正月甲戌朔，是夕陣雲掩北斗之魁星。 乙亥，滄、恒、貝、鄴馳告，契丹前鋒趙延壽、趙延昭引五萬騎入寇，將及甘陵，青州楊光遠召之也。 己卯，契丹陷貝州，知州吳巒死之。 庚辰，以宋州節度使高行周為北面行營都部署，以河陽節度使符彥卿為馬軍左廂排陣使，以右神武統軍皇甫遇為馬軍右廂排陣使，以陜州節度使王周為步軍左廂排陣使，以左羽林統軍潘環為步軍右廂排陣使。 太原奏，契丹入雁門，圍忻、代二州。 恒、滄、邢三州上言，契丹大至。 是歲，天下餓死者數十萬人，詔逐處長吏瘞之。 壬午，詔取此月十三日車駕北征，以前邠州節度使李周為權東京留守。 乙酉，車駕發東京。 丁亥，敵騎至黎陽。 以侍衛馬軍都指揮使李守貞為前軍都虞候。 河北危蹙，諸州求救者人使相望。 戊子，車駕至澶州。 以貝州節度使王令溫為鄧州節度使，時令溫弟令崇自契丹至，訴以舉族陷於甘陵，故有是命。 辛卯，鄴都留守張從恩遣人夜縋城間行，奏契丹主以鐵騎三四萬建牙帳於元城，以趙延壽為魏博節度使，改封魏王，延壽日率騎軍摩壘而退。 甲午，以北京留守劉知遠為幽州道行營招討使，以恒州節度使杜威副之，定州節度使馬全節為都虞候，其職員將校委招討使便宜署置。 乙未，大霧中有白虹相偶，占者曰：「斯為海淫，其下必將有戰。 」詔率天下公私之馬以資騎軍。 丙申，契丹攻黎陽。 遣右武衛上將軍張彥澤等率勁騎三千以禦之。 己亥，遣譯詔官孟守忠致書於契丹主，求修舊好。 守忠自敵帳回，契丹主復書曰：「已成之勢，不可改也。 」辛丑，太原奏，與契丹戰於秀谷，斬首三千級，生擒五百人，獲敵將一十七人，賊軍散入鴉鳴谷，已進軍追襲。
On the first day of the first month of spring in the first year of Kaiyun, battle-array clouds that night covered the leader star of the Northern Dipper. On yihai, Cangzhou, Hengzhou, Beizhou, and Ye sent urgent reports that the Khitan vanguard Zhao Yanshou and Zhao Yanzhao were leading fifty thousand cavalry in invasion and were about to reach Ganling—Yang Guangyuan of Qingzhou had summoned them. On jimao, the Khitan captured Beizhou; Administrator Wu Luan died defending the city. On gengchen, Gao Xingzhou, prefect of Songzhou, was appointed overall commander of the Northern Field Headquarters; Fu Yanqing, prefect of Heyang, was made left-wing cavalry battle-array officer; Huangfu Yu, commander of the Right Divine Martial Army, was made right-wing cavalry battle-array officer; Wang Zhou, prefect of Shan Prefecture, was made left-wing infantry battle-array officer; and Pan Huan, commander of the Left Feathered Forest Army, was made right-wing infantry battle-array officer. Taiyuan reported that the Khitan had entered Yanmen Pass and were besieging Xinzhou and Daizhou. Hengzhou, Cangzhou, and Xingzhou reported that the Khitan had arrived in force. That year, hundreds of thousands of people throughout the realm died of starvation; an edict ordered local officials everywhere to bury the dead. On renwu, an edict set the thirteenth day of that month for the emperor's northern campaign; Li Zhou, former prefect of Binzhou, was appointed acting surveillance commissioner of the Eastern Capital. On yiyou, the emperor departed the Eastern Capital. On dinghai, enemy cavalry reached Liyang. Li Shouzhen, Palace Guard Cavalry Commander, was appointed chief adjutant of the vanguard army. Hebei was in dire peril, and envoys seeking relief from the various prefectures came one after another. On wuzi, the emperor reached Chuan Prefecture. Wang Lingwen, prefect of Beizhou, was appointed prefect of Dengzhou; at the time his younger brother Lingchong had arrived from the Khitan and reported that their entire clan was trapped at Ganling, hence the appointment. On xinmao, Zhang Cong'en, surveillance commissioner of Yedu, sent men who lowered themselves from the wall at night and traveled by secret routes to report that the Khitan ruler had established his command tent at Yuancheng with thirty or forty thousand iron cavalry, appointed Zhao Yanshou prefect of Weibo and enfeoffed him as Prince of Wei, and that Yanshou daily led cavalry to press the walls and then withdraw. On jiawu, Liu Zhiyuan, surveillance commissioner of the Northern Capital, was appointed pacification commissioner of the Youzhou Circuit Field Headquarters; Du Wei, prefect of Hengzhou, served as his deputy; Ma Quanjie, prefect of Dingzhou, was made chief adjutant; staff officers and commanders were left to the pacification commissioner's discretion to appoint. On yiwei, amid heavy fog a paired white rainbow appeared; diviners said: "This is an omen of inundation from the sea; battle will surely follow below. An edict ordered all public and private horses throughout the realm requisitioned to supply the cavalry. On bingshen, the Khitan attacked Liyang. Zhang Yanze, General of the Right Martial Guard, and others were dispatched to lead three thousand crack cavalry to resist them. On jihai, the interpreter-official Meng Shouzhong was dispatched with a letter to the Khitan ruler seeking to restore the former friendship. When Shouzhong returned from the enemy camp, the Khitan ruler replied: "The situation already established cannot be changed. On xinchou, Taiyuan reported that in battle with the Khitan at Xiugu, three thousand heads were taken, five hundred men captured alive, and seventeen enemy generals seized; the rebel army scattered into Yamming Valley, and troops had already advanced in pursuit.

六月辛丑朔，王師拔淄州，斬楊光遠偽署刺史劉翰。 癸卯，以太尉、兼侍中馮道為檢校太師、兼侍中，充同州節度使。 丙午，詔復置樞密院。 丁未，以侍中桑維翰為中書令，充樞密使。 權開封府尹李周卒，輟朝，贈太師。 辛亥，以邢州節度使安叔千為晉州節度使，加同平章事； 以晉州節度使宋彥筠為陜州節度使； 以吏部郎中李穀充樞密直學士。 丙辰，滑州河決，漂註曹、單、濮、鄆等州之境，環梁山合於汶、濟。 〈 （《宋史·楊昭儉傳》：河決數郡，大發丁夫，以本部帥董其役，既而塞之。 晉少主喜，詔立碑紀其事。 昭儉表諫曰：「陛下刻石紀功，不若降哀痛之詔； 摛翰頌美，不若頒罪己之文。 」言甚切至，少主嗟賞之，卒罷其事。）〉 戊午，升府州為團練使額。 庚申，襄州獻白鵲。 甲子，復置翰林學士。 乙丑，宰臣等三上表請聽樂，詔允之。 戊辰，以門下侍郎王松為左丞； 以右丞王易簡為吏部侍郎； 以右散騎常侍蕭願為秘書監； 以右諫議大夫王仁裕為給事中； 以給事中李式為左散騎常侍； 以金部郎中、知制誥徐臺符為翰林學士； 以禮部郎中李浣本官知制誥，充翰林學士； 以刑部郎中劉溫叟改都官郎中，充翰林學士； 以主客員外郎範質充翰林學士； 御史張宜改倉部員外郎、知制誥。 庚午，以前晉州節度使周密為左龍武統軍，以同州節度使李懷忠為左羽林統軍。
On the first day of the sixth month, the imperial army captured Zizhou and beheaded Liu Han, whom Yang Guangyuan had falsely appointed prefect. On guimao, Feng Dao, Grand Commandant and concurrent Palace Attendant, was appointed honorary Grand Preceptor and concurrent Palace Attendant, serving as prefect of Tongzhou. On bingwu, an edict restored the Bureau of Military Affairs. On dingwei, Palace Attendant Sang Weihan was appointed Grand Secretary and commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs. Li Zhou, acting Prefect of Kaifeng, died; court was suspended, and he was posthumously honored as Grand Preceptor. On xinhai, An Shuqian, prefect of Xingzhou, was appointed prefect of Jinzhou and given the title of Associate Grand Councilor; Song Yanyun, prefect of Jinzhou, was appointed prefect of Shan Prefecture; Li Gu, director in the Ministry of Personnel, was appointed direct academician of the Bureau of Military Affairs. On bingchen, the Yellow River breached its banks at Huazhou, flooding Cao, Dan, Pu, Yan, and other prefectures; it encircled Liangshan and joined the Wen and Ji rivers. Opening editorial annotation. (The 《History of Song》, Biography of Yang Zhaojian, records that the river breached in several commanderies; corvée labor was massively mobilized; the commander of the local circuit supervised the work, and afterward the breach was sealed. The Later Jin emperor was pleased and ordered a stele erected to commemorate the affair. Zhaojian submitted a memorial remonstrating: "Your Majesty, carving stone to record merit is not as good as issuing an edict of grief and compassion; composing prose in praise is not as good as promulgating a text of self-reproach. His words were very earnest; the emperor sighed in admiration and in the end abandoned the matter.) Closing editorial annotation. On wuwu, Fu Prefecture was elevated to training commissioner status. On gengshen, Xiang Prefecture presented a white magpie. On jiazi, Hanlin Academicians were restored. On yichou, the chief ministers and others submitted memorials three times requesting permission for music; an edict granted their request. On wuchen, Wang Song, Vice Minister of the Department of State Affairs, was appointed Left Vice Director; Wang Yijian, Right Vice Director, was appointed Vice Minister of Personnel; Xiao Yuan, Right Regular Attendant, was appointed Director of the Imperial Library; Wang Renyu, Right Remonstrance Grandee, was appointed Attendant Gentleman; Li Shi, Attendant Gentleman, was appointed Left Regular Attendant; Xu Taifu, director in the Ministry of Revenue and drafter of edicts, was appointed Hanlin Academician; Li Huan, director in the Ministry of Rites, retained his original office and served as drafter of edicts, acting as Hanlin Academician; Liu Wensou, director in the Ministry of Justice, was reassigned to Director in the Bureau of Punishments and appointed Hanlin Academician; Fan Zhi, assistant director in the Bureau of Receptions, was appointed Hanlin Academician; Censor Zhang Yi was reassigned to Assistant Director in the Bureau of Granaries and appointed drafter of edicts. On gengwu, Zhou Mi, former prefect of Jinzhou, was appointed commander of the Left Dragon Martial Army, and Li Huaizhong, prefect of Tongzhou, was appointed commander of the Left Feathered Forest Army.
