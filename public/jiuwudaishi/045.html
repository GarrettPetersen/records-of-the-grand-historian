1
閔帝，諱從厚，小字菩薩奴，明宗第三子也。 母昭懿皇后夏氏，以天祐十一年歲在甲戌，十一月二十八日庚申，生帝於晉陽舊第。 帝髫齔好讀《春秋》，略通大義，貌類明宗，尤鍾愛。
Emperor Min, whose taboo name was Conghou and childhood name Pusa Nu, was Mingzong's third son. His mother was Empress Zhaoyi, Lady Xia. On gengshen, the twenty-eighth day of the eleventh month in jiaxu—the eleventh year of Tianyou—he was born at the old residence in Jinyang. As a boy he loved reading the Spring and Autumn Annals and understood its main themes; he looked like Mingzong and was especially favored.
2
四年十一月二十日，秦王誅。 翌日，明宗遣宣徽使孟漢瓊馳驛召帝，二十六日，明宗崩，二十九日，帝至自鄴。 十二月癸卯朔，發喪於西宮，帝於柩前即位。 丁未，群臣上表請聽政，表再上，詔允。 己酉，中外將士給賜有差。 庚戌，帝縗服見群臣於廣壽門之東廡下，宰臣馮道進曰：「陛下久居哀毀，臣等鹹願一睹聖顏。 」朱宏昭前舉帽，群臣再拜而退。 禦光政樓存問軍民。 辛亥，賜司衣王氏死，坐秦王事也。 癸丑，以前鎮州節度使、涇王從敏權知河南府事，尋以盧質代之。 乙卯，賜司儀康氏死，事連王氏也。 丙辰，以天雄軍節度判官唐汭為諫議大夫，掌書記趙彖為起居郎，元從都押衙宋令詢為磁州刺史。 丁巳，以左僕射、平章事馮道為山陵使，戶部尚書韓彥惲為副，中書舍人王延為判官，禮部尚書王權為禮儀使，兵部尚書李鈴為鹵簿使，御史中丞龍敏為儀仗使，右僕射、權知河南府盧質為橋道頓遞使。 庚申，以前相州刺史郝瓊為右驍衛大將軍，充宣徽北院使； 以光祿卿、充三司副使王玫為三司使。 癸亥，故檢校太尉、右衛上將軍、充三司使孫嶽贈太尉、齊國公。 丁卯，帝釋縗服，群臣三上表，請復常膳，禦正殿，從之。 辛未，帝御中興殿，群臣列位，馮道升階進酒。 帝曰：「比於此物無愛，除賓友之會，不近樽斝。 況在沉痛之中，安事飲啖！ 」命徹之。
In the fourth year, on the twentieth day of the eleventh month, the Prince of Qin was put to death. The next day Mingzong sent palace secretariat commissioner Meng Hanqiong by relay to summon Conghou. Mingzong died on the twenty-sixth, and the emperor arrived from Ye on the twenty-ninth. On the guimao new moon of the twelfth month mourning was proclaimed at the Western Palace, and Conghou took the throne before the coffin. On dingwei officials submitted memorials asking him to assume rule; they submitted again, and an edict granted the request. On jiwei court and field forces received graded rewards. On gengxu the emperor, still in mourning hemp, received officials in the eastern portico of Guangshou Gate. Grand councilor Feng Dao stepped forward: "Your Majesty has mourned so long—we all long to see your face. Zhu Hongzhao stepped forward and raised the hat; the officials bowed again and withdrew. He appeared at Guangzheng Tower to address soldiers and civilians. On xinhai palace dress steward Lady Wang was executed in connection with the Prince of Qin case. On guichou former Zhenzhou commissioner and Prince of Jing Cong Min was named acting Henan prefect, but Lu Zhi soon replaced him. On yimao palace ritual steward Lady Kang was executed in the same investigation as Lady Wang. On bingchen Tang Ni, adjutant to the Tianxiong commissioner, became Remonstrance and Criticism Grand Master; Zhao Luan, secretary, became diarist; and Song Lingxun, chief adjutant from the old retinue, became prefect of Ci. On dingsi left vice director and grand councilor Feng Dao was named imperial tomb commissioner, with Han Yanhun as deputy, Wang Yan as judge, Wang Quan as ritual commissioner, Li Ling as escort commissioner, Long Min as guard commissioner, and Lu Zhi, right vice director and acting Henan prefect, as bridge-and-relay commissioner. On gengshen former Yanzhou governor Hao Qiong became Right Undaunted Guard general-in-chief and Northern Courtyard palace secretariat commissioner; Court provisioner and deputy Three Departments commissioner Wang Mei became Three Departments commissioner. On guihai the late honorary grand commandant Sun Yue, senior general of the Right Guard and Three Departments commissioner, was posthumously made grand commandant and Duke of Qi. On dingmao the emperor ended mourning dress. Officials memorialized three times asking him to resume normal meals and hold court in the main hall, and he agreed. On xinwei the emperor held court in Zhongxing Hall. Officials took their ranks, and Feng Dao ascended to offer wine. The emperor said, "I have no taste for this; except at gatherings with friends I do not touch wine. In deep mourning, why would I eat and drink!" He ordered the wine removed.
3
庚辰，宰臣馮道加司空，李愚加右僕射，劉煦加吏部尚書，餘並如故。 壬午，侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使、河陽節度使康義誠加檢校太尉、兼侍中，判六軍諸衛事。 甲申，以侍衛馬軍都指揮使、寧國軍節度使安彥威為河中節度使； 以侍衛步軍都指揮使、忠正軍節度使張從賓為涇州節度使，並加檢校太傅； 以捧聖左右廂都指揮使、欽州刺史朱洪實為寧國軍節度使，加檢校太保，充侍衛馬軍都指揮使； 以嚴衛左右廂都指揮使、岩州刺史皇甫遇為忠正軍節度使、檢校太保，充侍衛步軍都指揮使。 戊子，樞密使、檢校太尉、同平章事朱宏昭，樞密使、檢校太尉、同中書門下二品馮贇並加兼中書令。 北京留守、河東節度使兼大同彰國振武威塞等軍蕃漢馬步總管石敬瑭加兼中書令； 幽州節度使、檢校太尉、兼中書令趙德鈞加檢校太師、兼中書令。 樞密使馮贇表堅讓中書令，製改兼侍中，封邠國公。 庚寅，鳳翔節度使、潞王從珂加兼侍中； 青州節度使、檢校太尉、兼中書令房知溫加檢校太師。 辛卯，以翰林學士承旨、尚書右丞李懌為工部尚書，以秘書監盧文紀為太常卿，充山陵禮儀使。 壬辰，荊南節度使、檢校太尉、兼中書令高從誨封南平王； 湖南節度使、檢校太尉、兼中書令馬希範封楚王。 甲午，兩浙節度使、檢校太師、守中書令、吳王錢元瓘進封吳越王； 前洺州團練使皇甫立加檢校太保，充鄜州節度使； 前彰義軍節度使康福加檢校太傅，充邠州節度使； 劍南東、西兩川節度使、檢校太尉、兼中書令、蜀王孟知祥加檢校太師。 製下，知祥辭不受命。 丙申，鎮州節度使、檢校太尉、兼侍中範延光，汴州節度使、檢校太尉、兼侍中趙延壽，並加檢校太師。 戊戌，山南西道節度使、檢校太傅、同平章事張虔釗，襄州節度使趙在禮，並加檢校太尉。 辛丑，以振武軍節度使、安北都護楊檀兼大同、彰國、振武、威塞等軍都虞候，充北面馬軍都指揮使。
On gengchen grand councilor Feng Dao was also named Minister of Works; Li Yu became right vice director; Liu Xu became Minister of Personnel; others kept their posts. On renwu Kang Yicheng, overall commander of the Personal Guard and Heyang commissioner, was also named honorary grand commandant and palace attendant with charge of the Six Armies and palace guards. On jiashen An Yanwei, Personal Guard cavalry commander and Ningguo commissioner, became Hezhong commissioner; Zhang Congbin, infantry commander and Zhongzheng commissioner, became Jingzhou commissioner; both were also named honorary grand tutor; Zhu Hongshi of the Sacred Guard and Qinzhou was made Ningguo commissioner, honorary grand guardian, and Personal Guard cavalry commander; Huangfu Yu of the Strict Guard and Yanzhou became Zhongzheng commissioner and honorary grand guardian, commanding Personal Guard infantry. On wuzi Zhu Hongzhao and Feng Yun, both military affairs commissioners and honorary grand commandants, were also named director of the Secretariat. Northern Capital regent and Hedong commissioner Shi Jingtang, overall commander of Datong, Zhangguo, Zhenwu, Weisai, and allied horse and foot, was also named director of the Secretariat; Youzhou commissioner Zhao Dejun, honorary grand commandant and director of the Secretariat, was also named honorary grand preceptor and director of the Secretariat. Feng Yun firmly declined director of the Secretariat in a memorial; the edict changed the appointment to palace attendant instead and enfeoffed him as Duke of Bin. On gengyin Fengxiang commissioner and Prince of Lu Cong Ke was also named palace attendant; Qingzhou commissioner Fang Zhiwen, honorary grand commandant and director of the Secretariat, was also named honorary grand preceptor. On xinmao Hanlin academician-in-chief Li Yi became Minister of Works; Lu Wenji, director of the palace library, became Director of Imperial Sacrifices and imperial tomb ritual commissioner. On renchen Jingnan commissioner Gao Conghui, honorary grand commandant and director of the Secretariat, was enfeoffed as Prince of Nanping; Ma Xifan of Hunan, honorary grand commandant and director of the Secretariat, was enfeoffed as Prince of Chu. On jiawu Qian Yuanguan of Liang-Zhe, honorary grand preceptor, acting director of the Secretariat, and Prince of Wu, was promoted to Prince of Wuyue; Former Luo regiment trainer Huangfu Li was also named honorary grand guardian and Fuzhou commissioner; Former Zhangyi commissioner Kang Fu was also named honorary grand tutor and Binzhou commissioner; Meng Zhixiang of eastern and western Jiannan, honorary grand commandant, director of the Secretariat, and Prince of Shu, was also named honorary grand preceptor. When the edict came down, Meng Zhixiang refused the appointment. On bingshen Fan Yanguang of Zhenzhou and Zhao Yanshou of Bianzhou, both honorary grand commandants and palace attendants, were also named honorary grand preceptor. On wuxu Zhang Qianzhao of Shannan West and Xiangzhou commissioner Zhao Zaili were both named honorary grand commandant. On xinchou Yang Tan of Zhenwu and Protector-General of Pacifying the North was also named overall adjutant of Datong, Zhangguo, Zhenwu, Weisai, and allied armies and northern cavalry commander.
4
閏月壬寅朔，群臣赴西宮臨。 癸卯，禦文明殿入閣。 以前右僕射、權知河南府事盧質為太子少傅兼河南尹。 以諫議大夫唐汭、膳部郎中知制誥陳乂並為給事中，充樞密院直學士。 〈 （《通鑒》：汭以文學從帝，曆三鎮在幕府。 及即位，將佐之有才者，朱、馮皆斥逐之。 汭性迂疏，朱、馮恐帝含怒有時而發，乃引汭於密近，以其黨陳乂監之。）〉 宣徽南院使、驃騎大將軍、左衛上將軍、知內侍省孟漢瓊加開府儀同三司，賜忠貞扶運保泰功臣。 丙午，正衙命使冊皇太后曹氏。 戊申，以前雄武軍節度使劉仲殷為右衛上將軍，邢州節度使趙鳳加爵邑。 自是諸藩鎮文武臣僚皆次第加恩，帝嗣位覃恩澤也。 以翰林學士、中書舍人崔棁為工部侍郎，依前充職。 以給事中張鵬為御史中丞，以御史中丞龍敏為兵部侍郎，以太僕少卿竇維為大理卿。 甲寅，正衙命使冊皇太妃王氏。 集賢院上言：「準敕書修創淩煙閣，尋奉詔問閣高下等級。 謹按淩煙閣，都長安時在西內三清殿側，畫像皆北面，閣有中隔，隔內面北寫功高宰輔，南面寫功高諸侯王，隔外面次第圖畫功臣題讚。 自西京板蕩，四十餘年，舊日主掌官吏及畫像工人，並已淪喪，集賢院所管寫真官、畫真官人數不少，都洛後廢職。 今將起閣，望先定佐命功臣人數，請下翰林院預令寫真本，及下將作監興功，次序間架修建。 」乃詔集賢御書院復置寫真官、畫真官各一員，餘依所奏。 丁巳，安州奏，此月七日夜，節度使符彥超為部曲王希全所害，廢朝一日。 戊午，以前振武軍節度使、安北都護高行周為彰武節度使。 辛酉，以前鄆州使範政為少府監。 丙寅，幸至德宮。 車駕至興教門，有飛鳶自空而墜，僵於御前。 是日大風晦冥。
On the renchen new moon of the intercalary month officials attended mourning at the Western Palace. On guimao he held court at Civilization Hall. Former right vice director and acting Henan prefect Lu Zhi became junior mentor to the heir apparent and Henan prefect. Tang Ni, remonstrance grand master, and Chen Yi, Board of Rites director and edict drafter, both became supervising drafters and direct academicians of the Military Affairs Bureau. [Supplement begins] (The 《Comprehensive Mirror》 says Tang Ni entered the emperor's service through literary talent and served on the staffs of three military commissions. Once enthroned, Zhu and Feng drove away every talented aide. Tang Ni was blunt and naive; Zhu and Feng feared the emperor's anger might break out eventually, so they kept Ni close at hand and posted their ally Chen Yi to watch him.)〉 Meng Hanqiong, southern palace secretariat commissioner and senior general of the Left Guard, was also named Open-House Three Excellencies Equal in Honor with the merit title Upright Loyalty, Support-the-Transport, Preserve-and-Secure. On bingwu the court commissioned an envoy to invest Empress Dowager Cao. On wushen former Xiongwu commissioner Liu Zhongyin became Right Guard senior general, and Xingzhou commissioner Zhao Feng received additional titles and fiefs. Thereafter civil and military officials across the circuits were rewarded in turn—a general amnesty for the new reign. Hanlin academician Cui Chong became Vice Minister of Works while keeping his post. Supervising drafter Zhang Peng became vice censor-in-chief; Long Min became Minister of War; Dou Wei became director of the Court of Judicature and Revision. On jiayin the court commissioned an envoy to invest Imperial Noble Consort Wang. The Hall of Worthies reported: "By imperial order we are to rebuild the Lingyan Pavilion, and we have received an edict asking about its height and ranking. Lingyan Pavilion in Chang'an stood beside the Three Pure Hall in the inner palace. Portraits faced north; inside a central partition north-facing panels showed chief ministers of highest merit and south-facing panels regional princes; outside, meritorious ministers were painted in order with eulogies. More than forty years since the fall of Chang'an, the old managers and painters are gone. The Hall of Worthies once had many portrait officials, but the posts were abolished after the move to Luoyang. Before rebuilding we ask to fix the number of founding ministers; let the Hanlin Academy prepare portrait drafts and the Directorate of Palace Buildings handle construction in proper sequence. " An edict restored one portrait draftsman and one painter each to the Hall of Worthies and Imperial Calligraphy Academy, and approved the rest of the plan. On dingsi Anzhou reported that on the seventh night of the month commissioner Fu Yanchao was killed by his officer Wang Xiquan; the court suspended audiences for one day. On wuwu former Zhenwu commissioner and Protector-General Gao Xingzhou became Zhangwu commissioner. On xinyou former Yanzhou envoy Fan Zheng became director of the Court of the Imperial Treasury. On bingyin he visited Zhide Palace. At Xingjiao Gate a kite fell from the sky and dropped dead before the imperial carriage. That day fierce winds darkened the sky.
5
二月乙亥，以前鎮州節度使、涇王從敏為宋州節度使。 己卯，以前徐州節度使、檢校太傅李敬周為安州節度使。 是日，宣授鳳翔節度使、潞王從珂為權北京留守； 以北京留守石敬瑭權知鎮州軍州事； 以鎮州範延光權知鄴都留守事； 以前河中節度使、洋王從璋權知鳳翔軍軍府事。 庚寅，幸山陵工作所。 是日，西京留守王思同奏，鳳翔節度使、潞王從珂拒命。 丁酉，王思同加同平章事，充西面行營都部署； 以前邠州節度使藥彥稠為副部署。 以河中節度使安彥威為西面兵馬都監，以前定州節度使李德充為權北京留守。 山陵使奏：「伏睹御劄，皇帝親奉靈駕至園陵。 伏見累朝故事，人君無親送葬之儀，請車駕不行。 」不從。 乙未，樞密使馮贇起復視事，時贇丁母憂也。 己亥，以司農卿張鎛為殿中監。 庚子，殿直楚匡祚上言，監取亳州團練使李重吉至宋州，係於軍院。 重吉，潞王之長子，及幽於宋州，帝猶以金帛賜之，及聞西師鹹叛，方遣使殺之。
On yihai of the second month former Zhenzhou commissioner and Prince of Jing Cong Min became Songzhou commissioner. On jimao former Xuzhou commissioner and honorary grand tutor Li Jingzhou became Anzhou commissioner. That day Fengxiang commissioner and Prince of Lu Cong Ke was named acting Northern Capital regent; Northern Capital regent Shi Jingtang was made acting Zhenzhou commissioner; Fan Yanguang of Zhenzhou became acting regent of Yedu; Former Hezhong commissioner and Prince of Yang Cong Zhang became acting Fengxiang headquarters commissioner. On gengyin he inspected the imperial tomb construction site. That day Western Capital regent Wang Sitong reported that Fengxiang commissioner and Prince of Lu Cong Ke was defying orders. On dingyou Wang Sitong was also named associate grand councilor and overall commander of the western campaign; Former Binzhou commissioner Yao Yanchou became deputy commander. Hezhong commissioner An Yanwei became western horse-and-foot inspector; former Dingzhou commissioner Li Dechong became acting Northern Capital regent. The tomb commissioner reported: "We have seen the emperor's note that he will personally escort the spirit carriage to the burial mound. Past precedent shows sovereigns did not personally attend burials; we ask that the emperor not go. He refused. On yimao military affairs commissioner Feng Yun returned from mourning; he was still in mourning for his mother. On jihai granary director Zhang Bo became director of the palace domestic service. On gengzi palace attendant Chu Kuangzuo reported that he had escorted Bo prefecture regiment trainer Li Chongji to Songzhou and detained him in the garrison compound. Chongji was the Prince of Lu's eldest son. Though imprisoned at Songzhou, the emperor still sent him gifts—until he learned the western army had rebelled, when he sent agents to kill him.
6
三月甲辰，以前太僕少卿魏仁鍔為太僕卿。 興元節度使張虔釗奏，會合討鳳翔。 丙午，以右領衛上將軍武延翰為郢州刺史。 丁未，洋州孫漢韶奏，至興元與張虔釗同議進軍。 己酉，以鎮州節度使範延光依前檢校太師、兼侍中，行興唐尹，充天雄軍節度使、北面水陸轉運製置使； 以北京留守、河東節度使石敬瑭依前檢校太尉、兼中書令，其真定尹、充鎮州節度使、大同彰國振武威塞等軍蕃漢馬步總管如故。 辛亥，以前定州節度使李德充為北京留守，充河東節度使。 許王從益加檢校太保，前河中節度使、洋王從璋加檢校太傅。 詔：「藩侯帶平章事以上薨，許立神道碑，差官撰文。 未帶平章事及刺史，準令式合立碑者，其文任自製撰，不在奏聞。 」乙卯，興元張虔釗奏，自鎮將兵赴鳳翔，收大散關。 宗正寺奏：「準故事，諸陵有令、丞各一員，近例更委本縣令兼之。 緣河南洛陽是京邑，兼令、丞不便。 」詔特置陵台令、丞各一員。 己未，以前金吾大將軍李肅為左衛上將軍，充山陵修奉上下宮都部署。
On jiachen of the third month former stud junior director Wei Ren'e became stud director. Xingyuan commissioner Zhang Qianzhao reported that allied forces were converging to attack Fengxiang. On bingwu Right Lead Guard senior general Wu Yanhan became prefect of E. On dingwei Sun Hanzhao of Yangzhou reported reaching Xingyuan to plan the advance with Zhang Qianzhao. On jiyou Fan Yanguang of Zhenzhou kept honorary grand preceptor and palace attendant, became acting Xing Tang prefect, and was named Tianxiong commissioner and northern land-and-water transport commissioner; Northern Capital regent and Hedong commissioner Shi Jingtang kept honorary grand commandant and director of the Secretariat, continuing as Zhending prefect, Zhenzhou commissioner, and overall commander of Datong, Zhangguo, Zhenwu, Weisai, and allied forces. On xinhai former Dingzhou commissioner Li Dechong became Northern Capital regent and Hedong commissioner. Prince of Xu Cong Yi was also named honorary grand guardian; former Hezhong commissioner and Prince of Yang Cong Zhang was also named honorary grand tutor. An edict said: "When a frontier lord of associate grand councilor rank or higher dies, a spirit-way stele may be erected and an official appointed to write the inscription. Those without associate grand councilor rank or a prefecture who were nonetheless entitled to a stele under statute could compose the inscription themselves without submitting it for approval. " On yimao Zhang Qianzhao of Xingyuan reported leading troops from his post to Fengxiang to retake Dasan Pass. The Court of the Imperial Clan reported: "By precedent each imperial tomb had one director and one aide; recently the local county prefect had been assigned both posts. Because Henan and Luoyang formed the capital region, holding both posts concurrently was impractical. " An edict then created dedicated Tomb Platform director and aide posts, one of each. On jiwei former Golden Guard senior general Li Su became Left Guard senior general and overall director of upper and lower palace tomb works.
7
庚申，西面步軍都監王景從等自軍前至，奏：「今月十五日，大軍進攻鳳翔。 十六日，嚴衛右廂都指揮使尹暉引軍東面入城，右羽林都指揮使楊思權引軍西面入城，山南軍潰。 」帝聞之，謂康義誠等曰：「朕幼年嗣位，委政大臣，兄弟之間，必無榛梗。 諸公大計見告，朕獨難違，事至於此，何方轉禍？ 朕當與左右自往鳳翔，迎兄主社稷； 朕自歸藩，於理為便。 」朱宏昭、馮贇不對，義誠曰：「西師驚潰，蓋由主將失策。 今駕下兵甲尚多，臣請自往關西，振其兵威，扼其衝要。 」義誠又累奏請行，帝召侍衛都將以下宣曰：「先皇帝棄萬國，朕於兄弟之中，無心爭立，一旦被召主喪，便委社稷，岐陽兄長，果致猜嫌。 卿等頃從先朝千征萬戰，今日之事，寧不痛心！ 今據府庫，悉以頒賜，卿等勉之！ 」乃出銀絹錢厚賜於諸軍。 是時方事山陵，復有此賜，府藏為之一空，軍士猶負賞物揚言於路曰：「到鳳翔更請一分。 」其驕誕無畏如是。 辛酉，幸左藏庫，視給將士金帛。 是日，誅馬軍都指揮使朱洪實，坐與康義誠忿爭故也。
On gengshen Wang Jingcong, Western Infantry overall supervisor, and others arrived from the front and reported: "On the fifteenth the main army attacked Fengxiang. On the sixteenth Yanwei Right Wing commander Yin Hui entered from the east and Right Imperial Guard commander Yang Siquan from the west; the Shannan army broke and fled. " When the emperor heard this he told Kang Yicheng and the others: "I took the throne young and left affairs to my ministers; between brothers there should be no thorns in the path. You have told me your plan, and I alone find it hard to refuse. Now that things have come to this, how can we turn disaster aside? I shall go myself with my attendants to Fengxiang to welcome my elder brother to rule the realm; and I shall return to my fief, which would be the proper course. " Zhu Hongzhao and Feng Yun said nothing. Yicheng replied: "The western army panicked and broke because the commander blundered. Your Majesty still has many troops at hand. I ask to go west myself, restore their fighting spirit, and hold the key passes. " Yicheng memorialized again and again to go. The emperor summoned the palace guard generals and declared: "When the late emperor died I had no wish among my brothers to seize the throne; once summoned to lead the mourning I took the realm, and my elder brother at Qiyang has indeed grown suspicious. You followed the late emperor through countless campaigns—how can today's events not break your hearts! I shall open the treasury and give you everything—do your best! " He then distributed silver, silk, and cash in generous gifts to the armies. Tomb works were already draining the treasury, and this largesse emptied it completely; soldiers still carrying their rewards shouted in the streets: "When we reach Fengxiang we'll demand another share. " Such was their arrogance and brazen insolence. On xinyou the emperor visited the Left Treasury to oversee gifts of gold and silk to officers and soldiers. That day Horse Army commander Zhu Hongshi was executed for quarreling with Kang Yicheng.
8
癸亥，以康義誠為鳳翔行營都招討使，餘如故。 以王思同為副招討使； 以安從進為順化軍節度使，充侍衛馬軍都指揮使。 詔左右羽林軍四十指揮改為嚴衛，左右龍武、神武軍改為捧聖。 甲子，陝州奏，潞王至潼關，害西面都部署王思同。 乙亥，宣諭西面行營將士，俟平鳳翔日，人賞二百千，府庫不足，以宮闈服玩增給。 詔侍衛馬軍都指揮使安從進京城巡檢。 是日，從進已得潞王書檄，潛布腹心矣。 丁卯，潞王至陝州。 戊辰，帝急召孟漢瓊，不至； 召朱宏昭，宏昭懼，投於井。 安從進尋殺馮贇於其第。 是夜，帝以百騎出元武門，謂控鶴指揮使慕容遷曰：「爾誠有馬，控鶴從予。 」及駕出，即闔門不行。 遷乃帝素親信者也，臨危如是，人皆惡之。
On guihai Kang Yicheng became overall commander of the Fengxiang field headquarters; his other posts were unchanged. Wang Sitong was named deputy commander; An Congjin became Shunhua army commissioner and palace guard Horse Army commander. An edict renamed the forty Left and Right Imperial Guard commands as Yanwei and the Left and Right Dragon Martial and Divine Martial armies as Paladin-Sacred. On jiazi Shanzhou reported that the Prince of Lu had reached Tong Pass and killed Western overall director Wang Sitong. On yihai the western field army was promised two hundred strings per man once Fengxiang fell; if the treasury ran short, palace garments and curios would make up the difference. An edict ordered palace guard Horse Army commander An Congjin to patrol the capital. That same day Congjin had already received the Prince of Lu's proclamation and quietly posted his own agents throughout the city. On dingmao the Prince of Lu reached Shanzhou. On wuchen the emperor urgently summoned Meng Hanqiong, who did not appear; Zhu Hongzhao was summoned; terrified, he threw himself into a well. An Congjin soon killed Feng Yun at his home. That night the emperor rode out Xuanyuan Gate with a hundred horsemen and told Crane-Control commander Murong Qian: "If you truly have horses, bring Crane-Control and follow me. " Once the emperor was out, the gate was shut and they did not follow. Qian had long been the emperor's trusted favorite; to abandon him at the crisis earned universal contempt.
9
是月二十九日夜，帝至衛州東七八里，遇騎從自東來不避，左右叱之，乃曰：「鎮州節度使石敬瑭也。 」帝喜，敬瑭拜舞於路，帝下馬慟哭，諭以「潞王危社稷，康義誠以下叛我，無以自庇，長公主見教，逆爾於路，謀社稷大計。 」敬瑭曰：「衛州王宏贄宿舊諳事，且就宏贄圖之。 」敬瑭即馳騎而前，見宏贄曰：「主上播遷，至此危迫，吾戚屬也，何以圖全？ 」宏贄曰：「天子避狄，古亦有之，然於奔迫之中，亦有將相、國寶、法物，所以軍長瞻奉，不覺其亡也。 今宰職近臣從乎？ 寶玉、法物從乎？ 」詢之無有。 宏贄曰：「大樹將顛，非一繩所維。 今以五十騎奔竄，無將相一人擁從，安能興復大計！ 所謂蛟龍失雲雨者也。 今六軍將士總在潞邸矣，公縱以戚藩念舊，無奈之何！ 」遂與宏贄同謁於驛亭，宣坐謀之。 敬瑭以宏贄所陳以聞，弓箭庫使沙守榮、奔洪進前謂敬瑭曰：「主上即明宗愛子，公即明宗愛婿，富貴既同受，休戚合共之。 今謀於戚藩，欲期安復，翻索從臣、國寶，欲以此為辭，為賊算天子耶！ 」乃抽佩刀刺敬瑭，敬瑭親將陳暉捍之，守榮與暉單戰而死，洪進亦自刎。 是日，敬瑭盡誅帝之從騎五十餘輩，獨留帝於驛，乃馳騎趨洛。
On the night of the twenty-ninth the emperor reached a spot seven or eight li east of Weizhou. Horsemen approached from the east without yielding; when his attendants shouted, they answered: "It is Zhenzhou commissioner Shi Jingtang. " The emperor rejoiced. Jingtang bowed on the road; the emperor dismounted and wept, telling him: "The Prince of Lu threatens the realm; Kang Yicheng and the rest have turned against me and I have nowhere to hide. The Eldest Princess urged me to meet you here and plan for the state. " Jingtang said: "Wang Hongzan of Weizhou is an old friend who knows how things are done—let us consult him. " Jingtang galloped ahead and said to Hongzan: "The sovereign is in flight and in grave danger. I am his kinsman by marriage—how can we keep him safe? " Hongzan replied: "Emperors have fled invaders before, but even in flight they still had ministers, regalia, and ritual objects so commanders could see and serve them and not know the throne was lost. Do chief ministers and close attendants follow him now? Do jade treasures and ritual regalia follow? " There were none. Hongzan said: "When a great tree is falling, one rope cannot hold it up. He flees with fifty horsemen and not a single minister or general—how can he hope to restore the throne! This is a dragon without cloud and rain. " The armies are all with the Prince of Lu now; however much kinship moves you, nothing can be done! " They then went with Hongzan to the relay station, where the emperor was seated to confer. Jingtang relayed Hongzan's words. Bow-and-Arrow Store envoy Sha Shourong and Ben Hongjin stepped forward and told Jingtang: "The sovereign is Mingzong's beloved son and you his beloved son-in-law; you shared his favor and should share his fate. You seek restoration through a kinsman's domain, yet he demands ministers and regalia as excuses—is this bargaining the emperor away to the rebels! " They drew their swords and stabbed at Jingtang. His personal officer Chen Hui defended him; Shourong and Hui fought alone and died, and Hongjin cut his own throat. That day Jingtang killed all fifty-odd of the emperor's escort, left the emperor alone at the relay station, and galloped toward Luoyang.
10
史臣曰：閔帝爰自衝年，素有令問，及征從代邸，入踐堯階，屬軒皇之弓劍初遺，吳王之几杖未賜，遽生猜間，遂至奔亡。 蓋輔臣無安國之謀，非少主有不君之咎。 以至越在草莽，失守宗祧，斯蓋天命之難忱，土德之將謝故也。
The historian writes: Emperor Min had been admired from youth; summoned from the Dai residence he ascended Yao's throne—yet while the Yellow Emperor's bow and sword were still fresh in hand and the King of Wu's seat and staff not yet granted, suspicion sprang up at once and drove him to flight and ruin. The fault lay with ministers who lacked a plan to secure the realm, not with a young emperor who had done nothing unworthy of the throne. That he ended wandering in the wilds and lost the ancestral shrine shows Heaven's mandate could no longer be trusted and the virtue of Earth was fading away.
