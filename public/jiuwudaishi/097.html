範延光，字子環，鄴郡臨漳人也。 少隸於郡牙，唐明宗牧相州，收為親校。 同光中，明宗下鄆州，梁兵屯楊劉口以扼之，先鋒將康延孝潛使人送款於明宗。 明宗欲使人達機事於莊宗，方難其選，延光請行，遂以蠟書授之。 延光既至，奏莊宗曰：「楊劉渡控扼已定，未可圖也。 請築壘馬家口，以通汶陽之路。 」莊宗從之，復遣歸鄆州。 俄而梁將王彥章攻馬家口所築新壘，明宗恐城中不備，又遣間行告莊宗，請益兵。 中夜至河上，為梁兵所獲，送夷門下獄，榜笞數百，威以白刃，終不泄其事。 復為獄吏所護，在獄半年，不復理問。 及莊宗將至汴城，獄吏即去其桎梏，拜謝而出之，乃見於路側。 莊宗喜，授銀青光祿大夫、檢校工部尚書。 明宗登極，擢為宣徽使。 與霍彥威平青州王公儼，遷檢校司徒。 明宗之幸夷門也，至滎陽，聞朱守殷拒命，延光曰：「若不急攻，賊堅矣。 請騎兵五百，臣先赴之，則人心必駭。 」明宗從其請。 延光自酉時至夜央，馳二百餘里，奄至城下，與賊交鬥。 翌日，守陴者望見乘輿，乃相率開門，延光先入，與賊巷戰，至厚載門，盡殲其黨，明宗喜之。 明年，遷樞密使，權知鎮州軍府事，尋正授節旄，加檢校太保。 長興中，以安重誨得罪，再入為樞密使，加同平章事。 既而以秦王從榮不軌，恐及其禍，屢請外任，明宗久之方許，遂出鎮常山。 清泰中，復詔為樞密使，未幾，出為汴州節度使。 會魏府屯將張令昭逐其帥劉延皓，據城以叛，唐末帝命延光討而平之，遂授鄴都留守，加檢校太師、兼中書令。 門下有術士張生者，自雲妙通術數，當延光微時，言將來必為將相，延光既貴，酷信其言。 歷數鎮，嘗館於上舍，延光謂之曰：「余夢大蛇，自臍入腹，半而掣去之，是何祥也？ 」張生曰：「蛇者龍也，入腹為帝王之兆明矣。 」延光自是稍萌僭竊之意。
Fan Yanguang, whose style name was Zihuan, was a native of Linzhang in Ye Commandery. In his youth he served under the commandery's military commissioner; when Emperor Mingzong of Tang governed Xiangzhou, he was recruited as a personal guard. During the Tongguang era, Mingzong marched on Yanzhou; Liang forces encamped at Yangliukou to block his advance, while the vanguard general Kang Yanxiao secretly sent envoys to pledge allegiance to Mingzong. Mingzong wanted to send someone to relay confidential intelligence to Emperor Zhuangzong and was at a loss for whom to choose; Yanguang offered to go and was given a wax-sealed dispatch. Once Yanguang arrived, he reported to Emperor Zhuangzong: "The Yangliu crossing is already firmly held; it cannot be taken for now. I ask that we build fortifications at Majiakou to open a path to Wenyang. " Zhuangzong assented and sent him back to Yanzhou. Soon afterward the Liang general Wang Yanzhang attacked the new fort built at Majiakou; fearing the city was unprepared, Mingzong again sent Yanguang by a secret route to inform Zhuangzong and ask for more troops. He reached the Yellow River at midnight and was captured by Liang troops; they sent him to prison below Yimen Gate, beat him hundreds of strokes with the cudgel, and menaced him with bare blades, yet he never disclosed the mission. The jailers again shielded him, and he remained in prison half a year without being questioned further. When Zhuangzong was about to reach Bian, the jailers at once removed his shackles, bowed in thanks, and released him; he then presented himself by the roadside. Zhuangzong was delighted and appointed him Silver-Gleaming Grand Master for Splendid Happiness and Acting Minister of Works. When Mingzong ascended the throne, Yanguang was promoted to Palace Attendant. Together with Huo Yanwei he pacified Wang Gongyan of Qingzhou and was promoted to Acting Grand Preceptor. When Mingzong visited Yimen and reached Xingyang, he learned that Zhu Shouyin was defying orders; Yanguang said, "Unless we attack at once, the rebels will dig in. Give me five hundred horsemen and let me go ahead first—the enemy's morale will surely collapse. " Mingzong granted his request. From the you hour until midnight Yanguang galloped more than two hundred li, suddenly appeared below the walls, and fought the rebels hand to hand. The next day the defenders on the walls saw the imperial carriage and opened the gates one after another; Yanguang entered first, fought the rebels street by street to Houzai Gate, and wiped out their band; Mingzong was delighted. The following year he was made Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs and acting administrator of the Zhenzhou military government; soon he received the commandery standard in full and was given the additional title Acting Grand Guardian. During the Changxing era, after An Chonghui fell from grace, Yanguang again became Commissioner of Military Affairs and was made Associate Grand Councilor. Then, when Prince of Qin Congrong turned disloyal, fearing he would be swept up in the disaster, he repeatedly asked for a provincial post; Mingzong only agreed after a long delay, and Yanguang went out to govern Changshan. During the Qingtai era he was again appointed Commissioner of Military Affairs; before long he was sent out as military commissioner of Bianzhou. When the Wei prefecture garrison officer Zhang Lingzhao expelled his commander Liu Yanhao and held the city in revolt, the last emperor of Later Tang ordered Yanguang to suppress him; after victory he was made garrison commander of the Ye capital, with the added titles Acting Grand Preceptor and concurrent Director of the Secretariat. Among his household was a diviner named Zhang who claimed to be skilled in numerology; when Yanguang was still obscure, Zhang said he would surely rise to general or minister, and once Yanguang had grown powerful he trusted those words utterly. As he governed several posts he once lodged Zhang in the main guest quarters; Yanguang told him, "I dreamed a great serpent entered my belly through the navel and was pulled out halfway—what omen is that? " Zhang said, "The serpent is the dragon; entering the belly is plainly a sign that you will become emperor. " From that time Yanguang gradually nursed ambitions of usurpation.

及高祖建義於太原，唐末帝遣延光以本部二萬屯遼州，與趙延壽掎角合勢，及延壽兵敗，延光促還，故心不自安。 高祖入洛，尋封臨清王，以寬其反側。 後延光擅殺齊州防禦使秘瓊，而聚兵部下，復收部內刺史入城，高祖甚疑之，乃東幸夷門。 時延光有牙校孫銳者，與延光有鄉曲之舊，軍機民政，一以委焉。 故魏博六州之賦，無半錢上供，符奏之間，有不如意者，銳即對延光毀之，其兇戾也如此。 初，朝廷遣使封延光為臨清王，因會僚屬，延光暴得疾，伏枕經旬，銳乃密惑群小，召澶州刺史馮暉等，以不臣之謀逼於延光，延光亦惑於術者，因而聽之。 天福二年夏六月，遣銳與暉將步騎二萬，南抵黎陽。 〈 （《通鑒》云：延光以馮暉為都部署，以孫銳為兵馬都監。）〉 時銳以女妓十餘輩從之，擁蓋操扇，必歌吹而後食，將士煩熱，睹之解體，尋為王師所敗，賊眾退還鄴城。 高祖繼遣楊光遠討之，延光知事不濟，乃殺孫銳以歸其罪，發人賫表待罪，且邀姑息，高祖不許。 及經歲受圍，城中饑窘，高祖以師老民勞，思解其役，遣謁者入謂之曰：「卿既危蹙，破在旦夕。 能返掌轉規，改節歸我，我當以大藩處之。 如降而殺之，則何以享國？ 明明白日，可質是言。 」因賜鐵券，改封高平郡王，移鎮太平。 延光謂門人李式曰：「主上敦信明義，言無不踐，許以不死，則不死矣。 」因撤去守備， 〈 （《通鑒》：延光猶遷延未決，宣徽南院使劉處讓復入諭之，延光意乃決。）〉 素服請降。 及赴汶上，逾月入覲。 尋表請罷免，高祖再三答諭方允，制以延光為太子太師致仕。 居闕下期歲，高祖每召賜飲宴，待之與群臣無間。 一日，從容上奏，願就河陽私邸，以便頤養，高祖許之。 延光攜妻子輦奇貨從焉，每過郡邑，多為關吏所糾。 時楊光遠居守洛下，兼領孟、懷，既利其財，復漸測朝廷密旨，遂奏云：「延光國之奸臣，若不羈縻，必北出塞，南入吳，請召令西都居止。 」高祖允之。 光遠使其子承勛以兵環其第，逼令自裁。 延光曰：「明天子在上，賜金書許我不死，爾之父子何得脅制如此？ 」明旦，則以白刃驅之，令上馬之浮橋，排於水中。 光遠紿奏云：「延光投河自溺而死。 」水運軍使曹千獲其屍於郡東繆家灘。 高祖聞之，輟朝二日，詔許歸葬於鄴，仍贈太師。
When Gaozu of Later Jin raised his banner at Taiyuan, the last emperor of Later Tang sent Yanguang with twenty thousand of his own troops to camp at Liaozhou to coordinate with Zhao Yanshou in a pincer; when Yanshou's army was defeated, Yanguang was pressed to withdraw, and he no longer felt secure. After Gaozu entered Luoyang, he soon enfeoffed Yanguang as Prince of Linqing to reassure his wavering loyalty. Later Yanguang killed on his own authority Mi Qiong, defense commissioner of Qi prefecture, gathered troops under his command, and again summoned the prefects within his circuit into the city; Gaozu grew deeply suspicious and traveled east to Yimen. At that time Yanguang had a military aide named Sun Rui, an old acquaintance from his home district; he entrusted all military and civil affairs to him. As a result not half a coin of the taxes from Weibo's six prefectures reached the court; whenever a memorial displeased him, Rui would tear it up in Yanguang's presence—such was his brutish arrogance. When the court first sent envoys to enfeoff Yanguang as Prince of Linqing, he was meeting his staff when he suddenly fell ill and lay abed for ten days; Rui secretly swayed petty followers, summoned Cao prefecture's governor Feng Hui and others, and pressed a treasonous plot on Yanguang, who was also deluded by the diviner and therefore went along. In the sixth month of summer in the second year of Tianfu he sent Rui and Hui south with twenty thousand infantry and cavalry as far as Liyang. ( (The 《Comprehensive Mirror》 states that Yanguang made Feng Hui overall commander and Sun Rui military overseer.) At that time Rui traveled with more than ten female entertainers, borne under parasols while servants fanned him; he would not dine until musicians played; the troops, sweltering and exhausted, lost all fighting spirit at the sight; soon the imperial army defeated them and the rebels fell back to Ye. Gaozu then sent Yang Guangyuan against him; seeing the cause was lost, Yanguang killed Sun Rui to lay the blame on him, dispatched envoys with a memorial submitting to judgment, and also pleaded for mercy; Gaozu refused. After a year under siege the city was starving and desperate; Gaozu, seeing the army worn down and the people exhausted, wished to end the campaign and sent a chamberlain in to say, "You are already cornered; your fall is only a matter of days. If you can turn back, change your allegiance, and submit to me, I will give you a great frontier command. If I killed you after you surrendered, how could I hold the realm in honor? Under the bright sun you may hold me to this word. " He then granted an iron covenant of mercy, changed his title to Prince of Gaoping, and reassigned him to govern Taiping. Yanguang told his retainer Li Shi, "Our lord is steadfast in faith and righteous in deed; he never breaks his word; if he promises you will not die, you will not die. " He then ordered the defenses dismantled, ( (The 《Comprehensive Mirror》 records that Yanguang still hesitated; Commissioner Liu Churang of the Southern Bureau entered again to persuade him, and only then did Yanguang make up his mind.) He put on plain robes and asked to surrender. After he went to Wenshang, more than a month passed before he presented himself at court. Soon he submitted a memorial asking to be relieved; only after Gaozu repeatedly reassured him did he agree, and by edict Yanguang was made Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent and retired from office. He lived at the capital nearly a year; Gaozu often summoned him to feasts and treated him no differently from the other ministers. One day he calmly reported that he wished to retire to his private house at Heyang for his health; Gaozu agreed. Yanguang took his wife and children and carts laden with rare goods; whenever he passed a commandery or county the barrier officers often detained him. At that time Yang Guangyuan was holding Luoyang and also governed Meng and Huai; coveting Yanguang's wealth and gradually sensing the court's secret intent, he memorialized, "Yanguang is a traitor to the state; unless he is restrained he will flee north beyond the frontier or south into Wu; I ask that he be summoned to reside in the western capital. " Gaozu agreed. Guangyuan had his son Chengxun surround the residence with troops and pressed him to kill himself. Yanguang said, "The enlightened Son of Heaven sits above and granted me a golden writ promising I would not die—how dare you father and son coerce me like this? " At dawn they drove him at sword point onto the pontoon bridge and pushed him into the river. Guangyuan falsely reported, "Yanguang threw himself into the river and drowned. " The water-transport army commissioner Cao Qian recovered his body at Miaojia Beach east of the prefecture. When Gaozu heard of it he suspended court for two days, permitted burial at Ye, and still posthumously granted him Grand Preceptor.

延光初為近臣，及領重鎮，禮賢接士，動皆由禮，故甚獲當時之譽。 洎鎮常山日，以部將梁漢塘獲王都名馬，入罪而取之； 在魏州日，以齊州防禦使秘瓊獲董溫琪珠金妓妾，及經其境，復害而奪之。 物議由是減之。 及懼罪以謀叛，復忍恥以偷生，不能引決，遂至強死，何非夫之甚也！
When he was first a close minister and later held a major command, he honored the worthy and received scholars with courtesy; his conduct was always proper, and he won great esteem in his day. When he governed Changshan, his subordinate Liang Hantang had obtained Wang Du's famous horses; Yanguang charged him with a crime and took the horses for himself; While at Weizhou, when Qi prefecture's defense commissioner Mi Qiong had obtained Dong Wenqi's pearls, gold, and courtesans, Yanguang killed him when he passed through the territory and seized everything. Public esteem for him fell accordingly. When he feared punishment and rebelled, then endured disgrace to cling to life, unable to take his own life, and was killed by force—how utterly he failed the measure of a man!

張從賓，未詳何許人也。 始事唐莊宗為小校，從戰有功。 唐天成中，自捧聖指揮使領澄州刺史，遷左右羽林都校。 從藥彥稠討楊彥溫於河中，平之。 長興中，領壽州忠正軍節度使，加檢校太保、侍衛步軍都指揮使。 從賓素便佞，每進言，明宗多納之。 有供奉官丁延徽者，性貪狡，時奉詔監廩，以犯贓下獄，權貴多為救解，明宗怒，不許。 從賓因奏他事，言及延徽，明宗曰：「非但爾言，蘇秦說予，亦不得也。 」延徽竟就戮。 長興末，從賓出鎮靈武，加檢校太傅。 高祖即位，受代入覲，會駕東幸，留從賓警巡洛下。 一日，逢留司御史於天津橋，從兵百人，不分路而過，排御史於水中，從賓紿奏其酒醉，其兇傲如此。 及範延光據鄴城叛，詔從賓為副部署使，從楊光遠同討延光。 會延光使人誘從賓，從賓時在河陽，乃起兵以應之。 先害皇子重信，及入洛，又害皇子重乂，取內庫金帛以給部伍，因東據汜水關，且欲觀望軍勢。 高祖命杜重威、侯益分兵討之，從賓大敗，乘馬入河，溺水而死焉。
Zhang Congbin—his place of origin is unknown. He first served Emperor Zhuangzong of Tang as a junior officer and distinguished himself in battle. During the Tiancheng era of Tang he rose from commander of the Pingsheng Guard to govern Chen prefecture and was promoted to chief commander of the Left and Right Forest of Feathers Guards. He followed Yao Yanzhou in suppressing Yang Yanwen at Hezhong and pacified the region. During the Changxing era he was military commissioner of Shou prefecture for the Zhongzheng Army, with the added titles Acting Grand Guardian and overall commander of the Palace Guard infantry. Congbin was naturally skilled at flattery; Mingzong often accepted his advice. There was a palace attendant named Ding Yanhui, greedy and cunning; ordered at the time to oversee the granaries, he was imprisoned for embezzlement; many powerful men interceded for him, but Mingzong was angry and refused. While reporting on another matter Congbin mentioned Yanhui; Mingzong said, "Even if not only you spoke—even if Su Qin himself pleaded for him, I would not grant it. " Yanhui was executed in the end. At the end of the Changxing era Congbin was sent out to govern Lingwu and was given the added title Acting Grand Tutor. When Gaozu took the throne, Congbin was relieved and came to court; when the emperor traveled east he was left to patrol Luoyang. One day he met the capital duty censor at Tianjin Bridge with a hundred followers; they refused to yield the road and shoved the censor into the water; Congbin falsely reported that the censor was drunk—such was his brutish arrogance. When Fan Yanguang held Ye in rebellion, Congbin was ordered deputy overall commander to join Yang Guangyuan in suppressing Yanguang. Yanguang sent envoys to win Congbin over; Congbin was then at Heyang and raised troops in support. He first killed Prince Chongxin; after entering Luoyang he also killed Prince Chongyi, seized gold and silk from the inner treasury to pay his troops, then seized Sishui Pass to the east and waited to see how the fighting would go. Gaozu ordered Du Chongwei and Hou Yi to attack him with divided forces; Congbin was routed, rode into the river, and drowned.

楊光遠，小字阿檀，及長，止名檀，唐天成中，以明宗改御名為亶，以偏傍字犯之，始改名光遠，字德明，其先沙陀部人也。 父阿噔啜，後改名瑊，事唐武皇為隊長。 光遠事莊宗為騎將，唐天祐中，莊宗遣振武節度使周德威討劉守光於幽州，因令光遠隸於德威麾下。 後與德威拒契丹於新州，一軍以深入致敗，因傷其臂，遂廢，罷於家。 莊宗即位，思其戰功，命為幽州馬步軍都指揮使、檢校尚書右僕射，戍瓦橋關久之。 明宗朝，歷媯、瀛、易、冀四州刺史。 光遠雖不識字，然有口辯，通於吏理，在郡有政聲，明宗頗重之。 長興中，契丹有中山之敗，生擒其將紮拉等數十人，送於闕下，其後契丹既通和，遣使乞歸之，明宗與大臣謀議，特放還蕃。 一日，召光遠於便殿言其事，光遠曰：「紮拉等北土之善戰者，彼失之如喪手足； 又在此累年，備諳中國事，若放還非便。 」明宗曰：「蕃人重盟誓，既通歡好，必不相負。 」光遠曰：「臣恐後悔不及也。 」明宗遂止，深嘉其抗直。 後自振武節度使移鎮中山，累加檢校太傅，將兵戍蔚州。
Yang Guangyuan's childhood name was Atan; when he grew up he used only Tan; during the Tiancheng era of Tang, when Mingzong changed his imperial name to Dan and the component characters became taboo, he renamed himself Guangyuan, style name Deming; his forebears were Shatuo. His father Adengchuo later renamed himself Jian and served Tang's Martial Emperor as a squad leader. Guangyuan served Zhuangzong as a cavalry officer; in the Tianyou era Zhuangzong sent Zhenwu military commissioner Zhou Dewei against Liu Shouguang at Youzhou and placed Guangyuan under Dewei. Later he and Dewei fought the Khitans at Xinzhou; the army was defeated after advancing too far, Guangyuan's arm was wounded, he was disabled, and sent home. When Zhuangzong took the throne he remembered his service and appointed him overall commander of Youzhou horse and foot and Acting Vice Director of the Right Secretariat, and he garrisoned Waqiao Pass for many years. Under Mingzong he served in turn as governor of Gui, Ying, Yi, and Ji prefectures. Although illiterate, Guangyuan was eloquent and skilled in administration; his prefectures won praise for good government, and Mingzong held him in high regard. During Changxing the Khitans were defeated at Zhongshan; dozens of captives including their general Zhala were sent to court; after peace the Khitans asked for their return; Mingzong consulted his ministers and ordered them released to the steppe. One day Mingzong summoned Guangyuan to the informal hall to discuss it; Guangyuan said, "Zhala and the others are the Khitans' best fighters; to lose them is like losing hands and feet; moreover they have been here for years and know our affairs inside out—it would not be wise to send them back. " Mingzong said, "The Khitans honor their oaths; now that we are at peace they will not betray us. " Guangyuan said, "I fear Your Majesty will regret it when it is too late. " Mingzong held back and deeply praised his blunt honesty. Later he moved from Zhenwu military commissioner to Zhongshan, was repeatedly promoted to Acting Grand Tutor, and led troops garrisoning You prefecture.

高祖舉義於太原，唐末帝遣光遠與張敬達屯兵於城下，俄而契丹大至，為其所敗，圍其寨久之，軍中糧絕，光遠乃與次將安審琦等殺敬達，擁眾歸命。 從高祖入洛，加檢校太尉，充宣武軍節度使、同平章事，判六軍諸衛事。 是時，光遠每對高祖，常悒然不樂，高祖慮有不足，密遣近臣訊之。 光遠附奏曰：「臣貴為將相，非有不足，但以張生鐵死得其所，臣弗如也，衷心內愧，是以不樂。 」生鐵，蓋敬達之小字也。 高祖聞其言，以光遠為忠純之最者也。 其實光遠故為其言，以邀高祖之重信也。 明年，範延光據鄴城叛，高祖命光遠率師討之。 將濟河，會滑州軍亂，時軍眾欲推光遠為主。 光遠曰：「自古有折臂天子乎？ 且天子豈公輩販弄之物？ 晉陽之降，乃勢所窮迫，今若為之，直反賊也。 」由是其下惕然，無復言者。 高祖聞之，尤加寵重。 光遠既圍延光，尋授魏博行府節度使。 兵柄在手，以為高祖懼己，稍干預朝政，或抗有所奏，高祖亦曲從之。 復下詔以其子承祚尚長安公主，次子承信皆授美官。 恩渥殊等，為當時之冠。 桑維翰為樞密使，往往彈射其事，光遠心銜之。 及延光降，光遠入朝，面奏維翰擅權。 高祖以光遠方有功於國，乃出維翰鎮相州，光遠為西京留守，兼鎮河陽，因罷其兵權。 光遠由此怨望，潛貯異志，多以珍玩奉契丹，訴己之屈，又私養部曲千餘人，撓法犯禁，河、洛之人，恒如備盜。 尋冊拜太尉、兼中書令。
When Gaozu raised his banner at Taiyuan, the last emperor of Later Tang sent Guangyuan and Zhang Jingda to camp below the city; the Khitans soon arrived in force, defeated them, and besieged their camp until food ran out; Guangyuan then joined the second-in-command An Shenqi and others in killing Jingda and leading the army to surrender. He followed Gaozu into Luoyang, was made Acting Grand Commandant, military commissioner of the Xuanwu Army and Associate Grand Councilor, and placed in charge of the Six Armies and palace guards. At that time Guangyuan often looked gloomy in Gaozu's presence; Gaozu feared he was dissatisfied and secretly sent a close minister to ask why. Guangyuan added a memorial saying, "I stand honored as general and minister and want for nothing; yet Zhang Raw Iron died as he should, and I am not his equal. I am ashamed at heart, and that is why I am unhappy. " ShengTie — "raw iron" — was Zhang Jingda's childhood name. Gaozu heard this and judged Guangyuan the most loyal and upright of all. In truth Guangyuan had said this on purpose to win Gaozu's deepest trust. The next year Fan Yanguang seized Ye city and rebelled; Gaozu ordered Guangyuan to lead an army against him. As he was about to cross the river, the army at Huazhou mutinied, and the troops wanted to make Guangyuan their leader. Guangyuan said, "Has there ever in history been an emperor with a broken arm? And is the Son of Heaven some trinket for the likes of you to bandy about? Our surrender at Jinyang was forced by desperate circumstances; to do this now would make me nothing but a rebel. " His men were shaken into silence, and no one spoke of it again. When Gaozu heard of this, he favored Guangyuan all the more. After Guangyuan besieged Yanguang, he was soon made provisional military commissioner of Weibo. With the army in his hands, he believed Gaozu feared him; he began meddling in court affairs, and when he stubbornly pressed his views, Gaozu indulged him. An edict further had his son Chengzuo marry Princess Chang'an and gave his second son Chengxin distinguished offices. His favor was unmatched, the greatest of the day. Sang Weihan, as Commissioner of Military Affairs, often attacked him on these matters, and Guangyuan nursed a grudge. When Yanguang surrendered, Guangyuan came to court and accused Weihan face to face of monopolizing power. Because Guangyuan had just rendered great service, Gaozu sent Weihan out to govern Xiangzhou, made Guangyuan defender of the Western Capital and concurrently military commissioner of Heyang, and thereby stripped him of military command. From this Guangyuan turned resentful, secretly harbored other ambitions, often sent precious curios to the Khitans to plead his grievances, and privately kept more than a thousand retainers who flouted the law; the people of the Yellow River region and Luoyang lived in constant fear, as if guarding against bandits. Soon he was invested as Grand Commandant and concurrently Director of the Secretariat.

時範延光致仕，輦囊裝妓妾，居於河陽。 光遠利其奇貨，且慮為子孫之讎，因奏延光不家汴、洛，出舍外藩，非南走淮夷，則北走契丹，宜早除之。 高祖以許之不死，鐵券在焉，持疑未允。 光遠乃遣子承勛以甲士圍其第，逼令自裁。 延光曰：「天子在上，安得如此！ 」乃遣使者乞移居洛下，行及河橋，擯於流而溺殺之，矯奏雲延光自投河，朝廷以適會其意，弗之理。 後逾歲入覲，高祖為置曲宴，教坊伶人以光遠暴斂重賦，因陳戲譏之，光遠殊無慚色。 高祖謂光遠曰：「元城之役，卿左右皆立功，未曾旌賞，今各與一郡，俾厘任以榮之。 」因命為刺史者凡數人。
At that time Fan Yanguang had retired, loading carts with concubines and courtesans, and was living at Heyang. Guangyuan coveted his wealth and also feared he would become an enemy to his descendants; he memorialized that Yanguang, not living in Bian or Luoyang but staying in an outer province, would either flee south to the Huai tribes or north to the Khitans, and should be eliminated at once. Gaozu, having promised that Yanguang would not be put to death and with the iron certificate still in force, hesitated and would not agree. Guangyuan then sent his son Chengxun with armored troops to surround Yanguang's residence and forced him to take his own life. Yanguang said, "The Son of Heaven is above — how dare you do this! " He then sent an envoy begging to move to Luoyang; when he reached the river bridge they shoved him into the current and drowned him, falsely reporting that Yanguang had thrown himself into the river. The court, pleased that this suited their purpose, paid no attention. More than a year later, when he came to court, Gaozu held a banquet with music; entertainers from the Music Office satirized Guangyuan's harsh levies and heavy taxes in a skit, yet Guangyuan showed not the slightest shame. Gaozu said to Guangyuan, "At the Yuancheng campaign your close followers all rendered service yet were never rewarded; now each shall receive a prefecture, that they may take office and be honored. " He then appointed several of them prefect.

時王建立自青州移鎮上黨，乃以光遠為平盧軍節度使，封東平王。 光遠面奏，請與長子同行，尋授承勛萊州防禦使。 及赴任，仆從妓妾至千餘騎，滿盈僭侈，為方嶽之最。 下車之後，惟以刻剝為事。 少帝嗣位，冊拜太師，封壽王。 〈 （《宋史·馬仁鎬傳》：晉天福中，青州楊光遠將圖不軌，以仁鎬為節度副使，伺其動靜。 歷二年，或譖仁鎬於朝，改護國軍行軍司馬。 仁鎬至河中數月，光遠反書聞。）〉 後因景延廣上言，請取光遠麾下所借官馬二百匹，光遠怒曰：「此馬先帝賜我，何以復取？ 是疑我也。 」遂遣人潛召取子承祚自單州奔歸，朝廷乃就除淄州刺史，以從其便。 光遠益驕，因此構契丹，述少帝違好之短，且言大饑之後，國用空虛，此時一舉可以平定。
When Wang Jianli moved from Qingzhou to govern Shangdang, Guangyuan was made military commissioner of the Pinglu Army and enfeoffed as Prince of Dongping. Guangyuan memorialized in person asking to travel with his eldest son; Chengxun was soon made defense commissioner of Laizhou. When he took up his post, retainers and courtesans numbered more than a thousand mounted followers — display so lavish and arrogance so great that no regional lord surpassed him. After he took office, he devoted himself solely to extortion. When the Last Emperor succeeded to the throne, Guangyuan was invested as Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as Prince of Shou. ( (The Song History, biography of Ma Rendegao, records that in the Tianfu era of Later Jin, Yang Guangyuan of Qingzhou was plotting rebellion and Rendegao was made deputy military commissioner to watch his movements. After two years someone slandered Rendegao at court, and he was transferred to army marshal of the Huguo Army. When Rendegao had been at Hezhong several months, word of Guangyuan's rebellion arrived.) Later, when Jing Yanguang proposed reclaiming two hundred government horses lent to Guangyuan's command, Guangyuan angrily said, "These horses the former emperor gave me — why take them back? This means you distrust me. " He then secretly sent men to summon his son Chengzuo back from Shan prefecture; the court thereupon appointed Chengzuo prefect of Zi to suit his convenience. Guangyuan grew all the more arrogant; he therefore incited the Khitans, recounting the Last Emperor's breaches of the alliance and saying that after the great famine the treasury was empty — one stroke now could settle everything.

開運元年正月，契丹南牧，陷我博陵，少帝幸澶淵。 三月，契丹退，命李守貞、符彥卿率師東討。 光遠素無兵眾，惟嬰城自守，守貞以長連城圍之。 冬十一月，承勛與弟承信、承祚見城中人民相食將盡，知事不濟，勸光遠乞降，冀免於赤族。 光遠不納，曰：「我在代北時，嘗以紙錢駝馬祭天池，皆沈沒，人言合有天子分，宜且待時，勿輕言降也。 」承勛慮禍在朝夕，與諸弟同謀，殺節度判官邱濤，親校杜延壽、楊瞻、白延祚等，梟其首，乃遣承祚送於守貞。 因縱火大噪，劫其父幽於私第，以城納款，遣即墨縣令王德柔貢表待罪，光遠亦上章自首。 少帝以頃歲太原歸命，欲曲全之，執政曰：「豈有逆狀滔天而赦之也？ 」乃命守貞便宜處置。 守貞遣人拉殺之， 〈 （《歐陽史》：守貞遣客省副使何延祚殺之於其家。 以病卒聞。）〉 漢高祖即位，詔贈尚書令，追封齊王，仍令立碑。 未幾，其碑石無故自折，可知其陰責也。 〈 （《五代史補》：楊光遠滅範延光之後，朝廷以其功高，授青州節度，封東平王，奄有登、萊、沂、密數郡。 既而自負強盛，舉兵反，朝廷以宋州節度李守貞嘗與光遠有隙，乃命李討之。 李受詔欣然，志在必取，莫不身先矢石。 光遠見而懼之，度不能禦，遂降。 初，光遠反書至，中外大震，時百官起居次，忽有朝士揚言於眾曰：「楊光遠欲謀大事，吾不信也。 光遠素患禿瘡，其妻又跛，自古豈有禿頭天子、跛腳皇后耶？ 」於是人心頓安，未幾，光遠果降。）〉
In the first month of the first year of Kaiyun, the Khitans drove south, seized Boling, and the Last Emperor went to Chanyuan. In the third month, after the Khitans withdrew, he ordered Li Shouzhen and Fu Yanqing to lead an army east against Guangyuan. Guangyuan had never had many troops; he could only hold the city and defend it while Shouzhen besieged him with a long linked encampment. In the eleventh month of winter, Chengxun and his brothers Chengxin and Chengzuo saw the people in the city eating one another and near starvation; knowing the cause was lost, they urged Guangyuan to surrender, hoping to escape the extermination of the whole clan. Guangyuan refused and said, "When I was in Daibei I once sacrificed paper money, camels, and horses at the Heavenly Lake and they all sank; people said I had the portion of a Son of Heaven. I should wait for the right moment and not speak lightly of surrender. " Chengxun feared disaster was imminent; with his brothers he plotted to kill the military commissioner's aide Qiu Tao and the close officers Du Yanshou, Yang Zhan, Bai Yanzuo, and others, displayed their heads, and sent Chengzuo to Shouzhen. They then set fires and raised a great uproar, seized their father and confined him in a private residence, surrendered the city, sent Jimo magistrate Wang Derou with a memorial accepting guilt, and Guangyuan also submitted a confession of his own. The Last Emperor, recalling that Taiyuan had submitted a few years before, wished to spare him; the chief ministers said, "How can treason filling heaven be pardoned? " He then ordered Shouzhen to dispose of the matter as he saw fit. Shouzhen sent men to strangle him to death, ( (The Ouyang History records that Shouzhen sent Deputy Chief of the Reception Bureau He Yanzuo to kill him at his home. It was reported that he had died of illness.) When Gaozu of Han took the throne, an edict posthumously made Guangyuan Director of the Secretariat, enfeoffed him as Prince of Qi, and ordered a stele erected. Before long the stele stone broke in two for no apparent reason — a sign of hidden guilt. ( (The Supplement to the History of the Five Dynasties records that after Yang Guangyuan destroyed Fan Yanguang, the court because of his great merit made him military commissioner of Qingzhou and Prince of Dongping, holding Deng, Lai, Yi, and Mi prefectures. Having grown proud of his strength, he raised troops in rebellion; the court, because Songzhou military commissioner Li Shouzhen had once been at odds with Guangyuan, ordered Li to suppress him. Li received the order with delight, determined to take him, and all led from the front under arrow and stone. Guangyuan saw this and was afraid; judging he could not resist, he surrendered. At first, when Guangyuan's letter of rebellion arrived, court and countryside were greatly shaken; during the officials' audience, suddenly a court gentleman proclaimed to the crowd, "Yang Guangyuan intends a great enterprise — I do not believe it. Guangyuan has always suffered from bald sores, and his wife is lame — since antiquity has there ever been a bald emperor or a lame empress? " Hearts were instantly calmed; before long Guangyuan indeed surrendered.)

承勛，光遠之長子也。 始名承貴，避少帝名改焉。 以父蔭歷光、濮州刺史，光遠兼鎮河陽，命制置三城事。 光遠移鎮青州，授萊州防禦使。 在郡亦頗理，嘗憤父側之奸黨，欲殺之，每省父，父為匿焉。 及光遠構釁，嬰城以叛，承勛赴之，敵退，為王師所圍。 逾歲糧盡，與其弟承祚背父之命，出降王師，朝廷授汝州防禦使，尋改鄭州。 〈 （《宋史·楊承信傳》：光遠死，承信與弟承祚詣闕請死。 詔釋之，以承信為右羽林將軍，承祚為右驍衛將軍，放歸，服喪私第，尋安置鄭州。）〉 及契丹入汴，遣騎士自圃田召至，責其害父背己，使臠其肉而殺之。 以其弟承信為青州節度使。
Chengxun was Guangyuan's eldest son. Originally named Chenggui; the name was changed to avoid the Last Emperor's personal name. Through his father's privilege he served as prefect of Guang and Pu; when Guangyuan concurrently governed Heyang, he was ordered to manage affairs in the three cities. When Guangyuan moved to Qingzhou, Chengxun was made defense commissioner of Laizhou. In the prefecture he also governed fairly well; he once raged at the scheming faction around his father and wished to kill them, but whenever he visited his father, his father hid them. When Guangyuan incited trouble and held the city in rebellion, Chengxun went to join him; when the enemy withdrew, they were besieged by the imperial army. After more than a year, when provisions were exhausted, he and his brother Chengzuo defied their father's orders and surrendered to the imperial army; the court made him defense commissioner of Ru and soon transferred him to Zheng. ( (The Song History, biography of Yang Chengxin, records that after Guangyuan died, Chengxin and his brother Chengzuo came to court begging to be put to death. An edict released them; Chengxin was made General of the Right Feathered Forest and Chengzuo General of the Right Valiant Cavalry; they were sent home to mourn at their private residence and were soon settled at Zheng prefecture.) When the Khitans entered Bian, they sent horsemen from Putian to summon him, blamed him for killing his father and betraying them, had his flesh sliced off, and killed him. His brother Chengxin was made military commissioner of Qingzhou.

盧文進，字國用，范陽人也。 身長七尺，飲啖過人，望之偉如也。 少事劉守光為騎將，唐莊宗攻燕，以文進首降，遙授壽州刺史。 初，莊宗得山後八軍，以愛弟存矩為新州圍練使以總領之。 莊宗與劉鄩對壘於莘縣，命存矩於山後召募勁兵，又命山北居民出戰馬器仗，每鬻牛十頭易馬一匹，人心怨咨。 時存矩團結五百騎，令文進將之，與存矩俱行。 至祁溝關，軍士聚謀曰：「我輩邊人，棄父母妻子，為他血戰，千里送死，固不能也。 」眾曰：「擁盧將軍卻還新州，據城自守，奈我何！ 」因大呼揮戈，趣傳舍，害存矩於榻下。 文進撫膺曰：「奴輩累我矣。 」因環屍而泣曰：「此輩既害郎君，我何面目見王！」 〈 （案《遼史》：存矩取文進女為側室，文進心常內愧，因與亂軍殺存矩。）〉 與薛史異因為亂軍所擁。 反攻新州，不克； 〈 （馬令《南唐書》：文進攻新州，不克，夜走墜塹，一躍而出，明日視之，乃郡之黑龍潭也，絕岸數丈，深不可測。 又嘗有大蛇，徑至座間，引首及膝，文進取食飼之而去。 由是自負。）〉 又攻武州，又不利。 周德威命將追討，文進遂奔契丹，命為幽州兵馬留後，部分漢軍，常別為營寨。 未幾，文進引契丹寇新州。 自是北師數至，驅擄數州士女，教其織纴工作，中國所為者悉備，契丹所以強盛者，得文進之故也。 〈 （案《遼史》云：文進引契丹軍攻新州，刺史安金全不能守，棄城去。 周德威援之，進攻新州，契丹眾數萬，德威不勝，大敗奔歸。 文進與契丹攻幽州，且二百日，城中圍困，晉王親將兵救之，方始解去。 契丹以文進為幽州節度使，又以為盧龍節度使。）〉 同光之世，為患尤深。 文進在平州，率奚族勁騎，鳥擊獸搏，倏來忽往，燕、趙諸州，荊榛滿目。 軍屯涿州，每歲運糧，自瓦橋至幽州，勁兵猛將，援遞糧車，然猶為契丹所鈔，奔命不暇，皆文進導之也。
Lu Wenjin, whose style name was Guoyong, was a native of Fanyang. Seven feet tall, he ate and drank more than other men; seen from afar he looked imposing. In youth he served Liu Shouguang as a cavalry commander; when Tang's Emperor Zhuangzong attacked Yan, Wenjin was the first to surrender and was appointed from afar as prefect of Shou. At first Zhuangzong obtained the eight armies behind the mountains and made his beloved younger brother Cunju regimental commissioner of Xin to command them overall. Zhuangzong faced Liu Yan in camp at Shen county and ordered Cunju to recruit elite troops behind the mountains; he also ordered the northern mountain residents to supply war horses and weapons — ten oxen for one horse — and resentment spread among the people. At that time Cunju assembled five hundred horsemen, had Wenjin lead them, and traveled together with Cunju. Reaching Qigou Pass, the soldiers gathered and plotted, "We frontier men, abandoning parents, wives, and children to spill blood fighting for others — death sent a thousand li away — we truly cannot do it. " The crowd said, "Let us make General Lu turn back to Xin, hold the city, and defend ourselves — what can they do to us! " They then shouted, waved weapons, rushed to the relay station, and killed Cunju beneath his couch. Wenjin clutched his chest and said, "You slaves have ruined me. " He then circled the corpse weeping and said, "Since you wretches have killed the young lord, with what face can I see the prince!" ( (According to the Liao History, Cunju took Wenjin's daughter as a concubine; Wenjin was always inwardly ashamed, and together with the mutinous troops killed Cunju.) This differs from Xue's History — he was therefore taken up by the mutinous army. He counterattacked Xin but could not take it; ( (Ma Ling's History of Southern Tang records that Wenjin attacked Xin but failed; fleeing by night he fell into a moat and leaped out in one bound; the next day he looked and saw it was the prefecture's Black Dragon Pool — cliffs several zhang high and depths unfathomable. Again, once a great serpent came straight to his seat and raised its head to his knee; Wenjin took food, fed it, and it departed. From this he grew self-confident.) He again attacked Wu prefecture and again failed. Zhou Dewei ordered generals to pursue him; Wenjin then fled to the Khitans, was appointed rear commander of Youzhou's horse and troops, organized the Han armies, and always camped separately. Before long Wenjin led Khitans to raid Xin. From then on northern armies came repeatedly; they drove off and captured men and women of several prefectures and taught them weaving and crafts — everything done in China was fully available. That the Khitans grew strong was owing to obtaining Wenjin. ( (According to the 《Liao History》, Wenjin led Khitan armies against Xin; Prefect An Jinquan could not hold the city and fled. Zhou Dewei marched to relieve the city and attacked Xin, but Khitan forces numbered in the tens of thousands; Dewei was routed and fled back in defeat. Wenjin and the Khitans besieged Youzhou for nearly two hundred days until the Prince of Jin led an army in person to raise the siege. The Khitans appointed Wenjin military commissioner of Youzhou and also of Lulong.) In the Tongguang era the damage he did was especially grave. From Pingzhou Wenjin led fierce Xi horsemen who struck like hunting birds and beasts — appearing and vanishing in an instant — until thorns and ruin covered the lands of Yan and Zhao. Troops were stationed at Zhuo, and every year grain was hauled from Waqiao to Youzhou under escort of elite soldiers and fierce generals — yet the Khitans still cut off the convoys, and men raced to and fro without rest; Wenjin guided it all.

及明宗即位之明年，文進自平州率所部十餘萬眾來奔。 行及幽州，先遣使上表曰：「頃以新州團練使李存矩，提衡郡邑，掌握恩威，虐黎庶則毒甚於豺狼，聚賦斂則貪盈於溝壑，人不堪命，士各離心，臣即拋父母之邦，入朔漠之地。 幾年雁塞，徒向日以傾心； 一望家山，每銷魂而斷目。 李子卿之河畔，空有怨辭； 石季倫之樂中，莫陳歸引。 近聞皇帝陛下，皇天眷命，清明在躬，握紀乘乾，鼎新革故，始知大幸，有路朝宗，便貯歸心，祗伺良會。 臣十月十日，決計殺在城契丹，取十一日離州，押七八千車乘，領十五萬生靈，十四日已達幽州」云。 洎至洛陽，明宗寵待彌厚，授滑州節度使、檢校太尉。 歲餘，移鎮鄧州，累加同平章事，入為上將軍。 長興中，復出鎮潞州，擒奸恤隱，甚獲當時之譽。 清泰中，改安州節度使。 及高祖即位，與契丹敦好，文進以嘗背契丹，居不自安。 〈 （馬令《南唐書》：文進居數鎮，頗有善政，兵民愛之。 其將行也，從數騎至營中，別其裨將李藏機，告以避契丹之意，將士皆拜為訣。）〉 天福元年十二月，乃殺行軍司馬馮知兆、節度副使杜重貴等，率其部眾渡淮奔於金陵。 李棨待之尤重， 〈 （馬令《南唐書》云：烈祖以文進為天雄統軍。）〉 偽命為宣州節度使，後卒於江南。 〈 （《金陵志》：文進自潤州召還，以左衛上將軍、兼中書令、范陽郡王奉朝請。）〉
The year after Mingzong took the throne, Wenjin led more than a hundred thousand followers from Pingzhou to defect. When he reached Youzhou, he first sent an envoy with a memorial: "Of late the regimental commissioner of Xin, Li Cunju, held the scales of the commandery and wielded both favor and punishment; in oppressing the people he was more poisonous than wolves, and in gathering taxes his greed filled every ditch; the people could not bear their lot and the officers lost heart one by one — so your subject cast aside the land of his parents and entered the northern desert. Years at the wild-goose frontier, turning only toward the sun to pour out my heart in vain; One glimpse of home mountains — each time my soul withers and my sight fails. Like Li Ziqing on the riverbank, I had only empty words of grievance; like Shi Jilun amid his music, I could not even voice the plea to return home. Lately I have heard that Your Majesty, Heaven's chosen, clear in virtue and bright in person, has grasped the age and mounted the sovereign hexagram, renewing all things and casting off the old — and I know my great fortune: there is a road back to the capital; I have kept my heart of return and reverently await the right moment. On the tenth day of the tenth month your subject resolved to kill the Khitans in the city; on the eleventh I left the prefecture, escorting seven or eight thousand carts and leading a hundred and fifty thousand people; on the fourteenth I had already reached Youzhou." — so the memorial ran. When he reached Luoyang, Mingzong treated him with ever greater favor and made him military commissioner of Hua and honorary Grand Commandant. After more than a year he was transferred to Deng, promoted step by step to Co-Director of the Department of State Affairs, and entered court as Upper General. During Changxing he again took up the garrison at Lu, rooting out wrongdoers and showing mercy to the oppressed, and won great praise at the time. During Qingtai he was reassigned as military commissioner of An. When Gaozu took the throne and renewed friendly ties with the Khitans, Wenjin — who had once betrayed them — could not feel secure in his post. ( (Ma Ling's 《History of Southern Tang》 records that Wenjin governed several garrisons with notable benevolence, and soldiers and civilians alike loved him. When he was about to leave, he rode with a few followers into camp to bid farewell to his lieutenant Li Cangji and explain that he meant to escape the Khitans; officers and soldiers all bowed in parting.) In the twelfth month of Tianfu 1 he killed the marching army marshal Feng Zhizhao, deputy military commissioner Du Chonggui, and others, then led his troops across the Huai and fled to Jinling. Li Qi treated him with especial honor, ( (Ma Ling's 《History of Southern Tang》 states that Liezu appointed Wenjin commander of Tianxiong.) The Southern regime appointed him military commissioner of Xuan, and he later died in Jiangnan. ( (The 《Jinling Gazetteer》 records that Wenjin was recalled from Runzhou and made Left Guard Upper General, concurrently Director of the Chancellery, and Prince of Fanyang attending court.)
13
李金全，本唐明宗之小豎也。 其先出於吐谷渾。 金全驍勇，善騎射，少從明宗征伐，以力戰有功，明宗即位，連典大郡。 天成中，授涇州節度使，在鎮數年，以掊斂為務。 長興中，受代歸闕，始進馬數十匹，不數日又進之。 明宗召而謂之曰：「卿患馬多耶，何進貢之數也？ 」又謂曰：「卿在涇州日，為理如何，無乃以馬為事否？ 」金全慚謝而退。 四年夏，授滄州節度使，累官至檢校太傅。 清泰中，罷鎮歸闕，久留於京師。 高祖即位之明年，安州屯將王暉殺節度使周瑰，詔遣金全以騎兵千人鎮撫其地。 未及境，而暉為部下所殺。 金全至，亂軍數百人皆不安，金全說遣赴闕，密伏兵於野，盡殺之，又擒其軍校武彥和等數十人，斬之。 初，金全之將行也，高祖戒之曰：「王暉之亂，罪莫大焉，但慮封守不寧，則民受其弊。 」因折矢飛詔，約以不戮一人，仍許以暉為唐州刺史。 又謂金全曰：「卿之此行，無失吾信。 」及金全至，聞彥和等當為亂之日，劫掠郡城，所獲財貨，悉在其第，遂殺而奪之。 〈 （《通鑒》：彥和且死，呼曰：「王暉首惡，天子猶赦之； 我輩脅從，何罪乎！」）〉 高祖聞之，以姑息金全故，不究其事，尋授以旄節。
Li Jinquan had originally been a young attendant in Mingzong of Tang's household. His family traced its origins to Tuyuhun. Jinquan was fierce and brave and skilled at riding and archery; from youth he followed Mingzong on campaign and won distinction in hard fighting; when Mingzong took the throne, he was put in charge of one great commandery after another. During Tiancheng he was appointed military commissioner of Jing, and for several years in his garrison he made extortion his chief occupation. During Changxing, after being replaced and recalled to court, he first presented several dozen horses, and within a few days presented again. Mingzong summoned him and said, "Do you have too many horses, that you keep sending them as tribute? " He added, "When you were at Jing, how did you govern — was it not chiefly by trading in horses? " Jinquan withdrew in shame and apology. In the summer of the fourth year he was appointed military commissioner of Cang, and by successive promotions reached Honorary Grand Mentor. During Qingtai he was removed from his garrison and recalled to court, where he remained for a long time. The year after Gaozu took the throne, Wang Hui, a garrison commander at An, killed Military Commissioner Zhou Gui; an edict sent Jinquan with a thousand cavalry to pacify the region. Before Jinquan reached the border, Hui was killed by his own men. When Jinquan arrived, several hundred mutinous troops were all on edge; he persuaded them to go to court, then secretly set an ambush in the open country and killed them all; he also captured several dozen of their officers, including Wu Yanhe, and beheaded them. When Jinquan was about to set out, Gaozu warned him: "Wang Hui's rebellion was a crime without equal, yet if the border garrison is unsettled, the people will suffer for it. " He then broke an arrow and sent a flying edict, pledging that not a single man would be killed, and even promised to appoint Hui prefect of Tang. He also told Jinquan, "On this mission, do not betray my word. " When Jinquan arrived, he learned that on the day of the mutiny Yanhe and the others had plundered the commandery city and that the loot was all in their houses; he killed them and seized it. ( (The 《Comprehensive Mirror》 records that as Yanhe was about to die, he cried out, "Wang Hui was the chief villain, and the Son of Heaven still pardoned him; we were compelled to follow — what crime is ours!") When Gaozu heard of it, he indulged Jinquan and did not pursue the matter; soon he invested him with the staff and banner of command.
14
金全有親吏胡漢筠者，勇譎嗇褊，貪詐殘忍，軍府之政，一以委之。 高祖聞其事，遣吏賈仁紹往代其職，且召漢筠。 漢筠內疚惶怖，金全乃列狀稱疾以聞。 及仁紹至，漢筠鴆而殺之。 〈 （馬令《南唐書》：胡漢榮所為多不法，晉高祖患之，不欲因漢榮以累功臣，為選廉吏賈仁沼代之，且召漢榮。 漢榮教金全留己而不遣。 金全客龐令圖諫曰：「仁沼昔事王晏球，有大功，晏球欲厚賞之，仁沼退而不言，此天下之忠臣也。 及頒賜所俘物，仁沼悉以分故人親戚之貧者，此天下之廉士也。 宜納仁沼而遣漢榮。 」漢榮聞之，夜使人殺令圖而鴆仁沼。）〉 天福五年夏，高祖命馬全節為安州節度使，以代金全。 漢筠自以昔嘗拒命，復聞仁紹二子將訴置毒之事，居不自安，乃紿謂金全曰：「邸吏劉珂使健步倍道兼行，密傳其意，雲受代之後，朝廷將以仁紹之事詰公之罪。 」金全大駭，命從事張緯函表送款於淮夷。 淮人遣偽將李承裕以代金全，金全即日南竄，其妓樂、車馬、珍奇、帑藏，皆為承裕所奪。 與其黨數百人束身夜出，曉至氵義州，引領北望，泣下而去。 及至金陵，李棨授以節鎮。 〈 （馬令《南唐書》云：烈祖以金全為天威統軍，遷潤州節度使。）〉 後卒於江南。
Jinquan had a personal clerk named Hu Hanyun — brave, crafty, miserly, and narrow-minded, greedy, deceitful, and cruel — and he entrusted the whole government of the garrison to him. When Gaozu heard of this, he sent clerk Jia Renshao to replace Hanyun and summoned Hanyun to court. Hanyun was inwardly guilty and terrified; Jinquan then submitted a memorial claiming illness and reported it to court. When Renshao arrived, Hanyun poisoned and killed him. ( (Ma Ling's 《History of Southern Tang》 records that Hu Hanrong's conduct was largely unlawful; Later Jin's Gaozu was troubled by it and, not wishing to ruin a meritorious minister because of Hanrong, chose the upright clerk Jia Renzhao to replace him and summoned Hanrong to court. Hanrong urged Jinquan to keep him and refuse to send him away. Jinquan's guest Pang Lingtu remonstrated, "Renzhao once served Wang Yanqiu with great merit; when Yanqiu wished to reward him generously, Renzhao withdrew and said nothing — that is a loyal minister under Heaven. When captured goods were distributed, Renzhao gave his share entirely to old friends and poor relatives — that is an upright man under Heaven. You should accept Renzhao and send Hanrong away. " When Hanrong heard of this, he sent men by night to kill Lingtu and poison Renzhao.) In the summer of Tianfu 5, Gaozu appointed Ma Quanjie military commissioner of An to replace Jinquan. Hanyun, knowing he had once defied orders and hearing that Renshao's two sons would sue over the poisoning, could not feel secure; he deceived Jinquan, saying, "Clerk Liu Ke sent a swift courier by forced march with a secret message: once you accept replacement, the court will hold you to account for the Renshao affair. " Jinquan was greatly alarmed and ordered his staff officer Zhang Wei to seal a memorial and send surrender to the Huai regime. The Huai regime sent the general Li Chengyu to replace Jinquan; Jinquan fled south that same day, and Chengyu seized his courtesans, musicians, carriages, horses, rarities, and treasury stores. With several hundred followers he went out by night in bonds; at dawn they reached Yi prefecture, turned their faces north, wept, and went on. When he reached Jinling, Li Qi invested him with a commandery garrison. ( (Ma Ling's 《History of Southern Tang》 states that Liezu made Jinquan commander of Tianwei and transferred him to military commissioner of Run.) He later died in Jiangnan.
15
史臣曰：延光昔為唐臣，綽有令譽，洎逢晉祚，顯恣狂謀，既力屈以來降，尚顏而惜死，孟津之歿，乃取笑於千載也。 從賓而下，俱怙亂以滅身，亦何足與議也。 文進懼強敵之威，金全為輿臺所賣，事雖弗類，叛則攸同，鹹附島夷，皆可醜也。
The historian remarks: Yanguang was once a Tang minister of fine reputation; when the Jin dynasty arose he openly indulged reckless plots; though broken in strength and come to surrender, he still clung to face and feared death — and his end at Mengjin has been a laughingstock for a thousand years. From Congbin downward, all relied on rebellion to destroy themselves — what is there worth discussing? Wenjin feared a powerful enemy; Jinquan was betrayed by his own servants; though their cases differ, in rebellion they are alike — both clinging to the island barbarians, and both are shameful.
