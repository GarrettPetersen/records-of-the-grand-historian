1
少帝，名重貴，高祖之從子也。 考諱敬儒，母安氏，以唐天祐十一年六月二十七日生帝於太原汾陽裏。 敬儒嘗為後唐莊宗騎將，早薨，高祖以帝為子。 帝少而謹厚，高祖愛之。 洎歷方鎮，嘗遣從行，委以庶事，但性好馳射，有祖禰之風。 高祖鎮太原，命瑯琊王震以《禮記》教帝，不能領其大義，謂震曰：「非我家事業也。 」及高祖受圍於太原，親冒矢石，數獻可於左右，高祖愈重焉。 高祖受契丹冊，將入洛，欲留一子撫晉陽，先謀於戎王，戎王曰：「使諸子盡出，吾當擇之。 」乃於行中指帝謂高祖曰：「此眼大者可矣。 」遂以帝為北京留守，授金紫光祿大夫、檢校司徒，行太原尹，知河東管內節度觀察事。 天福二年九月，征赴闕，授光祿大夫、檢校太保、右金吾衛上將軍。 三年十二月，授開封尹，加檢校太傅，封鄭王，增食邑三千戶。 俄加檢校太尉、同中書門下平章事。 六年，高祖幸鄴，改廣晉尹，進封齊王。 〈 （以下疑脫「七年正月，加兼侍中」八字。）〉
Emperor Shao, whose personal name was Chonggui, was Gaozu's nephew. His father bore the taboo name Jingru and his mother was of the An clan. He was born on the twenty-seventh day of the sixth month of the eleventh year of Tang Tianyou (914) in Fenyang Lane, Taiyuan. Jingru had served as a cavalry general under Later Tang's Emperor Zhuangzong. After he died young, Gaozu adopted the future emperor as his own son. As a youth he was careful and steady in character, and Gaozu doted on him. Once he began serving in the provinces, Gaozu often had him travel in attendance and entrusted him with day-to-day business, though he naturally loved riding and archery and bore something of his ancestors' martial spirit. While Gaozu was stationed at Taiyuan, he had Prince Langya Wang Zhen instruct the future emperor in the Book of Rites, but the prince could not grasp its larger meaning and told Zhen, "That is not the calling of our house. " When Gaozu was besieged at Taiyuan, the prince personally braved arrow and stone fire, repeatedly offering sound counsel at his side, and Gaozu came to esteem him all the more. After Gaozu received Khitan investiture and was preparing to enter Luoyang, he wished to leave one son behind to oversee Jinyang and first sought Prince Rong's counsel. Prince Rong said, "Bring all your sons out; I shall choose among them. " Then, as they traveled, he pointed at the future emperor and told Gaozu, "This one with the large eyes will do. " He was accordingly made regent of the Northern Capital, appointed Grand Master of Splendid Happiness with Golden Seal and Purple Ribbon and acting Minister of Education, acting prefect of Taiyuan, and given charge of the Hedong circuit's military governorship and surveillance. In the ninth month of Tianfu 2 (937), he was summoned to court and appointed Grand Master for Splendid Happiness, acting Grand Mentor, and General-in-Chief of the Right Golden Guard. In the twelfth month of year three, he was appointed prefect of Kaifeng, given the additional title of acting Grand Tutor, enfeoffed as Prince of Zheng, and granted three thousand additional fief households. Soon after he was also made acting Grand Commandant and Co-Manager of Affairs under the Secretariat-Chancellery. In the sixth year, when Gaozu visited Ye, he was made prefect of Guangjin and promoted to Prince of Qi. ( (The following eight characters are suspected to be missing here: "In the first month of the seventh year, he was additionally made concurrent Palace Attendant.") )
2
是歲六月十三日乙丑，高祖崩，承遺制命柩前即皇帝位。 帝在并州未著人望，及保釐浚郊，大有寬裕之稱。 從幸鄴都，是歲遇旱，高祖遣祈雨於白龍潭，有白龍見於潭心，是夜澍雨尺餘，人皆異之，至是果登大位焉。 丁卯，賜侍衛諸軍將校錢一百貫下至五貫，以初即位示賫也。 戊辰，宰臣馮道等率百僚請聽政，凡三上表，允之。 庚午，始聽政於崇德殿門偏廊，分命廷臣以嗣位奏告天地宗廟社稷。 遣右驍衛將軍石德超等押先皇御馬二匹，往相州西山撲祭，用北俗禮也。 丙子，以司徒、兼侍中馮道為大行皇帝山陵使，門下侍郎竇貞固副之，太常卿崔棁為禮儀使，戶部侍郎呂琦為鹵簿使，御史中丞王易簡為儀仗使。 〈 （徐無黨《五代史記註》云：舊史實錄無橋道頓遞使，疑不置或闕書，漢高祖亦然。）〉 己卯，遣判四方館事朱崇節、右金吾大將軍梁言持國信物使於契丹。 是時，河南、河北、關西並奏蝗害稼。
On the thirteenth day of the sixth month of that year, the day yichou, Gaozu died, and by testamentary decree he assumed the throne before the coffin. While he was at Bingzhou he had not yet won wide esteem, but once he secured the realm, improved the capital outskirts, and took up rule, he gained a strong reputation for magnanimity. On a visit with Gaozu to the Ye capital that year there was drought; Gaozu sent men to pray for rain at White Dragon Pool, a white dragon appeared at the pool's center, and that night more than a foot of soaking rain fell. Everyone took it as a marvel, and when he did in fact ascend the throne it seemed a sign fulfilled. On dingmao, commanders and officers of the guard armies were granted cash gifts ranging from one hundred strings down to five, a bounty to mark his first accession. On wuchen, chief ministers led by Feng Dao and the full bureaucracy petitioned him three times to assume governance, and he assented. On gengwu he held his first audience in the side corridor of Chongde Hall's gate and separately ordered court officials to report his succession to Heaven and Earth, the imperial temples, and the altars of soil and grain. He dispatched Right Valiant Cavalry General Shi Dechao and others to escort two of the late emperor's imperial horses to the western hills of Xiangzhou for the pou sacrifice, following northern custom. On bingzi, Minister of Education and concurrent Palace Attendant Feng Dao was appointed commissioner for the late emperor's mausoleum, with Vice Director of the Chancellery Dou Zhengu as deputy; Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices Cui Rong was made ritual commissioner; Vice Minister of Revenue Lü Qi, commissioner for the imperial train; and Vice Censor-in-Chief Wang Yijian, commissioner for ceremonial regalia. ( (Xu Wudang's Commentary on the New History of the Five Dynasties notes: The veritable records in the Old History list no commissioner for bridge roads and relay posts; the office may never have been established or the entry may have been omitted—the same is true for Han Gaozu.) ) On jimao he sent Zhu Chongjie, overseer of the Bureau for All Directions, and Right Golden Guard General-in-Chief Liang Yan as envoys bearing state gifts to the Khitan. At this time Henan, Hebei, and Guanxi all reported locust damage to the crops.
3
秋七月癸未朔，百官素服臨於天清殿。 戊子，詔應宮殿、州縣及官名、府號、人姓名，與先帝諱同音者改之。 改西京明堂殿為宣德殿，中書政事堂為政事廳，堂後官房頭為錄事，余為主事。 〈 （《東都事略·陶穀傳》：穀本姓唐，避晉祖諱改姓陶，蓋當時避諱之體如此。）〉 己丑，大行皇帝大祥，帝釋缞服，百官衣縿。 辛卯，帝除禫服，百官吉服。 壬辰，太皇太后劉氏崩，高祖之庶母也。 遺詔服紀園陵毋用後禮，皇帝不得廢軍國機務。 既而禮官奏：「準令式，為祖父母齊缞周； 又準喪葬令，皇帝本服周者，三哭而止。 請準後唐同光三年，皇太妃北京薨，莊宗於洛京西內發哀素服，不視事三日。 」從之。 仍遣國子祭酒兼戶部侍郎田敏奏告高祖靈座。 癸巳，右諫議大夫鄭受益、中書舍人楊昭儉並停見任，以請假在外，不赴國喪故也。 丁酉，宰臣馮道等率文武百僚詣崇德殿門拜表，請禦正殿，凡三上表，允之。 安州奏，水平地深七尺。 庚子，帝御正殿，宣制：「大赦天下，諸道州府各色罪犯，除十惡五逆、殺人強盜、官典犯贓、合作毒藥、屠牛鑄錢外，其餘罪犯，咸赦除之。 襄州安從進如能果決輸誠，並從釋放。 其中外臣僚將校，並與加恩。 天下有蟲蝗處，並與除放租稅。 」辛丑，恒州順國軍節度使杜威、河東節度使劉知遠，並加檢校太師，仍增爵邑。 青州平盧軍節度使楊光遠加守太師。 癸卯，鄆州天平軍節度使兼侍衛馬步都虞候景延廣加特進、同中書門下平章事，充侍衛親軍都指揮使。 滑州義成軍節度使兼侍衛馬軍都指揮使李守貞，相州彰德軍節度使、侍衛步軍都指揮使郭謹，並加檢校太傅，仍增爵邑。 宰臣馮道等上表，請依舊置樞密使，略曰：「竊以樞密使創自前朝，置諸近侍，其來已久，所便尤多。 頃歲樞密使劉處讓偶屬家艱，爰拘喪制，既從罷免，暫議改更，不曾顯降敕文，永停使額。 所願各歸職分，豈敢茍避繁難。 伏請依舊置樞密使。 」初，高祖事後唐明宗，睹樞密使安重誨秉政擅權，賞罰由己，常惡之，及登極，故斷意廢罷，一委中書。 至是馮道等厭其事繁，故復請置之，庶分其權。 表凡三上，不允。 乙巳，徐州節度使李從溫、宋州節度使安彥威並加兼中書令，西都留守、充襄州行營都部署高行周加兼侍中，鳳翔節度使李從嚴加守太保。 遣中使就中書賜宰臣馮道生辰器幣，道以幼屬亂離，早喪父母，不記生日，堅辭不受。 丙午，以給事中羅周嶽為左散騎常侍，以右諫議大夫符蒙為給事中，以秘書少監兼廣晉少尹邊蔚為右散騎常侍，以廣晉少尹張煦為右諫議大夫，以廣晉府判官、光祿少卿邊光範為右諫議大夫。 丁未，荊南節度使、南平王高從誨加兼尚書令，湖南節度使、楚王馬希範加守太傅。 自是藩侯郡守，皆第加官封，示溥恩也。 是月，州郡十七蝗。
On the first day of the seventh month, guimwei, the full bureaucracy in plain mourning dress attended at Tianqing Hall. On wuzi an edict ordered that palace names, prefectures and counties, official titles, bureau names, and personal names that shared the sound of the late emperor's taboo name be changed. The Bright Hall of the Western Capital was renamed Hall of Proclaiming Virtue; the Secretariat's Hall of Government Affairs became the Government Affairs Office; senior clerks in the rear offices were renamed recorders and the rest section chiefs. ( (Eastern Capital Miscellany, Biography of Tao Gu: Gu's original surname was Tang; to avoid the Jin founder's taboo he changed it to Tao—such was contemporary taboo practice.) ) On jichou the late emperor reached the day of great auspice; the emperor removed his coarse hemp mourning dress and the officials wore fine hemp. On xinmao the emperor ended his final mourning garments and the officials wore formal dress. On renchen Grand Empress Dowager Lady Liu died; she had been Gaozu's concubine mother. Her testament ordered that mourning and her tomb not follow empress rites and that the emperor not suspend military and state business. The ritual officials then memorialized: "According to the statutes, mourning for grandparents requires unhemmed sackcloth for one year; and according to the burial ordinance, when the emperor's proper mourning period is one year, he stops after three cries. We ask that precedent be taken from Later Tang Tongguang 3, when the imperial consort mother died at the Northern Capital and Emperor Zhuangzong mourned in plain dress in Luoyang's western inner palace without holding audience for three days. " The request was approved. He also sent Director of the Imperial Academy and Vice Minister of Revenue Tian Min to report at Gaozu's spirit seat. On guisi Right Remonstrating Censor Zheng Shouyi and Secretariat Drafter Yang Zhaojian were both removed from active duty because they were away on leave and failed to attend the state mourning. On dingyou chief ministers led by Feng Dao and the civil and military officials petitioned three times at Chongde Hall's gate that he hold court in the main hall, and he assented. Anzhou reported standing water seven feet deep on level ground. On gengzi the emperor held court in the main hall and proclaimed: "A general amnesty is granted throughout the realm. For crimes in every circuit, prefecture, and county, except the ten abominations, the five rebellions, murder, robbery, official corruption, compounding poison, slaughtering cattle, and counterfeiting coin, all other offenses are pardoned. If An Congjin of Xiangzhou can decisively submit in loyalty, he too shall be released. Civil and military officials and commanders at court and in the provinces shall all receive added favor. Wherever locusts have struck, rents and taxes shall be remitted. " On xinchou Shunguo Army military governor of Hengzhou Du Wei and Hedong military governor Liu Zhiyuan were both made acting Grand Preceptor and given increased fiefs and ranks. Pinglu Army military governor of Qingzhou Yang Guangyuan was given the honorary title of Grand Preceptor. On guimao Tianping Army military governor of Yunzhou and chief inspector of the palace guard horse and foot Jing Yanguang was given special advancement and made Co-Manager of Affairs under the Secretariat-Chancellery and commander-in-chief of the palace guard personal armies. Yicheng Army military governor of Huazhou and commander of the palace guard horse Li Shouzhen, and Zhangde Army military governor of Xiangzhou and commander of the palace guard foot Guo Jin, were both made acting Grand Tutor with increased fiefs and ranks. Chief ministers led by Feng Dao memorialized to restore the office of Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs as before, stating in summary: "The commissioner was created in the previous dynasty and placed among close attendants; the office is long established and especially convenient. In recent years Commissioner Liu Churang happened to suffer a family bereavement and was bound by mourning rules; once he was removed, a temporary change was discussed, but no explicit edict ever permanently abolished the commission. We wish each to return to his proper duties and would not shirk difficulty for mere convenience. We humbly request that the Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs be restored as before. " Earlier, while serving Later Tang's Emperor Mingzong, Gaozu had seen Commissioner An Chonghui monopolize power and control rewards and punishments himself, which he deeply resented; on ascending the throne he therefore resolved to abolish the office and entrust everything to the Secretariat. Now Feng Dao and the others wearied of the burden of affairs and again requested its restoration, hoping to divide the power. The memorial was submitted three times and was denied each time. On yisi Xuzhou military governor Li Congwen and Songzhou military governor An Yanwei were both made concurrent Director of the Secretariat; Western Capital regent and overall commander of the Xiangzhou field army Gao Xingzhou was made concurrent Palace Attendant; and Fengxiang military governor Li Congyan was given the honorary title of Grand Mentor. An imperial envoy was sent to the Secretariat to bestow birthday gifts on chief minister Feng Dao; Dao, orphaned young amid turmoil and having lost his parents early, did not know his birthday and firmly declined. On bingwu Supervising Censor Luo Zhouyue was made Left Regular Attendant; Right Remonstrating Censor Fu Meng, Supervising Censor; Vice Director of the Secretariat and concurrent vice prefect of Guangjin Bian Wei, Right Regular Attendant; vice prefect of Guangjin Zhang Xu, Right Remonstrating Censor; and Guangjin prefectural judge and Vice Director for Splendid Happiness Bian Guangfan, Right Remonstrating Censor. On dingwei Jingnan military governor and King of Nanping Gao Conghui was made concurrent Director of the Department of State Affairs, and Hunan military governor and King of Chu Ma Xifan was given the honorary title of Grand Tutor. From this point regional lords and prefects were promoted in rank and enfeoffment in turn, displaying broad favor. That month locusts were reported in seventeen prefectures.
4
八月壬子朔，百官素服臨於天清殿。 乙卯，以左散騎常侍羅周嶽為東京副留守。 庚申，以山陵禮儀使、太常卿崔棁為太子賓客，分司西都，病故也。 壬戌，晉昌軍節度使桑維翰加檢校太傅。 甲子，宰臣馮道加守太尉，趙瑩加中書令，李崧加左僕射兼門下侍郎，和凝加右僕射。 契丹遣使致慰禮馬二十匹及羅絹等物。 是日，襄州行營都部署高行周奏，收復襄州，安從進自焚而死，生擒男宏贊斬之。 前河東節度使康福卒，贈太師，謚曰武安。 戊辰，以太子太保兼尚書左僕射劉句為太子太傅。 詔賜襄州城內百姓粟，大戶二斛，小戶一斛，以久困重圍也。 己巳，以太子賓客趙元輔權判太常卿事，充山陵禮儀使。 庚午，葬太皇太后於魏縣秦固村。 癸酉，契丹遣使致祭於高祖，賻禮御馬二匹、羊千口、絹千匹。 契丹主母亦遣使來慰。 詔免襄州城內人戶今年夏秋來屋稅，其城外下營處與放二年租稅。 應被安從進脅從者，一切不問。 是月，河中、河東、河西、徐、晉、商、汝等州蝗。
On the first day of the eighth month, renzi, the full bureaucracy in plain mourning dress attended at Tianqing Hall. On yimao Left Regular Attendant Luo Zhouyue was made deputy regent of the Eastern Capital. On gengshen mausoleum ritual commissioner and Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices Cui Rong was posthumously made Mentor of the Heir Apparent with duty at the Western Capital, having died of illness. On renxu Jinchang Army military governor Sang Weihan was made acting Grand Tutor. On jiazi chief minister Feng Dao was given the honorary title of Grand Commandant; Zhao Ying was made Director of the Secretariat; Li Song was made Left Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs and concurrent Vice Director of the Chancellery; and He Ning was made Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs. The Khitan sent envoys with condolence gifts of twenty horses, silks, and other goods. That day overall commander of the Xiangzhou field army Gao Xingzhou reported the recovery of Xiangzhou; An Congjin burned himself to death, and his son Hongzan was captured alive and beheaded. Former Hedong military governor Kang Fu died; he was posthumously made Grand Preceptor with the posthumous name Martial Tranquility. On wuchen Grand Mentor of the Heir Apparent and concurrent Left Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs Liu Gou was made Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent. An edict granted grain to the people within Xiangzhou city—two hu to large households and one hu to small—because they had long suffered under siege. On jisi Mentor of the Heir Apparent Zhao Yuanfu was temporarily assigned to act as Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices and appointed mausoleum ritual commissioner. On gengwu the Grand Empress Dowager was buried at Qinggu Village in Wei County. On guiyou the Khitan sent envoys to offer sacrifice for Gaozu, with funeral gifts of two imperial horses, a thousand sheep, and a thousand bolts of silk. The Khitan emperor's mother also sent envoys with condolences. An edict remitted this year's summer and autumn dwelling taxes for households within Xiangzhou and granted a two-year remission of rents and taxes for camps outside the walls. All who had been coerced into following An Congjin were exempt from prosecution. That month locusts were reported in Hezhong, Hedong, Hexi, Xu, Jin, Shang, Ru, and other prefectures.

On xinchou Golden Guard General-in-Chief and acting commissioner of the Three Departments Dong Yu was made commissioner of the Three Departments. An edict stated: "Prefectures and districts shall tax salt: transit tax seven cash per jin, residence tax ten cash per jin; prefectural salt offices and provincial agencies shall dispatch men to oversee collection. " Earlier, apart from silkworm salt, the various prefectures annually collected about seventeen million strings in salt prices from maritime salt boundaries. Because prohibitions everywhere produced many violators, Gaozu opened the salt ban and permitted free trade, ordering prefectures to levy a salt-money assessment on households—one thousand cash for upper households and two hundred for lower, divided into five grades—which was convenient at the time. At this time those in charge of revenue wished to increase profits but found it difficult to change the former law abruptly, so they increased market and pass duties, intending to cut off private trade and return profit to the state. Thereafter the salt prohibition remained as before and the salt money was also levied—an abuse that continues to this day. General-in-chief of the Right Golden Guard Liu Churang died; he was posthumously made Grand Commandant.
11
三月己卯朔，以中書令、監修國史趙瑩為晉昌軍節度使，以晉昌軍節度使桑維翰為侍中、監修國史。 〈 （《通鑒》作晉昌節度使、兼侍中桑維翰為侍中。 胡三省註云：桑維翰始居藩鎮而兼侍中，今入朝，正為門下省長官。）〉 辛巳，以左散騎常侍盧重為秘書監，以東京副留守羅周嶽為右散騎常侍。 癸未，青州節度使、東平王楊光遠進封壽王，北京留守劉知遠、恒州節度使杜威並加兼中書令。 乙酉，以鄜州節度使符彥卿為河陽節度使，以權鄴都留守、前開封尹張從恩為鄴都留守、廣晉尹，以右羽林統軍丁審琪為鄜州節度使。 丁亥，天策上將軍、湖南節度使、楚王馬希範加守尚書令、兼中書令。 己丑，桂州節度使馬希杲依前檢校太尉、兼侍中，兼知朗州軍州事； 朗州武平軍節度使馬希萼加檢校太尉，進封爵邑。 以武平軍節度副使、岳州團練使馬希瞻為檢校太尉，領盧州昭信軍節度使； 以武安軍節度副使、永州團練使馬希廣為檢校太尉，領洪州鎮南軍節度使； 皆楚王馬希範之弟也。 庚寅，以宣徽北院使李承福為右武衛大將軍，充宣徽南院使； 以前鄭州防禦使劉繼勛為左千牛衛大將軍，充宣徽北院使。 國子祭酒兼戶部侍郎田敏以印本《五經》書上進，賜帛五十段。 甲午，有白烏棲作坊桐樹，作坊使周務掠捕而進之。 辛丑，引進使、太府卿孟承誨使契丹。 詔京百司攝官親公事及五年，與授初官。 癸卯，以左諫議大夫司徒詡為給事中，左司郎中王仁裕為右諫議大夫，前鴻臚卿王均為少府監。
On the first day of the third month, jimao, Director of the Secretariat and supervisor of the national history Zhao Ying was made Jinchang Army military governor, and Jinchang Army military governor Sang Weihan was made Palace Attendant and supervisor of the national history. ( (Comprehensive Mirror records: Jinchang military governor and concurrent Palace Attendant Sang Weihan was made Palace Attendant. Hu Sanxing's commentary states: Sang Weihan had first held a regional command while also serving as Palace Attendant; now entering court, he properly became chief of the Chancellery.) ) On xinsi Left Regular Attendant Lu Chong was made Director of the Secretariat; Eastern Capital deputy regent Luo Zhouyue was made Right Regular Attendant. On guimwei Qingzhou military governor and Prince of Dongping Yang Guangyuan was advanced to Prince of Shou; Northern Capital regent Liu Zhiyuan and Hengzhou military governor Du Wei were both made concurrent Director of the Secretariat. On yiyou Yanzhou military governor Fu Yanqing was made Heyang military governor; acting regent of the Ye capital and former prefect of Kaifeng Zhang Congen was made regent of the Ye capital and prefect of Guangjin; and commander of the Right Forest of Feathers Army Ding Shenqi was made Yanzhou military governor. On dinghai Grand General of the Heavenly Stratagem, Hunan military governor, and King of Chu Ma Xifan was given the honorary title of Director of the Department of State Affairs and made concurrent Director of the Secretariat. On jichou Guizhou military governor Ma Xigao retained his former additional titles of acting Grand Commandant and concurrent Palace Attendant and was also given charge of Langzhou military and civil affairs; Wuping Army military governor of Langzhou Ma Xi'e was made acting Grand Commandant and advanced in enfeoffment and fief. Wuping Army vice military governor and Luzhou training commissioner Ma Xizhan was made acting Grand Commandant and appointed military governor of the Zhaoxin Army at Luzhou; Wu'an Army vice military governor and Yongzhou training commissioner Ma Xiguang was made acting Grand Commandant and appointed military governor of the Zhennan Army at Hongzhou; all were younger brothers of King of Chu Ma Xifan. On gengyin Commissioner of the Northern Bureau of the Palace Secretariat Li Chenfu was made general-in-chief of the Right Martial Guard and appointed commissioner of the Southern Bureau of the Palace Secretariat; former defense commissioner of Zhengzhou Liu Jixun was made general-in-chief of the Left Thousand-Ox Guard and appointed commissioner of the Northern Bureau of the Palace Secretariat. Director of the Imperial Academy and Vice Minister of Revenue Tian Min presented printed copies of the Five Classics and was granted fifty bolts of silk. On jiawu a white crow perched in a paulownia tree at the workshops; Workshop Commissioner Zhou Wu captured it and presented it. On xinchou Commissioner of Tribute and Director of the Court of the Imperial Treasury Meng Chenghui was sent as envoy to the Khitan. An edict ordered that acting officials in the capital bureaus who had handled personal official business for five years should be granted initial regular appointments. On guimao Left Remonstrating Censor Situ Xu was made Supervising Censor; Left Bureau Director Wang Renyu was made Right Remonstrating Censor; and former Director of the Court of the Imperial Banquets Wang Jun was made Director of the Court of the Imperial Manufactories.
12
夏四月戊申朔，日有食之。 庚戌，以許州節度使趙在禮為徐州節度使，以徐州節度使李從溫為許州節度使。 己巳，中書門下奏：「請以六月二十七日降誕日為啟聖節。 」從之。 是月，河南、河北、關西諸州旱蝗，分命使臣捕之。
On the first day of the fourth month, wushen, there was a solar eclipse. On gengxu Xuzhou military governor Zhao Zaili was made military governor of Xuzhou, and Xuzhou military governor Li Congwen was made military governor of Xu. On jisi the Secretariat-Chancellery memorialized: "We request that the twenty-seventh day of the sixth month, the day of birth, be established as the Festival of Opening Sagacity. " The request was approved. That month Henan, Hebei, and Guanxi prefectures suffered drought and locusts, and envoys were separately dispatched to capture them.
13
五月己卯，追封皇故長姊為吳國長公主。 癸未，皇侄女永福縣主薨，輟朝三日，追封平昌郡主。 丁亥，皇第二叔祖贈太師萬友追封秦王； 皇第三叔祖贈太尉萬銓贈太師，追封趙王。 皇伯贈太傅敬儒贈太師，追封宋王； 皇叔贈太尉福王德贈太師，追封如故； 皇叔贈太傅暉贈太師，追封韓王； 皇叔贈太尉通王殷、皇叔贈太尉廣王威、皇兄贈太傅郯王重裔並贈太師，追封如故。 皇兄贈太師沂王重信追封楚王； 皇兄贈太傅虢王重乂、皇兄贈太師夔王重進、皇弟贈太尉陳王重杲等並贈太師，追封如故。 仍令所司擇日冊命。 辛卯，以御史中丞王易簡為尚書左丞，以禮部侍郎張允為御史中丞，以中書舍人吳承範為禮部侍郎，以吏部侍郎王延為尚書右丞，以尚書右丞王松為吏部侍郎，以兵部侍郎張昭遠為吏部侍郎，以戶部侍郎呂琦為兵部侍郎，以刑部侍郎韋勛為戶部侍郎，以工部侍郎李詳為刑部侍郎。 癸巳，命宰臣等分詣寺觀禱雨。 己亥，飛蝗自北翳天而南。 太子賓客李棁卒。 甲辰，詔：「諸道州府見禁罪人，除十惡五逆、行劫殺人、偽行印信、合造毒藥、官典犯贓各減一等外，餘並放。 」是時所在旱蝗，故有是詔。 乙巳，幸相國寺祈雨。
In the fifth month, on jimao, the late emperor's elder sister was posthumously advanced to Grand Princess of Wu. On guimwei the emperor's niece, District Mistress of Yongfu, died; court was suspended for three days, and she was posthumously advanced to Commandery Mistress of Pingchang. On dinghai the emperor's second great-uncle, posthumously made Grand Preceptor Wan You, was posthumously enfeoffed as Prince of Qin; the emperor's third great-uncle, posthumously made Grand Commandant Wan Quan, was posthumously made Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as Prince of Zhao. the emperor's uncle, posthumously made Grand Tutor Jingru, was posthumously made Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as Prince of Song; the emperor's uncle, posthumously made Grand Commandant Prince of Fu Wang De, was posthumously made Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as before; the emperor's uncle, posthumously made Grand Tutor Hui, was posthumously made Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as Prince of Han; the emperor's uncles, posthumously made Grand Commandant Prince of Tong Wang Yin and Prince of Guang Wang Wei, and the emperor's elder brother, posthumously made Grand Tutor Prince of Tan Chongyi, were all posthumously made Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as before. the emperor's elder brother, posthumously made Grand Preceptor Prince of Yi Chongxin, was posthumously advanced to Prince of Chu; the emperor's elder brothers, posthumously made Grand Tutor Prince of Guo Chongyi and Grand Preceptor Prince of Kui Chongjin, and the emperor's younger brother, posthumously made Grand Commandant Prince of Chen Chonggao, and others were all posthumously made Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as before. The responsible offices were further ordered to choose a day for the investiture edict. On xinmao Vice Censor-in-Chief Wang Yijian was made Left Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs; Vice Minister of Rites Zhang Yun, Vice Censor-in-Chief; Secretariat drafter Wu Chengfang, Vice Minister of Rites; Vice Minister of Personnel Wang Yan, Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs; Right Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs Wang Song, Vice Minister of Personnel; Vice Minister of War Zhang Zhaoyuan, Vice Minister of Personnel; Vice Minister of Revenue Lü Qi, Vice Minister of War; Vice Minister of Justice Wei Xun, Vice Minister of Revenue; and Vice Minister of Works Li Xiang, Vice Minister of Justice. On guisi the chief ministers and others were ordered to go separately to temples and monasteries to pray for rain. On jihai flying locusts came from the north, darkening the sky as they moved south. Mentor of the Heir Apparent Li Rong died. On jiachen an edict stated: "For prisoners currently held in the various circuits, prefectures, and districts, except the ten abominations, the five rebellions, highway robbery and murder, forging seals and credentials, compounding poison, and official corruption, each of which shall be reduced one grade in punishment, all the rest shall be released. " At this time drought and locusts prevailed everywhere, and therefore this edict was issued. On yisi the emperor visited Xiangguo Temple to pray for rain.
14
六月庚戌，以螟蝗為害，詔侍衛馬步軍都指揮使李守貞往臯門祭告，仍遣諸司使梁進超等七人分往開封府界捕之。 乙卯，以左羽林統軍安審暉為潞州節度使。 宿州奏，飛蝗抱草幹死。 丙辰，貝州奏，逃戶凡三千七百。 遣供奉官衛延韜詣嵩山投龍祈雨。 戊午，以西京留守馬從斌為左監門衛上將軍。 開封府界飛蝗自死。 庚申，河南府奏，飛蝗大下，遍滿山野，草苗木葉食之皆盡，人多餓死。 禮部侍郎吳承範卒。 丙寅，以將冊皇太后，遣尚書左丞王易簡奏告天地。 陜州奏，蝗飛入界，傷食五稼及竹木之葉，逃戶凡八千一百。 丁卯，以給事中符蒙為禮部侍郎，以左諫議大夫裴坦為給事中。 辛未，遣內外臣僚二十八人分往諸道州府率借粟麥。 時使臣希旨，立法甚峻。 民間碓硙泥封之、隱其數者皆斃之，由是人不聊生，物情胥怨。 是月，諸州郡大蝗，所至草木皆盡。
In the sixth month, on gengxu, because caterpillars and locusts were causing harm, an edict ordered commander-in-chief of the palace guard horse and foot Li Shouzhen to go to Gaomen to offer sacrifice and report, and also dispatched seven bureau commissioners including Liang Jinchao separately into the Kaifeng prefecture boundaries to capture them. On yimao commander of the Left Forest of Feathers Army An Shenhui was made military governor of Luzhou. Suzhou reported that flying locusts clung to the grass and died dry. On bingchen Beizhou reported fugitive households numbering three thousand seven hundred. Palace attendant Wei Yantao was dispatched to Mount Song to cast a dragon petition and pray for rain. On wuwu Western Capital regent Ma Congbin was made general-in-chief of the Left Gate Guard. Within the Kaifeng prefecture boundaries, flying locusts died on their own. On gengshen Henan prefecture reported that flying locusts descended in great numbers, filling the mountains and wilds; grass, trees, and leaves were all eaten clean, and many people starved to death. Vice Minister of Rites Wu Chengfang died. On bingyin, because the Empress Dowager was to be invested, Left Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs Wang Yijian was dispatched to report to Heaven and Earth. Shanzhou reported that locusts flew into its borders, damaging the five grains and the leaves of bamboo and trees; fugitive households numbered eight thousand one hundred. On dingmao Supervising Censor Fu Meng was made Vice Minister of Rites, and Left Remonstrating Censor Pei Tan was made Supervising Censor. On xinwei twenty-eight civil and military officials were dispatched separately to the various circuits, prefectures, and districts to compel loans of grain and wheat. At the time the envoys sought to please the court and established laws that were very harsh. Among the people, mortars and mills were sealed with mud, and those who concealed their quantities were all put to death; thereby people could not sustain life, and sentiment everywhere turned to resentment. That month great locust plagues struck the various prefectures and districts; wherever they went, grass and trees were consumed entirely.
