1
開運元年秋七月辛未朔，帝御崇元殿，大赦天下，改天福九年為開運元年。 河北諸州，曾經契丹蹂踐處，與免今年秋稅。 諸軍將士等第各賜優給。 諸州率借錢帛，赦書到日，畫時罷征，出一千貫已上者與免科徭，一萬貫已上者與授本州上佐雲。 是日宣赦未畢，會大雷雨，匆遽而罷。 時都下震死者數百人，明德門內震落石龍之首，識者以為石乃國姓，蓋不祥之甚也。 癸酉，以定州節度使馬全節為鄴都留守，加兼侍中； 以昭義節度使安審暉為邢州節度使，加檢校太師。 乙亥，前陜州節度使王周加檢校太尉，改定州節度使； 鄴都留守張從恩改鄆州節度使。 禮官奏：「天子三年喪畢，祫享於太廟，高祖聖文章武明德孝皇帝今年八月喪終畢，合以十月行大祫之禮，冬季祠祭，改薦為祫。 」從之。 丁丑，虞部員外郎、知制誥陶穀改倉部郎中、知制誥，大理卿吳德謙改秘書監致仕。 辛巳，以左龍武統軍李從敏為潞州節度使，天策府都護軍、桂州節度使、知郎州軍事馬希杲加檢校太師。 壬午，降金州為防禦州，降萊州為刺史州。 戶部侍郎田敏改兵部侍郎； 刑部侍郎李祥改尚書右丞； 以潁州團練使馮玉為戶部侍郎，充端明殿學士； 中書舍人趙上交改刑部侍郎。 己丑，以樞密使、中書令桑維翰充宏文館大學士，太子太傅、譙國公劉句為守司空兼門下侍郎平章事、監修國史、判三司，宰臣李崧、和凝進封爵邑。 庚寅，宣徽北院使劉繼勛改宣徽南院使，三司使董遇改宣徽北院使。 辛卯，以前陜州節度使石赟為鄧州節度使。 同州節度使李承福卒，贈太傅。
On the new moon of the seventh month of autumn in Kaiyun 1, the emperor appeared at Chongyuan Hall, granted a general amnesty, and renamed the ninth Tianfu year as Kaiyun 1. Prefectures in Hebei that the Khitan had ravaged were exempted from the autumn levy for that year. Soldiers and officers in every army received graded bonuses according to merit. Forced prefectural loans in cash and silk were to cease the moment the amnesty arrived; lenders of a thousand strings or more were freed from tax and corvée, and those of ten thousand or more were appointed senior aides in their home prefecture. That day the amnesty reading had not finished when a violent thunderstorm broke out, and the ceremony was cut short in haste. Lightning killed several hundred people in the capital, and at the Mingde Gate a stone dragon's head was struck loose. Omens readers noted that stone was the dynastic surname—a dire portent indeed. On guiyou Ma Quanjie of Dingzhou became Yedu military governor and was also made Palace Attendant; An Shenhui of Zhaoyi was transferred to Xingzhou as commissioner and given acting Grand Preceptor. On yihai former Shaanzhou commissioner Wang Zhou received acting Grand Marshal and was reassigned to Dingzhou; Yedu governor Zhang Cong'en became Yanzhou military commissioner. Ritual officials reported that after the three-year mourning the emperor should offer the he rite in the ancestral temple; Gaozu's mourning would end in the eighth month, so the great he ceremony should be held in the tenth month, and the winter seasonal service should be changed from jian to he. The court accepted this. On dingchou Tao Gu moved from the Ministry of Works to the Ministry of Revenue while keeping his drafting duties; Wu Deqian left the Court of Judicial Review to retire as Director of the Secretariat. On xinsi Li Congmin of the Left Dragon Martial Army became Luzhou commissioner, and Ma Xigao of Guizhou and Langzhou received acting Grand Preceptor. On renwu Jinzhou was downgraded to a defense command and Laizhou to a standard prefecture. Tian Min moved from Revenue to War as vice minister; Li Xiang of Justice became Right Vice Director of State Affairs; Yingzhou training commissioner Feng Yu became Revenue vice minister and a Duanming Hall academician; Secretariat drafter Zhao Shangjiao became Justice vice minister. On jichou Sang Weihan of the Bureau of Military Affairs became Hongwen Hall grand academician; Liu Xu of Qiao received acting Minister of Works with chancellor, historiography, and fiscal duties; chief ministers Li Song and He Ning gained higher titles and fiefs. On gengyin Liu Jixun moved from the northern to the southern Palace Secretariat court, and Dong Yu of the Three Commissions took the northern post. On xinmao former Shaanzhou commissioner Shi Yun was appointed to Dengzhou. Tongzhou commissioner Li Chengfu died and was posthumously honored as Grand Tutor.

On xinchou in the eighth month fifteen generals were ordered to meet the Khitan, [ (Brief Account of the Eastern Capital, Biography of Fan Zhi: the Jin Deposed Emperor sent fifteen generals on campaign.) On renyin Min king Wang Yanxi was murdered by Lian Chongyu and Zhu Wenjin; the troops backed Wenjin as acting governor, kept the Tianfu era name, and sent word by a back road. On jiachen Lu Wenji rose from junior to grand tutor of the heir; Li Lin became grand guardian; Li Yi moved from Justice to Revenue; Situ Xu became right cavalier attendant; and Fuzhou prefect Zhe Congruan was named northern protector and Zhenwu commissioner. That night Mars entered the constellation of the Southern Dipper. On yisi the throne restored the Classics Specialist and Youth civil-service categories. On jiyou Dengzhou commissioner Wang Lingwen was transferred to Yanzhou. On guichou Zhu Wenjin, acting Weiwu commander and provisional ruler of Min, received acting Grand Tutor, became Fuzhou Weiwu commissioner, and continued to govern Min. 3
九月庚午朔，日有蝕之。 乙酉，以戶部侍郎韋勛為太子賓客，以前棣州刺史段希堯為戶部侍郎，以光祿卿張仁願為大理卿。 己丑，禮部侍郎符蒙卒。 壬辰，太原奏，代州刺史白文珂破契丹於七里烽，斬首千餘級，生擒將校七十餘人。 癸巳，以前隴州防禦使翟光鄴為宣徽北院使。 己亥，以滄州節度使王廷胤卒輟朝，贈中書令。
The new moon of the ninth month brought a solar eclipse. On yiyou Wei Xun of Revenue became mentor to the heir; former Dizhou prefect Duan Xiyao became Revenue vice minister; and Zhang Renyuan of the Court of Imperial Entertainments became director of judicial review. On jichou Rites vice minister Fu Meng died. On renchen Taiyuan reported that Daizhou prefect Bai Wenke had routed the Khitan at Qili Beacon, taking more than a thousand heads and seventy officers alive. On guisi former Longzhou defense commissioner Zhai Guangye became northern Palace Secretariat commissioner. On jihai court mourning was observed for the death of Cangzhou commissioner Wang Tingyin, who was posthumously named Palace Attendant-in-Chief.
4
冬十月壬寅，兩浙節度使、吳越國王錢宏佐加守太尉。 庚戌，以徐州節度使、北面行營馬步都虞候趙在禮為北面行營副都統，鄴都留守馬全節為北面行營副招討使。 甲寅，以起居郎、知制誥賈緯為戶部郎中、知制誥。 戊午，詔曰：
In the tenth winter month, on renyin, Wuyue king and Two Zhes commissioner Qian Hongzuo received acting Grand Marshal. On gengxu Zhao Zaili of Xuzhou became deputy northern commander, and Yedu governor Ma Quanjie became deputy chief pacification commander. On jiayin recorder and drafting drafter Jia Wei became Revenue director while keeping his drafting post. On wuwu the throne issued an edict:
5
朕虔承顧命，獲嗣丕基。 常懼顛危，不克負荷，宵分日昃，罔敢怠寧，夕惕晨興，每懷祗畏。 但以恩信未著，德教未敷，理道不明，咎征斯至。
We have reverently received the late emperor's charge and succeeded to the great foundation of the realm. We live in constant fear of ruin and of failing to bear the burden; from midnight until sundown we dare not rest; each evening we take warning and each dawn we rise, ever mindful of reverent awe. Yet because grace and trust have not been made clear, moral teaching has not spread, and the principles of rule remain obscure, signs of blame have come upon us.
6
向者，頻年災沴，稼穡不登，道堇相望，上天垂譴，涼德所招。 仍屬干戈尚興，邊陲多事。 倉廩不足，則輟人之糇食； 帑藏不足，則率人之資財； 兵士不足，則取人之丁中； 戰騎不足，則假人之乘馬。 雖事不獲已，而理將若何！ 訪聞差去使臣，殊乖體認，不有敦於勉諭，而乃臨以威刑，自有所聞，益深愧悼。 旋屬守臣叛命，敵騎入邊，致使甲兵不暇休息，軍旅有征戰之苦，人民有飛免之勞，疲瘵未蘇，科徭尚急，言念於茲，寢食何安！ 得不省過興懷，側身罪已，載深減損，思召和平？ 所宜去無用之資，罷不急之務，棄華取實，惜費省功，一則符先帝慈儉之規，一則慕前王樸素之德。
In recent years disasters and pestilence have followed one upon another, harvests have failed, and the starved lie unburied along the roads; Heaven's reproof reflects the weakness of our virtue. Moreover war still rages and the frontiers are in constant turmoil. When the granaries run short, we seize the people's grain; when the treasury fails, we levy the people's wealth; when troops are lacking, we conscript men from the registers; when cavalry mounts fall short, we press the people's horses into service. Though such measures could not be avoided, what becomes of right principle! We have learned that the envoys we sent have grossly misunderstood our intent, threatening the people with harsh punishments rather than earnestly exhorting them; hearing of this, we are all the more ashamed and grieved. Soon a frontier commander rebelled and enemy cavalry crossed the border, so that arms never rested, the armies knew campaign hardship, and the people bore the burden of urgent transport. The sick have not recovered, yet levies remain harsh. At the thought of this, how can we eat or sleep in peace! Should we not examine our faults, reproach ourselves, cut our spending deeply, and seek to restore peace? We should cut useless spending, drop nonessential tasks, choose substance over display, and husband resources—honoring the late emperor's merciful frugality and the plain virtue of kings of old.
7
向者，造作軍器，破用稍多，但取堅剛，不須華楚； 今後作坊制器械，不得更用金銀裝飾。 比於遊畋，素非所好，凡諸服禦，尤欲去奢，應天下府州不得以珍寶玩好及鷹犬為貢。 在昔聖帝明君，無非惡衣菲食，況於薄德，所合恭行，今後大官尚膳，減去多品，衣服帷帳，務去華飾，在禦寒溫而已。 峻宇雕墻，昔人所誡，玉杯象箸，前代攸非，今後凡有營繕之處，丹堊雕鏤，不得過度，宮闈之內，有非理費用，一切禁止。
Recently arms manufacture has been costly; we need only strength and durability, not lavish decoration; from now on workshops must not adorn weapons with gold or silver. We have never cared for the chase; for court dress and equipage we especially wish to strip away luxury. No prefecture in the realm may offer jewels, curios, hawks, or hounds as tribute. Sage rulers of old wore plain clothes and ate simple food; how much more should we of little virtue do the same. Henceforth the imperial kitchen shall serve fewer dishes; clothing and hangings shall shed ornament and serve only warmth and shelter. Lofty halls and carved walls were condemned by the ancients, as were jade cups and ivory chopsticks. Henceforth all building work must avoid excessive paint and carving, and every unreasonable expense within the palace is forbidden.
8
於戲！ 繼聖承祧，握樞臨極，昧於至道，若履春冰。 屬以天災流行，國步多梗，因時致懼，引咎推誠，期於將來，庶幾有補。 更賴王公、將相，貴戚、豪宗，各啟乃心，率由茲道，共臻富庶，以致康寧。 凡百臣僚，宜體朕意。
Alas! Having succeeded the sage and received the ancestral line, we hold the throne yet are blind to the supreme Way, as if walking on spring ice. With disasters abroad and the realm in peril, we take warning from the times, accept blame, and offer our sincerity, hoping the future may yet be set right. We call upon princes, ministers, noble kin, and great families alike to open your hearts, follow this course, and together bring prosperity and peace to the realm. Let every official take this to heart.
9
十一月壬申，詔曰：「蕃寇未平，邊陲多事，即日雖無侵軼，亦須廣設提防。 朕將親率虎貔，躬擐甲胄，候聞南牧，即便北征，不須先定日辰，別行告諭。 所有供億，宜令三司預行計度，合隨從諸司職員，並宜常備行計」云。 己卯，以陳州刺史梁漢璋充侍衛馬軍都指揮使。 壬午，以貝州節度使何建為澶州節度使兼北面行營馬軍右廂排陣使，以澶州節度使史威為貝州節度使。 丙戌，以前金州節度使田武為滄州節度使兼北面行營步軍右廂都指揮使，以前相州節度使郭謹為鄜州節度使。
On renshen in the eleventh month an edict declared: "The barbarian enemy is not yet subdued and the frontiers remain unsettled. Though there is no raid today, we must still prepare our defenses widely." We shall lead the army in person and buckle on armor; when word comes that the enemy is driving south, we shall march north at once without fixing a date in advance or issuing a separate notice. All supplies should be planned in advance by the Three Commissions, and every official of the accompanying offices should keep travel arrangements ready at all times." On jimao Chenzhou prefect Liang Hanzhang was made overall commander of the Palace Cavalry. On renwu Beizhou commissioner He Jian became Chunzhou commissioner and cavalry right-wing array marshal of the northern headquarters, while Chunzhou commissioner Shi Wei was transferred to Beizhou. On bingxu former Jinzhou commissioner Tian Wu became Cangzhou commissioner and infantry right-wing commander of the northern headquarters, and former Xiangzhou commissioner Guo Jin was appointed to Fuzhou.
10
十二月己亥朔，幸臯門，射中白兔。 癸丑，福州節度使朱文進加同平章事，封閩國王。 丁巳，青州楊光遠降。 光遠子承勛等斬觀察判官邱濤、牙將白延祚、楊贍、杜延壽等首級，送於招討使李守貞，乃縱火大噪，劫其父處於私第，以城納款，遣即墨縣令王德柔貢表待罪。 楊光遠亦遣節度判官楊麟奉表請死。 詔釋之。
On the new moon of the twelfth month the emperor visited Gao Gate and shot a white hare. On guichou Fuzhou commissioner Zhu Wenjin was made grand councilor and enfeoffed as King of Min. On dingsi Yang Guangyuan of Qingzhou surrendered. Guangyuan's sons Chengxun and the rest killed observation aide Qiu Tao and guard officers Bai Yanzuo, Yang Zhan, and Du Yanshou, sending their heads to pacification commissioner Li Shouzhen. They then set fires, raised an uproar, seized their father and held him in a private house, surrendered the city, and sent Jimo magistrate Wang Derou with a memorial awaiting judgment. Yang Guangyuan also sent commissioner aide Yang Lin with a memorial asking to die. An edict granted him pardon.
11
閏月庚午，以楊承信為右羽林將軍，承祚為右驍騎衛將軍。 皆光遠之子，先詣闕請罪，故特授是官。 癸酉，李守貞奏，楊光遠卒。 初，光遠既上表送降，帝以光遠頃歲太原歸命，欲曲全之，議者曰：「豈有反狀滔天而赦之也！ 」乃命守貞便宜處置。 守貞遣人拉殺之，以病卒聞。 乙酉，前登州刺史張萬迪削奪官爵處斬，青州節度判官楊麟配流威州，掌書記任邈配流原州，支使徐晏配流武州。 縱逢恩赦，不在放還之限，並以楊光遠叛故也。 工部尚書、權知貢舉竇貞固奏：「試進士諸科舉人入策，舊例夜試，以三條燭盡為限，天成二年改令晝試，今欲依舊夜試。 」從之。 曲赦青州管內罪人，立功將士各賜優給，青州吏民為楊光遠詿誤者，一切不問。 青州行營招討使、兗州節度使兼侍衛都虞候李守貞加同平章事，副招討使、河陽節度使符彥卿改許州節度使。 丙戌，降青州為防禦使額，以萊州刺史楊承勛為汝州防禦使。 己丑，以工部尚書竇貞固為禮部尚書，太常卿王延為工部尚書，左丞王松為太常卿，以前尚書右丞龍敏為尚書左丞。 癸巳，以前安州防禦使李建崇為河陽兵馬留後，以宣徽使翟光鄴為青州防禦使，以內客省使李彥韜為宣徽北院使。 甲午，以給事中邊光範為左散騎常侍，以樞密直學士、吏部郎中李穀為給事中，依前充職。 是月，契丹耶律德光與趙延壽領全軍入寇，圍恒州，分兵陷鼓城、槁城、元氏、高邑、昭慶、寧晉、蒲澤、欒城、柏鄉等縣，前鋒至邢州，河北諸州告急。 詔張從恩、馬全節、安審琦率師屯邢州，趙在禮屯鄴都。
On gengwu in the intercalary month Yang Chengxin was made a general of the Right Forest of Feathers, and Chengzuo a general of the Right Valiant Cavalry Guard. Both were Guangyuan's sons who had come to court first to plead guilt, and were therefore specially given these posts. On guiyou Li Shouzhen reported that Yang Guangyuan was dead. When Guangyuan first submitted his surrender memorial, the emperor, remembering that he had submitted at Taiyuan only a few years earlier, wished to spare him. Counselors objected: "How can treason of such magnitude be pardoned!" The emperor then ordered Shouzhen to deal with him as he saw fit. Shouzhen had him strangled and reported that he had died of illness. On yiyou former Dengzhou prefect Zhang Wandi was stripped of rank and executed; Qingzhou aide Yang Lin was exiled to Weizhou; secretary Ren Miao to Yuanzhou; and administrative aide Xu Yan to Wuzhou. Even future amnesties would not restore them, all on account of Yang Guangyuan's rebellion. Dou Zhengu, Minister of Works and acting examination director, reported that policy examinations for Presented Scholars and other candidates had formerly been held at night, limited by three candles; daytime exams were introduced in Tiancheng 2, and he asked to restore the night examination. The court accepted this. The generals all pressed for an attack. Du Wei said, "Wait until the wind eases and watch whether they advance or pull back." Shouzhen said, "This wind is on our side." They outnumber us, and in this black wind no one can tell how many they are. If we wait for the wind to die, none of us will get out alive. He then ordered the whole army to attack together. Zhang Yanzhe, Fu Yanqing, Huangfu Yu, and others led the cavalry in a furious charge through a wind so fierce that sand and dust turned day to night, and the enemy was routed. [ (History of Song, Biography of Fu Yanqing: the Jin army was downwind and could not use bows or crossbows as battle was joined.) Yanqing told Zhang Yanzhe and Huangfu Yu, "Better to die fighting than be taken—and we may not die at all." Yanzhe agreed, sent troops around behind the enemy, struck with the wind, and routed the Khitan. Biography of Yao Yuanfu adds that Shouzhen and Yuanfu said, "The army is starving and parched; if we wait for the wind to shift, we are finished." They assume we cannot fight into the wind; we should take them by surprise—that is the classic stratagem. Yuanfu led his men through the abatis; the other generals followed, and the Khitan was routed.)〉 Infantry and cavalry pressed the pursuit for more than twenty li. East of Yangcheng the enemy began to form ranks; our cavalry struck again and drove them across the river. Shouzhen said, "Today was as desperate as it gets; thanks to your fighting with everything you had, we have been saved." For two days men and horses have gone without water; now that they have drunk, their legs are heavy. We should pull back to Dingzhou while we can—that is the best course. The generals then reformed the army and withdrew. The Khitan ruler had been in his carriage; when he fled, the carriage went ten li before pursuers closed in, and he escaped on a captured camel. On yichou Du Wei and the main army marched back from Dingzhou and entered Hengzhou.

夏四月丙子，以車駕將還京，差官往西京告天地宗廟社稷。 辛巳，駕發澶州。 甲申，至京師，曲赦在京禁囚。 己亥，詔鄴都依舊為天雄軍。 庚寅，河東節度使劉知遠封北平王； 恒州節度使杜威加守太傅； 徐州趙在禮移鎮兗州； 宋州節度使兼侍衛親軍馬步都指揮使高行周移鎮鄆州，侍衛如故； 鄴都留守馬全節改天雄軍節度使； 兗州節度使兼侍衛都虞候李守貞移鎮宋州，加檢校太師兼侍衛親軍副指揮使； 河中節度使安審琦加兼侍中，移鎮許州； 許州節度使符彥卿加同平章事，移鎮徐州； 滑州節度使皇甫遇加同平章事。 壬辰，西京留守景延廣加邑封，改功臣； 泰州節度使侯益移鎮河中； 定州節度使王周加檢校太師。
On bingzi in the fourth summer month, with the emperor about to return to the capital, envoys were sent to the Western Capital to announce the news at the altars of Heaven, Earth, the ancestral temples, and the state. On xinsi the imperial procession left Chunzhou. On jiashen the court reached the capital and granted a partial amnesty to prisoners in the capital. On jihai an edict restored Yedu to its status as the Tianxiong Army. On gengyin Hedong commissioner Liu Zhiyuan was enfeoffed as Prince of Beiping; Hengzhou commissioner Du Wei received acting Grand Tutor; Zhao Zaili moved from Xuzhou to Yanzhou; Gao Xingzhou of Songzhou, director of the Palace Guard, was transferred to Yanzhou while keeping his guard post; Yedu governor Ma Quanjie became Tianxiong Army commissioner; Yanzhou commissioner and guard chief inspector Li Shouzhen moved to Songzhou with acting Grand Preceptor and deputy guard command; Hezhong commissioner An Shenqi received Palace Attendant and was transferred to Xuzhou; Xu Prefecture commissioner Fu Yanqing was made grand councilor and transferred to Xuzhou; Huazhou commissioner Huangfu Yu was made grand councilor. On renchen Western Capital governor Jing Yanguang received an enlarged fief and a new merit title; Taizhou commissioner Hou Yi was transferred to Hezhong; Dingzhou commissioner Wang Zhou received acting Grand Preceptor.
