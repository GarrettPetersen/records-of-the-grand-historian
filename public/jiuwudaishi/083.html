1
開運元年秋七月辛未朔，帝御崇元殿，大赦天下，改天福九年為開運元年。 河北諸州，曾經契丹蹂踐處，與免今年秋稅。 諸軍將士等第各賜優給。 諸州率借錢帛，赦書到日，畫時罷征，出一千貫已上者與免科徭，一萬貫已上者與授本州上佐雲。 是日宣赦未畢，會大雷雨，匆遽而罷。 時都下震死者數百人，明德門內震落石龍之首，識者以為石乃國姓，蓋不祥之甚也。 癸酉，以定州節度使馬全節為鄴都留守，加兼侍中； 以昭義節度使安審暉為邢州節度使，加檢校太師。 乙亥，前陜州節度使王周加檢校太尉，改定州節度使； 鄴都留守張從恩改鄆州節度使。 禮官奏：「天子三年喪畢，祫享於太廟，高祖聖文章武明德孝皇帝今年八月喪終畢，合以十月行大祫之禮，冬季祠祭，改薦為祫。 」從之。 丁丑，虞部員外郎、知制誥陶穀改倉部郎中、知制誥，大理卿吳德謙改秘書監致仕。 辛巳，以左龍武統軍李從敏為潞州節度使，天策府都護軍、桂州節度使、知郎州軍事馬希杲加檢校太師。 壬午，降金州為防禦州，降萊州為刺史州。 戶部侍郎田敏改兵部侍郎； 刑部侍郎李祥改尚書右丞； 以潁州團練使馮玉為戶部侍郎，充端明殿學士； 中書舍人趙上交改刑部侍郎。 己丑，以樞密使、中書令桑維翰充宏文館大學士，太子太傅、譙國公劉句為守司空兼門下侍郎平章事、監修國史、判三司，宰臣李崧、和凝進封爵邑。 庚寅，宣徽北院使劉繼勛改宣徽南院使，三司使董遇改宣徽北院使。 辛卯，以前陜州節度使石赟為鄧州節度使。 同州節度使李承福卒，贈太傅。
On the first day of the seventh month in autumn of the first year of Kaiyun, the emperor took the throne at Chongyuan Hall, proclaimed a general amnesty, and renamed the ninth year of Tianfu as the first year of Kaiyun. Every prefecture in Hebei that had suffered Khitan raids was exempted from the autumn tax for that year. Officers and soldiers of every rank received preferential grants according to their standing. Wherever prefectures had imposed forced loans of money and silk, collection was to stop the moment the amnesty edict arrived; contributors of a thousand guan or more were exempted from corvée duties, and those of ten thousand guan or more were appointed to senior assistant offices in their home prefecture. That day, before the amnesty could be fully proclaimed, a violent thunderstorm broke out and the ceremony was cut short in haste. The tremors killed several hundred people in the capital; at the Mingde Gate the head of a stone dragon fell away, and those versed in such signs said that stone was the dynasty's surname—a portent of the gravest ill fortune. On guiyou, Ma Quanjie, military governor of Dingzhou, was made garrison commander of Yedu and additionally appointed Vice Director of the Secretariat; An Shenhui, military governor of Zhaoyi, was transferred to Xingzhou as military governor and given the acting rank of Grand Preceptor. On yihai, Wang Zhou, former military governor of Shanzhou, was promoted to acting Grand Commandant and made military governor of Dingzhou; Zhang Cong'en, garrison commander of Yedu, was reassigned as military governor of Yanzhou. The ritual officials reported: "After the emperor completes three years of mourning, he should perform the di sacrifice in the Imperial Ancestral Temple. Mourning for the Sagely Emperor Gaozu, the Filial and Martial Emperor of Illustrious Virtue, ends this eighth month; the great di rite should be held in the tenth month, and the winter seasonal sacrifices should be changed from jian to di." The emperor approved. On dingchou, Tao Gu, Vice Director of the Waterways Bureau and edict drafter, became Director of the Granaries Bureau while retaining his drafting post; Wu Deqian, Director of the Court of Judicial Review, retired with the title Director of the Palace Library. On xinsi, Li Congmin, commander of the Left Dragon Martial Army, was appointed military governor of Luzhou; Ma Xigao, chief commander of the Heavenly Strategies Office, military governor of Guizhou, and overseer of military affairs at Langzhou, was promoted to acting Grand Preceptor. On renwu, Jinzhou was downgraded to a defense command and Laizhou to a prefectural command. Tian Min, Vice Director of the Revenue Bureau, became Vice Director of the Military Bureau; Li Xiang, Vice Director of Punishments, was appointed Right Vice Director of the State Secretariat; Feng Yu, regimental training commissioner of Yingzhou, was made Vice Director of the Revenue Bureau and appointed academician of the Duanming Hall; Zhao Shangjiao, Secretariat Drafter, was transferred to Vice Director of Punishments. On jichou, Sang Weihan, Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs and Director of the Secretariat, was made Grand Academician of the Hongwen Hall; Liu Gou, Grand Tutor of the Heir and Duke of Qiao, was appointed acting Minister of Works with concurrent Vice Director of the Chancellery, Grand Councillor, Supervisor of the National History, and Commissioner of the Three Offices; the chief councillors Li Song and He Ning were advanced in rank and fief. On gengyin, Liu Jixun moved from the Northern to the Southern Bureau of the Palace Directorate; Dong Yu, commissioner of the Three Offices, took over the Northern Bureau. On xinmao, Shi Yun, former military governor of Shanzhou, was made military governor of Dengzhou. Li Chengfu, military governor of Tongzhou, died and was posthumously honored as Grand Tutor.
2
八月辛丑，命十五將以禦契丹， 〈 （《東都事略·範質傳》：晉出帝命十五將出征。 是夕，質宿直，出帝命諸學士分草制，質曰：「宮城已閉，慮泄機事。 」遂獨為之。）〉 北京留守劉知遠充北面行營都統，鎮州節度使杜威充北面行營都招討使，鄆州節度使張從恩充馬步軍都監，西京留守景延廣充馬步軍都排陣使，徐州節度使趙在禮充馬步軍都虞候，晉州節度使安叔千充馬步軍左廂排陣使，前兗州節度使安審信充馬軍右廂排陣使，河中節度使安審琦充馬步軍都指揮使，河陽節度使符彥卿充馬軍左廂都指揮使，滑州節度使皇甫遇充馬步軍右廂都指揮使，右神武統軍張彥澤充馬軍排陣使，滄州節度使王廷胤充馬軍左廂都指揮使，陜州節度使宋彥筠充步軍右廂都指揮使，前金州節度使田武充步軍左廂排陣使，左神武統軍潘環充步軍右廂排陣使。 壬寅，閩王王延羲為其下連重遇、朱文進所害，眾推文進知留後事，稱天福年號，間道以聞。 甲辰，太子少傅盧文紀改太子太傅，太子少保李麟改太子太保，刑部尚書李懌改戶部尚書，給事中司徒詡改右散騎常侍，以府州刺史折從阮為安北都護，充振武節度使。 是夜，熒惑入南斗。 乙巳，詔復置明經、童子二科。 己酉，以鄧州節度使王令溫為延州節度使。 癸丑，以威武軍兵馬留後、權知閩國事朱文進為檢校太傅、福州威武軍節度使，知閩國事。 癸亥，升澶州為節鎮，以鎮寧為軍額，割濮州為屬郡。 甲子，以延州節度使史威為澶州節度使。
On the twenty-first day of the eighth month, fifteen generals were appointed to resist the Khitan, [ (From the 《Brief Account of the Eastern Capital, Biography of Fan Zhi》: the Deposed Emperor of Jin appointed fifteen generals to take the field. That evening Fan was on palace night duty. The Deposed Emperor told the academicians to divide the edicts among themselves, but Fan said, "The palace gates are already shut—we must not let military plans leak out. " So he drafted them all himself.)〉 Liu Zhiyuan, garrison commander of the Northern Capital, became overall commander of the Northern Campaign Headquarters; Du Wei of Zhenzhou became chief suppression commander; Zhang Cong'en of Yanzhou became overall inspector of the combined armies; Jing Yanguang of the Western Capital became chief array marshal; Zhao Zaili of Xuzhou became chief adjutant; An Shuqian of Jinzhou became left-wing array marshal; the former Yanzhou governor An Shenxin became right-wing cavalry array marshal; An Shenqi of Hezhong became overall commander of horse and foot; Fu Yanqing of Heyang became left-wing cavalry commander; Huangfu Yu of Huazhou became right-wing commander; Zhang Yanzhe, Right Divine Martial Commander, became cavalry array marshal; Wang Tingyin of Cangzhou became left-wing cavalry commander; Song Yanyun of Shanzhou became right-wing infantry commander; the former Jinzhou governor Tian Wu became left-wing infantry array marshal; and Pan Huan, Left Divine Martial Commander, became right-wing infantry array marshal. On renyin, the King of Min, Wang Yanxi, was murdered by his officers Lian Chongyu and Zhu Wenjin; the troops acclaimed Wenjin to handle garrison affairs, adopted the Tianfu reign title, and sent word through a clandestine courier route. On jiachen, Lu Wenji advanced from Junior to Grand Tutor of the Heir; Li Lin from Junior to Grand Guardian; Li Yin moved from Director of Punishments to Director of Revenue; Situ Xu became Right Regular Attendant of the Cavalry; and Zhe Congruan, prefect of Fuzhou, was appointed Protector-General of the Pacified North and military governor of Zhenwu. That night Mars entered the constellation of the Southern Dipper. On yisi, an edict reinstated the Mingjing and Tongzi civil-service examinations. On jiyou, Wang Lingwen, military governor of Dengzhou, was transferred to Yanzhou as military governor. On guichou, Zhu Wenjin, acting commander of the Weiwu Army and provisional overseer of Min, was promoted to acting Grand Tutor, made military governor of the Weiwu Army at Fuzhou, and confirmed as overseer of Min affairs. On guihai, Cangzhou was raised to a full military commission under the designation Zhenning, with Puzhou carved out as its subordinate prefecture. On jiazi, Shi Wei, military governor of Yanzhou, was made military governor of Cangzhou.
3
九月庚午朔，日有蝕之。 乙酉，以戶部侍郎韋勛為太子賓客，以前棣州刺史段希堯為戶部侍郎，以光祿卿張仁願為大理卿。 己丑，禮部侍郎符蒙卒。 壬辰，太原奏，代州刺史白文珂破契丹於七里烽，斬首千餘級，生擒將校七十餘人。 癸巳，以前隴州防禦使翟光鄴為宣徽北院使。 己亥，以滄州節度使王廷胤卒輟朝，贈中書令。
On the first day of the ninth month, the sun was eclipsed. On yiyou, Wei Xun, Vice Director of Revenue, became Mentor of the Heir; the former Di prefect Duan Xiyao was made Vice Director of Revenue; and Zhang Renyuan, Director of the Imperial Household, was appointed Director of the Court of Judicial Review. On jichou, Fu Meng, Vice Director of Rites, died. On renchen, Taiyuan reported that Bai Wenke, prefect of Daizhou, had routed the Khitan at Qili Beacon, taking more than a thousand heads and seventy-odd officers alive. On guisi, Zhai Guangye, former defense commissioner of Longzhou, was made commissioner of the Northern Bureau of the Palace Directorate. On jihai, court was suspended upon the death of Wang Tingyin, military governor of Cangzhou, who was posthumously honored as Director of the Secretariat.
4
冬十月壬寅，兩浙節度使、吳越國王錢宏佐加守太尉。 庚戌，以徐州節度使、北面行營馬步都虞候趙在禮為北面行營副都統，鄴都留守馬全節為北面行營副招討使。 甲寅，以起居郎、知制誥賈緯為戶部郎中、知制誥。 戊午，詔曰：
In the tenth month of winter, on renyin day, Qian Hongzuo, military governor of the Two Zhes and King of Wuyue, was promoted to acting Grand Commandant. On gengxu, Zhao Zaili of Xuzhou, chief adjutant of the Northern Campaign armies, became deputy overall commander; Ma Quanjie, garrison commander of Yedu, became deputy chief suppression commander. On jiayin, Jia Wei, Attendant of the Imperial Diary and edict drafter, became Director of the Revenue Bureau while retaining his drafting duties. On wuwu, an edict proclaimed:
5
朕虔承顧命，獲嗣丕基。 常懼顛危，不克負荷，宵分日昃，罔敢怠寧，夕惕晨興，每懷祗畏。 但以恩信未著，德教未敷，理道不明，咎征斯至。
I reverently received my predecessor's final charge and succeeded to the great foundation of the state. I live in constant fear that the realm may founder and that I cannot bear its weight; from midnight until the sun sets I dare not rest; evening vigilance and dawn rising are my daily practice, and reverent awe is always in my heart. Yet because kindness and trust have not been made plain, virtue and instruction have not reached the people, the principles of governance remain unclear, and signs of blame have appeared.
6
向者，頻年災沴，稼穡不登，道堇相望，上天垂譴，涼德所招。 仍屬干戈尚興，邊陲多事。 倉廩不足，則輟人之糇食； 帑藏不足，則率人之資財； 兵士不足，則取人之丁中； 戰騎不足，則假人之乘馬。 雖事不獲已，而理將若何！ 訪聞差去使臣，殊乖體認，不有敦於勉諭，而乃臨以威刑，自有所聞，益深愧悼。 旋屬守臣叛命，敵騎入邊，致使甲兵不暇休息，軍旅有征戰之苦，人民有飛免之勞，疲瘵未蘇，科徭尚急，言念於茲，寢食何安！ 得不省過興懷，側身罪已，載深減損，思召和平？ 所宜去無用之資，罷不急之務，棄華取實，惜費省功，一則符先帝慈儉之規，一則慕前王樸素之德。
In recent years disasters and pestilence have come in succession, harvests have failed, and the roads are lined with those who starved—Heaven's reproof, all drawn down by my own deficient virtue. Moreover, war still rages and the frontiers are in constant turmoil. When the granaries run short, we cut off the people's grain; when the treasury is empty, we levy the people's wealth; when troops are lacking, we draft men from the adult registers; when war horses fall short, we press the people's mounts into service. Though these measures could not be avoided, what has become of right principle! I have learned that the envoys we sent out have grossly misunderstood their charge: rather than earnestly encouraging and instructing the people, they have confronted them with harsh punishments. Since this came to my ears, my shame and grief have only deepened. Soon border commanders turned rebel and enemy horsemen crossed the frontier, leaving our troops no respite; the armies bore the hardships of campaign, the people the labor of frantic flight. The sick and exhausted had scarcely recovered, yet corvée demands remained pressing—at the thought of this, how can I eat or sleep in peace! Should I not examine my faults, turn inward in self-reproach, cut expenditures to the bone, and strive to restore peace? We should strip away useless spending, suspend nonessential projects, choose substance over ornament, and husband resources while reducing labor—both to honor the late emperor's rule of kindness and frugality and to emulate the plain virtue of kings of old.
7
向者，造作軍器，破用稍多，但取堅剛，不須華楚； 今後作坊制器械，不得更用金銀裝飾。 比於遊畋，素非所好，凡諸服禦，尤欲去奢，應天下府州不得以珍寶玩好及鷹犬為貢。 在昔聖帝明君，無非惡衣菲食，況於薄德，所合恭行，今後大官尚膳，減去多品，衣服帷帳，務去華飾，在禦寒溫而已。 峻宇雕墻，昔人所誡，玉杯象箸，前代攸非，今後凡有營繕之處，丹堊雕鏤，不得過度，宮闈之內，有非理費用，一切禁止。
Recently the manufacture of military equipment has cost too much; what is required is strength and durability, not ornament and display; from now on workshops producing arms and gear must not use gold or silver decoration. Hunting has never been to my taste; for all court dress and equipage I especially wish to cut extravagance; no prefecture or district in the realm may present precious objects, curios, hawks, or hounds as tribute. Sage emperors and enlightened rulers of old all wore coarse clothes and ate plain food; how much more should one of my slight virtue do the same. Henceforth the Imperial Kitchen shall cut back its many dishes; clothing and hangings must shed ornament and serve only to keep off cold and heat. Lofty halls and carved walls were condemned by the ancients, jade cups and ivory chopsticks by former ages. Henceforth all construction must limit cinnabar plaster and carved ornament; within the palace any unreasonable expense is forbidden altogether.
8
於戲！ 繼聖承祧，握樞臨極，昧於至道，若履春冰。 屬以天災流行，國步多梗，因時致懼，引咎推誠，期於將來，庶幾有補。 更賴王公、將相，貴戚、豪宗，各啟乃心，率由茲道，共臻富庶，以致康寧。 凡百臣僚，宜體朕意。
Alas! Having inherited the sacred line and received the ancestral temple, I hold the reins at the summit of power yet am ignorant of the supreme Way, as though walking on spring ice. Heaven's disasters are abroad and the state's path is beset with obstacles; in this season of peril I feel fear, take blame upon myself, and offer sincerity, hoping that the future may yet see some remedy. I further look to princes and dukes, generals and ministers, noble kin and great clans to open your hearts, follow this path together, bring the realm to prosperity, and secure peace and tranquility. Let every official take my meaning to heart.
9
十一月壬申，詔曰：「蕃寇未平，邊陲多事，即日雖無侵軼，亦須廣設提防。 朕將親率虎貔，躬擐甲胄，候聞南牧，即便北征，不須先定日辰，別行告諭。 所有供億，宜令三司預行計度，合隨從諸司職員，並宜常備行計」云。 己卯，以陳州刺史梁漢璋充侍衛馬軍都指揮使。 壬午，以貝州節度使何建為澶州節度使兼北面行營馬軍右廂排陣使，以澶州節度使史威為貝州節度使。 丙戌，以前金州節度使田武為滄州節度使兼北面行營步軍右廂都指揮使，以前相州節度使郭謹為鄜州節度使。
On renshen day of the eleventh month, an edict stated: "The barbarian foe is not yet subdued and the frontiers remain troubled; though there is no raid at this moment, defenses must still be widely prepared. I shall personally lead the tiger guards and don armor myself; when word comes that they are driving south, I shall march north at once—there is no need to fix a date in advance or issue separate notices. All supplies should be calculated in advance by the Three Offices, and every official of the departments that will accompany the campaign should keep travel arrangements in constant readiness." Thus it ran. On jimao, Liang Hanzhang, prefect of Chenzhou, was made overall commander of the Palace Guard cavalry. On renwu, He Jian of Beizhou became military governor of Cangzhou and right-wing cavalry array marshal of the Northern Campaign; Shi Wei of Cangzhou was transferred to Beizhou as military governor. On bingxu, the former Jinzhou governor Tian Wu became military governor of Cangzhou and right-wing infantry commander of the Northern Campaign; the former Xiangzhou governor Guo Jin was made military governor of Fuzhou.
10
十二月己亥朔，幸臯門，射中白兔。 癸丑，福州節度使朱文進加同平章事，封閩國王。 丁巳，青州楊光遠降。 光遠子承勛等斬觀察判官邱濤、牙將白延祚、楊贍、杜延壽等首級，送於招討使李守貞，乃縱火大噪，劫其父處於私第，以城納款，遣即墨縣令王德柔貢表待罪。 楊光遠亦遣節度判官楊麟奉表請死。 詔釋之。
On the first day of the twelfth month, the emperor visited Gao Gate and shot a white hare. On guichou, Zhu Wenjin, military governor of Fuzhou, was made Grand Councillor and enfeoffed as King of Min. On dingsi, Yang Guangyuan of Qingzhou surrendered. Guangyuan's sons Chengxun and the others beheaded the surveillance commissioner Qiu Tao and the military officers Bai Yanzuo, Yang Zhan, and Du Yanshou, sending their heads to the suppression commander Li Shouzhen; then they set fires, raised a great uproar, seized their father and held him in a private residence, surrendered the city, and dispatched Jimo magistrate Wang Derou to present a memorial awaiting judgment. Yang Guangyuan also sent his commissioner Yang Lin with a memorial asking to be put to death. An edict pardoned him.

閏月庚午，以楊承信為右羽林將軍，承祚為右驍騎衛將軍。 皆光遠之子，先詣闕請罪，故特授是官。 癸酉，李守貞奏，楊光遠卒。 初，光遠既上表送降，帝以光遠頃歲太原歸命，欲曲全之，議者曰：「豈有反狀滔天而赦之也！ 」乃命守貞便宜處置。 守貞遣人拉殺之，以病卒聞。 乙酉，前登州刺史張萬迪削奪官爵處斬，青州節度判官楊麟配流威州，掌書記任邈配流原州，支使徐晏配流武州。 縱逢恩赦，不在放還之限，並以楊光遠叛故也。 工部尚書、權知貢舉竇貞固奏：「試進士諸科舉人入策，舊例夜試，以三條燭盡為限，天成二年改令晝試，今欲依舊夜試。 」從之。 曲赦青州管內罪人，立功將士各賜優給，青州吏民為楊光遠詿誤者，一切不問。 青州行營招討使、兗州節度使兼侍衛都虞候李守貞加同平章事，副招討使、河陽節度使符彥卿改許州節度使。 丙戌，降青州為防禦使額，以萊州刺史楊承勛為汝州防禦使。 己丑，以工部尚書竇貞固為禮部尚書，太常卿王延為工部尚書，左丞王松為太常卿，以前尚書右丞龍敏為尚書左丞。 癸巳，以前安州防禦使李建崇為河陽兵馬留後，以宣徽使翟光鄴為青州防禦使，以內客省使李彥韜為宣徽北院使。 甲午，以給事中邊光範為左散騎常侍，以樞密直學士、吏部郎中李穀為給事中，依前充職。 是月，契丹耶律德光與趙延壽領全軍入寇，圍恒州，分兵陷鼓城、槁城、元氏、高邑、昭慶、寧晉、蒲澤、欒城、柏鄉等縣，前鋒至邢州，河北諸州告急。 詔張從恩、馬全節、安審琦率師屯邢州，趙在禮屯鄴都。
In the intercalary month, on gengwu day, Yang Chengxin was appointed General of the Right Forest of Feathers Army and Chengzuo General of the Right Valiant Cavalry Guard. Both were Guangyuan's sons; because they had come to court first to plead guilty, they were specially granted these posts. On guiyou, Li Shouzhen reported that Yang Guangyuan had died. At first, after Guangyuan had submitted his surrender memorial, the emperor wished to spare him because he had only recently submitted from Taiyuan; but advisers said, "How can treason of such magnitude be pardoned! The emperor then ordered Shouzhen to handle the matter as he saw fit. Shouzhen sent men to strangle him and reported that he had died of illness. On yiyou, Zhang Wandi, former prefect of Dengzhou, was stripped of rank and executed; Yang Lin, Qingzhou commissioner, was banished to Weizhou; secretary Ren Miao to Yuanzhou; and staff officer Xu Yan to Wuzhou. Even if an amnesty were proclaimed, they would not be eligible for release—all on account of Yang Guangyuan's rebellion. Dou Zhengu, Director of Works and acting overseer of the examinations, reported: "For jinshi candidates and examinees in other categories taking the policy examination, the old practice was a night session limited by three candles; in Tiancheng 2 this was changed to daytime testing; we now ask to restore the night examination." The emperor approved. A partial amnesty was proclaimed for criminals in Qingzhou; meritorious troops received preferential grants; and officials and commoners of Qingzhou who had been misled by Yang Guangyuan were not prosecuted at all. Li Shouzhen, suppression commander at Qingzhou, military governor of Yanzhou, and chief adjutant of the Palace Guard, was made Grand Councillor; Fu Yanqing, deputy suppression commander and military governor of Heyang, was transferred to Xuzhou as military governor. On bingxu, Qingzhou was downgraded to a defense command, and Yang Chengxun, prefect of Laizhou, was made defense commissioner of Ruzhou. On jichou, Dou Zhengu moved from Director of Works to Director of Rites; Wang Yan from Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices to Director of Works; Wang Song from Left Vice Director to Director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices; and the former Right Vice Director Long Min became Left Vice Director of the Secretariat. On guisi, Li Jianchong, former defense commissioner of Anzhou, became acting commander of Heyang forces; Zhai Guangye, Palace Directorate commissioner, was made defense commissioner of Qingzhou; and Li Yantao, commissioner of the Inner Guests Bureau, took the Northern Bureau of the Palace Directorate. On jiawu, Bian Guangfan, Supervising Secretary, became Left Regular Attendant of the Cavalry; Li Gu, Direct Academician of the Bureau of Military Affairs and Director of the Personnel Bureau, was made Supervising Secretary while retaining his former duties. That month the Khitan ruler Yelü Deguang and Zhao Yanshou led the full army in invasion, besieged Hengzhou, and sent detachments to seize Gucheng, Gaocheng, Yuanshi, Gaoyi, Zhaoqing, Ningjin, Puzhe, Luancheng, Baixiang, and other counties; the vanguard reached Xingzhou, and every prefecture in Hebei sent urgent alarms. The court ordered Zhang Cong'en, Ma Quanjie, and An Shenqi to take their armies to Xingzhou, while Zhao Zaili held Yedu.

開運二年春正月戊戌朔，帝不受朝賀，不豫故也。 己亥，張從恩部領兵士自邢州退至相州，人情震恐。 趙在禮還屯澶州，馬全節歸鄴都，遣右神武統軍張彥澤屯黎陽，詔西京留守景延廣將兵守胡梁渡。 契丹寇邢州。 侍衛馬軍都指揮使梁漢璋改鄭州防禦使，典軍如故。 以齊州防禦使劉在明為相州留後。 癸卯，以客省使孟承誨為內客省使。 滑州奏，今月二日至四日，相州路烽火不至。 甲辰，以前汝州防禦使宋光鄴為左驍衛大將軍。 詔青州行營將校，自副兵馬使以上，各賜功臣名號。 乙巳，帝復常膳。 以左武衛上將軍袁{山義}為客省使，上將軍如故。 詔滑州節度使皇甫遇率兵赴邢州，馬全節赴相州。 契丹寇洺、磁，犯鄴都西北界，所在告急。 壬子，王師與契丹相拒於相州北安陽河上，皇甫遇、慕容彥超率前鋒與敵騎戰於榆林店，遇馬中流矢，僅而獲免。 〈 （《遼史》：皇甫遇與濮州刺史慕容彥超將兵千騎，來覘遼軍。 至鄴都，遇遼軍數萬，且戰且卻，至榆林店，遼軍繼至，遇與彥超力戰百餘合，遇馬斃步戰，安審琦引騎兵逾水以救，遼軍乃還。）〉 是夜，張從恩引軍退保黎陽，唯留五百人守安陽河橋。 既而知州符彥倫與軍校謀曰：「此夜紛紜，人無固誌，五百疲兵，安能守橋！ 」即抽入相州，嬰城為備。 至曙，賊軍萬餘騎已陣於安陽河北，彥倫令城上揚旗鼓噪，賊不之測。 至辰時，渡河而南，悉陣甲騎於城下，如攻城之狀。 彥倫曰：「此敵將走矣。 」乃出甲士五百於城北，張弓弩以待之，契丹果引去。 當皇甫遇榆林戰時，至晚敵眾自相驚曰：「晉軍悉至矣。 」戎王在邯鄲聞之，即時北遁，官軍亦南保黎陽。 甲寅，以河陽留後李建崇為邢州留後，以鳳州防禦使 〈 （案：此下有闕文。）〉 為河陽留後。 詔李守貞領兵屯滑州，以宣徽北院使李彥韜權侍衛馬步都虞候。 改諸道武定軍為天威軍。 己未，以前許州節度使李從溫為北面行營都招撫使，以鄆州節度使張從恩權東京留守。 辛酉，相州奏，契丹抽退，其鄉村避寇百姓，已發遣各歸本家營種。 初，帝以不豫初平，未任親禦軍旅，既而張從恩、馬全節相次奏賊軍充斥，恒州杜威告事勢危急，帝曰：「北敵未平，固難安寢，當悉眾一戰，以救朔方生靈。 若宴安遲疑，則大河以北，淪為寇壤矣。 」即日命諸將點閱，以定行計。 辛酉，下詔親征。 誅楊光遠部下指揮使張迥等五人，以戎事方興，慮其扇搖故也。 癸亥，以樞密直學士李穀為三司副使，判留司三司公事。 乙丑，車駕發離京師。 是月，京城北壕春冰之上有文，若大樹花葉，凡數十株，宛若圖畫，觀者如堵。
On the first day of the first month in spring of the second year of Kaiyun, the emperor declined the New Year court audience on account of illness. On jihai, Zhang Cong'en withdrew his troops from Xingzhou to Xiangzhou, spreading panic among the people. Zhao Zaili pulled back to Chazhou, Ma Quanjie to Yedu; Zhang Yanze of the Right Divine Guard was posted at Liyang; and Jing Yanguang, garrison commander of Luoyang, was ordered to hold Huliang Ford. Khitan forces attacked Xingzhou. Liang Hanzhang, commanding general of the Palace Guard cavalry, was made defense commissioner of Zhengzhou while retaining command of his troops. Liu Zaiming, defense commissioner of Qizhou, was appointed acting commander of Xiangzhou. On guimao, Meng Chenghui moved from the Guests Bureau to the Inner Guests Bureau. Huazhou reported that from the second through the fourth of the month, no beacon signals came from the Xiangzhou road. On jiachen, Song Guangye, former defense commissioner of Ruzhou, was appointed grand general of the Left Martial Guard. The court awarded meritorious service titles to every Qingzhou field officer from deputy troop commander up. On yisi, the emperor returned to his normal diet. Yuan [uncertain character], senior general of the Left Martial Guard, became commissioner of the Guests Bureau while retaining his rank. Huangfu Yu, military governor of Huazhou, was ordered to Xingzhou with his troops, and Ma Quanjie to Xiangzhou. Khitan raiders struck Ming and Ci, crossed into the northwestern border of Yedu, and alarms rang out on every side. On renzi the imperial army and the Khitan confronted each other on the Anyang River north of Xiangzhou. Huangfu Yu and Murong Yanchao led the vanguard against enemy cavalry at Yulin Inn; Yu's horse took an arrow and he barely escaped with his life. [ (From the 《History of Liao》: Huangfu Yu and Murong Yanchao, prefect of Puzhou, led a thousand horsemen to scout the Liao forces. Near Yedu they met tens of thousands of Liao troops and fought while falling back. At Yulin Inn more Liao forces arrived; Yu and Yanchao battled fiercely for more than a hundred engagements. When Yu's horse was killed he fought on foot until An Shenqi led cavalry across the river to his aid, whereupon the Liao forces withdrew.)〉 That night Zhang Cong'en withdrew to Liyang, leaving only five hundred men to hold the Anyang River bridge. Presently the prefect Fu Yanlun consulted his officers: "Tonight everything is in turmoil and morale is unsettled—how can five hundred exhausted men hold a bridge! " They at once withdrew into Xiangzhou and shut the gates to prepare a defense. By dawn more than ten thousand enemy horsemen had formed up north of the Anyang River. Yanlun had banners raised and drums beaten on the walls with loud shouting, and the enemy could not tell what they faced. Around mid-morning they crossed south and arrayed armored cavalry beneath the walls as though ready to assault the city. Yanlun said, "The enemy is about to withdraw. " He then sent five hundred armored troops out north of the city with bows and crossbows drawn, and the Khitan duly withdrew. During Huangfu Yu's battle at Yulin, by evening the enemy camp panicked among themselves, crying, "All the Jin armies have come! " The Khitan ruler at Handan heard this and at once fled north, while the imperial army also withdrew south to Liyang. On jiayin, Li Jianchong, acting commander of Heyang, was appointed acting commander of Xingzhou, and the Fengzhou defense commissioner [ (Note: text is missing here.)〉 was appointed acting commander of Heyang. Li Shouzhen was ordered to Huazhou with his troops, and Li Yantao of the Northern Bureau of the Palace Directorate was made acting chief adjutant of the Palace Guard. The Wuding Army units on every circuit were redesignated the Tianwei Army. On jiwei, Li Congwen, former military governor of Xuzhou, became overall suppression commissioner of the northern field army, and Zhang Cong'en, military governor of Yanzhou, acting garrison commander of the Eastern Capital. On xinyou Xiangzhou reported that the Khitan had pulled back and that villagers who had fled the raids were being sent home to resume planting. At first, with his illness only recently recovered, the emperor was not yet fit to take the field in person. Then Zhang Cong'en and Ma Quanjie reported in turn that enemy armies were everywhere, and Du Wei of Hengzhou warned that the situation was desperate. The emperor said, "While the northern foe remains undefeated I cannot rest. We must commit our full strength in one battle to save the people of the north. If we linger in ease and hesitation, everything north of the Yellow River will fall to the invaders. " That same day he ordered the generals to review the troops and fix the expedition plan. On xinyou the court issued an edict announcing the emperor's personal campaign. Five commanders under Yang Guangyuan, including Zhang Jiong, were executed, lest they stir up trouble while the war was under way. On guihai, Li Gu, Direct Academician of the Bureau of Military Affairs, was made deputy commissioner of the Three Departments and charged with Three Departments affairs at the capital. On yichou the emperor left the capital. That month patterns like flowering trees appeared on the spring ice of the capital's northern moat—several dozen of them, as vivid as paintings—and crowds of onlookers packed the banks.

二月戊辰朔，車駕次滑州。 己巳，渡浮橋，幸黎陽勞軍，至晚還滑州。 以滄州節度使田武充東北面行營都部署。 甲戌，幸澶州，以景延廣為隨駕馬步軍都鈐轄。 丙子，大閱諸軍於戚城，帝親臨之。 戊寅，北面行營副招討使馬全節、行營都監李守貞、右神武統軍張彥澤等以前軍先發。 己卯，以許州節度使符彥卿為北面行營馬軍都指揮使，以左神武統軍潘環為北面行營步軍都指揮使。 辛巳，幸楊村故壘。 符彥卿、皇甫遇、李殷率諸軍進發。 以左散騎常侍邊光範為樞密直學士。 詔河北諸州，應蕃騎經由之地，吏民遭殺害者，委所在收瘞，量事祭奠。 詔恒州杜威與馬全節等會合進軍。 丙戌，幸鐵丘閱馬，因幸趙在禮、李從溫軍。 是日大雪。 戊子，安審琦、梁漢璋領兵北征。 府州防禦使折從阮奏，部領兵士攻圍契丹勝州，降之，見進兵趨朔州。 甲午，以河中節度使安審琦為北面行營馬步軍都虞候，許州節度使符彥卿充馬步軍左廂都指揮使，滑州節度使皇甫遇充馬步軍右廂都指揮使，侍衛馬軍都指揮使梁漢璋充馬軍左右廂都指揮使，侍衛步軍都指揮使李殷充步軍左右廂都指揮使，左神武統軍張彥澤充馬軍左右廂都排陣使，右神武統軍潘環充步軍左右廂排陣使。 丙申，以端明殿學士、尚書戶部侍郎馮玉為戶部尚書，充樞密使。
On the first day of the second month the emperor stopped at Huazhou. On jisi he crossed the pontoon bridge, went to Liyang to review the troops, and returned to Huazhou that evening. Tian Wu, military governor of Cangzhou, was appointed overall commander of the northeastern field army. On jiaxu he went to Chazhou and made Jing Yanguang overall commander of the horse and foot troops accompanying the imperial progress. On bingzi all armies were reviewed at Qicheng under the emperor's personal supervision. On wuyin Ma Quanjie, deputy suppression commissioner of the northern field army, Li Shouzhen, field superintendent, Zhang Yanze of the Right Divine Guard, and others marched out with the vanguard. On jimao Fu Yanqing, military governor of Xuzhou, became commanding general of the northern field cavalry, and Pan Huan of the Left Divine Guard commanding general of the northern field infantry. On xinsi the emperor visited the old camp at Yang Village. Fu Yanqing, Huangfu Yu, and Li Yin marched the armies forward. Bian Guangfan, Left Regular Attendant of the Cavalry, was appointed Direct Academician of the Bureau of Military Affairs. The court ordered every Hebei prefecture where Khitan cavalry had passed to gather and bury officials and commoners who had been killed and to perform appropriate funeral rites. Du Wei of Hengzhou was ordered to link up with Ma Quanjie and the others and advance. On bingxu he reviewed horses at Tieqiu and then inspected the armies of Zhao Zaili and Li Congwen. Zhe Congruan, defense commissioner of Fuzhou, reported that his troops had besieged and captured the Khitan Shengzhou and were now advancing toward Shuozhou. On jiawu An Shenqi, military governor of Hezhong, became chief adjutant of the northern field horse and foot; Fu Yanqing of Xuzhou overall commander of the left wing; Huangfu Yu of Huazhou overall commander of the right wing; Liang Hanzhang, Palace Guard cavalry commander, overall commander of both cavalry wings; Li Yin, Palace Guard infantry commander, overall commander of both infantry wings; Zhang Yanze of the Left Divine Guard battle-array commander of both cavalry wings; and Pan Huan of the Right Divine Guard battle-array commander of both infantry wings. On bingshen Feng Yu, Hanlin Academician of the Duanming Hall and Vice Minister of Revenue, became Minister of Revenue and Chief of the Bureau of Military Affairs.

三月戊戌，契丹陷祁州，刺史沈斌死之。 乙巳，左補闕袁範先陷契丹，自賊中逃歸。 杜威奏，與李守貞、馬全節、安審琦、皇甫遇部領大軍赴定州。 易州刺史安審約奏，二月三日夜，差壯丁斫敵營，殺敵十餘人。 是日，以符彥卿為北面行營馬步軍左右廂都排陣使，以皇甫遇為北面行營馬步軍左廂排陣使，以王周為馬步軍右廂排陣使。 丁未，畋於戚城，還幸景延廣、安審信軍。 庚戌，王師攻泰州，刺史晉庭謙以城降。 易州奏，郎山塞將孫方簡破契丹千餘人，斬蕃將諧裏相公，擄其妻以獻。 甲寅，杜威奏，收復滿城，獲契丹首領沒剌相公，並蕃漢兵士二千人。 以前戶部尚書李懌為兵部尚書。 乙卯，杜威奏，收復遂城。 丙辰奏，大軍自遂城卻退至滿城。 時賊將趙延壽部曲來降，言：「契丹主昨至古北口，幽州走報，漢軍大下，收卻泰州。 尋下令諸部，令輜重入塞，輕騎卻回。 戎王率五萬餘騎，來勢極盛，明日前鋒必至，請為之備。 」杜威、李守貞謀曰：「我師糧運不繼，深入賊疆，而逢大敵，亡之道也。 不如退還泰州，觀其兵勢強弱而禦之。 」軍士皆以為然。 是日，還滿城。 丁巳，至泰州。 戊午，契丹前鋒已至。 己未，大軍發泰州而南，契丹踵其後。 是日，次陽城。 庚申，敵騎如墻而來，我步軍為方陣以禦之。 選勁騎擊賊，鬥二十餘合，南行十餘里，賊勢稍卻，渡白溝而去。 辛酉，杜威召諸將議曰：「戎首自來，實為敵，若不血戰，吾輩何以求免。 」諸將然之。 是日，敵騎還繞官軍，相去數里。 明日，我軍成列而行，蕃漢轉鬥，殺聲震地，才行十餘里，軍中人馬饑乏。 癸亥，大軍至白團衛村下營。 人馬俱渴，營中掘井，及水輒壞，兵士取其泥絞汁而飲。 敵眾圍繞，漸束其營。 〈 （《宋史·藥元福傳》：晉師列方陣，設拒馬為行寨，契丹以奇兵出陣後，斷糧道。）〉 是日，東北風猛，揚塵折樹。 契丹主坐車中謂眾曰：「漢軍盡來，只有此耳，今日並可生擒，然後平定天下。 」令下馬拔鹿角，飛矢雨集。 軍士大呼曰：「招討使何不用軍，而令士卒虛死！ 」諸將鹹請擊之。 杜威曰：「俟風勢稍慢，觀其進退。 」守貞曰：「此風助我也。 彼眾我寡，黑風之內，莫測多少，若候風止，我輩無噍類矣。 」即呼眾軍齊力擊賊。 張彥澤、符彥卿、皇甫遇等率騎奮擊，風勢尤猛，沙塵如夜，敵遂大敗。 〈 （《宋史·符彥卿傳》：時晉師居下風，將戰，弓弩莫施。 彥卿謂張彥澤、皇甫遇曰：「與其束手就擒，曷若死戰，然未必死。 」彥澤然之，遂潛兵尾其後，順風擊之，契丹大敗。 又，《藥元福傳》：守貞與元福謀曰：「軍中饑渴已甚，若候風反出戰，吾屬為虜矣。 彼謂我不能逆風以戰，宜出其不意以擊之，此兵家之奇也。 」元福乃率麾下開拒馬出戰，諸將繼至，契丹大敗。）〉 時步騎齊進，追襲二十餘里。 至陽城東，賊軍稍稍成列，我騎復擊之，乃渡河而去。 守貞曰：「今日危急極矣，幸諸君奮命，吾事獲濟。 兩日以來，人馬渴乏，今吃水之後，腳重難行，速宜收軍定州，保全而還，上策也。 」由是諸將整眾而還。 是時，契丹主坐車中，及敗走，車行十餘里，追兵既急，獲一橐駝，乘之而走。 乙丑，杜威等大軍自定州班師入恒州。
In the third month, on wuxu, the Khitan seized Qizhou; Prefect Shen Bin was killed. On yisi Yuan Fan, Left Remonstrator, who had earlier fallen to the Khitan, escaped from the enemy and returned. Du Wei reported that he was marching a great army toward Dingzhou with Li Shouzhen, Ma Quanjie, An Shenqi, and Huangfu Yu. An Shenyao, prefect of Yizhou, reported that on the third night of the second month he sent strong men to raid the enemy camp and killed more than ten foes. That day Fu Yanqing became battle-array commander of both wings of the northern field horse and foot; Huangfu Yu battle-array commander of the left wing; and Wang Zhou battle-array commander of the right wing. On dingwei he hunted at Qicheng and then inspected the armies of Jing Yanguang and An Shenxin. On gengxu the imperial army besieged Taizhou, and Prefect Jin Tingqian surrendered the city. Yizhou reported that Sun Fangjian, commander at Langshan Pass, routed more than a thousand Khitan, killed the barbarian general Xieli Xianggong, and sent his captured wife as tribute. On jiayin Du Wei reported the recapture of Mancheng, the seizure of the Khitan leader Molie Xianggong, and two thousand barbarian and Han troops. Li Yi, former Minister of Revenue, was appointed Minister of War. On yimao Du Wei reported the recapture of Suicheng. On bingchen came word that the main army had pulled back from Suicheng to Mancheng. At that time followers of the renegade general Zhao Yanshou surrendered and reported, "The Khitan ruler reached Gubei Pass yesterday; urgent word from Youzhou said Han armies were descending in force and had retaken Taizhou. He soon ordered his divisions to send baggage into the passes and turn the light cavalry back. The Khitan ruler is coming with more than fifty thousand horsemen in overwhelming force; the vanguard will arrive tomorrow—please make ready. " Du Wei and Li Shouzhen conferred: "Our supplies cannot keep up; we are deep in enemy country facing a great force—this is the road to ruin. Better to fall back to Taizhou, gauge their strength, and meet them from there. " The troops all agreed. That day the army fell back to Mancheng. On dingsi they reached Taizhou. On wuwei the Khitan vanguard was already on the field. On jiwei the main army marched south from Taizhou with the Khitan close behind. That day they camped at Yangcheng. On gengshen enemy horsemen came on like a wall, and our infantry formed square formations to meet them. Elite cavalry were chosen to attack the enemy. After more than twenty engagements they marched south some ten li; the enemy pressure eased, and they crossed the White Ditch and withdrew. On xinyou Du Wei called the generals together: "The enemy chieftain has come in person; if we do not fight to the blood, how are we to escape? " The generals assented. That day enemy horsemen circled the imperial army, keeping several li away. The next day our army marched in column while barbarian and Han forces traded blows; the battle cries shook the ground, yet after only ten li the troops and horses were starving and spent. On guihai the main army encamped at Bai Tuanwei Village. Men and horses were parched; wells dug in camp collapsed as soon as water was struck, and soldiers squeezed juice from the mud to drink. Enemy masses closed in and steadily tightened the ring around the camp. [ (From the 《History of Song, Biography of Yao Yuanfu》: the Jin army drew up in squares with chevaux-de-frise for a marching camp; the Khitan sent elite troops around behind the formation and severed the supply line.)〉 That day a fierce northeast wind whipped up dust and snapped trees. Seated in his carriage, the Khitan ruler told his followers, "All the Han armies have come, and this is all there is—today we can take them alive and then pacify the realm. " He ordered his men to dismount and uproot the chevaux-de-frise, and arrows fell like rain. The soldiers shouted, "Suppression Commissioner, why will you not fight, yet leave the troops to die in vain! The generals all pressed for an attack. Du Wei said, "Let us wait until the wind eases and see whether they advance or pull back. Shouzhen said, "This wind is on our side. They outnumber us, and in this black wind no one can tell how many they are. If we wait for the wind to die, not one of us will survive. He immediately ordered the whole army to attack the enemy with full force. Zhang Yanzhe, Fu Yanqing, Huangfu Yu, and others led the cavalry in a furious charge. The wind blew harder still, sand and dust turned day to night, and the enemy was routed. [ (From the 《History of Song, Biography of Fu Yanqing》: The Jin army was then on the leeward side, and with battle imminent their bows and crossbows were useless. Yanqing told Zhang Yanzhe and Huangfu Yu, "Better to fight to the death than be taken with hands bound—and we may not die at all. Yanzhe agreed, sent troops quietly in their rear, struck with the wind at their backs, and the Khitan were routed. Also from the 《Biography of Yao Yuanfu》: Shouzhen and Yuanfu plotted together, saying, "The troops are already desperate with hunger and thirst. If we wait for the wind to shift before fighting, we shall all be taken captive. They assume we cannot fight into the wind. We should strike where they do not expect it—that is the classic stratagem of surprise. Yuanfu led his men out through the chevaux-de-frise; the other generals joined him, and the Khitan were routed.)〉 Infantry and cavalry advanced together and pursued the enemy for more than twenty li. East of Yangcheng the enemy slowly re-formed their ranks; our cavalry struck again, and they crossed the river and withdrew. Shouzhen said, "Today brought us to the brink of ruin. Thanks to your desperate fighting, we have been saved. For two days men and horses have gone without water. Now that they have drunk, their legs are heavy and marching is hard. We should withdraw quickly to Dingzhou, preserve the army, and return—that is the best course. The generals then re-formed their troops and marched back. The Khitan ruler had been seated in his carriage. When he fled in defeat, his carriage had gone little more than ten li before the pursuers closed in; he seized a camel, mounted it, and rode away. On yichou, Du Wei and the main army marched back from Dingzhou and entered Hengzhou.
15
夏四月丙子，以車駕將還京，差官往西京告天地宗廟社稷。 辛巳，駕發澶州。 甲申，至京師，曲赦在京禁囚。 己亥，詔鄴都依舊為天雄軍。 庚寅，河東節度使劉知遠封北平王； 恒州節度使杜威加守太傅； 徐州趙在禮移鎮兗州； 宋州節度使兼侍衛親軍馬步都指揮使高行周移鎮鄆州，侍衛如故； 鄴都留守馬全節改天雄軍節度使； 兗州節度使兼侍衛都虞候李守貞移鎮宋州，加檢校太師兼侍衛親軍副指揮使； 河中節度使安審琦加兼侍中，移鎮許州； 許州節度使符彥卿加同平章事，移鎮徐州； 滑州節度使皇甫遇加同平章事。 壬辰，西京留守景延廣加邑封，改功臣； 泰州節度使侯益移鎮河中； 定州節度使王周加檢校太師。
In the fourth month of summer, on bingzi day, with the emperor about to return to the capital, envoys were sent to the Western Capital to announce the return to Heaven and Earth, the ancestral temples, and the altars of soil and grain. On xinsi, the imperial procession left Cangzhou. On jiashen, the emperor reached the capital and granted a partial amnesty to prisoners confined there. On jihai, an edict restored Yedu to its former status as the Tianxiong Army command. On gengyin, Liu Zhiyuan, military governor of Hedong, was enfeoffed as King of Beiping; Du Wei of Hengzhou was promoted to acting Grand Tutor; Zhao Zaili of Xuzhou was transferred to Yanzhou as military governor; Gao Xingzhou of Songzhou, overall commander of the Palace Guard armies, was transferred to Yanzhou as military governor while keeping his guard post; Ma Quanjie, garrison commander of Yedu, became military governor of the Tianxiong Army; Li Shouzhen of Yanzhou, chief adjutant of the Palace Guard, was transferred to Songzhou as military governor, promoted to acting Grand Preceptor, and made deputy commander of the Palace Guard; An Shenqi of Hezhong was additionally appointed Vice Director of the Secretariat and transferred to Xu Prefecture as military governor; Fu Yanqing of Xu Prefecture was made Grand Councillor and transferred to Xuzhou as military governor; Huangfu Yu, military governor of Huazhou, was made Grand Councillor. On renchen, Jing Yanguang, garrison commander of the Western Capital, received an enlarged fief and a new merit-title; Hou Yi of Taizhou was transferred to Hezhong as military governor; Wang Zhou, military governor of Dingzhou, was promoted to acting Grand Preceptor.
