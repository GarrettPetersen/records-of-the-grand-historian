1
豆盧革，祖籍，同州刺史。 父瓚，舒州刺史。 〈 （《宣和書譜》云：失其世係。）〉 革少值亂離，避地鄜、延，轉入中山，王處直禮之，辟於幕下，有奏記之譽。 因牡丹會賦詩，諷處直以桑柘為意，言甚古雅，漸加器仰，轉節度判官。 而理家無法，獨請謁處直，處直慮布政有缺，有所規諫，斂版出迎，乃為嬖人祈軍職矣。
Dou Luge came of a house whose pedigree is lost to the record; a forebear had held the prefecture of Tong. His father Zan had been prefect of Shu Prefecture. ( (The Xuande Calligraphy Catalogue notes that his family line was lost.)) As a young man Ge lived through chaos and exile, taking refuge in Fu and Yan before settling in Zhongshan, where Wang Chuzhi honored him, brought him onto his staff, and he won renown for his memorials. At a peony festival he wrote a poem urging Chuzhi to heed the mulberry and catalpa—the staple crops of the people—in elegantly archaic verse; Chuzhi grew to admire him and made him adjutant to the military governor. Yet he ran his household without order and went alone to see Chuzhi. When Chuzhi, worried about flaws in his administration, tried to counsel him, Ge put away his tablet and came out to greet him—only to ask a military commission for a favorite.
2
天祐末，莊宗將即位，講求輔相，盧質以名家子舉之，征拜行台左丞相。 同光初，拜平章事。 及登廊廟，事多錯亂，至於官階擬議，前後倒置，屢為省郎蕭希甫駿正，革改之，無難色。 莊宗初定汴、洛，革引薦韋說，冀諳事體，與己同功。 說既登庸，復事流品，舉止輕脫，怨歸於革。 又革、說之子俱授拾遺，父子同官，為人所刺，遂改授員外郎。 革請說之子濤為宏文館學士，說請革之子升為集賢學士，交易市恩，有同市井，識者醜之。 革自作相之後，不以進賢勸能為務，唯事修煉，求長生之術； 嘗服丹砂，嘔血數日，垂死而愈。
Near the end of Tianyou, as Zhuangzong prepared to ascend the throne and searched for chief ministers, Lu Zhi nominated him as a scion of a great clan, and he was called to serve as Left Chancellor of the Mobile Secretariat. In the opening year of Tongguang he was made Grand Councillor. Once he reached the council chamber, business was often confused—even rank proposals were put in the wrong order—and Secretariat officer Xiao Xifu had to correct him again and again; Ge changed his drafts without taking offense. When Zhuangzong first secured Bian and Luoyang, Ge recommended Wei Shuo, expecting someone who understood government to share the burden with him. After Shuo took office he again obsessed over pedigree and rank, behaved with careless levity, and public blame settled on Ge. Both men's sons were also made Remonstrance Officials—fathers and sons serving together—which drew sharp criticism, and the appointments were changed to outside-director posts. Ge secured Shuo's son Tao a post at the Hongwen Institute while Shuo secured Ge's son Sheng one at Jixian—bartering patronage like market traders, to the revulsion of anyone with judgment. Once Ge was chief minister himself, he no longer troubled to advance the worthy or encourage ability, but devoted himself to alchemical cultivation in search of immortality; He once ingested cinnabar, spat blood for days, and was near death before he recovered.
3
天成初，將葬莊宗，以革為山陵使。 及木主歸廟，不出私第，專俟旄鎮，數日無耗，為親友促令入朝。 安重誨對眾辱之曰：「山陵使名銜尚在，不候新命，便履公朝，意謂邊人可欺也。 」側目者聞之，思有所中。 初，蕭希甫有正諫之望，革嘗阻之，遂上疏論革與說苟且自容，致君無狀。 復誣其縱田客殺人，冒元亨上第。 遂貶為辰州刺史，仍令所在馳驛發遣。 後鄭玨、任圜等連上三章，請不行後命，乃下製曰：「豆盧革、韋說等，身為輔相，手握權衡，或端坐稱臣，或半笑奏事，於君無禮，舉世寧容。 革則暫委利權，便私俸祿，文武百辟皆從五月起支，父子二人偏自正初給遣。 說則自居重位，全紊大綱。 敘蔭貪榮，亂兒孫於昭穆； 賣官潤屋，換令錄之身名。 醜行疊彰，群情共怒，雖居牧守，示塞非尤。 革可責授費州司戶參軍，說可夷州司戶參軍，皆員外置同正員，並所在馳驛發遣。 」尋貶陵州長流百姓，委長吏常知所在。 天成二年夏，詔令逐處刺史監賜自盡，其骨肉並放逐便。
Early in Tiancheng, when Zhuangzong was to be interred, Ge was appointed commissioner for the imperial tomb. After the spirit tablet was installed in the temple he stayed home, waiting only for a border governorship; when days passed without news, friends pressed him to go to court. An Chonghui shamed him before the assembly: "You still bear the title of Mausoleum Commissioner. You enter the public court without waiting for a new appointment—as if frontier men were fools to be tricked." " Those who had long watched him with envy heard this and looked for a chance to bring him down. Xiao Xifu had once hoped to serve as a forthright remonstrator, but Ge had blocked him; he now memorialized that Ge and Shuo were self-serving survivors who had disgraced their service to the throne. He further charged that Ge had allowed estate tenants to commit murder and had falsely claimed top honors in the Yuanheng examinations. Ge was demoted to prefect of Chen and ordered sent off at once by relay post. Zheng Jue, Ren Huan, and others then submitted three memorials asking that the harsher sentence be set aside; the throne replied by edict: "Dou Luge, Wei Shuo, and their kind held the chief ministry and the scales of power, yet addressed the throne either with stiff formality or with an insolent half-smile—conduct no ruler could abide and no age could excuse." Ge had briefly handed off lucrative power only to seize salaries for himself: every other official was paid from the fifth month, while he and his son drew pay from the first. Shuo, holding high office, had thrown the whole administrative order into confusion. greedily trading inherited privilege for rank, scrambling the proper order of descent in the ancestral halls; selling offices to fatten their households, bartering prefectural and registrar posts for private gain. Their disgrace multiplied and public wrath united; even assigning them prefectures would not bury the outrage. Ge is stripped to Revenue Section adjutant at Fei, Shuo to the same rank at Yi—outside appointments with nominal full status—and both are to be hustled off by relay post. " Soon both were banished as common exiles to Ling, with local authorities ordered to keep constant track of them. In the summer of Tiancheng 2 an edict commanded every prefect to oversee their compelled suicide, and their families were to be exiled forthwith.
4
子升，官至檢校正郎，服金紫，尋亦削奪。 〈 （《寶晉齋法書讚》載豆盧革《田園帖》云：大德欲要一居處，畿甸間舊無田園，鄜州雖有三兩處莊子，緣百姓租佃多年，累有令公大王書請，卻給還人戶，蓋不欲侵奪疲民，兼慮無知之輩，妄有影庇包役云云。 嶽珂曰：此帖乃與僧往還書，其畏強藩避罪罟，蓋栗栗淵冰，然其後卒以故縱田客貶夜郎，正坐所畏，信乎亂邦之不可居也。 是時據鄜乃高萬興，官檢校太師、中書令，封北平王，即革所謂「令公大王」者。 官故梁授，唐命維新，而顓麵正朝者，不能致褫鞶之誅，而反竊貢秉旄之佞，唐之不競，有自來矣。）〉
His son Sheng had risen to Regular Rectifying Gentleman and worn gold and purple insignia, but soon lost both. ( (Baojin Studio's Calligraphy Eulogies preserves Ge's "Estate Letter," in which he writes that he needed a home near the capital but owned no land there; though Fu had a few manors, tenants had farmed them for years and the "Lord Prince" had repeatedly demanded them back, so he returned the holdings rather than prey on exhausted commoners or let the ignorant use false patronage to dodge labor obligations." Yue Ke comments that the letter was exchanged with a monk: Ge's dread of powerful regional lords and of the law's snare was like walking on ice above an abyss—yet he was later exiled to Yelang for indulging estate tenants, punished for the very danger he had tried to evade; a chaotic age, indeed, was no home for an honest man. Fu was then held by Gao Wanxing, Acting Grand Preceptor and Secretariat Director, enfeoffed as Prince of Beiping—the "Lord Prince" Ge meant in the letter. His titles were leftovers from Liang, while Tang had only just renewed the mandate; yet the court that faced him could not strip his honors, and instead took his gifts and the sycophancy of a man clutching a commander's staff—Tang's weakness, it seems, was nothing new.))
5
韋說，福建觀察使岫之子也。 〈 （案：以下有闕文。）〉 莊宗定汴、洛，說與趙光允同製拜平章事。 說性謹重，奉職常不造事端。 時郭崇韜秉政，說等承順而已，政事得失，無所措言。 初，或有言於崇韜，銓選逾濫，選人或取他人出身銜，或取父兄資緒，與令史囊橐罔冒，崇韜乃條奏其事。 其後郊天，行事官數千人，多有告敕偽濫，因定去留，塗毀告身者甚眾，選人號哭都門之外。 議者亦以為積弊累年，一旦澄汰太細，懼失惟新含垢之意。 時說與郭崇韜國列，不能執而止之，頗遭物議。 說之親黨告之，說曰：「此郭漢子之意也。 」及崇韜得罪，說懼流言所鍾，乃令門人左拾遺王鬆、吏部員外郎李慎儀等上疏，云：「崇韜往日專權，不閑故實，塞仕進之門，非獎善之道。 」疏下中書，說等覆奏，深詆崇韜，識者非之。 又有王傪者，能以多岐取事，納賂於說，說以其名犯祖諱，遂改之為「操」，擬官於近甸。 及明宗即位，說常慮身危，每求庇於任圜，常保護之。 說居有井，昔與鄰家共之，因嫌鄙雜，築垣於外。 鄰人訟之，為希甫疏論，以為井有貨財，及案之本人，惟稱有破釜一所，反招虛妄。 初貶敘州刺史，尋責授夷州司戶參軍。
Wei Shuo was the son of Xu, commissioner of Fujian. ( (Note: Text is missing below.)) After Zhuangzong secured Bian and Luoyang, Shuo and Zhao Guangyun were appointed Grand Councillors by the same edict. Shuo was cautious and sober by nature and usually made no trouble in office. Guo Chongtao then dominated government; Shuo and his colleagues only assented and had no voice in policy right or wrong. Someone had told Chongtao that appointments had grown corrupt—candidates using others' credentials or their fathers' seniority while clerks took bribes—and Chongtao memorialized the abuses in detail. Later, at the suburban sacrifice, thousands of ritual officers were found with forged commissions; as keepers and dismissals were decided, credentials were crossed out in great numbers and rejected candidates wept outside the capital gates. Critics argued that years of rot could not be purged in one ruthless sweep without betraying the new reign's promise of forbearance. Shuo then ranked with Guo Chongtao in the state hierarchy yet could not restrain him, and public opinion turned sharply against him. When kinsmen warned him, Shuo said, "This is that Guo fellow's doing." " After Chongtao fell, Shuo feared the backlash would engulf him and had his protégés Wang Song and Li Shenyi memorialize that Chongtao had monopolized power, ignored precedent, and blocked the path of merit—no way to reward the worthy. " When the memorial reached the Secretariat, Shuo and his circle replied with a still harsher attack on Chongtao, which men of judgment condemned. A man named Wang Can, skilled at working every angle, bribed Shuo; Shuo, claiming the name violated a taboo, changed it to Cao and arranged a post for him near the capital. After Mingzong ascended the throne, Shuo lived in fear for his life and repeatedly sought Ren Huan's protection, which Ren usually gave. Shuo's house had a well once shared with neighbors; finding them coarse and intrusive, he walled it off from the street. The neighbors sued; Xifu accused him of hoarding valuables in the well, but when the case was examined Shuo could point only to a broken pot—exposing the charge as fabrication. He was first demoted to prefect of Xu, then reduced to revenue adjutant at Yi.
6
初，說在江陵，與高季興相知，及入中書，亦常通信幣。 自討西蜀，季興請攻峽內，莊宗許之：「如能得三州，俾為屬郡。 」西川既定，季興無尺寸之功。 洎明宗纘承，季興頻請三郡，朝廷不得已而與之。 革、說方在中書，亦預其議。 及季興占據，獨歸其罪，流於合州。 明年夏，詔曰：「陵州、合州長流百姓豆盧革、韋說，頃在先朝，擢居重任，欺公害物，黷貨賣官。 靜惟肇亂之端，更有難容之事，且夔、忠、萬三州，地連巴蜀，路扼荊蠻，藉皇都弭難之初，徇逆帥僭求之勢，罔予視聽，率意割移。 將千之土疆，開通狡穴； 動兩川之兵賦，禦捍經年。 致朕莫遂偃戈，猶煩運策。 近者西方鄴雖復要害，高季興尚固窠巢，增吾旰食之憂，職爾朋奸之計。 而又自居貶所，繼出流言。 苟刑戮之稽時，處忠良於何地？ 宜令逐處刺史監賜自盡。」 〈 （《歐陽史》：說子濤，晉天福初，為尚書膳部員外郎，卒。）〉
While Shuo was at Jiangling he had known Gao Jixing, and after joining the Secretariat he continued to exchange gifts and correspondence. During the western Shu campaign Jixing asked to strike the gorge region; Zhuangzong agreed: "If you take the three prefectures, they shall be yours as subordinate districts." " Shu was pacified, yet Jixing had won not a foot of ground. When Mingzong succeeded the throne, Jixing pressed repeatedly for the three districts, and the court reluctantly yielded. Ge and Shuo were then in the Secretariat and took part in the decision. When Jixing kept the territories, blame fell on them alone and they were exiled to He. The following summer an edict declared: "The exiles Dou Luge at Ling and Wei Shuo at He were lately elevated to high office under the former emperor, yet harmed the realm, preyed on the people, and sold offices for bribes." Worse still were acts that could not be borne: Kui, Zhong, and Wan lie against Ba-Shu and guard the routes into Jing and the south; at the very hour the capital was settling turmoil they indulged a rebel commander's brazen demands, ignored the throne, and casually ceded territory. a thousand li of frontier about to be opened to enemy lairs; forcing the Two Shu to mobilize troops and taxes in defense year after year. so that We could not yet sheathe the sword but still had to scheme for war. Though Ye on the western front has lately been recovered, Jixing still clings to his nest, deepening Our sleepless worry—thanks to your faction's scheming. And from your places of exile you have kept spreading slander. If punishment is long delayed, what place remains for the loyal? Let every prefect oversee their compelled suicide." ( (Ouyang's History notes that Shuo's son Tao served as Vice Director of Provisions under Jin in the early Tianfu era and died.))
7
盧程，唐朝右族。 祖懿，父蘊，曆仕通顯。 程，天復末登進士第，崔魏公領鹽鐵，署為巡官。 昭宗遷洛陽，柳璨陷右族，程避地河朔，客遊燕、趙，或衣道士服，幹謁藩伯，人未知之。 豆盧革客遊中山，依王處直，盧汝弼來太原。 程與革、弼皆朝族知舊，因往來依革，處直禮遇未優，故投於太原； 汝弼因為延譽，莊宗署為推官，尋改支使。 程褊淺無他才，惟矜恃門第，口多是非，篤厚君子尤薄之。
Lu Cheng came of a leading Tang clan. His grandfather Yi and father Yun had both risen to eminent office. Cheng passed the jinshi late in Tianfu; when Cui Yin headed the Salt and Iron Commission he made him a touring officer. When Zhaozong moved the court to Luoyang and Liu Can persecuted the great families, Cheng fled to Hebei, wandered as a guest through Yan and Zhao, sometimes in Taoist dress importuning regional lords, unknown to all. Dou Luge was then a guest in Zhongshan under Wang Chuzhi, and Lu Rubi had come to Taiyuan. Cheng, Ge, and Bi were old friends among the capital elite; Cheng attached himself to Ge, but Chuzhi treated him poorly, so he went to Taiyuan; Rubi recommended him; Zhuangzong made him investigating officer, then staff adjutant. Cheng was shallow and talentless, proud only of birth, and full of gossip; men of real substance despised him.
8
初，判官王緘從軍掌文翰，胡柳之役，緘歿於軍。 莊宗歸寧太原，置酒公宴，舉酒謂張承業曰：「予今於此會取一書記，先以卮酒辟之。 」即舉酒屬巡官馮道，道以所舉非次，抗酒辭避。 莊宗曰：「勿謙挹，無逾於卿也。 」時以職列序遷，則程當為書記，汝弼亦左右之。 程既失職，私懷憤惋，謂人曰：「主上不重人物，使田里兒居餘上。 」先是，莊宗嘗於帳中召程草奏，程曰：「叨忝成名，不閑筆硯。 」由是文翰之選，不及於程。 時張承業專製河東留守事，人皆敬憚。 舊例，支使監諸廩出納，程訴於承業曰：「此事非仆所長，請擇能者。 」承業叱之曰：「公稱文士，即合飛文染翰，以濟霸國，嘗命草辭，自陳短拙，及留職務，又以為辭，公所能者何也？ 」程垂泣謝之。 後曆觀察判官。
Adjutant Wang Jian had managed the army's paperwork; he was killed at Huliu. When Zhuangzong returned to Taiyuan for a family visit he held a public feast and told Zhang Chengye, raising his cup: "Tonight I shall choose a secretary—let this cup announce it." " He turned to touring officer Feng Dao; Dao, saying the choice skipped proper order, refused the cup. Zhuangzong said, "Do not demur—no one is better suited." " By seniority Cheng should have been secretary, and Rubi had been pressing for him. Denied the post, Cheng nursed private resentment and told others, "The ruler cares nothing for talent, setting a village upstart above me." " Earlier Zhuangzong had asked him to draft a memorial in camp; Cheng replied, "I am unworthy of my fame and no hand at writing." " After that he was never considered for literary office. Zhang Chengye then controlled Hedong as rear-president, and everyone feared him. By custom the staff adjutant oversaw granary accounts; Cheng told Chengye, "This is not my strength—choose someone able." " Chengye snapped, "You call yourself a scholar—then write for the cause! You pleaded incompetence when asked to draft, and now you shirk accounts—what can you do at all?" " Cheng wept and apologized. He later served as adjutant on several circuits.
9
及汴將王彥章陷德勝南城，爭攻楊劉，莊宗禦軍苦戰，臣下憂之，鹹白宰臣，欲連章規諫，請不躬禦士伍。 豆盧革言及漢高臨廣武事，矢及於胸，紿雲中足。 程曰：「此劉季失策。 」眾皆縮頸。 嘗論近世士族，或曰：「員外郎孔明龜，善和宰相之令緒，宣聖之係孫，得非盛歟！ 」程曰：「止於孔子之後，盛則吾不知也。 」親黨有假驢夫於程者，程帖府給之，府吏訴雲無例，程怒鞭吏背。 時任圜為興唐少尹，莊宗從姊婿也，憑其寵戚，因詣程。 程方衣鶴氅、華陽巾，憑幾決事，見圜怒詈曰：「是何蟲豸，恃婦力耶！ 宰相取給於府縣，得不識舊體！ 」圜不言而退，是夜，馳至博平，麵訴於莊宗。 莊宗怒，謂郭崇韜曰：「朕誤相此癡物，敢辱予九卿。 」促令自盡。 崇韜亦怒，事幾不測，賴盧質橫身解之，遂降為右庶子。 莊宗既定河南，程隨百官從幸洛陽，沿路墜馬，因病風而卒。 贈禮部尚書。
When Wang Yanzhang of Bian took southern Desheng and pressed Yangliu, Zhuangzong fought at the head of the army; alarmed ministers urged the chief councillors to petition jointly that he not expose himself in the ranks. Dou Luge cited how Gaozu of Han at Guangwu took an arrow in the chest yet claimed it had only grazed his foot. Cheng said, "That was Liu Bang's blunder." " Everyone recoiled. Discussing recent great families, someone said, "Vice Director Kongming Gui can match a chief minister's command, and he is Confucius' descendant—is that not splendid!" " Cheng replied, "He is merely Confucius' descendant; as for glory, I see none." " When a relative asked Cheng for a donkey driver, he ordered the prefecture to provide one; the clerk protested there was no precedent, and Cheng had him flogged. Ren Huan was then Junior Governor of Xingtang, Zhuangzong's cousin-in-law, and came to Cheng relying on his court connections. Cheng sat in crane cloak and Huayang cap judging affairs at his desk; seeing Ren he raged, "What vermin is this, trading on his wife's family?" A chief minister draws supplies from the prefectures—has he no sense of proper form! " Ren left without a word; that night he raced to Boping and told Zhuangzong face to face. Zhuangzong fumed and told Guo Chongtao, "I was wrong to make this fool a minister—he dares insult my high officials." " He ordered Cheng to kill himself. Chongtao was furious too and matters nearly spun out of control until Lu Zhi intervened bodily; Cheng was reduced to Right Subordinate of the Heir Apparent. When Zhuangzong secured Henan, Cheng followed the court to Luoyang, fell from his horse on the road, and died of a stroke. He was posthumously honored as Minister of Rites.

李嗣昭典兵於晉陽，與圜遊處甚洽，及鎮澤潞，請為觀察支使，解褐，賜朱紱。 圜美姿容，有口辯。 嗣昭為人間諜於莊宗，方有微隙，圜奉使往來，常申理之，克成友於之道，圜之力也。 及丁母憂，莊宗承製起復潞州觀察判官，賜紫。 常山之役，嗣昭為帥，卒於軍，圜代總其事，號令如一，敵人不知。 莊宗聞之，倍加獎賞。 是秋，復以上黨之師攻常山，城中萬人突出，大將孫文進死之，賊逼我軍，圜麾騎士擊之，頗有殺獲。 嘗以禍福諭其城中，鎮人信之，使乞降。 及城潰，誅元惡之外，官吏鹹保其家屬，亦圜所庇護焉。 莊宗改鎮州為北京，以圜為工部尚書兼真定尹、北京副留守，行留守事。 明年，郭崇韜兼鎮，改行軍司馬，充北面水陸轉運使，仍知府事。 同光三年，歸朝，守工部尚書。
Li Sizhao commanded at Jinyang and was close to Huan; when he governed Ze-Lu he made Huan his adjutant, and Huan entered official life with the scarlet sash. Huan was handsome and eloquent. Sizhao had spies watching Zhuangzong and a rift had opened; Huan shuttled between them, reconciled them, and restored their brotherly bond—his doing. After his mother's mourning Zhuangzong recalled him to adjutant on the Lu circuit and granted purple robes. At Changshan Sizhao commanded and died in battle; Huan took command seamlessly so the enemy never knew. Zhuangzong heard and rewarded him doubly. That autumn he again attacked Changshan with the Shangdang army; ten thousand defenders sallied forth and killed General Sun Wenjin; Huan led the cavalry counterattack with heavy kills. He preached fortune and ruin to the city; the garrison believed him and sued for surrender. When the city fell, beyond executing the ringleaders, he spared officials and their families—Huan's protection. Zhuangzong made Zhenzhou the Northern Capital and appointed Huan Minister of Works, Governor of Zhending, and acting deputy rear-president of the Northern Capital. The next year, under Guo Chongtao's joint command, he became army secretary and Northern Front transport commissioner while still governing the prefecture. In Tongguang 3 he returned to court as Minister of Works.
20
崇韜伐蜀，奏令從征，西蜀平，署圜黔南節度使，懇辭遂止。 魏王班師，行及利州，康延孝叛，以勁兵八千回劫西川。 繼岌聞之，夜半命中使李廷安召圜，圜方寢，廷安登其床以告之，圜衣不及帶，遽見繼岌。 繼岌泣而言曰：「紹琛負恩，非尚書不能制。 」即署圜為招討副使，與都指揮使梁漢顒等率兵攻延孝於漢州，擒之。 旋至渭南，繼岌遇害。 圜代總全師，朝於洛陽。 明宗嘉其功，拜平章事，判三司。 圜揀拔賢俊，杜絕幸門，百官俸入為孔謙減折。 圜以廷臣為國家羽儀，故優假班行，禁其虛估，期月之內，府庫充贍，朝廷修葺，軍民鹹足。 雖憂國如家，而切於功名，故為安重誨所忌。 嘗與重誨會於私第，有妓善歌，重誨求之不得，嫌隙自茲而深矣。 先是，使人食券，皆出於戶部，重誨止之，俾須內出，爭於御前，往復數四，竟為所沮， 〈 （《通鑒》：安重誨與圜爭於上前，往復數四，聲色俱厲。 上退朝，宮人問上：「適與重誨論事為誰？ 」上曰：「宰相。 」宮人曰：「妾在長安宮中，未嘗見宰相、樞密奏事敢如是者，蓋輕大家耳！ 」上愈不悅。）〉 因求罷三司。
When Chongtao campaigned against Shu, Huan accompanied him; after Shu fell he was offered Qiannan but earnestly declined. As the prince withdrew, reaching Lizhou, Kang Yanxiao rebelled with eight thousand elite troops and raided Shu. Jiji heard at midnight and sent Li Ting'an to summon Huan from his bed; Huan rushed to him without belting his robe. Jiji wept and said, "Shaochen has turned traitor—only you can stop him." " He made Huan deputy pacification commissioner; with Liang Hanyong and others he attacked Yanxiao at Hanzhou and captured him. Soon after, at Weinan, Jiji was murdered. Huan took command of the whole army and presented himself at Luoyang. Mingzong rewarded him with the Grand Councillorship and charge of the Three Offices. Huan promoted the worthy and closed the door to favoritism; Kong Qian had been cutting officials' salaries. Huan treated the court as the state's public face, forbade inflated valuations, and within a month the treasury was full, the palace restored, and army and people provided for. Though he cared for the state as his own home, his hunger for glory made An Chonghui resent him. Once at a private gathering a singer pleased Chonghui, who could not obtain her from Huan—their rift deepened from that day. Envoy ration vouchers had come from the Ministry of Revenue; Chonghui required them from the inner palace instead; they argued back and forth before the throne four times, and Huan was blocked, ( (Zizhi Tongjian records that An Chonghui and Huan argued before the throne four times over, both fierce in voice and face. After court a palace woman asked, "Who was arguing with Chonghui just now?" " The emperor said, "The chief minister." " She said, "In Chang'an I never saw a minister or military commissioner speak so boldly at audience—he must despise Your Majesty!" " The emperor grew angrier still.)) He therefore asked to be relieved of the Three Offices.
21
天成二年，除太子少保致仕，出居磁州。 及朱守殷叛，重誨乘間誣其結構，立遣人稱製就害之，乃下詔曰：「太子少保致仕任圜，早推勳舊，曾委重難，既退免於劇權，俾優閑於外地，而乃不遵禮分，潛附守殷，緘題罔避於嫌疑，情旨頗彰於怨望。 自收汴壘，備見蹤由，若務含宏，是孤典憲，尚全大體，止罪一身。 宜令本州於私第賜自盡。 」圜受命之日，聚族酣飲，神情不撓。 清泰中，製贈太傅。
In Tiancheng 2 he retired as Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent and moved to Cizhou. When Zhu Shouyin rebelled, Chonghui accused Ren of conspiracy and sent an agent with an edict to kill him at once, then proclaimed: "The retired Junior Guardian Ren Huan, once a meritorious minister entrusted in crisis, was granted ease abroad—yet he broke propriety, secretly aided Shouyin, exchanged letters without regard to suspicion, and his resentment was plain." " On the day the order came, he feasted with his clan and did not flinch. In Qingtai he was posthumously honored as Grand Tutor.

史臣曰：革、說承舊族之胄，佐新造之邦，業雖謝於財成，罪未聞於昭著，而乃為權臣之所忌，顧後命以無逃，靜而言之，亦可憫也。 盧程器狹如是，形渥攸宜。 趙鳳、李愚，咸以文學之名，俱踐岩廊之位，校其貞節，愚復優焉。 任圜有縱橫濟物之才，無明哲保身之道，退猶不免，籲可悲哉！
The historian writes: Ge and Shuo came of old noble houses and served a newly founded dynasty; their work fell short of perfection, yet their crimes were hardly notorious—still powerful ministers hated them, and when the final sentence came they could not escape; calmly considered, they deserve pity. Lu Cheng's talents were so meager—his rank suited his measure. Zhao Feng and Li Yu both rose by literary fame to the council hall; in integrity Yu was the greater. Ren Huan had the talent to serve the realm broadly but not the wisdom to save himself—even in retirement he could not escape; how lamentable!
