1
豆盧革，祖籍，同州刺史。 父瓚，舒州刺史。 〈 （《宣和書譜》云：失其世係。）〉 革少值亂離，避地鄜、延，轉入中山，王處直禮之，辟於幕下，有奏記之譽。 因牡丹會賦詩，諷處直以桑柘為意，言甚古雅，漸加器仰，轉節度判官。 而理家無法，獨請謁處直，處直慮布政有缺，有所規諫，斂版出迎，乃為嬖人祈軍職矣。
Dou Luge, whose ancestral home is not recorded, served as prefect of Tongzhou. His father Zan was prefect of Shuzhou. 〈Note: (The 《Xuanhe Catalog of Calligraphy》 states that his family line was lost.)〉 In his youth Ge lived through the upheavals of the age, taking refuge in Fu and Yan before moving to Zhongshan. Wang Chuzhi received him graciously and brought him onto his staff, where he won renown for his memorials. At a peony festival he wrote a poem that, under the guise of mulberry and catalpa trees, admonished Chuzhi. The verse was strikingly refined, and Chuzhi came to value him more and more, eventually promoting him to adjutant of the military governorship. Yet he ran his household without restraint and sought a private audience with Chuzhi. Chuzhi, worried that his own administration might be found wanting, came out with folded placard in hand ready to remonstrate—only to discover that Ge had come to secure a military appointment for a favorite.
2
天祐末，莊宗將即位，講求輔相，盧質以名家子舉之，征拜行台左丞相。 同光初，拜平章事。 及登廊廟，事多錯亂，至於官階擬議，前後倒置，屢為省郎蕭希甫駿正，革改之，無難色。 莊宗初定汴、洛，革引薦韋說，冀諳事體，與己同功。 說既登庸，復事流品，舉止輕脫，怨歸於革。 又革、說之子俱授拾遺，父子同官，為人所刺，遂改授員外郎。 革請說之子濤為宏文館學士，說請革之子升為集賢學士，交易市恩，有同市井，識者醜之。 革自作相之後，不以進賢勸能為務，唯事修煉，求長生之術； 嘗服丹砂，嘔血數日，垂死而愈。
Near the end of the Tianyou reign, as Zhuangzong prepared to take the throne, he looked for chief ministers. Lu Zhi recommended Ge as a scion of a great house, and Ge was summoned to serve as Left Chancellor of the Mobile Imperial Secretariat. At the opening of the Tongguang era he was made Grand Councilor. Once he reached the highest council, his handling of affairs was often muddled—even rank proposals were put in the wrong order. Secretariat Registrar Xiao Xifu corrected him again and again, and Ge would revise his decisions without a trace of resentment. When Zhuangzong first secured Bianzhou and Luoyang, Ge recommended Wei Shuo, hoping that Shuo understood how government worked and would share the burden with him. After Shuo took high office, he again busied himself with pedigree and rank while behaving with careless levity, and public blame fell on Ge for having brought him in. Both Ge's and Shuo's sons were also appointed Reminder censors, and the spectacle of fathers and sons serving in the same office drew sharp criticism, so the appointments were changed to outside regular posts. Ge secured Shuo's son Tao a post as academician of the Hongwen Hall, while Shuo secured Ge's son Sheng a post as academician of the Jixian Institute. They traded favors like market hawkers, and men of judgment were disgusted. After Ge became chief minister, he no longer made promoting the worthy and encouraging talent his concern, but devoted himself solely to alchemical refinement in search of the art of long life; he once ingested cinnabar, vomited blood for days, and was near death before he recovered.
3
天成初，將葬莊宗，以革為山陵使。 及木主歸廟，不出私第，專俟旄鎮，數日無耗，為親友促令入朝。 安重誨對眾辱之曰：「山陵使名銜尚在，不候新命，便履公朝，意謂邊人可欺也。 」側目者聞之，思有所中。 初，蕭希甫有正諫之望，革嘗阻之，遂上疏論革與說苟且自容，致君無狀。 復誣其縱田客殺人，冒元亨上第。 遂貶為辰州刺史，仍令所在馳驛發遣。 後鄭玨、任圜等連上三章，請不行後命，乃下製曰：「豆盧革、韋說等，身為輔相，手握權衡，或端坐稱臣，或半笑奏事，於君無禮，舉世寧容。 革則暫委利權，便私俸祿，文武百辟皆從五月起支，父子二人偏自正初給遣。 說則自居重位，全紊大綱。 敘蔭貪榮，亂兒孫於昭穆； 賣官潤屋，換令錄之身名。 醜行疊彰，群情共怒，雖居牧守，示塞非尤。 革可責授費州司戶參軍，說可夷州司戶參軍，皆員外置同正員，並所在馳驛發遣。 」尋貶陵州長流百姓，委長吏常知所在。 天成二年夏，詔令逐處刺史監賜自盡，其骨肉並放逐便。
Early in the Tiancheng era, as Zhuangzong was to be buried, Ge was appointed commissioner for the imperial mausoleum. When the spirit tablet had been returned to the ancestral temple, he stayed in his private residence, waiting only for appointment as military governor. Days passed without word, until kinsmen and friends pressed him to attend court. An Chonghui humiliated him before the assembly, saying, "The title of mausoleum commissioner is still on the books. Without waiting for a new appointment you walk into open court—you must think a man from the frontier can be fooled. " Those who already watched him with suspicion heard this and began to look for a way to bring him down. Xiao Xifu had earlier hoped to serve as a forthright remonstrator, but Ge had once stood in his way. Xifu now submitted a memorial charging that Ge and Shuo compromised themselves to stay in office and had served their ruler without propriety. He further accused Ge of allowing tenant farmers to commit murder and of fraudulently obtaining the top rank in the Yuanheng examination. Ge was demoted to prefect of Chenzhou, with orders that the local authorities dispatch him by express relay. Later Zheng Jue, Ren Huan, and others submitted three memorials in succession asking that the later order not stand. An edict was then issued: "Dou Luge, Wei Shuo, and the rest, as chief ministers holding the scales of power, either sat stiffly styling themselves ministers or half-smiled when reporting affairs. Such conduct toward the ruler is without courtesy; none in the world could tolerate it. Ge temporarily entrusted profitable authority to himself and took private salary: all civil and military officials had their pay begin in the fifth month, but father and son alone drew theirs from the first of the year. Shuo, occupying a weighty post, wholly disrupted the great framework of government. He sought glory through inherited privilege and disorderly placed descendants in the ancestral hall; sold offices to enrich his house and traded register names for appointments. Their ugly conduct piled up and popular anger was shared. Though they were given governorships, this was to show that serious fault would not be overlooked. Ge is stripped of rank and appointed registrar of Feizhou; Shuo is appointed registrar of Yizhou. Both are supplemental appointees with standing equal to regular posts, and each locality shall dispatch them by express relay. " Shortly afterward they were demoted to commoners banished to Lingzhou, with orders that the local chief official always know their whereabouts. In the summer of the second year of Tiancheng, an edict ordered the prefect in each place to supervise and grant them suicide, and their kin were all banished forthwith.
4
子升，官至檢校正郎，服金紫，尋亦削奪。 〈 （《寶晉齋法書讚》載豆盧革《田園帖》云：大德欲要一居處，畿甸間舊無田園，鄜州雖有三兩處莊子，緣百姓租佃多年，累有令公大王書請，卻給還人戶，蓋不欲侵奪疲民，兼慮無知之輩，妄有影庇包役云云。 嶽珂曰：此帖乃與僧往還書，其畏強藩避罪罟，蓋栗栗淵冰，然其後卒以故縱田客貶夜郎，正坐所畏，信乎亂邦之不可居也。 是時據鄜乃高萬興，官檢校太師、中書令，封北平王，即革所謂「令公大王」者。 官故梁授，唐命維新，而顓麵正朝者，不能致褫鞶之誅，而反竊貢秉旄之佞，唐之不競，有自來矣。）〉
His son Sheng had risen to examining rectifier and wore gold and purple insignia, but soon lost these honors as well. 〈Note: (The 《Baojinzhai Praise of Calligraphy》 records Dou Luge's 《Pastoral Letter》, which reads in part: "Great Virtue wishes to have a dwelling. In the capital region I formerly had no estate. Although Fu Prefecture has two or three manor farms, the common people have rented them for many years, and there have repeatedly been letters from the Lord Prince asking that they be returned to the tenants. I do not wish to seize from weary folk, and I also fear that ignorant persons might falsely shelter others or bundle corvée obligations," and so on.) Yue Ke remarks: This letter is correspondence with a monk. His fear of powerful frontier lords and his effort to avoid the net of punishment were like treading on thin ice over a deep abyss—yet in the end he was banished to Yelang for letting tenant farmers run wild, precisely the danger he had feared. Truly, a chaotic age is no place to live. At that time Fu was held by Gao Wanxing, whose titles were acting grand preceptor and director of the secretariat and who was enfeoffed as Prince of Beiping—the "Lord Prince" to whom Ge referred. His offices had been granted by Liang, and Tang had renewed its mandate—yet those who faced the court with solemn countenance could not bring down the punishment of stripping his regalia, but instead secretly accepted tribute from this frontier commander. That Tang could not contend had been clear for a long time.)〉
5
韋說，福建觀察使岫之子也。 〈 （案：以下有闕文。）〉 莊宗定汴、洛，說與趙光允同製拜平章事。 說性謹重，奉職常不造事端。 時郭崇韜秉政，說等承順而已，政事得失，無所措言。 初，或有言於崇韜，銓選逾濫，選人或取他人出身銜，或取父兄資緒，與令史囊橐罔冒，崇韜乃條奏其事。 其後郊天，行事官數千人，多有告敕偽濫，因定去留，塗毀告身者甚眾，選人號哭都門之外。 議者亦以為積弊累年，一旦澄汰太細，懼失惟新含垢之意。 時說與郭崇韜國列，不能執而止之，頗遭物議。 說之親黨告之，說曰：「此郭漢子之意也。 」及崇韜得罪，說懼流言所鍾，乃令門人左拾遺王鬆、吏部員外郎李慎儀等上疏，云：「崇韜往日專權，不閑故實，塞仕進之門，非獎善之道。 」疏下中書，說等覆奏，深詆崇韜，識者非之。 又有王傪者，能以多岐取事，納賂於說，說以其名犯祖諱，遂改之為「操」，擬官於近甸。 及明宗即位，說常慮身危，每求庇於任圜，常保護之。 說居有井，昔與鄰家共之，因嫌鄙雜，築垣於外。 鄰人訟之，為希甫疏論，以為井有貨財，及案之本人，惟稱有破釜一所，反招虛妄。 初貶敘州刺史，尋責授夷州司戶參軍。
Wei Shuo was the son of Wei Yu, military commissioner of Fujian. 〈Note: (Note: the text below is missing.)〉 When Zhuangzong secured Bianzhou and Luoyang, Shuo and Zhao Guangyun were jointly appointed as grand councilors by edict. Shuo was cautious and grave by nature and ordinarily did not stir up trouble in office. Guo Chongtao held power at the time, and Shuo and the others merely followed his lead, with nothing to say about what government got right or wrong. Someone had earlier told Chongtao that appointments were excessively lax: candidates took others' examination credentials or borrowed fathers' and elder brothers' qualifications, while clerks took bribes for false claims. Chongtao then submitted a detailed memorial on the matter. Later, at the suburban sacrifice to Heaven, several thousand participating officials were found to hold many forged or improper appointment documents. When retention and dismissal were decided, many credentials were defaced, and candidates wailed outside the capital gate. Commentators also held that abuses had piled up for years, and that to purge too finely at once risked losing the spirit of renewal and forbearance. Shuo then held equal rank with Guo Chongtao but could not hold firm and stop the purge, and he drew considerable public criticism. Shuo's kinsmen and associates told him of the criticism. Shuo said, "This is that Guo fellow's idea. " When Chongtao fell from grace, Shuo feared being targeted by rumor and had his disciples Left Reminder Wang Song and Ministry of Personnel Outside Regular Li Shenyi submit a memorial saying, "Chongtao in the past monopolized power, was unversed in precedent, blocked the gate of advancement, and this is not the way to reward the good. " The memorial was sent down to the Secretariat, and Shuo and the others replied with a memorial that deeply denounced Chongtao. Men of judgment disapproved. There was also a Wang Can who could secure appointments by many devious paths and paid bribes to Shuo. Because his name violated an ancestral taboo, Shuo changed it to "Cao" and proposed an appointment in the near capital districts. When Mingzong took the throne, Shuo often feared for his safety and repeatedly sought protection from Ren Huan, who regularly shielded him. Shuo's residence had a well that he had once shared with a neighbor. Disliking the vulgar crowding, he built a wall around it. The neighbor sued. Xifu discussed the case in a memorial, arguing that the well held property of value; when the case was investigated, the man himself said only that there was one broken cauldron, which instead invited the charge of falsehood. He was first demoted to prefect of Xuzhou, then stripped of rank and appointed registrar of Yizhou.
6
初，說在江陵，與高季興相知，及入中書，亦常通信幣。 自討西蜀，季興請攻峽內，莊宗許之：「如能得三州，俾為屬郡。 」西川既定，季興無尺寸之功。 洎明宗纘承，季興頻請三郡，朝廷不得已而與之。 革、說方在中書，亦預其議。 及季興占據，獨歸其罪，流於合州。 明年夏，詔曰：「陵州、合州長流百姓豆盧革、韋說，頃在先朝，擢居重任，欺公害物，黷貨賣官。 靜惟肇亂之端，更有難容之事，且夔、忠、萬三州，地連巴蜀，路扼荊蠻，藉皇都弭難之初，徇逆帥僭求之勢，罔予視聽，率意割移。 將千之土疆，開通狡穴； 動兩川之兵賦，禦捍經年。 致朕莫遂偃戈，猶煩運策。 近者西方鄴雖復要害，高季興尚固窠巢，增吾旰食之憂，職爾朋奸之計。 而又自居貶所，繼出流言。 苟刑戮之稽時，處忠良於何地？ 宜令逐處刺史監賜自盡。」 〈 （《歐陽史》：說子濤，晉天福初，為尚書膳部員外郎，卒。）〉
Earlier, when Shuo was at Jiangling, he was on good terms with Gao Jixing, and after he entered the Secretariat they still often exchanged letters and gifts. When the campaign against Western Shu began, Jixing asked to attack the inner gorges. Zhuangzong agreed: "If you can take the three prefectures, they shall be made your dependent commanderies. " When Western Shu was settled, Jixing had not earned a fraction of merit. When Mingzong succeeded to the throne, Jixing repeatedly asked for the three commanderies, and the court, having no choice, granted them. Ge and Shuo were then in the Secretariat and also took part in the deliberation. When Jixing occupied the territories, the blame alone was laid on Shuo, and he was banished to Hezhou. The next summer an edict said, "The banished commoners Dou Luge of Lingzhou and Wei Shuo of Hezhou were recently raised to weighty posts in the former court. They harmed the public, injured the realm, took bribes, and sold offices. On quiet reflection, the origin of disorder has still more that cannot be tolerated. The three prefectures of Kui, Zhong, and Wan border Ba-Shu and straddle the route that throttles Jing and the southern tribes. Taking advantage of the capital's first effort to quell turmoil, they yielded to a rebellious commander's presumptuous demands, disregarded Our authority, and willfully ceded territory. A thousand li of territory was about to be opened to cunning lairs; the military levies of the two Shu were stirred, and defense dragged on for years. This kept Us from laying down Our weapons and still troubled Our strategy. Recently, though the western Ye stronghold was recovered, Gao Jixing still held his nest, increasing Our anxiety at mealtimes—this was the work of your faction's scheming. Moreover, while dwelling in their places of demotion, they continued to spread slander. If punishment is delayed, where shall the loyal and good stand? Let the prefect in each place be ordered to supervise and grant them suicide." 〈Note: (The 《New History of the Five Dynasties》: Shuo's son Tao, at the beginning of the Jin Tianfu era, served as outside regular of the Ministry of Rites Provisioners Section and died.)〉
7
盧程，唐朝右族。 祖懿，父蘊，曆仕通顯。 程，天復末登進士第，崔魏公領鹽鐵，署為巡官。 昭宗遷洛陽，柳璨陷右族，程避地河朔，客遊燕、趙，或衣道士服，幹謁藩伯，人未知之。 豆盧革客遊中山，依王處直，盧汝弼來太原。 程與革、弼皆朝族知舊，因往來依革，處直禮遇未優，故投於太原； 汝弼因為延譽，莊宗署為推官，尋改支使。 程褊淺無他才，惟矜恃門第，口多是非，篤厚君子尤薄之。
Lu Cheng came from a great Tang clan of the establishment. His grandfather Yi and father Yun had both served in prominent posts through their careers. Cheng passed the jinshi examination near the end of the Tianfu era. When Duke Cui of Wei headed the Salt and Iron Commission, he appointed Cheng touring official. When Zhaozong moved to Luoyang, Liu Can persecuted the great establishment clans. Cheng fled to Heshuo and wandered as a guest in Yan and Zhao, sometimes wearing Daoist robes and seeking audiences with frontier lords, and people did not know who he was. Dou Luge had traveled as a guest to Zhongshan and relied on Wang Chuzhi, while Lu Rubi came to Taiyuan. Cheng, Ge, and Bi were all old acquaintances among court clans. Cheng therefore went to rely on Ge, but Chuzhi's courtesy was not generous, so he went on to Taiyuan; Rubi recommended him, and Zhuangzong appointed Cheng investigating officer, soon changing the post to administrative aide. Cheng was narrow and shallow and had no other talent. He prided himself only on pedigree, his mouth full of gossip, and earnest gentlemen especially despised him.
8
初，判官王緘從軍掌文翰，胡柳之役，緘歿於軍。 莊宗歸寧太原，置酒公宴，舉酒謂張承業曰：「予今於此會取一書記，先以卮酒辟之。 」即舉酒屬巡官馮道，道以所舉非次，抗酒辭避。 莊宗曰：「勿謙挹，無逾於卿也。 」時以職列序遷，則程當為書記，汝弼亦左右之。 程既失職，私懷憤惋，謂人曰：「主上不重人物，使田里兒居餘上。 」先是，莊宗嘗於帳中召程草奏，程曰：「叨忝成名，不閑筆硯。 」由是文翰之選，不及於程。 時張承業專製河東留守事，人皆敬憚。 舊例，支使監諸廩出納，程訴於承業曰：「此事非仆所長，請擇能者。 」承業叱之曰：「公稱文士，即合飛文染翰，以濟霸國，嘗命草辭，自陳短拙，及留職務，又以為辭，公所能者何也？ 」程垂泣謝之。 後曆觀察判官。
Earlier, adjutant Wang Jian had followed the army in charge of documents; at the battle of Huliu he died in the field. Zhuangzong returned to Taiyuan to pay his respects and held a public banquet. He raised his cup and said to Zhang Chengye, "At this gathering I shall choose a secretary. First I offer this cup of wine to declare the choice. " He at once offered the wine to touring official Feng Dao. Because the appointment was out of order, Dao raised the cup in refusal and declined. Zhuangzong said, "Do not be modest—none surpasses you. " By order of rank in office, Cheng ought to have become secretary, and Rubi also supported him. Having lost the post, Cheng privately harbored resentment and said to others, "Our lord does not value men of quality, letting a village lad rank above me. " Earlier Zhuangzong had once summoned Cheng in his tent to draft a memorial. Cheng said, "I have undeservedly achieved fame and am unskilled with brush and inkstone. " For this reason the choice for documents did not reach Cheng. At the time Zhang Chengye monopolized the Hedong regent's affairs, and all feared and respected him. By old custom the administrative aide supervised all granary receipts and disbursements. Cheng complained to Chengye, "This matter is not what your servant excels at; please choose someone capable. " Chengye rebuked him: "You call yourself a man of letters, so you ought to wield the brush and aid the hegemonic state. When ordered to draft a text you declared your inadequacy; when left with duties you again make excuses—what can you do? " Cheng wept and apologized. Later he served successively as observation commissioner adjutant.
9
及汴將王彥章陷德勝南城，爭攻楊劉，莊宗禦軍苦戰，臣下憂之，鹹白宰臣，欲連章規諫，請不躬禦士伍。 豆盧革言及漢高臨廣武事，矢及於胸，紿雲中足。 程曰：「此劉季失策。 」眾皆縮頸。 嘗論近世士族，或曰：「員外郎孔明龜，善和宰相之令緒，宣聖之係孫，得非盛歟！ 」程曰：「止於孔子之後，盛則吾不知也。 」親黨有假驢夫於程者，程帖府給之，府吏訴雲無例，程怒鞭吏背。 時任圜為興唐少尹，莊宗從姊婿也，憑其寵戚，因詣程。 程方衣鶴氅、華陽巾，憑幾決事，見圜怒詈曰：「是何蟲豸，恃婦力耶！ 宰相取給於府縣，得不識舊體！ 」圜不言而退，是夜，馳至博平，麵訴於莊宗。 莊宗怒，謂郭崇韜曰：「朕誤相此癡物，敢辱予九卿。 」促令自盡。 崇韜亦怒，事幾不測，賴盧質橫身解之，遂降為右庶子。 莊宗既定河南，程隨百官從幸洛陽，沿路墜馬，因病風而卒。 贈禮部尚書。
When the Bian general Wang Yanzhang took the south city of Desheng and pressed the attack on Yangliu, Zhuangzong led the army in bitter fighting. His ministers were anxious; all told the chief ministers they wished to submit joint memorials asking that he not personally lead the ranks. Dou Luge spoke of Emperor Gaozu of Han at Guangwu, where an arrow reached his chest and he deceived Yunzhong into staying. Cheng said, "That was Liu Ji's mistake in strategy. " Everyone shrank back. Once appointed chief minister, the government lent him the guest reception hall to dwell in. Once when he was ill, an edict ordered a nearby minister to convey imperial concern. He received the envoy in the central hall with only reed matting for seating, which the envoy reported. Mingzong specially granted curtains, screens, and bedding. 〈Note: (The 《Zhiguan Fenji》 says: In the fourth year of Changxing, Yu was ill and Mingzong sent a palace envoy to inquire. Yu's sleeping chamber was bare on four walls, with only a sick couch and a worn rug. The palace envoy fully reported the matter. The emperor said, "How much is a chief minister's monthly salary? Yet he is so exhausted and worn. " An edict granted one hundred bolts of silk, a hundred thousand cash, and thirteen kinds of curtains, screens, and furnishings.)〉
17
閔帝嗣位，誌修德政，易月之制才除，便延訪學士讀《貞觀政要》、《太宗實錄》，有意於致理。 愚私謂同列曰：「吾君延訪，少及吾輩，位高責重，事亦堪憂，奈宗社何！ 」皆惕息而不敢言。 以恩例進位左僕射。 清泰初，徽陵禮畢，馮道出鎮同州，愚加特進、太微宮使、宏文館大學士。 宰相劉句與馮道為婚家，道既出鎮，兩人在中書，或舊事不便要厘革者，對論不定。 愚性太峻，因曰：「此事賢家翁所為，更之不亦便乎！ 」句憾其言切，於是每言必相折難，或至喧呼。 無幾，兩人俱罷相守本官。 清泰二年秋，愚已嬰疾，率多請告，累表乞骸，不允，卒於位。
When Emperor Min succeeded, he resolved to cultivate virtuous government. The mourning period had barely ended when he summoned academicians to read the 《Essentials of Government from the Zhenguan Era》 and the 《Veritable Records of Taizong》, intending to bring order to rule. Yu privately said to his colleagues, "Our lord in his audiences seldom reaches us. Our positions are high and our responsibilities heavy—the matter is also worrisome. What will become of the altars of state! " All held their breath in fear and dared not speak. By favor he was advanced to left vice director. At the beginning of Qingtai, when the Huiling rites were complete, Feng Dao went out to govern Tongzhou. Yu was given special advancement, commissioner of the Taiwei Palace, and grand academician of the Hongwen Hall. Chief minister Liu Gou and Feng Dao were related by marriage. Once Dao had gone out to govern, the two men remained in the secretariat; on old matters inconvenient to reform, they debated face to face without resolution. Yu was excessively stern by nature and said, "This matter was done by your worthy father-in-law—would it not be more convenient to change it! " Gou resented his cutting words; from then on whenever they spoke they inevitably refuted each other, sometimes even shouting. Before long both men were dismissed and kept their original posts. In the autumn of the second year of Qingtai, Yu was already gravely ill, mostly requesting leave, and repeatedly submitted memorials asking to retire; this was not granted, and he died in office.
18
任圜，京兆三原人。 祖清，成都少尹。 父茂宏，避地太原，奏授西河令； 有子五人，曰圖、回、圜、團、冏，風彩俱異。 武皇愛之，以宗女妻團，歷代、憲二郡刺史。
Ren Huan was a native of Sanyuan in Jingzhao. His grandfather Qing was vice governor of Chengdu. His father Maohong fled to Taiyuan and was memorialized for appointment as magistrate of Xihe; he had five sons named Tu, Hui, Huan, Tuan, and Jiong, each with a distinctive bearing. The Martial Emperor favored them; he married a clanswoman to Tuan, who served successively as prefect of Dai and Xian commanderies.
19
李嗣昭典兵於晉陽，與圜遊處甚洽，及鎮澤潞，請為觀察支使，解褐，賜朱紱。 圜美姿容，有口辯。 嗣昭為人間諜於莊宗，方有微隙，圜奉使往來，常申理之，克成友於之道，圜之力也。 及丁母憂，莊宗承製起復潞州觀察判官，賜紫。 常山之役，嗣昭為帥，卒於軍，圜代總其事，號令如一，敵人不知。 莊宗聞之，倍加獎賞。 是秋，復以上黨之師攻常山，城中萬人突出，大將孫文進死之，賊逼我軍，圜麾騎士擊之，頗有殺獲。 嘗以禍福諭其城中，鎮人信之，使乞降。 及城潰，誅元惡之外，官吏鹹保其家屬，亦圜所庇護焉。 莊宗改鎮州為北京，以圜為工部尚書兼真定尹、北京副留守，行留守事。 明年，郭崇韜兼鎮，改行軍司馬，充北面水陸轉運使，仍知府事。 同光三年，歸朝，守工部尚書。
Li Sizhao commanded troops at Jinyang and was on very friendly terms with Huan. When Sizhao governed Ze-Lu, he requested Huan as observation administrative aide. Huan left the commoner's garb and was granted a scarlet sash. Huan had handsome features and was eloquent. Sizhao served as an intermediary for Zhuangzong; when there was a slight rift, Huan went back and forth as envoy and often explained matters, achieving brotherly harmony—this was Huan's doing. When he entered mourning for his mother, Zhuangzong by imperial order recalled him from mourning as Ze-Lu observation adjutant and granted purple robes. In the Changshan campaign Sizhao was commander and died in the army. Huan took over all affairs; commands continued unchanged, and the enemy did not know. Zhuangzong heard of this and doubled his rewards. That autumn he again led the Shangdang army to attack Changshan. Ten thousand men sallied from the city; the great general Sun Wenjin was killed. The rebels pressed our army; Huan commanded horsemen to strike them and killed and captured many. He once instructed the city on fortune and disaster; the people of Zhen believed him and sent someone to beg surrender. When the city fell, beyond executing the chief culprits, officials and clerks all preserved their families—this too was under Huan's protection. Zhuangzong changed Zhenzhou to the Northern Capital and appointed Huan minister of works and concurrently governor of Zhending, Northern Capital vice regent, acting in the regent's affairs. The next year Guo Chongtao also held the command; Huan was changed to campaigning marshal, served as northern land-and-water transport commissioner, and still managed prefectural affairs. In the third year of Tongguang he returned to court and held the post of minister of works.
20
崇韜伐蜀，奏令從征，西蜀平，署圜黔南節度使，懇辭遂止。 魏王班師，行及利州，康延孝叛，以勁兵八千回劫西川。 繼岌聞之，夜半命中使李廷安召圜，圜方寢，廷安登其床以告之，圜衣不及帶，遽見繼岌。 繼岌泣而言曰：「紹琛負恩，非尚書不能制。 」即署圜為招討副使，與都指揮使梁漢顒等率兵攻延孝於漢州，擒之。 旋至渭南，繼岌遇害。 圜代總全師，朝於洛陽。 明宗嘉其功，拜平章事，判三司。 圜揀拔賢俊，杜絕幸門，百官俸入為孔謙減折。 圜以廷臣為國家羽儀，故優假班行，禁其虛估，期月之內，府庫充贍，朝廷修葺，軍民鹹足。 雖憂國如家，而切於功名，故為安重誨所忌。 嘗與重誨會於私第，有妓善歌，重誨求之不得，嫌隙自茲而深矣。 先是，使人食券，皆出於戶部，重誨止之，俾須內出，爭於御前，往復數四，竟為所沮， 〈 （《通鑒》：安重誨與圜爭於上前，往復數四，聲色俱厲。 上退朝，宮人問上：「適與重誨論事為誰？ 」上曰：「宰相。 」宮人曰：「妾在長安宮中，未嘗見宰相、樞密奏事敢如是者，蓋輕大家耳！ 」上愈不悅。）〉 因求罷三司。
When Chongtao campaigned against Shu he memorialized that Huan should follow the campaign. When Western Shu was pacified he appointed Huan military governor of Qiannan; Huan earnestly declined and the appointment was dropped. When the Prince of Wei withdrew the army and reached Lizhou, Kang Yanxiao rebelled and with eight thousand crack troops turned back to raid Western Shu. Jiji heard of it and at midnight ordered the palace envoy Li Ting'an to summon Huan. Huan was asleep; Ting'an climbed onto his bed to tell him. Huan had no time to belt his robe and hurried to see Jiji. Jiji wept and said, "Shaochen has betrayed us—only you, Minister, can control him. " He at once appointed Huan deputy pacification commissioner; with the commander-in-chief Liang Hanyong and others he led troops to attack Yanxiao at Hanzhou and captured him. Soon they reached Weinan, where Jiji was murdered. Huan took command of the entire army and presented himself at court in Luoyang. Mingzong praised his merit and appointed him grand councilor, with charge of the three offices. Huan selected worthy and talented men and blocked the gate of favor; officials' salaries had been reduced and discounted by Kong Qian. Huan held that court ministers were the state's ceremonial plumes and banners, so he granted favorable treatment to the official ranks and forbade false valuation. Within a month the treasury was full, the court was repaired, and army and people were all provided for. Though he grieved for the state as for his own family, he was keen on fame and achievement, and therefore was resented by An Chonghui. Once he met Chonghui at a private residence. There was a singing girl skilled in song; Chonghui asked for her but could not obtain her, and enmity from this grew deep. Earlier, envoys' food vouchers all came from the Ministry of Revenue. Chonghui stopped this and required inner-court issuance instead. They disputed before the throne, back and forth several times, and in the end were blocked by him, 〈Note: (The 《Comprehensive Mirror》: An Chonghui and Huan disputed before the throne, back and forth several times, both voice and countenance fierce. When the emperor left court, a palace woman asked, "With whom was Your Majesty just now discussing affairs with Chonghui? " The emperor said, "The chief minister. " The palace woman said, "When I was in the Chang'an palace, I never saw a chief minister or privy councilor report affairs dare to be like this—they must look down on the imperial house! " The emperor was still more displeased.)〉 Huan therefore asked to resign charge of the three offices.
21
天成二年，除太子少保致仕，出居磁州。 及朱守殷叛，重誨乘間誣其結構，立遣人稱製就害之，乃下詔曰：「太子少保致仕任圜，早推勳舊，曾委重難，既退免於劇權，俾優閑於外地，而乃不遵禮分，潛附守殷，緘題罔避於嫌疑，情旨頗彰於怨望。 自收汴壘，備見蹤由，若務含宏，是孤典憲，尚全大體，止罪一身。 宜令本州於私第賜自盡。 」圜受命之日，聚族酣飲，神情不撓。 清泰中，製贈太傅。
In the second year of Tiancheng he was made junior mentor of the heir apparent and retired, going out to dwell in Cizhou. When Zhu Shouyin rebelled, Chonghui seized the opportunity to accuse him of conspiracy and at once sent a man bearing an imperial order to kill him on the spot. An edict was then issued: "The retired junior mentor of the heir apparent Ren Huan was early esteemed as an old merit holder and once entrusted with weighty responsibility. Having retired from intense power, he was granted ease in an outer region—yet he did not observe ritual propriety, secretly attached himself to Shouyin, sealed letters without avoiding suspicion, and his sentiments clearly showed resentment. Since the Bian stronghold was taken, his tracks have been fully seen. If We sought forbearance, We would betray the statutes; yet in preserving the larger frame, We punish only one man. Let the local prefecture grant him suicide at his private residence. " On the day Huan received the order, he gathered his clan for hearty drinking; his spirit was unshaken. During the Qingtai era, by edict he was posthumously awarded grand tutor.
22
子徹，仕皇朝，位至度支郎中，卒。
His son Che served the imperial court, reaching director of the expenditure section, and died.
23
史臣曰：革、說承舊族之胄，佐新造之邦，業雖謝於財成，罪未聞於昭著，而乃為權臣之所忌，顧後命以無逃，靜而言之，亦可憫也。 盧程器狹如是，形渥攸宜。 趙鳳、李愚，咸以文學之名，俱踐岩廊之位，校其貞節，愚復優焉。 任圜有縱橫濟物之才，無明哲保身之道，退猶不免，籲可悲哉！
The historian says: Ge and Shuo inherited the pedigrees of old clans and assisted a newly founded state. Their achievements, though short of completion, had no conspicuous crimes—yet they were resented by powerful ministers and could not escape their fate. Spoken calmly, this too is pitiable. That Lu Cheng's capacity was so narrow—his punishment was fitting. Zhao Feng and Li Yu both bore literary fame and together reached the highest council. Comparing their integrity, Yu was again superior. Ren Huan had talent to traverse and aid the realm but lacked the way of the wise to preserve himself; even in retirement he could not escape—alas, how lamentable!
