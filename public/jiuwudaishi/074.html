1
三年，討蜀，以延孝為西南行營馬步軍先鋒、排陣斬斫等使。 延孝性驍健，徇利奮不顧身。 以前鋒下鳳州，收固鎮，降興州，敗王衍軍於三泉，所俘蜀軍皆諭而釋之，自是晝夜兼行。 王衍自利州奔歸成都，斷吉柏津浮梁，以絕諸軍。 延孝復造浮梁以渡，進收綿州，王衍復斷綿江浮梁而去。 水深無舟楫可渡，延孝謂招撫使李嚴曰：「吾懸軍深入，利在急兵。 乘王衍破膽之時，人心離沮，但得百騎過鹿頭關，彼即迎降不暇。 如俟修繕津梁，便留數日，若王衍堅閉近關，折吾兵勢，儻延旬浹，則勝負莫可知也，宜促騎渡江。 」因與李嚴乘馬浮江，於是得濟者僅千人，步軍溺死者亦千餘人。 延孝既濟，長驅通鹿頭，進據漢州。 居三日，部下後軍方至。 偽蜀六軍使王宗弼令人持牛酒幣馬歸款。 旬日，兩川平定，延孝止漢州以俟繼岌。 平蜀之功，延孝居最。
In the third year, during the campaign against Shu, Yanxiao was made vanguard of the southwest expedition's combined cavalry and infantry, with additional titles for assault and battle-formation duties. Yanxiao was by nature bold and hardy, and in pursuit of advantage he fought without thought for his own safety. Leading the vanguard, he took Feng Prefecture, recovered Gu Town, accepted the surrender of Xing Prefecture, and routed Wang Yan's forces at Sanquan. Every Shu soldier he captured he reasoned with and set free, and from that point he pressed forward day and night. Wang Yan fled from Lizhou to Chengdu, cut the pontoon bridge at Jibo Ford, and thereby blocked the advancing armies. Yanxiao built another pontoon bridge to cross, advanced and seized Mian Prefecture, and Wang Yan in turn cut the Mian River bridge and retreated. The river ran too deep for boats, and Yanxiao told the pacification commissioner Li Yan, "I have driven a detached force deep into hostile country; our advantage lies in speed. While Wang Yan is still terrified and his people's morale is collapsing, a mere hundred horsemen through Lutou Pass will leave them scrambling to surrender. If we wait to rebuild the crossing, we lose days; if Wang Yan seals the nearby passes and breaks our momentum, and the campaign drags on another ten days, the outcome becomes uncertain. We must get the cavalry across the river now. " He and Li Yan then rode their horses across the river. Only about a thousand men made it over, while more than a thousand foot soldiers drowned. Once across, Yanxiao drove straight through Lutou Pass and seized Han Prefecture. Three days later, his rear guard finally caught up. Wang Zongbi, the Six Armies commissioner of the Shu regime, sent envoys with cattle, wine, silks, and horses to offer surrender. Within ten days both river circuits were pacified, and Yanxiao held Han Prefecture to await Jiji. In the conquest of Shu, Yanxiao's merit stood first.
2
時邠州節度使董璋為行營右廂馬步使，華州節度使毛璋為行營左廂馬步使，以軍禮當事延孝。 郭崇韜以私愛董璋，及西川平定之後，崇韜每有兵機，必召璋參決，延孝不平。 時延孝軍於城西，毛璋軍於城東，董璋軍於城中。 閏十二月，延孝因酒酣謂董璋曰：「吾有平蜀之功，公等仆蔌相從，反首鼠於侍中之門，謀相傾陷，吾為都將，公乃裨校，力能斬首。 」璋惶恐，謝之而退。 酒罷，璋訴於郭崇韜，崇韜陰銜之，乃署董璋為東川節度使，落軍職。 延孝怒，謂毛璋曰：「吾冒白刃，犯險阻，平定兩川，董璋何功，遽有其地！ 」二人因謁見崇韜，曰：「東川重地，宜擇良帥，工部任尚書有文武才幹，甚洽眾心，請表為東川帥。 」崇韜怒曰：「紹琛反耶？ 敢違吾節度！ 」延孝等惶恐而退。 未幾，崇韜為繼岌所害，二人因責董璋曰：「公復首鼠何門？ 」璋俯首祈哀而已。
At the time Dong Zhang, military governor of Bin, was right-wing horse-and-foot commander of the expedition, and Mao Zhang of Hua was left-wing commander; by military protocol both answered to Yanxiao. Guo Chongtao favored Dong Zhang personally, and after western Sichuan was pacified he consulted Zhang on every military matter. Yanxiao resented it. Yanxiao camped west of the city, Mao Zhang east of it, and Dong Zhang inside the walls. In the intercalary twelfth month, Yanxiao, drunk, told Dong Zhang, "I won Shu. You lot tagged along like weeds, yet you scurry between loyalties at the Vice Director's door and plot against me. I am commander-in-chief and you a subordinate—I could take your head. " Zhang, terrified, apologized and withdrew. After the banquet Zhang appealed to Guo Chongtao, who secretly took offense at Yanxiao and appointed Dong Zhang military governor of the eastern circuit, removing him from the army command. Yanxiao, furious, said to Mao Zhang, "I faced blades and hardship to conquer both circuits—what has Dong Zhang done to deserve that land! " The two then saw Chongtao and said, "Eastern Sichuan is too important for Dong Zhang. Minister Ren has both civil and military ability and the troops' trust; we ask that he be named its commander. " Chongtao snapped, "Has Shaochen turned rebel? How dare he defy my orders! " Yanxiao and the others withdrew in alarm. Soon afterward Chongtao was killed on Jiji's orders, and the two confronted Dong Zhang: "Which door will you run to now? " Zhang could only bow his head and beg for mercy.
3
四年正月甲申，大軍發成都，繼岌令延孝以一萬二千人為後軍。 二月癸巳，中軍次武連，中使詔至，諭以西平王朱友謙有罪伏誅，命繼岌殺其子遂州節度使令德，延孝大驚。 俄而董璋率兵之遂州，遇延孝不謁，延孝怒，謂諸校曰：「南平梁汴，西定巴邛，畫策之謀，始於郭公，而汗馬之勞，力摧強敵，即吾也。 若以背偽歸國，犄角而成霸業，即西平王之功第一。 西平與郭公皆以無罪赤族，歸朝之後，次當及我矣！ 」丙申，延孝次劍州。 時延孝部下皆鄜、延、河中舊將，焦武等知西平王被禍，兼誅令德，號哭軍門，訴於延孝曰：「西平無罪，二百口伏誅，河中舊將，無不從坐，某等必死矣！ 」時魏王繼岌到泥溪，延孝報繼岌云：「河中兵士號哭，欲為亂。 」丁酉，延孝至劍州，遂擁眾回，自稱西川節度、三川製置等使，以檄招諭蜀人，三日間，眾及五萬。 己亥，繼岌至利州。 是夜，守吉柏津使密告魏王曰：「得紹琛文字，令斷吉柏浮梁。 」繼岌懼，乃令梁漢顒以兵控吉柏津。 延孝已擁眾急趨西川，繼岌遣人馳書諭之。 夜半，令監軍使李廷安召任圜，因署為副招討使。 令圜率兵七千騎，與都指揮使梁漢顒、監軍李廷安討之。 辛丑，先令都將何建崇擊劍門，下之。 甲寅，圜以大軍至漢州，延孝來逆戰，圜令董璋以東川懦卒當其鋒，伏精兵於其後，延孝擊退東川之兵，急追之，遇伏兵起，延孝敗，馳入漢州，閉壁不出。 西川孟知祥以兵二萬，與圜合勢攻之。 〈 （《九國誌·李延厚傳》：康延孝入漢州，知祥遣延厚率兵二千會李仁罕討之，將行，誓士卒曰：「今出師不三旬必破賊，乃立功圖賞之日也。 士卒忠奮者立東廂，衰疾者立西廂，無自苦也。 」得請行者七百人，逐延孝西寨，斬首百餘級，竟拔其城。）〉 漢州四面樹竹木為柵。
On jiashen, the first month of the fourth year, the main army left Chengdu, with Yanxiao commanding twelve thousand men as rear guard. On guisi in the second month the main force reached Wulian. An imperial messenger arrived with orders that Zhu Youqian, Prince of Xiping, had been executed for treason and that Jiji must kill his son Lingde, military governor of Suizhou. Yanxiao was stunned. Soon Dong Zhang marched to Suizhou and, meeting Yanxiao, did not call on him. Yanxiao raged at his officers: "We pacified Liang and Bian in the south and secured Ba and Qiong in the west. Guo Chongtao planned it, but the fighting that broke the enemy—that was me. If merit lies in abandoning a false master to serve the throne and helping build the empire, the Prince of Xiping stood first. Xiping and Guo were wiped out though innocent. After we return to court, I will be next! " On bingshen, Yanxiao reached Jian Prefecture. Yanxiao's officers were veterans from Fu, Yan, and Hezhong. When Jiao Wu and others learned of Xiping's death and Lingde's execution, they wept at the camp gate and told Yanxiao, "Xiping was innocent, yet two hundred of his kin were killed. Every old Hezhong officer will be implicated—we are dead men! " The Prince of Wei Jiji had reached Nixi. Yanxiao reported, "The Hezhong troops are wailing and may mutiny. " On dingyou he reached Jian Prefecture, turned his force around, and declared himself military governor of western Sichuan and commissioner for the three river circuits. Within three days his proclamations had rallied fifty thousand men. On jihai, Jiji reached Li Prefecture. That night the officer at Jibo Ford secretly warned the prince, "I have a message from Shaochen ordering the Jibo bridge cut. " Alarmed, Jiji sent Liang Hanyong to seize the ford. Yanxiao was already racing west with his army, and Jiji sent urgent letters ordering him to stop. At midnight he had the army supervisor Li Ting'an summon Ren Huan and appoint him deputy pacification commissioner. Ren Huan was to lead seven thousand cavalry, with chief commander Liang Hanyong and supervisor Li Ting'an, against Yanxiao. On xinchou he sent chief commander He Jianchong to storm Jianmen Pass and take it. On jiayin Ren Huan reached Han Prefecture with the main force. Yanxiao met him in battle. Ren Huan put Dong Zhang's weak eastern troops in the front line and hid elite troops behind them. Yanxiao routed the eastern force and pursued recklessly into the ambush. Defeated, he fled into Han Prefecture and shut the gates. Meng Zhixiang of western Sichuan brought twenty thousand men to join Ren Huan's assault. [ (From Li Yanhou's biography in the 《Records of the Nine States》: When Kang Yanxiao entered Han Prefecture, Zhixiang sent Yanhou with two thousand men to join Li Renhan. Before marching, he swore to his troops, "We will break the rebels within thirty days. This is the day to win merit and reward. Let the loyal and eager stand on the east side, the weak and sick on the west. Do not force yourselves. " Seven hundred volunteered, stormed Yanxiao's western camp, took more than a hundred heads, and finally captured the fort.)〉 Han Prefecture was ringed with palisades of bamboo and timber.
4
三月乙丑，圜陣於金雁橋，即率諸軍鼓噪而進，四面縱火，風焰亙空。 於是延孝危急，引騎出戰，遇陣於金雁橋，又敗之，以十數騎奔綿州； 何建崇追及，擒之，任圜命載以檻車。 時孟知祥與任圜、董璋置酒高會，因引令延孝檻車至會。 知祥問曰：「明公頃自梁朝脫身歸命，才平汴水，節制陝郊，近領前鋒，克平劍外，歸朝之後，授爵冊勳，巨鎮尊官，誰與為競！ 奈何躁憤，自毀功庸，入此檻車，還為鄧艾，深可痛惜，誰肯湣之！ 」知祥因手自注杯以飲之。 延孝曰：「自知富貴難消，官職已足。 然郭崇韜佐命元勳，輔成大業，不動干戈，收獲兩川，自古殊功，但恐不及，一旦何罪，闔門被誅； 延孝之徒，何保首領。 以此思慮，不敢歸朝，天道相違，一旦至此，亦其命也，夫復何言！ 」及圜班師，行次鳳翔，中使向延嗣齎詔至，遂誅之。 部下懷其首級，瘞於昭應縣民陳暉地。 天成初，其子發之攜去。
On yichou in the third month Ren Huan drew up at Golden Goose Bridge, led the armies forward with drums and shouts, and set fires on every side until flames filled the sky. Yanxiao, desperate, led his cavalry out, was beaten again at Golden Goose Bridge, and fled toward Mian Prefecture with only a dozen horsemen; He Jianchong overtook and captured him, and Ren Huan had him placed in a cage cart. Meng Zhixiang was feasting with Ren Huan and Dong Zhang and had Yanxiao brought to the banquet in his cage cart. Zhixiang asked, "You left the Liang court to serve the throne, pacified Bian, governed Shaan, led the vanguard and conquered the lands beyond Jian, and on your return received titles, honors, and a great command. Who could rival you! Why then let anger destroy your achievements, end up in this cage, and become another Deng Ai? It is a bitter waste. Who will pity you now! " Zhixiang filled a cup himself and had him drink. Yanxiao said, "I knew wealth and rank were more than I could bear, and my offices were enough. Yet Guo Chongtao, a founding minister who helped build the empire and took both circuits without a battle—a feat perhaps unmatched in history—was executed with his entire household in a single day, for no crime anyone could name; How could men like me hope to keep our heads? That is why I dared not return to court. Heaven turned against me, and in a day I came to this. It is fate. What more can I say! " When Ren Huan withdrew and reached Fengxiang, the imperial envoy Xiang Yansi arrived with the execution order, and Yanxiao was put to death. His men kept his head and buried it on land belonging to Chen Hui, a commoner of Zhaoying County. Early in the Tiancheng reign his son exhumed it and took it away.
5
朱守殷，小字會兒。 莊宗就學，以廝養之役給事左右。 及莊宗即位，為長直軍使，雖列戎行，不聞戰攻。 每構人之短長，中於莊宗，漸以心腹受委。 河上對壘，稍遷蕃漢馬步都虞候。 守殷守德勝寨，為梁將王彥章所攻，守殷無備，遂陷南寨。 莊宗聞之曰：「駑才大誤予事！ 」因撤北寨，往固楊劉。 明宗在鄆州，密請以覆軍之罪罪之，莊宗私於腹心，忍而不問。 同光二年，為振武節度使，不之任，仍兼領蕃漢馬步軍。 京城初定，內外警巡，恃憑主恩，蔑視勳舊，與景進互相表裏，又強作宿德之態，言語遲緩，自謂沉厚。 及郭從謙犯興教門，步軍始亂，中使急召騎士，守殷按甲不進，莊宗獨領宦官斫射，屢退，而騎軍終不至。 莊宗既崩，守殷擁眾方在北邙，憩於茂林之下。 迨聞凶問，乃入內，選嬪御及珍寶以歸，恣軍士劫掠京都，翌日方定，率諸校迎明宗於東郊。 天成初，授河南尹，判六軍諸衛事，加侍中，移汴州節度使。 車駕將巡幸，外議喧然，初以為平吳，又云製置東諸侯。 守殷乃生雲夢之疑，遂殺都校馬彥超、副使宋敬。 〈 （《歐陽史》：守殷將叛，召都指揮使馬彥超與計事，彥超不從，守殷殺之。 明宗憐彥超之死，以其子承祚為洺州長史。）〉 守殷驅市人閉壁以叛，明宗途次京水，聞之，親統禁軍，倍程直抵其壘，長圍夾攻，縋城甚眾。 守殷力屈，盡殺其族，引頸令左右盡其命。 王師入城，索其黨，盡誅之。 詔鞭守殷屍，梟首懸於都市，滿七日，傳送洛陽。
Zhu Shouyin, known in childhood as Hui'er. While Zhuangzong was still a student, he served him in menial attendance. When Zhuangzong took the throne, he was made chief of the long-service guard. Though he held military rank, he was never known for fighting. He constantly played on others' faults and virtues to sway Zhuangzong and gradually became a trusted intimate. During the standoff on the Yellow River he was promoted to chief adjutant of the combined tribal and Han forces. Shouyin held Desheng Stockade. The Liang general Wang Yanzhang attacked it, and Shouyin, unprepared, lost the southern stockade. Zhuangzong said when he heard it, "That fool has ruined my campaign! " He withdrew from the northern stockade and went to secure Yangliu. Mingzong, then at Yan Prefecture, privately asked that he be punished for losing the army, but Zhuangzong, treating him as a personal favorite, bore the fault and did nothing. In the second year of Tongguang he was named military governor of Zhenwu but never took up the post, while retaining command of the combined tribal and Han forces. When the capital was first secured he took charge of internal and external patrols. Relying on imperial favor, he scorned the old meritocrats, worked hand in glove with Jing Jin, and affected the manner of a seasoned elder—slow of speech, calling himself grave and steady. When Guo Congqian attacked Xingjiao Gate and the infantry broke, palace envoys urgently summoned the cavalry, but Shouyin held his troops back. Zhuangzong alone led eunuchs in the fighting, was driven back again and again, and the cavalry never came. After Zhuangzong's death Shouyin was encamped with his troops on North Mang, resting in a grove. When he heard the news he entered the palace, took consorts and treasures for himself, and let his troops loot the capital. Order was restored only the next day, when he led his officers to welcome Mingzong at the eastern suburb. Early in Tiancheng he was made intendant of Henan with authority over the Six Armies and guards, promoted to palace attendant, and transferred to military governor of Bian. When the emperor was about to go on tour, rumor ran wild—first that he would pacify Wu, then that he would reorganize the eastern lords. Shouyin, seized with paranoid suspicion, killed chief commander Ma Yanchao and deputy commissioner Song Jing. [ (From the 《History of the Five Dynasties by Ouyang Xiu》: When Shouyin was about to rebel he summoned chief commander Ma Yanchao to plot with him. Yanchao refused, and Shouyin killed him. Mingzong pitied Yanchao and appointed his son Chengzuo long secretary of Luozhou.)〉 Shouyin drove the townspeople to shut the walls and rebel. Mingzong, marching at Jingshui, heard the news, took personal command of the palace guard, raced to the city, laid siege from all sides, and many defenders were lowered on ropes. Shouyin, exhausted, killed his entire clan, bared his neck, and ordered his attendants to finish him. The imperial army entered the city, hunted down his followers, and executed them all. An edict ordered his corpse flogged and his head displayed in the market. After seven days the head was sent to Luoyang.
6
楊立者，潞州之小校。 初事李嗣昭及李繼韜，皆畜養甚厚。 繼韜被誅，憤憤失誌。 同光二年四月，有詔以潞兵三萬人戍涿州，將發，其眾謀曰：「我輩事故使二十年，衣食豐足，未嘗邊塞征行，苟於邊上差跌，白骨何歸？ 不如據城自固，事成則富貴耳。 」因聚徒百餘輩，攻子城東門，城中大擾。 副使李繼珂及監軍張機祚出奔。 立自稱留後，率軍民上表請旄節。 莊宗怒，命明宗與李紹真攻討，一月拔之，生擒立及其同惡十餘人，送於闕下，皆磔於市。 潞州城峻而隍深，攻立輒敢據之，莊宗因茲詔諸道撤防城之備焉。
Yang Li was a junior officer at Luzhou. He had first served Li Sizhao and Li Jiyao, both of whom treated him generously. When Jiyao was executed, he grew bitter and lost heart. In the fourth month of the second year of Tongguang an edict ordered thirty thousand Luzhou troops to garrison Zhuo. Before they marched, the men plotted: "We served our old lord for twenty years in comfort and never fought on the frontier. If we fail out there, where will our bones lie? Better to hold the city. If we succeed, we gain wealth and rank. " More than a hundred men gathered, stormed the inner east gate, and threw the city into chaos. Deputy commissioner Li Jike and army supervisor Zhang Jizuo fled. Li declared himself acting military governor and led the troops and people in petitioning for the commander's banner and staff. Zhuangzong, furious, ordered Mingzong and Li Shaozhen to attack them. Within a month the city fell. Li and more than ten accomplices were captured alive, sent to the capital, and dismembered in the market. Luzhou's walls were high and its moat deep, yet Li had dared to hold it. Zhuangzong then ordered all circuits to dismantle their city defenses.
7
竇廷琬者，世為青州牙將，梁祖擢置左右。 同光初，為復州遊奕使，奸盜屏跡，曆貝州刺史。 未幾，請製置慶州鹽池，逐年出絹十萬匹，米十萬斛，遂以廷琬為慶州防禦使。 俾製置之，由是嚴刑峻法，屢撓邊人。 課利不集，詔移任於金州。 廷琬據慶州叛，詔邠州節度使李敬周率兵討平之，夷其族。
Dou Tingwan came from a family of military aides at Qing Prefecture. The Liang founder promoted him to personal attendance. Early in Tongguang he was patrol commissioner of Fu Prefecture, where thieves disappeared from his jurisdiction, and he later served as prefect of Bei. Soon he petitioned to take charge of Qing Prefecture's salt ponds, promising an annual tribute of one hundred thousand bolts of silk and one hundred thousand hu of grain, and was appointed defense commissioner of Qing. Once placed in charge, he imposed harsh laws that repeatedly harassed the border population. When the expected revenues failed to materialize, an edict transferred him to Jin Prefecture. Tingwan seized Qing Prefecture and rebelled. Li Jingzhou, military governor of Bin, was ordered to crush the revolt, and Tingwan's entire clan was exterminated.

張虔釗，遼州人也。 〈 （《九國志》云：虔釗，遼州榆社人。 父簡，唐檢校尚書左僕射。）〉 初為太原牙校，以武勇聞於流輩，武皇、莊宗之世，累補左右突騎軍使。 〈 （《九國志》：莊宗嘗以偏師取鎮陽，命虔釗率騎為先鋒，屢挫賊銳，遂陷其城。）〉 明宗素聞虔釗有將帥才，及即位，擢為護駕親軍都指揮使，領春州刺史。 天成中，與諸將圍王都於中山，大敗契丹於嘉山之下，及定州平，以功授滄州節度使。 〈 （《北夢瑣言》：虔釗鎮滄州日，因亢旱民饑，發廩賑之，方上聞，帝甚嘉獎。 他日秋成，倍鬥征斂，朝論鄙之。）〉 移鎮徐州。 長興中，為山南西道節度使兼西面馬步軍都部署。 及末帝起於鳳翔，閔帝詔令虔釗帥部兵會王師於岐下。 洎西師俱變，虔釗憤惋，退歸興元，因與洋州節度使孫漢韶俱送款於蜀。 孟知祥待之尤厚，偽授本鎮節度使，俾知祥坐獲山南之地，由虔釗之故也。 〈 （《北夢瑣言》：入蜀，取人產業，黷貨無厭，蜀民怨之。）〉 孟昶嗣偽位，加檢校太師、兼中書令。 晉開運末，蜀人聞契丹入洛，令虔釗率眾數萬，將寇秦、雍。 俄聞漢高祖已定中原，虔釗無功而退。 〈 （《九國志》云：曆左右匡聖馬步軍都指揮使，出為昭武軍節度使。 及漢祖即位，乃移鎮梁州，以觀朝廷之變。 會晉昌軍節度使趙匡讚、鳳翔節度使侯益俱謀歸蜀，遂以虔釗為北面行營招討使，應接經營。 俄而趙匡讚、侯益請昶出師，掠定三秦，因命虔釗與韓保貞等總師五萬出散關，雄武軍節度使何重建出隴右，奉鑾肅衛都虞候李廷珪出子午穀，會於雍州。 廷珪始出子午穀，聞匡讚為王景崇所逼，棄城自拔東去，遂先退師。 時虔釗、福誠、保貞師次陳倉，謀不相葉，而侯益聞匡讚已去，廷珪班師，亦誠款中變，閉壘不出。 司天監趙廷樞累以雲氣不利為諷，保貞乃與福誠率所部取隴州道，會重建歸蜀，虔釗留寶雞，以勢孤不可深入，遂班師。）〉 行至興州，感憤而卒。
Zhang Qianzhao was from Liao Prefecture. [ (The 《Records of the Nine States》 says Qianzhao was from Yushe in Liao Prefecture. His father Jian had served as Tang acting left vice director of the Department of State Affairs.)〉 He began as a military aide at Taiyuan and won fame among his peers for courage. Under the Martial Emperor and Zhuangzong he rose repeatedly to command the imperial shock cavalry. [ (The 《Records of the Nine States》: When Zhuangzong took Zhenyang with a detached column, he made Qianzhao cavalry vanguard. Qianzhao repeatedly broke the enemy's strength and finally captured the city.)〉 Mingzong had long heard of Qianzhao's generalship. On his accession he made him chief commander of the imperial escort guard and prefect of Chun. During Tiancheng he joined the generals besieging Wang Du at Zhongshan and routed the Khitan below Jia Mountain. When Dingzhou fell, he was rewarded with the military governorship of Cang. [ (The 《Idle Talk from Northern Dreams》: While governing Cang, Qianzhao opened the granaries during a severe drought to feed the starving people. The emperor praised him warmly when the report arrived. But when harvest came he doubled the grain levies, and court opinion turned against him.)〉 He was transferred to Xu Prefecture. During Changxing he became military governor of Shannan West Circuit and grand deployer of the western horse-and-foot forces. When the Last Emperor rose at Fengxiang, Emperor Min ordered Qianzhao to lead his troops to join the imperial army at Qi. When the western armies all defected, Qianzhao, bitter and aggrieved, withdrew to Xingyuan and, with Sun Hanshao of Yang Prefecture, submitted to Shu. Meng Zhixiang treated him generously, confirmed him in his former command, and thereby gained Shannan without a fight—largely thanks to Qianzhao. [ (The 《Idle Talk from Northern Dreams》: In Shu he seized people's property, took bribes without limit, and earned the people's hatred.)〉 When Meng Chang succeeded to the Shu throne, Qianzhao was made acting grand preceptor and concurrent director of the Secretariat. Late in Jin Kaixyun, hearing that the Khitan had entered Luoyang, Shu ordered Qianzhao to lead tens of thousands against Qin and Yong. Soon word came that the Han founder had secured the Central Plains, and Qianzhao withdrew without success. [ (The 《Records of the Nine States》 says he served as chief commander of the left and right imperial guard forces, then became military governor of Zhaowu. When the Han founder took the throne, he was transferred to Liang Prefecture to watch how the court developed. When Zhao Kuangzan of Jinchang and Hou Yi of Fengxiang both plotted to defect to Shu, Qianzhao was made northern campaign pacification commissioner to coordinate the effort. Soon Zhao and Hou asked Chang to march, conquer the Three Qins, and ordered Qianzhao and Han Baozhen to lead fifty thousand men through San Pass, He Chongjian of Xiongwu through Longyou, and Li Tinggui through Ziwu Valley to converge on Yongzhou. Tinggui had barely left Ziwu Valley when he learned Kuangzan was besieged by Wang Jingchong, abandoned his position, and withdrew east—so his column retreated first. Qianzhao, Fucheng, and Baozhen halted at Chencang with divided plans. When Hou Yi learned Kuangzan had fled and Tinggui had withdrawn, he too abandoned his pledge, shut his fort, and refused to march out. The director of astronomy Zhao Tingshu repeatedly cited ill omens in the clouds. Baozhen and Fucheng led their troops toward Long Prefecture, He Chongjian returned to Shu, and Qianzhao, left isolated at Baoji, judged the position untenable and withdrew.)〉 He reached Xing Prefecture and died of grief and resentment.

楊彥溫，汴州人，本梁朝之小校也。 莊宗朝，累遷裨將。 天成中，為河中副指揮使，及末帝鎮河中，尤善待之，因奏為衙內都指揮使。 長興元年四月，乘末帝閱馬於黃龍莊，據城謀叛。 末帝遣人詰之曰：「吾善待汝，何苦為叛？ 」彥溫報曰：「某非敢負恩，緣奉樞密院宣頭，令某拒命，請相公但歸朝廷。 」數日，詔末帝歸朝。 明宗疑其詐，不欲興兵，授彥溫絳州刺史。 安重誨堅請出師，即命西京留守索自通、侍衛步軍指揮使藥彥稠等帥兵攻之。 五日而拔，自閉門及敗，凡十三日。 初，彥稠出師，明宗戒之曰：「與朕生致彥溫，吾將自訊之。 」及收城，斬首傳送，明宗深怒彥稠等。 時議者以當時四海恬然，五兵載戢，蒲非邊郡，近在國門，而彥溫安敢狂悖。 皆以為安重誨方弄國權，尤忌末帝之名，故巧作窺圖，究莫能傾陷也。 彥溫愚昧，為人所嗾，故滅其族焉。
Yang Yanwen of Bianzhou had begun as a petty officer of the Liang. Under Zhuangzong he rose repeatedly to field officer. During Tiancheng he was deputy commander at Hezhong. When the Last Emperor governed there he favored Yanwen and had him named chief of the inner yamen guard. In the fourth month of the first year of Changxing, while the Last Emperor was reviewing horses at Yellow Dragon Manor, Yanwen seized the city and rebelled. The Last Emperor sent someone to demand, "I have treated you well. Why rebel? " Yanwen replied, "I do not mean to betray you. I obeyed a written order from the Bureau of Military Affairs to refuse your command. I ask only that you return to court. " Within days an edict ordered the Last Emperor back to court. Mingzong suspected a ruse and declined to send troops, appointing Yanwen prefect of Jiang. An Chonghui insisted on military action and ordered Western Capital regent Suo Zitong, palace guard foot commander Yao Yanchou, and others to attack. The city fell in five days; from the closing of the gates to defeat, thirteen days in all. Before Yanchou marched, Mingzong warned him, "Bring Yanwen to me alive. I will question him myself. " When the city fell they sent his severed head instead, and Mingzong was furious with Yanchou and his officers. Contemporaries noted that the realm was at peace and arms were sheathed, that Pu was no frontier but lay at the capital's doorstep—how could Yanwen dare such recklessness? Many believed An Chonghui was manipulating state power and feared the Last Emperor's standing, and had engineered the affair to undermine him—yet failed to bring him down. Yanwen was a fool used by others, and his clan was destroyed for it.

史臣曰：《春秋傳》云：「夫不令之臣，天下之所惡也。 」故不復較其優劣焉。 唯虔釗因避地以偷生，彥溫乃為人之所嗾，比諸叛臣，亦可矜也。
The historiographer remarks: The 《Zuo Commentary》 says, "Unworthy ministers are hated throughout the realm. " I therefore do not rank them by relative merit. Qianzhao fled only to survive, and Yanwen was another man's tool; compared with other rebels, both deserve some pity.
