1
天福三年正月戊申朔，帝御崇元殿受朝賀，仗衛如式。 己酉，百官守司，以太史先奏日蝕故也。 至是不虧，內外稱賀。 壬戌，是夜以上元張燈於京城，縱都人遊樂，帝御大寧宮門樓觀之。 丙寅，端明殿學士、禮部侍郎和凝兼判度支； 工部侍郎、判度支王松改尚書刑部侍郎； 戶部郎中高延賞改左諫議大夫，充諸道鹽鐵轉運副使。 壬申，以前右諫議大夫薛融為左諫議大夫。 前興元節度使張筠卒於西京，輟視朝一日。 〈 （《五代會要》：太常禮院申：「準故事，前節度使無例輟朝。 」敕：「宜特輟一日朝參。」）〉
On New Year's Day in the first month of Tianfu 3, the emperor held audience in the Chongyuan Hall to receive felicitations; the guard of honor followed the usual form. On the following day, all officials stayed at their posts, since the Director of Astronomy had already announced an impending solar eclipse. When the moment arrived the sun was not obscured, and court and capital alike rejoiced. That night, for the Lantern Festival, the capital was hung with lights and the urban populace was free to roam in celebration; the emperor watched from the gate tower of the Daning Palace. On bingyin, He Ning, academician of the Duanming Hall and Vice Minister of Rites, was also put in charge of the Bureau of Revenue; Wang Song, Vice Minister of Works who had been adjudicating the Bureau of Revenue, was transferred to Vice Minister of Punishments in the Secretariat; Gao Yanshang, a section member in the Bureau of Accounts, was made Left Remonstrance Councilor and deputy commissioner for salt-and-iron transport on all circuits. On renshen, the former Right Remonstrance Councilor Xue Rong was appointed Left Remonstrance Councilor. The former military governor of Xingyuan, Zhang Yun, died in the Western Capital, and court audiences were suspended for one day. [Editorial note: (From the Documents of the Five Dynasties: The Court of Imperial Sacrifices Directorate of Ceremonies reported, "By precedent, court is never suspended for a former military governor." " Imperial edict: "Suspend court attendance for one day as an exceptional mark of respect.")〉
2
二月庚辰，左散騎常侍張允進《駁赦論》。 帝覽而嘉之，降詔獎飾，仍付史館。 甲申，荊南節度使高從誨加食邑實封。 戊子，翰林學士李浣賜緋魚袋。 以尚書屯田員外郎、知制誥吳承範為庫部員外郎，充樞密院直學士。 乙未，御劄曰：「曾有宣示百官，令進封事，今據到者未及十人。 朕雖無德，自行敕後已是數月，至於假手於人，也合各有一件事敷奏。 食祿於朝，豈當如是！ 言而不用，朕所甘心； 用而不言，誰之責也。 」丙申，制武清軍節度使馬希萼改威武軍節度使。 辛丑，中書上言：「《禮經》云：『禮不諱嫌名，二名不偏諱。 』註云：「『嫌名，謂音聲相近，若禹與宇、邱與區也。 二名不偏諱，謂孔子之母名徵在，言在不稱徵，言徵不稱在。 』此古禮也。 唐太宗二名並諱，玄宗二名亦同； 人姓與國諱音聲相近是嫌名者，亦改姓氏，與古禮有異。 廟諱平聲字，即不諱余三聲； 諱側聲，即不諱平聲字。 所諱字正文及偏旁闕點畫，望依令式施行。 」詔曰：「朝廷之制，今古相沿，道在人宏，禮非天降。 方開歷數，虔奉祖宗，雖逾孔子之文，未爽周公之訓。 所為二名及嫌名事，宜依唐禮施行。」 〈 （案：太原縣有史匡翰碑，立於天福八年。 匡翰，建瑭之子也。 碑於「瑭」字空文以避諱，而建瑭父敬思，仍書「敬」字，蓋當時避諱之體如此。）〉 乙巳，天和節，宴近臣於廣政殿。
In the second month, on gengchen, Zhang Yun, Left Regular Attendant, submitted his "Treatise Refuting Amnesties." The emperor read it with approval, issued an edict praising him, and had the work forwarded to the Historiography Office. On jiashen, Gao Conghui, military governor of Jingnan, was granted additional fief income and substantive enfeoffment. On wuzi, the Hanlin academician Li Huan was awarded the scarlet fish tally pouch. Wu Chengfan, an outer Secretariat official in the state farms section who drafted edicts, was made an outer official in the Storehouse Bureau and appointed a direct academician of the Privy Council. On yiwei, an imperial note read: "I once directed all officials to submit sealed memorials, yet fewer than ten have arrived." I may lack virtue, but months have passed since that edict; even those who rely on subordinates ought each to have brought at least one matter before me. You draw salaries from the court—can this be acceptable? If you speak and I do not act on your words, I can accept that; but if I need counsel and you stay silent—whose fault is that? " On bingshen, Ma Xi'e, military governor of the Wuqing Army, was reassigned as military governor of the Weiwu Army. On xinchou, the Secretariat submitted: "The Book of Rites states, 'Ritual does not require avoiding homophonic names, and for double names one does not avoid only one of the two.' ' The commentary explains: "'Homophonic names' are names whose sounds are close, as with Yu and yu, or Qiu and qu. 'Not avoiding only one of a double name' refers to Confucius's mother, named Zhengzai: when using zai one did not say zheng, and when using zheng one did not say zai. ' Such was the ancient ritual. Emperor Taizong of Tang avoided both elements of double names, and Emperor Xuanzong followed the same practice; when a personal surname sounded like the imperial taboo, the surname was changed as well, which diverged from ancient practice. When the temple taboo was a level-tone character, the other three tones need not be avoided; when the taboo was in oblique tone, level-tone forms were not avoided. For taboo characters, whether written in full or with strokes omitted in the character or its radicals, we ask that the prescribed regulations be followed. " An edict stated: "Court institutions have been handed down through the ages; the Way is enlarged by human effort, and ritual does not fall from heaven. As we inaugurate our dynasty and reverently honor the ancestors, though we go beyond Confucius's letter, we have not strayed from the Duke of Zhou's intent. Matters concerning double names and homophonic names should follow Tang ritual practice. [Editorial note: (Note: Taiyuan County has a stele for Shi Kuanghan, erected in Tianfu 8. Kuanghan was the son of Jiantang. On the stele the character tang was left blank for taboo avoidance, yet Jiantang's father Jingsi still appears with the character jing written out—such was taboo practice at the time.)〉 On yisi, the Tianhe festival, the emperor entertained close ministers in the Guangzheng Hall.
3
四月丁亥，以尚書吏部侍郎盧詹為尚書左丞。 中書舍人李詳上疏：「請沙汰在朝文武臣僚，以減冗食。 仍條貫藩侯郡守，凡遇傅恩，不得多奏衙前職員，妄邀恩澤。 」疏奏，嘉之。 戊子，宣武軍節度、侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使、廣晉府行營都招討使楊光遠加兼中書令。 昭義節度使、侍衛馬軍都指揮使、廣晉府行營都排陣使杜重威，河陽節度使兼奉國左右廂都指揮使、廣晉府行營馬步都虞候侯益，並加檢校太傅。 鳳翔節度使、檢校太師、兼中書令、岐王李從嚴進封秦王，平盧軍節度使、檢校太尉、兼中書令、臨淄王王建立進封東平王。 甲午，泰寧軍節度使李從溫、西京留守京兆尹李周、歸德軍節度使趙在禮，並加兼侍中。 是月，諸道藩侯郡守皆等第加恩。 改雍熙樓為章和樓，避廟諱也。
In the fourth month, on dinghai, Lu Zhan, Vice Minister of Personnel in the Secretariat, was appointed Left Vice Director of the Secretariat. Li Xiang, a drafter in the Palace Secretariat, submitted a memorial: "I request a review to weed out redundant civil and military officials at court and reduce wasteful salaries." Also lay down rules for regional lords and prefects: whenever favors are granted, they must not inflate lists of yamen staff to seek unmerited rewards. The memorial was submitted and received imperial approval. On wuzi, Yang Guangyuan, military governor of Xuanwu, overall commander of the Palace Guard horse and foot, and grand campaign commander of the Guangjin field headquarters, was also appointed Grand Councilor. Du Chongwei, military governor of Zhaoyi, cavalry commander of the Palace Guard, and array marshal of the Guangjin headquarters, and Hou Yi, military governor of Heyang, commander of the Fengguo wings, and chief adjutant of horse and foot at Guangjin, were both promoted to honorary Grand Mentor. Li Congyan of Fengxiang, honorary Grand Preceptor and Grand Councilor, Prince of Qi, was raised to Prince of Qin; Wang Jianli of Pinglu, honorary Grand Marshal and Grand Councilor, Prince of Linzi, was raised to Prince of Dongping. On jiawu, Li Congwen of Taining, Li Zhou, regent of the Western Capital and metropolitan governor of Jingzhao, and Zhao Zaili of Guide were all also appointed Palace Attendant. That month, regional lords and prefects in every circuit received graded imperial favors. The Yongxi Tower was renamed the Zhanghe Tower to avoid the imperial temple taboo.
4
五月丁未朔，帝御崇元殿受朝，仗衛如式。 丁巳，詔應諸州縣名犯廟諱者並改之。 庚申，以楊光遠男承祚為檢校工部尚書、左威衛將軍、駙馬都尉。 丁卯，魏府行營步軍都指揮使、檢校司徒、右神武統軍王周加檢校太保。 戊辰，故振武節度使李嗣本贈太尉。 己巳，詔：「中外臣僚，帶平章事、侍中、中書令及諸道節度使，並許私門立戟，仍並官給及據官品依令式處分。」
On the first day of the fifth month, the emperor held audience in the Chongyuan Hall; the guard of honor followed the usual form. On dingsi, an edict ordered all prefectures and counties whose names violated the imperial temple taboo to be renamed. On gengshen, Chengzuo, son of Yang Guangyuan, was appointed honorary Minister of Works, general of the Left Weiwu Guard, and Commandant of the Imperial Sons-in-law. On dingmao, Wang Zhou, foot commander at the Weizhou field headquarters, honorary Minister of Education, and commander of the Right Shenwu Army, was promoted to honorary Grand Guardian. On wuchen, the late military governor of Zhenwu, Li Siben, was posthumously granted the title Grand Marshal. On jisi, an edict declared: "Officials at court and in the provinces who hold the titles of Grand Councilor, Palace Attendant, Secretariat Director, or military governor may erect ceremonial halberds at their private gates; the state shall supply them, and the number shall follow rank as prescribed in the regulations."
5
六月丁丑，右監門衛上將軍王彥璘卒。 甲申，以太子詹事王居敏制置安邑、解縣兩池榷鹽事。 左諫議大夫薛融上疏，請停修洛京大內。 優詔褒之，尋罷營造。 庚寅，翰林學士、尚書者工部郎中、知制誥竇貞固改中書舍人充職。 戶部尚書致仕蕭蘧卒，贈右僕射。 詔貢舉宜權停一年，以員闕少而選人多，常調有淹滯故也。 丁酉，詔：「尚書司門應管諸闕令丞等，宜準唐天成四年四月四日敕，本司不得差補，只委闕鎮使鈐轄，見差補者，並畫時勒停訖奏聞。 應常帶使相節度使，自楊光遠已下凡七人，並改鄉里名號。」
In the sixth month, on dingchou, Wang Yanlin, senior general of the Right Gate Guard, died. On jiashen, Wang Jumin, Supervisor of the Heir Apparent's Household, was appointed to administer the salt monopoly at the Anyi and Jie pools. Xue Rong, Left Remonstrance Councilor, submitted a memorial requesting a halt to construction of the great inner palace at Luoyang. The emperor issued a gracious edict praising him, and construction was soon halted. On gengyin, Dou Zhengu, Hanlin academician, Secretariat section member in the Ministry of Works, and edict drafter, was transferred to Palace Secretariat drafter to fill the post. Xiao Qu, the retired Minister of Revenue, died and was posthumously granted Right Vice Director of the Secretariat. An edict suspended the civil service examinations for one year, since vacancies were scarce, candidates numerous, and regular appointees long delayed. On dingyou, an edict declared: "For all pass magistrates and assistants under the Secretariat Office of Passes, follow the Tang edict of Tiancheng 4, month 4, day 4: this office may not make appointments; only pass-town commissioners have authority; any improper appointments must cease immediately and be reported." All military governors who regularly hold commissioner-and-councilor rank, seven in all from Yang Guangyuan down, must also change their native-place district names.
6
七月丙午朔，差左諫議大夫薛融、秘書監呂琦、駕部員外郎兼侍御史知雜事劉皞、刑部郎中司徒詡、大理正張仁彖，同共詳定唐明宗朝編敕。 庚戌，御史中丞王延改尚書右丞，尚書右丞盧導改尚書吏部侍郎，以左諫議大夫薛融為御史中丞。 辛酉，制皇帝受命寶，以「受天明命，惟德允昌」為文。 據《六典》，受命寶者，天子修封禪、禮神祇則用之，其始皆破皇業錢以制之。 皇業者，藩邸主事之所有也。 〈 （《五代會要》：天福三年六月，中書門下奏：「準敕，制皇帝受命寶。 今按唐貞觀十六年，太宗文皇帝所刻之璽，白玉為螭首，其文曰『皇帝景命，有德者昌』。 」敕：「宜以『受天明命，惟德允昌』為文刻之。」）〉 壬戌，虞部郎中、知制誥於遘改中書舍人。 宰臣趙瑩、桑維翰、李崧各改鄉里名號。 荊南節度使高從誨本貫汴州浚儀縣王畿鄉表節東坊，改為擁旌鄉浴鳳裏。
On the first day of the seventh month, Xue Rong, Lü Qi, Liu Hao, Situ Xu, and Zhang Renmin were jointly assigned to review and finalize the compiled edicts of Emperor Mingzong of Tang. On gengxu, Wang Yan, Censor-in-chief, became Right Vice Director of the Secretariat; Lu Dao, Right Vice Director, became Vice Minister of Personnel; and Xue Rong, Left Remonstrance Councilor, became Censor-in-chief. On xinyou, the imperial Seal of Received Mandate was ordered cast with the inscription "Receiving Heaven's clear mandate; only virtue brings flourishing." According to the Six Institutions, the Seal of Received Mandate was used when the Son of Heaven performed the feng and shan rites or worshipped the spirits; originally each was cast by melting coin from the imperial estate. Imperial-estate coin was property held by the prince's household steward. [Editorial note: (From the Documents of the Five Dynasties: In the sixth month of Tianfu 3, the Secretariat reported, "By edict, the imperial Seal of Received Mandate is to be cast." We find that in Tang Zhenguan 16, Emperor Taizong had a seal carved of white jade with a dragon head, inscribed "The Emperor's bright mandate: he who has virtue flourishes." " Edict: "Inscribe it with the text 'Receiving Heaven's clear mandate; only virtue brings flourishing.'")〉 On renxu, Yu Gou, section member in the Parks Bureau and edict drafter, was appointed Palace Secretariat drafter. The chief ministers Zhao Ying, Sang Weihan, and Li Song each changed their native-place district names. Gao Conghui of Jingnan, originally registered in Biaojie East ward, Wangji township, Junyi county, Bianzhou, had his registration changed to Yufeng lane, Yongjing township.
7
八月戊寅，以左僕射劉句為契丹冊禮使，左散騎常侍韋勛副之，給事中盧重為契丹皇太后冊禮使。 壬午，魏府軍前奏，前澶州刺史馮暉自逆城來歸。 定州奏，境內旱，民多流散。 詔曰：「朕自臨寰宇，每念生民，務切撫綏，期於富庶，屬干戈之未戢，慮徭役之或煩。 惟彼中山，偶經夏旱，因茲疾苦，遽至流移，達我聽聞，深懷憫惻。 應定州所差軍前夫役逃戶夏秋稅並放。 」甲申，襄州奏，漢江水漲一丈一尺。 己丑，以前澶州刺史馮暉為檢校太保，充義成軍節度使。 詔：「河府、同州、絳州等三處災旱，逃移人戶下所欠累年殘稅，並今年夏稅差科，及麥苗子沿征諸色錢物等並放。 其逃戶下秋苗，據見檢到數不計是元額及出剩頃畝，並放一半。 委觀察使散行曉諭，專切招攜。 應歸業戶人，仍指揮逐縣切加安撫。 」丙申，翰林學士、中書舍人竇貞固上言：「請令文武百僚，逐司之內，各奏舉一人，述其人有某能，堪為某官某職。 據所薦否藏，定舉主黜陟。」 〈 （《宋史·竇貞固傳》載此疏，略云：為國之要，進賢是先。 陛下方樹丕基，宜求多士。 乞降詔百僚，令各司議定一人，有何能識，堪何職官，朝廷依奏用之。 若能符薦引，果謂當才，所奏之官，望加獎賞。 如乖其舉，或涉徇私，所奏之官，宜加黜罰。 自然官由德序，位以才升。 三人同行，尚聞擇善； 十目所視，必不濫知。 臣職在論思，敢陳狂狷。）〉 疏奏，嘉之。 仍令文武百官於搢紳之內、草澤之中，知灼然有才器者，列名以奏。 宴契丹冊禮使於廣政殿。 戊戌，鄆州奏，陽谷縣界河決。 青州王建立奏，高麗國宿衛質子王仁翟乞放歸鄉里。 從之。 辛丑，鎮、邢、定三州奏，奉詔共差樂官六十七人往契丹。 詔：「魏府城下，自屯軍已來，墳墓多經劚掘。 雖已差人收掩，今更遣太僕卿邢德昭往伸祭奠。」
In the eighth month, on wuyin, Liu Gou, Left Vice Director, was appointed Khitan investiture envoy, with Wei Xun, Left Regular Attendant, as deputy; Lu Chong, Drafting Attendant, was appointed investiture envoy to the Khitan empress dowager. On renwu, the Weizhou field army reported that Feng Hui, former prefect of Chanzhou, had surrendered from the rebel stronghold. Dingzhou reported drought within its territory and widespread displacement of the populace. An edict read: "Since I took the throne I have constantly thought of the people, striving to comfort them and bring prosperity; yet with war not yet ended, I fear that labor levies may become oppressive." Zhongshan alone has suffered an unexpected summer drought; hardship has driven people to flee in numbers that have reached my ears and stirred deep compassion. All summer and autumn taxes owed by fugitive households previously levied for military labor service in Dingzhou are remitted. On jiashen, Xiangzhou reported that the Han River had risen eleven feet. On jichou, Feng Hui, former prefect of Chanzhou, was appointed honorary Grand Guardian and military governor of Yicheng. An edict declared: "For He prefecture, Tongzhou, and Jiangzhou, where drought has brought disaster, all tax arrears owed by displaced households, this year's summer levies, and all assorted charges including wheat-seedling taxes are remitted." For autumn grain tax on fugitive households, based on verified amounts regardless of original quota or surplus acreage, half is remitted. Surveillance commissioners are charged to circulate proclamations and devote themselves to bringing people back. Households that return to their occupations are to be earnestly reassured, county by county, as directed. On bingshen, Dou Zhengu, Hanlin academician and Palace Secretariat drafter, proposed: "Let every civil and military bureau each recommend one person, stating his abilities and the office or post for which he is suited." Promotion or demotion of the recommender shall depend on whether the recommendation proves true or false. [Editorial note: (The biography of Dou Zhengu in the Song History records this memorial, in summary: The essential task of governing a state is to advance the worthy first. Your Majesty is just laying the foundations of the dynasty; you should seek many capable men. I beg that Your Majesty issue an edict to all officials, directing each bureau to nominate one person, stating his abilities and the office for which he is suited, and the court will appoint him as recommended. If the recommendation proves true and the nominee is truly capable, the office he holds should receive added reward. If the recommendation fails or involves favoritism, the recommender's office should face added demotion or punishment. Thus offices will be ordered by virtue and positions advanced by talent. Even when three walk together, one may still choose the good among them; with ten eyes upon him, talent will surely not be misjudged. My office is to counsel the throne; I venture this frank and unorthodox proposal.)〉 The memorial was presented and received imperial approval. He further directed all civil and military officials to nominate, from among the gentry and from the common people, anyone of manifest talent, reporting their names in memorials. The emperor entertained the Khitan envoy bearing the investiture patent in the Guangzheng Hall. On wuxu, Yanzhou reported a breach of the river at the Yanggu county boundary. Wang Jianli of Qingzhou memorialized that Wang Renzhai, a Goryeo hostage serving in the imperial guard, asked to be sent home. The request was granted. On xinchou, Zhenzhou, Xingzhou, and Dingzhou reported that by imperial order they had jointly sent sixty-seven music officers to the Khitan. An edict stated: "Around Weifu, since troops were encamped there, many graves have been plundered by digging. Although reburial parties were already sent, Xing Dezhao, Minister of the Imperial Studs, is now additionally dispatched to offer sacrifices and rites of mourning."

On the first day of the twelfth month, the former Minister of War Liang Wenju was appointed Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent; Fu Meng, deputy military governor of Zhenzhou, became Right Remonstrance Councilor; and Cao Guozhen, a section member in the Ministry of Personnel, became Left Remonstrance Councilor. On bingzi, Li Dechong, former military governor of Zhangyi at Jingzhou, was appointed military governor of the Jianxiong Army at Jinzhou and Associate Chief Minister. Chonggui, senior general of the Right Golden Crow Guard to the Heir Apparent, was appointed honorary Grand Tutor and metropolitan administrator of Kaifeng, enfeoffed as Prince of Zheng, and granted three thousand additional households of fief income. On wuyin, Ma Jirong, tribute envoy of Great Treasure Khotan and honorary Grand Marshal, was appointed General Who Stabilizes the State; his deputy Zhang Zaitong, General of the Yellow Gate and Vice Director of the Directorate of Education, was made acting Commandant of the Court of the Imperial Guards; and Wu Shungui, supervising envoy and palace edict bearer, was made acting Vice Director of the Directorate of Works. Li Wanjin, commissioner of the Uyghur mission, was appointed General of Righteous Submission; Lei Deshun, supervising envoy, was appointed General of Harmonious Transformation. That day an edict declared: "Throughout the empire, whether public or private, anyone with copper who wishes to cast coin may do so freely, determining weight as they see fit." On wuzi, the former Hidden Dragon residence at Heyang was converted into the Kaijin Chan monastery, and the former Hidden Dragon residence at Xingzhou into the Guangfa Chan monastery. Li Congchang, commander of the Dragon Martial Army, died; court audiences were suspended for one day, and he was posthumously granted the title Grand Marshal.
