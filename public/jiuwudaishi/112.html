1
廣順元年冬十月己丑朔，宰臣王峻獻唐張蘊古《大寶箴》、謝偃《惟皇誡德賦》二圖。 詔報曰：「朕生長軍戎，勤勞南北，雖用心於《鈐》、《匱》，且無暇於《詩》、《書》，世務時艱，粗經閱歷，前言往行，未甚討尋。 卿有佐命立國之勛，居代天調鼎之任，恒慮眇德，未及古人。 於是采掇箴規，弼諧寡昧，披文閱理，懌意怡神，究為君治國之源，審修己御人之要。 帝王之道，盡在於茲，辭翰俱高，珠寶何貴！ 再三省覽，深用愧嘉。 其所進圖，已令於行坐處張懸，所冀出入看讀，用為鑒戒。 」壬辰，潞州奏，巡檢使陳思讓、監軍向訓破河東賊軍於虒亭。 癸巳，以刑部侍郎司徒詡為戶部侍郎，以左散騎常侍張煦為刑部侍郎，以給事中呂咸休為左散騎常侍。 甲午，絳州防禦使孫漢英卒。 辛丑，荊南奏，湖南亂，大將軍陸孟俊執偽節度使馬希萼遷於衡州，立希萼弟希崇為留後，將吏二千餘人，遇害者半，牙署庫藏焚燒殆盡。 乙巳，詔並吏部三銓為一銓，委本司長官通判。 丙午，晉州巡檢王萬敢奏，河東劉崇入寇，營於州北。 辛亥，潞州奏，河東賊軍寇境。 乙卯，荊南奏，淮南遣鄂州節度使劉仁贍，以戰船二百艘於今月二十五日入岳州。 丙辰，詔樞密使王峻率兵援晉州。 丁巳，以左衛將軍申師厚為河西軍節度使、檢校太保。 師厚素與王峻善，及峻貴，師厚羈旅無依，日於峻馬前望塵而拜，會西涼請帥，帝令擇之，無欲去者，峻乃以師厚奏之，師厚亦欣然求往，尋自前鎮將授左衛將軍、檢校工部尚書。 翌日，乃有涼州之命，賜旌節、駝馬、繒帛以遣之。
On the new moon of the tenth month, winter, Guangshun year 1, Chief Minister Wang Jun presented two illustrated works from the Tang: Zhang Yungu's Great Treasure Admonition and Xie Yan's Exhortation on Imperial Virtue. An edict in reply said: "I grew up in the camps and have labored north and south. Though I have bent my mind to military registers and ordnance chests, I have had no leisure for the Odes and Documents. The troubles of the age I have only roughly lived through, and the words and deeds of former times I have not deeply studied." You have the merit of aiding the Mandate and establishing the state, and you hold the charge of tuning the national tripod on Heaven's behalf; you have always worried that my slight virtue does not measure up to the ancients. You therefore gathered admonitions and maxims to assist my dull understanding. Reading the text and weighing its principles delights the mind and calms the spirit; it investigates the source of ruling and governing the state, and clearly discerns the essentials of cultivating oneself and directing others. The way of emperors and kings is wholly contained in these works; the prose and calligraphy are both superb — what jewel could be more precious! I have read them again and again and am deeply ashamed of my shortcomings, yet greatly pleased by your gift. The scrolls you presented have been ordered hung wherever I walk or sit, so that as I come and go I may read them and take them as a mirror and warning. " On renchen, Lu Prefecture reported that Patrol Commissioner Chen Siyang and Military Supervisor Xiang Xun had defeated the Hedong rebel army at Si Ting. On guisi, Situ Xu, Vice Minister of the Ministry of Punishment, was made Vice Minister of Revenue; Zhang Xu, Left Regular Attendant, was made Vice Minister of Punishment; and Lü Xianxiu, Secretariat Drafter, was made Left Regular Attendant. On jiawu, Sun Hanying, Defense Commissioner of Jiang Prefecture, died. On xinchou, Jingnan reported that Hunan was in disorder: Grand General Lu Mengjun seized the puppet Military Commissioner Ma Xie, relocated him to Heng Prefecture, and installed Xie's younger brother Xichong as acting commissioner. Of more than two thousand officers and clerks, half were killed, and the headquarters stores were burned almost to nothing. On yisi, an edict merged the Ministry of Personnel's three selection boards into one and entrusted joint oversight to the ministry's chief official. On bingwu, Jin Prefecture Patrol Officer Wang Wangan reported that Liu Chong of Hedong had invaded and was encamped north of the prefecture. On xinhai, Lu Prefecture reported that Hedong rebel forces were raiding the border. On yimao, Jingnan reported that Huainan had dispatched E Prefecture Military Commissioner Liu Renzan with two hundred warships, which entered Yue Prefecture on the twenty-fifth of this month. On bingchen, an edict ordered Privy Council Commissioner Wang Jun to lead troops to relieve Jin Prefecture. On dingsi, Shen Shihou, General of the Left Guard, was appointed Military Commissioner of the Hexi Army and Acting Grand Guardian. Shihou had long been friendly with Wang Jun. When Jun rose to power, Shihou, adrift without patronage, daily bowed in the dust before Jun's horse. When Western Liang requested a commander, the emperor ordered someone chosen, but no one wished to go. Jun then recommended Shihou, who gladly sought the post. Shortly before, he had been promoted from a former frontier commander to Left Guard General and Acting Minister of Works. The next day the appointment to Liang Prefecture was issued, and he was sent off with banners of authority, camels, horses, and silks.
2
十一月己未朔，荊南奏，淮南大將邊鎬率兵三萬，自袁州路趨潭州，馬希崇遣從事送牌印，納器仗。 鎬入城，稱武安軍節度使，馬氏諸族及將吏千餘人皆徙於金陵。 甲子夜，東南白虹亙天。 以新晉州節度使王彥超為晉絳行營馬軍都虞候。 乙丑，命王峻出征晉州，帝幸西莊以餞之。 甲戌，日南至，群臣拜表稱賀。 甲申，葬故貴妃張氏。 丁亥，詔：「唐朝五廟，舊在至德宮安置，應屬徽陵莊田園舍，宜令新除右監門將軍李重玉為主。 其緣陵緣廟法物，除合留外，所有金銀器物，充遷葬故淑妃王氏及許王從益外，其餘並給與重玉及尼惠英、惠燈、惠能、惠嚴等。 令重玉以時祀陵廟，務在豐潔。 」重玉，故皇城使李從璨之子，明宗之孫，惠英等亦明宗親屬也，故帝授重玉官秩，令主先祀，恤王者之後也。
On the new moon of the eleventh month, jiawei, Jingnan reported that Huainan's Grand General Bian Hao was leading thirty thousand men from the Yuan Prefecture route toward Tan Prefecture, and that Ma Xichong had sent an aide to deliver his seals and insignia and surrender his arms. Hao entered the city and took the title Military Commissioner of the Wu'an Army; more than a thousand members of the Ma clan and their officers were relocated to Jinling. On the night of jiazi, a white rainbow spanned the sky in the southeast. Wang Yanchao, the newly appointed Military Commissioner of Jin Prefecture, was made Chief Adjutant of Cavalry for the Jin-Jiang campaign headquarters. On yichou, Wang Jun was ordered to march on Jin Prefecture, and the emperor went to the Western Villa to give him a farewell feast. On jiaxu, the winter solstice arrived, and the ministers presented congratulatory memorials. On jiashen, the late Honored Consort Zhang was buried. On dinghai, an edict said: "The Tang dynasty's five ancestral temples, formerly kept at Zhide Palace, together with the estates and gardens belonging to Huiling, should be entrusted to the newly appointed Right Gate General Li Chongyu. Of the ritual objects for the tomb and temples, aside from what must be kept, all gold and silver vessels—except those needed to reinter the late Shufei Wang and Prince Xu Congyi—were to be given to Chongyu and the nuns Huiying, Huideng, Huineng, Huiyan, and the rest. Chongyu was ordered to perform timely sacrifices at the tomb and temples, with offerings to be ample and splendid. " Chongyu was the son of the former Imperial City Commissioner Li Congcan and a grandson of Emperor Mingzong; Huiying and the others were also Mingzong's kin. The emperor therefore granted Chongyu office and rank, put him in charge of the former sacrifices, and showed compassion for a king's descendants.
3
十二月戊子朔，詔以劉崇入寇，取當月三日暫幸西京。 庚寅，詔巡幸宜停。 時王峻駐軍陜府，聞帝西巡，遣使馳奏，不勞車駕順動，帝乃止。 乙未，幸西莊。 兗州慕容彥超上言，乞朝覲，詔允之，尋稱部內草寇起，不敢離鎮。 戊申，鄆州奏，慕容彥超據城反。 己酉，王峻奏，劉崇逃遁，王師已入晉州。 〈 （《宋史·陳思讓傳》：王峻援晉州，以思讓與康延昭分為左右廂排陣使，令率軍自烏嶺路至絳州，與大軍合。 崇燒營遁去，思讓又與藥元福襲破之。）〉
On the new moon of the twelfth month, wuzi, an edict—because Liu Chong had invaded—set the third day of that month for a temporary visit to the Western Capital. On gengyin, an edict ordered the tour suspended. Wang Jun was then encamped at Shan Prefecture. When he heard the emperor was touring west, he sent a fast messenger urging that the imperial carriage need not move; the emperor then halted the plan. On yiwei, the emperor visited the Western Villa. Murong Yanchao of Yan Prefecture memorialized asking to come to court; the edict assented, but soon he claimed bandits had risen in his territory and dared not leave his post. On wushen, Yun Prefecture reported that Murong Yanchao had seized the city and rebelled. On jiyou, Wang Jun reported that Liu Chong had fled and that imperial forces had already taken Jin Prefecture. [Editorial note: (From the Song History, Biography of Chen Siyang: When Wang Jun relieved Jin Prefecture, he made Siyang and Kang Yanzhao marshals of the left and right wings, ordering them to lead troops from Wuling Road to Jiang Prefecture to join the main army. Chong burned his camp and fled; Siyang again joined Yao Yuanfu in a surprise attack and routed him.)
4
廣順二年春正月戊午朔，不受朝賀，以宿兵在外故也。 庚申，王峻奏，起近鎮丁夫二萬城晉州。 壬戌，修東京羅城，凡役丁夫五萬五千，兩旬而罷。 甲子，以侍衛步軍都指揮使曹英為兗州行營都部署，以齊州防禦使史延韜為副部署，以皇城使向訓為兵馬都監，陳州防禦使藥元福為馬步都虞候，率兵討慕容彥超。 〈 （《隆平集》：慕容彥超盜據兗、海，周祖命曹英為帥，向訓副之，參用藥元福以兵從。 謂元福曰：「已敕英、訓，勿以軍禮見汝。 」及元福至，英、訓皆父事焉。）〉 諸軍入兗州界，不得下路停止村舍，犯者以軍法從事。 丙寅，徐州巡檢供給官張令彬奏，破淮賊於沭陽，斬首千餘級，擒賊將燕敬權。 時慕容彥超求援於淮南，淮南偽主李景發兵援之，師於下邳，聞官軍至，退趨沭陽，遂破之。 庚午，高麗權知國事王昭遣使貢方物。 壬申，鎮州何福進差人部送先擒獲到河東賊軍二百餘人至闕下，詔給巾履衫袴以釋之。 戊寅，徐州部送沭陽所獲賊將燕敬權等四人至闕下，詔賜衣服金帛，放歸本土，敬權等感泣謝罪。 帝召見謂之曰：「夫惡兇邪，獎忠順，天下一也。 我之賊臣，撓亂國法，嬰城作逆，殃及生靈，不意吳人助茲兇惡，非良算也，爾當歸言之於爾君。 」初，漢末遣三司軍將路昌祚於湖南市茶，屬淮南將邊鎬陷長沙，昌祚被賊送金陵。 及敬權自大朝歸，具以帝言告於李景，景乃召昌祚，延坐從容久之，且稱美大朝皇帝聖德廣被，恩沾鄰土，深有依附國家之意。 及罷，遣偽宰相宋齊丘宴昌祚於別館，又令訪昌祚在湖南遭變之時，亡失綱運之數，命依數償之，給茗荈萬八千斤，遣水運至江夏，仍厚給行裝，遣之歸闕。
On the new moon of the first month, spring, Guangshun year 2, wuwu, the court did not receive congratulatory audiences, because troops were still in the field. On gengshen, Wang Jun reported that twenty thousand corvée laborers from nearby prefectures had been levied to fortify Jin Prefecture. On renxu, the outer wall of the Eastern Capital was repaired; fifty-five thousand corvée laborers were employed, and the work was completed in twenty days. On jiazi, Cao Ying, Commander of the Imperial Guard Infantry, was made Overall Commander of the Yan campaign headquarters; Shi Yantao, Defense Commissioner of Qi Prefecture, was made Deputy Commander; Xiang Xun, Imperial City Commissioner, was made Overall Military Supervisor; Yao Yuanfu, Defense Commissioner of Chen Prefecture, was made Chief Adjutant of Horse and Foot, and led troops against Murong Yanchao. [Editorial note: (From the Longping Collection: Murong Yanchao seized Yan and Hai by force. The Zhou founder appointed Cao Ying commander and Xiang Xun his deputy, with Yao Yuanfu joining the army. He told Yuanfu: "I have already ordered Ying and Xun not to receive you with full military ceremony. " When Yuanfu arrived, both Ying and Xun treated him with the deference due a father.) When the armies entered Yan territory, they were forbidden to leave the road and halt in villages; violators were punished under military law. On bingyin, Xuzhou Patrol Supply Officer Zhang Lingbin reported defeating Huai rebels at Shuyang, beheading more than a thousand and capturing the rebel general Yan Jingquan. Murong Yanchao was then seeking aid from Huainan; Huainan's puppet ruler Li Jing sent troops to help him. The army was at Xiapi; when it heard the imperial army had arrived, it retreated toward Shuyang and was defeated there. On gengwu, Wang Zhao, Acting Ruler of Goryeo, sent envoys presenting tribute. On renshen, He Fujin of Zhen Prefecture sent men escorting more than two hundred previously captured Hedong rebel troops to the capital; an edict gave them caps, shoes, shirts, and trousers and released them. On wuyin, Xuzhou delivered to the capital four captives taken at Shuyang, among them the rebel general Yan Jingquan. An edict awarded them clothing and silk and sent them home; Jingquan and the others wept in gratitude and begged forgiveness. The emperor summoned them and said: "To hate the vicious and reward the loyal is one principle throughout the realm. My rebel subject has disturbed the laws of the state, held his city in revolt, and harmed the people. I did not expect the Wu people to aid such wickedness — it is no sound policy. You should return and tell your lord. " Earlier, toward the end of Han, the Three Departments had dispatched the army officer Lu Changzuo to Hunan to purchase tea. When the Huainan general Bian Hao captured Changsha, Changzuo was taken by the rebels and sent to Jinling. When Jingquan returned from the Great Court, he fully reported the emperor's words to Li Jing. Jing then summoned Changzuo and entertained him at leisure for a long while, praising the Great Court emperor's sagely virtue and his grace reaching neighboring lands, and showing deep intent to attach to the state. When the audience ended, he sent the puppet chancellor Song Qiqiu to feast Changzuo at a separate lodge, and also had him inquire how much tea revenue Changzuo had lost when disaster struck in Hunan, ordering compensation in full; he gave eighteen thousand jin of tea, sent it by water to Jiangxia, gave generous travel supplies, and sent him back to court.
5
二月庚寅，府州防卸使折德扆奏，河東賊軍寇境，率州兵破之，斬首二千級。 辛卯，太白經天。 癸巳，以權知高麗國事王昭為高麗國王。 庚子，府州防卸使折德扆奏，收河東界岢嵐軍。 癸卯，詔先獲河東鄉軍一百餘人，各給錢鞋放歸鄉里。 壬子，太子太師致仕安審暉卒。
In the second month, on gengyin, Zhe Dewei, Defense Commissioner of Fu Prefecture, reported that Hedong rebel troops raided the border; he led prefectural troops, defeated them, and beheaded two thousand. On xinmao, Venus crossed the heavens. On guisi, Wang Zhao, Acting Ruler of Goryeo, was made King of Goryeo. On gengzi, Zhe Dewei, Defense Commissioner of Fu Prefecture, reported recovering Kelan Army on the Hedong border. On guimao, an edict ordered that each of the more than a hundred Hedong militiamen already captured be given money and shoes and sent back to their home districts. On renzi, An Shenhui, Retired Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent, died.
6
三月庚申，幸南莊，令從臣習射。 戊辰，以樞密院直學士、左諫議大夫王溥為中書舍人，充翰林學士； 以內客省使、恩州團練使鄭仁誨為樞密副使。 詔宣徽北院使翟光鄴權知永興軍府事。 甲戌，回鶻遣使貢方物。 庚辰，詔：「西京莊宅司、內侍省、宮苑司、內園等四司，所管諸巡系稅戶二千五百並還府縣。 其廣德、升平二宮並停廢。 應行從諸莊園林、亭殿、房舍、什物課利，宜令逐司依舊收管。」
In the third month, on gengshen, the emperor visited the Southern Villa and ordered his attendants to practice archery. On wuchen, Wang Pu, Privy Council Academician and Left Remonstrance Counselor, was appointed Secretariat Drafter and Hanlin Academician; Zheng Renhui, Inner Palace Commissioner and Militia Commissioner of En Prefecture, was made Vice Commissioner of the Privy Council. An edict ordered Zhai Guangye, North Courtyard Commissioner of the Palace Secretariat, to serve as acting administrator of Yongxing Military Prefecture. On jiaxu, the Uyghurs sent envoys presenting tribute. On gengchen, an edict said: "The four offices—the Western Capital Estates Office, Inner Service Directorate, Palace Parks Office, and Inner Gardens—together with the 2,500 tax households under their patrol districts, are all to be returned to prefectures and counties. The Guangde and Shengping palaces are both to be abolished. Income from the estates, gardens, pavilions, halls, dwellings, and goods that serve the imperial entourage should still be collected by each office according to former practice."
7
夏四月丙戌朔，日有食之，帝避正殿，百官守司。 丁亥，詔停蔡州鄉軍。 戊子，以京師旱，分命群臣禱雨。 癸巳，制削奪慕容彥超在身官爵。 甲午，高麗國冊使、衛尉卿劉皞卒。 乙卯，詔取來月五日，車駕赴兗州城下，慰勞將士。 以樞密副使鄭仁誨為右衛大將軍，依前充職，兼權大內都點檢； 以中書侍郎、平章事、判三司李穀為權東京留守，兼判開封府事。
In the fourth month of summer, on the new moon, bingxu, there was an eclipse; the emperor avoided the main hall and officials kept their posts. On dinghai, an edict halted the Cai Prefecture militia. On wuzi, because the capital suffered drought, various ministers were separately ordered to pray for rain. On guisi, a decree stripped Murong Yanchao of all offices and ranks he held. On jiawu, Liu Hao, Goryeo investiture envoy and Minister of the Imperial Clan, died. On yimao, an edict set the fifth day of the coming month for the imperial carriage to go below the walls of Yan Prefecture to comfort the troops. Zheng Renhui, Vice Commissioner of the Privy Council, was made General of the Right Guard, continuing in his former post and also acting as overall inspector of the Inner Palace; Li Gu, Vice Director of the Secretariat, Grand Councilor, and Commissioner of the Three Departments, was made acting Eastern Capital regent and also acting administrator of Kaifeng Prefecture.
8
五月丙辰朔，帝御崇元殿受朝，仗衛如儀。 庚申，車駕發京師。 戊辰，至兗州城下。 乙亥，收復兗州，斬慕容彥超，夷其族。 詔端明殿學士顏衎權知兗州軍州事。 壬午，曲赦兗州管內罪人，取五月二十七日已前所犯罪，大辟已下，咸赦除之。 慕容彥超徒黨，有逃避潛竄者，及城內將吏等並放罪。 自慕容彥超違背以來，鄉州內有接便為非者，一切不問。 諸軍將士歿於王事者，各與賻贈，都頭已上與贈官。 兗州城內及官軍下寨四面去州五里內，今年所征夏秋稅及沿征錢物並放； 十里內，只放夏稅； 一州管界，今夏苗子三分放一分。 城內百姓遭毀拆舍屋及遭燒焚者，給賜材木。 諸處差到人夫內，有遭矢石死者，各給絹三匹，仍放戶下三年徭役雲。 癸未，詔兗州降為防禦州，仍為望州。
On the new moon of the fifth month, bingchen, the emperor held court at Chongyuan Hall with guards and insignia as prescribed. On gengshen, the imperial carriage departed the capital. On wuchen, it reached below the walls of Yan Prefecture. On yihai, Yan Prefecture was retaken; Murong Yanchao was executed and his entire clan destroyed. An edict made Yan Yan, Academician of the Duanming Hall, acting military commissioner of Yan Prefecture. On renwu, a partial amnesty was issued for criminals within Yan Prefecture, taking crimes committed before the twenty-seventh day of the fifth month; from capital offenses down, all were pardoned. Those among Murong Yanchao's followers who had fled and gone into hiding, together with officers and clerks inside the city, were all pardoned. Since Murong Yanchao's defection, anyone in the countryside who had seized the moment to commit wrongdoing was not to be prosecuted. Officers and soldiers who died in the service of the throne each received burial subsidies; unit heads and above were granted posthumous titles. Within five li of Yan city and of the army camps on all four sides, this year's summer and autumn taxes and all additional levies were waived; within ten li, only the summer tax was remitted; throughout the prefecture's jurisdiction, one-third of this summer's seed tax was remitted. City residents whose dwellings had been torn down or burned were given building timber. Among the corvée laborers drafted from various districts who died from arrows and stones, each household received three bolts of silk and was exempted from three years of labor service. On guiwei, an edict reduced Yan Prefecture to a defense prefecture while still ranking it a premier prefecture.

" They answered: "The former magistrate of Qufu, Kong Renyu, who inherited the title Duke of Cultured Propagation, is the forty-third-generation descendant of Zhongni; there is also Yan She, who presented the Three Rites by provincial examination and is a descendant of Yan Yuan. An order was also issued to Yan Prefecture to repair Confucius's temple buildings and to forbid woodcutting and foraging beside the tomb. On bingxu, the imperial carriage returned to the capital. Earlier, the emperor had reached Yan Prefecture on the thirteenth day of the fifth month while the rebels still held out. On the seventeenth, in a daytime dream a Taoist presented a book whose opening line read, "The imperial carriage will return to the capital on the second day of next month"; the text below was exceedingly long and could not all be remembered. When he awoke, he told the chief ministers of the dream; four days later the city fell. From the emperor's arrival at the army until the rebels were pacified, he halted his progress for nine days in all and indeed departed from below the city walls on the second day of the sixth month. In recent times no personal campaign had won victory so swiftly. That day brought heavy rain; at the traveling palace below the city, the water stood several feet deep. That evening, upon reaching Zhongdu County, the emperor smiled and said to his attendants: "Had we not left below the city today, we would have been drowned in the flood. On gengyin, an edict declared: "When prefects leave their posts or come to court, they may not offer weapons as tribute." Previously, each circuit and prefecture maintained its own workshops, charged every month with making military equipment and every quarter sending it to the capital as tribute. Every year each prefecture held back a considerable portion of provincial funds and goods under what were called "armor-material" quotas, then imposed broad assessments on local products within its territory, collecting several times the proper amount; the people bore the burden bitterly. Apart from military equipment supplied to the court, military commissioners and prefects also privately made armor and weapons under the pretext of tribute; the labor and expense were again doubled, all taken from the people. Because armor and weapons made in the various prefectures were poorly crafted and provincial goods were excessively retained, the emperor ordered this stopped. Skilled artisans from the circuits were also chosen and sent to the capital workshops to serve when needed. " On jimao, the winter solstice, the emperor ascended Chongyuan Hall to receive the court's congratulations; guards and ceremonial honors followed the prescribed rites.

十二月丙戌，權武平軍留後劉言遣牙將張崇嗣入奏，於十月十三日，與節度副使王進逵、行軍司馬何敬貞、指揮使周行逢等，同共部領戰棹，攻收湖南，偽節度使邊鎬當夜出奔，王進逵等已入潭州。 〈 （《九國誌·王逵傳》：逵，朗州武陵人，或名進逵。 邊鎬為武安軍節度使，召劉言入覲，言不行，謀於逵曰：「江南召我，不往，必加兵於我矣，為之奈何！ 」逵曰：「鎬之此來，以制置潭、朗為名，公如速行，正入其算。 武陵負江湖之阻，帶甲百萬，乃欲拱手臣異姓乎？ 鎬新至長沙，經略未定，乘人心憤怒，引兵攻鎬，可一鼓而擒也。 」言然之，乃遣與何景真等同起兵於武陵，號十指揮使，以攻邊鎬。 逵率舟師南上，至長沙，邊鎬大駭，以所部奔歸江南，諸州屯守皆罷之，盡復湖外之地。）〉 癸巳，太子太師致仕安叔千卒。 甲午，詔今後諸侯入朝，不得進奉買宴。 丁酉，皇子澶州節度使榮落起復，加同平章事。 戊戌，太子少傅致仕王延卒。 壬寅，幸西莊。 乙巳，以端明殿學士顏衎權知開封府事。 御史臺奏：「請改左右衛復為左右屯衛。 」從之，避御名也。 是冬無雪。
On bingxu in the twelfth month, Liu Yan, Acting Administrator of Wuping Army, sent his adjutant Zhang Chongsi to report that on the thirteenth day of the tenth month he, together with Vice Military Commissioner Wang Jinkui, Chief of Staff He Jingzhen, and Commander Zhou Xingfeng and others, had jointly led war vessels to attack and recover Hunan; the false Military Commissioner Bian Hao fled by night, and Wang Jinkui and the others had already entered Tan Prefecture. [Editorial note: (From the 《Record of the Nine States》, Biography of Wang Kui: Kui was a native of Wuling in Lang Prefecture, also called Jinkui. Bian Hao was Military Commissioner of Wu'an Army and summoned Liu Yan to court; Yan would not go and consulted Kui, saying: "The Jiangnan court summons me; if I do not go, they will surely send troops against me. What is to be done! " Kui said: "Hao has come this time under the pretext of administering Tan and Lang. If you move swiftly, you will walk straight into his trap. Wuling stands behind rivers and lakes that form a natural barrier and can put a million armed men in the field — yet you would meekly bow to a ruler of another surname? Hao has only just reached Changsha and his grip is not yet secure. Strike while the people are angry, lead your troops against Hao, and you can take him in a single charge. " Yan assented and sent him, together with He Jingzhen and others, to raise troops at Wuling. They styled themselves the Ten-Finger Commanders and marched against Bian Hao. Kui led the river fleet south to Changsha. Bian Hao was terrified and fled with his troops back to Jiangnan. Garrisons in every prefecture were pulled back, and all the lands beyond the lake were recovered.) On guisi, An Shuqian, retired Grand Mentor of the Heir Apparent, died. On jiawu, an edict declared that henceforth when feudatories came to court, they might not present gifts to purchase banquets. On dingyou, Prince Rong, Military Commissioner of Chanzhou, left mourning and resumed office, and was made Associate Grand Councilor. On wuxu, Wang Yan, retired Junior Tutor of the Heir Apparent, died. On renyin, the emperor visited West Manor. On yisi, Yan Kan, Academician of the Hall of Brilliant Clarity, was appointed acting administrator of Kaifeng Prefecture. The Censorate memorialized: "We ask that the Left and Right Guard be restored to their former names, Left and Right Garrison Guard. " Approved — to avoid the emperor's taboo name. That winter there was no snow.
16
廣順三年春正月壬子朔，帝御崇元殿受朝賀，仗衛如儀。 幸太平宮起居漢太后。 甲寅，賜群臣射於內鞠場。 乙卯，武平軍兵馬留後劉言奏：「潭州干戈之後，焚燒殆盡，乞移使府於武陵。 」從之。 詔升朗州為大都督府，在潭州之上。 丙辰，以武平軍節度使留後、檢校太尉劉言為檢校太師、同平章事，行朗州大都督，充武平軍節度兼三司水陸轉運等使，制置武安、靜江等軍事，進封彭城郡公； 武平軍節度副使、權知潭州軍州事、檢校太傅王進逵為檢校太尉，行潭州刺史，充武安軍節度使； 以武安軍行軍司馬兼衙內步軍都指揮使、檢校太傅何敬貞為檢校太尉，行桂州刺史，充靜江軍節度使； 以張仿領眉州刺史，充武平軍節度副使； 以朱元琇領黃州刺史，充靜江軍節度副使； 以周行逢領集州刺史，充武安軍節度行軍司馬。 自進逵而下，皆劉言將校也。 邠州奏，慶州略蕃部野雞族略奪商旅，侵擾州界。 詔遣寧州刺史張建武等率兵掩襲，仍先賜敕書安撫，如不從命，即進軍問罪。 辛酉，詔賜朗州劉言應兩京及諸道舊屬湖南樓店邸第。 乙丑，詔：「諸道州府系屬戶部營田及租稅課利等，除京兆府莊宅務、贍國軍榷鹽務、兩京行從莊外，其餘並割屬州縣，所征租稅課利，官中只管舊額，其職員節級一切停廢。 應有客戶元佃系省莊田、桑土、舍宇，便賜逐戶，充為永業，仍仰縣司給與憑由。 應諸處元屬營田戶部院及系縣人戶所納租中課利，起今年後並與除放。 所有見牛犢並賜本戶，官中永不收系」云。 帝在民間，素知營田之弊，至是以天下系官莊田僅萬計，悉以分賜見佃戶充永業。 是歲出戶三萬餘，百姓既得為己業，比戶欣然，於是葺屋植樹，敢致功力。 又，東南郡邑各有租牛課戶，往因梁太祖渡淮，軍士掠民牛以千萬計，梁太祖盡給與諸州民，輸租課，自是六十餘載，時移代改，牛租猶在，百姓苦之，至是特與除放。 未幾，京兆府莊宅務及榷鹽務亦歸州縣，依例處分。 或有上言，以天下系官莊田，甚有可惜者，若遣貨之，當得三十萬緡，亦可資國用。 帝曰：「茍利於民，與資國何異。 」丁卯，戶部侍郎、權知貢舉趙上交奏：「諸科舉人，欲等第各加對義場數； 進士除詩賦外，別試雜文一場。 」從之。 兩浙吊祭使、左諫議大夫李知損責授登州司馬，員外置，仍令所在馳驛放遣。 知損銜命江、浙，所經藩郡，皆強貸於侯伯，為青州知州張凝所奏，故有是命。 己巳，幸南莊。 臨水亭，見雙鳧戲於池上，帝引弓射之，一發疊貫，從臣稱賀。 庚午，以前邠州節度使侯章為鄧州節度使。 前萊州刺史葉仁魯賜死，坐為民所訟故也。 辛未，詔樞密使王峻巡視河堤。 峻請行，故從之。 辛巳，幸南莊。
On the new moon of the first month, spring, Guangshun year 3, the emperor took the throne at Chongyuan Hall to receive court congratulations; guards and honors followed the rites. He visited Taiping Palace to inquire after the Han Empress Dowager. On jiayin, the emperor granted the ministers archery at the inner ball court. On yimao, Liu Yan, Acting Administrator of Wuping Army, memorialized: "After the fighting at Tan Prefecture the city was burned nearly to ash. We beg leave to move the commissionerate to Wuling. " Approved. An edict raised Lang Prefecture to a great metropolitan prefecture, placing it above Tan Prefecture in rank. On bingchen, Liu Yan, Acting Administrator of Wuping Army and Honorary Grand Guardian, was appointed Honorary Grand Preceptor and Associate Grand Councilor, made acting Metropolitan Prefect of Lang, Military Commissioner of Wuping Army and Commissioner for the Three Departments' land and water transport, given authority over military affairs in Wu'an and Jingjiang, and advanced to Duke of Pengcheng Commandery; Wang Jinkui, Vice Military Commissioner of Wuping Army, acting administrator of Tan, and Honorary Grand Mentor, was made Honorary Grand Guardian, acting Prefect of Tan, and Military Commissioner of Wu'an Army; He Jingzhen, Chief of Staff of Wu'an Army, Commander of the Inner Step Army, and Honorary Grand Mentor, was appointed Honorary Grand Guardian, acting Prefect of Gui, and Military Commissioner of Jingjiang Army; Zhang Fang was appointed acting Prefect of Mei and Vice Military Commissioner of Wuping Army; Zhu Yuanxiu was appointed acting Prefect of Huang and Vice Military Commissioner of Jingjiang Army; Zhou Xingfeng was appointed acting Prefect of Ji and Chief of Staff of Wu'an Army. From Jinkui downward, all were Liu Yan's officers. Bin Prefecture reported that the Yezi, a frontier tribe under Qing Prefecture, had robbed travelers and raided across the prefectural border. An edict sent Zhang Jianwu of Ning Prefecture and others to lead troops in a surprise strike, first dispatching a reassuring edict; if the tribe refused to comply, the army was to advance and punish them. On xinyou, an edict granted Liu Yan of Lang Prefecture the shops and mansions in the two capitals and on the circuits that had formerly belonged to Hunan. On yichou, an edict declared: "Revenues from camp fields, taxes, and commercial levies under the Ministry of Revenue in prefectures and circuits throughout the empire — except for the Estate Office of Jingzhao Prefecture, the Salt Monopoly Office of the Army Supporting the State, and the traveling estates of the two capitals — shall all be transferred to prefectures and counties. Collected taxes and levies shall not exceed the old quotas, and all related staff ranks and grades are abolished. Tenant clients who had long rented state estates, mulberry land, and buildings would receive them household by household as permanent property, with the county office issuing title certificates. Commercial levies embedded in the rents paid by households formerly tied to camp-field offices of the Ministry of Revenue and to county households would all be waived from this year on. All oxen and calves currently in the system would be granted to their original households, and the court would never reclaim them," the edict said. The emperor had known the abuses of camp fields since his days among the people. Now nearly ten thousand state estates throughout the empire were divided and granted outright to the tenants who worked them. That year more than thirty thousand households were freed. Once the land was truly theirs, household after household rejoiced; people repaired their homes, planted trees, and at last dared invest their labor. In the southeastern commanderies and districts there were also rent-ox levy households. Long ago, when Emperor Taizu of Liang crossed the Huai, his soldiers had seized civilian oxen by the millions; the emperor then distributed them to the people of the various prefectures to meet rent obligations. For more than sixty years since, through changing dynasties, the ox rent had never been lifted, and the people suffered under it. Now it was specially waived. Before long, the Estate Office of Jingzhao Prefecture and the Salt Monopoly Office were also returned to prefectural and county control and administered according to precedent. Some memorialized that many state estates throughout the empire were far too valuable to waste; if sold, they might bring in three hundred thousand strings of cash and help fill the treasury. The emperor said: "If it benefits the people, how is that different from enriching the state. " On dingmao, Zhao Shangjiao, Vice Minister of Revenue and Acting Examination Commissioner, memorialized: "Candidates in every examination category should, according to rank, be given additional oral-examination sessions; For jinshi candidates, aside from poetry and fu, there should be an additional examination in miscellaneous prose. " Approved. Li Zhisun, Mourning Commissioner for the Two Zhes and Left Remonstrance Censor, was demoted to Regional Administrator of Deng Prefecture on an extra-statutory appointment and ordered expelled by urgent relay from wherever he was posted. Zhisun had been dispatched on imperial business to Jiang and Zhe; at every prefecture he passed through he extorted loans from the regional lords. Zhang Ning, Prefect of Qing, memorialized against him, and this order followed. On jisi, the emperor visited South Manor. At the waterside pavilion he saw two mandarin ducks playing on the pond. The emperor drew his bow and shot once, transfixing both; his attendants cried their congratulations. On gengwu, Hou Zhang, former Military Commissioner of Bin, was appointed Military Commissioner of Deng. Ye Renlu, former Prefect of Lai, was granted permission to take his own life after the people brought suit against him. On xinwei, an edict ordered Privy Council Commissioner Wang Jun to inspect the river dikes. Jun had requested to go, and therefore the order was granted. On xinsi, the emperor visited South Manor.

閏月甲申，朗州劉言、潭州王進逵奏，廣賊占據桂管，深入永州界俘劫，遣朗州行軍司馬何敬貞與指揮使朱全琇、陳順等，率水陸軍五萬進擊。 丙戊，回鶻遣使貢方物。 詔故梁租庸使趙巖侄崇勛，見居陳州，量賜系官店宅，從王峻之請也。 辛卯，定州奏，契丹攻義豐軍，出勁兵夜斫蕃營，斬首六十級，契丹遁去。 甲午，鎮州奏，契丹寇境，遣兵追襲，至無極而還。 丙申，皇子澶州節度使榮來朝。 壬寅，以樞密使、尚書左僕射、同平章事、監修國史王峻兼青州節度使，餘如故。 延州衙內指揮使高紹基奏言：「父允權患腳膝，令臣權知軍州事。 」癸卯，陳州奏：「吏民請與前刺史李穀立祠堂。 」從之。 時穀為宰相，聞郡人陳請，遜讓數四，乃止。 甲辰，鄴都留守王殷加檢校太尉，依前同平章事。 丙午，鎮州節度使何福進、河陽節度使王彥超並加檢校太尉，潞州節度使李筠加檢校太傅。 丁未，延州節度使高允權卒。 己酉，開封府奏，都城內錄到無名額僧尼寺院五十八所。 詔廢之。
On the intercalary month, jiashen, Liu Yan of Lang and Wang Jinkui of Tan memorialized that Guang bandits held Guiguan, raided deep into Yong Prefecture, and carried off captives and loot. They dispatched He Jingzhen, Chief of Staff of Lang, with Commanders Zhu Quanxiu, Chen Shun, and others to lead fifty thousand land and water troops in an advance against them. On bingxu, the Uighurs sent envoys presenting local products. By edict, Chongxun, nephew of the late Revenue Commissioner Zhao Yan of Liang and then living in Chen Prefecture, was granted state shops and mansions in due measure — at Wang Jun's request. On xinmao, Ding Prefecture reported that the Khitan had attacked Yifeng Army. Elite troops went out by night, struck the barbarian camp, took sixty heads, and the Khitan withdrew. On jiawu, Zhen Prefecture reported a Khitan border raid. Troops were sent in pursuit as far as Wuji and then withdrew. On bingshen, Prince Rong, Military Commissioner of Chanzhou, came to court. On renyin, Wang Jun — Privy Council Commissioner, Left Vice Minister of the Department of State Affairs, Associate Grand Councilor, and Commissioner for the Compilation of National History — was additionally appointed Military Commissioner of Qing; his other titles remained unchanged. Gao Shaoji, Inner Garrison Commander of Yan Prefecture, memorialized: "My father Yunquan is afflicted in the knees and feet and has directed me to serve as acting administrator of the prefecture and army. " On guimao, Chen Prefecture memorialized: "Officials and commoners ask that a shrine be built to the former Prefect Li Gu. " Approved. Gu was chief councilor at the time. When he learned that the people of the commandery had petitioned, he demurred again and again, and the plan was abandoned. On jiachen, Wang Yin, Administrator of Yedu, was advanced to Honorary Grand Guardian while retaining his Associate Grand Councilor title. On bingwu, He Fujin, Military Commissioner of Zhen, and Wang Yanchao, Military Commissioner of Heyang, were both promoted to Honorary Grand Guardian; Li Yun, Military Commissioner of Lu, was promoted to Honorary Grand Mentor. On dingwei, Gao Yunquan, Military Commissioner of Yan Prefecture, died. On jiyou, Kaifeng Prefecture reported that fifty-eight temples and monasteries — Buddhist and Daoist alike — lacking registered name plaques had been logged within the capital. An edict abolished them.

二月辛亥朔，以前西京留守白文珂為太子太師致仕，進封韓國公。 癸丑，安州節度使李洪義、侍衛馬軍都指揮使郭崇、侍衛步軍都指揮使曹英，並加檢校太尉。 唐州方城縣令陳守愚棄市，坐克留戶民蠶鹽一千五百斤入己也。 內制國寶兩座，詔中書令馮道書寶文，其一以「皇帝承天受命之寶」為文，其一以「皇帝神寶」為文。 案，傳國寶始自秦始皇，令李斯篆之，歷代傳授，事具前史。 至唐末帝自燔之際，以寶隨身，遂俱焚焉。 晉高祖受命，特制寶一座，開運末，契丹犯闕，少帝遣其子延煦送於戎王，戎主，訝其非真，少帝上表具訴其事，及契丹北歸，賫以入蕃。 漢朝二帝，未暇別制，至是始創為之。 庚申，遣將作監李瓊知陜州軍州事。 甲子，樞密使、平盧軍節度使、尚書左僕射、平章事、監修國史王峻責授商州司馬，員外置，所在馳驛發遣。 戊辰，左監門上將軍李建崇卒。 延州牙內都指揮使高紹基奏，交割軍府與副使張圖。 己巳，朗州劉言奏，當道先遣行軍司馬何敬貞率兵掩擊廣賊，行及潭州，部眾奔潰，湖南王進逵以敬貞失律，已梟首訖。 以樞密直學士、工部侍郎陳觀為秘書監。 壬申，鳳翔少尹桑能責授鄧州長史。 能，晉相維翰之庶弟也，坐據維翰別第為人所訟故也。 癸酉，以戶部侍郎、知貢舉趙上交為太子詹事。 是歲，新進士中有李觀者，不當策名，物議喧然，中書門下以觀所試詩賦失韻，勾落姓名，故上交移官。 丁丑，幸南莊，賜從官射。 命客省使向訓權知延州軍州事。
On the new moon of the second month, Bai Wenke, former Administrator of the Western Capital, was made retired Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent and advanced to Duke of Han. On guichou, Li Hongyi, Military Commissioner of An; Guo Chong, Commander of the Palace Horse Guard; and Cao Ying, Commander of the Palace Step Guard were all advanced to Honorary Grand Guardian. Chen Shouyu, Magistrate of Fangcheng in Tang Prefecture, was executed in the marketplace for keeping fifteen hundred jin of silkworm salt levied from the households for himself. By inner-court order two imperial seals were cast; the emperor commanded Secretariat Director Feng Dao to inscribe them — one reading "Seal of the Emperor Receiving the Mandate from Heaven," the other "Seal of the Divine Emperor." Note: the seal of dynastic transmission dates to Qin Shihuang, who had Li Si cut its inscription in seal script; it passed from dynasty to dynasty, as the earlier histories record. When the last Tang emperor set himself ablaze, he kept the seal on his person, and it was consumed with him in the fire. When Emperor Gaozu of Jin received the Mandate, a new seal was specially made. At the end of Kaiyun, when the Khitan stormed the capital, the last emperor sent his son Yans Xu to deliver it to the Khitan ruler. The Khitan king doubted its authenticity; the last emperor submitted a memorial setting out the full facts. When the Khitan withdrew north, the seal was carried off into their lands. The two Han emperors had never found time to replace it; only now was a new seal first fashioned. On gengshen, Li Qiong, Director of the Directorate of Palace Buildings, was dispatched to govern Shaan Prefecture. On jiazi, Wang Jun — Privy Council Commissioner, Military Commissioner of Pinglu Army, Left Vice Minister of the Department of State Affairs, Grand Councilor, and Commissioner for the Compilation of National History — was demoted to Regional Administrator of Shang Prefecture on an extra-statutory appointment and ordered expelled by urgent relay from wherever he was posted. On wuchen, Li Jianchong, Left Gate General of the Upper Rank, died. Gao Shaoji, Inner Garrison Commander of Yan Prefecture, memorialized that he had handed over military headquarters to Vice Commissioner Zhang Tu. On jisi, Liu Yan of Lang memorialized that his circuit had earlier sent Chief of Staff He Jingzhen to lead a surprise strike against Guang bandits. When the column reached Tan Prefecture the troops broke and fled. Wang Jinkui of Hunan, finding that Jingzhen had violated military discipline, had already executed him. Chen Guan, Privy Council Academician and Vice Minister of Works, was appointed Director of the Secretariat. On renshen, Sang Neng, Assistant Administrator of Feng, was demoted to Senior Administrator of Deng. Neng was the younger half-brother of the Jin chancellor Sang Weihan. He was punished because he had seized Weihan's private residence and others brought suit against him. On guiyou, Zhao Shangjiao, Vice Minister of Revenue and Examination Commissioner, was appointed Mentor of the Heir Apparent. That year among the newly passed jinshi was a candidate named Li Guan who should never have been listed. Public outcry was fierce. The Secretariat and Chancellery found that Guan's examination poetry and fu broke the rhyme rules and struck his name from the rolls; Shangjiao was reassigned as a result. On dingchou, the emperor visited South Manor and granted archery to his attendants. The Envoy of the Reception Bureau Xiang Xun was appointed acting administrator of Yan Prefecture.
