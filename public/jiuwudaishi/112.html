1
廣順元年冬十月己丑朔，宰臣王峻獻唐張蘊古《大寶箴》、謝偃《惟皇誡德賦》二圖。 詔報曰：「朕生長軍戎，勤勞南北，雖用心於《鈐》、《匱》，且無暇於《詩》、《書》，世務時艱，粗經閱歷，前言往行，未甚討尋。 卿有佐命立國之勛，居代天調鼎之任，恒慮眇德，未及古人。 於是采掇箴規，弼諧寡昧，披文閱理，懌意怡神，究為君治國之源，審修己御人之要。 帝王之道，盡在於茲，辭翰俱高，珠寶何貴！ 再三省覽，深用愧嘉。 其所進圖，已令於行坐處張懸，所冀出入看讀，用為鑒戒。 」壬辰，潞州奏，巡檢使陳思讓、監軍向訓破河東賊軍於虒亭。 癸巳，以刑部侍郎司徒詡為戶部侍郎，以左散騎常侍張煦為刑部侍郎，以給事中呂咸休為左散騎常侍。 甲午，絳州防禦使孫漢英卒。 辛丑，荊南奏，湖南亂，大將軍陸孟俊執偽節度使馬希萼遷於衡州，立希萼弟希崇為留後，將吏二千餘人，遇害者半，牙署庫藏焚燒殆盡。 乙巳，詔並吏部三銓為一銓，委本司長官通判。 丙午，晉州巡檢王萬敢奏，河東劉崇入寇，營於州北。 辛亥，潞州奏，河東賊軍寇境。 乙卯，荊南奏，淮南遣鄂州節度使劉仁贍，以戰船二百艘於今月二十五日入岳州。 丙辰，詔樞密使王峻率兵援晉州。 丁巳，以左衛將軍申師厚為河西軍節度使、檢校太保。 師厚素與王峻善，及峻貴，師厚羈旅無依，日於峻馬前望塵而拜，會西涼請帥，帝令擇之，無欲去者，峻乃以師厚奏之，師厚亦欣然求往，尋自前鎮將授左衛將軍、檢校工部尚書。 翌日，乃有涼州之命，賜旌節、駝馬、繒帛以遣之。
On the new moon of the tenth month of winter in the first year of Guangshun, Grand Councilor Wang Jun presented two illustrated works from the Tang—Zhang Wenggu's Admonition on the Great Treasure and Xie Yan's Rhapsody Admonishing the Sovereign's Virtue. The emperor replied by edict: "I was raised in the military camps and have labored on campaigns north and south. Though I have given my attention to manuals of war and strategy, I have had little time for the Odes and Documents. I have only a rough acquaintance with the hardships of our times, and I have not deeply studied the wisdom of former ages. You aided the founding of the dynasty and now bear the weight of governing the realm in Heaven's stead; I constantly worry that my modest virtue falls short of the ancients. You have therefore gathered these admonitions to guide my limited understanding. Reading them has delighted and refreshed me, for they probe the roots of kingship and statecraft and clarify what it means to cultivate oneself and govern others. The way of emperors and kings is fully set forth here. Both works are superb in style—what could pearls and jewels add? I have read them again and again and am deeply humbled and grateful. I have ordered your scrolls hung wherever I walk or sit, so that I may read them at every turn and keep their lessons before me. " On renchen, Luzhou reported that Inspection Commissioner Chen Siyang and Army Supervisor Xiang Xun had routed the Hedong rebel forces at Siting. On guisi, Situ Xu was transferred from Vice Minister of Justice to Vice Minister of Revenue; Zhang Xu moved from Left Honorary Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary to Vice Minister of Justice; and Drafting Counselor Lü Xianxiu was appointed Left Honorary Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary. On jiawu, Sun Hanying, military commissioner of Jiang Prefecture, died. On xinchou, Jingnan reported turmoil in Hunan: Grand General Lu Mengjun seized the Southern Chu ruler Ma Xie, removed him to Heng Prefecture, and installed Xie's younger brother Xichong as acting commissioner. Of more than two thousand officials and troops, half were killed, and the headquarters treasury was almost wholly destroyed by fire. On yisi, an edict merged the Ministry of Personnel's three selection boards into one, placing overall responsibility with the ministry's chief official. On bingwu, Jin Prefecture inspector Wang Wangan reported that Liu Chong of Hedong had raided the border and pitched camp north of the prefectural seat. On xinhai, Luzhou reported Hedong rebel forces raiding the frontier. On yimao, Jingnan reported that Huainan had dispatched E Prefecture military commissioner Liu Renshan with two hundred warships, which entered Yue Prefecture on the twenty-fifth of that month. On bingchen, the emperor ordered Privy Council Commissioner Wang Jun to march to the relief of Jin Prefecture. On dingsi, Left Guard General Shen Shihou was appointed military commissioner of the Hexi army and honorary grand guardian. Shihou had long been friendly with Wang Jun. After Jun rose to power, Shihou, stranded without patronage, would bow in the dust before Jun's horse every day. When the Western Liang region requested a military commissioner, the emperor ordered a choice made, but no one wanted the post. Jun therefore recommended Shihou, who eagerly volunteered. Soon afterward he was promoted from garrison commander to Left Guard General and honorary minister of works. The following day the Liangzhou appointment came through, and he was sent off with insignia of command, camels, horses, and silks.
2
十一月己未朔，荊南奏，淮南大將邊鎬率兵三萬，自袁州路趨潭州，馬希崇遣從事送牌印，納器仗。 鎬入城，稱武安軍節度使，馬氏諸族及將吏千餘人皆徙於金陵。 甲子夜，東南白虹亙天。 以新晉州節度使王彥超為晉絳行營馬軍都虞候。 乙丑，命王峻出征晉州，帝幸西莊以餞之。 甲戌，日南至，群臣拜表稱賀。 甲申，葬故貴妃張氏。 丁亥，詔：「唐朝五廟，舊在至德宮安置，應屬徽陵莊田園舍，宜令新除右監門將軍李重玉為主。 其緣陵緣廟法物，除合留外，所有金銀器物，充遷葬故淑妃王氏及許王從益外，其餘並給與重玉及尼惠英、惠燈、惠能、惠嚴等。 令重玉以時祀陵廟，務在豐潔。 」重玉，故皇城使李從璨之子，明宗之孫，惠英等亦明宗親屬也，故帝授重玉官秩，令主先祀，恤王者之後也。
On the new moon of the eleventh month, jiwei, Jingnan reported that Huainan's chief general Bian Hao was marching thirty thousand men from Yuan Prefecture toward Tan. Ma Xichong sent an aide to deliver his seals of office and surrender his arms. Bian Hao entered the city and took the title of military commissioner of the Wu'an army. More than a thousand members of the Ma clan and their officials were resettled at Jinling. On the night of jiazi, a white rainbow arched across the southeastern sky. Wang Yanchao, the newly appointed military commissioner of Jin Prefecture, was named chief controller of cavalry for the Jin-Jiang campaign headquarters. On yichou, Wang Jun was ordered to take the field against Jin Prefecture, and the emperor went to the Western Villa to see him off. On jiaxu, the winter solstice, the court officials submitted congratulatory memorials. On jiashen, the late Honored Consort Zhang was interred. On dinghai, an edict declared: "The five Tang ancestral temples, formerly kept at Zhide Palace, along with the estates and gardens attached to Emperor Zhuangzong's mausoleum, shall be placed under the newly appointed Right Gate General Li Chongyu as custodian. Of the ritual objects tied to the mausoleum and temples, everything that need not be kept—all gold and silver vessels except those required to rebury the late Virtuous Consort Wang and Prince Xu Congyi—shall be given to Chongyu and to the nuns Huiying, Huideng, Huineng, Huiyan, and the rest. Chongyu was charged to perform the scheduled sacrifices at the mausoleum and temples with full and proper offerings. " Chongyu was the son of the former director of the imperial city, Li Congcan, and a grandson of Emperor Mingzong of Tang; Huiying and the other nuns were also Mingzong's kin. The emperor therefore gave Chongyu an official post and put him in charge of the ancestral rites—a gesture of care toward the fallen dynasty's line.
3
十二月戊子朔，詔以劉崇入寇，取當月三日暫幸西京。 庚寅，詔巡幸宜停。 時王峻駐軍陜府，聞帝西巡，遣使馳奏，不勞車駕順動，帝乃止。 乙未，幸西莊。 兗州慕容彥超上言，乞朝覲，詔允之，尋稱部內草寇起，不敢離鎮。 戊申，鄆州奏，慕容彥超據城反。 己酉，王峻奏，劉崇逃遁，王師已入晉州。 〈 （《宋史·陳思讓傳》：王峻援晉州，以思讓與康延昭分為左右廂排陣使，令率軍自烏嶺路至絳州，與大軍合。 崇燒營遁去，思讓又與藥元福襲破之。）〉
On the new moon of the twelfth month, wuzi, an edict announced that because Liu Chong had invaded, the emperor would make a temporary visit to the Western Capital on the third of the month. On gengyin, an edict canceled the planned tour. Wang Jun was then encamped at Shan Prefecture. When he heard the emperor planned to travel west, he sent an urgent dispatch urging that the imperial progress was unnecessary, and the emperor called off the journey. On yiwei, the emperor visited the Western Villa. Murong Yanchao of Yan Prefecture asked permission to come to court; the emperor assented, but Yanchao soon claimed that bandits had broken out in his territory and that he dared not leave his post. On wushen, Yan Prefecture reported that Murong Yanchao had rebelled and seized the city. On jiyou, Wang Jun reported that Liu Chong had fled and the imperial forces had taken Jin Prefecture. [ (According to the Song History, Biography of Chen Siyang: When Wang Jun marched to relieve Jin Prefecture, he appointed Siyang and Kang Yanzhao marshals of the left and right wings and ordered them to advance from the Wuling route to Jiang Prefecture to join the main army. Chong burned his camp and fled; Siyang then joined Yao Yuanfu in a surprise attack that routed him.)]
4
廣順二年春正月戊午朔，不受朝賀，以宿兵在外故也。 庚申，王峻奏，起近鎮丁夫二萬城晉州。 壬戌，修東京羅城，凡役丁夫五萬五千，兩旬而罷。 甲子，以侍衛步軍都指揮使曹英為兗州行營都部署，以齊州防禦使史延韜為副部署，以皇城使向訓為兵馬都監，陳州防禦使藥元福為馬步都虞候，率兵討慕容彥超。 〈 （《隆平集》：慕容彥超盜據兗、海，周祖命曹英為帥，向訓副之，參用藥元福以兵從。 謂元福曰：「已敕英、訓，勿以軍禮見汝。 」及元福至，英、訓皆父事焉。）〉 諸軍入兗州界，不得下路停止村舍，犯者以軍法從事。 丙寅，徐州巡檢供給官張令彬奏，破淮賊於沭陽，斬首千餘級，擒賊將燕敬權。 時慕容彥超求援於淮南，淮南偽主李景發兵援之，師於下邳，聞官軍至，退趨沭陽，遂破之。 庚午，高麗權知國事王昭遣使貢方物。 壬申，鎮州何福進差人部送先擒獲到河東賊軍二百餘人至闕下，詔給巾履衫袴以釋之。 戊寅，徐州部送沭陽所獲賊將燕敬權等四人至闕下，詔賜衣服金帛，放歸本土，敬權等感泣謝罪。 帝召見謂之曰：「夫惡兇邪，獎忠順，天下一也。 我之賊臣，撓亂國法，嬰城作逆，殃及生靈，不意吳人助茲兇惡，非良算也，爾當歸言之於爾君。 」初，漢末遣三司軍將路昌祚於湖南市茶，屬淮南將邊鎬陷長沙，昌祚被賊送金陵。 及敬權自大朝歸，具以帝言告於李景，景乃召昌祚，延坐從容久之，且稱美大朝皇帝聖德廣被，恩沾鄰土，深有依附國家之意。 及罷，遣偽宰相宋齊丘宴昌祚於別館，又令訪昌祚在湖南遭變之時，亡失綱運之數，命依數償之，給茗荈萬八千斤，遣水運至江夏，仍厚給行裝，遣之歸闕。
On the new moon of the first month of spring in the second year of Guangshun, wuwu, the court declined the usual New Year congratulations because troops were still on campaign. On gengshen, Wang Jun reported that twenty thousand laborers from nearby garrisons had been drafted to rebuild Jin Prefecture's walls. On renxu, repairs began on the outer wall of the Eastern Capital. Fifty-five thousand laborers were conscripted, and the work was finished in twenty days. On jiazi, Palace Guard infantry commander Cao Ying was appointed overall commander of the Yanzhou campaign; Qi Prefecture defense commissioner Shi Yantao became his deputy; imperial city commissioner Xiang Xun was named supervisor of troops and horses; and Chen Prefecture defense commissioner Yao Yuanfu was made chief controller of cavalry and infantry. They marched to suppress Murong Yanchao. [ (According to the Longping Collection: Murong Yanchao had seized Yan and Hai by force. The Zhou founder appointed Cao Ying commander, with Xiang Xun as his deputy, and brought Yao Yuanfu along with the army. He told Yuanfu, "I have already instructed Ying and Xun not to treat you with military deference. " When Yuanfu arrived, however, both Ying and Xun treated him with the respect due a father.)] As the armies entered Yan Prefecture, they were forbidden to leave the main road or billet in villages; offenders were punished under military law. On bingyin, Xuzhou inspector and supply officer Zhang Lingbin reported a victory over Huainan forces at Shuyang: more than a thousand heads taken and the rebel general Yan Jingquan captured. Murong Yanchao had asked Southern Tang for help, and Li Jing of Huainan sent reinforcements, encamping at Xiapi. When they learned the imperial army was coming, they fell back toward Shuyang and were defeated there. On gengwu, Wang Zhao, who was acting as regent of Goryeo, sent envoys bearing tribute goods. On renshen, He Fujin of Zhen Prefecture sent to the capital more than two hundred captured Hedong soldiers. The emperor ordered them given clothing and footwear and released. On wuyin, Xuzhou delivered to court the four enemy officers captured at Shuyang, including Yan Jingquan. The emperor gave them clothing and gold silks and sent them home. Jingquan and his companions wept as they thanked him for their lives. The emperor received them and said, "To condemn wickedness and reward loyalty is a single principle throughout the realm. My own rebel has defied the law, walled himself in, and brought suffering on the people. I did not expect the Wu court to aid such villainy—that was no wise policy. Go home and tell your sovereign what I have said. " Earlier, near the end of the Later Han dynasty, the court had sent Revenue Bureau officer Lu Changzuo to Hunan to buy tea. When Huainan's Bian Hao took Changsha, Changzuo was carried off to Jinling as a captive. When Jingquan returned from the Zhou court, he repeated the emperor's message to Li Jing. Jing summoned Changzuo, received him at length with courtesy, and spoke admiringly of the Zhou emperor's far-reaching virtue, his kindness toward neighboring lands, and his wish to draw their states closer. Afterward he had his chancellor Song Qiqiu entertain Changzuo at a guest lodge and inquired how much tax tea had been lost when disaster struck in Hunan. Jing ordered full compensation, presented eighteen thousand jin of tea, shipped it by water to Jiangxia, furnished generous traveling expenses, and sent Changzuo back to the Zhou capital.
5
二月庚寅，府州防卸使折德扆奏，河東賊軍寇境，率州兵破之，斬首二千級。 辛卯，太白經天。 癸巳，以權知高麗國事王昭為高麗國王。 庚子，府州防卸使折德扆奏，收河東界岢嵐軍。 癸卯，詔先獲河東鄉軍一百餘人，各給錢鞋放歸鄉里。 壬子，太子太師致仕安審暉卒。
On gengyin in the second month, Fu Prefecture defense commissioner Zhe Deyi reported a Hedong raid on his border. He led the prefectural troops to victory and took two thousand heads. On xinmao, Venus crossed the heavens. On guisi, Wang Zhao, who had been acting regent of Goryeo, was recognized as king of Goryeo. On gengzi, Zhe Deyi reported the recapture of Kelan Garrison on the Hedong frontier. On guimao, an edict ordered that the more than one hundred captured Hedong militiamen be given money and shoes and sent home. On renzi, An Shenhui, retired grand preceptor of the heir apparent, died.
6
三月庚申，幸南莊，令從臣習射。 戊辰，以樞密院直學士、左諫議大夫王溥為中書舍人，充翰林學士； 以內客省使、恩州團練使鄭仁誨為樞密副使。 詔宣徽北院使翟光鄴權知永興軍府事。 甲戌，回鶻遣使貢方物。 庚辰，詔：「西京莊宅司、內侍省、宮苑司、內園等四司，所管諸巡系稅戶二千五百並還府縣。 其廣德、升平二宮並停廢。 應行從諸莊園林、亭殿、房舍、什物課利，宜令逐司依舊收管。」
On gengshen in the third month, the emperor visited the Southern Villa and had his attendants practice archery. On wuchen, Privy Council academician and Left Reminder Wang Pu was appointed drafting secretary in the Secretariat and Hanlin academician; and Inner Palace Service commissioner and En Prefecture militia commissioner Zheng Renhui was made vice commissioner of the Privy Council. An edict named Northern Bureau commissioner of the Palace Secretariat Zhai Guangye acting administrator of Yongxing. On jiaxu, Uyghur envoys arrived with tribute goods. On gengchen, an edict declared: "The twenty-five hundred tax households administered by the Western Capital Estates Office, the Inner Palace Service, the Palace Parks Office, the Inner Gardens, and related agencies shall all be returned to their prefectures and counties. The Guangde and Shengping palaces are to be abolished. Revenues from the estates, gardens, pavilions, halls, houses, and goods used for imperial processions shall continue to be collected by the respective offices as before."
7
夏四月丙戌朔，日有食之，帝避正殿，百官守司。 丁亥，詔停蔡州鄉軍。 戊子，以京師旱，分命群臣禱雨。 癸巳，制削奪慕容彥超在身官爵。 甲午，高麗國冊使、衛尉卿劉皞卒。 乙卯，詔取來月五日，車駕赴兗州城下，慰勞將士。 以樞密副使鄭仁誨為右衛大將軍，依前充職，兼權大內都點檢； 以中書侍郎、平章事、判三司李穀為權東京留守，兼判開封府事。
On the new moon of the fourth month of summer, bingxu, a solar eclipse occurred. The emperor left the main hall, and the officials remained at their duties. On dinghai, an edict disbanded the local militia of Cai Prefecture. On wuzi, because drought had struck the capital, the emperor dispatched various ministers to pray for rain. On guisi, a decree stripped Murong Yanchao of all his ranks and titles. On jiawu, Liu Hao, who had been envoy for the investiture of the king of Goryeo and director of the Court of Imperial Entertainments, died. On yimao, an edict scheduled the fifth of the following month for the emperor to go to Yanzhou to encourage the troops under the city walls. Privy Council vice commissioner Zheng Renhui was appointed general of the Right Guards while retaining his former post, and was also made acting chief inspector of the inner palace; and Vice Director of the Secretariat, grand councilor, and commissioner of the Revenue Bureau Li Gu was named acting regent of the Eastern Capital and commissioner of Kaifeng.
8
五月丙辰朔，帝御崇元殿受朝，仗衛如儀。 庚申，車駕發京師。 戊辰，至兗州城下。 乙亥，收復兗州，斬慕容彥超，夷其族。 詔端明殿學士顏衎權知兗州軍州事。 壬午，曲赦兗州管內罪人，取五月二十七日已前所犯罪，大辟已下，咸赦除之。 慕容彥超徒黨，有逃避潛竄者，及城內將吏等並放罪。 自慕容彥超違背以來，鄉州內有接便為非者，一切不問。 諸軍將士歿於王事者，各與賻贈，都頭已上與贈官。 兗州城內及官軍下寨四面去州五里內，今年所征夏秋稅及沿征錢物並放； 十里內，只放夏稅； 一州管界，今夏苗子三分放一分。 城內百姓遭毀拆舍屋及遭燒焚者，給賜材木。 諸處差到人夫內，有遭矢石死者，各給絹三匹，仍放戶下三年徭役雲。 癸未，詔兗州降為防禦州，仍為望州。
On the new moon of the fifth month, bingchen, the emperor held court in Chongyuan Hall with full ceremonial guard. On gengshen, the emperor left the capital. On wuchen, he reached Yanzhou and camped below the city walls. On yihai, Yanzhou was recovered. Murong Yanchao was executed and his entire clan wiped out. An edict appointed Duanming Hall academician Yan Yan acting administrator of Yanzhou. On renwu, a partial amnesty was granted to criminals in Yanzhou: all offenses committed before the twenty-seventh of the fifth month, down to capital crimes, were pardoned. Followers of Murong Yanchao who had fled into hiding, along with the city's officers and clerks, were all pardoned. Anyone in the prefecture who had taken advantage of the rebellion to commit wrongdoing was likewise not prosecuted. Soldiers who had died in the imperial service were granted funeral gifts, and those of company-head rank and above received posthumous offices. Within Yanzhou city and within five li of the army camps on every side, that year's summer and autumn taxes and all supplementary levies were remitted; within ten li, only the summer tax was remitted; and throughout the entire prefecture, one-third of the summer seedling tax was forgiven. City residents whose homes had been torn down or burned were given lumber to rebuild. Among the laborers conscripted from various districts, families of those killed by missiles were given three bolts of silk each, and their households were exempted from corvée labor for three years. On guiwei, an edict reduced Yanzhou from a military commission to a defense prefecture, though it retained its status as a prestigious prefecture.

After the offering, as he was about to perform a full prostration, his attendants said, "Confucius was only a subject—Your Majesty should not bow fully. " The emperor replied, "The Cultured and Exalted King was teacher to rulers for a hundred generations—how could I fail to show him respect! " He then performed a full bow and offering before the shrine. The wine vessels and silver censers used in the offering were left at the shrine. He then visited the Kong family grove and paid his respects at Confucius's tomb. The emperor asked his close advisers, "Who survives today among the descendants of Confucius and Yan Hui? " They answered, "The former magistrate of Qufu, hereditary Duke of Cultured Exaltation Kong Renyu, is Confucius's forty-third-generation descendant; and Yan She, a local candidate in the Three Rites, is a descendant of Yan Hui. " Both were summoned at once. Renyu was given crimson robes and orally appointed magistrate of Qufu; Yan She was made registrar and ordered to take up office immediately. Yanzhou was also ordered to repair the temple of Confucius, and cutting timber or gathering fuel beside the tomb was forbidden. On bingxu, the emperor returned to the capital. When the emperor had reached Yanzhou on the thirteenth of the fifth month, the rebels were still holding the city. On the seventeenth he dreamed by day that a Daoist presented a scroll whose opening line read, "The imperial carriage will return to the capital on the second of next month." The rest was too long to remember in full. On waking he told his chief ministers the dream, and four days later the city fell. From the emperor's arrival at the army until the rebels were crushed, only nine days passed. He left the city on the second of the sixth month, exactly as the dream had foretold—no imperial campaign in recent memory had achieved victory so quickly. That day brought heavy rain, and the water rose several feet around the temporary palace below the walls. [ (According to the Chronicle of the Nine States, Biography of Wang Kui: Kui was from Wuling in Lang Prefecture and was also known as Jinkui. Bian Hao was military commissioner of the Wu'an army and summoned Liu Yan to court. Yan refused and asked Kui, "The south has summoned me. If I do not go, they will attack—what should I do? " Kui replied, "Hao has come under the pretext of administering Tan and Lang. If you go to court now, you walk straight into his trap. Wuling lies behind the Yangzi's barriers with a hundred thousand armed men—will you simply bow to a foreign ruler? Hao has only just reached Changsha and his control is not secure. Strike while the people are angry, attack him at once, and you can take him in a single assault. " Yan agreed and sent He Jingzhen and others to raise troops at Wuling under the title Ten-Finger Commanders to attack Bian Hao. Kui led the fleet south to Changsha. Bian Hao fled in alarm to Jiangnan with his troops, the garrisons were withdrawn, and all territory beyond the lakes was recovered.)] On guisi, retired grand preceptor of the heir apparent An Shuqian died. On jiawu, an edict forbade feudatories coming to court from presenting tribute to pay for banquets. On dingyou, Prince Rong of Chanzhou ended mourning and was recalled to office as associate grand councilor. On wuxu, retired junior tutor of the heir apparent Wang Yan died. On renyin, the emperor visited the Western Villa. On yisi, Duanming Hall academician Yan Yan was appointed acting commissioner of Kaifeng. The Censorate memorialized: "We ask that the Left and Right Guards be renamed the Left and Right Garrison Guards. " The request was granted, to avoid the emperor's personal name. That winter brought no snow.
16
廣順三年春正月壬子朔，帝御崇元殿受朝賀，仗衛如儀。 幸太平宮起居漢太后。 甲寅，賜群臣射於內鞠場。 乙卯，武平軍兵馬留後劉言奏：「潭州干戈之後，焚燒殆盡，乞移使府於武陵。 」從之。 詔升朗州為大都督府，在潭州之上。 丙辰，以武平軍節度使留後、檢校太尉劉言為檢校太師、同平章事，行朗州大都督，充武平軍節度兼三司水陸轉運等使，制置武安、靜江等軍事，進封彭城郡公； 武平軍節度副使、權知潭州軍州事、檢校太傅王進逵為檢校太尉，行潭州刺史，充武安軍節度使； 以武安軍行軍司馬兼衙內步軍都指揮使、檢校太傅何敬貞為檢校太尉，行桂州刺史，充靜江軍節度使； 以張仿領眉州刺史，充武平軍節度副使； 以朱元琇領黃州刺史，充靜江軍節度副使； 以周行逢領集州刺史，充武安軍節度行軍司馬。 自進逵而下，皆劉言將校也。 邠州奏，慶州略蕃部野雞族略奪商旅，侵擾州界。 詔遣寧州刺史張建武等率兵掩襲，仍先賜敕書安撫，如不從命，即進軍問罪。 辛酉，詔賜朗州劉言應兩京及諸道舊屬湖南樓店邸第。 乙丑，詔：「諸道州府系屬戶部營田及租稅課利等，除京兆府莊宅務、贍國軍榷鹽務、兩京行從莊外，其餘並割屬州縣，所征租稅課利，官中只管舊額，其職員節級一切停廢。 應有客戶元佃系省莊田、桑土、舍宇，便賜逐戶，充為永業，仍仰縣司給與憑由。 應諸處元屬營田戶部院及系縣人戶所納租中課利，起今年後並與除放。 所有見牛犢並賜本戶，官中永不收系」云。 帝在民間，素知營田之弊，至是以天下系官莊田僅萬計，悉以分賜見佃戶充永業。 是歲出戶三萬餘，百姓既得為己業，比戶欣然，於是葺屋植樹，敢致功力。 又，東南郡邑各有租牛課戶，往因梁太祖渡淮，軍士掠民牛以千萬計，梁太祖盡給與諸州民，輸租課，自是六十餘載，時移代改，牛租猶在，百姓苦之，至是特與除放。 未幾，京兆府莊宅務及榷鹽務亦歸州縣，依例處分。 或有上言，以天下系官莊田，甚有可惜者，若遣貨之，當得三十萬緡，亦可資國用。 帝曰：「茍利於民，與資國何異。 」丁卯，戶部侍郎、權知貢舉趙上交奏：「諸科舉人，欲等第各加對義場數； 進士除詩賦外，別試雜文一場。 」從之。 兩浙吊祭使、左諫議大夫李知損責授登州司馬，員外置，仍令所在馳驛放遣。 知損銜命江、浙，所經藩郡，皆強貸於侯伯，為青州知州張凝所奏，故有是命。 己巳，幸南莊。 臨水亭，見雙鳧戲於池上，帝引弓射之，一發疊貫，從臣稱賀。 庚午，以前邠州節度使侯章為鄧州節度使。 前萊州刺史葉仁魯賜死，坐為民所訟故也。 辛未，詔樞密使王峻巡視河堤。 峻請行，故從之。 辛巳，幸南莊。
On the new moon of the first month of spring in the third year of Guangshun, renzi, the emperor held court in Chongyuan Hall with full ceremonial guard. He visited Taiping Palace to pay his respects to the Han empress dowager. On jiayin, the emperor granted the officials an archery session in the inner ball court. On yimao, acting Wuping army commander Liu Yan memorialized: "Tanzhou has been nearly destroyed by war. We ask that the commissionerate be moved to Wuling. " The request was granted. An edict elevated Langzhou to a great military governorship ranking above Tanzhou. On bingchen, acting Wuping commissioner Liu Yan was made honorary grand preceptor and associate grand councilor, acting great military governor of Langzhou, commissioner of Wuping army and water and land transport, controller of Wu'an and Jingjiang military affairs, and enfeoffed as duke of Pengcheng; deputy commissioner Wang Jinkui was made honorary grand guardian, acting prefect of Tanzhou, and military commissioner of the Wu'an army; campaign marshal He Jingzhen was made honorary grand guardian, acting prefect of Guizhou, and military commissioner of the Jingjiang army; Zhang Fang was made prefect of Meizhou and deputy commissioner of Wuping army; Zhu Yuanxiu was made prefect of Huangzhou and deputy commissioner of Jingjiang army; and Zhou Xingfeng was made prefect of Jizhou and campaign marshal of the Wu'an army. From Jinkui downward, all were officers under Liu Yan. Binzhou reported that the Yeji tribe on Qingzhou's frontier was robbing merchants and raiding the border. An edict sent Ningzhou prefect Zhang Jianwu and others to strike by surprise, but first sent a letter of reassurance; if the tribe refused, they were to march and punish them. On xinyou, an edict granted Liu Yan the shops and mansions in both capitals and the circuits that had formerly belonged to Hunan. On yichou, an edict declared: "Revenue farms and tax levies under the Ministry of Revenue in the circuits—except the Jingzhao Estates Office, the state salt monopoly, and the two capitals' processional estates—shall be transferred to prefectures and counties. Collections shall not exceed former quotas, and all related posts are abolished. Tenant farmers on former provincial estates, mulberry lands, and houses shall receive them as permanent property, with certificates issued by the county offices. All levies formerly paid on revenue-farm and county household rents are remitted from this year forward. All oxen and calves in official hands are granted to their households, and the court will never seize them again." Having known the abuses of revenue farms from his years among the people, the emperor now distributed nearly ten thousand court estates throughout the realm to their tenant farmers as permanent property. More than thirty thousand households benefited that year. Once the land was their own, families rejoiced, repaired their homes, planted trees, and labored with new confidence. Southeastern districts also had ox-rent households dating from when Liang Taizu crossed the Huai and his soldiers seized millions of civilian oxen, which he then distributed to the people for rent payments. For more than sixty years, through successive dynasties, the ox rent had remained a burden—now it was finally abolished. Soon the Jingzhao Estates Office and the salt monopoly were also transferred to prefectural control under the same policy. Someone suggested that selling the court estates could bring three hundred thousand strings of cash for state revenue. The emperor replied, "If it benefits the people, how is that different from enriching the state? " On dingmao, vice minister of revenue and acting examination commissioner Zhao Shangjiao memorialized that additional policy-discourse sessions be added for each examination rank; and that jinshi candidates, beyond poetry and rhapsody, take an additional examination in miscellaneous prose. " The proposal was approved. Li Zhisun, condolence envoy to the two Zhes and Left Reminder, was demoted to supernumerary vice-prefect of Dengzhou and ordered sent home by express relay. On his mission to Jiang and Zhe, Zhisun had extorted loans from local lords at every stop. Qingzhou prefect Zhang Ning reported him, leading to this punishment. On jisi, the emperor visited the Southern Villa. At the waterside pavilion he saw two mandarin ducks on the pond, drew his bow, and pierced both with a single arrow. His attendants congratulated him. On gengwu, former Binzhou military commissioner Hou Zhang was appointed military commissioner of Dengzhou. Former Laizhou prefect Ye Renlu was ordered to take his own life after the people brought suit against him. On xinwei, an edict ordered Privy Council Commissioner Wang Jun to inspect the Yellow River dikes. Jun had asked to make the tour, and the emperor agreed. On xinsi, the emperor visited the Southern Villa.
17
閏月甲申，朗州劉言、潭州王進逵奏，廣賊占據桂管，深入永州界俘劫，遣朗州行軍司馬何敬貞與指揮使朱全琇、陳順等，率水陸軍五萬進擊。 丙戊，回鶻遣使貢方物。 詔故梁租庸使趙巖侄崇勛，見居陳州，量賜系官店宅，從王峻之請也。 辛卯，定州奏，契丹攻義豐軍，出勁兵夜斫蕃營，斬首六十級，契丹遁去。 甲午，鎮州奏，契丹寇境，遣兵追襲，至無極而還。 丙申，皇子澶州節度使榮來朝。 壬寅，以樞密使、尚書左僕射、同平章事、監修國史王峻兼青州節度使，餘如故。 延州衙內指揮使高紹基奏言：「父允權患腳膝，令臣權知軍州事。 」癸卯，陳州奏：「吏民請與前刺史李穀立祠堂。 」從之。 時穀為宰相，聞郡人陳請，遜讓數四，乃止。 甲辰，鄴都留守王殷加檢校太尉，依前同平章事。 丙午，鎮州節度使何福進、河陽節度使王彥超並加檢校太尉，潞州節度使李筠加檢校太傅。 丁未，延州節度使高允權卒。 己酉，開封府奏，都城內錄到無名額僧尼寺院五十八所。 詔廢之。
On jiashen in the intercalary month, Liu Yan and Wang Jinkui reported that Guang bandits held Guiguan and were raiding deep into Yongzhou. They had sent campaign marshal He Jingzhen with Zhu Quanxiu, Chen Shun, and others at the head of fifty thousand troops by land and water. On bingxu, Uyghur envoys arrived with tribute goods. An edict granted court shops and mansions in Chenzhou to Chongxun, nephew of the former Liang rent commissioner Zhao Yan, at Wang Jun's request. On xinmao, Dingzhou reported a Khitan attack on Yifeng Garrison. Elite troops struck the enemy camp by night, took sixty heads, and the Khitan withdrew. On jiawu, Zhenzhou reported a Khitan border raid. Pursuit troops chased them as far as Wuji and then returned. On bingshen, Prince Rong of Chanzhou came to court. On renyin, privy council commissioner and grand councilor Wang Jun was also appointed military commissioner of Qingzhou while retaining his other posts. Yanzhou headquarters commander Gao Shaoji reported: "My father Yunquan is ill in the knee and has placed me in charge of military and civil affairs. " On guimao, Chenzhou reported that officials and commoners wished to erect a shrine to the former prefect Li Gu. " The request was granted. Gu was then grand councilor. When he heard of the petition, he declined four times, and the plan was dropped. On jiachen, Yedu regent Wang Yin was made honorary grand guardian while retaining his post as grand councilor. On bingwu, Zhenzhou commissioner He Fujin and Heyang commissioner Wang Yanchao were made honorary grand guardians, and Luzhou commissioner Li Yun honorary grand tutor. On dingwei, Yanzhou military commissioner Gao Yunquan died. On jiyou, Kaifeng reported fifty-eight unregistered Buddhist and Daoist establishments in the capital. An edict ordered them closed.
18
二月辛亥朔，以前西京留守白文珂為太子太師致仕，進封韓國公。 癸丑，安州節度使李洪義、侍衛馬軍都指揮使郭崇、侍衛步軍都指揮使曹英，並加檢校太尉。 唐州方城縣令陳守愚棄市，坐克留戶民蠶鹽一千五百斤入己也。 內制國寶兩座，詔中書令馮道書寶文，其一以「皇帝承天受命之寶」為文，其一以「皇帝神寶」為文。 案，傳國寶始自秦始皇，令李斯篆之，歷代傳授，事具前史。 至唐末帝自燔之際，以寶隨身，遂俱焚焉。 晉高祖受命，特制寶一座，開運末，契丹犯闕，少帝遣其子延煦送於戎王，戎主，訝其非真，少帝上表具訴其事，及契丹北歸，賫以入蕃。 漢朝二帝，未暇別制，至是始創為之。 庚申，遣將作監李瓊知陜州軍州事。 甲子，樞密使、平盧軍節度使、尚書左僕射、平章事、監修國史王峻責授商州司馬，員外置，所在馳驛發遣。 戊辰，左監門上將軍李建崇卒。 延州牙內都指揮使高紹基奏，交割軍府與副使張圖。 己巳，朗州劉言奏，當道先遣行軍司馬何敬貞率兵掩擊廣賊，行及潭州，部眾奔潰，湖南王進逵以敬貞失律，已梟首訖。 以樞密直學士、工部侍郎陳觀為秘書監。 壬申，鳳翔少尹桑能責授鄧州長史。 能，晉相維翰之庶弟也，坐據維翰別第為人所訟故也。 癸酉，以戶部侍郎、知貢舉趙上交為太子詹事。 是歲，新進士中有李觀者，不當策名，物議喧然，中書門下以觀所試詩賦失韻，勾落姓名，故上交移官。 丁丑，幸南莊，賜從官射。 命客省使向訓權知延州軍州事。
On the new moon of the second month, xinhai, former Western Capital regent Bai Wenke retired as grand preceptor of the heir apparent and was enfeoffed as duke of Han. On guichou, Anzhou commissioner Li Hongyi, cavalry commander Guo Chong, and infantry commander Cao Ying were all made honorary grand guardians. Chen Shouyu, magistrate of Fangcheng in Tangzhou, was executed for embezzling fifteen hundred jin of household silkworm salt. Two imperial seals were commissioned, and Chief Councilor Feng Dao was ordered to inscribe them—one reading "Seal of the Emperor Who Receives Heaven's Mandate," the other "Imperial Divine Seal." The transmission seal dated from the First Emperor of Qin, who had Li Si engrave it. It passed through successive dynasties, as earlier histories record. When the last Tang emperor burned himself, he took the seal with him, and it perished in the flames. Jin Gaozu had a new seal made when he took the throne. At the end of Kaiyun, when the Khitan seized the capital, the young emperor sent his son Yanshu to present it to the Khitan ruler, who doubted its authenticity. The young emperor explained in a memorial, but when the Khitan withdrew north, they carried the seal away. The two Later Han emperors had had no time to commission new ones; only now were they created. On gengshen, director of palace construction Li Qiong was sent to administer Shanzhou. On jiazi, privy council commissioner and grand councilor Wang Jun was demoted to supernumerary vice-prefect of Shangzhou and ordered sent away by express relay. On wuchen, Left Gate general Li Jianchong died. Yanzhou headquarters commander Gao Shaoji reported transferring command of the prefecture to vice commissioner Zhang Tu. On jisi, Liu Yan reported that campaign marshal He Jingzhen had been sent to attack Guang bandits, but his troops broke and fled at Tanzhou. Wang Jinkui of Hunan had executed him for military failure. Privy council academician Chen Guan was appointed director of the Palace Library. On renshen, Fengxiang junior administrator Sang Neng was demoted to senior recorder of Dengzhou. Neng was the younger half-brother of Jin chancellor Sang Weihan and was punished for illegally occupying Weihan's private residence. On guiyou, vice minister of revenue and examination commissioner Zhao Shangjiao was appointed steward of the heir apparent. That year a new jinshi named Li Guan provoked public outcry because he should not have passed. The Secretariat struck his name from the rolls for faulty rhyme in his examination poetry, and Shangjiao was transferred as a result. On dingchou, the emperor visited the Southern Villa and granted his attendants an archery session. Guest Service commissioner Xiang Xun was appointed acting administrator of Yanzhou.
