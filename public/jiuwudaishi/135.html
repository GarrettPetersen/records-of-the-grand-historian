1
劉守光，深州樂壽人也。 其父仁恭，初隨父晟客於范陽，晟以軍吏補新興鎮將，事節度使李可舉。 仁恭幼多智機，數陳力於軍中。 李全忠之攻易、定也，別將于晏圍易州，累月不能拔，仁恭穴地道以陷之，軍中號曰「劉窟頭」，稍遷裨校。 仁恭誌大氣豪，自言嘗夢大佛幡出於指端，或云年四十九當領旄節。 此言頗泄，燕帥李匡威惡之，不欲令典軍，改為府掾，出為景城令。 屬瀛州軍亂，殺郡守，仁恭募白丁千人討平之，匡威壯其才，復使為帳中爪牙，令將兵戍蔚州。 兵士以過期不代，思歸流怨，會李匡儔奪兄位，戍軍擁仁恭為帥，欲攻幽州，比至居庸關，為府兵所敗，仁恭挈族奔於太原。 武皇遇之甚厚，賜田宅以處之，出為壽陽鎮將，從征吐渾。 仁恭數進畫於蓋寓，言幽州可圖之狀，願得步騎萬人，即指期可取，武皇從之。 洎仁恭舉兵，屢不克捷。
Liu Shouguang came from Leshou in Shen Prefecture. His father Liu Rengong had first accompanied his own father Liu Sheng as a client at Fanyang. Sheng rose from army clerk to command the Xinxing garrison and served under Military Governor Li Keju. From childhood Rengong was sharp and inventive, and he repeatedly proved his worth in camp. During Li Quanzhong's attack on Yi and Ding, Deputy Commander Yu Yan had besieged Yi Prefecture for months without success. Rengong undermined the walls by tunnel and earned the camp nickname "Liu the Burrower," after which he was steadily promoted to adjutant. Rengong was proud and bold. He claimed to have dreamed of a great Buddhist banner streaming from his fingertips, and rumor held that at forty-nine he would receive the command baton. Word of this spread. Yan commander Li Kuangwei disliked him and kept him from field command, reassigning him to staff duty and then posting him out as magistrate of Jingcheng. When troops in Ying Prefecture mutinied and killed the prefect, Rengong raised a thousand local militiamen and put the disturbance down. Kuangwei admired his ability, restored him as a personal guard, and sent him to garrison Weizhou. The men, long overdue for rotation and resentful, rose up just as Li Kuangchou displaced his brother. The garrison troops proclaimed Rengong commander and marched on Youzhou, but at Juyong Pass prefectural forces defeated them, and Rengong fled with his family to Taiyuan. The Prince of Jin treated him with exceptional favor, gave him lands and a house, and later made him commander of the Shouyang garrison and took him on campaign against the Tuyuhun. Rengong repeatedly urged plans on Gai Yu, describing Youzhou as ripe for conquest and asking for ten thousand infantry and cavalry with a fixed date for its capture. The Prince of Jin consented. Yet once Rengong took the field he could win no victory.
2
唐乾寧元年十一月，武皇親征匡儔。 十二月，破燕軍於威塞，進拔媯州，收居庸。 二十六日，匡儔棄城而遁，武皇令李存審與仁恭入城撫勞，封府庫，即以仁恭為幽州節度使，留腹心燕留德等十餘人分典軍政，武皇乃還。 二年七月，武皇討王行瑜，師於渭北，上章請授仁恭節鉞。 九月，天子以仁恭為檢校司空、幽州廬龍軍節度使。 三年，羅宏信背盟，武皇遣李存信攻魏州，征兵於燕，仁恭托以契丹入寇，俟敵退聽命。 四年七月，武皇聞兗、鄆俱陷，復征兵於仁恭，數月之間，使車結轍，仁恭辭旨不遜。 武皇以書讓之，仁恭覽書嫚罵，拘其使人，晉之戍兵在燕者皆拘之，復以厚利誘晉之驍將，由是亡命者眾矣。 八月，武皇討仁恭。 九月五日，次安塞軍。 九日，渡木瓜澗，大為燕軍所敗，死傷大半。 既而仁恭告捷於梁祖，梁祖聞之喜，因表仁恭加平章事。 仁恭又遣使於武皇，自陳邊將擅興之罪，武皇以書報之。 仁恭既絕於晉，恒懼討罰，募兵練眾，常無虛月。
In the eleventh month of the first year of Qianning (894), the Prince of Jin led a personal campaign against Kuangchou. In the twelfth month he routed the Yan army at Weisai, advanced to Ji Prefecture, and recovered Juyong. On the twenty-sixth Kuangchou abandoned the city and fled. The Prince of Jin sent Li Cunshen and Rengong to enter, comfort the people, and seal the treasury, then appointed Rengong military governor of Youzhou. He left more than ten trusted men including Yan Liude to divide civil and military affairs and returned home. In the seventh month of the second year the Prince of Jin attacked Wang Xingyu, halted north of the Wei River, and memorialized the court to grant Rengong the command baton. In the ninth month the emperor appointed Rengong acting Minister of Works and military governor of the Lulong army at Youzhou. In the third year Luo Hongxin broke the alliance. The Prince of Jin sent Li Cunxin against Weizhou and called on Yan for troops, but Rengong pleaded Khitan invasion and asked to march only after the enemy withdrew. In the seventh month of the fourth year, learning that Yan and Yun had both fallen, the Prince of Jin again demanded troops from Rengong. Within months messengers came in an unbroken stream, and Rengong's replies turned insolent. The Prince of Jin rebuked him by letter. Rengong read it and answered with abuse, detained the envoys, imprisoned every Jin soldier in Yan, and tempted Jin's boldest commanders with rich rewards until deserters swelled in number. In the eighth month the Prince of Jin marched against Rengong. On the fifth day of the ninth month he halted at Anse Army. On the ninth he crossed Mugua Stream and was badly beaten by the Yan army; more than half his force was killed or wounded. Rengong then reported victory to the Liang founder, who rejoiced and memorialized to make Rengong Grand Councilor. Rengong also sent envoys to the Prince of Jin, confessing that a frontier commander had acted on his own. The Prince of Jin replied by letter. Cut off from Jin, Rengong lived in constant fear of attack, recruiting and drilling troops without a month's pause.
3
光化元年三月，令其長子襲淪州，盧彥威委城而遁，遂兼有滄、景、德三郡，以守文為留後，請節鉞於朝。 昭宗怒其擅興，不時與之。 會中使至范陽，仁恭私之曰：「旄節吾自有，但要長安本色耳，何以累章見阻？ 為吾言之。 」其悖戾如此。 仁恭兵鋒益盛，每戰多捷，以為天贊，遂有吞噬河朔之志。 二年正月，仁恭率幽、滄步騎十萬，號三十萬，將兼並魏博、鎮定。 師次貝州，一鼓而拔，無少長皆屠之，清水為之不流。 羅紹威求援於汴，汴將李思安、葛從周赴之，思安屯內黃。 仁恭兵圍魏州，聞汴軍在內黃，戒其子守文曰：「李思安怯懦，汝之智勇，比之十倍，當先殄此鼠輩，次擄紹威。 」守文與單可及率漁陽精甲五萬，夾清水而上。 思安設伏於內黃清水之左，袁象先設伏於內黃清水之右。 思安逆戰於繁陽城，偽不勝，徐退，燕人追躡，至於內黃，思安步兵成列，回擊之。 燕人將引退，左右伏兵發，燕軍大敗，臨陣斬單可及，守文單騎僅免，五萬之眾無生還者。 時葛從周率邢、洺之眾入魏州，與賀德倫、李暉出擊賊營。 是夜，仁恭燒營遁走，汴人長驅追擊，自魏至長河數百里，僵屍蔽地，敗旗折戟，累累於路。 鎮人又邀擊於東境，燕軍復敗。 仁恭自是垂翅不振者累年。 汴人乘勝攻滄州，仁恭率師援之，營於乾寧軍。 汴將氏叔琮逆戰，燕軍逗撓，退保瓦橋，乃卑辭厚禮乞師於晉，武皇遣兵逼邢、洺以應之。 十月，汴人陷瀛、鄚二州，晉將周德威將兵出飛狐，仁恭復修好於晉。
In the third month of the first year of Guanghua (898) he ordered his eldest son to attack Cangzhou. Lu Yanwei abandoned the city and fled, and he then held Cang, Jing, and De, appointing Shouwen acting commander and requesting the command baton from court. Emperor Zhaozong was angered by the unauthorized campaign and did not grant it promptly. When a palace envoy reached Fanyang, Rengong told him privately, "I already hold the command baton myself. I only want the proper Chang'an style. Why should repeated memorials stand in my way? Make that plain for me. " His arrogance went that far. Rengong's armies grew stronger and won battle after battle. Taking this as Heaven's favor, he formed the ambition to swallow Hebei. In the first month of the second year Rengong led a hundred thousand foot and horse from You and Cang, claiming three hundred thousand, intending to annex Weibo and Zhen and Ding. The army halted at Bei Prefecture and took it at the first assault. None, young or old, was spared, and the clear water ceased to flow. Luo Shaowei sought aid from Bian. Bian generals Li Si'an and Ge Congzhou hurried to him, and Si'an encamped at Neihuang. Rengong besieged Weizhou. Hearing that Bian troops were at Neihuang, he warned his son Shouwen, "Li Si'an is a coward. Your wit and courage are ten times his. Destroy these rats first, then seize Shaowei. " Shouwen and Shan Keji led fifty thousand elite troops from Yuyang up the clear stream. Si'an set an ambush on the left bank of the clear stream at Neihuang, and Yuan Xiangxian set one on the right. Si'an met them at Fanyang city, feigned defeat, and slowly withdrew. The Yan troops pursued to Neihuang, where Si'an's infantry formed ranks and struck back. As the Yan troops were about to withdraw, ambushers on both flanks sprang up. The Yan army was routed. Shan Keji was beheaded on the field, Shouwen escaped alone on horseback, and of the fifty thousand none returned alive. At that time Ge Congzhou led troops from Xing and Ming into Weizhou and, with He Delun and Li Hui, sallied out against the enemy camp. That night Rengong burned his camp and fled. Bian troops pursued at full speed from Wei to Changhe for several hundred li, corpses covering the ground and broken flags and snapped halberds littering the road. Zhen troops intercepted them again on the eastern border, and the Yan army was beaten once more. From then on Rengong's wings were clipped and he did not recover for years. The Bian army pressed its advantage against Cangzhou. Rengong led troops to relieve it and encamped at Qianning Army. Bian commander Shi Shuzong met him in battle. The Yan army wavered, fell back to defend Wa Bridge, then sent humble words and rich gifts to beg troops from Jin. The Prince of Jin sent forces to press Xing and Ming in response. In the tenth month the Bian army took Ying and Mo. Jin commander Zhou Dewei led troops out through Flying Fox, and Rengong renewed friendly ties with Jin.
4
天祐三年七月，梁祖自將兵攻滄州，營於長蘆。 仁恭師徒屢喪，乃酷法盡發部內男子十五已上、七十已下，各自備兵糧以從軍，閭裏為之一空。 部內男子無貴踐，並黥其面，文曰「定霸都」，士人黥其臂，文曰「一心事主」。 由是燕、薊人民例多黥涅，或伏竄而免。 仁恭閱眾，得二十萬，進至瓦橋，汴人深溝高壘以攻滄州，內外阻絕，仁恭不能合戰，城中大饑，人相篡啖，析骸而爨，丸土而食，轉死骨立者十之六七。 自七月至十月，仁恭遣使求援於晉，前後百餘輩，武皇乃征兵於燕，仁恭遣都將李溥夏侯景、監軍張居翰、書記馬郁等，以兵三萬來會。 十二月，合晉師以攻潞州，降丁會，乃解滄州之圍。 是時，天子播遷，中原多故，仁恭嘯傲薊門，誌意盈滿，師道士王若訥，祈長生羽化之道。 幽州西有名山曰大安山，仁恭乃於其上盛飾館宇，僭擬宮掖，聚室女艷婦，窮極侈麗。 又招聚緇黃，合仙丹，講求法要。 又以瑾泥作錢，令部內行使，盡斂銅錢於大安山巔，鑿穴以藏之，藏畢即殺匠石以滅其口。 又禁江表茶商，自擷山中草葉為茶，以邀厚利。 改山名為大恩山。 仁恭有嬖妾曰羅氏，美姿色，其子守光烝之，事泄，仁恭怒，笞守光，謫而不齒。
In the seventh month of the third year of Tianyou (906), the Liang founder personally led troops against Cangzhou and encamped at Changlu. Rengong had lost battle after battle and then enforced harsh law, mustering every man in the circuit aged fifteen or above and seventy or below, each to supply his own arms and grain, until the districts were emptied. Every man in the circuit, noble or base alike, was tattooed on the face with the words "Capital of Conquest," and scholars on the arm with "Wholehearted in the lord's service." Thus the people of Yan and Ji were commonly tattooed, while some hid themselves to escape. Rengong reviewed his forces and mustered two hundred thousand, advancing to Wa Bridge. The Bian army dug deep trenches and built high ramparts against Cangzhou, severing inside from outside so Rengong could not join battle. Inside the city famine was extreme: men devoured one another, split bones for fuel, and kneaded clay for food; seven or eight in ten starved to death. From the seventh to the tenth month Rengong sent more than a hundred envoys begging aid from Jin. The Prince of Jin then called for troops from Yan, and Rengong sent commanders Li Pu and Xia Houjing, supervisor Zhang Juhun, secretary Ma Yu, and others with thirty thousand men to join him. In the twelfth month they joined Jin forces to attack Luzhou, induced Ding Hui to surrender, and thus lifted the siege of Cangzhou. At that time the emperor was driven from the capital and the central plains were in turmoil. Rengong swaggered at Jimen, his ambition full, followed the Daoist Wang Ruona, and sought the way of long life and ascension. West of Youzhou stood the famous Mount Da'an. Rengong lavishly adorned pavilions on its summit in imitation of the palace, gathering palace women and courtesans in the utmost luxury. He also gathered monks and priests to compound elixirs and study ritual essentials. He also made coins of clay for use within his circuit, collected all copper cash to the summit of Mount Da'an, carved caves to store it, and when storage was finished killed the craftsmen to seal their mouths. He also barred southern tea merchants and picked mountain leaves himself for tea to reap heavy profit. He changed the mountain's name to Mount Da'en. Rengong had a favorite concubine named Luo, of beautiful appearance. His son Shouguang violated her, and when the affair was exposed Rengong in anger flogged Shouguang and cast him out.
5
四年四月，汴將李思安以急兵攻幽州，營於石子河，仁恭在大安山，城中無備，守光自外帥兵來援，登城拒守。 汴軍既退，守光乃自為幽州節度，令其部將李小喜、元行欽將兵攻大安山。 仁恭遣兵拒戰，為小喜所敗，乃擄仁恭歸幽州，囚於別室。 仁恭左右，迨至婢媵，與守光不協者畢誅之。 其兄守文在滄州，聞父被囚，聚兵大哭，諭之曰：「哀哀父母，生我劬勞。 自古豈有讎父者，吾家生此梟獍，吾生不如死！ 」即率滄、德之師討之。 守光逆戰於雞蘇，為守文所敗。 既而守文詐悲，單馬立於陣場，泣諭於眾曰：「勿殺吾弟！ 」時守光驍將元行欽識之，被擒，滄兵失帥自潰。 守光乃縶兄於別室，圍以叢棘，乘勝進攻滄州。 滄州賓佐孫鶴、呂兗已推守文子延祚為帥，守光攜守文於城下，攻圍累月。 城中乏食，米斗直三萬，人首一級亦直十千，軍士食人，百姓食墐土，驢馬相遇，食其鬃尾，士人出入，多為強者屠殺。 久之，延祚力窮，以城降於守光，守文尋亦遇害。
In the fourth month of the fourth year Bian general Li Si'an attacked Youzhou at urgent speed and encamped at Shizi River. Rengong was on Mount Da'an and the city was unprepared. Shouguang led troops from outside to relieve it and mounted the walls to defend. Once the Bian army withdrew, Shouguang made himself military governor of Youzhou and ordered commanders Li Xiaoxi and Yuan Xingqin to attack Mount Da'an. Rengong sent troops to resist but was defeated by Xiaoxi, seized, and brought back to Youzhou, where he was imprisoned in a separate room. Everyone close to Rengong, down to maids and concubines, who did not get on with Shouguang was put to death. His elder brother Shouwen was at Cangzhou. Hearing that his father was imprisoned, he gathered troops and wept aloud, telling them, "Alas, alas, our parents — to bear and raise us is labor indeed. " Since antiquity has any man avenged a father on his son? Our house has bred this monster; I would rather die than live! " At once he led the armies of Cang and De to attack. Shouguang met him at Jisu and was defeated by Shouwen. Shouwen then feigned grief, stood alone on horseback in the battle line, and wept to the host, "Do not kill my younger brother! " At that time Shouguang's bold commander Yuan Xingqin recognized him and seized him. The Cang troops lost their leader and scattered. Shouguang then bound his elder brother in a separate room, surrounded him with thorn bushes, and pressed the attack on Cangzhou. Cangzhou aides Sun He and Lü Yan had already made Shouwen's son Yanzuo commander. Shouguang brought Shouwen beneath the walls and besieged them for months. Inside the city food was exhausted. Rice cost thirty thousand per dou, a human head ten thousand. Soldiers ate men, commoners ate wall plaster. When donkeys and horses met they ate each other's manes and tails. Scholars going out were often slaughtered by the strong. After a long time Yanzuo, his strength spent, surrendered the city to Shouguang, and Shouwen was soon killed as well.
6
守光性本庸昧，以父兄失勢，謂天所助，淫虐滋甚。 每刑人必以鐵籠盛之，薪火四逼，又為鐵刷劀剔人面。 嘗衣赭黃袍，顧謂將吏曰：「當今海內四分五裂，吾欲南面以朝天下，諸君以為何如？ 」賓佐有孫鶴者，骨鯁方略之士也，率先對曰：「王西有並、汾之患，北有契丹之虞，乘時觀釁，專待薄人，彼若結黨連衡，侵我疆場，地形雖險，勢不可支，甲兵雖多，守恐不暇，縱能卻敵，未免生憂。 王但撫士愛民，補兵完賦，義聲馳於天下，諸侯自然推戴。 今若恃兵與險，未見良圖。 」守光不悅，及梁軍據深、冀，王镕乞師於守光，孫鶴勸守光出援軍以圖霸業，守光不從。 及莊宗有柏鄉之捷，守光謀攻易、定，諷動鎮人，欲為河朔元帥。 莊宗乃與鎮州節度使王镕、易定節度使王處直、昭義節度使李嗣昭、振武節度使周德威、天德軍節度使宋瑤，同遣使奉冊，推守光為尚父，以稔其惡。 守光不悟，謂藩鎮畏己，仍以諸鎮狀送梁祖，言：「臣被晉王等推臣為尚父，堅辭不獲，又難推違。 臣竊料所宜，不如陛下與臣河北道都統，則並、鎮之叛，不足平殄矣。 」梁祖知其詐，優答之。 仍命閣門使王瞳、供奉官史彥璋等使於燕，冊守光為河北道采訪使。
Shouguang was by nature dull and foolish. Because his father and brother had lost power he thought Heaven was helping him, and his cruelty grew worse. Whenever he punished a man he placed him in an iron cage with fire on all sides and also used an iron brush to scrape the face. Once he wore an ochre-yellow robe and asked his commanders and clerks, "Now the realm is split four and five ways. I wish to face south and rule all under Heaven — what do you think? " Among his aides was Sun He, a man of firm principle and strategy, who answered first, "Your Highness has trouble from Bing and Fen in the west and Khitan in the north. If you seize the moment only to watch for openings and wait on the weak, should they league together and invade your borders, though the terrain is rugged the position cannot be held, and though arms are many you will scarcely have leisure to defend. Even if you repel the enemy, you will not escape fresh trouble. Your Highness need only comfort the troops and love the people, repair the army and complete the tax rolls, let righteous fame spread through the realm, and the feudatories will naturally acclaim you. Now if you rely on arms and rugged terrain alone, I see no good plan. " Shouguang was displeased. When the Liang army held Shen and Ji, Wang Yin asked Shouguang for troops, and Sun He urged him to send relief to advance his hegemony, but Shouguang would not listen. When Emperor Zhuangzong won at Baxiang, Shouguang plotted to attack Yi and Ding, stirring the Zhen men and wishing to become commander of Hebei. Emperor Zhuangzong then joined with Zhenzhou Military Governor Wang Yin, Yiding Military Governor Wang Chuzhi, Zhaoyi Military Governor Li Sizhao, Zhenwu Military Governor Zhou Dewei, and Tiande Military Governor Song Yao in sending envoys with a memorial to acclaim Shouguang as Imperial Father, thereby ripening his wickedness. Shouguang did not understand. He thought the feudatories feared him and still sent the memorials of all circuits to the Liang founder, saying, "Your subjects were pushed by the Prince of Jin and others to make me Imperial Father. I firmly refused but could not escape, and it was hard to turn them away. Your subject reckons what is fitting: better that Your Majesty and your subject become overall commanders of the Hebei circuit, then the rebellions of Bing and Zhen would be easy to crush. " The Liang founder knew it was deceit and answered graciously. He still ordered Palace Gate Commissioner Wang Tong and Palace Attendant Shi Yanzhang and others to go to Yan and invest Shouguang as Hebei investigating commissioner.
7
六月，梁使至，守光令所司定尚父采訪使儀註，所司取唐朝冊太尉禮以示之。 守光曰：「此儀註中何無郊天改元之事？ 」梁使曰：「尚父雖尊，猶是人臣。 」守光怒，投於地，謂將吏曰：「方今天下鼎沸，英雄角逐。 朱公創號於夷門，楊渭假名於淮海，王建自尊於巴蜀，茂貞矯制於岐陽，皆因茅土之封，自假帝王之制，然兵虛力寡，疆場多虞。 我大燕地方二千里，帶甲三十萬，東有魚鹽之饒，北有塞馬之利，我南面稱帝，誰如我何！ 今為尚父，孰當帝者！ 公等促具帝者之儀，予且為河朔天子。 」燕之將吏竊議，以為不可。 守光置斧锧於庭，令將佐曰：「今三方協贊，予難重違，擇日而帝矣。 從我者賞，橫議者誅。 孫鶴對曰：「滄州破敗，仆乃罪人，大王寬容，乃至今日，不敢阿旨，以誤家國，茍聽臣言，死且無侮。 」守光大怒，推之伏锧，令軍士割其肉生啖之。 鶴大呼曰：「百日之外，必有急兵矣！ 」守光命窒其口，寸斬之，有識為之嗟惋。 乃悉召部內官吏，教習朝儀，邊人既非素習，舉措失容，相顧誚笑。 八月十三日，守光僭號大燕皇帝，改年曰應天。 以梁使王瞳、判官齊涉為宰相，史彥璋為御史大夫。 偽冊之日，契丹陷平州。 莊宗聞之大笑，監軍張承業曰：「惡不積不足以滅身，老氏所謂『將欲取之，必先與之』。 今守光狂蹶，請遣使省問，以觀其釁。 」十月，莊宗令太原少尹李承勛往使。 承勛至，守光怒不稱臣，械之於獄。
In the sixth month the Liang envoys arrived. Shouguang ordered his office to fix the ceremonial for Imperial Father and investigating commissioner. The office took the Tang rite for investing the Grand Marshal and showed it to him. Shouguang said, "Why in this ceremonial is there no offering to Heaven and change of reign title? " The Liang envoy said, "Though the Imperial Father is honored, he is still a subject. " Shouguang in anger threw it to the ground and told his commanders and clerks, "Now the realm boils and heroes contend. The Duke Zhu founded his title at Yimen, Yang Wei borrowed a name at Huaihai, Wang Jian exalted himself in Bashu, and Maozhen forged edicts at Qiyang — all took fief through enfeoffment and falsely assumed imperial forms, yet their forces were weak and their borders troubled. My Great Yan has two thousand li of territory and three hundred thousand armored men. In the east are the riches of fish and salt, in the north the profit of frontier horses. If I face south and style myself emperor, who can match me! Now I am made Imperial Father — who is fit to be emperor! You will quickly prepare the rites of an emperor; I shall become Son of Heaven of Hebei. " The Yan commanders and clerks whispered that it could not be done. Shouguang set axes and execution blocks in the courtyard and told his commanders and aides, "Now the three sides have joined in praise. I can hardly refuse again — the day of enthronement is chosen. Who follows me will be rewarded; who argues across me will be killed. Sun He answered, "When Cangzhou fell I was already a guilty servant. Your Highness in your tolerance brought me to this day. I dare not flatter to ruin house and state. If you heed my words, I shall die without regret. " Shouguang in great anger pushed him down before the block and ordered the soldiers to cut his flesh and eat it alive. He cried out, "Within a hundred days urgent troops will surely come!" Shouguang ordered his mouth stopped and had him cut inch by inch. Men of judgment sighed in pity. He then summoned all officials in his circuit to teach them court ritual. The frontier people were unaccustomed to it, their movements were ungainly, and they mocked one another. On the thirteenth day of the eighth month Shouguang styled himself Emperor of Great Yan and changed the reign title to Yingtian. He made the Liang envoys Wang Tong and judge Qi She grand councilors and Shi Yanzhang censor-in-chief. On the day of his false investiture the Khitan took Ping Prefecture. When Emperor Zhuangzong heard it he laughed greatly. Supervisor Zhang Chengye said, "Evil that does not accumulate is not enough to destroy the body — what Laozi called, if you wish to take something, you must first give it." "Now Shouguang is wildly arrogant. Please send envoys to inquire and observe his openings." In the tenth month Emperor Zhuangzong ordered Taiyuan Vice Director Li Chengxun to go as envoy. When Chengxun arrived, Shouguang in anger refused to address him as subject and put him in fetters in prison.
8
十二月，莊宗遣周德威出飛狐，會鎮、定之師以討之，德威攻圍歷年，屬郡皆下。 守光堅保幽州，求援於梁，北誘契丹，救終不至。 十年十月，守光遣使持幣馬見德威乞降，又乘城呼曰：「予俟晉王至即出城。 」十一月，莊宗親征。 二十三日，至幽州，單騎臨城，召守光曰：「丈夫成敗，須決所向，公將何如？ 」守光曰：「某俎上肉耳！ 」莊宗湣之，折弓為盟，許其保全。 守光辭以他日，莊宗乃令諸軍攻之。 二十四日，四面畢攻，莊宗登燕太子墓觀之。 俄而數騎執仁恭並其孥來獻，檀州遊奕將李彥暉於燕樂縣獲守光，並妻李氏、祝氏，男繼珣、繼方、繼祚等來獻。 初，守光城破後，攜其妻子將走關內依劉守奇，沿路寒瘡足踵，經日不食。 至燕樂縣，匿於坑谷，令妻祝氏乞食於田父張師造家，怪婦人異狀，詰之，遂俱擒焉。 莊宗方宴府第，引仁恭、守光至席，父子號泣謝罪，莊宗慰撫之曰：「往事不復言。 人誰無過，改之為貴。 」乃歸之傳舍。 是月己卯，晉人執守光及仁恭，露布表其罪，驅以班師。
In the twelfth month Emperor Zhuangzong sent Zhou Dewei out through Flying Fox to join the armies of Zhen and Ding in attacking him. Dewei besieged for more than a year and the subordinate prefectures all fell. Shouguang held Youzhou firmly, begged aid from Liang, and tempted the Khitan in the north, but rescue never came. In the tenth month of the tenth year Shouguang sent envoys with gifts and horses to see Dewei and beg surrender, and also called from the walls, "I shall leave the city as soon as the Prince of Jin arrives." In the eleventh month Emperor Zhuangzong campaigned in person. On the twenty-third he reached Youzhou, rode alone to the walls, and summoned Shouguang. "A man's success or failure must be decided where he is headed — what will you do?" Shouguang said, "I am but meat on the chopping block!" Emperor Zhuangzong pitied him, broke a bow to seal an alliance, and promised to spare him. Shouguang pleaded for another day. Emperor Zhuangzong then ordered the armies to attack. On the twenty-fourth attack came from all four sides. Emperor Zhuangzong mounted the tomb of the Yan crown prince to watch. Soon several horsemen brought Rengong and his family as captives. Tanzhou patrol commander Li Yanhui at Yanle County seized Shouguang with his wife Lady Li and Lady Zhu and sons Jixun, Jifang, Jizuo, and others as captives. Earlier, after the city fell Shouguang took his wife and children intending to flee into the passes to rely on Liu Shouqi. On the road cold sores afflicted his heels and he went a day without food. Reaching Yanle County he hid in a ravine and sent his wife Lady Zhu to beg food from the farmer Zhang Shizao. Shizao thought the woman's appearance strange, questioned her, and seized them all. Emperor Zhuangzong was feasting at the prefectural residence and led Rengong and Shouguang to the seats. Father and son wept and begged pardon. Emperor Zhuangzong comforted them, "The past need not be spoken of again. Who is without fault? To reform is what is noble." " He then returned them to the guest lodge. That month on the day jimao the Jin men seized Shouguang and Rengong, posted a victory bulletin listing their crimes, and drove them back with the army.
9
十一年正月，至晉陽，仁恭父子荷校於露布之下，父母唾面罵守光曰：「逆賊，破家如是！ 」守光俯首不顧。 自范陽至晉陽，涉千餘里，所在聚觀，呼守光為「劉黑子」，略無愧色。 莊守以仁恭、守光徇於都城，即告南宮七廟，禮畢，守光與李小喜、鄭藏斐、劉延卿及其二妻皆伏誅。 李小喜者，本晉之小校，先奔於燕，守光以為愛將。 守光雖兇淫出於天性，然而稔惡侈毒，抑亦小喜贊成。 守光將敗，前一日來降。 守光將死，大呼曰：「臣之誤計，小喜熒惑故也，若罪人不死，臣必訴於地下。 」莊宗急召小喜至，令證辯。 小喜瞋目叱守光曰：「囚父殺兄，烝淫骨肉，亦我教耶！ 」莊宗怒小喜失禮，先斬之。 守光慟哭曰：「王將定天下，臣精於騎，何不且留指使。 」二妻讓之曰：「皇帝，事勢及此，生不如死！ 」即延頸就戮。 守光猶哀訴不已。 既誅，命判官司馬揆備彗櫝祭叕，瘞於城西三里龍山下。 令副使盧汝弼、李存霸拘送仁恭至代州，於武皇靈前刺心血以祭，誅於雁門山下。 自仁恭乾寧二年春入幽州，至天祐十年，父子相承，十九年而滅。
In the first month of the eleventh year they reached Jinyang. Rengong and his son wore the cangue beneath the victory bulletin. Father and mother spat in Shouguang's face and cursed him, "Rebel traitor — you have destroyed the family like this!" Shouguang bowed his head and paid no heed. From Fanyang to Jinyang, more than a thousand li, wherever they passed crowds gathered to watch and called Shouguang "Liu the Black One." He showed scarcely any shame. Emperor Zhuangzong paraded Rengong and Shouguang through the capital, then announced it at the seven temples of the Southern Palace. When the rites were finished Shouguang, Li Xiaoxi, Zheng Zangfei, Liu Yanqing, and his two wives were all executed. Li Xiaoxi had originally been a petty officer of Jin who first fled to Yan. Shouguang made him a favorite commander. Though Shouguang's cruelty and lust sprang from his nature, the ripening of wickedness and extravagance was also aided by Xiaoxi. The day before Shouguang was to be defeated Xiaoxi came over in surrender. As Shouguang was about to die he cried out, "My mistaken plans were because Xiaoxi deluded me. If the guilty are not put to death I shall plead against him in the underworld." Emperor Zhuangzong quickly summoned Xiaoxi and ordered him to testify. Xiaoxi glared and shouted at Shouguang, "Imprisoning your father and killing your brother, violating kin with incest — did I teach you that?" Emperor Zhuangzong was angered at Xiaoxi's lack of decorum and beheaded him first. Shouguang wailed, "When Your Highness is about to settle the realm, I am skilled in horsemanship — why not keep me to command for a time?" His two wives reproached him, "Emperor, matters have come to this — life is worse than death!" They at once stretched their necks to receive the blade. Shouguang still pleaded piteously without end. After the executions he ordered judge Sima Kui to prepare brooms and coffin boards for burial and inter them three li west of the city at Long Mountain. He ordered Vice Commissioner Lu Rubi and Li Cunba to escort Rengong to Dai Prefecture, pierce his heart's blood before the Prince of Jin's spirit tablet as offering, and execute him beneath Yanmen Mountain. From the spring of the second year of Qianning when Rengong entered Youzhou to the tenth year of Tianyou, father and son succeeded one another for nineteen years and then perished.
10
劉陟，即劉龑，初名陟。 其先彭城人，祖仁安，仕唐為潮州長史，因家嶺表。 父謙，素有才識。 唐咸通中，宰相韋宙出鎮南海，謙時為牙校，職級甚卑，然氣貌殊常，宙以猶女妻之。 妻以非其類，堅止之，宙曰：「此人非常流也，他日我子孫或可依之。 」謙後果以軍功拜封州刺史兼賀水鎮使，甚有稱譽。 謙之長子曰隱，即韋氏女所生也，幼而奇特。 及謙卒，賀水諸將有無賴者，幸變作亂，隱定計誅之。 連帥劉崇龜聞其才，署為右都校，復領賀水鎮，俄奏兼封州刺史，用法清肅，威望頗振。 唐昭宗以嗣薛王知柔石門扈蹕功，授清海軍節度使。 詔下，有府之牙將盧琚、譚玘謀不稟朝命，隱舉部兵誅琚、玘以聞，知柔至，深德之，辟為行軍司馬，委以兵賦。 唐昭宗命宰相徐彥若代知柔復署前職。 彥若在鎮二年，臨薨，手表奏隱為兩使留後，昭宗未之許，命宰相崔遠為節度使。 遠行及江陵，聞嶺表多盜，懼隱違詔，遲留不進，會遠復入相，乃詔以隱為留後，然久未即真。 及梁祖為元帥，隱遣使持重賂以求保薦，梁祖即表其事，遂降旄節。 梁開平初，恩寵殊厚，遷檢校太尉、兼侍中，封大彭郡王。 梁祖郊禋，禮畢，加檢校太師、兼中書令，又命兼領安南都護，充清海、靜海兩軍節度使，進封南海王。 開平四年三月卒。
Liu Zhi — that is, Liu Yan — was at first named Zhi. His ancestors were men of Pengcheng. His grandfather Ren'an served Tang as long secretary of Chaozhou and settled his family in Lingbiao. His father Qian was by nature talented and perceptive. In the Xiantong era of Tang, Grand Councilor Wei Zhou went out to command the Southern Sea. Qian was then a guard officer of very low rank, yet his bearing was extraordinary, and Zhou gave him his niece in marriage. His wife, thinking him not of her kind, firmly opposed it. Zhou said, "This man is no ordinary stream — someday my descendants may rely on him." Qian later indeed won merit in battle and was appointed prefect of Feng and concurrently commander of Hesui garrison, winning considerable praise. Qian's eldest son was Yin, born of the Wei clan woman. He was remarkable from childhood. When Qian died, among the Hesui commanders were ruffians who hoped for disorder. Yin formed a plan and executed them. Circuit commander Liu Chonggui heard of his talent, appointed him right commander of the guard, again put him in charge of Hesui garrison, and soon memorialized that he also hold Feng Prefecture. His administration was clear and stern and his prestige grew. Emperor Zhaozong, because the heir Prince of Xue Zhirou had escorted the Son of Heaven at Shimen, appointed him military governor of the Qinghai army. When the edict came down, prefectural guard officers Lu Ju and Tan Qi plotted not to obey the court command. Yin raised his troops, executed Ju and Qi, and reported it. When Zhirou arrived he was deeply grateful, made him acting commander on campaign, and entrusted him with troops and supplies. Emperor Zhaozong ordered Grand Councilor Xu Yanruo to replace Zhirou and again invest Yin in his former post. Yanruo was in command for two years. Facing death he wrote a memorial by hand recommending Yin as acting commissioner of the two circuits. Zhaozong did not approve and ordered Grand Councilor Cui Yuan as military governor. Yuan reached Jiangling and heard that bandits were numerous in Lingbiao. Fearing Yin would defy the edict he delayed and did not advance. When Yuan returned to the council seat an edict then made Yin acting commander, yet for long he was not confirmed in office. When the Liang founder became commander-in-chief, Yin sent envoys with heavy bribes to seek protection and recommendation. The Liang founder at once memorialized the matter and then granted the command baton. In the first year of Kaiping of Liang favor was especially thick. He was promoted acting Grand Guardian and concurrent Palace Attendant and enfeoffed as Prince of Dapeng commandery. When the Liang founder performed suburban sacrifice and the rites were finished, he was further made acting Grand Preceptor and concurrent Director of the Secretariat, also ordered to hold concurrently Protector-General of Annan and military governor of the Qinghai and Jinghai armies, and advanced to Prince of the Southern Sea. In the third month of the fourth year of Kaiping he died.

陟，隱之弟也，隱卒，代據其位。 及梁末帝嗣位，務行姑息之政，乃盡以隱之官爵授陟。 先是，邕州葉廣略、容州龐巨源，或自擅兵賦，數侵廣之西鄙，陟舉兵討之，邕、容皆敗，因附庸於陟。 又，交州土豪曲承美亦專據其地，送款於梁，因正授旄鉞。 陟不平之，遣將李知順伐之，執承美以獻，陟自是盡有嶺表之地。 及聞錢镠冊封吳越王，陟恥稱南海之號，乃嘆曰：「中原多故，誰為真主，安能萬里梯航而事偽庭乎！ 」梁貞明三年八月，陟乃僭號於廣州，國號大漢，偽改元為乾亨。 明年，僭行郊禮，赦其境內，及改名巖。 陟僭位之後，廣聚南海珠璣，西通黔、蜀，得其珍玩，窮奢極侈，娛僭一方，與嶺北諸藩歲時交聘。 及聞莊宗平梁，遣偽宮苑使何詞來聘，稱「大漢國王致書上大唐皇帝」。 莊守召見於鄴宮，問南海事狀，且言本國已發使臣，大陳物貢，期今秋即至。 初，陟聞莊宗兵威甚盛，故令何詞來視虛實，時朝政已紊，莊宗亦不能以道制禦遠方，南海貢亦不至，自是與中國遂絕。 唐同光三年冬，白龍見於南海，改偽乾亨元年為白龍元年，陟又改名龔，以符龍之瑞也。 白龍四年春，又改大有元年。 是歲，陟僭行耤田之禮。 陟之季年，有梵僧善占算之術，謂陟不利名龔，他年慮有此姓敗事，陟又改名纻。 纻讀為儼，古文無此字，蓋妄撰也。
Zhi was Yin's younger brother. When Yin died he seized his position. When the Last Emperor of Liang succeeded the throne and pursued a policy of indulgence, he granted Zhi all of Yin's offices and titles. Earlier Ye Guanglue of Yong Prefecture and Pang Juyuan of Rong Prefecture had each on their own held troops and taxes and repeatedly raided the western borders of Guang. Zhi raised troops to attack them. Yong and Rong were both defeated and then became his vassals. Also the local magnate of Jiaozhou Qu Chenmei held that region on his own, sent allegiance to Liang, and was duly invested with the command baton. Zhi was displeased and sent commander Li Zhishun to attack him, seized Chenmei and presented him as captive. From then on Zhi held all the lands of Lingbiao. Hearing that Qian Liu was invested as Prince of Wu-Yue, Zhi was shamed at the title of Southern Sea and sighed, "The central plains are in turmoil — who is the true lord? How can one sail ten thousand li by ladder-ship to serve a false court!" In the eighth month of the third year of Zhenming of Liang Zhi styled himself at Guangzhou, state name Great Han, and falsely changed the reign title to Qianheng. The next year he falsely performed suburban sacrifice, amnestied his territory, and also changed his name to Yan. After Zhi styled himself he amassed pearls and jade of the Southern Sea, reached west through Qian and Shu to obtain their treasures, pursued luxury to the extreme, and entertained himself as usurper in one region, exchanging envoys yearly with the feudatories north of the ranges. Hearing that Emperor Zhuangzong had pacified Liang, he sent the false Palace Gardens Commissioner He Ci to pay court, addressing "the King of Great Han presents a letter to the Great Tang Emperor." Emperor Zhuangzong summoned him at Ye Palace, asked about affairs of the Southern Sea, and said his own kingdom had already sent envoys with great displays of tribute, expected that autumn. Earlier Zhi had heard that Emperor Zhuangzong's military power was very great and therefore sent He Ci to observe the truth. At that time court affairs were already disordered and Emperor Zhuangzong also could not by principle control distant regions. The Southern Sea tribute never arrived, and from then on contact with China was severed. In the winter of the third year of Tongguang of Tang a white dragon appeared in the Southern Sea. He falsely changed the first year of Qianheng to the first year of Bailong, and Zhi also changed his name to Gong to match the dragon's omen. In the spring of the fourth year of Bailong he again changed to the first year of Dayou. That year Zhi falsely performed the plowing ritual. In Zhi's later years a Buddhist monk skilled in divination said the name Gong was unlucky for Zhi and that in future he feared someone of that surname would ruin matters. Zhi again changed his name to Zhu. Zhu is read as Yan. There is no such character in ancient writing — it was simply fabricated.

陟性雖聰辯，然好行苛虐，至有炮烙、刳剔、截舌、灌鼻之刑，一方之民，若據爐炭。 惟厚自奉養，廣務華靡，末年起玉堂珠殿，飾以金碧翠羽，嶺北行商或至其國，皆召而示之，誇其壯麗。 每對北人自言家本鹹秦，恥為蠻夷之主。 又呼中國帝王為洛州刺史，其妄自尊大，皆此類也。 晉天福七年夏四月，陟以疾卒，凡僭號二十六年，年五十四。 偽謚為天皇大帝，廟號高祖，陵曰康陵。 子玢嗣。
Though clever and eloquent by nature, Zhi loved harsh cruelty. He even had branding, disemboweling, tongue-cutting, and nose-pouring punishments, and the people of his region were as if standing on burning coals. Only for himself did he provide lavishly, pursuing splendor widely. In his later years he raised halls of jade and pearl, adorned with gold, green, and kingfisher feathers. Merchants north of the ranges who reached his state were all summoned and shown them to boast of their grandeur. Facing northerners he always said his family was originally from Xian and Qin and was ashamed to be lord of the barbarians. He also called the Son of Heaven of China "Prefect of Luozhou." His arrogant self-exaltation was all of this kind. In the fourth month of summer of the seventh year of Tianfu of Jin Zhi died of illness. He had styled himself for twenty-six years and was fifty-four. His false posthumous title was Great Emperor of Heaven, temple name Gaozu, tomb name Kang. His son Bin succeeded.
13
晟，陟第二子也。 偽封勤王，又封晉王。 玢之立也，多行淫虐，人皆患之，晟因與其弟偽越王昌等同謀弒玢，自立為帝，改元為應乾，又改為乾和。 晟率性荒暴，得志之後，專以威刑禦下，多誅滅舊臣及其昆仲，數年之間，宗族殆盡。 又造生地獄，凡湯鑊、鐵床之類，無不備焉。 人有小過，咸被其苦。 及湖南馬氏昆弟尋戈，晟因其釁，遣兵攻桂林管內諸郡及郴、連、梧、賀等州，皆克之，自此全有南越之地。 周顯德五年秋八月，晟以疾卒，偽謚曰文武光聖明孝皇帝，廟號中宗，陵曰昭陵。 是歲，晟以六月望夜宴於甘泉宮，是夕月有食之，測在牛女之度，晟自覽占書，既而投之於地，曰：「自古豈有長存者乎！ 」縱長夜之飲，至是而卒。
Sheng was Zhi's second son. He was falsely enfeoffed Diligent King and again enfeoffed Prince of Jin. When Bin was established he practiced much debauchery and cruelty and all men resented it. Sheng therefore joined his younger brother the false King of Yue Chang and others in plotting to assassinate Bin and styled himself emperor, changing the reign title to Yingqian and again to Qianhe. Sheng was by nature reckless and violent. After he got his wish he governed subordinates solely by harsh punishment, executing many old ministers and their brothers, and within several years the clan was nearly exterminated. He also built a hell on earth. Boiling cauldrons, iron beds, and the like were all provided. Whoever had a small fault suffered these torments. When the Ma brothers of Hunan fought among themselves Sheng seized the opening, sent troops to attack the prefectures within Guilin circuit and Chen, Lian, Wu, He, and others, all of which were taken. From then he held all the lands of the Southern Yue. In the eighth month of autumn of the fifth year of Xiande of Zhou Sheng died of illness. His false posthumous title was Civil and Martial, Bright and Sacred, Filial Emperor, temple name Zhongzong, tomb name Zhao. That year on the night of the full moon of the sixth month Sheng feasted at Ganquan Palace. That evening the moon was eclipsed, the measurement falling in the Ox and Woman degrees. Sheng himself looked at prognostic books, then threw them to the ground and said, "Since antiquity has anyone lived forever?" " He then gave himself to all-night drinking and died at that time.
14
鋹，晟長子也。 偽封衛王。 晟卒，乃襲偽位，時年十七，改元為大寶。 鋹性庸懦，不能治其國，政事鹹委於閹官，復有宮人具冠帶、預職官、理外事者，由是綱紀大壞。 先是，廣州法性寺有菩提樹一株，高一百四十尺，大十圍，傳云蕭梁時西域僧真諦之所手植，蓋四百餘年矣，皇朝乾德五年夏，為大風所拔。 是歲秋，鋹之寢室屢為雷震，識者知其必亡。 皇朝開寶三年夏，王師始議南征。 四年二月五日，王師壓廣州，鋹盡焚其府庫，將赴火而死，既而不能引決，尋為王師所擒，舉族遷於京師。 皇上赦而不誅，仍賜爵為恩赦侯，其後事具皇家日曆。 陟始自梁貞明三年僭號，歷三世四主，至皇朝開寶四年，凡五十五年而亡。
Chang was Sheng's eldest son. He was falsely enfeoffed Prince of Wei. When Sheng died he seized the false throne at age seventeen and changed the reign title to Dabao. Chang was by nature timid and weak and could not govern his state. Affairs were all entrusted to eunuchs, and again palace women wore caps and belts, held nominal offices, and handled external affairs, so that discipline was utterly ruined. Earlier at Guangzhou's Faxi Temple stood a bodhi tree one hundred forty chi tall and ten arm-spans around. Tradition held that the Western monk Zhendi had planted it in the Liang era, more than four hundred years before. In the summer of the fifth year of Qiande of our dynasty a great wind uprooted it. That autumn thunder repeatedly struck Chang's sleeping chamber. Men of judgment knew he would surely perish. In the summer of the third year of Kaibao the imperial army first deliberated a southern campaign. On the fifth day of the second month of the fourth year the imperial army pressed Guangzhou. Chang burned the treasury and meant to throw himself into the fire, yet could not bring himself to it. He was soon seized and the whole clan was moved to the capital. The emperor pardoned him without execution and granted the title Marquis of Enshi. His later affairs are recorded in the imperial calendar. From the time Zhi first styled himself in the third year of Zhenming of Liang through three generations and four rulers to the fourth year of Kaibao of our dynasty, fifty-five years passed and the state perished.
15
劉崇，太原人，漢高祖之從弟也。 少無賴，好陸博意錢之戲，弱冠隸河東軍。 唐長興中，遷虢州軍校。 漢祖鎮並、汾，奏為河東步軍都指揮使。 逾年，授麟州刺史，復為河東馬步軍都指揮使兼三城巡檢使，遙領泗州防禦使。 漢祖起義於河東，以崇為特進、檢校太尉、行太原尹。 是歲五月，漢祖南行，以崇為北京留守，尋加同平章事。 隱帝嗣位，加檢校太師、兼侍中。 乾祐二年九月，加兼中書令。 時漢隱帝以幼年在位，政在大臣，崇亦招募亡命，繕完兵甲，為自全之計，朝廷命令，多不稟行，征斂一方，略無虛日，人甚苦之。 三年十一月，隱帝遇害，朝廷議立崇之子徐州節度使赟為主，會周太祖為軍眾所推，降封赟為湘陰公。 崇乃遣牙將李筠奉書求赟歸藩，會赟已死，唯以優辭答之。
Liu Chong was a man of Taiyuan, a younger cousin of Han founder Gaozu. From youth he was a ruffian, fond of gambling with loaded dice. At weak cap he entered the Hedong army. In the Changxing era of Tang he was promoted to guard officer of Guo Prefecture. When Han founder Gaozu held Bing and Fen he memorialized for Chong as commander of the Hedong foot army. After more than a year he was appointed prefect of Lin, again made commander of the Hedong horse and foot army and concurrently inspector of the three cities, and remotely held defender of Si Prefecture. When Han founder raised righteousness in Hedong, Chong was made Special Advancement, acting Grand Guardian, and acting Taiyuan Director. In the fifth month of that year Han founder marched south. Chong was made northern capital regent and soon added Grand Councilor. When Emperor Yin succeeded he was further made acting Grand Preceptor and concurrent Palace Attendant. In the ninth month of the second year of Qianyou he was further made concurrent Director of the Secretariat. At that time young Emperor Yin held the throne and great ministers held power. Chong recruited fugitives, repaired arms, and planned for self-preservation. Many court commands he ignored, and levies in his region scarcely paused a day — the people suffered greatly. In the eleventh month of the third year Emperor Yin was murdered. The court deliberated making Chong's son Yun, military governor of Xuzhou, ruler. When Zhou Taizu was pushed by the army Yun was demoted to Duke of Xiangyin. Chong then sent guard officer Li Yun with a letter begging Yun's return to his fief. When Yun had already died he answered only with gracious words.
16
周廣順元年正月，崇僭號於河東，稱漢，改名旻，仍以乾祐為年號。 署其子承鈞為侍衛親軍都指揮使、太原尹，以判官鄭拱、趙華為宰相，副使李瑰、代州刺史張暉為腹心，尋遣承鈞率兵攻晉、隰二州，不克而退。 九月，崇自領兵由陰地關寇晉州，乞師於契丹，契丹以五千騎助之，合兵以攻平陽，又分兵寇昭義。 周太祖遣樞密使王峻等率大軍以援晉、絳，崇聞周師至，遂焚營而遁。 是歲，晉、絳大雪，崇駐軍六十餘日，邊民走險自固，兵無所掠，士有饑色，比至太原，十亡三四。 二年二月，崇遣兵三千餘眾寇府州，為折德扆所破，其所部岢嵐軍為德扆所取。 崇自僭稱之後，以重幣求援於契丹，仍稱侄以事之，契丹偽冊為英武皇帝。 及周世宗嗣位，崇復乞師於契丹，以圖入寇，契丹遣將楊袞合勢大舉，來迫潞州。 顯德元年三月，周世宗親征，與崇戰於高平，大敗之。 崇與親騎十數人逾山而遁，中夜迷懵，不知所適，劫村民使為鄉導，誤趨晉州路，行百餘里方覺。 崇怒，殺鄉導者，得他路而去，乃易名號，被毛褐、張樺笠而行。 至沁州，與從者三五騎止於郊舍，寒餒尤甚，潛令告偽刺史李廷誨，廷誨饋盤餐、解衣裘而與之。 每至屬邑，縣吏奉食，匕箸未舉，聞周師至，即蒼黃而去。 崇年老力憊，伏於馬上，日夜奔竄，僅能支持。 距太原一舍，其子承鈞夜以兵百人迎之而入。 及周師臨城下，崇氣懾，自固閉壘不出。 月餘，世宗乃旋軍。
In the first month of the first year of Guangshun of Zhou Chong styled himself in Hedong, called his state Han, changed his name to Min, and still used Qianyou as reign title. He appointed his son Chengjun commander of the personal guard and Taiyuan Director, made judges Zheng Gong and Zhao Hua grand councilors, and vice commissioners Li Gui and Dai commissioner Zhang Hui his inner circle. He soon sent Chengjun against Jin and Xi but could not take them and withdrew. In the ninth month Chong personally led troops through Yindi Pass to raid Jin Prefecture, begged troops from the Khitan, and received five thousand horse. They joined to attack Pingyang and divided troops to raid Zhaoyi. Zhou Taizu sent Palace Director Wang Jun and others with a great army to relieve Jin and Jiang. When Chong heard Zhou troops had come he burned his camp and fled. That year Jin and Jiang had heavy snow. Chong halted more than sixty days while border people fled to rugged places and troops had nothing to plunder. The soldiers showed hunger, and by Taiyuan three or four in ten had perished. In the second month of the second year Chong sent more than three thousand troops to raid Fu Prefecture and was defeated by Zhe Dewei, who also took his Kelan army. After he styled himself Chong used heavy gifts to seek Khitan aid and called himself nephew in serving them. The Khitan falsely invested him as Martial and Martial Emperor. When Zhou Shizong succeeded Chong again begged Khitan troops to plan invasion. The Khitan sent commander Yang Gun in great combined force against Luzhou. In the third month of the first year of Xiande Zhou Shizong campaigned in person and routed Chong greatly at Gaoping. Chong fled over the mountains with a dozen personal horse. In the middle of the night, dazed, he knew not where he was, seized villagers as guides, mistakenly took the Jin Prefecture road, and only after more than a hundred li realized it. Chong in anger killed the guides, took another road, changed his name, wore coarse wool and a birch-bark hat, and went on. Reaching Qin Prefecture he halted with three or five horsemen at a suburban lodge in extreme cold and hunger. He secretly sent word to false prefect Li Tinghui, who gave him a meal and lent a fur coat. Whenever he reached a subordinate district clerks offered food. Before he raised chopsticks he heard Zhou troops had come and fled in panic. Chong, old and exhausted, lay prostrate on his horse, fleeing day and night, barely able to hold on. One stage from Taiyuan his son Chengjun met him at night with a hundred troops and brought him in. When Zhou troops came beneath the walls Chong's spirit was cowed and he held behind closed ramparts without sallying. After more than a month Shizong turned the army back.
17
顯德二年十一月，崇以病死，其子承鈞襲偽位。 鈞之事跡具皇家日曆。
In the eleventh month of the second year of Xiande Chong died of illness and his son Chengjun seized the false throne. Chengjun's deeds are recorded in the imperial calendar.
18
史臣曰：守光逆天反道，從古所無，迨至臨刑，尚求免死，非唯惡之極也，抑亦愚之甚也。 劉晟據南極以稱雄，屬中原之多事，洎乎奕世，遇我昌朝，力憊而亡，不泯其嗣，亦其幸也。 劉崇以亡國之餘，竊偽王之號，多見其不知量也。 今元惡雖斃，遣孽尚存，勢蹙民殘，不亡何待！
The historiographer says: Shouguang defied Heaven and reversed the Way — from antiquity there has been none like him. When he came to execution he still begged to escape death. This is wickedness at its extreme and stupidity at its height. Liu Sheng held the far south to proclaim supremacy while the central plains were in turmoil. Through generations until our flourishing dynasty his strength was spent and he perished, yet his line was not extinguished — this too was fortune. Liu Chong, remnant of a perished state, stole the title of false king — one often sees he did not know his measure. Now though the chief villain is dead, surviving evil remains. His power is cramped and his people ruined — if he does not perish, what is he waiting for!
