1
天福六年秋七月己未朔，帝御崇元殿視朝。 庚申，升陳州為防禦使額。 辛酉，以前鄧州節度使焦方為貝州節度使。 壬戌，涇州奏，西涼府留後李文謙，今年二月四日閉宅門自焚，遣元入西涼府譯語官與來人賫三部族蕃書進之。 以三司使劉審交為陳州防禦使。 癸亥，以前鄆州節度使趙在禮為許州節度使，以前鄴都留守、廣晉尹高行周為河南尹、西都留守。 詔改拱辰、威和、內直等軍並為興順。 甲子，以宣徽使、權西京留守張從恩判三司。 己巳，以鄴都留守兼侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使、廣晉尹劉知遠為太原尹，充北京留守、河東節度使，仍割遼、沁二州卻隸河東。 以北京留守李德充為廣晉尹，充鄴都留守； 以昭義節度使馬全節為邢州節度使，加同平章事。 甲戌，詔：「今後諸道行軍副使，不得奏薦骨肉為殿直供奉官。 」己卯，以前陜州節度使李從敏為昭義軍節度使，以陜州節度使景延廣為河陽三城節度使兼侍衛親軍馬步軍都虞候，以河陽節度使石赟為陜州節度使。 壬午，突厥遣使朝貢。 以遙領壽州忠正軍節度使兼侍衛馬軍都指揮使李懷忠為同州節度使，以宣徽北院使李守貞遙領忠正軍節度使、侍衛馬軍指揮使。 甲申，降御劄，取八月五日暫幸鄴都，沿路供頓，並委所司以官物排比，州縣不得科率人戶。 丙戌，以右諫議大夫趙遠為中書舍人，吏部郎中鄭受益為右諫議大夫，刑部郎中殷鵬為水部郎中、知制誥。
On the jiuwei new moon of the seventh month of Tianfu 6, the Emperor held court in Chongyuan Hall. On gengshen Chen Prefecture was raised to defense-commissioner status. On xinyou Jiao Fang, former governor of Deng, was appointed governor of Bei. On renxu Jing reported that Li Wenqian, regent of Xiliang, had shut his gates and burned himself on the fourth of the second month; a Xiliang native interpreter came with the envoy bearing tribal documents from three clans. Liu Shenjiao, Three Departments commissioner, was named defense commissioner of Chen. On guihai Zhao Zaili, former Yan governor, took Xu; Gao Xingzhou, former Yedu regent and Guangjin prefect, became Henan prefect and Western Capital regent. An edict renamed the Gongchen, Weihe, Neizhi, and related armies Xingshun. On jiazi Zhang Cong'en, palace commissioner and acting Western Capital regent, was put in charge of the Three Departments. On jisi Liu Zhiyuan, Yedu regent, palace army commander, and Guangjin prefect, became Taiyuan prefect, Northern Capital regent, and Hedong governor; Liao and Qin were restored to Hedong. Li Dechong, Northern Capital regent, became Guangjin prefect and Yedu regent; Ma Quanjie, Zhaoyi governor, was transferred to Xing and made co-director of the Secretariat-Chancellery. On jiaxu an edict declared: "Campaign deputies on all circuits may no longer recommend relatives for palace attendant or tribute posts." " On jimao Li Congmin, former Shan governor, took Zhaoyi; Jing Yanguang left Shan for the Three Cities of Heyang and became palace army chief adjutant; Shi Yun moved from Heyang to Shan. On renwu Turk envoys arrived with tribute. Li Huaizhong, titular Zhongzheng governor at Shou and palace horse commander, became Tong governor; Li Shouzhen of the Northern Palace Bureau was named titular Zhongzheng governor and horse army commander. On jiashen a rescript set the fifth of the eighth month for a temporary visit to Yedu; route supplies were to come from official stores only—no levies on households. On bingxu Zhao Yuan became Secretariat drafter; Zheng Shouyi moved from Personnel to right remonstrator; Yin Peng became Works director and edict drafter.
2
八月戊子朔，以皇子開封尹、鄭王重貴為東京留守，以天平軍節度使兼侍衛親軍馬步軍副都指揮使杜重威為侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使，以宣徽南院使張從恩為東京內外兵馬都監。 改奉德馬軍為護聖。 放文武百官朝參，取便先赴鄴都。 壬辰，車駕發東京。 己亥，至鄴，左右金吾六軍儀仗排列如儀，迎引入內。 改舊澶州為德清軍。 以內客省使劉遂清為宣徽北院使，判三司。 壬寅，制：「應天福六年八月十五日昧爽已前，諸色罪犯，常赦所不原者，咸赦除之； 其持仗行劫及殺人賊，並免罪移鄉，配逐處軍都收管； 犯枉法贓者，雖免罪不得再任用； 諸徒流人並放還； 貶降官未量移與量移者，約資敘用。 天福五年終已前殘稅並放。 應河東起義之初及收復鄴都、汜水立功將校，並與加恩； 亡歿者與追贈。 自東京至鄴都緣路，昨因行幸，有損踐田苗處，據頃畝與放今年租稅。 鄴都管內，有潛龍時在職者，並與加恩。 耆年八十已上者，版授上佐官。 天下農器，並許百姓自鑄造。 亡命山澤者，招喚歸業； 百日不出者，復罪如初。 唐梁國公狄仁傑宜追贈官秩。 應天福三年已前，敗闕場院官無家業者，並與除放，其人免罪，永不任使。 私下債負征利及一倍者並放，主持者不在此限。 」丁未，以客省使、將作監丁知浚為內客省使，引進使、鴻臚卿王景崇為客省使，殿中監、判四方館事劉政恩為引進使。 壬子，改鄴都皇城南門應天門為乾明門，大明館為都亭驛。 甲寅，遣光祿卿張澄、國子博士謝攀使高麗行冊禮。
On the wuzi new moon of the eighth month Prince Chonggui of Zheng, Kaifeng prefect, became Eastern Capital regent; Du Chongwei, Tianping governor and palace army deputy, took full command; Zhang Cong'en supervised all Eastern Capital troops. The Fengde horse army was renamed Husheng. Civil and military officials were excused from court and could go on to Yedu when convenient. On renchen the Emperor left the Eastern Capital. On jihai the court reached Ye; Golden Crow guards and the six armies formed the prescribed escort and led the Emperor in. Former Chanzou was redesignated the Deqing army. Liu Suqing, Inner Guests commissioner, became Northern Palace commissioner and took charge of the Three Departments. On renyin a statute declared: "All crimes before dawn on the fifteenth of the eighth month of Tianfu 6—even those outside ordinary amnesties—are fully pardoned;" armed robbers and murderers are spared but banished to local army custody; bribe-takers are pardoned but barred from office forever; all exiles and convicts are sent home; demoted officials, reassigned or not, may be considered for reinstatement by qualification. All tax arrears through the end of Tianfu 5 are forgiven. Officers who distinguished themselves in the Hedong uprising and the recoveries of Yedu and Sishui are to be rewarded; the dead are to receive posthumous honors. Crops damaged along the Eastern Capital–Yedu route during the imperial tour are exempt from this year's rent and tax by acreage. All who served in Yedu during Gaozu's rise there are to be rewarded. Those eighty and older receive honorary upper-assistant titles. Commoners may cast their own farm tools throughout the realm. Fugitives in the wilds are called home to their trades; those who fail to appear within a hundred days face their original penalties. Di Renjie, Tang's Duke of Liang, is to receive posthumous rank. Market-yard officials who failed before Tianfu 3 and lacked estates are dismissed, pardoned, and barred from office forever. Private debts with interest up to double principal are forgiven—except for brokers. " On dingwei Ding Zhijun became Inner Guests commissioner; Wang Jingchong took the Guests Bureau; Liu Zheng'en became introduction commissioner. On renzi Yedu's south gate Yingtian became Qianming, and the Daming Hostel became the capital relay station. On jiayin Zhang Cheng and Xie Pan were dispatched to Goryeo for investiture ceremonies.
3
九月己未，以兵部侍郎閻至為吏部侍郎。 辛酉，滑州河決，一溉東流，鄉村戶民攜老幼登丘冢，為水所隔，餓死者甚眾。 壬申，忠武建武等軍節度使、守太傅、兼中書令、行蘇州睦州刺史錢元尞進封彭城郡王，遙領廣州清海軍節度使、判婺州軍州事錢元懿為檢校太師。 乙亥，遣前邢州節度使楊彥詢使於契丹，錫賫甚厚。 丁丑，吐渾遣使朝貢。 壬午夜，有彗星出於西方，長二丈餘，在房一度，尾跡穿天市垣東行，逾月而滅。 丙戌，兗州上言，水自西來，漂沒秋稼。
On jiwei in the ninth month Yan Zhi moved from War to Personnel vice minister. On xinyou the river broke at Hua and poured east; villagers fled with families to high ground, trapped by floodwaters, and starvation was widespread. On renshen Qian Yuanliao, Zhongwu-Jianwu governor and acting Suzhou-Muzhou prefect, was made Prince of Pengcheng; Qian Yuanyi, titular Qinghai governor at Guangzhou and Wuzhou administrator, was named honorary Grand Preceptor. On yihai former Xing governor Yang Yanxun was sent to the Khitan with lavish gifts. On dingchou Tuyuhun envoys arrived with tribute. At midnight on renwu a comet appeared in the west, over two zhang long in Fang's first degree; its tail crossed the eastern Market Enclosure eastward and faded after a month. On bingxu Yan reported western floodwaters had destroyed the autumn harvest.
4
冬十月丁亥朔，遣鴻臚少卿魏比等四人，分往滑、濮、鄆、澶視水害苗稼。 己丑，詔以胡梁度月城為大通軍，浮橋為大通橋。 壬寅，詔唐梁國公狄仁傑可贈太師。
On the dinghai new moon of the tenth month Wei Bi and three others were dispatched to Hua, Pu, Yan, and Chan to survey flood damage. On jichou an edict renamed the Huliang ferry settlement Datong army and its pontoon Datong Bridge. On renyin an edict granted Di Renjie, Tang's Duke of Liang, posthumous Grand Preceptor.
5
十二月丙戌朔，以東京留守、開封尹、鄭王重貴為廣晉尹，進封齊王； 以鄴都留守、廣晉尹李德充為開封尹，充東京留守。 南面軍前奏，十一月二十七日，武德使焦繼勛、先鋒都指揮使郭金海等於唐州南遇安從進賊軍一萬餘人，大破之， 〈 （《宋史·陳思讓傳》：思讓為先鋒右廂都監，從武德焦繼勛領兵進討，遇從進之師於唐州花山下，急擊，大破之。）〉 生擒衙內都指揮使安宏義，獲山南東道之印，其安從進單騎奔逸。 丁亥，詔襄州行營都部署高行周權知襄州軍州事。 是日，鎮州節度使安重榮稱兵向闕，以侍衛親軍馬步軍都指揮使杜重威為北面行營招討使，率兵擊之，以邢州節度使馬全節為副，以前貝州節度使王周為馬步軍都虞候。 癸巳，武德使焦繼勛奏，安從進遣弟從貴領兵千人，取接均州刺史蔡行遇，尋領所部兵掩殺賊軍七百餘人，生擒安從貴，截其雙腕，卻放入城。 戊戌，以皇子重睿為銀青光祿大夫、檢校尚書左僕射。 己亥，北面軍前奏，十三日未時，於宗城縣西南大破鎮州賊軍，殺一萬五千人，餘黨走保宗城縣。 是夜三更，破縣城，前深州刺史史虔武自縛歸降。 獲馬三千匹，絹三萬餘匹，餘物稱是。 安重榮脫身遁走。 是日，百官稱賀。 癸卯，削奪安從進、安重榮在身官爵。 右金吾上將軍萇從簡卒，廢朝，贈太師。 乙巳，天下兵馬都元帥、守尚書令、吳越國王錢元瓘薨，廢朝三日，謚曰文穆。 是日，帝習射於後苑，諸軍都指揮使已上悉預焉，賜物有差。 丁未，南面行營都部署高行周奏，今月十三日，部領大軍至襄州城下，相次降賊軍二千人。 其降兵馬軍詔以「彰聖」為號，步軍以「歸順」為號。 庚戌，以權知吳越國事錢宏佐為起復鎮軍大將軍、檢校太師、兼中書令、杭州越州大都督、鎮海鎮東等軍節度使，封吳越國王。 壬子，杜重威部領大軍至鎮州城下。
On the bingxu new moon of the twelfth month Chonggui of Zheng, Eastern Capital regent and Kaifeng prefect, became Guangjin prefect and was advanced to Prince of Qi; Li Dechong, Yedu regent and Guangjin prefect, became Kaifeng prefect and Eastern Capital regent. The southern front reported that on the twenty-seventh of the eleventh month Jiao Jixun, Guo Jinhai, and others met over ten thousand of An Congjin's rebels south of Tang and crushed them, 〈Note (The 《Song History · Chen Sirang》 says Sirang commanded the vanguard right wing under Jiao Jixun, met Congjin below Flower Mountain in Tang, attacked at once, and routed him.)〉 taking An Hongyi, yamen commander, alive and capturing the Shannan East seal; Congjin himself fled alone. On dinghai Gao Xingzhou, Xiangzhou field commander, was named acting Xiangzhou administrator. That same day Zhen governor An Chongrong marched on the capital; palace army commander Du Chongwei was named northern campaign commissioner, with Xing governor Ma Quanjie as deputy and former Bei governor Wang Zhou as chief adjutant. On guisi Jiao Jixun reported Congjin's brother Conggui led a thousand men to join Jun prefect Cai Xingyu, then ambushed and killed seven hundred rebels, captured Conggui, severed both wrists, and sent him back into the city. On wuxu Prince Chongrui was made Silver-Gleam grand master and honorary Left Vice Director of the Secretariat. On jihai the northern front reported that on the thirteenth at the wei hour they routed Zhen rebels southwest of Zongcheng, killing fifteen thousand; survivors fled into Zongcheng County. That night at the third watch they took the county seat; former Shen prefect Shi Qianwu bound himself and surrendered. They seized three thousand horses, over thirty thousand bolts of silk, and comparable other booty. An Chongrong escaped. That day the court offered congratulations. On guimao Congjin and Chongrong were stripped of all titles and ranks. Right Golden Crow general-in-chief Chang Congjian died; court was suspended and he was posthumously named Grand Preceptor. On yisi Wuyue king Qian Yuanguan, commander-in-chief and Secretariat director, died; court mourned three days and gave him the posthumous title Wenmu. That day the Emperor practiced archery in the rear garden; army commanders and above attended and received graded gifts. On dingwei Gao Xingzhou reported reaching Xiangzhou on the thirteenth with the main army; two thousand rebels surrendered in succession. Surrendered horse troops were named Zhangsheng; foot troops, Guishun. On gengxu Qian Hongzuo, acting Wuyue administrator, was recalled from mourning as army general-in-chief, honorary Grand Preceptor, Secretariat director, Hangzhou-Yuezhou protector, Zhenhai-Zhendong governor, and King of Wuyue. On renzi Du Chongwei brought the main army to Zhen.
6
天福七年春正月丙辰朔，不受朝賀，用兵故也。 戊午，以前將作監李鍇為少府監。 北面招討使杜重威奏，今月二日收復鎮州，斬安重榮，傳首闕下。 帝御乾明樓，宣露布訖，大理卿受馘，付市徇之，百官稱賀。 曲赦廣晉府禁囚。 〈 （《遼史》云：戊辰，晉函安重榮首來獻，上數欲親討重榮，至是乃止。）〉 辛酉，追贈皇弟三人：故沂州馬步軍都指揮使、贈太傅德再贈太尉，追封福王； 故檢校太子賓客、贈太傅殷再贈太尉，追封通王； 故彰聖右第三軍都指揮使長州刺史、贈大傅威再贈太尉，追封廣王。 壬戌，追贈皇子五人：故右衛將軍、贈太保重英再贈太傅，追封虢王； 故權東京留守、河南尹、贈太傅重乂再贈太尉，追封壽王； 故皇城副使、贈太保重裔再贈太傅，追封郯王； 故河陽節度使、贈太尉重信再贈太師，追封沂王； 故左金吾衛將軍、贈太保重進再贈太傅，追封夔王。 癸亥，改鎮州為恒州，成德軍為順國軍。 丙寅，以門下侍郎、平章事、監修國史趙瑩為侍中； 青州節度使楊光遠加食邑，改賜功臣名號； 兗州節度使桑維翰加檢校太保； 河東節度使劉知遠加兼侍中； 以鄆州節度使、北面行營招討使、侍衛親軍都指揮使杜重威為恒州順國軍節度使，加兼侍中； 皇子廣晉尹兼功德使、齊王重貴加兼侍中； 秦州節度使侯益加特進，增食邑。 丁卯，以判四方館事孟承誨為太府卿充職。 戊辰，以滄州節度使安叔千為邢州節度使，以北面行營副招討使、邢州節度使馬全節為定州節度使，以定州節度使王庭胤為滄州節度使，以前邢州節度使楊彥詢為華州節度使。 恒州立功將校王溫以降等第除郡。 庚午，契丹遣使來聘。 是日上元節，六街諸寺燃燈，禦乾明門觀之，夜半還宮。 壬申，延州節度使丁審琦加爵邑，鄧州節度使安審徽加檢校太傅，陜州節度使石赟加檢校太傅。 乙亥，契丹遣使來聘。 河陽節度使兼侍衛馬步軍都虞候景延廣加檢校太尉，改鄆州節度使，典軍如故。 以前貝州節度使、北面行營馬步軍都虞候王周為河陽節度使，加檢校太保。 丁丑，以刑部侍郎竇貞固為門下侍郎，以禮部郎中邊歸讜為比部郎中、知制誥。 壬午，以河陽節度使王周為涇州節度使，以恒州節度副使王欽祚為殿中監。
On the bingchen new moon of the first month of Tianfu 7 the court skipped New Year congratulations because of the war. On wuwu former construction director Li Kai became Palace Workshops director. Northern campaign commissioner Du Chongwei reported recovering Zhen on the second of the month, beheading Chongrong, and sending his head to the capital. The Emperor appeared at Qianming Tower; after the victory bulletin the chief justice took the head for public display in the market, and the court congratulated him. A partial amnesty freed Guangjin jail prisoners. 〈Note (The 《Liao History》 says on wuchen Jin presented Chongrong's head in a box; the Liao emperor, who had repeatedly planned to campaign against him in person, then desisted.)〉 On xinyou three younger brothers were posthumously honored: De, former Yi army commander, was raised from Grand Mentor to Grand Marshal and enfeoffed as Prince of Fu; Yin, former honorary heir-apparent guest, was raised to Grand Marshal and enfeoffed as Prince of Tong; Wei, former Zhangsheng Right Third Army commander and Chang prefect, was raised to Grand Marshal and enfeoffed as Prince of Guang. On renxu five sons were posthumously honored: Chongying, former Right Guard general, was raised from Grand Mentor to Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as Prince of Guo; Chongyi, former acting Eastern Capital regent and Henan prefect, was raised to Grand Marshal and enfeoffed as Prince of Shou; Chongyi, former Imperial City deputy, was raised to Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as Prince of Tan; Chongxin, former Heyang governor, was raised to Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as Prince of Yi; Chongjin, former Left Golden Crow general, was raised to Grand Preceptor and enfeoffed as Prince of Kui. On guihai Zhen became Heng and the Chengde army became Shunguo. On bingyin Zhao Ying, Chancellery vice director, chief minister, and history overseer, became Palace Attendant; Qing governor Yang Guangyuan received added fief income and a new merit title; Yan governor Sang Weihan was named honorary Grand Guardian; Hedong governor Liu Zhiyuan was also made Palace Attendant; Yan governor, northern campaign commissioner, and palace commander Du Chongwei became Shunguo governor at Heng and Palace Attendant; Prince of Qi Chonggui, Guangjin prefect and merit commissioner, was also made Palace Attendant; Qin governor Hou Yi received special advancement and added fief income. On dingmao Meng Chenghui, Four Directions Office overseer, became Grand Storehouse director. On wuchen Cang governor An Shuqian took Xing; northern deputy campaign commissioner and Xing governor Ma Quanjie took Ding; Ding governor Wang Tingyin took Cang; former Xing governor Yang Yanxun became Hua governor. Heng officer Wang Wen, honored for merit, received a prefectural post at a reduced rank grade. On gengwu Khitan envoys arrived on a courtesy visit. That day, on the Lantern Festival, lamps blazed in temples along the six wards; the Emperor viewed them from Qianming Gate and returned to the palace at midnight. On renshen Yan'an governor Ding Shenqi received added noble rank and fief; Deng governor An Shenhui and Shan governor Shi Yun were named honorary Grand Tutor. As he prepared to raise troops at Taiyuan, wolves ran in packs through the capital at night and often entered the palace; Emperor Min ordered the best archers of the guard to hunt them, calling it "shooting wolves." Meeting people on the road they would ask, "Where are you coming from?" " They would answer, "To watch the wolf-shooting." " Before long Gaozu arrived. For the rhyme of "shoot" (she) was close to "stone" (shi). The 《Lost Texts of the History of the Five Dynasties》 records that early in Liang's Kaiping era Lu field commissioner Li Si'an reported: In Hanguan's Rang township a felled tree split in two, revealing six characters in left-hand script: "Heaven, fourteen reigns, Stone advances." The Liang emperor stored it in the armory; none then knew what it meant. When he took the throne interpreters said: "Heaven" with two strokes of "Four" added beside it forms "Bing"; "Four" with its middle strokes removed and "Ten" added makes "Shen." The year of his accession was indeed bingshen. "Advances" meant Jin; "Stone" was the surname. This commentator notes: Tianyou 20 was guiwei, when Zhuangzong took the throne as Tongguang 1; by Qingtai 3, bingshen, when the Jin founder acceded as Tianfu 1, fourteen reign-years had passed from wei to shen. So "Heaven, fourteen reigns, Stone advances" means fourteen years after Tianyou the Shi house rose in Jin—is that not plain! But splitting characters to force a bingshen reading is wrong.)〉

史臣曰：晉祖潛躍之前沈毅而已。 及其為君也，旰食宵衣，禮賢從諫，慕黃、老之教，樂清凈之風，以絁為衣，以麻為履，故能保其社稷，高朗令終。 然而圖事之初，召戎來援，獫狁自茲而孔熾，黔黎由是以罹殃。 迨至嗣君，兵連禍結，卒使都城失守，舉族為俘。 亦猶決鯨海以救焚，何逃沒溺； 飲鴆漿而止渴，終取喪亡。 謀之不臧，何至於是！ 倘使非由外援之力，自副皇天之命，以茲睿德，惠彼蒸民，雖未足以方駕前王，亦可謂仁慈恭儉之主也。
The historiographer writes: Before his rise Gaozu was known only for quiet resolve. As ruler he labored from dawn to dusk, honored worthies and heeded counsel, cherished the teachings of Huangdi and Laozi and the way of simplicity, wore coarse silk and hemp shoes, and so preserved the realm and died with honor. Yet in seeking power he called in the northern tribes; from then the frontier grew ever more dangerous and the people paid the price. Under his successor war and disaster compounded until the capital fell and the house was taken captive. It was like breaching the sea to fight a fire—drowning was inevitable; drinking poison to slake thirst, and inviting ruin in the end. Poor counsel brought matters to this pass! Without reliance on foreign arms, had he truly matched Heaven's mandate and blessed the people with this wisdom, he might not have equaled the greatest of antiquity, yet he could still be called a benevolent, respectful, and frugal sovereign.
