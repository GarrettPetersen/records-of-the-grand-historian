高行周，字尚質，幽州人也。 生於媯州懷戎軍之雕窠裏。 曾祖順厲，世戍懷戎。 父思繼，昆仲三人，俱雄豪有武幹，聲馳朔方。 唐武皇之平幽州也，表劉仁恭為帥，仍留兵以戍之。 以思繼兄為先鋒都將、媯州刺史，思繼為中軍都將、順州刺史，思繼弟為後軍都將，昆仲分掌燕兵。 部下士伍，皆山北之豪也，仁恭深憚之。 武皇將歸，私謂仁恭曰：「高先鋒兄弟，勢傾州府，為燕患者，必此族也，宜善籌之。 」久之，太原戍軍恣橫，思繼兄弟制之以法，所殺者多。 太祖怒，詬讓仁恭，乃訴以高氏兄弟，遂並遇害。 仁恭因以先鋒子行珪為牙將，諸子並列帳下，厚撫之以慰其心。 時行周十餘歲，亦補職，在仁恭左右。 行珪別有傳，在《唐書》。
Gao Xingzhou, whose courtesy name was Shangzhi, came from Youzhou. He was born in Diaoke village, in the Huairong garrison district of Guizhou. His great-grandfather Shunli had for generations served as a garrison soldier at Huairong. His father Siji and his two brothers were all bold warriors of proven martial skill, and their renown rang across the northern marches. When Li Keyong pacified Youzhou, he installed Liu Rengong as military governor and left a garrison force behind. Siji's elder brother became vanguard commander and prefect of Guizhou; Siji took the central army and the prefecture of Shunzhou; the youngest brother commanded the rear guard — between them the three brothers controlled the Yan region's military forces. The men in their ranks were powerful northern chieftains, and Rengong viewed them with deep apprehension. Before Li Keyong withdrew, he took Rengong aside: "The Gao brothers command more power than your government can hold. They are the men who will become Yan's curse. You must deal with them wisely." In time the Taiyuan garrison grew insubordinate; the Siji brothers enforced discipline upon them, and many troopers were executed. The founding emperor was furious and berated Rengong, who shifted blame to the Gao brothers — whereupon all three were killed. Rengong appointed the slain vanguard's son Xinggui to his personal guard and brought the surviving sons under his command, treating them generously to win their loyalty. Xingzhou was then barely past ten; he too was given a post and remained at Rengong's side. Xinggui's biography appears separately in the Book of Tang.

及莊宗收燕，以行周隸明宗帳下，常與唐末帝分率牙兵。 明宗征燕，率其下擁從。 鄉人趙德鈞謂明宗曰：「行周心甚謹厚，必享貴位。 」梁將劉鄩之據莘也，與太原軍對壘，旦夕轉鬥。 嘗一日，兩軍成列，元行欽為敵軍追躡，劍中其面，血戰未解。 行周以麾下精騎突陣解之，行欽獲免。 莊宗方寵行欽，召行周撫諭賞勞，而欲置之帳下，又念於明宗帳下已奪行欽，更取行周，恐傷其意，密令人以利祿誘之。 行周辭曰：「總管用人，亦為國家，事總管猶事王也。 余家昆仲，脫難再生，承總管之厚恩，忍背之乎！ 」及兩軍屯於河上，覘知梁軍自汴入楊村寨，明宗晨至斗門，設伏將邀之，眾寡不敵，反為所乘。 時矛槊叢萃，勢甚危蹙。 行周聞之，出騎橫擊梁軍，遂得解去。 明宗之襲鄆州也，行周為前鋒。 會夜分澍雨，人無進誌，行周曰：「此天贊也，彼必無備。 」是夜，涉河入東城，比曙平之。 莊宗平河南，累加檢校太保，領端州刺史。 同光末，出守絳州。 明宗即位，特深委遇。 天成中，從王晏球圍定州，敗王都，擒禿諾，皆有功。 賊平，遷潁州團練使。 長興初，以北邊鄰戎王，用為振武節度使。 明年，以河西用軍，移鎮延安。 清泰初，改潞州節度使。 晉祖建義於太原，唐末帝命張敬達征之，行周與符彥卿為左右排陣使。 契丹主入援太原也，行周、彥卿引騎拒之，尋為契丹所敗，遂與敬達保晉安寨，累月救軍不至。 楊光遠欲圖敬達，行周知之，引壯士護之。 敬達性戇，不知其營護，謂人曰：「行周每踵余後，其意何也？ 」繇是不復敢然，敬達遂為光遠所害。
After Zhuangzong conquered Yan, Xingzhou was assigned to Mingzong's staff and often shared command of the guard corps with the future Last Emperor of Tang. When Mingzong marched on Yan, Xingzhou led his men in loyal attendance. A fellow northerner, Zhao Dejun, told Mingzong: "Xingzhou is remarkably steady and loyal. He is destined for high office." When the Liang general Liu Yan held Xin, the armies of Liang and Taiyuan confronted each other and skirmished without cease. Once, with both armies in battle order, Yuan Xingqin was chased by the enemy; a blade cut his face, yet he fought on, drenched in blood and unable to break free. Xingzhou led his elite horsemen in a charge that broke the encirclement, and Xingqin was saved. Zhuangzong, who doted on Xingqin, summoned Xingzhou to praise and reward him and wished to add him to his own household guard. But he had already lured Xingqin away from Mingzong; to take Xingzhou too might offend Mingzong, so he secretly sent envoys to tempt Xingzhou with rank and riches. Xingzhou refused: "My commander hires men for the nation's sake. To serve him is to serve the throne." My brothers and I were spared from extinction and given new life by his generosity. How could we turn our backs on him? While the two armies held the north bank of the Yellow River, scouts reported Liang troops advancing from Bian toward Yangji village. Mingzong reached Doumen at dawn to lay an ambush, but his force was too small and he was overrun instead. Spears and halberds pressed in on every side; the situation was desperate. Hearing of the crisis, Xingzhou rode out and struck the Liang flank, allowing Mingzong's force to break away. When Mingzong attacked Yanzhou, Xingzhou led the van. At midnight a downpour began and the troops lost their appetite for advance. Xingzhou said: "Heaven favors us. The enemy will have no guard up." That night they forded the river, took the eastern quarter, and by dawn the city was his. After Zhuangzong subdued Henan, Xingzhou was promoted to acting Grand Guardian and appointed prefect of Duanzhou. In the final years of the Tongguang reign, he was sent to defend Jiangzhou. When Mingzong acceded, Xingzhou enjoyed his exceptional trust. During the Tiancheng period he joined Wang Yanqiu's siege of Dingzhou, helped defeat Wang Du, and captured Tuo Nuo — winning distinction in each action. After the rebellion ended, he was made regimental commissioner of Yingzhou. Early in Changxing, because the northern frontier bordered Rong and Wang tribal powers, he was appointed military governor of Zhenwu. The following year, with campaigns underway in the Hexi, he was reassigned to Yan'an. At the opening of the Qingtai era he became military governor of Luzhou. When Gaozu of Later Jin rebelled at Taiyuan, the Last Emperor sent Zhang Jingda against him, with Xingzhou and Fu Yanqing as his left and right wing commanders. When the Khitan khan marched to relieve Taiyuan, Xingzhou and Yanqing met him with cavalry but were soon routed. They fell back to Jin'an Stockade with Jingda, where they waited months for reinforcements that never came. Learning that Yang Guangyuan meant to harm Jingda, Xingzhou gathered trusted warriors to guard him. Jingda was obtuse and unaware of the protection; he complained, "Why does Xingzhou dog my every step?" Yang no longer dared act openly, but Jingda was nevertheless murdered by Guangyuan.

晉祖入洛，令行周還藩，加同平章事。 晉祖都汴，以行周為西京留守，未幾，移鄴都。 晉祖幸鄴，會安從進叛，命行周為襄州行營都部署。 明年秋，平定漢南。 晉少帝嗣位，加兼侍中，移鎮睢陽。 開運初，從幸澶淵，拒敵於河上。 車駕還京，代景延廣為侍衛親軍都指揮使，移鄆州節度使。 時李彥韜為侍衛都虞候，可否在己。 行周雖典禁兵，心遊事外，退朝歸第，門宇翛然，賓友過從，但引滿而已。 尋改歸德軍節度使，以李守貞代掌兵柄，許行周歸藩。 晉軍降於中渡也，少帝命行周與符彥卿同守澶州。 契丹入汴，召赴京師，會草寇攻宋州急，遣行周歸鎮。 〈 （《宋史·高懷德傳》：杜重威降契丹，京東諸州群盜大起，懷德堅壁清野，敵不能入，行周率兵歸鎮，敵遂解去。）〉 及契丹主死於欒城，契丹將蕭翰立許王李從益知南朝軍國事，遣死士召行周，辭之以疾，退謂人曰：「衰世難輔，況兒戲乎！」
After Gaozu entered Luoyang, Xingzhou was sent back to his command with the added rank of Grand Councillor. When Gaozu established his court at Bian, Xingzhou became regent of the Western Capital, then was soon transferred to Ye. During Gaozu's visit to Ye, An Congjin rebelled; Xingzhou was named supreme field commander for the Xiangzhou campaign. The following autumn the Han River southlands were pacified. When Emperor Chudi succeeded, Xingzhou was made concurrent Palace Attendant and transferred to Suiyang. Early in Kaiyun he accompanied the emperor to Chanzhou and held the enemy at the Yellow River. After the emperor returned to the capital, Xingzhou replaced Jing Yanguang as commander of the palace guard and was made military governor of Yanzhou. Li Yantao served as chief adjutant of the guard and controlled every decision himself. Though nominally in charge of the palace armies, Xingzhou kept his mind elsewhere. After court he retired to a quiet household; when friends visited, he merely filled their cups and his own. He was soon made military governor of Guide, with Li Shouzhen taking the guard command while Xingzhou was allowed to return to his province. After the Jin armies surrendered at Zhongdu Ford, Chudi ordered Xingzhou and Fu Yanqing to hold Chanzhou together. When the Khitan took Bian, Xingzhou was called to court, but raiders were pressing Songzhou hard, so he was sent back to his post. [ (History of Song, Biography of Gao Huaide: After Du Chongwei surrendered to the Khitan, brigands rose across the eastern provinces. Huaide sealed his walls and stripped the countryside bare so raiders could not penetrate. When Xingzhou marched back to his post, the enemy lifted the siege.)] After the Khitan emperor died at Luancheng, the Khitan general Xiao Han installed Prince Xu Li Congyi to govern the southern realm's military and civil affairs and sent sworn agents to summon Xingzhou. He pleaded illness and later told others, "A dying age is not worth serving — and this is mere child's play besides!"

子懷德，皇朝駙馬都尉、宋州節度使。
His son Huaide served the present dynasty as imperial son-in-law and military governor of Songzhou.

安審琦，字國瑞，其先沙陀部人也。 祖山盛，朔州牢城都校，贈太傅。 父金全，安北都護、振武軍節度使，累贈太師，《唐書》有傳。 審琦性驍果，善騎射，幼以良家子事莊宗為義直軍使，遷本軍指揮使。 天成初，唐末帝由潞邸出鎮河中，奏審琦為牙兵都校，未幾，入為歸化指揮使。 王師伐蜀，充行營馬軍都指揮使，及凱旋，改龍武右廂都校，領富州刺史。 清泰初，為捧聖指揮使，領順化軍節度使。 其年鎮邢州，兼北面行營排軍陣使，從張敬達圍太原。 及楊光遠舉晉安寨降於晉祖，審琦亦預焉。 晉祖踐阼，加檢校太傅、同平章事，充天平軍節度使兼侍衛馬步軍都指揮使，旋以母喪起復。 天福三年，就加檢校太尉，尋改晉昌軍節度使、京兆尹。 七年，移鎮河中。 晉少帝嗣位，加檢校太師。 開運末，朝廷以北戎入寇，以審琦為北面行營馬軍左右廂都指揮使，與諸將會兵於洺州。 俄而敵騎大至，時皇甫遇、慕容彥超亦預其行，乃率所部兵與敵戰於安陽河上。 時遇馬為流矢所中，勢已危蹙，諸將相顧，莫有敢救者。 審琦謂首將張從恩曰：「皇甫遇等未至，必為敵騎所圍，若不急救，則為擒矣。 」從恩曰：「敵勢甚盛，無以枝梧，將軍獨往何益？ 」審琦曰：「成敗命也，若不濟，與之俱死，假令失此二將，何面目以見天子！ 」遂率鐵騎北渡。 敵見塵起，謂救兵至，乃引去。 遂救遇與彥超而還。 晉少帝嘉之，加兼侍中，移領許州，未幾，移鎮兗海。
An Shenqi, whose courtesy name was Guorui, came from Shatuo stock. His grandfather Shansheng had been garrison commander of the Shuozhou prison fort and was posthumously honored as Grand Tutor. His father Jinquan had served as Protector-General of the Pacified North and military governor of Zhenwu, was posthumously raised to Grand Preceptor, and has a biography in the Book of Tang. Shenqi was bold and decisive, an expert horseman and archer. As a youth of good family he entered Zhuangzong's service as commissioner of the Righteous and Upright Army and rose to command that corps. Early in Tiancheng, when the future Last Emperor left his princely estate at Lu to govern Hezhong, he recommended Shenqi as chief of his guard. Soon afterward Shenqi was summoned to court as commander of the Submissive and Transformed Army. During the campaign against Shu he commanded the field army's cavalry. After the victory he became colonel of the Right Dragon Martial Guards and prefect of Fuzhou. At the opening of Qingtai he became commander of the Upholding the Sage Army and military governor of Shunhua. That same year he was posted to Xingzhou as northern frontier array marshal and joined Zhang Jingda's siege of Taiyuan. When Yang Guangyuan surrendered Jin'an Stockade to Gaozu, Shenqi was among those who followed. When Gaozu took the throne, Shenqi was made acting Grand Tutor and Grand Councillor, military governor of Tianping, and commander of the palace horse and foot. He was soon recalled from mourning for his mother. In the third year of Tianfu he was promoted to acting Grand Commandant, then reassigned as military governor of Jinchang and metropolitan magistrate of Chang'an. In the seventh year he was transferred to Hezhong. When Emperor Chudi succeeded, Shenqi was made acting Grand Preceptor. Late in Kaiyun, with northern tribes raiding the frontier, the court appointed Shenqi commander of both wings of the northern field cavalry and marched with other generals to Mingzhou. Soon enemy horsemen arrived in force. Huangfu Yu and Murong Yanchao were also on the campaign, and Shenqi led his troops into battle on the Anyang River. Yu's horse was hit by a stray arrow and the situation turned desperate. The generals exchanged glances, but none dared advance to his aid. Shenqi told the chief commander Zhang Cong'en, "Huangfu Yu and the others have not yet broken through. The enemy cavalry will encircle them. If we do not rescue them now, they will be taken." Cong'en replied, "The enemy is too strong to hold off. What good can you do going alone?" Shenqi said, "Victory or defeat is heaven's decree. If we fail, we die with them. But if we lose these two generals, how could we face the emperor?" He then led his armored cavalry across the river to the north bank. Seeing the dust of his charge, the enemy thought reinforcements had arrived and withdrew. He rescued Yu and Yanchao and brought them back. Emperor Chudi praised his deed, made him concurrent Palace Attendant, and transferred him to Xuzhou; soon afterward he was posted to Yanhai.

漢有天下，授襄州節度使、兼中書令。 屬荊人叛命，潛遣舟師數千屠襄、郢，審琦禦之而遁，朝廷賞功，就加守太保，進封齊國公。 歲餘，又加守太傅。 國初，封南陽王。 顯德初，進封陳王。 世宗嗣位，加守太尉。 三年，拜章請覲，優詔許之，加守太師，增食邑至一萬五百戶，食實封二千三百戶。 審琦鎮襄、沔僅一紀，嚴而不殘，威而不暴，故南邦之民甚懷其惠。 五年，移平盧軍節度使，承詔赴鎮，因朝於京師，世宗以國之元老，禮遇甚厚，車駕親幸其第以寵之。 六年正月七日夜，為其隸人安友進、安萬合所害，時年六十三。 初，友進與審琦之愛妾私通，有年數矣。 其妾常慮事泄見誅，因與友進謀害審琦，友進甚有難色。 其妾曰：「爾若不從，我當反告。 」友進乃許之。 至是夕，審琦沈醉，寢於帳中，其妾乃取審琦所枕劍與友進，友進猶惶駭不敢剚刃，遽召其黨安萬合，便殺審琦。 既而慮事泄，乃引其帳下數妓，盡殺以滅其跡。 不數日，友進等竟敗，悉為子守忠臠而戮之。 世宗聞之震悼，輟視朝三日，詔贈尚書令，追封齊王。
When the Han dynasty held the empire, he was made military governor of Xiangzhou and concurrent Director of the Secretariat. When the Jing region rebelled, rebels secretly sent thousands of river troops to ravage Xiang and Ying. Shenqi drove them off. The court rewarded him with acting Grand Guardian and enfeoffed him as Duke of Qi. A year later he was further honored as acting Grand Tutor. At the founding of the dynasty he was enfeoffed as Prince of Nanyang. Early in Xiande he was raised to Prince of Chen. When Emperor Shizong acceded, Shenqi was made acting Grand Commandant. In the third year he memorialized requesting an audience at court, and a gracious edict granted it. He was made acting Grand Preceptor, his nominal fief was raised to ten thousand five hundred households, and his actual enfeoffment to two thousand three hundred. Shenqi governed Xiang and Mian for nearly twelve years. He was strict but not cruel, imposing but not brutal, and the southern peoples deeply cherished his benevolence. In the fifth year he was transferred to military governor of Pinglu. En route to his post he attended court, and Shizong, honoring him as a senior pillar of the state, received him with exceptional ceremony and even visited his residence in person. On the night of the seventh day of the first month in the sixth year, his retainers An Youjin and An Wanhe murdered him. He was sixty-three. Youjin had been carrying on an affair with Shenqi's favorite concubine for years. The concubine constantly feared exposure and execution, so she plotted with Youjin to kill Shenqi, though Youjin was deeply reluctant. She told him, "If you refuse, I will accuse you instead." Youjin finally agreed. That evening Shenqi lay drunk in his tent. The concubine took the sword he slept upon and handed it to Youjin, who was still too terrified to strike. He summoned his accomplice An Wanhe, and together they killed Shenqi. Fearing discovery, they then summoned several entertainers from his household and killed them all to destroy the evidence. Within days Youjin and his accomplices were captured and dismembered on the orders of Shenqi's son Shouzhong. Shizong was shaken with grief, suspended court for three days, and ordered him posthumously honored as Director of the Secretariat and enfeoffed as Prince of Qi.

守忠仕皇朝，累為郡守。
Shouzhong served the present dynasty and held several prefectural posts in succession.
8
安審暉，字明遠，審琦之兄也。 起家自長直軍使，轉外衙左廂軍使。 從莊宗平幽、薊，戰山東，定河南，皆預其功。 同光中，授蔚州刺史。 天成初，改汝州防禦副使，歷鳳翔徐州節度副使、河東行軍司馬。 晉高祖龍飛，以霸府上僚授振武兵馬留後，遷河陽節度使，不逾月移鎮鄜州，丁內艱，起復視事。 五年，李金全據安州叛，詔馬全節為都部署，領兵討之，以審暉為副。 安睦平，移鎮鄧州，進位檢校太傅。 六年冬，襄州安從進叛，舉漢南之眾北攻南陽。 南陽素無城壁，唯守衙城，賊傅城下，審暉登陴，召賊帥以讓之，從進不克而退。 襄州平，就加檢校太尉。 少帝嗣位，加檢校太師，罷鎮，授右羽林統軍。 歲餘，出鎮上黨，屬契丹內侵，授邢州節度使。 居無何，目疾暴作，上章求代，歸於京師，養疾累年。 太祖即位，召於內殿，從容顧問，尤所嘆重。 將以祿起之，審暉辭以暮齒，願就頤養。 拜太子太師致仕，封魯國公，累食邑五千戶，實封四百戶。 廣順二年春卒，年六十三。 廢朝二日，詔贈侍中，謚曰靜。
An Shenhui, whose courtesy name was Mingyuan, was Shenqi's elder brother. He entered service as commissioner of the Long Straight Army and rose to commander of the left wing of the outer guard. He followed Zhuangzong in pacifying You and Ji, fought in Shandong, and helped secure Henan, earning distinction in each campaign. During the Tongguang era he was made prefect of Weizhou. Early in Tiancheng he became deputy defense commissioner of Ruzhou, then served as deputy military governor at Fengxiang and Xuzhou and as field marshal of Hedong. When Gaozu of Later Jin took the throne, Shenhui was made acting commander of Zhenwu cavalry and infantry as a senior member of the founding staff. He was soon posted to Heyang, then within a month to Fuzhou. After mourning his mother he was recalled to duty. In the fifth year Li Jinquan rebelled and held Anzhou. Ma Quanjie was named supreme commander to suppress him, with Shenhui as his deputy. After An and Mu were pacified, he was transferred to Dengzhou and promoted to acting Grand Tutor. That winter An Congjin rebelled at Xiangzhou and marched the forces of the Han River southlands north against Nanyang. Nanyang had no outer walls and held only the administrative compound. When rebels pressed the walls, Shenhui mounted the ramparts and summoned their leader to rebuke him. Congjin failed to take the city and withdrew. After Xiangzhou was pacified, he was promoted to acting Grand Commandant. When Emperor Chudi succeeded, Shenhui was made acting Grand Preceptor, relieved of his provincial post, and appointed commander of the Right Forest Guard. A year later he was posted to Shangdang. He was posthumously honored as Palace Attendant.

宋彥筠，雍丘人也。 初隸滑州軍，梁氏與莊宗夾河之戰，彥筠時為戰棹都指揮使，以勞遷開封府牙校。 莊宗有天下，擢領禁軍。 伐蜀之役，率所部從康延孝為前鋒，蜀平，歷維、渝二州刺史。 明宗在位，連典數郡。 晉初，自汝州防禦使討安從進於襄陽，以功拜鄧州節度使，累官至檢校太尉。 未幾，歷晉、陜二鎮。 晉少帝嗣位，再領鄧州，尋移鎮河中。 漢初，授太子太師致仕。 國初，拜左衛上將軍。 世宗嗣位，復為太子太師致仕。 顯德四年冬，卒於西京之私第。 輟視朝一日，詔贈侍中。
Song Yanyun came from Yongqiu. He first served in the Huazhou army. During the Liang–Zhuangzong battles along the Yellow River he commanded war boats and was promoted to a guards officer of Kaifeng for his service. When Zhuangzong won the empire, Yanyun was promoted to command the palace guard. During the Shu campaign he led his troops as vanguard under Kang Yanxiao. After Shu fell he served in succession as prefect of Wei and Yu. During Mingzong's reign he governed several prefectures in succession. Early in the Jin dynasty, as defense commissioner of Ruzhou he campaigned against An Congjin at Xiangyang. For his merit he was made military governor of Dengzhou and rose to acting Grand Commandant. Soon afterward he served in succession at Jin and Shan. When Emperor Chudi succeeded, Yanyun again held Dengzhou, then was soon transferred to Hezhong. Early in the Han dynasty he was made Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent in retirement. At the founding of the dynasty he was made general of the Left Guard. When Shizong acceded, Yanyun again became Grand Preceptor of the Heir Apparent in retirement. He died in the winter of the fourth year of Xiande at his private residence in the Western Capital. Court was suspended for one day, and he was posthumously honored as Palace Attendant.
17
初，彥筠入成都，據一甲第，第中資貨鉅萬，妓女數十輩，盡為其所有。 一旦，與其主母微忿，遽擊殺之，自後常有所睹，彥筠心不自安，乃修浮屠法以禳之，因而溺誌於釋氏。 其後，每歲至金仙入涅之日，常衣斬缞號慟於其像前，其佞佛也如是。 家有侍婢數十人，皆令削髮披緇，以侍左右，大為當時所誚。 又性好貨殖，能圖什一之利，良田甲第，相望於郡國。 將終，以伊、洛之間田莊十數區上進，並籍於官焉。
When Yanyun entered Chengdu he seized a grand mansion stocked with wealth worth tens of thousands and dozens of entertainers, all of which became his. One day, after a minor quarrel with the lady of the house, he suddenly killed her. Thereafter he was haunted by visions and grew uneasy. He turned to Buddhist rites for exorcism and became obsessed with the faith. Every year on the anniversary of the Buddha's nirvana he donned mourning garments and wailed before the Buddha's image. Such was his fanatical devotion. He had dozens of maidservants shave their heads and don monastic robes to attend him, to the great ridicule of his contemporaries. He also had a passion for commerce and could turn a ten-percent profit. Fine estates and grand mansions dotted the provinces under his control. Near death he presented more than ten estates between the Yi and Luo to the throne, and all were registered as state property.
18
史臣曰：近代領戎藩，列王爵，祿厚而君子不議，望重而人主不疑，能自晦於飲酌之間，保功名於始終之際，如行周之比者，幾何人哉！ 奕世藩翰，固亦宜然。 審琦有分閫之勞，乏禦家之道，峰摧玉折，蓋不幸也。 其餘雖擁戎旃，未聞閫政，固不足與文、召、龔、黃為比也。
The historiographer writes: In recent times, how many men have held frontier commands and princely rank, enjoyed lavish emoluments without drawing censure, wielded great prestige without arousing suspicion, kept themselves unobtrusive amid feasting, and preserved their fame from first to last — men comparable to Xingzhou? For a family to serve generation after generation as pillars of the frontier — that is only fitting. Shenqi had the merit of frontier command but lacked the art of governing his household. That his peak was shattered and jade broken was surely misfortune. The others, though they bore military banners, left no mark in provincial governance and cannot be compared with the likes of Duke Wen of Jin, Duke Shao of Zhou, Gongye Chang, or Huang Xian.
