列傳第二十五吉翰劉道產杜驥申恬
Biography 25: Ji Han, Liu Daochan, Du Ji, and Shen Tian.
2
吉翰，字休文，馮翊池陽人也。 初為龍驤將軍道憐參軍，隨府轉征虜左軍參軍，員外散騎侍郎。 隨道憐北征廣固，賜爵建城縣五等男。 轉道憐驃騎中兵參軍，從事中郎。 為將佐十餘年，清謹剛正，甚為高祖所知賞。 永初三年，轉道憐太尉司馬。
Ji Han, whose courtesy name was Xiuwen, came from Chiyang in Fengyi commandery. He began as a staff officer under Liu Daolian, General of the Dragon Cavalry, and moved with the headquarters to become left-army staff officer for the expeditionary command and then Extraordinary Attendant-in-Ordinary of the Scattered Cavalry. He accompanied Daolian on the northern campaign against Guanggu and was ennobled as fifth-rank Baron of Jiancheng County. He was then made central army staff officer on Daolian's Rapid Cavalry staff and Attendant Gentleman. For more than ten years as a military aide he was scrupulous and upright, and Emperor Gaozu came to know and esteem him highly. In Yongchu 3 (422) he became staff officer to Daolian as Grand Marshal.
3
時太祖經略河南，以翰為持節、監司、雍、并三州諸軍事、司州刺史，將軍如故。 會前鋒諸軍到彥之等敗退，明年，復為司徒司馬，將軍如故。 其年，又假節、監徐、兗二州、豫州之梁郡諸軍事、徐州刺史，將軍如故。 時有死罪囚，典籤意欲活之，因翰入關齎呈其事。 翰省訖，語「今且去，明可便呈」。 明旦，典籤不敢復入，呼之乃來。 取昨所呈事視訖，謂之曰：「卿意當欲宥此囚死命。 昨於齋坐見其事，亦有心活之。 但此囚罪重，不可全貸，既欲加恩，卿便當代任其罪。 」因命左右收典籤付獄殺之，原此囚生命。 其刑政如此，其下畏服，莫敢犯禁。 明年卒官，時年六十。 追贈征虜將軍，持節、監、刺史如故。
While Emperor Wen was pressing his strategy south of the Yellow River, he appointed Han Bearer of the Staff, supervisor of military affairs in Si, Yong, and Bing provinces, and Inspector of Si Province, with his general's rank unchanged. After the vanguard under Dao Yanzhi and others was beaten and forced to retreat, the following year he was again made staff officer to the Grand Minister of State, with his general's rank unchanged. That same year he was also given acting Bearer of the Staff, supervision of military affairs in Xu and Yan provinces and Liang commandery in Yu Province, and the post of Inspector of Xu Province, with his general's rank unchanged. There was then a prisoner under sentence of death; the clerk in charge hoped to save him and, when Han entered the pass, had the petition brought to him. Han read it through and said, "Leave for now; you may bring it in tomorrow." The next morning the clerk did not dare come in again and appeared only when called. He took up yesterday's petition, read it through, and said to him, "You mean to obtain a pardon for this man's life." Yesterday, while seated in abstinence, I saw the petition and was myself inclined to spare him. But this man's crime is grave and cannot be wholly forgiven; since you wish to show him favor, you must bear his guilt in his stead. " He then ordered his attendants to seize the clerk, commit him to prison, and execute him, while pardoning the prisoner. Such was his way with law and punishment; his subordinates feared and obeyed him, and none dared break the rules. The following year he died in office, aged sixty. He was posthumously enfeoffed as General Who Punishes the Barbarians, with Bearer of the Staff, supervisory authority, and inspector's rank unchanged.
4
劉道產，彭城呂人，太尉諮議參軍簡之子也。 簡之事在弟子康祖傳。 道產初為輔國參軍，無錫令，在縣有能名。 高祖版為中軍行參軍，又為道憐驃騎參軍，襲父爵晉安縣五等侯。 廣州群盜因刺史謝道欣死為寇，攻沒州城，道憐加道產振武將軍南討，會始興謙之已平廣州，道產未至而反。
Liu Daochan came from Lü in Pengcheng; he was the son of Jian, who had served as advisory staff officer to the Grand Marshal. Jian's career is treated in the biography of his younger brother Kangzu. Daochan began as Staff Officer Who Supports the State and as magistrate of Wuxi, where he won a reputation for capable administration. Emperor Gaozu appointed him by patent as acting staff officer of the central army and then as staff officer on Daolian's Rapid Cavalry staff; he inherited his father's fifth-rank marquisate of Jin'an County. When the death of Inspector Xie Daoxin emboldened the bandit gangs of Guangzhou to raid and seize the provincial capital, Daolian promoted Daochan to General Who Quells the Martial and sent him south on campaign; Shizhi of Shixing had already pacified Guangzhou, however, and Daochan turned back before he arrived.
5
元年，除寧遠將軍、巴西、梓潼二郡太守。 郡人黃公生、任肅之、張石之等竝譙縱餘燼，與姻親侯攬、羅奧等招引白水氐，規欲為亂。 道產誅公生等二十一家，宥其餘黨。 還為彭城王義康驃騎中兵參軍。 元嘉三年，督梁、南秦二州諸軍事、寧遠將軍、西戎校尉、梁、南秦二州刺史。 在州有惠化，關中流民，前後出漢川歸之者甚多。 六年，道產表置隴西、宋康二郡以領之。 七年，徵為後軍將軍。 明年，遷竟陵王義宣左將軍諮議參軍，仍為持節、督雍、梁、南秦三州、荊州之南陽、竟陵、順陽、襄陽、新野、隨六郡諸軍事、寧遠將軍、寧蠻校尉、雍州刺史、襄陽太守。 善於臨民，在雍部政績尤著，蠻夷前後叛戾不受化者，竝皆順服，悉出緣沔為居。 百姓樂業，民戶豐贍，由此有《襄陽樂歌》，自道產始也。
In the first year of the reign he was appointed General of Distant Pacification and grand administrator of Baxi and Zitong commanderies. Local men Huang Gongsheng, Ren Suzhi, Zhang Shizhi, and others were remnants of Qiao Zong's revolt; with affinal kin Hou Lan, Luo Ao, and others they drew in the Baishui Di and plotted an uprising. Daochan put Gongsheng and twenty other families to death and pardoned the remaining conspirators. He then returned to serve as central army staff officer on the Rapid Cavalry staff of Prince Yikang of Pengcheng. In Yuanjia 3 (426) he was made supervisor of military affairs in Liang and Nanqin provinces, General of Distant Pacification, Colonel of the Western Rong, and inspector of both provinces. In his province he governed with benevolence, and many refugees from Guanzhong came out along the Han to submit to him. In the sixth year Daochan memorialized to establish Longxi and Songkang commanderies to take charge of them. In the seventh year he was recalled to court as General of the Rear Army. The following year he became advisory staff officer to the left general under Prince Yixuan of Jingling and was also appointed Bearer of the Staff, supervisor of military affairs in Yong, Liang, and Nanqin provinces and six Jing commanderies (Nanyang, Jingling, Shunyang, Xiangyang, Xinye, and Sui), General of Distant Pacification, Colonel Who Pacifies the Man, Inspector of Yong Province, and grand administrator of Xiangyang. He was skilled at governing the people, and his achievements in the Yong region were especially notable; tribes that had rebelled and refused assimilation all submitted and moved out to settle along the Han. The people prospered in their work and households grew wealthy; from this came the "Songs of Joy from Xiangyang," which began with Daochan.
6
十三年，進號輔國將軍。 十九年卒，追贈征虜將軍，諡曰襄侯。 道產惠澤被於西土，及喪還，諸蠻皆備衰絰，號哭追送，至於沔口。 荊州刺史衡陽王義季啟太祖曰：「故輔國將軍劉道產患背癰，疾遂不救。 道產自鎮漢南，境接凶寇，政績既著，威懷兼舉。 年時猶可，方宣其用，奄至殞沒，傷怨特深。 伏惟聖懷，愍惜兼至。 」長子延孫，別有傳。 延孫弟延熙，因延孫之廕，大明中，為司徒右長史，黃門郎，臨海、義興太守。 泰始初，與四方同反，伏誅。
In the thirteenth year his title was advanced to General Who Supports the State. He died in the nineteenth year and was posthumously enfeoffed as General Who Punishes the Barbarians, with the posthumous title Marquis Xiang. Daochan's kindness had spread through the western regions; when his coffin was borne home, the tribal peoples all donned mourning garb, wailed, and escorted him as far as the mouth of the Han. Prince Yiji of Hengyang, inspector of Jing Province, reported to Emperor Wen: "The late General Who Supports the State Liu Daochan suffered from a carbuncle on his back; the illness proved fatal." From the time he held the south of the Han, his territory bordered hostile enemies; his governance was already distinguished, and he combined firm authority with winning hearts. He was still in his prime and only beginning to show what he could do when death came suddenly; the grief and resentment are especially keen. I trust that Your Majesty's heart will feel both pity and deep regret. His eldest son Yansun has a separate biography. Yansun's younger brother Yanxi, benefiting from Yansun's hereditary privilege, in the Daming era served as right chief clerk to the Grand Minister of State, Gentleman at the Yellow Gate, and grand administrator of Linhai and Yixing. At the beginning of Taishi he joined the widespread rebellions and was put to death.
7
道產弟道錫，巴西、梓潼二郡太守。 元嘉十八年，為氐寇所攻，道錫保城退敵，太祖嘉之。 下詔曰：「前者兵寇攻逼，邊情波駭，廣威將軍、巴西、梓潼二郡太守劉道錫，將率文武，盡心固守，保全之績，厥效可書。 可冠軍。 諮議參軍、前建威將軍、晉壽太守申坦，孤城弱眾，厲志致果，死傷參半，壯氣不衰，雖力屈陷沒，在誠宜甄。 可建威將軍、巴西、梓潼二郡太守。 」初，氐寇至，城內眾寡，道錫募吏民守城，復租布二十年。 及賊退，朝議：「賊雖攻城，一戰便走，聽依本要，於事為優。 」右衛將軍沈演之、丹陽尹羊玄保、後軍長史范曄竝謂：「宜隨功勞裁量，不可全用本誓，多者不得過十年。 」從之。 二十一年，遷揚烈將軍、廣州刺史。 二十七年，坐貪縱過度，自杖治中荀齊文垂死，乘輿出城行，與阿尼同載，為有司所糾。 值赦，明年散徵。 又以赦後餘贓，收下廷尉，被宥病卒。
Daochan's younger brother Daoxi served as grand administrator of Baxi and Zitong commanderies. In Yuanjia 18 (441) he was attacked by Di raiders; Daoxi held the city and drove them off, and Emperor Wen praised him. An edict was issued: "When enemy forces pressed the attack earlier, the border was thrown into alarm. Liu Daoxi, General of Broad Prestige and grand administrator of Baxi and Zitong commanderies, led his civil and military officers in a resolute defense of the city; his success in preserving it deserves to be recorded." Let him be promoted to Champion [General]." Shen Tan, advisory staff officer and former General Who Establishes Prestige and grand administrator of Jinshou, held a lone city with few troops yet fought with fierce resolve; half his men were killed or wounded, yet his spirit never broke; though overwhelmed in the end, his loyalty deserves reward. Let him be made General Who Establishes Prestige and grand administrator of Baxi and Zitong commanderies." When the Di raiders first arrived, the garrison was weak; Daoxi recruited officials and townspeople to defend the city and promised a twenty-year remission of land tax and cloth levies. After the raiders withdrew, the court debated the matter: "Although the enemy attacked the city, they fled after a single engagement; it would be best to honor the original promise." General of the Right Guard Shen Yanzhi, governor of Danyang Yang Xuanbao, and chief clerk of the rear army Fan Ye all argued: "Relief should be scaled to actual merit; the full original pledge cannot be kept—the maximum remission should be ten years." The court adopted their view. In the twenty-first year he was transferred to General Who Displays Martial Glory and inspector of Guangzhou. In the twenty-seventh year he was impeached for gross greed and license: he had personally beaten his aide Qi Wen of Xun nearly to death, taken his carriage out of the city on pleasure outings, and ridden in the same cart with a Buddhist nun. An amnesty intervened; the following year he was dismissed from office. He was again charged with corruption remaining after the amnesty and handed over to the Minister of Justice; he was pardoned but died of illness.
8
杜驥，字度世，京兆杜陵人也。 高祖預，晉征南將軍。 曾祖耽，避難河西，因仕張氏。 苻堅平涼州，父祖始還關中。 兄坦，頗涉史傳。 高祖征長安，席捲隨從南還。 太祖元嘉中，任遇甚厚，歷後軍將軍，龍驤將軍，青、冀二州刺史，南平王鑠右將軍司馬。 晚度北人，朝廷常以傖荒遇之，雖復人才可施，每為清塗所隔，坦以此慨然。 嘗與太祖言及史籍，上曰：「金日磾忠孝淳深，漢朝莫及，恨今世無復如此輩人。 」坦曰：「日磾之美，誠如聖詔。 假使生乎今世，養馬不暇，豈辦見知。 」上變色曰：「卿何量朝廷之薄也。 」坦曰：「請以臣言之。 臣本中華高族，亡曾祖晉氏喪亂，播遷涼土，世葉相承，不殞其舊。 直以南度不早，便以荒傖賜隔。 日磾胡人，身為牧圉，便超入內侍，齒列名賢。 聖朝雖復拔才，臣恐未必能也。 」上默然。
Du Ji, whose courtesy name was Dushi, came from Duling in Jingzhao commandery. His great-grandfather Yu had served Jin as General Who Conquers the South. His great-great-grandfather Dan had fled the turmoil to Hexi and entered the service of the Zhang regime. When Fu Jian pacified Liang Province, his father and grandfather at last returned to Guanzhong. His elder brother Tan was well read in historical writings. When Emperor Gaozu campaigned against Chang'an, they swept along in his train and returned south with him. Under Emperor Wen in the Yuanjia era, Tan enjoyed exceptional favor, serving as General of the Rear Army, General of the Dragon Cavalry, inspector of Qing and Ji provinces, and staff officer to the right general under Prince Shuo of Nanping. As a northerner who had crossed south late, the court always treated him as crude and backward; though his talents could have been used, he was repeatedly shut out of the elite career track, and Tan brooded bitterly over this. Once, in conversation with Emperor Wen about historical writings, the emperor said: "Jin Midi's loyalty and filial devotion were pure and profound; the Han had no one to equal him—I regret that our age has produced no one of that kind." Tan replied: "Jin Midi's excellence is indeed as Your Majesty says." Had he lived in our day, he would have had no time for anything but tending horses—how could he ever have won notice? The emperor's expression changed. "Why do you think so little of the court?" he said. Tan said: "Let me speak from my own case." I come from a distinguished Central Plains family; my late great-great-grandfather, when Jin was torn by chaos, was driven to the Liang region, yet our line endured generation after generation without losing its standing. Simply because I crossed south late, I have been dismissed as crude and backward. Jin Midi was a foreigner who tended horses, yet he was promoted straight into the inner service and ranked among the celebrated worthies. Though our sage dynasty may again promote talent, I fear it would not be possible for a man like me. The emperor said nothing.
9
北土舊法，問疾必遣子弟。 驥年十三，父使候同郡韋華。 華子玄有高名，見而異之，以女妻焉。 桂陽公義真鎮長安，辟為州主簿，後為義真車騎行參軍，員外散騎侍郎，江夏王義恭撫軍刑獄參軍，尚書都官郎，長沙王義欣後軍錄事參軍。
By northern custom, when inquiring after someone's health one always sent a son or younger relative. When Ji was thirteen, his father sent him to call on Wei Hua of the same commandery. Wei's son Xuan enjoyed a high reputation; struck by the boy, he gave him his daughter in marriage. When Duke Yizhen of Guiyang garrisoned Chang'an, Ji was recruited as provincial chief clerk; he later served on Yizhen's chariots-and-cavalry staff, as Extraordinary Attendant-in-Ordinary of the Scattered Cavalry, as criminal affairs staff officer on Prince Yigong of Jiangxia's pacification army staff, as Gentleman of the Ministry of Justice, and as recording staff officer on Prince Yixin of Changsha's rear army staff.
10
元嘉七年，隨到彥之入河南，加建武將軍。 索虜撤河南戍悉歸河北，彥之使驥守洛陽。 洛陽城不治既久，又無糧食，及彥之敗退，驥欲棄城走，慮為太祖所誅。 初，高祖平關洛，致鐘虡舊器南還，一大鐘墜洛水。 至是太祖遣將姚聳夫領千五百人迎致之。 時聳夫政率所領牽鐘于洛水，驥乃誑之曰：「虜既南渡，洛城勢弱，今修理城池，竝已堅固，軍糧又足，所乏者人耳。 君率眾見就，共守此城，大功既立，取鐘無晚。 」聳夫信之，率所領就驥。 既至，見城不可守，又無糧食，於是引眾去。 驥亦委城南奔，白太祖曰：「本欲以死固守，姚聳夫及城便走，人情沮敗，不可復禁。 」上大怒，使建威將軍鄭順之殺聳夫於壽陽。 聳夫，吳興武康人。 勇果有氣力，宋世偏裨小將莫及。 始隨到彥之北伐，與虜遇，聳夫手斬托跋燾叔父英文特勒首，燾以馬百匹贖之。
In Yuanjia 7 (430) he followed Dao Yanzhi south of the Yellow River and was promoted to General Who Establishes Martial [Prestige]. The northern enemy withdrew all garrisons south of the Yellow River back to the north bank; Yanzhi ordered Ji to hold Luoyang. Luoyang had long been neglected and had no provisions; when Yanzhi was beaten and forced to retreat, Ji wanted to abandon the city and flee but feared execution by Emperor Wen. When Emperor Gaozu had pacified Guanzhong and Luoyang, he had the old bells and ritual vessels brought south; one great bell fell into the Luo River. Emperor Wen now sent the general Yao Songfu with fifteen hundred men to recover it. Songfu was then leading his men to haul the bell at the Luo River; Ji deceived him, saying: "The enemy has already crossed south, and Luoyang looks weak—but we have repaired the walls and they are now solid, our grain stores are full, and all we lack is manpower." Bring your men here and help us hold the city; once that great deed is done, there will be time enough to fetch the bell." Songfu believed him and led his men to Ji. When they arrived, they saw the city could not be held and there was no food, and so they led their troops away. Ji too abandoned the city and fled south, reporting to Emperor Wen: "I had intended to defend the city to the death, but when Yao Songfu arrived he fled at once; morale collapsed and could no longer be held." The emperor was furious and ordered General Who Establishes Prestige Zheng Shunzhi to execute Songfu at Shouyang. Songfu came from Wukang in Wuxing commandery. Brave, resolute, and powerful, no junior officer of the Song could match him. He had first followed Dao Yanzhi on the northern campaign; in an encounter with the enemy, Songfu personally beheaded Tuoba Tao's uncle Yingwen Tele; Tao ransomed the head with a hundred horses.
11
以驥為通直郎，射聲校尉，世祖征虜諮議參軍。 十七年，出督青、冀二州、徐州之東莞、東安二郡諸軍事、寧遠將軍、青、冀二州刺史。 在任八年，惠化著於齊土。 自義熙至於宋末，刺史唯羊穆之及驥，為吏民所稱詠。 二十四年，徵左軍將軍，兄坦代為刺史，北土以為榮焉。 坦長子琬為員外散騎侍郎，太祖嘗有函詔敕坦，琬輒開視。 信未發又追取之，敕函已發，大相推檢。 丞都答云：「諸郎開視。 」上遣主書詰責，驥答曰：「開函是臣第四子季文，伏待刑坐。 」上特原不問。 二十七年，卒，時年六十四。
Ji was appointed Direct Attendant, Colonel of the Archers Who Shoot at Sound, and advisory staff officer on Emperor Xiaowu's expeditionary staff. In the seventeenth year he was sent out to supervise military affairs in Qing and Ji provinces and Dongguan and Dong'an commanderies in Xu Province as General of Distant Pacification and inspector of Qing and Ji provinces. During eight years in office his benevolent rule was renowned throughout the Qi region. From the Yixi era to the end of Song, among provincial inspectors only Yang Muzhi and Ji were praised and celebrated by officials and commoners. In the twenty-fourth year he was recalled as General of the Left Army; his elder brother Tan replaced him as provincial inspector, and northerners took this as a mark of honor. Tan's eldest son Wan served as Extraordinary Attendant-in-Ordinary of the Scattered Cavalry; Emperor Wen once sent Tan a sealed edict, and Wan promptly opened and read it. Before the letter could be dispatched a recall was issued, but the sealed packet had already been sent, and a major investigation followed. The aides all answered: "The young masters opened and read it." The emperor sent a chief clerk to question him; Ji replied: "The one who opened the packet was my fourth son Jiwen; he awaits punishment." The emperor specially pardoned him and took no further action. In the twenty-seventh year he died, aged sixty-four.
12
長子長文，早卒。 第五子幼文，薄於行。 太宗初，以軍功為驍騎將軍，封邵陽縣男，食邑三百戶。 尋坐巧佞奪爵。 後以發太尉廬江王禕謀反事，拜黃門侍郎。 出為輔國將軍、梁、南秦二州刺史。 廢帝元徽中，為散騎常侍。 幼文所蒞貪橫，家累千金，女伎數十人，絲竹晝夜不絕，與沈勃、孫超之居止接近，常相從，又竝與阮佃夫厚善。 佃夫死，廢帝深疾之。 帝微行夜出，輒在幼文門牆之間，聽其弦管，積久轉不能平，於是自率宿衛兵誅幼文、勃、超之等。 幼文兄叔文為長水校尉，及諸子侄在京邑方鎮者竝誅。 唯幼文兄季文、弟希文等數人，逃亡得免。
His eldest son Changwen died young. His fifth son Youwen was morally deficient. At the beginning of Emperor Xiaowen's reign he was made General of Valiant Cavalry for military merit and enfeoffed as Baron of Shaoyang County with a fief of three hundred households. Soon afterward his noble rank was stripped for craftiness and flattery. Later, for exposing the rebellion plot of Grand Marshal Prince Yi of Lujiang, he was appointed Gentleman at the Yellow Gate. He was sent out as General Who Supports the State and inspector of Liang and Nanqin provinces. Under the deposed emperor in the Yuanhui era he served as Regular Attendant-in-Ordinary of the Scattered Cavalry. In every post he held Youwen was greedy and overbearing; his household amassed great wealth, he kept dozens of female performers, and music never ceased day or night; he lived near Shen Bo and Sun Chaozhi and constantly kept their company, and was also close to Ruan Tianfu. When Tianfu died, the deposed emperor came to resent them deeply. The emperor would slip out at night and linger by Youwen's gate to listen to his music; as this went on his resentment grew until at last he personally led the palace guard to execute Youwen, Bo, Chaozhi, and the others. Youwen's elder brother Shuwen, who was Colonel of the Long River, and all sons, nephews, and kin in the capital or in provincial posts were likewise executed. Only Youwen's elder brother Jiwen, younger brother Xiwen, and a few others escaped by fleeing.
13
申恬，字公休，魏郡魏人也。 曾祖鍾，為石虎司徒。 高祖平廣固，恬父宣、宣從父兄永皆得歸國，竝以幹用見知。 永歷青、兗二州刺史。 高祖踐祚，拜太中大夫。 宣，太祖元嘉初，亦歷兗、青二州刺史。 恬兄謨，與朱脩之守滑臺，為虜所沒，後得叛還。 元嘉中，為竟陵太守。
Shen Tian, whose courtesy name was Gongxiu, came from Wei in Wei commandery. His great-great-grandfather Zhong had served Shi Hu as Grand Minister of State. When Emperor Gaozu pacified Guanggu, Tian's father Xuan and Xuan's paternal cousin Yong were both able to return to the realm and were recognized for their competence. Yong served as inspector of Qing and Yan provinces. When Emperor Gaozu ascended the throne, he was appointed Grand Master of Palace Counsel. Xuan, at the beginning of Yuanjia under Emperor Wen, also served as inspector of Yan and Qing provinces. Tian's elder brother Mo had defended Huatai together with Zhu Xiuzhi, was captured by the enemy, and later defected back to the Song. In the Yuanjia era he served as grand administrator of Jingling.
14
恬初為驃騎道憐長兼行參軍。 高祖踐祚，拜東宮殿中將軍，度還臺。 直省十載，不請休息。 轉員外散騎侍郎，出為綏遠將軍、下邳太守。 轉在北海，加寧遠將軍。 所至皆有政績。 又為北譙、梁二郡太守，將軍如故。 郡境邊接任榛，屢被寇抄。 恬到，密知賊來，仍伏兵要害，出其不意，悉皆禽殄。 元嘉十二年，遷督魯、東平、濟北三郡軍事、泰山太守，將軍如故。 惠威兼著，吏民便之。 臨川王義慶鎮江陵，為平西中兵參軍、河東太守。 衡陽王義季代義慶，又度安西府，加寧朔將軍。 召拜太子屯騎校尉，母憂去職。
Tian began as acting staff officer on the long-term staff of Daolian's Rapid Cavalry command. When Emperor Gaozu ascended the throne, he was appointed Palace General of the Eastern Palace and then transferred back to the capital. He served on palace duty for ten years without ever requesting leave. He was transferred to Extraordinary Attendant-in-Ordinary of the Scattered Cavalry and sent out as General Who Pacifies the Distance and grand administrator of Xiapi. He was transferred to Beihai and promoted to General of Distant Pacification. Everywhere he served he achieved notable administrative success. He again served as grand administrator of Beiqiao and Liang commanderies, with his general's rank unchanged. The commandery bordered the territory of the outlaw Ren Zhen and was repeatedly raided. When Tian arrived he learned secretly that the raiders were coming, laid troops in ambush at a critical point, and taking them by surprise captured and destroyed them all. In Yuanjia 12 (435) he was transferred to supervise military affairs in Lu, Dongping, and Jibei commanderies as grand administrator of Taishan, with his general's rank unchanged. His benevolence and authority were both notable, and officials and commoners alike benefited from his rule. When Prince Yiqing of Linchuan garrisoned Jiangling, he served as central army staff officer of the Pacification of the West and grand administrator of Hedong. When Prince Yiji of Hengyang replaced Yiqing, he again transferred to the Pacification of the West headquarters and was promoted to General of Pacifying the North. He was summoned and appointed Colonel of the Garrison Cavalry of the Heir Apparent, then left office to observe mourning for his mother.
15
二十一年，冀州移鎮歷下，以恬督冀州、青州之濟南、樂安、太原三郡諸軍事、揚烈將軍、冀州刺史，明年，加濟南太守。 時又遷換諸郡守，恬上表曰：「伏聞朝恩當加臣濟南太守，仰惟優旨，荒心散越。 臣殃咎之餘，遭蒙逾忝，寵私罔己，復兼今授，豈其愚迷，所能上答。 臣近至止，即履行所統，究其形宜。 河、濟之間，應置戍捍，其中四處，急須修立，甕口故城，又是要所，宜移太原，委以邊事。 緣山諸邏，並得除省，防衛綏懷，利便非一。 呂綽誠效益著，深同臣意，百姓聞者，咸皆附說，急有同異，二三未宜。 但房紹之蒞郡經年，軍民粗狎，改以帶臣，有乖永事。 遠牽太原，於民為苦。 而甕口之計，復成交互，人情非樂，容有不安。 疆場威刑，患不開廣，若得依先處分，公私允緝。 」上從之。 詔有司曰：「恬所陳當是事宜，近諸除授可悉停。」
In the twenty-first year the Ji garrison was moved to Lixia; Tian was made supervisor of military affairs in Ji Province and Jinan, Le'an, and Taiyuan commanderies in Qing Province, General Who Displays Martial Glory, and inspector of Ji Province; the following year he was also made grand administrator of Jinan. At that time there was also a general rotation of commandery administrators; Tian submitted a memorial saying: "I have learned that the court intends to add the post of grand administrator of Jinan to my duties; at this gracious decree my unsettled heart is overwhelmed with gratitude." I am one who survives after calamity and fault, yet have received favor beyond my deserts; private grace has known no limit, and now this additional appointment—how could my dull wits possibly repay it? Since I recently arrived and took up my post, I have at once toured the territory under my command and examined its strategic conditions. Between the Yellow and Ji rivers defensive garrisons ought to be established; four sites urgently need to be built up, and the old city at Wengkou is again a critical point—Taiyuan commandery should be moved there and entrusted with border affairs. The various mountain outposts could all be abolished and streamlined; defense, pacification, and reassurance would gain more than one advantage. Lu Chuo's loyal effectiveness is notable and deeply accords with my view; common people who hear of it all support the proposal—only on two or three urgent points of disagreement are matters not yet suitable. But Fang Shaozhi has governed the commandery for years and army and people have grown accustomed to him; to replace him with me runs counter to stable administration. Moving Taiyuan far away would be a hardship for the people. Moreover the Wengkou plan would again create mutual complications; popular feeling would not welcome it, and unrest might follow. On the frontier, authority and punishment need room to operate; if we can follow the prior disposition, public and private interests will both be well ordered." The emperor accepted his advice. An edict to the relevant offices said: "What Tian stated is appropriate; recent appointments may all be suspended."
16
北虜入寇，恬摧擊之，為虜所破，被徴還都。 二十七年，起為通直常侍。 是歲，索虜南寇，其武昌王向青州。 遣恬援東陽，因與輔國司馬、齊郡太守龐秀之保城固守。 蕭斌遣青州別駕解榮之率垣護之還援恬等，仍傍南山得入。 賊朝來脅城，日晚輒退。 城內乃出車北門外，環塹為營，欲挑戰，賊不敢逼。 停五日，東過抄略清河郡及驛道南數千家，從東安、東莞出下邳。 下邳太守垣閬閉城距守，保全二千餘家。 虜退，以恬為寧朔將軍、山陽太守。 善於治民，所蒞有績。 世祖踐阼，遷青州刺史，將軍如故。 尋加督徐州之東莞、東安二郡諸軍事。 明年，又督冀州。 齊地連歲興兵，百姓凋弊，恬初防衛邊境，勸課農桑，二三年間，遂皆優實。 性清約，頻處州郡，妻子不免饑寒，世以此稱之。 進號輔國將軍。
When the northern enemy invaded, Tian attacked them but was defeated and was recalled to the capital. In the twenty-seventh year he was recalled to serve as Regular Attendant for Direct Communication. That year the northern enemy raided south; their Prince of Wuchang advanced on Qing Province. Tian was sent to reinforce Dongyang and, together with the pacification army staff officer and grand administrator of Qi commandery Pang Xiuzhi, held the city in stout defense. Xiao Bin sent Qing Province vice-prefect Xie Rongzhi to lead Yuan Huzhi back to relieve Tian and the others; they entered along the southern mountains. The enemy came each morning to threaten the city and each evening withdrew. The defenders then sent chariots outside the north gate, ringed the moat to form a camp, and sought battle; the enemy did not dare press close. After five days they passed east, raiding Qinghe commandery and several thousand households south of the post road, and left through Dong'an and Dongguan toward Xiapi. Grand administrator of Xiapi Yuan Lang closed the city and resisted, preserving more than two thousand households. When the enemy withdrew, Tian was made General of Pacifying the North and grand administrator of Shanyang. Skilled at governing the people, he achieved results in every post he held. When Emperor Xiaowu ascended the throne, he was transferred to inspector of Qing Province, with his general's rank unchanged. Soon he was also given supervision of military affairs in Dongguan and Dong'an commanderies in Xu Province. The following year he also supervised Ji Province. The Qi region had raised armies year after year and the people were depleted; when Tian first defended the border he urged agriculture and sericulture, and within two or three years all became prosperous and secure. By nature he was pure and frugal; though he repeatedly held provincial and commandery posts, his wife and children still knew hunger and cold, and the age praised him for this. His title was advanced to General Who Supports the State.
17
孝建二年，遷督豫州軍事、寧朔將軍、豫州刺史。 明年，疾病徵還，於道卒，時年六十九。 死之日，家無遺財。 子實，南譙郡太守，早卒。
In Xiaojian 2 (455) he was transferred to supervise military affairs in Yu Province as General of Pacifying the North and inspector of Yu Province. The following year, ill, he was recalled and died on the road, aged sixty-nine. On the day of his death his household had no property left. His son Shi served as grand administrator of Nanqiao commandery and died young.
18
謨子元嗣，海陵、廣陵太守。 元嗣弟謙，太始初，以軍功歷軍校，官至輔國將軍、臨川內史。 永子坦，自巴西、梓潼遷梁、南秦二州刺史。 元嘉二十六年，為世祖鎮軍諮議參軍，與王玄謨圍滑臺不克，免官。 青州刺史蕭斌板行建威將軍、濟南、平原二郡太守，復攻確磝，敗退，下歷城。 蕭思話起義討元兇，假坦輔國將軍，為前鋒。 世祖至新亭，坦亦進克京城。 孝建初，為太子右衛率，寧朔將軍、徐州刺史。 大明元年，虜寇兗州，世祖遣太子衛率薛安都、新除東陽太守沈法系北討，至兗州，虜已去。 坦建議：「任榛亡命，屢犯邊民，軍出無功，宜因此翦撲。 」上從之。 亡命先已聞知，舉村逃走，安都與法系坐白衣領職，坦棄市。 群臣為之請，莫能得。 將行刑，始興公沈慶之入市抱坦慟哭曰：「卿無罪，為朝廷所枉誅，我入市亦當不久。 」市官以白上，乃原生命，繫尚方。 尋被宥，復為驍騎將軍，病卒。
Mo's son Yuansi served as grand administrator of Hailing and Guangling. Yuansi's younger brother Qian, at the beginning of Taishi, rose through army command posts for military merit to General Who Supports the State and interior administrator of Linchuan. Yong's son Tan was transferred from Baxi and Zitong to inspector of Liang and Nanqin provinces. In Yuanjia 26 (449) he was advisory staff officer on Emperor Xiaowu's pacification army staff; with Wang Xuanmo he besieged Huatai without success and was dismissed from office. Inspector of Qing Province Xiao Bin appointed him by patent as acting General Who Establishes Prestige and grand administrator of Jinan and Pingyuan commanderies; he again attacked Que'ao, was defeated and withdrew, and fell back to Licheng. When Xiao Sihua raised forces to punish the usurper, Tan was given acting rank as General Who Supports the State and served as vanguard. When Emperor Xiaowu reached Xinting, Tan also advanced and captured the capital. At the beginning of Xiaojian he was right commandant of the heir apparent's guard, General of Pacifying the North, and inspector of Xu Province. In Daming 1 (457) the enemy raided Yan Province; Emperor Xiaowu sent the heir apparent's guard commandant Xue Andu and the newly appointed grand administrator of Dongyang Shen Faxi on a northern campaign; when they reached Yan Province the enemy had already gone. Tan proposed: "The outlaw Ren Zhen has repeatedly raided border peoples; the campaign achieved nothing—we ought to take this opportunity to destroy him." The emperor accepted his proposal. The outlaw had already heard in advance; whole villages fled. Andu and Faxi were punished by serving in plain clothes while retaining office; Tan was executed in the marketplace. The ministers all pleaded for him but could not save him. As the execution was about to be carried out, Duke Qingzhi of Shixing entered the marketplace, embraced Tan, and wept aloud: "You are innocent and are being wrongly put to death by the court; I too will not be long in joining you here." The marketplace officer reported this to the emperor, who then spared Tan's life and imprisoned him in the Imperial Workshop. Soon he was pardoned, again made General of Valiant Cavalry, and died of illness.
19
子令孫，前廢帝景和中，為永嘉王子仁左軍司馬、廣陵太守。 太宗以為寧朔將軍、徐州刺史，討薛安都。 行至淮陽，即與安都合。 弟闡，時為濟陰太守，戍睢陵城，奉順不同安都，安都攻圍不能克。 會令孫至，遣往睢陵令說闡降，闡既降，殺之，令孫亦見殺。 先是，清河崔諲亦以將吏見知高祖，永初末，為振威將軍、東萊太守。 少帝初，亡命司馬靈期、司馬順之千餘人圍東萊，諲擊之，斬靈期等三十級。 太祖元嘉中，至青州刺史。
His son Lingsun, in the Jinghe era under the deposed emperor, was left army staff officer to Prince Ziren of Yongjia and grand administrator of Guangling. Emperor Xiaowen made him General of Pacifying the North and inspector of Xu Province to campaign against Xue Andu. When he reached Huaiyang he at once joined Andu. His younger brother Chan was then grand administrator of Jiyin, garrisoning Suiling city; he remained loyal to the court and would not follow Andu, and Andu besieged him without being able to take the city. When Lingsun arrived he sent someone to Suiling to persuade Chan to surrender; once Chan had surrendered he killed him, and Lingsun was also killed. Earlier, Cui Yin of Qinghe was also known to Emperor Gaozu as a military officer; at the end of Yongchu he was General Who Displays Martial Prestige and grand administrator of Donglai. At the beginning of the brief reign, more than a thousand outlaws under Sima Lingqi and Sima Shunzhi besieged Donglai; Yin attacked them and beheaded Lingqi and thirty others. In the Yuanjia era under Emperor Wen he rose to inspector of Qing Province.
20
史臣曰：漢之良吏，居官者或長子孫，孫、曹之世，善職者亦二三十載，皆敷政以盡民和，興讓以存簡久。 及晚代風烈漸衰，非才有起伏，蓋所遭之時異也。 劉道產之在漢南，歷年逾十，惠化流於樊沔，頗有前世遺風，故能樹績垂名，斯為美矣！
The historian says: Among the good officials of Han, some held office long enough to see their sons and grandsons grow up; in the ages of Sun and Cao, capable administrators also served twenty or thirty years, all spreading good governance to achieve popular harmony and promoting yielding to preserve simplicity and endurance. By the late age moral force gradually declined; it was not that talent rose and fell, but that the times men encountered differed. Liu Daochan held the south of the Han for more than ten years; his benevolent rule spread through Fan and Mian, bearing much of the style of earlier ages—thus he could establish lasting achievement and leave a celebrated name. This is truly admirable!
