1
前廢帝諱子業，小字法師，孝武帝長子也。 元嘉二十六年正月甲申生。 世祖鎮尋陽，子業留京邑。 三十年，世祖入伐元凶，被囚侍中下省，將見害者數矣，卒得無恙。
Emperor Fei I was named Ziye; his childhood name was Fashi. He was the eldest son of Emperor Xiao Wu. He was born on jiashen day in the first month of the twenty-sixth year of Yuanjia. While Shizu held Xunyang, Ziye stayed behind in the capital. In the thirtieth year, when Shizu marched in to attack the Original Culprit, Ziye was held prisoner in the attendants' lower bureau; he was nearly killed more than once, but in the end escaped unharmed.
2
世祖踐阼，立為皇太子。 始未之東宮，中庶子、二率並入直永福省。 大明二年，出居東宮。 [1]四年，講孝經於崇正殿。 七年，加元服。
When Shizu took the throne, Ziye was made crown prince. Before he moved to the Eastern Palace, his palace tutors and the two commandants of the guard all served on duty at the Yongfu Bureau. In the second year of Daming he took up residence in the Eastern Palace. [1] In the fourth year he lectured on the Classic of Filial Piety in the Chongzheng Hall. In the seventh year he underwent the capping ceremony.
3
八年閏五月庚申，世祖崩，其日，太子即皇帝位。 大赦天下。 太宰江夏王義恭解尚書令，加中書監，驃騎大將軍柳元景加尚書令。 甲子，置錄尚書，太宰江夏王義恭錄尚書事。 驃騎大將軍柳元景加開府儀同三司。 丹陽尹永嘉王子仁為南豫州刺史。 [2]
In the intercalary fifth month of the eighth year, on gengshen day, Shizu died; that same day the crown prince ascended the throne. A general amnesty was proclaimed throughout the realm. Grand Tutor Prince of Jiangxia Yigong relinquished the directorship of the Masters of Writing and was made supervisor of the secretariat; General of Agile Cavalry Liu Yuanjing was appointed director of the Masters of Writing. On jiazi day the office of recorder of the Masters of Writing was established, and Grand Tutor Prince of Jiangxia Yigong was appointed to it. General of Agile Cavalry Liu Yuanjing was granted the additional rank of bearer of the staff with equal protocol to the Three Ducal Ministers. Prince of Zinan Yongjia was appointed inspector of Southern Yuzhou. [2] Editorial note two stands in the received text at this point in the narrative.
4
六月辛未，詔曰：「朕以眇身，夙紹洪業，敬御天威，欽對靈命。 仰遵凝緒，日鑒前圖，實可以拱默守成，詒風長世。 而寶位告始，萬宇改屬，惟德弗明，昧于大道。 思宣睿範，引茲簡恤，可具詢執事，詳訪民隱。 凡曲令密文，繁而傷治，[3]關市僦稅，事施一時，而姦吏舞文，妄興威福，加以氣緯舛互，[4]偏頗滋甚。 宜其寬傜輕憲，以救民切。 御府諸署，事不須廣，雕文篆刻，無施於今。 悉宜并省，以酬氓願。 藩王貿貨，壹皆禁斷。 外便具條以聞。 」戊寅，以豫州之淮南郡復為南梁郡，復分宣城還置淮南郡。 庚辰，以南海太守袁曇遠為廣州刺史。
On xinwei day in the sixth month an edict declared: "With my slight person I have long inherited the great enterprise, reverently bearing Heaven's majesty and respectfully answering the numinous mandate. Looking up, I follow the settled tradition; day by day I take the former models as my mirror—truly one might fold one's hands in silence and guard what has been achieved, transmitting the wind of virtue to long ages. Yet as the precious throne is only now announced and the myriad realms change their allegiance, my virtue is not yet clear and I am obscure to the Great Way. I wish to proclaim enlightened standards and extend this simplified compassion; let all responsible officials be consulted and the people's hardships investigated in detail. All circuitous ordinances and secret statutes, elaborate and harmful to governance, [3] market tolls and lease taxes imposed for a single occasion—yet corrupt clerks twist the written law and presumptuously wield authority and favor; added to this, the qi-lines and calendars are at odds, [4] and partiality grows ever worse. Corvée and penal statutes should be relaxed and lightened to relieve the people's urgent distress. The various offices of the imperial household need not be extensive; carved inscriptions and seal-cut ornament have no place in the present age. All alike should be merged and reduced, to answer the common people's wishes. Princes of the frontier trading in goods are one and all forbidden. Those beyond the capital should draw up detailed lists and report. " On wuyin day the Huainan commandery in Yuzhou was restored as the Nanliang commandery, and Xuancheng was again separated out to re-establish Huainan commandery. On gengchen day Yuan Tanyuan, administrator of Nanhai, was appointed inspector of Guangzhou.
5
秋七月己亥，鎮軍將軍、雍州刺史晉安王子勛改為江州刺史，中護軍宗慤為安西將軍、雍州刺史，鎮北將軍、徐州刺史湘東王彧為護軍將軍，中軍將軍義陽王昶為征北將軍、徐州刺史。 庚戌，婆皇國遣使獻方物。 崇皇太后曰太皇太后，皇后曰皇太后。 乙卯，罷南北二馳道。 孝建以來所改制度，還依元嘉。 丙辰，追崇獻妃為獻皇后。 乙丑，撫軍將軍、南徐州刺史新安王子鸞解領司徒。
On jihai day in the seventh month of autumn, General Who Pacifies the Army and inspector of Yongzhou Prince of Jin'an Zixun was transferred to inspector of Jiangzhou; Central Army Guard Zong Que was made general who pacifies the west and inspector of Yongzhou; General Who Pacifies the North and inspector of Xuzhou Prince of Xiangdong Yu was made general of the guards; General of the Central Army Prince of Yiyang Chang was made general who campaigns north and inspector of Xuzhou. On gengxu day the state of Pohuang sent envoys presenting local products. The Honored Empress Dowager was styled Grand Empress Dowager; the empress was styled Empress Dowager. On yimao day the northern and southern imperial speedways were abolished. Institutions changed since the Xiaojian era were restored to follow the Yuanjia pattern. On bingchen day the Honored Consort Xian was posthumously honored as Empress Xian. On yichou day General Who Pacifies the Army and inspector of Southern Xuzhou Prince of Xin'an Ziluan was relieved of his concurrent post as minister of works.
6
八月丁卯，領軍將軍王玄謨為鎮北將軍、青冀二州刺史。 [5]己巳，以青、冀二州刺史蕭惠開為益州刺史。 己丑，[6]皇太后崩。 京師雨水。 庚寅，[7]遣御史與官長隨宜賑卹。
In the eighth month, on dingmao day, General of the Guards Wang Xuamo was made general who pacifies the north and inspector of Qing and Ji provinces. [5] On jisi day Xiao Hui'ai, inspector of Qing and Ji provinces, was appointed inspector of Yizhou. On jichou day, [6] the Empress Dowager died. In the capital region there was flooding from rain. On gengyin day, [7] censors were dispatched together with office heads to give relief as circumstances required.
7
九月辛丑，護軍將軍湘東王彧為領軍將軍。 癸卯，以尚書左僕射劉遵考為特進、右光祿大夫。 乙卯，文穆皇后祔葬景寧陵。
In the ninth month, on xinchou day, General of the Guards Prince of Xiangdong Yu was made general of the guards. On guimao day Left Vice-Director of the Masters of Writing Liu Zunkao was made special grand master and right household master. On yimao day the Wenmu Empress was enshrined at the Jingning Mausoleum.
8
冬十月甲戌，太常建安王休仁為護軍將軍。 戊寅，輔國將軍宗越為司州刺史。 [8]庚辰，原除揚、南徐州大明七年逋租。
In the tenth month of winter, on jiaxu day, Minister of Ceremonies Prince of Jian'an Xiuren was made general of the guards. On wuyin day General Who Supports the State Zong Yue was appointed inspector of Sizhou. [8] On gengchen day overdue rents from the seventh year of Daming in Yang and Southern Xuzhou were remitted.
9
十二月乙酉，以尚書右僕射顏師伯為尚書僕射。 [9]壬辰，以王畿諸郡為揚州，以揚州為東揚州。 癸巳，以車騎將軍、揚州刺史豫章王子尚為司徒、揚州刺史。
In the twelfth month, on yiyou day, Right Vice-Director of the Masters of Writing Yan Shibo was made vice-director of the Masters of Writing. [9] On renchen day the commanderies within the imperial domain were made Yangzhou, and Yangzhou was made Eastern Yangzhou. On guisi day General of Chariots and Cavalry and inspector of Yangzhou Prince of Yuzhang Zishang was made minister of works and inspector of Yangzhou.
10
去歲及是歲，東諸郡大旱，甚者米一升數百，京邑亦至百餘，餓死者十有六七。 孝建以來，又立錢署鑄錢，百姓因此盜鑄，錢轉偽小，商貨不行。
In the previous year and in this year the eastern commanderies suffered great drought; in the worst places a sheng of grain cost several hundred, and in the capital as well it reached over a hundred; of those who starved to death, six or seven out of ten. Since the Xiaojian era mint offices had also been established to cast coin; because of this the common people illicitly cast as well, and the money grew ever more counterfeit and small, so that commerce would not circulate.
11
永光元年春正月乙未朔，改元。 大赦天下。 乙巳，省諸州臺傳。 戊午，以領軍將軍湘東王彧為衞將軍、南豫州刺史，護軍將軍建安王休仁為領軍將軍，祕書監山陽王休祐為豫州刺史，左衞將軍桂陽王休範為中護軍，南豫州刺史尋陽王子房為東揚州刺史。
On yiwei day, the first day of the first month of spring in the first year of Yongguang, the era name was changed. A general amnesty was proclaimed throughout the realm. On yisi day the relay stations of the various provinces were abolished. On wuwu day General of the Guards Prince of Xiangdong Yu was made general of the guard and inspector of Southern Yuzhou; General of the Guards Prince of Jian'an Xiuren was made general of the guards; Supervisor of the Secretariat Prince of Shanyang Xiuyou was appointed inspector of Yuzhou; Left Guard General Prince of Guiyang Xiufan was made central army guard; Inspector of Southern Yuzhou Prince of Xunyang Zifang was appointed inspector of Eastern Yangzhou.
12
二月乙丑，減州郡縣田祿之半。 [10]庚寅，鑄二銖錢。
On yichou day in the second month the field stipends of provinces, commanderies, and counties were reduced by half. [10] On gengyin day two-zhu coin was cast.
13
三月甲辰，罷臨江郡。
On jiachen day in the third month Linjiang commandery was abolished.
14
五月己亥，割郢州隨郡屬雍州。 丙午，以後軍司馬張牧為交州刺史。
On jihai day in the fifth month Suo commandery in Yingzhou was detached and placed under Yongzhou. On bingwu day Zhang Mu, former marshal of the rear army, was appointed inspector of Jiaozhou.
15
六月己巳，左軍長史劉道隆為梁、南秦二州刺史。 乙亥，安西將軍、雍州刺史宗慤卒。 壬午，衞將軍、南豫州刺史湘東王彧改為雍州刺史。 [11]尚書令、驃騎大將軍柳元景加南豫州刺史。
On jisi day in the sixth month Liu Daolong, chief clerk of the left army, was appointed inspector of Liang and Southern Qin provinces. On yihai day General Who Pacifies the West and inspector of Yongzhou Zong Que died. On renwu day General of the Guard and inspector of Southern Yuzhou Prince of Xiangdong Yu was transferred to inspector of Yongzhou. [11] Director of the Masters of Writing and General of Agile Cavalry Liu Yuanjing was given the additional post of inspector of Southern Yuzhou.
16
秋八月辛酉，越騎校尉戴法興有罪，賜死。 庚午，以尚書僕射顏師伯為尚書左僕射，[12]吏部尚書王景文為尚書右僕射。 癸酉，帝自率宿衞兵，誅太宰江夏王義恭、尚書令驃騎大將軍柳元景、尚書左僕射顏師伯、[13]廷尉劉德願。 改元為景和元年。 文武賜位二等。 以領軍將軍建安王休仁為安西將軍、雍州刺史，衞將軍湘東王彧還為南豫州刺史。 甲戌，司徒、揚州刺史豫章王子尚領尚書令，射聲校尉沈文秀為青州刺史，左軍司馬崔道固為冀州刺史。 乙亥，詔曰：「昔凝神佇逸，磻溪讚道，湛慮思才，傅巖毗化。 朕位御三極，風澄萬宇，資鈇電斷，正卯斯戮。 思所以仰宣遺烈，俯弘景祚，每結夢庖鼎，瞻言板築，有劬日昃，無忘昧旦。 可甄訪郡國，招聘閭部：其有孝性忠節，幽居遯棲，信誠義行，廉正表俗，文敏博識，幹事治民，務加旌舉，隨才引擢。 庶官克順，彝倫咸叙。 主者精加詳括，稱朕意焉。 」以始興公沈慶之為太尉，鎮北將軍、青冀二州刺史王玄謨為領軍將軍。 庚辰，以石頭城為長樂宮，東府城為未央宮。 罷東揚州并揚州。 甲申，以北邸為建章宮，南第為長楊宮。 以冠軍將軍邵陵王子元為湘州刺史。 丙戌，原除吳、吳興、義興、晉陵、琅邪五郡大明八年以前逋租。 己丑，復立南北二馳道。
In the eighth month of autumn, on xinyou day, Commandant of Upright Cavalry Dai Faxiang was guilty of a crime and was granted death. On gengwu day Vice-Director of the Masters of Writing Yan Shibo was made left vice-director of the Masters of Writing; [12] Minister of Personnel Wang Jingwen was made right vice-director of the Masters of Writing. On guiyou day the emperor himself led the palace guards and killed Grand Tutor Prince of Jiangxia Yigong, Director of the Masters of Writing and General of Agile Cavalry Liu Yuanjing, Left Vice-Director of the Masters of Writing Yan Shibo, [13] and Minister of Justice Liu Deyuan. The era name was changed to the first year of Jinghe. Civil and military officials were granted two ranks of advancement. General of the Guards Prince of Jian'an Xiuren was made general who pacifies the west and inspector of Yongzhou; General of the Guard Prince of Xiangdong Yu was returned to inspector of Southern Yuzhou. On jiaxu day Minister of Works and inspector of Yangzhou Prince of Yuzhang Zishang took charge of the Masters of Writing; Commandant of Archers Shen Wenxiu was appointed inspector of Qingzhou; Left Army Marshal Cui Daogu was appointed inspector of Jizhou. On yihai day an edict declared: "In antiquity, concentrating the spirit and waiting in ease, Panxi praised the Way; steeping thought and pondering talent, Fuyan assisted in transformation. I occupy the three extremities, the wind is clear throughout the myriad realms; with the axe and lightning of judgment I sever, and at the hour of zhengmao I execute. I think how to look up and proclaim the legacy left behind, and look down to extend the splendor of the age—night after night I dream of the cauldron in the kitchen, and when I gaze I speak of building with planks; from sun to sun I toil, and I do not forget the hour before dawn. Let the commanderies and kingdoms be searched and the hamlets and districts recruited: those who have filial conduct and loyal integrity, dwell in seclusion and hide their traces, have faith and sincerity and righteous conduct, are incorrupt and upright and set an example for custom, are refined in letters and broadly learned, or are capable in affairs and govern the people—all should be especially commended and promoted according to talent. Thus officials will fulfill their duties and the constant norms will all be ordered. The responsible offices should examine this carefully in detail and report in accord with my intent. " Shen Qingzhi of Shixing was made grand marshal; General Who Pacifies the North and inspector of Qing and Ji provinces Wang Xuamo was made general of the guards. On gengchen day Stone City was made the Changle Palace and the Eastern Mansion was made the Weiyang Palace. Eastern Yangzhou was abolished and merged into Yangzhou. On jiashen day the northern residence was made the Jianzhang Palace and the southern mansion was made the Zhangyang Palace. General Who Conquers the Champions Prince of Shaoling Ziyuan was appointed inspector of Xiangzhou. On bingxu day overdue rents before the eighth year of Daming in the five commanderies of Wu, Wuxing, Yixing, Jinling, and Langye were remitted. On jichou day the northern and southern imperial speedways were re-established.
17
九月癸巳，車駕幸湖熟，奏鼓吹。 戊戌，車駕還宮。 庚子，以南兗州刺史永嘉王子仁為南徐州刺史，丹陽尹始安王子真為南兗州刺史。 辛丑，撫軍將軍、南徐州刺史新安王子鸞免為庶人，賜死。 丙午，以兗州刺史薛安都為平北將軍、徐州刺史。 丁未，衞將軍湘東王彧加開府儀同三司，特進、右光祿大夫劉遵考為安西將軍、南豫州刺史，寧朔將軍殷孝祖為兗州刺史。 戊申，以前梁、南秦二州刺史柳元怙復為梁、南秦二州刺史。 己酉，車駕討征北將軍、徐州刺史義陽王昶，內外戒嚴。 昶奔于索虜。 辛亥，右將軍、豫州刺史山陽王休祐進號鎮西大將軍。 甲寅，以安西長史袁顗為雍州刺史。 戊午，以左民尚書劉思考為益州刺史。 是日解嚴，車駕幸瓜步。 開百姓鑄錢。
In the ninth month, on guisi day, the imperial carriage visited Hushu and martial music was performed. On wuxu day the imperial carriage returned to the palace. On gengzi day Inspector of Southern Yanzhou Prince of Yongjia Ziren was made inspector of Southern Xuzhou; Administrator of Danyang Prince of Shi'an Zizhen was appointed inspector of Southern Yanzhou. On xinchou day General Who Pacifies the Army and inspector of Southern Xuzhou Prince of Xin'an Ziluan was demoted to commoner and granted death. On bingwu day Xue Andu, inspector of Yanzhou, was made general who pacifies the north and inspector of Xuzhou. On dingwei day General of the Guard Prince of Xiangdong Yu was granted the additional rank of bearer of the staff with equal protocol to the Three Ducal Ministers; Special Grand Master and Right Household Master Liu Zunkao was made general who pacifies the west and inspector of Southern Yuzhou; General Who Pacifies the North Yin Xiaozu was appointed inspector of Yanzhou. On wushen day Liu Yuanhu, former inspector of Liang and Southern Qin provinces, was restored as inspector of Liang and Southern Qin provinces. On jiyou day the imperial carriage campaigned against General Who Campaigns North and inspector of Xuzhou Prince of Yiyang Chang; within and without the court martial law was proclaimed. Chang fled to the northern barbarians. On xinhai day General of the Right and inspector of Yuzhou Prince of Shanyang Xiuyou was advanced in rank to general who pacifies the west. On jiayin day Yuan Yan, chief clerk of the pacifying west army, was appointed inspector of Yongzhou. On wuwu day Minister of the Left Liu Kaokao was appointed inspector of Yizhou. That same day martial law was lifted; the imperial carriage visited Guabu. The common people were permitted to cast coin.
18
冬十月癸亥，曲赦徐州。 丙寅，車駕還宮。 以建安王休仁為護軍將軍。 己卯，東陽太守王藻下獄死。 以宮人謝貴嬪為夫人，加虎賁靸戟，鸞輅龍旂，出警入蹕，實新蔡公主也。 乙酉，以鎮西大將軍、豫州刺史山陽王休祐為鎮軍大將軍、開府儀同三司。 [14]
In the tenth month of winter, on guihai day, Xuzhou was given a partial amnesty. On bingyin day the imperial carriage returned to the palace. Prince of Jian'an Xiuren was made general of the guards. On jimao day Wang Zao, administrator of Dongyang, was imprisoned and died. The palace woman Xie Guibin was made lady, with tiger guards bearing halberds, imperial carriage and dragon banners, and imperial escort on going out and returning—in fact she was Princess of Xincai. On yiyou day General Who Pacifies the West and inspector of Yuzhou Prince of Shanyang Xiuyou was made general who pacifies the army and bearer of the staff with equal protocol to the Three Ducal Ministers. [14] Editorial note fourteen stands in the received text at this point in the narrative.
19
十一月壬辰，寧朔將軍何邁下獄死。 新除太尉沈慶之薨。 壬寅，立皇后路氏，四廂奏樂。 赦揚、南徐二州。 護軍將軍建安王休仁加特進、左光祿大夫。 中護軍桂陽王休範遷職。 丁未，皇子生，少府劉勝之子也。 [15]大赦天下。 贓汙淫盜，悉皆原除。 賜為父後者爵一級。 壬子，以特進、左光祿大夫、護軍將軍建安王休仁為驃騎大將軍、開府儀同三司。 戊午，南平王敬猷、廬陵王敬先、安南侯敬淵並賜死。
In the eleventh month, on renchen day, General Who Pacifies the North He Mai was imprisoned and died. The newly appointed grand marshal Shen Qingzhi died. On renyin day Lady Lu was installed as empress; music was performed in all four side chambers. Yang and Southern Xu provinces were granted amnesty. General of the Guards Prince of Jian'an Xiuren was given the additional ranks of special grand master and left household master. Central Army Guard Prince of Guiyang Xiufan was transferred in office. On dingwei day a prince was born—the son of Liu Sheng of the palace treasury. [15] A general amnesty was proclaimed throughout the realm. Corruption, licentiousness, and theft were all alike remitted. Those who had succeeded their fathers were granted one rank of nobility. On renzi day Special Grand Master, Left Household Master, and General of the Guards Prince of Jian'an Xiuren was made general of agile cavalry and bearer of the staff with equal protocol to the Three Ducal Ministers. On wuwu day Prince of Nanping Jingyou, Prince of Luling Jingxian, and Marquis of Annan Jingyuan were all granted death.
20
時帝凶悖日甚，誅殺相繼，內外百司，不保首領。 先是訛言云：「湘中出天子。 」帝將南巡荊、湘二州以厭之。 先欲誅諸叔，然後發引。 太宗與左右阮佃夫、王道隆、李道兒密結帝左右壽寂之、姜產之等十一人，謀共廢帝。 戊午夜，帝於華林園竹林堂射鬼。 時巫覡云：「此堂有鬼。 」故帝自射之。 壽寂之懷刀直入，姜產之為副。 帝欲走，寂之追而殞之。 時年十七。 太皇太后令曰：
At this time the emperor's ferocity and perversity grew daily worse; executions followed one after another, and within and without the hundred offices none could keep their heads. Earlier a false rumor had said: "From within Xiang a Son of Heaven will arise. The emperor intended to tour the two provinces of Jing and Xiang to quell it. He wished first to execute his uncles, and only then set out on the journey. Taizong secretly joined with his attendants Ruan Tianfu, Wang Daolong, and Li Dao'er to ally with the emperor's attendants Shou Jizhi, Jiang Chan'zhi, and eleven others in all, plotting together to depose the emperor. At midnight on wuwu day the emperor was at the Bamboo Grove Hall in the Hualin Garden shooting at ghosts. At the time the shamans had said: "This hall has ghosts. Therefore the emperor shot at them himself. Shou Jizhi entered straight in with a knife concealed on his person; Jiang Chan'zhi was his second. The emperor tried to flee; Jizhi pursued and killed him. He was seventeen years old. The Grand Empress Dowager issued an order, saying:
21
司徒領護軍八座：子業雖曰嫡長，少稟凶毒，不仁不孝，著自髫齔。 孝武棄世，屬當辰曆。 自梓宮在殯，喜容靦然，天罰重離，歡恣滋甚。 逼以內外維持，忍虐未露，而凶慘難抑，一旦肆禍，遂縱戮上宰，殄害輔臣。 子鸞兄弟，先帝鍾愛，含怨既往，枉加屠酷。 昶茂親作扞，橫相徵討。 新蔡公主逼離夫族，幽置深宮，詭云薨殞。 襄事甫爾，喪禮頓釋，昏酣長夜，庶事傾遺。 朝賢舊勳，棄若遺土。 管絃不輟，珍羞備膳。 詈辱祖考，以為戲謔。 行游莫止，淫縱無度。 肆宴園陵，規圖發掘。 誅剪無辜，籍略婦女。 建樹偽豎，莫知誰息。 拜嬪立后，慶過恒典。 宗室密戚，遇若婢僕，鞭捶陵曳，無復尊卑。 南平一門，特鍾其酷。 反天滅理，顯暴萬端。 苛罰酷令，終無紀極，夏桀、殷辛，未足以譬。 闔朝業業，人不自保，百姓遑遑，手足靡厝。 行穢禽獸，罪盈三千。 高祖之業將泯，七廟之享幾絕。 吾老疾沈篤，每規禍鴆，憂煎漏刻，氣命無幾。 開闢以降，所未嘗聞。 遠近思奮，十室而九。
To the minister of works, who concurrently commands the guards, and the Eight Dignitaries: Although Ziye is called the eldest son of the proper wife, from youth he received a fierce and poisonous nature; lack of benevolence and lack of filial piety were evident from his earliest years. When Emperor Xiao Wu abandoned the world, the succession fell to him by the calendar of the age. From the time the imperial coffin was in mourning, his pleased countenance was clear and bright; Heaven's punishment weighed upon him in separation, yet his wanton joy grew ever greater. Compelled by the need to maintain appearances within and without, his cruel tyranny was not yet exposed; but his savage wickedness could not be restrained, and in a single day he unleashed disaster—then he wantonly slaughtered the chief ministers and annihilated assisting officials. The brothers Ziluan and the rest were beloved of the late emperor; he bore resentment for the past and wrongly added cruel slaughter. Chang, a close kinsman, served as a shield, yet he was summarily attacked and punished. The Princess of Xincai was forced away from her husband's clan and secretly placed in the deep palace; it was falsely announced that she had died. The mourning rites had scarcely begun when he abruptly cast them off; through long nights of drunken stupor he neglected all affairs. Court worthies of old merit he cast aside as if they were discarded soil. Pipes and strings never ceased; rare delicacies filled his table. He reviled and insulted his ancestors and forefathers, taking it all as jest. His roaming knew no stop; his lewdness and excess had no measure. He held feasts in the imperial parks and mausoleums and plotted to excavate them. He executed and cut down the innocent and seized women by force. He raised false heirs, and none knew whose line would survive. In enfeoffing consorts and installing empresses, his celebrations exceeded the constant statutes. Clansmen and close kin he treated like maidservants and slaves; with whipping and dragging he trampled them, and no distinction of high and low remained. The house of Nanping alone received his cruelty in special measure. He turned against Heaven and extinguished principle; his manifest violence had ten thousand faces. Harsh punishments and cruel ordinances knew no end or limit; Xia Jie and Yin Xin are not enough for comparison. Throughout the court, each man in his occupation could not keep himself safe; the common people were in alarm, and their hands and feet had no place to rest. His conduct was filthy as a beast's; his crimes filled three thousand. The enterprise of the High Ancestor was nearly extinguished; the sacrifices of the seven temples were almost cut off. My old illness is deep and grave; I have often foreseen this poisoned calamity; worry and torment drip with each quarter-hour, and my breath and life will not long endure. Since the opening of the age, such a thing has never been heard of. Near and far yearn to rise up; nine houses in ten.
22
衞將軍湘東王體自太祖，天縱英聖，文皇鍾愛，寵冠列藩。 吾早識神睿，特兼常禮。 潛運宏規，義士投袂，獨夫既殞，懸首白旗，社稷再興，宗祏永固，人鬼屬心，大命允集。 且勳德高邈，大業攸歸，宜遵漢、晉，纂承皇極。 主者詳舊典以時奉行。
General of the Guard Prince of Xiangdong Yu is by origin from the Great Ancestor; Heaven endowed him with heroic sagacity; Emperor Wen cherished him, and his favor surpassed the other princes. I early recognized his divine perspicacity and treated him with special courtesy beyond the ordinary. He secretly carried out a great design; men of righteousness threw down their sleeves; the lone tyrant was already slain and his head hung from the white banner; the altars of state were renewed and the ancestral temple secured forever; men and ghosts turned their hearts to him, and the great mandate was fully gathered. Moreover his merit and virtue are lofty and far-reaching; the great enterprise is properly his to receive. He should follow Han and Jin and succeed to the imperial apex. The responsible offices should examine the old statutes and carry them out in timely fashion.
23
未亡人餘年不幸嬰此百艱，永尋情事，雖存若殞。 當復奈何！ 當復奈何！
I, who have not yet perished, have unluckily encountered this hundredfold hardship; forever tracing the circumstances in my mind, though I live it is as if I were dead. What is to be done! What is to be done!
24
葬廢帝丹陽秣陵縣南郊壇西。
The Deposed Emperor was buried west of the southern suburban altar in Moling county, Danyang.
25
帝幼而狷急，在東宮每為世祖所責。 世祖西巡，子業啟參承起居，書迹不謹，上詰讓之。 子業啟事陳謝，上又答曰：「書不長進，此是一條耳。 聞汝素都懈怠，狷戾日甚，何以頑固乃爾邪！ 」初踐阼，受璽紱，悖然無哀容。 始猶難諸大臣及戴法興等，既殺法興，諸大臣莫不震懾。 於是又誅羣公。 元凱以下，皆被毆捶牽曳。 內外危懼，殿省騷然。 初太后疾篤，遣呼帝。 帝曰：「病人間多鬼，可畏，那可往。 」太后怒，語侍者：「將刀來，破我腹，那得生如此寧馨兒！ 」及太后崩後數日，帝夢太后謂之曰：「汝不孝不仁，本無人君之相。 子尚愚悖如此，亦非運祚所及。 孝武險虐滅道，怨結人神，兒子雖多，並無天命。 大運所歸，應還文帝之子。 」其後湘東王紹位，果文帝子也。 故帝聚諸叔京邑，慮在外為患。 山陰公主淫恣過度，謂帝曰：「妾與陛下，雖男女有殊，俱託體先帝。 陛下六宮萬數，而妾唯駙馬一人。 事不均平，一何至此！ 」帝乃為主置面首左右三十人； 進爵會稽郡長公主，秩同郡王，食湯沐邑二千戶，[16]給鼓吹一部，加班劍二十人。 帝每出，與朝臣常共陪輦。 主以吏部郎褚淵貌美，就帝請以自侍，帝許之。 淵侍主十日，備見逼迫，誓死不回，遂得免。 帝所幸閹人華願兒，官至散騎常侍，加將軍帶郡。 帝少好讀書，[17]頗識古事，自造世祖誄及雜篇章，往往有辭采。 以魏武帝有發丘中郎將、摸金校尉，乃置此二官。 以建安王休仁、山陽王休祐領之。 [18]其餘事迹，分見諸列傳。
The emperor from childhood was impatient and narrow; in the Eastern Palace he was often rebuked by Shizu. When Shizu toured the west, Ziye submitted memorials on daily conduct and deportment; his handwriting was not careful, and the emperor interrogated and reproved him. Ziye submitted a memorial apologizing, and the emperor again replied: "Your writing does not improve—this is one matter. I hear that you are by nature always lax and remiss, and your perversity grows worse day by day—how can you be so obstinately stupid!" When he first ascended the throne and received the seal and cord, his manner was perverse and he had no look of mourning. At first he still found the great ministers and Dai Faxiang and the rest difficult to deal with; after he killed Faxiang, none of the great ministers was not shaken with fear. Thereupon he again executed the host of lords. From Yuan Kai downward, all were beaten, dragged, and hauled. Within and without there was peril; the palace halls were in uproar. When the empress dowager's illness first became grave, she sent to summon the emperor. The emperor said: "Among sick people there are many ghosts—it is fearful; how could I go?" The empress dowager was enraged and said to her attendants: "Bring a knife—I will cut open my belly; how could I have borne such a fine son!" Several days after the empress dowager died, the emperor dreamed that the empress dowager said to him: "You are unfilial and unkind; by nature you have no aspect of a ruler. Zishang is foolish and perverse like this as well; he too is not what fortune and the age can sustain. Emperor Xiao Wu was dangerous and cruel and extinguished the Way; resentment bound men and spirits; though he had many sons, none had Heaven's mandate. To whom the great fortune belongs, it should return to a son of Emperor Wen. Afterward Prince of Xiangdong Yu succeeded to the throne—indeed he was a son of Emperor Wen. Therefore the emperor gathered his uncles in the capital region, fearing they would become a threat if left outside. The Princess of Shanyin was licentious and excessive beyond measure; she said to the emperor: "Your servant and Your Majesty, though male and female differ, both owe our bodies to the late emperor. Your Majesty has tens of thousands in the six palaces, while your servant has only one husband. Matters are not equally balanced—how can it be so extreme!" The emperor thereupon set up thirty male favorites at her left and right; her rank was advanced to Princess of Kuaiji commandery with precedence equal to a prince of a commandery, a fief for bathing of two thousand households, [16] one set of martial music granted, and twenty guards with ceremonial swords added. Whenever the emperor went out, he and the court ministers commonly accompanied the carriage together. The princess, because the minister of personnel Chu Yuan was handsome in appearance, asked the emperor that he serve her; the emperor consented. Yuan attended the princess for ten days and was fully subjected to coercion; he vowed he would rather die than yield, and so was able to escape. The eunuch Hua Yuan'er, whom the emperor favored, reached the post of regular attendant of scattered cavalry and was given the additional rank of general with commandery. The emperor from youth loved reading; [17] he was quite knowledgeable about ancient matters, and the eulogy for Shizu and miscellaneous compositions that he himself composed often had literary color. Because Emperor Wu of Wei had the office of Director of the Mound and the Colonel Who Seizes Gold, he established these two offices. Prince of Jian'an Xiuren and Prince of Shanyang Xiuyou were placed in charge of them. [18] His remaining deeds are separately treated in the various biographies.
26
史臣曰：廢帝之事行著于篇。 若夫武王數殷紂之釁，不能絓其萬一； 霍光書昌邑之過，未足舉其毫釐。 假以中才之君，有一于此，足以霣社殘宗，汚宮瀦廟，況總斯惡以萃一人之體乎！ 其得亡亦為幸矣。
The historian says: The deeds of the Deposed Emperor are set forth in this chapter. If King Wu numbered the crimes of King Zhou of Yin, they could not bind even one part in ten thousand; if Huo Guang wrote down the faults of Liu He of Changyi, they would not suffice to lift even a hair's breadth. Suppose a ruler of middling talent had even one of these—it would be enough to topple the altars of soil and grain and ruin the ancestral temple; how much the more when all these evils are gathered in the body of a single man! That he escaped destruction was also a piece of good fortune.

校勘記
Textual collation notes
28
出居東宮各本並脫「居」字，據元龜二五六補。
Every edition lacks "reside" in the phrase about moving to the Eastern Palace; the character is restored per Yuan Gui 256.
29
丹陽尹永嘉王子仁為南豫州刺史「南豫」子仁傳作「南兗」。
The appointment of Prince of Yongjia Ziren as inspector of Southern Yuzhou appears in his biography as Southern Yanzhou instead.
30
繁而傷治「傷」各本並作「作」，據元龜一九一改。
Where the text reads that elaborate laws are harmful to governance, every edition has "make" for "harmful"; emended per Yuan Gui 191.
31
氣緯舛互「互」各本並作「玄」，蓋形近而譌，今改正。
The phrase about celestial signs being at odds appears as "dark" in all editions—probably a graphic corruption, now corrected.
32
領軍將軍王玄謨為鎮北將軍青冀二州刺史各本「鎮北將軍」下並衍「南徐州刺史新安王子鸞為」十一字。 按新安王子鸞傳，不言曾為青、冀二州刺史，而王玄謨傳，言玄謨時徙為青、冀二州刺史。 今訂正。
After Wang Xuamo's appointment as general who pacifies the north, every edition inserts eleven spurious characters naming Prince of Xin'an Ziluan as inspector of Southern Xuzhou. Ziluan's biography never places him in Qing and Ji, while Xuamo's biography shows him transferred there at this date. The text is emended on that basis.
33
己丑各本並作「己未」。 按是月丁卯朔，二十三日己丑，無己未、乙丑。 今據建康實錄及通鑑改正。
The date is given as jiwei in every edition, but should read jichou. That month began on dingmao; the twenty-third day was jichou—neither jiwei nor yichou occurred. The date is corrected to match the Veritable Records of Jiankang and the Comprehensive Mirror.
34
庚寅各本並作「庚子」。 按是月丁卯朔，無庚子，二十三日己丑後，二十四日有庚寅。 今改正。
Every edition gives gengzi where gengyin is required. With dingmao as the first of the month there was no gengzi; the day after jichou was gengyin. The reading is corrected.
35
輔國將軍宗越為司州刺史「宗越」書中多誤作「宋越」，今據宗越傳改正。 以下類此者皆逕改，不具校。
Zong Yue's name is frequently miswritten as Song Yue; corrected here from his biography. Like cases below are corrected without repeating full notes.
36
以尚書右僕射顏師伯為尚書僕射「尚書僕射」各本作「尚書左僕射」，南史無「左」字。 李慈銘宋書札記云：「左字衍，當據南史刪。 」按是時但置尚書僕射，不分立左右。 今據南史刪「左」字。
Yan Shibo's promotion is written with a superfluous "Left" in every edition; the History of the Southern Dynasties omits it. Li Ciming notes that "Left" is intrusive and should be dropped per the History of the Southern Dynasties. At this time there was only a single vice-director of the Masters of Writing, not separate left and right posts. The intrusive "Left" is removed following the History of the Southern Dynasties.
37
減州郡縣田祿之半宋本、三朝本、北監本、毛本及南史作「田祿」。 殿本、局本作「田租」。 建康實錄作「祿秩」。 按普減國內田租之半，非封建統治者所肯為，此必減州郡縣官吏田祿之半。 故建康實錄改稱為「祿秩」。
The Song, Three-Dynasties, Beijian, and Mao editions and the History of the Southern Dynasties all read "field stipends" for the halved payments. The Palace and Bureau editions read "field rent" instead. The Veritable Records of Jiankang call them "stipend ranks." A realm-wide halving of field rent is implausible for a feudal court; the measure must halve officials' field stipends in provinces, commanderies, and counties. Hence the Veritable Records of Jiankang reworded the passage as "stipend ranks."
38
衞將軍南豫州刺史湘東王彧改為雍州刺史各本並脫「南」字。 按明帝紀，永光元年為南豫州刺史，鎮姑孰。 又按州郡志，此時南豫州鎮姑孰，而豫州則未嘗以姑孰為州治。 今據明帝紀補。
Prince of Xiangdong Yu's transfer from inspector of Southern Yuzhou to Yongzhou lacks "Southern" in every edition. Emperor Ming's annals place him at Southern Yuzhou, stationed at Gushu, in the first year of Yongguang. The Treatise on Provinces and Commanderies likewise puts Southern Yuzhou at Gushu while Yuzhou itself never used Gushu as its seat. "Southern" is restored on the authority of Emperor Ming's annals.
39
尚書左僕射顏師伯「尚書左僕射」各本並作「尚書僕射」，據南史改，說見前。
Yan Shibo's title is given without "Left" in every edition; the History of the Southern Dynasties supports the shorter form, as noted above.
40
以鎮西大將軍豫州刺史山陽王休祐為鎮軍大將軍開府儀同三司鎮西大將軍各本並作「鎮北大將軍」。 上九月辛亥下作鎮西大將軍，休祐傳亦作鎮西，今改正。
Prince of Shanyang Xiuyou's rank of general who pacifies the west is written as pacifies the north in every edition. The ninth-month xinhai entry and Xiuyou's biography both read Pacifies the West; the text is corrected accordingly.
41
少府劉勝之子也「劉勝」元龜一九七及二０七同本紀。 符瑞志及始安王休仁傳、宋略、南史帝紀作劉矇，南史休仁傳作劉蒙。
Yuan Gui 197 and 207 in this annal read Liu Sheng for the palace treasury official's son. The Treatise on Omens, Xiuren's biography, the Song Summary, and the Southern Dynasties annals variously read Liu Meng for this name.
42
食湯沐邑二千戶「食」各本並作「侯」。 據御覽一五二改。
The princess's bathing fief of two thousand households appears as "marquis" in every edition. Emended per Imperial Readings 152.
43
帝少好讀書「讀書」各本並作「講書」，據南史、元龜一九二、御覽一二八引改。
Every edition has "lecturing" where "reading" is meant; corrected per the History of the Southern Dynasties, Yuan Gui 192, and Imperial Readings 128.
44
以建安王休仁山陽王休祐領之各本並脫「休仁山陽王」五字，據南史補。 張燴讀史舉正云：「按建安王乃休仁，休祐則山陽王也。 南史作『以建安王休仁、山陽王休祐領之』為是。」
All editions drop the five characters naming Xiuren and Prince of Shanyang; they are restored per the History of the Southern Dynasties. Zhang Hui explains that Prince of Jian'an is Xiuren and Xiuyou is Prince of Shanyang. The Southern Dynasties version naming both princes as in charge is therefore correct."
