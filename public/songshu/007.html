1
前廢帝諱子業，小字法師，孝武帝長子也。 元嘉二十六年正月甲申生。 世祖鎮尋陽，子業留京邑。 三十年，世祖入伐元凶，被囚侍中下省，將見害者數矣，卒得無恙。
Emperor Fei I, whose personal name was Ziye and whose childhood name was Fashi, was the eldest son of Emperor Xiaowu. He was born on the jiazi day of the first month in the twenty-sixth year of Yuanjia. While the Emperor was posted at Xunyang, Ziye stayed behind in the capital. In the thirtieth year, when the Emperor marched in to punish the Assassin Prince, Ziye was held in the lower bureau of the Attendant-Censorate. He was nearly killed more than once before he finally came through unscathed.
2
世祖踐阼，立為皇太子。 始未之東宮，中庶子、二率並入直永福省。 大明二年，出居東宮。 [1]四年，講孝經於崇正殿。 七年，加元服。
After the Emperor took the throne, Ziye was installed as crown prince. At first he had not yet moved into the Eastern Palace; the Palace Prefect and the two guard commandants all served on duty at Yongfu Palace. In the second year of Daming he took up residence in the Eastern Palace. [1] In the fourth year he lectured on the Classic of Filial Piety in Chongzheng Hall. In the seventh year he underwent the capping ceremony.
3
八年閏五月庚申，世祖崩，其日，太子即皇帝位。 大赦天下。 太宰江夏王義恭解尚書令，加中書監，驃騎大將軍柳元景加尚書令。 甲子，置錄尚書，太宰江夏王義恭錄尚書事。 驃騎大將軍柳元景加開府儀同三司。 丹陽尹永嘉王子仁為南豫州刺史。 [2]
On the gengshen day of the intercalary fifth month in the eighth year, the Emperor died; that same day the crown prince ascended the throne. A general amnesty was proclaimed throughout the empire. Grand Tutor Prince Jiangxia of Yixia stepped down as Director of the Masters of Writing and was made Supervisor of the Masters of Writing instead. General-in-Chief Who Runs Fast Liu Yuanjing was appointed Director of the Masters of Writing. On the jiazi day the post of Recorder of the Masters of Writing was created, and Grand Tutor Prince Jiangxia of Yixia was appointed to it. General-in-Chief Who Runs Fast Liu Yuanjing was granted the Staff of Authority with protocol equal to a three-division office. Prince Yongjia of Yongxing, who was serving as Director of Danyang, was appointed Inspector of Southern Yuzhou. [Note 2: The text here reads Southern Yuzhou, but Prince Ziren's biography gives the appointment as Southern Yanzhou.]
4
六月辛未，詔曰：「朕以眇身，夙紹洪業，敬御天威，欽對靈命。 仰遵凝緒，日鑒前圖，實可以拱默守成，詒風長世。 而寶位告始，萬宇改屬，惟德弗明，昧于大道。 思宣睿範，引茲簡恤，可具詢執事，詳訪民隱。 凡曲令密文，繁而傷治，[3]關市僦稅，事施一時，而姦吏舞文，妄興威福，加以氣緯舛互，[4]偏頗滋甚。 宜其寬傜輕憲，以救民切。 御府諸署，事不須廣，雕文篆刻，無施於今。 悉宜并省，以酬氓願。 藩王貿貨，壹皆禁斷。 外便具條以聞。 」戊寅，以豫州之淮南郡復為南梁郡，復分宣城還置淮南郡。 庚辰，以南海太守袁曇遠為廣州刺史。
On the xinwei day of the sixth month an edict said: "I, slight in person, have early inherited the great enterprise, reverently bearing Heaven's majesty and facing the numinous mandate. Upholding the settled line of succession and taking past models as my daily mirror, I might truly have folded my arms in silence, guarded what was won, and let good governance endure for generations. Yet no sooner had the throne been announced than the realm changed hands; my virtue is not yet clear, and I remain blind to the great Way. I wish to proclaim an enlightened standard and extend this measure of relief: let every responsible office be consulted and the people's hardships thoroughly investigated. Every crooked statute and secret regulation that is elaborate yet injures good governance, [3] every market toll and transit tax imposed only for a season—yet corrupt clerks twist the written law and recklessly wield power, while the qi configurations clash and conflict [4] and partiality grows worse by the day. Corvée should be eased and penalties lightened to relieve the people's urgent distress. The various offices of the Imperial Household need not be numerous; carved ornament and seal engraving have no place today. They should all be consolidated and cut back to answer the people's wish. Trade in goods by imperial princes is forbidden altogether. Let the outer offices prepare detailed reports and submit them. " On the wuyin day Huainan commandery in Yuzhou was restored as Southern Liang commandery, and Xuancheng was split off again to re-establish Huainan commandery. On the gengchen day Yuan Tanyuan, Administrator of Nanhai, was appointed Inspector of Guangzhou.
5
秋七月己亥，鎮軍將軍、雍州刺史晉安王子勛改為江州刺史，中護軍宗慤為安西將軍、雍州刺史，鎮北將軍、徐州刺史湘東王彧為護軍將軍，中軍將軍義陽王昶為征北將軍、徐州刺史。 庚戌，婆皇國遣使獻方物。 崇皇太后曰太皇太后，皇后曰皇太后。 乙卯，罷南北二馳道。 孝建以來所改制度，還依元嘉。 丙辰，追崇獻妃為獻皇后。 乙丑，撫軍將軍、南徐州刺史新安王子鸞解領司徒。
On the jihai day of the seventh month, General Who Pacifies the Army and Inspector of Yong Prince Jin'an of Jin'an was reassigned as Inspector of Jiangzhou; Colonel of the Guard Zong Que became General Who Pacifies the West and Inspector of Yong; General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Xuzhou Prince Xiangdong of Xiangdong was made General Who Protects the Army; and General of the Center Prince Yiyang of Yiyang was appointed General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Xuzhou. On the gengxu day the kingdom of Po Huang sent envoys bearing tribute goods. The Honored Empress Dowager was elevated to Grand Empress Dowager, and the empress was styled Empress Dowager. On the yimao day the northern and southern imperial expressways were abolished. Institutions altered since the Xiaojian era were restored to the Yuanjia standard. On the bingchen day the late Consort Xian was posthumously honored as Empress Xian. On the yichou day General Who Pacifies the Army and Inspector of Southern Xuzhou Prince Xin'an of Xin'an relinquished his concurrent office as Minister.
6
八月丁卯，領軍將軍王玄謨為鎮北將軍、青冀二州刺史。 [5]己巳，以青、冀二州刺史蕭惠開為益州刺史。 己丑，[6]皇太后崩。 京師雨水。 庚寅，[7]遣御史與官長隨宜賑卹。
On the dingmao day of the eighth month Colonel of the Garrison Wang Xuemo was appointed General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Qing and Ji. [5] On the jisi day Xiao Huikai, Inspector of Qing and Ji, was appointed Inspector of Yizhou. [6] On the jichou day the Empress Dowager died. Rain fell without cease in the capital region. [7] On the gengyin day censors were sent out with local officials to provide relief wherever it was needed.
7
九月辛丑，護軍將軍湘東王彧為領軍將軍。 癸卯，以尚書左僕射劉遵考為特進、右光祿大夫。 乙卯，文穆皇后祔葬景寧陵。
On the xinchou day of the ninth month General Who Protects the Army Prince Xiangdong of Xiangdong was appointed Colonel of the Garrison. On the guimao day Left Vice Director of the Masters of Writing Liu Zunkao was promoted to Special Grand Master and Right Minister of the Palace. On the yimao day Empress Wenmu was enshrined beside the late Emperor at Jingning Mausoleum.
8
冬十月甲戌，太常建安王休仁為護軍將軍。 戊寅，輔國將軍宗越為司州刺史。 [8]庚辰，原除揚、南徐州大明七年逋租。
On the jiaxu day of the tenth month Minister of Ceremonies Prince Jian'an of Jian'an was appointed General Who Protects the Army. On the wuyin day General Who Assists the State Zong Yue was appointed Inspector of Sizhou. [8] On the gengchen day overdue rents from the seventh year of Daming in Yang and Southern Xuzhou were remitted.
9
十二月乙酉，以尚書右僕射顏師伯為尚書僕射。 [9]壬辰，以王畿諸郡為揚州，以揚州為東揚州。 癸巳，以車騎將軍、揚州刺史豫章王子尚為司徒、揚州刺史。
On the yiyou day of the twelfth month Right Vice Director of the Masters of Writing Yan Shibo was promoted to Vice Director of the Masters of Writing. [9] On the renchen day the commanderies of the metropolitan belt were reorganized as Yangzhou, while the former Yangzhou became Eastern Yangzhou. On the guisi day General of Chariots and Cavalry and Inspector of Yangzhou Prince Yuzhang of Yuzhang was appointed Minister and Inspector of Yangzhou.
10
去歲及是歲，東諸郡大旱，甚者米一升數百，京邑亦至百餘，餓死者十有六七。 孝建以來，又立錢署鑄錢，百姓因此盜鑄，錢轉偽小，商貨不行。
In the previous year and this year the eastern commanderies suffered severe drought; in the worst places a sheng of grain cost several hundred cash, and even in the capital more than a hundred. Six or seven out of every ten people starved to death. Since the Xiaojian era mints had been set up to cast coin, and the people responded by counterfeiting in turn until the money grew false and light and trade ceased to circulate.
11
永光元年春正月乙未朔，改元。 大赦天下。 乙巳，省諸州臺傳。 戊午，以領軍將軍湘東王彧為衞將軍、南豫州刺史，護軍將軍建安王休仁為領軍將軍，祕書監山陽王休祐為豫州刺史，左衞將軍桂陽王休範為中護軍，南豫州刺史尋陽王子房為東揚州刺史。
On the yiwei day, the first day of the first month of spring in the first year of Yongguang, the era name was changed. A general amnesty was proclaimed throughout the empire. On the yisi day the provincial relay offices were abolished. On the wuwu day Colonel of the Garrison Prince Xiangdong of Xiangdong became General of the Guard and Inspector of Southern Yuzhou; General Who Protects the Army Prince Jian'an of Jian'an was made Colonel of the Garrison; Supervisor of the Masters of Writing Prince Shanyang of Shanyang was appointed Inspector of Yuzhou; Colonel of the Left Guard Prince Guiyang of Guiyang became Colonel of the Guard; and Inspector of Southern Yuzhou Prince Xunyang of Xunyang was made Inspector of Eastern Yangzhou.
12
二月乙丑，減州郡縣田祿之半。 [10]庚寅，鑄二銖錢。
On the yichou day of the second month the field-salary allotments of provinces, commanderies, and counties were cut by half. [10] On the gengyin day two-zhu cash was cast.
13
三月甲辰，罷臨江郡。
On the jiachen day of the third month Linjiang commandery was abolished.
14
五月己亥，割郢州隨郡屬雍州。 丙午，以後軍司馬張牧為交州刺史。
On the jihai day of the fifth month Suo commandery in Yingzhou was transferred to Yongzhou. On the bingwu day Rear Army Major Zhang Mu was appointed Inspector of Jiaozhou.
15
六月己巳，左軍長史劉道隆為梁、南秦二州刺史。 乙亥，安西將軍、雍州刺史宗慤卒。 壬午，衞將軍、南豫州刺史湘東王彧改為雍州刺史。 [11]尚書令、驃騎大將軍柳元景加南豫州刺史。
On the jisi day of the sixth month Left Army Chief Clerk Liu Daolong was appointed Inspector of Liang and Southern Qin. On the yihai day General Who Pacifies the West and Inspector of Yong Zong Que died. On the renwu day General of the Guard and Inspector of Southern Yuzhou Prince Xiangdong of Xiangdong was reassigned as Inspector of Yong. [11] Director of the Masters of Writing and General-in-Chief Who Runs Fast Liu Yuanjing was also made Inspector of Southern Yuzhou.
16
秋八月辛酉，越騎校尉戴法興有罪，賜死。 庚午，以尚書僕射顏師伯為尚書左僕射，[12]吏部尚書王景文為尚書右僕射。 癸酉，帝自率宿衞兵，誅太宰江夏王義恭、尚書令驃騎大將軍柳元景、尚書左僕射顏師伯、[13]廷尉劉德願。 改元為景和元年。 文武賜位二等。 以領軍將軍建安王休仁為安西將軍、雍州刺史，衞將軍湘東王彧還為南豫州刺史。 甲戌，司徒、揚州刺史豫章王子尚領尚書令，射聲校尉沈文秀為青州刺史，左軍司馬崔道固為冀州刺史。 乙亥，詔曰：「昔凝神佇逸，磻溪讚道，湛慮思才，傅巖毗化。 朕位御三極，風澄萬宇，資鈇電斷，正卯斯戮。 思所以仰宣遺烈，俯弘景祚，每結夢庖鼎，瞻言板築，有劬日昃，無忘昧旦。 可甄訪郡國，招聘閭部：其有孝性忠節，幽居遯棲，信誠義行，廉正表俗，文敏博識，幹事治民，務加旌舉，隨才引擢。 庶官克順，彝倫咸叙。 主者精加詳括，稱朕意焉。 」以始興公沈慶之為太尉，鎮北將軍、青冀二州刺史王玄謨為領軍將軍。 庚辰，以石頭城為長樂宮，東府城為未央宮。 罷東揚州并揚州。 甲申，以北邸為建章宮，南第為長楊宮。 以冠軍將軍邵陵王子元為湘州刺史。 丙戌，原除吳、吳興、義興、晉陵、琅邪五郡大明八年以前逋租。 己丑，復立南北二馳道。
On the xinyou day of the eighth month Colonel of the Swift Cavalry Dai Faxing was found guilty and ordered to take his own life. On the gengwu day Vice Director of the Masters of Writing Yan Shibo was promoted to Left Vice Director; [12] Director of the Ministry of Personnel Wang Jingwen was made Right Vice Director of the Masters of Writing. On the guiyou day the Emperor personally led the palace guards and put to death Grand Tutor Prince Jiangxia of Yixia, Director of the Masters of Writing and General-in-Chief Who Runs Fast Liu Yuanjing, Left Vice Director of the Masters of Writing Yan Shibo, [13] and Commandant of Justice Liu Deyuan. The era name was changed to Jinghe. Civil and military officials were promoted two ranks in nobility. Colonel of the Garrison Prince Jian'an of Jian'an was appointed General Who Pacifies the West and Inspector of Yong, and General of the Guard Prince Xiangdong of Xiangdong returned to his post as Inspector of Southern Yuzhou. On the jiaxu day Minister and Inspector of Yangzhou Prince Yuzhang of Yuzhang assumed the directorship of the Masters of Writing; Colonel of the Archers Shen Wenxiu was appointed Inspector of Qingzhou; and Left Army Major Cui Daogu was made Inspector of Jizhou. On the yihai day an edict said: "In antiquity, when one concentrated the spirit and waited in stillness, Panxi praised the Way; when one pondered talent in deep thought, Fuyan aided in transformation. I occupy the throne at the three extremities, and the winds are clear throughout the realm; relying on the axe of judgment and the lightning of authority, at the hour of mao I carry out punishment. I mean to look up and proclaim the legacy I have received and look down to extend the dynasty's fortune; night after night I dream of the ding cauldron, and every word recalls the builder's plank—my toil runs from dawn to dusk, and I do not forget the hour before dawn. Let every commandery and state be searched and talent recruited from the districts: anyone showing filial piety and loyal integrity, living in seclusion far from office, demonstrating trustworthiness, sincerity, and righteous conduct, upright integrity that sets an example for the people, literary refinement and broad learning, or skill in affairs and governance—such persons should be commended and promoted according to their abilities. Then officials will perform their duties and the moral order will be restored. The responsible offices should examine the matter carefully and report in accord with my intent. " Duke of Shixing Shen Qingzhi was appointed Grand Commandant, and General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Qing and Ji Wang Xuemo was made Colonel of the Garrison. On the gengchen day Stone City was renamed Everlasting Joy Palace and the Eastern Headquarters was renamed Weiyang Palace. Eastern Yangzhou was abolished and merged back into Yangzhou. On the jiashen day the northern residence became Jianzhang Palace and the southern mansion became Changyang Palace. General Who Conquers the Champions Prince Shaoling of Shaoling was appointed Inspector of Xiangzhou. On the bingxu day overdue rents owed before the eighth year of Daming in the five commanderies of Wu, Wuxing, Yixing, Jinling, and Langye were remitted. On the jichou day the northern and southern imperial expressways were restored.
17
九月癸巳，車駕幸湖熟，奏鼓吹。 戊戌，車駕還宮。 庚子，以南兗州刺史永嘉王子仁為南徐州刺史，丹陽尹始安王子真為南兗州刺史。 辛丑，撫軍將軍、南徐州刺史新安王子鸞免為庶人，賜死。 丙午，以兗州刺史薛安都為平北將軍、徐州刺史。 丁未，衞將軍湘東王彧加開府儀同三司，特進、右光祿大夫劉遵考為安西將軍、南豫州刺史，寧朔將軍殷孝祖為兗州刺史。 戊申，以前梁、南秦二州刺史柳元怙復為梁、南秦二州刺史。 己酉，車駕討征北將軍、徐州刺史義陽王昶，內外戒嚴。 昶奔于索虜。 辛亥，右將軍、豫州刺史山陽王休祐進號鎮西大將軍。 甲寅，以安西長史袁顗為雍州刺史。 戊午，以左民尚書劉思考為益州刺史。 是日解嚴，車駕幸瓜步。 開百姓鑄錢。
On the guisi day of the ninth month the emperor visited Hushu, where martial music was performed. On the wuxu day the emperor returned to the palace. On the gengzi day Inspector of Southern Yanzhou Prince Yongjia of Yongxing was reassigned as Inspector of Southern Xuzhou, and Director of Danyang Prince Shi'an of Shi'an was appointed Inspector of Southern Yanzhou. On the xinchou day General Who Pacifies the Army and Inspector of Southern Xuzhou Prince Xin'an of Xin'an was stripped of rank, reduced to commoner status, and ordered to take his own life. On the bingwu day Inspector of Yanzhou Xue Andu was appointed General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Xuzhou. On the dingwei day General of the Guard Prince Xiangdong of Xiangdong was granted protocol equal to a three-division office; Special Grand Master and Right Minister of the Palace Liu Zunkao became General Who Pacifies the West and Inspector of Southern Yuzhou; and General Who Pacifies the Barbarians Yin Xiaozu was appointed Inspector of Yanzhou. On the wushen day former Inspector of Liang and Southern Qin Liu Yuanhu was reappointed Inspector of Liang and Southern Qin. On the jiyou day the emperor marched out to attack General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Xuzhou Prince Yiyang of Yiyang, and martial law was proclaimed throughout the capital. Chang fled to the northern barbarians. On the xinhai day General of the Right and Inspector of Yuzhou Prince Shanyang of Shanyang was promoted to General Who Pacifies the West. On the jiayin day Chief Clerk of the Army of the Pacified West Yuan Yi was appointed Inspector of Yong. On the wuwu day Director of the Left Household Liu Kaokao was appointed Inspector of Yizhou. That same day martial law was lifted and the emperor went to Guabu. Private casting of coin by the common people was permitted.
18
冬十月癸亥，曲赦徐州。 丙寅，車駕還宮。 以建安王休仁為護軍將軍。 己卯，東陽太守王藻下獄死。 以宮人謝貴嬪為夫人，加虎賁靸戟，鸞輅龍旂，出警入蹕，實新蔡公主也。 乙酉，以鎮西大將軍、豫州刺史山陽王休祐為鎮軍大將軍、開府儀同三司。 [14]
On the guihai day of the tenth month a partial amnesty was proclaimed for Xuzhou. On the bingyin day the emperor returned to the palace. Prince Jian'an of Jian'an was appointed General Who Protects the Army. On the jimao day Wang Zao, Administrator of Dongyang, was thrown into prison and died there. The palace woman Honored Consort Xie was elevated to Lady, granted Tiger Guards bearing halberds, an imperial carriage with dragon banners, and full escort on her comings and goings—she was in fact Princess Xincai in disguise. On the yiyou day General Who Pacifies the West and Inspector of Yuzhou Prince Shanyang of Shanyang was promoted to General Who Pacifies the Army with protocol equal to a three-division office. [Note 14: Various editions read "General Who Pacifies the North" where the received text has "General Who Pacifies the West"; the ninth-month entry and Prince Shanyang's biography support the latter reading.]
19
十一月壬辰，寧朔將軍何邁下獄死。 新除太尉沈慶之薨。 壬寅，立皇后路氏，四廂奏樂。 赦揚、南徐二州。 護軍將軍建安王休仁加特進、左光祿大夫。 中護軍桂陽王休範遷職。 丁未，皇子生，少府劉勝之子也。 [15]大赦天下。 贓汙淫盜，悉皆原除。 賜為父後者爵一級。 壬子，以特進、左光祿大夫、護軍將軍建安王休仁為驃騎大將軍、開府儀同三司。 戊午，南平王敬猷、廬陵王敬先、安南侯敬淵並賜死。
On the renchen day of the eleventh month General Who Pacifies the Barbarians He Mai was imprisoned and put to death. Shen Qingzhi, who had just been appointed Grand Commandant, died. On the renyin day Empress Lu was installed, with music performed in all four side chambers of the palace. An amnesty was proclaimed for Yang and Southern Xuzhou. General Who Protects the Army Prince Jian'an of Jian'an was further promoted to Special Grand Master and Left Minister of the Palace. Colonel of the Guard Prince Guiyang of Guiyang was reassigned. On the dingwei day a prince was born, the son of Director of the Palace Treasury Liu Sheng. [15] A general amnesty was proclaimed throughout the empire. Offenses of corruption, licentiousness, and theft were all pardoned. Those who had become heirs for their fathers were granted one step in noble rank. On the renzi day Special Grand Master, Left Minister of the Palace, and General Who Protects the Army Prince Jian'an of Jian'an was appointed General-in-Chief Who Runs Fast with protocol equal to a three-division office. On the wuwu day Prince Jingyou of Nanping, Prince Jingxian of Luling, and Marquis Jingyuan of Annan were all ordered to take their own lives.

時帝凶悖日甚，誅殺相繼，內外百司，不保首領。 先是訛言云：「湘中出天子。 」帝將南巡荊、湘二州以厭之。 先欲誅諸叔，然後發引。 太宗與左右阮佃夫、王道隆、李道兒密結帝左右壽寂之、姜產之等十一人，謀共廢帝。 戊午夜，帝於華林園竹林堂射鬼。 時巫覡云：「此堂有鬼。 」故帝自射之。 壽寂之懷刀直入，姜產之為副。 帝欲走，寂之追而殞之。 時年十七。 太皇太后令曰：
By then the Emperor's cruelty and perversity were growing worse by the day. Executions followed one after another, and throughout the bureaucracy no one could be sure of keeping his head. Earlier a rumor had spread: "A Son of Heaven will arise in Xiang." " The Emperor planned a southern tour of Jing and Xiang to counter the omen. He intended first to execute his uncles, then set out on the tour. The Founding Emperor secretly allied with his confidants Ruan Tianfu, Wang Daolong, and Li Dao'er to win over eleven of the Emperor's attendants, including Shou Jizhi and Jiang Chanzhi, and together they plotted to depose him. At midnight on the wuwu day the Emperor was in Bamboo Grove Hall in Huangling Garden, shooting at ghosts. The shamans had said, "This hall is haunted." " That was why the Emperor had gone there to shoot at them himself. Shou Jizhi entered with a hidden blade; Jiang Chanzhi followed as his second. 司徒領護軍八座：子業雖曰嫡長，少稟凶毒，不仁不孝，著自髫齔。 孝武棄世，屬當辰曆。 自梓宮在殯，喜容靦然，天罰重離，歡恣滋甚。 逼以內外維持，忍虐未露，而凶慘難抑，一旦肆禍，遂縱戮上宰，殄害輔臣。 子鸞兄弟，先帝鍾愛，含怨既往，枉加屠酷。 昶茂親作扞，橫相徵討。 新蔡公主逼離夫族，幽置深宮，詭云薨殞。 襄事甫爾，喪禮頓釋，昏酣長夜，庶事傾遺。 朝賢舊勳，棄若遺土。 管絃不輟，珍羞備膳。 詈辱祖考，以為戲謔。 行游莫止，淫縱無度。 肆宴園陵，規圖發掘。 誅剪無辜，籍略婦女。 建樹偽豎，莫知誰息。 拜嬪立后，慶過恒典。 宗室密戚，遇若婢僕，鞭捶陵曳，無復尊卑。 南平一門，特鍾其酷。 反天滅理，顯暴萬端。 苛罰酷令，終無紀極，夏桀、殷辛，未足以譬。 闔朝業業，人不自保，百姓遑遑，手足靡厝。 行穢禽獸，罪盈三千。 高祖之業將泯，七廟之享幾絕。 吾老疾沈篤，每規禍鴆，憂煎漏刻，氣命無幾。 開闢以降，所未嘗聞。 遠近思奮，十室而九。
To the Minister, Colonel of the Garrison, and the Eight Seats: Although Ziye was the legitimate eldest son, from childhood he showed a vicious and poisonous nature; his want of benevolence and filial piety has been plain since he was in his topknot. When Emperor Xiaowu died, the succession fell to him by the calendar of Heaven. From the moment the late Emperor's coffin lay in state, his face was bright and untroubled; though Heaven had punished the realm with double bereavement, his licentious delight only grew. Outward appearances within and without the court still had to be maintained, so for a time he restrained his cruelty; but his savage depravity could not be held back. In a single morning he unleashed catastrophe, wantonly slaughtering the chief ministers and destroying his chief advisers. Prince Luan and his brothers had been the late Emperor's favorites; nursing old resentments, the Emperor had them slaughtered without cause. Chang, a close imperial kinsman, had stood as a bulwark for the house, yet the Emperor raised troops against him without cause. Princess Xincai was torn from her husband's family, hidden away in the inner palace, and falsely declared dead. Hardly had mourning begun when funeral observances were cast aside; he drank through the long nights while affairs of state were left in ruins. Veteran ministers of merit were cast aside like worthless dust. Music never ceased; every meal was heaped with delicacies. He reviled his ancestors and made a jest of it. His wanderings knew no bounds; his licentious excess knew no limit. He feasted at the imperial tombs and plotted their desecration. He put the innocent to death and seized women by force. He set up false heirs, and no one knew whose line would survive. In elevating consorts and installing an empress, his celebrations exceeded all precedent. Imperial kinsmen and close relatives were treated like servants; flogging, beating, and dragging them about erased every distinction of rank. The house of Nanping alone bore the full brunt of his cruelty. He reversed Heaven and extinguished moral principle; his open violence took ten thousand forms. His harsh punishments and cruel edicts knew no end; even Jie of Xia and Xin of Yin would not suffice for comparison. The whole court trembled; no official could feel safe; the people lived in dread, with nowhere to set hand or foot. His conduct was filthier than a beast's; his crimes filled every category of the law. The founding emperor's legacy was on the verge of extinction; the sacrifices of the seven temples nearly came to an end. I am aged and gravely ill; day after day I foresee disaster; anxiety consumes me hour by hour, and my life has not long to run. Since the dynasty was founded, nothing like this has been heard of. Near and far the people yearn to rise against him; nine households in ten.
22
衞將軍湘東王體自太祖，天縱英聖，文皇鍾愛，寵冠列藩。 吾早識神睿，特兼常禮。 潛運宏規，義士投袂，獨夫既殞，懸首白旗，社稷再興，宗祏永固，人鬼屬心，大命允集。 且勳德高邈，大業攸歸，宜遵漢、晉，纂承皇極。 主者詳舊典以時奉行。
General of the Guard Prince Xiangdong of Xiangdong is descended from the Great Ancestor; Heaven endowed him with heroic sagacity; Emperor Wen cherished him above all the feudal princes. I recognized his brilliance early and treated him with honors beyond the ordinary. He carried out his great design in secret; men of righteousness rose up; the tyrant has fallen, his head displayed on the white banner; the altars of state are restored, the ancestral temple secured; men and spirits alike turn to him; the great mandate is truly his. His merit and virtue are lofty; the great enterprise belongs to him; he should follow the precedents of Han and Jin in ascending the throne. Let the responsible offices examine the old statutes and carry them out without delay.

未亡人餘年不幸嬰此百艱，永尋情事，雖存若殞。 當復奈何！ 當復奈何！
In my remaining years I have met this hundredfold calamity; whenever I recall what has happened, I am as good as dead though I still live. What is to be done! What is to be done! What is to be done! What is to be done!

葬廢帝丹陽秣陵縣南郊壇西。
The deposed Emperor was buried west of the southern suburban altar in Moling county, Danyang.
25
帝幼而狷急，在東宮每為世祖所責。 世祖西巡，子業啟參承起居，書迹不謹，上詰讓之。 子業啟事陳謝，上又答曰：「書不長進，此是一條耳。 聞汝素都懈怠，狷戾日甚，何以頑固乃爾邪！ 」初踐阼，受璽紱，悖然無哀容。 始猶難諸大臣及戴法興等，既殺法興，諸大臣莫不震懾。 於是又誅羣公。 元凱以下，皆被毆捶牽曳。 內外危懼，殿省騷然。 初太后疾篤，遣呼帝。 帝曰：「病人間多鬼，可畏，那可往。 」太后怒，語侍者：「將刀來，破我腹，那得生如此寧馨兒！ 」及太后崩後數日，帝夢太后謂之曰：「汝不孝不仁，本無人君之相。 子尚愚悖如此，亦非運祚所及。 孝武險虐滅道，怨結人神，兒子雖多，並無天命。 大運所歸，應還文帝之子。 」其後湘東王紹位，果文帝子也。 故帝聚諸叔京邑，慮在外為患。 山陰公主淫恣過度，謂帝曰：「妾與陛下，雖男女有殊，俱託體先帝。 陛下六宮萬數，而妾唯駙馬一人。 事不均平，一何至此！ 」帝乃為主置面首左右三十人； 進爵會稽郡長公主，秩同郡王，食湯沐邑二千戶，[16]給鼓吹一部，加班劍二十人。 帝每出，與朝臣常共陪輦。 主以吏部郎褚淵貌美，就帝請以自侍，帝許之。 淵侍主十日，備見逼迫，誓死不回，遂得免。 帝所幸閹人華願兒，官至散騎常侍，加將軍帶郡。 帝少好讀書，[17]頗識古事，自造世祖誄及雜篇章，往往有辭采。 以魏武帝有發丘中郎將、摸金校尉，乃置此二官。 以建安王休仁、山陽王休祐領之。 [18]其餘事迹，分見諸列傳。
From childhood the Emperor was narrow and quick-tempered; in the Eastern Palace he was often rebuked by his father. When his father toured the west, Ziye sent memorials reporting on court routine; his handwriting was careless, and the Emperor questioned and reproached him. Ziye submitted a memorial of apology; the Emperor replied again: "Your writing shows no improvement—that is one fault among many. I hear you have always been idle and lax, and your perversity grows worse by the day—how can you be so obstinate!" " When he first took the throne and received the imperial seal and ribbon, his face showed no grief. At first he still found the senior ministers and Dai Faxing hard to control; once Faxing was killed, every senior minister trembled with fear. Then he turned to slaughter the great nobles as well. From Yuan Kai downward, all were beaten, flogged, and dragged about the palace. Inside and outside the palace there was terror; the offices were in uproar. When the Empress Dowager first fell gravely ill, she sent for the Emperor. The Emperor said, "Sickrooms are full of ghosts—it is terrifying. How could I go there?" " In anger the Empress Dowager told her attendants, "Bring a knife and cut open my belly—how could I have given birth to such a precious little monster!" " A few days after the Empress Dowager died, the Emperor dreamed that she said to him, "You are unfilial and unkind; you never had the bearing of a ruler. Zishang is as foolish and perverse as you; he too lies outside the mandate of fortune. Emperor Xiaowu was violent and cruel and turned his back on the Way; his deeds bound men and spirits in resentment; though he had many sons, none possessed Heaven's mandate. The great turn of fortune belongs elsewhere: it should return to a son of Emperor Wen." When Prince Xiangdong of Xiangdong later took the throne, he was indeed a son of Emperor Wen. For that reason the Emperor gathered his uncles in the capital, fearing trouble if they remained in the provinces. The Princess of Shanyin was extravagantly licentious and said to the Emperor, "Your Majesty and I, though man and woman differ, both owe our bodies to the late Emperor. Your Majesty has ten thousand consorts in the six palaces, while I have only one husband. The disparity is outrageous—how did it come to this!" " The Emperor then provided her with thirty male companions at court; she was advanced to Grand Princess of Kuaiji with rank equal to a commandery prince, granted a maintenance fief of two thousand households, [16] given a full set of martial music, and assigned twenty ceremonial sword bearers. Whenever the Emperor went out, court ministers commonly shared his carriage. The Princess, finding Director of the Ministry of Personnel Chu Yuan handsome, asked the Emperor to let him attend her; the Emperor agreed. Yuan attended her for ten days under relentless pressure, swore he would rather die than submit, and was finally released. The favored eunuch Hua Yuan'er rose to Attendant at the Palace Gate and was further made a general with a nominal commandery. In youth the Emperor loved reading, [17] knew antiquity well, and composed his father's eulogy and other pieces that often showed real literary grace. Because Emperor Wu of Wei had created the offices of Director of Tomb Opening and Colonel Who Searches for Gold, he established these two posts as well. Prince Jian'an of Jian'an and Prince Shanyang of Shanyang were put in charge of them. [Note 18: His remaining deeds are treated separately in the various biographical chapters.]
26
史臣曰：廢帝之事行著于篇。 若夫武王數殷紂之釁，不能絓其萬一； 霍光書昌邑之過，未足舉其毫釐。 假以中才之君，有一于此，足以霣社殘宗，汚宮瀦廟，況總斯惡以萃一人之體乎！ 其得亡亦為幸矣。
The historian comments: The deeds of the deposed Emperor are set forth in this chapter. When King Wu counted the crimes of King Zhou of Yin, he could not exhaust one part in ten thousand; when Huo Guang recorded the faults of the Marquis of Changyi, he could not capture one hair's breadth of them. A ruler of merely middling talent who committed even one such act would suffice to topple the altars, ruin his house, and defile the ancestral temple—how much more when every evil was gathered in a single man! That the dynasty survived his reign at all was itself a kind of fortune.
