列傳第四十六殷孝祖劉勔
Biography 46: Yin Xiaozu and Liu Mian
2
殷孝祖，陳郡長平人也。 曾祖羨，晉光祿勳。 父祖並不達。 孝祖少誕節，好酒色，有氣幹。 太祖元嘉末，為奉朝請，員外散騎侍郎。 世祖以其有武用，除奮武將軍、濟北太守。 入為積射將軍。 大明初，索虜寇青州，上遣孝祖北援，受刺史顏師伯節度，累與虜戰，頻大破之，事在師伯傳。 還授太子旅賁中郎將，加龍驤將軍。 竟陵王誕據廣陵為逆，孝祖隸沈慶之攻誕，又有戰功，遷西陽王子尚撫軍、寧朔將軍、南濟陰太守。 出為盱眙太守，將軍如故。 還為虎賁中郎將，仍除寧朔將軍、陽平、東平二郡太守。 又遷濟南、南郡，將軍如故。
Yin Xiaozu came from Changping in Chen Commandery. His great-grandfather Yin Xian had served as Grand Master of Splendid Happiness under the Jin. Neither his father nor his grandfather had risen to prominence. In youth Xiaozu was unconventional, fond of wine and women, and possessed real martial spirit. At the end of the Yuanjia era of Emperor Wen, he held the posts of court gentleman for the imperial audience and supernumerary gentleman attendant at the palace secretariat. Emperor Xiaowu, valuing his military ability, appointed him General Who Exerts Martial Power and administrator of Jibei. He was recalled to the capital as General of Accumulated Archery. Early in the Daming era, when the Northern Wei raided Qingzhou, the court sent Xiaozu north in relief under Inspector Yan Shibo's command. He fought the enemy again and again and won repeated crushing victories; the account is given in Yan Shibo's biography. On returning he was made central commandant of the crown prince's Rapid-as-Tigers guard and given the additional rank of General of the Dragon Cavalry. When the Prince of Jingling, Liu Dan, seized Guangling in revolt, Xiaozu served under Shen Qingzhi against him and again distinguished himself in battle. He was then transferred to staff officer of the army for Prince Zishang of Xiyang, with the ranks of General Who Pacifies the North and administrator of Southern Jiyin. He was sent out as administrator of Xuyi, retaining his general's rank. Recalled, he became tiger guard commandant and was also appointed General Who Pacifies the North and administrator of Yangping and Dongping. He was later transferred to Jinan and Nanjun, still holding his general's rank.
3
前廢帝景和元年，以本號督兗州諸軍事、兗州刺史。 太宗初即位，四方反叛，孝祖外甥司徒參軍潁川葛僧韶建議銜命徵孝祖入朝，上遣之。 時徐州刺史薛安都遣薛索兒等屯據津徑，僧韶間行得至，說孝祖曰：「景和兇狂，開闢未有，朝野危極，假命漏刻。 主上聖德天挺，神武在躬，曾不浹辰，夷凶翦暴，更造天地，未足為言。 國亂朝危，宜立長生，公卿百辟，人無異議，泰平之隆，非旦則夕。 而群小相煽，構造無端，貪利幼弱，競懷希望。 使天道助逆，群凶事申，則主幼時艱，權柄不一，兵難互起，豈有自容之地。 舅少有立功之志，長以氣節成名，若便能控濟、河義勇，還奉朝廷，非唯匡主靜亂，乃可以垂名竹帛。 」孝祖具問朝廷消息，僧韶隨方酬譬，並陳兵甲精強，主上欲委以前驅之任。 孝祖即日棄妻子，率文武二千人隨僧韶還都。
In the first year of Jinghe under the Former Deposed Emperor, he was made supervisor of military affairs in Yanzhou and inspector of Yanzhou, retaining his existing rank. When Emperor Ming first ascended the throne, revolts flared on every side. Xiaozu's nephew Ge Sengshao of Yingchuan, a staff officer of the secretariat, urged that an imperial summons bring Xiaozu to court, and the emperor dispatched him. Xue Andu, inspector of Xuzhou, had sent Xue Suo'er and others to hold the ferry crossings. Sengshao slipped through by hidden routes and urged Xiaozu: "The Jinghe reign was savage and lawless beyond anything since the world's beginning. Court and countryside hung by a thread, life measured out in clepsydra drips. Our sovereign's sagely virtue is heaven-born, his divine martial prowess in his own person. In less than ten days he has crushed the wicked and cut down the violent, remaking the realm—words can scarcely do it justice. With the state in turmoil and the court in peril, the time called for a mature ruler. Among the hundred officials of state there was no dissent. An age of great peace would come not tomorrow but today. Yet petty men stirred one another up, fabricating groundless plots, greedy for advantage from a young and weak sovereign, each nursing his own hopes. If heaven favored the rebels and the wicked prevailed, the sovereign would be young, the times harsh, and power divided. War would break out on every side—where would any of us find safety? Uncle, you have wanted glory since youth and are famed for spirit and integrity. If you can rally the loyal volunteers of Ji and the Yellow River and return to serve the court, you will not only restore the sovereign and quell the chaos—you will win a name that lives in the histories. " Xiaozu questioned him closely about affairs at court. Sengshao answered to each point and reported that the army was strong and well equipped, and that the sovereign wished to entrust him with the vanguard command. That very day Xiaozu left wife and children behind and led two thousand civil and military followers back to the capital with Sengshao.
4
時普天同逆，朝廷唯保丹陽一郡，而永世縣尋又反叛。 義興賊垂至延陵，內外憂危，咸欲奔散。 孝祖忽至，眾力不少，並傖楚壯士，人情於是大安。 進孝祖號冠軍，假節、督前鋒諸軍事，遣向虎檻，拒對南賊。 御仗先有諸葛亮筒袖鎧帽，二十五石弩射之不能入，上悉以賜孝祖。 孝祖負其誠節，凌轢諸將，臺軍有父子兄弟在南者，孝祖並欲推治。 由是人情乖離，莫樂為用。 進使持節、都督兗州、青、冀、幽四州諸軍事、撫軍將軍，刺史如故。 時賊據赭圻，孝祖將進攻之，與大統王玄謨別，悲不自勝，眾並駭怪。 泰始二年三月三日，與賊合戰，常以鼓蓋自隨，軍中人相謂曰：「殷統軍可謂死矣。 今與賊交鋒，而以羽儀自標顯，若善射者十士攢射，欲不斃，得乎？ 」是日，於陣為矢所中死，時年五十二。 追贈散騎常侍、征北將軍，持節、都督如故。 封秭歸縣侯，食邑千戶。 四年，追改封建安縣，諡曰忠侯。 孝祖子悉為薛安都所殺，以從兄子慧達繼封。 齊受禪，國除。
All the empire had risen in revolt; the court held only Danyang commandery, and before long Yongshi county rebelled too. Rebels from Yixing were nearly at Yanling. Inside and outside the court were in dread, and everyone wanted to flee. When Xiaozu arrived unexpectedly, his force was substantial—all northern and Chu veterans—and morale at once steadied. Xiaozu was promoted to General Who Conquers the Champions, given temporary credentials as supervisor of vanguard forces, and sent to Tiger Cage to face the southern rebels. The imperial armory held tube-sleeve armor and caps of the Zhuge Liang pattern, proof even against a twenty-five-stone crossbow. The emperor gave them all to Xiaozu. Confident in his own loyalty, Xiaozu bullied the other generals. Where capital troops had fathers, sons, or brothers serving with the rebels in the south, he wanted them all investigated and punished. Men's hearts turned from him, and none were willing to fight wholeheartedly under his command. He was further promoted to bearer of the staff of authority, supervisor of military affairs in Yan, Qing, Ji, and You, and General Who Pacifies the Army, while retaining his inspectorship. The rebels held Zheqi, and Xiaozu was about to attack. When he took leave of Grand Commander Wang Xuanmo, he was overcome with grief. All who saw it were astonished. On the third day of the third month of the second year of Taishi he met the rebels in battle, always keeping his drum and battle canopy with him. Men in the ranks said to one another, "Commander Yin might as well be dead already. He is in the thick of the fight yet flaunts his ceremonial regalia. If ten good archers shoot at once, how can he fail to fall?" " That day he was struck by an arrow in the battle line and died at the age of fifty-two. He was posthumously honored as attendant-in-ordinary at the palace secretariat and General Who Campaigns North, retaining his credentials as bearer of the staff and his supervisory command. He was enfeoffed as Marquis of Zigui with a fief of one thousand households. In the fourth year his fief was changed posthumously to Jian'an county, with the posthumous title Loyal Marquis. All of Xiaozu's sons were killed by Xue Andu, and his cousin's son Huida inherited the title. When Qi received the abdication, the fief was abolished.
5
劉勔，字伯猷，彭城人也。 祖懷義，始興太守。 父穎之，汝南、新蔡二郡太守，征林邑，遇疾卒。 勔少有志節，兼好文義。 家貧，為廣州增城令，廣州刺史劉道錫引為揚烈府主簿。 元嘉二十七年，索虜南侵，道錫遣勔奉使詣京都，太祖引見之，酬對稱旨，除寧遠將軍、綏遠太守。 元嘉末，蕭簡據廣州為亂，勔起義討之，燒其南門。 廣州刺史宗愨又命為軍府主簿，以功封大亭侯。 除員外散騎侍郎。 孝建初，荊、江反叛，宗愨以勔行寧朔將軍、湘東內史，領軍出安陸。 會事平，以本號為晉康太守，又徙鬱林太守。 大明初還都，徐州刺史劉道隆請為寧朔司馬。 竟陵王誕據廣陵為逆，勔隨道隆受沈慶之節度，事平，封金城縣五等侯。 除西陽王子尚撫軍參軍，入直閣。 先是，遣費沈伐陳檀，不克，乃除勔龍驤將軍、西江督護、鬱林太守。 勔既至，率軍進討，隨宜翦定，大致名馬，并獻珊瑚連理樹，上甚悅。 還除新安王子鸞撫軍中兵參軍，遭母憂，不拜。 前廢帝即位，起為振威將軍、屯騎校尉，入直閣。
Liu Mian, courtesy name Boyou, came from Pengcheng. His grandfather Liu Huaiyi had been administrator of Shixing. His father Liu Yingzhi had been administrator of Runan and Xincai. On campaign against Linyi he fell ill and died. From youth Mian showed resolve and integrity and also loved literary learning. His family was poor. He served as magistrate of Zengcheng in Guangzhou until Inspector Liu Daoxi took him on as chief clerk of the Yanglie headquarters. In the twenty-seventh year of Yuanjia, when the Northern Wei invaded the south, Daoxi sent Mian to the capital on embassy. Emperor Wen received him in audience and was pleased with his replies. He was appointed General Who Pacifies the Distant and administrator of Suiyuan. At the end of Yuanjia, Xiao Jian seized Guangzhou in revolt. Mian raised loyal troops against him and burned the southern gate. Inspector Zong Que of Guangzhou again made him chief clerk of the army headquarters, and for his merit he was enfeoffed as Marquis of Dating. He was appointed supernumerary gentleman attendant at the palace secretariat. Early in the Xiaojian era, when Jing and Jiang rebelled, Zong Que had Mian serve as General Who Pacifies the North and interior minister of Xiangdong, leading troops out from Anlu. When the revolt was settled, he was made administrator of Jinkang under his existing rank, then transferred to Yulin. Early in the Daming era he returned to the capital, and Inspector Liu Daolong of Xuzhou asked to have him as his chief of staff for the Pacification of the North. When the Prince of Jingling, Liu Dan, seized Guangling in revolt, Mian followed Daolong under Shen Qingzhi's command. When the revolt was crushed he was enfeoffed as fifth-rank marquis of Jincheng. He was made army staff officer to Prince Zishang of Xiyang's pacification army and entered duty in the inner pavilion. Earlier, Fei Shen had been sent against Chen Tan without success. Mian was then appointed General of the Dragon Cavalry, protector of the Western River, and administrator of Yulin. Once Mian arrived, he led his troops forward, pacifying the region as circumstances required. He obtained many fine horses and also presented a coral tree with conjoined trunks. The emperor was greatly pleased. On returning he was appointed central army staff officer to Prince Ziluan of Xin'an's pacification army, but when his mother died he declined the post. When the Former Deposed Emperor ascended the throne, Mian was recalled to serve as General Who Shakes Might and colonel of the garrison cavalry, again on duty in the inner pavilion.
6
太宗即位，加寧朔將軍，校尉如故。 江州刺史晉安王子勛為逆，四方回應，勔以本官領建平王景素輔國司馬，進據梁山。 會豫州刺史殷琰反叛，徵勔還都，假輔國將軍，率眾討琰，甲仗三十人入六門。 復兼山陽王休祐驃騎司馬，餘如故。 破琰將劉順於宛唐，杜叔寶於橫塘，事在琰傳。 除輔國將軍、山陽王休祐驃騎諮議參軍、梁郡太守、假節，不拜。 琰嬰城固守，自始春至於末冬，薛道標、龐孟虯並向壽陽，勔內攻外禦，戰無不捷。 善撫將帥，以寬厚為眾所依。 將軍王廣之求勔所自乘馬，諸將帥並忿廣之叨冒，勸勔以法裁之，勔歡笑，即時解馬與廣之。 復除使持節、督廣、交二州諸軍事、平越中郎將、廣州刺史，將軍如故，不拜。 及琰開門請降，勔約令三軍，不得妄動。 城內士民，秋毫無所失，百姓感悅，咸曰來蘇。 百姓生為立碑。 改督益、寧二州諸軍事、益州刺史，持節、將軍如故，又不拜。 還京都，拜太子左衛率，封鄱陽縣侯，食邑千戶。
When Emperor Ming ascended the throne, he was given the additional rank of General Who Pacifies the North while retaining his colonelcy. When Prince Zixun of Jin'an, inspector of Jiangzhou, rebelled and the empire answered him, Mian retained his existing post while also serving as chief of staff to the state-assisting army of Prince Jingsu of Jianping, advancing to hold Liangshan. When Yin Yan, inspector of Yuzhou, rebelled, Mian was recalled to the capital, given temporary rank as General Who Assists the State, and sent to attack him with thirty armed men admitted through the Six Gates. He also served concurrently as chief of staff to the rapid cavalry of Prince Xiuyou of Shanyang, his other posts unchanged. He defeated Yan's generals Liu Shun at Wantang and Du Shubao at Hengtang; the account is given in Yin Yan's biography. He was offered the posts of General Who Assists the State, staff adviser to Prince Xiuyou's rapid cavalry, and administrator of Liang, with temporary credentials—but he declined. Yan held the city from early spring to late winter. Xue Daobiao and Pang Mengjiao both marched on Shouyang. Mian attacked within and defended without, winning every engagement. He was skilled at winning over his officers and relied on generosity and breadth of spirit to hold their loyalty. General Wang Guangzhi asked for Mian's own mount. The other commanders resented his presumption and urged Mian to punish him. Mian only laughed and at once gave him the horse. He was again offered the posts of bearer of the staff, supervisor of military affairs in Guang and Jiao, General Who Pacifies the Yue, and inspector of Guangzhou, while retaining his general's rank—but again he declined. When Yan opened the gates to surrender, Mian bound the three armies by strict order not to act rashly. The people in the city lost not a hair's breadth of their property. Grateful and rejoicing, they said the savior had come again. In his lifetime the people erected a monument to him. He was offered supervision of military affairs in Yi and Ning and the inspectorship of Yi, retaining his credentials and general's rank—but again he declined. On returning to the capital he was appointed left commandant of the crown prince's guard and enfeoffed as Marquis of Poyang with a fief of one thousand households.
7
琰初求救索虜，虜大眾屯據汝南。 泰始三年，以勔為征虜將軍、督西討前鋒諸軍事，假節、置佐、本官如故。 先是，常珍奇據汝南，與琰為逆，琰降，因據戍降虜，事在琰傳。 至是引虜西河公、長社公攻圍輔國將軍、汝陰太守張景遠。 景遠與軍主楊文萇拒擊，大破之。 景遠尋病卒，太宗嘉其功，追贈冠軍將軍、豫州刺史，追封含洭縣男，食邑三百戶，以文萇代為汝陰太守。 除勔右衛將軍，仍以為使持節、都督豫、司二州諸軍事、征虜將軍、豫州刺史，餘如故。 四年，除侍中，領射聲校尉，又不受。 進號右將軍。 其年，虜遣汝陽司馬趙懷仁步騎五百，寇武津縣。 勔遣龍驤將軍曲元德輕兵進討，虜眾驚散。 虜子都公閼於拔又率三百人防運車囗囗千兩，於汝陽臺東水上結營。 元德單騎直入，斬拔首，因進攻汝陽臺，即陷外壘，獲車一千三百乘，斬首一百五十級。 勔又使司徒參軍孫曇瓘督弋陽以西，會虜寇義陽，曇瓘大破之。 虜上其北豫州租，有車二千兩，勔招荒人，邀擊於許昌，虜眾奔散，焚燒米穀。
Yan had first sought aid from the Northern Wei, and a great barbarian host now held Runan. In the third year of Taishi, Mian was appointed General Who Campaigns Against the Barbarians and supervisor of the western punitive vanguard, with temporary credentials and a full staff, while retaining his existing posts. Earlier, Chang Zhenqi had held Runan and rebelled alongside Yin Yan. When Yan surrendered, Zhenqi held the garrison and went over to the barbarians; the account is in Yin Yan's biography. He now brought in the barbarian princes of Xihe and Changshe to attack and besiege General Who Assists the State Zhang Jingyuan, administrator of Ruyin. Jingyuan and army commander Yang Wenchang met them in battle and won a crushing victory. Jingyuan soon died of illness. Emperor Ming praised his achievement and posthumously honored him as General Who Conquers the Champions and inspector of Yuzhou, enfeoffing him as Baron of Hanqiu with three hundred households. Yang Wenchang succeeded him as administrator of Ruyin. Mian was appointed General of the Right Guard and also made bearer of the staff, supervisor of military affairs in Yu and Si, General Who Campaigns Against the Barbarians, and inspector of Yuzhou, his other posts unchanged. In the fourth year he was offered the posts of palace attendant and colonel of the archers who shoot at sound, but again he declined. His rank was advanced to General of the Right. That year the barbarians sent Zhao Huairen, chief of staff of Ruyang, with five hundred infantry and cavalry to raid Wujin county. Mian sent General of the Dragon Cavalry Qu Yuande with light troops against them, and the barbarian force scattered in alarm. The barbarian prince of the capital district, Yan Yu Ba, again led three hundred men to guard transport wagons—one thousand three hundred loads, by parallel accounts—and encamped on the east bank at Ruyang Terrace. Yuande charged in alone on horseback, beheaded Ba, then attacked Ruyang Terrace, took the outer rampart at once, captured thirteen hundred wagons, and took one hundred fifty heads. Mian also sent secretariat staff officer Sun Tanhuan to command the region west of Yiyang. When the barbarians raided Yiyang, Tanhuan won a crushing victory. The barbarians were moving the tax grain of Northern Yuzhou in two thousand wagons. Mian rallied frontier people and ambushed them at Xuchang. The barbarian force fled in disorder, and the grain was burned.

Jia Yuanyou of Huaixi memorialized Emperor Ming urging a northern attack on Xuanhu that would recover Chen, Nandun, Runan, and Xincai. The emperor showed the memorial to Mian and ordered him to reply point by point. Mian replied:

元友稱：「虜主幼弱，姦偽競起，內外規亂，天亡有期。 」臣以為獯醜侵縱，乘藉王境，盤據州郡，百姓殘亡。 去冬眾軍失耕，今春連城圍逼，國家復境之略，實有不遑，滅虜未及。 元友又云：「有七千餘家，穀米豐積，可供二萬人數年資儲。 」臣又以為二萬人歲食米四十八萬斛，五年合須米二百四十萬斛，既理不容有，恐事難稱言。 元友又云：「虜於懸瓠開驛保，虜已先據，若不足恃，此不須囗。 」俱是攻城，便應先圖懸瓠，何更越先取郾，以受腹背之災。 且七千餘家豐積，而虜猶當遠運為糧，是威不制民，民非異計。 元友又云：「虜欲水陸運糧，以救軍命，可襲之機，在於今日。 」臣又以為開立驛道，據守堅城，觀其形候，不似蹙弱。 可乘之機，恐為難驗。 元友又云：「四郡民人，遭虜二十七年之毒，皆欲雪仇報恥，伏待朝威。 」臣又以為垣式寶等受國重恩，今猶驅略車營，翻還就賊，蓋是戀本之情深，非報怨之宜，何可輕試。 元友又云：「請敕荊、雍兩州，遣二千精兵，從義陽依西山北下，直據郾城。 」臣又以為郾城是賊驛路要戍，且經蠻接險，數百里中，裹糧潛進，方出平地，攻賊堅城，自古名將，未有能以此濟者。 假其克捷，不知足南抗懸瓠，北捍長社與不？ 且賊擁據數城，水陸通便，而今使官以二千斷其資運，於事為難。 元友又云：「虜圍逼汝陰，遊魂二歲，為張景遠所挫，不敢渡淮。 」臣又以為景遠兵力寡弱，不能自固，遠遣救援，方得少克。 今定是為賊所畏不？ 景遠前所摧傷，裁至數百，虜步騎四萬，猶不敢前，而今必勸國家以輕兵遠討，指掌可克，言理相背，莫復過此。 元友又云：「龍山雉水，魯奴、王景直等並受朝爵，馬步萬餘。 進討之宜，唯須敕命。 」臣以為魯奴與虜交關，彌歷年世，去歲送誠朝廷，誓欲立功。 自蒙榮爵，便即逃遁，殊類姦猾，豈易闇期。 兼王景直是一亡命，部曲不過數十人，既不可言，又未足恃。 萬餘之言，似不近實。 元友又云：「四郡恨忿此非類，車營連結，廢田二載，生業已盡，賊無所資，糧儲已罄。 斷其運道，最是要略。 」臣又以斷運須兵，兵應資食，而當此過懸瓠二百里中，使兵食兼足，何處求辦？
Yuanyou claims: "The barbarian ruler is young and weak, treachery and deceit abound, turmoil is plotted within and without, and heaven's destruction of them is only a matter of time." " I hold that the barbarians invade wantonly, exploiting our royal domain, entrenched in provinces and commanderies, while the people are slaughtered and ruined. Last winter our armies missed the planting season. This spring linked cities are under siege. The state's strategy of recovering lost territory truly allows no leisure for destroying the barbarians—not yet. Yuanyou also says: "There are more than seven thousand households with grain abundantly stored, enough to supply twenty thousand men for several years." " I further calculate that twenty thousand men consume four hundred eighty thousand hu of rice a year; over five years that would be two million four hundred thousand hu. Reason cannot allow such stores, and I fear the claim cannot be squared with fact. Yuanyou also says: "The barbarians have opened relay stations and forts at Xuanhu and already hold them—if those cannot be relied upon, there is no need to worry about this point [text defective]." " Both operations are sieges, so we ought to take Xuanhu first. Why leap ahead to seize Yan and invite disaster from front and rear? Moreover, if more than seven thousand households had such abundance, the barbarians would not still be hauling grain from afar. That shows their authority does not control the people, and the people are not truly on their side. Yuanyou also says: "The barbarians plan to move grain by land and water to sustain their army. The moment to strike is now." " I further observe that they have opened relay roads and hold strong cities. Judging by their bearing, they do not look pressed or weak. Any opportunity to exploit them will be hard to verify in advance. Yuanyou also says: "The people of the four commanderies have suffered under the barbarians for twenty-seven years. They all long to avenge their wrongs and await the court's authority." " I further note that Yuan Shibao and others received great favor from the state, yet still raid wagon camps and return to the enemy. That shows deep attachment to their homeland, not a will to revenge. How can we lightly trust them? Yuanyou also asks that Jing and Yong be ordered to send two thousand elite troops from Yiyang along the western hills northward to seize Yancheng directly. " Yancheng is a vital post on the enemy relay route, and the way passes through barbarian country and rugged terrain. To march several hundred li with bundled grain in secret, then emerge onto open ground and assault a strong enemy city—no famous general of old ever succeeded by such a plan. Even if they won, who knows whether they could hold Xuanhu in the south and Changshe in the north? Moreover the enemy holds several cities with easy land and water communications. To cut their supply lines with only two thousand men is impracticable. Yuanyou also says: "The barbarians besieged Ruyin for two years like lost souls until Zhang Jingyuan checked them, and they did not dare cross the Huai." " Jingyuan's force was small and weak and could not hold on its own. Only when distant relief arrived did he win a modest success. Are they now truly feared by the enemy? Jingyuan's earlier blows killed only a few hundred. Forty thousand barbarian horse and foot still would not advance—yet now he urges the state to campaign far with light troops and promises victory at a gesture. Nothing could be more contrary to reason. Yuanyou also says: "At Longshan and Zhishui, Lu Nu, Wang Jingzhi, and others all hold court titles and command more than ten thousand horse and foot." They need only an imperial order to advance. " Lu Nu has dealt with the barbarians for many years. Last year he offered loyalty to the court and swore to win merit. Once he received an honored title he fled at once. Such men are treacherous beyond reckoning. How can we count on them in secret? Wang Jingzhi is only a fugitive with a few dozen followers. He is scarcely worth mentioning and cannot be relied upon. The claim of more than ten thousand men seems far from fact. Yuanyou also says: "The four commanderies hate these alien invaders. Their wagon camps are linked, fields lie waste for two years, livelihoods are gone, the enemy has no resources, and grain stores are empty." Cutting their supply routes is the essential strategy. " Cutting supply lines requires troops, and troops require food. In two hundred li of country beyond Xuanhu, where are we to obtain both men and provisions?
10
臣竊尋元嘉以來，傖荒遠人，多干國議，負儋歸闕，皆勸討虜。 魯爽誕說，實挫國威，徒失兵力，虛費金寶。 凡此之徒，每規近說，從來信納，皆詒後悔。 界上之人，唯視強弱，王師至境，必壺漿候塗，裁見退軍，便抄截蜂起。 首領回師，何嘗不為河畔所弊。
I have reviewed affairs since the Yuanjia era: frontier and northern peoples have often meddled in state policy, coming to court laden with arguments, all urging campaigns against the barbarians. The schemes urged by Lu Shuang and Liu Dan truly damaged national prestige, wasting troops and treasure to no purpose. Such men always urge immediate action. Whenever the court has believed them, it has later regretted it. Border people watch only which side is strong. When the imperial army arrives they bring broth to the road, but the moment they see troops withdraw, raiders swarm up like bees. When commanders withdraw, when have they not been harried along the riverbanks?
11
太宗納之，元友議遂寢。 勔與常珍奇書，勸令反虜，珍奇乃與子超越、羽林監式寶，於譙殺虜子都公費拔等凡三千餘人。 勔馳驛以聞，太宗大喜，以珍奇為使持節、都督司、北豫二州諸軍事、平北將軍、司州刺史，汝南、新蔡縣侯，食邑千戶； 超越輔國將軍、北豫州刺史，潁川、汝陽、囗囗三郡太守，安陽縣男； 式寶輔國將軍、陳、南頓二郡太守，真陽縣男，食邑三百戶。 珍奇為虜所攻，引軍南出，虜追擊破之，珍奇走依山，得至壽陽，超越、式寶為人所殺。
Emperor Ming accepted Mian's view, and Yuanyou's proposal was set aside. Mian wrote to Chang Zhenqi urging him to turn against the barbarians. Zhenqi then joined his son Chaoyue and feathered forest inspector Shibao at Qiao in killing the barbarian prince of the capital district, Fei Ba, and more than three thousand others. Mian reported by relay horse. Emperor Ming was overjoyed and made Zhenqi bearer of the staff, supervisor of military affairs in Si and Northern Yu, General Who Pacifies the North, and inspector of Sizhou, enfeoffing him as Marquis of Runan and Xincai with a fief of one thousand households; Chaoyue as General Who Assists the State and inspector of Northern Yu, administrator of Yingchuan, Ruyang, and two other commanderies named in the text but now lost, and Baron of Anyang; Shibao as General Who Assists the State and administrator of Chen and Nandun, Baron of Zhenyang with a fief of three hundred households. The barbarians attacked Zhenqi, who led his army south. They pursued and defeated him. Zhenqi fled into the hills and reached Shouyang, but Chaoyue and Shibao were killed.
12
五年，汝陰太守楊文萇又頻破虜於荊亭及戍西。 詔進勔號平西將軍、豫州刺史，餘如故，不拜。 其年，徵拜散騎常侍、中領軍。 勔以世路糾紛，有懷止足，求東陽郡。 上以勔啟遍示朝臣，自尚書僕射袁粲以下，莫不稱讚，咸謂宜許。 上曰：「巴陵、建平二王，並有獨往之志。 若世道寧晏，皆當申其所請。 」勔經始鍾嶺之南，以為棲息，聚石蓄水，仿佛丘中，朝士愛素者，多往遊之。 六年，改常侍為侍中。 其年，南兗州刺史齊王出鎮淮陰，以勔為使持節、都督南徐、兗、青、冀、囗五州諸軍事、平北將軍，侍中、中領軍如故，出鎮廣陵。 固辭侍中、軍號，許之，以為假平北將軍。 七年，解都督、假號、并節。 太宗臨崩，顧命以為守尚書右僕射，中領軍如故，給鼓吹一部。 廢帝即位，加兵五百人。
In the fifth year, Yang Wenchang, administrator of Ruyin, again defeated the barbarians repeatedly at Jingting and west of the garrison. An edict advanced Mian to General Who Pacifies the West and inspector of Yuzhou, his other posts unchanged, but he declined. That year he was summoned to serve as attendant-in-ordinary at the palace secretariat and general of the palace guards. Troubled by the turmoil of the times, Mian wished to know when enough was enough and asked for Dongyang commandery. The emperor showed Mian's memorial to the whole court. From Vice Director Yuan Can downward, all praised it and said his request should be granted. The emperor said: "The princes of Baling and Jianping both have the will to withdraw from public life. When the times grow peaceful, each of them should have his request granted." " Mian laid out a retreat south of Zhongling, piling stones and storing water in the manner of a hill hermitage. Court gentlemen who loved simplicity often visited him there. In the sixth year his title of attendant-in-ordinary was changed to palace attendant. That year the Prince of Qi, inspector of Southern Yanzhou, went out to garrison Huaiyin. Mian was made bearer of the staff, supervisor of military affairs in Southern Xu, Yan, Qing, Ji, and You, General Who Pacifies the North, retaining his posts as palace attendant and general of the palace guards, and went out to garrison Guangling. He firmly declined the posts of palace attendant and full general. The court agreed and made him Acting General Who Pacifies the North. In the seventh year he was relieved of his supervisory command, acting rank, and staff of authority. On his deathbed Emperor Ming entrusted him with the acting post of right vice director of the secretariat, retaining his command of the palace guards, and granted him a full set of martial music. When the Deposed Emperor ascended the throne, five hundred troops were added to his guard.
13
元徽初，月犯右執法，太白犯上將，或勸勔解職。 勔曰：「吾執心行己，無愧幽明。 若才輕任重，災眚必及，天道密微，避豈得免。 」桂陽王休範為亂，奄至京邑，加勔使持節、領軍，置佐史，鎮扞石頭。 既而賊眾屯朱雀航南，右軍王道隆率宿衛向朱雀，聞賊已至，急信召勔。 勔至，命閉航，道隆不聽，催勔渡航進戰。 率所領於航南戰敗，臨陳死之，時年五十七。 事平，詔曰：「夫義實天經，忠惟人則，篆素流采，金石宣輝，自非識洞情靈，理感生極，豈有捐軀衛主，捨命匡朝者哉！ 故持節、鎮軍將軍、守尚書右僕射、中領軍鄱陽縣開國侯勔，思懷亮粹，體業淹明，弘勳樹績，譽洽華野。 綢繆顧託，契闊屯夷，方倚謀猷，翌康帝道。 逆蕃扇禍，逼擾京甸，援桴誓旅，奉律行師。 身與事滅，名隨操遠。 朕用傷悼，震慟於厥心。 昔王允秉誠，卞壼峻節，均風往德，歸茂先軌。 泉途就永，冤逝無追，思崇徽策，式光惇史。 可贈散騎常侍、司空，本官、侯如故，諡曰忠昭公。」
Early in the Yuanhui era, the moon crossed the Right Enforcer of Law and Venus crossed the Great General. Some urged Mian to resign. Mian said: "In heart and conduct I have nothing to fear before the living or the dead. If my talent is slight for my burden, calamity will find me. Heaven's way is subtle—how can fleeing avoid it?" " When the Prince of Guiyang, Liu Xiufan, rebelled and suddenly reached the capital, Mian was given the staff of authority and command of the palace guards, with a full staff, and garrisoned Stone Citadel. Soon the rebels encamped south of the Zhuque Floating Bridge. General of the Right Wang Daolong led the palace guard toward Zhuque and, hearing the enemy had arrived, urgently summoned Mian. When Mian arrived he ordered the bridge closed. Daolong refused and pressed Mian to cross and give battle. Leading his troops he was defeated south of the bridge and died in the fighting line at the age of fifty-seven. When the revolt was crushed, an edict said: "Righteousness is heaven's constant and loyalty mankind's rule. They shine in silk and stone. Unless one's spirit is moved to the utmost, who would give his body to guard his sovereign and his life to set the dynasty aright? The late bearer of the staff, General Who Pacifies the Army, acting right vice director of the secretariat, general of the palace guards, and founding marquis of Poyang, Liu Mian, was bright and pure in mind, accomplished in person and office, built great merit, and won fame throughout the realm. Intimately entrusted at the emperor's deathbed, bound to him through hardship and turmoil, he was just then relied on to counsel and secure the imperial way. The rebel fanned disaster and pressed upon the capital. He took up the drum, pledged his troops, and marched under discipline to meet the enemy. His body perished with the cause, but his name follows his conduct into the distance. We grieve and are shaken to the depths of Our heart. Of old Wang Yun held fast to loyalty and Bian Hu to stern integrity. Mian matches their ancient virtue and returns honor to their path. The road to the springs is forever; his wrongful death cannot be undone. We would exalt him with honored titles and let his glory shine in the histories. Let him be posthumously honored as attendant-in-ordinary at the palace secretariat and Minister of Works, his original offices and marquisate unchanged, with the posthumous title Loyal and Illustrious Duke."
14
子悛嗣，順帝升明末，為廣州刺史。 齊受禪，國除。 勔弟斅，泰始中，為寧朔將軍、交州刺史，於道遇病卒。 先有都鄉侯爵，諡曰質侯。
His son Juan succeeded him and, at the end of the Shengming era of Emperor Shun, served as inspector of Guangzhou. When Qi received the abdication, the fief was abolished. Mian's younger brother Jiao served in the Taishi era as General Who Pacifies the North and inspector of Jiaozhou but fell ill on the road and died. He had earlier held the rank of marquis of the capital district, with the posthumous title Marquis of Integrity.
15
史臣曰：吳漢平蜀，城內流血沾踝，而其後無聞於漢； 陸抗定西陵，步氏禍及嬰孩，而機、雲為戮上國。 劉勔克壽春，士民無遺芻委粒之歎； 莫不扶老攜幼，歌唱而出重圍，美矣！
The historian writes: When Wu Han pacified Shu, blood in the city reached men's ankles, yet afterward Han heard no more of it; when Lu Kang secured Xiling, the Bu clan's punishment reached even infants in arms, yet Lu Ji and Lu Yun were executed in the upper kingdom. When Liu Mian took Shouchun, officers and people had no cause to lament scattered fodder and grain; all came out of the siege supporting the old and leading the young, singing as they went—how fine a deed!
