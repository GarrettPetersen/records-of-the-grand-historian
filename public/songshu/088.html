1
列傳第四十八薛安都沈文秀崔道固
Biographies 48: Xue Andou, Shen Wenxiu, and Cui Daogu
2
薛安都，河東汾陰人也。 世為強族，同姓有三千家。 父廣，為宗豪，高祖定關、河，以為上黨太守。 安都少以勇聞，身長七尺八寸，便弓馬。 索虜使助秦州刺史北賀汨擊反胡白龍子，滅之。 由是為偽雍、秦二州都統，州各有刺史，都統總其事。 元嘉二十一年，索虜主拓跋燾擊芮芮大敗，安都與宗人薛永宗起義，永宗營汾曲，安都襲得弘農。 會北地人蓋吳起兵，遂連衡相應。 燾自率眾擊永宗，滅其族，進擊蓋吳。 安都料眾寡不敵，率壯士辛靈度等，棄弘農歸國。 太祖延見之，求北還構扇河、陝，招聚義眾。 上許之，給錦百匹，雜繒三百匹。 復襲弘農，虜已增戍，城不可克，蓋吳又死，乃退還上洛。 世祖鎮襄陽，板為揚武將軍、北弘農太守。 虜漸強盛，安都乃歸襄陽。 從叔沈亦同歸國，官至綏遠將軍、新野太守。
Xue Andou was a native of Fenyin in Hedong. His family had been a powerful clan for generations, with three thousand households of the same surname. His father Guang was a leader of the clan; when the Founding Emperor secured Guan and the Yellow River lands, he appointed him Administrator of Shangdang. Andou was famed from youth for his bravery. He stood seven feet eight inches tall and was expert with bow and horse. The Northern Wei sent him to aid Qinzhou Inspector Bei Hewen in crushing the rebel Hu Bailongzi. He was then made overall commander of the puppet Yong and Qin provinces, each with its own inspector while he held supreme command. In Yuanjia year 21, after Tuoba Tao of the Northern Wei suffered a crushing defeat against the Rouran, Andou joined his kinsman Xue Yongzong in revolt. Yongzong camped along the Fen River bend while Andou took Hongnong by surprise. When Gai Wu of Beidi rose in arms, they allied and acted in concert. Tao led his army in person against Yongzong, wiped out his clan, and marched on Gai Wu. Seeing his forces outmatched, Andou led brave men such as Xin Lingdu, gave up Hongnong, and defected to Song. The Founding Emperor granted him an audience. Andou asked to return north to raise rebellion in the Yellow River and Shaanxi regions and rally loyal forces. The emperor agreed and gave him a hundred bolts of brocade and three hundred bolts of assorted silks. He struck at Hongnong again, but the enemy had reinforced the garrison and the city would not fall. Gai Wu had also died, so he fell back to Shangluo. When the Heir Apparent held Xiangyang, Andou was appointed on his staff as General Who Raises Martiality and Administrator of Northern Hongnong. As the Northern Wei grew stronger, Andou withdrew to Xiangyang. His cousin Shen defected with him and eventually reached the rank of General Who Pacifies the Distance and Administrator of Xinye.
3
二十七年，隨王誕版安都為建武將軍，隨柳元景向關、陝，率步騎居前，所向克捷，事在元景傳。 軍還，誕版為後軍行參軍。 二十九年，除始興王濬征北行參軍，加建武將軍。 魯爽向虎牢，安都復隨元景北出，即據關城，期俱濟河取蒲阪。 會爽退，安都復率所領隨元景引還。 仍伐西陽五水蠻。
In year 27, Prince Dan of Sui commissioned Andou as General Who Establishes Martiality. With Liu Yuanjing he advanced on Guan and Shaanxi at the head of the infantry and cavalry and carried every fight—the full account is in Yuanjing's biography. After the campaign, Dan appointed him acting staff officer of the rear army. In year 29 he was made acting staff officer on Prince Jun of Shixing's northern campaign, with the additional rank of General Who Establishes Martiality. When Lu Shuang marched on Hulao, Andou again went north with Yuanjing, seized Tong Pass at once, and planned to cross the river together and take Puban. When Shuang retreated, Andou led his troops back with Yuanjing. He then campaigned against the Five-Water Man tribes of Xiyang.
4
世祖伐逆，轉參軍事，加寧朔將軍，領馬軍，與柳元景俱發。 四月十四日，至朱雀航，橫矛瞋目，叱賊將皇甫安民等曰：「賊弑君父，何心事之！ 」世祖踐阼，除右軍將軍。 五月四日，率所領騎為前鋒，直入殿庭。 賊尚有數百人，一時奔散。 以功封南鄉縣男，食邑五百戶。 安都從征關、陝，至臼口，夢仰頭視天，正見天門開，謂左右曰：「汝見天門開不？ 」至是歎曰：「夢天開，乃中興之象邪！」
When the Heir Apparent marched against the usurper, Andou was transferred to staff duty, promoted to General Who Pacifies the North, and given command of the cavalry. He set out with Liu Yuanjing. On the fourteenth day of the fourth month they reached Zhuque Ford. Andou levelled his spear, glared, and shouted at the rebel generals Huangfu Anmin and the rest: "These rebels murdered their lord and father—why do you serve them? " When the Heir Apparent took the throne, Andou was appointed General of the Right Army. On the fourth day of the fifth month he led his cavalry as vanguard straight into the palace courtyard. Several hundred rebels still held the ground, but they broke and fled at once. For his service he was enfeoffed as Baron of Nanxiang with a fief of five hundred households. On the Guan-Shaanxi campaign, at Jiukou, Andou dreamed he looked up and saw the gates of heaven open. He asked his attendants: "Do you see the gates of heaven open? " Now he sighed and said: "A dream of heaven opening—surely that is an omen of dynastic renewal!"
5
從弟道生，亦以軍功為大司馬參軍。 犯罪，為秣陵令庾淑之所鞭。 安都大怒，乃乘馬從數十人，令左右執矟，欲往殺淑之。 行至朱雀航，逢柳元景。 元景遙問：「薛公何處去？ 」安都躍馬至車後曰：「小子庾淑之鞭我從弟，今詣往刺殺之。 」元景慮其不可駐，乃紿之曰：「小子無宜適，卿往與手，甚快。 」安都既回馬，復追呼之：「別宜與卿有所論。 」令下馬入車。 既入車，因責讓之曰：「卿從弟服章言論，與寒細不異，雖復人士，庾淑之亦何由得知？ 且人身犯罪，理應加罰，卿為朝廷勳臣，宜崇奉法憲，云何放恣，輒欲於都邑殺人？ 非唯科律所不容，主上亦無辭以相宥。 」因載之俱歸，安都乃止。 其年，以憚直免官。
His cousin Daosheng had likewise won a post as staff officer to the Grand Marshal through military service. He broke the law and was flogged by Yu Shuzhi, magistrate of Moling. Andou flew into a rage, mounted his horse with several dozen followers, had his men take spears, and set out to kill Shuzhi. At Zhuque Ford he met Liu Yuanjing. Yuanjing called from a distance: "Lord Xue, where are you bound? " Andou spurred up to the rear of the carriage and said: "That whelp Yu Shuzhi flogged my cousin. I am on my way to kill him. " Yuanjing feared he could not be stopped, so he deceived him: "The wretch had it coming. If you go and strike him yourself, all the better. " Andou had already wheeled about when Yuanjing called after him: "Wait—I have something else to say to you. " He told him to dismount and get into the carriage. Once Andou was inside, Yuanjing rebuked him: "Your cousin's dress and speech are no better than a commoner's. Even if he were a gentleman, how was Yu Shuzhi to know? Besides, when a man breaks the law he deserves punishment. You are a meritorious servant of the throne and ought to uphold the statutes. Why give way to rage and try to kill a man in the capital? The law will not tolerate it, and the emperor will have no grounds to spare you. " He took him back in the carriage, and Andou abandoned his plan. That year he was removed from office for his blunt fearlessness.
6
孝建元年，復除左軍將軍。 二月，魯爽反叛，遣安都及冗從僕射胡子反、龍驤將軍宗越率步騎據歷陽。 爽遣將鄭德玄戍大峴，德玄使前鋒楊胡與輕兵向歷陽。 安都遣宗越及歷陽太守程天祚逆擊破之，斬胡與及其軍副。 德玄復使其司馬梁嚴屯峴東，安都幢主周文恭晨往偵候，因而襲之，悉禽； 賊未敢進。 世祖詔安都留三百人守歷陽，渡還採石，遷輔國將軍、竟陵內史。 四月，魯爽使弟瑜率三千人出小峴，爽尋以大眾阻大峴。 又遣安都步騎八千度江，與歷陽太守張幼緒等討爽。 安都軍副建武將軍譚金率數十騎挑戰，斬其偏帥。 幼緒恇怯，輒引軍退還，安都復還歷陽。 臧質久不至，世祖復遣沈慶之濟江督統諸軍。 爽軍食少，引退，慶之使安都率輕騎追之； 四月丙戌，及爽於小峴，爽自與腹心壯騎繼後。 譚金先薄之，不能入，安都望見爽，便躍馬大呼，直往刺之，應手而倒，左右範雙斬爽首。 爽累世梟猛，生習戰陳，咸云萬人敵。 安都單騎直入，斬之而反，時人皆云關羽之斬顏良，不是過也。 進爵為侯，增邑五百戶，并前千戶。
In Xiaojian year 1 he was reappointed General of the Left Army. In the second month Lu Shuang rebelled. The court sent Andou, Supernumerary Attendant Hu Zifan, and General Who Leads the Dragon Cavalry Zong Yue with infantry and cavalry to hold Liyang. Shuang sent his general Zheng Dexuan to garrison Great Xian. Dexuan sent the vanguard Yang Hu with light troops against Liyang. Andou sent Zong Yue and Liyang Administrator Cheng Tianzuo to meet them, routed them, and beheaded Hu Yu and his second-in-command. Dexuan sent his marshal Liang Yan to camp east of Xian. Andou's banner chief Zhou Wengong went out at dawn to scout, then attacked and took them all prisoner. The rebels did not dare advance. The emperor ordered Andou to leave three hundred men at Liyang, cross back to Caishi, and promoted him to General Who Assists the State and Interior Minister of Jingling. In the fourth month Lu Shuang sent his brother Yu with three thousand men through Little Xian while he himself blocked Great Xian with the main army. He again sent Andou with eight thousand foot and horse across the Yangzi, together with Liyang Administrator Zhang Youxu and others, to crush Shuang. Andou's deputy, General Who Establishes Martiality Tan Jin, led several dozen horsemen into the fight and killed a rebel flank commander. Youxu lost his nerve and pulled his troops back, so Andou withdrew to Liyang again. Zang Zhi was slow to arrive, so the emperor sent Shen Qingzhi across the river to take command of the armies. Shuang's army ran short of provisions and retreated. Qingzhi had Andou lead light cavalry in pursuit. On bingxu day in the fourth month they caught Shuang at Little Xian. Shuang himself followed with his picked cavalry. Tan Jin closed first but could not break through. Andou saw Shuang, spurred forward with a shout, and ran him through at a single thrust. His attendant Fan Shuang took Shuang's head. Shuang came from a line famed for fierceness and had trained in battle from boyhood. Men called him a match for ten thousand. Andou charged in alone, killed him, and rode back. Men of the day said it was no less than Guan Yu's killing of Yan Liang. He was advanced to marquis, with five hundred more households added to his fief for a total of one thousand.
7
時王玄謨距南郡王義宣、臧質於梁山，安都復領騎為支軍。 賊有水步營在蕪湖，安都遣將呂興壽率數十騎襲之，賊眾驚亂，斬首及赴水死者甚眾。 義宣遣將劉湛之及質攻玄謨，玄謨命眾軍擊之，使安都引騎出賊陣右。 譚金三歷賊陳，乘其隙縱騎突之，諸將係進。 是朝，賊馬軍發蕪湖，欲來會戰，望安都騎甚盛，隱山不敢出。 賊陣東南猶堅，安都橫擊陷之，賊遂大潰。 安都隊主劉元儒於艦中斬湛之首。 轉太子左衛率。 大明元年，虜向無鹽，東平太守劉胡出戰失利。 二月，遣安都領馬軍北討，東陽太守沈法系水軍向彭城，並受徐州刺史申坦節度。 上戒之曰：「賊若可及，便盡力殄之。 若度已回，可過河耀威而反。 」時虜已去，坦求回軍討任榛，見許。 安都當向左城，左城去滑臺二百餘里，安都以去虜鎮近，軍少不宜分行。 至東坊城，遇任榛三騎，討擒其一，餘兩騎得走。 任榛聞知，皆得逃散。 時天旱，水泉多竭，人馬疲困，不能遠追。 安都、法系並白衣領職，坦繫尚方。 任榛大抵在任城界，積世逋叛所聚，所在皆棘榛深密，難為用師，故能久自保藏，屢為民患。 安都明年復職，改封武昌縣侯，加散騎常侍。 七年，又加征虜將軍，為太子左衛率十年，終世祖世不轉。
At that time Wang Xuanmo was holding Prince Yixuan of Nanjun and Zang Zhi at Liangshan. Andou again commanded the cavalry as a flanking force. The rebels had river and land camps at Wuhu. Andou sent Lü Xingshou with several dozen horsemen to strike them. The enemy broke in panic, and a great many were killed or drowned. Yixuan sent Liu Chenzhi and Zang Zhi against Xuanmo. Xuanmo ordered a general assault and sent Andou with the cavalry to the enemy's right flank. Tan Jin rode three times through the rebel lines, then charged through a gap with his cavalry while the other generals followed up. That morning rebel cavalry set out from Wuhu to join the fight, but seeing Andou's horsemen in such strength they hid in the hills and would not come out. The enemy's southeast wing still held firm until Andou struck across and broke it. The rebels then fled in complete rout. Andou's squad chief Liu Yuanru cut off Chen's head on one of the ships. He was transferred to Left Commandant of the Heir Apparent's Guard. In Daming year 1 the Northern Wei advanced on Wuyan. Dongping Administrator Liu Hu met them in battle and was defeated. In the second month the court sent Andou north at the head of the cavalry and Dongyang Administrator Shen Faxing with the fleet toward Pengcheng, both under Xuzhou Inspector Shen Tan. The emperor warned them: "If you can catch the enemy, destroy them with all your strength. If they have already withdrawn, cross the river to show your strength and come back. " By then the enemy had already left. Tan asked to turn the army against Ren Zhen, and the request was granted. Andou was to march on Zuocheng, more than two hundred li from Huatai. He judged that they were too close to enemy garrisons and that with so few troops they should not divide their force. At Dongfangcheng they met three of Ren Zhen's horsemen, captured one, and the other two got away. When Ren Zhen heard of it, his men all scattered and escaped. A drought had dried up most of the springs. Men and horses were exhausted and could not pursue far. Andou and Faxing were reduced to holding office in plain dress, and Tan was imprisoned in the palace workshops. Ren Zhen operated chiefly in Rencheng, where generations of fugitive rebels had gathered. The country was thick with brambles and hard to campaign in, so they could hide for years and plague the people. The next year Andou was restored to office, enfeoffed as Marquis of Wuchang, and made Regular Attendant of the Scattered Cavalry. In year 7 he was again made General Who Conquers the Barbarians. He held the post of Left Commandant of the Heir Apparent's Guard for ten years and was never transferred for the rest of the emperor's reign.
8
泰始二年正月，索兒、光世竝在省，安都信催令速去，二人俱自省逃出，攜安都諸子及家累，席捲北奔。 青州刺史沈文秀、冀州刺史崔道固並皆同反。 文秀遣劉彌之、張靈慶、崔僧琁三軍，道固遣子景征、傅靈越領眾，並應安都。 彌之等南出下邳，靈越自泰山道向彭城。 時濟陰太守申闡據睢陵城起義，索兒率靈越等攻之。 安都使同黨裴祖隆守下邳城，彌之等至下邳，改計歸順，因進軍攻祖隆，僧琁不同，率所領歸安都。 索兒聞彌之有異志，舍睢陵馳赴下邳，彌之等未戰潰散，竝為索兒所執，見殺。
In the first month of Taishi year 2, Suo'er and Guangshi were both at court. Andou's letter urged them to flee at once. The two escaped from the palace, took Andou's sons and families, and fled north in haste. Qingzhou Inspector Shen Wenxiu and Jizhou Inspector Cui Daogu rebelled with him. Wenxiu sent the armies of Liu Mizhi, Zhang Lingqing, and Cui Sengqin. Daogu sent his son Jingzheng and Fu Lingyue with troops. All joined Andou. Mizhi and his force marched south from Xiapi while Lingyue advanced on Pengcheng by the Mount Tai road. Jiyin Administrator Shen Chan had risen at Suiling. Suo'er led Lingyue and the rest against him. Andou had his ally Pei Zulong hold Xiapi. When Mizhi reached the city he changed sides and attacked Zulong. Sengqin refused and marched his men back to Andou. Suo'er heard that Mizhi had turned coat, abandoned Suiling, and raced to Xiapi. Mizhi's men broke without a fight, were seized by Suo'er, and executed.
9
時太宗以申令孫為徐州，代安都。 令孫進據淮陽，密有反志，遣人告索兒曰：「欲相從順，而百口在都。 可進軍見攻，若戰敗被執，家人可得免禍。 」索兒乃遣靈越向淮陽，令孫出城，為相距之形，既而奔散，北投索兒。 索兒使令孫說闡令降，闡既降，索兒執闡及令孫，並殺之。 索兒因引軍渡淮，軍糧不給，掠奔百姓穀食。 太宗遣齊王率前將軍張永、寧朔將軍垣山寶、王寬、員外散騎侍郎張寘震、蕭順之、龍驤將軍張季和、黃文玉等諸軍北討。 其年五月，軍次平原，索兒等率馬步五千，列陳距戰，擊大破之。 索兒又虜掠民穀，固守石樑，齊王又率鎮北參軍趙曇之、呂湛之擊之。 索兒軍無資實，所資野掠，既見攻逼，無以自守，於是奔散； 又追破之於葛家白鵠。 索兒走向樂平縣界，為申令孫子孝叔所斬。 安都子道智、大將范雙走向合肥，詣南汝陰太守裴季降。
Emperor Ming then appointed Shen Lingsun Inspector of Xuzhou in Andou's place. Lingsun advanced and seized Huaiyang, secretly planning to rebel. He sent word to Suo'er: "I want to join you, but my whole family is in the capital. March against me as if in earnest. If I am beaten and taken, my family may be spared. " Suo'er sent Lingyue against Huaiyang. Lingsun came out as if to fight, then broke and fled north to join Suo'er. Suo'er had Lingsun persuade Chan to surrender. When Chan yielded, Suo'er seized both Chan and Lingsun and killed them. Suo'er then crossed the Huai. His army ran short of food and seized grain from fleeing civilians. Emperor Ming sent the Prince of Qi north at the head of Forward General Zhang Yong, General Who Pacifies the North Yuan Shanbao, Wang Kuan, Supernumerary Gentleman Zhang Zhenzhen, Xiao Shunzhi, General Who Leads the Dragon Cavalry Zhang Jihe, Huang Wenyu, and the rest. That May the army halted at Pingyuan. Suo'er led five thousand horse and foot into battle and was routed. Suo'er plundered grain again and held Shiliang. The Prince of Qi led his staff officers Zhao Tanzhi and Lü Zhanzhi against him. Suo'er's army had no real stores and lived by raiding the countryside. Once hard pressed, they could not hold and broke apart. They were pursued and defeated again at Gejia Baihe. Suo'er fled toward Leping county and was beheaded by Shen Lingsun's son Xiaoshu. Andou's sons Daozhi and the great general Fan Shuang fled to Hefei and surrendered to Southern Ruyin Administrator Pei Ji.
10
時武衛將軍王廣之領軍隸劉勔，攻殷琰於壽陽。 傅靈越奔逃，為廣之軍人所生禽，厲聲曰：「我傅靈越也。 汝得賊何不即殺？ 」生送詣勔，勔躬自慰勞，詰其叛逆。 對曰：「九州唱義，豈獨在我。 」勔又問：「四方阻逆，無戰不禽，主上皆加以曠蕩，即其才用。 卿何不早歸天闕，乃逃命草間乎？ 」靈越答曰：「薛公舉兵淮北，威震天下，不能專任智勇，委付子侄，致敗之由，實在於此。 然事之始末，備皆參豫，人生歸於一死，實無面求活。 」勔壯其意，送還京師。 太宗欲加原宥，靈越辭對如一，終不回改，乃殺之。 靈越，清河人也。 時輔國將軍、山陽內史程天祚據郡同安都，攻圍彌時，然後歸順。
At that time Martial Guard General Wang Guangzhi commanded troops under Liu Xu and besieged Yin Yan at Shouyang. Fu Lingyue fled but was taken alive by Guangzhi's men. He shouted: "I am Fu Lingyue. You have your rebel—why not kill me now? " He was sent alive to Xu, who questioned him personally about his revolt. He answered: "The whole realm rose in revolt. How can that be laid on me alone? " Xu asked again: "Rebels everywhere have been beaten and taken. The emperor has been generous and used them according to their gifts. Why did you not come back to court sooner instead of hiding in the grass? " Lingyue answered: "Lord Xue raised the north of the Huai and shook the realm, yet he would not rely on the wise and brave alone but handed power to sons and nephews. That is why he was beaten. Still, I took part in it from start to finish. A man's life ends in death, and I have no face left to beg for my life. " Xu admired his spirit and sent him to the capital. Emperor Ming wished to spare him, but Lingyue answered as before and would not yield. He was executed. Lingyue was a native of Qinghe. At that time General Who Assists the State and Interior Minister of Shanyang Cheng Tianzuo had held his command with Andou. After a long siege he submitted.
11
子勛平定，安都遣別駕從事史畢眾愛、下邳太守王煥等奉啟書詣太宗歸款，曰：「臣庸隸荒萌，偷生上國，過蒙世祖孝武皇帝過常之恩，犬馬有心，實感恩遇。 是以晉安始唱，投誠孤往，不期生榮，實存死報。 今天命大歸，群迷改屬，輒率領所部，束骸待誅，違拒之罪，伏聽湯鑊。 」索兒之死也，安都使柳光世守下邳，至是亦率所領歸降。 太宗以四方已平，欲示威於淮外，遣張永、沈攸之以重軍迎之。 安都謂既已歸順，不應遣重兵，懼不免罪，乃遣信要引索虜。 三年正月，索虜遣博陵公尉遲苟人、城陽公孔伯恭二萬騎救之。 永等引退，安都開門納虜，虜即授安都徐州刺史、河東公。 四年三月，召還桑乾。 五年，死於虜中，時年六十。
After Zixun was crushed, Andou sent his registrar Bi Zhong'ai, Xiapi Administrator Wang Huan, and others to Emperor Ming with a letter of surrender: "I am a crude man from the frontier who has lived on your grace. Emperor Xiaowu favored me beyond merit, and this humble servant is truly grateful. When Jin'an first rose I went to him alone in good faith, not hoping for glory in life but ready to repay with death. Now Heaven's mandate has returned and the deluded have turned back. I lead my men to bind ourselves and await punishment. For resisting, I submit to whatever sentence you decree. " After Suo'er's death Andou had Liu Guangshi hold Xiapi. He too now surrendered with his troops. With the realm pacified, Emperor Ming wished to show his might beyond the Huai and sent Zhang Yong and Shen Youzhi with a large army to receive him. Andou thought that having submitted he should not face a large army. Fearing punishment, he sent to invite the Northern Wei. In the first month of year 3 the Northern Wei sent Duke Yuchi Gouren of Boling and Duke Kong Bogong of Chengyang with twenty thousand horsemen to his aid. Yong and the others withdrew. Andou opened the gates to the enemy, who made him Inspector of Xuzhou and Duke of Hedong. In the third month of year 4 he was summoned back to Sanggan. In year 5 he died in the north, aged sixty.
12
初，安都起兵，長史蘭陵儼密欲圖之，見殺。 安都未向桑乾，前軍將軍裴祖隆謀殺苟人，舉彭城歸順，事泄，見誅。 員外散騎侍郎孫耿之擊索兒戰死，及劉彌之、張靈慶皆戰敗見殺，並為太宗所哀，追贈儼光祿勳，祖隆寧朔將軍、兗州刺史，耿之羽林監，彌之輔國將軍、青州刺史，靈慶寧朔將軍、冀州刺史。
When Andou first rebelled, his chief clerk Lanling Yan secretly plotted against him and was put to death. Before Andou went to Sanggan, Forward General Pei Zulong plotted to kill Gouren and surrender Pengcheng. The plot was discovered and he was executed. Supernumerary Gentleman Sun Gengzhi died fighting Suo'er. Liu Mizhi and Zhang Lingqing were also killed in battle. Emperor Ming mourned them all and posthumously honored Yan as Grand Master of Splendid Happiness, Zulong as General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Yanzhou, Gengzhi as Colonel of the Forest of Feathers, Mizhi as General Who Assists the State and Inspector of Qingzhou, and Lingqing as General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Jizhou.
13
安都子伯令、環龍，亡命梁、雍二州之間。 三年，率亡命數千人襲廣平，執太守劉冥虯，攻順陽，克之，略有義成、扶風，置立守宰。 雍州刺史巴陵王休若遣南陽太守張敬兒、新野太守劉攘兵擊破之，並禽。 先是，東安、東莞二郡太守張讜守團城，在彭城東北。 始同安都，末亦歸順，太宗以為東徐州刺史，復為虜所沒。
Andou's sons Boling and Huanlong became outlaws between Liang and Yong. In year 3 they led several thousand outlaws against Guangping, seized Administrator Liu Mingqiu, took Shunyang, and briefly held Yicheng and Fufeng, appointing local officials. Yongzhou Inspector Prince Xiuruo of Baling sent Nanyang Administrator Zhang Jing'er and Xinye Administrator Liu Rangbing against them. Both brothers were defeated and captured. Earlier, Dong'an and Dongguan Administrator Zhang Chan had held Tuancheng, northeast of Pengcheng. He had first joined Andou but later submitted. Emperor Ming made him Inspector of Eastern Xuzhou, but the region was lost again to the enemy.
14
景和元年，遷督青州之東莞、東安二郡諸軍事、建威將軍、青州刺史。 時帝狂悖無道，內外憂危，文秀將之鎮，部曲出屯白下，說慶之曰：「主上狂暴如此，土崩將至，而一門受其寵任，萬物皆謂與之同心。 且此人性情無常，猜忌特甚，將來之禍，事又難測。 今因此眾力，圖之易於反掌，千載一時，萬不可失。 」慶之不從。 文秀固請非一，言輒流涕，終不回。 文秀既行，慶之果為帝所殺。 慶之死後，帝遣直閣江方興領兵誅文秀，方興未至，太宗已定亂，馳驛駐之。 方興既至，為文秀所執。 尋見釋，遣還京師。
In Yuanhe year 1 he was made supervisor of military affairs in Dongguan and Dong'an in Qingzhou, General Who Establishes Martiality, and Inspector of Qingzhou. The emperor was mad and cruel, and the court was in peril. As Wenxiu set out for his post his troops camped at Baixia. He told Qingzhi: "The sovereign is so violent that the realm will soon fall apart, yet our whole house enjoys his favor and everyone thinks us loyal to him. His temper is fickle and his suspicion extreme. The disaster ahead is impossible to foresee. With this force at hand we could overthrow him as easily as turning the hand. Such a chance comes once in a thousand years and must not be missed. " Qingzhi refused. Wenxiu pleaded again and again, weeping as he spoke, but Qingzhi would not change his mind. After Wenxiu left, Qingzhi was indeed killed by the emperor. After Qingzhi's death the emperor sent Palace Attendant Jiang Fangxing to kill Wenxiu. Before Fangxing arrived, Emperor Ming had already put down the revolt and sent orders to stop him. When Fangxing arrived, Wenxiu seized him. He was soon released and sent back to the capital.
15
時晉安王子勛據尋陽反叛，六師外討，徵兵於文秀。 文秀遣劉彌之、張靈慶、崔僧琁三軍赴朝廷。 時徐州刺史薛安都已同子勛，遣使報文秀，以四方齊舉，勸令同逆，文秀即令彌之等回應安都。 彌之等尋歸順，事在安都傳。 彌之青州強姓，門族甚多，諸宗從相合率奔北海，據城以拒文秀。 平原、樂安二郡太守王玄默據琅邪，清河、廣川二郡太守王玄邈據盤陽城，高陽、勃海二郡太守劉乘民據臨濟城，並起義。 文秀司馬房文慶謀應之，為文秀所殺。 文秀遣軍主解彥士攻北海陷之，乘民從弟伯宗合率鄉兵，復克北海，因率所領向青州所治東陽城。 文秀拒之，伯宗戰敗被創，弟天愛扶持將去，伯宗曰：「丈夫當死戰場，以身殉國，安能歸死兒女手中乎！ 弟可速去，無為兩亡。 」乃見殺，追贈龍驤將軍、長廣太守。
Prince Zixun of Jin'an held Xunyang in rebellion. The six armies marched out and called on Wenxiu for troops. Wenxiu sent the armies of Liu Mizhi, Zhang Lingqing, and Cui Sengqin to the court. Xuzhou Inspector Xue Andou had already joined Zixun. He sent word that the whole realm was rising and urged Wenxiu to rebel too. Wenxiu ordered Mizhi and the rest to join Andou. Mizhi and the others soon submitted—the account is in Andou's biography. Mizhi came from a powerful Qingzhou clan with many kinsmen. His relatives fled to Beihai together and held the city against Wenxiu. Pingyuan and Le'an Administrator Wang Xuanmo held Langye; Qinghe and Guangchuan Administrator Wang Xuanyao held Panyang; Gaoyang and Bohai Administrator Liu Chengmin held Linji—all rose against Wenxiu. Wenxiu's marshal Fang Wenqing plotted to join them and was executed by Wenxiu. Wenxiu sent army commander Xie Yanshi against Beihai and took it. Chengmin's cousin Bozong rallied local militia, retook Beihai, and marched on Dongyang, the Qingzhou capital. Wenxiu resisted him. Bozong was beaten and wounded. His brother Tian'ai tried to carry him away, but Bozong said: "A man should die on the battlefield for his country. How can I go home to die like a child in women's hands? Brother, go at once. Do not let us both die. " He was then killed. He was posthumously made General Who Leads the Dragon Cavalry and Administrator of Changguang.
16
太宗遣青州刺史明僧皓、東莞、東安二郡太守李靈謙率軍伐文秀。 玄邈、乘民、僧皓等並進軍攻城，每戰輒為文秀所破，離而復合，如此者十餘。 泰始二年八月，尋陽平定，太宗遣尚書度支郎崔元孫慰勞諸義軍，隨僧皓戰敗見殺，追贈寧朔將軍、冀州刺史。 上遣文秀弟文炳詔文秀曰：「皇帝前問督青州、徐州之東莞、東安二郡諸軍事、建威將軍、青州刺史，朕去歲撥亂，功振普天，於卿一門，特有殊澤，卿得延命至今，誰之力邪？ 何故背國負恩，遠同逆豎。 今天下已定，四方寧一，卿獨守窮城，何所歸奉？ 且卿百口在都，兼有墳墓，想情非木石，猶或顧懷。 故指遣文炳具相宣示。 凡諸逆郎，親為戎首，一不加罪，文炳所具。 卿獨何人，而能自立。 便可速率部曲，同到軍門，別詔有司，一無所問。 如其不爾，國有常刑，非惟戮及弟息，亦當夷卿墳壟，既以謝齊土百姓，亦以勞將士之心。 故有今詔。 」三年二月，文秀歸命請罪，即安本任。
Emperor Ming sent Qingzhou Inspector Ming Senghao and Dongguan and Dong'an Administrator Li Lingqian against Wenxiu. Xuanyao, Chengmin, Senghao, and the others besieged the city. Wenxiu beat them in every fight. They withdrew and returned more than ten times. In the eighth month of Taishi year 2 Xunyang fell. Emperor Ming sent Secretary Cui Yuansun to reward the loyal armies. He followed Senghao, was defeated and killed, and was posthumously made General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Jizhou. The emperor sent Wenxiu's brother Wenbing with an edict: "The emperor asks the supervisor of Qingzhou and of Dongguan and Dong'an in Xuzhou, General Who Establishes Martiality and Inspector of Qingzhou: Last year I restored order and my merit shook the realm. Your house alone I favored. You have lived until now—whose doing is that? Why then betray the state and join rebels far away? The realm is settled and the four quarters at peace. Why do you alone hold this doomed city? To whom will you turn? Your whole family is in the capital, and you have ancestral graves there. You are not made of wood and stone—you must still care for them. That is why I send Wenbing to tell you all this. All the rebel leaders who led the armies in person have been spared punishment, as Wenbing will explain. Who are you to stand apart alone? Come at once with your troops to the army gate. A separate edict will spare you from all inquiry. If you refuse, the state has its fixed punishments. Not only your sons and brothers will die—your ancestral graves will be destroyed as well, both to answer the people of Qi and to satisfy the army. Hence this edict. " In the second month of year 3 Wenxiu submitted and begged pardon, and was restored to his original post.
17
先是，冀州刺史崔道固亦據歷城同逆，為土人起義所攻，與文秀俱遣信引虜； 虜遣將慕輿白曜率大眾援之，文秀已受朝命，乃乘虜無備，縱兵掩擊，殺傷甚多。 虜乃進軍圍城，文秀善於撫御，將士咸為盡力，每與虜戰，輒摧破之，掩擊營寨，往無不捷。 太宗進文秀號輔國將軍。 其年八月，虜蜀郡公拔式等馬步數萬人入西郭，直至城下。 文秀使輔國將軍垣諶擊破之。 九月，又逼城東。 十月，進攻南郭。 文秀使員外散騎侍郎黃彌之等邀擊，斬獲數千。 四年，又進文秀號右將軍，封新城縣侯，食邑五百戶。 虜青州刺史王隆顯於安丘縣又為軍主高崇仁所破，死者數百人。 虜圍青州積久，太宗所遣救兵並不敢進，乃以文秀弟征北中兵參軍文靜為輔國將軍，統高密、北海、平昌、長廣、東萊五郡軍事，從海道救青州。 文靜至東萊之不其城，為虜所斷遏，不得進，因保城自守，又為虜所攻，屢戰輒克，太宗加其東青州刺史。 四年，不其城為虜所陷，文靜見殺。
Earlier Jizhou Inspector Cui Daogu had also held Licheng in rebellion. Local uprisings attacked him, and he and Wenxiu both invited the Northern Wei. The enemy sent General Murong Baiyao with a large force to aid them. Wenxiu had already received the court's commission and, catching the enemy unprepared, struck by surprise and killed or wounded many. The enemy then besieged the city. Wenxiu was skilled at winning men's hearts, and his officers and soldiers fought with all their strength. In every battle he broke the enemy, and in raids on their camps he never failed. Emperor Ming promoted Wenxiu to General Who Assists the State. That August Duke Bashi of Shujun and others led tens of thousands of horse and foot into the western suburb and up to the walls. Wenxiu sent General Who Assists the State Yuan Chen and routed them. In the ninth month they pressed the eastern wall again. In the tenth month they attacked the southern suburb. Wenxiu sent Supernumerary Gentleman Huang Mizhi and others to intercept them and killed or captured several thousand. In year 4 Wenxiu was promoted to General of the Right Army and enfeoffed as Marquis of Xincheng with a fief of five hundred households. The enemy Qingzhou Inspector Wang Longxian was defeated again at Anqiu by army commander Gao Chongren, with several hundred dead. The enemy had long besieged Qingzhou, and the relief armies the court sent would not advance. Wenxiu's brother Wen Jing, acting staff officer of the northern campaign, was made General Who Assists the State to command five commanderies and relieve Qingzhou by sea. Wen Jing reached Buqi in Donglai but was blocked by the enemy and could not advance. He held the city and beat off repeated attacks. Emperor Ming made him Inspector of Eastern Qingzhou. In year 4 Buqi fell to the enemy and Wen Jing was killed.
18
文秀被圍三載，外無援軍，士卒為之用命，無離叛者，日夜戰鬥，甲胄生蟣虱。 五年正月二十四日，遂為虜所陷。 城敗之日，解釋戎衣，緩服靜坐，命左右取所持節。 虜既入，兵刃交至，問曰：「青州刺史沈文秀何在？ 」文秀厲聲曰：「身是。 」因執之，牽出聽事前，剝取衣服。 時白曜在城西南角樓，裸縛文秀至曜前，執之者令拜。 文秀曰：「各二國大臣，無相拜之禮。 」曜命還其衣，為設酒食，鏁送桑乾。 其餘為亂兵所殺，死者甚眾。 太宗先遣尚書功論郎何如真選青州文武，亦為虜所殺。 文秀在桑乾凡十九年，齊之永明四年，病死，時年六十一。
Wenxiu was besieged for three years with no relief from outside, yet his men fought to the death and none deserted. They fought day and night until lice bred in their armor. On the twenty-fourth day of the first month of year 5 the city fell to the enemy. On the day the city fell he took off his armor, sat calmly in plain robes, and ordered his attendants to bring his staff of office. When the enemy entered, weapons clashed all around. They asked: "Where is Qingzhou Inspector Shen Wenxiu? " Wenxiu shouted: "I am he. " He was seized and dragged before the hall, where his clothes were stripped off. Baiyao was on the tower at the southwest corner. Wenxiu was bound naked and brought before him. His captors ordered him to bow. " Wenxiu said: "We are ministers of two states. There is no bowing between us. " Baiyao had his clothes returned, gave him food and wine, and sent him in chains to Sanggan. The rest were killed by the disorderly troops. The dead were very many. Emperor Ming had earlier sent Secretary He Ruzhen to select Qingzhou officials. He too was killed by the enemy. Wenxiu spent nineteen years at Sanggan. In Yongming year 4 of Qi he died of illness, aged sixty-one.
19
崔道固，清河人也。 世祖世，以幹用見知，歷太子屯騎校尉，左軍將軍。 大明三年，出為齊、北海二郡太守。 民焦恭破古塚，得玉鎧，道固檢得，獻之，執繫恭。 入為新安王子鸞北中郎諮議參軍，永嘉王子仁左軍司馬。 景和元年，出為寧朔將軍、冀州刺史，鎮歷城。 泰始二年，進號輔國將軍，又進號征虜將軍。 時徐州刺史薛安都同逆，上即還道固本號為徐州代之。 道固不受命，遣子景微、軍主傅靈越率眾赴安都。 既而為土人起義所攻，屢戰失利，閉門自守。 會四方平定，上遣使宣慰，道固奉詔歸順。 先是與沈文秀共引虜，虜既至，固守距之，因被圍逼。 虜每進，輒為道固所摧。 三年，以為都督冀、青、兗、幽、并五州諸軍事、前將軍、冀州刺史，加節，又進號平北將軍。 其年，為虜所陷，被送桑乾，死於虜中。
Cui Daogu was a native of Qinghe. Under the emperor he was known for ability and served as Colonel of the Heir Apparent's Escort Cavalry and General of the Left Army. In Daming year 3 he was made Administrator of Qi and Beihai. A commoner named Jiao Gong opened an ancient tomb and found a jade suit of armor. Daogu seized it, presented it to the throne, and had Gong arrested. He entered court as staff officer to Northern Central Commander Prince Ziluan of Xin'an and as marshal of the left army to Prince Ziren of Yongjia. In Yuanhe year 1 he was made General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Jizhou, with his seat at Licheng. In Taishi year 2 he was promoted to General Who Assists the State and then to General Who Conquers the Barbarians. Xuzhou Inspector Xue Andou had rebelled. The emperor at once restored Daogu's former rank and made him Inspector of Xuzhou in Andou's place. Daogu refused the appointment and sent his son Jingwei and army commander Fu Lingyue with troops to join Andou. He was soon attacked by local rebels, lost battle after battle, and shut himself in the city. When the realm was pacified, the emperor sent envoys to reassure him. Daogu accepted the edict and submitted. He and Shen Wenxiu had earlier invited the Northern Wei together. When the enemy arrived he held the city against them and was besieged. Whenever the enemy advanced, Daogu beat them back. In year 3 he was made commander of military affairs in Jizhou, Qingzhou, Yanzhou, Youzhou, and Bingzhou, Forward General and Inspector of Jizhou with staff of authority, and promoted to General Who Pacifies the North. That year the city fell. He was sent to Sanggan and died in the north.
20
史臣曰：《春秋》列國大夫得罪，皆先致其邑而後去，唯邾、莒三臣，書以叛人之目，蓋重地也。 安都勤王之略，義闕於藩屏，以地外奔，罪同於三叛。 《詩》云：「誰生厲階，至今為梗。 」其此之謂乎？
The historian says: In the Spring and Autumn Annals, when feudal ministers offended their lords they surrendered their fiefs before leaving. Only the three ministers of Zhu and Ju are called rebels in the text—because land is what matters most. Andou's plan to aid the throne failed the duty of a frontier shield. He fled north with his territory, and his crime was the same as those three rebels. The Odes say: "Who raised this trouble? Down to today it remains a stumbling block. " Is this not what is meant?
