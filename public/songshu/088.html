1
列傳第四十八薛安都沈文秀崔道固
Biography 48: Xue Andou, Shen Wenxiu, and Cui Daogu
2
薛安都，河東汾陰人也。 世為強族，同姓有三千家。 父廣，為宗豪，高祖定關、河，以為上黨太守。 安都少以勇聞，身長七尺八寸，便弓馬。 索虜使助秦州刺史北賀汨擊反胡白龍子，滅之。 由是為偽雍、秦二州都統，州各有刺史，都統總其事。 元嘉二十一年，索虜主拓跋燾擊芮芮大敗，安都與宗人薛永宗起義，永宗營汾曲，安都襲得弘農。 會北地人蓋吳起兵，遂連衡相應。 燾自率眾擊永宗，滅其族，進擊蓋吳。 安都料眾寡不敵，率壯士辛靈度等，棄弘農歸國。 太祖延見之，求北還構扇河、陝，招聚義眾。 上許之，給錦百匹，雜繒三百匹。 復襲弘農，虜已增戍，城不可克，蓋吳又死，乃退還上洛。 世祖鎮襄陽，板為揚武將軍、北弘農太守。 虜漸強盛，安都乃歸襄陽。 從叔沈亦同歸國，官至綏遠將軍、新野太守。
Xue Andou was from Fenyin in Hedong. His family had been a powerful clan for generations, with three thousand households bearing the same surname. His father Guang was a local magnate. When Emperor Gaozu secured the Guan and He regions, he made him Governor of Shangdang. Andou won fame early for his courage. He stood seven foot eight in height and was expert with bow and horse. The Wei sent him to aid Qinzhou Governor Bei He'ni in crushing the rebel Hu leader BAI Longzi. He was then made supreme commander over the puppet Yong and Qin prefectures, each of which still had its own governor while he held overall authority. In the twenty-first year of Yuanjia, when the Wei ruler Tuoba Tao was routed by the Rouran, Andou and his kinsman Xue Yongzong rose in rebellion. Yongzong encamped along the Fen bend while Andou seized Hongnong in a surprise attack. When Gai Wu of Beidi also took up arms, the rebel forces linked up in mutual support. Tao personally led his army against Yongzong, wiped out his clan, and then marched on Gai Wu. Judging the odds impossible, Andou led stalwarts including Xin Lingdu, abandoned Hongnong, and submitted to the Song. Emperor Gaozu received him and approved his plan to go back north to stir rebellion along the Yellow River and in Shaanxi and rally loyalists. The emperor agreed and gave him a hundred bolts of brocade and three hundred bolts of assorted silks. He struck at Hongnong again, but the barbarians had reinforced the garrison and the city would not fall. After Gai Wu died as well, he withdrew to Shangluo. When Emperor Xiaowu held Xiangyang, Andou was commissioned General Who Displays Martial Might and Governor of Northern Hongnong. As the barbarians grew stronger, Andou returned to Xiangyang. His cousin Shen returned with him and eventually reached the rank of General Who Pacifies the Distance and Governor of Xinye.
3
二十七年，隨王誕版安都為建武將軍，隨柳元景向關、陝，率步騎居前，所向克捷，事在元景傳。 軍還，誕版為後軍行參軍。 二十九年，除始興王濬征北行參軍，加建武將軍。 魯爽向虎牢，安都復隨元景北出，即據關城，期俱濟河取蒲阪。 會爽退，安都復率所領隨元景引還。 仍伐西陽五水蠻。
In year twenty-seven Prince Dan of Sui appointed Andou General Who Establishes Martial Power. Andou marched with Liu Yuanjing toward Guan and Shaanxi at the head of the army and won every engagement—the full account is in Yuanjing's biography. After the campaign Dan appointed him Aide-de-Camp of the Rear Army. In year twenty-nine he was appointed Aide-de-Camp on the Northern Campaign of Prince Jun of Shixing, with the additional rank of General Who Establishes Martial Power. When Lu Shuang moved on Hulao, Andou again went north with Yuanjing, seized the pass city, and planned to ford the river together and take Puban. When Shuang retreated, Andou again led his troops back with Yuanjing. He then campaigned against the Wushui barbarians of Xiyang.
4
世祖伐逆，轉參軍事，加寧朔將軍，領馬軍，與柳元景俱發。 四月十四日，至朱雀航，橫矛瞋目，叱賊將皇甫安民等曰：「賊弑君父，何心事之！ 」世祖踐阼，除右軍將軍。 五月四日，率所領騎為前鋒，直入殿庭。 賊尚有數百人，一時奔散。 以功封南鄉縣男，食邑五百戶。 安都從征關、陝，至臼口，夢仰頭視天，正見天門開，謂左右曰：「汝見天門開不？ 」至是歎曰：「夢天開，乃中興之象邪！」
When Emperor Xiaowu marched to suppress the usurpation, Andou was transferred to staff duty, made General Who Pacifies the North, and given command of the cavalry, setting out with Liu Yuanjing. On the fourteenth day of the fourth month they reached the Zhuque ford. Andou levelled his spear, glared, and shouted at the rebel generals Huangfu Anmin and the rest: "You serve men who murdered their lord and father—how can you bear to follow them?" Emperor Xiaowu ascended the throne and appointed him General of the Right Army. On the fourth day of the fifth month he led his cavalry as vanguard straight into the palace courtyard. Several hundred rebels were still there; they scattered at once. For his merit he was enfeoffed as Baron of Nanxiang with a fief of five hundred households. On campaign in Guan and Shaanxi, Andou reached Jiukou and dreamed that he looked up and saw the Heavenly Gate standing open. He asked those beside him, "Do you see the Heavenly Gate open?" At this he sighed and said, "To dream the heavens open—is that not a sign of renewal for the dynasty!"
5
從弟道生，亦以軍功為大司馬參軍。 犯罪，為秣陵令庾淑之所鞭。 安都大怒，乃乘馬從數十人，令左右執矟，欲往殺淑之。 行至朱雀航，逢柳元景。 元景遙問：「薛公何處去？ 」安都躍馬至車後曰：「小子庾淑之鞭我從弟，今詣往刺殺之。 」元景慮其不可駐，乃紿之曰：「小子無宜適，卿往與手，甚快。 」安都既回馬，復追呼之：「別宜與卿有所論。 」令下馬入車。 既入車，因責讓之曰：「卿從弟服章言論，與寒細不異，雖復人士，庾淑之亦何由得知？ 且人身犯罪，理應加罰，卿為朝廷勳臣，宜崇奉法憲，云何放恣，輒欲於都邑殺人？ 非唯科律所不容，主上亦無辭以相宥。 」因載之俱歸，安都乃止。 其年，以憚直免官。
His younger cousin Daosheng also won rank through military merit as Aide to the Grand Marshal. Daosheng broke the law and was flogged by Moling Magistrate Yu Shuzhi. Andou was furious. He mounted his horse with several dozen men, ordered his attendants to take up spears, and set out to kill Shuzhi. On the road he reached the Zhuque ford and met Liu Yuanjing. Yuanjing called from a distance, "Lord Xue, where are you going?" Andou spurred up behind the carriage and said, "That whelp Yu Shuzhi flogged my cousin. I am on my way to stab him to death." Fearing he could not be stopped, Yuanjing deceived him: "The lad deserves no better. Go beat him yourself—you will feel much better." Andou had already turned his horse when Yuanjing called him back: "Wait—I have something else to say to you." He told Andou to dismount and get into the carriage. Once inside he reproved him: "Your cousin's dress and speech are no different from a commoner's. Even if he were a gentleman, how was Yu Shuzhi to know?" Besides, when a man commits a crime he ought to be punished. You are a meritorious servant of the court and should uphold the law—why indulge yourself and try to kill someone in the capital?" That is forbidden not only by law; the sovereign would have no grounds to pardon you. He took Andou back with him in the carriage, and Andou stopped. That year he was removed from office for his outspokenness.
6
孝建元年，復除左軍將軍。 二月，魯爽反叛，遣安都及冗從僕射胡子反、龍驤將軍宗越率步騎據歷陽。 爽遣將鄭德玄戍大峴，德玄使前鋒楊胡與輕兵向歷陽。 安都遣宗越及歷陽太守程天祚逆擊破之，斬胡與及其軍副。 德玄復使其司馬梁嚴屯峴東，安都幢主周文恭晨往偵候，因而襲之，悉禽； 賊未敢進。 世祖詔安都留三百人守歷陽，渡還採石，遷輔國將軍、竟陵內史。 四月，魯爽使弟瑜率三千人出小峴，爽尋以大眾阻大峴。 又遣安都步騎八千度江，與歷陽太守張幼緒等討爽。 安都軍副建武將軍譚金率數十騎挑戰，斬其偏帥。 幼緒恇怯，輒引軍退還，安都復還歷陽。 臧質久不至，世祖復遣沈慶之濟江督統諸軍。 爽軍食少，引退，慶之使安都率輕騎追之； 四月丙戌，及爽於小峴，爽自與腹心壯騎繼後。 譚金先薄之，不能入，安都望見爽，便躍馬大呼，直往刺之，應手而倒，左右範雙斬爽首。 爽累世梟猛，生習戰陳，咸云萬人敵。 安都單騎直入，斬之而反，時人皆云關羽之斬顏良，不是過也。 進爵為侯，增邑五百戶，并前千戶。
In the first year of Xiaojian he was again appointed General of the Left Army. In the second month, when Lu Shuang rebelled, the court sent Andou with Supernumerary Attendant Hu Zifan and General of the Soaring Dragon Zong Yue to hold Liyang with infantry and cavalry. Shuang sent his general Zheng Dexuan to garrison Great Xian. Dexuan sent his vanguard Yang Hu with light troops toward Liyang. Andou sent Zong Yue and Liyang Governor Cheng Tianzuo to meet and defeat them, beheading Hu Yu and his deputy commander. Dexuan again sent his marshal Liang Yan to camp east of the pass. Andou's banner commander Zhou Wengong went out at dawn to scout and then raided them, capturing them all; The rebels dared not advance. Emperor Xiaowu ordered Andou to leave three hundred men at Liyang, cross back to Caishi, and promoted him to General Who Assists the State and Internal Administrator of Jingling. In the fourth month Lu Shuang sent his younger brother Yu with three thousand men through Little Xian, while Shuang himself soon blocked Great Xian with his main army. Andou was sent again with eight thousand infantry and cavalry across the river, together with Liyang Governor Zhang Youxu and others, to attack Shuang. Andou's deputy Tan Jin, General Who Establishes Martial Power, led several dozen horsemen to challenge battle and cut down an enemy flank commander. Youxu was timid and at once pulled his army back; Andou returned again to Liyang. Zang Zhi was slow to arrive. Emperor Xiaowu again sent Shen Qingzhi across the river to take overall command of the armies. Shuang's army was short of food and withdrew. Qingzhi had Andou lead light cavalry in pursuit; On the day bingxu of the fourth month they caught Shuang at Little Xian. Shuang himself followed at the rear with his trusted crack horsemen. Tan Jin closed first but could not break through. Andou saw Shuang, spurred forward with a great shout, ran straight at him and thrust—Shuang fell at the blow, and his attendant Fan Shuang cut off Shuang's head. Shuang's family had been fierce for generations; he had known battle from birth, and all said he was a match for ten thousand men. Andou charged in alone on horseback, killed him, and returned. People said even Guan Yu's slaying of Yan Liang was no greater feat. He was raised to marquis, his fief increased by five hundred households to one thousand in all.
7
時王玄謨距南郡王義宣、臧質於梁山，安都復領騎為支軍。 賊有水步營在蕪湖，安都遣將呂興壽率數十騎襲之，賊眾驚亂，斬首及赴水死者甚眾。 義宣遣將劉湛之及質攻玄謨，玄謨命眾軍擊之，使安都引騎出賊陣右。 譚金三歷賊陳，乘其隙縱騎突之，諸將係進。 是朝，賊馬軍發蕪湖，欲來會戰，望安都騎甚盛，隱山不敢出。 賊陣東南猶堅，安都橫擊陷之，賊遂大潰。 安都隊主劉元儒於艦中斬湛之首。 轉太子左衛率。 大明元年，虜向無鹽，東平太守劉胡出戰失利。 二月，遣安都領馬軍北討，東陽太守沈法系水軍向彭城，並受徐州刺史申坦節度。 上戒之曰：「賊若可及，便盡力殄之。 若度已回，可過河耀威而反。 」時虜已去，坦求回軍討任榛，見許。 安都當向左城，左城去滑臺二百餘里，安都以去虜鎮近，軍少不宜分行。 至東坊城，遇任榛三騎，討擒其一，餘兩騎得走。 任榛聞知，皆得逃散。 時天旱，水泉多竭，人馬疲困，不能遠追。 安都、法系並白衣領職，坦繫尚方。 任榛大抵在任城界，積世逋叛所聚，所在皆棘榛深密，難為用師，故能久自保藏，屢為民患。 安都明年復職，改封武昌縣侯，加散騎常侍。 七年，又加征虜將軍，為太子左衛率十年，終世祖世不轉。
When Wang Xuamo held the Prince of Nan command Yishuan and Zang Zhi at Liangshan, Andou again commanded cavalry as a supporting force. The rebels had river and land camps at Wuhu. Andou sent his officer Lu Xingshou with several dozen horsemen to raid them; the enemy panicked, and many were beheaded or drowned. Yishuan sent Liu Zhanzhi and Zhi to attack Xuamo. Xuamo ordered the armies to strike and had Andou lead cavalry out to the enemy's right. Tan Jin crossed the enemy formation three times, seized a gap to charge with his horsemen, and the other generals followed in support. That morning the rebel cavalry set out from Wuhu intending to join battle, but seeing Andou's horsemen in great strength they hid in the hills and did not emerge. The enemy's southeast wing was still holding; Andou struck across and broke it, and the rebels collapsed completely. Andou's squad leader Liu Yuanru cut off Zhan's head on board a ship. He was moved to Leader of the Crown Prince's Left Guard. In the first year of Daming the barbarians advanced on Wuyan. Dongping Governor Liu Hu fought and was defeated. In the second month Andou was sent north at the head of the cavalry; Dongyang Governor Shen Faxing led the river force toward Pengcheng. Both were under Xuzhou Inspector Shen Tan. The emperor warned them: "If the enemy can still be caught, destroy them with all your strength." If they have already crossed back north, you may cross the river to show your might and return." The barbarians had already withdrawn. Tan asked to turn the army against the Ren bandits and was permitted. Andou was to move on Zuocheng, more than two hundred li from Huatai. Andou argued that with the barbarian garrison so near and their army so small, they should not split their forces. At Dongfangcheng they met three Ren bandit horsemen, captured one in the pursuit, and the other two got away. When the Ren bandits heard, they all fled and scattered. There was drought and the springs ran dry; men and horses were exhausted and could not pursue far. Andou and Faxing both retained their posts in plain dress as punishment; Tan was imprisoned in the Imperial Workshops. The Ren bandits lay mostly within Rencheng—a gathering place for long-accumulated fugitives. Everywhere the thorn scrub ran deep and dense, so armies could not operate effectively; thus they long preserved themselves and repeatedly plagued the people. The following year Andou was restored to office, refiefed as Marquis of Wuchang, and made Regular Attendant at the Imperial Gate. In the seventh year he was again made General Who Subdues the Barbarians. He remained Leader of the Crown Prince's Left Guard for ten years and was never transferred through the rest of Emperor Xiaowu's reign.
8
泰始二年正月，索兒、光世竝在省，安都信催令速去，二人俱自省逃出，攜安都諸子及家累，席捲北奔。 青州刺史沈文秀、冀州刺史崔道固並皆同反。 文秀遣劉彌之、張靈慶、崔僧琁三軍，道固遣子景征、傅靈越領眾，並應安都。 彌之等南出下邳，靈越自泰山道向彭城。 時濟陰太守申闡據睢陵城起義，索兒率靈越等攻之。 安都使同黨裴祖隆守下邳城，彌之等至下邳，改計歸順，因進軍攻祖隆，僧琁不同，率所領歸安都。 索兒聞彌之有異志，舍睢陵馳赴下邳，彌之等未戰潰散，竝為索兒所執，見殺。
In the first month of the second year of Taishi, Suo'er and Guangshi were both at court. Trusting urgent word to leave quickly, Andou urged them on—and both fled from court, taking Andou's sons and household and sweeping north in flight. Qingzhou Inspector Shen Wenxiu and Jizhou Inspector Cui Daogu rebelled as well. Wenxiu sent the three armies of Liu Mizhi, Zhang Lingqing, and Cui Sengqin; Daogu sent his son Jingzheng and Fu Lingyue with troops—all to support Andou. Mizhi and the others marched south from Xiapi; Lingyue took the Mount Tai route toward Pengcheng. Jiyin Governor Shen Chan had raised the standard of loyalty at Suiling. Suo'er led Lingyue and others to attack him. Andou had his associate Pei Zulong hold Xiapi. When Mizhi and the others reached Xiapi they changed plans and submitted, then advanced to attack Zulong. Sengqin disagreed and led his troops back to Andou. Suo'er heard Mizhi was wavering, abandoned Suiling, and raced to Xiapi. Mizhi's men collapsed without fighting and were all seized and killed by Suo'er.
9
時太宗以申令孫為徐州，代安都。 令孫進據淮陽，密有反志，遣人告索兒曰：「欲相從順，而百口在都。 可進軍見攻，若戰敗被執，家人可得免禍。 」索兒乃遣靈越向淮陽，令孫出城，為相距之形，既而奔散，北投索兒。 索兒使令孫說闡令降，闡既降，索兒執闡及令孫，並殺之。 索兒因引軍渡淮，軍糧不給，掠奔百姓穀食。 太宗遣齊王率前將軍張永、寧朔將軍垣山寶、王寬、員外散騎侍郎張寘震、蕭順之、龍驤將軍張季和、黃文玉等諸軍北討。 其年五月，軍次平原，索兒等率馬步五千，列陳距戰，擊大破之。 索兒又虜掠民穀，固守石樑，齊王又率鎮北參軍趙曇之、呂湛之擊之。 索兒軍無資實，所資野掠，既見攻逼，無以自守，於是奔散； 又追破之於葛家白鵠。 索兒走向樂平縣界，為申令孫子孝叔所斬。 安都子道智、大將范雙走向合肥，詣南汝陰太守裴季降。
Emperor Ming made Shen Lingsun Inspector of Xuzhou in place of Andou. Lingsun advanced and held Huaiyang, secretly harboring rebellious intent, and sent word to Suo'er: "I wish to join you, but my whole household is in the capital." Come advance and attack me openly. If I am defeated and captured, my family may be spared." Suo'er then sent Lingyue toward Huaiyang. Lingsun came out as if to oppose him, then scattered his troops and fled north to Suo'er. Suo'er had Lingsun persuade Chan to surrender. Once Chan had submitted, Suo'er seized Chan and Lingsun and killed them both. Suo'er then crossed the Huai with his army. With no rations, they plundered fleeing commoners for grain. Emperor Ming sent the Prince of Qi at the head of Former General Zhang Yong, General Who Pacifies the North Yuan Shanbao, Wang Kuan, Supernumerary Gentleman-at-Large Zhang Zhenzhen, Xiao Shunzhi, General of the Soaring Dragon Zhang Jihe, Huang Wenyu, and other armies northward. That fifth month the army halted at Pingyuan. Suo'er and the others led five thousand horse and foot, drew up in battle array, and were utterly defeated. Suo'er plundered civilian grain again and held firm at Stone Bridge. The Prince of Qi again led Northern Campaign Aides Zhao Tanzhi and Lu Zhenzhi to attack them. Suo'er's army had no real supplies and lived on countryside plunder. Under attack they could not hold and broke and fled; They were pursued and defeated again at Gejia Baihe. Suo'er fled toward Leping county and was beheaded by Shen Lingsun's son Xiaoshu. Andou's son Daozhi and his great general Fan Shuang fled to Hefei and surrendered to Southern Runan Governor Pei Ji.
10
時武衛將軍王廣之領軍隸劉勔，攻殷琰於壽陽。 傅靈越奔逃，為廣之軍人所生禽，厲聲曰：「我傅靈越也。 汝得賊何不即殺？ 」生送詣勔，勔躬自慰勞，詰其叛逆。 對曰：「九州唱義，豈獨在我。 」勔又問：「四方阻逆，無戰不禽，主上皆加以曠蕩，即其才用。 卿何不早歸天闕，乃逃命草間乎？ 」靈越答曰：「薛公舉兵淮北，威震天下，不能專任智勇，委付子侄，致敗之由，實在於此。 然事之始末，備皆參豫，人生歸於一死，實無面求活。 」勔壯其意，送還京師。 太宗欲加原宥，靈越辭對如一，終不回改，乃殺之。 靈越，清河人也。 時輔國將軍、山陽內史程天祚據郡同安都，攻圍彌時，然後歸順。
At that time Martial Guard General Wang Guangzhi led troops under Liu Xu in the attack on Yin Yan at Shouyang. Fu Lingyue fled and was captured alive by Guangzhi's men. He shouted, "I am Fu Lingyue. You have caught a rebel—why not kill me at once?" They took him alive to Xu, who personally comforted him and questioned him about his rebellion. He replied, "The whole realm rose in righteous revolt—how could the fault be mine alone?" Xu asked again, "Rebels everywhere have been blocked and captured in battle after battle; the sovereign has shown great clemency and employed each according to his talent. Why did you not return early to the imperial court instead of fleeing for your life among the weeds?" Lingyue replied, "Lord Xue raised arms north of the Huai and his might shook the realm, yet he could not rely on wisdom and courage alone but entrusted command to sons and nephews—the root of defeat lies here." Yet I took part from beginning to end. A man's life ends in death; I have no face left to beg for life." Xu admired his bearing and sent him to the capital. Emperor Ming wished to pardon him, but Lingyue answered as before and would not change; he was therefore executed. Lingyue was from Qinghe. At that time Cheng Tianzuo, General Who Assists the State and Internal Administrator of Shanyang, had held his commandery with Andou; after a long siege he submitted.
11
子勛平定，安都遣別駕從事史畢眾愛、下邳太守王煥等奉啟書詣太宗歸款，曰：「臣庸隸荒萌，偷生上國，過蒙世祖孝武皇帝過常之恩，犬馬有心，實感恩遇。 是以晉安始唱，投誠孤往，不期生榮，實存死報。 今天命大歸，群迷改屬，輒率領所部，束骸待誅，違拒之罪，伏聽湯鑊。 」索兒之死也，安都使柳光世守下邳，至是亦率所領歸降。 太宗以四方已平，欲示威於淮外，遣張永、沈攸之以重軍迎之。 安都謂既已歸順，不應遣重兵，懼不免罪，乃遣信要引索虜。 三年正月，索虜遣博陵公尉遲苟人、城陽公孔伯恭二萬騎救之。 永等引退，安都開門納虜，虜即授安都徐州刺史、河東公。 四年三月，召還桑乾。 五年，死於虜中，時年六十。
After Zixun was pacified, Andou sent his Registrar Bi Zhong'ai, Xiapi Governor Wang Huan, and others with a memorial to Emperor Ming offering submission, saying, "Your servant, a crude man from the borderlands, stole life in the upper kingdom and received beyond measure the exceptional grace of Emperor Xiaowu; this dog and horse has a heart and is truly grateful for the favor shown. So when the Prince of Jin'an first raised the call, I went alone in loyalty, not hoping for glory in life but ready to repay with death." "Now that Heaven's mandate has returned and the deluded have changed allegiance, I lead my command, bind my body and await execution, and for the crime of resistance submit to the boiling cauldron." After Suo'er's death Andou had Liu Guangshi hold Xiapi; he now led his troops in submission as well. Emperor Ming, with the four quarters already pacified, wished to display might beyond the Huai and sent Zhang Yong and Shen Youzhi with a heavy army to receive Andou. Andou thought that since he had already submitted, a heavy army should not have been sent. Fearing he would not escape punishment, he sent messages inviting the northern barbarians. In the first month of the third year the barbarians sent the Duke of Boling Yuanchi Gouren and the Duke of Chengyang Kong Bogong with twenty thousand horsemen to his aid. Yong withdrew; Andou opened the gates to the barbarians, who immediately made him Inspector of Xuzhou and Duke of Hedong. In the third month of the fourth year he was recalled to Sanggan. In the fifth year he died in barbarian lands, aged sixty.
12
初，安都起兵，長史蘭陵儼密欲圖之，見殺。 安都未向桑乾，前軍將軍裴祖隆謀殺苟人，舉彭城歸順，事泄，見誅。 員外散騎侍郎孫耿之擊索兒戰死，及劉彌之、張靈慶皆戰敗見殺，並為太宗所哀，追贈儼光祿勳，祖隆寧朔將軍、兗州刺史，耿之羽林監，彌之輔國將軍、青州刺史，靈慶寧朔將軍、冀州刺史。
When Andou first took up arms, his Chief Clerk Yan of Lanling plotted against him in secret and was put to death. Before Andou went to Sanggan, Vanguard General Pei Zulong plotted to kill Gouren and hand Pengcheng over to the Song; the plot leaked and he was executed. Supernumerary Gentleman-at-Large Sun Gengzhi died fighting Suo'er; Liu Mizhi, Zhang Lingqing, and others were defeated and killed in battle—all were mourned by Emperor Ming, who posthumously enfeoffed Yan as Grand Master of Splendid Happiness, Zulong as General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Yanzhou, Gengzhi as Inspector of the Feathered Forest, Mizhi as General Who Assists the State and Inspector of Qingzhou, and Lingqing as General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Jizhou.
13
安都子伯令、環龍，亡命梁、雍二州之間。 三年，率亡命數千人襲廣平，執太守劉冥虯，攻順陽，克之，略有義成、扶風，置立守宰。 雍州刺史巴陵王休若遣南陽太守張敬兒、新野太守劉攘兵擊破之，並禽。 先是，東安、東莞二郡太守張讜守團城，在彭城東北。 始同安都，末亦歸順，太宗以為東徐州刺史，復為虜所沒。
Andou's sons Boling and Huanlong lived as outlaws between Liang and Yong. In the third year they led several thousand outlaws against Guangping, seized Governor Liu Mingjiao, took Shunyang by assault, overran parts of Yicheng and Fufeng, and set up local officials. Yongzhou Inspector Prince Xiuruo of Baling sent Nanyang Governor Zhang Jing'er and Xinye Governor Liu Rangbing to defeat them and take them both captive. Earlier Zhang Chan, Governor of Dong'an and Dongguan, had held Tuancheng northeast of Pengcheng. He first joined Andou but in the end also submitted; Emperor Ming made him Inspector of Eastern Xuzhou, but the territory was again lost to the barbarians.
14
景和元年，遷督青州之東莞、東安二郡諸軍事、建威將軍、青州刺史。 時帝狂悖無道，內外憂危，文秀將之鎮，部曲出屯白下，說慶之曰：「主上狂暴如此，土崩將至，而一門受其寵任，萬物皆謂與之同心。 且此人性情無常，猜忌特甚，將來之禍，事又難測。 今因此眾力，圖之易於反掌，千載一時，萬不可失。 」慶之不從。 文秀固請非一，言輒流涕，終不回。 文秀既行，慶之果為帝所殺。 慶之死後，帝遣直閣江方興領兵誅文秀，方興未至，太宗已定亂，馳驛駐之。 方興既至，為文秀所執。 尋見釋，遣還京師。
In the first year of Jinghe he was made Commander over military affairs in Dongguan and Dong'an of Qingzhou, General Who Establishes Martial Power, and Inspector of Qingzhou. The emperor was then mad and lawless and peril threatened within and without. As Wenxiu was about to take up his post his troops encamped at Baixia, and he told Qingzhi, "The sovereign is violent to this degree; the realm is about to collapse. Yet our whole house enjoys his special favor, and everyone thinks we are of one mind with him. And this man's nature is inconstant and his suspicion extreme—the coming calamity will be hard to foresee. With the force we have now, to strike at him would be easier than turning one's hand—a chance that comes once in a thousand years and must not be lost." Qingzhi refused. Wenxiu pleaded again and again, weeping as he spoke, but could not move him in the end. After Wenxiu had left, Qingzhi was indeed killed by the emperor. After Qingzhi's death the emperor sent Palace Attendant Jiang Fangxing with troops to execute Wenxiu. Before Fangxing arrived, Emperor Ming had already settled the turmoil and sent a fast courier to halt him. Fangxing arrived and was seized by Wenxiu. He was released soon after and sent back to the capital.
15
時晉安王子勛據尋陽反叛，六師外討，徵兵於文秀。 文秀遣劉彌之、張靈慶、崔僧琁三軍赴朝廷。 時徐州刺史薛安都已同子勛，遣使報文秀，以四方齊舉，勸令同逆，文秀即令彌之等回應安都。 彌之等尋歸順，事在安都傳。 彌之青州強姓，門族甚多，諸宗從相合率奔北海，據城以拒文秀。 平原、樂安二郡太守王玄默據琅邪，清河、廣川二郡太守王玄邈據盤陽城，高陽、勃海二郡太守劉乘民據臨濟城，並起義。 文秀司馬房文慶謀應之，為文秀所殺。 文秀遣軍主解彥士攻北海陷之，乘民從弟伯宗合率鄉兵，復克北海，因率所領向青州所治東陽城。 文秀拒之，伯宗戰敗被創，弟天愛扶持將去，伯宗曰：「丈夫當死戰場，以身殉國，安能歸死兒女手中乎！ 弟可速去，無為兩亡。 」乃見殺，追贈龍驤將軍、長廣太守。
The Prince of Jin'an Zixun then held Xunyang in rebellion while the six armies campaigned abroad, and troops were levied from Wenxiu. Wenxiu sent the three armies of Liu Mizhi, Zhang Lingqing, and Cui Sengqin to serve the court. Xuzhou Inspector Xue Andou had already joined Zixun and sent word urging Wenxiu on, saying the four quarters had all risen; Wenxiu at once ordered Mizhi and the others to answer Andou. Mizhi and the others soon came over—the account is in Andou's biography. Mizhi belonged to a powerful Qingzhou clan with many kinsmen; the clansmen banded together, fled to Beihai, and held the city against Wenxiu. Wang Xuanmo, Governor of Pingyuan and Le'an, held Langye; Wang Xuanmiao of Qinghe and Guangchuan held Panyang; Liu Chenmin of Gaoyang and Bohai held Linji—all rose in loyal revolt. Wenxiu's marshal Fang Wenqing plotted to join them and Wenxiu had him killed. Wenxiu sent Commander Xie Yanshi to attack and take Beihai. Chenmin's cousin Bozong rallied local militia, retook Beihai, and marched on Dongyang, the seat of Qingzhou. Wenxiu resisted him. Bozong was beaten and wounded; his brother Tian'ai helped him as he was about to withdraw. Bozong said, "A man should die on the battlefield for the state—how can he go home to die in the hands of women and children! Brother, go at once—do not let us both die." He was killed and posthumously made General of the Soaring Dragon and Governor of Changguang.
16
太宗遣青州刺史明僧皓、東莞、東安二郡太守李靈謙率軍伐文秀。 玄邈、乘民、僧皓等並進軍攻城，每戰輒為文秀所破，離而復合，如此者十餘。 泰始二年八月，尋陽平定，太宗遣尚書度支郎崔元孫慰勞諸義軍，隨僧皓戰敗見殺，追贈寧朔將軍、冀州刺史。 上遣文秀弟文炳詔文秀曰：「皇帝前問督青州、徐州之東莞、東安二郡諸軍事、建威將軍、青州刺史，朕去歲撥亂，功振普天，於卿一門，特有殊澤，卿得延命至今，誰之力邪？ 何故背國負恩，遠同逆豎。 今天下已定，四方寧一，卿獨守窮城，何所歸奉？ 且卿百口在都，兼有墳墓，想情非木石，猶或顧懷。 故指遣文炳具相宣示。 凡諸逆郎，親為戎首，一不加罪，文炳所具。 卿獨何人，而能自立。 便可速率部曲，同到軍門，別詔有司，一無所問。 如其不爾，國有常刑，非惟戮及弟息，亦當夷卿墳壟，既以謝齊土百姓，亦以勞將士之心。 故有今詔。 」三年二月，文秀歸命請罪，即安本任。
Emperor Ming sent Qingzhou Inspector Ming Senghao and Dongguan and Dong'an Governor Li Lingqian with armies against Wenxiu. Xuanmiao, Chenmin, Senghao, and the rest all advanced to besiege the cities; Wenxiu defeated them in every battle, yet they withdrew and gathered again—more than ten times over. In the eighth month of the second year of Taishi Xunyang was pacified. Emperor Ming sent Minister of Revenue Cui Yuansun to comfort the loyalist armies; he was defeated in battle with Senghao and killed, and posthumously made General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Jizhou. The court sent Wenxiu's younger brother Wenbing with an edict saying, "The emperor asks the Commander over military affairs in Dongguan and Dong'an of Qingzhou, General Who Establishes Martial Power, and Inspector of Qingzhou: I put down disorder last year and my merit shook the realm; to your house alone I showed special grace. You have lived until now—by whose power? Why betray the state and fail in gratitude, joining rebels from afar? The realm is settled and the four quarters at peace—why do you alone hold this desperate city, and to whom will you turn? Your whole household is in the capital and you have ancestral graves besides; you are not wood or stone—surely something still moves you. I have therefore sent Wenbing to lay all this before you. All the other rebel leaders, though they commanded armies in person, were punished not at all—the details are in Wenbing's report. Who are you, to stand alone? Lead your command at once to the army gate; a separate edict will tell the officials to question you about nothing. If you refuse, the state has its regular punishments—not only your sons and brothers will die, but your ancestral graves will be destroyed as well, both to answer the people of Qi and to satisfy the armies. Hence this edict." In the second month of the third year Wenxiu submitted and begged pardon, and kept his original post.
17
先是，冀州刺史崔道固亦據歷城同逆，為土人起義所攻，與文秀俱遣信引虜； 虜遣將慕輿白曜率大眾援之，文秀已受朝命，乃乘虜無備，縱兵掩擊，殺傷甚多。 虜乃進軍圍城，文秀善於撫御，將士咸為盡力，每與虜戰，輒摧破之，掩擊營寨，往無不捷。 太宗進文秀號輔國將軍。 其年八月，虜蜀郡公拔式等馬步數萬人入西郭，直至城下。 文秀使輔國將軍垣諶擊破之。 九月，又逼城東。 十月，進攻南郭。 文秀使員外散騎侍郎黃彌之等邀擊，斬獲數千。 四年，又進文秀號右將軍，封新城縣侯，食邑五百戶。 虜青州刺史王隆顯於安丘縣又為軍主高崇仁所破，死者數百人。 虜圍青州積久，太宗所遣救兵並不敢進，乃以文秀弟征北中兵參軍文靜為輔國將軍，統高密、北海、平昌、長廣、東萊五郡軍事，從海道救青州。 文靜至東萊之不其城，為虜所斷遏，不得進，因保城自守，又為虜所攻，屢戰輒克，太宗加其東青州刺史。 四年，不其城為虜所陷，文靜見殺。
Earlier Jizhou Inspector Cui Daogu had also held Licheng with the rebels and was attacked by local loyalists; like Wenxiu he too sent for the barbarians; The barbarians sent General Murong Baiyao with a great host to aid them. Wenxiu already held the court's commission and, catching the barbarians off guard, loosed a surprise attack that killed and wounded many. The barbarians then advanced and besieged the city. Wenxiu knew how to win men's hearts; officers and soldiers fought their hardest. Every clash broke the barbarians; every raid on their camps succeeded. Emperor Ming advanced Wenxiu to General Who Assists the State. In the eighth month of that year the barbarian Duke of Shu Jun Ba Shi and others led tens of thousands of horse and foot into the western suburb right to the foot of the wall. Wenxiu sent Yuan Shen, General Who Assists the State, to defeat them. In the ninth month they pressed the east wall. In the tenth month they assaulted the south wall. Wenxiu sent Huang Mizhi, Supernumerary Gentleman-at-Large, and others to intercept them and killed or captured several thousand. In the fourth year Wenxiu was promoted to General of the Right Army and enfeoffed as Marquis of Xincheng with five hundred households. The barbarian Qingzhou Inspector Wang Longxian was again beaten at Anqiu by Commander Gao Chongren; several hundred perished. The barbarians had besieged Qingzhou a long time and the relief armies Emperor Ming sent dared not advance; he therefore made Wenxiu's younger brother Northern Campaign Aide Wen Jing General Who Assists the State, commanding military affairs in five commanderies, to rescue Qingzhou by sea. Jing reached Buji in Donglai but was blocked by the barbarians and could not advance; he therefore held the city and defended himself, was again attacked by the barbarians, yet won battle after battle; Emperor Ming added the title Eastern Qingzhou Inspector. In the fourth year Buji fell and Jing was killed.
18
文秀被圍三載，外無援軍，士卒為之用命，無離叛者，日夜戰鬥，甲胄生蟣虱。 五年正月二十四日，遂為虜所陷。 城敗之日，解釋戎衣，緩服靜坐，命左右取所持節。 虜既入，兵刃交至，問曰：「青州刺史沈文秀何在？ 」文秀厲聲曰：「身是。 」因執之，牽出聽事前，剝取衣服。 時白曜在城西南角樓，裸縛文秀至曜前，執之者令拜。 文秀曰：「各二國大臣，無相拜之禮。 」曜命還其衣，為設酒食，鏁送桑乾。 其餘為亂兵所殺，死者甚眾。 太宗先遣尚書功論郎何如真選青州文武，亦為虜所殺。 文秀在桑乾凡十九年，齊之永明四年，病死，時年六十一。
Wenxiu was besieged three years without relief; his men stayed loyal and none deserted. They fought day and night until lice swarmed in their armour. On the twenty-fourth day of the first month of the fifth year the city finally fell to the barbarians. When the city fell he took off his armour, put on plain clothes, and sat quietly, telling his attendants to bring the staff of office he carried. Once the barbarians were inside, weapons flashed before him and they cried, "Where is Qingzhou Inspector Shen Wenxiu?" Wenxiu called out, "It is I." They seized him and dragged him before the main hall, where they stripped off his clothes. Baiyao was then in the tower at the southwest corner. Wenxiu was bound naked and brought before him; his captors ordered him to bow. Wenxiu said, "We are ministers of two states—there is no bowing between us." Baiyao had his clothes returned, set out food and wine, and sent him under guard to Sanggan. The rest were cut down by the disorderly troops; the dead were very numerous. Emperor Ming had earlier sent Minister of Works Reviewer He Ruzhen to select Qingzhou civil and military officers; he too was killed by the barbarians. Wenxiu spent nineteen years at Sanggan in all. In the fourth year of Yongming of Qi he died of illness, aged sixty-one.
19
崔道固，清河人也。 世祖世，以幹用見知，歷太子屯騎校尉，左軍將軍。 大明三年，出為齊、北海二郡太守。 民焦恭破古塚，得玉鎧，道固檢得，獻之，執繫恭。 入為新安王子鸞北中郎諮議參軍，永嘉王子仁左軍司馬。 景和元年，出為寧朔將軍、冀州刺史，鎮歷城。 泰始二年，進號輔國將軍，又進號征虜將軍。 時徐州刺史薛安都同逆，上即還道固本號為徐州代之。 道固不受命，遣子景微、軍主傅靈越率眾赴安都。 既而為土人起義所攻，屢戰失利，閉門自守。 會四方平定，上遣使宣慰，道固奉詔歸順。 先是與沈文秀共引虜，虜既至，固守距之，因被圍逼。 虜每進，輒為道固所摧。 三年，以為都督冀、青、兗、幽、并五州諸軍事、前將軍、冀州刺史，加節，又進號平北將軍。 其年，為虜所陷，被送桑乾，死於虜中。
Cui Daogu was from Qinghe. During Emperor Xiaowu's reign he was known for practical ability and served as Crown Prince Commandant of the Escort Cavalry and General of the Left Army. In the third year of Daming he was sent out as Governor of Qi and Beihai. A commoner named Jiao Gong broke open an ancient tomb and found a jade suit of armour; Daogu seized it on inspection, presented it to the throne, and arrested Gong. He entered court as Consultant to the Northern Gentlemen of Prince Ziluan of Xin'an and as Left Army Marshal to Prince Ziren of Yongjia. In the first year of Jinghe he was sent out as General Who Pacifies the North and Inspector of Jizhou, with his seat at Licheng. In the second year of Taishi he was promoted to General Who Assists the State and then again to General Who Subdues the Barbarians. At that time Xuzhou Inspector Xue Andou had joined the rebellion; the court at once restored Daogu to his former rank and made him Inspector of Xuzhou in Andou's place. Daogu refused the commission and sent his son Jingwei and Commander Fu Lingyue with troops to support Andou. He was then attacked by local loyalist forces, suffered defeat after defeat, and shut the gates to hold the city. When the four quarters were pacified the court sent envoys to reassure him, and Daogu accepted the edict and submitted. Earlier he and Shen Wenxiu had together invited the barbarians; when they arrived he held firm and resisted them and was besieged. Whenever the barbarians advanced they were beaten back by Daogu. In the third year he was made Commander over military affairs in Ji, Qing, Yan, You, and Bing, Former General, and Inspector of Jizhou with staff of authority, and was again advanced to General Who Pacifies the North. That year the city fell to the barbarians; he was sent to Sanggan and died in captivity.
20
史臣曰：《春秋》列國大夫得罪，皆先致其邑而後去，唯邾、莒三臣，書以叛人之目，蓋重地也。 安都勤王之略，義闕於藩屏，以地外奔，罪同於三叛。 《詩》云：「誰生厲階，至今為梗。 」其此之謂乎？
The historiographer says: In the Spring and Autumn Annals, when great officers of the feudal states fell into disgrace, all first surrendered their fiefs before leaving—only the three ministers of Zhu and Ju are entered as rebels, because territory was held in the highest regard. Andou's scheme to aid the sovereign fell short of the duty owed a frontier shield; fleeing beyond the borders with his territory, his crime matched that of the three rebels. As the Odes have it: "Who raised this grievous stair? Even now it remains a stumbling block." Is this not what those words describe?
