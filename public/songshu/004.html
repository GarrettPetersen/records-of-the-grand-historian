1
永初三年五月癸亥，武帝崩，是日，太子即皇帝位。 大赦。 尊皇太后曰太皇太后。
On guihai, the fifth month of Yongchu year 3, Emperor Wu died; that same day the crown prince took the throne. A general amnesty was proclaimed. The empress dowager was elevated to grand empress dowager.
2
六月壬申，以尚書僕射傅亮為中書監，司空徐羨之、領軍將軍謝晦及亮輔政。 戊子，太尉長沙王道憐薨。
In the sixth month, on renshen, Fu Liang, Vice Director of the Masters of Writing, was made Director of the Palace Secretariat; Xu Xianzhi, Minister of Works, Xie Hui, General Who Leads the Army, and Liang were named regents. On wuzi, Grand Marshal Prince of Changsha Dao Ling died.
3
秋九月丁未，有司奏武皇帝配南郊，武敬皇后配北郊。
In the ninth month of autumn, on dingwei, the relevant offices reported that Emperor Wu should be honored at the southern suburb and Empress Wu Jing at the northern suburb.
4
冬十一月戊午，有星孛于營室。
In the eleventh month of winter, on wuwu, a comet appeared in the Encampment constellation.
5
十二月庚戌，魏軍克滑臺。
In the twelfth month, on gengxu, Wei forces took Huatai.
6
明年春正月己亥朔，大赦，改元為景平元年。 文武進位二等。 辛丑，祀南郊。 [3]虜將達奚卬破金墉，進圍虎牢。 [4]毛德祖擊虜敗之，虜退而復合。 拓跋木末又遣安平公涉歸寇青州。 [5]癸卯，河南郡失守。 乙卯，有星孛于東壁。
At the new moon of the first month of spring the next year, on jihai, there was a general amnesty and the reign title was changed to Jingping year 1. Civil and military officials were promoted two ranks. On xinchou, sacrifices were offered at the southern suburb. [3] The enemy general Daxi Ang took Jin Yong and pressed on to besiege Hulao. [4] Mao Dezu attacked the enemy and defeated them; they withdrew, then rallied again. Tuoba Mumo also sent Duke of Anping Shegui to raid Qing Province. [5] On maoyin, Henan Commandery fell. On yimao, a comet appeared in the Eastern Wall.
7
二月丁丑，太皇太后崩。 沮渠蒙遜、吐谷渾阿犲並遣使朝貢。 庚辰，[6]爵蒙遜為驃騎大將軍，[7]封河西王。 以阿犲為安西將軍、沙州刺史，封澆河公。 辛未，富陽人孫法光反，[8]寇山陰，會稽太守褚淡之遣山陰令陸劭討敗之。 [9]
In the second month, on dingchou, the grand empress dowager died. Juqu Mengxun and Tuyuhun Aqi both sent envoys to court with tribute. On gengchen, [6] Mengxun was enfeoffed as General of Agile Cavalry [7] and created Prince of Hexi. Aqi was appointed General Who Pacifies the West and Inspector of Liang Province, and enfeoffed as Duke of Qiaohe. On xinwei, Sun Faguang of Fuyang rose in revolt [8] and raided Shanyin; Kuaiji governor Chu Danzhi sent Shanyin magistrate Lu Shao to defeat him. Textual annotation marker 9.
8
三月壬寅，孝懿皇后祔葬于興寧陵。 是月，高麗國遣使朝貢。 甲子，豫州刺史劉粹遣軍襲許昌，殺虜潁川太守庾龍。 [10]乙丑，虜騎寇高平。 初虜自河北之敗，請修和親； 及聞高祖崩，因復侵擾，河、洛之地騷然矣。
In the third month, on renyin, Empress Xiao Yi was enshrined at Xingning Mausoleum. That month, Goguryeo sent envoys to court with tribute. On jiazi, Yu inspector Liu Cui sent troops to raid Xuchang and killed the enemy Yingchuan administrator Yu Long. [10] On yichou, enemy horsemen raided Gaoping. After their defeat north of the Yellow River, the northerners had sought a peace marriage; but when they learned that Gaozu had died, they invaded again; the Yellow and Luo regions were thrown into turmoil.
9
夏四月，檀道濟北征，次臨朐，焚虜攻具。 乙未，魏軍克虎牢，執司州刺史毛德祖以歸。
In the fourth month of summer, Tan Daoji marched north, camped at Linqu, and burned the enemy siege equipment. On yiwei, Wei forces took Hulao and carried off Si inspector Mao Dezu.
10
秋七月癸酉，尊所生張夫人為皇太后。 丁丑，以旱，詔赦五歲刑以下罪人。 [11]
In the seventh month of autumn, on guiyou, his birth mother Lady Zhang was made empress dowager. On dingchou, owing to drought, an edict pardoned those sentenced to five years or less. Textual annotation marker 11.
11
冬十月己未，有星孛于氐，指尾，貫攝提，向大角，仲月在危，季月掃天倉而後滅。 是歲，魏主拓跋嗣薨，子燾立。
In the tenth month of winter, on jiwei, a comet appeared in Di, aimed at Wei, crossed Sheti, and turned toward Great Horn; in mid-season it stood in Wei, in the last month it swept the Celestial Granary, then disappeared. That year, Wei ruler Tuoba Si died and his son Tao succeeded him.
12
十二月丙寅，[12]省寧州之江陽、犍為、安上三郡，合為宋昌郡。
On bingyin in the twelfth month, [12] Ning Province’s Jiangyang, Qianwei, and Anshang commanderies were abolished and merged into Songchang Commandery.
13
二年春二月癸巳朔，日有蝕之。 [13]廢南豫州刺史廬陵王義真為庶人，徙新安郡。 乙未，以皇弟義恭為冠軍將軍，南豫州刺史。 乙巳，大風，[14]天有五色雲，占者以為有兵。 高麗國遣使貢獻。 執政使使者誅義真于新安。
In year 2, at the new moon of the second month of spring, on guisi, the sun was eclipsed. [13] South Yu inspector Prince of Luling Yizhen was deposed to commoner status and exiled to Xin’an Commandery. On yiwei, the emperor’s younger brother Yigong was made General Who Crowns the Army and South Yu inspector. On yisi, a great wind rose; [14] five-colored clouds appeared in the sky, and diviners read them as a sign of war. Goguryeo sent envoys with tribute. The regents sent an agent to have Yizhen put to death at Xin’an.
14
夏五月，江州刺史王弘、南兗州刺史檀道濟入朝。 [15]帝居處所為多過失。 乙酉，皇太后令曰：
In the fifth month of summer, Jiang inspector Wang Hong and South Yan inspector Tan Daoji came to court. [15] In his daily conduct the emperor committed many faults. On yiyou, the empress dowager issued an order, saying:
15
王室不造，天禍未悔，先帝創業弗永，棄世登遐。 義符長嗣，屬當天位，不謂窮凶極悖，一至於此。 大行在殯，宇內哀惶，幸災肆於悖詞，喜容表於在慼。 至乃徵召樂府，鳩集伶官，優倡管絃，靡不備奏，珍羞甘膳，有加平日。 採擇媵御，產子就宮，靦然無怍，醜聲四達。 及懿后崩背，重加天罰，親與左右執紼歌呼，推排梓宮，抃掌笑謔，殿省備聞。 加復日夜媟狎，羣小慢戲，興造千計，費用萬端，帑藏空虛，人力殫盡。 刑罰苛虐，幽囚日增。 居帝王之位，好皁隸之役，處萬乘之尊，悅廝養之事。 親執鞭撲，毆擊無辜，以為笑樂。 穿池築觀，朝成暮毀，徵發工匠，疲極兆民。 遠近歎嗟，人神怨怒，社稷將墜，豈可復嗣守洪業，君臨萬邦。 今廢為營陽王，一依漢昌邑、晉海西故事。
The royal house is ill-fated; Heaven’s wrath has not yet lifted; the late emperor’s founding work did not long endure, and he has departed this world. Yifu, as eldest son, was fit for the throne; none could have imagined his wickedness would reach this extreme. While the late emperor still lay in state and the realm mourned, he spoke recklessly as though rejoicing in calamity and showed pleasure amid grief. He even summoned the Music Office, gathered players, and had singers, actors, and musicians perform without stint; delicacies exceeded those of ordinary days. He selected concubines and, when they bore children, brought them into the palace without shame; scandal spread in every direction. When Empress Yi died, he heaped Heaven’s punishment upon grief: he personally took mourning cords with his attendants and sang and shouted, pushed the inner coffin about, clapped his hands and laughed—the whole palace heard it. Day and night he indulged in lewd sport; petty men mocked him; constructions multiplied and expenses soared; the treasury was emptied and the people exhausted. Punishments grew harsh and cruel; prisoners increased daily. Though he sat on the imperial throne, he delighted in menial labor; though he held supreme rank, he took pleasure in stable-boy tasks. He personally wielded whip and cudgel, beating innocents for amusement. He dug ponds and built towers, finishing them by morning and tearing them down by evening; he conscripted artisans until the people were worn to exhaustion. Near and far lamented; men and spirits were enraged; the altars were about to fall—how could he still inherit the great enterprise and rule the myriad states. He is now deposed and made Prince of Yingyang, following the precedents of Han’s Prince of Changyi and Jin’s Prince of Haixi.
16
鎮〔西將軍宜都王，仁明孝弟，著自幼辰。 德業沖粹，識心明允。 宜纂洪統，光臨億兆。 主者詳依典故，以時奉迎。 未亡人嬰此百罹，雖存若隕。 永悼情事，撫心摧塞。 〕[16]
The General Who Guards the West, Prince of Yidu, is humane, bright, filial, and brotherly, renowned from youth. His virtue is pure and his judgment clear. He should inherit the great succession and rule the hundred millions. Let the responsible officials follow precedent and, at the proper time, escort him to the throne. I, who survive him, have met this hundredfold calamity; though I live, it is as though I had perished. Forever grieving these affairs, I press my heart and am overwhelmed. Closing bracket and textual annotation marker 16.
17
始徐羨之、傅亮將廢帝，諷王弘、檀道濟求赴國訃。 弘等來朝。 使中書舍人邢安泰、潘盛為內應。 是旦，道濟、謝晦領兵居前，羨之等隨後，因東掖門開，入自雲龍門。 盛等先戒宿衞，莫有禦者。 時帝於華林園為列肆，親自酤賣。 又開瀆聚土，以象破岡埭，與左右引船唱呼，以為歡樂。 夕游天淵池，即龍舟而寢。 其朝未興，兵士進，殺二侍者於帝側，傷帝指。 夫出東閤，就收璽紱，羣臣拜辭，送於東宮，遂幽於吳郡。 是日，赦死罪以下。 太后令奉還璽紱。 檀道濟入守朝堂。 六月癸丑，徐羨之等使中書舍人邢安泰弒帝於金昌亭。 帝有勇力，不即受制，突走出昌門，追以門關踣之，致殞。 時年十九。 [17]
At first Xu Xianzhi and Fu Liang planned to depose the emperor; they urged Wang Hong and Tan Daoji to request leave for the state mourning. Hong and the others came to court. They stationed Palace Secretariat attendants Xing Antai and Pan Sheng as agents within. That morning Tan Daoji and Xie Hui led troops in front, Xu Xianzhi and the others behind; when the eastern side gate opened they entered through the Cloud Dragon Gate. Sheng and the others had first warned the night guards; none resisted. At the time the emperor was in Hualin Park setting up market stalls and selling wine himself. He also opened a channel and piled earth to imitate the Pogang dam, and with his companions pulled boats, shouting for sport. In the evening he toured Tianyuan Pool and slept aboard the dragon boat. Before court had risen that morning, soldiers entered, killed two attendants beside the emperor, and wounded his finger. He went out through the eastern pavilion; they took the seals and cords; officials bowed in farewell and escorted him to the eastern palace; he was then confined in Wu Commandery. That day, those sentenced to death or below were pardoned. The empress dowager ordered the seals and cords returned. Tan Daoji entered to guard the court hall. On guichou in the sixth month, Xu Xianzhi and the others had Palace Secretariat attendant Xing Antai assassinate the emperor at Jinchang Pavilion. The emperor was strong and did not submit at once; he burst out through Chang Gate, and pursuers struck him down with the gate bar and killed him. He was nineteen. Textual annotation marker 17.

Textual collation notes
20
武帝長子也廿二史考異云：「按紀傳書諸帝皆稱廟號，獨此紀書武帝者四，而仍有稱高祖者。 又它篇例稱魏為索虜，而此紀一云魏軍克滑臺，一云魏主拓跋嗣薨，全非休文之例。 又如義熙十二年正月，以豫章公世子為西中郎將、豫州刺史。 三月，除征虜將軍、徐兗二州刺史，鎮京口。 十四年六月，除中軍將軍，副貳相國府。 若宜見於本紀，而略不及。 卷末無史臣論。 其非休文書顯然。 蓋此篇久亡，後人雜採它書以補之，故義例乖舛如此。」
He was Emperor Wu’s eldest son. Qian Daxin’s Examination of Discrepancies in the Twenty-Two Histories says: “The annals and biographies all use temple names for emperors; only this annal calls Emperor Wu by that title four times, and there are still places that call him Gaozu. Moreover, other chapters regularly call Wei the “northern barbarians,” yet this annal once says Wei forces captured Huatai and once says Wei ruler Tuoba Si died—nothing like Shen Yue’s usage. Again, in the first month of Yixi year 12, the heir of the Duke of Yuzhang was made General of the West Center and Yu inspector. In the third month he was relieved as General Who Conquers the Barbarians and made inspector of Xu and Yan, stationed at Jingkou. In the sixth month of year 14 he was relieved as General of the Center Army and made deputy to the Chancellor’s Office. Events that ought to appear in this annal are altogether omitted. At the end of the scroll there is no historian’s judgment. That this is not Shen Yue’s writing is obvious. Presumably this chapter was long lost; later men pieced it together from other books, which is why its usage is so at odds."
21
年十歲拜豫章公世子按少帝義符生於義熙二年，而據五行志授豫章世子，在義熙七年，則當云六歲，此云十歲，疑有誤。
At age ten he was invested heir of the Duke of Yuzhang. Yifu was born in Yixi year 2, while the Treatise on the Five Phases places the Yuzhang heir investiture in Yixi year 7, so it ought to read six years old; “ten” is suspected to be an error.
22
辛丑祀南郊「辛丑」各本作「辛巳」，據局本及南史改。 按是年正月己亥朔，無辛巳，初三日辛丑。
Sacrifice to the southern suburb on xinchou: all editions read “xinsi”; changed per the Bureau edition and the History of the Southern Dynasties. That year’s first month had its new moon on jihai; there was no xinsi; the third day was xinchou.
23
虜將達奚卬破金墉進圍虎牢按據魏書奚斤傳，圍虎牢者為奚斤，斤本姓達奚氏。 「卬」當是「斤」字之譌。
The enemy general Daxi Ang broke Jin Yong and besieged Hulao. According to the Wei History biography of Xi Jin, the besieger of Hulao was Xi Jin, whose clan name was originally Daxi. “Ang” is probably a corruption of the character “Jin.”
24
拓跋木末又遣安平公涉歸寇青州拓跋木末即魏主拓跋嗣。 本卷後又稱嗣。 蓋所採雜異，故前後不一致。 「安平公」各本作「平安公」，今從局本。 按索虜傳：「景平元年，虜遣安平公涉歸幡能健……東擊青州。 」涉歸幡能健即叔孫建，魏書有傳，魏道武時，曾賜爵安平公。
Tuoba Mumo also sent Duke of Anping Shegui to raid Qing Province: Tuoba Mumo is Wei ruler Tuoba Si. Later in this scroll he is again called Si. The sources were evidently mixed, so the text is inconsistent throughout. “Duke of Anping”: all editions read “Duke of Ping’an”; now following the Bureau edition. According to the Account of the Northern Barbarians: “In Jingping year 1, the enemy sent Duke of Anping Shegui Fannengjian… to attack east into Qing Province.” ” Shegui Fannengjian is Shusun Jian; the Wei History has his biography; under Emperor Daowu of Wei he had once been granted the rank Duke of Anping.
25
庚辰張熷讀史舉正云：「二月丁丑、庚辰書辛未前，誤。 」按張說是。 二月戊辰朔，初四日辛未，初十日丁丑，十三日庚辰，辛未不當在庚辰後。
Gengchen. Zhang Huang’s Corrections in Reading History says: “In the second month, dingchou and gengchen are recorded before xinwei—an error.” ” Zhang’s view is correct. The second month’s new moon was wuchen; the fourth day was xinwei, the tenth dingchou, the thirteenth gengchen—xinwei cannot come after gengchen.
26
爵蒙遜為驃騎大將軍各本皆無「驃騎」二字。 按本書文帝紀，元嘉二年，驃騎大將軍涼州牧大沮渠蒙遜改為車騎大將軍。 則各本並奪「驃騎」二字。 今據南史補。
Enfeoffing Mengxun as General of Agile Cavalry: all editions lack the two characters “Agile Cavalry.” According to this book’s Annals of Emperor Wen, in Yuanjia year 2, General of Agile Cavalry and Liang governor the great Juqu Mengxun was changed to General of Chariots and Cavalry. Thus all editions together omit “Agile Cavalry.” Now supplemented from the History of the Southern Dynasties.
27
富陽人孫法光反「孫法光」，褚叔度傳作「孫法亮」。
Sun Faguang of Fuyang rebelled: the biography of Chu Shudu has “Sun Faliang.”
28
會稽太守褚淡之遣山陰令陸劭討敗之「褚淡之」各本並作「褚談」，據褚叔度傳，時會稽太守為褚淡之，「談」蓋「淡」之形譌。 按南北朝人名後之「之」字，有時可以省去。 今仍改「談」作「淡」，並補「之」字。
Kuaiji governor Chu Danzhi sent Shanyin magistrate Lu Shao to defeat him: all editions read “Chu Tan”; Chu Shudu’s biography shows the Kuaiji governor was Chu Danzhi—“Tan” is probably a corruption of “Dan.” In Northern and Southern Dynasties personal names, the final character zhi is sometimes omitted. “Tan” is changed to “Dan” and the character zhi restored.
29
殺虜潁川太守庾龍「潁川」各本並作「潁州」，據劉粹傳改。
Killed the enemy Yingchuan administrator Yu Long: all editions read “Yingzhou”; changed per Liu Cui’s biography.
30
以旱詔赦五歲刑以下罪人各本並脫「刑」字，據南史補。
Edict pardoning those sentenced to five years or less because of drought: all editions omit “punishment”; supplemented from the History of the Southern Dynasties.
31
十二月丙寅按景平元年十二月癸巳朔，無丙寅。
Twelfth month bingyin: in Jingping year 1 the twelfth month’s new moon was guisi—there was no bingyin.
32
二年春二月癸巳朔日有蝕之「二月癸巳朔」，局本同，宋本、北監本、毛本、殿本、通鑑考異引宋略、建康實錄作「正月癸巳朔」，南史宋本紀作「二月己卯朔」。 按陳垣朔閏表，景平二年正月癸亥朔，二月壬辰朔。 查正月無癸巳，癸巳為二月初二日。 然日蝕當在朔日，是年正月祇二十九日，作二月壬辰朔者，蓋後人定朔有誤。 宋書五行志作「二月癸巳朔」。 今改從局本。
Year 2, spring, second month, new moon guisi, solar eclipse: “second month, new moon guisi”—the Bureau edition agrees; Song, Northern Directorate, Mao, Palace editions and Zizhi Tongjian Examination citing Song Lüe and Jiankang shilu have “first month, new moon guisi”; the Song annals in the History of the Southern Dynasties has “second month, new moon jimao.” Chen Yuan’s table of new moons and intercalations gives Jingping year 2 first month new moon guihai and second month renchen. The first month has no guisi; guisi is the second day of the second month. A solar eclipse should fall on the new-moon day; that year the first month had only twenty-nine days—making the second month’s new moon renchen probably reflects a later error in fixing the calendar. The Song History Treatise on the Five Phases has “second month, new moon guisi.” The text now follows the Bureau edition.
33
乙未以皇弟義恭為冠軍將軍南豫州刺史乙巳大風按陳垣朔閏表景平二年正月癸亥朔，是月無乙未、乙巳。 二月壬辰朔，初三日乙未，十三日乙巳。 可證上之春正月應作春二月。
Yiwei: younger brother Yigong made General Who Crowns the Army and South Yu inspector; yisi: great wind. Chen Yuan’s table gives Jingping year 2 first month new moon guihai—that month had no yiwei or yisi. The second month’s new moon was renchen; the third day was yiwei, the thirteenth yisi. This shows the “first month of spring” above should read “second month of spring.”
34
江州刺史王弘南兗州刺史檀道濟入朝各本並作「江州刺史檀道濟、揚州刺史王弘入朝」。 建康實錄作「江州刺史王弘、南兗州刺史檀道濟來朝」，是，今據改。 錢大昕廿二史考異云：「按是時道濟為南兗州刺史，非江州； 弘為江州刺史，非揚州也。 揚州治輦下，時以司空徐羨之領之。 紀所書皆誤。」
Jiang inspector Wang Hong and South Yan inspector Tan Daoji entered court: all editions read “Jiang inspector Tan Daoji and Yang inspector Wang Hong entered court.” Jiankang shilu has “Jiang inspector Wang Hong and South Yan inspector Tan Daoji came to court,” which is correct; the text is changed accordingly. Qian Daxin’s Examination says: “At this time Tan Daoji was South Yan inspector, not Jiang; Hong was Jiang inspector, not Yang. Yang Province governed the capital; Minister of Works Xu Xianzhi held it concurrently. What the annal records is all wrong.”
35
