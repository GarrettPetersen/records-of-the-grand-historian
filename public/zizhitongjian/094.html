【晉紀十六】起著雍困敦，盡重光單閼，凡四年。
[Jin Records 16] Spanning the years 328–331 CE (from wuzi through xinchou in the sexagenary cycle), four years in all.
3
顯宗成皇帝上之下咸和三年（ 戊子，公元三二八年）
From the lower fascicle of the reign of Emperor Cheng (Manifest Ancestor), Xianhe year 3 ( wuzi, corresponding to 328 CE)
4
春，正月，溫嶠入救建康，軍於尋陽。
In spring, the first month, Wen Jiao set out to relieve Jiankang and halted his army at Xunyang.
5
韓晃襲司馬流於慈湖； 流素懦怯，將戰，食炙不知口處，兵敗而死。
Han Huang attacked Sima Liu at Cihu; Liu had always been timid; on the eve of battle, while eating roast meat he could not even find his mouth, and after his army was routed he was killed.
6
丁未，蘇峻帥祖渙、許柳等眾二萬人，濟自橫江，登牛渚，軍於陵口。 台兵御之，屢敗。 二月，庚戌，峻至蔣陵覆舟山。 陶回謂庾亮曰：「峻知石頭有重戍，不敢直下，必向小丹楊南道步來； 宜伏兵邀之，可一戰擒也。 」亮不從。 峻果自小丹楊來，迷失道，夜行，無復部分。 亮聞，乃悔之。
On dingwei, Su Jun led Zu Huan, Xu Liu, and the rest with twenty thousand troops, crossed at Hengjiang, took Niuzhu, and encamped at Lingkou. The capital forces met them and were beaten again and again. In the second month, on gengxu, Jun reached Jiangling at Mount Fuzhou. Tao Hui told Yu Liang, "Jun knows Shitou is heavily garrisoned and will not dare come straight in; he is sure to march along the southern road through Lesser Danyang; we should lay an ambush and take him in one fight." " Liang would not heed him. Jun did come by Lesser Danyang, lost the road, marched at night, and his army fell into disorder. When Liang heard this, he regretted not having listened.
7
朝士以京邑危逼，多遣家人入東避難，左衛將軍劉超獨遷妻孥入居宮內。
With the capital under threat, many officials sent their families east to safety; only Left Guard General Liu Chao brought his wife and children into the palace.
8
詔以卞壼都督大桁東諸軍事，與侍中鐘雅帥郭默、趙胤等軍及峻戰於西陵。 壼等大敗，死傷以千數。 丙辰，峻攻青溪柵，卞壼帥諸軍拒擊，不能禁。 峻因風縱火，燒台省及諸營寺署，一時蕩盡。 壼背癰新愈，創猶未合，力疾帥左右苦戰而死； 二子□□、盱隨父後，亦赴敵而死。 其母撫屍哭曰：「父為忠臣，子為孝子，夫何恨乎！ 」丹楊尹羊曼勒兵守雲龍門，與黃門侍郎周導、廬江太守陶瞻皆戰死。 庾亮帥眾將陳於宣陽門內，未及成列，士眾皆棄甲走，亮與弟懌、條、翼及郭默、趙胤俱奔尋陽。 將行，顧謂鐘雅曰：「後事深以相委。 」雅曰：「棟折榱崩，誰之咎也！ 」亮曰：「今日之事，不容復言。 」亮乘小船，亂兵相剝掠； 亮左右射賊，誤中柁工，應弦而倒。 船上咸失色慾散，亮不動，徐曰：「此手何可使著賊！ 」眾乃安。
Bian Kun was ordered to command all forces east of the Great Pontoon Bridge; with Palace Attendant Zhong Ya he led Guo Mo, Zhao Yin, and the rest against Jun at Xiling. Kun's force was routed, with thousands killed or wounded. On bingchen, Jun assaulted the Qingxi stockade; Bian Kun led the armies in defense but could not stop him. Jun used the wind to set fires that consumed the central offices and every camp, monastery, and bureau in one sweep. Kun's back abscess had only just healed and the wound was still open; straining himself despite his illness, he led his personal guard in fierce fighting until he fell; his two sons Zhun and Xu followed their father into the fight and were also killed. Their mother embraced the bodies and wept: "A loyal father and filial sons—what more could one ask?" " Danyang intendant Yang Man held the Cloud Dragon Gate with his troops; Yellow Gate Attendant Zhou Dao and Lujiang administrator Tao Zhan all fell in the fighting. Yu Liang drew up his generals inside the Xuanyang Gate, but before the ranks were formed the troops threw off their armor and ran. Liang fled to Xunyang with his brothers Yi, Tiao, and Yi and with Guo Mo and Zhao Yin. As he was leaving, he turned to Zhong Ya and said, "I leave what follows entirely in your hands." " Ya replied, "When the ridgepole snaps and the rafters fall, whose fault is that?" " Liang said, "There is nothing more to say about today." " Liang took a small boat; in the chaos soldiers robbed one another; his men shot at marauders and accidentally hit the helmsman, who dropped at once. Everyone on board turned pale and nearly panicked; Liang did not move and said calmly, "How could we let our arrows strike our own people!" " The men then calmed down.
9
峻兵入台城，司徒導謂侍中褚翜曰：「至尊當御正殿，君可啟令速出。 」翜即入上閤，躬自抱帝登太極前殿； 導及光祿大夫陸曄、荀崧、尚書張闓共登御床，擁衛帝。 以劉超為右衛將軍，使與鐘雅、褚翜侍立左右，太常孔愉朝服守宗廟。 時百官奔散，殿省蕭然。 峻兵既入，叱褚翜令下翜正立不動，呵之曰：「蘇冠軍來覲至尊，軍人豈得侵逼！ 」由是峻兵不敢上殿，突入後宮，宮人及太后左右侍人皆見掠奪。 峻兵驅役百官，光祿勳王彬等皆被捶撻，令負提登蔣山。 裸剝士女，皆以壞席苦苫草自鄣，無草者坐地以土自覆； 哀號之聲，震動內外。
When Jun's troops entered the Terrace City, Grand Mentor Wang Dao told Palace Attendant Chu Pou, "His Majesty should take the main throne hall—please announce and bring him out at once." " Pou went straight to the upper pavilion, lifted the emperor in his arms, and carried him to the front hall of the Hall of Supreme Pole; Dao, Household Counsellor Lu Ye, Xun Song, and Secretary Zhang Kai climbed onto the imperial couch to shield the emperor. Liu Chao was appointed Right Guard General to stand with Zhong Ya and Chu Pou at the emperor's side, while Minister of Ceremonies Kong Yu in full court dress guarded the ancestral temple. By then the officials had scattered, and the palace halls stood empty. When Jun's troops entered, they ordered Chu Pou down; Pou stood firm and rebuked them: "Champion-General Su has come to pay his respects to His Majesty—soldiers must not crowd the throne!" " Jun's men dared not enter the hall; they stormed the inner palace instead, and palace women and the empress dowager's attendants were looted. Jun's men pressed the officials into labor; Household Excellency Wang Bin and others were beaten and forced to haul burdens up Mount Jiang. Men and women were stripped; they shielded themselves with torn mats and coarse rush, and those without even rush sat on the ground and covered themselves with dirt; their cries of anguish shook the city inside and out.
10
初，姑孰既陷，尚書左丞孔坦謂人曰：「觀峻之勢，必破台城，自非戰士，不須戎服。 」及台城陷，戎服者多死，白衣者無他。
Earlier, after Gushu fell, Left Assistant Kong Tan had told people, "Jun's momentum means the Terrace City will fall—unless you are a soldier, do not wear armor." " When the city fell, many in armor died, while those in civilian dress were spared.
11
時官有布二十萬匹，金銀五千斤，錢億萬，絹數萬匹，他物稱是，峻盡費之； 太官惟有燒餘米數石以供御膳。
The treasury held two hundred thousand bolts of cloth, five thousand jin of gold and silver, a hundred million cash, and tens of thousands of bolts of silk, with other stores to match; Jun squandered it all; the Imperial Kitchen had only a few piculs of fire-scorched rice left for the emperor's table.
12
或謂鐘雅曰：「君性亮直，必不容於寇仇，盍早為之計！ 」雅曰：「國亂不能匡，君危不能濟，各遁逃以求免，何以為臣！ 」
Someone urged Zhong Ya, "You are forthright by nature; the rebels will never spare you—why not act while you can?" " Ya replied, "When the realm is in chaos and the ruler in peril, if every man flees to save himself, what sort of minister is that?" "
13
丁巳，峻稱詔大赦，惟庾亮兄弟不在原例。 以王導有德望，猶使以本官居己之右。 祖約為侍中、太尉、尚書令，峻自為驃騎將軍、錄尚書事，許柳為丹楊尹，馬雄為左衛將軍，祖渙為驍騎將軍。 弋陽王羕詣峻，稱述峻功，峻復以羕為西陽王、太宰、錄尚書事。
On dingsi, Jun proclaimed a general amnesty by imperial edict, excluding the Yu Liang brothers. Because of Wang Dao's prestige, Jun let him keep his rank and seat him to his own right. Zu Yue became Palace Attendant, Grand Commandant, and Director of the Masters of Writing; Jun took for himself General of Agile Cavalry and Recorder of the Masters of Writing; Xu Liu became Danyang intendant, Ma Xiong Left Guard General, and Zu Huan General of Valiant Cavalry. Prince of Yiyang Sima Yi came to Jun and praised his exploits; Jun in return made him Prince of Xiyang, Grand Tutor, and Recorder of the Masters of Writing.
14
峻遣兵攻吳國內史庾冰，冰不能御，棄郡奔會稽，至浙江，峻購之甚急。 吳鈴下卒引冰入船，以蘧蒢覆之，呤嘯鼓枻，溯流而去。 每逢邏所，輒以杖叩船曰：「何處覓庾冰，庚冰正在此。 」人以為醉，不疑之，冰僅免。 峻以侍中蔡謨為吳國內史。
Jun sent troops against Wu interior administrator Yu Bing; Bing could not hold the commandery, fled toward Kuaiji, and by the time he reached the Zhe River Jun had posted a heavy price on his head. A Wu commandery runner took Bing aboard, covered him with rush matting, and rowed upstream humming and whistling as though drunk. At every patrol station he would rap the boat with his staff and shout, "Where's Yu Bing? Geng Bing's right here!" " They took him for a drunk and never searched the boat; Bing barely got away. Jun made Palace Attendant Cai Mo the new Wu interior administrator.
15
溫嶠聞建康不守，號慟； 人有候之者，悲哭相對。 庾亮至尋陽，宣太后詔，以嶠為驃騎將軍、開府儀同三司，又加徐州刺史郗鑒司空。 嶠曰：「今日當以滅賊為急，未有功而先拜官，將何以示天下！ 」遂不受。 嶠素重亮，亮雖奔敗，嶠愈推奉之，分兵給亮。
When Wen Jiao learned that Jiankang had fallen, he broke into loud mourning; and anyone who came to see him joined in weeping face to face. When Yu Liang reached Xunyang he announced the empress dowager's edict appointing Jiao General of Agile Cavalry with privilege of an independent staff, and promoting Xu Province inspector Xi Jian to Grand Marshal. Jiao said, "Our urgent task is to destroy the rebels—how can we accept promotion before we have won? What would the empire think?" " He declined the appointment. Jiao had always respected Liang; despite Liang's rout, Jiao treated him with even greater deference and gave him a share of the army.
16
後趙大赦，改元太和。 三月，丙子，庾太后以憂崩。
Later Zhao declared a general amnesty and adopted the era name Taihe. In the third month, on bingzi, Empress Dowager Yu died of grief.
17
蘇峻南屯於湖。
Su Jun moved south and encamped at Yuhu.
18
夏，四月，後趙將石堪攻宛，南陽太守王國降之； 遂進攻祖約軍於淮上。 約將陳光起兵攻約，約左右閻禿，貌類約，光謂為約而擒之。 約逾垣獲免，光奔後趙。
In summer, the fourth month, Later Zhao general Shi Kan attacked Wan, and Nanyang administrator Wang Guo surrendered; then pressed on to attack Zu Yue's army on the Huai. Yue's general Chen Guang rose against him; Yan Tu, a man at Yue's side who looked like him, was mistaken for Yue and captured. Yue climbed a wall and escaped; Guang fled to Later Zhao.
19
壬申，葬明穆皇后於武平陵。
On renshen, Empress Mingmu was interred at Wuping Mausoleum.
20
庾亮、溫嶠將起兵討蘇峻，而道路斷絕，不知建康聲聞。 會南陽范汪至尋陽，言「峻政令不壹，貪暴縱橫，滅亡已兆，雖強易弱，朝廷有倒懸之急，宜時進討。 」嶠深納之。 亮辟汪參護軍事。
Yu Liang and Wen Jiao were preparing to march against Su Jun, but the roads were cut and they had no word from Jiankang. Fan Wang of Nanyang arrived at Xunyang and said, "Jun's rule is chaotic and his greed and violence unrestrained—the omens of collapse are already clear. Though he seems strong he is easily beaten. The court is in desperate peril; you should attack now." " Jiao took his advice to heart. Liang appointed Wang to his military staff.
21
亮、嶠互相推為盟主，嶠從弟充曰：「陶征西位重兵強，宜共推之。 」嶠乃遣督護王愆期詣荊州，邀陶侃與之同赴國難。 侃猶以不豫顧命為恨，答曰：「吾疆場外將，不敢越局。 」嶠屢說，不能回； 乃順侃意，遣使謂之曰：「仁公且守，僕當先下。 」使者去已二日，平南參軍滎陽毛寶別使還，聞之，說嶠曰：「凡舉大事，當與天下共之。 師克在和，不宜異同。 假令可疑，猶當外示不覺，況自為攜貳邪！ 宜急追信改書，言必應俱進； 若不及前信，當更遣使。 」嶠意悟，即追使者，改書； 侃果許之，遣督護龔登帥兵詣嶠。 嶠有眾七千，於是列上尚書，陳祖約、蘇峻罪狀，移告征鎮，灑泣登舟。
Liang and Jiao each declined leadership in favor of the other; Jiao's cousin Wen Chong said, "General Tao the Western Campaigner outranks us and commands the strongest army—we should make him our leader." " Jiao sent Supervisor Wang Yanqi to Jing Province to invite Tao Kan to join them in saving the realm. Kan still resented being left out of the deathbed regency, and replied, "I am a frontier commander—I dare not overstep my post." " Jiao pleaded repeatedly but could not move him; so he yielded and sent word: "My lord, hold your ground; I will march first." " Two days after the messenger left, staff officer Mao Bao of Xingyang returned from another errand, heard what had been sent, and told Jiao, "Any great undertaking must be shared with all under heaven." Victory depends on unity; you must not sow discord. Even if he were suspect, you should show no sign of it—how much less deliberately divide the alliance! You must overtake that messenger and change the letter to say you will advance together; if you cannot catch him, send another envoy at once. " Jiao saw the point, overtook the messenger, and rewrote the letter; Kan agreed and sent Supervisor Gong Deng with troops to join Jiao. With seven thousand men, Jiao memorialized the Masters of Writing listing the crimes of Zu Yue and Su Jun, sent proclamations to the regional commanders, and boarded his fleet in tears.
22
陶侃復追龔登還。 嶠遺侃書曰：「夫軍有進而無退，可增而不可減。 近已移檄遠近，言於盟府，刻後月半大舉，諸郡軍並在路次，惟須仁公軍至，便齊進耳。 仁公今召軍還，疑惑遠近，成敗之由，將在於此。 僕才輕任重，實憑仁公篤愛，遠稟成規； 至於首啟戎行，不敢有辭，僕與仁公，如首尾相衛，脣齒相依也。 恐或者不達高旨，將謂仁公緩於討賊，此聲難追。 僕與仁公並受方岳之任，安危休戚，理既同之。 且自頃之顧，綢繆往來，情深義重，一旦有急，亦望仁公悉眾見救，況社稷之難乎！ 今日之憂，豈惟僕一州，文武莫不翹企。 假令此州不守，約、峻樹置官長於此，荊楚西逼強胡，東接逆賊，因之以饑饉，將來之危，乃當甚於此州之今日也。 仁公進當為大晉之忠臣，參桓、文之功； 退當以慈父之情，雪愛子之痛。 今約、峻凶逆無道，痛感天地，人心齊壹，咸皆切齒。 今之進討，若以石投卵耳； 苟復召兵還，是為敗於幾成也。 願深察所陳！ 」王愆期謂侃曰：「蘇峻，豺狼也，如得遂志，四海雖廣，公寧有容足之地乎！ 」侃深感悟，即戎服登舟； 瞻喪至不臨，晝夜兼道而進。
Tao Kan then recalled Gong Deng. Jiao wrote to Kan: "An army may advance but never retreat; it may grow but never shrink." I have already proclaimed to the alliance that we strike in mid-month; every commandery army is on the road, waiting only for your troops to march together." If you recall your army now, you will sow doubt everywhere, and success or failure may turn on this alone. My abilities are slight for so heavy a charge; I rely on your steadfast support and take my guidance from you; as for leading the march, I make no complaint—you and I, my lord, are head and tail, lips and teeth. I fear some will not understand your intent and will say you are slow to punish the rebels—a rumor once spread is hard to recall. We both hold regional command; in safety and danger, weal and woe, our fates are bound together. Our recent friendship has been close and our obligations weighty—if trouble came I would still expect you to march to my aid with all your forces; how much more now, when the altars of state are at stake! Today's crisis is not mine alone—every official in the empire watches and waits. If we lose this province and Yue and Jun set up their officials here, Jing and Chu will be squeezed between the barbarians in the west and the rebels in the east; with famine on top of that, the danger ahead will exceed what we face today. If you advance, you will be a loyal minister of Jin, ranking with Duke Huan and Duke Wen of old; if you hold back, you can as a grieving father avenge a beloved son's death. Yue and Jun are monstrous rebels whose crimes shock heaven and earth; all men are of one mind and grind their teeth in rage. To march against them now is like throwing a stone at an egg; if you recall your army again, you will snatch defeat from the brink of victory. I beg you to weigh these words carefully! " Wang Yanqi told Kan, "Su Jun is a wolf. If he gets his way, will you have room to stand anywhere under heaven?" " Deeply moved, Kan put on armor and boarded his ship at once; he did not pause even when Tao Zhan's funeral arrived, but marched day and night without rest.
23
郗鑒在廣陵，城孤糧少，逼近胡寇，人無固志。 得詔書，即流涕誓眾，入赴國難，將士爭奮。 遣將軍夏侯長等間行謂溫嶠曰：「或聞賊欲挾天子東入會稽，當先立營壘，屯據要害，既防其越逸，又斷賊糧運，然後清野堅壁以待賊。 賊攻城不拔，野無所掠，東道既斷，糧運自絕，必自潰矣。 」嶠深以為然。
Xi Jian held Guangling, where the city was isolated, provisions were low, barbarian raiders pressed near, and morale was uncertain. When the edict arrived he wept before his troops, swore them to the cause, and marched to save the realm; officers and men vied to follow. He sent General Xiahou Chang and others by secret route to Wen Jiao: "Some say the rebels mean to take the emperor east to Kuaiji. We should first build camps at key points to block their escape and cut their supply lines, then strip the countryside and hold our walls until they come." They will fail to take our cities, find nothing to loot in the fields, and with the eastern route cut and supplies gone, they will collapse on their own. " Jiao agreed wholeheartedly.
24
五月，陶侃帥眾至尋陽。 議者咸謂侃欲誅庾亮以謝天下； 亮甚懼，用溫嶠計，詣侃拜謝。 侃驚，止之曰：「庾元規乃拜陶士行邪！ 」亮引咎自責，風止可觀，侃不覺釋然，曰：「君侯修石頭以擬老子，今日反見求邪！ 」即與之談宴終日，遂與亮、嶠同趣建康。 戎卒四萬，旌旗七百餘里，鉦鼓之聲，震於遠近。
In the fifth month Tao Kan brought his army to Xunyang. Many believed Kan intended to execute Yu Liang to satisfy the realm; Liang was terrified and, on Wen Jiao's advice, went to Kan to apologize in person. Kan was astonished and stopped him: "Yu Yuangui bowing to Tao Shixing!" " Liang took all blame on himself with such dignity that Kan's anger melted. He said, "You fortified Shitou to rival me—yet today you come begging my pardon?" " They talked and feasted all day, then marched with Liang and Jiao toward Jiankang. Forty thousand soldiers marched under banners stretching seven hundred li; gongs and drums thundered across the land.
25
蘇峻聞西方兵起，用參軍賈寧計，自姑孰還據石頭，分兵以拒侃等。
When Su Jun heard of the western armies, he followed staff officer Jia Ning's advice, withdrew from Gushu to Shitou, and divided his forces to block Kan and the allies.
26
乙未，峻逼遷帝於石頭。 司徒導固爭，不從。 帝哀泣升車，宮中慟哭。 時天大雨，道路泥濘，劉超、鐘雅步侍左右。 峻給馬，不肯乘，而悲哀慷慨。 峻聞而惡之，然未敢殺也。 以其親信許方等補司馬督、殿中監，外托宿衛，內實防禦超等。 峻以倉屋為帝宮，日來帝前肆丑言。 劉超、鐘雅與右光祿大夫荀崧、金紫光祿大夫華恆、尚書荀邃、侍中丁潭侍從，不離帝側。 時饑饉，米貴，峻問遺，超一無所受，繾綣朝夕，臣節愈恭； 雖居幽厄之中，超猶啟帝，授《孝經》、《論語》。
On yiwei, Jun forced the emperor to relocate to Shitou. Grand Mentor Wang Dao protested fiercely but was overruled. The emperor wept as he boarded the carriage; the palace echoed with wailing. Rain poured down and the roads were mud; Liu Chao and Zhong Ya walked beside the carriage. Jun offered horses; they refused and walked on, grief and defiance plain in their bearing. Jun hated them for it but did not dare kill them. He installed his confidant Xu Fang and others as palace guards, ostensibly to protect the emperor but in fact to watch Chao and his companions. Jun housed the emperor in a granary and came each day to heap abuse on him. Liu Chao, Zhong Ya, Xun Song, Hua Heng, Xun Sui, and Ding Tan never left the emperor's side. Famine made rice costly; Jun sent provisions, but Chao accepted nothing and attended the emperor morning and night with ever greater devotion; even in captivity Chao continued to instruct the emperor in the Classic of Filial Piety and the Analects.

29

Tan's mother Lady Sun told him, "Give your life for the right cause—do not let my age hold you back!" " She sent every household servant to the army and sold her jewelry for war funds. Cai Mo resigned his post immediately so Bing could resume his former command.
30
蘇峻聞東方兵起，遣其將管商、張健、弘徽等拒之； 虞潭等與戰，互有勝負，未能得前。
When Su Jun heard of the eastern uprising, he sent Guan Shang, Zhang Jian, Hong Hui, and others to oppose it; Yu Tan and the allies fought them to a standstill and could not break through.
31
陶侃、溫嶠軍於茄子浦； 嶠以南兵習水，蘇峻兵便步，令將士：「有上岸者死！ 」會峻送米萬斛饋祖約，約遣司馬桓撫等迎之。 毛寶帥千人為嶠前鋒，告其眾曰：「兵法：『軍令有所不從』，豈可視賊可擊，不上岸擊之邪！ 」乃擅往襲撫，悉獲其米，斬獲萬計，約由是饑乏。 嶠表寶為廬江太守。
Tao Kan and Wen Jiao encamped at Qiezi Ford; Jiao said the southern armies knew water while Su Jun's men fought best on land, and ordered: "Anyone who goes ashore dies!" " Jun was sending ten thousand hu of rice to Zu Yue; Yue sent Staff Officer Huan Fu and others to fetch it. Mao Bao led a thousand men as vanguard and told his troops, "The art of war says some orders may be disobeyed—how can we see the enemy vulnerable and not attack ashore!" " He attacked on his own, seized all the grain, and killed or captured tens of thousands; Yue's army soon went hungry. Jiao recommended Bao for appointment as Lujiang administrator.
32

Jian crossed the river with his army and joined Kan at Qiezi Ford; Yong Province inspector Wei Gai brought his troops as well.
33

34

Liang sent his seal of office to apologize; Kan replied, "The ancients lost three battles—you have only lost two; the crisis is too grave for repeated apologies." " Liang's aide Yin Rong came to apologize: "The general acted on his own—this was not our decision." " Wang Zhang then said, "I acted on my own—the general knew nothing." " Kan said, "Once Yin Rong was the gentleman and Wang Zhang the scoundrel; now Wang Zhang is the gentleman and Yin Rong the scoundrel." "
35

When Huan Yi, Xuancheng administrator, heard the capital had fallen, he wept openly and marched to Jing county. Many provinces were sending envoys to surrender; Pi Hui urged Yi to open talks with Jun to stave off immediate attack. Yi said, "The state has honored me deeply; my duty is to die for it—how can I shame myself by treating with a rebel!" If I fail, that is fate." " Yi sent General Yu Zong to hold Lanshi; Jun sent Han Huang to attack him. As Zong's line was breaking, his men urged retreat. Zong said, "Lord Huan has shown me great favor; I owe him my life." I cannot betray him any more than he can betray the state." " He fought to the death. Huang then besieged Yi; in the sixth month the city fell, Yi was captured, and executed.
36
諸軍初至石頭，即欲決戰，陶侃曰：「賊眾方盛，難與爭鋒，當以歲月，智計破之。 」既而屢戰無功，監軍部將李根請築白石壘，侃從之。 夜築壘，至曉而成。 聞峻軍嚴聲，諸將咸懼其來攻。 孔坦曰：「不然。 若峻攻壘，必須東北風急，令我水軍不得往救； 今天清靜，賊必不來。 所以嚴者，必遣軍出江乘，掠京口以東矣。 」已而果然。 侃使庾亮以二千人守白石，峻帥步騎萬餘四面攻之，不克。

" After several failed assaults, supervising general Li Gen proposed building a fort at Baishi, and Kan agreed. They built it overnight; by dawn it stood complete. Hearing Jun's troops mustering, the generals feared an immediate assault. Kong Tan said, "Not so." If Jun meant to attack the fort, he would need a fierce northeast wind to keep our fleet from helping; the sky is clear today—the rebels will not come. They are mustering because he is sending troops through Jiangcheng to raid east of Jingkou." " He was right. Kan left Yu Liang with two thousand men at Baishi; Jun attacked with over ten thousand infantry and cavalry from all sides but failed.
37
王舒、虞潭等數與峻兵戰，不利。 孔坦曰：「本不須召郗公，遂使東門無限。 今宜遣還，雖晚，猶勝不也。 」侃乃令鑒與後將軍郭默還據京口，立大業、曲阿、庱亭三壘以分峻之兵勢，使郭默守大業。
Wang Shu and Yu Tan fought Jun's forces repeatedly without success. Kong Tan said, "We should never have summoned Xi Jian and left the eastern front uncovered." Send him back now—late as it is, that is still better than leaving him here." " Kan sent Jian and Rear General Guo Mo back to hold Jingkou, building forts at Daye, Qu'a, and Chanting to divide Jun's forces, with Guo Mo at Daye.

On renchen, Wei Gai died.
39

Zu Yue sent Zu Huan and Huan Fu to raid Penkou. Tao Kan heard and prepared to attack them himself. Mao Bao said, "The allied army depends on you—you must not leave; let me go." " Kan agreed. Huan and Fu passed Wan and attacked Qiao administrator Huan Xuan. Bao went to his aid and was beaten. An arrow pierced his thigh and the saddle; he had a man stand on the saddle to pull it out, and blood filled his boots. He counterattacked, routed them, and Xuan escaped to join Wen Jiao. Bao attacked Yue's army at Dongguan and took the Hefei garrison, then returned to Shitou when Jiao recalled him.
40
祖約諸將陰與後趙通謀，許為內應。 後趙將石聰，石堪引兵濟淮，攻壽春。 秋，七月，約眾潰，奔歷陽，聰等虜壽春二萬餘戶而歸。
Several of Zu Yue's generals secretly plotted with Later Zhao to act as inside collaborators. Later Zhao generals Shi Cong and Shi Kan crossed the Huai and attacked Shouchun. In autumn, the seventh month, Yue's army collapsed and fled to Liyang; Cong and the others carried off more than twenty thousand Shouchun households and withdrew.
41
後趙中山公虎帥眾四萬自軹關西入，擊趙河東。 應之者五十餘縣，遂進攻蒲阪。 趙主曜遣河間王述發氐、羌之眾屯秦州以備張駿、楊難敵，自將中外精銳水陸諸軍以救蒲阪，自衛關北濟； 虎懼，引退。 曜追之，八月，及於高候，與虎戰，大破之，斬石瞻； 枕屍二百餘里，收其資仗億計，虎奔朝歌。 曜濟自大陽，攻石生於金墉，決千金堨以灌之。 分遣諸將攻汲郡、河內，後趙滎陽太守尹矩、野王太守張進等皆降之。 襄國大震。
Later Zhao Duke of Zhongshan Shi Hu led forty thousand men west through Zhi Pass and struck at Zhao's Hedong region. More than fifty counties answered his call, and he pressed on to attack Puban. Zhao's ruler Liu Yao sent Prince of Hejian Liu Shu to raise Di and Qiang troops and hold Qin Province against Zhang Jun and Yang Nandi, while he himself led the court's finest land and river forces to relieve Puban, crossing north from Wei Pass; Hu was frightened and pulled back. Yao pursued; in the eighth month he caught Hu at Gaohou, fought a great battle, shattered his army, and beheaded Shi Zhan; Corpses lay stacked for more than two hundred li; he took arms and gear beyond counting; Hu fled to Chaoge. Yao crossed from Dayang and attacked Shi Sheng at Jinyong, breaking the Qianjin dyke to flood the city. He sent generals against Ji Prefecture and Henei; Yin Ju, Later Zhao's governor of Xingyang, Zhang Jin, governor of Yewang, and others all submitted. Xiangguo was thrown into panic.
42

Zhang Jun gathered his army, planning to exploit the opening and strike Chang'an. Court Gentleman Su Xun of the Bureau of Justice remonstrated: "Though Liu Yao is campaigning in the east, his son Yin still holds Chang'an. The city is not to be taken lightly. Even if we win a small gain, should he abandon his eastern campaign and turn to fight us— no one can say when calamity would fall." Zhang Jun then abandoned the plan. Su Jun's inner circle—Lu Yong, Kuang Shu, and Jia Ning—heard that Zu Yue had been beaten and, fearing the enterprise would collapse, urged Jun to kill all the chief ministers, including Grand Tutor Wang Dao, and raise a new set of trusted men; Jun had always held Dao in esteem and refused. Yong and the others again wavered toward Jun; Dao sent his staff officer Yuan Dan to win Yong over in secret. In the ninth month, on wushen, Dao took his two sons and fled with Yong to Baishi. Dan was a great-grandson of Yuan Huan.
43
陶侃、溫嶠等與蘇峻久相持不決，峻分遣諸將東西攻掠，所向多捷，人情心匈懼。 朝士之奔西軍者皆曰：「峻狡黠有膽決，其徒驍勇，所向無敵。 若天討有罪，則峻終滅亡； 止以人事言之，未易除也。 」溫嶠怒曰：「諸君怯懦，乃更譽賊！ 」及累戰不勝，嶠亦憚之。
Tao Kan, Wen Jiao, and the others had been deadlocked with Su Jun for a long time; Jun sent generals east and west to raid, winning almost everywhere they went, and fear spread through every heart. Court officials who had fled to the western army kept saying: "Jun is cunning and resolute, his men are fierce—nothing can stop them. If Heaven punishes the guilty, Jun will be destroyed in the end; but judged by human strength alone, he will not be easy to remove." " Wen Jiao burst out in anger: "You are cowards—and you praise the rebel! " After one defeat after another, Jiao too began to fear him.
44
嶠軍食盡，貸於陶侃。 侃怒曰：「使君前雲不憂無良將及兵食，惟欲得老僕為主耳。 今數戰皆北，良將安在！ 荊州接胡、蜀二虜，當備不虞； 若復無食，僕便欲西歸，更思良算。 徐來殄賊，不為晚也。 」嶠曰：「凡師克在和，古之善教也。 光武之濟昆陽，曹公之拔官渡，以寡敵眾，杖義故也。 峻、約小豎，凶逆滔天，何憂不滅！ 峻驟勝而驕，自謂無前，今挑之戰，可一鼓而擒也。 奈何捨垂立之功，設進退之計乎！ 且天子幽逼，社稷危殆，乃四海臣子肝腦塗地之日。 嶠等與公並受國恩，事若克濟，則臣主同祚； 如其不捷，當灰身以謝先帝耳。 今之事勢，義無旋踵，譬如騎虎，安可中下哉！ 公若違眾獨返，人心必沮； 沮眾敗事，義旗將回指於公矣。 」毛寶言於嶠曰：「下官能留陶公。 」乃往說侃曰：「公本應鎮蕪湖，為南北勢援，前既已下，勢不可還。 且軍政有進無退，非直整齊三軍，示眾必死而已，亦謂退無所據，終至滅亡。 往者杜弢非不強盛，公竟滅之，何至於峻，獨不可破邪！ 賊亦畏死，非皆勇健，公可試與寶兵，使上岸斷賊資糧。 若寶不立效，然後公去，人心不恨矣。 」侃然之，加寶督護而遣之。 竟陵太守李陽說侃曰：「今大事若不濟，公雖有粟，安得而食諸！ 」侃乃分米五萬石以餉嶠軍。 毛寶燒峻句容、湖孰積聚，峻軍乏食，侃遂留不去。
Jiao's army ran out of food and asked Tao Kan for supplies. Kan said angrily: "You said before that you had no worry about good generals or grain—you only wanted this old servant to take command. Now you have lost battle after battle—where are those good generals! Jing Province faces the Hu and Shu barbarians on two sides; we must stay ready for surprise; if we run short of grain again, I mean to go west and think of a better plan. We can still wipe out the rebels in good time—it will not be too late." " Jiao said: "Every army wins through unity—that is what the ancients taught best. Guangwu's rescue at Kunyang and Cao Gong's victory at Guandu—both used the few against the many because they stood on righteousness. Jun and Yue are petty rebels whose crimes reach heaven—why doubt they can be destroyed! Jun's streak of victories has made him arrogant; he thinks himself invincible. Provoke him now and we can take him at one stroke. Why throw away a victory within reach and talk of advancing and retreating! The emperor is held captive and the altars of state are in peril—this is the day when every subject in the realm should lay down life and limb. You and I alike have received the state's grace; if we succeed, lord and ministers share the reward; if we fail, we should burn our bodies to answer to the late emperor. The situation now allows no turning back—like riding a tiger, how can one get off halfway! If you go against the host and return alone, every heart will sink; once morale breaks and the cause fails, the banner of righteousness will turn on you." " Mao Bao said to Jiao: "Your humble officer can keep Lord Tao here. " He then went to Kan and said: "You were meant to hold Wuhu as the pivot between north and south. Having come down already, you cannot turn back. In war one advances, never retreats—not only to drill the three armies and show that all must die, but because retreat leaves nowhere to stand and ends in ruin. Du Tao was no weak foe, yet you destroyed him in the end—why should Jun alone be impossible to break! The rebels fear death too; not all are heroes. Give Bao troops and let him go ashore to cut their grain. If Bao fails, then you may leave—and no one will resent you." " Kan agreed, made Bao superintendent of defense, and sent him off. " Jingling Governor Li Yang said to Kan: "If the great cause fails now, though you have grain, who will eat it! " Kan then sent fifty thousand shi of rice to supply Jiao's army. Mao Bao burned Jun's stores at Jurong and Hushu; Jun's army ran short of food, and Kan stayed.
45

Zhang Jian, Han Huang, and others pressed the attack on Daye; inside the fortress water ran out and men drank foul drainage. Guo Mo was afraid; he broke out in secret and escaped, leaving troops to hold the fortress. Xi Jian was at Jingkou; when his soldiers heard of it they all turned pale. Staff officer Cao Na said: "Daye is Jingkou's shield. If it falls at once, rebel troops will come straight through and we cannot stop them. I ask that we fall back to Guangling and wait for a later move." " Jian called his officers together and rebuked Na: "I bear the late emperor's charge of trust. Even to die and lie in the nine springs would not repay it. Now the enemy is close and every heart is in peril—you are my closest aide, yet you raise division. How am I to lead the loyal host and hold the three armies firm! " He was about to behead him but at last was persuaded to spare him.
46

The next day Liang again prostrated himself to beg forgiveness, asked to retire from office, and wished to take his entire household and flee into remote mountains and seas. The emperor sent a personal edict through the Minister of the Secretariat and a Palace Attendant to comfort him: "This is a crisis of the realm—not your fault, Uncle." " Liang submitted a memorial of self-accusation: "Zu Yue and Su Jun ran rampant in treason—and I set it in motion. Dismemberment and execution would not atone to the spirits of the seven temples or answer the reproach of the empire." On what grounds could the court rank me among other men? In winter, the twelfth month, an edict summoned Rear General Guo Mo to serve as General of the Right Army. Mo preferred frontier command to palace guard duty and appealed to Yin on personal grounds. Yin replied: "That is not for a petty man like me to decide." " As Mo prepared to answer the summons, he asked Yin for supplies. Yin refused, and Mo came to resent him. Yin's chief clerk Zhang Man and others had long looked down on Mo, sometimes receiving him while half dressed, and Mo gnashed his teeth in fury. On the La festival day Yin sent Mo pork and wine; Mo threw them into the water before the messenger. Just then the relevant offices memorialized: "The court is bankrupt and officials go unpaid, relying solely on grain shipped from Jiangzhou—yet Yin fills the roads with his private trade, putting personal profit before public duty. " He entered the inner chamber, dragged Yin down, and beheaded him; Then he seized Yin's aides Zhang Man and others, falsely charged them with treason, and beheaded them all. He sent Yin's head to the capital, forged an edict, and proclaimed it throughout the court and realm. He plundered Yin's daughter and concubines along with gold and jewels and returned to his boat. At first he claimed he would sail to the capital, but instead he stayed at Yin's former residence. He tried to win over Huan Xuan, administrator of Qiaoguo, but Xuan held firm and refused.

If you mean to march, you should wait for orders from the capital. " Kan said sternly: "The throne is still young—edicts do not issue from a man's own breast." Liu Yin was a man the court honored. 83
初，祖逖有胡奴曰王安，逖甚愛之。 在雍丘，謂安曰：「石勒是汝種類，吾亦無在爾一人。 」厚資送而遣之。 安以勇干，仕趙，為左衛將軍。 及約之誅，安歎曰：「豈可使祖土稚無後乎？ 」乃往就市觀刑。 逖庶子道重，始十歲，安竊取以歸，匿之，變服為沙門。 及石氏亡，道重複歸江南。
Earlier, Zu Ti had a barbarian bondservant named Wang An whom he greatly favored. At Yongqiu he told Wang An: "Shi Le is of your people, and I have no need to keep you here alone. " He gave Wang An generous provisions and sent him on his way. Wang An, known for courage and ability, entered Zhao service and became General of the Left Guard. When Zu Yue was executed, Wang An sighed: "How can I let Zu Tuzhi die without heirs? " He went to the marketplace to watch the execution. Zu Ti's bastard son Daozhong was only ten. Wang An stole him away, hid him, and disguised him as a Buddhist monk. When the Shi regime fell, Daozhong returned to the south.
84
郭默欲南據豫章，會太尉侃兵至，默出戰，不利，入城固守，聚米為壘，以示有餘。 侃築土山臨之。 三月，庾亮兵至湓口，諸軍大集。 夏，五月，乙卯，默將宋侯縛默父子出降。 侃斬默於軍門，傳首建康，同黨死者四十人。 詔以侃都督江州，領刺史； 以鄧岳督交、廣諸軍事，領廣州刺史。 侃還巴陵，因移鎮武昌。 庾亮還蕪湖，辭爵賞不受。
Guo Mo tried to hold Yuzhang in the south, but when Grand Commandant Tao Kan's army arrived he gave battle, was defeated, retreated into the city, and piled rice into ramparts to feign abundance. Tao Kan built siege mounds overlooking the city. In the third month Yu Liang's forces reached Pukou and the allied armies massed. In summer, the fifth month, on yimao, Guo Mo's general Song Hou bound Mo and his son and surrendered. Tao Kan executed Guo Mo at the camp gate, sent his head to Jiankang, and forty accomplices were put to death. An edict appointed Tao Kan commander of Jiang Province and concurrent provincial governor; Deng Yue was made overseer of military affairs in Jiao and Guang and governor of Guang Province. Tao Kan returned to Baling and then transferred his headquarters to Wuchang. Yu Liang returned to Wuhu and declined all titles and rewards.
85
趙將劉征帥眾數千，浮海抄東南諸縣，殺南沙都尉許儒。
Later Zhao general Liu Zheng led several thousand men by sea to raid southeastern counties and killed Nansha commandant Xu Ru.
86
張駿因前趙之亡，復收河南地，至於狄道，置五屯護軍，與趙分境。 六月，趙遣鴻臚孟毅拜駿征西大將軍、涼州牧，加九錫。 駿恥為之臣，不受，留毅不遣。
Zhang Jun exploited Former Zhao's collapse to recover Henan as far as Didao, established five colony-protectorates, and drew a border with Zhao. In the sixth month Zhao sent Grand Master of Ceremonies Meng Yi to invest Zhang Jun as General Who Conquers the West and Governor of Liang Province with the Nine Bestowals. Zhang Jun was ashamed to submit and refused; he kept Meng Yi and would not send him back.
87
初，丁零翟斌，世居康居，後徙中國，至是入朝於趙； 趙以斌為句町王。
Earlier, the Dingling chieftain Zhai Bin's clan had lived in Kangju before migrating to China; now he came to pay court to Zhao; Zhao enfeoffed Zhai Bin as King of Gouding.
88
趙群臣固請正尊號，秋，九月，趙王勒即皇帝位。 大赦，改元建平。 文武封進各有差。 立其妻劉氏為皇后，太子弘為皇太子。
Zhao's ministers repeatedly urged Shi Le to take the full imperial title; in autumn, the ninth month, King Shi Le ascended the throne. He proclaimed a general amnesty and adopted the era name Jianping. Civil and military officials received promotions and titles according to merit. He made Lady Liu empress and Crown Prince Hong heir apparent.
89
弘好屬文，親敬儒素。 勒謂徐光曰：「大雅愔愔，殊不似將家子。 」光曰：「漢祖以馬上取天下，孝文以玄默守之。 聖人之後，必有勝殘去殺者，天之道也。 」勒甚悅。 光因說曰：「皇太子仁孝溫恭，中山王雄暴多詐，陛下一旦不諱，臣恐社稷非太子所有也。 宜漸奪中山王權，使太子早參朝政。 」勒心然之，而未能從。
Hong loved literary composition and personally honored plain scholarly virtue. Shi Le said to Xu Guang: "Daye is gentle and quiet—nothing like a general's son. " Xu Guang replied: "The Han founder won the realm on horseback; Emperor Wen kept it through quiet restraint. After a sage always comes one who curbs cruelty and ends killing—that is Heaven's way. " Shi Le was greatly pleased. Xu Guang then urged: "The crown prince is benevolent, filial, and mild; the King of Zhongshan is fierce and treacherous. When Your Majesty passes away, I fear the realm will not remain the crown prince's. You should gradually strip the King of Zhongshan of power and let the crown prince take part in government early. " Shi Le agreed in his heart but could not bring himself to act.
90
趙荊州監軍郭敬寇襄陽。 南中郎將周撫監沔北軍事，屯襄陽。 趙主勒以驛書敕敬退屯樊城，使之偃藏旗幟，寂若無人，曰：「彼若使人觀察，則告之曰：『汝宜自愛堅守，後七八日，大騎將至，相策，不復得走矣。 』」敬使人浴馬於津，週而復始，晝夜不絕。 偵者還以告周撫，撫以為趙兵大至，懼，奔武昌。 敬入襄陽，中州流民悉降於趙； 魏該弟遐帥其部眾自石城降敬。 敬毀襄陽城，遷其民於沔北，城樊城以戍之。 趙以敬為荊州刺史。 周撫坐免官。
Later Zhao Jing Province military supervisor Guo Jing raided Xiangyang. General of the Southern Gentlemen Zhou Fu oversaw military affairs north of the Han and was stationed at Xiangyang. Zhao ruler Shi Le dispatched orders by post horse telling Guo Jing to withdraw to Fancheng, hide his banners, and act as if deserted, saying: "If they send scouts, tell them: 'Hold fast and take care—in seven or eight days a great cavalry force will arrive, and you will not be able to escape.' " " Guo Jing had men watering horses at the ford in an endless cycle, day and night. Scouts reported back to Zhou Fu, who believed a great Zhao army was coming, panicked, and fled to Wuchang. Guo Jing entered Xiangyang, and refugees from the central provinces all submitted to Zhao; Wei Gai's brother Xia led his followers from Shicheng to surrender to Guo Jing. Guo Jing razed Xiangyang, relocated its people north of the Han, and fortified Fancheng as a garrison. Zhao appointed Guo Jing governor of Jing Province. Zhou Fu was dismissed from office.
91
休屠王羌叛趙，趙河東王生擊破之，羌奔涼州。 西平公駿懼，遣孟毅還，使其長史馬詵稱臣入貢於趙。
Xiutu king Qiang rebelled against Zhao; Hedong King Shi Sheng defeated him, and Qiang fled to Liang Province. Duke of Xiping Zhang Jun, alarmed, sent Meng Yi back and had his chief clerk Ma Shen submit tribute to Zhao as a vassal.
92
更造新宮。 甲辰，徙樂成王欽為河間王，封彭城王紘子浚為高密王。
A new palace was built. On jiachen, King of Lecheng Qin was moved to Hejian, and King of Pengcheng Hong's son Jun was enfeoffed as King of Gaomi.
93
冬，十月，成大將軍壽督征南將軍費黑等攻巴東建平，拔之。 巴東太守楊謙、監軍毌丘奧退保宜都。
In winter, the tenth month, Great General Shou led General Who Conquers the South Fei Hei and others against Jianping in Badong and took it. Badong administrator Yang Qian and military supervisor Guanqiu Ao withdrew to Yidu.
94
顯宗成皇帝上之下咸和六年（ 辛卯，公元三三一年）
From the lower fascicle of the reign of Emperor Cheng (Manifest Ancestor), Xianhe year 6 ( xinmao, corresponding to 331 CE)
95
春，正月，趙劉征復寇婁縣，掠武進，郗鑒擊卻之。
In spring, the first month, Later Zhao's Liu Zheng again raided Lou County and plundered Wujin; Xi Jian drove him back.
96
三月，壬戌朔，日有食之。
In the third month, on renxu the first day of the month, there was a solar eclipse.
97
夏，趙主勒如鄴，將營新宮； 廷尉上黨續咸苦諫，勒怒，欲斬之。 中書令徐光曰：「咸言不可用，亦當容之，奈何一旦以直言斬列卿乎！ 」勒歎曰：「為人君，不得自專如是乎！ 匹夫家貲滿百匹，猶欲市宅，況富有四海乎！ 此宮終當營之，且敕停作，以成吾直臣之氣。 」因賜咸絹百匹，稻百斛。 又詔公卿以下歲舉賢良方正，仍令舉人得更相薦引，以廣求賢之路。 起明堂、辟雍、靈台於襄國城西。
In summer Zhao ruler Shi Le went to Ye to build a new palace; Commandant of Justice Xu Xian of Shangdang remonstrated fiercely; Shi Le grew angry and wanted to execute him. Director of the Secretariat Xu Guang said: "Even if you cannot use what Xu Xian says, you should still tolerate him—how can you execute a senior minister for speaking plainly! " Shi Le sighed: "Can a ruler not even decide such things for himself! A commoner with a hundred bolts of silk still wants to buy a house—how much more one who owns the realm! This palace will be built in the end; for now I order work halted to honor my blunt-speaking ministers. " He awarded Xu Xian a hundred bolts of silk and a hundred hu of rice. He also decreed that officials should annually recommend worthy and upright men, and that nominees might recommend others in turn, widening the path for seeking talent. He built the Bright Hall, Imperial Academy, and Spirit Terrace west of Xiangguo.
98
秋，七月，成大將軍壽攻陰平、武都，楊難敵降之。
In autumn, the seventh month, Great General Shou attacked Yinping and Wudu, and Yang Nan Di submitted.
99
九月，趙主勒復營鄴宮，以洛陽為南都，置行台。
In the ninth month Shi Le resumed building at Ye, made Luoyang the southern capital, and established a regional headquarters there.
100
冬，蒸祭太廟，詔歸胙於司徒導，且命無下拜； 導辭疾不敢當。 初，帝即位沖幼，每見導必拜，與導手詔則云「惶恐言」，中書作詔則曰「敬問」。 有司議：「元會日，帝應敬導不？ 」博士郭熙、杜援議，以為：「禮無拜臣之文，謂宜除敬。 」侍中馮懷議，以為：「天子臨辟雍，拜三老，況先帝師傅！ 謂宜盡敬。 」侍中荀弈議，以為：「三朝之首，宜明君臣之體，則不應敬。 若他日小會，自可盡禮。 」詔從之。 弈，組之子也。
In winter, at the winter temple sacrifice, an edict sent sacrificial meat to Minister of Works Wang Dao and exempted him from kneeling; Wang Dao pleaded illness and declined. When the emperor acceded to the throne he was very young; whenever he saw Wang Dao he bowed. Personal edicts to Dao began "I speak in trepidation"; Secretariat drafts read "Respectful inquiry." Officials debated: "On New Year's Day, should the emperor show Wang Dao formal respect? " Erudites Guo Xi and Du Yuan argued that the rites contained no precedent for an emperor bowing to a minister and that formal respect should be dropped. " Palace Attendant Feng Huai argued that when the Son of Heaven visited the Imperial Academy he bowed to the Three Elders, and Wang Dao had been the former emperor's tutor. Full respect was appropriate. " Palace Attendant Xun Yi argued that New Year's Day should clarify the ruler-minister relationship, so formal respect was inappropriate. On lesser occasions, full ceremony would still be fitting. " The edict followed Xun Yi's view. Xun Yi was the son of Xun Zu.
101
慕容廆遣使與太尉陶侃箋，勸以興兵北伐，共清中原。 僚屬宋該等共議，以「廆立功一隅，位卑任重，等差無別，不足以鎮華、夷，宜表請進廆官爵。 」參軍韓恆駁曰：「夫立功者患信義不著，不患名位不高。 桓、文有匡復之功，不先求禮命以令諸侯。 宜繕甲兵，除群凶，功成之後，九錫自至。 比於邀君以求寵，不亦榮乎！ 」廆不悅，出恆為新昌令。 於是東夷校尉封抽等疏上侃府，請封廆為燕王，行大將軍事。 侃復書曰：「夫功成進爵，古之成制也。 車騎雖未能為官摧勒，然忠義竭誠； 今騰箋上聽，可不、遲速，當在天台也。 」
Murong Hui sent envoys with a letter to Grand Commandant Tao Kan, urging him to march north and together recover the Central Plains. His aides Song Gai and others argued that Hui had won merit on the frontier while holding rank too low for his burden, without the honors needed to command both Chinese and barbarian followers, and that they should petition to advance his titles. " Staff officer Han Heng objected: "The man who wins merit should fear that his faith and righteousness are not seen, not that his rank is too low." Dukes Huan and Wen restored the realm, yet they did not first seek formal appointment before rallying the feudal lords. We should sharpen arms, destroy the rebels, and after victory the Nine Bestowals will come in their own time. Is that not more honorable than begging the throne for rewards?" " Hui took offense and posted Heng to be magistrate of Xinchang. Then Eastern Yi Commandant Feng Chou and others memorialized Kan's headquarters to enfeoff Hui as King of Yan with authority as Grand General. Kan wrote back: "Promotion after merit is the ancient rule. Though the General of Chariots and Cavalry has not yet won the court's full commission, his loyalty is beyond question; your petition is now before the throne; approval and timing rest with the Son of Heaven." "
