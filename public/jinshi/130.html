Biography 68 — Exemplary Women: the wife of A Lin; the wife of Li Baoxin; the wife of Han Qingmin; Lady Shi of Leifu; Kang Zhuzhu; the wife of Li Wen; the wife of Li Ying; the wife of Xiang Qi; A Luzhen; the wife of Sahe Nian; the wife of Xu Gu; Feng Miaozhen; Lady Pucha; Lady Wugulun; the wife of Su Lan; the wife of Mang Ge; Lady Yin; Lady Bai; Nie Xiaonu; the wife of Zhong De; Lady Li of Baofu; Zhang Fengnu
2
漢成帝時，劉向始述三代賢妃淑女，及淫泆奢僭、興亡盛衰之所由，匯分類別，號《列女傳》，因以諷諫。 范曄始載之漢史。 古者女子生十年有女師，漸長，有麻枲絲繭之事，有祭祀助奠之事； 既嫁，職在中饋而已，故以無非無儀為賢。 若乃嫠居寡處，患難顛沛，是皆婦人之不幸也。 一遇不幸，卓然能自樹立，有烈丈夫之風，是以君子異之。
Under Emperor Cheng of Han, Liu Xiang first set down the worthy consorts and virtuous ladies of the Three Dynasties, together with accounts of debauchery, extravagance, presumption, and the causes of dynastic rise and fall; he sorted these into categories under the title Biographies of Exemplary Women, using them as moral instruction. Fan Ye was the first to include such figures in the History of Han. In ancient times a girl of ten was given a female tutor; as she grew she was taught hemp and ramie work, silk and sericulture, and how to assist at sacrifices and offerings; After marriage her sphere was the inner household alone, and virtue meant simply never giving cause for blame or scandal. Widowhood, isolation, hardship, and ruin are among the greatest misfortunes that can befall a woman. Yet when misfortune strikes and a woman can still hold herself upright with the mettle of a resolute man, the cultivated reader takes special note of her.
3
阿鄰妻
The Wife of A Lin
4
阿鄰妻沙裏質者，金源郡王銀術可之妹。 天輔六年，黃龍府叛卒攻鈔旁近部族。 是時，阿鄰從軍，沙裏質糾集附近居民得男女五百人，樹營柵為保守計。 賊千余來攻，沙裏質以氈為甲，以裳為旗，男夫授甲，婦女鼓噪，沙裏質仗劍督戰，凡三日賊去。 皇統二年，論功封金源郡夫人。 大定間，以其孫藥師為謀克。
A Lin's wife Shalizhi was a younger sister of Yinsuke, Prince of Jinyuan Commandery. In the sixth year of Tianfu (1122), mutineers from Huanglong Prefecture raided neighboring tribal settlements. A Lin was away on campaign; Shalizhi rallied local residents until she had five hundred men and women, then threw up stockades to hold the position. When more than a thousand rebels attacked, she improvised armor from felt and standards from skirts, armed the men, set the women to shouting, and herself took sword in hand to direct the fight; after three days the enemy withdrew. In the second year of Huangtong (1142) her service was rewarded with the title Lady of Jinyuan Commandery. Under Emperor Shizong (Dading reign), her grandson Yaoshi was appointed a company commander (mouke).
5
李寶信妻
The Wife of Li Baoxin
6
李寶信妻王氏。 寶信為義豐縣令，張覺以平州叛，王氏陷賊中。 賊欲逼室之，王氏罵賊，賊怒遂支解之。 大定十二年，贈「貞烈縣君」。
Li Baoxin's wife was a woman of the Wang clan. Baoxin served as magistrate of Yifeng County; when Zhang Jue rose in rebellion at Pingzhou, Lady Wang was captured by the rebels. The rebels tried to take her into their quarters; she cursed them, and in fury they hacked her to pieces. In the twelfth year of Dading (1172) she was posthumously ennobled as Chaste and Steadfast County Lady.
7
韓慶民妻
The Wife of Han Qingmin
8
韓慶民妻者，不知何許人，亦不知其姓氏。 慶民事遼為宜州節度使。 天會中，攻破宜州，慶民不屈而死，以其妻配將士，其妻誓死不從，遂自殺。 世宗讀《太宗實錄》，見慶民夫婦事，歎曰：「如此節操，可謂難矣。」
Han Qingmin's wife — neither her native place nor her family name is recorded. Qingmin had served the Liao dynasty as military commissioner of Yizhou. During the Tianhui period the Jin took Yizhou; Qingmin refused to submit and was put to death, and his wife was allotted to the troops; she swore she would never yield and took her own life. When Emperor Shizong read the Veritable Records of Taizong and came upon the story of Qingmin and his wife, he sighed and said, "Devotion of that order is truly hard to find."

雷婦師氏
Lady Shi of Leifu
10
雷婦師氏，夫亡，孝養舅姑。 姑病，刲臂肉飼之，姑即愈。 舅姑既歿。 兄師逵與夫侄規其財產，乃偽立謀證致之官，欲必嫁之。 縣官不能辨曲直，師氏畏逼，乃投縣署井中死。 詔有司祭其墓，賜諡曰「節」。
Lady Shi of Leifu lost her husband and devoted herself to caring for her parents-in-law. When her mother-in-law fell ill, she cut flesh from her own arm to feed her, and the elder woman soon recovered. After both parents-in-law had died, her elder brother Shi Kui and her husband's nephew schemed for her estate; they fabricated evidence and hauled her before the magistrate, intent on forcing her to remarry. The county magistrate could not settle the dispute; cornered by pressure, Lady Shi threw herself into the county well and drowned. The court ordered official sacrifices at her tomb and granted her the posthumous epithet Chaste.
11
康住住
Kang Zhuzhu
12
康住住，鄜州人。 夫早亡，服闋，父取之歸家，許嚴沂為妻。 康氏誓死弗聽，欲還夫家不可得，乃投崖而死。 詔有司致祭其墓。
Kang Zhuzhu came from Fuzhou. Her husband died young; when her mourning ended her father brought her home and promised her to Yan Yi. Lady Kang swore she would die before obeying; unable to return to her husband's household, she leapt from a cliff to her death. The court ordered official rites at her grave.
13
李文妻
The Wife of Li Wen
14
李文妻史氏，同州白水人。 夫亡，服闋，誓死弗嫁。 父強取之歸，許邑人姚乙為妻。 史氏不聽，姚訴之官，被逮，遂自縊死。 詔有司致祭其墓。
Li Wen's wife, née Shi, was from Baishui in Tongzhou. After her husband's death and the end of mourning, she swore never to marry again. Her father seized her back and betrothed her to a local man named Yao Yi. Lady Shi refused; Yao sued before the magistrate; when she was taken into custody she hanged herself. The court ordered sacrifices at her tomb.

李英妻
The Wife of Li Ying
16
李英妻張氏。 英初為監察禦史，在中都，張居濰州。 貞祐元年冬，大元兵取濰州，入其家，張氏盡以所有財物與之。 既而，令張氏上馬，張曰：「我盡以物與汝，猶不見贖邪？ 」答曰「汝品官妻，當複為夫人。 」張曰：「我死則為李氏鬼。 頓坐不起，遂見殺。 追封隴西郡夫人，諡「莊潔」。 英仕至御史中丞，有傳。
Li Ying's wife was a woman of the Zhang clan. Ying had first served as supervising censor at the Central Capital while Lady Zhang remained at Weizhou. In the winter of the first year of Zhenyou (1213) the Mongol army captured Weizhou and entered their home; Lady Zhang surrendered everything she owned. Then they told her to mount a horse; she said, "I have given you everything I own — will you still not let me go? " They replied, "You are a ranking official's wife — you will be a lady again. " She said, "If I die, I die a ghost of the Li household. She dropped to the ground and would not rise, and they killed her. She was posthumously created Lady of Longxi Commandery with the epithet Solemn and Pure. Ying rose to vice censor-in-chief and has his own biography.
17
相琪妻
The Wife of Xiang Qi
18
相琪妻欒氏，有姿色。 琪為萊州掖縣司吏。 貞祐三年八月，紅祆賊陷掖縣，琪與欒氏及子俱為所得。 賊見欒悅之，殺琪及其子而誘欒。 欒奮起以頭觸賊而僕，罵曰：「我豈為犬彘所汙者哉。 」賊怒，殺之。 追封西河縣君，諡「莊潔」。
Xiang Qi's wife, née Luan, was a woman of striking beauty. Qi served as a clerk in Ye County, Laizhou. In the eighth month of the third year of Zhenyou (1215) Red Turban rebels overran Ye County; Qi, Lady Luan, and their son were all captured. The rebels took a fancy to Luan, murdered Qi and their son, and tried to seduce her. Luan sprang up, butted the rebel to the ground, and cried, "Would I let myself be fouled by dogs and swine! " Enraged, they slew her. She was posthumously created County Lady of Xihe with the epithet Solemn and Pure.

撒合輦妻
The Wife of Sahe Nian
21
獨吉氏，平章政事千家奴之女，護衛銀術可妹也。 自幼動有禮法，及適內族撒合輦。 閨門肅如。 撒合輦為中京留守，大兵圍之，撒合輦疽發背不能軍，獨吉氏度城必破，謂撒合輦曰：「公本無功能，徒以宗室故嘗在禁近，以至提點近侍局，同判睦親府，今又為留守外路第一等官，受國家恩最厚。 今大兵臨城，公不幸病，不能戰禦，設若城破，公當率精銳奪門而出，攜一子走京師。 不能則獨赴京師，又不能，戰而死猶可報國，幸無以我為慮。 」撒合輦出巡城，獨吉氏乃取平日衣服妝具玩好布之臥榻，資貨悉散之家人，豔妝盛服過於平日，且戒女使曰：「我死則扶置榻上，以衾覆面，四圍舉火焚之。 無使兵見吾面。 」言訖，閉門自經而死。 家人如言，臥屍榻上，以衾覆之。 撒合輦從外室，家人告以夫人之死，撒合輦拊榻曰：「夫人不辱我，我肯辱朝廷乎！ 」因命焚之。 年三十有六。 少頃，城破，撒合輦率死士欲奪門出，不果，投壕水死，有傳。
Lady Dugu was a daughter of Grand Councilor Qianjianu and younger sister of the guardsman Yinsuke. From girlhood her conduct was governed by ritual; she was given in marriage within the imperial clan to Sahe Nian. Her household was run with the gravity of a court audience hall. Sahe Nian was military governor of the Central Capital when a great army laid siege to the city; a carbuncle on his back left him unable to take the field. Lady Dugu saw that the city must fall and told him, "You never had real talent — you rose only because you are of the imperial clan, first near the inner palace, then superintendent of the Inner Service Bureau and associate director of the Bureau of Imperial Kin, and now military governor, the highest post on an outer circuit, enjoying the deepest favor of the state. Now the enemy is at the walls and you are too ill to defend them. If the city falls, lead your best troops through the gate, take one son, and flee to the capital. If you cannot manage that, go to the capital alone; if you cannot do even that, die fighting and you will still have repaid the dynasty — but do not trouble yourself over me. " Sahe Nian went out to inspect the defenses. Lady Dugu laid out her everyday clothes, cosmetics, and favorite things on the bed, gave away all the household valuables, dressed more richly than on any ordinary day, and instructed her maids: "When I am dead, lay me on the bed, cover my face with a quilt, and burn everything around me. Let no soldier see my face. " With that she shut the door and hanged herself. The household obeyed: they laid her body on the bed and covered it. Sahe Nian returned from outside; when they told him his wife was dead he beat the bed and cried, "My wife did not shame me — shall I shame the throne! " He ordered the pyre lit. She was thirty-six years old. Soon the city fell; Sahe Nian led a band of men willing to die trying to break out through the gate, failed, and drowned himself in the moat; he too has a biography.

許古妻
The Wife of Xu Gu
23
許古妻劉氏，定海軍節度使仲洙之女也。 貞祐初，古挈家僑居蒲城，從留劉氏母子于蒲，仕於朝。 既而，兵圍蒲，劉謂二女曰：「汝父在朝，而兵勢如此，事不可保。 若城破被驅，一為所汙奈何？ 不若俱死以自全。 」已而，攻城益急，於是劉氏與二女相繼自盡。 有司以聞於朝，四年五月，追封劉氏為郡君，諡曰「貞潔」，其長女諡曰「定薑」，次「肅薑」，以其事付史館。
Xu Gu's wife, née Liu, was a daughter of Liu Zhongzhu, military commissioner of the Dinghai Army. Early in the Zhenyou era Gu moved his family to Pucheng, leaving Lady Liu and their children there while he took office at court. When troops soon besieged Pucheng, Lady Liu told her two daughters, "Your father is at court and the armies press so hard that nothing is secure. If the city falls and we are dragged away, how could we bear to be violated? Better that we die together and keep ourselves whole. The assault intensified; Lady Liu and her two daughters killed themselves one after another. Officials reported the matter to court; in the fifth month of the fourth year Lady Liu was posthumously created a commandery lady with the epithet Chaste and Pure, her elder daughter Steadfast Jiang and the younger Solemn Jiang, and the case was entered in the historiographical records.

馮妙真
Feng Miaozhen
25
馮妙真，刑部尚書延登之女也。 生十有八年，適進士張綎。 興定五年，綎為洛川主簿。 大元兵破葭州、綏德，遂入鄜延。 鄜人震恐具守備，守臣以西路輸芻粟不時至，檄慥詣平涼督之。 時延登為平涼行省員外郎，綎欲偕妙真以往，妙真辭曰：「舅姑老矣。 雖有叔姒，妾能安乎。 子行，妾留奉養。 」十一月，洛川破，妙真從舅姑匿窟室，兵索得之。 妙真泣與舅姑訣曰：「婦生不辰，不得終執箕帚，義不從辱。 」即攜三子赴井死。 縣人從而死者數十人。 明年春，綎發井得屍，殯於縣之東郭外。 死時年二十四。
Feng Miaozhen was a daughter of Feng Yandeng, Minister of Justice. At eighteen she married Zhang Chao, a holder of the jinshi degree. In the fifth year of Xingding (1221) Chao became chief clerk of Luochuan. The Mongol army took Jiazhou and Suide and swept into the Yan'an region. The people of Yan'an were terrified and threw up defenses; because fodder for the western line failed to arrive on schedule, the defenders ordered Chao to Pingliang to hurry the supplies. Yandeng was then an outside member of the Pingliang Branch Secretariat; Chao wanted Miaozhen to accompany him, but she refused, saying, "My parents-in-law are elderly. Though my husband's uncle's wife is here, how could I be easy in mind? You go; I will stay and care for them. In the eleventh month Luochuan fell; Miaozhen hid with her in-laws in a cellar until soldiers found them. Weeping, she bade her in-laws farewell: "I was born under an ill star and could not serve you to the end; I will not live to be shamed. She took her three sons and threw herself into a well. Several dozen townspeople followed her into death. The next spring Chao opened the well, recovered the bodies, and buried them outside the eastern gate of the county seat. She was twenty-four when she died.

蒲察氏
Lady Pucha
27
蒲察氏，字明秀，鄜州帥訥申之女，完顏長樂之妻也。 哀宗遷歸德，以長樂為總領，將兵扈從。 將行，屬蒲察氏曰：「無他言，夫人慎毋辱此身。 」明秀曰：「君第致身事上，無以妾為念。 妾必不辱。 」長樂一子在幼，出妻柴氏所生也，明秀撫育如己出。 崔立之變，驅從官妻子于省中，人自閱之。 蒲察氏聞，以幼子付婢僕，且與之金幣，親具衣棺祭物，與家人訣曰：「崔立不道，強人妻女，兵在城下，吾何所逃，惟一死不負吾夫耳。 汝等惟善養幼子。 」遂自縊而死，欣然若不以死為難者。 時年二十七。
Lady Pucha, courtesy name Mingxiu, was a daughter of Neshen, military commander of Fuzhou, and the wife of Wanyan Changle. When Emperor Aizong withdrew to Guide, Changle was appointed chief commander to lead the escort force. Before he marched he told Lady Pucha, "Say nothing else — only see that you do not shame yourself. " Mingxiu replied, "Serve the throne with all your strength; do not give me a thought. I shall not bring disgrace upon you. Changle had one young son by his former wife of the Chai clan; Mingxiu raised him as her own. During Cui Li's coup the wives and children of court officials were herded into the provincial compound to be picked over one by one. When she heard, she handed her little son to a maid with gold and coins, laid out her burial clothes herself, and bade her family farewell: "Cui Li is lawless, seizing men's wives and daughters; with the enemy at the walls I have nowhere to run — only death can keep me from shaming my husband. See only that you raise the boy well. Then she hanged herself, serene as though death were no hardship at all. She was twenty-seven.

烏古論氏
Lady Wugulun
29
烏古論氏，伯祥之妹，臨洮總管陀滿胡土門之妻也。 伯祥朝貴中聲譽藉甚，胡土門死王事。 崔立之變，衣冠家婦女多為所汙，烏古論氏謂家人曰：「吾夫不辱朝廷，我敢辱吾兄及吾夫乎。 」即自縊。 一婢從死。
Lady Wugulun was a younger sister of Boxiang and the wife of Tuoman Hutumen, military commander of Lintao. Boxiang stood high among the court nobility; Hutumen died in the king's service. When Cui Li's coup came, many gentry women were violated; Lady Wugulun told her household, "My husband did not shame the throne — how could I shame my brother and my husband! " She hanged herself at once. One maid died with her.

素蘭妻
The Wife of Su Lan
31
參政完顏素蘭妻，亡其姓氏。 當崔立之變，謂所親曰：「吾夫有天下重名，吾豈肯隨眾陷身以辱吾夫乎。 今日一死固當，但不可無名而死，亦不可離吾家而死。 」即自縊於室。
The wife of Grand Councilor Wanyan Su Lan — her family name is not recorded. During Cui Li's upheaval she told her intimates, "My husband's name weighs on the empire — how could I sink with the mob and shame him! Death today is only right — but one must not die nameless, nor die outside one's own roof. She hanged herself in her own room.

忙哥妻
The Wife of Mang Ge
33
溫特罕氏，夫完顏忙哥，五朵山宣差提控回裏不之子也，系出蕭王。 忙哥叔父益都，節度秦州，為大元兵所攻，適病不能軍，忙哥為提控，獨當一面。 兵退而益都死，忙哥以城守功世襲謀克，收充奉禦。 及崔立之變，忙哥義不受辱，與其妻訣。 妻曰：「君能為國家死，我不能為君死乎。 」一婢曰：「主死，婢將安歸。 」是日，夫婦以一繩同縊，婢從之。
Of the Wentehan clan — wife of Wanyan Mang Ge, son of the frontier commissioner Tikuilibu of Wuduoshan and a descendant of Prince Xiao. Mang Ge's uncle Yidu held Qinzhou when the Mongols attacked; Yidu fell ill and could not take the field, and Mang Ge as frontier commissioner held one sector alone. After the enemy withdrew Yidu died; Mang Ge inherited a company commandership for holding the city and entered service as an inner attendant. When Cui Li's coup broke out, Mang Ge, holding that he could not live in dishonor, bade his wife farewell. His wife said, "You can die for the dynasty — cannot I die for you? A maid said, "If my master dies, where shall I go? That day husband and wife shared one rope; the maid followed.
34
尹氏，完顏豬兒之妻也。 豬兒系出蕭王，天興二年正月從哀宗為南面元帥，戰死黃陵岡。 其妻金源郡夫人聞豬兒死，聚家資焚之，遂自縊，年三十一。 豬兒贈官，弟長住即日詔補護衛。
Lady Yin was the wife of Wanyan Zhuer, a descendant of Prince Xiao; in the first month of the second year of Tianxing (1233) he followed Emperor Aizong as southern-route commander and was killed at Huangling Ridge. When Lady of Jinyuan Commandery heard of his death she burned the family wealth and hanged herself at thirty-one. Zhuer received posthumous honors; his younger brother Changzhu was the same day enrolled in the guard by imperial order.

聶孝女
Nie Xiaonu
36
聶孝女，字舜英，尚書左右司員外郎天驥之長女也。 年二十三，適進士張伯豪。 伯豪卒，歸父母家。 及哀宗遷歸德，天驥留汴。 崔立劫殺宰相，天驥被創甚，日夜悲泣，恨不即死。 舜英謁醫救療百方，至刲其股雜他肉以進，而天驥竟死。 時京城圍久食盡，閭巷間有嫁妻易一飽者，重以崔立之變，剽奪暴淩，無複人理。 舜英頗讀書知義理，自以年尚少艾，夫既亡，父又死非命，比為兵所汙，何若從吾父于地下乎。 葬其父之明日，絕脰而死。 一時士女賢之，有為泣下者。 其家以舜英合葬張伯豪之墓。
Nie Xiaonu, courtesy name Shunying, was the eldest daughter of Nie Tianji, an outside member of the Left and Right Secretariat bureaus. At twenty-three she married Zhang Bohao, a jinshi degree holder. When Bohao died she returned to her father's house. When Emperor Aizong withdrew to Guide, Tianji stayed behind at Bianliang. When Cui Li seized and murdered the grand councilors, Tianji was badly wounded; day and night he wept, bitter that he could not die at once. Shunying summoned doctors and tried every cure; she even cut flesh from her thigh, mixed it with other meat, and fed it to him — yet Tianji died all the same. The capital had been besieged so long that food was gone; in the alleys men sold their wives for a single meal; on top of Cui Li's coup came looting and outrage until human decency vanished. Shunying had read and understood moral principle; she reasoned that she was still young and comely, her husband gone, her father slain unnaturally — if soldiers violated her, was it not better to join her father underground? The day after her father's burial she broke her own neck and died. Contemporaries praised her; some wept at her death. Her family buried her beside Zhang Bohao's grave.

仲德妻
The Wife of Zhong De
38
完顏仲德妻，不知其族氏。 崔立之變，妻自毀其容服，攜妾及二子紿以采蔬，自汴走蔡。 蔡被圍，丁男皆乘城拒守，謂仲德曰：「事勢若此，丈夫能為國出力，婦人獨不能耶！ 」率諸命婦自作一軍，親運矢石於城下，城中婦女爭出繼之。 城破自盡。
Wanyan Zhong De's wife — her clan is not recorded. During Cui Li's coup she disfigured her face and ruined her fine clothes, took a concubine and two sons, pretended to go out gathering greens, and fled from Bianliang to Cai Prefecture. When Cai was besieged and every able man manned the walls, she told Zhong De, "Matters stand so — a man can give his strength for the state; cannot a woman do the same! " She rallied the noble wives into a corps of their own and herself hauled arrows and stones at the foot of the wall; women throughout the city rushed out to join her. When the city fell she killed herself.

寶符李氏
Lady Li of Baofu
40
哀宗寶符李氏，國亡從後妃北遷，至宣德州，居摩訶院，日夕寢處佛殿中，作幡旆。 會當赴龍庭，將發，即於佛像前自縊死，且自書門紙曰：「寶符禦侍此處身故。 」後人至其處，見其遺跡，憐而哀之。
Lady Li of Baofu, a consort of Emperor Aizong, followed the imperial women north when the dynasty fell; at Xuande Prefecture she lived in the Mahā monastery, sleeping in the Buddha hall by day and night and sewing banners. When they were to set out for the northern court she hanged herself before the Buddha image and wrote on the door, "Baofu the imperial attendant died here. Those who came afterward, finding what she left, pitied and mourned her.
41
張鳳奴
Zhang Fengnu
42
天興元年，北兵攻城，矢石之際忽見一女子呼於城下曰：「我倡女張鳳奴也，許州破被俘至此。 彼軍不日去矣，諸君努力為國堅守，無為所欺也。 」言竟，投濠而死。 朝廷遣使馳祭於西門。
In the first year of Tianxing (1232) the northern army besieged the city; amid flying arrows and stones a woman suddenly cried from below the wall, "I am the courtesan Zhang Fengnu; when Xuzhou fell I was taken captive and brought here. Their army will leave before long — hold fast for the dynasty and do not be fooled! She finished speaking and threw herself into the moat. The court sent an envoy at once to sacrifice at the west gate.
43
正大、天興之際，婦人節義可知者特數人耳。 鳳奴之事別史錄之。 蓋亦有所激雲。
In the Zhengda and Tianxing eras only a handful of women whose fidelity can be known appear in the record. Fengnu's story is entered elsewhere in the histories. Perhaps each was moved by something beyond herself — so one may say.
