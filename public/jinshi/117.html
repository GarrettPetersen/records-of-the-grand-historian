1
徒單益都
Tudan Yidu
2
徒單益都，不詳其履歷，嘗累官為延安總管。 正大九年正月，行省事於徐州。 時慶山奴撤東方之備入援，未至睢州，徐、邳義勝軍總領侯進、杜政、張興率本軍降大兵於永州。 辛丑，大兵守徐張盆渡。 益都到官才三日，懼兵少不能守，即令移剌長壽率甲士千人迎大兵。 長壽軍無紀律，大兵掩之，一軍皆覆，徐危甚。 益都籍州人及運糧埽兵得萬人。 乙巳，大兵傅城，燒南關而去。 侯進既降北，即以為京東行省，進遂請千人來襲。
Tudan Yidu's background is unclear, but he had risen through repeated appointments and had served as Yan'an commander-in-chief. In the first month of Zhengda 9 (1232), he took up provincial duties at Xuzhou. While Qing Shannu was pulling eastern garrisons westward to reinforce the capital, his column had not yet reached Suizhou when Hou Jin, Du Zheng, and Zhang Xing of the Xu–Pi Yisheng Army surrendered their units to the Mongols at Yongzhou. On the xinchou day, Mongol forces occupied Zhangpen Ford near Xuzhou. Only three days after taking office, Yidu, fearing he lacked the strength to defend the city, sent Yelü Changshou with a thousand armored men to go out and receive the Mongols. Changshou's men were undisciplined; the Mongols struck them by surprise and wiped out the entire detachment, leaving Xuzhou in dire straits. Yidu drafted local men and grain-barge labor troops until he had ten thousand soldiers. On yisi the Mongols pressed the siege, torched the southern suburb, and then drew off. After Hou Jin defected to the Mongols, they made him Eastern Hebei commissioner, and he then asked for a thousand men to raid Xuzhou.
3
二月庚申，未明，大兵坎南城而上，守者皆散走，城中大呼曰：「大兵入南門矣！ 」益都聞之不及甲，率州署夜直兵三百，由黃樓而南，力戰禦敵。 亂定，遷賞有差。 由是軍勢稍振，複奪張盆渡，取蕭縣，破白塔，戰于土山，救被俘老幼五千還徐。 既而，侯進亡命駐靈璧，杜政、張興亦慮為北所害，窮窘自歸。 益都撫而納之，興留徐，杜政還邳州。
On gengshen in the second month, before daybreak, Mongol troops broke in through the southern wall; the garrison broke and ran as the city cried out that the enemy had entered the south gate. Yidu, hearing the alarm, had no time to don armor; he led three hundred night guards from the yamen south through Huanglou and fought the enemy head-on. Once order was restored, men were promoted and rewarded according to their deeds. The army's spirit rallied; they recovered Zhangpen Ford, took Xiao County, stormed White Tower, fought at Tushan, and brought five thousand captured civilians back to Xuzhou. Soon Hou Jin was hiding at Lingbi, while Du Zheng and Zhang Xing, fearing the Mongols would kill them, came back in desperation. Yidu received and reassured them; Zhang Xing stayed at Xuzhou while Du Zheng went back to Pizhou.
4
益都資稟仁厚，持大體，二子兩侄為軍將，頗侵漁軍民。 青州人王祐為埽兵總領。 將兵千七百人，益都常倚之，雖有過亦不責。 以故祐亦橫恣，與河間張祚、下邑令李閏、義勝都統封仙、遙授永州刺史成進忠輩，乘軍政廢弛，城中空虛。 以六月丁巳夜燒草場作亂。 時張興臥病，祐恐事不成，起興與同行。 益都疑左右皆叛，挈妻子縋城而出，就從宜眾僧奴及東面總領劉安國軍。 張興推祐為都元帥，複懼祐圖己，遂誅祐，並張祚殺之。 因大掠城中。 壬戌，國用安以行山東路尚書省事率兵至徐，張興率甲士迎之。 用安輕騎而入，執興與其党十餘人，斬之於市，遂以封仙為元帥，兼節度使，主徐州。
Yidu was by nature humane and broad-minded, yet his sons and nephews in command posts plundered soldiers and civilians. Wang You of Qingzhou served as commander of the transport-labor troops. With seventeen hundred men under him, Yidu leaned on him heavily and overlooked his misconduct. Emboldened by Yidu's indulgence, You grew tyrannical; together with Zhang Zuo, Li Run, Feng Xian, Cheng Jinzhong, and others he exploited slack discipline and the city's weakness. On the night of dingsi in the sixth month they set the fodder depot ablaze and mutinied. Zhang Xing was bedridden; fearing failure, You forced him to join the rising. Believing his entire staff had turned, Yidu lowered his family over the wall by rope and fled to Zhongsengnu and Liu Anguo's eastern command. Zhang Xing proclaimed You supreme commander, then, fearing betrayal, killed You and Zhang Zuo alike. The mutineers then looted the city wholesale. On renxu Guo Yong'an arrived at Xuzhou as acting Shandong minister; Zhang Xing went out with armored troops to receive him. Yong'an rode in lightly, arrested Xing and a dozen conspirators, and executed them publicly; he installed Feng Xian as commander and commissioner of Xuzhou.
5
益都窘無所歸，乃奔宿州，節度使紇石烈阿虎以益都為人所逐不納，乃與諸將駐于城南。 時宿之鎮防軍有逃還者，阿虎以為叛歸亦不納。 城中鎮防千戶高臘哥，結小吏郭仲安，謀就徐州將士內外相應以取宿，因歸楊妙真。 甲戌夜半，開門納徐州總領王德全及妻弟高元哥軍。 劉安國尋亦入城，縛阿虎父子殺之。 州中請益都主帥府事，益都不從，曰：「吾國家舊人，為將帥亦久，以資性疏迂，不能周防，遂失重鎮。 今大事已去，方逃罪不暇，豈有改易髻發、奪人城池以降外方乎！ 」即日，率官吏而行，至穀熟東，遇大兵，不屈而死。
Homeless, Yidu fled to Suzhou; Commissioner Heshenlie Ahu would not take him in, so he encamped south of the walls with his officers. When Suzhou garrison men who had fled began drifting back, Ahu treated them as traitors and shut them out as well. Inside the city, garrison commander Gao Lage and clerk Guo Zhong'an plotted with Xuzhou commanders to open the gates and hand Suzhou to Yang Miaozhen. At midnight on jiaxu they opened the gates to Wang Dequan and Gao Yuange's troops. Liu Anguo then entered, seized Ahu and his son, and put them to death. The city asked Yidu to take command; he refused, saying, "I am an old Jin servant and long a general; my dull, careless nature cost us a great fortress. The dynasty is finished and I am fleeing for my life — I will not shave my head and seize cities to submit to outsiders!" That day he led his staff away; east of Gushu he met Mongol troops and died rather than submit.
6
徐州既歸海州，邳帥兀林答某亦讓印于杜政，遂送款于用安。 已而宿州王德全、劉安國亦送款海州。 惟益都不改髻發，以至於死雲。
Xuzhou went over to Haizhou; the Pizhou commander surrendered his seal to Du Zheng, who then submitted to Yong'an. Soon Wang Dequan and Liu Anguo of Suzhou did the same. Only Yidu kept his Jurchen queue to the end.
7
粘哥荊山
Nian Ge Jingshan
8
粘哥荊山，不知其所始，正大中，累官亳州節度使。 九年正月己丑，游騎自鄧至亳，鈔鹿邑，營于衛真西北五十里。 鹿邑令高昂霄知太康已降，即夜趨亳，道出衛真，呼縣令楚珩約同行。 珩知勢不支，即明諭縣人以避遷之意，遂同走亳。 丁未，二邑皆降。 是日，軍至亳州城下。 州止有單州兵四百人，號「鎮安軍」，提控楊春、邢某、都統戴興屯已六年。 荊山悉籍城中丁壯為軍，修守具，而大兵亦不暇攻。 四月，擁降民而北，城門閉，不之知也。
Nian Ge Jingshan's origins are unrecorded; under Emperor Aizong's predecessor he rose to Bozhou military commissioner. On jichou in the first month of year nine, Mongol scouts from Deng reached Bozhou, raided Luyi, and camped northwest of Weizhen. Luyi's magistrate Gao Angxiao, hearing Taikang had fallen, fled by night toward Bozhou and summoned Weizhen's Chu Heng to accompany him. Chu Heng knew the position was hopeless; he told the county openly they must evacuate, then fled with Gao to Bozhou. On dingwei both counties surrendered. That same day the Mongol army appeared before Bozhou. Bozhou held only four hundred Danzhou troops, the "Pacifying Army," under Yang Chun, Xing, and Dai Xing, who had been posted there six years. Jingshan conscripted every able man, repaired defenses, and the Mongols likewise did not press a full assault. In the fourth month the Mongols herded surrendered civilians north; the gates stayed shut and the garrison knew nothing.
9
五月，縱遷民收麥，老幼得出，丁壯悉留之。 民往往不肯留而遁，數日，城為之空。 荊山遣將領各詣所屬招之，並將領亦不返。 「鎮安」者皆紅祆餘黨，力盡來歸，變詐反復，朝廷終以盜賊待之。 荊山以遷民為軍，蓋防之也。 及召外兵不至，乃請於歸德，得甲騎百餘，兩總領統之。 既至，「鎮安」疑其謀己，乃乘將士新到不設備，至夜，掩殺殆盡。 荊山出走衛真，楚珩與之馬而去，州中豪貴悉被剽略。
In the fifth month evacuees were allowed to harvest wheat — elders and children could leave, but every able-bodied man was held. People refused detention and slipped away until within days the city stood empty. Jingshan sent officers to recall the men, but those officers never came back either. The Pacifying Army were Red Turban holdouts — spent, treacherous, and always regarded by the court as bandits. Jingshan drafted the evacuees into service chiefly to keep them under control. With outside reinforcements failing, he begged Guide for a hundred-odd cavalry under two commanders. The Pacifying Army feared a plot; that night, while the newcomers were off guard, they ambushed and slaughtered almost the entire detachment. Jingshan fled to Weizhen; Chu Heng gave him a horse and escaped; Bozhou's elite were looted clean.
10
劉堅者，初為大兵守城父，亳州複，擒之，囚之於獄。 楊春謀欲北降，乃出之，使為宣差。 乙巳，大兵石總管入州，改州為順天府，春為總管，戴興為同知，劉順治中，留党項軍千人戍之。 屬縣皆下，惟城父令李用宜不降，其妻子在亳，春以為質，竟不屈而死。 春既據州，與劉堅坐樓上，召副提控邢某。 邢剛直循理，將士嚴憚之，時臥病，聞春亂，流涕不自禁。 春遣人舁致之，邢指春大罵，春慚恧無言。 春欲殺荊山家，邢力勸止之，且令給道路費送之出城，邢尋病卒。 二年夏四月，北省忒木泬攻歸德，春以戴興提精卒以往，獨與疲弱者守城。 州人王賓遂反正，春渡河北遁。 既而崔七斤為亂，殺王賓。 朝廷不得已，以七斤為節度使，就其兵仗入蔡。 八月，劉順攻亳州，破之，七斤為城父令所殺。 未幾，單州軍以州人殺其家屬，召大兵來攻，蚧能拔，殺屬縣民而去。 既渡河，知亳人不疑，複來攻，州竟為春所破。 是年六月，宋人來攻，春出降，劉堅北走。
Liu Jian had once held Chengfù for the Mongols; when Bozhou was retaken, he was captured and jailed. Yang Chun, planning to defect north, released him and appointed him envoy. On yisi Mongol Commissioner Shi took the city, renamed it Shuntian Prefecture, installed Yang Chun and Dai Xing, and left a thousand Tangut guards. Every subordinate county submitted except Chengfù's Li Yongyi; though Chun held his family hostage in Bozhou, he refused to yield and was killed. Once Yang Chun held Bozhou, he and Liu Jian sat in a tower and sent for Vice Commander Xing. Xing was rigidly upright and feared by the troops; bedridden, he wept uncontrollably when he heard of Chun's treason. Chun had him carried in; Xing cursed him to his face while Chun stood mute with shame. Chun meant to massacre Jingshan's household; Xing stopped him and paid their passage out of the city; Xing soon died. In the fourth month of year two, Temüge of the northern command attacked Guide; Chun sent Dai Xing with elites while he stayed with the weak. Wang Bin of Bozhou restored Jin rule; Yang Chun fled north across the Yellow River. Soon Cui Qijin mutinied and killed Wang Bin. The court, helpless, made Qijin commissioner and marched his troops to Cai. In the eighth month Liu Shun retook Bozhou; Qijin was killed by Chengfù's magistrate. Soon Danzhou troops, whose families had been killed locally, called in the Mongols; after a long siege they withdrew, massacring county populations. Crossing back, they found Bozhou unwary, attacked again, and Yang Chun finally took the city. That sixth month the Song attacked; Chun surrendered and Liu Jian fled north.

附:劉均
Appendix: Liu Jun
12
劉均者，林慮人，時為亳州觀察判官。 春既逐荊山，納款大兵，脅均同降。 均佯應之，歸其家取朝服服之，顧謂妻子曰：「我起身刀筆，仰荷上知，始列朝著，又佐大籓，死亦足矣。 今頭顱已如此，假使有十年壽，何以見先帝於地下乎。 」即仰藥而死。
Liu Jun of Linyu was then Bozhou's administrative aide. After driving out Jingshan and submitting to the Mongols, Yang Chun forced Jun to join him. Jun feigned consent, donned court robes at home, and told his wife, "I began as a clerk, was raised to court rank and a great commission — I have lived enough. My head is already forfeit; even ten more years could not let me face our late emperor in the grave." He drank poison and died.
13
王賓，字德卿，亳州人。 貞祐二年進士。 外若曠達，而深有謀畫。 初調蘭陵主簿，辟虹縣令，尋入為尚書省令史，坐事罷歸鄉里。 天興元年正月，亳州軍變，節度使粘哥荊山出走，楊春以州出降。 既而，自以羸兵守之。 賓與前譙縣尉王進、魏節亨、呂鈞約城中軍民複其州，楊春遂遁，遣節亨詣歸德以聞。 哀宗嘉之，授進節度使，賓同知節度使，節亨節度副使，鈞觀察判官。 楊春複以兵來攻，月餘不能拔，即渡河而北。
Wang Bin, courtesy name Deqing, was a native of Bozhou. He passed the jinshi examination in Zhenyou 2 (1214). He seemed easygoing in manner but was deeply calculating. He served as Lanling registrar, then Hong county magistrate, then a Ministry clerk until dismissed and sent home. In Tianxing 1's first month the Bozhou garrison mutinied; Jingshan fled and Yang Chun surrendered the city. He then held the city with only a weak remnant force. Bin joined Wang Jin, Wei Jieheng, and Lü Jun to rally the city, drive off Yang Chun, and send Jieheng to Guide with the news. Emperor Aizong rewarded them: Jin as commissioner, Bin as vice commissioner, Jieheng as deputy, Jun as administrative aide. Yang Chun attacked again for over a month, failed, and retreated north across the river.
14
六月，哀宗遷蔡，賓奉迎於州北之高安。 上與語，大悅，恨用之晚，擢為行部尚書、世襲謀克。 上初至亳，賓等適征民丁負鐵甲入蔡，及會計忠孝軍家屬口糧，故留參知政事張天綱董之，就遷有功將士。 時亳之糧儲不廣，賓等常吝惜，軍士以此歸怨。 及運甲之役，複不欲行。 會天綱與賓等於一樓上銓次立功等第，鎮防軍崔複哥、王六十之徒擐甲嘩噪登樓，天綱問曰：「即欲見殺，容我望闕拜辭。 」賊曰：「無預相公。 」即拽賓及呂鈞往市中。 鈞且行且跪，涕淚俱下。 賓岸然不懼，大叫曰：「不過殺我。 但殺，但殺！ 」乃並害之。 節度副使魏節亨、節度判官孫良、觀察副使孫九住皆被害。 又數日，殺節度使王進。 進嘗應荊山之募，由間道入汴京納奏，賞以物不受，又散家所有濟貧民，以死自勵。 至汴，以勞遷本州節度判官。 賜以白金，亦不受，一時甚稱之。
In the sixth month, as the emperor moved to Cai, Bin welcomed him at Gao'an north of Bozhou. The emperor delighted in him, lamented his late discovery, and made him Travelling Minister and hereditary mouke. When the court reached Bozhou, Bin was conscripting porters for Cai and rationing Zhongxiao dependents; Zhang Tiangang was left to manage promotions. Bozhou's stores were thin; Bin's frugality bred deep resentment among the troops. When orders came to haul armor to Cai, the men refused to march. As Tiangang ranked merit on a tower, Cui Fuge and Wang Liushi stormed up in armor; Tiangang asked only to bow toward the palace before dying. The mutineers said, "Not you, minister." They dragged Bin and Lü Jun into the street. Lü Jun crawled along, weeping as he went. Bin stood erect and cried, "Kill me if you will." Kill me — kill me!" They murdered them both. Deputy Commissioner Wei Jieheng, aide Sun Liang, and vice aide Sun Jiuzhu were slain as well. A few days later the mutineers killed Commissioner Wang Jin. Jin had entered Bianjing by a secret route with Jingshan's summons, refused imperial gifts, gave his wealth to the poor, and held himself ready to die. At the capital he was promoted to Bozhou administrative aide for his service. Even offered silver he would not take it, and for a time won wide praise.

附:王進等
Appendix: Wang Jin and others
16
有李喜住者，本宿州眾僧奴下宣差。 天興二年四月，進糧入歸德，將還，聞亳州王進反正，制旨以喜住為振武都尉，將兵三千應援。 是時，太赤圍亳步騎十萬，喜住以眾寡不敵，獨與三人間道入城，王進方議遷左軍林，喜住不可，進即以兵付喜住。 大兵攻八日不能下。 五月壬子，兵退。 己未，官奴與阿裏合提忠孝軍百人至亳，與諸將議遷可否。 以為不可，當留輜重于蔡，選軍扈從入聖朵就武仙軍，遂入關中。 關中地利可恃，又有郭蝦蟆等軍在西可恃。
One Li Xizhu had served as an envoy under Suzhou's Zhongsengnu. In Tianxing 2's fourth month, while hauling grain to Guide, he learned Jin had restored Bozhou; the court named him Pacifying Martial Commandant with three thousand men. Taichi had a hundred thousand men around Bozhou; Xizhu slipped in with three companions while Jin was debating evacuation — Xizhu refused, and Jin gave him the army. The Mongols assaulted the city eight days without success. On renzi in the fifth month the besiegers withdrew. On jiwei Guannu and Aligeti arrived with a hundred Zhongxiao troops to debate moving the court. They argued the court should leave stores at Cai, march into Shengdou to join Wuxian, and withdraw into Guanzhong. Guanzhong offered defensible ground and Guo Xiamo's forces in the west.
17
五月甲子，召官奴還歸德，不赴，再召，留其軍半於亳乃赴。 六月壬辰，車駕舟行至亳，王進奏：「臣本軍伍，不知治體，如李喜住扈從入蔡，則毫不守矣。 乞留治此州。 」詔以喜住為集慶軍節度使，便宜從事，進領帥職。 七月，進死。 喜住先往城父督糧餫，聞亂遂不敢入亳，後投宋。
Summoned twice to Guide, Guannu kept half his force at Bozhou before obeying. In the sixth month the emperor reached Bozhou by boat; Jin pleaded, "I am a soldier, not an administrator — if Xizhu follows Your Majesty to Cai, Bozhou will be left empty." Let me stay and hold this prefecture." The court made Xizhu Jiqing commissioner with full discretion while Jin retained supreme command. In the seventh month Jin died. Xizhu, away at Chengfù procuring grain, dared not return after the mutiny and eventually defected to Song.
18
論曰：金季之亂，軍士欲代其偏裨，偏裨欲代其主將，即群起而僨之。 無複忌憚。 益都、荊山皆忠亮之士，賓、進才略尤足取焉，而並不免於難，惜哉！
The commentary notes that in Jin's final chaos soldiers overthrew platoon leaders and platoon leaders overthrew generals without restraint. No one hesitated any longer. Yidu and Jingshan were loyal; Bin and Jin were gifted — yet all perished, a bitter loss.

國用安
Guo Yong'an
20
國用安，先名安用，本名咬兒，淄州人。 紅祆賊楊安兒、李全餘黨也。 嘗歸順大元，為都元帥、行山東路尚書省事。 天興元年六月，徐州埽兵總領王祐、義勝軍都統封仙、總領張興等夜燒草場作亂，逐元帥徒單益都。 安用率兵入徐，執張興與其党十餘人斬之，以封仙為元帥兼節度使，主徐州。 宿州鎮防軍千戶高臘哥與東面總帥劉安國構徐州總帥王德全，殺宿帥紇石烈阿虎，以其州歸海州。 邳州從宜兀林答某亦讓州于杜政，送款海州。 既而皆歸安用。
Guo Yong'an — born An Yong, nicknamed Bite-er, from Zizhou. He had been a follower of the Red Turban leaders Yang An'er and Li Quan. He once submitted to the Mongols as supreme commander and Shandong minister. In Tianxing 1's sixth month Wang You, Feng Xian, and Zhang Xing mutinied at Xuzhou and expelled Yidu. An Yong entered Xuzhou, executed Zhang Xing's faction, and installed Feng Xian as commander. Gao Lage and Liu Anguo killed Suzhou's commissioner and handed the city to Haizhou. Pizhou's acting commander surrendered to Du Zheng and Haizhou. Soon all fell under An Yong.
21
北大將阿術魯聞安用據徐、宿、邳，大怒曰：「此三州我當攻取，安用何人，輒受降。 」遣信安、張進等率兵入徐，欲圖安用，奪其軍。 安用懼，謀于德全，劫殺張進及海州元帥田福等數百人，與楊妙真絕，乃還邳州。 會山東諸將及徐、宿、邳主帥，刑馬結盟，誓歸金朝。 既盟，諸將皆散去，安用無所歸，遂同德全、安國托從宜眾僧奴自通於朝廷。 眾僧奴遣人上奏：「安用以數州反正，功甚大。 且其兵力強盛，材略可稱。 國家果欲倚用，非極品重權不足以堅其許國之心。 」未報。 安用率兵萬人攻海州，未至，眾稍散去。 安國因勸安用當赤心歸國，安用亦自知反復失計，事已無可奈何，於是複金朝衣冠。 妙真怒其叛己，又懼為所圖，悉屠安用家走益都。 安用遂選兵分將，期必得妙真，自此淮海之上無寧歲矣。
Mongol general Ashulu raged that An Yong had accepted Xu, Suzhou, and Pizhou — "I was to take those cities — who is this man?" He sent Zhang Jin into Xuzhou to destroy An Yong and seize his troops. Fearing Ashulu, he murdered Zhang Jin and Tian Fu, broke with Yang Miaozhen, and retreated to Pizhou. He met Shandong commanders, sacrificed a horse, and swore allegiance back to Jin. When the allies dispersed, he had Zhongsengnu petition the Jin court for him. Zhongsengnu wrote, "An Yong's restoration of several prefectures is a great service." His troops are strong and his talent proven." Only the highest rank will bind his loyalty." No answer came. He marched ten thousand men on Haizhou, but they melted away before he arrived. Anguo persuaded him to return wholeheartedly; knowing his schemes had failed, An Yong donned Jin dress again. Miaozhen massacred his household and fled to Yidu. He hunted Miaozhen relentlessly, and the Huai-Hai knew no peace thereafter.
22
未幾，朝廷遣近侍局直長因世英、都事高天祐持手詔至邳，以安用為開府儀同三司、平章政事、兼都元帥、京東山東等路行尚書省事，特封兗王，賜號「英烈戡難保節忠臣」，錫姓完顏，附屬籍，改名用安，賜金鍍銀印、駝紐金印、金虎符、世襲千戶宣命、敕樣、牌樣、禦畫體宣、空頭河朔山東赦文，便宜從事，且以彭王妃誥委用安招妙真。 用安始聞使者至，猶豫未決，以總領楊懋迎使者入，監於州廨，問所以來。 世英對以封建事，意頗順。 諸帥王、杜輩皆不欲宣言，欲殺使者。 明日，用安乃出見使者，跪揖如等夷。 坐定，語世英曰：「予向隨大兵攻汴，嘗于開陽門下與侯摯議內外夾擊。 此時大兵病死者眾，十七頭項皆在京城，若從吾計出軍，中興久矣。 朝廷乃無一人敢決者，今日悔將何及。 」言竟而起。 既而選人取朝廷賜物遍觀之，喜見顏色。 複與使者私議，欲不以朝禮受之，世英等不可，即設宴拜授如儀，以主事常謹等隨使者奉表入謝。
The court sent Yin Shiying with a handwritten edict enfeoffing him Prince of Yan, renaming him Guo Yong'an with Wanyan surname and lavish seals, and tasked him to win over Miaozhen. Yong'an hesitated until Yang Mao brought the envoys in under guard and questioned them. Shiying spoke of the enfeoffment and seemed agreeable. Wang, Du, and other warlords wanted the envoys killed. Next day he met them and bowed as to peers. Seated, he told Shiying he had once urged Hou Zhi to strike Bian from within and without at Kaiyang Gate. Had the seventeen Mongol commanders in Bian followed his plan, Jin might have revived long before. Yet no one at court dared act — now regret was useless. He rose when he finished. Soon he had the imperial gifts displayed and brightened at the sight. He tried to avoid court ceremony; the envoys insisted; after a banquet he accepted, and Chang Jin went to court with thanks.
23
上複遺世英、天祐賜以鐵券一、虎符六、龍文衣一、玉魚帶一、弓矢二、封贈其父母妻誥命，及郡王宣、世襲宣、大信牌、玉兔鶻帶各十，聽同盟可賜者賜之。 使者至邳，用安迎受如禮，始有入援意。 及聞上將遷蔡州，乃遣人以蠟書言遷蔡有六不可，大率以謂：「歸德環城皆水，卒難攻擊，蔡無此險，一也。 歸德雖乏糧儲，而魚芡可以取足，蔡若受圍，廩食有限，二也。 大兵所以去歸德者，非畏我也，縱之出而躡其後，舍其難而就其易者攻焉，三也。 蔡去宋境不百里，萬一資敵兵糧，禍不可解，四也。 歸德不保，水道東行猶可以去，蔡若不守，去將安之，五也。 時方暑雨，千里泥淖，聖體豐澤，不便鞍馬，倉卒遇敵，非臣子所敢言，六也。 雖然，陛下必欲去歸德，莫如權幸山東。 山東富庶甲天下，臣略有其地，東連沂、海，西接徐、邳，南扼盱、楚，北控淄、齊。 若鑾輿少停，臣仰賴威靈，河朔之地可傳檄而定。 惟陛下審察。 」上以其言示宰臣。 宰臣奏用安反復，本無匡輔志，此必參議張介等議之，業已遷蔡，議遂寢。
The emperor added an iron certificate, tallies, robes, belts, bows, ennoblements, and patents for allies. At Pi he received them properly and began to consider marching to aid the emperor. Hearing the move to Cai, he sent a wax letter listing six objections, first that Guide's moats were safer than Cai's. Second, Guide could still feed itself from rivers; besieged Cai could not. Third, the Mongols were luring the court from a defensible city to an easy target. Fourth, Cai lay within a hundred li of Song territory and might feed the enemy. Fifth, if Guide fell one could still flee east by water; from Cai there was no escape. Sixth, summer rains and mud made flight dangerous for the emperor's person — a subject dared not say more. Still, if the emperor must leave Guide, he should visit Shandong first. Shandong was the richest region; he held lands from Yi to the sea, Xu to Pi, Huai to Qi. If the emperor paused there, he could recover Hebei by proclamation. He begged the emperor to consider it. The emperor showed the letter to his ministers. Ministers said Yong'an was treacherous and the plan, likely Zhang Jie's, was ignored once the move to Cai began.
24
初，世英等過徐，王德全、劉安國說之曰：「朝廷恩命豈宜出自用安，郡王宣吾二人最當得者，乞就留之。 」世英乃留郡王宣、世襲宣、玉帶各二。 由是與用安有隙，又懼為所圖，皆不聽其節制。 十郡王者，李明德、封仙、張瑀、張友、卓翼、康琮、杜政、吳歪頭、王德全、劉安國也。 用安必欲取山東，累征徐、宿兵，止以勤王為辭，二帥不應。 用安怒，令杜政等率兵三千，以取糧為名，襲徐、宿。 既入城，德全覺之，就留杜政、封仙不遣。 用安愈怒，謂德全、安國必有謀，乃執桃園帥吳某等八九人下獄鞫問。 二帥遣溫特罕張哥以杜政、封仙欲襲取徐州白用安，不聽，驅吳帥、張哥輩九人並斬之。 張哥將死大呼曰：「國咬兒，汝無尺寸功，受國家大封爵，何負於汝，而從杜政等變亂，又殺無罪之人。 今雖死，當與汝辨於地下矣。 」會上遣臧國昌以密詔徵兵東方，故用安假朝命聲言入援，檄劉安國為前鋒，親率兵三千駐徐州城下招德全。 德全終疑見圖，不出，系封仙於獄，殺之，遣杜政出城。 安國既至宿州，用安複召安國還，安國不從，獨與眾僧奴赴援。 行及臨渙龍山寺，用安使人劫殺之，遂攻徐州，逾三月不能下，退歸漣水。 於是，因世英以用安終不赴援，乃還朝，至宿州西，遇大兵，不屈而死，事聞，贈汝州防禦使。
Passing Xuzhou, Dequan and Anguo demanded the prince patents meant for them, not Yong'an. Shiying left two of each. They feuded with Yong'an and ignored his orders. Ten warlords — Li Mingde, Feng Xian, Zhang Yu, Zhang You, Zhuo Yi, Kang Cong, Du Zheng, Wu Wai'tou, Dequan, and Anguo — ruled their fiefs. Yong'an repeatedly demanded Xu and Su troops under the pretense of relieving the emperor; both refused. He sent Du Zheng with three thousand men to seize grain — in truth to attack Xu and Su. Dequan detected the plot and held Du Zheng and Feng Xian. He imprisoned Taoyuan's Wu and others, suspecting Dequan and Anguo. They sent Zhang Ge to warn him; he ignored it and executed nine men including Zhang Ge. Dying, Zhang Ge cursed "State Bite-er" for rewarding treachery and murdering innocents. I will face you in the grave!" The emperor's secret summons let Yong'an claim he was marching to aid the throne while besieging Dequan. Dequan refused to emerge, killed Feng Xian, and expelled Du Zheng. Anguo reached Suzhou but ignored recall and marched with Zhongsengnu to aid the emperor. At Linhuan's Longshan Temple he ambushed and killed them, failed for three months to take Xuzhou, and retreated to Lianshui. Yin Shiying, seeing Yong'an would not aid the emperor, returned west of Suzhou, met Mongols, and died unyielding — posthumously Defender of Ruzhou.
25
既而用安軍食不給，乞糧于宋，宋陽許之，即改從宋衣冠，而私與朝使相親。 尋益乏食，軍民多亡去，乃命蕭均以嚴刑禁亡者，血流滿道。 大元東平萬戶查剌將兵至漣水，遂降焉。 查剌既渡河，趨蔡州，用安以詭計還漣水，複叛歸於宋，受浙東總管、忠州團練使，隸淮閫。 甲午正月，聞大兵圍沛，用安往救之，敗走徐州。 會移兵攻徐，用安投水死，求得其屍，皮刂面系馬尾，為怨家田福一軍臠食而盡。
Starving, he begged Song grain, donned Song dress, yet courted Jin envoys. As more fled, Xiao Jun's draconian orders left blood on the roads. Mongol commander Chala of Dongping took Lianshui and Yong'an surrendered. Chala marched on Cai; Yong'an tricked his way back to Lianshui, rejoined Song as Huai commander. In jiawu's first month he failed to relieve Pei and fled to Xuzhou. Besieged at Xu, he drowned; his corpse was flayed and Tian Fu's men ate his flesh to the last scrap.
26
用安形狀短小無須，喜與輕薄子遊，日擊鞠衢市間，顧眄自矜，無將帥大體。
Short, beardless, and vain, he played cuju in the streets — no bearing of a commander.
27
介字介甫，平州人，正大元年經義進士第一，時為用安參議。
Zhang Jie of Pingzhou, top classics graduate of Zhengda 1, served as his councillor.
28
初，天祐等出汴，微服間行，經北軍營幕，至通許崔橋，始有義軍招撫司官府，去京師二百里矣。 至陳州，防禦使粘葛奴申始立州事。 留二日，至項城，縣令硃珍立縣事，有士卒千二百人。 至泰和縣，縣令王義立縣已五月矣。 八月，至宿州，眾僧奴得報，且知朝廷授以權宿州節度使、兼元帥左都監之命，具彩輿儀衛出城五里奉迎。 時東方不知朝廷音問已八月矣，官民見使者至，且拜且哭。 有張顯者任俠尚氣知義理，即謂天祐曰：「東方不知朝廷音問已數月，今見使者，百姓皆感動。 若不以聖旨撫慰之，恐失東民之必。 我欲矯稱制旨宣諭，如何。 」天祐書生，守規矩，不敢從，但以宰相旨集州民慰撫之，州民複大哭。 明日，往徐州。
When Gao Tianyou left Bian in disguise, he passed Mongol camps to Cuiqiao — two hundred li from the capital — before finding any Jin office. At Chenzhou Defender Nianshenu was just organizing the prefecture. At Xiangcheng Magistrate Zhu Zhen had twelve hundred men after five months' rule. Taihe's Wang Yi had governed five months. In the eighth month at Suzhou, Zhongsengnu learned he was named commissioner and marched out five li with full honors. The east had heard nothing from court for eight months; officials and people wept at the envoys. Zhang Xian, a man of honor, urged Tianyou: "Months without word from court — the people are moved to tears by your arrival." Without an imperial edict to reassure them, the east may turn away. May I proclaim an edict in the emperor's name? he asked. Tianyou, a cautious scholar, refused; he comforted the people by ministerial order, and they wept again. The next day they proceeded to Xuzhou.
29
時青，滕陽人。 初與叔父全俱為紅祆賊，及楊安兒、劉二祖敗，承赦來降，隸軍中。 興定初，青為濟州義軍萬戶。 是時，叔父全為行樞密院經歷官。 興定二年冬，全馳驛過東平，青來見，因告全將叛入宋，全秘之。 頃之，青率其眾入于宋。 宋人置之淮南，屯龜山，有眾數萬。
Shi Qing of Tengyang He and his uncle Quan had been Red Turban rebels; after amnesty they entered Jin service. In early Xingding he commanded Jizhou's Righteous Army. His uncle Quan then served the Travelling Secretariat. In Xingding 2's winter Quan learned at Dongping that Qing meant to defect to Song and kept silent. Soon Qing took his band into Song lands. The Song posted them at Guishan in Huainan with tens of thousands of men.
30
興定四年，泗州行元帥府紇石烈牙吾塔遣人招之，青以書來。 書曰：「青本滕陽良民，遭時亂離，扶老攜幼避地草莽。 官吏不明此心，目以叛逆，無所逃死，竄匿淮海。 離親舊、去鄉邑，豈人情之所樂哉。 僕雖偷生寄食他國，首丘之念，未嘗一日忘之。 如朝廷赦青之罪，乞假邳州以屯老幼。 當襲取盱眙，盡定淮南，以贖往昔之過。 」牙吾塔複書曰：「公等初亦無罪，誠能為國建功，全軍來歸，即吾人也。 邳州吾城，以吾人居之，亦何不可。 《易》曰：'君子見幾而作，不俟終日。 '公其亟圖之。 生還父母之邦，富貴終身，傳芳後世，與其羈縻異域，目以兵虜，孰愈哉？ 」牙吾塔奏其事。 十月，詔加青銀青榮祿大夫，封滕陽公，仍為本處兵馬總領元帥、兼宣撫使。 青潛表陳謝，複以邳州為請。 樞密院奏：「恐青意止欲得邳州。 可諭牙吾塔，若青誠實來歸，即當授之。 如審其詐，可使人入宋境宣佈往來之言，及所援官爵，亦行間之術也。 」青既不得邳州，複為宋守。
In Xingding 4 Sizhou's Yawuta sent envoys; Qing replied by letter. He wrote, "I was a peaceful man of Tengyang driven to the wilds with my family." Officials called me rebel; I hid on the Huai-Hai." Exile from home is no man's joy." Though I eat Song rice, I dream of home every day." Pardon me and lend me Pizhou for my families." I will take Xuyi and recover Huainan to atone." Yawuta answered, "Return with your whole army and you are one of us again." Pizhou is ours — why not give it to you?" The Changes says the nobleman acts when he sees the sign." Act quickly." Come home to honor rather than live as a Song captive." Yawuta reported to court. In the tenth month the court enfeoffed him Duke of Tengyang and named him pacification commissioner. Qing secretly thanked them and again begged Pizhou. The Secretariat warned he only wanted Pizhou. Tell Yawuta to grant Pizhou if he is sincere. If he deceives us, spread word of his offers in Song — a stratagem of discord." Denied Pizhou, Qing remained Song's man.
31
興定五年正月二十五日夜，青襲破泗州西城，提控王祿遇害。 是時，時全為同簽樞密院事，朝廷不知青襲破西城，止稱宋人而已。 詔全往督泗州兵取西城。 全至泗州，獲紅祆賊一人，詰問之，乃知青為宋京東鈐轄，襲破西城。 全頗喜，乃殺其人以滅口。 牙吾塔晝夜力戰，募死士以梯沖逼城，青縋兵出拒不得前。 牙吾塔遣提控王應孫穴城，東北隅，青夜出兵來襲，擊卻之。 越二日，複出又卻之。 攻城益急，青以舟兵二千合城中兵來犯牙吾塔營，提控斡魯朵先知，設伏掩擊，青兵大敗，溺淮水死者千人，自是不復出矣。 王應孫穴城將及城中，青隧地然薪，逼出之。 青乘城指麾，流矢中其目，餘眾往往被創，樓堞相繼摧壞，城中恟懼，遂無固志。 二月二十六日夜，青拔眾走，遂複西城。
On Xingding 5's first month, day 25, Qing stormed Sizhou's west gate and killed Commander Wang Lu. Shi Quan was at court; they thought only Song troops had struck. Quan was ordered to retake the west gate. At Sizhou he caught a Red Turban deserter and learned Qing had led the raid. Pleased at the intelligence, Quan killed the prisoner to silence him. Yawuta assaulted day and night; Qing's rope troops held him off. Wang Yingsun tunneled; Qing's night attack repulsed him. Two days later another sortie failed. Qing's river assault was ambushed; a thousand drowned; he never sallied again. Yingsun nearly broke through; Qing counter-mined with fire and drove him out. An arrow blinded one eye; walls crumbled; panic spread. On the twenty-sixth of the second month Qing fled; Sizhou recovered the west city.
32
五月，兵還，距淮二十里，諸軍將渡，全矯稱密詔「諸軍且留收淮南麥」，遂下令人獲麥三石以給軍。 眾惑之，訛可及諸將佐勸之不聽，軍留三日。 訛可謂全曰：「今淮水淺狹，可以速濟。 時方暑雨，若值暴漲，宋乘其後，將不得完歸矣。 」全力拒之。 從宜達阿、移失不、斜烈、李辛稍稍不平，全怒曰：「訛可一帥耳，汝曹黨之。 汝曹致身至此，皆吾之力。 吾院官也，於汝無不可者。 」眾乃不敢言。 是夜，大雨。 明日，淮水暴漲，乃為橋渡軍。 宋兵襲之，軍遂敗績。 橋壞，全以輕舟先濟，士卒皆覆沒。 宣宗乃下詔誅之，遣官招集潰軍，詔曰：「大軍渡淮，每立功效。 諸將謬誤，部曲散亡，流離憂苦，朕甚閔焉。 各歸舊營，勉圖自效。 」又詔曰：「陣亡把軍品官子孫，十五以上者依品官子孫例隨局承應，十五以下、十歲以上者依品從隨局給俸，至成人本局差使。 無子孫官，依例給俸。 應贈官、賻錢、軍人家口當養贍者。 並如舊制。」
Returning in the fifth month, Quan forged an edict to detain the army for Huainan wheat. Officers protested; he held them three days. Eke warned the Huai was shallow and they must cross quickly. Summer rains might swell the river and trap them against Song pursuers. Quan refused. Subordinates murmured; Quan raged that they owed their posts to him. You are here because of me." As Secretariat officer I can do as I please with you." They fell silent. That night the rains came. Next day the Huai flooded; they bridged it. Song attacked; the army was shattered. The bridge collapsed; Quan fled in a light boat while his men drowned. Emperor Xuanzong ordered Quan executed and issued edicts recalling the scattered troops. He lamented the generals' errors and the soldiers' suffering. Return to your camps and fight anew." Another edict cared for fallen officers' sons. Childless officers' families were provided for. Ennoblements, funeral funds, and dependents followed old rules. All as before."

贊曰：金自章宗季年，宋韓侂胄構難，招誘鄰境亡命以撓中原，事竟無成。 而青、徐、淮海之郊民心一搖，歲遇饑饉，盜賊蜂起，相為長雄，又自屠滅，害及無辜，十餘年糜沸未息。 宣宗不思靖難，複為伐宋之舉，迄金之亡，其禍尤甚。 簡書所載國用安、時青等遺事，至今仁人君子讀之猶蹙頞終日。 當時烝黎，如魚在釜，其何以自存乎。 兵，兇器也。 金以兵得國，亦以兵失國，可不慎哉，可不慎哉！
The eulogy: after Emperor Zhangzong, Song's Han Tuozhou stirred trouble by harboring border fugitives — to no avail. Yet Qing, Xu, and Huai-Hai boiled with famine, bandit chiefs, and mutual slaughter for a decade. Xuanzong did not pacify the realm but attacked Song again — worsening Jin's fall. Records of Yong'an and Qing still make humane readers wince. The people were fish in a boiling pot. War is an ill omen. Jin won its empire by the sword and lost it by the sword — take heed, take heed!
