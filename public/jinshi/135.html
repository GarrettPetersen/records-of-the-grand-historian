1
外國下
Foreign Countries (Part 2)
2
高麗國王，王楷。 其地鴨綠江以東，曷懶路以南，東南皆至於海。 自遼時，歲時遣使修貢，事具《遼史》。
The king of Goryeo was Wang Kai. His realm stretched east of the Yalu River and south along the Halin Route, reaching the sea in the southeast. From the Liao period onward they sent annual tribute missions; the particulars are given in the History of Liao.
3
唐初，靺鞨有粟末、黑水兩部，皆臣屬於高麗。 唐滅高麗，粟末保東牟山漸強大，號渤海，姓大氏，有文物禮樂。 至唐末稍衰，自後不復有聞。 金伐遼，渤海來歸，蓋其遺裔也。 黑水靺鞨居古肅慎地，有山曰白山，蓋長白山，金國之所起焉。 女直雖舊屬高麗，不復相通者久矣。 及金滅遼，高麗以事遼舊禮稱臣于金。
Early in the Tang, the Mohe were divided into the Sumo and Heishui branches, both subordinate to Goryeo. After Tang destroyed Goryeo, the Sumo, entrenched at Dongmoushan, grew steadily stronger, proclaimed the state of Bohai under the Da clan, and cultivated civil institutions, ritual, and music. By the late Tang they had waned, and after that they passed from record. When the Jin campaigned against Liao, Bohai submitted—likely their descendants. The Heishui Mohe lived in the old land of the Sushen; their Mount Baishan is Mount Changbai, the place from which the Jin state sprang. Though the Jurchens had once been under Goryeo, they had long since ceased all contact. Once Jin overthrew Liao, Goryeo adopted the same tributary etiquette they had shown Liao and acknowledged Jin as suzerain.
4
初，有醫者善治疾，本高麗人，不知其始自何而來，亦不著其姓名，居女直之完顏部。 穆宗時戚屬有疾，此醫者診視之，穆宗謂醫者曰：「汝能使此人病癒，則吾遣人送汝歸汝鄉國。 」醫者曰：「諾。 」其人疾果愈，穆宗乃以初約歸之。 乙離骨嶺僕散部胡石來勃堇居高麗、女直之兩間，穆宗使族人叟阿招之，因使叟阿送醫者，歸之高麗境上。 醫者歸至高麗，因謂高麗人，女直居黑水部者部族日強，兵益精悍，年穀屢稔。 高麗王聞之。 乃通使於女直。 既而，胡石來來歸，遂率乙離骨嶺東諸部皆內附。
In the beginning there was a physician skilled in healing, a native of Goryeo whose origins and name were unknown, who resided among the Wanyan Jurchens. A kinsman of Emperor Muzong fell ill, and this physician attended him. Muzong told the physician, "If you can cure this man, I will have you escorted home to your country." The physician replied, "I will." The patient recovered, and Muzong kept his word and sent him back. Hushilai, a Pusan chieftain of the Yilibone Ridge district, dwelt between Goryeo and the Jurchens. Muzong dispatched his kinsman Sou'a to summon him and had Sou'a escort the physician to the Goryeo frontier. Back in Goryeo, the physician reported that the Jurchen tribes of the Heishui region were growing daily in strength, their soldiers ever more formidable, and their harvests repeatedly plentiful. The king of Goryeo heard of it. He accordingly opened diplomatic contact with the Jurchens. Before long Hushilai submitted, and the tribes east of Yilibone Ridge followed him into Jin allegiance.
5
穆宗十年癸未，阿疏自遼使其徒達紀來說曷懶甸人，曷懶甸人執之。 穆宗以達紀送高麗，謂高麗王曰：「前此為亂於汝鄙者，皆此輩也。 」及破蕭海里，使斡魯罕往高麗報捷，高麗亦使使來賀。 未幾，複使斜葛與斡魯罕往聘，高麗王曰：「斜葛，女直之族弟也，其禮有加矣。 」乃以一大銀盤為謝。
In Muzong's tenth year, guwei (1043), Ashu sent his man Daji from Liao to sway the people of the Halin district, but they seized him. Muzong handed Daji over to Goryeo, telling the king, "The troublemakers on your frontier have been men of this kind." After defeating Xiao Haili, he sent Woluhan to Goryeo with news of the victory, and Goryeo sent envoys in congratulation. Soon he sent Xiege and Woluhan again on a friendly mission. The king of Goryeo said, "Xiege is a Jurchen kinsman, and he has been honored with exceptional ceremony." He presented a large silver platter in gratitude.
6
厥後，曷懶甸諸部盡欲來附，高麗聞之不欲使來附，恐近於己而不利也，使人邀止之。 斜葛在高麗及往來曷懶道中，具知其事，遂使石適歡往納曷懶甸人。 未行而穆宗沒，康宗嗣，遣石適歡以星顯統門之兵往至乙離骨嶺，益募兵趨活涅水，徇地曷懶甸，收叛亡七城。 高麗使人來告曰：「事有當議者。 」曷懶甸官屬使斜勒詳穩、冶剌保詳穩往，石適歡亦使杯魯往，高麗執冶剌保等，而遣杯魯曰：「無與爾事。 」於是，五水之民皆附於高麗，團練使陷者十四人。
Later every tribe of the Halin district sought to submit. Goryeo, fearing neighbors so close, sent men to block them. Xiege, while in Goryeo and traveling the Halin routes, learned the whole affair and sent Shishihuan to bring the Halin people in. Before Shishihuan marched, Muzong died and Kangzong succeeded. Kangzong sent him with Xingxian Tongmen troops to Yilibone Ridge, raised more men, advanced to the Huonie River, pacified the Halin district, and recovered seven rebel towns. Goryeo sent word that certain matters required discussion. Halin officials sent Xiele Xiangwen and Yeliba Xiangwen; Shishihuan sent Peilu as well. Goryeo detained Yeliba and his party and returned Peilu with the message, "This does not concern you." Thereupon the Five Waters people all went over to Goryeo, and fourteen regimental commanders were captured.
7
二年甲申，高麗來攻，石適歡大破之，殺獲甚眾，追入其境，焚略其戍守而還。 四月，高麗複來攻，石適歡以五百人禦于辟登水，複大破之，追入辟登水，逐其殘眾逾境。 於是，高麗王曰：「告邊釁者皆官屬祥丹、傍都里、昔畢罕輩也。 」十四團練、六路使人在高麗者，皆歸之，遣使來請和。 遂使斜葛經正疆界，至乙離骨水、曷懶甸活禰水，留之兩月。 斜葛不能聽訟，每一事輒至枝蔓，民頗苦之。 康宗召斜葛還，而遣石適歡往。 石適歡立幕府于三潺水，其嘗陰與高麗往來為亂階者，即正其罪，餘無所問。 康宗以為能。
In the second year, jiashen (1044), Goryeo attacked. Shishihuan routed them with heavy casualties, pursued into their land, burned their frontier posts, and withdrew. In the fourth month Goryeo attacked again. With five hundred men Shishihuan held the Bideng River, won another great victory, pursued into the river, and drove the survivors beyond the border. The king of Goryeo then said, "The border provocations were the work of officials Xiangdan, Bangduli, Xibihan, and the like." The fourteen regimental commanders and six-route commissioners held in Goryeo were released, and envoys came to sue for peace. Xiege was sent to fix the border as far as the Yilibone and Halin Huonie rivers and stayed two months. Xiege could not judge cases cleanly; every dispute sprawled into tangents, and the people suffered. Kangzong recalled Xiege and sent Shishihuan instead. Shishihuan set up headquarters at the Sanshan River, punished those who had secretly dealt with Goryeo and fomented disorder, and questioned no one else. Kangzong judged him capable.
8
四年丙戌，高麗使使黑歡方石來賀嗣位，康宗使杯魯報聘，且尋前約，取亡命之民，高麗許之。 曰：「使使至境上受之。 」康宗以為信然，使完顏部阿聒、烏林答部勝昆往境上受之。 康宗畋于馬紀嶺乙只村以待之。 阿聒、勝昆至境上，高麗遣人殺之，而出兵曷懶甸，築九城。
In the fourth year, bingxu (1046), Goryeo sent Heihuan and Fangshi to congratulate the succession. Kangzong sent Peilu on a return visit to renew the earlier pact to recover fugitives, and Goryeo agreed. They said, "Have your envoys receive them at the frontier." Kangzong took them at their word and sent Wanyan's Asuo and Ulinada's Shengkun to the border to take delivery. Kangzong hunted at Yizhi Village on Maji Ridge to await them. At the frontier Goryeo killed Asuo and Shengkun, marched into the Halin district, and built nine forts.
9
康宗歸，眾鹹曰：「不可舉兵也，恐遼人將以罪我。 」太祖獨曰：「若不舉兵，豈止失曷懶甸，諸部皆非吾有也。 」康宗以為然，乃使斡塞將兵伐之，大破高麗兵。 六月，高麗率眾來戰，斡塞敗之，進圍其城。 七月，高麗複請和，康宗曰：「事若酌中，則與之和。 」高麗許歸亡入之民，罷九城之戍，複所侵故地，遂與之和。
On his return everyone urged, "Do not raise troops, lest Liao hold us accountable." Taizu alone said, "If we do not fight, we lose more than Halin—every tribe will slip from our grasp." Kangzong agreed and sent Wansai, who crushed the Goryeo force. In the sixth month Goryeo came in force; Wansai defeated them and besieged their city. In the seventh month Goryeo sued for peace again. Kangzong said, "If the terms are fair, accept peace." Goryeo promised to return fugitives, dismantle the nine forts, and restore seized lands, and peace was concluded.
10
收國元年九月，太祖已克黃龍府，命加古撒喝攻保州。 保州近高麗，遼侵高麗置保州。 至是，命撒喝取之，久不下，撒喝請濟師，且言高麗王將遣使來。 太祖使納合烏蠢以百騎益之，詔撒喝曰：「汝領偏師，屢破重敵，多所俘獲，及聞胡沙數戰有功，朕甚嘉之。 若保州未下，但守邊戍，吾已克黃龍府，聞遼主且至，俟破大敵複益汝兵。 所言高麗遣使事，未知果否，至則護送以來。 邊境之事，慎之毋忽。 」十一月，系遼女直麻懣太彎等十五人皆降，攻開州取之，盡降保州諸部女直。 太祖以撒喝為保州路都統。
In the ninth month of Shouguo 1 (1117), after Taizu had taken Huanglong Prefecture, he ordered Jagusa to attack Baozhou. Baozhou lay near Goryeo; Liao had seized it from Goryeo and garrisoned it. Now Sagua was told to take it. The siege dragged on; he asked for reinforcements and reported that the Goryeo king might send envoys. Taizu sent Nahe Wuchan with a hundred cavalry and told Sagua, "You lead a detached column, have repeatedly broken strong foes, and taken many captives; I was greatly pleased to hear Husha distinguished himself in battle." If Baozhou still holds, hold the frontier; I have taken Huanglong and hear the Liao emperor approaches—after the main battle I will send more troops. Whether Goryeo truly sends envoys is uncertain; if they come, escort them to me. Guard the frontier carefully and neglect nothing. In the eleventh month fifteen Liao Jurchens including Mamantaiwan surrendered; Kaizhou was taken and every Jurchen tribe of Baozhou submitted. Taizu made Sagua supreme commander of the Baozhou circuit.
11
太祖已破走遼主軍，撒喝破合主、順化二城，複請濟師攻保州，使斡魯以甲士千人往。 二年閏月，高麗遣使來賀捷，且曰：「保州本吾舊地，願以見還。 」太祖謂使者曰：「爾其自取之。 」詔撒喝、烏蠢等曰：「若高麗來取保州，益以胡剌古、習顯等軍備之，或欲合兵，無得輒往，但謹守邊戍。 」及撒喝、阿實賚等攻保州，遼守將遁去，而高麗兵已在城中。 既而，高麗國王使蒲馬請保州，詔諭高麗王曰：「保州近爾邊境，聽爾自取，今乃勤我師徒，破敵城下。 且蒲馬止是口陳，俟有表請，即當別議。」
After Taizu had routed the Liao emperor's army, Sagua took Hezhu and Shunhua and again asked for troops against Baozhou; Wolu went with a thousand armored men. In the intercalary second month of year 2, Goryeo congratulated the victory and said, "Baozhou was ours; we ask that it be returned." Taizu told the envoys, "Take it yourselves." He ordered Sagua and Wuchan, "If Goryeo comes for Baozhou, reinforce them with Hulagu, Xixian, and the rest; if they want joint action, do not rush in—only hold the frontier." When Sagua and Ashizhen attacked Baozhou, the Liao commander fled, but Goryeo troops were already in the city. Soon the Goryeo king sent Puma to ask for Baozhou. The edict read, "Baozhou borders you and we let you take it yourself; now you have drawn our army to fight beneath its walls." Puma spoke only by mouth; when a formal petition arrives, we shall decide separately."
12
天輔二年十二月，詔諭高麗國王曰：「朕始興師伐遼，已嘗佈告，賴皇天助順，屢敗敵兵，北自上京，南至於海，其間京府州縣部族人民悉皆撫定。 今遣孛堇朮孛報諭，仍賜馬一匹，至可領也。」
In the twelfth month of Tianfu 2 (1118), an edict told the Goryeo king, "When I first marched against Liao I proclaimed my intent; Heaven aided the righteous cause and we repeatedly defeated the foe; from Shangjing north to the sea south, every capital, prefecture, county, tribe, and people between have been settled." I now send Commander Zhu Bao to notify you and grant one horse for you to accept."
13
三年，高麗增築長城三尺，邊吏發兵止之，弗從，報曰：「修補舊城。 」曷懶甸孛堇胡剌古、習顯以聞，詔曰：「毋得侵軼生事，但慎固營壘，廣布耳目而已。」
In year 3 Goryeo raised the Great Wall three feet. Border officials sent troops to stop them; they refused, saying they were repairing the old wall. Halin commanders Hulagu and Xixian reported it. The edict said, "Do not raid or provoke; only fortify camps and extend scouts."
14
四年，咸州路都統司以兵分屯於保州、畢里圍二城，請益兵，詔曰：「汝等分列屯戍，以固封守，甚善。 高麗累世臣事於遼，或有交通，可常遣人偵伺。」
In year 4 the Xianzhou circuit headquarters divided troops between Baozhou and Biliwei and asked for reinforcements. The edict said, "Separate garrisons to secure the border—well done." Goryeo served Liao for generations; they may still communicate—send scouts regularly."
15
使習顯以獲遼國州郡諭高麗，其國方誅亂者，使謂習顯曰：「此與先父國王之書。 」習顯就館。 凡誅戮官僚七十余人，即依舊禮接見，而以表來賀，並貢方物。 複以遼帝亡入夏國報之。
Xixian was sent to tell Goryeo of captured Liao prefectures. The court was executing rebels; they told Xixian, "This is a letter from my late father's reign." Xixian went to the lodge. More than seventy officials were executed; he was then received under the old forms, presented a congratulatory memorial, and sent local tribute. They also reported that the Liao emperor had fled into Western Xia.
16
高隨、斜野奉使高麗，至境上，接待之禮不遜，隨等不敢往，太宗曰：「高麗世臣於遼，當以事遼之禮事我，而我國有新喪，遼主未獲，勿遽強之。 」命高隨等還。 天會二年，同知南路都統鶻實答奏，高麗納叛亡、增邊備，必有異圖。 詔曰：「凡有通問，毋違常式。 或來侵略，則整爾行列與之從事。 敢先犯彼者，雖捷必罰。 」詔闍母以甲士千人戍海島，以備之。
Gao Sui and Xieye were sent to Goryeo; at the frontier the reception was disrespectful and they dared not enter. Taizong said, "Goryeo long served Liao and should treat us with Liao etiquette, but we mourn a new death and the Liao emperor is not yet taken—do not press them." He ordered Gao Sui and his party to return. In Tianhui 2 (1124), the southern route deputy reported Goryeo sheltering fugitives and strengthening defenses, surely with hostile intent. The edict said, "In all exchanges keep to established protocol. If they invade, form your ranks and fight. Whoever strikes first, though victorious, will be punished." Chanmu was ordered to garrison the sea islands with a thousand armored men as precaution.
17
四年，國王王楷遣使奉表稱籓，優詔答之。 上使高伯淑、烏至忠使高麗，凡遣使往來當盡循遼舊，仍取保州路及邊地人口在彼界者，須盡數發還。 敕伯淑曰：「若一一聽從，即以保州地賜之。 」高伯淑至高麗，王楷附表謝，一依事遼舊制。 八年，楷上表，乞免索保州亡入邊戶。 是歲，高麗十人捕魚，大風飄其船抵海岸，曷蘇館人獲之，詔還其國。 既而勖上表請不索保州亡入高麗戶口，太宗從之，自是保州封域始定。
In year 4 King Wang Kai sent a memorial declaring vassalage; a gracious edict answered. The court sent Gao Boshu and Wuzhi Zhong to Goryeo. All missions were to follow Liao precedent and recover every Baozhou and border subject on their side. Boshu was told, "If they obey on every point, grant them Baozhou." At Goryeo Wang Kai added a memorial of thanks, fully following Liao forms. In year 8 Kai petitioned to be excused from reclaiming Baozhou fugitives on the border. That year ten Goryeo fishermen were blown ashore; Yasuguan people seized them and an edict returned them home. Soon Xu petitioned not to reclaim Baozhou fugitives in Goryeo; Taizong agreed, and Baozhou's borders were fixed.
18
皇統二年，詔加楷開府儀同三司、上柱國。 六年，楷薨，子晛嗣立。
In Huangtong 2 (1142) Kai was given Kaifu Yitong Sansi and Shangzhuguo. In year 6 Kai died and his son Xian succeeded.
19
大定四年，鴨綠江堡戍頗被侵越焚毀。 五年正月，世宗因正旦使朝辭，諭之曰：「邊境小小不虞，爾主使然邪，疆吏為之邪？ 若果疆吏為之，爾主亦當懲戒之也。 」初，高麗使者別有私進禮物以為常，是歲萬春節，上以使者私進不應典禮，詔罷之。
In Dading 4 (1164) Yalu River garrison forts suffered repeated raids and burning. In the first month of year 5, as New Year envoys took leave, Shizong told them, "Were these small border troubles your king's doing or his officers'? If his officers did it, your king should punish them too." Goryeo envoys had habitually offered private gifts aside from tribute; that Ten Thousand Springs festival the court ruled these improper and abolished them.
20
十年，王晛弟翼陽公皓廢晛自立。 十月，賜生日使、大宗正丞颭至界上，高麗邊吏稱前王已讓位，不肯受使者。 十一年三月，王皓以讓國來奏告，詔婆速路勿受，有司移文詳問。 高麗告曰：「前王久病，昏耄不治，以母弟皓權攝國事。 」上曰：「讓國大事也，何以不先陳請。 」詔有司再詳問。 高麗乃以王晛讓國表來，大略稱先臣楷遺訓傳位於弟，又言其子有罪不可立之意。 上疑之，以問宰執，丞相良弼奏曰：「此不可信。 晛止一子，往年生孫，嘗有表自陳生孫之喜，一也。 皓嘗作亂，晛囚之，二也。 今晛不遣使，皓乃遣使，三也。 朝廷賜晛生日使，皓不轉達於晛，乃稱未敢奉受，四也。 今皓篡兄誣于天子，安可忍也。 」右丞孟浩曰：「當詢彼國士民，果皆推服，即當遣使封冊。 」上曰：「封一國之君詢於民眾，此與除拜猛安謀克何異。 」乃卻其使者，而以詔書詳問王晛，吏部侍郎靖為宣問王晛使。
In year 10 Wang Xian's younger brother Duke Hao of Yiyang deposed Xian and took the throne. In the tenth month birthday envoy Zheng of the Imperial Clan Directorate reached the border; Goryeo officials said the former king had abdicated and would not receive him. In the third month of year 11 Wang Hao came to announce his succession; an edict told Bian Su circuit not to receive him and the ministry sent documents for full inquiry. Goryeo reported that the former king had long been ill, senile and unfit to rule, and that his uterine younger brother Hao was acting as regent. The emperor said, "Surrendering the throne is a grave affair—why was it not announced in advance?" An edict ordered the ministries to investigate further. Goryeo then sent Wang Xian's abdication memorial, claiming Kai's deathbed wish to pass the throne to his brother and that his son was unworthy to succeed. The emperor was doubtful and consulted his ministers. Chancellor Liang Bi said, "This cannot be trusted." First, Xian has only one son; he once memorialized rejoicing at a grandson's birth." Second, Hao once rebelled and Xian imprisoned him." Third, Xian sends no envoys while Hao does." Fourth, the court's birthday envoys for Xian were not forwarded; Hao said he dared not receive them." Hao has seized power from his brother and deceived the throne—this cannot be endured." Vice Chancellor Meng Hao said, "Inquire whether their people truly accept him; only then send investiture envoys." The emperor said, "Consulting the masses to enfeoff a king is no different from appointing a Meng'an or Mouke." He rejected their envoys and sent an edict questioning Wang Xian; Vice Minister Jing was made inquiry envoy.
21
皓實篡國，囚晛於海島。 靖至高麗，皓稱王晛已避位出居他所，病加無損，不能就位拜命，往復險遠，非使者所宜往。 靖竟不得見晛，乃以詔授皓，轉取晛表附奏，其言與前表大概相同。 靖還，上問大臣，皆曰：「晛表如此，可遂封之。 」丞相良弼、平章政事守道曰：「待皓祈請未晚也。 」十二月，皓遣其禮部侍郎張翼明等請封。 十二年三月，遂賜封冊。 皓生日在正月十九日，是歲十二月將盡，未及遣使，有司請至來歲舉行焉。
Hao had indeed usurped the throne and imprisoned Xian on a sea island. At Goryeo, Hao said Xian had abdicated and lived elsewhere, was too ill to receive the mandate, and the route was too dangerous for an envoy. Jing never saw Xian; he gave the edict to Hao and attached Xian's memorial, which matched the earlier one. On Jing's return the ministers said Xian's memorial sufficed for investiture. Liang Bi and Shoudao urged waiting until Hao himself petitioned. In the twelfth month Hao sent Zhang Yiming of the Ministry of Rites to request investiture. In the third month of year 12 investiture was granted. Hao's birthday was the nineteenth of the first month; the year was nearly over, so the office asked to defer the birthday mission until the next year.
22
十五年，高麗西京留守趙位寵叛皓，遣徐彥等九十六人上表曰：「前王本非避讓，大將軍鄭沖夫、郎將李義方實弑之。 臣位寵請以慈悲嶺以西至鴨綠江四十餘城內屬，請兵助援。 」上曰：「王皓已加封冊，位寵輒敢稱兵為亂，且欲納土，朕懷撫萬邦，豈助叛臣為虐。 」詔執徐彥等送高麗。 頃之，王皓定趙位寵之亂，遣使奏謝，自位寵之亂，皓所遣生日回謝、橫賜回謝、賀正旦、進奉、萬春節等使，皆阻不通，至是，皓並奏之。 詔答其意，其合遣人使令節次入朝。
In year 15 Zhao Weichong, Goryeo's Western Capital commissioner, rebelled and sent Xu Yan and ninety-six men claiming the former king had not abdicated but was murdered by Zheng Chongfu and Li Yifang. Weichong asked that more than forty cities from Cibei Ridge west to the Yalu be annexed and troops sent to aid him. The emperor said, "Hao is already invested; Weichong rebels and offers land—I comfort all realms and will not aid a rebel." An edict sent Xu Yan and his party to Goryeo in custody. Hao soon suppressed Weichong and sent thanks; he also reported that birthday, gift, New Year, tribute, and festival missions had all been blocked during the revolt. The court acknowledged this and ordered the proper missions to resume in turn.
23
十七年，賀正旦禮物，玉帶乃石似玉者，有司請移問，上曰：「彼小國無能識者，誤以為玉耳，不必移問。 」乃止。 十二月，有司奏高麗下節押馬官順成例外將帶甲三過界，上以使人所坐罪重，但令發還本國而已。 二十三年，皓母任氏薨，皓乞免賜生日及賀謝等事，詔從之。
In year 17 their New Year gift included a stone belt mistaken for jade. The office wanted to inquire; the emperor said the small state could not tell jade from stone and no note was needed. The matter was dropped. In the twelfth month the office reported Goryeo's escort Shuncheng had thrice led armed men across the border illegally; though the offense was grave, they were only sent home. In year 23 Hao's mother Lady Ren died; he asked to suspend birthday and congratulatory missions and the court agreed.
24
承安二年，皓表自陳衰病，以國讓其弟镡。 镡權國事。 是歲，皓廢，镡嗣立。
In Cheng'an 2 Hao memorialized that he was aged and ill and yielded the throne to his brother Chan. Chan acted as regent. That year Hao was deposed and Chan succeeded.
25
泰和四年正月乙丑朔，高麗傔人以小佩刀割梨廡下巡廊，奉職見而糾之，詔館伴官自今前期移文禁止。 是歲，王镡薨，子韺嗣立。
On New Year's Day of Taihe 4, Goryeo attendants cut pears in the side corridor with small knives; an attendant reported it and hosting officials were told to forbid it by advance notice. That year Wang Chan died and his son Yin succeeded.
26
泰和七年正月，是時用兵伐宋，夏亦有故，獨高麗遣正旦使，詔不賜曲宴。 及天壽節，夏、高麗使者皆在，有司奏：「大定初，宋未請和，夏、高麗使者賜曲宴，今請依大定故事。 」詔從之。
In the first month of Taihe 7, while campaigning against Song and Xia had their own troubles, only Goryeo sent New Year envoys; the curved banquet was withheld. At the Heavenly Longevity festival both Xia and Goryeo envoys were present; the office asked to follow the Dading precedent of granting curved banquets. The edict approved.
27
至甯元年八月，王祦薨，嗣子未行起複。 九月，宣宗即位，邊吏奏：「高麗牒稱，嗣子未起複，不可以凶服迎吉詔，又不可以草土名銜署表。 」禮官議：「人臣不以私恩廢公義，宜權用吉服迎詔，署表用權國事名銜。 俟高麗告哀使至闕，然後遣使致祭、慰問及行封冊。 」制可。
In the eighth month of Zhi'ning 1 Wang Yin died before the heir had begun mourning. In the ninth month Xuanzong's border officers reported Goryeo's dispatch: the heir had not begun mourning and could not receive auspicious edicts in mourning dress or sign memorials as one in rough hemp. The ritualists ruled that private grief must not override public duty: he should temporarily wear auspicious dress, receive the edict, and sign as regent. After Goryeo's mourning envoys arrive, the court may send sacrifice, condolence, and investiture missions." Approved.
28
明年，宣宗遷汴，遼東道路不通，興定三年，遼東行省奏高麗複有奉表朝貢之意，宰臣奏：「可令行省受其表章，其朝貢之禮俟他日徐議。 」宣宗以為然，乃遣使撫諭高麗，終以道路不通，未遑迎迓，詔行省且羈縻勿絕其好，然自是不復通問矣。
The next year Xuanzong moved to Bian and the eastern route was cut. In Xingding 3 Eastern Liao reported Goryeo wished to resume tribute; ministers said the branch could accept memorials but defer full ceremony. Xuanzong agreed and sent comforting envoys, but the roads remained blocked; the branch was told to keep ties without cutting them off—yet contact effectively ended.
29
贊曰：金人本出鞨靺之附於高麗者，始通好為鄰國，既而為君臣，貞祐以後道路不通，僅一再見而已。 入聖朝猶子孫相傳自為治，故不復備論，論其與金事相涉者焉。
Commentary: The Jin arose from Mohe once subject to Goryeo; they began as neighbors, became lord and vassal, and after Zhenyou were barely seen again. Under the present dynasty they still rule in hereditary succession and need no full account here; only affairs touching Jin are treated.

金國語解
Explanation of the Jin State Language
31
今文《尚書》辭多奇澀，蓋亦當世之方言也。 《金史》所載本國之語，得諸重譯，而可解者何可闕焉。 若其臣僚之小字，或以賤，或以疾，猶有古人尚質之風，不可文也。 國姓為某，漢姓為某，後魏孝文以來已有之矣。 存諸篇終，以備考索。
The received Book of Documents is often obscure because much of it too is the dialect of its age. The native terms in the History of Jin, passed through repeated translation, include many we can still parse and must not omit. Officials' pet names, marking low birth or illness, preserve an ancient plain style that should not be polished away. National clan names and Han surnames have coexisted since Emperor Xiaowen of Later Wei. They are kept at chapters' ends for reference.
