1
世宗昭德皇后生顯宗、趙王孰輦、越王斜魯。 元妃張氏生鄗王允中、越王允功。 元妃李氏生鄭王允蹈、衛紹王允濟、潞王允德。 昭儀梁氏生豫王允成。 才人石抹氏生夔王允升。 孰輦、斜魯皆早卒。
Empress Zhaode, consort of Emperor Shizong, gave birth to Xianzong, Prince Shunian of Zhao, and Prince Xielu of Yue. Primary Consort Zhang bore Prince Yunzhong of Hao and Prince Yun Gong of Yue. Primary Consort Li bore Prince Yundao of Zheng, Prince Yunji of Wei Shao, and Prince Yunde of Lu. Worthy Lady Liang bore Prince Yuncheng of Yu. Talented Lady Shimota bore Prince Yunsheng of Kui. Shunian and Xielu both died young.
2
二十一年，改判大宗正事。 永中不悅，顯宗勸之曰：「宗正之職，自親及疏，自近及遠，此親賢之任也。 且皇子之貴，豈以官職閑劇為計邪？ 」永中乃喜。 二十四年，世宗幸上京，顯宗居守，並留永中。 顯宗先遣章宗、宣宗奉表問起居於上京，既而遣永中子光祿大夫石古乃奉表。 世宗喜謂豫國公主曰：「皇太子孝德天成，先遣二子，繼遣此子，兄弟之際相友愛如此也。」
In the twenty-first year, he was reassigned to serve as Director of the Imperial Clan Court. Yong Zhong was displeased, but Xianzong persuaded him, saying, "The Imperial Clan Director's duty runs from the closest kin to the most distant, from those near at hand to those far away — it is a trust to honor the worthy among one's relatives." Besides, is a prince's standing to be reckoned by how busy or idle his post may be? At that Yong Zhong was satisfied. In the twenty-fourth year, Emperor Shizong traveled to the Upper Capital. Xianzong stayed behind to guard the capital, and Yong Zhong was kept there as well. Xianzong first dispatched Zhangzong and Xuanzong to the Upper Capital with memorials inquiring after the emperor's health; he then sent Yong Zhong's son Shigu Nai, Grand Master of Brilliant Happiness, with another memorial. Emperor Shizong said with pleasure to the Princess of Yüguo, "The crown prince's filial devotion is innate — first he sent his two sons, then this son. Such brotherly affection as this!"
3
明昌二年正月辛酉，孝懿皇后崩。 判真定府事吳王永成、判定武軍節度使隋王永升奔喪後期，各罰俸一月，杖其長史五十。 永中適有寒疾，不能至。 上怒，頗意諸王有輕慢心，遣使責永中曰：「已近公除，亦不須來。 」二月丙戌，禫祭，永中始至，入臨。 辛卯，始克行燒飯禮。 壬辰，永中及諸王朝辭，賜遺留物，禮遇雖在，而嫌忌自此始矣。
In the first month of the second year of Mingchang, on the day xinyou, Empress Xiaoyi died. Prince Wu Yongcheng, administering Zhending Prefecture, and Prince Sui Yongsheng, military commissioner of Dingwu Army, arrived late for the mourning observances. Each was fined one month's salary, and their chief administrators were flogged fifty strokes. Yong Zhong happened to be ill with a chill and could not come. The emperor was angry and suspected the princes of treating the matter lightly. He sent a messenger to rebuke Yong Zhong: "The public mourning is nearly ended — you need not come at all." On the day bingxu in the second month, at the end-of-mourning rites, Yong Zhong finally arrived and entered to mourn. On the day xinmao, he was at last able to perform the burnt-offering rite. On the day renchen, Yong Zhong and the other princes took leave at court and were granted items from the late empress's estate. The formal courtesies remained, but suspicion and resentment began from this point.
4
四月，進封並王。 三年，判平陽府事，進封鎬王。 初置王傅、府尉官，名為官屬，實檢制之也。 府尉希望風旨，過為苛細。 永中自以世宗長子，且老矣，動有掣制，情思不堪，殊鬱鬱，乃表乞閒居。 詔不許。 四年，鄭王永蹈以謀逆誅。 增置諸王司馬一員，檢察門戶出入，球獵遊宴皆有制限，家人出入皆有禁防。 河東提刑判官把里海坐私謁永中，杖一百，解職。 前近侍局副使裴滿可孫嘗受永中請托，為石古乃求除官，可孫已改同知西京留守，猶坐免。 故尚書右丞張汝弼，永中母舅也。 汝弼妻高陀斡自大定間畫永中母像，奉之甚謹，挾左道為永中求福，希覬非望。 明昌五年，高陀斡坐詛祝誅。 上疑事在永中，未有以發也。
In the fourth month, he was promoted to Prince of Bing. In the third year, he was assigned to administer Pingyang Prefecture and promoted to Prince of Hao. Tutors and prefectural guard officers were established for the first time. In name they were staff, but in fact they were there to supervise and restrain the princes. The prefectural guard officers tried to read the court's wishes and went to excessive lengths in their strictness. Yong Zhong regarded himself as Emperor Shizong's eldest son and, now old, found himself constantly checked at every turn. The strain was more than he could bear, and deeply depressed, he submitted a memorial asking to live in retirement. An edict denied his request. In the fourth year, Prince Zheng Yong Dao was executed for plotting rebellion. An additional intendant was assigned to each prince to inspect comings and goings at the gate. Ball games, hunts, and banquets were all restricted, and the movements of household members were forbidden and guarded. Bali Hai, vice commissioner of the Hedong circuit office of justice, was punished for paying a private visit to Yong Zhong: he was flogged one hundred strokes and removed from office. Peiman Kesun, former deputy commissioner of the Inner Attendants' Bureau, had once accepted a request from Yong Zhong and sought an appointment for Shigu Nai. Although Kesun had already been transferred to vice commissioner of the Western Capital military commission, he was still dismissed on this account. The late Right Vice Director of the Ministry of Personnel Zhang Rubi was Yong Zhong's maternal uncle. Rubi's wife Gaotuohe had painted a portrait of Yong Zhong's mother since the Dading era and venerated it with great devotion. Using heterodox practices, she sought blessings for Yong Zhong and harbored ambitions beyond what was proper. In the fifth year of Mingchang, Gaotuohe was executed for casting curses and prayers. The emperor suspected the affair touched Yong Zhong, but had no grounds on which to proceed.
5
會鎬王傅尉奏永中第四子阿離合懣因防禁嚴密，語涉不道。 詔同簽大睦親府事袴、禦史中丞孫即康鞫問，並求得第二子神徒門所撰詞曲有不遜語。 家奴德哥首永中嘗與侍妾瑞雪言：「我得天下，子為大王，以爾為妃。 」詔遣官複按狀同。 再遣禮部尚書張暐、兵部侍郎烏古論慶裔複之。 上謂宰臣曰：「鎬王只以語言得罪，與永蹈罪異。 」參知政事馬琪曰：「永中與永蹈罪狀雖異，人臣無將，則一也。 」上曰：「大王何故輒出此言？ 」左丞相清臣曰：「素有妄想之心也。 」詔以永中罪狀宣示百官雜議，五品以下附奏，四品以上入對便殿。 皆曰：「請論如律。 」惟宮籍監丞盧利用乞貸其死。 詔賜永中死，神徒門、阿離合懣等皆棄市。 敕有司用國公禮收葬永中，平陽府監護，官給葬具，妻子威州安置。 泰和七年，詔複永中王爵，賜諡曰厲。 敕石古乃於威州擇地，以禮改葬，歲時祭奠。 貞祐二年，詔徙永中妻、子石古乃等鄭州安置。
It happened that the tutor and guard officers of Prince Hao reported that Yong Zhong's fourth son Aliheman, chafing under the strict restrictions, had spoken words that touched on treason. An edict ordered Ku, co-signer of the Great Kinship Office, and Censor-in-Chief Sun Jikang to investigate. They also obtained song lyrics composed by the second son Shentumen containing disrespectful language. A household slave named Dego reported that Yong Zhong had once said to his concubine Ruixue, "If I gain the realm, my son shall be a great prince and you shall be his consort." An edict dispatched officials to reinvestigate, and the facts matched. Minister of Rites Zhang Wei and Vice Director of the Ministry of War Wugulun Qingyi were again sent to reinvestigate. The emperor said to his ministers, "Prince Hao has offended only through words; his crime is not the same as Yong Dao's." Vice Director Ma Qi said, "Although Yong Zhong's and Yong Dao's crimes differ in form, the principle that no subject may harbor rebellious intent is the same." The emperor said, "Great Prince, why do you speak so rashly?" Left Director Jia Gu Qingchen said, "He has long harbored delusions of grandeur." An edict ordered Yong Zhong's crimes displayed for deliberation by all officials. Those of fifth rank and below submitted memorials; those of fourth rank and above entered the Side Hall to speak in person. All said, "We request judgment according to law." Only Lu Liyong, supervisor of the palace register, pleaded that his life be spared. An edict granted Yong Zhong death by suicide. Shentumen, Aliheman, and others were all executed in the marketplace. An order commanded the authorities to bury Yong Zhong with the rites due a Duke of the State. Pingyang Prefecture was to supervise, official funeral goods were provided, and his wife and children were resettled at Weizhou. In the seventh year of Taihe, an edict restored Yong Zhong's princely title and granted the posthumous name Li. Shigu Nai was ordered to choose a site at Weizhou and rebury his father according to proper rites, with seasonal sacrifices. In the second year of Zhenyou, an edict moved Yong Zhong's wife, his son Shigu Nai, and the others to resettlement at Zhengzhou.
6
貞祐三年，太康縣人劉全嘗為盜，亡入衛真界，詭稱愛王。 所謂愛王，指石古乃。 石古乃實未嘗有王封，小人妄以此目之。 劉全欲為亂，因假託以惑眾，誘王氏女為妻，且言其子方聚兵河北。 東平人李甯居嵩山，有妖術。 全同縣人時溫稱寧可論大事，乃使範元書偽號召之。 甯至，推為國師，議僭立。 事覺，全、溫、寧皆伏誅。
In the third year of Zhenyou, Liu Quan of Taikang County, who had once been a bandit, fled into Weizhen district and falsely claimed to be the Beloved Prince. The so-called Beloved Prince referred to Shigu Nai. Shigu Nai had in fact never held a princely title; common people had recklessly applied this label to him. Liu Quan wished to raise rebellion and used this false pretense to mislead the people. He enticed a Wang clan woman as his wife and claimed his son was gathering troops in Hebei. Li Ning of Dongping lived on Mount Song and possessed occult arts. Liu Quan's fellow townsman Shi Wen said Ning could discuss great affairs, and so had Fan Yuan write a forged summons under a false title to summon him. When Ning arrived, they acclaimed him as state preceptor and deliberated on usurping the throne. When the plot was discovered, Liu Quan, Shi Wen, and Ning were all executed.
7
貞祐四年，潼關破，徙永中子孫于南京。 興定二年，亳州譙縣人孫學究私造妖言雲：「愛王終當奮發，今匿跡民間，自號劉二。 」衛真百姓王深等皆信以為誠然。 有劉二者出而當之，遣歐榮輩結構逆黨，市兵仗，大署旌旗，謀忄栗立。 事覺，誅死者五十二人，緣坐者六十餘人。 永中子孫禁錮，自明昌至於正大末，幾四十年。 天興初，詔弛禁錮。 未幾，南京亦不守雲。
In the fourth year of Zhenyou, when Tong Pass fell, Yong Zhong's descendants were moved to the Southern Capital. In the second year of Xingding, Sun Xuejiu of Qiao County in Bozhou privately fabricated occult prophecies saying, "The Beloved Prince will ultimately rise up; he now hides among the people and calls himself Liu Er." The common people of Weizhen, Wang Shen and others, all believed this to be true. Someone claiming to be Liu Er stepped forward to play the role. He sent Ou Rong and others to organize a rebel faction, purchased weapons, raised banners on a grand scale, and plotted usurpation. When the plot was discovered, fifty-two were executed and more than sixty were implicated by association. Yong Zhong's descendants were confined from Mingchang nearly until the end of the Zhengda era — almost forty years. At the start of Tianxing, an edict relaxed the confinement. Before long, the Southern Capital too was lost.
8
初，崔溫、郭諫、馬太初與永蹈家奴畢慶壽私說讖記災祥，畢慶壽以告永蹈：「郭諫頗能相人。 」永蹈乃召郭諫相已及妻子。 諫說永蹈曰：「大王相貌非常，王妃及二子皆大貴。 」又曰：「大王，元妃長子，不與諸王比也。 」永蹈召崔溫、馬太初論讖記天象。 崔溫曰：「醜年有兵災，屬兔命者來年春當收兵得位。 」郭諫曰：「昨見赤氣犯紫微，白虹貫月，皆注醜後寅前兵戈忄栗亂事。 」永蹈深信其說，乃陰結內侍鄭雨兒伺上起居，以崔溫為謀主，郭諫、馬太初往來遊說。 河南統軍使僕散揆尚永蹈妹韓國公主，永蹈謀取河南軍以為助，與妹澤國公主長樂謀，使駙馬都尉蒲剌睹致書於揆，且先請婚，以觀其意。 揆拒不許結婚，使者不敢複言不軌事。 永蹈家奴董壽諫永蹈，不聽。 董壽以語同輩奴千家奴，上變。 是時，永蹈在京師，詔平章政事完顏守貞、參知政事胥持國、戶部尚書楊伯通、知大興府事尼龐古鑒鞫問，連引甚眾，久不能決。 上怒，召守貞等問狀。 右丞相夾谷清臣奏曰：「事貴速絕，以安人心。 」於是，賜永蹈及妃卞玉，二子按春、阿辛，公主長樂自盡。 蒲剌睹、崔溫、郭諫、馬太初等皆伏誅。 僕散揆雖不聞問，猶坐除名。 董壽免死，隸監籍。 千家奴賞錢二千貫，特遷五官雜班敘使。 自是諸王制限防禁密矣。
At first, Cui Wen, Guo Jian, and Ma Taichu privately discussed prognostic texts and omens of disaster and good fortune with Yong Dao's household slave Bi Qingshou. Bi Qingshou told Yong Dao, "Guo Jian is quite skilled at reading faces." Yong Dao then summoned Guo Jian to read the faces of himself, his wife, and his children. Jian said to Yong Dao, "Your Highness's appearance is extraordinary; the princess consort and your two sons are all destined for great eminence." He also said, "Your Highness, as eldest son of the primary consort, cannot be compared with the other princes." Yong Dao summoned Cui Wen and Ma Taichu to discuss prognostic texts and celestial signs. Cui Wen said, "In the year of the ox there will be military disaster; one born in the year of the rabbit will gather troops and gain the throne the following spring." Guo Jian said, "Yesterday I saw red vapor encroach on the Purple Forbidden Enclosure and a white rainbow pierce the moon — both portend military upheaval and usurpation between the ox and tiger." Yong Dao deeply believed these words. He secretly enlisted the inner attendant Zheng Yu'er to watch the emperor's movements, made Cui Wen his chief plotter, and had Guo Jian and Ma Taichu go back and forth to win others over. Pusan Kui, commander-in-chief of Henan, was married to Yong Dao's younger sister, the Princess of Hanguo. Yong Dao plotted to win the Henan army as support and conspired with his younger sister, the Princess of Zeguo Chang Le, to have the commandant of cavalry Pula Du write to Kui and first request a marriage alliance to test his intentions. Kui refused to permit the marriage alliance, and the messenger did not dare again speak of disloyal matters. Yong Dao's household slave Dong Shou remonstrated with Yong Dao, but he would not listen. Dong Shou told his fellow slave Qianjianu, who reported the plot to the throne. At this time Yong Dao was in the capital. An edict ordered Grand Councilor Wanyan Shouzhen, Vice Director Xu Chiguo, Minister of Revenue Yang Botong, and Prefect of Daxing Ni Pangu Jian to investigate. Many were implicated in succession, and for a long time no decision could be reached. The emperor was angry and summoned Shouzhen and the others to question them on the case. Right Director Jia Gu Qingchen memorialized, "The matter should be settled quickly to reassure the people's hearts." Thereupon Yong Dao and his consort Bian Yu, his two sons An Chun and A Xin, and the Princess Chang Le were granted death by suicide. Pula Du, Cui Wen, Guo Jian, Ma Taichu, and others were all executed. Although Pusan Kui was not questioned, he was still punished by removal from office. Dong Shou was spared death and enrolled in the supervised register. Qianjianu was rewarded with two thousand strings of cash and specially promoted to clerk of the Five Offices Miscellaneous Class. From this point restrictions and guards on the princes became strict.
9
泰和七年，詔複王封，備禮改葬，賜諡曰剌，以衛王永濟子按辰為永蹈後，奉其祭祀。
In the seventh year of Taihe, an edict restored his princely title, provided full rites for reburial, and granted the posthumous name La. Prince Wei Yongji's son An Chen was made Yong Dao's heir to perform his sacrifices.
10
越王永功，本名宋葛，又名廣孫，貞元二年生。 沉默寡言笑，勇健絕人，涉書史，好法書名畫。 大定四年，封鄭王。 七年，進封隋王。 十一年，進封曹王。 十五年，除刑部尚書。 上曰：「侍郎張汝霖，汝外舅行也，可學為政。 」十七年，授活活土世襲猛安。 十八年，改大興尹。
Prince Yue Yong Gong, original name Songge, also called Guangsun, was born in the second year of Zhenyuan. Silent and sparing of speech and laughter, surpassingly brave and strong, versed in books and history, and fond of calligraphy and famous paintings. In the fourth year of Dading, he was enfeoffed as Prince of Zheng. In the seventh year, he was promoted to Prince of Sui. In the eleventh year, he was promoted to Prince of Cao. In the fifteenth year, he was appointed Minister of Justice. The emperor said, "Vice Director Zhang Rulin is your maternal uncle by marriage — you may learn governance from him." In the seventeenth year, he was granted the hereditary meng'an of Huohuotu. In the eighteenth year, he was appointed Prefect of Daxing.
11
世宗幸金蓮川，始出中都，親軍二蒼頭縱馬食民田，詔永功：「蒼頭各杖一百。 彈壓百戶二人失覺察，勒停。 」上次望京澱，永功奏曰：「親軍人止一蒼頭、兩彈壓服勤，為日久矣。 臣昧死違詔，量決蒼頭，使彈壓待罪，可使償其田直，惟陛下憐察。 」上皆從之。
When Emperor Shizong visited Jinlianchuan and first left the Central Capital, two personal-guard servants let their horses graze on common people's fields. An edict to Yong Gong ordered, "Each servant shall be flogged one hundred strokes." The two centurions of the suppression force who failed to detect this were ordered to cease service. On the emperor's last visit to Jingjingdian, Yong Gong memorialized, "Each personal guard has only one servant and two centurions on duty, and they have been at it for a long time." I disobey your edict at the risk of death. I have punished the servant leniently and made the centurions await judgment. They can compensate the value of the fields. I beg Your Majesty to show compassion and understand. The emperor approved all of it.
12
老嫗與男婦憩道傍，婦與所私相從亡去，或告嫗曰：「向見年少婦人自水邊小徑去矣。 」嫗告伍長蹤跡之。 有男子私殺牛，手持血刃，望見伍長，意其捕己，即走避之。 嫗與伍長疑是殺其婦也，捕送縣，不勝楚毒，遂誣服。 問屍安在？ 詭曰：「棄之水中矣。 」求之水中，果獲一屍，已半腐。 縣吏以為是男子真殺若婦矣，即具獄上。 永功疑之曰：「婦死幾何日，而屍遽半腐哉。 」頃之，嫗得其婦於所私者。 永功曰：「是男子偶以殺人就獄，其拷掠足以稱殺牛之科矣。 」遂釋之而去。 武清黃氏、望雲王氏豪猾不逞，永功發其罪，畿內肅然。
An old woman and her daughter-in-law rested by the roadside. The daughter-in-law ran off with her lover. Someone told the old woman, "I just saw a young woman leave by the small path beside the water." The old woman told the squad leader to follow her trail. A man who had secretly slaughtered an ox, holding a bloody knife, saw the squad leader coming and, thinking he had come to arrest him, fled. The old woman and the squad leader suspected he had killed the daughter-in-law. They arrested him and sent him to the county office. Unable to endure torture, he falsely confessed. They asked where the body was. He falsely said, "I threw it into the water." They searched the water and indeed found a corpse, already half decomposed. The county officials concluded the man had truly killed the woman and immediately prepared the case for submission. Yong Gong had doubts and said, "How many days has the woman been dead, that the corpse is already half decomposed?" Before long, the old woman found her daughter-in-law with the lover. Yong Gong said, "This man happened to land in prison on a murder charge. The beating he has already suffered is punishment enough for slaughtering an ox." He then released him and let him go. The powerful and unruly Huang clan of Wuqing and Wang clan of Wangyun — Yong Gong exposed their crimes, and order was restored throughout the capital region.
13
二十三年，判東京留守。 是月，改河間尹。 閱月，改北京留守。 居無何，上謂宰臣曰：「朕聞永功到北京為政無良，雖朕子，萬一敗露，法可廢乎。 朕已戒敕永功，卿等可諭其長史，俾匡正之。 」到北京凡七月，改東京留守。 世宗幸上京，過東京，永功從。 明年，上還至天平山好水川，皇太子薨。 詔永功護喪事，尋拜御史大夫。 章宗封原王，加開府儀同三司。 趙王永中及永功兄弟皆加開府儀同三司。 明年，判大宗正事。
In the twenty-third year, he was appointed military commissioner of the Eastern Capital. That same month, he was transferred to Prefect of Hejian. After a month, he was transferred to military commissioner of the Northern Capital. Before long, the emperor said to his ministers, "I hear that Yong Gong's governance at the Northern Capital has been poor. Though he is my son, if misconduct comes to light, can the law be set aside?" I have already admonished Yong Gong. You should instruct his chief administrator to set him straight. After seven months at the Northern Capital, he was transferred to military commissioner of the Eastern Capital. Emperor Shizong traveled to the Upper Capital and passed through the Eastern Capital; Yong Gong accompanied him. The following year, as the emperor returned to Tianping Mountain at Haoshuichuan, the crown prince died. An edict ordered Yong Gong to oversee the funeral rites, and soon afterward he was appointed censor-in-chief. Zhangzong was enfeoffed as Prince of Yuan and granted the rank of Grand Master with the Golden Seal and Purple Ribbon. Prince Zhao Yong Zhong and Yong Gong's brothers were all granted the rank of Grand Master with the Golden Seal and Purple Ribbon. The following year, he was appointed director of the Imperial Clan Court.
14
應州僧與永功有舊，將訴事于彰國軍節度使移剌胡剌，求永功手書與胡剌為地。 胡剌得書，奏之。 上謂宰臣曰：「永功以書囑事胡剌，此雖細微，不可不懲也。 凡人小過不治，遂至大咎。 有犯必懲，庶幾能改，是亦教也。 」皆曰：「陛下用法無私，臣下敢不敬畏。 」於是永功解職。 未幾，複判大宗正事。
A monk from Yingzhou who had old ties with Yong Gong was about to lodge a complaint with Yelü Hula, military commissioner of Zhangguo Army, and asked Yong Gong for a handwritten letter to Hula to pave the way. Hula received the letter and reported it to the throne. The emperor said to his ministers, "Yong Gong used a letter to ask Hula to handle a matter. Though this is a small thing, it cannot go unpunished." If small faults are not corrected, they lead to great calamity. When there is an offense, punishment must follow — then perhaps one can reform. This too is a form of instruction. All said, "Your Majesty applies the law without favoritism — how dare your subjects not revere and fear it?" Thereupon Yong Gong was removed from office. Before long, he was again appointed director of the Imperial Clan Court.
15
宣宗即位，免常參。 明年，從遷汴京。 久之，詔永功每月朔一朝。 興定四年，詔永功無朝。 五年，有疾，賜禦藥。 疾革，賜尚醫診視，一日五遣使候問。 是歲，薨。 上哭之慟，諡曰忠簡。
When Xuanzong ascended the throne, Yong Gong was exempted from regular court attendance. The following year, he followed the court in moving to Bianjing. After some time, an edict ordered Yong Gong to attend court once on the first day of each month. In the fourth year of Xingding, an edict exempted Yong Gong from court attendance altogether. In the fifth year, he fell ill and was granted imperial medicine. When his illness became critical, imperial physicians were sent to examine him, and messengers were dispatched five times a day to inquire after him. That year, he died. The emperor wept bitterly for him and granted the posthumous name Zhongjian.
16
子福孫、壽孫、粘沒曷。 大定二十六年，詔賜福孫名璐，壽孫名璹，粘沒曷名琳。 是年，璐加奉國上將軍。 章宗即位，加銀青榮祿大夫，封蕭國公。 初為興陵崇妃養子，常居京師，奉朝請。 泰和五年，卒。 章宗輟朝，百官進名奉慰。
His sons were Fusun, Shousun, and Nianmohe. In the twenty-sixth year of Dading, an edict granted Fusun the name Lu, Shousun the name Shu, and Nianmohe the name Lin. That year, Lu was promoted to General-in-Chief Who Supports the State. When Zhangzong ascended the throne, Lu was granted the rank of Grand Master of Splendid Happiness with the Silver Seal and Blue Ribbon and enfeoffed as Duke of Xiao. He had originally been the adopted son of the Worthy Consort of Xingling and regularly resided in the capital, attending court. In the fifth year of Taihe, he died. Zhangzong suspended court, and all officials submitted their names to offer condolences.
17
子璹
Son: Shu
18
璹本名壽孫，世宗賜名，字仲實，一字子瑜。 資質簡重，博學有俊才，喜為詩，工真草書。 大定二十七年，加奉國上將軍。 明昌初，加銀青榮祿大夫。 衛紹王時，加開府儀同三司。 貞佑中，封胙國公。 正大初，進封密國公。
Shu's original name was Shousun. Emperor Shizong granted him the name Shu. His style was Zhongshi, and he also used the style Ziyu. Simple and dignified in character, broadly learned and possessed of outstanding talent, he delighted in composing poetry and was skilled in regular and cursive script. In the twenty-seventh year of Dading, he was promoted to General-in-Chief Who Supports the State. At the start of Mingchang, he was granted the rank of Grand Master of Splendid Happiness with the Silver Seal and Blue Ribbon. During the reign of Emperor Weishao, he was granted the rank of Grand Master with the Golden Seal and Purple Ribbon. During Zhenyou, he was enfeoffed as Duke of Zuo. At the start of Zhengda, he was promoted to Duke of Mi.
19
璹奉朝請四十年，日以講誦吟詠為事，時時潛與士大夫唱酬，然不敢明白往來。 永功薨後，稍得出遊，與文士趙秉文、楊雲翼、雷淵、元好問、李汾、王飛伯輩交善。 初，宣宗南遷，諸王宗室顛沛奔走，璹乃盡載其家法書名畫，一帙不遺。 居汴中，家人口多，俸入少，客至，貧不能具酒肴，蔬飯共食，焚香煮茗，盡出藏書，談大定、明昌以來故事，終日不聽客去，樂而不厭也。
Shu attended court for forty years, spending his days in study, recitation, and poetic composition. From time to time he secretly exchanged poems with scholar-officials, but did not dare to associate openly. After Yong Gong's death, he was able to go out somewhat more freely and formed friendships with literary men Zhao Bingwen, Yang Yunyi, Lei Yuan, Yuan Haowen, Li Fen, Wang Feibo, and others. When Xuanzong moved south, the princes and imperial clan members were scattered in flight. Shu loaded all his family's calligraphy and famous paintings and did not leave a single scroll behind. Living in Bian, with many mouths in the household and little salary income, when guests came he was too poor to provide wine and dishes. They shared plain vegetable meals, burned incense and brewed tea, brought out all his stored books, and talked of events since Dading and Mingchang — all day he would not let guests leave, delighting in it without tiring.
20
平生詩文甚多。 自刪其詩，存三百首，樂府一百首，號《如庵小稿》。 第五子守禧，字慶之，風神秀徹，璹特鍾愛，嘗曰：「平日所蓄書畫將以付斯子。 」及汴城降，守禧病卒，年未三十。
In his lifetime he wrote a great deal of poetry and prose. He edited his own poems, keeping three hundred poems and one hundred yuefu pieces, which he titled Collected Drafts from the Ru'an Studio. His fifth son Shouxi, style Qingzhi, was graceful and bright in bearing. Shu especially cherished him and once said, "The books and paintings I have collected over the years I shall give to this son." When Bian fell, Shouxi died of illness, not yet thirty years old.
21
豫王永成，本名鶴野，又曰婁室。 母昭儀梁氏。 永成風姿奇偉，博學，善屬文。 世宗尤愛重之。 大定七年，始封沈王，以太學博士王彥潛為府文學，永成師事之。 十一年，進封豳。 十五年，就外第。 十六年，判秘書監。 明年，授世襲山東東路把魯古猛安，判大睦親府事。 既而改中都路胡土靄哥蠻猛安。 二十年，改授翰林學士承旨。 二十三年，判定武軍節度使事，尋改判廣寧府。 二十五年，世宗幸上京，命留守中都，判吏部尚書，進開府儀同三司，為御史大夫。
Prince Yu Yong Cheng, original name Heye, also called Loushi. His mother was Worthy Lady Liang. Yong Cheng had an extraordinary and imposing bearing, was broadly learned, and was skilled at literary composition. Emperor Shizong especially loved and valued him. In the seventh year of Dading, he was first enfeoffed as Prince of Shen. Wang Yanqian, Erudite of the Imperial Academy, was made literary instructor of the princely establishment, and Yong Cheng studied under him. In the eleventh year, he was promoted to Prince of Bin. In the fifteenth year, he took up residence outside the palace. In the sixteenth year, he was appointed director of the Palace Library. The following year, he was granted the hereditary meng'an of Balugu on the Eastern Shandong Route and appointed director of the Great Kinship Office. Soon afterward he was transferred to the meng'an of Hutuaigeman on the Central Capital Route. In the twentieth year, he was appointed Academician Expositor-in-Chief of the Hanlin Academy. In the twenty-third year, he was appointed military commissioner of Dingwu Army; soon afterward he was transferred to administer Guangning Prefecture. In the twenty-fifth year, Emperor Shizong traveled to the Upper Capital and ordered Yong Cheng to remain and guard the Central Capital as Minister of Personnel, promoted to Grand Master with the Golden Seal and Purple Ribbon, and appointed censor-in-chief.
22
章宗即位，起複，進封吳，判真定府事。 明昌元年，改山東西路盆買必剌猛安。 明年，進封兗。 坐率軍民圍獵，解職，奉表謝罪。 上賜手詔曰：「卿親實肺腑，夙著忠純，侍顯考于春宮，曲盡友于之愛，洎沖人之繼統，愈明忠赤之心，艱難之中，多所裨益。 朕心簡在，毫楮莫窮，用是起之苫塊之中，授以維城之任。 自典籓服，歲月薦更，蕞爾趙邦，知驥足之難展，眇哉鎮府，固牛刀之莫施。 方思驛召以赴朝，何意遽罹于國憲。 偶因時獵，頗擾部民，法所不寬，憲台聞上。 朕尚含容累月，未忍即行，雖欲遂於私恩，竟莫違于公議，解卿前職，即乃世封。 噫，祖宗立法，非一人之敢私； 骨肉至親，豈千里而能間。 以此退閑之小誡，欲成終始之洪恩。 《經》雲：'在上不驕，高而不危。 '是以知節慎者修身之本，驕矜者敗德之源。 朕每自勵，今以戒卿。 昔東平樂善，能成不朽之名，梁孝奢淫，卒致憂疑之悔。 前人所行，可為龜鑑。 卿兼資文武，多藝多才，履道而行，何施不可。 如能德業日新，無慮牽複之晚。 朕素不工詞翰，臨文草草，直寫所懷，冀不以辭害意也。 」未幾，授沁南軍節度使。 三年，改判咸平府事，未赴，移判太原府事。 上以永成誕日，親為詩以賜，有「美譽自應輝玉牒，忠誠不待啟金滕」之語，當世榮之。
When Zhangzong ascended the throne, Yong Cheng was recalled to service, promoted to Prince of Wu, and appointed to administer Zhending Prefecture. In the first year of Mingchang, he was transferred to the meng'an of Penmaibila on the Western Shandong Route. The following year, he was promoted to Prince of Yan. For leading troops and civilians in a surrounding hunt, he was removed from office and submitted a memorial asking forgiveness. The emperor granted a personal edict: "You are truly of my flesh and blood, long known for loyalty and purity. You served my late father in the Eastern Palace and fully fulfilled the love between brothers. When the young sovereign succeeded to the throne, your loyal and devoted heart became even clearer, and in difficult times you contributed much." My heart holds you in special regard — words cannot exhaust it — and therefore I raised you from your mourning garb and entrusted you with the charge of guarding the realm. Since you governed the fief, years have passed in succession. In that tiny Zhao domain, you found a thoroughbred's legs hard to stretch; in that modest prefectural seat, a butcher's cleaver had no room to be wielded. I was just thinking to summon you by post to court — how could I have expected you would suddenly fall under the penalties of state law? By chance, a seasonal hunt greatly disturbed the people under your jurisdiction. The law allows no leniency, and the Censorate reported it to me. I still showed forbearance for many months and could not bear to act at once. Though I wished to follow private affection, I could not ultimately defy public opinion. I relieve you of your former post and restore your hereditary enfeoffment. Alas — the laws established by our ancestors are not for any one person to treat as a private matter. Kin of flesh and blood — can a thousand li come between them? With this small admonition of retirement I wish to achieve a great grace that lasts to the end. The Classic says, "When one is above others, one must not be arrogant — then though high, one is not in peril." From this we know that restraint and caution are the root of self-cultivation, and arrogance and conceit are the source of moral ruin. I constantly exhort myself — now I use this to admonish you. In former times, the Prince of Dongping delighted in goodness and achieved an immortal name; Prince Xiao of Liang was extravagant and debauched and in the end brought upon himself regret born of suspicion and anxiety. The conduct of those who came before can serve as a mirror for warning. You possess both civil and military gifts, many skills and many talents. Walk in the Way — what could you not accomplish? If your virtue and achievement renew themselves day by day, there is no need to worry that recall will come too late. I have never been skilled at literary composition. When I write, I do so hastily, setting down directly what is in my heart, hoping the words will not obscure the meaning. Before long, he was appointed military commissioner of Qinnan Army. In the third year, he was transferred to administer Xianping Prefecture. Before he took up the post, he was reassigned to administer Taiyuan Prefecture. On Yong Cheng's birthday, the emperor personally composed a poem to bestow on him, containing the lines "Fine reputation should naturally shine in the imperial genealogical records; loyalty needs no opening of the golden casket to prove it." His contemporaries regarded this as a great honor.
23
七年，改判平陽府事。 承安改元，以覃恩進封豫。 明年冬，進馬八十疋，以資守禦之備。 上賜詔獎諭曰：「卿夙有雋望，時惟茂親，通達古今，砥礪忠義。 方分憂於外服，來輸駿於上閑，欲助邊防，以增武備。 惟盡心于體國，乃因物以見誠。 載念懇勤，良深嘉獎。 」五年，再任。 俄召還，以疾不能入見。 上親幸其第臨視。 泰和四年，薨。 訃聞，上為之震悼，賻贈甚厚，諡曰忠獻。
In the seventh year, he was transferred to administer Pingyang Prefecture. When the era name was changed to Cheng'an, he was promoted to Prince of Yu as part of a general amnesty. The following winter, he presented eighty horses to supply the preparations for frontier defense. The emperor granted an edict of commendation: "You have long enjoyed an outstanding reputation and are at this time a flourishing kinsman. Well versed in past and present, you have honed loyalty and righteousness." While sharing the cares of governance in your outer fief, you have sent fine steeds to the imperial stables, wishing to aid frontier defense and augment military readiness. Only by devoting your heart to serving the state can one show sincerity through such gifts. Reflecting on your earnest diligence, I deeply commend you. In the fifth year, he was appointed to a second term. Soon he was recalled, but because of illness he could not enter to have an audience. The emperor personally visited his residence to look in on him. In the fourth year of Taihe, he died. When word of his death arrived, the emperor was deeply shaken and grieved. The funeral gifts were very generous, and the posthumous name Zhongxian was granted.
24
永成自幼喜讀書，晚年所學益醇，每暇日引文士相與切磋，接之以禮，未嘗見驕色。 自號曰「樂善居士」，有文集行於世雲。
From youth Yong Cheng delighted in reading. In his later years his learning grew ever more refined. On every free day he invited literary men to study together with him, treating them with courtesy and never showing an arrogant bearing. He styled himself the Lay Buddhist "Delighting in Goodness," and a collected works circulated in the world.
25
贊曰：世宗保全宗室，無所不至，雖矯海陵之失，亦由天資仁厚而然也。 其子永中、永蹈皆死章宗之手，其理蓋有不可詰者。 章宗無後，則厥報不爽矣。
Commentary: Emperor Shizong preserved the imperial clan by every means — though this corrected the errors of Hailing, it also arose from his naturally generous and humane disposition. His sons Yong Zhong and Yong Dao both died at Zhangzong's hands — the principle behind this perhaps has aspects that cannot be fully questioned. When Zhangzong left no heir, retribution was not long in coming.
