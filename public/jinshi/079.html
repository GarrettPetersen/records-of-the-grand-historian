列傳第十七○酈瓊李成孔彥舟徐文施宜生張中孚張中彥宇文虛中王倫
Biographies 17: Li Qiong, Li Cheng, Kong Yanzhou, Xu Wen, Shi Yisheng, Zhang Zhongfu, Zhang Zhongyan, Yu Wenxuzhong, and Wang Lun
2
酈瓊，字國寶，相州臨漳人。 補州學生。 宋宣和間，盜賊起，瓊乃更學擊刺挽強，試弓馬，隸宗澤軍，駐於磁州。 未幾告歸，括集義軍七百人，複從澤，澤署瓊為七百人長。 澤死，調戍滑州。 時宗望伐宋，將渡河。 戍軍亂，殺其統制趙世彥，而推瓊為主。 瓊因誘眾，號為勤王，行且收兵。 比渡淮，有眾萬餘。 康王以為楚州安撫使、淮南東路兵馬鈐轄，累遷武泰軍承宣使。 未幾，率所領步騎十余萬附于齊，授靜難軍節度使，知拱州。 齊國廢，以為博州防禦使。 用廉，遷驃騎上將軍。 宗弼複河南，以瓊為山東路弩手千戶，知亳州事。 丁母憂，去官。
Li Qiong, courtesy name Guobao, was a native of Linzhang in Xiangzhou. He entered service as a prefectural student. In the Xuanhe period of Song, when banditry broke out, Qiong turned instead to training in close combat and the strong bow, passed examinations in archery and horsemanship, and joined Zong Ze's army, which was stationed at Cizhou. Before long he took leave to go home, mustered seven hundred men from the local militias, rejoined Ze, and Ze appointed him commander of those seven hundred. After Ze's death, he was reassigned to garrison Huazhou. At that time Zongwang was campaigning against Song and was preparing to cross the Yellow River. The garrison mutinied, killed their commander Zhao Shiyan, and raised Qiong as their chief. Qiong then misled the troops, proclaiming that he was marching to save the dynasty, and advanced while gathering more forces. By the time he crossed the Huai, his following had grown to more than ten thousand. Prince Kang made him Pacification Commissioner of Chuzhou and Military Intendant of the Eastern Circuit of Huainan, and he rose in time to Military Commissioner of the Wutai Army. Before long he led the more than a hundred thousand infantry and cavalry under his command to submit to Qi, and was appointed Military Governor of the Jingnan Army with concurrent charge of Gongzhou. After Qi was abolished, he was made Defender of Bozhou. For integrity in office he was promoted to General of Rapid Cavalry on the Left. When Zongbi recovered Henan, Qiong was appointed commander of a thousand crossbowmen on the Shandong Circuit and put in charge of Bozhou. He resigned office to observe mourning for his mother.
3
宗弼再伐江南，以瓊素知南方山川險易，召至軍與計事。 從容語同列曰：「瓊嘗從大軍南伐，每見元帥國王親臨陣督戰，矢石交集，而王免胄，指麾三軍，意氣自若，用兵制勝，皆與孫、吳合，可謂命世雄材矣。 至於親冒鋒鏑，進不避難，將士視之，孰敢愛死乎。 宜其所向無前，日辟國千里也。 江南諸帥，才能不及中人。 每當出兵，必身居數百裏外，謂之持重。 或督召軍旅，易置將校，僅以一介之士持虛文諭之，謂之調發。 制敵決勝委之偏裨，是以智者解體，愚者喪師。 幸一小捷，則露布飛馳，增加俘級以為己功，斂怨將士。 縱或親臨，亦必先遁。 而又國政不綱，才有微功，已加厚賞，或有大罪，乃置而不誅。 不即覆亡，已為天幸，何能振起耶？ 」眾以為確論。 元帥，謂宗弼也。
When Zongbi campaigned against Jiangnan again, he summoned Qiong to headquarters because Qiong already knew the southern landscape well, and consulted him on strategy. Speaking calmly to those around him, he said: "When I campaigned south with the main army, I often saw our prince-commander at the front personally directing the fight. Arrows and stones fell thick as rain, yet he would take off his helmet and command the three armies with perfect composure. The way he won battles matched Sunzi and Wuzi — he is truly a once-in-an-age champion." When he himself faced the clash of blades and advanced without flinching, what soldier watching him would dare hold back his life? No wonder nothing could stand in his way, and the realm expanded by a thousand li a day. The commanders of Jiangnan are not even of middling talent. Whenever they took the field, they kept themselves several hundred li from the front and called it prudent restraint. When they needed troops, they would shuffle commanders at will and send a lone messenger with empty formal orders — that was what they called mobilization. They left the defeat of the enemy and the winning of battles to junior officers, so the able lost heart and the foolish lost their armies. If they chanced on a minor victory, victory bulletins flew at once and they inflated the tally of prisoners and heads to claim credit, earning the resentment of their troops. Even when they did appear in person, they were always the first to flee. Moreover, the government lacked discipline: the slightest merit brought lavish reward, while grave crimes went unpunished. That they have not already collapsed is sheer fortune; how could they ever recover? " Everyone present agreed that this was sound reasoning. "Marshal" here means Zongbi.
4
及宗弼問瓊以江南成敗，誰敢相拒者。 瓊曰：「江南軍勢怯弱，皆敗亡之餘，又無良帥，何以禦我。 頗聞秦檜當國用事。 檜，老儒，所謂亡國之大夫，兢兢自守，惟顛覆是懼。 吾以大軍臨之，彼之君臣，方且心破膽裂，將哀鳴不暇，蓋傷弓之鳥可以虛弦下也。 」既而江南果稱臣，宗弼喜瓊為知言。
When Zongbi asked Qiong about Jiangnan's prospects, Qiong said that no one would dare stand against them. Qiong said: "Jiangnan's armies are timid and weak, mere remnants of defeat, and they have no capable commanders — how could they resist us? I have heard that Qin Hui now holds power in the state. Hui is an aging scholar-official, one of those ministers of a doomed state who clings anxiously to his position and fears only ruin. When our great army advances, their ruler and ministers will be terrified out of their wits and will scarcely have time to cry out in despair — like a bird once struck by an arrow, they can be brought down by the twang of an empty bowstring. " Before long Jiangnan did indeed submit as a vassal, and Zongbi was pleased that Qiong had spoken so perceptively.
5
李成，字伯友，雄州歸信人。 勇力絕倫，能挽弓三百斤。 宋宣和初，試弓手，挽強異等。 累官淮南招捉使。 成乃聚眾為盜，鈔掠江南，宋遣兵破之，成遂歸齊，累除知開德府，從大軍伐宋。 齊廢，再除安武軍節度使。
Li Cheng, courtesy name Boyou, was a native of Guixin in Xiongzhou. His courage and strength were unmatched; he could draw a bow weighing three hundred jin. Early in the Xuanhe reign of Song, he passed the archer examination with an exceptional rating in drawing the strong bow. He rose to the post of Commissioner for Pursuit and Capture in Huainan. Cheng then gathered followers and turned bandit, raiding across Jiangnan until Song forces defeated him. He thereupon submitted to Qi, was repeatedly appointed prefect of Kaide, and followed the main army in the invasion of Song. After Qi was abolished, he was again appointed Military Governor of the Anwu Army.
6
成在降附諸將中最勇鷙，號令甚嚴，眾莫敢犯。 臨陣身先諸將。 士卒未食不先食，有病者親視之。 不持雨具，雖沾濕自如也。 有告成反者，宗弼察其誣，使成自治，成杖而釋之，其不校如此。 以此士樂為用，所至克捷。
Among the defecting generals, Cheng was the fiercest; his discipline was severe, and no one dared disobey him. In battle he always led from the front. He would not eat until his men had eaten, and he personally tended the sick. He carried no rain gear and was quite unperturbed even when drenched. When someone accused Cheng of plotting rebellion, Zongbi saw that the charge was false and had Cheng handle the matter himself. Cheng had the accuser beaten and then released him — that was how little he troubled himself over slights. For this reason his men were eager to follow him, and wherever he went he won swift victories.
7
宗弼再取河南，宋李興據河南府。 成引軍入孟津。 興率眾薄城，鼓噪請戰，成不應。 日下昃，興士卒倦且饑，成開門急擊，大破之。 興走漢南，成遂取洛陽、嵩、汝等。 河南平，宗弼奏成為河南尹，都管押本路兵馬。 嘗取官羨粟充公費，坐奪兩官，解職。 正隆間，起為真定尹，封郡王，例封濟國公。 卒，年六十九。
When Zongbi campaigned to retake Henan, the Song officer Li Xing held Henan Prefecture. Cheng led his army into Mengjin. Xing brought his force up to the walls, beating drums and clamoring for battle, but Cheng would not engage. As the sun sank in the west, Xing's troops grew weary and hungry. Cheng threw open the gates and attacked at once, inflicting a crushing defeat. Xing fled south of the Han, and Cheng then took Luoyang, Song, Ru, and the surrounding districts. After Henan was pacified, Zongbi recommended Cheng as Intendant of Henan with overall command of the circuit's forces. He once diverted surplus official grain to cover public expenses, for which he was demoted two ranks and dismissed from office. During the Zhenglong era he was recalled as Intendant of Zhending, enfeoffed as a commandery prince, and by precedent given the title Duke of Ji. He died at the age of sixty-nine.
8
孔彥舟
Kong Yanzhou
9
孔彥舟，字巨濟，相州林慮人。 亡賴，不事生產，避罪之汴，占籍軍中。 坐事系獄，說守者解其縛，乘夜逾城遁去。 已而殺人，亡命為盜。 宋靖康初，應募，累官京東西路兵馬鈐轄。 聞大軍將至山東，遂率所部，劫殺居民，燒廬舍，掠財物，渡河南去。 宋人複招之，以為沿江招捉使。 彥舟暴橫，不奉約束，宋人將以兵執之，彥舟走之齊，從劉麟伐宋，為行軍都統，改行營左總管。
Kong Yanzhou, courtesy name Juji, was a native of Linlu in Xiangzhou. A wastrel who would not work for a living, he fled to Bian to escape punishment and had his name entered on the army rolls. Imprisoned on a charge, he persuaded his guards to loosen his bonds and escaped over the wall by night. He soon killed a man and became an outlaw bandit. Early in the Jingkang reign of Song he answered the recruitment call and rose to Military Intendant of the Eastern and Western Circuits of Jingdong. When he heard that the Jin main army was approaching Shandong, he led his troops in looting and killing civilians, burning houses, seizing goods, and crossing south of the Yellow River. The Song court recruited him again and appointed him Commissioner for Pursuit and Capture along the Yangzi. Yanzhou was brutal and insubordinate. When the Song prepared to seize him by force, he fled to Qi, followed Liu Lin in the invasion of Song as Grand Commander of the Field Army, and was later redesignated Left Grand Commander of the Field Camp.
10
齊國廢，累知淄州。 從宗弼取河南，克鄭州，擒其守劉政，破孟邦傑于登封，授鄭州防禦使。 討平太行車轅嶺賊。 從征江南，渡淮，破孫暉兵萬餘人，下安豐、霍丘。 及攻濠州，以彥舟為先鋒，順流薄城，擒其水軍統制邵青，遂克濠州。 師還，累官工、兵部尚書，河南尹，封廣平郡王。 正隆例降金紫光祿大夫，改西京留守。
After Qi was abolished, he served repeatedly as prefect of Zizhou. Serving under Zongbi in the recovery of Henan, he took Zhengzhou, captured its defender Liu Zheng, defeated Meng Bangjie at Dengfeng, and was appointed Defender of Zhengzhou. He suppressed the bandits at Cheyuan Ridge in the Taihang Mountains. In the Jiangnan campaign he crossed the Huai, defeated more than ten thousand troops under Sun Hui, and captured Anfeng and Huoqiu. In the assault on Haozhou he served as vanguard, sailed downstream to the walls, captured the naval commander Shao Qing, and took the city. After the army returned, he rose to Minister of Works, Minister of War, and Intendant of Henan, and was enfeoffed as Prince of Guangping. Under the Zhenglong precedent he was reduced to Grand Master of Splendid Happiness Bearing Golden Regalia and reassigned as Military Governor of the Western Capital.
11
彥舟荒於色，有禽獸行。 妾生女姿麗，彥舟苦虐其母，使自陳非己女，遂納為妾。 其官屬負官錢，私其妻與折券。 惟破濠州時，諸軍凡系獲皆殺之，彥舟號令毋輒殺，免者數千人，人頗以此稱之。 然自幼至老常在行伍，習兵事，知利鈍。 海陵欲以為征南將佐，正隆五年，除南京留守。
Yanzhou was dissolute in lust and behaved like an animal. When a concubine bore a beautiful daughter, Yanzhou brutally mistreated the mother until she declared the child was not his, and then took the girl as his own concubine. When a subordinate owed money to the government, Yanzhou took the man's wife for himself and applied her value against the debt. Only when Haozhou fell, while other armies killed every captive they took, Yanzhou ordered that no one be killed without cause, sparing several thousand lives — for which he won a measure of praise. Yet from youth to old age he had lived in the ranks, knew warfare well, and understood when to press and when to hold back. Hailing intended to use him as an assistant in the southern campaign; in the fifth year of Zhenglong he was appointed Military Governor of the Southern Capital.
12
彥舟有疾，朝臣有傳彥舟死者，而彥舟尚無恙，海陵盡杖妄傳彥舟死者，以激勵之。 無何竟死於汴，年五十五。 遺表言「伐宋當先取淮南」雲。
When Yanzhou fell ill, some at court spread word that he had died, though he was still alive. Hailing had everyone who had falsely reported his death beaten, to spur him on. Before long he did die at Bian, aged fifty-five. In his final memorial he wrote that in attacking Song one should first seize Huainan.
13
徐文，字彥武，萊州掖縣人，徙膠水。 少時販鹽為業，往來瀕海數州，剛勇尚氣，儕輩皆憚之。 宋季盜起，募戰士，為密州板橋左十將。 勇力過人，揮巨刀重五十斤，所向無前，人呼為「徐大刀」。 後隸王龍圖麾下，與夏人戰，生擒一將，補進武校尉。 東還，破群賊楊進等，轉承信郎。
Xu Wen, courtesy name Yanwu, was a native of Yexian in Laizhou and later moved to Jiaoshui. In his youth he traded salt along several coastal prefectures. Bold and quick to take offense, his peers all feared him. Late in the Song, when banditry broke out, he recruited fighters and became Left Tenth Commander at Banqiao in Mizhou. His strength was extraordinary: wielding a great blade of fifty jin, he swept all before him, and men called him "Great Blade Xu." He later served under Wang Longtu, fought the Tanguts, captured an enemy general alive, and was appointed Supplementary Martial Corps Commander. On his return east he defeated the bandit leaders Yang Jin and others, and was promoted to Gentleman of Sustained Trust.
14
東海縣人徐元、張旺作亂，縣人房真等三人走海州，及走總管府，上變。 州、府皆遣使效隨真等詣東海觀賊形勢，皆為賊所害。 州、府合兵攻之，累月不下。 海陵且欲伐宋，惡聞其事，詔文與步軍指揮使張弘信、同知大興尹李惟忠、宿直將軍蕭阿窊率舟師九百浮海討之，謂文等曰：「朕意不在一邑，將以試舟師耳。 」文等至東海，與賊戰，敗之，斬首五千餘級，獲徐元、張旺，餘眾請降。 是役也，張弘信行至萊州，稱疾留止，日與妓樂飲酒。 海陵聞之，師還，杖弘信二百。 文遷定海軍節度使。 房真三人官賞有差。 死賊者皆贈官三級，以銀百兩、絹百匹賜其家。
When Xu Yuan and Zhang Wang of Donghai county rose in rebellion, three local men including Fang Zhen fled first to Haizhou and then to the Military Commissioner's office to report the uprising. Both prefecture and commissioner's office sent envoys, following Fang Zhen's lead to Donghai to scout the rebels, and all were killed. The prefectural and commission forces attacked together, but could not bring them down for months. Hailing was preparing to invade Song and did not wish to hear of unrest at home. He ordered Wen, Infantry Commander Zhang Hongxin, Vice Intendant of Daxing Li Weizhong, and Palace Duty General Xiao A'ao to lead nine hundred marines by sea against the rebels, telling them: "My concern is not one county alone — I mean to test the fleet." " When Wen's force reached Donghai, they defeated the rebels, took more than five thousand heads, captured Xu Yuan and Zhang Wang, and the rest surrendered. During this campaign Zhang Hongxin reached Laizhou, pleaded illness, and stayed behind drinking daily with musicians and courtesans. When Hailing heard of this, he had Hongxin beaten two hundred strokes after the army returned. Wen was promoted to Military Governor of the Dinghai Army. Fang Zhen and his two companions received official rewards of varying degree. Those who died fighting the rebels were posthumously promoted three ranks, and their families received one hundred taels of silver and one hundred bolts of silk.
15
大定二年，詣闕自陳年老目昏，懇求致仕。 許之。 以覃恩遷龍虎衛上將軍，卒于家。
In the second year of Dading he appeared at court in person, saying that he was old and his sight failing, and earnestly requested retirement. His request was granted. By grace of a general amnesty he was promoted to General-in-Chief of the Dragon-Tiger Guard and died at home.
16
施宜生
Shi Yisheng
17
四年冬，為宋國正旦使。 宜生自以得罪北走，恥見宋人，力辭，不許。 宋命張燾館之都亭，因間以首丘風之。 宜生顧其介不在旁，為廋語曰：「今日北風甚勁。 」又取幾間筆扣之曰：「筆來，筆來。 」於是宋始警。 其副使耶律辟離剌使還以聞，坐是烹死。
In the winter of the fourth year he served as New Year's envoy to Song. Yisheng felt he had offended and fled north, and was ashamed to face the Song; he pleaded earnestly to be excused, but was refused. Song appointed Zhang Tao to host him at the Capital Pavilion and, when an opportunity arose, subtly urged him to return home. Noticing that his attendant was out of earshot, Yisheng spoke in veiled words: "The north wind is very strong today." " He then tapped a brush on the table and said: "The brush comes, the brush comes" — a homophone for "they will surely come." " Only then did the Song court take alarm. His vice-envoy Yelü Bilici reported what he had heard on returning from the mission, and for this was executed by boiling.
18
初，宜生困於場屋，遇僧善風鑒，謂之曰：「子面有權骨，可公可卿。 而視子身之毛，皆逆上，且覆腕，必有以合乎此而後可貴也。 」宜生聞其言，大喜，竟從范汝為於建、劍。 已而汝為敗，變服為傭泰之吳翁家三年。 翁異之，一日屏人詰其姓名，宜生曰：「我服傭事惟謹，主人乃亦置疑邪？ 」翁固詰之，則請其故。 翁曰：「日者燕客，執事咸餕，而汝獨孫諸儕，且撤器有歎聲，是以識汝非真傭也。 」宜生遂告之故。 翁贐之金，夜濟淮以歸。 試《一日獲熊三十六賦》擢第一，其後竟如僧言。
Earlier, while struggling in the civil examinations, Yisheng met a monk skilled in physiognomy who told him: "Your face shows the bone of authority — you may rise to duke or minister." But I see the hair on your body grows against the grain, and your wrists turn back — something must fulfill this sign before you can attain high rank." " Delighted by this prophecy, he eventually followed Fan Ruwei in the Jian and Jian circuits. When Ruwei was defeated, he disguised himself and worked as a hired hand in the home of Old Wu in Taizhou for three years. The old man found him odd. One day he sent everyone away and pressed him for his real name. Yisheng said: "I have served you diligently as a hired hand — must you doubt me as well?" " The old man persisted, so Yisheng asked why. The old man said: "At the feast the other day, all the servants ate their fill, but you alone ate more slowly than the rest, and sighed as you cleared the dishes — that is how I knew you were no ordinary hired hand." " Yisheng then told him the whole story. The old man gave him traveling money, and he crossed the Huai by night to make his way home. He took first place in the examination on the fu "Thirty-six Bears Taken in a Single Day," and afterward rose just as the monk had foretold.
19
張中孚
Zhang Zhongfu
20
張中孚，字信甫，其先自安定徙居張義堡。 父達，仕宋至太師，封慶國公。 中孚以父任補承節郎。 宗翰圍太原，其父戰歿，中孚泣涕請跡父屍，乃獨率部曲十余人入大軍中，竟得其屍以還。 累官知鎮戎軍兼安撫使，屢從吳玠、張浚以兵拒大軍。 浚走巴蜀，中孚權帥事。 天會八年，睿宗以左副元帥次涇州，中孚率其將吏來降，睿宗以為鎮洮軍節度使知渭州，兼涇原路經略安撫使。
Zhang Zhongfu, courtesy name Xinfu, came from a family that had moved from Anding to Zhangyi Fort. His father Da had served Song to Grand Preceptor and been enfeoffed as Duke of Qing. Zhongfu entered office through his father's privilege as Gentleman of Sustained Integrity. When Zonghan besieged Taiyuan, his father fell in battle. Zhongfu wept and begged permission to recover the body, then led a dozen of his men alone into the Jin camp and brought his father's corpse back. He rose to command Zhenrong Army with concurrent appointment as Pacification Commissioner, and repeatedly served under Wu Jie and Zhang Jun in resisting the Jin armies. When Zhang Jun fled to Ba-Shu, Zhongfu assumed command in his place. In the eighth year of Tianhui, when Ruizong was Left Vice Marshal stationed at Jingzhou, Zhongfu led his officers to submit. Ruizong appointed him Military Governor of the Zhentao Army with charge of Weizhou and concurrent Commissioner for Military Affairs and Pacification of the Jingyuan Circuit.
21
齊國建，以什一法括民田，籍丁壯為鄉軍。 中孚以為涇原地瘠無良田，且保甲之法行之已習，今遽紛更，人必逃徙，只見其害，未見其利也。 竟執不行。 時齊政甚急，莫敢違，人為中孚懼，而中孚不之顧。 未幾齊國廢，一路獨免掊克之患。
When Qi was established, it used the one-tenth levy to seize civilian land and registered able-bodied men as local militia. Zhongfu argued that Jingyuan was barren and lacked good farmland, and that the baojia system was already well established there; a sudden change would only drive people to flee, with harm and no benefit. He held firm and refused to implement the policy. Qi's government was harsh and no one dared disobey; people feared for Zhongfu, but he paid no heed. Before long Qi was abolished, and his circuit alone was spared the scourge of exactions.
22
中孚天性孝友剛毅，與弟中彥居，未嘗有間言。 喜讀書，頗能書翰。 其禦士卒嚴而有恩，西人尤畏愛之。 葬之日，老稚扶柩流涕蓋數萬人，至為罷市，其得西人之望如此。 正隆例封崇進、原國公。
Zhongfu was by nature filial, loyal to his kin, and resolute; he lived with his younger brother Zhongyan without ever a harsh word between them. He loved reading and was accomplished in calligraphy. He commanded his troops with strict discipline and genuine kindness, and the people of the west especially revered him. On the day of his burial, tens of thousands of old and young followed the bier in tears, closing the markets in mourning — such was the esteem he held in the west. Under the Zhenglong precedent he was enfeoffed as Duke of Chongjin and Duke of Yuan.
23
張中彥
Zhang Zhongyan
24
張中彥，字才甫，中孚弟。 少以父任仕宋，為涇原副將，知德順軍事。 睿宗經略陝西，中彥降，除招撫使。 從下熙、河、階、成州，授彰武軍承宣使，為本路兵馬鈐轄，遷都總管。
Zhang Zhongyan, courtesy name Caifu, was Zhongfu's younger brother. In his youth he entered Song service through his father's privilege as deputy general of Jingyuan with charge of Deshun military affairs. When Ruizong directed affairs in Shaanxi, Zhongyan submitted and was appointed Commissioner for Pacification and Recruitment. After the capture of Xi, He, Jie, and Cheng prefectures, he was appointed Military Commissioner of the Zhangwu Army, made Military Intendant of the circuit, and promoted to Metropolitan Grand Commander.
25
宋將關師古圍鞏州，與秦鳳李彥琦會兵攻之。 王師下饒風關，得金、洋諸州，以中彥領興元尹，撫輯新附。 師還，代彥琦為秦鳳經略使。 秦州當要衝而城不可守，中彥徙治北山，因險為壘，今秦州是也。 築臘家諸城，以扼蜀道。 帥秦凡十年，改涇原路經略使知平涼府。
When the Song general Guan Shigu besieged Gongzhou, Zhongyan joined Li Yanqi of Qinfeng in attacking him. As the imperial army took Raofeng Pass and captured Jin, Yang, and other prefectures, Zhongyan was made Intendant of Xingyuan to pacify the newly submitted territories. After the army returned, he replaced Yanqi as Commissioner for Military Affairs of Qinfeng. Qinzhou lay on a vital route but its city was indefensible; Zhongyan moved the seat to North Mountain and built fortifications along the terrain — the present Qinzhou. He built Lajia and other fortified towns to block the road into Shu. He commanded Qin for ten years, then was reassigned as Commissioner for Military Affairs of the Jingyuan Circuit with charge of Pingliang.
26
朝廷以河南、陝西賜宋，中孚以官守隨例當留關中。 熙河經略使慕洧謀入夏，將窺關、陝，中彥與環慶趙彬會兩路兵討之，洧敗入于夏。 中彥與兄中孚俱至臨安，被留，以為龍神衛四廂都指揮使，清遠軍承宣使，提舉佑神觀，靖海軍節度使。
When the court ceded Henan and Shaanxi to Song, Zhongfu, as an official holding his post, should by precedent have remained in Guanzhong. Mu Wei, Commissioner for Military Affairs of Xihe, plotted to enter Xia territory and threaten Guanzhong and Shaanxi. Zhongyan joined Zhao Bin of Huanqing in a two-circuit campaign against him; Wei was defeated and fled into Xia. Zhongyan and his brother Zhongfu both went to Lin'an and were detained. He was appointed Commander of the Four Wings of the Dragon Divine Guard, Military Commissioner of the Qingyuan Army, Superintendent of the Youshen Observatory, and Military Governor of the Jinghai Army.
27
皇統初，恢復河南，詔征中彥兄弟北歸，為靜難軍節度使，曆彰化軍、鳳翔尹，改尹慶陽，兼慶原路兵馬都總管、甯州刺史。 宗室宗淵毆死僚佐梁鬱。 鬱遠人，家貧無能赴告者。 中彥力為正其罪，竟置於法。 改彰德軍節度使，均賦調法，奸豪無所蔽匿，人服其明。
Early in Huangtong, when Henan was recovered, the brothers were summoned north. Zhongyan was made Military Governor of the Jingnan Army, then Military Governor of the Zhanghua Army and Intendant of Fengxiang, and later Intendant of Qingyang with concurrent command of the Qingyuan Circuit and the prefecture of Ningzhou. The imperial clansman Zongyuan beat his subordinate Liang Yu to death. Yu was an outsider, and his impoverished family had no means to seek justice. Zhongyan pressed the case and had Zongyuan punished by law. Transferred to Military Governor of the Zhangde Army, he standardized tax and corvée assessments so that the powerful could no longer conceal their holdings, and the people respected his fairness.
28
正隆營汴京新宮，中彥采運關中材木。 青峰山巨木最多，而高深阻絕，唐、宋以來不能致。 中彥使構崖駕壑，起長橋十數裏，以車運木，若行平地，開六盤山水洛之路，遂通汴梁。 明年，作河上浮梁，複領其役。 舟之始制，匠者未得其法，中彥手制小舟才數寸許，不假膠漆而首尾自相鉤帶，謂之「鼓子卯」，諸匠無不駭服，其智巧如此。 浮梁巨艦畢功，將發旁郡民曳之就水。 中彥召役夫數十人，治地勢順下傾瀉於河，取新秫秸密佈於地，複以大木限其旁，淩晨督眾乘霜滑曳之，殊不勞力而致諸水。
During Zhenglong, when the new palace at Bianjing was under construction, Zhongyan was charged with supplying timber from Guanzhong. Qingfeng Mountain held the finest timber, but its slopes were steep and inaccessible — since Tang and Song times no one had been able to bring it out. Zhongyan bridged cliffs and ravines, built bridges more than ten li long, and hauled timber by cart as if on level ground. He opened the road through Liupan Mountain along the Shui and Luo rivers all the way to Bianliang. The following year, when a floating bridge was built across the river, he directed that project as well. When the great ships were first designed, the craftsmen could not master the joinery. Zhongyan made a model boat only a few inches long whose bow and stern locked together without glue or lacquer — the "drum-child mortise" — and every craftsman was astonished at such ingenuity. When the great vessels for the floating bridge were finished, the plan was to mobilize civilians from neighboring prefectures to drag them to the river. Zhongyan summoned several dozen laborers, graded the ground to slope toward the river, laid fresh sorghum stalks thick on the surface, and bounded the path with heavy timbers. At dawn he had the workers haul the ships over the frost-slick slope, bringing them to the water with remarkably little effort.
29
逾年，除南京留守。 時淮楚用兵，土民與戍兵雜居，訟牒紛紜，所司皆依違不決。 中彥得戍兵為盜者，悉論如法，帥府怒其專決，劾奏之，朝廷置而不問。 秩滿，轉真定尹兼河北西路兵馬都總管。 未幾，致仕，西歸京兆。 明年，起為臨洮尹兼熙秦路兵馬都總管。 鞏州劉海構亂，既敗，籍民之從亂者數千人，中彥惟論為首者戮之。
A year later he was appointed Military Governor of the Southern Capital. War was raging in Huai-Chu; locals and garrison troops lived intermingled, lawsuits piled up, and the local offices vacillated without deciding cases. When he caught garrison soldiers stealing, he punished them all according to law. The commandery resented his independent judgments and memorialized against him, but the court took no action. When his term expired, he was transferred to Intendant of Zhending with concurrent command of the Western Circuit of Hebei. Before long he retired and returned west to Jingzhao. The following year he was recalled as Intendant of Lintao with concurrent command of the Xi-Qin Circuit. After Liu Hai of Gongzhou rebelled and was defeated, several thousand civilians who had joined him were registered for punishment; Zhongyan executed only the ringleaders.
30
西羌吹折、密臧、隴逋、龐拜四族恃險不服，使侍御史沙醇之就中彥論方略，中彥曰：「此羌服叛不常，若非中彥自行，勢必不可。 」即至積石達南寺，酋長四人來，與之約降，事遂定，賞而遣之。 還奏，上大悅，遣張汝玉馳驛勞之，賜以球文金帶，用郊恩加儀同三司。 以疾卒官，年七十五。 百姓哀號輟市，立像祀之。
Four western Qiang tribes — Chuizhe, Mizang, Longbu, and Pangbai — held difficult terrain and refused to submit. The court sent Supervising Censor Sha Chunzhi to consult Zhongyan, who said: "These Qiang submit and rebel unpredictably; unless I go in person, the matter cannot be settled." " He went to Jishi and reached Nansi Monastery, where four chieftains came to negotiate submission. The matter was settled, and he rewarded and dismissed them. On his return the emperor was delighted, sent Zhang Ruyu by relay post to congratulate him, granted him a golden belt with orb patterns, and by grace of the suburban sacrifice promoted him to Palace Attendant of the Third Order. He died in office of illness at the age of seventy-five. The people mourned with such grief that markets closed, and they erected images in his honor.
31
贊曰：自古健將武夫，其不才者，遭世變遷，賣降恐後。 此其常態，君子之所不責也，酈瓊、徐文是已。 施宜生反覆壬人，李成盜賊之靡，孔彥舟漁色親出，自絕人類，又何責也。 張中孚、中彥雖有小惠足稱，然以宋大臣之子，父戰沒于金，若金若齊，義皆不共戴天之仇。 金以地與齊則甘心臣齊，以地歸宋則忍恥臣宋，金取其地則又比肩臣金，若趨市然，唯利所在，于斯時也，豈複知所謂綱常也哉。 籲！
The appraiser writes: Throughout history, when the times turned, untalented martial men have scrambled to sell their submission, each fearing to be too late. That is their common pattern, and gentlemen do not hold it against them — such were Li Qiong and Xu Wen. Shi Yisheng was a fickle schemer, Li Cheng wallowed among bandits, and Kong Yanzhou preyed on beauty and took his own daughter — men who cut themselves off from humanity deserve no further blame. Zhang Zhongfu and Zhongyan, though they had minor virtues worth noting, were sons of a Song minister whose father had died fighting Jin — toward Jin and Qi alike they owed the debt of an enemy with whom one cannot share the sky. When Jin gave them land they gladly served Qi; when the land went back to Song they swallowed their shame and served Song; when Jin reclaimed the land they again stood as Jin subjects side by side — like merchants rushing to market, following profit wherever it led. At such a moment, how could they still know the bonds of moral duty? Alas!
32
宇文虛中
Yu Wenxuzhong
33
虛中恃才輕肆，好譏訕，凡見女直人輒以礦鹵目之，貴人達官往往積不能平。 虛中嘗撰宮殿榜署，本皆嘉美之名，惡虛中者擿其字以為謗訕朝廷，由是媒糵以成其罪矣。 六年二月，唐括酬斡家奴杜天佛留告虛中謀反，詔有司鞫治無狀，乃羅織虛中家圖書為反具，虛中曰：「死自吾分。 至於圖籍，南來士大夫家家有之，高士談圖書尤多於我家，豈亦反耶？ 」有司承順風旨並殺士談，至今冤之。
Xuzhong was talented but insolent and fond of mockery; whenever he saw Jurchen he called them crude salt, and many powerful officials nursed grudges against him. Xuzhong had composed inscriptions for palace plaques, all originally auspicious names; his enemies picked out individual characters to claim they slandered the court, and thus fabricated charges against him. In the second month of the sixth year, a slave named Du Tianfoliu reported that Xuzhong was plotting rebellion. The offices investigated and found no substance, then fabricated charges from the books in his home as evidence of treason. Xuzhong said: "Death is my allotted portion." As for books, every southern scholar-official household has them; Gao Shitan's library is far larger than mine — is he a rebel as well? " The offices, following the prevailing intent, killed Shitan as well — a wrong that is remembered to this day.
34
士談字季默，高瓊之後。 宣和末，為忻州戶曹參軍。 入朝，官至翰林直學士。 虛中、士談俱有文集行於世。
Shitan, courtesy name Jimo, was a descendant of Gao Qiong. Late in the Xuanhe reign he served as Revenue Section Aide of Xinzhou. He entered court service and rose to Academician Expositor-in-Ordinary of the Hanlin Academy. Both Xuzhong and Shitan left collected writings that circulated in their time.
35
王倫，字正道，故宋宰相王旦弟王勉玄孫。 俠邪無賴，年四十余尚與市井惡少群游汴中。 天會五年，宋人以倫為假刑部侍郎，與閣門舍人硃弁充通問使。 是時，方議伐宋，凡宋使者如倫及宇文虛中、魏行可、顧縱、張邵等，皆留之不遣。 居數年，倫久困，乃唱為和議求歸。 元帥府使人謂之曰：「此非江南情實，特汝自為此言耳。 」倫曰：「使事有指，不然何為來哉。 惟元帥察之。」
Wang Lun, courtesy name Zhengdao, was the great-great-grandson of Wang Mian, younger brother of the former Song chancellor Wang Dan. A swaggering ruffian, he was past forty and still roaming Bian with the vicious youths of the market wards. In the fifth year of Tianhui, Song appointed Lun Acting Vice Minister of Justice and, together with Gate Attendant Zhu Bian, sent him as envoy to open communications. At that time the Jin were planning to invade Song, and all Song envoys including Lun, Yu Wenxuzhong, Wei Xingke, Gu Zong, and Zhang Shao were detained. After several years of detention, Lun, desperate to return, began advocating peace. An envoy from the Marshal's headquarters told him: "That is not the true situation in Jiangnan — you are speaking only for yourself." " Lun replied: "An envoy's mission has its purpose — otherwise why would I have come? I ask only that the Marshal discern this."
36
天會十年，劉豫連歲出師皆無功，撻懶為元帥左監軍經略南邊，密主和議，乃遣倫歸。 先此，宋已遣使乞和，朝廷未之許也。 倫見康王言和議事，康王大喜，遷倫官，並官其子弟。 宋方與齊用兵，未可和。
In the tenth year of Tianhui, Liu Yu's campaigns had failed year after year. Tadun, Left Overseer of the Army on the southern frontier, secretly favored peace and sent Lun back to Song. Before this, Song had already sent envoys begging for peace, but the Jin court had not agreed. Lun met Prince Kang and spoke of peace negotiations. The prince was delighted, promoted Lun, and gave offices to his sons and younger brothers as well. Song was still at war with Qi, so peace could not yet be concluded.
37
明年，宋以倫為端明殿學士，簽書樞密院事，進金器千兩、銀器萬兩，複來請天水郡王喪柩，及請母韋氏兄弟宗族等。 保信軍節度使藍公佐副之。 是歲，宗磐、宗雋、撻懶皆以謀反屬吏，熙宗誅宗磐、宗雋，以撻懶屬尊，赦其死，以為行台尚書省事左丞相，奪其兵權。 右副元帥宗弼奏曰：「撻懶、宗磐陰與宋人交通，遂以河南、陝西地與宋人。 」會撻懶複謀反，捕而殺之于祁州。 倫至上京，有司詳讀康王表文，不書年，閱進奉狀，稱禮物不言職貢，上使宰相責問倫曰：「汝但知有元帥，豈知有上國耶。 」遂留不遣，遣其副藍公佐歸。
The following year Song appointed Lun Academician of the Hall of Brilliant Clarity and Acting Secretary of the Bureau of Military Affairs, sent one thousand taels of gold vessels and ten thousand taels of silver vessels, and again came to request the bier of the late Prince of Tianshui, along with his mother Lady Wei, his brothers, and other kin. Lan Gongzuo, Military Governor of the Baoxin Army, served as his deputy. That year Zongpan, Zongjun, and Tadun were all implicated in plots of rebellion. Xizong executed Zongpan and Zongjun; because Tadun was of honored lineage, his life was spared and he was made Left Chancellor of the Branch Secretariat, though his military authority was stripped away. Right Vice Marshal Zongbi memorialized: "Tadun and Zongpan secretly dealt with the Song and ceded the lands of Henan and Shaanxi to them." " Before long Tadun plotted rebellion again; he was captured and executed at Qizhou. When Lun reached Shangjing, the offices read Prince Kang's memorial and found no reign year recorded; the presentation document spoke of gifts but not of tribute. The emperor had the chancellor rebuke Lun: "You know only the marshal — do you know there is a superior state?" " Lun was detained, and only his deputy Lan Gongzuo was sent back.
38
四年，以倫為平州路轉運使，倫已受命，複辭遜，上曰：「此反復之人也。 」遂殺之於上京，年六十一。
In the fourth year he was appointed Transport Commissioner of the Pingzhou Circuit. Lun had already accepted the appointment, then declined again with false modesty. The emperor said: "This is a man who blows with every wind." " He was executed at Shangjing, aged sixty-one.
39
贊曰：孔子雲：「行己有恥，使于四方不辱君命，可謂士矣」。 宇文虛中朝至上京，夕受官爵。 王倫紈袴之子，市井為徒。 此豈「行己有恥」之士，可以專使者耶？ 二子之死雖冤，其自取亦多矣。
The appraiser writes: Confucius said, "If in conduct one has a sense of shame, and when sent abroad one does not disgrace one's ruler's commission, one may be called a true gentleman." Yu Wenxuzhong reached Shangjing in the morning and accepted office and rank by evening. Wang Lun was a pampered scion who consorted with market riffraff. Were these men of shameful conduct fit to serve as sole envoys? Though their deaths were wrongful, they had largely brought their fates upon themselves.
