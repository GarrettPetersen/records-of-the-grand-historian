Biography 61 — Loyalty and Righteousness, Part 3: Tu Danhang; Wanyan Chenheshang; Yang Woyan; Wugulun Heihan; Tuoman Hutumen; Ji Ruzuo; Ai Shen; Ma Jianlong (Supplementary biographies) Yu Xian; Zhang Bangxian; Liu Quan
2
徒單航
Tu Danhang
3
完顏陳和尚
Wanyan Chenheshang
4
完顏陳和尚，名彝，字良佐，亦以小字行，豐州人。 系出蕭王諸孫。 父乞哥，泰和南征，以功授同知階州軍事，及宋複階州，乞哥戰歿于嘉陵江。 貞祐中，陳和尚年二十余，為北兵所掠，大帥甚愛之，置帳下。 時陳和尚母留豐州，從兄安平都尉斜烈事之甚謹。 陳和尚在北歲餘，托以省母，乞還。 大帥以卒監之至豐，乃與斜烈劫殺監卒。 奪馬奉其母南奔，大兵覺，合騎追之，由他路得免。 既而失馬，母老不能行，載以鹿角車，兄弟共挽，南渡河。 宣宗奇之。
Wanyan Chenheshang, whose given name was Yi and courtesy name Liangzuo, was commonly known by his childhood name. He came from Fengzhou. His lineage traced to the grandsons of Prince Xiao. His father Qige served on the southern campaigns of the Taihe reign and, for his achievements, was made vice commissioner of military affairs at Jiezhou. When the Song retook Jiezhou, Qige was killed in action on the Jialing River. During the Zhenyou period, when Chenheshang was in his early twenties, northern forces captured him. Their supreme commander grew very fond of him and kept him in his own camp. His mother stayed behind in Fengzhou, where his cousin Xielie, commandant of Anping, looked after her with scrupulous devotion. After more than a year with the northern army, Chenheshang asked leave to go home, saying he wished to visit his mother. The commander sent troops to escort him to Fengzhou. There Chenheshang and Xielie fell upon the escort and killed them. They seized horses, placed his mother upon one, and fled south. The enemy soon gave chase with combined cavalry, but they slipped away by another route. They soon lost their horses. His mother was too old to walk, so they put her in a deer-antler sled and the brothers hauled it together across the river to the south. Emperor Xuanzong was astonished by the feat.
5
斜烈以世官授都統，陳和尚試補護衛，未幾轉奉禦。 及斜烈行壽、泗元帥府事，奏陳和尚自隨，詔以充宣差提控，佩金符。 斜烈辟太原王渥為經歷。 渥字仲澤，文章論議與雷淵、李獻能相上下，故得師友之。 陳和尚天資高明，雅好文史，自居禁衛日，人以秀才目之。 至是，渥授以《孝經》、《小學》、《論語》、《春秋左氏傳》，略通其義。 軍中無事，則窗下作牛毛細字，如寒苦之士，其視世味漠然。
Xielie inherited the post of commander-in-chief, while Chenheshang entered service as a probationary guardsman and soon became a palace attendant. When Xielie took charge of the Shou-Si marshal's headquarters, he asked that Chenheshang accompany him. The court appointed Chenheshang an imperial commissioner and military controller and granted him a gold tally. Xielie engaged Wang Wo of Taiyuan as his administrative officer. Wang Wo, courtesy name Zhongze, ranked with Lei Yuan and Li Xianneng in literary skill and debate, and Chenheshang treated him as both teacher and friend. Naturally gifted and devoted to literature and history, Chenheshang had been regarded as a scholar ever since his days in the inner guard. Wang Wo now taught him the Classic of Filial Piety, the Elementary Learning, the Analects, and Zuo's Commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals, and Chenheshang gained a working understanding of them. Whenever the camp was idle, he would sit at the window writing characters fine as ox hair, like a scholar in poverty, and worldly pleasures left him unmoved.
6
正大二年，斜烈落帥職，例為總領，屯方城。 陳和尚隨以往，凡兄軍中事皆預知之。 斜烈時在病，軍中李太和者，與方城鎮防軍葛宜翁相毆，訴于陳和尚，宜翁事不直，即量笞之。 宜翁素兇悍，恥以理屈受杖，竟鬱鬱以死，留語其妻，必報陳和尚。 妻訟陳和尚以私忿侵官，故殺其夫，訴於台省，於近侍，積薪龍津橋南，約不得報，則自焚以謝其夫。 以故陳和尚系獄。 議者疑陳和尚，狃于禁近，倚兵閫之重，必橫恣違法，當以大辟。 奏上，久不能決。 陳和尚聚書獄中讀之，凡十有八月。 明年，斜烈病癒，詔提兵而西，入朝，哀宗怪其瘦甚，問：「卿甯以方城獄未決故耶？ 卿但行，吾今赦之矣。 」以台諫複有言，不敢赦。 未幾，斜烈卒。 上聞，始馳赦陳和尚，曰：「有司奏汝以私忿殺人。 汝兄死，失吾一名將。 今以汝兄故，曲法赦汝，天下必有議我者。 他日，汝奮發立功名，國家得汝力，始以我為不妄赦矣。 」陳和尚且泣且拜，悲動左右，不能出一言為謝。 乃以白衣領紫微軍都統，逾年轉忠孝軍提控。
In the second year of the Zhengda reign, Xielie was relieved of command and, by precedent, became chief administrator, with his forces encamped at Fangcheng. Chenheshang went with him and was kept informed of every matter in his cousin's command. Xielie was ill at the time. A soldier named Li Taihe had fought with Ge Yiweng of the Fangcheng garrison and brought the case to Chenheshang. Finding Yiweng in the wrong, Chenheshang had him beaten on the spot. Yiweng had always been brutal and overbearing. Ashamed to be beaten when he was clearly in the wrong, he brooded himself to death, telling his wife she must have revenge on Chenheshang. His wife accused Chenheshang of abusing his authority out of private spite and deliberately causing her husband's death. She appealed to the Censorate and to the palace attendants, piled firewood south of Longjin Bridge, and vowed to burn herself if she received no justice for her husband. For this Chenheshang was thrown into prison. Counselors suspected that Chenheshang, accustomed to palace proximity and backed by a powerful command, must have acted with arrogant lawlessness and deserved execution. The memorial went up, yet for a long time the court could reach no decision. Chenheshang gathered books in his cell and read for eighteen months. The following year Xielie recovered and was ordered west with the army. When he came to court, Emperor Aizong was shocked at how thin he had grown and asked, "Is it because the Fangcheng case still hangs unresolved? Go on your way — I shall pardon him now. " But the censorate and remonstrance officials spoke up again, and the emperor did not dare to grant the pardon. Before long Xielie died. When he heard the news, the emperor at last sent a hurried pardon to Chenheshang, saying, "The authorities reported that you killed a man out of private spite. Your cousin is dead, and I have lost a great general. For your cousin's sake I now bend the law to pardon you. There will surely be those in the realm who criticize me for it. If one day you throw yourself into service and win merit and fame, and the state gains your strength, then they will know I did not pardon you in vain. " Chenheshang wept as he bowed, moving all who stood by to tears, and could not find a word of thanks. He was then placed in command of the Ziwei Army while still in plain dress, and a year later was made military controller of the Zhongxiao Army.
7
五年，北兵入大昌原，平章合達問誰可為前鋒者，陳和尚出應命。 先已沐浴易衣，若將就木然者，擐甲上馬不反顧。 是日，以四百騎破八千眾，三軍之士踴躍思戰，蓋自軍興二十年始有此捷。 奏功第一，手詔褒諭，授定遠大將軍、平涼府判官，世襲謀克。 一日名動天下。
In the fifth year, northern forces entered Dachangyuan. Grand Councilor Heda asked who could lead the vanguard, and Chenheshang stepped forward. He had already bathed and changed his clothes as though preparing for his coffin. He buckled on his armor, mounted, and rode off without a backward glance. That day four hundred horsemen under his command routed eight thousand. The whole army brimmed with eagerness to fight — the first such victory in twenty years of war. His merit was reported first in the dispatch. The emperor wrote him a personal commendation and appointed him General Who Stabilizes the Distance and judge of Pingliang Prefecture, with hereditary mouke status. In a single day his name resounded throughout the realm.
8
忠孝一軍，皆回紇、乃滿、羌、渾及中原被俘避罪來歸者，鷙狠淩突，號難制。 陳和尚禦之有方，坐作進退皆中程式，所過州邑常料所給外秋毫無犯，街曲間不復喧雜，每戰則先登陷陣，疾若風雨，諸軍倚以為重。 六年，有衛州之勝。 八年，有倒回穀之勝。 自刑徒不四五遷為禦侮中郎將。
The Zhongxiao Army was made up of Uyghurs, Naimans, Qiang, Hun, and men from the Central Plains who had been captured or fled punishment to join the Jin — fierce, brutal, and overbearing, and notoriously hard to control. Chenheshang governed them with skill. In camp and on the march they followed regulations to the letter. Wherever he passed, his men took nothing beyond the regular rations, and the streets fell quiet. In every battle he was first into the breach, swift as wind and rain, and the armies looked to him as their pillar. In the sixth year came the victory at Weizhou. In the eighth year came the victory at Daohuigu. From convicted criminal to General of the Guard against Insult he rose in scarcely four or five steps.
9
副樞移剌蒲阿無持重之略，嘗一日夜馳二百里趨小利，軍中莫敢諫止。 陳和尚私謂同列曰：「副樞以大將軍為剽略之事，今日得生口三百，明日得牛羊一二千，士卒喘死者則不復計。 國家數年所積，一旦必為是人破除盡矣。 」或以告蒲阿，一日，置酒會諸將飲，酒行至陳和尚，蒲阿曰：「汝曾短長我，又謂國家兵力當由我盡壞，誠有否？ 」陳和尚飲畢，徐曰「有。 」蒲阿見其無懼容，漫為好語雲：「有過當面論，無後言也。」
Vice Marshal Yila Pu'a lacked a steady strategic mind. Once he rode two hundred li in a day and a night chasing petty gains, and no one in the army dared to stop him. Chenheshang said privately to his peers, "The vice marshal uses a great general for raiding. Today three hundred captives, tomorrow a thousand or two head of cattle and sheep — and the men who drop dead from exhaustion are not counted at all. What the state has built up over years will be squandered to nothing by such a man in a single stroke. " Someone reported this to Pu'a. One day he set out wine for the generals, and when the cup reached Chenheshang, Pu'a said, "You once spoke ill of me and said the state's military strength would be ruined entirely by me — is that true? " Chenheshang drained his cup and said calmly, "It is so. " Pu'a saw he showed no fear and made light friendly talk: "If you find fault, say it to my face — none of this talk behind my back."
10
九年正月，三峰山之敗，走鈞州。 城破，大兵入，即縱軍巷戰。 陳和尚趨避隱處，殺掠稍定乃出，自言曰：「我金國大將，欲見白事。 」兵士以數騎夾之，詣行帳前。 問其姓名，曰：「我忠孝軍總領陳和尚也。 大昌原之勝者我也，衛州之勝亦我也，倒回穀之勝亦我也。 我死亂軍中，人將謂我負國家，今日明白死，天下必有知我者。 」時欲其降，斫足脛折不為屈，豁口吻至耳，噀血而呼，至死不絕。 大將義之，酹以馬湩，祝曰：「好男子，他日再生，當令我得之。 」時年四十一。 是年六月，詔贈鎮南軍節度使，塑像褒忠廟，勒石紀其忠烈。
In the first month of the ninth year, after the defeat at Sanfeng Mountain, he fled to Junzhou. When the city fell and the enemy entered, he at once ordered street fighting. Chenheshang hid until the slaughter had subsided, then came out and declared, "I am a great general of the Jin and wish to make a report. " Soldiers took him under escort of several horsemen to the field headquarters. Asked his name, he said, "I am Chenheshang, commander of the Zhongxiao Army. I won at Dachangyuan. I won at Weizhou. I won at Daohuigu. If I die in this rout, men will say I betrayed my country. Today I die openly, and there will be those in the realm who know what I was. " They wanted his surrender. They hacked his shins until they broke, yet he would not yield. They split his mouth to the ear; he spat blood and shouted until he died without ceasing. The enemy commander admired his spirit, poured kumiss in libation, and prayed, "A fine man — if he is born again, may I win him for my own. " He was forty-one years old. In the sixth month of that year an edict posthumously made him military commissioner of the Army That Pacifies the South. A statue was placed in the Temple of Commended Loyalty, and stone was carved to record his loyal valor.
11
斜烈名鼎，字國器，畢里海世襲猛安。 年二十，以善戰知名。 自壽、泗元帥轉安平都尉，鎮商州，威望甚重，敬賢下士，有古賢將之風。 初至商州，一日搜伏，于大竹林中得歐陽修子孫，問而知之，並其族屬鄉里三千餘人皆縱遣之。
Xielie, whose name was Ding and courtesy name Guoqi, held the hereditary meng'an of Bilihai. At twenty he was already famed for prowess in battle. Transferred from the Shou-Si command to commandant of Anping, he garrisoned Shangzhou with great prestige, honoring the worthy and humbling himself before scholars in the manner of the great generals of old. Soon after he reached Shangzhou, a search for hidden persons in a great bamboo grove turned up descendants of Ouyang Xiu. Once he learned who they were, he released them together with more than three thousand clansmen and fellow townsmen.
12
楊沃衍
Yang Woyan
13
楊沃衍，一名斡烈，賜姓兀林答，朔州靜邊官莊人，本屬唐括迪剌部族。 少嘗為北邊屯田小吏，會大元兵入境，朝命徙唐括族內地，沃衍留不徙，率本部族願從者入保朔州南山茶杞溝，有眾數千，推沃衍為招撫使，號其溝曰府，故殘破鎮縣徒黨日集，官軍不能制。 又與大兵戰，連獲小捷，及乏食，遂行剽劫。 官軍捕之，拒戰不下，轉走寧、隩、武、朔、甯邊諸州，民以為病。 朝廷遣人招之，沃衍即以眾來歸。 時宣宗適南遷，次淇門，聞之甚喜，遂以為武州刺史。
Yang Woyan, also called Wolie, was granted the surname Ulin'da. He came from the Jingbian Official Estate in Shuozhou and was originally of the Tangut Dila tribe. In youth he had served as a minor clerk on the northern frontier colony fields. When the armies of the Great Yuan crossed the border, the court ordered the Tangut tribes moved inland. Woyan stayed behind, leading willing clansmen into Chaqi Ravine in the southern mountains of Shuozhou. Several thousand men gathered, made him pacification commissioner, and called his ravine a prefecture. As ruined towns and counties sent more displaced bands every day, government troops could not control them. He fought the enemy repeatedly and won a string of small victories, but when food ran short he turned to raiding. Government troops pursued him, but he resisted and could not be taken, moving through Ning, You, Wu, Shuo, and Ningbian. The people suffered under him. The court sent envoys to summon him, and Woyan came over at once with his followers. Emperor Xuanzong was then moving the capital south and had halted at Qimen. Delighted at the news, he appointed Woyan prefect of Wuzhou.
14
武州屢經殘毀，沃衍入州未幾，而大兵來攻，死戰二十七晝夜不能拔，乃退，時貞祐二年二月也。 既而朝廷以武州終不可守，令沃衍遷其軍民駐岢嵐州，以武州功擢為本州防禦使。 俄升岢嵐為節鎮，以沃衍為節度使，仍詔諭曰：「卿于國盡忠，累有勞績。 今特升三品，恩亦厚矣，其益勵忠勤，與宣撫司輯睦以安軍民。 」沃衍自奉詔即以身許國，曰：「為人不死王事而死於家，非大丈夫也。」
Wuzhou had been ravaged again and again. Woyan had scarcely taken office when the enemy attacked. He fought to the death for twenty-seven days and nights until they could not take the city and withdrew. This was in the second month of the second year of Zhenyou. The court soon decided Wuzhou could not be held long term and ordered Woyan to move his soldiers and civilians to Kelan. For his defense of Wuzhou he was promoted to defense commissioner of Kelan. Kelan was soon raised to a military commission, with Woyan as its commissioner. An edict told him, "You have served the state with utter loyalty and won repeated merit. You are now specially promoted three ranks — a generous favor. Press on in loyalty and diligence, and work in harmony with the Pacification Commission to settle soldiers and civilians. " From the moment he received the edict, Woyan pledged his life to the state, saying, "For a man to die at home rather than in the king's service is not the act of a great man."
15
三年，奉旨屯涇、邠、隴三州，沃衍分其軍九千人為十翼五都統，親統者十之四。 是冬，西夏四萬餘騎圍定西州，元帥右都監完顏賽不以沃衍提控軍事，率兵與夏人戰，斬首幾二千，生擒數十人，獲馬八百餘匹，器械稱是，餘悉遁去。 詔陝西行省視功官賞之。
In the third year he was ordered to garrison Jing, Bin, and Long. Woyan divided his nine thousand men into ten wings under five commanders-in-chief, keeping four-tenths under his own command. That winter more than forty thousand Xia horsemen besieged Dingxi. Marshal's right overseer Wanyan Saibu placed Woyan in command. He met the Xia in battle, beheaded nearly two thousand, took several dozen prisoners, seized more than eight hundred horses and a full complement of weapons, and drove the rest off. An edict ordered the Shaanxi Branch Secretariat to review his merit and grant rewards.
16
元光元年正月，遙授中京留守。 六月，進拜元帥右監軍，仍世襲納古胡裏愛必剌謀克。 二年春，北兵遊騎數百掠延安而南，沃衍率兵追之，戰於野豬嶺，獲四人而還。 俄而，兵大至，駐德安寨，複擊走之。 未幾，大兵攻鳳翔還，道出保安，沃衍遣提控完顏查剌破于石樓臺，前後獲馬二百、符印數十。 詔有司論賞。 初，聞野豬嶺有兵，沃衍約陀滿胡土門以步軍會戰。 胡土門宿將，常輕沃衍，至是失期。 沃衍戰還，會諸將欲斬胡土門，諸將哀請乃釋之。 時大兵聲勢益振，陝西行省檄沃衍清野，不從，曰； 「我若清野，明年民何所得食？ 」遂隔大澗持勢使民畢麥事。 正大二年，進拜元帥左監軍，遙領中京留守。
In the first month of the first year of Yuanguang he was appointed in absentia defender of the Central Capital. In the sixth month he was promoted to marshal's right overseer of the army, retaining hereditary mouke status of Naguhulai Aibila. In the spring of the second year several hundred northern raiders plundered Yan'an and moved south. Woyan pursued, fought at Wild Boar Ridge, took four prisoners, and returned. Soon a larger force arrived and encamped at De'an Stockade. He attacked again and drove them off. Before long the main army attacked Fengxiang and withdrew by way of Bao'an. Woyan sent military controller Wanyan Zhala to defeat them at Shilou Terrace, capturing two hundred horses and several dozen tallies and seals. An edict ordered the relevant offices to deliberate his reward. When he first heard of troops at Wild Boar Ridge, Woyan had arranged with Tuoman Hutumen to join him with infantry. Hutumen was a veteran who had always looked down on Woyan. This time he missed the rendezvous. When Woyan returned from battle he gathered the generals and wished to execute Hutumen. They pleaded piteously until he relented. The enemy's momentum was growing. The Shaanxi Branch Secretariat ordered Woyan to clear the countryside. He refused, saying: "If I clear the countryside, what will the people eat next year? " He held position across the great ravine and let the people finish the wheat harvest. In the second year of Zhengda he was promoted to marshal's left overseer of the army and continued to hold the absentee post of defender of the Central Capital.
17
八年冬，平章合達、參政蒲阿由鄧州而西，沃衍自豐陽川遇於五朵山下，問禹山之戰如何，合達曰：「我軍雖勝，而大兵已散漫趨京師矣。 」沃衍憤雲：「平章、參政蒙國厚恩，握兵柄，失事機，不能戰禦，乃縱兵深入，尚何言耶！」
That winter in the eighth year, Grand Councilor Heda and Administrative Assistant Pu'a marched west from Dengzhou. Woyan came from Fengyang River and met them at the foot of Mount Wuduo. He asked how the battle at Yushan had gone. Heda said, "We won, but the enemy army had already scattered and was rushing toward the capital. " Woyan said angrily, "Grand Councilor, Administrative Assistant — you enjoyed the state's deep favor and held military command. You missed your chance, failed to fight and defend, and let the enemy advance unchecked. What more is there to say!"
18
三峰山之敗，沃衍走鈞州。 其部曲白留奴、呆劉勝既降，請于大帥，願入鈞招沃衍。 大帥質留奴，令勝入鈞見沃衍，道大帥意，降則當授大官。 沃衍善言慰撫之，使前，拔劍斫之，曰：「我起身細微，蒙國大恩，汝欲以此汙我耶！ 」遂遺語部曲後事，望汴京拜且哭曰：「無面目見朝廷，惟有一死耳。 」即自縊。 部曲舉火並所寓屋焚之，從死者十余人。 沃衍死時年五十二。
After the defeat at Mount Sanfeng, Woyan fled to Junzhou. His retainers Bailiunu and Dull Liu Sheng had already surrendered. They asked the chief commander for permission to enter Junzhou and persuade Woyan to surrender. The chief commander held Bailiunu hostage and sent Liu Sheng into Junzhou to see Woyan, conveying the commander's offer: surrender and he would receive high office. Woyan spoke kindly to reassure him and had him come forward — then drew his sword and cut him down. "I rose from humble origins and received the state's great grace," he said. "Do you mean to stain me with this? " He then entrusted final affairs to his retainers, turned toward Bianjing, bowed, and wept. "I have no face left to meet the court," he said. "There is nothing for me but death. " He hanged himself. His retainers set fire and burned the house where he was staying. More than ten died with him. Woyan was fifty-two when he died.
19
初，大兵破西夏，長驅而至，關輔千里皆洶洶不安，雖智者亦無如之何。 沃衍與其部將劉興哥者率兵往來邠、隴間，屢戰屢勝，故大軍猝不能東下。
Earlier, when the enemy swept through Western Xia and rode hard eastward, unrest spread for a thousand li across the Guanzhong region. Even the wisest could see no remedy. Woyan and his subordinate Liu Xingge led troops between Bin and Long, winning battle after battle — so the enemy could not push east at once.
20
興哥，鳳翔虢縣人，起於群盜，人呼曰「熱劉」。 後於清化戰死，大兵至酹酒以吊，西州耆老語之。 至為泣下。
Xingge came from Guo County in Fengxiang. He had risen from bandit bands, and people called him "Hot Liu." He later died fighting at Qinghua. When the enemy army arrived they poured wine in mourning, and the elders of the western prefectures spoke of his death. They wept.
21
烏古論黑漢
Wugulun Heihan
22
烏古論黑漢，初以親軍入仕，嘗為唐、鄧元帥府把軍官。 天興二年，唐州刺史內族斜魯病卒，鄧州總帥府以蒲察都尉權唐州事。 宋軍兩來圍唐，又唐之糧多為鄧州所取，以故乏食。 六月，遣萬戶夾穀定住入歸德，奏請軍糧，不報。 七月，鎮防軍馮總領、甄改住為變，殺蒲察都尉。 時朝廷道梗，帥府承制以黑漢權刺史行帥府事。
Wugulun Heihan entered service as an imperial guardsman and had once served as a garrison officer under the Tang-Deng marshal's headquarters. In the second year of Tianxing, Tang Prefect Xielu of the imperial clan died of illness. The Dengzhou supreme commander's headquarters put Captain Pucha in charge of Tang Prefecture. Song forces twice besieged Tang Prefecture, and much of its grain had been taken by Dengzhou — so the city went hungry. In the sixth month he sent Commander Jiagu Dingzhu to Guide to request military grain from the court. No reply came. In the seventh month, garrison commanders Feng and Zhen Gaizhu mutinied and killed Captain Pucha. With the roads to court cut off, the commander's headquarters appointed Heihan acting prefect and had him run headquarters affairs.
23
既而鎮防軍有歸宋之謀，時裕州大成山聶都統一軍五百人在州，獨不欲歸宋，與鎮防軍為敵，鎮防不能勝，棄老幼奔棗陽，宋人以故知唐之虛實。 會鄧帥移剌瑗以城叛歸於宋，遺書招黑漢，黑漢殺其使者不報。 宋王安撫率兵攻唐，鄂司王太尉繼至，攻益急。 黑漢聞哀宗遷蔡，遣人求救，上命權參政兀林答胡土將兵以往。 宋人設伏，縱其半入城，邀擊之，胡土大敗，僅存三十騎以還。
Soon the garrison troops plotted to defect to Song. Commander Nie of Dacheng Mountain in Yuzhou had five hundred men in the prefecture who refused to join them. They fought the garrison and held their ground; the garrison could not defeat them and fled to Zaoyang with their dependents, letting the Song learn how weak Tang really was. Meanwhile Deng Commander Yila Yuan betrayed the city and surrendered to Song. He sent a letter inviting Heihan to join him. Heihan killed the messenger and refused. Song Commissioner Wang led troops against Tang Prefecture. Grand Marshal Wang of the E Circuit arrived in support, and the assault intensified. Hearing that Emperor Aizong had moved to Cai, Heihan sent messengers begging for relief. The emperor ordered Acting Administrative Assistant Ulin'da Hutu to march to his aid. The Song laid an ambush, let half his force enter the city, then struck. Hutu was routed and returned with only thirty horsemen left.
24
城中糧盡，人相食，黑漢殺其愛妾啖士，士爭殺其妻子。 官屢聚議欲降，黑漢與聶都統執議益堅，馮總領乃私出城與王安撫會飲，約明日宋軍入城。 馮歸，宋軍不得入，聶都統請馮議事，即坐中斬之。 及其黨皆死。 總領趙醜兒者初與馮同謀，內不自安，開西門納宋軍。 黑漢率大成山軍巷戰，自辰至午，宋軍大敗而出，殺傷無數。 宋人城下大呼趙醜兒，約並力殺大成山軍。 大成軍敗，宋人獲黑漢，脅使降，黑漢不屈，為所殺。 其得脫走者十餘人，總領移剌望軍、女奚烈軍、醜兒走蔡州，皆得遷賞，後俱死於甲午之難。
When the city's grain ran out, people ate one another. Heihan killed his beloved concubine to feed his men. Soldiers began killing their own wives and children. Officials repeatedly met to discuss surrender, but Heihan and Commander Nie held firm. Commandant Feng slipped out to drink with Commissioner Wang and arranged for the Song to enter the city the next day. Feng returned before the Song could enter. Commander Nie summoned him to discuss business and executed him on the spot. He and his followers were all killed. Commandant Zhao Chou'er, Feng's co-conspirator, lost his nerve and opened the west gate to the Song. Heihan led the Dacheng Mountain troops in street fighting from morning until midday. The Song were routed with countless casualties. Below the walls the Song shouted for Zhao Chou'er, urging him to join them in destroying the Dacheng Mountain troops. The Dacheng Mountain force was beaten. The Song captured Heihan and pressed him to surrender. He refused and was killed. A dozen or so men escaped. Commandants Yila Wangjun, Nuxilie Jun, and Chou'er fled to Caizhou and were all promoted and rewarded — only to die later in the jiawu disaster.
25
陀滿胡土門
Tuoman Hutumen
26
陀滿胡土門，字子秀，策論進士也。 累官翰林待制。 貞祐二年，遷知中山府。 三年，改知臨洮府、兼本路兵馬都總管。 叛賊蘭州程陳僧等誘夏人入寇，圍臨洮凡半月，城中兵數千而粟且不支，眾皆危之。 胡土門日為開諭逆順禍福，皆自奮。 因捕其黨欲為內應者二十人，斬之，擲首城外。 賊四面來攻，乃夜出襲賊壘，夏兵大亂，金軍乘之，遂大捷，夏人遁去。
Tuoman Hutumen, courtesy name Zixiu, had passed the jinshi examination in policy essays. He rose through the ranks to Hanlin Attendant Drafting. In the second year of Zhenyou he was appointed prefect of Zhongshan. The next year he became prefect of Lin'tao and concurrent overall commander of the circuit's forces. Rebels such as Cheng Chenseng of Lanzhou induced the Xia to raid. They besieged Lin'tao for half a month. The city had several thousand troops but grain was running out, and morale collapsed. Hutumen daily explained the rewards of loyalty and the cost of treason, and the men rallied. He seized twenty men plotting to open the gates from within, beheaded them, and hurled their heads over the wall. When the enemy attacked from every side, he sallied out at night and struck their camp. The Xia broke in confusion; the Jin pressed the advantage and won a great victory, and the raiders fled.
27
四年，知河中府事，權河東南路宣撫副使。 十月，進元帥右監軍、兼前職。 興定二年，為絳陽軍節度使、兼絳州管內觀察使。 十月，遷元帥左監軍、行元帥府事、兼知晉安府、河東南路兵馬都總管。 於是，修城池，繕甲兵，積芻糧，以備戰守。 民不悅，行省胥鼎聞之，遺以書曰：「元帥始鎮河中，惠愛在民，移ＩＭ晉安，遠近忻仰。 去歲兵入，平陽不守，河東保完者惟絳而已。 蓋公坐籌制勝，威德素著，故不動聲氣以至無虞也。 邇來傳聞，治政太剛，科征太重，鼎切憂之。 古人有言，禦下不寬則人多懼禍，用人有疑則士不盡心。 況大兵在邇，鄰境已虛，小人易動，誠不可不慮也。 願公以謙虛待下，忠孝結人，明賞罰，平賦稅，上以分聖主宵旰之憂，下以為河東長城之托。 」胡土門得書，懼民不從且或生變，乃上言：「臣本瑣材，猥膺重寄，方將治隍碑、積芻糧為捍禦之計，而小民難與慮始，以臣政令頗急，皆有怨言，遂貽行省之憂。 自聞訓諭，措身無所，內自悛悔，外加寬撫，庶幾少慰眾心。 而近以朝命分軍過河，則又喧言帥臣不益兵保守，而反助河南，將棄我也。 人心如此，恐一旦遂生他變。 向者李革在平陽，人不安之，而革隱忍不言，以至於敗。 臣實拙繆，無以服人，敢以鼎書上聞，惟朝廷圖之。 」朝廷以鼎言，遣吏部尚書守顏閭山代之。 或曰，胡土門欲以計去晉安，乃大興役，恣為殺戮，務失民心，故鼎言及之。 未幾，晉安失守，死者幾百萬人，遂失河東。
In the fourth year he governed Hezhong and served as deputy military commissioner of the Hedong South Circuit. In the tenth month he was promoted to marshal's right overseer of the army while keeping his other posts. In the second year of Xingding he became military governor of Jiangyang Army and concurrent observation commissioner of Jiang Prefecture. In the tenth month he was made marshal's left overseer of the army, acting head of the marshal's headquarters, prefect of Jin'an, and overall commander of the Hedong South Circuit. He repaired the walls, sharpened arms, and stockpiled fodder and grain to prepare for siege and defense. The people were unhappy. Branch Secretariat Chief Xu Ding heard of it and wrote: "When you first held command at Hezhong, the people felt your kindness. When you moved your command to Jin'an, men near and far rejoiced in you. Last year when the enemy invaded, Pingyang fell. Of all Hedong, only Jiang Prefecture held firm. You won through careful planning; your authority and virtue were already famed — so you kept the peace without raising an alarm. Lately I hear your rule has grown too harsh and your levies too heavy. This troubles me deeply. The ancients said: rule your subordinates too harshly and men will fear for their lives; doubt the men you employ and they will not give you their best. The enemy is near, neighboring territories lie stripped, and petty men are easily swayed. This must not be ignored. Treat your men with humility. Win them through loyalty and fairness. Make rewards and punishments clear and keep taxes level — easing the emperor's sleepless worry above and standing as Hedong's bulwark below. " Hutumen received the letter, feared the people would not obey and might revolt, and memorialized: "I am a man of little talent who unworthily bears heavy responsibility. I have been repairing walls and moats and stockpiling grain for defense, but common people resist plans before they see results. My orders have been strict, grumbling has spread, and I have troubled the Branch Secretariat. Since your admonition I have been at a loss. I repent inwardly and soften my rule outwardly, hoping to ease popular anger. Then the court ordered troops sent across the river, and rumor spread that the commander would not reinforce the defense but was aiding Henan and meant to abandon us. Morale is this fragile. I fear a sudden uprising. When Li Ge was at Pingyang the people were restless, but he kept silent until disaster came. I am too clumsy to command men's loyalty. I submit Chief Xu Ding's letter for the court's judgment. " On Xu Ding's advice the court sent Minister of Personnel Shou Yan Lüshan to replace him. Some said Hutumen wanted to leave Jin'an by design — that he imposed heavy labor, killed freely, and deliberately alienated the people, which prompted Xu Ding's letter. Before long Jin'an fell. Nearly a million people died, and Hedong was lost.
28
三年八月，改太常卿、權簽樞密院事、知歸德府事。 元光二年二月，坐上書不實，削一官。 正大三年七月，複為臨洮府總管。 四年五月，城破被執，誘之降不應，使之跪不從，以刀亂斫其膝脛，終不為屈，遂殺之。 五年，詔贈中京留守，立像褒忠廟，錄用其子孫。 其妻烏古論氏亦死節，有傳。
In the eighth month of the third year he became Minister of Ceremonies, acting privy council signatory, and governor of Guide. In the second month of Yuanguang 2 he was demoted one rank for filing a false report. In the seventh month of Zhengda 3 he was restored as overall commander of Lin'tao. In the fifth month of the fourth year the city fell. Captured, he refused to surrender or kneel. They hacked at his knees and shins but could not break him, and killed him. In the fifth year the court posthumously made him Defender of the Central Capital, placed his portrait in the Hall of Rewarding Loyalty, and enrolled his descendants in office. His wife, Lady Wugulun, also died loyal — she has her own biography.
29
姬汝作
Ji Ruzuo
30
姬汝作，字欽之，汝陽人，全州節度副使端修之侄孫也。 父懋，以廕試部掾，轉尚書省令史。 汝作讀書知義理，性豪宕，不拘細行，平日以才量稱。 正大末，避兵崧山，保鄉鄰數百家，眾以長事之。 後徙居交牙山砦，會近侍局使烏古論四和撫諭西山，以便宜授汝作北山招撫使，佩銀符，遂遷入汝州。
Ji Ruzuo, courtesy name Qinzhi, came from Ruyang. He was the grand-nephew of Quanzhou Vice Military Governor Duanxiu. His father Mao entered service by yin privilege as a ministry clerk and became a secretary in the Ministry of Personnel. Ruzuo was well read and principled — bold and unconstrained by petty scruples, known for ability and breadth of mind. At the end of Zhengda he took refuge on Song Mountain and sheltered several hundred neighboring households, who looked to him as their leader. He later moved to Jiaoya Mountain Fortress. Palace Attendant Commissioner Wugulun Sihe was pacifying the western hills and appointed Ruzuo Pacification Commissioner of the Northern Hills with a silver tally. Ruzuo then moved into Ruzhou.
31
初，汝州殘破之後，天興元年正月，同知宣徽院事張楷授防禦使，自汴率襄、郟縣土兵百餘人入青陽垛。 時呼延實者領青陽砦事。 實趙城人，本楊沃衍部曲，以戰功至寶昌軍節度使，閒居汝之西山。 楷自揣不能服眾，乃以州事托實，尋往鄧州從恆山公武仙。 後大元兵至，城破，殺數千人，乃許降，以張宣差者管州事。 三月，鈞州潰軍柳千戶者入州，張逃去，柳遂據之。 未幾，城複破。 及汝作至，北兵雖去，但空城爾。 汝作招集散亡，複立市井，北兵屢招之不從，數戰互有勝負。 已而北兵複來攻，汝作親督士卒，以死拒之。 兵退，間道納奏，哀宗宣諭：「此州無險固可恃，汝乃能為國用命，今授以同知汝州防禦使，便宜從事。」
After Ruzhou lay in ruins, in the first month of Tianxing 1 Zhang Kai, associate director of the Palace Attendant Bureau, was made defense commissioner. He led more than a hundred local troops from Xiang and Jia counties from Bian to Qingyang Rampart. At that time Huyan Shi held Qingyang Fortress. Shi came from Zhaocheng and had been a retainer of Yang Woyan. Battle honors had made him military governor of Baochang Army, and he was living in retirement in the western hills of Ruzhou. Kai knew he could not command the troops and turned prefectural affairs over to Shi, then went to Dengzhou to serve Duke of Hengshan Wu Xian. When the Yuan army arrived the city fell and thousands were killed. Surrender was accepted and Commissioner Zhang was put in charge of the prefecture. In the third month routed Junzhou troops under Commander Liu entered the city. Zhang fled and Liu seized the prefecture. Soon the city fell again. By the time Ruzuo arrived the northern troops were gone — but the city was empty. Ruzuo gathered the scattered population and rebuilt the market streets. The northern troops repeatedly summoned him; he refused. They fought several times with mixed results. The northern army returned to attack. Ruzuo personally led his men and fought to the death to hold them off. When the enemy withdrew he sent a memorial by secret route. Emperor Aizong proclaimed: "This prefecture has no natural defenses to speak of, yet you have served the state at mortal risk. I appoint you associate defense commissioner of Ruzhou with full discretionary powers."
32
是時，此州南通鄧州，西接洛陽，東則汴京，使傳所出，供億三面，傳通音耗。 然呼延實在青陽為總帥，忌汝作城守之功，不能相下，州事動為所制。 實欲遷州入山，謂他日必為大兵所破。 汝作以為「倉中糧尚多，四面潰軍日至，此輩經百死，激之皆可用，朝廷倚我守此州，總帥乃欲棄之，何心哉。 」讒間既行，有相圖之隙，詳議官楊鵬釋之曰：「外難未解而顧私忿。 」語甚諄切。 實乃還山，鵬因勸汝作納奏，乞死守此州，以堅軍民之心。 其冬，戰於襄、郟，得馬百餘，士氣頗振，遂以汝作為總帥，不復與實相關矣。
The prefecture linked south to Deng, west to Luoyang, and east to Bianjing. Couriers passed through it; it supplied three fronts and carried news in every direction. But Huyan Shi, supreme commander at Qingyang, envied Ruzuo's success in holding the city. Neither would defer to the other, and Ruzuo's work was constantly blocked. Shi wanted to move the prefecture into the hills, convinced the enemy would eventually take the city. Ruzuo argued: "The granaries still hold grain. Routed troops arrive daily from every direction — men who have survived a hundred battles. Rouse them and they will fight. The court relies on us to hold this prefecture, and the supreme commander wants to abandon it — what kind of heart is that? " Slander bred mistrust and the two men nearly came to blows. Detail Review Officer Yang Peng intervened: "The external enemy is not yet defeated, yet you indulge private grudges. " He spoke with earnest urgency. Shi returned to the hills. Peng urged Ruzuo to memorialize the court, begging to defend the prefecture to the death and steady the hearts of troops and people. That winter they fought at Xiang and Jia and captured more than a hundred horses. Morale revived, and Ruzuo was made supreme commander — no longer answerable to Shi.
33
天興二年六月，哀宗在蔡州，遣使徵兵入援。 州人為邏騎所擾，農事盡廢，城中糧亦垂盡。 是月，中京破，部曲私議有脣亡之懼，計以城降，懼汝作，不敢言，乃以遷州入山白之。 汝作怒曰：「吾家父祖食祿百年，今朝延又以州事帥職委我，吾生為金民，死為金鬼。 汝輩欲避於山，非欲降乎？ 有再言遷者吾必斬之。」
In the sixth month of Tianxing 2 Emperor Aizong, at Caizhou, sent envoys to call up troops for relief. Patrol cavalry harried the people; farming ceased; and the city's grain was nearly gone. That month the Central Capital fell. Ruzuo's retainers privately feared that when the lips are gone the teeth freeze — they plotted to surrender the city but dared not speak openly for fear of Ruzuo, and broached the idea of moving into the hills instead. Ruzuo said in anger, "My forefathers have drawn salary from the state for a hundred years. The court has now entrusted me with both the prefecture and supreme command. Living, I am a subject of Jin; dead, I shall be a ghost of Jin. You want to flee to the mountains — is that not surrender in disguise? Whoever speaks of moving again, I shall behead."
34
八月，塔察將大兵攻蔡，經汝州。 州人梁皋作亂，與故吏溫澤、王和七八人徑入州廨，汝作不為備，遂為所殺。 時宣使石珪體究洛陽所以破及強伸死節事，以路阻，留汝州驛。 梁皋既殺汝作，走告珪曰：「汝作私積糧斛，不恤軍民，眾怒殺之矣。 皋不圖汝作官職，惟宣使裁之。 」圭懼，乃以皋權汝州防禦使、行帥府事。 脫走入蔡，以皋殺汝作事聞。 哀宗甚嗟惜之，遣近侍張天錫贈汝作昌武軍節度使，子孫世襲謀克，仍詔峴山帥呼延實、登封帥范真並力討皋。 天錫避峴山遠，先約範真，真以麾下李某者往，以撫諭軍民為名。 皋率軍士迎于東門，知朝廷圖己，陰為之備，李猶豫不敢發。 皋館天錫于望崧樓，隱毒於食，天錫遂中毒而死。 皋後為大元兵所殺。
In the eighth month Tacha led a great army against Cai and passed through Ruzhou. A townsman named Liang Gao rebelled. With former clerks Wen Ze, Wang He, and seven or eight others he burst into the prefectural offices. Ruzuo was caught unprepared and killed. Imperial commissioner Shi Gui was then investigating how Luoyang had fallen and how Qiang Shen had died for his principles. Blocked by the roads, he stayed at the Ruzhou post station. After killing Ruzuo, Liang Gao ran to Gui and said, "Ruzuo hoarded grain for himself and neglected soldiers and civilians. The people killed him in anger. I do not seek Ruzuo's office — let the imperial commissioner decide. " Gui, afraid, made Gao acting defense commissioner of Ruzhou and head of the marshal's headquarters. He slipped away to Cai and reported that Gao had killed Ruzuo. Emperor Aizong deeply lamented his death. He sent inner attendant Zhang Tianxi to posthumously make Ruzuo military commissioner of the Changwu Army, with hereditary mouke status for his descendants, and ordered Xianshan commander Huyan Shi and Dengfeng commander Fan Zhen to join forces against Gao. Tianxi, finding Xianshan too distant, arranged first with Fan Zhen, who sent a certain Li from his command under the pretext of reassuring soldiers and civilians. Gao led soldiers to welcome them at the east gate. Learning that the court plotted against him, he made secret preparations. Li hesitated and did not dare strike. Gao lodged Tianxi at Wangsong Tower and hid poison in his food. Tianxi died of poisoning. Gao was later killed by the armies of the Great Yuan.

楊鵬字飛卿，能詩。
Yang Peng, courtesy name Feiqing, was a poet.
36
愛申，逸其族與名，或曰一名忙哥。 本虢縣鎮防軍，累功遷軍中總領。 李文秀據秦州，宣宗詔鳳翔軍討之，軍圍秦州城。 時愛申在軍中，有罪當死。 宣宗問之樞帥，有知其名者奏此人將帥材，忠實可倚。 宣宗命馳赦之，以為德順節度使、行元帥府事。 正大四年春，大兵西來，擬以德順為坐夏之所，德順無軍，人甚危之。 愛申識鳳翔馬肩龍舜卿者可與謀事，乃遺書招之，肩龍得書欲行，鳳翔總管禾速嘉國鑒以大兵方進，吾城可恃，德順決不可守，勸勿往。 肩龍曰：「愛申平生未嘗識我，一見許為知己。 我知德順不可守，往則必死，然以知己故。 不得不為之死耳。 」乃舉行橐付族父，明為死別，冒險而去。 既至，不數日受圍，城中惟有義兵鄉軍八九千人，大兵舉天下之勢攻之。 愛申假舜卿鳳翔總管府判官，守禦一與共之。 凡攻百二十晝夜，力盡乃破，愛申以劍自剄，時年五十三。 軍中募生致肩龍，而不知所終。 台諫有言當贈德順死事者官，以勸中外。 詔各贈官，配食褒忠廟。
Ai Shen — his clan and given name are lost to history; some say he was also called Mangge. He began in the Guo County garrison defense force and, through repeated merit, rose to commander-in-chief of the army. When Li Wenxiu seized Qinzhou, Emperor Xuanzong ordered the Fengxiang army against him, and they besieged the city. Ai Shen was then in the army and had committed a capital offense. The emperor asked the privy marshal, and one who knew him reported that the man had the talent of a general and was loyal and dependable. The emperor sent a hurried pardon and made him military commissioner of Defu, acting head of the marshal's headquarters. In the spring of the fourth year of Zhengda the great army came from the west, intending to make Defu their summer camp. Defu had no army, and the situation was desperate. Ai Shen knew that Ma Jianlong Shunqing of Fengxiang was a man to consult. He sent a letter summoning him. Jianlong wished to go, but Fengxiang overseer Hesujia Guojian urged him not to: the great army was advancing, their own city could be held, but Defu could not be defended. Jianlong said, "Ai Shen never knew me in his life, yet at one meeting he called me his friend. I know Defu cannot be held and that if I go I shall surely die — yet for the sake of that friendship. I cannot but die for him. " He gave his traveling bag to a clansman elder, plainly taking leave as for death, and set out through peril. Once he arrived, the city was besieged within days. Only eight or nine thousand militia and local troops remained inside, while the great army brought the full weight of the realm against them. Ai Shen borrowed Shunqing from the Fengxiang overseer's staff as judge, and they shared the defense together. The siege lasted one hundred twenty days and nights. When strength was exhausted the city fell. Ai Shen ran himself through with a sword. He was fifty-three. The army offered a reward to take Jianlong alive, but his fate was never known. Censors and remonstrance officials urged that offices be granted to those who had died for Defu, to encourage loyalty throughout the realm. An edict granted each a posthumous office and enshrined them in the Hall of Rewarding Loyalty.

馬肩龍 (附)
Ma Jianlong (Supplementary biography)
38
肩龍字舜卿，宛平人。 先世遼大族，有知興中府者，故人號興中馬氏。 祖大中，金初登科，節度全、錦兩州。 父成誼，明昌五年登科，仕為京兆府路統軍司判官。 肩龍在太學有賦聲。 宣宗初，有誣宗室從坦殺人，將置之死。 人不敢言其冤，肩龍上書，大略謂：「從坦有將帥材，少出其右者，臣一介書生，無用于世，願代從坦死，留為天子將兵。 」書奏，詔問：「汝與從坦交分厚歟？ 」肩龍對曰：「臣知有從坦，從坦未嘗識臣。 從坦冤人，不敢言，臣以死保之。 」宣宗感悟，赦從坦，授肩龍東平錄事，委行省試驗。 宰相侯摯與語不契，留數月罷歸，將渡河，與排岸官紛競，搜篋中，得軍馬糧料名數及利害數事，疑其為奸人偵伺者，系歸德獄根勘。 適從坦至，立救出之。 正大三年，客鳳翔，元帥愛申深器重之，至是，同死於難。
Jianlong, courtesy name Shunqing, came from Wanping. His forebears were a great clan of Liao. One had governed Xingzhong Prefecture, and people called them the Ma clan of Xingzhong. His grandfather Dazhong passed the examinations at the founding of Jin and served as military commissioner of Quan and Jin. His father Chengyi passed the examinations in the fifth year of Mingchang and served as judge of the Jingzhao Circuit commandery-in-chief office. At the Imperial Academy Jianlong was known for his fu compositions. Early in Emperor Xuanzong's reign, the imperial clansman Congtan was falsely accused of murder and faced execution. No one dared speak of his innocence. Jianlong submitted a memorial saying, in substance, "Congtan has the talent of a general; few surpass him. I am a mere scholar, of no use to the world — I wish to die in his place and remain to lead troops for the Son of Heaven. " When the memorial arrived, the emperor asked, "Are you and Congtan close friends? " Jianlong replied, "I know of Congtan, but Congtan has never known me. Congtan is innocent, yet none dares speak. I guarantee him with my life. " The emperor was moved, pardoned Congtan, appointed Jianlong recorder of Dongping, and referred him to the Branch Secretariat for examination. Chief councilor Hou Zhi took no liking to him in conversation. After several months he was dismissed and sent home. About to cross the river, he quarreled with a shore patrol officer who searched his bag and found lists of army horses, grain supplies, and several items of strategic intelligence. Suspecting him a spy, they imprisoned him in Guide for investigation. Congtan happened to arrive and rescued him at once. In the third year of Zhengda he was a guest at Fengxiang, where Marshal Ai Shen held him in high regard. In the end they died together in the siege.

禹顯，雁門人。 貞祐初，隸上党公張開，累以戰功授義勝軍節度使、兼沁州招撫副使。 元光二年四月，大帥達兒泬、按察兒攻河東，張開遣顯扼龍豬穀，夾攻敗之，擒元帥韓光國，獲輜重甲仗甚眾，追至祁縣而還，所曆州縣悉複之。 顯將軍三百人，守襄垣，八年不遷。 大帥嘗集河朔步騎數萬攻之，至於數四不能拔。 既而，戰于玉女寨，大獲。 開言于朝，權元帥右都監。 正大六年冬十二月，軍內變，城破被擒。 帥義之，不欲加害。 初以鐵繩鈐之，既而密與舊部曲二十人遁去，聞上黨公軍複振，將往從之。 大兵四向來追，顯適與負釜一兵相失，乞飯山寺中，僧走報焉，被執不屈死，時年四十一。
Yu Xian came from Yanmen. At the beginning of Zhenyou he served under Duke Shangdang Zhang Kai. Repeated battle honors made him military commissioner of the Yisheng Army and vice pacification commissioner of Qinzhou. In the fourth month of the second year of Yuanguang the great generals Da'erba and Ancha'er attacked Hedong. Zhang Kai sent Xian to hold Longzhu Valley. A pincer attack routed them, capturing marshal Han Guangguo and vast stores of baggage, armor, and weapons. They pursued to Qi County and returned, recovering every prefecture and county along the way. Xian held Xiangyuan with three hundred men and received no transfer for eight years. The enemy marshal once gathered tens of thousands of infantry and cavalry from north of the river to attack — four times without success. Soon afterward they fought at Jade Maiden Stockade and won a great victory. Kai reported to court, and Xian was made acting marshal's right overseer. In the twelfth month of the sixth year of Zhengda the army mutinied. The city fell and he was captured. The enemy commander admired his spirit and did not wish to harm him. At first they shackled him with iron chains. Before long he fled secretly with twenty old retainers, intending to join Duke Shangdang's revived army. The enemy pursued from four directions. Xian lost contact with a soldier carrying a cooking pot and begged food at a mountain temple. A monk ran to report him. Seized, he would not yield and was killed. He was forty-one.

Zhang Bangxian
41
秦州人張邦憲，字正叔，登正大中進士第，為永固令。 天興二年，避兵徐州。 卓翼率兵至城，邦憲被執，將驅之北，邦憲罵曰：「我進士也，誤蒙朝廷用為邑長，可從汝曹反耶！ 」遂遇害。
Zhang Bangxian of Qinzhou, courtesy name Zhengshu, passed the jinshi examination in the middle Zhengda period and served as magistrate of Yonggu. In the second year of Tianxing he fled the war to Xuzhou. Zhuo Yi led troops to the city. Seized and about to be driven north, Bangxian cursed them: "I am a jinshi graduate whom the court wrongly made a district chief — how could I follow you rebels! " He was then killed.

劉全者，彭城民也。 率鄉鄰數百避兵沫溝，推為砦主。 北兵至徐，盡俘其老幼，全父亦在其中，北兵質之以招全，全縛其人送徐州，因竊其父以歸。 徐帥益都嘉其忠，承制以為昭信校尉，遙領彭城縣尉。 後遇國用安，怒其不附己，見殺。
Liu Quan was a commoner of Pengcheng. He led several hundred neighbors to flee the war to Mogou Ravine, where they made him stockade chief. When northern troops reached Xu they captured all the old and young, including Quan's father, whom they held hostage to lure Quan. Quan bound their men and sent them to Xuzhou, then secretly brought his father home. The Xu commander Yidu praised his loyalty and, by provisional order, made him Colonel of Manifest Trust with the absentee post of Pengcheng county lieutenant. Later he encountered Guo Yong'an, who resented his refusal to submit and had him killed.
