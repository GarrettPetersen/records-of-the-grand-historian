1
古書「畔」與「叛」通，畔之為言界也。 《左氏》曰，政猶「農之有畔」，是也。 君臣上下之定分，猶此疆彼界之截然，違此向彼，即為叛矣。 善惡判于跬步，禍患極於懷襄，吁，可畏哉！ 作《叛臣傳》。
In ancient texts the characters pan meaning 'border' and pan meaning 'rebel' were used interchangeably, because pan in the border sense denotes a boundary. The Zuo Commentary observes that governing a state is like a farmer keeping to his furrows — and so it is. The settled order between ruler and subject, superior and inferior, is as clear-cut as one border against another; to abandon this side for that is to rebel. Right and wrong may turn on a single step, and calamity may swell to the scale of the Huai and Xiang deluges — how dreadful! Hence this "Biographies of Rebel Ministers."
2
張覺，亦書作鐧，平州義豐人也。 在遼第進士，仕至遼興軍節度副使。 太祖定燕京，時立愛以平州降，當時宋人以海上之盟求燕京及西京地，太祖以燕京、涿、易、檀、順、景、薊與之。 平州自入契丹別為一軍，故弗與，而以平州為南京，覺為留守。 既而聞覺有異志，上遣使劉彥宗及斜缽諭之，詔曰：「平山一郡今為南京，節度使今為留守。 恩亦厚矣。 或言汝等陰有異圖，何為當此農時輒相扇動，非去危就安之計也。 其諭朕意。 」太祖每收城邑，往往徙其民以實京師，民心多不安，故時立愛因降表曾言及之。 及以燕京與宋而遷其人，獨以空城與之，遷者道出平州，故覺因之以作亂。 天輔七年五月，左企弓、虞仲文、曹勇義、康公弼赴廣甯，過平州，覺使人殺之于栗林下，遂據南京叛入于宋，宋人納之。
Zhang Jue — the name is also written Zhang Jian — came from Yifeng in Ping Prefecture. He had passed the Liao jinshi examination and served as vice military commissioner of the Liaoxing Army circuit. After the Taizu Emperor took Yanjing, Shi Li'ai surrendered Ping Prefecture. The Song, citing the maritime alliance, demanded Yanjing and the Western Capital lands; the Taizu Emperor yielded Yanjing along with Zhuo, Yi, Tan, Shun, Jing, and Ji. Ping Prefecture had become a separate military district under the Khitan and was therefore withheld; it was made the Southern Capital instead, with Jue as military governor. When reports reached the court that Jue was disloyal, the emperor dispatched Liu Yanzong and Xiebo to admonish him. The edict read: "The Pingshan region is now the Southern Capital, and your former title of military commissioner is now military governor. Our favor toward you has been ample. Some claim you are plotting in secret. Why stir unrest in the middle of the planting season? That is no way to trade peril for security. Make our intent known to them." Whenever the Taizu Emperor captured a city he tended to relocate its population to bolster the capital, which unsettled the people — a grievance Shi Li'ai had raised in his surrender memorial. When Yanjing was ceded to the Song its inhabitants were uprooted, leaving an empty city behind. The displaced traveled through Ping Prefecture, and Jue seized the moment to rebel. In the fifth month of Tianfu 7 (1123), Zuo Qigong, Yu Zhongwen, Cao Yongyi, and Kang Gongbi were bound for Guangning and passed through Ping. Jue had them ambushed in the chestnut grove, seized the Southern Capital, and defected to the Song, who welcomed him.
3
太祖下詔諭南京官吏，詔曰：「朕初駐蹕燕京，嘉爾吏民率先降附，故升府治以為南京，減徭役，薄賦稅，恩亦至矣，何苦輒為叛逆？ 今欲進兵攻取，時方農月，不忍以一惡人而害及眾庶。 且遼國舉為我有，孤城自守，終欲何為？ 今止坐首惡，餘並釋之。」
The Taizu Emperor addressed the Southern Capital officials: "When I first encamped at Yanjing I commended you for leading the surrender, elevated your prefecture to the Southern Capital, eased corvée burdens, and lightened taxes. Our kindness has been full. Why court rebellion? I am ready to march and take the city, yet it is planting season and I will not let one villain bring ruin on the common people. The Liao realm is already mine. What end can a lone stronghold serve? Only the ringleader will be punished; the rest shall go free."
4
覺兵五萬屯潤州近郊，欲脅遷、來、潤、隰四州。 闍母自錦州往討之，已敗覺兵，欲乘勝攻南京，時暑雨不可進，退屯於海堧。 無何，闍母再敗覺兵，復與戰於兔耳山，闍母大敗，覺報捷于宋。 宋建平州為泰寧軍，以覺為節度使，張敦固等皆加徽猶閣待制，以銀絹數萬犒軍。
Jue massed fifty thousand troops near Run Prefecture, aiming to intimidate Qian, Lai, Run, and Xi into submission. Zhamu marched from Jin Prefecture, routed Jue's force, and prepared to storm the Southern Capital, but summer rains stalled the advance and he fell back to camp on the coastal levee. Soon afterward Zhamu beat Jue again, but at Rabbit-Ear Mountain Zhamu was routed in turn. Jue sent word of victory to the Song court. The Song reorganized Ping as the Taiping Army circuit, named Jue its military commissioner, promoted Zhang Dun'gu and others to Huayou Pavilion academicians, and sent tens of thousands in silver and silk to reward the troops.
5
宗望軍至南京城東，覺兵大敗宵遁，遂奔宋，入於燕京。 宗望以納叛責宋宣撫司，索張覺。 宣撫王安中匿之於甲仗庫，紿曰：「無之。 」宗望索愈急，安中乃斬貌類覺者一人當之，金人識之曰：「非覺也。 」安中不得已，引覺出。 數以罪，覺罵宋人不容口，遂殺覺函其首以與金人。 燕京降將及常勝軍皆泣下，郭藥師自言曰：「若來索藥師當奈何？ 」自是，降將卒皆解體。 及金人伐宋，竟以納平州之叛為執言雲。 子僅言。
When Zongwang's army reached the eastern approaches of the Southern Capital, Jue's force was shattered and fled under cover of night. He escaped to the Song and took refuge in Yanjing. Zongwang accused the Song Pacification Commission of sheltering a rebel and demanded Zhang Jue's surrender. Pacification Commissioner Wang Anzhong hid him in the armory and lied, "He is not here. "As Zongwang pressed harder, Anzhong executed a look-alike and presented the head. The Jurchens recognized the deception: "That is not Jue. "Forced to comply, Anzhong produced Jue. After listing his crimes, Jue poured unceasing abuse on the Song. They executed him, packed his head in a box, and handed it to the Jurchens. Yanjing's surrendered commanders and the Ever-Victorious Army wept openly. Guo Yaoshi muttered, "If they come for me next, what then? "From that day the morale of every surrendered officer and soldier collapsed. When the Jurchens later invaded the Song, they cited the sheltering of the Ping Prefecture rebel as their justification. His son Jinyan.

子僅言
Son: Jinyan
7
僅言幼名元奴。 宗望攻下平山，僅言在繈褓間，裏人劉承宣得之，養於家。 其鄰韓夫人甚愛之。 年數歲，因隨韓夫人得見貞懿皇后。 留之籓邸，稍長，侍世宗讀書，遂使僅言主家事，繩檢部曲，一府憚之。
Jinyan's childhood name was Yuannu. When Zongwang took Pingshan, Jinyan was still an infant. A neighbor, Liu Chengxuan, took him in and raised him. A neighbor, Lady Han, doted on the boy. At the age of a few years he accompanied Lady Han to an audience with Empress Zhenyi. She kept him at the princely residence. As he grew he attended Prince Shizong's studies, then put Jinyan in charge of the household, tightening discipline over the retainers until the entire mansion stood in awe of him.
8
僅言能心計，世宗倚任之，凡宮室營造、府庫出納、行幸頓舍皆委之。 世宗嘗曰：「一經僅言，無不愜朕意者。 」六年，提舉修內役事，役夫掘地得白金匿之，事覺，法當死，僅言責取其物與官，釋其罪。 尋兼祗應司。 遷少府監，提控宮籍監、祗應司如故。 護作太甯宮，引宮左流泉溉田，歲獲稻萬斛。 十七年，複提點內藏，典領昭德皇后山陵，遷勸農使，領諸職如故。
Jinyan had a keen head for accounts, and the Shizong Emperor relied on him for palace construction, treasury affairs, and every detail of imperial travel and lodging. The Shizong Emperor once remarked, "Whatever passes through Jinyan's hands suits me perfectly. "In year 6 he oversaw inner-palace repairs. Laborers unearthed hidden silver and kept it secret. When the case surfaced the statute demanded death, but Jinyan made them turn the metal over to the treasury and spared their lives. He was soon given concurrent charge of the Attendance Office. He rose to director of the Palace Workshops while retaining supervision of the Palace Registry and Attendance offices. Supervising construction of Taining Palace, he channeled a spring west of the palace to irrigate fields that yielded ten thousand bushels of rice a year. In year 17 he again oversaw the inner treasury, directed Empress Zhaode's tomb works, was promoted to Commissioner for Encouraging Agriculture, and kept his other posts.
9
僅言雖舊臣，出入左右，然世宗終不假以權任。 二十一年，尚書省奏，宮苑司直長黎倫在職十六年，請與遷敘。 上曰：「此朕之家臣，質直人也，今已老矣。 如勸農使張僅言亦朕舊臣，純實頗解事，凡朝廷議論，內外除授，未嘗得干預。 朕觀自古人君為讒諂蒙蔽者多矣，朕雖不及古人，然近習憸言未嘗入耳。 」宰臣曰：「誠如聖訓，此國家之福也。 」世宗欲以為橫海軍節度使，而不可去左右，遂止。
Though Jinyan was a longtime companion who attended him constantly, the Shizong Emperor never gave him substantive authority. In year 21 the Secretariat reported that Li Lun, chief clerk of the Palace Parks Office, had served sixteen years and sought a promotion. The emperor said, "He is a household retainer of mine, plain and honest, and he is old now. Commissioner for Encouraging Agriculture Zhang Jinyan is another old retainer — sincere and capable — yet he has never been permitted to meddle in court debates or civil and military appointments. I see that many rulers of old were blinded by flatterers and slanderers. I may not match the ancients, but the honeyed words of favorites have never reached my ears. "The chief ministers replied, "Your words are true — this is the state's good fortune. "The emperor considered appointing him military commissioner of the Henghai Army but could not part with his constant presence and let the matter drop.
10
僅言始得疾，猶扶杖視事，疾亟，詔太醫診視，近侍問訊相屬。 及卒，上深惜之，遣官致祭，賻銀五百兩、重彩十端、絹二百匹，棺槨、衣衾、銀汞、斂物、葬地皆官給，贈輔國上將軍。
When Jinyan first fell ill he still leaned on his staff to work. As the sickness worsened the emperor ordered imperial physicians to attend him, and close attendants came in steady succession to inquire after him. At his death the emperor grieved deeply, dispatched officials to offer sacrifices, and granted five hundred taels of silver, ten bolts of fine silk, and two hundred bolts of plain silk. Coffin, shroud, silver grave goods, encoffining supplies, and burial ground were all provided by the state, and he was posthumously enfeoffed Supporter-of-the-State Grand General.

耶律余睹
Yelü Yudu
12
太祖已取臨潢府，賜詔余睹曰：「汝將兵在東路，前後戰未嘗不敗。 今聞汝收合散亡，以拒我師。 朕已於今月十五日克上京，今將往取遼主矣。 汝若治兵一決勝負，可指地期日相報。 若知不敵，當率眾來降，無貽後悔。 」及太祖班師，闍母等還至遼河，方渡，余睹來襲，完顏背答、烏塔等殿，力戰卻之，獲甲馬五百匹。
After the Taizu Emperor captured Linhuang, he sent Yudu an edict: "You command the eastern route, and battle after battle you have never won. Now I hear you are rallying the scattered remnants to resist my armies. On the fifteenth of this month I took the Upper Capital, and I am now marching to capture the Liao emperor. If you wish to muster your forces and settle matters in a single battle, name the place and day and send word. If you know you cannot prevail, lead your men in surrender before regret overtakes you. "When the Taizu Emperor withdrew, Zhamu and the others recrossed the Liao River. Midway across, Yudu struck. Wanyan Beida, Wuta, and others held the rear, fought him off, and seized five hundred sets of armor and mounts.
13
天輔五年，余睹送款于咸州路都統，以所部來降，乞援接于桑林渡。 都統司以聞，詔曰：「余睹到日，使與其官屬偕來，餘眾處之便地。 」無何，余睹送上所受遼國宣誥，及器甲旗幟等，與將吏韓福奴、阿八、謝老、太師奴、蕭慶、醜和尚、高佛留、蒲答、謝家奴、五哥等來降。
In Tianfu 5 Yudu submitted his allegiance to the Xianzhou circuit commander, came over with his command, and asked to be received at Sanglin Ford. The commander's office reported to court. The edict read: "When Yudu arrives, let him come with his officers; settle the rest of his followers where convenient. "Shortly afterward Yudu presented the Liao commissions and patents in his possession, along with arms, armor, banners, and standards, and surrendered with his officers Han Funu, Aba, Xie Lao, Taishinu, Xiao Qing, Chouheshang, Gao Foliu, Puda, Xiejianu, Wuge, and the rest.
14
余睹作書，具言所以降之意，大概以謂：「遼主沉湎荒于遊畋，不恤政事，好佞人，遠忠直，淫刑吝賞，政煩賦重，民不聊生。 」又言：「樞密使得裏底本無材能，但阿諛取容，其子磨哥任以軍事。 」又言：「文妃長子晉王素系人望，宜為儲副，得裏底以元妃諸子己所自出，使晉王出繼文妃。 」又言：「晉王與駙馬乙信謀複其樞密使，來告余睹共定大計，而所圖不成。 」又言：「己粗更軍事，進策遼主，得裏底蔽之，遼主亦不省察。 」又曰：「大金疆土日辟，余睹灼知天命，遂自去年春與耶律慎思等定議，約以今夏來降。 近聞得裏底、高十捏等欲發，倉卒之際不及收合四遠，但率傍近部族戶三千、車五千兩、畜產數萬、遼北軍都統以兵追襲，遂棄輜重，轉戰至此。 所有官事職位姓名、人戶畜產之數，遣韓福奴具錄以聞。 」遂與其將吏來見，上撫慰之，遂賜坐，班同宰相，賜宴盡醉而罷。 上命余睹以舊官領所部。 且諭之曰：「若能為國立功，別當獎用。 」自余睹降，益知遼人虛實矣。
Yudu wrote at length explaining why he had surrendered. In essence he said: "The Liao ruler is sunk in pleasure and idle hunting, neglects government, favors flatterers and keeps loyal men at bay, imposes cruel punishments yet is stingy with rewards, and burdens the people with heavy taxes until they cannot survive. "He added that Bureau chief Delidi had no ability and survived only by flattery, while his son Moge had been put in charge of military affairs. "He said the Prince of Jin, eldest son of Consort Wen, enjoyed wide esteem and should have been heir, but Delidi — whose own sons were born to the Primary Consort — had the prince adopted to Consort Wen instead. "He reported that the Prince of Jin and imperial son-in-law Yixin had plotted to restore the prince's bureau post, sought Yudu's help to carry out a larger design, and failed. "He said he had reorganized military affairs and offered counsel to the Liao emperor, but Delidi suppressed it and the emperor never looked into the matter. "He declared that as the Jin realm expanded daily he saw Heaven's mandate clearly, and from the previous spring had agreed with Yelü Shensi and others to surrender that summer. Learning recently that Delidi and Gao Shinie were about to strike, he had no time to rally distant tribes and led only three thousand nearby households, five thousand carts, and vast herds. The Northern Liao army commander pursued, forcing him to abandon his baggage train and fight his way here. Han Funu was dispatched to record and report every office, name, household, and head of livestock in detail. "He then came to audience with his officers. The emperor received him kindly, seated him in the rank of chief ministers, and feasted the company until all were drunk. The emperor ordered Yudu to command his following under his former title. He added, "Serve the state well and further rewards will follow. "After Yudu's surrender the court understood the Liao's true weakness all the more clearly.
15
余睹在軍中屢乞侍妾及子，太祖疑之，詔咸州路都統司曰：「余睹家屬，善監護之。 」複詔曰：「余睹降時，其民多強率而來者，恐在邊生變，宜徙之內地。 」都統杲取中京，余睹為鄉導，與希尹等招撫奚部。 奉聖州降，其官吏皆遁去，余睹舉前監酒李師夔為節度使，進士沈璋為副使，州吏裴賾為觀察判官。 沈璋招集居民還業者三千餘，遷太常少卿。
In camp Yudu repeatedly asked for concubines and his son. The Taizu Emperor grew suspicious and ordered the Xianzhou commander's office, "Guard Yudu's family closely. "A second edict warned, "Many of his people followed under coercion when he surrendered. Move them inland lest they stir trouble on the frontier. "When Commander Gao took the Central Capital, Yudu guided the advance and, with Xiyin and others, pacified the Xi tribes. When Fengsheng Prefecture surrendered its officials fled. Yudu nominated the former wine supervisor Li Shikui as military commissioner, jinshi Shen Zhang as vice commissioner, and prefectural clerk Pei Ze as administrative judge. Shen Zhang brought back more than three thousand residents to their livelihoods and was promoted to vice director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices.
16
久之，耶律麻者告余睹、吳十、劉剌結黨謀叛，及其未發宜先收捕。 上召余睹等從容謂之曰：「今聞汝謀叛，誠然邪，其各無隱。 若果去，必須鞍馬甲胄器械之屬，當悉付汝，吾不食言。 若再被擒，無祈免死。 欲留事我，則無懷異志，吾不汝疑。 」余睹等戰慄不能對，乃杖鐸剌七十，餘皆不問。
Eventually Yelü Mazhe reported that Yudu, Wu Shi, and Liu La were plotting rebellion together and should be arrested before they acted. The emperor summoned Yudu and the others and said calmly, "I hear you are plotting rebellion. Is it so? Speak plainly. If you truly mean to go, you will need horses, armor, and arms — I will furnish them all. I do not break my word. If you are captured again, do not expect mercy. If you wish to stay and serve me, harbor no second thoughts and I will not doubt you. "Yudu and the others trembled and could not speak. Duo La alone was beaten seventy strokes; the rest went unpunished.
17
天會三年，大舉伐宋，余睹為元帥右都監，宋兵四萬救太原，余睹、屋里海逆擊於汾河北，擒其帥郝仲連、張關索，統制馬忠，殺萬餘人。 宗翰伐宋，余睹留西京。 天會十年，余睹謀反，雲內節度使耶律奴哥等告之。 余睹亡去，其黨燕京統軍蕭高六伏誅，蔚州節度使蕭特謀自殺。 邊部斬余睹及其諸子，函其首以獻。 耶律奴哥加守太保兼侍中，趙公鑒、劉儒信、劉君輔等並授遙鎮節度使以賞之。
In Tianhui 3 the Jin launched a major invasion of the Song. Yudu served as right overseer under the grand marshal. Forty thousand Song troops marched to relieve Taiyuan; Yudu and Wulihai met them north of the Fen River, captured commanders Hao Zhonglian and Zhang Guansuo and controller Ma Zhong, and killed more than ten thousand. When Zonghan invaded the Song, Yudu stayed behind at the Western Capital. In Tianhui 10 Yudu plotted rebellion. Yunnei military commissioner Yelü Nuge and others exposed the plot. Yudu fled. His confederate Yanjing army commander Xiao Gao Liu was executed, and Weizhou military commissioner Xiao Te tried to take his own life. Frontier forces hunted down Yudu and his sons, boxed their heads, and sent them to court. Yelü Nuge was promoted to acting Grand Guardian and concurrent Palace Secretariat director. Zhao Gongjian, Liu Ruxin, Liu Junfu, and others received distant military commissions as rewards.
18
移剌窩斡，西北路契丹部族。 先從撒八為亂，受其偽署，後殺撒八，遂有其眾。
Yila Wo Wo belonged to the Khitan tribes of the Northwestern Route. He first followed Saba's rebellion, accepted a post in Saba's regime, then killed Saba and took over his followers.
19
撒八者，初為招討司譯史。 正隆五年，海陵征諸道兵伐宋，使牌印燥合、楊葛盡征西北路契丹丁壯，契丹人曰：「西北路接近鄰國，世世征伐，相為仇怨。 若男丁盡從軍，彼以兵來，則老弱必盡系累矣。 幸使者入朝言之。 」燥合畏罪不敢言，楊葛深念後西北有事得罪，遂以憂死。 燥合複與牌印耶律娜、尚書省令史沒答涅合督起西北路兵。 契丹聞男丁當盡起，於是撒八、孛特補與部眾殺招討使完顏沃側及燥合，而執耶律娜、沒答涅合，取招討司貯甲三千，遂反。 議立豫王延禧子孫，眾推都監老和尚為招討使，山后四群牧、山前諸群牧皆應之。 迪斡群牧使徒單賽裏、耶魯瓦群牧使鶴壽等皆遇害，語在《鶴壽傳》中。 五院司部人老和尚那也亦殺節度使術甲兀者以應撒八。
Saba had begun as a translator-clerk in the Pacification Commission. In Zhenglong 5 the Hailing Emperor mobilized troops from every circuit for the Song campaign and sent Paoyin Zaohe and Yang Ge to draft every able Khitan man on the Northwestern Route. The Khitan protested: "Our northwestern frontier borders enemy states. For generations we have fought them and nursed grudges. If every fighting man is marched away and they attack, our elders, women, and children will all be taken captive. We beg the envoy to go to court and report this for us. " Zaohe feared punishment and kept silent. Yang Ge Shen, dreading that he would be held accountable when trouble broke out in the northwest, died of anxiety. Zaohe again joined Paoyin Yelü Na and Ministry of Works clerk Modaniehe in mustering Northwestern Route forces. Learning that every fighting man was to be drafted, Saba and Letebu led their tribesmen in killing Pacification Commissioner Wanyan Woce and Zaohe, seizing Yelü Na and Modaniehe, stripping three thousand suits of armor from the Pacification Commission stores, and rising in rebellion. They debated placing a descendant of Prince Yu and Emperor Yanshi on the throne. The rebels acclaimed Supervisor Old Monk as pacification commissioner, and the four pasture offices south of the mountains and all the pasture offices to the north joined them. Diwo Pasture Commissioner Tushan Saili, Yeluwa Pasture Commissioner He Shou, and others were killed; their story is told in the "Biography of He Shou." Old Monk Naye of the Wuyuan Office tribes likewise killed Military Commissioner Shujia Wuzhe to join Saba's cause.
20
會甯八猛安牧馬於山后，至迪謀魯，賊盡奪其馬。 辟沙河千戶十哥等與前招討使完顏麻潑殺烏古迪列招討使烏林答蒲盧虎，以所部趨西北路。 室魯部節度使阿廝列追擊敗之，十哥與數騎遁去，合於撒八。
Ningba Meng'an happened to be grazing horses in the southern hills. At Dimolu the rebels stripped him of every mount. Bisha River chiliarch Shige and others joined former Pacification Commissioner Wanyan Mapo in killing Ughudilie Pacification Commissioner Ulin Da Puluhu, then marched their bands toward the Northwestern Route. Shilu Tribal Military Commissioner Asilie pursued and routed them. Shige escaped with a handful of riders and rejoined Saba.
21
咸平府謀克括裏，與所部自山后逃歸，咸平少尹完顏餘裏野欲收捕括裏家屬，括裏與其党招誘富家奴隸，數日得眾二千，遂攻陷韓州及柳河縣，遂趨咸平。 餘裏野發兵迎擊之，兵敗，賊遂據咸平，於是繕完器甲，出府庫財物以募兵，賊勢益張。 權曹家山猛安綽質，集兵千餘，扼幹夜河，賊不得東。 綽質兵敗，括裏遂犯濟州。 會宿直將軍孛術魯吳括剌徵兵于速頻路，遇括裏於信州，與猛安烏延查剌兵二千，擊敗括裏。 括裏收余眾趨東京，是時世宗為東京留守，以兵四百人拒之。 賊至常安縣，聞空中擊鼓聲如數千鼓者，候見旌旗蔽野，傳言留守以十萬兵至矣，即引還，亦以其眾合於撒八。
Xianping Prefecture company commander Kuoli fled back from the southern hills with his band. When Xianping Vice Prefect Wanyan Yuliye moved to arrest Kuoli's kin, Kuoli and his confederates enticed slaves from wealthy households. Within days they mustered two thousand men, seized Han Prefecture and Liuhe County, and marched on Xianping. Yuliye marched out to intercept them but was defeated. The rebels took Xianping, repaired arms and armor, emptied the treasury to enlist men, and their strength swelled. Acting Caojiashan Meng'an Chuo Zhi mustered more than a thousand men and held the Ganye River, blocking the rebels' eastward advance. Chuo Zhi was defeated, and Kuoli turned to attack Ji Prefecture. Palace Duty General Beshilu Wuguala was raising troops on the Supin Route when he met Kuoli at Xin Prefecture. With Meng'an Wuyan Zhala and two thousand soldiers he routed Kuoli. Kuoli regrouped his survivors and struck for the Eastern Capital. Shizong, then military governor of the Eastern Capital, opposed him with four hundred men. At Chang'an County the rebels heard drumbeats in the air as if thousands were sounding together. Scouts reported banners blotting out the plain and rumor that the military governor had come with a hundred thousand men. They immediately retreated and merged their forces with Saba's.
22
海陵使樞密使僕散忽土、西京留守蕭懷忠將兵一萬，與右衛將軍蕭禿剌討平之。 禿剌與之相持數日，連與戰皆無功，而糧餉不繼，禿剌退歸臨潢。 禿剌雖不能克敵，而撒八自度大軍必相繼而至，勢不可支，謀歸於大石，乃率眾沿龍駒河西出。 及僕散忽土、蕭懷忠等兵至，與禿剌合兵追至河上，不及而還。 忽土、懷忠、禿剌坐逗遛不即追賊，皆誅死。 北京留守蕭賾不能制其下，殺降人而取其婦女，亦坐誅。 於是，白彥恭為北面兵馬都統，紇石烈志寧副之，守顏彀英為西北面兵馬都統，西北路招討使唐括孛姑的副之，以討撒八等。
The Hailing Emperor dispatched Privy Councilor Pusan Hutu and Western Capital Military Governor Xiao Huaizhong with ten thousand troops, along with Right Guard General Xiao Tula, to crush the rebellion. Tula skirmished with them for days without success. When provisions failed he fell back to Linhuang. Though Tula could not break the rebels, Saba judged that imperial armies would keep coming and that he could not hold out. He resolved to join Dashi and led his followers west along the Longju River. Pusan Hutu and Xiao Huaizhong arrived and united with Tula to pursue the rebels to the river, but failed to catch them and turned back. Hutu, Huaizhong, and Tula were condemned for dallying instead of pressing the pursuit and were all put to death. Northern Capital Military Governor Xiao Ze failed to restrain his men, who murdered surrendered rebels and seized their women. He too was executed. Bai Yangong was appointed northern frontier commander-in-chief with Heishilie Zhining as deputy. Shouyan Gouying became northwestern frontier commander-in-chief with Northwestern Route Pacification Commissioner Tanggu Bogede as deputy. They were sent to suppress Saba and his allies.
23
撒八既西行，而舊居山前者皆不欲往，偽署六院節度使移刺窩斡、兵官陳家殺撒八，執老和尚、孛特補等。 至是，窩斡始自為都元帥，陳家為都監，擁眾東還，至臨潢府東南新羅寨。 世宗使移剌紮八、前押軍謀克播斡、前牌印麻駭、利涉軍節度判官馬腦等招之。 紮八等見窩斡，以上意諭之。 窩斡已約降，已而複謂紮八曰：「若降，爾能保我輩無事乎？ 」紮八曰：「我知招降耳，其他豈能必哉。」
Once Saba marched west, his followers from the northern country refused to follow. The rebels' Six-District Military Commissioner Yila Wo Wo and military officer Chen Jia killed Saba and seized Old Monk, Letebu, and the rest. Wo Wo then declared himself grand marshal and made Chen Jia chief supervisor. He led his followers back east as far as Xinluo Stockade southeast of Linhuang. Shizong dispatched Yila Zhahe, former Escort Army company commander Bowo, former Paoyin Mahai, and Lishe Army commissioner judge Ma Nao to offer terms of surrender. Zhahe and his party met Wo Wo and conveyed the emperor's message. Wo Wo had already agreed to submit, but then asked Zhahe, "If we surrender, can you guarantee our safety? " Zhahe replied, "My charge is to offer surrender. Beyond that I can promise nothing."
24
紮八見窩斡兵眾強，車帳滿野，意其可以有成，因說之曰：「我之始來，以汝輩不能有為，今觀兵勢強盛如此，汝等欲如群羊為人所驅去乎，將欲待天時乎？ 若果有大志，吾亦不復還矣。 」賊將有前孛特本部族節度使逐斡者，言：「昔谷神丞相，賢能人也，嘗說他日西北部族當有事。 今日正合此語，恐不可降也。 」於是，窩斡遂決意不復肯降矣。 紮八亦留賊中，惟麻駭、播斡還歸。 窩斡乃引兵攻臨潢府，總管移室懣出城戰，兵少被執，賊遂圍臨潢，眾至五萬。 正隆六年十二月己亥，窩斡遂稱帝，改元天正。
Seeing Wo Wo's strength — carts and tents covering the plain — Zhahe decided the rebels might yet prevail and urged him: "When I arrived I thought you could achieve nothing. Yet your host is this powerful. Will you go like sheep driven by others, or wait upon Heaven's moment? If you truly mean to fight on, I will not return either. " Rebel general Zhuwozhe, formerly Letebu's tribal military commissioner, spoke up: "Chancellor Gushen was a wise man. He once said the northwestern tribes would one day rise. Today his words have come true. I fear we must not surrender. " Wo Wo then made up his mind never to submit. Zhahe stayed with the rebels. Only Mahai and Bowo came back. Wo Wo marched on Linhuang. Commander Yishi Men sallied out to fight but was captured when his force proved too small. The rebels besieged the city until their numbers swelled to fifty thousand. On the jihai day of the twelfth month of Zhenglong 6, Wo Wo proclaimed himself emperor and adopted the era name Tianzheng.
25
是時，北面都統白彥敬、副統紇石烈志寧在北京，聞世宗即位，以兵來歸。 世宗使元帥左都監吾紮忽、同知北京留守事完顏骨只救臨潢，晝夜兼行，比至臨潢，賊已解圍去攻泰州。 吾紮忽追及於窊曆，兩軍已陣將戰，押軍猛安契丹忽剌叔以所部兵應賊，吾紮忽軍遂敗。
Northern Frontier Commander-in-Chief Bai Yanjing and Deputy Commander Heishilie Zhining were then at the Northern Capital. Learning of Shizong's accession, they marched south to join him. Shizong sent Grand Marshal Left Overseer Wuzhahu and Northern Capital co-governor Wanyan Guzhi to relieve Linhuang. They forced the march day and night, but when they arrived the rebels had already abandoned the siege and moved against Taizhou. Wuzhahu caught up with them at Walie. The two armies were forming for battle when Escort Army Meng'an Khitan Hulashu went over to the rebels with his command, and Wuzhahu's force was routed.
26
泰州節度使烏裏雅率千餘騎與窩斡遇，烏裏雅兵複敗，僅以數騎脫歸。 賊勢愈振，城中震駭，莫敢出戰。 賊四面登城，押軍猛安烏古孫阿裏補率軍士數人，各持刀以身率先循城擊賊力戰，斫刈甚眾，賊乃退走，城賴以完。 泰州司吏顏盞蒲查奏捷，除忠翊校尉，賜銀五十兩、重彩十端。
Taizhou Military Commissioner Wuliya met Wo Wo with more than a thousand cavalry but was beaten again and fled home with only a few companions. The rebels' momentum swelled. Terror gripped the city and no one dared sally forth. Rebels scaled the walls on every side. Escort Army Meng'an Wugusun Alibu led a handful of soldiers along the ramparts, blades in hand, cutting down many rebels until they withdrew. The city was saved. Taizhou clerk Yanshan Pucha reported the victory. He was made Loyal Assistant Commandant and rewarded with fifty taels of silver and ten bolts of fine silk.
27
二年正月，右副元帥完顏謀衍率諸軍北征窩斡。 二月壬戌詔曰：「應諸人若能於契丹賊中自拔歸者，更不問元初首從及被威脅之由，奴婢、良人罪無輕重並行免放。 曾有官職及糾率人眾來歸者，仍與官賞，依本品量材敘使。 其同來人各從所願處收系，有才能者亦與錄用。 內外官員郎君群牧直撒百姓人家驅奴、宮籍監人等，並放為良，亦從所願處收系，與免三年差役。 或能捕殺首領而歸者，准上施行，仍驗勞績約量遷賞。 如捕獲窩斡者，猛安加三品官授節度使，謀克加四品官授防禦使，庶人加五品官授刺史。 」詔曰：「尚書省，如節度防禦使捉獲窩斡者與世襲猛安，刺史捉獲者與世襲謀克，驅奴、宮籍監人亦與庶人同。 」複詔宰臣，遍諭將士，能捕殺窩斡者加特進、授真總管。
In the first month of year 2, Right Deputy Grand Marshal Wanyan Moyan Yan led the combined armies north against Wo Wo. On the renxu day of the second month an edict declared: "Anyone who breaks away from the Khitan rebels and returns shall not be questioned about original guilt, leadership, or coercion. Slaves and commoners alike, whatever their offense, shall be pardoned and freed. Former officials and those who bring followers back shall receive offices and rewards commensurate with their former rank and ability. Their companions may settle wherever they choose, and capable men among them shall be given posts. Inner and outer officials, langjun, pasture-household commoners, household slaves, palace-registry overseers, and the like shall all be freed to commoner status, registered where they choose, and exempted from corvée for three years. Those who capture or kill rebel leaders shall receive the rewards above, with promotion scaled to their deeds. Whoever captures Wo Wo shall receive a meng'an promotion of three ranks to military commissioner, a company commander promotion of four ranks to defense commissioner, or a commoner promotion of five ranks to prefect. " Another edict instructed the Ministry of Works: "A military or defense commissioner who captures Wo Wo shall receive an hereditary meng'an; a prefect who captures him shall receive an hereditary company command. Household slaves and palace-registry overseers shall be rewarded like commoners. " The chief ministers were further ordered to announce to every officer and soldier that whoever captured or killed Wo Wo would receive extraordinary promotion and appointment as commander-in-chief.
28
於是，括裏將犯韓州，聞元帥兵至，不戰遁去，將轉趨懿、宜州。 謀衍屯懿州慶雲縣，及屯川州武平縣，奏請糧運當遣人護送，兵仗乞選精良者付之。 詔以南征逃還軍士就往屯戍，如不足，量于富家簽調，就近地簽步軍，給仗護送糧運。 詔平章政事移剌元宜往泰州規措邊事。 前安遠大將軍斡裏嫋、猛安七斤、庶人阿裏葛、磨哥等自窩斡中來降，斡裏嫋、七斤加昭武大將軍，阿裏葛武義將軍，磨哥忠勇校尉。
Kuoli was marching on Han Prefecture when news of the grand marshal's approach sent him fleeing without a fight. He turned toward Yi and Yizhou. Moyan Yan encamped at Qingyun County in Yi Prefecture and Wuping County in Chuan Prefecture. He memorialized that grain convoys required escorts and asked that the best arms be issued to them. The court ordered soldiers who had deserted the southern campaign assigned to garrison duty. If their numbers fell short, levies would be drawn from wealthy households and local infantry conscripted nearby, armed and sent to guard the grain convoys. Grand Councilor Yila Yuanyi was ordered to Taizhou to manage frontier affairs. Former Anyuan General Woli Niao, Meng'an Qijin, commoners Alige and Moge, and others defected from Wo Wo's camp. Woli Niao and Qijin were made Illustrious Martial Generals, Alige Martial Righteousness General, and Moge Loyal Valor Commandant.
29
窩斡遂自泰州往攻濟州，欲邀糧運。 元帥完顏謀衍與右監軍完顏福壽、左都監吾紮忽合兵，甲士萬三千人，曷懶路總管徒單克甯、廣甯尹僕散渾坦、同知廣甯尹完顏岩雅、肇州防禦使唐括烏也為左翼，臨海節度使紇石烈志寧、曷速館節度使神土懣、同知北京留守完顏骨只、淄州刺史尼龐古鈔兀為右翼，至術虎崖，盡委輜重，士卒齎數日糧，輕騎襲之。
Wo Wo left Taizhou to strike Ji Prefecture, aiming to cut the grain route. Grand Marshal Wanyan Moyan Yan united with Right Overseer Wanyan Fushou and Left Overseer Wuzhahu — thirteen thousand armored men in all. Supin Route Commander Tushan Kening, Guangning Prefect Pusan Huntan, Guangning Vice Prefect Wanyan Yanya, and Zhao Defense Commissioner Tanggu Wuye led the left wing. Linhai Military Commissioner Heishilie Zhining, Yisuguan Military Commissioner Shen Tu Men, Northern Capital co-governor Wanyan Guzhi, and Zibo Prefect Nipanggu Chaowu led the right. At Shuhu Cliff they abandoned their baggage train, each man carrying several days' grain, and swept down in a light-cavalry raid.
30
糺椀群牧人契丹颭者，與其弟孛迭、挼剌，皆棄家自賊中來降。 糺者謂謀衍曰：「賊中馬肥健，官軍馬疲弱，此去賊八十里，比遇賊馬已憊。 賊輜重去此不遠，我攻之，賊必救其巢穴，賊至馬必疲，我馬少得息，所謂攻其所必救，以逸待勞者也。 」謀衍從之，乘夜亟發，會大風路暗不能辨，遲明行三十里許，與賊輜重相近，整兵少憩。 窩斡趨濟州，知大軍取其輜重，乃還救，遇於長濼。 既陣，謀衍別設伏於左翼之側，賊四百餘騎突出左翼伏兵之間，徒單克寧射卻之。 是日，別部諸將與賊對者，勝負未分，相去五里許而立。 左翼萬戶襄別與賊戰，賊陣動，襄麾軍乘之，突出其後，俱與大軍不相及。 襄以善射者二十騎，率眾自賊後擊之，賊不能支，乘勢麾軍擊其一偏，賊遂卻。 襄遂與大軍合，而別部諸將皆至，整陣力戰，忽反風揚砂石，賊陣亂，官軍馳擊，大破之。 追北十餘裏，斬獲甚眾。 詔以糺者為武義將軍，孛迭昭信校尉，挼剌忠翊校尉。 糺除同知建州事，未之官，卒。 孛迭取家賊中，遂被害，上憫之，後以挼剌為汝州都巡檢使。
Jiuwan Pasture Khitan Zhanzhe and his brothers Bedie and Ruola abandoned their families and defected from the rebels. Zhanzhe told Moyan Yan, "The rebels' horses are strong and ours are spent. They are eighty li away — by the time we reach them their mounts will be exhausted. Their baggage train is close by. Strike it and they must turn back to save their camp. When they arrive their horses will be blown and ours will have rested — attack where they must respond and meet fatigue with fresh strength. " Moyan Yan agreed and marched through the night. A gale and darkness made the road impossible to follow. At dawn, after some thirty li, they drew near the rebel baggage train, reformed ranks, and rested briefly. Wo Wo was hurrying toward Ji Prefecture when he learned the imperial army had seized his baggage. He wheeled about to save it and met them at Chang Marsh. As the armies formed, Moyan Yan hid troops beside the left wing. More than four hundred rebel horsemen burst through the gap, but Tushan Kening drove them back with arrows. That day the detached commanders facing the rebels were still locked in stalemate, their lines some five li apart. Left-wing chiliarch Xiang Bie engaged the rebels. When their line wavered he signaled the charge and broke through behind them, leaving both his command and the main army out of contact. Xiang took twenty expert archers and struck from the rear. The rebels buckled. He pressed the advantage, smashed one flank, and drove them back. Xiang rejoined the main force as the detached commanders closed in. They reformed and fought hard until a sudden reverse wind whipped sand and gravel into the rebel ranks. The imperial cavalry charged and broke them utterly. The pursuit ran more than ten li, with heavy slaughter and many captives. Zhanzhe was made Martial Righteousness General, Bedie Trusted Faith Commandant, and Ruola Loyal Assistant Commandant. Zhanzhe was appointed vice commissioner of Jian Prefecture but died before assuming the post. Bedie returned to the rebel camp to fetch his family and was killed. The emperor mourned him and later made Ruola Chief Inspector of Ruzhou.
31
窩斡率其眾西走，謀衍追及之於霿𩃭河。 賊已濟，毀其津口，紇石烈志寧軍先至，不克渡，乃對岸為疑兵，以夾穀清臣、徒單海羅兩萬戶於下流渡河，值支港兩岸鬥絕且濘淖，命軍士束柳填港而過。 追之數裏，得平地，方食，賊眾奄至。 志寧軍急整陣，賊自南岡馳下，沖陣者三，志寧力戰，流矢中左臂，戰自若。 大軍畢至，左翼騎兵先與賊接，賊據上風縱火，乘煙擊官軍，官軍步兵亦至，並力合戰，凡十余合，軍士苦風煙皆植立如癡。 會天降雨，風止，官軍奮擊，大敗之。 徒單克寧追奔十五里，賊前厄溪澗不得亟渡，多殺傷。 賊既渡，官軍亦渡，少憩，賊反旆來攻，克甯以大軍不繼，令軍士皆下馬射賊。 賊引卻而南，克寧亦將引而北，士未及騎馬，賊複來衝突，官軍少卻，回渡澗北。 大軍至，賊遂引去。
Wo Wo fled west with his followers. Moyan Yan caught up with him at the Menglong River. The rebels had crossed and wrecked the ford. Zhining's vanguard arrived first but could not follow. He feinted from the far bank while chiliarchs Jiagu Qingchen and Tushan Hailuo forded downstream. Where side channels ran deep and miry between steep banks, he had his men bundle willows and fill the channels to cross. They pursued several li to open ground and had just begun to eat when the rebels fell upon them. Zhining's men scrambled into formation as rebels charged down the southern ridge three times. An arrow struck Zhining's left arm, but he fought on as if untouched. The main force arrived and the left-wing cavalry engaged first. The rebels held the windward ground, set fires, and struck through the smoke. Imperial infantry joined the fight. For more than ten clashes the soldiers choked on wind and smoke and stood dazed as if stunned. Rain fell, the wind died, and the imperial army surged forward to crush them. Tushan Kening pursued fifteen li. Streams and gullies blocked the rebels' path and many were cut down before they could cross. Once the rebels had crossed, the imperial troops followed and paused briefly. The rebels wheeled about and attacked. With the main army not yet up, Kening ordered his men to dismount and shoot. The rebels pulled back south. Kening prepared to withdraw north, but before his men could remount the rebels charged again. The imperial line gave ground and fell back across the stream. When the main army arrived the rebels withdrew.
32
四月，詔元帥府曰； 「應契丹賊人，與大兵未戰已前投降者，不得殺傷，仍加安撫。 敗走以後，招誘來降者，除奴婢准已虜為定外，親屬分付圓聚。 仍官為換贖。」
In the fourth month the court addressed the Grand Marshal's headquarters: "Khitan rebels who surrender before battle with the main army must not be harmed and shall be treated with leniency. Those who surrender after defeat shall have their kin reunited, except for slaves and maidservants already taken captive. The state shall redeem them where ransom is required."
33
窩斡既敗，謀衍不復追討，駐軍白濼。 窩斡攻懿州不克，遂殘破川州，將遁於山西，而北京亦不邀擊之。 於是，發驍騎軍二千、曷懶路留屯京師軍三千，號稱二萬，會寧濟州軍六千亦號二萬。 元帥左都監高忠建總兵，沃州刺史烏古論蒲查為曷懶路押軍萬戶，祁州刺史烏林答剌撒為濟州押軍萬戶，右驍騎副都指揮使烏延查剌為驍騎萬戶，祁州刺史宗甯為會甯路押軍萬戶，右宣徽使宗亨為北京路都統，吏部郎中完顏達吉為副統，會元帥府討擊之。
After Wo Wo's defeat Moyan Yan ceased pursuit and encamped at Bai Marsh. Wo Wo failed to take Yi Prefecture, ravaged Chuan Prefecture, and prepared to escape into the western hills. The Northern Capital force did not intercept him. Two thousand crack cavalry were mobilized, and three thousand Supin Route troops left in the capital were reported as twenty thousand. Six thousand men from Huining and Ji Prefecture were likewise counted as twenty thousand. Grand Marshal Left Overseer Gao Zhongjian took overall command. Wo Prefect Wugulun Pucha became Supin Route Escort Army chiliarch, Qi Prefect Ulin Da Lasa Ji Prefecture Escort Army chiliarch, Right Crack-Cavalry Deputy Commander Wuyan Zhala crack-cavalry chiliarch, and Qi Prefect Zong Ning Huining Route Escort Army chiliarch. Right Palace Commissioner Zong Heng became Northern Capital Route commander-in-chief with Ministry of Personnel director Wanyan Daji as deputy. They joined the Grand Marshal's headquarters to hunt the rebels.
34
詔使尚廄局副使蒲察蒲盧渾往懿州戒敕將帥，上曰：「朕委卿等討賊，乃聞不就賊趨戰，而駐兵閑緩，經涉累月，雖曾追襲，乃不由有水草之地，以致馬疲弱不能百里而還。 後雖破賊，而縱諸軍劫掠，數日後方追北霿𩃭河，亦不乘勝，輒複引還。 賊遂入涉近地，北京、懿州由此受兵。 朕欲重譴汝等，以方任兵事，且圖後功。 當盡心一力，毋得似前怠弛。 」上謂蒲盧渾曰：「卿若聞賊在近，即當監督討伐。 用命力戰者疏記以聞，朕將約量遷賞。 無或承徇上官，抑有功、濫署無功者。 善戢士卒、勿縱虜掠。 」以紇石烈志甯為元帥右監軍，右副元帥完顏謀衍、元帥右監軍完顏福壽召還京師，咸平路總管完顏兀帶復舊職。 謀衍男斜哥在軍中多暴橫，詔押歸本管。 窩斡使所親招節度使移裏堇窟域，窟域執其使送官，與窩斡連戰有功，遷宣武將軍，賜銀五百兩、衣二襲。 起運在中都弓萬五千、箭一百五十萬赴懿州。
Imperial Stud Deputy Commissioner Pucha Puluhun was sent to Yi Prefecture to rebuke the commanders. The emperor said, "I entrusted you with suppressing the rebels, yet you linger in camp instead of closing with the enemy. Months have passed. Though you pursued once, you avoided watered pasture and ruined your horses so they could not ride a hundred li and return. Later, though you broke the rebels, you let your troops plunder. Only days afterward did you pursue to the Menglong River, and even then you failed to press the advantage and withdrew again. The rebels then raided the nearby country, and the Northern Capital and Yi Prefecture bore the brunt. I would have punished you severely, but with war still in hand I withhold judgment and look to your future service. Devote yourselves wholly and do not repeat your former slackness. " The emperor told Puluhun, "When you hear the rebels are near, you must oversee the campaign yourself. Record those who obey orders and fight hard, report them to me, and I shall reward them according to their merit. None of you must curry favor with your superiors by suppressing the meritorious or recklessly crediting the undeserving. Keep your troops disciplined and do not permit looting. " Heishilie Zhining was appointed Grand Marshal Right Overseer. Right Deputy Grand Marshal Wanyan Moyan Yan and Grand Marshal Right Overseer Wanyan Fushou were recalled to the capital, and Xianping Route commander Wanyan Wudai was restored to his former office. Moyan Yan's son Xiege had been brutal and overbearing in the army. The court ordered him arrested and returned to his home command. Wo Wo sent a trusted agent to summon Military Commissioner Yiliqin Kuyu, but Kuyu seized the envoy and delivered him to the authorities. For repeated victories over Wo Wo he was promoted to Xuanwu General and rewarded with five hundred taels of silver and two suits of clothing. Fifteen thousand bows and a million and a half arrows were shipped from the Central Capital to Yi Prefecture.
35
平章政事移剌元宜、甯昌軍節度使宗敘入見，詔使自中道卻還軍中，宣諭元宜、謀衍注意經略邊事。 師久無功，尚書右丞僕散忠義願效死力除邊患，世宗嘉歎。 六月，忠義拜平章政事兼右副元帥，宗敘為兵部尚書，各賜弓矢、具鞍勒馬。 出內府金銀十萬兩佐軍用。 詔曰：「軍中將士有犯，除連職奏聞，餘依軍法約量決責，有功者依格遷賞。 」以大名尹宗尹為河南路統軍使，河南路統軍都監蒲察世傑為西北路副統，賜弓矢佩刀廄馬，從忠義征行。 詔諭諸軍將士曰：「兵久駐邊陲，蠹費財用無成功，百姓不得休息。 今命平章政事僕散忠義兼右副元帥，同心戮力以底戡定。 右副元帥謀衍罷為同判大宗正事。」
Grand Councilor Yila Yuanyi and Ningchang Army Military Commissioner Zong Xu were summoned to court, then ordered to turn back and return to their commands. Yuanyi and Moyan Yan were instructed to devote themselves to frontier strategy. The campaign had dragged on without victory. Right Vice Commissioner of the Ministry of Works Pusan Zhongyi volunteered to stake his life on clearing the frontier threat. Shizong praised him warmly. In the sixth month Zhongyi became Grand Councilor and Right Deputy Grand Marshal. Zong Xu was made Minister of War. Both received bows, arrows, and fully equipped horses. A hundred thousand taels of gold and silver from the inner treasury were released for the army. An edict proclaimed: "Army offenses, except where the whole chain of command must be reported to the throne, shall be judged and punished under military law. Meritorious service shall be promoted and rewarded by regulation. " Da Ming Prefect Zong Yin became Henan Route army commander, and Henan Route deputy overseer Pucha Shijie became Northwestern Route deputy commander. Both received bows, arrows, girdled swords, and mounts from the imperial stables and marched with Zhongyi. The court addressed every officer and soldier: "Your armies have lingered on the frontier for months, draining the treasury without victory while the people know no peace. Grand Councilor Pusan Zhongyi is now Right Deputy Grand Marshal. With one heart and one will, bring this war to its end. Right Deputy Grand Marshal Moyan Yan was relieved and appointed Associate Director of the Imperial Clan Office."
36
詔居庸關、古北口譏察契丹奸細，捕獲者加官賞。 萬戶溫蒂罕阿魯帶以兵四千屯古北口，薊州、石門關等處各以五百人守之。 海陵末年，阿魯帶為猛安，移剌娜為牌印祗候，起契丹部族兵被執，至是挺身來降。 世宗以阿魯帶為濟州押軍萬戶，移剌娜為同知濼州事。
The court ordered heightened scrutiny at Juyong Pass and Gubeikou for Khitan spies, with promotions and rewards for those captured. Chiliarch Wendihan Arudai took four thousand men to Gubeikou. Jizhou, Shimen Pass, and other posts each received five-hundred-man garrisons. In the last years of the Hailing reign Arudai had been a meng'an and Yila Na a seal attendant. Both had been seized when Khitan tribal levies were conscripted. Now they escaped and surrendered. Shizong appointed Arudai Escort Army chiliarch of Ji Prefecture and Yila Na associate prefect of Luzhou.
37
西南路招討使完顏思敬為都統，賜金牌一、銀牌二，西北路招討使唐括孛古底副之。 以兵五千往會燕子城舊戍軍，視地形沖要或於狗濼屯駐，遠斥候，賊至即戰，不以晝夜為限。 詔思敬曰：「契丹賊敗必走山后，可選新馬三千，加芻纇以備追襲。」
Southwest Route Pacification Commissioner Wanyan Sijing became overall commander, granted one gold tally and two silver tallies, with Northwestern Route Pacification Commissioner Tanggu Bogede as his deputy. With five thousand men he was to unite with the garrison at Swallow City and encamp wherever the terrain demanded, perhaps at Gou Marsh. Long-range scouts were to be posted; when rebels appeared the army was to engage without pause, day or night. Sijing was instructed: "Defeated Khitan rebels will flee into the northern hills. Prepare three thousand fresh horses with ample fodder for the pursuit."
38
僕散忠義至軍中。 是時，窩斡西走花道，眾尚八萬。 忠義、高忠建軍與賊遇，萬戶查剌、蒲查為左翼，宗亨統之； 宗寧、剌撒為右翼，宗敘統之； 世傑亦在左翼中，與賊夾河為陣。 賊渡河，以兵四萬餘先犯左翼軍，查剌以六百騎奮擊敗之。 複以四萬眾與左翼軍戰，宗亨、世傑七謀克指畫失宜，陣亂敗於賊。 世傑挺身投于查剌軍中，賊圍查剌軍，查剌力戰，宗敘以右翼軍來救，賊乃去。
Pusan Zhongyi reached the front. Wo Wo was then fleeing west along Huadao with a force still eighty thousand strong. Zhongyi and Gao Zhongjian's armies met the rebels. Chiliarchs Zhala and Pucha led the left wing under Zong Heng; Zong Ning and Lasa led the right wing under Zong Xu; Shijie fought in the left wing as the armies formed on opposite banks of a river. The rebels crossed the river and hit the left wing first with more than forty thousand men. Zhala led six hundred horsemen in a fierce charge and broke them. The rebels returned with forty thousand men. Zong Heng's and Shijie's seven company commanders misdirected the battle, the line collapsed, and the left wing was routed. Shijie threw himself in with Zhala's detachment. The rebels encircled Zhala, who fought on grimly until Zong Xu brought the right wing to the rescue and drove them off.
39
詔曰：「自契丹作逆，有為賊詿誤者，不問如何從賊，但能複業，與免本罪。 如能率眾來附，或能殺捕首領而降，或執送賊所扇誘作亂之人，皆與量加官爵。 朕念正隆南征，猛安亡者招還被戮，已命其子孫襲其職。 爾等勿懲前事，故懷遲疑。 賊軍今既破散，山后諸處皆命將士遏其逃路，爾等雖欲不降終將安往？ 若猶疑貳，俱就焚滅，悔及矣。」
An edict declared: "Since the Khitan rebellion began, all who were misled into joining the rebels — no matter how they came to serve them — shall be pardoned if they return to honest livelihood. Those who bring followers in surrender, kill or capture rebel leaders, or deliver those the rebels incited to revolt shall be promoted and ennobled according to merit. I remember the Zhenglong southern campaign, when recalled deserter meng'an were executed. I have already ordered their sons and grandsons to inherit those offices. Do not let those past punishments make you hesitate now. The rebel army is shattered. Every pass beyond the mountains is blocked. Even if you refuse to submit, where can you go? If you still hold back, you will all be destroyed together — and then it will be too late for regret."
40
窩斡自花道西走，僕散忠義、紇石烈志甯以大軍追及於嫋嶺西陷泉。 明日，賊軍三萬騎涉水而東。 大軍先據南岡，左翼軍自岡為陣，迤邐而北，步軍繼之，右翼軍繼步軍北引而東，作偃月陣，步軍居中，騎兵據其兩端，使賊不見首尾。 是日，大霧晦冥，既陣霧開，少頃晴霽，賊見左翼據南岡不敢擊，擊右翼軍，烏延查剌力戰，賊稍卻。 志甯與夾穀清臣、烏林答剌撒、鐸剌合戰，賊大敗，將涉水去，泥濘不得亟渡。 大軍逐北，人馬相蹂踐而死，不可勝數，陷泉皆平，餘眾蹈籍而過，或奔潰竄匿林莽間。 大軍踵擊之，俘斬萬計，生擒其弟偽六院司大王嫋。 窩斡僅與數騎脫去，鈔兀、清臣追四十餘裏不及，斬千餘級，獲車帳甚眾。 其母徐輦舉營自落括岡西走，志寧追之，盡獲輜重，俘五萬餘人，雜畜不可勝計。 偽節度使六及其部族皆降。
Wo Wo fled west from Huadao. Pusan Zhongyi and Heishilie Zhining overtook him with the main army west of Niao Ridge at Xian Spring. The next day thirty thousand rebel horsemen forded eastward. The imperial army seized the southern ridge first. The left wing formed along it and wound north, infantry following. The right wing trailed the infantry north and then east in a crescent formation — infantry in the center, cavalry at both horns — so the rebels could see neither head nor tail. That day fog hung thick. As the armies formed it lifted, and soon the sky cleared. Seeing the left wing hold the southern ridge, the rebels struck the right instead. Wuyan Zhala fought hard and drove them back slightly. Zhining, Jiagu Qingchen, Ulin Da Lasa, and Dela closed in. The rebels were routed as they tried to ford the river, but the mud held them fast. The imperial army pressed the pursuit. Men and horses trampled each other to death beyond count until Xian Spring ran level with the ground. Survivors crossed on the fallen bodies; others fled into the brush. The army hunted them down, taking tens of thousands killed or captured alive, including Wo Wo's younger brother, the pretender Prince Niao of the Six Yuan Offices. Wo Wo escaped with only a handful of riders. Chaowu and Qingchen pursued forty li without catching him, taking more than a thousand heads and vast numbers of carts and tents. Wo Wo's mother Xulian broke camp and fled west from Luoguo Ridge. Zhining overtook her, seizing all her baggage, more than fifty thousand captives, and livestock beyond reckoning. The pretender military commissioner Liu and his clansmen surrendered en masse.
41
詔北京副統完顏達吉括本部馬，規辦芻糧，仍使達吉為監戰官，錄有功者聞奏。 詔選中都、西京兩路新舊軍萬人備守禦，以窩斡敗走，恐或衝突也。
Northern Capital deputy commander Wanyan Daji was ordered to gather his command's horses and procure fodder. He was also made battle overseer to record merit and report to the throne. Ten thousand troops from the Central Capital and Western Capital routes were mobilized for defense, lest the fleeing Wo Wo break through somewhere.
42
僕散忠義使使奏捷，詔略曰：「平章政事右副元帥忠義使使來奏大捷。 或被軍俘獲，或自能來服，或無所歸而投拜，或將全屬歸附，或分領家族來降，或嘗受偽命，及自來曾與官軍鬥敵，皆釋其罪。 其散亡人內，除窩斡一身，不以大小官員是何名色，卻來歸附者，亦准釋放。 有能誅捕窩斡，或於不從招納亡去人內誅捕以來，及或能率眾于掌軍官及隨處官司投降者，並給官賞。 各路撫納來者，毋得輒加侵損。 無資給者，不以是何路分，隨有糧處安置，仍官為養濟。」
Pusan Zhongyi sent messengers bearing news of victory. The edict read in part: "Grand Councilor and Right Deputy Grand Marshal Zhongyi's envoys report a great victory. Whether taken in battle, coming forward to submit, bowing allegiance with nowhere else to turn, bringing whole households, leading clansmen in surrender, having served under the pretender, or having fought imperial troops — all shall be pardoned. Among the scattered remnant, apart from Wo Wo himself, all who return to submit — whatever rank or title they once held — shall likewise be pardoned. Whoever kills or captures Wo Wo, or delivers those who fled after refusing surrender, or leads groups to surrender to local commanders or officials shall receive official reward. Every command that receives surrendering rebels must not molest or plunder them. Those without means shall be settled wherever grain is available, regardless of route, and maintained at public expense."
43
窩斡收合散卒萬餘人，遂入奚部，以諸奚自益，時時出兵寇速魯古澱、古北口、興化之間。 溫蒂罕阿魯帶守古北口，與戰敗焉。 詔完顏謀衍、蒲察烏裏雅、蒲察蒲盧渾以兵三千，合舊屯兵五千，擊之。 詔守顏思敬以所部兵入奚地，會大軍討窩斡。
Wo Wo regrouped more than ten thousand scattered troops and entered Xi territory, recruiting Xi warriors to his cause. He raided repeatedly between Sulugu Marsh, Gubeikou, and Xinghua. Wendihan Arudai held Gubeikou and was defeated. Wanyan Moyan Yan, Pucha Wuliya, and Pucha Puluhun were ordered to take three thousand men, combined with five thousand already in garrison, to attack him. Wanyan Sijing was ordered to march his command into Xi territory and join the main army against Wo Wo.
44
賊黨霿𩃭河猛安蒲速越遣人至帥府約降，詔令擒捕窩斡，許以官賞。 賊將降者甚眾。 其散走者聞詔書招降，亦多降者。 其餘多疾疫而死，無複鬥志。 窩斡自度勢窮，乃謀自羊城道西京奔夏國，大軍追之益急，其眾複多亡去，度不得西，乃北走沙陀間。 詔尚書省：「凡脅從之家被俘掠遂致離散，宜從改正。 將士往往藏匿其人，有司檢括分付。」
Rebel meng'an Pusuyue of the Menglong River sent emissaries to the headquarters offering surrender. The court promised official reward for capturing Wo Wo. Rebel officers surrendered in great numbers. Stragglers who heard the surrender proclamation also came in in droves. The remainder mostly died of plague and lost all will to fight. Seeing his cause hopeless, Wo Wo planned to flee west through Sheep City Road and the Western Capital into Xia territory. Pursuit tightened and more men deserted. Unable to break west, he turned north into the Shatuo country. The Ministry of Works was instructed: "Families of those forced into rebellion, scattered by capture and plunder, shall have their household registers corrected. Officers and soldiers have often hidden such people. The authorities shall search them out and restore them to their families."
45
監軍志寧獲賊稍合住，釋而弗殺，縱還賊中，使誘其親近捕窩斡以自效，許以官賞。 九月庚子，稍合住與神獨斡執窩斡，詣右都臨完顏思敬降，並獲其母徐輦及其妻、子、子婦、弟、侄，盡收偽金銀牌印。 唐括孛古底獲前胡裏改節度使什溫及其家屬。 西北路招討使李家奴獲偽樞密使逐斡等三十餘人，複與猛安泥本婆果追偽監軍那也至天成縣，那也乃降，乃獲偽都元帥醜哥及金牌一、銀牌五。 志甯與清臣、宗甯、速哥等追餘黨至燕子城，盡得其黨。 前至抹拔裏達之地，悉獲之，逆党遂平。
Overseer Zhining captured the rebel Shaoxinghe, spared his life, and sent him back among the rebels to persuade his confederates to capture Wo Wo in exchange for reward. On gengzi day of the ninth month Shaoxinghe and Shenduwo seized Wo Wo and surrendered to Right Deputy Overseer Wanyan Sijing. They also took his mother Xulian, wife, sons, daughters-in-law, brother, and nephews, and collected all the rebel gold and silver tallies. Tanggu Bogede captured former Huligai Military Commissioner Shiwen and his household. Northwestern Route Pacification Commissioner Li Jianu captured the pretender Privy Councilor Zhuwo and more than thirty others. With meng'an Niben Boguo he pursued pretender overseer Naye to Tiancheng County. Naye surrendered, and they also took pretender Grand Marshal Chuge along with one gold tally and five silver tallies. Zhining, Qingchen, Zong Ning, Suge, and others hunted the remnant to Swallow City and captured them every one. They pressed on to Mabali Dada and took all who remained. The rebellion was fully suppressed.
46
甲辰，皇太子率百官上表賀。 乙巳，詔天下。 辛亥，完顏思敬獻俘于京師，窩斡梟首於市，磔其手足，分懸諸京府。 其母徐輦及妻子皆戮之。 契丹降人皆拘其器仗，貧不能自給者官為養濟。
On jiachen day the crown prince led the court in a memorial of congratulation. On yisi day the victory was proclaimed throughout the realm. On xinhai day Wanyan Sijing presented the captives at court. Wo Wo's head was exposed in the marketplace, his limbs quartered and displayed in capitals across the realm. His mother Xulian, his wife, and his children were all put to death. Surrendered Khitan were disarmed. Those too poor to live on their own were maintained at public expense.
47
括裏、紮八率眾南走，詔左宣徽使宗亨追及之。 紮八詐稱降，宗亨信其言，遂不與戰。 紮八紿之曰：「括裏驚走，願追之。 」宗亨縱紮八去。 益都猛安欲以所部追括裏、紮八，宗亨恐分其功，不聽，而縱軍士取賊所棄資囊人畜而自有之。 括裏、紮入由是得亡去，遂奔于宋。 宗亨降甯州剌史。 其後，宋李世輔用括裏、紮八，遂取宿州，頗為邊患。
Kuoli and Zhahe fled south with their followers. Left Palace Commissioner Zong Heng was ordered to overtake them. Zhahe feigned surrender. Zong Heng believed him and held his army back. Zhahe tricked him, saying, "Kuoli has fled in panic. Allow me to pursue him. " Zong Heng let Zhahe go. The Yidu meng'an asked to pursue Kuoli and Zhahe with his command. Zong Heng, fearing a rival for credit, refused — and instead let his soldiers help themselves to the rebels' abandoned loot and livestock. Kuoli and Zhahe thus escaped and fled to Song. Zong Heng was demoted to prefect of Ningzhou. Later the Song general Li Shipu employed Kuoli and Zhahe to seize Suzhou, and they became a lasting frontier threat.
48
神獨斡除同知安化軍節度使，稍合住除同知震武軍節度使事。 大定六年，點檢司奏，親軍中有逆黨子弟，請一切罷去。 詔曰：「身預逆黨者罷之，餘勿問。」
Shenduwo was appointed associate military commissioner of Anhua Army and Shaoxinghe associate military commissioner of Zhenwu Army. In Dading year six the Palace Inspectorate reported that sons and brothers of rebel families served in the imperial guard and requested their dismissal. The emperor replied: "Dismiss only those who joined the rebellion themselves. Leave the rest alone."
49
贊曰：金人以燕山與宋，遂啟張覺跳樑之心，覺豈為宋者哉，蓋欲乘時以徼利耳。 耶律余睹從宗望追天祚，曾不遺餘力，功成驕溢，自取誅滅，咈哉。 正隆佳兵，契丹作難，《傳》曰：「夫兵猶火也，弗戢將自焚。 」可不戒哉！
The historian writes: Ceding Yanshan to Song planted in Zhang Jue the ambition of the beam-vaulter. Jue was no servant of Song — he sought only to grab profit while fortune smiled. Ye Lü Yudu followed Zongwang in hunting down Emperor Tianzuo and held nothing back. Success bred arrogance and brought him to the execution ground. Pitiful. The Zhenglong reign glorified war, and the Khitan rose in revolt. The Commentary says: "War is like fire; unchecked, it consumes its master. " Let this be a warning!
