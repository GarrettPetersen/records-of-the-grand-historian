古書「畔」與「叛」通，畔之為言界也。 《左氏》曰，政猶「農之有畔」，是也。 君臣上下之定分，猶此疆彼界之截然，違此向彼，即為叛矣。 善惡判于跬步，禍患極於懷襄，吁，可畏哉！ 作《叛臣傳》。
In classical texts, the characters for pan (boundary) and pan (rebel) are used interchangeably; etymologically, pan denotes a boundary. As the Zuo Commentary puts it: governing a state is like farming a field—one must keep clear boundaries. The proper hierarchy between ruler and subject is as sharply drawn as a border between fields; to step across that line is treason. A single misstep can decide one's fate between good and evil, and disaster can swell to flood-tide proportions—how terrifying! Hence this "Biographies of Traitors."
2
張覺，亦書作鐧，平州義豐人也。 在遼第進士，仕至遼興軍節度副使。 太祖定燕京，時立愛以平州降，當時宋人以海上之盟求燕京及西京地，太祖以燕京、涿、易、檀、順、景、薊與之。 平州自入契丹別為一軍，故弗與，而以平州為南京，覺為留守。 既而聞覺有異志，上遣使劉彥宗及斜缽諭之，詔曰：「平山一郡今為南京，節度使今為留守。 恩亦厚矣。 或言汝等陰有異圖，何為當此農時輒相扇動，非去危就安之計也。 其諭朕意。 」太祖每收城邑，往往徙其民以實京師，民心多不安，故時立愛因降表曾言及之。 及以燕京與宋而遷其人，獨以空城與之，遷者道出平州，故覺因之以作亂。 天輔七年五月，左企弓、虞仲文、曹勇義、康公弼赴廣甯，過平州，覺使人殺之于栗林下，遂據南京叛入于宋，宋人納之。
Zhang Jue (also written Jian) was from Yifeng in Ping Prefecture. He took first place in the Liao jinshi examination and rose to vice military commissioner of Liaoxing Circuit. After Emperor Taizu took Yanjing, Shi Liai surrendered Ping Prefecture. The Song, citing the maritime alliance, demanded Yanjing and the Western Capital lands; Taizu ceded Yanjing along with Zhuo, Yi, Tan, Shun, Jing, and Ji. Ping Prefecture had long been a separate military district under the Khitan, so it was withheld; Ping was made the Southern Capital instead, with Jue as resident commissioner. When word came that Jue harbored rebellious designs, the emperor sent Liu Yanzong and Xiebo to warn him with an edict: "The commandery of Pingshan is now the Southern Capital, and the post of military commissioner is now resident commissioner. My favor toward you is already generous. Some report that you secretly plot rebellion—why, in the middle of the planting season, do you incite unrest? That is no way to escape danger and find security. Convey Our meaning to them. Whenever Taizu captured a city, he often relocated its people to fill the capital, leaving the populace uneasy—Shi Liai had raised this very concern in his surrender memorial. When Yanjing was handed to the Song and its inhabitants relocated, the Song received only an empty city; the evacuees passed through Ping Prefecture, and Jue seized the moment to rebel. In the fifth month of Tianfu 7 (1123), Zuo Qigong, Yu Zhongwen, Cao Yongyi, and Kang Gongbi were en route to Guangning and passed through Ping; Jue had them murdered in a chestnut grove, seized the Southern Capital, defected to the Song, and was welcomed by them.
3
太祖下詔諭南京官吏，詔曰：「朕初駐蹕燕京，嘉爾吏民率先降附，故升府治以為南京，減徭役，薄賦稅，恩亦至矣，何苦輒為叛逆？ 今欲進兵攻取，時方農月，不忍以一惡人而害及眾庶。 且遼國舉為我有，孤城自守，終欲何為？ 今止坐首惡，餘並釋之。」
Taizu issued an edict to the officials of the Southern Capital: "When I first encamped at Yanjing, I commended your officials and people for being the first to submit; I elevated your prefecture to the Southern Capital, eased corvée duties, and reduced taxes—My kindness has been ample. Why court rebellion? I am ready to march and take the city by force, but it is planting season, and I cannot bear to harm the common people for the sake of one villain. The whole Liao realm is already mine—what can a lone city hope to accomplish by holding out? I will punish only the ringleader and release all the rest."
4
覺兵五萬屯潤州近郊，欲脅遷、來、潤、隰四州。 闍母自錦州往討之，已敗覺兵，欲乘勝攻南京，時暑雨不可進，退屯於海堧。 無何，闍母再敗覺兵，復與戰於兔耳山，闍母大敗，覺報捷于宋。 宋建平州為泰寧軍，以覺為節度使，張敦固等皆加徽猶閣待制，以銀絹數萬犒軍。
Jue stationed fifty thousand troops in the suburbs of Run Prefecture, aiming to intimidate Qian, Lai, Run, and Xi into submission. Chamu marched from Jinzhou to suppress him, routed Jue's forces, and tried to press on to the Southern Capital, but summer rains blocked the advance; he withdrew to camp at Haichuan. Soon Chamu defeated Jue again, but at Rabbit-Ear Mountain Chamu himself was routed; Jue sent word of victory to the Song. The Song reorganized Ping Prefecture as Taining Circuit, made Jue its military commissioner, promoted Zhang Dun'gu and others to Huaiyou Pavilion attendant-in-waiting, and lavished tens of thousands in silver and silk on the troops.
5
宗望軍至南京城東，覺兵大敗宵遁，遂奔宋，入於燕京。 宗望以納叛責宋宣撫司，索張覺。 宣撫王安中匿之於甲仗庫，紿曰：「無之。 」宗望索愈急，安中乃斬貌類覺者一人當之，金人識之曰：「非覺也。 」安中不得已，引覺出。 數以罪，覺罵宋人不容口，遂殺覺函其首以與金人。 燕京降將及常勝軍皆泣下，郭藥師自言曰：「若來索藥師當奈何？ 」自是，降將卒皆解體。 及金人伐宋，竟以納平州之叛為執言雲。 子僅言。
When Zongwang's army reached the east gate of the Southern Capital, Jue's troops were routed and fled by night; he fled to the Song and took refuge in Yanjing. Zongwang accused the Song Pacification Commission of sheltering a traitor and demanded Zhang Jue be handed over. Pacification Commissioner Wang Anzhong hid him in the armory and lied: "We do not have him. As Zongwang pressed harder, Anzhong beheaded a look-alike and presented the head; the Jurchens recognized the deception: "This is not Jue. With no alternative, Anzhong produced Jue. After listing his crimes, Jue cursed the Song without cease; they executed him, boxed his head, and delivered it to the Jurchens. Surrendered generals in Yanjing and the Ever-Victorious Army wept openly; Guo Yaoshi muttered, "If they come for me next, what then? From that day on, surrendered officers and troops lost all morale. When the Jurchens later invaded the Song, they cited the sheltering of the Ping Prefecture rebel as their casus belli. His son Jinyan.

子僅言
Son: Jinyan
7
僅言幼名元奴。 宗望攻下平山，僅言在繈褓間，裏人劉承宣得之，養於家。 其鄰韓夫人甚愛之。 年數歲，因隨韓夫人得見貞懿皇后。 留之籓邸，稍長，侍世宗讀書，遂使僅言主家事，繩檢部曲，一府憚之。
Jinyan's childhood name was Yuannu. When Zongwang took Pingshan, Jinyan was still an infant; a neighbor, Liu Chengxuan, found him and raised him in his household. A neighbor, Lady Han, adored him. At the age of several years, he accompanied Lady Han to an audience with Empress Zhenyi. She kept him at the princely residence; as he grew up he studied with the future Emperor Shizong, who later put him in charge of household affairs and discipline over the retainers—the whole establishment stood in awe of him.
8
僅言能心計，世宗倚任之，凡宮室營造、府庫出納、行幸頓舍皆委之。 世宗嘗曰：「一經僅言，無不愜朕意者。 」六年，提舉修內役事，役夫掘地得白金匿之，事覺，法當死，僅言責取其物與官，釋其罪。 尋兼祗應司。 遷少府監，提控宮籍監、祗應司如故。 護作太甯宮，引宮左流泉溉田，歲獲稻萬斛。 十七年，複提點內藏，典領昭德皇后山陵，遷勸農使，領諸職如故。
Jinyan had a keen head for figures; Shizong relied on him for palace construction, treasury accounts, and all arrangements for imperial tours and encampments. Shizong once remarked, "Once Jinyan has handled a matter, it never fails to please me. In year 6 he oversaw inner-palace construction; laborers unearthed hidden silver, a capital offense; Jinyan made them surrender the treasure to the state and spared their lives. He was soon also appointed to the Attendance Office. He was promoted to director of the Palace Workshops while retaining charge of the Palace Registry and Attendance offices. While supervising construction of Taining Palace, he channeled a spring west of the palace to irrigate fields, yielding ten thousand piculs of rice a year. In year 17 he again oversaw the inner treasury, directed Empress Zhaode's mausoleum, was promoted to commissioner for encouraging agriculture, and kept his other posts.
9
僅言雖舊臣，出入左右，然世宗終不假以權任。 二十一年，尚書省奏，宮苑司直長黎倫在職十六年，請與遷敘。 上曰：「此朕之家臣，質直人也，今已老矣。 如勸農使張僅言亦朕舊臣，純實頗解事，凡朝廷議論，內外除授，未嘗得干預。 朕觀自古人君為讒諂蒙蔽者多矣，朕雖不及古人，然近習憸言未嘗入耳。 」宰臣曰：「誠如聖訓，此國家之福也。 」世宗欲以為橫海軍節度使，而不可去左右，遂止。
Though Jinyan was a longtime companion who attended him daily, Shizong never granted him real political authority. In year 21 the Secretariat reported that Li Lun, chief clerk of the Palace Parks Office, had served sixteen years and sought a promotion. The emperor said, "He is a household retainer of mine, honest and upright, but he is old now. Commissioner Zhang Jinyan is another old retainer—sincere and capable—but he has never been permitted to meddle in court debates or appointments, internal or external. I know that many rulers throughout history were blinded by slander and flattery; I may not equal the ancients, but flattery from my intimates has never reached my ears. The chief ministers replied, "As Your Majesty says—this is the state's good fortune. Shizong considered making him military commissioner of Henghai Circuit, but could not spare him from his side, and let the matter drop.
10
僅言始得疾，猶扶杖視事，疾亟，詔太醫診視，近侍問訊相屬。 及卒，上深惜之，遣官致祭，賻銀五百兩、重彩十端、絹二百匹，棺槨、衣衾、銀汞、斂物、葬地皆官給，贈輔國上將軍。
When Jinyan first fell ill he still attended to business with a staff in hand; as he worsened, imperial physicians were summoned and close attendants came one after another to inquire. At his death the emperor mourned him deeply, sent officials to perform sacrifices, and granted five hundred taels of silver, ten bolts of fine silk, and two hundred bolts of plain silk; coffin, shroud, embalming materials, burial goods, and a grave site were all provided by the court; he was posthumously enfeoffed Supporter-of-the-State Grand General.

12
太祖已取臨潢府，賜詔余睹曰：「汝將兵在東路，前後戰未嘗不敗。 今聞汝收合散亡，以拒我師。 朕已於今月十五日克上京，今將往取遼主矣。 汝若治兵一決勝負，可指地期日相報。 若知不敵，當率眾來降，無貽後悔。 」及太祖班師，闍母等還至遼河，方渡，余睹來襲，完顏背答、烏塔等殿，力戰卻之，獲甲馬五百匹。
After Taizu captured Linhuang, he sent Yudu an edict: "You command troops on the eastern front, and battle after battle you have lost. Now I hear you are rallying scattered forces to resist my army. I took the Upper Capital on the fifteenth of this month and am now marching to capture the Liao emperor. If you wish to muster your forces and fight it out, name a place and a date and send word. If you know you cannot win, lead your men in surrender—do not invite regret. When Taizu withdrew, Chamu's forces were crossing the Liao River when Yudu attacked; Wanyan Beida, Wuta, and others held the rear, fought him off, and captured five hundred sets of armor and horses.
13
天輔五年，余睹送款于咸州路都統，以所部來降，乞援接于桑林渡。 都統司以聞，詔曰：「余睹到日，使與其官屬偕來，餘眾處之便地。 」無何，余睹送上所受遼國宣誥，及器甲旗幟等，與將吏韓福奴、阿八、謝老、太師奴、蕭慶、醜和尚、高佛留、蒲答、謝家奴、五哥等來降。
In Tianfu 5 (1121), Yudu sent surrender terms to the Xianzhou circuit commander, came in with his troops, and asked to be met at Sanglin Ford. The command reported upward; an edict ordered: "When Yudu arrives, have him come with his officers; settle the rest of his men wherever convenient. Soon Yudu presented the Liao patents and edicts he had held, along with arms, armor, and banners, and surrendered with officers including Han Funu, Aba, Xie Lao, Taishinu, Xiao Qing, Chouheshang, Gao Foliu, Puda, Xiejianu, and Wuge.
14
余睹作書，具言所以降之意，大概以謂：「遼主沉湎荒于遊畋，不恤政事，好佞人，遠忠直，淫刑吝賞，政煩賦重，民不聊生。 」又言：「樞密使得裏底本無材能，但阿諛取容，其子磨哥任以軍事。 」又言：「文妃長子晉王素系人望，宜為儲副，得裏底以元妃諸子己所自出，使晉王出繼文妃。 」又言：「晉王與駙馬乙信謀複其樞密使，來告余睹共定大計，而所圖不成。 」又言：「己粗更軍事，進策遼主，得裏底蔽之，遼主亦不省察。 」又曰：「大金疆土日辟，余睹灼知天命，遂自去年春與耶律慎思等定議，約以今夏來降。 近聞得裏底、高十捏等欲發，倉卒之際不及收合四遠，但率傍近部族戶三千、車五千兩、畜產數萬、遼北軍都統以兵追襲，遂棄輜重，轉戰至此。 所有官事職位姓名、人戶畜產之數，遣韓福奴具錄以聞。 」遂與其將吏來見，上撫慰之，遂賜坐，班同宰相，賜宴盡醉而罷。 上命余睹以舊官領所部。 且諭之曰：「若能為國立功，別當獎用。 」自余睹降，益知遼人虛實矣。
Yudu wrote at length explaining why he surrendered, saying in essence: "The Liao emperor is sunk in pleasure and obsessed with the hunt, neglects government, favors flatterers and shuns the loyal, imposes cruel punishments and grudging rewards, and burdens the people with heavy taxes until they cannot live. He also wrote: "Grand Councilor Delidi has no ability but flatters his way to favor; his son Moge has been put in charge of military affairs. He also wrote: "Consort Wen's eldest son, the Prince of Jin, has long enjoyed public esteem and should be heir apparent; Delidi, whose own sons were born to the Primary Consort, had the Prince of Jin adopted to Consort Wen instead. He also wrote: "The Prince of Jin and the imperial son-in-law Yixin plotted to restore the prince's post as grand councilor; they confided in Yudu and laid a grand plan, but it came to nothing. He also wrote: "I had reorganized military affairs and presented plans to the Liao emperor, but Delidi suppressed them and the emperor never looked into it. He also said: "The Jin realm grows daily; I clearly see where Heaven's mandate lies; from last spring I agreed with Yelü Shensi and others to surrender this summer. When I heard Delidi and Gao Shinie were about to move against me, I had no time to rally distant tribes; I led only three thousand nearby households, five thousand carts, and tens of thousands of livestock; the northern Liao commander pursued me, and I abandoned my baggage fighting my way here. Han Funu will submit a full roster of offices, names, households, and livestock. He then came to court with his officers; the emperor received him graciously, seated him in rank equal to the chief councilors, and held a banquet at which all drank their fill. The emperor ordered Yudu to command his followers under his former title. He added: "If you render service to the state, you will be rewarded accordingly. After Yudu's surrender, the Jin learned far more about the Liao's real strength.
15
余睹在軍中屢乞侍妾及子，太祖疑之，詔咸州路都統司曰：「余睹家屬，善監護之。 」複詔曰：「余睹降時，其民多強率而來者，恐在邊生變，宜徙之內地。 」都統杲取中京，余睹為鄉導，與希尹等招撫奚部。 奉聖州降，其官吏皆遁去，余睹舉前監酒李師夔為節度使，進士沈璋為副使，州吏裴賾為觀察判官。 沈璋招集居民還業者三千餘，遷太常少卿。
In camp Yudu repeatedly asked for concubines and to see his son; Taizu grew suspicious and ordered the Xianzhou command: "Guard Yudu's family closely. A further edict said: "Many of Yudu's people were forcibly brought along when he surrendered; fearing unrest on the frontier, relocate them inland. When Commander Gao took the Central Capital, Yudu guided the way and, with Xiyin and others, pacified the Xi tribes. When Fengsheng Prefecture surrendered, its officials fled; Yudu recommended former wine supervisor Li Shikui as military commissioner, jinshi Shen Zhang as vice commissioner, and clerk Pei Ze as observation commissioner. Shen Zhang brought back more than three thousand residents to their livelihoods and was promoted to vice director of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices.
16
久之，耶律麻者告余睹、吳十、劉剌結黨謀叛，及其未發宜先收捕。 上召余睹等從容謂之曰：「今聞汝謀叛，誠然邪，其各無隱。 若果去，必須鞍馬甲胄器械之屬，當悉付汝，吾不食言。 若再被擒，無祈免死。 欲留事我，則無懷異志，吾不汝疑。 」余睹等戰慄不能對，乃杖鐸剌七十，餘皆不問。
Some time later Yelü Mazhe reported that Yudu, Wu Shi, and Liu La had formed a clique to rebel and should be arrested before they acted. The emperor summoned Yudu and the others and said calmly: "I hear you plot rebellion—is it so? Speak plainly. If you truly mean to leave, you will need horses, armor, and weapons—I will give you everything; I do not break my word. If you are captured again, do not expect mercy. If you wish to stay and serve me, harbor no other designs—I will not doubt you. Yudu and the others trembled and could not answer; the emperor had Duola beaten seventy strokes and questioned no one else.
17
天會三年，大舉伐宋，余睹為元帥右都監，宋兵四萬救太原，余睹、屋里海逆擊於汾河北，擒其帥郝仲連、張關索，統制馬忠，殺萬餘人。 宗翰伐宋，余睹留西京。 天會十年，余睹謀反，雲內節度使耶律奴哥等告之。 余睹亡去，其黨燕京統軍蕭高六伏誅，蔚州節度使蕭特謀自殺。 邊部斬余睹及其諸子，函其首以獻。 耶律奴哥加守太保兼侍中，趙公鑒、劉儒信、劉君輔等並授遙鎮節度使以賞之。
In Tianhui 3 (1125) a major invasion of the Song was launched; Yudu served as right deputy commander-in-chief; forty thousand Song troops marched to relieve Taiyuan; Yudu and Wulihai counterattacked north of the Fen River, capturing commanders Hao Zhonglian and Zhang Guansuo and controller Ma Zhong, and killing more than ten thousand. When Zonghan invaded the Song, Yudu stayed behind at the Western Capital. In Tianhui 10 (1132) Yudu plotted rebellion; Yunnei military commissioner Yelü Nuge and others informed on him. Yudu fled; his accomplice Yanjing commander Xiao Gaoliu was executed, and Weizhou military commissioner Xiao Temou killed himself. Frontier troops beheaded Yudu and his sons and sent their heads in tribute. Yelü Nuge was promoted to Defender-in-Chief and Palace Attendant; Zhao Gongjian, Liu Ruxin, Liu Junfu, and others were rewarded with appointments as distant military commissioners.
18
移剌窩斡，西北路契丹部族。 先從撒八為亂，受其偽署，後殺撒八，遂有其眾。
Yelü Wo Wo, of the Khitan tribes of the Northwestern Route. He first followed Saba's rebellion and accepted a post in his regime, then killed Saba and took over his forces.
19
撒八者，初為招討司譯史。 正隆五年，海陵征諸道兵伐宋，使牌印燥合、楊葛盡征西北路契丹丁壯，契丹人曰：「西北路接近鄰國，世世征伐，相為仇怨。 若男丁盡從軍，彼以兵來，則老弱必盡系累矣。 幸使者入朝言之。 」燥合畏罪不敢言，楊葛深念後西北有事得罪，遂以憂死。 燥合複與牌印耶律娜、尚書省令史沒答涅合督起西北路兵。 契丹聞男丁當盡起，於是撒八、孛特補與部眾殺招討使完顏沃側及燥合，而執耶律娜、沒答涅合，取招討司貯甲三千，遂反。 議立豫王延禧子孫，眾推都監老和尚為招討使，山后四群牧、山前諸群牧皆應之。 迪斡群牧使徒單賽裏、耶魯瓦群牧使鶴壽等皆遇害，語在《鶴壽傳》中。 五院司部人老和尚那也亦殺節度使術甲兀者以應撒八。
Saba had originally served as a translator-clerk in the Pacification Commission. In Zhenglong 5 (1160) Hailing conscripted troops from every circuit for the Song campaign, sending Paoyin Zaohe and Yang Ge to draft every able Khitan man on the Northwestern Route; the Khitan protested: "The northwest borders enemy states; we have fought them generation after generation. If every fighting man is taken south, the enemy will march in and our women, children, and elderly will all be captured. We beg that the envoy be sent to court to plead our case. " Zaohe, fearing punishment, kept silent; Yang Ge, brooding that if trouble later broke out in the northwest he would be blamed, died of anxiety. Zaohe again, with Paoyin Yelü Na and Secretariat clerk Modaniehe, pressed the conscription of Northwestern Route troops. Learning that every fighting man was to be drafted, Saba and Betebu led their followers to kill Pacification Commissioner Wanyan Woce and Zaohe, seize Yelü Na and Modaniehe, strip three thousand sets of armor from the Pacification Commission stores, and rise in rebellion. They debated enthroning a descendant of Prince Yu Yannian; the rebels acclaimed Old Monk the Superintendent as pacification commissioner, and the pasture domains north and south of the mountains all joined. Dihua Pasturage Commissioner Tushan Saili, Yelüwa Pasturage Commissioner Hesou, and others were killed; their story is told in the "Biography of Hesou." Old Monk Naye of the Wuyuan Department also killed Military Commissioner Shujiawuzhe to join Saba's revolt.
20
會甯八猛安牧馬於山后，至迪謀魯，賊盡奪其馬。 辟沙河千戶十哥等與前招討使完顏麻潑殺烏古迪列招討使烏林答蒲盧虎，以所部趨西北路。 室魯部節度使阿廝列追擊敗之，十哥與數騎遁去，合於撒八。
Ningba Company Commander was grazing horses in the northern hills; when he reached Dimeru, the rebels seized every mount. Company Commander Shige of Pishahe, together with former Pacification Commissioner Wanyan Mapo, killed Uguidilie Pacification Commissioner Ulinada Puluhu and marched their units toward the Northwestern Route. Shilu Department Military Commissioner Asilie pursued and routed them; Shige escaped with a few riders and rejoined Saba.
21
咸平府謀克括裏，與所部自山后逃歸，咸平少尹完顏餘裏野欲收捕括裏家屬，括裏與其党招誘富家奴隸，數日得眾二千，遂攻陷韓州及柳河縣，遂趨咸平。 餘裏野發兵迎擊之，兵敗，賊遂據咸平，於是繕完器甲，出府庫財物以募兵，賊勢益張。 權曹家山猛安綽質，集兵千餘，扼幹夜河，賊不得東。 綽質兵敗，括裏遂犯濟州。 會宿直將軍孛術魯吳括剌徵兵于速頻路，遇括裏於信州，與猛安烏延查剌兵二千，擊敗括裏。 括裏收余眾趨東京，是時世宗為東京留守，以兵四百人拒之。 賊至常安縣，聞空中擊鼓聲如數千鼓者，候見旌旗蔽野，傳言留守以十萬兵至矣，即引還，亦以其眾合於撒八。
Company Commander Kuoli of Xianping Prefecture fled back from the north with his command; when Vice Magistrate Wanyan Yuliye tried to seize Kuoli's family, Kuoli and his faction recruited slaves from wealthy households and within days rallied two thousand men, captured Han Prefecture and Liuhe County, and marched on Xianping. Yuliye marched out to meet them and was defeated; the rebels seized Xianping, refitted arms and armor, spent treasury funds to recruit troops, and their strength swelled. Acting Caojiashan Company Commander Chuezhi mustered over a thousand men and held the Ganye River, blocking the rebels' eastward advance. Chuezhi was defeated, and Kuoli invaded Ji Prefecture. Palace Duty General Bieshulu Wukuala was raising troops on the Supin Route when he met Kuoli at Xin Prefecture; with two thousand men under Company Commander Wuyan Zhala he routed Kuoli. Kuoli regrouped and marched on the Eastern Capital; Emperor Shizong, then resident commissioner there, met him with four hundred troops. At Chang'an County the rebels heard thunderous drumming as of a thousand drums, saw banners blanketing the fields, and heard that the resident commissioner was coming with a hundred thousand men; they retreated and rejoined Saba.
22
海陵使樞密使僕散忽土、西京留守蕭懷忠將兵一萬，與右衛將軍蕭禿剌討平之。 禿剌與之相持數日，連與戰皆無功，而糧餉不繼，禿剌退歸臨潢。 禿剌雖不能克敵，而撒八自度大軍必相繼而至，勢不可支，謀歸於大石，乃率眾沿龍駒河西出。 及僕散忽土、蕭懷忠等兵至，與禿剌合兵追至河上，不及而還。 忽土、懷忠、禿剌坐逗遛不即追賊，皆誅死。 北京留守蕭賾不能制其下，殺降人而取其婦女，亦坐誅。 於是，白彥恭為北面兵馬都統，紇石烈志寧副之，守顏彀英為西北面兵馬都統，西北路招討使唐括孛姑的副之，以討撒八等。
Hailing dispatched Privy Councilor Pusan Hutu and Western Capital Resident Commissioner Xiao Huaizhong with ten thousand troops, along with Right Guard General Xiao Tuci, to put down the rebellion. Tuci faced them for days but won no battle; supplies ran short, and he withdrew to Linhuang. Though Tuci failed to crush them, Saba judged that Jin armies would keep coming and his position was untenable; he planned to join Da Shi and led his forces west along the Longju River. When Pusan Hutu and Xiao Huaizhong arrived and combined with Tuci to pursue to the river, they failed to catch Saba and turned back. Hutu, Huaizhong, and Tuci were executed for dragging their feet and failing to pursue the rebels immediately. Northern Capital Resident Commissioner Xiao Ze lost control of his men, who slaughtered captives and seized their women; he too was executed. Bai Yangong was appointed Northern Route commander-in-chief with Heshenlie Zhining as his deputy; Shouyan Gouying became Northwestern Route commander-in-chief with Northwestern Route Pacification Commissioner Tangguo Begede as deputy, to campaign against Saba.
23
撒八既西行，而舊居山前者皆不欲往，偽署六院節度使移刺窩斡、兵官陳家殺撒八，執老和尚、孛特補等。 至是，窩斡始自為都元帥，陳家為都監，擁眾東還，至臨潢府東南新羅寨。 世宗使移剌紮八、前押軍謀克播斡、前牌印麻駭、利涉軍節度判官馬腦等招之。 紮八等見窩斡，以上意諭之。 窩斡已約降，已而複謂紮八曰：「若降，爾能保我輩無事乎？ 」紮八曰：「我知招降耳，其他豈能必哉。」
Once Saba marched west, his followers from south of the mountains refused to follow; the rebel Six Circuits Military Commissioner Yelü Wo Wo and Military Officer Chen Jia killed Saba and seized Old Monk, Betebu, and the rest. Wo Wo now declared himself commander-in-chief, with Chen Jia as superintendent, and led his forces east to Xinlou Stockade southeast of Linhuang. Emperor Shizong sent Yelü Zhaba, former Escort Company Commander Bowo, former Paoyin Mahai, and Lishe Army adjutant Ma Nao to offer them surrender terms. Zhaba and his party met Wo Wo and delivered the emperor's message. Wo Wo had agreed to surrender, then asked Zhaba: "If we submit, can you guarantee our safety? " Zhaba replied, "I can only bring you terms of surrender—beyond that I cannot promise anything."
24
紮八見窩斡兵眾強，車帳滿野，意其可以有成，因說之曰：「我之始來，以汝輩不能有為，今觀兵勢強盛如此，汝等欲如群羊為人所驅去乎，將欲待天時乎？ 若果有大志，吾亦不復還矣。 」賊將有前孛特本部族節度使逐斡者，言：「昔谷神丞相，賢能人也，嘗說他日西北部族當有事。 今日正合此語，恐不可降也。 」於是，窩斡遂決意不復肯降矣。 紮八亦留賊中，惟麻駭、播斡還歸。 窩斡乃引兵攻臨潢府，總管移室懣出城戰，兵少被執，賊遂圍臨潢，眾至五萬。 正隆六年十二月己亥，窩斡遂稱帝，改元天正。
Seeing Wo Wo's vast encampment, Zhaba changed his mind and urged him on: "When I arrived I thought you had no chance—but with strength like this, will you be driven like sheep, or wait for Heaven's moment? If you truly mean to fight, I will not go back. " Rebel general Zhuwo, former Betebu Tribal Military Commissioner, said: "Chancellor Gushen once prophesied that the northwestern tribes would rise one day. Today proves him right—we cannot surrender. Wo Wo then firmly refused to surrender. Zhaba stayed with the rebels; only Mahai and Bowo returned. Wo Wo marched on Linhuang; Supervisor Yishi Men sallied out with too few men, was captured, and the rebels besieged the city with fifty thousand troops. On the jihai day of the twelfth month of Zhenglong 6 (1161), Wo Wo declared himself emperor under the era name Tianzheng.
25
是時，北面都統白彥敬、副統紇石烈志寧在北京，聞世宗即位，以兵來歸。 世宗使元帥左都監吾紮忽、同知北京留守事完顏骨只救臨潢，晝夜兼行，比至臨潢，賊已解圍去攻泰州。 吾紮忽追及於窊曆，兩軍已陣將戰，押軍猛安契丹忽剌叔以所部兵應賊，吾紮忽軍遂敗。
Northern Route commander Bai Yanjing and his deputy Heshenlie Zhining were at the Northern Capital; on hearing of Emperor Shizong's accession they marched south to submit. Emperor Shizong sent Left Army Supervisor Wuzhahu and Associate Northern Capital Resident Commissioner Wanyan Guzhi to relieve Linhuang; they marched day and night, but the rebels had already broken off the siege and moved on Taizhou. Wuzhahu caught them at Wali as both sides deployed for battle; Escort Company Commander Hulashu the Khitan went over to the rebels with his unit, and Wuzhahu was routed.
26
泰州節度使烏裏雅率千餘騎與窩斡遇，烏裏雅兵複敗，僅以數騎脫歸。 賊勢愈振，城中震駭，莫敢出戰。 賊四面登城，押軍猛安烏古孫阿裏補率軍士數人，各持刀以身率先循城擊賊力戰，斫刈甚眾，賊乃退走，城賴以完。 泰州司吏顏盞蒲查奏捷，除忠翊校尉，賜銀五十兩、重彩十端。
Taizhou Military Commissioner Wuliya met Wo Wo with over a thousand cavalry, was routed again, and escaped with only a handful of riders. The rebels' momentum swelled; terror gripped the city and no one dared sally out. Rebels scaled the walls on every side; Escort Company Commander Wugusun Alibu led a handful of soldiers sword in hand along the ramparts and cut down many rebels until they withdrew; the city held. Taizhou clerk Yanzhan Pucha reported the victory and was made Loyal Assistant Commander, rewarded with fifty taels of silver and ten bolts of brocade.
27
二年正月，右副元帥完顏謀衍率諸軍北征窩斡。 二月壬戌詔曰：「應諸人若能於契丹賊中自拔歸者，更不問元初首從及被威脅之由，奴婢、良人罪無輕重並行免放。 曾有官職及糾率人眾來歸者，仍與官賞，依本品量材敘使。 其同來人各從所願處收系，有才能者亦與錄用。 內外官員郎君群牧直撒百姓人家驅奴、宮籍監人等，並放為良，亦從所願處收系，與免三年差役。 或能捕殺首領而歸者，准上施行，仍驗勞績約量遷賞。 如捕獲窩斡者，猛安加三品官授節度使，謀克加四品官授防禦使，庶人加五品官授刺史。 」詔曰：「尚書省，如節度防禦使捉獲窩斡者與世襲猛安，刺史捉獲者與世襲謀克，驅奴、宮籍監人亦與庶人同。 」複詔宰臣，遍諭將士，能捕殺窩斡者加特進、授真總管。
In the first month of year 2, Right Deputy Commander Wanyan Moyan led the armies north against Wo Wo. On the renxu day of the second month an edict declared: "Anyone who breaks away from the Khitan rebels and returns shall not be questioned about past leadership or coercion; slaves and commoners alike, whatever their offense, shall be pardoned. Former officials and those who bring followers home shall still receive rank and reward, appointed according to their prior rank and merit. Their companions may settle wherever they choose; the capable shall also be enlisted. Officials, langjun, pasture guards, zhi sa commoners, household slaves, palace registry overseers, and the like shall all be freed; they too may settle where they wish and be exempt from corvée for three years. Those who capture or kill rebel leaders shall receive the rewards above, with promotion scaled to their merit. Whoever captures Wo Wo shall receive: company commanders promoted three ranks to military commissioner, platoon commanders four ranks to defense commissioner, commoners five ranks to prefect. " A further edict to the Secretariat: "Military or defense commissioners who capture Wo Wo receive hereditary company commands; prefects receive hereditary platoon commands; bondsmen and palace overseers are treated as commoners. " The chief ministers were further instructed to tell every soldier that whoever captures or kills Wo Wo shall be made Grand Mentor and appointed full commander-in-chief.
28
於是，括裏將犯韓州，聞元帥兵至，不戰遁去，將轉趨懿、宜州。 謀衍屯懿州慶雲縣，及屯川州武平縣，奏請糧運當遣人護送，兵仗乞選精良者付之。 詔以南征逃還軍士就往屯戍，如不足，量于富家簽調，就近地簽步軍，給仗護送糧運。 詔平章政事移剌元宜往泰州規措邊事。 前安遠大將軍斡裏嫋、猛安七斤、庶人阿裏葛、磨哥等自窩斡中來降，斡裏嫋、七斤加昭武大將軍，阿裏葛武義將軍，磨哥忠勇校尉。
Kuoli was marching on Han Prefecture when he heard the imperial army was coming; he fled without a fight and turned toward Yi and Yizhou. Moyan encamped at Qingyun in Yi Prefecture and Wuping in Chuan Prefecture, requesting escorts for supply convoys and the best arms for them. An edict ordered soldiers returned from the southern campaign reassigned to garrison duty; if more were needed, conscript from wealthy households and local infantry to escort the supply trains. Grand Counselor Yelü Yuanyi was ordered to Taizhou to manage frontier affairs. Former Anyuan General Woli Niao, Company Commander Qijin, and commoners Alige and Moge defected from Wo Wo's camp; Woli Niao and Qijin were made Generals of Manifest Martiality, Alige General of Martial Righteousness, and Moge Loyal Valor Commander.
29
窩斡遂自泰州往攻濟州，欲邀糧運。 元帥完顏謀衍與右監軍完顏福壽、左都監吾紮忽合兵，甲士萬三千人，曷懶路總管徒單克甯、廣甯尹僕散渾坦、同知廣甯尹完顏岩雅、肇州防禦使唐括烏也為左翼，臨海節度使紇石烈志寧、曷速館節度使神土懣、同知北京留守完顏骨只、淄州刺史尼龐古鈔兀為右翼，至術虎崖，盡委輜重，士卒齎數日糧，輕騎襲之。
Wo Wo marched from Taizhou to attack Ji Prefecture, aiming to intercept the supply convoys. Commander Wanyan Moyan joined Right Army Supervisor Wanyan Fushou and Left Army Supervisor Wuzhahu with thirteen thousand armored troops; the left wing under Helan Route Supervisor Tushan Kening, Guangning Prefect Pusan Hundan, Associate Guangning Prefect Wanyan Yanya, and Zhaozhou Defense Commissioner Tangguo Wuye, and the right wing under Linhai Military Commissioner Heshenlie Zhining, Esuguan Military Commissioner Sheng Tumu, Associate Northern Capital Resident Commissioner Wanyan Guzhi, and Zizhou Prefect Nipanggu Chaowu, reached Shuhuya, abandoned all baggage, and with several days' rations carried by the troops struck with light cavalry.
30
糺椀群牧人契丹颭者，與其弟孛迭、挼剌，皆棄家自賊中來降。 糺者謂謀衍曰：「賊中馬肥健，官軍馬疲弱，此去賊八十里，比遇賊馬已憊。 賊輜重去此不遠，我攻之，賊必救其巢穴，賊至馬必疲，我馬少得息，所謂攻其所必救，以逸待勞者也。 」謀衍從之，乘夜亟發，會大風路暗不能辨，遲明行三十里許，與賊輜重相近，整兵少憩。 窩斡趨濟州，知大軍取其輜重，乃還救，遇於長濼。 既陣，謀衍別設伏於左翼之側，賊四百餘騎突出左翼伏兵之間，徒單克寧射卻之。 是日，別部諸將與賊對者，勝負未分，相去五里許而立。 左翼萬戶襄別與賊戰，賊陣動，襄麾軍乘之，突出其後，俱與大軍不相及。 襄以善射者二十騎，率眾自賊後擊之，賊不能支，乘勢麾軍擊其一偏，賊遂卻。 襄遂與大軍合，而別部諸將皆至，整陣力戰，忽反風揚砂石，賊陣亂，官軍馳擊，大破之。 追北十餘裏，斬獲甚眾。 詔以糺者為武義將軍，孛迭昭信校尉，挼剌忠翊校尉。 糺除同知建州事，未之官，卒。 孛迭取家賊中，遂被害，上憫之，後以挼剌為汝州都巡檢使。
Zhanzhe the Khitan of Jiuwan Pasturage, with his brothers Bedie and Roula, deserted their families and defected from the rebels. Zhanzhe told Moyan: "The rebels' horses are fresh and strong, ours are tired; they are eighty li away—by the time we meet them their mounts will be spent. Their baggage train is close; strike it and they must come to its defense—they will arrive exhausted while ours rest. That is the classic 'attack what they must save' and meet strength with rest. Moyan agreed and marched by night; a gale blew and the road was dark; at dawn, after thirty li, they were near the rebel baggage and halted to regroup. Wo Wo was racing toward Ji Prefecture when he learned the Jin army had seized his baggage; he turned back to rescue it and met the Jin forces at Changpo. As the armies deployed, Moyan hid troops on the left flank; four hundred rebel riders burst through the ambush line, and Tushan Kening drove them back with arrows. That day other detachments faced the rebels without a clear outcome, standing some five li apart. Left-wing commander Xiangbie engaged the rebels; when their line wavered he pressed the attack, swung behind them, and was cut off from the main force. Xiang took twenty crack archers and attacked from the rear; the rebels buckled; he pressed one flank and drove them back. Xiang rejoined the main army; all detachments converged and fought hard; a sudden headwind whipped up sand and gravel; the rebel line broke; the imperial army charged and smashed them. The pursuit ran more than ten li, with heavy casualties and captures. Zhanzhe was made General of Martial Righteousness, Bedie Trustworthy Commander, and Roula Loyal Assistant Commander. Zhan was appointed Associate Administrator of Jianzhou but died before taking office. Bedie went back into rebel territory to fetch his family and was killed; the emperor mourned him and later made Roula Chief Inspector of Youzhou.
31
窩斡率其眾西走，謀衍追及之於霿𩃭河。 賊已濟，毀其津口，紇石烈志寧軍先至，不克渡，乃對岸為疑兵，以夾穀清臣、徒單海羅兩萬戶於下流渡河，值支港兩岸鬥絕且濘淖，命軍士束柳填港而過。 追之數裏，得平地，方食，賊眾奄至。 志寧軍急整陣，賊自南岡馳下，沖陣者三，志寧力戰，流矢中左臂，戰自若。 大軍畢至，左翼騎兵先與賊接，賊據上風縱火，乘煙擊官軍，官軍步兵亦至，並力合戰，凡十余合，軍士苦風煙皆植立如癡。 會天降雨，風止，官軍奮擊，大敗之。 徒單克寧追奔十五里，賊前厄溪澗不得亟渡，多殺傷。 賊既渡，官軍亦渡，少憩，賊反旆來攻，克甯以大軍不繼，令軍士皆下馬射賊。 賊引卻而南，克寧亦將引而北，士未及騎馬，賊複來衝突，官軍少卻，回渡澗北。 大軍至，賊遂引去。
Wo Wo fled west; Moyan caught up with him at the Menglong River. The rebels had already crossed and destroyed the ford; Heshenlie Zhining's vanguard could not follow; he feinted from the opposite bank while Ten-thousand Household Commanders Jiagu Qingchen and Tushan Hailuo forded downstream; where side channels ran steep and miry, he ordered willow bundles packed into the mud to bridge a crossing. Several li into the pursuit they reached open ground and were at meals when rebels appeared without warning. Zhining's troops scrambled into formation as rebels swept down from the southern ridge and charged three times; an arrow struck Zhining's left arm, but he fought on without faltering. The main army arrived; left-wing cavalry engaged first as the rebels, holding the upwind, set fires and struck through the smoke; infantry joined and both sides fought through more than ten clashes; choked by wind and smoke, soldiers stood dazed as if stunned. Rain fell, the wind died, and the imperial army struck hard and routed them. Tushan Kening pursued fifteen li; rebels bottlenecked at streams and ravines suffered heavy losses. After both sides crossed, the rebels wheeled about and attacked; with the main army not yet up, Kening ordered his men to dismount and shoot. The rebels pulled back south; Kening began to withdraw north, but before his men could remount the rebels charged again; the imperial troops fell back across the stream. When the main army arrived, the rebels withdrew.
32
四月，詔元帥府曰； 「應契丹賊人，與大兵未戰已前投降者，不得殺傷，仍加安撫。 敗走以後，招誘來降者，除奴婢准已虜為定外，親屬分付圓聚。 仍官為換贖。」
In the fourth month an edict was sent to the Marshal's Headquarters: "Khitan rebels who surrender before battle shall not be harmed and shall be reassured. Those who surrender after defeat, aside from bondsmen already taken captive, shall have their kin gathered and reunited. The state shall also redeem them."
33
窩斡既敗，謀衍不復追討，駐軍白濼。 窩斡攻懿州不克，遂殘破川州，將遁於山西，而北京亦不邀擊之。 於是，發驍騎軍二千、曷懶路留屯京師軍三千，號稱二萬，會寧濟州軍六千亦號二萬。 元帥左都監高忠建總兵，沃州刺史烏古論蒲查為曷懶路押軍萬戶，祁州刺史烏林答剌撒為濟州押軍萬戶，右驍騎副都指揮使烏延查剌為驍騎萬戶，祁州刺史宗甯為會甯路押軍萬戶，右宣徽使宗亨為北京路都統，吏部郎中完顏達吉為副統，會元帥府討擊之。
After Wo Wo's defeat, Moyan stopped pursuing and encamped at Baipo. Wo Wo failed to take Yi Prefecture, ravaged Chuan Prefecture, and prepared to flee west into the hills, yet the Northern Capital force did not intercept him. Two thousand Elite Cavalry and three thousand Helan Route garrison troops from the capital were dispatched under the banner of twenty thousand; six thousand from Huining and Ji Prefecture were likewise counted as twenty thousand. Left Army Supervisor Gao Zhongjian took overall command; Wozhou Prefect Wugulun Pucha became Helan Route escort commander, Qizhou Prefect Ulinada Lasa Ji Prefecture escort commander, Right Elite Cavalry Deputy Commander Wuyan Zhala Elite Cavalry commander, Qizhou Prefect Zongning Huining Route escort commander, Right Palace Commissioner Zongheng Northern Capital Route commander-in-chief, and Ministry of Personnel Director Wanyan Daji his deputy; together they joined the Marshal's Headquarters to hunt the rebels down.
34
詔使尚廄局副使蒲察蒲盧渾往懿州戒敕將帥，上曰：「朕委卿等討賊，乃聞不就賊趨戰，而駐兵閑緩，經涉累月，雖曾追襲，乃不由有水草之地，以致馬疲弱不能百里而還。 後雖破賊，而縱諸軍劫掠，數日後方追北霿𩃭河，亦不乘勝，輒複引還。 賊遂入涉近地，北京、懿州由此受兵。 朕欲重譴汝等，以方任兵事，且圖後功。 當盡心一力，毋得似前怠弛。 」上謂蒲盧渾曰：「卿若聞賊在近，即當監督討伐。 用命力戰者疏記以聞，朕將約量遷賞。 無或承徇上官，抑有功、濫署無功者。 善戢士卒、勿縱虜掠。 」以紇石烈志甯為元帥右監軍，右副元帥完顏謀衍、元帥右監軍完顏福壽召還京師，咸平路總管完顏兀帶復舊職。 謀衍男斜哥在軍中多暴橫，詔押歸本管。 窩斡使所親招節度使移裏堇窟域，窟域執其使送官，與窩斡連戰有功，遷宣武將軍，賜銀五百兩、衣二襲。 起運在中都弓萬五千、箭一百五十萬赴懿州。
Deputy Stable Commissioner Pucha Puluhun was sent to Yi Prefecture to rebuke the generals; the emperor said: "I charged you to suppress the rebels, yet you linger in camp instead of closing with the enemy; though you pursued once, you avoided watered pasture and your horses grew too weak to chase a hundred li. Even after you broke them, you allowed looting and waited days before pursuing to the Menglong River—and then failed to press the advantage and withdrew again. The rebels then raided the frontier; Beijing and Yi Prefecture bore the brunt. I would punish you severely, but with the campaign still in your hands I am giving you one more chance. Devote yourselves fully and do not repeat such negligence. " He told Puluhun: "If you hear the rebels are near, supervise the attack at once. Report those who fight with all their strength; I shall reward them according to merit." Do not curry favor with superiors, suppress the deserving, or promote the undeserving. Keep your troops under discipline and do not permit looting. " Heshenlie Zhining was appointed Right Army Supervisor; Right Deputy Commander Wanyan Moyan and Right Army Supervisor Wanyan Fushou were recalled to the capital, and Xianping Route Supervisor Wanyan Udai returned to his former post. Moyan's son Xiege had been bullying the troops; he was ordered escorted back to his home command. Wo Wo sent an agent to win over Military Commissioner Yiliqin Kuyu; Kuyu arrested the envoy and turned him in; for repeated victories against Wo Wo he was promoted to General of Manifest Martiality and given five hundred taels of silver and two suits of clothing. Fifteen thousand bows and one and a half million arrows were shipped from the Central Capital to Yi Prefecture.
35
平章政事移剌元宜、甯昌軍節度使宗敘入見，詔使自中道卻還軍中，宣諭元宜、謀衍注意經略邊事。 師久無功，尚書右丞僕散忠義願效死力除邊患，世宗嘉歎。 六月，忠義拜平章政事兼右副元帥，宗敘為兵部尚書，各賜弓矢、具鞍勒馬。 出內府金銀十萬兩佐軍用。 詔曰：「軍中將士有犯，除連職奏聞，餘依軍法約量決責，有功者依格遷賞。 」以大名尹宗尹為河南路統軍使，河南路統軍都監蒲察世傑為西北路副統，賜弓矢佩刀廄馬，從忠義征行。 詔諭諸軍將士曰：「兵久駐邊陲，蠹費財用無成功，百姓不得休息。 今命平章政事僕散忠義兼右副元帥，同心戮力以底戡定。 右副元帥謀衍罷為同判大宗正事。」
Grand Councilor Yili Yuanxuan and Ningchang Army Military Commissioner Zongxu were summoned to court, then ordered to turn back mid-journey and return to their commands, with instructions that Yuanxuan and Moyan must give priority to border strategy. The campaign had dragged on without success; Vice Director Pusan Zhongyi offered to give his life to end the border crisis; Emperor Shizong praised him warmly. In the sixth month Zhongyi was made Grand Councilor and Right Deputy Commander, Zongxu Minister of War; each received bow and arrows and a saddled war horse. One hundred thousand taels of gold and silver from the palace treasury were allocated for the campaign. An edict declared: "Offenses among the rank and file shall be punished under military law at the commander's discretion, except cases implicating an entire chain of command, which must be reported to the throne; the meritorious shall be promoted according to regulation. " Daming Prefect Zongyin was appointed Henan Route commander, Army Supervisor Pucha Shijie Northwestern Route deputy commander; each received bow and arrows, sword, and remount, and joined Zhongyi's campaign. An edict to the armies read: "Your long encampment on the frontier has drained the treasury without victory, and the people know no peace. Grand Councilor Pusan Zhongyi is now Right Deputy Commander; unite in full force to bring this rebellion to an end. Right Deputy Commander Moyan is relieved and appointed Associate Minister of the Imperial Clan Office."
36
詔居庸關、古北口譏察契丹奸細，捕獲者加官賞。 萬戶溫蒂罕阿魯帶以兵四千屯古北口，薊州、石門關等處各以五百人守之。 海陵末年，阿魯帶為猛安，移剌娜為牌印祗候，起契丹部族兵被執，至是挺身來降。 世宗以阿魯帶為濟州押軍萬戶，移剌娜為同知濼州事。
Posts at Juyong Pass and Gubeikou were ordered to watch for Khitan spies; captors would receive promotion and reward. Commander Wendihan Aludai took four thousand men to Gubeikou; Jizhou, Shimen Pass, and other posts received five hundred men each. In Hailing's final years Aludai had been a meng'an and Yili Na a paoyin attendant; when Khitan tribal levies were seized for conscription, both now came forward to surrender. Emperor Shizong appointed Aludai Ji Prefecture escort commander and Yili Na associate prefect of Woluo.
37
西南路招討使完顏思敬為都統，賜金牌一、銀牌二，西北路招討使唐括孛古底副之。 以兵五千往會燕子城舊戍軍，視地形沖要或於狗濼屯駐，遠斥候，賊至即戰，不以晝夜為限。 詔思敬曰：「契丹賊敗必走山后，可選新馬三千，加芻纇以備追襲。」
Southwestern Route Pacification Commissioner Wanyan Sijing was made overall commander with one gold and two silver tablets; Northwestern Route Pacification Commissioner Tangguo Begede served as his deputy. He was to join the old garrison at Swallow City with five thousand men, encamp at any strategic point such as Goupo, post distant scouts, and engage the enemy whenever they appeared, day or night. Sijing was instructed: "Defeated Khitan rebels will flee north into the hills; ready three thousand fresh horses with fodder for the pursuit."
38
僕散忠義至軍中。 是時，窩斡西走花道，眾尚八萬。 忠義、高忠建軍與賊遇，萬戶查剌、蒲查為左翼，宗亨統之； 宗寧、剌撒為右翼，宗敘統之； 世傑亦在左翼中，與賊夾河為陣。 賊渡河，以兵四萬餘先犯左翼軍，查剌以六百騎奮擊敗之。 複以四萬眾與左翼軍戰，宗亨、世傑七謀克指畫失宜，陣亂敗於賊。 世傑挺身投于查剌軍中，賊圍查剌軍，查剌力戰，宗敘以右翼軍來救，賊乃去。
Pusan Zhongyi reached the front. Wo Wo was fleeing west along Huadao with some eighty thousand men still in his train. Zhongyi and Gao Zhongjian's forces met the rebels; Commanders Zhala and Pucha formed the left wing under Zongheng; Zongning and Lasa the right wing under Zongxu; Shijie fought in the left wing; the two sides faced each other across a river. The rebels forded the river and hit the left wing first with forty thousand men; Zhala charged with six hundred horsemen and broke them. They returned with forty thousand and assailed the left wing again; Zongheng and Shijie's seven company commanders bungled their deployment, the line collapsed, and the rebels prevailed. Shijie plunged into Zhala's ranks; the rebels surrounded Zhala's contingent; Zhala fought on until Zongxu brought the right wing to the rescue and the enemy withdrew.
39
詔曰：「自契丹作逆，有為賊詿誤者，不問如何從賊，但能複業，與免本罪。 如能率眾來附，或能殺捕首領而降，或執送賊所扇誘作亂之人，皆與量加官爵。 朕念正隆南征，猛安亡者招還被戮，已命其子孫襲其職。 爾等勿懲前事，故懷遲疑。 賊軍今既破散，山后諸處皆命將士遏其逃路，爾等雖欲不降終將安往？ 若猶疑貳，俱就焚滅，悔及矣。」
An edict declared: "Since the Khitan rebellion began, all who were led astray—whatever their past offense—shall be pardoned if they return to honest livelihood. Those who bring followers to submit, capture rebel leaders, or deliver the agitators shall receive rank and honors according to their deeds. Recall that in Hailing's southern campaign, fugitive meng'an who returned were put to death—I have already ordered their heirs restored to office. Do not let past examples make you hesitate. The rebel host is shattered, and troops throughout the northern hills block every escape route—if you will not surrender, where can you go? Persist in doubt and you will all be destroyed—and then it will be too late for regret."
40
窩斡自花道西走，僕散忠義、紇石烈志甯以大軍追及於嫋嶺西陷泉。 明日，賊軍三萬騎涉水而東。 大軍先據南岡，左翼軍自岡為陣，迤邐而北，步軍繼之，右翼軍繼步軍北引而東，作偃月陣，步軍居中，騎兵據其兩端，使賊不見首尾。 是日，大霧晦冥，既陣霧開，少頃晴霽，賊見左翼據南岡不敢擊，擊右翼軍，烏延查剌力戰，賊稍卻。 志甯與夾穀清臣、烏林答剌撒、鐸剌合戰，賊大敗，將涉水去，泥濘不得亟渡。 大軍逐北，人馬相蹂踐而死，不可勝數，陷泉皆平，餘眾蹈籍而過，或奔潰竄匿林莽間。 大軍踵擊之，俘斬萬計，生擒其弟偽六院司大王嫋。 窩斡僅與數騎脫去，鈔兀、清臣追四十餘裏不及，斬千餘級，獲車帳甚眾。 其母徐輦舉營自落括岡西走，志寧追之，盡獲輜重，俘五萬餘人，雜畜不可勝計。 偽節度使六及其部族皆降。
Wo Wo fled west from Huadao; Pusan Zhongyi and Heshenlie Zhining overtook him with the main army at Xianquan, west of Niaoling. The next day thirty thousand rebel horsemen forded the stream heading east. The main force seized the southern ridge first; the left wing lined the ridge northward, infantry behind, the right wing trailing north then east in a crescent—the infantry in the center, cavalry at both horns, hiding the army's full extent from the enemy. That day a dense fog shrouded the field; when the lines were set the fog lifted and sunshine followed; seeing the left wing hold the ridge, the rebels attacked the right instead; Wuyan Zhala fought hard and drove them back. Zhining, Jiagu Qingchen, Ulinada Lasa, and Dola pressed the attack; the rebels broke utterly; as they tried to ford the stream, mud trapped them on the bank. The Jin army pressed the pursuit; men and horses trampled one another to death beyond count until Xianquan was level with the corpses; survivors scrambled over the dead or fled into the brush. The pursuit continued; tens of thousands were killed or captured, including Wo Wo's brother, the rebel Six Circuits Commissioner Prince Niao. Wo Wo escaped with a handful of riders; Chaowu and Qingchen chased forty li without catching him, cutting down over a thousand and seizing vast stores of wagons and tents. Wo Wo's mother Xulian broke camp west of Luoguo Ridge; Zhining pursued, seizing all her baggage, taking fifty thousand prisoners, and livestock beyond count. The rebel Military Commissioner Liu and his followers all submitted.
41
詔北京副統完顏達吉括本部馬，規辦芻糧，仍使達吉為監戰官，錄有功者聞奏。 詔選中都、西京兩路新舊軍萬人備守禦，以窩斡敗走，恐或衝突也。
Northern Capital Deputy Commander Wanyan Daji was ordered to round up his own command's horses and gather fodder; he was made battle overseer and instructed to report all meritorious service. Ten thousand troops from the Central and Western Capital circuits were mobilized for defense, lest the fleeing Wo Wo strike at vulnerable points.
42
僕散忠義使使奏捷，詔略曰：「平章政事右副元帥忠義使使來奏大捷。 或被軍俘獲，或自能來服，或無所歸而投拜，或將全屬歸附，或分領家族來降，或嘗受偽命，及自來曾與官軍鬥敵，皆釋其罪。 其散亡人內，除窩斡一身，不以大小官員是何名色，卻來歸附者，亦准釋放。 有能誅捕窩斡，或於不從招納亡去人內誅捕以來，及或能率眾于掌軍官及隨處官司投降者，並給官賞。 各路撫納來者，毋得輒加侵損。 無資給者，不以是何路分，隨有糧處安置，仍官為養濟。」
Pusan Zhongyi's envoys reported a great victory; the edict in part read: "Grand Councilor and Right Deputy Commander Zhongyi's messengers report a decisive victory. Whether taken in battle, coming of their own will, having nowhere else to turn, bringing whole followings or families to submit, or even if they had held rebel office and fought the imperial army—all crimes are forgiven. Among the scattered fugitives, all who return to submit—regardless of rank or title—shall be pardoned, save Wo Wo alone. Whoever kills or captures Wo Wo, or brings in fugitives who refused surrender, or leads followers to submit to any army post or local office shall receive rank and reward. Every circuit receiving surrendering rebels must not molest them. Those without means shall be settled wherever food is available, regardless of jurisdiction, and supported at public expense."
43
窩斡收合散卒萬餘人，遂入奚部，以諸奚自益，時時出兵寇速魯古澱、古北口、興化之間。 溫蒂罕阿魯帶守古北口，與戰敗焉。 詔完顏謀衍、蒲察烏裏雅、蒲察蒲盧渾以兵三千，合舊屯兵五千，擊之。 詔守顏思敬以所部兵入奚地，會大軍討窩斡。
Wo Wo regrouped over ten thousand scattered troops and entered Xi territory, recruiting Xi bands to his strength and raiding between Surug Lake, Gubeikou, and Xinghua. Wendihan Aludai, defending Gubeikou, was defeated. Wanyan Moyan, Pucha Wuliya, and Pucha Puluhun were ordered to attack with three thousand men, joined to five thousand garrison troops. Wanyan Sijing was ordered to take his command into Xi territory and join the main army against Wo Wo.
44
賊黨霿𩃭河猛安蒲速越遣人至帥府約降，詔令擒捕窩斡，許以官賞。 賊將降者甚眾。 其散走者聞詔書招降，亦多降者。 其餘多疾疫而死，無複鬥志。 窩斡自度勢窮，乃謀自羊城道西京奔夏國，大軍追之益急，其眾複多亡去，度不得西，乃北走沙陀間。 詔尚書省：「凡脅從之家被俘掠遂致離散，宜從改正。 將士往往藏匿其人，有司檢括分付。」
Menglong River meng'an Pususan Zhongyi, a rebel partisan, sent envoys to the marshal's headquarters offering to surrender on condition that Wo Wo be captured; rank and reward were promised. Many rebel officers surrendered. Scattered fugitives who heard the surrender edict also came in in great numbers. The rest died mostly of plague and lost all will to fight. Seeing his cause hopeless, Wo Wo planned to flee west through the Western Capital toward Xi Xia; pursued ever more closely, his followers deserted again; unable to go west, he turned north into Shatuo country. The Secretariat was instructed: "Families of the coerced who were captured and separated must be restored according to policy. Officers who hide such people must be searched out and the victims returned to their families."
45
監軍志寧獲賊稍合住，釋而弗殺，縱還賊中，使誘其親近捕窩斡以自效，許以官賞。 九月庚子，稍合住與神獨斡執窩斡，詣右都臨完顏思敬降，並獲其母徐輦及其妻、子、子婦、弟、侄，盡收偽金銀牌印。 唐括孛古底獲前胡裏改節度使什溫及其家屬。 西北路招討使李家奴獲偽樞密使逐斡等三十餘人，複與猛安泥本婆果追偽監軍那也至天成縣，那也乃降，乃獲偽都元帥醜哥及金牌一、銀牌五。 志甯與清臣、宗甯、速哥等追餘黨至燕子城，盡得其黨。 前至抹拔裏達之地，悉獲之，逆党遂平。
Supervisor Zhining captured the rebel Shahezhu, spared his life, and sent him back to the enemy camp to persuade his kinsmen to capture Wo Wo in exchange for rank and reward. On the gengzi day of the ninth month Shahezhu and Shenduwo seized Wo Wo and surrendered to Right Army Supervisor Wanyan Sijing, together with his mother Xulian, wife, sons, daughters-in-law, brother, and nephews; all rebel gold and silver seals were confiscated. Tangguo Begede captured former Huligai Military Commissioner Shiwen and his family. Northwestern Route Pacification Commissioner Lijianu captured the rebel Privy Councillor Zhuwo and thirty others; with meng'an Niben Boguo he chased rebel Army Supervisor Naye to Tiancheng County, where Naye surrendered and rebel Commander-in-Chief Chouge was taken along with one gold and five silver tablets. Zhining, Qingchen, Zongning, and Suge pursued the remaining rebels to Swallow City and rounded up the entire band. They pressed on to Mobalida and captured them all; the rebellion was fully suppressed.
46
甲辰，皇太子率百官上表賀。 乙巳，詔天下。 辛亥，完顏思敬獻俘于京師，窩斡梟首於市，磔其手足，分懸諸京府。 其母徐輦及妻子皆戮之。 契丹降人皆拘其器仗，貧不能自給者官為養濟。
On jiachen the crown prince led the court in a congratulatory memorial. On yisi an edict went out to the realm. On xinhai Wanyan Sijing presented the captives at the capital; Wo Wo's head was displayed in the marketplace, his limbs dismembered and hung in the major cities. His mother Xulian, wife, and children were all put to death. Surrendering Khitan were disarmed; those too poor to live on their own were maintained at public expense.
47
括裏、紮八率眾南走，詔左宣徽使宗亨追及之。 紮八詐稱降，宗亨信其言，遂不與戰。 紮八紿之曰：「括裏驚走，願追之。 」宗亨縱紮八去。 益都猛安欲以所部追括裏、紮八，宗亨恐分其功，不聽，而縱軍士取賊所棄資囊人畜而自有之。 括裏、紮入由是得亡去，遂奔于宋。 宗亨降甯州剌史。 其後，宋李世輔用括裏、紮八，遂取宿州，頗為邊患。
Kuoli and Zhaba fled south with their followers; Left Palace Commissioner Zongheng was ordered to overtake them. Zhaba feigned surrender; Zongheng believed him and withheld his attack. Zhaba deceived him: "Kuoli fled in panic—I wish to pursue him. " Zongheng released Zhaba. The Yidu meng'an wanted to chase Kuoli and Zhaba with his own men; Zongheng, fearing shared credit, refused—and instead let his soldiers help themselves to the rebels' abandoned baggage and livestock. Kuoli and Zhaba thus escaped and fled to the Song. Zongheng was demoted to prefect of Ning Prefecture. Later the Song general Li Shipu employed Kuoli and Zhaba to capture Suzhou, and they became a serious border threat.
48
神獨斡除同知安化軍節度使，稍合住除同知震武軍節度使事。 大定六年，點檢司奏，親軍中有逆黨子弟，請一切罷去。 詔曰：「身預逆黨者罷之，餘勿問。」
Shenduwo was made associate military commissioner of Anhua Army; Shahezhu associate military commissioner of Zhenwu Army. In Dading 6 (1166) the Inspectorate reported that the imperial guard contained descendants of rebel families and requested their wholesale dismissal. The edict replied: "Dismiss only those who took part in the rebellion; leave the rest alone."
49
贊曰：金人以燕山與宋，遂啟張覺跳樑之心，覺豈為宋者哉，蓋欲乘時以徼利耳。 耶律余睹從宗望追天祚，曾不遺餘力，功成驕溢，自取誅滅，咈哉。 正隆佳兵，契丹作難，《傳》曰：「夫兵猶火也，弗戢將自焚。 」可不戒哉！
Commentary: By ceding the Yan Mountains to the Song, the Jin gave Zhang Jue the idea of playing both sides—yet Jue was no friend of the Song; he simply meant to seize the moment for profit. Yelü Yudu followed Zongwang in hunting down Emperor Tianzuo with unstinting zeal—then, swollen with pride at his success, brought ruin on himself. How perverse! Hailing glorified war, and the Khitan rose in revolt. As the Commentary says: "War is like fire—if you do not restrain it, it burns you. " A warning not to be ignored!
