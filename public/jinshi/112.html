1
完顏合達
Wanyan Heda
2
完顏合達，名瞻，字景山。 少長兵間，習弓馬，能得人死力。 貞祐初，以親衛軍送岐國公主，充護衛。 三年，授臨潢府推官，權元帥右監軍。 時臨潢避遷，與全、慶兩州之民共壁平州。 合達隸其經略使烏林答乞住，乞住以便宜授軍中都統，累遷提控，佩金符。 未幾，會燕南諸帥將兵複中都城，行至平州遷安縣，臨潢、全慶兩軍變，殺乞住，擁合達還平州，推為帥，統乞住軍。 合達以計誅首亂者數人。 其年六月，北兵大將喊得不遣監戰提軍至平州城下，以州人黃裳入城招降，父老不從，合達引兵逆戰，知事勢不敵，以本軍降於陣。 監戰以合達北上，留半發，令還守平州。 已而，謀自拔歸，乃遣奉先縣令紇石烈布裏哥、北京教授蒲察胡裏安、右三部檢法蒲察蒲女涉海來報。
Wanyan Heda, whose given name was Zhan and whose courtesy name was Jingshan. He had grown up in military camps since childhood, trained in archery and horsemanship, and knew how to command men's utmost loyalty. Early in the Zhenyou reign (1213), he served with the imperial guard escort on the mission to deliver the Princess of Qi and acted as her escort. In the third year of the reign he was appointed investigating censor of Linhuang Prefecture and served concurrently as acting right army supervisor under the marshal. At that time Linhuang had been evacuated; its people, together with those of the Quan and Qing prefectures, made a joint stand at Pingzhou. Heda served under the regional commissioner Wulindada Qizhu, who by expedient authority appointed him chief commander in the army; through successive promotions he rose to tiju and was awarded a golden tally. Before long he joined the southern Yan commanders marching to recover the Central Capital; when they reached Qian'an County in Pingzhou, the Linhuang and Quan-Qing forces mutinied, killed Qizhu, escorted Heda back to Pingzhou, and made him commander over Qizhu's former army. Heda used stratagem to execute several of the ringleaders. That year in the sixth month the great northern general Jebe sent a supervisory battle officer with Tijun troops to Pingzhou; he used a local man, Huang Shang, to enter the city and offer terms, but the elders refused. Heda led his troops out to meet them, saw that the odds were hopeless, and surrendered his army on the field. The supervisory battle officer took Heda north but left Banfa behind and ordered Heda to return and hold Pingzhou. Before long, plotting to escape and return home, he sent Fengxian magistrate Heshenlie Bulige, Beiping instructor Pucha Hulian, and Right Three Departments inspector Pucha Punü across the sea to report.
3
四年十一月，合達果率所部及州民並海西南歸國。 詔進官三階，升鎮南軍節度使，駐益都，與元帥蒙古綱相應接，充宣差都提控。 十二月，大元兵徇地博興、樂安、壽光，東涉濰州之境，蒙古綱遣合達率兵屢戰于壽光、臨淄。 興定元年正月，轉通遠軍節度使、兼鞏州管內觀察使。 七月，改平西軍節度使、兼河州管內觀察使。 二年正月，知延安府事、兼鄜延路兵馬都總管。
In the eleventh month of the fourth year Heda did indeed lead his troops and the people of the prefecture by sea southwest to return to the Jin state. An edict advanced him three ranks and made him military commissioner of the Zhennan Army, stationed at Yidu to coordinate with Marshal Mongol Gang; he also served as supreme tiju on imperial commission. In the twelfth month Yuan forces overran Boxing, Le'an, and Shouguang and pushed east into Weizhou; Mongol Gang sent Heda to lead troops in repeated engagements at Shouguang and Linzi. In the first month of the first year of Xingding (1217) he was transferred to military commissioner of the Tongyuan Army and concurrently made commissioner for observation within Gong Prefecture. In the seventh month he was reassigned as military commissioner of the Pingxi Army and concurrently commissioner for observation within He Prefecture. In the first month of the second year he became prefect of Yan'an and concurrently overall commander of military affairs on the Fuyan circuit.
4
三年正月，詔伐宋，以合達為元帥右都監。 三月，破宋兵于梅林關，擒統領張時。 又敗宋兵于馬嶺堡，獲馬百匹。 又拔麻城縣，獲其令張倜、幹辦官郭守紀。
In the first month of the third year an edict ordered an attack on Song, and Heda was made right army supervisor under the marshal. In the third month he defeated Song forces at Meilin Pass and captured the commander Zhang Shi. He again defeated Song troops at Maling Fort and seized a hundred horses. He also captured Macheng County and took its magistrate Zhang Ti and staff officer Guo Shouji.
5
四月，夏人犯通秦寨，合達出兵安塞堡，抵隆州，夏人自城中出步騎二千逆戰，進兵擊之，斬首數十級，俘十人，遂攻隆州，陷其西南隅，會日暮乃還。 六月，行元帥府事于唐、鄧，上遣諭曰：「以卿才幹，故委卿，無使敵人侵軼，第固吾圉可也。 」四年正月，複為元帥右都監，屯延安。 十月，夏人攻綏德州，駐兵於拄天山。 合達將兵擊之，別遣先鋒提控樊澤等各率所部分三道以進，畢會於山顛。 見夏人數萬余傅山而陣，即縱兵分擊。 澤先登，摧其左軍，諸將繼攻其右，敗之。 五年五月，知延安府事，兼前職。 上言：「諸軍官以屢徙，故往往不知所居地形迂直險易，緩急之際恐至敗事，自今乞勿徙。 」又言：「河南、陝西鎮防軍皆分屯諸路，在營惟老稚而已。 乞選老成人為各路統軍以鎮撫之，且督其子弟習騎射，將來可用。 」皆從之。
In the fourth month the Western Xia attacked Tongqin Stockade. Heda marched from Ansaibao to Longzhou; the Xia sent two thousand foot and horse out of the city to meet him. He advanced, killed several dozen, and took ten prisoners, then assaulted Longzhou and breached its southwest corner before withdrawing at dusk. In the sixth month he conducted marshal headquarters affairs at Tang and Deng. The emperor sent word: "Because of your ability I entrust this post to you. Do not let the enemy encroach; it is enough if you hold our borders secure. " In the first month of the fourth year he was again made right army supervisor under the marshal and stationed at Yan'an. In the tenth month the Western Xia attacked Suide Prefecture and encamped on Zhutian Mountain. Heda led troops against them and separately sent the vanguard tiju Fan Ze and others, each leading his division along one of three routes until all met at the summit. Seeing more than ten thousand Xia troops arrayed along the mountain, he at once sent his forces to attack in separate columns. Fan Ze led the ascent and broke their left wing; the other generals then struck their right and routed them. In the fifth month of the fifth year he became prefect of Yan'an while retaining his former posts. He memorialized: "Because officers are moved so often, they often do not know the lay of the land where they serve—where the roads bend, where the passes are easy or hard. In a crisis that can mean defeat. I ask that transfers cease. " He also said: "The garrison armies of Henan and Shaanxi are scattered across the circuits, and in camp there are only the old and the young. Choose seasoned men as circuit commanders to steady them, and have their sons and younger brothers trained in horsemanship and archery so they will be ready when needed. " The court approved both proposals.
6
十一月，夏人攻安塞堡，其軍先至，合達與征行元帥納合買住禦之。 合達策之曰：「比北方兵至，先破夏人則後易為力。 」於是潛軍裹糧倍道兼進，夜襲其營，夏人果大潰，追殺四十裏，墜崖谷死者不可勝計。 上聞之，賜金各五十兩、重幣十端，且詔諭曰：「卿等克成大功，朕聞之良喜。 經畫如此，彼當知畏，期之數年，卿等可以休息矣。 」仍詔以合達之功遍諭河南帥臣。 是月，與元帥買住又戰延安，皆被重創。 十二月，以保延安功賜金帶一、玉吐鶻一，重幣十端。
In the eleventh month the Western Xia attacked Ansaibao. Their army arrived first, and Heda joined campaigning marshal Naha Maizhu in the defense. Heda reasoned: "Before the northern armies arrive, if we break the Xia first the rest will be easier. " He then moved troops in secret with supplies, marching at double speed, and raided their camp by night. The Xia broke and fled; the pursuit ran forty li, and countless men fell to their deaths in cliffs and ravines. When the emperor heard, he rewarded each with fifty taels of gold and ten bolts of heavy silk, and sent word: "You have won a great victory, and I am deeply pleased. With planning like this they should learn to fear us; in a few years you may be able to rest. " He also ordered Heda's achievement proclaimed to all the commanders in Henan. That month he fought again at Yan'an with Marshal Maizhu; both were seriously wounded. In the twelfth month, for preserving Yan'an, he was granted a gold belt, a jade tuhu bird ornament, and ten bolts of heavy silk.
7
葭州提控王公佐言於合達曰：「去歲十月，北兵既破葭州，構浮梁河上。 公佐寓州治北石山子，招集餘眾得二千餘人，欲複州城。 以士卒皆自北逃歸者，且無鎧仗，故嘗請兵于帥府，將焚其浮橋，以取葭州，帥府不聽。 又請兵援護老幼稍徙內地，而帥府亦不應。 今葭州之民迫於敵境，皆有動搖之心。 若是秋敵騎複來，則公佐力屈死于敵手，而遺民亦俱屠矣。 」合達乃上言：「臣願馳至延安，與元帥買住議，以兵護公佐軍民來屯吳堡，伺隙而動。 」詔省院議之，於是命合達率兵取葭州。 行至鄜州，千戶張子政等殺萬戶陳紋，將掠城中。 合達已勒兵為備，子政等乃出城走，合達追及之，眾複來歸，斬首惡數十人，軍乃定。
Jiazhou tiju Wang Gongzuo told Heda: "Last year in the tenth month, after the northern troops had taken Jiazhou, they built a pontoon bridge across the river. I lodged north of the prefectural seat at Shishanzi, gathered more than two thousand survivors, and hoped to recover the city. My men were all refugees who had fled south from the north, and we had no armor or weapons. I asked headquarters for troops to burn the pontoon bridge and retake Jiazhou, but they refused. I also asked for troops to escort the elderly and children inland, but headquarters would not answer that either. Now the people of Jiazhou, pressed against enemy territory, are all losing heart. If enemy horsemen come again this autumn, I will be spent and die at their hands, and the survivors will be slaughtered as well. " Heda then memorialized: "I wish to ride at once to Yan'an, consult Marshal Maizhu, and use troops to escort Gongzuo's soldiers and civilians to Wubao, where we can wait for an opportunity to strike. " The court ordered the Secretariat and Court to deliberate, and then commanded Heda to lead troops to recover Jiazhou. When he reached Fuzhou, chiliarch Zhang Zizheng and others killed myriarch Chen Wen and were about to loot the city. Heda had already mustered his troops. Zizheng and the others fled the city, but Heda overtook them, the men returned to the ranks, he executed several dozen ringleaders, and order was restored.
8
六月，合達上言：「累獲諜者，皆雲北方已約夏人，將由河中、葭州以入陝西。 防秋在近，宜預為計。 今陝西重兵兩行省分制之，然京兆抵平涼六百余裏，萬一敵梗其間，，使不得通，是自孤也。 宜令平涼行省內族白撒領軍東下，與臣協力禦敵，以屏潼、陝，敵退後復議分司為便。 」詔許之。 二年二月，以保鳳翔之功進官，賜金幣及通犀帶一。 是時，河中已破，合達提兵複取之。
In the sixth month Heda memorialized: "We have captured spy after spy, and all report that the north has already made terms with the Western Xia and will enter Shaanxi through Hezhong and Jiazhou. The autumn defense season is near, and we should prepare now. Shaanxi's main forces are now split between two branch secretariats, yet Jingzhao and Pingliang are more than six hundred li apart. If the enemy blocks the route between them, the two wings will be cut off from each other. I ask that Baisa of the imperial clan in the Pingliang branch secretariat be ordered east with his army to join me in repelling the enemy and shielding Tong Pass and Shaanxi. After the enemy withdraw we can discuss dividing command again. " The court approved. In the second month of the second year he was promoted for preserving Fengxiang and granted gold, coins, and a rhinoceros-horn belt. By then Hezhong had fallen, and Heda led troops to recover it.
9
正大二年七月，陝西旱甚，合達齋戒請雨，雨澍，是歲大稔，民立石頌德。 延安既殘毀，合達令于西路買牛付主者，招集散亡，助其耕墾，自是延安之民稍複耕稼之利。 八月，鞏州田瑞反，合達討之，諸軍進攻，合達移文諭之曰：「罪止田瑞一身，餘無所問。 」不數日，瑞弟濟殺瑞以降，合達如約撫定一州，民賴以寧。 三年，詔遷平涼行省。 四年二月，征還，拜平章政事，芮國公。 七年七月庚寅朔，以平章政事妨職樞密副使。 初，蒲阿面奏：「合達在軍中久，今日多事之際乃在於省，用違其長。 臣等欲與樞密協力軍務，擢之相位似亦未晚。 」故有此授。
In the seventh month of the second year of Zhengda (1225), Shaanxi suffered severe drought. Heda fasted and prayed for rain; rain came in abundance, the harvest that year was plentiful, and the people erected a stone to praise his virtue. Yan'an lay in ruins. Heda ordered cattle bought on the western circuit and given to householders, gathered the displaced, and helped them plow and reclaim land. Gradually the people of Yan'an returned to farming. In the eighth month Tian Rui of Gongzhou rebelled. Heda marched against him, and as the armies advanced he sent word: "Guilt rests on Tian Rui alone; no one else will be punished. " Within days Rui's younger brother Ji killed him and surrendered. Heda kept his promise and pacified the prefecture, and the people were spared further harm. In the third year an edict transferred him to the Pingliang branch secretariat. In the second month of the fourth year he was recalled, appointed Grand Councilor, and enfeoffed as Duke of Ruiguo. On the gengyin new moon of the seventh month of the seventh year he left the Grand Councilorship to become vice commissioner of the Privy Council. Earlier Pua had memorialized in audience: "Heda has spent his life in the army. In these troubled times he sits in the Secretariat—that is not where his strength lies. We wish to join the Privy Council in managing military affairs; promoting him to the council later would not be too late. " Hence this appointment.
10
十月己未朔，詔合達及樞密副使蒲阿救衛州。 初，朝廷以恆山公仙屯衛州，公府節制不一，欲合而一之。 至是，河朔諸軍圍衛，內外不通已連月，但見塔上時舉火而已。 合達等既至，先以親衛兵三千嘗之，北兵小退，翼日圍解。 上登承天門犒軍，皆授世襲謀克，賜良馬玉帶，全給月俸本色，蓋異恩也。
On the jiwei new moon of the tenth month an edict ordered Heda and Vice Commissioner of the Privy Council Pua to relieve Weizhou. Earlier the court had wanted to unify command at Weizhou, where Duke of Hengshan Gong Xian was stationed, because the prince's household and the government issued conflicting orders. By then the armies of Hebei had besieged Wei for months without contact between inside and out; only occasional signal fires on the pagoda showed that the city still held. When Heda and his force arrived, they first sent three thousand imperial guards to probe the enemy. The northern troops drew back slightly, and the siege was lifted the next day. The emperor ascended Chengtian Gate to reward the army. All were granted hereditary mouke status, given fine horses and jade belts, and paid their full monthly salaries in kind—an exceptional honor.
11
未幾，以蒲阿權參和政事，同合達行省事於閿鄉，以備潼關。 先是，陝省言備禦策，朝官集議，上策親征，中策幸陝，下策棄秦保潼關。 議者謂止可助陝西軍以決一戰，使陝西不守，河南亦不可保。 至是，自陝以西亦不守矣。
Before long Pua was made acting participant in Harmonious Government Affairs and joined Heda in conducting branch secretariat affairs at Wenxiang to guard Tong Pass. Earlier the Shaanxi secretariat had proposed defense plans, and the court debated them: the best plan was for the emperor to lead the campaign in person; the middle plan was to move the court to Shaanxi; the worst was to abandon Qin and hold only Tong Pass. The debaters argued that the court should reinforce the Shaanxi army for one decisive battle, for if Shaanxi could not be held, Henan could not be saved either. By then everything west of Shaanxi was lost as well.
12
八年正月，北帥速不泬攻破小關，殘盧氏、硃陽，散漫百餘里間。 潼關總帥納合買住率夾穀移迪烈、都尉高英拒之，求救地二省。 省以陳和尚忠孝軍一千，都尉夾穀澤軍一萬往應，北軍退，追至穀口而還。 兩省輒稱大捷，以聞。 既而北軍攻風翔，二省提兵出關二十裏，與渭北軍交，至晚複收兵入關，鳳翔遂破。 二省遂棄京兆，與牙古塔起遷居民于河南，留慶山奴守之。 九月，北兵入河中，時二相防秋還陝，量以軍馬出冷水穀以為聲援。
In the first month of the eighth year the northern commander Subutai broke through Xiaoguan and ravaged Lushi and Zhuyang over more than a hundred li. Naha Maizhu, overall commander of Tong Pass, led Jiagu Yidilie and Commandant Gao Ying to resist them and asked both branch secretariats for reinforcements. The secretariats sent Chen Heshang with one thousand Zhongxiao troops and Commandant Jiagu Ze with ten thousand men. The northern army withdrew, and they pursued to the valley mouth before turning back. Both branch secretariats promptly reported a great victory. Soon afterward the northern army attacked Fengxiang. The two branch secretariats led troops twenty li beyond the pass, fought the Weibei army, and withdrew into the pass by evening. Fengxiang then fell. The two branch secretariats then abandoned Jingzhao, and with Yaguta began relocating the population to Henan, leaving Qingshan Nu to hold the city. In the ninth month northern troops entered Hezhong. The two chancellors were returning from the autumn defense to Shaanxi and sent what troops they could through Lengshuigu as a show of support.
13
十一月，鄧州報，北兵道饒峰關，由金州而東。 於是，兩省軍入鄧，遣提控劉天山以劄付下襄陽制置司，約同禦北兵，且索軍食。 兩省以前月癸卯行，留楊沃衍軍守閿鄉。 沃衍尋被旨取洛南路入商州，屯豐陽川備上津，與恆山公仙相掎角。 合達複留禦侮中郎將完顏陳和尚于閿鄉南十五裏，乃行。 陳和尚亦隨而往。 沃衍軍八千及商州之木瓜平，一日夜馳三百里入桃花堡，知北兵由豐陽而東，亦東還，會大軍于鎮平。 恆山公仙萬人元駐胡陵關，至是亦由荊子口、順陽來會。 十二月朔，俱至鄧下，屯順陽。 乃遣天山入宋。
In the eleventh month Dengzhou reported that northern troops were passing Raofeng Pass and marching east from Jinzhou. The armies of both branch secretariats then entered Deng. They sent tiju Liu Tianshan with a letter to the Xiangyang Pacification Commission proposing a joint defense against the northern army and requesting provisions. The two branch secretariats had marched on guimao of the previous month, leaving Yang Woyan's army to hold Wenxiang. Woyan soon received orders to take the Luonan route into Shangzhou, encamp at Fengyangchuan to guard Shangjin, and coordinate with Duke of Hengshan Gong Xian. Heda left Central Commander for Repelling the Enemy Wanyan Chen Heshang fifteen li south of Wenxiang and then marched on. Chen Heshang followed as well. Woyan's eight thousand men and the Mugua Plain detachment from Shangzhou rode three hundred li in a day and night to Taohuabao. Learning that the northern army was moving east from Fengyang, they turned east as well and joined the main force at Zhenping. Duke of Hengshan Gong Xian's ten thousand men had been stationed at Huling Pass; they now came to join via Jingzikou and Shunyang. On the new moon of the twelfth month all reached the outskirts of Deng and encamped at Shunyang. They then sent Liu Tianshan into Song territory.
14
初，宋人于國朝君之、伯之、叔之，納歲幣將百年。 南渡以後，宋以我為不足慮，絕不往來。 故宣宗南伐，士馬折耗十不一存，雖攻陷淮上數州，徒使驕將悍卒恣其殺虜、飽其私欲而已。 又宣徽使奧敦阿虎使北方，北中大臣有以輿地圖指示之曰：「商州至此中軍馬幾何？ 」又指興元雲：「我不從商州，則取興元路入汝界矣。 」阿虎還奏，宣宗甚憂之。 哀宗即位，群臣建言，可因國喪遣使報哀，副以遺留物，因與之講解，盡撤邊備，共守武休之險。 遂下省院議之，而當國者有仰而不能俯之疾，皆以朝廷先遣人則於國體有虧為辭。 元年，上諭南鄙諸帥，遣人往滁州與宋通好。 宋人每以奏稟為辭，和事遂不講。 然十年之間，朝廷屢敕邊將不妄侵掠，彼我稍得休息，宋人始信之，遂有繼好之意。 及天山以劄付至宋，劄付者指揮之別名，宋制使陳該怒辱天山，且以惡語複之。 報至，識者皆為竊歎。
For nearly a century the Song had addressed our dynasty as lord, elder brother, and uncle, and paid annual tribute. After the Song crossed south, they ceased to regard us as a threat and broke off all contact. When Emperor Xuanzong campaigned south, our armies were shattered so that fewer than one in ten returned. Though we took several prefectures on the Huai, the campaign only let arrogant generals and brutal soldiers kill and plunder as they pleased. When Palace Secretariat Commissioner Odun Ahu was envoy to the north, a northern minister pointed to a map and asked: "From Shangzhou to here, how many troops do you have in between? " He then pointed to Xingyuan and said: "If I do not come by Shangzhou, I will take the Xingyuan route into your territory. " When Ahu returned and reported, Emperor Xuanzong was deeply alarmed. When Emperor Aizong came to the throne, his ministers proposed that Jin take advantage of the Song mourning period to send envoys of condolence with gifts left behind, negotiate peace, withdraw border garrisons, and hold the Wuxiu pass together. The matter was referred to the Secretariat and Court, but those in power could not bring themselves to bow; all refused on the grounds that if the court sent envoys first it would damage national dignity. In the first year the emperor instructed the southern frontier commanders to send envoys to Chuzhou to open friendly relations with Song. The Song each time pleaded the need to report to their court, and peace talks went nowhere. Over the next ten years the court repeatedly ordered border generals not to raid wantonly. Both sides gained a measure of peace, the Song began to trust Jin, and the idea of renewed friendship took hold. When Liu Tianshan reached Song with a zhafu—a dispatch bearing orders—the Song commissioner Chen Gai insulted him in anger and answered with abusive words. When word of this returned, thoughtful men sighed in private.
15
戊辰，北兵渡漢江而北，諸將以為可乘其半渡擊之，蒲阿不從。 丙子，兵畢渡，戰于禹山之前，北兵小卻，營於三十裏之外。 二相以大捷驛報，百官表賀，諸相置酒省中，左丞李蹊且喜且泣曰：「非今日之捷，生靈之禍，可勝言哉！ 」蓋以為實然也。 先是，河南聞北兵出饒峰，百姓往往入城壁、保險固，及聞敵已退，至有晏然不動者，不二三日遊騎至，人無所逃，悉為捷書所誤。
On wuchen the northern army crossed the Han River heading north. The generals urged striking while they were half across, but Pua refused. On bingzi the army finished crossing and fought before Mount Yu. The northern troops drew back slightly and encamped thirty li away. The two chancellors reported a great victory by courier. Officials submitted congratulations, and the chancellors feasted in the Secretariat. Left Chancellor Li Xi wept with joy and said: "Without today's victory, the calamity that would have befallen the people is beyond words! " They believed it was real. Earlier, when Henan heard that northern troops had come through Raofeng Pass, people fled into walled towns and strongholds. When word came that the enemy had withdrawn, some stayed put in false security. Within two or three days roaming cavalry arrived and there was nowhere to flee—all had been deceived by reports of victory.
16
九年正月丁酉，兩省軍潰于陽翟之三峰山。 初，禹山之戰，兩軍相拒，北軍散漫而北，金軍懼其乘虛襲京城，乃謀入援。 時北兵遣三千騎趨河上，已二十餘日，泌陽、南陽、方城、襄、郟至京諸縣皆破，所有積聚焚毀無餘。 金軍由鄧而東，無所仰給，乃並山入陽翟。 既行，北兵即襲之，且行且戰，北兵傷折亦多。 恆山一軍為突騎三千所沖，軍殊死鬥，北騎退走。 追奔之際，忽大霧四塞，兩省命收軍。 少之，霧散乃前，前一大澗，長闊數裏，非此霧則北兵人馬滿中矣。 明日，至三峰山，遂潰，事載蒲阿傳。 合達知大事已去，欲下馬戰，而蒲阿已失所在。 合達以數百騎走鈞州，北兵塹其城外攻之，走門不得出，匿窟室中，城破，北兵發而殺之。 時朝廷不知其死，或雲已走京兆，賜以手詔，募人訪之。 及攻汴，乃揚言曰：「汝家所恃，惟黃河與合達耳。 今合達為我殺，黃河為我有，不降何待？」
On dingyou of the first month of the ninth year the armies of both branch secretariats were routed at Sanfeng Mountain in Yangzhai. After the battle at Mount Yu the two armies faced each other while the northern army drifted north. The Jin feared a thrust at the capital and planned to march home to reinforce it. Northern troops had sent three thousand cavalry toward the Yellow River for more than twenty days. Biyang, Nanyang, Fangcheng, Xiang, Qiao, and every county on the road to the capital had fallen, and all stored supplies were burned. The Jin army marched east from Deng without supplies and entered Yangzhai along the mountain roads. As soon as they marched, the northern army attacked. They fought as they marched, and the northerners also took heavy losses. The Hengshan army was charged by three thousand shock cavalry but fought to the death and drove the northern horsemen back. During the pursuit a thick fog suddenly closed in on every side, and both branch secretariats ordered a halt. When the fog lifted they advanced and found a great ravine several li long and wide ahead. Without the fog, the northern army would have filled it. The next day they reached Sanfeng Mountain and were routed; the full account is in Pua's biography. Heda knew all was lost and meant to dismount and fight to the end, but Pua had already vanished. Heda fled to Junzhou with a few hundred horsemen. The northern army besieged the city, and he could not escape through the gates. He hid in a cave-room; when the city fell they dug him out and killed him. The court did not yet know he was dead; some said he had fled to Jingzhao. The emperor issued a handwritten edict offering a reward for word of him. When they attacked Bian they proclaimed: "Your house relies on only two things—the Yellow River and Heda. Heda is dead at our hands and the Yellow River is ours. Why do you not surrender—what are you waiting for?"
17
合達熟知敵情，習於行陣，且重義輕財，與下同甘苦，有俘獲即分給，遇敵則身先之而不避，眾亦樂為之用，其為人亦可知矣。 左丞張行信嘗薦之曰：「完顏合達，今之良將也。」
Heda knew the enemy well and was skilled in battle. He valued honor over wealth, shared hardship with his men, and divided spoils at once. In battle he led from the front without flinching, and his soldiers were glad to follow him. Such was the man. Left Chancellor Zhang Xingxin once recommended him, saying, "Wanyan Heda is the finest general of our day."
18
移剌蒲阿
Yila Pua
19
正大四年十二月，河朔軍突入商州，殘硃陽、盧氏。 蒲阿逆戰至靈寶東，遇游騎十餘，獲一人，餘即退，蒲阿輒以捷聞。 賞世襲謀克，仍厚賜之。 人共知其罔上，而無敢言，吏部郎中楊居仁以微言取怒。
In the twelfth month of the fourth year of Zhengda armies from Hebei suddenly entered Shangzhou and ravaged Zhuyang and Lushi. Pua marched to meet them east of Lingbao, encountered a dozen roaming horsemen, captured one man, and when the rest withdrew he promptly reported a victory. He was granted hereditary mouke status and richly rewarded. Everyone knew he was deceiving the throne, but none dared speak. Yang Juren of the Ministry of Personnel earned his wrath with a mild remark.
20
六年二月丙辰，以蒲阿權樞密副使。 自去年夏，北軍之在陝西者駸駸至涇州，且阻慶陽糧道。 蒲阿奏：「陝西設兩行省，本以籓衛河南，今北軍之來三年於茲，行省統軍馬二三十萬，未嘗對壘，亦未嘗得一折箭，何用行省。 」院官亦俱奏將來須用密院軍馬勾當，上不語者久之。 是後，以丞相賽不行尚書省事於關中，召平章政事合達還朝，白撒亦召至闕，蒲阿率完顏陳和尚忠孝軍一千駐邠州，且令觀北勢。 八月丙申，蒲阿再複潞州。 十月乙未朔，蒲阿東還。
On bingchen of the second month of the sixth year Pua was made acting vice commissioner of the Privy Council. Since the previous summer the northern forces in Shaanxi had pressed on to Jingzhou and cut the grain route to Qingyang. Pua memorialized: "Shaanxi was given two branch secretariats to shield Henan. The northern army has been here three years. Those secretariats command two or three hundred thousand men, yet have never faced the enemy in battle or broken a single arrow. What use are they? " The Privy Council officials also memorialized that imperial troops would have to be used. The emperor was silent for a long while. After this Chancellor Saibu was sent to run the Ministry of Revenue in Guanzhong, Grand Councilor Heda was recalled, Baisa was summoned to court, and Pua led Wanyan Chen Heshang with one thousand Zhongxiao troops to Binzhou to watch the northern armies. On bingshen of the eighth month Pua recovered Luzhou again. On the yiwei new moon of the tenth month Pua returned east.
21
十二月乙未，詔蒲阿與總帥牙吾塔、權簽樞密院事訛可救慶陽。 七年正月，戰北兵于大昌原，北軍還，慶陽圍解。 詔以訛可屯邠州，蒲阿、牙吾塔還京兆。 未幾，以權參知政事與合達行省於閿鄉。 八年正月，北軍入陝西，鳳翔破，兩行省棄京兆而東，至洛陽驛，被召議河中事，語在白華傳。
On yiwei of the twelfth month an edict ordered Pua, overall commander Yawuta, and acting Privy Council signatory Eke to relieve Qingyang. In the first month of the seventh year they fought the northern army at Dachangyuan. The northerners withdrew and the siege of Qingyang was lifted. An edict ordered Eke to hold Binzhou while Pua and Yawuta returned to Jingzhao. Before long he was made acting participant in government affairs and joined Heda in conducting branch secretariat affairs at Wenxiang. In the first month of the eighth year the northern army entered Shaanxi, Fengxiang fell, and both branch secretariats abandoned Jingzhao and marched east. At Luoyang post station they were summoned to discuss Hezhong; that account is in Bai Hua's biography.
22
十二月，北兵濟自漢江，兩省軍入鄧州，議敵所從出，謂由光化截江戰為便，放之渡而戰為便、張惠以「截江為便，縱之渡，我腹空虛，能不為所潰乎？ 」蒲阿麾之曰：「汝但知南事，于北事何知。 我向於裕州得制旨雲，'使彼在沙磧，且當往求之'，況今自來乎。 汝等更勿似大昌原、舊衛州、扇車回縱出之。 」定住、高、樊皆謂蒲阿此言為然。 合達乃問按得木，木以為不然。 軍中以木北人，知其軍情，此言為有理，然不能奪蒲阿之議。
In the twelfth month the northern army crossed the Han. The two branch secretariats entered Deng and debated strategy. Some favored intercepting them at Guanghua on the river; others favored letting them cross and then fighting. Zhang Hui said, "If we let them cross, our rear is empty. How can we avoid being shattered? " Pua waved him aside and said, "You know only southern warfare. What do you know of the north? At Yuzhou I once received an imperial order: 'Even if they are in the desert, go seek them out.' How much more when they come to us of their own accord. Do not repeat what happened at Dachangyuan, old Weizhou, and Shanche Hui, when we let them escape. " Dingzhu, Gao, and Fan all agreed with Pua. Heda then asked Andemu, who disagreed. The army regarded Andemu as a northerner who knew the enemy's ways, and his argument made sense, but they could not overturn Pua's decision.
23
順陽留二十日，光化探騎至，雲「千騎已北渡」，兩省是夜進軍，比曉至禹山，探者續雲「北騎已盡濟」。 癸酉，北軍將近，兩省立軍高山，各分據地勢，步迎於山前，騎屯於山后。 甲戌，日未出，北兵至，大帥以兩小旗前導來觀，觀竟不前，散如雁翅，轉山麓出騎兵之後，分三隊而進，輜重外余二萬人。 合達令諸軍，「觀今日事勢，不當戰，且待之。 」俄而北騎突前，金兵不得不戰，至以短兵相接，戰三交，北騎少退。 北兵之在西者望蒲阿親繞甲騎後而突之，至於三，為蒲察定住力拒而退。 大帥以旗聚諸將，議良久。 合達知北兵意向。 時高英軍方北顧，而北兵出其背擁之，英軍動，合達幾斬英，英複督軍力戰。 北兵稍卻觀變，英軍定，複擁樊澤軍，合達斬一千夫長，軍殊死鬥，乃卻之。
They stayed at Shunyang twenty days until scouts from Guanghua reported that a thousand horsemen had crossed north. Both secretariats marched that night and reached Mount Yu by dawn, where scouts reported that the northern cavalry had all crossed. On guiyou the northern army approached. Both secretariats drew up on a high mountain, infantry before the slope and cavalry behind. On jiaxu, before sunrise, the northern army arrived. The commander came forward with two small flags to reconnoiter, then withdrew. His men spread like geese' wings, swung around the foothills behind the Jin cavalry, and advanced in three columns—twenty thousand men besides the baggage train. Heda ordered the armies: "Given today's situation we should not fight yet. Wait. " Soon the northern cavalry charged. The Jin had to fight at close quarters. After three clashes the northern horsemen drew back slightly. Northern troops on the west saw Pua personally lead armored cavalry around to charge them three times; each time Pucha Dingzhu drove them back. The commander gathered his generals by signal flag and deliberated for a long while. Heda understood what the northern army intended. Gao Ying's force was watching north when the northern army struck from behind. His line wavered and Heda nearly had him executed, but Ying rallied his men and fought on. The northerners drew back to watch. Ying's force steadied, then the enemy pressed Fan Ze. Heda beheaded a chiliarch, and his men fought to the death and drove them back.
24
北兵回陣，南向來路。 兩省復議：「彼雖號三萬，而輜重三之一焉。 又相持二三日不得食，乘其卻退當擁之。 」張惠主此議，蒲阿言：「江路已絕，黃河不冰，彼入重地，將安歸乎？ 何以速為。 」不從。 乙亥，北兵忽不知所在，營火寂無一耗。 兩省及諸將議，四日不見軍，又不見營，鄧州津送及路人不絕，而亦無見者，豈南渡而歸乎？ 己卯，邏騎乃知北軍在光化對岸棗林中，晝作食，夜不下馬，望林中往來，不五六十步而不聞音響，其有謀可知矣。
The northern army re-formed and faced south along their line of march. Both secretariats debated again: "Though they claim thirty thousand men, a third are baggage. They have gone two or three days without food. We should press them as they withdraw. " Zhang Hui favored this plan, but Pua said, "The river route is cut and the Yellow River is not frozen. They are deep in our territory—where can they go? Why hurry? " They did not follow his advice. On yihai the northern army vanished without a trace; not a single campfire remained. The two secretariats and their generals debated: for four days they saw neither army nor camp. Travelers on the Deng road reported constantly, yet none had seen them. Had they crossed south and gone home? On jimao patrols found the northern army in the jujube woods across from Guanghua. They cooked by day and did not dismount by night. Men moved in the woods without a sound within fifty or sixty steps—clearly they were up to something.
25
初，禹山戰罷，有二騎迷入營，問之，知北兵凡七頭項，大將統之。 複有詐降者十人，弊衣羸馬泣訴艱苦，兩省信之，易以肥馬，飲之酒，及暖衣食而置之陣後，十人者皆鞭馬而去，始悟其為覘騎也。
After the Mount Yu battle two lost horsemen wandered into camp. From them they learned that the northern army had seven commanders under a great general. Ten men came feigning surrender in rags on thin horses, weeping of hardship. The two secretariats believed them, gave them fresh horses and wine, warmed their clothes, and placed them behind the lines. All ten whipped their horses away—they had been spies.
26
庚辰，兩省議入鄧就糧，辰巳間到林後，北兵忽來突，兩省軍迎擊，交綏之際，北兵以百騎邀輜重而去，金兵幾不成列，逮夜乃入城，懼軍士迷路，鳴鐘招之。 樊澤屯城西，高英屯城東。 九年正月壬午朔，耀兵于鄧城下。 北兵不與戰，大將使來索酒，兩省與之二十瓶。 癸未，大軍發鄧州，趨京師，騎二萬，步十三萬，騎帥蒲察定住，蒲察答吉卜，郎將按忒木，忠孝軍總領夾穀愛答、內族達魯歡，總領夾穀移特剌，提控步軍臨淄郡王張惠，殄寇都尉完顏阿排、高英、樊澤，中軍陳和尚，與恆山公武仙、楊沃衍軍合。 是日，次五朵山下，取鴉路，北兵以三千騎尾之，遂駐營待楊武。
On gengchen both secretariats decided to enter Deng for supplies. Between chen and si they reached the woods when the northern army suddenly attacked. In the clash a hundred northern horsemen seized the baggage train and fled. The Jin lines nearly broke. They entered the city only at night and rang bells lest soldiers lose their way. Fan Ze encamped west of the city and Gao Ying east of it. On the renwu new moon of the first month of the ninth year they paraded their army below Deng. The northern army would not fight. The commander sent for wine, and the two secretariats gave him twenty bottles. On guiwei the main army left Deng for the capital: twenty thousand horse and one hundred thirty thousand foot. The cavalry commanders were Pucha Dingzhu, Pucha Dajibu, and langjiang Andemu; the Zhongxiao commanders Jiagu Aida and imperial clansman Daluhuan; Jiagu Yitelie commanded infantry; tiju and Linzi Prince Zhang Hui led the foot; commandants Wanyan Apai, Gao Ying, and Fan Ze led the extermination bands; Chen Heshang held the center; they joined Duke of Hengshan Wu Xian and Yang Woyan. That day they halted below Wuduo Mountain and took the Crow Road. Three thousand northern horsemen followed, so they camped to wait for Yang Wu.
27
楊武至，知申、裕兩州已降。 七日至夜，議北騎明日當複襲我，彼止騎三千，而我示以弱，將為所輕，當與之戰。 乃伏騎五十于鄧州道。 明日軍行，北騎襲之如故，金以萬人擁之而東，伏發，北兵南避。 是日雨，宿竹林中。 庚寅，頓安皋。 辛卯，宿鴉路、魯山。 河西軍已獻申、裕，擁老幼牛羊取鴉路，金軍適值之，奪其牛羊餉軍。
When Yang Wu arrived they learned that Shen and Yu prefectures had already surrendered. On the seventh day at night they debated that the northern horsemen would raid again tomorrow. The enemy had only three thousand horse, and if Jin showed weakness they would be despised—they should fight. They hid fifty horsemen on the Deng road. The next day as the army marched the northern horsemen attacked as before. Jin sent ten thousand men to press them east; the ambush struck and the northerners fled south. It rained that day and they camped in bamboo groves. On gengyin they halted at Angao. On xinmao they camped at Crow Road and Lushan. The Hexi army had surrendered Shen and Yu and were driving families and livestock along the Crow Road. The Jin army met them and seized the livestock for rations.
28
癸巳，望鈞州，至沙河，北騎五千待于河北，金軍奪橋以過，北軍即西首斂避。 金軍縱擊，北軍不戰，複南渡沙河。 金軍欲盤營，北軍複渡河來襲。 金軍不能得食，又不得休息。 合昏，雨作，明旦變雪。 北兵增及萬人，且行且戰，致黃榆店，望鈞州二十五裏，雨雪不能進，盤營三日。 丙申，一近侍入軍中傳旨，集諸帥聽處分，制旨雲：「兩省軍悉赴京師，我禦門犒軍，換易禦馬，然後出戰未晚。 」複有密旨雲：「近知張家灣透漏二三百騎，已遷衛、孟兩州，兩省當常切防備。 」領旨訖，蒲阿拂袖而起，合達欲再議，蒲阿言：「止此而已，複何所議。 」蓋已奪魄矣。 軍即行。
On guisi, in sight of Junzhou, they reached the Shahe River. Five thousand northern horsemen waited on the north bank. The Jin seized the bridge and crossed; the northerners withdrew west. The Jin pursued, but the northern army would not fight and crossed the Shahe again to the south. When the Jin tried to make camp, the northern army crossed again to attack. The Jin could neither eat nor rest. At dusk it rained; by morning it had turned to snow. The northern force grew to ten thousand and fought as it marched to Huangyudian, twenty-five li from Junzhou. Rain and snow stopped them, and they camped three days. On bingshen a palace attendant brought word summoning the commanders. The order read: "Both armies are to march to the capital. I will reward them at the Imperial Gate, exchange horses, and then battle will not be too late. " A secret order added: "Two or three hundred horsemen have broken through at Zhangjiawan. Wei and Meng prefectures have been evacuated. Both secretariats must stay on guard. " When the orders were received, Pua flung up his sleeves and rose. Heda wanted to debate further, but Pua said, "That is all. What more is there to discuss? " He had already lost his nerve. The army marched at once.
29
北軍自北渡者畢集，前後以大樹塞其軍路，沃衍軍奪路，得之。 合達又議陳和尚先擁山上大勢，比再整頓，金軍已接竹林，去鈞州止十餘裏矣。 金軍遂進，北軍果卻三峰之東北、西南。 武、高前鋒擁其西南，楊、樊擁其東北，北兵俱卻，止有三峰之東。 張惠、按得林立山上望北兵二三十萬，約厚二十裏。 按得木與張惠謀曰：「此地不戰，欲何為耶？ 」乃率騎兵萬餘乘上而下擁之，北兵卻。 須臾雪大作，白霧蔽空，人不相覿。 時雪已三日，戰地多麻田，往往耕四五過，人馬所踐泥淖沒脛。 軍士被甲骨僵立雪中，槍槊結凍如椽，軍士有不食至三日者。 北兵與河北軍合，四週邊之，熾薪燔牛羊肉，更遞休息。 乘金困憊，乃開鈞州路縱之走，而以生軍夾擊之。 金軍遂潰，聲如崩山，忽天氣開霽，日光皎然，金軍無一人得逃者。
All the northern troops who had crossed from the north were now gathered. They blocked the road ahead and behind with felled trees, but Woyan's army forced a passage through. Heda again urged that Chen Heshang seize the high ground first, but by the time they regrouped the Jin army had already reached the bamboo groves, barely ten li from Junzhou. The Jin army advanced, and the northern troops withdrew from the northeast and southwest of Sanfeng. Wu and Gao pressed them on the southwest, Yang and Fan on the northeast, and the northern army fell back, leaving only the east side of Sanfeng open. Zhang Hui and Andemu stood on the mountain and saw two or three hundred thousand northern troops spread across some twenty li. Andemu said to Zhang Hui, "If we do not fight here, what are we waiting for? " He led more than ten thousand horsemen charging downhill against them, and the northern army drew back. Soon snow fell in torrents, white mist filled the sky, and men could not see one another. Snow had already fallen three days. The battlefield was covered with hemp fields plowed four or five times over, and mud sucked men and horses to their shins. Soldiers stood rigid in armor in the snow. Spears and halberds froze thick as rafters. Some had not eaten for three days. The northern army joined forces from Hebei, surrounded them on every side, roasted beef and mutton over great fires, and rested in relays. When the Jin were spent, they opened the road to Junzhou and let them flee, then struck with fresh troops from both flanks. The Jin army broke with a sound like collapsing mountains. Suddenly the sky cleared and sunlight shone bright. Not one Jin soldier escaped.
30
武仙率三十騎入竹林中，楊、樊、張三軍爭路，北兵圍之數重，及高英殘兵共戰于柿林村南，沃衍、澤、英皆死，惟張惠步持大槍奮戰而歿。 蒲阿走京師，未至，追及，擒之。 七月，械至官山，召問降否，往復數百言，但曰：「我金國大臣，惟當金國境內死耳。 」遂見殺。
Wu Xian led thirty horsemen into the bamboo groves. Yang, Fan, and Zhang's forces fought over the road as the northern army surrounded them again and again. With Gao Ying's survivors they fought south of Shilin Village. Woyan, Fan Ze, and Gao Ying all died. Only Zhang Hui on foot, wielding a great spear, fought to the end. Pua fled toward the capital but was overtaken and captured before he arrived. In the seventh month he was brought in fetters to Guanshan and asked to surrender. After hundreds of words back and forth he said only, "I am a minister of Jin. I can die only within Jin territory. " He was then executed.
31
贊曰：金自南渡，用兵克捷之功，史不絕書，然而地不加辟，殺傷相當，君子疑之。 異時伐宋，唐州之役，喪師七百，主將訛論匿之，而以捷聞。 禦史納蘭糾之，宣宗獎禦史，而不罪訛論，是君臣相率而為虛聲也。 禹山之捷，兩省為欺，遂致誤國，豈非宣宗前事有以啟之耶？ 至於三峰山之敗，不可收拾，上下暿咢眙，而金事已去十九。 天朝取道襄、漢，懸軍深入，機權若神，又獲天助，用能犯兵家之所忌，以建萬世之俊功，合達雖良將，何足以當之。 蒲阿無謀，獨以一死無愧，猶足取焉爾。
The commentator writes: Since the Jin crossed south, their histories record one military victory after another, yet they gained no territory and their casualties matched the enemy's—thoughtful men found this suspect. When they campaigned against Song, at Tangzhou they lost seven hundred men, but the commander Elun concealed it and reported a victory. Censor Nalan impeached him. Emperor Xuanzong rewarded the censor but did not punish Elun—ruler and ministers were alike in cultivating empty glory. The victory at Mount Yu was a deception by both secretariats that misled the state. Did not Emperor Xuanzong's earlier example open the way? The defeat at Sanfeng Mountain could not be remedied. Court and army were stunned, and the Jin cause was nine-tenths lost. The Celestial Dynasty marched through Xiang and Han, drove deep with an army in peril, maneuvered like a god, and enjoyed heaven's aid. Thus it could do what strategists forbid and win glory for ages. Heda was a fine general, but how could he stand against that? Pua had no stratagem, but in dying without shame he is still worthy of respect.
