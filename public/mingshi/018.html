1
【世宗二】
Annals: Shizong II.
2
二十二年春正月丙午朔，日有食之。 三月庚戌，復遣使采木湖廣。 是春，俺答屢入塞。 秋八月，犯延綏，總兵官吳瑛等擊敗之。 冬十月，朵顏入寇，殺守備陳舜。 十二月乙酉，免南畿被災稅糧。 是年，占城、土魯番、撒馬兒罕、天方、烏斯藏入貢。
In spring of Jiajing twenty-two, on the bingwu new moon of the first month, there was a solar eclipse. In the third month, on gengxu, the court again sent envoys to gather timber in Huguang. That spring Anda raided across the frontier again and again. In the eighth month of autumn, Anda invaded Yan-sui and was defeated by Regional Commander Wu Ying and others. In the tenth month of winter, Duoyan invaded and killed Garrison Commander Chen Shun. In the twelfth month, on yiyou, tax grain was forgiven in the disaster-hit Southern Metropolitan Region. That year Champa, Turpan, Samarkand, Tianfang, and U-Tsang sent tribute missions.
3
二十三年春正月丙寅，俺答犯黃崖口。 二月戊寅，犯大水谷。 三月癸丑，犯龍門所。 丁巳，賜秦鳴雷等進士及第、出身有差。 秋七月，俺答犯大同，總兵官周尚文戰於黑山，敗之。 八月甲午，翟鑾罷。 九月癸卯，免浙江被災稅糧。 丁未，吏部尚書許贊兼文淵閣大學士，禮部尚書張璧兼東閣大學士，預機務。 壬子，振湖廣災。 冬十月戊辰，免河南被災稅糧。 甲戌，小王子入萬全右衛。 戊寅，掠蔚州，至於完縣。 京師戒嚴。 乙酉，逮總督宣大兵部尚書翟鵬、巡撫薊鎮僉都御史硃方下獄，鵬謫戍，方杖死。 十一月庚子，京師解嚴。 加方士陶仲文少師。 十二月丙子，振江西災。 是年，安南入貢，日本以無表卻之。
In spring of Jiajing twenty-three, on bingyin of the first month, Anda invaded Huangyakou. In the second month, on wuyin, he invaded Dashuigu. In the third month, on guichou, he invaded Longmen Garrison. On dingsi the palace examination was held; Qin Minglei and others received jinshi ranks, palace degrees, and provincial degrees according to merit. In the seventh month of autumn, Anda invaded Datong; Regional Commander Zhou Shangwen fought him at Heishan and won. In the eighth month, on jiawu, Zhai Luan was removed from office. In the ninth month, on guimao, tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken Zhejiang. On dingwei Xu Zan, minister of personnel, was also made grand secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion, and Zhang Bi, minister of rites, was also made grand secretary of the Eastern Pavilion; both entered state deliberations. On renzi the court sent disaster relief to Huguang. In the tenth month of winter, on wuchen, tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken Henan. On jiaxu the Little Prince entered Wanquan Right Guard. On wuyin he plundered Weizhou and pushed as far as Wanxian. Beijing was placed under martial law. On yiyou Grand Coordinator of Xuanda and Minister of War Zhai Peng and Grand Coordinator of Jizhen Zhu Fang were thrown into prison; Peng was banished to frontier service and Fang was beaten to death. In the eleventh month, on gengzi, martial law in the capital was lifted. Tao Zhongwen, a court Daoist adept, was given the rank of junior preceptor. In the twelfth month, on bingzi, the court sent disaster relief to Jiangxi. That year Annam sent tribute; Japan was turned away for presenting no formal memorial of submission.
4
二十四年春二月戊申，詔流民復業，予牛種，開墾閒田者給復十年。 三月壬午，逮總督宣大兵部侍郎張漢下獄，謫戍。 夏五月壬戌朔，日有食之。 六月壬辰，太廟成。 是夏，免畿輔、山西、陝西被災稅糧。 秋七秋壬戌，有事於太廟，赦徒罪以下。 八月丙午，瘞暴骸。 己酉，張璧卒。 庚戌，俺答犯松子嶺，殺守備張文瀚。 是月，犯大同，參將張鳳、指揮劉欽等戰死。 九月丁丑，召夏言入閣。 冬十一月辛巳，許贊罷。 是年，安南、琉球、烏斯藏入貢。
In spring of Jiajing twenty-four, on wushen of the second month, an edict urged displaced peasants back to their fields, supplied them with oxen and seed, and granted ten years' tax remission to those who reclaimed wasteland. In the third month, on renwu, Grand Coordinator of Xuanda Zhang Han, vice minister of war, was arrested, imprisoned, and banished to frontier service. In the fifth month of summer, on the renxu new moon, there was a solar eclipse. In the sixth month, on renchen, the Imperial Ancestral Temple was finished. That summer tax grain was forgiven in the disaster-hit metropolitan suburbs, Shanxi, and Shaanxi. In the seventh month of autumn, on renxu, the court sacrificed at the Imperial Ancestral Temple and proclaimed a general amnesty down to convict offenses. In the eighth month, on bingwu, the court ordered the burial of exposed corpses. On jiyou Zhang Bi died. On gengxu Anda invaded Songziling and killed Garrison Commander Zhang Wenhan. That month he invaded Datong; Assistant Regional Commander Zhang Feng, Commander Liu Qin, and others were killed in battle. In the ninth month, on dingchou, Xia Yan was recalled to the Grand Secretariat. In the eleventh month of winter, on xinsi, Xu Zan was removed from office. That year Annam, Ryukyu, and U-Tsang sent tribute missions.
5
二十五年春三月戊辰，四川白草番亂。 夏五月戊辰，俺答款大同塞，邊將殺其使。 六月甲辰，犯宣府，千戶汪洪戰死。 秋七月癸酉，以醴泉出承華殿，廷臣表賀，停諸司封事二十日。 嗣後，慶賀齋祀悉停封奏。 是月，俺答犯延安、慶陽。 八月壬子，免山東被災稅糧。 九月，俺答犯寧夏。 冬十月丁亥，犯清平堡，遊擊高極戰死。 癸巳，代府奉國將軍充灼謀反，伏誅。 甲午，殺故建昌侯張延齡。 十二月丁未，免河南被災稅糧。 是年，土魯番入貢。
In spring of Jiajing twenty-five, on wuchen of the third month, the Baicao tribes rose in rebellion in Sichuan. In the fifth month of summer, on wuchen, Anda sued for peace at the Datong frontier and frontier generals killed his envoys. In the sixth month, on jiachen, he invaded Xuanfu and Battalion Commander Wang Hong was killed in battle. In the seventh month of autumn, on guiyou, a sweet spring appeared at Chenghua Hall; court officials submitted congratulatory memorials, and sealed memorials from all offices were suspended for twenty days. After that, congratulatory and fasting-sacrifice memorials were suspended altogether. That month Anda invaded Yan'an and Qingyang. In the eighth month, on renzi, tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken Shandong. In the ninth month Anda invaded Ningxia. In the tenth month of winter, on dinghai, he invaded Qingping Fort and Mobile Corps Commander Gao Ji was killed in battle. On guisi Fengguo General Chong Zhuo of the Dai princely establishment plotted rebellion and was put to death. On jiawu the former Marquis of Jianchang Zhang Yanling was executed. In the twelfth month, on dingwei, tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken Henan. That year Turpan sent a tribute mission.
6
二十六年春三月庚午，賜李春芳等進士及第、出身有差。 夏四月乙巳，巡撫四川都御史張時徹、副總兵何卿討平白草叛番。 己酉，俺答求貢，拒之。 秋七月丙辰，河決曹縣。 八月丙戌，免陝西被災稅糧。 九月戊辰，戶部尚書王杲以科臣劾其通賄下獄，遣戍。 閏月丙午，振成都饑。 冬十一月壬午，大內火，釋楊爵於獄。 乙未，皇后崩。 十二月辛酉，逮甘肅總兵官仇鸞。 乙亥，海寇犯寧波、台州。 是年，琉球入貢。
In spring of Jiajing twenty-six, on gengwu of the third month, Li Chunfang and others received jinshi ranks, palace degrees, and provincial degrees according to merit. In the fourth month of summer, on yisi, Grand Coordinator of Sichuan Zhang Shiche and Vice Regional Commander He Qing put down the Baicao rebels. On jiyou Anda asked to send tribute; the court refused. In the seventh month of autumn, on bingchen, the Yellow River broke its banks at Caoxian. In the eighth month, on bingxu, tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken Shaanxi. In the ninth month, on wuchen, Minister of Revenue Wang Gao was impeached by censorial officials for bribery, cast into prison, and banished to frontier service. In the intercalary month, on bingwu, the court sent famine relief to Chengdu. In the eleventh month of winter, on renwu, fire broke out in the Inner Palace and Yang Jue was released from prison. On yiwei the empress died. In the twelfth month, on xinyou, Regional Commander of Gansu Qiu Luan was arrested. On yihai sea bandits raided Ningbo and Taizhou. That year Ryukyu sent a tribute mission.
7
二十七年春正月，把都兒寇廣甯，參將閻振戰死。 癸未，以議復河套，逮總督陝西三邊侍郎曾銑，杖給事中御史於廷。 罷夏言。 三月癸巳，殺曾銑，逮夏言。 癸卯，出仇鸞於獄。 夏五月丙戌，葬孝烈皇后。 秋七月戊寅，京師地震。 庚子，西苑進嘉谷，薦於太廟。 八月丁巳，俺答犯大同，指揮顧相等戰死，周尚文追敗之於次野口。 九月壬午，犯宣府，深入永寧、懷來、隆慶，守備魯承恩等戰死。 乙未，免陝西被災稅糧。 冬十月癸卯，殺夏言。 十一月乙未，詔撫按官采生沙金。 是年，日本入貢。
In spring of Jiajing twenty-seven, in the first month, Batu raided Guangning and Assistant Regional Commander Yan Zhen was killed in battle. On guiwei, amid debate over recovering the Ordos, Grand Coordinator of the Shaanxi Three Frontiers Zeng Xian was arrested and supervising secretaries and censors were beaten in open court. Xia Yan was removed from office. In the third month, on guisi, Zeng Xian was executed and Xia Yan was arrested. On guimao Qiu Luan was released from prison. In the fifth month of summer, on bingxu, Empress Xiaolie was buried. In the seventh month of autumn, on wuyin, an earthquake struck the capital. On gengzi auspicious grain from the Western Park was presented and offered at the Imperial Ancestral Temple. In the eighth month, on dingsi, Anda invaded Datong; Commander Gu Xiang and others were killed in battle, and Zhou Shangwen pursued and defeated him at Ciyekou. In the ninth month, on renwu, he invaded Xuanfu and pushed deep into Yongning, Huailai, and Longqing; Garrison Commander Lu Chengen and others were killed in battle. On yiwei tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken Shaanxi. In the tenth month of winter, on guimao, Xia Yan was put to death. In the eleventh month, on yiwei, an edict directed grand coordinators and regional inspectors to collect raw placer gold. That year Japan sent a tribute mission.
8
二十九年春三月壬午，賜唐汝楫等進士及第、出身有差。 是月，瓊州黎賊平。 夏六月丁巳，俺答犯大同，總兵官張達、副總兵林椿戰死。 是夏，免陝西、河南、江北被災夏稅。 秋八月丙寅，封方士陶仲文為恭誠伯。 丁丑，俺答大舉入寇，攻古北口，薊鎮兵潰。 戊寅，掠通州，駐白河，分掠畿甸州縣，京師戒嚴。 召大同總兵官仇鸞及河南、山東兵入援。 壬午，薄都城。 仇鸞為平虜大將軍，節制諸路兵馬，巡撫保定都御史楊守謙提督軍務，左諭德趙貞吉宣諭諸軍。 癸未，始禦奉天殿，戒敕群臣。 甲申，寇退。 逮守通州都御史王儀。 丙戌，京師解嚴。 杖趙貞吉，謫外任。 丁亥，仇鸞敗績于白羊口。 兵部尚書丁汝夔、巡撫侍郎楊守謙有罪，棄市。 杖左都御史屠僑、刑部侍郎彭黯。 九月辛卯，振畿內被寇者。 乙未，罷團營，復三大營舊制，設戎政府，以仇鸞總督之。 丁酉，罷領營中官。 戊申，免畿內被災稅糧。 壬子，廢鄭王厚烷為庶人。 冬十月甲戌，張治卒。 十一月癸巳，分遣御史選邊軍入衛。 壬寅，祧仁宗，祔孝烈皇后于太廟。 是年，琉球入貢。
In spring of Jiajing twenty-nine, on renwu of the third month, Tang Ruji and others received jinshi ranks, palace degrees, and provincial degrees according to merit. That month the Li rebels of Qiongzhou were suppressed. In the sixth month of summer, on dingsi, Anda invaded Datong; Regional Commander Zhang Da and Vice Regional Commander Lin Chun were killed in battle. That summer summer tax was forgiven in disaster-stricken Shaanxi, Henan, and Jiangbei. In the eighth month of autumn, on bingyin, court Daoist adept Tao Zhongwen was enfeoffed as Baron of Gongcheng. On dingchou Anda launched a major invasion, assaulted Gubeikou, and routed the Jizhen garrison. On wuyin he plundered Tongzhou, camped at Baihe, and sent detachments to raid prefectures and counties around the capital; Beijing was placed under martial law. Qiu Luan, regional commander of Datong, and troops from Henan and Shandong were called in to relieve the capital. On renwu the raiders drew near the capital. Qiu Luan was appointed General Who Pacifies the Barbarians with command over all route armies; Grand Coordinator of Baoding Yang Shouqian took charge of military affairs; and Left Lecturer Zhao Zhenji proclaimed the imperial will to the troops. On guiwei the emperor held his first audience at the Hall of Upholding Heaven and issued stern admonitions to the ministers. On jiashen the raiders withdrew. Wang Yi, censor-in-chief responsible for Tongzhou's defense, was arrested. On bingxu martial law in the capital was lifted. Zhao Zhenji was beaten with the staff and banished to an outside appointment. On dinghai Qiu Luan was defeated at Baiyangkou. Minister of War Ding Ruqie and Grand Coordinator Yang Shouqian were found guilty and executed in the marketplace. Tu Qiao, left censor-in-chief, and Peng An, vice minister of justice, were flogged at court. In the ninth month, on xinmao, the court sent relief to those in the metropolitan region harmed by the raid. On yiwei the Corps Camps were abolished, the old Three Great Camps system was restored, a Military Affairs Bureau was set up, and Qiu Luan was made its director-general. On dingyou the eunuchs who commanded the camps were removed from their posts. On wushen tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken areas within the metropolitan region. On renzi Prince of Zheng Hou Yan was stripped of his title and reduced to commoner status. In the tenth month of winter, on jiaxu, Zhang Zhi died. In the eleventh month, on guisi, censors were sent out separately to select frontier troops for service in the capital garrison. On renyin Emperor Renzong was removed from the main line of the ancestral temple, and Empress Xiaolie was enshrined in the Imperial Ancestral Temple. That year Ryukyu sent a tribute mission.
9
三十年春三月壬辰，開馬市于宣府、大同，兵部侍郎史道經理之。 夏四月壬午，下經略京城副都御史商大節於獄。 秋九月乙未，京師地震，詔修省。 冬十一月，俺答犯大同。 是年，免兩畿、河南、江西、遼東、貴州、山東、山西被災稅糧。
In the third month of spring in the thirtieth year, on renchen, horse markets were opened at Xuanfu and Datong under the supervision of Vice Minister of War Shi Dao. In the fourth month of summer, on renwu, Vice Censor-in-Chief Shang Dajie, commissioner for capital defense, was cast into prison. In the ninth month of autumn, on yiwei, an earthquake struck the capital, and an edict ordered the court to examine its conduct and reform. In the eleventh month of winter, Anda invaded Datong. That year taxes and grain levies were forgiven in disaster-stricken regions of the two metropolitan circuits, Henan, Jiangxi, Liaodong, Guizhou, Shandong, and Shanxi.
10
三十一年春正月壬辰，俺答犯大同。 甲午，入弘賜堡。 二月癸丑，振宣、大饑。 辛酉，俺答犯懷仁川，指揮僉事王恭戰死。 己巳，建內府營，操練內侍。 三月戊子，大將軍仇鸞帥師赴大同。 辛卯，禮部尚書徐階兼東閣大學士，預機務。 夏四月丙寅，把都兒、辛愛犯新興堡，指揮王相等戰死。 丙子，倭寇浙江。 五月甲申，召仇鸞還。 戊申，倭陷黃岩。 秋七月丙申，免陝西被災夏稅。 壬寅，以倭警命山東巡撫都御史王忬巡視浙江。 八月己未，收仇鸞大將軍印，尋病死。 乙亥，戮仇鸞屍，傳首九邊。 己卯，俺答犯大同，分掠朔、應、山陰、馬邑。 九月乙酉，犯山西三關。 壬辰，犯寧夏。 丁酉，河決徐州。 庚子，兵部侍郎蔣應奎、左通政唐國卿以冒邊功杖於廷。 癸卯，罷各邊馬市。 冬十月己未，兵部尚書趙錦坐仇鸞黨戍邊。 壬戌，免江西被災稅糧。 十二月丁巳。 光祿少卿馬從謙坐誹謗杖死。
In the first month of spring in the thirty-first year, on renchen, Anda invaded Datong. On jiawu he broke into Hongci Fort. In the second month, on guichou, famine relief was sent to Xuanfu and Datong. On xinyou Anda struck Huairen Stream, and Commander Wang Gong was killed in battle. On jisi the Inner Palace Camp was established to train eunuch attendants. In the third month, on wuzi, Great General Qiu Luan marched to Datong at the head of the army. On xinmao Xu Jie, minister of rites, was also made grand secretary of the Eastern Lodge and entered state deliberations. In the fourth month of summer, on bingyin, Batu and Xin Ai attacked Xinxing Fort, and Commander Wang Xiang and others were killed in battle. On bingzi Japanese pirates struck Zhejiang. In the fifth month, on jiashen, Qiu Luan was recalled to court. On wushen the Japanese overran Huangyan. In the seventh month of autumn, on bingshen, summer taxes were forgiven in disaster-stricken Shaanxi. On renyin, because of the Japanese alarm, Grand Coordinator Wang Yu of Shandong was ordered to inspect the defenses of Zhejiang. In the eighth month, on jiwei, Qiu Luan's seals as great general were confiscated, and he soon died of illness. On yihai Qiu Luan's corpse was posthumously executed, and his head was sent to the nine border regions. On jimao Anda invaded Datong and sent raiders to plunder Shuozhou, Yingzhou, Shanyin, and Mayi. In the ninth month, on yiyou, he struck the three passes of Shanxi. On renchen he invaded Ningxia. On dingyou the Yellow River burst its banks at Xuzhou. On gengzi Vice Minister of War Jiang Yingkui and Left Vice Commissioner Tang Guoqing were beaten at court for falsely claiming frontier merit. On guimao horse markets on all frontiers were closed. In the tenth month of winter, on jiwei, Minister of War Zhao Jin was exiled to the frontier as an associate of Qiu Luan's faction. On renxu taxes and grain levies were forgiven in disaster-stricken Jiangxi. In the twelfth month, on dingsi. Vice Director of the Imperial Household Ma Congqian was beaten to death at court for slander.
11
三十二年春正月戊寅朔，日食，陰雲不見。 己卯，侍郎吳鵬振淮、徐水災。 二月甲子，倭犯溫州。 壬申，俺答犯宣府，參將史略戰死。 三月丁丑，振陝西饑。 辛巳，吉能犯延綏，殺副總兵李梅。 壬午，兵部侍郎楊博巡邊。 甲申，振山東饑。 甲午，賜陳謹等進士及第、出身有差。 甲辰，俺答犯宣府，副總兵郭都戰死。 閏三月，海賊汪直糾倭寇瀕海諸郡，至六月始去。 秋七月戊午，俺答大舉入寇，犯靈丘、廣昌。 乙丑，河套諸部犯延綏。 己巳，俺答犯浮圖峪，遊擊陳鳳、硃玉禦之。 庚午，河南賊師尚詔陷歸德及柘城、鹿邑。 八月丙子，小王子犯赤城。 丙申，師尚詔攻太康，官軍與戰于鄢陵，敗績。 戊戌，振山東災，免稅糧。 九月丙午，俺答犯廣武，巡撫都御史趙時春敗績，總兵官李淶、參將馮恩等力戰死。 辛酉，以敵退告謝郊廟。 冬十月甲戌，振河南、山東饑。 庚子，師尚詔伏誅，賊平。 辛丑，京師外城成。 是年，琉球入貢。
In the first month of spring in the thirty-second year, on the new moon of wuyin, there was a solar eclipse, but clouds hid it from view. On jimao Vice Minister Wu Peng sent relief for flood disasters in the Huai region and Xuzhou. In the second month, on jiazi, Japanese pirates struck Wenzhou. On renshen Anda invaded Xuanfu, and Brigade Commander Shi Lue was killed in battle. In the third month, on dingchou, famine relief was sent to Shaanxi. On xinsi Ji Neng invaded Yan-Sui and killed Assistant Regional Commander Li Mei. On renwu Vice Minister of War Yang Bo toured the frontier defenses. On jiashen famine relief was sent to Shandong. On jiawu palace graduates including Chen Jin were granted jinshi honors with distinctions between top-tier and regular passes. On jiachen Anda invaded Xuanfu again, and Assistant Regional Commander Guo Du was killed in battle. In the intercalary third month the pirate Wang Zhi rallied Japanese raiders against the coastal commanderies, and they did not withdraw until the sixth month. In the seventh month of autumn, on wuwu, Anda launched a major invasion and struck Lingqiu and Guangchang. On yichou the Ordos tribes invaded Yan-Sui. On jisi Anda invaded Futuyu Pass, and Mobile Corps Commanders Chen Feng and Zhu Yu held him off. On gengwu the Henan bandit Shi Shangzhao seized Guide and the counties of Zhecheng and Luyi. In the eighth month, on bingzi, the Little Prince invaded Chicheng. On bingshen Shi Shangzhao attacked Taikang; government troops fought him at Yanling and were routed. On wuxu famine relief was sent to disaster-stricken Shandong, and taxes and grain levies were forgiven. Tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken Shaanxi and Huguang, and for Fujian areas harmed by raids. That year Ryukyu sent tribute.
21
四十二年春正月戊申，俺答犯宣府，南掠隆慶。 夏四月庚申，倭犯福清，總兵官劉顯、俞大猷合兵殲之。 丁卯，副總兵戚繼光破倭于平海衛。 秋八月乙亥，總兵官楊照襲寇于廣寧塞外，力戰死。 冬十月丁卯，辛愛、把都兒破牆子嶺入寇，京師戒嚴，詔諸鎮兵入援。 戊辰，掠順義、三河，總兵官孫臏敗死。 乙亥，大同總兵官姜應熊禦寇密雲，敗之。 十一月丁丑，京師解嚴。 是年，琉球入貢。
In the forty-second year, in the first month of spring, on wushen, Anda invaded Xuanfu and raided south as far as Longqing. In the fourth month of summer, on gengshen, pirates invaded Fuqing; Regional Commanders Liu Xian and Yu Dayou combined forces and wiped them out. On dingmao Assistant Regional Commander Qi Jiguang defeated the pirates at Pinghai Guard. In the eighth month of autumn, on yihai, Regional Commander Yang Zhao raided the enemy beyond Guangning Pass and was killed after a fierce fight. In the tenth month of winter, on dingmao, Xinai and Badu'er broke through Qiangzi Ridge and invaded; the capital was placed on alert and troops from all garrisons were ordered to reinforce. On wuchen they plundered Shunyi and Sanhe, and Regional Commander Sun Lin was defeated and killed. On yihai Jiang Yingxiong, regional commander of Datong, met the raiders at Miyun and defeated them. In the eleventh month, on dingchou, the capital's alert was lifted. That year Ryukyu sent tribute.
22
四十三年春正月壬辰，土蠻黑石炭寇薊鎮，總兵官胡鎮、參將白文智禦卻之。 二月己酉，伊王典楧有罪，廢為庶人。 戊午，倭犯仙游，總兵官戚繼光大敗之，福建倭平。 閏月丙申，盜據漳平，知縣魏文瑞死之。 三月己未，官軍擊潮州倭，破之。 夏四月乙亥，免畿內被災稅糧。 五月壬寅朔，日有食之。 乙卯，獲桃於禦幄，群臣表賀。 六月辛卯，倭犯海豐，俞大猷破之。 冬十二月，南韶賊起，守備賀鐸、指揮蔡胤元被執死之。 俺答犯山西，遊擊梁平、守備祁謀戰死。 是年，西番、哈密、安南入貢，魯迷國貢獅子。
In the forty-third year, in the first month of spring, on renchen, the Tu Man chieftain Heishitan raided Jizhou; Regional Commander Hu Zhen and Assistant Commander Bai Wenzhi drove them back. In the second month, on jiyou, Prince Dian of Yi was found guilty and degraded to commoner status. On wuwu pirates invaded Xianyou; Regional Commander Qi Jiguang routed them, and the pirates in Fujian were pacified. In the intercalary month, on bingshen, bandits seized Zhangping and Magistrate Wei Wenrui was killed. In the third month, on jiwei, government troops attacked the pirates in Chaozhou and defeated them. In the fourth month of summer, on yihai, tax grain was forgiven in disaster-stricken areas of the metropolitan region. In the fifth month, on the renyin new moon, there was a solar eclipse. On yimao a peach was found in the imperial canopy, and the court officials submitted congratulatory memorials. In the sixth month, on xinmao, pirates invaded Haifeng and Yu Dayou defeated them. In the twelfth month of winter bandits rose in Nanshao; Garrison Commander He Duo and Commander Cai Yinyuan were captured and killed. Anda invaded Shanxi; Mobile Corps Commander Liang Ping and Garrison Commander Qi Mou were killed in battle. That year Xifan, Hami, and Annam sent tribute, and the land of Rum presented a lion.
23
四十四年春三月丁巳，賜范應期等進士及第、出身有差。 己未，袁煒致仕。 辛酉，嚴世蕃伏誅。 是月，土蠻犯遼東，都指揮糸泉補袞、楊維籓戰死。 夏四月庚辰，吏部尚書嚴訥、禮部尚書李春芳並兼武英殿大學士，預機務。 壬午，俺答犯肅州，總兵官劉承業禦卻之。 六月甲戌，芝生睿宗原廟柱，告廟受賀，遂建玉芝宮。 秋八月壬午，獲仙藥於御座，告廟。 冬十一月癸卯，嚴訥致仕。 戊申，奉安獻皇帝、後神主于玉芝宮。 是年，琉球入貢。
In the forty-fourth year, in the third month of spring, on dingsi, jinshi including Fan Yingqi received their degrees and appointments with differing grades. On jiwei Yuan Wei retired from office. On xinyou Yan Shifan was executed. That month Tu Man raiders invaded Liaodong, and Commanders Xian Bugun and Yang Weifan were killed in battle. In the fourth month of summer, on gengchen, Yan Ne, minister of personnel, and Li Chunfang, minister of rites, were also made grand secretaries of the Wuying Hall and entered state deliberations. On renwu Anda invaded Suzhou and Regional Commander Liu Chengye drove him back. In the sixth month, on jiaxu, sacred fungus appeared on a pillar of Emperor Ruizong's original temple; the court announced it at the ancestral temple, received congratulations, and then built the Jade Fungus Palace. In the eighth month of autumn, on renwu, an elixir was found at the imperial throne and the court reported to the ancestral temple. In the eleventh month of winter, on guimao, Yan Ne retired from office. On wushen the spirit tablets of Emperor Xian and the Empress were installed in the Jade Fungus Palace. That year Ryukyu sent tribute.
24
贊曰：世宗禦極之初，力除一切弊政，天下翕然稱治。 顧迭議大禮，輿論沸騰，幸臣假託，尋興大獄。 夫天性至情，君親大義，追尊立廟，禮亦宜之； 然升祔太廟，而躋于武宗之上，不已過乎！ 若其時紛紜多故，將疲于邊，賊訌於內，而崇尚道教，享祀弗經，營建繁興，府藏告匱，百餘年富庶治平之業，因以漸替。 雖剪剔權奸，威柄在禦，要亦中材之主也矣。
Appraisal: At the start of Shizong's reign he energetically swept away corrupt practices, and the realm united in praising good governance. Yet when the Great Rites controversy erupted again and again, public opinion boiled over with it; favored ministers seized the moment, and soon great prosecutions followed. Natural feeling runs deepest; the duty owed to ruler and parent is a great moral principle—to posthumously honor one's father and establish a temple for him was ritually fitting as well; Yet to install him in the Imperial Ancestral Temple and place him above Emperor Wuzong—was that not going too far? As for his reign, affairs proliferated without end; armies were worn down on the frontier and rebellion broke out within, even as he exalted Daoism, conducted irregular sacrifices, and launched construction on every side until the treasury was empty—more than a century's work of prosperity and order thus ebbed away. Although he cut down powerful schemers and kept authority firmly in his own hands, on the whole he was a ruler of only moderate gifts.
