洪武元年春正月乙亥，祀天地於南郊，即皇帝位。 定有天下之號曰明，建元洪武。 追尊高祖考曰玄皇帝，廟號德祖，曾祖考曰恆皇帝，廟號懿祖； 祖考曰裕皇帝，廟號熙祖，皇考曰淳皇帝，廟號仁祖，妣皆皇后。 立妃馬氏為皇后，世子標為皇太子。 以李善長、徐達為左、右丞相，諸功臣進爵有差。 丙子，頒即位詔於天下。 追封皇伯考以下皆為王。 辛巳，李善長、徐達等兼東宮官。 甲申，遣使覈浙西田賦。 壬辰，胡廷瑞克建寧。 庚子，鄧愈為征戍將軍，略南陽以北州郡。 湯和克延平，執元平章陳友定，福建平。 是月，天下府州縣官來朝。 諭曰：「天下始定，民財力俱困，要在休養安息，惟廉者能約己而利人，勉之。 」二月壬寅，定郊社宗廟禮，歲必親祀，以為常。 癸卯，湯和提督海運。 廖永忠為征南將軍，硃亮祖副之，由海道取廣東。 丁未，乙太牢祀先師孔子於國學。 戊申，祀社稷。 壬子，詔衣冠如唐制。 癸丑，常遇春克東昌，山東平。 甲寅，楊璟克寶慶。 三月辛未，詔儒臣修女誡，戒後妃毋預政。 壬申，周德興克全州。 丁酉，鄧愈克南陽。 己亥，徐達徇汴梁，左君弼降。 夏四月辛丑，蘄州進竹簟，卻之，命四方毋妄獻。 廖永忠師至廣州，元守臣何真降，廣東平。 丁未，祫享太廟。 戊申，徐達、常遇春大破元兵於洛水北，遂圍河南。 梁王阿魯溫降，河南平。 丁巳，楊璟克永州。 甲子，幸汴梁。 丙寅，馮勝克潼關，李思齊、張思道遁。 五月己卯，廖永忠下梧州，潯、貴、容、郁林諸州皆降。 辛卯，改汴梁路為開封府。 六月庚子，徐達朝行在。 甲辰，海南、海北諸道降。 壬戌，楊璟、硃亮祖克靖江。 秋七月戊子，廖永忠下象州，廣西平。 庚寅，振恤中原貧民。 辛卯，將還應天，諭達等曰：「中原之民，久為群雄所苦，流離相望，故命將北征，拯民水火。 元祖宗功德在人，其子孫罔恤民隱，天厭棄之。 君則有罪，民復何辜。 前代革命之際，肆行屠戮，違天虐民，朕實不忍。 諸將克城，毋肆焚掠妄殺人，元之宗戚，咸俾保全。 庶幾上答天心，下慰人望，以副朕伐罪安民之意。 不恭命者，罰無赦。 」丙申，命馮勝留守開封。 閏月丁未，至自開封。 己酉，徐達會諸將兵於臨清。 壬子，常遇春克德州。 丙寅，克通州，元帝趨上都。 是月，征天下賢才為守令。 免吳江、慶德、太平、甯國、滁、和被災田租。 八月己巳，以應天為南京，開封為北京。 庚午，徐達入元都，封府庫圖籍，守宮門，禁士卒侵暴，遣將巡古北口諸隘。 壬申，以京師火，四方水旱，詔中書省集議便民事。 丁丑，定六部官制。 御史中丞劉基致仕。 己卯，赦殊死以下。 將士從征者恤其家，逋逃許自首。 新克州郡毋妄殺。 輸賦道遠者，官為轉運，災荒以實聞。 免鎮江租稅。 避亂民復業者，聽墾荒地，復三年。 衍聖公襲封及授曲阜知縣，並如前代制。 有司以禮聘致賢士，學校毋事虛文。 平刑，毋非時決囚。 除書籍田器稅，民間逋負免征。 蒙古、色目人有才能者，許擢用。 鰥寡孤獨廢疾者，存恤之。 民年七十以上，一子復。 他利害當興革不在詔內者，有司具以聞。 壬午，幸北京。 改大都路曰北平府。 征元故臣。 癸未，詔徐達、常遇春取山西。 甲午，放元宮人。 九月癸亥，詔曰：「天下之治，天下之賢共理之。 今賢士多隱岩穴，豈有司失於敦勸歟，朝廷疏於禮待歟，抑朕寡昧不足致賢，將在位者壅蔽使不上達歟？ 不然，賢士大夫，幼學壯行，豈甘沒世而已哉。 天下甫定，朕願與諸儒講明治道。 有能輔朕濟民者，有司禮遣。 」乙丑，常遇春下保定，遂下真定。 冬十月庚午，馮勝、湯和下懷慶，澤、潞相繼下。 丁丑，至自北京。 戊寅，以元都平，詔天下。 十一月己亥，遣使分行天下，訪求賢才。 庚子，始祀上帝於圜丘。 癸亥，詔劉基還。 十二月丁卯，徐達克太原，擴廓帖木兒走甘肅，山西平。 己巳，置登聞鼓。 壬辰，以書諭明昇。
In spring of the first year of Hongwu, on the yihai day of the first month, the Hongwu Emperor offered sacrifice to Heaven and Earth at the southern altar and took the throne. He proclaimed the dynastic name Ming for his rule over the realm and adopted Hongwu as the reign title. He posthumously elevated his great-great-grandfather to Emperor Xuan, with the temple name Dezū, and his great-grandfather to Emperor Heng, with the temple name Yìzǔ; his grandfather to Emperor Yu, temple name Xīzǔ; his father to Emperor Chun, temple name Rénzǔ—with each of their wives honored as Empress. He made Consort Ma empress and named the heir apparent, Zhu Biao, crown prince. Li Shanchang and Xu Da were named left and right grand councillors, and the founding ministers received promotions of varying degree. On the bingzi day, the enthronement edict was issued throughout the empire. He posthumously granted royal titles to his father's elder brothers and their descendants. On the xinsi day, Li Shanchang, Xu Da, and others were given additional posts in the crown prince's establishment. On the jiashen day, commissioners were sent to audit land taxes in western Zhejiang. On the renchen day, Hu Tingrui took Jianning. On the gengzi day, Deng Yu was appointed campaigning garrison general and marched to subdue the regions north of Nanyang. Tang He seized Yanping and captured the Yuan vice councillor Chen Youding, bringing Fujian under control. That month, magistrates from prefectures, departments, and counties across the empire presented themselves at court. He admonished them: "The realm has only just been pacified, and the people's resources are utterly depleted. What matters now is recovery and repose. Only the upright, who curb themselves and serve others, can achieve this—strive for that." In the second month, on the renyin day, the rituals for the suburban sacrifices, the altars of soil and grain, and the ancestral temples were standardized; the emperor would perform them in person every year as established custom. On the guimao day, Tang He was appointed to oversee sea transport. Liao Yongzhong was named southern campaigning general, with Zhu Liangzu as his deputy, to seize Guangdong by sea. On the dingwei day, he offered the great sacrifice to Confucius at the National University. On the wushen day, he performed the rites at the altars of soil and grain. On the renzi day, an edict decreed that court dress should follow Tang dynasty regulations. On the guichou day, Chang Yuchun took Dongchang, completing the pacification of Shandong. On the jiayin day, Yang Jing captured Baoqing. In the third month, on the xinwei day, an edict directed scholarly officials to compile Admonitions for Women, forbidding imperial consorts from meddling in state affairs. On the renshen day, Zhou Dexing seized Quanzhou. On the dingyou day, Deng Yu took Nanyang. On the jihai day, Xu Da marched on Bianliang, and Zuo Junbi submitted. In the fourth month of summer, on the xinchou day, Qizhou offered bamboo sleeping mats; the emperor refused them and commanded that no region should send unsolicited tribute. Liao Yongzhong's forces reached Guangzhou, where the Yuan defender He Zhen surrendered, bringing Guangdong under Ming control. On the dingwei day, the seasonal rites merging ancestral spirits were held at the imperial ancestral temple. On the wushen day, Xu Da and Chang Yuchun routed the Yuan army north of the Luo River and laid siege to Henan. The Prince of Liang, Aruwen, submitted, and Henan was pacified. On the dingsi day, Yang Jing took Yongzhou. On the jiazi day, the emperor traveled to Bianliang. On the bingyin day, Feng Sheng seized Tong Pass while Li Siqi and Zhang Sidao fled. In the fifth month, on the jimao day, Liao Yongzhong captured Wuzhou, and the prefectures of Xun, Gui, Rong, and Yulin all submitted. On the xinmao day, Bianliang Circuit was redesignated Kaifeng Prefecture. In the sixth month, on the gengzi day, Xu Da presented himself at the imperial headquarters. On the jiachen day, the circuits of Hainan and Haibei submitted. On the renxu day, Yang Jing and Zhu Liangzu took Jingjiang. In autumn of the seventh month, on the wuzi day, Liao Yongzhong captured Xiangzhou, completing the pacification of Guangxi. On the gengyin day, relief was extended to the destitute of the Central Plains. On the xinmao day, as he prepared to return to Yingtian, he addressed Xu Da and his commanders: "The people of the Central Plains have endured years of warlord depredation, scattering in desperate exile. That is why I sent you north—to pluck them from fire and flood." The founders of the Yuan earned the people's gratitude, yet their heirs ignored the people's plight, until Heaven itself turned against them. If the sovereign is guilty, what fault lies with the common people? Past dynastic upheavals brought slaughter that defied Heaven and ravaged the people—something I cannot bring myself to repeat. When you take a city, do not burn, loot, or kill indiscriminately. Preserve the Yuan imperial kin. Thus you may answer Heaven above and satisfy the people's hopes below, fulfilling my purpose of punishing the guilty while sparing the innocent. Whoever disobeys this order shall be punished without mercy. On the bingshen day, Feng Sheng was ordered to remain and guard Kaifeng. On the dingwei day of the intercalary month, he returned from Kaifeng. On the jiyou day, Xu Da gathered the commanders' forces at Linqing. On the renzi day, Chang Yuchun took Dezhou. On the bingyin day, Tongzhou fell, and the Yuan emperor fled north to Shangdu. That month, the court recruited talented men empire-wide to serve as local administrators. Land taxes were waived for disaster-affected districts in Wujiang, Qingde, Taiping, Ningguo, Chu, and He. In the eighth month, on the jisi day, Yingtian was designated the Southern Capital and Kaifeng the Northern Capital. On the gengwu day, Xu Da entered the Yuan capital, sealed the treasuries and archives, posted guards at the palace gates, prohibited looting by the troops, and sent officers to patrol the passes at Gubeikou and elsewhere. On the renshen day, in response to fires in the capital and floods and droughts across the realm, the Secretariat was ordered to convene and propose measures to ease the people's burdens. On the dingchou day, the bureaucratic structure of the six ministries was established. Liu Ji, vice censor-in-chief, resigned his post. On the jimao day, amnesty was granted for crimes short of capital punishment. Families of soldiers on campaign received relief; deserters were allowed to surrender without penalty. Indiscriminate killing was forbidden in newly conquered territories. Where tax transport was impractically distant, officials would handle conveyance; reports of famine and disaster must reflect actual conditions. Taxes in Zhenjiang were remitted. Refugees returning to their livelihoods might reclaim wasteland with a three-year tax holiday. The title Duke Yansheng was conferred in succession, and an official was appointed magistrate of Qufu, following precedent of earlier dynasties. Local authorities were to recruit worthy scholars with proper ceremony; schools must abandon hollow formalism. Punishments were to be applied equitably, with no irregular executions. Taxes on books, farming tools, and land were repealed, and private debts to the state were forgiven. Talented Mongols and Central Asians (Semu) were eligible for government appointment. The widowed, orphaned, childless, disabled, and infirm were to receive state care. Households with a member over seventy received corvée exemption for one son. Other reforms not covered by the edict were to be reported by officials in detail. On the renwu day, the emperor traveled to the Northern Capital. Dadu Circuit was redesignated Beiping Prefecture. Former Yuan officials were summoned to serve. On the guiwei day, Xu Da and Chang Yuchun were ordered to conquer Shanxi. On the jiawu day, women of the Yuan palace were set free. In the ninth month, on the guihai day, an edict declared: "To govern the realm requires the worthy of the realm to govern together with the throne." Yet many worthy men remain in seclusion. Is it that local officials have failed to encourage them, that the court has neglected them, that I lack the virtue to attract talent, or that those in power block their path to the throne? Surely men of learning and principle, trained from youth for service, do not wish to die obscure and unused. The realm is newly pacified, and I desire to deliberate on good governance with the scholarly elite. Let those who can aid me in serving the people be respectfully escorted to court by local authorities. On the yichou day, Chang Yuchun captured Baoding and then Zhending. In the tenth month of winter, on the gengwu day, Feng Sheng and Tang He captured Huaiqing, followed by the fall of Ze and Lu. On the dingchou day, the emperor returned from the Northern Capital. On the wuyin day, an empire-wide edict announced the pacification of the Yuan capital. In the eleventh month, on the jihai day, envoys were dispatched throughout the empire to seek out men of talent. On the gengzi day, the emperor performed the first sacrifice to Heaven at the Circular Mound Altar. On the guihai day, Liu Ji was summoned back to office. In the twelfth month, on the dingmao day, Xu Da took Taiyuan; Köke Temür fled to Gansu, and Shanxi was pacified. On the jisi day, the Petition Drum was established at court. On the renchen day, a letter was sent instructing Ming Sheng.

二年春正月乙巳，立功臣廟於雞籠山。 丁未，享太廟。 庚戌，詔曰：「朕淮右布衣，因天下亂，率眾渡江，保民圖治，今十有五年。 荷天眷祐，悉皆戡定。 用是命將北征，齊魯之民饋糧給軍，不憚千里。 朕軫厥勞，已免元年田租。 遭旱民未蘇，其更賜一年。 頃者大軍平燕都，下晉、冀，民被兵燹，困征斂，北平、燕南、河東、山西今年田租亦與蠲免。 河南諸郡歸附，久欲惠之，西北未平，師過其地，是以未遑。 今晉、冀平矣，西抵潼關，北界大河，南至唐、鄧、光、息，今年稅糧悉除之。 」又詔曰：「應天、太平、鎮江、宣城、廣德供億浩穰。 去歲蠲租，遇旱惠不及下。 其再免諸郡及無為州今年租稅。 」庚申，常遇春取大同。 是月，倭寇山東濱海郡縣。 二月丙寅朔，詔修元史。 壬午，耕耤田。 三月庚子，徐達至奉元，張思道遁。 振陝西饑，戶米三石。 丙午，常遇春至鳳翔，李思齊奔臨洮。 夏四月丙寅，遇春還師北平。 己巳，諸王子受經於博士孔克仁。 令功臣子弟入學。 乙亥，編《祖訓錄》，定封建諸王之制。 徐達下鞏昌。 丙子，賜秦、隴新附州縣稅糧。 丁丑，馮勝至臨洮，李思齊降。 乙酉，徐達襲破元豫王於西寧。 五月甲午朔，日有食之。 丁酉，徐達下平涼、延安。 張良臣以慶陽降，尋叛。 癸卯，始祀地於方丘。 六月己卯，常遇春克開平，元帝北走。 壬午，封陳日煃為安南國王。 秋七月己亥，鄂國公常遇春卒於軍，詔李文忠領其眾。 辛亥，擴廓帖木兒遣將破原州、涇州。 辛酉，馮勝擊走之。 丙辰，明昇遣使來。 八月丙寅，元兵攻大同，李文忠擊敗之。 己巳，定內侍官制。 諭吏部曰：「內臣但備使令，毋多人，古來若輩擅權，可為鑒戒。 馭之之道，當使之畏法，勿令有功，有功則驕恣矣。 」癸酉，《元史》成。 丙子，封王顓為高麗國王。 癸未，徐達克慶陽，斬張良臣，陝西平。 是月，命儒臣纂禮書。 九月辛丑，召徐達、湯和還，馮勝留總軍事。 癸卯，以臨濠為中都。 戊午，征南師還。 冬十月壬戌，遣楊璟諭明昇。 甲戌，甘露降於鐘山，群臣請告廟，不許。 辛卯，詔天下郡縣立學。 是月，遣使貽元帝書。 十一月乙巳，祀上帝於圜丘，以仁祖配。 十二月甲戌，封阿答阿者為占城國王。 甲申，振西安諸府饑，戶米二石。 己丑，大賚平定中原及征南將士。 庚寅，擴廓帖木兒攻蘭州，指揮於光死之。 是年，占城、安南、高麗入貢。
In spring of the second year, on the yisi day of the first month, a temple honoring meritorious officials was established on Jilong Mountain. On the dingwei day, offerings were presented at the imperial ancestral temple. On the gengxu day, an edict proclaimed: "I was but a commoner of Huaiyou. When the realm fell into chaos, I led my followers across the Yangzi to shelter the people and seek order—now fifteen years have passed." By Heaven's grace, all adversaries have been subdued. Hence I sent armies north; the people of Qi and Lu supplied grain to the troops without hesitation, traveling a thousand li to do so. I have taken pity on their labor and already remitted the land tax for the first year. Because drought had struck and the people had not yet recovered, another year's exemption was granted. Not long ago the main army had pacified the Yan capital and overrun Jin and Ji. The people had suffered war's devastation and crushing levies; land tax for the current year in Beiping, southern Yan, Hedong, and Shanxi was likewise remitted. The prefectures of Henan had long since submitted, and I had long wished to show them favor; because the northwest was not yet pacified and armies marched through their territory, I had been unable to do so until now. Now that Jin and Ji were pacified—west to Tong Pass, north to the Yellow River, south through Tang, Deng, Guang, and Xi—all grain taxes for the current year were abolished. Another edict read: "Yingtian, Taiping, Zhenjiang, Xuancheng, and Guangde bear extraordinarily heavy supply burdens. Last year's rent remission, combined with drought, had not fully benefited the common people. Rent and tax for the current year were again remitted in those prefectures and in Wuhu." On the gengshen day, Chang Yuchun captured Datong. That month, Japanese pirates attacked coastal prefectures and counties in Shandong. On the first day of the second month, the bingyin day, an edict ordered work on the History of the Yuan. On the renwu day, the emperor plowed the sacred field. On the gengzi day of the third month, Xu Da reached Fengyuan and Zhang Sidao fled. Famine relief was sent to Shaanxi at three shi of grain per household. On the bingwu day, Chang Yuchun reached Fengxiang while Li Siqi fled to Lintao. In the fourth month of summer, on the bingyin day, Yuchun marched his army back to Beiping. On the jisi day, the imperial princes began studying the classics under Erudite Kong Keren. Sons of founding ministers were ordered to enroll in the schools. On the yihai day, the Records of Ancestral Admonitions was compiled and regulations for enfeoffing princes were established. Xu Da took Gongchang. On the bingzi day, grain taxes were remitted in the newly submitted prefectures and counties of Qin and Long. On the dingchou day, Feng Sheng reached Lintao and Li Siqi surrendered. On the yiyou day, Xu Da routed the Yuan Prince of Yu in a surprise attack at Xining. On the first day of the fifth month, the jiawu day, a solar eclipse occurred. On the dingyou day, Xu Da captured Pingliang and Yan'an. Zhang Liangchen surrendered from Qingyang but soon rebelled. On the guimao day, sacrifice to Earth was first performed at the square altar. On the jimao day of the sixth month, Chang Yuchun took Kaiping and the Yuan emperor fled north. On the renwu day, Chen Rixuan was enfeoffed as king of Annan. On the jihai day of the seventh month in autumn, Duke of E Chang Yuchun died in the field; Li Wenzhong was ordered to take command of his army. On the xinhai day, Köke Temür's generals seized Yuanzhou and Jingzhou. On the xinyou day, Feng Sheng attacked and drove them back. On the bingchen day, Ming Sheng dispatched envoys to court. On the bingyin day of the eighth month, Yuan forces attacked Datong and Li Wenzhong repulsed them. On the jisi day, regulations for the eunuch bureaucracy were established. He instructed the Ministry of Personnel: "Eunuchs are only to carry out orders—do not employ too many. History shows how such men seize power; let that be your warning. The way to govern them is to make them fear the law. Do not let them earn merit, for merit breeds arrogance and excess." On the guiyou day, the History of the Yuan was completed. On the bingzi day, Wang Zhuan was enfeoffed as king of Goryeo. On the guiwei day, Xu Da took Qingyang, executed Zhang Liangchen, and Shaanxi was pacified. That month, scholar-officials were ordered to compile a ritual compendium. On the xinchou day of the ninth month, Xu Da and Tang He were recalled while Feng Sheng stayed behind as supreme commander. On the guimao day, Linhao was designated the central capital. On the wuwu day, the southern expeditionary force returned. On the renxu day of the tenth month in winter, Yang Jing was sent to deliver an imperial message to Ming Sheng. On the jiaxu day, sweet dew fell on Zhong Mountain. The court officials asked to report the omen at the ancestral temple, but the emperor refused. On the xinmao day, an edict ordered schools established in every prefecture and county. That month, envoys were dispatched with a letter to the Yuan emperor. On the yisi day of the eleventh month, sacrifice was offered to Heaven at the round altar, with the Benevolent Ancestor as co-sacrifice. On the jiaxu day of the twelfth month, A Da'a Zhe was enfeoffed as king of Champa. On the jiashen day, famine relief was sent to the prefectures around Xi'an at two shi per household. On the jichou day, lavish rewards were granted to the armies that had pacified the Central Plain and campaigned in the south. On the gengyin day, Köke Temür attacked Lanzhou and Commander Yu Guang was killed in the defense. That year, Champa, Annan, and Goryeo presented tribute.

In spring of the third year, on the guisi day of the first month, Xu Da was appointed grand general for the northern expedition; Li Wenzhong, Feng Sheng, Deng Yu, and Tang He served as his deputies, each marching north by a separate route. On the guiwei day of the second month, Guo Zixing was posthumously enfeoffed as Prince of Chuyang. On the wuzi day, an edict called for talented men fit to serve in the six ministries. That month, Li Wenzhong took Xinghe, marched on Chaghan-nur, and captured the Yuan vice councillor Zhu Zhen. On the gengyin day of the third month, this year's land rent was remitted in the southern capital region, Henan, Shandong, Beiping, eastern Zhejiang, Guangxin in Jiangxi, and Raozhou. On the yichou day of the fourth month in summer, the imperial sons were enfeoffed: Shu as prince of Qin, Gang as prince of Jin, Di as prince of Yan, Su as prince of Wu, Zhen as prince of Chu, Fu as prince of Qi, Zi as prince of Tan, Qi as prince of Zhao, Tan as prince of Lu; the emperor's grandnephew Shouqian was made prince of Jingjiang. At Shen'er Valley Xu Da routed Köke Temür, accepted the surrender of his entire army, and Köke Temür fled to Karakorum. On the bingxu day, the Yuan emperor died at Yingchang and was succeeded by his son Ayushiridara. That month, the tribal magistrate Tan Hu of Cili rose in rebellion. On the jichou day of the fifth month, Xu Da captured Xingyuan. Deng Yu was sent separately to summon and win over the Tibetans. On the dingyou day, an edict ordered local officials to recommend scholars of proven learning and upright character. On the jihai day, the civil service examination was instituted. On the jiachen day, Li Wenzhong took Yingchang. The Yuan heir fled north. His son Maidiribala was captured and more than fifty thousand men surrendered. Pursuit continued as far as northern Qingzhou, but they could not overtake him and turned back. On the dingwei day, an edict ordered observance of the great archery rite. On the wushen day, sacrifice to Earth was offered at the square altar, with the Benevolent Ancestor as co-sacrifice. On the xinhai day, Xu Da captured Xingyuan. Deng Yu took Hezhou. On the dingsi day, an edict provided stipends for the families of founding commanders who had left no male heir. That month drought struck. The emperor fasted and purified himself; empresses and consorts personally cooked at the hearth; the crown prince and princes brought offerings to the fasting lodge. On the first day of the sixth month, the wuwu day, dressed in plain white and straw sandals, he walked to the mountain and river altars to pray, sleeping outdoors for three days before returning to continue his fast in the western gallery. On the xinyou day, troops were rewarded and prisoners reviewed; officials were ordered to seek men learned in the classics, skilled in administration, and sound in principle. On the renxu day, heavy rain fell. On the renshen day, Li Wenzhong's victory report arrived. The emperor ordered that former Yuan officials should not offer congratulations. The Yuan emperor was posthumously titled Emperor Shun. On the guiyou day, Maidiribala arrived in the capital. Court officials asked to present him as a captive. The emperor asked, "Did King Wu use captives this way when he overthrew the Shang?" The ministers cited Emperor Taizong of Tang, who had done so. The emperor replied, "Taizong was dealing with Wang Shichong. Had he faced the descendant of the Sui, I doubt he would have done the same." He refused. Finding the victory memorial full of boastful language, he told the chancellor: "The Yuan ruled China for a century. Our parents all lived under their rule—how can you write such unworthy triumphalism? Change it at once." On the yihai day, Maidiribala was enfeoffed as Marquis of Chongli. On the bingzi day, victory was reported at the southern altar. On the dingchou day, victory was announced at the imperial ancestral temple and an edict was promulgated throughout the realm. On the xinsi day, idle residents of Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, Huzhou, and Hangzhou were resettled on farmland at Linhao, given grain, oxen, and seed, and granted three years' tax exemption. That month, Japanese pirates raided coastal prefectures and counties in Shandong, Zhejiang, and Fujian. On the bingchen day of the seventh month in autumn, Wu Youren, a general of Ming Sheng, raided Hanzhong and Vice Councillor Fu Youde drove him back. Left vice grand councillor Yang Xian was executed for his crimes. On the yiyou day of the eighth month, envoys were sent to inter the unburied dead of the Central Plain. On the bingchen day of the tenth month in winter, an edict ordered scholars to take turns at the Meridian Gate lecturing military officers on the classics and histories. On the guihai day, Zhou Dexing was appointed southern campaign general to suppress Tan Hu, who fled. On the xinsi day, a letter was sent to the Yuan heir. On the renchen day of the eleventh month, the northern expeditionary army returned. On the jiawu day, the emperor reported the fulfillment of his military mandate at the suburban altars. On the bingshen day, founding ministers received lavish enfeoffments. Li Shanchang was promoted to Duke of Han and Xu Da to Duke of Wei; Li Wenzhong became Duke of Cao, Feng Sheng Duke of Song, Deng Yu Duke of Wei, Chang Yuchun's son Mao Duke of Zheng, and Tang He together with twenty-eight others were created marquises. On the jihai day, he erected an altar and offered sacrifice in person to the generals fallen in war. On the gengxu day, he conducted the suburban sacrifice at the Circular Mound Altar. On the xinhai day, an edict directed the Board of Revenue to institute household registers and household certificates, with yearly accounts of gains and losses reported to the throne and codified as standing law. On the yimao day, Wang Guangyang, right vice grand councillor, was created Count of Zhongqin, and Liu Ji, censor-in-chief, Count of Chengyi. In the twelfth month, on the guihai day, he again wrote to the Yuan successor and issued instructions to the Karakorum tribes. On the jiazi day, the Hall for Venerating the Ancestors was erected. On the gengwu day, envoys were sent to offer sacrifice at the tombs of former emperors and to undertake their restoration. On the jimao day, land grants were bestowed on the founding ministers. On the renwu day, noting that sunspots had appeared repeatedly at noon from the first month onward, he ordered the court to deliberate on the empire's merits and failings. That year, embassies arrived from Champa, Java, and the Western Ocean.

In the twelfth month, Xu Da came back to court. That year, tribute missions arrived from Annam, Poni, Goryeo, Sanfoqi, Siam, Japan, and Zhenla.

On the gengzi day, Deng Yu subdued the Sanmao cave tribes and their neighbors. In the fifth month, on the renzi day, Xu Da engaged the Yuan forces north of the steppe and suffered defeat. That month, an edict proclaimed: "The realm is at last united; ritual and custom must be brought into order. All who were enslaved during the wars shall be restored to free status. The cold and hungry shall receive loans from prosperous neighbors; orphans, widows, and the infirm shall be maintained by the state, so that none lack a home. In every community, age shall govern precedence; neighbors shall greet one another with due ceremony and not depart from propriety. Marriage shall not be made a matter of price. Funerals shall suit a family's means; no one shall be swayed by geomantic superstition to leave coffins unburied and exposed. Refugees who return to farming may till according to their strength, without being bound to their old holdings alone. Buddhist and Daoist rites that mingle the sexes and end in revelry shall be rigorously suppressed by local authorities. Great houses in Fujian and Guangdong must not castrate their sons for service as household eunuchs; offenders shall be punished to the full extent of the law." In the sixth month, on the bingzi day, regulations governing eunuchs were promulgated. On the dingchou day, the ranks and duties of palace women were codified. On the guisi day, the responsibilities of the Six Ministries and the rules for annual evaluation were established. On the renyin day, Wu Liang pacified the tribes of Jingzhou. In the eighth month, on the bingshen day, Wu Liang subdued the tribes of Wukai, Guzhou, and neighboring districts. On the jiachen day, Yuan forces raided Yunnei; Vice Commissioner Huang Li was killed defending it. In the ninth month, on the wuwu day, Zhou Dexing pacified the Lanfeng, Antian, and related tribes. In the tenth month of winter, on the dingyou day, Feng Sheng's expedition marched home. That month, grain rents were waived in Yingtian, Taiping, Zhenjiang, Ningguo, and Guangde. In the eleventh month, on the xinyou day, he conducted the suburban sacrifice at the Circular Mound Altar. On the jiazi day, the southern expedition returned. On the renshen day, Naghachu raided Liaodong. That month, Xu Da and Li Wenzhong were summoned back. On the xinsi day, officials were required to submit memorials to the crown prince before the throne. On the gengzi day, Deng Yu was appointed campaigning west general to march against Tibet. Goryeo sent tribute twice; the court directed that future missions should come only once every three years.

六年春正月甲寅，謫汪廣洋為廣東參政。 二月乙未，諭暫罷科舉，察舉賢才。 壬寅，命御史及按察使考察有司。 三月癸卯朔，日有食之。 頒《昭鑒錄》，訓誡諸王。 戊申，太閱。 壬子，徐達為征虜大將軍，李文忠、馮勝、鄧愈、湯和副之，備邊山西、北平。 甲子，指揮使於顯為總兵官，備倭。 夏四月己丑，令有司上山川險易圖。 六月壬午，盱眙獻瑞麥，薦宗廟。 壬辰，擴廓帖木兒遣兵攻雁門，指揮吳均擊卻之。 是月，免北平、河間、河南、開封、延安、汾州被災田租。 秋七月壬寅，命戶部稽渡江以來各省水旱災傷分數，優恤之。 壬子，胡惟庸為右丞相，八月乙亥，詔祀三皇及歷代帝王。 冬十月辛巳，召徐達、馮勝還。 十一月壬子，擴廓帖木兒犯大同，徐達遣將擊敗之，達仍留鎮。 甲子，遣兵部尚書劉仁振真定饑。 丙寅，冬至，帝不豫，改卜郊。 閏月乙亥，錄故功臣子孫未嗣者二百九人。 壬午，有事於圜丘。 庚寅，頒定《大明律》。 是年，暹羅、高麗、占城、真臘、三佛齊入貢。 命安南陳叔明權知國事。
In spring of the sixth year, on the jiayin day of the first month, Wang Guangyang was demoted to administrator of Guangdong. In the second month, on the yiwei day, an edict suspended the civil examinations for the time being and called for the recommendation of worthy men. On the renyin day, censors and provincial surveillance commissioners were charged with inspecting local magistrates. On the first day of the third month, guimao, the sun was eclipsed. The Bright Mirror Record was issued as instruction to the imperial princes. On the wushen day, the emperor held a grand review of the troops. On the renzi day, Xu Da was appointed grand campaigning general against the barbarians, with Li Wenzhong, Feng Sheng, Deng Yu, and Tang He as his lieutenants, to secure the frontiers of Shanxi and Beiping. On the jiazi day, Commander Yu Xian was appointed supreme commander to defend against Japanese raiders. In the fourth month of summer, on the jichou day, local authorities were ordered to submit maps of difficult and easy terrain. In the sixth month, on the renwu day, Xuyi presented auspicious wheat, which was offered at the imperial ancestral temple. On the renchen day, Köke Temür sent troops against Yanmen Pass; Commander Wu Jun drove them back. That month, land tax was remitted on fields stricken by disaster in Beiping, Hejian, Henan, Kaifeng, Yan'an, and Fenzhou. In autumn of the seventh month, on the renyin day, the Ministry of Revenue was ordered to tally flood and drought losses in each province since the Yangzi crossing and extend preferential relief. On the renzi day, Hu Weiyong was named right grand councillor. In the eighth month, on the yihai day, an edict ordered sacrifice to the Three Sovereigns and to successive dynastic founders. In the tenth month of winter, on the xinsi day, Xu Da and Feng Sheng were recalled to court. In the eleventh month, on the renzi day, Köke Temür invaded Datong; Xu Da sent generals who defeated him, and Xu himself remained on garrison duty. On the jiazi day, Minister of War Liu Ren was sent to relieve famine in Zhending. On the bingyin day, the winter solstice, the emperor fell ill and postponed the suburban sacrifice. In the intercalary month, on the yihai day, two hundred nine descendants of meritorious officials who had not yet received hereditary honors were entered on the rolls. On the renwu day, the emperor sacrificed to Heaven at the Circular Mound Altar. On the gengyin day, the Great Ming Code was issued in its definitive form. That year, embassies arrived from Siam, Korea, Champa, Cambodia, and Srivijaya. Chen Shuming of Annam was authorized to govern the realm on a provisional basis.
7
七年春正月甲戌，都督僉事王簡、王誠、平章李伯升屯田河南、山東、北平。 靖海侯吳禎為總兵官，都督於顯副之，巡海捕倭。 二月丁酉朔，日有食之。 戊午，修曲阜孔子廟，設孔、顏、孟三氏學。 是月，平陽、太原、汾州、曆城、汲縣旱蝗，並免租稅。 夏四月己亥，都督藍玉敗元兵於白酒泉，遂拔興和。 壬寅，金吾指揮陸齡討永、道諸州蠻，平之。 五月丙子，免真定等四十二禕府州縣被災田租。 辛巳，振蘇州饑民三十萬戶。 癸巳，減蘇、松、嘉、湖極重田租之半。 六月，陝西平涼、延安、靖寧、鄜州雨雹，山西、山東、北平、河南蝗，並蠲田租。 秋七月甲子，李文忠破元兵於大寧、高州。 壬申，倭寇登、萊。 八月甲午朔，祀歷代帝王廟。 辛丑，詔軍士陣歿父母妻子不能自存者，官為存養。 百姓避兵離散或客死，遺老幼，並資遣還。 遠宦卒官，妻子不能歸者，有司給舟車資送。 庚申，振河間、廣平、順德、真定饑，蠲租稅。 九月丁丑，遣崇禮侯買的裏八剌歸，遺元嗣君書。 冬十一月壬戌，納哈出犯遼陽，千戶吳壽擊走之。 辛未，有事於圜丘。 十二月戊戌，召鄧愈、湯和還。 是年，阿難功德國、暹羅、琉球、三佛齊、烏斯藏、撒裏、畏兀兒入貢。
In spring of the seventh year, on the jiaxu day of the first month, Assistant Chief Commanders Wang Jian and Wang Cheng and Vice Minister Li Bosheng were stationed to farm garrison lands in Henan, Shandong, and Beiping. Wu Zhen, Marquis of Pacifying the Sea, was appointed supreme commander, with Chief Commander Yu Xian as his deputy, to patrol the coast and hunt Japanese pirates. On the new moon of the dingyou day in the second month, the sun was eclipsed. On the wuwu day, the Confucius Temple in Qufu was restored, and schools were established for the Kong, Yan, and Meng clans. That month, drought and locusts struck Pingyang, Taiyuan, Fenzhou, Licheng, and Jixian; land tax was remitted throughout the affected region. In the fourth month of summer, on the jihai day, Chief Commander Lan Yu routed Yuan forces at Baijiu Spring and then captured Xinghe. On the renyin day, Golden Guard Commander Lu Ling campaigned against the tribal peoples of Yong and Dao prefectures and brought them to submission. In the fifth month, on the bingzi day, land tax was remitted on disaster-stricken fields in forty-two prefectures and counties, including Zhending. On the xinsi day, relief was extended to three hundred thousand famine-stricken households in Suzhou. On the guisi day, the crushing land tax in Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou was cut by half. In the sixth month, hail struck Pingliang, Yan'an, Jingning, and Fuzhou in Shaanxi, while locusts ravaged Shanxi, Shandong, Beiping, and Henan; land tax was remitted throughout. In autumn of the seventh month, on the jiazi day, Li Wenzhong defeated Yuan forces at Daining and Gaozhou. On the renshen day, Japanese pirates raided Dengzhou and Laizhou. On the new moon of the jiawu day in the eighth month, sacrifice was offered at the Temple of Successive Emperors. On the xinchou day, an edict declared that when soldiers died in battle and their families could not support themselves, the state would provide for them. Civilians displaced by war, or those who died far from home leaving elderly kin and children behind, were all given travel funds and escorted home. When officials died in distant posts and their families could not afford the journey home, local authorities were to provide boats, carts, and travel expenses. On the gengshen day, famine relief was extended to Hejian, Guangping, Shunde, and Zhending, and rent and tax were remitted. In the ninth month, on the dingchou day, the Honored Marquis Maidiliba'er was sent home to the north, bearing a letter for the Yuan heir. In the eleventh month of winter, on the renxu day, Naghachu invaded Liaoyang; Battalion Commander Wu Shou repelled him. On the xinwei day, the emperor sacrificed to Heaven at the Circular Mound Altar. In the twelfth month, on the wuxu day, Deng Yu and Tang He were recalled to court. That year, embassies arrived from A-nan Gongde, Siam, Ryukyu, Srivijaya, U-Tsang, Sarir, and the Uighur lands.
8
八年春正月辛未，增祀雞籠山功臣廟一百八人。 癸酉，命有司察窮民無告者，給屋舍衣食。 辛巳，鄧愈、湯和等十三人屯戍北平、陝西、河南。 丁亥，詔天下立社學。 是月，河決開封，發民夫塞之。 二月甲午，宥雜犯死罪以下及官犯私罪者，謫鳳陽輸作屯種贖罪。 癸丑，耕耤田。 召徐達、李文忠、馮勝還，傅友德等留鎮北平。 三月辛酉，立鈔法。 辛巳，罷寶源局鑄錢。
In spring of the eighth year, on the xinwei day of the first month, one hundred eight more names were added to the Meritorious Officials Temple on Jilong Mountain. On the guiyou day, local authorities were ordered to find the destitute who had no one to speak for them and provide housing, food, and clothing. On the xinsi day, Deng Yu, Tang He, and twelve other commanders took up garrison duty in Beiping, Shaanxi, and Henan. On the dinghai day, an edict ordered community schools established throughout the empire. That month, the Yellow River broke through at Kaifeng; corvée laborers were mobilized to stem the flood. In the second month, on the jiawu day, those convicted of lesser capital crimes and officials guilty of private offenses were pardoned and sent to Fengyang to labor and farm in settlement of their guilt. On the guichou day, the emperor plowed the sacred field. Xu Da, Li Wenzhong, and Feng Sheng were recalled to court; Fu Youde and others remained to guard Beiping. In the third month, on the xinyou day, the paper currency system was established by law. On the xinsi day, the Baoyuan Bureau ceased minting copper coin.
9
夏四月辛卯，幸中都。 丁巳，至自中都。 免彰德、大名、臨洮、平涼、河州被災田租。 罷營中都。 致仕誠意怕劉基卒。 五月己巳，永嘉候硃亮祖偕傅友德鎮北平。 六月壬寅，指揮同知胡汝平貴州蠻。
In the fourth month of summer, on the xinmao day, the emperor traveled to the Central Capital. On the dingsi day, he returned from the Central Capital. Land tax was remitted on disaster-stricken fields in Zhangde, Daming, Lintao, Pingliang, and Hezhou. Work on the Central Capital was suspended. Liu Ji, Lord of Sincerity, who had retired from office, died. In the fifth month, on the jisi day, Zhu Liangzu, Marquis of Yongjia, joined Fu Youde in guarding Beiping. In the sixth month, on the renyin day, Assistant Commander Hu Ruping pacified the tribal peoples of Guizhou.
10
秋七月己未朔，日有食之。 辛酉，改作太廟。 壬戌，召傅友德、硃亮祖還，李文忠、顧時鎮山西、北平。 戊辰，詔百官奔父母喪不俟報。 京師地震。 丁丑，免應天、太平、甯國鎮江及蘄、黃諸府被災田租。 八月己酉，元擴廓帖木兒卒。
In autumn of the seventh month, on the new moon of the jiwei day, the sun was eclipsed. On the xinyou day, the Grand Ancestral Temple was reconstructed. On the renxu day, Fu Youde and Zhu Liangzu were recalled; Li Wenzhong and Gu Shi took up garrison duty in Shanxi and Beiping. On the wuchen day, an edict declared that officials need not await imperial leave to mourn a parent. An earthquake struck the capital. On the dingchou day, land tax was remitted on disaster-stricken fields in Yingtian, Taiping, Ningguo, Zhenjiang, and the Qi and Huang prefectures. In the eighth month, on the jiyou day, Köke Temür of the Yuan died.
11
冬十月丁亥，詔舉富民素行端潔達時務者。 壬子，命皇太子諸王講武中都。 十一月丁丑，有事於圓丘。 十二月戊子，京師地震。 甲寅，遣使振蘇州、湖州、嘉興、松江、常州、太平、甯國、杭州水災。 是月，納哈出犯遼東，指揮馬雲、葉旺大敗之。
In the tenth month of winter, on the dinghai day, an edict called for the recommendation of prosperous men of upright character who understood public affairs. On the renzi day, the crown prince and the imperial princes were ordered to conduct military exercises at the Central Capital. In the eleventh month, on the dingchou day, the emperor sacrificed to Heaven at the Circular Mound Altar. In the twelfth month, on the wuzi day, another earthquake struck the capital. On the jiayin day, commissioners were sent to relieve flooding in Suzhou, Huzhou, Jiaxing, Songjiang, Changzhou, Taiping, Ningguo, and Hangzhou. That month, Naghachu invaded Liaodong; Commanders Ma Yun and Ye Wang routed his forces.
12
是年，撒裏、高麗、占城、暹羅、日本、爪哇、三佛齊入貢。
That year, embassies arrived from Sarir, Korea, Champa, Siam, Japan, Java, and Srivijaya.
13
九年春正月，中山侯湯和，潁川侯傅友德，都督僉事藍玉、王弼，中書右丞丁玉，備邊延安。 三月己卯，詔曰：「比年西征燉煌，北伐沙漠，軍需甲仗，皆資山、陝，又以秦、晉二府宮殿之役，重困吾民。 平定以來，閭閻未息。 國都始建，土木屢興。 畿輔既極煩勞，外郡疲於轉運。 今蓄儲有餘，其淮、揚、安、徽、池五府及山西、陝西、河南、福建、江西、浙江、北平、湖廣今年租賦，悉免之。」
In spring of the ninth year, in the first month, Tang He, Marquis of Zhongshan, Fu Youde, Marquis of Yingchuan, Assistant Chief Commanders Lan Yu and Wang Bi, and Vice Minister Ding Yu took up frontier defense at Yan'an. In the third month, on the jimao day, an edict declared: "Recent western campaigns to Dunhuang and northern expeditions into the desert have drained Shanxi and Shaanxi for arms and provisions; the palace works of the Qin and Jin princedoms have weighed still more heavily on the people." "Since the realm was pacified, the common people have known no respite." "With the founding of the capital, building projects have arisen again and again." "The heartland has been driven to exhaustion, and outer provinces worn down by transport duties." "Now the granaries are full. This year's rent and tax are remitted for the five prefectures of Huai, Yang, An, Hui, and Chizhou, and for Shanxi, Shaanxi, Henan, Fujian, Jiangxi, Zhejiang, Beiping, and Huguang."
14
夏四月庚戌，京師自去年八月不雨，是日始雨。 五月癸酉，自庚戌雨，至是日始霽。 六月甲午，改行中書省為承宣佈政使司。 辛丑，李文忠還。
In the fourth month of summer, on the gengxu day, rain fell at last on the capital, which had gone without rain since the eighth month of the previous year. In the fifth month, on the guiyou day, the skies cleared after continuous rain since the gengxu day. In the sixth month, on the jiawu day, the Branch Secretariat was renamed the Provincial Administration Commission. On the xinchou day, Li Wenzhong returned to court.
15
秋七月癸丑朔，日有食之。 是月，蠲蘇、松、嘉、湖水災田租，振永平旱災。 元將伯顏帖木兒犯延安，傅友德敗降之。 八月己酉，遣官省歷代帝王陵寢，禁芻牧，置守陵戶。 忠臣烈士祠，有司以時葺治。 分遣國子生修嶽鎮海瀆祠。 西番朵兒只巴寇罕東，河州指揮甯正擊走之。 閏九月庚寅，以災異詔求直言。
In autumn of the seventh month, on the new moon of the guichou day, the sun was eclipsed. That month, land tax was remitted on flood-stricken fields in Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou, and drought relief was extended to Yongping. The Yuan general Bayan Temür invaded Yan'an; Fu Youde defeated him and accepted his surrender. In the eighth month, on the jiyou day, commissioners were sent to inspect the tombs of successive emperors; grazing was forbidden and guardian households were assigned. Shrines to loyal ministers and martyrs were to be kept in repair on schedule by local authorities. Students of the National University were sent out in separate parties to restore the shrines of the sacred mountains, guardian peaks, and great rivers and seas. The Tibetan leader Dorji Ba raided Handong; Commander Ning Zheng of Hezhou repelled him. In the intercalary ninth month, on the gengyin day, an edict called for candid memorials in response to omens and disasters.
16
冬十月己未，太廟成，自是行合享禮。 丙子，命秦、晉、燕、吳、楚、齊諸王治兵鳳陽。 十一月壬午，有事於圓丘。 戊子，徙山西及真定民無產者田鳳陽。 十二月甲寅，振畿內、浙江、湖北水災。 己卯，遣都督同知沐英乘傳詣陝西問民疾苦。
In the tenth month of winter, on the jiwei day, the Grand Ancestral Temple was completed, and joint sacrifices were thereafter performed. On the bingzi day, the princes of Qin, Jin, Yan, Wu, Chu, and Qi were ordered to conduct military exercises at Fengyang. In the eleventh month, on the renwu day, the emperor sacrificed to Heaven at the Circular Mound Altar. On the wuzi day, landless families from Shanxi and Zhending were resettled on farmland at Fengyang. In the twelfth month, on the jiayin day, flood relief was extended to the metropolitan region, Zhejiang, and Hubei. On the jimao day, Assistant Chief Commander Mu Ying was dispatched by relay post to Shaanxi to inquire into the people's hardships.
17
是年，覽邦、琉球、安南、日本、烏斯藏、高麗入貢。
That year, embassies arrived from Lambri, Ryukyu, Annam, Japan, U-Tsang, and Korea.
18
十年春正月辛卯，以羽林等衛軍益秦、晉、燕三府護衛。 是春，振蘇、松、嘉、湖水災。
In spring of the tenth year, on the xinmao day of the first month, guardsmen of the Imperial Guard and other units were assigned to reinforce the protective establishments of the Qin, Jin, and Yan princedoms. That spring, flood relief was extended to Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou.
19
夏四月己酉，鄧愈為征西將軍，沐英為副將軍，率師討吐番，大破之。 是月，振太平、甯國及宜興、錢塘諸縣水災。 五月庚子，韓國公李善長、曹國公李文忠總中書省、大都督府、御史台，議軍國重事。 癸卯，振湖廣水災。 丙午，戶部主事趙乾振荊、蘄遲緩，伏誅。 六月丁巳，詔臣民言事者，實封達御前。 丙寅，命政事啟皇太子裁決奏聞。
In the fourth month of summer, on the jiyou day, Deng Yu was appointed Western Expedition General with Mu Ying as his deputy; they led an army against the Tibetans and won a crushing victory. That month, flood relief was extended to Taiping, Ningguo, and the counties of Yixing and Qiantang. In the fifth month, on the gengzi day, Li Shanchang, Duke of Han, and Li Wenzhong, Duke of Cao, were placed jointly in charge of the Secretariat, the Chief Military Commission, and the Censorate to deliberate matters of military and state import. On the guimao day, flood relief was ordered for Huguang. On the bingwu day, Zhao Gan, a principal clerk in the Ministry of Revenue, was put to death for delaying flood relief in Jing and Qi. In the sixth month on the dingsi day, an edict required that petitions from officials and commoners be sent in sealed envelopes directly to the emperor. On the bingyin day, the emperor directed that state affairs be presented to the crown prince for decision before being reported to him.
20
秋七月甲申，置通政司。 是月，始遣御史巡按州縣。 八月庚戌，改建大祀殿於南郊。 癸丑，選武臣子弟讀書國子監。 九月丙申，振紹興、金華、衢州水災。 辛丑，胡惟庸為左丞相，汪廣洋為右丞相。
In autumn of the seventh month, on the jiashen day, the Office of Transmission was established. That same month, censorial officials were first sent on inspection tours of the prefectures and counties. In the eighth month on the gengxu day, the Hall of Grand Sacrifice was rebuilt at the southern suburb. On the guichou day, sons of military officers were enrolled to study at the Imperial Academy. In the ninth month on the bingshen day, flood relief was ordered for Shaoxing, Jinhua, and Quzhou. On the xinchou day, Hu Weiyong was appointed left grand councillor and Wang Guangyang right grand councillor.
21
冬十月戊午，封沐英四平侯。 辛酉，賜百官公田。 十一月癸未，衛國公鄧愈卒。 丁亥，合祀天地於奉天殿。 是月，免河南、陝西、廣東、湖廣田租。 威茂蠻叛，御史大夫丁玉為平羌將軍，討平之。 十二月乙巳朔，日有食之。 丁未，錄故功臣子孫百百餘人，授官有差。
In winter of the tenth month, on the wuwu day, Mu Ying was created Marquis of Siping. On the xinyou day, the court granted stipend fields to civil officials. In the eleventh month on the guiwei day, Deng Yu, Duke of Wei, died. On the dinghai day, Heaven and Earth were worshipped together in the Hall of Receiving Heaven. That month, land tax was remitted in Henan, Shaanxi, Guangdong, and Huguang. The Weimao tribes rebelled; Grand Censor Ding Yu was appointed Pacification General of the Qiang and quelled the uprising. On the new moon of the twelfth month, the yisi day, there was a solar eclipse. On the dingwei day, over a hundred descendants of deceased meritorious officials were registered and appointed to posts according to merit.
22
是年，占城、三佛齊、暹羅、爪哇、真臘入貢。 高麗使五至，以嗣王未立，卻之。
That year, tribute missions arrived from Champa, Srivijaya, Siam, Java, and Cambodia. Korean envoys arrived five times, but each embassy was refused because the heir to the throne had not yet been enthroned.
23
十一年春正月甲戌，封皇子椿為蜀王，柏湘王，桂豫王，模漢王，植衛王。 改封吳王橚為周王。 己卯，進封湯和信國公。 是月，徵天下布政使及知府來朝。 二月，指揮胡淵平茂州蠻。 三月壬午，命奏事毋關白中書省。 是月，第來朝官為三等。
In spring of the eleventh year, on the jiaxu day of the first month, the emperor enfeoffed his sons Zhu Chun as Prince of Shu, Zhu Bai as Prince of Xiang, Zhu Gui as Prince of Yu, Zhu Mo as Prince of Han, and Zhu Zhi as Prince of Wei. Zhu Shuang, Prince of Wu, was transferred to the Zhou princedom. On the jimao day, Tang He was promoted to Duke of Xin. That month, provincial intendants and prefectural magistrates from across the empire were summoned to the capital. In the second month, Commander Hu Yuan pacified the Maozhou tribes. In the third month on the renwu day, memorials were ordered to bypass the Central Secretariat. That month, officials attending court were ranked in three tiers.
24
夏四月，元嗣君愛猷識理達臘殂，子脫古思帖木兒嗣。 五月丁酉，存問蘇、松、嘉、湖被水災民，戶賜米一石，蠲逋賦六十五萬有奇。 六月壬子，遣使祭故元嗣君。 己巳，五開蠻叛，殺靖州指揮過興，以辰州指揮楊仲名為總兵官，討之。
In summer of the fourth month, the Yuan heir Ayushiridara died and was succeeded by his son Toghus Temür. In the fifth month on the dingyou day, the court inquired after flood victims in Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou, granting each household one picul of grain and remitting over 650,000 in arrears. In the sixth month on the renzi day, envoys were sent to perform rites for the deceased Yuan heir. On the jisi day, the Wukai tribes rebelled and killed Guo Xing, commander of Jingzhou; Yang Zhongming, commander of Chenzhou, was appointed supreme commander to suppress them.
25
秋七月丁丑，振平陽饑。 是月，蘇、松、揚、臺海溢，遣官存恤。 八月，免應天、太平、鎮江、甯國、廣德諸府州秋糧。 九月丙申，追封劉繼祖為義惠侯。
In autumn of the seventh month, on the dingchou day, famine relief was ordered for Pingyang. That month, coastal flooding struck Suzhou, Songjiang, Yangzhou, and Taizhou, and officials were sent to provide relief. In the eighth month, autumn grain tax was remitted in Yingtian, Taiping, Zhenjiang, Ningguo, and Guangde. In the ninth month on the bingshen day, Liu Jizu was posthumously enfeoffed as Marquis of Yihui.
26
冬十月甲子，大祀殿成。 十一月庚午，征西將軍西平侯沐英率都督藍玉、玉弼討西番。 是月，五開蠻平。
In winter of the tenth month, on the jiazi day, the Hall of Grand Sacrifice was completed. In the eleventh month on the gengwu day, Western Expedition General Mu Ying, Marquis of Xiping, led the commanders Lan Yu and Yubi in a campaign against the western tribes. That month, the Wukai uprising was suppressed.
27
是年，暹羅、闍婆、高麗、琉球、占城、三佛齊、朵甘、烏斯藏、彭亨、百花入貢。
That year, tribute missions arrived from Siam, Java, Korea, Ryukyu, Champa, Srivijaya, Do-kham, U-Tsang, Pahang, and Baihua.
28
十二年春正月己卯，始合禮天地於南郊。 甲申，洮州十八族番叛，命沐英移兵討之。 丙申，丁玉平松州蠻。 二月戊戌，李文忠督理河、岷、臨、鞏軍事。 乙巳，詔曰：「今春雨雪經旬。 天下貧民困於饑寒者多有，其令有司給以鈔。 」丙寅，信國公湯和率列候練兵臨清。
In spring of the twelfth year, on the jimao day of the first month, Heaven and Earth were first worshipped together at the southern altar according to the new ritual. On the jiashen day, the eighteen tribal groups of Taozhou rebelled, and Mu Ying was ordered to redeploy his forces to suppress them. On the bingshen day, Ding Yu pacified the Songzhou tribes. In the second month on the wuxu day, Li Wenzhong was placed in charge of military affairs along the He, Min, Lin, and Gong frontiers. On the yisi day, an edict proclaimed: "Spring rains and snow have persisted for more than ten days. Many of the empire's poor now suffer hunger and cold. Let the appropriate offices distribute paper currency in relief." On the bingyin day, Tang He, Duke of Xin, led the marquises in military exercises at Linqing.
29
夏五月癸未，蠲北平田租。 六月丁卯，都督馬雲征大寧。 秋七月丙辰，丁玉回師討眉縣賊，平之。 己未，李文忠還掌大都督府事。 八月辛巳，詔凡致仕官復其家，終身無所與。 九月己亥，沐英大破西番，擒其部長三副使。
In summer of the fifth month, on the guiwei day, land tax was remitted in Beiping. In the sixth month on the dingmao day, Director Ma Yun marched against Daining. In autumn of the seventh month, on the bingchen day, Ding Yu wheeled his army about to crush the Meixian rebels and pacified the district. On the jiwei day, Li Wenzhong resumed direction of the Metropolitan Military Commission. In the eighth month on the xinsi day, an edict declared that retired officials' households would be restored and that they would owe the state no further service for life. In the ninth month on the jihai day, Mu Ying routed the western tribes and captured three of their deputy chieftains.
30
冬十一月甲午，沐英班師，封仇成、藍玉等十二人為侯。 庚申，大寧平。 十二月，汪廣洋貶廣南，賜死。 徵天下博學老成之士至京師。
In winter of the eleventh month, on the jiawu day, Mu Ying withdrew from the field; Chou Cheng, Lan Yu, and twelve others were created marquises. On the gengshen day, Daining was pacified. In the twelfth month, Wang Guangyang was banished to Guangnan and compelled to take his own life. Erudite and seasoned scholars from across the empire were summoned to the capital.
31
是年，占城、爪哇、暹羅、日本、安南、高麗入貢。 高麗貢黃金百斤、白金萬兩，以不如約，卻之。
That year, tribute missions arrived from Champa, Java, Siam, Japan, Annam, and Korea. Korea offered a hundred jin of gold and ten thousand liang of silver, but the embassy was turned away for failing to observe the stipulated protocol.
32
十三年春正月戊戌，左丞相胡惟庸謀反，及其黨御史大夫陳甯、中丞塗節等伏誅。 癸卯，大祀天地於南郊。 罷中書省，廢丞相等官，更定六部官秩，改大都督府為中、左、右、前、後五軍都督府。 二月壬戌朔，詔舉聰明正直、孝弟力田、賢良方正、文學術數之士。 發丹符，驗天下金穀之數。 戊辰，文武官年六十以上者聽致仕，給以誥敕。 三月壬辰，減蘇、松、嘉、湖重賦十之二。 壬寅，燕王隸之國北平。 壬子，沐英襲元將脫火赤於亦集乃，擒之，盡降其眾。
In spring of the thirteenth year, on the wuxu day of the first month, left grand councillor Hu Weiyong was executed for treason, along with his confederates Grand Censor Chen Ning, Vice Censor Tu Jie, and others. On the guimao day, the emperor offered the great joint sacrifice to Heaven and Earth at the southern altar. The Central Secretariat was abolished and the chancellorship eliminated; the Six Ministries were reorganized, and the Metropolitan Military Commission was divided into five army commissions—Center, Left, Right, Forward, and Rear. On the renxu new moon of the second month, an edict called for recommendations of the intelligent and upright, the filial and industrious farmers, the worthy and upright, and men accomplished in letters and the occult arts. Red tally tokens were dispatched to audit grain stores and treasury holdings throughout the realm. On the wuchen day, civil and military officials aged sixty or above were permitted to retire with patents of honor. In the third month on the renchen day, the crushing tax burden of Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou was cut by twenty percent. On the renyin day, Zhu Di, Prince of Yan, departed for his princedom at Beiping. On the renzi day, Mu Ying surprised the Yuan general Tohochu at Yijinai, took him captive, and received the surrender of his entire force.
33
夏四月己丑，命群臣各舉所知。 五月甲午，雷震謹身殿。 乙未，大赦。 丙申，釋在京及臨濠屯田輸作者。 己亥，免天下田租。 吏以過誤罷者還其職。 壬寅，都督濮英進兵赤斤站，獲故元豳王亦憐真及其部曲而還。 是月，罷御史台。 命從征士卒老疾者許以子代，老而無子及寡婦，有司資遣還。 六月丙寅，雷震奉天門，避正殿省愆。 丁卯，罷王府工役。 丁丑，置諫院官。
In summer of the fourth month, on the jichou day, every minister was ordered to recommend men of talent known to him. In the fifth month on the jiawu day, lightning struck the Hall of Self-Cultivation. On the yiwei day, a general amnesty was proclaimed. On the bingshen day, transport corvée laborers in the capital and at the Linhuang garrisons were released from service. On the jihai day, land tax was remitted empire-wide. Officials removed for trifling errors were restored to office. On the renyin day, Director Pu Ying marched to Chijin Station, seized the former Yuan Prince of Bin Yilianzhen together with his followers, and withdrew. That month, the Censorate was abolished. Veterans of the campaigns who were aged or infirm might send sons in their stead; those without sons, and army widows, were to be given travel funds by local authorities and sent home. In the sixth month on the bingyin day, lightning struck the Gate of Receiving Heaven; the emperor withdrew from the main hall to examine his own conduct. On the dingmao day, corvée labor for princely establishments was abolished. On the dingchou day, posts in the Remonstrance Bureau were established.
34
秋八月，命天下學校師生，日給廩膳。 九月辛卯，景川侯曹震、營陽侯楊璟、永城侯恭顯屯田北平。 乙巳，天壽節，始受群臣朝賀，賜宴於謹身殿，後以為常。 丙午，置四輔官，告於太廟。 以儒士王本、估佑、襲斅、杜斅、趙民望、吳源為春、夏官。 是月，詔陝西衛軍以三分之二屯田。 安置翰林學士承旨宋濂於茂州，道卒。
In autumn of the eighth month, teachers and students in schools across the empire were granted daily grain rations. In the ninth month on the xinmao day, Marquis Cao Zhen of Jingchuan, Marquis Yang Jing of Yingyang, and Marquis Gong Xian of Yongcheng were stationed in Beiping to farm garrison lands. On the yisi day, the emperor's birthday was celebrated for the first time with court congratulations and a feast in the Hall of Self-Cultivation—a practice thereafter observed annually. On the bingwu day, the Four Assistants were installed and their appointment announced at the Imperial Ancestral Temple. The scholar-officials Wang Ben, Gu You, Xi Xiao, Du Jiao, Zhao Minwang, and Wu Yuan were appointed to the Spring and Summer posts among the Four Assistants. That month, an edict directed that two-thirds of the Shaanxi garrison troops be assigned to military colonies. Hanlin academician Song Lian was sent into exile at Maozhou and died on the journey.
35
冬十一月乙未，徐達還。 丙午，元平章完者不花、乃兒不花犯永平，指揮劉廣戰沒，千戶王輅擊敗之，擒完者不花。 十二月，天下府州縣所舉士至者八百六十餘人，授官有差。 南雄侯趙庸鎮廣東，討陽春蠻。
In winter of the eleventh month, on the yiwei day, Xu Da returned to court. On the bingwu day, the Yuan ministers Wan Zhebughua and Nayir Buqa raided Yongping; Commander Liu Guang fell in battle, but Chiliarch Wang He routed the invaders and captured Wan Zhebughua. In the twelfth month, more than eight hundred and sixty locally recommended scholars reached the capital and were appointed to posts according to merit. Zhao Yong, Marquis of Nanxiong, was stationed in Guangdong to suppress the Yangchun tribes.
36
是年，琉球、日本、安南、占城、真臘、爪哇入貢，日本以無表卻之。
That year, tribute arrived from Ryukyu, Japan, Annam, Champa, Cambodia, and Java, though the Japanese embassy was turned away for presenting no formal memorial.
37
十四年春正月戊子，徐達為征虜大將軍，湯和、傅友德為左、右副將軍，帥師討乃兒不花。 命新授官者各舉所知。 乙未，大祀天地於南郊。 壬子，罷天下歲造兵器。 癸丑，命公候子弟入國學。 丙辰，詔求隱逸。 二月庚辰，核天下官田。 三月丙戌，大赦。 辛丑，頒《五經》、《四書》於北方學校。
In spring of the fourteenth year, on the wuzi day of the first month, Xu Da was appointed Grand General Expelling the Barbarians, with Tang He and Fu Youde as his left and right deputies, to lead a punitive expedition against Nayir Buqa. Every newly appointed official was ordered to recommend men of talent known to him. On the yiwei day, the emperor offered the great joint sacrifice to Heaven and Earth at the southern altar. On the renzi day, the annual empire-wide manufacture of weapons was halted. On the guichou day, sons of dukes and marquises were ordered to enroll in the Imperial Academy. On the bingchen day, an edict called for hidden men of talent to come forward. In the second month on the gengchen day, official landholdings throughout the empire were audited. In the third month on the bingxu day, a general amnesty was proclaimed. On the xinchou day, copies of the Five Classics and the Four Books were issued to schools in the north.
38
夏四月庚午，徐達率諸將出塞，至北黃河，擊破元兵，獲全寧四部以歸。 五月，五溪蠻叛，江夏侯周德興討平之。
In the fourth month of summer, on the gengwu day, Xu Da led his commanders out of the frontier to the northern Yellow River, routed the Yuan forces, and brought back the four Quanning tribes in triumph. In the fifth month, the Wuxi tribes rose in revolt; Marquis Zhou Dexing of Jiangxia marched against them and restored order.
39
秋八月丙子，詔求明經老成之士，有司禮送京師。 庚辰，河決原武、祥符、中牟。 辛巳，徐達還。 九月壬午朔，傅友德為征南將軍，藍玉、沐英為左、右副將軍，帥征支南。 徐達鎮北平。 丙午，周德興移師討施州蠻，平之。
In the eighth month of autumn, on the bingzi day, the throne called for seasoned classicists; local authorities were to conduct them to the capital with full ceremonial honors. On the gengchen day, the Yellow River broke through at Yuanwu, Xiangfu, and Zhongmou. On the xinsi day, Xu Da returned from campaign. On the first day of the ninth month, Fu Youde was appointed general of the southern expedition, with Lan Yu and Mu Ying as his left and right deputies, and they took the field against Yunnan. Xu Da was posted to hold Beiping. On the bingwu day, Zhou Dexing redeployed his army against the Shizhou tribes and subdued them.
40
冬十月壬子朔，日有食之。 癸丑，命法司錄囚，會翰林院給事中及春坊官會議平允以聞。 甲寅，免應天、太平、應德、鎮江、甯國田租。 癸亥，分遣御史錄囚。 己卯，延安侯唐勝宗帥師討浙東山寇，平之。 十一月壬午，吉安侯陸仲亨鎮成都。 庚戌，趙庸討廣州海寇，大破之。 十二月丁巳，命翰林春坊官考駁諸司章奏。 戊辰，傅友德大敗元兵於白石江，遂下曲靖。 壬申，元梁王把匝剌瓦爾密走普寧自殺。
In the tenth month of winter, on the first day of the month, a solar eclipse was observed. On the guichou day, the judiciary was commanded to review cases in custody; Hanlin supervising secretaries and Eastern Palace officers met to judge what was equitable and report upward. On the jiayin day, land taxes were waived in Yingtian, Taiping, Yingde, Zhenjiang, and Ningguo. On the guihai day, investigating censors were sent out in separate circuits to review prisoners. On the jimao day, Marquis Tang Shengzong of Yan'an led a force against the mountain rebels of eastern Zhejiang and brought them to heel. In the eleventh month, on the renwu day, Marquis Lu Zhongheng of Ji'an was stationed at Chengdu. On the gengxu day, Zhao Yong struck the pirates off Guangzhou and broke them decisively. In the twelfth month, on the dingsi day, Hanlin and Eastern Palace officers were charged with reviewing and correcting memorials submitted by the ministries. On the wuchen day, Fu Youde routed the Yuan army at the Baishi River and pressed on to capture Qujing. On the renshen day, the Yuan prince of Liang, Bazhala'ermi, fled to Puning and took his own life.
41
是年，暹羅、安南、爪哇、朵甘、烏斯藏入貢。 以安南寇思明，不納。
That year, embassies arrived bearing tribute from Siam, Annam, Java, Duogan, and Ü-Tsang. Tribute from Annam was refused, for its forces had raided Siming.
