洪武元年春正月乙亥，祀天地於南郊，即皇帝位。 定有天下之號曰明，建元洪武。 追尊高祖考曰玄皇帝，廟號德祖，曾祖考曰恆皇帝，廟號懿祖； 祖考曰裕皇帝，廟號熙祖，皇考曰淳皇帝，廟號仁祖，妣皆皇后。 立妃馬氏為皇后，世子標為皇太子。 以李善長、徐達為左、右丞相，諸功臣進爵有差。 丙子，頒即位詔於天下。 追封皇伯考以下皆為王。 辛巳，李善長、徐達等兼東宮官。 甲申，遣使覈浙西田賦。 壬辰，胡廷瑞克建寧。 庚子，鄧愈為征戍將軍，略南陽以北州郡。 湯和克延平，執元平章陳友定，福建平。 是月，天下府州縣官來朝。 諭曰：「天下始定，民財力俱困，要在休養安息，惟廉者能約己而利人，勉之。 」二月壬寅，定郊社宗廟禮，歲必親祀，以為常。 癸卯，湯和提督海運。 廖永忠為征南將軍，硃亮祖副之，由海道取廣東。 丁未，乙太牢祀先師孔子於國學。 戊申，祀社稷。 壬子，詔衣冠如唐制。 癸丑，常遇春克東昌，山東平。 甲寅，楊璟克寶慶。 三月辛未，詔儒臣修女誡，戒後妃毋預政。 壬申，周德興克全州。 丁酉，鄧愈克南陽。 己亥，徐達徇汴梁，左君弼降。 夏四月辛丑，蘄州進竹簟，卻之，命四方毋妄獻。 廖永忠師至廣州，元守臣何真降，廣東平。 丁未，祫享太廟。 戊申，徐達、常遇春大破元兵於洛水北，遂圍河南。 梁王阿魯溫降，河南平。 丁巳，楊璟克永州。 甲子，幸汴梁。 丙寅，馮勝克潼關，李思齊、張思道遁。 五月己卯，廖永忠下梧州，潯、貴、容、郁林諸州皆降。 辛卯，改汴梁路為開封府。 六月庚子，徐達朝行在。 甲辰，海南、海北諸道降。 壬戌，楊璟、硃亮祖克靖江。 秋七月戊子，廖永忠下象州，廣西平。 庚寅，振恤中原貧民。 辛卯，將還應天，諭達等曰：「中原之民，久為群雄所苦，流離相望，故命將北征，拯民水火。 元祖宗功德在人，其子孫罔恤民隱，天厭棄之。 君則有罪，民復何辜。 前代革命之際，肆行屠戮，違天虐民，朕實不忍。 諸將克城，毋肆焚掠妄殺人，元之宗戚，咸俾保全。 庶幾上答天心，下慰人望，以副朕伐罪安民之意。 不恭命者，罰無赦。 」丙申，命馮勝留守開封。 閏月丁未，至自開封。 己酉，徐達會諸將兵於臨清。 壬子，常遇春克德州。 丙寅，克通州，元帝趨上都。 是月，征天下賢才為守令。 免吳江、慶德、太平、甯國、滁、和被災田租。 八月己巳，以應天為南京，開封為北京。 庚午，徐達入元都，封府庫圖籍，守宮門，禁士卒侵暴，遣將巡古北口諸隘。 壬申，以京師火，四方水旱，詔中書省集議便民事。 丁丑，定六部官制。 御史中丞劉基致仕。 己卯，赦殊死以下。 將士從征者恤其家，逋逃許自首。 新克州郡毋妄殺。 輸賦道遠者，官為轉運，災荒以實聞。 免鎮江租稅。 避亂民復業者，聽墾荒地，復三年。 衍聖公襲封及授曲阜知縣，並如前代制。 有司以禮聘致賢士，學校毋事虛文。 平刑，毋非時決囚。 除書籍田器稅，民間逋負免征。 蒙古、色目人有才能者，許擢用。 鰥寡孤獨廢疾者，存恤之。 民年七十以上，一子復。 他利害當興革不在詔內者，有司具以聞。 壬午，幸北京。 改大都路曰北平府。 征元故臣。 癸未，詔徐達、常遇春取山西。 甲午，放元宮人。 九月癸亥，詔曰：「天下之治，天下之賢共理之。 今賢士多隱岩穴，豈有司失於敦勸歟，朝廷疏於禮待歟，抑朕寡昧不足致賢，將在位者壅蔽使不上達歟？ 不然，賢士大夫，幼學壯行，豈甘沒世而已哉。 天下甫定，朕願與諸儒講明治道。 有能輔朕濟民者，有司禮遣。 」乙丑，常遇春下保定，遂下真定。 冬十月庚午，馮勝、湯和下懷慶，澤、潞相繼下。 丁丑，至自北京。 戊寅，以元都平，詔天下。 十一月己亥，遣使分行天下，訪求賢才。 庚子，始祀上帝於圜丘。 癸亥，詔劉基還。 十二月丁卯，徐達克太原，擴廓帖木兒走甘肅，山西平。 己巳，置登聞鼓。 壬辰，以書諭明昇。
On yihai, the first day of the first month of spring in the inaugural year of Hongwu, the emperor sacrificed to Heaven and Earth at the Southern Suburb and took the throne. He fixed the name of the realm as Ming and proclaimed the Hongwu era. He posthumously honored his great-great-grandfather as Emperor Xuan, temple name Dezu, and his great-grandfather as Emperor Heng, temple name Yizu; his grandfather as Emperor Yu, temple name Xizu, and his father as Emperor Chun, temple name Renzu, with all their consorts honored as empresses. He installed Lady Ma as empress and his heir Biao as crown prince. He appointed Li Shanchang and Xu Da left and right chief ministers and raised the founding ministers to peerages in varying degrees. On bingzi he issued his accession edict to the entire empire. He posthumously enfeoffed his father's elder brothers and their descendants as princes. On xinsi Li Shanchang, Xu Da, and others were given concurrent posts in the crown prince's household. On jiashen he sent officials to audit land-tax registers in western Zhejiang. On renchen Hu Tingrui took Jianning. On gengzi Deng Yu was named campaigning garrison general to subdue the prefectures north of Nanyang. Tang He took Yanping, captured the Yuan minister Chen Youding, and pacified Fujian. That month magistrates from across the empire presented themselves at court. He admonished them: "The empire has only just been pacified, and the people's resources are exhausted. Your task is to let them recuperate in peace. Only the upright can discipline themselves and serve the public—see that you do so." " On renyin in the second month he established the rites for the suburban sacrifices, the altars of soil and grain, and the ancestral temples, decreeing that he would perform them in person every year as a standing practice. On guimao Tang He was put in charge of maritime grain transport. Liao Yongzhong was appointed southern campaign general with Zhu Liangzu as his second, to seize Guangdong by sea. On dingwei he offered the great victim to Confucius at the Imperial Academy. On wushen he sacrificed to the altars of soil and grain. On renzi he decreed court dress after the Tang model. On guichou Chang Yuchun took Dongchang and pacified Shandong. On jiayin Yang Jing took Baoqing. In the third month, on xinwei, he ordered scholar-officials to compile Admonitions for Women and forbade imperial consorts to meddle in affairs of state. On renshen Zhou Dexing took Quanzhou. On dingyou Deng Yu took Nanyang. On jihai Xu Da advanced on Bianliang, and Zuo Junbi submitted. In the fourth month of summer, on xinchou, Qizhou offered bamboo mats; he refused them and ordered the provinces to stop sending unsolicited tribute. When Liao Yongzhong's forces reached Guangzhou, the Yuan defender He Zhen surrendered and Guangdong was pacified. On dingwei he performed the collective ancestral rite in the Grand Temple. On wushen Xu Da and Chang Yuchun routed the Yuan army north of the Luo and laid siege to Henan. The Prince of Liang, Aruwen, surrendered, and Henan was pacified. On dingsi Yang Jing took Yongzhou. On jiazi he traveled to Bianliang. On bingyin Feng Sheng took Tong Pass while Li Siqi and Zhang Sidao fled. In the fifth month, on jimao, Liao Yongzhong captured Wuzhou, and the prefectures of Xun, Gui, Rong, and Yulin all submitted. On xinmao he redesignated the Bianliang circuit as Kaifeng prefecture. In the sixth month, on gengzi, Xu Da presented himself at the temporary capital. On jiachen the Hainan and Haibei circuits submitted. On renxu Yang Jing and Zhu Liangzu took Jingjiang. In the seventh month of autumn, on wuzi, Liao Yongzhong captured Xiangzhou and pacified Guangxi. On gengyin he provided relief to the impoverished of the central plains. On xinmao, as he prepared to return to Yingtian, he told Xu Da and the others: "The people of the central plains have long been tormented by rival warlords, displaced and destitute. That is why I sent you north—to lift them out of misery." The Yuan founders' beneficence still lives in men's memory, yet their heirs ignored the people's plight until Heaven itself turned against them. The fault lies with the rulers—what wrong have the common people done? In past dynastic upheavals armies butchered without restraint, defying Heaven and crushing the people—something I cannot bring myself to do. When you take a city, do not burn, loot, or kill indiscriminately, and see that the Yuan imperial clan are all kept safe. In this way you may answer Heaven above and satisfy the people's hopes below, and fulfill my aim of punishing the guilty while bringing peace to the people. Whoever disobeys this order will be punished without mercy. " On bingchen he ordered Feng Sheng to stay behind and garrison Kaifeng. In the intercalary month, on dingwei, he returned from Kaifeng. On jiyou Xu Da gathered the commanders' forces at Linqing. On renzi Chang Yuchun took Dezhou. On bingyin they took Tongzhou, and the Yuan emperor fled toward Shangdu. That month he summoned men of talent from across the empire to serve as prefects and magistrates. He remitted land tax on fields stricken by disaster in Wujiang, Qingde, Taiping, Ningguo, Chu, and He prefectures. In the eighth month, on jisi, he designated Yingtian the Southern Capital and Kaifeng the Northern Capital. On gengwu Xu Da entered the Yuan capital, sealed the treasuries and archives, secured the palace gates, forbade looting by the troops, and sent generals to patrol the passes at Gubeikou and elsewhere. On renshen, in response to fires in the capital and floods and droughts in the provinces, he ordered the Secretariat to deliberate measures to ease the people's burdens. On dingchou he established the bureaucratic structure of the Six Ministries. Liu Ji, vice censor-in-chief, retired from office. On jimao he granted amnesty for all crimes short of capital offenses. Families of soldiers on campaign were granted relief, and fugitives were allowed to turn themselves in. Indiscriminate killing was forbidden in newly conquered prefectures. Where taxpayers lived far from collection points, officials would arrange transport of grain; reports of disaster and famine had to reflect actual conditions. He remitted rent and tax in Zhenjiang. Refugees returning to their livelihoods were allowed to open wasteland with a three-year tax exemption. The Duke of Confucius' line received hereditary enfeoffment and appointment as magistrate of Qufu, following precedent of earlier dynasties. Local officials were to recruit worthy men with proper ceremony, and schools were to eschew empty formalism. Punishments were to be applied fairly, and executions were not to be carried out at improper times. Taxes on books and farm implements were abolished, and private debts in arrears were forgiven. Capable Mongols and Central Asians were permitted to enter government service. Widowers, widows, orphans, the childless, the disabled, and the infirm were to receive care and relief. For commoners aged seventy or more, one son was exempted from corvée and tax. Any other reforms of benefit or harm not covered in the edict were to be reported in full by local officials. On renwu he traveled to the Northern Capital. He redesignated the Dadu circuit as Beiping prefecture. He summoned former Yuan officials to serve. On guiwei he ordered Xu Da and Chang Yuchun to conquer Shanxi. On jiawu he released the women of the Yuan palace. In the ninth month, on guihai, he issued an edict: "The good order of the empire is achieved when the empire's worthies govern it together." Yet many men of talent now hide in seclusion. Is it that local officials have failed to encourage them earnestly, that the court has been remiss in honoring them, that I myself lack the virtue to draw them forth, or that those in power have blocked their path to me? Surely not—for men of learning and integrity, trained in youth and eager to act in their prime, cannot wish merely to die unknown. The empire is newly pacified, and I wish to discuss the principles of good government with scholars. Whoever can aid me in bringing relief to the people, let officials escort him here with due ceremony. " On yichou Chang Yuchun captured Baoding and then Zhending. In the tenth month of winter, on gengwu, Feng Sheng and Tang He took Huaiqing, and Ze and Lu prefectures fell in turn. On dingchou he returned from the Northern Capital. On wuyin, with the Yuan capital pacified, he proclaimed the news throughout the empire. In the eleventh month, on jihai, he sent envoys throughout the empire to seek out men of talent. On gengzi he performed the first sacrifice to the Supreme Lord at the Circular Mound Altar. On guihai he recalled Liu Ji to office. In the twelfth month, on dingmao, Xu Da took Taiyuan; Köke Temür fled to Gansu, and Shanxi was pacified. On jisi he established the Petition Drum for direct appeals to the throne. On renchen he sent a letter of instruction to Ming Sheng.

二年春正月乙巳，立功臣廟於雞籠山。 丁未，享太廟。 庚戌，詔曰：「朕淮右布衣，因天下亂，率眾渡江，保民圖治，今十有五年。 荷天眷祐，悉皆戡定。 用是命將北征，齊魯之民饋糧給軍，不憚千里。 朕軫厥勞，已免元年田租。 遭旱民未蘇，其更賜一年。 頃者大軍平燕都，下晉、冀，民被兵燹，困征斂，北平、燕南、河東、山西今年田租亦與蠲免。 河南諸郡歸附，久欲惠之，西北未平，師過其地，是以未遑。 今晉、冀平矣，西抵潼關，北界大河，南至唐、鄧、光、息，今年稅糧悉除之。 」又詔曰：「應天、太平、鎮江、宣城、廣德供億浩穰。 去歲蠲租，遇旱惠不及下。 其再免諸郡及無為州今年租稅。 」庚申，常遇春取大同。 是月，倭寇山東濱海郡縣。 二月丙寅朔，詔修元史。 壬午，耕耤田。 三月庚子，徐達至奉元，張思道遁。 振陝西饑，戶米三石。 丙午，常遇春至鳳翔，李思齊奔臨洮。 夏四月丙寅，遇春還師北平。 己巳，諸王子受經於博士孔克仁。 令功臣子弟入學。 乙亥，編《祖訓錄》，定封建諸王之制。 徐達下鞏昌。 丙子，賜秦、隴新附州縣稅糧。 丁丑，馮勝至臨洮，李思齊降。 乙酉，徐達襲破元豫王於西寧。 五月甲午朔，日有食之。 丁酉，徐達下平涼、延安。 張良臣以慶陽降，尋叛。 癸卯，始祀地於方丘。 六月己卯，常遇春克開平，元帝北走。 壬午，封陳日煃為安南國王。 秋七月己亥，鄂國公常遇春卒於軍，詔李文忠領其眾。 辛亥，擴廓帖木兒遣將破原州、涇州。 辛酉，馮勝擊走之。 丙辰，明昇遣使來。 八月丙寅，元兵攻大同，李文忠擊敗之。 己巳，定內侍官制。 諭吏部曰：「內臣但備使令，毋多人，古來若輩擅權，可為鑒戒。 馭之之道，當使之畏法，勿令有功，有功則驕恣矣。 」癸酉，《元史》成。 丙子，封王顓為高麗國王。 癸未，徐達克慶陽，斬張良臣，陝西平。 是月，命儒臣纂禮書。 九月辛丑，召徐達、湯和還，馮勝留總軍事。 癸卯，以臨濠為中都。 戊午，征南師還。 冬十月壬戌，遣楊璟諭明昇。 甲戌，甘露降於鐘山，群臣請告廟，不許。 辛卯，詔天下郡縣立學。 是月，遣使貽元帝書。 十一月乙巳，祀上帝於圜丘，以仁祖配。 十二月甲戌，封阿答阿者為占城國王。 甲申，振西安諸府饑，戶米二石。 己丑，大賚平定中原及征南將士。 庚寅，擴廓帖木兒攻蘭州，指揮於光死之。 是年，占城、安南、高麗入貢。
In the second year, on yisi of the first month of spring, he founded a temple to his meritorious ministers on Cock's Cage Mountain. On dingwei he offered sacrifice in the Grand Temple. On gengxu he issued an edict: "I was a commoner of the Huai region. When the empire fell into chaos I led my followers across the Yangzi to protect the people and seek good government—now fifteen years have passed." By Heaven's favor all has been brought to order. Therefore I sent my generals north, and the people of Qi and Lu supplied the army with provisions, traveling a thousand li without complaint. Mindful of their toil, I had already remitted the first year's land tax. Because drought had struck and the people had not yet recovered, I granted them another year's exemption. When my armies recently pacified the Yan capital and subdued Jin and Ji, the people suffered war and crushing levies; land tax for this year in Beiping, southern Yan, Hedong, and Shanxi is likewise remitted. The prefectures of Henan had long since submitted, and I had wished to show them favor, but the northwest was not yet pacified and my armies passed through on campaign, so I had no leisure to do so. Now that Jin and Ji are pacified, from Tong Pass in the west to the Great River in the north and Tang, Deng, Guang, and Xi in the south, all tax grain for this year is abolished. " He issued another edict: "Yingtian, Taiping, Zhenjiang, Xuancheng, and Guangde have borne the heaviest burden of supply. Last year I remitted rent, but drought struck and the relief did not reach the lowest ranks. I therefore remit this year's rent and tax again for those prefectures and for Wuzhou. On gengshen Chang Yuchun took Datong. That month Japanese pirates raided coastal prefectures and counties in Shandong. On the first day of the second month, bingyin, he ordered the compilation of the History of Yuan. On renwu he performed the ceremonial plowing of the sacred field. In the third month, on gengzi, Xu Da reached Fengyuan and Zhang Sidao fled. He provided famine relief in Shaanxi at three shi of grain per household. On bingwu Chang Yuchun reached Fengxiang, and Li Siqi fled to Lintao. In the fourth month of summer, on bingyin, Yuchun brought the army back to Beiping. On jisi the imperial princes began studying the classics under the erudite Kong Keren. He ordered the sons of meritorious officials to enter the schools. On yihai he compiled the Records of Ancestral Instruction and established the system of enfeoffing imperial princes. Xu Da took Gongchang. On bingzi he granted tax grain to newly submitted prefectures and counties in Qin and Long. On dingchou Feng Sheng reached Lintao and Li Siqi surrendered. On yiyou Xu Da raided and defeated the Yuan Prince of Yu at Xining. On the first day of the fifth month, jiawu, there was a solar eclipse. On dingyou Xu Da took Pingliang and Yan'an. Zhang Liangchen surrendered at Qingyang, then soon rebelled. On guimao he performed the first sacrifice to Earth at the Square Mound Altar. In the sixth month, on jimao, Chang Yuchun captured Kaiping and the Yuan emperor fled north. On renwu Chen Rijiong was enfeoffed as king of Annan. In the seventh month of autumn, on jihai, Chang Yuchun, Duke of E, died on campaign; Li Wenzhong was ordered to take command of his forces. On xinhai Köke Temür sent generals who recaptured Yuanzhou and Jingzhou. On xinyou Feng Sheng attacked and drove them off. On bingchen Ming Sheng sent envoys. In the eighth month, on bingyin, Yuan troops attacked Datong and Li Wenzhong defeated them. On jisi he established the eunuch bureaucracy. He instructed the Ministry of Personnel: "Palace eunuchs are only to run errands; do not employ too many. Throughout history such men have seized power—let that be your warning. Control them by making them fear the law; do not let them earn merit, for merit breeds arrogance and license. " On guiyou the History of Yuan was completed. On bingzi Wang Zhuan was enfeoffed as king of Goryeo. On guiwei Xu Da captured Qingyang, executed Zhang Liangchen, and pacified Shaanxi. That month he ordered scholar-officials to compile books of ritual. In the ninth month, on xinchou, Xu Da and Tang He were recalled while Feng Sheng remained in overall command. On guimao he made Linhuang the Central Capital. On wuwu the southern campaign army returned. In the tenth month of winter, on renxu, Yang Jing was sent to instruct Ming Sheng. On jiaxu sweet dew fell on Bell Mountain; the officials asked to report it at the ancestral temple, but he refused. On xinmao he ordered schools established in every prefecture and county in the empire. That month he sent envoys bearing a letter to the Yuan emperor. In the eleventh month, on yisi, he sacrificed to the Supreme Lord at the Circular Mound, with Renzu as associate. In the twelfth month, on jiaxu, A Daazhe was enfeoffed as king of Champa. On jiashen he provided famine relief in the Xi'an prefectures at two shi of grain per household. On jichou he bestowed great rewards on those who had pacified the central plains and on the southern campaign generals. On gengyin Köke Temür attacked Lanzhou, and Commander Yu Guang died defending it. That year Champa, Annan, and Goryeo sent tribute missions.

三年春正月癸巳，徐達為征虜大將軍，李文忠、馮勝、鄧愈、湯和副之，分道北征。 二月癸未，追封郭子興滁陽王。 戊子，詔求賢才可任六部者。 是月，李文忠下興和，進兵察罕腦兒，執元平章竹貞。 三月庚寅，免南畿、河南、山東、北平、浙東、江西廣信、饒州今年田租。 夏四月乙丑，封皇子樉為秦王，棡晉王，棣燕王，橚吳王，楨楚王，榑齊王，梓潭王，杞趙王，檀魯王，從孫守謙靖江王。 徐達大破擴廓帖木兒於沈兒峪，盡降其眾，擴廓走和林。 丙戌，元帝崩於應昌，子愛猷識理達臘嗣。 是月，慈利土官覃垕作亂。 五月己丑，徐達取興元。 分遣鄧愈招諭吐蕃。 丁酉，詔守令舉學識篤行之士。 己亥，設科取士。 甲辰，李文忠克應昌。 元嗣君北走，獲其子買的裏八剌，降五萬餘人，窮追至北慶州，不及而還。 丁未，詔行大射禮。 戊申，祀地於方丘，以仁祖配。 辛亥，徐達下興元。 鄧愈克河州。 丁巳，詔開國時將帥無嗣者祿其家。 是月旱，齋戒，後妃親執爨，皇太子諸王饋於齋所。 六月戊午朔，素服草屨，步禱山川壇，露宿凡三日，還齋於西廡。 辛酉，賚將士，省獄囚，命有司訪求通經術明治道者。 壬戌，大雨。 壬申，李文忠捷奏至，命仕元者勿賀。 諡元主曰順帝。 癸酉，買的裏八剌至京師，群臣請獻俘。 帝曰：「武王伐殷用之乎？ 」省臣以唐太宗嘗行之對。 帝曰：「太宗是待王世充耳。 若遇隋之子孫，恐不爾也。 」遂不許。 又以捷奏多侈辭，謂宰相曰：「元主中國百年，朕與卿等父母皆賴其生養，奈何為此浮薄之言？ 亟改之。 」乙亥，封買的裏八剌為崇禮侯。 丙子，告捷於南郊。 丁丑，告太廟，詔示天下。 辛巳，徙蘇州、松江、嘉興、湖州、杭州民無業者田臨濠，給資糧牛種，復三年。 是月，倭寇山東、浙江、福建濱海州縣。 秋七月丙辰，明昇將吳友仁寇漢中，參政傅友德擊卻之。 中書左丞楊憲有罪誅。 八月乙酉，遣使瘞中原遺骸。 冬十月丙辰，詔儒士更直午門，為武臣講經史。 癸亥，周德興為征南將軍，討覃垕，垕遁。 辛巳，貽元嗣君書。 十一月壬辰，北征師還。 甲午，告武成於郊廟。 丙申，大封功臣。 進李善長韓國公，徐達魏國公，封李文忠曹國公，馮勝宋國公，鄧愈衛國公，常遇春子茂鄭國公，湯和等侯者二十八人。 己亥，設壇親祭戰沒將士。 庚戌，有事於圜丘。 辛亥，詔戶部置戶籍、戶帖，歲計登耗以聞，著為令。 乙卯，封中書右丞汪廣洋忠勤伯，御史中丞劉基誠意伯。 十二月癸亥，復貽元嗣君書，並諭和林諸部。 甲子，建奉先殿。 庚午，遣使祭歷代帝王陵寢，並加修葺。 己卯，賜勳臣田。 壬午，以正月至是月，日中屢有黑子，詔廷臣言得失。 是年，占城、爪哇、西洋入貢。
In the third year, on guisi of the first month of spring, Xu Da was appointed great general for the northern campaign, with Li Wenzhong, Feng Sheng, Deng Yu, and Tang He as his deputies, to advance north by separate routes. In the second month, on guiwei, Guo Zixing was posthumously enfeoffed as Prince of Chuyang. On wuzi he issued an edict seeking men of talent fit for the Six Ministries. That month Li Wenzhong took Xinghe, advanced to Chaghan-nur, and captured the Yuan minister Zhuzhen. In the third month, on gengyin, he remitted this year's land rent in the southern capital region, Henan, Shandong, Beiping, eastern Zhe, Jiangxi Guangxin, and Raozhou. In the fourth month of summer, on yichou, he enfeoffed his sons: Shu as King of Qin, Gang as Jin, Di as Yan, Chu as Wu, Zhen as Chu, Gui as Qi, Zi as Tan, Qi as Zhao, Tan as Lu, and his collateral descendant Shouqian as Prince of Jingjiang. At Shen'er Valley Xu Da routed Köke Temür, accepted the surrender of his entire force, and Köke fled to Karakorum. On bingxu the Yuan emperor died at Yingchang, and his son Ayushiridara succeeded him. That month Tan Hao, a native official of Cili, rebelled. In the fifth month, on jichou, Xu Da took Xingyuan. Deng Yu was separately dispatched to summon and win over the Tibetans. On dingyou he ordered prefects and magistrates to recommend scholars of solid learning and upright conduct. On jihai he established the civil service examination. On jiachen Li Wenzhong captured Yingchang. The Yuan heir fled north; his son Maidiliba was captured and more than fifty thousand men surrendered; the pursuers pressed on to Beiqingzhou but could not overtake him and turned back. On dingwei he ordered the great archery rite performed. On wushen he sacrificed to Earth at the Square Mound, with Renzu as associate. On xinhai Xu Da took Xingyuan. Deng Yu captured Hezhou. On dingsi he ordered stipends for the families of founding generals who left no heirs. That month, during drought, he observed fasting and purification; empresses and consorts cooked in person, and the crown prince and princes brought food to the fasting hall. On the first day of the sixth month, wuwu, dressed plainly in straw sandals he walked to pray at the altars of mountains and rivers, sleeping in the open for three days before returning to fast in the western gallery. On xinyou he rewarded the troops, reviewed prisoners, and ordered officials to seek men versed in the classics and skilled in governance. On renxu heavy rain fell. On renshen Li Wenzhong's victory report arrived, and he ordered former Yuan officials not to offer congratulations. He gave the Yuan ruler the posthumous title Emperor Shun. On guiyou Maidiliba reached the capital, and the officials asked to present him as a captive. The emperor said, "When King Wu attacked the Yin, did he do this?" The Secretariat officials replied that Emperor Taizong of Tang had once done so. The emperor said, "Taizong was dealing with Wang Shichong. Had he met a descendant of the Sui, I doubt he would have done the same. " He thereupon refused. Finding the victory report full of inflated language, he told his chief ministers, "The Yuan ruled China for a century; you and I, and our parents before us, all lived under their nurture—how can you write such frivolous words?" Change it at once. On yihai he enfeoffed Maidiliba as Marquis of Chongli. On bingzi he announced victory at the Southern Suburb. On dingchou he reported to the Grand Temple and issued an edict to the entire empire. On xinsi he relocated unemployed people from Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, Huzhou, and Hangzhou to farm at Linhuang, providing provisions, grain, cattle, and seed, with a three-year tax exemption. That month Japanese pirates raided coastal prefectures and counties in Shandong, Zhejiang, and Fujian. In the seventh month of autumn, on bingchen, Ming Sheng's general Wu Youren attacked Hanzhong, and Administrator Fu Youde repelled him. Yang Xian, left vice director of the Secretariat, was found guilty and executed. In the eighth month, on yiyou, he sent envoys to bury the scattered bones of the central plains. In the tenth month of winter, on bingchen, he ordered Confucian scholars on rotating duty at the Meridian Gate to lecture military officials in the classics and histories. On guihai Zhou Dexing was appointed southern campaign general to suppress Tan Hao, who fled. On xinsi he sent a letter to the Yuan heir. In the eleventh month, on renchen, the northern campaign army returned. On jiawu he announced martial accomplishment at the suburban temples. On bingshen he enfeoffed his meritorious officials on a grand scale. Li Shanchang was advanced to Duke of Han and Xu Da to Duke of Wei; Li Wenzhong was made Duke of Cao, Feng Sheng Duke of Song, Deng Yu Duke of Wei, Chang Yuchun's son Mao Duke of Zheng, and Tang He and twenty-eight others were made marquises. On jihai he set up an altar and personally sacrificed to the generals who had died in battle. On gengxu he performed rites at the Circular Mound. On xinhai he ordered the Ministry of Revenue to establish household registers and placards, with annual reports of surplus and deficit, and made this a standing regulation. On yimao Wang Guangyang, right vice director of the Secretariat, was enfeoffed as Earl of Loyal Diligence, and Liu Ji, vice censor-in-chief, as Earl of Sincere Intent. In the twelfth month, on guihai, he again sent a letter to the Yuan heir and instructed the tribes of Karakorum. On jiazi he built the Hall for Veneration of Ancestors. On gengwu he sent envoys to sacrifice at the tombs of emperors of successive dynasties and ordered repairs to them. On jimao he granted fields to meritorious officials. On renwu, from the first month to this month black spots had repeatedly appeared on the sun at midday, and he ordered court officials to speak frankly of the empire's gains and losses. That year Champa, Java, and the Western Ocean sent tribute missions.

四年春正月丙戌，李善長罷，汪廣洋為右丞相。 丁亥，中山侯湯和為征西將軍，江夏侯周德興、德慶侯廖永忠副之，率舟師由瞿塘，潁川侯傅友德為征虜前將軍，濟甯侯顧時副之，率步騎由秦隴伐蜀。 魏國公徐達練兵北平。 戊子，衛國公鄧愈督餉給征蜀軍。 庚寅，建郊廟於中都。 丁未，詔設科取士，連舉三年，嗣後三年一舉。 戊申，免山西旱災田租。 二月甲戌，幸中都。 壬午，至自中都。 元平章劉益以遼東降。 是月，蠲太平、鎮江、甯國田租。 三月乙酉朔，始策試天下貢士，賜吳伯宗等進士及第、出身有差。 乙巳，徙山後民萬七千戶屯北平。 丁未，誠意伯劉基致仕。 夏四月丙戌，傅友德克階州，文、隆、綿三州相繼下。 五月，免江西、浙江秋糧。 六月壬午，傅友德克漢州。 辛卯，廖永忠克夔州。 戊戌，明昇將丁世貞破文州，守將硃顯忠死之。 癸卯，湯和至重慶，明昇降。 戊申，倭寇膠州。 是月，徙山後民三萬五千戶於內地，又徙沙漠遺民三萬二千戶屯田北平。 秋七月辛亥，徐達練兵山西。 辛酉，傅友德下成都，四川平。 乙丑，明昇至京師，封歸義侯。 八月甲午，免中都、淮、揚及泰、滁、無為田租。 己酉，振陝西饑。 是月，高州海寇亂，通判王名善死之。 九月庚戌朔，日有食之。 冬十月丙申，征蜀師還。 十一月丙辰，有事於圜丘。 庚申，命官吏犯贓者罪勿貸。 是月，免陝西、河南被災田租。 十二月，徐達還。 是年，安南、浡泥、高麗、三佛齊、暹羅、日本、真臘入貢。
In the fourth year, on bingxu of the first month of spring, Li Shanchang was dismissed and Wang Guangyang became right chief minister. On dinghai Tang He, Marquis of Zhongshan, was made western campaign general with Zhou Dexing, Marquis of Jiangxia, and Liao Yongzhong, Marquis of Deqing, as his deputies, to lead the fleet through Qutang; Fu Youde, Marquis of Yingchuan, was made front general for the barbarian campaign with Gu Shi, Marquis of Jining, as deputy, to lead infantry and cavalry through Qin and Long against Shu. Xu Da, Duke of Wei, drilled troops at Beiping. On wuzi Deng Yu, Duke of Wei, supervised provisions for the Shu campaign army. On gengyin he built suburban temples at the Central Capital. On dingwei he established the civil examination, to be held three years in succession and thereafter once every three years. On wushen he remitted land rent in Shanxi stricken by drought. In the second month, on jiaxu, he visited the Central Capital. On renwu he returned from the Central Capital. The Yuan minister Liu Yi surrendered with Liaodong. That month he remitted land rent in Taiping, Zhenjiang, and Ningguo. On the first day of the third month, yiyou, he held the palace examination for tribute scholars from across the empire and granted Wu Bozong and others the jinshi degree and lower ranks in varying degrees. On yisi he relocated seventeen thousand households from beyond the mountains to garrison Beiping. On dingwei Liu Ji, Earl of Sincere Intent, retired from office. In the fourth month of summer, on bingxu, Fu Youde captured Jiezhou, and Wen, Long, and Mian prefectures fell in turn. In the fifth month he remitted autumn grain tax in Jiangxi and Zhejiang. In the sixth month, on renwu, Fu Youde captured Hanzhou. On xinmao Liao Yongzhong captured Kuizhou. On wuxu Ming Sheng's general Ding Shizhen took Wenzhou, and the defending general Zhu Xianzhong died in the defense. On guimao Tang He reached Chongqing and Ming Sheng surrendered. On wushen Japanese pirates raided Jiaozhou. That month he relocated thirty-five thousand households from beyond the mountains to the interior, and another thirty-two thousand desert remnant people to farm at Beiping. In the seventh month of autumn, on xinhai, Xu Da drilled troops in Shanxi. On xinyou Fu Youde took Chengdu and pacified Sichuan. On yichou Ming Sheng reached the capital and was enfeoffed Marquis of Returning to Righteousness. In the eighth month, on jiawu, he remitted land rent in the Central Capital, Huai, Yang, Tai, Chu, and Wuzhou. On jiyou he provided famine relief in Shaanxi. That month sea bandits rebelled in Gaozhou, and Assistant Prefect Wang Mingshan died defending the city. On the first day of the ninth month, gengxu, there was a solar eclipse. In the tenth month of winter, on bingshen, the Shu campaign army returned. In the eleventh month, on bingchen, he performed rites at the Circular Mound. On gengshen he ordered that officials guilty of corruption were not to be pardoned. That month he remitted disaster-stricken land rent in Shaanxi and Henan. In the twelfth month Xu Da returned. That year Annan, Poni, Goryeo, Sanfoqi, Siam, Japan, and Zhenla sent tribute missions.

五年春正月癸丑，待制王禕使雲南，詔諭元梁王把匝剌瓦爾密。 禕至，不屈死。 乙丑，徙陳理、明升於高麗。 甲戌，魏國公徐達為征虜大將軍，出雁門，趨和林，曹國公李文忠為左副將軍，出應昌，宋國公馮勝為征西將軍，取甘肅，征擴廓帖木兒。 靖海侯吳禎督海運，餉遼東。 衛國公鄧愈為征南將軍，江夏侯周德興、江陰侯吳良副之，分道討湖南、廣西洞蠻。 二月丙戌，安南陳叔明弒其主日熞自立，遣使入貢，卻之。 三月丁卯，都督僉事藍玉敗擴廓於土剌河。 夏四月己卯，振濟南、萊州饑。 戊戌，始行鄉飲酒禮。 庚子，鄧愈平散毛諸洞蠻。 五月壬子，徐達及元兵戰於嶺北，敗績。 是月，詔曰：「天下大定，禮儀風俗不可不正。 諸遭亂為人奴隸者復為民。 凍餒者里中富室假貸之，孤寡殘疾者官養之，毋失所。 鄉黨論齒，相見揖拜，毋違禮。 婚姻毋論財。 喪事稱家有無，毋惑陰陽拘忌，停柩暴露。 流民復業者各就丁力耕種，毋以舊田為限。 僧道齋醮雜男女，恣飲食，有司嚴治之。 閩、粵豪家毋閹人子為火者，犯者抵罪。 」六月丙子，定宦官禁令。 丁丑，定宮官女職之制。 戊寅，馮勝克甘肅，追敗元兵於瓜、沙州。 癸巳，定六部職掌及歲終考績法。 壬寅，吳良平靖州蠻。 甲辰，李文忠敗元兵於阿魯渾河，宣甯侯曹良臣戰沒。 乙巳，作鐵榜誡功臣。 是月，振山東饑，免被災郡縣田租。 秋七月丙辰，湯和及元兵戰於斷頭山，敗績。 八月丙申，吳良平五開、古州諸蠻。 甲辰，元兵犯雲內，同知黃里死之。 九月戊午，周德興平婪鳳、安田諸蠻。 冬十月丁酉，馮勝師還。 是月，免應天、太平、鎮江、甯國、廣德田租。 十一月辛酉，有事於圜丘。 甲子，征南師還。 壬申，納哈出犯遼東。 是月，召徐達、李文忠還。 十二月甲戌，詔以農桑學校課有司。 辛巳，命百官奏事啟皇太子。 庚子，鄧愈為征西將軍，征吐番。 壬寅，貽元嗣君書。 是年，瑣里、占城、高麗、琉球、烏斯藏入貢。 高麗貢使再至，諭自後三年一貢。
In the fifth year, on guichou of the first month of spring, Academician Wang Wei was sent to Yunnan with an edict instructing the Yuan Prince of Liang, Bazalawarmi. When Wei arrived, the prince refused to submit and Wei was put to death. On yichou Chen Li and Ming Sheng were relocated to Goryeo. On jiaxu Xu Da, Duke of Wei, was made great general for the northern campaign, to exit Yanmen and advance on Karakorum; Li Wenzhong, Duke of Cao, was made left deputy general to exit Yingchang; Feng Sheng, Duke of Song, was made western campaign general to take Gansu and campaign against Köke Temür. Wu Zhen, Marquis of Pacifying the Sea, supervised sea transport to provision Liaodong. Deng Yu, Duke of Wei, was made southern campaign general with Zhou Dexing, Marquis of Jiangxia, and Wu Liang, Marquis of Jiangyin, as deputies, to campaign by separate routes against the cave barbarians of Hunan and Guangxi. In the second month, on bingxu, Chen Shuming of Annan murdered his lord Rijie and seized the throne, sending envoys with tribute; the emperor refused them. In the third month, on dingmao, Regional Commander Lan Yu defeated Köke at the Tula River. In the fourth month of summer, on jimao, he provided famine relief in Jinan and Laizhou. On wuxu he first performed the village drinking ceremony. On gengzi Deng Yu pacified the Sanmao and various cave barbarians. In the fifth month, on renzi, Xu Da fought Yuan troops north of the mountains and was defeated. That month he issued an edict: "The empire is now largely at peace, and ritual and custom must be set right. Those who in the turmoil became others' slaves shall be restored as free commoners. Those suffering cold and hunger shall be loaned to by wealthy households in their communities; orphans, widows, and the disabled shall be supported by the government so that none lack a home. Villages shall honor age in their dealings, and on meeting shall bow and salute without violating proper ritual. Marriage arrangements shall not be made on the basis of wealth. Funerals shall be conducted according to the family's means, without being bound by yin-yang taboos that leave coffins exposed. Returned refugees shall each farm according to their labor capacity, without being limited to their former holdings. Buddhist and Daoist assemblies that mix men and women and indulge in feasting shall be strictly punished by local officials. Powerful families of Fujian and Guangdong must not castrate sons to serve as fire-keepers; violators shall be punished. " In the sixth month, on bingzi, he fixed prohibitions governing eunuchs. On dingchou he fixed the system of palace women's offices. On wuyin Feng Sheng captured Gansu and pursued and defeated Yuan troops at Guazhou and Shazhou. On guisi he fixed the duties of the Six Ministries and the law for year-end performance review. On renyin Wu Liang pacified the Jingzhou barbarians. On jiachen Li Wenzhong defeated Yuan troops at the Aruhun River; Cao Liangchen, Marquis of Xuanning, died in battle. On yisi he cast an iron tablet admonishing the meritorious officials. That month he provided famine relief in Shandong and remitted land rent in disaster-stricken prefectures and counties. In the seventh month of autumn, on bingchen, Tang He fought Yuan troops at Broken Head Mountain and was defeated. In the eighth month, on bingshen, Wu Liang pacified the Wukai and Guzhou barbarians. On jiachen Yuan troops invaded Yunnei, and Vice Director Huang Li died defending it. In the ninth month, on wuwu, Zhou Dexing pacified the Lanfeng and Antian barbarians. In the tenth month of winter, on dingyou, Feng Sheng's army returned. That month he remitted land rent in Yingtian, Taiping, Zhenjiang, Ningguo, and Guangde. In the eleventh month, on xinyou, he performed rites at the Circular Mound. On jiazi the southern campaign army returned. On renshen Naghachu invaded Liaodong. That month Xu Da and Li Wenzhong were recalled. In the twelfth month, on jiaxu, he ordered officials assessed on agriculture, sericulture, and schools. On xinsi he ordered all officials presenting memorials to inform the crown prince. On gengzi Deng Yu was made western campaign general to campaign against the Tibetans. On renyin he sent a letter to the Yuan heir. That year Suoli, Champa, Goryeo, Ryukyu, and U-Tsang sent tribute missions. When Goryeo tribute envoys came again, he instructed them that henceforth tribute would be sent once every three years.

六年春正月甲寅，謫汪廣洋為廣東參政。 二月乙未，諭暫罷科舉，察舉賢才。 壬寅，命御史及按察使考察有司。 三月癸卯朔，日有食之。 頒《昭鑒錄》，訓誡諸王。 戊申，太閱。 壬子，徐達為征虜大將軍，李文忠、馮勝、鄧愈、湯和副之，備邊山西、北平。 甲子，指揮使於顯為總兵官，備倭。 夏四月己丑，令有司上山川險易圖。 六月壬午，盱眙獻瑞麥，薦宗廟。 壬辰，擴廓帖木兒遣兵攻雁門，指揮吳均擊卻之。 是月，免北平、河間、河南、開封、延安、汾州被災田租。 秋七月壬寅，命戶部稽渡江以來各省水旱災傷分數，優恤之。 壬子，胡惟庸為右丞相，八月乙亥，詔祀三皇及歷代帝王。 冬十月辛巳，召徐達、馮勝還。 十一月壬子，擴廓帖木兒犯大同，徐達遣將擊敗之，達仍留鎮。 甲子，遣兵部尚書劉仁振真定饑。 丙寅，冬至，帝不豫，改卜郊。 閏月乙亥，錄故功臣子孫未嗣者二百九人。 壬午，有事於圜丘。 庚寅，頒定《大明律》。 是年，暹羅、高麗、占城、真臘、三佛齊入貢。 命安南陳叔明權知國事。
In the sixth year, on jiayin of the first month of spring, Wang Guangyang was demoted to administrator of Guangdong. In the second month, on yiwei, he temporarily suspended the civil examination and ordered investigation and recommendation of worthy talent. On renyin he ordered censors and surveillance commissioners to investigate local officials. On the first day of the third month, guimao, there was a solar eclipse. He promulgated the Records for Reflection to admonish the imperial princes. On wushen he held a grand military review. On renzi Xu Da was made great general for the northern campaign, with Li Wenzhong, Feng Sheng, Deng Yu, and Tang He as his deputies, to guard the borders of Shanxi and Beiping. On jiazi Commander Yu Xian was appointed overall military officer to guard against Japanese pirates. In the fourth month of summer, on jichou, he ordered local officials to submit maps of terrain and waterways. In the sixth month, on renwu, Xuyi presented auspicious wheat, which was offered at the ancestral temple. On renchen Köke Temür sent troops to attack Yanmen, and Commander Wu Jun repelled them. That month he remitted disaster-stricken land rent in Beiping, Hejian, Henan, Kaifeng, Yan'an, and Fenzhou. In the seventh month of autumn, on renyin, he ordered the Ministry of Revenue to verify flood and drought damage in each province since the crossing of the Yangzi and provide preferential relief. On renzi Hu Weiyong became right chief minister; in the eighth month, on yihai, he ordered sacrifice to the Three Sovereigns and emperors of successive dynasties. In the tenth month of winter, on xinsi, Xu Da and Feng Sheng were recalled. In the eleventh month, on renzi, Köke Temür invaded Datong; Xu Da sent generals who defeated him, and Xu Da remained to garrison the region. On jiazi he sent Minister of War Liu Ren to relieve famine in Zhending. On bingyin, the winter solstice, the emperor was unwell and the suburban sacrifice was postponed. In the intercalary month, on yihai, he recorded two hundred nine descendants of former meritorious officials who had no heirs. On renwu he performed rites at the Circular Mound. On gengyin he promulgated the fixed Great Ming Code. That year Siam, Goryeo, Champa, Zhenla, and Sanfoqi sent tribute missions. Chen Shuming of Annan was authorized to act as regent of state affairs.
7
七年春正月甲戌，都督僉事王簡、王誠、平章李伯升屯田河南、山東、北平。 靖海侯吳禎為總兵官，都督於顯副之，巡海捕倭。 二月丁酉朔，日有食之。 戊午，修曲阜孔子廟，設孔、顏、孟三氏學。 是月，平陽、太原、汾州、曆城、汲縣旱蝗，並免租稅。 夏四月己亥，都督藍玉敗元兵於白酒泉，遂拔興和。 壬寅，金吾指揮陸齡討永、道諸州蠻，平之。 五月丙子，免真定等四十二禕府州縣被災田租。 辛巳，振蘇州饑民三十萬戶。 癸巳，減蘇、松、嘉、湖極重田租之半。 六月，陝西平涼、延安、靖寧、鄜州雨雹，山西、山東、北平、河南蝗，並蠲田租。 秋七月甲子，李文忠破元兵於大寧、高州。 壬申，倭寇登、萊。 八月甲午朔，祀歷代帝王廟。 辛丑，詔軍士陣歿父母妻子不能自存者，官為存養。 百姓避兵離散或客死，遺老幼，並資遣還。 遠宦卒官，妻子不能歸者，有司給舟車資送。 庚申，振河間、廣平、順德、真定饑，蠲租稅。 九月丁丑，遣崇禮侯買的裏八剌歸，遺元嗣君書。 冬十一月壬戌，納哈出犯遼陽，千戶吳壽擊走之。 辛未，有事於圜丘。 十二月戊戌，召鄧愈、湯和還。 是年，阿難功德國、暹羅、琉球、三佛齊、烏斯藏、撒裏、畏兀兒入貢。
In the seventh year, on jiaxu of the first month of spring, Regional Commanders Wang Jian and Wang Cheng and Pingzhang Li Bosheng organized garrison farming in Henan, Shandong, and Beiping. Wu Zhen, Marquis of Pacifying the Sea, was made overall military officer with Regional Commander Yu Xian as deputy, to patrol the sea and capture Japanese pirates. On the first day of the second month, dingyou, there was a solar eclipse. On wuwu he repaired the Confucius temple at Qufu and established schools for the Kong, Yan, and Meng clans. That month he remitted rent and tax in Pingyang, Taiyuan, Fenzhou, Licheng, and Jixian, which suffered drought and locusts. In the fourth month of summer, on jihai, Regional Commander Lan Yu defeated Yuan troops at White Wine Spring and then took Xinghe. On renyin Golden Guard Commander Lu Ling suppressed the barbarians of Yong and Dao prefectures and pacified them. In the fifth month, on bingzi, he remitted disaster-stricken land rent in forty-two prefectures, departments, and counties including Zhending. On xinsi he provided relief to three hundred thousand famine households in Suzhou. On guisi he reduced by half the extremely heavy land rent of Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou. In the sixth month hail struck Pingliang, Yan'an, Jingning, and Fuzhou in Shaanxi, and locusts struck Shanxi, Shandong, Beiping, and Henan; land rent was remitted in all affected areas. In the seventh month of autumn, on jiazi, Li Wenzhong defeated Yuan troops at Daning and Gaozhou. On renshen Japanese pirates raided Deng and Lai. On the first day of the eighth month, jiawu, he sacrificed at the temple of emperors of successive dynasties. On xinchou he ordered that where soldiers died in battle and their parents, wives, and children could not support themselves, the government was to care for them. Where common people fled war and scattered, or died away from home leaving old and young behind, all were given resources and sent home. Where officials died in distant posts and their wives and children could not return home, local offices were to provide boats, carts, and funds to escort them. On gengshen he provided famine relief in Hejian, Guangping, Shunde, and Zhending and remitted rent and tax. In the ninth month, on dingchou, he sent Marquis of Chongli Maidiliba home with a letter to the Yuan heir. In the eleventh month of winter, on renxu, Naghachu invaded Liaoyang and Commander Wu Shou drove him off. On xinwei he performed rites at the Circular Mound. In the twelfth month, on wuxu, Deng Yu and Tang He were recalled. That year the State of Ananade, Siam, Ryukyu, Sanfoqi, U-Tsang, Sali, and the Uighurs sent tribute missions.
8
八年春正月辛未，增祀雞籠山功臣廟一百八人。 癸酉，命有司察窮民無告者，給屋舍衣食。 辛巳，鄧愈、湯和等十三人屯戍北平、陝西、河南。 丁亥，詔天下立社學。 是月，河決開封，發民夫塞之。 二月甲午，宥雜犯死罪以下及官犯私罪者，謫鳳陽輸作屯種贖罪。 癸丑，耕耤田。 召徐達、李文忠、馮勝還，傅友德等留鎮北平。 三月辛酉，立鈔法。 辛巳，罷寶源局鑄錢。
In the eighth year, on xinwei of the first month of spring, he added one hundred eight names to the meritorious officials' temple on Cock's Cage Mountain. On guiyou he ordered officials to investigate destitute people with no one to speak for them and provide housing, food, and clothing. On xinsi Deng Yu, Tang He, and thirteen others garrisoned Beiping, Shaanxi, and Henan. On dinghai he ordered community schools established throughout the empire. That month the Yellow River burst its banks at Kaifeng and he mobilized laborers to block it. In the second month, on jiawu, he pardoned those guilty of miscellaneous crimes below death and officials guilty of private crimes, relegating them to Fengyang to perform labor and garrison farming to redeem their guilt. On guichou he performed the ceremonial plowing of the sacred field. Xu Da, Li Wenzhong, and Feng Sheng were recalled while Fu Youde and others remained to garrison Beiping. In the third month, on xinyou, he established paper currency regulations. On xinsi he abolished coin minting at the Baoyuan Bureau.
9
夏四月辛卯，幸中都。 丁巳，至自中都。 免彰德、大名、臨洮、平涼、河州被災田租。 罷營中都。 致仕誠意怕劉基卒。 五月己巳，永嘉候硃亮祖偕傅友德鎮北平。 六月壬寅，指揮同知胡汝平貴州蠻。
In the fourth month of summer, on xinmao, he visited the Central Capital. On dingsi he returned from the Central Capital. He remitted disaster-stricken land rent in Zhangde, Daming, Lintao, Pingliang, and Hezhou. He halted construction of the Central Capital. Liu Ji, retired Earl of Sincere Intent, died. In the fifth month, on jisi, Zhu Liangzu, Marquis of Yongjia, together with Fu Youde garrisoned Beiping. In the sixth month, on renyin, Vice Director of Command Hu Ruping pacified the Guizhou barbarians.
10
秋七月己未朔，日有食之。 辛酉，改作太廟。 壬戌，召傅友德、硃亮祖還，李文忠、顧時鎮山西、北平。 戊辰，詔百官奔父母喪不俟報。 京師地震。 丁丑，免應天、太平、甯國鎮江及蘄、黃諸府被災田租。 八月己酉，元擴廓帖木兒卒。
On the first day of the seventh month, jiwei, there was a solar eclipse. On xinyou he rebuilt the Grand Temple. On renxu Fu Youde and Zhu Liangzu were recalled while Li Wenzhong and Gu Shi garrisoned Shanxi and Beiping. On wuchen he ordered all officials to hurry home for parents' funerals without awaiting approval. The capital region was struck by an earthquake. On dingchou he remitted disaster-stricken land rent in Yingtian, Taiping, Ningguo, Zhenjiang, and the prefectures of Qi and Huang. In the eighth month, on jiyou, the Yuan minister Köke Temür died.
11
冬十月丁亥，詔舉富民素行端潔達時務者。 壬子，命皇太子諸王講武中都。 十一月丁丑，有事於圓丘。 十二月戊子，京師地震。 甲寅，遣使振蘇州、湖州、嘉興、松江、常州、太平、甯國、杭州水災。 是月，納哈出犯遼東，指揮馬雲、葉旺大敗之。
In the tenth month of winter, on dinghai, he issued an edict seeking wealthy commoners of upright conduct who understood current affairs. On renzi he ordered the crown prince and princes to practice martial arts at the Central Capital. In the eleventh month, on dingchou, he performed rites at the Circular Mound. In the twelfth month, on wuzi, the capital region was struck by an earthquake. On jiayin he sent envoys to relieve flood disaster in Suzhou, Huzhou, Jiaxing, Songjiang, Changzhou, Taiping, Ningguo, and Hangzhou. That month Naghachu invaded Liaodong and Commanders Ma Yun and Ye Wang routed him.
12
是年，撒裏、高麗、占城、暹羅、日本、爪哇、三佛齊入貢。
That year Sali, Goryeo, Champa, Siam, Japan, Java, and Sanfoqi sent tribute missions.
13
九年春正月，中山侯湯和，潁川侯傅友德，都督僉事藍玉、王弼，中書右丞丁玉，備邊延安。 三月己卯，詔曰：「比年西征燉煌，北伐沙漠，軍需甲仗，皆資山、陝，又以秦、晉二府宮殿之役，重困吾民。 平定以來，閭閻未息。 國都始建，土木屢興。 畿輔既極煩勞，外郡疲於轉運。 今蓄儲有餘，其淮、揚、安、徽、池五府及山西、陝西、河南、福建、江西、浙江、北平、湖廣今年租賦，悉免之。」
In the ninth year, in the first month of spring, Tang He, Marquis of Zhongshan, Fu Youde, Marquis of Yingchuan, Regional Commanders Lan Yu and Wang Bi, and Right Vice Director Ding Yu guarded the border at Yan'an. In the third month, on jimao, he issued an edict: "In recent years the western campaign reached Dunhuang and the northern campaign the desert; military supplies and armor all came from Shanxi and Shaanxi, and the palace construction of the Qin and Jin princely establishments has heavily burdened my people. Since pacification the common people have not rested. The capital was newly established and construction arose repeatedly. The metropolitan region has been taxed to the utmost, and outer commanderies exhausted by transport. Now stores are sufficient; rent and tax for this year are entirely remitted in the five prefectures of Huai, Yang, An, Hui, and Chi and in Shanxi, Shaanxi, Henan, Fujian, Jiangxi, Zhejiang, Beiping, and Huguang."
14
夏四月庚戌，京師自去年八月不雨，是日始雨。 五月癸酉，自庚戌雨，至是日始霽。 六月甲午，改行中書省為承宣佈政使司。 辛丑，李文忠還。
In the fourth month of summer, on gengxu, rain finally fell in the capital, which had had no rain since the eighth month of the previous year. In the fifth month, on guiyou, rain that had fallen since gengxu finally cleared. In the sixth month, on jiawu, he changed the Branch Secretariat to the Provincial Administration Commission. On xinchou Li Wenzhong returned.
15
秋七月癸丑朔，日有食之。 是月，蠲蘇、松、嘉、湖水災田租，振永平旱災。 元將伯顏帖木兒犯延安，傅友德敗降之。 八月己酉，遣官省歷代帝王陵寢，禁芻牧，置守陵戶。 忠臣烈士祠，有司以時葺治。 分遣國子生修嶽鎮海瀆祠。 西番朵兒只巴寇罕東，河州指揮甯正擊走之。 閏九月庚寅，以災異詔求直言。
On the first day of the seventh month, guichou, there was a solar eclipse. That month he remitted flood disaster land rent in Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou and provided drought relief in Yongping. The Yuan general Bayan Temür invaded Yan'an; Fu Youde defeated him and accepted his surrender. In the eighth month, on jiyou, he sent officials to inspect tombs of emperors of successive dynasties, forbade grazing, and established tomb-guard households. Shrines to loyal ministers and martyrs were to be repaired seasonally by local officials. He separately dispatched National University students to repair shrines of peaks, mountains, and rivers. The Western Tibetan Dorbajiba raided Han East; Hezhou Commander Ning Zheng attacked and drove him off. In the intercalary ninth month, on gengyin, in response to calamities and anomalies he issued an edict seeking frank counsel.
16
冬十月己未，太廟成，自是行合享禮。 丙子，命秦、晉、燕、吳、楚、齊諸王治兵鳳陽。 十一月壬午，有事於圓丘。 戊子，徙山西及真定民無產者田鳳陽。 十二月甲寅，振畿內、浙江、湖北水災。 己卯，遣都督同知沐英乘傳詣陝西問民疾苦。
In the tenth month of winter, on jiwei, the Grand Temple was completed, and from this the collective offering rite was performed. On bingzi he ordered the kings of Qin, Jin, Yan, Wu, Chu, and Qi to drill troops at Fengyang. In the eleventh month, on renwu, he performed rites at the Circular Mound. On wuzi he relocated propertyless people from Shanxi and Zhending to farm at Fengyang. In the twelfth month, on jiayin, he provided relief for flood disaster in the capital region, Zhejiang, and Hubei. On jimao he dispatched Vice Regional Commander Mu Ying by rapid relay to Shaanxi to inquire into the people's hardships.
17
是年，覽邦、琉球、安南、日本、烏斯藏、高麗入貢。
That year Lanbang, Ryukyu, Annan, Japan, U-Tsang, and Goryeo sent tribute missions.
18
十年春正月辛卯，以羽林等衛軍益秦、晉、燕三府護衛。 是春，振蘇、松、嘉、湖水災。
In the tenth year, on xinmao of the first month of spring, he increased the guards of the Qin, Jin, and Yan princely establishments with troops from the Feathered Forest and other guards. That spring he provided relief for flood disaster in Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou.
19
夏四月己酉，鄧愈為征西將軍，沐英為副將軍，率師討吐番，大破之。 是月，振太平、甯國及宜興、錢塘諸縣水災。 五月庚子，韓國公李善長、曹國公李文忠總中書省、大都督府、御史台，議軍國重事。 癸卯，振湖廣水災。 丙午，戶部主事趙乾振荊、蘄遲緩，伏誅。 六月丁巳，詔臣民言事者，實封達御前。 丙寅，命政事啟皇太子裁決奏聞。
In the fourth month of summer, on jiyou, Deng Yu was made western campaign general with Mu Ying as deputy, leading an army against the Tibetans and routing them. That month he provided relief for flood disaster in Taiping, Ningguo, and the counties of Yixing and Qiantang. In the fifth month, on gengzi, Li Shanchang, Duke of Han, and Li Wenzhong, Duke of Cao, jointly directed the Secretariat, the Chief Military Commission, and the Censorate to deliberate important state and military affairs. On guimao he provided relief for flood disaster in Huguang. On bingwu Zhao Gan, a director in the Ministry of Revenue, was executed for delaying relief in Jing and Qi. In the sixth month, on dingsi, he ordered that memorials from officials and commoners be sealed and delivered directly to the throne. On bingyin he ordered that government affairs be submitted to the crown prince for decision and then reported.
20
秋七月甲申，置通政司。 是月，始遣御史巡按州縣。 八月庚戌，改建大祀殿於南郊。 癸丑，選武臣子弟讀書國子監。 九月丙申，振紹興、金華、衢州水災。 辛丑，胡惟庸為左丞相，汪廣洋為右丞相。
In the seventh month of autumn, on jiashen, he established the Office of Transmission. That month he first dispatched censors to inspect prefectures and counties. In the eighth month, on gengxu, he rebuilt the Great Sacrifice Hall at the Southern Suburb. On guichou he selected sons of military officials to study at the National University. In the ninth month, on bingshen, he provided relief for flood disaster in Shaoxing, Jinhua, and Quzhou. On xinchou Hu Weiyong became left chief minister and Wang Guangyang right chief minister.
21
冬十月戊午，封沐英四平侯。 辛酉，賜百官公田。 十一月癸未，衛國公鄧愈卒。 丁亥，合祀天地於奉天殿。 是月，免河南、陝西、廣東、湖廣田租。 威茂蠻叛，御史大夫丁玉為平羌將軍，討平之。 十二月乙巳朔，日有食之。 丁未，錄故功臣子孫百百餘人，授官有差。
In the tenth month of winter, on wuwu, Mu Ying was enfeoffed Marquis of Xiping. On xinyou he granted public fields to all officials. In the eleventh month, on guiwei, Deng Yu, Duke of Wei, died. On dinghai he jointly sacrificed to Heaven and Earth in the Hall of Complying with Heaven. That month he remitted land rent in Henan, Shaanxi, Guangdong, and Huguang. The Weimao barbarians rebelled; Ding Yu, censor-in-chief, was made Pacify Qiang general and suppressed them. On the first day of the twelfth month, yisi, there was a solar eclipse. On dingwei he recorded more than one hundred descendants of former meritorious officials and appointed them to offices in varying degrees.
22
是年，占城、三佛齊、暹羅、爪哇、真臘入貢。 高麗使五至，以嗣王未立，卻之。
That year Champa, Sanfoqi, Siam, Java, and Zhenla sent tribute missions. Goryeo envoys came five times, but because the heir king was not yet established, their tribute was declined.
23
十一年春正月甲戌，封皇子椿為蜀王，柏湘王，桂豫王，模漢王，植衛王。 改封吳王橚為周王。 己卯，進封湯和信國公。 是月，徵天下布政使及知府來朝。 二月，指揮胡淵平茂州蠻。 三月壬午，命奏事毋關白中書省。 是月，第來朝官為三等。
In the eleventh year, on jiaxu of the first month of spring, he enfeoffed his sons Chun as King of Shu, Bai as King of Xiang, Gui as King of Yu, Mo as King of Han, and Zhi as King of Wei. The King of Wu, Chu, was redesignated King of Zhou. On jimao Tang He, Marquis of Zhongshan, was advanced to Duke of Xin. That month he summoned provincial administration commissioners and prefectural magistrates from across the empire to court. In the second month Commander Hu Yuan pacified the Maozhou barbarians. In the third month, on renwu, he ordered that memorials not pass through the Secretariat for clearance. That month he ranked officials coming to court in three grades.
24
夏四月，元嗣君愛猷識理達臘殂，子脫古思帖木兒嗣。 五月丁酉，存問蘇、松、嘉、湖被水災民，戶賜米一石，蠲逋賦六十五萬有奇。 六月壬子，遣使祭故元嗣君。 己巳，五開蠻叛，殺靖州指揮過興，以辰州指揮楊仲名為總兵官，討之。
In the fourth month of summer the Yuan heir Ayushiridara died and his son Toghus Temür succeeded him. In the fifth month, on dingyou, he inquired after people of Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou stricken by flood, granted one shi of grain per household, and remitted more than 650,000 in tax arrears. In the sixth month, on renzi, he sent envoys to sacrifice to the former Yuan heir. On jisi the Wukai barbarians rebelled and killed Jingzhou Commander Guo Xing; Yang Zhongming, commander of Chenzhou, was made overall military officer to suppress them.
25
秋七月丁丑，振平陽饑。 是月，蘇、松、揚、臺海溢，遣官存恤。 八月，免應天、太平、鎮江、甯國、廣德諸府州秋糧。 九月丙申，追封劉繼祖為義惠侯。
In the seventh month of autumn, on dingchou, he provided famine relief in Pingyang. That month the seas at Suzhou, Songjiang, Yangzhou, and Taizhou overflowed, and he sent officials to provide relief. In the eighth month he remitted autumn grain tax in the prefectures of Yingtian, Taiping, Zhenjiang, Ningguo, and Guangde. In the ninth month, on bingshen, Liu Jizu was posthumously enfeoffed Marquis of Righteous Favor.
26
冬十月甲子，大祀殿成。 十一月庚午，征西將軍西平侯沐英率都督藍玉、玉弼討西番。 是月，五開蠻平。
In the tenth month of winter, on jiazi, the Great Sacrifice Hall was completed. In the eleventh month, on gengwu, Mu Ying, Marquis of Xiping and western campaign general, led Regional Commanders Lan Yu and Wang Bi against the Western Barbarians. That month the Wukai barbarians were pacified.
27
是年，暹羅、闍婆、高麗、琉球、占城、三佛齊、朵甘、烏斯藏、彭亨、百花入貢。
That year Siam, Java, Goryeo, Ryukyu, Champa, Sanfoqi, Do-kham, U-Tsang, Pahang, and Baihua sent tribute missions.
28
十二年春正月己卯，始合禮天地於南郊。 甲申，洮州十八族番叛，命沐英移兵討之。 丙申，丁玉平松州蠻。 二月戊戌，李文忠督理河、岷、臨、鞏軍事。 乙巳，詔曰：「今春雨雪經旬。 天下貧民困於饑寒者多有，其令有司給以鈔。 」丙寅，信國公湯和率列候練兵臨清。
In the twelfth year, on jimao of the first month of spring, he first jointly sacrificed to Heaven and Earth at the Southern Suburb. On jiashen the eighteen tribes of Taozhou rebelled, and Mu Ying was ordered to move troops to suppress them. On bingshen Ding Yu pacified the Songzhou barbarians. In the second month, on wuxu, Li Wenzhong supervised military affairs in He, Min, Lin, and Gong. On yisi he issued an edict: "Spring rain and snow have now lasted more than ten days. Many poor people throughout the empire suffer hunger and cold; I order local officials to give them paper money. " On bingyin Tang He, Duke of Xin, led the ranked marquises to drill troops at Linqing.
29
夏五月癸未，蠲北平田租。 六月丁卯，都督馬雲征大寧。 秋七月丙辰，丁玉回師討眉縣賊，平之。 己未，李文忠還掌大都督府事。 八月辛巳，詔凡致仕官復其家，終身無所與。 九月己亥，沐英大破西番，擒其部長三副使。
In the fifth month of summer, on guiwei, he remitted land rent in Beiping. In the sixth month, on dingmao, Regional Commander Ma Yun campaigned against Daning. In the seventh month of autumn, on bingchen, Ding Yu brought the army back to suppress bandits in Meixian and pacified them. On jiwei Li Wenzhong returned to direct affairs of the Chief Military Commission. In the eighth month, on xinsi, he ordered that the families of retired officials be restored and exempted from obligations for life. In the ninth month, on jihai, Mu Ying routed the Western Barbarians and captured three of their vice chieftains.
30
冬十一月甲午，沐英班師，封仇成、藍玉等十二人為侯。 庚申，大寧平。 十二月，汪廣洋貶廣南，賜死。 徵天下博學老成之士至京師。
In the eleventh month of winter, on jiawu, Mu Ying returned the army; Qiu Cheng, Lan Yu, and twelve others were enfeoffed as marquises. On gengshen Daning was pacified. In the twelfth month Wang Guangyang was demoted to Guangnan and ordered to take his own life. He summoned learned and accomplished elderly scholars from across the empire to the capital.
31
是年，占城、爪哇、暹羅、日本、安南、高麗入貢。 高麗貢黃金百斤、白金萬兩，以不如約，卻之。
That year Champa, Java, Siam, Japan, Annan, and Goryeo sent tribute missions. Goryeo presented one hundred jin of gold and ten thousand liang of silver, but because it did not accord with the agreement, the tribute was declined.
32
十三年春正月戊戌，左丞相胡惟庸謀反，及其黨御史大夫陳甯、中丞塗節等伏誅。 癸卯，大祀天地於南郊。 罷中書省，廢丞相等官，更定六部官秩，改大都督府為中、左、右、前、後五軍都督府。 二月壬戌朔，詔舉聰明正直、孝弟力田、賢良方正、文學術數之士。 發丹符，驗天下金穀之數。 戊辰，文武官年六十以上者聽致仕，給以誥敕。 三月壬辰，減蘇、松、嘉、湖重賦十之二。 壬寅，燕王隸之國北平。 壬子，沐英襲元將脫火赤於亦集乃，擒之，盡降其眾。
In the thirteenth year, on wuxu of the first month of spring, Left Chief Minister Hu Weiyong plotted rebellion; he and his associates Chen Ning, censor-in-chief, Zhong Tu, vice censor, and others were executed. On guimao he performed the great sacrifice to Heaven and Earth at the Southern Suburb. He abolished the Secretariat and the office of chief minister, revised the ranks of the Six Ministries, and divided the Chief Military Commission into the five military commissions of Central, Left, Right, Front, and Rear. On the first day of the second month, renxu, he issued an edict seeking men of clear intelligence, upright conduct, filial piety, field labor, worthy talent, literary learning, and numerological skill. He issued red tallies to verify gold and grain accounts throughout the empire. On wuchen he permitted civil and military officials aged sixty and above to retire and granted them patent letters of appointment. In the third month, on renchen, he reduced the heavy levies of Suzhou, Songjiang, Jiaxing, and Huzhou by two tenths. On renyin the Prince of Yan took up his fief at Beiping. On renzi Mu Ying raided the Yuan general Tohochu at Ejinay, captured him, and accepted the surrender of his entire force.
33
夏四月己丑，命群臣各舉所知。 五月甲午，雷震謹身殿。 乙未，大赦。 丙申，釋在京及臨濠屯田輸作者。 己亥，免天下田租。 吏以過誤罷者還其職。 壬寅，都督濮英進兵赤斤站，獲故元豳王亦憐真及其部曲而還。 是月，罷御史台。 命從征士卒老疾者許以子代，老而無子及寡婦，有司資遣還。 六月丙寅，雷震奉天門，避正殿省愆。 丁卯，罷王府工役。 丁丑，置諫院官。
In the fourth month of summer, on jichou, he ordered each minister to recommend men he knew. In the fifth month, on jiawu, lightning struck the Hall of Self-Cultivation. On yiwei he granted a general amnesty. On bingshen he released those performing labor at the capital and at Linhuang garrison farms. On jihai he remitted land rent throughout the empire. Officials dismissed for minor errors were restored to office. On renyin Regional Commander Pu Ying advanced to Chijin Station, captured the former Yuan Prince of Bin, Yilianzhen, and his followers, and returned. That month he abolished the Censorate. Campaign soldiers who were old or ill were permitted to have sons substitute for them; those old without sons and widows were to be given resources and sent home by local officials. In the sixth month, on bingyin, lightning struck the Gate of Complying with Heaven, and the emperor avoided the main hall to examine his faults. On dingmao he abolished corvée labor for princely establishments. On dingchou he established remonstrance officials.
34
秋八月，命天下學校師生，日給廩膳。 九月辛卯，景川侯曹震、營陽侯楊璟、永城侯恭顯屯田北平。 乙巳，天壽節，始受群臣朝賀，賜宴於謹身殿，後以為常。 丙午，置四輔官，告於太廟。 以儒士王本、估佑、襲斅、杜斅、趙民望、吳源為春、夏官。 是月，詔陝西衛軍以三分之二屯田。 安置翰林學士承旨宋濂於茂州，道卒。
In the eighth month of autumn he ordered daily grain provisions for teachers and students of schools throughout the empire. In the ninth month, on xinmao, Cao Zhen, Marquis of Jingchuan, Yang Jing, Marquis of Yingyang, and Gong Xian, Marquis of Yongcheng, organized garrison farming at Beiping. On yisi, the Heavenly Longevity Festival, he first received congratulations from his ministers and granted a banquet in the Hall of Self-Cultivation, thereafter making it a standing practice. On bingwu he established four assisting officials and announced them at the Grand Temple. The Confucian scholars Wang Ben, Hu You, Xi Zhuo, Du Zhuo, Zhao Minwang, and Wu Yuan were appointed spring and summer officials. That month he ordered Shaanxi guard troops to devote two thirds of their time to garrison farming. Hanlin Academician Emeritus Song Lian was assigned to Maozhou and died on the road.
35
冬十一月乙未，徐達還。 丙午，元平章完者不花、乃兒不花犯永平，指揮劉廣戰沒，千戶王輅擊敗之，擒完者不花。 十二月，天下府州縣所舉士至者八百六十餘人，授官有差。 南雄侯趙庸鎮廣東，討陽春蠻。
In the eleventh month of winter, on yiwei, Xu Da returned. On bingwu the Yuan ministers Wanzehua and Erdenbu invaded Yongping; Commander Liu Guang died in battle; Commander Wang Luo defeated them and captured Wanzehua. In the twelfth month more than eight hundred sixty scholars recommended by prefectures and counties across the empire arrived and were appointed to offices in varying degrees. Zhao Yong, Marquis of Nanxiong, garrisoned Guangdong and suppressed the Yangchun barbarians.
36
是年，琉球、日本、安南、占城、真臘、爪哇入貢，日本以無表卻之。
That year Ryukyu, Japan, Annan, Champa, Zhenla, and Java sent tribute; Japan was declined for lack of a formal memorial.
37
十四年春正月戊子，徐達為征虜大將軍，湯和、傅友德為左、右副將軍，帥師討乃兒不花。 命新授官者各舉所知。 乙未，大祀天地於南郊。 壬子，罷天下歲造兵器。 癸丑，命公候子弟入國學。 丙辰，詔求隱逸。 二月庚辰，核天下官田。 三月丙戌，大赦。 辛丑，頒《五經》、《四書》於北方學校。
In the fourteenth year, on wuzi of the first month of spring, Xu Da was made great general for the northern campaign, with Tang He and Fu Youde as left and right deputy generals, to lead an army against Erdenbu. He ordered each newly appointed official to recommend men he knew. On yiwei he performed the great sacrifice to Heaven and Earth at the Southern Suburb. On renzi he abolished annual manufacture of weapons throughout the empire. On guichou he ordered sons of dukes and marquises to enter the National University. On bingchen he issued an edict seeking reclusive scholars. In the second month, on gengchen, he audited official fields throughout the empire. In the third month, on bingxu, he granted a general amnesty. On xinchou he promulgated the Five Classics and Four Books to schools in the north.
38
夏四月庚午，徐達率諸將出塞，至北黃河，擊破元兵，獲全寧四部以歸。 五月，五溪蠻叛，江夏侯周德興討平之。
In the fourth month of summer, on gengwu, Xu Da led his generals beyond the frontier to the Northern Yellow River, defeated Yuan troops, and returned with the four divisions of Quanning. In the fifth month the Wuxi barbarians rebelled, and Zhou Dexing, Marquis of Jiangxia, suppressed and pacified them.
39
秋八月丙子，詔求明經老成之士，有司禮送京師。 庚辰，河決原武、祥符、中牟。 辛巳，徐達還。 九月壬午朔，傅友德為征南將軍，藍玉、沐英為左、右副將軍，帥征支南。 徐達鎮北平。 丙午，周德興移師討施州蠻，平之。
In the eighth month of autumn, on bingzi, he issued an edict seeking elderly scholars versed in the classics, and ordered local officials to escort them to the capital with proper ceremony. On gengchen the Yellow River burst its banks at Yuanwu, Xiangfu, and Zhongmou. On xinsi Xu Da returned. On the first day of the ninth month, renwu, Fu Youde was made southern campaign general with Lan Yu and Mu Ying as left and right deputies, to lead a campaign against Yunnan. Xu Da garrisoned Beiping. On bingwu Zhou Dexing moved his army to suppress the Shizhou barbarians and pacified them.
40
冬十月壬子朔，日有食之。 癸丑，命法司錄囚，會翰林院給事中及春坊官會議平允以聞。 甲寅，免應天、太平、應德、鎮江、甯國田租。 癸亥，分遣御史錄囚。 己卯，延安侯唐勝宗帥師討浙東山寇，平之。 十一月壬午，吉安侯陸仲亨鎮成都。 庚戌，趙庸討廣州海寇，大破之。 十二月丁巳，命翰林春坊官考駁諸司章奏。 戊辰，傅友德大敗元兵於白石江，遂下曲靖。 壬申，元梁王把匝剌瓦爾密走普寧自殺。
On the first day of the tenth month of winter, renzi, there was a solar eclipse. On guichou he ordered the judiciary to review prisoners, with Hanlin supervising secretaries and Eastern Palace officials to meet, discuss fairness, and report. On jiayin he remitted land rent in Yingtian, Taiping, Guangde, Zhenjiang, and Ningguo. On guihai he separately dispatched censors to review prisoners. On jimao Tang Shengzong, Marquis of Yan'an, led troops to suppress mountain bandits in eastern Zhe and pacified them. In the eleventh month, on renwu, Lu Zhongheng, Marquis of Ji'an, garrisoned Chengdu. On gengxu Zhao Yong campaigned against sea bandits in Guangzhou and routed them. In the twelfth month, on dingsi, he ordered Hanlin and Eastern Palace officials to examine memorials from the various offices. On wuchen Fu Youde routed Yuan troops at the White Stone River and then took Qujing. On renshen the Yuan Prince of Liang, Bazalawarmi, fled to Puning and killed himself.
41
是年，暹羅、安南、爪哇、朵甘、烏斯藏入貢。 以安南寇思明，不納。
That year Siam, Annan, Java, Do-kham, and U-Tsang sent tribute missions. Because Annan had raided Siming, their tribute was not accepted.
