龐尚鵬宋儀望張嶽李材陸樹德蕭廩賈三近李頤朱鴻謨蕭彥 〈 （弟雍查鐸）〉 孫維城謝傑郭惟賢萬象春鐘化民吳達可
Pang Shangpeng, Song Yiwang, Zhang Yue, Li Cai, Lu Shude, Xiao Lin, Jia Sanjin, Li Yi, Zhu Hongmo, and Xiao Yan [Supplementary biographies: Younger Brother Yong, Zha Tuo] Sun Weicheng, Xie Jie, Guo Weixian, Wan Xiangchun, Zhong Huamin, and Wu Dake
2
明年春，朝議興九邊屯、鹽。 擢尚鵬右僉都御史，與副都御史鄒應龍、唐繼祿分理。 尚鵬轄兩淮、長蘆、山東三運司，兼理畿輔、河南、山東、江北、遼東屯務。 抵昌平，劾內侍張恩擅殺人，兩淮巡鹽孫以仁贓罪，皆獲譴。 其秋，應龍等召還，命尚鵬兼領九邊屯務。 疏列鹽政二十事，鹺利大興。 乃自江北躬歷九邊，先後列上屯政便宜，江北者四，薊鎮者九，遼東、宣、大者各十一，寧夏者四，甘肅者七。 奏輒報可。 尚鵬權既重，自負經濟才，慷慨任事。 諸御史督鹽政者以事權見奪，欲攻去之。 河東巡鹽郜永春劾尚鵬行事乖違，吏部尚書楊博議留之。 會中官惡博，激帝怒，譙讓，罷博而落尚鵬職，汰屯鹽都御史官。 時三年十二月也。 明年，復坐按浙時驗進宮幣不中程，斥為民。
The next spring, the court took up plans to revive military colonies and the salt administration across the Nine Border Regions. Shangpeng was promoted to Right Assistant Censor-in-Chief and, along with Vice Censors-in-Chief Zou Yinglong and Tang Jilu, given a share of the work. Shangpeng oversaw the Liang-Huai, Changlu, and Shandong salt transport commissions and at the same time handled military colony affairs in the capital region, Henan, Shandong, Jiangbei, and Liaodong. When he reached Changping, he impeached the eunuch Zhang En for killing without authorization and the Liang-Huai salt inspector Sun Yiren for corruption; both men were punished. That autumn Yinglong and his colleagues were recalled, and Shangpeng was ordered to take on the Nine Border military colonies as well. He memorialized the throne with twenty proposals on salt administration, and salt revenues rose sharply. He then traveled in person through the Nine Border Regions from Jiangbei and submitted one set of colony reforms after another: four for Jiangbei, nine for Jizhen, eleven each for Liaodong and Xuan-Da, four for Ningxia, and seven for Gansu. His memorials were approved as soon as they arrived. With his authority greatly expanded, Shangpeng, confident in his talent for practical administration, threw himself into his duties with bold energy. The censors charged with overseeing salt policy, finding their power curtailed, tried to force him out. Gao Yongchun, the Hedong salt inspector, impeached Shangpeng for misconduct, but Minister of Personnel Yang Bo argued that he should be kept in office. Palace eunuchs who hated Bo provoked the emperor's wrath; after a sharp rebuke Bo was dismissed, Shangpeng was stripped of his post, and the office of censor-in-chief over colonies and salt was abolished altogether. This took place in the twelfth month of the third year. The next year he was punished again: while serving as inspector in Zhejiang, the palace silks he had certified had failed to meet standard, and he was reduced to commoner status.
3
神宗立，御史計坤亨等交薦，保定巡撫宋纁亦白其無罪。 萬曆四年冬，始以故官撫福建。 奏蠲逋餉銀，推行一條鞭法。 劾罷總兵官胡守仁，屬吏咸奉職。 張居正奪情，重譴言者。 尚鵬移書救，居正深銜之。 會拜左副都御史，居正令吏科陳三謨以給由歲月有誤劾之，遂罷去。 家居四年卒。 浙江、福建暨其鄉廣東皆以徭輕故德尚鵬，立祠祀。 天啟中，賜謚惠敏。
After the Shenzong Emperor came to the throne, Censor Ji Kunheng and others recommended him in turn, and Song Li, grand coordinator of Baoding, also testified to his innocence. In the winter of Wanli 4 he was at last restored to his former rank and made grand coordinator of Fujian. He memorialized for the remission of overdue tax payments and introduced the Single Whip Method of taxation. He impeached and dismissed the regional commander Hu Shouren, after which all his subordinates performed their duties as they should. When Zhang Juzheng sought to remain in office rather than observe mourning, those who objected were punished severely. Shangpeng wrote in their defense, and Juzheng took deep offense. When he was appointed Left Vice Censor-in-Chief, Juzheng had Chen Sanmo of the Ministry of Personnel section impeach him over a mistake in the dates on his appointment papers, and he was removed from office. He lived in retirement for four years and then died. The people of Zhejiang, Fujian, and his native Guangdong all revered Shangpeng for the lighter corvée burdens he had brought them, and erected temples in his honor. During the Tianqi reign he was posthumously granted the temple name Huimin.
4
宋儀望，字望之，吉安永豐人。 嘉靖二十六年進士。 授吳縣知縣。 民輸白糧京師，輒破家。 儀望令諸區各出公田，計役授田贍之。 禁火葬，創子遊祠，建書院，惠績甚著。 征授御史。 劾大將軍仇鸞挾寇自重，疏留中。 已，陳時務十二策。 巡鹽河東，請開桑乾河通宣、大餉道，言：「河發源金龍池下甕城驛古定橋，會眾水，東流千餘里，入盧溝橋。 其間惟大同卜村有叢石，宣府黑龍灣石崖稍險，然不逾五十里，水淺者猶二三尺，疏鑿甚易。 曩大同巡撫侯鉞嘗乘小艇赴懷來，歷卜村、黑龍灣，安行無虞。 又自懷來溯流，載米三十石達之古定河，足利漕可徵。 」時方行挖運，率三十石致一石。 儀望疏至，下廷議。 兵部尚書聶豹言：「河成便漕，兼制敵騎。 」工部尚書歐陽必進言：「道遠役重。 」遂報罷。
Song Yiwang, styled Wangzhi, came from Yongfeng in Ji'an. He passed the jinshi examination in the twenty-sixth year of the Jiajing reign. He was appointed magistrate of Wu County. Delivering tribute grain to the capital routinely ruined local households. Yiwang had each district set aside public fields and, according to labor quotas, assign land to support those who carried the grain. He banned cremation, founded the Ziyou Shrine, established an academy, and won wide renown for his benevolent rule. He was recalled to the capital and appointed censor. He impeached the great general Qiu Luan for using the threat of the enemy to build his own power; the memorial was shelved at court. He later submitted twelve proposals on affairs of state. While inspecting salt in Hedong, he asked that the Sanggan River be opened as a supply route to Xuanfu and Datong, saying: "The river rises below Jinlong Pool at Guding Bridge by Wengcheng Station, gathers tributary streams, flows east for more than a thousand li, and reaches the Lugou Bridge. Along the way only Bucun in Datong has clustered rocks, and the stone cliffs at Heilongwan in Xuanfu are somewhat steep, yet this stretch is no more than fifty li; even in the shallowest places the water is still two or three chi deep, and dredging would be easy. In the past Hou Yue, grand coordinator of Datong, had taken a small boat to Huailai by way of Bucun and Heilongwan and traveled safely without trouble. He also went upstream from Huailai and carried thirty shi of grain as far as Guding River, proving that the route could profitably be used for grain transport. " At the time overland transport was in use, and it took roughly thirty shi dispatched to deliver one shi to its destination. When Yiwang's memorial arrived, the court referred it for deliberation. Minister of War Nie Bao said: "Once the river is opened, transport will be easier and enemy cavalry can be contained as well. " Minister of Works Ouyang Bijin said: "The route is too long and the labor burden too heavy. " The proposal was rejected.
5
儀望尋省母歸。 還朝，發胡宗憲、阮鶚奸貪狀，鶚被逮。 二人皆嚴嵩私人，嵩由是不悅。 及受命督三殿門工，嵩子世蕃私賈人金，屬必進俾與工事，儀望執不可。 工竣，敘勞，擢大理右寺丞。 世蕃以為德，儀望請急歸，無所謝，世蕃益怒。 會災異考察京官，必進遷吏部，遂坐以浮躁，貶夷陵判官。 嵩敗，擢霸州兵備僉事。 請城涿州，除馬戶逋稅。 進大名兵備副使，改福建。 與總兵官戚繼光合兵破倭，因列海防善後事。 詔從其請。 隆慶二年，吏部尚書楊博欲黜儀望，考功郎劉一儒持之，乃鐫二秩，補四川僉事。 四遷大理少卿。
Yiwang soon went home on leave to visit his mother. When he returned to court, he exposed the corruption of Hu Zongxian and Ruan E, and E was arrested. Both were protégés of Yan Song, who was displeased by the attack. When he was assigned to supervise work on the gates of the Three Halls, Song's son Shifan had secretly taken money from merchants and asked Bijin to put them on the project; Yiwang refused. When the work was finished, rewards were distributed and he was promoted to Right Assistant Director of the Court of Judicial Review. Shifan took this as a personal favor, but Yiwang asked for urgent leave to go home and offered no thanks, which made Shifan angrier still. During an evaluation of capital officials prompted by omens and disasters, Bijin was transferred to the Ministry of Personnel, and Yiwang was found frivolous and demoted to assistant magistrate of Yiling. After Song's downfall he was promoted to military intendant of Bazhou. He asked that Zhuozhou be fortified and that overdue taxes owed by horse-rearing households be remitted. He was promoted to vice commissioner of military preparedness in Daming and then transferred to Fujian. He joined regional commander Qi Jiguang in defeating the pirates and then laid out plans for strengthening coastal defense afterward. The throne approved his proposals. In Longqing 2, Minister of Personnel Yang Bo wanted to dismiss Yiwang, but Merit Evaluation Officer Liu Yiru defended him; Yiwang was reduced two ranks instead and appointed assistant commissioner in Sichuan. After four promotions he rose to Vice Minister of the Court of Judicial Review.
6
萬曆二年，張居正當國，雅知儀望才，擢右僉都御史，巡撫應天諸府。 奏減屬郡災賦。 海警稍定，將吏諱言兵，儀望與副使王叔果修戰備。 倭果至，禦之黑水洋，斬獲多，進右副都御史。 先有詔雪建文諸臣，儀望創表忠祠祀之南京。 宋忠臣楊邦晙，儀望鄉人也，葬江寧，歲久漸湮，儀望為封其墓，載其祠祀典。 故太常卿袁洪愈、祭酒姜寶皆不為居正所喜，儀望薦之朝，漸失居正意。 四年，稍遷南京大理卿。 逾年改北，被劾罷歸。
In Wanli 2, with Zhang Juzheng in power, he promoted Yiwang, whose ability he had long respected, to Right Assistant Censor-in-Chief and grand coordinator of the Yingtian prefectures. He memorialized for reduced disaster levies in the subordinate prefectures. As coastal alarms eased, officers avoided discussing military matters, but Yiwang and Vice Commissioner Wang Shuguo set about restoring defenses. The pirates did come; he fought them at Heishuiyang, killed and captured many, and was promoted to Right Vice Censor-in-Chief. After an earlier edict had rehabilitated the ministers who served the Jianwen Emperor, Yiwang founded the Shrine of Loyalty in Nanjing to honor them. The Song loyalist Yang Bangjun, a fellow townsman of Yiwang, was buried in Jiangning; after years the grave had fallen into neglect, and Yiwang restored the tomb and had his worship entered in the state ritual canon. Former Director of Ceremonies Yuan Hongyu and National Academy Chancellor Jiang Bao were both out of Juzheng's favor; when Yiwang recommended them at court he gradually lost Juzheng's goodwill. In the fourth year he was transferred to Chief Minister of the Nanjing Court of Judicial Review. The next year he was transferred to Beijing, was impeached, dismissed, and sent home.
7
儀望少師聶豹，私淑王守仁，又從鄒守益、歐陽德、羅洪先遊。 守仁從祀，儀望有力焉。 家居數年卒。
In youth Yiwang studied under Nie Bao, privately admired Wang Shouren, and also learned from Zou Shouyi, Ouyang De, and Luo Hongxian. Yiwang had played a major part in securing posthumous worship for Shouren in the Confucian temple. He lived in retirement for several years and then died.
8
張嶽，字汝宗，餘姚人。 嘉靖三十八年進士。 授行人。 擢禮科給事中。 巡視內府庫藏，奏行厘弊八事。 已，又陳時政，極言講學者以富貴功名鼓動士大夫，談虛論寂，靡然成風。 又今吏治方清，獨兵部無振刷，推用總兵黃印、韓承慶等，非庸即狡。 曹司條例淆亂無章，胥吏朋奸，搏噬將校，其咎必有所歸。 時徐階當國，為講學會，而楊博在兵部，意蓋指二人也。 博奏辨乞罷，帝慰留之。 博自是惡嶽。 及掌吏部，嶽已遷工科左給事中，遂出為雲南參議。 再遷河南參政。
Zhang Yue, styled Ruzong, came from Yuyao. He passed the jinshi examination in the thirty-eighth year of the Jiajing reign. He was appointed palace courier. He was promoted to supervising secretary in the Ministry of Rites section. While inspecting the inner palace storehouses, he memorialized eight measures to rectify abuses and had them carried out. He later addressed current affairs as well, arguing at length that lecturers were stirring officials with promises of wealth and rank and that empty talk of quietism had become a widespread fashion. He also said that while civil administration was being cleaned up, the Ministry of War alone had not been revived, and the regional commanders it promoted, such as Huang Yin and Han Chengqing, were either mediocre or cunning. Department regulations were chaotic and disordered; clerks formed factions to commit fraud and prey on officers, and someone had to be held responsible. At the time Xu Jie held power and led lecture gatherings, while Yang Bo headed the Ministry of War; he clearly had the two of them in mind. Bo memorialized in his defense and asked to resign; the emperor reassured him and kept him in office. From then on Bo hated Yue. When Bo took charge of the Ministry of Personnel, Yue had already been transferred to Left Supervising Secretary in the Ministry of Works section and was then sent out as administrative vice commissioner of Yunnan. He was later transferred to administrative commissioner of Henan.
9
萬曆初，張居正雅知嶽，用為太僕少卿。 再遷南京右僉都御史，督操江。 甫到官，會居正父喪謀奪情，南京尚書潘晟及諸給事、御史，咸上疏請留居正。 嶽獨馳疏請令馳驛奔喪，居正大怒。 會大計京官，給事中傅作舟等承風劾嶽，貶一秩調外，嶽遂歸。 久之，操江僉都御史呂藿、給事中吳綰知居正憾未釋，摭劾嶽落職閑住。 甫兩月，居正死，南京御史方萬山薦嶽，劾作舟。 作舟坐斥，起嶽四川參議。 旋擢右僉都御史，巡撫南、贛。 入為左僉都御史，獻時政四議。 其一言宗藩宜以世次遞殺，親盡則停，俾習四民之業。 其一言治河之策，夏鎮固當開，沽頭亦不可廢。 並報寢。 進左副都御史，上疏評議廷臣賢否，為給事中袁國臣等所論。 時已遷刑部右侍郎，坐罷歸。
Early in the Wanli reign Zhang Juzheng, who had long respected Yue's ability, appointed him Vice Minister of the Court of the Imperial Stud. He was then transferred to Right Assistant Censor-in-Chief in Nanjing to supervise the Yangtze defense force. He had barely taken office when Juzheng, mourning his father's death, sought to remain in office; Minister Pan Sheng of Nanjing and the supervising secretaries and censors all memorialized asking that Juzheng be kept at his post. Yue alone sent an urgent memorial asking that Juzheng be ordered to travel by post relay to observe mourning, and Juzheng was furious. During the grand evaluation of capital officials, Supervising Secretary Fu Zuozhou and others, taking their cue from the prevailing mood, impeached Yue; he was demoted one rank and transferred outside the capital, and Yue went home. Some time later, Yangtze defense Assistant Censor-in-Chief Lü Huo and Supervising Secretary Wu Wan, knowing Juzheng's resentment had not faded, gathered charges against Yue and had him dismissed to idle residence. Barely two months later Juzheng died; Nanjing Censor Fang Wanshan recommended Yue and impeached Zuozhou. Zuozhou was dismissed, and Yue was recalled as administrative vice commissioner of Sichuan. He was soon promoted to Right Assistant Censor-in-Chief and made grand coordinator of Nan and Gan. He was brought into the capital as Left Assistant Censor-in-Chief and submitted four proposals on current policy. One argued that imperial clansmen should be reduced in rank generation by generation, stopping when the degree of kinship was exhausted, so that they might take up the ordinary occupations of common people. Another addressed river-control policy: Xiazhen should indeed be opened, but Gutou should not be abandoned. Both proposals were shelved. He was promoted to Left Vice Censor-in-Chief and submitted a memorial assessing which court officials were worthy and which were not; Supervising Secretary Yuan Guochen and others impeached him for it. By then he had already been transferred to Right Vice Minister of Justice; on this charge he was dismissed and sent home.

李材，字孟誠，豐城人，尚書遂子也。 舉嘉靖四十一年進士，授刑部主事。 素從鄒守益講學。 自以學未成，乞假歸。 訪唐樞、王畿、錢德洪，與問難。 隆慶中還朝。 由兵部郎中稍遷廣東僉事。 羅旁賊猖獗，材襲破之周高山，設屯以守。 賊有三巢在新會境，調副總兵梁守愚由恩平，遊擊王瑞由德慶入，身出肇慶中道，夜半斬賊五百級，毀廬舍千餘，空其地，募人田之。 亡何，倭五千攻陷電白，大掠而去。 材追破之石城，設伏海口，伺其遁而殲之，奪還婦女三千餘。 會奸人引倭自黃山間道潰而東。 材聲言大軍數道至以疑賊，而返故道迎擊，盡殺之。 又追襲雷州倭至英利，皆遁去，降賊渠許恩於陽江。 錄功，進副使。
Li Cai, styled Mengcheng, came from Fengcheng and was the son of Minister Li Sui. He passed the jinshi examination in the forty-first year of the Jiajing reign and was appointed a secretary in the Ministry of Justice. He had long studied under Zou Shouyi. Believing his studies were not yet finished, he asked for leave to go home. He visited Tang Shu, Wang Ji, and Qian Dehong and debated with them. During the Longqing reign he returned to the capital. Starting as a director in the Ministry of War, he was gradually promoted to vice commissioner of Guangdong. When the Luopang bandits grew bold, Cai routed them in a surprise attack at Zhougao Mountain and set up garrison colonies to hold the area. The bandits maintained three strongholds in Xinhui. Cai sent Vice Regional Commander Liang Shouyu in from Enping and Mobile Corps Commander Wang Rui from Deqing, while he himself marched from Zhaoqing along the central route. At midnight they killed five hundred bandits, destroyed over a thousand dwellings, emptied the territory, and recruited settlers to farm the land. Before long, five thousand Japanese pirates attacked and seized Dianbai, plundered the region, and withdrew. Cai pursued them, defeated them at Shicheng, and laid an ambush at Haikou. When they fled, he wiped them out and recovered more than three thousand captive women. Traitors then guided the pirates along a hidden route through Huangshan, and they broke out and fled east. Cai spread word that large forces were converging from several directions to unsettle the enemy, then doubled back along the original route to intercept them and killed them to the last man. He also pursued the Leizhou pirates as far as Yingli; they all escaped, and the bandit leader Xu En surrendered at Yangjiang. His achievements were recognized and he was promoted to vice commissioner.
11
萬曆初，張居正柄國，不悅材，遂引疾去。 居正卒，起官山東。 以才調遼東開原。 尋遷雲南洱海參政，進按察使，備兵金騰。 金騰地接緬甸，而孟養、蠻莫兩土司介其間，叛服不常。 緬部目曰大曩長、曰散奪者，率數千人據其地。 材謂不收兩土司無以制緬，遣人招兩土司來歸，而間討抗命夷阿坡。 居頃之，緬遣兵爭蠻莫，材合兩土司兵敗緬眾，殺大曩長，逐散奪去。 緬帥莽應裏益兵至孟養，復擊沈其舟，斬其將一人，乃退。 有猛密者，地在緬境，數為緬侵奪，舉族內徙，有司居之戶碗。 至是，緬勢稍屈，材資遣還故土。 亡何，緬人驅象陣大舉復仇，兩土司告急。 材遣遊擊劉天俸率把總寇崇德等出威緬，渡金沙江，與孟養兵會遮浪，迎擊之。 賊大敗，生擒繡衣賊將三人。 巡撫劉世曾、總兵官沐昌祚以大捷聞，詔令覆勘。 未上，而材擢右僉都御史，撫治鄖陽。
Early in the Wanli reign, Zhang Juzheng dominated the government. Unhappy with Cai, Juzheng drove him out on the pretext of illness. After Juzheng's death, Cai was recalled to office in Shandong. Recognizing his talent, the court transferred him to Kaiyuan in Liaodong. He was soon made administrative commissioner of Erhai in Yunnan, then promoted to surveillance commissioner with responsibility for military affairs at Jinteng. Jinteng bordered Burma, with the Mengyang and Manmo chieftaincies lying between them—tribes whose loyalty shifted unpredictably. Two Burmese chiefs, Da Nangchang and San Duo, led several thousand men in seizing the area. Cai argued that without bringing the two chieftaincies back into allegiance he could not control Burma. He sent envoys to win them over while quietly moving against the defiant tribesman A Po. Soon Burma sent troops to seize Manmo. Cai joined forces with the two chieftaincies, routed the Burmese army, killed Da Nangchang, and drove San Duo off. The Burmese commander Mang Yingli reinforced his army and marched on Mengyang. Moreover, in the past Ningxia lacked rations, and ten thousand taels of gold were sent each year to exchange for twenty-seven thousand shi of grain; later the responsible officials embezzled the funds and indiscriminately levied the people. He requested that reclaimed-field grain be used to cover the shortfall and that the levies be abolished permanently. " The court approved both requests. While inspecting Shandong during a drought year, he requested that tax remissions and relief be dispensed first and reported afterward. He was implicated for taking official silver for social purposes while in Ningxia, was impeached by Director of the Court of Imperial Regalia Zhou Hongdiao, and was transferred to serve as Recorder of the Messenger Office. He rose through several ranks to become Director of Ceremonies in the Ministry of Rites. Cheng Yao, Prince of Shen, had succeeded from a collateral line and asked that his secondary son be enfeoffed as a commandery prince; Huamin objected that this should not be allowed. The emperor sent word through a messenger, saying: "I would only give him an empty title so he can marry by it. " Huamin memorialized, saying: "Which is closer — the Prince of Shen's son or the heir apparent? If the prince's son is not promptly enfeoffed for fear of blocking marriage, is the heir apparent not promptly established without fear of blocking his early education? " The emperor grew angry, but since Huamin's words were blunt and straight, he had no way to rebut them. When the emperor ordered the simultaneous enfeoffment of three princes, Huamin, together with Gu Yuncheng and others, confronted Wang Xijue face to face in the court corridor. Soon afterward he was promoted to Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Entertainments. In the twenty-second year a great famine struck Henan, and people ate one another; the emperor ordered Huamin to serve concurrently as Henan circuit censor and go to provide relief. He carried out famine relief in full, and the people were greatly pleased. When the work was finished, he submitted illustrated reports to the throne. The emperor praised him and commended him twice. He was promoted to Vice Minister of Imperial Sacrifices. In the twenty-fourth year he was appointed Right Assistant Censor-in-Chief and made grand coordinator of Henan, where he suppressed the mining bandits of Nanyang. When bandits at Jiahe gathered in the thousands, he again led troops and defeated them. Mining was then underway, and he submitted a forceful memorial in opposition.

化民短小精悍，多智計。 居官勤厲，所至有聲。 遍歷八府，延父老問疾苦。 勞瘁卒官，士民相率頌於朝。 詔贈右副都御史，賜祠曰忠惠。
Huamin was short, compact, and sharp, and full of clever stratagems. Diligent and exacting in office, he won a reputation wherever he served. He visited all eight prefectures and summoned elders to inquire into their hardships. He worked himself to exhaustion and died in office; officials and commoners successively praised him at court. An edict posthumously granted him the title of Right Vice Censor-in-Chief and bestowed on his shrine the name Zhonghui, Loyal and Kind.

吳達可，字安節，宜興人，尚書儼從孫也。 萬曆五年進士。 歷知會稽、上高、豐城，並有聲。 選授御史。 疏請御經筵勤學，時與大臣臺諫面議政務，報聞。 大學士趙誌臯久疾乞休，未得請。 達可力言志臯衰庸，宜罷，不納。 二十八年正月，請因始和布令，舉皇長子冊立冠婚禮，簡輔臣補臺諫，撤礦稅中使，不報。 視鹽長蘆。 歲侵，繪上饑民十四圖，力請振貸。 稅使馬堂、張日華議加鹽稅，奸商妄稱嘉靖中大同用兵貸其貲三萬六千金，請於鹽課補給，戶部許之。 達可皆抗爭，事得已。 改按江西。 稅使潘相毆折輔國將軍謀圮肢，並系宗人宗達，誣以劫課，劾上饒知縣李鴻主使。 帝切責謀圮等，奪鴻官。 達可言：「宗人無故受刑，又重之以詰責，將使天潢人人自危。 鴻無辜，不當黜。 願亟正相罪，復鴻官。 」同官湯兆京亦極論相罪，且言遼東高淮、陜西梁永、山東陳增、廣東李鳳、雲南楊榮皆元惡，為民害，不可一日留。 皆弗聽。 鴻，吳人，大學士申時行之婿。 萬曆十六年舉北闈鄉試，為吏部郎中高桂所攻。 後七年成進士。 至是，抗相，以強直稱。 相又請開廣信銅塘山，采取大木，鑿泰和斌姥山石膏，達可復極諫不可，閣臣亦爭之，乃寢。 還掌河南道事。 佐溫純大計京官。 尋陳新政要機，痛規首輔沈一貫。 疏留中。 擢太僕少卿，再遷南京太僕卿。 召改光祿，進通政使。 鎮撫史晉以罪罷，妄投封章詆朝貴。 達可封其疏而劾之，晉尋得罪。 奏請正疏式、屏讒邪、重駁正、懲奸宄數事，帝嘉納焉。 尋上疏乞休去。 卒，贈右副都御史。
Wu Dake, whose courtesy name was Anjie, came from Yixing and was a grand-nephew of Minister Wu Yan. He received his jinshi degree in the fifth year of the Wanli reign. He served successively as magistrate of Kuaiji, Shanggao, and Fengcheng, winning a reputation in each post. He was selected and appointed an imperial censor. He memorialized requesting that the emperor attend the Classics Lectures, study diligently, and meet regularly with ministers and censorial officials to discuss state affairs face to face; the court acknowledged receipt but took no action. Grand Secretary Zhao Zhigao had long been ill and requested retirement, but his request was not granted. Dake forcefully argued that Zhao was decrepit and mediocre and should be dismissed, but the emperor would not accept it. In the first month of the twenty-eighth year he requested that, at the Beginning of Harmony, edicts be issued to carry out the investiture, capping, and marriage of the eldest imperial son, select assistant ministers, fill censorial posts, and withdraw the eunuch envoys collecting mining taxes; there was no response. He inspected salt administration at Changlu. In a year of crop failure he submitted fourteen paintings of starving people and forcefully requested relief and loans. The tax agents Ma Tang and Zhang Rihua proposed raising the salt tax; scheming merchants falsely claimed that during the Jiajing reign the campaign at Datong had borrowed thirty-six thousand jin of their capital and asked that it be repaid from salt revenues; the Ministry of Revenue approved the request. Dake resisted all of these proposals, and the matters were dropped. He was transferred to conduct an inspection in Jiangxi. The tax agent Pan Xiang beat and broke the limbs of Mou Pi, Auxiliary State General, and also detained the clansman Zongda, falsely accusing him of robbing tax collection and impeaching Li Hong, magistrate of Shangrao, as the mastermind. The emperor severely rebuked Mou Pi and the others and stripped Li Hong of his office. Dake wrote: "A clansman was punished without cause, and on top of that was subjected to stern rebuke; this will make every member of the imperial lineage feel endangered. Li Hong is innocent and should not be dismissed. I beg that Pan Xiang be promptly punished and Li Hong's office restored. " His colleague Tang Zhaojing also argued forcefully for Pan Xiang's guilt, and further said that Gao Huai in Liaodong, Liang Yong in Shaanxi, Chen Zeng in Shandong, Li Feng in Guangdong, and Yang Rong in Yunnan were all arch-villains and scourges to the people who could not be allowed to remain even a day. None of this was heeded. Li Hong was a native of Wu and the son-in-law of Grand Secretary Shen Shixing. In the sixteenth year of Wanli he passed the northern provincial examination, and was attacked by Gao Gui, a director in the Ministry of Personnel. Seven years later he received his jinshi degree. On this occasion he resisted Pan Xiang and was praised for his firm integrity. Pan Xiang also requested opening Tongtang Mountain in Guangxin to harvest timber and quarrying gypsum at Binlao Mountain in Taihe; Dake again memorialized forcefully against it, the grand secretaries also objected, and the proposal was dropped. He returned to take charge of the Henan section. He assisted Wen Chun in conducting the grand evaluation of capital officials. He soon set forth the essential points of the new administration and sharply admonished Chief Grand Secretary Shen Yiguan. The memorial was shelved at court. He was promoted to Vice Minister of the Court of the Imperial Stud and later transferred to the Nanjing post. He was summoned to the Court of Imperial Entertainments and then promoted to Commissioner of the Office of Transmission. Guard commander Shi Jin, dismissed for misconduct, recklessly submitted a sealed memorial slandering senior officials at court. Dake sealed the memorial and impeached him; Jin was soon punished. He memorialized on correcting memorial format, removing slanderers, restoring the weight of rebuttal and review, and punishing wrongdoers; the emperor praised and accepted his proposals. He soon submitted a memorial asking to retire. After his death he was posthumously granted the rank of Right Vice Censor-in-Chief.

The commentator writes: Pang Shangpeng and the others served both at court and in the provinces; capable and practical in affairs, each had achievements worth noting. In addressing current policy, Jia Sanjin spoke often with the wisdom of a seasoned elder; his remarks on rank and qualification cut to the heart of long-standing abuses. Xie Jie's refusal of gifts from subordinate officials likewise did honor to the example of Yang Zhen.
