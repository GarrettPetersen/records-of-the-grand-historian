建文初，封弟允熥爲吳王，允熞爲衡王，允熙爲徐王，皆懿文太子子也。 福王時，追諡吳王曰悼，衡王曰愍，徐王曰哀，見福王傳。
Early in the Jianwen reign, the emperor enfeoffed his younger brothers Yun Song as Prince of Wu, Yun Jian as Prince of Heng, and Yun Xi as Prince of Xu; all were sons of Crown Prince Yiwen. Under the Prince of Fu, posthumous titles were conferred: the Prince of Wu as Dao (Lamented), the Prince of Heng as Min (Pitied), and the Prince of Xu as Ai (Grieving); see the biography of the Prince of Fu.
吳 悼王允熥，懿文太子嫡三子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封廣澤王，居漳州。其年九月召還，廢爲庶人。永樂十五年卒。
衡 愍王允熞，懿文太子庶四子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封懷恩王，居建昌。其年，與吳王俱召還，廢爲庶人。
徐 哀簡王允熙，懿文太子庶五子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封敷惠王，隨母居太子陵。永樂二年改封甌寧王，奉懿文祀。四年卒。無子，除。
Wu: Prince Dao Yun Song, Crown Prince Yiwen's third son by his principal consort, received his enfeoffment in Jianwen 1 but never took up his fief. After Chengzu entered the capital, Yun Song was demoted to Prince of Guangze and sent to Zhangzhou; recalled that ninth month and stripped to commoner rank. He died in Yongle 15.
Heng: Prince Min Yun Jian, the fourth son of Crown Prince Yiwen by a secondary consort, was enfeoffed in Jianwen 1 but never went to his fief. When Chengzu took the capital, he was demoted to Prince of Huai'en at Jianchang; that same year he and the Prince of Wu were recalled together and reduced to commoners.
Xu: Prince Ai the Simple Yun Xi, Crown Prince Yiwen's fifth son by a secondary consort, was enfeoffed in Jianwen 1 but never took up his fief. After Chengzu entered the capital he was demoted to Prince of Fuhui and lived with his mother at the crown prince's mausoleum; in Yongle 2 he was re-enfeoffed as Prince of Ouning to tend Yiwen's cult. He died in Yongle 4 without heirs, and the line was extinguished.
