建文初，封弟允熥爲吳王，允熞爲衡王，允熙爲徐王，皆懿文太子子也。 福王時，追諡吳王曰悼，衡王曰愍，徐王曰哀，見福王傳。
Early in the Jianwen reign, the emperor enfeoffed his younger brothers Yun Ting as Prince of Wu, Yun Chi as Prince of Heng, and Yun Xi as Prince of Xu; all were sons of the Crown Prince Yi Wen. Under the Prince of Fu, posthumous titles were conferred on the Prince of Wu as Dao, the Prince of Heng as Min, and the Prince of Xu as Ai; see the biography of the Prince of Fu.
吳 悼王允熥，懿文太子嫡三子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封廣澤王，居漳州。其年九月召還，廢爲庶人。永樂十五年卒。
衡 愍王允熞，懿文太子庶四子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封懷恩王，居建昌。其年，與吳王俱召還，廢爲庶人。
徐 哀簡王允熙，懿文太子庶五子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封敷惠王，隨母居太子陵。永樂二年改封甌寧王，奉懿文祀。四年卒。無子，除。
Wu princedom — Prince Dao Yun Ting, the Crown Prince Yi Wen's third legitimate son, was enfeoffed in Jianwen year 1 but never took up his fief. After Chengzu entered the capital, he was demoted to Prince of Guangze and lodged at Zhangzhou; recalled that ninth month and reduced to a commoner. He died in Yongle year 15.
Heng princedom — Prince Min Yun Chi, the Crown Prince Yi Wen's fourth son by a secondary consort, was enfeoffed in Jianwen year 1 but never went to his fief. After Chengzu took the capital he was demoted to Prince of Huai'en at Jianchang; that same year he and the Prince of Wu were recalled and stripped to commoner rank.
Xu princedom — Prince Ai Jian Yun Xi, the Crown Prince Yi Wen's fifth son by a concubine, was enfeoffed in Jianwen year 1 but never took his fief. After Chengzu entered the capital he was demoted to Prince of Fuhui and dwelt with his mother at the crown prince's mausoleum; in Yongle year 2 he was made Prince of Ouning to tend Yi Wen's cult. He died in year 4 without heirs, and the line was extinguished.

成祖四子。 仁宗外，高爔未封。 其得封者二王，曰漢王高煦，曰趙簡王高燧。
Chengzu had four sons. Aside from the future Renzong, Gao Xi received no princely enfeoffment. Two sons were enfeoffed: Gao Xu as Prince of Han and Gao Sui as Prince Jian of Zhao.
漢 高煦，成祖嫡二子。洪武二十八年封高陽王，永樂二年進封。十五年就藩樂安州。宣德元年八月反，削爵錮西內，焚死。世子瞻坦先卒。韋妃及九子俱從死，除。

南樂 安懿王祁鉷，惠嫡七子，天順元年封。弘治十六年薨。榮僖王見瀾，安懿庶一子，正德十一年襲封。嘉靖元年薨。宣靖王祐檟，榮僖庶一子，嘉靖四年襲封。十八年薨。康順王厚𰞬，宣靖嫡一子，嘉靖二十二年襲封。萬曆八年薨。恭恪王載墲，康順庶一子，萬曆十二年襲封。十三年薨。王翊𰽅，恭恪庶一子，萬曆十六年襲封，薨。王常汰，翊𰽅嫡一子，萬曆三十年封長子，既而襲封。

Han princedom — Gao Xu, Chengzu's second legitimate son, was made Prince of Gaoyang in Hongwu year 28, promoted in Yongle year 2, and took up his fief at Le'an in year 15. He rebelled in the eighth month of Xuande year 1, was stripped of rank, confined in the western palace, and burned to death. His heir Zhan Tan had predeceased him; Consort Wei and nine sons perished with him, and the line ended.

仁宗十子。 宣宗外，瞻垠未封。 其得封者八王，曰鄭靖王瞻埈，曰越靖王瞻墉，曰襄憲王瞻墡，曰荊憲王瞻堈，曰淮靖王瞻墺，曰滕懷王瞻塏，曰梁莊王瞻垍，曰衞恭王瞻埏。
Renzong had ten sons. Aside from the future Xuanzong, Zhan Yin received no princely enfeoffment. Eight sons were enfeoffed: Zhan Jun as Prince Jing of Zheng, Zhan Yong as Prince Jing of Yue, Zhan Fan as Prince Xian of Xiang, Zhan Gang as Prince Xian of Jing, Zhan Ao as Prince Jing of Huai, Zhan Kai as Prince Huai of Teng, Zhan Ji as Prince Zhuang of Liang, and Zhan Yan as Prince Gong of Wei.
