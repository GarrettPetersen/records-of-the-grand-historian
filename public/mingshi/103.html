建文初，封弟允熥爲吳王，允熞爲衡王，允熙爲徐王，皆懿文太子子也。 福王時，追諡吳王曰悼，衡王曰愍，徐王曰哀，見福王傳。
Early in the Jianwen reign, the emperor enfeoffed his younger brothers Yun Song as Prince of Wu, Yun Ban as Prince of Heng, and Yun Xi as Prince of Xu — all sons of Crown Prince Yiwen. Under the Prince of Fu, Wu was posthumously titled Dao, Heng Min, and Xu Ai; see the biography of the Prince of Fu.
吳 悼王允熥，懿文太子嫡三子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封廣澤王，居漳州。其年九月召還，廢爲庶人。永樂十五年卒。
衡 愍王允熞，懿文太子庶四子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封懷恩王，居建昌。其年，與吳王俱召還，廢爲庶人。
徐 哀簡王允熙，懿文太子庶五子，建文元年封，未就藩。成祖入京師，降封敷惠王，隨母居太子陵。永樂二年改封甌寧王，奉懿文祀。四年卒。無子，除。
Wu — Prince Dao Yun Song, Crown Prince Yiwen's third son by his principal consort, was enfeoffed in Jianwen year 1 but never took up his fief. When Chengzu entered the capital, he was demoted to Prince of Guangze and lodged at Zhangzhou. That same year, in the ninth month, he was summoned back and degraded to commoner status. He died in Yongle year 15.
Heng — Prince Min Yun Ban, Crown Prince Yiwen's fourth son by a concubine, was enfeoffed in Jianwen year 1 but never took up his fief. When Chengzu entered the capital, he was demoted to Prince of Huai'en and lodged at Jianchang. That year he and the Prince of Wu were both summoned back and degraded to commoner status.
Xu — Prince Ai Jian Yun Xi, Crown Prince Yiwen's fifth son by a concubine, was enfeoffed in Jianwen year 1 but never took up his fief. When Chengzu entered the capital, he was demoted to Prince of Fuhui and lived with his mother at the crown prince's tomb. In Yongle year 2 he was re-enfeoffed as Prince of Ouning to maintain sacrifices to Yiwen. He died in year 4 without heirs; the line was abolished.

Han — Gao Xu, Chengzu's second son by his principal consort, was made Prince of Gaoyang in Hongwu year 28 and promoted in Yongle year 2. He took up his fief at Le'an in year 15. He rebelled in the eighth month of Xuande year 1; his title was stripped, he was confined in the Western Palace, and burned to death. Heir apparent Zhan Tan had predeceased him; Consort Wei and nine sons were all put to death with him, and the line was abolished.
