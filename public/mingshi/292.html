2
張允登，漢州人。 萬歷三十八年進士。 歷知咸寧、咸陽，有善政。 其成進士，出湯賓尹之門，賓尹弗善也，而東林以賓尹故，惡之。 舉卓異，得刑部主事，累遷河西兵備副使。 鄜、延歲饑，亟遭盜，允登拊循備至，士民德之。 崇禎四年閏十一月督餉至甘泉，降卒潛與流賊通，殺知縣郭永固，劫餉。 允登力禦，不敵死。 鄜人素服迎其喪，哭聲震十里，罷市三日。
Zhang Yundeng came from Hanzhou. He passed the metropolitan examination in the thirty-eighth year of the Wanli reign. He served in turn as magistrate of Xianning and Xianyang, where his administration won praise. As a new jinshi he had studied under Tang Binyi, whom he had displeased; the Donglin partisans therefore turned against him on Binyi's account. After an outstanding evaluation he entered the Ministry of Punishments as a principal secretary and rose step by step to Assistant Military Commissioner for the Hexi circuit. Fuzhou and Yan'an endured famine year after year and repeated raids; Yundeng comforted and provisioned the people with tireless care, and gentry and commoners alike were devoted to him. In the intercalary eleventh month of the fourth year of Chongzhen he was escorting grain rations to Ganquan when surrendered soldiers secretly joined the roving bandits, killed Magistrate Guo Yonggu, and seized the supplies. Yundeng fought back with all his strength but was overpowered and killed. The people of Fuzhou went out in plain white to receive his coffin; mourning cries were heard for ten li around, and the markets closed for three days.
3
當是時，流賊日熾，總督洪承疇往來奔擊，日不暇給。 逾月陷宜君，又陷葭州，僉事郭景嵩死之。 明年二月陷鄜州，兵備副使郭應響死之。 應響，福清人，萬歷丙午舉鄉試第一。 寧塞余賊來犯，應響禦之，斬賊常山虎等十五人。 至是，混天猴率眾夜突至，應響登北關，集士卒拒守，手殺三賊，力不支遂死。 事聞，贈光祿寺少卿，謚忠烈，予祭葬，蔭一子入監讀書。
By then the roving bandits were growing stronger daily; Governor-General Hong Chengchou raced from place to place in pursuit and scarcely had a moment's rest. Within a month they took Yijun and then Jiazhou; Vice Commissioner Guo Jingsong died defending the latter. The next year, in the second month, they captured Fuzhou; Assistant Military Commissioner Guo Yingxiang died in its defense. Yingxiang was a native of Fuqing and had placed first in the provincial examinations in the bingwu year of Wanli. When remnant bandits from Ningsai raided the district, Yingxiang drove them off and beheaded fifteen of them, including the outlaw Changshanhu. On this occasion Huntianhou led his men in a night assault; Yingxiang mounted the North Gate, rallied the garrison to hold the wall, and killed three bandits with his own hand before his strength failed and he fell. When word reached the court he was posthumously appointed Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Entertainments, given the posthumous name Loyal and Fierce, granted state sacrifices and burial, and one son was admitted to the Directorate of Education by yin privilege.
4
張光奎，澤州人。 仕至山東右參政。 崇禎五年，流賊躪山西，監司王肇生以便宜署歙人吳開先為將，使擊賊，戰澤州城西。 賊敗去，從沁水轉掠陽城。 開先恃勇渡沁，戰北留墩下，擊斬數百人，炮盡無援，一軍盡沒。 賊乃再犯澤州，光奎方里居，與兄守備光璽、千總劉自安等率眾固守八日，援兵不至，城陷，並死之。 澤，大州也，遠近為震動。 事聞，贈光祿卿，光璽等贈恤有差。
Zhang Guangkui came from Zezhou. He had risen to Right Administration Vice Commissioner of Shandong. In the fifth year of Chongzhen roving bandits swept through Shanxi; Supervising Secretary Wang Zhaosheng, exercising emergency powers, appointed Wu Kaixian of She county as a general and sent him against the bandits; the battle was fought west of Zezhou. The bandits were beaten off but then swung from Qinshui to raid Yangcheng. Kaixian, relying on his boldness, crossed the Qin and fought below Beiliudun, cutting down several hundred men; when his artillery was exhausted and no aid arrived, his entire command perished. The bandits then struck Zezhou again; Guangkui was at home on leave and, with his elder brother the garrison commander Guangxi, Company Commander Liu Zi'an, and others, led the townspeople in an eight-day defense; when no relief came the city fell and they all died together. Zezhou was a major prefecture, and the news sent shock through the region. When word reached the court Guangkui was posthumously made Director of the Court of Imperial Entertainments; Guangxi and the others received posthumous honors and relief in varying degrees.
5
是歲，紫金梁等寇遼州，裏居行人楊於楷與主事張友程，佐知州信陽李呈章拒守，力屈城陷，於楷被執，罵賊死。 呈章、友程及舉人趙一亨、侯標並死之。 明年六月，賊陷和順，裏居昌平副使樂濟眾被傷，不屈，投井死。 贈於楷光祿少卿，濟眾太仆少卿。 有徐明揚者，浮梁人，由選貢生為平順知縣。 六年四月，賊來犯，設策守禦，城破不屈死。
That year Zijinliang and others attacked Liaozhou; the retired Ministry of Rites messenger Yang Yukai and Principal Secretary Zhang Youcheng aided Magistrate Li Chengzhang of Xinyang in holding the city; when resistance collapsed Yukai was captured and died reviling the bandits. Chengzhang, Youcheng, and the licentiates Zhao Yiheng and Hou Biao likewise perished. The following sixth month the bandits took Heshun; the retired Changping Vice Commissioner Yue Jizhong was wounded, would not yield, and drowned himself in a well. Yukai was posthumously made Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Entertainments; Jizhong was made Vice Director of the Court of the Imperial Stud. There was also Xu Mingyang of Fuliang, who entered office as magistrate of Pingshun through the tribute-student route. In the fourth month of the sixth year the bandits attacked; he devised a defense, and when the city fell he would not submit and was killed.
6
李中正，盧氏人。 萬歷末，舉會試，以天啟二年赴廷對，授承天府推官，遷兵部主事。 崇禎初，謝病歸。 六年，群盜大亂河北。 其冬，乘冰渡河，遂由澠池犯盧氏。 中州承平久，不設備。 驟聞賊至，吏民惶駴，知縣金會嘉棄城遁。 十二月，賊入城，中正勒家眾及里中壯士奮擊，眾寡不敵，力戰死。 賊縱掠城中，執舉人靳謙書，使跪，不屈，大罵而死。
Li Zhongzheng came from Lushi. Near the end of Wanli he passed the metropolitan examination; in the second year of Tianqi he took the palace examination and was appointed investigating censor of Chengtian Prefecture, later transferring to the Ministry of War as a principal secretary. Early in the Chongzhen reign he retired on grounds of illness. In the sixth year bandit armies threw Hebei into turmoil. That winter they crossed the frozen river and advanced through Mianchi against Lushi. The central provinces had known peace for so long that they were wholly unprepared. At the sudden news of the bandits, officials and townspeople panicked; Magistrate Jin Huijia abandoned the city and fled. In the twelfth month the bandits entered the city; Zhongzheng rallied his household and local stalwarts to fight back; outnumbered, he battled until he was killed. The bandits looted the city at will; they seized the licentiate Jin Qianshu and forced him to kneel; he refused and died after hurling abuse at them.
7
賊以是冬始入河南，自是屢陷名城，殺將吏無算，鄉官舉貢多被難。 其宜陽馬足輕，靈寶許煇，新安劉君培、馬山、李登英，偃師裴君合，陜州張我正、張我德，孟津孫挺生，嵩縣傅世濟、李佩玉，上蔡劉時寵輩，則先後以布衣抗節顯。
The bandits first entered Henan that winter; from then on they repeatedly captured great cities and killed commanders and officials beyond count, while many local gentry and licentiates perished in the turmoil. Among them were Ma Zuqing of Yiyang, Xu Hui of Lingbao, Liu Junpei, Ma Shan, and Li Dengying of Xin'an, Pei Junhe of Yanshi, Zhang Wozheng and Zhang Wode of Shaanzhou, Sun Tingsheng of Mengjin, Fu Shiji and Li Peiyu of Song county, and Liu Shichong of Shangcai—each in turn distinguished himself as a commoner who held fast to his principles.
8
足輕，性孝友。 弟惑婦言，迫分產，乃取田磽薄者自予。 萬歷末，歲大兇，出粟六百石以振，焚券千余。 崇禎六年冬，流賊渡河而南，挈家避之石龍崖。 三女皆殊色，慮賊汙，悉投崖死。 足輕被執，厲聲大罵。 賊怒，並三子殺之。 家眾皆遇害，惟存次子駿一人，後登鄉薦。 煇為縣陰陽官，為賊所掠，大罵見殺。
Zuqing was known for filial devotion and brotherly affection. When his younger brother, swayed by his wife, pressed for a division of the family property, Zuqing kept only the poorer, thinner fields for himself. Near the end of Wanli, in a year of terrible famine, he gave out six hundred dan of grain for relief and burned more than a thousand IOUs. In the winter of the sixth year of Chongzhen the roving bandits crossed the river heading south, and he took his family to shelter at Shilong Cliff. His three daughters were all exceptionally beautiful; fearing defilement by the bandits, they threw themselves from the cliff and died. Zuqing was captured and hurled abuse at them in a thunderous voice. Enraged, the bandits killed him along with his three sons. The whole household was killed; only his second son, Jun, survived and later passed the provincial examinations. Hui served as the county yin-yang official; when the bandits seized him he cursed them loudly and was killed.
9
君培有義行，攜子及從孫避難，道遇賊，欲殺其從孫。 君培曰：「我尚有男，此子乃遺孤，幸舍之而殺我。 」賊如其言，二子獲免。
Junpei was known for righteous conduct; while fleeing with his son and a younger nephew he met bandits on the road who meant to kill the boy. Junpei said, "I still have a grown son; this child is an orphan. Please spare him and kill me instead." The bandits did as he asked, and both boys were spared.
10
山性剛直，土寇於大中陷新安，獲山，使負米。 叱曰：「我天朝百性，肯為賊負米邪！ 」大罵而死。 登英亦以罵賊死。
Shan was stern and upright; when Yu Dazhong's local bandits took Xin'an they seized Shan and forced him to carry grain. He shouted, "I am a subject of the celestial dynasty—would I carry grain for bandits?" He cursed them fiercely and was killed. Dengying likewise died after reviling the bandits.
11
君合幼孤，母苦節，孝養惟謹。 賊至，聚眾保沙岸寨。 攻圍十晝夜不克，說之降，大罵不從。 寨破，被磔。
Junhe lost his father in childhood; his mother lived in chaste widowhood, and he supported her with scrupulous filial devotion. When the bandits came he rallied the people to hold Sha'an Stockade. After ten days and nights of siege they could not take it; when they urged him to surrender he cursed them and refused. When the stockade fell he was dismembered alive.
12
我正素豪俠，集眾保鄉里，一方賴之。 十四年勒眾禦賊，馘三人。 俄賊大至，眾悉奔，奮臂獨戰。 賊愛其男，欲生致之，詬罵自刎死。 我德知賊至，恐妻子受辱，驅一家二十七人登樓自焚。
Wozheng had long been bold and chivalrous; he gathered men to defend the countryside, and the whole region relied on him. In the fourteenth year he led the people against the bandits and cut off three heads. Soon a great bandit force arrived; everyone fled, but he fought on alone. The bandits admired his courage and wished to take him alive; he reviled them and cut his own throat. Wode, learning that bandits were coming and fearing dishonor for his wife and children, led twenty-seven members of his household upstairs and burned themselves together.
13
挺生精星術，預卜十五年有寇禍，編茅河渚以居。 賊蹤跡得之，語其妻梁氏曰：「此匹夫徇義之秋也。 」夫婦對泣，詬賊而死。 世濟與兄世舟並為土寇於大中所執，將殺之。 兄弟相抱泣，賊議釋其一，世濟即奪賊刀自殺，世舟獲免。
Tingsheng was skilled in astrology and foretold raiders fifteen years in advance; he built a thatched hut on a river islet and lived there. When the bandits tracked him down he told his wife, Lady Liang, "This is the moment for an ordinary man to die for righteousness." Husband and wife wept face to face, cursed the bandits, and died. Shiji and his elder brother Shizhou were both seized by Yu Dazhong's local bandits and were about to be executed. The brothers embraced and wept; when the bandits debated releasing one of them, Shiji seized a bandit's knife and killed himself, and Shizhou was spared.
14
佩玉者，御史興元孫也。 崇禎末，中州盡殘，佩玉結遺民捍鄉井，與鄰寨相掎角，往往尾賊後，奪其輜重。 賊憚之，不敢出其境。 後大舉圍別寨，佩玉往救，力戰而死，裏人聚哭之。
Peiyu was the grandson of Censor Xingyuan. By the end of Chongzhen the central provinces lay in ruins; Peiyu rallied survivors to defend the villages, coordinated with neighboring stockades, and often trailed the bandits to seize their baggage trains. The bandits feared him and dared not leave his territory. Later, when the bandits besieged another stockade in force, Peiyu went to its relief, fought to the death, and the townspeople gathered to mourn him.
15
時寵有孝行。 賊陷城，其父宗祀以年老不能行，命之速避，遂自殺。 時寵慟哭，刺殺一子、三女，夫婦並自剄。 其妹適歸寧，亦從死，一家死者八人。
Shichong was known for filial devotion. When the bandits took the city, his father Zongsi, too old to flee, urged him to escape at once and then killed himself. Shichong wept in anguish, stabbed one son and three daughters, and he and his wife cut their throats together. His sister, who had come home on a visit, died with them as well; eight members of the family perished.
16
方國儒，字道醇，歙縣人。 四歲失父，奉母以孝聞。 天啟元年舉於鄉。 崇禎間，授保康知縣。 流賊大入湖廣，將吏率望風先奔。 保康小邑素無兵，七年正月賊至，國儒急率鄉兵出禦，力不支，城遂陷。 亡何，賊退，國儒還入城。 逾月復至，督吏民固拒。 賊至益眾，復陷。 國儒官服坐堂上，被執大罵，身中七刃死。
Fang Guoru, whose courtesy name was Daochun, came from She county. He lost his father at the age of four and became known for supporting his mother with filial devotion. In the first year of Tianqi he passed the provincial examinations. During the Chongzhen reign he was appointed magistrate of Baokang. When roving bandits poured into Huguang, commanders and officials for the most part fled at the first rumor of their approach. Baokang was a small district with scarcely any troops; in the first month of the seventh year the bandits arrived, and Guoru hastily led the militia out to resist, but when his strength failed the city fell. Before long the bandits withdrew, and Guoru returned to the city. A month later they returned, and he urged officials and townspeople to hold firm. The bandits came in greater numbers, and the city fell again. Guoru sat on the dais in his official robes; seized by the bandits, he cursed them loudly and died after seven blade wounds.
17
賊陷竹谿，訓導王紹正死之。 谷城舉人常存畏會試赴京，道遇賊，欲劫為首領，罵不絕口死。 他賊犯興山，知縣劉定國堅守。 城將陷，遣吏懷印送上官，罵賊死。
When the bandits took Zhuxi, Instructor Wang Shaozheng died in its defense. Chang Cunwei, a licentiate of Gucheng traveling to the capital for the metropolitan examination, met bandits on the road who wished to make him their leader; he cursed them without cease and was killed. Other bandits attacked Xingshan, and Magistrate Liu Dingguo held the city firm. As the city was about to fall he sent an official bearing the seal to his superiors and died cursing the bandits.
18
何承光，貴州鎮遠人。 萬歷四十年舉於鄉。 崇禎中，歷夔州同知。 七年二月，賊由荊州入夔門，犯夔州。 副使周士登在涪州，城中倉猝無備，通判、推官、知縣悉遁。 承光攝府事，率吏民固守，力竭城陷。 承光整冠帶危坐，賊入殺之，投屍於江。 事聞，贈承光夔州知府。
He Chengguang came from Zhenyuan in Guizhou. In the fortieth year of Wanli he passed the provincial examinations. During the Chongzhen reign he served as Assistant Prefect of Kuizhou. In the second month of the seventh year the bandits entered through Jingzhou and the Kuimen and attacked Kuizhou. Vice Commissioner Zhou Shideng was at Fuzhou; the city was caught unprepared, and the subprefect, investigating censor, and magistrate all fled. Chengguang took charge of the prefecture, led officials and townspeople in a stubborn defense, and when their strength failed the city fell. Chengguang straightened his hat and sash and sat upright; when the bandits entered they killed him and cast his body into the river. When word reached the court, Chengguang was posthumously appointed Prefect of Kuizhou.
19
自賊起陜西，轉寇山西、畿輔、河南、北及湖廣、四川，陷州縣以數十許，未有破大郡者，至是天下為震動。
Since the bandits had risen in Shaanxi and turned to raid Shanxi, the capital region, Henan, and then Huguang and Sichuan, they had taken dozens of prefectures and districts but had not yet breached a major commandery—now the whole realm was shaken.
20
其他部自漢中犯大寧，知縣高日臨見勢弱不能守，嚙指書牒乞援上官，率眾禦之北門。 兵敗被執，大罵不屈，賊碎其體焚之。 訓導高錫及妻女，巡檢陳國俊及妻，皆遇害。 日臨，字儼若，鄱陽恩貢生。
Another band entered from Hanzhong against Daning; Magistrate Gao Rilin, seeing that the force was too weak to hold the city, bit his finger and wrote a dispatch begging aid from his superiors, then led the people to defend the North Gate. Defeated and captured, he cursed them and refused to submit; the bandits shattered his body and burned it. Instructor Gao Xi and his wife and daughters, and Patrol Inspector Chen Guojun and his wife, all perished. Rilin, whose courtesy name was Yanruo, was a favored tribute student from Poyang.
21
賊陷夔州，他賊即以次日陷巫山，通江巡檢郭纘化陣沒，通江指揮王永年力戰死。 至四月，守備郭震辰、指揮田實擊賊百丈關，兵敗被執，罵賊死。
When the bandits took Kuizhou, another band took Wushan the very next day; Tongjiang Patrol Inspector Guo Xuanhua fell in battle, and Tongjiang Commander Wang Yongnian died fighting to the end. By the fourth month Garrison Commander Guo Zhenchen and Commander Tian Shi attacked the bandits at Baizhang Pass; defeated and captured, they died cursing the bandits.
22
龐瑜，字堅白，公安人。 家貧，躬耕自給。 夏轉水灌田，執書從牛後，朗誦不輟。 由歲貢生授京山訓導。 崇禎七年擢陜西崇信知縣。 縣無城，兵荒，貧民止百余戶。 瑜知賊必至，言於監司陸夢龍，以無兵辭。 瑜集士民築土垣以守，流涕誓死職。 閏八月天大雨，土垣盡圮。 賊掩至，瑜急解印遣家人賫送上官，端坐堂上以待。 賊至，捽令跪。 瑜罵曰：「賊奴敢辱官長！ 」拔刀脅之，罵益厲。 賊掠城中無所有，執至野外，剖心裂屍而去。 贈固原知州。
Pang Yu, whose courtesy name was Jianbai, came from Gong'an. His family was poor, and he supported himself by farming with his own hands. In summer, when the turning water irrigated the fields, he would walk behind the ox with a book in hand, reciting aloud without pause. Through the annual tribute-student route he was appointed Instructor of Jingshan. In the seventh year of Chongzhen he was promoted to magistrate of Chongxin in Shaanxi. The district had no city wall, had been ravaged by war, and had barely more than a hundred poor households. Yu knew the bandits were sure to come and told Supervising Secretary Lu Menglong, declining the post on grounds that he had no troops. Xu Shangqing was from Nanping. He passed the provincial examinations and served as magistrate of Jianzhou. In the tenth month of Chongzhen 10, Li Zicheng, Hui Dengxiang, and others invaded Sichuan with an army of several hundred thousand men. Ruan Zhitian, styled Shifu, was a licentiate from Tongcheng. During the Chongzhen reign the throne issued an edict soliciting recommendations of able men. Liu Ruozai, instructor of the same prefecture, nominated Zhitian, who was then appointed magistrate of Gucheng. In the first month of the eleventh year, before Zhitian had even taken up his post, Zhang Xianzhong stormed and seized the city and occupied it while demanding surrender on terms. Grand Secretary Xiong Wen'can agreed to the terms and encamped tens of thousands of rebels in the outskirts, while the townspeople panicked and prepared to flee. When Zhitian arrived, he worked tirelessly to keep order until the people regained some calm, then memorialized the throne: "Xianzhong sits like a tiger in our county seat, and his intentions are impossible to read. The ground he demands lies on the vital route by which troops and supplies move — the strategic junction where the Qin and Shu roads meet — and he now holds it all. The wicked willingly serve him, while honest people are all forced into compliance. I am an official charged with guarding the land and caring for the people, yet I have no land left to guard and no people left to govern. The treasury is bare, the people's goods have been seized, and there is no revenue left to collect. I bear the title of county magistrate, but in truth I am little more than a figurehead. Yet at court they insist on pacification alone. I venture to say that pacification and suppression are two sides of one policy and must not be treated as separate courses, for to do so damages the state's prestige and breaks the army's spirit. At the time, however, his advice was not heeded. Rebel bands increasingly ranged into the countryside to plunder. Zhitian would seize the culprits and report them to their camp officers, who meted out minor punishments under military law. When he reported them again, no one responded, saying, "The authorities simply withhold our pay. Once we are paid, the raids will stop. Villagers fled until almost none remained, and the looting soon reached the market districts of the town. Any resistance at all was met with drawn blades; people died every day, and the entire city was thrown into chaos. Army superintendent and vice commissioner Zhang Dajing was sent by Wen'can to restore order, but he too proved unable to check them.

The following year Xianzhong's intent to rebel became increasingly plain. Zhitian went to remonstrate with him: "General, your earlier conduct was deeply wrong, but you are now fortunate enough to serve the throne. You should take the field, win distinction, and leave your name in the histories. Have you not seen what became of General Liu Guoneng? In the fifth month Xianzhong rebelled as expected, plundered the treasury, freed the prisoners, and tore down the city walls. In the spring of the sixth year rebels attacked Wu'an. Garrison commander Cao Ming'e was killed in battle, and the rebels then pressed on to Huixian. Kejian mounted the walls and held firm. The jailers also accepted large bribes from the rebels. Security was completely relaxed, and Du'ao and the others cast off their shackles and drank freely. Sichang sent a dispatch warning him. Chengzeng laughed and said, "Can they fly here? When the crisis came, Du'ao did rise from within the jail. Chengzeng led his men and fought their way out through the gate in flight. When the report reached the court, an order was issued for his arrest and punishment. Henan was also in turmoil at the time. Pursued for a long time, he was never captured, and his ultimate fate is unknown.
70
徐世淳，字中明，秀水人。 父必達，字德夫，萬歷二十年進士。 知溧水縣，築石臼湖堤，奏除齊泰姻戚子孫軍籍二十六家。 累遷吏部考功郎中，與吏科給事中儲純臣同領察事。 純臣受贓吏賕，當大計日，必達進狀請黜純臣，面揖之退，一座大驚。 遷光祿丞，陳白糧利弊十一事，悉允行。 進少卿，巡漕御史孫居相以船壞不治，請雇民船濟運，必達爭止之。 天啟初，以右僉都御史督操江軍。 白蓮賊將窺徐州，必達募銳卒會山東兵擊破之。 遷兵部右侍郎，以拾遺罷歸，卒。
Xu Shichun, courtesy name Zhongming, was a native of Xiushui. His father Bida, courtesy name Defu, passed the metropolitan examination in the twentieth year of the Wanli reign. As magistrate of Lishui County he built the Shijiu Lake embankment and memorialized to remove twenty-six families descended from Qi Tai's affinal kin from the military register. He rose through successive appointments to Director of the Ministry of Personnel's Appraisal Section and, together with Chu Chunchen, supervising secretary of the Personnel Section, jointly conducted the triennial evaluation of officials. Chunchen had accepted bribes from corrupt officials. On the day of the major evaluation Bida submitted a memorial requesting Chunchen's dismissal, bowed to him in person, and withdrew. The entire assembly was stunned. He was promoted to Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Entertainments and memorialized eleven points on the pros and cons of white-grain transport; the court approved them all. He rose to Vice Director. When Grain-Transport Censor Sun Juxiang, finding the official boats damaged and unrepaired, proposed hiring civilian vessels to keep the grain moving, Bida argued against it and blocked the plan. Early in the Tianqi reign he was appointed Right Vice Censor-in-Chief to supervise the Caojiang army. When White Lotus bandit generals threatened Xuzhou, Bida recruited crack troops, joined forces with the Shandong army, and routed them. He was transferred to Right Vice Minister of War, but after submitting a remonstrance memorial he was dismissed and returned home, where he later died.
71
世淳，崇禎中舉人。 十三年冬，歷隨州知州。 州嘗被賊，居民蕭然。 世淳知賊必復至，集士民誓以死守。 會歲大荒，士多就食粥廠，嘆曰：「可使士以餒失禮乎？ 」分粟振之。 潰兵過隨索餉，世淳授兵登陴，而單騎入見軍帥曰：「軍食不供，有司罪也。 殺我足矣，請械我以見督師。 」帥氣奪，斂眾去。
Shichun passed the provincial examinations during the Chongzhen reign. In the winter of the thirteenth year he was appointed magistrate of Suizhou. The prefecture had already been ravaged by bandits, and the people were in dire straits. Shichun knew the bandits would return; he rallied gentry and townspeople and swore to hold the city to the death. That year brought a terrible famine, and many scholars went to the public gruel kitchens. He sighed and said, "Can we let scholars lose their dignity to hunger? " He distributed grain to sustain them. Routed troops passing through Suizhou demanded pay. Shichun armed the militia and sent them to the walls, then rode alone to the army commander and said, "If the army lacks provisions, that is the officials' fault. Killing me is enough. Please bind me and take me before the supervising commander. " The commander lost his nerve, gathered his men, and withdrew.
72
明年三月，張獻忠自襄陽來犯，世淳寢食南城譙樓，曉夜固守，告急於巡撫宋一鶴。 一鶴遣兵來援，為監司守承天者邀去。 守月余，援絕力窮，賊急攻南城，而潛兵墮北城以入。 世淳命子肇梁{艹}印廨後，勒馬巷戰，矢貫頤，耳鼻橫斷，墜馬，亂刃斫死。 肇梁奔赴，且哭且罵，賊將殺之，呼州人告以{艹}印處，乃死。 世淳妾趙、王及臧獲十八人皆死。 後贈太仆少卿，建祠，以肇梁祔。
The following third month Zhang Xianzhong marched from Xiangyang against the city; Shichun ate and slept in the south gate watchtower, defended day and night, and sent urgent appeals to Grand Coordinator Song Yihe. Yihe dispatched relief troops, but they were diverted by the supervising official charged with defending Chengtian. After more than a month of defense, relief failed and their strength gave out; the bandits stormed the south gate while secretly lowering men over the north wall to enter the city. Shichun ordered his son Zhaoliang to bury the seal behind the yamen, then charged into the lanes on horseback; an arrow pierced his cheek, his nose and ears were cut away, he fell from his horse, and was hacked to death. Zhaoliang rushed forward, weeping and cursing; as the bandit leader was about to kill him, he called out to the townspeople to reveal where the seal was buried, and then died. Shichun's concubines Zhao and Wang and eighteen household servants all perished. Later he was posthumously made Vice Director of the Court of the Imperial Stud; a shrine was erected and Zhaoliang was worshiped beside him.
73
隨自十年正月陷，及是再陷，至七月復陷，判官余塙死焉。 三陷之後，城中幾無孑遺。
Suizhou had first fallen in the first month of the tenth year; it fell again on this occasion and once more in the seventh month, when Vice Magistrate Yu Qian died. After falling three times, the city was left with scarcely a survivor.
