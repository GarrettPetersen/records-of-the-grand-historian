1
曹文詔 〈 （弟文耀）〉 周遇吉黃得功
Cao Wenzhao [ (his younger brother Wenyao)] Zhou Yuji and Huang Degong
2
曹文詔，大同人。 勇毅有智略。 從軍遼左，歷事熊廷弼、孫承宗，積功至遊擊。 崇禎二年冬，從袁崇煥入衛京師。 明年二月，總理馬世龍畀所賜尚方劍，令率參將王承胤、張叔嘉、都司左良玉等伏玉田、枯樹、洪橋，鏖戰有功，遷參將。 自大塹山轉戰逼遵化，又從世龍等克大安城及鮎魚諸關。 以與復四城功，加都督僉事。 七月，陜西賊熾，擢延綏東路副總兵。
Cao Wenzhao was a native of Datong. He was brave, resolute, and strategically gifted. He entered military service in eastern Liaodong, served under Xiong Tingbi and Sun Chengzong, and through accumulated merit was promoted to roaming striker. In the winter of the second year of the Chongzhen reign, he followed Yuan Chonghuan to the defense of the capital. The following February, supreme commander Ma Shilong entrusted him with the imperial sword he had received and ordered him to lead deputy generals Wang Chengyin, Zhang Shujia, bureau commander Zuo Liangyu, and others in ambush at Yutian, Kushu, and Hongqiao. After fierce fighting with notable success, he was promoted to deputy general. He fought his way from Dachan Mountain toward Zunhua, then joined Shilong and others in capturing Da'an City and the Nianyu passes. For his part in recovering the four cities, he was promoted to vice commissioner-in-chief. In the seventh month, as rebellion in Shaanxi intensified, he was appointed deputy regional commander of the eastern Yan'an route.
3
賊渠王嘉胤久據河曲。 四年四月，文詔克其城。 嘉胤脫走，轉掠至陽城南山。 文詔追及之，其下斬以降，以功擢臨洮總兵官。
The rebel leader Wang Jiayin had long held Hequ. In the fourth month of the fourth year, Wenzhao took the city. Jiayin broke free and ranged south as far as the Yangcheng hills. Wenzhao caught up with him; his followers killed him and submitted. For this achievement Wenzhao was appointed regional commander of Lintao.
4
點燈子自陜入山西。 文詔追之，及於稷山，諭降七百人。 點燈子遁，尋被獲，伏誅。
Lamp-Lighter crossed from Shaanxi into Shanxi. Wenzhao pursued him to Jishan and induced seven hundred men to surrender. Lamp-Lighter escaped but was soon captured and put to death.
5
李老柴、獨行狼陷中部，巡撫練國事、延綏總兵王承恩圍之。 五月，慶陽賊郝臨庵、劉道江援之。 會文詔西旋，與榆林參政張福臻合剿，馘老柴及其黨一條龍，余黨奔摩雲谷。 副將張弘業、遊擊李明輔戰死。 文詔乃與遊擊左光先、崔宗蔭、李國奇分剿綏德、宜君、清澗、米脂賊，戰懷寧川、黑泉峪、封家溝、綿湖峪，皆大捷，掃地王授首。
Li Laochai and Lone Wolf overran Zhongbu; Grand Coordinator Lian Guoshi and Yan'an regional commander Wang Chengen laid siege to them. In the fifth month, the Qingyang rebels Hao Lin'an and Liu Daojiang marched to relieve them. Wenzhao was then returning westward; he joined Yulin administrator Zhang Fuzhen in a joint campaign, killed Laochai and his follower One Dragon, and drove the rest toward Moyun Valley. Deputy general Zhang Hongye and roaming striker Li Mingfu were killed in action. Wenzhao then divided forces with roaming strikers Zuo Guangxian, Cui Zongyin, and Li Guoqi to hunt rebels in Suide, Yijun, Qingjian, and Mizhi. Battles at Huaining River, Heiquan Pass, Fengjiagou, and Mianhu Valley all ended in decisive victories, and Sweeps-the-Earth King was beheaded.
6
紅軍友、李都司、杜三、楊老柴者，神一魁余黨也，屯鎮原，將犯平涼。 國事檄甘肅總兵楊嘉謨、副將王性善扼之，賊走慶陽。 文詔從鄜州間道與嘉謨、性善合。 五年三月，大戰西濠，斬千級，生擒杜三、楊老柴。 余黨糾他賊掠武安監，陷華亭，攻莊浪。 文詔、嘉謨至，賊屯張麻村。 官軍掩擊，賊走高山。 遊擊曹變蛟、馮舉、劉成功、平安等噪而上，賊潰走。 變蛟者，文詔從子也。 會性善及甘肅副將李鴻嗣、參將莫與京等至，共擊斬五百二十余級。 追敗之鹹寧關，又敗之關上嶺。 追至隴安，嘉謨、變蛟夾擊，復敗之。 賊余眾數千欲走漢南，為遊擊趙光遠所遏，乃由長寧驛走張家川。 其逸出清水者，副將蔣一陽遇之敗，都司李宮用被執。 文詔乃縱反間，紿其黨，殺紅軍友，遂蹙敗之水落城。 追至靜寧州，賊奔據唐毛山，變蛟先登，殄其眾。
Red Army Friend, Li Dusi, Du Three, and Yang Laochai were survivors of Shen Yikui's faction. They held Zhenyuan and were preparing to strike Pingliang. Guoshi ordered Gansu regional commander Yang Jiamo and deputy general Wang Xingshan to intercept them, and the rebels retreated toward Qingyang. Wenzhao took a hidden route from Fuzhou and united with Jiamo and Xingshan. In the third month of the fifth year they fought a major battle at Ximao, killing a thousand rebels and taking Du Three and Yang Laochai alive. The survivors rallied other rebels, raided Wu'an Post, overran Huating, and attacked Zhuanglang. When Wenzhao and Jiamo arrived, the rebels were encamped at Zhangma Village. Government forces struck by surprise, and the rebels fled into the high mountains. Roaming strikers Cao Bianjiao, Feng Ju, Liu Chenggong, Ping An, and others charged uphill with a roar, and the rebels broke and fled. Bianjiao was Wenzhao's nephew. When Xingshan arrived with Gansu deputy general Li Hongsi, deputy general Mo Yujing, and others, the combined force killed more than five hundred twenty rebels. They pursued and routed them at Xianning Pass and again at Guanshang Ridge. The pursuit reached Long'an, where Jiamo and Bianjiao attacked from both flanks and defeated them again. Several thousand survivors tried to flee south into Han territory but were blocked by roaming striker Zhao Guangyuan and turned instead toward Zhangjiachuan by way of Changning Post. Those who broke out at Qingshui were defeated by deputy general Jiang Yiyang, and bureau commander Li Gongyong was taken prisoner. Wenzhao then used counter-intelligence to deceive the rebel ranks, had Red Army Friend killed, and drove the rest to defeat at Shuiluo City. The pursuit reached Jingning Prefecture, where the rebels fled to Tangmao Mountain. Bianjiao was first up the slope and wiped out their force.
7
可天飛、郝臨庵，劉道江為王承恩所敗，退保鐵角城。 獨行狼、李都司走與合，可天飛、劉道江遂圍合水。 文詔往救。 賊匿精銳，以千騎逆戰，誘抵南原，伏大起。 城上人言曹將軍已歿。 文詔持矛左右突，匹馬縈萬眾中。 諸軍望見，夾擊，賊大敗，僵屍蔽野，余走銅川橋。 文詔率變蛟、舉、嘉謨及參將方茂功等追及之，大戰陷陣，賊復大敗。 尋與寧夏總兵賀虎臣、固原總兵楊麒破賊甘泉之虎兕凹。 麒復追賊安口河、崇信窯、白茅山，皆大獲。 總督洪承疇斬可天飛、李都司於平涼，降其將白廣恩，余賊分竄。 文詔追擊之隴州、平、鳳間。 十月三戰三敗之，遂蹙賊耀州錐子山，其黨殺獨行狼、郝臨庵以降。 承疇戮四百人，余散遣。 關中巨寇略平。
Ke Tianfei, Hao Lin'an, and Liu Daojiang were beaten by Wang Chengen and fell back to defend Tie'ao City. Lone Wolf and Li Dusi fled to join them, after which Ke Tianfei and Liu Daojiang laid siege to Heshui. Wenzhao marched to relieve the city. The rebels concealed their best troops, met him with a thousand horsemen, lured him onto the southern plain, and then sprang a massive ambush. Men on the city walls cried that General Cao was already dead. Wenzhao charged left and right with his spear, a lone rider wheeling through a sea of enemies. The troops saw him and attacked from both flanks. The rebels were routed, the field strewn with corpses, and the survivors fled toward Tongchuan Bridge. Wenzhao led Bianjiao, Ju, Jiamo, deputy general Fang Maogong, and others in pursuit, smashed into their ranks, and routed them again. Soon afterward he joined Ningxia regional commander He Huchen and Guyuan regional commander Yang Qi in defeating the rebels at Husi'ao in Ganquan. Qi pursued them again at Ankou River, Chongxin Kiln, and Baimao Mountain, winning major victories each time. Grand Coordinator Hong Chengchou executed Ke Tianfei and Li Dusi at Pingliang, accepted the surrender of their general Bai Guang'en, and the remaining rebels scattered. Wenzhao pursued them through the country between Longzhou, Pingliang, and Fengxiang. In the tenth month he fought three battles and won three victories, then cornered the rebels on Zuizi Mountain in Yaozhou, where their followers killed Lone Wolf and Hao Lin'an and submitted. Chengchou executed four hundred men and released the rest. The great rebel bands of Guanzhong were largely subdued.
8
巡撫御史範復粹匯奏首功凡三萬六千六百有奇，文詔功第一，嘉謨次之，承恩、麒又次之。 文詔在陜西，大小數十戰，功最多，承疇不為敘。 巡按御史吳甡推獎甚至，復粹疏復上。 兵部抑其功，卒不敘。
Grand Coordinator Censor Fan Fucui reported a combined tally of more than thirty-six thousand six hundred enemy heads. Wenzhao ranked first in merit, Jiamo second, and Chengen and Qi after them. In Shaanxi Wenzhao fought several dozen engagements and won the greatest share of merit, yet Chengchou did not report his achievements. Investigating Censor Wu Shen praised him at length, and Fucui submitted another memorial on his behalf. The Ministry of War downplayed his achievements, and in the end he received no formal recognition.
9
當是時，賊見陜兵盛，多流入山西，其魁紫金梁、混世王、姬關鎖、八大王、曹操、闖塌天、興加哈利七大部，多者萬人，少亦半之，蹂躪汾州、太原、平陽。 御史張宸極言：「賊自秦中來。 秦將曹文詔威名宿著，士民為之謠曰『軍中有一曹，西賊聞之心膽搖』。 且嘗立功晉中，而秦賊滅且盡。 宜敕令入晉協剿。 」於是命陜西、山西諸將並受文詔節制。
At this time the rebels, finding Shaanxi heavily garrisoned, poured into Shanxi in seven major bands led by Purple-Gold Beam, World-Ruiner King, Ji Gate-Lock, Eight Great King, Cao Cao, Rush-Collapse Heaven, and Xing Jia Hali. The largest numbered ten thousand men and the smallest half that, and they ravaged Fenzhou, Taiyuan, and Pingyang. Censor Zhang Chen said, "These rebels come from Qin territory. The Qin general Cao Wenzhao has long been famous, and soldiers and civilians sing of him: 'In the army there is a Cao; when western rebels hear his name, their hearts turn to water.' He has already won distinction in Jin, and the rebels of Qin have been nearly wiped out. He should be ordered into Jin to join the suppression campaign. " The court then ordered all generals of Shaanxi and Shanxi to serve under Wenzhao's command.
10
六年正月抵霍州，敗賊汾河、盂系，追及於壽陽。 巡撫許鼎臣遣謀士張宰先大軍嘗賊，賊驚潰。 二月，文詔追擊之，斬混世王於碧霞村。 余黨為猛如虎逐走，遇文詔兵方山，復敗。 五臺、盂、定襄、壽陽賊盡平。 鼎臣命文詔軍平定，備太原東，張應昌軍汾州，備太原西。 文詔連敗賊太谷、範村、榆社，太原賊幾盡。
In the first month of the sixth year he reached Huozhou, defeated the rebels along the Fen River and in the Yu region, and overtook them at Shouyang. Grand Coordinator Xu Dingchen sent strategist Zhang Zai ahead of the main army to test the rebels, who broke and fled in alarm. In the second month Wenzhao pursued them and killed World-Ruiner King at Bixia Village. The survivors were driven off by Meng Ruhu, met Wenzhao's troops at Fangshan, and were defeated again. The rebels of Wutai, Yu, Dingxiang, and Shouyang were all subdued. Dingchen stationed Wenzhao's army at Pingding to guard eastern Taiyuan and Zhang Yingchang's army at Fenzhou to guard the west. Wenzhao defeated the rebels in succession at Taigu, Fancun, and Yushe, and the rebels around Taiyuan were nearly eliminated.
11
帝以文詔功多，敕所過地多積糗糧以犒，並敕文詔速平賊。 山西監視中官劉中允言文詔剿賊徐溝、盂、定襄，所司不給米，反以炮石傷士卒。 帝即下御史按問。 三月，賊從河內上太行，文詔大敗之澤州。 賊走潞安，文詔至陽城遇賊不擊，自沁水潛師，還擊之芹地、劉村寨，斬首千余。 四月，賊屯潤城，其他部陷平順，殺知縣徐明揚。 文詔至，賊走，乃夜半襲潤城，斬賊千五百。 紫金梁、老回回自榆社走武鄉，過天星走高澤山，文詔皆擊敗。 他賊圍涉縣，聞文詔破賊黎城，解去。
Because Wenzhao's achievements were so great, the emperor ordered the districts he passed through to stock dried provisions for his troops and commanded him to pacify the rebels quickly. Shanxi supervising eunuch Liu Zhongyun reported that while Wenzhao was suppressing rebels at Xugou, Yu, and Dingxiang, local officials failed to supply grain and even injured his soldiers with artillery. The emperor immediately dispatched censors to investigate. In the third month the rebels climbed the Taihang from Henei, and Wenzhao routed them at Zezhou. The rebels fled to Lu'an. Wenzhao reached Yangcheng, met them but held back, then moved troops secretly from Qinshui and struck them at Qindi and Liucunzhai, killing more than a thousand. In the fourth month the rebels encamped at Runcheng while another band overran Pingshun and killed magistrate Xu Mingyang. When Wenzhao arrived the rebels fled, so he raided Runcheng at midnight and killed fifteen hundred of them. Purple-Gold Beam and Old Huihui fled from Yushe toward Wuxiang, Crossing Heaven Star toward Gaozeshan, and Wenzhao defeated them all. Other rebels were besieging She County, but when they heard that Wenzhao had defeated a rebel force at Licheng, they raised the siege and withdrew.
12
五月，帝遣中官孫茂霖為文詔內中軍。 賊犯沁水，文詔大敗之，擒其魁大虎，又敗之遼城毛嶺山西。 賊既屢敗，乃避文詔鋒，多流入河北。 帝乃命文詔移師往討。 而賊已敗鄧玘兵於林縣，文詔率五營軍夜襲破之。 七月大敗懷慶賊柴陵村，馘其魁滾地龍，又追斬老回回於濟源。
In the fifth month the emperor appointed eunuch Sun Maolin as Wenzhao's internal army commander. When the rebels attacked Qinshui, Wenzhao routed them, captured their leader Great Tiger, and defeated them again west of Liaocheng Maoling. After repeated defeats the rebels avoided Wenzhao's spearhead and poured into Hebei. The emperor then ordered Wenzhao to march into Hebei against them. The rebels had already defeated Deng Yin's force at Lin County, but Wenzhao led his five-camp army in a night attack and shattered them. In the seventh month he routed the Huaiqing rebels at Chailing Village, killed their leader Rolling Earth Dragon, and pursued Old Huihui to Jiyuan and killed him there.
13
文詔在洪洞時，與裏居御史劉令譽忤。 及是，令譽按河南，而四川石矽土官馬鳳儀軍敗沒於侯家莊，賴文詔馳退賊。 甫解甲，與令譽相見，語復相失。 文詔拂衣起，面叱之。 令譽怒，遂以鳳儀之敗為文詔罪。 部議文詔怙勝而騎，乃調之大同。
While stationed at Hongtong, Wenzhao had fallen out with retired censor Liu Lingyu. By then Lingyu was investigating Henan, while the Sichuan native-official Ma Fengyi of Shixi had been wiped out at Houjiazhuang. Only Wenzhao's rapid march drove the rebels back. He had barely doffed his armor when he met Lingyu, and their words clashed again. Wenzhao rose abruptly and rebuked him to his face. Enraged, Lingyu blamed Fengyi's defeat on Wenzhao. The ministry ruled that Wenzhao had grown arrogant in victory, and he was transferred to Datong.
14
七年七月，大清兵西征插漢，還師入大同境，攻拔得勝堡、參將李全自經，遂攻圍懷仁縣及井坪堡、應州。 文詔偕總督張宗衡先駐懷仁固守。 八月，圍解，即移駐鎮城，挑戰敗還。 已而靈丘及他屯堡多失陷，大清兵亦旋。 十一月論罪，文詔、宗衡及巡撫胡沾恩並充軍邊衛。 令甫下，山西巡撫吳甡薦文詔知兵善戰，請用之晉中。 乃命為援剿總兵官，立功自贖。 當是時，河南禍尤劇，帝已允兵部議，敕文詔馳剿河南賊。 甡復抗疏力爭，請令先平晉賊，後入豫，帝不許。 而文詔以甡有恩，竟取道太原，為甡所留。
In the seventh month of the seventh year the Qing army marched west against Chakhar, then returned into Datong territory, took Desheng Fort, and deputy general Li Quan hanged himself. They went on to besiege Huairen County, Jingping Fort, and Yingzhou. Wenzhao and Grand Coordinator Zhang Zongheng took post at Huairen to hold the line. In the eighth month the siege was lifted. He then moved to Zhencheng, offered battle, was beaten back, and withdrew. Soon afterward Lingqiu and many other border forts fell, and the Qing army withdrew as well. In the eleventh month they were found guilty. Wenzhao, Zongheng, and Grand Coordinator Hu Zhan'en were all sentenced to military exile on the frontier. The order had barely been issued when Shanxi Grand Coordinator Wu Shen recommended Wenzhao as a skilled commander and asked that he be employed in Jin. He was then appointed relief-suppression regional commander, with orders to redeem himself through merit. At that time the crisis in Henan was especially grave. The emperor had already approved the Ministry of War's plan and ordered Wenzhao to ride south at once to suppress the rebels in Henan. Wu Shen submitted another memorial arguing forcefully that Wenzhao should first pacify the rebels in Shanxi before entering Henan. The emperor refused. But Wenzhao, grateful to Wu Shen for his earlier support, nevertheless routed his march through Taiyuan, where Shen detained him.
15
會賊高加計已殲，而鳳陽告陷，遂整兵南，以八年三月會總督洪承疇於信陽。 承疇大喜，即令擊賊隨州，文詔追斬賊三百八十有奇。 四月，承疇次汝州。 以賊盡入關中，議還顧根本。 分命諸將扼要害，檄文詔入關，文詔乃馳至靈寶謁承疇。 承疇以賊在商、雒，聞官兵至，必先走漢中，而大軍由潼關入，反在其後，乃令文詔由閿鄉取山路至雒南、商州，直搗賊巢，復從山陽、鎮安、洵陽馳入漢中，遏其奔軼。 曰：「此行也，道路回遠，將軍甚勞苦，吾集關中兵以待將軍。 」拊其背而遣之，文詔跌馬去。 五月五日抵商州。 賊去城三十里，營火滿山。 文詔夜半率從子參將變蛟、守備鼎蛟、都司白廣恩等敗賊深林中，追至金嶺川。 賊據險以千騎逆戰，變蛟大呼陷陣，諸軍並進，賊敗走。 變蛟勇冠三軍，賊中聞大小曹將軍名，皆怖懾。
By then the rebel Gao Jiaji had already been wiped out, but word came that Fengyang had fallen. Wenzhao reorganized his forces and marched south, joining Governor-General Hong Chengchou at Xinyang in the third month of the eighth year. Chengchou was delighted and immediately ordered an assault on the rebels at Suizhou. Wenzhao pursued them and killed more than three hundred and eighty. In the fourth month Chengchou made camp at Ruzhou. Since the rebels had all poured into Guanzhong, the commanders debated turning back to protect the heartland. He assigned his generals to hold the critical passes and summoned Wenzhao into the pass. Wenzhao rode at once to Lingbao to report to Chengchou. Chengchou judged that the rebels, encamped at Shang and Luo, would flee toward Hanzhong as soon as they heard government troops were coming, leaving the main army entering through Tong Pass behind them. He ordered Wenzhao to take the mountain route from Wenxiang to Luonan and Shangzhou, strike directly at the rebel stronghold, then race through Shanyang, Zhen'an, and Xunyang into Hanzhong to cut off their escape. He said, "This march will take you over long and winding roads, General, and the hardship will be great. I will gather the Guanzhong troops and wait for you. " He clapped Wenzhao on the back and sent him on his way. Wenzhao vaulted into the saddle and rode off. On the fifth day of the fifth month he arrived at Shangzhou. The rebels were camped thirty li from the city, their fires lighting up the hills. At midnight Wenzhao led his nephew, Deputy General Cao Bianjiao, Garrison Commander Dingjiao, Bureau Commander Bai Guang'en, and others in a night attack that routed the rebels in the deep woods and chased them to Jinling River. The rebels held the high ground and counterattacked with a thousand horsemen. Bianjiao shouted and plunged into their ranks; the whole force pressed forward together and drove the rebels off. Bianjiao's bravery was unmatched in the army. Among the rebels, the very names of the Elder and Younger Cao Generals inspired dread.
16

The court urgently sent Lu Jiude to stop him, and Degong then moved his garrison to Luzhou. In the fourth month Zuo Liangyu marched east under the pretext of clearing evil from the emperor's side. He reached Jiujiang, died of illness, and the army installed his son Menggeng in his place. Degong was ordered to hurry upriver to oppose him and encamped at Digang. Degong defeated Menggeng at Tongling and broke the siege. He was ordered to move his household and garrison Taiping, devoting himself entirely to fighting the enemy, and for his merit was made Left Pillar of State.
26
時大清兵已渡江，知福王奔，分兵襲太平。 得功方收兵屯蕪湖，福王潛入其營。 得功驚泣曰：「陛下死守京城，臣等猶可盡力，奈何聽奸人言，倉卒到此！ 且臣方對敵，安能扈駕？ 」王曰：「非卿無可仗者。 」得功泣曰：「願效死。 」得功戰荻港時，傷臂幾墮。 衣葛衣，以帛絡臂，佩刀坐小舟，督麾下八總兵結束前迎敵。 而劉良佐已先歸命，大呼岸上招降。 得功怒叱曰：「汝乃降乎！ 」忽飛矢至，中其喉偏左。 得功知不可為，擲刀拾所拔箭刺吭死。 其妻聞之，亦自經。 總兵翁之琪投江死，中軍田雄遂挾福王降。
By then Qing troops had already crossed the Yangtze. Learning that the Prince of Fu had fled, they split their forces and struck at Taiping. Degong was just gathering his troops at Wuhu when the Prince of Fu slipped secretly into his camp. Degong cried out in shock and grief, "Your Majesty, if you had held the capital to the death, we could still have done our utmost. Why listen to villainous counsel and come here in such haste! And I am facing the enemy now—how can I escort Your Majesty? " The Prince said, "There is no one else I can rely on but you. " Degong wept and said, "Then I am willing to die in your service. " He had already fought at Digang with an arm so badly wounded it nearly came off. Clad in plain hemp, his arm bound with silk, sword at his belt, he sat in a small boat and ordered his eight regional commanders to arm themselves and advance to meet the enemy. But Liu Liangzuo had already surrendered and was shouting from the shore, urging them to submit. Degong roared in rebuke, "You would surrender! " Suddenly a flying arrow struck him just to the left of the throat. Degong knew all was lost. He cast aside his sword, took the arrow he had pulled out, and drove it into his throat. When his wife heard of it, she hanged herself. Regional commander Weng Zhiqi threw himself into the river and died. Staff officer Tian Xiong then seized the Prince of Fu and surrendered.
27
得功粗猛不識文義。 江南初立，王詔書指揮，多出群小。 得功得詔紙或對使罵裂之。 然忠義出天性，聞以國事相規誡者，輒屈己改不旋踵。 北來太子之獄，得功抗疏爭曰：「東宮未必假冒，先帝子即上子，未有了無證明，混然雷同者。 臣恐在廷諸臣，諂徇者多，抗顏者少，即明白識認，亦不敢抗詞取禍矣。 」時太子真偽莫敢決，而得功忠憤不阿如此。 得功每戰，飲酒數鬥，酒酣氣益厲。 喜持鐵鞭戰，鞭漬血沾手腕，以水濡之，久乃得脫，軍中呼為黃闖子。 始為偏裨，隨大帥立功名，未嘗一當大敵。 及專鎮封侯，不及一年余而南北轉徙，主逃將潰，無所一用其力，束手就殪，與國俱亡而已。 其軍行紀律嚴，下無敢犯，所至人感其德。 廬州、桐城、定遠皆為立生祠。 葬儀真方山母墓側。
Degong was rough and fierce and had little understanding of written language. When the Jiangnan court was first established, the Prince's edicts and orders mostly came from petty men at court. When Degong received an edict, he would sometimes curse the messenger and tear the paper to shreds. Yet loyalty and righteousness were in his nature. When anyone admonished him on matters of state, he would humble himself and change at once. In the case of the crown prince who had arrived from the north, Degong submitted a remonstrance saying, "The Eastern Palace may not necessarily be an impostor. A son of the late emperor is a son of the present sovereign. It is not that there is no evidence at all, yet everyone echoes the same line. I fear that at court those who flatter and yield are many and those who speak plainly are few. Even if they clearly recognize the truth, they dare not argue against it and invite disaster. " At that time none dared settle whether the crown prince was genuine or false, yet Degong's loyal indignation and refusal to bow were such as this. Before every battle Degong drank several pecks of wine, and once the wine had taken hold his fighting spirit grew fiercer still. He liked to fight with an iron whip. When blood soaked the whip and clung to his wrist, he had to wet it with water before it could finally be pulled free. The army called him Yellow Crasher. At first, as a junior officer, he followed great commanders to win distinction and had never once borne the brunt of a major enemy alone. But once he held independent command and was enfeoffed as a marquis, in little more than a year he was driven back and forth across north and south—the ruler fled, the armies collapsed, and he had nowhere to bring his full strength to bear. He was cut down with his hands tied, and perished together with the dynasty. His army marched under strict discipline, none below dared violate it, and wherever he went the people were moved by his virtue. Living shrines were erected to him in Luzhou, Tongcheng, and Dingyuan. He was buried on Mount Fang in Yizhen, beside his mother's tomb.
28
贊曰：曹文詔等秉驍猛之資，所向摧敗，皆所稱萬人敵也。 大命既傾，良將顛蹶。 三人者忠勇最著，死事亦最烈，故別著於篇。
The encomium says: Cao Wenzhao and the others possessed fierce and valiant gifts. Wherever they turned they shattered the foe—they were all men reckoned a match for ten thousand. When the great mandate had already toppled, good generals stumbled and fell. Of the three, their loyalty and courage stood out most, and their deaths in service were the fiercest; therefore they are given a separate account in this chapter.
