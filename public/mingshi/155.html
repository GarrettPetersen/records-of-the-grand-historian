Song Cheng and Xue Lu (Guo Yi, Jin Yu) Liu Rong, Zhu Rong, Fei Huan, Tan Guang, and Chen Huai (Ma Liang) Jiang Gui (Sun Wan)
2
任禮趙安趙輔劉聚宋晟，字景陽，定遠人。 父朝用，兄國興，並從渡江，皆積功至元帥。 攻集慶，國興戰歿，晟嗣其職。 既而朝用請老，晟方從鄧愈克徽州，召還，襲父官。 累進都指揮同知，歷鎮江西、大同、陜西。 洪武十二年坐法降涼州衛指揮使。 十七年五月討西番叛酋，至亦集乃路，擒元海道千戶也先帖木兒、國公吳把都剌赤等，俘獲萬八千人，送酋長京師，簡其精銳千人補卒伍，余悉放遣。 召還，復為都指揮，進右軍都督僉事，仍鎮涼州。 二十四年充總兵官，與都督劉真討哈梅裏。 其地去肅州千餘里。 晟令軍中多具糧糗，倍道疾馳，乘夜至城下。 質明，金鼓聲震地，闔城股栗，遂克之。 擒其王子別兒怯帖木兒，及偽國公以下三十餘人，收其部落輜重以歸。 自是番戎慴服，兵威極於西域。 明年五月從藍玉征罕東，徇阿真川，土酋哈昝等遁去。 師還，調中軍都督僉事。 二十八年六月，從總兵官周興出開原，至忽剌江。 部長西陽哈遁，追至甫答迷城，俘人畜而還。 明年拜征南右副將軍，討廣西帡幪諸寨苗，擒斬七千餘人。 又明年，總羽林八衛兵討平五開、龍裏苗。 三十一年出鎮開平，從燕王出塞，還城萬全諸衛。 建文改元，仍鎮甘肅。 成祖即位，入朝，進後軍左都督，拜平羌將軍，遣還鎮。 永樂三年招降把都帖木兒、倫都兒灰等部落五千人，獲馬駝牛羊萬六千。 封西寧侯，祿千一百石，世指揮使。 晟凡四鎮涼州，前後二十餘年，威信著絕域。 帝以晟舊臣，有大將材，專任以邊事，所奏請輒報可。 御史劾晟自專。 帝曰：「任人不專則不能成功，況大將統制一邊，寧能盡拘文法。」 即敕晟以便宜從事。 晟嘗請入朝。 報曰：「西北邊務，一以委卿，非召命，毋輒來。」 尋命營河西牧地，及圖出塞方略。 會病卒，五年七月也。 晟三子。 長瑄，建文中為府軍右衛指揮使，戰靈璧，先登，斬數級，力鬥死。 瑄弟琥，尚成祖女安成公主，得嗣侯，予世券。 八年佩前將軍印，鎮甘肅。 十年與李彬捕叛酋老的罕，俘斬甚眾。 召還。 洪熙元年坐不敬奪爵，並削駙馬都尉官。 宣德中復都尉。 琥既廢，弟瑛嗣。 瑛尚鹹寧公主。 正統中，歷掌左軍前府事。 瓦剌也先入寇，瑛充總兵官，督大同守將朱冕、石亨等戰陽和，全軍敗沒，瑛及冕皆戰死。 贈鄆國公，謚忠順。 子傑嗣。 景泰中典禁兵宿衛，以謹慎稱。 卒，子誠嗣。 署右府事，復佩平羌將軍印，鎮甘肅。 誠有材武，嘗出獵至涼州，遇寇掠牛馬北去。 誠三矢殪三人，寇驚散，盡驅所掠還。 九傳至孫裕德，死流寇難。
Ren Li, Zhao An, Zhao Fu, and Liu Ju. Song Cheng, styled Jingyang, was a native of Dingyuan. His father Chaoyong and elder brother Guoxing both followed in the crossing of the Yangtze and, through accumulated merit, rose to the rank of commander. During the attack on Jiqing, Guoxing was killed in battle, and Cheng succeeded to his command. When Chaoyong later requested retirement, Cheng was with Deng Yu capturing Huizhou. Recalled from the campaign, he inherited his father's post. He was promoted step by step to regional military commissioner of the third rank and successively served as regional commander in Jiangxi, Datong, and Shaanxi. In the twelfth year of Hongwu he was demoted for an offense to garrison commander of Liangzhou Guard. In the fifth month of the seventeenth year he marched against rebel chieftains of the western tribes. Reaching Yijina'i Road, he captured the Yuan maritime-route chiliarch Esentemür, the state duke Wu Badur chi, and others—altogether eighteen thousand captives. He sent the chieftains to the capital, drafted one thousand of their best troops into the ranks, and released the remainder. Recalled to court, he was restored as regional commander, promoted to assistant regional commander of the Rear Army, and continued to hold command at Liangzhou. In the twenty-fourth year he served as overall commander and, with Commissioner-in-Chief Liu Zhen, campaigned against Hami. Hami lay more than a thousand li from Suzhou. Cheng had the army carry abundant dry rations and forced a rapid march, reaching the city walls under cover of night. At daybreak, drums and gongs shook the earth; the entire city trembled with fear, and the place was taken. He captured Hami's prince Bie'erqietemür and more than thirty others down to the false state duke, then withdrew with the tribe's baggage train. From then on the frontier peoples submitted in fear, and Ming military prestige reached its height in the Western Regions. The following May he followed Lan Yu on the campaign against Handong, advancing along the Azhen River while native chieftains such as Haza fled. When the army returned, he was reassigned as assistant regional commander of the Central Army. In the sixth month of the twenty-eighth year he joined overall commander Zhou Xing in marching from Kaiyuan to the Hula River. The tribal chief Xiyangha fled; Cheng pursued him to Pudami City, took captives and livestock, and withdrew. The following year he was appointed Right Vice General for the Southern Expedition and campaigned against Miao stockades at Pingmeng in Guangxi, capturing or killing more than seven thousand. The year after that he led the eight Palace Guard garrisons and pacified the Miao of Wukai and Longli. In the thirty-first year he took command at Kaiping, accompanied the Prince of Yan on a campaign beyond the frontier, and on the return fortified the garrisons at Wanquan. When the Jianwen era began, he continued to hold command in Gansu. After Emperor Chengzu took the throne, he came to court, was promoted to Left Regional Commander of the Rear Army, appointed General Who Pacifies the Qiang, and sent back to his frontier command. In the third year of Yongle he induced five thousand tribesmen under Badutemür, Lundu'erhui, and others to submit, and seized sixteen thousand horses, camels, cattle, and sheep. He was enfeoffed as Marquis of Xining with an annual stipend of 1,100 shi and the hereditary rank of garrison commander. In all Cheng held command at Liangzhou four times over more than twenty years, and his prestige reached the farthest frontiers. The emperor, regarding Cheng as a veteran with true generalship, gave him exclusive charge of frontier affairs, and his proposals were invariably approved. A censor impeached Cheng for acting autocratically. The emperor replied: "If one does not give an appointee full authority, nothing can be accomplished—and when a major general controls an entire frontier, how can he be shackled by every rule in the code?" He immediately authorized Cheng to act at his discretion as circumstances required. Cheng once requested leave to come to court. The reply read: "Northwestern frontier affairs are entirely yours; unless you are summoned, do not come on your own." He was soon ordered to develop pasture lands west of the Yellow River and to draft plans for campaigns beyond the frontier. He then fell ill and died, in the seventh month of the fifth year. Cheng had three sons. The eldest, Xuan, was garrison commander of the Right Guard of the Palace Army during the Jianwen reign. At Lingbi he was first over the wall, killed several enemies, and died fighting. Xuan's younger brother Hu married Emperor Chengzu's daughter, Princess Ancheng, succeeded to the marquisate, and received a patent of hereditary entitlement. In the eighth year he bore the former general's seal and held command in Gansu. In the tenth year he and Li Bin captured the rebel chieftain Laodehan, taking many prisoners and heads. He was then recalled. In the first year of Hongxi he was stripped of his title for disrespect and also lost his post as Commandant of Attendants-in-Ordinary. During the Xuande reign his commandant rank was restored. After Hu's deposition, his younger brother Ying succeeded to the title. Ying married Princess Xianning. During the Zhengtong reign he successively directed affairs in the Front Office of the Left Army. When Esen of the Oirats invaded, Ying served as overall commander, directing Datong garrison commanders Zhu Mian and Shi Heng in the battle at Yanghe. The entire force was destroyed, and both Ying and Mian were killed. He was posthumously ennobled as Duke of Yun with the posthumous title Loyal and Obedient. His son Jie succeeded him. During the Jingtai reign he commanded the palace guard, noted for his careful conduct. On his death his son Cheng succeeded. He acted in the Right Office, again bore the seal of General Who Pacifies the Qiang, and held command in Gansu. Cheng was skilled in arms; once while hunting near Liangzhou he encountered raiders driving stolen cattle and horses northward. Cheng killed three raiders with three arrows; the rest fled in panic, and he recovered all the stolen livestock. After nine generations the line reached his grandson Yude, who was killed in the roving-bandit upheavals.
3
薛祿，膠人。 行六，軍中呼曰「薛六」。 既貴，乃更名「祿」。 祿以卒伍從燕起兵，首奪九門。 真定之戰，左副將軍李堅迎鬥。 鋒始交，祿持槊刺堅墜馬，擒之。 擢指揮僉事。 從援永平，下大寧、富峪、會州、寬河。 還救北平，先驅敗南軍遊騎。 進指揮同知。 攻大同，為先鋒。 戰白溝河，追奔至濟南，遷指揮使。 戰東昌，以五十騎敗南兵數百。 時成祖為盛庸所敗，還走北平。 庸檄真定諸將屯威縣、深州，邀燕歸路。
Xue Lu was a native of Jiao. The sixth son in his family, he was called "Xue Six" in the army. After he rose to prominence, he adopted the name "Lu". Lu rose from the ranks to follow the Prince of Yan in rebellion and was first to seize the Nine Gates. At the battle of Zhending, Left Vice General Li Jian came out to give battle. As the vanguards clashed, Lu impaled Jian with a spear, knocked him from his horse, and took him captive. He was promoted to assistant garrison commander. He joined the relief of Yongping and helped capture Daning, Fuyu, Huizhou, and Kuanhe. On the return to relieve Beiping, he led the vanguard and routed the enemy's scouting cavalry. He was promoted to garrison commander of the third rank. In the attack on Datong he served as vanguard. After fighting at the Baigou River and pursuing the rout as far as Jinan, he was promoted to garrison commander. At Dongchang he routed several hundred Southern troops with only fifty horsemen. At the time Emperor Chengzu had been defeated by Sheng Yong and was retreating toward Beiping. Yong ordered the Zhending generals to occupy Wei County and Shenzhou and cut off the Prince of Yan's line of retreat.
4
祿皆擊走之。 戰滹沱河，右軍卻。 祿馳赴陣，出入數十戰，破之。 追奔至夾河，斬馘無算。 戰單家橋，為平安所執。 奮脫縛，拔刀殺守卒，馳還復戰，大敗安軍。 掠順德、大名、彰德。 攻西水寨，生擒都指揮花英。 乘勝下東阿、東平、汶上，連戰淝河、小河、靈璧，功最。 入京師，擢都督僉事。 永樂六年進同知。 八年充驃騎將軍，從北征，進右都督。 十年上言：「自古用人，必資豫教。 今武臣子弟閑暇不教，恐緩急無可使者。」 帝韙其言。
Lu attacked and drove them off in every case. At the battle of the Hutuo River the right wing gave ground. Lu galloped to the front, fought through dozens of clashes, and broke the enemy. He pursued the rout to the Jiahe River and took an incalculable number of heads. At Danjia Bridge he was captured by Ping An. He broke his bonds, killed the guards, galloped back into battle, and routed Ping An's forces. He raided Shunde, Daming, and Zhangde. In the assault on the Western Water Stockade he took Regional Commander Hua Ying alive. Following up his victory he took Dong'e, Dongping, and Wenshang, and in successive battles at the Fei River, Xiao River, and Lingbi his achievements ranked first. After entering the capital he was promoted to assistant regional commander. In the sixth year of Yongle he was promoted to regional commissioner of the third rank. In the eighth year he served as Flying Cavalry General on the northern expedition and was promoted to Right Regional Commander. In the tenth year of Yongle he memorialized: "Since antiquity, the use of men has depended on prior training. Today the sons of military families are left idle without training; I fear that in an emergency there will be no one fit to serve." The emperor approved his proposal.
5
會四方送幼軍數萬至，悉隸祿操習之。 十五年以行在後軍都督董營造。 十八年十二月定都北京，授奉天靖難推誠宣力武臣，封陽武侯，祿千一百石。 二十一年將右哨從北征。 還，討平長興盜。 二十二年再領右哨從北征。 仁宗即位，命掌左府，加太子太保，予世券。 洪熙元年充總兵官，備禦塞外。 尋以獲寇功，益祿五百石。 是年頒諸將軍印於各邊鎮，祿佩鎮朔大將軍印，巡開平，至大同邊。 宣宗即位，召還，陳備邊五事。 尋復遣巡邊。 宣德元年從征樂安，為前鋒。 高煦就擒，留祿與尚書張本鎮撫之。 明年春，奉詔巡視畿南諸府城池，嚴戒軍士毋擾民，違者以軍法論。
When several tens of thousands of young recruits arrived from all quarters, they were all assigned to Lu for training. In the fifteenth year he served as touring Rear Army Regional Commander supervising construction projects. When the capital was established at Beijing in the twelfth month of the eighteenth year, he received the title Meritous Military Subject Who Supports Heaven, Settles Difficulties, Extends Sincerity, and Deploys Strength, was enfeoffed as Marquis of Yangwu with a stipend of 1,100 shi. In the twenty-first year he led the right wing on the northern expedition. On his return he suppressed banditry in Changxing. In the twenty-second year he again led the right wing on the northern expedition. When Emperor Renzong took the throne, he was placed in charge of the Left Office, named Grand Mentor to the Heir Apparent, and granted a patent of hereditary entitlement. In the first year of Hongxi he served as overall commander defending the outer frontier. He soon received an additional stipend of 500 shi for capturing raiders. That year frontier commanders received their general's seals; Lu bore the seal of Great General Who Pacifies the North and patrolled from Kaiping to the Datong frontier. When Emperor Xuanzong took the throne, Lu was recalled and presented five proposals for frontier defense. He was soon sent out again to inspect the frontier. In the first year of Xuande he served as vanguard in the campaign against Le'an. After Gao Xu was captured, Lu and Minister Zhang Ben were left to pacify the area. The following spring he was ordered to inspect the fortifications of prefectures south of the capital, with strict orders that troops must not harass civilians; violators would be punished under military law.
6
是夏復佩大將軍印，北巡開平，還駐宣府。 敵犯開平，無所得而退，去城三百餘里。 祿帥精兵晝伏夜行，三夕至。 縱輕騎蹂敵營，破之，大獲人畜。 師還，敵躡其後，復奮擊敗之，敵由是遠遁。 召還。 三年從北征，破敵於寬河，留鎮薊州、永平。 復數佩鎮朔印，巡邊護餉，出開平、宣府間。 五年遇敵於鳳凰嶺，斬獲多，加太保。 上言永寧衛團山及雕鶚、赤城、雲州、獨石宜築城堡，便守禦。 詔發軍民三萬六千赴工，精騎一千五百護之，皆聽祿節制。 臨行賜詩，以山甫、南仲為比。 祿武人不知書，以問楊士奇。 士奇曰：「上以古賢人待君也。」 祿拊心曰：「祿安敢望前賢，然敢不勉圖報上恩萬一。」
That summer he again bore the great general's seal, patrolled north to Kaiping, and on returning took up post at Xuanfu. Enemy raiders struck Kaiping, withdrew empty-handed, and were more than three hundred li from the city. Lu led elite troops by day marches and night marches, reaching the enemy in three nights. He sent light cavalry to overrun the enemy camp, broke them, and captured large numbers of people and livestock. On the return march the enemy pursued from the rear; Lu turned and routed them, after which the raiders fled deep into the steppe. He was then recalled. In the third year he joined the northern expedition, defeated the enemy at the Kuanhe River, and remained to garrison Jizhou and Yongping. He several times again bore the Pacify-the-North seal, patrolling the frontier, escorting supplies, and operating between Kaiping and Xuanfu. In the fifth year he met the enemy at Fenghuang Ridge, scored many kills and captures, and was named Grand Guardian. He memorialized that fortresses should be built at Tuanshan in Yongning Guard and at Diao'e, Chicheng, Yunzhou, and Dushi to strengthen the defenses. An edict mobilized 36,000 soldiers and civilians for the project, with 1,500 elite cavalry to protect them; all were placed under Lu's command. Before his departure the emperor presented him with a poem comparing him to the ancient ministers Shanfu and Nanzhong. Lu, a soldier who was not literate, asked Yang Shiqi what the poem meant. Shiqi replied: "The emperor is treating you as one of the sages of old." Lu placed his hand on his heart and said: "How dare I compare myself to the great men of antiquity? Yet I shall strive with all my might to repay even a fraction of the emperor's kindness."
7
其年六月有疾，召還。 逾月卒。 贈鄞國公，謚忠武。 祿有勇而好謀，謀定後戰，戰必勝。 善撫士卒，同甘苦，人樂為用。 「靖難」諸功臣，張玉、朱能及祿三人為最，而祿逮事三朝，巋然為時宿將。 孫詵嗣。 至曾孫翰卒，無子，族人爭襲，久之不得請，田宅並入官，世絕者三十餘年。 萬歷五年乃復封翰族子鋹為侯。 再傳至濂。 崇禎末，京師陷，被害。
That June he fell ill and was recalled. He died a month later. He was posthumously ennobled as Duke of Yin with the posthumous title Loyal and Martial. Lu was brave yet thoughtful: he planned first and fought afterward, and he invariably won. He treated his soldiers well, sharing their hardships, and they served him willingly. Among the meritorious supporters of the "Pacification of Difficulties," Zhang Yu, Zhu Neng, and Lu ranked highest; Lu alone served three reigns and stood as the age's foremost veteran commander. His grandson Chuan succeeded to the title. When his great-grandson Han died without an heir, clansmen fought over the title; for years no claimant was approved, the estates reverted to the state, and the line lapsed for more than thirty years. Not until the fifth year of Wanli was Han's kinsman Qiang re-enfeoffed as marquis. Two generations later the line reached Lian. At the end of the Chongzhen reign, when the capital fell, he was killed.
8
永樂中從起兵北平，後積功至大將，封侯伯不以「靖難」功者，薛祿及郭義、金玉、劉榮、朱榮凡五人，而義、玉與祿同日封雲。
During Yongle, five men who had joined the Beiping rebellion and later rose to high command were enfeoffed as marquises or earls on grounds other than "Pacification of Difficulties" merit: Xue Lu, Guo Yi, Jin Yu, Liu Rong, and Zhu Rong; Guo Yi and Jin Yu received their titles on the same day as Lu.
9
郭義，濟寧人。 洪武時，累功為燕山千戶。 從成祖入京師，累遷左都督。 永樂九年坐曠職謫交阯，立功，已而宥之。 數從出塞，有功，封安陽侯，祿千一百石，亦授奉天靖難武臣號。 時義在南京，疾革，聞命而卒。
Guo Yi was a native of Jining. During the Hongwu reign he rose through accumulated merit to chiliarch of Yanshan. He followed Emperor Chengzu into the capital and was promoted step by step to Left Regional Commander. In the ninth year of Yongle he was banished to Jiaozhi for absenteeism; after distinguishing himself there he was pardoned. After several successful campaigns beyond the frontier he was enfeoffed as Marquis of Anyang with a stipend of 1,100 shi and granted the title Meritous Military Subject Who Supports Heaven and Pacifies Difficulties. Yi was in Nanjing at the time; gravely ill, he died upon receiving the enfeoffment.
10
金玉，江浦人。 襲父官為羽林衛百戶，調燕山護衛。 從起兵有功，累遷河南都指揮使。 永樂三年進都督僉事。 八年充鷹揚將軍從北征。 師旋，為殿。 至長秀川，收敵所棄牛羊雜畜亙數十里。 十四年討平山西妖賊劉子進。 論前後功，封惠安伯，祿九百石。 十九年卒。 妾田氏自經以殉，贈淑人。
Jin Yu was a native of Jiangpu. He inherited his father's post as centurion of the Palace Guard and was transferred to the Yanshan Bodyguard. He distinguished himself in the rebellion and was promoted step by step to regional garrison commander of Henan. In the third year of Yongle he was promoted to assistant regional commander. In the eighth year he served as Soaring Hawk General on the northern expedition. On the return march he commanded the rearguard. At the Changxiu River he collected enemy livestock abandoned along a front several tens of li long—cattle, sheep, and other animals. In the fourteenth year he suppressed the Shanxi sorcerer-rebel Liu Zijin. In recognition of his cumulative service he was enfeoffed as Earl of Huian with a stipend of 900 shi. He died in the nineteenth year. His concubine Lady Tian hanged herself in fidelity; she was posthumously granted the title Lady of Virtuous Conduct.

論功進左都督，以內援特封寧晉伯。 八年冬代趙輔為將軍，總陜西諸鎮兵。 寇入花馬池，率副總兵孫鉞、遊擊將軍王璽等擊卻之。 還至高家堡，寇復至，敗之。 追奔至漫天嶺，伏起夾擊，又敗之。 鉞、璽亦別破賊於井油山。 捷聞，予世券。 其冬，孛羅忽、滿都魯、乚加思蘭連兵深入，至秦州、安定、會寧諸州縣，縱橫數千里。 賊退，適王越自紅鹽池還，妄以大捷聞，璽書嘉勞。 頃之，紀功兵部員外郎張謹劾聚及總兵官範瑾等六將殺被掠者，冒功。 部科及御史交章劾。 詔遣給事中韓文往勘，還奏如謹言：所報首功百五十，僅十九級。 帝以寇既遁，置不問。 聚尋卒。 贈侯，謚威勇。
For his achievements he was promoted to left commissioner-in-chief and, owing to backing within the court, was specially enfeoffed as Marquis of Ningjin. In the winter of the eighth year he replaced Zhao Fu as commanding general with authority over all the Shaanxi garrisons. When raiders entered Huamachi, Liu Ju led Deputy Regional Commander Sun Yue, Mobile Corps General Wang Xi, and others to drive them back. On returning to Gaojiabao he encountered the raiders again and defeated them. Pursuing the fleeing enemy to Mantian Ridge, he sprang an ambush in a pincer attack and routed them again. Sun Yue and Wang Xi also separately defeated the enemy at Jingyoushan. When news of victory reached the throne, he was granted a hereditary certificate of investiture. That winter Boluo Hu, Mandulu, and Yijiasilan combined their forces in a deep raid reaching the prefectures and counties of Qinzhou, Anding, and Huining, ravaging an area spanning thousands of li. After the raiders withdrew, Wang Yue, returning from Hongyanchi, falsely reported a great victory, and the emperor responded with an edict of commendation. Before long Merit-Recording Vice Director of the Ministry of War Zhang Jin impeached Liu Ju, Regional Commander Fan Jin, and five other generals for killing captives taken by the raiders and fraudulently claiming credit. Ministry departments and censorial officials submitted a barrage of joint impeachments. The emperor dispatched Supervising Secretary Han Wen to investigate; on his return Han reported as Zhang Jin had alleged: of 150 severed heads reported as trophies of merit, only 19 were verified. Since the raiders had already fled, the emperor declined to pursue the matter. Liu Ju soon died. He was posthumously ennobled as marquis with the posthumous epithet Weiyong ("Formidable and Brave").

傳子祿及福，福，弘治中掌三千營，加太子太保。 卒，子嶽嗣。 卒，從子文請嗣。 吏部言聚無大功，子孫不宜再襲。 世宗不允，命文嗣。 亦傳至明亡乃絕。
The title passed to his sons Lu and Fu; Fu, during the Hongzhi reign, commanded the Three Thousand Camp and was promoted to Grand Guardian to the Crown Prince. Upon his death his son Yue succeeded. After Yue's death his cousin Wen petitioned to succeed to the title. The Ministry of Personnel argued that Liu Ju had never achieved outstanding merit and that his descendants should not inherit the title again. The Jiajing Emperor overruled them and ordered Wen to succeed. The line likewise continued until the fall of the Ming dynasty, when it became extinct.

贊曰：宋晟在太祖時，即與開國諸元勛參跡戎行，其後四鎮涼州，威著西鄙。 兩子尚主，世列徹侯，功名盛矣。 薛祿以下諸人，皆與「靖難」。 祿東昌、滹沱之戰，劉榮守永平，譚廣守保定，宣力最著。 雖策勛之日，未即剖符，而各以積閥受封。 其善撫士卒，慎固封守，恪謹奉職，有足尚者。 趙輔、劉聚猷績遠遜前人，而帶礪之盟，與國終始，誠厚幸哉。 諸人並以勛爵鎮禦邊陲，故類著於篇。
The eulogy states: Song Sheng, already serving alongside the founding meritorious lords under the founding emperor, later held the Liangzhou command four times, earning renown on the western frontier. With two sons marrying imperial princesses and the family ranking among full marquises for generations, their glory was truly magnificent. From Xue Lu onward, all these men took part in the "Pacification of Difficulties" (the Yongle succession war). Xue Lu distinguished himself at the battles of Dongchang and the Hutuo River; Liu Rong held Yongping and Tan Guang held Baoding — each made outstanding contributions. Though they did not receive enfeoffment immediately upon the initial reckoning of merit, each was eventually ennobled for his accumulated battlefield record. Their care for their troops, vigilance in frontier defense, and scrupulous devotion to duty are qualities worthy of admiration. Zhao Fu and Liu Ju fell far short of their predecessors in achievement, yet they enjoyed the "covenant of the girdle and whetstone" and retained their honors until the dynasty's end — a mark of extraordinary good fortune indeed. As all these men held ennobled commands on the frontier, they are grouped together in this chapter.
