1
保寧，圖伯特氏，蒙古正白旗人，靖逆將軍納穆札勒子。 乾隆中，納穆札勒殉節回疆，錫封三等公。
Bao Ning, of the Tubote clan, was a Mongol bannerman of the Plain White Banner and the son of Pacification General Namuzhale. In the Qianlong era, Namuzhale gave his life in the Muslim borderlands and was posthumously granted the rank of third-class duke.
2
保寧由親軍襲爵，授乾清門侍衛。 從征金川，力戰，迭克要隘，​​將軍阿桂薦其才，擢陝西興漢鎮總兵。 金川平，繪像紫光閣，御製贊，褒其膽勇持重，少年如宿將。 尋調河南南陽鎮、直隸馬蘭鎮，兼總管內務府大臣。 擢江南提督。
Bao Ning inherited his father's title through service in the Imperial Guard and was appointed a gate guard of the Palace of Heavenly Purity. On the Jinchuan campaign he fought hard and took one vital pass after another; General Agui praised his ability, and he was promoted to regional commander of Xinghan Garrison in Shaanxi. After Jinchuan was pacified, his portrait was hung in the Hall of Purple Splendor with an imperial inscription praising his boldness and composure—a young man who fought like a seasoned commander. He was soon moved to command Nanyang Garrison in Henan and Malan Garrison in Zhili, while also serving as superintendent of the Imperial Household Department. He was then promoted to provincial military commander of Jiangnan.
3
四十九年，授成都將軍。 甘肅石峰堡回叛，命選屯練番兵赴鞏昌、安定助剿，平之。 五十一年，授四川總督。 保寧謹慎有操守，盡心邊事。 邊夷上下孟董、九子等寨生齒日繁，請增設營員，以屯練有勞績者拔補； 改修打箭爐城，扼要築卡，駐兵捍衛； 改黃梁、大定、白雞、白鹿等八寨熟苗編入民戶：並協機宜。
In the forty-ninth year of the reign, he was appointed general of Chengdu. When Muslims at Shifeng Fort in Gansu rose in revolt, he was ordered to send trained garrison and native troops to Gongchang and Anding to help suppress them, and the rebellion was put down. In the fifty-first year he was made governor-general of Sichuan. Bao Ning was careful, upright, and wholly devoted to border administration. Frontier communities at stockades such as upper and lower Mengdong and Jiuzi were growing rapidly; he asked for more garrison officers, to be drawn from trained troops who had distinguished themselves; had Dartsedo repaired, built barrier posts at key points, and stationed troops to guard them; and registered the acculturated Miao of eight stockades, including Huangliang, Dading, Baiji, and Bailu, as regular civilian households—all of which he coordinated to good effect.
4
次年，調伊犁將軍，兼內大臣，籌備倉儲。 疏言：「伊犁一年支糧十六萬六千餘石，不敷二萬三千石，歷就舊儲五十餘萬石內填補。 現賸三十餘萬石，雖尚可敷十餘年之用，地處極邊，若不補籌餘糧，偶遇歉收，或有需糧之事，慮難接濟。 請撥兵丁七百名，增開七屯，自來年耕種，歲可收糧一萬九千餘石，永遠備貯。 」從之。 又奏添設惠遠城鳥槍步甲四百名。 五十五年，入覲，途次命赴四川暫署總督事。 次年，回任，加太子少保，授御前大臣。 惠遠城創立三十餘年，戶口日繁，於城東展築，擴舊城四分之一。 伊犁無通曉俄羅斯語言者，請於京師俄館選派一人來教習官兵子弟，五年期滿，試最優者充筆帖式。 俄屬烏梁海潛往哈屯河外汗山地方游牧，帝慮其滋事，命保寧察視，疏言：「烏梁海居住甚安戢，不必驅逐，飭邊卡防範，無庸添兵。 」察哈爾兵丁及土爾扈特私竊哈薩克馬匹，緝獲，置之法。 帝嘉保寧無偏袒，得外籓心，予議敘。
The next year he was transferred to general of Ili, also serving as a grand minister of the interior, and set about organizing grain reserves. He memorialized: "Ili needs more than 166,000 shi of grain each year, with a shortfall of 23,000 shi that has been covered from old stores of over 500,000 shi. Only a little over 300,000 shi remain. That might still last more than ten years, but Ili is at the empire's farthest edge. Unless we build up reserves again, a bad harvest or sudden demand for grain could leave us unable to meet the need." He asked for seven hundred troops to open seven new military farms; planting would begin the next year, yielding more than 19,000 shi a year for permanent storage. " The emperor approved. He also asked to add four hundred musket infantry at Huining City. In the fifty-fifth year, on his way to court for an audience, he was ordered en route to Sichuan to serve temporarily as governor-general. The next year he resumed his post, was made junior guardian of the heir apparent, and appointed a grand minister in attendance. Huining City was more than thirty years old and its population was swelling; he extended the walls on the east side, adding a quarter to the old city. Because no one in Ili knew Russian, he asked that someone be sent from the Russian hostel in Beijing to instruct officers' and soldiers' sons; after five years the top student would be appointed a clerk. Uriankhai under Russian rule had quietly moved to pasture beyond the Hatun River near Khan Mountain. The emperor feared trouble and sent Bao Ning to look into it. He reported that the Uriankhai were living peacefully, need not be driven off, and that frontier posts should simply stay alert—no extra troops were required. Chahar soldiers and Torghuts caught stealing Kazakh horses privately were arrested and punished by law. The emperor commended Bao Ning for impartiality that won over the frontier peoples, and ordered his merits considered for reward.
5
六十年，召授吏部尚書，兼鑲黃旗漢軍都統，甫數月，復出為伊犁將軍。 嘉慶二年，協辦大學士，尋拜武英殿大學士，加太子太保，任邊事如故。 土爾扈特家奴三吉污主母孀婦伯克木庫殞命，特詔予伯克木庫旌表。 保寧疏陳駐防孀婦守節，未舉旌表之典，請照內地一體辦理。 於是採訪各城，請旌者凡七十人，後著為令。 七年，召還京，授領侍衛內大臣，管理兵部，兼管三庫。 八年，因孝淑皇后山陵典禮會疏措詞不經，褫銜鐫級留任。
In the sixtieth year he was recalled to be minister of personnel and commander of the Bordered Yellow Banner Chinese forces; within a few months he was sent back out as general of Ili. In Jiaqing 2 he became associate grand secretary, then grand secretary of the Hall of Military Eminence, with the additional title senior guardian of the heir apparent, and continued frontier duties as before. The Torghut bondservant Sanji assaulted his mistress's widowed daughter-in-law Berkemuku, who died; the throne specially ordered Berkemuku honored with a commemorative tablet. Bao Ning noted that chaste widows among the garrisons had not received commemorative honors and asked that inland practice be followed throughout. He then surveyed every garrison city and recommended seventy women for honors; the practice was later codified as regulation. In the seventh year he was recalled to Beijing, made chief grand minister of the imperial bodyguard, put in charge of the Ministry of War, and also given oversight of the three treasuries. In the eighth year a joint memorial on Empress Xiaoshu's tomb rites used improper language; his titles were stripped and rank reduced, but he kept his post.
6
保寧兩鎮伊犁，歷十餘年，西陲無事，籓部悅服。 既去任，朝廷遇邊疆興革，每諮決焉。 十一年，以疾乞休，命在家食公爵全俸。 逾兩年，卒，賜金優恤，諡文端，祠祀伊犁。
Bao Ning held Ili twice over more than a decade; the western frontier stayed quiet and the frontier peoples were content. After he left office, the court still consulted him on every major frontier reform. In the eleventh year he asked to retire for illness and was allowed to remain at home on a duke's full stipend. More than two years later he died; the court granted gold for generous mourning, gave him the posthumous title Wenduan, and enshrined him at Ili.
7
子慶祥嗣爵，殉回疆之難，自有傳。 次子慶惠，由廕生授侍衛，歷官侍郎，三以罪黜復起。 道光中，官至熱河都統，以疾歸，卒，諡勤僖。
His son Qingxiang inherited the title and died in the Muslim borderlands crisis; he has his own biography. His second son Qinghui entered service by hereditary privilege as a bodyguard, rose to vice minister, and was dismissed three times for misconduct yet restored each time. Under Daoguang he became commander at Rehe, retired ill, died, and was given the posthumous title Qinxi.
8
松筠，字湘浦，瑪拉特氏，蒙古正藍旗人。 繙譯生員，考授理籓院筆帖式，充軍機章京，能任事，為高宗所知。 累遷銀庫員外郎。 乾隆四十八年，超擢內閣學士，兼副都統。
Songyun, styled Xiangpu, of the Marat clan, was a Mongol bannerman of the Plain Blue Banner. A translation licentiate, he passed examination for a clerkship in the Court of Colonial Affairs, served on the Grand Council secretariat, proved capable, and won Emperor Qianlong's notice. He rose in succession to vice director of the Silver Treasury. In Qianlong 48 he was abruptly promoted to grand secretary of the Secretariat and made vice commander as well.
9
五十年，命往庫倫治俄羅斯貿易事。 先是，俄屬布哩雅特人劫掠庫倫商貨，俄官不依例交犯，僅罰償，流之遠地，檄問未聽命，詔停恰克圖貿易。 松筠至，尋充辦事大臣。 閉關後，邊禁嚴而不擾，遇俄人皆開誠待之。 擢戶部侍郎。 俄羅斯以貿易久停，有悔意，撤舊官，屢請開市，未許。 卡倫兵出巡，復為布哩雅特人所殺。 松筠曰：「舊事未了，又生旁支，然亦了事之機也。 」檄俄官縛送三人，親訊於界上，斬其二，流其一，請兩案並結。 詔斥專擅，褫職，仍留庫倫效力。 會西路土爾扈特喇嘛薩邁林者，迷路入哈薩克，歸攜書信，訛言俄人誘致土爾扈特謀亂，下松筠察狀。 疏言俄羅斯實恭順，無可疑。 俄人亦自陳證薩邁林書信出偽造。 詔置薩邁林於法，許復開市。 五十七年，召俄官會議定約，親蒞俄帳宴飲，諭以恩信，大悅服。 事曆八年然後定。 召還京，授御前侍衛、內務府大臣、軍機大臣。 命護送英吉利貢使回廣東，凡所要索皆嚴拒。
In the fiftieth year he was sent to Khuree to handle Russian trade. Earlier Buryats under Russian rule had robbed Khuree merchants. Russian officials refused to surrender the culprits as required, fined and exiled them instead, and ignored official demands—so trade at Kyakhta was halted. Songyun arrived and soon became commissioner in charge of affairs. After the border closed, restrictions were strict but not abusive; he met every Russian with frank goodwill. He was promoted to vice minister of revenue. With trade long suspended, Russia showed regret, replaced its old officials, and repeatedly asked to reopen the market, but was refused. Frontier patrol troops on circuit were again killed by Buryats. Songyun said, "The old case is unfinished and a new one has sprung up—but this may be the chance to close both." He demanded the Russians deliver three men bound, questioned them personally at the border, executed two and exiled one, and asked to close both cases at once. The throne rebuked his unauthorized action, stripped his post, but kept him at Khuree to serve. Meanwhile the western-route Torghut lama Samailin, lost in Kazakh territory, returned with letters falsely claiming Russia had incited the Torghut to rebel; Songyun was ordered to investigate. He reported that Russia was genuinely submissive and not to be suspected. The Russians themselves proved Samailin's letters were forged. Samailin was punished by law and trade was allowed to reopen. In the fifty-seventh year he summoned Russian officials to settle a treaty, dined in their tent himself, spoke of imperial grace and good faith, and won their deep satisfaction. Eight years passed before the matter was finally settled. Recalled to Beijing, he was made imperial bodyguard, minister of the imperial household, and grand councilor. He was ordered to escort the British tribute mission back to Guangdong and refused every demand they made.
10
五十九年，署吉林將軍。 尋命往荊州察稅務，道出衛輝，大水環城，率守令開倉賑恤。 詔嘉獎，授工部尚書兼都統。 充駐藏大臣，撫番多惠政。 和珅用事，松筠不為屈，遂久留邊地。 在藏凡五年。
In the fifty-ninth year he served as acting general of Jilin. He was soon sent to Jingzhou to inspect taxes; passing Weihui, he found the city ringed by flood and led local officials to open granaries for relief. The emperor commended him and appointed him minister of works with concurrent command of a banner. As resident minister in Tibet he governed the frontier peoples with many benevolent measures. When Heshen dominated court politics, Songyun refused to bend and was kept on the frontier for years. He spent five years in Tibet altogether.
11
嘉慶四年春，召為戶部尚書。 尋授陝甘總督，加太子少保。 時教匪張漢潮及藍號、白號諸黨擾陝、甘。 松筠至，駐漢中，治糧餉給諸軍。 自軍興，給陝西餉銀一千一百萬兩，至是續撥一百五十萬，設局清釐，按旬諮部。 命陳諸將優劣，密疏言：「明亮知兵而罔實效； 恆瑞前戰湖北功最，年近六旬，精力大減； 慶成有勇無謀； 永保無謀無勇，不能治兵，並不能治民； 惟額勒登保、德楞泰能辦賊。 」仁宗深嘉納之。 明亮劾永保、慶成避賊，下松筠逮治。 永保亦與荊州將軍興肇訐明亮誑報軍功，詔並褫職，遣尚書那彥成赴陝會鞫。 會明亮已擊斃張漢潮，松筠請緩其獄，又請留撒拉爾回兵，令慶成率以協剿，帝不允。 既而那彥成劾忄互瑞棄藍號垂盡之賊，折回陝西，由松筠所誤。 詔褫松筠宮銜、侍衛，仍留總督任。 川匪犯南鄭，復分犯西鄉、沔縣、略陽。 松筠素謂匪多脅從，可諭降，欲單騎赴之。 副將韓嘉業固諫曰：「諭之不從而喪總督，大損國威，為天下笑。 請先往。 」嘉業果被害。 賊竄徽縣、兩當。 五年春，額勒登保、那彥成會剿，乃分路遁。 於是命長麟代為陝甘總督，授松筠伊犁將軍，未之任，暫署湖廣總督。 自請入覲面陳軍事，先在陝上疏言：「賊不患不平，而患在將平之時。 既平之後，請弛私鹽、私鑄之禁，俾餘匪散勇有所謀生。 」帝以其言迂闊，置之。 至京，復以為請，忤旨，降副都統銜，充伊犁領隊大臣。
In the spring of Jiaqing 4 he was recalled as minister of revenue. He was soon made governor-general of Shaanxi and Gansu, with the additional title junior guardian of the heir apparent. Sect rebels led by Zhang Hanchao and the Blue and White factions were ravaging Shaanxi and Gansu. Songyun arrived, based himself at Hanzhong, and organized grain and pay for the armies. Since the war began Shaanxi had received eleven million taels in pay; he secured another 1.5 million, set up an office to clear accounts, and reported to the ministry every ten days. Ordered to assess the generals, he wrote secretly: "Mingliang knows war but delivers no real results; Heng Rui did best in the earlier Hubei fighting, but he is nearly sixty and much diminished in vigor; Qingcheng is brave but lacks strategy; Yongbao has neither strategy nor courage, cannot command troops, and cannot govern civilians either; only Erledengbao and Delengtai can handle the rebels." Emperor Renzong warmly approved. Mingliang accused Yongbao and Qingcheng of shirking the enemy; Songyun was ordered to arrest and try them. Yongbao also joined Jingzhou general Xingzhao in accusing Mingliang of falsifying battle reports; all were stripped of office and minister Nayancheng was sent to Shaanxi for a joint trial. By then Mingliang had killed Zhang Hanchao in battle; Songyun asked to lighten his case and to keep Salar Muslim troops under Qingcheng for joint operations—the emperor refused. Nayancheng then accused Heng Rui of leaving nearly defeated Blue Banner rebels and returning to Shaanxi—blaming Songyun for the mistake. Songyun lost his court rank and bodyguard title but kept the governorship. Sichuan rebels struck Nanzheng and sent columns against Xixiang, Mian County, and Lueyang. Songyun had long believed most rebels were coerced and could be talked into surrendering, and wanted to ride out to them alone. Vice general Han Jiaye urged him firmly: "If they refuse your appeal and the governor-general is killed, the empire's prestige will suffer and the world will laugh. Let me go first." Jiaye was indeed killed. The rebels fled into Huixian and Liangdang. In the spring of the fifth year Erledengbao and Nayancheng joined forces against them, and the rebels split up and fled. Changlin replaced him as governor-general of Shaanxi and Gansu; Songyun was appointed general of Ili but never took up the post and temporarily governed Huguang instead. He asked to come to court in person on military affairs and first memorialized from Shaanxi: "Rebels are not hardest to defeat while unrest continues, but when suppression is nearly finished. After pacification, relax bans on private salt and coining so leftover rebels and discharged soldiers can make a living." The emperor thought this impractical and ignored it. In Beijing he pressed the same point again, offended the throne, was reduced to vice commander, and sent as brigade commander at Ili.
12
七年，擢伊犁將軍。 乾隆中屢詔伊犁屯田，皆以灌溉乏水未大興，松筠力任其事，預計安插官兵。 惠遠城需八萬畝，惠寧城需四萬畝，乃於伊犁河北引水開渠，逶迤數十里，又於城西北導水泉。 凡兩城有水之地皆開渠，授田為世業，給穀種、田器、馬牛。 然旗人多驕逸，或殺食所給牛，鬻田器棄不耕，反覆曉諭始聽命。 比去任，凡墾田六萬四千畝。 寧遠叛兵蒲大芳等譴戍塔爾巴哈台，其黨馬友元等分戍南路諸城。 十三年冬，大芳复謀逆，捕其黨五十餘人誅之。 次年，檄調馬友元等百餘人赴伊犁種地，悉斬於途。 詔斥未鞫而殺，失政體，降喀什噶爾參贊大臣。 复授陝甘總督。
In the seventh year he was promoted to general of Ili. Under Qianlong repeated orders had called for military farming at Ili, but poor irrigation had kept it small; Songyun threw himself into the work and planned settlements for officers and troops. Huining needed eighty thousand mu and Huining New City forty thousand; he drew water from north of the Ili River into canals winding for miles and channeled springs northwest of the cities. Every watered tract in both cities was canalized; fields were granted as hereditary holdings, with seed grain, tools, horses, and cattle provided. But many bannermen were proud and lazy: some slaughtered the cattle issued to them, sold their tools, and left fields untilled—only after repeated admonition did they comply. When he left office, sixty-four thousand mu had been brought under cultivation. Ningyuan rebels such as Pu Dafang were exiled to Tarbagatai; followers like Ma Youyuan were posted to southern-route garrison towns. In the winter of the thirteenth year Dafang plotted rebellion again; over fifty accomplices were arrested and executed. The next year he summoned Ma Youyuan and more than a hundred others to farm at Ili—and had them all executed en route. The throne rebuked the killings without trial as a breach of proper governance and demoted him to Kashgar commissioner. He was again appointed governor-general of Shaanxi and Gansu.
13
調兩江總督。 南河自馬港口墊陷，黃水倒漾，淤運阻漕。 偕河督吳璥察勘海口，請復故道。 制疏沙器具，試之河口果驗； 又造撥船千艘，改小運船，親駐河干督趲，渡黃回空皆迅速。 迭疏論河務，宜引沁入衛，可利漕運。 又謂吳璥於黃泥嘴、俞家灘逢灣取直，以致停淤，為璥等論駁。 复密陳吳璥、徐端所論不實，工程虛捏，自請調任總河察其弊，又薦蔣攸銛、孫玉庭可任。 帝以鬆筠忠實，治河非所長，用攸銛為河督，責令相助為理。 尋兼署河督事。 十六年，調兩廣總督，協辦大學士，兼內大臣。 召為吏部尚書。
He was transferred to governor-general of the Two Jiangs. At Mapu Harbor the Southern Canal had collapsed; Yellow River water backed up, silted the transport channel, and blocked tribute grain. With canal director Wu Jin he surveyed the estuary and asked to restore the old channel. He devised dredging equipment; trials at the estuary proved it effective; he built a thousand lighters, refitted small transport boats, and personally supervised on the riverbank so Yellow River crossings and empty returns all went swiftly. In repeated memorials he argued that bringing the Qin River into the Wei would aid tribute transport. He also charged that at Huangnizui and Yujiatan Wu Jin had straightened bends and caused silt to stall—charges Jin and others rebutted. He secretly reported Wu Jin and Xu Duan's claims false and projects inflated, asked transfer as director-general of canals to investigate, and recommended Jiang Youqian and Sun Yuting. The emperor deemed Songyun honest but no river specialist, made Youqian canal director, and ordered Songyun to assist. Soon he also acted as canal director. In the sixteenth year he became governor-general of Guangdong and Guangxi, associate grand secretary, and grand minister of the interior. He was summoned as minister of personnel.
14
十七年，命往盛京會勘陵工，兼籌移駐宗室事，疏請小東門外建屋七十所，居閒散宗室七十戶，戶給田三十六畝。 又言：「西廠大凌河東有可耕地三千頃，可移駐二千餘戶。 東廠週數百里，地多積水，其水自北山柳條邊來，若相地開河，可涸出沃壤； 又東柳河溝亦多積水，若自北山東橫開大渠，可得沃壤數千頃。 」「續勘彰武台邊門外迤西牧廠閒地，橫三四十里，縱六七十里，並可移駐。 請於大凌河西廠東界先試墾種。 」詔並允行。 而試墾事為將軍晉昌奏罷，論者惜之。 回京，授軍機大臣。 未幾罷，改授御前大臣。
In the seventeenth year he was sent to Shenyang to survey tomb works and plan relocating clansmen; he asked for seventy houses outside the Small East Gate for seventy idle clansmen households, thirty-six mu each. He also wrote: "East of the West Factory on the Daliao River lie three thousand qing of arable land, enough for more than two thousand households." The East Factory spans hundreds of li with much standing water from the northern Willow Palisade hills; proper canal work could drain out rich soil; the Dongliu gully is likewise waterlogged; a major canal eastward from the northern hills could reclaim thousands of qing. "Further survey west of Zhangwu Platform found pasture idle land thirty to forty li wide and sixty to seventy long—also fit for relocation. He asked to begin trial farming on the West Factory's eastern border along the Daliao. " The throne approved all. Trial farming was halted by General Jin Chang's memorial, to later regret. Back in Beijing he was made grand councilor. Soon removed, he became grand minister in attendance.
15
十八年，復出為伊犁將軍，拜東閣大學士，改武英殿大學士。 以平定滑縣教匪，敘功，加太子太保。 詔偕參贊長齡通籌新疆南北諸城出納，量減內地饋運。 疏言：「北路塔爾巴哈台歲需內地銀四萬數千兩，南路回疆八城歲需內地銀五萬數千兩，地方貢賦皆入經費之內，無庸議減。 伊犁歲需內地經費銀六十萬兩，可撙節者無幾。 惟烏魯木齊為新疆腹地，歲需銀一百一十餘萬兩，宜裁減。 請復屯田，廣墾蘆灘荒地，開採銅鉛各礦，抽收迪化州、吐魯番木稅。 」又議綠營糧餉，凡倉儲充裕處，改給銀米各半，並復乾隆四十六年以前捐監之例，使邊地就近納粟。 所議或行或不行，於內地歲輸卒未大減。
In the eighteenth year he returned as general of Ili, grand secretary of the Eastern Pavilion, then of the Hall of Military Eminence. For pacifying Huaxian sect rebels he was rewarded with senior guardian of the heir apparent. He was ordered with Chang Ling to plan Xinjiang finances north and south and cut inland supply shipments. He wrote: "Tarbagatai needs over forty thousand taels a year from the interior; the eight southern cities over fifty thousand—local revenue already covers costs and need not be cut. Ili needs six hundred thousand taels yearly from the interior; little can be saved. Only Urumqi, Xinjiang's core, at over 1.1 million taels a year, should be trimmed. He asked to restore military farms, reclaim reed wasteland, open copper and lead mines, and levy timber taxes at Dihua and Turfan. " He also proposed Green Standard rations: where stores were ample, half silver and half grain, and restoring pre-Qianlong 46 grain purchase rules for nearby delivery. Some plans were adopted, some not; inland shipments were barely reduced.
16
喀什噶爾阿奇木伯克玉努斯聽其妻色奇納言，多不法，私與浩罕酋愛瑪爾交通。 愛瑪爾欲使尊為汗，遣使請自設哈子伯克，用浩罕稅例徵安集延商。 十九年，松筠巡視回疆，誅色奇納，械玉努斯，禁錮伊犁； 拒浩罕之請，斥去其使。 二十年，喀什噶爾回人仔牙敦作亂，親往治之。 仔牙敦就獲，與布魯特比圖爾第邁莫特並置極刑。 詔斥松筠不待命，削宮銜，召還京。 松筠初任時，築四堡於伊犁河北，議移置八旗散丁，事未竟而去。 再至，乃築室堡中，堡置百戶，戶授田三四十畝，三時務農，冬則肄武。 規畫粗備，以屬代者，而代者不置意，田遂荒。
Kashgar akim beg Yunus obeyed his wife Sekina in many unlawful acts and dealt secretly with Kokand chief Aimair. Aimair wanted him recognized as khan, asked to appoint a Khazi beg, and tax Andijan merchants by Kokand rates. In the nineteenth year Songyun toured the Muslim territories, executed Sekina, shackled Yunus, and imprisoned him at Ili; he refused Kokand and expelled its envoys. In the twentieth year Kashgar Muslim Ziyadun rebelled; Songyun went in person. Ziyadun was captured and, with Kirghiz chief Tuturdi Maimote, executed. The throne rebuked him for acting without orders, stripped rank, and recalled him. On his first tour he built four forts north of the Ili River to resettle scattered banner households but left before finishing. On his return he built housing in the forts—hundred households each, thirty to forty mu per household, farming three seasons and drilling in winter. The plan was largely ready for his successor, who ignored it, and the fields went fallow.
17
二十二年，詔來年幸盛京，抗疏諫阻，罷大學士，出為察哈爾都統，署綏遠城將軍。 逾年，子熙昌歿，帝憐之，召還為正白旗漢軍都統。 尋授禮部尚書，調兵部，复御前兼職。 未幾，出為盛京將軍。 松筠素以忠諒見重，在朝時，凡燕遊執御之事，乘間直言無避。 既屢忤旨，二十五年，以兵部遺失行印，追論，降山海關副都統。 復以事，迭降為驍騎校。 是年秋，仁宗崩於熱河，梓宮回京，宣宗步行於班僚中見之，扶而哭，翌日授左副都御史，擢左都御史。 其復起也，甚負時望，然卒不安於位，未一月，出為熱河都統。
In the twenty-second year, when a Shenyang tour was announced, he remonstrated, lost his grand secretary post, and was sent as Chahar commander and acting Suiyuan general. A year later his son Xichang died; the emperor pitied him and recalled him as Plain White Banner Chinese commander. Soon he was minister of rites, then war, again with attendance duties. Before long he was sent out as Shenyang general. Songyun was valued for loyalty and candor; at court he spoke frankly on banquets and outings without holding back. After repeated clashes with the throne, in the twenty-fifth year the War Ministry lost its traveling seal; past faults were pursued and he was demoted to Shanhaiguan vice commander. Further incidents reduced him step by step to vanguard commandant. That autumn Renzong died at Rehe; as the coffin returned, Daoguang walking among officials saw him, held him and wept, and next day made him left vice censor-in-chief, then left censor-in-chief. His return raised high hopes, yet within a month he was sent out as Rehe commander.
18
道光元年，召授兵部尚書，調吏部，復為軍機大臣。 二年，暫署直隸總督。 以代改理籓院奏稿，忤尚書禧恩，被劾，降六部員外郎。 尋授光祿寺卿，遷左都御史。 又出為盛京將軍，調吉林。 數年之中，兩召還朝，為左都御史、禮部尚書； 迭出署烏里雅蘇台將軍、熱河都統、直隸總督。 九年，調兵部尚書，往科布多鞫獄。 十年，往山西按巡撫徐炘被控事。 回疆方用兵，密疏有所論列，詔令陳善後方略，多被採納。 是年秋，自以衰病請罷，數日復請任使，詔斥進退自由，負優禮大臣之意。 又以前赴科布多囑道員徐寅代購什物，罷職，予三品頂戴休致。
In Daoguang 1 he was recalled as war minister, moved to personnel, and again grand councilor. In Daoguang 2 he briefly governed Zhili. Altering a Colonial Affairs draft for someone else offended Xi En; impeached, he was reduced to sixth-rank director. Soon he was director of imperial sacrifices, then left censor-in-chief. Again Shenyang general, then Jilin. Within years he was twice recalled as left censor-in-chief and minister of rites; and repeatedly sent to act as Uliasutai general, Rehe commander, and Zhili governor-general. In the ninth year he became war minister and went to Kobdo to judge cases. In the tenth year he went to Shanxi to investigate Governor Xu Xin. With war in the Muslim territories he wrote a secret memorial; ordered to propose postwar policy, much was adopted. That autumn he pleaded age and illness to retire, then days later asked for duty again; the throne rebuked such coming and going as unworthy of favored ministers. Because on the Kobdo trip he had intendant Xu Yin buy goods for him, he was dismissed with third-rank button and retired.
19
至十二年，浩罕遣使進表，松筠曾言浩罕通商，邊境可靖，帝思其言，复頭品頂戴，署正黃旗漢軍副都統。 命赴歸化城勘達爾漢、茂明安、土默特三部爭地，據乾隆朝圖記判定，三部皆悅服。 還，授理籓院侍郎，調工部，進正藍旗蒙古都統。 十四年，以都統銜休致。 逾年，卒，年八十有二，贈太子太保，依尚書例賜卹，諡文清，祀伊犁名宦祠。
By the twelfth year Kokand sent tribute; remembering Songyun's claim that Kokand trade would pacify the border, the emperor restored his third-rank button and made him acting Bordered Yellow vice commander. At Guihua he settled land disputes among Darhan, Maominggan, and Tumed by Qianlong maps; all three accepted. Returning, he was Colonial Affairs vice minister, then works minister, then Plain Blue Mongol commander. In the fourteenth year he retired with commander rank. A year later he died at eighty-two; posthumously senior guardian of the heir apparent, ministerial mourning grants, posthumous title Wenqing, enshrined at Ili.
20
松筠廉直坦易，脫略文法，不隨時俯仰，屢起屢蹶。 晚年益多挫折，剛果不克如前，實心為國，未嘗改也。 服膺宋儒，亦喜談禪。 尤施惠貧民，名滿海內，要以治邊功最多。
Songyun was upright, frank, and informal, unwilling to bend with fashion, and rose and fell many times. Late in life setbacks multiplied and his vigor faded, yet his sincere service never changed. He revered Song Neo-Confucianism and also loved Chan talk. He was especially kind to the poor; famed nationwide, his greatest work was on the frontier.
21
子熙昌，以廕生官至刑、工兩部侍郎，署熱河都統兼護軍統領。 數奉使赴各省按事，亦被信用。 嘉慶二十三年，卒於長沙，帝深惜之，贈都統，諡敬慎。
His son Xichang, by hereditary privilege rose to punishments and works vice minister, acting Rehe commander and army protector. He was often sent to provinces on imperial commissions and was trusted. In Jiaqing 23 he died at Changsha; the emperor mourned him, posthumously made him commander with posthumous title Jingjin.
22
富俊，字松岩，卓特氏，蒙古正黃旗人。 繙譯進士，授禮部主事，歷郎中。 累遷內閣蒙古侍讀學士、內閣學士，兼副都統。 嘉慶元年，擢兵部侍郎，充科布多參贊大臣。 四年，授烏魯木齊都統，調喀什噶爾參贊大臣。 歷葉爾羌辦事大臣、烏里雅蘇台參贊大臣。 召署鑲紅旗漢軍都統、兵部侍郎。
Fu Jun, styled Songyan, of the Zhuote clan, was a Bordered Yellow Mongol bannerman. A translation jinshi, he was a Rites director and rose to department director. He rose to Mongol reader in the Secretariat, grand secretary, and vice commander. In Jiaqing 1 he became war vice minister and Kobdo commissioner. In the fourth year he was Urumqi commander, then Kashgar commissioner. He served at Yarkand and Uliasutai. Recalled, he acted as Bordered Red Chinese commander and war vice minister.
23
八年，出為吉林將軍，調盛京。 清治民典旗地，限年首官，不首者治罪，追典價租息入官。 富俊疏言：「一年之內，一千六百餘案，應追繳者不下萬人，年久轉典，株連繁多。 旗、民多窮苦，既獲罪，又迫追呼，情實可憫，請悉寬免。 」允之。 十二年，考覈軍政，以潔己奉公，邊陲安輯，特詔褒美，予議敘。 十五年，因採葠攙雜，受屬員蔽，褫職，遣往吉林效力。 既而言官論關東三省賭博風熾，仁宗念富俊在官時曾嚴禁，即起授盛京工部侍郎，兼管奉天府尹及六邊邊門事務。 十八年，授黑龍江將軍，疏請內外臣工三年更調，及禁奢、講武數事，詔以更調非可限年，餘並嘉納。 又以東三省官兵技藝優嫺，每屆五年挑送京營，著為令。
In the eighth year he was Jilin general, then Shenyang. Qing rules required bannermen who mortgaged land to civilians to report yearly; otherwise they were punished and mortgage payments and interest seized. Fu Jun wrote: "In one year there were over 1,600 cases involving nearly ten thousand people to be pursued; years of sub-mortgages entangled many." Bannermen and civilians are mostly poor; convicted, they face collection—the case is pitiable; I ask full remission." " This was granted. In the twelfth year his military review won special praise for integrity and pacifying the frontier, with rewards ordered. In the fifteenth year adulterated ginseng and deception by subordinates cost him his post; he was sent to serve at Jilin. When censors decried gambling in the northeast, Renzong recalled Fu Jun's strict ban and restored him as Shenyang works vice minister overseeing Fengtian and six frontier gates. In the eighteenth year he became Heilongjiang general and proposed three-year rotations, bans on luxury, and military drill—the throne rejected fixed rotation but approved the rest. Because northeastern troops were skilled, the best were sent to capital garrisons every five years—codified as regulation.
24
十九年，調吉林將軍。 先是，議籌八旗生計，詔勘吉林荒地開墾，移駐京旗，將軍賽衝阿言拉林近地閒荒可墾，未有規畫。 富俊至，疏言：「乾隆中移駐京旗，建屋墾地，多藉吉林兵力，墾而不種，酌留數人教耕，一年後裁汰。 京旗蘇拉不能耕作，始而僱覓流民，久之田為民有，殊失國家愛育旗人之意。 今籌試墾，莫若先辦屯田。 請發吉林閒散旗人一千名為屯丁，每丁給銀二十五兩、耔種二石，官置牛具，人給荒地三十晌。 墾種二十晌，留荒十晌，四年徵糧，每晌一石。 十年後移駐京旗，人給熟地十五晌，荒五晌，餘十晌荒、熟各半，給原駐屯丁為忄互產，免徵其租。 因利而利，糜帑無多，將來京旗移到，得種熟地，與本處旗屯犬牙相錯，學耕夥種，實為有益。 」並詳列屯墾、出納、設官、經理事宜，詔如議行。
In the nineteenth year he was transferred to Jilin general. Earlier, planning banner livelihoods, the court ordered Jilin wasteland surveyed for reclamation and relocating capital banner households; General Saichonga said land near Lalin could be farmed, but no plan existed. Fu Jun wrote: "Under Qianlong, relocating capital banner households used Jilin labor to build and clear land, but little was planted—only a few instructors were kept a year. Capital banner bondservants could not farm; they hired civilians until the land became civilian holdings—defeating the state's care for bannermen. For trial reclamation, military colonies should come first. He asked for a thousand idle Jilin bannermen as colonists, each with twenty-five taels, two shi of seed, government oxen and tools, and thirty shang of wasteland. They would farm twenty shang and leave ten waste; after four years tax would be one shi per shang. After ten years capital households would relocate, each getting fifteen shang mature and five waste; ten shang half mature would become permanent property for the original colonists, rent-free. With modest expense, capital households would gain mature fields mingled with local colonies and learn joint farming—clear benefit. " He detailed colonization, finances, officials, and administration; the throne approved.
25
二十年，富俊親駐雙城子，地在拉林河西北，橫一百三十里，縱七十餘裡，沃衍宜耕。 遣員履丈，分撥伐木於拉林河上游，建立屯屋。 分五屯，設協領一、佐領二，分左右翼統治之，即名屯地曰雙城堡，於二十一年一律開墾。 是年霜早歉收，屯丁僅足餬口，又挈妻子者不敷居住，間有逃亡。 乃展緩徵糧一年，添蓋窩棚，借給耔種，心始安。 二十二年，調盛京。 疏陳雙城堡餘荒尚多，續發盛京、吉林旗丁各千名往墾，分左、右二屯，舊屯名為中屯，遂復調富俊吉林，任其事。 二十四年，先到屯丁千名，盛京旗人多有親族偕來，自原入屯，惟隸寧古塔者，因近地亦可耕荒，不原輕離鄉土，聽其還，以空額二百名改撥盛京。 二十五年，复續到千名。 富俊巡曆三屯，疏陳：「比屋環居，安土樂業，有井田遺風。 中屯開墾在先，麥苗暢發，男耕婦饁，俱極勤勞。 」仁宗大悅，報曰：「滿洲故里，佃田宅宅，洵善事也。 」續議三屯應增事宜，詔嘉實心任事，予議敘。 道光元年，疏言：「三屯開墾九萬數千晌，已著成效，可移駐京旗三千戶。 請自道光四年始，每歲移駐二百戶，給資裝車馬，分起送屯，官給房屋牛具。 」報可。 二年，召授理籓院尚書，與玉瀾堂十五老臣宴，御製詩有「勤勞三省，不凋松柏」之褒。
In the twentieth year Fu Jun personally oversaw Shuangchengzi, northwest of the Lalin River—130 by 70 li of fertile land. Officials measured the land and allocated logging on the upper Lalin to build colony housing. Five colonies were set with one assistant commander and two colonels for left and right wings; the settlement was named Shuangcheng Fort and opened in the twenty-first year. Early frost that year left colonists barely fed; families lacked housing and some fled. Tax was deferred a year, huts added, and seed lent until order returned. In the twenty-second year he was transferred to Shenyang. He reported much waste remained at Shuangcheng Fort; another thousand bannermen each from Shenyang and Jilin were sent, forming left and right colonies with the old as central—and Fu Jun returned to Jilin to manage it. In the twenty-fourth year the first thousand arrived; Shenyang bannermen often brought kin; Ningguta men, able to farm nearby, stayed home—two hundred slots went to Shenyang instead. In the twenty-fifth year another thousand arrived. Fu Jun toured three colonies and wrote: "Homes ring together; people are settled—a trace of well-field order. The central colony opened first; wheat thrived and men plowed while women brought meals—all very industrious. " Renzong was delighted: "In the Manchu homeland, farming and building homes—truly fine. " Further plans for the three colonies won praise for sincere service and reward consideration. In Daoguang 1 he wrote: "Three colonies have opened over ninety thousand shang with clear success; three thousand capital households can relocate. From Daoguang 4, two hundred households a year should relocate with travel funds, carts, and government housing and oxen. " Approved. In the second year he became Colonial Affairs minister and banqueted with the fifteen Jade Wave Hall elders; an imperial poem praised his diligence in three provinces.
26
四年，復出為吉林將軍。 方雙城堡之興屯也，富俊欲推其法於伯都訥圍場，以旗戶往往賴幫丁助耕，不如迳招民墾。 前後疏六七上，為廷議所格。 至是，復言伯都訥圍場荒地二十餘萬晌，募民屯墾，較雙城堡費半功倍，始允之。 五年，丈地分屯，申畫經界，名曰新城屯。 分八旗為兩翼，每翼初立二十五屯，後定為十五屯。 每屯三十戶，以「治本於​​農務滋稼穡」八字為號。 以次撥地，同時並墾。 至七年，陸續認佃三千六百戶，總為一百二十屯，與雙城堡相為表裡。 初議京旗每歲二百戶移駐雙城堡，至六年，僅陸續移到二百七十戶； 七年，續移八十五戶：而地利頓興，自此雙城堡、伯都訥兩地號邊方繁庶之區焉。
In the fourth year he was again Jilin general. When Shuangcheng Fort began, Fu Jun wanted the same for Boduna grounds, where bannermen relied on hired labor—better to recruit civilians. Six or seven memorials were blocked in court debate. He again said Boduna had over two hundred thousand shang of waste; civilian colonization would cost half Shuangcheng Fort—and won approval. In the fifth year land was surveyed, boundaries fixed, and the site named New City Colony. Eight Banners split into two wings, each first with twenty-five colonies, later fifteen. Each colony had thirty households, named by the phrase governing the root in farming to nourish grain. Land was allocated in turn and reclaimed together. By the seventh year 3,600 households formed 120 colonies alongside Shuangcheng Fort. Originally two hundred capital households a year were to move to Shuangcheng Fort; by the sixth year only 270 had arrived; in the seventh year another eighty-five; still the land prospered, and both became famed prosperous borderlands.
27
墾事既定，復召為理籓院尚書，協辦大學士，兼鑲黃旗漢軍都統。 次年，京察，以在吉林宣勞，予議敘。 疏言：「京、外競尚浮奢，官民服飾及冠婚、喪祭，任意踰制，有關風俗人心。 請依會典儀制，刊布規條，宣諭民間。 」詔下有司議行。 時富俊年逾八十，渥被優禮，遇常朝免其入直。 迭讞獄盛京、吉林，俱稱旨。 十年，調工部，拜東閣大學士，管理理籓院。 十二年，復請禁僭用服色，犯者拿捕，詔斥徒滋擾累，寢其議。 尋以天時亢旱，自稱奉職無狀，引年乞罷，不許。 授內大臣。 疏言：「科舉保薦，並認師生，餽遺關通，成為陋習。 請嚴禁，以端仕進。 」詔嘉納，申誡臣工務除積習。 十四年，卒。 帝悼惜，稱其「清慎公勤，克盡厥職」，贈太子太傅，親臨奠醊，諡文誠，入祀賢良祠。
Colonization done, he was again Colonial Affairs minister, associate grand secretary, and Bordered Yellow Chinese commander. Next year capital review ordered reward for his Jilin service. He wrote that capital and provinces competed in luxury, with dress and rites exceeding regulations—affecting morals. He asked to publish rules from the statutes and proclaim them. " Local officials were ordered to implement. Over eighty and richly favored, he was excused regular attendance. Judging cases in Shenyang and Jilin, he always pleased the throne. In the tenth year he became works minister, Eastern Pavilion grand secretary, and head of Colonial Affairs. In the twelfth year he again sought bans on forbidden dress colors; the throne called it harassment and dropped it. During drought he confessed failure and asked to retire; refused. He was made grand minister of the interior. He wrote that examinations and recommendations were corrupted by teacher-student favor and gifts. He asked strict prohibition to clean up advancement. " Praised and accepted, with orders to officials to end bad habits. In the fourteenth year he died. The emperor mourned him as "pure, cautious, diligent, and dutiful," posthumously grand tutor, libation in person, posthumous title Wencheng, enshrined among worthy officials.
28
富俊尚廉節，好禮賢士。 在吉林時，請調黑龍江戍員馬瑞辰掌教白山書院，且被嚴斥。 其治屯墾，專任竇心傳，卒以成功。
Fu Jun valued integrity and honored scholars. In Jilin he sought Ma Ruichen from Heilongjiang for Baishan Academy and was sternly rebuked. Colonization he entrusted solely to Dou Xinchuan and succeeded.
29
心傳，山西人。 以進士官奉天寧海知縣，坐東巡治御道有誤，罷職。 富俊知其才，闢佐墾務，規畫悉出手定，始終在事，以勞复官。 世比諸陳潢之佐靳輔治河。
Xinchuan was from Shanxi. A jinshi, he was Ninghai magistrate in Fengtian; dismissed for errors on an eastern tour road. Fu Jun knew his talent, used him for colonization—all plans were his; he stayed throughout and was restored for merit. He was compared to Chen Huang assisting Jin Fu on rivers.
30
博啟圖，一等誠嘉毅勇公明瑞孫。 嘉慶初襲爵，授頭等侍衛。 歷兵部侍郎、察哈爾都統。 道光七年，調吉林將軍，繼富俊之後，守其成規。 治邊有法，富俊請以屯墾專任之。 時京旗以邊地早寒，又助耕乏人，原往者少。 博啟圖疏請減戶增田，許其買僕代耕，統居中屯，改建住屋，俾便禦寒； 雖得請，尋召授工部尚書兼領侍衛內大臣，繼任者不果行其議，故移駐卒未如額。 十四年，卒，贈太子太保，諡敬僖。
Bo Qitu was grandson of first-rank Duke Mingrui. In early Jiaqing he inherited the title and became first-rank bodyguard. He was war vice minister and Chahar commander. In Daoguang 7 he became Jilin general after Fu Jun and kept his rules. He governed the border well; Fu Jun had wanted colonization solely his charge. Capital bannermen shunned the cold border and scarce labor and were reluctant to go. Bo Qitu asked fewer households with more land, servants to farm for them, central colony concentration, and warmer housing; approved, but soon summoned as works minister and chief bodyguard; his successor did not act, so relocation fell short. In the fourteenth year he died, posthumously senior guardian with posthumous title Jingxi.
31
論曰：保寧、松筠、富俊並出自籓族，久膺邊寄，晉綸扉，稱名相，伊犁、吉林屯田，利在百世； 然限於事勢，收效未盡如所規畫，甚矣締造之艱也！ 松筠在吉林，請開小綏芬屯墾，當時以不急之務沮之； 至咸、同間，其地竟劃歸俄界。 苟早經營，奚致輕棄？ 實邊之計，顧可忽哉！
The commentary: Bao Ning, Songyun, and Fu Jun came from frontier clans, long held border posts, reached the Grand Secretariat, and were famed ministers; Ili and Jilin colonies benefit generations; yet circumstances limited them and results fell short of plans—how hard the founding was! Songyun in Jilin asked to open Xiaosuifen for colonization but was blocked as non-urgent; by Xianfeng and Tongzhi it was ceded to Russia. Had it been developed earlier, how could it have been abandoned? Plans to secure the frontier must not be neglected!
