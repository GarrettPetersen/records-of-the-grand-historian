=李鶴年=李鶴年，字子和，奉天義州人。 道光二十五年進士，由編修改御史，轉給事中。 父憂歸，服除，命赴河南襄辦軍務。 同治元年，授常鎮通海道，署河南按察使，調直隸，授布政使。 四年，擢湖北巡撫，調河南。
Li Henian, whose courtesy name was Zihe, came from Yizhou in Fengtian. He earned his jinshi degree in the twenty-fifth year of Daoguang (1845), rose from Hanlin compiler to censor, and was then appointed supervising secretary. After returning home to observe mourning for his father and completing the mourning period, he was ordered to Henan to help manage military affairs. In the first year of Tongzhi (1862) he was made intendant of the Chang-Zhen-Tong-Hai circuit, served as acting judicial commissioner of Henan, transferred to Zhili, and was appointed provincial treasurer. In the fourth year (1865) he was promoted to governor of Hubei, then transferred to Henan.
2
時捻匪由山東南竄，鶴年以為十餘年來賊屢擾歸、陳、南、汝間，即去而他竄，必假道於豫。 乃增募兩軍各萬餘人，一曰毅軍，宋慶統之； 一曰嵩武軍，張曜統之； 更以馬隊屬善慶，與兩軍為犄角。 於是宋慶等軍大破張總愚睢州，鶴年親赴陳、留、杞督戰。 任、賴各逆复乘虛北擾，鶴年以賊踪無定，防河尤急。 賊果犯中牟，以有備不得逞，乃於省治西決堤引水南流，擾及長垣。 鶴年飛檄水陸各軍沿堤剿堵。 賊西走湖北麻城、黃岡，詔飭宋慶一軍越境會剿，殲賊無算。 鶴年自駐許州策應，賊竄裕州，慶擊敗之。 善慶及淮軍劉銘傳大敗賊贛榆，任柱被戕死。 賜鶴年頭品頂戴。 七年，奉命督師出境，馳抵磁州。 捻犯近畿，更由滑、濬等處沿河東趨。 坐防堵不力，再議處。 豫軍告捷，賞還頂戴。 總愚溺死，捻匪平，照一等軍功議敘。
When Nian rebels fled south from Shandong, Henian reasoned that for more than a decade they had repeatedly raided the Gui, Chen, Nan, and Ru region, and that whenever they moved on they would have to cross Henan. He accordingly recruited two new armies of more than ten thousand men each—the Yijun under Song Qing; and the Songwu Army under Zhang Yao; he also placed a cavalry detachment under Shan Qing to operate in concert with the two infantry forces. Song Qing's forces then routed Zhang Zongyu at Sui Prefecture, while Henian went in person to Chen, Liu, and Qi to direct operations. The rebel leaders Ren and Lai meanwhile slipped north to raid again; with rebel movements unpredictable, Henian regarded river defense as the top priority. The rebels did strike Zhongmou but were thwarted by prepared defenses; they then breached a dike west of the provincial seat to divert the river south, spreading havoc as far as Changyuan. Henian issued urgent orders to every land and river unit along the embankment to pursue and seal off the enemy. The rebels fled west into Macheng and Huanggang in Hubei; the court ordered Song Qing's army to cross the border for joint operations, killing rebels beyond count. Henian established his headquarters at Xuzhou to coordinate the campaign; when the rebels slipped into Yuzhou, Song Qing drove them back. Shan Qing and Liu Mingchuan of the Huai Army inflicted a crushing defeat on the rebels at Ganyu, where Ren Zhu was killed. Henian was awarded the first-rank official hat button. In the seventh year (1868) he was ordered to lead troops outside the province and rushed to Cizhou. Nian rebels threatened the capital region and pressed east along the river through Hua, Jun, and neighboring counties. He was censured for inadequate river defense and faced further disciplinary action. After Henan forces reported victory, his official hat button was restored as a reward. When Zongyu drowned and the Nian rebellion was suppressed, Henian was recommended for rewards commensurate with first-class military merit.
3
十年，擢閩浙總督。 明年，陛見，賜紫禁城騎馬。 旋署福州將軍，兼署巡撫。 詔詢海防事宜，覆奏言：「海防之策，莫重於練兵、籌餉、制器、用人四端。 四者之中，以用人為急務，而尤在專其責成。 沿海疆臣固責無旁貸，第無統率大員，仍恐意見紛歧，臨事推諉。 」上韙其議。
In the tenth year (1871) he was promoted to governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. The following year he was received in audience and granted the rare honor of riding a horse within the Forbidden City. He soon served as acting Fuzhou general and acting governor as well. When the court asked about coastal defense, he replied in a memorial: 'Coastal defense depends above all on four things: training troops, securing funds, manufacturing weapons, and appointing the right men. Of these four, personnel is the most urgent—and above all, each post must have a single official held clearly accountable. Coastal governors cannot evade their duty, yet without a senior commander in charge, opinions will splinter and officials will pass blame when crisis comes. ' The emperor endorsed his proposal.
4
光緒元年，調河東河道總督，兼署河南巡撫。 七年，授河南巡撫，仍兼河督。 十年，坐審辦盜犯胡體安連疏抗辨，部議革職，以祝嘏恩賞降二級職銜。 十三年，署河督，疏言：「黃河分流，自宋時河決澶州，分為二派。 明築黃陵岡，始合為一。 河性上漫則下淤，今兩路皆淤，急宜疏支河以預籌宣洩。 」報可。 逾年，鄭工復決，發軍台效力。 未幾釋歸，並賞三品銜。 十六年，卒。 宣統元年，開復原官。
In the first year of Guangxu (1875) he was made director-general of the Eastern Henan waterways and also served as acting governor of Henan. In the seventh year (1881) he was appointed governor of Henan while retaining his post as river commissioner. In the tenth year (1884) his repeated memorials disputing the trial of the bandit Hu Ti'an led the Board to recommend dismissal; under a congratulatory amnesty his post was spared but his rank was reduced by two grades. In the thirteenth year (1887) he served as acting river commissioner and memorialized: 'The Yellow River has long divided its current—since the breach at Chan Prefecture in Song times it has run in two channels. Only after the Ming built up Huangling Hill did the streams reunite into one. The river's habit is to deposit silt downstream when the upper course floods; with both channels now choked, branch streams must be dredged at once to provide outlets for floodwater. ' The memorial was approved. The following year the Zhengzhou embankment failed again, and he was sentenced to penal service at a military garrison. He was soon released to return home and was also granted a third-rank honorary title. He died in the sixteenth year (1890). In the first year of Xuantong (1909) his original rank was posthumously restored.
5
鶴年有知人鑑，少與文祥同學相淬厲。 及居言職，嚴疏劾肅順跋扈，而奏起曾國籓於家，謂必能辦賊。 拔宋慶、張曜統豫軍，後皆為名將。 治豫久，多善政，豫人刻石頌之。 始任河督，黑岡堤潰，不絕如縷。 鶴年親督工二十餘晝夜，險工克濟。 德宗嘗詢李鴻藻以善治河者，鴻藻舉鶴年，上亦識前事之枉也，故再任河督。 其卒也，豫民有流涕者。 三子葆恂博學多文，尤知名。
Henian was known for recognizing talent; in his youth he studied alongside Wen Xiang, and the two sharpened each other. As a censor he submitted stern memorials impeaching Sushun for arrogance and power, and urged the court to recall Zeng Guofan from retirement, declaring that Zeng alone could defeat the rebels. He elevated Song Qing and Zhang Yao to command Henan forces—both later became celebrated generals. His long tenure in Henan produced many sound policies, and locals erected stone inscriptions in his praise. When he first became river commissioner, the Heigang embankment was breached and barely held together. Henian personally directed repairs for more than twenty days and nights until the critical breach was secured. Emperor Dezong once asked Li Hongzao who excelled at river work; Hongzao named Henian, and the emperor, recognizing the earlier injustice done to him, appointed him river commissioner again. When he died, some Henan commoners wept openly. His third son Baoxun was widely learned and accomplished in letters and became especially renowned.

=文彬=文彬，字質夫，納喇氏，內務府滿洲正白旗人。 咸豐二年進士，授戶部主事。 十年，以員外郎隨扈幸熱河。 明年，遷郎中，出知山東沂州府。 捻匪逼府城，會師攻拔賊巢，擒匪首孫化詳等。 敘功，以道員用。 同治四年，隨布政使丁寶楨敗賊滕縣臨城驛，更繞赴東平防賊北竄。 補兗沂曹濟道，擢按察使。 收復海豐，擢布政使。 十年，署巡撫，補漕運總督。 再署巡撫，旋還任。
Wen Bin, courtesy name Zhifu, of the Nara clan, was a Manchu bannerman of the Plain White Banner attached to the Imperial Household Department. He passed the jinshi examination in the second year of Xianfeng (1852) and was appointed a secretary in the Ministry of Revenue. In the tenth year (1860) he accompanied the court to Rehe as an assistant department director. The following year he was promoted to department director and appointed prefect of Yizhou in Shandong. When Nian rebels threatened the prefectural seat, he coordinated a joint assault that took their stronghold and captured the chieftain Sun Huaxiang and others. For this achievement he was recommended for appointment as a circuit intendant. In the fourth year of Tongzhi (1865) he fought under Provincial Treasurer Ding Baozhen to defeat rebels at Lincheng Post in Teng County, then swung to Dongping to block a northward rebel breakout. He was appointed intendant of the Yan-Yi-Cao-Ji circuit and promoted to provincial judicial commissioner. After recovering Haifeng he was promoted to provincial treasurer. In the tenth year (1871) he served as acting governor and was appointed director-general of grain transport. He served again as acting governor, then returned to his transport post.
7
光緒五年，督漕北上，因請陛見，並與河督李鶴年、巡撫週恆祺會商運河事宜，通籌河道寬深，改設運口，導引衛河，設立堤壩，繪具圖說以進。 略謂：「現時北運口在張秋南八里廟，與南運口斜對，相距二十餘裡。 黃流至此雖收束，而溜勢散漫，歧汊甚多。 大抵溜勢近南則北口淤墊，近北則南口淺阻。 故漕船出南運口入黃後，必東北行二十里，至黃溜匯一之史家橋，再南行二十里，至八里廟北運口，汛水大漲，方能入運。 今擬移北運口於史家橋北六里。 黃河西岸，由阿城徬東堤開河一道至陶長堡，為出黃入運口門，築壩灌塘，則黃水不至奪溜，可免牽挽之難。 黃、運之間，自賈工合龍後，每伏秋大雨，水無所洩，民間低地有積水數年不得耕種者，若將陂水引歸一塘，不惟蓄水濟運，又可涸复民田。 運口既定，即可導引衛河。 自直隸元城集東三里衛河曲處鑿新河一道，經直隸之南樂、山東之朝城，至張秋南之蕭口涵洞入運。 計衛高於運九丈餘，長百五十餘裡，導以濟運，勢如建瓴。 更有大小二丹水，亦可由衛濟運。 凡建四徬二壩及挑河築堤，估銀七十六萬。 較之借黃濟運旋挑旋淤者，相去遠矣。 」
In the fifth year of Guangxu (1879) he led the grain fleet north and sought an audience, meeting with River Commissioner Li Henian and Governor Zhou Hengqi to plan the Grand Canal—widening and deepening the channel, relocating the transport junction, tapping the Wei River, building dikes and dams—and submitted detailed illustrated proposals. He summarized: 'Today the northern transport mouth lies at Balimiao south of Zhangqiu, set obliquely opposite the southern mouth some twenty-odd li away. The Yellow River current is somewhat narrowed here, yet the flow spreads out with many side channels. As a rule, when the current hugs the south bank the northern mouth silts up; when it hugs the north, the southern mouth shoals and blocks. Grain boats leaving the southern mouth must therefore sail northeast twenty li to Shijia Bridge where the Yellow currents meet, then south another twenty li to the northern mouth at Balimiao—and only when flood season raises the water high enough can they enter the Canal. We propose moving the northern transport mouth to a site six li north of Shijia Bridge. On the west bank we would cut a channel from the Acheng embankment eastward to Taochang Fort as the new Yellow-to-Canal junction; with dams and storage ponds, Yellow water would no longer capture the current and the arduous towing upstream could be avoided. Between the Yellow and the Canal, ever since the Jia embankment was closed, summer and autumn floods have had nowhere to go, leaving lowlands waterlogged for years. Channeling that standing water into a single reservoir would both store water for transport and let farmers reclaim their fields. Once the transport junction is fixed, the Wei River can be brought in. A new channel would be cut from a bend in the Wei three li east of Yuancheng Market in Zhili, running through Nanle in Zhili and Chaocheng in Shandong to enter the Canal at the Xiaokou culvert south of Zhangqiu. The Wei stands more than nine zhang above the Canal along a course of over 150 li—diverted into the Canal its flow would pour down like water from a rooftop eave. The greater and lesser Dan Rivers could likewise feed the Canal through the Wei. Building four sluices, two dams, and the dredging and diking work is estimated at 760,000 taels of silver. Compared with the present practice of borrowing Yellow River water for transport—where dredging is undone as quickly as it is done—the improvement would be immense. '
8
又嘗偕兩江總督吳元炳奏复淮流故道，略謂：「淮水匯四十餘河瀦於洪澤湖，楊莊以下云梯關為入海故道，餘波入運濟漕。 遇旱，复蓄淮流由運河分入淮揚各徬洞，以溉民田。 自洪澤湖不能瀦水，張、福引河又不通暢，每遇盛漲，運河一線東堤，其勢岌岌。 儻竟衝溢，不至以里下河為壑不止。 論者謂必設法束水，然與其上游議堵，何如下游深通。 」因條上疏濬楊莊以下舊河入海故道。
He also joined Governor-General Wu Yuanbing of the Two Jiang in a memorial to restore the Huai River's old channel, arguing: 'The Huai gathers more than forty tributaries into Hongze Lake; below Yangzhuang, Yunti Pass was its historic outlet to the sea, with surplus water feeding the Grand Canal for grain transport. In drought years, stored Huai water was distributed through Canal sluices across the Huai-Yang region to irrigate farmland. Since Hongze Lake can no longer hold water and the Zhang and Fu diversion channels are choked, every major flood puts the Canal's lone eastern embankment in grave danger. If it finally breaches, the Lixia River lowlands will be devastated. Some argue that the water must be confined—but rather than blocking it upstream, far better to open a deep channel downstream. ' He therefore submitted a detailed plan to dredge the old outlet to the sea below Yangzhuang.

未幾，卒，有詔褒錫。 兩江總督劉坤一以文彬遺愛在民，請建專祠清江浦，允之。 子延煜，舉人，四川鹽茶道； 延熙，舉人，九江知府； 延燮，進士，武昌知縣； 延照，舉人，禮部員外郎。
He died soon afterward, and the throne issued an edict of commendation. Governor-General Liu Kunyi of the Two Jiang, citing Wen Bin's enduring goodwill among the people, petitioned for a dedicated shrine at Qingjiangpu, which was approved. His son Yanxu, a provincial graduate, served as Sichuan salt and tea intendant; Yanxi, a provincial graduate, was prefect of Jiujiang; Yanxie, a jinshi, was magistrate of Wuchang; Yanzhao, a provincial graduate, was an assistant department director in the Ministry of Rites.

=任道鎔=任道鎔，字筱沅，江蘇宜興人。 拔貢，考授教職。 咸豐中，在籍襄辦團練，除奉賢訓導。 以籌餉勞，晉秩知縣，銓當陽，多善政，調江夏。 同治二年，擢知順德府。 畿南匪起，行堅壁清野法，修治城堡，屢擊賊於沙河、平鄉間。 會捻眾北犯，道鎔率練勇守沙河。 夜與賊遇，揮眾奮擊，矛傷及身，不退，賊徐引去，晉秩道員。 洺河自廣平入，久淤塞。 道鎔與鄰郡合濬，又濬郡北響水河，复民田萬餘頃。 總督曾國籓、李鴻章迭薦之。 十一年，調保定，尋擢開歸陳許道。 剔河工積弊，驗工料必以實。 嘗冒風雨搶護中河險工，四晝夜始定。
Ren Daorong, courtesy name Xiaoyuan, came from Yixing in Jiangsu. As a selected tribute student he passed the examination and received an educational appointment. During the Xianfeng era he helped organize local militia while at home and was appointed director of studies at Fengxian. For his work raising military funds he was promoted to magistrate, appointed to Dangyang where he earned a reputation for sound governance, then transferred to Jiangxia. In the second year of Tongzhi (1863) he was promoted to prefect of Shunde. When bandits rose south of the capital region, he enforced fortified villages and cleared countryside, repaired fortifications, and repeatedly defeated rebels between Shahe and Pingxiang. When Nian forces pushed north, Daorong led local militia to hold Shahe. In a night encounter he led his men in a fierce charge, took a spear wound yet held his ground until the rebels slowly withdrew; he was then promoted to circuit intendant. The Ming River, entering from Guangping, had long been choked with silt. Daorong joined neighboring prefectures in dredging it and also cleared the Xiangshui River north of the prefecture, restoring more than ten thousand qing of farmland. Governors-general Zeng Guofan and Li Hongzhang repeatedly recommended him. In the eleventh year (1872) he was transferred to Baoding and soon promoted to intendant of the Kai-Gui-Chen-Xu circuit. He rooted out longstanding abuses in river engineering and insisted on verifying materials and labor on the ground. He once worked through wind and rain to shore up a critical breach on the middle reach of the river, and the danger was not contained until four days and nights had passed.
11
光緒元年，署按察使。 授江西按察使，省獄羈囚四百餘人，道鎔便宜訊決，三月而清。 四年，遷浙江布政使，調直隸。 直隸自軍興，州縣報銷未清，又數值謁陵大差，交代糾葛。 道鎔分別新舊案，定限清結。 裁革州縣攤捐，實發養廉銀以卹吏，勸屬縣積穀備荒。 七年，擢山東巡撫，疏陳營務廢弛，易置統將，以綠營額餉練新軍，責郡縣勤緝捕。 泰山、沂水之間，驛路崎嶇，發卒開治平坦，行旅便之。 旋以保獎已革知府潘駿群被議，又以失察編修林國柱預報起復，被劾褫職，降道員。 家居久之。
In the first year of Guangxu (1875) he served as acting provincial judicial commissioner. As judicial commissioner of Jiangxi he found more than four hundred prisoners languishing in the provincial jail; exercising discretionary authority to hear and decide cases, he cleared the backlog within three months. In the fourth year (1878) he was made provincial treasurer of Zhejiang, then transferred to Zhili. Since the civil wars began, Zhili's prefectures and counties had left accounts unsettled, and repeated imperial tomb duties had tangled successive administrations in disputes over handovers. Daorong sorted old from new cases and set firm deadlines for settlement. He abolished arbitrary county levies, paid out integrity stipends in full to support officials, and urged subordinate counties to build grain reserves against famine. In the seventh year (1881) he was promoted to governor of Shandong and memorialized that military discipline had collapsed, urging replacement of commanders, use of Green Standard funds to train new troops, and strict pursuit of bandits by prefectures and counties. Between Mount Tai and the Yi River the post road was treacherous; he sent workers to level it, greatly easing travel. Soon afterward he was censured for having recommended the dismissed prefect Pan Junqun for reward, and impeached for failing to detect that Compiler Lin Guozhu had been tipped off about his restoration from mourning; he was stripped of office and reduced to circuit intendant. He lived in retirement for many years.
12
二十一年，起河道總督。 故事，河督，開封、濟寧並設行署。 自咸豐時，常駐開封，山東河事由巡撫專治。 至是復改議河督駐濟寧，而河南巡撫兼治河。 道鎔言：「官吏不相屬，則令難行，不如仍舊便。 」報可。 時河患多在下游，河督專司上游，事簡。 道鎔務節費，歲以餘帑還司庫。 二十六年，拳匪起，河南奸民乘機煽亂。 道鎔處以鎮靜，練河標三營助省防。 次年，調浙江巡撫。 承國威新挫後，民教相閧，案多未結，持平訊決之。 籌集償款，衡其緩急，民不重困。 二十八年，乞病歸。 逾三年，卒於家，年八十三。
In the twenty-first year (1895) he was recalled to serve as director-general of waterways. By precedent the river commissioner maintained branch offices at both Kaifeng and Jining. Since the Xianfeng era the commissioner had usually resided at Kaifeng, while Shandong river affairs were handled by the provincial governor. Now it was proposed again that the river commissioner reside at Jining while the Henan governor would also oversee river work. Daorong argued: 'When officials do not stand in a clear chain of command, orders cannot be enforced; it is better to keep the existing arrangement. ' The proposal was approved. River disasters were then concentrated in the lower reaches, while the commissioner was responsible only for the upper course—a lighter burden. Daorong practiced strict economy and each year returned surplus funds to the provincial treasury. In the twenty-sixth year (1900) the Boxer movement erupted, and Henan ruffians seized the chance to stir up disorder. Daorong kept the situation calm and drilled three battalions of river troops to reinforce provincial defenses. The following year he was transferred to governor of Zhejiang. After the recent humiliation of national prestige, clashes between locals and missionaries had left many cases unresolved; he heard them impartially and brought them to judgment. He raised indemnity payments according to what was urgent and what could wait, sparing the people a crushing double burden. In the twenty-eighth year (1902) he retired on grounds of illness. More than three years later he died at home, aged eighty-three.
13
=許振禕=許振禕，字仙屏，江西奉新人。 咸豐初，以拔貢生參曾國籓戎幕。 迨楚軍困於江西，都邑相繼陷，振禕偕內閣中書鄧輔綸募鄉兵擊賊進賢、東鄉，旋复吉安。 敘功，以同知銓選。 同治二年，成進士，授職編修，出督陝甘學政。 時河州降回复叛，而西寧諸郡回、漢民亦日相仇殺，試事久停不舉。 振禕始按試各郡，多錄降人子弟，補行八次歲科試，入學者數千人，回民大服。 建味經書院於涇陽，廣置書籍，以化其獷俗。 又請陝、甘分闈鄉試，各設學政，允之。 總督左宗棠以謂邊氓長治久安之效，胥基於此。 父憂歸。
Xu Zhenyi, courtesy name Xianping, came from Fengxin in Jiangxi. Early in the Xianfeng era he joined Zeng Guofan's staff as a selected tribute student. When Zeng's Hunan Army was trapped in Jiangxi and town after town fell, Zhenyi joined Secretariat Drafter Deng Fulun in raising local militia to attack rebels at Jinxian and Dongxiang, and soon recovered Ji'an. For this service he was recommended for appointment as sub-prefect. In the second year of Tongzhi (1863) he passed the jinshi examination, was appointed Hanlin compiler, and sent out as educational commissioner of Shaanxi and Gansu. Surrendered Muslims in Hezhou had rebelled again, and in the Xining region Hui and Han communities were killing one another daily; civil examinations had long been suspended. Zhenyi began holding examinations in each prefecture, enrolling many sons of former rebels, and made up eight years of missed annual tests; thousands entered the schools, and the Muslim population was deeply impressed. He founded the Weijing Academy at Jingyang and stocked it with books to soften the region's rough frontier ways. He also petitioned for separate provincial examination compounds and educational commissioners for Shaanxi and Gansu, which was approved. Governor-General Zuo Zongtang declared that lasting peace on the frontier would rest on measures such as these. He returned home to observe mourning for his father.
14
光緒二年，起故官。 八年，授彰衛懷道，減屬縣差徭費歲二十餘萬。 豫修里河堤防，淮海各鹽區得免水患。 十六年，擢河東河道總督，築滎澤大壩，胡家屯、米童寨各石壩，河賴以無患。 其要尤在嚴稽察，不私財權，令七廳徑赴司庫支領，故積弊徐而工堅。 二十一年，遷廣東巡撫，禁賭闈姓，粵民利賴之。 二十四年，裁廣東、雲南、湖北三巡撫缺，振禕調內用。 乞假歸，逾年卒。 附祀江蘇、河南曾國籓祠。
In the second year of Guangxu (1876) he was recalled to his former post. In the eighth year (1882) he was appointed intendant of the Zhang-Wei-Huai circuit and cut corvée and levy costs in subordinate counties by more than two hundred thousand taels a year. He repaired inner-river embankments in advance, sparing the Huai and Hai salt districts from flooding. In the sixteenth year (1890) he was promoted to director-general of the Eastern Henan waterways, built the great dam at Xingze and stone dams at Hujiatun and Mitongzhai, and the river remained secure. His key reform was strict auditing: he refused to hoard financial authority and required all seven river offices to draw funds directly from the treasury, so abuses faded and construction grew sound. In the twenty-first year (1895) he was made governor of Guangdong, banned gambling and the surname lottery, to the great benefit of the Cantonese people. In the twenty-fourth year (1898) the governorships of Guangdong, Yunnan, and Hubei were abolished, and Zhenyi was transferred to the capital. He took leave to return home and died the following year. He was given a place in Zeng Guofan's memorial shrines in Jiangsu and Henan.
15
=吳大澂=吳大澂，字清卿，江蘇吳縣人。 同治元年秋，彗星見西北，詔求直言。 大澂方為諸生，入都應京兆試。 上書言：「致治之本，在興儉舉廉，不言理財而財自裕。 若專務掊克，罔恤民艱，其國必敝。 」後六年成進士，授編修。 穆宗大婚典禮隆縟，疏請裁減繁費，直聲震朝右。 出為陝甘學政，奏以倉頡列祀典，允之。 又薦諸生賀瑞麟、楊樹椿篤志正學，給瑞麟國子監學正銜，樹椿翰林院待詔銜，士風為之一變。 時詔修頤和園，大澂復言時事艱難，請停止工作。 疏入，留中。
Wu Dacheng, courtesy name Qingqing, came from Wu County in Jiangsu. In the autumn of the first year of Tongzhi (1862) a comet appeared in the northwest, and the throne called for candid memorials. Dacheng was then a common student and had come to the capital for the metropolitan examination. He submitted a memorial stating: 'The foundation of good government is to promote frugality and advance the incorrupt; speak not of revenue schemes, and the treasury will fill of itself. But if officials devote themselves solely to squeezing the people and ignore their hardship, the state will surely be ruined. ' Six years later he passed the jinshi examination and was appointed Hanlin compiler. When Emperor Muzong's lavish wedding ceremonies were planned, he memorialized to cut redundant expenses, and his forthright reputation resounded through the court. As educational commissioner of Shaanxi and Gansu he memorialized to add Cangjie, inventor of writing, to the state sacrificial canon, which was approved. He also recommended the scholars He Ruilin and Yang Shuchun for their devotion to orthodox learning; Ruilin received the title of Imperial Academy director of studies and Shuchun the Hanlin awaiting-edict title, and scholarly standards in the region were transformed. When the court ordered repairs to the Summer Palace, Dacheng again memorialized that times were hard and urged that the work be stopped. The memorial was received but shelved without action.
16
光緒三年，山、陝大饑，奉命襄辦賑務。 躬履災區查勘，全活甚眾。 左宗棠、曾國荃、李鴻章等交章論薦。 四年，授河北道。 時比歲薦飢，貧民減價鬻田，十不得一。 巡撫塗宗瀛飭荒歲賤價之田準取贖，然往往為勢家所持，以故失業者眾。 惟大澂能判決如巡撫恉。
In the third year of Guangxu (1877) Shanxi and Shaanxi suffered severe famine, and he was ordered to assist relief efforts. He toured the disaster zone in person and saved countless lives. Zuo Zongtang, Zeng Guoquan, Li Hongzhang, and others submitted repeated memorials recommending him. In the fourth year (1878) he was appointed intendant of the Hebei circuit. Famine had struck year after year, and poor peasants sold their land at a fraction of its value—often receiving only a tenth of what it was worth. Governor Tu Zongying had ruled that land sold cheaply in famine years could be redeemed at the sale price, but powerful families often blocked redemption, leaving many peasants landless. Only Dacheng enforced the governor's policy as intended.
17
六年，詔給三品卿銜，隨吉林將軍銘​​安辦理西北邊防。 大澂週曆要隘，始知琿春黑頂子地久為俄人侵占。 因請頒舊界圖，將定期與俄官抗議，未得旨。 時有韓效忠者，登州人，傭於復州侯氏。 負博進，遁往吉林夾皮溝。 地產金，在寧古塔、三姓東，萬山環繞，廣袤七八百里。 流冗嘯聚其中，亡慮四五萬，咸受效忠約束。 效忠嚴而不擾，眾服其公允，屢抗大軍不出。 大澂單騎抵其巢，留宿三日，勸效忠出，效忠猶豫，意難之。 大澂曰：「我不疑若，若乃疑我耶？ 」對曰：「非敢疑公。 某負罪久，萬一主兵者執前事為罪。 某死不恨，辜公意奈何？ 」大澂挺以自任，遂與效忠出，奏給五品頂戴，子七品，孫登舉有平寇功，授參將。 七年，授太僕寺卿。 法越事起，會辦北洋軍務，駐防樂亭、昌黎。
In the sixth year (1880) he was granted third-rank minister status and sent with Jilin General Ming'an to handle northwestern frontier defense. Touring the strategic passes, Dacheng discovered that Hundingzi near Hunchun had long been occupied by Russia. He petitioned for the old boundary maps and planned to protest to Russian officials on a fixed date, but received no imperial authorization. At that time there was a man named Han Xiaozhong from Dengzhou, employed by the Hou family of Fuzhou. Deep in gambling debt, he fled to Jiapigou in Jilin. The region produced gold, lying east of Ningguta and Sanxing, ringed by mountains and stretching seven or eight hundred li. Vagrant laborers had gathered there, perhaps forty or fifty thousand in all, all under Xiaozhong's authority. Xiaozhong ruled strictly but fairly, winning the people's respect, and had repeatedly defied government armies from his mountain stronghold. Dacheng rode alone into the stronghold, stayed three days, and urged Xiaozhong to surrender; Xiaozhong hesitated, troubled in his mind. Dacheng said: 'I do not doubt you—are you doubting me? ' Xiaozhong replied: 'I would not dare doubt Your Excellency. I have long been a wanted man; if the commander in chief holds my past against me— my own death would not trouble me, but how could I betray Your Excellency's trust? ' Dacheng pledged his personal guarantee, and Xiaozhong came out with him. The court awarded Xiaozhong a fifth-rank hat button, his son seventh rank, and his grandson Dengju a colonel's commission for helping pacify the bandits. In the seventh year (1881) he was appointed vice minister of the Court of the Imperial Stud. When the Franco-Vietnamese crisis erupted he was assigned to coordinate Beiyang military affairs and garrisoned Leting and Changli.
18
十年，遷左副都御史。 俄，命使朝鮮，定其內亂，鹽運使續昌副之。 至則日本使​​臣井上馨避不肯見，而挾朝鮮左議政金宏集於議政院，索償兵費三十萬。 大澂謂續昌曰：「是蔑我也！ 」立率兵至議政院，排闥入，責數宏集：「柄國敗壞國事。 今定約稍不慎，便滋異日紛，非所以靖國也。 」宏集唯唯，井上馨亦氣懾，減索兵費十一萬而去。
In the tenth year (1884) he was promoted to left vice censor-in-chief. Soon he was appointed envoy to Korea to settle its civil strife, with Salt Transport Commissioner Xuchang as his deputy. On arrival he found the Japanese minister Inoue Kaoru avoiding him, while holding Korea's chief councilor Kim Hong-jip at the State Council and demanding three hundred thousand taels in military indemnity. Dacheng said to Xuchang: 'This is an insult to me! ' He immediately led troops to the State Council, forced his way in, and rebuked Kim Hong-jip: 'You have wielded power and ruined the country's affairs. Any carelessness in the treaty you sign now will breed future conflict—this is no way to secure the country. ' Kim Hong-jip could only assent; Inoue Kaoru was intimidated as well and reduced the indemnity demand to one hundred ten thousand taels before withdrawing.
19
十一年，詔赴吉林，會同副都統伊克唐阿與俄使勘侵界，即所侵琿春黑頂子地也。 遂援咸豐十一年舊界圖立碑五座，建銅柱，自篆銘曰：「疆域有表國有維，此柱可立不可移。 」於是侵界復歸中國，而船之出入圖們江者亦卒以通航無阻。 十二年，擢廣東巡撫。 葡萄牙侵界至澳門香山。 總署與立約通商，畫澳門歸葡轄。 大澂持不可，條上駁議，不報。
In the eleventh year (1885) he was ordered to Jilin to join Vice Commander Yiktangga and the Russian envoy in surveying the encroached border—the Hundingzi territory near Hunchun. Using the boundary map of the eleventh year of Xianfeng (1861), he erected five boundary steles and bronze pillars, personally inscribing them: 'Boundaries have their markers; the state has its pillars—this pillar may be raised but never moved. ' The encroached territory was restored to China, and shipping on the Tumen River was secured without further obstruction. In the twelfth year (1886) he was promoted to governor of Guangdong. Portugal had encroached on Chinese territory as far as Xiangshan near Macau. The Zongli Yamen negotiated a commercial treaty ceding Macau to Portuguese jurisdiction. Dacheng objected and submitted a detailed rebuttal; no response came.
20
十四年，鄭州河再決，上震怒，褫河督李鶴年職，以大澂代之。 是年冬，河工合龍，大澂力居多。 大澂盛負時譽，會海軍議起，以醇親王奕枻為總理。 大澂素與王善，治河功成，實授河道總督，加頭品頂戴。 大澂遂疏請尊崇醇親王稱號禮節。 疏入，孝欽顯皇后震怒，出醇親王元年所上預杜妄論疏頒示天下。 大澂幾得嚴譴，以母喪歸，乃已。
In the fourteenth year (1888) the Zhengzhou embankment failed again; the emperor was furious, dismissed River Commissioner Li Henian, and replaced him with Dacheng. That winter the breach was closed, largely through Dacheng's efforts. Dacheng enjoyed great contemporary renown; when naval affairs came under discussion, Prince Chun Yixuan was appointed superintendent. Dacheng had long been on good terms with the prince; after his river success he was formally appointed director-general of waterways and awarded the first-rank hat button. Dacheng then memorialized requesting elevated titles and ceremonial honors for Prince Chun. When the memorial arrived, Empress Dowager Cixi was furious and ordered circulated throughout the empire the memorial Prince Chun had submitted in his first year warning against reckless court debate. Dacheng nearly faced severe punishment, but returned home to observe mourning for his mother, and the matter was dropped.
21
十八年，授湖南巡撫。 朝鮮東學黨之亂也，日本與中國開釁，朝議皆主戰。 大澂因自請率湘軍赴前敵，優詔允之。 二十一年，出關會諸軍規复海城，而日本由間道取牛莊。 魏光燾往禦，戰不利。 李光久馳救之，亦敗，僅以數騎免。 大澂憤湘軍盡覆，拔劍欲自裁，王同愈在側，格阻之，同愈以編修參大澂軍事也。 光燾請申軍法，大澂歎曰：「餘實不能軍，當自請嚴議。 」退入關，奉革職留任之旨。 乃還湖南，尋命開缺。 二十四年，复降旨革職永不敘用。 二十八年，卒，年六十八。
In the eighteenth year (1892) he was appointed governor of Hunan. During the Donghak rebellion in Korea, Japan and China went to war, and the court overwhelmingly favored fighting. Dacheng volunteered to lead Hunan troops to the front, and the throne graciously approved. In the twenty-first year (1895) he crossed the frontier to join allied forces in an attempt to recover Haicheng, while Japan seized Niuzhuang by a flanking route. Wei Guangtao went to meet them but was defeated. Li Guangjiu galloped to the rescue but was beaten as well, escaping with only a handful of horsemen. Dacheng, furious that the Hunan army had been annihilated, drew his sword to kill himself; Wang Tongyu, who served on his staff as a Hanlin compiler, seized his arm and stopped him. Guangtao called for military justice; Dacheng sighed and said: 'I am no commander—I should petition for severe punishment myself. ' He withdrew beyond the frontier and received an order removing him from office while temporarily retaining his post. He returned to Hunan and was soon ordered to vacate his post entirely. In the twenty-fourth year (1898) a further edict permanently dismissed him from office with no prospect of reinstatement. He died in the twenty-eighth year (1902), aged sixty-eight.
22
大澂善篆籀，罷官後，貧甚，售書畫、古銅器自給。 著有古籀補、古玉圖考、權衡度量考、恆軒古金錄、齋詩文集。
Dacheng was accomplished in seal and bronze script; after leaving office he lived in great poverty, supporting himself by selling calligraphy, paintings, and antiquities. His published works include Supplement to Ancient Seal Script, Study of Ancient Jades Illustrated, Study of Weights and Measures, Hengxuan Record of Ancient Bronzes, and collected poetry and prose.
23
=【論】=論曰：河患日棘，而河臣但歲慶安瀾，即為奇績，久未聞統全局而防永患，求治難矣。 鶴年以善治河稱，文彬論治河改運口，复淮流，亦頗有識。 道鎔剔河工積弊，務節減，振禕督工嚴，盡革中飽，尤以勤廉者，皆足收一時之效，然徒治標，非治本計也。 大澂治河有名，而好言兵，才氣自喜，卒以虛憍敗，惜哉！
Commentary: River disasters grow more pressing by the year, yet river officials treat each year's calm waters as a marvel and call it achievement; for ages no one has managed the whole system to prevent lasting catastrophe—real order remains elusive. Henian was renowned for river work; Wen Bin's proposals to relocate the transport junction and restore the Huai River also showed real insight. Daorong rooted out longstanding river abuses and practiced economy; Zhenyi supervised construction strictly and stamped out graft—all diligent and incorruptible men who achieved real short-term results, yet their efforts treated symptoms, not causes. Dacheng was famous for river work but loved military glory, self-confident and boastful of his talents, and in the end was ruined by empty arrogance—a pity indeed!
