太宗系
Line of Emperor Taizong (Hong Taiji)
福玄胤弘永綿奕載溥毓恆啟燾闓
豪格。太宗第一子。初封貝勒。天聰六年，晉和碩貝勒。崇德元年，以功晉肅親王。尋緣事降貝勒。二年，復封肅親王。六年，降郡王。七年，以功復肅親王。順治元年，緣事削爵。尋復封肅親王。五年，革爵幽禁自盡。七年，以無辜被害，追封肅親王。十三年，追諡曰武。乾隆四十三年，以佐命殊功配享太廟。國泰。豪格第一子。順治十年，封輔國將軍。康熙三年，緣事革退。
握赫納。豪格第三子。順治十年，封輔國將軍。康熙元年，卒。武禮。握赫納第一子。康熙元年，襲三等奉國將軍。五十一年，緣事革退。國訥。武禮子。封奉國將軍。緣事革退。松健。圖訥子。封奉恩將軍。
額爾德蒙額。圖訥子。封奉恩將軍。
富綬豪格第四子。順治八年，襲親王，改號曰顯。康熙八年，薨。諡曰慤。丹臻富綬第四子。康熙九年，襲顯親王。四十一年，薨。諡曰密。成信丹臻第二子。康熙四十七年，封三等奉國將軍。乾隆二十三年，卒。四十三年，追封肅親王。永俊成信第二子。乾隆十七年，封奉恩將軍。二十八年，卒。春韶永俊第三子。乾隆二十二年，封奉恩將軍。四十七年，卒。
永錫成信第五子。乾隆四十三年，襲肅親王。道光元年，薨。諡曰恭。敬敏永錫第一子。乾隆六十年，封不入八分輔國公。道光元年，襲肅親王。咸豐二年，薨。諡曰慎。華連敬敏第一子。嘉慶十七年，封三等輔國將軍。道光九年，卒。無嗣。
華豐敬敏第三子。道光四年，封三等鎮國將軍。九年，封不入八分輔國公。咸豐三年，襲肅親王。同治八年，薨。諡曰恪。隆懃華豐第三子。同治元年，封二等鎮國將軍。九年，襲肅親王。光緒二十四年，薨。諡曰良。善耆隆懃第一子。光緒十二年，封二等鎮國將軍。二十四年，襲肅親王。憲章善耆第一子。光緒三十二年，封不入八分輔國公。
憲德善耆第二子。光緒三十二年，封不入八分輔國公。
善豫隆懃第二子。光緒十四年，封二等鎮國將軍。

隆普華豐第十子。光緒三年，封二等鎮國將軍。
隆鑑華豐第十一子。光緒十四年，封二等鎮國將軍。

恆訓敬敦第二子。道光二十四年，封輔國將軍。光緒九年，卒。盛昆恆訓第一子。光緒十年，襲奉國將軍。

延德丹臻第七子。康熙五十年，封三等奉國將軍。乾隆二十四年，因病告退。寶銘延德第三子。乾隆二十四年，襲奉恩將軍。五十八年，卒。純裕寶銘第一子。乾隆五十九年，襲奉恩將軍。尋卒。祥璧純裕第一子。乾隆六十年，襲奉恩將軍。道光二十七年，卒。靈秀祥璧第二子。道光二十八年，襲奉恩將軍。同治三年，因病告退。崇恩靈秀第一子。同治四年，襲奉恩將軍。光緒二十年，卒。襲次已盡，不襲。
拜察禮富綬第五子。康熙二十年，封三等輔國將軍。四十七年，卒。乾隆三十七年，追封顯親王。蘊著拜察禮第三子。康熙四十七年，襲三等奉國將軍。乾隆三十七年，襲顯親王。四十三年，復始封之號曰肅。尋薨，諡曰勤。舒明蘊著第一子。乾隆八年，封奉恩將軍。二十八年，卒。忠靈舒明第三子。乾隆二十八年，襲奉恩將軍。五十七年，卒。聰順忠靈第一子。乾隆五十七年，襲奉恩將軍。嘉慶十一年，卒。

Hooge, Taizong's eldest son, was first made a beile. In Tiancong 6 he was raised to Prince of the First Rank; in Chongde 1, for merit, to Prince Su. He was soon demoted to beile for cause, then in year 2 restored as Prince Su. In year 6 he was lowered to Prince of the Second Rank; in year 7, again raised to Prince Su for merit. In Shunzhi 1 his title was stripped for cause, then soon restored. In year 5 his rank was revoked; he was confined and died by his own hand. In year 7, as he had been killed without cause, he was posthumously re-enfeoffed as Prince Su; in year 13 given the posthumous name Wu. In Qianlong 43 he was admitted to worship in the Imperial Ancestral Temple for signal service at the founding.Guotai, Hooge's eldest son, was made Defender of the State General in Shunzhi 10. In Kangxi 3 his title was revoked for cause.
Wohenna, Hooge's third son, was made Defender of the State General in Shunzhi 10 and died in Kangxi 1.Wuli, Wohenna's eldest son, inherited third-class Supporter of the State General in Kangxi 1. In year 51 his title was revoked for cause.Guone, son of Wuli, was made Supporter of the State General but later lost the title for cause.Songjian, son of Tune, was enfeoffed as Supporter of Grace General.
Eerdemenge, son of Tune, was enfeoffed as Supporter of Grace General.
Fushou, Hooge's fourth son, inherited the princedom in Shunzhi 8, when the title was renamed Prince Xian. He died in Kangxi 8 and was posthumously styled Que.Danzhen, Fushou's fourth son, succeeded as Prince Xian in Kangxi 9. He died in year 41 and was posthumously styled Mi.Chengxin, Danzhen's second son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Kangxi 47. He died in Qianlong 23; in year 43 he was posthumously raised to Prince Su.Yongjun, Chengxin's second son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 17 and died in year 28.Chunshao, Yongjun's third son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 22 and died in year 47.
Yongxi, Chengxin's fifth son, succeeded as Prince Su in Qianlong 43. He died in Daoguang 1 and was posthumously styled Gong.Jingmin, Yongxi's eldest son, was made Auxiliary State Duke (without the eight stipends) in Qianlong 60, succeeded as Prince Su in Daoguang 1, died in Xianfeng 2, and was posthumously styled Shen.Hualian, Jingmin's eldest son, was made third-class Defender of the State General in Jiaqing 17, died in Daoguang 9 without issue.
Huafeng, Jingmin's third son, was made third-class Suppressor of the State General in Daoguang 4 and Auxiliary State Duke in year 9. He succeeded as Prince Su in Xianfeng 3, died in Tongzhi 8, and was posthumously styled Ke.Longqin, Huafeng's third son, was made second-class Suppressor of the State General in Tongzhi 1, succeeded as Prince Su in year 9, died in Guangxu 24, and was posthumously styled Liang.Shanqi, Longqin's eldest son, was made second-class Suppressor of the State General in Guangxu 12 and succeeded as Prince Su in year 24.Xianzhang, Shanqi's eldest son, was made Auxiliary State Duke (without the eight stipends) in Guangxu 32.
Xiande, Shanqi's second son, was made Auxiliary State Duke (without the eight stipends) in Guangxu 32.
Shanyu, Longqin's second son, was made second-class Suppressor of the State General in Guangxu 14.

牛鈕世祖第一子。早卒。
福全世祖第二子。康熙六年，封裕親王。四十二年，薨。諡曰憲。保泰福全第三子。康熙四十一年，封世子。四十二年，襲裕親王。雍正二年，緣事革爵。廣善保泰第一子。康熙六十一年，封世子。雍正二年，緣事革退，封公品級。六年，又革退。
廣華保泰第三子。雍正二年，封輔國公。尋緣事革退。
保綬福全第五子。初封輔國公品級。康熙四十五年，卒。雍正三年，追封親王，諡曰悼。廣靈保綬第二子。雍正二年，襲裕親王。四年，緣事革爵。
廣祿保綬第三子。雍正四年，襲裕親王。乾隆五十年，薨。諡曰莊。亮景廣祿第三子。乾隆十四年，封三等鎮國將軍。十七年，卒。恆國亮景第三子。乾隆十八年，襲輔國將軍。十九年，卒。無嗣。
亮智廣祿第七子。乾隆二十一年，封一等輔國將軍。三十八年，緣事革退。

明範斐蘇第十二子。乾隆四十九年，封奉恩將軍。嘉慶二十年，卒。
明昆斐蘇第十四子。乾隆四十九年，封奉恩將軍。嘉慶十八年，因病告退。道光五年，卒。恆春明昆第二子。嘉慶十九年，襲奉恩將軍。道光二十四年，卒。榮秀恆春第一子。道光二十四年，襲奉恩將軍。光緒九年，卒。襲次已盡，不襲。
對清額常穎第四子。康熙三十九年，封三等輔國將軍。乾隆五年，因病告退。愛隆阿對清額第四子。乾隆五年，襲奉國將軍。九年，卒。安楚杭阿愛隆阿第三子。乾隆九年，襲奉恩將軍。十年，卒。無嗣。
福格對清額第八子。乾隆二十二年，封奉恩將軍。四十二年，卒。無嗣。
奇臣對清額第九子。乾隆二十二年，封三等奉國將軍。嘉慶十二年，緣事革退。
卓泰常穎第五子。康熙四十一年，封三等輔國將軍。四十四年，卒。無嗣。
奇授世祖第六子。早卒。無嗣。
隆禧世祖第七子。康熙十三年，封純親王。十八年，薨。諡曰靖。富爾祜倫隆禧子。康熙十九年，襲純親王。尋薨。無嗣。
永幹世祖第八子。早卒。無嗣。

Niuniu, Emperor Shunzhi's eldest son, died young.
Fuquan, Emperor Shunzhi's second son, was made Prince Yu in Kangxi 6. He died in year 42 and was posthumously styled Xian.Baotai, Fuquan's third son, was made Heir Apparent in Kangxi 41 and succeeded as Prince Yu in year 42. In Yongzheng 2 his title was revoked for cause.Guangshan, Baotai's eldest son, was made Heir Apparent in Kangxi 61. In Yongzheng 2 his title was revoked for cause and he was granted ducal rank; in year 6 he was stripped again.
Guanghua, Baotai's third son, was made Auxiliary State Duke in Yongzheng 2 and was soon stripped for cause.
Baoshou, Fuquan's fifth son, was first granted Auxiliary State Duke rank. He died in Kangxi 45; in Yongzheng 3 he was posthumously enfeoffed as Prince of the First Rank and styled Dao.Guangling, Baoshou's second son, succeeded as Prince Yu in Yongzheng 2. In year 4 his title was revoked for cause.
Guanglu, Baoshou's third son, succeeded as Prince Yu in Yongzheng 4. He died in Qianlong 50 and was posthumously styled Zhuang.Liangjing, Guanglu's third son, was made third-class Suppressor of the State General in Qianlong 14 and died in year 17.Hengguo, Liangjing's third son, inherited Defender of the State General in Qianlong 18 and died in year 19 without issue.
Liangzhi, Guanglu's seventh son, was made first-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 21. In year 38 his title was revoked for cause.
Liangqing, Guanglu's eighth son, was made second-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 35 and died in year 41.Hengwei, Liangqing's eldest son, inherited second-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 41 and died in Jiaqing 14.Wenyan, Hengwei's eldest son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Jiaqing 10 and died in Daoguang 8.Xianglai, Wenyan's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 9 and died in Guangxu 1.Chunrong, Xianglai's second son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Guangxu 2 and died in year 33. The succession was exhausted; no further inheritance.
Wenhu, Hengwei's third son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Jiaqing 21 and died in Tongzhi 1.
Liangzhu, Guanglu's eleventh son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 27 and died in year 48.Hengbi, Liangzhu's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 48 and died in Jiaqing 4.
Lianghuan, Guanglu's twelfth son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 27 and succeeded as Prince Yu (Commandery) in year 51. He died in Jiaqing 13 and was posthumously styled Xi.Hengcun, Lianghuan's second son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 49 and died in Jiaqing 1. In year 13 he was posthumously enfeoffed as beile.Wenhe, Hengcun's son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Jiaqing 2 and beile in year 13. He died in year 20.Xiangduan, Wenhe's eldest son, inherited as beizi in Jiaqing 21 and died in Daoguang 16.Jishan, Xiangrui's eldest son and Xiangduan's adopted heir, succeeded as Duke Who Suppresses the State in Daoguang 16 and died in Xianfeng 11.Rongyu, Jishan's eldest son, succeeded as Duke Who Suppresses the State in Xianfeng 11 and died in Guangxu 23.Kuizhang, Rongyu's son, succeeded as Duke Who Suppresses the State in Guangxu 24.
Wenjie, Hengcun's second son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Jiaqing 7 and died in Daoguang 14.Xiangrui, Wenjie's third son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 15 and died in year 17.
Henglue, Lianghuan's fourth son, was made first-class Supporter of the State General in Jiaqing 4 and died in year 12.Wenzheng, Henglue's third son, inherited first-class Supporter of the State General in Jiaqing 12 and died in Daoguang 21.Xiangdeng, Wenzheng's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 21 and resigned for illness in Guangxu 7.Jifeng, Xiangdeng's second son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Guangxu 7 and died in year 17.Rongchang, Jifeng's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Guangxu 17.
Xianghan, Wenzheng's second son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 30 and died in Guangxu 18.Jilin, son of Xianghan.Rongzhao, Jilin's son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Guangxu 18.
Xiangheng, Wenzheng's third son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 30 and died in Guangxu 30 without issue.
Hengjin, Lianghuan's fifth son, was made third-class Suppressor of the State General in Jiaqing 7 and died in Daoguang 26.Wenyi, Hengjin's seventh son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Daoguang 13 and died in Xianfeng 8 without issue.
Hengjin, Lianghuan's seventh son, was made Supporter of the State General in Jiaqing 15 and died in Daoguang 18.Wenxi, Hengjin's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 18 and died in Tongzhi 8 without issue.
Liangkui, Guanglu's thirteenth son, was made third-class Suppressor of the State General in Qianlong 27 and resigned for illness in Jiaqing 21.Hengchi, Liangkui's second son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 55 and resigned in Jiaqing 12.Wenqian, Hengchi's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Jiaqing 22 and soon inherited third-class Supporter of the State General. He died in year 25.Xiangshan, Wenqian's son, inherited Supporter of the State General in Jiaqing 25 and died in Daoguang 1 without issue.
Liangqing, Guanglu's fourteenth son, was made third-class Suppressor of the State General in Qianlong 27 and died in year 52.Hengduo, Liangqing's son, inherited third-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 53 and died in Jiaqing 14.
Liangyuan, Guanglu's seventeenth son, was made second-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 35 and died in Jiaqing 13.Henggui, Liangyuan's eldest son, inherited Supporter of the State General in Jiaqing 13 and died in year 15 without issue.
Lianghu, Guanglu's eighteenth son, was made second-class Suppressor of the State General in Qianlong 40 and died in Jiaqing 2 without issue.
Liangcong, Guanglu's twenty-first son, was made second-class Suppressor of the State General in Qianlong 49 and died in Jiaqing 2.Henghan, Liangcong's eldest son, inherited second-class Supporter of the State General in Jiaqing 2 and died in Tongzhi 1.Wenchu, Henghan's fourth son, inherited second-class Supporter of the State General in Tongzhi 2 and died in Guangxu 28 without issue.
Prince Rong, Emperor Shunzhi's fourth son, was never named. He died in Shunzhi 15 without issue.
Changying, Emperor Shunzhi's fifth son, was made Prince Gong in Kangxi 10 and died in year 42.Yongshou, Changying's eldest son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Kangxi 24 and died in year 25 without issue.
Manduhu, Changying's second son, inherited as beile in Kangxi 51. In Yongzheng 4 he was lowered to beizi for cause, then soon reduced again to Duke Who Suppresses the State. He died in year 9.
Haishan, Changying's third son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Kangxi 34 and inherited as beile in year 42. In year 51 his title was revoked for cause. In Yongzheng 10 he was re-enfeoffed as beile. He died in Qianlong 8 and was posthumously styled Ximin.Lumubu, son of Haishan.Feisu, Lumubu's son, inherited as beile in Yongzheng 9 and died in Qianlong 28.Mingshao, Feisu's second son, inherited as beizi in Qianlong 28 and died in year 52.Jinchang, Mingshao's eldest son, was made Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 40 and succeeded as Duke Who Suppresses the State in year 53. In Jiaqing 8 his title was revoked for cause; in year 22 he again inherited as Auxiliary State Duke. He died in Daoguang 8.Xianglin, Jinchang's second son, inherited as Auxiliary State Duke (without the eight stipends) in Daoguang 8 and resigned for illness in year 14.Chengxi, Xianglin's eldest son, inherited as Auxiliary State Duke (without the eight stipends) in Daoguang 28 and died in Guangxu 17.Chonglue, Chengxi's eldest son, inherited as Auxiliary State Duke (without the eight stipends) in Guangxu 18 and died in year 20.Deyin, Chonglue's eldest son, inherited as Auxiliary State Duke (without the eight stipends) in Guangxu 20 and died in year 21.
Demao, Chonglue's second son, inherited as Auxiliary State Duke (without the eight stipends) in Guangxu 22.
Jinlong, Mingshao's second son, was made first-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 49 and inherited as Auxiliary State Duke in Jiaqing 8. In year 22 his title was revoked for cause.Yucai, Jinlong's eldest son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Jiaqing 10 and resigned for illness in Daoguang 13.
Minggong, Feisu's fifth son, was made second-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 30 and died in Jiaqing 1.Jiapei, Minggong's second son, inherited third-class Supporter of the State General in Jiaqing 2 and died in year 22.Lianxi, Jiapei's second son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Jiaqing 23. In Daoguang 16 his title was revoked for cause.
Mingpei, Feisu's sixth son, was made second-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 30 and died in Daoguang 5.Chungu, son of Mingpei.Qinglin, Chungu's son, inherited Supporter of the State General in Daoguang 5 and died in year 20.Lingrui, Qinglin's second son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 20 and died in Xianfeng 7 without issue.
Mingzuan, Feisu's eleventh son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 40 and died in year 59.Yigui, Mingzuan's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 60 and died in Daoguang 27.Guanrui, Yigui's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 28 and died in Xianfeng 7. The line's allotted inheritances were used up, so the title was not passed on again.
Minggai, Feisu's eleventh son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 40 and resigned for illness in year 57.Yuxian, Minggai's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 59 and died in Jiaqing 15 without issue.
Mingfan, Feisu's twelfth son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 49 and died in Jiaqing 20.
Mingkun, Feisu's fourteenth son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 49, resigned for illness in Jiaqing 18, and died in Daoguang 5.Hengchun, Mingkun's second son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Jiaqing 19 and died in Daoguang 24.Rongxiu, Hengchun's eldest son, inherited Supporter of Grace General in Daoguang 24 and died in Guangxu 9. The line's allotted inheritances were used up, so the title was not passed on again.

Fuge, Duiqing'e's eighth son, was made Supporter of Grace General in Qianlong 22 and died in year 42 without issue.
Qichen, Duiqing'e's ninth son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Qianlong 22. In Jiaqing 12 his title was revoked for cause.
Zhuotai, Changying's fifth son, was made third-class Supporter of the State General in Kangxi 41 and died in year 44 without issue.
Qishou, Emperor Shunzhi's sixth son, died young without issue.
Longxi, Emperor Shunzhi's seventh son, was made Prince Chun in Kangxi 13. He died in year 18 and was posthumously styled Jing.Fuerhulun, Longxi's son, succeeded as Prince Chun in Kangxi 19 and soon died without issue.
Yonggan, Emperor Shunzhi's eighth son, died young without issue.
