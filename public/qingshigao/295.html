1
隆科多，佟佳氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人，一等公佟國維子，孝懿仁皇后弟也。
Long Keduo, of the Tunggiya clan, belonged to the Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner. He was the son of first-rank Duke Tong Guowei and the younger brother of Empress Xiaoyiren.
2
康熙二十七年，授一等侍衛，擢鑾儀使，兼正藍旗蒙古副都統。
In Kangxi 27 he was made a first-class bodyguard, then promoted to commissioner of the imperial equipage and concurrently appointed Mongol deputy lieutenant-general of the Plain Blue Banner.
3
四十四年，以所屬人違法，上責隆科多不實心任事，罷副都統、鑾儀使，在一等侍衛上行走。
In the forty-fourth year, when men under his command broke the law, the emperor blamed Long Keduo for lack of diligence, stripped him of his posts as deputy lieutenant-general and commissioner of the imperial equipage, and reduced him to serving as a first-class bodyguard.
4
五十年，授步軍統領。
In the fiftieth year he was appointed commander of the metropolitan infantry.
5
五十九年，擢理籓院尚書，仍管步軍統領。
In the fifty-ninth year he was promoted to minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs while retaining command of the metropolitan infantry.
6
六十一年十一月，聖祖大漸，召受顧命。 世宗即位，命與大學士馬齊總理事務，襲一等公，授吏部尚書。 旋以總理事務勞，加一等阿達哈哈番，以其長子岳興阿襲。 次子玉柱，自侍衛授鑾儀使。
In the eleventh month of Kangxi 61, as the Kangxi Emperor lay dying, he was summoned to receive the dying emperor's testament. When the Yongzheng Emperor ascended the throne, Long Keduo was ordered with Grand Secretary Ma Qi to oversee state affairs, inherited the first-rank dukedom, and was appointed minister of the Board of Civil Officials. Soon afterward, for his service in overseeing affairs, he was granted the first-rank addaha han, which his eldest son Yuexing'a inherited. His second son Yuzhu was promoted from bodyguard to commissioner of the imperial equipage.
7
雍正元年，與川陝總督年羹堯同加太保。
In Yongzheng 1 he and Sichuan-Shaanxi Governor-General Nian Gengyao were both appointed grand guardians.
8
二年，兼領理籓院事。 纂修《聖祖實錄》、《大清會典》並充總裁，監修《明史》。 復與羹堯同賜雙眼花翎、四團龍補服、黃帶、紫轡。
In the second year he was additionally placed in charge of the Court of Colonial Affairs. He helped compile the Veritable Records of the Kangxi Emperor and the Collected Statutes of the Great Qing as chief compiler, and supervised compilation of the History of the Ming. He again received, together with Gengyao, double-eyed peacock feathers, four-claw dragon rank patches, a yellow belt, and a purple bridle.
9
三年，解步軍統領。 玉柱以行止甚劣，奪官，交隆科多管束。 羹堯得罪，上以都統範時捷疏劾欺罔貪婪諸狀，及妄劾道員金南瑛等，並下吏部議處。 上諭曰：「前以隆科多、年羹堯頗著勤勞，予以異數，乃交結專擅，諸事欺隱。 」命繳上所賜四團龍補服，並不得復用雙眼花翎、黃帶、紫轡。 及議上，以時捷劾，請罷羹堯任； 以妄劾南瑛，請嚴加治罪。 上以前議徇庇，後議復過當，責隆科多有意擾亂，削太保及一等阿達哈哈番世職，命往阿蘭善等處修城墾地，諭曰：「朕御極之初，隆科多、年羹堯皆寄以心腹，毫無猜防。 孰知朕視為一德，彼竟有二心，招權納賄，擅作威福，欺罔悖負，朕豈能姑息養奸耶？ 向日明珠、索額圖結黨行私，聖祖解其要職，置之閒散，何嘗更加信用？ 隆科多、年羹堯若不知恐懼，痛改前非，欲如明珠等，萬不能也！ 殊典不可再邀，覆轍不可屢蹈，各宜警懼，毋自乾誅滅。 」
In the third year he was relieved of command of the metropolitan infantry. Yuzhu was stripped of office for gross misconduct and placed under Long Keduo's supervision. When Gengyao fell from favor, the emperor received a memorial from Metropolitan Banner Commander Fan Shijie charging deception, greed, and other misconduct, as well as false impeachment of Circuit Intendant Jin Nanying and others, and referred all matters to the Board of Civil Officials for deliberation. The emperor said: "Long Keduo and Nian Gengyao had earned exceptional favor for their past service, yet they formed factions, monopolized power, and deceived me at every turn. " He ordered them to surrender their four-claw dragon rank patches and forbade them ever again to wear double-eyed peacock feathers, the yellow belt, or the purple bridle. When the board reported its findings, it recommended dismissing Gengyao on the basis of Shijie's charges; and for the false impeachment of Nanying, it recommended severe punishment. The emperor held that the first board report had been too lenient and the second too harsh, blamed Long Keduo for deliberately sowing disorder, stripped him of grand guardian and the hereditary first-rank addaha han, and sent him to Alashan and elsewhere to build fortifications and open land. He said: "When I first took the throne I entrusted Long Keduo and Nian Gengyao with my full confidence, without a trace of suspicion. Yet while I treated them as loyal to a man, they were divided in heart, gathered power, took bribes, abused their authority, and betrayed me—how could I indulge such men? Mingzhu and Songgotu once formed factions for private ends; the Kangxi Emperor removed them from key posts and left them idle—he never trusted them again. If Long Keduo and Nian Gengyao do not learn fear and mend their ways, they can never hope to fare like Mingzhu and his like! Extraordinary honors cannot be sought twice, nor the same mistakes repeated. Let each take warning and not court his own ruin. "
10
四年，隆科多家僕牛倫挾勢索賕，事發，逮下法司，鞫得隆科多受羹堯及總督趙世顯、滿保，巡撫甘國璧、蘇克濟賄。 讞上，上命斬倫，罷隆科多尚書，令料理阿爾泰等路邊疆事務。 尋命勘議俄羅斯邊界。
In the fourth year Long Keduo's servant Niulun was found extorting bribes by abusing his master's power. After arrest and trial, Long Keduo was shown to have taken bribes from Gengyao, Governors Zhao Shixian and Manbao, and Governors-General Gan Guobi and Sukeji. When the verdict was reported, the emperor ordered Niulun executed, dismissed Long Keduo as minister, and assigned him to manage frontier affairs along the Altai route and elsewhere. He was soon ordered to survey and negotiate the Russian border.
11
初，隆科多與阿靈阿、揆敘相黨附，繼又與羹堯交結。 至是，上盡發阿靈阿、揆敘及羹堯罪狀，宣示中外。 又侍郎查嗣庭為隆科多所薦，坐悖逆誅死，上詰隆科多，隆科多不以實對。
Long Keduo had first aligned with Aling'a and Kuisu, and later also formed ties with Gengyao. The emperor now published the full record of crimes by Aling'a, Kuisu, and Gengyao at home and abroad. Vice Minister Zha Siting, whom Long Keduo had recommended, was executed for treason. When questioned, Long Keduo did not tell the truth.
12
五年，宗人府復奏劾輔國公阿布蘭以玉牒畀隆科多藏於家，阿布蘭坐奪爵幽禁。 上命奪隆科多爵，召還京，命王大臣會鞫。 以聖祖升遐，隆科多未在上前，妄言身藏匕首以防不測； 又自擬諸葛亮，奏稱「白帝城受命之日，即死期將至之時」； 上躬祀壇廟，妄謂防刺客，令於案下搜查； 上謁陵，妄奏「諸王心變」。 具獄辭：大不敬之罪五，欺罔之罪四，紊亂朝政之罪三，黨姦之罪六，不法之罪七，貪婪之罪十六，凡四十一款，當斬，妻子入辛者庫，財產入官。 上諭曰：「隆科多罪不容誅，但皇考升遐，大臣承旨者惟隆科多一人。 今以罪誅，朕心有所不忍，可免其正法，於暢春園外築屋三楹，永遠禁錮； 妻子免入辛者庫，岳興阿奪官，玉柱發黑龍江。 」
In the fifth year the Court of the Imperial Clan reported that Assistant Duke Ablan had given the imperial genealogy to Long Keduo to keep at home. Ablan was stripped of his title and confined. The emperor stripped Long Keduo of his noble rank, recalled him to the capital, and ordered princes and grand ministers to try him jointly. When the Kangxi Emperor died, Long Keduo falsely claimed that though not in the emperor's presence he had carried a dagger against unforeseen danger; he compared himself to Zhuge Liang and wrote, "The day one receives the mandate at Baidicheng is the day one's death draws near"; when the emperor sacrificed at the altars and temples, he invented fears of assassins and ordered searches beneath the altar tables; and when the emperor visited the imperial tombs, he falsely reported that "the princes' hearts have turned." The indictment listed forty-one counts in all: five of great irreverence, four of deception, three of disordering the court, six of factional villainy, seven of lawlessness, and sixteen of greed. He was sentenced to death, his family to the Sinchi Ward, and his property confiscated. The emperor said: "Long Keduo's crimes deserve death, yet when my late father passed away Long Keduo alone among the ministers received his testament. To execute him now would grieve me. Spare him formal execution; build three rooms outside the Garden of Refreshing Spring and imprison him there for life; his wife and children shall not enter the Sinchi Ward; Yuexing'a shall be stripped of office, and Yuzhu exiled to Heilongjiang. "
13
六年六月，隆科多死於禁所，賜金治喪。
In the sixth month of the sixth year Long Keduo died in confinement, and the court granted funds for his funeral.
14
年羹堯，字亮工，漢軍鑲黃旗人。 父遐齡，自筆帖式授兵部主事，再遷刑部郎中。 康熙二十二年，授河南道御史。 四遷工部侍郎，出為湖廣巡撫。 湖北武昌等七府歲徵匠役班價銀千餘，戶絕額缺，為官民累。 遐齡請歸地丁徵收，下部議，從之。 疏劾黃梅知縣李錦虧賦，奪官。 錦清廉得民，民爭完逋賦，諸生吳士光等聚眾閉城留錦。 事聞，上命調錦直隸，士光等發奉天，遐齡與總督郭琇俱降級留任。 四十三年，遐齡以病乞休。
Nian Gengyao, whose style was Lianggong, belonged to the Chinese Bordered Yellow Banner. His father Xialing rose from clerk to director in the Board of War and was later transferred to director in the Board of Punishments. In Kangxi 22 he was appointed censor of the Henan circuit. After four promotions he became vice minister of the Board of Works, then was appointed governor of Huguang. In Hubei, Wuchang and six other prefectures levied more than a thousand taels a year in artisan-service fees; extinct households left quotas unfilled, burdening officials and commoners alike. Xialing asked that the levy be folded into the land-and-poll tax; the ministry approved. He impeached Li Jin, magistrate of Huangmei, for tax shortfalls, and Jin was dismissed. Jin was honest and beloved; the people rushed to pay arrears, and licentiate Wu Shiguang and others gathered a mob, shut the city gates, and refused to let him leave. When word reached the throne, Jin was transferred to Zhili, Shiguang and his fellows were sent to Fengtian, and both Xialing and Governor Guo Xiu were demoted one rank but kept in office. In the forty-third year Xialing retired on grounds of illness.
15
羹堯，康熙三十九年進士，改庶吉士，授檢討。 迭充四川、廣東鄉試考官，累遷內閣學士。 四十八年，擢四川巡撫。 四十九年，斡偉生番羅都等掠寧番衛，戕游擊周玉麟。 上命羹堯與提督岳昇龍剿撫。 昇龍率兵討之，擒羅都，羹堯至平番衛，聞羅都已擒，引還。 川陝總督音泰疏劾，部議當奪官，上命留任。 五十六年，越巂衛屬番與普雄土千戶那交等為亂，羹堯遣游擊張玉剿平之。
Gengyao passed the civil examinations in Kangxi 39, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and was appointed reviser. He served as examiner for the Sichuan and Guangdong provincial examinations in turn and rose to Hanlin academician of the Grand Secretariat. In the forty-eighth year he was promoted to governor of Sichuan. In the forty-ninth year the Wo-wei Raw Tribesmen under Luo Du raided Ningfan Guard and killed Assistant Commander Zhou Yulin. The emperor ordered Gengyao and Provincial Military Commissioner Yue Shenglong to suppress and pacify them. Shenglong led troops against them and captured Luo Du. Gengyao reached Pingfan Guard, learned Luo Du was already taken, and withdrew. Sichuan-Shaanxi Governor-General Yintai impeached him; the ministry recommended dismissal, but the emperor let him keep his post. In the fifty-sixth year tribes under Yuexi Guard and Puxiong native chieftain Najiao rebelled; Gengyao sent Assistant Commander Zhang Yu to put them down.
16
是歲，策妄阿喇布坦遣其將策淩敦多卜襲西藏，戕拉藏汗。 四川提督康泰率兵出黃勝關，兵譁，引還。 羹堯遣參將楊盡信撫諭之，密奏泰失兵心，不可用，請親赴松潘協理軍務。 上嘉其實心任事，遣都統法喇率兵赴四川助剿。 五十七年，羹堯令護軍統領溫普進駐里塘，增設打箭爐至里塘驛站，尋請增設四川駐防兵，皆允之。 上嘉羹堯治事明敏，巡撫無督兵責，特授四川總督，兼管巡撫事。 五十八年，羹堯以敵情叵測，請赴藏為備。 廷議以鬆潘諸路軍事重要，令羹堯毋率兵出邊，檄法喇進師。 法喇率副將岳鍾琪撫定里塘、巴塘。 羹堯亦遣知府遲維德招降乍丫、察木多、察哇諸番目，因請召法喇師還，從之。
That year Tsewang Araptan sent General Tsering Dondup to raid Tibet and kill Regent Lozang Khan. Sichuan Provincial Military Commissioner Kang Tai led troops through Huang Sheng Pass, but the men mutinied and he withdrew. Gengyao sent Brigade Commander Yang Jinxin to pacify the troops and secretly reported that Kang had lost the soldiers' loyalty and should not be used, asking to go in person to Songpan to oversee military affairs. The emperor praised his diligence and sent Banner Commander Fala with troops to Sichuan to assist. In the fifty-seventh year Gengyao ordered Garrison Commander Wenpu to advance to Litang, added courier stations from Dartsedo to Litang, and soon won approval for more garrison troops in Sichuan. The emperor praised Gengyao's sharp administration. Because governors did not command troops, he was specially made governor-general of Sichuan while retaining governorship duties. In the fifty-eighth year, finding the enemy situation unpredictable, Gengyao asked to go to Tibet as a precaution. The court held that the Songpan front was critical and ordered Gengyao not to lead troops beyond the border, while dispatching Fala to advance. Fala led Deputy Commander Yue Zhongqi to pacify Litang and Batang. Gengyao also sent Prefect Chi Weide to win over the chiefs of Chaya, Qamdo, and Chawa, then asked that Fala's army be recalled; the request was granted.
17
五十九年，上命平逆將軍延信率兵自青海入西藏，授羹堯定西將軍印，自拉里會師，並諮羹堯孰可署總督者。 羹堯言一時不得其人，請以將軍印畀護軍統領噶爾弼，而移法喇軍駐打箭爐，上用其議。 巴塘、里塘本雲南麗江土府屬地，既撫定，雲貴總督蔣陳錫請仍隸麗江土知府木興； 羹堯言二地為入藏運糧要路，宜屬四川，從之。 興率兵往收地，至喇皮，擊殺番酋巴桑，羹堯疏劾。 上命逮興，囚雲南省城。 八月，噶爾弼、延信兩軍先後入西藏，策淩敦多卜敗走，西藏平。 上諭羹堯護凱旋諸軍入邊，召法喇還京師。
In the fifty-ninth year the emperor ordered Pacification General Yansin to enter Tibet from Qinghai, gave Gengyao the seal of Pacifier of the West, and ordered a rendezvous at Lhari, while asking whom Gengyao could leave to act as governor. Gengyao said no suitable replacement could be found at once and asked that the general's seal go to Garrison Commander Ga'erbi while Fala's army garrisoned Dartsedo; the emperor agreed. Batang and Litang had belonged to the Lijiang native prefecture in Yunnan. Once pacified, Yunnan-Guizhou Governor-General Jiang Chenxi asked that they again be placed under Lijiang native prefect Musi; Gengyao argued that both were vital grain routes into Tibet and should belong to Sichuan; the court agreed. Musi led troops to recover the territory, killed tribal chief Basang at Lapi, and was impeached by Gengyao. The emperor ordered Musi arrested and imprisoned in the Yunnan capital. In the eighth month the armies of Ga'erbi and Yansin entered Tibet in turn; Tsering Dondup was defeated and fled, and Tibet was pacified. The emperor ordered Gengyao to escort the victorious armies back within the border and recalled Fala to the capital.
18
羹堯尋遣兵撫定里塘屬上下牙色、上下雅尼，巴塘屬桑阿壩、林卡石諸生番。 六十年，入覲，命兼理四川陝西總督，辭，還鎮，賜弓矢。 上命噶爾弼率兵駐守西藏，行次瀘定橋，噶爾弼病不能行，羹堯以聞。 上命公策旺諾爾佈署將軍，額駙阿寶、都統武格參贊軍務，駐西藏。 青海索羅木之西有郭羅克上中下三部，為唐古特種人，屢出肆掠。 阿寶以聞，上令羹堯與鍾琪度形勢，策進討。 羹堯疏言：「郭羅克有隘口三，悉險峻，宜步不宜騎。 若多調兵，塞上傳聞，使賊得為備，不如以番攻番。 臣素知瓦斯、雜穀諸土司亦憾郭羅克肆惡，原出兵助剿。 臣已移鍾琪令速赴松潘，出塞督土兵進剿。 」尋，鍾琪督兵擊敗郭羅克，下番寨四十餘，獲其渠，餘眾悉降。
Gengyao soon sent troops to pacify the Raw Tribes of Upper and Lower Yase and Yani under Litang, and Sang'aba, Linkashi, and others under Batang. In the sixtieth year he came to court, was offered the Sichuan-Shaanxi governor-generalship in addition to his duties, declined, returned to his post, and was granted bow and arrows. The emperor ordered Ga'erbi to garrison Tibet with his troops, but at Luding Bridge Ga'erbi fell too ill to continue, and Gengyao reported it. The emperor appointed Duke Tsewang Norbu acting resident general, with Imperial Son-in-law Abao and Banner Commander Wuge to assist in military affairs and garrison Tibet. West of Suoluomu in Qinghai lay the Upper, Middle, and Lower Golok divisions, Tangut peoples who repeatedly raided at will. Abao reported the raids, and the emperor ordered Gengyao and Zhongqi to survey the terrain and plan an offensive. Gengyao wrote: "Golok has three passes, all steep and narrow—suited to foot soldiers, not cavalry. If we mobilize many troops, word will spread on the frontier and the enemy will prepare. Better to set tribe against tribe. I have long known that the Wasi, Zagun, and other native chiefs also resent Golok's outrages and are willing to send troops to help. I have already ordered Zhongqi to hurry to Songpan, cross the frontier, and lead native troops against them. " Soon afterward Zhongqi led the campaign, defeated Golok, took more than forty stockades, captured their leader, and the rest surrendered.
19
六十一年，羹堯密疏言：「西藏喇嘛楚爾齊木臧布及知府石如金呈策旺諾爾布委靡，副都統常齡、侍讀學士滿都、員外郎巴特瑪等任意生事，致在藏官兵不睦。 」因請撤駐藏官兵。 下廷臣議，以羹堯擅議撤兵，請下部嚴議，上原之，命召滿都、巴特瑪、石如金、楚爾齊木臧布等來京師，遣四​​川巡撫色爾圖、陝西布政使塔琳赴西藏，佐策旺諾爾布駐守。
In the sixty-first year Gengyao sent a secret memorial: "The Tibetan lama Chuerqimu Zangbu and Prefect Shi Rujin reported that Tsewang Norbu was ineffectual, while Deputy Lieutenant-General Chang Ling, Reader Mandu, Department Director Batma, and others acted arbitrarily, sowing discord among the troops in Tibet. " He therefore asked that the garrison in Tibet be withdrawn. The court deliberated and, because Gengyao had proposed withdrawal on his own authority, recommended severe censure; the emperor excused him, summoned Mandu, Batma, Shi Rujin, Chuerqimu Zangbu, and others to the capital, and sent Sichuan Governor Se'ertu and Shaanxi Administration Commissioner Talin to Tibet to assist Tsewang Norbu.
20
自軍興，陝西州縣饋運供億，庫帑多虧缺。 羹堯累疏論劾州縣吏，嚴督追償。 陝西巡撫噶什圖密奏虧項不能速完，又與羹堯請加徵火耗墊補。 上諭曰：「各省錢糧皆有虧空，陝西尤甚。 蓋自用兵以來，師所經行，資助馬匹、盤費、衣服、食物，倉卒無可措辦，勢必挪用庫帑。 及撤兵時亦然。 即如自藏回京，將軍以至士卒，途中所得，反多於正項。 各官費用，動至萬金，但知取用，不問其出自何項也。 羹堯等欲追虧項以充兵餉，追比不得，又議加徵火耗。 火耗止可議減，豈可加增？ 朕在位六十一年，從未加徵火耗。 今若聽其加派，必致與正項一例催徵，肆無忌憚矣。 著傳旨申飭。 」命發帑銀五十萬送陝西資餉。
Since the war began, Shaanxi's prefectures and counties had supplied transport and provisions, leaving the treasury deeply in deficit. Gengyao repeatedly impeached local officials and pressed hard for recovery of the shortfalls. Shaanxi Governor Gashetu secretly reported that the deficits could not be made up quickly and, together with Gengyao, asked to levy additional surcharge fees to cover them. The emperor said: "Every province has tax deficits, but Shaanxi's are especially grave. Since the war began, wherever the army passed it needed horses, travel costs, clothing, and food that could not be arranged in haste, so officials inevitably dipped into the treasury. The same was true when the troops withdrew. Even on the march back from Tibet to the capital, generals and common soldiers alike often took more along the road than their proper allotments. Officials' expenses often ran to ten thousand taels; they drew funds without asking where the money came from. Gengyao and others tried to recover the deficits for military pay but could not collect them in time, and again proposed raising surcharge levies. Surcharges should only be reduced—how can they be increased? In my sixty-one years on the throne I have never raised surcharge levies. If they are allowed to add extra levies now, surcharges will be collected like regular tax grain, with no restraint at all. Issue a stern admonition. " He ordered five hundred thousand taels from the treasury sent to Shaanxi for military supplies.

世宗即位，召撫遠大將軍允還京師，命羹堯管理大將軍印務。 雍正元年，授羹堯二等阿達哈哈番世職，並加遐齡尚書銜。 尋又加羹堯太保。 詔撤西藏駐防官軍。 羹堯疏陳邊防諸事，請於打箭爐邊外中渡河口築土城，移嵐州守備駐守； 大河南保縣，移威茂營千總駐守； 越巂衛地方寥闊，蠻、倮出沒，改設游擊，增兵駐守； 松潘邊外諸番，阿樹為最要，給長官司職銜； 大金川土目莎羅奔從征羊峒有功，給安撫司職銜； 烏蒙蠻目達木等兇暴，土舍祿鼎坤等請擒獻，俟其至，給土職，分轄其地。 下部議，從之。 論平西藏功，以羹堯運糧守隘，封三等公，世襲。
When the Yongzheng Emperor took the throne, he recalled Pacification General Yunsi to the capital and put Gengyao in charge of the great general's seal and affairs. In Yongzheng 1 Gengyao was granted the hereditary second-rank addaha han, and Xialing was given ministerial rank. Gengyao was soon made grand guardian as well. An edict ordered the Tibetan garrison withdrawn. Gengyao memorialized on frontier defense, asking for an earthen fort at Zhongdu River mouth beyond Dartsedo and the Lanzhou garrison commander moved there; at Dahe in Nanbao County, with a Weimao battalion commander stationed there; Yuexi Guard's territory was vast and empty, with Man and Luo tribes raiding at will; an assistant commander was appointed and more troops garrisoned; among the tribes beyond Songpan, Ashu was the most important and was given the rank of native district chief; Saloben, native chieftain of Greater Jinchuan, earned merit at Yangdong and was given the rank of pacification commissioner; the Wumeng chieftain Damu and others were violent; native officer Ludingkun asked to capture and deliver them, after which native offices would be granted and the land divided among them. The ministry approved the plan. For pacifying Tibet, Gengyao was enfeoffed as a hereditary third-rank duke for transporting grain and holding the passes.
22
青海台吉羅卜藏丹津為顧實汗孫，糾諸台吉吹拉克諾木齊、阿爾布坦溫布、藏巴札布等，劫親王察罕丹津叛，掠青海諸部。 上命羹堯進討，諭撫遠大將軍延信及防邊理餉諸大臣，四川、陝西、雲南督、撫、提、鎮，軍事皆告羹堯。 十月，羹堯率師自甘州至西寧，改延信平逆將軍，解撫遠大將軍印授羹堯，盡護諸軍。 羹堯請以前鋒統領素丹、提督岳鍾琪為參贊大臣，從之。 論平郭羅克功，進公爵二等。
Qinghai taiji Lobzang Danjin, grandson of Gushri Khan, rallied Chilarqenomi, Arbatan Ombo, Zangbazhab, and other taiji, seized Prince Chahan Dorji in revolt, and raided the Qinghai tribes. The emperor ordered Gengyao to advance and instructed Pacification General Yansin, frontier ministers, and all military officials of Sichuan, Shaanxi, and Yunnan to report military affairs to Gengyao. In the tenth month Gengyao led his army from Ganzhou to Xining, made Yansin Pacification General, gave Gengyao the Pacification General's seal, and took full command of all armies. Gengyao asked that Vanguard Commander Sudan and Provincial Military Commissioner Yue Zhongqi serve as assisting grand ministers; the request was granted. For pacifying Golok, his dukedom was raised to second rank.
23
羹堯初至西寧，師未集，羅卜藏丹津诇知之，乃入寇，悉破傍城諸堡，移兵向城。 羹堯率左右數十人坐城樓不動，羅卜藏丹津稍引退，圍南堡。 羹堯令兵斫賊壘，敵知兵少，不為備，驅桌子山土番當前隊； 砲發，土番死者無算。 鍾琪兵至，直攻敵營，羅卜藏丹津敗奔，師從之，大潰，僅率百人遁走。 羹堯乃部署諸軍，令總兵官周瑛率兵截敵走西藏路，都統穆森駐吐魯番，副將軍阿喇納出​​噶斯，暫駐布隆吉爾，又遣參將孫繼宗將二千人與阿喇納師會。 敵侵鎮海堡，都統武格赴援，敵圍堡，戰六晝夜，參將宋可進等赴援，敵敗走，斬六百餘級，獲多巴囊素阿旺丹津。 羅卜藏丹津攻西寧南川口，師保申中堡。 敵圍堡，堡內囊素與敵通，欲鑿牆而入。 守備馬有仁等力禦，可進等赴援，夾擊，敵敗走，諸囊素助敵者皆殺之。 羹堯先後疏聞，並請副都統花色等將鄂爾多斯兵，副都統查克丹等將歸化土默特兵，總兵馬覿伯將大同鎮兵，會甘州助戰，從之。
Gengyao had only just reached Xining when Lobzang Danjin learned the army was not yet assembled, invaded, overran the nearby forts, and marched on the city. Gengyao sat unmoved on the city tower with a few dozen attendants. Lobzang Danjin drew back slightly and besieged the southern fort. Gengyao ordered an attack on the enemy stockade. The enemy, seeing how few troops he had, took no precautions and drove the Zhuozi Mountain tribesmen forward as the vanguard; When the cannon fired, the tribesmen fell beyond counting. Zhongqi's troops arrived and struck the enemy camp directly. Lobzang Danjin fled in defeat; the army pursued and routed him utterly, and he escaped with only a hundred men. Gengyao then deployed his forces: Regional Commander Zhou Ying to cut off the route to Tibet, Banner Commander Musen to garrison Turfan, Vice General Arna to advance through Gasi and encamp at Burungir, and Brigade Commander Sun Jizong with two thousand men to join Arna. The enemy attacked Zhenhai Fort. Banner Commander Wuge relieved it, and after six days and nights of siege Brigade Commander Song Kejin and others arrived. The enemy fled; more than six hundred were killed, and Tobanangsu Awang Danjin was captured. Lobzang Danjin attacked Xining's Nanchuan Pass while the army held Shenzhong Fort. The enemy besieged the fort, and inside the nangso colluded with them and tried to break through the wall to admit them. Garrison Commander Ma Youren and others held firm; Kejin and others relieved the fort and attacked from both sides. The enemy fled, and every nangso who had aided them was killed. Gengyao reported in successive memorials and asked that Deputy Lieutenant-General Huase lead Ordos troops, Chakdan lead Guihua Tumed troops, and Regional Commander Ma Xibo lead Datong garrison troops to Ganzhou; the request was granted.
24
西寧北川、上下北塔蒙回諸眾將起應羅卜藏丹津，羹堯遣千總馬忠孝撫定下北塔三十餘莊。 上北塔未服，忠孝率兵往剿，擒戮其渠，餘眾悉降。 察罕丹津走河州，羅卜藏丹津欲劫以去。 羹堯令移察罕丹津及其族屬入居蘭州。 青海台吉索諾木達什為羅卜藏丹津誘擒，脫出來歸，羹堯奏聞，命封貝子，令羹堯撫慰。 敵掠新城堡，羹堯令西寧總兵黃喜林等往剿，斬千五百餘級，擒其渠七，得器械、駝馬、牛羊無算。 以天寒，羹堯令引師還西寧。
Mongols and Muslims of Xining's north river valley and Upper and Lower Beita rose for Lobzang Danjin; Gengyao sent Battalion Commander Ma Zhongxiao to pacify more than thirty villages of Lower Beita. Upper Beita had not submitted. Zhongxiao led troops against them, captured and executed their leader, and the rest surrendered. Chahan Dorji fled to Hezhou, and Lobzang Danjin tried to seize him and carry him off. Gengyao moved Chahan Dorji and his clansmen to Lanzhou. Qinghai taiji Sonam Dash, lured and captured by Lobzang Danjin, escaped and submitted. Gengyao reported it; the court enfeoffed him as beile and ordered Gengyao to reassure him. The enemy raided Xincheng Fort. Gengyao sent Xining Regional Commander Huang Xilin and others against them; more than fifteen hundred were killed, seven leaders captured, and countless weapons, camels, horses, cattle, and sheep taken. With the weather turning cold, Gengyao ordered the army back to Xining.

He soon planned the next year's campaign and wrote: "Select nineteen thousand Green Banner troops from Xi'an, Guyuan, Ningxia, Sichuan, Datong, Yulin, and Mongol units, with Zhongqi and others commanding separate columns by four routes from Xining, Songpan, Ganzhou, and Burungir, while holding Xining, Ganzhou, and Burungir and garrisoning Yongchang, Batang, Litang, Huang Sheng Pass, and Qamdo. Chahan Dorji's followers killed Lobzang Danjin's garrisons and submitted. Gengyao proclaimed the emperor's intent and settled them beyond the Sichuan border. Mergen Daiqing Lazhab had joined Lobzang Danjin in seizing Chahan Dorji. His son Chahan Labtan and others submitted, and Gengyao ordered Lazhab brought in. Khutuktu Nomon Khan, a nephew of Chahan Dorji and lama of Ta'er Monastery, had rebelled with the enemy and rallied men to resist; he too now submitted. Gengyao recited his crimes and executed him. Lobzang Danjin invaded Burungir and was defeated by Jizong and Deputy Commander Pan Zhishan. Beyond Xining's Nanchuan Pass the nine Gomi divisions repeatedly raided; Gengyao won three divisions to submission. The rest continued raiding as before, especially the Chenku and Wo'erjia divisions. Gengyao sent Zhongqi with Wasi and Zagun native troops to Guide Fort, pacified Upper and Lower Sidongcebu, annihilated the Chenku division, captured and executed the Wo'erjia chief, and the rest surrendered.
27

In the second year the emperor, holding that Lobzang Danjin's rebellion could not be pardoned, made Zhongqi Striking Might General and urged Gengyao to advance. Lamas of Guolong Monastery northeast of Xining had joined Lobzang Danjin. Gengyao sent Zhongqi, Sudan, and others against them; the rebels held Halazhigou. The army pressed forward, crossed three ridges, and destroyed ten stockades. Kejin, Xilin, and Regional Commander Wu Zheng'an all took kills; they destroyed seven more stockades and burned the dwellings. Outside the monastery the rebels hid in the valleys, heaped fuel, and set fires; more than six thousand were killed, the monastery destroyed, and its leaders executed. Qinghai beile Lobzang Chahan, beizi Jikejizhab, and taiji Gunbusebutengnahan brought their families to Gengyao to submit; he gave them tea and barley and settled them along the border. Gengyao sent Zhongqi, Zheng'an, Xilin, Kejin, Bodyguard Dainai, and Deputy Commanders Wang Song and Ji Chengbin with six thousand men deep into enemy country, leaving Sudan at Xining to assist in administration.
28
二月，鍾琪師進次伊克喀爾吉，搜山，獲阿爾布坦溫布，喜林亦得其酋巴珠爾阿喇布坦等。 師復進，羹堯诇知阿岡都番助敵，別遣涼莊道蔣泂等督兵攻之，戮其囊素。 复擊破石門寺喇嘛，殺六百餘人，焚其寺。 鍾琪師復進次席爾哈羅色，遣兵攻噶斯，逐吹拉克諾木齊。 三月，鍾琪師復進次佈爾哈屯。 羅卜藏丹津所居地曰額母訥布隆吉，鍾琪督兵直入，分兵北防柴旦木，斷往噶斯道。 羅卜藏丹津走烏蘭穆和兒，复走柴旦木，師從之，獲其母阿爾太哈屯及其戚屬等，並男婦、牛羊、駝馬無算。 分兵攻烏蘭白克，獲吹拉克諾木齊及助亂八台吉。 時藏巴扎布已先就擒，羅卜藏丹津以二百餘人遁走。 青海部落悉平。 論功，進羹堯爵一等，別授精奇尼哈番，令其子斌襲，封遐齡如羹堯爵，加太傅； 並授素丹、可進三等阿達哈哈番，喜林二等阿達哈哈番，按察使王景灝及達鼐、瑛、嵩、成斌拜他喇布勒哈番，提督郝玉麟及正安拖沙喇哈番。
In the second month Zhongqi's army encamped at Yikeka'erji, searched the mountains, and captured Arbatan Ombo; Xilin also captured chieftains Bazhu'er Alabutan and others. The army advanced again. Gengyao learned the Agangdu tribes were aiding the enemy and sent Liangzhuang Circuit Intendant Jiang Jiong and others against them, killing their nangso. They again defeated the lamas of Shimen Monastery, killed more than six hundred, and burned the monastery. Zhongqi's army encamped at Xi'erhaluose, attacked Gasi, and drove out Chilarqenomi. In the third month Zhongqi's army encamped at Bu'erhatun. Lobzang Danjin dwelt at Emunebulongji. Zhongqi led a direct advance, detached a force to guard Chaidanmu to the north, and cut the route to Gasi. Lobzang Danjin fled to Ulan Muhe'er, then to Chaidanmu. The army pursued and captured his mother Altai Hatun and her kin, with countless men, women, cattle, sheep, and camels. A detached force attacked Ulan Baike and captured Chilarqenomi and eight taiji who had aided the rebellion. Zangbazhab had already been captured; Lobzang Danjin escaped with only two hundred men. The Qinghai tribes were all pacified. For his merit Gengyao's rank was raised to first-rank duke and he was granted jingqini han, which his son Bin inherited; Xialing was enfeoffed at the same rank and made grand tutor; Sudan and Kejin received third-rank addaha han, Xilin second-rank addaha han, Surveillance Commissioner Wang Jinghao with Dainai, Ying, Song, and Chengbin baitalabule han, and Provincial Military Commissioner Hao Yulin with Zheng'an tuosala ha.

阿拉布坦蘇巴泰等截路行劫，羹堯令繼宗往剿，逐至推墨爾，阿拉布坦蘇巴泰將妻子遁走。 成斌等搜戮餘賊至梭羅木，擊斬堪布夾木燦垂扎木素。 羹堯遣達鼐及成斌攻布哈色布蘇，獲台吉阿布濟車陳； 又遣副將岳超龍討平河州塞外鐵布等七十八寨，殺二千一百餘人，得人口、牲畜無算。 羹堯執吹拉克諾木齊、阿爾布坦溫布、藏巴扎布檻送京師。 上祭告廟、社、景陵、禦午門受俘。 羹堯策防邊諸事，以策妄阿喇布坦遣使乞降，請罷北征師，分駐巴里坤、吐魯番、哈密城、布隆吉爾駐兵守焉，轄以總兵，每營撥餘丁屯赤金衛、柳溝所田； 設同知理民事，衛守備理屯糧，游牧蒙古令分居布隆吉爾迤南山中。 寧夏邊外阿拉善以滿洲兵駐防。 上悉從所請。
Alabutan Subatai and others waylaid travelers; Gengyao sent Jizong against them, pursued to Tuimo'er, and Subatai fled with his family. Chengbin and others hunted down remaining bandits to Suoluomu and killed Khutuktu Jiamucanchuizhamusu. Gengyao sent Dainai and Chengbin against Buhaisebusu and captured taiji Abujichechen; he also sent Deputy Commander Yue Chaolong to pacify seventy-eight stockades beyond Hezhou Pass, including Tiebu, killing more than twenty-one hundred and taking countless people and livestock. Beyond the Ningxia border, Alashan was to be garrisoned by Manchu troops. The emperor approved all requests.
30
莊浪邊外謝爾蘇部土番據桌子、棋子二山為巢，皆穴地而居，官軍駐其地，奴使之； 兵或縱掠，番御之，盡殲，置不問，番始橫。 涼州南崇寺沙馬拉木扎木巴等掠新城張義諸堡。 又有郭隆寺逸出喇嘛，與西寧納硃公寺、朝天堂、加爾多寺諸番相結，糾謝爾蘇部土番謀為亂。 羹堯遣鍾琪等督兵討之，納硃公寺喇嘛降。 師進次朝天堂，遣成斌、喜林及副將張玉等四道攻加爾多寺，殺數百人，餘眾多入水死，焚其寺。 游擊馬忠孝、王大勳戰和石溝，王序吉、范世雄戰石門口，泂戰喜逢堡，蘇丹師次旁伯拉夏口，土番偽降，诇之，方置伏，縱兵擊之，所殺傷甚眾。 泂搜剿棋子山，逐賊巴洞溝，土司魯華齡逐賊天王溝，先密寺喇嘛縛其渠阿旺策淩以獻。 師入，轉戰五十餘日，殺土番殆盡。 羹堯以先密寺喇嘛反覆不常，並焚其寺，徙其眾加爾多寺外桌子山； 餘眾降，羹堯令隸華齡受約束。
Beyond Zhuanglang the Xie'ersu tribes held Zhuozi and Qizi mountains as their lair, living in caves; government troops stationed there enslaved and abused them; when soldiers plundered at will, the tribes resisted and were wiped out with impunity, and the survivors grew defiant. Shamalamuzhamuba of Chong Monastery south of Liangzhou and others raided Xincheng, Zhangyi, and other forts. Lamas who had escaped from Guolong Monastery also joined tribes of Nazhugong, Chaotian Hall, and Ji'erduo monasteries near Xining and rallied the Xie'ersu to rebel. Gengyao sent Zhongqi and others against them; the Nazhugong lamas surrendered. The army encamped at Chaotian Hall and sent Chengbin, Xilin, Deputy Commander Zhang Yu, and others in four columns against Ji'erduo Monastery; several hundred were killed, many drowned, and the monastery was burned. Assistant Commander Ma Zhongxiao and Wang Daxun fought at Heshigou, Wang Xuji and Fan Shixiong at Shimenkou, Jiang Jiong at Xifeng Fort, and Sudan encamped at Pangbolaxiakou. When the tribes feigned surrender, he learned of it, set an ambush, and struck; casualties were heavy. Jiong swept Qizi Mountain and pursued the bandits to Badonggou; native chief Lu Hualing pursued them to Tianwanggou, and Xianmi lamas bound their leader Awang Celing and delivered him. The army entered and fought for more than fifty days, nearly exterminating the tribes. Gengyao burned Xianmi Monastery for its lamas' treachery and moved its people to Zhuozi Mountain outside Ji'erduo Monastery; the rest surrendered and were placed under Hualing's authority.
31
條上青海善後諸事，請以青海諸部編置佐領。 三年一入貢，開市那拉薩拉。 陝西、雲南、四川三省邊外諸番，增設衛所撫治。 諸廟不得過二百楹，喇嘛不得過三百。 西寧北川邊外築邊牆，建城堡。 大通河設總兵，鹽池、保安堡及打箭爐外木雅吉達、巴塘、里塘諸路皆設兵。 發直隸、山西、河南、山東、陝西五省軍罪當遣者，往大通河、布隆吉爾屯田； 而令鍾琪將四千人駐西寧，撫綏諸番。 下王大臣議行。 十月，羹堯入覲，賜雙眼花翎、四團龍補服、黃帶、紫轡、金幣。 敘功，加一等阿思哈尼哈番世職，令其子富襲。
He listed Qinghai post-pacification measures and asked that the Qinghai tribes be organized into assistant banners. Tribute would be presented every three years, and a market opened at Nalasala. For tribes beyond Shaanxi, Yunnan, and Sichuan, additional guards and posts were established to govern them. No monastery might exceed two hundred bays or three hundred lamas. Beyond Xining's north river valley a frontier wall and fortresses were built. A regional commander was posted at the Datong River, and troops were stationed at Yanchi, Bao'an Fort, and along routes beyond Dartsedo to Muyajida, Batang, and Litang. Soldiers from Zhili, Shanxi, Henan, Shandong, and Shaanxi due for military banishment were sent to farm at the Datong River and Burungir; while Zhongqi was to garrison four thousand men at Xining to pacify the tribes. Princes and grand ministers were ordered to deliberate and implement the plan. In the tenth month Gengyao came to court and received double-eyed peacock feathers, four-claw dragon rank patches, a yellow belt, a purple bridle, and gold coins. For his merit he was granted the hereditary first-rank asihani han, which his son Fu inherited.
32
羹堯才氣凌厲，恃上眷遇，師出屢有功，驕縱。 行文諸督撫，書官斥姓名。 請發侍衛從軍，使為前後導引，執鞭墜鐙。 入覲，令總督李維鈞、巡撫範時捷跪道送迎。 至京師，行絕馳道。 王大臣郊迎，不為禮。 在邊，蒙古諸王公見必跪，額駙阿寶入謁亦如之。 嘗薦陝西布政使胡期恆及景灝可大用，劾四川巡撫蔡珽逮治，上即以授景灝，又擢期恆甘肅巡撫。 羹堯僕桑成鼎、魏之耀皆以從軍屢擢，成鼎布政使，之耀副將。 羹堯請發將吏數十從軍，上許之。 覲還，即劾罷驛道金南瑛等，而請以從軍主事丁松署糧道。 上責羹堯題奏錯誤，命期恆率所劾官吏詣京師。 三年正月，珽逮至，上召入見，備言羹堯暴貪誣陷狀，上特宥珽罪。
Gengyao's talent was fierce and overbearing. Relying on imperial favor and repeated victories in the field, he grew arrogant and unrestrained. In correspondence to governors-general and governors he used their titles and personal names—a mark of disrespect. He asked for bodyguards to accompany the army as guides and to hold his whip and stirrup. When he came to court he ordered Governor-General Li Weijun and Governor Fan Shijie to kneel along the road to escort him. In the capital he traveled the roadway reserved for the emperor alone. When princes and grand ministers met him outside the city, he returned no courtesy. On the frontier every Mongol prince and duke knelt before him; even Imperial Son-in-law Abao knelt when he paid his respects. He recommended Shaanxi Administration Commissioner Hu Qiheng and Jinghao for high office, impeached Sichuan Governor Cai Ting and had him arrested; the emperor gave Cai's post to Jinghao and made Qiheng governor of Gansu. Gengyao's servants Sang Chengding and Wei Zhiyao were repeatedly promoted for following the army—Chengding to provincial administration commissioner, Zhiyao to deputy commander. Gengyao asked that several dozen officers and officials accompany the army; the emperor agreed. On returning from court he immediately impeached courier-route officials including Jin Nanying and asked that Ding Song, a director who had followed the army, act as grain-route commissioner. The emperor reproached Gengyao for errors in his memorial headings and ordered Qiheng to bring the impeached officials to the capital. In the first month of the third year Ting was brought to the capital. The emperor summoned him, heard a full account of Gengyao's violence, greed, and false charges, and specially pardoned Ting.
33
二月庚午，日月合璧，五星聯珠，羹堯疏賀，用「夕惕朝乾」語，上怒，責羹堯有意倒置，諭曰：「羹堯不以朝乾夕惕許朕，則羹堯青海之功，亦在朕許不許之間而未定也。 」會期恆至，入見，上以奏對悖謬，奪官。 上命更定打箭爐外增汰官兵諸事，不用羹堯議。 四月，上諭曰：「羹堯舉劾失當，遣將士築城南坪，不惜番民，致驚惶生事，反以降番复叛具奏。 青海蒙古饑饉，匿不上聞。 怠玩昏憒，不可復任總督，改授杭州將軍。 」而以鍾琪署總督，命上撫遠大將軍印。 羹堯既受代，疏言：「臣不敢久居陝西，亦不敢遽赴浙江，今於儀徵水陸交通之處候旨。 」上益怒，促羹堯赴任。 山西巡撫伊都立、都統前山西巡撫範時捷、川陝總督岳鍾琪、河南巡撫田文鏡、侍郎黃炳、鴻臚少卿單疇書、原任直隸巡撫趙之垣交章發羹堯罪狀，侍郎史貽直、高其佩赴山西按時捷劾羹堯遣兵圍郃陽民堡殺戮無辜，亦以讞辭入奏，上命分案議罪。 罷羹堯將軍，授閒散章京，自二等公遞降至拜他喇布勒哈番，乃盡削羹堯職。
On gengwu in the second month the sun and moon joined and the five planets aligned; Gengyao sent a congratulatory memorial using the inverted phrase "vigilant at evening, dry at morning" instead of the proper "dry in the morning, vigilant at evening." The emperor was furious and said: "If Gengyao will not grant that I am vigilant morning and evening, then his Qinghai merit remains for me to grant or withhold as I choose. " When Qiheng arrived for audience, the emperor stripped him of office for absurd answers in memorials. The emperor reordered troop increases and reductions beyond Dartsedo and rejected Gengyao's proposals. In the fourth month the emperor said: "Gengyao's recommendations and impeachments were improper. He sent troops to build a walled city at Nanping without regard for the tribes, causing alarm and unrest, then reported that submitted tribes had rebelled again. Qinghai Mongols were starving, yet he concealed it from the throne. Negligent and muddled, he could no longer serve as governor-general and was made general of Hangzhou instead. " Zhongqi was made acting governor-general and ordered to present the Pacification General's seal. After his replacement Gengyao wrote: "I dare not remain long in Shaanxi, nor hurry to Zhejiang; I now wait for instructions at Yizheng, where land and water routes meet. " The emperor grew angrier still and ordered Gengyao to proceed to his post at once. Governors and ministers including Yiduli, Fan Shijie, Yue Zhongqi, Tian Wenjing, Huang Bing, Dan Choushu, and Zhao Zhiyuan exposed Gengyao's crimes in successive memorials. Shi Yizhi and Gao Qipei investigated Shijie's charge that Gengyao had besieged a civilian fort at Heyang and slaughtered innocents. The emperor ordered separate deliberations on punishment. Gengyao was dismissed as general and given an idle clerkship; his rank fell step by step from second-rank duke to baitalabule han, and all his offices were stripped away.
34
十二月，逮至京師，下議政大臣、三法司、九卿會鞫。 是月甲戌，具獄辭：羹堯大逆之罪五，欺罔之罪九，僭越之罪十六，狂悖之罪十三，專擅之罪六，忌刻之罪六，殘忍之罪四，貪黷之罪十八，侵蝕之罪十五，凡九十二款，當大辟，親屬緣坐。 上諭曰：「羹堯謀逆雖實，而事蹟未著，朕念青海之功，不忍加極刑。 」遣領侍衛內大臣馬爾賽、步軍統領阿齊圖齎詔諭羹堯獄中令自裁。 遐齡及羹堯兄希堯奪官，免其罪； 斬其子富； 諸子年十五以上皆戍極邊。 羹堯幕客鄒魯、汪景祺先後皆坐斬，親屬給披甲為奴。 又有靜一道人者，四川巡撫憲德捕送京師，亦誅死。 五年，赦羹堯諸子，交遐齡管束。 遐齡旋卒，還原職，賜祭。
In the twelfth month he was arrested and brought to the capital for joint trial by Deliberative Ministers, the Three Judicial Offices, and the Nine Ministers. On jiaxu the indictment listed ninety-two counts: five of great treason, nine of deception, sixteen of overstepping rank, thirteen of reckless impiety, six of monopolizing authority, six of jealousy, four of cruelty, eighteen of corruption, and fifteen of embezzlement. He was sentenced to death by dismemberment, with relatives punished by association. The emperor said: "Though Gengyao's treason is real, the evidence is not fully clear. Remembering his Qinghai service, I cannot bear the extreme penalty. " He sent Chief Bodyguard Grand Minister Ma'ersai and Commander of the Metropolitan Infantry Aqitu with an edict ordering Gengyao to take his own life in prison. Xialing and Gengyao's elder brother Xiyao were stripped of office but spared; his son Fu was executed; all sons aged fifteen and above were exiled to the extreme frontier. Gengyao's retainers Zou Lu and Wang Jingqi were both executed, and their relatives were enslaved as bond servants. The Daoist Jingyi was also arrested by Sichuan Governor Xiande, sent to the capital, and executed. In the fifth year Gengyao's sons were pardoned and placed under Xialing's supervision. Xialing soon died; his original rank was restored and sacrificial rites granted.
35
希堯，初自筆帖式累擢工部侍郎。 既，奪官，復起內務府總管，命榷稅淮安，加左都御史。 十三年，為江蘇巡撫高其倬劾罷。 乾隆三年，卒。
Xiyao rose from clerk to vice minister of the Board of Works. After dismissal he was reappointed superintendent of the Imperial Household Department, ordered to levy taxes at Huai'an, and made left censor-in-chief. In the thirteenth year Jiangsu Governor Gao Qizhuo impeached and dismissed him. He died in Qianlong 3.
36
胡期恆，字元方，湖廣武陵人。 祖統虞，明崇禎末進士。 國初授檢討，官至秘書院學士。 父獻徵，自廕生授都察院經歷，官至湖北布政使。 期恆，康熙四十四年舉人。 獻徵與遐齡友，歡若弟昆，期恆少從羹堯遊。 上南巡，獻詩，授翰林院典籍。 出為夔州通判，有恩信，民為建生祠。 羹堯為巡撫，薦期恆，遷夔州知府，再遷川東道。 羹堯兼督陝西，复薦遷陝西布政使。 期恆通曉朝章國故，才敏，善理繁劇，羹堯深倚之。 羹堯挾貴而驕，惟期恆能以微言救其失。 羹堯奴辱咸陽知縣，期恆執而杖之，自是諸奴稍斂戢。 嘗諷羹堯善持盈，羹堯勿能用。 及羹堯敗，諸為羹堯引進者，爭劾羹堯以自解； 期恆惟引咎，終不言羹堯，乃下獄頌系。 至高宗即位，始得釋。 僑居江南，久之，卒。
Hu Qiheng, whose style was Yuanfang, was from Wuling in Huguang. His grandfather Tongyu was a jinshi in late Chongzhen. At the dynasty's founding he was made reviser and rose to Secretariat academician. His father Xianzheng entered through yin privilege as a Censorate secretary and rose to Hubei administration commissioner. Qiheng passed the provincial examinations in Kangxi 44. Xianzheng was as close to Xialing as a brother, and Qiheng associated with Gengyao from youth. When the emperor toured the south, he presented a poem and was appointed Hanlin archivist. As subprefect of Kuizhou he won the people's trust, and they built a living shrine in his honor. When Gengyao was governor he recommended Qiheng, who became prefect of Kuizhou and then Eastern Sichuan circuit intendant. When Gengyao also supervised Shaanxi, he again recommended Qiheng as Shaanxi administration commissioner. Qiheng knew court regulations and national affairs thoroughly, was quick and capable with complex business, and Gengyao relied on him deeply. Gengyao grew arrogant with power; only Qiheng could correct him with subtle words. When Gengyao's slave insulted the magistrate of Xianyang, Qiheng had him arrested and beaten, and the slaves thereafter restrained themselves somewhat. He once urged Gengyao to know when to hold back, but Gengyao would not listen. When Gengyao fell, all whom he had advanced rushed to impeach him to clear themselves; Qiheng alone took the blame and never spoke against Gengyao; he was imprisoned awaiting trial. He was released only when the Qianlong Emperor took the throne. He lived in exile in Jiangnan and died there long afterward.
37
論曰：雍正初，隆科多以貴戚，年羹堯以戰多，內外夾輔為重臣。 乃不旋踵，幽囚誅夷，亡也忽諸。 當其貴盛侈汰，隆科多恃元舅之親，受顧命之重； 羹堯自代允禵為大將軍，師所向有功。 方且憑藉權勢，無復顧忌，即於覆滅而不自怵。 臣罔作威福，古聖所誡，可不謹歟！
The commentary says: At the beginning of Yongzheng, Long Keduo by imperial kinship and Nian Gengyao by battlefield merit were the great ministers supporting the throne at court and in the field. Yet almost at once they were imprisoned and executed, perishing as suddenly as they had risen. At the height of their power and extravagance, Long Keduo relied on his kinship as the emperor's uncle by marriage and on having received the dying emperor's testament; Gengyao had replaced Yunsi as great general, and his armies won victory wherever they marched. They relied on power without restraint and did not check themselves even as ruin approached. Ministers must not arrogate authority and bestow favors—the ancient sages warned of this. How can one not take heed!
