年分大學士協辦
嘉慶元年丙辰阿桂九月壬戌辭兼管兵部。和珅王杰十月己卯辭兼禮部。福康安五月壬申卒。孫士毅七月辛亥卒。董誥十月己卯授大學士，兼管禮部事。
嘉慶二年丁巳阿桂八月己未卒。和珅王杰董誥三月壬戌丁憂。劉墉蘇淩阿九月甲申任東閣大學士。保寧（十二月協辦大學士，仍留伊犁將軍。）
嘉慶三年戊午和珅八月戊戌封公爵。王杰劉墉蘇淩阿保寧
嘉慶四年己未和珅正月丁卯科道列欵糾劾，下獄。王杰劉墉二月己亥加太子少保。蘇淩阿正月乙亥以原品致仕。保寧正月戊辰任武英殿大學士。二月加太子太保。慶桂三月己未任文淵閣大學士，管刑部。九月庚申加太子太傅。董誥五月甲申服闋，授文華殿大學士。二月己亥加太子太保。九月庚午加太子太傅。保寧正月遷。慶桂正月戊辰以刑部尚書協辦大學士。書麟三月癸亥協辦大學士。
嘉慶五年庚申王杰劉墉保寧慶桂董誥六月丁卯管刑部。書麟
嘉慶六年辛酉王杰劉墉保寧慶桂四月管吏部。董誥書麟四月卒。吉慶四月協辦大學士，仍留兩廣總督。
嘉慶七年壬戌王杰七月甲申致仕，加太子太傅，食俸十年。劉墉保寧慶桂十二月癸丑賞給騎都尉世職。董誥十二月癸丑賞給騎都尉世職。吉慶十月降。琳寧十一月以吏部尚書協辦大學士，加太子少保。朱珪八月以戶部尚書協辦。
嘉慶八年癸亥劉墉保寧慶桂董誥琳寧朱珪
嘉慶九年甲子劉墉十二月庚辰卒。保寧慶桂董誥琳寧六月免。祿康六月協辦。朱珪正月遷。
嘉慶十年乙丑保寧慶桂董誥朱珪正月辛亥任體仁閣大學士，管工部事。祿康紀昀正月授。二月卒。劉權之二月授。閏六月降。費淳閏六月授。
嘉慶十一年丙寅保寧十月丁酉疾免，令食公爵全俸。慶桂董誥朱珪十二月戊寅卒。祿康十一月己未任東閣大學士，管戶部。祿康十一月遷。長麟十一月授。費淳
嘉慶十二年丁卯慶桂董誥祿康費淳長麟費淳正月遷。戴衢亨正月授。
嘉慶十三年戊辰慶桂董誥祿康費淳長麟戴衢亨
嘉慶十四年己巳慶桂正月辛酉加太子太師。董誥正月辛酉加太子太師。祿康十二月辛丑降。費淳十二月庚戌降。長麟祿康十二月降授。戴衢亨
嘉慶十五年庚午慶桂五月管戶部。董誥勒保正月庚辰任武英殿大學士。五月癸亥革降。祿康五月癸亥仍復東閣大學士，管吏部。戴衢亨五月癸酉任體仁閣大學士，管工部。祿康明亮五月授。戴衢亨劉權之正月復授。
嘉慶十六年辛未慶桂董誥祿康六月癸丑革。戴衢亨四月戊申卒。劉權之五月辛巳任體仁閣大學士，管工部。勒保六月丁巳任武英殿大學士，管吏部。明亮六月革。松筠六月授。劉權之鄒炳泰五月授。
嘉慶十七年壬申慶桂董誥劉權之勒保松筠鄒炳泰
嘉慶十八年癸酉慶桂九月癸未休。董誥劉權之九月癸未休。勒保松筠九月甲申任東閣大學士，仍兼伊犁將軍。曹振鏞九月甲申任體仁閣大學士，管工部。托津九月授。鄒炳泰九月降。曹振鏞九月授。百齡九月授。
嘉慶十九年甲戌董誥勒保八月辛未病休。松筠曹振鏞托津八月辛未任東閣大學士，管戶部。九月癸巳加太子太保。托津百齡十二月革。明亮八月以兵部尚書協辦。章煦十二月授。
嘉慶二十年乙亥董誥松筠曹振鏞托津明亮章煦
嘉慶二十一年丙子董誥松筠九月乙未朔復太子太保。曹振鏞托津明亮章煦
嘉慶二十二年丁丑董誥六月管刑部。松筠六月甲戌革。曹振鏞托津六月管理藩院。明亮六月甲戌任武英殿大學士，管兵部。辛巳加太子太保。明亮伯麟六月甲戌協辦，仍留雲貴總督。章煦三月病免。戴均元三月授。
嘉慶二十三年戊寅董誥二月乙亥以疾致仕。曹振鏞托津明亮章煦三月庚戌任文淵閣大學士，管刑部。伯麟戴均元
嘉慶二十四年己卯曹振鏞托津明亮章煦伯麟戴均元
嘉慶二十五年庚辰曹振鏞托津明亮章煦二月癸卯以疾致仕。戴均元二月癸卯任文淵閣大學士，加太子太保，管理刑部。伯麟戴均元二月遷。吳璥二月授。
道光元年辛巳曹振鏞三月己巳加太子太傅。托津三月己巳加太子太傅。明亮四月癸巳致仕。戴均元五月己未任體仁閣大學士，兼管兵部。伯麟五月遷。長齡五月庚子協辦大學士，仍留陝甘總督任。吳璥二月戊戌予告。孫玉庭二月庚子協辦大學士，仍留兩江總督任。
道光二年壬午曹振鏞托津戴均元伯麟六月癸丑休，仍充實錄館總裁。長齡六月戊辰任文華殿大學士，兼管理藩院事。長齡六月遷。英和六月戊辰以戶部尚書協辦大學士。孫玉庭
道光三年癸未曹振鏞托津正月革。戴均元伯麟長齡英和孫玉庭
道光四年甲申曹振鏞托津戴均元三月戊辰太子太保。七月辛巳休。長齡孫玉庭閏七月丁未任體仁閣大學士。英和孫玉庭閏七月遷。蔣攸銛閏七月丁未協辦大學士，仍留直隸總督任。
道光五年乙酉曹振鏞托津長齡孫玉庭六月戊午降。蔣攸銛六月戊午任體仁閣大學士，仍留直隸總督任。十一月召入，管刑部。英和蔣攸銛六月遷。汪廷珍六月戊午以尚書協辦。
道光六年丙戌曹振鏞托津長齡蔣攸銛英和汪廷珍
道光七年丁亥曹振鏞七月壬申晉太子太師。托津五月管刑部。長齡五月管戶部。蔣攸銛五月授兩江總督。七月壬申加太子太保。英和七月己未免。富俊七月己未以理藩院尚書協辦。汪廷珍七月卒。盧蔭溥七月丁巳以吏部尚書協辦。
道光八年戊子曹振鏞正月乙丑晉加太傅銜，賞用紫韁。托津長齡二月丁丑封二等威勇公。五月庚戌晉太保，賞三眼花翎。蔣攸銛正月丙寅晉太子太傅。富俊盧蔭溥
道光九月己丑曹振鏞十月賞還雙眼花翎。托津長齡蔣攸銛富俊盧蔭溥
道光十年庚寅曹振鏞托津十二月解管刑部。長齡蔣攸銛九月丁丑免。盧蔭溥九月戊寅任體仁閣大學士。富俊盧蔭溥九月遷。李鴻賓九月戊寅協辦，仍留兩廣總督。
道光十一年辛卯曹振鏞托津十一月丙辰病免。長齡十二月管兵部。盧蔭溥富俊十二月乙酉任東閣大學士，管兵部。丁亥改管理藩院。富俊十二月遷。文孚十二月乙酉以尚書協辦。李鴻賓

道光十三年癸巳曹振鏞長齡盧蔭溥三月丙子致仕，加太子太保，在籍食俸。富俊潘世恩四月己酉任體仁閣大學士。文孚阮元
道光十四年甲午曹振鏞正月丁卯加恩在紫禁城內乘車轎。長齡正月丁卯加恩在紫禁城內乘車轎。富俊正月丁卯加恩在紫禁城內乘車轎。二月乙丑卒。贈太子太傅。潘世恩文孚十一月丙戌任東閣大學士，管吏部事。文孚十一月遷。穆彰阿十二月授。阮元

道光十六年丙申長齡潘世恩文孚七月丙申致仕。阮元穆彰阿七月庚子任武英殿大學士，管理工部。穆彰阿七月遷。琦善七月庚子協辦，仍留直隸總督。王鼎
道光十七年丁酉長齡正月己卯賞四開褉袍。潘世恩正月己卯太子太保。阮元穆彰阿琦善王鼎
道光十八年戊戌長齡正月乙亥卒。潘世恩五月癸丑改武英殿大學士。阮元五月癸丑致仕。穆彰阿五月癸丑改文華殿大學士。八月壬辰丁母憂。琦善二月癸卯任文淵閣大學士，仍留直隸總督。王鼎五月癸丑任東閣大學士，仍管刑部。琦善伊里布二月癸卯協辦，仍留雲貴總督。王鼎五月遷。湯金釗五月癸丑以戶部尚書協辦。

道光二十年庚子潘世恩穆彰阿琦善王鼎正月壬辰加太子太保。伊里布湯金釗

道光二十二年壬寅潘世恩穆彰阿王鼎二月加太子太保。四月卒。寶興奕經十月甲午革。敬徵十月乙未以戶部尚書協辦。卓秉恬

道光二十五年乙巳潘世恩穆彰阿寶興卓秉恬七月辛未管兵部事。敬徵二月革。耆英二月授。陳官俊

道光二十九年己酉潘世恩穆彰阿卓秉恬耆英琦善陳官俊七月卒。祁寯藻七月授。

Fifteenth year of Guangxu (jichou)Li Hongzhang, Elehebu (in the first month given the added title of Grand Guardian of the Crown Prince), Encheng (on gengchen in the second month changed to Grand Secretary of the Dongge), and Zhang Zhiwan (on xinyou in the first month became Grand Secretary, was placed in charge of the Board of Revenue, and was given the added title of Grand Guardian of the Crown Prince).Fukun (in the first month given the added title of Grand Guardian of the Crown Prince), Zhang Zhiwan (promoted in the first month), and Xu Tong (on xinyou in the first month appointed Associate Grand Secretary as Minister of Personnel).
Sixteenth year of Guangxu (gengyin)Li Hongzhang, Elehebu, Encheng, and Zhang Zhiwan.Fukun and Xu Tong.
Seventeenth year of Guangxu (xinmao)Li Hongzhang, Elehebu, Encheng, and Zhang Zhiwan.Fukun and Xu Tong.
Eighteenth year of Guangxu (renchen)Li Hongzhang, Elehebu, Encheng (died in the intercalary sixth month), Zhang Zhiwan (on jiashen in the eighth month changed to charge of the Board of Personnel; on jiawu in the ninth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Dongge), and Fukun (on jiashen in the eighth month appointed Grand Secretary and placed in charge of the Board of Revenue; on jiawu in the ninth month became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion).Fukun (promoted in the eighth month), Linshu (on jiashen in the eighth month appointed Associate Grand Secretary as Minister of Personnel), and Xu Tong.
Nineteenth year of Guangxu (guisi)Li Hongzhang, Elehebu, Zhang Zhiwan, and Fukun.Linshu and Xu Tong.
Twentieth year of Guangxu (jiawu)Li Hongzhang (granted a triple-eyed peacock feather in the first month), Elehebu, Zhang Zhiwan, and Fukun.Linshu and Xu Tong.
Twenty-first year of Guangxu (yiwei)Li Hongzhang (in the first month his triple-eyed peacock feather was restored and he was dispatched to Japan to negotiate treaties; in the seventh month he entered the Grand Secretariat to handle state affairs), Elehebu, Zhang Zhiwan, Fukun (retired in the intercalary fifth month), and Linshu (on yiyou in the sixth month appointed Grand Secretary and placed in charge of the Board of Works; on bingshen became Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion).Linshu (promoted in the sixth month), Kungang (appointed in the sixth month), and Xu Tong.
Twenty-second year of Guangxu (bingchen)Li Hongzhang, Elehebu (retired on wuxu in the third month), Zhang Zhiwan (retired on dingwei in the ninth month), Linshu (on bingxu in the fourth month changed to charge of the Board of Revenue; on jihai in the fifth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Wuying Palace), Kungang (appointed Grand Secretary on wuzi in the fourth month; on bingxu placed in charge of the Board of Works; on jihai in the fifth month became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion; on bingshen in the eleventh month became Grand Secretary of the Dongge), and Xu Tong (appointed Grand Secretary on jichou in the tenth month and placed in charge of the Board of Personnel; on bingshen in the eleventh month became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion).Kungang (promoted in the fourth month), Xu Tong (promoted in the tenth month), Ronglu (on wuzi in the fourth month appointed Associate Grand Secretary as Minister of War), and Li Hongzao (in the tenth month again appointed Associate Grand Secretary as Minister of Rites).
Twenty-third year of Guangxu (dingyou)Li Hongzhang, Linshu, Kungang, and Xu Tong.Ronglu. Li Hongzao died in the seventh month. Weng Tonghe was appointed in the eighth month.
Twenty-fourth year of Guangxu (wuxu)Li Hongzhang. Linshu died on bingchen in the intercalary third month. Kungang, Xu Tong, and Ronglu; on jiachen in the fourth month, Ronglu was appointed Grand Secretary and placed in charge of the Board of Revenue. On bingchen in the fifth month he became Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion. On dingsi he was appointed Governor-General of Zhili. In the eighth month he was summoned in, placed in charge of the Board of War, and given command over all Beiyang forces.Ronglu. On jiachen in the fourth month, Gangyi, as Minister of War, was appointed Associate Grand Secretary. Weng Tonghe was removed in the fourth month. Sun Jianai, as Minister of Personnel, was appointed Associate Grand Secretary in the fifth month.
Twenty-fifth year of Guangxu (jihai)Li Hongzhang, Kungang, Xu Tong, and Ronglu.Gangyi. Sun Jianai was relieved due to illness in the eleventh month. Wang Wenshao, as Minister of Revenue, was appointed Associate Grand Secretary in the eleventh month.
Twenty-sixth year of Guangxu (gengzi)Li Hongzhang, Kungang, and Xu Tong; Xu Tong committed suicide on guiwei in the eleventh month. Ronglu and Wang Wenshao; on guichou in the tenth month, Wang Wenshao was appointed Grand Secretary and placed in charge of the Board of Revenue. On jiashen in the eleventh month he became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion.Gangyi died in the ninth month. Chongli was appointed in the tenth month. Wang Wenshao was transferred in the tenth month. Xu Fu was appointed in the tenth month.
Twenty-seventh year of Guangxu (xinchou)Li Hongzhang died on jiawu in the tenth month. Kungang was reassigned to manage the Board of War in the sixth month. Ronglu was reassigned to manage the Board of Revenue in the sixth month and, on bingchen in the twelfth month, was reassigned as Grand Secretary of the Wenhua Hall. Wang Wenshao became Minister of Foreign Affairs in the sixth month and, on bingchen in the twelfth month, was reassigned as Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion. Sun Jianai was appointed Grand Secretary on jiachen in the twelfth month and, on bingchen, became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion, managing the Board of Personnel.Chongli and Xu Fu.
Twenty-eighth year of Guangxu (renyin)Kungang, Ronglu, Wang Wenshao, and Sun Jianai.Chongli and Xu Fu.
Twenty-ninth year of Guangxu (guimao)On wuwu in the fifth month, Kungang was reassigned as Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion; on xinmao in the seventh month, he retired from office. Ronglu died on jisi in the third month. On wuwu in the fifth month, Wang Wenshao was reassigned as Grand Secretary of the Wuying Hall; in the ninth month, he was relieved as Minister of Foreign Affairs and placed in charge of the Board of Revenue. Sun Jianai was reassigned as Grand Secretary of the Dongge Pavilion on bingzi in the eighth month. Chongli was appointed Grand Secretary on xinhai in the fourth month, became Grand Secretary of the Dongge Pavilion on wuwu in the fifth month, and was reassigned as Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion on bingzi in the eighth month. Jingxin was appointed Grand Secretary on renyin in the eighth month and, on bingzi, became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion.Chongli was transferred in the fourth month. Jingxin was appointed in the fourth month and transferred in the eighth month. Yude was appointed in the eighth month. Xu Fu.
Thirtieth year of Guangxu (jiachen)Wang Wenshao, Sun Jianai, Chongli, Jingxin (relieved due to illness on guiwei in the ninth month), and Yude (appointed Grand Secretary on dingwei in the tenth month; on jiyou became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion).Yude (promoted in the tenth month), Shixu (appointed in the tenth month), and Xu Fu.
Thirty-first year of Guangxu (yisi)Wang Wenshao, Sun Jianai (on bingxu in the sixth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion), Chongli (relieved due to illness on jihai in the fifth month), Yude (on bingyin in the sixth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Dongge; died on xinwei in the eleventh month), Shixu (appointed Grand Secretary on jiwei in the sixth month; on bingyin became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion; on jiayin in the twelfth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Dongge), and Natong (appointed Grand Secretary on xinhai in the twelfth month; on jiayin became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion).Shixu (promoted in the sixth month), Natong (appointed in the sixth month and promoted in the twelfth month), Rongqing (appointed in the twelfth month), and Xu Fu.
Thirty-second year of Guangxu (bingwu)Wang Wenshao, Sun Jianai, Shixu, and Natong.Rongqing, Xu Fu (retired in the first month), and Qu Hongji (appointed in the first month).
Thirty-third year of Guangxu (dingwei)Wang Wenshao (retired on xinchou in the fifth month), Sun Jianai (on dingchou in the sixth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Wuying Palace), Shixu (on dingchou in the sixth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion), Natong (on dingchou in the sixth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Dongge), and Zhang Zhidong (appointed Grand Secretary on guiyou in the sixth month; on dingchou became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion).Rongqing, Qu Hongji (vacated office in the first month), Zhang Zhidong (appointed in the fifth month and promoted in the sixth month), and Lu Chuanlin (appointed in the sixth month).
Thirty-fourth year of Guangxu (wushen)Sun Jianai, Shixu, Natong, and Zhang Zhidong.Rongqing and Lu Chuanlin.
First year of Xuantong (jiyou)Sun Jianai (died in the tenth month), Shixu (in the eleventh month changed to Grand Secretary of the Wenhua Hall), Natong (in the eleventh month changed to Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion), Zhang Zhidong (died in the eighth month), Lu Chuanlin (appointed Grand Secretary in the ninth month and became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion; in the eleventh month changed to Grand Secretary of the Dongge), and Lu Runxiang (appointed Grand Secretary in the ninth month; in the eleventh month became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion).Rongqing, Lu Chuanlin (promoted in the ninth month), Lu Runxiang (appointed and promoted in the ninth month), and Dai Hongci (appointed in the eleventh month).
Second year of Xuantong (gengxu)Shixu, Natong, Lu Chuanlin (died in the seventh month), Lu Runxiang (in the eighth month changed to Grand Secretary of the Dongge), and Xu Shichang (appointed Grand Secretary in the eighth month and became Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion).Rongqing, Dai Hongci (died in the first month), and Xu Shichang (appointed in the first month and promoted in the eighth month).
Third year of Xuantong (xinhai)Shixu, Natong, Lu Runxiang, and Xu Shichang.Rongqing and Li Dianlin.
