高天喜鄂實三格和起唐喀祿阿敏道滿福
Gao Tianxi, E Shi, San Ge, He Qi, Tang Kalu, A Min Dao, Man Fu

4
高天喜，甘肅西寧人。 天喜本準噶爾人，雍正中為我師所俘。 高氏撫為子，因從其族籍。 從軍，累擢保寧堡守備。 乾隆二十二年，副將軍兆惠擊伊犁，天喜從參將邁斯漢赴援。 遇噶勒雜特賊百餘，擊殺之，獲其駝馬。 既，聞兆惠被困濟爾哈朗，議馳救，邁斯漢怯不進。 巴里坤辦事大臣雅爾哈善以聞，上即奪邁斯漢官以命天喜。 尋遷金塔協副將。 再遷西寧鎮總兵，授領隊大臣。 二十三年十月，師攻葉爾羌，兆惠議出間道襲取賊輜重，渡黑水。 天喜督兵修橋渡師，未及半，賊大至。 天喜聞兆惠陷賊陣，舍橋亟赴之，奮與賊戰，與鄂實、三格、特通額俱沒於陣。 上賦詩惜之。 諡果義，又賜其家白金千。
Gao Tianxi was from Xining, Gansu. Tianxi was originally a Dzungar who had been taken prisoner by Qing forces in the Yongzheng era. The Gao family adopted him as a son, and he was entered on their clan register. He joined the army and rose step by step to garrison commander of Baoning Fort. In Qianlong 22, when Deputy General Zhaohui marched on Ili, Tianxi went with Major Maisihan to reinforce him. They encountered over a hundred Galzat rebels, defeated them, and captured their camels and horses. When they learned Zhaohui was besieged at Jierhalang, they debated a dash to relieve him, but Maisihan shrank from advancing. Commissioner Yarghasan at Barkol reported it; the emperor immediately removed Maisihan and put Tianxi in command. He was soon made assistant deputy commander at Jinta. He was later promoted to Xining garrison commander and made a column commander. In the tenth month of year 23, during the assault on Yarkand, Zhaohui proposed a flanking march to seize rebel supplies and cross the Black River. Tianxi directed men to build a bridge for the crossing; before it was half finished, the enemy came up in strength. Learning Zhaohui was caught in the enemy line, Tianxi left the bridge and charged to his aid; he fought until he fell, along with E Shi, San Ge, and Tetunge. The emperor wrote a poem mourning his death. He received the posthumous name Guoyi, and his family was given a thousand taels of silver.
5
鄂實，西林覺羅氏，滿洲鑲藍旗人，大學士鄂爾泰第二子。 出為叔父鄂禮後。 自廕生授三等侍衛。 累遷本旗副都統、左翼前鋒統領。 兄鄂容安死阿睦爾撒納之亂，鄂實請從軍，授參贊大臣，佐定邊將軍成袞扎布，出西路。 二十二年夏，成袞扎布令逐捕扎那噶爾布，鄂實以地險馬疲，中道引還。 上手詔詰責曰：「若謂地險，賊何以能行？ 若謂馬疲，賊馬何獨能壯健？ 」左授藍翎侍衛。 是冬，鄂實逐扎哈沁賊，斬一百四十餘級，獲牲械。 上謂：「今當大雪，馬力應疲乏，尚能勦賊。 彼時鄂實為參贊大臣，有事但諉諸將軍。 茲以負罪，乃直前勦賊，朕知其隱矣。 」量遷三等侍衛。 死事，上令仍視前鋒統領賜卹，諡果壯。
E Shi of the Xilin Gioro clan, a Bordered Blue Banner Manchu, was Grand Secretary Ortai’s second son. He was adopted as heir to his uncle E Li. Through the yinsheng quota he received a third-rank bodyguard post. He rose to deputy lieutenant-general of his banner and commander of the Left Vanguard. After his brother E Rong’an died in Amursana’s revolt, E Shi volunteered for service and was made consulting commissioner under Pacification General Chenggezhabu on the western front. In the summer of year 22 Chenggezhabu ordered pursuit of Zanagarbu; citing rough country and spent horses, E Shi led the force back before the hunt was done. The emperor rebuked him in an edict: “If the ground is too difficult for you, how do the rebels manage to cross it? If your horses are exhausted, why are the rebels’ mounts alone fresh and strong?” He was demoted to blue-plume bodyguard. That winter he pursued Zahakin rebels, killed more than 140, and took livestock and weapons. The emperor remarked: “Snow lies deep and horses should be spent, yet you still routed the enemy. When he was consulting commissioner, he had only pushed every difficulty onto the general. Now, under a stain on his record, he leads the charge himself—I see through him.” He was raised again to third-rank bodyguard. He died in battle; the emperor granted funeral honors as for a vanguard commander and the name Guozhuang.
6
三格，棟鄂氏，滿洲正白旗人。 自諸生授藍翎侍衛。 累遷黑龍江副都統。 命將索倫、巴爾呼兵三千，佐參贊大臣策楞出西路，為領隊大臣。 策楞以怯懦逮，三格亦坐奪官。 旋复授正白旗蒙古副都統。 攻呼爾璊台吉賽音伯勒克等，再戰，掠其牧地，予三等輕車都尉世職。 二十二年春，定邊將軍成袞扎布令逐捕扎那噶爾布，未得。 秋，師至博羅和羅，遇叛黨額林沁達瓦等百餘戶，三格與戰。 會布魯古特台吉琿齊、呼爾璊台吉達瓦斬扎那噶爾布偽請降，並請招額林沁達瓦，三格信之，遽引師還，琿齊等旋遁去。 坐奪官，並削世職，以兵伍自效。 死事，上命仍視副都統賜卹，諡剛勇。
San Ge of the Donggo clan was a Plain White Banner Manchu. He entered service from student status as a blue-plume bodyguard. He rose to deputy lieutenant-general of Heilongjiang. He was told to lead three thousand Solon and Barhu soldiers under Consulting Commissioner Celeng on the western route as column commander. When Celeng was seized for cowardice, San Ge too was stripped of rank. He was soon reappointed Mongol deputy lieutenant-general of the Plain White Banner. In attacks on Khurun Prince Sainberlek and others he fought twice, overran their pastures, and received a hereditary third-class commandant of light chariots. In the spring of year 22 Pacification General Chenggezhabu ordered Zanagarbu hunted down, without success. That autumn, at Boluheluo, the column met over a hundred rebel households under Erinkindawa; San Ge gave battle. Burut Prince Hunchi and Khurun Prince Dawa killed Zanagarbu and feigned submission, asking also to bring in Erinkindawa; San Ge trusted them, withdrew at once, and Hunchi and his party soon escaped. He lost office and hereditary rank and served on as a private soldier. He died in battle; the emperor granted funeral honors as for a deputy lieutenant-general and the name Gangyong.
7
天喜、鄂實、三格並祀昭忠祠，予騎都尉兼雲騎尉世職。 回部平，圖形紫光閣。 特通額，策楞子也，附見策楞傳。
Tianxi, E Shi, and San Ge were all entered in the Shrine of Loyalty and granted hereditary commandant of cavalry with cloud-rider. After the Muslim regions were pacified, their likenesses were hung in the Hall of Purple Splendor. Tetunge was Celeng’s son; see Celeng’s biography.
8
和起，馬佳氏，滿洲鑲藍旗人。 其先世阿音布，國初以軍功授拜他喇布勒哈番世職。 和起襲職，授盛京協領。 累擢寧夏副都統。 乾隆十九年，命與侍衛海福將千人佐定西將軍永常討達瓦齊，遷寧夏將軍。 永常劾和起兵不及額，而和起先疏言將九百人以往，留百人護輜重，上得永常疏，不之罪也。 尋又命偕提督豆斌為巴里坤辦事大臣，策楞代永常為定西將軍，复劾和起送兵馬遲誤，當奪官，留任。 旋复官，授欽差大臣關防，召詣京師諮軍事。 達什達瓦所屬宰桑訥默庫、曼集、烏達瑚們都等在軍私還游牧，命和起嚴鞫得實，以降人請予寬典，上不許，命正軍法。
He Qi of the Majia clan was a Bordered Blue Banner Manchu. His forebear Ayinbu, in the dynasty’s early wars, won the hereditary rank of baitalabuleha fan. He Qi succeeded to the rank and became assistant commandant at Mukden. He rose to deputy lieutenant-general of Ningxia. In Qianlong 19 he and Bodyguard Haifu were told to lead a thousand men under Pacification General of the West Yongchang against Dawachi; he was then made Ningxia garrison commander. Yongchang charged He Qi with shorting the troop quota, but He Qi had already written that he would advance nine hundred men and leave a hundred with the baggage; the emperor read Yongchang’s charge and did not blame him. He was soon paired with Provincial Commander Dou Bin as commissioner at Barkol; when Celeng replaced Yongchang, he impeached He Qi again for late delivery of men and horses—a fault that should have meant dismissal—but He Qi was left in place. His rank was soon restored; he received an imperial commissioner’s seal and was called to the capital for military consultation. Zaisang Nemuku, Manji, Udahu, and others of Dashidawa’s following had slipped away to pasture while nominally in service; He Qi was ordered to investigate, proved the case, and when surrendering tribesmen pleaded for mercy the emperor refused and commanded standard martial punishment.
9
二十一年十一月，輝特台吉巴雅爾叛，掠扎哈沁五百餘戶。 定邊右副將軍兆惠令和起將索倫兵百人往按，檄吐魯番伯克莽阿里克等集闢展，而噶勒雜特宰桑哈薩克錫喇、布魯特台吉尼瑪陰應巴雅爾，詭以兵五百會。 和起望兵至，疑之。 令莽阿里克诇之，紿告曰：「我兵也！ 」逾時，尼瑪等操戈前，莽阿里克自後譟，賊眾蜂集。 和起所將兵僅百人，負重創，手刃數賊，股中槍，徒步轉戰，至夜力盡。 和起垂死，命索倫侍衛努古德、彰金布突圍出，以所戴孔雀翎為識報兆惠，遂死之。 諡武烈，追封一​​等伯，以一等子世襲，祀賢良、昭忠二祠。 二十三年，師還，獲尼瑪及其子檻送京師，命戮於和起墓前。 子和隆武，自有傳。
In the eleventh month of year 21 the Khoyit prince Bayar rebelled and seized more than five hundred Zahakin households. Deputy Pacification General Zhaohui sent He Qi with a hundred Solon to look into it and called Turfan beg Mangalik and others to muster at Bicheng; meanwhile Galzat zaisang Hasak Sira and Burut prince Nima backed Bayar in secret and falsely offered five hundred men. When He Qi saw soldiers approaching he became suspicious. He told Mangalik to reconnoiter; they lied to him, saying, “They are our men!” Before long Nima and his party came forward armed; Mangalik shouted from the rear, and the enemy massed around them. He Qi had only a hundred men; though gravely wounded he cut down several rebels himself, took a musket ball in the thigh, and fought on foot until night when he could fight no more. Near death he told Solon guards Nugude and Zhangjinbu to break out and reach Zhaohui, using the peacock plume he wore as proof; then he perished. He received the posthumous name Wulie, was raised after death to first-class baron with heirs as first-class viscount, and was honored in the Shrines of Worthies and of Loyalty. In year 23, when the army came home, Nima and his son were taken in cages to the capital and ordered put to death before He Qi’s grave. His son Helongwu has a separate biography.
10
唐喀祿，他塔喇氏，蒙古正藍旗人。 自筆帖式再遷理籓院員外郎。 乾隆十九年，賜副都統銜，命赴北路軍董理新降輝特台吉阿睦爾撒納、班珠爾等游牧地。 唐喀祿疏言：「班珠爾所屬多老稚不能耕，慮飢餒。 」上以距耕時尚遠，責其瑣屑，命撤還。 扎薩克林丕勒多爾濟初命同董理游牧，將軍別有指揮，唐喀祿疏請留。 上責其不當，左遷理籓院筆帖式。 尋复授員外郎，命送濟隆呼圖克圖自巴林赴伊犁，董理定邊右副將軍薩喇爾游牧。 复賜副都統銜，授領隊大臣，將駐防扎布堪兵千人，從定邊右副將軍哈達哈赴哈薩克，逐捕阿睦爾撒納。 賊渠固爾班和卓遁入烏梁海，唐喀祿報哈達哈督兵擒之，賜孔雀翎。 阿睦爾撒納令其徒達瓦藏布入掠，唐喀祿令索倫總管鄂博什將五百人御之，降其眾三百。 尋命屯科布多。 授理籓院侍郎、鑲藍旗蒙古副都統。
Tang Kalu of the Tatara clan was a Mongol of the Plain Blue Banner. He rose from clerk to assistant director in the Court of Colonial Affairs. In Qianlong 19 he received deputy lieutenant-general rank and was sent to the northern front to manage pastures of the newly surrendered Khoyit princes Amursana and Banzhuer. Tang Kalu wrote: “Many under Banzhuer are old or young and cannot farm; I fear they will starve.” The emperor said planting season was still distant, called the report petty, and ordered him back. Jasak Lin Pil Duoerji had been assigned with him to oversee nomadizing, but the general had other orders; Tang Kalu still asked to stay. The emperor rebuked his presumption and demoted him to clerk in the Court of Colonial Affairs. Soon he was assistant director again, told to escort the Jilong khutuktu from Baring to Ili and to manage pastures for Right Deputy Pacification General Sarlar. He received deputy lieutenant-general rank again, became column commander, and with Right Deputy Pacification General Hadaha led a thousand Zhabukan garrison troops into Kazakhstan after Amursana. Rebel chief Guerban Hezhuo fled into Urianghai country; Tang Kalu urged Hadaha to take him and was given a peacock plume. Amursana sent Davazangbu to plunder; Tang Kalu had Solon commander Eboshi meet them with five hundred men and brought three hundred of the enemy over. He was soon ordered to hold Kobdo. He became vice-president of the Court of Colonial Affairs and Mongol deputy lieutenant-general of the Bordered Blue Banner.
11
唐喀祿行按諸部，輝特降人屯紮克賽，每自相劫奪，請移屯呼倫貝爾、齊齊哈爾諸地； 喀爾喀俘獲扎哈沁、特楞古特、奇爾吉斯、烏爾罕濟蘭諸部人萬餘，請以扎哈沁人移駐卡倫內； 特楞古特、奇爾吉斯、烏爾罕濟蘭人給東三省兵丁為奴； 杜爾伯特游牧請移烏蘭固木：上並從其請。 師出西路擊哈薩克錫喇，命唐喀祿屯額爾齊斯為聲援。 阿睦爾撒納敗走，唐喀祿诇知杜爾伯特貝勒巴圖博羅特、台吉阿喇善等潛與相結； 遣兵攻之輝巴朗山，擒阿喇善等，並戮烏梁海五十餘戶，遂赴塔爾巴哈台逐捕阿睦爾撒納及哈薩克錫喇，賜御用荷包、鼻煙壺。 師至塔爾巴哈台，糧罄馬乏，唐喀祿引師退，疏言遵旨撤兵，上怒，左授藍翎侍衛，佐定邊左副將軍納穆扎爾出北路。 降人和碩齊，上擢用至散秩大臣，至是令護哈薩克來使入邊，上命納穆扎爾遣唐喀祿將二百人迎之。 阿睦爾撒納竄俄羅斯，上命唐喀祿偕和碩齊駐額爾齊斯偵禦。
On inspection Tang Kalu found Khoyit surrenderers at Zakusai constantly raiding one another and asked to move them to Hulunbuir, Qiqihar, and elsewhere; Khalkha captives from Zahakin, Terengut, Kirghiz, and Uryan Jilan numbered over ten thousand; he asked to station Zahakin people inside the border posts; Terengut, Kirghiz, and Uryan Jilan captives were to be assigned as slaves to troops in the Three Eastern Provinces; the Durbot asked to move their pastures to Ulan Gumu: the emperor approved every request. As columns on the western route struck the Kazakh Sira, Tang Kalu was told to hold the Irtysh in support. When Amursana fled in defeat, Tang Kalu learned that Durbot Beile Batubolot, Taiji Alashan, and others were secretly in league with him; he sent troops against them at Huibolang Mountain, took Alashan and others, put more than fifty Urianghai households to death, then marched to Tarbagatai after Amursana and Kazakh Sira and received an imperial purse and snuff bottle. At Tarbagatai supplies and horses gave out; Tang Kalu withdrew and wrote that he was obeying orders to pull back; the emperor was furious, demoted him to blue-plume bodyguard, and set him under Left Deputy Pacification General Namuzha’er on the northern route. The surrendered Hoshochi, raised to honorary grand minister, was now to escort a Kazakh envoy inward; the emperor told Namuzha’er to send Tang Kalu with two hundred men to receive them. Amursana slipped into Russia; the emperor ordered Tang Kalu and Hoshochi to hold the Irtysh on guard.
12
二十三年三月，土爾扈特舍棱等謀走俄羅斯，上命偕和碩齊逐捕。 四月，師次佈固圖河，獲舍棱弟勞章扎卜。 勞章扎卜詭為兄乞降，唐喀祿未敢信，和碩齊遽縱之還。 越日，舍棱詭約降，獻酒，和碩齊飲之，邀唐喀祿過其營，賊噪而起。 唐喀祿及侍衛富錫爾、穆倫保、佛爾慶額力戰，均遇害，和碩齊更衣降。 事聞，賜騎都尉世職，祀昭忠祠。 富錫爾、佛爾慶額，皆滿洲鑲黃旗人； 穆倫保，滿洲正白旗人：皆賜雲騎尉世職。
In the third month of year 23, when Torghut Shereng and others plotted escape to Russia, he was ordered to hunt them down with Hoshochi. In the fourth month the column camped on the Bugutu River and took Shereng’s brother Lao Zhangzhabu. Lao Zhangzhabu feigned surrender for his brother; Tang Kalu would not trust him, but Hoshochi immediately sent him back. Next day Shereng again feigned submission and offered wine; Hoshochi drank and invited Tang Kalu into camp; the rebels sprang up with a shout. Tang Kalu and bodyguards Fuxi’er, Mulunbao, and Fo’erqing’e fought until they were slain; Hoshochi changed clothes and submitted. On report of his death he received a hereditary commandant of cavalry and a place in the Shrine of Loyalty. Fuxi’er and Fo’erqing’e were both Bordered Yellow Banner Manchus; Mulunbao was a Plain White Banner Manchu: all received hereditary cloud-rider rank.
13
阿敏道，圖爾格期氏，蒙古鑲紅旗人，世居察哈爾。 父阿吉斯，康熙間討噶爾丹，以員外郎從軍，中道糧匱，兵苦飢。 阿吉斯言於眾曰：「我等官兵世受國恩，甘斃道路。 誓竭力前進。 」眾皆諾。 於是有昭莫多之勝。 聖祖嘉其能，予拖沙喇哈番世職。 卒。
A Min Dao of the Turgut clan was a Bordered Red Banner Mongol whose family had long lived in Chahar. His father Ajisi, in the Kangxi wars against Galdan, went as an assistant director; on the march grain ran out and the soldiers starved. Ajisi told the men: “We have eaten the dynasty’s bread for generations; we are ready to die on the march. We swear to drive forward with all our might.” Everyone agreed. That is how the victory at Jao Modo was won. Kangxi praised his spirit and gave him the hereditary rank of tuoshalaha fan. He died in office.
14
阿敏道，襲職。 雍正初，累遷二等侍衛。 九年，命將巴里呼兵百人自固爾班塞堪赴巴爾坤佐軍，又命偕侍讀學士查克丹調喀爾喀兵三千率之往。 尋复偕護軍統領費雅思哈赴烏爾輝音扎罕練兵。 乾隆元年，準噶爾乞和，撤軍，阿敏道還京，授鑲藍旗察哈爾總管。 十九年，師收烏梁海，將察哈爾兵以從，加副都統銜。 二十年，遷所獲巴爾沁人等於齊拉罕。 師定伊犁，定北將軍班第奏以阿敏道督台站。 是年，阿睦爾撒納叛，班第陷賊。 阿巴噶斯、哈丹附逆肆掠，台站中斷。 阿敏道輒督兵巡徼，使驛遞恆得相續。 會定西將軍永常自木壘退駐烏爾圖布拉克，撤阿敏道還。 上奪永常官，以策楞代將。 命阿敏道將精騎詣伊犁求班第消息。 策楞不即遣，上詰責之。 尋將千人捕阿巴噶斯、哈丹賊眾。
A Min Dao succeeded to the rank. Early in Yongzheng he rose to second-rank bodyguard. In year 9 he was told to lead a hundred Barhu soldiers from Guerban Saikan to Barkol and, with Hanlin Reader Chakedan, to raise three thousand Khalkha troops and march them there. He was soon sent with Guard Commander Feiyasaha to Wuerhui Yinzahan to train troops. In Qianlong 1 the Dzungars sued for peace, the army came home, and A Min Dao was made Chahar commander of the Bordered Blue Banner. In year 19, during the recovery of Urianghai, he led Chahar troops and received deputy lieutenant-general rank. In year 20 he resettled captured Barjin people at Qilahan. After Ili was secured, Northern Pacification General Bandi asked that A Min Dao oversee the courier stations. That same year Amursana rebelled and Bandi was trapped among the enemy. Abagasi and Hadan sided with the rebels and ravaged the routes until the stations were severed. A Min Dao patrolled again and again so the post relays never quite failed. When Western Pacification General Yongchang fell back from Mulei to Wuer Tubulake, A Min Dao was pulled back as well. The emperor removed Yongchang and put Celeng in command. A Min Dao was told to take elite horsemen to Ili for word of Bandi. Celeng delayed sending him; the emperor scolded him. He soon led a thousand men against the bands of Abagasi and Hadan.
15
二十一年，授鑲藍旗蒙古副都統。 時回酋布拉呢敦、霍集占有異志，定邊右副將軍兆惠诇知之，遣阿敏道將索倫兵百、厄魯特兵三千赴葉爾羌、喀什噶爾慰撫，且使致二渠。 至庫車，霍集占佈在焉，閉城拒我師。 阿敏道斬遊騎四十餘，圍之。 城人詭言曰：「厄魯特吾仇，慮為害。 撤還即納降。 」阿敏道遂命厄魯特兵退，僅留索倫兵百。 或慮有變，阿敏道曰：「吾招撫回眾，惟期於國有濟，何暇他慮？ 」遂入，為霍集占所執。
In year 21 he became Mongol deputy lieutenant-general of the Bordered Blue Banner. The Muslim leaders Burhanuddin and Khojaji were turning disloyal; Zhaohui learned of it and sent A Min Dao with a hundred Solon and three thousand Olot troops to Yarkand and Kashgar to reassure the people and summon the two chiefs. At Kucha, Khojaji was inside and shut the gates on the column. A Min Dao killed more than forty mounted scouts and invested the town. The townspeople lied: “The Olot are our enemies; we fear they will hurt us. Send them away and we will surrender at once.” A Min Dao ordered the Olot back, keeping only a hundred Solon. Some warned of a trap; A Min Dao said: “I am winning the Muslims over for the state’s good—why should I fret about anything else? He went in and Khojaji took him prisoner.
16
二十二年，上諭諸將檄霍集占送阿敏道還，不從，謀加害。 庫車伯克呼岱巴爾以告，阿敏道謀脫歸，不克，死之。 二等男署察哈爾營總旺扎勒及諸裨將繃科、耨金吹、扎木蘇
In year 22 the emperor told the generals to demand A Min Dao back in a proclamation to Khojaji; Khojaji refused and plotted his death. Kucha beg Hudai Baer warned him; A Min Dao tried to break out, failed, and was killed. Acting Chahar commander Wangzhale, a second-class baron, and lieutenants Bengke, Noujinchui, and Zhamusu

After the region was pacified, heroes were painted in the Hall of Purple Splendor; A Min Dao was ranked among the fifty later honorees, raised to commandant of cavalry with one cloud-rider, and placed in the Shrine of Loyalty. Wangzhale gained an added cloud-rider; Bengke and the rest received hereditary cloud-rider rank.
18
滿福，瓜爾佳氏，滿洲鑲藍旗人。 自世管佐領累擢拉林副都統。 乾隆二十二年，遷都統，駐巴里坤。 命將吉林兵千人屯吐魯番，尋授領隊大臣。 定邊將軍成袞扎佈出珠勒都斯，令滿福將三百人巡視阿勒輝至烏納哈特十三台站，搜剿嗎哈沁。 沙拉斯、嗎唬斯既降复叛，掠台站，上命滿福自阿勒輝往剿，又令巴里坤辦事大臣阿里袞帥師與會。 阿里袞未至，滿福師次肯色嶺，與賊遇，擊之，賊敗走，偽遣人乞降，且言賊渠已就縛，請除道迎。 滿福信之，行次哈喇和落，徑險林密，下臨深溝。 滿福悟為賊所紿，急麾前隊返。 賊千餘突自林中出，圍我師。 滿福厲聲督兵力戰，被創墜溝，死之。 上以滿福雖為賊所愚，愍其捐軀，命如陣亡例議卹，諡武毅，祀昭忠祠，圖形紫光閣。
Man Fu of the Guwalgiya clan was a Bordered Blue Banner Manchu. From hereditary assistant commandant he rose to deputy lieutenant-general of Lalin. In Qianlong 22 he became lieutenant-general at Barkol. He was told to garrison Turfan with a thousand Jilin troops and soon made column commander. When Pacification General Chenggezhabu moved from Zhuledusi, Man Fu patrolled thirteen stations from Alehui to Wunahate with three hundred men and hunted mahajin. Shalasi and Mahusi had submitted, rebelled again, and raided the posts; the emperor ordered Man Fu out from Alehui and Commissioner Alihun to bring a column to meet him. Aliahun had not come up when Man Fu met the enemy at Kense Ridge, routed them, and received false envoys claiming the chiefs were bound and begging him to clear the road. Man Fu trusted them; at Halaluhe the path plunged through thick woods above a deep gorge. He saw the trap and ordered the van to wheel about. Over a thousand rebels rushed from the trees and ringed the column. Man Fu roared them on, was struck, fell into the gorge, and died. The emperor said Man Fu had been duped yet pitied his death, granted battle honors, the name Wuyi, the Shrine of Loyalty, and a place in the Hall of Purple Splendor.
19
豆斌，陝西固原人。 初以馬兵入提標，累遷肅州鎮標中營守備。 雍正間，從征準噶爾。 力戰受創，賜白金四百。 遷川陝督標前營游擊。 準噶爾犯科舍圖，率兵擊走之。 乾隆初，累遷提督，自廣東移廣西。 疏言：「各營鳥槍，舊式大小參差，坐臥倚伏，不能應手； 又質薄易熱，難收實用。 請照陝西威字號纏絲槍式改制。 」下兩廣總督議行。 俄，調還固原。 又命以提督銜領湖北宜昌鎮總兵事。 尋复歷甘肅、安西提督。 命討準噶爾，帥將標兵出駐巴里坤，以輸軍馬後時，下吏議。 旋乞病，罷。
Dou Bin came from Guyuan in Shaanxi. He began as a horse soldier under the provincial commander and rose to middle-battalion garrison commander at the Suzhou standard. In Yongzheng he campaigned against the Dzungars. He fought fiercely, was wounded, and received four hundred taels of silver. He became vanguard colonel of the governor-general’s front battalion in Shaanxi-Gansu. When the Dzungars struck Kesetu he beat them back. Early in Qianlong he became provincial commander, moving from Guangdong to Guangxi. He wrote: “Camp muskets are old mixed sizes; whether sitting, lying, or kneeling, men cannot handle them cleanly; they are thin, overheat, and fail in action. Remake them on the Shaanxi coiled-silk Wei pattern.” The two governors-general were ordered to follow his plan. He was soon sent back to Guyuan. He was given provincial commander’s rank over Yichang in Hubei. He then held Gansu and Anxi in succession. Ordered against the Dzungars, he garrisoned Barkol with his standard; late delivery of army horses brought him before the magistrates. He soon pleaded illness and left office.
20
居數月，复授安西提督，仍令赴巴里坤兆惠師。 師攻霍集占於庫車，命斌將所部從，充領隊大臣，徼巡魯克察克、闢展、庫車諸地驛路。 兆惠被圍黑水，斌從副將軍富德自阿克蘇兼程赴援。 師次呼爾璊，霍集占以五千人迎戰，我師分兩翼，賊據高岡，斌率中軍火器進攻。 賊知我師馬力乏，擁眾相偪。 阿里袞解馬至，斌偕眾將夾擊，脅中創，仍力戰，賊大敗。 創甚遂卒，諡壯節，祀昭忠祠，予騎都尉兼雲騎尉。 上製詩惜之。 回部平，圖形紫光閣。 孫{澍}，襲世職，官至山東登州鎮總兵。
Months later he was Anxi commander again and sent to Zhaohui at Barkol. During the siege of Khojaji at Kucha, Bin followed as column commander and patrolled the Lukchak, Bicheng, and Kucha routes. When Zhaohui was trapped at the Black River, Bin marched with Deputy General Fude from Aksu to relieve him. At Khurun, Khojaji met them with five thousand men; the column split wings while the enemy held the heights; Bin led the center with firearms. Seeing our horses spent, the enemy closed in thick ranks. Alihun sent remounts; Bin and the other commanders struck from both flanks; though wounded in the armpit he fought on and the enemy was shattered. He died of his wounds, received the name Zhuangjie, the Shrine of Loyalty, and commandant of cavalry with cloud-rider. The emperor wrote a poem mourning him. After the Muslim regions were pacified, his likeness joined the Hall of Purple Splendor. His grandson inherited the rank and became Dengzhou garrison commander in Shandong.
21
端濟布，瓜爾佳氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人。 自前鋒累遷頭等侍衛、鑲黃旗察哈爾總管。 乾隆二十二年，上令選兵千佐定邊將軍兆惠出西路。 自硃爾圖斯赴瑪納斯，獲得木齊鄂羅斯，並所部三百人、馬駝牛羊二千餘。 扎哈沁頭人巴哈曼集叛走，端濟布偕侍衛奎瑪岱追捕，至小衛和勒津，降所部二百戶，又得掠台站賊札木布。 師捕治厄魯特頭人噶爾藏多爾濟、扎那噶爾布等，布魯古特台吉琿齊、呼爾璊台吉達瓦斬扎那噶爾布，詣端濟布軍請降。 端濟布遽引師還，琿齊、達瓦复叛去。 上懲端濟布惟事姑息，命靖逆將軍雅爾哈善按治。 師至羅克倫孟古圖嶺，獲噶爾藏多爾濟宰桑羅卜札尼瑪、得木齊敦多克，檻送巴里坤。 上聞，命貸端濟布罪。
Duan Ji Bu of the Guwalgiya clan was a Bordered Yellow Banner Manchu. From vanguard he rose to first-rank bodyguard and Chahar commander of the Bordered Yellow Banner. In Qianlong 22 the emperor chose a thousand men to reinforce Zhaohui on the western front. From Zhuer Tusi he marched to Manas, took Demuchi Eluosi, and with three hundred followers and over two thousand head of livestock. When Zahakin chief Bahamanji rebelled, Duan Ji Bu and Bodyguard Kuimadai chased him to Xiao Weihelejin, won two hundred households, and seized station-raider Zhamubu. During the suppression of Olot chiefs Gierzang Duoerji and Zanagarbu, Burut Prince Hunchi and Khurun Prince Dawa killed Zanagarbu and came to Duan Ji Bu to surrender. Duan Ji Bu withdrew at once; Hunchi and Dawa soon rebelled again. The emperor blamed his indulgence and ordered Pacification General Yarghasan to try the case. At Luokelun Menggutu Ridge they took Gierzang Duoerji’s zaisang Luobuzhanima and Demuchi Dunduoke in cages to Barkol. On the report the emperor pardoned Duan Ji Bu.
22
扎哈沁得木齊哈勒拜等謀掠台站，參贊大臣哈寧阿檄端濟布往捕，至瑪納斯，得間諜十餘。 渡河至美羅託山，賊遁，收其游牧牲畜。 師圍庫車，端濟布將吉林、厄魯特兵以從。 霍集占將三千人自賽里木來援，屯高阜。 端濟布偕侍衛順德納等奮擊，斬二千餘級。 師攻葉爾羌，霍集占築台城東北。 端濟布及侍衛諾爾本將右翼後隊攻之，賊拒戰，复斬二千餘級。 兆惠被圍於黑水，端濟布從定邊左副將軍富德赴援，十餘戰，至呼爾璊，與兆惠軍會，賜三等輕車都尉世職，授鑲紅旗滿洲副都統。
When Zahakin demuchi Halebai plotted against the posts, Consulting Commissioner Haning’a called Duan Ji Bu; at Manas he seized more than ten spies. He crossed to Meituoshan; the enemy fled and their herds were taken. At the siege of Kucha he followed with Jilin and Olot troops. Khojaji marched three thousand men from Sayram to relieve the town and camped on the heights. Duan Ji Bu and Bodyguard Shunden’a charged and cut down more than two thousand. At Yarkand, Khojaji built a fort northeast of the walls. Duan Ji Bu and Bodyguard Nuo Er Ben led the right rear; the enemy stood and another two thousand fell. When Zhaohui was trapped at the Black River, Duan Ji Bu followed Fude through more than ten fights to Khurun, linked with Zhaohui, and received a hereditary third-class commandant of light chariots and the Bordered Red deputy lieutenant-generalship.
23
師逐賊，戰於阿爾楚爾，再戰於伊西洱庫爾淖爾，端濟布將二百人截賊逃路。 偵山有賊寨，越嶺攻之，被創，賜號塔什巴圖魯。 師還，圖形紫光閣，列前五十功臣。 卒，贈都統，諡壯節，祀昭忠祠。 諭以「端濟布力戰受傷，與陣亡者無異也」。
In pursuit they fought at Aertuxur and again at Yixi’erku’er Lake; Duan Ji Bu with two hundred men blocked the retreat. Learning of a mountain stronghold, he crossed the ridge, was wounded, and received the name Tash Batulu. On the army’s return his likeness stood in the Hall of Purple Splendor among the first fifty honorees. He died; posthumously he was made lieutenant-general, named Zhuangjie, and honored in the Shrine of Loyalty. An edict read: “Duan Ji Bu fought until he was wounded—no different from the men who died in the ranks.”
24
諾爾本，吳機格忒氏，滿洲正藍旗人。 以前鋒從軍。 富德獲宰桑烏巴什，遣諾爾本送兆惠軍。 道遇賊，力戰，賜號克籌巴圖魯。 師圍庫車，霍集占來援。 諾爾本偕公袞楚克，侍衛齊凌札卜、齊努渾等擊賊右翼，賊敗走，逐之六十餘裡，至鄂根河口，斬獲甚眾； 賊逃入蘇巴什山，复偕齊努渾入山搜戮：溫詔嘉焉。 師攻葉爾羌，偕端濟布戰城東，敗賊。 師還，命在乾清門行走，賚銀帛，賜騎都尉加一云騎尉世職，圖形紫光閣。 擢頭等侍衛，從明瑞徵緬甸，擊賊被創。 尋令將兵屯騰越。 還京，擢圍場總管，加副都統銜。 卒。
Nuo Er Ben of the Wujigete clan was a Plain Blue Banner Manchu. He entered the campaign as a vanguard. When Fude took the zaisang Ubashi, he sent Nuo Er Ben to escort the prisoner to Zhaohui’s camp. Rebels blocked the road; he fought through and was given the name Kechou Batulu. During the siege of Kucha, Khojaji marched to relieve the city. Nuo Er Ben with Gonggunchuke, Bodyguards Qilingzhabu, Qinuhun, and others hit the enemy’s right wing; the rebels broke and ran; the pursuit ran more than sixty li to the mouth of the Egen River with heavy kills and captures; when the enemy took refuge in Subashi Mountain he went in again with Qinuhun to hunt them down—the emperor commended him warmly in an edict. At the assault on Yarkand he fought east of the walls with Duan Ji Bu and routed the enemy. After the campaign he was assigned to the Gate of Heavenly Purity, rewarded with silver and silks, given commandant of cavalry with an added cloud-rider inheritance, and painted in the Hall of Purple Splendor. Raised to first-rank bodyguard, he followed Mingrui against Burma, fought the enemy, and was wounded. He was soon told to hold Tengyue with a garrison command. Back in the capital he became director of the hunting grounds with deputy lieutenant-general rank. He died in office.
25
論曰：高天喜驍勇善戰，與鄂實、三格奮鬥破陣，死事為最烈。 和起等倉卒為賊陷，慷慨授命。 斌與端濟布以力戰受創，得與戰死者同其血食。 旌勇勵忠，當如是也。
In summary: Gao Tianxi was a fierce fighter; with E Shi and San Ge he broke enemy lines at the cost of his life—the most resolute of these deaths. He Qi and the rest, caught unawares by the enemy, surrendered their lives without flinching. Because Bin and Duan Ji Bu fought until they were wounded, they share the sacrificial honors of the men who fell in the ranks. Honoring bravery and stirring loyalty ought to be done just so.
