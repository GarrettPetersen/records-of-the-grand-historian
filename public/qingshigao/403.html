1
勝保，字克齋，蘇完瓜爾佳氏，滿洲鑲白旗人。 道光二十年舉人，考授順天府教授。 遷贊善，大考二等，擢侍講，累遷祭酒。 屢上疏言事，甚著風采。 歷光祿寺卿、內閣學士。
Sheng Bao, whose style was Kezhai, belonged to the Suwan Guwalgiya clan and was a Manchu of the Bordered White Banner. He passed the provincial examination in the twentieth year of the Daoguang reign and, after further examination, was appointed professor of Shuntian Prefecture. After serving as Reader-in-Waiting, he placed in the second class in the grand examination, was promoted to Lecturer-in-Waiting, and rose by stages to Director of the Imperial Academy. He submitted memorial after memorial on affairs of state and won a strong reputation for forthright conduct. He held the posts of Minister of the Court of Imperial Entertainments and Grand Secretariat Academician.
2
咸豐二年，因天變上疏論時政，言甚切直，略謂：「廣西賊勢猖獗，廣東、湖南皆可憂。 賽尚阿督師無功，請明賞罰以振紀綱。 河決不治河員之罪，刑輕盜風日熾，應明敕法以肅典常。 臣工奏摺多留中，恐滋流弊。 一切事務，硃批多而諭旨少。 市井細民，時​​或私論聖德。 」疏入，下樞臣傳問疏末兩端，令直言無隱。 覆奏曰：「硃批因事垂訓，臣工奉到遵行，他人不與聞，非若諭旨頒示天下。 近日諸臣條奏雖依議，而原奏之人不知； 交部重案，覆奏依議，外人並不知作何發落。 古者象魏懸書，俾眾屬目。 似宜通行宣示，以昭朝廷之令甲，而杜胥吏之蔽欺。 至愚賤私議，或謂皇上勵精之心不如初政，或謂勤儉之德不及先皇。 今遊觀之所，煥然一新。 釋服之後，必將有適性陶情之事，現在內府已有採辦犁園服飾以備進御者。 夫鼓樂田獵，何損聖德。 然自古帝王必先天下之憂而憂，後天下之樂而樂。 書曰：'無於水監，當於民監。 '誠不可不察也。 」文宗不懌，明諭指駁，以其意存諷諫，不之罪也。 尋因自行撤回封奏，降四品京堂。
In the second year of Xianfeng, citing omens in the heavens, he memorialized on current affairs in language that was sharply direct. In substance he wrote: 'The rebels in Guangxi are raging out of control, and Guangdong and Hunan are both deeply worrying. Sai Shang'a has led the armies without success; rewards and punishments should be made explicit so that discipline may be restored. When floods breach the dikes, the officials responsible escape punishment; because penalties are too lenient, lawlessness grows worse by the day, and the statutes ought to be enforced clearly to uphold proper order. Too many officials' memorials are held back in the palace, a practice that is likely to breed lasting abuses. On every matter there are numerous vermilion rescripts but few formal edicts promulgated to the realm. Even common people in the markets sometimes whisper privately about the emperor's virtue. ' When the memorial reached the throne, the Grand Council was instructed to question him about the two closing points and to speak with complete candor. In his follow-up memorial he wrote: 'Vermilion rescripts teach lessons on particular cases; the official who receives one obeys it, but no one else learns of it, unlike an edict that is proclaimed to the whole empire. Lately, even when a minister's proposal is approved in deliberation, the minister who first submitted it is never told what was decided; and in grave cases handed to the ministries, once the deliberated reply is filed, the public has no idea what sentence or outcome was imposed. In antiquity edicts were posted at the Elephant Watchtower so that all could see them plainly. It would be better to announce decisions openly throughout the realm, both to make the court's commands visible and to shut off the concealment and fraud of clerks and runners. As for vulgar private gossip, some say Your Majesty's zeal is no longer what it was at the start of the reign, while others say your thrift and austerity fall short of the late emperor's example. The pleasure grounds and scenic sites have lately been refurbished until they look wholly new. After the mourning period ends, there will surely be occasions for ease and diversion, and the Inner Palace is already purchasing opera costumes and finery to present to Your Majesty. Music, pageantry, and the hunt in themselves do no injury to sage virtue. Yet from antiquity true rulers have taken the empire's anxieties upon themselves before they have tasted its pleasures. The Classic says: 'Do not mirror yourself in water; mirror yourself in the people. ' This is a lesson that truly cannot be ignored. ' Emperor Wenzong was displeased and issued an edict point by point to refute him, yet because his purpose was admonitory counsel, he was not punished. Shortly afterward, because he had withdrawn a sealed memorial on his own initiative, he was demoted to a fourth-rank post in the capital bureaucracy.
3
會粵匪犯武昌，勝保疏陳辦賊方略，命馳往河南，交欽差大臣琦善差遣。 三年春，偕提督陳金綬率兵援湖北、安徽，而江寧告急。 至則城已陷，駐兵江浦。 勝保疏陳軍事稱旨，命以內閣學士會辦軍務，克浦口而賊陷揚州，偕陳金綬進剿。 擊賊鎮海寺南，破之，薄揚州城下，賜花翎。 又連破賊於天寧、廣儲門外。
When the Cantonese rebels threatened Wuchang, Sheng Bao laid out a plan for suppressing them and was ordered posthaste to Henan, where he was placed at the disposal of Imperial Commissioner Qishan. In the spring of the third year he marched with Regional Commander Chen Jinshou to reinforce Hubei and Anhui, even as Jiangning sent desperate appeals for aid. By the time he arrived the city had already fallen, and he stationed his army at Jiangpu. Sheng Bao's reports on military affairs pleased the court, and he was appointed Grand Secretariat Academician to assist in the campaign. He recovered Pukou, but the rebels then seized Yangzhou, and he advanced with Chen Jinshou to drive them back. He struck the rebels south of Zhenhai Temple, routed them, and pressed up to the walls of Yangzhou, for which he was awarded the peacock feather. He won further victories over the rebels outside Tianning Temple and beyond Guangchu Gate.
4
奉命赴安徽勦賊，而賊已入河南，渡河圍懷慶。 勝保會諸軍進擊，將軍托明阿軍其東，勝保軍其南。 時督師大學士訥爾經額遙駐臨洺關，援軍數路久頓城下，惟二軍戰較力，命勝保幫辦河北軍務。 七月，分三路進攻賊壘，大破之，懷慶圍解，加都統銜，賜黃馬褂，予霍鑾巴圖魯名號。 賊竄山西，連陷數縣，諸軍遷延，惟勝保率善祿、西凌阿兵四千尾追，一破之封門山口，再破之平陽，繞出賊前，扼韓侯嶺，尋复洪洞、平陽。 劾逗留諸將托雲保、董占元、烏勒欣泰等，罪之； 詔嘉勝保果勇有為，授欽差大臣，代訥爾經額督師，節制各路，特賜康熙朝安親王所進神雀刀，凡貽誤軍情者，副將以下立斬以聞。
Ordered to Anhui to suppress the rebels, he found they had already pushed into Henan, crossed the Yellow River, and were besieging Huaiqing. Sheng Bao coordinated the allied armies in the assault: General Tuo Ming'a held the eastern sector and Sheng Bao the southern. The supreme commander, Grand Secretary Ne'erjing'e, remained far to the rear at Linzhang Pass while relief columns on several routes had stalled for months beneath the walls. Only these two armies fought with real vigor, and Sheng Bao was ordered to assist in the Hebei campaign. In the seventh month they assaulted the rebel camps on three fronts and won a crushing victory that lifted the siege of Huaiqing. He was given the rank of Commandant-in-Chief, awarded the yellow riding jacket, and granted the title Huoluan Baturu. The rebels broke into Shanxi and overran several counties in succession while other armies dawdled. Sheng Bao alone, with Shanlu and Xiling'a and four thousand men, pursued hard: he defeated them once at Fengmen Pass and again at Pingyang, swung ahead of their line, seized Hanhou Ridge, and soon recovered Hongtong and Pingyang. He impeached the generals who had lingered—Tuo Yunbao, Dong Zhanyuan, Wulexintai, and others—and they were punished. An edict commended Sheng Bao as bold and effective, made him Imperial Commissioner in Ne'erjing'e's place as supreme commander with authority over every column, and specially granted him the 'divine sparrow' sword once presented by the Kangxi-era Prince of An. Anyone from deputy general downward who jeopardized operations was to be beheaded on the spot and reported to the throne.
5
賊既不得北竄，轉而南，由澤、潞間道入直隸境。 訥爾經額師潰於臨洺關，賊复猖獗，竄順德、趙州、正定。 勝保由井陘一路迎截，坐追賊不力，鐫二級。 命惠親王綿愉為大將軍，科爾沁郡王僧格林沁為參贊大臣，駐軍涿州，直隸軍務仍責勝保專任，而以西凌阿、善祿副之。 賊東竄，由深州、河間窺天津，勝保轉戰追賊至靜海。 賊由獨流分踞楊柳青，迭擊之，遂聚於靜海、獨流，負嵎久踞。 詔僧格林沁進軍合剿。 四年春，賊突圍走阜城，追擊，殲賊數千，陣斃悍酋吉文元。 而援賊由江北偷渡黃河擾山東，命勝保移兵往剿，臨清失守，坐褫職，戴罪自效。 尋破賊，克臨清，餘賊南走，追擊迭破之，解散甚眾。 及竄入豐縣，僅千餘人，蹙之河岸，悉數殲除。 捷聞，復職，加太子少保。 僧格林沁圍林鳳祥、李開芳於連鎮，久未下，命勝保回軍會剿。 開芳突出，分股竄山東，勝保親率輕騎追之，賊陷高唐踞守，圍之數月不能克。 迭詔詰責，褫職逮京治罪，遣戍新疆。 直隸、山東賊既平，予藍翎侍衛，充伊犁領隊大臣。
Unable to break northward, the rebels turned south and slipped into Zhili by the mountain routes between Zezhou and Lu'an. Ne'erjing'e's army was routed at Linzhang Pass, the rebels surged out again, and they swept through Shunde, Zhaozhou, and Zhengding. Sheng Bao marched by the Jingxing road to block them, but because his pursuit had lacked vigor he was demoted two ranks. Prince Mianyu of Hui was named Grand General and the Khorchin Prince Sengge Rinchen Assisting Commissioner, with headquarters at Zhuozhou. Zhili operations remained Sheng Bao's sole responsibility, with Xiling'a and Shanlu as his deputies. The rebels bolted eastward, probing toward Tianjin from Shenzhou and Hejian, while Sheng Bao fought his way in pursuit as far as Jinghai. From Duliu the rebels fanned out to seize Yangliuqing; he struck them again and again until they massed at Jinghai and Duliu, where they dug in and held their ground for a long time. An edict ordered Sengge Rinchen forward to join the combined suppression. In the spring of the fourth year the rebels broke out toward Fucheng. In the pursuit several thousand were destroyed and the fierce rebel leader Ji Wenyuan was killed on the field. Meanwhile rebel reinforcements stole across the Yellow River from the north bank to ravage Shandong, and Sheng Bao was ordered to shift his forces thither. Linqing fell, he was stripped of his post for the lapse, and was ordered to redeem himself in the field. He soon routed the rebels, retook Linqing, and as the survivors fled south he defeated them again and again and broke up large numbers of them. When the last band slipped into Feng County there were barely a thousand left; he cornered them on the riverbank and wiped them out to the last man. When news of the victory arrived he was restored to office and promoted to Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. Sengge Rinchen had long besieged Lin Fengxiang and Li Kaifang at Lianzhen without success, and Sheng Bao was ordered to bring his troops back for a combined assault. Li Kaifang broke out, split his force, and fled into Shandong. Sheng Bao personally led light cavalry in pursuit, but the rebels seized Gaotang and held it, and though he besieged the city for months he could not take it. Edict after edict rebuked him. He was stripped of office, brought to the capital for trial, and banished to Xinjiang. Once the rebels in Zhili and Shandong had been pacified, he was made a Blue Feather Bodyguard and appointed Brigade Commander at Ili.
6
六年，召還，發往安徽軍營差遣。 七年，予副都統銜，幫辦河南軍務。 捻匪方熾，勝保至，連破之方家集、烏龍集、柳溝集，克三河尖老巢。 又克河關，复霍丘，大捷於正陽關，斬捻首魏藍奇等，加頭品頂戴。 八年，平酆家集、喬家廟、趙屯諸捻巢。 粵匪大股圍固始，擊破之，殲賊萬餘，斬偽顯天侯卜占魁等，固始圍解。 詔嘉謀勇兼優，遇都統缺出題奏，复黃馬褂、巴圖魯，免其弟廉保遣戍罪。 粵匪陳玉成、李侍賢合陷廬州、鳳陽，授勝保鑲黃旗蒙古都統，命為欽差大臣，督辦安徽軍務，連破賊於定遠池河、高橋。 督軍抵三河，賊遁走。 捻首李兆受久踞江、淮間，與粵匪勾結。 及見粵匪屢挫，漸持兩端。 勝保親至清流關密招之，許歸誠後免罪授官。 兆受以其部下家屬在江寧，請緩發。 至是進攻天長，兆受內應，克之，遂獻滁州，奏授參將職，改名世忠，安置降★，自為一軍。 九年，克六安，捻首張元龍以鳳陽降，复臨淮關。 進克霍山、盱眙，破賊清水鎮，斬其酋吳加孝，遂克懷遠，而廬州、定遠久未下，賊仍蔓延。 丁母憂，奪情留軍。
In the sixth year he was recalled from exile and dispatched to the Anhui theater. In the seventh year he was given the rank of Vice Commandant-in-Chief and assigned to assist in the Henan campaign. The Nian rebels were at the height of their power when Sheng Bao arrived. He defeated them in succession at Fangjiaji, Wulongji, and Liugouji and captured their stronghold at Sanhejian. He also took Heguan, recovered Huoqiu, and won a major victory at Zhengyang Pass, where he beheaded the Nian leader Wei Lanqi and others. He was awarded the first-rank official's hat button. In the eighth year he wiped out Nian strongholds at Fengjiaji, Qiaojiamiao, Zhaotun, and other sites. A large Taiping column besieged Gushi; he broke the siege, killed more than ten thousand rebels, and beheaded the false 'Manifest Heaven Marquis' Bu Zhan'kui and others. An edict commended his strategic skill and battlefield courage, directed that he be recommended whenever a Commandant-in-Chief post fell vacant, restored his yellow riding jacket and Baturu title, and remitted the banishment sentence on his younger brother Lianbao. The Taiping generals Chen Yucheng and Li Shixian together seized Luzhou and Fengyang. Sheng Bao was made Mongol Commandant-in-Chief of the Bordered Yellow Banner, appointed Imperial Commissioner to oversee Anhui, and won successive victories at Dingyuan Chihe and Gaoqiao. He led his forces to Sanhe, and the rebels withdrew. The Nian leader Li Zhaoshou had long dominated the Yangzi–Huai region in collusion with the Taiping forces. As he watched the Taiping armies suffer repeated defeats, he began to hedge between the two sides. Sheng Bao went in person to Qingliuguan to recruit him secretly, promising pardon and an official post if he submitted. Zhaoshou asked to delay the rising because his men's families were still held in Jiangning. When the attack on Tianchang began, Zhaoshou rose from within, the city fell, and he then surrendered Chuzhou. A memorial recommended him for regimental colonel, gave him the name Shizhong, settled his surrendered followers, and allowed him to keep a separate command. In the ninth year he captured Lu'an; the Nian leader Zhang Yuanlong surrendered Fengyang; and Linhuai Pass was recovered. He went on to take Huoshan and Xuyi, routed the rebels at Qingshui Town and beheaded their leader Wu Jiaxiao, and then recovered Huaiyuan. Yet Luzhou and Dingyuan remained untaken for a long time, and rebel bands still spread across the province. When his mother died he was granted leave from mourning to remain at his post with the army.
7
十年，罷欽差大臣，命赴河南剿匪。 御史林之望論劾，降授鑲藍旗漢軍副都統。 复坐剿匪不力，降授光祿寺卿，召回京。 甫至，會英法聯軍內犯，命率八旗禁軍駐定福莊，偕僧格林沁、瑞麟進戰通州八里橋，敗績，勝保受傷，退保京師。 停戰議和，勝保收集各路潰軍及勤王師續至者共萬餘人。 疏陳京兵亟應訓練，擬議章程以進。 命兼管圓明園八旗、內務府包衣三旗，親督操練，是為改練京兵之始。
In the tenth year he was relieved as Imperial Commissioner and ordered to Henan to suppress banditry. Censor Lin Zhiwang impeached him, and he was demoted to Vice Commandant-in-Chief of the Han Bordered Blue Banner. Again, for failing to suppress the bandits effectively, he was demoted to Minister of the Court of Imperial Entertainments and recalled to Beijing. He had scarcely arrived when the Anglo-French expedition advanced inland. He was ordered to lead the Eight Banner palace guards at Dingfuzhuang and, with Sengge Rinchen and Ruilin, to meet the enemy at Baliqiao near Tongzhou. They were defeated, Sheng Bao was wounded, and the army fell back to defend Beijing. After the truce and peace negotiations, Sheng Bao gathered routed troops from every column and relief forces still arriving, more than ten thousand men in all. He memorialized that the capital garrison urgently needed training and submitted a draft set of regulations. He was ordered to take charge as well of the Yuanmingyuan Eight Banners and the three bondservant banners of the Imperial Household Department, personally supervising their drill—the beginning of the capital army's reorganization and training.
8
十一年，擢兵部侍郎，捻匪擾山東，詔分所部五千人畀僧格林沁往剿。 尋命勝保赴直、東交界治防，連克丘縣、館陶、冠縣、莘縣，破賊老巢。 招降捻首宋景詩，率眾隨軍。 复朝城、觀城，命督辦河南、安徽剿匪事宜。 河北肅清，予優敘。
In the eleventh year he was promoted to Vice Minister of War. When Nian rebels ravaged Shandong, an edict detached five thousand of his men and placed them under Sengge Rinchen for the campaign there. He was soon ordered to the Zhili–Shandong border to organize defenses, took Qiu County, Guantao, Guan County, and Shen County in succession, and destroyed the rebels' main stronghold. He induced the Nian leader Song Jingshi to surrender, and Song marched with his followers in Sheng Bao's train. He recovered Chaocheng and Guancheng and was ordered to oversee bandit suppression in Henan and Anhui. With Hebei pacified, he received special commendation.
9
是年七月，文宗崩於行在，穆宗嗣位，肅順、載垣、端華等輔政專擅。 勝保昌言將入清君側，肅順等頗忌憚之。 洎回鑾，上疏曰：「政柄操之自上，非臣下所得專。 皇上沖齡嗣位，輔政得人，方足以資治理。 怡親王載垣、鄭親王端華等非不宣力有年，赫赫師尹，民具爾瞻； 今竟攬君國大權，以臣僕而代綸音，挾至尊而令天下，實無以副寄託之望，而饜四海之心。 該王等以承寫硃諭為辭，居之不疑。 不知皇上纘承大統，天與人歸，原不以硃諭之有無為重。 至贊襄政務，當以親親尊賢為斷，不當專以承寫為憑。 先皇帝彌留之際，近支親王多不在側。 仰窺顧命苦衷，所以未留親筆硃諭者，未必非以輔政之難得其人，待皇上自擇而任之，以成未竟之志也。 嗣聖既未親政，皇太后又不臨朝，是政柄盡付之該王等數人。 其託諸掣籤簡放，鈐用符信圖章，以此取信於人，無如人皆不信，民碞可畏，天下難欺。 近如御史董元醇條陳，極有關係，應準應駁，惟當斷自聖裁，廣集廷議，以定行止。 乃徑行擬旨駁斥，已開矯竊之端，大失臣民之望。 道路之人皆曰：'此非吾君之言也，非母后聖母之意也。 '一切發號施令，真偽難分。 眾情洶洶，咸懷不服。 夫天下者，宣宗成皇帝之天下，傳之文宗顯皇帝以付之我皇上者也。 昔我文皇后雖無垂簾之明文，而有聽政之實用。 為今之計，非皇太后親理萬幾，召對群臣，無以通下情而正國體； 非特簡近支親王佐理庶政，盡心匡弼，無以振紀綱而順人心。 惟有籥懇皇上俯察芻蕘，即奉皇太后權宜聽政，而於近支親王擇賢而任，仍秉命而行，以成郅治。 」奏上，會大學士周祖培等亦以為言，下廷議，從之。 肅順等並伏法。 尋授鑲黃旗滿洲都統兼正藍旗護軍統領。
In the seventh month of that year Emperor Wenzong died at the traveling palace and Emperor Muzong succeeded to the throne, while Sushun, Zaiyuan, Duanhua, and others seized exclusive control of the regency. Sheng Bao openly declared that he would march on the capital to purge the emperor's inner circle, and Sushun and his allies were deeply wary of him. When the court returned to Beijing he memorialized: 'Political authority belongs to the throne above; it is not something subjects may seize for themselves. Your Majesty succeeded while still a child; only with the right regents can the realm be governed well. Prince Yi Zaiyuan, Prince Zheng Duanhua, and the others have served diligently for years and stand before the people as eminent ministers; yet they have now seized the great power of state, letting servants speak in place of the Son of Heaven and wielding the young emperor to command the empire. This cannot fulfill the late emperor's trust or satisfy the hearts of the realm. These princes cite their commission to copy the late emperor's vermilion rescript and hold power without hesitation. They forget that Your Majesty inherited the Mandate—granted by Heaven and affirmed by the people—and that the throne never depended on whether a vermilion rescript existed. As for assisting in government, the standard should be honoring kin and respecting the worthy, not merely having been named to copy a rescript. At the late emperor's deathbed most of the close imperial princes were not present. One may infer from the anguish of his final charge that he left no rescript in his own hand precisely because worthy regents are hard to find—leaving Your Majesty to choose and appoint them yourself and so fulfill his unfinished will. The young emperor has not yet assumed personal rule, and the empress dowager does not hold court, so political power has passed entirely to these few princes. They rely on drawing lots for appointments and stamping documents with seals and cipher marks to win trust, yet no one believes them. Popular discontent is to be feared; the realm cannot be deceived. Take the recent submission of Censor Dong Yuanchun: it was a matter of the highest importance, to be approved or rejected only by Your Majesty's own judgment after full deliberation in court. Yet they drafted and issued a rejection on their own authority, opening the door to usurpation and deeply disappointing officials and commoners alike. People in the streets say: 'These are not our emperor's words, nor the empress dowager's will. ' With every order and command that goes forth, no one can tell what is genuine and what is not. Public sentiment is in uproar, and everyone feels aggrieved. The empire belongs to Emperor Xuanzong, passed down through Emperor Wenzong to our present sovereign. In former times Empress Wen, though never formally said to 'hold the curtain behind the throne,' in practice governed from behind it. For the present, unless the empress dowager personally handles state affairs and receives the ministers in audience, there is no way to learn what the people feel or to set the polity right; and unless close imperial princes are chosen to assist in government and give loyal counsel, there is no way to restore discipline or win the people's hearts. I beg Your Majesty to consider this humble advice: let the empress dowager provisionally hear government at once, choose worthy princes of the close line to assist, and let them still act only on your command, so that perfect order may be achieved. ' When the memorial was submitted, Grand Secretary Zhou Zupei and others made the same argument. The court deliberated and adopted the proposal. Sushun and his associates were all executed. He was soon appointed Manchu Commandant-in-Chief of the Bordered Yellow Banner and concurrently commander of the Plain Blue Banner guards.
10
時捻匪肆擾皖、豫間，以張洛行為最強。 苗沛霖自踞壽州，逼走巡撫翁同書後，佯稱就撫，陰與粵匪陳玉成勾結。 署巡撫賈臻被圍於潁州，久不解。 楚軍已克安慶，陳玉成退踞廬州。 朝廷本意安徽軍事屬之李續宜，用為巡撫。 沛霖舊隸勝保部下，心憚楚軍，揚言勝保來始薙發。 賈臻以聞，詔促勝保援潁州。 同治元年，遣軍先進，為賊所挫。 三月，勝保至，擊破賊壘，圍乃解，加兵部尚書銜。 多隆阿等克廬州，陳玉成遁走，沛霖誘擒之，獻於勝保軍。 詔於軍前誅玉成，赦沛霖罪，許立功後復官。 沛霖擁眾號十萬，所屬二百餘圩。 與張洛行勢敵相仇，自請剿之，心實叵測。 詔詢曾國籓、官文、李續宜、袁甲三等，皆主剿。 獨勝保一意主撫，上疏言事權不一，身為客軍，地方掣肘，請以安徽、河南兩巡撫幫辦軍務，允之。 迭詔訓飭，褒其才略，戒其驕愎。 卒不悛，力言沛霖無他，而為李續宜所疑，恐激變。 續宜奉旨進駐潁州，亦迄不至。
At that time Nian rebels ravaged the Anhui–Henan borderlands, and Zhang Luoxing was the strongest of their leaders. Miao Peilin had seized Shouzhou on his own authority and, after forcing out Governor Weng Tongshu, pretended to submit while secretly colluding with the Taiping general Chen Yucheng. Acting Governor Jia Zhen was besieged at Yingzhou, and the siege dragged on without relief. The Hunan army had already captured Anqing, and Chen Yucheng had fallen back to Luzhou. The court had intended that Anhui operations be placed under Li Xuyi, who was appointed governor. Peilin had once served under Sheng Bao. He feared the Hunan army and declared that he would shave his head and submit only when Sheng Bao arrived. Jia Zhen reported the situation, and an edict ordered Sheng Bao to hurry to the relief of Yingzhou. In the first year of Tongzhi he sent an advance force ahead, and it was beaten by the rebels. In the third month Sheng Bao arrived, stormed the rebel camps, and lifted the siege. He was given the rank of Minister of War. Duolong'a and others captured Luzhou. Chen Yucheng fled, but Peilin lured him into capture and delivered him to Sheng Bao's camp. An edict ordered Chen Yucheng executed before the army, pardoned Peilin, and promised restoration of his rank if he rendered meritorious service. Peilin commanded a force said to number one hundred thousand men across more than two hundred fortified villages. He was Zhang Luoxing's equal in strength and his bitter enemy, and he volunteered to suppress him—yet his true intentions were impossible to read. The court consulted Zeng Guofan, Guanwen, Li Xuyi, Yuan Jiasan, and others; all favored suppressing Peilin by force. Sheng Bao alone insisted on pacification. He memorialized that command authority was fragmented, that as an outside army he was hamstrung by local officials, and asked that the governors of Anhui and Henan be ordered to assist him; the request was granted. Edict after edict admonished him, praising his ability while warning him against arrogance and obstinacy. He never mended his ways, insisting that Peilin meant no harm, while Li Xuyi distrusted Peilin and feared provoking an uprising. Li Xuyi was ordered to advance and take post at Yingzhou, but he never came.
11
會陝西回亂熾，多隆阿援軍阻隔不能遽達。 遂授勝保欽差大臣，督辦陝西軍務。 八月，轉戰至西安，解其圍。 降捻宋景詩中途率眾叛走。 東路同州、朝邑猶為回踞，詔責勝保專剿東路，命多隆阿進軍分任西路。 勝保力不能制賊，而忌多隆阿，擅調苗沛霖率兵赴陝，嚴詔斥阻，不聽。 命僧格林沁大軍監製，乃止。 於是中外交章劾勝保驕縱貪婬，冒餉納賄，擁兵縱寇，欺罔貽誤，下僧格林沁及山西巡撫英桂、西安副都統德興阿察實奏上，密詔多隆阿率師至陝，傳旨宣布勝保罪狀，褫職逮京，交刑部治罪，籍其家。
Meanwhile the Muslim uprising in Shaanxi grew fierce, and Duolong'a's relief column was blocked and could not reach the province quickly. Sheng Bao was then appointed Imperial Commissioner to oversee military affairs in Shaanxi. In the eighth month he fought his way to Xi'an and lifted the siege. The surrendered Nian leader Song Jingshi rebelled en route and fled with his followers. On the eastern front Tongzhou and Chaoyi were still in rebel hands. An edict made Sheng Bao responsible for the eastern campaign and ordered Duolong'a to take the western front. Sheng Bao could not master the rebels yet resented Duolong'a. On his own authority he ordered Miao Peilin to march into Shaanxi; stern edicts forbade it, but he refused to obey. Only when Sengge Rinchen's main force was ordered to restrain him did he desist. Memorials then poured in from every quarter impeaching Sheng Bao for arrogance, debauchery, embezzlement, bribery, keeping troops idle while rebels roamed free, and deceiving the throne. Sengge Rinchen, Shanxi Governor Yinggui, and Xi'an Vice Commandant De Xing'a were ordered to investigate and report. A secret edict sent Duolong'a into Shaanxi to proclaim Sheng Bao's crimes, strip him of office, bring him to the capital for trial by the Ministry of Punishments, and confiscate his property.
12
二年，王大臣會鞫，勝保僅自承攜妾隨營，呈訴參劾諸人誣告之罪。 詔斥其貪污欺罔，天下共知，苗沛霖已戕官踞城，宋景詩反覆背叛，皆其養筴貽患，不得謂無挾制朝廷之意； 念其戰功足錄，從寬賜自盡，並逮其從官論罪有差。 當其被逮也，降捻李世忠已擢至提督，請黜己官為之贖罪，不許。 御史吳台壽疏言勝保有克敵禦侮之功，無失地喪師之罪，請從末減。 台壽兄台朗在勝保軍中，詔斥黨附，褫台壽職。
In the second year a joint tribunal of princes and ministers tried him. Sheng Bao admitted only to bringing a concubine on campaign and pleaded that his accusers had slandered him. An edict rebuked him: his corruption and deceit were known to all. Miao Peilin had murdered officials and seized cities; Song Jingshi had betrayed the court again and again—troubles he himself had nurtured. He could not be said free of intent to coerce the throne; yet in consideration of his past victories he was granted the lenient sentence of suicide, and his subordinate officers were arrested and punished according to their degrees of guilt. At the time of his arrest the surrendered Nian leader Li Shizhong, by then a regional commander, asked to give up his own rank to redeem him; the request was denied. Censor Wu Taishou memorialized that Sheng Bao had defeated enemies and defended the realm, had never lost territory or squandered an army, and asked that the sentence be reduced. Taishou's elder brother Tailang had served in Sheng Bao's army. An edict denounced the memorial as partisan favoritism and stripped Taishou of his post.
13
托明阿，棟鄂氏，滿洲正紅旗人。 由侍衛擢護軍參領，出為山東兗州營游擊。 從巡撫武隆阿徵回疆，以功賜花翎。 累擢曹州鎮總兵，調四川松潘、重慶二鎮。 道光二十四年，擢四川提督，以病去職。 二十七年，起授烏魯木齊提督。 調陝西，擢綏遠城將軍，整飭戎政，勤於訓練。
Tuo Ming'a, of the Dong'e clan, was a Manchu of the Plain Red Banner. Promoted from the imperial bodyguard to a guards company command, he was then posted as brigade commander of the Yanzhou garrison in Shandong. He campaigned in the Western Regions under Governor Wulong'a and was awarded the peacock feather for his service. He rose by stages to regional commander of the Caozhou garrison and was later transferred to command the Songpan and Chongqing garrisons in Sichuan. In the twenty-fourth year of Daoguang he was made provincial military commander of Sichuan, then resigned on grounds of illness. In the twenty-seventh year he was recalled to service as provincial military commander of Urumqi. Transferred to Shaanxi, he was promoted to General of Suiyuan and set about restoring military discipline with tireless attention to drill.
14
咸豐三年，粵匪林鳳祥等陷揚州，逼淮、徐，命率所部赴江南、山東交界防堵，進屯清江浦。 賊竄滁州，托明阿赴援，與週天爵會剿。 遂追賊至河南，迭戰於睢州、杞縣、陳留、中牟，進克汜水，殲賊千餘，被珍賚，命襄辦軍務。 賊竄河北，圍懷慶，乃渡河會諸軍分路進攻，迭有斬獲。 賊築土城樹木柵以拒，合攻破之，擒斬數千。 賊始遁，懷慶圍解。 論功，賜黃馬褂，予西林巴圖魯名號。 追賊山西，詔以勝保督師，命托明阿襄辦。 賊竄入直隸境，坐降五級留任，尋以傷劇解職回旗。 四年春，病痊，命赴直隸，仍幫辦僧格林沁、勝保軍務。 賊方踞阜城，堅守不出，諸軍圍之。 托明阿屯東北，賊來撲，輒擊退，突由東南隅竄出，踞連鎮，夾運河。 托明阿與都統西凌阿軍東西兩岸，圍復合。
In the third year of Xianfeng the Taiping generals Lin Fengxiang and others seized Yangzhou and threatened the Huai and Xu regions. Tuo Ming'a was ordered to the Jiangnan–Shandong border with his troops and encamped at Qingjiangpu. When the rebels moved on Chuzhou, Tuo Ming'a marched to its relief and joined Zhou Tianjue in a combined campaign. He pursued the rebels into Henan and fought successive engagements at Suizhou, Qi County, Chenliu, and Zhongmou before taking Sishui and killing more than a thousand rebels. He received imperial rewards and was ordered to assist in the campaign. The rebels broke into Hebei and besieged Huaiqing. He crossed the river, joined the allied armies in a multi-pronged assault, and won repeated victories. The rebels threw up earthen walls and wooden palisades to hold them off; the allied forces stormed the works and killed or captured several thousand. The rebels finally fled, and the siege of Huaiqing was lifted. For his merits he was awarded the yellow riding jacket and the title Xilin Baturu. As the pursuit continued into Shanxi, an edict made Sheng Bao supreme commander and ordered Tuo Ming'a to assist him. When the rebels broke into Zhili he was demoted five ranks but kept his post; soon afterward, his wounds being grave, he was relieved and sent back to his banner. In the spring of the fourth year, recovered from illness, he was ordered to Zhili to assist again in the campaigns of Sengge Rinchen and Sheng Bao. The rebels were holding Fucheng, refusing to come out, while the allied armies tightened the siege around them. Tuo Ming'a held the northeast sector and repelled every rebel assault, but the enemy suddenly broke out from the southeast, seized Lianzhen, and straddled the Grand Canal. Tuo Ming'a and Commandant-in-Chief Xiling'a took the eastern and western banks of the canal, and the encirclement was restored.
15
會琦善督師揚州，卒於軍，命托明阿為欽差大臣，馳往代之，授江寧將軍。 自賊踞江寧，鎮江、揚州皆陷，南北梗阻，大軍分兩路，向榮軍江南，琦善軍江北。 江北軍攻揚州不能克，賊棄城去，聚於瓜洲，與南岸鎮江相犄角。 江寧賊時乘鉅簰順流而下，陸師不能扼，水師力薄，亦不能制賊。 上游浦口最當衝要，賊於沙洲結營，時圖進竄。 恃總兵武慶一軍及道員溫紹原六合練勇為屏蔽，亦不能進取。 托明阿至軍，令副將鞠殿華毀運河鐵钅巢，提督陳金綬循東岸進攻，小有斬獲。 又截擊賊簰，斃偽丞相黃起茅。 自督舟師渡江，略北固山、金山而還。 五年，瓜洲、鎮江賊合犯儀徵，令副都統德興阿、總兵李志和擊退。 又進軍三汊河，誘賊敗之。 托明阿見僧格林沁於連鎮、馮官屯皆以圍牆制賊，議仿其法，於瓜洲築長圍以困之。 然瓜洲濱大江，江路不斷，且地勢袤長不易守，實無足恃。 圍成，屢偕陳金綬進攻，無大勝利。 江寧賊踞江浦石磯橋，武慶​​、西昌阿等馳擊，克之。 巡撫吉爾杭阿督師攻鎮江甚急，於是議南北同時進剿。
Qishan, who had been commanding at Yangzhou, died in camp. Tuo Ming'a was appointed Imperial Commissioner, ordered posthaste to replace him, and made General of Jiangning. Ever since the rebels seized Jiangning, Zhenjiang and Yangzhou had fallen and communications between north and south were cut. The main forces were divided: Xiang Rong's army in Jiangnan and Qishan's in Jiangbei. The Jiangbei army failed to retake Yangzhou. The rebels abandoned the city, massed at Guazhou, and with Zhenjiang on the opposite bank formed a mutually supporting pair of strongholds. Rebel forces from Jiangning often swept downstream on great rafts. The land army could not stop them, and the navy, being too weak, could not bring them to heel either. Farther upstream, Pukou was the most critical point. The rebels camped on the sandbars and repeatedly tried to break through. Even with Regional Commander Wu Qing's force and Intendant Wen Shaoyuan's Liuhe militia as a screen, government troops still could not advance. When Tuo Ming'a took command he ordered Deputy General Ju Dianhua to clear the iron barriers from the canal and had Regional Commander Chen Jinshou attack along the eastern bank, winning minor successes. He also intercepted rebel rafts and killed the false chancellor Huang Qimao. He personally led the fleet across the Yangzi, raided Beigu Hill and Jinshan, and withdrew. In the fifth year rebels from Guazhou and Zhenjiang jointly attacked Yizheng. He ordered Vice Commandant De Xing'a and Regional Commander Li Zhihe to drive them back. He advanced again to Sancha River, lured the rebels into an ambush, and defeated them. Seeing that Sengge Rinchen had contained rebels at Lianzhen and Fengguantun with encircling walls, Tuo Ming'a proposed the same method at Guazhou and began building a long cordon to starve the enemy out. Yet Guazhou lay on the great river, the water route remained open, and the ground was too extended to defend easily—the plan was fundamentally unsound. When the cordon was finished he attacked again and again with Chen Jinshou but won no decisive victory. Rebels from Jiangning held Shiji Bridge at Jiangpu until Wu Qing, Xichang'a, and others stormed it and recovered the position. Governor Jirhang'a was pressing the assault on Zhenjiang with great urgency, and the commanders resolved on a coordinated north–south offensive.
16
六年二月，江寧賊大舉援鎮江，未得逞。 渡江與瓜洲賊合，突越土圍，四出縱火。 官軍戰土橋竟日，傷亡多​​。 托明阿營壘被毀，退三汊河，又退秦家橋，幾不能軍。 陳金綬、雷以諴等亦退走，揚州遂陷。 諸營潰散，惟德興阿猶整軍力戰。 向榮遣鄧紹良渡江來援。 越十日，复揚州，而江浦亦為賊踞。 詔褫托明阿職，留營效力，尋以病歸。
In the second month of the sixth year a large rebel force from Jiangning marched to relieve Zhenjiang but failed. They crossed the river, united with the Guazhou garrison, broke through the earthen cordon, and set fires on every side. Government troops fought at Tuqiao all day and suffered heavy casualties. Tuo Ming'a's camps were overrun. He fell back to Sancha River, then to Qinjia Bridge, and his army was nearly destroyed. Chen Jinshou, Lei Yixian, and others also fled, and Yangzhou fell. The camps collapsed in rout; only De Xing'a kept his force intact and fought on. Xiang Rong sent Deng Shaoliang across the river with reinforcements. Ten days later Yangzhou was recovered, but Jiangpu had in turn fallen to the rebels. An edict stripped Tuo Ming'a of rank but allowed him to remain with the army. He soon went home on grounds of illness.

八年，予頭等侍衛，率兵駐楊村防英兵內犯，授直隸提督，遷西安將軍。 同治元年，以傷病乞休，四年，卒。
In the eighth year he was made a first-class bodyguard, stationed troops at Yangcun to guard against a British advance inland, was appointed provincial military commander of Zhili, and later transferred to General of Xi'an. In the first year of Tongzhi he retired on grounds of wounds and illness, and died in the fourth year.
18
陳金綬，四川岳池人。 從剿教匪，授把總，積功至都司。 道光初，從征回疆，破賊於佳噶賴，功最，賜號逸勇巴圖魯，擢留壩營游擊。 十三年，直隸總督琦善調司教練，累擢督標中軍副將，琦善倚之，以堪勝總兵薦，擢天津鎮。
Chen Jinshou was a native of Yuechi in Sichuan. He fought in the campaigns against sectarian rebels, was made platoon commander, and rose by accumulated merit to battalion commander. Early in the Daoguang reign he campaigned in the Western Regions, won the decisive victory at Jiagalai, was granted the title Yiyong Baturu, and was promoted to brigade commander of the Liuba garrison. In the thirteenth year Zhili Governor-General Qishan put him in charge of drill. He rose to deputy commander of the governor-general's central brigade, and Qishan, relying on him heavily, recommended him for regional command and had him promoted to the Tianjin garrison.
19
二十二年，擢直隸提督。 及琦善督師剿粵匪，率所部三千以從。 詔金綬為楊遇春舊部，命幫辦軍務，率兵先發。 又以其不諳文字，命勝保偕行。 咸豐三年春，趣援江寧，偕勝保克浦口，詔責專防江北。 揚州陷，由六合、儀徵趨援。 琦善大軍始至，合攻揚州。 琦善軍其北，金綬、勝保軍其西，累戰皆捷。 賊堅守數月不下，而瓜洲一路通江，兵少不能合圍。 賊分犯浦口踞之，進陷滁州，遂北竄。 勝保率兵赴安徽應援，迭詔以孤城久抗，責攻益急。 總兵雙來奮進，緣梯登城，金綬策應。 兵不聽命，雙來以無援負創退，尋歿於軍，自此不敢力攻，而賊時由瓜洲窺伺來援，屢卻之。 十一月，賊陷儀徵，兩路同時來犯。 參將馮景尼守楊子橋，先潰，諸軍多失利。 城賊擁輜重突出趨瓜洲，琦善、金綬不能截擊，並坐褫職留軍。 揚州雖復，賊久踞瓜洲。 四年春，琦善卒於軍，金綬暫署關防。 托明阿至，偕金綬進攻瓜洲，毀賊砲台。 尋攻新橋賊壘，金綬之侄能義及游擊海明殞於陣。
In the twenty-second year he was made provincial military commander of Zhili. When Qishan took command against the Taiping rebels, Chen Jinshou marched with three thousand of his own men. An edict noted that Jinshou had served under Yang Yuchun, ordered him to assist in the campaign, and directed him to march ahead with the vanguard. Because he was not literate in written Chinese, Sheng Bao was ordered to accompany him. In the spring of the third year of Xianfeng he was ordered to hurry to the relief of Jiangning. With Sheng Bao he recovered Pukou, and an edict made him solely responsible for the defense of Jiangbei. When Yangzhou fell he marched to its relief by way of Liuhe and Yizheng. Qishan's main force had only just arrived when they launched a combined assault on Yangzhou. Qishan held the northern sector and Jinshou and Sheng Bao the western, and they won victory after victory. The rebels held out for months, but the Guazhou line remained open to the river, and government forces were too few to close the ring completely. The rebels split their force, seized Pukou, pushed on to take Chuzhou, and then bolted northward. Sheng Bao marched to Anhui with reinforcements, while edict after edict rebuked the commanders for letting a single city resist so long and ordered fiercer assaults. Regional Commander Shuang Lai led a bold assault up scaling ladders while Jinshou coordinated the supporting attack. The troops refused to follow orders. Shuang Lai, unsupported and wounded, fell back and soon died in camp. Thereafter the army dared not press a full assault, though rebel relief columns probing from Guazhou were turned back again and again. In the eleventh month the rebels captured Yizheng and attacked on two fronts at once. Regimental Commander Feng Jingni, defending Yangzi Bridge, broke first, and most of the government columns were beaten. The city's defenders broke out with their baggage train toward Guazhou. Qishan and Jinshou failed to cut them off and were both stripped of rank but left with the army. Yangzhou was recovered, but the rebels continued to hold Guazhou for a long time. In the spring of the fourth year Qishan died in camp, and Jinshou temporarily assumed command. When Tuo Ming'a arrived he joined Jinshou in an assault on Guazhou and destroyed the rebel batteries. They soon assaulted the rebel camp at Xinqiao, where Jinshou's nephew Nengyi and Brigade Commander Haiming were killed in action.
20
江北軍多疲玩，金綬年老，文宗以其謹願，姑容之。 閱時輒報小捷，屢以虛飾被斥。 至托明阿兵潰土橋，金綬及雷以諴駐萬福橋，望風而走。 事後飾辭自辨，又奏隨同克復揚州，為德興阿論劾，應治罪，金綬已先歿於軍矣。
The Jiangbei army had grown slack. Jinshou was elderly, and Emperor Wenzong, valuing his dutiful character, for the time being indulged him. He reported minor victories from time to time and was repeatedly rebuked for inflating his reports. When Tuo Ming'a's army was routed at Tuqiao, Jinshou and Lei Yixian, posted at Wanfu Bridge, fled at the first sign of defeat. Afterward he dressed up his account in self-defense and claimed to have helped recover Yangzhou. De Xing'a impeached him as deserving punishment, but Jinshou had already died in camp.
21
德興阿，喬佳氏，滿洲正黃旗人，黑龍江駐防。 道光末，由駐京前鋒授藍翎侍衛、乾清門行走，累擢頭等侍衛。 以善​​騎射受文宗知，曾手擒奔馬，賜黃馬褂。
De Xing'a, of the Qiaojia clan, was a Manchu of the Plain Yellow Banner from the Heilongjiang garrison. Late in the Daoguang reign he rose from a capital-stationed vanguard to Blue Feather Bodyguard and attendant at the Gate of Heavenly Purity, and by stages to first-class bodyguard. His skill in riding and archery won Emperor Wenzong's favor. He once seized a runaway horse with his bare hands and was awarded the yellow riding jacket.
22
咸豐二年，命率黑龍江兵赴琦善軍。 三年，從攻揚州，屯蔣家廟，為通儀徵要路，城賊竄出，奮擊敗之。 瓜洲援賊進踞虹橋，與守備毛三元夾擊於三汊河。 德興阿單騎陷陣，射殪其酋，大破賊，加副都統銜。 別賊破儀徵，分兩路來犯。 德興阿急趨東石人頭，毀賊浮橋。 而瓜洲賊又進築土城於河西，偪三汊河，與儀徵賊相犄角。 德興阿偕總兵瞿騰龍渡河毀賊營，賊乃不能西進。 是年冬，賊棄揚州城退踞瓜洲，官軍進复儀徵，授正白旗漢軍副都統。 四年，偕瞿騰龍進攻瓜洲，騰龍深入，為賊所襲，殞於陣。 德興阿率勁騎馳援，賊敗走，軍賴以全，賜號博奇巴圖魯。 尋复敗賊三汊河，賊埋地雷誘官軍，德興阿偵知，揮軍繞路而前，賊伏壘不出，遂分軍兩路夾攻，斬馘過當，奪獲大砲地雷。 捷聞，晉御前侍衛。 五年，迭攻瓜洲賊壘，又截擊竄賊於虹橋、八江口等處，皆獲勝。 六年，托明阿兵敗於土橋，揚州复陷，諸軍渙散，獨德興阿軍未動。 詔黜托明阿，以德興阿為欽差大臣，加都統銜。 敗賊薛家樓，進規郡城。 賊萬人迎敵，德興阿身先士卒，斬賊酋一，諸軍乘之，賊大潰，乘勝复揚州。 同時江浦、浦口並為賊踞，令總兵武慶攻克之。
In the second year of Xianfeng he was ordered to bring Heilongjiang troops to Qishan's command. In the third year he joined the assault on Yangzhou and encamped at Jiangjiamiao, the vital route to Yizheng, where he routed rebels breaking out of the city. Rebel reinforcements from Guazhou seized Hongqiao. He and Garrison Commander Mao Sanyuan caught them in a pincer at Sancha River. De Xing'a charged alone into the enemy ranks, shot down their leader, and won a crushing victory. He was given the rank of Vice Commandant-in-Chief. Another rebel column captured Yizheng and attacked on two fronts. De Xing'a rushed to East Shirentou and destroyed the rebels' pontoon bridge. Meanwhile the Guazhou garrison threw up earthen walls on the west bank, pressed toward Sancha River, and linked with the Yizheng force in a mutually supporting position. De Xing'a and Regional Commander Qu Tenglong crossed the river, destroyed the rebel camps, and blocked any further westward advance. That winter the rebels abandoned Yangzhou and fell back on Guazhou. Government troops recovered Yizheng, and he was made Vice Commandant-in-Chief of the Han Plain White Banner. In the fourth year he advanced on Guazhou with Qu Tenglong, who pushed too far, was ambushed, and killed in action. De Xing'a led crack cavalry to the rescue, routed the rebels, and saved the army from destruction. He was granted the title Boqi Baturu. He soon won another victory at Sancha River. The rebels had laid land mines to trap government troops, but De Xing'a discovered the ruse, swung his force around by a flank march, and when the enemy refused to leave their stockade, attacked from two sides. The slaughter was immense, and he captured heavy guns and the rebel mine stores. When news of the victory arrived he was promoted to bodyguard before the throne. In the fifth year he stormed the Guazhou rebel camps again and again and intercepted fleeing rebels at Hongqiao, Bakou, and other points, winning every engagement. In the sixth year Tuo Ming'a's army was routed at Tuqiao and Yangzhou fell again. The armies scattered in rout, but De Xing'a's force alone held its ground. An edict dismissed Tuo Ming'a and made De Xing'a Imperial Commissioner with the rank of Commandant-in-Chief. He defeated the rebels at Xuejialou and advanced to threaten the prefectural seat. Ten thousand rebels came out to meet them. De Xing'a led the charge, killed a rebel chieftain, and the whole army pressed the advantage until the enemy broke completely. Yangzhou was recovered in the pursuit. Jiangpu and Pukou had also fallen to the rebels, and he ordered Regional Commander Wu Qing to recover them.
23
德興阿戰功素為江北諸軍冠，惟不曉漢文，命少詹事翁同書為幫辦。 添調新兵，軍聲稍振，進規瓜洲。 七年，參將富明阿破賊於土橋、四里鋪，水師又擊沉賊船，斬偽將軍陳磊。 是年夏，合水陸諸軍進攻，毀賊艦及砲台。 德興阿親督戰，更番進逼，至十一月，大破之，复瓜洲。 賊負嵎歷四年，至是始克。 詔嘉調度有方，賜雙眼花翎，予騎都尉世職。 乘勝逼金山，剿平新河口、龍王廟等處餘匪。 江南軍亦同日克鎮江，專力進攻江寧。 八年春，德興阿進軍江浦，獲勝。 江寧賊勢日蹙，悍黨陳玉成等由安徽糾眾來援，德興阿兵敗於浦口，退保六合，褫雙眼花翎，革職留任。 賊連陷江浦、天長、儀徵，德興阿不能救，揚州亦陷，褫世職。 尋張國樑率兵渡江复揚州，而德興阿擁兵邵伯，觀望不前，嚴旨斥責。 溫紹原守六合歷數年，為江北屏蔽，至是亦以援絕被陷，紹原死之。 翌日而張國樑馳至，已無及。 國樑以江寧軍事急，移軍渡江，詔責德興阿規复六合，軍已不振，迄無功。
De Xing'a's battlefield record had long been the best in the Jiangbei armies, but he could not read Chinese. Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent Weng Tongshu was appointed to assist him. Fresh troops were brought in, morale improved somewhat, and he advanced against Guazhou. In the seventh year Regimental Commander Fu Ming'a defeated the rebels at Tuqiao and Silipu, while the navy sank rebel vessels and killed the false general Chen Lei. That summer the combined land and naval forces attacked together and destroyed rebel ships and batteries. De Xing'a personally directed the fighting, pressing forward in relays until the eleventh month, when he won a decisive victory and recovered Guazhou. The rebels had dug in for four years; only now were they finally overcome. An edict commended his skillful coordination, awarded him double-eyed peacock feathers, and granted him the hereditary office of Cavalry Commandant. Pressing the advantage, he drove on Jinshan and mopped up remnant rebel bands at Xinhekou, Longwang Temple, and elsewhere. The Jiangnan army captured Zhenjiang the same day and turned its full strength against Jiangning. In the spring of the eighth year De Xing'a advanced on Jiangpu and won a victory. As the Jiangning rebels grew desperate, Chen Yucheng and other hard fighters rallied reinforcements from Anhui. De Xing'a was beaten at Pukou, fell back to Liuhe, was stripped of his peacock feathers, and dismissed from office though left on duty. The rebels overran Jiangpu, Tianchang, and Yizheng in succession. De Xing'a could not stop them, Yangzhou fell again, and his hereditary rank was revoked. Zhang Guoliang soon crossed the river and recovered Yangzhou, while De Xing'a sat at Shaobo with his troops, hesitating to advance, and was sharply rebuked by edict. Wen Shaoyuan had held Liuhe for years as the shield of Jiangbei. Now, cut off from relief, the city fell and Wen Shaoyuan died defending it. Zhang Guoliang rushed up the next day, but it was too late. Guoliang shifted his army across the river because the Jiangning front was urgent. An edict ordered De Xing'a to recover Liuhe, but his force had lost its fighting spirit and he achieved nothing.
24
何桂清疏劾：「德興阿秉性粗率，初賴翁同書相助，得克瓜洲。 自同書調任安徽巡撫去後，左右無人，毫無謀略，貽誤軍事。 」和春亦劾其舉動乖謬，難以圖功。 文宗猶念其前勞，未遽加譴，九年，以圍攻六合久不下，革任召還。 自此江北不置帥，軍務統歸和春節制。 尋予六品頂戴，交僧格林沁差遣。
He Guiqing memorialized: 'De Xing'a is coarse and rash by nature. He took Guazhou only because Weng Tongshu assisted him at first. Since Weng Tongshu left for the Anhui governorship he has had no capable advisers, shown no strategic sense, and repeatedly bungled operations. ' He Chun also impeached him for erratic conduct and said he was unlikely ever to achieve success. Emperor Wenzong still remembered his earlier service and did not punish him immediately. In the ninth year, because the siege of Liuhe dragged on without success, he was dismissed and recalled. Thereafter Jiangbei had no separate commander-in-chief; all operations came under He Chun's control. He was soon given a sixth-rank hat button and assigned to Sengge Rinchen's command.
25
十一年，署密雲副都統。 同治初，授西安右翼副都統，留辦山西防務，又移駐陝西同、朝一帶防剿。 五年，充塔爾巴哈台參贊大臣，授正紅旗漢軍副都統，幫辦新疆北路軍務。 六年，丁母憂回旗。 尋卒，依都統例賜卹，諡威恪。
In the eleventh year he served as acting Vice Commandant at Miyun. Early in Tongzhi he was made Vice Commandant of the Right Wing at Xi'an, stayed on to manage Shanxi defenses, and was later posted to the Tongzhou and Chaoyi sector of Shaanxi for defense and suppression. In the fifth year he served as Assistant Commissioner at Tarbagatai, was made Vice Commandant-in-Chief of the Han Plain Red Banner, and assisted in the northern Xinjiang campaign. In the sixth year, upon his mother's death, he returned to his banner for mourning. He died soon afterward. Funeral grants were awarded according to the precedent for a Commandant-in-Chief, and he was given the posthumous title Weike.
26
論曰：勝保初以直諫稱。 及出治軍，膽略機警，數著功績。 然負氣凌人，雖僧格林沁不相下。 自餘疆臣共事，無不齟《齒吾》互劾。 文宗嚴馭之，屢躓屢起，蓋惜其才也。 始終以客軍辦賊，無自練之兵，無治餉之權； 撫用悍寇而紊紀律，濫收廢員而通賄賂，又縱淫侈不自檢束。 卒因袒庇苗沛霖，與楚軍不相能，朝廷苦心調和而不之喻，遂致獲罪，功過固莫掩也。 托明阿、德興阿皆戰將，非獨當一面之才，負乘僨事，宜哉。 斯又不足與勝保並論矣。
The commentators say: Sheng Bao first won renown for blunt remonstrance. Once in the field he proved bold, resourceful, and quick-witted, and won repeated victories. Yet he was overbearing by nature and would not defer even to Sengge Rinchen. With every other frontier official who served alongside him he clashed and exchanged impeachments. Emperor Wenzong disciplined him sternly, yet raised him up again and again after each fall—no doubt because his talent was valued. He always fought rebels with borrowed armies, without troops of his own training and without authority over supplies; he enlisted fierce bandit leaders and thereby wrecked discipline, took in disgraced officials indiscriminately and trafficked in bribes, and indulged debauchery without restraint. In the end he fell because he shielded Miao Peilin and could not work with the Hunan army, though the court tried hard to reconcile them and he would not listen. His merits and faults cannot be hidden. Tuo Ming'a and De Xing'a were fighting generals, not men fit to command a theater on their own. That they were promoted beyond their capacity and brought disaster upon themselves was only to be expected. They scarcely merit comparison with Sheng Bao at all.
