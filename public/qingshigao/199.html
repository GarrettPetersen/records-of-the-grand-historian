嘉慶元年丙辰六月遷。署。十一月丙辰赴寧剿匪。暫署。六月癸卯卒。代。六月乙亥兩廣總督。八月辛丑降。兩廣總督。五月丙寅降。漕運總督。 Jiaqing 1 (bingchen) Transferred in the sixth month; acting appointment. On bingchen of the eleventh month he went to Ningxia to suppress bandits; temporarily acting. Died on guimao of the sixth month; replaced. On yihai of the sixth month became Liangguang governor-general. On xinchou of the eighth month was demoted while still holding Liangguang governor-general. Demoted on bingyin of the fifth month to grain-transport governor-general.

嘉慶二年丁巳九月甲申遷。兩江總督。五月乙丑革。代。十月陝甘總督。降。五月乙丑兼辦四川總督事務。十月遷。七月辛巳卒。署。九月己丑湖廣總督。九月己丑遷。雲貴總督。十二月戊申卒。江南河道總督。九月甲申遷。任。十二月戊申遷。河東河道總督。 Jiaqing 2 (dingsi) Transferred on jiashen of the ninth month to Liangjiang governor-general. Dismissed on yichou of the fifth month and replaced. In the tenth month became Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general. Demoted. On yichou of the fifth month also handled Sichuan governor-general affairs. Transferred in the tenth month. Died on xinsi of the seventh month; acting appointment. On jichou of the ninth month became Huguang governor-general. Transferred on jichou of the ninth month to Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Died on wushen of the twelfth month while serving as Jiangnan River Works Governor-General. Transferred on jiashen of the ninth month and took office. On wushen of the twelfth month transferred to Hedong River Works Governor-General.

嘉慶三年戊午正月庚午遷。直隸總督。正月甲申四川總督。十一月甲子憂免。暫署閩浙總督。正月甲申遷。三月癸酉湖廣總督。六月甲寅卒。雲貴總督。六月甲寅遷。漕運總督。假。署。 Jiaqing 3 (wuwu) Transferred on gengwu of the first month to Zhili governor-general. On jiashen of the first month became Sichuan governor-general. Excused from office on jiazi of the eleventh month for mourning; temporarily acting Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general. Transferred on jiashen of the first month. On guiyou of the third month became Huguang governor-general. Died on jiayin of the sixth month while serving as Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Transferred on jiayin of the sixth month to grain-transport governor-general. On leave; acting appointment.

嘉慶四年己未二月辛丑卒。兩江總督。正月丙戌免。署。二月己丑陝甘總督。七月癸酉革。署四川總督。三月甲子調。兼署。十月戊子閩浙總督。署。三月庚午來京。湖廣總督。八月癸巳憂免。雲貴總督。十月戊子遷。雲貴總督。二月戊申免。漕運總督。十二月壬辰免。漕運總督。十一月壬戌實授。 Jiaqing 4 (jiwei) Died on xinchou of the second month while serving as Liangjiang governor-general. Excused from office on bingxu of the first month; acting appointment. On jichou of the second month became Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general. Dismissed on guiyou of the seventh month; acting Sichuan governor-general. Reassigned on jiazi of the third month while also acting. On wuzi of the tenth month became Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general; acting appointment. Summoned to the capital on gengwu of the third month as Huguang governor-general. Excused from office on guisi of the eighth month for mourning while holding Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Transferred on wuzi of the tenth month to Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Excused from office on wushen of the second month as grain-transport governor-general. Excused again on renchen of the twelfth month as grain-transport governor-general. Received formal appointment on renxu of the eleventh month.

嘉慶五年庚申九月丁亥假。護。戊戌，直隸總督。正月辛酉遷。陝甘總督。三月辛巳革。四川總督。正月辛酉遷。閩浙總督。正月丁丑免。湖廣總督。署。九月戊戌湖廣總督。九月戊戌遷。雲貴總督。二月戊子革。江南河道總督。二月戊子遷。河東河道總督。 Jiaqing 5 (gengshen) On dinghai of the ninth month went on leave with a guarded acting appointment. Transferred on xinyou of the first month to Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general. Excused from office on dingchou of the first month as Huguang governor-general; acting appointment. On wuxu of the ninth month became Huguang governor-general. Transferred on wuxu of the ninth month to Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Dismissed on wuzi of the second month from Jiangnan River Works Governor-General. Transferred on wuzi of the second month to Hedong River Works Governor-General.

嘉慶六年辛酉六月甲寅革。署。九月直隸總督。十一月丁丑來京。陝甘總督。卒。四月壬戌湖廣總督。 Jiaqing 6 (xinyou) Dismissed on jiayin of the sixth month; acting appointment. In the ninth month became Zhili governor-general. 嘉慶七年壬戌四月辛丑病免。暫署。甲辰，署直隸總督。九月己巳實授。十一月庚寅解。兩廣總督。署。十一月辛卯遷。漕運總督。卒。八月癸卯署河東河道總督。 Jiaqing 7 (renxu) Excused from office on xinchou of the fourth month for illness; temporarily acting. 嘉慶八年癸亥六月戊子調。兩江總督。回本任。正月庚午兩廣總督。 Jiaqing 8 (guihai) Reassigned on wuzi of the sixth month to Liangjiang governor-general. Returned to his original post. On gengwu of the first month became Liangguang governor-general.

嘉慶九年甲子六月乙亥卒。署陝甘總督。十一月己酉陝甘總督。遷。十二月己酉兩廣總督。七月己亥卒。雲貴總督。署。十二月丙寅來京。江南河道總督。四月丙子來京。署。十二月丙寅遷。河東河道總督。 Jiaqing 9 (jiazi) Died on yihai of the sixth month; acting Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general. On jiyou of the eleventh month became Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general. Transferred. On jiyou of the twelfth month became Liangguang governor-general. Died on jihai of the seventh month while serving as Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general; acting appointment. Summoned to the capital on bingyin of the twelfth month as Jiangnan River Works Governor-General. Summoned to the capital on bingzi of the fourth month; acting appointment. Transferred on bingyin of the twelfth month to Hedong River Works Governor-General.

嘉慶十年乙丑六月庚申降。直隸總督。十月辛丑遷。署直隸總督。正月辛亥遷。兩江總督。六月庚申遷。湖廣總督。十一月丙辰革。湖廣總督。十月辛丑來京。兩廣總督。 Jiaqing 10 (yichou) Demoted on gengshen of the sixth month while holding Zhili governor-general. Transferred on gengshen of the sixth month to Huguang governor-general. Dismissed on bingchen of the eleventh month from Huguang governor-general. Summoned to the capital on xinchou of the tenth month as Liangguang governor-general.

嘉慶十一年丙寅九月壬申卒。署。十月丁亥署直隸總督。十月甲申來京。陝甘總督。署。十一月癸丑統兵剿匪。署四川總督。五月丙寅革。閩浙總督。署。十月甲申遷。湖廣總督。署。改副總河。六月庚寅江南河道總督。四月癸巳革。河東河道總督。 Jiaqing 11 (bingyin) Died on renshen of the ninth month; acting appointment. 嘉慶十二年丁卯九月甲寅授直隸總督。五月己未病免。陝甘總督。護。五月己未遷。漕運總督。 Jiaqing 12 (dingmao) Appointed Zhili governor-general on jiayin of the ninth month. 道光七年丁亥十一月庚戌差。署直隸總督。五月丙戌來京。兩江總督。 Daoguang 7 (dinghai) Dispatched on gengxu of the eleventh month on special duty as acting Zhili governor-general. Summoned to the capital on bingxu of the fifth month as Liangjiang governor-general.

道光八年戊子 Daoguang 8 (wuzi)

道光九年己丑四月己卯降。署署。六月己丑回直隸總督任。四月癸酉來京。四川總督。三月戊午調。漕運總督。 Daoguang 9 (jichou) Demoted on jimao of the fourth month with double acting appointments. On jichou of the sixth month returned to the post of Zhili governor-general. Summoned to the capital on guiyou of the fourth month as Sichuan governor-general. Reassigned on wuwu of the third month to grain-transport governor-general.

道光十年庚寅六月辛卯假。兩江總督。九月戊午赴肅州辦後路軍需。署陝甘總督。十一月壬午降。湖廣總督。八月壬午調。署漕運總督。 Daoguang 10 (gengyin) Went on leave on xinmao of the sixth month as Liangjiang governor-general. On wuwu of the ninth month went to Suzhou to manage rear-area military supplies as acting Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general. Demoted on renwu of the eleventh month while holding Huguang governor-general. Reassigned on renwu of the eighth month as acting grain-transport governor-general.

道光十一年辛卯二月乙未革。直隸總督。正月丁丑回。二月乙未調。四川總督。十二月乙巳調。漕運總督。兼署。十月乙酉病免。河東河道總督。 Daoguang 11 (xinmao) Dismissed on yiwei of the second month from Zhili governor-general. Returned in the first month on dingchou. Reassigned on yiwei of the second month to Sichuan governor-general. Reassigned on yisi of the twelfth month to grain-transport governor-general with an additional acting appointment. Excused from office on yiyou of the tenth month for illness as Hedong River Works Governor-General.

道光十二年壬辰二月乙未卒。閩浙總督。八月甲午調。湖廣總督。八月甲午革。兩廣總督。署。九月丁未革，留。二月乙未調。河東河道總督。 Daoguang 12 (renchen) Died on yiwei of the second month while serving as Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general. Reassigned on jiawu of the eighth month to Huguang governor-general. Dismissed on jiawu of the eighth month from Liangguang governor-general; acting appointment. Dismissed on dingwei of the ninth month but retained in office. Reassigned on yiwei of the second month to Hedong River Works Governor-General.

道光十三年癸巳入覲。兼署雲貴總督。八月回任。四月戊申遷。漕運總督。九月壬辰遷。漕運總督。署。三月戊戌遷。江南河道總督。四月庚申丁憂。暫署。八月甲辰仍署江南河道總督。 Daoguang 13 (guisi) Attended imperial audience while also acting as Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Returned to his post in the eighth month. Transferred on wushen of the fourth month to grain-transport governor-general. Transferred on renchen of the ninth month to grain-transport governor-general; acting appointment. Transferred on wuxu of the third month to Jiangnan River Works Governor-General. Entered mourning on gengshen of the fourth month; temporarily acting. On jiachen of the eighth month again became acting Jiangnan River Works Governor-General.

道光十四年甲午五月己巳遷。漕運總督。十一月壬申遷。漕運總督。 Daoguang 14 (jiawu) Transferred on jisi of the fifth month to grain-transport governor-general. Transferred on renshen of the eleventh month to grain-transport governor-general.

道光十五年乙未正月丙戌病免。陝甘總督。七月庚辰卒。兩廣總督。署。二月己亥入閣。雲貴總督。八月丁丑假。漕運總督。五月戊寅來京。河東河道總督。十月辛酉覲。署。 Daoguang 15 (yiwei) Excused from office on bingxu of the first month for illness as Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general. Died on gengchen of the seventh month while serving as Liangguang governor-general; acting appointment. Entered the Grand Council on jihai of the second month while serving as Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Went on leave on dingchou of the eighth month as grain-transport governor-general. Summoned to the capital on wuyin of the fifth month as Hedong River Works Governor-General. Granted imperial audience on xinyou of the tenth month; acting appointment.

道光十六年丙申七月癸未憂。閩浙總督。署。 Daoguang 16 (bingshen) Entered mourning leave on guiwei of the seventh month as Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general; acting appointment.

道光十七年丁酉三月丁亥憂。署。六月乙未仍署直隸總督。正月庚子降。湖廣總督。五月己卯遷。漕運總督。 Daoguang 17 (dingyou) Entered mourning leave on dinghai of the third month; acting appointment. On yiwei of the sixth month again became acting Zhili governor-general. Demoted on gengzi of the first month while holding Huguang governor-general. Transferred on jimao of the fifth month to grain-transport governor-general.

道光十八年戊戌閏四月甲申遷。四川總督。七月戊申遷。署。十一月壬子降。四川總督。九月庚申召。兼署。十一月癸丑差。署湖廣總督。十一月癸丑調。署漕運總督。 Daoguang 18 (wuxu) Transferred on jiashen of the intercalary fourth month to Sichuan governor-general. Transferred on wushen of the seventh month; acting appointment. Demoted on renzi of the eleventh month while still holding Sichuan governor-general. Summoned on gengshen of the ninth month with an additional acting appointment. Dispatched on guichou of the eleventh month on special duty as acting Huguang governor-general. Reassigned on guichou of the eleventh month as acting grain-transport governor-general.

道光十九年己亥三月乙巳病免。兩江總督。署。十二月癸亥卒。調。署。己卯兩江總督。六月丙寅革。閩浙總督。辛卯調。閩浙總督。十二月甲申調閩浙總督。三月乙巳調。湖廣總督。辛卯湖廣總督。十二月己卯調。兩廣總督。十二月己卯調。雲貴總督。甲申遷。雲貴總督。兼署。回。四月丁丑調。漕運總督。 Daoguang 19 (jihai) Excused from office on yisi of the third month for illness as Liangjiang governor-general; acting appointment. Died on guihai of the twelfth month; reassigned with acting appointment. On jimao became Liangjiang governor-general. Dismissed on bingyin of the sixth month from Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general. Reassigned on xinmao as Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general. Reassigned again on jiashen of the twelfth month as Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general. Reassigned on yisi of the third month to Huguang governor-general. On xinmao became Huguang governor-general. Reassigned on jimao of the twelfth month to Liangguang governor-general. Reassigned on jimao of the twelfth month to Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Transferred on jiashen as Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general with an additional acting appointment. Returned to duty. Reassigned on dingchou of the fourth month to grain-transport governor-general.

道光二十年庚子八月己卯差。調署直隸總督。七月丁酉差。署兩江總督。六月戊子遷。陝甘總督。八月己卯調。署。十二月癸未召。署。九月辛卯來京。閩浙總督。護。乙未革。十一戊申革。湖廣總督。九月庚寅來京。署兩廣總督。暫護。二月甲申卒。河東河道總督。 Daoguang 20 (gengzi) Dispatched on jimao of the eighth month on special duty and reassigned as acting Zhili governor-general. Dispatched on dingyou of the seventh month on special duty as acting Liangjiang governor-general. Transferred on wuzi of the sixth month to Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general. Reassigned on jimao of the eighth month; acting appointment. Summoned on guiwei of the twelfth month; acting appointment. Summoned to the capital on xinmao of the ninth month as Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general with a guarded acting appointment. Dismissed on yiwei. Dismissed on wushen of the eleventh month from Huguang governor-general. Summoned to the capital on gengyin of the ninth month as acting Liangguang governor-general with temporary guarded duties. Died on jiashen of the second month while serving as Hedong River Works Governor-General.

道光二十一年辛丑正月癸丑差。護。九月丙辰署。己未殉難，兩江總督。二月辛酉授陝甘總督。十二月戊子革。閩浙總督。二月辛酉革。兩廣總督。革。八月庚寅河東河道總督。署。 Daoguang 21 (xinchou) Dispatched on guichou of the first month on special duty with a guarded acting appointment. Acting appointment on bingchen of the ninth month. Died in service on jiwei as Liangjiang governor-general. Appointed Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general on xinyou of the second month. Dismissed on wuzi of the twelfth month from Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general. Dismissed on xinyou of the second month from Liangguang governor-general. Dismissed from office. On gengyin of the eighth month became Hedong River Works Governor-General; acting appointment.

道光二十二年壬寅九月己未革，逮。兩江總督。三月丙子卒。陝甘總督。正月丙辰病免。閩浙總督。九月戊午回籍。署。十一月丙辰憂。署。十二月辛巳來京。署漕運總督。八月戊子革。江南河道總督。九月辛酉署河東河道總督。 Daoguang 22 (renyin) Dismissed on jiwei of the ninth month and arrested while holding Liangjiang governor-general. Died on bingzi of the third month while serving as Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general. Excused from office on bingchen of the first month for illness as Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general. Returned to his native place on wuwu of the ninth month; acting appointment. Mourning leave on bingchen of the eleventh month; acting appointment. Summoned to the capital on xinsi of the twelfth month as acting grain-transport governor-general. Dismissed on wuzi of the eighth month from Jiangnan River Works Governor-General. Became acting Hedong River Works Governor-General on xinyou of the ninth month.

道光二十三年癸卯三月庚戌差。署。護。九月己酉仍回任。五月戊辰病免。閩浙總督。七月戊申革。河東河道總督。 Daoguang 23 (guimao) Dispatched on gengxu of the third month on special duty; acting appointment with guarded duties. Dismissed on wushen of the seventh month from Hedong River Works Governor-General.

道光二十四年甲辰二月戊戌遷。兩江總督。護。二月戊戌病免。兩江總督。十一月己巳覲。兼雲貴總督。三月丙申丁憂。漕運總督。 Daoguang 24 (jiachen) Transferred on wuxu of the second month to Liangjiang governor-general with a guarded acting appointment. 道光二十五年乙巳四月壬子卒。任。署。十一月辛酉卒。陝甘總督。署。四月癸卯免。雲貴總督。壬子覲。兼署。正月庚午遷。漕運總督。 Daoguang 25 (yisi) Died on renzi of the fourth month after taking office; acting appointment. 道光二十六年丙午十二月庚午來京。四川總督。八月乙亥降。雲貴總督。署。十二月戊申調。署漕運總督。 Daoguang 26 (bingwu) Summoned to the capital on gengwu of the twelfth month as Sichuan governor-general. Demoted on yihai of the eighth month while holding Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general; acting appointment. Reassigned on wushen of the twelfth month as acting grain-transport governor-general.

道光二十七年丁未正月乙酉覲。署。三月丁未遷。兩江總督。八月甲子赴肅州督剿。署。九月辛巳回陝甘總督。十二月甲戌來京。署兩廣總督。三月乙未調。雲貴總督。署。 Daoguang 27 (dingwei) Granted imperial audience on yiyou of the first month; acting appointment. Transferred on dingwei of the third month to Liangjiang governor-general. On jiazi of the eighth month went to Suzhou to supervise suppression operations; acting appointment. Returned to Shaanxi-Gansu governor-general on xinsi of the ninth month. Summoned to the capital on jiaxu of the twelfth month as acting Liangguang governor-general. Reassigned on yiwei of the third month to Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general; acting appointment.

道光二十八年戊申六月丙午留京。授兩廣總督。九月甲戌病免。江南河道總督。署。 Daoguang 28 (wushen) Remained at the capital on bingwu of the sixth month and was appointed Liangguang governor-general. Excused from office on jiaxu of the ninth month for illness as Jiangnan River Works Governor-General; acting appointment.

道光二十九年己酉四月壬寅病免。兩江總督。九月甲辰病免。署陝甘總督。九月己酉實授。九月甲辰調。署。己酉四川總督。七月己未病免。雲貴總督。閏四月辛未卒。河東河道總督。署。 Daoguang 29 (jiyou) Excused from office on renyin of the fourth month for illness as Liangjiang governor-general. Excused from office on jiwei of the seventh month for illness as Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. 道光三十年庚戌十一月丙午病卸。閩浙總督。署。十一月丙午調。湖廣總督。署。十一月丙午調。雲貴總督。十二月壬申署。 Daoguang 30 (gengxu) Relieved on bingwu of the eleventh month due to illness as Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general; acting appointment.
