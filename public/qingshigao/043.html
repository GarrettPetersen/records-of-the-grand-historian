洪範曰：「金曰從革。 」金不從革，則為咎徵。 凡恒暘、詩妖、毛蟲之孽、犬禍、金石之妖、白眚、白祥皆屬之於金。
The Great Plan states, "Metal is defined as following change and transformation. When metal fails in this office, the result is a sign of cosmic blame." Persistent drought, poetic portents, infestations of hairy insects, canine calamities, prodigies of metal and stone, white blights, and white omens—all these belong to the element of metal.

同治元年二月，青縣旱。 六月，孝義廳、皋蘭旱。 七月，葦縣、棲霞、咸寧、江夏旱。 二年，嵊縣旱。 三年夏，常山旱。 秋，崇陽、撫寧旱。 四年春，蘄水大旱荒，民有鬻子女者。 秋，麻城旱，高鄉自冬至次年夏不雨。 五年夏，江夏、江山旱。 九月，崇陽、漢陽旱。 六年夏，昌平、玉田、黃陂、荊門、德州旱。 秋，邢台、懷來、武昌、黃州旱。 七年春，皋蘭旱。 冬，陵縣旱。 八年春，青縣旱。 九年春，新樂、黃縣旱。 十年春，清苑大旱，無麥。 十一年，皋蘭春、夏旱。 十二年五月，公安、枝江旱。 十三年三月，江陵、公安、枝江旱。 秋，均州旱。
In the second month of the first year of Tongzhi, Qing County was drought-stricken. In the sixth month, Xiaoyi Department and Gaolan were drought-stricken. In the seventh month, Wei County, Qixia, Xianning, and Jiangxia endured drought. In the second year, Sheng County was drought-stricken. In the summer of the third year, Changshan endured drought. That autumn, Chongyang and Funing were drought-stricken. In the spring of the fourth year, Qishui was ravaged by severe drought and famine, and some people were driven to sell their children. That autumn, Macheng was drought-stricken, while Gaoxiang saw no rain from winter through the following summer. In the summer of the fifth year, Jiangxia and Jiangshan endured drought. In the ninth month, Chongyang and Hanyang were drought-stricken. In the summer of the sixth year, Changping, Yutian, Huangpi, Jingmen, and Dezhou were drought-stricken. That autumn, Xingtai, Huailai, Wuchang, and Huangzhou endured drought. In the spring of the seventh year, Gaolan was drought-stricken. That winter, Ling County endured drought. In the spring of the eighth year, Qing County was drought-stricken. In the spring of the ninth year, Xingle and Huang County endured drought. In the spring of the tenth year, Qingyuan was gripped by severe drought, and the wheat crop failed entirely. In the eleventh year, Gaolan was drought-stricken through spring and summer. In the fifth month of the twelfth year, Gong'an and Zhijiang were drought-stricken. In the third month of the thirteenth year, Jiangling, Gong'an, and Zhijiang endured drought. That autumn, Junzhou was drought-stricken.
23
光緒元年，青縣夏、秋旱。 二年春，望都、蠡縣、灤州、臨榆旱。 五月，肥城旱。 八月，槁城旱。 三年四月，武進、霑化、寧陽、南樂、唐山旱，應山夏、秋大旱。 四年春，東平、三原旱。 七月，內丘、井陘、順天、唐山、平鄉、臨榆旱。 八月，京山旱。 六年秋，甘泉、魚台、邢台旱。 八年六月，均州、雲夢、鶴峰州旱。 十一年秋，東光旱。 十三年七月，靖遠、東光旱。 十六年，皋蘭春、夏旱。 十七年，靜寧、合水旱。 十八年六月，皋蘭、金華、靜寧、通渭、洮州、安化旱。 十九年五月，太平旱。 二十年，太平自七月至十月不雨，大旱。 二十一年六月，太平旱。 二十四年九月，寧津旱。 二十六年六月，涇州、皋蘭、平涼、莊浪、固原、洮州旱。 閏八月，南樂、邢台旱。 二十七年春，皋蘭、平涼、莊浪、固原、洮州大旱。 三十三年，皋蘭旱。 三十四年八月，蘭州、靜寧大旱。
In the first year of Guangxu, Qing County was drought-stricken through summer and autumn. In the spring of the second year, Wangdu, Li County, Luanzhou, and Linyu were drought-stricken. In the fifth month, Feicheng endured drought. In the eighth month, Gaocheng was drought-stricken. In the fourth month of the third year, Wujin, Zhanhua, Ningyang, Nanle, and Tangshan were drought-stricken, while Yingshan endured severe drought through summer and autumn. In the spring of the fourth year, Dongping and Sanyuan were drought-stricken. In the seventh month, Neiqiu, Jingxing, Shuntian, Tangshan, Pingxiang, and Linyu endured drought. In the eighth month, Jingshan was drought-stricken. In the autumn of the sixth year, Ganquan, Yutai, and Xingtai were drought-stricken. In the sixth month of the eighth year, Junzhou, Yunmeng, and Hefeng Prefecture endured drought. In the autumn of the eleventh year, Dongguang was drought-stricken. In the seventh month of the thirteenth year, Jingyuan and Dongguang endured drought. In the sixteenth year, Gaolan was drought-stricken through spring and summer. In the seventeenth year, Jingning and Heshui were drought-stricken. In the sixth month of the eighteenth year, Gaolan, Jinhua, Jingning, Tongwei, Taozhou, and Anhua endured drought. In the fifth month of the nineteenth year, Taiping was drought-stricken. In the twentieth year, Taiping went without rain from the seventh month through the tenth, suffering severe drought. In the sixth month of the twenty-first year, Taiping was drought-stricken. In the ninth month of the twenty-fourth year, Ningjin endured drought. In the sixth month of the twenty-sixth year, Jingzhou, Gaolan, Pingliang, Zhuanglang, Guyuan, and Taozhou were drought-stricken. In the intercalary eighth month, Nanle and Xingtai endured drought. In the spring of the twenty-seventh year, Gaolan, Pingliang, Zhuanglang, Guyuan, and Taozhou were ravaged by severe drought. In the thirty-third year, Gaolan was drought-stricken. In the eighth month of the thirty-fourth year, Lanzhou and Jingning were gripped by severe drought.
24
康熙二年十一月，平度民間獲兔，八足、四耳、兩尾。 二十七年十二月十六日，有黃熊鳴於合浦西門，十七夜復鳴。 乾隆十八年，畢節熊入城，傷二人。
In the eleventh month of the second year of Kangxi, the people of Pingdu captured a rabbit with eight feet, four ears, and two tails. On the sixteenth day of the twelfth month of the twenty-seventh year, a yellow bear was heard crying at the west gate of Hepu, and it cried again on the night of the seventeenth. In the eighteenth year of Qianlong, a bear entered the city of Bijie and injured two people.
25
嘉慶七年，陸川有熊傷人。 二十三年七月，黃縣有熊走入荏苒村，土人以槍殺之。 順治八年，泰山元君廟鍾鼓自鳴。
In the seventh year of Jiaqing, a bear in Luchuan attacked and injured people. In the seventh month of the twenty-third year, a bear entered Renran Village in Huang County, and the local people shot it dead. In the eighth year of Shunzhi, the bells and drums at the Princess Bixia Temple on Mount Tai rang of their own accord.
26
康熙十八年正月，六安州金鐵出火。 三十九年十一月，海陽馬王廟鍾自鳴，越三日復鳴。 雍正九年五月，七姑廟鍾自鳴。
In the first month of the eighteenth year of Kangxi, gold and iron were seen emitting fire in Lu'an Prefecture. In the eleventh month of the thirty-ninth year, the bell at the Horse King Temple in Haiyang rang of its own accord, and rang again three days later. In the fifth month of the ninth year of Yongzheng, the bell at the Seven Maidens Temple rang of its own accord.
27
乾隆三十八年十二月除夕，黃縣叢林、冶基、寶塔三寺鍾鼓自鳴。 嘉慶十四年冬，泰州雨箭。 咸豐八年四月，魚台兵器夜吐火光。
On New Year's Eve in the twelfth month of the thirty-eighth year of Qianlong, the bells and drums at the three temples of Conglin, Yeji, and Baota in Huang County rang of their own accord. In the winter of the fourteenth year of Jiaqing, Taizhou was struck by a rain of arrows. In the fourth month of the eighth year of Xianfeng, weapons in Yutai were seen emitting firelight at night.
28
同治三年九月，東嶽廟鍾自鳴。 八年九月，彭澤長嶺酒店釜鳴，聲聞數里許，月餘方止。
In the ninth month of the third year of Tongzhi, the bell at the Eastern Peak Temple rang of its own accord. In the ninth month of the eighth year, a pot at an inn in Changling, Pengze, began to ring; the sound carried for several li and did not cease for more than a month.
29
順治四年，崇明民家犬生六足。 七年，商州民李旺家有犬坐坑上，作人言：「老的忒老，小的忒小。 」縛而殺之。 十三年，鄒平民生子，犬頭猴身，能吠。
In the fourth year of Shunzhi, a dog at a common household in Chongming whelped a pup with six legs. In the seventh year, at the home of Li Wang, a commoner of Shangzhou, a dog sat atop a pit and spoke in human words, saying, "The elder is too old, the younger too young. " It was bound and put to death. In the thirteenth year, a commoner of Zouping gave birth to a child with a dog's head and a monkey's body that could bark.
