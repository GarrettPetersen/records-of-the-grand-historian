The second year of the Guangxu reign (bingzi). On the jiayin day of the tenth month he was transferred to Minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs. On the jiayin day of the tenth month he was transferred to Left Vice Minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs; on the yiwei day of the eleventh month he was transferred again, retaining that same post. On the jiayin day of the tenth month he was transferred to Right Vice Minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs; on the yiwei day of the eleventh month he was transferred again, retaining that same post. The third year of the Guangxu reign (dingchou). On the guihai day of the first month he was transferred to Left Censor-in-Chief. The fifth year of the Guangxu reign (jimao). On the jiawu day of the fifth month he was transferred to Left Vice Minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs. On the wuchen day of the first month he took leave as Right Vice Minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs; on the guiwei day of the second month and the jiawu day of the fifth month he was transferred, retaining that same post. On the guisi day of the eleventh month the post was left vacant; on the xinmao day he was appointed Left Censor-in-Chief. On the gengwu day of the first month and the yimao day of the third month he was transferred to Left Censor-in-Chief. On the wuchen day of the first month he was transferred; on the renzi day of the third month he became Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. On the renshen day of the first month he became Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. On the gengshen day of the fourth month he was transferred; on the gengxu day of the sixth month he became Left Vice Censor-in-Chief; on jiwei he was dismissed after impeachment; on the renyin day of the eighth month he was appointed Left Vice Censor-in-Chief but never took office; on the bingwu day he served in an acting capacity. The sixth year of the Guangxu reign (gengchen). On the renxu day of the tenth month he died in office as Minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs. On the renxu day of the tenth month he was transferred to Left Censor-in-Chief. On the renxu day of the tenth month he was transferred; on the wuzi day of the eleventh month he became Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. He continued to serve in an acting capacity.
