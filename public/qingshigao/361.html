劉清傅鼐嚴如熤子正基
Liu Qing, Fu Nai, and Yan Ruyi's son Zheng Ji
3
劉清，字天一，貴州廣順人。 由拔貢議敘，授四川冕寧縣丞，擢南充知縣，政聲為一省之冠。
Liu Qing, style name Tianyi, came from Guangshun in Guizhou. Selected through the juren quota for appointment, he was made assistant magistrate of Minning in Sichuan and then promoted to magistrate of Nanchong, where his administrative reputation stood at the top of the province.
4
嘉慶元年，教匪起，清得民心，募鄉勇五百人擊賊，人樂為用。 賊自為民時知其名，遇輒避之。 繼從總督英善剿達州匪徐天德，數捷，率鄉勇羅思舉赴賊營諭降羅其清，未得要領； 而徐天德與王三槐、冷天祿合陷東鄉，二年春，始復之，遂署東鄉。 進克清谿場，擒賊黨王學禮，天德之舅也，言天德與王三槐皆有歸順意。 總督宜綿令清往招三槐，遍歷諸賊壘，迎送奉酒食甚謹，宣示招撫，皆聽命，夜宿其帳中。 三槐隨至大營，約期率所部出降，然實藉覘虛實，非真意。 屆期，三槐詭稱於雙廟投降，伏匪為掩襲計，官軍預設備，擊敗之。 時羅其清、冉文儔並聚方山坪，清偕總兵百祥奪多福山賊壘，會諸路兵攻方山坪，克之。 賊竄通江、巴州，與徐天德、王三槐合，清所部鄉勇增至千餘人，桂涵、李子青等皆驍勇善戰，偕諸軍擊賊，疊有殲獲，羅、冉二匪漸蹙。
In the first year of the Jiaqing reign, when sectarian rebels broke out, Qing won popular trust, raised five hundred local militia to strike the rebels, and men were eager to follow him. The rebels had known his name even when they were ordinary people, and they would turn aside whenever they encountered him. He next served under Governor-General Yingshan against Xu Tiande, the Dazhou rebel, and won repeated victories. He sent the militia leader Luo Siju to the rebel camp to negotiate the surrender of Luo Qiqing, but no definite terms were reached; Meanwhile Xu Tiande joined Wang Sanhuai and Leng Tianlu in capturing Dongxiang. In the spring of the second year Qing helped recover it and was put in charge of the district. He advanced and took Qingxi Field, capturing the rebel Wang Xueli, Xu Tiande's uncle, who reported that both Xu Tiande and Wang Sanhuai were willing to surrender. Governor-General Yimian sent Qing to negotiate with Wang Sanhuai. He toured every rebel camp, was escorted in and out with scrupulous hospitality, announced the government's offer of amnesty, and found them all compliant—even sleeping in their tents at night. Sanhuai came with him to headquarters and set a date to bring his men out in surrender, but he was really reconnoitering the army's strength and had no genuine intention of yielding. When the appointed day arrived, Sanhuai pretended he would surrender at Shuangmiao while hiding rebels for a surprise attack. The government forces were ready and routed them. Luo Qiqing and Ran Wenchou were then encamped together at Fangshanping. Qing joined Regional Commander Baixiang in storming the rebel stronghold on Duofu Mountain, then combined with troops from every direction to attack Fangshanping and capture it. The rebels fled into Tongjiang and Bazhou and linked up with Xu Tiande and Wang Sanhuai. Qing's militia swelled to more than a thousand men, including the formidable fighters Gui Han and Li Ziqing. Fighting alongside the regular forces, they won victory after victory, and the Luo and Ran factions were gradually driven into a corner.
5
三年，署廣元縣事。 總督勒保攻王三槐於安樂坪，未下，復令清往招撫。 三槐恃前此出入大營無忌，留隨人劉星渠等為質，三槐遂詣軍門，勒保奏報大捷，俘三槐至京。 廷訊時，言：「官逼民反。 」仁宗詰之曰：「四川一省官皆不善耶？ 」對曰：「惟有劉青天一人。 」劉青天者，川民以呼清也。 帝深嘉之，特諭曰：「朕聞劉清官聲甚好，每率眾禦敵，賊以其廉吏，往往退避引去。 如果始終奮勇，民情愛戴，著勒保據實保奏。 」尋以清治績戰功奏上，晉秩同知直隸州，賜花翎。 於是劉青天之名聞天下。
In the third year he was put in charge of Guangyuan County. Governor-General Lebao besieged Wang Sanhuai at Anleping without success and again sent Qing to negotiate his surrender. Confident from his earlier unhindered visits to headquarters, Sanhuai left Liu Xingqu and others behind as hostages and then presented himself at the army gate. Lebao reported a great victory, and Sanhuai was taken captive to Beijing. At his interrogation before the throne he said, "The officials drove the people to rebel." Emperor Renzong pressed him: "Were every official in Sichuan corrupt?" He replied, "There was only one—Liu Qingtian." "Liu Qingtian"—"Blue-Sky Liu"—was the name the people of Sichuan gave to Qing. The emperor was deeply pleased and issued a special instruction: "We have heard that Liu Qing enjoys an excellent reputation. Whenever he leads the people against the enemy, the rebels, recognizing him as an incorruptible official, often withdraw of their own accord." "If he continues to fight bravely and retains the people's affection, Lebao is to recommend him on the basis of the facts." Shortly afterward Lebao reported Qing's civil and military achievements, and he was promoted to subprefect of Zhili Prefecture and awarded the peacock feather. From that point the name Liu Qingtian was known throughout the empire.
6
四年，補忠州，加知府銜。 參贊額勒登保破冉天元、張子聰於竹峪關，令清於通江、巴州招撫餘匪。 自王三槐被誘，諸賊首皆疑憚不敢出； 然感清無他，不忍加害，每至賊營，必留宿盡禮，其脅從者先後投出二萬餘人，遣散歸農，以功加道銜。 命隨副都御史廣興駐達州治軍餉，擢建昌道。 五年，冉天元等合諸路賊渡嘉陵江，總督魁倫退守鹽亭鳳凰山，令清集民團守潼河，上下三百餘裡，多淺灘，盡撤防兵； 清爭之，不可。 賊果於太和鎮上游王家嘴偷渡，委罪於清，奪職，命以知縣用，留營效力。 既而德楞泰破賊，天元伏誅，諸路竄賊旁皇通、巴之間，勒保以清去歲招降成效，責籌安撫。 時川匪父子兄弟一家中不盡習教為賊，而奔竄往來，過鄉里輒歸視。 清屯要隘，且剿且撫，遣人存問賊首家屬有歸誠之意者，潛令圖之，展轉相引，賊遂瓦解。 藍號鮮大川，巴州人，號為狡悍。 其族人文炳、路保及黨楊似山，清皆厚卹其家，感恩原效死，乃使文炳勸大川降，不可，且與似山謀殺文炳。 似山乘間殺大川，與文炳、路保同降。 巴州匪遂滅。 六年，以功復原官，仍授建昌道。 七年春，破賊於南江五方坪，擒賊首李彬及辛文等，加按察使銜，尋授四川按察使。 敗藍號齊國典餘匪於兩河口，追擒其黨葛成勝。 諸匪以次平，大功告蕆，下部議敘。
In the fourth year he was confirmed as magistrate of Zhongzhou and granted prefectural rank. He served under Eledengbao when Ran Tianyuan and Zhang Zicong were defeated at Zhuyu Pass, then was assigned to win over the remaining rebels in Tongjiang and Bazhou. After Wang Sanhuai had been tricked into surrender, every rebel leader was wary and refused to show himself; but trusting that Qing meant them no harm, they would not hurt him and always received him with full hospitality whenever he entered their camps. More than twenty thousand coerced followers came out in succession and were sent home to farm. For this he was given circuit-intendant rank. He was ordered to accompany Vice Censor-in-Chief Guangxing, who was stationed at Dazhou to manage military supplies, and was promoted to intendant of Jianchang Circuit. In the fifth year Ran Tianyuan united rebels from every direction and crossed the Jialing. Governor-General Kuilun fell back to Fenghuang Mountain at Yanting and ordered Qing to rally local militia to hold the Tong River along some three hundred li of mostly shallow fords, while withdrawing all regular defenders; Qing protested in vain. The rebels duly crossed secretly at Wangjiazui above Taihe Town. Qing was blamed, stripped of rank, and ordered to serve only as a magistrate while remaining in camp on active duty. Delin'tai soon broke the rebels and Tianyuan was executed. Fugitives from every route drifted between Tongjiang and Bazhou, and Lebao, recalling Qing's success the year before in winning surrenders, put him in charge of pacification. Not every member of a rebel household in Sichuan had taken up the sect and turned bandit; many shuttled back and forth and slipped home whenever they passed their native place. Qing held the key passes, alternating suppression with persuasion. He sent agents to visit the families of chiefs who showed signs of sincerity, worked on them in secret, and through a chain of introductions the rebel bands fell apart. Xian Dachuan of the Blue Banner, from Bazhou, had a reputation for cunning and ferocity. Qing lavished support on the families of his kinsmen Wen Bing and Lu Bao and of his follower Yang Sishan, who in gratitude offered their lives. He sent Wen Bing to persuade Dachuan to surrender, but Dachuan refused and even plotted with Sishan to kill Wen Bing. Sishan seized his chance, killed Dachuan, and surrendered together with Wen Bing and Lu Bao. The Bazhou rebels were thus wiped out. In the sixth year his former rank was restored for his achievements and he was again made intendant of Jianchang Circuit. In the spring of the seventh year he routed rebels at Wufangping in Nanjiang and captured the chiefs Li Bin, Xin Wen, and others. He was given surveillance-commissioner rank and soon appointed provincial surveillance commissioner of Sichuan. He defeated the remnant Blue Banner forces of Qi Guodian at Lianghekou and ran down his follower Ge Chengsheng. The remaining rebel bands were subdued one after another, the great campaign was declared complete, and the ministries were asked to record rewards.
7
清在軍七年，先後招降三萬餘人。 有業者歸鄉里，無業及有業原從者為鄉勇，後立戰功者​​三十餘人。 其中苟崇勳、苟文耀、李彬、辛文、李世玉、趙文相，皆賊魁也。 崇勳即苟文通，已奏報殲斃而改名。 及軍事竣，當遣，清以諸人田廬焚盪，驟散將復為賊，臨行重犒之。 自向富室巨商貸金，人感其誠，多響應。 事畢，積逋負至十萬。
Over seven years in the field Qing had won the surrender of more than thirty thousand men. Those with trades went home; those without, and those who had trades but had served under him, stayed on as militia, and more than thirty of them later distinguished themselves in battle. Among them were Gou Chongxun, Gou Wenyao, Li Bin, Xin Wen, Li Shiyu, and Zhao Wenxiang—all former rebel chiefs. Chongxun was in fact Gou Wentong, already reported dead in battle, who had taken a new name. When the campaign ended and they were to be discharged, Qing feared that with their homes burned they would turn bandit again if dispersed abruptly, and on leaving gave them heavy rewards. He borrowed the money himself from wealthy families and great merchants; moved by his sincerity, many came forward. When all was done he owed a hundred thousand in accumulated debt.
8
八年，陝西餘匪自南山竄出棧道，清馳扼廣元，遣卒招撫被戕，詔斥輕信縱賊，以前功免罪，命理糧餉及搜捕餘匪、裁撤鄉勇。 十年，事竣入覲，賜御製詩，有曰：「循吏清名遐邇傳，蜀民何幸見青天！ 誠心到處能和眾，本性從來不愛錢。 」時以異數榮之。 丁繼母憂，去官，服闋，授山西按察使，遷布政使。 忤巡撫初彭齡，劾其袒護屬吏，降四級，以從四品京堂用。 清亦自陳不勝籓司之任，詔斥冒昧，降補刑部員外郎。 熱河新設理刑司員，以清往，邊方草創，多持大體，斷獄平允，蒙民亦以青天呼之。
In the eighth year Shaanxi remnant rebels broke out of the Southern Mountains along the plank road. Qing rushed to hold Guangyuan and sent men to negotiate their surrender, but they were killed in action. An edict rebuked him for trusting rebels too readily, yet his earlier service spared him punishment and he was ordered to manage supplies, hunt down stragglers, and disband the militia. In the tenth year, when his duties were finished, he was received in audience and given an imperial poem that read: "The upright magistrate's fame spreads far and wide—how fortunate are the people of Shu to behold the blue sky!" "Wherever his sincere heart goes he wins the people; by nature he has never loved money." At the time he was honored with this exceptional mark of favor. After mourning his stepmother he left office, and when the mourning period ended was made surveillance commissioner of Shanxi and then provincial treasurer. He clashed with Governor Chu Pengling, who impeached him for shielding subordinates. He was demoted four ranks and given a fourth-rank capital post. Qing also confessed himself unfit for provincial treasurer's duties. An edict rebuked his presumption and he was demoted to vice director in the Ministry of Justice. When Rehe created a new judicial post for criminal cases, Qing was sent there. The frontier was still being organized, and he generally ruled by broad principles. His judgments were fair, and the Mongols too called him Blue-Sky.
9
十七年，授山東鹽運使。 十八年，河南教匪起，山東賊黨硃成良等應之，陷定陶、曹縣，巡撫同興恇懼，清自請將兵。 承平久，兵習晏安，清躡草屩先之，以五百人敗賊於仿山，复定陶，又敗之於韓家廟，殪賊二千，進攻扈家集，縱火焚柵，賊突出皆死，誅賊首硃成良、王奇山，自滑縣奔至者並殲焉，兩閱月而事平。 賊初起時，煽惑甚眾，清先解散其脅從，成良勢孤不得逞，故得速定​​。 上嘉其以文職身先士卒，特詔褒獎，加布政使銜。 尋授雲南布政使，仍留舊任。
In the seventeenth year he was appointed salt transport commissioner of Shandong. In the eighteenth year sectarian rebels broke out in Henan and Shandong partisans such as Zhu Chengliang joined them, capturing Dingtao and Caoxian. Governor Tongxing was alarmed, and Qing volunteered to take command. After long peace the troops had grown soft. Qing went ahead in straw sandals, led five hundred men to defeat the rebels at Fangshan, retook Dingtao, and routed them again at Hanjiamiao, killing two thousand. At Hujiaji he burned the stockade; the rebels who rushed out were all killed. Zhu Chengliang and Wang Qishan were executed, fugitives from Huaxian were wiped out, and within two months the rising was over. At first the rebels had drawn a huge following, but Qing first broke up their coerced supporters, leaving Chengliang isolated and unable to act, which allowed a swift settlement. The emperor praised a civil official who led from the front, issued a special commendation, and gave him provincial-treasurer rank. He was soon appointed provincial treasurer of Yunnan while remaining in his former post.
10
清性坦率，厭苛禮，不合於上官，又不耐簿書錢穀，遂乞病，上亦知之，改授山東登州鎮總兵，調曹州鎮。 道光二年，以老休致，命在籍食全俸。 八年，卒，賜祭葬，祀山東名宦，官其孫熾昌為兵部主事； 瑩，舉人。
Frank by nature, Qing disliked stiff ceremony, got on poorly with superiors, and had no patience for paperwork and accounts, so he asked to retire on grounds of illness. The emperor understood and made him regional commander of Dengzhou Garrison in Shandong, then transferred him to Caozhou Garrison. In the second year of Daoguang he retired on account of age and was granted his full salary at home. In the eighth year he died. Imperial funeral rites were granted, he was entered in Shandong's shrine of distinguished officials, and his grandson Chichang was given a secretaryship in the Ministry of War; Ying had passed the provincial examination.
11
傅鼐，字重庵，順天宛平人，原籍浙江山陰。 由吏員入貲為府經歷，發雲南，擢寧洱知縣。 乾隆末，福康安徵苗疆，調赴湖南軍營司餉運，晉秩同知直隸州，賜花翎。
Fu Nai, style name Chong'an, came from Wanping in Shuntian; his ancestral home was Shanyin in Zhejiang. Starting as a clerk, he bought his way into a prefectural registrar's post, was posted to Yunnan, and was promoted to magistrate of Ning'er. Late in Qianlong, when Fuk'anggan campaigned in the Miao country, Nai was transferred to the Hunan army to manage supplies, promoted to subprefect of Zhili Prefecture, and awarded the peacock feather.
12
嘉慶元年，授鳳凰廳同知。 治當苗衝，會大軍移徵湖北教匪，降苗要求苗地歸苗，當事議允之。 鼐知愈撫且愈驕，乃招流亡，團丁壯，於要害築碉堡，防苗出沒。 苗以死力來攻，且戰且修，閱三年而碉堡成。 有哨台以守望，砲台以御敵，邊牆相接百餘裡。 每警，哨台舉銃角，婦女、牲畜立歸堡，環數十里皆戒嚴。 四年，擒苗酋吳陳受，加知府銜。 巡撫姜晟疏薦鼐能勝艱鉅，方治鎮筸一帶荒田，均給丁壯，請俟事竣送部。 時鎮筸左、右營黑苗最為邊患，五年，跴金塘苗出掠瀘溪，偕總兵富志那夜分三路搗其巢，伏兵隘路苟岩要擊，痛殲之，斃首逆吳尚保，苗始奪氣。 詔嘉獎，命在任食知府俸。
In the first year of Jiaqing he was made subprefect of Fenghuang Department. His district lay on the Miao frontier. When the main army marched off against the Hubei sect rebels, the pacified Miao demanded their lands back, and the authorities agreed. Nai saw that indulgence only made the Miao bolder. He gathered refugees, mustered able-bodied men, and built blockhouses at key points to check Miao raids. The Miao attacked with desperate force, but he fought on while building, and after three years the blockhouse line was complete. Watch towers stood guard, artillery platforms faced the enemy, and border walls ran together for more than a hundred li. At every alarm the watch towers sounded muskets and horns; women and livestock were rushed into the forts, and strict guard was kept for miles around. In the fourth year he captured the Miao chief Wu Chenshou and was given prefectural rank. Governor Jiang Sheng memorialized that Nai was equal to the hardest tasks, noting that he was reclaiming wasteland around Zhenqiong and distributing it among able-bodied men, and asked that he be forwarded to the ministry when the work was done. The Black Miao of Zhenqiong's left and right camps were then the worst frontier menace. In the fifth year the Caijintang Miao raided Luxi. Nai joined Regional Commander Fu Zhina in a night attack in three columns on their stronghold, ambushed them at the Gouyan pass, crushed them, killed the rebel chief Wu Shangbao, and broke Miao morale. An edict praised him and ordered that he draw a prefect's salary while remaining in post.
13
六年，貴州苗复亂，湖南環苗地東、南、北三面七百餘裡，其西二百餘裡接貴州，未設備。 石峴苗煽十四寨糾湖南苗叛，鼐率鄉勇千五百馳赴銅仁。 貴州巡撫伊桑阿以招撫戡定上聞，各寨實尚沸然，槍械未繳。 總督琅玕至，急檄鼐會剿崖屯溝，黔兵攻其前，鼐夜由山徑入，連破五巢。 上下湖山峽尤險，夜分兵圍攻，至次日克之，火其寨。 三日中盡破諸寨，殲苗二千有奇。 仿湖南法，建碉堡守之。 伊桑阿因冒功誤邊伏法，錄鼐功，加道銜，總理邊務，並命以苗疆道員用。 七年，丁父憂，詔鼐辦理邊防善後，民、苗悅服，難易生手，命留任。 初，鼐建議遷永綏城於花園，副將營於茶洞，而貴州方藉永綏為聲援，尼其事。 至是詔琅玕察奏，乃赴銅仁面陳永綏孤懸苗中，形如釜底，有二難、三可慮； 並請移湖南守備於貴州邊境螺螄堡，以為犄角，乃決議移之。 既而群苗率眾來爭，鼐率鄉勇深入，苗大集，環之數重，以奇計突圍出。 尋議勒繳槍械，苗酋石崇四等抗命，並阻丈田，十年，與其黨石貴銀糾眾數千來犯，敗之夯都河，追至孟陽岡，殲賊甚眾，生擒石崇四、石貴銀。 是役因賊戕良苗，故得用苗兵深入，戰月餘，破寨十六，餘皆乞降，永綏苗遂平。 廳屬高都、兩頭羊二寨皆震懾，無敢抗。 事聞，予優敘，擢辰沅永靖道。
In the sixth year the Guizhou Miao rebelled again. Hunan's Miao frontier ran more than seven hundred li on the east, south, and north, while the western two hundred li bordering Guizhou had no defenses in place. The Shixian Miao stirred fourteen stockades to join Hunan Miao in revolt. Nai rushed to Tongren at the head of fifteen hundred militia. Guizhou Governor Yisang'a reported that pacification had ended the trouble, but the stockades were still in turmoil and arms had not been turned in. Governor-General Langgan arrived and urgently summoned Nai to join the attack on Yaitungou. Guizhou troops struck from the front while Nai entered by a mountain path at night and took five nests in succession. The Shangxiahu Mountain gorge was especially dangerous. He split his forces for a night assault, took it the next day, and burned the stockade. Within three days he had overrun every stockade and killed more than two thousand Miao. Following the Hunan model, he built blockhouses to hold the ground. Yisang'a was executed for false claims that had harmed the frontier. Nai's achievements were recorded, he was given circuit-intendant rank and put in charge of frontier affairs, and ordered to serve as a Miao frontier official. In the seventh year he entered mourning for his father. An edict ordered Nai to manage frontier defense and its aftermath; people and Miao alike were content under him, and because the work would have been hard for a newcomer he was ordered to stay on. Earlier Nai had proposed moving Yongshui city to Huayuan and placing the vice commander's camp at Chadong, but Guizhou, which relied on Yongshui as a rear base, blocked the move. An edict then ordered Langgan to investigate and report. Nai went to Tongren and explained in person that Yongshui stood isolated among the Miao like the bottom of a cauldron, with two grave difficulties and three serious concerns; he also asked to shift the Hunan garrison commander to Luosi Fort on the Guizhou border as a supporting flank, and the relocation was approved. The Miao then massed to resist. Nai led militia deep into their country, was surrounded in several rings, and broke out by a clever stratagem. A policy was then adopted to compel the surrender of arms. Chiefs such as Shi Chongsi resisted and blocked land surveys. In the tenth year Shi Chongsi and his follower Shi Guiyin mustered several thousand men to attack. Nai defeated them at Hangdu River, pursued to Mengyanggang, killed many, and took both chiefs alive. Because the rebels had killed loyal Miao, he was able to use Miao auxiliaries on deep raids. After more than a month of fighting he took sixteen stockades; the rest surrendered, and the Yongshui Miao were pacified. The Gaodu and Liangtouyang stockades under his jurisdiction were cowed into submission and dared not resist. When the report reached court he received exceptional recognition and was promoted to intendant of Chenyuan Yongjing Circuit.
14
鼐治苗專用雕剿法，大小百戰，所用僅鄉勇數千。 苗人於穹山峭壁驀越如平地，無部伍行列，伏箐中從暗擊明，銃銳且長，隨山起伏，多命中。 鼐因苗地用苗技訓練士卒，囊沙輕走，習藤牌閃躍，狹路則用短兵。 每戰後輒嚴汰，數年始得精卒千，號「飛隊」，風雨不亂行列，遺資道路無反顧，甘苦與共，是以能致死。
In pacifying the Miao Nai relied chiefly on blockhouse warfare, fought more than a hundred engagements large and small, and used only a few thousand militia. The Miao crossed sheer cliffs and high mountains as if on flat ground, without formal ranks. They hid in the thickets and struck from cover; their long muskets, fired along the rise and fall of the hills, seldom missed. Nai trained his men in Miao country by Miao methods—running with sand-filled sacks for lightness, drilling in rattan-shield dodging, and using short weapons on narrow paths. After every battle he ruthlessly weeded out weak men. Only after several years did he have a thousand elite troops, the "Flying Corps," who kept formation in wind and rain, abandoned baggage on the road without a backward glance, and shared every hardship with their commander—men who would fight to the death.

先是議興屯田，上書巡撫高杞曰：「防邊之道，兵民相輔。 湖南苗疆，環以鳳凰、永綏、乾州、古丈坪、保靖五廳縣，犬牙相錯，營汛相距各數里。 元年班師後苗擾如故，鼐竭心籌之，制勝無如碉堡。 募丁壯數千，與苗從事。 來則痛擊，去則修邊，前戈矛，後邪許。 得險即守，寸步而前，然後苗銳挫望絕。 湖南自乙卯二載用兵，耗帑七百餘萬。 國家經費有常，頑苗叛服無定。 募勇不得不散，則碉堡不得不虛； 後患不得不慮，則自圖不得不亟。 通力合作，且耕且戰，所以招亡拯患也。 均田屯丁，自養自衛，所以一勞永逸也。 相其距苗遠近、碉堡疏密，為屯田多少：鳳凰廳碉堡八百，需丁四千輪守，並留千人備戰，需田三萬餘畝； 乾州廳碉堡九十餘，守丁八百，屯田三千餘畝； 保靖縣碉堡四十餘，守丁三百，屯田千五百餘畝； 古丈坪廳苗馴，止設碉堡十餘，守丁百，屯田五百餘畝； 永綏廳新建碉堡百餘，留勇丁二千，亦屯田萬畝：而後邊無餘隙，環苗以成圈圍之勢，峻國防、省國計也。 異族逼處，非碉堡無以固，碉堡非勇丁無以守，勇丁非屯田無以贍。 邊民瀕近鋒鏑，固原割世業而保身家； 後路同資屏蔽，亦樂捐有餘以補不足。 所募土丁，非其子弟即其親族。 距邊稍遠者，仍佃本戶輸租，視古來屯戍以客卒雜處，勢燕越矣。 與其一旦散數千驍健無業子弟流為盜賊，何如收駕輕就熟之用而不費大帑一錢？ 惟執事圖之！ 」於是收叛產分給無業窮苗佃種。
Earlier, when frontier garrison farming was debated, he wrote to Governor Gao Qi: "To defend the border, soldiers and civilians must work in tandem. Hunan's Miao frontier is ringed by five prefectural units—Fenghuang, Yongshou, Qianzhou, Guzhangping, and Baojing—jigsawed together, with military posts standing several li apart. After the troops withdrew in the first year, Miao raids resumed unchanged. Nai threw himself into the problem and concluded that blockhouses were the surest way to prevail. He recruited several thousand able-bodied men to take on the Miao. When the Miao attacked, he met them with force; when they withdrew, he rebuilt the frontier—spears forward, work songs behind. He seized and held every strong point, advancing inch by inch, until the Miao's fighting spirit broke and their hopes died. Hunan had fought for two years since the yimao year, spending more than seven million taels from the treasury. State finances are finite, but the stubborn Miao alternate between rebellion and submission with no predictable pattern. When hired braves must be discharged, the blockhouses must inevitably stand empty; and when future trouble cannot be ignored, urgent measures become unavoidable. Pooling effort to farm and fight at once is how to reclaim the lost and relieve hardship. Allotting land to colonists who provision and defend themselves is how to solve the problem once and for all. He scaled garrison farms to distance from Miao settlements and blockhouse density: Fenghuang's eight hundred blockhouses needed four thousand rotating defenders, a thousand reserves, and over thirty thousand mu of farmland; Qianzhou: more than ninety blockhouses, eight hundred men, and over three thousand mu; Baojing: over forty blockhouses, three hundred men, and fifteen hundred mu; Guzhangping, where the Miao were already tame, needed only a dozen blockhouses, a hundred men, and five hundred mu; Yongshou added a hundred new blockhouses and two thousand braves with ten thousand mu—closing every gap behind the line, ringing the Miao, strengthening the frontier, and saving the treasury. Alien peoples on the doorstep require blockhouses to hold ground, braves to man them, and garrison farms to feed them. Frontier families under the shadow of war would gladly surrender ancestral holdings to save their lives; and inland communities that gained the same protection were willing to give from their surplus to cover the shortfall. The local militia he recruited were either men's own sons or their close kin. Men posted farther back still farmed their own family land and paid rent to it—a far cry from the old frontier system of mixing unrelated garrison troops, as alien to one another as northerners and southerners. Better to harness thousands of tough, jobless young men who already knew the work than to discharge them into banditry—and do it without spending a single string from the treasury. I beg Your Excellency to weigh this plan. The memorial bore fruit: rebel estates were confiscated and leased to penniless Miao with no other means of support.

自擒石崇四，餘匪原返侵地，永綏得萬餘畝，乾州、鳳凰二廳次之，乃續墾沿邊隙地二萬畝，曰「官墾田」，贖苗質民田萬餘畝，曰「官贖田」。 以廩屯官授屯長，給老幼，籌補助，備犒賞，暨歲修城堡、神祠、學校、育嬰、養濟諸費。 復以兵威勒交苗佔民田三萬五千餘畝，苗自獻田七千餘畝。 其經費田則佃租變價，屯丁田則附碉躬耕，訓練講武，設屯田守備掌之，轄於兵備道。 屯政舉，使兵農為一以相衛，民、苗為二以相安。 與官及兵民約曰：「毋擅入苗寨，毋稍役苗夫。 」與苗約曰：「毋巫鬼椎牛群飲以糜財，毋挾槍矛尋睚眥釀釁。 」請乾、鳳、永、保四廳編立邊字號，廣鄉試中額一名； 苗生編立田字號，加中額一名，苗益感奮。 十三年，屯務竣，入覲，詔曰：「傅鼐任苗疆十餘年，鋤莠安良，興利除弊，建碉堡千有餘所，屯田十二萬餘畝，收卹難民十餘萬戶，練兵八千人，收繳苗寨兵器四萬餘件； 又多方化導，設書院六，義學百，近日苗民向學，革面革心。 朕久聞其任勞任怨，不顧身家。 今召見，果安詳諳練，明白誠實，洵為傑出之才，堪為岩疆保障。 其加按察使銜，以風有位。 」
After the capture of Shi Chongsi, surrendered rebels returned seized land—Yongshou gained more than ten thousand mu, Qianzhou and Fenghuang somewhat less—and Nai opened another twenty thousand mu along the border as "official reclamation," while redeeming over ten thousand mu of civilian land held as Miao collateral as "official redemption." Revenue from granary garrison land paid garrison chiefs, supported the aged and young, funded subsidies and rewards, and covered annual upkeep of forts, shrines, schools, orphanages, and relief houses. By force of arms he recovered more than thirty-five thousand mu of civilian land held by Miao; the Miao voluntarily yielded another seven thousand mu. Income fields were leased for cash rent; colonists farmed beside their blockhouses and drilled under a garrison-field commandant reporting to the circuit military intendant. With garrison policy in place, soldier and farmer became one for mutual defense, while Han and Miao remained distinct but at peace. He bound officials, troops, and settlers: "Do not enter Miao villages without leave; do not press Miao men into corvée lightly. " He bound the Miao: "Do not squander your substance on shamans, buffalo sacrifices, and drinking feasts; do not carry arms to settle grudges and stir up trouble. " He petitioned for frontier examination quotas in Qianzhou, Fenghuang, Yongshou, and Baojing, each with one extra provincial degree; and a separate quota for Miao candidates, which stirred them to study all the harder. In the thirteenth year, with the garrison project complete, he presented himself at court. The Emperor's edict read: "Fu Nai has served on the Miao frontier for more than ten years—clearing out ruffians, protecting the peaceable, promoting what helps and abolishing what harms. He has built more than a thousand blockhouses and opened a hundred and twenty thousand mu of garrison land; succored more than a hundred thousand refugee households; trained eight thousand troops; and collected more than forty thousand weapons from Miao villages; he has guided them by every means, founding six academies and a hundred charity schools. Lately the Miao have turned to learning and shown a change of heart. We have long heard that he bears every hardship without complaint and never spares himself. Now that We have met him, he proves composed, seasoned, candid, and honest—a truly outstanding man fit to guard a critical frontier. Grant him the rank of surveillance commissioner, as an example to officials everywhere. "

十四年，擢湖南按察使。 苗人籥留，命每年秋一赴苗疆撫慰邊人。 鼐在苗疆，設木匭於門，訴者投牒其中，夜出閱之，黎明起視事，剖決立盡。 兵民白事，直至榻前。 及為按察使，一如同知時。 下無壅情，事無不舉。 十五年，兼署布政使。 十六年，卒於官，仁宗深悼惜，詔謂：「倚畀方隆，正欲簡任疆寄。 加恩贈巡撫銜，照贈官賜卹，賜祭一壇。 」苗疆建專祠，祀湖南名宦。 光緒中，追諡壯肅。
In the fourteenth year he was promoted to Hunan surveillance commissioner. The Miao begged him to stay; the court ordered him to visit the frontier once each autumn to comfort the border population. In the Miao country Nai placed a wooden complaint box at his gate. Petitioners dropped their papers inside; he read them at night, rose at dawn to hold court, and disposed of every case before the day was out. Soldiers and civilians brought their business to his bedside. As surveillance commissioner he worked exactly as he had as subprefect. No complaint was buried; no task left undone. In the fifteenth year he served concurrently as acting provincial treasurer. In the sixteenth year he died in office. Emperor Renzong mourned him deeply. The edict read: "Our reliance on him had only just deepened; We were on the point of entrusting him with a frontier command. Extend grace: posthumously grant him the rank of provincial governor, award the usual mourning gifts, and bestow one state sacrifice. The Miao frontier raised a shrine to him, enrolling him among Hunan's eminent officials. Under Guangxu he received the posthumous name Zhuangsu ("Stalwart and Solemn").

初，鼐排眾議以事攻剿，為大吏所惎，將中以開邊釁罪。 監司阿意，旁掣其肘，鎮筸總兵富志那獨保全之。 富志那從征金川，習知山碉設險之利，鼐實從受之，卒以成功。 鼐歿後，二妾寡居，饣於粥不給，其廉操尤著雲。
Early on Nai had defied majority opinion to press the attack; a senior official bore him a grudge and nearly had him charged with provoking a border war. Circuit officials curried favor and pulled at his sleeve; only Fu Zhina, the Zhenqian commander-in-chief, shielded him. Fu Zhina had fought in Jinchuan and knew the value of mountain blockhouses; Nai learned the method from him and succeeded by it. After Nai died his two concubines lived as widows on thin gruel they could scarcely afford—proof of his integrity that men still speak of.

嚴如熤，字炳文，湖南溆浦人。 年十三，補諸生，舉優貢。 研究輿圖、兵法、星卜之書，尤留心兵事。
Yan Ruyi, style name Bingwen, was from Xupu in Hunan. At thirteen he became a licentiate; later he was nominated as an outstanding tribute scholar. He studied cartography, military science, and astrology, but kept his keenest eye on military affairs.

乾隆六十年，貴州苗亂，湖南巡撫姜晟闢佐幕，上平苗​​議十二事，言宜急复乾州，進永綏，與保靖、松桃、鎮筸聲勢可通。 攻乾州道瀘溪，必先得大小章。 大小章者，故土司遺民，名曰仡佬，驍健，與苗世仇。 如熤募能仡佬語者往，開示利害，挾其酋六人出，推誠與同臥起，乃送質，率其屬陽投乾州為內應，約一舉破賊，因黔師牽掣未果。 次年，卒賴其眾，救兩鎮兵於河溪。 後復平隴，戰花園，皆為軍鋒。 大小章於大府檄或不受，必得如熤手書始行雲。
In Qianlong year 60, when Guizhou's Miao rose in revolt, Hunan Governor Jiang Sheng took him onto his staff. Yan submitted twelve proposals for pacifying the Miao, urging the swift recovery of Qianzhou and an advance on Yongshou so that Baojing, Songtao, and Zhenqian could link their forces. The road to Qianzhou through Luxi required first winning over the Daxiaozhang. The Daxiaozhang were descendants of former native chieftains, called Gelao—fierce fighters and hereditary enemies of the Miao. Ruyi found a Gelao speaker and sent him to explain the stakes. He brought out six chiefs, shared their bed and board in token of trust, took hostages, and persuaded their people to feign submission to Qianzhou as inside allies—a plan to crush the rebels in one blow that Guizhou's armies held up and foiled. The following year their warriors at last rescued two garrison forces at Hexi. They again led the van at the pacification of Long and the battle of Huayuan. The Daxiaozhang might ignore a grand coordinator's orders, but would move only on a personal letter from Ruyi.

嘉慶五年，舉孝廉方正。 廷試平定川、楚、陝三省方略策，如熤對幾萬言，略謂：「軍興數載，師老財匱。 以數万罷憊之眾，與猾賊追逐數千里長林深谷中。 投誠之賊，無地安置，則已降复亂； 流離之民，生活無資，則良亦從亂。 鄉勇戍卒，多遊手募充。 慮一旦兵撤餉停，則反思延亂。 如此，則亂何由弭？ 臣愚以為莫若仿古屯田之法。 三省自遭蹂躪，叛亡各產不下億萬畝，舉流民降賊之無歸、鄉勇戍卒之無業者，悉編入屯，團練捍衛，計可養勝兵數十萬。 餉省而兵增，化盜為民，計無逾此。 」仁宗親擢第一。 次日，召詣軍機處詢屯政，复條上十二事。 召見，以知縣發陝西。 下其疏於三省大吏，令採行。
In Jiaqing year 5 he was nominated as Filial and Incorrupt. At the palace examination on strategy for pacifying Sichuan, Huguang, and Shaanxi, Ruyi wrote nearly ten thousand characters. In essence he argued: "The war has dragged on for years; the army is exhausted and the treasury empty. Tens of thousands of spent soldiers are chasing cunning rebels through thousands of li of forest and ravine. Surrendered rebels have nowhere to go, so men who have already submitted rise again; and displaced civilians with no livelihood join them. Local militia and garrison troops are largely recruited from the idle and disorderly. Once the army withdraws and pay stops, they will have every reason to keep the turmoil alive. Under these conditions, how can rebellion ever end? I humbly believe the answer is to revive the ancient garrison-farming system. The three provinces, ravaged by war, hold hundreds of millions of mu abandoned by rebels and refugees. Enroll every homeless refugee, every surrendered bandit, every idle militiaman and discharged soldier into garrison colonies, drill them for defense, and you could maintain several hundred thousand effective troops. Payroll shrinks while strength grows; bandits become farmers. No policy surpasses it. Emperor Renzong ranked his paper first. The next day he was summoned to the Grand Council to discuss garrison policy and submitted twelve detailed proposals. After audience he was sent to Shaanxi as a county magistrate. His memorial went to the governors of all three provinces with orders to put it into practice.

六年，補洵陽，縣在萬山中，與湖北邊界相錯，兵賊往來如織。 時方厲行堅壁清野，如熤於築堡練團，措置尤力。 賊至無可掠，去則抄其尾。 又擇堅寨當衝者，儲糧供給官軍。 徐天德、樊人傑敗於張家坪，因馬鞍寨阻其前，故不得竄。 楊遇春破張天倫，亦賴太平寨夾擊之力。 以功加知州銜，賜花翎。 八年，擊湖北逸匪於蜀河口，斬王祥，擒方孝德，晉秩同知直隸州。 新設定遠廳，即以如熤補授。 九年，建新城，复於西南百餘裡黎壩、漁渡壩築二石城為犄角。 治團如洵陽，賊至輒殲，先後擒陳心元、馮世週。 丁母憂，大吏議留任，辭不可，服闋，十三年，補潼關廳。 尋擢漢中知府。 兵燹後，民困兵驕，散勇逸匪，心猶未革。 如熤聯營伍，立保甲，治堡寨，問民疾苦。 興勸農事，行區田法，教紡織，使務本計。 修復褒城山河堰及城固五門、楊填二堰，各灌田數万畝，他小堰百餘，皆履勘濬治，水利普興。 复漢中書院，親臨講授。 於華州渭南開諭悍回，縛獻亡命數十人； 於寧羌解散湖北流民； 於城固擒教首陳恆義：皆治渠魁，寬脅從。 令行禁止，人心帖服，南山遂大定。
In year 6 he took Xunyang—a county lost in ten thousand hills, its border tangled with Hubei, soldiers and bandits crossing like shuttles on a loom. The government was enforcing scorched-earth fortification; Ruyi threw himself especially hard into building forts and drilling militia. When rebels arrived they found nothing to loot; when they withdrew he harried their rear. He stocked grain in strong stockades on the main routes to feed government troops. Xu Tiande and Fan Renjie were beaten at Zhangjiaping; Ma'an Stockade blocked their path and they could not escape. Yang Yuchun's defeat of Zhang Tianlun likewise depended on a flanking strike from Taiping Stockade. For his service he received the rank of subprefect and the peacock feather. In year 8 he attacked Hubei fugitive bandits at Shuhekou, beheaded Wang Xiang, captured Fang Xiaode, and was promoted to subprefect of Zhili Prefecture. When Dingyuan Department was newly created, Ruyi was appointed to fill the post. In year 9 he built a new city and, a hundred li southwest at Liba and Yuduba, two stone forts as pincers. He organized militia as at Xunyang; rebels who came were destroyed; he captured Chen Xinyuan and Feng Shizhou. On his mother's death superiors urged him to stay in office; he refused. After mourning, in year 13 he took Tongguan Department. Soon after he was promoted to prefect of Hanzhong. After the war the people were ruined, the troops insolent, and discharged braves and roaming bandits had not yet changed their ways. Ruyi reorganized the camps, set up baojia registers, repaired stockades, and went among the people to hear their grievances. He promoted farming, introduced field-allotment farming, taught spinning and weaving, and turned people back to honest livelihood. He restored Baocheng's Shanhe Weir and Chenggu's Wumen and Yangtian weirs, each watering tens of thousands of mu, and personally surveyed and dredged more than a hundred smaller weirs until irrigation flourished throughout the prefecture. He revived the Hanzhong Academy and taught there in person. At Weinan in Huazhou he persuaded the unruly Hui factions and delivered several dozen fugitives bound; at Ningqiang he sent home displaced people from Hubei; at Chenggu he seized the sect leader Chen Hengyi—in each case punishing the chiefs while sparing those who had been compelled to follow. His orders were obeyed and his prohibitions heeded; the people submitted, and the Southern Mountains were finally at peace.
23
道光元年，擢陝安道。 會廷議川、楚、陝邊防建設事宜，下三省察勘，以如熤任其事，週曆相度，析官移治，增營改汛，建城口、白河、磚坪、太平、佛坪五廳，移駐文武。 奏上，報可。 如熤嘗言：「山內州縣距省遠，多推諉牽掣。 宜仿古梁州自為一道及明鄖陽巡撫之制，專設大員鎮撫，割三省州縣以附益之，庶勢專權一，可百世無患。 」以更張重大，未竟其議。 三年，宣宗以如熤在陝年久，熟於南山情形，任事以來，地方安靖，特詔嘉獎，加按察使銜，以示旌異。 巡撫盧坤尤重之，採其議增廳治於盩厔、洋縣界，增營汛於商州及略陽； 檄勘全秦水利，於灃、涇、滻、渭諸川，鄭白、龍首諸渠，規畫俱備。 社倉、義學，亦以次推行。 五年，擢貴州按察使，未到官。 六年，入覲，仍調陝西，抵任數日而卒，贈布政使。 陝民請比硃邑桐鄉故事，留葬南山，勿得，乃請祀名宦。 湖南亦祀鄉賢。
In 1821 he was promoted to intendant of the Shaan-An circuit. When the court took up border defenses along the Sichuan-Huguang-Shaanxi frontier, it commissioned surveys in all three provinces and put Ruyi in charge. He traveled the region end to end, reorganized county seats, reinforced garrison posts, created five new subprefectures at Chengkou, Baihe, Zhuanping, Taiping, and Foping, and redeployed civil and military officials to staff them. His memorial was submitted and approved. Ruyi once argued: "Mountain counties lie far from provincial capitals, and officials endlessly shift blame and thwart one another. We should revive the ancient Liang circuit and the Ming office of Xunyang grand coordinator—a senior official devoted solely to the region, with counties carved from all three provinces placed under him—so power would be concentrated and the trouble ended for generations. " The scale of reform was deemed too great, and his plan never went forward. In 1823 the Daoguang Emperor noted that Ruyi had served many years in Shaanxi, knew the Southern Mountains intimately, and had kept the region quiet throughout his tenure. He issued a special commendation and granted him the nominal rank of surveillance commissioner as exceptional recognition. Governor Lu Kun prized him highly, followed his advice to establish a new subprefecture on the Zhouzhi–Yangxian border, and reinforced garrisons at Shangzhou and Lueyang; he ordered a province-wide survey of irrigation, and drew up comprehensive plans for the Fen, Jing, Chan, and Wei rivers and the Zheng-Bai and Longshou canals. Community granaries and charity schools were introduced in turn. In 1825 he was promoted to surveillance commissioner of Guizhou but never took up the appointment. In 1826 he was called to court, reassigned to Shaanxi, took office, and died within days; he was posthumously granted the rank of provincial treasurer. The people of Shaanxi asked, by the example of Zhu Yi of Tongxiang, to bury him in the Southern Mountains; when refused, they petitioned for his place in the shrine of distinguished officials. Hunan enshrined him among its local worthies as well.
24
如熤自為縣令至臬司，皆出特擢。 在漢中十餘年不調，得成其鎮撫南山之功。 宣宗每論疆吏才，必首及之。 將大用，已不及待。 為人性豪邁，去邊幅，泊榮利，視之如田夫野老。 於輿地險要，如聚米畫沙。 所規畫常在數十年外，措施略見所著書。 嘗佐那彥成籌海寇，有洋防備覽； 佐姜晟籌苗疆，有苗防備覽； 佐傅鼐籌屯田，有屯防書。 又有三省邊防備覽，漢江南北、三省山內各圖，漢中府誌及樂園詩文集。
Every step from county magistrate to provincial judge, Ruyi rose by special appointment. More than ten years in Hanzhong without reassignment let him finish pacifying the Southern Mountains. Whenever the Daoguang Emperor spoke of frontier talent, Ruyi's name came first. The throne meant to promote him further, but death came too soon. By nature he was bold and unceremonious, indifferent to rank and gain, and comported himself like an ordinary peasant. On strategic terrain he could lay out the lie of the land as easily as piling grain or tracing lines in sand. His designs looked decades ahead; his practical measures are outlined in his books. He assisted Na Yancheng against coastal pirates and wrote the Maritime Defense Survey; he helped Jiang Sheng on the Miao frontier and wrote the Miao Frontier Defense Survey; he assisted Fu Nai on military colonies and wrote the Colony Defense Treatise. He also produced the Three-Provinces Border Defense Survey, maps of the Han valley and the interior mountains, the Hanzhong Gazetteer, and the Leyuan collected writings.

子正基，原名芝，字山舫。 副貢生。 少隨父練習吏事。 道光中，官河南知縣，有聲。 擢鄭州知州。 治賈魯河，息水患。 河決開封，正基佐守護。 治河兵獄，雪其冤，得河兵死力，城賴以完。 母憂歸，服闋，補奉天復州。 興屯練，捕盜有法，民殺盜者勿論。 奉天治吏素弛，府尹下所屬，以正基為法，盜風為戢。 引疾去。 江南大吏疏調，擢授常州知府。 二十九年，大水，勘災勤至，郡人感之，輸錢二十餘萬助賑，全活甚眾。 累署淮揚道、按察使。 咸豐初，侍郎曾國籓、呂賢基交章薦之，命赴廣西治軍需，授右江道。 擢河南布政使，留廣西。 時粵匪披猖，將帥齟《齒吾》，師久無功。 正基曲為調和，疏論其事，謂：「師克在和，事期共濟。 統兵大帥與地方大吏，宜定紛更不齊之勢，聯疏闊難合之情。 布德信以服人心，明功罪以揚士氣。 勿因賊盛而生推諉，勿因兵單而務自救，勿以小忿而不為應援，勿以偶挫而坐觀成敗。 庶逆氛可殄，大功可成。 」時以為讜言。 二年，桂林圍解，賜花翎。 尋隨大軍赴湖北，時武昌初复，命馳往撫卹難民，署湖北布政使。 調廣東，复赴廣西清覈軍需。 內召授通政副使，遷通政使。 七年，引疾歸，卒。
His son Zheng Ji, born Zhi, style name Shanfang. He held the rank of deputy tribute student. From youth he trained in administration at his father's side. During the Daoguang reign he served as magistrate in Henan and won a strong reputation. He was promoted to prefect of Zhengzhou. He improved the Jia-Lu River works and ended the flooding. When the Yellow River broke at Kaifeng, Zheng Ji aided the emergency defense. He investigated a scandal among the river-defense troops, cleared the men's names, and won such loyalty that the city held. After his mother's death he retired for mourning, then was appointed to Fuzhou in Fengtian. He raised militia on the colonies, policed bandits systematically, and exempted civilians who killed robbers from prosecution. Fengtian had long been slack in controlling officials; the prefect held Zheng Ji up as the standard for his subordinates, and banditry subsided. He resigned on grounds of illness. A senior Jiangnan official requested him by memorial, and he was appointed prefect of Changzhou. In 1849 a great flood struck; his relief surveys were tireless, and moved by his effort the prefecture donated more than two hundred thousand strings of cash for famine relief, saving countless lives. He served in succession as acting intendant of the Huai-Yang circuit and surveillance commissioner. Early in the Xianfeng reign Vice Ministers Zeng Guofan and Lü Xianji recommended him in joint memorials; he was sent to Guangxi to manage army supplies and made intendant of the Right River circuit. He was promoted to treasurer of Henan but kept on in Guangxi. Guangxi rebels were rampant, commanders feuded, and the campaign dragged on without victory. Zheng Ji worked to mediate and memorialized on the crisis: "Victory depends on unity, and the campaign requires shared effort. Field commanders and provincial governors must settle their clashing policies and bridge the distance between them. Win hearts through integrity and trust; make merit and blame clear to lift morale. Do not use the enemy's strength as an excuse to shift blame; do not hoard your few troops for self-preservation alone; do not withhold aid over petty quarrels; do not watch from the sidelines after a single setback. Only then can the rebellion be crushed and final victory won. " His contemporaries hailed it as forthright counsel. In 1852, when the siege of Guilin was lifted, he was awarded the peacock feather. He soon followed the main force to Hubei; Wuchang had just been recovered, and he was dispatched to care for refugees as acting treasurer of Hubei. Transferred to Guangdong, he returned again to Guangxi to audit army supplies. Recalled to the capital, he was made vice commissioner of the Transmission Office and then commissioner. In 1857 he retired for illness, returned home, and died.
26
論曰：亂之所由起與亂之所由平，亦在民之能治否耳。 教匪起於官逼民叛，其間獨一得民心之劉清，卒賴以招撫，助誅剿之成功。 徵苗頻煩大兵，而未杜亂源，傅鼐乃以一廳一道之力，剿撫兼施，岩疆綏定。 南山善後，嚴如熤始終其事，化榛莽為桑麻。 此其功皆在一時節鉞之上，光於史策矣。
The historian comments: Whether turmoil breaks out and whether it ends both turn on whether the people can be well governed. The sect rebels rose because officials drove the people to revolt; only Liu Qing, who won the people's trust, could in the end pacify them by persuasion and help complete their destruction. Campaigns against the Miao repeatedly mobilized large armies without stopping the trouble at its source; Fu Nai, with only one subprefecture and one circuit to work with, combined force and conciliation until the rugged frontier was secure. The Southern Mountains' recovery was Yan Ruyi's work from beginning to end—turning wilderness into farmland. Their achievements exceeded those of the generals of their day and deserve to blaze from the historical record.
