訥親傅恆子福靈安福隆安福隆安子丰紳濟倫福長安
Ne Qin; Fu Heng; Fu Ling'an and Fu Long'an; Feng Shenjilun, son of Fu Long'an; and Fu Chang'an
3
訥親，鈕祜祿氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人，額亦都曾孫。 父尹德，附見其父遏必隆傳，訥親其次子。 雍正五年，襲公爵，授散秩大臣。 十年，授鑾儀使。 十一年十二月，命在辦理軍機處行走。 十三年，世宗疾大漸，訥親預顧命。 高宗即位，莊親王允祿、果親王允禮、鄂爾泰、張廷玉輔政，號「總理王大臣」。 授訥親鑲白旗滿洲都統、領侍衛內大臣，協辦總理事務。 十二月，敕獎訥親勤慎，因推孝昭仁皇后外家恩，進一等公。 乾隆元年，遷鑲黃旗滿洲都統。 二年，遷兵部尚書。 十一月，莊親王等請罷總理事務，訥親授軍機大臣。 敘勞，加拖沙喇哈番世職。 三年二月，領戶部三庫。 九月，命協辦戶部。 直隸總督李衛劾總河硃藻詐欺貪虐，命訥親與尚書孫嘉淦勘讞，藻坐流。 訥親因與嘉淦條上永定河南北岸建築閘壩諸事。 十二月，遷吏部尚書。 四年五月，加太子太保。
Ne Qin, of the Niohuru clan, came from the Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner and was a great-grandson of E Yidu. His father Yin De is treated in the biography of Ebilun; Ne Qin was Ebilun's second son. In Yongzheng 5 he succeeded to the dukedom and was made an attendant minister without portfolio. In the tenth year he became director of the imperial equipage. In the twelfth month of the eleventh year he was assigned to the Grand Council. In the thirteenth year, as the Yongzheng Emperor lay critically ill, Ne Qin was included among those entrusted at the deathbed. After Qianlong took the throne, Prince Zhuang Yunlu, Prince Guo Yunli, Ortai, and Zhang Tingyu formed the regency styled the Grand Ministers in Charge of General Affairs. Ne Qin was made Manchu commander of the Bordered White Banner and chief grand minister of the imperial bodyguard, with a role in the general administration. That twelfth month an edict commended Ne Qin's diligence; citing the debt owed Empress Xiaozhaoren's family, the court raised him to a first-class duke. In Qianlong 1 he became Manchu commander of the Bordered Yellow Banner. In the second year he was made minister of war. In the eleventh month Prince Zhuang and the others petitioned to end the general-affairs regency, and Ne Qin was named a Grand Council minister. For his service he received a hereditary cavalry commandant's rank. In the second month of the third year he assumed charge of the Board of Revenue's three treasuries. In the ninth month he was assigned to assist the Board of Revenue. When Zhili governor-general Li Wei accused waterways director Zhu Zao of fraud and brutal corruption, Ne Qin and Minister Sun Jiaheng were sent to try the case; Zhu was banished. Ne Qin then joined Jiaheng in a detailed memorial on sluices and dams along both banks of the Yongding River. In the twelfth month he was made minister of personnel. In the fifth month of the fourth year he was made Guardian of the Heir Apparent.
4
訥親貴戚勳舊，少侍禁近，受世宗知，以為可大用。 迨高宗，恩眷尤厚。 訥親勤敏當上意，尤以廉介自敕，人不敢干以私。 其居第巨獒縛扉側，絕無車馬跡。 然以早貴，意氣驕溢，治事務刻深。 左都御史劉統勳疏論訥親領事過多，任事過銳。 上諭曰：「訥親為尚書，模棱推諉，固所不可，但治事未當，亦所不免，朕時時戒毋自滿。 今見此奏，益當自勉。 」語詳統勳傳。
A noble kinsman of an old military house, Ne Qin had served near the throne from youth; Yongzheng knew him well and believed he was fit for high office. Under Qianlong his favor was deeper still. Diligent and alert to the emperor's wishes, he above all held himself to strict integrity, and no one dared press him with private business. A great mastiff was tied beside the door of his house, and no carriage ever came near. Yet having risen young to high rank, he grew proud and overbearing, and in government he was relentlessly severe. Left censor-in-chief Liu Tongxun memorialized that Ne Qin held too many portfolios and drove his work too hard. The emperor replied: "As a minister Ne Qin must not hedge and pass the buck—that will not do—but it is also inevitable that his handling of affairs will sometimes miss the mark; I am always warning him not to grow complacent. Seeing this memorial, he should redouble his efforts." " The full exchange is recorded in Liu Tongxun's biography.
5
九年正月，命訥親閱河南、江南、山東諸省營伍，並勘海塘、河工。 時直隸天津、河間二府方以災治賑，令順道先往察覈。 疏請展賑一月，從之。 訥親使事既蕆，分疏上陳，其勘諸省營伍，言：「遍閱三省督撫、河漕、提鎮為標者十七，優絀互見。 惟河南南陽、江南蘇松水師二鎮最劣。 請下部覈賞罰。 」其勘江、浙海塘，言：「舊日浙江潮自蜀山中小亹出入，近海寧為北大亹，近蕭山為南大亹，漲沙寬闊，為杭州、紹興二府保障。 迨中小亹漸湮，潮趨蜀山北，震盪為患。 若濬中小亹故道，減大亹潮力，上下塘工悉可安堵； 即中小亹未可遽复，則當擇險要多為坦坡，木石戧壩，俾撇水積淤資以御潮。 至諸處柴塘，停沙阻水，無煩議改石工。 入江南境，地平而潮緩，華亭舊塘堅緻，寶山新塘尺度參差，工作又不中程。 金山、奉賢、南匯、上海皆土塘，距海稍遠，所司守護如法，當無他虞。 」其勘洪澤湖，請濬鹽河俾通江，疏串場河俾達海，並停天然二壩、高堰下游二堤。 其勘南旺湖，請以湖中涸地貸貧民耕稼。 別疏言：「各直省政事，督撫下司道，司道下州縣，州縣官惟以簿書錢穀為事，戶口貧富、土地肥瘠、物產豐嗇、民情向背、風俗美惡、以及山川原隰、橋樑道路，皆漫置不省。 官但有條教，民惟責納賦，浮文常多，實意殊少。 請敕各直省督撫，令州縣官遍歷境內，何事當興舉，何事當整飭，行之有無治效，以實報長官，長官即是為殿最，以實達朝廷。 似亦崇實效、去虛文、飭吏治、厚民生之一端也。 」皆下部議行。
In the first month of the ninth year he was sent to inspect garrisons in Henan, Jiangnan, and Shandong and to survey seawalls and river works. Tianjin and Hejian in Zhili were then under disaster relief, and he was told to go there first on his route. He asked to extend relief by a month, and the request was granted. When his tour was done he filed separate reports; on the garrisons he wrote: "Across the three provinces I reviewed seventeen governors, grain-transport and river officials, and regional commanders as benchmarks—some did well, some poorly. Only the Nanyang command in Henan and the Susong naval command in Jiangnan were the worst. I ask the ministries to verify this and fix rewards and punishments." On the Jiangsu and Zhejiang seawalls he reported: "Once the Zhejiang tide ran in and out through the middle channel at Shushan; near Haining lay the northern great channel and near Xiaoshan the southern, with wide sand bars that sheltered Hangzhou and Shaoxing. As the middle channel silted up, the tide swung north of Shushan and began to pound the coast. If the middle channel were dredged open again, the great channels' force would ease and the seawalls up and down the coast could be made secure; and even if the middle channel could not soon be reopened, gentle slopes and timber-and-stone spur dikes should be built at the danger points to deflect the water, let silt build up, and hold back the tide. As for the brushwood seawalls elsewhere, they trap sand and slow the water; there is no need to debate converting them to stone. In Jiangnan the land is flat and the tide gentle; Huating's old seawall is sound, but Baoshan's new wall varies in scale and the work does not meet specification. Jinshan, Fengxian, Nanhui, and Shanghai all rely on earthen walls set somewhat back from the sea; if their keepers maintain them properly, they should give no further trouble. On Hongze Lake he proposed dredging the salt canal to the Yangzi, clearing the Chuanchang canal to the sea, and suspending work on the two natural dams and the two dikes below Gaoyan. At Nanwang Lake he proposed lending reclaimed lake bed to the poor for farming. In another memorial he wrote: "In each province governors rule through intendants and they through magistrates, yet county officials care only for paperwork and taxes. Population, land, produce, popular sentiment, custom, terrain, bridges, and roads—all go unexamined. Officials issue rules; the people pay taxes—form fills the files, substance scarcely appears. I ask that governors order magistrates to tour their districts, report what should be built or reformed, and show results; superiors should rank them on that basis and report truthfully to the throne. That would be one way to prize results over paperwork, tighten rule, and improve people's lives." All were sent to the ministries for action.
6
十年三月，協辦大學士。 五月，授保和殿大學士，仍兼吏部尚書。 十二年四月，命如山西會巡撫愛必達讞萬全民張世祿、安邑民張遠等挾眾抗官狀，論如律。 愛必達及總兵羅俊、蒲州知府硃發等皆坐譴黜。 十三年正月，命如浙江會大學士高斌覆勘巡撫常安貪婪狀，未至，高斌鞫得常安實受賕，訥親與共奏，論如律。 三月，復命如山東會巡撫阿里袞治賑。
In the third month of the tenth year he became associate grand secretary. In the fifth month he was made grand secretary of the Hall of Preserving Harmony while keeping the ministry of personnel. In the fourth month of the twelfth year he went to Shanxi with Governor Aibidai to try Zhang Shilu of Wanquan, Zhang Yuan of Anyi, and others who had led mobs against the magistrates; they were sentenced by law. Aibidai, regional commander Luo Jun, Puzhou prefect Zhu Fa, and others were all reprimanded and removed. In the first month of the thirteenth year he was sent to Zhejiang with Grand Secretary Gao Bin to reinvestigate Governor Chang An's corruption; before Ne Qin arrived, Gao Bin had already proved the bribes, and together they memorialized for sentencing under law. In the third month he was again sent to Shandong with Governor Aligun to oversee relief.
7
時大金川土司莎羅奔攻革布什咱土司犯邊，上命川陝總督張廣泗討之。 大金川地絕險，阻山為石壘，名曰碉，師進攻弗克。 四月，召訥親還京師，授經略大臣，率禁旅出視師。 六月，訥親至軍，下令期三日克噶拉依，噶拉依者，莎羅奔結寨地也。 師循色爾力石梁而下，攻碉未即克，署總兵任舉勇敢善戰，為諸軍先，沒於陣。 訥親為氣奪，乃議督諸軍築碉，與敵共險，為持久。 疏入，上重失任舉，又以築碉非計，手詔戒訥親，因時度勢，以為進止。 訥親與廣泗合疏言：「天時地利皆賊得其長，我兵無機可乘。 冬春間當減兵駐守，明歲加調精銳三萬，於四月進剿，足以成功，至遲亦不逾秋令。 」訥親又別疏言：「來歲增兵，計需費數百萬。 若俟二三年後有機可乘，亦未可定。 」疏入，上諭曰：「卿等身在戎行，目擊情狀，不能確有成算，游移兩可。 朕於數千里外，何從遙度？ 我師至四萬，彼止三千餘，何以彼應我則有餘，我攻彼則不足？ 卿等當審定應攻應罷，毋為兩歧語。 」上知訥親不足辦敵，諭軍機大臣議召訥親還； 又念大金川非大敵，重臣視師，無功而還，傷國體，為四夷姍笑。 密以諭訥親，冀激奮克敵。 居數月，師雖有小胜，卒未得尺寸地。 訥親惟請還京面對，乃召訥親及廣泗詣京師，以岳鍾琪攝經略，傅爾丹攝川陝總督，復遣尚書班第同治軍事。 尋奪訥親官，令自具鞍馬，從討噶爾丹贖罪，逮廣泗。
The Greater Jinchuan chieftain Slob Dpon had attacked Gelebusizan and raided the frontier, and the emperor ordered Sichuan-Shaanxi governor-general Zhang Guangsi against him. Greater Jinchuan was desperately rugged; the tribes walled the mountains in stone forts called diao, and the army could not break them. In the fourth month Ne Qin was recalled, made commissioner-general, and sent out with the imperial guard to take command. In the sixth month he reached the front and ordered Galai, Slob Dpon's stronghold, taken within three days. The troops moved down the Serli stone ridge against the forts without immediate success; acting regional commander Ren Ju, bold in battle, led the van and was killed. Ne Qin lost heart and proposed that the army build diao of its own and settle into a long contest on the same deadly ground as the enemy. When the report arrived the emperor mourned Ren Ju deeply and judged that building diao was folly; he wrote Ne Qin in his own hand to weigh time and circumstance before advancing or holding back. Ne Qin and Zhang Guangsi jointly reported: "Season and terrain favor the enemy; our troops find no opening. We should cut forces to garrison strength through winter and spring, add thirty thousand elite troops next year, and strike in the fourth month—enough to win, and at latest by autumn." Ne Qin also wrote separately: "Reinforcements next year will cost millions. Whether an opening will appear in two or three years is equally uncertain." When the reports arrived the emperor replied: "You are on the spot and see the fighting yourselves, yet you offer no firm plan and hedge both ways. How can I judge from a thousand li away? We field forty thousand men against barely three thousand—why do they have strength to spare when we meet them, yet we fall short when we attack? Decide whether to fight or withdraw, and do not speak with two tongues." The emperor knew Ne Qin could not handle the enemy and told the Grand Council to discuss recalling him; yet Greater Jinchuan was no great power, and for a senior minister to command in person and return empty-handed would shame the state and invite the border peoples' scorn. He secretly urged Ne Qin on, hoping to stir him to victory. After several months the army won minor skirmishes but never took an inch of ground. Ne Qin could only ask to return for an audience; he and Zhang Guangsi were recalled, Yue Zhongqi was made acting commissioner-general, Fu Erdan acting governor-general of Sichuan-Shaanxi, and Minister Ban Di was again sent to the front. Soon Ne Qin was stripped of rank and told to furnish his own horse and follow the Dzungar campaign to atone; Zhang Guangsi was arrested.
8
九月，命大學士傅恆代為經略，別遣侍衛富成逮訥親，責置對，並令富成錄訥親舉止言語以聞。 上前後手詔罪訥親恆數千百言，略謂：「訥親受命總戎，乖張畏縮。 疏言軍夜攻碉，自帳中望見火光，知未嘗臨敵。 又言督軍攻阿利山，既回營，我軍數十人各鳥獸散。 知偶臨敵，又先士卒退。 富成疏訥親語'金川事大難，不可輕舉，此言不敢入奏'。 訥親受恩久，何事不可言？ 如固不能克，當實陳請罷兵。 乃事敗欲以不可輕舉歸過朝廷，狡詐出意外。 又值續調兵過，輒言'此皆我罪，令如許滿洲兵受苦'。 滿洲兵聞調，鼓舞振躍，志切同仇。 訥親以為受苦，實嫉他人成功，搖眾心，不顧國事。 孤恩藐法，罪不可逭。 」
In the ninth month Grand Secretary Fu Heng replaced him as commissioner-general; guard Fu Cheng was sent to seize Ne Qin for interrogation and to report his words and conduct. The emperor wrote thousands of words in his own hand condemning Ne Qin, saying in essence: "Ne Qin took command of the army yet was obstinate and timid. He wrote that when the army attacked the forts by night he watched the flames from his tent—proof he never faced the enemy. He also said that after he led the attack on Ali Mountain and returned to camp, dozens of our men scattered like frightened animals. So even when he did approach the fighting, he withdrew before the troops. Fu Cheng reported Ne Qin's words: "The Jinchuan war is desperately hard and must not be undertaken lightly—I dare not say this in a memorial." Ne Qin has enjoyed favor for years—what could he not say to the throne? If he truly could not win, he should have asked plainly to stop the war. Instead, after defeat he would blame the court with "must not be lightly undertaken"—cunning beyond belief. When fresh troops marched past he would say, "This is all my fault, making so many Manchu soldiers suffer." The Manchu troops, hearing they were called up, were eager and burning to fight. Ne Qin called it suffering, but in truth he envied others' success, undermined morale, and ignored the state's interest. Ungrateful and lawless, his crime admits no pardon. "]
9
十月，諭「訥親先世以軍功封二等公，為孝昭仁皇后戚屬，供職勤慎，進一等公。 獲罪，應仍以二等公俾其兄策楞襲爵」。 訥親恃上恩，尚冀入見上自解，上复迭降手詔，謂：「軍旅事重，平日治事詳慎，操守潔清，舉不足言。 」又謂：「訥親小心謹密，而方寸一壞，天奪其魄，雖欲倖免而不能。 」十二月，廣泗既誅，上封遏必隆遺刀授侍衛鄂實，監訥親還軍，誅以警眾。 十四年正月，上命傅恆班師，复諭鄂實即途中行法。 是月戊寅，鄂實監訥親行至班攔山，聞後命，遂誅訥親。
In the tenth month an edict ruled: "Ne Qin's house had been enfeoffed as a second-class duke for military merit; as kin of Empress Xiaozhaoren he served diligently and was raised to first-class duke. Having fallen, the rank should revert to second-class duke for his elder brother Celeng to inherit." Ne Qin still counted on imperial favor and hoped to explain himself in audience; the emperor again wrote in his own hand: "War is grave business—his careful administration and clean conduct in peacetime count for nothing. He added: "Ne Qin was careful in small things, but once his heart failed Heaven took his wits—he could not escape even if he wished." In the twelfth month, after Zhang Guangsi was executed, the emperor sealed Ebilun's heirloom blade and gave it to guard E Shi to escort Ne Qin back to the army and execute him as a warning. In the first month of the fourteenth year the emperor ordered Fu Heng to withdraw and told E Shi to execute Ne Qin on the way. On wuyin day that month E Shi, escorting Ne Qin, reached Banlan Mountain; on receiving the final order he executed him.
10
傅恆，字春和，富察氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人，孝賢純皇后弟也。 父李榮保，附見其父米思翰傳。 傅恆自侍衛洊擢戶部侍郎。 乾隆十年六月，命在軍機處行走。 十二年，擢戶部尚書。 十三年三月，孝賢純皇后從上南巡，還至德州崩，傅恆扈行，典喪儀。 四月，敕獎其勤恪，加太子太保。 時訥親視師金川，解尚書阿克敦協辦大學士以授傅恆，並兼領吏部。 訥親既無功，九月，命傅恆暫管川陝總督，經略軍務。 尋授保和殿大學士，發京師及諸行省滿、漢兵三萬五千，以部庫及諸行省銀四百萬供軍儲，又出內帑十萬備犒賞。 十一月，師行，上詣堂子告察，遣皇子及大學士來保等送至良鄉。 傅恆既行，上日降手詔褒勉。 傅恆道陝西，言驛政不修誤軍興，上命協辦大學士尚書尹繼善攝陝西總督，主饋運。 入四川境，馬不給，上又命尹繼善往來川、陝督察。 旋以傅恆師行甚速，紀律嚴明，命議敘，部議加太子太傅，特命加太保。 固辭，不允，發京師及山西、湖北馬七千佐軍。 傅恆發成都，經天赦山，雪後道險，步行七十里至驛。 上聞，賜雙眼孔雀翎，复固辭。
Fu Heng, courtesy name Chunhe, of the Fuca clan, came from the Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner and was the younger brother of Empress Xiaoxian. His father Li Rongbao is treated in the biography of Misanhan. Fu Heng rose from the guards to vice minister of revenue. In the sixth month of Qianlong 10 he joined the Grand Council. In the twelfth year he became minister of revenue. In the third month of the thirteenth year Empress Xiaoxian accompanied the southern tour; she died at Dezhou on the return, and Fu Heng, who had attended her, directed the mourning rites. In the fourth month an edict commended his diligence and made him Guardian of the Heir Apparent. While Ne Qin commanded in Jinchuan, Minister Agedun was replaced as associate grand secretary by Fu Heng, who also kept the ministry of personnel. When Ne Qin failed, Fu Heng was made acting governor-general of Sichuan-Shaanxi and commissioner-general in the ninth month. He was soon made grand secretary of the Hall of Preserving Harmony; thirty-five thousand Manchu and Han troops were mobilized from the capital and provinces, four million taels allocated for supplies, and one hundred thousand from the privy purse for rewards. In the eleventh month the army marched; the emperor prayed at the tangse for success, and princes and Grand Secretary Laibao escorted the column to Liangxiang. After Fu Heng marched, the emperor wrote him daily with praise and encouragement. Passing through Shaanxi, Fu Heng reported that bad courier service was crippling the campaign; the emperor put Associate Grand Secretary Yin Jishan in charge of Shaanxi transport as acting governor-general. In Sichuan horses ran short, and Yin Jishan was again ordered to shuttle between Sichuan and Shaanxi to supervise. When Fu Heng's column proved swift and disciplined, the ministries proposed Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent; the emperor specially made him Grand Protector. He declined but was overruled, and seven thousand horses were sent from the capital, Shanxi, and Hubei. Leaving Chengdu he crossed Tianshe Mountain; after snow the trail was deadly, and he walked seventy li to the next station. On hearing this the emperor gave him double-eyed peacock feathers; he declined again.
11
初，小金川土舍良爾吉間其兄澤旺於莎羅奔，奪其印，即烝於嫂阿扣。 莎羅奔之犯邊也，良爾吉實從之，後詐降為賊諜。 張廣泗入奸民王秋言，使領蠻兵，我師舉動，賊輒知之。 傅恆途中疏請誅良爾吉等，將至軍，使副將馬良柱招良爾吉來迎，至邦噶山，正其罪，並阿扣、王秋悉誅之。 事聞，上褒傅恆明斷，命拜前賜雙眼孔雀翎，毋更固辭。
Earlier Lesser Jinchuan chieftain Lang Erji had turned Slob Dpon against his brother Zewang, seized Zewang's seal, and taken his sister-in-law A Kou. Lang Erji had joined Slob Dpon's border raids and later feigned surrender to spy for the enemy. Zhang Guangsi employed the traitor Wang Qiu to lead tribal auxiliaries, so the enemy always knew our moves. En route Fu Heng asked to execute Lang Erji; near the front he had vice commander Ma Liangzhu lure Lang Erji to Bangga Mountain, tried him, and executed A Kou and Wang Qiu as well. The emperor praised his decisiveness and ordered him to accept the peacock feathers without further refusal.
12
十月，至卡撒，以屯軍地狹隘，與賊相望，且雜處番民巿肆中，乃相度移舊壘前，令總兵冶大雄監營壘。 十四年正月，上疏言：「臣至軍，察用兵始末：當紀山進討之始，馬良柱轉戰而前，逾沃日收小金川直抵丹噶，其鋒甚銳。 彼時張廣泗若速進師，賊備未嚴，殄滅尚易； 乃坐失事機，宋宗璋宿留於雜穀，許應虎敗衄於的郊，賊得盡據險要，增碉備禦。 訥親初至，督戰甚急，任舉敗沒，銳挫氣索，軍無鬥志，一以軍事委張廣泗。 廣泗又為奸人所愚，專主攻碉。 先後殺傷數千人，匿不以聞。 臣惟攻碉最為下策，槍砲不能洞堅壁，於賊無所傷。 賊不過數人，自暗擊明，槍不虛發。 是我惟攻石，而賊實攻人。 賊於碉外為濠，兵不能越，賊伏其中，自下擊上。 其碉銳立，高於浮屠，建作甚捷，數日可成，旋缺旋補。 且眾心甚固，碉盡碎而不去，砲方過而復起。 客主勞佚，形勢迥殊，攻一碉難於克一城。 即臣所駐卡撒，左右山巔三百餘碉，計日以攻，非數年不能盡。 且得一碉輒傷數十百人，得不償失。 兵法，攻堅則瑕者堅，攻瑕則堅者瑕。 惟使賊失所恃，我兵乃可用其所長。 擬俟諸軍大集，分道而進。 別選銳師，旁探間道，裹糧直入，逾碉勿攻，繞出其後。 番眾不多，外備既密，內守必虛。 我兵既自捷徑深入，守者各懷內顧，人無固志，均可不攻自潰。 卡撒為進噶拉依正道，嶺高溝窄，臣當親任其難。 黨壩隘險，亦幾同卡撒，酌益新軍。 兩道並進，直搗巢穴，取其渠魁。 期四月間奏捷。 」上以金川非大敵，勞師兩載，誅大臣，失良將，內不懌。 及是聞其地險難下，益不欲竟其事，遂以孝聖憲皇后諭命班師，而傅恆方督總兵哈攀龍、哈尚德等攻下數碉。 上以金川水土惡，賜傅恆人蓡三斤，並及諸將有差，屢詔召傅恆還。 又以孝聖憲皇后諭封一等忠勇公，賜寶石頂、四團龍補服。 傅恆奏言：「金川事一誤，今復輕率蕆事，賊焰愈張。 眾土司皆罹其毒，邊宇將無寧日。 審度形勢，賊碉非盡當道，其巢皆老弱，我兵且戰且前，自昔嶺中峰直抵噶拉依，破竹建瓴，功在垂成，棄之可惜。 且臣受詔出師，若不掃穴擒渠，何顏返命？ 」並力辭封賞，上不允，手詔謂：「匈奴未滅，無以家為，乃驃姚武人銳往之概。 大學士抒誠贊化，豈與兜鍪閫帥爭一日之績？ 」反复累數千言，复賜詩喻指。
In the tenth month he reached Kasa; the camp was cramped, face to face with the enemy amid tribal markets, so he moved the lines forward and put regional commander Ye Daxiong in charge of building new works. In the first month of the fourteenth year he wrote: "At the army I reviewed the whole campaign: when the advance began from Jishan, Ma Liangzhu fought through Wo Ri, took Lesser Jinchuan, and drove to Danga with fierce momentum. Had Zhang Guangsi pressed on then, before the enemy was ready, victory would have been easy; instead Song Zongzhang stalled at Zagu, Xu Yinghu was beaten at Dijiao, and the enemy seized every strong point and threw up diao. Ne Qin at first drove the fighting hard; Ren Ju's death broke morale, and he handed everything to Zhang Guangsi. Zhang Guangsi, duped by traitors, fixated on storming the diao. Thousands died in these assaults, which he never reported. Attacking diao is the worst strategy: muskets and cannon cannot breach stone walls and hurt no one inside. Only a few defenders fire from cover into the open, and every shot tells. We batter stone while they kill men. They dig moats our men cannot cross and shoot downward from ambush. The forts rise higher than pagodas, go up in days, and are rebuilt as fast as we smash them. Their will is iron: though a fort is shattered they do not flee, and as our guns move on they rebuild. We labor, they rest; taking one diao is harder than storming a city. Around Kasa alone more than three hundred diao crown the ridges; at this pace years would not clear them. Each fort costs scores or hundreds of lives—the price is not worth paying. As the strategists say: strike the strong and the weak grow strong; strike the weak and the strong grow weak. Only by depriving them of their advantage can our troops fight as they are meant to. I propose to wait until the armies assemble and advance on several routes. Pick elite columns to find side paths, march in with rations, bypass the diao without fighting, and take the enemy from behind. The tribes are few; with their strength on the perimeter, the interior must be hollow. Once our men cut in by fast routes, the garrisons will look to their rear, lose heart, and collapse without a fight. Kasa is the main road to Galai, with high ridges and narrow defiles—I will take that hard road myself. Dangba is nearly as tight as Kasa; I will reinforce that column. Both columns will strike the nest together and take the chiefs. I expect victory by the fourth month." The emperor, who had never regarded Jinchuan as a great foe, was inwardly troubled after two years of war, executed ministers, and lost generals. Learning how hard the country was, he was even less willing to continue and ordered withdrawal on the Empress Dowager Xiaosheng's advice—just as Fu Heng was taking several diao under Ha Panlong and Ha Shangde. Because Jinchuan's climate was harsh, he sent Fu Heng three jin of ginseng and gifts to the generals, and repeatedly ordered him home. By the empress dowager's command he was made first-class Loyal and Brave Duke with a jeweled finial and four-dragon surcoat. Fu Heng wrote: "Jinchuan was mishandled once; to quit lightly now will only embolden the enemy. Every tusi will suffer, and the frontier will never know peace. The enemy's forts do not all block the road; their nests hold only the old and weak; our men are fighting forward from Xiling Peak straight to Galai like splitting bamboo—victory is at hand, and to abandon it would be a waste. I was ordered to campaign; if I do not root them out and take their chief, how can I return?" He also refused the honors; the emperor would not hear of it and wrote: "While the Xiongnu are not destroyed one cannot make a home—that is the spirit of Huo Qubing. A grand secretary who aids the throne in civil rule—why should he vie with field commanders for a day's glory?" He wrote thousands of words in reply and also sent a poem to make his meaning plain.
13
時傅恆及提督岳鍾琪決策深入，莎羅奔遣頭人乞降，傅恆令自縛詣軍門。 莎羅奔复介綽斯甲等詣岳鍾琪乞貸死，鍾琪親入勒烏圍，挈莎羅奔及其子郎卡詣軍門。 語詳鍾琪傳。 傅恆遂受莎羅奔父子降，莎羅奔等焚香作樂，誓六事：無犯鄰比諸番，反其侵地，供役視諸土司，執獻諸酋抗我師者，還所掠內地民馬，納軍械槍砲，乃承製赦其罪。 莎羅奔獻​​佛像一、白金萬，傅恆卻其金，莎羅奔請以金為傅恆建祠。 翌日，傅恆率師還。 上優詔嘉獎，命用揚古利故事，賜豹尾槍二桿、親軍二名。 三月，師至京師，命皇長子及裕親王等郊迎。 上御殿受賀，行飲至禮。 傅恆疏辭四團龍補服，上命服以入朝，復命用額亦都、佟國維故事，建宗祠，祀曾祖哈什屯以下，並追予李榮保諡，賜第東安門內，以詩落其成。
Fu Heng and regional commander-in-chief Yue Zhongqi then decided on a deep thrust; Slob Dpon sent headmen to sue for peace, and Fu Heng ordered them bound and brought to camp. Slob Dpon then sent Chuosi and others to Yue Zhongqi to beg for their lives; Zhongqi entered Lewuwei in person and brought Slob Dpon and his son Langka to camp. The full account is in Yue Zhongqi's biography. Fu Heng accepted their surrender; they burned incense and swore six terms: no raids on neighbors, return of seized lands, labor like other tusi, delivery of chiefs who had fought us, return of plundered subjects and horses, surrender of arms—then an imperial pardon followed. Slob Dpon offered an image and ten thousand taels of silver; Fu Heng refused the silver, and Slob Dpon asked to build a shrine in his honor instead. The next day Fu Heng withdrew the army. The emperor praised him in a gracious edict and, following the precedent of Yangguli, gave him two leopard-tail spears and two personal guards. In the third month the army reached the capital; the heir apparent and Prince Yu were sent to welcome them outside the city. The emperor received congratulations in court and held the ritual feast for the returning army. Fu Heng declined the four-dragon surcoat; the emperor made him wear it at court and, following the precedents of E Yidu and Tong Guowei, ordered an ancestral hall for Hashitun and his descendants, posthumous honors for Li Rongbao, and a mansion inside Dong'an Gate, which the emperor marked with a poem at its completion.
14
十九年，準噶爾內亂，諸部台吉多內附。 上將用兵，諮廷臣，惟傅恆贊其議。 二十年，師克伊犁，俘達瓦齊以歸，諭再封一等公，傅恆固辭，至泣下，乃允之。 尋圖功臣像紫光閣，上親制贊，仍以為冠，舉蕭何不戰居首功為比。 二十一年四月，將軍策楞追捕阿睦爾撒納未獲，上命傅恆出視師，赴額林哈畢爾噶，集蒙古諸台吉飭軍事。 傅恆行日，策楞疏至，已率兵深入，復召傅恆還。
In the nineteenth year the Dzungars fell into civil war and many tribal taiji submitted. When the emperor planned war he consulted the court; only Fu Heng supported it. In the twentieth year Yili fell and Dawachi was brought back captive; the emperor offered to make him a first-class duke again; Fu Heng wept and refused until the emperor yielded. His portrait was soon painted in the Hall of Purple Splendor with an imperial encomium placing him first, compared to Xiao He, who won the highest merit without fighting. In the fourth month of the twenty-first year General Celeng failed to catch Amursana; Fu Heng was sent to Elinhabirga to oversee the army and rally the Mongol taiji. The day Fu Heng set out, Celeng reported that he had already driven deep in pursuit, and Fu Heng was recalled.
15
三十三年，將軍明瑞徵緬甸敗績，二月，授傅恆經略，出督師。 時阿里袞以副將軍主軍事，上並授阿桂副將軍、舒赫德參贊大臣，命舒赫德先赴雲南，與阿里袞籌畫進軍。 三十四年二月，傅恆師行，發京師及滿、蒙兵一萬三千六百人從征，上御太和殿賜敕，賚御用甲胄。 四月，至騰越，傅恆決策，師循戛鳩江而進，大兵出江西，取道猛拱、猛養，直搗木梳，水師沿江順流下，水陸相應。 偏師出江東取猛密，夾擊老官屯。 往歲以避瘴，九月後進兵，緬甸得為備。 傅恆互議先數十日出不意，攻其未備，水師當具舟。 上初命阿里袞造舟濟師，阿里袞等言崖險澗窄不宜舟，傍江亦無造舟所。 上又命三泰、傅顯往視，言與阿里袞等同。 及傅恆至軍，諮土司頭人，知蠻暮有山曰翁古多木，旁有地曰野牛壩，野人所居，涼爽無瘴。 即地伐木造舟，野人樂受值，執役甚謹。 傅恆即使傅顯佐蒞事。 舟成，督滿、漢兵並從行奴僕，更番轉搬。 又得茂隆廠附近砲工，令範銅為砲。 狀聞，輒降旨嘉獎，為賦造舟行焉。
In the thirty-third year, after General Mingrui's defeat in Burma, Fu Heng was made commissioner-general in the second month and sent to command. Aligun was then deputy commander; Agui was made deputy commander and Shuhede staff minister, with Shuhede sent ahead to Yunnan to plan the advance with Aligun. In the second month of the thirty-fourth year he marched with thirteen thousand six hundred Manchu and Mongol troops from the capital; the emperor gave him his command in the Hall of Supreme Harmony and imperial armor. In the fourth month at Tengyue he decided the main column would follow the Jiujiang west through Menggong and Mengyang to Mushu while the fleet moved downstream in support. A flanking force east of the river would take Mengmi and pinch Laoguantun. Past campaigns had waited until after the ninth month to avoid malaria, giving Burma time to prepare. Fu Heng proposed advancing weeks early by surprise and insisted the fleet be ready. The emperor had first ordered Aligun to build boats; Aligun replied that the cliffs and narrow streams forbade shipping and that there was no boatyard on the river. Santai and Fu Xian were sent to inspect and reported the same. At the front Fu Heng learned from tusi headmen of Wengguduomu mountain in Manmu and nearby Yeniuba, a cool hill country without malaria. There they felled timber and built boats; the hill people worked gladly for pay. Fu Heng put Fu Xian in charge of the work. When the boats were ready, Manchu and Han troops and servants relayed supplies along the river. He also found gun founders near Maolong and had them cast cannon. Each report brought imperial praise, and the emperor wrote a poem on the boat-building.
16
傅恆初議自將九千三百人渡戛鳩而西，師未集，七月，將四千人發騰越。 上以經略自將師寡，促諸軍速集如初議。 八月，傅恆自南蚌趨戛鳩。 奏至，上方行圍木蘭，入圍獲狍，畀福隆安以賜傅恆。 傅恆道南底壩至允帽，臨戛鳩江，時猛拱大頭人脫猛烏猛、頭人賀丙等，詣傅恆請降。 師至，脫猛烏猛將夾江諸夷寨頭人來迎，與賀丙具舟。 傅恆命分兵徐濟，夾江為寨猛拱后土司渾覺亦請降，獻馴象四。 上賚三眼孔雀翎，傅恆疏辭。 師復進，取猛養，破寨四，誅頭人拉匿拉賽。 設台站，令瑚爾起以七百人駐守。 遂至南董幹，攻南準寨，獲頭人木波猛等三十五人。 進次暮臘，再進次新街。
Fu Heng had planned to lead nine thousand three hundred men across the Jiujiang himself; before the army assembled he sent four thousand from Tengyue in the seventh month. The emperor, seeing the commissioner-general leading with too few men, urged the armies to assemble as planned. In the eighth month Fu Heng pushed from Nanbang toward the Jiujiang. When the report arrived the emperor was hunting at Mulan; he took a roe deer in the enclosure and sent it to Fu Heng through Fu Long'an. Fu Heng reached Yunmao on the Jiujiang by way of Nandiba; Menggong's headmen Tuomengwumeng and He Bing came to surrender. When the army arrived Tuomengwumeng brought the riverine chiefs to welcome them, and he and He Bing supplied boats. Fu Heng ferried troops over in detachments and set camps on both banks; Menggong's queen tusi Hunjue also surrendered and gave four tame elephants. The emperor gave him triple-eyed peacock feathers; Fu Heng declined by memorial. The army advanced again, took Mengyang, stormed four stockades, and executed headman Lanilasai. Relay stations were set up and Hu Erqi garrisoned them with seven hundred men. They then reached Nandonggan, took Nanzhun stockade, and captured headman Mubomeng and thirty-five others. They halted at Mula, then advanced again to Xinjie.
17
傅恆自渡戛鳩江，未嘗與緬甸兵戰，刈禾為糧，行二千里不血刃，而士馬觸暑雨多疾病。 會阿桂將萬餘人自虎踞關出野牛壩，造舟畢成，徵廣東、福建水師亦至，乃合軍並進。 哈國興將水師，阿桂、阿里袞將陸師，阿桂出江東，阿里袞出江西。 緬兵壘金沙江兩岸，又以舟師扼江口。 阿桂先與緬兵遇，麾步兵發銃矢，又以騎兵陷陣，緬兵潰。 哈國興督舟師乘風蹴敵，緬兵舟相擊，死者數千。 阿里袞亦破西岸緬兵，傅恆以所獲纛進。 上復為賦詩，阿里袞感瘴而病，改將水師，旋卒。 十一月，傅恆復進攻老官屯，老官屯在金沙江東，東猛密，西猛墅，北猛拱、猛養，南緬都阿瓦，為水陸通衢。 緬兵伐木立寨甚固，哈國興督諸軍力攻，未即克。 師破東南木寨，緬兵夜自水寨出，傅恆令海蘭察御之，又令伊勒圖督舟師掩擊，复獲船纛。 緬兵潛至江岸築壘，又自林箐中出，海蘭察擊之，屢有斬馘。
Since crossing the Jiujiang Fu Heng had not fought the Burmese; living off the harvest he marched two thousand li without battle, while heat and rain sickened men and horses. Agui meanwhile led ten thousand men from Hujuguan through Yeniuba; when the boats were ready and Guangdong and Fujian fleets arrived, the columns united and advanced. Ha Guoxing led the fleet; Agui and Aligun the land forces—Agui east of the river, Aligun west. The Burmese fortified both banks of the Jinsha and blocked the river mouth with boats. Agui met the Burmese first, had infantry fire muskets and arrows and cavalry charge the line, and routed them. Ha Guoxing drove the fleet downwind into the enemy; thousands died as the Burmese boats crashed together. Aligun broke the western Burmese as well, and Fu Heng forwarded the captured banners. The emperor wrote another poem; Aligun sickened with malaria, took command of the fleet, and soon died. In the eleventh month Fu Heng attacked Laoguantun, east of the Jinsha between Mengmi and Mengshu, north of Menggong and Mengyang and south of Ava—the junction of land and water routes. The Burmese built a stout timber stockade; Ha Guoxing assaulted it hard without immediate success. Our men took the southeastern timber fort; the Burmese sallied from the water fort by night; Fu Heng had Hailancha hold them off and Yiletuo strike by water, capturing more banners. The Burmese built riverbank works and struck from the woods; Hailancha beat them back with repeated kills.
18
師久攻堅，士卒染瘴多物故，水陸軍三萬一千，至是僅存一萬三千。 傅恆以入告，上命罷兵，召傅恆還京。 傅恆俄亦病，阿桂以聞。 上令即馳驛還，而以軍事付阿桂。 會緬甸酋懵駁遣頭人諾爾塔齎蒲葉書乞罷兵，傅恆奏入，上許其行成。 傅恆附疏言：「用兵之始，眾以為難。 臣執意請行，負委任，請從重治罪。 」上手詔謂：「用兵非得已，如以為非是，朕當首任其過。 皇祖時，吳三桂請撤籓，諮於群臣，議撤者惟米思翰、明珠數人。 及三桂反，眾請誅議撤諸臣，皇祖深闢其非。 朕仰紹祖訓，傅恆此事，可援以相比。 傅恆收猛拱，當賜三眼孔雀翎，疏辭，俟功成拜賜。 今既未克賊巢，當繳進賜翎，以稱其請罪之意。 」懵駁遣頭人詣軍獻方物。 十月，傅恆還駐虎踞關，上命傅恆會雲貴總督彰寶議減雲南總兵、知府員缺，釐正州縣舊制。 三十四年二月，班師。 三月，上幸天津，傅恆朝行在。 既而緬甸酋謝罪表久不至，上謂傅恆方病，不忍治其罪。 七月，卒，上親臨其第酹酒，命喪葬視宗室鎮國公，諡文忠。 又命入祀前所建宗祠。 其後上复幸天津，念傅恆於此復命，又經傅恆墓賜奠，皆紀以詩。 及賦懷舊詩，許為「社稷臣」。 嘉慶元年，以福康安平苗功，贈貝子。 福康安卒，推恩贈郡王銜，旋並命配享太廟。
After long assaults malaria killed many; of thirty-one thousand land and naval troops only thirteen thousand remained. Fu Heng reported in; the emperor ordered the war stopped and Fu Heng recalled. Fu Heng soon fell ill, which Agui reported. The emperor ordered him home by express relay and left the army to Agui. The Burmese chief Meng Bo sent Nuoerta with a palm-leaf plea for peace; Fu Heng reported it and the emperor agreed to truce. Fu Heng added: "When the war began everyone called it hard. I insisted on going and bore the charge; I ask severe punishment." The emperor wrote: "War is unavoidable; if it was wrong, I should bear the blame first. Under Kangxi, when Wu Sangui asked to give up his fief, only Misanhan, Nalan Mingzhu, and a few others favored accepting. When San Gui rebelled many wanted those ministers killed; Kangxi firmly refused. I follow that teaching; Fu Heng's case is like it." For recovering Menggong he should have received triple-eyed peacock feathers; he declined until victory. Since the nest was not taken he should return the feathers in keeping with his plea for guilt." Meng Bo sent headmen with gifts to the army. In the tenth month Fu Heng returned to Hujuguan; the emperor had him meet Governor-General Zhang Bao to trim Yunnan military and civil posts and restore county administration. In the second month of the thirty-fourth year the army withdrew. In the third month the emperor went to Tianjin and Fu Heng attended at the traveling court. When Burma's apology long failed to come, the emperor said Fu Heng was too ill to punish. In the seventh month he died; the emperor poured a libation at his house, gave him funeral honors equal to a third-rank imperial prince, and posthumous title Wenzhong. He was also enshrined in the hall built for him earlier. On later visits to Tianjin he remembered Fu Heng's report there and marked his tomb with verse. In a poem of remembrance he called him a pillar of the state. In Jiaqing 1, for Fukang'an's pacification of the Miao, he was posthumously made beile. When Fukang'an died he was given a prince's title and soon also enshrined in the Imperial Ancestral Temple.
19
傅恆直軍機處二十三年，日侍左右，以勤慎得上眷。 故事，軍機處諸臣不同入見，乾隆初，惟訥親承旨。 迨傅恆自陳不能多識，乞諸大臣同入見。 上晚膳後有所諮訪，又召傅恆獨對，時謂之「晚面」。 又軍機處諸大臣既承旨，退自屬草，至傅恆始命章京具稿以進。 上倚傅恆為重臣，然偶有小節疏失，即加以戒約。 傅恆益謙下，治事不敢自擅。 敬禮士大夫，翼後進使盡其才。 行軍與士卒同甘苦。 卒時未五十，上尤惜之。
Fu Heng served twenty-three years in the Grand Council at the emperor's side and won deep favor through diligence. By precedent council ministers did not audience together; early in Qianlong only Ne Qin took edicts. Fu Heng said he could not remember enough and asked that all ministers audience together, which was granted. After supper when he wished to consult he summoned Fu Heng alone—the "evening audience." After receiving edicts ministers had drafted themselves; under Fu Heng clerks prepared drafts. The emperor relied on him heavily, yet admonished the smallest lapse. He grew more humble and would not act on his own authority. He respected scholars and helped juniors use their talents. On campaign he shared the soldiers' hardships. He died before fifty, which the emperor especially mourned.
20
子福靈安、福隆安、福康安、福長安。 福康安自有傳。
His sons were Fu Ling'an, Fu Long'an, Fukang'an, and Fu Chang'an. Fukang'an has a separate biography.
21
福靈安，多羅額駙，授侍衛。 準噶爾之役，從將軍兆惠戰於葉爾羌，有功，予雲騎尉世職。 三十二年，授正白旗滿洲副都統。 署雲南永北鎮總兵。 卒。
Fu Ling'an, a first-rank prince consort, became a guard. In the Dzungar war he followed General Zhaohui at Yarkand with merit and received a hereditary yunqiwei rank. In the thirty-second year he became vice commander of the Manchu Plain White Banner. He served as acting regional commander at Yongbei in Yunnan. He died.
22
福隆安，尚高宗女和嘉公主，授和碩額駙、御前侍衛。 三十三年，擢兵部尚書、軍機處行走，移工部尚書。 三十五年，襲一等忠勇公。 三十六年，用兵金川，總兵宋元俊劾四川總督桂林，命福隆安往讞。 福隆安直桂林，抵元俊罪。 四十一年，复授兵部尚書，仍領工部。 金川平，畫像紫光閣。 四十九年，卒，諡勤恪。
Fu Long'an married Princess Hejia and became second-rank prince consort and imperial guard. In the thirty-third year he became minister of war and joined the Grand Council, then minister of works. In the thirty-fifth year he inherited the first-class Loyal and Brave Dukedom. In the thirty-sixth year, during the Jinchuan campaign, Song Yuanjun impeached Governor-General Guilin and Fu Long'an was sent to try the case. Fu Long'an upheld Guilin and convicted Yuanjun. In the forty-first year he was again minister of war while keeping works. When Jinchuan was pacified his portrait joined the Hall of Purple Splendor. In the forty-ninth year he died with posthumous title Qinke.
23
子丰紳濟倫，初以公主子，命視和碩額駙品秩，授鑲藍旗漢軍副都統、奉宸苑卿。 四十九年，襲爵。 累遷兵部尚書，領鑾儀衛。 嘉慶間，再坐事，官終盛京兵部侍郎。 十二年，卒。 子富勒渾翁珠，襲爵。
His son Feng Shenjilun, as the princess's son, was given second-rank consort privileges and made vice commander of the Han Bordered Blue Banner and director of the Imperial Parks. In the forty-ninth year he inherited the title. He rose to minister of war and directed the imperial equipage. Under Jiaqing he was twice punished and ended as Mukden vice minister of war. In the twelfth year he died. His son Fulehunwengzhu inherited the title.
24
福長安，自藍翎侍衛累遷至正紅旗滿洲副都統、武備院卿，領內務府。 乾隆四十五年，命在軍機處學習行走。 累遷戶部尚書。 五十三年，台灣平。 五十七年，廓爾喀平。 諸功臣畫像紫光閣，福長安皆與焉。 嘉慶三年，俘王三槐，福長安以直軍機處得侯。 四年，高宗崩，大學士和珅得罪，仁宗以福長安阿附，逮下獄，奪爵，籍其家。 諸大臣議用朋黨律坐立斬，上命改監候，而賜和珅死，使監福長安詣和珅死所跪視。 旋遣往裕陵充供茶拜唐阿，就遷員外郎。 六年，以請還京，奪職，發盛京披甲。 旋自驍騎校屢遷：再為圍場總管，一為馬蘭鎮總兵，再署古北口提督。 屢坐事譴謫。 二十一年，授正黃旗滿洲副都統。 二十二年，卒。
Fu Chang'an rose from blue-lance guard to Plain Red Banner vice commander, Armory director, and Imperial Household head. In Qianlong 45 he was assigned to study in the Grand Council. He rose to minister of revenue. In the fifty-third year Taiwan was pacified. In the fifty-seventh year Gurkha was pacified. He was among the portraits in the Hall of Purple Splendor for both campaigns. In Jiaqing 3, when Wang Sanhuai was taken, he received a marquisate for Grand Council service. In the fourth year Qianlong died and Heshen fell; Jiaqing arrested Fu Chang'an for fawning on Heshen, stripped his rank, and confiscated his estate. Ministers proposed the faction law and death; the emperor commuted to imprisonment and made him kneel where Heshen was executed. He was then sent to serve at Yuling mausoleum and made a department director. In the sixth year he asked to return to the capital, lost his post, and was sent to Mukden as a soldier. He was later promoted from cavalry commandant to park superintendent, Malan regional commander, and twice acting Gubeikou commander. He was repeatedly punished and demoted. In the twenty-first year he became Plain Yellow Banner vice commander. In the twenty-second year he died.
25
論曰：高宗初政，寬大而清明，舉國熙熙，樂見太平。 是時鄂爾泰、張廷玉負夾輔之重，然居中用事為天子喉舌，厥惟訥親，繼之者傅恆也。 高宗手詔謂當鄂爾泰在朝，培養陶成，得一訥親； 訥親在朝，培養陶成，得一傅恆。 又謂訥親受恩第一，次則傅恆。 訥親視師失上指，坐誅，終不沒其勤廉； 傅恆再以受降還師，德心孚契，自以其謹慎，非徒藉貴戚功閥重也。
The commentator says: Early in Qianlong's reign the government was lenient and clear, and the realm rejoiced in peace. Ortai and Zhang Tingyu bore the weight of assisting rule, but the emperor's voice at court was Ne Qin, then Fu Heng. The emperor wrote that while Ortai was in court he nurtured talent until he had Ne Qin; while Ne Qin was in court he nurtured talent until he had Fu Heng. He also said Ne Qin received favor first, then Fu Heng. Ne Qin lost the emperor's favor in command and was executed, yet his diligence and integrity were not erased; Fu Heng accepted surrender and withdrew, and the emperor's heart was truly with him—not merely because he was a noble kinsman of an old military house.
