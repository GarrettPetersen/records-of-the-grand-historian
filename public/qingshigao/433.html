2
金國琛，字逸亭，江蘇江陰人。 咸豐中，以諸生謁羅澤南於江西，使參軍事。 每出戰，部伍嚴整，倉猝犯之，屹然不亂。 轉戰弋陽、廣信、武昌、黃州，累功擢知縣。 七年，李續賓代澤南，使總理營務。 率師會襲湖口，克之。 進復彭澤小姑洑、泰坪關，擊退援賊，晉秩同知直隸州。 八年，從克九江，窺安徽，下太湖、潛山、桐城。 續賓戰歿三河，國琛與其弟續宜招集散亡，勞徠撫慰，重申紀律，軍勢復振。
Jin Guochen, whose courtesy name was Yiting, came from Jiangyin in Jiangsu. During the Xianfeng reign, while still a licentiate he sought out Luo Zeyong in Jiangxi and was assigned to military staff work. On every campaign his units kept tight formation; even under sudden assault they held their ground without breaking. After fighting at Yiyang, Guangxin, Wuchang, and Huangzhou, he rose through repeated victories to the rank of county magistrate. In the seventh year of the reign, when Li Xubing succeeded Zeyong, Guochen was put in charge of camp administration. He led a combined force in a surprise attack on Hukou and took the city. Pressing forward, he recovered Pengze, Xiaogufu, and Taiping Pass, repulsed the relief force, and was promoted to vice-prefect of Zhili. In the eighth year, after Jiujiang fell, he turned toward Anhui and seized Taihu, Qianshan, and Tongcheng. After Xubing was killed at Sanhe, Guochen and his younger brother Xuyi rallied the fugitives, consoled and reassured them, restored discipline, and the army's fighting spirit returned.
3
九年秋，石達開犯湖南，圍寶慶。 國琛從續宜赴援，毀田家渡賊壘，又敗賊賀家坳，斬悍賊胡德孝，賊走廣西，擢知府。 其冬，胡林翼、曾國籓規皖，精兵猛將萃於潛山、太湖。 陳玉成糾眾數十萬，結捻匪龔瞎子圍鮑超於小池驛，救兵迭失利。 先是林翼以國琛行軍善規地勢，令率十四營冒雪趨天堂備援。 至事急，乃出高橫嶺，屯仰天庵，俯視賊營，皆在目中。 賊驟見旗幟，大驚。 十年正月，賊乘霧登山來犯，國琛揮軍突起躪之，合山下軍奮擊，斬馘逾萬，乘勝克潛山、太湖。 林翼疏陳：「非鮑軍之堅忍，不能久持； 非國琛之出奇制勝，不能轉危為安。 」論功，擢道員。
That autumn of the ninth year, Shi Dakai invaded Hunan and laid siege to Baoqing. Guochen marched with Xuyi to relieve the city, razed the rebel stockade at Tianjiadu, routed the enemy again at Hejia'ao, and slew the formidable rebel Hu Dexiao; the rebels withdrew into Guangxi, and he was promoted to prefect. That winter Hu Linyi and Zeng Guofan laid plans for Anhui, concentrating elite troops and veteran commanders at Qianshan and Taihu. Chen Yucheng mustered several hundred thousand men and, allying with the Nian chieftain Gong Xiazi, pinned Bao Chao down at Xiaochiyi while one relief column after another was beaten back. Earlier, Linyi had noted Guochen's knack for reading terrain on the march and ordered him to lead fourteen battalions through the snow toward Tiantang as a reserve force. When the situation turned critical, he marched out by Gaohengling and encamped at Yangtian'an, with every rebel camp spread out below him. The rebels caught sight of his banners all at once and were thrown into panic. In the first month of the tenth year the rebels came up the mountain through the fog; Guochen threw his men forward in a sudden charge and drove them down, while the troops below joined in a furious assault that left more than ten thousand dead; riding the momentum he retook Qianshan and Taihu. Linyi wrote in a memorial: "Without the endurance of Bao's army, the position could not have been held; without Guochen's unexpected stroke of victory, peril could not have been turned to safety." For his achievements he was promoted to circuit intendant.
4
十一年，粵匪復犯湖北，國琛馳援武昌，連復孝感、云夢，進攻德安。 賊酋馬融和死斗，卒以長圍克之，加布政使銜。 尋授湖北安襄鄖荊道，仍兼治軍。 樊城地沖要，商賈所集，督軍士筑土城，不煩民力，賴為保障。 時捻匪西擾關中，命國琛率師赴援，以鄖西戒嚴，留未行。
In the eleventh year the Taiping rebels struck Hubei again; Guochen raced to Wuchang's relief, retook Xiaogan and Yunmeng in succession, and pressed the attack on De'an. The rebel leader Ma Ronghe resisted to the last, but after a prolonged siege the city fell; Guochen was given the brevet rank of provincial administration commissioner. He was soon appointed to the An-Xiang-Yun-Jing circuit in Hubei while continuing to command troops. Fancheng stood at a vital crossroads where merchants thronged; he had his soldiers raise an earthen wall without calling on civilian labor, and the town came to rely on it for protection. When Nian bandits began raiding west into Guanzhong, he was ordered to march to their relief, but with western Yun on high alert he was held back and never set out.
5
同治元年，馬融和以眾六萬圍南陽，國琛越境往援，力戰解城圍，拔出難民數萬。 巡撫嚴樹森忌之，劾其不遵調度，以同知降補。 二年，曾國籓調統義從營。 擊賊徽州，屢捷於豹嶺、佛嶺、黃傋口、小溪。 皖南肅清，復原官，補甘肅鞏秦階道。 以母老假歸。 光緒元年，起復廣東督糧道，擢按察使。 五年，卒于官。
In the first year of Tongzhi, Ma Ronghe invested Nanyang with sixty thousand men; Guochen crossed into the neighboring province to help, fought his way through to raise the siege, and brought out tens of thousands of refugees. Governor Yan Shusen, envious of his success, impeached him for disobeying orders and had him reduced to supplemental vice-prefect. In the second year Zeng Guofan assigned him to command the Yicong Battalion. Fighting rebels in Huizhou, he won repeated victories at Baoling, Foling, Huangdangkou, and Xiaoxi. After southern Anhui was cleared of rebels, his former rank was restored and he was appointed to the Gong-Qin-Jie circuit in Gansu. He took leave to go home when his mother grew old. In the first year of Guangxu he was recalled to serve as grain transport commissioner in Guangdong and promoted to surveillance commissioner. In the fifth year he died in office.
6
國琛以儒生治軍十餘年，堅苦踔厲，號為名將。 居官亦有政聲。
A scholar who had taken up arms, Guochen led troops for more than a decade with relentless grit and was hailed as one of the great commanders of his day. In civil office, too, he earned a reputation for sound governance.
7
黃淳熙，字子春，江西鄱陽人。 道光二十七年進士，湖南即用知縣，歷署綏寧、會同。 剛直為時所忌，引疾閒居。 咸豐三年，巡撫駱秉章廉知其賢，使強起之。 七年，署湘鄉，有異政。 尋丁父憂。 鄱陽方陷賊，移家於湘鄉。 曾國籓方起督浙江軍，辟參軍事，不就。 九年，石達開犯湖南，秉章檄淳熙募勇千六百人防省城，時出剿賊。 達開由寶慶竄踞嶺東，分黨犯江華，淳熙破之於掛勾嶺，遂夜襲嶺東賊營，躡至江、藍，殲殪甚眾。 進剿賊黨賴裕新，乘霧敗之，破杉木根、黃馬寨而還。 十年，達開黨眾四出，淳熙轉戰於永、道、綏、靖諸州，復宜章、桂陽。 前后三十餘戰，皆捷，累擢知府，以道員記名。 所部曰果毅營，增至三千人。
Huang Chunxi, whose courtesy name was Zichun, came from Poyang in Jiangxi. A jinshi of 1847, he was posted to Hunan as an acting magistrate and served in succession at Suining and Huitong. His blunt integrity made him enemies, and he pleaded illness and withdrew from public life. In the third year of Xianfeng, Governor Luo Bingzhang, having heard of his talent, pressed him back into service. In the seventh year he served as acting magistrate of Xiangxiang and distinguished himself in office. He soon went into mourning for his father. Poyang had just fallen to the rebels, so he moved his household to Xiangxiang. Zeng Guofan, newly appointed to command the Zhejiang campaign, invited him onto his staff, but he declined. In the ninth year, when Shi Dakai invaded Hunan, Bingzhang ordered Chunxi to raise sixteen hundred militiamen to guard the provincial capital and sent him out from time to time against the rebels. Dakai broke out of Baoqing and seized the passes in eastern Hunan; when his detachments struck Jianghua, Chunxi routed them at Guagouling, then raided the rebel camps in the eastern hills by night and pursued as far as Jiang and Lan, killing a great number. Pressing on against the rebel leader Lai Yuxin, he caught the enemy in the fog, smashed their positions at Shanmugen and Huangmaizhai, and withdrew. In the tenth year, as Dakai's forces fanned out in every direction, Chunxi fought across Yong, Dao, Sui, and Jing and recovered Yizhang and Guiyang. In more than thirty engagements over this period he was never defeated; he was promoted repeatedly to prefect and placed on the list for circuit intendant. His command, known as the Guoyi Battalion, grew to three thousand men.
8
駱秉章奉命赴四川督師，湘軍名將勁兵多從曾國籓、胡林翼，劉蓉薦淳熙兵精善戰，秉章遂以淳熙與劉岳昭從行。 至荊州，岳昭復留，獨淳熙以所部當軍鋒。 分道溯峽上，次萬縣，聞順慶被圍，率師赴援。 五月，至，賊走定遠，追之，距定遠二十里，望賊屯城西南，連十餘里，城東北江水環繞，賊方造浮橋渡水。 淳熙分三路進，遇賊即前搏之，擲火焚其屯，賊大亂爭走，二十餘壘悉潰，擒斬數千。 殲首賊何國樑，解散脅從萬餘人。 賊黨彭紹福率眾千餘屯東岸，收集潰賊，竄二郎場，淳熙銳進，欲一戰平之。 二郎場在山中，羊腸曲逕，通遂寧兩路，均為涪江阻。 別賊硃甲眾數千由青岡壩至，四路設伏。 淳熙遣偵不見賊，五鼓師行，賊燕子窠，擊走之，逼二郎場。 賊分兩路繞山麓上，淳熙知有伏，令諸營左右搜捕，自率中軍策應。 兵分，伏賊起，遍布山岡。 官軍走田塍，泥深輒陷。 淳熙率親卒拒戰，被圍，策馬突陣，陷淖中，棄馬，手刃十餘賊，中矛仆，擁至場，支解燔之。 賊懾其軍勇猛，不復追，餘部整隊還，賊亦遁走。 淳熙雖戰歿，湘軍之威因之頓振。 詔贈布政使，賜恤，加贈內閣學士，謚忠壯。
When Luo Bingzhang was sent to Sichuan to take command, most of the Hunan army's star generals and crack units had gone with Zeng Guofan and Hu Linyi; Liu Rong praised Chunxi's men as first-rate fighters, and Bingzhang brought Chunxi and Liu Yuezhao along. At Jingzhou, Yuezhao was left behind again, and Chunxi alone led his brigade at the head of the army. They split up to move up the gorges and halted at Wanxian; when word came that Shunqing was besieged, he marched to its relief. He arrived in the fifth month as the rebels fell back on Dingyuan; twenty li short of the city he saw their camps spread along more than ten li southwest of the walls, while to the northeast the river curved around the town and the rebels were throwing up pontoon bridges to cross. Chunxi advanced on three fronts, closing with the enemy wherever he met them, setting fire to their camps until they broke and fled; more than twenty stockades were overrun and several thousand were killed or captured. He killed the rebel chief He Guoliang and released more than ten thousand men who had been forced to follow the rebels. The rebel leader Peng Shaofu rallied more than a thousand men on the east bank, gathered the fugitives, and withdrew to Erlangchang; Chunxi pushed hard, hoping to settle the matter in a single fight. Erlangchang lay deep in the hills along narrow, twisting tracks; the two roads toward Suining were both cut by the Fujiang. Another rebel force under Zhu Jia, several thousand strong, came up from Qinggangba and laid ambushes along four approaches. Chunxi's scouts reported no enemy in sight; at the fifth watch his column moved out, brushed aside rebels at Yanziwu, and pressed on toward Erlangchang. The rebels came around the foothills on two paths; sensing an ambush, Chunxi sent his flanking battalions to sweep the slopes while he led the center in support. As his men spread out, the hidden rebels sprang up all along the ridges. The government troops retreated along the field dikes and sank in the deep mud. Chunxi led his bodyguard in a last stand; surrounded, he charged the enemy line on horseback, foundered in the mire, abandoned his mount, and cut down more than ten rebels with his own hand before a spear brought him down; the rebels carried his body to the field, dismembered it, and burned it. Awed by the ferocity of his men, the rebels did not press the pursuit; the survivors re-formed and withdrew, and the enemy melted away as well. Though Chunxi fell in battle, the fighting reputation of the Hunan army was suddenly restored. An edict posthumously made him a provincial administration commissioner, granted mourning favors, and further honored him as a Grand Secretariat academician with the posthumous name Loyal and Valiant.
9
吳坤修，字竹莊，江西新建人。 捐納從九，分發湖南。 道光二十九年，賑湘陰水災，勤於事。 從剿李沅發，以府經歷、縣丞補用。 咸豐二年，粵匪犯長沙，以守城功擢知縣。 曾國籓創立水師，坤修司軍械。 四年，水師攻九江，入鄱陽湖，為賊所阻不得出，令坤修單騎往南康，導往吳城、南昌。 五年，率舟師防瑞豐。 以父憂歸。 既而武昌復陷，坤修從羅澤南援湖北，復咸寧、蒲圻、崇陽、通城，累擢同知，賜花翎。 進規武昌。
Wu Kunxiu, whose courtesy name was Zhuzhuang, came from Xinjian in Jiangxi. He bought his way to the rank of sub-official and was posted to Hunan. In 1849 he directed flood relief at Xiangyin and threw himself diligently into the work. After campaigning against Li Yuanfa, he was appointed prefectural assistant and county assistant. In the second year of Xianfeng, when the Taiping rebels besieged Changsha, he was promoted to magistrate for his part in the defense. When Zeng Guofan founded the river fleet, Kunxiu was put in charge of ordnance and supplies. In the fourth year the fleet attacked Jiujiang and entered Poyang Lake, only to be trapped by the rebels; Zeng sent Kunxiu alone on horseback to Nankang to guide the ships out toward Wucheng and Nanchang. In the fifth year he commanded the river force guarding Ruifeng. He went home to observe mourning for his father. When Wuchang fell again, Kunxiu marched with Luo Zeyong into Hubei, retook Xianning, Puqi, Chongyang, and Tongcheng, rose through repeated promotions to vice-prefect, and was awarded the peacock feather. He pressed operations toward the recovery of Wuchang.
10
六年，江西軍事不利，胡林翼令坤修領新募軍曰彪字營，會湘軍援江西。 復新昌、上高。 由新昌取道羅坊攻奉新，梯城而登，賊死守不能拔，乃先下安義、靖安，后萃軍奉新。 時江西餉絀，坤修傾家貲，并勸族里富人出銀米餉軍； 又籌銀四萬兩解省垣，收集平江潰勇。 七年春，克奉新，累擢道員。 尋授廣東南韶連道，仍留軍，克瑞州。 是年冬，東鄉師潰，被劾褫職。 九年，駐師撫州。 江西巡撫耆齡檄督辦撫、建、寧三屬團練，始立團防營，駐貴溪。 移德興，出援徽州。 十年，克建德。 秋，徽防軍潰，坤修方假歸，其弟修敳攝軍事，守嶺外郭村。 調回江西，曾國籓令守湖口，而巡撫毓科檄援建昌。 賊由金谿竄東鄉，坤修自撫州迎擊於鄧家埠，大破之。 賊復出貴溪竄安仁，遏之不得渡河，乃竄德興、萬年，將擾景德鎮。 坤修由饒州馳援景德，以固祁門大軍后路。 會賊由建德上犯，國籓令援湖口。 坤修且戰且進，先賊至，城恃以完，加鹽運使銜。
In the sixth year, as the war in Jiangxi turned against the government, Hu Linyi put Kunxiu at the head of a newly raised force called the Biaozi Battalion to join the Hunan army in relieving the province. He recovered Xinchang and Shanggao. From Xinchang he marched by way of Luofang against Fengxin and sent men up scaling ladders onto the walls, but the defenders held so stubbornly that the city would not fall; he took Anyi and Jing'an first, then massed his forces again at Fengxin. Funds in Jiangxi were exhausted; Kunxiu poured out his family fortune and persuaded wealthy kinsmen to supply silver and grain for the troops; he also raised forty thousand taels for the provincial capital and rallied the scattered militia from Pingjiang. In the spring of the seventh year Fengxin fell, and he was promoted repeatedly until he reached circuit intendant. He was soon appointed to the Nan-Shao-Lian circuit in Guangdong but remained with the army and took Ruizhou. That winter his force was routed at Dongxiang; he was impeached and dismissed from office. In the ninth year he kept his troops at Fuzhou in Jiangxi. Jiangxi Governor Qi Ling ordered him to organize militia in the Fu, Jian, and Ning districts; he formed the Tuanfang Battalion and encamped at Guixi. He shifted to Dexing and marched out to relieve Huizhou. In the tenth year he captured Jiande. That autumn the Huizhou garrison collapsed while Kunxiu was home on leave; his younger brother Xiuyi took command and held Guocun beyond the pass. Recalled to Jiangxi, he was ordered by Zeng Guofan to hold Hukou, but Governor Yu Ke summoned him to relieve Jianchang. When the rebels broke out of Jinxi toward Dongxiang, Kunxiu marched from Fuzhou and shattered them at Dengjiabu. The rebels slipped out of Guixi toward Anren; Kunxiu barred the river crossings and drove them off toward Dexing and Wannian, where they threatened Jingdezhen. Kunxiu raced from Raozhou to Jingdezhen to shield the rear of the main force at Qimen. When rebels moved up from Jiande, Guofan ordered him to relieve Hukou. Kunxiu fought his way forward and reached the city ahead of the enemy, saving it from capture; he was given the brevet rank of salt transport commissioner.
11
同治元年，李秀成自蘇州援江寧，分犯蕪湖，會軍擊卻之，又會克金保圩、高淳、溧水及溧陽、東壩各要隘，遣散降眾數萬。 三年，加布政使銜。 江寧克復，以按察使記名。 四年，署徽寧池太廣道，授安徽按察使。 五年，署布政使。 六年，巡撫英翰駐潁州，出境剿捻，坤修轉輸餉運，未嘗遲乏。 七年，署巡撫，實授布政使。 東捻平，請假回籍補終父母喪。 九年，回任。 十一年，卒。 巡撫英翰疏陳其戰功政績，賜恤，贈內閣學士。
In the first year of Tongzhi, Li Xiucheng marched from Suzhou to relieve Nanjing and sent detachments against Wuhu; Kunxiu joined the allied force that drove them back, then helped take Jinbaowei, Gaochun, Lishui, and the key positions at Liyang and Dongba, disbanding tens of thousands of surrendered rebels. In the third year he was given the brevet rank of provincial administration commissioner. After Nanjing was recovered, he was placed on the list for surveillance commissioner. In the fourth year he served as acting intendant of the Hui-Ning-Chi-Tai-Guang circuit and was appointed surveillance commissioner of Anhui. In the fifth year he served as acting provincial administration commissioner. In the sixth year, when Governor Ying Han was based at Yingzhou and marched out to fight the Nian, Kunxiu handled supply transport and never once let the army go wanting. In the seventh year he served as acting governor and received a substantive appointment as provincial administration commissioner. After the eastern Nian were suppressed, he took leave to go home and finish the mourning period for his parents. In the ninth year he resumed office. In the eleventh year he died. Governor Ying Han memorialized his battlefield record and civil achievements; the court granted mourning favors and posthumously made him a Grand Secretariat academician.
12
康國器，初名以泰，字交修，廣東南海人。 少為吏員。 道光末，從軍，以勞授江西贛縣桂源司巡檢。 咸豐初，粵匪犯江西，土寇蜂起，國器募死士三百，贛南道周玉衡檄擊賊烏兜、良口，克萬安。 造船三十艘，習水戰。 六年，從克饒州，累擢知縣，署南城。 石達開陷瑞、撫、臨、吉四郡，國器從克樟樹鎮，連戰瑞州、臨江、鉛山、安仁，擢同知。 十一年，廣東巡撫耆齡檄剿陽山賊。 賊踞藍山，地阻絕，負隅十餘年。 國器緣崖歷磵出賊后，破石柵九，奪砲臺，毀其老巢。 遣子熊飛單騎說降劇賊練四虎，其魁梁柱走豬頭寨，穴山攻獲之。 進軍赫巖，擒賊渠周裕等。 藍山平，擢知府。 同治元年，援浙，從蔣益澧圍湯溪，明年春，克之，擢道員。 三年，克餘杭，功最多，授福建延建邵道，始專統一軍。
Kang Guoqi, originally named Yitai and styled Jiaoxiu, came from Nanhai in Guangdong. In his youth he served as a minor clerk. At the close of the Daoguang reign he took up arms and, for his service, was appointed sub-inspector at Guiyuan in Gan County, Jiangxi. Early in Xianfeng, when the Taiping struck Jiangxi and local bandits swarmed, Guoqi raised three hundred die-hard fighters; Zhou Yuheng, intendant of southern Gan, sent him against the rebels at Wudou and Liangkou, and he took Wan'an. He built thirty boats and drilled his men in river fighting. In the sixth year he took part in the capture of Raozhou, rose through repeated promotions to magistrate, and served as acting magistrate of Nancheng. When Shi Dakai overran the four prefectures of Rui, Fu, Lin, and Ji, Guoqi helped retake Zhangshu Town and fought through Ruizhou, Linjiang, Qianshan, and Anren in turn, earning promotion to vice-prefect. In the eleventh year Guangdong Governor Qi Ling ordered him to suppress the rebels in Yangshan. The rebels had held Lanshan for more than a decade, the terrain so rugged that government troops could not reach them. Guoqi worked his way along cliffs and ravines to strike from behind, smashed nine stone stockades, took their gun emplacements, and destroyed their stronghold. He sent his son Xiongfei alone to talk the fierce rebel Lian Sihu into surrender; when the chief Liang Zhu fled to Zhutou Stockade, they dug into the hill and took him. Pressing on to Heyan, he captured the rebel chiefs Zhou Yu and others. With Lanshan pacified, he was promoted to prefect. In the first year of Tongzhi he went to aid Zhejiang, joined Jiang Yizeng in the siege of Tangxi, and took the city the following spring; he was promoted to circuit intendant. In the third year he led the capture of Yuhang with the greatest share of the credit, was appointed to the Yan-Jian-Shao circuit in Fujian, and at last held sole command of an army.
13
粵匪汪海洋犯閩，陷武平、永平，李世賢踞漳州、龍巖與之合，旁郡縣多沒於賊。 左宗棠議三路進兵，國器自請當龍巖。 進軍雁石，令熊飛壁鐵石洋，三戰薄城下，破其眾數萬，并敗古田援賊。 四年正月，遂克龍巖。 賊走永定，分踞苦竹、奎洋，勢猶熾。 國器進擊苦竹，乘夜大霧，火賊營，破二十餘壘。 海洋以悍黨來援，敗之於東阬，又敗之大溪，乃竄廣東大浦境。 未幾，海洋復犯永定，國器馳毀羅灘橋； 賊分七路來撲，海洋自陣獅龍嶺，所部皆死黨，旗幟遍巖谷。 國器曰：「賊精銳盡萃於此，若摧之，餘眾必奔。 」乃堅壁深溝，伺怠出擊。 先破其伏，分道猛進，斬馘數千，盡獲其軍實，海洋跳而免。 時漳州亦下，李世賢西遁，遇國器於塔下，縱兵擊之，降其眾二萬人。 海洋走廣東，踞鎮平。 國器進壁鎮平東南高思塘，分軍扼程官埠，賊數來犯，卻之。 國器知海洋將襲高思而虛攻程官埠，乃戒程官軍勿為動，設伏兩山間。 海洋果率悍黨來撲，誘入，伏突起，槍斃其梟汪大力、黃十四，海洋傷腕，陣斃及墮巖磵死者無數。 胡瞎子攻程官，亦敗走。 尋克鎮平。 十二月，會諸軍擊賊嘉應，海洋伏誅，餘孽悉平。
The Taiping commander Wang Haiyang invaded Fujian and took Wuping and Yongping; Li Shixian held Zhangzhou and Longyan in alliance with him, and rebel power spread through the surrounding districts. Zuo Zongtang proposed a three-pronged advance, and Guoqi asked to take the Longyan sector himself. He marched to Yanshi and had Xiongfei entrench at Tieshiyang; after three fights under the walls he shattered a rebel host of tens of thousands and routed the relief column from Gutian. In the first month of the fourth year he at last took Longyan. The rebels withdrew to Yongding and split between Kuzhu and Kuiyang, where they remained formidable. Guoqi struck at Kuzhu under cover of a thick night fog, burning the rebel camps and overrunning more than twenty stockades. Haiyang came up with his toughest fighters; Guoqi beat them at Dongkeng and again at Daxi, driving them into Dapu in Guangdong. Soon Haiyang struck Yongding again; Guoqi raced ahead to burn the Luotan Bridge; the rebels attacked in seven columns while Haiyang held Shilongling in person; his men were fanatics, and their banners covered the hills. Guoqi said, "The enemy has massed his best troops here; break this force and the rest will scatter. " He strengthened his defenses and waited for them to tire before sallying out. He smashed their ambush first, then charged along several routes, killing thousands and seizing their stores while Haiyang escaped by a hair. Zhangzhou had fallen by then; Li Shixian fled west, ran into Guoqi below the pagoda, gave battle, and twenty thousand of his men surrendered. Haiyang fled into Guangdong and entrenched himself at Zhenping. Guoqi camped at Gaositang southeast of Zhenping and posted detachments at Chengguanbu; the rebels attacked repeatedly and were driven back. Guoqi judged that Haiyang would hit Gaosi while feinting at Chengguanbu; he ordered the garrison there to hold firm and laid ambushes in the hills. Haiyang did lead his hard core against the trap; the ambush sprang up, musket fire killed Wang Dali and Huang Shisi, Haiyang was wounded in the wrist, and countless rebels died in the fight or on the cliffs. Hu Xiazi's attack on Chengguan failed as well, and he too withdrew. Zhenping fell soon after. In the twelfth month the allied armies attacked Jiaying; Haiyang was killed; the last of the rebels were wiped out.
14
五年，擢按察使。 七年，遷廣西布政使。 十年，護理巡撫。 十一年，內召，以疾歸。 光緒十年，卒。 左宗棠疏陳戰績，請恤，格於吏議，特詔允之。
In the fifth year he was promoted to surveillance commissioner. In the seventh year he was moved to provincial administration commissioner of Guangxi. In the tenth year he served as acting governor. In the eleventh year he was called to court but went home ill. In the tenth year of Guangxu he died. Zuo Zongtang memorialized his campaigns and asked for posthumous honors; the Board objected on procedural grounds, but the throne overruled them by special edict.
15
國器治軍能以少擊眾，常傷足而跛，軍中號康拐子，悍賊皆畏之。 子熊飛，積功至浙江候補道，勇而有謀，常為軍鋒。 國器數獲奇捷，實資其力云。
Guoqi was famed for beating larger forces with smaller ones; a leg wound left him lame, and the army called him Lame Kang—feared by every tough rebel in the field. His son Xiongfei rose to acting circuit intendant in Zhejiang; bold and shrewd, he usually spearheaded the attacks. Many of Guoqi's startling victories owed their success to his son's prowess.
16
李鶴章，字季荃，安徽合肥人，大學士鴻章弟。 諸生。 從父兄治本籍團練，屢出戰有功，以州同用。 咸豐十一年，從克菱湖賊壘，復安慶，擢知縣，賜花翎。 同治元年，從鴻章援江蘇，常率親兵佐督戰。 北新涇、四江口諸役，功皆最。 又攻枝福山、許浦海口賊壘，招降常熟踞賊錢森仁。 鴻章引嫌，奏捷不敘其勞，特旨詢問，命一體議敘，以知州用，加四品銜。 二年，會克太倉，規蘇州。 分諸軍為兩路，其進昆山一路，以程學啟為總統； 由常熟進江陰者，鶴章督之。 迭戰於常熟之王莊，江陰之南漍、北漍、顧山，毀賊壘，破援賊，會克江陰，擢知府。 進攻無錫，踞賊黃子隆死守，李秀成屢來援； 及蘇州既克，潰賊亦麕聚，鶴章督水陸諸軍力戰克之，以道員記名簡放。 詔嘉鶴章：「能與兄同心戮力，為國宣勤。 此次未行破格之獎，為鴻章功不自私，俾得報勞將士，鼓舞眾心。 指日常州、金陵次第奏捷，克成全功，更當與郭松林、劉銘傳等同膺懋賞。 」鶴章進趨常州，與劉銘傳會攻，破援賊，解奔牛之圍。 三年四月，克常州，賜黃馬褂，授甘肅甘涼道。 是年冬，曾國籓調其軍赴湖北。
Li Hezhang, whose courtesy name was Jiquan, came from Hefei in Anhui and was the younger brother of Grand Secretary Li Hongzhang. He held the licentiate degree. He followed his father and elder brother in raising home-district militia, fought with distinction on repeated campaigns, and was appointed supplemental sub-prefect. In the eleventh year of Xianfeng he helped take the rebel stockade at Linghu and recover Anqing, was promoted to magistrate, and received the peacock feather. In the first year of Tongzhi he marched with Hongzhang to Jiangsu and regularly led his bodyguard in the field alongside the commander. At Beixinjing, Sikou, and other battles his contributions ranked first. He also stormed the rebel forts at Zhifushan and Xupu Haikou and persuaded Qian Senren, the rebel holding Changshu, to surrender. Hongzhang, avoiding nepotism, omitted his brother's name from the victory report; the throne asked why, then ordered his merits counted like any other officer's; he was appointed supplemental prefect with fourth-rank brevet. In the second year he took part in the capture of Taicang and the advance on Suzhou. The force was split in two; Cheng Xueqi commanded the column moving on Kunshan; the column coming from Changshu toward Jiangyin was under Hezhang's command. He fought through Wangzhuang in Changshu and Nangan, Beian, and Gushan in Jiangyin, destroying rebel forts and relief columns until Jiangyin fell; he was promoted to prefect. He pressed the attack on Wuxi, where Huang Zilong held out grimly while Li Xiucheng sent relief again and again; after Suzhou fell, fugitive rebels massed there too; Hezhang led the combined land and river force that finally took Wuxi and was placed on the list for circuit intendant. The throne praised Hezhang: "He has served the state alongside his brother with one heart and one will. No extraordinary reward was given this time so that Hongzhang, not favoring kin, could still honor his officers and keep morale high. Soon victories at Changzhou and Nanjing will follow; when the campaign is complete, he should stand with Guo Songlin, Liu Mingchuan, and the rest among the chief honorees. " Hezhang pushed on toward Changzhou, joined Liu Mingchuan, routed the relief force, and raised the siege of Benniu. In the fourth month of the third year Changzhou fell; he received the yellow riding jacket and was appointed to the Gan-Liang circuit in Gansu. That winter Zeng Guofan ordered his army to Hubei.
17
四年，以甘肅回亂棘，命赴本任，鶴章以傷發未行。 尋疾甚，國籓為奏請開缺，留襄營務。 未久，乞病歸，遂不出。 以捐助山西賑金，加二品銜。 光緒六年，卒於家。 曾國荃疏陳：「李鴻章平江蘇，鶴章與程學啟各分統一路。 請將戰績宣付史館，於立功地建專祠。 」允之。 子經羲，官至云貴總督。
In the fourth year, with the Muslim revolt in Gansu growing severe, he was ordered to his post, but his old wounds reopened and he could not travel. His illness soon worsened; Guofan asked that he be relieved of his post but kept on to help with camp business. Before long he retired on grounds of illness and never returned to public life. For contributing to Shanxi famine relief he was given second-rank brevet. In the sixth year of Guangxu he died at home. Zeng Guoquan wrote: "In Li Hongzhang's pacification of Jiangsu, Hezhang and Cheng Xueqi each commanded a separate column. He asked that the campaigns be recorded in the Historiographical Office and a shrine erected where Hezhang had won his fame. The request was granted. His son Jingyi rose to governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou.
18
弟昭慶，初從曾國籓軍，淮軍既立，國籓留五營，令昭慶領之，駐防無為、廬江。 同治元年，從鴻章至上海，解常熟圍，克嘉興、常州，皆在事有功。 四年，國籓督師剿捻匪，昭慶總理營務，統武毅、忠樸等軍。 及鴻章代國籓，令赴前敵擊賊，馳逐鄂、皖、東、豫之間，累擢至記名鹽運使。 捻匪平，留防江、淮。 十二年，卒，贈太常寺卿。
His younger brother Zhaoqing first served under Zeng Guofan; when the Huai Army was formed, Guofan kept five battalions under Zhaoqing's command at Wuwei and Lujiang. In the first year of Tongzhi he went with Hongzhang to Shanghai, raised the siege of Changshu, and helped take Jiaxing and Changzhou. In the fourth year, when Guofan took the field against the Nian, Zhaoqing ran camp administration and commanded the Wuyi, Zhongpu, and related forces. When Hongzhang succeeded Guofan, Zhaoqing was sent to the front and harried the rebels across Hubei, Anhui, Shandong, and Henan until he was registered for salt transport commissioner. After the Nian were suppressed he stayed on garrison duty along the Yangzi and Huai. In the twelfth year he died and was posthumously made vice-president of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices.
19
吳毓蘭，安徽合肥人。 咸豐十年，粵、捻合擾皖北，毓蘭以從九品偕兄毓芬集團練助剿鳳、潁間，從解壽州圍，擢縣丞。 同治元年，李鴻章率師援上海，毓蘭從軍東下，克柘林、奉賢、南匯、川沙、青浦、金山，皆與有功，擢知縣。 二年，克嘉定，解北新涇、四江口之圍，加同知銜，領華字副營。 擊賊吳江八斥、牛尾墩、同里等處，進克平望、黎里，調守嘉善。 三年，率所部從總兵程學啟攻嘉興，戰於合歡橋。 毓蘭率槍船冒險渡河，先破賊卡，繞出賊營后，立拔之。 進抵城下，賊以巨砲拒河口，學啟被傷，毓蘭率先鋒攻益厲，掘河口架橋濟師，晝夜環攻，轟陷城垣百餘丈。 賊死抗不下，賊酋黃文金自湖州來援，力擊走之，遂克嘉興。 毓蘭緣梯先登，擢直隸州知州，賜花翎。
Wu Yulan came from Hefei in Anhui. In the tenth year of Xianfeng, when Taiping and Nian raided northern Anhui together, Yulan—then a purchased sub-official—joined his brother Yufen in raising militia between Fengyang and Yingzhou, helped relieve Shouzhou, and was promoted to county assistant. In the first year of Tongzhi, when Li Hongzhang marched to Shanghai, Yulan went east with the army, fought at Zhelin, Fengxian, Nanhui, Chuansha, Qingpu, and Jinshan, and was promoted to magistrate. In the second year he took Jiading, helped lift the sieges of Beixinjing and Sikou, received vice-prefect brevet, and commanded the Huazi secondary battalion. He fought rebels at Bachie, Niuweidun, and Tongli in Wujiang, took Pingwang and Lili, and was posted to guard Jiashan. In the third year he led his men under Major Cheng Xueqi against Jiaxing and fought at Hehuan Bridge. Yulan took his gunboats across under fire, smashed the rebel barrier first, swung behind the camp, and overran it. At the walls the rebels blocked the river with heavy guns; when Xueqi was wounded, Yulan led the van in a still harder assault, dug approaches and threw bridges across the river, and for days and nights hammered the walls until more than a hundred zhang collapsed. The defenders fought to the last; Huang Wengin marched from Huzhou to relieve them; Yulan beat him back and the city fell. Yulan was first over the wall; he was promoted to magistrate of Zhili and given the peacock feather.
20
調守溧陽，降賊屯城中，勢岌岌，突有金壇賊至，毓蘭與兄毓芬議乘賊初至破之，設伏以誘。 賊敗走烏鴉嶺，毓蘭與毓芬兩路夾擊，擒斬無算。 窮追至建平境，陣斬賊目林得英、黃有才，擒黃金龍。 溧陽既定，調守長興。 時大軍已破湖州，毓蘭偵賊將竄泗安鎮，與毓芬夜率健卒八百冒雨疾走，潛渡觀音橋，賊不意兵至，棄糧械而走。 追至泗安，降者數千，敘功擢知府。 四年，調守揚州，移廬州。 五年，回屯揚州。 追論平浙西功，以道員選用。
Posted to Liyang, with surrendered rebels still in the city and danger pressing in, Jin Tan rebels suddenly appeared; Yulan and his brother Yufen agreed to hit them before they settled in and laid an ambush. The rebels broke and ran to Wuya Ridge; Yulan and Yufen caught them in a two-winged attack and killed without number. The pursuit carried into Jianping, where they killed the rebel officers Lin Deying and Huang Youcai and captured Huang Jinlong. Once Liyang was secure, he was posted to guard Changxing. The main army had already taken Huzhou; Yulan learned the rebels meant to slip away to Si'an Town and, with Yufen, led eight hundred picked men through the rain by night, crossed Guanyin Bridge unnoticed, and caught the enemy so suddenly that they fled, abandoning grain and arms. He pursued them to Si'an, where thousands surrendered; for this he was promoted to prefect. In the fourth year he was posted to Yangzhou and then to Luzhou. In the fifth year he returned to camp at Yangzhou. His role in pacifying western Zhejiang was reviewed and he was selected for appointment as circuit intendant.
21
六年，捻匪賴文光敗竄至揚州，為毓蘭所獲，以道員記名簡放。 七年，尋加布政使銜。 十年，李鴻章調充海防營務處，筦天津機器局。 光緒六年，授天津河間兵備道。 濱海多盜，毓蘭按名捕置諸法。 修南運河、子牙河堤，及千里堤灣，靜海、軍糧城河道，興水利。 八年，卒，優恤，附祀曾國籓天津專祠，揚州建專祠。
In the sixth year the defeated Nian leader Lai Wenguang fled to Yangzhou and was captured by Yulan; he was placed on the list for circuit intendant. In the seventh year he received the brevet rank of provincial administration commissioner. In the tenth year Li Hongzhang assigned him to coastal-defense staff work and placed him over the Tianjin arsenal. In the sixth year of Guangxu he was appointed to the Tianjin-Hejian military preparedness circuit. The coast was infested with pirates; Yulan tracked them down by name and punished them under the law. He repaired the Southern Grand Canal, the Ziya River dikes, Qianli Embankment Bay, and the waterways at Jinghai and Junliangcheng, advancing hydraulic works throughout the district. In the eighth year he died; the court granted exceptional mourning honors, added him to the side altar in Zeng Guofan's Tianjin shrine, and built him a temple of his own at Yangzhou.
22
論曰：金國琛為羅、李舊部。 黃淳熙后起，獨立一幟，雖非楚籍，并為湘軍名將。 淳熙戰勝殞身，國琛遭忌鎩羽，皆未盡其才。 吳坤修、康國器起於令尉，功施爛然。 李鶴章才績出眾，堪膺大用，后竟不出。 吳毓蘭以擒獲巨憝顯名。 功名之際，遭際固難測哉！
The historian comments: Jin Guochen belonged to the old school of Luo Zeyong and Li Xubing. Huang Chunxi came up later and stood as a force in his own right; though not a Hunan man, both he and Guochen ranked among the great Hunan commanders. Chunxi won glory and died in battle; Guochen was checked by envy—neither man lived to exhaust his abilities. Wu Kunxiu and Kang Guoqi rose from the lowest ranks, yet their deeds shone brilliantly. Li Hezhang's ability and record were outstanding and he was fit for the highest posts, yet he never returned to public service. Wu Yulan made his name by capturing a chief rebel leader. When merit and reward meet, who can tell what fate will allow!
