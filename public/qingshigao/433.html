2
金國琛，字逸亭，江蘇江陰人。 咸豐中，以諸生謁羅澤南於江西，使參軍事。 每出戰，部伍嚴整，倉猝犯之，屹然不亂。 轉戰弋陽、廣信、武昌、黃州，累功擢知縣。 七年，李續賓代澤南，使總理營務。 率師會襲湖口，克之。 進復彭澤小姑洑、泰坪關，擊退援賊，晉秩同知直隸州。 八年，從克九江，窺安徽，下太湖、潛山、桐城。 續賓戰歿三河，國琛與其弟續宜招集散亡，勞徠撫慰，重申紀律，軍勢復振。
Jin Guochen, style name Yiting, came from Jiangyin in Jiangsu Province. During the Xianfeng reign, while still a licentiate scholar, he sought out Luo Zinan in Jiangxi and was placed on his staff to assist with military affairs. On every campaign his units held tight formation, and even when struck without warning they stood their ground without falling into disorder. After fighting across Yiyang, Guangxin, Wuchang, and Huangzhou, he was promoted to magistrate for his accumulated service. In the seventh year of the reign, Li Xubin succeeded Luo Zinan and put him in charge of overall camp administration. He led his forces in a joint assault on Hukou and took the town. Pushing forward, he retook Xiaogufu in Pengze and Taiping Pass, drove off rebel reinforcements, and was promoted to sub-prefect of Zhili Prefecture. In the eighth year he took part in the capture of Jiujiang, then turned toward Anhui and seized Taihu, Qianshan, and Tongcheng. After Li Xubin was killed at Sanhe, Guochen and Xubin's brother Xuyi rallied the scattered troops, soothed and reassured them, restored discipline, and brought the army back to fighting strength.
3
九年秋，石達開犯湖南，圍寶慶。 國琛從續宜赴援，毀田家渡賊壘，又敗賊賀家坳，斬悍賊胡德孝，賊走廣西，擢知府。 其冬，胡林翼、曾國籓規皖，精兵猛將萃於潛山、太湖。 陳玉成糾眾數十萬，結捻匪龔瞎子圍鮑超於小池驛，救兵迭失利。 先是林翼以國琛行軍善規地勢，令率十四營冒雪趨天堂備援。 至事急，乃出高橫嶺，屯仰天庵，俯視賊營，皆在目中。 賊驟見旗幟，大驚。 十年正月，賊乘霧登山來犯，國琛揮軍突起躪之，合山下軍奮擊，斬馘逾萬，乘勝克潛山、太湖。 林翼疏陳：「非鮑軍之堅忍，不能久持； 非國琛之出奇制勝，不能轉危為安。 」論功，擢道員。
In the ninth year, autumn, Shi Dakai marched into Hunan and laid siege to Baoqing. Guochen marched with Xuyi to relieve the siege, destroyed the rebel fort at Tianjiadu, then routed the rebels at Hejia'ao and killed the fierce rebel leader Hu Dexiao. The rebels fled into Guangxi, and Guochen was promoted to prefect. That winter Hu Lin Yisi and Zeng Guofan turned their attention to Anhui, concentrating crack troops and seasoned commanders at Qianshan and Taihu. Chen Yucheng raised several hundred thousand men, allied with the Nian bandit known as Blind Gong, and besieged Bao Chao at Xiao Chi Post, while successive relief columns met with defeat. Earlier, knowing that Guochen was adept at reading terrain on campaign, Hu Lin Yisi had ordered him to lead fourteen battalions through the snow to Tiantang to prepare for relief operations. When the crisis came to a head, he marched out through Gaoheng Ridge and encamped at Yangtian'an, from which he could look down and see every rebel camp below. The rebels were startled when they suddenly caught sight of his banners. In the first month of the tenth year the rebels used the fog to climb the heights and attack. Guochen led his troops in a sudden uphill charge, joined with the forces below in a fierce assault, and killed or captured more than ten thousand men. Pressing the advantage, he then took Qianshan and Taihu. Hu Lin Yisi wrote in a memorial: "Without the steadfast endurance of Bao's troops, the position could not have been held for so long; without Guochen's surprise tactics and decisive victory, danger could not have been turned to safety. " For his merits he was promoted to circuit intendant.
4
十一年，粵匪復犯湖北，國琛馳援武昌，連復孝感、云夢，進攻德安。 賊酋馬融和死斗，卒以長圍克之，加布政使銜。 尋授湖北安襄鄖荊道，仍兼治軍。 樊城地沖要，商賈所集，督軍士筑土城，不煩民力，賴為保障。 時捻匪西擾關中，命國琛率師赴援，以鄖西戒嚴，留未行。
In the eleventh year the Taiping rebels again invaded Hubei. Guochen rushed to relieve Wuchang, retook Xiaogan and Yunmeng in succession, and advanced against De'an. The rebel chieftain Ma Ronghe fought to the death, but in the end the city fell after a prolonged siege, and Guochen was granted the nominal rank of provincial administration commissioner. He was soon appointed intendant of the An-Xiang-Yun-Jing Circuit in Hubei, while continuing to command troops as well. Fancheng stood at a strategic crossroads and drew merchants from far and wide. He had his soldiers build an earthen wall without calling on the local populace for labor, and the fortification served as a reliable defense. When Nian bandits began raiding west into the Guanzhong region, he was ordered to lead troops to their relief, but martial law was declared in Yunxi and he was kept from setting out.
5
同治元年，馬融和以眾六萬圍南陽，國琛越境往援，力戰解城圍，拔出難民數萬。 巡撫嚴樹森忌之，劾其不遵調度，以同知降補。 二年，曾國籓調統義從營。 擊賊徽州，屢捷於豹嶺、佛嶺、黃傋口、小溪。 皖南肅清，復原官，補甘肅鞏秦階道。 以母老假歸。 光緒元年，起復廣東督糧道，擢按察使。 五年，卒于官。
In the first year of the Tongzhi reign Ma Ronghe besieged Nanyang with sixty thousand men. Guochen crossed into the neighboring province to relieve the city, fought fiercely to break the siege, and rescued tens of thousands of civilians. Governor Yan Shusen, jealous of his success, impeached him for disobeying orders, and he was demoted and reassigned at the rank of sub-prefect. In the second year Zeng Guofan transferred him to command the Volunteer Followers Battalion. He fought the rebels in Huizhou and won repeated victories at Baoling, Foling, Huangkangkou, and Xiaoxi. After southern Anhui was pacified, his original rank was restored and he was appointed intendant of the Gong-Qin-Jie Circuit in Gansu. He took leave to return home because his mother was elderly. In the first year of the Guangxu reign he was recalled to office as grain transport commissioner of Guangdong and promoted to provincial judicial commissioner. In the fifth year he died in office.
6
國琛以儒生治軍十餘年，堅苦踔厲，號為名將。 居官亦有政聲。
Guochen had led troops for more than a decade despite being a scholar by training; resolute and tireless in hardship, he was acclaimed as a famous general. As a civil official he also earned a reputation for effective administration.
7
黃淳熙，字子春，江西鄱陽人。 道光二十七年進士，湖南即用知縣，歷署綏寧、會同。 剛直為時所忌，引疾閒居。 咸豐三年，巡撫駱秉章廉知其賢，使強起之。 七年，署湘鄉，有異政。 尋丁父憂。 鄱陽方陷賊，移家於湘鄉。 曾國籓方起督浙江軍，辟參軍事，不就。 九年，石達開犯湖南，秉章檄淳熙募勇千六百人防省城，時出剿賊。 達開由寶慶竄踞嶺東，分黨犯江華，淳熙破之於掛勾嶺，遂夜襲嶺東賊營，躡至江、藍，殲殪甚眾。 進剿賊黨賴裕新，乘霧敗之，破杉木根、黃馬寨而還。 十年，達開黨眾四出，淳熙轉戰於永、道、綏、靖諸州，復宜章、桂陽。 前后三十餘戰，皆捷，累擢知府，以道員記名。 所部曰果毅營，增至三千人。
Huang Chunxi, style name Zichun, came from Poyang in Jiangxi Province. He passed the jinshi examination in the twenty-seventh year of the Daoguang reign and was appointed directly as a Hunan magistrate, serving in succession as acting magistrate of Suining and Huitong. His blunt integrity made him unpopular, and he pleaded illness and withdrew into private life. In the third year of the Xianfeng reign Governor Luo Bingzhang, having learned of his integrity and ability, pressed him to return to office. In the seventh year he served as acting magistrate of Xiangxiang and distinguished himself in office. He soon entered mourning upon his father's death. Poyang had just fallen to the rebels, so he moved his family to Xiangxiang. Zeng Guofan had just taken command of the Zhejiang campaign and invited him to join his staff, but he declined. In the ninth year, when Shi Dakai invaded Hunan, Luo Bingzhang ordered Chunxi to raise sixteen hundred militiamen to defend the provincial capital and sent him out repeatedly to suppress rebel bands. Shi Dakai broke out from Baoqing and seized Lingdong, then sent detachments against Jianghua. Chunxi routed them at Guagou Ridge, launched a night attack on the rebel camp at Lingdong, pursued the enemy as far as Jiang and Lan, and killed a great many. He advanced against the rebel band led by Lai Yuxin, used the fog to defeat them, stormed Shanmugen and Huangmazhai, and then withdrew. In the tenth year Shi Dakai's followers fanned out in all directions. Chunxi fought across Yong, Dao, Sui, and Jing prefectures and recovered Yizhang and Guiyang. In more than thirty engagements he was victorious every time, was repeatedly promoted to prefect, and was registered by name for appointment as circuit intendant. His command, known as the Guoyi Battalion, grew to three thousand men.
8
駱秉章奉命赴四川督師，湘軍名將勁兵多從曾國籓、胡林翼，劉蓉薦淳熙兵精善戰，秉章遂以淳熙與劉岳昭從行。 至荊州，岳昭復留，獨淳熙以所部當軍鋒。 分道溯峽上，次萬縣，聞順慶被圍，率師赴援。 五月，至，賊走定遠，追之，距定遠二十里，望賊屯城西南，連十餘里，城東北江水環繞，賊方造浮橋渡水。 淳熙分三路進，遇賊即前搏之，擲火焚其屯，賊大亂爭走，二十餘壘悉潰，擒斬數千。 殲首賊何國樑，解散脅從萬餘人。 賊黨彭紹福率眾千餘屯東岸，收集潰賊，竄二郎場，淳熙銳進，欲一戰平之。 二郎場在山中，羊腸曲逕，通遂寧兩路，均為涪江阻。 別賊硃甲眾數千由青岡壩至，四路設伏。 淳熙遣偵不見賊，五鼓師行，賊燕子窠，擊走之，逼二郎場。 賊分兩路繞山麓上，淳熙知有伏，令諸營左右搜捕，自率中軍策應。 兵分，伏賊起，遍布山岡。 官軍走田塍，泥深輒陷。 淳熙率親卒拒戰，被圍，策馬突陣，陷淖中，棄馬，手刃十餘賊，中矛仆，擁至場，支解燔之。 賊懾其軍勇猛，不復追，餘部整隊還，賊亦遁走。 淳熙雖戰歿，湘軍之威因之頓振。 詔贈布政使，賜恤，加贈內閣學士，謚忠壯。
When Luo Bingzhang was ordered to Sichuan to take command, most of the Hunan Army's famous generals and crack troops remained with Zeng Guofan and Hu Lin Yisi. Liu Rong recommended Chunxi as commanding elite troops skilled in battle, and Bingzhang accordingly took Chunxi and Liu Yuezhao with him. At Jingzhou Liu Yuezhao was again held back, and Chunxi alone led his own troops at the army's spearhead. They divided their forces and ascended the gorges, halting at Wanxian. When word came that Shunqing was besieged, Chunxi led his troops to relieve the city. He arrived in the fifth month. The rebels withdrew toward Dingyuan, and he pursued them. Twenty li from Dingyuan he saw their encampment southwest of the city stretching for more than ten li. The river wrapped around the northeast side of the city, and the rebels were building a pontoon bridge to cross it. Chunxi advanced in three columns, closed with the rebels wherever he met them, and set fire to their camps. The rebels broke in panic, more than twenty forts collapsed, and several thousand were killed or captured. He killed the rebel chieftain He Guoliang and released more than ten thousand coerced followers. The rebel Peng Shaofu rallied more than a thousand men on the east bank, gathered the routed rebels, and withdrew to Erlangchang. Chunxi pressed forward aggressively, intending to finish them in a single battle. Erlangchang lay in the mountains along narrow, winding tracks. Two routes led toward Suining, both cut off by the Fu River. Another rebel band, the Zhujia force numbering several thousand, came from Qinggangba and laid ambushes along four routes. Chunxi sent out scouts but found no rebels. At the fifth watch his army marched out, drove off rebels at Swallow Nest, and pressed on toward Erlangchang. The rebels split into two columns and circled up along the foot of the mountain. Sensing an ambush, Chunxi ordered his battalions to search the flanks while he personally led the central force in support. As his troops spread out, ambushing rebels sprang up all along the hill ridges. The government troops fell back along the field embankments, sinking deep into the mud wherever they stepped. Chunxi led his personal guard in a stand, but was surrounded. He spurred his horse to break through the enemy lines, sank into the mire, abandoned his mount, and killed more than ten rebels with his own blade before a spear brought him down. The rebels dragged him to the field, dismembered his body, and burned it. Overawed by the army's ferocity, the rebels did not pursue further. The surviving units re-formed and withdrew, and the rebels also fled. Although Chunxi died in battle, the shock of his stand restored the Hunan Army's prestige overnight. An edict posthumously granted him the rank of provincial administration commissioner, awarded state mourning honors, further granted him the rank of Grand Secretariat academician, and gave him the posthumous title Loyal and Valiant.
9
吳坤修，字竹莊，江西新建人。 捐納從九，分發湖南。 道光二十九年，賑湘陰水災，勤於事。 從剿李沅發，以府經歷、縣丞補用。 咸豐二年，粵匪犯長沙，以守城功擢知縣。 曾國籓創立水師，坤修司軍械。 四年，水師攻九江，入鄱陽湖，為賊所阻不得出，令坤修單騎往南康，導往吳城、南昌。 五年，率舟師防瑞豐。 以父憂歸。 既而武昌復陷，坤修從羅澤南援湖北，復咸寧、蒲圻、崇陽、通城，累擢同知，賜花翎。 進規武昌。
Wu Kunxiu, style name Zhuzhuang, came from Xinjian in Jiangxi Province. He purchased appointment as a ninth-rank subofficial and was assigned to Hunan. In the twenty-ninth year of the Daoguang reign he directed flood relief in Xiangyin and proved diligent in the work. After taking part in the campaign against Li Yuanfa, he was appointed prefectural registrar and county assistant. In the second year of the Xianfeng reign, when the Taiping rebels attacked Changsha, he was promoted to magistrate for his service in the city's defense. When Zeng Guofan established the river fleet, Kunxiu was put in charge of ordnance. In the fourth year the river fleet attacked Jiujiang and entered Poyang Lake, but rebel forces blocked the exit. Zeng Guofan sent Kunxiu alone on horseback to Nankang to guide the fleet to Wucheng and Nanchang. In the fifth year he led the fleet to guard Ruifeng. He returned home to observe mourning for his father. When Wuchang fell again, Kunxiu followed Luo Zinan to relieve Hubei, recovered Xianning, Puqi, Chongyang, and Tongcheng, was repeatedly promoted to sub-prefect, and was awarded the peacock feather. He then turned to the campaign against Wuchang.
10
六年，江西軍事不利，胡林翼令坤修領新募軍曰彪字營，會湘軍援江西。 復新昌、上高。 由新昌取道羅坊攻奉新，梯城而登，賊死守不能拔，乃先下安義、靖安，后萃軍奉新。 時江西餉絀，坤修傾家貲，并勸族里富人出銀米餉軍； 又籌銀四萬兩解省垣，收集平江潰勇。 七年春，克奉新，累擢道員。 尋授廣東南韶連道，仍留軍，克瑞州。 是年冬，東鄉師潰，被劾褫職。 九年，駐師撫州。 江西巡撫耆齡檄督辦撫、建、寧三屬團練，始立團防營，駐貴溪。 移德興，出援徽州。 十年，克建德。 秋，徽防軍潰，坤修方假歸，其弟修敳攝軍事，守嶺外郭村。 調回江西，曾國籓令守湖口，而巡撫毓科檄援建昌。 賊由金谿竄東鄉，坤修自撫州迎擊於鄧家埠，大破之。 賊復出貴溪竄安仁，遏之不得渡河，乃竄德興、萬年，將擾景德鎮。 坤修由饒州馳援景德，以固祁門大軍后路。 會賊由建德上犯，國籓令援湖口。 坤修且戰且進，先賊至，城恃以完，加鹽運使銜。
In the sixth year, when the Jiangxi campaign faltered, Hu Lin Yisi ordered Kunxiu to lead newly recruited troops known as the Biaozi Battalion and join the Hunan Army in relieving Jiangxi. He recovered Xinchang and Shanggao. From Xinchang he marched by way of Luofang to attack Fengxin, scaling the walls with ladders. The rebels held stubbornly and the city could not be taken, so he first captured Anyi and Jing'an, then concentrated his forces at Fengxin. At the time Jiangxi's supplies were exhausted. Kunxiu poured out his family fortune and also persuaded wealthy men in his clan and district to contribute silver and grain to supply the army; he also raised forty thousand taels of silver for the provincial capital and gathered the routed militiamen of Pingjiang. In the spring of the seventh year he captured Fengxin and was repeatedly promoted to circuit intendant. He was soon appointed intendant of the Nan-Shao-Lian Circuit in Guangdong but remained with the army and captured Ruizhou. That winter, after the army was routed at Dongxiang, he was impeached and stripped of his post. In the ninth year he stationed his troops at Fuzhou. Jiangxi Governor Qiling ordered him to supervise militia training in Fu, Jian, and Ning districts. He established the Tuanfang Battalion and stationed it at Guixi. He moved to Dexing and marched out to relieve Huizhou. In the tenth year he captured Jiande. That autumn the Huizhou garrison collapsed. Kunxiu was on leave, and his younger brother Xiuyi took command and held Guocun outside the pass. He was transferred back to Jiangxi. Zeng Guofan ordered him to guard Hukou, but Governor Yuke ordered him to relieve Jianchang instead. When the rebels broke out from Jinxi into Dongxiang, Kunxiu marched from Fuzhou to intercept them at Dengjiabu and routed them. The rebels broke out again from Guixi toward Anren. He blocked their crossing of the river, so they fled into Dexing and Wannian, intending to raid Jingdezhen. Kunxiu rushed from Raozhou to relieve Jingde and secure the rear of the main army at Qimen. Just then the rebels advanced from Jiande, and Zeng Guofan ordered him to relieve Hukou. Kunxiu fought his way forward and reached the city before the rebels, saving it from capture. He was granted the nominal rank of salt transport commissioner.
11
同治元年，李秀成自蘇州援江寧，分犯蕪湖，會軍擊卻之，又會克金保圩、高淳、溧水及溧陽、東壩各要隘，遣散降眾數萬。 三年，加布政使銜。 江寧克復，以按察使記名。 四年，署徽寧池太廣道，授安徽按察使。 五年，署布政使。 六年，巡撫英翰駐潁州，出境剿捻，坤修轉輸餉運，未嘗遲乏。 七年，署巡撫，實授布政使。 東捻平，請假回籍補終父母喪。 九年，回任。 十一年，卒。 巡撫英翰疏陳其戰功政績，賜恤，贈內閣學士。
In the first year of the Tongzhi reign Li Xiucheng marched from Suzhou to relieve Jiangning and sent detachments against Wuhu. Kunxiu joined the allied forces in repulsing them, then helped capture the key positions of Jinbaowei, Gaochun, Lishui, Liyang, and Dongba, and disbanded tens of thousands of surrendered rebels. In the third year he was granted the nominal rank of provincial administration commissioner. After Jiangning was recovered, he was registered by name for appointment as provincial judicial commissioner. In the fourth year he served as acting intendant of the Hui-Ning-Chi-Tai-Guang Circuit and was appointed provincial judicial commissioner of Anhui. In the fifth year he served as acting provincial administration commissioner. In the sixth year Governor Yinghan was stationed at Yingzhou and marched beyond the border to suppress the Nian bandits. Kunxiu handled supply transport and never once fell behind or came up short. In the seventh year he served as acting governor and was formally appointed provincial administration commissioner. After the eastern Nian were pacified, he took leave to return home and complete the mourning period for his parents. In the ninth year he returned to his post. In the eleventh year he died. Governor Yinghan memorialized his military and administrative achievements. The court granted mourning honors and posthumously awarded him the rank of Grand Secretariat academician.
12
康國器，初名以泰，字交修，廣東南海人。 少為吏員。 道光末，從軍，以勞授江西贛縣桂源司巡檢。 咸豐初，粵匪犯江西，土寇蜂起，國器募死士三百，贛南道周玉衡檄擊賊烏兜、良口，克萬安。 造船三十艘，習水戰。 六年，從克饒州，累擢知縣，署南城。 石達開陷瑞、撫、臨、吉四郡，國器從克樟樹鎮，連戰瑞州、臨江、鉛山、安仁，擢同知。 十一年，廣東巡撫耆齡檄剿陽山賊。 賊踞藍山，地阻絕，負隅十餘年。 國器緣崖歷磵出賊后，破石柵九，奪砲臺，毀其老巢。 遣子熊飛單騎說降劇賊練四虎，其魁梁柱走豬頭寨，穴山攻獲之。 進軍赫巖，擒賊渠周裕等。 藍山平，擢知府。 同治元年，援浙，從蔣益澧圍湯溪，明年春，克之，擢道員。 三年，克餘杭，功最多，授福建延建邵道，始專統一軍。
Kang Guoqi, originally named Yitai, style name Jiaoxiu, came from Nanhai in Guangdong Province. In his youth he served as a minor clerk. At the end of the Daoguang reign he joined the army and, for his service, was appointed patrol inspector of Guiyuan Subdistrict in Gan County, Jiangxi. At the beginning of the Xianfeng reign the Taiping rebels invaded Jiangxi and local bandits rose everywhere. Guoqi recruited three hundred dare-to-die fighters, and Gannan Circuit Intendant Zhou Yuheng ordered attacks on rebel positions at Wudou and Liangkou, capturing Wan'an. He built thirty boats and trained his men in river warfare. In the sixth year he took part in the capture of Raozhou, was repeatedly promoted to magistrate, and served as acting magistrate of Nancheng. When Shi Dakai overran the four prefectures of Rui, Fu, Lin, and Ji, Guoqi took part in the capture of Zhangshu Town and fought continuously at Ruizhou, Linjiang, Qianshan, and Anren, earning promotion to sub-prefect. In the eleventh year Guangdong Governor Qiling ordered him to suppress the rebels in Yangshan. The rebels held Lanshan, where the terrain was nearly impassable, and had defied the government there for more than a decade. Guoqi climbed cliffs and crossed streams to get behind the rebels, stormed nine stone palisades, seized their artillery emplacements, and destroyed their stronghold. He sent his son Xiongfei alone on horseback to persuade the fierce rebel Lian Sihu to surrender. When the chieftain Liang Zhu fled to Zhutou Stockade, Guoqi's forces tunneled into the mountain and captured him. Advancing to Heyan, he captured the rebel leaders Zhou Yu and others. After Lanshan was pacified, he was promoted to prefect. In the first year of the Tongzhi reign he marched to relieve Zhejiang, joined Jiang Yili in besieging Tangxi, and captured the city the following spring, earning promotion to circuit intendant. In the third year he captured Yuhang with the greatest distinction, was appointed intendant of the Yan-Jian-Shao Circuit in Fujian, and for the first time commanded an army of his own.
13
粵匪汪海洋犯閩，陷武平、永平，李世賢踞漳州、龍巖與之合，旁郡縣多沒於賊。 左宗棠議三路進兵，國器自請當龍巖。 進軍雁石，令熊飛壁鐵石洋，三戰薄城下，破其眾數萬，并敗古田援賊。 四年正月，遂克龍巖。 賊走永定，分踞苦竹、奎洋，勢猶熾。 國器進擊苦竹，乘夜大霧，火賊營，破二十餘壘。 海洋以悍黨來援，敗之於東阬，又敗之大溪，乃竄廣東大浦境。 未幾，海洋復犯永定，國器馳毀羅灘橋； 賊分七路來撲，海洋自陣獅龍嶺，所部皆死黨，旗幟遍巖谷。 國器曰：「賊精銳盡萃於此，若摧之，餘眾必奔。 」乃堅壁深溝，伺怠出擊。 先破其伏，分道猛進，斬馘數千，盡獲其軍實，海洋跳而免。 時漳州亦下，李世賢西遁，遇國器於塔下，縱兵擊之，降其眾二萬人。 海洋走廣東，踞鎮平。 國器進壁鎮平東南高思塘，分軍扼程官埠，賊數來犯，卻之。 國器知海洋將襲高思而虛攻程官埠，乃戒程官軍勿為動，設伏兩山間。 海洋果率悍黨來撲，誘入，伏突起，槍斃其梟汪大力、黃十四，海洋傷腕，陣斃及墮巖磵死者無數。 胡瞎子攻程官，亦敗走。 尋克鎮平。 十二月，會諸軍擊賊嘉應，海洋伏誅，餘孽悉平。
The Taiping rebel Wang Haiyang invaded Fujian, capturing Wuping and Yongping, while Li Shixian held Zhangzhou and Longyan in alliance with him. Many neighboring prefectures and counties fell to the rebels. Zuo Zongtang proposed a three-column advance, and Guoqi volunteered to take Longyan himself. Advancing to Yanshi, he ordered Xiongfei to fortify Tieshiyang. After three battles he pressed to the city walls, routed a rebel force of tens of thousands, and also defeated relief troops from Gutian. In the first month of the fourth year he finally captured Longyan. The rebels fled to Yongding and held Kuchu and Kuiyang separately, and their strength remained formidable. Guoqi advanced against Kuchu, used a heavy night fog to set fire to the rebel camps, and stormed more than twenty forts. Haiyang came with fierce followers to relieve them. Guoqi defeated them at Dongkeng and again at Daxi, and they fled into the Dapu region of Guangdong. Before long Haiyang invaded Yongding again, and Guoqi rushed to destroy the Luotan Bridge; the rebels attacked in seven columns, with Haiyang himself commanding at Shilong Ridge. His followers were all die-hard loyalists, and their banners filled the cliffs and valleys. Guoqi said, "The rebels' best troops are all gathered here. If we break them, the rest will surely flee. " He therefore strengthened his defenses and dug deep trenches, waited for the enemy to slacken, and then struck. First he broke their ambush, then advanced fiercely in separate columns, killing or capturing several thousand and seizing all their military stores. Haiyang leaped away and escaped. By then Zhangzhou had also fallen. Li Shixian fled west, encountered Guoqi below the pagoda, and Guoqi attacked him and accepted the surrender of twenty thousand men. Haiyang fled to Guangdong and seized Zhenping. Guoqi advanced and encamped at Gaositang southeast of Zhenping, sent detachments to hold Chengguanbu, and repulsed several rebel attacks. Guoqi knew Haiyang would strike Gaosi while making a feint against Chengguanbu, so he ordered the Chengguan garrison to hold its position and set ambushes between the two mountains. Haiyang indeed led fierce followers in an attack. They were lured in, the ambush sprang up, and gunfire killed the chieftains Wang Dali and Huang Shisi. Haiyang was wounded in the wrist, and countless rebels were killed in battle or fell from cliffs and streams to their deaths. Blind Hu attacked Chengguan and was likewise defeated and driven off. He soon captured Zhenping. In the twelfth month he joined the allied armies in attacking the rebels at Jiaying. Haiyang was killed in an ambush, and the remaining rebels were all pacified.
14
五年，擢按察使。 七年，遷廣西布政使。 十年，護理巡撫。 十一年，內召，以疾歸。 光緒十年，卒。 左宗棠疏陳戰績，請恤，格於吏議，特詔允之。
In the fifth year he was promoted to provincial judicial commissioner. In the seventh year he was transferred to provincial administration commissioner of Guangxi. In the tenth year he served as acting governor. In the eleventh year he was summoned to the capital but returned home due to illness. In the tenth year of the Guangxu reign he died. Zuo Zongtang memorialized his battle achievements and requested mourning honors. The Board of Civil Office blocked the request, but a special edict approved it.
15
國器治軍能以少擊眾，常傷足而跛，軍中號康拐子，悍賊皆畏之。 子熊飛，積功至浙江候補道，勇而有謀，常為軍鋒。 國器數獲奇捷，實資其力云。
Guoqi commanded troops with a gift for defeating larger forces with smaller ones. A recurring foot wound left him lame, and in the army he was called Lame Kang; even the fiercest rebels feared him. His son Xiongfei rose through accumulated merit to expectant circuit intendant in Zhejiang. Brave and resourceful, he often led the army's spearhead. Guoqi won many extraordinary victories, and in truth owed much of his success to his son's strength.
16
李鶴章，字季荃，安徽合肥人，大學士鴻章弟。 諸生。 從父兄治本籍團練，屢出戰有功，以州同用。 咸豐十一年，從克菱湖賊壘，復安慶，擢知縣，賜花翎。 同治元年，從鴻章援江蘇，常率親兵佐督戰。 北新涇、四江口諸役，功皆最。 又攻枝福山、許浦海口賊壘，招降常熟踞賊錢森仁。 鴻章引嫌，奏捷不敘其勞，特旨詢問，命一體議敘，以知州用，加四品銜。 二年，會克太倉，規蘇州。 分諸軍為兩路，其進昆山一路，以程學啟為總統； 由常熟進江陰者，鶴章督之。 迭戰於常熟之王莊，江陰之南漍、北漍、顧山，毀賊壘，破援賊，會克江陰，擢知府。 進攻無錫，踞賊黃子隆死守，李秀成屢來援； 及蘇州既克，潰賊亦麕聚，鶴章督水陸諸軍力戰克之，以道員記名簡放。 詔嘉鶴章：「能與兄同心戮力，為國宣勤。 此次未行破格之獎，為鴻章功不自私，俾得報勞將士，鼓舞眾心。 指日常州、金陵次第奏捷，克成全功，更當與郭松林、劉銘傳等同膺懋賞。 」鶴章進趨常州，與劉銘傳會攻，破援賊，解奔牛之圍。 三年四月，克常州，賜黃馬褂，授甘肅甘涼道。 是年冬，曾國籓調其軍赴湖北。
Li Hezhang, style name Jiquan, came from Hefei in Anhui Province and was the younger brother of Grand Secretary Li Hongzhang. He was a licentiate scholar. He followed his father and elder brother in organizing local militia in his native district, repeatedly took the field with distinction, and was appointed sub-prefect. In the eleventh year of the Xianfeng reign he took part in capturing the rebel fort at Linghu and recovering Anqing, was promoted to magistrate, and was awarded the peacock feather. In the first year of the Tongzhi reign he followed Li Hongzhang to relieve Jiangsu and often led his personal guard in directing the fighting. At Beixinjing, Sikoujiang, and other engagements, his merit ranked first. He also attacked the rebel forts at Zhifushan and the Xupu river mouth and persuaded Qian Senren, the rebel holding Changshu, to surrender. Li Hongzhang declined on grounds of nepotism and omitted Hezhang's service from his victory report. A special edict inquired into the matter and ordered that his merits be considered on equal terms; he was appointed prefect with fourth-rank nominal rank. In the second year he took part in capturing Taicang and turned toward Suzhou. The armies were divided into two columns; the column advancing on Kunshan was placed under Cheng Xueqi as overall commander; the column advancing from Changshu toward Jiangyin was commanded by Hezhang. He fought repeatedly at Wangzhuang in Changshu and at Nanjiang, Beijiang, and Gushan in Jiangyin, destroyed rebel forts, routed relief forces, and took part in capturing Jiangyin, earning promotion to prefect. Advancing against Wuxi, he found the holding rebel Huang Zilong defending stubbornly while Li Xiucheng repeatedly came to his relief; after Suzhou fell, routed rebels also gathered there. Hezhang commanded land and river forces in fierce fighting and captured the city, and was registered by name for appointment as circuit intendant. An edict praised Hezhang: "You have worked in one heart and one effort with your elder brother and served the state with loyal exertion. On this occasion no extraordinary reward was granted, so that Li Hongzhang's merit would not appear self-serving and he could reward his officers and soldiers and inspire their morale. When Changzhou and Jinling report victories in succession and the full campaign is completed, you should likewise receive rich rewards together with Guo Songlin, Liu Mingchuan, and the others. " Hezhang advanced toward Changzhou, joined Liu Mingchuan in the attack, routed relief forces, and lifted the siege of Benniu. In the fourth month of the third year he captured Changzhou, was granted the yellow riding jacket, and was appointed intendant of the Gan-Liang Circuit in Gansu. That winter Zeng Guofan transferred his army to Hubei.
17
四年，以甘肅回亂棘，命赴本任，鶴章以傷發未行。 尋疾甚，國籓為奏請開缺，留襄營務。 未久，乞病歸，遂不出。 以捐助山西賑金，加二品銜。 光緒六年，卒於家。 曾國荃疏陳：「李鴻章平江蘇，鶴章與程學啟各分統一路。 請將戰績宣付史館，於立功地建專祠。 」允之。 子經羲，官至云貴總督。
In the fourth year, with the Muslim rebellion in Gansu growing urgent, he was ordered to proceed to his post, but Hezhang's old wounds reopened and he did not go. His illness soon grew severe. Zeng Guofan memorialized to declare his post vacant and kept him on to assist with camp affairs. Before long he pleaded illness and returned home, and never took office again. For contributing relief funds to Shanxi, he was granted second-rank nominal rank. In the sixth year of the Guangxu reign he died at home. Zeng Guoquan wrote in a memorial: "When Li Hongzhang pacified Jiangsu, Hezhang and Cheng Xueqi each commanded a separate column. I request that their battle achievements be sent to the Historiography Institute and that dedicated shrines be built at the places where they earned their merit. " The request was approved. His son Jingyi rose to governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou.
18
弟昭慶，初從曾國籓軍，淮軍既立，國籓留五營，令昭慶領之，駐防無為、廬江。 同治元年，從鴻章至上海，解常熟圍，克嘉興、常州，皆在事有功。 四年，國籓督師剿捻匪，昭慶總理營務，統武毅、忠樸等軍。 及鴻章代國籓，令赴前敵擊賊，馳逐鄂、皖、東、豫之間，累擢至記名鹽運使。 捻匪平，留防江、淮。 十二年，卒，贈太常寺卿。
His younger brother Zhaoqing at first served in Zeng Guofan's army. After the Huai Army was established, Guofan retained five battalions under Zhaoqing's command, stationed at Wuwei and Lujiang. In the first year of the Tongzhi reign he followed Li Hongzhang to Shanghai, lifted the siege of Changshu, and captured Jiaxing and Changzhou, distinguishing himself in each engagement. In the fourth year Zeng Guofan took command of the campaign against the Nian bandits. Zhaoqing supervised overall camp administration and commanded the Wuyi, Zhongpu, and other armies. When Li Hongzhang replaced Zeng Guofan, Zhaoqing was ordered to the front to fight the rebels and pursued them across Hubei, Anhui, Shandong, and Henan, rising in repeated promotions to registered salt transport commissioner. After the Nian bandits were pacified, he remained to garrison the Yangzi and Huai regions. In the twelfth year he died and was posthumously granted the rank of Vice Minister of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices.
19
吳毓蘭，安徽合肥人。 咸豐十年，粵、捻合擾皖北，毓蘭以從九品偕兄毓芬集團練助剿鳳、潁間，從解壽州圍，擢縣丞。 同治元年，李鴻章率師援上海，毓蘭從軍東下，克柘林、奉賢、南匯、川沙、青浦、金山，皆與有功，擢知縣。 二年，克嘉定，解北新涇、四江口之圍，加同知銜，領華字副營。 擊賊吳江八斥、牛尾墩、同里等處，進克平望、黎里，調守嘉善。 三年，率所部從總兵程學啟攻嘉興，戰於合歡橋。 毓蘭率槍船冒險渡河，先破賊卡，繞出賊營后，立拔之。 進抵城下，賊以巨砲拒河口，學啟被傷，毓蘭率先鋒攻益厲，掘河口架橋濟師，晝夜環攻，轟陷城垣百餘丈。 賊死抗不下，賊酋黃文金自湖州來援，力擊走之，遂克嘉興。 毓蘭緣梯先登，擢直隸州知州，賜花翎。
Wu Yulan came from Hefei in Anhui Province. In the tenth year of the Xianfeng reign the Taiping and Nian rebels jointly raided northern Anhui. Yulan, a ninth-rank subofficial, joined his elder brother Yufen in raising militia to suppress the region between Fengyang and Yingzhou, took part in lifting the siege of Shouzhou, and was promoted to county assistant. In the first year of the Tongzhi reign Li Hongzhang led troops to relieve Shanghai. Yulan marched east with the army, took part in capturing Zhelin, Fengxian, Nanhui, Chuansha, Qingpu, and Jinshan, and was promoted to magistrate. In the second year he captured Jiading, lifted the sieges of Beixinjing and Sikoujiang, was granted sub-prefect nominal rank, and took command of the Huazi Auxiliary Battalion. He fought rebels at Bachikou, Niuweidun, and Tongli in Wujiang, advanced to capture Pingwang and Lili, and was transferred to garrison Jiashan. In the third year he led his troops under Major General Cheng Xueqi to attack Jiaxing and fought at Hehuan Bridge. Yulan led gunboats across the river at great risk, stormed the rebel checkpoint first, circled behind the rebel camp, and captured it at once. Reaching the city walls, they found the rebels blocking the river mouth with heavy cannon. When Xueqi was wounded, Yulan led the vanguard in an even fiercer assault, dug out the river mouth and built a bridge to ferry troops across, and after day-and-night bombardment blasted a breach more than a hundred zhang wide in the city wall. The rebels resisted to the death and would not yield. When the rebel chieftain Huang Wenjin came from Huzhou to relieve them, Yulan struck hard and drove him off, and then captured Jiaxing. Yulan was the first to scale the walls by ladder, was promoted to magistrate of Zhili Prefecture, and was awarded the peacock feather.
20
調守溧陽，降賊屯城中，勢岌岌，突有金壇賊至，毓蘭與兄毓芬議乘賊初至破之，設伏以誘。 賊敗走烏鴉嶺，毓蘭與毓芬兩路夾擊，擒斬無算。 窮追至建平境，陣斬賊目林得英、黃有才，擒黃金龍。 溧陽既定，調守長興。 時大軍已破湖州，毓蘭偵賊將竄泗安鎮，與毓芬夜率健卒八百冒雨疾走，潛渡觀音橋，賊不意兵至，棄糧械而走。 追至泗安，降者數千，敘功擢知府。 四年，調守揚州，移廬州。 五年，回屯揚州。 追論平浙西功，以道員選用。
Transferred to garrison Liyang, he found surrendered rebels encamped in the city and the situation perilous. When rebels from Jintan suddenly arrived, Yulan and his elder brother Yufen agreed to strike while the enemy was still fresh and laid an ambush to lure them in. The rebels were defeated and fled to Wuya Ridge, where Yulan and Yufen attacked from two directions and killed or captured beyond count. Pursuing them to the border of Jianping, they killed the rebel officers Lin Deying and Huang Youcai in battle and captured Huang Jinlong. After Liyang was pacified, he was transferred to garrison Changxing. By then the main army had already taken Huzhou. Learning that the rebels would flee toward Si'an Town, Yulan and Yufen led eight hundred picked men on a forced night march through the rain, secretly crossed Guanyin Bridge, and caught the rebels unprepared, so they abandoned their grain and weapons and fled. Pursuing them to Si'an, he accepted the surrender of several thousand rebels and was promoted to prefect for his service. In the fourth year he was transferred to garrison Yangzhou and then moved to Luzhou. In the fifth year he returned to encamp at Yangzhou. His merit in pacifying western Zhejiang was assessed retroactively, and he was selected for appointment as circuit intendant.
21
六年，捻匪賴文光敗竄至揚州，為毓蘭所獲，以道員記名簡放。 七年，尋加布政使銜。 十年，李鴻章調充海防營務處，筦天津機器局。 光緒六年，授天津河間兵備道。 濱海多盜，毓蘭按名捕置諸法。 修南運河、子牙河堤，及千里堤灣，靜海、軍糧城河道，興水利。 八年，卒，優恤，附祀曾國籓天津專祠，揚州建專祠。
In the sixth year the Nian bandit Lai Wenguang, defeated and on the run, reached Yangzhou and was captured by Yulan, who was registered by name for appointment as circuit intendant. In the seventh year he was soon granted the nominal rank of provincial administration commissioner. In the tenth year Li Hongzhang transferred him to the Coastal Defense Camp Affairs Office and put him in charge of the Tianjin Machine Works. In the sixth year of the Guangxu reign he was appointed military intendant of Tianjin and Hejian. Bandits were numerous along the coast, and Yulan tracked them down by name and punished them according to law. He repaired the dikes of the Southern Grand Canal and the Ziya River, as well as Qianli Dibay and the waterways of Jinghai and Junliangcheng, promoting water conservancy projects. In the eighth year he died. The court granted superior mourning honors, accorded him attendant worship at Zeng Guofan's dedicated shrine in Tianjin, and built a dedicated shrine for him in Yangzhou.
22
論曰：金國琛為羅、李舊部。 黃淳熙后起，獨立一幟，雖非楚籍，并為湘軍名將。 淳熙戰勝殞身，國琛遭忌鎩羽，皆未盡其才。 吳坤修、康國器起於令尉，功施爛然。 李鶴章才績出眾，堪膺大用，后竟不出。 吳毓蘭以擒獲巨憝顯名。 功名之際，遭際固難測哉！
The commentator writes: Jin Guochen was an old follower of Luo Zinan and Li Xubin. Huang Chunxi rose later and commanded a force of his own. Though not a native of Hunan, both men were acclaimed as famous generals of the Hunan Army. Chunxi won battles and died in the field; Guochen fell victim to jealousy and was cut short in his career. Neither man fully realized his talent. Wu Kunxiu and Kang Guoqi rose from minor local offices, and their achievements shone brilliantly. Li Hezhang's talent and achievements were outstanding and marked him for high office, yet in the end he never returned to public service. Wu Yulan won fame for capturing a major rebel leader. At the turning point of merit and fame, how unpredictable are the turns of fortune!
