1
雲南古滇國。 自越巂蠻夷任貴自領太守，漢光武即授以印綬，不以內地官守例之。 若爨、若蒙，皆以本土大姓，就官累世，為一方長。 元封梁王於滇，與大理之段分治。 明破梁王，滅大理，就土官而統馭之，分宣慰使、宣撫使、安撫使、正副長官司、土府、土州以治之。
In antiquity Yunnan was the realm of Dian. Once Ren Gui, a tribal leader of Yuexi, had seized the prefecture for himself, Emperor Guangwu of the Han immediately invested him with seal and ribbon, treating him outside the usual rules for officials of the heartland. Families like the Cuan and the Meng were powerful local lineages that held office for generations and ruled their districts as chieftains. The Yuan enfeoffed a Liang prince in Dian while the Duan of Dali held the other half of the country. The Ming overthrew the Liang house, destroyed Dali, and ruled through native chiefs, parceling out Pacification Commissioners, Pacification Superintendents, Pacification Envoys, chief and deputy native prefects, native prefectures, and native departments.
2
清順治十七年，平西王吳三桂定雲南，明永明王走緬甸，以沐府舊地封三桂，永鎮雲南。 康熙十四年，撤籓，三桂遂叛。 三桂死，其孫世璠襲。 二十一年，克之，世璠自殺，雲南大定。
In Shunzhi 17 (1660) Wu Sangui, Prince of Pingxi, took Yunnan; the Ming Yongli Emperor fled into Burma; and the old Mu domains were granted to Sangui to guard Yunnan forever. In Kangxi 14 (1675) the fief was revoked and Sangui rose in rebellion. After Sangui died his grandson Shifan inherited the cause. In Kangxi 21 (1682) the rebels were crushed; Shifan took his own life and Yunnan was brought to order.
3
雍正初，改土歸流之議起。 四年夏，先革東川土目，即進圖烏蒙。 時烏蒙土府祿萬鍾、鎮雄土府隴慶侯皆年少，兵權皆握于其叔祿鼎坤、隴聯星。 鄂爾泰令總兵劉起元屯東川，招降祿鼎坤。 惟祿萬鍾制于漢奸，約鎮雄兵三千攻鼎坤于魯甸，鄂爾泰遣遊擊哈元生敗之； 又檄其相仇之阿底土兵共搗烏蒙，連破關隘，賊遂敗走鎮雄。 鄂爾泰復招降隴聯星，而鼎坤亦以兵三千攻鎮雄之脅，兩酋皆遁四川，於是兩土府旬日平。 以烏蒙設府，鎮雄設州，又設鎮于烏蒙，控制三屬，由四川改隸雲南，以一事權。 其東川法戛土目祿天祐、烏蒙米貼土目祿永孝，尚各據巢患邊。 六年春，遣兵破擒法戛，又遣副將郭壽域以兵三百捕米貼賊，逃渡小金沙江，糾四川沙馬司及建昌、涼山各夷倮數千潛回，襲陷官兵。 鄂爾泰遣總兵張耀祖、參將哈元生三路搜討。 詔四川建昌、永寧官兵聽鄂爾泰節制。 於是自小金沙江外，沙馬、雷波、吞都、黃螂諸土司地，直抵建昌，袤千餘里，皆置營汛，形聯勢控，並擒雷波土司楊明義； 而哈元生回軍復敗阿盧土司之眾數千，屯田東川，歲收二萬餘石，課礦歲萬金，資兵餉。 軍甫定，祿鼎坤以功擢河南參將，怏怏失望。 其子祿萬福乞回魯甸治產，見總兵劉起元軍律不肅，陰會其舊部謀變。 時烏蒙商民萬計，有險可扼，且賊止標弩，無大砲，而劉起元惟媮餒賄和，賊遂陷鎮城，盡戕兵民，遍煽東川、鎮雄及四川涼山蠻數萬叛。 鄂爾泰奏言：「臣用人僨事，請別簡大臣總督兩省，暫假臣提督，將兵討賊雪憤。 」世宗慰留之。 鄂爾泰調官兵萬餘，土兵半之，三路進攻。 先令總兵魏翥國率兵二千，七日馳抵東川，得不陷； 而魏翥國旋為祿鼎明刺傷，乃以官祿代翥國。 烏蒙委總兵哈元生、副將徐成貴，鎮雄委參將韓勳。 勳以兵四百扼奎鄉，敗賊四千，連破四寨。 哈元生以千餘兵討烏蒙，先至得勝坡，遇賊二萬。 其黑寡、暮末二渠皆萬人敵。 黑寡持長槍，直犯元生，元生左格槍，右拔矢，應手殪之； 又射殪暮末，即竿揭二首以進，賊奪氣。 再戰再捷，進至倚那岡。 賊數萬，連營十餘里。 我兵三千、土兵千，夜設伏賊營左右，而嚴陣以待。 黎明，賊數路來犯，不動。 將偪陣，砲起，大呼奮擊，山后伏兵左右夾攻，賊大潰，盡破其八十餘營，獲甲械輜重山積。 即日抵烏蒙，賊見元生旗，即反走，克三關，祿萬福兄弟、祿鼎坤均伏誅。
Early in the Yongzheng reign the policy of "replacing chiefs with magistrates" came to the fore. In the summer of Yongzheng 4 (1726) the Dongchuan native headmen were abolished first, and operations against Wumeng followed immediately. Lu Wanzhong of Wumeng and Long Qinghou of Zhenxiong were both minors; real authority rested with their uncles Lu Dingkun and Long Lianxing. Ortai stationed Regional Commander Liu Qiyuan at Dongchuan and called on Lu Dingkun to submit. Wanzhong, however, was dominated by a Han collaborator who persuaded Zhenxiong to send three thousand men against Dingkun at Ludian; Ortai dispatched Guerrilla Commander Ha Yuansheng and broke them. He then directed the feuding Adi native levies to join in an assault on Wumeng, storming one barrier after another until the rebels broke and ran toward Zhenxiong. Ortai won over Long Lianxing as well, while Dingkun marched three thousand men against Zhenxiong's rear; both chiefs escaped into Sichuan, and the two prefectures were settled within ten days. Wumeng became a prefecture and Zhenxiong a department; a garrison was planted at Wumeng to oversee three dependencies; and the region was shifted from Sichuan to Yunnan so that authority would rest in one hand. Lu Tianyou, Faga headman of Dongchuan, and Lu Yongxiao, Mitie headman of Wumeng, still clung to their strongholds and harassed the frontier. In the spring of Yongzheng 6 Faga was stormed and its chief taken; Deputy General Guo Shouyu led three hundred men against the Mitie rebels, who crossed the Lesser Jinsha, mustered thousands of tribal Yi and Nuosu from Shama in Sichuan and from Jianchang and Liangshan, crept back, and overwhelmed the detachment. Ortai sent Regional Commander Zhang Yaozu and Colonel Ha Yuansheng on three columns to hunt them down. The throne ordered the Jianchang and Yongning forces of Sichuan to obey Ortai. From beyond the Lesser Jinsha, across the domains of Shama, Leibo, Tundu, Huanglang, and other chiefs, all the way to Jianchang—over a thousand li—camps and posts were strung together in a chain of control, and Leibo chief Yang Mingyi was taken as well. On his return Ha Yuansheng routed several thousand men of the Alu chief, opened military farms in Dongchuan yielding more than twenty thousand piculs a year, and drew ten thousand taels annually from mines to pay the troops. Hardly had the army stood down when Lu Dingkun, promoted to Henan colonel for his service, grew resentful and disaffected. His son Lu Wanfu asked leave to return to Ludian to tend his property; finding Regional Commander Liu Qiyuan's troops slack, he secretly rallied former adherents to revolt. Wumeng held tens of thousands of traders and townspeople and terrain that could be bottled up; the rebels carried only poisoned darts, no siege guns—yet Liu Qiyuan neglected duty, grew slack, took bribes, and bought peace. The rebels then took the prefectural seat, massacred troops and civilians, and stirred tens of thousands of tribesmen in Dongchuan, Zhenxiong, and Liangshan in Sichuan to rise. Ortai reported: "I have misused men and brought disaster; please appoint another minister to govern both provinces and lend me the post of provincial military commissioner so I may lead troops to crush the rebels and redeem this failure." The Yongzheng Emperor comforted him and kept him at his post. Ortai mustered more than ten thousand regulars, half as many native auxiliaries, and advanced on three fronts. He sent Regional Commander Wei Zhuguo with two thousand men; they reached Dongchuan in seven days and saved the city. Wei Zhuguo was soon stabbed and wounded by Lu Dingming, and Guan Lu replaced him in command. Ha Yuansheng and Deputy General Xu Chenggui took Wumeng; Colonel Han Xun took Zhenxiong. Han Xun held Kuixiang with four hundred men, routed four thousand rebels, and stormed four stockades in a row. Ha Yuansheng marched on Wumeng with a little over a thousand men, reached Desheng Slope first, and met twenty thousand rebels. Two of their leaders, Heiguo and Mumo, were each said to be worth ten thousand fighters. Heiguo charged with a long spear; Yuansheng parried left-handed, snatched an arrow right-handed, and dropped him where he stood. He shot Mumo as well, hoisted both heads on spears, and pressed forward; the enemy's spirit broke. After another victory they pushed on to Yina Ridge. Tens of thousands of rebels lay encamped along more than ten li. Three thousand regulars and a thousand native auxiliaries laid night ambushes on both flanks while forming a firm line. At dawn the rebels came on several roads; our line held firm. When they neared our line the guns opened, our men shouted and charged, and the hill ambushers struck both flanks; the rebels collapsed, more than eighty camps were overrun, and arms and baggage heaped up like hills. That day they entered Wumeng; at Ha Yuansheng's standard the rebels fled; three passes fell; Lu Wanfu, his brothers, and Lu Dingkun were all put to death.
4
六年，鄂爾泰總督三省，其土州安於蕃、鎮沅土府刁澣，及赭樂長官土司、威遠州、廣南府各土目，先後劾黜。 惟刁氏之族舍土目煽糾威遠黑倮復反，戕知府劉洪度。 於是盡徙已革土司土目他省安置，並搜剿黨逆之威遠、新平諸倮，冒瘴突入，擒斬千計，而我將士亦患瘴死二百餘。 又進剿瀾滄江內孟養、茶山土夷，即明王驥兵十二萬，大舉再征，諸蠻驚謂「自古漢兵所未至者」也。 鄂爾泰先檄車里土兵截諸江外，官兵各持斧鍬開路，焚柵湮溝，連破險隘，直抵孟養，據蠻坡通餉道； 其六茶山巢穴四十餘寨，乃用降夷鄉導，以賊攻賊，於是深入數千里，無險不搜。 惟江外歸車里土司，江內地全改流。 升普洱為府； 移沅江協副將駐之。 于思茅、橄欖壩各設官戍兵，以扼蒙緬、老撾門戶。 於是廣南府土同知、富州土知州，各原增歲糧二三千石，並捐建府、州城垣。 孟連土司獻銀廠，怒江野夷輸皮幣，而老撾、景邁二國皆來貢象，緬甸震焉。 乾隆三十四年，遷孟拱土司於關外。 緬甸事詳見緬甸傳。
In Yongzheng 6 Ortai governed three provinces; the native departments of Anyufan and Zhenyuan under Diao Huan, the Zhele chief prefecture, and the native headmen of Weiyuan and Guangnan were dismissed in turn. Only Diao clansmen who had lost their posts stirred the Black Nuosu of Weiyuan to rise again and murdered Prefect Liu Hongdu. All deposed chiefs and headmen were then resettled in other provinces while troops hunted Nuosu allies in Weiyuan and Xinping, pushing through malarial country, killing more than a thousand—and losing more than two hundred of their own to fever. Columns pushed up the Lancang against the Mengyang and Chashan tribes—the ground Wang Ji had invaded in the Ming with one hundred twenty thousand men—where the locals marveled that "Han armies have never come here since ancient times." Ortai first blocked the far bank with Cheli native levies; regulars cut roads with axes and spades, burned palisades and filled trenches, stormed pass after pass to Mengyang, and held Mangpo to secure supplies. Six Chashan strongholds with more than forty camps were taken with surrendered tribes as guides, tribe turned against tribe, until the army had swept several thousand li and left no refuge untouched. Beyond the river lands went back to the Cheli chief; everything within the river was placed under direct rule. Pu'er was raised to prefectural rank; and the Yuanjiang Command deputy regional commander was posted there. Magistrates and garrisons were planted at Simao and Ganlanba to guard the approaches to Burma and Laos. The native associate prefect of Guangnan and the native prefect of Fuzhou each added two or three thousand piculs to their annual grain payments and contributed to city walls. Menglian presented a silver mine; Nu River tribes brought furs and cloth; Laos and Jingmai sent tribute elephants—and Burma trembled. In Qianlong 34 (1769) the Menggong native chief was relocated beyond the frontier pass. Burma affairs are treated fully in the Burma biography.

18
老窩土千總，居老窩。 清順治十六年，土知州段德壽歸附，後裁。 乾隆十二年，德壽孫維精剿秤戛夷賊有功，十七年，授土千總世職。 道光元年，永北軍務，段克勳帶練擒賊，給五品頂戴。
The Laowo native battalion commander resided at Laowo. In Shunzhi 16 native prefect Duan Deshou submitted; the post was later abolished. In Qianlong 12 (1747) Deshou's grandson Weijing helped crush the Chengga rebels; in Qianlong 17 he received the hereditary battalion command. In Daoguang 1 (1821), in the Yongbei campaign, Duan Kexun led militia, captured rebels, and received a fifth-rank cap button.

The Liuku native battalion commander was at Liuku. His forebear Duan Fujian was the seventeenth-generation descendant of the Ming native prefect Duan Bao. In Qianlong 12, for service against the Chengga rebels, he received the hereditary battalion command in Qianlong 17. In Daoguang 1 Duan Lüren led militia, took rebels, and earned a fifth-rank cap button.

In Qianlong 12 Wencan's great-grandson Zuo Shiying served against the Chengga rebels and received the hereditary platoon command.

In Kangxi 20 Li Shiyuan was permitted to inherit.
26
溪處長官司副長官，在府西南三百一十五里。 清順治十六年，長官司恩忠歸附，仍授副長官世職。 康熙四年，附祿昌賢叛，伏誅，改土舍。
The Xichu deputy native prefecture lay three hundred fifteen li southwest of the prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 native prefect En Zhong submitted and kept the hereditary deputy prefecture. In Kangxi 4 he followed Lu Changxian in revolt, was put to death, and the office became a headmanship.
27
瓦渣鄉長官司，在府西南二百四十里。 清順治十六年，錢覺耀歸附，仍授副長官世職。 康熙四年，通王祿叛，官兵擒斬之，職除，改土舍。
The Wazha Township native prefecture was two hundred forty li southwest of the prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 Qian Jueyao submitted and kept the hereditary deputy prefecture. In Kangxi 4 he colluded with Wang Lu; troops captured and executed him; the post was abolished and made a headmanship.
28
左能寨長官司，在府西南二百三十里。 清順治十六年，吳應科歸附，以非滇志所載，下臨安府查核，稽其譜系，蓋應科為明蚌頗十一世孫，因改土舍，准襲。
The Zuoneng Stockade native prefecture lay two hundred thirty li southwest of the prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 Wu Yingke submitted; absent from the Yunnan gazetteer, his claim went to Lin'an for review; genealogy showed him an eleventh-generation descendant of the Ming chief Bangpo, so he received a permitted hereditary headmanship.
29
落恐甸長官司，在府西南二百里。 清順治十六年，明授副長官司陳玉歸附，因號紙無存，給便委土舍，仍准世襲。
The Luokong Dian native prefecture was two hundred li southwest of the prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 Chen Yu, Ming-appointed deputy prefect, submitted; his patent was missing, so he received a provisional headmanship with hereditary right preserved.
30
阿邦鄉土舍，在府東南二百一十里。 明授土守備。 清順治十六年，土守備陶順祖歸附，守職如故。 旋議土司不宜加武職，改土舍。
The Abang Township native headman resided two hundred ten li southeast of the prefecture. Under the Ming he held the rank of native garrison commander. In Shunzhi 16 native garrison commander Tao Shunzu submitted and retained his post. Officials soon ruled that chiefs should not hold military rank, and the office became a headmanship.
31
慢車鄉土舍，在府西南一百四十里。 清順治間，元江土夷亂，漫車土目刀岡隨官軍協剿，授土舍世職。
The Manche Township native headman lay one hundred forty li southwest of the prefecture. During Shunzhi, amid Yuanjiang tribal unrest, headman Dao Gang aided government troops and received a hereditary headmanship.
32
稿吾卡土把總，在府東南二百八十里。 清雍正間，納更土目龍在渭隨征元普逆夷有功，給土把總職銜。 嘉慶二十二年，江外夷匪滋事，龍定國父子陣亡，奏准世襲土把總。
The Gaowuka native platoon commander was two hundred eighty li southeast of the prefecture. In the Yongzheng era Nageng headman Long Zaiwei earned merit against Yuan-Pu rebels and received the platoon command. In Jiaqing 22, when tribes beyond the river rose, Long Dingguo and his son fell in battle; the throne approved a hereditary platoon command.
33
十五猛，縱橫四百餘里。 明初為沐氏勳莊。 清順治十七年，吳三桂請並雲南荒田給與籓下壯丁耕種。 康熙七年，奏旨圈撥。 叛後，變價歸建水徵收。 猛各設一掌寨，督辦錢糧。 管有猛喇、猛丁、猛梭、猛賴、猛蚌、茨桶壩、五畝、五邦、者米、猛弄、馬龍、瓦遮、鬥岩、阿土、水塘十五寨。
The Fifteen Meng covered more than four hundred li across. Early in the Ming they formed part of the Mu family's reward lands. In Shunzhi 17 Wu Sangui asked that Yunnan's abandoned fields be granted his fiefsmen to farm. In Kangxi 7 the throne ordered them surveyed and allotted. After the revolt they were sold and the revenue routed through Jianshui. Each district received a headman to oversee tax grain. They comprised fifteen stockades: Mengla, Mengding, Mengsuo, Menglai, Mengbang, Citongba, Wumu, Wubang, Zhemi, Mengnong, Malong, Wazhe, Douyan, Atu, and Shuitang.
34
教化三部長官副長官。 清順治十六年，副長官龍升歸附，仍以張長壽為名，許之，授世職。 康熙四年，附王祿叛，誅之，以其地為開化府，設流官。
The Jiaohua Three Departments deputy native prefects. In Shunzhi 16 deputy prefect Long Sheng submitted under the name Zhang Changshou, which was accepted, and he kept the hereditary post. In Kangxi 4 he followed Wang Lu, was executed, and his lands became Kaihua Prefecture under regular magistrates.
35
王弄山長官司副長官。 清順治十六年，副長官王朔歸附，授世職。 康熙四年，朔與祿昌賢叛，官兵討之，朔自焚死，以其地屬開化府。
The Wangnong Mountain deputy native prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 deputy prefect Wang Shuo submitted and received the hereditary post. In Kangxi 4 Shuo rebelled with Lu Changxian; when troops closed in he burned himself, and his lands went to Kaihua Prefecture.
36
阿迷州土知州，舊有土目李阿側。 清康熙四年，從討王朔有功，授土知州世職。 傳至李純，濫派橫征，為倮夷所控。 雍正四年，籍其產，安置江西，改流。
Ami Prefecture's native prefecture had once included headman Li Ace. In Kangxi 4, for service against Wang Shuo, he received the hereditary native prefecture. The line reached Li Chun, who taxed arbitrarily until the tribal Yi held him in check. In Yongzheng 4 his estates were seized, he was sent to Jiangxi, and the district was placed under regular rule.
37
寧州土知州，清順治十六年，祿昌賢歸附，仍授世職。 十七年，降州同。 明年，以舉首梅道人等謀逆，復原職。 康熙四年，以叛伏誅。
Ningzhou native prefect: in Shunzhi 16 Lu Changxian submitted and kept the hereditary post. In Shunzhi 17 he was demoted to prefectural sub-prefect. The following year, for revealing Mei Daoren's conspiracy, his old rank was restored. In Kangxi 4 he was executed for rebellion.
38
寧州土州判。 清康熙十九年，滇有李者祿歸附，准世襲州判。 後絕，停襲。
The Ningzhou native sub-prefecture. In Kangxi 19, after Yunnan was recovered, Li Zhelu submitted and received a hereditary sub-prefecture. The line later ended and inheritance lapsed.
39
嶍峨縣土知縣。 清順治十六年，祿益歸附，仍授世職。 康熙四年，與祿昌賢等叛改流。
The Xi'e native magistracy. In Shunzhi 16 Lu Yi submitted and kept the hereditary post. In Kangxi 4 he rebelled with Lu Changxian and the office was abolished for regular rule.
40
嶍峨縣土主簿。 清順治十六年，王揚祖歸附，仍授世職。 康熙四年，與祿昌賢等叛，伏誅，職除。
The Xi'e native registrarship. In Shunzhi 16 Wang Yangzu submitted and kept the hereditary post. In Kangxi 4 he followed Lu Changxian, was executed, and the post was abolished.
41
蒙自縣土縣丞。 土知縣陸氏被黜，其土舍寧州祿重據土官村，溺於酒色，不能馭下。 其目把李輔舜等叛歸沙源，源以兵乘之，遂破有土官村。 沙定洲踞會城，令李輔舜子日芳竊據蒙自。 定洲敗，日芳遂家於蒙。 清康熙四年，日芳弟日森子世籓、世屏附寧州祿昌賢叛，總兵閻鎮破之。 世籓遁，追斬之； 世屏出降，免死，充大理軍。 後吳三桂反，給世屏偽總兵劄。 大師復滇，世屏持劄歸附，授蒙自縣土縣丞職，不准世襲。
The Mengzi County native assistant prefecture. The Lu native magistrate was removed; headman Lu Chong of Ningzhou held Tuguan Village, drowned in drink and women, and lost control of his men. Subordinate headmen Li Fushun and others defected to Sha Yuan, who stormed in and took Tuguan Village. Sha Dingzhou held the provincial capital and set Li Fushun's son Rifang over Mengzi. After Dingzhou's fall, Rifang settled in Mengzi. In Kangxi 4 Risun's sons Shifan and Shiping joined Lu Changxian of Ningzhou; Regional Commander Yan Zhen crushed them. Shifan escaped but was hunted down and killed; Shiping surrendered, was spared, and was enrolled in the Dali garrison. When Wu Sangui rebelled he gave Shiping a forged regional commander's patent. When imperial forces retook Yunnan, Shiping submitted his patent and received the Mengzi assistant prefecture without inheritance.
42
楚雄縣土縣丞。 清順治十六年，土縣丞楊春盛歸附，仍授舊職。 乾隆五十年，改為正八品土官，世襲。
The Chuxiong County native assistant prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 assistant prefect Yang Chunsheng submitted and kept his post. In Qianlong 50 it became a hereditary eighth-rank native official.
43
鎮南州土州同，居本城。 清順治十六年，土州同段光贊歸附，仍授世職。
The Zhennan native sub-prefecture was in the prefectural seat. In Shunzhi 16 sub-prefect Duan Guangzan submitted and kept the hereditary post.
44
鎮南州土州判，居鎮南州城東北。 清順治十六年，土州判陳昌虞歸附，仍授世職。
The second Zhennan native sub-prefecture lay northeast of the city. In Shunzhi 16 sub-prefect Chen Changyu submitted and kept the hereditary post.
45
阿雄關土巡檢，居鎮南州屬。 清順治十六年，土巡檢者光祖歸附，仍授世職。
The Axiong Pass native inspection post belonged to Zhennan Prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 native inspector Zhe Guangzu submitted and kept the hereditary post.
46
鎮南關土巡檢。 清順治十六年，土巡檢楊繼祖歸附，仍授舊職，傳至楊文輝，乾隆五十年，改為從九品土官，世襲。
The Zhennan Pass native inspection post. In Shunzhi 16 inspector Yang Jizu submitted and kept his post; the line reached Yang Wenhui; in Qianlong 50 it became a hereditary ninth-rank native official.
47
姚州土州同，居姚州西界彌興官莊。 清順治十六年，土州同高顯錫歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至高配忝，乾隆五十年，改為從六品土官，世襲。
The Yao Prefecture native sub-prefecture was at the Mixing official estate on the western border. In Shunzhi 16 sub-prefect Gao Xianxi submitted and kept his post. The line reached Gao Peitian; in Qianlong 50 it became a hereditary sixth-rank native official.
48
回磴關土巡檢，居回磴關。 清順治十六年，土巡檢楊忠藎歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至楊怡，乾隆五十年，改為從九品土官，世襲。
The Huideng Pass native inspection post stood at Huideng Pass. In Shunzhi 16 inspector Yang Zhongyi submitted and kept his post. The line reached Yang Yi; in Qianlong 50 it became a hereditary ninth-rank native official.
49
沙矣舊土巡檢。 清順治十六年，土巡檢蘇鑒歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至蘇敬，乾隆五十年，改為從九品土官，世襲。
The Shayi Old Station native inspection post. In Shunzhi 16 inspector Su Jian submitted and kept his post. The line reached Su Jing; in Qianlong 50 it became a hereditary ninth-rank native official.
50
定遠縣土主簿，居本城。 清順治十六年，土主簿李世卿歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至李毓英，乾隆五十年，改為正九品土官，世襲。
The Dingyuan County native registrar lived in the county seat. In Shunzhi 16 registrar Li Shiqing submitted and kept his post. The line reached Li Yuying; in Qianlong 50 it became a hereditary ninth-rank native official.
51
姚安府土同知。 清順治十六年，土同知高奣映歸附，仍授世職。 傳至李厚德，雍正三年，以不法革職，安置江南。
The Yao'an Prefecture native associate prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 associate prefect Gao Angying submitted and kept the hereditary post. The line reached Gao Houde; in Yongzheng 3 he was removed for misconduct and sent to Jiangnan.
52
新興州土州判，居州南研和邑。 清康熙十九年，復滇，土人王鳳授偽遊擊，迎至廣西路投誠； 隨征石門檻、馬別河、黃草壩皆有功，授土州判世職。
The Xinxing native sub-prefecture was at Yanhe, south of the prefecture. In Kangxi 19, when Yunnan was recovered, local leader Wang Fengshou bore a rebel guerrilla patent and was brought to Guangxi to surrender; He served at Shimenjian, the Mabie River, and Huangcaoba and received the hereditary native sub-prefecture.
53
河陽縣安插土官。 清順治初，土官刀韜歸附，止給劄，仍准世襲。 沿至刀廷俊，裁革。
Native officials resettled in Heyang County. Early in Shunzhi native official Dao Tao submitted and received a commission with hereditary right. The line continued to Dao Tingjun, when the post was abolished.
54
鐵爐關土巡檢。 清順治十六年，土巡檢王先榮歸附，授世職。 康熙四年，同王耀祖叛，削除。
The Tielu Pass native inspection post. In Shunzhi 16 native inspector Wang Xianrong submitted and received the hereditary post. In Kangxi 4 he rebelled with Wang Yaozu and the office was abolished.
55
廣南府土同知。 清順治十六年，儂鵬歸附，授同知世職。 傳至儂毓榮，乾隆三十一年，從征普洱、緬甸。 三十七年，頒給土同知關防。 子世昌，嘉慶二年從征貴州仲苗，加銜一等，賞戴花翎，世襲。
The Guangnan Prefecture native associate prefecture. In Shunzhi 16 Nong Peng submitted and received the hereditary associate prefecture. The line reached Nong Yurong, who campaigned in Pu'er and Burma in Qianlong 31. In Qianlong 37 he received the associate prefect's official seal. His son Shichang served against the Guizhou Zhong Miao in Jiaqing 2, gained a first-rank title and peacock feathers, and inherited the post.
56
富州土知州，在府東二百六十五里。 清順治十六年，土知州沈昆巘歸附，仍授世職。 康熙九年，頒給州印。 後以罪黜，傳至沈肇乾。 雍正八年，肇乾復以罪黜。
The Fu Prefecture native prefecture lay two hundred sixty-five li east of the prefectural seat. In Shunzhi 16 native prefect Shen Kunxi submitted and kept the hereditary post. In Kangxi 9 the prefectural seal was granted. He was later dismissed for misconduct; the line reached Shen Zhaoqian. In Yongzheng 8 Zhaoqian was dismissed again for misconduct.
57
大猛麻土巡檢。 清順治十六年，土巡檢俸新命歸附，仍授世職。
The Damengma native inspection post. In Shunzhi 16 native inspector Feng Xinming submitted and kept the hereditary post.
58
猛猛土巡檢，明末奔竄，失其印信號紙，未能請襲。 傳子紫芝，清康熙五十四年歸附，貢象，仍授世職，頒給鈐記。 乾隆二十九年，改屬順寧府。
The Mengmeng inspection post: in the late Ming the holders fled and lost seal and patent, so succession lapsed. His son Zizhi submitted in Kangxi 54, sent tribute elephants, recovered the hereditary post, and received an official seal. In Qianlong 29 jurisdiction passed to Shunning Prefecture.
59
孟連宣撫司，在順甯府邊外南境，舊隸於永昌府。 清康熙四十八年，刁派鼎貢象，歸附，授宣撫司世職。 派鼎死，子刁派春年幼，叔祖刁派烈撫孤。 有刁派猷謀殺派烈，奪印爭職，安插省城，另給宣撫司鈐記便委。 傳至刁派新，因地處極邊，界連外域，定為經制宣撫司，頒給印信號紙。 乾隆二十九年，改隸順寧府。
The Menglian Pacification Superintendency lay on Shunning's southern frontier beyond the pass and had belonged to Yongchang. In Kangxi 48 Diao Paiding sent tribute elephants, submitted, and received the hereditary pacification superintendency. Paiding died leaving young Paichun; grand-uncle Pailie raised the boy. Paiyou plotted to murder Pailie and seize the seal; he was sent to the capital and given a provisional pacification seal. The line reached Paixin; as the domain bordered foreign territory it was made a regular pacification superintendency with full seal and patent. In Qianlong 29 it was placed under Shunning Prefecture.
60
猛緬長官司，清乾隆十一年，歸流，改其地為緬寧，設流官通判駐其地。
Mengmian native prefecture: in Qianlong 11 it was abolished for regular rule; the district became Mianning with a regular sub-prefect.
61
平彝縣土縣丞，居平彝縣竹園村。 清順治初，土縣丞龍闊歸附，仍舊世襲。
The Pingyi County native assistant prefect resided at Zhuyuan Village. Early in Shunzhi assistant prefect Long Kuo submitted and kept the hereditary post.
