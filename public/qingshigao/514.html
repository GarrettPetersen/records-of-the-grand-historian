1
雲南古滇國。 自越巂蠻夷任貴自領太守，漢光武即授以印綬，不以內地官守例之。 若爨、若蒙，皆以本土大姓，就官累世，為一方長。 元封梁王於滇，與大理之段分治。 明破梁王，滅大理，就土官而統馭之，分宣慰使、宣撫使、安撫使、正副長官司、土府、土州以治之。
Yunnan was in antiquity the kingdom of Dian. When the Yuexi tribesman Ren Gui took the grand administrator's post on his own authority, Emperor Guangwu of Han immediately invested him with official seals and cords, without applying the usual rules for administrators in the interior. Clans such as the Cuan and the Meng drew on powerful local lineages, held office for generations, and served as regional leaders. Under the Yuan, a Prince of Liang was enfeoffed in Dian and governed apart from the Duan clan of Dali. The Ming overthrew the Prince of Liang and extinguished Dali, then ruled through native chieftains, dividing authority among pacification commissioners, superintendents, consolation commissioners, chief offices of the first and second rank, native prefectures, and native departments.
2
清順治十七年，平西王吳三桂定雲南，明永明王走緬甸，以沐府舊地封三桂，永鎮雲南。 康熙十四年，撤籓，三桂遂叛。 三桂死，其孫世璠襲。 二十一年，克之，世璠自殺，雲南大定。
In the seventeenth year of the Shunzhi reign, the Prince of Pingxi, Wu Sangui, secured Yunnan; the Ming Yongming Prince fled to Burma; Sangui was granted the former Mu estate and charged to hold Yunnan in perpetuity. In the fourteenth year of the Kangxi reign the princely fief was withdrawn, and Sangui rose in rebellion. After Sangui's death his grandson Shihu took over. In the twenty-first year the province was taken; Shihu committed suicide, and Yunnan was fully pacified.
3
雍正初，改土歸流之議起。 四年夏，先革東川土目，即進圖烏蒙。 時烏蒙土府祿萬鍾、鎮雄土府隴慶侯皆年少，兵權皆握于其叔祿鼎坤、隴聯星。 鄂爾泰令總兵劉起元屯東川，招降祿鼎坤。 惟祿萬鍾制于漢奸，約鎮雄兵三千攻鼎坤于魯甸，鄂爾泰遣遊擊哈元生敗之； 又檄其相仇之阿底土兵共搗烏蒙，連破關隘，賊遂敗走鎮雄。 鄂爾泰復招降隴聯星，而鼎坤亦以兵三千攻鎮雄之脅，兩酋皆遁四川，於是兩土府旬日平。 以烏蒙設府，鎮雄設州，又設鎮于烏蒙，控制三屬，由四川改隸雲南，以一事權。 其東川法戛土目祿天祐、烏蒙米貼土目祿永孝，尚各據巢患邊。 六年春，遣兵破擒法戛，又遣副將郭壽域以兵三百捕米貼賊，逃渡小金沙江，糾四川沙馬司及建昌、涼山各夷倮數千潛回，襲陷官兵。 鄂爾泰遣總兵張耀祖、參將哈元生三路搜討。 詔四川建昌、永寧官兵聽鄂爾泰節制。 於是自小金沙江外，沙馬、雷波、吞都、黃螂諸土司地，直抵建昌，袤千餘里，皆置營汛，形聯勢控，並擒雷波土司楊明義； 而哈元生回軍復敗阿盧土司之眾數千，屯田東川，歲收二萬餘石，課礦歲萬金，資兵餉。 軍甫定，祿鼎坤以功擢河南參將，怏怏失望。 其子祿萬福乞回魯甸治產，見總兵劉起元軍律不肅，陰會其舊部謀變。 時烏蒙商民萬計，有險可扼，且賊止標弩，無大砲，而劉起元惟媮餒賄和，賊遂陷鎮城，盡戕兵民，遍煽東川、鎮雄及四川涼山蠻數萬叛。 鄂爾泰奏言：「臣用人僨事，請別簡大臣總督兩省，暫假臣提督，將兵討賊雪憤。 」世宗慰留之。 鄂爾泰調官兵萬餘，土兵半之，三路進攻。 先令總兵魏翥國率兵二千，七日馳抵東川，得不陷； 而魏翥國旋為祿鼎明刺傷，乃以官祿代翥國。 烏蒙委總兵哈元生、副將徐成貴，鎮雄委參將韓勳。 勳以兵四百扼奎鄉，敗賊四千，連破四寨。 哈元生以千餘兵討烏蒙，先至得勝坡，遇賊二萬。 其黑寡、暮末二渠皆萬人敵。 黑寡持長槍，直犯元生，元生左格槍，右拔矢，應手殪之； 又射殪暮末，即竿揭二首以進，賊奪氣。 再戰再捷，進至倚那岡。 賊數萬，連營十餘里。 我兵三千、土兵千，夜設伏賊營左右，而嚴陣以待。 黎明，賊數路來犯，不動。 將偪陣，砲起，大呼奮擊，山后伏兵左右夾攻，賊大潰，盡破其八十餘營，獲甲械輜重山積。 即日抵烏蒙，賊見元生旗，即反走，克三關，祿萬福兄弟、祿鼎坤均伏誅。
Early in the Yongzheng reign the policy of replacing native chieftain rule with regular administration came to the fore. That summer they first abolished the native headmen of Dongchuan and then turned to Wumeng. Lu Wanzhong of Wumeng and Long Qinghou of Zhenxiong were both young; their uncles Lu Dingkun and Long Lianxing held the real military power. Ortai stationed Regional Commander Liu Qiyuan at Dongchuan to win Lu Dingkun over. Lu Wanzhong, however, was manipulated by a Han collaborator and mustered three thousand Zhenxiong troops to strike Dingkun at Ludian; Ortai sent Guerrilla Commander Ha Yuansheng, who routed them; He also directed the mutually hostile Adi native forces to strike Wumeng together; they stormed pass after pass until the rebels fled toward Zhenxiong. Ortai won Long Lianxing over as well, while Dingkun led three thousand men against Zhenxiong's flank; both chiefs fled into Sichuan, and the two native prefectures were pacified within ten days. Wumeng became a prefecture and Zhenxiong a department; a garrison at Wumeng controlled three subordinate districts; the area was transferred from Sichuan to Yunnan to consolidate authority. Lu Tianyou, the Faga headman of Dongchuan, and Lu Yongxiao, the Mitie headman of Wumeng, still held strongholds and harassed the frontier. In the spring of the sixth year troops broke Faga and took its leader; Assistant Regional Commander Guo Shouyu led three hundred men after the Mitie rebels, who crossed the Lesser Jinsha, rallied several thousand Yi from Shama in Sichuan and from Jianchang and Liangshan, slipped back in secret, and overwhelmed the government force. Ortai sent Regional Commander Zhang Yaozu and Assistant Regional Commander Ha Yuansheng on three routes to hunt them down. An edict placed the Jianchang and Yongning forces of Sichuan under Ortai's command. From beyond the Lesser Jinsha through the domains of Shama, Leibo, Tundu, Huanglang, and on to Jianchang—more than a thousand li in extent—garrison posts were set up in a linked network, and the Leibo chieftain Yang Mingyi was captured; Ha Yuansheng, on his return, routed several thousand followers of the Alu chieftain, established military colonies in Dongchuan yielding more than twenty thousand piculs a year, and drew ten thousand taels annually from mines to fund the army. Hardly had order been restored when Lu Dingkun was promoted to assistant regional commander in Henan—a posting that left him resentful and disappointed. His son Lu Wanfu asked to return to Ludian to manage family property; seeing Liu Qiyuan's lax discipline, he secretly rallied former followers to plot a revolt. Wumeng then held tens of thousands of merchants and civilians and defensible passes; the rebels had only poisoned bolts and crossbows, no heavy cannon; yet Liu Qiyuan plundered rations and bought peace with bribes; the rebels seized the garrison town, massacred troops and civilians, and stirred tens of thousands in Dongchuan, Zhenxiong, and Liangshan in Sichuan to revolt. Ortai memorialized: "I have mishandled personnel; please appoint another senior minister to govern both provinces; grant me temporarily the post of provincial military commissioner so I may lead troops to crush the rebels and redeem this disgrace." " The Yongzheng Emperor comforted him and kept him in office. Ortai mustered more than ten thousand regular troops and half as many native auxiliaries, advancing on three fronts. He first sent Regional Commander Wei Zhuguo with two thousand men; in seven days they reached Dongchuan and kept it from falling; Wei Zhuguo was soon stabbed by Lu Dingming; an officer surnamed Lu then replaced him. Wumeng was assigned to Regional Commander Ha Yuansheng and Vice Commander Xu Chenggui; Zhenxiong to Assistant Regional Commander Han Xun. Han Xun held Kuixiang with four hundred men, routed four thousand rebels, and took four stockades in succession. Ha Yuansheng marched on Wumeng with a little over a thousand men and first reached Desheng Slope, where he faced twenty thousand rebels. Their two leaders, Heiguo and Mumu, were each said to be a match for ten thousand men. Heiguo charged with a long spear; Yuansheng parried with his left hand, drew an arrow with his right, and killed him on the spot; He shot Mumu dead as well, raised both heads on poles, and advanced; the rebels' morale collapsed. He fought again and won again, pushing forward to Yina Ridge. Tens of thousands of rebels had camps stretching more than ten li. With three thousand regulars and a thousand native auxiliaries, they laid night ambushes on both flanks of the rebel camps while holding a tight battle line. At dawn the rebels attacked on several routes; our line held firm. As the rebels closed in, cannon opened fire and our men charged with a great shout; ambushers behind the hill struck both flanks; the rebels broke completely; more than eighty camps were destroyed; armor, weapons, and baggage piled up like hills. That same day they reached Wumeng; at sight of Yuansheng's banner the rebels fled; three passes were taken; Lu Wanfu and his brothers and Lu Dingkun were all put to death.
4
六年，鄂爾泰總督三省，其土州安於蕃、鎮沅土府刁澣，及赭樂長官土司、威遠州、廣南府各土目，先後劾黜。 惟刁氏之族舍土目煽糾威遠黑倮復反，戕知府劉洪度。 於是盡徙已革土司土目他省安置，並搜剿黨逆之威遠、新平諸倮，冒瘴突入，擒斬千計，而我將士亦患瘴死二百餘。 又進剿瀾滄江內孟養、茶山土夷，即明王驥兵十二萬，大舉再征，諸蠻驚謂「自古漢兵所未至者」也。 鄂爾泰先檄車里土兵截諸江外，官兵各持斧鍬開路，焚柵湮溝，連破險隘，直抵孟養，據蠻坡通餉道； 其六茶山巢穴四十餘寨，乃用降夷鄉導，以賊攻賊，於是深入數千里，無險不搜。 惟江外歸車里土司，江內地全改流。 升普洱為府； 移沅江協副將駐之。 于思茅、橄欖壩各設官戍兵，以扼蒙緬、老撾門戶。 於是廣南府土同知、富州土知州，各原增歲糧二三千石，並捐建府、州城垣。 孟連土司獻銀廠，怒江野夷輸皮幣，而老撾、景邁二國皆來貢象，緬甸震焉。 乾隆三十四年，遷孟拱土司於關外。 緬甸事詳見緬甸傳。
In the sixth year Ortai became governor-general of three provinces; the Anyufan native subprefecture, the Zhenyuan native prefecture under Diao Huan, the Chashi chief office, and native headmen in Weiyuan and Guangnan were removed one after another. Former Diao clansmen stirred the black Yi of Weiyuan to rebel again and killed Prefect Liu Hongdu. All deposed chieftains and headmen were resettled in other provinces; Yi rebels in Weiyuan and Xinping were hunted down; troops braved miasma and killed thousands, while more than two hundred of our own men died of the same pestilence. They also pushed into the Lancang basin against the Mengyang and Chashan tribes—the region where the Ming general Wang Ji had once led one hundred twenty thousand men on a second great campaign—where the tribes exclaimed that Han armies had never reached them before. Ortai first sent Cheli native troops to seal the far side of the rivers; regulars cut roads with axes and spades, burned stockades and filled ditches, stormed pass after pass to Mengyang, and held Manpo to secure the supply line; Six Chashan strongholds held more than forty stockades; surrendered tribesmen served as guides and rebel bands were turned against one another; the army pushed thousands of li into country where no pass was left unsearched. Only territory beyond the river remained under the Cheli chieftain; everything on the near bank was placed under regular administration. Pu'er was elevated to prefectural status; and the vice commander of the Yuanjiang garrison was posted there. Officials and garrisons were placed at Simao and Ganlanba to guard the approaches to Burma and Laos. The native associate prefect of Guangnan and the native prefect of Fuzhou each pledged an extra two or three thousand piculs of annual grain and helped fund new city walls. The Menglian chieftain offered a silver mine; wild tribes of the Nujiang paid tribute in hides; Laos and Jingmai sent elephants; and Burma was shaken. In the thirty-fourth year of the Qianlong reign the Menggong chieftain was moved beyond the frontier pass. Burma is treated in full in the Burma biography.

煉象關土巡檢，居煉象關大街。 清順治十六年，土巡檢李文秀歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至李東祚，乾隆五十年，改為從九品土官，世襲。
The native inspector of Lianxiang Pass had his seat on the pass's main street. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native inspector Li Wenxiu submitted and retained his former office. The line reached Li Dongzuo; in the fiftieth year of Qianlong the post became a hereditary ninth-rank native official.

The native battalion commander of Laowo was seated at Laowo. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native prefect Duan Deshou submitted; the office was later abolished. In the first year of Daoguang, during operations at Yongbei, Duan Kexun led militia to capture rebels and was awarded a fifth-rank cap button.

In the first year of Daoguang, during operations at Yongbei, Duan Lvren led militia to capture rebels and was awarded a fifth-rank cap button.

The native prefect of Dengchuan: in the sixteenth year of Shunzhi A Shangkui submitted and kept the hereditary office. His great-grandson A Kouyuan indulged bandits and harmed the people; in the fourth year of Yongzheng the post was abolished and he was resettled in Jiangxi.

The chief office of Kuirongdian lay one hundred forty li southwest of the prefectural seat. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Chief Sun Dachang submitted and kept the hereditary associate chieftaincy.
25
思陀鄉土舍，在府西南二百五十里。 清順治十六年，長官司李秉忠歸附，仍授長官司、副長官世職。 後絕，改土舍。 康熙二十年，以李世元繼襲。
The native headman of Situo Township was seated two hundred fifty li southwest of the prefectural seat. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Chief Li Bingzhong submitted and kept the hereditary chief and associate chieftaincies. The line later died out and the post became a native headmanship. In the twentieth year of Kangxi Li Shiyuan inherited the post.
26
溪處長官司副長官，在府西南三百一十五里。 清順治十六年，長官司恩忠歸附，仍授副長官世職。 康熙四年，附祿昌賢叛，伏誅，改土舍。
The associate chief of the Xichu chief office was posted three hundred fifteen li southwest of the prefectural seat. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Chief Enzhong submitted and kept the hereditary associate chieftaincy. In the fourth year of Kangxi he joined Lu Changxian's rebellion, was executed, and the office became a native headmanship.
27
瓦渣鄉長官司，在府西南二百四十里。 清順治十六年，錢覺耀歸附，仍授副長官世職。 康熙四年，通王祿叛，官兵擒斬之，職除，改土舍。
The chief office of Wazha Township lay two hundred forty li southwest of the prefectural seat. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Qian Jueyao submitted and kept the hereditary associate chieftaincy. In the fourth year of Kangxi he colluded with Wang Lu; government troops captured and executed him; the office was abolished and replaced by a native headmanship.
28
左能寨長官司，在府西南二百三十里。 清順治十六年，吳應科歸附，以非滇志所載，下臨安府查核，稽其譜系，蓋應科為明蚌頗十一世孫，因改土舍，准襲。
The chief office of Zuoneng Stockade lay two hundred thirty li southwest of the prefectural seat. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Wu Yingke submitted; as he was not listed in the Yunnan gazetteer, Lin'an Prefecture verified his genealogy and found him to be the eleventh-generation descendant of the Ming chieftain Bangpo; the post became a native headmanship with permission to inherit.
29
落恐甸長官司，在府西南二百里。 清順治十六年，明授副長官司陳玉歸附，因號紙無存，給便委土舍，仍准世襲。
The chief office of Luokongdian lay two hundred li southwest of the prefectural seat. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Chen Yu, Ming-appointed associate chief, submitted; as his patent was lost, he received a provisional native headmanship while hereditary succession was still allowed.
30
阿邦鄉土舍，在府東南二百一十里。 明授土守備。 清順治十六年，土守備陶順祖歸附，守職如故。 旋議土司不宜加武職，改土舍。
The native headman of Abang Township was seated two hundred ten li southeast of the prefectural seat. Under the Ming he held the post of native garrison commander. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native garrison commander Tao Shunzu submitted and retained his duties. It was soon decided that native chieftains should not hold military titles; the post became a native headmanship.
31
慢車鄉土舍，在府西南一百四十里。 清順治間，元江土夷亂，漫車土目刀岡隨官軍協剿，授土舍世職。
The native headman of Manche Township was seated one hundred forty li southwest of the prefectural seat. During the Shunzhi reign, when Yuanjiang tribes rebelled, the Manche headman Dao Gang aided government troops and received the hereditary headmanship.
32
稿吾卡土把總，在府東南二百八十里。 清雍正間，納更土目龍在渭隨征元普逆夷有功，給土把總職銜。 嘉慶二十二年，江外夷匪滋事，龍定國父子陣亡，奏准世襲土把總。
The native company commander of Gaowuka was posted two hundred eighty li southeast of the prefectural seat. During the Yongzheng reign the Nageng headman Long Zaiwei served against the Yuanpu rebels with distinction and received the rank of native company commander. In the twenty-second year of Jiaqing, when bandits beyond the river rose up, Long Dingguo and his son fell in battle; the court approved the hereditary company command.
33
十五猛，縱橫四百餘里。 明初為沐氏勳莊。 清順治十七年，吳三桂請並雲南荒田給與籓下壯丁耕種。 康熙七年，奏旨圈撥。 叛後，變價歸建水徵收。 猛各設一掌寨，督辦錢糧。 管有猛喇、猛丁、猛梭、猛賴、猛蚌、茨桶壩、五畝、五邦、者米、猛弄、馬龍、瓦遮、鬥岩、阿土、水塘十五寨。
The Fifteen Meng covered more than four hundred li in every direction. In early Ming times it was a merit estate of the Mu clan. In the seventeenth year of Shunzhi Wu Sangui asked that Yunnan's wasteland be granted to men of the princely household for cultivation. In the seventh year of Kangxi an imperial order reclaimed the land. After the rebellion it was sold off and its revenue assigned to Jianshui. Each meng had a stockade chief to oversee tax collection. It governed fifteen stockades: Mengla, Mengding, Mengsuo, Menglai, Mengbang, Citongba, Wumu, Wubang, Zhemi, Mengnong, Malong, Wazhe, Douyan, Atu, and Shuitang.
34
教化三部長官副長官。 清順治十六年，副長官龍升歸附，仍以張長壽為名，許之，授世職。 康熙四年，附王祿叛，誅之，以其地為開化府，設流官。
The associate chief of the three Jiaohua departments. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Associate Chief Long Sheng submitted under the name Zhang Changshou; the court accepted this and granted the hereditary office. In the fourth year of Kangxi he joined Wang Lu's rebellion and was executed; his territory became Kaifu Prefecture under regular administration.
35
王弄山長官司副長官。 清順治十六年，副長官王朔歸附，授世職。 康熙四年，朔與祿昌賢叛，官兵討之，朔自焚死，以其地屬開化府。
The associate chief of the Wangnongshan chief office. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Associate Chief Wang Shuo submitted and received the hereditary office. In the fourth year of Kangxi Shuo joined Lu Changxian in rebellion; government troops attacked; Shuo committed suicide by fire; his territory was annexed to Kaifu Prefecture.
36
阿迷州土知州，舊有土目李阿側。 清康熙四年，從討王朔有功，授土知州世職。 傳至李純，濫派橫征，為倮夷所控。 雍正四年，籍其產，安置江西，改流。
The native prefect of Ami Prefecture: formerly the native headman Li Ace held sway. In the fourth year of Kangxi he served against Wang Shuo with distinction and received the hereditary native prefecture. The line reached Li Chun, who imposed arbitrary levies until the Yi brought charges against him. In the fourth year of Yongzheng his estates were confiscated, he was resettled in Jiangxi, and regular administration was installed.
37
寧州土知州，清順治十六年，祿昌賢歸附，仍授世職。 十七年，降州同。 明年，以舉首梅道人等謀逆，復原職。 康熙四年，以叛伏誅。
The native prefect of Ningzhou: in the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Lu Changxian submitted and kept the hereditary office. In the seventeenth year he was demoted to department vice prefect. The following year he exposed a plot by the Daoist Mei Daoren and others and was restored to his former office. In the fourth year of Kangxi he was executed for rebellion.
38
寧州土州判。 清康熙十九年，滇有李者祿歸附，准世襲州判。 後絕，停襲。
The native department judge of Ningzhou. In the nineteenth year of Kangxi, when Yunnan was recovered, Li Zhelu submitted and was granted the hereditary department judgeship. The line later died out and succession ceased.
39
嶍峨縣土知縣。 清順治十六年，祿益歸附，仍授世職。 康熙四年，與祿昌賢等叛改流。
The native magistrate of Xie'e County. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Lu Yi submitted and kept the hereditary office. In the fourth year of Kangxi he joined Lu Changxian's rebellion and the post was abolished in favor of regular administration.
40
嶍峨縣土主簿。 清順治十六年，王揚祖歸附，仍授世職。 康熙四年，與祿昌賢等叛，伏誅，職除。
The native registrar of Xie'e County. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Wang Yangzu submitted and kept the hereditary office. In the fourth year of Kangxi he joined Lu Changxian's rebellion, was executed, and the office was abolished.
41
蒙自縣土縣丞。 土知縣陸氏被黜，其土舍寧州祿重據土官村，溺於酒色，不能馭下。 其目把李輔舜等叛歸沙源，源以兵乘之，遂破有土官村。 沙定洲踞會城，令李輔舜子日芳竊據蒙自。 定洲敗，日芳遂家於蒙。 清康熙四年，日芳弟日森子世籓、世屏附寧州祿昌賢叛，總兵閻鎮破之。 世籓遁，追斬之； 世屏出降，免死，充大理軍。 後吳三桂反，給世屏偽總兵劄。 大師復滇，世屏持劄歸附，授蒙自縣土縣丞職，不准世襲。
The native assistant magistrate of Mengzi County. The Lu native magistrate was removed; the Ningzhou headman Lu Chong seized Tuguan Village, indulged in wine and women, and lost control of his followers. His subordinates Li Fushun and others defected to Sha Yuan, who attacked and seized Tuguan Village. Sha Dingzhou held the provincial capital and sent Li Fushun's son Rifang to seize Mengzi. When Dingzhou fell, Rifang settled his family in Mengzi. In the fourth year of Kangxi Risun's sons Shifan and Shiping joined Lu Changxian of Ningzhou in rebellion; Regional Commander Yan Zhen defeated them. Shifan fled and was hunted down and killed; Shiping surrendered, was spared, and enrolled in the Dali garrison. When Wu Sangui later rebelled, Shiping received a forged commission as regional commander. When the imperial army recovered Yunnan, Shiping submitted with his commission and was made native assistant magistrate of Mengzi, without hereditary right.
42
楚雄縣土縣丞。 清順治十六年，土縣丞楊春盛歸附，仍授舊職。 乾隆五十年，改為正八品土官，世襲。
The native assistant magistrate of Chuxiong County. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native assistant magistrate Yang Chunsheng submitted and retained his former office. In the fiftieth year of Qianlong the post became a hereditary eighth-rank native official.
43
鎮南州土州同，居本城。 清順治十六年，土州同段光贊歸附，仍授世職。
The native department vice prefect of Zhennan was seated in the prefectural city. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native department vice prefect Duan Guangzan submitted and kept the hereditary office.
44
鎮南州土州判，居鎮南州城東北。 清順治十六年，土州判陳昌虞歸附，仍授世職。
The native department judge of Zhennan was seated northeast of the prefectural city. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native department judge Chen Changyu submitted and kept the hereditary office.
45
阿雄關土巡檢，居鎮南州屬。 清順治十六年，土巡檢者光祖歸附，仍授世職。
The native inspector of Axiong Pass was posted within Zhennan Prefecture. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native inspector Zhe Guangzu submitted and kept the hereditary office.
46
鎮南關土巡檢。 清順治十六年，土巡檢楊繼祖歸附，仍授舊職，傳至楊文輝，乾隆五十年，改為從九品土官，世襲。
The native inspector of Zhennan Pass. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native inspector Yang Jizu submitted and retained his office; the line reached Yang Wenhui; in the fiftieth year of Qianlong the post became a hereditary ninth-rank native official.
47
姚州土州同，居姚州西界彌興官莊。 清順治十六年，土州同高顯錫歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至高配忝，乾隆五十年，改為從六品土官，世襲。
The native department vice prefect of Yaozhou was seated at the Mixing official manor on the prefecture's western border. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native department vice prefect Gao Xianxi submitted and retained his former office. The line reached Gao Peitian; in the fiftieth year of Qianlong the post became a hereditary sixth-rank native official.
48
回磴關土巡檢，居回磴關。 清順治十六年，土巡檢楊忠藎歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至楊怡，乾隆五十年，改為從九品土官，世襲。
The native inspector of Huideng Pass was posted at Huideng Pass. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native inspector Yang Zhongjin submitted and retained his former office. The line reached Yang Yi; in the fiftieth year of Qianlong the post became a hereditary ninth-rank native official.
49
沙矣舊土巡檢。 清順治十六年，土巡檢蘇鑒歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至蘇敬，乾隆五十年，改為從九品土官，世襲。
The native inspector of Shayiji. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native inspector Su Jian submitted and retained his former office. The line reached Su Jing; in the fiftieth year of Qianlong the post became a hereditary ninth-rank native official.
50
定遠縣土主簿，居本城。 清順治十六年，土主簿李世卿歸附，仍授舊職。 傳至李毓英，乾隆五十年，改為正九品土官，世襲。
The native registrar of Dingyuan County was seated in the county town. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native registrar Li Shiqing submitted and retained his former office. The line reached Li Yuying; in the fiftieth year of Qianlong the post became a hereditary upper ninth-rank native official.
51
姚安府土同知。 清順治十六年，土同知高奣映歸附，仍授世職。 傳至李厚德，雍正三年，以不法革職，安置江南。
The native associate prefect of Yao'an Prefecture. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native associate prefect Gao Benying submitted and kept the hereditary office. The line reached Li Houde; in the third year of Yongzheng he was removed for misconduct and resettled in Jiangnan.
52
新興州土州判，居州南研和邑。 清康熙十九年，復滇，土人王鳳授偽遊擊，迎至廣西路投誠； 隨征石門檻、馬別河、黃草壩皆有功，授土州判世職。
The native department judge of Xinxing was seated at Yanhe, south of the prefectural city. In the nineteenth year of Kangxi, when Yunnan was recovered, the local leader Wang Feng, who had held a rebel guerrilla commission, was received on the Guangxi route to submit; He served with distinction at Shimenkan, the Mabie River, and Huangcaoba and received the hereditary department judgeship.
53
河陽縣安插土官。 清順治初，土官刀韜歸附，止給劄，仍准世襲。 沿至刀廷俊，裁革。
The resettled native official of Heyang County. Early in the Shunzhi reign the native official Dao Tao submitted; he received only a commission but was allowed to inherit the post. The line continued until Dao Tingjun, when the office was abolished.
54
鐵爐關土巡檢。 清順治十六年，土巡檢王先榮歸附，授世職。 康熙四年，同王耀祖叛，削除。
The native inspector of Tielu Pass. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native inspector Wang Xianrong submitted and received the hereditary office. In the fourth year of Kangxi he joined Wang Yaozu in rebellion and the office was abolished.
55
廣南府土同知。 清順治十六年，儂鵬歸附，授同知世職。 傳至儂毓榮，乾隆三十一年，從征普洱、緬甸。 三十七年，頒給土同知關防。 子世昌，嘉慶二年從征貴州仲苗，加銜一等，賞戴花翎，世襲。
The native associate prefect of Guangnan Prefecture. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi Nong Peng submitted and received the hereditary associate prefecture. The line reached Nong Yurong, who in the thirty-first year of Qianlong served in campaigns in Pu'er and Burma. In the thirty-seventh year he received the official seal of the native associate prefect. His son Shichang served against the Zhong Miao of Guizhou in the second year of Jiaqing, was promoted one rank, awarded the peacock feather, and granted hereditary succession.
56
富州土知州，在府東二百六十五里。 清順治十六年，土知州沈昆巘歸附，仍授世職。 康熙九年，頒給州印。 後以罪黜，傳至沈肇乾。 雍正八年，肇乾復以罪黜。
The native prefect of Fuzhou lay two hundred sixty-five li east of the prefectural seat. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native prefect Shen Kunyan submitted and kept the hereditary office. In the ninth year of Kangxi the departmental seal was issued. He was later removed for misconduct; the line reached Shen Zhaoqian. In the eighth year of Yongzheng Zhaoqian was again removed for misconduct.
57
大猛麻土巡檢。 清順治十六年，土巡檢俸新命歸附，仍授世職。
The native inspector of Damengma. In the sixteenth year of Shunzhi the native inspector Feng Xinming submitted and kept the hereditary office.
58
猛猛土巡檢，明末奔竄，失其印信號紙，未能請襲。 傳子紫芝，清康熙五十四年歸附，貢象，仍授世職，頒給鈐記。 乾隆二十九年，改屬順寧府。
The Mengmeng native inspector fled at the end of the Ming, lost his seal and patent, and could not secure succession. His son Zizhi submitted in the fifty-fourth year of Kangxi, presented tribute elephants, recovered the hereditary office, and received an official chop. In the twenty-ninth year of Qianlong it was transferred to Shunning Prefecture.
59
孟連宣撫司，在順甯府邊外南境，舊隸於永昌府。 清康熙四十八年，刁派鼎貢象，歸附，授宣撫司世職。 派鼎死，子刁派春年幼，叔祖刁派烈撫孤。 有刁派猷謀殺派烈，奪印爭職，安插省城，另給宣撫司鈐記便委。 傳至刁派新，因地處極邊，界連外域，定為經制宣撫司，頒給印信號紙。 乾隆二十九年，改隸順寧府。
The Menglian pacification superintendency lay on the southern frontier beyond Shunning and had formerly been under Yongchang Prefecture. In the forty-eighth year of Kangxi Diao Pai Ding presented tribute elephants, submitted, and received the hereditary pacification superintendency. When Pai Ding died, his young son Diao Pai Chun was raised by his great-uncle Diao Pai Lie. Diao Pai You plotted to kill Pai Lie and seize the office; he was resettled in the provincial capital while a provisional chop for the superintendency was issued. The line reached Diao Pai Xin; because the domain lay on the outer frontier adjoining foreign lands, it was made a regular pacification superintendency with full seal and patent. In the twenty-ninth year of Qianlong it was placed under Shunning Prefecture.
60
猛緬長官司，清乾隆十一年，歸流，改其地為緬寧，設流官通判駐其地。
The Mengmian chief office: in the eleventh year of Qianlong it was abolished; the territory became Mianning under a regular subprefect.
61
平彝縣土縣丞，居平彝縣竹園村。 清順治初，土縣丞龍闊歸附，仍舊世襲。
The native assistant magistrate of Pingyi County was seated at Zhuyuan Village. Early in the Shunzhi reign the native assistant magistrate Long Kuo submitted and kept the hereditary office.
