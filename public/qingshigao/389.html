1
柏葰，原名松葰，字靜濤，巴魯特氏，蒙古正藍旗人。 道光六年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 累遷內閣學士，兼正紅旗漢軍副都統。 十八年，出為盛京工部侍郎，調刑部，兼管奉天府尹。 二十年，召授刑部侍郎，調吏部，又調戶部。 二十三年，充諭祭朝鮮正使，例有餽贐，奏卻之。 二十五年，充總管內務府大臣。 二十六年，典江南鄉試。 疏言：「徵漕大戶短欠，取償小戶，劣紳挾制官吏，大戶包攬小戶，畸輕畸重，旗丁需索，加增津貼諸弊，請嚴禁。 」如議行。 尋偕倉場侍郎陳孚恩盤查山東籓庫，劾布政使王篤濫用幕友及地方官縱盜，巡撫崇恩以下議譴有差。 二十八年，擢左都御史。 三十年，遷兵部尚書，授內大臣。 尋調吏部，管理三庫，兼翰林院掌院學士。 咸豐三年，命偕侍郎善燾赴盛京按協領塔芬布輕聽謠言，調兵護宅，幾至激變，得實，論遣戍。 將軍奕興坐袒護，革任。 尋以前在鑲白旗蒙古都統任揀選承襲有誤，罷內務府大臣，降授左副都御史。 未幾，出為馬蘭鎮總兵。 五年，擢熱河都統，搜捕山匪。 疏言：「熱河將惰兵疲，州縣不諳吏治。 行使大錢，民皆罷市。 礦匪佔踞山場，委員侵蝕商款。 」詔嚴切查辦。 召授戶部尚書，兼正黃旗漢軍都統。 六年，命在軍機大臣上行走，兼翰林院掌院學士。 尋以戶部尚書協辦大學士。 八年，典順天鄉試，拜文淵閣大學士。
Bo Yi—originally Song Yi, courtesy name Jingtao—belonged to the Balute clan and came from the Mongol Plain Blue Banner. In Daoguang 6 (1826) he became a jinshi, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and received appointment as compiler. He rose step by step to Grand Secretariat secretary, while also serving as vice lieutenant-general of the Plain Red Banner Chinese Brigade. In year 18 he was posted as Mukden vice minister of Works, moved to Justice, and took charge of the Mukden metropolitan magistrate as well. In year 20 he was recalled to the capital as vice minister of Justice, then shifted to Personnel and afterward to Revenue. In year 23 he headed the Korean condolence mission; though presents were customary, he reported that he refused them. In year 25 he became superintendent of the Imperial Household Department. In year 26 he served as chief examiner for the Jiangnan provincial examinations. In a memorial he wrote: "Large transport households that default shift the loss onto small ones; low-grade gentry bully officials; large households bundle small accounts so levies fall unevenly; banner couriers extort extras and transit subsidies keep rising—these abuses must be stopped." The court adopted his proposal. He soon joined Granary vice minister Chen Fuen in auditing Shandong's provincial treasury, impeached treasurer Wang Du for misusing staff and local officials for tolerating bandits, and Chong En the governor and others were reprimanded in due measure. In year 28 he was made left censor-in-chief. In year 30 he became minister of War and an inner-court grandee. Shortly after he moved to Personnel, took charge of the three treasuries, and also became Hanlin Academy chancellor. In Xianfeng 3 he and Vice Minister Shan Xi were sent to Mukden to investigate Assistant Commandant Tafenbu, who had heeded rumors, called out troops to guard his house, and almost sparked a mutiny; the case proved true and he was banished. General Yi Xing was punished for covering for him and removed from his post. Soon after, an earlier mistake in choosing an heir while Mongol lieutenant-general of the Bordered White Banner cost him the Household post and left him demoted to left vice censor-in-chief. Not long after he was sent out as commandant of Malan Garrison. In year 5 he was promoted to Rehe lieutenant-general and hunted mountain bandits. He reported: "Rehe's command is lax and its soldiers worn down; local officials lack sound administration. Issuing large cash coins has driven the people to close their markets. Mining bandits hold the high ground, and commissioners skim merchants' payments." The throne ordered a stern inquiry. Recalled, he became minister of Revenue and lieutenant-general of the Plain Yellow Banner Chinese Brigade. In year 6 he was placed on the Grand Council while keeping the Hanlin chancellorship. Soon afterward, as revenue minister, he was made associate grand secretary. In year 8 he ran the Shuntian provincial exams and was appointed Wenyuan Pavilion grand secretary.
2
柏葰素持正，自登樞府，與載垣、端華、肅順等不協。 會御史孟傳金疏劾本科士論未孚，命覆勘試卷，應議者五十卷，文宗震怒，褫柏葰等職，命載垣等會鞫，得柏葰聽信家人靳祥言，取中羅鴻繹情事，靳祥斃於獄。 九年，讞上，上猶有矜全之意，為肅順等所持。 乃召見王大臣等諭曰：「科場為掄才大典，交通舞弊，定例綦嚴。 自來典試諸臣，從無敢以身試法者。 不意柏葰以一品大員，辜恩藐法，至於如是！ 柏葰身任大臣，且系科甲進士出身，豈不知科場定例？ 竟以家人幹請，輒即撤換試卷。 若使靳祥尚在，加以夾訊，何難盡情吐露？ 既有成憲可循，即不為已甚，就所供各節，情雖可原，法難寬宥，言念及此，不禁垂淚！ 」柏葰遂伏法。
Bo Yi was known for integrity; once he joined the Grand Council he clashed with Zaiyuan, Duanhua, Sushun, and their faction. Censor Meng Chuanjin charged that the new jinshi class was discontented; the papers were re-checked and fifty were faulted. Emperor Wenzong in rage dismissed Bo Yi and others and had Zaiyuan's group investigate jointly. They proved Bo Yi had heeded his servant Jin Xiang and passed Luo Hongyi; Jin Xiang died in custody. In year 9 the sentence went up; the emperor still wished to show mercy, but Sushun and his allies prevailed. He summoned the princes and senior ministers and said: "Examinations are the grand rite for choosing talent; bribery and fraud are barred by iron precedent. No examiner has ever dared break the law on his own account. Yet Bo Yi, a first-rank minister, has scorned imperial favor and flouted the law so brazenly!" Bo Yi holds high rank and is himself a jinshi—how could he not know the rules? Still, at a household servant's request, he swapped papers without hesitation. If Jin Xiang were alive and racked, would he not have told all? Precedent stands; though I might not press every extremity, on what has been admitted the facts may plead for pity but the code cannot bend—at the thought I cannot hold back tears!" Bo Yi was executed.
3
十一年，穆宗即位，肅順等既敗，御史任兆堅疏請昭雪，下禮、刑兩部詳議，議上，詔曰：「柏葰聽受囑託，罪無可辭。 惟載垣、端華、肅順等因律無僅關囑託明文，比賄買關節之例，擬以斬決。 由載垣等平日與柏葰挾有私仇，欲因擅作威福，竟以牽連蒙混之詞，致罹重闢。 皇考聖諭有'不禁垂淚'之語，仰見不為已甚之心。 今兩宮皇太后政令維新，事事務從寬大平允。 柏葰不能謂無罪，該御史措詞失當。 念柏葰受恩兩朝，內廷行走多年，平日勤慎，雖已置重典，當推皇考法外之仁。 」於是錄其子候選員外郎鍾濂賜四品卿銜，以六部郎中遇缺即選。 鍾濂後官盛京兵部侍郎。
In year 11, after Muzong succeeded and Sushun's party fell, Censor Ren Zhaojian asked that Bo Yi be vindicated; Rites and Justice deliberated and reported. The edict read: "Bo Yi took bribes of influence—his guilt is clear. Yet Zaiyuan, Duanhua, and Sushun, finding no statute that covered influence alone, matched him to the bribery precedent and urged decapitation. They had long nursed private enmity and, abusing their power, smeared him with vague charges until he faced death. His late father's words, 'cannot hold back tears,' show he did not wish to be harsh. Now the two empress dowagers reform rule and seek fairness in every matter. Bo Yi was not innocent, and the censor spoke out of turn. Yet he served two reigns, walked the inner court for years, and was usually careful; though the law took his life, his father's grace beyond the code should be remembered. His son Zhong Lian, a candidate vice director, was given fourth-rank courtier standing and priority selection as a department director. Zhong Lian later rose to Mukden vice minister of War.
4
麟魁，字梅谷，索綽羅氏，滿洲鑲白旗人。 道光六年二甲一名進士，選庶吉士，散館改刑部主事，遷中允。 歷庶子、侍講學士、詹事、通政使、左副都御史。 十七年，出為盛京刑部侍郎。 十八年，召授刑部侍郎，兼鑲紅旗漢軍副都統。 二十年，署倉場侍郎。 命偕侍郎吳其濬赴湖北按事，劾總督週天爵酷刑，罷之，其濬留署總督。 麟魁復往江西鞫鬧漕京控之獄，及江蘇邳州知州賈輝山被劾濫用非刑等事，並治如律。 調戶部，又調吏部，充總管內務府大臣。 二十二年，出署山東巡撫。 英兵犯江南，疏陳登州突出黃、渤，三面環海，敵兵船砲堅利，計難與爭，請移兵扼陸路險要。 尋偕侍郎王植赴湖南鞫獄，並勘湖南、江蘇、山東水災，奏請蠲緩，如所請行。 二十三年，擢禮部尚書，管理太常寺、鴻​​臚寺。 河決中牟，命偕尚書廖鴻荃往督工，東西兩​​壩成而屢蟄，褫職，予七品頂戴，仍留工，以料缺水增請緩，复褫頂戴。 召還，予三等侍衛，充葉爾羌參贊大臣，調烏里雅蘇台參贊大臣。
Lin Kui, courtesy name Meigu, of the Suochiluo clan, was a Manchu of the Bordered White Banner. In Daoguang 6 he ranked second on the jinshi list, entered the Hanlin, left the academy for a Justice clerkship, and became vice director of the Supervisorate. He served as vice director, lecturer, junior tutor, grand tutor, transmission commissioner, and left vice censor-in-chief in turn. In year 17 he was posted Mukden vice minister of Justice. In year 18 he was recalled as vice minister of Justice and vice lieutenant-general of the Bordered Red Banner Chinese Brigade. In year 20 he acted as granary vice minister. Ordered with Vice Minister Wu Qijun to Hubei, he impeached Governor Zhou Tianjue for torture and had him dismissed; Qijun stayed as acting governor. He then tried Jiangxi grain-riot appeals and the case of Pizhou prefect Jia Huishan in Jiangsu for illegal torture; offenders were punished by law. He moved to Revenue, then Personnel, and became Household superintendent. In year 22 he acted as Shandong governor. As British forces struck Jiangnan he argued Dengzhou thrusts into the Yellow and Bohai with sea on three sides; enemy ships and cannon were too strong at sea, and troops should hold the land passes instead. Soon he and Vice Minister Wang Zhi tried Hunan cases and surveyed floods in Hunan, Jiangsu, and Shandong; relief and tax remission were granted as he asked. In year 23 he became minister of Rites, overseeing the Sacrifice and Ceremonies courts. After the Yellow River burst at Zhongmou he and Minister Liao Hongquan supervised repairs; east and west dikes were finished yet kept failing. He lost his post, kept seventh-rank insignia on the job, and when supplies ran short asked delay—then lost the insignia too. Recalled, he received third-rank bodyguard rank, served as Yarkand assistant minister, then Uliastai assistant minister.
5
二十七年，召授禮部侍郎，調刑部。 二十八年，复授禮部尚書，兼翰林院掌院學士。 以前在山東收受陋規，降三級調用，予副都統銜，充烏什辦事大臣。 咸豐元年，疏陳時事，略曰：「廣西逆匪，勞師糜餉。 其始不過星星之火，當時牧令苟安畏事，諱盜不言； 久之蒂固蔓延，養成巨患。 請飭封疆大吏嚴查地方，如有教匪、土匪聚眾以及搶劫，隨時查拏，視緝捕之勤惰以為勸懲。 近開捐例，實朝廷萬不得已之舉，各省清查，屢經申令。 宜飭部臣按時詳覈徵解多寡，實行賞罰章程，俾生愧奮。 否則名託清查，事仍敷衍，國儲不裕，官紀益荒，甚非朝廷澄清吏治之意。 」奏入，下所司議行。 授察哈爾副都統，召為戶部侍郎。
In year 27 he was recalled as vice minister of Rites, then Justice. In year 28 he again became rites minister and Hanlin chancellor. Earlier Shandong "customary fees" cost him three ranks, vice lieutenant-general insignia, and a posting as Ush minister in charge. In Xianfeng 1 he memorialized on affairs of the day: "Guangxi rebels wear out armies and treasury. They began as scattered sparks; magistrates, clinging to ease and fearing trouble, hid banditry and kept silent; until roots ran deep, the plague spread, and a great scourge was bred. He asked frontier officials to search their ground: wherever sectaries, hill bandits, or robbers gathered, arrest them at once and reward or punish by results. The newly opened sale of offices is a last resort; provinces have been told again and again to audit. Ministries should verify collections and deliveries on schedule and enforce rewards and penalties so officials feel shame and stir themselves. Otherwise audits stay nominal, the treasury thin, and discipline lax—far from clarifying rule. The memorial went in and responsible offices were told to act. He became Chahar vice lieutenant-general and was recalled as revenue vice minister.
6
二年，命在軍機大臣上行走，擢工部尚書。 三年，調禮部，充總管內務府大臣，罷直軍機，調刑部。 八年，復調禮部，補內大臣。 十年，因謝恩摺失檢，降授刑部侍郎。 是年秋，車駕幸熱河，命署右翼總兵，充巡防大臣。 英法兵入京師，麟魁部勒僚屬，戒都人守望相助，令家人閉戶厝薪，曰：「事急即燔！ 」自宿於巡防廨中，相持數月。 和議成，赴行在，籥請回鑾，為載垣、端華、肅順等所阻。 十一年，遷左都御史，兼正白旗蒙古都統，尋授兵部尚書。 同治元年，協辦大學士。 時方奉命偕尚書沈兆霖赴甘肅按事，至蘭州，數日遽卒，詔依大學士例賜血卩，賜其子恩壽舉人，諡文端。 恩壽，同治十三年進士，官至陝西巡撫。
In year 2 he joined the Grand Council and became works minister. In year 3 he moved to Rites, became Household superintendent, left the Council, and shifted to Justice. In year 8 he returned to Rites and regained inner-court grandee status. In year 10 a lost thanksgiving memorial cost him the justice vice ministership. That autumn, as the court went to Rehe, he acted as right-wing commandant and patrol defense commissioner. When Anglo-French troops entered Beijing, Lin Kui rallied his staff, urged citizens to watch and aid one another, and told his household to bar doors and pile fuel: "Burn everything if it comes to that!" He slept in the patrol office and held on for months. After peace he went to the traveling court and pleaded for the emperor's return; Zaiyuan, Duanhua, and Sushun blocked him. In year 11 he became left censor-in-chief and Plain White Mongol lieutenant-general, then war minister. In Tongzhi 1 he was made associate grand secretary. Just then he and Minister Shen Zhaolin were sent to Gansu; he reached Lanzhou and died within days. Mourning followed grand-secretary precedent, his son Enshou received juren rank, and he was posthumously titled Wenduan. Enshou became a Tongzhi 13 jinshi and rose to Shaanxi governor.
7
瑞常，字芝生，石爾德特氏，蒙古鑲紅旗人，杭州駐防。 道光十二年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 大考二等，六遷至少詹事。 二十四年，連擢光祿寺卿、內閣學士。 二十五年，遷兵部侍郎，兼鑲紅旗漢軍副都統。 二十九年，充冊封朝鮮正使。 調吏部，歷兼左、右翼總兵。 咸豐元年，典江南鄉試，就勘徐州豐北河決，疏陳災情、賑務、漕務，請飭地方官嚴防匪徒蠢擾，報聞。 定郡王載銓管步軍統領，越次題升主事，瑞常力爭不得。 尋解左翼總兵職。 七年，擢左都御史。 八年，遷理籓院尚書，兼正藍旗漢軍都統，署步軍統領，調刑部尚書。 十年，寶源局監督張仁政因侵蝕畏罪自盡，命瑞常偕尚書沈兆霖按之，得前任監督奎麟、瑞琇贓私狀，並論大辟，追贓後遣戍。 文宗幸熱河，留京辦事，督防巡防。 十一年，調工部，又調戶部。
Rui Chang, courtesy name Zhisheng, of the Shierdete clan, was a Bordered Red Mongol garrisoned at Hangzhou. In Daoguang 12 he became jinshi, Hanlin bachelor, and compiler. In the palace examination he took second class and rose six steps to junior vice director of the Supervisorate. In year 24 he rose in quick succession to Court of Entertainments director and Grand Secretariat secretary. In year 25 he became war vice minister and Bordered Red Chinese vice lieutenant-general. In year 29 he headed the Korean investiture mission. He moved to Personnel and held both wing commandancies in turn. In Xianfeng 1 he ran the Jiangnan provincial exams; inspecting the Fengbei breach at Xuzhou he reported on disaster, relief, and transport and warned officials to watch for bandits—the court took note. Prince of Ding Zaiquan oversaw the infantry and promoted a clerk out of turn; Rui Chang fought it in vain. He soon left the left-wing command. In year 7 he became left censor-in-chief. In year 8 he became colonial affairs minister, Plain Blue Chinese lieutenant-general, acting infantry superintendent, then justice minister. In year 10 Baoyuan supervisor Zhang Renzheng killed himself after embezzlement; Rui Chang and Shen Zhaolin found former supervisors Kui Lin and Rui Xiu guilty of graft; both were condemned to death, then banished after restitution. When Wenzong went to Rehe, Rui Chang stayed in Beijing to manage affairs and oversee patrol defense. In year 11 he moved to Works, then to Revenue.
8
同治元年，以吏部尚書協辦大學士。 皇太后命南書房、上書房翰林纂輯史事以昭法戒，書成，賜名治平寶鑑，遴擇大臣輪班進講，瑞常與焉。 四年，充總管內務府大臣。 時陝西巡撫劉蓉驟起膺疆寄，為編修蔡壽祺所劾，蓉自陳辯，疏中引及胡林翼密薦之詞，又倚任布政使林壽圖，為人所忌。 言官遂劾壽圖湎酒廢事，舉劾不公，並訐蓉漏洩之罪，於是命瑞常偕尚書羅惇衍往按之，疏白其無罪，惟坐壽圖演戲及蓉陳奏失當，並予薄譴。 定陵奉安禮成，題神主，加太子少保。 歷工部、刑部尚書，兼翰林院掌院學士，管理戶部三庫。 六年，赴天津驗收漕糧，復命盤查北新倉，得虧米六萬餘石狀，論所司罪如律。 十年，拜文淵閣大學士，管理刑部。
In Tongzhi 1, as personnel minister, he became associate grand secretary. The empress dowager had Southern and Upper Studio Hanlin compile historical lessons; the finished work was titled Precious Mirror for Ordering the Age, and chosen ministers lectured in turn—Rui Chang among them. In year 4 he became Household superintendent. Shaanxi Governor Liu Rong, suddenly elevated to frontier duty, was impeached by Compiler Cai Shouqi; Rong defended himself, citing Hu Linyi's private recommendation and leaning on Treasurer Lin Shoutu—both of which bred resentment. Censors charged Shoutu with drunken neglect and unfair personnel acts, and Rong with leaking secrets; Rui Chang and Minister Luo Dunyan were sent to investigate, cleared them of major guilt, but lightly censured Shoutu for theater and Rong for faulty memorials. After Ding Mausoleum interment he wrote the spirit tablet and received Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. He served as works and justice minister, Hanlin chancellor, and manager of Revenue's three treasuries. In year 6 he inspected tribute grain at Tianjin, then audited Beixin Granary and found over sixty thousand shi missing; offenders were punished by law. In year 10 he became Wenyuan grand secretary and oversaw Justice.
9
瑞常歷事三朝，端謹無過，累司文柄，時稱耆碩。 十一年，卒，贈太保，祀賢良祠，諡文端。 子文暉，官至盛京禮部侍郎。
Rui Chang served three reigns, was scrupulous and blameless, often held literary power, and was hailed as an elder statesman. In year 11 he died, was posthumously made Grand Guardian, entered the Temple of Worthies, and received the posthumous title Wenduan. His son Wenhui became Mukden vice minister of Rites.
10
全慶，字小汀，葉赫納喇氏，滿洲正白旗人，尚書那清安子。 道光九年進士，選庶吉士，授編修，累遷侍講。 大考二等，擢侍讀學士。 歷少詹事、詹事、大理寺卿。 以誤班鐫級。 二十一年，予頭等侍衛，充古城領隊大臣，調喀喇沙爾辦事大臣。 召還，未行，會回疆興墾，伊犁將軍布彥泰疏留全慶偕林則徐往勘。 二十五年，至葉爾羌，疏言：「和爾罕地膏腴，哈拉木札什水渠可資灌溉。 又巴爾楚克為回疆扼要之地，道光十二年已奏開墾屯田，未種者尚多，應先侭安插民戶，俾成重鎮。 」詔如所請行。 先是，全慶疏陳喀喇沙爾環城荒地，及庫爾勒、北山根，可墾田萬餘畝，命辦事大臣常清籌辦。 至是復偕則徐詳勘，疏言：「庫爾勒應於此大渠南岸接開中渠，引入新墾之地，分開支渠二。 其北山根展寬開都河龍口，別開大渠，與舊渠並行； 再分支渠四，別開退水渠一。 」又疏言：「伊拉里克在吐魯番托克遜軍台西，地平土潤，土人謂之'板土戈壁'。 其西為'沙石戈壁'，有大小阿拉渾兩水，彙為一河。 此次引水自西而東，鑿成大渠，復多開支渠以資灌溉。 伊拉里克西南沿山為蒙古出入之路，墾地在滿卡南附近，東西兩面，以'人壽年豐'四字分號，各設正副戶長一，鄉約四，擇誠實農民充之，承領耕種。 又吐魯番為南北樞紐，應安置內地民戶，戶領地五十畝，農田以水利為首務。 此次開渠，自龍口至黑山頭，地勢高低，碎石夾沙，渠身易淤，酌定經久修治章程。 」並如所請行。 自是回疆南路凡墾田六十餘萬畝。
Quan Qing, courtesy name Xiaoting, of the Yehe Nara clan, was a Plain White Manchu and son of Minister Na Qingan. In Daoguang 9 he became jinshi and Hanlin bachelor, then compiler and lecturer in turn. In the palace examination he took second class and became Hanlin reader. He served as junior vice director, grand tutor, and clan court president. A court-attendance error cost him one rank. In year 21 he received first-rank bodyguard rank, led at Gucheng, then became Karashahr minister in charge. Recalled before departure, Western Regions reclamation began; Ili General Buyantai asked to keep Quan Qing to survey with Lin Zexu. In year 25 at Yarkand he reported: "Heerhan soil is rich; the Khalamuzhash canal can irrigate it. Barkul is a strategic pass; Daoguang 12 already approved colonies, yet much land lies idle—settlers should come first to make it a stronghold. The throne approved. Earlier he had reported Karashahr's outskirts, Korla, and the northern foothills could yield ten thousand-plus mu; Minister Chang Qing was told to plan it. He surveyed again with Lin Zexu: "At Korla open a middle canal south of the main trunk into new land, with two branches. At the northern hills widen the Kaidu mouth and open a new main canal parallel to the old; then four branches and one drainage canal. He also wrote: "Ilarik lies west of Toksun on the Turfan route—level, moist ground locals call 'plate-soil gobi.'" West lies 'gravel gobi,' where great and small Alahun streams unite. Water will run west to east through a main canal and many branches for irrigation. Southwest of Ilarik is the Mongol route; colonies near Manka split east and west under the motto 'long life, rich harvest,' each with chief and deputy heads and four elders—honest farmers who receive plots. Turfan is the north-south hinge; inland settlers should get fifty mu each—water comes first in farming. From river mouth to Heishantou the slope varies, gravel and sand clog the bed—lasting repair rules were set. All was approved. Thereafter the southern route had over six hundred thousand mu under cultivation.
11
回京，擢內閣學士，兼正紅旗漢軍副都統。 歷刑部、吏部、戶部、倉場侍郎。 咸豐四年，擢工部尚書，兼正紅旗漢軍都統。 七年，調兵部。 九年，命赴天津驗收漕糧。 時英兵犯大沽，僧格林沁擊卻之。 全慶疏陳兵事，略謂：「敵軍戰敗之後，不進不退，心實叵測。 竊恐別有舉動，未必從此就撫而去。 我之精銳，盡萃大沽，旁無應援，後無擁護。 雙港之旅，已調前敵； 津門之備，但資土練； 北塘一帶，又頗空虛。 應請速簡重臣，發勁旅，嚴近畿海口之備，為僧格林沁之援，令廣東義勇搗香港以牽其援兵，登州水師合旅順以截其歸路，然後國威可振，撫局可成。 」疏入，被嘉納。 調吏部尚書。
Back in the capital he became Grand Secretariat secretary and Plain Red Chinese vice lieutenant-general. He served as justice, personnel, revenue, and granary vice minister. In Xianfeng 4 he became works minister and Plain Red Chinese lieutenant-general. In year 7 he moved to War. In year 9 he was sent to Tianjin to inspect tribute grain. British forces struck Dagu and Sengge Rinchen drove them back. He memorialized on war: "After defeat the enemy neither advances nor retreats—its intent is hard to read. They may not simply accept terms and leave. Our best troops cluster at Dagu without flank support or rear reserve. Shuanggang's troops are already at the front; Tianjin relies on local militia alone; Beitang is largely undefended. He asked for a senior commander, fresh troops, strengthened sea defenses to aid Sengge Rinchen, Guangdong militia to raid Hong Kong, and Dengzhou ships with Lüshun to cut retreat—then prestige and peace could follow. The memorial won praise. He became personnel minister.
12
十年，授內大臣，兼翰林院掌院學士。 十一年，充總管內務府大臣。 同治元年，追論大學士柏葰科場之獄原讞未允，全慶坐附和定讞，鐫四級，降授大理寺卿。 歷內閣學士、工部侍郎、左都御史。 五年，授禮部尚書，調刑部。 十一年，協辦大學士，兼翰林院掌院學士。 十二年，典順天鄉試，以中式舉人徐景春試卷疵謬，鐫二級去職。
In year 10 he became inner-court grandee and Hanlin chancellor. In year 11 he became Household superintendent. In Tongzhi 1, review of Bo Yi's examination verdict punished Quan Qing for concurring—four ranks lost, demoted to clan court president. He served as Grand Secretariat secretary, works vice minister, and left censor-in-chief. In year 5 he became rites minister, then justice minister. In year 11 he became associate grand secretary and Hanlin chancellor. In year 12 he ran the Shuntian exams; faulty papers by graduate Xu Jingchun cost him two ranks and his post.
13
全慶易攵歷清要，累掌文衡，更閱四朝，雖屢黜，尋即錄用。 光緒元年，授內閣學士。 复歷禮部侍郎、左都御史、刑部尚書、協辦大學士。 五年，鄉舉重逢，加太子少保。 六年，拜體仁閣大學士。 七年，致仕，食全俸。 八年，卒，晉贈太子太保，祀賢良祠，諡文恪。
Quan Qing had held key central posts, often ran examinations, and spanned four reigns—demoted often, yet soon re-employed. In Guangxu 1 he became Grand Secretariat secretary. He again served as rites vice minister, left censor-in-chief, justice minister, and associate grand secretary. In year 5, encountering the provincial exam again in senior service, he received Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. In year 6 he became Tiren Pavilion grand secretary. In year 7 he retired on full pay. In year 8 he died, was raised posthumously to Grand Guardian, entered the Temple of Worthies, and was titled Wenkai.
14
論曰：自道光以來，科場請託，習為故常，寒門才士，為之抑遏。 柏葰立朝正直，且所不免，其罹大辟也，出於肅順等之構陷。 然自此司文衡者懍懍畏法，科場清肅，曆三十年，至光緒中始漸弛，弊竇复滋，終未至如前此之甚者，實文宗用重典之效，足以挽回風氣也。 麟魁、瑞常、全慶皆起家文學，洊陟綸扉，其建白猶有可紀焉。
The appraisal says: Since Daoguang, examination favoritism became habit and poor scholars were shut out. Bo Yi stood upright yet could not escape; his death came from Sushun's faction framing him. Afterward examiners feared the law; halls stayed clean thirty years until Guangxu slackened—yet never as bad as before—Wenzong's stern example had turned custom. Lin Kui, Rui Chang, and Quan Qing all rose through letters to the grand secretariat; their counsel is still worth noting.
