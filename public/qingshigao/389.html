柏葰，原名松葰，字靜濤，巴魯特氏，蒙古正藍旗人。 道光六年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 累遷內閣學士，兼正紅旗漢軍副都統。 十八年，出為盛京工部侍郎，調刑部，兼管奉天府尹。 二十年，召授刑部侍郎，調吏部，又調戶部。 二十三年，充諭祭朝鮮正使，例有餽贐，奏卻之。 二十五年，充總管內務府大臣。 二十六年，典江南鄉試。 疏言：「徵漕大戶短欠，取償小戶，劣紳挾制官吏，大戶包攬小戶，畸輕畸重，旗丁需索，加增津貼諸弊，請嚴禁。 」如議行。 尋偕倉場侍郎陳孚恩盤查山東籓庫，劾布政使王篤濫用幕友及地方官縱盜，巡撫崇恩以下議譴有差。 二十八年，擢左都御史。 三十年，遷兵部尚書，授內大臣。 尋調吏部，管理三庫，兼翰林院掌院學士。 咸豐三年，命偕侍郎善燾赴盛京按協領塔芬布輕聽謠言，調兵護宅，幾至激變，得實，論遣戍。 將軍奕興坐袒護，革任。 尋以前在鑲白旗蒙古都統任揀選承襲有誤，罷內務府大臣，降授左副都御史。 未幾，出為馬蘭鎮總兵。 五年，擢熱河都統，搜捕山匪。 疏言：「熱河將惰兵疲，州縣不諳吏治。 行使大錢，民皆罷市。 礦匪佔踞山場，委員侵蝕商款。 」詔嚴切查辦。 召授戶部尚書，兼正黃旗漢軍都統。 六年，命在軍機大臣上行走，兼翰林院掌院學士。 尋以戶部尚書協辦大學士。 八年，典順天鄉試，拜文淵閣大學士。
Baizhan, whose original name was Songzhan and whose style was Jingtao, was of the Balute clan, a Mongol bannerman of the Plain Blue Banner. In Daoguang 6 he became a jinshi, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and was appointed a compiler. He rose in succession to grand secretary of the Grand Secretariat and concurrently served as vice commander-in-chief of the Chinese Plain Red Banner. In year 18 he was posted as vice minister of Works at Mukden, transferred to Punishments, and concurrently served as Mukden prefect. In year 20 he was recalled and appointed vice minister of Punishments, then moved to Personnel and again to Revenue. In year 23 he served as chief envoy to Korea to announce imperial sacrifices; customary gifts were offered, and he memorialized that he declined them. In year 25 he was appointed superintendent of the Imperial Household. In year 26 he presided over the Jiangnan provincial civil examination. In a memorial he wrote: "When grain transport taxes are collected, large households' shortfalls are made up from small ones; disreputable gentry coerce officials; large households bundle small accounts so burdens fall unevenly; banner troops extort fees; and surcharges on transport allowances multiply—please forbid these abuses strictly." The court approved the proposal and put it into effect. Soon he joined Granary Vice Minister Chen Fu'en in auditing the Shandong provincial treasury, impeached Administration Commissioner Wang Du for abusing private secretaries and for local officials who tolerated banditry, and Chong'en the governor and subordinates were recommended for censures of varying severity. In year 28 he was promoted to left censor-in-chief. In year 30 he became minister of War and was appointed an imperial chamberlain. He was soon moved to Personnel, put in charge of the three treasuries, and made chancellor of the Hanlin Academy as well. In Xianfeng 3 he was sent with Vice Minister Shanyan to Mukden to investigate Assistant Commandant Tafenbu, who had credulously acted on rumors, mobilized troops to guard his house, and nearly sparked a mutiny; the case proved true and he was sentenced to exile. General Yixing was stripped of his post for covering up the affair. Soon afterward, for an error in selecting succession heirs while he had been commander of the Mongol Bordered White Banner, he was dismissed as superintendent of the Imperial Household and demoted to left vice censor-in-chief. Before long he was posted as regional commander at Malan. In year 5 he was made commander-in-chief of Rehe and led operations against mountain bandits. He memorialized: "Rehe generals are lax and troops exhausted; local officials lack administrative competence. Large-denomination coins are forced on the people, and markets have shut down everywhere. Mining bandits hold the hills, and commissioners skim merchants' payments." The throne ordered a rigorous investigation. He was recalled and made minister of Revenue, concurrently commander-in-chief of the Chinese Plain Yellow Banner. In year 6 he was assigned to the Grand Council and retained the chancellorship of the Hanlin Academy. Soon he was made associate grand secretary while serving as minister of Revenue. In year 8 he oversaw the Shuntian provincial examination and was appointed grand secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion.
2
柏葰素持正，自登樞府，與載垣、端華、肅順等不協。 會御史孟傳金疏劾本科士論未孚，命覆勘試卷，應議者五十卷，文宗震怒，褫柏葰等職，命載垣等會鞫，得柏葰聽信家人靳祥言，取中羅鴻繹情事，靳祥斃於獄。 九年，讞上，上猶有矜全之意，為肅順等所持。 乃召見王大臣等諭曰：「科場為掄才大典，交通舞弊，定例綦嚴。 自來典試諸臣，從無敢以身試法者。 不意柏葰以一品大員，辜恩藐法，至於如是！ 柏葰身任大臣，且系科甲進士出身，豈不知科場定例？ 竟以家人幹請，輒即撤換試卷。 若使靳祥尚在，加以夾訊，何難盡情吐露？ 既有成憲可循，即不為已甚，就所供各節，情雖可原，法難寬宥，言念及此，不禁垂淚！ 」柏葰遂伏法。
Baizhan had long been known for integrity; once he joined the Council he clashed with Zaiyuan, Duhua, Sushun, and their faction. When Censor Meng Chuanjin impeached the graduates' essays as failing to command consensus, the papers were re-examined and fifty were flagged for review. Wenzong was furious, stripped Baizhan and others of rank, and ordered Zaiyuan and colleagues to investigate jointly. They found Baizhan had heeded his retainer Jin Xiang and passed Luo Hongyi; Jin Xiang died in custody. In year 9 the verdict went up; the emperor still meant to spare him, but Sushun and his allies prevailed. He summoned the princes and senior ministers and said: "The civil examinations are the great institution for choosing talent; bribery and cheating are forbidden by the sternest precedent. Presiding examiners have never before dared defy the law themselves. I never imagined that Baizhan, a minister of the first rank, would repay imperial favor with contempt for the law to such an extent! Baizhan holds ministerial rank and is himself a jinshi graduate—how could he not know the rules of the examination hall? Yet at a retainer's request he simply swapped examination papers. Had Jin Xiang still been alive and put to torture, how hard would it have been to make him confess everything? Established law must be followed; even setting aside excess severity, on what was confessed the circumstances might be pardoned but the law cannot be waived. At the thought of this I cannot keep from weeping!" Baizhan was then executed.
3
十一年，穆宗即位，肅順等既敗，御史任兆堅疏請昭雪，下禮、刑兩部詳議，議上，詔曰：「柏葰聽受囑託，罪無可辭。 惟載垣、端華、肅順等因律無僅關囑託明文，比賄買關節之例，擬以斬決。 由載垣等平日與柏葰挾有私仇，欲因擅作威福，竟以牽連蒙混之詞，致罹重闢。 皇考聖諭有'不禁垂淚'之語，仰見不為已甚之心。 今兩宮皇太后政令維新，事事務從寬大平允。 柏葰不能謂無罪，該御史措詞失當。 念柏葰受恩兩朝，內廷行走多年，平日勤慎，雖已置重典，當推皇考法外之仁。 」於是錄其子候選員外郎鍾濂賜四品卿銜，以六部郎中遇缺即選。 鍾濂後官盛京兵部侍郎。
In year 11 Muzong came to the throne; after Sushun and his allies fell, Censor Ren Zhaojian asked that Baizhan's case be reconsidered. The Ministries of Rites and Punishments deliberated and reported back. An edict declared: "Baizhan accepted improper entreaties; his guilt cannot be denied. Yet Zaiyuan, Duhua, and Sushun, finding no statute that treated entreaties alone as capital, applied the precedent for bribery in examination fraud and proposed beheading. Zaiyuan and the rest had long nursed private grudges against Baizhan and, seeking to monopolize power, used muddled charges of implication to bring him to the severest punishment. Our late father's edict spoke of being unable to keep from weeping, revealing his reluctance to punish too harshly. Now that the two empress dowagers govern with renewed policies, every matter is to be handled with generosity and fairness. Baizhan cannot be called innocent; the censor's language was inappropriate. Remembering that Baizhan had served two reigns, walked the inner court for years, and was ordinarily diligent, though the heavy sentence had already been carried out, our late father's mercy beyond the strict letter of the law should be extended. His son Zhong Lian, a candidate for vice director, was then enrolled, granted a fourth-rank Qing title, and made eligible for immediate appointment as a Six Ministries director whenever a vacancy opened. Zhong Lian later rose to vice minister of War at Mukden.
4
麟魁，字梅谷，索綽羅氏，滿洲鑲白旗人。 道光六年二甲一名進士，選庶吉士，散館改刑部主事，遷中允。 歷庶子、侍講學士、詹事、通政使、左副都御史。 十七年，出為盛京刑部侍郎。 十八年，召授刑部侍郎，兼鑲紅旗漢軍副都統。 二十年，署倉場侍郎。 命偕侍郎吳其濬赴湖北按事，劾總督週天爵酷刑，罷之，其濬留署總督。 麟魁復往江西鞫鬧漕京控之獄，及江蘇邳州知州賈輝山被劾濫用非刑等事，並治如律。 調戶部，又調吏部，充總管內務府大臣。 二十二年，出署山東巡撫。 英兵犯江南，疏陳登州突出黃、渤，三面環海，敵兵船砲堅利，計難與爭，請移兵扼陸路險要。 尋偕侍郎王植赴湖南鞫獄，並勘湖南、江蘇、山東水災，奏請蠲緩，如所請行。 二十三年，擢禮部尚書，管理太常寺、鴻​​臚寺。 河決中牟，命偕尚書廖鴻荃往督工，東西兩​​壩成而屢蟄，褫職，予七品頂戴，仍留工，以料缺水增請緩，复褫頂戴。 召還，予三等侍衛，充葉爾羌參贊大臣，調烏里雅蘇台參贊大臣。
Lin Kui, whose style was Meigu, was of the Suochiluo clan, a Manchu bannerman of the Bordered White Banner. In Daoguang 6 he ranked first in the second class of jinshi, entered the Hanlin Academy, and on leaving was posted to the Ministry of Punishments as a secretary, then promoted to vice director of the Academy. He rose through the posts of vice tutor, expositor-in-waiting, grand tutor, transmission commissioner, and left vice censor-in-chief. In year 17 he was posted as vice minister of Punishments at Mukden. In year 18 he was recalled as vice minister of Punishments and concurrently made vice commander-in-chief of the Chinese Bordered Red Banner. In year 20 he served as acting granary vice minister. He was sent with Vice Minister Wu Qijun to investigate in Hubei, impeached Governor Zhou Tianjue for torture, secured his dismissal, and Wu Qijun stayed on as acting governor. Lin Kui then went to Jiangxi to try grain-transport riots appealed to Beijing, and the case of Peizhou Prefect Jia Huishan in Jiangsu, impeached for illegal torture; all were punished as the law required. He was moved to Revenue, then Personnel, and appointed superintendent of the Imperial Household. In year 22 he served as acting governor of Shandong. When British forces threatened Jiangnan, he memorialized that Dengzhou stands between the Yellow and Bohai seas with water on three sides; enemy ships and artillery are formidable and naval battle ill-advised—troops should be redeployed to hold strategic points on land. Soon he joined Vice Minister Wang Zhi in Hunan to hear cases and survey floods in Hunan, Jiangsu, and Shandong; his memorial for tax relief and deferrals was approved. In year 23 he was promoted to minister of Rites and placed over the Courts of Imperial Sacrifices and Ceremonial. When the Yellow River burst at Zhongmou, he was sent with Minister Liao Hongquan to supervise repairs; the eastern and western dikes were finished but kept failing; he was dismissed, given a seventh-rank button, and kept on the project; when materials and water ran short he asked for delay and lost his button again. Recalled to court, he was made a third-rank guardsman, posted as assistant military governor at Yarkand, then transferred to Uliastai.
5
二十七年，召授禮部侍郎，調刑部。 二十八年，复授禮部尚書，兼翰林院掌院學士。 以前在山東收受陋規，降三級調用，予副都統銜，充烏什辦事大臣。 咸豐元年，疏陳時事，略曰：「廣西逆匪，勞師糜餉。 其始不過星星之火，當時牧令苟安畏事，諱盜不言； 久之蒂固蔓延，養成巨患。 請飭封疆大吏嚴查地方，如有教匪、土匪聚眾以及搶劫，隨時查拏，視緝捕之勤惰以為勸懲。 近開捐例，實朝廷萬不得已之舉，各省清查，屢經申令。 宜飭部臣按時詳覈徵解多寡，實行賞罰章程，俾生愧奮。 否則名託清查，事仍敷衍，國儲不裕，官紀益荒，甚非朝廷澄清吏治之意。 」奏入，下所司議行。 授察哈爾副都統，召為戶部侍郎。
In year 27 he was recalled as vice minister of Rites, then moved to Punishments. In year 28 he was again made minister of Rites and chancellor of the Hanlin Academy. For accepting improper fees while in Shandong, he was demoted three ranks and reassigned with vice commander-in-chief rank as minister resident at Ush. In Xianfeng 1 he memorialized on affairs of state, writing in part: "The Guangxi rebels drain troops and treasury. At first they were no more than scattered sparks; local magistrates sought quiet lives, feared trouble, and hid banditry without reporting it; over time the roots deepened and spread until a great calamity had been nurtured. I ask that frontier governors be ordered to investigate their jurisdictions strictly: wherever sect rebels, bandits, or robbers gather, arrest them promptly, and reward or punish officials according to their zeal in pursuit. The recent sale-of-rank quotas were a measure the court adopted only in desperation; provinces have been repeatedly ordered to audit them. Ministry officials should be ordered to verify regularly how much has been collected and remitted, and to enforce reward and punishment so officials feel shame and redouble their efforts. Otherwise audits will remain nominal, work perfunctory, the treasury empty, and official discipline lax—far from the court's aim to restore good government. The memorial was referred to the relevant offices for implementation. He was made vice commander-in-chief of Chahar and recalled as vice minister of Revenue.
6
二年，命在軍機大臣上行走，擢工部尚書。 三年，調禮部，充總管內務府大臣，罷直軍機，調刑部。 八年，復調禮部，補內大臣。 十年，因謝恩摺失檢，降授刑部侍郎。 是年秋，車駕幸熱河，命署右翼總兵，充巡防大臣。 英法兵入京師，麟魁部勒僚屬，戒都人守望相助，令家人閉戶厝薪，曰：「事急即燔！ 」自宿於巡防廨中，相持數月。 和議成，赴行在，籥請回鑾，為載垣、端華、肅順等所阻。 十一年，遷左都御史，兼正白旗蒙古都統，尋授兵部尚書。 同治元年，協辦大學士。 時方奉命偕尚書沈兆霖赴甘肅按事，至蘭州，數日遽卒，詔依大學士例賜血卩，賜其子恩壽舉人，諡文端。 恩壽，同治十三年進士，官至陝西巡撫。
In year 2 he joined the Grand Council and was promoted to minister of Works. In year 3 he moved to Rites, became superintendent of the Imperial Household, left regular Council duty, and was transferred to Punishments. In year 8 he returned to Rites and was restored as imperial chamberlain. In year 10 a lost memorial of thanks for imperial grace cost him his rank; he was demoted to vice minister of Punishments. That autumn the court fled to Rehe; he was made acting commander of the Right Wing and patrol-defense minister. When British and French troops entered Beijing, Lin Kui drilled his staff, urged the capital's people to stand watch together, and told his family to bar the doors and stack firewood, saying: "If the worst comes, set fire at once!" He slept in the patrol office himself and held the line for months. After peace was concluded he went to the temporary court and asked that the emperor return to Beijing, but Zaiyuan, Duhua, Sushun, and their faction blocked him. In year 11 he became left censor-in-chief and commander of the Mongol Plain White Banner, and soon minister of War. In Tongzhi 1 he was made associate grand secretary. He had just been ordered with Minister Shen Zhaolin to investigate in Gansu; reaching Lanzhou, he died within days. An edict granted him the grand secretary rites of blood sacrifice, made his son Enshou a provincial graduate, and gave him the posthumous title Wenduan. Enshou became a jinshi in Tongzhi 13 and rose to governor of Shaanxi.
7
瑞常，字芝生，石爾德特氏，蒙古鑲紅旗人，杭州駐防。 道光十二年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 大考二等，六遷至少詹事。 二十四年，連擢光祿寺卿、內閣學士。 二十五年，遷兵部侍郎，兼鑲紅旗漢軍副都統。 二十九年，充冊封朝鮮正使。 調吏部，歷兼左、右翼總兵。 咸豐元年，典江南鄉試，就勘徐州豐北河決，疏陳災情、賑務、漕務，請飭地方官嚴防匪徒蠢擾，報聞。 定郡王載銓管步軍統領，越次題升主事，瑞常力爭不得。 尋解左翼總兵職。 七年，擢左都御史。 八年，遷理籓院尚書，兼正藍旗漢軍都統，署步軍統領，調刑部尚書。 十年，寶源局監督張仁政因侵蝕畏罪自盡，命瑞常偕尚書沈兆霖按之，得前任監督奎麟、瑞琇贓私狀，並論大辟，追贓後遣戍。 文宗幸熱河，留京辦事，督防巡防。 十一年，調工部，又調戶部。
Rui Chang, whose style was Zhisheng, was of the Shierdete clan, a Mongol bannerman of the Bordered Red Banner garrisoned at Hangzhou. In Daoguang 12 he became a jinshi, entered the Hanlin Academy, and was appointed a compiler. In the palace examination he ranked second class and was promoted six times to junior tutor of the heir apparent. In year 24 he was promoted in quick succession to director of the Court of Imperial Entertainments and grand secretary of the Grand Secretariat. In year 25 he became vice minister of War and concurrently vice commander-in-chief of the Chinese Bordered Red Banner. In year 29 he served as chief envoy on the Korean investiture mission. He moved to Personnel and concurrently commanded the Left and Right Wings in turn. In Xianfeng 1 he presided over the Jiangnan provincial examination; while inspecting the Yellow River breach north of Feng in Xuzhou, he memorialized on the disaster, relief, and grain transport, urging local officials to guard against bandit unrest; the court took note. Prince Ding Zaiquan, who headed the Metropolitan Gendarmerie, promoted a secretary out of turn; Rui Chang protested vigorously but in vain. He was soon relieved of command of the Left Wing. In year 7 he was promoted to left censor-in-chief. In year 8 he became minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs, commander-in-chief of the Chinese Plain Blue Banner, acting metropolitan gendarmerie commander, and then minister of Punishments. In year 10 Zhang Renzheng, superintendent of the Baoyuan Bureau, killed himself after embezzlement was exposed; Rui Chang and Minister Shen Zhaolin investigated, uncovered corruption by former superintendents Kuilin and Ruixiu, sentenced both to death, recovered the stolen goods, and sent them into exile. When Wenzong fled to Rehe, Rui Chang stayed in Beijing on duty and directed patrol defense. In year 11 he was moved to the Ministry of Works, then to Revenue.
8
同治元年，以吏部尚書協辦大學士。 皇太后命南書房、上書房翰林纂輯史事以昭法戒，書成，賜名治平寶鑑，遴擇大臣輪班進講，瑞常與焉。 四年，充總管內務府大臣。 時陝西巡撫劉蓉驟起膺疆寄，為編修蔡壽祺所劾，蓉自陳辯，疏中引及胡林翼密薦之詞，又倚任布政使林壽圖，為人所忌。 言官遂劾壽圖湎酒廢事，舉劾不公，並訐蓉漏洩之罪，於是命瑞常偕尚書羅惇衍往按之，疏白其無罪，惟坐壽圖演戲及蓉陳奏失當，並予薄譴。 定陵奉安禮成，題神主，加太子少保。 歷工部、刑部尚書，兼翰林院掌院學士，管理戶部三庫。 六年，赴天津驗收漕糧，復命盤查北新倉，得虧米六萬餘石狀，論所司罪如律。 十年，拜文淵閣大學士，管理刑部。
In Tongzhi 1, while minister of Personnel, he was made associate grand secretary. The empress dowager ordered Hanlin scholars of the Southern and Upper Studios to compile historical cases as moral lessons; when the work was done it was named the Mirror of Ordered Peace, and senior ministers were chosen to lecture on it in rotation—Rui Chang among them. In year 4 he was appointed superintendent of the Imperial Household. Shaanxi Governor Liu Rong had risen abruptly to a frontier post and was impeached by Compiler Cai Shouqi; Rong defended himself in a memorial citing Hu Linyi's private recommendation, and his reliance on Administration Commissioner Lin Shoutu also drew envy. Censors then impeached Lin Shoutu for drinking and neglect, biased recommendations, and accused Liu Rong of leaking secrets; Rui Chang and Minister Luo Dunyan were sent to investigate. Their memorial cleared both men of serious guilt, but censured Shoutu for attending plays and Rong for improper memorial wording—light penalties only. After the Ding Mausoleum interment rites he received the additional title of Junior Tutor of the Heir Apparent. He served as minister of Works and Punishments in turn, was chancellor of the Hanlin Academy, and managed the three Revenue treasuries. In year 6 he went to Tianjin to inspect tribute grain, then audited Beixin granary and found a shortfall of over sixty thousand shi of rice; those responsible were punished as the law required. In year 10 he was appointed grand secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion and put in charge of the Ministry of Punishments.
9
瑞常歷事三朝，端謹無過，累司文柄，時稱耆碩。 十一年，卒，贈太保，祀賢良祠，諡文端。 子文暉，官至盛京禮部侍郎。
Rui Chang served three reigns, was upright and faultless, repeatedly held charge of literary affairs, and was hailed as a senior statesman of learning. In year 11 he died; he was posthumously made Grand Tutor, enshrined in the Shrine of Worthies, and given the posthumous title Wenduan. His son Wenhui rose to vice minister of Rites at Mukden.
10
全慶，字小汀，葉赫納喇氏，滿洲正白旗人，尚書那清安子。 道光九年進士，選庶吉士，授編修，累遷侍講。 大考二等，擢侍讀學士。 歷少詹事、詹事、大理寺卿。 以誤班鐫級。 二十一年，予頭等侍衛，充古城領隊大臣，調喀喇沙爾辦事大臣。 召還，未行，會回疆興墾，伊犁將軍布彥泰疏留全慶偕林則徐往勘。 二十五年，至葉爾羌，疏言：「和爾罕地膏腴，哈拉木札什水渠可資灌溉。 又巴爾楚克為回疆扼要之地，道光十二年已奏開墾屯田，未種者尚多，應先侭安插民戶，俾成重鎮。 」詔如所請行。 先是，全慶疏陳喀喇沙爾環城荒地，及庫爾勒、北山根，可墾田萬餘畝，命辦事大臣常清籌辦。 至是復偕則徐詳勘，疏言：「庫爾勒應於此大渠南岸接開中渠，引入新墾之地，分開支渠二。 其北山根展寬開都河龍口，別開大渠，與舊渠並行； 再分支渠四，別開退水渠一。 」又疏言：「伊拉里克在吐魯番托克遜軍台西，地平土潤，土人謂之'板土戈壁'。 其西為'沙石戈壁'，有大小阿拉渾兩水，彙為一河。 此次引水自西而東，鑿成大渠，復多開支渠以資灌溉。 伊拉里克西南沿山為蒙古出入之路，墾地在滿卡南附近，東西兩面，以'人壽年豐'四字分號，各設正副戶長一，鄉約四，擇誠實農民充之，承領耕種。 又吐魯番為南北樞紐，應安置內地民戶，戶領地五十畝，農田以水利為首務。 此次開渠，自龍口至黑山頭，地勢高低，碎石夾沙，渠身易淤，酌定經久修治章程。 」並如所請行。 自是回疆南路凡墾田六十餘萬畝。
Quan Qing, whose style was Xiaoting, was of the Yehe Nara clan, a Manchu bannerman of the Plain White Banner and son of Minister Na Qing'an. In Daoguang 9 he became a jinshi, entered the Hanlin Academy, was appointed a compiler, and rose to expositor. In the palace examination he ranked second class and was promoted to reader-in-waiting. He rose through the posts of junior tutor, grand tutor, and director of the Court of Judicial Review. He was demoted one rank for missing court attendance. In year 21 he was made a first-rank guardsman, posted as commandant of the Gucheng detachment, then transferred to minister resident at Karashahr. He was recalled but had not yet left when frontier reclamation began; Ili General Buyantai asked that Quan Qing stay to survey the land with Lin Zexu. In year 25 he reached Yarkand and memorialized: "The He'erhan district is fertile; the Khalamuzhash canal can irrigate it. Barkul is also a strategic point on the frontier; reclamation was approved in Daoguang 12, but much land remains unplanted—civilian settlers should be placed there first to make it a strong garrison." The court approved and implemented the proposal. Earlier Quan Qing had reported wasteland around Karashahr, Korla, and the northern foothills suitable for over ten thousand mu of cultivation; Minister Resident Chang Qing was ordered to organize the project. He then surveyed again with Lin Zexu and memorialized: "At Korla a middle canal should be opened south of the main canal to water newly reclaimed land, with two branch canals. At the northern foothills the Kaidu River intake should be widened and a new main canal opened parallel to the old one; then four more branch canals and one drainage canal." He also wrote: "Yilirike lies west of the Toksun courier station in Turfan; the land is level and fertile—locals call it the 'plate-soil gobi.' West of it lies the 'sand-and-stone gobi,' where the Great and Small Alahun streams join into one river. Water should be led from west to east, a main canal cut, and many branches opened for irrigation. Southwest of Yilirike a mountain route serves Mongol traffic; reclaimed plots lie near Manka. East and west are divided under the motto 'long life, abundant harvests'; each side gets a chief and deputy household head and four village officers—honest farmers chosen to organize cultivation. Turfan is the north-south hub; inland settlers should be placed there, fifty mu per household, with irrigation the first priority for farming. The new canal from intake to Heishantou runs over uneven ground with gravel and sand and silts easily—lasting maintenance rules should be established. All proposals were approved and implemented. From then on over six hundred thousand mu were reclaimed on the southern frontier route.
11
回京，擢內閣學士，兼正紅旗漢軍副都統。 歷刑部、吏部、戶部、倉場侍郎。 咸豐四年，擢工部尚書，兼正紅旗漢軍都統。 七年，調兵部。 九年，命赴天津驗收漕糧。 時英兵犯大沽，僧格林沁擊卻之。 全慶疏陳兵事，略謂：「敵軍戰敗之後，不進不退，心實叵測。 竊恐別有舉動，未必從此就撫而去。 我之精銳，盡萃大沽，旁無應援，後無擁護。 雙港之旅，已調前敵； 津門之備，但資土練； 北塘一帶，又頗空虛。 應請速簡重臣，發勁旅，嚴近畿海口之備，為僧格林沁之援，令廣東義勇搗香港以牽其援兵，登州水師合旅順以截其歸路，然後國威可振，撫局可成。 」疏入，被嘉納。 調吏部尚書。
Back in the capital he was promoted to grand secretary of the Grand Secretariat and made vice commander-in-chief of the Chinese Plain Red Banner. He served as vice minister of Punishments, Personnel, Revenue, and granary affairs in turn. In Xianfeng 4 he was promoted to minister of Works and commander-in-chief of the Chinese Plain Red Banner. In year 7 he was transferred to the Ministry of War. In year 9 he was ordered to Tianjin to inspect tribute grain. British forces attacked Dagu at the time, and Sengge Rinchen drove them back. Quan Qing memorialized on military affairs, writing in part: "After their defeat the enemy neither advances nor withdraws—their intentions are hard to read. I fear they have other designs and may not simply accept terms and leave. Our best troops are massed at Dagu with no flank support and no reserves behind them. The Shuangang garrison has already been moved to the front; Tianjin's defenses rely only on local militia; and the Beitang sector is again largely undefended. A senior minister should be chosen at once, elite troops sent, and defenses strengthened at the capital's sea ports to support Sengge Rinchen; Guangdong volunteers should strike Hong Kong to pin down reinforcements, and the Dengzhou fleet join Lüshun to cut the enemy's retreat—only then can prestige be restored and peace secured." The memorial was well received. He was made minister of Personnel.
12
十年，授內大臣，兼翰林院掌院學士。 十一年，充總管內務府大臣。 同治元年，追論大學士柏葰科場之獄原讞未允，全慶坐附和定讞，鐫四級，降授大理寺卿。 歷內閣學士、工部侍郎、左都御史。 五年，授禮部尚書，調刑部。 十一年，協辦大學士，兼翰林院掌院學士。 十二年，典順天鄉試，以中式舉人徐景春試卷疵謬，鐫二級去職。
In year 10 he was appointed imperial chamberlain and chancellor of the Hanlin Academy. In year 11 he was appointed superintendent of the Imperial Household. In Tongzhi 1, when Baizhan's examination scandal was reopened and the original verdict questioned, Quan Qing was punished for endorsing it, demoted four ranks, and made director of the Court of Judicial Review. He served as grand secretary, vice minister of Works, and left censor-in-chief in turn. In year 5 he was made minister of Rites, then transferred to Punishments. In year 11 he was made associate grand secretary and chancellor of the Hanlin Academy. In year 12 he oversaw the Shuntian provincial examination; the papers of successful candidate Xu Jingchun were found defective, and Quan Qing was demoted two ranks and dismissed.
13
全慶易攵歷清要，累掌文衡，更閱四朝，雖屢黜，尋即錄用。 光緒元年，授內閣學士。 复歷禮部侍郎、左都御史、刑部尚書、協辦大學士。 五年，鄉舉重逢，加太子少保。 六年，拜體仁閣大學士。 七年，致仕，食全俸。 八年，卒，晉贈太子太保，祀賢良祠，諡文恪。
Quan Qing had served in high office, repeatedly presided over examinations, and spanned four reigns; though often demoted, he was soon reappointed. In Guangxu 1 he was appointed grand secretary of the Grand Secretariat. He again served as vice minister of Rites, left censor-in-chief, minister of Punishments, and associate grand secretary. In year 5, on the jubilee of his provincial examination, he received the additional title of Junior Tutor of the Heir Apparent. In year 6 he was appointed grand secretary of the Tiren Pavilion. In year 7 he retired on full pension. In year 8 he died; he was posthumously made Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent, enshrined in the Shrine of Worthies, and given the posthumous title Wenkai.
14
論曰：自道光以來，科場請託，習為故常，寒門才士，為之抑遏。 柏葰立朝正直，且所不免，其罹大辟也，出於肅順等之構陷。 然自此司文衡者懍懍畏法，科場清肅，曆三十年，至光緒中始漸弛，弊竇复滋，終未至如前此之甚者，實文宗用重典之效，足以挽回風氣也。 麟魁、瑞常、全慶皆起家文學，洊陟綸扉，其建白猶有可紀焉。
The appraisal observes: Since the Daoguang era, examination favoritism had become routine, and talented men from humble families were shut out. Baizhan was upright in office and not without fault, yet his execution stemmed from framing by Sushun and his faction. Yet from then on examination officials feared the law; the halls stayed clean for thirty years, and though abuses returned in the Guangxu era they never matched the old excess—truly the effect of Wenzong's stern example in restoring public morals. Lin Kui, Rui Chang, and Quan Qing all rose through literary careers to the highest councils, and their policy memorials still deserve notice.
