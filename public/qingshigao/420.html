1
韓超，字南溪，直隸昌黎人。 道光十四年，副貢。 二十二年，天津治海防，超詣軍門獻策，事平，獎敘州判。 尋以府經歷揀發貴州，歷署三角屯州同、獨山知州。 獨山多盜，號難治。 超募勇訓練，用土民為鄉導，擒其渠。 胡林翼守黎平，深倚重之，言之巡撫蔣霨遠，超由是知名。
Han Chao, whose courtesy name was Nanxi, came from Changli in Zhili. In 1834 he qualified as a secondary tribute graduate. In 1842, while Tianjin was building up its coastal defenses, Chao presented plans at army headquarters; after the crisis passed he received a commendation and an acting post as prefectural judge. He was soon selected as a prefectural clerk and dispatched to Guizhou, where he served in turn as acting subprefect of Sanjiaotun and magistrate of Dushan. Dushan was rife with bandits and had a reputation as a hard district to govern. Chao raised and drilled a militia, employed locals as guides, and seized the bandit chiefs. Hu Linyi, then holding Liping, came to depend on him deeply and recommended him to Governor Jiang Yuanyuan, and Chao's name spread from that time.
2
咸豐元年，烏沙苗倡亂，超從林翼進剿，馳風雪中，先後斬獲數百人，餘黨悉平。 論功，以知縣用。 二年，署清江通判。 知黔將亂，捐俸豢勇士八十人，練成勁卒。 四年，獨山土匪結粵匪內犯，超率兵練迎擊，分軍出賊後，攻其不備，擒賊首楊元保，复深入廣西南丹州境，擊諸匪平之，加同知銜，賜花翎。 桐梓匪楊鳳竄永寧，合黔西匪王三扎巴連陷數城，圍遵義，超馳至，敗賊南關，陣斬王三扎巴，立解城圍。 复追敗諸葛章司河，擒楊鳳斬之，餘黨盡殲，擢知府。 五年，苗亂蔓延，超馳援台拱，解黃平、平越圍； 轉戰至施秉、鎮遠，賊塹山斷道，以阻官軍。 超以孤軍馳突其間，大小數十戰，補石阡知府。
In 1851 the Wusha Miao rebelled; Chao marched with Linyi to suppress them, fighting through blizzards until he had killed or captured hundreds and the last rebels were subdued. For his service he was nominated for appointment as a county magistrate. The following year he served as acting subprefect of Qingjiang. Foreseeing turmoil in Guizhou, he spent his own salary to keep eighty fighting men and drilled them into a hardened corps. In 1854 local bandits in Dushan allied with Cantonese rebels in a joint assault; Chao led regulars and militia to meet them, sent a detachment around the enemy rear for a surprise attack, captured the chief Yang Yuanbao, then pushed into Nanzhou prefecture in Guangxi and cleared the bandits there, earning an acting subprefect's rank and the peacock feather. Yang Feng of Tongzi fled to Yongning and, with the Qianxi rebel Wang Sanzhaba, took several towns and encircled Zunyi; Chao hurried up, routed the enemy at the south gate, killed Wang Sanzhaba on the field, and relieved the city at once. He pursued them again at Zhugezhang Sihe, took Yang Feng and beheaded him, and wiped out the survivors, for which he was promoted to prefect. In 1855, as the Miao revolt widened, he rushed to Taigong and broke the sieges of Huangping and Pingyue; he fought on toward Shibing and Zhenyuan, where the rebels had dug in on the heights and severed the roads to stop the imperial columns. Chao drove a solitary column through their lines in dozens of engagements and was appointed prefect of Shiqian.
3
超性剛直，有膽略，每與上官爭執是非，上官責以勦賊而靳其餉，飢師轉戰，往往求協助於鄰省。 四川總督駱秉章、湖北巡撫胡林翼交章論薦，侍郎王茂廕亦疏薦之，詔下巡撫問狀，以道員記名。 九年，授貴州糧儲道。 時苗、教各匪連陷諸郡縣，駐軍卭水汛，扼其中，使苗、教不得合，且遏其下竄湖南之路。 賊出全力撲之，超約楚軍夾擊，賊大潰。 剿思州響鼓坪，施秉土地坪，鎮遠金鼎𡉫、鋒嚴𡉫、唐家營𡉫諸賊巢，擒賊目張東山、歐光義等，鎮遠所屬皆平。
By nature he was blunt and principled, bold and resourceful; he frequently argued policy with his superiors, who ordered him to fight rebels while stinting his supplies, so his half-starved men campaigned on and he often had to beg help from neighboring provinces. Governors Luo Bingzhang of Sichuan and Hu Linyi of Hubei both filed joint recommendations, Vice Minister Wang Maoyin also recommended him, the throne ordered the provincial governor to report on his record, and he was registered for promotion to circuit intendant. In 1859 he was made Grain Intendant of Guizhou. Miao and religious rebels had by then overrun one district after another; he held the Qingshui post in the middle to keep the two rebel streams apart and to choke off their path south into Hunan. The rebels hurled their full force at him; Chao coordinated a converging strike with Hunan troops and broke them completely. He stormed rebel strongholds at Xiangguping in Sizhou, Tudiping in Shibing, and the Jinding, Fengyan, and Tangjiaying stockades in Zhenyuan, took chiefs such as Zhang Dongshan and Ou Guangyi, and brought the whole Zhenyuan region under control.
4
民團舊以十戶養一壯丁，超因其意稍變通之，官募士而民輸糧； 又籍叛產分授降眾、流人，以田代餉； 行之二年，得兵三千人。 自軍興，協餉不至，地方官吏爭抽取釐金以為補助。 超建議釐金統一，一抽之後，不復再抽，商無滯累，餉用差給。 十年，命幫辦貴州剿匪事。 十一年，署按察使。 提督田興恕疏陳超前後戰功，加布政使銜，賜號武勇巴圖魯。 詔予二品頂戴，​​署貴州巡撫。 田興恕方以欽差大臣督辦軍務，超久在行間，亦以肅清全黔為己任。
Under the old militia rule ten households supported one fighting man; Chao adapted the idea so that the state enlisted troops while civilians paid grain; he also parceled out confiscated rebel land to surrendered rebels and refugees in lieu of rations; and within two years had raised three thousand men. After the war began, cooperative subsidies never came, and local officials vied to levy likin transit taxes to make up the gap. Chao urged a single standardized likin rate—one levy only, no repeat exactions—so trade would flow and pay would be roughly met. In 1860 he was assigned to help direct the Guizhou pacification. The next year he served as acting provincial judicial commissioner. Provincial commander Tian Xingshu reported Chao's campaigns in detail; he received the rank of provincial treasurer and the title Brave Martial Batulu. The court granted him a second-rank cap button and made him acting governor of Guizhou. Tian Xingshu was then Imperial Commissioner in charge of operations; Chao, who had spent years in the field, likewise made clearing the whole province his personal charge.
5
同治元年，田興恕罷，乃命超辦理防剿事宜。 時尚大坪、玉華山兩處為賊巢，遵義、安順、思南、大定、銅仁、石阡諸府所在皆賊，五月，回匪陷興義，雲南叛回潰勇擾境，粵匪亦由川竄至正陽、廟堂並桐梓、松坎諸地。 超令總兵吳安康進剿，用內應夜縱火攻破賊巢，擒匪首倪老帽斬之，出難民二千餘人。 六月，閔家場踞賊糾集苗、教諸黨偪江口，天柱匪首亦糾合土匪攻陷縣城，分股竄湖南晃州廳、高寨，陷卭水、青谿兩城，謀截楚軍糧道。 超令總兵羅孝連、道員趙國澍進攻安順仲匪，夷其壘，擒斬賊酋韋登鳳等。 尚大坪賊复約苗、教分掠江內，超令孝連斷其歸路，國澍等馳軍迎擊，復令副將趙德元出冷水河、梯子岩進襲尚大坪，立破之，卬水汛城同時克復。 進平玉華山賊巢，攻拔瓦寨，复天柱縣城，特詔嘉獎。 道員鄧爾巽、總兵李有恆，破王家苗寨、夾馬洞諸賊巢，獲其酋李玉榮等。 黃、白號，教匪竄遵義，知府李德莪擊破之於三台山，奪五里坎諸隘口。 副將周宏順進攻石阡，毀老王𡉫賊巢，諸𡉫就撫。
In 1862, when Tian Xingshu was removed, Chao was put in charge of defense and pacification. Rebel bases still held Shangdaping and Yuhuashan; bandits overran Zunyi, Anshun, Sinan, Dading, Tongren, Shiqian, and neighboring prefectures; in the fifth month Hui rebels took Xingyi, fugitive Yunnan Hui troops raided the frontier, and Cantonese rebels slipped in from Sichuan toward Zhengyang, Miaotang, Tongzi, Songkan, and the like. Chao sent Brigadier Wu Ankang against them; with inside help the troops fired the camp at night, stormed the lair, seized the chief Ni Laomao and executed him, and freed more than two thousand civilians. In the sixth month rebels at Minjiachang mustered Miao and religious allies against Jiangkou; a Tianzhu chief joined hill bandits to storm the county town, sent columns into Hunan's Huangzhou and Gaozhai, took Qingshui and Qingxi, and tried to sever the Hunan army's grain route. Chao sent Brigadier Luo Xiaolian and Circuit Intendant Zhao Guoshu against the Zhong rebels of Anshun, razed their forts, and captured and beheaded chiefs such as Wei Dengfeng. Shangdaping rebels again allied with Miao and sectarians to loot the inner river districts; Chao had Xiaolian block their retreat while Guoshu met them head-on and Zhao Deyuan struck from Lengshuihe and Tiziyan; Shangdaping fell at once and the Qingshui post was retaken the same day. He moved on to crush the Yuhuashan nest, took Wazhai, and restored Tianzhu county—a deed singled out for imperial praise. Circuit Intendant Deng Erxun and Brigadier Li Youheng overran the Wangjia Miao and Jiamadong strongholds and took chiefs such as Li Yurong. Yellow- and White-Sect rebels broke into Zunyi; Prefect Li De'e routed them on Santaishan and recaptured the Wulikan passes. Vice Brigadier Zhou Hongshun marched on Shiqian, burned the Laowangzhai nest, and one stockade after another surrendered.
6
石阡、銅仁苗匪攻毀鎮遠營壘，卭水戍軍亦潰，遂南掠松桃，北攻天柱。 湖南援師至，賊始引去。 詔斥超專恃援軍，有負疆寄。 雲南方議撫回，巡撫徐之銘諮會停剿，而回匪益恣，竄陷安南、興義，分擾郎岱、永寧、歸化，詔原其誤信撫議，免議處。 石達開自川回竄，分三路，一走遵義，一走黔西，一走桐梓。 遣沈宏富、李有恆、余祖凱擊之。 田興恕以教案獲譴去官，黔軍益單。 二年，乞病回籍。 光緒四年，卒於家，年七十有九。 詔念前功，賜卹，諡果靖。 貴州請建專祠，並附祀胡林翼祠。
Miao rebels in Shiqian and Tongren smashed the Zhenyuan camps, the Qingshui garrison broke, and the enemy swept south into Songtao and north against Tianzhu. Only when Hunan reinforcements came did the rebels pull back. The throne rebuked Chao for depending too much on outside aid and neglecting his frontier mandate. Yunnan was debating a conciliatory policy toward the Hui; Governor Xu Zhiming agreed to suspend fighting, yet the Hui grew bolder, seized Annan and Xingyi, and raided Langdai, Yongning, and Guihua; the court forgave Chao's misplaced faith in negotiation and dropped the charges. Shi Dakai doubled back from Sichuan in three columns toward Zunyi, Qianxi, and Tongzi. Chao sent Shen Hongfu, Li Youheng, and Yu Zukai to meet them. Tian Xingshu was disgraced and dismissed over the missionary affair, leaving the Guizhou forces still more understrength. In 1863 he resigned on grounds of illness and went home. He died at home in 1878, in his seventy-ninth year. The court remembered his service, granted mourning honors, and posthumously titled him Guojing. Guizhou petitioned for a shrine in his honor and for his tablet to be placed in Hu Linyi's temple as well.
7
田興恕，字忠普，湖南鎮筸人。 年十六，充行伍，隸鎮筸鎮標。 咸豐二年，從守長沙。 賊屯湘江西岸，軍中募敢死士夜驚賊營，興恕請行，夜浮小舟往，潛燔賊營，賊騎數百追之，泅水免。 巡撫駱秉章奇之，委充哨官。 五年，從克郴州。 六年，領五百人號虎威營。 從蕭啟江援江西，克萬載、袁州。 七年，戰上高英岡嶺，深入被圍，左手受創，亡馬，步戰，他將馳救，得免。 是役以少擊眾，斃賊千數。 進攻臨江，掘地道轟城，先登，再被創，賊死拒未下。 援賊大至，啟江議暫退，興恕不可，曰：「兵在精不在多，原為前鋒。 」率所部直貫賊陣，賊張左右翼圍之，後軍望見興恕旗指東麾西，賊皆披靡，夾擊，賊大敗竄走，遂復臨江。 八年，克崇仁、樂安、宜黃、南豐，積功至副將，加總兵銜，賜號尚勇、摯勇兩巴圖魯。
Tian Xingshu, courtesy name Zhongpu, was from Zhenjiang in Hunan. At sixteen he joined the army under the Zhenjiang garrison standard. In 1852 he took part in the defense of Changsha. Rebels held the west bank of the Xiang; the garrison sought volunteers for a night raid, and Xingshu went alone in a skiff, burned the enemy camp, outswam hundreds of pursuers, and got away. Governor Luo Bingzhang was impressed and made him a platoon officer. In 1855 he helped take Chenzhou. In 1856 he commanded five hundred men in the Tiger Might Battalion. He marched with Xiao Qijiang to relieve Jiangxi and captured Wanzai and Yuanzhou. In 1857 at Shanggao Yinggangling he pushed too far, was surrounded, took a wound in the left hand, lost his horse, fought on foot until another officer rode in and pulled him out. In that fight his small force killed rebels by the thousand. At Linjiang his men mined the walls; he led the escalade, was wounded again, and the defenders held so fiercely that the city still did not fall. Enemy reinforcements poured in; Qijiang urged a pullback, but Xingshu refused, saying, "Soldiers win by skill, not numbers—I am here to lead the van. " He drove his men straight through the rebel line; when the enemy tried to envelop him, the rear columns saw his banner wheel east and west, the rebels broke, a converging attack crushed them, and Linjiang was retaken. In 1858 he took Chongren, Le'an, Yihuang, and Nanfeng, rose to vice brigadier and then brigadier, and received the titles Valiant and Steadfast Batulu.
8
貴州苗、教匪熾，黎平府被圍久。 興恕奉檄赴援，至即攻破賊營，連戰三日而圍解，進克古州、永從，署古州鎮總兵。 九年，石達開圍寶慶，興恕率四千五百人赴援，扼九鞏橋，無日不戰，歷月餘，糧藥將罄，選死士欲以一戰決勝負，會李續宜援軍至，內外夾擊，毀附城營三，連日攻下，勢如破竹。 達開竄廣西，遂移軍靖州防黔邊，命署貴州提督，督辦貴州軍務，增軍盈二萬。 十年，道銅仁，取印江，分軍略思南、石阡，進克貓貓山賊巢。
Miao and sectarian rebels raged in Guizhou, and Liping had been under siege for a long time. Ordered to the rescue, he smashed the rebel camps as soon as he arrived, fought three days straight to lift the siege, then took Guzhou and Yongcong and served as commander of the Guzhou garrison. In 1859 Shi Dakai besieged Baoxing; Xingshu brought forty-five hundred men to Jiugong Bridge and fought daily for over a month until supplies ran low; he picked shock troops for a decisive battle just as Li Xuyi's reinforcements came, and together they tore down three camps by the walls and swept the rest away. Dakai fled into Guangxi; Xingshu shifted to Jingzhou to watch the Guizhou frontier, was made acting provincial commander, took charge of Guizhou operations, and expanded his force past twenty thousand. In 1860, marching through Tongren, he seized Yinjiang, sent columns through Sinan and Shiqian, and stormed the Maomaoshan rebel nest.
9
石達開由廣西入貴州，連陷數縣，省城大震。 巡撫劉源灝趣赴援，興恕奏言：「黔省上游道路分歧，賊若以一軍擾黔，一軍入蜀，道遠兵單，斷難兼顧。 已檄韓超防鎮遠，沈宏富守湄潭，劉義方進松桃，臣駐石阡，居中調度。 賊如上竄，則親會川軍以攻之； 窺楚，即馳還靖州。 」時興恕已實授提督，詔授欽差大臣，命援省城。 師至，部署省防，督軍赴定番迎剿，賊棄城而走。
Shi Dakai entered Guizhou from Guangxi, took county after county, and panic spread in the capital. Governor Liu Yuanhao pressed him to hurry; Xingshu replied that upstream Guizhou's roads forked everywhere—if rebels tied down Guizhou while another column entered Sichuan, distance and thin numbers would make both fronts impossible. He had already posted Han Chao at Zhenyuan, Shen Hongfu at Meitan, and Liu Yifang toward Songtao, while he himself held Shiqian to coordinate. If they moved upriver he would join Sichuan forces in person; if they threatened Hunan he would ride back to Jingzhou. By then he had been confirmed as provincial commander; the court named him Imperial Commissioner and ordered him to save the capital. On arrival he organized the capital's defenses, led troops out to Dingfan, and the rebels abandoned the city and ran.
10
十一年，兼署巡撫。 時回、仲、苗、教諸匪分擾，上下游幾無完土。 興恕分兵援剿，戰屢捷。 招撫匪首唐天佑、賈福保、陳大六、柳天成等，克復歸化、荔波、定番、廣順、獨山諸城，疏通驛路，軍威漸振。 興恕年甫二十有四，驟膺疆寄，恃功而驕，又不諳文法，左右用事，屢被論劾，乃罷兼職，以韓超代之。
In 1861 he also served as acting governor. Hui, Zhong, Miao, and sectarian rebels then harried the province in every quarter until scarcely a district was untouched. Xingshu split his forces for joint campaigns and won battle after battle. He induced chiefs such as Tang Tianyou, Jia Fubao, Chen Daliu, and Liu Tiancheng to submit, retook Guihua, Libo, Dingfan, Guangshun, Dushan, and other towns, reopened the post roads, and slowly restored the army's standing. Only twenty-four, he had suddenly been given a frontier command; flush with success he turned arrogant, ignorant of civil procedure, and let favorites rule for him until repeated impeachments cost him the governorship, which Han Chao took over.
11
同治元年，罷欽差大臣。 會法國教士文乃爾傳教入黔，因事齟齬，興恕惡其倔強，殺之，坐褫職，赴四川聽候查辦。 經遵義旺超，值雲貴總督勞崇光為賊所困。 興恕驟馬衝入，大呼：「田某在此！ 」賊驚潰，翼崇光出。 尋論罪遣戍新疆，行至甘肅，總督左宗棠奏請留防秦州。 十二年，釋歸。 光緒三年，卒於家。
In 1862 he lost the Imperial Commissionership. When the French missionary Wén Nai'er entered Guizhou and a dispute turned ugly, Xingshu, angered by his defiance, had him killed; he was stripped of office and ordered to Sichuan to await trial. On the road through Zunyi at Wangchao he found Governor-General Lao Chongguang trapped by rebels. Xingshu spurred straight in and shouted, "Tian is here! " The rebels scattered in panic and he escorted Lao out. He was soon condemned to exile in Xinjiang; in Gansu, Governor-General Zuo Zongtang pleaded to keep him for defense at Qinzhou. In 1873 he was pardoned and sent home. He died at home in 1877.
12
曾璧光，字樞垣，四川洪雅人。 道光三十年進士，選庶吉士，授編修，記名御史。 入直上書房，授恭親王奕訢、醇郡王奕枻讀。 咸豐九年，出為貴州鎮遠知府。 同治元年，署貴東道。 二年，剿平銅仁踞賊蕭文魁，賜花翎。 雲貴總督勞崇光薦其才，迭署糧道、按察使、布政使。
Ceng Biguang, courtesy name Shuyuan, was from Hongya in Sichuan. In 1850 he earned his jinshi, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, became a compiler, and was marked for appointment as a censor. He joined the Upper Study Hall and tutored Prince Gong Yixin and Prince Chun Yixuan. In 1859 he left the capital to serve as prefect of Zhenyuan in Guizhou. In 1862 he acted as intendant of Guizhou East Circuit. In 1863 he crushed the Tongren rebels under Xiao Wenkui and received the peacock feather. Governor-General Lao Chongguang praised his ability, and he served in turn as acting grain intendant, judicial commissioner, and provincial treasurer.
13
六年，予二品頂戴，​​署貴州巡撫。 七年，實授。 貴州地瘠亂久，北境接四川，東境接湖南，軍事悉倚鄰援，​​本省餉既艱窘，將多驕蹇。 總兵林自清劾罷後，戕興義縣令，率所部萬人擾川境。 八年，璧光密遣提督陳希祥擒斬之，令吳宗蘭剿青山餘匪，克普安、安南。 時席寶田軍已由東路進規台拱，省城附近諸匪糅雜，出沒無常。 九年，周達武調任貴州提督，率川軍至貴陽，漸次勘定。 自軍興鄉試久停，至是年始補行，人心益定。 與達武議增兵扼要駐守，令道員蹇訚破遵義賊，擒其酋吳三； 令提督劉士奇克都勻，殪其酋吳章。
In 1867 he received a second-rank cap button and was made acting governor of Guizhou. The following year his appointment was confirmed. Guizhou was poor soil and had been at war for years; its north touched Sichuan and its east Hunan, so every campaign leaned on neighbors, while provincial pay was exhausted and many commanders were arrogant and unruly. After Brigadier Lin Ziqing was cashiered, he murdered the Xingyi magistrate and led ten thousand men to raid the Sichuan frontier. In 1869 Biguang secretly sent Provincial Commander Chen Xixiang to seize and behead Lin, had Wu Zonglan clear the Qingshan remnants, and recovered Pu'an and Annan. Xi Baotian was already pushing east toward Taigong, while mixed rebel bands around the capital raided without pattern. In 1870 Zhou Dawu became Guizhou commander, brought Sichuan troops to Guiyang, and step by step brought order. Provincial exams had been suspended since the war; they were finally held again that year, and public morale steadied further. With Dawu he agreed to reinforce key posts, sent Circuit Intendant Jian Yin against the Zunyi rebels, and took their chief Wu San; he sent Provincial Commander Liu Shiji to take Duyun and kill its chief Wu Zhang.
14
十年，令提督鍾有思等進剿上游，克永寧、威寧，下游諸軍擒悍賊潘得洪，收復八寨等城。 又收復上江、下江、三腳各城，平上游鎮寧、歸化賊巢，殪永城踞賊侯大五，斬郎岱金家硐踞賊金大七，盤江北岸肅清。 又破畢節、威寧諸匪，清八寨、三角餘賊，毀其巢。 令總兵何世華擊斬安南賊酋潘么，進克貞豐，西路悉平。 十一年，周達武率所部會楚軍定苗疆，詔嘉調度有方，予優敘。
In 1871 he sent Zhong Yousi upriver to take Yongning and Weining, while downstream columns seized the fierce Pan Dehong and retook Bazhai and neighboring towns. He also retook Shangjiang, Xiajiang, and Sanjiao, cleared rebel nests at Zhenning and Guihua, killed the Yongcheng chief Hou Dawu and the Langdai strongman Jin Daqi, and swept the north bank of the Pan. He smashed the Bijie and Weining rebels, mopped up Bazhai and Sanjiao survivors, and burned their lairs. Brigadier He Shihua killed the Annan chief Pan Yao, took Zhenfeng, and the whole western front was quiet. In 1872 Zhou Dawu and Hunan allies finished the Miao frontier; the court praised his coordination and gave him preferential honors.
15
十二年，會滇軍克新城老巢，全省肅清，加太子少保、頭品頂戴，予雲騎尉世職。 尋新城防軍索餉譁變，匪首何玉亭攻新城，遣其黨黎正關攻興義，分軍馳剿，捕誅其渠，事旋定。 光緒元年，卒於官，追贈太子太保，依總督例賜卹，諡文誠。 四川、貴州請建專祠。
In 1873 Yunnan troops helped take the Xincheng nest, the province was declared clear, and Biguang received Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent, a first-rank button, and a hereditary yunqiwei rank. Soon the Xincheng garrison mutinied for pay; chief He Yuting stormed Xincheng while Li Zhengguan hit Xingyi; columns rushed to suppress them, the leaders were taken and killed, and order returned. He died in office in 1875; the court posthumously made him Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, granted governor-level mourning honors, and titled him Wencheng. Sichuan and Guizhou petitioned for shrines in his honor.
16
席寶田，字研薌，湖南東安人。 諸生。 咸豐二年，率鄉團殺賊，复縣城，獎敘訓導。 六年，劉長佑援江西，招參軍事，遂從轉戰，積功累擢同知直隸州。 九年，石達開由廣西犯湖南，寶田從解寶慶圍，擢知府。 十年，駱秉章令募千人號精毅營，防湖南邊。 廣東賊犯郴州、桂陽，擊走之。 同治元年，石達開復由廣西入境，連敗之於黔陽，克來鳳，以道員記名，加按察使銜。
Xi Baotian, courtesy name Yanxiang, came from Dong'an in Hunan. He held the licentiate degree. In 1852 he led local militia against bandits, retook the county seat, and earned an acting post as director of studies. In 1856 Liu Changyou marched to Jiangxi, took him on staff, and through successive campaigns he rose to subprefect of a Zhili prefecture. In 1859, when Shi Dakai invaded Hunan from Guangxi, Baotian helped lift the siege of Baoxing and was promoted to prefect. In 1860 Luo Bingzhang had him raise a thousand-man Elite Steadfast Battalion for the Hunan frontier. When Cantonese rebels struck Chenzhou and Guiyang, he beat them back. In 1862 Shi Dakai again came from Guangxi; Baotian routed him at Qianyang, took Laifeng, was marked for circuit intendant, and received the rank of provincial judicial commissioner.
17
二年，粵匪黃文金大舉犯江西，命提督江忠義赴援，寶田副之，戰饒州桃溪渡，大破之； 又迭破之於湖口、洋塘、石門、青山橋，賊引去，趨池州，圍青陽。 寶田襲石嶺，破賊卡，分軍遮其前，命水陸夾擊，文金遁走，遂解青陽圍，累功賜號業鏗額巴圖魯，加布政使銜。 江忠義卒於軍，寶田代領其眾，留防江西。 三年，李世賢、黃文金復合犯江西，將以遙掣江寧之師，寶田逆擊白沙關，奪梘橋要隘，鈔擊於大濟關、泥嶺關，賊竄山谷，復金谿，以按察使記名，授雲南按察使。
In 1863 Huang Wenjin's Cantonese rebels swept into Jiangxi; Jiang Zhongyi was sent to meet them with Baotian as deputy; at Raozhou Taoxi Ford they won a crushing victory; then beat him again at Hukou, Yangtang, Shimen, and Qingshan Bridge until the rebels withdrew toward Chizhou and besieged Qingyang. Baotian stormed Shiling, smashed the rebel post, blocked their front, ordered a combined land-and-water strike, and Wenjin fled; Qingyang was relieved, and he earned the title Yekenge Batulu and provincial treasurer's rank. When Jiang Zhongyi died in camp, Baotian took over his troops and stayed to hold Jiangxi. In 1864 Li Shixian and Huang Wenjin again joined to raid Jiangxi and threaten Nanjing from afar; Baotian met them at Baishaguan, took Ji Bridge pass, ambushed them at Dajiguan and Niliguan, drove them into the hills, retook Jinx, was marked for provincial judicial commissioner, and was appointed to Yunnan.
18
時楊岳斌初至江西督辦軍務，檄寶田援南豐，坐遷延被劾，降知府，留軍。 會大軍克江寧，群賊擁洪秀全子福瑱逸出，由開化犯玉山，走瀘溪，寶田邀擊於新城，進至石城楊家牌，擒洪仁玕、洪仁政、黃文英等。 福瑱匿山谷中，捕得之，檻送南昌，伏誅。 詔复寶田原官，予雲騎尉世職，賜黃馬褂，授貴州按察使。 時餘賊汪海洋等走廣東，四年，寶田自平遠邀擊，降萬餘人，又扼鐵石嶺，降者二萬，諸軍合擊於嘉應州，全數蕩平。 論功，江西軍以寶田為第一，詔以布政使記名，遇缺題奏。 軍事既定，請回籍終養，允之。
Yang Yuebin had just arrived to direct Jiangxi operations and ordered Baotian to Nanfeng; impeached for delay, he was demoted to prefect but kept with the army. When Nanjing fell, rebels escorted Hong Xiuquan's son Fuzhen out through Kaihua into Yushan and on to Luxi; Baotian caught them at Xincheng and at Shicheng Yangjiapai took Hong Rengan, Hong Renzheng, Huang Wenying, and others. Fuzhen was found hiding in the hills, caged to Nanchang, and executed. The court restored his former rank, gave him a hereditary yunqiwei, the yellow riding jacket, and the Guizhou judicial commissionership. Remnants such as Wang Haiyang fled to Guangdong; in 1865 Baotian from Pingyuan cut them off and accepted more than ten thousand surrenders, then at Tieshiling took twenty thousand more until a joint strike at Jiaying wiped them out. Jiangxi ranked him first in merit; the throne marked him for provincial treasurer at the next vacancy. After the fighting ended he asked to go home and mourn his mother, and the request was granted.
19
貴州苗、教諸匪構亂十有餘年，東路素倚湖南援軍，自粵匪平後，議大舉剿平。 先是授兆琛為貴州布政使，偕總兵周洪印率師往，積歲無功。 李元度圍荊竹園，亦久不下。 巡撫李瀚章、劉昆先後劾罷兆琛、洪印，元度亦鐫級，薦起寶田招集舊部萬人入貴州，總統東路諸軍。
Miao and religious rebels had troubled Guizhou for more than a decade, the east depending on Hunan aid; once the Cantonese rebels were gone, the court planned a full pacification. Zhao Chen had earlier been sent as provincial treasurer with Brigadier Zhou Hongyin, but years passed without success. Li Yuandu besieged Jingzhuyuan just as long without result. Governors Li Hanzhang and Liu Kun cashiered Zhao Chen and Hongyin, demoted Yuandu, and brought Baotian back to lead ten thousand veterans into Guizhou as commander of the eastern front.
20
六年冬，進軍石阡，荊竹園為教匪老巢，寶田審視地勢高峻，匪砦環列，惟北面平夷可掩入。 七年元旦，進攻，部將黃元果先登，諸將肉薄壘下，一日平十八砦，克荊竹園，擒斬匪首蕭桂盛、何瑞堂，其旁三十六砦相繼攻下。 捷聞，被珍賚。 夏，進規寨頭。 寨頭為苗疆門戶，諸苗帑賄資糧所萃，連拔東西三屯，陣斬苗酋桂金保，破援賊張臭迷，攻下台笠、丁耙塘諸砦，遂克寨頭。 分軍克天柱，斬其酋陳大六。
That winter he marched on Shiqian; Jingzhuyuan was the sectarians' main nest on steep ground ringed with forts, with only the northern slope low enough for a surprise approach. On New Year's Day 1868 he attacked; Huang Yuanguo led the escalade, officers fought at the walls, eighteen forts fell in a day, Jingzhuyuan was taken, chiefs Xiao Guisheng and He Ruitang were captured and killed, and thirty-six neighboring forts followed. Victory brought imperial rewards. That summer he turned toward Zhaotou. Zhaotou guarded the Miao frontier and held their tribute stores; he took the eastern and western Santun, killed chief Gui Jinbao in battle, routed Zhang Choumi's relief column, stormed Taizhai and Dingbatang, and seized Zhaotou. A detachment took Tianzhu and beheaded its chief Chen Daliu.
21
會丁繼母憂，回籍治喪，提督榮維善暫領其眾，尋詔奪情趣赴​​軍，進攻台拱。 台拱苗最強，踞清江、鎮遠二城為犄角。 寶田請增兵萬人，按察使黃潤昌、道員鄧子垣領之出晃州為北路，寶田自當南路，令榮維善用雕剿法，轉戰山谷間，破諸苗砦，漸近鎮遠。 潤昌、子垣由思州進攻鎮遠府城，克之。 八年二月，維善連破董敖、公鵝兩隘，遂克清江廳城。 兩軍合趨黃飄，山地狹峻，人行頂趾相接，遇伏。 維善軍疾行先出險，潤昌軍誤以為陷伏中，爭道相擠，為賊所乘，潤昌、子垣皆戰歿。 維善聞變，率二百人馳救，被圍，為苗所擒，遇害。 於是苗氛复熾。
When his stepmother died he went home to mourn; Rong Weishan held his command until an edict cut mourning short and sent him against Taigong. The Taigong Miao were the strongest, using Qingjiang and Zhenyuan as paired strongpoints. Baotian asked for ten thousand reinforcements; Huang Runchang and Deng Ziyuan led the northern column from Huangzhou while he took the south and had Rong Weishan harry Miao forts through the valleys toward Zhenyuan. Runchang and Ziyuan marched from Sizhou and took Zhenyuan city. In the second month of 1869 Weishan broke the Dong'ao and Gong'e passes and took Qingjiang. Both columns pushed toward Huangpiao on trails so narrow men marched toe to heel—and walked into an ambush. Weishan's men sprinted clear first; Runchang's troops thought themselves trapped, jammed the path, and were cut down—Runchang and Ziyuan both fell. Weishan heard, rushed two hundred men to help, was surrounded, captured by the Miao, and killed. Miao resistance flared anew.
22
張秀眉犯巴冶，寶田親督軍擊走之，進克稿米，令龔繼昌、蘇元春破苗寨，擊走張臭迷等，分軍守鎮遠、施秉。 時以寶田軍苦戰年餘，尚未深入，議罷其軍，劉昆仍主專任，復增兵萬人，分三路進。 九年，會攻施洞，克之。 苗走九股河，白洗苗來援，擊敗之。 進攻台拱，破革夷諸砦，薄台拱城下，苗棄城走，克之，加頭品頂戴。 進軍九股河，分別剿撫，凡平黑苗砦二百餘所。 雞講、丹江苗皆請歸化。 十年，進攻凱里，一鼓而下。 苗潰走雷公山，麕眾六七萬人，黃茅嶺、雷口坪、九眼塘、燕子窩諸寨皆絕險，寶田督諸軍冒暑入山，合擊張臭迷，斬馘三萬，燔其廬舍，剿洗一空。 駐軍施洞口，寶田遽病風痺，乞假醫療，命部將龔繼昌、蘇元春、唐本有、謝蘭階分統其軍。 軍事進止機宜，仍禀命於寶田。
Zhang Xiumei struck Baye; Baotian led the counterattack, took Gaomi, had Gong Jichang and Su Yuanchun storm Miao forts, routed Zhang Choumi, and garrisoned Zhenyuan and Shibing. After a year of hard fighting without deep penetration, some urged disbanding his force; Liu Kun kept him in sole command, added ten thousand men, and opened three routes. In 1870 they jointly stormed and took Shidong. The Miao fled to Jiugu River; Baixi Miao reinforcements were beaten off. Pressing on Taigong they stormed the Geyi forts, reached the walls, the Miao abandoned the city, Taigong fell, and Baotian received a first-rank button. At Jiugu River they alternately fought and accepted surrender until more than two hundred Black Miao forts were cleared. The Jijiang and Danjiang Miao all came in. In 1871 they took Kaili at the first rush. The Miao broke toward Leigong Mountain with sixty or seventy thousand in the fastnesses of Huangmaoling, Leikouping, Jiuyantang, and Yanziwu; Baotian led columns into the summer heat, crushed Zhang Choumi, killed or captured thirty thousand, burned their villages, and scoured the range bare. Camped at Shidongkou he was struck by paralytic stroke, took medical leave, and left Gong Jichang, Su Yuanchun, Tang Benyou, and Xie Lanjie to command his divisions. They still reported every move to Baotian for approval.
23
十一年，三路進兵，凱北以北悉定。 合攻烏雅坡，諸酋皆在，以長圍困之。 迭戰，斬九大白、岩大五於陣，先後降者數万。 四月，擒張秀眉、楊大六、金大五等，檻送長沙，伏誅。 張臭迷先逸，捕得戮之。 諸酋或降或斬，無脫者。 苗疆平，詔晉寶田騎都尉世職，家居養疴。 光緒十二年，詔以寶田前擒洪福瑱功，命曾國荃繪其像以進。 十五年，卒，贈太子少保，優恤。 原籍及江西、貴州建專祠。
In 1872 the three columns advanced and secured everything north of Kaibei. At Wuyapo they besieged the chiefs together in a long encirclement. Battle after battle killed Jiubai and Yan Dawu on the field; tens of thousands surrendered in waves. In the fourth month they took Zhang Xiumei, Yang Daliu, Jin Dawu, and others, caged them to Changsha, and executed them. Zhang Choumi, who had escaped earlier, was caught and put to death. Every chief either submitted or was killed; none escaped. The Miao frontier was settled; the court raised his hereditary cavalry commandant rank and he retired home to convalesce. In 1886, honoring his capture of Hong Fuzhen, the court had Zeng Guoquan paint his portrait for presentation. He died in 1889, posthumously made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent with special mourning honors. Shrines were raised in his home county, Jiangxi, and Guizhou.
24
論曰：貴州之匪，總名有六：曰苗匪、教匪，曰黃號、白號，其小者曰槓匪、仲匪，其他濫練、游勇、逆回、悍夷，揭竿踵起，不可悉數。 始於咸豐四年，無兵無餉，不能制也。 韓超有辦賊之才，久屈下僚，事權不屬。 田興恕入黔，兵威始振，超亦驟起，未久相繼去。 張亮基治黔數年，亦僅補苴。 中原大定，曾國籓乃議以湖南兵力、餉力為平黔根本，而駱秉章亦令劉岳昭剿黔北以保川邊。 後專倚席寶田，戡定苗疆。 自周達武以川兵、川餉濟黔之不及，曾璧光賴之以竟全功。 蓋閱二十年而後大定。 古云：「蠻夷之人，先叛後服。 」蓋以地勢使然。 然使若韓超者早膺疆寄，其延禍或不致如是之甚。 弭亂之道，在得其人，用人之道，必盡其才，固古今不爽者爾。
The historian remarks: Guizhou rebels fell under six broad heads—Miao and religious sectarians, Yellow and White Sects, plus lesser gang and Zhong bands—while mutinous militia, drifters, Hui rebels, and fierce tribesmen rose in such numbers they cannot all be listed. From 1854 onward the province lacked troops and money and could not contain them. Han Chao had real talent for suppression yet long remained a subordinate without full command. When Tian Xingshu entered Guizhou the army's standing revived and Chao rose with him, yet both soon departed. Zhang Liangji governed Guizhou for years and achieved little more than stopgaps. Once the heartland was calm, Zeng Guofan made Hunan's troops and treasury the basis for pacifying Guizhou, while Luo Bingzhang sent Liu Yuezhao to secure the north and protect Sichuan. Later the work fell chiefly to Xi Baotian, who settled the Miao frontier. When Zhou Dawu brought Sichuan men and money to fill Guizhou's gaps, Ceng Biguang leaned on that partnership to finish the task. Only after more than twenty years was the province truly at peace. The ancients said, "Barbarian peoples rebel first and submit afterward. "—and terrain makes it so. Yet had a man like Han Chao been given frontier command early, the catastrophe might never have grown so grave. Pacification depends on the right man; employment must use talent to the full—a truth that has never changed in any age.
