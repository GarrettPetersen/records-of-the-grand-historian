Training
4
清代訓練軍士，綜京、外水陸各營，咸有成規。 而歷朝整軍經武之諭，則隨時訓練，因地制宜。 茲分述之。
Under the Qing, drill for troops was standardized across every garrison in the capital and in the provinces, on land and at sea. Imperial orders through the dynasties to keep the forces in fighting trim, however, called for training whenever circumstances required and for methods suited to each region. Both kinds are described below.
5
其定期訓練者，為領侍衛府三旗親軍訓練之制，鑲黃旗、正黃旗、正白旗每月分期習騎射二次，習步射四次。 八旗驍騎營訓練之制，每月分期習射六次，都統以下各官親督之。 春秋二季，擐甲習步射，由本旗定期。 擐甲習騎射，由部臣定期。 春月分操二次，合操一次，秋月合操二次，預奏操期。 仲春孟秋，登城操習，兵部稽察之。 歲以為常。 八旗漢兵訓練之制，於春秋月試砲於盧溝穚，各旗咸出砲十位演放，五日而畢。 越三年，鳥槍營兵與砲兵合演槍砲藤牌於盧溝橋。 其春秋季常操，四旗合操四次，八旗合操二次，初冬則分遣各旗演習步圍。 前鋒營訓練之制，月習步射六次，春秋擐甲習騎射二次，左右翼各分前鋒之半，兼習鳥槍，月習十次，均由統領督率。 每年秋季，前鋒統領會同護軍統領奏聞，率所屬兵演習步圍二、三次。 護軍營訓練之制，月習步射六次，春秋擐甲習騎射二次，與前鋒同。 圓明園八旗護軍營訓練之制，月習步射六次，春秋習騎射，兼習鳥槍。 步軍營及巡捕營訓練之制，八旗步軍習步射，城門驍騎習鳥槍，均以春秋操演。 內九門，外七門，咸設砲位。 每屆三年，隨同八旗兵運砲至盧溝橋演放。 巡捕營參將、游擊，月考其屬之弓矢，守備等各練其汛兵。 春秋兼習鳥槍，與城門驍騎同。 內府三旗訓練之制，月習步射六次，春秋擐甲習射二次，立冬後，內府護軍及尚虞處執事等演習步圍，別選三旗護軍習馬射各技。 火器營訓練之制，月習步射六次，騎射六次，馬上技藝六次。 統轄鳥槍砲兵護軍驍騎各官，按日於本旗考驗。 至合操之日，八旗分左右翼列陣，環施槍砲。 秋季至盧溝橋演砲五日。 健銳營訓練之制，月習雲梯鳥槍各藝六次，騎射步射鞭刀等藝六次，餘日於本期習槍箭。 值駐蹕圓明園，左右翼各以舟演習水戰。 旗營校閱之時，自七月開操至次年四月，設教場於九門外，將軍、都統、副都統掌校閱騎射槍砲之事，第其優劣，以為賞罰。 春秋合操，與京營同。
Scheduled drill included the system for the three Imperial Bodyguard banners: the Bordered Yellow, Plain Yellow, and Plain White banners each held mounted archery twice a month and foot archery four times, on a rotating schedule. For the Eight Banners' vanguard cavalry, archery was practiced six times monthly on a rotating basis, with every officer from the banner commander down supervising in person. Each spring and autumn, foot archery in armor was scheduled by the banner itself. Mounted archery in armor was scheduled by officials of the Board of War. Spring brought two separate drills and one joint exercise; autumn brought two joint exercises, with dates submitted beforehand. Mid-spring and early autumn saw wall-top drills, inspected by the Board of War. These were standing annual exercises. Han-banner troops tested artillery at the Marco Polo Bridge each spring and autumn: every banner sent ten guns for a five-day firing exercise. Every third year, musketeers and gunners staged combined firearms, artillery, and rattan-shield drills at the Marco Polo Bridge. Routine spring and autumn maneuvers called for four-banner joint drills four times and full eight-banner drills twice; in early winter each banner held its own foot battue. The vanguard brigade practiced foot archery six times a month and armored mounted archery twice each spring and autumn; each wing took half the corps, also drilling muskets ten times monthly, all under the brigade commander's direction. Each autumn the vanguard and guards commanders reported to court and led two or three foot battues with their men. The guards brigade matched the vanguard: six monthly foot-archery sessions and two armored mounted-archery drills each spring and autumn. Palace guards at the Yuanmingyuan practiced foot archery six times monthly, mounted archery each spring and autumn, and musketry besides. Foot soldiers practiced foot archery and gate cavalry practiced muskets, all on spring and autumn schedules, under the foot-soldier and patrol brigades. Cannon emplacements were set at all nine inner and seven outer gates of the capital. Every three years they hauled guns to the Marco Polo Bridge with the banner forces for live fire. Patrol brigadiers and regimental commanders held monthly archery tests for their men; garrison commanders drilled the troops at their posts. They added musket drill each spring and autumn, as the gate cavalry did. The three inner-palace banners drilled foot archery six times monthly and armored shooting twice each spring and autumn; after winter began, palace guards and hunt-office staff held foot battues, while selected guards from all three banners trained in mounted archery and related skills. The firearms brigade practiced foot archery, mounted archery, and horsemanship six times each per month. Officers over musketeers, gunners, guards, and vanguard cavalry inspected their men daily within the banner. On joint-drill days the eight banners formed left and right wings with muskets and cannon deployed around the line. Each autumn they spent five days firing artillery at the Marco Polo Bridge. The elite brigade drilled siege-ladder and musket skills six times monthly, plus mounted and foot archery, whip, and blade work six times; other days in the cycle were for firearms and archery. When the court stayed at the Yuanmingyuan, each wing rehearsed river fighting from boats. Banner garrisons drilled from the seventh month through the following April on grounds outside the Nine Gates; generals and banner commanders inspected archery, firearms, and artillery and ranked men for rewards or penalties. Spring and autumn joint maneuvers followed the same rules as the capital camps.
6
陸路綠旗營訓練之制，總督所屬為督標兵，巡撫所屬為撫標兵，提督所屬為提標兵，總兵所屬為鎮標兵。 每歲秋季霜降日，先期各營將弁肅伍赴教場，設軍幕。 屆時軍士擐甲列陣，中軍建大纛於場中，統兵大臣於將台上傳令合操，中軍揚旗麾眾，台下舉砲三，軍中鳴角伐鼓，步騎甲士列隊行陣，施放火槍，連環無間，如京營之制。 若長矛、藤牌、扁刀、短刀之屬，各因其地之宜，以教士卒，咸有成法。 閱竟，試材官將士騎射技勇，申明賞罰，犒軍，釋甲歸伍。 漕運總督標、河道總督標訓練之制，咸與京營同，各營將弁率其所屬，按日督練。 八旗水師營訓練之制，每年春秋二季，將軍、都統、副都統督率官兵，分駕戰艦，奉天、福建、浙江、廣東水師，各赴海口，齊齊哈爾、墨爾根、江寧省水師，各赴江面，天津水師赴海口洋面。 每年自四月至八月，於潮平風順時，張帆起碇，列陣出洋，以次鳴砲操演，餘日各率所屬講習水務。 其綠旗水師營，有內河水師、江海水師，出洋會哨，信候各省不同。 每歲春秋之季，乘艦列陣，揚帆駛風，鳴角發砲，操演咸如軍律。
Green Standard land forces were organized by command: governor-general's standard, governor's standard, provincial commander's standard, and regional commander's standard. On Frost's Descent each autumn, officers marshaled their units early to the drill ground and pitched command tents. On the day itself, troops formed in armor with a great banner at center; the commanding general on the review stand ordered the joint exercise; signal flags directed the lines; three guns fired from below; horns and drums sounded as infantry and cavalry maneuvered and fired muskets in rolling volleys, following capital-garrison practice. Long spears, rattan shields, broad swords, and short swords were taught according to local needs, each with fixed methods. After the review they tested officers and men in archery and martial skill, announced rewards and penalties, feasted the troops, and sent them back to quarters out of armor. Grain-transport and river-conservancy standards followed capital-garrison drill; officers led daily training for their commands. Each spring and autumn, banner naval commanders led their crews to sea in warships: Fengtian, Fujian, Zhejiang, and Guangdong fleets to harbor mouths; Qiqihar, Mergen, and Jiangning fleets to the rivers; Tianjin's fleet to the open coast off the port. From the fourth through the eighth month, when tides and winds allowed, they sailed out in formation and fired guns in sequence; on other days commanders instructed their crews in seamanship. Green Standard naval forces split between inland river and coastal fleets; patrol schedules and signal timings varied by province. Each spring and autumn they put to sea in formation, sails full, horns sounding and guns firing, all drill per military law.
7
其隨時訓練者，天聰七年，太宗始舉大閱之典。 八旗護軍、漢軍馬步、滿洲步軍咸集。 分八旗為左右翼，漢軍、滿洲步兵為二營，四方環立，前設紅衣砲三十位。 上擐甲乘馬，諸貝勒率護軍如對嚴敵，親軍為後盾。 傳令聞砲而進，聞蒙古角聲而退。 次漢軍馬步，次滿洲步軍，進攻砲軍。 大閱禮成。 嚴申退後之令。 崇德八年，大閱於瀋陽北郊，前列漢軍砲手，次滿洲步兵、蒙古步兵，次騎兵，次守城應援兵，次守城砲兵，綿亙二十里，聞砲合戰。 上親臨簡閱，步伐止齊，軍容整肅。
Ad hoc training began in Tiancong 7 (1633), when Taizong inaugurated the grand military review. Banner guards, Chinese cavalry and infantry, and Manchu foot troops all mustered. The eight banners formed left and right wings; Chinese and Manchu infantry made two camps in a square ring, with thirty red-coated guns to the front. The ruler rode in armor; princes led the guards as if facing a real enemy, with the sovereign's guard in reserve. Orders ran: advance at the gun signal, withdraw at the Mongol horn. Then Chinese horse and foot, then Manchu infantry, advanced against the gun line. The grand review concluded. Orders on orderly withdrawal were read out in the strictest terms. In Chongde 8 (1643) a grand review on Shenyang's north outskirts lined up Chinese gunners first, then Manchu and Mongol infantry, then cavalry, then garrison relief and artillery—a twenty-li front that engaged at the gun signal. The ruler inspected in person; ranks moved in step and presented a disciplined front.
8
順治七年，誡各將領勿以太平而忘武備，弓馬務造精良。 十一年，定每年閱操賞銀之制。 定騎射各兵分期演習之制。 定督、撫、提、鎮獎賞優等弁兵之制。
In Shunzhi 7 (1650) commanders were warned not to neglect arms in peacetime and to keep bows and mounts in top condition. In year 11 the annual silver rewards for drill review were standardized. Mounted-archery units were put on a rotating drill schedule. Rules were set for provincial commanders to reward outstanding officers and men.
9
康熙十一年，令各省營伍，須武職大員巡察。 嗣後各鎮臣以巡察之期上聞，不得擾累各營。 十二年，以漢軍不能騎射者甚多，每旗宜增練火器。 尋議八旗漢軍驍騎，每佐領下，增鳥槍兵十八名。 十六年，令各營於安營駐宿之道，馳騁奔走之勞，皆須習練，不得僅拘操演成法，直省提、鎮，每歲督選標兵行圍，以習勞苦。 十九年，定每年演放紅衣大砲之期。 二十八年，定演砲之制。 每年九月朔，八旗各運大砲十位至盧溝橋西，設槍營、砲營各一，都統率參領、佐領、散秩官、驍騎砲手咸往。 工部修砲車，治火藥。 日演百出，及進步連環槍砲。 越十日開操。 太常寺奏簡都統承祭，兵部奏簡兵部大臣驗操。 各旗演砲十齣，記中的之數。 即於砲場合隊操演，嚴鼓而進，鳴金而止，槍砲均演九進十連環，鳴螺收陣還營。 三十年，定春操之制。 每旗出砲十位，火器營兵千五百名。 漢軍每旗出砲十位，鳥槍兵千五百名。 每佐領下之護軍鳥槍兵、護軍驍騎，每參領下之散秩官、驍騎校，及前鋒參領、護軍參領、侍衛等，更番以從。 既成列，演放鳥槍，鳴螺進兵，至所指處，分兵殿後而歸。 五十年，定火器營合操陣式。 八旗砲兵、鳥槍兵，護軍驍騎，分立十六營。 中列鑲黃、正黃二旗，次六旗，按左右翼列隊，將台在中，兩翼各建令纛為表。 每旗鳥槍護軍在前，次砲兵，次鳥槍兵，次驍騎。 台下鳴海螺者三，以次整械結隊出營。 施號槍三，台下及陣內海螺遞鳴，乃開陣演槍砲九次至十次，砲與鳥槍連環無間。
In Kangxi 11 (1672) every provincial garrison was ordered inspected by senior military officers. Regional commanders thereafter reported inspection dates to court and were forbidden to burden the camps. In year 12, with many Han-banner men unable to ride and shoot, each banner was told to expand firearms drill. Soon each Han-banner vanguard company was authorized eighteen additional musketeers. In year 16 camps had to train on march routes and forced marches, not only parade-ground routines; provincial commanders yearly led standard troops on battue marches to harden them. In year 19 the annual schedule for firing the great red-coated guns was set. In year 28 artillery-drill regulations were codified. On the first of the ninth month each banner hauled ten great guns west of the Marco Polo Bridge, set up separate musket and artillery camps, and the banner commander brought company and platoon officers plus vanguard gunners. The Ministry of Works serviced gun carriages and prepared powder. Daily practice included a hundred rounds plus advancing rolling volleys of muskets and guns. After ten days the formal maneuvers began. The Court of Sacrifices nominated a banner commander to lead the rites; the Board of War nominated a minister to inspect the drill. Each banner fired ten gun rounds and recorded hits. On the gun ground they then maneuvered: advance at drum, halt at gong; muskets and cannon ran the nine-advance, ten-volley sequence; conch called the formation back to camp. In year 30 spring-drill regulations were established. Each banner fielded ten guns and 1,500 firearms-brigade troops. Each Han-banner contingent sent ten guns and 1,500 musketeers. Guard musketeers and cavalry under each company, unattached officers and vanguard lieutenants under each battalion, plus vanguard and guard battalion commanders and bodyguards, rotated in attendance. Once formed, they fired muskets, advanced at the conch to the mark, then split a rear guard and marched back. In year 50 the firearms brigade's joint-drill formation was standardized. Banner gunners, musketeers, and guard cavalry were organized into sixteen camps. The Bordered and Plain Yellow banners stood center, then the other six, in left and right wings; the command stand sat in the middle with a command flag on each wing. Each banner lined up guard musketeers first, then gunners, musketeers, and vanguard cavalry. Three conch blasts from below the stand sent each unit out of camp in order, weapons ready. Three signal guns and answering conches opened the line for nine or ten musket-and-cannon sequences in uninterrupted rolling fire.
10
雍正四年，改定盧溝橋演槍砲為三年一次，均演一月。 兵校等火藥器用，由工部預儲。 五年，以滿洲夙重騎射，不可專習鳥槍而廢弓矢，有馬上槍箭熟習者，勉以優等。 七年，以直隸營汛多演空槍，通飭直省將帥，令各營以鉛子演准。 八年，劉汝麟建議，漢軍應習步圍。 尋諭各旗兵於初冬行步圍，每旗行二、三次，統以各旗大臣，步行較獵，侍衛、打牲人等，一律學習。 九年，以八旗官兵未能精整，統兵各官，擇不堪騎射者，立為一營，稍優者，別立一營。 每營千人，勤加操練，化弱為強。 又以兵丁重在步行，凡八旗兵給限一年習步，以日行百四十里為率，優者賞之。 十年，以邊陲用兵，操演加勤，免各旗輪班值日，專習騎射長槍。 十二年，定八旗漢軍驍騎演習鳥槍之制。 春季二月為始，秋季八月為始，各習槍四十五日，本旗四翼仍合操二次。
In Yongzheng 4 (1726) Marco Polo Bridge firearms drill was set to once every three years for one month each. The Ministry of Works stocked powder and gear for troops in advance. In year 5, with Manchu tradition in mounted archery, troops must not drop the bow for muskets alone; men skilled in both mounted musket and bow were graded favorably. In year 7, with Zhili posts firing blanks, all provincial commanders were ordered to drill with live shot for accuracy. In year 8 Liu Rulin proposed that Han-banner troops should train in foot battues. Soon each banner was ordered to hold two or three foot battues in early winter under its minister; bodyguards and hunt staff had to take part. In year 9, with banner forces still slack, commanders sorted poor riders into one camp and middling ones into another. Each thousand-man camp drilled hard to turn weak troops strong. Foot marching was stressed: every banner soldier had one year to train, with 140 li per day as the benchmark; top performers were rewarded. In year 10 frontier war brought harder drill: rotating guard duty was suspended so troops could focus on mounted archery and the long spear. In year 12 Han-banner vanguard musket drill was codified. Drill began in the second month of spring and the eighth of autumn, forty-five days of muskets each, with two joint wing exercises per banner.

乾隆四年，定旗兵合操之制。 每年春季，本旗各營官兵，於本旗教場分操二次，八旗各營官兵，於鑲黃、正黃二旗教場合操一次。 至秋季合各營大操，其隊伍號令，旗纛器械，均遵大閱之制。 六年，議准八旗驍騎營步射由本旗定期，騎射由兵部定期。 八年，令八旗漢軍至盧溝橋演放槍砲，於九月朔為始，演放一月，簡都統大臣監視，日演十齣，兵部閱操之日，每旗各演百出，演畢，合操槍砲。 其金鼓號令，悉如大閱之制。 十年，以沿海水師，經大臣察閱，其操演多屬具文，未諳水務，通飭將軍、督、撫、提、鎮，實心訓練甄別。 十四年，以旗兵習練雲梯，隨征金川有功，凱旋後，別立健銳營，雲梯兵千名為一營，統以大臣，專練雲梯、鳥槍、馬步射及鞭刀等藝，並隨侍行圍。 又於昆明湖設趕繒船，以前鋒軍習水戰駕船駛風之技。 是年，莽阿納上言，整頓邊省營伍章程：一、步弓均改五力以上，一、馬射與步射一式，一、馬兵騎射宜槍箭二技，一、鳥槍專練準頭，一、槍兵兼習弓矢，一、定優劣賞罰，一、預儲軍械，以固邊陲。 十七年，定八旗漢軍藤牌兵之制，春季與旗兵一律操演，遇大閱及諸營合操，則守護砲位，入隊演習。 三十六年，令鳥槍兵宜遵定例，於演槍時，檢回鉛子，以勵勤能。 三十八年，定各營增演馬上四箭四槍之制。 三十九年，以金川用兵，京城之健銳、火器二營，功績最多，令各省綠營習鳥槍兵弁，悉仿火器營進步連環之法操練，不得虛演陣式。 尋定各營槍兵升補之序，以資鼓勵。 四十年，令健銳營兵月習槍十二日，定三等為賞罰。 四十三年，令各省習槍兵弁，仿京營火器操練之法，各總兵於巡閱時，有進步連環精熟者紀功。 四十四年，令各省綠營兵習射，以五矢中三為一等。 五十年，以綠營陣法，向習兩儀四象方圓等舊式，無裨實用，改仿京營陣式，由提督頒發各標鎮，如式教演。 各營每月定期合操，並演九進十連環之陣。 其堆撥應差兵丁，暇日一律練習。 又以各省巡撫標兵，向供給使，訓練甚稀，飭各撫臣實力整理。 其舊式之藤牌兵，均兼習鳥槍。 五十五年，令軍機大臣會同兵部，審定演放砲位步數及懲勸之例。
In Qianlong 4 (1739) joint banner-drill rules were set. Each spring every banner drilled separately twice on its own ground, then all eight banners joined once on the Bordered and Plain Yellow drill fields. Autumn brought a full-camp grand maneuver with formations, signals, flags, and gear per grand-review rules. In year 6 banner vanguard foot archery was scheduled by the banner, mounted archery by the Board of War. In year 8 Han-banner forces fired muskets and guns at the Marco Polo Bridge for the month starting the first of the ninth month under a supervising banner commander—ten rounds daily, a hundred per banner on Board review day, then a joint firearms exercise. Drums, gongs, and signals followed grand-review practice throughout. In year 10 inspections showed coastal fleets drilling mostly on paper and ignorant of seamanship; all naval commanders were ordered to train in earnest and weed out the unfit. In year 14 banner ladder troops distinguished themselves in the Jinchuan war; after victory the Elite Brigade was formed—1,000 ladder men per battalion under a minister, drilling ladders, muskets, archery, and blades, and joining imperial hunts. Rope-towed boats on Kunming Lake let the vanguard practice naval fighting, handling, and sailing. That year Mang'ana proposed frontier camp reforms: foot bows at five-strength minimum; unified mounted and foot archery; cavalry skilled in both musket and bow; muskets drilled for accuracy; gunners also trained in archery; clear reward and penalty rules; and advance arms stockpiles to hold the border. In year 17 Han-banner rattan-shield troops were regulated: spring drill with other banner units; at grand reviews they guarded guns and joined the line. In year 36 musketeers were told to follow regulations and collect spent balls after drill to reward diligence. In year 38 every camp added mounted drill: four arrows and four musket shots from horseback. In year 39 the capital's elite and firearms brigades led the Jinchuan war; every province's Green Standard musket officers had to drill the firearms brigade's advancing rolling volleys, not empty parade forms. Promotion rules for musketeers in each camp were soon set to encourage merit. In year 40 elite troops drilled muskets twelve days a month with a three-tier reward and penalty scale. In year 43 provincial musket officers copied capital firearms drill; regional commanders on inspection tours credited men who mastered advancing rolling fire. In year 44 Green Standard archery was graded: three hits in five arrows ranked first class. In year 50 obsolete Green Standard formations like Two Principles and Four Images were dropped for capital-garrison patterns issued by provincial commanders to every unit. Each camp held monthly joint drills including the nine-advance, ten-volley formation. Men on labor or courier duty also drilled on their off days. Governors' personal standards, long used as servants, rarely drilled; governors were ordered to put them in real order. Old-style rattan-shield units all added musket training. In year 55 the Grand Council and Board of War set artillery-range paces and reward-and-penalty rules.
12
嘉慶二年，罷水師冬令鳧水習藝，以恤兵艱。 四年，令水營兵丁一律兼習陸戰。 又令新疆屯田之兵，每營分半屯種，餘悉回營操練。 令各省督撫，修理營汛墩台。 督操將、備，加力振奮。 九年，令各統兵官習射以六力弓為度，習槍以迅速命中為度，申明教誡，力挽積習，不得養尊處優。 十一年，令德楞泰等兵丁，以十成之一兼習長矛，其制不得逾丈。
In Jiaqing 2 the navy's winter swimming drill was ended to spare the men's hardship. In year 4 all naval-camp troops were ordered to train in land fighting too. Xinjiang garrison-farmers were split: half farmed, the rest returned to camp for drill. Provincial governors were ordered to repair garrison posts and watchtowers. Drill supervisors among generals and garrison commanders were told to redouble effort. In year 9 commanders had to shoot with six-strength bows and fire muskets for speed and accuracy; they were warned to break bad habits and not live in ease. In year 11 one soldier in ten under Delengtai's command also trained with long spears no longer than one zhang.
13
道光元年，令各軍均習長矛步槍，不得專精馬槍。 是年，楊芳上言：「兵丁於練騎射槍矛之外，加以車騎合步連環三項，融結參合，日操一隊，以五隊更番演習，六日合操為一陣。 直隸額兵，抽練四成，得一萬五千三百餘人，成二百四十隊，按圖操演，以齊勇怯而節進退。 」允之。 二年，以廣東營伍廢弛，嚴飭撫臣，實力練習，不得多立章程。 四年，罷撤梅花車砲陣式，專習部頒九進連環陣式。 五年，允英和之請，以八旗圈馬四百匹，改撥巡捕營，令滿洲、蒙古馬兵演習騎射，春秋二季，步軍統領會同左右翼總兵簡閱，三年後親臨大閱，八年，令那彥成等回疆增設防兵，籌給餉糈，議定操兵章程，並於喀什噶爾防兵內，抽練二千名，伊犁滿兵亦勤習騎射，由參贊大臣及總兵督操。 十五年，以山西滿、漢營伍廢弛，嚴飭閱兵大臣嚴明甄別。 是年，常大淳上言，新疆、湖南、廣東、四川各營伍，日久生玩，滿營則奢靡自逸，漢營則糧額多虛。 由於拔補之循私，操演之不實，以國家養兵之資，為眾人雇役之用。 請飭將軍、督、撫，力除積習。 遇剿匪保案，不得冒濫，以勵戎行。 允之。 並令各州縣額設民壯，一律充補訓練。 十七年，令各省民壯，每月隨營操演，授以紀律，以輔兵力所不及。 十八年，令盛京滿洲兵各勤操務，遇行圍之時，不得有雇役情弊。 十九年，以四川各營，技疏膽怯，致夷匪日張，特簡大臣，督率鎮、道，親往校閱。 二十二年，令天津增兵六千餘人，飭各將、備率新舊兵丁，悉加練習，首火砲，次鳥槍刀矛，輔以馬隊。 遇警則各營聯合南北砲台。 命精能武員，專司稽察，講求方略。 二十六年，令各州縣民壯，隨營調考刀矛雜技。 三十年，令各督、撫、提、鎮，汰老弱冗濫之兵，抽練精壯，俾各營皆有選鋒勁旅。 不得以工匠僕役，虛占兵糧。
In Daoguang 1 every army had to train with long spears and foot muskets, not only mounted firearms. That year Yang Fang proposed adding chariot, cavalry, and foot rolling-fire drills to archery and firearms: one company daily, five companies rotating, with a full-array joint drill every sixth day. Forty percent of Zhili's roster—over 15,300 men in 240 companies—drilled by diagram to even out skill levels and coordinate movement. The proposal was approved. In year 2 Guangdong's slack garrisons brought a strict order to the governor to drill hard and stop piling up rules on paper. In year 4 the obsolete plum-blossom gun-carriage formation was dropped for the Board's nine-advance rolling-fire drill. In year 5 Yinghe won approval to transfer 400 banner horses to the patrol brigade for Manchu and Mongol cavalry archery, inspected each spring and autumn by the foot-soldier commander and wing generals, with an imperial grand review in three years. In year 8 Nayancheng expanded Western Region garrisons with rations and drill rules; 2,000 Kashgar troops and Ili Manchu cavalry drilled under the frontier commissioner and regional commander. In year 15 slack Manchu and Han garrisons in Shanxi brought strict orders to the reviewing minister to sort the ranks. That year Chang Dachun reported that Xinjiang, Hunan, Guangdong, and Sichuan garrisons had grown lax over time—Manchu camps luxurious, Han rolls padded with phantom men. Nepotism in promotions and sham drill turned state military pay into wages for private servants. He asked that generals and governors root out these entrenched abuses. Bandit-fighting commendations must not be inflated, to keep the army honest. The court approved. Every county's quota militia were ordered filled out and trained. In year 17 provincial militia drilled monthly with regular camps and learned discipline to fill gaps in regular strength. In year 18 Mukden Manchu troops were ordered to drill diligently and forbidden to send hired substitutes on imperial hunts. In year 19 Sichuan's timid, unskilled garrisons let frontier unrest spread; a minister was sent to lead commanders in person on inspection tours. In year 22 Tianjin added 6,000 troops; commanders trained all men with artillery first, then muskets and blades, supported by cavalry. In an alarm all camps would coordinate with the north and south forts. Skilled military officers were assigned to inspect and develop tactics. In year 26 county militia were tested with regular camps on blades, spears, and related skills. In year 30 provincial commanders were told to cut the old and weak, train the fit, and give every camp a picked strike force. Artisans and servants must not pad the rolls for rations.

咸豐元年，奕山等以伊犁及烏魯木齊二處滿洲營增練鳥槍，擬定考驗章程，並綠營一律辦理。 三年，綜各省綠營額兵共六十餘萬人，除徵調之兵，所餘存營者，汰弱留強，定期分練。 各省駐防旗兵亦如之。 五年，令健銳、火器、圓明園八旗營，及前鋒、護軍、八旗漢軍營，飭閱兵大臣核實校令，分別勸懲。 又令僧格林沁等增滿洲火器營操演陣式。 十一年，以盛京、吉林、黑龍江馬隊官兵，日就疲弱，飭將軍、副都統，無論在城在屯，一體挑練，可造者多方鼓勵，貧苦者酌量周恤，遇行圍兵數不足，以餘丁隨同操演。
In Xianfeng 1 Yishan set musket-drill exams for Manchu camps at Ili and Urumqi and extended the same rules to the Green Standard. In year 3 China's Green Standard rolls totaled over 600,000; troops not on campaign were to shed the weak, keep the strong, and drill on a rotation schedule. Provincial garrison banner troops followed the same policy. In year 5 the elite, firearms, Yuanmingyuan, vanguard, guards, and Han-banner camps were ordered inspected in fact with differentiated rewards and penalties. Senggelinqin was also told to expand Manchu firearms-brigade drill formations. In year 11 weakening cavalry in Mukden, Jilin, and Heilongjiang brought orders to drill all men in town or colony, encourage the promising, aid the poor, and use surplus males when hunt quotas fell short.

同治元年，以上海、寧波等海口官兵，延歐洲人訓練，令曾國籓、李鴻章、左宗棠等，酌選武員數十人，在上海、寧波習外國兵法，以副、參大員統之，學成之後，自行教練中國兵丁。 又以廣東、福建營伍久弛，飭耆齡、劉長佑等於旗、綠營營內，擇驍勇員弁，習外國兵法。 天津練軍亦如之。 其內地營兵，仍遵舊章，隨時訓練。 是年，令文煜等定京營綠旗兵槍隊砲車合陣之制。 四年，醇郡王等訓練神機營兵及練兵三萬餘人，操演漸著成效，綠營亦就整肅。 令仍隸醇郡王節制，督操閱兵大臣，一併閱看。 是年，令崇厚率洋槍隊千五百人赴畿南，飭天津鎮、蘆台鎮選擇標兵，增練新式洋槍。 六年，以丁寶楨所擬訓練馬隊章程十四條，飭特普欽於黑龍江所屬、富明阿于吉林所屬打牲人內，招募壯丁三千人，遵章速練馬隊，以剿捻匪。 曾經出師回旗之員，分起訓練，入關候調。 十年，曾國籓建議，用兵十餘年，綠營幾同虛設。 查閱江南營伍，約有四宗：曰經制綠營，曰新設水師，曰挑練新兵，曰留防勇營。 凡陸兵四十一營，水師十一營，新兵十一營，防勇十二營，兵數實存二萬四千餘人。 舊習宜改者，約有四端：一、兵丁應差與操演分為二事，應差以分塘分汛為額，操演以分營分哨為額。 一、綠營餉薄兵疲，宜仿新軍練軍之制，裁兵加餉。 一、舊用鳥槍土藥，不利戰陣，各營宜以次悉改洋槍。 一、水師不得仍沿馬兵、戰兵、守兵之名，各省水師，皆應籌造船之費，以船為家，但兼陸操，不得居陸，外海、內洋、里河水師，器械船隻，力求精整。 凡此皆事關全局，請特旨通行內外臣工，合議遵行。 是年，令長江水師，及外海、內洋、里河水師，均應專習槍砲，不得藉口演習弓矢，致開陸居之漸。 沿海兵輪水師，亦免習弓矢。 十二年，沈葆楨以各兵輪雖分駐各省，而操演徵調必應聲勢聯絡，請飭兵輪統領，躬歷各海口，隨時調操。 十三年，李鴻章以八旗、綠營兵，用弓矢刀矛抬槍鳥槍舊法訓練，固難制勝，即新練各軍，用洋槍者已少，用後膛槍及炸砲者更少，可靖內匪，而不可御外侮。 曾國籓曾擬以新械練兵，沿海七省，共練陸兵九萬人，沿江三省，共練三萬人，計年餉八百萬兩，總理衙門議以制勝之洋槍隊練習水戰，丁日昌議合各省練精兵十萬人，皆以費重未能遽行。 陸軍與水師規制各殊，訓練亦異，水師猶可陸戰，陸軍不能操舟。 請以現有陸營，一律選練洋槍，裁綠營疲弱之額，加新軍之餉，沿海防營，悉改後膛槍，于海岸要口，屯大支勁旅，專講操練及築壘諸事。 各海口修洋式沙土砲台，置十餘寸口大砲，擇良將勁兵練習，以命中及遠為度，以固海疆。
In Tongzhi 1 port troops at Shanghai and Ningbo were trained by Europeans; Zeng, Li, and Zuo selected dozens of officers to learn foreign tactics there under senior commanders, then train Chinese troops themselves. Guangdong and Fujian's long-neglected garrisons were told to pick brave officers from banner and Green Standard units to learn foreign tactics. Tianjin's training corps followed suit. Interior garrisons still followed the old rules for training as needed. That year Wenyu set capital Green Standard rules for combined musket and gun-carriage formations. In year 4 Prince Chun trained the Shenji brigade and 30,000 drilled troops with growing success; the Green Standard was also brought into line. They remained under Prince Chun; the drill and review minister inspected them jointly. That year Chonghou led 1,500 foreign-musket troops south of Beijing; Tianjin and Lutai were told to pick standard troops for new-style rifle drill. In year 6, under Ding Baozhen's fourteen cavalry-training articles, Tepuqin in Heilongjiang and Fuming'a in Jilin recruited 3,000 strong men from hunt communities for rapid cavalry training against the Nian. Banner officers back from campaign were re-mustered for training and held inside the passes pending orders. In year 10 Zeng Guofan reported that after a decade of war the Green Standard was nearly a dead letter. Jiangnan's forces fell into four types: regular Green Standard, new navy, picked new troops, and retained defense braves. In all: 41 land camps, 11 naval, 11 new-troop, and 12 defense-brave camps—about 24,000 men actually present. Four old habits needed reform: first, separate courier duty from drill—duty quotas by post, drill quotas by camp and platoon. Second, thin pay and worn-out men called for the new-army model: fewer troops, higher pay. Third, native muskets and powder were useless in battle; every camp should switch to foreign rifles in sequence. Fourth, navies should drop obsolete rank names, fund ships as home bases, drill on land but not live ashore, and keep open-sea, coastal, and river fleets fully equipped. These reforms touched the whole system; he asked for an imperial edict to all officials to deliberate and implement them. That year Yangzi and all coastal and river fleets were ordered to train only in firearms, not use archery as an excuse to move ashore. Coastal steam navies were also excused from archery drill. In year 12 Shen Baozhen urged the steam fleet commander to tour every port in person so scattered ships could drill and mobilize as one force. In year 13 Li Hongzhang argued that banner and Green Standard troops drilling with bows, blades, wall guns, and old muskets could not win; even new armies rarely had foreign rifles, breechloaders, or shells—enough for bandits, not foreign powers. Zeng had proposed 90,000 coastal and 30,000 riverine troops with new arms at eight million taels a year; the Zongli Yamen wanted a winning rifle corps for naval drill; Ding Richang wanted 100,000 elites nationwide—all stalled on cost. Army and navy had different rules and training; sailors could fight ashore, soldiers could not sail. He asked to retrain all land camps with foreign rifles, cut weak Green Standard rolls, raise pay like the new armies, arm coastal defenses with breechloaders, and post strong forces at key ports focused on drill and fortification. Every port should build Western-style earth forts with ten-inch guns, train picked officers and men for accuracy and range, and hold the coast.
16
光緒五年，李鴻章以德國陸軍步隊尤精，得力在每日林操，熟演料敵應變之法，夏秋大操，熟演露宿野戰攻守之法。 其法備於一哨，擴而充之，可營可軍。 前于海防營內，選游擊等七員，赴德國學習林操及迎敵、設伏、布陣、繪圖各法三年餘，學成回國。 乃於親軍營內，挑選哨隊，仿德國一哨之制，依法教練，漸次擴充。 九年，李鴻章始創設水師學堂於天津，習駕駛等藝。 十一年，張之洞酌定海防各營操練章程，舊式刀叉弓矢已無實用，改用新操，一練臥槍，一練過山砲隊，一練掘造地營，一練安放水雷，一練修築砲台，一練臨敵散隊，一練洋式火箭，一練安設行軍電線，一練疾步逾濠越嶺，一練夜戰，一練堅守地營及濬濠築牆一切工程。 是年，李鴻章以外洋留學生回華，於操法、陣法、電學、水雷、旱雷，均有心得，飭分赴各營教練弁兵，並設武備學堂。 十二年，張之洞以廣東省駐防營，於光緒六年，選甲兵千五百人，改練洋槍洋砲及陣法，乃裁汰旗營水師，附入步軍，編為兩翼，合陣操演。 飭製造局移解新式槍砲，增練砲隊。 十三年，李鴻章以北洋武備學堂學生，於砲台、營壘、馬隊、步隊、砲隊諸新法，咸有成就，飭令回營，轉相傳授。 是年，張之洞始於廣東設水師、陸師學堂，水師分管輪及駕駛攻戰二種，陸師分馬步、槍砲、營造三種，兼采各國之長。 二十年，張之洞之南洋水師學堂著有成效，加以獎勵。 又於江寧省設陸軍學堂，講求地理、測量、營壘諸術，馬、步、砲隊諸法。
In Guangxu 5 Li Hongzhang noted that German infantry excelled through daily woodland drill, enemy assessment, and summer-autumn field exercises in bivouac attack and defense. The method worked at platoon scale and could expand to battalion or army. Seven coastal-defense regimental commanders had studied in Germany for three years in woodland drill, ambush, deployment, and mapping, then returned. They then picked platoons in the personal guard to train on the German model and expand gradually. In year 9 Li Hongzhang founded a naval academy at Tianjin for navigation and related skills. In year 11 Zhang Zhidong set coastal drill rules replacing obsolete blades and bows with eleven new skills: prone shooting, mountain guns, fieldworks, mines, forts, skirmish lines, rockets, field telegraph, forced marches, night fighting, and siege engineering. That year Li Hongzhang sent returned overseas students—trained in drill, formations, electricity, and mines—to teach in the camps and opened a military academy. In year 12 Zhang Zhidong reported that Guangdong's garrison had retrained 1,500 men in foreign arms since Guangxu 6, cut the banner navy, attached it to infantry as two wings, and drilled joint formations. The arsenal was told to supply new guns and expand artillery drill. In year 13 Li Hongzhang sent Beiyang academy graduates skilled in forts, fieldworks, cavalry, infantry, and artillery back to camp to train others. That year Zhang Zhidong opened naval and army schools in Guangdong—naval in engineering and combat navigation, army in cavalry-infantry, firearms, and construction—drawing on foreign models. In year 20 Zhang Zhidong's Nanyang naval academy was rewarded for strong results. An army school was also opened in Jiangning for geography, surveying, fieldworks, and cavalry, infantry, and artillery methods.
17
二十一年，張之洞建議，舊營積弊太深。 人皆烏合，來去無恆，一弊也。 兵皆缺額，且充雜差，二弊也。 里居不確，良莠不分，三弊也。 攤派刻扣，四弊也。 新式槍砲，拋棄損壞，五弊也。 營壘工程，不知講求，六弊也。 營弁習尚奢華，七弊也。 若以洋將統之，期其額必足，人必壯，餉必裕，軍火必精，技藝必嫺，勇丁不供雜差，將領不得濫充，此七者練兵之必要。 所聘德國武將三十五人已來華，即仿德國營制，設步隊八營，二百五十人分為五哨，馬隊二營，一百八十騎分為三哨，砲隊二營，二百人分為四哨，工程隊一營百人，醫官、槍匠等咸備。 凡勇丁二千八百六十人，餉四十四萬兩。 俟操練有效，推廣加練，增至萬人。 以此軍洋將移練第二軍，俾次第以成勁旅。 是年，胡燏芬建議，新練各軍，宜用一律槍砲。 北洋先練五萬人為大軍，南洋練三萬人，廣東、湖北練二萬人，餘省萬人，操法軍械，務歸一律，以便徵調。 各省應一律設立武備學堂。
In year 21 Zhang Zhidong argued that old garrison abuses ran too deep. First, men were a shifting rabble with no steady roster. Second, rolls were short and men were used for odd jobs. Third, home addresses were uncertain and good and bad men were not separated. Fourth, levies and harsh deductions. Fifth, new rifles and guns were abandoned or ruined. Sixth, fieldworks were neglected. Seventh, officers lived in luxury. Under foreign officers, rolls would be full, men fit, pay adequate, arms modern, skills sharp, soldiers free of odd jobs, and commands not padded—the seven essentials of real training. Thirty-five German officers had arrived; the army copied German organization: eight infantry battalions of 250 men in five platoons each, two cavalry battalions of 180 in three platoons, two artillery battalions of 200 in four platoons, one 100-man engineer company, plus doctors and armorers. The force totaled 2,860 men at 440,000 taels in pay. Once drill proved effective, training would expand to 10,000 men. Those foreign instructors would then train a second army, building crack units in turn. That year Hu Yufen urged standardized rifles and guns for all new armies. Beiyang would train 50,000 first, Nanyang 30,000, Guangdong and Hubei 20,000 each, other provinces 10,000—with uniform drill and arms for mobilization. Every province should open a military academy.
18
二十二年，始以新法訓練海陸各軍。 各省設立學堂，同時舉辦。 是年，張之洞始裁撤湖北武防等三旗，改練洋操二營，工程隊一營，仿直隸武毅軍新練洋操章程，參用德國軍制，聘德國武員為教習，以開風氣。 是年，盛宣懷建議，全國綠營兵歲餉千餘萬，練勇歲餉亦千餘萬，凡八十餘萬人，徒耗財力，無裨實用，宜悉行裁撤。 共練新軍三十萬人，就各省情形輕重，定兵數多寡，徵募訓練，悉仿西法。 旋總理衙門以各省營伍，驟難盡裁，先就北洋新練兩軍，及江南自強軍、湖北洋操隊，切實教練。 俟裁兵節餉，次第推廣。 飭兩江、兩湖督臣，較准製造局槍砲畫一辦理。 又於武昌城設武備學堂，聘洋員教習。
In year 22 land and naval forces began training under the new system. Provinces opened academies at the same time. That year Zhang Zhidong cut three Hubei defense banners and formed two Western-drill battalions and one engineer company on the Zhili model with German instructors. That year Sheng Xuanhuai urged abolishing the Green Standard and drilled braves—over 800,000 men costing more than 20 million taels yearly with little return. Instead, train 300,000 new troops nationwide, sized by provincial need, recruited and drilled on Western lines. The Zongli Yamen replied that full cuts were impossible at once and ordered earnest training first for Beiyang's two new armies, Jiangnan's Self-Strengthening Army, and Hubei's Western-drill corps. Expansion would follow as cuts freed funds. Jiangnan and Huguang governors were told to standardize arsenal arms. A military academy was also opened in Wuchang with foreign instructors.
19
二十四年，令各省稽察缺額攤派之弊，嚴行革除。 至操練之法，宜不拘成格，儘力變通，飭督辦軍務王大臣議之。 尋以神機營、火器營、健銳營、武勝新隊，操演嫺熟，賞統兵大臣有差。 令滿、蒙、漢各軍驍騎營、兩翼前鋒、護軍營，五成改習洋操，五成改用洋槍，八旗漢軍砲隊營、藤牌營，一併改練，神機營汰弱留強，共練馬步兵萬人。 其陣法器械營制餉章，酌仿泰西兵制。 是年秋，上親詣團河及天津大閱新操。 又令各省增水師學堂學額，增造練船，習駕駛諸術。 二十五年，以北洋各軍訓練三年，飭統兵大臣取各種操法，繪圖貼說以聞。 步隊以起伏分合為主。 砲隊以攻堅挫銳為期。 馬隊以出奇馳驟為能。 工程隊以擴地利、備軍資為事。 以平時操練之法，備異日戰陣之需。 二十六年，鄧華熙於安徽省城設立武備學堂，習槍砲戰陣諸學。
In year 24 provinces were ordered to root out padded rolls and illegal levies. Drill methods should break old forms; the prince supervising military affairs was told to deliberate. Soon the Shenji, firearms, elite, and Wusheng units drilled well and their commanders were rewarded. Half of Manchu, Mongol, and Han vanguard and guard units would take Western drill, half foreign rifles; Han artillery and rattan-shield units retrained too; the Shenji brigade kept 10,000 fit horse and foot. Formations, arms, organization, and pay followed Western models where appropriate. That autumn the emperor reviewed the new drill in person at Tuanhe and Tianjin. Provinces were also told to expand naval schools, build training ships, and teach seamanship. In year 25, after three years of Beiyang training, commanders reported each drill method with diagrams and notes. Infantry stressed movement, dispersal, and reassembly. Artillery trained to breach defenses and break enemy elites. Cavalry specialized in surprise and rapid maneuver. Engineers focused on terrain and logistics. Peacetime drill was meant to ready troops for war. In year 26 Deng Huaxi opened a military academy in Anhui's capital for firearms and tactics.
20
二十七年，以各省制兵防勇，積弊甚深，飭將軍、督、撫，就原有各營，嚴行裁汰，精選若干營，分為常備、續備、巡警等軍，更定餉章，一律操習新式槍砲。 又令南北洋、湖北之武備學堂，山東之隨營學堂，酌量擴充，認真訓練。 是年，劉坤一、張之洞等，以二十年來，各省練習洋操，屢經整頓，而舊日將領，於新操多未諳習。 東西各國教將練兵要旨，約有十二：一曰教士以禮，使知有恥自重，一曰調護士卒起處飲食，一曰講明槍砲彈藥質性源流之法，一曰槍砲線路取准之法，一曰掘濠築壘避槍砲之法，一曰馬步砲各隊擇地借勢之法，一曰測量繪圖之法，一曰隊伍分合轉變之法，一曰守衛偵探之法，一曰行軍工程製造之法，一曰籌備行軍衣糧輜重之法，一曰行軍醫藥之法。 各疆臣均應選擇統領、營、哨各官，均切實研究。 練兵固亟，練將尤要。 數年以後，非武備學堂出身者，不得充將弁。 更請仿英、法之總營務處，日本之參謀部，於都城專設衙門，掌全國水陸兵制、餉章、地理繪圖、操練法式、儲備糧餉、轉運舟車、外交偵探等事。 平日之預籌，臨時之調度，悉以此官掌之。 兼采眾長，務求實用。 令內外臣工合議。 二十八年，設北洋行營將弁學堂，實演戰擊諸法。 此歷朝訓練之規也。
In year 27 deep abuses in provincial regulars and braves brought orders to cut ruthlessly, form standing, reserve, and patrol forces, revise pay, and drill all troops in modern arms. Beiyang, Nanyang, Hubei, and Shandong military schools were also expanded and run seriously. That year Liu Kunyi and Zhang Zhidong noted that after twenty years of Western drill reforms, many old officers still did not know the new methods. East and West agreed on twelve essentials for training officers: instill discipline and self-respect; care for soldiers' daily needs; teach ballistics; teach aiming; teach fieldworks; teach terrain use for horse, foot, and guns; teach surveying and mapping; teach maneuver; teach guard and reconnaissance; teach march engineering; teach logistics; teach field medicine. Every governor should pick brigade, battalion, and platoon officers to master these subjects. Training troops was urgent, but training officers mattered even more. Within a few years only military-academy graduates could hold command. They also asked for a capital war office on the British, French, and Japanese models to run organization, pay, mapping, drill, logistics, transport, and intelligence nationwide. That office would handle both peacetime planning and wartime deployment. It would combine foreign strengths for practical effect. Court and provincial officials were ordered to deliberate together. In year 28 Beiyang opened a field officers' school for live combat drill. Such were the training regulations of successive reigns.
