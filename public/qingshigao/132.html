4
防軍初皆召募，於八旗、綠營以外，別自成營，兵數多寡不定，分佈郡縣，遇寇警則隸於專征將帥，二百年間，調發徵戍，咸出於此。 若乾隆年台灣之役，乾、嘉間黔、楚征苗之役，嘉慶間川、陝教匪之役，道光年洋艘征撫之役，皆暫募勇營，事平旋撤。 故嘉慶七年，楚北初設提督，即以勇丁充補標兵，道光十七年，以練勇隸於鎮筸鎮標，二十三年，以防守海疆之水陸義勇三萬六千人仍遣回本籍，無防、練軍之名也。 道、咸間，粵匪事起，各省多募勇自衛，張國樑募潮州勇丁最多。 咸豐二年，命曾國籓治湖南練勇，定湘軍營哨之制，為防軍營制所昉。 迨國籓奉命東征，湘勇外益以淮勇，多至二百營。 左宗棠平西陲，所部楚軍亦百數十營。 軍事甫定，各省險要，悉以勇營留防，舊日綠營，遂同虛設。 綠營兵月餉不及防勇四分之一，升擢擁滯，咸辭兵就勇。 粵、捻既平，左宗棠諸臣建議，防營誠為勁旅，有事則兵不如勇，無事則分汛巡守，宜以制兵為練兵，而於直隸、江、淮南北扼要之處，留勇營屯駐，遂有防軍之稱。
Defense forces were originally raised entirely by recruitment. Operating outside the Eight Banners and the Green Standard Army, they formed independent camps of varying strength, posted across prefectures and counties. When bandit alarms broke out, they came under commanders appointed for specific campaigns. For two centuries, virtually every mobilization and frontier garrison assignment drew on these troops. Examples include the Taiwan campaign under Qianlong, the Qianlong–Jiaqing Miao campaigns in Guizhou and Huguang, the Jiaqing campaign against the Teaching Bandits in Sichuan and Shaanxi, and the Daoguang campaign against foreign ships—all of which relied on temporarily recruited brave camps that were disbanded as soon as peace returned. Thus in Jiaqing 7, when a provincial commander-in-chief was first appointed in northern Huguang, brave recruits were used to fill regular banner vacancies. In Daoguang 17 drilled braves were placed under the Zhen'ao garrison banner, and in Daoguang 23 the thirty-six thousand land and sea volunteers guarding the coast were sent home to their native districts. At this point the terms "defense army" and "trained army" did not yet exist. During the Daoguang and Xianfeng reigns, when the Guangdong rebellion broke out, provinces across the empire recruited braves for local defense. Zhang Guoliang raised the largest contingent of Chaozhou braves. In Xianfeng 2 Zeng Guofan was ordered to organize drilled braves in Hunan and established the camp-and-platoon system of the Xiang Army—the model from which later defense-force camp organization would derive. When Guofan was ordered east on campaign, Huai braves were added to the Xiang forces, bringing the total to as many as two hundred camps. When Zuo Zongtang pacified the western frontier, the Chu Army under his command likewise numbered well over a hundred camps. Once the fighting subsided, strategic points in every province were garrisoned by brave camps left on defense duty, and the old Green Standard Army became little more than a formality. Green Standard soldiers received monthly pay amounting to less than a quarter of what defense braves earned; promotion stalled, and men left regular service en masse to join the braves. After the Guangdong and Nian rebellions were suppressed, Zuo Zongtang and other officials argued that defense camps had proved themselves crack troops: in crisis regular soldiers could not match the braves, yet in peacetime the braves could be assigned to garrison patrols. Quota regulars should be retrained as drilled troops, while brave camps would remain garrisoned at strategic points in Zhili and along the Yangtze–Huai line—thus the term "defense forces" came into use.
5
練軍始自咸豐間，以勇營日多，屢令統兵大臣以勇補兵額，而以餘勇備緩急，尚無別練之師。 至同治元年，始令各疆吏以練勇人數口糧，悉數報部稽核。 是年於天津創練洋槍隊。 二年，以直隸額兵酌改練軍。 四年，兵部、戶部諸臣會議選練直隸六軍，始定練軍之名。 各省練軍乃踵行之。 練軍雖在額設制兵內選擇，而營哨餉章，悉准湘、淮軍制，與防軍同。 其綠營制兵，分佈列郡汛地，練軍則屯聚於通都重鎮，簡器械，勤訓練，以散為整，重在屯防要地，其用亦與防軍同，故練軍亦防軍也。
Trained armies date from the Xianfeng period. As brave camps multiplied, commanders were repeatedly ordered to use braves to fill regular troop quotas while keeping surplus braves in reserve for emergencies; there was as yet no separately drilled standing force. Not until Tongzhi 1 were frontier governors ordered to report the full head count and rations of drilled braves to the ministry for audit. That year a foreign-rifle drill corps was established at Tianjin. In Tongzhi 2 quota troops in Zhili were selectively reorganized as trained armies. In Tongzhi 4 officials of the Board of War and Board of Revenue jointly deliberated on selecting and drilling Zhili's six armies, formally establishing the name "trained army." Provinces across the empire then followed suit. Although trained armies were drawn from established regular quotas, their camp, platoon, and pay regulations followed the Xiang and Huai army systems—the same as defense forces. Green Standard regulars were scattered across garrison posts throughout the prefectures, whereas trained armies were concentrated in major cities and strategic towns, with weapons streamlined and training intensified—turning dispersed units into cohesive forces focused on garrisoning key points. Their function matched that of defense forces; in effect, trained armies were defense forces.

同治、光緒間，各省所增編防、練軍，兵部、戶部於光緒二十四年核其總數，直隸練軍一萬一千人，留防淮軍三萬一千人，新軍一萬一千四百人，毅軍一萬人，奉天練軍一萬一千四百人，吉林防軍八千五百九十八人，練軍四千四百三十八人，黑龍江練軍七千九百七十一人，山西練軍四千九百人，河南防軍九千一百九十人，陝西防、練軍一萬四千四百五十人，甘肅防軍一萬二千五百人，新疆防軍二萬七千八百四十五人，塔爾巴哈台勇營二千四百三十二人，四川勇營一萬五千六百九十八人，雲南防軍一萬五千三十三人，貴州練軍九千四百八十六人，廣東勇營一萬一千八百人，廣西勇營一萬六千九百四十人，湖南練軍一萬二千九百七十人，湖北勇營一萬二千六百九十人，新軍一千九十三人，江西防軍九千三百六十三人，安徽防、練軍一萬一千二百九十人，江蘇防軍二萬三千七百九十人，自強軍三千一百七十人，得勝軍三千人，浙江防軍二萬一千三百人，山東防軍一萬三千九百五十人，福建防軍一萬五百四十人，各省防軍、練勇凡三十六萬餘人，歲需餉銀二千餘萬兩。 其後綠營兵屢加裁汰，各省衛戍之責，遂專屬於防、練軍。 光緒中葉後，防、練軍改為巡防隊。 光、宣之間，又改為陸軍。 至宣統三年，各省巡防隊猶未裁盡也。 茲列同、光、宣三朝改設防、練軍規畫於篇，而以陸軍新制附焉。
Between the Tongzhi and Guangxu reigns, as provinces expanded their defense and trained armies, the Boards of War and Revenue in Guangxu 24 audited the totals: Zhili trained army, 11,000; Huai Army on garrison duty, 31,000; New Army, 11,400; Yi Army, 10,000; Fengtian trained army, 11,400; Jilin defense forces, 8,598, trained army, 4,438; Heilongjiang trained army, 7,971; Shanxi trained army, 4,900; Henan defense forces, 9,190; Shaanxi defense and trained armies, 14,450; Gansu defense forces, 12,500; Xinjiang defense forces, 27,845; Tarbagatai brave camp, 2,432; Sichuan brave camp, 15,698; Yunnan defense forces, 15,033; Guizhou trained army, 9,486; Guangdong brave camp, 11,800; Guangxi brave camp, 16,940; Hunan trained army, 12,970; Hubei brave camp, 12,690, New Army, 1,093; Jiangxi defense forces, 9,363; Anhui defense and trained armies, 11,290; Jiangsu defense forces, 23,790, Self-Strengthening Army, 3,170, Desheng Army, 3,000; Zhejiang defense forces, 21,300; Shandong defense forces, 13,950; Fujian defense forces, 10,540. Defense forces and drilled braves nationwide totaled more than 360,000 men, consuming over twenty million taels of silver in annual pay. Thereafter Green Standard troops were repeatedly cut back, and garrison duty in each province fell exclusively to defense and trained armies. After the mid-Guangxu period, defense and trained armies were reorganized as patrol-defense corps. Between the Guangxu and Xuantong reigns, they were renamed again as the army. As late as Xuantong 3, patrol-defense corps in several provinces had still not been fully disbanded. This chapter sets forth the plans for establishing defense and trained armies during the Tongzhi, Guangxu, and Xuantong reigns, with the new army system appended.
7
防軍，同治元年，直隸省於大沽協標六營內選練五百人，復增至二千五百人，分為五營，營分十隊，設總統一人，翼長二人，各營管帶一人，副管帶二人，正副令官二人，帶隊官十人，分隊官二十人。 沈葆楨於江西省額兵一萬二千人內，嚴汰老弱，增補精銳，分為二班，一班調至省城操練，一班留防汛地，半年換班。 其赴操者，酌加練費，較募勇之費不及其半，練成即調赴前敵助戰。
Defense forces: In Tongzhi 1 Zhili selected and drilled five hundred men from the six camps of the Dagu cooperative banner, then expanded the force to two thousand five hundred, organized into five camps of ten companies each. The command structure comprised one commander-in-chief, two wing commanders, one camp commander per camp with two deputies, two chief and deputy order officers, ten company commanders, and twenty platoon commanders. Shen Baozhen, working from Jiangxi's quota of twelve thousand troops, rigorously eliminated the old and weak and replaced them with elite soldiers, dividing the force into two shifts—one drilling at the provincial capital, one holding garrison posts—with rotations every six months. Men sent to drill received supplemental training allowances at less than half the cost of recruiting braves; once trained, they were dispatched to the front as reinforcements.
8
二年，劉長佑以直隸省營務積年廢弛，各營兵數多寡懸殊，號令不一，乃改仿湘軍成規，以五百人為一營，設營官、哨隊官及親兵，分別隊伍旂幟，申明號令，改設六軍，凡築營結陣諸法，一律講求。 其步隊營制，設營官一人，哨官四人，哨長五人，什長四十人，正兵三百六十人，營官親兵五十人，哨官護兵四十人，營官自率中哨，凡五百人。 其馬隊營制，設營官一人，幫辦二人，督隊官五人，每哨五棚，每棚什長一人，正兵九人，營官自率中哨，合夥兵、馬夫凡三百十六人。 保定練軍，馬、步、守兵一千九百五十人為一軍，宣化練軍，一千四百八十人為一軍，古北口練軍，二千四百十人為一軍，大名練軍，一千二百三十四人為一軍，正定練軍，一千四百八十人為一軍，通永練軍，一千七百五十四人為一軍，共編為六軍。
In Tongzhi 2 Liu Changyou, finding that camp administration in Zhili had been neglected for years—with wildly uneven troop strengths and inconsistent orders—reformed the forces on the Xiang Army model: five hundred men per camp, with camp officers, platoon and company officers, and personal guards; distinct companies, flags, and banners; standardized commands; and six reorganized armies, with uniform instruction in camp construction and battle formation. The infantry camp establishment comprised one camp officer, four platoon officers, five platoon leaders, forty squad leaders, three hundred sixty regular soldiers, fifty of the camp officer's personal guards, and forty guard soldiers for the platoon officers; the camp officer personally commanded the center platoon—for a total of five hundred men. The cavalry camp establishment comprised one camp officer, two assistants, five company supervisors, five tents per platoon with one squad leader and nine regular soldiers each; the camp officer personally commanded the center platoon—for a combined total of three hundred sixteen soldiers and grooms. The Baoding trained army—cavalry, infantry, and garrison troops totaling 1,950 men—formed one army; Xuanhua, 1,480; Gubeikou, 2,410; Daming, 1,234; Zhengding, 1,480; and Tongyong, 1,754—altogether six armies.
9
五年，令遵化等處各駐防軍，每軍定為步隊二千人，馬隊五百人，在督標、提標內選取，凡一萬五千人，分為六軍，頒練兵章程十七條，隸總督節制，以防畿輔。 又於六軍外續練防勇二軍。 以奉天留防隊伍調補直隸練軍缺額。 其訓練京營，由神機營量增兵額。 是年，左宗棠以福建省綠營額冘餉薄，乃裁兵十成之四，即以裁餉加留營之兵，並營操練。
In Tongzhi 5 garrison forces at Zunhua and other posts were fixed at two thousand infantry and five hundred cavalry per army, drawn from the governor's and provincial commander's banners—fifteen thousand men in six armies. Seventeen articles of drill regulations were promulgated, all under the governor's command, to defend the capital region. Beyond the six armies, two additional defense-brave armies were drilled. Garrison forces retained in Fengtian were transferred to fill vacancies in the Zhili trained army. Training of the capital garrison was handled by incrementally expanding the Shenji Camp's troop quotas. That year Zuo Zongtang, finding Fujian's Green Standard quota overmanned and underpaid, cut troop strength by forty percent, redirected the savings to the remaining garrison troops, and consolidated camps for drill.

六年，丁寶楨于山東省增練馬隊三千人。
In Tongzhi 6 Ding Baozhen added three thousand drilled cavalry in Shandong.
11
七年，以各省綠營日益孱弱，令各省以壯健練勇易之。 令曾國籓經理直隸省練兵事宜，就全省綠營內抽練六千人，仿勇營規制，分地巡防。 海防議起，調駐天津，分中、左、右、前、後五營，與勇營相犄角。
In Tongzhi 7, as Green Standard forces in the provinces grew ever weaker, each province was ordered to replace them with sturdy drilled braves. Zeng Guofan was ordered to oversee drill affairs in Zhili, selecting six thousand men from the province's Green Standard forces, organizing them on the brave-camp model for regional patrol and defense. When coastal defense became a priority, they were transferred to Tianjin and organized into center, left, right, front, and rear camps, mutually supporting the brave camps.
12
八年，曾國籓以軍事既竣，宜練兵不宜練勇，而勇營良法為練軍所當參用者，一、文法宜簡，一、事權宜專，一、情意宜洽。 減兵增餉，汰弱留強，嚴杜頂替之弊。 於原有練軍四千人外，古北口、正定、保定各練千人，統以東南戰將。 練成之後，分為四軍。 以二軍駐京北，二軍駐京南，俟功效既著，增練五千人。 全省防營於未撤之九營外，以劉銘傳全部淮軍駐防張秋，以督標親軍砲隊營及前營副營駐天津，以親軍砲隊營駐大沽砲台，以盛字中軍六營、左軍三營，仁軍二營，馬隊五營駐馬廠、青縣，於運河西岸築砲台五座，駐盛字前軍三營、右軍三營、老左軍一營，於滄洲駐樂字中、左各一營，其盛字營兼辦屯田，以衛畿輔。 是年，丁日昌以江蘇省自淮軍全部撤防以後，江蘇撫標兵僅有一千六百餘人，乃裁汰老弱，補以勇丁，分左右二營，練習洋槍及開花砲諸技。 馬新貽以江南全省額兵一萬二千七百餘人，分防各處，徒有其名，必須化散為整，始能轉弱為強，乃於督標內選千人為左右營，浦口、瓜洲營內選五百人為中營，揚州、泰州營內選五百人為前營，駐省城訓練，於徐州鎮標內選千人為徐防新兵左右營，以地方之輕重，定練兵之多寡。 劉錦棠以新疆全境自回民亂後，旗營零落殆盡，乃於烏魯木齊創設標兵，於天山南北路各置額兵，新疆所有駐防旗兵，歸併伊犁整頓，別以精騎重兵居中屯駐，為南北各路策應之師。 崇實以四川省軍事漸定，酌裁防軍，選練旗、綠各營。
In Tongzhi 8 Zeng Guofan argued that with the fighting over, regular troops—not braves—should be drilled, yet trained armies ought to adopt three proven practices of the brave camps: keep regulations simple, concentrate authority, and foster mutual trust. Cut troop numbers and raise pay, eliminate the weak and retain the strong, and strictly prevent the abuse of substitute enlistment. Beyond the existing four thousand trained troops, one thousand more were drilled at each of Gubeikou, Zhengding, and Baoding, all commanded by battle-tested generals from the southeast. Once training was complete, they were organized into four armies. Two armies were posted north of the capital and two south; once results were clearly demonstrated, an additional five thousand would be drilled. Across the province's defense camps—apart from nine not yet withdrawn—Liu Mingchuan's entire Huai Army garrisoned Zhangqiu; the governor's personal artillery camp and front-camp deputy camp held Tianjin; a personal artillery camp held the Dagu forts; six Sheng center-army, three left-army, two Ren-army, and five cavalry camps held Machang and Qing County; five forts were built on the Grand Canal's west bank, garrisoned by three Sheng front-army, three right-army, and one old left-army camp; at Cangzhou one each of the Le center and left camps were posted. The Sheng camps also managed agricultural colonies to defend the capital region. That year Ding Richang, finding that after the Huai Army's full withdrawal from Jiangsu the governor's banner numbered barely sixteen hundred men, eliminated the old and weak, supplemented with brave recruits, and organized left and right camps to drill in foreign rifles and explosive-shell artillery. Ma Xinyi argued that Jiangnan's quota of over twelve thousand seven hundred troops, scattered across garrison posts, existed in name only; only by consolidating dispersed units into cohesive forces could weakness become strength. He selected one thousand from the governor's banner for left and right camps; five hundred from the Pukou and Guazhou camps for a center camp; five hundred from the Yangzhou and Taizhou camps for a front camp—all drilling at the provincial capital; and one thousand from the Xuzhou garrison banner for left and right camps of new Xuzhou defense troops, scaling drill numbers to each locality's strategic importance. Liu Jintang, finding that banner garrisons across Xinjiang had been nearly destroyed in the Muslim rebellion, created banner troops at Urumqi, established quota troops on both the northern and southern Tianshan routes, consolidated all Xinjiang garrison banner troops at Yili for reorganization, and stationed elite cavalry and heavy troops at the center as a reserve to support both routes. As military affairs in Sichuan gradually stabilized, Chong Shi selectively cut defense forces and chose banner and Green Standard camps for drill.
13
九年，曾國籓於直隸省增募馬勇千人，分為四營，原有額兵，增足萬人，分練馬隊、步隊，奏定各營哨之制，及底餉、練餉、出征加餉之制，為北方重鎮。
In Tongzhi 9 Zeng Guofan recruited an additional one thousand cavalry braves in Zhili, organized into four camps; brought existing quota troops up to ten thousand; and drilled separate cavalry and infantry columns. He memorialized fixing camp and platoon organization plus base pay, drill pay, and campaign supplemental pay—establishing Zhili as a major northern stronghold.
14
十年，鮑源深以山西省撫標兵仿曾國籓直隸練兵之法，選練馬隊一營，步隊二營，以次推行各鎮。 吳棠以四川全省額兵類多疲弱，乃歸併訓練，得精壯萬人。 王文韶以苗疆戡定，所有湖南省留防軍三十營，分布於湖南、貴州接壤之區，又於撫標、提標內各選練精壯一營。
In Tongzhi 10 Bao Yuanshen, applying Zeng Guofan's Zhili drill methods to the Shanxi governor's banner, selected one cavalry camp and two infantry camps for drill, extending the program garrison by garrison. Wu Tang, finding Sichuan's quota troops mostly weak, consolidated and trained them into a force of ten thousand elite soldiers. After the Miao frontier was pacified, Wang Wenshao retained thirty Hunan defense camps along the Hunan–Guizhou border, and from the governor's and provincial commander's banners selected one elite camp each for drill.

In Tongzhi 12 the governors-general Zuo Zongtang of Shaanxi-Gansu and Cen Yuying of Yunnan-Guizhou were each ordered to select brave recruits from their forces to fill regular camp quotas. At this time officials at court and in the provinces alike focused on drilling troops. Li Zongxi argued that braves and regular soldiers differed in status, cohesion, and discipline, and that braves could not simply be converted into regular soldiers. Wang Kaitai argued that drilled troops should rotate garrison duty in shifts; after Yunnan and Guizhou were pacified, regular troops in both provinces should likewise rotate garrisons for drill, to prevent the slackening that comes from long stationary posting. Board of War officials jointly noted that in the early Tongzhi period, when troop drill was first proposed, the capital's Shenji Camp and Zhili's six armies received separate drill-pay funding and independent camp systems. Fujian, Zhejiang, Guangdong, Jiangsu, and other provinces redirected savings from troop cuts to increase trained-army pay. Henan, Shanxi, Shandong, Hunan, and other provinces followed the Zhili method—selecting troops from regular quotas for drill and adding modest training allowances beyond base pay. Gansu, with military affairs only recently settled, began by drilling fifteen hundred men. Yet in each province, troops selected for drill amounted to no more than twenty or thirty percent of the original quota. If the remaining troops were left unattended, they would inevitably grow weak. Provincial commanders should be ordered to give drilled troops periodic rest while transferring undrilled troops for drill in sequence, until every quota soldier in the province became a crack trooper.

In Guangxu 6 frontier officials were ordered to cut troop strength as appropriate. Even after disbandments, defense forces in the provinces still numbered many. Zhili, Shaanxi, and Gansu needed frontier defense; Yunnan and Guizhou had relatively few defense forces; all other provinces should make major cuts. After steam warships were introduced, the old-style war-junk navy was also selectively retained or eliminated. Soon afterward Guangxi governor Qing Yu, finding the province undermanned and underpaid, selectively cut defense forces and, using the savings and following Zhili drill regulations, selected two camps each from the provincial and commander's banners and one camp each from the Left and Right River garrisons. Qiyuan noted that since mounted bandits had run rampant in Fengtian during the Tongzhi period, guest armies had been repeatedly borrowed and banner and Green Standard camps added and drilled—yet camp systems and pay regulations remained unstandardized. In Guangxu 5 guest armies from Zhili were consolidated into Fengtian; rifle, artillery, cavalry, and infantry units were combined, camp systems standardized, and five Feng-character center, left, right, front, and rear cavalry-infantry camps organized, with one additional infantry camp added to the center army. Ding Baozhen noted that since military operations began in Sichuan, forces beyond Sichuan braves—including Hunan and Guizhou armies—had reached over sixty thousand men; after peace returned they were cut and consolidated step by step, and by Guangxu 3 over ten thousand defense troops remained garrisoning strategic passes and could not be cut further. Guizhou's defense forces were smaller than other provinces'; after Guangxu 5 Li Mingchi successively cut over four thousand men. Li Hanzhang argued that Hubei defense forces—including three Sheng-character camps, eight Zhongyi camps, seven Wuyi camps, and seven naval camps—all held strategic posts and should not be cut; instead, over three thousand men were selectively eliminated from Hubei's provincial quota. Yulu reported that after the Nian rebellion in Anhui, over eighteen thousand garrison troops in southern and northern Anhui were consolidated and trained step by step, leaving ten thousand land and water defense forces.

二十四年，王毓藻練貴州軍隊，先就省防三營改習洋操，次第推及各營。 王文韶挑留直隸全省淮、練各軍二萬餘人，編為二十營，分左右翼，駐守大沽口及山海關，以練軍三十三營分防內地及熱河等處。 色楞額以熱河兼轄蒙古兩盟十七旗，而馬步防兵僅有千人，乃增練壯丁五百人為一營，馬隊五百人為二營，佐以砲隊百人。 增祺以福建省多山，新練防軍，宜重步隊，參以砲隊，增制過山快砲十二尊。 胡聘之以東、直、秦、豫各省皆有防軍，支餉自數十萬至百萬不等，而山西省屏蔽畿疆，僅有練軍五千人，乃增練新軍，固西路之防。 榮祿因北洋四大軍訓練已成，分路駐防，以武毅軍駐蘆台為前軍，甘軍駐薊州為後軍，毅軍駐山海關為左軍，新建軍駐小站為右軍，別練萬人駐南苑為中軍，軍械不足，令江南機器局撥解新式快槍三千枝，快砲七尊，原有之淮軍一萬二千人，防、練軍一萬九千人，歸併訓練。 劉坤一以江南省之江寧、鎮江、吳淞、江陰、徐州五路防軍悉改習洋操，所用軍械，統歸一律。 是年，令王大臣選京師神機營馬步萬人為選鋒營。 令北方各省營伍，由新建軍遣員教習，南方各省營伍，由自強軍遣員教習。 東三省防練各營伍，由北洋武備學堂遣人教習。
In Guangxu 24 Wang Yuzao drilled Guizhou's armies, first converting the three provincial-defense camps to Western drill and then extending the reform camp by camp. Northern provincial camps were to be instructed by instructors from the Newly Created Army; southern provincial camps by instructors from the Self-Strengthening Army. Defense and trained camps in the Northeast were to be instructed by instructors from the Northern Ocean Military Academy.

二十五年，李秉衡上言奉天仁、育二軍，訓練已成，應擇地修築營壘，俾成重鎮。 裕祿以直隸防、練各軍為數太多，乃挑留馬步精兵一萬八百餘人，編為練軍步隊十二營，馬隊二十營，更定營制，步隊以三百人為一營，馬隊以二百餘人為一營，凡三十二營，分為直隸練軍左右翼，以通永鎮總兵統左翼，天津鎮總兵統右翼，其新建等軍，仍與宋慶之二十五營各守原防。 劉樹堂以浙江防軍雲字、吉字、勝字、旅字各營凡十一營二十三旗，並為五軍，名為兩浙新軍，用北洋武毅軍操法訓練。 松壽以江西省防軍有忠新等營二千餘人，內江及贛防水師二千四百餘人，武威等營旗三千餘人，分佈各路，乃在省城設全省營務處，為訓練各軍之總匯。 劉坤一以江南各軍歸併為三十七營，加以新法教練，漸有成效。 文興以盛京八旗制兵，汰弱留強，仿北洋練軍新法教練。 裕祥就四川駐防旗兵內選精銳為一營，陣法營制，與防軍一式。 松壽以江西省新練防軍三千人，撥解南北洋新式槍砲，以資操練。 黃槐森選廣西省各軍，先就省標、提標及左右江各營挑練一千四百人，為各軍模範。 廖壽豐以浙江省寧波、鎮海各營次第改習洋操，省防各軍先練步隊三哨，砲隊一哨，凡標營及防、練軍，俟四哨教成，更番改練，推及全省。
In Guangxu 25 Li Bingheng memorialized that Fengtian's Ren and Yu armies had completed training and that camps and fortifications should be built at selected sites to make them strategic strongpoints. Yulu, finding Zhili's defense and trained forces too numerous, retained over ten thousand eight hundred elite cavalry-infantry, organizing them into twelve trained-army infantry camps and twenty cavalry camps; infantry camps were fixed at three hundred men and cavalry camps at over two hundred, for thirty-two camps divided into Zhili trained army's left and right wings under the Tongzhou and Tianjin garrison commanders; the Newly Created Army and other forces, together with Song Qing's twenty-five camps, continued to hold their original garrisons. Liu Kunyi consolidated Jiangnan's various armies into thirty-seven camps and introduced new-method instruction with gradually improving results. Wen Xing reorganized Shengjing's Eight Banner regulars by eliminating the weak and retaining the strong, training them on Northern Ocean trained-army methods. Yuxiang selected elite banner troops garrisoned in Sichuan into one camp, with battle formations and camp organization identical to the defense forces. Song Shou allocated new-style rifles and guns from the Northern and Southern Ocean arsenals to Jiangxi's newly drilled three-thousand-man defense force to support drill and training. Huang Huaisen selected Guangxi's various armies, first drilling fourteen hundred men from the provincial banner, the commander's banner, and the Left and Right River camps as models for all other forces. Liao Shoufeng had Ningbo and Zhenhai camps in Zhejiang converted to Western drill in sequence; provincial-defense forces first drilled three infantry platoons and one artillery platoon; once those four platoons were trained, all garrison-banner, defense, and trained troops were to be converted in rotation until the reform covered the whole province.

二十六年，端方以陝西新練洋操之馬步十三旗，分防南北山隘。 是年，令各省疆臣嚴定將弁貪墨之刑，並整理浙江省防營積弊。

二十七年，李興銳以江西防軍人數不一，乃分為五路，釐定人數，以中軍為常備軍，前、後、左、右軍為續備軍，軍各五營，營各五哨。 劉坤一以江南武衛先鋒軍、江勝軍各二千人為常備左右軍，其餘防軍四十餘營悉編為續備軍。 岑春煊以山西省兵制紛歧，有練軍、防軍、晉威軍之判，乃仿北洋武衛軍制，以省標三千人分左右翼為常備軍，以太原、大同二鎮兵共練三千人為續備軍。 魏光燾以雲南省防軍二十四營，營各二百五十人，改編為常備軍十二營，營各三百人，舊有練軍改為續備軍，均練習洋操。 丁振鐸於廣西省防軍三營內選千人為常備軍，各屬防軍，就人數多寡，練一、二隊不等。 鄧華熙以貴州防軍及威遠營並練五營，凡千五百人，為常備軍，東西路練軍及緝捕營共二十九營，選練五千七百人，為續備軍，分防各隘。 是年，設軍政司於天津，總司直隸省淮、練各防軍操防事宜。
In Guangxu 27 Li Xingrui, finding Jiangxi's defense forces uneven in strength, divided them into five routes with fixed quotas: the center army as the standing force and the front, rear, left, and right armies as reserves; each army had five camps of five platoons. Liu Kunyi designated Jiangnan's two-thousand-man Wuyi Vanguard Army and Jiangsheng Army as the left and right standing armies and reorganized the remaining forty-plus defense camps as the reserve army. Cen Chunxuan, finding Shanxi's military system confused among trained army, defense army, and Jinwei Army, followed the Northern Ocean Wuyi Army model: the provincial banner's three thousand men formed left and right standing wings, and Taiyuan and Datong garrisons' three thousand drilled men formed the reserve army. Wei Guangtao reorganized Yunnan's twenty-four defense camps of two hundred fifty men each into twelve standing camps of three hundred men, converted the old trained army into the reserve army, and had all units drill Western methods. Ding Zhenduo selected one thousand men from Guangxi's three defense camps as the standing army; each subordinate defense force drilled one or two companies according to its strength. Deng Huaxi combined Guizhou's defense forces and Weiyuan Camp with five drilled camps—fifteen hundred men in all—as the standing army; twenty-nine east- and west-route trained and suppression camps had five thousand seven hundred selected and drilled as the reserve army, posted to defend the various passes. That year a Military Administration Bureau was established at Tianjin to oversee drill and defense for all Huai, trained, and defense forces in Zhili.

二十八年，升允以陝西省新舊各軍均已改習洋操，乃選精銳六旗為常備新軍，其忠靖八旗兩翼步隊，武威兩翼馬隊，改為步隊十二旗，以六旗為續備防軍，六旗為續備長軍，防軍有地方之責，長軍為開荒之需，以馬隊砲隊佐之。
In Guangxu 28 Sheng Yun, finding all old and new Shaanxi armies already converted to Western drill, selected six elite banners as the new standing army; the Zhongjing Eight Banner two-wing infantry and Wuwei two-wing cavalry became twelve infantry banners—six as reserve defense troops with local responsibilities and six as reserve reclamation troops for frontier opening, assisted by cavalry and artillery.

二十九年，夏時以江西省新軍僅有千二百人，江防重要，殊苦不足，九江為全省門戶，乃別募一軍，亦為常備軍，合中、前常備兩軍共十營，專防省城及九江二處，以左、右、後續備三軍分防各地。
In Guangxu 29 Xia Shi, finding Jiangxi's new army numbered only twelve hundred men while river defense at the provincial gateway of Jiujiang remained critically understrength, separately recruited another standing army; together with the center and front standing armies there were ten camps defending the provincial capital and Jiujiang, while left, right, and rear reserve armies garrisoned the rest of the province.

三十年，曹鴻勛以貴州各軍於光緒二十六年改編為常備軍、續備軍，共二十四營，嗣因沿邊戒嚴，增募防勇十九營，而籌餉艱難，遂每營酌減人數，凡防、練軍及親兵減存一萬五百餘人，次第改習洋操。 潘效蘇於新疆標、防、巡、練各軍三萬二千餘人內，選存正勇一萬三千餘人，於南北各路勺配分防。
In Guangxu 30 Cao Hongxun noted that Guizhou's armies had been reorganized in Guangxu 26 into twenty-four standing and reserve camps, then augmented by nineteen defense-brave camps during frontier alerts; funding being scarce, he reduced each camp's strength as appropriate, retaining over ten thousand five hundred defense, trained, and personal-guard troops and converting them to Western drill in sequence. Pan Xiaosu, from among Xinjiang's garrison, defense, patrol, and trained forces totaling over thirty-two thousand men, selected and retained over thirteen thousand regular braves, distributing them proportionally for defense on the northern and southern routes.

三十一年，練兵處王大臣以山東省武衛先鋒隊二十營分防散漫，令擇地屯駐，增募成鎮。 是年，命鐵良校閱江蘇、安徽、江西、湖北各省防軍、練軍、陸軍、旗兵、巡警兵。 鐵良遍閱各軍，大都軍械不一，操法亦未盡嫺，舊營改練，進步甚遲。 惟安徽練軍二隊，九江常備五營，湖北二鎮，較為生色。
In Guangxu 31 the Army Training Office minister, finding Shandong's twenty Wuyi Vanguard camps scattered across garrison posts, ordered them concentrated at selected sites and expanded into a garrison town. That year Tie Liang was ordered to inspect defense forces, trained armies, land forces, banner troops, and patrol police in Jiangsu, Anhui, Jiangxi, and Hubei. Tie Liang's inspection found that most units lacked uniform weapons and fully proficient drill; old camps converted to new methods progressed very slowly. Only Anhui's two trained-army companies, Jiujiang's five standing camps, and Hubei's two garrisons stood out as comparatively impressive.

三十三年，張之洞以沿江督捕營、下游緝匪營改編為水陸巡緝隊。 王士珍以江北巡防隊改為步隊六營，馬隊二營，其餘淮海水師、練軍衛隊，悉仍其舊。 錫良以雲南防軍二十七營，鐵路巡防十一營，土勇一營，凡三十九營，次第改編新軍，以全省防軍每營二百五十人為定額，分南防、西防、普防、江防、鐵路巡防為五路，凡四十七營。

Xi Liang reorganized Yunnan's twenty-seven defense camps, eleven railway-patrol camps, and one native-brave camp—thirty-nine camps in all—into the new army step by step, fixing provincial-defense camps at two hundred fifty men and organizing forty-seven camps across five routes: southern defense, western defense, general defense, river defense, and railway patrol.

宣統元年，以熱河巡防強勝營改編常備軍，以察哈爾原有之精壯、精健等營改編為巡防馬隊一營，步隊二營。 徐世昌以奉天巡防隊分駐五路剿匪，旋合編為步隊一標，其河防營亦一律改編。 王士珍因江南防軍步隊六營、砲隊二營改隸江北，乃合原有之巡防隊及留防各營編為巡防第七營，共巡防步隊八營，以備練成一鎮，原有衛隊，增募一哨，編為一營，尚有練軍三百人，水師十棚，均改為巡防隊。 沈秉堃以雲南防軍內有各屬之保衛隊，系舊日團營，名為營隊，實即鄉團，未能遽改為巡防隊。 廣福以伊犁軍標漢隊，系金順西征營勇之舊，其營制餉章，均仿湘軍，乃遵新章，以步隊一營、馬隊二旗為左路巡防隊，馬隊二旗為右路巡防隊，分駐惠遠、惠寧各城。 袁樹勛以山東省原駐淮軍，於光緒二十四年移防長江，新增防兵二營駐兗、沂二府及德州，均當南北要道，未能遽裁。 聯魁以新疆籌餉維艱，就原有防營改編為步隊三營、馬隊二營，又增編工程兵一隊，馬隊一營，勉成一協。 寶棻以山西省軍隊，向分太原、大同、口外三大支巡防隊，乃歸併分編為中、前、後三路，各以統領節制之，凡馬步二十二隊。 吳重熹以河南省巡防營不合部章，就通省巡防步隊二十八營、馬隊十二營分為五路，豫正左軍為中路，南陽鎮為前路，歸德鎮為左路，河州鎮為右路，豫正右軍為後路。 趙爾巽以四川省防軍二十九營，編為六軍，每軍六營，分中、前、後、左、右、副中為六路，分駐防境。 其防守寧遠之靖字二營、游擊步隊二營，增募寧遠之靖字後營，改為巡防副左路、副右路兩軍，每三營為一軍。 成都駐防滿營亦改編巡防隊三營，俾臻一律。 瑞澂以江蘇省各營練成一協外，尚有太湖水師巡防隊、陸師左右巡防隊，系陸路三旗及蘇捕營衛隊等先後改編者，乃次第換防調操，以免弛懈。

44
二年，岑春蓂改編湖南省巡防隊，酌定餉章，即日成軍，其餘緝私三旗，改為南路巡防隊。 孫寶琦改編山東省巡防隊，所有中、前、後、左、右五路，各就坐營之中哨改編，其砲隊以快砲六尊為一隊，各府州縣巡勇悉改為巡防隊，兗、沂、曹三府原有之巡防營，亦遵新章編練。 恩壽以陝西省巡警軍悉改為巡防隊。 楊文鼎以湖南省巡防隊分為中、東、西、南四路，駐防各府。 昆源以察哈爾八旗壯丁編練巡防馬隊。 松壽以裁撤福建全省之綠營兵改為巡防隊十六營，分五路駐防各府。 張人駿以兩江巡緝隊及師船十艘改為探訪隊，其沿江巡防隊深資得力，以協解北洋之淮軍餉為巡防軍餉，並以江防軍分駐江寧省城。 錫良以奉天原有之協巡隊、備補隊、砲隊、衛隊各防營，遵章改編為陸軍步隊一標、砲隊一營。 是年，山東、山西撫臣咸擬緩裁巡防軍，以靖地方。
In Xuantong 2 Cen Chunming reorganized Hunan's patrol corps, fixed pay regulations, and completed the army immediately; the remaining three anti-smuggling banners became the southern-route patrol corps. Sun Baoqi reorganized Shandong's patrol corps: on each of the center, front, rear, left, and right routes the central platoon of the garrison camp was reorganized; artillery companies were fixed at six rapid-fire guns each; patrol braves in all prefectures, departments, and counties became patrol corps, and original patrol camps in Yan, Yi, and Cao prefectures were also reorganized under the new regulations. En Shou converted all of Shaanxi's patrol-police army into patrol corps. Yang Wending divided Hunan's patrol corps into center, east, west, and south routes garrisoned in the various prefectures. Kun Yuan selected and drilled patrol cavalry from Chahar's Eight Banner strong youths. Song Shou converted all of Fujian's disbanded Green Standard troops into sixteen patrol camps garrisoned on five routes across the province. Zhang Renjun converted the Two-Rivers patrol corps and ten command ships into an investigation corps; the river-bank patrol corps proved especially effective; patrol-army funds came from coordinated Huai Army pay allocations to the Northern Ocean, and river-defense troops were also garrisoned in the Jiangning provincial capital. Xi Liang reorganized Fengtian's original cooperative-patrol, reserve-replacement, artillery, and guard defense camps under the new regulations into one infantry regiment and one artillery camp. That year the governors of Shandong and Shanxi both proposed deferring cuts to patrol forces to preserve local order.
45
三年，張人駿以兩江巡防軍關係重要，其屬於江寧者，馬步三十二營，屬於江蘇者，步隊六營，屬於江北者，步隊八營一哨、馬隊一營，江南北地勢扼要，未可議裁，並擬以新兵中副二營留防三隊改為第一、二、三巡防隊，以一哨為提督衛隊。 丁寶銓以山西太原滿營，於光緒二十八年已改練新操，乃遵章改編為巡防隊。 恩壽以陝西省巡警軍已改編巡防隊，並設馬步巡防營務處。 慶恕以青海墾荒，已開墾六萬餘畝，原有巡防隊不敷分佈，增練防軍一旗。 誠勛以熱河雖有直隸練軍八營，僅防朝、建一帶，其先後所練巡防隊十三營，分防各屬，未能遽改陸軍。 張勳以長江巡防馬、步、砲隊十三營，分駐浦口、六合、江寧、蘇州、懷遠各府縣，並在沿江一帶廣布偵探，以靖盜源。 瑞澂以湖南六營已裁，所有撫標之兵，選精壯編巡防一營。 此改設防練軍之大略也。
In Xuantong 3 Zhang Renjun, finding the Two-Rivers patrol army critically important—thirty-two cavalry-infantry camps under Jiangning, six infantry camps under Jiangsu, and eight infantry camps plus one platoon and one cavalry camp under the northern river district—and noting that terrain north and south of the river was too strategically vital to cut, also proposed converting three retained companies from the new army's deputy second camp into the first, second, and third patrol corps, with one platoon as the commander's guard. Ding Baojuan, noting that Taiyuan's Manchu garrison had already converted to new drill in Guangxu 28, reorganized it under the new regulations as patrol corps. En Shou, after reorganizing Shaanxi's patrol-police army as patrol corps, also established a cavalry-infantry patrol camp-affairs office. Qing Shu, finding that Qinghai reclamation had already opened over sixty thousand mu of land while existing patrol corps were insufficient for distribution, added one drilled defense-army banner. Cheng Xun noted that although Rehe had eight Zhili trained-army camps defending only the Chao and Jian region, its thirteen previously drilled patrol corps garrisoned subordinate districts and could not immediately be converted to land forces. Zhang Xun garrisoned thirteen Yangtze patrol cavalry, infantry, and artillery camps at Pukou, Liuhe, Jiangning, Suzhou, Huaiyuan, and other prefectures and counties, and widely deployed scouts along the riverbank to suppress banditry at its source. Rui Cheng, after cutting six Hunan camps, selected elite governor's-banner troops and organized one patrol camp. This is the general outline of establishing and reorganizing defense and trained armies.
46
自咸豐軍興，由綠營改為勇營，為留防營，為練軍，為巡防隊，為陸軍，兵制變而益新。 至宣統年，非特綠營盡汰，即湘、淮營勇駐防南北洋者，所存亦無幾矣。
From the military mobilization of the Xianfeng period onward, the Green Standard Army gave way to brave camps, then garrison-defense camps, trained armies, patrol corps, and land armies—the military system growing ever more modern. By the Xuantong years, not only had Green Standard forces been entirely eliminated, but even the Xiang and Huai braves garrisoned north and south of the Yangtze had nearly vanished.
47
陸軍新制，始於甲午戰後，步軍統領榮祿疏保溫處道袁世凱練新軍，是曰新建陸軍。 復練兵小站，名曰定武軍。 兩江總督張之洞聘德人教練新軍，名曰江南自強軍。 其後榮祿以兵部尚書協辦大學士節制北洋海陸各軍，益練新軍，是為武衛軍。
The new land-force system began after the Sino-Japanese War, when Infantry Commander Ronglu memorialized recommending Wenzhou Circuit Intendant Yuan Shikai to drill the new army—the Newly Created Land Army. Drill resumed at Xiaozhan under the name Dingwu Army. Governor-General of the Two Rivers Zhang Zhidong engaged German instructors to drill the new army—the Jiangnan Self-Strengthening Army. Thereafter Ronglu, as Minister of War and Associate Grand Secretary directing the Northern Ocean's land and sea forces, further drilled the new army—the Wuyi Army.
48
庚子亂後，各省皆起練新軍，或就防軍改編，或用新式招練。 至光緒三十年，畫定軍制，京師設練兵處，各省設督練公所，改定新軍區為三十六鎮，新軍制始畫一。
After the Boxer uprising, every province raised new-army drill, either reorganizing defense forces or recruiting and training by new methods. By Guangxu 30 the military system was fixed: an Army Training Office was established in the capital and drill supervision offices in each province; new-army districts were set at thirty-six garrisons, and the new-army system was first unified empire-wide.
49
三十三年，京、外新練陸軍，除禁衛軍外，統計近畿第一鎮駐京北仰山窪，官七百四十八員，兵一萬一千七百六十四名。 第六鎮駐南苑，官七百四十七員，兵一萬一千八百四十六名。 直隸第二鎮駐保定、永平等府，官七百三十七員，兵一萬一千七百三十一名。 第四鎮駐馬廠，官七百四十八員，兵一萬一千七百五十六名。 山東第五鎮駐省城、濰縣、昌邑等處，官七百四十八員，兵一萬一千七百六十四名。 江蘇第二十三混成協駐蘇州等處，官二百七十四員，兵四千三百四十五名。 江北第十三混成協駐清江浦，官三百七十六員，兵二千四百八十一名。 安徽步隊二標、馬隊一營、砲隊一隊駐省城，官二百五十三員，兵四千一百五十五名。 江南第九鎮步隊一營、馬隊二隊駐省城，官七百八十九員，兵八千二百五十五名。 江西步隊一協、馬隊二隊駐省城，官二百三十一員，兵四千二百八十七名。 河南第二十九混成協駐省城，官三百三十八員，兵五千六百十八名，步隊一協、馬砲隊各一營調駐京城，官一百六十二員，兵三千八十五名。 湖南步隊一協、砲隊一營駐省城，官二百四十八員，兵四千五十六名。 湖北第八鎮駐省城，官七百二員，兵一萬五百二名，第二十一混成協駐武昌、漢陽及京漢鐵路，官二百八十八員，兵四千六百十二名。 浙江步隊一協駐省城，官一百五十九員，兵二千三百八十四名。 福建第十鎮駐省城及福寧、延平等處，官四百五十五員，兵六千七百八十八名。 雲南步隊一協、砲隊一營駐省城及臨安，官二百三十八員，兵四千二百四十八名。 貴州步隊一標、砲隊一隊駐省城，官一百七員，兵一千八百四十六名。 四川步隊駐省城，官十二員，兵六十一名。 山西步隊二標、馬砲隊各一營駐省城，官二百六十二員，兵四千五百五十七名。 陝西步隊一協、砲隊一隊駐省城，官二百二十員，兵三千九百三十六名。 甘肅步隊二標、砲隊一營駐省城、河州、固原、西寧，官二百二十一員，兵四千一百二十八名。 新疆步隊一協、馬隊一標、砲隊一營駐省城，官一百六十七員，兵二千三百二十二名。 東三省第三鎮駐吉林省城、長春、寧安、延吉及奉天錦州等處，官七百五十三員，兵一萬一千八百八十三名，第一混成協駐奉天省城，官三百三員，兵三千五十九名，第二混成協駐奉天新民等處，官三百四員，兵五千五十三名，步隊一協一標、砲隊一營駐吉林，官三百六十一員，兵七千八百七十名。 宣統三年統計，除前列外，浙江成第二十一鎮，雲南成第十九鎮，四川成第十七鎮，奉天成第二十鎮，吉林成第二十三鎮，廣東成第二十六鎮駐省城，廣西成第二十五鎮駐省城及桂林等處，先後共成二十六鎮。 未幾，武昌陸軍先變，各省應之，而三十六鎮卒未全立雲。
In Guangxu 33 newly drilled land armies inside and outside the capital, apart from the Imperial Guard Army, included the near-capital First Garrison at Yangshanwa north of Beijing with seven hundred forty-eight officers and eleven thousand seven hundred sixty-four soldiers. The Sixth Brigade was garrisoned at Nanyuan, with 747 officers and 11,846 soldiers. The Second Zhili Brigade was garrisoned at Baoding, Yongping, and other prefectures, with 737 officers and 11,731 soldiers. The Fourth Brigade was garrisoned at Machang, with 748 officers and 11,756 soldiers. The Fifth Shandong Brigade was garrisoned at the provincial capital, Weixian, Changyi, and other posts, with 748 officers and 11,764 soldiers. The Twenty-third Jiangsu Mixed Brigade was garrisoned at Suzhou and other posts, with 274 officers and 4,345 soldiers. The Thirteenth Jiangbei Mixed Brigade was garrisoned at Qingjiangpu, with 376 officers and 2,481 soldiers. Anhui fielded two infantry regiments, one cavalry camp, and one artillery company at the provincial capital, with 253 officers and 4,155 soldiers. Jiangnan's Ninth Brigade garrisoned one infantry camp and two cavalry companies at the provincial capital, with 789 officers and 8,255 soldiers. Jiangxi garrisoned one infantry brigade and two cavalry companies at the provincial capital, with 231 officers and 4,287 soldiers. Henan's Twenty-ninth Mixed Brigade was garrisoned at the provincial capital with 338 officers and 5,618 soldiers; one infantry brigade plus one camp each of cavalry and artillery were transferred to the capital, with 162 officers and 3,085 soldiers. Hunan garrisoned one infantry brigade and one artillery camp at the provincial capital, with 248 officers and 4,056 soldiers. Hubei's Eighth Brigade was garrisoned at the provincial capital with 702 officers and 10,502 soldiers; the Twenty-first Mixed Brigade held Wuchang, Hanyang, and the Beijing-Hankou Railway line with 288 officers and 4,612 soldiers. Zhejiang garrisoned one infantry brigade at the provincial capital, with 159 officers and 2,384 soldiers. Fujian's Tenth Brigade was garrisoned at the provincial capital, Funing, Yanping, and other posts, with 455 officers and 6,788 soldiers. Yunnan garrisoned one infantry brigade and one artillery camp at the provincial capital and Lin'an, with 238 officers and 4,248 soldiers. Guizhou garrisoned one infantry regiment and one artillery company at the provincial capital, with 107 officers and 1,846 soldiers. Sichuan's infantry at the provincial capital numbered only twelve officers and sixty-one soldiers. Shanxi garrisoned two infantry regiments plus one camp each of cavalry and artillery at the provincial capital, with 262 officers and 4,557 soldiers. Shaanxi garrisoned one infantry brigade and one artillery company at the provincial capital, with 220 officers and 3,936 soldiers. Gansu garrisoned two infantry regiments and one artillery camp at the provincial capital, Hezhou, Guyuan, and Xining, with 221 officers and 4,128 soldiers. Xinjiang garrisoned one infantry brigade, one cavalry regiment, and one artillery camp at the provincial capital, with 167 officers and 2,322 soldiers. The Third Brigade of the Northeast garrisoned Jilin City, Changchun, Ning'an, Yanji, Jinzhou, and other posts with 753 officers and 11,883 soldiers. By Xuantong 3, beyond those already listed, Zhejiang had formed the Twenty-first Brigade, Yunnan the Nineteenth, Sichuan the Seventeenth, Fengtian the Twentieth, Jilin the Twenty-third, Guangdong the Twenty-sixth at the provincial capital, and Guangxi the Twenty-fifth at the provincial capital and Guilin—twenty-six brigades in all, completed in sequence. Before long the Wuchang garrison rose first, provinces across the empire followed suit, and the planned thirty-six brigades were never fully realized.
