郭松林李長樂楊鼎勳唐殿魁唐定奎
Guo Songlin, Li Changle, Yang Dingxun, Tang Diankui, and Tang Dingkui
3
滕嗣武駱國忠
Teng Siwu and Luo Guozhong
4
郭松林，字子美，湖南湘潭人。 咸豐六年，隸曾國荃軍，從援江西，克安福，從剿永新、太和、萬安、蓮花廳、龍泉，敘獎把總。 進圍吉安府城，七年，石達開率悍黨來援，邀擊於吉水三曲灘，松林首陷陣，多斬獲，收復新喻、峽江、吉水。 八年，隨克吉安，擢守備。 九年，克景德、浮梁，賜花翎。 十年，圍安慶，會剿陳玉成於小池驛，進壁集賢關，每戰皆捷。 十一年，克安慶，擢游擊，賜號奮勇巴圖魯。 克廬江、無為、運漕鎮，下沿江要隘，擢參將。
Guo Songlin, styled Zimei, came from Xiangtan in Hunan. In 1856 he joined Zeng Guoquan's forces, marched to relieve Jiangxi, took Anfu, and helped pacify Yongxin, Taihe, Wan'an, Lianhua, and Longquan, for which he received the rank of dartai. When the army pressed the siege of Ji'an, in 1857 Shi Dakai brought a fierce relief force; Songlin led the attack at Sanqu Beach on the Jishui River, was first into the enemy ranks, won heavy kills and captures, and recovered Xinyu, Xiajiang, and Jishui. In 1858 he took part in the fall of Ji'an and was promoted to garrison commander. In 1859 he helped capture Jingde and Fuliang and was awarded the peacock-feather insignia. In 1860 he joined the siege of Anqing, fought Chen Yucheng at Xiaochi Post, pushed forward to hold Jixian Pass, and won every engagement. In 1861 Anqing fell; he was made battalion commander and given the honorific title Fenyong Baturu ("Valiant Baturu"). He helped take Lujiang, Wuwei, and Yuncao Town and seize the vital Yangtze strongpoints, and was promoted to brigade general.
5
同治元年，李鴻章率淮軍八千赴上海，松林從，與偽忠王李秀成偽慕王譚紹光大戰滬西，破賊眾十萬。 會攻太倉，砲擊城隳，士卒爭進，浮橋斷，賊乘之，死數百人，松林力禦，始得收軍。 二年，克太倉，松林敗賊茜涇、支塘，會克崑山、新陽，以總兵用。 李秀成合水陸數十萬援江陰，犯常熟，劉銘傳謀乘賊未定擊之。 賊北自北漍，南至張涇橋，東自陳市，西至長壽，縱橫六七十里，築壘憑河，勢大熾。 銘傳進北漍攻其左，松林進南漍攻其右，周盛波等進麥市橋為中路，黃翼升以水師助之。 松林敗賊陳市，越南漍趨張涇，揮刀盪決，血染衣盡赤，賊大潰走。 銘傳、盛波等同破賊，自顧山以西皆盡，以總兵記名。 尋克江陰，以提督記名。 又破賊緱山、梅村、麻塘橋，松林受矛傷，既而蘇州、無錫皆复，加頭品頂戴。
In 1862 Li Hongzhang led eight thousand Huai Army men to Shanghai; Songlin went with him and, facing the Taiping Loyal King Li Xiucheng and Admiring King Tan Shaoguang, fought a major battle west of the city and routed a rebel host of one hundred thousand. During the assault on Taicang, artillery smashed the wall and troops rushed the breach; the pontoon bridge gave way and rebels surged in, killing several hundred men before Songlin fought them off and pulled the force back. In 1863 Taicang fell; Songlin routed rebels at Qianjing and Zhitang and helped take Kunshan and Xinyang, and was appointed acting regional commander. Li Xiucheng mustered several hundred thousand men by land and water to relieve Jiangyin and threatened Changshu; Liu Mingchuan proposed striking before the rebels could dig in. The rebels stretched from Beigang in the north to Zhangjing Bridge in the south and from Chenshi in the east to Changshou in the west—sixty or seventy li of entrenchments along the waterways—and their position looked formidable. Mingchuan attacked from Beigang on the left, Songlin from Nangang on the right, Zhou Shengbo and others up the center at Maishi Bridge, and Huang Yisheng's fleet supported from the water. Songlin routed the rebels at Chenshi, crossed south of Nangang toward Zhangjing, and hacked his way through until his clothes were soaked red with blood; the enemy broke and fled. Mingchuan, Shengbo, and the rest drove the rebels out until everything west of Gushan was cleared, and Songlin was entered on the rolls as regional commander. Jiangyin fell soon after, and he was entered on the rolls as provincial military commander. He again routed the rebels at Goushan, Meicun, and Matang Bridge; Songlin was wounded by a spear, but Suzhou and Wuxi were soon recovered, and he received the first-rank cap button.
6
三年，克宜興、荊溪，敗賊張渚，毀賊壘，收溧陽，解常熟圍，授福山鎮總兵。 大破三河口賊營，賊爭道，六浮橋盡斷，屍塞河，水為不流。 克常州，進剿浙西，克長興，复湖州，功皆最。 賊走廣德、徽州，合江寧、杭州賊自江西竄閩。 四年，李鴻章檄松林率五千人航海赴援，克漳州、漳浦、雲霄、詔安，賊竄廣東嘉應，遂破滅。
In 1864 he took Yixing and Jingxi, defeated the rebels at Zhangzhu, destroyed their fortifications, recovered Liyang, and lifted the siege of Changshu, and was made regional commander at Fushan Town. He smashed the rebel camp at Sanhekou; as the enemy fled in a crush, six pontoon bridges collapsed under them and corpses choked the river until the water barely moved. Changzhou fell; he pushed into western Zhejiang, took Changxing, and recovered Huzhou, ranking first in merit in each action. The rebels retreated toward Guangde and Huizhou, while survivors from Nanjing and Hangzhou joined forces with rebels driven from Jiangxi and slipped into Fujian. In 1865 Li Hongzhang ordered Songlin to sail south with five thousand men; they took Zhangzhou, Zhangpu, Yunxiao, and Zhao'an, and the rebels who fled into Jiaying, Guangdong, were finally wiped out.
7
五年，曾國荃調松林率新募湘軍剿捻匪於德安，克應城、雲夢，复敗之皁河、楊澤。 追至臼口，中伏，松林傷足，臥地不能起。 將卒不見松林，复闖入陣，負而出之。 弟芳珍戰死。 松林以創重假歸。 六年，創愈，李鴻章令統萬人號武毅軍。 時東捻任柱已斃，餘黨走壽光，松林要擊，破之杞城。 賊沿海南走，阻瀰河，捻酋牛喜子麾白旗賊犯劉銘傳軍； 賴文光麾藍旗賊犯松林軍。 兩軍縱擊，賊大潰，壽光民圩皆出助殺，賊赴瀰河死，浮屍二萬餘，俘萬餘人，奪獲騾馬二萬匹。 賊酋徐昌先、范汝增、任定皆伏誅。 賴文光鳧水南奔，松林疾馳六百里，追至清江。 文光死奔，至揚州瓦窯鋪，為吳毓蘭所擒。 東捻平。
In 1866 Zeng Guoquan sent Songlin with freshly raised Xiang troops to fight the Nian in De'an; they took Yingcheng and Yunmeng and again defeated the bandits at Zaohe and Yangze. Pursuit carried them to Jiukou, where they walked into an ambush; Songlin was shot in the foot and could not get up. When his men could not find him, they charged back into the fight and carried him out. His younger brother Fangzhen was killed in the fighting. Songlin went home on leave to recover from his serious wounds. In 1867, once he had healed, Li Hongzhang put him in command of ten thousand men under the name Wuyi Army ("Army of Martial Resolution"). By then the eastern Nian leader Ren Zhu was dead and the survivors were heading for Shouguang; Songlin intercepted them and crushed them at Qicheng. The bandits fled south along the coast until they were stopped at the Mi River; the Nian chief Niu Xizi led the White Banner force against Liu Mingchuan; while Lai Wenguang led the Blue Banner against Songlin. Both columns pressed the attack and the bandits broke; militia from the Shouguang stockades joined the slaughter, and thousands drowned in the Mi River—over twenty thousand bodies floated on the water, more than ten thousand were taken alive, and twenty thousand pack animals were captured. The bandit leaders Xu Changxian, Fan Ruzeng, and Ren Ding were all put to death. Lai Wenguang swam south to escape; Songlin raced six hundred li in pursuit as far as Qingjiang. Wenguang fled for his life to Wayao Post near Yangzhou, where Wu Yulan captured him. The eastern Nian rebellion was ended.
8
七年春，西捻犯畿輔，松林敗之安平，再破之茌平。 自臨邑築長圍至馬頰河，松林偕潘鼎新、王心安守之，敗賊於海豐，追至德州，歷十六晝夜，斬捕過半。 六月，松林會潘鼎新大破之沙河，俘斬四千。 捻走黃、運、徒駭河間，松林與銘傳縱橫要擊，張總愚赴水死。 西捻平，賜黃馬褂，予輕車都尉世職。 授湖北提督，調直隸。 光緒六年，卒於官，優恤，建專祠，諡武壯。
In the spring of 1868 the western Nian raided the capital districts; Songlin defeated them at Anping and again at Chiping. A cordon was thrown from Linyi to the Majia River, which Songlin held with Pan Dingxin and Wang Xin'an; they routed the bandits at Haifeng and chased them to Dezhou in sixteen days and nights of pursuit, killing or capturing more than half the force. In June he and Pan Dingxin inflicted a crushing defeat at Shahe, killing or capturing four thousand men. The Nian fled among the Yellow, Grand Canal, and Tuhai rivers; Songlin and Mingchuan cut them off wherever they turned, until Zhang Zongyu drowned himself. With the western Nian suppressed, he received the yellow riding jacket and a hereditary Commandant of Light Chariots title. He was appointed provincial commander of Hubei and then transferred to Zhili. He died in office in 1880; the court granted generous mourning honors, built him a dedicated shrine, and gave him the posthumous name Wuzhuang ("Martial and Robust").
9
李長樂，字漢春，安徽盱眙人。 同治元年，以外委從郭松林隸淮軍，充營官。 克柘林、奉賢、南匯、川沙、金山，解松江圍，复青浦，擢千總。 戰四江口，松林軍方泰鎮，長樂率所部深入，近賊壘。 夜半，趣軍士起，曰：「今陷賊中，旦明賊覺，無得脫者。 盍出奇計劫之！ 」遂投火賊幕，鼓角乘之，賊驚擾，長樂奮呼進，大破之。 又設伏黃渡，擊之半濟，又敗之吳淞江南。 四江口圍解，擢都司，賜花翎。
Li Changle, styled Hanchun, came from Xuyi in Anhui. In 1862, serving as an external deputy, he followed Guo Songlin into the Huai Army as battalion commander. He helped take Zhelin, Fengxian, Nanhui, Chuansha, and Jinshan, lifted the siege of Songjiang, and recovered Qingpu, and was promoted to company commander. At the battle of Sijiangkou, while Songlin's main force was at Fangtai, Changle led his men deep behind the lines, almost on top of the rebel camp. At midnight he roused his men and said, "We are trapped in the middle of the enemy camp; at daybreak they will find us and none of us will get out. Why not strike first with a surprise raid? He set fire to the rebel tents, sounded drums and horns, and as the enemy panicked he charged with a war cry and routed them completely. He ambushed them at Huangdu while they were crossing the river, and defeated them again south of the Wusong. The siege at Sijiangkou was lifted; he was promoted to battalion commander and awarded the peacock-feather insignia.
10
二年，進屯常熟王莊，援賊踞陳市，阻官軍進路。 從松林自南漍攻賊右，連破賊營，直趨長涇。 長樂陷陣傷脛，裹創力戰敗賊，擢參將，賜號侃勇巴圖魯。 尋克江陰，規無錫，出新塘橋。 賊憑壘鳴砲俯擊，長樂濡絮裹身越溝進，敗之； 逐奔至亭子橋，刺賊酋黃子隆中肩，又設伏兵敗援賊。 李秀成圍大橋角營，從松林往援，奪其舟，賊退走，盡平梅村諸壘。 會諸軍圍攻無錫，率輕騎掩至，梯城入，黃子隆就擒，長樂獲其子德懋。 尋坐失察部勇，褫職留軍。 進規常州，援總兵唐殿魁於奔牛，解其圍。 三年，敗賊上湖橋，克宜興，复官。 移軍溧陽、金壇，戰皆捷。 回援常熟，解其圍，連破賊於楊舍、華墅、周莊、三河口，會攻常州。 四月，合圍，長樂先登，賊酋陳坤書、黃和錦就擒，復常州，擢副將，賜號尚勇巴圖魯。
In 1863 he advanced to Wangzhuang near Changshu, where relieving rebels held Chenshi and blocked the government advance. He followed Songlin's attack on the rebel right from Nangang, stormed camp after camp, and drove straight for Changjing. Changle led the charge and was wounded in the leg; he bound the wound and kept fighting until the rebels broke; he was made brigade general and given the title Kan'yong Baturu ("Outspoken and Brave Baturu"). Jiangyin fell soon after; he turned toward Wuxi and advanced from Xintang Bridge. The rebels shelled from their ramparts; Changle wrapped himself in wet cotton padding, crossed the ditch under fire, and routed them; pursued them to Tingzi Bridge, stabbed the rebel chief Huang Zilong in the shoulder, and ambushed and defeated the relief column. When Li Xiucheng besieged the Daqiaojiao camp, Changle went with Songlin to relieve it, seized the rebel boats, drove them off, and cleared every fort around Meicun. During the siege of Wuxi he led light cavalry in a surprise assault, scaled the walls, captured Huang Zilong, and took his son Demao prisoner. He was soon stripped of rank for lax discipline among his troops but kept with the army. As the army moved on Changzhou, he relieved regional commander Tang Diankui at Benniu and broke the siege there. In 1864 he defeated the rebels at Shanghu Bridge, took Yixing, and had his rank restored. He shifted operations to Liyang and Jintan and won every engagement there. He returned to relieve Changshu, lifted the siege, and routed the rebels at Yangshe, Huashu, Zhouzhuang, and Sanhekou before joining the assault on Changzhou. In April, as the encirclement closed, Changle was first over the wall; the rebel chiefs Chen Kunshu and Huang Hejin were taken, Changzhou was recovered, and he was made deputy regional commander with the title Shangyong Baturu ("Esteemed and Brave Baturu").
11
從松林進克浙江長興，以總兵記名。 進湖州，破呂山賊。 攻賊酋黃文金於尹隆橋，官軍不利，長樂率三營別屯李家港，保糧道。 賊傾巢來爭，長樂偕易用剛夾擊之，斬賊酋黃十四，破尹隆橋，遂復湖州。 四年春，從松林援福建，戰於海澄赤嶺。 松林分兵為八隊，長樂居中當賊首李世賢，破走之，竄漳州。 長樂進屯古田，據山東形勝，賊悉銳力爭，擊卻之，复漳浦、雲霄。 南趨詔安，破之梅村，復其城，加提督銜。 福建平，旋師江蘇，屯鎮江。
He followed Songlin into Zhejiang and helped take Changxing, and was entered on the rolls as regional commander. Pressing on Huzhou, he routed the rebels at Lüshan. An attack on the rebel chief Huang Wenjin at Yinlong Bridge went badly; Changle took three battalions to hold Lijiagang and keep the supply line open. When the rebels threw their full strength at the position, Changle and Yi Yonggang caught them in a pincer, killed the rebel chief Huang Fourteen, broke through at Yinlong Bridge, and recovered Huzhou. In the spring of 1865 he sailed with Songlin to Fujian and fought at Chiling near Haicheng. Songlin split his force into eight columns; Changle took the center against the rebel chief Li Shixian, routed him, and drove him toward Zhangzhou. Changle pushed into Gutian and held the high ground east of the mountains; when the rebels threw their best troops at him he beat them back and recovered Zhangpu and Yunxiao. He marched south to Zhao'an, defeated the rebels at Meicun, retook the city, and received brevet provincial commander rank. With Fujian pacified, the force returned to Jiangsu and encamped at Zhenjiang.
12
曾國籓督師剿捻匪，松林已歸，長樂代將其眾以從，兼統忠樸三營，為游擊之師，轉戰河南、山東間。 六年，李鴻章代國籓督師，松林復至軍，增松林軍至二十餘營，號武毅軍，長樂所部曰武毅軍前軍。 破任柱於贛榆，要賴文光於濰縣，長樂等並力奮擊，賊鳧水東走，躡至餘家寨，賊受創甚鉅，复要之壽光南北洋河、巨瀰河間，擒斬三萬，文光竄揚州被擒，賜黃馬褂。
When Zeng Guofan took command against the Nian, Songlin had already gone home and Changle led his old troops in the field, also taking the three Zhongpu battalions as a mobile strike force across Henan and Shandong. In 1867 Li Hongzhang replaced Guofan as commander; Songlin rejoined the campaign and expanded his command to more than twenty battalions under the name Wuyi Army, with Changle's unit as the Wuyi vanguard. They broke Ren Zhu at Ganyu and caught Lai Wenguang at Weixian; Changle and the others pressed the attack until the bandits swam east in flight; pursuit reached Yujiazhai and then cut them off between the Yang Rivers and the great Mi River at Shouguang, killing or capturing thirty thousand; Wenguang fled to Yangzhou and was taken, and Changle received the yellow riding jacket.
13
七年，從剿西捻，戰安平，馬軍失利，長樂等以步卒馳援，賊大潰； 追至饒陽楊家村，又要之深州李家村，破其馬隊，斬獲無算。 三月，敗賊大坯山。 援提督陳振邦於大河村，解其圍，追挫之茌平、滄州，援副都統春壽於海豐郝家寨。 六月，追至樂陵，擒總愚子正江、弟得華，戰商河，槍傷總愚。 西捻平，以提督總兵遇缺題奏，晉博奇巴圖魯。 十年，署湖北提督，尋實授。 光緒五年，調湖南。 六年，調直隸。 近畿海防重要，奏令長樂駐蘆臺，扼大沽、北塘門戶。 十五年，卒官，優恤，諡勤勇。
In 1868 he joined the campaign against the western Nian; at Anping, when the cavalry faltered, Changle rushed infantry to their aid and the bandits broke completely; pursuit carried to Yangjia Village in Raoyang and another ambush at Lijia Village in Shenzhou shattered the rebel horse troops, with kills and captures beyond count. In March he defeated the bandits at Dapishan. He relieved provincial commander Chen Zhenbang at Dahecun, chased the bandits back at Chiping and Cangzhou, and rescued vice commander-in-chief Chun Shou at Haojiazhai in Haifeng. In June the pursuit reached Leling, where Zongyu's son Zhengjiang and brother Dehua were captured; at Shanghe Changle wounded Zongyu with a musket. With the western Nian suppressed, he was recommended for the next provincial or regional commander vacancy and advanced to Boqi Baturu. In 1871 he served as acting provincial commander of Hubei and soon received the full appointment. In 1879 he was transferred to Hunan. In 1880 he was transferred to Zhili. Because coastal defense near the capital mattered greatly, the court ordered Changle to Lutai to guard the approaches to Dagu and Beitang. He died in office in 1889; the court granted generous mourning honors and the posthumous name Qinyong ("Diligent and Brave").
14
楊鼎勳，字少銘，四川華陽人。 咸豐二年，應募從軍，初隸湖北按察使李孟群，克漢陽，擢把總。 七年，隸提督鮑超軍。 八年，戰湖口，擢千總。 十年，鮑超與陳玉成大戰小池驛，鼎勳見玉成立陣中指揮，獨從壯士十數人突前擊之，玉成駭走，复太湖、潛山兩城。 敘功，賜花翎。 李秀成踞黟縣，鼎勳擊賊城下，奪門入，大軍繼之，復其城。 十一年，復建德，擢都司。 破安慶赤岡嶺賊壘，擢游擊。 初，小池驛之戰，鮑超嘉其功，令將五百人，所向有功。 諸將嫉之，譖於超。
Yang Dingxun, styled Shaoming, came from Huayang in Sichuan. In 1852 he enlisted, first under Hubei surveillance commissioner Li Mengqun; when Hanyang fell he was promoted to dartai. In 1857 he joined provincial commander Bao Chao's army. In 1858 he fought at Hukou and was promoted to company commander. In 1860 Bao Chao fought Chen Yucheng at Xiaochi Post; Dingxun saw Yucheng directing from within the enemy line and led a dozen picked men in a headlong charge that sent Yucheng fleeing and recovered Taihu and Qianshan. For this he was awarded the peacock-feather insignia. When Li Xiucheng held Yi County, Dingxun stormed the walls, forced a gate, and led the main force in to retake the city. In 1861 he helped recover Jiande and was promoted to battalion commander. He stormed the rebel fort at Chigangling near Anqing and was made battalion commander. After the fight at Xiaochi Post, Bao Chao praised his performance and gave him five hundred men, with whom he won success wherever he was sent. Other commanders grew jealous and slandered him to Bao Chao.
15
同治元年，李鴻章督師上海，遂去超從淮軍。 虹橋、四江口諸戰有功，累擢副將。 募淮勇千人，號勳字軍，屯金山張堰，扼平湖乍浦要衝，習西洋槍隊，每戰輒為軍鋒。 二年，破新昌賊壘，連克楓涇，斬賊四千，生擒五百； 再戰西塘，裹創奮擊，大敗之，擢總兵，賜號鋒勇巴圖魯。 從程學啟規蘇州，鼎勳攻下城外堅壘，蘇州复，加提督銜。 三年，會克宜興、荊溪、溧陽，解常熟、無錫圍。 攻常州，賊因蘇州之殺降，惟死守。 鼎勳以蜀人將淮勇，懼諸將輕己，每戰輒先，晝夜環攻，盡毀城外賊壘，血戰三日。 造浮橋，率死士先登城，砲彈洞胸達背，左右扶之，絕而復蘇，遂克常州，以提督記名簡放。 創愈，進克浦口，复長興，招降湖州賊黨，會克其城。 追賊至皖境，克廣德。 四年，偕郭松林援福建，攻烏頭門賊壘，复漳州，授江蘇蘇松鎮總兵。
In 1862, when Li Hongzhang took command at Shanghai, he left Bao Chao and joined the Huai Army. He distinguished himself at Hongqiao, Sijiangkou, and other engagements and was steadily promoted to deputy regional commander. He raised a thousand Huai braves as the Xunzi Army, held Zhangyan in Jinshan to block the Pinghu–Zhabu corridor, drilled them in Western musketry, and led the van in every fight. In 1863 he smashed the rebel fort at Xinchang, took Fengjing in succession, killed four thousand rebels, and captured five hundred alive; then fought again at Xitang, bound his wounds, and charged until the enemy broke; he was made regional commander and given the title Fengyong Baturu. He joined Cheng Xueqi's campaign against Suzhou, took the outer fortifications, and when Suzhou fell received brevet provincial commander rank. In 1864 he helped take Yixing, Jingxi, and Liyang and lift the sieges of Changshu and Wuxi. At Changzhou the rebels, remembering the massacre of surrendering troops at Suzhou, fought only to the death. A Sichuanese leading Huai troops, Dingxun feared his peers would despise him, so he always led the assault, besieged day and night, destroyed every outer fort, and fought three days of bloody combat. He threw a pontoon bridge across the moat and led picked men up the wall first; a round passed through his chest and out his back, and his aides caught him as he collapsed, but he came to and Changzhou fell; he was entered on the rolls for provincial commander appointment. Once healed, he took Pukou, recovered Changxing, induced Huzhou rebels to surrender, and helped capture the city. Pursuing the enemy into Anhui, he took Guangde. In 1865 he sailed with Guo Songlin to Fujian, stormed the fort at Wutoumen, retook Zhangzhou, and was made regional commander of Susong in Jiangsu.
16
五年，調赴河南剿捻匪，敗賊硃仙鎮，躡擊至定陶、睢寧。 六年，破賊於黃陂、孝感，擢浙江提督，調湖南。 十月，破賊於山東濰縣，追至​​夏灣，賊酋陳懷忠乞降，分軍出周家寨襲賊，大破之。 追擊於諸城、膠州。 東捻平，論功，予騎都尉世職。 七年，馳援畿輔，破捻匪於安平，追至楊家村，降賊酋張志清。 偕郭松林擊賊濬縣大邳山，又敗之衛輝，陣斬賊酋王建瀛、熊八，擒悍賊何士喜、周久於龍王廟。 賊竄山東，自德州趨天津，鼎勳守運河，修牆浚壕，賊來犯，輒擊走之。 會舊傷發，遽卒，數日而西捻平。 李鴻章疏聞，贈太子少保，諡忠勤，建專祠。
In 1866 he was sent to Henan against the Nian, defeated them at Zhuxian Town, and pursued them to Dingtao and Suining. In 1867 he defeated bandits at Huangpi and Xiaogan, was made provincial commander of Zhejiang, and transferred to Hunan. In October he routed the bandits at Weixian in Shandong and pursued them to Xiawan; when the chief Chen Huaizhong offered to surrender, Dingxun sent a detachment from Zhoujiazhai to strike the main body and broke them completely. The pursuit carried through Zhucheng and Jiaozhou. After the eastern Nian were suppressed, he received a hereditary Commandant of Cavalry title for his service. In 1868 he hurried to the capital districts, defeated the Nian at Anping, pursued them to Yangjia Village, and accepted the surrender of the bandit chief Zhang Zhiqing. With Guo Songlin he fought at Dapishan in Jun County and again at Weihui, killed the chiefs Wang Jianying and Xiong Ba in battle, and captured the fierce bandits He Shixi and Zhou Jiu at Longwang Temple. When the bandits slipped into Shandong and headed from Dezhou toward Tianjin, Dingxun held the Grand Canal line, strengthened walls and ditches, and beat back every attack. His old wounds reopened and he died suddenly; the western Nian were suppressed a few days later. Li Hongzhang reported his death to the throne; the court posthumously made him Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent, gave him the name Zhongqin, and built him a dedicated shrine.
17
唐殿魁，字藎臣，安徽合肥人。 咸豐十年，巡撫翁同書檄率鄉團援壽州，力解城圍。 又從克合肥三河汛，解六安圍，敘千總。 同治元年，李鴻章率淮軍援上海，殿魁從，隸劉銘傳，克南匯、川沙、奉賢、金山衛、柘林五城，積功累擢都司，賜花翎。 二年，克江陰楊舍汛城，擢游擊，賜號振勇巴圖魯。 复江陰縣城，擢參將。 尋克無錫，以總兵記名。
Tang Diankui, styled Jinchen, came from Hefei in Anhui. In 1860, on orders from Governor Weng Tonghe, he led local militia to relieve Shouzhou and broke the siege. He also helped take Sanhe Post at Hefei and lift the siege of Lu'an, and was promoted to company commander. In 1862 he joined Li Hongzhang's Huai Army at Shanghai under Liu Mingchuan, helped take Nanhui, Chuansha, Fengxian, Jinshanwei, and Zhelin, and rose to battalion commander with the peacock-feather insignia. In 1863 he took the Yangshe garrison in Jiangyin, was made battalion commander, and received the title Zhenyong Baturu. He recovered the Jiangyin county seat and was promoted to brigade general. Wuxi fell soon after, and he was entered on the rolls as regional commander.
18
從劉銘傳攻常州，銘傳受重傷，令殿魁偕副將黃桂蘭督兵進。 甫至奔牛鎮，而常州、丹陽兩路賊麕至，圍之。 殿魁據石營依河岸，壁壘悉為賊轟毀，堅守二十餘日。 銘傳裹創往救，殿魁從內夾擊，苦戰數日，圍始解。 三年，克常州，生擒賊首陳坤書，以提督記名。 四年，增募所部至三千人。 從劉銘傳渡淮剿捻匪，破張寨賊壘。 五年，授浙江衢州鎮總兵。 追賊至湖北，克黃陂。 捻匪自山東回竄，銘傳督兵追至烏官屯，殿魁繼進，殺賊五百餘。 六年，調廣西右江鎮。
During the assault on Changzhou, when Mingchuan was badly wounded, he put Diankui and deputy commander Huang Guilan in charge of the advance. He had barely reached Benniu when rebel columns from Changzhou and Danyang converged and surrounded him. Diankui held a stone camp on the riverbank; rebel fire demolished his works, but he held out for more than twenty days. Mingchuan came with his wounds bound, Diankui attacked from inside, and after several days of hard fighting the encirclement was broken. In 1864 Changzhou fell and the rebel chief Chen Kunshu was captured alive; Diankui was entered on the rolls as provincial commander. In 1865 he expanded his command to three thousand men. He crossed the Huai with Liu Mingchuan against the Nian and stormed the fort at Zhangzhai. In 1866 he was appointed regional commander at Quzhou in Zhejiang. Pursuing the bandits into Hubei, he took Huangpi. When the Nian doubled back from Shandong, Mingchuan pursued them to Wuguantun and Diankui followed up, killing more than five hundred. In 1867 he was transferred to Youjiang in Guangxi.
19
捻首張總愚竄安陸。 銘傳與鮑超約會戰於永漋河，銘傳欲先出，殿魁請少待，不從。 超未至，銘軍先遇賊，部將田履安、李錫增戰沒。 殿魁戰小挫，受重傷。 及聞霆軍大捷，复裹創力戰，遂殞於陣。 贈太子少保，予騎都尉兼雲騎尉世職，諡忠壯，建專祠。
The Nian chief Zhang Zongyu fled into Anlu. Mingchuan and Bao Chao had agreed to meet the enemy at the Yongzhang River; Mingchuan wanted to attack first, and though Diankui urged him to wait, he refused. Bao Chao had not yet arrived when Mingchuan's force met the bandits; generals Tian Lü'an and Li Xizeng were killed. Diankui's column was driven back and he was badly wounded. When word came of Bao Chao's great victory, he bound his wounds and fought on until he was killed in action. The court posthumously made him Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent, granted hereditary Commandant of Cavalry and Cloud Cavalry Captain titles, gave him the name Zhongzhuang, and built a dedicated shrine.
20
唐定奎，字俊侯，殿魁弟。 偕兄轉戰江蘇。 從劉銘傳剿捻於山東、河南、安徽、湖北，積功累擢副將，賜花翎。 同治六年，殿魁戰歿永漋河，定奎方省母回裡，奔赴軍，誓殺賊復仇，遂代領兄舊部，轉戰河南、山東。 六年，殄任柱於贛榆，破賴文光於壽光，所部殺賊最多。 東捻平，以提督記名。 七年，從剿西捻於直隸、山東，賜號呼敦巴圖魯。 銘軍凱旋，告歸終養。 九年，丁母憂。 劉銘傳赴陝西剿回匪，調定奎接統銘武軍，定奎請終制，命俟陝西軍事平，回籍終制。 十年，定奎回防徐州。
Tang Dingkui, styled Junhou, was Diankui's younger brother. He fought alongside his brother across Jiangsu. Under Liu Mingchuan he fought the Nian in Shandong, Henan, Anhui, and Hubei, rose to deputy regional commander, and received the peacock-feather insignia. In 1867 Diankui was killed at the Yongzhang River; Dingkui was home visiting his mother, hurried back to camp, swore to avenge his brother, took over the old command, and fought on in Henan and Shandong. That year his troops helped destroy Ren Zhu at Ganyu and break Lai Wenguang at Shouguang, and his unit accounted for more kills than any other. After the eastern Nian were suppressed, he was entered on the rolls as provincial commander. In 1868 he joined the campaign against the western Nian in Zhili and Shandong and received the title Hudun Baturu. When Mingchuan's army returned victorious, he asked to go home and care for his parents. In 1870 he went into mourning for his mother. When Liu Mingchuan went to Shaanxi against Muslim rebels, Dingkui was ordered to take the Mingwu Army; he asked to finish mourning first and was told to wait until the Shaanxi campaign ended, then return home. In 1871 he returned to garrison Xuzhou.
21
十三年，日本擾台灣，生番滋事。 船政大臣沈葆楨奏請援師，李鴻章薦定奎率所部往。 七月，至台灣，駐鳳山，擇險分屯。 龜紋番社引日兵與刺桐腳莊民尋仇相閧，定奎示以兵威，日人引去。 時疫流行，士卒先後死千餘人，定奎拊循周至，兵氣不衰，賜黃馬褂。
In 1874 Japan raided Taiwan and indigenous tribes rose in revolt. Naval minister Shen Baozhen asked for reinforcements, and Li Hongzhang recommended Dingkui to lead his troops to Taiwan. In July he landed in Taiwan, based himself at Fengshan, and posted troops at key strongpoints. When the Guiwen tribe drew Japanese troops into a feud with villagers at Citongjiao, Dingkui showed force and the Japanese pulled back. An epidemic killed more than a thousand of his men, but Dingkui nursed the troops so carefully that morale held, and he received the yellow riding jacket.
22
楓港、獅頭諸社番屢出戕害良民，光緒元年，游擊王開俊進剿，中伏死。 內外番社結黨劫殺，各社就撫，皆懷觀望。 定奎分遣七營屯東港南勢湖，自率四營當其衝，葆楨檄諸軍並聽節制。 定奎上書陳兵事，略曰：「逆番晝伏莽中，夜燎山頂，精於標槍，伺間輒發。 專恃深林密箐，狙擊我師，我進彼隱，我退彼見。 今欲掃其巢穴，必先翦其荊棘。 宜增募土勇，導引兵丁，隨山刊木，務絕根株，然後分道進兵，草薙擒狝。 其有奸民接濟鹽米火藥者，按軍法，庶幾一舉可以成功。 」葆楨據以入告。 於是開山進兵，攻克萃山、竹坑、本武諸社。 獅頭社猶負險抗拒，定奎令諸將得險即守，自剿獅頭兩社，別遣師扼斷外援，遂攻下之。 移營駐守，被脅十餘社皆歸命，給衣履酒食，譯示朝廷威德，咸受約束。 設招撫局，示約七條，曰：遵薙發，編戶口，獻凶逆，禁仇殺，立總目，墾番地，設番塾。 以龜紋番酋充諸社總目，赦其脅從。 台南大定，詔褒獎，命內渡休養士卒。 授直隸正定鎮總兵。 尋擢福建陸路提督。
Aboriginal bands around Fenggang and Shitou kept killing civilians; in 1875 battalion commander Wang Kaijun led a punitive expedition and was killed in an ambush. Interior and coastal tribes formed raiding bands; even those who came in to submit remained wary and undecided. Dingkui posted seven battalions at Donggang and Nanshihu, kept four under his own command on the main axis, and Baozhen placed all forces under his orders. Dingkui wrote to the court on the campaign, saying in part: "The hostile tribes hide in the brush by day, signal from the hilltops at night, are expert with javelins, and attack at every chance. They depend on deep forest and narrow ravines to ambush our columns: when we advance they vanish, when we pull back they show themselves. To destroy their strongholds we must first clear the jungle that shelters them. We should raise more local scouts to guide the troops, cut the forest as we advance along the ridges, root them out completely, then attack in separate columns and cut them down. Traitors who supply them salt, grain, and gunpowder must be punished under military law; only then can we hope to succeed in one campaign." Baozhen forwarded the memorial to Beijing. He then cut roads into the mountains and took the communities of Cuishan, Zhukeng, Benwu, and others. The Shitou tribes still held the high ground; Dingkui told his officers to secure every advantage gained, led the assault on the two Shitou communities himself, sent another column to block outside help, and took them. He moved camp to hold the region; more than ten coerced communities submitted; he gave them clothes, food, and wine, had interpreters explain imperial authority, and brought them all under control. He set up a pacification office and announced seven rules: adopt Qing hairstyle, register households, hand over criminals, end blood feuds, appoint headmen, open tribal land to farming, and establish tribal schools. He made the Guiwen chief headman over the communities and pardoned those who had been forced to join the revolt. With southern Taiwan pacified, the court praised his service and ordered him to bring his troops back to the mainland to recuperate. He was appointed regional commander at Zhengding in Zhili. He was soon promoted to provincial commander of Fujian's land forces.
23
沈葆楨調兩江總督，奏統所部駐防江陰。 九年，傷發乞休，不允。 法越用兵事起，海防戒嚴，詔促力疾赴防。 十一年，和議定，病請開缺，允之。 十三年，卒，優恤，諡果介。
When Shen Baozhen became governor-general of Liangjiang, he had Dingkui's troops stationed at Jiangyin under his command. In 1883 his old wounds flared and he asked to retire, but the court refused. When war broke out between France and Vietnam, coastal defenses were mobilized and he was ordered to take up his post despite illness. In 1885 peace was made; he resigned on grounds of illness and was allowed to step down. He died in 1887; the court granted generous mourning honors and the posthumous name Guojie.
24
滕嗣武，湖南麻陽人。 咸豐初，從軍湖北。 十年，小池驛之戰，功多，超擢都司。 從攻安慶，嗣武率所部扼要築砲壘，壘未成，賊突出萬餘來爭，嗣武力擊破之。 十一年，克安慶，敘功擢參將。 同治元年，改隸淮軍，從李鴻章至上海，解松江圍，賜號偉勇巴圖魯。 屯北簳山扼賊衝，賊犯寶山，與諸軍夾擊破之，進拔南匯，以總兵記名。
Teng Siwu came from Mayang in Hunan. In the early Xianfeng era he enlisted in Hubei. In 1860, at Xiaochi Post, his merit earned him an exceptional promotion to battalion commander. During the siege of Anqing he seized a key point to build batteries; before they were finished more than ten thousand rebels rushed out to take them, and Siwu beat them back. When Anqing fell in 1861 he was promoted to brigade general for his service. In 1862 he joined the Huai Army, followed Li Hongzhang to Shanghai, lifted the siege of Songjiang, and received the title Weiyong Baturu. He held Beigan Mountain to block the enemy line; when rebels struck Baoshan he helped rout them in a pincer, took Nanhui, and was entered on the rolls as regional commander.
25
二年，偕程學啟規蘇州，敗賊於正義鎮。 地當要衝，以嗣武守之，輔以水師，分軍伏橋口伺賊。 崑山賊勢蹙，啟西門遁。 伏起，水師以巨砲環擊，賊大潰，立復其城。 移軍會攻江陰。 賊自無錫來援，連營數十，柵壘棋布。 軍分三路進，嗣武率八營當中路，攻​​麥市橋，以輕兵伏河堤，燃砲毀賊壘，賊潰走，追及之三巴橋，殲獲殆盡。 進次無錫城下，賊首李世賢以全軍拒戰。 嗣武身先士卒，怒馬突陣，敗之謝家橋，又敗之蕩口。 賊退據硃王橋堅守，嗣武出奇兵襲擊，擒斬千餘，加提督銜。 既而克無錫，以提督記名。
In 1863 he joined Cheng Xueqi's campaign against Suzhou and defeated the rebels at Zhengyi Town. The position was strategically vital; Siwu held it with naval support while a detachment lay in ambush at the bridge. Hard pressed at Kunshan, the rebels opened the west gate and fled. The ambush sprang up, the fleet shelled them with heavy guns, the enemy broke completely, and Kunshan was retaken at once. He moved his troops to join the assault on Jiangyin. Relief forces came from Wuxi, pitching dozens of linked camps with fortifications spread across the ground like chessmen. The army advanced in three columns; Siwu led eight battalions up the center against Maishi Bridge, hid light troops along the embankment, burned out the rebel works with heated shot, routed the enemy, pursued them to Sanba Bridge, and killed or captured nearly all of them. He advanced and camped below Wuxi, where the rebel chief Li Shixian met him with his entire force. Siwu led from the front, charging on a fiery horse, and routed them at Xiejia Bridge and again at Dangkou. The rebels fell back to hold Zhuwang Bridge; Siwu sent a surprise column, killed or captured more than a thousand, and received brevet provincial commander rank. Wuxi fell soon after, and he was entered on the rolls as provincial commander.
26
三年，會攻常州，破援賊於奔牛鎮，攻下宜興、荊溪，嗣武傷右股。 四月，會圍常州，嗣武當南門，砲毀城垣，克之。 七年，從李鴻章剿捻。 畿輔事平。 八年，授湖北鄖陽鎮總兵。 十一年，卒，賜卹，諡武慎。
In 1864 he joined the assault on Changzhou, routed relief forces at Benniu, took Yixing and Jingxi, and was wounded in the right thigh. In April, as Changzhou was encircled, Siwu held the south gate, artillery breached the wall, and the city fell. In 1868 he joined Li Hongzhang's campaign against the Nian. The fighting around the capital was brought to an end. In 1869 he was appointed regional commander at Yunyang in Hubei. He died in 1872; the court granted mourning honors and the posthumous name Wushen.
27
駱國忠，安徽鳳陽人。 初陷於粵匪，授偽職，知賊必敗，陰圖反正。 常熟久為賊踞，福山與狼山夾江對峙，賊設屯以扼後路，國忠任城守。 同治元年，李鴻章蒞江蘇，兵威日振，國忠因水師游擊周興隆舉城薙發降。 鴻章令興隆、國忠選驍健萬人，分守水陸要衝，以防蘇州竄賊。 福山守賊胡經元、江勝海原約俱降，國忠遣人召之，比至福山，不得入。 國忠夜率兵往，令其弟國孝攻其南，自與興隆攻其北，斷賊登舟之路，槍殪賊將侯得龍，賊舟師遁走。 經元、勝海殺賊渠數人，率所部出，與國忠合。 國孝越重壕毀賊壘； 興隆等分兵盡拔許浦、白茅、徐涇諸壘，賊將錢壽仁亦自太倉率所部二千詣鴻章降。 總兵鞠耀乾率師船泊徐涇，千總袁光政入城助守。
Luo Guozhong came from Fengyang in Anhui. He had first been caught up with the Taiping rebels and given a false rank, but seeing they were doomed, he secretly planned to defect. Changshu had long been in rebel hands; Fushan and Langshan faced each other across the river, and the rebels posted garrisons to block the rear; Guozhong was put in charge of the city's defense. In 1862, as Li Hongzhang's power in Jiangsu grew, Guozhong surrendered the city through naval commander Zhou Xinglong, adopting the Qing hairstyle as a sign of submission. Li Hongzhang had them pick ten thousand tough fighters to hold key points on land and water against rebels slipping out of Suzhou. The Fushan garrison commanders Hu Jingyuan and Jiang Shenghai had agreed to surrender, but when Guozhong summoned them to Fushan they could not get in. Guozhong marched by night, sent his brother Guoxiao against the south while he and Xinglong attacked from the north, cut off access to the boats, killed the rebel commander Hou Delong, and drove off the rebel fleet. Jingyuan and Shenghai killed several rebel leaders, brought their men out, and joined Guozhong. Guoxiao crossed the deep moats and smashed the rebel works; Xinglong's columns took the forts at Xupu, Baimao, and Xujing, and rebel general Qian Shouren came from Taicang with two thousand men to surrender to Li Hongzhang. Regional commander Ju Yaoqian brought gunboats to Xujing, and company commander Yuan Guangzheng entered the city to reinforce the defense.
28
十二月，李秀成等以眾數万自蘇州來攻，連營十餘裡。 國忠乞濟師，常勝軍五百人自海道往援，而賊由江陰再陷福山，聲援隔絕。 鴻章令潘鼎新、劉銘傳、張樹珊以三千人趨福山，與黃翼升水師並進。 福山城小而堅，攻之不下。 常熟被圍愈亟，國忠斂兵入城，興隆屯城西虞山相犄角，為死守計。 二年，賊以砲壞城東垣，國忠力拒不得入，樹雲梯緣城，亦擊卻之。 賊增壘掘隧，數道並進，城危甚。 會鼎新、銘傳諸軍急攻福山，賊分兵赴援，留者僅數千，國忠乃開城出戰，毀賊壘，擒其渠硃衣點。 福山既克，諸軍來會，城圍始解。 捷聞，優詔嘉獎，擢授國忠副將，加總兵銜，編降眾為忠字八營。 會攻江陰，戰甚力，既克，賜號勁勇巴圖魯。 署京口水師副將，留守江陰。 三年，破丹陽援賊，以總兵記名。 尋克常州，以積勞傷發，乞假歸。
In December Li Xiucheng marched from Suzhou with tens of thousands of men and pitched a camp line more than ten li long. Guozhong asked for reinforcements; five hundred Ever-Victorious Army men came by sea, but rebels from Jiangyin retook Fushan and cut him off from help. Li Hongzhang sent Pan Dingxin, Liu Mingchuan, and Zhang Shushan with three thousand men toward Fushan, supported by Huang Yisheng's fleet. Fushan was small but stout and resisted assault. As the siege of Changshu tightened, Guozhong pulled his men inside the walls while Xinglong held Yushan west of the city in mutual support, preparing to fight to the last. In 1863 rebel artillery smashed the eastern wall, but Guozhong held them out; when they raised scaling ladders along the ramparts, he drove them back again. The rebels built more works and dug tunnels, pressing the attack on several fronts, and the city was in grave danger. When Dingxin and Mingchuan stormed Fushan, the rebels sent most of their force to relieve it, leaving only a few thousand; Guozhong sallied out, destroyed their works, and captured the ringleader Zhuyi Dian. After Fushan fell the relieving armies arrived and the siege was finally broken. News of the victory brought an imperial commendation; Guozhong was made deputy regional commander with brevet regional commander rank, and his surrendered troops were organized into eight Zhongzi battalions. He fought hard in the assault on Jiangyin and, after its fall, received the title Jinyong Baturu. He served as acting deputy commander of the Jingkou naval force and stayed to garrison Jiangyin. In 1864 he routed relief forces from Danyang and was entered on the rolls as regional commander. Changzhou fell soon after, but overwork reopened his old wounds and he went home on leave.
29
五年，從劉銘傳剿捻匪，轉戰湖北、河南、山東，所鄉有功。 六年，東捻平，以提督記名，賜黃馬褂。 九年，銘傳督辦陝西軍務，調國忠從行。 十二年，卒於乾州軍中，賜卹，諡勇肅。
In 1866 he joined Liu Mingchuan against the Nian and fought across Hubei, Henan, and Shandong with steady success. When the eastern Nian were suppressed in 1867, he was entered on the rolls as provincial commander and received the yellow riding jacket. In 1870, when Mingchuan took charge of operations in Shaanxi, Guozhong was ordered to go with him. He died in camp at Qianzhou in 1873; the court granted mourning honors and the posthumous name Yongsu.
30
論曰：郭松林、李長樂、楊鼎勳、滕嗣武皆由湘、楚舊部改隸淮軍，平吳、平捻，卓著聲績。 唐殿魁淮軍驍將，惜未竟功。 定奎席兄餘光，名位轉出其上，固有幸有不幸哉。 駱國忠智勇堅毅，識時為傑，當時名滿江南，成績可紀也。
The historian remarks: Guo Songlin, Li Changle, Yang Dingxun, and Teng Siwu all transferred from old Xiang and Chu units into the Huai Army and won outstanding renown pacifying Jiangnan and suppressing the Nian. Tang Diankui was a fierce Huai Army fighter who died before his full measure of achievement. Dingkui built on his brother's reputation yet surpassed him in rank and fame—was that fortune or misfortune? Luo Guozhong was intelligent, brave, and resolute, a man who read the times rightly; his name filled Jiangnan in his day, and his record stands as one worth preserving.
