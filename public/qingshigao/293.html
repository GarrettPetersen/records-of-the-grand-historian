李紱蔡珽謝濟世陳學海
Li Fu, Cai Ting, Xie Jishi, and Chen Xuehai
3
李紱，字巨來，江西臨川人。 少孤貧，好學，讀書經目成誦。 康熙四十八年，成進士，改庶吉士，散館授編修。 累遷侍講學士。 五十九年，擢內閣學士，尋遷左副都御史，仍兼學士。 六十年，充會試副考官。 出榜日，黃霧風霾，上語大學士等曰：「此榜或有亂臣賊子，否亦當有讀書積學之士不得中式，怨氣所致。 」命磨勘試卷，劣者停殿試。 又賜滿洲舉人留保、直隸舉人王蘭生進士。 下第舉子群聚紱門，投瓦石喧閧。 御史舒庫疏劾，下部議，責紱匿不奏，奪官，發永定河工效力。 雍正元年，特命复官，署吏部侍郎，赴山東催漕。 尋授兵部侍郎。 上令截留湖南等省漕糧於天津收貯，旋又命估價出糶。
Li Fu, whose style was Julai, came from Linchuan in Jiangxi. Left fatherless while still young and in poverty, he devoted himself to study and could recite a passage after a single reading. In Kangxi 48 he became a jinshi, entered the Hanlin as a bachelor, and on completing his academy term received appointment as a revising editor. He rose in stages to the post of Reader-in-Waiting. In year 59 he was promoted to Grand Secretariat academician and shortly thereafter moved to Left Vice Censor-in-Chief, still holding his academician title. In year 60 he served as deputy examiner for the metropolitan civil service examination. When the examination results were posted, yellow haze and windblown dust filled the air. The emperor told the grand secretaries and his colleagues, "This cohort may hold traitors and scoundrels—or else learned scholars who were shut out of the degrees, and their resentment has brought this on." " He ordered the papers re-examined and disqualified the poorest candidates from the palace test. He further conferred jinshi degrees on the Manchu provincial graduate Liubao and the Zhili graduate Wang Lansheng. Candidates who had failed clustered at Fu's gate, hurling tiles and stones amid an uproar. Censor Shu Ku impeached him. After ministry review Fu was found to have suppressed the riot without reporting it, deprived of his post, and sent to serve at the Yongding River project. In Yongzheng 1 he was specially reinstated as acting Vice Minister of Personnel and dispatched to Shandong to press the grain transport. He was soon appointed Vice Minister of War. The emperor directed that transport grain from Hunan and other provinces be stored at Tianjin, then soon ordered it sold off at market appraisal.
4
二年四月，授廣西巡撫。 奏言：「廣西賀縣大金、蕉木二山產礦砂，五十里外為廣東梅峒汛，又數里為宿塘寨，礦徒盤據，時時竊發。 臣方擬嚴禁，聞總督孔毓珣條陳開採，因而中止。 將來或恐滋事。 」毓珣奏同時至，廷議寢其事。 上命以諭毓珣者示紱，令協力禁止。 紱疏陳練兵，列舉嚴賞罰、演陣法、習用槍砲、豫備帳房鑼鍋諸事，上嘉其留心武備。 康熙中，巡撫陳元龍奏請開捐，都計收穀百十七萬石有奇，石折銀一兩一錢，而發州縣買穀石止三錢，不足以糴。 至紱上官，尚虧四萬餘石，紱奏請限一月補足。 會提督韓良輔條奏墾荒，下紱議，紱請以桂林、柳州、梧州、南寧四府收貯捐穀動支為開墾費。 上曰：「朕觀紱意，不過借開墾以銷捐穀。 當時陳元龍等首尾不清，朕知之甚詳。 應令元龍等往廣西料理。 」並諭紱詳察，毋隱諱瞻徇，自承虧空。 尋紱奏察出督撫、司道、府廳分得羨餘銀八十二萬有奇，勒限分償，上嘉紱秉公執正。 紱在吏部時，年羹堯子富等捐造營房，下部議敘，不肯從優，為羹堯所嫉； 及上命天津截漕估糶盈餘銀五千交守道桑成鼎貯庫，紱至廣西，成鼎使齎以畀紱。 紱具摺送直隸巡撫李維鈞會奏。 維鈞匿不上，紱乃奏聞。 先是，羹堯朝京師，入對，舉此訐紱，謂紱乾沒。 上以問維鈞，維鈞言紱取數百金治裝，餘尚貯庫。 紱奏至，上謂維鈞與羹堯比，欲陷紱。 諭獎紱，命留充公用。
In the fourth month of year 2 he was made Governor of Guangxi. He reported: "At Dajin and Jiaomu mountains in He County, Guangxi, ore is mined. Fifty li away stands Guangdong's Meidong garrison; a few li beyond that lies Sutang Stockade. Miners hold these sites and raid the countryside from time to time." "I was preparing a strict ban when word came that Governor-General Kong Yuqin had submitted plans to open the mines, so I halted enforcement." "Violence may yet follow." " Yuqin's own memorial arrived simultaneously, and the court set the proposal aside. The emperor sent Fu the instructions given Yuqin and told both men to work together to suppress mining. Fu submitted a plan for drilling troops, detailing strict rewards and punishments, formation practice, musket and artillery drill, and the provisioning of tents, gongs, and camp kettles. The throne commended his care for military preparedness. Under Kangxi, Governor Chen Yuanlong had opened the sale of offices for grain contributions. The treasury took in more than 1,170,000 shi, reckoned at 1.1 taels of silver per shi, yet when the prefectures and counties went to purchase grain the price set was only 3 cash per shi—far too little to fill the quotas. When Fu arrived there was still a shortfall of over forty thousand shi; he asked for one month to restore the full amount. Regional Commander Han Liangfu meanwhile submitted plans for opening wasteland. The matter went to Fu for review; Fu proposed spending contribution grain held in Guilin, Liuzhou, Wuzhou, and Nanning. The emperor said, "Fu's scheme is simply to clear the contribution shortfall under the cover of land reclamation." "Chen Yuanlong's accounts were a tangle from first to last—I know every thread of it." "Yuanlong and his associates should go to Guangxi and put the books in order." " The emperor also warned Fu to investigate without concealment, favoritism, or shouldering the loss himself. Fu soon reported that provincial officials had divided more than 820,000 taels in surpluses among themselves and ordered repayment on a fixed schedule. The throne praised his impartiality. At the Ministry of Personnel, Gengyao's sons and others had donated funds to erect barracks. When the ministry considered reward for the gift, Fu refused preferential treatment, and Gengyao came to resent him; Later the emperor ordered five thousand taels of surplus silver from the Tianjin grain operation placed with Surveillance Commissioner Sang Chengdian. When Fu reached Guangxi, Chengdian sent the sum to him. Fu drew up a memorial and sent it to Zhili Governor Li Weijun for joint presentation. Weijun withheld it, so Fu reported directly to the throne. Earlier Gengyao, on arriving at court, had denounced Fu on this ground, claiming embezzlement. The emperor questioned Weijun, who said Fu had taken a few hundred taels for traveling expenses while the remainder stayed in the treasury. Fu's memorial arrived, and the emperor concluded Weijun was conspiring with Gengyao to frame him. He praised Fu and directed that the silver be kept for public purposes.
5
三年六月，紱奏言：「太平、思恩府界流言安南內亂。 有潘騰龍者，自言為莫姓後，其黨黃把勢、陳亂彈等煽誘為亂。 嚴飭將吏捕治。 」上諭曰：「封疆之內，宜整理振作。 至於安邊柔遠，最忌貪利圖功，當慎之又慎！ 」九月，奏：「瑤、僮頑梗，修仁十排、天河三甿為尤甚，常出劫掠。 臣遣吏入十排，捕得其渠。 三甿阻萬山中，所種田在隘外。 臣發兵守隘，斷其收穫。 其渠今亦出自歸。 」上獎其辦理得宜。
In the sixth month of year three Fu reported: "Along the Taiping–Si'en border word spreads of civil strife in Annam." "A man named Pan Tenglong claims descent from the Mo royal house; his followers Huang Bashitou and Chen Luandan urge rebellion." "I have ordered officers to arrest and punish them without delay." " The emperor replied: "Within your jurisdiction you must set affairs in order and rouse the administration." "Pacifying borders and winning distant peoples demands the utmost caution against greed and vainglory—be doubly wary!" " In the ninth month he reported: "The Yao and Zhuang remain defiant—the Ten Rows of Xiuren and the Three Villages of Tianhe worst of all—plundering constantly." "I sent officials into the Ten Rows and seized the chief." "The Three Villages lie deep in the mountains; their crops stand beyond the passes." "I posted troops at the passes to deny them the harvest." "Their leader has now surrendered of his own accord." " The emperor praised his handling of the matter.
6
旋授直隸總督。 四年，紱入覲。 初，左都御史蔡珽薦起其故吏知縣黃振國授河南信陽知州，忤巡撫田文鏡。 文鏡馭吏嚴，尤惡科目，劾振國貪劣。 紱過河南，詰文鏡胡為有意蹂踐士人。 入對，因極言文鏡貪虐，且謂文鏡所劾屬吏，如振國及邵言綸、汪諴皆枉，振國已死獄中。 文鏡因紱語，先密疏聞，謂紱與振國同年袒護。 紱疏辨，上不直紱，而振國實未死，逮至京師，上更謂紱妄語。 良輔奏雲南、廣西所屬土司與貴州接壤者，皆改歸貴州安籠鎮節制，命紱往與雲貴總督高其倬會勘，疏請循舊制，從之。
He was soon made Governor-General of Zhili. In year four Fu came to court for audience. Earlier Left Censor-in-Chief Cai Ting had revived his former subordinate Magistrate Huang Zhenguo and secured his appointment as prefect of Xinyang, Henan—a move that angered Governor Tian Wenjing. Wenjing ran his staff with an iron hand and especially despised men who came up through the examinations; he impeached Zhenguo for corruption and baseness. Passing through Henan, he challenged Wenjing for deliberately humiliating the degree-holding class. At audience he spoke at length against Wenjing's greed and cruelty, saying the men Wenjing had impeached—including Zhenguo, Shao Yanlun, and Wang Shen—were innocent, and that Zhenguo had already died in prison. Because of Fu's remarks Wenjing filed a secret memorial first, alleging that Fu and Zhenguo, having passed the exams in the same year, were covering for each other. Fu memorialized in his own defense, but the emperor sided against him. Zhenguo was still alive and was brought to the capital, so the emperor charged Fu with false testimony. Liangfu proposed placing all Yunnan and Guangxi native offices bordering Guizhou under Anlong Garrison. Fu was sent to survey with Governor-General Gao Qishen; Fu asked to keep the old arrangement, and the throne agreed.
7
紱還直隸，時上譴責諸弟允禩、允禟等，更允禟名塞思黑，幽諸西寧，复移置保定，命胡什禮監送。 紱語胡什禮：「塞思黑至，當便宜行事。 」胡什禮以聞，上以為不可，命諭紱，紱奏初無此語。 塞思黑至保定，未幾，紱以病聞，尋遂死。 是冬，御史謝濟世劾文鏡貪虐，仍及誣劾振國等。 上奪濟世官，下大學士九卿會鞫，戍濟世阿爾泰軍前。 上以濟世奏與紱語同，疑紱與為黨，召紱授工部侍郎。 紱在廣西捕亂苗莫東旺置天河縣獄，獄未竟，紱移督直隸去。 久之，蠻、僮集眾破獄，劫東旺去。 五年春，良輔署廣西巡撫，奏聞。 上以詰紱，下部察議。 會都察院奏廣西州判程旦詣院訴土司羅文剛掠村落抗官兵，上責紱與繼任巡撫甘汝來逡巡貽害，命紱與汝來至廣西捕治，不獲，當重譴。 紱至廣西，東旺聞而自歸，文剛亦捕得。 直隸總督宜兆熊劾知府曾逢聖、知縣王遊虧空錢糧，上以逢聖、遊皆紱所薦，命詰紱。 戶部議覆，紱在直隸奏報懷來倉圮，穀為小民竊食，當下直隸總督詳察。 上曰：「穀至六千餘石，豈能竊食至盡？ 明系紱市恩，為縣吏脫罪。 當責紱償補，以成其市恩。 」兆熊又劾知縣李先枝私派累民，上以先枝亦紱所薦，責紱欺罔，奪官； 下刑部、議政大臣等會鞫，紱罪凡二十一事，當斬。 上諭曰：「紱既知悔過，情詞懇切，且其學問尚優，命免死，纂修八旗通志效力。 」
Returning to Zhili, Fu found the emperor rebuking his brothers Yinqi and Yinzi. Yinzi was renamed Seshei, imprisoned at Xining, then moved to Baoding under escort by Hu Shili. Fu told Hu Shili, "When Seshei arrives, deal with him as the situation demands." " Hu Shili reported the remark. The emperor rejected it and sent instructions to Fu, who memorialized that he had never spoken such words. Seshei reached Baoding. Before long Fu reported that Seshei was ill, and soon Seshei died. That winter Censor Xie Jishi impeached Wenjing again for greed and cruelty, including the false charges against Zhenguo. The emperor deprived Jishi of his post, convened the grand secretaries and Nine Ministers, and banished him to service before the Altai armies. Jishi's memorial echoed Fu's language, so the emperor suspected conspiracy and summoned Fu to make him Vice Minister of Works. In Guangxi Fu had arrested the rebel Miao Mo Dongwang and jailed him at Tianhe, but the case was unfinished when he left for Zhili. Later the tribesmen stormed the prison and freed Dongwang. In the spring of year five Liangfu, acting governor, reported the breakout. The emperor questioned Fu and sent the matter for ministry review. The Censorate reported that Judge Cheng Dan had petitioned the court: native chieftain Luo Wengang was raiding villages and fighting troops. The emperor blamed Fu and successor Gan Rulai for delay that let harm spread, and ordered both to Guangxi to capture Wengang—on failure they would be heavily punished. Fu reached Guangxi; Dongwang surrendered on hearing of his arrival, and Wengang was seized. Governor Yizhaoxiong impeached Prefect Zeng Fengsheng and Magistrate Wang You for grain deficits. Both had been Fu's appointees, so the emperor summoned Fu to account. Revenue deliberation noted that Fu had reported the Huailai granary wall collapsed and that villagers stole grain; the Zhili governor should investigate. The emperor said, "More than six thousand shi were stored—could villagers really eat it all?" "Fu was plainly currying favor to shield county clerks." "Make Fu pay restitution—that will complete his favor-buying." " Yizhaoxiong also impeached Magistrate Li Xianzhi for illegal exactions. Xianzhi too was Fu's choice; the emperor charged deceit and removed Fu from office; Punishments and the princely council tried him on twenty-one counts calling for execution. The emperor said: "Fu shows repentance and his plea is earnest; his learning remains sound. Spare his life and set him to compile the Comprehensive Gazetteer of the Eight Banners." "
8
七年，又以順承郡王錫保奏濟世在阿爾泰供言劾文鏡實受紱及珽指，下紱等刑部。 會曾靜、張熙獄起，上召王大臣宣諭，並命紱入，諭曰：「朕在籓邸，初不知珽、紱姓名。 有馬爾濟哈者，能醫。 朕問：'更有能醫者否？ '以珽對。 召珽來見，珽謂不當與諸王往來，辭不至，以是朕重之。 年羹堯來京，​​亟稱珽，朕告以嘗招之不來，羹堯以語珽，珽复辭不至，以是朕益重之。 及出為四川巡撫，詣熱河行在，始與相見，為朕言李紱。 朕知紱自此始。 既即位，延訪人才，起紱原官。 旋自侍郎出撫廣西，至為直隸總督，徇私廢公，沽名邀譽，致吏治廢弛，人心玩愒。 又如塞思黑自西大通調回，令暫住保定。 未幾，紱奏言遘病，不數​​日即死。 奸黨遂謂朕授意於紱，使之戕害。 今紱在此，試問朕嘗授意否乎？ 塞思黑罪本無可赦，豈料其遽死？ 紱不將其病死明白於眾，致生疑議，紱能辭其過乎？ 田文鏡公忠，而紱與珽極力陷害，使濟世誣劾，必欲遂其私怨。 此風何可長也？ 」復下紱刑部嚴鞫，獄上，請治罪，上寬之。
In year seven Prince Xibao reported that at Altai Jishi had testified the impeachment of Wenjing followed Fu's and Ting's orders; Fu was sent back to Punishments. When the Zeng Jing and Zhang Xi affair broke, the emperor summoned princes and ministers, and Fu as well, and said: "In my days as heir I did not even know Ting's and Fu's names." "A man named Ma'erjiha practiced medicine." "I asked whether anyone else could heal." "He mentioned Ting." "I summoned Ting, but he refused to visit princes, so I esteemed him." "Gengyao came to court praising Ting. I told him Ting had once refused my summons; Gengyao relayed that, and Ting refused again—so my regard deepened." "As Sichuan governor he came to my traveling palace at Rehe and spoke of Li Fu—then I first heard of Fu." "That was my first knowledge of Fu." "On my accession I sought talent and restored his former rank." "He rose from vice minister to Guangxi governor and eventually Zhili governor-general, yet showed favoritism, neglected duty, courted reputation, and let government and public spirit decay." "When Seshei was moved back from Xidatong I had him wait at Baoding." "Soon Fu reported illness; within days Seshei was dead." "Malcontents then claimed I had ordered Fu to kill him." "Fu stands here—did I ever give such an order?" "Seshei deserved death in any case—who expected him to die so soon?" "Fu failed to announce plainly that illness killed him, inviting suspicion—can he deny fault?" "Wenjing served the state faithfully, yet Fu and Ting schemed against him and set Jishi on false charges to settle private grudges." "How can such conduct be tolerated?" " Fu was again remanded to Punishments for rigorous questioning; the ministry sought conviction, but the emperor showed mercy.
9
高宗即位，賜侍郎銜，管戶部三庫，尋授戶部侍郎。 乾隆元年，方開博學鴻辭科，紱所舉已眾，又以所知囑副都御史孫國璽薦舉，事聞上，上詰紱，紱自承妄言，上謂「紱乃妄舉，非止妄言，避重就輕」。 降授詹事。 二年，以母憂歸。 六年，補光祿寺卿，遷內閣學士。
After Gaozong's accession he received vice-ministerial rank, oversaw Revenue's three treasuries, and soon became vice minister of revenue. In Qianlong 1, when the erudite literary examination opened, Fu had already nominated many candidates and had Sun Guoxuan, associate censor, recommend more of his acquaintances. Summoned before the throne, Fu confessed to false statements. The emperor replied that reckless nomination, not mere words, was the offense—he was shifting blame from the grave to the trivial. He was demoted to tutor of the heir apparent. In year two he went home to observe mourning for his mother. In year six he was reappointed director of court banquets and promoted to grand secretary.
10
紱偉岸自喜。 其論學大指，謂硃子道問學，陸九淵尊德性，不可偏廢，上聞而韙之。 八年，以病致仕，入辭，上問：「有欲所陳否？ 」紱以慎終如始對，賜詩獎及之。 十五年，卒。
Fu was tall and self-satisfied in manner. In scholarship he held that Zhu Xi's stress on inquiry and Lu Jiuyuan's honor of moral nature must stand together; the emperor heard and agreed. In year eight he retired for illness and came to bid farewell; the emperor asked, "Do you wish to say anything?" " He answered that one must end as one began; the emperor granted a poem in praise. In year fifteen he died.
11
孫友棠，乾隆十年進士，自編修累遷至工部侍郎。 新昌舉人王錫侯撰字貫，坐悖逆死。 友棠有題詩，並奪官，賜三品卿銜。 卒。
His grandson You Tang, jinshi in Qianlong 10, rose from compiler to vice minister of works. Wang Xi Hou of Xinchang, a licentiate who compiled the Zi Guan, was executed for sedition. You Tang had written a poem on the work; he too lost office but received a third-grade chamberlain's rank. He died.
12
蔡珽，字若璞，漢軍正白旗人，雲貴總督毓榮子。 康熙三十六年進士，改庶吉士，散館授檢討。 洊擢少詹事，進翰林院掌院學士，兼禮部侍郎。 時世宗在潛邸，聞其能醫，欲見之，珽謝不往。 六十年，四川巡撫年羹堯入覲，世​​宗命達意，仍堅辭。 六十一年，羹堯授川陝總督，以珽代為四川巡撫，覲聖祖熱河行在，世宗方扈從，乃詣謁而去。 雍正二年，羹堯請川、陝開採鼓鑄，珽疏言四川不產鉛，開採非便，羹堯劾珽阻撓，下部議，當奪官。 珽辱重慶知府蔣興仁，憤自殺，珽以病卒聞，羹堯劾之，上詰責再三，始自承。 下部議，擬斬，詔逮至京師，召入見，具言羹堯貪暴及所以抗拒羹堯狀，上諭曰：「珽罪應如律，然劾之者羹堯，人將謂朕以羹堯故殺珽，是羹堯得操威福柄也。 其免珽罪。 」特授左都御史，兼正白旗漢軍都統。 尋進兵部尚書，仍兼左都御史。 會羹堯得罪，直隸總督李維鈞隱其財產，上命珽偕內大臣馬爾賽往按，得實，奪維鈞官，以珽署總督。
Cai Ting, styled Ruopu, belonged to the Plain White Han Banner and was son of Governor Yurong of Yunnan and Guizhou. He took his jinshi in Kangxi 36, entered the Hanlin as a bachelor, and after leaving the academy became a reviser. Governors and commissioners hold a province's law and revenue—can a man who betrayed grace and bears guilt fill such posts? The Changes speak of sweat dispersing; the Rites call the edict the cord of command—trust alone matters. Yet first-year edicts are abandoned or changed in the second; a special order ended mourning in office, yet governors again seek permission to observe it. Under vast Heaven, is talent so scarce? The classics say that in war one may set mourning aside, and that a gentleman does not steal another's parent from him—what need is there for officials who take pay yet abandon their filial duty? A special order let purchased students take exams but barred them from the examination route to office. Examination qualification is the door to office; permitting both purchased status and qualification is to lay the groundwork for selling posts all over again. Even limiting grants to empty titles without real appointment still leaves later edicts contradicting earlier ones—such inconsistency should not stand. I recall the Great Learning's rebuke that without stepping back one cannot move forward. Gentlemen are scarce and small men plentiful; exposed wrongdoers are not removed, while the undetected grow reckless. 陳學海，字志澄，江西永豐人。 康熙五十二年進士，改庶吉士。 與濟世友，授山東恩縣知縣，行取刑部主事，遷員外郎。 文鏡劾振國等，上遣侍郎海壽、史貽直往按，請以學海從，得文鏡欺罔狀，將以實入告，繼乃反之，學海爭不得。 使還，擢御史，嘗以語濟世，濟世用是劾文鏡。 既譴，學海不自安，次年，以病告。 都察院劾偽病，並及與濟世交關狀，奪官，命與濟世同效力軍前。 雍正七年，召還，授檢討。 十一年，卒。
Chen Xuehai, whose style was Zhicheng, came from Yongfeng in Jiangxi. He became a jinshi in Kangxi 52 and entered the Hanlin as a bachelor. A friend of Jishi's, he served as magistrate of En County, Shandong, was selected for Punishments, and rose to vice director. When Wenjing impeached Zhenguo and others, the emperor sent Vice Ministers Hai Shou and Shi Yizhi to investigate with Xuehai. They found Wenjing's deceit and meant to report it, then reversed their findings; Xuehai could not change their minds. Back from the mission he became a censor and told Jishi what had happened; Jishi used it to impeach Wenjing. After the punishment Xuehai felt uneasy and the next year pleaded illness. The Censorate charged sham illness and his connection with Jishi, removed his rank, and sent him to the frontier with Jishi. In Yongzheng 7 he was recalled and made a reviser. He died in year eleven.

論曰：田文鏡與鄂爾泰、李衛同為世宗所激賞。 高宗謂三人者文鏡為​​最下，允哉！ 文鏡馭屬吏苛急，待士尤虐。 紱固以好士得時譽，宜其惡之深，而所爭以為枉者，為珽所薦吏。 濟世又繼以為言，世宗疑珽使紱入告，不納； 又嗾濟世露章論劾，互相結，務欲傾文鏡。 獄遂不可解，然終未即誅死。 高宗嗣服，諸人皆得湔祓，紱復起，濟世亦見用。 孰謂世宗嚴？ 不肯戮諫臣，固明言之矣。
The historian remarks: Tian Wenjing, like Ortai and Li Wei, won Yongzheng's highest praise. Gaozong judged Wenjing the least of the three—rightly so! Wenjing drove his staff relentlessly and treated the literati with special harshness. Fu's reputation rested on patronizing scholars, so Wenjing's hatred was natural—yet the men Fu defended were Ting's appointees. Jishi repeated the charge; Yongzheng suspected Ting had put Fu up to it and would not listen; then set Jishi to impeach Wenjing openly—they formed a faction bent on ruining him. The trials tangled beyond unraveling, yet none were put to death at once. Under Gaozong all were rehabilitated—Fu returned to office and Jishi served again. Who calls Yongzheng harsh? He said plainly enough that he would not kill men who remonstrated.
