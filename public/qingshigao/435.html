華爾勒伯勒東法爾第福戈登日意格德克碑赫德帛黎
Ward, Lebreton, Fard-Fourcand, Gordon, Degeorge, D'Aiguibelle, Hart, and Protet
3
華爾，美國紐約人。 嘗為其國將弁，以罪廢來上海，國人欲執之。 會粵匪陷蘇州，上海籌防，謀練精兵。 蘇松太道吳煦識其才，言於美領事，獲免，以是德之，原效力，俾領印度兵。 既撤，自陳原隸中國。 咸豐十年，粵匪陷松江，煦令募西兵數十為前驅； 華人數百，半西服、半常裝，從其後。 華爾誡曰：「有進無止，止者斬！ 」賊迎戰，槍砲雨下，令伏，無一傷者。 俄突起轟擊之，百二十槍齊發，凡三發，斃賊數百。 賊敗入城，躡之同入，巷戰，斬黃衣賊數人。 賊遁走，遂復松江，華爾亦被創。
Ward was a native of New York in the United States. He had once served as an officer in his own country, but was cashiered for misconduct and came to Shanghai, where his compatriots sought to arrest him. At that time the Taiping rebels had taken Suzhou, and Shanghai was organizing its defenses and planning to drill elite troops. The Su-Song-Tai Circuit Intendant Wu Xu recognized his ability, appealed to the American consul, and secured his release; grateful for this, Ward offered his services and was placed in command of Indian troops. After those troops were withdrawn, he stated that he wished to enter Chinese service. In the tenth year of Xianfeng (1860), the rebels took Songjiang; Xu ordered several dozen Western soldiers recruited as a vanguard; Several hundred Chinese followed behind, half in Western uniform and half in ordinary dress. Ward warned them: "Advance without halt; whoever halts will be beheaded! " The rebels met them in battle with a rain of musket and cannon fire; he ordered his men to lie flat, and not one was wounded. Suddenly he sprang up and opened fire; one hundred and twenty rifles discharged together in three volleys, killing several hundred rebels. The rebels were beaten and fled into the city; he pursued them inside, fought through the streets, and beheaded several rebels in yellow coats. The rebels fled, and Songjiang was recovered; Ward was wounded as well.
4
先是煦與華爾約，城克，罄賊所有以予。 至是入賊館，空無所得，以五千金酬之。 令守松江，又募練洋槍隊五百，服裝器械步伐皆效西人。 同治元年，賊又犯松江富林、塘橋，眾數万，直偪城下。 華爾以五百人御之，被圍，乃分其眾為數圓陣，陣五重，人四鄉，最內者平立，以次遞俯，槍皆外指。 華爾居中吹角，一響眾應，三發，死賊數百。 逐北辰山，再被創，力疾與戰，賊始退。 遂會諸軍搗敵營，殺守門者，爭先入毀之。 是役也，以寡敵眾，稱奇捷。 時浦東賊據高橋，偪上海，華爾約英、法兵守海濱，而自率所部進擊，賊大敗，加四品翎頂。
Earlier Xu and Ward had agreed that if the city fell, Ward would receive everything the rebels possessed. When they entered the rebel quarters they found nothing; he was paid five thousand taels of silver as compensation. He was ordered to defend Songjiang and recruited five hundred men into a drilled musket corps, with uniform, weapons, and drill all modeled on Western practice. In the first year of Tongzhi (1862) the rebels again attacked Fulin and Tangqiao near Songjiang with a force of tens of thousands, pressing directly against the walls. Ward met them with five hundred men; surrounded, he divided his force into several circular formations five ranks deep, four men facing each compass point; the innermost rank stood upright, each outer rank kneeling lower in turn, all rifles pointing outward. Ward stood at the center and blew a horn; at one blast all answered; three volleys killed several hundred rebels. He pursued them to Beichen Hill, was wounded again, and fought on despite his wounds until the rebels finally withdrew. He then joined the other armies in storming the enemy camp, killed the gate guards, and all strove to be first inside and destroy it. In this engagement, few against many, the victory was hailed as remarkable. At that time rebels in eastern Pudong held Gaoqiao and threatened Shanghai; Ward arranged for British and French troops to guard the coast while he led his own force in attack; the rebels were routed, and he was granted fourth-rank insignia and the peacock feather.
5
會李鴻章帥師至滬，乃隸戲下，令立常勝軍，益募兵三千俾教練，參將李恆嵩副之，餉倍發。 賊據王家寺，與英提督何伯等合攻。 華爾賈勇先入，大斬虜首，進偪南翔，賊亦悉眾轟拒，何伯負傷。 華爾冒煙直進，立毀其營，生獲八百餘人，遂復嘉定。 規取青浦，華爾略東門，城潰； 英、法兵自西入，華爾為承。 賊奔，爭赴水死。 攻奉賢，法提督卜羅德遇害，詔賞貂皮採絨，卹其家。 時恆嵩扼趙屯港、四江口，屢失利，嘉、青复危。 華爾方議直搗金山衛，聞敗，還守青浦。 而富林、泗涇又相繼失，乃棄青浦，簡壯士五百襲天馬山，破之。 入城挈守軍出，並力守松江，登陴轟擊兩晝夜不絕，賊宵遁，圍解。 官軍圖青浦，華爾攻南門，駕輪舶入濠，毀城十餘丈，麾眾登堞，賊鬥且走，追敗之白鶴江黃渡，復其城，晉副將銜，降敕褒賞。 俄偽慕王譚紹光復來犯，薄西門，與總兵黃翼升各軍擊之，賊潰，奔北岸，華爾毀其七營。 踰月，會西兵再复嘉定。
When Li Hongzhang arrived at Shanghai in command of the army, Ward was placed under him and ordered to form the Ever-Victorious Army; three thousand more men were recruited for him to train, with Assistant General Li Hengsong as deputy, at double pay. The rebels held Wangjia Temple; he joined British Admiral Hope and others in attacking it. Ward charged in first with desperate courage and took many heads; pressing toward Nanxiang, the rebels mustered their full strength and met him with heavy fire; Hope was wounded. Ward pushed straight through the smoke, immediately destroyed their camp, and took more than eight hundred prisoners alive; Jiading was recovered. Planning to take Qingpu, Ward attacked the east gate and the wall collapsed; British and French troops entered from the west while Ward served as the supporting force. The rebels fled, many throwing themselves into the water to drown. Attacking Fengxian, French Admiral Prol d'Vesques was killed; an edict granted sable fur and velvet as reward and provided relief to his family. At that time Li Hengsong held Zhao Tun Harbor and Sijiangkou but suffered repeated defeats, and Jiading and Qingpu were again imperiled. Ward was planning a direct strike on Jinshanwei when he heard of the defeat and returned to defend Qingpu. Fulin and Sijing were also lost in succession; he abandoned Qingpu, selected five hundred picked men to raid Tianma Hill, and broke the enemy there. He entered the city and brought out the garrison, combined forces to defend Songjiang, and for two days and nights shelled from the walls without cease; the rebels fled by night and the siege was lifted. The government armies planned the recovery of Qingpu; Ward attacked the south gate, drove a steamer into the moat, destroyed more than ten zhang of wall, directed his men onto the battlements; the rebels fought as they fled; he pursued and defeated them at Baihe River and Huangdu, recovered the city, was promoted to vice general, and received an edict of praise and reward. Soon the false Prince of Mo Wang Tan Shaoguang came again to attack, pressing the west gate; with Brigadier Huang Yisheng and the various armies they struck him; the rebels were routed and fled to the north bank, and Ward destroyed their seven camps. After a month he joined Western troops in recovering Jiading again.
6
其秋，賊十萬复犯上海，華爾自松江倍道應赴，與諸軍擊卻之。 時寧波戒嚴，巡道史致諤乞援，鴻章遣華爾偕往。 值廣艇與法兵構釁，引賊寇新城，從姚北紆道犯慈谿。 華爾約西兵駕輪舶三，一泊灌浦，一泊赭山，一自丈亭駛入太平橋、餘姚四門鎮，而自率軍數百至半浦。 平旦薄城，方以遠鏡了敵，忽槍丸洞胸，遽踣地，舁回舟。 餘眾悉力奮攻，賊啟北門走。 華爾至郡城，猶能叱其下卹軍事，越二日始卒。 以中國章服斂，從其志也。 鴻章請於朝，優恤之，予寧波、松江建祠。 初，喪歸，煦檢其篋，得金陵城圖，賊所居處及城垣丈尺方位纖悉畢具，論者頗稱其機密雲。
That autumn a hundred thousand rebels again attacked Shanghai; Ward came by forced march from Songjiang to answer the call and, with the other armies, drove them back. At that time Ningbo was on alert; Circuit Intendant Shi Zhiyi begged for aid, and Hongzhang sent Ward with the relief force. Just then Cantonese boats clashed with French troops, drawing rebels to raid Xincheng; they took a roundabout route north of Yao to attack Cixi. Ward arranged for three Western steamers—one moored at Guanpu, one at Zhushan, one entering from Zhangting through Taiping Bridge and Yuyao's Simen Town—while he himself led several hundred men to Banpu. At dawn they pressed the city; just as he was surveying the enemy through a telescope, a bullet pierced his chest and he fell suddenly; he was carried back to the boat. The rest of the force attacked with all their strength; the rebels opened the north gate and fled. Ward reached the prefectural city and could still command his subordinates regarding military affairs; only after two days did he die. He was buried in Chinese official dress, in accordance with his wish. Hongzhang memorialized the court for generous relief and requested temples built at Ningbo and Songjiang in his honor. When the coffin first returned, Xu searched his trunk and found a map of Jinling city, with the rebels' dwellings and the city's walls and dimensions all minutely marked; commentators praised its strategic value.
7
勒伯勒東德加理尼阿爾伯依都額爾，法國加爾襪多人。 初為本國水師參將。 咸豐十一年，來上海。 時寇據寧波，西人惡之，益兵戍守，遣勒伯勒東乘輪泊三江口。 同治元年，從官軍克府城，募壯丁千五百為洋槍隊，自陳願隸。 明年，權授浙江總兵，受巡撫、寧波道節度。 時上虞賊犯泗門、馬渚，勒伯勒東軍餘姚以待。 尋與同知銜謝採嶂直搗賊屯，賊赴水死者千餘，乘銳毀其卡，薄城先登，擊殺守陴悍賊，餘宵遁，城克。 赴蟶浦，略紹興，以賊遺土砲往，巡道張景渠止之，不聽，未幾，砲果裂，負傷而死，賜優恤。 以法參將法爾第福為江蘇副將，領其軍，退守百官。
Lebreton—full name De Graindorge Albert Edouard—was from Carentan in France. He had first served as a naval adjutant in his own country. In the eleventh year of Xianfeng (1861) he came to Shanghai. At that time rebels held Ningbo; Westerners detested them and increased garrison troops; Lebreton was sent by steamer to moor at Sanjiangkou. In the first year of Tongzhi (1862) he followed the government army in recovering the prefectural city, recruited fifteen hundred stout men as a foreign musket corps, and declared his wish to enter Chinese service. The next year he was provisionally appointed Zhejiang brigadier general, subject to the governor and Ningbo circuit intendant. At that time rebels from Shangyu attacked Simen and Mazhu; Lebreton stationed his army at Yuyao to await them. Soon he joined Expectant Sub-Prefect Xie Caizhang in a direct assault on the rebel camp; more than a thousand rebels drowned fleeing into the water; pressing their advantage they destroyed the stockade, closed on the city and scaled the wall first, killed fierce rebels defending the parapet, the rest fled by night, and the city was taken. He went to Qianpu and raided toward Shaoxing, taking along a captured earth cannon the rebels had left behind; Circuit Intendant Zhang Jingqu stopped him but he would not listen; before long the cannon burst and he died of his wounds; generous relief was granted. The French adjutant Fard-Fourcand was made Jiangsu vice general, given command of the army, and withdrew to defend Bai Guan.
8
法爾第福，又名買忒勒，頗讀華書。 後攻紹興，焚西郭門。 次日復戰，潰十餘丈，麾眾登城，賊殊死鬥，別有黑種人數十助之，遂遇害。 優恤之。
Fard-Fourcand, also called Maitele, had read Chinese books extensively. Later he attacked Shaoxing and burned the west outer gate. The next day they fought again; a breach of more than ten zhang was opened and he directed his men onto the wall; the rebels fought to the death; several dozen black men separately aided them, and he was killed. Generous relief was granted.
9
戈登，英國人。 同治二年，李鴻章檄領常勝軍二千攻常州、福山營。 別遣呂宋兵乘小舟薄賊壘，支木橋，伏死士城牆下。 日中，港東西賊營皆破，緣牆入，痛殲之，遂奪福山石城。 圍解，權授江蘇總兵。 進攻太倉，毀南門賊卡，戈登轟潰二石壘，官軍繼進，克之。 規取崑山，與總兵程學啟度地勢，以環昆多水，惟西南通進義，策先斷其歸路。 遂與駕輪舶以偏師繞而西，賊不虞其至也，即時敗奔，奪其四壘。 譚紹光構悍賊來爭，與諸軍大破之，薄昆城，偕李恆嵩夾擊，賊酋偽朝將先期逸去。 踰月，學啟攻東城，戈登自果浦河奄至，扼守西路，分道疾攻。 賊奪西門走，阻水，殲焉。 遂留駐昆城，策應各路。 移師攻花涇港，知賊必不誡，率眾擊北門，毀城外賊壘。 次日，賊降，收吳江、震澤而還。
Gordon was an Englishman. In the second year of Tongzhi (1863) Li Hongzhang ordered him to lead two thousand men of the Ever-Victorious Army to attack Changzhou and the Fushan camp. Separately he sent Filipino troops in small boats to press the rebel fort, set up wooden bridges, and posted picked men beneath the city wall. At midday the rebel camps east and west of the harbor were all broken; they climbed the wall and slaughtered them severely, and took the Fushan stone fort. The siege was lifted; he was provisionally appointed Jiangsu brigadier general. Advancing to attack Taicang, they destroyed the south gate rebel stockade; Gordon shelled and broke two stone forts; the government army followed and took the city. Planning to take Kunshan, he and Brigadier General Cheng Xueqi surveyed the terrain; as Kunshan was ringed by much water, only the southwest connected to Jinyi; the plan was first to cut off their line of retreat. He then took a steamer with a detached force to circle west; the rebels did not expect his arrival and immediately fled in defeat; four forts were taken. Tan Shaoguang mustered fierce rebels to contest the ground; with the various armies he inflicted a great defeat, pressed Kunshan city, and with Li Hengsong attacked from both sides; the rebel chieftain, the false Dynasty General, escaped beforehand. After a month Xueqi attacked the east wall; Gordon came suddenly along the Guopu River, held the west road, and they attacked swiftly on separate routes. The rebels seized the west gate and fled; blocked by water, they were annihilated. He then remained stationed at Kunshan city to support the various routes. He moved his army to attack Huajing Harbor; knowing the rebels would be unprepared, he led his force to strike the north gate and destroyed the rebel forts outside the wall. The next day the rebels surrendered; he recovered Wujiang and Zhenze and returned.
10
以事謁鴻章於上海。 先是白齊文閉松城索餉，既撤，潛通賊，領二百人入蘇州。 戈登诇知之，亟返崑山為備。 旋攻蘇城，率軍三千，與學啟俱力爭要害，稍剪城外賊壘。 偽忠王李秀成聞警赴援，屢敗； 而紹光所部每戰猶致死，自偽納王郜雲官以下，皆萌貳志，詣營乞降。 乃與學啟乘單舸會雲官等於洋澄湖，令斬秀成、紹光以獻，學啟與誓，戈登證之。 未幾，秀成遁，雲官殺紹光，開齊門迎降，賞頭等功牌、銀幣，並犒其軍。 助攻宜興、溧陽，並擊退楊舍賊。 進規常州，轟破南門，合諸軍掘壕築牆以敗之。 敘功，賞黃馬褂、花翎，賜提督品級章服。
He went to call on Hongzhang at Shanghai on business. Earlier Burgevine had closed Songjiang city demanding pay; after he was dismissed he secretly communicated with the rebels and led two hundred men into Suzhou. Gordon learned of it through intelligence and hastened back to Kunshan to prepare. Soon he attacked Suzhou city, leading three thousand troops; with Xueqi he jointly contested the key points and gradually cut down the rebel forts outside the wall. The false Loyal King Li Xiucheng heard the alarm and came to reinforce but was repeatedly defeated; yet Shaoguang's troops still fought to the death in every battle; from the false Minister King Gou Yun'guan downward all conceived thoughts of defection and came to the camp to beg surrender. He and Xueqi then took a single boat to meet Yun'guan and others at Yangcheng Lake, ordering them to behead Xiucheng and Shaoguang as proof; Xueqi swore an oath and Gordon witnessed it. Before long Xiucheng escaped; Yun'guan killed Shaoguang, opened Qi Gate to welcome surrender; first-class merit plaques and silver coins were granted, and their army was rewarded. He assisted in attacking Yixing and Liyang and also drove back the rebels at Yangshe. Advancing to plan for Changzhou, he shelled and broke the south gate; joining the various armies he dug trenches and built walls to defeat them. When merit was recorded, he was granted a yellow riding jacket and peacock feather and given grand coordinator rank regalia.
11
初，戈登與學啟為昆弟交，每戰必偕。 及誅降酋，頗不直其所為，捧雲官首而哭，誓不與見。 嗣聞學啟卒，悲不自勝，乞其戰時大旗二，攜歸國為遺念。 戈登歸後，埃及亂，督師討之，遇害。 朝廷遣使往吊焉。 戈登嘗言：「中國人民耐勞易使，果能教練，可轉弱為強。 」又曰：「中國海軍利於守，船砲之制，大不如小。 」當時稱其將略云。
Earlier Gordon and Xueqi had been as close as brothers and fought together in every battle. When the surrendered chieftains were executed he strongly disapproved of the act, held Yun'guan's head and wept, and swore never to see Xueqi again. Later hearing that Xueqi had died, he was overcome with grief and begged for two of his battle flags, carrying them home as mementos. After Gordon returned, rebellion broke out in Egypt; he commanded troops to suppress it and was killed. The court sent an envoy to offer condolences. Gordon once said: "The Chinese people endure hardship and are easy to command; if they can truly be trained, weakness can be turned to strength. " He also said: "China's navy is suited to defense; in the design of ships and cannon, large is far inferior to small. " At the time his generalship was praised.

日意格，法國人。 嘗為其國參將，駐防上海。 同治元年，改調稅務司。 徙寧波，复郡城，與有功。 官軍攻慈谿，遣法兵馳往策應。 會餘姚四​​門鎮陷，遂與前護提督陳世章勒兵往討，踰月，直搗上虞。 賊緣道築卡樹柵，悉奪毀之，薄城，並力轟擊，賊殊死戰，賈勇直前，被創，眾軍繼進，斬級千，賊始渡曹娥江去。 進攻奉化，與諸軍克之。 攻安吉思溪、雙福橋，駕小輪舶赴荻港，毀袁家匯賊壘，浙江平。 左宗棠令與德克碑討測西邦製造，仿造小輪舶試行。 五年，宗棠創福州船政局，充正監督，度地募工，殫心所事； 复籌設繪事院、小鐵廠。 七年，加提督銜，賞花翎。 十三年，以船政教導勞賞銀幣。 光緒年，卒。
Degeorge was a Frenchman. He had once been an adjutant of his country, garrisoned at Shanghai. In the first year of Tongzhi (1862) he was transferred to customs commissioner. He moved to Ningbo, recovered the prefectural city, and had a share in the merit. When government forces attacked Cixi, he dispatched French troops to reinforce them. When Simen Town in Yuyao fell, he joined former Acting Provincial Commander Chen Shizhang in leading troops against the rebels; after a month they advanced straight on Shangyu. The rebels built stockades and palisades along the road, all of which were seized and destroyed; closing on the city they shelled with combined force; the rebels fought to the death; he charged forward with desperate courage and was wounded; the main army followed and took a thousand heads; the rebels at last crossed the Cao'e River and withdrew. Advancing on Fenghua, he joined the other armies and captured it. Attacking Anji's Jixi and Shuangfu Bridge, he drove a small steamer to Dih Harbor, destroyed the rebel fort at Yuanjia Hui, and Zhejiang was pacified. Zuo Zongtang ordered him and D'Aiguibelle to survey Western manufacturing and trial-build small steamers. In the fifth year (1866) Zongtang founded the Fuzhou Naval Yard; Degeorge served as chief superintendent, selected the site and recruited workers, and devoted himself wholeheartedly to the task; He also planned and established a drawing academy and a small ironworks. In the seventh year (1868) he was given provincial commander rank and awarded the peacock feather. In the thirteenth year (1874) he was rewarded with silver coins for his labor in instructing the naval yard. He died during the Guangxu reign.

德克碑，法參將。 初，助攻奉化有功。 旋奉其公使檄，將受代歸，謁左宗棠，宗棠撫諭之。 德克碑感服，願易服色受節度。 令駐守蕭山。 蔣益澧攻杭城，檄助戰，游擊何文秀攻雞籠山，德克碑從寶塔嶺登岸，攻倚城賊壘。 會天大霧，賊扌冓嘉興援賊自萬松嶺偪都司張誌公營，勢張甚。 德克碑率眾助擊，敗之。 益澧督水陸軍並進，連破九壘，令總兵高連升據其五，德克碑據其二，屯饅頭山。 轟潰城數丈，毀鳳山門，官軍為承，城遂復。 賊潰，奔湖州。 攻安吉思溪，德克碑率所部助之，轟擊雙福橋，不克，駕小舟泊河汊，火八角亭，支木橋以濟。 賊阻兵中流不得進，德克碑賈勇偪岸，所部遇伏卻走，改趨荻港，越壕入，克三壘。 事寧，撤兵還上海。 五年，充船政局副監督。 七年，馬尾設船廠，督役興工，賞花翎。 九年，宗棠平回亂，檄調甘肅，隸麾下。 十三年，錄經始船政勞，膺獎賞。 後卒。
D'Aiguibelle was a French adjutant. Earlier he had distinguished himself in the joint attack on Fenghua. Soon he received orders from his minister to be relieved and return home; he called on Zuo Zongtang, who comforted and instructed him. Deeply moved, D'Aiguibelle offered to change his dress and accept Chinese command. He was ordered to garrison Xiaoshan. Jiang Yilin attacked Hangzhou and ordered him to assist; Guerrilla Officer He Wenxiu attacked Jilong Hill; D'Aiguibelle landed from Baota Ridge and attacked the rebel forts along the city wall. A great fog arose; Jiaxing relief rebels pressed from Wansong Ridge upon Battalion Commander Zhang Zhigong's camp with overwhelming force. D'Aiguibelle led his men to assist and defeated them. Yilin directed land and water forces forward together, breaking nine forts in succession; Brigadier Gao Liansheng was assigned five and D'Aiguibelle two, encamped at Mantou Hill. Shelling breached several zhang of wall and destroyed Fengshan Gate; government troops followed up as the supporting force, and the city was recovered. The rebels were routed and fled toward Huzhou. Attacking Anji's Jixi, D'Aiguibelle led his own troops to assist; shelling Shuangfu Bridge failed to take it; he drove a small boat to a river fork, set fire to Bajiao Pavilion, and laid wooden bridges to cross. Rebel troops blocked the stream and he could not advance; D'Aiguibelle pressed the shore with desperate courage; his unit met an ambush and fell back; he turned toward Dih Harbor, crossed the moat, entered, and took three forts. When affairs were settled the troops were withdrawn to Shanghai. In the fifth year (1866) he served as deputy superintendent of the naval yard. In the seventh year (1868) the shipyard was established at Mawei; he supervised construction and was awarded the peacock feather. In the ninth year (1870), when Zongtang pacified the Muslim rebellions, he was ordered to Gansu and placed under his command. In the thirteenth year (1874) his founding labor at the naval yard was recorded and he received reward. He died later.

赫德，字鷺賓，英國倍爾發司人。 咸豐四年，來中國，充寧波領事署繙譯官，調廣州。 又充香港督署書記官。 九年，改任粵海關副稅務司。 十一年，總稅務司李泰國奉令購戰艦，以赫德權代之，赴長江新開各口岸置新關。 同治二年，李泰國去職，赫德實授，徙駐上海。 三年，置台灣南北新關。 還駐京，加按察使銜。 八年，晉布政使銜，赴緣海各地度置鐙樓塔表。 光緒二年，佐定砲台條約。 十年，赴金陵與法使議越南案。 會巡船置鐙樓台灣洋，為法虜，乃遣駐英稅務司金登幹赴巴黎申理，乘機與議停戰草約，還。 未幾，其國授為清、韓駐使，不就。 逾年，賞花翎、雙龍二等第一寶星。
Hart, style name Lubin, was from Belfast in Britain. In the fourth year of Xianfeng (1854) he came to China, served as translator at the Ningbo consulate, and was transferred to Guangzhou. He also served as clerk at the Hong Kong governor's office. In the ninth year (1859) he was made deputy commissioner of the Guangdong Maritime Customs. In the eleventh year (1861) Inspector General Lay was ordered to purchase warships; Hart was appointed acting in his place and went to the newly opened Yangtze ports to establish new customs stations. In the second year of Tongzhi (1863) Lay left office; Hart received formal appointment and moved his headquarters to Shanghai. In the third year (1864) new customs were established in northern and southern Taiwan. Returning to station in the capital, he was given surveillance commissioner rank. In the eighth year (1869) he was promoted to provincial administration commissioner rank and went to coastal regions to survey and place beacon towers and channel markers. In the second year of Guangxu (1876) he assisted in drafting the fort treaty. In the tenth year (1884) he went to Jinling to negotiate the Annam case with the French minister. Just then a patrol boat placing beacons in Taiwan waters was captured by France; he sent Commissioner Robert Hart in Britain to Paris to state the case, and took the opportunity to negotiate a draft armistice, then returned. Before long his country offered him appointment as minister to Qing and Korea, but he declined. The next year he was granted the peacock feather and the first-class second-rank Double Dragon Precious Star.
15
十二年，赴香港、澳門，條議洋藥稅釐並徵，並置關九龍、拱北。 十三年，葡使來華，與訂澳門草約。 十五年，藏兵寇哲孟雄，英兵乘勢闌入，赫德遣其弟稅務司赫政馳往，與駐藏大臣會籌劃界諸事。 十九年，賞三代一品封典。 二十五年，與德使籌置膠海新關。 明年，各國聯軍入京，贊襄和議，晉太子少保。 二十八年，召入覲，賜「福」字。 三十一年，與德使更議膠關章程，改行無稅區地法。 尋與日使籌置大連灣新關，徵榷一如膠海。 三十三年，東三省度地置關。 逾年，謝病歸，詔許之，加尚書銜。
In the twelfth year (1886) he went to Hong Kong and Macao to negotiate joint levy of opium duties and to establish customs at Kowloon and Gongbei. In the thirteenth year (1887) the Portuguese minister came to China and he concluded the draft Macao treaty with him. In the fifteenth year (1889) Tibetan troops raided Bhutan; British troops took advantage and forced their way in; Hart sent his younger brother Commissioner Hart to hurry there and, with the resident in Tibet, jointly planned boundary matters. In the nineteenth year (1893) he was granted first-rank enfeoffment for three generations. In the twenty-fifth year (1899) he negotiated with the German minister to establish the new Jiaozhou customs. The next year (1900) the allied armies of various countries entered the capital; he assisted in the peace negotiations and was promoted to Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. In the twenty-eighth year (1902) he was summoned to audience and granted the character "Fu" (Blessing). In the thirty-first year (1905) he again negotiated Jiaozhou customs regulations with the German minister, changing to the duty-free zone law. Soon he negotiated with the Japanese minister to establish the new Dalian Bay customs, with taxation the same as Jiaozhou. In the thirty-third year (1907) customs were surveyed and established in the three eastern provinces. After a year he asked leave on grounds of illness and returned home; the edict permitted it and he was given minister rank.

赫德官中國垂五十年，頗與士大夫往還。 嘗教其子習製藝文，擬應試，未許。 總署嘗擬請授總海防司，道員薛福成以其陰鷙專利，常內西人而外中國，上書鴻章力爭之，議始寢。 辛亥後，病卒，賜優恤。
Hart held office in China for nearly fifty years and associated considerably with scholar-officials. He once taught his son the examination essay form, intending him to sit for the civil service examinations, but permission was not granted. The Zongli Yamen once planned to appoint him Director General of Coastal Defense; Circuit Intendant Xue Fucheng, deeming him secretive and self-seeking and often siding with Westerners against China, memorialized Hongzhang in strong opposition, and the plan was dropped. After the Xinhai revolution he died of illness; generous relief was granted.

帛黎，法國人。 同治八年，來中國，充福州船政學校教員。 十二年，賞五品銜，予雙龍獎牌。 明年，調充江海關稅務幫辦，歷鎮江、北海、甌海、臨海、粵海諸關。 光緒十九年，晉三品銜，調北京，遷稅務司。 二十二年，朝議行郵政，以赫德兼領其事，帛黎實參治之。 凡都會、省城、通商口岸，漸次置局，命曰「大清郵政」。 尋徙拱北。 二十六年，還京。 明年，遷郵政總辦，晉二品銜。 置代辦局於蕪湖​​。 二十九年，河南、山東、山西、貴州復置副總局，自是內地城鄉村鎮，街郵遍設。 時尚未入萬國郵政公會，即已與日本及英屬印度、香港聯約試行。 三十年，賞雙龍三等第一寶星。 與法、德及英屬那達商定聯郵章程。 先後成郵政六百餘局，代辦四千二百餘所。 宣統三年，改隸郵傳部，設總局，尚書盛宣懷疏薦之，遂被命為總辦，郵局置官自此始。 越二年，乞病歸。 未幾，卒。
Protet was a Frenchman. In the eighth year of Tongzhi (1869) he came to China and served as instructor at the Fuzhou Naval School. In the twelfth year (1873) he was granted fifth rank and given a Double Dragon medal. The next year he was transferred as assistant at the Jiangsu Maritime Customs, serving in succession at Zhenjiang, Beihai, Ouhai, Linhai, and Yuehai customs. In the nineteenth year of Guangxu (1893) he was promoted to third rank, transferred to Beijing, and appointed customs commissioner. In the twenty-second year (1896) the court debated establishing a postal service; Hart was placed in charge concurrently and Protet actually assisted in administering it. In all capitals, provincial seats, and treaty ports, offices were gradually established under the name "Great Qing Post." Soon he was transferred to Gongbei. In the twenty-sixth year (1900) he returned to the capital. The next year (1901) he was appointed Director General of Posts and promoted to second rank. Agency offices were established at Wuhu. In the twenty-ninth year (1903) sub-head offices were again established in Henan, Shandong, Shanxi, and Guizhou; from then on postal routes were set up throughout inland cities, towns, and villages. Although China had not yet entered the Universal Postal Union, it had already concluded trial agreements with Japan and with British India and Hong Kong. In the thirtieth year (1904) he was granted the third-rank first-class Double Dragon Precious Star. He concluded joint postal regulations with France, Germany, and British Nada. In all, more than six hundred post offices and more than four thousand two hundred agencies were established. In the third year of Xuantong (1911) control passed to the Ministry of Posts and Telegraphs and a head office was established; Minister Sheng Xuanhuai memorialized recommending him, and he was appointed director general—from this the postal service first received official appointments. After two years he begged leave on grounds of illness and returned home. Before long he died.

論曰：華爾、戈登先後領常勝軍，立功江、浙，世稱「洋將」，時傳其戰略。 日意格初亦參防戰，繼以船政著勞。 赫德久總稅務，兼司郵政，頗與聞交涉，號曰「客卿」，皆能不負所事。 茲數人者，受官職，易冠服，或原隸國籍。 食其祿者忠其事，實有足多，故並著於篇。
The commentator says: Ward and Gordon in succession led the Ever-Victorious Army, achieved merit in Jiangsu and Zhejiang, and were known as "foreign generals"; their battle plans were widely discussed at the time. Degeorge at first also took part in defense fighting, and later won distinction through the naval yard. Hart long headed the customs service and concurrently managed the post; he was much involved in diplomacy and was called a "guest minister"—all were able to fulfill their duties. These several men received official posts, changed their dress, or had originally held foreign nationality. Those who eat the ruler's salary are loyal to his affairs; they truly have much to commend them, and therefore are all recorded in this chapter.
