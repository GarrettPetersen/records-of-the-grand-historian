2
=宜里布=宜里布，他塔喇氏，滿洲正白旗人，阿濟格尼堪子也。 初授兵部副理事官。 順治八年，襲三等伯爵，兼管牛錄。 恩詔進一等伯。 擢刑部侍郎，調吏部。 鄭成功據台灣為亂，議者謂當徙瀕海居民入內地，以避剽掠，絕接濟，命宜里布與尚書蘇納海歷江南、浙江、福建勘疆界。 既定，還京師，擢正白旗蒙古都統。 康熙七年，調本旗滿洲都統，列議政大臣。
Biography of Yilibu. Yilibu, of the Tatara clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain White Banner and was a son of Ajige Nikan. He was first appointed associate director in the Ministry of War. In Shunzhi 8 (1651), he inherited a third-rank earldom and took charge of a niru as well. An amnesty edict promoted him to first-rank baron. He rose to vice minister of Justice and was then transferred to the Ministry of Personnel. When Zheng Chenggong held Taiwan in revolt, some argued that coastal residents should be relocated inland to escape raiding and to sever his supplies. Yilibu and Minister Sunahai were sent to survey the borderlands of Jiangnan, Zhejiang, and Fujian. After the survey was completed, he returned to the capital and was made Mongol commander-in-chief of the Plain White Banner. In Kangxi 7 (1668), he became Manchu commander-in-chief of his own banner and took a seat among the deliberative ministers.
3
吳三桂反，十三年，大將軍順承郡王勒爾錦率師討之，以宜里布參贊軍務。 既至荊州，三桂自常德攻陷松滋，襄陽總兵楊來嘉、副將洪福叛附之，壁穀城、鄖陽間，窺覗郡邑，詔宜里布守宜昌。 十四年，來嘉等犯南漳，順承郡王承製授宜里布討逆將軍印，與副都統根特往援。 來嘉等引退，旋复犯均州，壘武當山下，宜里布督兵擊之，斬千餘級，來嘉等復引退。
When Wu Sangui rebelled, in Kangxi 13 (1674) the great general Prince Shuncheng Leerjin marched against him, and Yilibu was assigned to assist in military affairs. Once the army reached Jingzhou, Sangui advanced from Changde and captured Songzi. Yang Laijia, regional commander of Xiangyang, and Vice Commander Hong Fu defected to him, entrenched between Gucheng and Yunyang, and menaced the surrounding prefectures. Yilibu was ordered to hold Yichang. In the fourteenth year Laijia and his allies attacked Nanzhang. Acting under imperial commission, the Prince of Shuncheng gave Yilibu the seal of Pacification General and sent him with Vice Commander-in-Chief Gent to relieve the city. Laijia and his men fell back, then soon attacked Junzhou again and fortified below Wudang Mountain. Yilibu led the troops against them, killing more than a thousand, and they retreated once more.
4
時三桂屯松滋北山，緣江置戰艦，謀水陸並進。 命宜里布與都統範達禮等守襄陽、均州諸處。 三桂遣其將張以誠與來嘉等寇南漳，宜里布與總督蔡毓榮分率勁旅夾擊，斬三千餘級。 十六年，命與將軍穆佔率荊州滿洲兵自岳州下長沙，克茶陵。 三桂兵奔攸縣，宜里布追擊之，斬四千餘級，俘百餘，克攸縣。
At that time Sangui held the north hill of Songzi, lined the river with warships, and planned a combined land-and-water advance. Yilibu was ordered, together with Commander-in-Chief Fandali and others, to hold Xiangyang, Junzhou, and the surrounding posts. Sangui sent his general Zhang Yicheng with Laijia and others to attack Nanzhang. Yilibu and Governor Cai Yurong led elite troops in a converging assault and killed more than three thousand. In the sixteenth year he was ordered to join General Muzhan in leading the Jingzhou Manchu troops from Yuezhou toward Changsha, and they captured Chaling. Sangui's army fled to You County. Yilibu pursued, killing more than four thousand, taking more than a hundred prisoners, and capturing You County.
5
十七年，穆佔師進克郴州、永興諸處，駐師郴州，而令宜里布守永興。 三桂遣其將馬寶、胡國柱等來犯，與副都統哈克三督兵御之，力戰，殞於陣。 喪還，遣內大臣奠茶酒，復遣侍衛諭其母曰：「宜里布侍朕久，深知其為人。 出師有勞績，方謂功成奏凱，即可相見。 忽聞陣沒，淒愴痛悼！ 爾家貧，予白金六百為治喪資。 」賜祭葬，諡武壯。 子阿什坦襲爵。
In the seventeenth year Muzhan's army captured Chenzhou, Yongxing, and other places, encamped at Chenzhou, and left Yilibu to defend Yongxing. Sangui sent his generals Ma Bao, Hu Guozhu, and others against the city. Yilibu and Vice Commander-in-Chief Hakesan led the defense; after a hard fight he was killed in battle. When his body was brought home, an inner-court minister was sent to offer libations, and a guardsman was sent again to tell his mother: "Yilibu has long served Us, and We know his character well. On campaign he earned merit; We had expected that once victory was won and the triumph reported, We would see him again. Suddenly We heard he had died in battle—how mournful and painful! Your family is poor; We grant you six hundred taels of silver for funeral expenses. Imperial funeral rites were granted, and he was posthumously titled Wuzhuang. His son Ashitan inherited the peerage.
6
=哈克三=哈克三，佟佳氏，滿洲正藍旗人。 父法薩里巴圖魯，以驍騎校從征戰沒。 哈克三初授禮部筆帖式，累遷員外郎。 順治十四年，改授巴牙喇甲喇章京。 康熙二年，李自成餘孽李來亨等據茅麓山，剽掠為民害，從將軍穆里瑪等討之。 賊入山，哈克三從巴牙喇纛章京堪泰自山後進，大破之； 復與總兵於大海夾擊，多所斬馘，來亨自縊死：擢正藍旗蒙古副都統。 十二年，調滿洲副都統，尋遷護軍統領。
Biography of Hakesan. Hakesan, of the Tunggiya clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain Blue Banner. His father Fasari Baturu served as a brave-rider corporal on campaign and was killed in action. Hakesan was first appointed a clerk in the Ministry of Rites and rose step by step to department director. In Shunzhi 14 (1657) he was transferred to the post of bayara janggin. In Kangxi 2 (1663), Li Zicheng's remnant Li Laiheng and others held Maolu Mountain and raided the countryside; Hakesan followed General Murima and others to suppress them. When the rebels took to the mountains, Hakesan followed Banner Commander Kantai in an attack from the rear of the range and routed them. He then joined Regional Commander Yu Dahai in a pincer assault, killing and capturing many; Laiheng hanged himself. Hakesan was promoted to Mongol vice commander-in-chief of the Plain Blue Banner. In the twelfth year he was transferred to Manchu vice commander-in-chief and soon made commander of the guards.
7
十四年，察哈爾布爾尼叛，大將軍信郡王鄂扎率師討之，哈克三參贊軍務。 師次達祿，布爾尼列陣以待，而隱兵山谷間以誘我師。 土默特兵遇伏，哈克三力禦敗之。 复督驍騎突賊陣，賊潰奔，斬馘甚眾，布爾尼以三十騎遁。 敘功，授三等阿達哈哈番。
In the fourteenth year the Chahar Bulwani rebelled; the great general Prince Xin Eza marched against him, and Hakesan assisted in military affairs. The army halted at Dalu; Bulwani formed battle lines to receive them while concealing troops in the valleys to lure Our forces. When Tumed troops ran into an ambush, Hakesan fought fiercely and beat the enemy back. He again led the brave cavalry in a charge on the rebel formation; the rebels broke and fled, and a great many were killed or captured; Bulwani escaped with thirty horsemen. His merit was recorded and he was granted a third-rank adahahafan.
8
十六年，大將軍簡親王喇布討吳三桂，哈克三參贊軍務。 三桂將韓大任據萬安，哈克三與副都統雅沁等分道進，大任渡河走。 哈克三以山迳隘不容騎，請調綠旗兵守隘，斷賊餉道，上責其稽延，敕窮追毋縱入楚。 賊竄興國山中，追擊之黃塘、新田鋪，師舍騎而徒，奮擊，賊大潰。 復選輕騎夜逐賊至姜坑嶺，賊據險自保，哈克三分兵環攻，斬千餘級。 大任等收餘眾走福建，屯老虎洞。 十七年三月，與都統巴雅爾，副都統錫三、雅沁、布舒庫等分隊奪隘，斬六千餘級，獲所置總兵以下三百餘。 大任窮蹙，率眾詣康親王軍降，哈克三還吉安。 旋命赴湖南，與將軍穆佔會師駐郴州。 三桂將馬寶、胡國柱攻永興急，穆佔令哈克三率師赴援，與都統宜里布力戰，同歿於陣。 喪還，遣內大臣奠茶酒，予白金五百治喪，賜祭葬，諡武毅，進世職一等阿達哈哈番兼拖沙喇哈番，無子，以弟之子巴爾泰襲。
In the sixteenth year the great general Prince Jian Labu campaigned against Wu Sangui, and Hakesan again assisted in military affairs. Sangui's general Han Daren held Wan'an; Hakesan, with Vice Commander-in-Chief Yaqin and others advancing by separate routes, drove Daren to flee across the river. Hakesan said the mountain paths were too narrow for cavalry and asked that Green Standard troops hold the passes and sever the rebels' supply line; the emperor rebuked him for delay and ordered relentless pursuit so that none might escape into Huguang. The rebels fled into the Xingguo mountains; pursuing them to Huangtang and Xintianpu, the army dismounted and fought on foot with a fierce assault, and the rebels were utterly routed. He again chose light cavalry to pursue the rebels by night to Jiangkeng Ridge; the rebels held the heights, and Hakesan divided his forces in a surrounding attack and killed more than a thousand. Daren and others gathered what remained of their forces and fled to Fujian, encamping at Laohudong. In the third month of the seventeenth year, with Commander-in-Chief Bayar and Vice Commanders-in-Chief Xisan, Yaqin, Bushuku, and others, he seized passes in separate columns, killing more than six thousand and capturing more than three hundred officers down to regional-commander rank. Daren, driven to extremity, led his followers to surrender to Prince Kang's army; Hakesan then returned to Ji'an. Soon he was ordered to Hunan to join General Muzhan, and they encamped together at Chenzhou. Sangui's generals Ma Bao and Hu Guozhu pressed the attack on Yongxing; Muzhan ordered Hakesan to lead troops to relieve the city; he and Commander-in-Chief Yilibu fought hard and both fell in battle. When his body was brought home, an inner-court minister offered libations; five hundred taels of silver were granted for the funeral, state rites were given, and he was posthumously titled Wuyi; his hereditary rank was advanced to first-rank adahahafan with tuosalahafan. He had no son, so his younger brother's son Baltai inherited.
9
=阿爾護=阿爾護，富察氏，滿洲鑲紅旗人，世居輝發。 父鄂拜，國初來歸，管牛錄。 從入關，擊走李自成。 復從徵福建有功，予世職一等阿達哈哈番。 累遷鑲紅旗蒙古副都統。 阿爾護初授王府長史。
Biography of Aerhu. Aerhu, of the Fuca clan, belonged to the Manchu Bordered Red Banner; his family had long lived in Huifa. His father Ebai submitted at the founding of the dynasty and administered a niru. He followed the armies south of the pass and helped drive off Li Zicheng. He again campaigned in Fujian with merit and was granted a first-rank hereditary adahahafan. He rose repeatedly to Mongol vice commander-in-chief of the Bordered Red Banner. Aerhu was first appointed chief steward of a princely establishment.
10
康熙十三年，命署副都統，與將軍坤巴圖魯率師出駐汝寧。 其冬，吳三桂將王屏籓等自四川窺陝西，提督王輔臣叛應之。 與坤巴圖魯赴西安，十四年，將五百人守寶雞。 賊來犯，擊之，敗去，追至天王村，撫定歸州十二堡，降其將七、兵千餘。 八月，詔分將軍佛尼埒兵六百授阿爾護，守棧道諸險要。 與三桂將彭時亨戰仰天池，大捷。 出螞蟻河口，望見賊營分立九龍山，即以銳師宵加之，賊大潰。 十月，三桂將石存禮等擁眾八千出棧道，據益門鎮口，分七營窺寶雞，兼為王輔臣聲援。 阿爾護令軍中曰：「有能攻剋隘口者，賞與克州縣城同。 」軍士氣奮，分三道上，直搗其巢，力戰，自巳至未，七營盡破，追奔十數里，射殪其將，獲旗幟、器械無算。 迭破賊仰天池山下，及益門鎮東邵家山、黃兒子溝、沈家坡諸處，自是賊不敢出棧道。
In Kangxi 13 (1674) he was ordered to act as vice commander-in-chief and, with General Kunbaturu, led troops to garrison Runing. That winter Sangui's generals Wang Pingfan and others menaced Shaanxi from Sichuan, and Regional Commander Wang Fuchen rebelled in support. He went with Kunbaturu to Xi'an; in the fourteenth year he led five hundred men to defend Baoji. When rebels attacked he defeated them and drove them off, pursued to Tianwang Village, pacified the twelve forts of Guizhou, and induced seven of their generals and more than a thousand troops to surrender. In the eighth month an edict detached six hundred of General Fonielie's troops for Aerhu to hold the strategic points along the plank road. He fought Sangui's general Peng Shiheng at Yangtianchi and won a great victory. Marching out through Mayi River Mouth, he saw rebel camps spread across Jiulong Mountain and at once sent elite troops to attack by night; the rebels were utterly routed. In the tenth month Sangui's generals Shi Cunli and others led eight thousand men out along the plank road, held the mouth of Yimen Pass, set up seven camps to menace Baoji, and also supported Wang Fuchen. Aerhu told the army: "Whoever can storm and take the pass shall receive the same reward as for capturing a prefectural or county seat." The soldiers' spirit soared; they advanced in three columns straight at the enemy stronghold, fought from si hour to wei hour, broke all seven camps, pursued the fugitives more than ten li, shot down their general, and captured countless banners and weapons. He repeatedly defeated the rebels below Yangtianchi Mountain and at Shaojiashan east of Yimen Pass, Huang'erzigou, Shenjiapo, and other places; thereafter the rebels did not dare emerge along the plank road.
11
十五年，授鑲紅旗蒙古副都統。 從將軍穆佔移師湖廣。 十六年，三桂將吳國貴犯長沙，力戰，死之。 事聞，諡敏壯，予三等阿達哈哈番。
In the fifteenth year he was appointed Mongol vice commander-in-chief of the Bordered Red Banner. He followed General Muzhan when the army was transferred to Huguang. In the sixteenth year Sangui's general Wu Guogui attacked Changsha; Aerhu fought hard and was killed. When the report arrived he was posthumously titled Minzhuang and granted a third-rank adahahafan.
12
=路什=路什，納喇氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人，世居章甲城。 父克恩，歸太祖。 路什以驍勇稱。 崇德七年，師入兗州，以雲梯攻城，路什先登，克之，賜號「巴圖魯」，予世職游擊。
Biography of Lushi. Lushi, of the Nara clan, belonged to the Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner; his family had long lived at Zhangjiacheng. His father Ken submitted to Taizu. Lushi was famed for valor. In Chongde 7 (1642), when the army entered Yanzhou, Lushi was first to climb the scaling ladders and take the city; he was granted the title "Baturu" and a hereditary colonelcy.
13
順治初，以甲喇額真從入關，與牛錄額真袞泰將步兵擊李自成； 復與梅勒額真阿哈尼堪逐自成至慶都，敗之，自成潰而西。 二年，從英親王阿濟格徇陝西，與梅勒額真阿喇善攻綏德，圍延安，七戰七克。 時自成南走商州，奔湖廣，躡追至武昌，獲其孥。 論功，進二等。
At the beginning of Shunzhi, as jalan ejen he followed the armies through the pass and, with niru ejen Guntai, led infantry against Li Zicheng. He again joined meiren ejen Ahanikan in pursuing Zicheng to Qingdu, defeated him, and Zicheng fled westward. In the second year he followed Prince Ying Ajige on campaign in Shaanxi; with meiren ejen Alashan he attacked Suide, besieged Yan'an, and won seven battles in seven engagements. At that time Zicheng fled south to Shangzhou and on into Huguang; they pursued to Wuchang and captured his family. His merit was assessed and he was advanced to second rank.
14
張獻忠據蜀，久不下。 三年，從肅親王豪格西征，會叛將賀珍等犯漢中，分兵守雞頭關，路什與巴牙喇纛章京鰲拜擊卻之； 追破珍於楚湖，入四川，所向皆捷。 獻忠既滅，分兵剿餘賊，俘斬甚眾，進三等阿思哈尼哈番。
Zhang Xianzhong held Sichuan and long refused to submit. In the third year he followed Prince Su Haoge on the western expedition; when the rebel general He Zhen and others attacked Hanzhong they divided troops to hold Jitou Pass; Lushi with Banner Commander Oboi drove them back. Pursuing, they defeated Zhen at Chuhu, entered Sichuan, and were victorious wherever they went. After Xianzhong was destroyed they divided troops to suppress remnant rebels, capturing and beheading very many, and Lushi was advanced to third-rank ashanihafan.
15
十五年，從信郡王多尼南征，師入貴州。 明桂王將羅大順出拒戰於黔西州十萬溪箐，路什與噶布什賢噶喇依昂邦鄂訥、梅勒額真噶褚哈分兵擊之，連破數營，敵大潰。 敘功，進二等。
In the fifteenth year he followed Prince Xin Duoni on the southern expedition, and the army entered Guizhou. The Southern Ming Prince of Gui's general Luo Dashun came out to fight at the Shiwangxi defile in Qianxi Prefecture; Lushi with Gabsihiyan Garai Angbang Ene and meiren ejen Guchuha divided forces to attack, broke several camps in succession, and the enemy was utterly routed. His merit was recorded and he was advanced to second rank.
16
吳三桂反，路什年已七十，請從征，遂從貝勒尚善徇湖南。 康熙十七年秋，以偏師取湘陰，進洞庭湖，守九馬嘴。 寇至，風大作，泊綠林灘，舟被擊，路什猶賈勇發矢石，擊殺十數人，力竭，死。 時七月二十八日也。 事聞，進一等兼拖沙喇哈番。
When Wu Sangui rebelled, Lushi was already seventy and asked to join the campaign; he followed Prince Shangshan in pacifying Hunan. In the autumn of Kangxi 17 (1678) he took Xiangyin with a detached force, advanced into Dongting Lake, and held Jiumazui. When the enemy came a great wind arose; their boats were moored at Lülin Beach and were battered; Lushi still fought bravely with arrows and stones, killing more than ten, but his strength gave out and he died. It was the twenty-eighth day of the seventh month. When the report arrived his hereditary rank was advanced to first rank with tuosalahafan.
17
子佈納海，襲。 聖祖親征噶爾丹，布納海從內大臣費揚古出西路，戰於昭莫多。 師有功，進三等精奇尼哈番。 卒，子瑚什屯，降襲二等阿思哈尼哈番。
His son Bunahai inherited. When the Kangxi Emperor personally campaigned against Galdan, Bunahai followed Inner Court Minister Feiyanggu on the western route and fought at Jebdor. The army won merit and he was advanced to third-rank jingginihafan. At his death his son Hushitun inherited, with the rank reduced to second-rank ashanihafan.
18
=雅賚=雅賚，納喇氏，滿洲正藍旗人。 初任王府長史，兼佐領。 康熙十三年，命署副都統，駐防江寧，未至，徙駐安慶。 耿精忠遣其將擾江西，廣信、建昌、饒州並陷。 大將軍安親王岳樂率禁旅南征，駐南昌，以雅賚與署領都統阿喀尼參贊軍務，移兵攻彭澤。 既，诇知賊據小姑山，先遣兵擊之。 賊結水寨拒戰，我軍出其後，陟山而下，斬其裨將，餘眾多被創赴水死。 師進攻彭澤，城西臨江，南北皆倚山，路險峻，乃督兵略其東，陟山，樹雲梯以登。 賊不能抗，縱火啟東門走，追擊敗之，遂進攻湖口。 安親王軍至，賊棄城走都昌，雅賚追及之，敗竄鄱陽湖，所置吏以湖口降。
Biography of Yaji. Yaji, of the Nara clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain Blue Banner. At first he served as chief steward of a princely establishment and concurrently as assistant banner commander. In Kangxi 13 (1674) he was ordered to act as vice commander-in-chief and garrison Jiangning; before he arrived he was reassigned to garrison Anqing. Geng Jingzhong sent his generals to raid Jiangxi; Guangxin, Jianchang, and Raozhou all fell. The great general Prince An Yuele led the imperial guards south, encamped at Nanchang, and had Yaji and acting commander-in-chief Akani assist in military affairs while shifting troops to attack Pengze. Once he learned the rebels held Xiaogushan, he sent troops ahead to strike them. The rebels formed a water camp to resist; Our army got behind them, came down the slope, killed their vice general, and many of the rest were wounded and drowned trying to flee. The army advanced on Pengze; the city lay along the river on the west with mountains to north and south and treacherous roads, so he led troops around the east, up the heights, and set cloud ladders to scale the walls. The rebels could not hold out, set fires, and fled through the east gate; he pursued, defeated them, and then advanced on Hukou. When Prince An's army arrived the rebels abandoned the city and fled toward Duchang; Yaji overtook them, routed them into Poyang Lake, and the officials they had installed surrendered Hukou.
19
十四年，將水師逐賊鄱陽湖，趨五桂寨，賊棄寨走，其將黃浩浮舟來犯，擊卻之。 追至梅溪、瑞洪、康山湖及壩口，先後得船數百，斬數千級，與陸軍會苜蓿灣，克餘幹縣。 復進徵建昌，精忠將邵連登據常興山，列營三十，雅賚攻其左，諸軍自右擊之，盡夷其巢，連登中流矢死。 復與都統霍特徵廣信，次石峽，方暑，士馬疲渴，猝遇伏，師少卻，雅賚直前奮戰，中砲死，賜祭葬，諡襄壯，予世職拜他喇布勒哈番。
In the fourteenth year he led the fleet to pursue rebels on Poyang Lake toward Wugui Stockade; the rebels abandoned the stockade, and when their general Huang Hao attacked by boat he was driven back. Pursuing to Meixi, Ruihong, Kangshan Lake, and Bakou, he captured several hundred boats in succession, killed several thousand, joined the land army at Alfalfa Bay, and took Yugan County. He advanced again on Jianchang; Jingzhong's general Shao Liandeng held Changxing Mountain with thirty camps; Yaji attacked on the left while other columns struck from the right, destroyed their strongholds completely, and Liandeng was killed by an arrow. He again joined Commander-in-Chief Huote in the Guangxin campaign; at Shixia, in midsummer, men and horses were exhausted and thirsty when they suddenly met an ambush and the army fell back slightly; Yaji charged forward and fought hard, was killed by cannon fire, and was granted state funeral rites, posthumously titled Xiangzhuang, with the hereditary rank baitalabulehafan.
20
=擴爾坤=擴爾坤，薩克達氏，滿洲鑲紅旗人，世居那穆都魯。 祖葉古德，歸太祖，編牛錄，俾統之。 父喜福，任兵部理事官。 崇德間，從征黑龍江，順治初，從征漢中，皆有功。 復出討姜瓖，瓖將屯寧武關，分據左衛。 喜福力戰，被巨創，卒於軍，世職累進二等阿達哈哈番。
Biography of Kuoerkun. Kuoerkun, of the Sakda clan, belonged to the Manchu Bordered Red Banner; his family had long lived at Namudulu. His grandfather Yegude submitted to Taizu, was enrolled in a niru, and was put in command of it. His father Xifu served as director in the Ministry of War. During the Chongde era he campaigned on the Amur; at the beginning of Shunzhi he campaigned in Hanzhong—in both he won merit. He again went out to suppress Jiang Xiang; Xiang's generals held Ningwu Pass and divided troops to occupy Zuowei. Xifu fought hard, suffered grave wounds, and died in the army; the hereditary rank was repeatedly advanced to second-rank adahahafan.
21
擴爾坤初授牛錄額真。 從征貴州，戰屢捷。 康熙初襲職，遷護軍參領，擢鑲紅旗蒙古副都統。 吳三桂反，命率師駐防太原。 十三年，徙駐西安。 會四川告警，命進駐漢中。 三桂將吳之茂犯廣元，遣兵敗之，復分水陸兼進，又擊之敗去。 之茂遣裨將賀騰龍劫糧二郎關，擴爾坤馳擊，獲騰龍。 之茂復遣裨將何德成犯廣元，分兵渡河擊卻之，逐北三十餘裡。 尋以七盤、朝天諸關复陷賊，詔還駐漢中。
Kuoerkun was first appointed niru ejen. On campaign in Guizhou he won repeated victories. At the beginning of Kangxi he inherited the office, became a guards adjutant, and was promoted Mongol vice commander-in-chief of the Bordered Red Banner. When Wu Sangui rebelled he was ordered to lead troops to garrison Taiyuan. In the thirteenth year he was transferred to garrison Xi'an. When alarm came from Sichuan he was ordered forward to garrison Hanzhong. Sangui's general Wu Zhimao attacked Guangyuan; Kuoerkun sent troops and defeated him, then advanced by land and water together and again struck until Zhimao fled. Zhimao sent Vice General He Tenglong to raid supplies at Erlang Pass; Kuoerkun rode out swiftly, attacked, and captured Tenglong. Zhimao again sent Vice General He Decheng against Guangyuan; Kuoerkun divided his force, crossed the river to repulse them, and pursued north more than thirty li. Soon, when Qipan, Chaotian, and other passes again fell to the rebels, an edict ordered him back to Hanzhong.
22
十四年，漢中乏餉，將軍錫卜臣領兵還城固，擴爾坤率右翼兵殿後。 三桂將彭時亨等擁眾八千據險邀阻，擴爾坤擊潰之，且戰且行三晝夜，次洋縣金水河，七戰皆捷。 諸軍前行，仍令擴爾坤殿，俄賊環偪，​​力戰中創，殞於陣，賜祭葬，進世職三等阿思哈尼哈番。 子遜扎齊，襲職​​，官至工部尚書。
In the fourteenth year Hanzhong ran short of supplies; General Xibuchen led the army back to Chenggu while Kuoerkun commanded the right wing as rearguard. Sangui's general Peng Shiheng and others, eight thousand strong, held the heights to block the road; Kuoerkun routed them and fought on the march for three days and nights, reaching Jinshui River in Yang County with seven victories in seven fights. The main columns went ahead but left Kuoerkun as rearguard; soon rebels closed in on every side; he fought hard, was wounded in the press, and died in battle; state funeral rites were granted and his hereditary rank advanced to third-rank ashanihafan. His son Sunzhaqi inherited the rank and eventually became minister of Works.
23
=王承業=王承業，字瓊山，江南廬江人。 少入伍。 康熙初，從軍福建，克金門、廈門。 累擢游擊，遷廣西副將。 十七年，將軍莽依圖徇廣西，以承業為新設援剿中營總兵，管副將事。 十八年，吳世琮犯梧州，承業擊敗之。 世琮棄營宵遁，克潯州。 世琮以十萬人分屯貴州、廣西諸要隘，而自將兵圍南寧。 承業赴援，設奇與城兵相犄角，戰新村西山之巔，斬六千餘級，世琮負重傷敗走，南寧圍解。 遂自陶鄧山進剿柳州，叛將馬承廕以二萬人拒戰，擊敗之，乘勝定象州，承廕遂降。
Biography of Wang Chengye. Wang Chengye, styled Qiongshan, was from Lujiang in Jiangnan. In his youth he entered military service. At the beginning of Kangxi he campaigned in Fujian and helped capture Kinmen and Xiamen. He rose repeatedly to colonel and was made vice commander in Guangxi. In the seventeenth year General Mangyitu campaigned in Guangxi and appointed Chengye commander of the newly established Central Relief and Suppression Battalion, with the duties of a vice commander. In the eighteenth year Wu Shizong attacked Wuzhou and Chengye defeated him. Shizong abandoned his camp and fled by night; Chengye then took Xunzhou. Shizong stationed a hundred thousand men at key passes in Guizhou and Guangxi while he personally besieged Nanning. Chengye marched to relieve the city, laid an ambush and coordinated with the garrison in a pincer, fought on the summit of Xincun West Mountain, killed more than six thousand, Shizong was badly wounded and fled, and the siege of Nanning was raised. He then advanced from Taodeng Mountain against Liuzhou; the rebel general Ma Chengyin met him with twenty thousand men; Chengye defeated them, pressed on to secure Xiangzhou, and Chengyin surrendered.
24
其冬，將軍賚塔自南寧直進雲南，檄承業至西隆。 吳世璠將何繼祖據石門坎，去安籠所三十里，地僻道險。 十九年正月，承業奮勇入，連奪二隘口，复所城。 繼祖退據黃草壩，列象拒戰，承業疾擊之，自卯至未，毀其營二十有二。 克曲靖，取霑益，下馬龍、楊林，大小三十餘戰，無不披靡。 既抵會城，壁城外歸化寺。 世璠將胡國柄、劉起龍出拒，承業引守備林廷燏鏖戰，自卯至午，突入賊陣，砲中額，墜馬死。 廷燏單騎馳救，賊矢雨集，亦殞於陣。 事聞，詔贈承業右都督，廷燏贈都司僉事。
That winter General Saicha advanced from Nanning straight into Yunnan and summoned Chengye to Xilong. Wu Shiyao's general He Jizu held Shimenkan, thirty li from Anlong, in rough and dangerous country. In the first month of the nineteenth year Chengye charged in boldly, took two passes in succession, and recovered the prefectural seat. Jizu fell back to Huangcaoba and arrayed war elephants to resist; Chengye attacked fiercely from mao hour to wei hour and destroyed twenty-two camps. He captured Qujing, took Zhanyi, and descended on Malong and Yanglin; in more than thirty engagements, large and small, none could stand before him. When he reached the provincial capital he encamped outside the walls at Guihua Temple. Shiyao's generals Hu Guobing and Liu Qilong came out to resist; Chengye led Garrison Commander Lin Tingye in fierce fighting from mao hour to wu hour, broke into the rebel formation, was struck by cannon on the forehead, fell from his horse, and died. Tingye rode alone to rescue him; rebel arrows fell like rain and he too was killed in battle. When the report arrived an edict posthumously made Chengye Right Censor-in-chief and Tingye Company Commander.
25
=王忠孝=王忠孝，奉天人。 以參將銜從軍屢有功，累擢署左翼總兵官。 從將軍賚塔下雲南，為前鋒。 克西隆，攻廣西縣，忠孝與所部游擊林桂選勇士數十人，越嶺疾馳下，大破賊。 攻石門坎，師盛旗幟，鳴鼓角，趨上游，忠孝與桂督兵涉水，出間道繞其後，破敵砦。 攻黃草壩，與桂引敵入谷，伏起，夾擊，斬世璠軍裨將。 既破隘，師進薄雲南會城。 國柄等出戰，忠孝與承業、廷燏同時戰死，贈都督同知。
Biography of Wang Zhongxiao. Wang Zhongxiao was from Fengtian. Serving with the rank of vice general he campaigned with repeated merit and rose to acting Left Wing regional commander. He followed General Saicha into Yunnan as vanguard. After taking Xilong and attacking Guangxi County, Zhongxiao and his subordinate Colonel Lin Gui chose several dozen brave men, crossed the ridge and charged down at speed, and routed the rebels. At Shimenkan the army raised banners and sounded drums and horns and moved upstream; Zhongxiao and Gui led men wading through the water, took a bypath around the rear, and stormed the enemy stockade. At Huangcaoba he and Gui lured the enemy into a valley; when the ambush rose and struck from both sides they killed a vice general of Shiyao's army. Once the pass was taken the army pressed close to the Yunnan provincial capital. Guobing and others came out to fight; Zhongxiao, Chengye, and Tingye all died in battle together, and he was posthumously granted Vice Censor-in-chief.
26
廷燏，廣東南海人。 桂，廣東番禺人。 忠孝既戰沒，桂佐賚塔定雲南，代為左翼總兵官。
Tingye was from Nanhai in Guangdong. Gui was from Panyu in Guangdong. After Zhongxiao fell in battle, Gui assisted Saicha in pacifying Yunnan and succeeded him as Left Wing regional commander.
27
=【論】=論曰：吳三桂白首舉事，號善用兵。 屯松滋數年，不敢渡江攻荊州。 晚欲通贛、粵道，宜理布、哈克三以死拒，然終不得達，安在其為善用兵也？ 阿爾護輩殺敵致果，授命疆埸。 承業戰沒雲南城下，悍敵致死，誠有不易當者。 故比而論之，亦以見戡定始末。 他死事者，語別見忠義傳，不能遍著也。
Commentary: Wu Sangui rose in rebellion in his old age and was reputed to be skilled in warfare. He held Songzi for years yet did not dare cross the Yangzi to attack Jingzhou. Late in the war he sought a route through Jiangxi and Guangdong; Yilibu and Hakesan blocked him at the cost of their lives, yet he never got through—where was his reputation for skill in arms? Men like Aerhu killed the enemy and won results, taking their orders on the frontier. Chengye died below the walls of Yunnan City; he faced fierce enemies and gave his life—a foe not easily matched. Comparing them in this way also shows how the whole course of the pacification may be understood. Others who died in service are recorded separately in the Biographies of Loyalty and Righteousness and cannot all be set down here.
