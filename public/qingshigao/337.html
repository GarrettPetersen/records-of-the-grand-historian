2
盧焯圖爾炳阿阿思哈宮兆麟楊景素閔鶚元
Lu Chao, Tu Erbinga, Asiha, Gong Zhaolin, Yang Jingsu, and Min Eyuan
3
盧焯，字光植，漢軍鑲黃旗人。 入貲授直隸武邑知縣。 縣舊有均徭錢供差費，遇差仍按裡派夫，焯革除之，又歸火耗於公，捕盜尤力。 雍正六年，解餉詣京師，世宗特召對。 遷江南亳州知州，禁械鬥。 再遷山東東昌知府，總督田文鏡遣官弁四出訪事，東昌民逮下獄甚眾，焯至，悉判遣之。 會有水災，焯疏運河，築護城長堤，動帑賑恤。 上遣大臣閱視，獨東昌得完。 九年，遷督糧道，移河南南汝道。 十年，授按察使。 十一年，遷布政使。
Lu Chao, whose style was Guangzhi, belonged to the Chinese Banner corps of the Bordered Yellow Banner. By purchasing office he received appointment as magistrate of Wuyi in Zhili. The county had long collected equitable corvée funds for official errands, yet whenever labor was requisitioned it still drafted men by neighborhood; Chao abolished the practice, restored surcharge grain to the public account, and pursued bandits with particular energy. In the sixth year of Yongzheng [1728], he delivered tax silver to the capital and was granted a special audience by Emperor Yongzheng. He was promoted to prefect of Bozhou in Jiangnan, where he suppressed armed faction fights. He was transferred again to serve as prefect of Dongchang in Shandong. Governor Tian Wenjing had dispatched officers in every direction to investigate affairs, and a great many Dongchang residents had been thrown into prison; when Chao took office he reviewed the cases and sent them all home. A flood followed; Chao dredged the Grand Canal, raised a long protective dike around the city walls, and opened the treasury for relief. The throne dispatched a minister to inspect the damage, and Dongchang alone emerged intact. In the ninth year [1731] he became Grain Transport Intendant, then was shifted to the Nanru Circuit in Henan. In the tenth year [1732] he received appointment as provincial judicial commissioner. In the eleventh year [1733] he was promoted to provincial treasurer.
4
十二年，擢福建巡撫，賜孔雀翎。 十三年，高宗即位，焯疏言被水州縣不成災，上諭曰：「被水雖不成災，仍須加意賑恤，毋使小民失所。 」乾隆元年，請查丈建陽民田，上諭曰：「小民畏查丈如水火。 汝初為加賦起見，今又以豁除掩非，一存觀望之心，所謂無一而可也。 」尋奏減邵武永安所、霞浦福寧衛屯田徵米科則，豁閩、侯官諸縣額缺田地。 又以平和、永安、清流諸縣田少丁多，請減免攤餘丁銀。 又奏教民蠶績，疏濬省會城河。
In the twelfth year [1734] he was elevated to governor of Fujian and awarded a peacock feather. In the thirteenth year [1735], when Emperor Qianlong took the throne, Chao reported that flood-hit districts had not yet qualified as full disasters. The emperor replied: "Even if the waters have not risen to disaster level, you must still relieve the people with special care and keep the poor from losing their homes. " In Qianlong 1 [1736] he asked to remeasure civilian fields in Jianyang. The throne answered: "Common people fear land surveys as they fear fire and flood. You began with an eye to raising assessments; now you cloak your errors in exemptions while still holding back—nothing here is acceptable. " He soon memorialized to lower grain quotas on Yongan garrison fields in Shaowu and on Funing Guard fields in Xiapu, and to write off registered shortfall lands in Min, Houguan, and other counties. Because Pinghe, Yongan, Qingliu, and other counties had little land but many registered males, he also asked to reduce or exempt the apportioned surplus male tax. He further urged instruction in sericulture among the people and dredging of the provincial capital moat.
5
三年，調浙江巡撫，兼鹽政。 奏請停仁和、海寧二縣草塘歲修銀，減嘉興屬七縣銀米十之二。 又奏陳鹽政諸事：請禁商人短秤； 飭州縣捕私鹽毋擾民； 毋捕肩挑小販； 鹽場徵課不得刑比。 上諭曰：「所奏各條皆是。 汝先過刻，茲乃事事以寬沽名。 過猶不及，汝其識之！ 」尋請裁鹽場協辦鹽大使，改海寧草塘為石塘。 既，又請濬備塘河運石。 五年，上諭曰：「盧焯至浙江，沽名邀譽，舉鄉賢名宦，絡繹不絕。 海塘外已漲沙數十里，焯既請停草塘歲修，又請改建石塘。 心無定見，惟事揣摩，已彰明較著矣。 」六年，左都御史劉吳龍劾焯營私受賄，上解焯任，命總督德沛、副都統旺扎爾按治，事皆實，請奪官刑訊。 事連嘉湖道呂守曾、嘉興知府楊景震。 守曾已擢山西布政使，逮至浙江，自殺。 杭州民數百為焯訟冤，毀副都統廳前鼓亭。 德沛等以聞，上諭責辦理不妥。 七年，讞上，焯、景震皆坐不枉法贓，擬絞。 八年，焯以完贓減等，戍軍台。 十六年，上南巡，閱海塘，念焯勞，召還。
In the third year [1738] he was transferred to governor of Zhejiang with concurrent charge of the salt administration. He asked to stop annual repair funds for grass revetments in Renhe and Haining and to cut grain and silver levies in the seven counties subordinate to Jiaxing by twenty percent. He also laid out salt reforms: merchants were not to use short weights; prefectures and counties were to seize contraband salt without troubling ordinary people; porters selling salt by the load were not to be arrested; and salt-field tax collection must not rely on torture to extract payment. The emperor replied: "Every point in your memorial is sound. You were once too harsh; now on every issue you court favor through indulgence. Excess is no better than deficiency—remember that! " He soon asked to abolish assistant salt commissioners at the salt fields and to convert Haining grass revetments into stone dikes. Later he also sought to dredge the backup dike canal to haul stone. In the fifth year [1740] the emperor said: "Since Lu Chao reached Zhejiang he has sought fame, recommending local worthies and famous officials without pause. Sand had already built up for dozens of li beyond the seawall; Chao had both asked to halt annual grass-dike repairs and asked to rebuild in stone. He has no fixed judgment and only reads the court mood—the inconsistency is plain for all to see. " In the sixth year [1741] Left Censor Liu Wulong accused Chao of private graft. The emperor dismissed him and ordered Governor Depu and Vice Commander Wangzhar to investigate; every charge proved true, and they recommended stripping his rank and putting him to the torture rack. The case also involved Jiahu Circuit Intendant Lü Shouzeng and Jiaxing Prefect Yang Jingzhen. Shouzeng had already been promoted to Shanxi treasurer; seized and brought to Zhejiang, he committed suicide. Several hundred Hangzhou residents pleaded Chao innocence and smashed the drum pavilion before the vice commander yamen. Depu reported the disturbance; the emperor rebuked their handling of the affair. In the seventh year [1742] the verdict came up; Chao and Jingzhen were both found guilty of accepting bribes without twisting justice and were sentenced to strangulation. In the eighth year [1743], because he had made full restitution, his sentence was reduced one grade and he was exiled to a military garrison. In the sixteenth year [1751], during the southern tour, the emperor inspected the seawall, recalled Chao's service, and summoned him back.
6
二十年，授鴻臚寺少卿，署陝西西安巡撫。 二十一年，調署湖北，以陳宏謀代焯。 宏謀未至，上命發歸化城米運金川饋軍，急驛諭宏謀。 焯發視，奏言：「歸化城雖產米，路遠費重； 西安有貯米，先發以饋軍。 仍請擅行罪。 」上嘉焯知大體，合機宜，實授湖北巡撫。 二十二年，西安布政使劉藻入覲，言焯在西安入貢方物，但量給薄值； 及調任湖北，欲借庫帑，未應付。 上責焯負恩，奪官，戍巴里坤。 二十六年，召還。 三十二年，卒。
In the twentieth year [1755] he became Vice Minister of the Court of State Ceremonial and acting governor of Xi'an in Shaanxi. In the twenty-first year [1756] he was shifted to act as governor of Hubei while Chen Hongmou was to replace him. Before Hongmou arrived the throne ordered grain from Guihua City forwarded to supply the Jinchuan campaign and sent an urgent courier dispatch to Hongmou. Chao inspected the order and reported: "Guihua City does produce grain, but the haul is long and costly; Xi'an holds stored grain—release that first to feed the troops. I still ask to be punished for acting without authorization. " The emperor praised Chao for seeing the larger pattern and seizing the moment, and confirmed him as governor of Hubei. In the twenty-second year [1757] Shaanxi treasurer Liu Zao attended court and said that in Xi'an Chao had accepted tribute goods yet paid only trifling sums; and that after moving to Hubei he had tried to borrow from the provincial treasury without receiving funds. The emperor charged Chao with ingratitude, stripped his rank, and exiled him to Barköl. In the twenty-sixth year [1761] he was recalled. In the thirty-second year [1767] he died.
7
圖爾炳阿，佟佳氏，滿洲正白旗人。 初授吏部筆帖式，累遷郎中。 乾隆三年，授陝西甘肅道。 累遷雲南布政使。 十二年，擢巡撫。 十五年，永嘉知縣楊茂虧銀米，圖爾炳阿令後政彌補結案。 總督碩色論劾，上責圖爾炳阿欺隱徇庇，奪官，逮京師，下刑部治罪，坐監守自盜，擬斬監候。 十七年，上以圖爾炳阿贓未入己，釋出獄。 授吏部員外郎。 未幾，授河南布政使，調山東，又復還河南。
Tu Erbinga of the Tunggiya clan was a Manchu bannerman of the Plain White Banner. He first entered the Board of Civil Appointments as a clerk and rose step by step to director. In Qianlong 3 [1738] he received the Shaanxi-Gansu circuit intendantship. He was promoted in turn to provincial treasurer of Yunnan. In the twelfth year [1747] he became governor. In the fifteenth year [1750] Yongjia Magistrate Yang Mao was short in silver and grain; Tu Erbinga told his successor to cover the deficit and closed the file. Governor Shuose memorialized against him; the emperor rebuked Tu Erbinga for covering up and showing favor, removed his rank, had him seized and brought to Beijing, and referred him to the Board of Punishments, which found embezzlement while in office and recommended beheading after the autumn assizes. In the seventeenth year [1752] the emperor held that Tu Erbinga gains had not enriched himself personally and ordered his release. He was appointed vice director in the Board of Civil Appointments. Soon he became Henan treasurer, was shifted to Shandong, then returned once more to Henan.
8
二十年，擢巡撫。 二十二年，上南巡，江蘇布政使夏邑彭家屏以病告家居，覲徐州行在，入對，言鄉縣被水。 上諮圖爾炳阿，圖爾炳阿奏收成至九分，上責圖爾炳阿文過。 圖爾炳阿又奏「去歲被水尚未成災」，上斥為怙惡不悛。 遣員外郎觀音保密察災狀得實，上奪圖爾炳阿官，髮烏里雅蘇台效力。 上發徐州，夏邑民張欽、劉元德詣行在訴知縣孫默諱災及治賑不實，上親鞫，元德言諸生段昌緒指使。 上復遣侍衛成林會圖爾炳阿至夏邑按治，於昌緒家得傳鈔吳三桂檄。 上諭曰：「圖爾炳阿察出逆檄，緝邪之功大，諱災之罪小。 且以如此梗不知化之民，而治其司牧者以罪，是不益長澆風乎？ 免圖爾炳阿罪，仍留巡撫任治賑。 圖爾炳阿若因有前此罪斥之旨，心存成見，或不釋然於災民，則是自取罪戾，亦不能逃朕洞鑑。 」尋家屏亦以藏禁書罪至死，圖爾炳阿仍以匿災下吏議，奪官，命留任。 逾數月，召詣京師，命往烏里雅蘇台治餉。
In the twentieth year [1755] he was promoted to governor. In the twenty-second year [1757], on the southern tour, Jiangsu treasurer Peng Jiaping of Xiayi, who had retired home on grounds of illness, came to the traveling palace at Xuzhou, was received in audience, and reported flooding in his native county. The emperor asked Tu Erbinga, who reported a harvest of nine-tenths; the throne rebuked him for whitewashing the facts. Tu Erbinga memorialized again that last year floods have not yet become a disaster; the emperor called him incorrigible. He sent Vice Director Guanyinbao on a secret inspection that confirmed the disaster; Tu Erbinga was dismissed and ordered to labor at Uliastai. Leaving Xuzhou, Xiayi residents Zhang Qin and Liu Yuande petitioned at the traveling palace that Magistrate Sun Mo had hidden the disaster and botched relief; the emperor questioned them himself, and Yuande named the licentiate Duan Changxu as instigator. The emperor again sent Guard Cheng Lin with Tu Erbinga to investigate in Xiayi; at Changxu's house they found a copied proclamation of Wu Sangui. The emperor said: "Tu Erbinga uncovered a seditious proclamation—his merit in rooting out treason outweighs his fault in hiding the flood. And to punish the magistrates who rule such stubborn, unregenerate people would only feed a corrupt popular spirit, would it not? Tu Erbinga penalty is remitted and he remains governor to oversee relief. If, because of the earlier dismissal, Tu Erbinga holds a grudge or cannot treat flood victims fairly, he will invite punishment himself and cannot escape my scrutiny. " Soon Jiaping was also sentenced to death for hiding banned books; Tu Erbinga was still referred for concealing the disaster, stripped of rank, yet told to remain in office. Several months later he was called to the capital and sent to Uliastai to manage supplies.
9
二十八年，授貴州巡撫，二十九年，調湖南。 三十年，病作，遣醫往視。 卒。
In the twenty-eighth year [1763] he became governor of Guizhou; in the twenty-ninth [1764] he was moved to Hunan. In the thirtieth year [1765] he fell ill and the emperor sent a physician. He died.
10
阿思哈，薩克達氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 自官學生考授內閣中書，累遷刑部郎中，充軍機處章京。 乾隆十年，擢甘肅布政使。 十四年，擢江西巡撫。 疏言：「各營操演槍砲，須實子彈。 營馬應令騎兵自飼。 技藝以純熟得用為要，步法、架勢不必朝更夕改。 」上嘉其言得要。 旋調山西。 十六年，平陽旱，未親往撫卹，詔責之。 十七年，蒲、解等處复災，請以平陽富民捐款解河東道加賑。 上諭之曰：「賑濟蠲緩，重者數百萬，少亦數十萬，悉動正帑，從無顧惜。 富戶所捐幾何，貯庫助賑，殊非體制。 此端一開，則偏災之地，貧民既苦艱食，富戶又令出貲。 國家撫卹災黎，何忍出此？ 」責阿思哈卑鄙錯謬，不勝巡撫任，召還，奪官。 尋授吏部員外郎。 二十年，命以布政使銜往準噶爾軍前經理糧運。 擢內閣學士。
Asiha of the Sakda clan was a Manchu of the Plain Yellow Banner. From the Imperial School he passed examination for secretary in the Grand Secretariat, rose through the Board of Punishments to director, and served as a Grand Council clerk. In Qianlong 10 [1745] he was promoted to Gansu treasurer. In the fourteenth year [1749] he became governor of Jiangxi. He memorialized: "Every garrison drilling with muskets and cannon must use live ammunition. Cavalry should feed their own mounts. Fighting skill should be judged by reliable mastery, not by changing stances every day. " The emperor praised his advice as hitting the essentials. He was soon transferred to Shanxi. In the sixteenth year [1751] Pingyang suffered drought; he did not go in person to comfort the people, and an edict rebuked him. In the seventeenth year [1752] Pu, Jie, and other districts were stricken again; he asked wealthy men in Pingyang to donate funds to be sent through the Hedong circuit for supplemental relief. The emperor replied: "Relief and tax remissions run from millions down to hundreds of thousands—all from the regular treasury without stint. What can wealthy donors supply? Banking their gifts to aid relief violates proper practice. Once that door opens, in partly stricken districts the poor already lack food while the rich are pressed for cash. When the state succors disaster victims, how can we impose such a burden? " He called Asiha base and wrong, unfit to govern, recalled him, and stripped his rank. Soon he was appointed vice director in the Board of Civil Appointments. In the twentieth year [1755] he was sent to the Dzungar front with treasurer rank to manage grain transport. He was promoted to Grand Secretariat academician.
11
二十二年，命署江西巡撫，蒞任，清理屯田，尋真除。 學政謝溶生劾阿思哈婪賄派累，命尚書劉統勳、侍郎常鈞等按鞫，得實，擬絞。 二十六年，詔免罪，以三品頂戴髮烏魯木齊效力。 二十八年，命往伊犁協同辦事。
In the twenty-second year [1757] he was ordered to act as governor of Jiangxi; on arrival he sorted out garrison fields and soon received formal appointment. Education Commissioner Xie Rongsheng accused Asiha of graft and extortion; the throne ordered Minister Liu Tongxun, Vice Minister Chang Jun, and others to investigate; every charge proved true, and they recommended strangulation. In the twenty-sixth year [1761] an edict remitted his sentence; he was sent to serve at Ürümqi wearing a third-rank peacock feather. In the twenty-eighth year [1763] he was ordered to Ili to assist in local administration.
12
二十九年，授廣東巡撫，調河南。 三十年，疏言：「衛河運道淺阻，濬縣三官廟、老鸛嘴諸地砂礓挺據河心，重載尤艱浮送。 向於上、下游淺處建築草壩以束水勢。 詳考河形，夏秋水盛，無須草壩； 冬令源澀，草壩亦屬無益。 不如於上游先期蓄水，臨時開放。 飭府縣督河員於九月望後起，至漕船出境止，暫閉外河以上民渠，使水歸官渠，重運自可疏通。 鑿去砂礓，並集夫疏濬浮沙，以利漕運。 」又請借司庫閒款，委員分購河工料物，以除沿河州縣按畝派累，均報聞。
In the twenty-ninth year [1764] he became governor of Guangdong, then was moved to Henan. In the thirtieth year [1765] he reported: "The Wei River grain route runs shallow and blocked; at Sanguan Temple, Laoguanzui, and other spots in Jun County, gravel ridges jut into mid-channel, and heavily laden boats can barely be floated through. Grass dams had been built at shallow reaches upstream and downstream to concentrate the current. A close study of the channel shows that in summer and autumn, when the water runs high, grass dams serve no purpose; in winter, when the sources run low, they are equally useless. It would be better to impound water upstream beforehand and release it when required. Prefectures, counties, and river officers should begin after the mid-ninth month and continue until the grain fleet has passed, temporarily shutting civilian canals above the outer channel so the water gathers in the official channel and heavy transport can move freely. The gravel ridges should be cut away and labor assembled to dredge drifting sand and ease grain transport. " He also asked to borrow idle treasury funds and send officers to buy river materials in bulk, ending the practice of levying burdens on riparian counties by the mu; all was reported and approved.
13
三十四年，擢雲貴總督。 師徵緬甸，阿思哈出銅壁關至蠻暮軍中，奏軍中糧馬不敷。 上責其畏難，解任，以副都統銜在領隊大臣上行走。 旋召為吏部侍郎，入對失上指，奪官，戍伊犁。 三十九年，釋回，仍充軍機章京。 擢左都御史。 大學士舒赫德師討王倫，命阿思哈偕額駙拉旺多爾濟率健銳、火器兩營以往。 事定，拉旺多爾濟言城北搜剿王倫餘黨，阿思哈未同往，下吏議，奪官，命留任。 四十一年，署吏部尚書，旋授漕運總督。 卒，賜祭葬，諡莊恪。
In the thirty-fourth year [1769] he was elevated to governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou. During the Burma campaign Asiha went from Tongbi Pass to the camp at Manmu and reported that army grain and horses were inadequate. The emperor rebuked him for shrinking from hardship, dismissed him, and ordered him to serve with vice commander rank above the expedition commanders. He was soon recalled as vice minister of the Board of Civil Appointments, but his audience displeased the emperor; his rank was stripped and he was exiled to Ili. In the thirty-ninth year [1774] he was released and again served as a Grand Council clerk. He was promoted to Left Censor-in-Chief. Grand Secretary Shuhede led troops against Wang Lun; Asiha was ordered to go with Imperial Son-in-Law Lawangduoerji at the head of the Vanguard and Firearms camps. After the rebellion was crushed, Lawangduoerji reported a sweep north of the city for Wang Lun's remnants, but Asiha had not accompanied him; he was referred for judgment, stripped of rank, yet told to remain in office. In the forty-first year [1776] he acted as minister of the Board of Civil Appointments, then became Grain Transport Governor-General. He died; the court granted funeral sacrifices and burial honors and gave him the posthumous title Zhuangke.
14
阿思哈初撫江西，上眷之獨厚。 廣西巡撫衛哲治入覲，上問各省督撫孰為最劣，哲治引罪，上謂：「姑置汝！ 」哲治舉阿思哈對，時以為難能。
When Asiha first governed Jiangxi, the emperor's favor toward him was unusually deep. Guangxi Governor Wei Zhezhi attended court; the emperor asked which governors and governors-general were worst; Zhezhi took the blame on himself, and the emperor said, "For now, I shall set you aside! " Zhezhi named Asiha instead; at the time people thought that took uncommon courage.
15
宮兆麟，字伯厚，江南懷遠人。 自貢生授湖北安陸通判，累遷至山東糧道。 乾隆三十一年，授湖南按察使。 桂陽州民侯七郎毆殺從兄岳添，賄其兄學添自承。 知州張宏燧讞上，巡撫李因培疑之，令兆麟詳鞫得實。 因培調福建去，巡撫常鈞庇宏燧，以七郎呼冤劾兆麟，兆麟亦入奏。 上遣侍郎期成額會總督定長按治，如兆麟讞； 兆麟又發宏燧買金行賄狀，期成額等奏聞，逮訊，買金非行賄，乃迎合因培及湖北布政使赫升額意指，代武陵知縣馮其柘補虧空。 因培、赫升額、常鈞、宏燧皆坐譴。
Gong Zhaolin, whose style was Bohou, came from Huaiyuan in Jiangnan. As a tribute student he received appointment as subprefectural judge of Anlu in Hubei and rose step by step to Shandong Grain Transport Intendant. In Qianlong 31 [1766] he became Hunan provincial judicial commissioner. Hou Qilang, a commoner of Guiyang Prefecture, beat and killed his cousin's servant Yue Tian and bribed his elder cousin Xue Tian to confess in his place. Prefect Zhang Hongsui sent up the verdict; Governor Li Yinpei was doubtful and had Zhaolin re-examine the case until the facts emerged. Yinpei was transferred to Fujian; Governor Chang Jun protected Hongsui; Qilang cried injustice and impeached Zhaolin, and Zhaolin memorialized the throne in turn. The emperor sent Vice Minister Qicheng'e with Governor Ding Chang to investigate; the findings matched Zhaolin's verdict; Zhaolin further exposed Hongsui's purchase of gold as bribery; Qicheng'e and others reported it; on arrest and questioning, the gold proved not a bribe but a move to accommodate Yinpei and Hubei treasurer He Sheng'e by covering Wuling Magistrate Feng Qituo's deficit. Yinpei, He Sheng'e, Chang Jun, and Hongsui were all censured.
16
三十二年，兆麟調雲南按察使。 三十三年，遷布政使，擢廣西巡撫。 雲南軍營需硝，敕兆麟籌畫，兆麟以廣西舊存硝七萬七千餘斤運剝隘，复撥通省營貯火藥二十萬斤繼運，得旨嘉許。 調湖南。
In the thirty-second year [1767] Zhaolin was transferred to Yunnan judicial commissioner. In the thirty-third year [1768] he became provincial treasurer and was elevated to governor of Guangxi. Yunnan camps needed saltpeter; the emperor ordered Zhaolin to arrange supplies; he shipped more than seventy-seven thousand jin of saltpeter stored in Guangxi to Bodun Pass and then forwarded two hundred thousand jin of gunpowder from camps across the province; the throne commended him. He was transferred to Hunan.
17
三十五年，又調貴州。 桐梓縣民為亂，命速赴任，會湖廣總督吳達善捕治。 亂定，古州黨堆寨苗香要等為亂，复偕吳達善督兵捕誅之。 兆麟奏黨堆寨苗老呴以阻香要亂被殺，令即寨立廟以祀良苗，並將死義被旌及香要叛逆伏誅狀，譯苗語榜廟門，俾令警戒； 並請移駐將吏，建下江營土城，駐兵鎮撫。 是夏，兆麟奏請於鄰省湖南、四川、廣西買米運貴州糶濟。 至秋，豐收，復奏請停運。 上斥其冒昧，勗令詳慎。 兆麟復奏請簡發知府三員赴貴州，上以「此端一開，各省效尤，妨吏部選法； 且開幸進之門」，下旨嚴飭。 會貴州布政使觀音保入覲，訐兆麟粗率喜自誇，口給便捷，人號為「鐵嘴」。 上曰：「觀音保人已粗率，今尚以兆麟為粗率，則粗率更甚可知。 」諭兆麟猛省痛改。 尋詔詣京師，降補甘肅按察使。 三十六年，坐貴州任內失察廠員虧欠鉛斤，奪官。 四十一年，東巡，兆麟迎駕，詔與三品銜。 四十六年，卒。
In the thirty-fifth year [1770] he was moved again to Guizhou. Commoners in Tongzi County rose in revolt; he was ordered to hurry to his post, where Huguang Governor-General Wu Dashan was already capturing and punishing the rebels. After that disturbance was settled, the Miao Xiangyao and others of Dangduizhai in Guzhou rebelled; he again joined Wu Dashan in leading troops to capture and execute them. Zhaolin reported that the Miao elder Lao Xu of Dangduizhai had been killed for resisting Xiangyao's rebellion and asked that a temple be built at the stockade to honor worthy Miao; he also had posted at the gate, in Miao translation, an account of the loyal dead who had been commended and of Xiangyao's rebellion and execution as a warning; he also asked to relocate stationed officers and soldiers, build an earthen walled camp at Xiajiang, and post troops to pacify the region. That summer Zhaolin asked to buy grain in neighboring Hunan, Sichuan, and Guangxi and ship it to Guizhou for famine relief. By autumn the harvest was abundant, and he memorialized again to halt the shipments. The emperor called him rash and urged him to be thorough and cautious. Zhaolin memorialized again asking that three prefects be sent to Guizhou; the emperor replied, "Once that door opens, every province will follow suit and obstruct the Board of Civil Appointments' selection rules; and it opens a path for opportunists to advance," and issued a stern rebuke. When Guizhou treasurer Guanyinbao attended court he accused Zhaolin of being crude, boastful, and glib; people called Zhaolin "Iron Mouth." The emperor said, "Guanyinbao is crude himself; if he still finds Zhaolin crude, Zhaolin must be cruder still. " He told Zhaolin to reflect deeply and reform at once. Soon he was summoned to the capital and demoted to Gansu judicial commissioner. In the thirty-sixth year [1771] he was stripped of rank for failing, while governing Guizhou, to detect factory officials' deficit in lead. In the forty-first year [1776], during the eastern tour, Zhaolin greeted the imperial procession and received a third-rank peacock feather. In the forty-sixth year [1781] he died.
18
楊景素，字樸園，江南甘泉人，提督捷孫。 父鑄，古北口總兵。 景素孱弱，不好章句，貧不能自給。 入貲授縣丞，發直隸河工效力。 乾隆三年，補蠡縣縣丞，累遷保定知府。 十八年，授福建汀漳龍道。 漳浦民蔡榮祖欲為亂，景素率營卒擒斬之。 調台灣道。 釐定漢民墾種地，並生熟番界址。 革遊民為通譯而不法者，代以熟番。 又禁入山採木，借修造戰船材料為名，累諸番。 三十三年，授河南按察使。 三十五年，擢甘肅布政使，調直隸。 命從尚書裘曰修勘察堤埝各工。 坐失察雄縣知縣胡錫瑛侵蝕災賑，下吏議，奪官，命留任，俟八年無過，方准開復。
Yang Jingsu, whose style was Puyuan, came from Ganquan in Jiangnan and was grandson of Regional Commander Jie. His father Zhu had been regional commander at Gubeikou. Jingsu was frail, cared little for classical learning, and was too poor to support himself. He purchased office and received appointment as county subprefect, then was sent to Zhili to serve on river works. In Qianlong 3 [1738] he became county subprefect of Lixian and rose step by step to prefect of Baoding. In the eighteenth year [1753] he received the Tingzhanglong circuit intendantship in Fujian. Cai Rongzu, a commoner of Zhangpu, plotted rebellion; Jingsu led camp soldiers to seize and behead him. He was transferred to Taiwan circuit intendant. He fixed boundaries for Han reclaimed land and between raw and acculturated aborigine territories. He removed vagrant interpreters who abused their office and replaced them with acculturated aborigines. He also forbade timber cutting in the mountains on the pretext of supplying warship repairs, a burden that fell on the aborigines. In the thirty-third year [1768] he became Henan provincial judicial commissioner. In the thirty-fifth year [1770] he was promoted to Gansu treasurer, then moved to Zhili. He was ordered to accompany Minister Qiu Yuexiu in inspecting dikes and embankment works. For failing to detect Xiongxian Magistrate Hu Xiying's embezzlement of disaster relief funds, he was referred for judgment, stripped of rank, yet told to remain in office; rehabilitation would be allowed only after eight years without fault.
19
三十九年，壽張民王倫為亂，大學士舒赫德督兵討之。 上命景素具車馬濟師，令分守河西。 賊以糧艘結浮橋欲渡，景素與總兵萬朝興、副將瑪爾當阿等督兵御之，董勸回民助師。 夜焚橋，賊不得渡。 事旋定，擢山東巡撫。 疏請編查保甲。 四十年，疏請選京師健銳、火器營裨佐發山東，司營伍教演。 四十一年，上東巡，臨視臨清毀橋斷道及亂民竄據所在，景素述當時戰狀，上嘉其勞，賜黃馬褂。 汶上宋家窪舊渠淤墊，瀦水淹民田。 四十二年，景素奏請濬舊渠，並開支河二，令仍趨南陽、昭陽二湖，下部議行。
In the thirty-ninth year [1774] Wang Lun, a commoner of Shouzhang, rebelled; Grand Secretary Shuhede led troops to suppress him. The emperor ordered Jingsu to furnish carts and horses for the army and to hold the west bank of the river. The rebels lashed grain boats into a pontoon bridge to cross; Jingsu, with Regional Commander Wan Chaoxing, Vice Commander Mardang'a, and others, led troops to block them and urged Hui communities to aid the army. They burned the bridge by night, and the rebels could not cross. When the rebellion was soon crushed he was promoted to governor of Shandong. He memorialized asking to compile and inspect the baojia household registers. In the fortieth year [1775] he asked that adjutants be chosen from the Beijing Vanguard and Firearms camps and sent to Shandong to oversee garrisons and drill troops. In the forty-first year [1776], on the eastern tour, the emperor inspected the ruined bridge and severed roads at Linqing and the places rebels had held; Jingsu described the fighting; the emperor praised his service and granted a yellow riding jacket. At Songjiawa in Wenshang an old channel had silted up, and backed-up water flooded civilian fields. In the forty-second year [1777] Jingsu asked to dredge the old channel and open two branch canals so the water would again reach Nanyang and Zhaoyang lakes; the ministry approved and carried it out.
20
擢兩廣總督，四十三年，調閩浙。 疏言：「浙西歉收，總督楊廷璋請撥台灣倉穀十萬接濟。 北風盛發，未能即到。 請於福州、福寧、興化、泉州四府屬撥倉穀十萬，聽商運赴嘉、湖出糶； 仍飭台灣運歸四府補倉。 」得旨嘉獎。 四十四年，調直隸。 薦於易簡為布政使，上以易簡為大學士敏中弟，責景素。 十二月，卒，贈太子太保，賜卹如例。
He was elevated to governor-general of Guangdong and Guangxi; in the forty-third year [1778] he was moved to Fujian and Zhejiang. He reported: "Western Zhejiang had a poor harvest; Governor-General Yang Tingzhang asked to allocate one hundred thousand shi of Taiwan granary grain for relief. Strong north winds were blowing, and the grain could not arrive in time. He asked to release one hundred thousand shi from granaries in the jurisdictions of Fuzhou, Funing, Xinghua, and Quanzhou and let merchants ship it to Jiaxing and Huzhou for sale; Taiwan grain would still be shipped back to refill the four prefectures' granaries. " The throne commended the plan. In the forty-fourth year [1779] he was transferred to Zhili. He recommended Yu Jian for provincial treasurer; the emperor noted that Yu Jian was the younger brother of Grand Secretary Minzhong and rebuked Jingsu. In the twelfth month he died; the court posthumously granted him Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent and mourning gifts according to precedent.
21
四十五年，兩廣總督巴延三奏景素操守不謹，並發官兵得贓縱盜狀。 兩江總督薩載勘有河堤城垣工程，罰景素家屬承修。 福康安又奏景素在兩廣婪索商捐六萬餘，責景素子炤限年繳還。 五十四年，以福建吏治廢弛，追咎景素，戍召伊犁。 五十九年，釋回。
In the forty-fifth year [1780] Guangdong-Guangxi Governor-General Bayansan reported that Jingsu's conduct had not been strict and also exposed troops who took bribes and released bandits. Liangjiang Governor-General Sa Zai found river-dike and city-wall projects outstanding and ordered Jingsu's family to complete the repairs at their own expense. Fuk'anggan also reported that in Guangdong and Guangxi Jingsu had extorted more than sixty thousand in merchant levies and ordered his son Zhao to repay within a set term. In the fifty-fourth year [1789], because Fujian governance had grown lax, Jingsu was held retrospectively accountable and banished to serve at Yili. In the fifty-ninth year [1794] he was released and returned home.
22
閔鶚元，字少儀，浙江歸安人。 乾隆十年進士，授刑部主事。 再遷郎中，督山東學政。 二十七年，自學政授山東按察使，調安徽。 遷湖北布政使，調廣西、江寧。 四十一年，遷安徽巡撫。 四十四年，雲貴總督李侍堯以贓敗，罪至斬，下大學士、九卿議，請從重立決； 復下各省督撫議，咸請如大學士九卿議。 鶚元窺上指欲寬侍堯，獨奏言：「侍堯歷任封疆，勤幹有為，中外推服。 請用議勤、議能例，稍寬一線。 」上從之，侍堯得復起。
Min Eyuan, whose style was Shaoyi, came from Gui'an in Zhejiang. In Qianlong 10 [1745] he passed the jinshi examination and was appointed a secretary in the Board of Punishments. He was promoted again to director and supervised education in Shandong. In the twenty-seventh year [1762] he moved from education commissioner to Shandong judicial commissioner, then to Anhui. He became Hubei provincial treasurer, then served in Guangxi and Jiangning. In the forty-first year [1776] he was transferred to governor of Anhui. In the forty-fourth year [1779] Yunnan-Guizhou Governor Li Shiyao fell to bribery; the penalty reached decapitation; the Grand Secretaries and Nine Ministers recommended immediate execution with aggravated severity; The case was sent again to provincial governors; all endorsed the capital recommendation. Eyuan sensed the emperor wished to spare Shiyao and memorialized alone: "Shiyao has held frontier posts, worked diligently, and won respect at court and abroad. I ask that the precedents weighing diligence and ability be applied to grant him a measure of mercy. " The emperor agreed, and Shiyao was restored to office.
23
四十五年，調江蘇。 四十六年，甘肅布政使王亶望坐偽災冒賑得罪，事連鶚元弟同知鵷元。 上責鶚元隱忍瞻徇，知其事而不舉，降三品頂戴，停廉俸。 四十八年，還原品頂戴，支廉俸如故。 五十年，江南旱。 五月，鶚元奏淮、徐、海三府如得雨二三寸，猶可種雜糧。 上諭曰：「得雨二三寸未為霑足，焉能種雜糧？ 地方雨水，民瘼攸關。 鶚元何得含混入告？ 」尋奏請截漕十萬石，淮、徐、海三府州被災較重，碾米治賑，如所議行。
In the forty-fifth year [1780] he was transferred to Jiangsu. In the forty-sixth year [1781] Gansu treasurer Wang Tanwang was punished for inventing disasters and embezzling relief funds; the case also involved Eyuan's brother, subprefect Yuanyuan. The emperor charged Eyuan with concealment and favoritism for knowing the affair yet failing to report it; his rank insignia was cut to third grade and his integrity stipend halted. In the forty-eighth year [1783] his original rank insignia and integrity stipend were restored. In the fiftieth year [1785] Jiangnan suffered drought. In the fifth month Eyuan reported that Huai, Xu, and Hai prefectures could still plant secondary grains if they received two or three inches of rain. The emperor replied: "Two or three inches of rain is not enough to soak the fields—how can secondary grains be planted? Rainfall in each district is bound up with the people's welfare. How can Eyuan blur fact and fiction in his reports? " He soon asked to divert one hundred thousand shi of tribute grain; Huai, Xu, and Hai had suffered worst, and husked rice was to be issued for relief as he proposed.
24
五十五年，高郵巡檢陳倚道察知書吏偽印重徵，知州吳瑍置不問； 牒上，鶚元亦置不問，揭報戶部。 上諮鶚元，鶚元猶庇瑍不以實陳，乃遣尚書慶桂、侍郎王昶按治； 責鶚元欺罔，奪官，逮鶚元等下刑部治罪。 巡撫福崧劾鶚元得句容知縣王光陛牒發糧書侵挪錢糧，但令江寧府察覈。 上責鶚元玩視民瘼，徇情骫法，命置重典。 獄具，擬斬立決，命改監候。 五十六年，釋還裡。 嘉慶二年，卒。
In the fifty-fifth year [1790] Gaoyou subinspector Chen Yidao found clerks forging seals to over-collect taxes; Prefect Wu Yin took no action; when the report reached him Eyuan also ignored it and merely notified the Board of Revenue. The emperor questioned Eyuan, who still shielded Yin and withheld the facts; Ministers Qinggui and Vice Minister Wang Chang were sent to investigate; Eyuan was charged with deception, dismissed, and he and others were arrested and referred to the Board of Punishments. Governor Fusong accused Eyuan of receiving Jurong Magistrate Wang Guangbi report on grain clerks embezzling funds yet only telling Jiangning Prefecture to look into it. The emperor charged Eyuan with neglecting the people's plight and twisting the law through favoritism, and ordered the heaviest penalty. The verdict recommended immediate decapitation; the emperor commuted it to imprisonment awaiting the autumn assizes. In the fifty-sixth year [1791] he was released and sent home. In Jiaqing 2 [1797] he died.
25
論曰：法者所以持天下之平。 人君馭群臣，既知其不肖，乃以一日之愛憎喜怒，屈法以從之，此非細故也。 焯、阿思哈、景素坐貪皆勘實，猶尚復起； 圖爾炳阿匿災至面謾，反誅告者； 兆麟口給，鶚元迎上指，至不勝疆政而始去之。 高宗常謂：「朕非甚懦弱姑息之主，不能執法。 」執法固難，自克其愛憎喜怒，尤不易言也。
Commentary: Law exists to uphold fairness under Heaven. When a ruler knows his ministers are unworthy yet bends the law to a day's whim of favor or anger, that is no trifling lapse. Chao, Asiha, and Jingsu were all proved guilty of graft, yet were brought back; Tu Erbinga hid disasters until he lied to the emperor's face, yet punished the petitioners; Zhaolin was all talk; Eyuan read the emperor's mood—only when they proved unfit for frontier rule were they finally dismissed. Emperor Qianlong often said: "I am no weak, indulgent sovereign who cannot enforce the law. " Enforcing the law is hard enough; mastering one's own passions is harder still.
