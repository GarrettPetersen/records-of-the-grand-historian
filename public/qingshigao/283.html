覺羅武默訥舒蘭拉錫拉錫子旺札爾孫博靈阿圖理琛何國宗
Jue Luo Wumune, Shulan, Laxi, Wangzhar son of Laxi, Sun Boling'a, Tulichen, and He Guozong
3
覺羅武默訥，正黃旗人，景祖第三兄索長阿四世孫也。 順治四年，授世職拖沙喇哈番，累進三等阿達哈哈番，擢一等侍衛。 康熙六年，授內大臣，管佐領。
Wumune of the Jue Luo clan, a Plain Yellow Bannerman, was a fourth-generation descendant of Suochang'a, the third elder brother of the dynastic founder Jingzu. In Shunzhi 4 (1647) he received the hereditary office of tuosashala hafan, rose through successive promotions to third-rank adaha hafan, and was appointed a first-class imperial guardsman. In Kangxi 6 (1667) he was made a Grand Minister of the Interior and given charge of a banner company.
4
十六年，命偕侍衛費耀色、塞護禮、索鼐瞻禮長白山，諭曰：「長白山祖宗發祥之地，爾等赴吉林，選識路之人，瞻視行禮，並巡視寧古塔諸處，於大暑前馳驛速往。 」五月己卯，武默訥等發京師； 己丑，至盛京，東行； 戊戌，至吉林。 詢土人，無知長白山路者。 得舊居額赫訥殷獵戶岱穆布魯，言其父曾獵長白山麓，負鹿歸，道經三宿，似去額赫訥殷不遠。 自吉林至額赫訥殷，陸行十日，舟行幾倍之。 寧古塔將軍巴海令運米十七艘詣額赫訥殷，先發，並令協領薩布素護武默訥等行。 六月丁未，武默訥等攜三月糧，陸行經溫德亨河、庫埒訥嶺、奇爾薩河、布爾堪河、納丹弗埒城、輝發江、法河、卓隆鄂河，抵訥殷江乾，米亦至。 乃乘小舟，與薩布素分道行，溯訥殷江逆流上。
In Kangxi 16 (1677) he was sent with the guardsmen Fei Yaose, Sai Huli, and Suonai to worship at Changbai Mountain. The emperor told them: 'Changbai Mountain is where our imperial ancestors first rose. Go to Jilin, find guides who know the way, perform the rites of veneration, and inspect the districts around Ningguta. Ride the courier routes and reach there before the height of summer.' ' On jimao day in the fifth month, Wumune and his party left the capital; on jichou day they reached Mukden and continued east; on wuxu day they arrived at Jilin. They asked the locals, yet none knew the road to Changbai Mountain. They found Daimuburu, a hunter who had once lived at Ehene Yin. He said his father had hunted on the lower slopes of Changbai Mountain, brought a deer home on his back, and been three nights on the trail—apparently not far from Ehene Yin. From Jilin to Ehene Yin was ten days overland; by river the journey took nearly twice as long. Bahai, military governor of Ningguta, sent seventeen grain boats ahead to Ehene Yin and ordered Assistant Commandant Sabusu to escort Wumune's party. On dingwei day in the sixth month they set out with three months' rations, marching overland past the Wendehun River, Kule'ne Ridge, the Qiersa, Bu'erkan, and Zhuolong'e rivers, Nadanfuluo, the Huifa and Fa rivers, until they reached the upper Ne Yin, where the grain convoy joined them. They then took small boats, parted from Sabusu's column, and poled upstream on the Ne Yin.
5
丙寅，會於額赫訥殷。 一望深林無路，薩布素率眾前行，伐木開道。 遣人還告：行三十里，得一山，升其巔，緣木而望，長白山乃在百餘里外，片片白光如積玉，視之甚晰。 戊辰，武默訥前行。 己巳，遇薩布素於林中。 壬申黎明，大霧，莫辨山所向。 聞鶴唳，尋聲往，遇鹿蹊，循行至山麓，見周遭密林，中間平迤圓繞，有草無木。 前臨小林，盡處有白樺木，整若栽植，及旋行林外，仍瀰漫無所見。 跪誦敕旨，拜畢，霧開，峰巒歷歷在目，登陟有路。 遙望之，山修而扈，既近，則堂平而宇圜，向所睹積玉光，冰雪所凝也。 山峻約百餘裡，巔有池，環以五峰，其四峰臨水拱峙，正南一峰稍低，分列雙闕。 池廣袤約三四十里，夾山澗水噴注，自左流者為松花江，右流者為大小訥殷河，繞山皆平林。 武默訥瞻拜而下。 峰巔群鹿奔逸，僕其七，墜武默訥等前。 時登山者正七人，方乏食，謝山靈賜。 卻行未里許，欻然霧合。 癸酉，還至前望處，終不復見山光。 七月庚辰，至恰庫河，馬疲甚。 甲申，自恰庫河乘舟還，經色克騰、圖伯赫、噶爾漢、噶達渾、薩穆、薩克錫、法克什、多琿諸河，至松花江。 八月丁未，還吉林，巡視寧古塔諸處。 乙丑，還京師。
On bingyin day they rendezvoused at Ehene Yin. Before them lay trackless forest. Sabusu went ahead with his men, felling trees to cut a path. Scouts returned to say that after thirty li they had climbed a hilltop and, peering over the treetops, sighted Changbai Mountain a hundred-odd li distant—streaks of white like heaped jade, startlingly clear. On wuchen day Wumune pressed ahead. On jisi day he rejoined Sabusu in the woods. At dawn on renshen day a thick fog hid the mountain entirely. They heard cranes calling, followed the sound, picked up a deer trail, and came to the mountain's foot. All around stood thick forest; in the center lay a broad, gently rounded clearing covered in grass but bare of trees. Ahead stood a small wood ending in white birches aligned as if planted by hand. They walked around the grove's edge, yet the fog still closed in and hid everything. They knelt, read aloud the imperial command, and bowed. When the rite ended the mist parted; the peaks stood sharp before them and a path upward opened. From a distance the mountain rose tall with attendant ridges; up close its summit was broad and its slopes rounded. The jade-like gleam they had glimpsed earlier was compacted ice and snow. The mountain climbed steeply for a hundred-odd li. On the summit lay a lake ringed by five peaks: four stood over the water in a guarding arc, while the southern peak, slightly lower, formed a double gateway. The lake stretched thirty or forty li across. Torrents poured in from the ravines on either flank—the left becoming the Songhua, the right the Greater and Lesser Ne Yin. Level woodland circled the mountain below. Wumune worshipped and came down from the summit. A herd of deer bolted across the summit; seven stumbled and fell at Wumune's feet. Only seven men had reached the summit and they were nearly out of provisions; they thanked the mountain spirit for the bounty. They had retraced their steps less than a li when the mist shut in once more. On guiyou day they came back to their earlier lookout, yet never again caught sight of the mountain's gleam. On gengchen day in the seventh month they reached the Qiaoku River, their horses spent. On jiashen day they took boats down from the Qiaoku, threading the Seketeng, Tubohe, Ga'erhan, Gadahun, Samu, Sakexi, Fakeshi, and Duohun rivers until they joined the Songhua. On dingwei day in the eighth month they were back at Jilin and toured the Ningguta region. On yichou day they reached the capital.
6
疏聞，詔封長白山之神，秩祀如五嶽。 十七年，命武默訥齎敕往封，歲時望祭如典禮。 十九年，召入養心殿，命工繪其像以賜。 諭曰：「以此像俾爾子孫世世供享，以昭恩寵。 」二十九年，卒，賜祭葬。
Their report reached the throne, and an edict ennobled the spirit of Changbai Mountain with rites equal to those of the Five Sacred Peaks. In Kangxi 17 (1678) Wumune was sent with an edict to invest the mountain spirit, and distant seasonal worship thereafter followed court precedent. In Kangxi 19 (1680) he was received in the Hall of Mental Cultivation and commanded to have his likeness painted and presented to him. The emperor told him: 'Let your descendants worship before this portrait for generations, as a sign of the grace shown you.' ' He died in Kangxi 29 (1690) and was granted state funeral rites.
7
舒蘭，納喇氏，滿洲正紅旗人。 父敦多哩，官刑部侍郎，兼佐領。 坐鞫總督蔡毓榮罪，附和尚書希福從輕比，奪官，戍黑龍江。
Shulan of the Nara clan was a Plain Red Banner Manchu. His father Dunduoli was vice minister of Justice and also commanded a banner company. While investigating Governor-General Cai Yurong's offense he sided with Minister Xifu in urging leniency, lost his post, and was exiled to Heilongjiang.
8
舒蘭自理籓院筆帖式遷主事。 康熙三十八年，從侍郎滿丕、都統烏達禪等，招降巴爾瑚三千餘人，安置察哈爾游牧地，編隸佐領。 未幾，巴爾瑚佐領額克圖叛，戕察哈爾副總管阿必達、驍騎校班第，掠馬駝以遁。 上命喀爾喀公錫卜推哈坦等率蒙古兵追剿，舒蘭持檄傳示蒙古諸貝勒台吉，並徵察哈爾、厄魯特兵，從烏達禪會剿，擒其渠。 遷內閣侍讀。
Shulan advanced from a clerk in the Court of Colonial Affairs to section chief. In Kangxi 38 (1699) he joined Vice Minister Manpi and Commander-in-Chief Udazan in receiving the submission of over three thousand Barhu, resettling them on Chahar grazing lands under banner companies. Soon after, the Barhu company officer Eketu rebelled, murdered Chahar Deputy Commander Abida and Captain Bandi, and fled with stolen horses and camels. The emperor sent the Khalkha duke Sibutuihatan with Mongol cavalry in pursuit. Shulan bore edicts to the Mongol nobles, mustered Chahar and Oirat auxiliaries, joined Udazan's column, and took the ringleader. He was promoted to Reader in the Grand Secretariat.
9
四十年，命偕侍衛拉錫往探河源，諭曰：「河源雖名古爾班索里瑪勒，其發源處人跡罕到。 爾等務窮其源，察視河流自何處入雪山邊內。 凡經流諸處，宜詳閱之。 」四月辛酉，舒蘭等發京師。 五月己亥，至青海。 庚子，至庫庫布拉克。 貝勒色卜騰扎勒與偕行。
In Kangxi 40 (1701) he was sent with the guardsman Laxi to trace the Yellow River to its source. The emperor said: 'The headwaters are called Gurban Suurimale, yet almost no traveler reaches the place where they rise.' You must follow it to the farthest spring and note where the stream first enters the inner country beyond the snow ranges. Every stretch the river traverses should be surveyed with care. ' On xinyou day in the fourth month Shulan's party left the capital. On jihai day in the fifth month they reached Qinghai. On gengzi day they arrived at Kukubulak. The beile Sebutengzhale accompanied them.
10
六月癸亥，至鄂棱諾爾。 甲子，西行至扎棱諾爾。 鄂棱週二百餘裡，扎棱週三百餘裡，二諾爾距三十里許。 乙丑，至星宿海，蒙古名「鄂敦塔拉」。 星宿海之源，小泉萬億，歷歷如星，眾山環之。 南有山曰古爾班圖勒哈，西南有山曰布瑚珠勒赫，西有山曰巴爾布哈，北有山曰阿克塔齊勒，東北有山曰烏闌都什，蒙古總名曰「庫爾坤」，即崑崙也。 山泉出自古爾班圖勒哈者，為噶爾瑪瑭； 出自巴爾布哈者，為噶爾瑪楚木朗； 出自阿克塔齊勒者，為噶爾瑪沁尼。 三山之泉，溢為三支河，即古爾班索里瑪勒也。 三河東流入扎棱諾爾，扎棱一支入鄂棱諾爾，黃河自鄂棱出。 其他山泉與平地水泉，淵淪縈繞，不可勝數，悉歸黃河東下。
On guihai day in the sixth month they reached Lake Eling. On jiazi day they went west to Lake Zhaling. Eling was over two hundred li around, Zhaling over three hundred; the two lakes lay some thirty li apart. On yichou day they reached the Sea of Stars, known in Mongol as Odun Tala. At its source countless little springs glittered like stars, ringed by mountains on every side. South stood Gurban Tuleha; southwest, Buhuzulehe; west, Barbuha; north, Aktaqile; northeast, Ulandushi—the Mongol name for the whole range was Kulun, the Kunlun. The stream from Gurban Tuleha was the Garma Tang; from Barbuha, the Garma Chumulang; from Aktaqile, the Garma Qinni. These three mountain springs fed three branches that together formed Gurban Suurimale. All three ran east into Zhaling; one arm of Zhaling fed Eling, and the Yellow River issued from Eling. Countless other hill springs and marsh pools spiraled together and all fed the river eastward.
11
丁卯，舒蘭等自星宿海還，舍故道，循河流東南行。 己巳，登哈爾吉山，見黃河折而東，至庫庫陀羅海山，又南繞薩楚克山，复北流，經巴爾陀羅海山之南。 庚午，達阿木尼瑪勒佔穆遜山，山最高，雲霧蔽之，不可端倪。 蒙古人言長三百餘裡，有九高峰，積冰終古不消。 常雨雪，一月得晴僅三四日。 舒蘭等自此返。 壬申，至錫喇庫特勒，又南過僧庫爾高嶺，更百餘裡，至黃河岸。 見黃河自巴爾陀羅海山東北流，經歸德堡北、達喀山南兩山峽中，流入蘭州。 自京師至星宿海，七千六百餘裡。 寧夏西自松山至星宿海，天氣漸低，地勢漸高，人氣閉塞，行多喘息。 九月，還京師，具疏述所經，並繪圖以進。
On dingmao day they left the Sea of Stars, forsook their inbound trail, and traced the river southeast. On jisi day they climbed Ha'erji Mountain and watched the Yellow River bend east toward Kukutuoluohai, loop south around Sachuke, swing north again, and run along the south flank of Ba'ertuoluohai. On gengwu day they reached Amunimale Zhanmuxun, the loftiest peak, wrapped in cloud so that its full extent could not be seen. Mongols said the range ran three hundred-odd li with nine towering summits whose ice never thawed. Snow and sleet were almost constant; a month might bring only three or four days of clear sky. From here Shulan and his companions turned back. On renshen day they reached Xilakutile, crossed Sengku'er Pass to the south, and after another hundred-odd li stood on the Yellow River's bank. They saw the river run northeast from Ba'ertuoluohai, thread the gorge north of Guide with Daka Mountain to the south, and flow on toward Lanzhou. The capital lay more than seven thousand six hundred li from the Sea of Stars. West of Ningxia, from Songshan up to the Sea of Stars, the air thinned as the land climbed; travelers labored for breath at every step. In the ninth month they were back in the capital with a memorial of their route and maps for the throne.
12
上諭廷臣曰：「朕於古今山川名號，雖在邊徼遐荒，必詳考圖籍，廣詢方言，務得其正。 故遣使至崑崙，目擊詳求，載入輿圖。 即如黃河源出西塞外庫爾坤山之東，眾泉渙散，燦如列星，蒙古謂之'鄂敦塔拉'，西番謂之'索里瑪勒'，中華謂之'星宿海'，是為河源。 彙為扎棱、鄂棱二澤。 東南行，折北，復東行，由歸德堡、積石關入蘭州，其原委可得而縷晰也。 」
The emperor told his ministers: 'Whether a place name is ancient or modern, even in the farthest borderlands I study the maps, question local usage, and insist on the correct form.' That is why I sent envoys to Kunlun to see for themselves and record what they found on the imperial maps. The Yellow River, for example, rises east of Kulun beyond the western frontier. Countless springs spread out like a constellation: Mongols call the place Odun Tala, Tibetans Suurimale, Chinese the Sea of Stars—that is the source. The waters pool in the twin lakes Zhaling and Eling. It runs southeast, bends north, then east again through Guide and Jishi Pass into Lanzhou—its entire course can now be traced in detail. '
13
舒蘭累擢內閣學士。 四十五年，命往西藏封拉藏為翊法恭順汗。 回京得風疾，遣太醫診視。 越二年，疾復發，乞休，許解任調治。 五十二年，疾愈，起故官。 是年以萬壽恩典，復其父敦多哩故秩。 尋遷工部侍郎。 未幾，坐事，降三秩調用。 五十九年，卒。
Shulan rose through successive promotions to Grand Secretariat academician. In Kangxi 45 (1706) he was sent to Tibet to invest Lozang as the Supporting-the-Dharma, Respectful and Obedient Khan. Back in the capital he fell ill with a paralytic disorder, and court physicians were dispatched to attend him. Two years later the malady returned; he asked to retire and was allowed to leave his post to recover. In Kangxi 52 (1713), cured of his illness, he was recalled to his former rank. That year, as part of the imperial birthday amnesty, his father Dunduoli's former rank was restored. He was soon appointed vice minister of Works. Shortly afterward he was implicated in an offense, stripped of three ranks, and transferred to a lesser post. He died in Kangxi 59 (1720).
14
拉錫，圖伯特氏，蒙古正白旗人。 自親軍校三遷二等侍衛，偕舒蘭窮河源，進一等。 雍正初，累擢本旗都統。 以治事明敏，予拜他喇布勒哈番世職，授議政大臣。 拉錫諳習旗務，奏事輒稱旨，累被褒嘉，加授拖沙喇哈番。 四年，以隱匿烏梁海事，盡削官職，降授一等侍衛，管太僕寺卿。 尋仍擢鑲白旗滿洲都統，迭署江寧將軍、天津滿洲水師營都統，授領侍衛內大臣。 卒。
Laxi of the Tubote clan was a Plain White Banner Mongol. He rose from an imperial guards cadet through three promotions to second-class guardsman; after tracing the Yellow River's source with Shulan he was made a first-class guardsman. Early in the Yongzheng reign he rose to commander-in-chief of his banner. Recognized for sharp and capable administration, he received the hereditary rank of baitalabule hafan and a seat on the Grand Council. Laxi knew banner administration inside out; his reports consistently pleased the throne, he won repeated commendation, and received an additional grant of tuosashala hafan. In Yongzheng 4 (1726) he lost every post for covering up an Ulianghai scandal and was reduced to first-class guardsman while serving as superintendent of the Imperial Stud. Soon after he was again promoted to commander-in-chief of the Bordered White Banner Manchus, served in turn as acting military governor of Jiangning and commander of the Tianjin Manchu naval camp, and was appointed Grand Minister of the Imperial Guards. He died.
15
子旺札爾，初授侍衛，襲世職。 使從侍郎阿克敦與噶爾丹定界。 如蘇州、如浙江按事。 累遷鑲白旗滿洲都統、理籓院侍郎、御前大臣。 命赴金川察沿途驛站。 金川平，擢領侍衛內大臣。 卒，諡恪慎。
His son Wangzhar was first appointed bodyguard and inherited the hereditary office. On mission he followed Vice Minister Akedun in delimiting the border with Galdan. He was sent on investigation to Suzhou and Zhejiang. He was promoted step by step to commander-in-chief of the Bordered White Banner Manchus, vice minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs, and minister in attendance before the throne. He was ordered to go to Jinchuan to inspect the relay stations along the route. When Jinchuan was pacified he was promoted to Grand Minister of the Imperial Guards. He died and was given the posthumous title Ke-shen.
16
孫博靈阿，襲世職。 初授侍衛，累遷正藍旗蒙古副都統。 從征金川，攻當噶爾拉，撲碉受創，卒。 贈都統銜，進世職一等輕車都尉，圖形紫光閣。
His grandson Sun Boling'a inherited the hereditary office. He was first appointed bodyguard and was promoted step by step to Mongol deputy commander-in-chief of the Plain Blue Banner. On the Jinchuan campaign he attacked Dangga'erla, stormed a blockhouse, was wounded, and died. He was posthumously granted the rank of commander-in-chief, advanced in hereditary office to first-class commandant of light chariots, and his portrait was placed in the Hall of Purple Splendor.
17
乾隆四十七年，高宗命侍衛阿彌達詣西寧祭河神，再窮河源。 還奏：「星宿海西南有水名阿勒坦郭勒，更西有巨石高數丈，名阿勒坦噶達素齊老。 蒙古語'阿勒坦'為黃金，'噶達素'為北極星，'郭勒'為河，'齊老'石也。 崖壁黃金色，上有池，池中泉噴湧，釃為百道，皆黃金色。 入阿勒坦郭勒，迴旋三百餘裡，入星宿海，為黃河真源。 」高宗命四庫館諸臣輯河源紀略識其事。 阿彌達更名阿必達。 大學士阿桂子，附見阿桂傳。
In the forty-seventh year of Qianlong the Gaozong ordered the guardsman Amita to go to Xining to sacrifice to the River God and once more trace the Yellow River to its source. On his return he memorialized: 'Southwest of the Sea of Stars is a stream called Altan Gol; farther west is a boulder several zhang high called Altan Kadak Su Qilao.' In Mongol 'altan' means gold, 'kadak su' the pole star, 'gol' a river, and 'qilao' a stone. The cliff face was golden in color; above was a pool whose springs gushed forth, dividing into a hundred channels, all golden in hue. They entered the Altan Gol, wound three hundred-odd li, and entered the Sea of Stars—this was the true source of the Yellow River. ' The Gaozong ordered scholars of the Siku project to compile a record of the river source to commemorate the event. Amita was renamed Abida. He was the son of Grand Secretary Agui; see Agui's biography.
18
圖理琛，阿顏覺羅氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 以國子生考授內閣中書，遷侍讀。 坐事，奪職。 康熙五十一年，特命復職，出使土爾扈特。
Tulichen, of the Ayanggioro clan, was a Manchu of the Plain Yellow Banner. As a student of the Imperial Academy he passed examination for secretary in the Grand Secretariat and was transferred to Reader. For an offense he was stripped of office. In the fifty-first year of Kangxi he was specially ordered restored and sent as envoy to the Torghuts.
19
初，土爾扈特汗阿玉奇從子阿喇布珠爾，假道準噶爾赴西藏謁達賴喇嘛。 準噶爾台吉策妄阿喇布坦與阿玉奇構怨，阿喇布珠爾不得歸，款關乞內屬，詔封貝子，賜牧嘉峪關外黨色爾騰。 嗣阿玉奇遣使入貢，上欲歸阿喇布珠爾。 命圖理琛偕侍讀學士殷扎納、郎中納顏齎敕諭阿玉奇，假道鄂羅斯。
Earlier, Ayuki Khan of the Torghuts had a nephew Alabuzhur who asked passage through the Dzungars to Tibet to visit the Dalai Lama. The Dzungar taiji Tsewang Araptan was at odds with Ayuki; Alabuzhur could not return, came to the border to seek incorporation, was enfeoffed as a beile, and was granted pasture outside Jiayuguan at Dangse'erteng. Later Ayuki sent tribute envoys; the emperor wished to return Alabuzhur. Tulichen was ordered, together with Reader-Academician Yin Zhana and Director Nayin, to carry an edict to Ayuki by way of Russia.
20
五月，圖理琛等自京師啟行，七月，至鄂羅斯境楚庫柏興。 以假道故，待其國察罕汗進止。 五十二年正月，許假道，乃行。 還烏的柏興，越柏海爾湖而北，抵厄爾庫。 鄂羅斯託波爾噶噶林遣其屬博爾科尼來迎。 噶噶林者，彼國所稱總管也。 圖理琛等欲行，博爾科尼言噶噶林令天使當自水路行，而昂噶拉河冰未泮，請稍駐俟之。 三月，自昂噶拉河乘舟抵伊聶謝柏興，登陸。 五月，抵麻科斯科，复乘舟自揭的河順流行，經那裡穆柏興、蘇爾呼特柏興、薩瑪爾斯科、狄穆演斯科諸地。 七月，至託波爾。 其地噶噶林名馬提飛費多里魚赤，迎至廨，留八日。 仍遣博爾科尼護之行，抵鴉班沁登陸。 自費耶爾和土爾斯科越佛落克嶺，抵索里喀穆斯科，以路濘，守凍十日。 復行，經改郭羅多、黑林諾付、喀山、西穆必爾斯科諸地。 十一月，至薩拉託付，是為鄂羅斯與土爾扈特界。 水自東北來，折而南，鄂羅斯號為佛爾格，土爾扈特號為額濟勒。 阿玉奇汗駐牧地曰瑪努託海，距此十日程，以雪盛不能行。
In the fifth month Tulichen and the others set out from the capital; in the seventh month they reached Chukoboin on Russian territory. Because they were borrowing the route, they awaited the Russian tsar's decision. In the first month of the fifty-second year passage was granted and they set out. They returned to Udoboin, crossed Lake Baikal northward, and reached Erku. The Russian Tobolsk governor-general sent his subordinate Bolikoni to welcome them. Gagalin was what that country called its chief administrator. When Tulichen and the others wished to proceed, Bolikoni said the gagalin ordered the envoys to travel by water, but the Angara was not yet thawed and asked them to wait. In the third month they took boats on the Angara to Iakutsk and went ashore. In the fifth month they reached Makovsk; again they took boats down the Ket River, passing Narym, Surgut, Samara, and Demyansk. In the seventh month they reached Tobolsk. The local gagalin, Matifeidoliyuchi, welcomed them to his office and detained them eight days. Bolikoni was again sent to escort them; they landed at Yabanchi. From Feierhe Tversk they crossed the Volok Ridge to Solikamsk; the road was mire and they waited on the freeze ten days. They traveled on through Gaigoluo, Heilinofu, Kazan, and Simbirsk. In the eleventh month they reached Saratov, the border between Russia and the Torghuts. Water came from the northeast and bent south; Russians called it the Volga, the Torghuts the Ili. Ayuki Khan's pasture was called Manutohai, ten days' journey away; heavy snow prevented travel.
21
五十三年四月，阿玉奇遣台吉祥偉徵等來迎。 五月，圖理琛等渡額濟勒河，阿喇布珠爾之父納扎爾瑪穆特遣獻馬，卻之。 六年朔，至瑪努託海，阿玉奇擇日聽宣敕。 圖理琛等以上意諭之曰：「阿喇布珠爾已賜爵優養，欲遣歸爾牧地，以策妄阿喇布坦方與爾交惡，恐為所戕。 爾若欲令阿喇布珠爾歸，當自鄂羅斯來迎。 」阿玉奇曰：「我雖外夷，然冠服與中國同。 鄂羅斯乃嗜慾不同、言語不通之國也，天使歸道當察其情狀。 鄂羅斯若以往來數故不假道，則我無由入貢矣。 阿喇布珠爾荷厚恩，與歸土爾扈特同，復何疑慮？ 」阿玉奇及納扎爾瑪穆特等各贈馬及方物，圖理琛等以越境無私交，卻不受。 阿玉奇待之有隆禮，留十四日，筵宴不絕。 复附表奏謝。 圖理琛等遂行，由舊路歸，鄂羅斯遣護如初。 五十四年三月，還京師。
In the fourth month of the fifty-third year Ayuki sent the taiji Weizheng and others to welcome them. In the fifth month Tulichen and the others crossed the Ili; Alabuzhur's father Nazarmamut sent horses as gifts, which they declined. On New Year's day of the fifty-fourth year they reached Manutohai; Ayuki chose a day to hear the edict proclaimed. Tulichen and the others conveyed the imperial intent: 'Alabuzhur has been granted rank and generous maintenance. We wish to send him back to your pastures, but as Tsewang Araptan is now at enmity with you, we fear he may be killed.' If you wish Alabuzhur to return, you must come to meet him through Russia yourself. ' Ayuki said: 'Though I am an outer barbarian, my dress is the same as China's. Russia is a land of different appetites and unintelligible speech; on your return you should observe its condition. If Russia, because of frequent traffic, refuses passage, I shall have no way to offer tribute. Alabuzhur owes deep grace; his return to the Torghuts is the same—what doubt remains? ' Ayuki and Nazarmamut each presented horses and local products; Tulichen and the others, having no private dealings across the border, declined them. Ayuki received them with great ceremony, keeping them fourteen days with feasting unceasing. He also submitted a memorial of thanks. Tulichen and the others then departed, returned by the former route, and Russia sent escorts as before. In the third month of the fifty-fourth year they returned to the capital.
22
是役也，往返三載餘，經行數万裡。 蓋土爾扈特為鄂羅斯所隔，遠阻聲教，而鄂羅斯又故導我使紆道行。 圖理琛奉使無辱命，既歸國，入對，述往還事狀，並撰異域錄，首冠輿圖，次為行記，呈上覽。 上嘉悅，尋授兵部員外郎。 阿喇布珠爾亦遂留牧黨色爾騰不復遣，再傳至其子丹忠，雍正中，遷牧額濟內河。
This mission lasted more than three years round trip and covered tens of thousands of li. The Torghuts were cut off by Russia, far from imperial influence, and Russia deliberately led our envoys by circuitous routes. Tulichen fulfilled his commission without disgrace; on returning he was received in audience, reported the journey, and compiled the Record of Foreign Lands, with maps first and a travel narrative following, which he presented for imperial review. The emperor was pleased and soon appointed him vice director in the Ministry of War. Alabuzhur likewise remained pasturing at Dangse'erteng and was not sent back; the line passed to his son Danzhong, who in the Yongzheng reign moved pasture to the Ili.
23
圖理琛遷郎中。 世宗即位，命赴廣東察籓庫，就擢廣東布政使。 調陝西。 三年，擢巡撫。 五年，召為兵部侍郎，調吏部。 偕喀爾喀郡王額駙策淩等往定喀爾喀與鄂羅斯界。 仍調兵部。 六年，追議前定界時，與鄂羅斯使臣薩瓦鳴砲謝天，私立木牌，並擅納鄂羅斯貿易人入界； 又前任陝西巡撫時，將天下兵數繕摺私給將軍延信，逮問論斬。 詔宥免，遣築扎克拜達里克城。 高宗即位，授內閣學士，遷工部侍郎。 乾隆元年，以老解侍郎任，仍為內閣學士。 二年，引疾去。 五年，卒。
Tulichen was transferred to director. When the Shizong took the throne he was sent to Guangdong to inspect the tribute treasury and was promoted on the spot to provincial treasurer of Guangdong. He was transferred to Shaanxi. In the third year he was promoted to governor. In the fifth year he was summoned as vice minister of War and transferred to Personnel. Together with the Khalkha prince consort Celeng and others he went to fix the border between Khalkha and Russia. He was again transferred to War. In the sixth year, on review of the earlier border mission, it was found that with the Russian envoy Sava he had fired cannon to thank Heaven, privately erected wooden markers, and on his own authority admitted Russian traders within the border; and while previously governor of Shaanxi he had copied the empire's troop totals into a memorial and privately given it to General Yanxin; he was arrested and sentenced to death. An edict pardoned him and sent him to build the city of Zhakebaidarike. When the Gaozong took the throne he was appointed Grand Secretariat academician and transferred to vice minister of Works. In the first year of Qianlong, on account of age he was released from the vice ministership but remained an academician. In the second year he resigned citing illness. In the fifth year he died.
24
何國宗，字翰如，順天大興人。 康熙五十一年進士，改庶吉士，命直內廷學算法。 五十二年，命編輯律曆淵源。 未散館，授編修。 三遷至庶子。 雍正初，授侍讀學士，再遷至內閣學士。
He Guozong, style Hanru, was a native of Daxing in Shuntian. In the fifty-first year of Kangxi he passed the metropolitan examination, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and was ordered to study mathematics in the inner court. In the fifty-second year he was ordered to edit the Origins of Calendrical Astronomy. Before leaving the Academy he was appointed compiler. He was promoted three times to junior tutor. At the beginning of Yongzheng he was appointed Reader-Academician and was promoted again to Grand Secretariat academician.
25
三年，命視黃、運河道，奏請增築戴村石壩，疏濬東昌城南七里河、城北魏家灣及德州城南減河； 又以汶、泗泉源紆遠，請專設管泉通判； 又請修高家堰石堤。 上皆允其請，並以高家堰石堤工衝要，命發帑興修。 復奏言：「運河自臨清以上，賴衛水以濟。 衛水發源百泉，益以丹、洹二水，其流始盛。 請疏百泉為三渠，洹河亦筑壩開渠引水，一分灌田，三分濟運。 」上從其議。 旋以山東巡撫塞楞額奏言國宗等奉使所經州縣，供億白金七千六百有奇。 上責國宗不惜物力，負任使，坐降調。 五年，授大理寺卿。 六年，复擢內閣學士，遷工部侍郎。 八年，命與侍郎牛鈕督修北運河減水壩，並濬引河。 國宗等議捍護河西務北堤及耍兒渡魚鱗壩，別開塌河淀下流賈家沽洩水河，建築三里淺、筐兒港、張家莊諸處挑水壩，上命如議速行。 九年，兼河東河道總督。 田文鏡奏戴村初建玲瓏、亂石、滾水三壩。 汶水盛漲，自壩面流入鹽河歸海。 國宗等增築石壩，水不能過，瀕河連年被患。 請毀石壩，復為亂石、滾水壩。 上責國宗勘工錯誤，貽害民間，奪官。
In the third year he was ordered to inspect the Yellow River and Grand Canal waterways and memorialized to build up the stone dam at Daicun and dredge the Qili River south of Dongchang, Weijiawan north of the city, and the Jianhe south of Dezhou; and because the sources of the Wen and Si were far apart, he asked that a special subprefect manage the springs; and he also asked to repair the stone embankment at Gaojiayan. The emperor approved all his requests, and because the Gaojiayan works were critical, ordered funds disbursed for construction. He memorialized again: 'Above Linqing the canal relies on the Wei River to supply it. The Wei rises at the Hundred Springs, augmented by the Dan and Huan, and only then becomes ample. He asked to divide the Hundred Springs into three channels and to build dams and canals on the Huan, one part for irrigation and three parts for transport. ' The emperor followed his proposal. Soon the Shandong governor Saileng'e memorialized that the counties Guozong's mission passed through had supplied more than seven thousand six hundred taels of silver. The emperor reproached Guozong for not sparing resources, held him responsible for the mission, and demoted him. In the fifth year he was appointed president of the Court of Judicial Review. In the sixth year he was again promoted to Grand Secretariat academician and transferred to vice minister of Works. In the eighth year he was ordered, together with Vice Minister Niu Niu, to supervise repair of flood-relief dams on the Northern Canal and dredging of diversion channels. Guozong and others proposed protecting the north bank at Hexiwu and the Yulin dam at Shua'erdu, opening a drainage channel at Jiajiagu below the Tahe marshes, and building diversion dams at Sanliqian, Kuang'ergang, and Zhangjiazhuang; the emperor ordered these carried out swiftly. In the ninth year he concurrently served as director-general of the Hedong waterways. Tian Wenjing reported that Daicun originally had Linglong, rubble, and rolling-water dams. When the Wen flooded, water spilled over the dam into the salt river and to the sea. Guozong's raised stone dam blocked the flow and brought floods year after year along the river. They urged tearing down the stone dam and restoring the rubble and rolling-water structures. The emperor blamed Guozong for faulty engineering that hurt the populace and removed him from office.
26
乾隆初，起充算學館、律呂館總裁。 九年，賜秩視三品。 尋授左副都御史。 十年，兼領飲天監正。 十三年，遷工部侍郎。
Early in Qianlong he was reappointed chief compiler of the Mathematics and Pitch-Pipe institutes. In the ninth year he received third-grade rank. He was soon made Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. In the tenth year he also headed the Astronomical Bureau. In the thirteenth year he became vice minister of Works.
27
康熙間，聖祖命製皇輿全覽圖，以天度定準望，一度當二百里，遣使如奉天，循行混同、鴨綠二江，至朝鮮分界處，測繪為圖。 以鴨綠、圖門二江間未詳晰，五十年，命烏喇總管穆克登偕按事部員復往詳察。 國宗弟國棟亦以通曆法直內廷。 五十三年，命國棟等周歷江以南諸行省，測北極高度及日景。 五十八年，圖成，為全圖一，離合凡三十二幀，別為分省圖，省各一幀。 命蔣廷錫示群臣，諭曰：「朕費三十餘年心力，始得告成。 山脈水道，俱與禹貢合。 爾以此與九卿詳閱，如有不合處，九卿有知者，舉出奏明。 」乃鐫以銅版，藏內府。
Under Kangxi the Shengzu commissioned the Complete Imperial Atlas, using celestial measurement at two hundred li per degree, and sent surveyors from Fengtian along the Hun and Yalu to Korea's border. The Yalu-Tumen sector remained unclear, so in Kangxi 50 Mukedeng of Ula and ministry agents were sent to survey it again. His brother Guodong, skilled in calendrical science, also served at court. In Kangxi 53 Guodong was sent through the southern provinces to measure polar altitude and noon shadows. In Kangxi 58 the atlas was complete—thirty-two folios for the whole empire plus one sheet per province. The emperor had Jiang Tingxi present it to the ministers, saying: 'More than thirty years of my labor went into this work.' Its mountains and rivers match the Yu Gong. Let the Nine Ministers study it; whoever finds an error should report it. ' The plates were cast in copper and stored in the imperial vault.
28
高宗既定準噶爾，乾隆二十一年，命國宗偕侍衛努克三、哈清阿率欽天監西洋人往伊犁，自巴里坤分西北兩路，測天度繪圖。 既還報，命署左都御史。 二十二年，授禮部尚書。 以京察舉弟國棟，​​坐徇庇，奪官。 尋授編修，直上書房。 二十八年，复授內閣學士。 是歲，上以諸回部悉定，復遣尚書明安圖等往測天度繪圖，是為乾隆內府皇輿圖。 二十六年，遷禮部侍郎。 二十七年，以老休致。 三十一年，卒。
After pacifying the Dzungars, in Qianlong 21 Guozong, Nukesan, Haking'a, and Astronomical Bureau Jesuits surveyed Ili from Barkul in northwestern teams. After they reported back he was appointed acting Left Censor-in-Chief. In the twenty-second year he became Minister of Rites. He recommended his brother Guodong at the capital review and lost his post for favoritism. He was soon made a compiler in the Upper Study. In the twenty-eighth year he again became a Grand Secretariat academician. That year, with the Muslim regions pacified, Ming Antu and others surveyed anew for the Qianlong palace atlas. In the twenty-sixth year he was made vice minister of Rites. In the twenty-seventh year he retired for old age. He died in the thirty-first year.
29
論曰：國家撫有疆宇，謂之版圖，版言乎其有民，圖言乎其有地。 聖祖東訪長白山，西探河源，北撫土爾扈特，武默訥、舒蘭、圖理琛奉使稱職。 觀所還奏，曲折詳盡，歷歷如繪。 國宗以明算事聖祖，又幸老壽，迨高宗朝，詣新疆測繪。 康熙、乾隆兩內府圖皆躬與編摹。 揆之於古，其裴秀、賈耽之倫歟？
The commentary observes: A pacified realm is called ban tu—ban denotes its people, tu its terrain. The Shengzu worshipped at Changbai, traced the Yellow River's source, and received the Torghuts; Wumune, Shulan, and Tulichen served with distinction. Their memorials are intricate yet complete, vivid as painted scrolls. Guozong's mathematics served Kangxi; he lived long enough to survey Xinjiang under Qianlong. He helped compile both the Kangxi and Qianlong palace maps. By ancient standards, does he not rank with Pei Xiu and Jia Dan?
