Hongwu, Yinmi's second son: created second-rank Duke-general Who Guards the State in Qianlong 28, promoted to beizi in Qianlong 39, was stripped and dismissed for cause in Qianlong 43, was created baron who receives grace in Qianlong 59 and stripped again in Jiaqing 4, was restored as baron who receives grace in Jiaqing 14, and died in Jiaqing 16.Yongsong, Hongwu's fifth son: inherited as baron who receives grace in Jiaqing 16, died in Daoguang 7, and was posthumously enfeoffed as beizi in Daoguang 17.Mianxun, Yongsong's second son: inherited as baron who receives grace in Daoguang 7, inherited Yongzhu's beizi title in Daoguang 26, and died in Guangxu 19.Yijun, Mianxun's second son: created Duke Who Guards the State in Tongzhi 7 and died in Tongzhi 10.Zaixin, Yijun's son: inherited as Assistant State Duke in Tongzhi 11, inherited as Duke Who Guards the State in Guangxu 20, and died in Guangxu 26.Puyun, Zaixin's first son: inherited as Duke Who Guards the State in Guangxu 28.
Puqi, Zaixin's second son: created Assistant State Duke in Guangxu 29.
Pubing, Zaixin's third son: created Assistant State Duke in Guangxu 29.
Pumu, Zaixin's fourth son: created Assistant State Duke in Guangxu 29.
Hongkang, Yinmi's third son: created second-rank Duke-general Who Guards the State in Qianlong 35, was granted Duke Who Assists the State outside the Eight Privileges rank in Jiaqing 14, and died in Jiaqing 19.Yongchun, Hongkang's first son: created State Duke in Jiaqing 7, inherited as Assistant State Duke in Jiaqing 19, and was stripped and dismissed for cause in Daoguang 11.
