=開泰=開泰，烏雅氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 雍正二年進士，改庶吉士，授編修。 九年，遷侍講。 上御門，開泰未入侍班，黜令乾清門行走。 十三年，复編修。 乾隆元年，遷國子監司業。 八年，遷祭酒。 督江蘇學政。 再遷內閣學士。 三遷兵部侍郎，仍留學政任。 十年，授湖北巡撫。 疏言：「社倉較常平尤近於民，而弊亦易滋。 湖北社倉穀麥五十二萬石有奇，散在諸鄉，恐多虧缺。 應飭道府按部所至，便宜抽驗。 」調江西。 十三年，又調湖南。 疏言：「戶部諮各省常平倉穀，以雍正舊額為準。 湖南溢額穀五十五萬餘石，令糶價儲庫。 臣維雍正舊額七十餘萬石。 湖南夙稱產米，乾隆二年至八年，諸省赴湖南購米，先後計百七十五萬有奇。 中間又撥運福建、江蘇。 若盡糶溢額之穀，遇本省需用或鄰疆告糴，必致倉儲缺額，買補不易。 」疏上，以留心積貯嘉之。 十五年，有壽掄元者，自言南河同知，赴湖南採木，布政使孫灝諭永州府為料理。 尋得其詐偽狀，開泰以聞，但言灝殊為未諳。 上以灝瞻徇，何得但言未諳，知為開泰門生，斥其徇庇，下吏部嚴議，議奪官，命留任。 尋調貴州。 十八年，疏言：「古州募軍屯田，戶上田六畝，中田八畝，下田十畝。 今食指日多，生計艱難，請準屯戶入伍充兵。 」許之。 擢湖廣總督，加太子少傅。
Kai Tai, of the Wuya clan, was a Manchu of the Plain Yellow Banner. In Yongzheng 2 he passed the jinshi examination, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and was appointed a compiler. In the ninth year he was promoted to Reader-in-waiting. When the emperor held audience at the Gate, Kai Tai had not taken his place in the attendance roster and was demoted to duty at the Gate of Heavenly Purity. In the thirteenth year he was reappointed compiler. In Qianlong 1 he became Vice-Director of the Imperial Academy. In the eighth year he was made Chancellor of the Imperial Academy. He served as educational commissioner for Jiangsu. He was promoted again to Grand Secretariat academician. After three more promotions he became Vice Minister of War while keeping his educational commission. In the tenth year he was appointed governor of Hubei. He submitted a memorial: "Community granaries lie even closer to the people than the Ever-Normal granaries, yet corruption is also easier to breed there. Hubei's community granaries held over 520,000 shi of grain and wheat spread through the countryside, and he feared heavy losses and shortages. He urged that circuit and prefectural officials be instructed to conduct spot checks wherever their tours took them. " He was then transferred to Jiangxi. In the thirteenth year he was moved to Hunan. He memorialized: "The Ministry of Revenue has asked each province about Ever-Normal granary stocks, using the Yongzheng quotas as the baseline. Hunan's surplus amounted to more than 550,000 shi above quota, with orders to sell the grain and deposit the proceeds in the treasury. I maintain that the Yongzheng quota was over 700,000 shi. Hunan has long been known as a rice-producing region; from Qianlong 2 through 8 other provinces bought rice there, totaling more than 1.75 million shi. During that period additional shipments were dispatched to Fujian and Jiangsu. If the entire surplus were sold, any local need or neighboring request for grain would leave the granaries short and hard to refill. " The emperor commended his care for grain reserves when the memorial arrived. In the fifteenth year one Shou Lunyuan claimed to be associate prefect of the Southern Rivers and came to Hunan to collect timber; Provincial Treasurer Sun Hao told Yongzhou Prefecture to handle his arrangements. When the fraud came to light, Kai Tai reported it but described Sun Hao only as seriously inexperienced. The emperor said Sun Hao had plainly shown favoritism and asked why Kai Tai spoke only of inexperience; learning Sun was Kai Tai's student, he denounced Kai Tai for shielding him, sent the case to the Ministry of Personnel for severe review, and though they recommended dismissal, ordered him to stay in office. He was soon transferred to Guizhou. In the eighteenth year he memorialized: "At Guzhou recruited troops farmed garrison fields allotting six mu of top-grade land per household, eight of middle grade, and ten of lower grade. As dependents increase and livelihoods grow strained, he asked that garrison households be allowed to enlist as regular soldiers. " The request was granted. He was promoted to governor-general of Huguang and made Junior Tutor of the Heir Apparent.
2
二十年，調四川。 金川土司莎羅奔與革布什咱土司色楞敦多布初為婚媾，繼乃相怨構兵。 旁近綽斯甲布、鄂克什、雜穀、巴旺、丹壩、明正、章谷、小金川諸土司皆不直莎羅奔。 二十三年，莎羅奔攻吉地。 吉地，色楞敦多布所居寨也。 開泰與提督岳鍾琪檄游擊楊青、都司夏尚德等率兵分屯章谷、泰宁，令鄂克什、雜穀援革布什咱，攻金川，莎羅奔引退。 尋復攻破吉地，色楞敦多布走泰宁求援，開泰复檄諸土司出兵助之，調雜穀土練千人分屯丹壩、章谷、泰宁，發黎、雅、峨邊兵屯打箭爐，諭郎卡撤兵。 郎卡，莎羅奔從子，為副酋，主兵事者也。 事聞，上謂：「番目相攻，於打箭爐何與？ 」疑郎卡擾邊，命開泰具實覆奏。 開泰尋疏報章谷、巴旺土兵擊敗金川，莎羅奔焚吉地走，盡复革布什咱境，留綽斯甲布、明正兩土司兵分守之，使色楞敦多布歸寨。 上諭曰：「番民挾仇攻擊，不必繩以內地官法。 宜以番攻番，處以靜鎮。 」旋加太子太保。 二十四年，松潘鎮總兵楊朝棟入覲，開泰與鍾琪奏朝棟衰老，難期勝任。 上責開泰何以不先奏，下吏部議，奪官，命仍留任。
In the twentieth year he was transferred to Sichuan. The Jinchuan chieftain Salobe and the Geshiza chieftain Selengdunduobu were first joined by marriage, then fell out and took up arms against each other. Nearby chieftains of Zhuosijia Bu, Eke Shi, Zagu, Bawang, Danba, Mingzheng, Zhanggu, and Lesser Jinchuan all sided against Salobe. In the twenty-third year Salobe attacked Jidi. Jidi was Selengdunduobu's home stockade. Kai Tai and Regional Commander Yue Zhongqi ordered Brigade Commander Yang Qing, Regimental Commander Xia Shangde, and others to station troops at Zhanggu and Taining, directed Eke Shi and Zagu to relieve Geshiza and strike Jinchuan, and Salobe pulled back. Salobe soon seized Jidi again; Selengdunduobu fled to Taining for help; Kai Tai again called on the chieftains for troops, posted a thousand Zagu militiamen across Danba, Zhanggu, and Taining, sent troops from Li, Ya, and Ebian to Dajianlu, and told Langka to withdraw. Langka, Salobe's nephew, served as deputy chieftain and handled military affairs. When the matter was reported, the emperor asked: "When native chiefs fight each other, what business is that of Dajianlu? " Suspecting Langka was harassing the frontier, he ordered Kai Tai to submit a truthful report. Kai Tai soon reported that Zhanggu and Bawang native soldiers had routed Jinchuan; Salobe burned Jidi and fled, Geshiza's territory was fully restored, troops from Zhuosijia Bu and Mingzheng were left to hold it, and Selengdunduobu returned to his stockade. The emperor directed: "When frontier tribes feud, they need not be judged by interior statutes. Let tribe fight tribe and manage affairs through quiet deterrence. " He was soon made Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent. In the twenty-fourth year Yang Chaodong, commander of the Songpan garrison, came to court; Kai Tai and Yue Zhongqi reported that he was too old to be relied on. The emperor faulted Kai Tai for not having reported this sooner, sent the case to the Ministry of Personnel, and though they voted to strip his rank, allowed him to keep his post.
3
二十七年，莎羅奔死，郎卡應襲。 例，土司承襲，鄰封諸土司具結。 開泰以郎卡與諸土司皆不協，令毋取結，疏聞，上許之，命嚴諭郎卡知恩守法。 未幾，郎卡侵丹壩，取所屬瑪讓，開泰檄綽斯甲布往援，使守備溫欽等赴金川詰責。 上諭曰：「郎卡狼子野心，即使詰責伏罪，豈肯永守約束？ 諸土司援兵既集，能協力剿除，分據其地，轉可相安； 若諸部不能並力剿除，而郎卡怙惡不悛，亦非開泰、岳鍾琪四川綠營兵能任其事，應臨時奏請進止。 」二十八年六月，開泰奏九土司大舉擊破金川。 上聞郎卡使人詣成都，開泰許進謁，撫慰之，而陰令九土司進兵，諭曰：「郎卡於綽斯甲布等屢肆欺凌，眾土司合力報復。 開泰既聞其事，惟應明白宣示，諭令悉銳往攻； 而於郎卡來人嚴為拒絕，且諭以爾結怨鄰境，誰肯甘心？ 斷不能曲為庇護。 如此，則郎卡既不敢逞強，綽斯甲布等亦可洩忿。 乃既用譎以籠絡郎卡，又隱為各土司援助，郎卡素狡黠，豈能掩其耳目？ 殊非駕馭邊夷之道。 」命奪官，以頭等侍衛赴伊犁辦事。 尋卒。
In the twenty-seventh year Salobe died and Langka was due to inherit. By custom, succession required guaranty bonds from neighboring chieftains. Kai Tai, finding Langka at odds with them all, waived the bonds; the emperor agreed and ordered Langka sternly reminded to show gratitude and keep the law. Soon Langka invaded Danba and took Marang under its jurisdiction; Kai Tai called Zhuosijia Bu to Danba's aid and sent Assistant Commander Wen Qin to Jinchuan to rebuke him. The emperor said: "Langka is wolf-hearted; even if rebuked into submission, will he forever keep his word? If allied chieftain forces are already assembled and can jointly destroy him and partition his territory, peace may follow; but if they cannot act together and Langka remains unreformed, Kai Tai and Yue Zhongqi's Sichuan Green Standard army cannot finish the job and must report to the throne for orders when needed. " In the sixth month of the twenty-eighth year Kai Tai reported that nine chieftains had mounted a major assault and crushed Jinchuan. Learning Langka had sent envoys to Chengdu, Kai Tai received them and offered reassurance while secretly urging the nine chieftains forward; the emperor said: "Langka has long bullied Zhuosijia Bu and others; the chieftains should unite to repay it. Once Kai Tai knew, he should have declared openly that they must strike with all their strength; toward Langka's envoys he should have refused them outright and said: You have offended every neighbor—who will tolerate you? He could never bend the rules to protect you. Then Langka would not dare press his advantage and Zhuosijia Bu and the others could satisfy their grievance. Instead he deceived Langka with conciliation while secretly helping the chieftains; Langka is shrewd—how could that escape him? That is no way to govern frontier peoples. " He was dismissed and sent to Yili as a first-class imperial bodyguard. He died soon afterward.
4
=阿爾泰=阿爾泰，伊爾根覺羅氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 雍正間，以副榜貢生授宗人府筆帖式。 乾隆中，屢遷至山東巡撫。 以山東產山綢，疏請令民間就山坡隙地廣植桲欏，免其昇科。 歲大水，阿爾泰先後濬兗州、沂州支渠三十有九，曹州、單縣順堤河二百餘裡； 培南旺、蜀山湖民埝； 導章丘珍珠、麻塘二泉，新城五龍河溉民田； 並及高苑、博興、惠民諸縣近水地，皆令蓺稻。 築洸河堤至於馬場湖，以衛濟寧州城，析白馬湖引入獨山湖以疏泗水，開汶上稻田數百頃。 濟東諸州縣瀕徒駭、馬頰兩河，支流相貫注，及哨馬營、四女寺支河，皆次第疏治。 濬衛河自德州至於館陶凡三百餘裡。 洩壽張積水自沙、趙二河入運，洩東平積水入會泉、大清諸河，洩濟南、東昌諸州縣積水。 開支河三十餘，循官道為壕，引水自壕入支河，自支河入徒駭、大清諸河。 漳、汶合流，開引河，增子埝，以防盛漲。 阿爾泰撫山東七年，治水利有績，擢四川總督，加太子太保。
Aertai, of the Irgen Gioro clan, was a Manchu of the Plain Yellow Banner. Under Yongzheng he entered service as a clerk in the Imperial Clan Court through the secondary jinshi list. Under Qianlong he rose through repeated promotions to governor of Shandong. Because Shandong produced mountain silk, he asked that commoners be encouraged to plant oak on spare hillsides without added land tax. In flood years Aertai dredged thirty-nine branch canals in Yanzhou and Yizhou and over two hundred li of levee channels in Caozhou and Shan County; he strengthened civilian dikes at Nanwang and Shushan lakes; he led the Zhangqiu Pearl and Matang springs and Xincheng's Wulong River to irrigate fields; and ordered rice planting on lowlands in Gaoyuan, Boxing, Huimin, and neighboring counties. He built Guang River levees to Machang Lake to shield Jining, diverted Baima Lake into Dushan Lake to relieve the Si, and opened hundreds of qing of paddy at Wenshang. Eastern Ji prefectures along the Tuhai and Maji had interlinked tributaries; the Shaoma Camp and Sinvsi branch channels were cleared in sequence. He dredged more than three hundred li of the Wei River from Dezhou to Guantao. He discharged Shouzhang floods through the Sha and Zhao into the Grand Canal, Dongping floods into the Huishui and Daqing rivers, and floodwater across Jinan and Dongchang. He cut more than thirty branch channels, trenched the highways, and routed water from trenches into branches and from branches into the Tuhai and Daqing. At the Zhang–Wen confluence he opened diversion works and added subsidiary dikes against peak surges. After seven years governing Shandong with notable hydraulic works, he was promoted to Sichuan governor-general and made Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent.
5
阿爾泰至四川，議平治道路：陸道北訖廣元，西達松潘，東抵夔州，護其傾欹，補其缺落，兼葺大渡河瀘定橋； 水道自萬縣入湖廣境，鑿治險灘凡一百有奇。 議以牧廠餘地招佃為田。 議置義倉，捐穀千餘石以倡。 議開南川金佛山磺礦。 議築都江大堰。 議松潘、雜穀、打箭爐三廳置倉儲麥稞，備邊儲。 上皆從其請。
On reaching Sichuan he proposed road works: land routes north to Guangyuan, west to Songpan, and east to Kuizhou, shoring slopes and gaps and repairing the Luding Bridge on the Dadu; water routes from Wan County into Huguang, with more than a hundred hazardous shoals cleared. He proposed leasing abandoned pasture to settlers for farming. He proposed charity granaries and gave over a thousand shi of grain to start them. He proposed opening the Jinfo Mountain sulfur mines in Nanchuan. He proposed restoring the great Dujiang irrigation dam. He proposed frontier granaries at Songpan, Zagu, and Dajianlu for wheat and barley reserves. The emperor approved every proposal.
6
初，徵金川，以頭人郎卡出降，罷兵。 三十一年，復為亂，掠丹壩、巴旺。 阿爾泰策以番攻番，令旁近綽斯甲布諸土司攻之。 秋出行邊，至雜穀腦。 郎卡使請還所侵丹壩碉卡。 復與提督董天弼進至康巴達，郎卡出謁，阿爾泰許如所請，並畀以新印。 疏聞，上戒毋遷就苟安。 三十五年，小金川頭人僧格桑掠鄂克什，阿爾泰赴達木巴宗，僧格桑出謁，還侵地。 尋授武英殿大學士，仍領總督。 三十六年，召還京，入閣治事。 既，復令出領總督。 金川頭人索諾木攻革布什咱，僧格桑亦圍達木巴宗，侵明正土司。 阿爾泰疏言：「兩金川相比，如議出師，需兵既多，糜餉亦鉅。 茲令董天弼臨之以兵，仍使游擊宋元俊宣諭索諾木。 」上責阿爾泰議非是，決策用兵，令定邊右副將軍溫福視師，佐以侍郎桂林，諭斥阿爾泰掩飾偷安，奪大學士、總督，留軍治餉，以桂林代為總督。 師克約咱，上以阿爾泰鑄大砲利軍行，予散秩大臣銜。
When Jinchuan was first suppressed, headman Langka surrendered and the campaign ended. In the thirty-first year he rebelled again and raided Danba and Bawang. Aertai again used tribe-against-tribe strategy and ordered neighboring chieftains including Zhuosijia Bu to attack. That autumn he toured the frontier as far as Zagunao. Langka asked to return the Danba forts he had seized. With Regional Commander Dong Tianbi he advanced to Kangbada; Langka came to audience; Aertai agreed and gave him a new seal. On report, the emperor warned him not to settle for an easy peace. In the thirty-fifth year Senggesang of Lesser Jinchuan raided Eke Shi; Aertai went to Damubazong; Senggesang attended and restored the territory. He was soon made Grand Secretary of the Hall of Military Glory while retaining the governorship. In the thirty-sixth year he was recalled to serve in the Grand Council at the capital. He was soon sent back to resume the governorship. Jinchuan headman Suonuomu attacked Geshiza; Senggesang besieged Damubazong and encroached on Mingzheng. Aertai memorialized: "Greater and Lesser Jinchuan border each other; any campaign would require large forces and immense supplies. He ordered Dong Tianbi to press them with troops while dispatching Brigade Commander Song Yuanjun to proclaim the court's will to Suonuomu. The emperor rejected Aertai's proposal, resolved on war, and put Frontier Deputy Right General Wen Fu in command with Vice Minister Guilin as his deputy; he condemned Aertai for evasion and complacency, stripped him of his grand secretary and governorship, kept him with the army to handle supplies, and replaced him with Guilin as governor. After Yuezha fell, the emperor praised Aertai's cannon casting for aiding the campaign and granted him minister-without-portfolio rank.
7
三十七年，與總兵宋元俊劾桂林覆軍諱敗，上為罷桂林，即命阿爾泰攝總督。 俄移督湖廣。 阿爾泰疏言：「各路轉餉，當招商承運。 西路去內地近，南路山險途長，商不肯應募，當增運值。 火藥已運罄，當令雲南、陝西協助。 」上謂：「阿爾泰專領轉餉，何不早籌畫？ 今福隆安、阿桂皆至南路，始以一奏塞責。 」命毋往湖廣，仍以散秩大臣留軍督餉。 未幾，阿桂疏言軍至卡丫，無五日之糧； 又言綽斯甲布轉餉將一月猶未至。 阿爾泰亦自陳請奪職從軍。 上責其倚老負恩，始終不肯以國事為念，命逮問。
In the thirty-seventh year he and Regional Commander Song Yuanjun accused Guilin of hiding a catastrophic defeat; the emperor removed Guilin and at once put Aertai in charge as acting governor. He was soon reassigned as governor-general of Huguang. Aertai memorialized: "Supply transport on every route should be contracted to merchants. The western route lies near the interior, but the southern route is mountainous and long, and merchants refuse to sign on unless transport fees are raised. Gunpowder stocks are exhausted; Yunnan and Shaanxi should be asked to help. The emperor said: "Aertai alone oversees supply transport—why did he not plan ahead? Only after Fukangan and Agui had both reached the southern front did he file one memorial to dodge responsibility. He was told not to take up Huguang and was kept with the army as minister without portfolio to supervise supplies. Soon Agui reported that troops at Kayak had less than five days' rations; and that Zhuosijia Bu's supply convoy was still a month late. Aertai also volunteered to be stripped of rank and serve in the field. The emperor denounced him for abusing his seniority and ingratitude, for never putting the state's interests first, and ordered him arrested.
8
阿爾泰初至四川，上以天壇立燈竿，下四川求楠木。 阿爾泰附運木材以進，言出養廉採獻。 既乃私語人，謂他日且以此負累。 語聞上，上心慊之。 至是，詔罪狀阿爾泰，猶及此事，斥為昧良飾詐。 川東道托隆入見，發阿爾泰贓私，下繼任總督富勒渾嚴鞫。 三十八年，獄具，擬斬，上命賜自盡。
When Aertai first arrived in Sichuan, the court was erecting lamp poles at the Temple of Heaven and requisitioning nanmu timber from the province. Aertai shipped timber along as a gift, claiming it came from his integrity stipend. Later he confided to someone that this gift would one day become his undoing. When the emperor heard of it, he took deep offense. Now the edict condemning Aertai still cited this affair, calling him deceitful and false-hearted. Eastern Sichuan Circuit Intendant Tuolong came to court and exposed Aertai's graft; his successor Fulehun was ordered to conduct a rigorous trial. In the thirty-eighth year the case was closed with a recommendation of decapitation; the emperor ordered him granted suicide instead.
9
=桂林=桂林，伊爾根覺羅氏，滿洲鑲藍旗人，兩廣總督鶴年子。 桂林自廩生入貲為工部主事。 累遷山西按察使。 乾隆三十六年三月，擢戶部侍郎、軍機處行走。 九月，命佐定邊右副將軍溫福討金川。 十一月，授四川總督。 小金川頭人在卡外投文餽土宜，桂林卻不受，檄罪狀其酋僧格桑。 旋督兵收約咱，進克其東山梁大小碉五、石卡二十餘。 疏請添調黔、陝兵五千益師，上許益陝、甘兵三千。 桂林旋督總兵宋元俊攻卡丫，進據墨爾多山梁。 上嘉其措置合宜，手詔謂：「無意中用汝，竟能得力。 亦賴在軍機處半年，日耹朕訓也。 」
Guilin, of the Irgen Gioro clan, was a Manchu of the Bordered Blue Banner and the son of Huguang governor-general Henian. Guilin bought his way into office from grain-student status and became a principal secretary in the Ministry of Works. He rose to judicial commissioner of Shanxi. In the third month of Qianlong 36 he became vice minister of revenue and an attendant of the Grand Council. In the ninth month he was ordered to assist Frontier Deputy Right General Wen Fu against Jinchuan. In the eleventh month he was made governor-general of Sichuan. Lesser Jinchuan headmen outside the passes sent letters and native gifts; Guilin refused them and issued a denunciation of chieftain Senggesang. He soon recovered Yuezha and took five blockhouses large and small on the eastern ridge plus more than twenty stone forts. He asked for five thousand reinforcements from Guizhou and Shaanxi; the emperor approved three thousand from Shaanxi and Gansu. Guilin then had Regional Commander Song Yuanjun assault Kayak and seize Moerduo Ridge. The emperor praised his handling of affairs and wrote personally: "I never meant to rely on you, yet you have proved capable. That owes something to six months in the Grand Council, hearing my guidance every day. "
10
三十七年，克卡丫，复破克郭松、甲木、噶爾金。 進克噶爾金後山梁，分兵攻東山梁，襲阿仰，自墨壟溝進取達烏圍。 是時大金川酋索諾木攻陷革布什咱，屯兵其地。 桂林議乘索諾木兵力未備、革布什咱人心未定，與元俊分兵五道並進，並約將軍溫福合擊，密令革布什咱降酋旺勒丹等約其戚加琿爾為內應，遂收革布什咱寨落七十餘裡。 旋令元俊及守備陳定國率綽斯甲布土兵屯甲爾壟壩，進攻默資溝、吉地，斷其水道，進攻丹東。 上獎桂林甚合機宜，促元俊乘勝深入取索諾木。
In the thirty-seventh year he captured Kayak and then Kesong, Jiamu, and Ga'erjin. He took the ridge behind Ga'erjin, sent detachments against the eastern ridge, struck A'yang, and advanced from Molong Gorge toward Dawuwei. By then Greater Jinchuan chieftain Suonuomu had overrun Geshiza and was encamped there. Guilin proposed striking while Suonuomu's forces were unprepared and Geshiza's loyalty still uncertain; he and Yuanjun advanced in five columns, coordinated a joint attack with General Wen Fu, and secretly had surrendered Geshiza chiefs including Wangledan enlist their kinsman Jiahuan'er as an inside agent, recovering more than seventy li of Geshiza settlements. He then sent Yuanjun and Assistant Commander Chen Dingguo with Zhuosijia Bu militia to hold Ji'erlongba, assault Mozigou and Jidi, cut the water supply, and press Dandong. The emperor applauded Guilin's timing and urged Yuanjun to follow up and capture Suonuomu.
11
桂林遣裨將自東山梁墨壟溝越嶺進攻，別遣兵出間道，自札哇窠山梁縋崖設伏師。 既度東山梁墨壟溝，札哇窠伏兵亦起，賊敗竄，克大碉一、石卡二十一。 別遣參將常泰環攻黨哩，都司李天貴等攻沙沖，革布什咱頭人為內應，賊盡殲。 黨哩、沙沖地並復。 總兵英泰等復攻克達烏官寨。 上嘉其功，賜御用玉韘。 再進攻克格烏巴桑及那隆山嶺。 元俊別攻克丹東及覺拉喇嘛寺，誅賊渠三百、番眾百三十餘。 革布什咱地盡复，桂林檄定國將所調綽斯甲布兵駐界上聽調。 上以革布什咱既复，正當乘勝進剿金川，攻其無備，責桂林失算。
Guilin sent subordinate officers over the eastern ridge via Molong Gorge and detachments by hidden paths to rappel down Zhawawo Ridge and lay ambushes. After crossing Molong Gorge on the eastern ridge, the Zhawawo ambush sprang up; the enemy broke and fled, and one major blockhouse and twenty-one stone forts fell. Other columns under Colonel Chang Tai besieged Dangli while Regimental Commander Li Tiangui attacked Shachong, with Geshiza headmen as inside help; the enemy was wiped out. Dangli and Shachong were recovered. Regional Commander Ying Tai and others retook the Dawu garrison stockade. The emperor rewarded their success with an imperial jade archer's ring. They pushed on to Gewubasang and Nalong Ridge. Yuanjun separately took Dandong and Jue La Lama Temple, killing three hundred rebel leaders and more than 130 tribesmen. With Geshiza fully restored, Guilin ordered Dingguo to hold the Zhuosijia Bu troops on the frontier for future orders. The emperor said that with Geshiza back they ought to press the victory into Jinchuan while the enemy was off guard, and he faulted Guilin for poor judgment.
12
桂林复督兵攻達烏東岸山梁，參將薛琮戰沒，琮驍將，深入糧盡。 桂林既失期不會師，又不以時遣援，軍盡覆，疏請治罪，述戰狀不敢盡。 元俊與散秩大臣阿爾泰劾其虛誑，並言桂林在卡丫建屋宇以居，迫屬僚供應，與副都統鐵保、提督汪騰龍等終日酣飲，諸將罕得見； 密令騰龍畀總兵王萬邦白金五百，贖被掠官兵，希圖掩飾。 上奪桂林職，命額駙、尚書、公福隆安馳往按治，尋奏所劾皆虛，惟官兵傷損不即察奏屬實； 至贖被掠官兵，乃在軍戶部郎中汪承霈聞巴旺、布拉克底土兵歸失道，官兵告桂林，發白金五百交騰龍備賞，事為元俊構陷，請分別治罪。 上以桂林在軍日親曲糱，止圖安逸，不能與士卒同甘苦，致北山梁傷損多兵，不得為無罪，命戍伊犁。 三十八年七月，予三等侍衛銜，仍詣軍前督糧運。 四十年，授頭等侍衛。 尋授四川提督，遷兩廣總督。 卒，加太子太保銜，諡壯敏。
Guilin again assaulted the eastern ridge at Dawu; Colonel Xue Cong was killed—a bold commander who had pushed deep until his supplies ran out. Guilin missed the rendezvous and failed to send timely relief; the army was annihilated; he asked to be punished and described the battle but withheld the full truth. Yuanjun and Aertai accused him of deception, reporting that Guilin had built quarters at Kayak, forced subordinates to wait on him, and spent days drinking with Associate Commissioner Tie Bao and Regional Commander Wang Tenglong while his generals seldom gained audience; he secretly told Tenglong to give Regional Commander Wang Wanbang five hundred taels to ransom captured troops, hoping to hide the disaster. The emperor removed Guilin and sent imperial son-in-law and minister Duke Fukangan to investigate; the inquiry found the charges largely false, though Guilin had indeed delayed reporting casualties; The silver for ransoming captives came when Ministry Director Wang Chengpei, attached to the army, learned that Bawang and Bulakdi militia had lost their way on return; troops reported to Guilin, who issued five hundred taels to Tenglong for rewards—an episode Yuanjun had fabricated; they asked punishments be apportioned accordingly. The emperor held that Guilin's fondness for drink and comfort in camp, and his failure to share hardships with his men, had contributed to heavy losses on the northern ridge; he could not be fully acquitted and was exiled to Yili. In the seventh month of the thirty-eighth year he was given third-class bodyguard rank and sent back to the front to supervise grain transport. In the fortieth year he was made a first-class bodyguard. He soon became Sichuan regional commander, then governor-general of Liangguang. He died; he was posthumously made Junior Grand Tutor and given the temple name Zhuangmin.
13
=溫福=溫福，字履綏，費莫氏，滿洲鑲紅旗人，文華殿大學士溫達孫也。 自繙譯舉人授兵部筆​​帖式。 乾隆初，累遷戶部郎中。 外擢湖南布政使，歷四年； 移貴州布政使，亦四年。 坐平遠民閧訟庭、按治草率，奪職，戍烏里雅蘇台。 二十三年，起內閣侍讀學士。 從定邊將軍兆惠討霍集占，戰葉爾羌，槍傷顴。 擢內閣學士，遷倉場侍郎，予雲騎尉世職。 外授福建巡撫，內遷吏部侍郎、軍機處行走，進理籓院尚書。
Wen Fu, styled Lüshou, of the Feimo clan, was a Manchu of the Bordered Red Banner and the grandson of Grand Secretary Wenda of the Hall of Literary Glory. He entered as a translator licentiate and became a clerk in the Ministry of War. Early in Qianlong he rose to director in the Ministry of Revenue. He was promoted to Hunan provincial treasurer and served four years; then Guizhou provincial treasurer for another four years. After commoners rioted in court at Pingyuan and he handled the case perfunctorily, he was dismissed and exiled to Uriankhai. In the twenty-third year he was recalled as a grand secretariat reader. He served under Frontier General Zhaohui against the Khoja rebels, fought at Yarkand, and was shot in the cheek. He was made a grand secretariat academician and vice minister of the granary and granted the hereditary yunqiwei rank. He served as Fujian governor, returned to court as vice minister of personnel and Grand Council attendant, and rose to minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs.
14
三十六年，師徵金川，授定邊右副將軍，以侍郎桂林佐之，共討賊。 溫福自汶川出西路，桂林自打箭爐出南路。 時小金川頭人澤旺子僧格桑割地乞援於大金川頭人索諾木，索諾木潛遣兵助之。 上命先剿小金​​川，且勿聲大金川罪。 溫福至打箭爐，分兵三道入：溫福出巴朗拉，提督董天弼自甲金達援達木巴宗，總督阿爾泰自約咱攻僧格桑。 十一月，擢武英殿大學士。 十二月，至巴朗拉，戰三晝夜，賊敗去。 三十七年正月，取達木巴宗。 進攻斯底葉安，而分軍出別斯滿、瑪爾瓦爾濟，兩路夾擊，進克資哩。 再進克東瑪，再進克路頂宗及喀木色爾，取諸碉寨。 再進得博爾根山梁，並攻克得瑪覺烏寨落，攻公雅山。 十二月，授定邊將軍，以阿桂、豐升額副之。 進克明郭宗，再進克底木達。 底木達者，僧格桑父澤旺所居寨也。 師至，俘澤旺，檻致京師，誅於市，而僧格桑奔大金川。 溫福檄索諾木令縛獻僧格桑，不應。
In the thirty-sixth year, when troops were mobilized against Jinchuan, he was made Frontier Deputy Right General with Vice Minister Guilin as deputy to suppress the rebels. Wen Fu advanced west from Wenchuan; Guilin advanced south from Dajianlu. Lesser Jinchuan's Senggesang, son of Zewang, ceded territory to Greater Jinchuan's Suonuomu for help, and Suonuomu secretly sent reinforcements. The emperor ordered them to crush Lesser Jinchuan first and not yet publicize Greater Jinchuan's guilt. At Dajianlu Wen Fu split his force three ways: he himself via Balang La, Regional Commander Dong Tianbi from Jiajinda toward Damubazong, and Governor-General Aertai from Yuezha against Senggesang. In the eleventh month he was made Grand Secretary of the Hall of Military Glory. In the twelfth month he reached Balang La and fought three days and nights until the enemy broke and withdrew. In the first month of the thirty-seventh year he captured Damubazong. He assaulted Sidiye'an while detachments moved through Biesiman and Ma'erwa'erji in a pincer and took Zili. Pressing on he took Dongma, then Ludingzong and Kamuse'er, seizing the surrounding blockhouses. He pushed to Bo'ergen Ridge, took Demajuewu, and assaulted Gongyashan. In the twelfth month he was made Frontier General with Agui and Fengsheng'e as deputies. He took Mingguozong and then Dimuda. Dimuda was the stockade of Senggesang's father Zewang. The army captured Zewang, sent him caged to the capital for execution, while Senggesang fled to Greater Jinchuan. Wen Fu ordered Suonuomu to turn over Senggesang in chains; Suonuomu refused.
15
上將進討大金川，溫福等疏言：「前此張廣泗徵金川，十路、七路分合不常，實祗有六路，皆以抵勒烏圍、噶爾依為主。 一為卡撒正路，自美諾至噶爾依，約五程，為傅恆進兵路； 一為丹壩，自維州橋經番地抵勒烏圍，約二十餘程，中有穆津岡天險，為岳鍾琪進兵路； 一地名僧格桑，自美諾抵噶爾依，六七程，即總兵馬良柱所行路； 一為革布什咱，一為馬爾邦，皆距噶爾依六七程，險狹難行； 一為綽斯甲布寨至勒烏圍三程，至噶爾依亦三程，均隔大河，碉寨林立，難攻。 此外又有俄坡一路，從綽斯甲布寨至勒烏圍，僅二程，路較平。 今當由卡撒正路進兵，其俄坡一路，既有綽斯甲布土司原出兵復其侵地，可為犄角。 其餘各路，分兵牽制，使不能兼顧。 」於是溫福自功噶爾拉入，阿桂自當噶爾拉入，豐升額自綽斯甲布入。 溫福性剛愎，不廣諮方略，惟襲訥親、張廣泗故事，以碉卡攻碉卡，修築千計。 所將兵二萬餘，強半散在各碉卡。 每逾數日當奏事，即督兵攻碉。 士卒多傷亡，諮怨無鬥志。 溫福日置酒高會，參贊伍岱歎曰：「焉有為帥若此而能制勝者？ 」因密疏聞上，溫福亦疏劾伍岱。 上命豐升額及額駙色布騰巴勒珠爾按治。 溫福又言色布騰巴勒珠爾朋比傾陷，上為奪伍岱職，令色布騰巴勒珠爾逮詣熱河行在，獄成，戍伍岱伊犁。
As the court prepared to attack Greater Jinchuan, Wen Fu memorialized: "When Zhang Guangsi campaigned in Jinchuan, talk of ten or seven routes masked six real axes, all aimed at Lewuwei and Ga'eryi. The main Kasai route from Meinuo to Ga'eryi, about five stages, was Fu Heng's line of march; Danba, from Weizhou Bridge through tribal country to Lewuwei, more than twenty stages with the Mujin Gang pass, was Yue Zhongqi's route; a route named Senggesang, six or seven stages from Meinuo to Ga'eryi, was taken by Regional Commander Ma Liangzhu; routes through Geshiza and Ma'erbang, each six or seven stages from Ga'eryi, were narrow and treacherous; Zhuosijia Bu was three stages to Lewuwei and three to Ga'eryi, but great rivers and dense blockhouses made both approaches hard to assault. There was also the E'po route from Zhuosijia Bu to Lewuwei—only two stages and comparatively level. We should advance on the main Kasai route; Zhuosijia Bu can support from the E'po route where it originally recovered seized territory, forming a pincer. Other routes should be held with detachments to keep the enemy from covering every front. Wen Fu then entered via Gongga'er La, Agui via Dangga'er La, and Fengsheng'e via Zhuosijia Bu. Wen Fu was headstrong, rarely consulted others on strategy, and copied Neqin and Zhang Guangsi—storming fort with fort and building hundreds of new blockhouses. Of his twenty thousand-odd troops, more than half were spread among the blockhouses. Every few days, when he owed the court a report, he pushed his men to storm another blockhouse instead. Casualties mounted, grievances spread, and the troops lost heart. Wen Fu feasted daily; Staff Secretary Wu Dai lamented: "What commander who behaves like this can ever win?" Wu Dai secretly reported to the throne; Wen Fu impeached Wu Dai in turn. The emperor sent Fengsheng'e and Imperial Son-in-law Sebuteng Balzhu'er to conduct an inquiry. Wen Fu claimed Sebuteng Balzhu'er had conspired against him; the emperor dismissed Wu Dai, summoned Sebuteng to the Rehe traveling palace, and after the trial exiled Wu Dai to Yili.
16
三十八年春，溫福師至功噶爾拉，賊阻險，不得進，別取道攻昔嶺，駐軍木果木； 令提督董天弼分軍屯底木達。 木果木、底木達皆故小金川地，索諾木陰使小金川頭人煽諸降番使復叛。 諸降番以師久頓不進，遂蜂起應之。 先攻底木達，天弼死之，次劫糧台，潛襲木果木。 溫福不嚴備山後要隘，賊突薄大營，奪砲局，斷汲道。 時大營兵尚萬餘，運糧役數千，爭避入大營，溫福堅閉壘門不納，轟而潰，聲如壞堤，於是軍心益震。 賊四面蹂入，溫福中槍死，各卡兵望風潰散。 參贊海蘭察聞警赴援，殿餘兵自間道出。 小金川地盡陷。 上初聞溫福死，詔予一等伯爵，世襲罔替，祀昭忠祠。 既，劉秉恬、海蘭察、富勒渾各疏言溫福僨事狀，命奪伯爵，予三等輕車都尉世職。 四十一年，命並罷之。 子勒保、永保，皆有傳。
In the spring of the thirty-eighth year Wen Fu reached Gongga'er La; the enemy held the passes and blocked him, so he turned to attack Xiling and camped at Mugumu; He posted Regional Commander Dong Tianbi at Dimuda with a detached force. Mugumu and Dimuda lay in old Lesser Jinchuan lands; Suonuomu secretly urged Lesser Jinchuan headmen to incite the surrendered tribes to rise again. Seeing the army bogged down, the surrendered tribes rose everywhere in response. They struck Dimuda first and killed Tianbi, then looted the grain depots and surprised Mugumu. Wen Fu left the rear passes weakly held; the enemy rushed the main camp, took the artillery yard, and cut the water supply. The main camp still held over ten thousand soldiers and thousands of carriers, all pressing to get inside; Wen Fu barred the gates; the mass broke out with a roar like a bursting levee, and panic spread through the army. The enemy poured in from every side; Wen Fu was shot dead; garrisons at the outposts scattered at once. Staff Secretary Hailancha hurried to help and covered the survivors as they slipped out by back trails. Lesser Jinchuan was lost entirely. On first news of Wen Fu's death the emperor made him a perpetual first-class earl and honored him in the Temple of Loyalty. Later Liu Bingtian, Hailancha, and Fulehun detailed how Wen Fu had ruined the campaign; his earldom was revoked and only a third-class light chariot commandant hereditary rank remained. In the forty-first year even that reduced honor was withdrawn. His sons Lebao and Yongbao are each given separate biographies.
17
=【論】=論曰：金川再亂，開泰、阿爾泰皆主以番攻番，遲回坐誤。 桂林有宋元俊不能用，反齮齕之，擁兵不進。 阿爾泰與元俊劾桂林，此其意以軍國為重，不屑屑阿貴近、疏卑遠，宜若可成功，乃坐蜚語敗。 溫福銳進，似勝開泰輩，乃又剛愎，有董天弼不能用，予兵至少，令僻處軍後，卒致僨潰，徒以身殉，豈不惜哉？
Commentary: In the second Jinchuan revolt, Kai Tai and Aertai both favored tribe-against-tribe policies and lost time through hesitation. Guilin failed to use Song Yuanjun and instead undermined him, keeping armies idle. When Aertai and Yuanjun denounced Guilin they meant to put the war first, not to flatter the powerful or neglect distant fronts; it looked promising, yet rumor undid them. Wen Fu marched boldly, as if better than Kai Tai and the rest, yet his obstinacy, refusal to use Dong Tianbi, and posting him in the rear with too few men brought on disaster and a useless death—is that not a bitter waste?
