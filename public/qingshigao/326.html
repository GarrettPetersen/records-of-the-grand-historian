=開泰=開泰，烏雅氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 雍正二年進士，改庶吉士，授編修。 九年，遷侍講。 上御門，開泰未入侍班，黜令乾清門行走。 十三年，复編修。 乾隆元年，遷國子監司業。 八年，遷祭酒。 督江蘇學政。 再遷內閣學士。 三遷兵部侍郎，仍留學政任。 十年，授湖北巡撫。 疏言：「社倉較常平尤近於民，而弊亦易滋。 湖北社倉穀麥五十二萬石有奇，散在諸鄉，恐多虧缺。 應飭道府按部所至，便宜抽驗。 」調江西。 十三年，又調湖南。 疏言：「戶部諮各省常平倉穀，以雍正舊額為準。 湖南溢額穀五十五萬餘石，令糶價儲庫。 臣維雍正舊額七十餘萬石。 湖南夙稱產米，乾隆二年至八年，諸省赴湖南購米，先後計百七十五萬有奇。 中間又撥運福建、江蘇。 若盡糶溢額之穀，遇本省需用或鄰疆告糴，必致倉儲缺額，買補不易。 」疏上，以留心積貯嘉之。 十五年，有壽掄元者，自言南河同知，赴湖南採木，布政使孫灝諭永州府為料理。 尋得其詐偽狀，開泰以聞，但言灝殊為未諳。 上以灝瞻徇，何得但言未諳，知為開泰門生，斥其徇庇，下吏部嚴議，議奪官，命留任。 尋調貴州。 十八年，疏言：「古州募軍屯田，戶上田六畝，中田八畝，下田十畝。 今食指日多，生計艱難，請準屯戶入伍充兵。 」許之。 擢湖廣總督，加太子少傅。
Kai Tai, of the Uya clan, was a Manchu bannerman of the Plain Yellow Banner. In Yongzheng 2 he passed the palace examination, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and was appointed compiler. In the ninth year he was promoted to reader-in-waiting. When the emperor held audience at the Gate, Kai Tai had failed to take his place in the attending roster and was demoted to duty at the Gate of Heavenly Purity. In the thirteenth year he was restored to the post of compiler. In Qianlong 1 he was promoted to vice director of the Imperial Academy. In the eighth year he was promoted to chancellor of the academy. He served as educational commissioner of Jiangsu. He was promoted again to grand secretary of the Grand Secretariat. A third promotion made him vice minister of War, though he retained his educational commission. In the tenth year he was appointed governor of Hubei. He memorialized: "Community granaries lie even closer to the people than ever-normal granaries, yet they are also more prone to abuse. Hubei's community granaries hold more than five hundred twenty thousand shi of grain and wheat, scattered across the countryside, and I fear many shortfalls. Circuit and prefectural officials should be ordered to conduct spot inspections wherever their rounds take them. " He was transferred to Jiangxi. In the thirteenth year he was transferred again, this time to Hunan. He memorialized: "The Board of Revenue has asked each province to report on ever-normal granary stocks, using the Yongzheng quota as the standard. Hunan's surplus of more than five hundred fifty thousand shi is to be sold off and the proceeds deposited in the treasury. I hold that the Yongzheng quota was over seven hundred thousand shi. Hunan has long been known as a rice-producing province; from Qianlong 2 through 8 other provinces purchased from Hunan a total of more than one million seven hundred fifty thousand shi. In the meantime grain was also requisitioned and shipped to Fujian and Jiangsu. If all surplus grain is sold, the province will lack reserves when its own needs arise or neighboring regions seek relief, and stocks will be hard to restore. " The emperor approved the memorial and praised his concern for grain reserves. In the fifteenth year a man named Shou Lunyuan, claiming to be an assistant prefect of the Southern Rivers, came to Hunan to procure timber, and the provincial treasurer Sun Hao told Yongzhou Prefecture to handle the matter. When the fraud was soon uncovered, Kai Tai reported it but said only that Hao had been quite unaware of the facts. The emperor said Hao had clearly shown favoritism and asked how Kai Tai could dismiss it as mere ignorance; learning that Hao was Kai Tai's student, he rebuked Kai Tai for shielding him, referred the case to the Board of Civil Appointments for severe deliberation, which recommended dismissal, though the emperor ordered Hao retained in office. Soon afterward he was transferred to Guizhou. In the eighteenth year he memorialized: "At Guzhou recruited soldiers farm garrison lands on allotments of six mu for top-grade fields, eight for middle-grade, and ten for lower-grade. As dependents multiply and livelihoods grow harder, I ask that garrison households be allowed to enlist as regular soldiers. " The request was granted. He was promoted to governor-general of Huguang and given the title Junior Tutor to the Heir Apparent.
2
二十年，調四川。 金川土司莎羅奔與革布什咱土司色楞敦多布初為婚媾，繼乃相怨構兵。 旁近綽斯甲布、鄂克什、雜穀、巴旺、丹壩、明正、章谷、小金川諸土司皆不直莎羅奔。 二十三年，莎羅奔攻吉地。 吉地，色楞敦多布所居寨也。 開泰與提督岳鍾琪檄游擊楊青、都司夏尚德等率兵分屯章谷、泰宁，令鄂克什、雜穀援革布什咱，攻金川，莎羅奔引退。 尋復攻破吉地，色楞敦多布走泰宁求援，開泰复檄諸土司出兵助之，調雜穀土練千人分屯丹壩、章谷、泰宁，發黎、雅、峨邊兵屯打箭爐，諭郎卡撤兵。 郎卡，莎羅奔從子，為副酋，主兵事者也。 事聞，上謂：「番目相攻，於打箭爐何與？ 」疑郎卡擾邊，命開泰具實覆奏。 開泰尋疏報章谷、巴旺土兵擊敗金川，莎羅奔焚吉地走，盡复革布什咱境，留綽斯甲布、明正兩土司兵分守之，使色楞敦多布歸寨。 上諭曰：「番民挾仇攻擊，不必繩以內地官法。 宜以番攻番，處以靜鎮。 」旋加太子太保。 二十四年，松潘鎮總兵楊朝棟入覲，開泰與鍾琪奏朝棟衰老，難期勝任。 上責開泰何以不先奏，下吏部議，奪官，命仍留任。
In the twentieth year he was transferred to Sichuan. The Jinchuan chieftain Suo Luo Ben and the Gebushizhai chieftain Se Leng Dun Duo Bu had first been joined by marriage, but later turned hostile and took up arms against each other. The neighboring chieftains of Zhuosijia Bu, E Ke Shi, Za Gu, Ba Wang, Dan Ba, Ming Zheng, Zhang Gu, and Lesser Jinchuan all sided against Suo Luo Ben. In the twenty-third year Suo Luo Ben attacked Jidi. Jidi was the stockade where Se Leng Dun Duo Bu lived. Kai Tai and provincial commander-in-chief Yue Zhongqi ordered brigade commander Yang Qing, company commander Xia Shangde, and others to divide their forces between Zhang Gu and Taining, sent E Ke Shi and Za Gu to relieve Gebushizhai and strike Jinchuan, and Suo Luo Ben pulled back. Jidi soon fell again; Se Leng Dun Duo Bu fled to Taining for help; Kai Tai again ordered the chieftains to send troops, posted a thousand Za Gu native militia at Dan Ba, Zhang Gu, and Taining, sent troops from Li, Ya, and Ebian to Dajianlu, and told Lang Ka to withdraw his forces. Lang Ka was Suo Luo Ben's nephew, the deputy chieftain who handled military affairs. When word reached the throne, the emperor said: "When tribal chiefs fight one another, what business is that of Dajianlu? " Suspecting Lang Ka of harassing the frontier, he ordered Kai Tai to report the facts in full. Kai Tai soon reported that native troops from Zhang Gu and Ba Wang had defeated Jinchuan; Suo Luo Ben burned Jidi and fled; Gebushizhai's territory was fully recovered; garrisons of Zhuosijia Bu and Ming Zheng troops were left to hold it; and Se Leng Dun Duo Bu returned to his stockade. The emperor instructed: "When frontier tribes attack one another over private feuds, there is no need to apply the laws of the interior. Let tribe fight tribe, and handle the matter with calm restraint. " He was soon given the title Grand Tutor to the Heir Apparent. In the twenty-fourth year Songpan garrison commander Yang Chaodong came to audience; Kai Tai and Zhongqi reported that Chaodong was aged and could no longer be expected to perform his duties. The emperor rebuked Kai Tai for not reporting this sooner, referred the case to the Board of Civil Appointments, which recommended dismissal, though the emperor ordered him retained in office.
3
二十七年，莎羅奔死，郎卡應襲。 例，土司承襲，鄰封諸土司具結。 開泰以郎卡與諸土司皆不協，令毋取結，疏聞，上許之，命嚴諭郎卡知恩守法。 未幾，郎卡侵丹壩，取所屬瑪讓，開泰檄綽斯甲布往援，使守備溫欽等赴金川詰責。 上諭曰：「郎卡狼子野心，即使詰責伏罪，豈肯永守約束？ 諸土司援兵既集，能協力剿除，分據其地，轉可相安； 若諸部不能並力剿除，而郎卡怙惡不悛，亦非開泰、岳鍾琪四川綠營兵能任其事，應臨時奏請進止。 」二十八年六月，開泰奏九土司大舉擊破金川。 上聞郎卡使人詣成都，開泰許進謁，撫慰之，而陰令九土司進兵，諭曰：「郎卡於綽斯甲布等屢肆欺凌，眾土司合力報復。 開泰既聞其事，惟應明白宣示，諭令悉銳往攻； 而於郎卡來人嚴為拒絕，且諭以爾結怨鄰境，誰肯甘心？ 斷不能曲為庇護。 如此，則郎卡既不敢逞強，綽斯甲布等亦可洩忿。 乃既用譎以籠絡郎卡，又隱為各土司援助，郎卡素狡黠，豈能掩其耳目？ 殊非駕馭邊夷之道。 」命奪官，以頭等侍衛赴伊犁辦事。 尋卒。
In the twenty-seventh year Suo Luo Ben died and Lang Ka was due to succeed. By precedent, when a native chieftain succeeded, neighboring chieftains had to submit guaranty statements. Because Lang Ka was at odds with the other chieftains, Kai Tai waived the guaranty requirement; when he reported this the emperor approved and ordered Lang Ka sternly reminded to show gratitude and obey the law. Before long Lang Ka invaded Dan Ba and seized the dependent territory of Ma Rang; Kai Tai ordered Zhuosijia Bu to relieve Dan Ba and sent garrison commander Wen Qin and others to Jinchuan to rebuke him. The emperor instructed: "Lang Ka has the heart of a wolf cub; even if rebuked into submission, how can he be expected to keep his obligations forever? Now that the allied chieftains' forces are assembled, if they can join in destroying him and divide his lands among themselves, lasting peace may follow; if the tribes cannot combine to destroy him and Lang Ka persists in his wickedness, this is not a task Sichuan's Green Standard troops under Kai Tai and Yue Zhongqi can handle alone; you should memorialize when the moment comes for instructions. " In the sixth month of the twenty-eighth year Kai Tai reported that nine chieftains had launched a major campaign and broken Jinchuan. The emperor learned that Lang Ka had sent envoys to Chengdu; Kai Tai received them and consoled them while secretly ordering the nine chieftains to advance; he instructed: "Lang Ka has repeatedly bullied Zhuosijia Bu and the others, and the chieftains have joined forces to retaliate. Now that Kai Tai has learned of this, he should declare it openly and order them to attack with full force; toward Lang Ka's envoys he should firmly refuse audience and tell them: You have made enemies of your neighbors—who will accept that? I absolutely cannot bend the rules to shield you. That way Lang Ka would not dare show force, and Zhuosijia Bu and the others could also vent their grievances. Yet he both used deceit to win Lang Ka over and secretly aided the chieftains; Lang Ka has always been cunning—how could he fail to see through it? This is no way to manage frontier tribes. " He was dismissed from office and sent to Yili as a first-class imperial bodyguard on assignment. He died soon afterward.
4
=阿爾泰=阿爾泰，伊爾根覺羅氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 雍正間，以副榜貢生授宗人府筆帖式。 乾隆中，屢遷至山東巡撫。 以山東產山綢，疏請令民間就山坡隙地廣植桲欏，免其昇科。 歲大水，阿爾泰先後濬兗州、沂州支渠三十有九，曹州、單縣順堤河二百餘裡； 培南旺、蜀山湖民埝； 導章丘珍珠、麻塘二泉，新城五龍河溉民田； 並及高苑、博興、惠民諸縣近水地，皆令蓺稻。 築洸河堤至於馬場湖，以衛濟寧州城，析白馬湖引入獨山湖以疏泗水，開汶上稻田數百頃。 濟東諸州縣瀕徒駭、馬頰兩河，支流相貫注，及哨馬營、四女寺支河，皆次第疏治。 濬衛河自德州至於館陶凡三百餘裡。 洩壽張積水自沙、趙二河入運，洩東平積水入會泉、大清諸河，洩濟南、東昌諸州縣積水。 開支河三十餘，循官道為壕，引水自壕入支河，自支河入徒駭、大清諸河。 漳、汶合流，開引河，增子埝，以防盛漲。 阿爾泰撫山東七年，治水利有績，擢四川總督，加太子太保。
A Er Tai, of the Irgen Gioro clan, was a Manchu bannerman of the Plain Yellow Banner. During the Yongzheng reign he entered through the secondary list as a tribute student and was appointed a clerk in the Imperial Clan Court. Under Qianlong he rose through repeated promotions until he became governor of Shandong. Because Shandong produced mountain silk, he memorialized asking that people be allowed to plant widely on hillside gaps and that such land be exempted from tax assessment. In years of severe flooding A Er Tai dredged thirty-nine branch canals in Yanzhou and Yizhou and more than two hundred li of embankment rivers in Caozhou and Shan County; raised the civilian dikes at Nanwang and Shanshan Lake; diverted the Pearl and Matang springs of Zhangqiu and the Wulong River of Xincheng to irrigate farmland; and in Gaoyuan, Boxing, Huimin, and other counties with nearby water he ordered rice planted throughout. He built the Guang River dike as far as Machang Lake to protect Jining city, diverted Baima Lake into Dushan Lake to drain the Si River, and opened several hundred qing of rice fields in Wenshang. In the eastern Ji prefectures and counties bordering the Tu Hai and Ma Jia rivers, where branch streams interlinked, as well as the Shaomaying and Sinvsi branch rivers, he dredged and repaired them in turn. He dredged the Wei River from Dezhou to Guantao, more than three hundred li in all. He drained Shouzhang's floodwater through the Sha and Zhao rivers into the Grand Canal, Dongping's floodwater into the Huiquan and Daqing rivers, and floodwater in Jinan, Dongchang, and other prefectures and counties. He opened more than thirty branch rivers, dug ditches along the official roads, led water from the ditches into branch rivers, and from branch rivers into the Tu Hai, Da Qing, and other rivers. Where the Zhang and Wen rivers joined, he opened diversion channels and added subsidiary dikes against high floods. After seven years governing Shandong with notable achievements in water control, A Er Tai was promoted to governor-general of Sichuan and given the title Grand Tutor to the Heir Apparent.
5
阿爾泰至四川，議平治道路：陸道北訖廣元，西達松潘，東抵夔州，護其傾欹，補其缺落，兼葺大渡河瀘定橋； 水道自萬縣入湖廣境，鑿治險灘凡一百有奇。 議以牧廠餘地招佃為田。 議置義倉，捐穀千餘石以倡。 議開南川金佛山磺礦。 議築都江大堰。 議松潘、雜穀、打箭爐三廳置倉儲麥稞，備邊儲。 上皆從其請。
When A Er Tai reached Sichuan he proposed repairing the roads: the land route north to Guangyuan, west to Songpan, and east to Kuizhou, shoring up what had collapsed, filling gaps, and also repairing the Luding Bridge on the Dadu River; the water route from Wan County into Huguang territory, where more than one hundred dangerous shoals were dredged and cleared. He proposed leasing unused pasture lands for farming. He proposed establishing charity granaries and donated more than one thousand shi of grain to set an example. He proposed opening the sulfur mines at Mount Jin Fo in Nanchuan. He proposed rebuilding the great Dujiang weir. He proposed establishing granaries at Songpan, Za Gu, and Dajianlu to store wheat and barley as frontier reserves. The emperor approved all his requests.
6
初，徵金川，以頭人郎卡出降，罷兵。 三十一年，復為亂，掠丹壩、巴旺。 阿爾泰策以番攻番，令旁近綽斯甲布諸土司攻之。 秋出行邊，至雜穀腦。 郎卡使請還所侵丹壩碉卡。 復與提督董天弼進至康巴達，郎卡出謁，阿爾泰許如所請，並畀以新印。 疏聞，上戒毋遷就苟安。 三十五年，小金川頭人僧格桑掠鄂克什，阿爾泰赴達木巴宗，僧格桑出謁，還侵地。 尋授武英殿大學士，仍領總督。 三十六年，召還京，入閣治事。 既，復令出領總督。 金川頭人索諾木攻革布什咱，僧格桑亦圍達木巴宗，侵明正土司。 阿爾泰疏言：「兩金川相比，如議出師，需兵既多，糜餉亦鉅。 茲令董天弼臨之以兵，仍使游擊宋元俊宣諭索諾木。 」上責阿爾泰議非是，決策用兵，令定邊右副將軍溫福視師，佐以侍郎桂林，諭斥阿爾泰掩飾偷安，奪大學士、總督，留軍治餉，以桂林代為總督。 師克約咱，上以阿爾泰鑄大砲利軍行，予散秩大臣銜。
Earlier, when Jinchuan was subdued, the headman Lang Ka surrendered and the campaign was halted. In the thirty-first year he rebelled again and raided Dan Ba and Ba Wang. A Er Tai planned to let tribe fight tribe and ordered the neighboring Zhuosijia Bu chieftains to attack him. That autumn he toured the frontier as far as Za Gu Nao. Lang Ka's envoy asked that the seized Dan Ba blockhouses be returned. He advanced again with provincial commander-in-chief Dong Tianbi to Kang Ba Da; Lang Ka came out to audience; A Er Tai granted his request and also gave him a new seal. When this was reported, the emperor warned against compromising for the sake of temporary peace. In the thirty-fifth year Lesser Jinchuan headman Sengge Sang raided E Ke Shi; A Er Tai went to Damu Ba Zong; Sengge Sang came out to audience and returned the seized territory. Soon he was appointed grand secretary of the Hall of Military Eminence while retaining the governorship. In the thirty-sixth year he was recalled to the capital to serve in the Grand Council. Afterward he was again ordered out to resume the governorship. Jinchuan headman Suonuo Mu attacked Gebushizhai; Sengge Sang also besieged Damu Ba Zong and invaded the Ming Zheng chieftaincy. A Er Tai memorialized: "Greater and Lesser Jinchuan adjoin each other; if troops are dispatched as proposed, many soldiers will be needed and the cost in supplies will be enormous. For now order Dong Tianbi to oversee the situation with troops, and have brigade commander Song Yuanjun proclaim the court's demands to Suonuo Mu. " The emperor rejected A Er Tai's proposal, decided on military action, ordered Right Deputy General of Pacification Wen Fu to take command with Vice Minister Gui Lin as his assistant, rebuked A Er Tai for covering up and seeking ease, stripped him of grand secretary and governor-general, kept him with the army to manage supplies, and made Gui Lin governor in his place. When the army took Yueza, the emperor rewarded A Er Tai's cast cannon, which had aided the campaign, with the rank of minister without portfolio.
7
三十七年，與總兵宋元俊劾桂林覆軍諱敗，上為罷桂林，即命阿爾泰攝總督。 俄移督湖廣。 阿爾泰疏言：「各路轉餉，當招商承運。 西路去內地近，南路山險途長，商不肯應募，當增運值。 火藥已運罄，當令雲南、陝西協助。 」上謂：「阿爾泰專領轉餉，何不早籌畫？ 今福隆安、阿桂皆至南路，始以一奏塞責。 」命毋往湖廣，仍以散秩大臣留軍督餉。 未幾，阿桂疏言軍至卡丫，無五日之糧； 又言綽斯甲布轉餉將一月猶未至。 阿爾泰亦自陳請奪職從軍。 上責其倚老負恩，始終不肯以國事為念，命逮問。
In the thirty-seventh year he and garrison commander Song Yuanjun impeached Gui Lin for concealing a rout; the emperor dismissed Gui Lin and immediately ordered A Er Tai to serve as acting governor. He was soon transferred to govern Huguang. A Er Tai memorialized: "Supply transport on all routes should be contracted to merchants. The western route lies close to the interior, but the southern route is mountainous, dangerous, and long, and merchants will not answer the call unless transport fees are raised. Gunpowder stocks have been exhausted in transport; Yunnan and Shaanxi should be asked to assist. " The emperor said: "A Er Tai was put solely in charge of supply transport—why did he not plan ahead? Only now that Fu Long'an and A Gui have both reached the southern route does he submit one memorial merely to cover himself. " He was ordered not to go to Huguang but to remain with the army as minister without portfolio supervising supplies. Before long A Gui reported that when the army reached Kaya it had less than five days' grain; and that Zhuosijia Bu's supply transport had been underway nearly a month without arriving. A Er Tai also petitioned to be stripped of office and serve with the army. The emperor rebuked him for relying on his age and repaying kindness with ingratitude, for never putting state affairs first, and ordered him arrested for investigation.
8
阿爾泰初至四川，上以天壇立燈竿，下四川求楠木。 阿爾泰附運木材以進，言出養廉採獻。 既乃私語人，謂他日且以此負累。 語聞上，上心慊之。 至是，詔罪狀阿爾泰，猶及此事，斥為昧良飾詐。 川東道托隆入見，發阿爾泰贓私，下繼任總督富勒渾嚴鞫。 三十八年，獄具，擬斬，上命賜自盡。
When A Er Tai first reached Sichuan, the emperor had lamp poles erected at the Temple of Heaven and ordered Sichuan to supply nanmu timber. A Er Tai sent timber along with the shipment and presented it, saying it had been procured and offered from his integrity-preservation stipend. Afterward he privately told others that one day this would come back to haunt him. When word reached the emperor, he took offense. At this time an edict listed A Er Tai's crimes, still mentioning this affair, and denounced him as a man who obscured good faith and disguised deceit. Eastern Sichuan intendant Tuo Long came to audience and exposed A Er Tai's corruption; the case was referred to his successor as governor, Fu Lehun, for strict interrogation. In the thirty-eighth year the case was complete; decapitation was recommended, and the emperor ordered him granted suicide.
9
=桂林=桂林，伊爾根覺羅氏，滿洲鑲藍旗人，兩廣總督鶴年子。 桂林自廩生入貲為工部主事。 累遷山西按察使。 乾隆三十六年三月，擢戶部侍郎、軍機處行走。 九月，命佐定邊右副將軍溫福討金川。 十一月，授四川總督。 小金川頭人在卡外投文餽土宜，桂林卻不受，檄罪狀其酋僧格桑。 旋督兵收約咱，進克其東山梁大小碉五、石卡二十餘。 疏請添調黔、陝兵五千益師，上許益陝、甘兵三千。 桂林旋督總兵宋元俊攻卡丫，進據墨爾多山梁。 上嘉其措置合宜，手詔謂：「無意中用汝，竟能得力。 亦賴在軍機處半年，日耹朕訓也。 」
Gui Lin, of the Irgen Gioro clan, was a Manchu bannerman of the Bordered Blue Banner and the son of Huguang governor-general He Nian. Gui Lin entered office from licentiate status by purchase and became a secretary in the Ministry of Works. He rose through repeated promotions until he became judicial commissioner of Shanxi. In the third month of Qianlong 36 he was promoted to vice minister of Revenue and assigned to the Grand Council. In the ninth month he was ordered to assist Right Deputy General of Pacification Wen Fu in suppressing Jinchuan. In the eleventh month he was appointed governor of Sichuan. Lesser Jinchuan headmen outside the pass submitted documents and offered local products; Gui Lin refused them and issued a proclamation listing the crimes of their chieftain Sengge Sang. Soon he directed troops to recover Yueza and advanced to take five large and small blockhouses and more than twenty stone forts on its eastern ridge. He memorialized requesting five thousand additional troops from Guizhou and Shaanxi; the emperor permitted three thousand from Shaanxi and Gansu. Gui Lin soon directed garrison commander Song Yuanjun to attack Kaya and advance to occupy the Mo Er Duo ridge. The emperor praised his arrangements as fitting and wrote in his own hand: "I used you almost by accident, yet you have proved effective. That also owes something to your six months in the Grand Council, hearing my instruction every day. "
10
三十七年，克卡丫，复破克郭松、甲木、噶爾金。 進克噶爾金後山梁，分兵攻東山梁，襲阿仰，自墨壟溝進取達烏圍。 是時大金川酋索諾木攻陷革布什咱，屯兵其地。 桂林議乘索諾木兵力未備、革布什咱人心未定，與元俊分兵五道並進，並約將軍溫福合擊，密令革布什咱降酋旺勒丹等約其戚加琿爾為內應，遂收革布什咱寨落七十餘裡。 旋令元俊及守備陳定國率綽斯甲布土兵屯甲爾壟壩，進攻默資溝、吉地，斷其水道，進攻丹東。 上獎桂林甚合機宜，促元俊乘勝深入取索諾木。
In the thirty-seventh year he took Kaya and again broke through and took Guo Song, Jia Mu, and Ga Er Jin. He advanced to take the rear ridge of Ga Er Jin, divided his forces to attack the eastern ridge, raided A Yang, and from Mo Long Gully advanced on Dawu Wei. At this time Greater Jinchuan chieftain Suonuo Mu attacked and took Gebushizhai and encamped there. Gui Lin proposed that while Suonuo Mu's forces were not yet ready and Gebushizhai's people were still unsettled, he and Yuanjun should advance in five columns, coordinate a joint attack with General Wen Fu, and secretly arrange with surrendered Gebushizhai chieftain Wang Ledan and others for their kinsman Jia Hu Er to serve as inside contact; they thus recovered more than seventy li of Gebushizhai stockades. Soon he ordered Yuanjun and garrison commander Chen Dingguo to lead Zhuosijia Bu native troops to encamp at Jia Er Long Ba, advance on Mo Zi Gou and Jidi, cut their water supply, and advance on Dan Dong. The emperor praised Gui Lin's timing and urged Yuanjun to follow up the victory with a deep advance to capture Suonuo Mu.
11
桂林遣裨將自東山梁墨壟溝越嶺進攻，別遣兵出間道，自札哇窠山梁縋崖設伏師。 既度東山梁墨壟溝，札哇窠伏兵亦起，賊敗竄，克大碉一、石卡二十一。 別遣參將常泰環攻黨哩，都司李天貴等攻沙沖，革布什咱頭人為內應，賊盡殲。 黨哩、沙沖地並復。 總兵英泰等復攻克達烏官寨。 上嘉其功，賜御用玉韘。 再進攻克格烏巴桑及那隆山嶺。 元俊別攻克丹東及覺拉喇嘛寺，誅賊渠三百、番眾百三十餘。 革布什咱地盡复，桂林檄定國將所調綽斯甲布兵駐界上聽調。 上以革布什咱既复，正當乘勝進剿金川，攻其無備，責桂林失算。
Gui Lin sent a lieutenant general from the eastern ridge's Mo Long Gully to cross the mountains and attack, and separately sent troops by a hidden route to rappel down cliffs from the Zha Wa Ke ridge and set an ambush. After crossing the eastern ridge's Mo Long Gully, the Zha Wa Ke ambush also struck; the rebels were defeated and fled; one large blockhouse and twenty-one stone forts were taken. He separately sent colonel Chang Tai to encircle Dang Li, company commander Li Tiangui and others to attack Sha Chong, with Gebushizhai headmen as inside contacts; the rebels were wiped out. The territories of Dang Li and Sha Chong were both recovered. Garrison commander Ying Tai and others again attacked and took the Dawu official stockade. The emperor praised his achievement and bestowed an imperial jade thumb ring. Advancing again he took Ge Wu Ba Sang and the Na Long mountain ridge. Yuanjun separately took Dan Dong and the Jue La Lama Temple, executing three hundred rebel leaders and more than one hundred thirty tribesmen. All Gebushizhai territory was recovered; Gui Lin ordered Dingguo to post the Zhuosijia Bu troops he had mobilized on the border awaiting orders. The emperor said that with Gebushizhai recovered, this was exactly the moment to follow victory with an advance against Jinchuan and strike while the enemy was unprepared; he rebuked Gui Lin for miscalculation.
12
桂林复督兵攻達烏東岸山梁，參將薛琮戰沒，琮驍將，深入糧盡。 桂林既失期不會師，又不以時遣援，軍盡覆，疏請治罪，述戰狀不敢盡。 元俊與散秩大臣阿爾泰劾其虛誑，並言桂林在卡丫建屋宇以居，迫屬僚供應，與副都統鐵保、提督汪騰龍等終日酣飲，諸將罕得見； 密令騰龍畀總兵王萬邦白金五百，贖被掠官兵，希圖掩飾。 上奪桂林職，命額駙、尚書、公福隆安馳往按治，尋奏所劾皆虛，惟官兵傷損不即察奏屬實； 至贖被掠官兵，乃在軍戶部郎中汪承霈聞巴旺、布拉克底土兵歸失道，官兵告桂林，發白金五百交騰龍備賞，事為元俊構陷，請分別治罪。 上以桂林在軍日親曲糱，止圖安逸，不能與士卒同甘苦，致北山梁傷損多兵，不得為無罪，命戍伊犁。 三十八年七月，予三等侍衛銜，仍詣軍前督糧運。 四十年，授頭等侍衛。 尋授四川提督，遷兩廣總督。 卒，加太子太保銜，諡壯敏。
Gui Lin again directed troops to attack the eastern-bank ridge of Dawu; colonel Xue Cong died in battle; Cong was a fierce general who had advanced deep and run out of supplies. Gui Lin both missed the rendezvous and failed to send relief in time; the army was destroyed; he memorialized requesting punishment and described the battle without daring to tell the whole story. Yuanjun and minister without portfolio A Er Tai impeached him for falsehood and also said that Gui Lin built houses for himself at Kaya, forced subordinates to supply him, and with vice commander Tie Bao, provincial commander-in-chief Wang Tenglong, and others drank all day while generals rarely saw him; he secretly ordered Tenglong to give garrison commander Wang Wanbang five hundred taels of silver to ransom captured officers and soldiers, hoping to cover up the affair. The emperor stripped Gui Lin of office and ordered imperial son-in-law, minister, and duke Fu Long'an to hurry there to investigate; the report soon found the impeachments false except for the charge that he had failed to report promptly on officers' and soldiers' casualties; as for ransoming captured officers and soldiers, army Ministry of Revenue director Wang Chengpei had heard that Ba Wang and Bulake Di native troops had lost their way on the return march; officers and soldiers reported to Gui Lin, who issued five hundred taels of silver to Tenglong for rewards—a matter Yuanjun had framed; separate punishments were requested. The emperor said that Gui Lin in camp daily indulged in wine, sought only ease, and could not share hardship with the soldiers, causing heavy casualties on the northern ridge—he could not be held guiltless—and ordered him exiled to Yili. In the seventh month of the thirty-eighth year he was given the rank of third-class imperial bodyguard and still ordered to the front to supervise grain transport. In the fortieth year he was appointed first-class imperial bodyguard. Soon he was appointed provincial commander-in-chief of Sichuan and transferred to governor-general of Liangguang. He died; he was given the posthumous title Grand Tutor to the Heir Apparent and the temple name Zhuangmin.
13
=溫福=溫福，字履綏，費莫氏，滿洲鑲紅旗人，文華殿大學士溫達孫也。 自繙譯舉人授兵部筆​​帖式。 乾隆初，累遷戶部郎中。 外擢湖南布政使，歷四年； 移貴州布政使，亦四年。 坐平遠民閧訟庭、按治草率，奪職，戍烏里雅蘇台。 二十三年，起內閣侍讀學士。 從定邊將軍兆惠討霍集占，戰葉爾羌，槍傷顴。 擢內閣學士，遷倉場侍郎，予雲騎尉世職。 外授福建巡撫，內遷吏部侍郎、軍機處行走，進理籓院尚書。
Wen Fu, whose courtesy name was Lüshou, of the Feimo clan, was a Manchu bannerman of the Bordered Red Banner and the grandson of Grand Secretary Wen Da of the Hall of Literary Glory. From translator licentiate he was appointed a clerk in the Ministry of War. In early Qianlong he rose through repeated promotions until he became a director in the Ministry of Revenue. He was promoted outside the capital to provincial treasurer of Hunan and served four years; he was transferred to provincial treasurer of Guizhou, also for four years. Because the Pingyuan people rioted in court and his investigation was perfunctory, he was stripped of office and exiled to Uliastai. In the twenty-third year he was recalled as reader-in-waiting of the Grand Secretariat. He followed Pacification General Zhaohui in suppressing Huo Jizhan, fought at Yarkand, and was shot in the cheek. He was promoted to grand secretary of the Grand Secretariat, transferred to vice minister of the Granary Administration, and given the hereditary office of Cloud Cavalry Captain. He was appointed governor of Fujian, transferred back to vice minister of Personnel and assignment to the Grand Council, and advanced to minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs.
14
三十六年，師徵金川，授定邊右副將軍，以侍郎桂林佐之，共討賊。 溫福自汶川出西路，桂林自打箭爐出南路。 時小金川頭人澤旺子僧格桑割地乞援於大金川頭人索諾木，索諾木潛遣兵助之。 上命先剿小金​​川，且勿聲大金川罪。 溫福至打箭爐，分兵三道入：溫福出巴朗拉，提督董天弼自甲金達援達木巴宗，總督阿爾泰自約咱攻僧格桑。 十一月，擢武英殿大學士。 十二月，至巴朗拉，戰三晝夜，賊敗去。 三十七年正月，取達木巴宗。 進攻斯底葉安，而分軍出別斯滿、瑪爾瓦爾濟，兩路夾擊，進克資哩。 再進克東瑪，再進克路頂宗及喀木色爾，取諸碉寨。 再進得博爾根山梁，並攻克得瑪覺烏寨落，攻公雅山。 十二月，授定邊將軍，以阿桂、豐升額副之。 進克明郭宗，再進克底木達。 底木達者，僧格桑父澤旺所居寨也。 師至，俘澤旺，檻致京師，誅於市，而僧格桑奔大金川。 溫福檄索諾木令縛獻僧格桑，不應。
In the thirty-sixth year, when troops were sent against Jinchuan, he was appointed Right Deputy General of Pacification, with Vice Minister Gui Lin as his assistant, to suppress the rebels together. Wen Fu marched out by the western route from Wenchuan; Gui Lin marched out by the southern route from Dajianlu. At this time Lesser Jinchuan headman Zewang's son Sengge Sang ceded land to beg aid from Greater Jinchuan headman Suonuo Mu, and Suonuo Mu secretly sent troops to help him. The emperor ordered that Lesser Jinchuan be suppressed first and that Greater Jinchuan's guilt not yet be proclaimed. When Wen Fu reached Dajianlu he divided his forces into three columns: Wen Fu by Balang La, provincial commander-in-chief Dong Tianbi from Jia Jin Da to relieve Damu Ba Zong, and Governor-General A Er Tai from Yueza to attack Sengge Sang. In the eleventh month he was promoted to grand secretary of the Hall of Military Eminence. In the twelfth month he reached Balang La, fought three days and nights, and the rebels were defeated and withdrew. In the first month of the thirty-seventh year he took Damu Ba Zong. He advanced against Si Di Ye An while dividing troops through Biesi Man and Ma Er Wa Er Ji for a two-pronged attack and took Zi Li. Advancing again he took Dong Ma, then Lu Ding Zong and Ka Mu Se Er, and captured the various blockhouses and stockades. Advancing again he took the Bo Er Gen ridge, also took the Ma Jue Wu stockades, and attacked Gong Ya Mountain. In the twelfth month he was appointed Pacification General, with A Gui and Feng Sheng'e as deputies. He advanced to take Ming Guo Zong and again advanced to take Di Mu Da. Di Mu Da was the stockade where Sengge Sang's father Zewang lived. When the army arrived, Zewang was captured, sent to the capital in a cage, and executed in the marketplace, while Sengge Sang fled to Greater Jinchuan. Wen Fu ordered Suonuo Mu by proclamation to bind and deliver Sengge Sang; he did not comply.
15
上將進討大金川，溫福等疏言：「前此張廣泗徵金川，十路、七路分合不常，實祗有六路，皆以抵勒烏圍、噶爾依為主。 一為卡撒正路，自美諾至噶爾依，約五程，為傅恆進兵路； 一為丹壩，自維州橋經番地抵勒烏圍，約二十餘程，中有穆津岡天險，為岳鍾琪進兵路； 一地名僧格桑，自美諾抵噶爾依，六七程，即總兵馬良柱所行路； 一為革布什咱，一為馬爾邦，皆距噶爾依六七程，險狹難行； 一為綽斯甲布寨至勒烏圍三程，至噶爾依亦三程，均隔大河，碉寨林立，難攻。 此外又有俄坡一路，從綽斯甲布寨至勒烏圍，僅二程，路較平。 今當由卡撒正路進兵，其俄坡一路，既有綽斯甲布土司原出兵復其侵地，可為犄角。 其餘各路，分兵牽制，使不能兼顧。 」於是溫福自功噶爾拉入，阿桂自當噶爾拉入，豐升額自綽斯甲布入。 溫福性剛愎，不廣諮方略，惟襲訥親、張廣泗故事，以碉卡攻碉卡，修築千計。 所將兵二萬餘，強半散在各碉卡。 每逾數日當奏事，即督兵攻碉。 士卒多傷亡，諮怨無鬥志。 溫福日置酒高會，參贊伍岱歎曰：「焉有為帥若此而能制勝者？ 」因密疏聞上，溫福亦疏劾伍岱。 上命豐升額及額駙色布騰巴勒珠爾按治。 溫福又言色布騰巴勒珠爾朋比傾陷，上為奪伍岱職，令色布騰巴勒珠爾逮詣熱河行在，獄成，戍伍岱伊犁。
The emperor was about to advance against Greater Jinchuan; Wen Fu and others memorialized: "When Zhang Guangsi earlier suppressed Jinchuan, routes numbered ten or seven and merged and split without fixed pattern; in fact there were only six routes, all aimed mainly at Lewu Wei and Ga Er Yi. One was the main Kasa route from Meinuo to Ga Er Yi, about five stages, the route by which Fu Heng advanced; one was Dan Ba, from Weizhou Bridge through tribal territory to Lewu Wei, more than twenty stages, with the natural barrier of Mu Jin Gang in between, the route by which Yue Zhongqi advanced; one place was called Sengge Sang, from Meinuo to Ga Er Yi, six or seven stages, the route taken by garrison commander Ma Liangzhu; one was Gebushizhai, one was Ma Er Bang—each six or seven stages from Ga Er Yi, narrow, dangerous, and hard to traverse; one ran from the Zhuosijia Bu stockade to Lewu Wei in three stages and to Ga Er Yi in three more—both separated by great rivers, with blockhouses and stockades standing in dense ranks, hard to attack. Besides these there was also the E Po route from the Zhuosijia Bu stockade to Lewu Wei, only two stages, with a relatively level road. Now troops should advance by the main Kasa route; as for the E Po route, since the Zhuosijia Bu chieftain originally sent troops to recover his seized lands, it can serve as a pincer. On the remaining routes, divide troops to pin down the enemy so they cannot attend to everything. " Thereupon Wen Fu entered by Gong Ga Er La, A Gui by Dang Ga Er La, and Feng Sheng'e by Zhuosijia Bu. Wen Fu was by nature stubborn and willful, did not widely consult on strategy, and only followed the precedents of Neqin and Zhang Guangsi, attacking blockhouses with blockhouses and building more than a thousand fortifications. The troops he commanded numbered more than twenty thousand, more than half scattered among the various blockhouses and forts. Every few days, when he should have been reporting to the throne, he instead directed troops to attack blockhouses. Soldiers suffered heavy casualties, grumbled in resentment, and lost their fighting spirit. Wen Fu held grand banquets every day; staff officer Wu Dai sighed and said: "How can a commander like this win victory? " He therefore memorialized secretly to the emperor; Wen Fu also memorialized to impeach Wu Dai. The emperor ordered Feng Sheng'e and imperial son-in-law Sebuteng Balzhu'er to investigate. Wen Fu also claimed Sebuteng Balzhu'er had formed a faction to frame him; the emperor stripped Wu Dai of office, ordered Sebuteng Balzhu'er arrested and sent to the imperial camp at Rehe; when the case was complete, Wu Dai was exiled to Yili.
16
三十八年春，溫福師至功噶爾拉，賊阻險，不得進，別取道攻昔嶺，駐軍木果木； 令提督董天弼分軍屯底木達。 木果木、底木達皆故小金川地，索諾木陰使小金川頭人煽諸降番使復叛。 諸降番以師久頓不進，遂蜂起應之。 先攻底木達，天弼死之，次劫糧台，潛襲木果木。 溫福不嚴備山後要隘，賊突薄大營，奪砲局，斷汲道。 時大營兵尚萬餘，運糧役數千，爭避入大營，溫福堅閉壘門不納，轟而潰，聲如壞堤，於是軍心益震。 賊四面蹂入，溫福中槍死，各卡兵望風潰散。 參贊海蘭察聞警赴援，殿餘兵自間道出。 小金川地盡陷。 上初聞溫福死，詔予一等伯爵，世襲罔替，祀昭忠祠。 既，劉秉恬、海蘭察、富勒渾各疏言溫福僨事狀，命奪伯爵，予三等輕車都尉世職。 四十一年，命並罷之。 子勒保、永保，皆有傳。
In the spring of the thirty-eighth year Wen Fu's army reached Gong Ga Er La; the rebels blocked the passes and he could not advance; he took another route to attack Xi Ling and encamped at Muguomu; He ordered provincial commander-in-chief Dong Tianbi to divide his forces and encamp at Di Mu Da. Muguomu and Di Mu Da were both former Lesser Jinchuan territory; Suonuo Mu secretly had Lesser Jinchuan headmen incite the surrendered tribes to rebel again. Because the army had long halted without advancing, the surrendered tribes rose in swarms to join them. They first attacked Di Mu Da, where Tianbi was killed; next they raided the grain depot and secretly struck Muguomu. Wen Fu did not strictly guard the vital passes behind the mountains; the rebels suddenly closed on the main camp, seized the artillery depot, and cut the water supply route. The main camp still held more than ten thousand troops, and several thousand transport laborers scrambled to flee inside; Wen Fu firmly closed the camp gates and refused them entry; the crowd burst through with a roar like a broken dike, and army morale collapsed. The rebels poured in from all sides; Wen Fu was shot and killed; troops at the various forts fled at the first sign of defeat. Staff officer Hailancha heard the alarm and rushed to aid; he covered the remaining troops as they withdrew by a hidden route. All Lesser Jinchuan territory was lost. When the emperor first heard of Wen Fu's death, he decreed a first-class countship for him, hereditary in perpetuity, and enshrinement in the Shrine of Loyal Martyrs. Afterward Liu Bingtian, Hailancha, and Fu Lehun each memorialized on Wen Fu's botched campaign; he was stripped of the countship and given instead the hereditary office of third-class Commandant of Light Chariots. In the forty-first year both honors were revoked. His sons Lebao and Yongbao both have separate biographies.
17
=【論】=論曰：金川再亂，開泰、阿爾泰皆主以番攻番，遲回坐誤。 桂林有宋元俊不能用，反齮齕之，擁兵不進。 阿爾泰與元俊劾桂林，此其意以軍國為重，不屑屑阿貴近、疏卑遠，宜若可成功，乃坐蜚語敗。 溫福銳進，似勝開泰輩，乃又剛愎，有董天弼不能用，予兵至少，令僻處軍後，卒致僨潰，徒以身殉，豈不惜哉？
The commentary says: When Jinchuan rebelled again, Kai Tai and A Er Tai both advocated letting tribe fight tribe, and through delay they sat by while opportunity was lost. Gui Lin had Song Yuanjun at his disposal but could not use him, instead undermined him, and held his troops back without advancing. A Er Tai and Yuanjun impeached Gui Lin; in this their intent was to put military affairs first and not fawn on the powerful while neglecting the humble—it seemed they might succeed, yet they were ruined by slander. Wen Fu advanced boldly and seemed to surpass Kai Tai and the others, yet he was also stubborn and willful; he had Dong Tianbi but could not use him, gave him too few troops, and posted him in an obscure position behind the army, ultimately causing collapse—he died for nothing; is that not lamentable?
