王紫綬袁州佐黎士弘多弘安佟國聘王繻田呈瑞張孟球
Wang Zishou, Yuan Zhouzuo, Li Shihong, Duo Hong'an, Tong Guopin, Wang Xu, Tian Chengrui, and Zhang Mengqiu
3
王紫綬，字金章，河南祥符人。 順治三年進士，選庶吉士。 散館，授編修。 乞養歸，僑寓蘇門山中，從孫奇逢講學。 居十有七年，母歿，服闋，康熙十二年，授江西贛南道副使。
Wang Zishou, whose courtesy name was Jinzhang, came from Xiangfu in Henan. He passed the jinshi examination in the third year of Shunzhi and was chosen as a Hanlin bachelor. When his term at the academy ended, he received an appointment as compiler. He asked to retire and support his parents, settled in the Sumen Mountains, and pursued his studies with Sun Qifeng. He remained there seventeen years. After his mother's death and the close of mourning, in the twelfth year of Kangxi he was made Vice Commissioner of the Gannan Circuit in Jiangxi.
4
吳三桂反，贛南總兵劉進寶有謀略，紫綬推誠結納，預籌防禦。 既而江西降眾屯墾者相繼叛，惟贛南尚未動。 紫綬與進寶謀：「閩、粵反已見端，贛南扼其間，應援前朝故事，設巡撫以資鎮攝。 」申疆吏上請，允之。 十四年，賊勢益熾，山寇蜂起，鎮兵疲於奔命，乃練鄉勇以輔之，屢殺賊有功。 十五年，巡撫白色純及進寶先後卒官，參將周球領鎮兵。 三桂將高得捷、韓大任據吉安，餉道絕，屬縣相繼陷。 大任屢致書勸降，送偽署巡撫劄，紫綬斬其使。 球以乏餉為難，紫綬集士商勸輸間架稅，得白金四萬畀球，餉以無缺。 鎮南將軍覺羅舒恕率禁旅下廣東，為尚之信將嚴自明所敗，兵退，距贛州三十里。 自明約得捷由吉安會師夾擊。 紫綬薦降將許盛率所部漳州水兵五百人益師，夜泅江斫賊營，禁旅繼之，擊敗自明。 得捷等勢孤，不敢复窺贛。 鎮兵出剿土寇，掠村民，紫綬曰：「鄉民脅從，若並以賊論，贛南二府十六縣將無孑遺。 」戒鎮將毋妄發兵，飭有司招撫，分別留遣，賑濟難民，境內稍安。 乃規复萬安、泰和兩縣。 自螺山間道達墨潭，可登舟，於是南昌道始通，運餉銀十萬至。 又發附近倉穀贍軍，人心大定。 巡撫佟國禎亦自間道至，始知紫綬已擢浙江督糧道參政。 贛南久不通驛報，大學士李霨言於朝曰：「紫綬死守危疆，三年於茲。 為國惜才，援而出之，猶可大用。 」故有是擢。 紫綬聞命泣下。
When Wu Sangui rose in rebellion, the Gannan regional commander Liu Jinbao proved a capable strategist. Zishou won his trust and laid defensive plans well ahead of time. Soon surrendered settlers farming in Jiangxi were rebelling in succession, yet Gannan alone still held firm. Zishou and Jinbao agreed: "Rebellion in Fujian and Guangdong is already taking shape. Gannan lies between them; we should follow earlier precedent and establish a governor-general to hold the region in check." They submitted the proposal through the provincial authorities, and the court approved it. In the fourteenth year rebel strength mounted and mountain bandits rose everywhere. The garrison was exhausted chasing them, so he trained local militia to reinforce the army and won repeated victories against the rebels. In the fifteenth year Governor-General Bai Sechun and Jinbao died in succession on duty, and Deputy Commander Zhou Qiu assumed command of the garrison. Sangui's generals Gao Dejie and Han Daren seized Ji'an, severing the supply lines, and the surrounding counties fell in turn. Daren wrote again and again urging him to surrender and sent a forged appointment as governor-general. Zishou had the messenger beheaded. Qiu was hamstrung by the shortage of rations. Zishou rallied local gentry and merchants to levy a building-tax surcharge, raised forty thousand taels of silver for Qiu, and kept the army supplied without interruption. The Pacifier of the South, Aisin Gioro Shushu, marched into Guangdong at the head of the banner army but was routed by Shang Zhixin's general Yan Ziming. His force fell back to within thirty li of Ganzhou. Ziming arranged with Dejie to unite their armies at Ji'an and attack Ganzhou from two sides. Zishou put forward the surrendered general Xu Sheng and his five hundred Zhangzhou marines. They swam the river at night and raided the rebel camp; the banner troops pressed the attack, and Ziming was driven off. Cut off and weakened, Dejie and his allies no longer dared threaten Ganzhou. When the garrison marched out against local bandits they looted the countryside. Zishou warned: "These villagers followed under duress. Treat them all as rebels and the two prefectures and sixteen counties of Gannan will be wiped clean." He forbade the commander to deploy troops recklessly, ordered civil officials to offer amnesty, sorted who should stay or leave, and fed the destitute until the region grew calm again. He then set out to recover Wan'an and Taihe. He opened a trail from Luoshan to Motan where boats could be taken, restoring the route to Nanchang for the first time in years and bringing in a hundred thousand taels of silver in supplies. He released grain from nearby storehouses to feed the troops, and morale steadied across the region. Governor-General Tong Guozhen reached him by the same back trail and learned only then that Zishou had already been promoted to Administrative Commissioner on the Zhejiang grain-transport circuit. Gannan had been cut off from courier reports for so long that Grand Secretary Li Ji told the court: "Zishou has held this desperate frontier for three years running." The state should not waste such a man; pull him out while there is still time and he can serve on a larger scale. That was how he came to receive the promotion. Zishou wept when he received the appointment.
5
十六年，上官，察積弊，歎曰：「糧官不可為也！ 漕截減而軍困，白折浮而民困，吾安忍竭東南之澤而漁之？ 」一月即引疾去。 迨開博學鴻詞科，魏象樞以紫綬與湯斌同薦入試。 放還。 卒。
In the sixteenth year he took up his new post, surveyed entrenched abuses, and exclaimed: "This grain office is impossible!" Cuts to the grain transport starve the army; inflated silver conversions crush the people. How could I drain the wealth of the southeast to fill official coffers? Within a month he pleaded illness and resigned. When the special erudition examination was convened, Wei Xiangshu nominated Zishou along with Tang Bin to sit for it. He was dismissed and sent home. He died.
6
袁州佐，字左之，山東濟寧人。 順治十二年進士，授陝西乾州知州。 入為工部員外郎，遷郎中。 有清直聲，胥吏不敢牟利。 時山陵工巨，經費浩穰，州佐曰：「民困極矣，寸縑尺縷，皆閭閻膏血！ 」力清乾沒，司焚帛，省金錢鉅萬。 出為陝西甘山道僉事。 青海蒙古諸部覬得大草灘為牧地。 康熙九年，偕提督張勇度地畫界，堅拒，寢其議。 自後青海蒙古諸部人不敢复窺邊。 歲協西寧餽運，負載千里，甘州民苦之，州佐力請得罷。 甘州駐兵數千，待餉急，力為籌備，軍得宿飽。 十年，遷直隸口北道參議。 地確民貧，逋課積累，倉儲歷歲侵漁，耗蝕無算。 州佐請按籍覈實，清宿蠹。 大吏懼以失察得罪，陽韙而陰沮之。 州佐擘畫盤錯，致疾乞休，未去官，卒。
Yuan Zhouzuo, whose courtesy name was Zuozhi, came from Jining in Shandong. He passed the jinshi examination in the twelfth year of Shunzhi and was appointed magistrate of Qianzhou in Shaanxi. He was called to the capital as a vice director in the Ministry of Works and later promoted to director. His reputation for integrity was such that clerks dared not profit at public expense. Imperial tomb construction was then at its height and costs were staggering. Zhouzuo protested: "The people are at their limit. Every scrap of cloth comes from their blood and sweat." He rooted out embezzlement and, by reforming the office that burned sacrificial silk, saved tens of thousands in silver. He was posted abroad as assistant commissioner of the Ganshan Circuit in Shaanxi. The Mongol tribes of Qinghai were pressing to take Dacaotan as grazing land. In the ninth year of Kangxi he joined Regional Commander Zhang Yong in surveying the border and held the line firm, and the proposal was dropped. After that the Qinghai Mongols no longer dared probe the frontier. Every year Ganzhou had to haul supplies a thousand li to support Xining, and the people were crushed by the burden. Zhouzuo petitioned until the practice was abolished. Several thousand troops were stationed at Ganzhou and needed rations urgently. He threw himself into provisioning them, and the army never went hungry. In the tenth year he was transferred to Administrative Supervisor of the Koubei Circuit in Zhili. The soil was poor and the people impoverished. Tax arrears mounted, and year after year granary officials plundered the stores until the losses were beyond reckoning. Zhouzuo asked to audit the registers and root out entrenched corruption. Senior officials feared being charged with negligence; they praised him to his face while undermining him behind the scenes. Zhouzuo threw himself into the knotted problems until illness forced him to request retirement. He died before he could leave office.
7
州佐在甘州久，言邊境要害戰守狀，原委斠然。 謂邊地民稀，宜用開中法，分河東鹽引三之一輸粟河西資軍食； 又宜簡練鄉勇，拔置卒伍，不待召募，可坐收精銳。 時詔簡監司具才望者入為卿貳，州佐在選，會卒，未及用。
After long service at Ganzhou, Zhouzuo could explain every strategic point on the frontier and how to defend it with complete clarity. He argued that the frontier was sparsely settled and should use the kaizhong system, dedicating one-third of Hedong salt certificates to shipping grain west of the river for the army's rations; and that local militia should be drilled and drafted into the ranks so that crack troops could be raised without waiting on recruitment. An edict then called circuit officials of talent to the capital for vice-ministerial posts. Zhouzuo was on the list, but he died before he could be appointed.
8
黎士弘，字媿曾，福建長汀人。 少讀書山中二十年，篤於孝友。 順治十一年，舉順天鄉試，授江西廣信府推官。 鋤強糾貪，姦宄斂戢。 理讞牘，脫無罪數百人，時為語曰：「遇黎則生。 」署玉山縣事。 兵后城中草三尺，不辨街巷，居民才三十二家。 士弘立學建治，招集流亡，墾田定賦，民復舊業。 裁缺，改授永新知縣。 政清獄簡，與民休息。 舊例，二月開徵，五月解其半。 士弘陳於上官曰：「縣小民窮，二月寫租十石，貸銀一兩，三月可減至六石，四月則三石。 請以四月開徵，五月解，展兩月之徵，已為窮民留數万之糧。 」布政使劉楗素寬仁，即允之。
Li Shihong, whose courtesy name was Kuizeng, came from Changting in Fujian. He spent twenty years studying in the mountains as a young man and was known for filial devotion and brotherly loyalty. In the eleventh year of Shunzhi he passed the provincial examination at Shuntian and was appointed investigating censor of Guangxin Prefecture in Jiangxi. He broke the power of local bullies, punished corruption, and drove villains to heel. Reviewing case files, he freed hundreds of innocent people. A saying spread: "Meet Li and you live." He was assigned to administer Yushan County. After the fighting, waist-high grass choked the streets of the county seat and only thirty-two households remained. Shihong founded schools, rebuilt local government, gathered refugees, opened fields, and fixed tax assessments until people returned to their old livelihoods. When his post was abolished, he was reassigned as magistrate of Yongxin. His government was clean, litigation light, and the people were given room to recover. By custom, tax collection began in the second month and half the levy was forwarded in the fifth. Shihong told his superiors: "This is a small, poor county. If collection starts in the second month, tenants must pledge ten piculs of rent and borrow a tael of silver. By the third month that shrinks to six piculs, and by the fourth to three." Let collection begin in the fourth month and remittance follow in the fifth. Delaying two months would leave tens of thousands of piculs in the hands of the poor." Provincial Treasurer Liu Jian, a man of known kindness, approved the request at once.
9
甲訴乙悔婚。 鄉俗婚書各裝為卷，書男女生辰。 兩造固鄰舊，女生辰所素悉，偽為卷為證。 士弘先問媒證：「乙得甲聘禮若干？ 行聘時有何客？ 」媒證出不意，妄舉以對。 復問甲，所對各異。 擘視捲軸，竹猶青，笑詰之曰：「若訂婚三載，捲軸竹色猶新，此非臨訟偽造者乎？ 」甲乃服罪。 縣吏左梅伯有叔富而無子，梅伯糾賊劫殺之，獲賊而梅伯逃。 士弘抵任，叔妻哭訴，陰跡梅伯匿安福勢宦家，故緩詞曰：「此舊事。 前官不了，余安能按之？ 」數月，梅伯歸，叔妻复訴，置不問。 梅伯且出收叔遺產，叔妻號於庭曰：「公號廉明，今寬殺人者罪，且佔寡婦田，何得為廉明！ 」陽怒，批其牘曰：「止問田土，不問人命。 」梅伯益自得，赴縣訴理，乃笑謂曰：「候汝三載矣！ 」批其牘曰：「止問人命，不問田土。 」梅伯遂伏法。 其善斷獄多類此。 考最，擢陝西甘州同知。 复考最，擢江南常州知府。
One party sued another for breaking off a betrothal. Local custom bound marriage contracts into scrolls bearing the birth dates of bride and groom. The parties were old neighbors, so the plaintiff knew the girl's birth date well and forged a scroll as proof. Shihong first questioned the matchmaker: "How much betrothal gift did the defendant receive from the plaintiff?" Who attended when the betrothal gifts were delivered?" Caught off guard, the matchmaker invented answers on the spot. When he questioned the plaintiff, the stories did not match. He split the scroll open: the bamboo was still green. Smiling, he said, "You claim a betrothal of three years, yet the bamboo looks freshly cut. Was this forged for the lawsuit?" The plaintiff confessed. County clerk Zuo Meibo had a wealthy, childless uncle. Meibo hired bandits to rob and murder him. The bandits were caught, but Meibo escaped. When Shihong took office, the widow came weeping to accuse Meibo. He traced Meibo secretly to the house of a powerful Anfu official and said mildly, "This is an old case." My predecessor never closed it. How can I take it up now?" Months later Meibo returned. The widow sued again, and Shihong ignored her. Meibo then moved to seize the estate. The widow wailed in court: "You are praised as just, yet you spare a murderer and let him take a widow's land. What justice is this?" He feigned anger and endorsed her petition: "Investigate the land only, not the killing." Emboldened, Meibo came to court to claim the land. Shihong smiled and said, "I have been waiting three years for you." He endorsed the petition: "Investigate the killing only, not the land." Meibo was executed. Many of his judgments followed this pattern. Rated at the top of his evaluation, he was promoted to subprefect of Ganzhou in Shaanxi. Rated highest again, he was promoted to prefect of Changzhou in Jiangnan.
10
吳三桂亂起，關隴震動，大吏疏請擢洮西道副使，未到官而洮、岷陷。 邊外群番乘亂內犯，肆剽掠，調署甘山道。 王輔臣叛，河東失守。 士弘以兵集當謀帥，言於巡撫，謂：「恢復河東，非用河西兵不可； 用河西兵，非責之提督張勇不可。 」疏入，授勇靖逆將軍，節制諸鎮。 复蘭州，士弘贊畫功為多。 署甘肅按察使，按失守官吏罪，務平允。 寧夏兵叛，殺提督陳福，調寧夏道。 嚴守禦，安反側，免衛所逋糧七萬五千石。 康熙十六年，寇平，以功進布政使參議。 母老乞歸，家居幾三十年。 卒，年八十。
When Wu Sangui rebelled, the Guanlong region was thrown into turmoil. Senior officials memorialized to promote him to Vice Commissioner of the Taoxi Circuit, but before he could take up the post, Tao and Min had fallen. Border tribes raided inward amid the chaos, and he was reassigned to act as commissioner of the Ganshan Circuit. When Wang Fuchen rebelled, the east bank of the river was lost. With armies massing, Shihong told the governor that a commander was needed: "Recovering the east bank will require troops from west of the river;" "and only Regional Commander Zhang Yong can lead them." The memorial was approved. Zhang Yong was appointed Pacifier of Rebellion General with authority over all garrisons. When Lanzhou was recovered, Shihong's planning counted for much of the success. Acting as Gansu Surveillance Commissioner, he tried officials who had lost their posts, always seeking fair judgment. When the Ningxia garrison mutinied and killed Regional Commander Chen Fu, Shihong was transferred to the Ningxia Circuit. He tightened defenses, calmed wavering loyalties, and remitted seventy-five thousand piculs of grain arrears owed by the garrison colonies. In the sixteenth year of Kangxi, after the rebels were suppressed, he was promoted to Administrative Supervisor in the provincial treasury for his service. When his mother grew old he asked to retire and lived at home for nearly thirty years. He died at the age of eighty.
11
士弘備兵甘山時，取晉辛憲英語：「軍旅之間可以濟者，惟仁與恕。 」因以名其堂。
While Shihong was organizing troops on Ganshan, he took a line from Jin-dynasty Xin Xianying: "In war, only benevolence and forbearance can carry the day." He named his hall after that saying.
12
多弘安，字君修，直隸阜城人。 順治五年，選拔貢生。 康熙初，授廣東靈山知縣。 兵後荒殘，居無衙舍。 弘安請免積年逋賦，招撫流移，捐給牛種，民得安耕稼。 葺城垣，創學宮，繕官廨，捕除盜賊，靈山大治，士民刊石紀其績。 七年，遷奉天承德知縣。 旗、民抗法者，送部懲治，皆懾服。 十年，擢陝西延安靖邊同知。 十六年，補江南淮安山盱河務同知。 時高堰長堤潰決，淮水注寶應、高郵，不復出清口敵黃。 黃水直注里河，運道淤淺，复隨淮入堰，無由會清口下云梯關入海，近海口盡淤墊。 弘安與河督靳輔籌策築高堰，束淮敵黃，治爛泥淺諸故道，導清水入里河，運道乃通。 修築兩岸及河口清江大閘，與淮工相表裡。 清河達雲梯關數百里，葭葦榛蕪，壅塞故道。 用以水攻沙法，塞周橋、高澗諸閘，使清淮無旁洩，蓄全力攻積沙。 十七年，大雨，淮盛漲，與黃併入海。 治淮、治黃、治運，並收成效。 十九年，擢淮安知府。 二十年，擢淮揚道。 二十四年，擢安徽按察使。 時方議浚下河、治高堰。 弘安入覲，疏陳：「高堰宜急治，無論下河開浚與否。 治堰法，砌石先安地釘，湖底水深，費帑甚繁。 如用板若掃，水勢盪掣，尤易摧殘。 惟密釘排椿，內實以碎石，庶可敵風浪，省金錢。 十餘年後，黃河刷深，則湖、河水俱卑，高堰既固，下河亦漸就理。 」二十八年，遷江西布政使，乞歸。 後值黃、運兩河潰溢，起用弘安。 會病卒，祀靈山名宦。
Duo Hong'an, whose courtesy name was Junxiu, came from Fucheng in Zhili. In the fifth year of Shunzhi he was selected as a tribute student by special examination. Early in the Kangxi reign he was appointed magistrate of Lingshan in Guangdong. The county lay in ruins after the fighting, and there was no government office for him to occupy. Hong'an secured remission of years of tax arrears, brought back refugees, and donated oxen and seed until the people could farm again in peace. He rebuilt the walls, founded a school, restored government buildings, and stamped out banditry until Lingshan prospered again. The gentry and people set up a stone monument to his work. In the seventh year he was transferred to magistrate of Chengde in Fengtian. Bannermen and commoners who defied the law were sent to the ministry for punishment, and all submitted in fear. In the tenth year he was promoted to subprefect of Jingbian in Yan'an, Shaanxi. In the sixteenth year he was appointed subprefect in charge of the Shanxu River works at Huai'an in Jiangnan. The long embankment of the Gaoyan dam had burst. The Huai poured into Baoying and Gaoyou and no longer met the Yellow River at Qingkou. Yellow River water flooded the inner channel, the transport route silted up, and the Huai was forced back into the dam. The route to Qingkou and down through Yunti Pass to the sea was blocked, and the estuary was choked with silt. Hong'an and River Commissioner Jin Fu planned the Gaoyan dam to hold the Huai against the Yellow River, cleared the old Lanni and Qian channels, and directed clear water into the inner channel until the transport route was open again. He strengthened both banks and built the great Qingjiang sluice at the estuary, works that complemented the Huai River project. For hundreds of li from the clear channel to Yunti Pass, reeds and brush choked the old waterway. He used the method of scouring sand with water, closed the Zhouqiao and Gaojian sluices, and kept the clear Huai from leaking sideways so its full force could scour the accumulated silt. In the seventeenth year, after heavy rains, the Huai rose high and carried the Yellow River with it into the sea. Control of the Huai, the Yellow River, and the transport route all succeeded together. In the nineteenth year he was promoted to prefect of Huai'an. In the twentieth year he was promoted to commissioner of the Huai-Yang Circuit. In the twenty-fourth year he was promoted to Anhui Surveillance Commissioner. The court was then debating whether to dredge the lower river and repair the Gaoyan dam. When Hong'an presented himself at court, he memorialized: "The Gaoyan dam must be repaired at once, whether or not the lower river is dredged." The usual method of stone facing requires ground nails driven first. The lake bed is deep, and the cost is enormous. Boards or fascines are worse still: the surge of water tears them apart all the more easily. Only close rows of piles packed with rubble within can stand against wind and waves and save money. In ten years or more the Yellow River will scour deeper, lake and river levels will fall, the Gaoyan dam will hold firm, and the lower river will gradually come right. In the twenty-eighth year he was made Jiangxi Provincial Treasurer and asked to retire. Later, when the Yellow River and the transport canal both burst their banks, Hong'an was called back into service. He fell ill and died before he could finish. Lingshan enshrined him among its notable officials.
13
佟國聘，字君莘，奉天人。 以廕生補吏部筆帖式。 康熙十年，授江南碭山知縣，縣當黃河衝，研求治河方略。 擢歸仁堤同知，調宿桃同知。 擢貴州平遠知府，河督靳輔疏留任，十餘年倚如左右手。 塞楊家莊、蕭家渡決口，建硃家堂、溫家廟二石壩，浚白洋引河九道，築黃河南、北兩岸堤，浚中河，靡役不從。 久之，擢山東濟寧道副使。 道地為漕運樞紐，卹夫役，減苛稅，除冗費，能舉其職。 復調監督高堰工程。 三十八年，卒於官。
Tong Guopin, whose courtesy name was Junxin, came from Fengtian. Through hereditary privilege he entered the Ministry of Personnel as a clerk. In the tenth year of Kangxi he was appointed magistrate of Dangshan in Jiangnan. The county lay in the Yellow River's path, and he devoted himself to studying river control. He was promoted to subprefect of the Guiren embankment and later transferred to subprefect of Sutao. He was promoted to prefect of Pingyuan in Guizhou, but River Commissioner Jin Fu memorialized to keep him. For more than ten years Jin Fu relied on him as his right hand. He closed the breaches at Yangjiazhuang and Xiaojiadu, built stone dams at Zhujiatang and Wenjiamiao, dredged nine channels of the Baiyang diversion, raised embankments on both banks of the Yellow River, and cleared the middle river. No project was left undone. Eventually he was promoted to Vice Commissioner of the Jining Circuit in Shandong. The circuit was the hub of the grain transport system. He eased the burden on laborers, cut harsh taxes, eliminated waste, and discharged his duties well. He was reassigned to supervise the Gaoyan dam project. In the thirty-eighth year he died in office.
14
王繻，字慎夫，河南睢州人。 少學於湯斌。 康熙二十五年，以歲貢生授直隸東明知縣。 糧賦多欺隱，易甲長，大戶使族長督之，飛灑不行，流亡來歸。 民間養官馬為累，力除之。 撫盜魁，責以緝捕，盜絕跡。 逃人誣攀良民，雪之。 民有繼妻素淫，欲並亂前妻女，不從，戕之死。 繻謂母道絕，當故殺妻前夫子律論斬，報可，因著為例。 母憂去，服闋，補獲鹿。 治驛有法，民不累於供億。 內遷戶部員外郎，擢郎中。 三十八年，出為江南糧儲道。 道舊有倉規銀鉅萬，繻一擯勿取。 將徵漕，扁舟行縣，懲其濫收者。 至宜興，宜興民曰：「吾民四十年不見糧道，今飛來耶？ 」號曰「飛糧道」。 道庫歲收銀八十五萬兩，為修船及弁丁運費。 運丁預支行糧，例扣月息，丁益困，繻悉罷之。
Wang Xu, whose courtesy name was Shenfu, came from Suizhou in Henan. As a young man he studied under Tang Bin. In the twenty-fifth year of Kangxi he was appointed magistrate of Dongming in Zhili as a tribute student. Grain taxes were widely concealed. He replaced village heads, had large households supervised by clan elders, ended tax-shifting abuses, and refugees began to return. The people were burdened by the duty of raising official horses, and he abolished the practice. He won over bandit chiefs and charged them with hunting down their fellows until banditry disappeared. When fugitives falsely implicated innocent people, he cleared their names. A man's stepmother, who was notoriously licentious, tried to force herself on his daughter by a previous wife. When the girl refused, the stepmother killed her. Xu held that she had severed all maternal duty and should be sentenced under the statute for killing a stepchild. The court approved, and the ruling became a precedent. He left office to mourn his mother and, when mourning ended, was appointed to Huolu. He ran the courier stations efficiently, and the people were not burdened by supply duties. He was transferred to the capital as a vice director in the Ministry of Revenue and later promoted to director. In the thirty-eighth year he was appointed Grain Storage Commissioner of Jiangnan. The office had long collected tens of thousands in customary granary fees. Xu refused every penny. Before the grain transport levy was collected, he toured the counties in a small boat and punished officials who overcharged. At Yixing the people said, "We have not seen a grain commissioner in forty years. Has one flown in today?" They nicknamed him the Flying Grain Commissioner. The commissioner's treasury took in 850,000 taels a year for ship repairs and transport costs. Transport workers received grain advances with monthly interest deducted by custom, which left them ever poorer. Xu abolished the practice entirely.
15
四十年，擢江蘇按察使。 治獄仁恕，多所平反。 宿州生攜妻子出客授，妻兄女來視，居數日，妻子併中毒死，妻兄素有隙，疑其女置毒，告官，被刑誣服。 繻疑之，問其室來往復何人，得十二歲學徒畏師嚴置鏚食中狀，事乃白。 無錫民毆攻皮匠，匠死，僧與民仇，證為鬥毆殺。 繻察鬥毆日月在保辜限外，詰曰：「傷重何不醫？ 」出醫方，則匠死於傷寒，僧乃服。 上南巡，入覲，顧宋犖曰：「朕聞繻督糧時官聲甚好。 」時繻已病，遣御醫視之，賜德里雅噶藥一器，溫旨慰諭，复賜御書。 繻曰：「按察任大責重，臥治即辜恩。 」引疾歸，年甫五十。 久之，卒於家。
In the fortieth year he was promoted to Jiangsu Surveillance Commissioner. He handled cases with mercy and reversed many wrongful convictions. A Suzhou scholar took his family to teach abroad. His wife's niece came to visit, and within days wife and children died of poison. The wife's brother, who bore a grudge, accused his daughter. Under torture she confessed falsely. Xu had doubts and asked who else visited the house. He learned that a twelve-year-old student, fearing his teacher's harshness, had put arsenic in the food. The truth came out. A Wuxi man beat a leather worker to death. A monk who hated the man testified that it was murder in a brawl. Xu noted that the brawl fell outside the statutory period for liability for wounds. He asked, "If the injury was serious, why was no doctor summoned?" The prescription showed the worker had died of typhoid fever, and the monk confessed. During an imperial southern tour, Xu presented himself at court. The emperor turned to Song Luo and said, "I hear Xu had an excellent reputation when he supervised grain transport." Xu was already ill. The emperor sent an imperial physician, gave him a dose of court medicine, spoke words of comfort, and bestowed imperial calligraphy. Xu replied, "The surveillance commissioner's post carries heavy responsibility. To govern from my sickbed would betray the emperor's grace." He pleaded illness and retired at the age of fifty. He died at home some years later.
16
田呈瑞，字介璞，山西汾陽人。 康熙中，仕為中書舍人。 出襄南河事。 有堤當水沖，曰：「此堤一壞，萬家其魚矣！ 土堤易修易敗，宜更以石。 」家素豐，出私錢成之。 以功擢大名道，未之任，調陝西臨洮道。 遇飢治賑，策馬行郡縣山谷間，豪右胥吏不敢為奸弊。 呈瑞念救荒無善策，於蘭州西石佛灣鑿渠，教民造水車，引以溉田，歲增粟十餘萬石，民為建生祠。 調浙江金衢嚴道，署糧儲道，徵漕積弊盡洗滌之。 值旱，冒暑省荒，感疾，乞歸不得。 五十九年，卒於官。
Tian Chengrui, whose courtesy name was Jiepu, came from Fenyang in Shanxi. During the Kangxi reign he served as a secretariat draftsman. He was sent out to manage the Xiangnan River works. An embankment stood in the direct path of the current. He warned, "If this dike fails, ten thousand households will be ruined!" An earthen dike is easy to repair and easy to lose. It should be rebuilt in stone. His family was wealthy, and he paid for the work out of his own funds. For this work he was promoted to the Daming Circuit, but before taking office he was transferred to the Lintao Circuit in Shaanxi. During a famine he organized relief, riding through the mountain valleys of the prefecture. Local magnates and clerks dared not cheat. Convinced that famine relief had no lasting answer, Chengrui cut a canal at Shifo Bay west of Lanzhou, taught the people to build water wheels, and irrigated the fields. Grain yields rose by more than a hundred thousand piculs a year, and the people built a shrine to him. He was transferred to the Jinhua-Quzhou-Yanzhou Circuit in Zhejiang, served as acting Grain Storage Commissioner, and swept away every abuse in the grain transport levy. During a drought he braved the heat to inspect stricken areas, fell ill, and asked to retire but was refused. In the fifty-ninth year he died in office.
17
張孟球，字夔石，江南長洲人。 康熙二十四年進士，授山東昌樂知縣。 入為工部主事。 累遷禮部郎中。 出督雲南學政，父​​憂去，服闋，補福建糧驛道。 駐防軍食取給於漕。 上游四郡阻灘險。 故事，徵解折色，官為採置，輒抑勒病商。 孟球於延、建產米地平價購米，僦民船運省城，不假吏胥，諸弊盡絕。 地多山嶺，官吏濫用驛夫，孟球禁革私冒。 遇大徭，預期發僱值，終其任無擾驛者。
Zhang Mengqiu, whose courtesy name was Kuishi, came from Changzhou in Jiangnan. He passed the jinshi examination in the twenty-fourth year of Kangxi and was appointed magistrate of Changle in Shandong. He was called to the capital as a principal clerk in the Ministry of Works. He rose through several posts to director in the Ministry of Rites. He served as educational commissioner of Yunnan, left to mourn his father, and after mourning was appointed to the Fujian Grain and Courier Circuit. The garrison's rations came from the grain transport system. The four upstream prefectures were blocked by shoals and dangerous currents. By precedent the levy was collected in commuted silver and officials made the purchases, invariably squeezing merchants. Mengqiu bought rice at fair prices in Yanping and Jianning, hired civilian boats to carry it to the provincial capital without using clerks, and ended every abuse. The region was mountainous, and officials abused courier labor. Mengqiu banned private imposts on the courier system. When major corvée duties fell due, he paid hire wages in advance, and throughout his term the courier system was left undisturbed.
18
調河南糧儲道。 河南漕糧，就衛輝水次收兌。 舊無倉廒，又無額役，運船調之他省。 天寒水涸，糧不時至，宿河干以待，遇雨雪則米濕霉變，又患盜竊。 孟球始以羨餘建倉。 署布政使。
He was transferred to the Henan Grain Storage Circuit. Henan's transport grain was received and exchanged at the Weihui landing. There had been no granaries and no allotted labor; transport boats were reassigned to other provinces. In cold weather when the rivers ran low, grain shipments were delayed. Boats waited on the dry riverbank, grain spoiled in rain and snow, and theft was constant. Mengqiu used surplus funds to build granaries for the first time. He served as acting Provincial Treasurer.
19
西藏用兵，調河南馬騾萬，凡騾馬三需一夫，剋期兩月。 孟球止宿郊外，躬自檢閱，西路近陝諸郡遣吏往督之，盡除需索留難諸弊。 凡五十四日，馬驢如數遣赴軍，而民不擾。 擢按察使。 蘭陽民硃復業附白蓮教，自稱明裔，煽惑數縣。 孟球檄杞縣知縣寧君佐馳往捕治，盡獲其黨。 上命尚書張廷樞往按，從孟球議，誅其與逆謀者，愚民被誘悉釋之。 淅川營兵博，知縣崔錫執而罪之，兵譁，執南陽知府沈淵，眾辱之，總兵高成不能治。 時巡撫張聖佐坐譴，孟球護巡撫，曰：「南陽地連襄、鄖，急則鋌而走險，事未可知。 」密令附近諸縣嚴守禦，諭：「止誅首惡，自首免罪。 」得倡亂者七人誅之，不數日而事定。
When war broke out in Tibet, Henan was ordered to supply ten thousand horses and mules, with one handler for every three animals, all within two months. Mengqiu camped in the suburbs and inspected the animals himself. He sent officials to supervise the western route and the Shaanxi border counties and eliminated every abuse of extortion and delay. Within fifty-four days the full quota reached the army, and the people were left unharmed. He was promoted to Surveillance Commissioner. Zhu Fuye of Lanyang joined the White Lotus sect, claimed descent from the Ming house, and stirred up several counties. Mengqiu ordered Qi County magistrate Ning Junzuo to ride out at once and arrest the conspirators. The whole band was taken. The emperor sent Minister Zhang Tingshu to investigate. Following Mengqiu's advice, those who had joined the plot were executed, and the common people who had been misled were all released. A soldier of the Xichuan garrison gambled. Magistrate Cui Xi arrested and punished him, and the troops mutinied. They seized Nanyang Prefect Shen Yuan and publicly humiliated him. Regional Commander Gao Cheng could not restore order. Governor Zhang Shengzuo was then under censure. Mengqiu shielded him and warned, "Nanyang borders Xiangyang and Yun. Push too hard and desperate men may turn to violence. The outcome is uncertain." He secretly ordered the neighboring counties to tighten their defenses and proclaimed, "Execute only the ringleaders. Those who surrender will be spared." Seven ringleaders were captured and executed, and within days the disturbance was over.
20
康熙末，乞歸，不復出。 乾隆初，卒，年八十。
Near the end of the Kangxi reign he asked to retire and never took office again. Early in the Qianlong reign he died at the age of eighty.
21
論曰：官監司卓卓有名氏，即平進至督撫，易耳。 如紫綬等皆早退，遂以監司終。 紫綬崎嶇兵間，捍偏隅為民保障； 州佐、士弘勤勤重民事； 弘安贊治河； 繻善斷獄； 孟球能應變：使得為督撫，其績效當有大於是者。 時方承平，仕得行其意，知止知足，必有說以自處矣。
The commentators say: Circuit officials of outstanding reputation could easily have risen by routine promotion to governor or governor-general. Men such as Zishou retired early and ended their careers at the circuit level. Zishou held a desperate frontier through years of war and shielded the people in a remote corner; Zhouzuo and Shihong devoted themselves tirelessly to the people's welfare; Hong'an was known for his work on river control; Xu was known for his skill in judging cases; Mengqiu could meet emergencies: had he reached the rank of governor or governor-general, his achievements would surely have been greater still. It was a time of peace; they could govern as they wished, knew when to stop and when they had enough, and no doubt had their own reasons for stepping back.
