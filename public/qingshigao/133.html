Local Militia
4
鄉兵始自雍、乾，旋募旋散，初非經制之師。 嘉慶間，平川、楚教匪，鄉兵之功始著。 道光之季，粵西寇起，各省舉辦團練，有駐守地方者，有隨營征剿者。 侍郎曾國籓以衡、湘團練討寇，練鄉兵為勇營，以兵制部勒之，卒平巨憝，其始皆鄉兵也。 而邊徼之地，剿有鄉兵。 其在東三省者，則寧古塔以東之赫哲部、克雅克部，混同江東北之鄂倫春部，不設佐領，惟設鄉兵姓長。 其在黑龍江者，有打牲人，在江以南之錫伯、卦勒察，江以北之索倫、達瑚爾，則附屬於滿營。 在蒙古者，蒙兵而外，有奇古民勇。 在山、陝邊外者，有番兵，有僧俗兵。 在四川、雲南、貴州邊境者，有夷兵，有土司兵，有黑裸勇丁。 在西藏者，有藏番兵。 皆與內地鄉兵不同，故不詳。 其各直省之鄉兵，曰屯練，曰民壯，曰鄉團，曰獵戶，曰漁團，曰沙民。 額數之多寡不齊，器械之良窳不一，餉章之增減不定，良以聚散無恆，故與額兵迥異，無編製之可紀。 茲特志其始末於後焉。
Village militia first appeared under the Yongzheng and Qianlong emperors, raised and released in cycles rather than as part of the standing army. It was during the Jiaqing era, in the campaigns against the Sichuan and Hubei sectarian rebels, that local militia first won clear renown. Late in the Daoguang reign, rebellion broke out in western Guangdong, and provinces across the empire set up tuanlian militia—some to hold their own districts, others to march with regular columns on punitive campaigns. Vice Minister Zeng Guofan mobilized the Heng and Xiang militia against the rebels, reshaped village troops into yongying brave battalions under regular military discipline, and finally crushed the main rebel force—every man of whom had started as a local militiaman. On the frontiers, too, local militia took part in pacification. In Manchuria, east of Ningguta lay the Hezhe and Keyak peoples, and northeast of the Sungari the Oroqen; these groups had no banner assistant commandants, only surname chiefs heading their village militia. In Heilongjiang were the fur-hunting peoples; the Xibe and Geleqi south of the river and the Solon and Dahur north of it were attached to Manchu garrisons. In Mongolia, beyond the regular Mongol forces, there were Qigu civilian braves. Beyond the Shanxi and Shaanxi frontier were frontier tribal levies and mixed monk and lay militia. Along the Sichuan, Yunnan, and Guizhou marches were Yi levies, tusi native-commander forces, and Heiluo brave companies. In Tibet there were Tibetan border levies. These all differed from interior local militia and are therefore not treated here in detail. In the provinces proper, local militia went under many names—garrison militia, civil braves, village corps, hunter bands, fishing militia, and sand-flat levies. Headcounts varied widely, arms ranged from excellent to poor, and pay tables shifted without pattern, for these forces gathered and dissolved at will; they bore no resemblance to regular quota troops and admit of no fixed table of organization. What follows is a chronological account of how this system arose and evolved.
5
雍正八年，鄂爾泰平西南夷烏蒙之亂，調官兵萬餘人，鄉兵半之，遂定東川，是為鄉兵之始。
In Yongzheng 8 (1730), Ortai put down the Wumeng uprising among the southwestern Yi, bringing more than ten thousand regulars and an equal number of local militia into the field; Dongchuan was pacified, and this marks the origin of the village militia system.
6
乾隆三十八年，用兵小金川，定邊將軍溫福、定西將軍阿桂疏言，調滿洲兵道遠費重，不如多用鄉兵，人地相宜。 四川鄉兵，以金川屯練為強，自平定金川以後，設屯練鄉兵，其糧餉倍於額兵，分屯大小金川兩路，春夏訓練，秋冬蒐獵，有戰事則搜剿山路，退兵則為殿後之用。
In Qianlong 38 (1773), during the Lesser Jinchuan campaign, Generals Wen Fu and Agui argued in memorials that hauling Manchu bannermen across vast distances was ruinously expensive and that local militia, matched to people and terrain, served far better. Among Sichuan village troops, the Jinchuan garrison militia were the hard core. After the Jinchuan wars, garrison militia were posted on both the Greater and Lesser Jinchuan routes at double regular pay; they drilled in spring and summer, hunted in autumn and winter, scoured mountain trails when fighting broke out, and covered the army's retreat when columns withdrew.
7
嘉慶初，苗疆事起，傅鼐以鄉兵平苗，功冠諸將。 詔以鼐總理邊務，令各省督撫以鼐練鄉兵之法練官兵。 川、楚教匪之役，官兵征討，而鄉兵之功為多。 其勛績最著者，文臣則四川按察使劉清，武臣則四川提督桂涵，湖北提督羅思舉，各統鄉兵，分路剿寇，大小數百戰，遂奏膚功。 嘉慶十七年，以雲南邊外野夷裸匪肆擾，而緬寧、騰越各隘皆瘴癘之地，難駐官兵，乃練鄉兵一千六百人，以八百人駐守緬寧之丙野山樑等處，以八百人駐守騰越蠻章山等處，省官兵徵調之勞。 其時苗疆底定，亦增設鄉兵，凡屯丁七千人，訓練之暇，開墾屯防田數十萬頃。
Early in Jiaqing, rebellion flared across the Miao frontier; Fu Nai crushed it with local militia and outshone every field commander. The throne put Fu Nai in charge of frontier affairs and instructed every provincial governor to drill regular troops using his militia-training methods. In the Sichuan and Hubei sectarian wars, regulars bore the official campaigns, but local militia won the greater share of credit. The standouts were the civil official Liu Qing, Sichuan surveillance commissioner, and the generals Gui Han of Sichuan and Luo Siju of Hubei; each led village militia along separate routes through hundreds of clashes large and small until the rebels were annihilated. In Jiaqing 17 (1812), wild tribesmen and Heiluo raiders harried Yunnan's borders; the Mianning and Tengyue passes were too malarial for regular garrisons, so sixteen hundred local militia were raised—eight hundred for Bingye Shanliang and neighboring posts in Mianning, eight hundred for Manzhang Shan and the Tengyue sector—relieving the army of constant redeployments. Once the Miao frontier had quieted, militia were expanded further: seven thousand garrison settlers, between drills, reclaimed hundreds of thousands of mu of frontier farmland.
8
道光二年，令直隸疆臣招集團練，修築土堡，互為策應。 十五年，令各州縣額設民壯，一律充補訓練，復令各省民壯每月隨營操演，范以紀律。 是年，調大小金川鄉兵千名，給以千人之糧，隨營征戰，歸屯則仍食五百人之糧。 二十一年，令山東巡撫於蓬萊、黃縣、榮成、寧海、掖縣、膠州、即墨所屬之十三梟，編練鄉兵，互相防衛。 又令沿海疆臣仿浙江定海縣土堡之法，凡近海村落，招募鄉兵，興築土堡，以聯聲勢。 二十三年，令廣東省以團練助防海口。 旋疆吏疏言廣東民風宜於鄉團，招集已得十萬人，以昇平學社為團練總匯之區，推及韶州、廉州等處，一律舉行。 二十六年，令各州縣民壯隨營考察技藝。 是年，甘肅沿邊番賊肆擾，令疆臣召募獵戶千人，編為一軍，供遠探近防之用。 及道光季年，張國樑募廣東潮州鄉兵追逐粵寇，轉戰東下，卒以獷悍不馴，遂至潰散。
In Daoguang 2 (1822), Zhili frontier officials were told to raise tuanlian militia, build earthen forts, and coordinate mutual defense. In the fifteenth year (1835), counties were to fill their civil-brave quotas and put them through uniform training; provinces were also to drill these men monthly alongside regular camps under strict discipline. That same year, a thousand Jinchuan militia were called up at full thousand-man rations while on campaign; on return to garrison their pay reverted to a five-hundred-man scale. In the twenty-first year (1841), the Shandong governor was to drill village militia across thirteen coastal subdistricts under Penglai, Huang, Rongcheng, Ninghai, Ye, Jiaozhou, and Jimo for mutual coastal defense. Coastal governors were also told to copy Dinghai County's earthen-fort model in Zhejiang—recruiting militia and building forts in seaside hamlets to knit local defenses together. In the twenty-third year (1843), Guangdong was ordered to use tuanlian militia to help guard the Pearl River estuary. A provincial memorial soon reported that Guangdong's local culture lent itself to village corps: a hundred thousand men were already enrolled, centered on the Shengping Academy society as the militia hub, with the same program spreading to Shaozhou, Lianzhou, and beyond. In the twenty-sixth year (1846), every county's civil braves were to drill with regular camps so their skills could be inspected. That year, tribal raiders along the Gansu frontier grew bold; officials there recruited a thousand hunter households into a single corps for long-range scouting and local defense. Late in Daoguang, Zhang Guoliang raised Chaozhou militia to chase the Cantonese rebels eastward; their ferocity and indiscipline eventually brought rout and dispersal.
9
咸豐二年，令在籍侍郎曾國籓辦理湖南鄉團。 旋國籓疏言先行練勇一千人，所辦者乃官勇非團丁，是為鄉團改勇營之始。 三年，令山東登、萊、青三府舉辦聯庄團練，給以兵械。 四年，令甘肅沿邊增募獵戶三千人以防番騎。 八年，安徽巡撫翁同書疏言皖省定遠、壽州、合肥等縣舉辦團練，旬日之間，遠近響應，和州踞賊屢出焚掠，多被鄉團擊回，以其深明大義，踴躍同仇，凡董事團總人等，傳諭嘉勉。 九年，河南巡撫恆福疏言，皖寇進偪豫境，令道府大員於接近皖寇之地，舉辦鄉團，睢州等州縣興築堡寨已數十處。 旋諭河南官紳訓練鄉勇，聯村築寨，迅速舉行。
In Xianfeng 2 (1852), the retired vice minister Zeng Guofan was put in charge of Hunan's village militia. Zeng soon reported that he would begin by drilling a thousand men—but these were government braves, not village militia youths, marking the first step from tuanlian corps to formal yongying battalions. In the third year (1853), Shandong's Deng, Lai, and Qing prefectures were to organize linked-village tuanlian and were issued arms. In the fourth year (1854), Gansu's frontier added three thousand hunter levies against tribal horsemen. In the eighth year (1858), Anhui governor Weng Tongshu reported tuanlian underway in Dingyuan, Shou, Hefei, and other counties, with the whole province rallying within ten days; rebels holding Hezhou were repeatedly driven back when they raided, and the directors and corps chiefs who had shown such public spirit received imperial commendation. In the ninth year (1859), Henan governor Heng Fu warned that Anhui rebels were nearing the border and ordered circuit and prefectural officers to raise village corps along the threatened line; Suizhou and neighboring counties had already thrown up dozens of stockaded forts. An edict soon followed instructing Henan's officials and gentry to drill village braves, link hamlets into fortified clusters, and act without delay.
10
十年，諭勝保等督辦鄉團，以資統率，酌定章程，凡辦團州縣一律遵行，惟鄉團更番調營，所領糧餉，易滋流弊，毋得冒濫。 又諭：「江蘇等省在籍紳士，除已經辦理團練外，其明曉大義律身公正者，自不乏人，所有在京直隸、江蘇、安徽、浙江、河南等省之大小官員，將如何舉行鄉團，隨同官兵剿賊，及防守等一切事宜，各陳所見，各舉所知，迅即上聞。 」
In the tenth year (1860), Sheng Bao and others were told to supervise village corps province-wide under uniform regulations, with a warning that rotating militia through regular camps invited pay fraud and must not be abused. Another edict ran: "Beyond gentry already running tuanlian in Jiangsu and elsewhere, many retired men of principle remain available. Every official from Zhili, Jiangsu, Anhui, Zhejiang, Henan, and the other affected provinces now in Beijing must submit plans for village corps, joint operations with regulars, and local defense, recommend capable men, and report at once." "
11
尋侍郎沈兆霖疏陳：「自咸豐三年以後，迭奉朝旨舉行鄉團，已至再至三，各省官紳士民，未嘗不遵旨辦理，而賊勢披猖，卒無成效。 良由苟且塗飾，未經實力講求，或募勇以充數，徒取外觀，或藉端以營私，轉成欲壑，無事則恃為威脅，擾害鄉閭，有警則首先遁逃，流為盜賊。 議者幾謂鄉團之無益而有損矣。 不知名為民團，即當以民為團，而不可以募勇塞責也。 民統於紳，則紳之邪正宜慎擇也。 紳倚於官，則官之賢否宜嚴辨也。 不歸併於一路，則督察無人，必不能一律堅固。 不專力於四鄉，則城守雖嚴，已難免四面受敵。 官與紳宜兩相孚，不宜兩相阨。 兵與民宜兩相顧，不宜兩相仇。 任封疆者，當知民本吾民，用兵數少，何如用民數多。 任將帥者，當知兵本吾民，我能救民，自然民能救我。 現在賊氛猖獗，非實辦民團，更無安全之法。 」乃擬上事宜十二條：「一、民團須招本地有業之民，不可招市井無賴也。 一、宜分別地段，以近賊一、二百里為最要，距賊稍遠，中隔一、二縣者為次要，其遠在三、四百里外者，則從緩辦團也。 一、各州縣要地，宜一律辦團，無使一處疏漏，俾寇得乘隙而入也。 一、辦團宜四鄉加密，有警則互相應援，無事則嚴詰姦宄，庶城守完固也。 一、牧令宜擇賢能，與辦團之紳，不得各存意見，亦不得任用劣紳也。 一、宜簡道府大員分路辦團，俾各縣聯為一氣也。 一、民團有急，官兵速往救援，不得觀望也。 一、宜擇要設卡盤查也。 一、民團祗可就地助戰，不宜調遣，變為練勇，失其恆業也。 一、立功宜即獎勵，視官兵稍優也。 一、團費宜自捐自辦，不得藉端漁利也。 一、民團辦成，則分防之兵可省，集合成軍，攻剿更為得力也。 」
Vice Minister Shen Zhaolin soon wrote: "Since Xianfeng 3 the court has ordered village corps again and again; every province's officials, gentry, and people have obeyed, yet the rebels still rage and the policy has failed." The fault lies in cosmetic compliance: some hired mercenaries merely to pad rolls; others turned the corps into extortion rackets—bullying neighbors in peacetime, running first when fighting started, and finally becoming bandits themselves. Critics were ready to declare village corps worse than useless. They forgot that a people's corps must be drawn from the people themselves, not filled out with hired mercenaries. Because commoners follow local gentry, only upright leaders should be chosen. Because gentry depend on magistrates, only capable and honest officials will do. Without a single chain of command, no one can supervise the corps and they will never stand firm as one. Unless the countryside is fortified as well, even the stoutest walled city will be besieged from every quarter. Magistrates and gentry must work in mutual trust, not mutual obstruction. Troops and civilians must protect one another, not treat each other as enemies. Frontier governors must remember that the people are their own people: a handful of soldiers cannot match the strength of mobilizing the many. Field commanders must know their soldiers are drawn from the same people: save the populace and the populace will save you. With rebellion raging today, only a genuine people's militia can bring safety. " He proposed twelve measures: "First, recruit only local men with honest livelihoods—never street loafers." Second, prioritize by distance: the belt within one or two hundred li of rebel territory is urgent; counties one or two removes away are secondary; places three or four hundred li off may proceed more slowly. Third, every strategic point in each county must be covered so rebels cannot slip through a gap. Fourth, thicken defenses across all four rural districts: mutual aid when alarm sounds, strict watch for spies in quiet times, so the walled city stands secure. Fifth, appoint capable magistrates who work in concert with corps-leading gentry—never petty factions, never corrupt local bosses. Sixth, assign senior circuit and prefectural officers to coordinate corps by sector so counties act as one body. Seventh, when militia are hard pressed, regulars must march to their relief without delay. Eighth, establish checkpoints at strategic points for search and inspection. Ninth, militia should fight only on home ground—not be redeployed as mercenary braves and stripped of their livelihoods. Tenth, reward valor on the spot, with terms slightly better than for regular soldiers. Eleventh, corps funds must be raised and spent locally—no pretext for profiteering. Twelfth, once militia are in place, garrison troops may be reduced and concentrated for more effective offensive campaigns. "
12
同時應詔陳言者，有載垣等所議團練章程十條，賈楨等所擬辦理章程八條。 旋命順天府府丞毛昶熙為督辦河南團練大臣，南汝光道鄭元善幫辦團練事宜，按照怡親王載垣等所擬章程辦理。 命戶部右侍郎杜為督辦山東團練大臣，登萊青道貢璜、登州府知府盧朝安幫辦團練事宜，按照大學士賈楨等所擬條款，並參酌河南章程，體察情形辦理。 又以皖南地方緊要，應一律辦團，令兩江總督曾國籓察看情形，擇其諳練軍務素有人望者，酌保一人，即令督辦皖南團練事宜。
Others answering the edict included Zaiyuan's ten-article tuanlian regulations and Jia Zhen's eight-article implementation plan. Mao Changxi, assistant prefect of Shuntian, was named grand supervisor of Henan tuanlian, with circuit intendant Zheng Yuanshan as his deputy, operating under Prince Yi's regulations. Vice Minister of Revenue Du Wei supervised Shandong tuanlian, assisted by circuit commissioner Gong Huang and Dengzhou prefect Lu Chao'an, following Jia Zhen's articles while adapting Henan's model to local conditions. Southern Anhui being critical, Zeng Guofan was told to survey conditions, nominate one man of military experience and public standing, and put him in charge of tuanlian there.
13
旋曾國籓覆陳：「鄉團本是良法，然奉行不善，縣官徒藉以斂費，局紳亦從而分肥，賊至則先行潰逃，賊退則重加苛派，轉為地方之弊。 所經過各省，從未見有鄉團能專打一股、專克一城者，不過隨官兵之後，勝則貪財，敗則先奔，常藉口於工食之太少。 而辦理歧異者，每多給錢文，團丁所領之餉，與官勇例價相同，且有過之。 其取之民間，無非勸捐抽釐之類。 是於團練已失其本義，于軍餉又大有妨礙。 今奉諭舉行皖南團練，皖南嶺隘紛歧，若築碉設卡，有險可憑，徽、寧各要隘，宜擇地築碉，以資防守。 有在籍翰林院編修宋夢蘭當賊由太平縣竄擾徽州，宋夢蘭督帶練丁協力嚴守，眾論翕然。 請即以該員辦理皖南團練事宜，會同委員，董勸各屬紳民，興築碉塞。 其未經克復者，官兵攻剿，概不令團丁隨往。 其已經克復者，紳耆修碉，團丁守之。 庶幾軍民兩利，名實相符矣。 」
Zeng Guofan replied bluntly: "Village corps are sound in principle, but in practice magistrates use them only to levy money, gentry skim the proceeds, men flee before the enemy arrives, and after the enemy leaves the people are taxed again—it has become a local scourge." In every province he had marched through, no village corps had ever destroyed a rebel band or taken a city on their own; they merely trailed the regulars—plundering after victories, running first in defeat, always complaining that pay was too low. Where organizers deviated from the rules, they often paid even more: militia youths sometimes drew the same rations as government braves—or more. All of this was wrung from the people through donation drives and transit levies. Tuanlian had lost its original purpose and was draining the regular war chest as well. Ordered now to organize southern Anhui tuanlian, I note that its mountain passes are numerous; blockhouses and checkpoints at defensible points—especially the Huizhou and Ningguo corridors—would anchor local defense. Retired Hanlin compiler Song Menglan, when rebels from Taiping raided Huizhou, led trained levies in a stout defense that won universal praise. I recommend appointing him to southern Anhui tuanlian, working with commissioners to rally local gentry and build blockhouses. In unreclaimed territory, regulars alone should conduct offensives—militia must not follow them. In recovered districts, gentry should build the forts and militia should garrison them. Thus army and people may both gain, and the system may live up to its name. "
14
又以四川地屬岩疆，毗連雲、貴，滇匪滋擾，未能肅清。 嘉慶間，四川舉辦鄉團，行堅壁清野之法，著有成效，自應仿辦。 所有應行事宜，諭四川在京各員，就地方情形，各抒所見。 官紳中有練達時務者，各舉所知，以俟後命。 同時尚書陳孚恩等以江西毗連安徽、浙江、廣東等省，疏請辦理團練，酌保辦事人員，並擬團練事宜八條。 疏入，允行。 命在籍翰林院修撰劉繹為江西督辦團練大臣，吉南贛寧道沈葆楨、甘肅安肅道劉於潯幫辦團練事宜，按照陳孚恩等所擬章程，妥為辦理。
Sichuan was also noted as a rugged frontier adjoining Yunnan and Guizhou, where Yunnan rebels still harassed the borders without full pacification. Under Jiaqing, Sichuan's village corps and fortified-clear-country strategy had proved effective and should be revived. All Sichuan officials then in Beijing were told to submit recommendations suited to local conditions. Capable officials and gentry were to recommend qualified men and await further orders. Minister Chen Fu'en meanwhile noted that Jiangxi adjoined Anhui, Zhejiang, and Guangdong, requested tuanlian, nominated officers to run it, and drafted eight implementation articles. The memorial was approved. Retired Hanlin compiler Liu Yi was named Jiangxi's tuanlian supervisor, with Shen Baozhen and Liu Yuqun as deputies, implementing Chen Fu'en's regulations.
15
同時督辦河南團練大臣、順天府府丞毛昶熙疏陳團練辦法，並酌擬規條：一、添築堡寨以扼要隘，一、講求險要以便堵御，一、慎擇首事以資統率，一、分選團丁以備訓練，一、攤派練費以備公用，一、互為聲援以資聯絡，一、申明號令以壹眾志，一、嚴定約束以禁頑暴，一、秉公賞罰以示勸懲，一、嚴察姦宄以防內應，一、旌表忠義以作民氣，一、事貴實力以冀成功。 疏入，允行。
Mao Changxi, Henan's tuanlian supervisor, also submitted methods and twelve rules: build stockades at key passes; study terrain for defense; choose reliable leaders; select and train corps members; apportion drill funds fairly; coordinate mutual support; unify command signals; enforce discipline; reward and punish impartially; watch for spies; honor loyal service; and above all rely on earnest effort. This too was approved.
16
毛昶熙又疏陳河南團練，以歸、陳二府為先。 前統兵大臣勝保，因調團不齊，勒派百姓出資雇丁，統計勇糧運費，較正供多至倍蓰，百姓苦累，紛紛稟請，以抽丁一項，民力已竭，鄉團勢難再辦。 其開封等府百姓聞歸、陳雇勇之苦，亦復觀望，不肯實辦。 團練之事，仍恐有名無實。 尋奉諭：「用民之法，總宜深得民心。 勝保等所辦章程既與民心不洽，自應改弦更張，以期得力。 慶廉即體察情形，將此項雇勇酌量裁撤。 毛昶熙按照載垣等會議章程，速即集團練勇，以輔兵力。 」又以甘肅控制西陲，地方遼闊，且與陝西、四川毗連，匪患未靖，自應一律舉辦團練，以靖邊陲。 所有甘肅省團練事宜，即命陝甘總督樂斌督辦，並命甘涼道蕭浚蘭、刑部員外郎吳可讀、江西候補道楊升幫辦團練。
Mao Changxi added that Henan tuanlian should begin with the Gui and Chen prefectures. Former commander Sheng Bao, finding his corps understrength, had forced the people to hire mercenaries at their own expense; pay and transport costs ran to several times normal taxes. Petitioners protested that conscription alone had drained the countryside and village corps could no longer be raised. When the people of Kaifeng and neighboring prefectures heard how Gui and Chen had suffered under hired mercenaries, they too held back and refused to commit in earnest. The tuanlian program still risked being all form and no substance. An edict soon arrived: "Any policy that relies on the people must first win their hearts. Sheng Bao's regulations have alienated the public and must be thoroughly revised if they are to work. Qing Lian was to assess local conditions and scale back the hired mercenaries accordingly. Mao Changxi was to follow the regulations agreed by Zaiyuan and his colleagues, muster tuanlian forces at once, and support the regular army. " Gansu, moreover, held the western marches: a vast territory adjoining Shaanxi and Sichuan where unrest was not yet subdued. Tuanlian should be organized there as well to secure the frontier. Gansu tuanlian was assigned to Governor-General Yue Bin, assisted by Circuit Intendant Xiao Junlan, Ministry of Justice secretary Wu Kedu, and expectant Jiangxi intendant Yang Sheng.
17
十一年，以歸化之番眾僧俗兵四千餘人，馬四千餘匹，防禦抱罕羌人。 是年，奉諭：「鄉團之設，原以濟兵力所不逮。 必須官紳一體，兵勇同心協力，內靖土匪，外御賊氛，於地方庶有裨益。 若如清盛疏劾山東章丘縣之水賽街、新城之南婁里等庄，以及博山、萊蕪等縣鄉團，遇有經過客商，往來差役，輒敢擅行殺戮，害及無辜。 撫署之差弁馬匹，亦被劫奪。 是團練御賊尚無成效，而抗官滋事竟有滋蔓之勢。 巡撫譚廷襄速將清盛所奏各情，嚴密查訪。 如有藉團為名，肆行不法，及私立黑團，聚眾抗官，立即嚴懲。 」又諭浙江巡撫：「前以浙省軍務未平，籌辦團練，勸諭捐輸，原以保衛民生。 若如王履謙疏劾辦團情形，雜亂無章，勸捐委員，令捐戶加捐至數十倍之多，並於捐戶加以威逼。 今賊氛逼近浙東，若因勸捐辦理不善，致失人心，必致激成內訌。 巡撫王有齡速即會同王履謙妥為勸辦，議定章程，不得徇私委派貪劣之員。 」
In the eleventh year, over four thousand Guihua tribal monks and laymen, with as many horses, were deployed against the Baohan Qiang. That same year came an edict: "Village corps were created to fill gaps the regular army could not cover. Officials and gentry must act together with soldiers and braves, pacify local bandits, hold off rebel incursions, and only then will the district gain real benefit. Yet Qing Sheng reported that in Zhangqiu's Shuisai Street, Xincheng's Nanlouli, and in Boshan and Laiwu, local corps killed passing merchants and government runners on their own authority, innocent victims included. Even couriers and horses from the provincial governor's yamen had been seized. The corps had failed against bandits while defying officials and breeding unrest. Governor Tan Tingxiang was ordered to investigate every detail in Qing Sheng's report without delay. Anyone who used the corps as cover for lawlessness, ran unauthorized "black" corps, or gathered mobs to defy officials was to be punished at once. " The Zhejiang governor was told separately: "With the province still at war, tuanlian and donation drives were urged to protect the people. Wang Lvqian reported chaotic management: commissioners demanding donations ten times the assessed amount and threatening donors who resisted. With rebels nearing eastern Zhejiang, bungled fundraising would alienate the people and invite internal revolt. Governor Wang Youling was to work with Wang Lvqian, set fair regulations, and appoint no corrupt or incompetent agents. "
18
是年，左副都御史潘祖廕疏言：「各省設立團練大臣，辦理年餘，曾無一效，請獎請敘，紛紛效尤，並未克復一城，其為無益，已可概見。 應將團練大臣分別裁撤，以一事機而節糜費。 」翰林院侍講學士顏宗儀疏言：「鄉團之設，原以百姓之財力，衛百姓之身家，果能眾志成城，同仇敵愾，即一舉、貢、生、監，足以統領之，無俟大員為之督率。 若必以大僚綜任之，幫辦司員分理之，是督撫之外又設督撫，僚屬之外又增僚屬，徒滋紛擾。 上年豫省辦團，各省團練大臣亦紛紛四齣。 旋因浙江、四川、陝西、甘肅等省情形不同，旋即裁撤。 而直隸、山東、江南、江北等處，則仍歸由團練大臣辦理。 於是幫辦人員假公濟私，百端紛擾。 或偪勒州縣供應，或苛派民間銀錢，或於官設捐局之外，團練再設捐局，或於官抽釐金之外，團練再抽釐金，或查閱各處團防，支應紛煩，地方告乏，或任令家人奴僕勒索規費，約束不嚴。 幫辦人員或十餘人，或數十人，薪水所出，皆刻剝民間。 刁生劣監，因以把持地方； 狡吏貪夫，藉以希圖名利，流弊實多。 各省團練大臣，直隸桑春榮操守尚嚴，山東杜已嘖有煩言，至於江北、江南所辦鄉團，自上年至今，未聞有團練大臣收復一州一縣者，徒以騷動天下，無益有損。 今山東杜已經撤回，河南毛昶熙較有成效，其直隸、江南、江北等處團練大臣，宜一併撤回。 其各省州縣距賊較遠者，停止辦團，以安民業。 其距賊較近之處，仍責地方官切實辦團，而以本省督撫總其成，庶事權不至紛歧，商民可免滋擾。 」
That year Left Vice Censor Pan Zuyin wrote: "Provincial tuanlian grand ministers have worked for over a year without a single success, yet officials clamor for rewards and promotions though not one city has been recovered—their futility is obvious. Those grand ministers should be abolished province by province to unify command and cut waste. " Expositor Yan Zongyi argued: "Village corps exist so the people may defend themselves with their own means. Where communities unite in common purpose, a licentiate or scholar is leadership enough—no senior official need supervise them. If mandarins must supervise while staff deputies divide the work, one creates governors atop governors and clerks atop clerks—nothing but confusion. When Henan organized corps the year before, tuanlian grand ministers fanned out across every province. Zhejiang, Sichuan, Shaanxi, and Gansu soon abolished the posts because local conditions differed. Zhili, Shandong, Jiangnan, and Jiangbei, however, still left affairs to the grand ministers. Their deputies then used public office for private gain in countless ways. Some forced prefectures and counties to feed them; some extorted silver from the people; some ran donation offices alongside official ones; some levied likin on top of government collections; some touring "inspections" exhausted districts with demands for hospitality; some let servants extort fees with no discipline. A single post might employ ten or thirty deputies, every salary gouged from the people. Shady scholars and degree-holders thus dominated local affairs; Cunning clerks and greedy men pursued profit and reputation by the same means, with abuses too many to list. Among the grand ministers, Zhili's Sang Chunrong remained relatively scrupulous, Shandong's Du Yi was already notorious, and in Jiangbei and Jiangnan not one prefecture or county had been recovered—only nationwide turmoil, harm without benefit. Du Yi had already been recalled from Shandong and Mao Changxi had done better in Henan; the Zhili, Jiangnan, and Jiangbei grand ministers should all be withdrawn. Counties far from the fighting should stop organizing corps and let people return to their livelihoods. Near the front, local officials should still run corps under provincial governors so authority stays unified and merchants are spared further harassment. "

旋奉諭：「直隸團練大臣桑春榮回京供職，直隸團練事宜，責成文煜辦理。 江西團練大臣劉繹來京任用，江西團練事宜，責成毓科督同官紳辦理。 其二省京官如有回籍辦團者，各部院查取職名，飭令來京供職。 江北團練大臣晏端書，江西團練大臣龐鍾璐，其辦理團練，是否仍須該員經理，抑或即可裁撤，令曾國籓、薛煥速議以聞。 王履謙幫辦浙江團練，兼辦浙東捐務，今浙江軍務方殷，自難遽撤。 令王有齡會同王履謙切實籌辦，以固疆圉。 毛昶熙在河南歸德著有成效，應否仍令毛昶熙督辦團練，及有無把握之處，令嚴樹森速議以聞。 」

Jiangxi's grand minister Liu Yi was summoned to the capital; Yu Ke would supervise tuanlian there with local officials and gentry. 旋兩江總督曾國籓覆陳：「團練之設，只能防小支千餘之游匪，不能剿大股數萬之悍賊。 其練丁口糧，若太多，則與募勇之價相等，不必僅以團名； 若太少，則與官勇之餉迥殊，不能得其死力。 其團防經費，若取諸丁、漕、釐、捐四者之中，則有礙督撫籌款之途； 若設法四者之外，則更無措手之處。 事權既無專屬，剛柔實覺兩難。 晏端書在江北不設餉局，但勸各邑築圩自保，龐鍾璐在江南激勸鄉民，俾知同仇敵愾之義，辦理極有斟酌。 今之賊勢，決非鄉團所能奏功。 應俟賊氛稍衰，大功將成，然後辦團練以善其後。 晏端書、龐鍾璐二員，清操雅望，內任最宜。 應請裁去團練差使，回京供職。 」疏入，允之。
Governor-General Zeng Guofan of Liangjiang soon replied: "Tuanlian can hold off roaming bands of a thousand men at most; they cannot destroy rebel columns tens of thousands strong. If drill stipends are too high, they match mercenary pay and the corps label adds nothing; if too low, they fall far below regular army pay and men will not fight to the death. If corps defense funds come from poll tax, grain transport, likin, or donations, they compete with provincial revenue; If funding is sought outside those four channels, there is nowhere else to turn. With no single authority in charge, it is genuinely hard to strike the right balance between firmness and leniency. In Jiangbei, Yan Duanshu declined to establish supply bureaus and instead urged each county to build fortified embankments for self-defense; in Jiangnan, Pang Zhonglu roused the villagers to a common hatred of the enemy. Both managed affairs with notable judgment. The rebels' power today is far beyond what village militia alone can overcome. Militia training should wait until the insurgency weakens and the main campaign nears completion, then be organized to secure the aftermath. Yan Duanshu and Pang Zhonglu are men of integrity and high standing, best suited for appointments at court. Their special militia assignments should be abolished and they recalled to Beijing to resume regular duties. " The memorial was submitted and approved.

同治元年，諭：「鄉團之設，原以使民自衛身家，藉可保全地方，以輔官兵。 前因各路辦理團練大臣隨帶多員，任意騷擾，有害無利，是以陸續裁撤，仍責令地方官切實經理。 乃邇來統兵大員，守土牧令，或恐其分餉而輕為裁撤，或疑其無益而視為具文，於是民心不固，盜賊橫行，所過州縣村莊，動遭劫掠，是又地方官不能因地制宜舉行團練之所致，因噎廢食，貽誤殊多。 嗣後各省團練，仍由督撫臣通飭各州縣，選公正紳士，實力興辦。 務使官不掣肘，民悉同心，城市鄉村，聲勢聯絡。 其有認真辦理保全地方者，將其實在勞績，聲明保獎。 」
In the first year of Tongzhi (1862), an edict declared: "Village militia were originally meant to let people defend their lives and property, preserve the locality, and support the regular army. Earlier, commissioners for militia training across the provinces had brought large staffs who harassed the populace at will, doing more harm than good; they were therefore phased out, and local officials were again ordered to manage militia affairs conscientiously. Recently, however, supreme commanders and local magistrates had either abolished militia rashly for fear they would divert military pay, or dismissed them as mere paperwork as useless—and popular morale collapsed, bandits ran wild, and county after county fell to looting. This was itself the fault of local officials who failed to adapt militia organization to local conditions: throwing out the baby with the bathwater, at grievous cost. Henceforth each province's militia shall again be directed by the governor-general or governor, who shall instruct every prefecture and county to choose upright gentry and establish militia in earnest. Officials must not hobble the effort; the people must stand united; cities and countryside must be linked in mutual support. Those who earnestly organize militia and secure their districts shall have their real achievements reported for official reward. "

二年，以都察院代遞山東貢生硃德秀條陳團練事宜，語多可采，命硃德秀回籍，隨同英桂、趙德轍辦理團練，並命英桂督飭官紳，就地方情形，認真辦團，毋得有名無實。
In the second year (1863), the Censorate transmitted a memorial from Zhu Dexiu, a Shandong tribute student, on militia organization; many of his points were sound. Zhu was ordered home to assist Ying Gui and Zhao Dezhe in organizing militia, and Ying Gui was charged to oversee officials and gentry in earnest, locally appropriate militia work—not empty forms.

是年，統兵大臣僧格林沁疏言：「各省練團築寨，本以助守望而禦寇盜，為權宜補救之法。 乃各團每以有寨可據，輒藐視官長，擅理詞訟，或聚眾抗糧，或挾仇械鬥，甚至謀為不軌，踞城戕官，如山東之劉德培，河南之李瞻，先後倡亂，而安徽之苗沛霖，尤為梟桀反覆，勞師糜餉，始得次第翦除。 辦團之舉，始則合一鄉為一團，繼則聯眾團為一練，地廣人多，良莠不齊，不肖團長有跋扈情形，承辦團練紳士又不能杜漸防微，隨時舉發，致有尾大不掉之勢。 況捻匪屢經竄擾之區，亦未見各團堵御得力。 其河南團練，均由侍郎毛昶熙管理。 毛昶熙於通省地方，勢難周曆兼顧，而各練既有專管大員，地方官轉至呼應不靈。 今賊氛漸平，請命毛昶熙回京供職，所有團練，視直隸、山東之例，歸地方官經理，以一事權。 並請飭河南巡撫嚴查各團，如有增置軍械等事，均責令稟請地方官允准置備。 如不肖團長借修圍制械，種種斂錢，以致苦累鄉民，即從嚴懲辦，庶幾權歸於上，免滋流弊。 」御史裘德俊疏言：「團練之舉，本屬有治人無治法。 今直隸善後章程，有抽撥鄉團訓練之議。 但抽撥鄉兵，必得賢明牧令，駕馭有方，乃能權不下移，民無擾累。 若遇不肖州縣，借端苛斂，抽丁派費，吏胥因緣為奸，上下咸思中飽，小民已不聊生； 加以每縣聚眾數百人，游手無著，以強凌弱，甚或恃眾把持，一有亂萌，尤易響應，不可不遠慮及之。 」
That year Commander Sengge Rinchen memorialized: "Fortified militia camps in the provinces were originally a stopgap to aid watch and defense against bandits, yet militia with walled camps often defied magistrates, adjudicated disputes on their own, resisted tax collection by force, or fought feuds with arms—some even plotted rebellion and seized cities, killing officials. Liu Depei of Shandong and Li Zhan of Henan had led such uprisings in turn; Miao Peilin of Anhui was the worst—a ruthless turncoat eliminated only after vast expenditure of troops and supplies. Organizing militia began with one corps per township, then linked corps into larger drill units across wide territories and large populations, mixing good men with bad. Unworthy corps leaders grew overbearing; responsible gentry failed to check abuses early and report them, until the movement grew too powerful to control. Even in regions repeatedly raided by Nian bandits, the corps had rarely mounted an effective defense. Henan militia were entirely overseen by Vice Minister Mao Changxi. Mao Changxi could hardly tour the entire province, and with dedicated overseers for each drill unit, local officials lost the ability to coordinate effectively. Now that the rebellion is subsiding, Mao Changxi should be recalled to Beijing; all militia should follow the Zhili and Shandong precedent and be placed under local officials, unifying authority. The Henan governor should also be ordered to inspect every corps strictly: any addition of arms must be reported to and approved by the local magistrate before procurement. Unworthy corps leaders who levy money under pretext of fortifications and armaments, oppressing the villages, must be punished severely so authority rests with the state and abuses do not spread. " Censor Qiu Dejun memorialized: "Militia training is fundamentally a matter of having the right men to govern, not laws to govern. The Zhili postwar regulations now propose drafting village militia for training. But drafting township militia requires wise magistrates skilled in control, so power does not devolve downward and the people are not harassed. Under corrupt prefectural and county officials, pretexts become harsh levies, conscription brings fees, clerks profit from the chaos, and everyone from top to bottom grabs a cut—the common people can barely survive; and each county assembles hundreds of idle men who bully the weak, some dominating by sheer numbers—at the first sign of unrest they will readily join in. This must be foreseen. "
24
旋奉諭：「山東鄉團已由官為經理，所有河南省團練事宜，亦統歸官辦，以一事權。 其直隸抽練團丁，督臣劉長佑權其利害，是否可行，如有窒礙之處，即據實以聞。 」
An edict soon followed: "Shandong village militia are already under official management; all Henan militia affairs shall likewise be handled by officials, unifying authority. As for Zhili's plan to draft militia trainees for drill, Governor Liu Changyou shall weigh the pros and cons and report frankly whether it is feasible or encounters obstacles. "
25
六年，李雲麟招募奇古民勇駐八里岡，與科布多、塔爾巴哈台蒙兵為犄角。
In the sixth year (1867), Li Yunlin recruited Qigu civilian braves and garrisoned them at Baligang in mutual support with Mongol troops from Kobdo and Tarbagatai.
26
七年，諭各疆臣：「捻寇蕩平，勇丁亦各還鄉里，誠恐江南、安徽、河南、山東從前被兵處所，不免伏莽潛匿，乘隙為害。 江、皖等省督撫，於徐、海、潁、亳、歸、汝、曹、沂等處，飭各地方官勸諭民間照舊修理圩寨，整頓鄉團，互相保衛。 此外各處民團，亦應一律整飭，慎選牧令，安良除暴，以靖地方。 」
In the seventh year (1868), frontier officials were instructed: "The Nian rebels have been suppressed and the braves have returned home, yet we fear that in war-scarred Jiangnan, Anhui, Henan, and Shandong, hidden malcontents may still lurk and strike when opportunity allows. Governors of Jiangsu, Anhui, and related provinces shall order local officials in the Xu, Hai, Ying, Bo, Gui, Ru, Cao, and Yi regions to urge the people to repair fortified embankments as before, reorganize village militia, and provide mutual defense. Civilian militia elsewhere should likewise be reorganized, magistrates chosen with care, the law-abiding protected and the violent suppressed, to secure the peace. "
27
十二年，因四川峨邊廳蠻族投誠，擇充千百戶等職，編製夷兵，建修碉堡。
In the twelfth year (1873), after the tribal peoples of Ebian Subprefecture in Sichuan submitted, selected leaders were appointed chiliarchs and centurions, Yi troops were organized, and blockhouses built and repaired.
28
光緒六年，兩廣總督張之洞募沙民千人助守虎門，楊玉科增募千人及惠清營五百人，鄭紹忠募安勇二千人，所募鄉兵，以防勇規制編之。 是年，命黑龍江將軍於增練馬隊外，秋冬之季，招集打牲人等，加以訓練。
In the sixth year of Guangxu (1880), Governor-General Zhang Zhidong of the Two Guangs recruited a thousand sand-dwellers to help garrison Humen; Yang Yuke added a thousand men plus five hundred for the Huiqing battalion; Zheng Shaozhong raised two thousand An braves—all enrolled as township militia under the defense-brave regulations. That year the Heilongjiang general was also ordered that, beyond expanding the cavalry companies, he should recruit hunting peoples in autumn and winter and put them through training.
29
八年，兩江總督左宗棠以江蘇沿江海州縣捕魚為業者甚多，於內江外海風濤沙線無不熟諳，而崇明尤為各海口漁戶爭趨之所。 其中有技勇而悉洋務者，所在不乏。 外洋船駛入內江者，每用漁戶為導。 江蘇自川沙迄贛榆二十二州縣，濱臨江海，漁戶約數萬人。 乃令蘇松太道員為沿海漁團督辦，於漁戶每百人中，選壯健三十人，練漁團五千名，設總局於吳淞口，設分局於濱海各縣，每月操練二次，習水勇技藝，用以捕盜緝私，兼備水師之選。
In the eighth year (1882), Governor-General Zuo Zongtang of the Two Jiangs observed that Jiangsu's coastal counties had vast numbers of fishermen thoroughly acquainted with inner rivers, open seas, winds, and shoals—and Chongming was the port where fishing fleets from every harbor converged. Many among them were skilled fighters well versed in maritime affairs. Foreign vessels entering inland waters routinely relied on fishermen as pilots. From Chuansha to Ganyu, twenty-two Jiangsu prefectures and counties front the coast; fishermen numbered in the tens of thousands. The Sutai circuit intendant was appointed to supervise coastal fishing militia: thirty sturdy men chosen from every hundred fishermen, five thousand trained in all, with a headquarters at Wusong and branch offices in coastal counties. They drilled twice monthly in naval skills to hunt pirates and suppress smuggling, and to serve as a pool for the fleet.
30
十一年，雲貴總督岑毓英釐定雲南通省營制，裸黑勇丁，編為六營，西南土防，編為二十五營。 又因雲南沿邊，由西而東南，皆野人山寨，布列於九隘之外，乃調兵二千人，與原有防軍及鄉團土司，協力警備。 督辦廣東軍務大臣彭玉麟以欽州、廉州地廣兵單，招募鄉團協守。 是年，吉林將軍增練防軍，佐以烏拉牲丁，凡萬五千人。
In the eleventh year (1885), Governor-General Cen Yuying of Yunnan and Guizhou reorganized Yunnan's provincial forces: Luohei braves into six battalions and southwestern frontier defense troops into twenty-five. Because Yunnan's frontier from west to southeast was lined with tribal stockades beyond the nine passes, two thousand troops were deployed to work with existing garrisons, village militia, and native chiefs in joint patrol. Grand Minister Peng Yulin, supervising Guangdong military affairs, recruited village militia to help garrison Qinzhou and Lianzhou, where territory was vast but regular troops few. That year the Jilin general expanded frontier defense forces, supplemented by fifteen thousand Wula hunting levies.
31
二十四年，都察院代陳湖南舉人何鎮圭條陳團練事宜，命兵部議奏。 又諭：「侍郎張廕桓疏請實行團練，同時臣工屢有仿西法練民兵之請。 若各省實行團練，即以鄉團為民兵，用更番替換之法，較諸遽練民兵為有把握。 廣西會匪滋事，尤宜速辦，以收捍禦之功。 各省督撫一律切實籌辦。 各省於三月內，廣東、廣西於一月內，將辦理情形，具疏以聞。 」
In the twenty-fourth year (1898), the Censorate forwarded Hunan graduate He Zhengui's proposals on tuanlian and referred them to the Ministry of War for deliberation. An edict added: "Vice Minister Zhang Yinhuan urged genuine tuanlian, while other officials repeatedly asked to drill a people's militia on Western lines." If provinces really organized tuanlian and treated village corps as militia on a rotating roster, that would be surer than rushing into a new militia system. Guangxi secret-society outbreaks made speed especially urgent if the policy was to defend local communities. Every provincial governor was to organize the program in earnest. Provinces were to report progress within three months; Guangdong and Guangxi within one. "
32
三十年，廣西巡撫柯逢時令廣西各州縣增募鄉勇八千人，給以毛瑟後膛槍，並令民間多築碉堡，共御外侮。
In the thirtieth year (1904), Guangxi governor Ke Fengshi raised eight thousand village braves across the province, armed them with Mauser rifles, and urged widespread blockhouse building against foreign encroachment.
33
三十一年，兩廣總督李經羲增練防營，並募土著鄉兵，備廣西邊境。 新疆巡撫潘效蘇以新疆兵費太重，改募土著，仿勇營訓練，次第遣散客軍。
In the thirty-first year (1905), Li Jingyi of the Two Guangs expanded defense battalions and recruited native village militia for the Guangxi frontier. Xinjiang governor Pan Xiaosu, finding military costs unbearable, replaced outsider troops with locally recruited men trained like yongying and discharged the guest armies in stages.
34
三十四年，雲南防軍裁併，於騰越、臨安兩路創設團練，藉資捍衛。
In the thirty-fourth year (1908), Yunnan trimmed its defense army and created tuanlian on the Tengyue and Lin'an routes for local defense.
35
宣統元年，各省改防營為巡防隊。 雲貴總督沈秉堃以雲南防軍內有各屬之保衛團，系昔日之鄉團，名為營隊，實即鄉兵，未能遽改為巡防隊，乃仍其舊。 此鄉兵舉廢之概略也。
In Xuantong 1 (1909), provinces renamed defense battalions as patrol companies. Governor-general Shen Bingkun reported that Yunnan's defense force still included old village guard units—battalions in name, militia in fact—and these could not be abruptly converted to patrol companies, so the former system remained. Such, in outline, is the story of how local militia rose and fell.
