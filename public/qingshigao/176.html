軍機處名不師古，而絲綸出納，職居密勿。 初祗秉廟謨商戎略而已，厥後軍國大計，罔不總攬。 自雍、乾後百八十年，威命所寄，不於內閣而於軍機處，盖隱然执政之府矣。 今詳著其人，庶後之考心腹股肱之佐，而究其時政化隆污消長之跡者，以覽觀焉。 作軍機大臣表。
The Grand Council bore a name with no ancient precedent, yet imperial edicts passed through it, and its role was confidential counsel close to the throne. At first it merely carried out the ruler's strategic decisions on military affairs; later it took charge of every major civil and military policy. For a hundred and eighty years after the Yongzheng and Qianlong eras, real authority lay not with the Grand Secretariat but with the Grand Council, which effectively became the hidden seat of power. This table records them in full, so that those who later study the emperor's trusted aides and trace how government flourished or declined may consult it. Tabular Biography of Grand Councilors.
