軍機處名不師古，而絲綸出納，職居密勿。 初祗秉廟謨商戎略而已，厥後軍國大計，罔不總攬。 自雍、乾後百八十年，威命所寄，不於內閣而於軍機處，盖隱然执政之府矣。 今詳著其人，庶後之考心腹股肱之佐，而究其時政化隆污消長之跡者，以覽觀焉。 作軍機大臣表。
The Grand Council had no ancient precedent for its name, yet it controlled the flow of imperial edicts and its work was among the most secret in the realm. At first it merely executed the emperor's designs and deliberated on military strategy; later it took charge of every major affair of state and war. For the hundred and eighty years after Yongzheng and Qianlong, real authority rested not with the Grand Secretariat but with the Grand Council — in effect, the hidden seat of government. Their names are now recorded in full, so that later readers who study the emperor's closest advisers and trace how the times rose, fell, and changed may consult this table.
