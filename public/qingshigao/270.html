郝浴子林楊素蘊郭琇
Hao Yu, his son Lin, Yang Suyun, and Guo Xiu
3
郝浴，字雪海，直隸定州人。 少有志操，負氣節。 順治六年進士，授刑部主事。 八年，改湖廣道御史，巡按四川。 時張獻忠將孫可望、李定國等降明，為桂王將，據川南為寇，師討之，郡縣吏率軍前除授，恣為貪虐。 浴至，嚴約束，廉民間疾苦，將吏始斂跡。 九年，平西王吳三桂與固山額真李國翰分兵復成都、嘉定、敘州、重慶。 已而兩路兵俱敗，三桂退駐綿州。 浴在保寧監臨鄉試，可望將數万人薄城，浴飛檄邀三桂，激以大義，謂「不死於賊，必死於法」。 踰月，三桂乃赴援，可望等引去。
Hao Yu, whose style was Xuehai, came from Dingzhou in Zhili. From youth he showed strong purpose and held fast to his principles. He became a jinshi in the sixth year of Shunzhi and was made a principal clerk in the Ministry of Punishments. In the eighth year he was reassigned as censor of the Huguang circuit and sent on an inspection tour of Sichuan. Sun Kewang, Li Dingguo, and other commanders once under Zhang Xianzhong had gone over to the Ming and now served the Prince of Gui, raiding from bases in southern Sichuan. As imperial forces moved against them, officials in the prefectures and counties were often appointed in advance of the army and abused their power with greed and cruelty. When Yu took up his post he enforced strict discipline, looked into the people's hardships, and the military and civil officers finally checked their excesses. In the ninth year the Prince of Pingxi, Wu Sangui, and the banner general Li Guohan led separate columns to retake Chengdu, Jiading, Xuzhou, and Chongqing. Soon both columns were beaten, and Sangui fell back to hold Mianzhou. While Yu was at Baoning overseeing the provincial examinations, Kewang advanced with tens of thousands and pressed the city. Yu sent an urgent message summoning Sangui, appealing to duty: "If you do not die fighting the rebels, you will die under the law." More than a month later Sangui finally marched to relieve the siege, and Kewang and his forces withdrew.
4
浴在圍城中，上詔詢收川方略，疏言：「秦兵苦轉餉，川兵苦待哺，故必秦不助川而後秦可保； 川不冀秦助而後川可圖。 成都地大且要，灌口一水，襟帶三十州縣。 若移兵成都，照籍屯田，開耕一年，可當秦運三年。 所難者牛種，倘令土司出牛，撫臣與立券，豐年還其值，當無不聽命。 嘉定據上游，饒茶、鹽，令暫易穀種，則牛、種俱不難辦也。 臣故謂開屯便。 川所患者滇寇也，滇寇所恃，不過皮兜、布鎧、鳥銃、濩刀，善於騰山踰嶺。 蜀中土官土兵，其技尤嫻於此。 若拔其精銳為前茅，以滿洲驍騎為後勁，疾雷迅霆，賊必鳥獸散。 臣故謂用土兵便。 」上以其言可採，下部議。 部議謂戰守事當聽三桂主之，遂報寢。 浴又言：「土賊投誠，給劄授官，恣行劫掠為民害。 請嗣後原歸伍者歸伍，原為民者，令有司造冊編丁，免牛租，除雜派，就熟地開徵，俾有定額。 」疏議行。
Still inside the besieged city, Yu received an imperial edict asking how Sichuan might be recovered. He memorialized: "The forces from Shaanxi are worn down hauling supplies, while the Sichuan troops are worn down waiting to be fed. Shaanxi must therefore stop subsidizing Sichuan before Shaanxi itself can be kept secure; and Sichuan must stop counting on Shaanxi's help before Sichuan can truly be brought under control." Chengdu is broad, fertile, and strategically vital; the irrigation channel at Guankou alone ties together thirty prefectures and counties. If troops were stationed at Chengdu and organized for garrison farming by household register, one year's cultivation could replace three years of grain shipped from Shaanxi. The hard part is oxen and seed stock. If the native chiefs were asked to supply oxen and the provincial commissioner issued bonds to be repaid in good harvest years, they would surely comply. Jiading sits upstream and is rich in tea and salt; if grain were temporarily traded there for seed, both oxen and seed could be obtained without great difficulty. I therefore urge that opening garrison farms is the better course. Sichuan's affliction is the Yunnan raiders, who rely on little more than leather shields, cloth armor, muskets, and broadswords, and on their skill at climbing mountains and crossing ridges. The native chiefs and native troops of Shu are even more practiced in such fighting. If their best fighters were taken as vanguard and Manchu cavalry as rear guard, striking like thunder, the rebels would scatter like startled birds and beasts. I therefore urge that using native troops is the better course. " The emperor found his advice worth adopting and referred it to the ministries for deliberation. The ministries ruled that campaigning and defense should be left to Sangui, and the proposal was shelved. Yu also wrote: "When native bandits surrender they are given warrants and offices, yet they still raid at will and prey on the people. Hereafter let those who were soldiers return to the ranks; for those who were civilians, let local officials register households, exempt ox rent, abolish miscellaneous levies, and levy taxes on cultivated land at fixed rates." " That memorial was approved and carried out.
5
三桂入四川，浸驕橫，部下多不法，憚浴嚴正，輒禁止沿路塘報。 浴上言：「臣忝司朝廷耳目，而壅閼若此，安用臣為？ 」及保寧圍解，頒賞將士，三桂以冠服與浴，浴不受。 疏言：「平賊乃平西王責。 臣司風憲，不預軍事，而以臣預賞，非黨臣則忌臣也。 」因陳三桂擁兵觀望狀，三桂深銜之。 浴劾永寧總兵柏永馥臨陣退縮，廣元副將胡一鵬驕悍不法，並命奪官逮治。 降將董顯忠等以副將銜題授司道，恣睢虐民，浴复疏劾，改原職。 三桂嗾顯忠等入京陳辨，浴坐鐫秩去。
After Sangui entered Sichuan he grew ever more overbearing. Many of his men broke the law, and fearing Yu's strictness they repeatedly blocked courier reports along the roads. Yu memorialized: "I am charged to serve as the court's eyes and ears. If communications are blocked like this, what use am I to the throne? " When the siege of Baoning was lifted and rewards were handed out, Sangui offered Yu ceremonial dress, but Yu refused. He wrote: "Crushing the rebels is the Prince of Pingxi's responsibility. I oversee discipline and had no part in the fighting. To include me in the reward is either to court me or to mark me for suspicion. " He also described how Sangui held his army back and waited on events, and Sangui came to hate him deeply. Yu impeached Bai Yongfu, regional commander at Yongning, for flinching in battle, and Hu Yipeng, vice commander at Guangyuan, for arrogance and lawlessness, and had both stripped of rank and arrested. Surrendered generals such as Dong Xianzhong had been nominated at vice-commander rank for provincial posts and abused the people. Yu impeached them again and they were reduced to their former ranks. Sangui prompted Xianzhong and the others to go to the capital to defend themselves, and Yu was demoted and dismissed.
6
十一年，大學士馮銓、成克鞏、呂宮等交章薦浴，三桂乃摭浴保寧奏捷疏有「親冒矢石」語，指為冒功，論劾，部議當坐死，上命寬之，流徙奉天。 大學士馮銓、成克鞏、呂宮皆以薦浴罣吏議。 浴至戍所，益潛心義理之學，嗜孟子及二程遺書，以「致知格物」顏其廬，刻苦厲志。 康熙十年，聖祖幸奉天，浴迎謁道左，具陳始末，上為動容，慰勞良久。
In the eleventh year Grand Secretaries Feng Quan, Cheng Kegong, Lü Gong, and others repeatedly recommended Yu. Sangui then seized on Yu's Baoning victory memorial, which said he had "personally braved arrows and stones," and accused him of false claims to merit. After impeachment the ministries ruled he deserved death, but the emperor commuted the sentence and exiled him to Fengtian. Grand Secretaries Feng Quan, Cheng Kegong, and Lü Gong were all censured for having recommended Yu. At his place of exile Yu threw himself into moral philosophy, devouring Mencius and the Cheng brothers' writings. He labeled his hut "Extending Knowledge and Investigating Things" and disciplined himself with stern resolve. In the tenth year of Kangxi the emperor visited Fengtian. Yu met him by the road, told the whole story of his case, and moved the emperor, who spoke with him at length in consolation.
7
十二年，三桂反，尚書王熙、給事中劉沛先薦浴，為部議所格。 十四年，侍郎魏象樞复疏言：「浴血性過人，才守學識，臣皆愧不及。 使在西蜀操尺寸之權，豈肯如羅森輩俯首從逆？ 臣子立朝，各有本末。 當日參浴者三桂也，使三桂始終恭順，方且任以腹心。 浴一書生耳，即老死徙所，誰復問之？ 今三桂叛矣，天下無不恨三桂，即無不憐浴。 浴當三桂身居王爵，手握兵柄，不畏威，不附勢，致為所仇。 三桂之所仇，正國家之所取，何忍棄之？ 」上乃召浴還，复授湖廣道御史。
In the twelfth year, when Sangui rebelled, Minister Wang Xi and supervising secretary Liu Peixian were the first to recommend Yu, but the ministries blocked the appointment. In the fourteenth year Vice Minister Wei Xiangshu memorialized again: "Yu's courage is beyond the ordinary. In talent, integrity, and learning I am ashamed to compare with him. Had he held even a small measure of authority in western Shu, would he have bowed to rebellion like Luo Sen and his kind? Every minister who serves at court has his own story from start to finish. The man who impeached Yu then was Sangui. Had Sangui remained loyal, the court would even have trusted him with its innermost affairs. Yu is only a scholar. Even if he died in exile, who would have noticed? Now Sangui has rebelled. All under Heaven hates Sangui—and pities Yu. When Sangui held princely rank and military power, Yu neither feared his might nor courted his favor, and so became his enemy. Whom Sangui hated is precisely whom the state should value. How can we abandon him? " The emperor recalled Yu and reappointed him censor of the Huguang circuit.
8
時陝西提督王輔臣叛應三桂，浴疏言：「大兵進剿平涼，宜於西安、潼關用重兵屯駐，以待策應。 用鄖陽之兵攻興安，調河南之兵入武關，直取漢中，逆賊計日可擒。 」上然之，下其疏諸帥。 復請禁苛徵，恤民困，止督、撫、提、鎮坐​​名題補之例。 章十數上，皆中時弊。 十六年，命巡視兩淮鹽政，嚴剔宿蠹，增課六十餘萬。 淮、揚大饑，發倉米賑救，全活甚眾。 十七年，擢左僉都御史，遷左副都御史。
When Shaanxi provincial commander Wang Fuchen rebelled in support of Sangui, Yu wrote: "As the main army advances against Pingliang, heavy forces should be stationed at Xi'an and Tongguan to support the campaign. Send the Xiangyang troops against Xing'an, move Henan troops through Wuguan, and strike straight for Hanzhong—the rebels can be taken within days." " The emperor agreed and sent the memorial to the field commanders. He also urged an end to harsh levies, relief for the people's hardships, and an end to the practice whereby governors, provincial commanders, and regional commanders merely signed their names on nominations. He submitted more than a dozen memorials, each hitting a current abuse squarely. In the sixteenth year he was ordered to inspect the Liang-Huai salt administration, root out entrenched corruption, and increase revenue by more than six hundred thousand taels. When famine struck the Huai and Yang regions he opened the granaries and saved a great many lives. In the seventeenth year he was promoted to Left Censor-in-Chief and then to Left Vice Censor-in-Chief.
9
十九年，授廣西巡撫。 廣西新經喪亂，民生凋瘵，浴專意撫綏，疏陳調劑四策，請裁兵、汰馬、防要害、簡精銳； 復請停鼓鑄，改米徵銀，复南寧、太平、思恩諸府縣行鹽舊制：上輒報可。 時南疆底定，滿洲兵撤還京師。 浴疏言撫標兵不宜裁減，下部議，留其半。 又請為死事巡撫馬雄鎮、傅弘烈建祠桂林，知府劉浩、知縣周岱生為孫延齡所戕，疏請予卹。 二十二年，卒官。 喪歸，士民泣送者數千里不絕。
In the nineteenth year he was appointed Governor of Guangxi. Guangxi had only lately emerged from rebellion and the people were destitute. Yu devoted himself to pacification and proposed four measures of adjustment: cut troops, cull horses, guard key points, and keep only elite units; he also asked to halt minting, convert grain taxes to silver payments, and restore the old salt regulations in Nanning, Taiping, Si'en, and other districts. The emperor approved each request. By then the southern frontier had been pacified and Manchu troops were withdrawn to the capital. Yu argued that the governor's banner troops should not be cut. After deliberation the ministries kept half their strength. He also asked for shrines at Guilin to the fallen governors Ma Xiongzhen and Fu Honglie, and posthumous honors for Prefect Liu Hao and Magistrate Zhou Daisheng, who had been killed by Sun Yanling. In the twenty-second year he died in office. As his coffin was borne home, mourners among officials and commoners lined the road for thousands of li without a break.
10
初，傅弘烈以軍事急，移庫金七萬有奇、米七千餘石供餉，浴請以庫項扣抵。 及卒，布政使崔維雅署巡撫，劾浴侵欺，命郎中蘇赫、陳光祖往按，如維雅言。 部議奪官追償。 上知浴廉，諭所動錢糧非入己，從寬免追。 二十五年，子林訟父冤，復原官，賜祭葬。
Earlier, when military needs were urgent, Fu Honglie had drawn more than seventy thousand taels from the treasury and more than seven thousand shi of grain for supplies. Yu asked that these sums be charged against the treasury accounts. After Yu's death, Provincial Administration Commissioner Cui Weiya, acting as governor, impeached him for embezzlement. The emperor sent Directors Su He and Chen Guangzu to investigate, and they confirmed Cui's charges. The ministries ruled that his rank should be posthumously revoked and restitution demanded. Knowing Yu's integrity, the emperor ruled that the funds and grain had not gone into his own pocket and waived restitution. In the twenty-fifth year his son Lin petitioned to clear his father's name. Yu's rank was restored and he was granted state sacrifices and burial honors.
11
林，字中美。 康熙二十一年進士，授中書科中書，歷吏部郎中，亦以廉正稱。 累遷禮部侍郎，加尚書銜。 致仕，卒。
Lin, whose style was Zhongmei, became a jinshi in the twenty-first year of Kangxi, served as a Secretariat secretary and later as a director in the Ministry of Personnel, and was likewise known for integrity. He rose through several posts to Vice Minister of Rites with ministerial rank. He retired and later died.
12
楊素蘊，字筠湄，陝西宜君人。 順治九年進士，授直隸東明知縣。 東明當河決後，官舍城垣悉敗，民居殆盡，遺民依丘阜，僅數十家。 素蘊至，為繕城郭，招集流亡，三年戶增至萬餘。 山東群盜任鳳亭等剽掠旁郡，擾及畿南。 素蘊設計降其渠，散其脅從。 十七年，舉卓異，行取，授四川道御史。 疏言：「臣言官也，宜以言為事。 然今天下所患，正在議論多而成功少。 國家建官分職，各有所事。 誠使司舉劾，籌財用，任封疆，理刑獄，各舉其職，則平天下無餘事。 更原皇上推誠御物，肅大閒，寬小眚，俾人人得展其才，尤端本澄源之要也。 」
Yang Suyun, whose style was Junmei, came from Yijun in Shaanxi. He became a jinshi in the ninth year of Shunzhi and was appointed magistrate of Dongming in Zhili. Dongming had been devastated by a river breach. Government buildings and city walls lay in ruins, homes were nearly gone, and survivors clustered on hillocks—only a few dozen households remained. When Suyun arrived he rebuilt the walls and city, gathered the displaced, and within three years households rose to more than ten thousand. Shandong bandits such as Ren Fengting raided neighboring prefectures and even threatened the southern approaches to the capital. Suyun devised a plan to induce the ringleaders to surrender and disperse their followers. In the seventeenth year he was rated outstanding, selected for capital service, and appointed censor of the Sichuan circuit. He memorialized: "I am a remonstrating official and ought to make speaking out my duty. Yet what afflicts the realm today is talk in abundance and results in short supply. The state establishes offices and divides duties, each with its proper charge. If those who oversee impeachment, plan revenue, hold frontier posts, and administer justice each did their proper work, pacifying the realm would require nothing more. I further pray that Your Majesty treat all with sincere openness, be stern in great matters and lenient in small faults, and let every man display his talent. That above all is how to rectify the root and clarify the source." "
13
時吳三桂鎮雲南，郡縣吏得自闢署，謂之「西選」。 漸乃題用朝臣，無復顧忌。 素蘊疏言：「三桂以上湖南道胡允等十員題補雲南各道，並有奉差部員在內，深足駭異。 爵祿者人主之大柄，綱紀者朝廷之大防，柄不可移，防不可潰。 前此經略用人，特命二部不得掣肘，亦惟以軍前效用及所轄五省各官酌量題請，從未聞敢以他行省及現任京官坐缺定銜者也。 且疏稱求於滇省既苦索駿無從，求於遠方又恐叱馭不速，則湖南、四川距雲南猶近，若京師、山東、江南相去萬里，不知其所謂遠者更在何方？ 皇上特假便宜，不過許其就近調補。 若盡天下之官，不分內外，不論遠近，皆可擇而取之，何如歸吏部銓授，尤為名正言順。 縱或云、貴新經開闢，料理乏人，諸臣才品為籓臣所素知，亦宜請旨令吏部簽補； 乃徑行擬用，不亦輕名器而褻國體乎？ 人臣忠邪之分，起於一念之敬肆。 籓臣易攵歷有年，應知大體。 此舉為封疆計，未必別有深心，然防微杜漸，當慎於幾先。 祈申飭籓臣，嗣後惟力圖進取，加意撫綏，一切威福大權，俱宜禀自朝廷，則君恩臣誼兩盡其善。 」疏下部。
At the time Wu Sangui governed Yunnan. Prefectural and county officials could be appointed at his own discretion—the so-called "western selection." Gradually he began nominating court officials as well, without further restraint. Suyun wrote: "Sangui has nominated ten men, including Hu Yun of the Hunan circuit, to fill Yunnan's circuit posts, and among them are ministry officials on imperial assignment. This is deeply alarming. Rank and stipend are the sovereign's great prerogative; discipline is the court's great bulwark. The prerogative must not be shifted, the bulwark must not be breached. When the frontier commissioner appointed men before, the two ministries were ordered not to obstruct him, yet nominations were weighed only for merit in the field and for officials within his five provinces. Never was it heard that he dared fix ranks for vacancies in other provinces or for serving capital officials. His memorial says that within Yunnan he can find no suitable men, and that men from afar may not arrive in time. Yet Hunan and Sichuan are still near Yunnan. If the capital, Shandong, and Jiangnan lie ten thousand li away, where exactly is his "afar"? The special discretion Your Majesty granted amounts to no more than allowing nearby transfers. If every office under Heaven, without regard to inner or outer, near or far, may be chosen at will, how much better to return appointments to the Ministry of Personnel—that would be proper in name and right. Even if Yunnan and Guizhou have only lately been opened and lack administrators, and the frontier lord knows these men's talents well, he should still request an edict and let the Ministry of Personnel sign and fill the posts; to proceed directly with appointments—is this not to slight the vessels of honor and profane the dignity of the state? The line between loyal and treacherous ministers begins in a single thought of reverence or license. The frontier lord has held power for many years and ought to understand what is fitting. This move may serve the frontier and need not spring from hidden designs, yet to check trouble before it spreads one must be cautious at the first sign. I pray that the frontier lord be sternly warned: hereafter let him strive for progress and give added care to pacification. All great powers of authority and favor should come from the court—then sovereign grace and ministerial duty will both be fulfilled. " The memorial was sent to the ministries.
14
十八年，聖祖即位，輔臣柄政，出素蘊為川北道。 三桂見素蘊前奏，惡之，具疏辨，並摘「防微杜漸」語，謂意含隱射，語伏危機。 詔責素蘊回奏，素蘊言：「防微杜漸，古今通義。 臣但期籓臣每事盡善，為聖世純臣，非有他也。 」下部議，坐素蘊巧飾，當降調，罷歸。
In the eighteenth year, when the Sacred Ancestor ascended the throne, the assisting ministers held power and transferred Suyun to the North Sichuan circuit. Sangui, seeing Suyun's earlier memorial, hated him and submitted a full rebuttal, seizing on the phrase "guard against trouble before it spreads" and claiming it carried hidden insinuation and concealed a threat. An edict rebuked Suyun and ordered a reply. Suyun said: "Guarding against trouble before it spreads is a universal principle of past and present. I only hope the frontier lord will do every matter as well as possible and be a loyal minister of this sage age. I had no other intent. " After deliberation the ministries found Suyun guilty of artful excuse, ruled he should be demoted, and dismissed him.
15
居十年，三桂反。 尚書郝惟訥、冀如錫，侍郎楊永寧交章請起用，惟訥詞尤切，略言：「素蘊首劾三桂，雲當防微杜漸。 在當日反狀未形，似屬杞憂。 由今觀之，則素蘊先見甚明，且為國直陳，奮不自顧，其剛腸正氣，實有大過人者！ 亟宜優錄。 」乃命發湖廣軍前，以原品用。 會丁父憂，服闋，乃赴軍前。 總督蔡毓榮題補湖廣提學道，部議當以現辦軍務參議道題補。 康熙十七年，題補下荊南道。 時襄陽總兵楊來嘉、副將洪福等叛應三桂。 大軍運餉，自襄至房、保路險C7，舟車不通，歲調襄陽、安陸、德安三郡丁夫擔負，餉苦不繼。 素蘊訪知穀城有小溪可通舟，乃按行山谷開餉道，由是水運通利，省丁夫什九，軍乃無乏。 遷山西提學道。 二十四年，任滿，薦舉擢通政司參議，累遷順天府尹。 二十六年，授安徽巡撫。 會歲饑，上疏請賑。 甫拜疏，即檄州縣開倉賑給，全活甚眾。
Ten years later Sangui rebelled. Ministers Hao Weina and Ji Ruxi and Vice Minister Yang Yongning repeatedly asked that he be reemployed. Weina was especially urgent, writing in summary: "Suyun was the first to impeach Sangui, warning that trouble must be checked before it spreads. At the time rebellion had not yet appeared, and it seemed idle worry. Seen from today, Suyun's foresight is unmistakable. He spoke straight for the state and strove without regard for himself. His firm integrity and upright spirit truly surpass ordinary men! He should urgently be honored with appointment. " He was ordered to the Huguang front at his former rank. He was in mourning for his father; when the mourning ended he went to the front. Governor-General Cai Yurong nominated him for the Huguang educational circuit, but the ministries ruled he should fill the military affairs participation circuit then in active service. In the seventeenth year of Kangxi he was appointed to the Lower Jingnan circuit. At the time Xiangyang regional commander Yang Laijia, Vice Commander Hong Fu, and others rebelled in support of Sangui. The main army moved supplies from Xiangyang toward Fang and Bao over rugged terrain where neither boats nor carts could pass. Each year corvée laborers from Xiangyang, Anlu, and De'an were levied to carry loads on their backs, and supplies constantly fell short. Suyun learned that a small stream at Gucheng could carry boats. He surveyed the mountain valleys and opened a supply route, so water transport became efficient, corvée labor fell by nine-tenths, and the army was no longer short of supplies. He was transferred to the Shanxi educational circuit. In the twenty-fourth year, when his term ended, he was promoted by recommendation to Vice Commissioner of the Court of Transmission and later became Intendant of Shuntian Prefecture. In the twenty-sixth year he was appointed Governor of Anhui. When famine struck that year he memorialized asking for relief. As soon as his memorial went up he ordered the prefectures and counties to open the granaries, saving a great many lives.
16
尋調湖廣巡撫。 夏逢龍亂初定，脅從尚眾，人情恇擾，一夕數驚。 素蘊首嚴告訐之禁，反側以安。 二十八年，大旱，疏請蠲免武昌等屬三十二州縣錢糧，上遣戶部郎中舒淑等會督撫勘災。 舒淑至武昌，素蘊適患暑疾，令布政使於養志從總督丁思孔往勘。 尋稱病乞休，上疑其託疾，奪官。 命甫下而素蘊已卒。
Soon he was transferred to Governor of Huguang. The rebellion of Xia Fenglong had only just been suppressed. Many who had been coerced still remained, hearts were fearful and unsettled, and in a single night alarms arose several times. Suyun was the first to enforce strictly the ban on informers, and the wavering were pacified. In the twenty-eighth year a great drought struck. He memorialized to exempt taxes and grain for thirty-two prefectures and counties under Wuchang and elsewhere. The emperor sent Ministry of Revenue Director Shu Shu and others to join the governor-general and governor in surveying the disaster. When Shu Shu reached Wuchang, Suyun was ill with summer heat and ordered Provincial Administration Commissioner Yu Yangzhi to accompany Governor-General Ding Sikong on the survey. Soon he pleaded illness and asked to retire. The emperor suspected he was feigning sickness and stripped him of office. The order had scarcely been issued when Suyun died.
17
先是，湖北郡縣疾苦最甚者，如沔陽、江陵、漢陽、嘉魚濱江地陷未蠲賦額，咸寧、黃陂、景陵谷折，江夏、崇陽、武昌、通城、漢陽、漢川、雲夢、孝感、應城穀田科重，監利一年兩賦，為民害數十年。 素蘊得其實，條為兩疏。 未及上而病革，口授入遺疏，曰：「此疏行，吾目瞑矣！ 」
Earlier, among the gravest abuses in Huguang: in Mianyang, Jiangling, Hanyang, and Jiayu, sunken riverside land still bore full taxes; in Xianning, Huangpi, and Jingling, grain yields were assessed at half the true harvest; in Jiangxia, Chongyang, Wuchang, Tongcheng, Hanyang, Hanchuan, Yunmeng, Xiaogan, and Yingcheng, grain-field taxes were crushing; in Jianli two levies were collected in a single year—burdens that had harmed the people for decades. Suyun verified the facts and set them forth in two memorials. Before he could submit them his illness turned critical. He dictated a deathbed memorial, saying: "When this memorial is carried out, I can close my eyes! "
18
郭琇，字華野，山東即墨人。 康熙九年進士。 十八年，授江南吳江知縣。 材力強幹，善斷疑獄。 徵賦行版串法，胥吏不能為奸。 居官七年，治行為江南最。 二十五年，巡撫湯斌薦琇居心恬淡，蒞事精銳，請遷擢。 部議以琇徵賦未如額，寢其奏，聖祖特許之，行取，授江南道御史。 時河督靳輔請停濬下河，築高家堰重堤，清丈堤外田畝以為屯田，謂可增歲收百餘萬。 巡撫於成龍議不​​合，上令尚書佛倫往勘，主輔議。 下九卿覈奏，尚書張玉書、左都御史徐乾學力言屯田擾民。 二十七年，琇疏劾輔治河無功，偏聽幕客陳潢阻濬下河。 上御乾清門，召諸大臣，下琇疏，令會同察議。 尋輔入覲，復召諸大臣與議。 琇申言屯田害民，輔坐罷，而擢琇僉都御史。
Guo Xiu, whose style was Huaye, came from Jimo in Shandong. He became a jinshi in the ninth year of Kangxi. In the eighteenth year he was appointed magistrate of Wujiang in Jiangnan. He was capable and forceful, and skilled at deciding difficult cases. He collected taxes by the linked-register method, so clerks could not practice fraud. After seven years in office his record was the finest in Jiangnan. In the twenty-fifth year Governor Tang Bin recommended Xiu as calm in mind and sharp in affairs, and asked that he be promoted. The ministries held that Xiu's tax collection had not met quota and shelved the memorial, but the Sacred Ancestor overruled them, selected him for capital service, and appointed him censor of the Jiangnan circuit. At the time Grand Canal Director Jin Fu asked to halt dredging of the lower river, build heavy dikes at the Gaojia Weir, and survey fields outside the dikes for garrison farms, claiming annual revenue could rise by more than a million taels. Governor Yu Chenglong disagreed. The emperor ordered Minister Folun to investigate and upheld Fu's plan. The matter went to the Nine Ministers for review. Minister Zhang Yushu and Left Censor-in-Chief Xu Qianxue strongly argued that garrison farms would harm the people. In the twenty-seventh year Xiu impeached Fu for achieving nothing in river control and for listening only to his secretary Chen Huang, who blocked dredging of the lower river. The emperor held court at the Gate of Heavenly Purity, summoned the ministers, circulated Xiu's memorial, and ordered a joint investigation. Soon Fu came to court for audience, and the ministers were summoned again to deliberate with him. Xiu again argued that garrison farms harmed the people. Fu was dismissed, and Xiu was promoted to Censor-in-Chief.
19
大學士明珠柄政，與餘國柱比，頗營賄賂，權傾一時，久之為上所覺。 琇疏劾明珠與國柱結黨行私，詳列諸罪狀，並及佛倫、傅拉塔與輔等交通狀，於是明珠等降黜有差。 琇直聲震天下。 遷太常寺卿，再遷內閣學士。 二十八年，复遷吏部侍郎，充經筵講官，擢左都御史。 疏劾少詹事高士奇與原任左都御史王鴻緒植黨為奸，給事中何楷、修撰陳元龍、編修王頊齡依附壞法，士奇等並休致回籍。
Grand Secretary Mingzhu held power alongside Yu Guozhu and engaged extensively in bribery. His authority dominated the age until the emperor finally perceived it. Xiu impeached Mingzhu and Guozhu for forming a faction and pursuing private gain, listing their crimes in detail, and also cited Folun, Fulata, and Fu for collusion. Mingzhu and the others were then demoted in varying degrees. Xiu's reputation for integrity shook the realm. He was made Minister of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices and then Grand Secretariat Academician. In the twenty-eighth year he became Vice Minister of Personnel, served as lecturer at the Classics Colloquium, and was promoted to Left Censor-in-Chief. He impeached Junior Mentor Gao Shiqi and former Left Censor-in-Chief Wang Hongxu for forming a corrupt clique, and cited supervising secretaries He Kai, Compiler Chen Yuanlong, and Editor Wang Xuling for abetting them. Shiqi and the others were all retired and sent home.
20
未幾，御史張星法劾山東巡撫錢鎯貪黷，鎯奏辨，因及琇嘗致書囑薦即墨知縣高上達等，卻之，遂挾嫌使星法誣劾，下法司訊。 獄未具，琇疏言：「左都御​​史馬齊於會訊時多方鍛鍊，必欲實以指使誣劾罪。 」詔責琇疑揣。 尋法司奏琇請託事實，當奪官。 上以琇平日鯁直敢言，改降五級調用。 二十九年，吏部推琇通政司參議，上命改令予琇休致。 江寧巡撫洪之傑以吳江縣虧漕項，事涉琇，牒山東追琇赴質。 時佛倫為山東巡撫，因劾琇違例逗留希進用，請奪官逮治； 又劾琇世父郭爾印乃明季御史黃宗昌家奴，琇父郭景昌原名爾標，嘗入賊黨伏法，琇私改父名請誥封，應追奪。 部議如所請，逮赴江寧勘治。 坐侵收運船飯米二千三百餘石，事發彌補，議遣戍，詔寬之。
Before long Censor Zhang Xingfa impeached Shandong Governor Qian Hao for corruption. Hao defended himself and said Xiu had once written asking him to recommend Jimo Magistrate Gao Shangda and others, which he had refused. Bearing a grudge, Hao had Xingfa bring a false charge, and the case went to the judiciary. Before the case concluded Xiu wrote: "Left Censor-in-Chief Ma Qi at the joint examination fabricated evidence extensively and was determined to convict me of directing a false impeachment. " An edict rebuked Xiu for casting suspicion. Soon the judiciary reported that Xiu's solicitation was proved and ruled he should be stripped of office. Because Xiu was normally upright and outspoken, the emperor commuted the penalty to demotion by five ranks and reassignment. In the twenty-ninth year the Ministry of Personnel recommended Xiu for Vice Commissioner of the Court of Transmission, but the emperor ordered him retired instead. Jiangning Governor Hong Zhijie, citing a shortfall in grain transport funds in Wujiang County that involved Xiu, summoned him from Shandong to answer the charge. Folun was then Governor of Shandong and impeached Xiu for lingering in violation of regulations in hope of reappointment, asking that he be stripped of rank and arrested; he also charged that Xiu's father's elder brother Guo Eryin had been a slave of the late-Ming censor Huang Zongchang, that Xiu's father Guo Jingchang had originally been named Erbiao and had joined bandits and been executed, and that Xiu had changed his father's name to obtain ennoblement—all of which should be revoked. The ministries approved, and he was arrested and sent to Jiangning for trial. He was found guilty of taking more than twenty-three hundred shi of rice meant for transport boats. When the matter came to light he made restitution. Banishment was proposed, but the emperor granted leniency.
21
三十八年，上南巡，琇迎駕德州。 既還京師，諭大學士阿蘭泰等曰：「原任左都御史郭琇，前為吳江令，居官甚善，百姓感頌至今。 其人有膽量，可授湖廣總督，令馳驛赴任。 」琇上官，疏言：「黃州、武昌二府兵米二萬七千有奇，運給荊州、鄖陽汛地，懸隔千里，輓輸費不貲，請改折色。 江夏等十三州縣有故明籓產，田瘠賦重，數倍民糧，請一律減徵。 江夏、嘉魚、漢陽三縣瀕江地，水齧土陊，有賦無田者三百餘頃，請豁免。 」皆允行。
In the thirty-eighth year, on the emperor's southern tour, Xiu met the procession at Dezhou. Back in the capital he told Grand Secretary Alantai and others: "Former Left Censor-in-Chief Guo Xiu, once magistrate of Wujiang, governed very well, and the people still praise him. He has courage. Appoint him Governor-General of Huguang and send him posthaste to take office. " On taking office Xiu wrote: "Huangzhou and Wuchang supply more than twenty-seven thousand units of military grain to garrisons at Jingzhou and Xiangyang a thousand li away. Transport costs are enormous. I ask that payment be converted to silver. Thirteen prefectures and counties including Jiangxia hold former Ming princely estates whose fields are poor yet taxed several times the ordinary rate. I ask uniform reduction. In Jiangxia, Jiayu, and Hanyang, more than three hundred qing of riverside land eroded by water are still taxed though no fields remain. I ask exemption. " All were approved.
22
三十九年，入覲，因奏言：「臣父景昌，即墨縣諸生，有冊可稽。 邑匪郭爾標本無妻室，安得有子？ 不知佛倫何所據，誣臣並及臣父。 」時佛倫為大學士，上詰之，以舛錯對，命仍予誥軸。 琇陛辭，奏請清丈地畝，並言湖南地廣人稀，恐清丈後賦當差減。 上問：「當減幾何？ 」琇言：「當減十分之三。 」上曰：「果益民，雖倍於此，亦不惜也！ 」尋條陳三事：一，嚴定築堤處分； 一，停造無用糧船； 一，通融調補苗疆官吏。 又疏禁徵賦諸弊政。 上嘉其實心除弊，並允行。 時紅苗就撫，琇陳善後之策，請頒詔敕，令勒石永遵。
In the thirty-ninth year he came to audience and said: "My father Jingchang was a licentiate of Jimo County, verifiable in the registers. The local bandit Guo Erbiao had no wife. How could he have had a son? I do not know what Folun relied on in slandering me and my father. " Folun was then Grand Secretary. The emperor questioned him, and his answers were confused. The emperor ordered that Xiu's patent of honor still be granted. At his farewell audience Xiu asked for a full land survey and said that Hunan is broad and sparsely peopled, so assessments would likely need substantial reduction after survey. The emperor asked: "By how much should they be reduced? " Xiu said: "By three-tenths. " The emperor said: "If it truly benefits the people, I would not regret even double that! " Soon he proposed three measures: first, strict penalties for dike construction; second, halt building useless grain boats; third, flexibly transfer and fill Miao frontier posts. He also memorialized to forbid abuses in tax collection. The emperor praised his sincere effort to remove abuses and approved all. The Red Miao had just submitted. Xiu proposed policies for the aftermath and asked that an edict be promulgated and inscribed on stone for perpetual observance.
23
四十年，以病乞休，上曰：「琇病甚，思一人代之不可得，能如琇者有幾人耶？ 」給事中馬士芳劾湖廣布政使任風厚久病，巡撫年遐齡徇庇不以聞。 遐齡奏風厚實無病。 風厚入覲，上見其未衰，因曰：「任風厚若不堪任使，郭琇豈肯徇庇耶？ 」未幾，琇以病劇再疏求罷，仍慰留。 黃梅知縣李錦催科不力，琇委員摘印。 錦得民心，民閉城拒之，乞留錦。 御史左必蕃劾琇，部議當奪官，上以清丈未畢，緩之。
In the fortieth year he pleaded illness and asked to retire. The emperor said: "Xiu is very ill. I cannot think of anyone to replace him. How many men are there like Xiu? " Supervising Secretary Ma Shifang impeached Huguang Provincial Administration Commissioner Ren Fenghou for long illness, charging that Governor Nian Xialing had shielded him and not reported it. Xialing reported that Fenghou was not ill. Fenghou came to audience. The emperor saw he was not enfeebled and said: "If Ren Fenghou were unfit for service, would Guo Xiu have shielded him? " Soon Xiu, gravely ill, again asked to be dismissed, but was comforted and retained. Huangmei Magistrate Li Jin was ineffective in tax collection. Xiu sent an officer to remove his seal. Jin had won the people's hearts. They closed the city and resisted, begging that he be kept. Censor Zuo Bifan impeached Xiu. The ministries ruled he should be stripped of office, but because the land survey was unfinished the emperor deferred action.
24
四十一年，鎮筸諸生李定等叩閽奏紅苗殺掠，總督、巡撫匿不以聞； 而給事中宋駿業亦劾琇向騖虛聲，近益衰廢，持祿養癰。 乃命侍郎傅繼祖、甘國樞，浙江巡撫趙申喬往按。 會琇報清丈畢，乞罷任。 上責其清丈稽延，與前奏不合，行不顧言； 並及匿報紅苗殺掠與黃梅拒命事。 琇自陳老病失察，請治罪。 初紅苗犯鎮筸，游擊沈長祿往剿，至大梅山，守備許邦垣、千總孫清俱陷賊，長祿私贖之歸，諱不報； 而副將硃紱報苗已就撫，琇據以入告。 繼祖等勘得狀，琇與提督林本植並奪官。 五十四年，卒。 尋祀鄉賢，並祀吳江名宦。
In the forty-first year students Li Ding and others of Zhenqian petitioned at the gate, reporting Red Miao killings and plunder that the governor-general and governor had concealed; and Supervising Secretary Song Junye also impeached Xiu for chasing empty reputation, lately growing negligent, and holding his stipend while nursing his malady. The emperor then ordered Vice Ministers Fu Jizu and Gan Guoshu and Zhejiang Governor Zhao Shenqiao to investigate. At the same time Xiu reported the land survey finished and asked to leave office. The emperor rebuked him for delaying the survey, contradicting his earlier memorial, and acting against his own words; and also cited concealing Red Miao raids and the Huangmei defiance of orders. Xiu confessed that age and illness had caused neglect and asked to be punished. Earlier, when the Red Miao attacked Zhenqian, Guerrilla Commander Shen Changlu went to suppress them. At Damei Mountain Garrison Commander Xu Bangyuan and Battalion Commander Sun Qing both fell to the rebels. Changlu privately ransomed them back but concealed the matter; yet Vice Commander Zhu Hong reported the Miao had submitted, and Xiu reported this to the throne on that basis. Jizu and the others verified the facts, and both Xiu and Provincial Commander Lin Benzhi were stripped of office. In the fifty-fourth year he died. Soon he was enshrined among local worthies and also among the distinguished officials of Wujiang.
25
論曰：郝浴、楊素蘊秉剛正之性，抗論強籓，曲突徙薪，防禍未形，甘竄逐而不悔。 郭琇抨擊權相，有直臣之風，震霆一鳴，僉壬解體。 蓋由聖祖已悟其奸，而琇遂得行其志。 然以浴之廉，蒙議於身後； 素蘊居官愛民，不終於位； 琇則​​橫被誣陷，廢置十年，始獲申雪。 得君如聖祖，猶不克善全，直道難行，不其然哉？
The commentator says: Hao Yu and Yang Suyun were upright and stern by nature. They spoke against powerful frontier lords, removed the fuel before the fire spread, and checked disaster before it formed. They accepted exile without regret. Guo Xiu struck at the powerful minister with the bearing of a true remonstrator. One peal of thunder, and the whole faction fell apart. This was because the Sacred Ancestor had already seen through the minister's treachery, and so Xiu was able to carry out his purpose. Yet Yu's integrity was questioned even after his death; Suyun loved the people in office but did not finish his career in post; Xiu was wantonly slandered, set aside for ten years, and only then cleared. Even with a ruler like the Sacred Ancestor they still could not be preserved intact. How hard the straight path is to walk—is it not so?
