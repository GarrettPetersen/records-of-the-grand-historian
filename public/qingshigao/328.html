常青，佟佳氏，滿洲正藍旗人。 父安圖，官至江西巡撫。 常青自寧郡王府長史累遷察哈爾都統，杭州、福州將軍。 乾隆五十一年，署閩浙總督。 諸羅縣民楊光勳與其弟爭家業，糾眾立會，縣吏捕治不服，常青令按察使李永祺往按。 上以台灣在海外，不可輕縱，諭勿使蔓延疏脫。 尋實授閩浙總督。 十二月，林爽文亂起，陷彰化，知縣俞峻死之。 常青檄水師提督黃仕簡自鹿耳門進，副將丁朝雄從海壇鎮總兵郝壯猷自淡水進，都司馬元勳屯鹿仔港，分道部署； 復如泉州會陸路提督任承恩調度，令金門鎮總兵羅英笈詣廈門彈壓。 尋復令承恩自鹿耳門繼進。 五十二年，奏賊陷諸羅。 台灣鎮總兵柴大紀堵剿，賊勢稍沮。 爽文漳州人，其徒率漳籍。 移會兩廣督臣防範，上責其張皇。 授李侍堯閩浙總督，而移常青湖廣。
Chang Qing, of the Tongjia clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain Blue Banner. His father Antu had served as governor of Jiangxi. Chang Qing advanced from chief steward of the Prince of Ning's household through the Chahar commandancy to the generalships at Hangzhou and Fuzhou. In 1786 he was appointed acting governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. When a Zhuluo county man named Yang Guangxun feuded with his brother over their inheritance, rallied a crowd into a secret society, and resisted arrest, Chang Qing dispatched the surveillance commissioner Li Yongqi to investigate. Because Taiwan lay overseas and could not be handled lightly, the emperor ordered that the trouble must neither spread nor be allowed to slip away through negligence. He was soon confirmed in the governorship of Fujian and Zhejiang. In the twelfth month Lin Shuangwen's rebellion erupted; the rebels took Zhanghua, and Magistrate Yu Jun was killed. Chang Qing directed Naval Commander Huang Shijian to advance through Lukang, Brigadier Ding Chaoxiong and Haitan Garrison Commander Hao Zhuangyou to move inland from Tamsui, and Company Commander Ma Yuanxun to hold Lukang Harbor, dividing his forces accordingly; He then met Land Commander Ren Chengen at Quanzhou to coordinate operations and sent Kinmen Garrison Commander Luo Yingji to Xiamen to keep order. He soon ordered Chengen to press forward again through Lukang. In 1787 he reported that the rebels had captured Zhuluo. Taiwan Garrison Commander Chai Daji held the line and harried the rebels, and their momentum slackened somewhat. Shuangwen came from Zhangzhou, and most of his followers were Zhangzhou men. He notified the governor-general of the Two Guangs to guard against spillover, but the emperor criticized him for overstating the danger. Li Shiyao was made governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang, and Chang Qing was transferred to Huguang.
3
既又命常青渡台視師，四月，至台灣。 劾仕簡、承恩遷延觀望，擁兵自衛； 壯猷守鳳山，賊至，棄城走。 諭逮承恩，罷仕簡候命，而誅壯猷，遂授常青為將軍。 賊攻府城，常青督諸軍禦戰，有所俘馘； 賊攻桶盤棧，令游擊蔡攀龍等分駐力禦。 奏入，上以常青年逾七十，能如此勇往督戰，手詔嘉獎，授其子刑部筆帖式喜明三等侍衛，馳驛往省，並賜御用搬指。 旋奏爽文還大里杙舊巢，其徒莊大田等萬餘人分擾南路，擬先南剿大田，乃北取爽文。 上韙之，下部優敘。 旋奏勦賊南潭，殲賊六百餘； 爽文之徒莊錫舍出降，擒偽軍師番婦金孃，請檻車送京師，上命授錫舍守備。 又奏進剿鳳山，出城未十里，賊三面並進，官兵奮勇擊退； 賊勢蔓延，請厚集兵力，遣大臣督戰。 上命陝甘總督福康安往視師。 旋奏：「賊犯府城，為丁朝雄擊退。 官軍攻莊大田於南潭，殺賊二百餘。 大營距府城未遠，勢相犄角，無後顧之虞。 」得旨嘉獎，賜雙眼孔雀翎。 旋迭奏鹽水港、笨港均為賊據，糧道既斷，諸羅勢甚危； 令總兵魏大斌赴援，戰賊失利，又令游擊田藍玉援大斌。 上以兵分力薄，飭常青調度失當。 又諭：「常青駐軍桶盤棧，距南潭不過五里，不將賊目莊大田先行剿除，乃結營自守。 肘腋之間，任其逼處。 」
He was soon ordered back to Taiwan to take personal command, reaching the island in the fourth month. He impeached Shijian and Chengen for dithering on the sidelines while keeping their troops for self-protection; Zhuangyou was holding Fengshan, but when the rebels came he abandoned the city and fled. The court ordered Chengen arrested, Shijian suspended pending further orders, and Zhuangyou executed; Chang Qing was then made commander-in-chief. When the rebels assaulted the prefectural seat, Chang Qing led the armies in defense and won some captives and heads; when they attacked Tongpan Stockade he stationed Brigade Commander Cai Panlong and others at separate points to hold the line. When the memorial arrived, the emperor praised Chang Qing—already past seventy—for leading the fight in person, wrote him a personal commendation, made his son Ximing, a Ministry of Justice clerk, a third-rank imperial guardsman, sent him posthaste to visit his father, and granted Chang Qing an imperial thumb ring. He soon reported that Shuangwen had withdrawn to his old base at Dali, that Zhuang Datian and more than ten thousand rebels were raiding the south, and that he intended to crush Datian in the south before turning north on Shuangwen. The emperor approved the plan and ordered that his merits be specially recorded. He soon reported a campaign at Nantan in which more than six hundred rebels were killed; Shuangwen's follower Zhuang Xishe defected, and the rebels' aboriginal woman strategist Jin Niang was taken; he asked to send her to the capital in a prison cart, and the emperor made Xishe a garrison commandant. He also reported an advance on Fengshan: less than ten li outside the city the rebels attacked from three sides, and the troops drove them back; as the rebellion spread he asked for heavier forces and a senior minister to take command. The emperor sent Shaanxi-Gansu Governor-General Fuk'anggan to take command. He soon reported: "The rebels assaulted the prefectural seat and were repulsed by Ding Chaoxiong. Government forces attacked Zhuang Datian at Nantan and killed more than two hundred rebels. The main camp lay close enough to the prefectural seat to support it like pincers, with no danger to the rear. " The emperor commended him and granted him the double-eyed peacock feather. He soon reported repeatedly that Yanshui Harbor and Bengang had fallen to the rebels, supplies were cut off, and Zhuluo was in grave danger; he sent Commander Wei Dabin to relieve the city, but Dabin was beaten in action, so he dispatched Brigade Commander Tian Lanyu to reinforce him. The emperor blamed Chang Qing for spreading his forces too thin and mishandling the campaign. He added: "Chang Qing holds Tongpan Stockade only five li from Nantan, yet instead of destroying the rebel chief Zhuang Datian first he has dug in and merely defended himself. He has let the enemy press in under his very arm. "
4
八月，命福康安為將軍，督諸將海蘭察、普爾普等大出師討爽文。 諭常青，謂：「非責其師無功，特以年已七十，軍旅非所素習。 福康安未至，仍當相機進​​剿。 」旋奏：「賊自南潭來攻，侍衛烏什哈達等擊敗之。 因雨後路滑，收兵； 又進攻南潭，焚草藔數百間，以天晚，山徑偪仄，不便深入。 」藔謂賊所居草屋也。 上以其屢稱遇雨路仄收兵，傳旨嚴飭。 上又聞賊诇知軍中暑濕多病，常青機事不密，又不督兵深入，屢詰責。 旋奏總兵梁朝桂勦賊多斬獲，提督柴大紀報諸羅圍急，令副將蔡攀龍赴援。 上諭令親援大紀，待福康安至，合軍進攻。 旋奏同江寧將軍永慶等在竹篙厝等處殲賊甚眾； 山豬毛社義民尤趫捷，獲砲一，生擒賊目張招。 又奏總兵普吉保克月眉莊，距諸羅五里，令與大紀並力固守； 又令諸生劉宗榮等給番社土目札諭防賊竄匿。 屢得旨嘉許。
In the eighth month Fuk'anggan was made commander-in-chief and led Hailancha, Pu'erpu, and other generals in a major campaign against Shuangwen. The emperor told Chang Qing: "This is not to blame you for failing in command; you are seventy, and warfare has never been your specialty. Until Fuk'anggan arrives you should still press the attack as opportunity allows." He soon reported: "Rebels advanced from Nantan and were beaten back by the guardsman Ushihada and others. After rain made the roads treacherous, the troops were withdrawn; they attacked Nantan again and burned several hundred rebel huts; night fell, the mountain paths were narrow, and a deep pursuit was impracticable." Cao here means the grass huts in which the rebels lived. Because he kept citing rain and bad roads as excuses for pulling back, the emperor sent a stern reprimand. Learning that the rebels had discovered the army's heat-sickness and that Chang Qing was leaky in counsel and still would not drive a deep offensive, the emperor rebuked him again and again. He reported that Commander Liang Chaogui had killed many rebels, that Commander-in-Chief Chai Daji warned that Zhuluo's siege was critical, and that he had sent Deputy Commander Cai Panlong to relieve it. The emperor ordered him to relieve Daji in person and, once Fuk'anggan arrived, to combine forces for a general assault. He soon reported that he and Jiangning General Yongqing had destroyed large numbers of rebels at Zhugecuo and elsewhere; the Shanzhumao militia proved especially agile, one cannon was taken, and rebel leader Zhang Zhao was captured alive. He also reported that Commander Pu Jibao had taken Yuemeizhuang, five li from Zhuluo, and ordered him to hold the line with Daji; and he had licentiate Liu Zongrong and others issue notices to aboriginal village headmen to block rebel hideouts. The emperor commended him repeatedly.
5
福康安渡台灣。 上授常青福州將軍，留辦善後，令從將軍職戴單眼孔雀翎。 福康安劾大紀貪劣狀，上責常青徇隱，奪職，交福康安嚴鞫。 福康安旋以常青自承徇隱，請交部治罪，上特宥之。 召詣京師，署鑲紅旗蒙古都統。 五十四年，授禮部尚書、鑲藍旗漢軍都統。 五十八年，卒，諡恭簡。 子喜明，官至徐州鎮總兵。
Fuk'anggan crossed to Taiwan. The emperor made Chang Qing Fuzhou general, left him to manage the aftermath, and let him keep his commander's rank with the single-eyed peacock feather. When Fuk'anggan impeached Daji for corruption, the emperor blamed Chang Qing for covering for him, stripped his post, and handed him to Fuk'anggan for investigation. Fuk'anggan reported that Chang Qing had confessed to shielding Daji and asked the ministry to punish him, but the emperor pardoned him. He was recalled to the capital and made acting commander of the Mongol Bordered Red Banner. In 1789 he was appointed minister of rites and commander of the Han Bordered Blue Banner. He died in 1793 and was posthumously titled Gongjian, "Respectful and Simple." His son Ximing rose to garrison commander at Xuzhou.
6
常青初視師，福州將軍恆瑞，水陸二提督任承恩、黃仕簡皆在行，戰無功。 承恩、仕簡以誤軍機坐斬，台灣平，赦出獄。 仕簡至狼山鎮總兵，承恩亦至副將，恆瑞自有傳。
When Chang Qing first took command, Fuzhou General Heng Rui and the land and naval commanders Ren Chengen and Huang Shijian were all in the field, yet they won nothing. Chengen and Shijian had been condemned to death for dereliction; after Taiwan was pacified they were pardoned and released. Shijian eventually became Langshan garrison commander and Chengen a deputy commander; Heng Rui has a separate biography.
7
藍元枚，字簡侯，福建漳浦人，提督廷珍孫。 父日寵，官福建銅山營水師參將。 元枚襲三等輕車都尉世職。 乾隆三十一年，命發廣東，以外海水師參將用，補海門營參將。 累遷總兵，歷台灣、金門、蘇松三鎮。 四十九年，授江南提督。 五十二年正月，台灣民林爽文為亂，命元枚馳驛往泉州，署福建陸路提督，駐蚶江策應。 至福州，奏言：「師渡台灣，亂民潰散，慮入內山與生番勾結。 」上諭令速捕治，俾盡根株。 水師提督黃仕簡率兵討爽文，坐逗留奪官，以命元枚，並賜孔雀翎，授參贊，趣率兵渡鹿仔港，會總督常青進討。 六月，元枚率兵次鹿仔港，與總兵普吉保師會，即夜，師分道自柴坑仔、大武隴入，殺賊甚眾。 上嘉之，賜雙眼孔雀翎。
Lan Yuanmei, styled Jianhou, came from Zhangpu in Fujian and was the grandson of Commander Lan Tingzhen. His father Richong had been naval deputy commander of the Tongshan garrison in Fujian. Yuanmei inherited the hereditary rank of third-class commandant of light chariots. In 1766 he was sent to Guangdong as a coastal naval deputy commander and appointed to the Haimen garrison. He rose through the garrison commands at Taiwan, Kinmen, and Susong. In 1784 he was made commander of Jiangnan. In the first month of 1787 Lin Shuangwen rebelled in Taiwan; Yuanmei was rushed to Quanzhou as acting Fujian land commander and stationed at Hanjiang to coordinate the response. At Fuzhou he warned: "Once our forces cross to Taiwan the rebels will scatter, and I fear they may flee into the mountains and ally with the aborigines. The emperor ordered them hunted down quickly so the rebellion could be uprooted entirely. Naval Commander Huang Shijian had been dismissed for delay against Shuangwen; Yuanmei replaced him, received the peacock feather, was made campaign assistant, and was ordered to cross at Lukang Harbor and join Governor-General Chang Qing. In the sixth month Yuanmei reached Lukang Harbor, united with Commander Pu Jibao, and that night split his force through Chaikengzi and Dawulong, killing many rebels. The emperor commended him and granted the double-eyed peacock feather.
8
元枚所將止浙江兵二千，奏請益師，上命總督李侍堯發福建兵二千、廣東兵三千益元枚。 時總兵柴大紀堅守諸羅，元枚使告大紀，期會兵攻鬥六門。 戰阿棟社，戰埤頭莊、大肚溪，屢殺賊。 復進攻西螺，焚條圳塘、中浦厝諸地賊莊。 元枚族人啟能等七十九人自賊中出，使為導。 元枚奏聞，並言如察出啟能等已從賊，當立誅。 上嘉其公當，賜緙絲蟒袍、上佩荷包，並諭：「啟能等既來歸，前此已否從賊，不須追詰。 」諸羅被圍已兩月，大紀屢就告急，上屢趣元枚赴援，諭：「廷珍平硃一貴，七日而事定。 元枚當效法其祖，毋負委任。 」七月，元枚病作。 八月，賊自竹子腳、大肚溪、柴坑仔三道來攻。 元枚力疾出戰，病益劇，越十日，卒於軍，贈太子太保，發白金千兩治喪，賜祭葬，諡襄毅。 元枚諡同廷珍，時稱小襄毅以別之。
Yuanmei had only two thousand Zhejiang troops; he asked for reinforcements, and the emperor ordered Li Shiyao to send two thousand Fujian and three thousand Guangdong troops to his aid. Commander Chai Daji was still holding Zhuluo; Yuanmei sent word arranging a joint attack on Douliumen. He fought at Adongshe, Pitouzhuang, and Dadu Creek, killing rebels again and again. He advanced on Xiluo and burned rebel villages at Tiaozhuntang, Zhongpucuo, and elsewhere. Seventy-nine of Yuanmei's kinsmen, including Qineng, escaped from the rebels and served as guides. Yuanmei reported this and added that if Qineng and the others were found to have served the rebels, they should be executed immediately. The emperor praised his fairness, gave him a brocaded python robe and an imperial purse, and said: "Now that Qineng and the others have surrendered, do not investigate whether they had served the rebels before. Zhuluo had been under siege for two months, and Daji's appeals grew desperate; the emperor repeatedly pressed Yuanmei to relieve the city, reminding him: "Tingzhen put down Zhu Yigui in seven days. Yuanmei should follow his grandfather's example and not betray the trust placed in him." In the seventh month Yuanmei fell ill. In the eighth month the rebels attacked along three routes—from Zhuzijiao, Dadu Creek, and Chaikengzi. Yuanmei dragged himself into battle despite his illness, which then worsened; ten days later he died in camp. He was posthumously made Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, given a thousand taels for his funeral and state burial honors, and titled Xiangyi, "Resolute and Steadfast." His posthumous title matched his grandfather's, and contemporaries called him "the lesser Xiangyi" to distinguish the two.
9
蔡攀龍，福建同安人。 自行伍屢遷至福建澎湖右營游擊。 乾隆五十一年，林爽文為亂，巡撫徐嗣曾檄詣軍。 五十二年，賊破鳳山，總兵柴大紀令督兵捕治。 賊攻台灣府城，攀龍出戰，屢破賊。 賊屯西園莊，攀龍率諸將瑚圖裡、丁朝雄分道攻之，殺賊三百。 賊復攻府城，總督常青令攀龍率諸將孫全謀​​、黃像新等禦戰。 賊乘東、南二門，攀龍等力戰，殺賊數百，奪九節砲。 論功，擢北路協副將，賜孔雀翎。 賊復至，攀龍督戰，复殺賊三百餘，予強勝巴圖魯名號。 七月，常青令攀龍援柴大紀諸羅，上命授海壇鎮總兵。 攀龍師至鹽水港，分八隊以進。 雨大至，賊乘雨合圍，諸將貴林、楊起麟、杭富皆戰死。 會大紀以師來迎，攀龍及全謀兵不及千人，偕運餉民三千人入諸羅，復出城殺賊。 總督李侍堯聞攀龍兵達諸羅，未知貴林等戰死狀，謂諸羅圍已解，入告。 上擢攀龍陸路提督，參贊軍務，貴林、起麟、全謀並遷官。 俄，侍堯复疏陳，上命卹戰死諸將。
Cai Panlong came from Tong'an in Fujian. He rose from the ranks to brigade commander of the Penghu right battalion in Fujian. In 1786, when Lin Shuangwen rebelled, Governor Xu Sizeng ordered him to the front. In 1787 the rebels took Fengshan, and Commander Chai Daji put him in charge of the pursuit. When the rebels assaulted the Taiwan prefectural seat, Panlong took the field and beat them back repeatedly. The rebels held Xiyuanzhuang; Panlong led Hutuli and Ding Chaoxiong in a pincer attack that killed three hundred rebels. When the rebels assaulted the prefectural seat again, Governor-General Chang Qing ordered Panlong to lead Sun Quanmou, Huang Xiangxin, and others in the defense. The rebels struck the east and south gates; Panlong and his men fought hard, killed hundreds of rebels, and seized a nine-section cannon. For his merit he was promoted to deputy commander of the northern route and given the peacock feather. When the rebels returned, Panlong led the fight and killed another three hundred; he received the title Strong Victor Baturu. In the seventh month Chang Qing sent Panlong to relieve Chai Daji at Zhuluo, and the emperor made him Haitan garrison commander. Panlong reached Yanshui Harbor and advanced in eight columns. A downpour came; the rebels used the rain to surround them, and commanders Guilin, Yang Qilin, and Hang Fu were all killed. Daji met them with troops; Panlong and Sun Quanmou had fewer than a thousand men; with three thousand supply porters they entered Zhuluo and sallied out again to strike the rebels. Governor-General Li Shiyao heard Panlong had reached Zhuluo and, unaware that Guilin and the others had been killed, reported the siege lifted. The emperor promoted Panlong to land commander and campaign assistant, and advanced Guilin, Qilin, and Quanmou. Soon Shiyao sent a corrective memorial, and the emperor ordered pensions for the fallen commanders.
10
福康安既解嘉義圍，疏劾大紀，因言攀龍軍嘉義西門外，並無出城殺賊事，自請奪職。 擬請令還海壇本任。 上謂攀龍屢戰有功，其過尚可寬。 五十三年，逮大紀治罪，移攀龍水師提督。 師攻大武隴，令攀龍駐灣里溪。 爽文既擒，其弟勇及賊渠莊大田猶窺伺府城，攻灣里溪，圖斷府城道。 福康安遣攀龍分道進攻，頗有斬獲。 事平，圖形紫光閣，列前二十功臣，上自為贊，許為台灣戰將中巨擘。 師還，諸將言攀龍平庸，福康安亦言未能勝任，左遷江南狼山鎮總兵。 嘉慶三年，卒。
After Fuk'anggan relieved Jiayi, he impeached Daji and noted that Panlong's troops had camped outside Jiayi's west gate with no record of sallying out to kill rebels; Panlong asked to be stripped of rank. The court proposed sending him back to his Haitan post. The emperor said Panlong had fought well repeatedly and his fault could be overlooked. In 1788 Daji was arrested for trial, and Panlong was made naval commander. During the assault on Dawulong, Panlong was stationed at Wanlixi. After Shuangwen's capture, his brother Yong and rebel chief Zhuang Datian still menaced the prefectural seat, attacked Wanlixi, and tried to sever its supply route. Fuk'anggan sent Panlong on divided attacks that yielded many kills and captures. After the rebellion ended his portrait joined the Hall of Purple Splendor's top twenty heroes; the emperor wrote his encomium himself and hailed him as the greatest of Taiwan's battlefield generals. After the army withdrew, other generals called Panlong mediocre; Fuk'anggan agreed he was unfit for high command, and he was demoted to Langshan garrison commander in Jiangnan. He died in 1798.
11
梁朝桂，甘肅中衛人。 乾隆三十七年，以中衛營外委從征金川，先後攻克路頂宗、布朗郭宗及功噶爾拉、丫口、昔嶺、阿喀木雅。 三十九年，克淜普，進攻喇穆喇穆山梁，奪日丫口。 四十年，剿勒吉爾博寨，先登被創。 四月，攻木思工噶克山，潛師入，盡克其城碉，據康薩爾至丫口山。 十月，克西里山。 錄功，賜孔雀翎。 累遷陝西潼關協副將。 金川平，列五十功臣，圖形紫光閣。 累遷甘肅肅州鎮總兵。 坐事罷。 復起，自福建福寧鎮移廣東高廉鎮。
Liang Chaogui came from Zhongwei in Gansu. In 1772, as a Zhongwei garrison agent, he joined the Jinchuan campaign and took Ludingzong, Bulangguozong, Gongga'erla, Yakou, Xiling, and Akemuya in turn. In 1774 he captured Qinpu, assaulted the Lamulamu ridge, and took Riyakou. In 1775 he stormed the Leji'erbo stockade, was first over the wall, and was wounded. In the fourth month he attacked Musigonggake Mountain with a stealth column, captured all its fortifications, and held the line from Kangsar to Yakoushan. In the tenth month he captured Xilishan. His merits were recorded and he received the peacock feather. He was promoted to deputy commander of the Tongguan command in Shaanxi. After Jinchuan was pacified he ranked among the fifty heroes and his portrait entered the Hall of Purple Splendor. He became garrison commander at Suzhou in Gansu. He was dismissed for misconduct. He was reappointed, moving from Funing in Fujian to Gaolian in Guangdong.
12
五十二年，台灣林爽文為亂，莊大田應之，別為南路賊。 朝桂率兵敗大田於蔦松，斬馘二百餘。 賊眾數千犯大營，擊卻之，斃賊三百。 將軍常青慮南路賊北擾諸羅，檄朝桂堵禦，連敗之南潭、中洲、十三里莊，殲數百人。 九月，常青移師北路剿爽文，以朝桂守台灣府城，賊來犯，擊走之。 其冬，援參贊恆瑞於鹽水港，毀賊藔，賜號奮勇巴圖魯； 復同恆瑞自鹿仔草進剿鎮平莊，受創，力戰敗賊。 時提督柴大紀被圍諸羅急，朝桂欲馳援，恆瑞不聽，大紀以聞，帝令將軍福康安察奏。 會福康安抵鹿仔港，檄朝桂仍駐守鹽水港及鹿仔草。
In 1787 Lin Shuangwen rebelled in Taiwan; Zhuang Datian joined him and led a separate southern rebel force. Chaogui defeated Datian at Niaosong, killing more than two hundred rebels. When several thousand rebels assaulted the main camp, he drove them off and killed three hundred. Fearing southern rebels would threaten Zhuluo from the north, Chang Qing ordered Chaogui to hold them; he beat them repeatedly at Nantan, Zhongzhou, and Shisanlizhuang, killing hundreds. In the ninth month Chang Qing marched north against Shuangwen and left Chaogui to defend the Taiwan prefectural seat; when rebels attacked, Chaogui repulsed them. That winter he reinforced Assistant Commander Heng Rui at Yanshui Harbor, burned rebel huts, and received the title Valiant Baturu; he joined Heng Rui again in an advance from Luzicao on Zhenpingzhuang, was wounded, and fought through to defeat the rebels. Commander-in-Chief Chai Daji was desperately besieged at Zhuluo; Chaogui wanted to relieve him, but Heng Rui refused; Daji reported this, and the emperor ordered Fuk'anggan to investigate. When Fuk'anggan reached Lukang Harbor he ordered Chaogui to hold Yanshui Harbor and Luzicao.
13
五十三年春，就擢福建陸路提督。 檄剿麻豆莊、大武隴屯賊，通郡城要道。 大田時據大武隴拒守，朝桂自茅港尾繞至阿里港迎截； 复赴打狗、竹仔各港口截其走路。 大田力不支，自牛莊竄極南之郎嶠，負山阻海。 福康安自風港進至柴城，分六隊直逼海岸，與朝桂環攻之，大田及他賊目四十餘悉就擒。 台灣平，再圖形紫光閣。 金門巡洋艦被劫，以朝桂不能戢盜，移廣西。 再移湖廣。 卒。
In the spring of 1788 he was promoted to Fujian land commander. He was ordered to clear rebels from Madouzhuang and the Dawulong garrison and reopen the route to the prefectural seat. Datian was holding Dawulong; Chaogui swung from Maogangwei to Ali Harbor to cut him off; he also blocked escape routes at the ports of Dagou and Zhuzai. Overmatched, Datian fled from Niuzhuang to remote Langqiao in the far south, backed against mountains and sea. Fuk'anggan advanced from Fenggang to Chaicheng, sent six columns to the coast, and with Chaogui encircled them; Datian and more than forty rebel chiefs were captured. After Taiwan was pacified his portrait again entered the Hall of Purple Splendor. When a Kinmen patrol ship was seized by pirates, Chaogui was blamed for failing to control banditry and transferred to Guangxi. He was later transferred to Huguang. He died.
14
普吉保，札庫塔氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 乾隆三十年，以藍翎侍衛從軍徵烏什，有功，補三等侍衛。 三十七年，從參贊大臣舒常攻日旁，有功。 三十九年，從副將軍豐升額攻凱立葉山，進抵迪噶拉穆札山。 賊分三隊，普吉保偕侍衛瑪爾占等夾攻，斃賊無算，賜衝捷巴圖魯名號。 四十年，攻噶爾丹寺諸地，連破木城、石碉。 上獎普吉保勇往，累擢福建汀州鎮總兵。 林爽文為亂，總督常青檄普吉保會剿，五十二年，率水師渡台灣，迭破賊鹿仔港、八卦山，上嘉其奮勉。 爽文見師至，退守鬥六門、大里杙。 普吉保以師進，爽文攻諸羅，赴援，抵笨港，率游擊海亮等殲賊數百，毀賊莊七，得旨嘉獎，賜玉搬指、荷包、蟒袍。 笨港潰賊糾眾截我兵，普吉保擊斬甚眾。 嗣以駐兵元長莊、月眉莊不進，旨嚴飭。 尋攻大埔林，收復鬥六門。 爽文竄內山，普吉保從諸將徒步陟山搜捕。 五十三年，以兵扼科仔坑口，合圍，俘爽文。 南路莊大田亦就擒。 台灣平，圖形紫光閣。 普吉保初克鹿仔港，以福康安疏薦，授台灣總兵。 明年，上念台灣初定，慮普吉保不能勝，命解任。 尋授廣西左江鎮，坐責把總黎振乾投水死，戍伊犁。 卒。
Pu Jibao, of the Zhakuta clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain Yellow Banner. In 1765, as a blue-feather guardsman, he joined the campaign against Ushi, distinguished himself, and was made a third-rank guardsman. In 1772 he followed Assistant Minister Shu Chang against Ripang and distinguished himself. In 1774 he followed Deputy General Feng Sheng'e against Kailiye Mountain and reached Digalamuzha Mountain. The rebels split into three columns; Pu Jibao and Guardsman Ma'erzhan attacked from both flanks and killed countless rebels; he received the title Swift Assault Baturu. In 1775 he attacked the Galdan Temple region and stormed wooden walls and stone blockhouses in succession. The emperor praised his boldness and promoted him to Tingzhou garrison commander in Fujian. When Lin Shuangwen rebelled, Chang Qing summoned Pu Jibao to the campaign; in 1787 he led the navy to Taiwan, took Lukang Harbor and Baguashan in succession, and won the emperor's praise for his vigor. Seeing the army arrive, Shuangwen withdrew to Douliumen and Dali. Pu Jibao advanced; when Shuangwen besieged Zhuluo he marched to relieve the city, reached Bengang, and with Brigade Commander Hailiang destroyed hundreds of rebels and seven rebel villages; the emperor commended him and gave him a jade thumb ring, purse, and python robe. Routed rebels at Bengang rallied to ambush government troops; Pu Jibao killed many of them. Later he was sternly rebuked for camping at Yuanchangzhuang and Yuemeizhuang without advancing. He soon attacked Dapulin and recovered Douliumen. Shuangwen fled into the mountains; Pu Jibao joined the other generals in climbing on foot to hunt him down. In 1788 his troops sealed Kezikengkou, joined the encirclement, and captured Shuangwen. The southern rebel chief Zhuang Datian was captured as well. After Taiwan was pacified his portrait entered the Hall of Purple Splendor. After Pu Jibao first took Lukang Harbor, Fuk'anggan recommended him and he was made Taiwan garrison commander. The next year, fearing Pu Jibao was unequal to governing newly pacified Taiwan, the emperor removed him from office. He was soon appointed to the Zuojiang garrison in Guangxi, but when a corporal under his command drowned himself he was exiled to Yili. He died.
15
丁朝雄，字伯宜，江蘇通州人。 自行伍累擢福建台灣水師副將。 乾隆五十一年，以任滿赴部引見，至省城，聞林爽文亂起。 朝雄策東港與鳳山犄角，爽文所必爭，白總督常青，請兵屯東港，斷其糧道。 常青不能用，遣朝雄還台灣，佐海壇鎮總兵郝壯猷討爽文。
Ding Chaoxiong, styled Boyi, came from Tongzhou in Jiangsu. He rose from the ranks to deputy commander of the Taiwan naval force in Fujian. In 1786, at the end of his term, he went to the capital for audience; at the provincial seat he learned Lin Shuangwen had rebelled. Chaoxiong argued that Donggang and Fengshan formed a strategic pair Shuangwen was bound to contest, told Chang Qing, and asked for troops at Donggang to sever rebel supplies. Chang Qing rejected the plan and sent Chaoxiong back to Taiwan to assist Haitan Garrison Commander Hao Zhuangyou against Shuangwen.
16
五十二年春，壯猷偕朝雄率兵二千餘擊賊，馘三百，俘二十五。 日將暮，賊復來攻，朝雄复殺賊百餘，賊始去。 攻鳳山，朝雄乘東門，首諸軍入，鳳山遂復。 黃仕簡檄朝雄守安平海口。 賊攻府城，朝雄偕知府楊廷樺督兵民力禦。 賊攻桶盤棧，朝雄為前鋒，出戰，台灣道永福、同知楊廷理率兵民繼，复殺賊百餘，賊敗走。 冬，朝雄偕游擊倪賓率兵千二百、義民二千餘攻東港。 東港賊數万，其渠吳豹以海岸淺，度舟不能至，不為備。 朝雄遣諜以水注賊砲，乘雨至水漲，遣兵民分道登岸殺賊，俘豹。 以兵寡不能克，報常青請益兵。 常青令駐港口護餉道。 既，令攻竹仔港，毀賊舟。
In the spring of 1787 Zhuangyou and Chaoxiong led more than two thousand men against the rebels, killing three hundred and capturing twenty-five. At dusk the rebels attacked again; Chaoxiong killed another hundred and they withdrew. In the assault on Fengshan, Chaoxiong took the east gate and led the army in; Fengshan was recovered. Huang Shijian ordered Chaoxiong to guard the Anping harbor mouth. When the rebels assaulted the prefectural seat, Chaoxiong and Prefect Yang Tinghua led troops and militia in a hard defense. When rebels attacked Tongpan Stockade, Chaoxiong led the vanguard; Intendant Yongfu and Subprefect Yang Tingli followed with troops and militia; they killed another hundred rebels and drove them off. That winter Chaoxiong and Brigade Commander Ni Bin led twelve hundred troops and more than two thousand militiamen against Donggang. Tens of thousands of rebels held Donggang; their chief Wu Bao, thinking the shallow coast would keep boats away, made no preparations. Chaoxiong sent agents to flood the rebel guns with water; when rain raised the water level he landed troops and militia along several routes, killed rebels, and captured Bao. Too few to finish the job, he asked Chang Qing for reinforcements. Chang Qing ordered him to hold the harbor and protect the supply line. He was later ordered to attack Zhuzai Harbor and destroy rebel boats.
17
五十三年春，復攻東港，仍遣諜以水注賊砲，督兵攻渡口，賊驚竄，逐三十餘裡，乃倚山而軍。 賊夜來犯，朝雄戒勿動； 及曉，賊倦，掩擊，大破之。 爽文遣其徒來援，朝雄築壘困之。 賊潰圍出，設伏斷其歸路，而自將追之，大破賊，遂復東港。 福康安上其功，授海壇鎮總兵。 既，福康安劾柴大紀受陋規，言朝雄為安平協副將時亦有此，當奪職戍軍台，上以朝雄攻東港戰有功，命留任。 林鬎舵、林明灼者，海盜渠也，五十四年，朝雄巡洋至汜澳，破盜巢，得鬎舵等； 而明灼拒殺參將張殿魁。 上責總督伍拉納，伍拉納以屬朝雄，督舟師出海，遇諸大麥洋，俟其近，發大砲，斃數酋，明灼窮蹙，躍入海，官軍鉤致，俘以歸。
In the spring of 1788 he attacked Donggang again, again flooded the rebel guns, stormed the crossing, routed the rebels for more than thirty li, and encamped against the hills. Rebels attacked by night; Chaoxiong ordered his men to hold still; at dawn, when the rebels were exhausted, he struck from ambush and routed them. Shuangwen sent reinforcements; Chaoxiong built stockades to trap them. The rebels broke out; he ambushed their retreat while leading the pursuit himself, crushed them, and recovered Donggang. Fuk'anggan reported his merits, and he was made Haitan garrison commander. Later Fuk'anggan impeached Chai Daji for taking petty bribes and said Chaoxiong had done the same as Anping deputy commander and should be stripped and exiled; the emperor kept him in office for his merits at Donggang. Pirate chiefs Lin Huduo and Lin Mingzhuo were active in 1789 when Chaoxiong on patrol reached Xiao'ao, destroyed their base, and captured Huduo; but Mingzhuo resisted and killed Deputy Commander Zhang Diankui. The emperor rebuked Governor-General Wulana, who put Chaoxiong in charge; Chaoxiong took the fleet to Zhudamaiyang, waited until the pirates closed, opened fire with heavy guns, and killed several chiefs; cornered, Mingzhuo leaped into the sea, was hooked up by government troops, and brought back captive.
18
五十五年，追論朝雄在台灣失察天地會邪教，當奪職； 上諮伍拉納朝雄在官狀，伍拉納言朝雄督水師捕盜有勞，命還任。 五十八年，攝水師提督。 五十九年，入覲，至清江浦，病篤。 乞罷歸，卒於上海舟中。
In 1790 he was retrospectively blamed for failing to detect the Heaven and Earth Society in Taiwan and was to be stripped of office; The emperor asked Wulana about Chaoxiong's conduct; Wulana said he had merit hunting pirates and ordered him restored to office. In 1793 he served as acting naval commander. In 1794, on his way to audience, he fell gravely ill at Qingjiangpu. He asked to resign and return home, and died aboard his boat at Shanghai.
19
鄂輝，碧魯氏，滿洲正白旗人。 自前鋒分發四川試用守備。 七遷建昌鎮總兵。 從大學士阿桂定蘭州回亂，予法什尚阿巴圖魯名號。 再遷成都將軍。 乾隆五十二年，署四川總督。 將軍福康安討台灣亂民林爽文，上命鄂輝率四川屯練降番濟師。 尋授參贊，從渡海援嘉義。 鄂輝屯東莊溪橋，攻克牛稠山竹柵，嘉義圍解。 逐賊至大排竹，殲之。 師攻鬥六門，賊自山下撲，鄂輝督兵衝截，賊奔逸，攻克大埔林、大埔尾二莊，賊潰。 爽文自所居大里杙奔內山番界，鄂輝逐之至集埔。 五十三年春，诇知爽文所匿地曰東勢角，福康安督鄂輝及舒亮追捕，自歸仔頭至麻著社，分軍，鄂輝自撲仔離東山路進，舒亮直取東勢角。 是役遂俘爽文，亂乃定。 上命台灣嘉義立諸將帥生祠，鄂輝與焉。 師還，圖形紫光閣，賜雙眼孔雀翎、雲騎尉世職。 鄂輝朝熱河行在。
E Hui, of the Bilu clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain White Banner. From the vanguard he was posted to Sichuan as a probationary garrison commandant. After seven promotions he became Jianchang garrison commander. He followed Grand Secretary Agui in suppressing the Lanzhou Muslim rebellion and received the title Fashishang'a Baturu. He was then promoted to Chengdu general. In 1787 he served as acting governor-general of Sichuan. When General Fuk'anggan campaigned against Lin Shuangwen in Taiwan, the emperor ordered E Hui to lead Sichuan militia and surrendered tribesmen as reinforcements. He was soon made campaign assistant and crossed the strait to relieve Jiayi. E Hui held Dongzhuangxi Bridge, took the Niuchoushan bamboo stockade, and lifted the siege of Jiayi. He pursued the rebels to Dapaizhu and annihilated them. During the assault on Douliumen rebels rushed down from the hills; E Hui led a countercharge, routed them, captured Dapulin and Dapuwei, and broke the rebel force. Shuangwen fled from Dali into the aboriginal mountains; E Hui pursued him to Jipu. In the spring of 1788 scouts found Shuangwen hiding at Dongshijiao; Fuk'anggan sent E Hui and Shu Liang after him; from Guizaitou to Mazheshe they split forces—E Hui took the eastern route from Puzaili while Shu Liang struck Dongshijiao directly. In that campaign Shuangwen was captured and the rebellion ended. The emperor ordered living shrines to the generals built at Jiayi in Taiwan; E Hui was included. After the army withdrew his portrait entered the Hall of Purple Splendor; he received the double-eyed peacock feather and the hereditary rank of Cloud Cavalry Captain. E Hui attended the emperor at the Rehe traveling palace.
20
廓爾喀侵西藏，據濟嚨、聶拉木諸地。 上促鄂輝還四川，與提督成德帥師赴援，又命侍郎巴忠往按。 巴忠先嘗為駐藏大臣，習藏事，示意噶布倫，令賂廓爾喀返侵地。 鄂輝等遂與議和，疏陳善後事。 尋授四川總督。 五十六年，廓爾喀渝盟，复侵濟嚨、聶拉木諸地。 上命將軍福康安督師討廓爾喀，責鄂輝誤用巴忠議致復生事，奪官，予副都統銜駐藏，聽福康安指揮，福康安令督餉。 工部尚書和琳劾鄂輝得廓爾喀貢表不以上聞，命奪副都統銜，逮赴前藏荷校示罰。 五十八年，命還京師，授拜唐阿。 加員外郎銜，遷熱河總管。
The Gurkhas invaded Tibet and seized Jilong, Nyalam, and other territories. The emperor pressed E Hui to return to Sichuan; he and Commander Cheng De led troops to relieve Tibet, and Vice Minister Ba Zhong was sent to investigate. Ba Zhong, a former Tibetan resident minister, instructed the kaloons to bribe the Gurkhas into returning the seized lands. E Hui and his colleagues then made peace and memorialized on the aftermath. He was soon appointed governor-general of Sichuan. In 1791 the Gurkhas broke the treaty and again invaded Jilong, Nyalam, and neighboring areas. The emperor sent General Fuk'anggan against the Gurkhas, blamed E Hui for following Ba Zhong's bribe scheme and letting the crisis recur, stripped his rank, sent him to Tibet as vice commander under Fuk'anggan, and put him in charge of supplies. Minister of Works Helin impeached E Hui for concealing Gurkha tribute memorials; he was stripped of vice commander rank, arrested, sent to front Tibet, and punished with the cangue. In 1793 he was recalled to the capital and appointed a baitangga attendant. He received vice-director rank and became superintendent of Rehe.
21
嘉慶初，命以侍衛詣荊州從剿教匪，戰有功，以都統銜加太子少保，授湖南提督。 屢破賊，與額勒登保等攻克石隆山，斬賊渠石柳鄧，封三等男。 二年，擢雲貴總督。 三年，卒，諡恪靖，祀賢良祠。 四年，追論在湖北軍中受餽白金四千，罷祀。
Early in the Jiaqing reign he was sent as a guardsman to Jingzhou against the White Lotus rebels, distinguished himself, was made commander-in-chief with Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent, and appointed Hunan commander. He beat the rebels repeatedly; with Eldengbao he took Shilong Mountain and killed rebel chief Shi Liudeng, and was enfeoffed as a third-class baron. In the second year he was promoted to governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou. He died in the third year, was posthumously titled Kejing, "Respectful and Tranquil," and enshrined in the Hall of Worthy Officials. In the fourth year he was posthumously condemned for accepting four thousand taels of silver in the Hubei army, and his shrine honors were revoked.
22
舒亮，蘇佳氏，滿洲正白旗人。 自前鋒累遷參領。 師徵金川，舒亮從副都統齊里克齊率健銳營為裨將。 攻穆谷，舒亮伏山下待賊，殺賊甚眾。 攻卡角，賊匿山溝，舒亮於密箐中望見火光，以火器就擊之，賊驚潰。 以功，累遷鑲黃旗滿洲副都統。 從克噶拉依，賜穆騰額巴圖魯名號。 師還，圖形紫光閣。 乾隆四十六年，大學士阿桂討撒拉爾亂回蘇四十三，舒亮從。 初至，破賊華林山。 賊掘濠設卡以自固。 阿桂令海蘭察自山西攻賊卡，舒亮自南山進，當賊鋒，賊競出，射舒亮，傷左股，舒亮拔箭裹創，复戰，奪賊卡四，殺賊百餘。 又與海蘭察诇賊不備，以土囊填濠渡軍，殲守濠賊，复奪十餘卡。 蘇四十三既誅，复剿華林寺餘匪。 事平，還京師。
Shu Liang, of the Sujia clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain White Banner. He rose from the vanguard to company commander. In the Jinchuan campaign Shu Liang served under Vice Commander Qilikeqi as deputy leader of the Vanguard Camp. At Mugu he ambushed the rebels below the mountain and killed many. At Kajiao rebels hid in ravines; Shu Liang spotted firelight in a thicket, fired into it, and routed them. For his merits he was promoted to vice commander of the Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner. He helped capture Gela'i and received the title Muteng'e Baturu. After the campaign his portrait entered the Hall of Purple Splendor. In 1781 Grand Secretary Agui suppressed the Salar rebel Su Forty-three; Shu Liang followed him. On arrival he defeated the rebels at Hualin Mountain. The rebels dug moats and set checkpoints to hold their ground. Agui sent Hailancha against the rebel checkpoints from the west while Shu Liang advanced from the south and took the brunt; rebels shot him in the left thigh; he pulled out the arrow, bound the wound, fought on, took four checkpoints, and killed more than a hundred rebels. With Hailancha he caught the rebels off guard, filled the moat with earth bags to cross, wiped out the moat guards, and took more than ten checkpoints. After Su Forty-three was killed he mopped up the remnants at Hualin Temple. When the rebellion ended he returned to the capital.
23
林爽文之亂，福康安出視師，舒亮以正黃旗護軍統領為領隊大臣。 至台灣，福康安軍道笨港救嘉義，令舒亮出別道分賊勢。 賊方據北大肚山拒我，舒亮迎擊，敗之，連破南大肚、王田、瀨湑、半山、坑子諸莊，遂克烏日莊。 會福康安軍夾擊，解嘉義圍。 五十三年，爽文竄匿東勢角。 福康安督舒亮等追逮，令舒亮直取東勢角，山徑峻險，將卒皆步上，殺賊二千餘。 爽文复走老衢峙，舒亮督諸軍急進，獲之，亂遂定。
During Lin Shuangwen's rebellion Fuk'anggan took command, and Shu Liang served as leading minister as commander of the Plain Yellow Banner guards. In Taiwan Fuk'anggan marched through Bengang to relieve Jiayi and sent Shu Liang by a separate route to split the rebel force. Rebels held Beidadu Mountain; Shu Liang attacked, defeated them, took Nandadu, Wangtian, Laixu, Banshan, Kengzi, and other villages in succession, and captured Wuri Village. Joining Fuk'anggan in a pincer attack, they lifted the siege of Jiayi. In 1788 Shuangwen hid at Dongshijiao. Fuk'anggan sent Shu Liang and others in pursuit and ordered Shu Liang to take Dongshijiao directly; on steep mountain paths the troops climbed on foot and killed more than two thousand rebels. Shuangwen fled again to Laoquzhi; Shu Liang pressed forward with the armies and captured him, ending the rebellion.
24
上以台灣遠在海外，主客民雜處，風俗素悍，命於府城及嘉義立諸將帥生祠，示威德。 祠成，命並及在事疆吏，首福康安，次海蘭察、李侍堯、普爾普、鄂輝、徐嗣曾，而以舒亮殿焉。 尋授鑲紅旗蒙古都統。 師還，命監爽文及其徒賴大等生致京師。 賴大道病，舒亮令誅之，不稱上意，命仍為護軍統領。 敘功，予雲騎尉世職，再圖形紫光閣。 出為荊州、黑龍江將軍。 在黑龍江，坐私市貂皮，奪官，削世職。
Because Taiwan lay far overseas, settlers and natives were mixed, and the region was notoriously unruly, the emperor ordered living shrines to the generals built at the prefectural seat and Jiayi to display imperial power and virtue. When the shrines were finished he included the frontier officials involved: Fuk'anggan first, then Hailancha, Li Shiyao, Pu'erpu, E Hui, and Xu Sizeng, with Shu Liang last. He was soon appointed commander of the Mongol Bordered Red Banner. On the army's return he was ordered to escort Shuangwen and his follower Lai Da alive to the capital. Lai Da fell ill en route; Shu Liang had him executed, displeasing the emperor, and was kept on as guards commander. His merits were recorded; he received the hereditary rank of Cloud Cavalry Captain and again entered the Hall of Purple Splendor. He served as general at Jingzhou and Heilongjiang. At Heilongjiang he was dismissed for illegal sable trade and lost his hereditary rank.
25
川、陝、楚教匪起，命以三等侍衛從軍。 嘉慶元年，戰襄陽，再戰劉家集，屢俘斬賊渠。 攻當陽，先登，額中槍，奮進，殺賊千餘，獲其酋，遂克當陽，賜孔雀翎，授鑲藍旗漢軍副都統。 賊自鍾祥分竄唐、鄧，設伏呂堰驛，西竄賊殲焉； 乃合兵逐東竄賊，戰草店，復中槍，賚銀絲盒、荷包。 旋以縱賊渡滾河，奪孔雀翎、巴圖魯。 二年，坐賊渡漢江，降三品頂戴。 三年，復以總督勒保劾勦賊不力，奪官，以兵丁留軍。 尋卒。
When White Lotus rebels rose in Sichuan, Shaanxi, and Hubei he was sent to the army as a third-rank guardsman. In the first year of Jiaqing he fought at Xiangyang and Liujiaji and repeatedly captured or killed rebel chiefs. At Dangyang he was first over the wall; shot in the forehead, he pressed on, killed more than a thousand rebels, captured their chief, and took the city; he received the peacock feather and was made vice commander of the Han Bordered Blue Banner. Rebels fleeing from Zhongxiang split toward Tang and Deng; he ambushed them at Luyan Post and destroyed the western band; he then pursued the eastern band, fought at Caodian, was shot again, and received a silver-thread box and purse. Soon he lost his peacock feather and Baturu title for letting rebels cross the Gun River. In the second year rebels crossed the Han River, and he was demoted to third-rank insignia. In the third year Governor-General Lebao impeached him for slack pursuit of rebels; he was stripped of rank and kept in the army as a common soldier. He soon died.
26
論曰：林爽文亂起，常青及福州將軍恆瑞並水陸二提督，躬率師東渡，徘徊坐誤。 高宗爵柴大紀，誅郝壯猷，欲以激勵諸將； 繼以元枚代，功未竟而卒，終煩禁旅，始克底定。 承平久，水陸諸軍不足用，不得獨為大紀罪也。 鄂輝、舒亮從福康安出師，與攀龍、朝雄皆有戰績； 然大紀力保危城，當時聲譽遠出諸將上。 功名之際，有幸有不幸，固如是夫！
The commentators observe: When Lin Shuangwen rebelled, Chang Qing, Fuzhou General Heng Rui, and the land and naval commanders crossed to Taiwan in person yet dithered and let the crisis worsen. Emperor Gaozong ennobled Chai Daji and executed Hao Zhuangyou to spur the other generals; Lan Yuanmei replaced him but died before finishing the work; only when imperial guards arrived was the rebellion finally suppressed. After long peace the land and naval forces had grown inadequate; Daji alone cannot bear all the blame. E Hui and Shu Liang campaigned under Fuk'anggan, as did Panlong and Chaoxiong, all with solid records; yet Daji had held a desperate city through sheer effort, and at the time his reputation far outshone the other generals. In the pursuit of fame and merit, fortune and misfortune go hand in hand—such is the way of things!
