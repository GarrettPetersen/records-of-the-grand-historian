First-rank marquisIrgen (Manchu Plain Yellow Banner). For accumulated military merit he was repeatedly raised to second-rank angban zhangjing; in the third month of the seventh year of Shunzhi an amnesty decree granted perpetual succession, and in the fifth month military merit raised him to first rank. In the first month of the ninth year an amnesty decree made him third-rank marquis; he was demoted to first-rank earl for an offense, then by amnesty decree from first-rank earl gained one tuo shala ha fan before being stripped again. In the ninth month of the fourth year he recovered his title for military merit and was soon promoted to first-rank marquis with another tuo shala ha fan; in present Chinese usage this became first-rank marquis, also styled one imperial bodyguard. He died; posthumous name Tingmin.Bahuntai (son of Irgen). Madegong (Chinese Bordered Yellow Banner).
