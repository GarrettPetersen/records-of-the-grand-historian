曾國籓
Zeng Guofan
3
曾國籓，初名子城，字滌生，湖南湘鄉人。 家世農。 祖玉屏，始慕鄉學。 父麟書，為縣學生，以孝聞。
Zeng Guofan, born Zicheng and styled Disheng, came from Xiangxiang in Hunan. For generations his family had been farmers. His grandfather Yuping was the first in the line to take up scholarly study in the countryside. His father Linshu held county student status and was known for his filial devotion.
4
國籓，道光十八年進士。 二十三年，以檢討典試四川，再轉侍讀，累遷內閣學士、禮部侍郎，署兵部。 時太常寺卿唐鑑講學京師，國籓與倭仁、吳廷棟、何桂珍嚴事之，治義理之學。 兼友梅曾亮及邵懿辰、劉傳瑩諸人，為詞章考據，尤留心天下人材。
Guofan received his jinshi degree in the eighteenth year of the Daoguang reign. In the twenty-third year he served as reviser and chief examiner in Sichuan, then became reader-in-waiting, and rose through Grand Secretariat academician and Vice Minister of Rites to acting Minister of War. Tang Jian of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices was then lecturing in the capital, and Guofan, together with Woren, Wu Tingdong, and He Guizhen, attended him devotedly and pursued the study of moral principle. He also kept company with Mei Zengliang, Shao Yichen, Liu Chuanying, and others in literary and evidential work, while keeping a keen eye on talent across the empire.
5
咸豐初，廣西兵事起，詔群臣言得失。 奏陳今日急務，首在用人，人才有轉移之道，有培養之方，有考察之法。 上稱其剴切明辨。 尋疏薦李棠階、吳廷棟、王慶雲、嚴正基、江忠源五人。 寇氛益熾，复上言：「國用不足，兵伍不精，二者為天下大患。 於歲入常額外，誠不可別求搜刮之術，增一分則民受一分之害。 至歲出之數，兵餉為鉅，綠營兵額六十四萬，常虛六七萬以資給軍用。 自乾隆中增兵議起，歲糜帑二百餘萬。 其時大學士阿桂即憂其難繼，嘉、道間兩次議裁，不及十之四，仍宜汰五萬，復舊額。 自古開國之初，兵少而國強，其後兵愈多則力愈弱，餉愈多則國愈貧。 應請皇上註意將才，但使七十一鎮中有十餘鎮足為心腹，則緩急可恃矣。 」又深痛內外臣工諂諛欺飾，無陳善責難之風。 因上敬陳聖德預防流弊一疏，切指帝躬，有人所難言者，上優詔答之。 歷署刑部、吏部侍郎。 二年，典試江西，中途丁母憂歸。
Early in the Xianfeng reign, fighting broke out in Guangxi, and the court called on the ministers to address what had gone right and wrong. In his memorial he argued that the day’s most pressing need was the proper use of men—that talent must be shifted, cultivated, and examined by deliberate methods. The emperor praised the memorial as earnest, sharp, and clear. He soon followed with a memorial recommending Li Tangjie, Wu Tingdong, Wang Qingyun, Yan Zhengji, and Jiang Zhongyuan. As the rebellion intensified, he wrote again: "Revenue is too thin and the troops are too slack—these are the empire’s gravest ills. Beyond ordinary revenue there must be no new squeezing; every extra levy is an equal wound to the people. Of yearly outlays, military pay is the largest; the Green Standard rolls claim six hundred forty thousand men, yet sixty or seventy thousand slots are routinely left empty to pad military funds. Since the mid-Qianlong push to enlarge the armies, more than two million taels a year have been drained from the treasury. Grand Secretary Agui had already warned the burden could not last; under Jiaqing and Daoguang reductions were debated twice yet cut less than four-tenths—five tens of thousands should still be struck to restore the old rolls. At every dynasty’s founding, few soldiers meant a strong state; later, more soldiers meant weaker armies and more pay meant a poorer treasury. He urged the emperor to prize real commanders: if even a dozen of the seventy-one garrisons were trustworthy core forces, the court could count on them in an emergency. " He also lamented that court and province alike had sunk into flattery and concealment, with none of the old spirit of frank counsel and mutual reproof. He therefore submitted a memorial on imperial virtue and the prevention of abuses, speaking bluntly to the throne where others would not; the emperor replied with a gracious edict. He served in turn as acting Vice Minister of Justice and of Personnel. In the second year he was appointed chief examiner in Jiangxi, but on the road he learned of his mother’s death and returned home in mourning.
6
三年，粵寇破江寧，據為偽都，分黨北犯河南、直隸，天下騷動，而國籓已前奉旨辦團練於長沙。 初，國籓欲疏請終制，郭嵩燾曰：「公素具澄清之抱，今不乘時自效，如君父何？ 且墨絰從戎，古制也。 」遂不復辭。 取明戚繼光遺法，募農民樸實壯健者，朝夕訓練之。 將領率用諸生，統眾數不逾五百，號「湘勇」。 騰書遐邇，雖卑賤與鈞禮。 山野材智之士感其誠，莫不往見，人人皆以曾公可與言事。 四境土匪發，聞警即以湘勇往。 立三等法，不以煩府縣獄。 旬月中，莠民猾胥，便宜捕斬二百餘人。 謗讟四起，自巡撫司道下皆心誹之，至以盛暑練操為虐士。 然見所奏輒得褒答受主知，未有以難也。 一日標兵與湘勇閧，至闌入國籓行台。 國籓親訴諸巡撫，巡撫漫謝之，不為理，即日移營城外避標兵。 或曰：「曷以聞？ 」國籓歎曰：「大難未已，吾人敢以私憤瀆君父乎？ 」
In the third year the Taiping rebels seized Jiangning as their capital, sent columns north into Henan and Zhili, and threw the empire into turmoil—while Guofan had already been ordered to raise militia at Changsha. At first Guofan meant to ask leave to finish mourning, but Guo Songtao said: "You have always wanted to set the realm right—if you will not act now, what becomes of your duty to sovereign and father? And taking the field in unbleached hemp is an ancient precedent. " With that he did not refuse again. He followed Qi Jiguang’s methods from the Ming and recruited plain, sturdy peasants, drilling them from dawn to dusk. He put literati in command, kept units under five hundred men, and called them the Xiang Braves. He wrote to men everywhere and treated even the humble as equals. Able men from the hills and countryside, stirred by his sincerity, came to call; all believed they could speak frankly with Lord Zeng. When local bandits flared up, he dispatched the Xiang Braves at the first report. He set up a three-tier code and kept cases out of the regular courts. Within a month he had seized and executed more than two hundred rogues and corrupt clerks on summary authority. Abuse spread everywhere; from the governor and intendants downward, officials seethed in private, some calling summer drill in the heat a torment to the men. Yet his memorials kept winning praise and imperial favor, and none dared openly stand in his way. One day the regular garrison fought the Xiang Braves and broke into Guofan’s headquarters. Guofan appealed to the governor in person; the governor brushed him off and did nothing, so that day he moved his camp outside the city to escape the garrison. Someone asked: "Why not report this to the throne? " Guofan sighed: "The great crisis is not over—how dare we trouble the throne with a private quarrel? "
7
嘗與嵩燾、忠源論東南形勢多阻水，欲勦賊非治水師不可，乃奏請造戰艦於衡州。 匠卒無曉船制者，短橈長槳，出自精思，以人力勝風水，遂成大小二百四十艦。 募水陸萬人，水軍以褚汝航、楊載福、彭玉麟領之，陸軍以塔齊布、羅澤南領之。 賊自江西上竄，再陷九江、安慶。 忠源戰歿廬州，吳文鎔督師黃州亦敗死。 漢陽失，武昌戒嚴，賊复乘勢擾湖南。 國籓銳欲討賊，率水陸軍東下。 舟師初出湖，大風，損數十艘。 陸師至岳州，前隊潰退，引還長沙。 賊陷湘潭，邀擊靖港，又敗，國籓憤投水，幕下士章壽麟掖起之，得不死。 而同時塔齊布大破賊湘潭，國籓營長沙高峰寺，重整軍實，人人捓揄之。 或請增兵，國籓曰：「吾水陸萬人非不多，而遇賊即潰。 岳州之敗，水師拒戰者惟載福一營； 湘潭之戰，陸師塔齊布、水師載福各兩營：以此知兵貴精不貴多。 故諸葛敗祁山，且謀減兵損食，勤求己過，非虛言也。 且古人用兵，先明功罪賞罰。 今世亂，賢人君子皆潛伏，吾以義聲倡導，同履危亡。 諸公之初從我，非以利動也，故於法亦有難施，其致敗由此。 」諸將聞之皆服。
Talking with Songtao and Zhongyuan about the southeast, he argued that waterways everywhere meant rebels could not be crushed without a fleet, and memorialized to build warships at Hengzhou. No craftsman knew naval design; he devised short oars and long sweeps so muscle could beat wind and current, and two hundred forty vessels large and small were built. He raised ten thousand men for land and river service: Chu Ruhang, Yang Zaifu, and Peng Yulin led the fleet; Ta Qibu and Luo Zemin the infantry. Rebels moving up from Jiangxi retook Jiujiang and Anqing. Zhongyuan fell at Luzhou; Wu Wengong, directing troops at Huangzhou, was defeated and killed as well. Hanyang fell, Wuchang was put on alert, and the rebels again raided Hunan. Guofan was eager to strike and led his combined force eastward. As the fleet first cleared the lake, a gale destroyed dozens of boats. The army reached Yuezhou, its van was broken and driven back to Changsha. Rebels seized Xiangtan and beat him again at Jinggang; in despair Guofan threw himself into the river, and Zhang Shoulin of his staff hauled him out alive. Yet at the same moment Ta Qibu smashed the rebels at Xiangtan; Guofan camped at Gaofeng Temple in Changsha to rebuild his force while men jeered at him. Some urged more troops; Guofan said: "Ten thousand men on land and water are not few, yet we break whenever we meet the enemy. At Yuezhou only Zaifu’s single battalion in the fleet stood firm; at Xiangtan Ta Qibu had two infantry battalions and Zaifu two on the river—that shows quality, not numbers, wins wars. When Zhuge Liang failed at Qishan he still meant to cut men and rations and examine his own faults—that was no idle saying. And in antiquity one first fixed merit, fault, reward, and punishment. In today’s chaos worthy men lie low; I call them out by righteousness and share their peril. You joined me not for gain, so strict law is hard—and that is why we lose. " The commanders, hearing this, were convinced.
8
陸師既克湘潭，巡撫、提督上功，而國籓請罪。 上詰責提督鮑起豹，免其官，以塔齊布代之。 受印日，士民聚觀，嘆詫國籓為知人，而天子能明見萬里也。 賊自岳州陷常德，旋北走，武昌再失。 國籓引兵趨岳州，斬賊梟將曾天養，連戰，下城陵磯。 會師金口，謀取武昌。 澤南沿江東岸攻花園寇屯，塔齊布伏兵洪山，載福舟師深入寇屯，士皆露立，不避鉛丸。 武昌、漢陽賊望見官軍盛，宵遁，遂復二郡。 國籓以前靖港敗，自請奪官，至是奏上，詔署湖北巡撫，尋加兵部侍郎銜，解署任，命督師東下。
After Xiangtan fell, the governor and commander reported victory while Guofan asked to be punished. The throne rebuked Bao Qibao and removed him, putting Ta Qibu in his place. When Ta Qibu took command, crowds gathered in wonder that Guofan knew men and that the emperor saw clear across the realm. Rebels from Yuezhou seized Changde, swept north, and Wuchang fell again. Guofan marched on Yuezhou, beheaded the rebel leader Zeng Tianyang, fought on, and took Chenglingji. Forces gathered at Jinkou to retake Wuchang. Luo Zemin struck rebel camps on the east bank at Huayuan; Ta Qibu hid at Hongshan; Zaifu’s boats drove into enemy lines while his men stood in the open, ignoring musket fire. Seeing the government host, the garrisons at Wuchang and Hanyang fled by night and both cities were recovered. He had asked to be stripped of rank after Jinggang; now the court made him acting governor of Hubei, soon Vice Minister of War, relieved of the governorship, and ordered east at the head of the army.
9
當是時，水師奮厲無前，大破賊田家鎮，斃賊數万，至於九江，前鋒薄湖口。 攻梅家洲賊壘不下，駛入鄱湖。 賊築壘湖口斷其後，舟不得出，於是外江、內湖阻絕。 外江戰船無小艇，賊乘舴艋夜襲營，擲火燒坐船，​​國籓跳而免，水師遂大亂。 上疏請罪，詔旨寬免，謂於大局無傷也。 五年，賊再陷武漢，擾荊襄。 國籓遣胡林翼等軍還援湖北，塔齊布留攻九江，而躬至南昌撫定水師之困內湖者。 澤南從征江西，复弋陽，拔廣信，破義寧，而塔齊布卒於軍。 國籓在江西與巡撫陳啟邁不相能，澤南奔命往來，上書國籓，言東南大勢在武昌，請率所部援鄂，國籓從之。 幕客劉蓉諫曰：「公所恃者塔、羅。 今塔將軍亡，羅又遠行，脫有急，誰堪使者？ 」國籓曰：「吾計之熟矣，東南大局宜如是，俱困於此無為也。 」嵩燾祖餞澤南曰：「曾公兵單，奈何？ 」澤南曰：「天苟不亡本朝，公必不死。 」九月，補授兵部侍郎。
The fleet then drove forward, shattered the rebels at Tianjiazhen, killed tens of thousands, reached Jiujiang, and the van pressed Hukou. Failing at Meijiazhou, they entered Poyang Lake. Rebels walled Hukou behind them; trapped, the outer Yangtze and inner lake were cut off from each other. The great ships had no tenders; rebels in light craft burned the flagships by night and Guofan leaped clear while the fleet collapsed. He asked to be punished; the edict was mild, saying the larger cause was unhurt. In the fifth year the rebels retook Wuhan and raided Jing and Xiang. He sent Hu Linyi to relieve Hubei, left Ta Qibu at Jiujiang, and went to Nanchang to steady the fleet trapped inland. Luo Zemin fought through Jiangxi, retook Yiyang and Guangxin, broke Yining, and Ta Qibu died on campaign. In Jiangxi he clashed with Governor Chen Qimai; Luo Zemin shuttled between fronts, wrote that the southeast turned on Wuchang, and begged to march to Hubei—Guofan consented. Liu Rong warned him: "You depend on Ta and Luo. Ta is dead and Luo is far away—if crisis strikes, whom will you send? " Guofan said: "I have weighed it: the southeast must be served this way—staying here helps nothing. " At Zemin’s farewell Songtao asked: "Lord Zeng’s troops are few—what then? " Zemin answered: "If Heaven means to keep the dynasty, he will not die. " In the ninth month he received formal appointment as Vice Minister of War.
10
六年，賊酋石達開由湖北竄江西，連陷八府一州，九江賊踞自如，湖南北聲息不相聞。 國籓困南昌，遣將分屯要地，羽檄交馳，不廢吟誦。 作水陸師得勝歌，教軍士戰守技藝、結營布陳之法，歌者咸感奮，以殺賊敢死為榮。 顧眾寡，終不能大挫賊。 議者爭請調澤南軍，上以武漢功垂成，不可棄。 澤南督戰益急，卒死於軍。 玉麟聞江西警，芒鞋走千里，穿賊中至南昌助守。 林翼已為湖北巡撫，國籓弟國華、國葆用父命乞師林翼，將五千人攻瑞州。 湖南巡撫駱秉章亦資國荃兵援吉安，兄弟皆會行間。 而國籓前所遣援湖北諸軍，久之再克武漢，直下九江，李續賓八千人軍城東。 續賓者，與弟續宜皆澤南高第弟子也。 載福戰船四百泊江兩岸，江寧將軍都興阿馬隊、鮑超步隊駐小池口，凡數万人。 國籓本以憂懼治軍，自南昌迎勞，見軍容甚盛，益申儆告誡之。 而是時江南大營潰，督師向榮退守丹陽，卒。 和春為欽差大臣，張國樑總統諸軍攻江寧。
In the sixth year Shi Dakai fled from Hubei into Jiangxi and swept eight prefectures and one department; Jiujiang rebels held on, and Hunan north and south lost touch. Besieged at Nanchang, he posted generals at key points, dispatch piled on dispatch, yet he still recited his texts. He wrote victory songs for army and fleet, drilling men in tactics and encampment; singers were roused to glory in killing rebels at the risk of death. But given his numbers, he still could not break the enemy decisively. Critics demanded Luo Zemin’s troops; the throne, seeing Wuhan almost won, refused. Zemin drove the fighting harder still and died on campaign. Peng Yulin, hearing of Jiangxi’s peril, walked a thousand li in sandals through rebel country to help hold Nanchang. Hu Linyi was now governor of Hubei; brothers Guohua and Guobao, by their father’s command, borrowed five thousand men from Linyi and attacked Ruizhou. Hunan’s Luo Bingzhang sent Guoquan to Ji’an, and the brothers joined in the field. Meanwhile his earlier Hubei relief force, after long fighting, retook Wuhan, pushed to Jiujiang, and Li Xubin encamped east of the city with eight thousand. Xubin and his brother Xuyi were both foremost pupils of Luo Zemin. Four hundred of Zaifu’s war boats lined the banks; Duxing’a’s Manchu horse and Bao Chao’s foot held Xiaochikou—tens of thousands altogether. He had led in worry; meeting them at Nanchang he saw their strength and preached caution all the harder. Yet then the great Jiangnan camp collapsed; Commander-in-chief Xiang Rong fell back to Danyang and died. He Chun was named Imperial Commissioner and Zhang Guoliang supreme commander of the forces against Jiangning.
11
七年二月，國籓聞父憂，迳歸。 給三月假治喪，堅請終制，允開侍郎缺。 林翼既定湖北，進圍九江，破湖口，水師絕數年復合。 載福連拔望江、東流，揚風過安慶，克銅陵泥汊，與江南軍通。 由是湘軍水師名天下。 林翼以此軍創始國籓，楊、彭皆其舊部，請起國籓視師。 會九江克復，石達開竄浙江，浸及福建，分股复犯江西，朝旨詔國籓出辦浙江軍務。
In the second month of the seventh year he learned of his father’s death and went home at once. The court gave him three months to mourn and he begged to observe full mourning; he was permitted to leave his vice-ministerial post open. Hu Linyi had pacified Hubei, besieged Jiujiang, retook Hukou, and after years the fleet was whole again. Yang Zaifu swept Wangjiang and Dongliu, passed Anqing, seized Tongling and Nizha, and joined the Jiangnan forces. The Xiang Army’s river force became famous empire-wide. Linyi, knowing the fleet was Guofan’s creation and that Yang and Peng were his men, asked the throne to recall Guofan to take command. Jiujiang fell, Shi Dakai fled toward Zhejiang and Fujian, rebel columns re-entered Jiangxi, and Guofan was ordered to take charge of Zhejiang.
12
國籓至江西，屯建昌，又詔援閩。 國籓以閩賊不足慮，而景德地衝要，遣將援贛北，攻景德。 國荃追賊至浮梁，江西列城次第复。 時石達開復竄湖南，圍寶慶。 上慮四川且有變，林翼亦以湖北餉倚川鹽，而國籓又久治兵，無疆寄，乃與官文合疏請國籓援蜀。 會賊竄廣西，上游兵事解，而陳玉成再破廬州，續賓戰歿三河，林翼以群盜蔓廬、壽間，終為楚患，乃改議留國籓合謀皖。 軍分三道，各萬人。 國籓由宿松、石牌規安慶，多隆阿、鮑超出太湖取桐城，林翼自英山鄉舒、六。 多隆阿等既大破賊小池，复太湖、潛山，遂軍桐城。 國荃率諸軍圍安慶，與桐城軍相犄角。 安慶未及下，而皖南賊陷廣德，襲破杭州。
He reached Jiangxi and camped at Jianchang, then was ordered to relieve Fujian as well. He judged Fujian a lesser threat but Jingde vital, and sent troops to aid northern Jiangxi and strike Jingde. Guoquan drove the enemy to Fuliang, and Jiangxi’s cities fell back in order. Shi Dakai then turned back into Hunan and besieged Baoqing. The court feared trouble in Sichuan; Linyi’s revenue depended on Sichuan salt, and Guofan had no provincial post—he and Guan Wen jointly asked that he march to Sichuan. Rebels then fled to Guangxi and the upper river quieted, but Chen Yucheng retook Luzhou and Li Xubin died at Sanhe; Linyi, seeing bandits between Luzhou and Shouzhou, kept Guofan for the Anhui campaign instead. Three columns of ten thousand men each were formed. Guofan advanced from Susong and Shipai on Anqing; Duo Long’a and Bao Chao from Taihu on Tongcheng; Hu Linyi from Yingshan on Shuzhou and Liuan. Duo Long’a smashed the enemy at Xiaochi, retook Taihu and Qianshan, and encamped at Tongcheng. Guoquan besieged Anqing while the Tongcheng army held the other horn. Anqing was still untaken when rebels in southern Anhui seized Guangde and broke Hangzhou.
13
李秀成大會群賊建平，分道援江寧，江南大營复潰，常州、蘇州相繼失，咸豐十年閏三月也。 左宗棠聞而歎曰：「此勝敗之轉機也！ 江南諸軍，將蹇兵疲久矣。 滌而清之，庶幾後來可藉手乎？ 」或問：「誰可當者？ 」林翼曰：「朝廷以江南事付曾公，天下不足平也。 」於是天子慎選帥，就加國籓兵部尚書銜，署理兩江總督，旋即真，授欽差大臣。 是時江、浙賊氛熾，或請撤安慶圍先所急。 國籓曰：「安慶一軍為克金陵張本，不可動也。 」遂南渡江，駐祁門。 江、浙官紳告急書日數十至，援蘇、援滬、援皖、援鎮江詔書亦疊下。 國籓至祁門未數日，賊陷寧國，陷徽州。 東南方困兵革，而英吉利复失好，以兵至。 僧格林沁敗績天津，文宗狩熱河，國籓聞警，請提兵北上，會和議成，乃止。
Li Xiucheng massed at Jianping, sent relief to Jiangning, the Jiangnan camp collapsed again, and Changzhou and Suzhou fell—it was the intercalary third month, tenth year of Xianfeng. Zuo Zongtang sighed: "Here is the turning point of the war! Jiangnan’s armies have had bad commanders and exhausted men for years. Wash them clean—perhaps we can use them again? " Someone asked: "Who is fit to lead? " Linyi said: "Put Jiangnan in Lord Zeng’s hands and the empire can be settled. The throne then raised Guofan to Minister of War, made him acting governor-general of the Two Jiangs, confirmed him, and named him Imperial Commissioner. Rebels blazed in Jiangsu and Zhejiang; some urged lifting the siege of Anqing for the crisis elsewhere. Guofan replied: "The force at Anqing is the key to Nanjing; it must not move. " He crossed the river and camped at Qimen. Daily pleas poured in from Jiangsu and Zhejiang, and edicts to save Suzhou, Shanghai, Anhui, and Zhenjiang followed one after another. Days after he reached Qimen, Ningguo and Huizhou fell. The southeast was ravaged by war, and Britain, relations broken, sent troops. Sengge Rinchen was beaten at Tianjin and Emperor Wenzong fled to Rehe; Guofan offered to march north, but peace was made and he held back.
14
其冬，大為賊困，一出祁門東陷婺源； 一出祁門西陷景德； 一入羊棧嶺攻大營。 軍報絕不通，將吏惵然有憂色，固請移營江乾就水師。 國籓曰：「無故退軍，兵家所忌。 」卒不從，使人間行檄鮑超、張運蘭亟引兵會。 身在軍中，意氣自如，時與賓佐酌酒論文。 自官京朝，即日記所言行，後履危困無稍間。 國籓駐祁門，本資餉江西，及景德失，議者爭言取徽州通浙米。 乃自將大軍次休寧，值天雨，八營皆潰，草遺囑寄家，誓死守休寧。 適宗棠大破賊樂平，運道通，移駐東流。 多隆阿連敗賊桐城，鮑超一軍游擊無定居，林翼復遣將助之。 十一年八月，國荃遂克安慶。 捷聞，而文宗崩，林翼亦卒。 穆宗即位，太后垂簾聽政，加國籓太子少保銜，命節制江蘇、安徽、江西、浙江四省。 國籓惶懼，疏辭，不允，朝有大政，諮而後行。
That winter rebels hemmed him in on three sides: one force east from Qimen took Wuyuan; one west from Qimen retook Jingde; one came through Yangzhan Ridge against headquarters. Couriers could not pass; officers begged to move to the river and the fleet. Guofan said: "A retreat without cause is forbidden in war. " He refused, but sent secret orders for Bao Chao and Zhang Yunlan to march at once. In camp his bearing stayed calm; he still drank and talked letters with his advisers. Since his Beijing days he had kept a daily journal, and in danger he never stopped. At Qimen he had lived on Jiangxi supplies; after Jingde fell, many said seize Huizhou for Zhejiang rice. He marched on Xiuning; rain broke eight camps; he wrote his will home and swore to hold Xiuning or die. Zuo Zongtang then smashed the enemy at Leping, supplies returned, and he shifted to Dongliu. Duo Long’a beat the enemy at Tongcheng again; Bao Chao ranged freely; Hu Linyi sent more help. In the eighth month of the eleventh year Guoquan took Anqing. News of victory came as Emperor Wenzong died—and Hu Linyi with him. Emperor Muzong reigned under the empress dowager’s regency; Guofan became Junior Mentor of the Heir Apparent and commander of four provinces. He begged off in fear; the court refused; major policy waited on his word.
15
當是時，偽天王洪秀全僭號踞金陵，偽忠王李秀成等犯蘇、滬，偽侍王李世賢等陷浙杭，偽輔王楊輔清等屯寧國，偽康王汪海洋窺江西，偽英王陳玉成屯廬州，捻首苗霈霖出入潁、壽，與玉成合，圖竄山東、河南，眾皆號數十萬。 國籓與國荃策進取，國荃曰：「急搗金陵，則寇必以全力護巢穴，而後蘇、杭可圖也。 」國籓然之。 乃以江寧事付國荃，以浙江事付宗棠，而以江蘇事付李鴻章。 鴻章故出國籓門，以編修為幕僚，改道員，至是令從淮上募勇八千，選良將付之，號「淮軍」。 同治元年，拜協辦大學士，督諸軍進討。 於是國荃有搗金陵之師，鴻章有徵蘇、滬之師，載福、玉麟有肅清下游之師； 大江以北，多隆阿有取廬州之師，續宜有援潁州之師； 大江以南，鮑超有攻寧國之師，運蘭有防剿徽州之師，宗棠有規復全浙之師：十道並出，皆受成於國籓。
Hong Xiuquan held Nanjing as Heavenly King; Li Xiucheng struck Suzhou and Shanghai; Li Shixian held Hangzhou; Yang Fuqing held Ningguo; Wang Haiyang menaced Jiangxi; Chen Yucheng held Luzhou; Miao Peilin’s Nian bands linked with Yucheng toward Shandong and Henan—each host claimed hundreds of thousands. Father and brother plotted the advance; Guoquan said: "Hit Nanjing hard and the enemy must guard their lair—then Suzhou and Hangzhou can follow. " Guofan agreed. He gave Nanjing to Guoquan, Zhejiang to Zuo Zongtang, and Jiangsu to Li Hongzhang. Hongzhang, once his pupil and staff reviser, now raised eight thousand Huai braves under picked generals—the Huai Army. In the first Tongzhi year he became Grand Secretariat Associate and directed the general advance. Guoquan besieged Nanjing, Hongzhang recovered Suzhou and Shanghai, Zaifu and Peng Yulin cleared the lower Yangtze; north of the river Duo Long’a marched on Luzhou and Li Xuyi relieved Yingzhou; south of it Bao Chao struck Ningguo, Zhang Yunlan held Huizhou, Zuo Zongtang reconquered Zhejiang—ten armies at once, all answering to Guofan.
16
賊之都金陵也，堅築壕壘，餉械足，猝不可拔。 疾疫大作，將士死亡山積，幾不能軍。 國籓自以德薄，請簡大臣馳赴軍，俾分己責，上優詔慰勉之，謂：「天災流行，豈卿一人之咎？ 意者朝廷政多缺失，我君臣當勉圖禳救，為民請命。 且環顧中外，才力、氣量無逾卿者！ 時勢艱難，無稍懈也。 」國籓讀詔感泣。 時洪秀全被圍久，召李秀成蘇州，李世賢浙江，悉眾來援，號六十萬，圍雨花台軍。 國荃拒戰六十四日，解去。 三年五月，水師克九洑洲，江寧城合圍。 十月，鴻章克蘇州。 四年二月，宗棠克杭州。 國籓以江寧久不下，請鴻章來會師，未發，國荃攻益急，克之。 江寧平，天子褒功，加太子太傅，封一等毅勇侯，賞雙眼翎。 開國以來，文臣封侯自是始。 朝野稱賀，而國籓功成不居，粥粥如畏。 穆宗每簡督撫，輒密詢其人，未敢指缺疏薦，以謂疆臣既專征伐，不當更分黜陟之柄，外重內輕之漸，不可不防。
At Nanjing they had built deep walls and ample stores; the city could not be rushed. Plague killed men in heaps until the army could hardly stand. Feeling unworthy, he asked for a senior minister to share command; the emperor comforted him: "Plague is Heaven’s doing—how is it yours alone? Perhaps the court has erred—we should pray for the people and mend policy. Look around—in talent and force of character none matches you! These times are hard—do not slacken. " Reading the edict, Guofan wept. Hong Xiuquan, long trapped, called Li Xiucheng from Suzhou and Li Shixian from Zhejiang; six hundred thousand men, they claimed, surrounded Yuhuatai. Guoquan held them sixty-four days until they broke off. In the fifth month of the third year the fleet took Jiufuzhou and the ring around Nanjing closed. In the tenth month Hongzhang took Suzhou. In the second month of the fourth year Zuo Zongtang took Hangzhou. Nanjing still held; Guofan summoned Hongzhang to join the siege, but before he arrived Guoquan pressed harder and the city fell. Nanjing pacified, the throne made him Junior Mentor of the Heir Apparent, First-Class Marquis Yiyong, with double-eyed peacock feathers. Never before had a civil official been enfeoffed marquis at the dynasty’s founding. The realm rejoiced, but Guofan, his work done, shrank from praise like a man afraid. The young emperor secretly asked his view of every appointment; he would not name men in memorials, fearing that commanders with armies should not also control civil appointments—lest outer power outweigh the center.
17
初，官軍積習深，勝不讓，敗不救。 國籓練湘軍，謂必萬眾一心，乃可辦賊，故以忠誠倡天下。 其後又謂淮上風氣勁，宜別立一軍。 湘勇利山徑，馳騁平原非所長，且用武十年，氣亦稍衰矣，故欲練淮士為湘勇之繼。 至是東南大定，裁湘軍，進淮軍，而捻匪事起。
Government troops had long refused to share credit in victory or aid in defeat. Guofan built the Xiang Army on the belief that only united hearts could defeat the rebels, and preached loyalty across the land. Later he said the Huai country was hardy and deserved its own army. Xiang men excelled in hills, not on plains; after ten years their edge had dulled—he meant the Huai Army to succeed them. The southeast was largely won; he disbanded the Xiang Army and raised the Huai, even as Nian rebels flared.
18
捻匪者，始於山東遊民相聚，其後剽掠光、固、潁、亳、淮、徐之間，捻紙燃脂，故謂之「捻」。 有眾數十萬，馬數万，蹂躪數千里，分合不常。 捻首四人，曰張總愚、任柱、牛洪、賴文光。 自洪寇、苗練嘗糾捻與官軍戰，益悉攻鬥，勝保、袁甲三不能禦。 僧格林沁征討數年，亦未能大創之。 國籓聞僧軍輕騎追賊，一日夜三百餘裡，曰：「此於兵法，必蹶上將軍。 」未幾而王果戰歿曹州，上聞大驚，詔國籓速赴山東剿捻，節制直隸、山東、河南三省，而鴻章代為總督，廷旨日促出師。 國籓上言：「楚軍裁撤殆盡，今調劉松山一軍及劉銘傳淮勇尚不足。 當更募徐州勇，以楚軍之規模，開齊、兗之風氣； 又增募馬隊及黃河水師，皆非旦夕可就。 直隸宜自籌防兵，分守河岸，不宜令河南之兵兼顧河北。 僧格林沁嘗週曆五省，臣不能也。 如以徐州為老營，則山東之兗、沂、曹、濟，河南之歸、陳，江甦之淮、徐、海，安徽之廬、鳳、潁、泗，此十三府州責之臣，而以其餘責各督撫。 汛地有專屬，則軍務乃漸有歸宿。 」又奏：「扼要駐軍臨淮關、周家口、濟寧、徐州，為四鎮。 一處有急，三處往援。 今賊已成流寇，若賊流而我與之俱流，必致疲於奔命。 故臣堅持初議，以有定之兵，制無定之寇，重迎剿，不重尾追。 」然督師年餘，捻馳突如故。 將士皆謂不苦戰而苦奔逐，乃起張秋抵清江築長牆，憑運河御之，未成而捻竄襄、鄧間，因移而西，修沙河、賈魯河，開壕置守。 分地甫定，而捻衝河南汛地，复突而東。 時議頗咎國籓計迂闊，然亦無他術可制捻也。
The Nian began as Shandong wanderers; they raided the Huai basin and lit twisted paper in grease—hence the name. They fielded hundreds of thousands of men and myriads of horses, ravaging thousands of li, merging and splitting without pattern. Four chiefs led them: Zhang Zongyu, Ren Zhu, Niu Hong, and Lai Wenguang. Taiping and Miao rebels had schooled them in battle; Sheng Bao and Yuan Jiasan could not hold them. Sengge Rinchen fought them for years without a decisive blow. Hearing that Seng’s light horse chased three hundred li in a day and night, Guofan said: "By the art of war that will break the general. " Soon the prince fell at Caozhou; the throne ordered Guofan to Shandong with command over three provinces while Hongzhang took his governorship, edicts pressing him daily. He wrote: "The Hunan army is almost gone; Liu Songshan and Liu Mingchuan’s Huai men are not enough. We must raise Xuzhou braves on the Hunan pattern and stir Qi and Yan; cavalry and a Yellow River fleet cannot be ready overnight. Zhili should guard its own banks; Henan should not cover Hebei too. Sengge Rinchen once held five provinces—I cannot match that. With Xuzhou as base, thirteen prefectures in four provinces fall to me; the rest to local governors. Fixed zones would give the war a center. " He proposed four garrisons: Linhuaiguan, Zhoujiakou, Jining, and Xuzhou. If one is attacked, three would relieve it. The enemy are roving bandits; to chase them everywhere is to exhaust ourselves. So I hold to fixed troops against roving enemies—block them, do not merely follow. " Yet after a year in command the Nian still ranged freely. Men complained they marched more than they fought; they walled the canal from Zhangqiu to Qingjiang; before it stood the Nian slipped into Xiang and Deng, so they moved west and trenched the Sha and Jia rivers. Hardly were the zones set when the Nian hit Henan’s line and surged east again. Critics called his strategy too slow, yet no other way could hold the Nian.
19
山東、河南民習見僧格林沁戰，皆怪國籓以督兵大臣安坐徐州，謗議盈路。 國籓在軍久，益慎用兵。 初立駐軍四鎮之議，次設扼守黃運河之策。 既數為言路所劾，亦自以防河無效，朝廷方起用國荃，乃奏請鴻章以江督出駐徐州，與魯撫會辦東路； 國荃以鄂撫出駐襄陽，與豫撫會辦西路：而自駐周家口策應之。 或又劾其驕妄，於是國籓念權位不可久處，益有憂讒畏譏之心矣。 匈病假數月，繼請開缺，以散員留軍效力； 又請削封爵：皆不許。
Shandong and Henan, used to Sengge Rinchen’s hard fighting, resented Guofan sitting in Xuzhou while slander filled the roads. Years in the field made him ever more cautious about battle. He began with four fixed garrisons, then the canal defense line. Censors attacked him repeatedly; doubting the river line, and with Guoquan rising at court, he asked Hongzhang as Jiangsu governor to camp at Xuzhou with the Shandong governor on the east; Guoquan as Hubei governor to hold Xiangyang with the Henan governor on the west, while he coordinated from Zhoujiakou. Fresh charges of arrogance made him feel power could not be held long, and he grew afraid of court intrigue. Chest trouble kept him on leave for months; he asked to resign his post yet stay with the army as a private; he begged to give up his marquisate—the court refused both.
20
五年冬，還任江南，而鴻章代督軍。 時牛洪死，張總愚竄陝西，任柱、賴文光竄湖北，自是有東西捻之號。 六年，就補大學士，留治所。 東捻由河南竄登、萊、青，李鴻章、劉長佑建議合四省兵力堵運河。 賊復引而西，越膠、萊、河南入海州。 官軍陣斬任柱，賴文光走死揚州。 以東捻平，加國籓雲騎尉世職。 西捻入陝後，為松山所敗。 乘堅冰渡河竄山西，入直隸，犯保定、天津。 松山繞出賊前，破之於獻縣。 諸帥勤王師大至，賊越運河竄東昌、武定。 鴻章移師德州，河水盛漲，扼河以困之。 國籓遣黃翼升領水師助剿，大破賊於荏平。 張總愚赴水死，而西捻平。 凡防河之策，皆國籓本謀也。 是年授武英殿大學士，調直隸總督。
In the fifth winter he returned to Jiangnan while Hongzhang took the field command. Niu Hong was dead; Zhang Zongyu fled to Shaanxi, Ren Zhu and Lai Wenguang to Hubei—the Eastern and Western Nian. In the sixth year he was confirmed Grand Secretary without leaving his post. Eastern Nian from Henan swept Deng, Lai, and Qing; Hongzhang and Liu Changyou proposed a four-province blockade of the canal. They turned west, crossed Jiao and Lai and Henan, and broke toward Haizhou on the sea. Ren Zhu was killed in battle; Lai Wenguang fled and died at Yangzhou. The Eastern Nian subdued, Guofan received the hereditary rank of Cloud Cavalry Captain. The Western Nian, entering Shaanxi, were beaten by Liu Songshan. They crossed the frozen Yellow River into Shanxi, entered Zhili, and threatened Baoding and Tianjin. Songshan cut ahead and broke them at Xian County. Relief armies gathered; the Nian crossed the canal toward Dongchang and Wuding. Hongzhang shifted to Dezhou, used high water to pen them in. Guofan sent Huang Yisheng’s fleet; the enemy was shattered at Renping. Zhang Zongyu drowned; the Western Nian were gone. Every canal defense had been Guofan’s idea. That year he entered the Hall of Military Glory and became governor-general of Zhili.
21
國籓為政務持大體，規全勢。 其策西事，議先清隴寇而後出關； 籌滇、黔，議以蜀、湘二省為根本。 皆初立一議，後數年卒如其說。 自西人入中國，交涉事日繁。 金陵未下，俄、美、英、法皆請以兵助，國籓婉拒之。 及廷議購機輪，置船械，則力贊其成，復建議選學童習藝歐洲。 每定約章，輒詔問可許不可許，國籓以為爭彼我之虛儀者可許，其奪吾民生計者勿許也。 既至直隸，以練兵、飭吏、治河三端為要務，次第興革，設清訟局、禮賢館，政教大行。
In civil rule he held to large patterns and the whole strategic picture. On the northwest he urged clearing Longzhou bandits before marching beyond the passes; on Yunnan and Guizhou he made Sichuan and Hunan the base. Each began as a single memorial—and years later events proved him right. After Westerners entered China, foreign affairs multiplied daily. Before Nanjing fell, Russia, America, Britain, and France offered troops; Guofan declined politely. When the court debated steamships and arsenals, he urged them on and proposed sending boys to learn crafts in Europe. On each treaty the throne asked what to concede; he would yield empty ceremony but never what stole the people’s livelihood. In Zhili he made training troops, disciplining officials, and river work his three tasks, reformed in order, opened bureaus for lawsuits and honoring talent—and rule flourished.
22
九年四月，天津民擊殺法領事豐大業，毀教堂，傷教民數十人。 通商大臣崇厚議嚴懲之，民不服。 國籓方病目，詔速赴津，乃務持平保和局，殺十七人，又遣戍府縣吏。 國籓之初至也，津民謂必反崇厚所為，備兵以抗法。 然當是時，海內初定，湘軍已散遣，天津咫尺京畿，民、教相閧，此小事不足啟兵端，而津民爭怨之。 平生故舊持高論者，日移書譙讓，省館至毀所署楹帖，而國籓深維中外兵勢強弱，和戰利害，惟自引咎，不一辯也。 丁日昌因上奏曰：「自古局外議論，不諒局中艱苦，一唱百和，亦足以熒上聽，撓大計。 卒之事勢決裂，國家受無窮之累，而局外不與其禍，反得力持清議之名，臣實痛之！ 」
In the ninth year’s fourth month Tianjin people killed the French consul Fontanier, wrecked churches, and wounded dozens of Christians. Trade Commissioner Chonghou wanted harsh punishment; the people rebelled at the idea. Half blind, he was ordered to Tianjin; seeking peace he had seventeen executed and prefectural officials exiled. At first Tianjin expected him to defy Chonghou and armed against France. The realm was barely pacified, the Xiang Army gone, Tianjin lay under the capital’s shadow—a brawl not worth war, yet the city hated him the more. High-minded friends daily scolded him by letter; lodges tore down his plaques; weighing strength and peace, he blamed only himself and never replied. Ding Richang wrote: "Outsiders never grasp the difficulty within the case; one cry becomes a chorus that dazzles the throne and wrecks policy. When things break the state pays endlessly while critics pay nothing yet keep their reputation for pure opinion—I grieve at it! "
23
國籓既負重謗，疾益劇，乃召鴻章治其獄，踰月事定，如初議。 會兩江缺出，遂調補江南，而以鴻章督直隸。 江南人聞其至，焚香以迎。 以亂後經籍就熸，設官書局印行，校刊皆精審。 禮聘名儒為書院山長，其幕府亦極一時之選，江南文化遂比隆盛時。
Weighed down by blame and illness, he yielded the case to Hongzhang; within a month it ended as first planned. A Jiangnan vacancy opened; he was shifted south and Hongzhang took Zhili. Jiangnan burned incense to welcome his return. War had nearly destroyed the classics; he founded an official press with careful editions. He hired great scholars to head academies and staffed his yamen with the day’s best—Jiangnan culture rose again.
24
國籓為人威重，美鬚髯，目三角有棱。 每對客，注視移時不語，見者竦然，退則記其優劣，無或爽者。 天性好文，治之終身不厭，有家法而不囿於一師。 其論學兼綜漢、宋，以謂先王治世之道，經緯萬端，一貫之以禮。 惜秦蕙田五禮通考闕食貨，乃輯補鹽課、海運、錢法、河堤為六卷； 又慨古禮殘闕無軍禮，軍禮要自有專篇，如戚敬元所紀者。 論者謂國籓所訂營制、營規，其於軍禮庶幾近之。 晚年頗以清靜化民，俸入悉以養士。 老儒宿學，群歸依之。 尤知人，善任使，所成就薦拔者，不可勝數。 一見輒品目其材，悉當。 時舉先世耕讀之訓，教誡其家。 遇將卒僚吏若子弟然，故雖嚴憚之，而樂為之用。 居江南久，功德最盛。
He was imposing, bearded, with sharp triangular eyes. With guests he would stare in silence until they quailed; afterward he noted their strengths without a miss. He loved letters to the end, with family learning but no single school. He joined Han and Song learning, holding that the kings’ governance, in all its strands, was one in ritual. Finding Qin Huitian’s Five Rites incomplete on economy, he added six scrolls on salt, sea transport, coinage, and rivers; he lamented lost ancient rite and missing military ritual, which deserved its own section as Qi Jingyuan had shown. Critics said his camp rules nearly embodied military ritual. Late in life he sought quiet to transform the people and spent his salary on scholars. Veteran scholars flocked to him. He knew men and employed them well beyond counting. One meeting sufficed to judge a man—and he was never wrong. He often preached his family’s farming-and-reading tradition to his kin. He treated officers and clerks like kin; they feared him yet served gladly. His longest service and greatest merit lay in Jiangnan.
25
論曰：國籓事功本於學問，善以禮運。 公誠之心，尤足格眾。 其治軍行政，務求蹈實。 凡規畫天下事，久無不驗，世皆稱之，至謂漢之諸葛亮、唐之裴度、明之王守仁，殆無以過，何其盛歟！ 國籓又嘗取古今聖哲三十三人，畫像贊記，以為師資，其平生志學大端，具見於此。 至功成名立，汲汲以薦舉人才為己任，疆臣閫帥，幾遍海內。 以人事君，皆能不負所知。 嗚呼！ 中興以來，一人而已。
The historian writes: Guofan’s feats grew from scholarship; he ruled through ritual. His utterly sincere heart especially won men’s hearts. In army and state he sought what was solid and real. His plans for the empire proved true in time; men compared him to Zhuge Liang, Pei Du, and Wang Yangming—perhaps unequaled—such was his glory! He painted and praised thirty-three ancient sages as his teachers; his life’s aspiration shows in that act. At the height of fame he made recommending talent his duty; his men commanded nearly every frontier. He served the throne through men, and none betrayed his trust. Alas! Since the restoration, there has been only this one man.
