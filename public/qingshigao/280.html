郎坦朋春薩布素瑪拉
Lang Tan, Peng Chun, Sabsu, and Ma La
3
郎坦，瓜爾佳氏，滿洲正白旗人，內大臣吳拜子。 年十四，授三等侍衛。 順治六年，進二等。 從端重親王博洛討叛將薑瓖，次渾源，圍城。 賊渡濠來犯，郎坦射其酋，貫心，殪，遂敗賊。 師還，進一等。 八年，以吳拜附和內大臣洛什等獲罪，並奪郎坦官。 尋復之。 康熙二年，代吳拜管佐領，遷護軍參領。 從定西將軍圖海討李自成餘黨李來亨等於茅麓山，深入賊巢，獲所置官十一。 四年，襲一等精奇尼哈番。 十二年，京師有陳三道者，設壇以邪教惑眾，命郎坦與諸侍衛捕治。 十三年，命行邊，獲逋盜張飛腿等。 擢正白旗蒙古副都統，調本旗滿洲。
Lang Tan, of the Guwalgiya clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain White Banner and was the son of Inner Minister Wubai. At fourteen he received appointment as a third-class bodyguard. In Shunzhi 6 he was promoted to the second rank. He followed Prince Duanzhong Bolo against the rebel Jiang Xiang, camped at Hunyuan, and laid siege to the city. Rebels crossed the moat to attack; Lang Tan shot their leader through the heart and killed him, routing the enemy. After the army returned he was promoted to first class. In the eighth year Wubai was condemned for siding with Inner Minister Luoshi and others, and Lang Tan was stripped of his post as well. Before long his office was restored. In Kangxi 2 he took over Wubai's assistant banner command and was made assistant commandant of the guards. He served under Pacify-the-West General Tu Hai against Li Zicheng's remnant Li Laiheng at Maolu Mountain, penetrated the rebel stronghold, and seized eleven officials they had installed. In the fourth year he inherited a first-class baronetcy (jingqi niha fan). In the twelfth year a certain Chen Sandao in the capital used an altar and a heterodox cult to mislead the people; the court ordered Lang Tan and fellow bodyguards to seize and punish him. In the thirteenth year he was sent on frontier patrol and captured the fugitive Zhang Feitui and his gang. He was raised to Mongol deputy lieutenant-general of the Plain White Banner, then shifted to the banner's Manchu command.
4
順治中，俄羅斯東部人犯黑龍江邊境，時稱為羅剎。 九年，駐防寧古塔章京海塞遣捕牲翼長希福率兵與戰，師敗績。 世祖命誅海塞，鞭希福百，仍駐寧古塔。 十一年，固山額真明安達里率師討之，敗敵黑龍江。 羅剎未大創，复侵入精奇里江諸處。 上命大理寺卿明愛等諭令撤回，遷延不即去，據雅克薩城，於其旁耕種漁獵； 又過牛滿、恆滾，侵擾索倫、赫哲、飛牙喀、奇勒爾諸部。
Under Shunzhi, Russians from the east raided the Amur borderlands and were then called Luocha. In the ninth year Ningguta commander Haise sent wing chief Xifu of the hunting detachment with troops to fight them and was defeated. Shunzhi ordered Haise put to death, Xifu given a hundred lashes, and the Ningguta garrison maintained. In the eleventh year banner commander Mingandali took an army against them and beat the foe on the Amur. The Russians were not broken decisively and again pushed into the Jingqili River country. The emperor sent Minister of Justice Ming Ai and others to order them back; they lingered, would not leave promptly, seized Albazin, and planted and fished around it; they crossed the Nioman and Henggun as well, raiding the Solon, Hezhe, Feiyaka, Qiler, and other peoples.
5
二十一年秋，遣郎坦及副都統朋春等率兵往索倫。 比行，諭曰：「羅剎犯我境，恃雅克薩城為巢穴，歷年已久，殺掠不已。 爾等至達呼爾、索倫，遣人往諭以來捕鹿。 因詳視陸路遠近，沿黑龍江行圍，迳薄雅克薩城，勘其形勢。 度羅剎不敢出戰，如出戰，姑勿交鋒，但率剎引退。 朕別有區畫。 」賜御用裘服、弓矢以行。 及冬，郎坦等還京師，疏言：「羅剎久踞雅克薩，恃有木城。 若發兵三千，與紅衣砲二十，即可攻取。 陸行自興安嶺以往，林木叢雜，冬雪堅冰，夏雨泥淖，惟輕裝可行。 自雅克薩還至愛滹城，於黑龍江順流行船，僅須半月，逆流行船，約須三月，倍於陸行，期於運糧餉、軍器、輜重為便。 現有大船四十、小船二十六，宜增造小船五十餘應用。 」上諭曰：「郎坦等奏攻取羅剎甚易，朕亦以為然。 第兵非善事，宜暫停攻取。 調烏拉、寧古塔兵千五百人，並製造船艦，發紅衣砲、鳥槍教之演習。 於愛琿、呼瑪爾二地建木城，與之對壘，相機舉行。 所需軍糧，取諸科爾沁十旗及錫伯、烏拉官屯，約得一萬二千石，可支三年。 愛琿城距索倫五宿可至，其間設一驛。 俟我兵將至精奇里烏拉，令索倫供牛羊。 如此，則羅剎不得納我逋逃，而彼之逋逃且絡繹來歸，自不能久存矣。 」尋擢郎坦前鋒統領。
That autumn of Kangxi 21 the court dispatched Lang Tan, Deputy Lieutenant-General Peng Chun, and others with troops toward Solon. On the eve of departure he was told: "The Russians invade our territory, using Albazin as their lair; year after year they kill and plunder without end. When you reach the Dahur and Solon, send envoys inviting them to come hunt deer. Use that occasion to measure overland distances in detail, run a battue along the Amur, close on Albazin directly, and reconnoiter its defenses. Expect the Russians not to dare fight; if they do, for now do not clash—only draw them off. I have a separate design." He was given imperial furs, bow, and arrows for the journey. By winter Lang Tan's party was back in Beijing and reported: "The Luocha have entrenched themselves at Albazin behind a timber palisade. Send three thousand men and twenty red-coated guns and it can be taken." The land route from the Greater Khingan is thick forest; in winter the snow freezes solid, in summer rain turns the trails to mire—only light columns can march. From Albazin to Aihui City, a downstream Amur voyage needs barely half a month; upstream needs roughly three months—double the land time—but suits grain, ordnance, and stores. We now have forty large junks and twenty-six small ones; fifty-odd more small craft should be built." The emperor replied: "Lang Tan says the Russians are easy to crush, and I agree. But war is no light thing—hold off the assault for the present. Raise fifteen hundred men from Ula and Ningguta, build ships, issue red-coated guns and muskets, and drill them. Erect wooden forts at Aihui and Humar to confront them and act when opportunity offers. Provisions can come from the ten Khorchin banners plus Xibe and Ula state farms—about twelve thousand shi, enough for three years. Aihui lies five marches from Solon; place a courier station en route. When our force nears Jingqili Ula, let Solon furnish cattle and sheep. Then the Russians cannot harbor our runaways, their own fugitives will stream to us, and they cannot last long." Lang Tan was soon made vanguard commander.
6
二十二年，命與黑龍江將軍薩布素會議，駐兵額蘇哩。 事還，奏額蘇哩七月即經霜雪，宜乘春和，以寧古塔兵分為三班，更番戍守。 上以更番戍守非久長策，不允。 二十三年，甄別八旗管兵官，罷郎坦前鋒統領，以世職隨旗行走。 二十四年，命都統朋春率師徵羅剎，郎坦以副都統銜隨徵。 師薄雅克薩城，羅剎酋額里克舍請降，郎坦宣詔宥其罪，引眾徙去，毀木城。 是冬羅剎復來，踞雅克薩築城。 二十五年，命郎坦偕副都統班達爾沙攜紅衣砲，率藤牌兵百人，往會將軍薩布素進兵。 上以郎坦諳悉地勢，即令參贊軍務。 六月，薄其城，鑿壕築壘，賊出拒，擊敗之，斬額里克舍。 尋，俄羅斯察罕汗上書請釋雅克薩圍，上許之，令郎坦撤軍，還駐寧古塔。 尋擢正白旗蒙古都統。 二十八年，上遣內大臣索額圖等與俄羅斯使人費耀多囉等會於尼布楚，立約定界，命郎坦與議，乃毀所築城徙去。
In Kangxi 22 he was told to consult Heilongjiang general Sabsu and station troops at Esuri. On return he noted Esuri frosts by the seventh month; only spring is mild—split Ningguta's men into three reliefs, he urged. The emperor judged rotation no lasting policy and refused. In Kangxi 23, during the review of banner officers, Lang Tan lost the vanguard command and kept only his hereditary title with the banner. In Kangxi 24 Commander Peng Chun was sent against the Russians; Lang Tan went as acting deputy lieutenant-general. At Albazin the Russian leader Yerikexie sued for peace; Lang Tan read the amnesty, moved the people out, and razed the timber fort. That winter the Russians returned, reoccupied Albazin, and rebuilt fortifications. In Kangxi 25 Lang Tan, with Deputy Lieutenant-General Bandarsha, red-coated guns, and a hundred rattan-shield men, was sent to reinforce General Sabsu. Knowing the country, Lang Tan was immediately named a campaign adviser. In the sixth month they closed on the town, dug trenches and breastworks; the garrison sallied, was beaten, and Yerikexie was killed. Soon Russia's Tsagan Khan asked to lift the siege; the emperor consented and ordered Lang Tan back to Ningguta. Shortly after he became Mongol lieutenant-general of the Plain White Banner. In Kangxi 28 Inner Minister Songgotu met the Russian envoy Feodor at Nerchinsk to delimit the frontier; Lang Tan joined the negotiations, after which the Russian posts were demolished and the settlers removed.
7
二十九年，古北口外盜起，命郎坦偕侍衛赫濟爾亨等督兵捕剿，盡殲之。 三十一年，噶爾丹侵喀爾喀部，擾及邊境，授郎坦安北將軍，率師駐大同。 疏請出邊駐喀喇穆倫偵寇，詔暫駐歸化城。 尋擢領侍衛內大臣，兼火器營總管，列議政大臣。 三十二年，授昭武將軍，率師駐甘州。 三十三年，移駐寧夏，與甘肅提督孫思克分道偵寇。 上聞噶爾丹將逼圖拉，命郎坦移兵禦剿，以圖拉無警，引還。 仍任領侍衛內大臣，列議政如故。 三十四年，往盛京巡閱邊隘，還入塞，疾劇，遣太醫馳驛往視。 尋卒，賜祭葬。
In Kangxi 29 brigands appeared beyond Gubeikou; Lang Tan, with Bodyguard Hejierheng and others, led pursuit troops and wiped them out. In Kangxi 31 Galdan struck the Khalkha and harassed the border; Lang Tan was named Pacify-the-North General and encamped at Datong. He asked to move forward to Karamuren to scout; the court told him to stay at Guihua City for the time being. He was soon chief inner bodyguard, head of the Firearms Corps, and a deliberating minister. In Kangxi 32 he became General Who Manifests Martiality and took the army to Ganzhou. In Kangxi 33 he moved to Ningxia and, with Gansu commissioner Sun Sike, patrolled on separate lines. Hearing Galdan might threaten Tula, the emperor ordered Lang Tan forward; finding Tula secure, he withdrew. He resumed his posts as chief bodyguard and deliberating minister. In Kangxi 34 he inspected the Shengjing frontier; re-entering the Great Wall he grew desperately ill, and a court physician was rushed to him. He died soon after; the court granted funeral honors.
8
朋春，棟鄂氏，滿洲正紅旗人，何和禮四世孫。 何和禮子和碩圖，進爵三等公； 子何爾本、哲爾本、蘇布遞襲，至袞布，以恩詔進一等。 朋春，哲爾本子也，順治九年，襲封。 康熙十五年，加太子太保，授正紅旗蒙古副都統，調本旗滿洲。
Peng Chun, of the Donggo clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain Red Banner and was Heheli's great-great-grandson. Heheli's son Hosotu was ennobled a third-class duke; his sons He'erben, Zhe'erben, and Subudi inherited in succession until Gunbu, promoted to first class by imperial grace. Peng Chun was Zhe'erben's son and inherited in Shunzhi 9. In Kangxi 15 he became Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, Mongol deputy lieutenant-general of the Plain Red Banner, then shifted to its Manchu wing.
9
二十一年，偕郎坦率兵至黑龍江覘羅剎形勢，賜御用裘服、弓矢。 與郎坦還奏，上命寧古塔將軍巴海、副都統薩布素，建木城於黑龍江、呼瑪爾，調取所部兵一千五百人往駐焉。 又命尚書伊桑阿赴寧古塔督造戰船。 尋擢朋春正紅旗滿洲副都統。 二十四年，詔選八旗及安置山東、河南、山西三省福建投誠藤牌兵，付左都督何祐率赴盛京，命朋春統之，進剿羅剎，以副都統班達爾沙、副都統銜瑪拉、鑾儀使建義侯林興珠、護軍統領佟寶參贊軍務，祐、興珠皆鄭氏將來降者也。 師既行，上遣侍衛關保至黑龍江傳諭曰：「兵凶戰危，朕以仁治天下，素不嗜殺。 以我兵馬精強，器械堅利，羅剎勢不能敵，必獻地歸誠。 爾時勿殺一人，俾還故土，宣朕柔遠至意。 」五月，師薄雅克薩城，遣人諭降，不從。 分水陸兵為兩路，列營夾攻，复移紅衣砲於前，積薪城下，示將焚焉。 羅剎頭目額里克舍詣軍前乞降，乃宥其罪，釋還俘虜，額里克舍引六百餘人徙去，毀木城，以歸附巴什裡等四十五戶及被掠索倫、達呼爾百餘戶安插內地。
In Kangxi 21 he joined Lang Tan on the Amur reconnaissance of the Russians and received imperial furs, bow, and arrows. On their return the emperor told Ningguta general Bahai and deputy Sabsu to erect wooden forts on the Amur and at Humar and station fifteen hundred men there. Minister Yisangga was also sent to Ningguta to oversee warship building. Peng Chun soon became Manchu deputy lieutenant-general of the Plain Red Banner. In Kangxi 24 the court picked Eight Banner troops plus Fujian rattan-shield levies settled in Shandong, Henan, and Shanxi, gave them to Left Commissioner He You for Shengjing, and put Peng Chun in command against the Russians with Bandarsha, acting deputy Ma La, Ceremonial Commissioner Lin Xingzhu (Marquis Jianyi), and Guards Commander Tong Bao on his staff—He You and Lin Xingzhu were Zheng Chenggong's former officers. As the army marched, Bodyguard Guanbao carried the emperor's word to the Amur: "War is cruel; I rule by benevolence and do not love bloodshed. Our men are seasoned and our arms superior—the Russians cannot resist and will surrender territory. When that hour comes, kill none; send them home and proclaim my care for distant peoples." In the fifth month the force reached Albazin and called on them to yield; they refused. Troops split by land and water, camps closed in from both sides, red-coated guns were advanced, and brush was heaped against the wall as if to burn it. Russian chief Yerikexie came to beg pardon; the court forgave him, freed prisoners, and let him withdraw six hundred-odd followers while the timber fort was razed; forty-five Bashiri households and over a hundred seized Solon and Dahur families were settled inland.
10
二十九年，厄魯特與喀爾喀構釁，命裕親王福全為撫遠大將軍，出邊剿噶爾丹，以朋春與都統蘇努參贊軍務。 蘇努率左翼，朋春率右翼，至烏闌布通。 噶爾丹依山列陣，朋春所部為泥淖所阻，蘇努督兵衝擊，大破之。 噶爾丹偽乞和，夜自大磧山遁走。 部議朋春坐奪官，上命寬之，降級留任。 三十一年，命解職赴西路軍前管隊。 三十五年，复授正紅旗蒙古都統。 旋以費揚古為撫遠大將軍，朋春仍參贊軍務，出西路，破噶爾丹於昭莫多。 師還，以本隊護軍驍騎十八人戰死未收其骸，下部議。 以師有功，免罪，仍錄戰績，增註敕書。 三十八年，因病解職。 尋卒。 子增壽，改襲三等公。
In Kangxi 29 Eleuths and Khalkha clashed; Prince Fuquan became Pacify-the-Distant Grand General against Galdan, with Peng Chun and Sunu advising. Sunu took the left, Peng Chun the right, to Ulan Butung. Galdan formed on the hills; Peng Chun's wing bogged in marsh; Sunu charged and routed them. Galdan feigned peace and fled by night over Great Ditch Mountain. The Board urged dismissal; the emperor was lenient, demoted him, and kept him on duty. In Kangxi 31 he was relieved and sent to the western front to command a column. In Kangxi 35 he again became Mongol lieutenant-general of the Plain Red Banner. When Feiyanggu became grand general, Peng Chun again advised him, marched the western route, and helped crush Galdan at Jao Modo. On return, eighteen of his guards killed in battle lay unburied; the case went to the Board. Because the campaign succeeded, he was spared punishment, his battle record kept, and his patent enlarged. In Kangxi 38 illness forced his resignation. He died soon after. His son Zengshou inherited as a third-class duke.
11
薩布素，富察氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人。 四世祖充順巴本，以勇力聞，世為岳克通鄂城長。 太祖時，其後人哈木都率所部來歸，屯吉林，遂家焉。 薩布素自領催授驍騎校，遷協領。 康熙十六年，聖祖遣內大臣覺羅武默訥等瞻禮長白山，至吉林，欲得識路者導引。 寧古塔將軍巴海令薩布素率兵二百，攜三月糧以從。 水陸行，至長白山麓，成禮而還，事具武默訥傳。
Sabsu, of the Fuca clan, belonged to the Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner. Four generations back Chongshun Baben was renowned for valor; his line long ruled Yueketong'e City. Under Taizu, descendant Hamudu brought his people in, settled in Jilin, and the family remained there. Sabsu rose from company commander to assistant commandant. In Kangxi 16 the emperor sent Inner Minister Wumune and others to worship at Mount Changbai; at Jilin they needed a pathfinder. Ningguta general Bahai told Sabsu to take two hundred men and three months' provisions. They went by land and water to the mountain's foot, completed the rites, and returned—the full account is in Wumune's biography.
12
十七年，授薩布素寧古塔副都統。 羅剎據雅克薩，二十一年，詔率兵偕郎坦等勘視雅克薩城形勢，並往視自額蘇哩至黑龍江及通寧古塔水陸道。 尋郎坦還奏羅剎可圖狀，命建木城於黑龍江、呼瑪爾兩地，以巴海與薩布素統寧古塔兵千五百人往駐，造船備砲。 二十二年，疏言：「黑龍江、呼瑪爾距雅克薩尚遠，若駐兵兩處，則勢分道阻，且過雅克薩有尼布楚等城。 羅剎倘水陸運糧，增兵救援，更難為計。 宜乘其積貯未備，速行征剿。 俟造船畢，度七月初旬能抵雅克薩，即統兵直薄城下。 」疏下王大臣議，如所請，上不許。 尋命巴海留守吉林，以薩布素偕寧古塔副都統瓦禮祜率兵駐額蘇哩。 額蘇哩在黑龍江、呼瑪爾之間，為進攻雅克薩要地，有田隴舊跡。 薩布素因移達呼爾防兵五百人赴其地耕種，並請調寧古塔兵三千更番戍守。 上念兵丁更戍勞苦，命在黑龍江建城，備攻具，設斥堠，計程置驛，運糧積貯，設將軍、副都統領之。 擢薩布素為黑龍江將軍，招撫羅剎降人，授以官職，更令轉相招撫。
In Kangxi 17 he became Ningguta deputy lieutenant-general. Russians held Albazin; in Kangxi 21 he was told to join Lang Tan in reconnoitering Albazin and the Esuri–Amur–Ningguta routes by land and water. When Lang Tan reported the Russians could be taken, the court ordered wooden forts on the Amur and at Humar; Bahai and Sabsu were to station fifteen hundred Ningguta men there, build ships, and ready guns. In Kangxi 22 he argued: "Aihui and Humar lie too far from Albazin—split garrisons divide our strength and block the road, while beyond Albazin stand Nerchinsk and other Russian posts. If they supply by river and road and reinforce, our task grows harder still. Strike before their stores are full—move quickly. When the fleet is ready—by early seventh month we can reach Albazin—drive straight to the walls." The princes endorsed his plan; the emperor refused. Bahai was left at Jilin; Sabsu and deputy Walihu took the army to Esuri. Esuri, between the Amur and Humar, is the choke point for Albazin and still shows old field ridges. Sabsu shifted five hundred Dahur guards to farm there and asked for three thousand Ningguta men in rotating reliefs. Pitying rotating garrisons, the emperor ordered an Amur fortress, siege gear, watch posts, courier stations, grain depots, and a general's command. Sabsu became Heilongjiang general, enrolled Russian defectors in office, and set them to recruit others.
13
上命都統瓦山、侍郎果丕與薩布素議師期，薩布素請以來年四月水陸並進，攻雅克薩城，不克，則刈其田禾。 上謂攻羅剎當期必克，倘謀事草率，將益肆猖狂。 二十四年，以朋春等統兵進攻，薩布素會師，克雅克薩城，乃命薩布素移駐墨爾根，建城防禦。 二十五年，疏言羅剎复踞雅克薩，請督修戰艦，俟冰泮進剿。 上遣郎中滿丕往诇得實，乃命薩布素暫停墨爾根兵丁遷移家口，速修戰監，率寧古塔兵二千人往攻。 又命郎坦、班達爾沙會師，抵雅克薩城。 城西瀕江，薩布素令於城三面掘壕築壘為長圍，對江駐水師，未冰時泊舟東西岸，截尼布楚援兵，冰時藏舟上流汊港內； 馬有疲羸者，分發墨爾根、黑龍江飼秣，計持久。 上因荷蘭貢使以書諭俄羅斯察罕汗，答書請遣使畫界，先釋雅克薩圍，上允之，命撤圍。 二十八年，俄羅斯使臣費耀多囉等至尼布楚，命內大臣索額圖等往會，令發黑龍江兵千五百人為衛。 尋議以大興安嶺及格爾必齊河為界，毀雅克薩城，徙其人去。 二十九年，薩布素入覲，賜賚優渥，命坐內大臣班。 尋命總管索倫等部貢物，疏陳各部生計土俗採捕之事，擬為則例以上，上悉允行。
Commander Walishan and Vice Minister Guobi were sent to fix the campaign date; Sabsu proposed a joint land–river assault next fourth month, cutting the fields if Albazin held. The emperor warned that war on the Russians must promise victory—rash plans would only embolden them. In Kangxi 24 Peng Chun led the assault; Sabsu joined, took Albazin, then moved to Merjen to build defenses. In Kangxi 25 he reported Russians back at Albazin and asked to ready the fleet for a spring offensive. Director Manpi confirmed the report; Sabsu was told to halt family removals from Merjen, finish the fleet, and take two thousand Ningguta men. Lang Tan and Bandarsha were ordered to unite on Albazin. The west wall hugs the river; Sabsu ringed three land faces with trenches and breastworks, posted the flotilla opposite, kept boats on both banks before freeze-up to block Nerchinsk relief, and hid them in upstream inlets in winter; tired horses went to Merjen and Heilongjiang to recover—he planned a long siege. A Dutch envoy's letter reached Russia's Tsagan Khan; Russia asked to delimit borders and lift the Albazin siege first—the emperor agreed and ordered the lines withdrawn. In Kangxi 28 Russian envoys led by Feodor came to Nerchinsk; Songgotu met them with fifteen hundred Heilongjiang guards. The treaty set the Greater Khingan and Gorbitschi River as boundary, razed Albazin, and relocated its people. In Kangxi 29 he attended court, received lavish gifts, and was seated among inner ministers. He was put in charge of Solon tribute, reported each tribe's economy and customs, drafted regulations, and won approval on every point.
14
三十一年，奏建齊齊哈爾及白都訥城，以科爾沁部獻進錫伯、卦爾察、達呼爾壯丁萬四千有奇分駐二城，編佐領，隸上三旗，並設防守尉、防禦等官。 噶爾丹入犯，疏陳進兵事宜，略言：「興安嶺北形勝地，以索約爾濟山為最。 已遣識路官兵自盛京、吉林、墨爾根審度至山遠近，分置驛站，其無水處，掘井以待。 山之東北呼倫貝爾等處有警，與臣駐軍地近，即率墨爾根兵先進，吉林、盛京繼之； 山之西烏勒輝等處有警，則盛京兵先進，臣率部下及吉林兵繼之：皆會於索約爾濟山。 」上可其奏。 三十五年，上親征噶爾丹，自獨石口出中路，大將軍費揚古自歸化城出西路，命薩布素扼其東路，督盛京、寧古塔、科爾沁兵，自索約爾濟山剋期進剿。 四月，上次克魯倫河，噶爾丹西竄，為費揚古所敗。 詔分薩布素所部兵五百人隸費揚古軍。 三十六年，召至京師，尋命回任。
In Kangxi 31 he proposed Qiqihar and Baidune, garrisoning fourteen thousand-odd Xibe, Guwarcha, and Dahur levies from Khorchin in both towns under new assistant commands of the upper three banners with defense officers. When Galdan struck, he outlined the march: "North of the Khingan, Mount Suoyorji is the key ground. Scouts from Shengjing, Jilin, and Merjen have measured routes, set relay stations, and dug wells where water fails. If Hulun Buir alarms the northeast near my post, Merjen marches first, Jilin and Shengjing follow; if Ulehui alarms the west, Shengjing leads and I with Jilin follow—all converging on Suoyorji." The emperor approved. In Kangxi 35 the emperor marched on Galdan from Dushikou while Feiyanggu took the west from Guihua; Sabsu blocked the east with Shengjing, Ningguta, and Khorchin columns timed from Suoyorji. In the fourth month the emperor reached the Kerulen; Galdan fled west into Feiyanggu's defeat. Five hundred of Sabsu's men were assigned to Feiyanggu. In Kangxi 36 he was called to Beijing, then sent back.
15
初，邊境有墨爾哲勒屯長，累世輸貢。 康熙初，屯長扎努喀布克托請率眾內移，寧古塔將軍巴海安輯於墨爾根，編四十佐領，號新滿洲。 薩布素奏於墨爾根兩翼立學，設助教，選新滿洲及錫伯、索倫、達呼爾每佐領下幼童一，教習書義。 是為黑龍江建學之始。 三十七年，上幸吉林，褒其勤勞，予一等阿達哈哈番世職，並御用冠服，於眾前宣諭賜之。 尋疏言黑龍江屯堡因災荒積欠米石，請俟年豐交倉。 上以薩布素曾奏革任總督蔡毓榮經理十二堡，著有成效； 嗣因官堡荒棄，請停止屯種，將壯丁改歸驛站，存貯倉米，支放無餘，致駐防兵餉匱乏，責令回奏。 薩布素具疏引罪，請以齊齊哈爾、墨爾根駐防兵每年輪派五百人往錫伯等處耕種官田，穫穀運齊齊哈爾交倉。 詔侍郎滿丕等往按，以薩布素將荒廢地妄報成效，並浮支穀石，應斬，命罷任，奪世職，在佐領上行走。 尋授散秩大臣。
On the frontier a Merenjeile company chief had long paid tribute. Early in Kangxi chief Zanukabukto asked to move inland; Bahai settled them at Merjen in forty companies styled New Manchus. Sabsu founded schools on both Merjen wings, appointed tutors, and enrolled one boy per company from New Manchus, Xibe, Solon, and Dahur to learn letters. Thus began Heilongjiang's schools. In Kangxi 37 at Jilin the emperor praised his service, gave him a first-class hereditary rank and imperial robes, and announced the gift before the host. He reported Heilongjiang farms in arrears after famine and asked to repay the granaries in good years. The emperor recalled Sabsu had urged removing Governor Cai Yurong from twelve forts with good results; later he halted farming, shifted labor to posts, drained the granaries, and left garrison pay short—the emperor demanded an account. Sabsu confessed and proposed rotating five hundred Qiqihar and Merjen garrisoners yearly to farm state land among the Xibe and ship grain to Qiqihar. Manpi investigated; Sabsu had inflated yields on wasteland and overspent grain—the Board sought execution; he was dismissed, lost his title, and kept only an assistant command. He was soon made minister without portfolio.
16
三十九年，卒。 乾隆間，敕修盛京通志，列名宦，且稱薩布素諳練明敏，得軍民心，其平羅剎及黑龍江興學，有文武幹濟才雲。
He died in Kangxi 39. Under Qianlong the Shengjing Gazetteer ranked him among eminent officials, praising his skill, soldiers' devotion, pacification of the Russians, and founding Heilongjiang schools—civil and military talent together.
17
瑪拉，那喇氏，滿洲鑲白旗人，尚書尼堪從子。 尼堪卒，無子，瑪拉與叔阿穆爾圖、阿錫圖及弟兆資分襲尼堪世職，瑪拉襲三等阿達哈哈番。 初任理籓院筆帖式。 順治五年，英親王阿濟格徵叛將薑瓖，圍大同，令瑪拉調蒙古兵以從。 累遷理籓院副理事官。 康熙十四年，察哈爾布爾尼叛，聖祖命信郡王鄂扎帥師討之。 瑪拉自陳久任理籓院習知蒙古狀，原赴軍前效力，遂命與員外郎色棱赴科爾沁諸部調選兵馬協剿。 師還，擢通政使，遷禮部侍郎。 十六年，擢工部尚書。 偕內大臣喀岱往科爾沁諸外籓宣諭禁令。 瑪拉初受任，上誡以工部積弊，宜殫心釐剔。 十九年，坐不能清積弊，議降五秩，詔從寬留任。 復以饗殿器用修造疏忽，奪尚書，仍留世職。
Ma La, Nara clan, Manchu Bordered White Banner, was Minister Nikan's nephew. Nikan died childless; Ma La, uncles Amurtu and Asitu, and brother Zhaozi split the title—Ma La took a third-class adaha fan. He began as a Colonial Affairs clerk. In Shunzhi 5 Prince Ying Ajige besieged Datong against Jiang Xiang; Ma La mustered Mongol auxiliaries. He rose to associate director in the Colonial Affairs office. In Kangxi 14 Chahar Borji rebelled; the emperor sent Prince Xin Eje against him. Ma La said long Colonial Affairs service taught him Mongolia and offered himself; he and Director Seleng were sent to Khorchin to levy horse and men. After the campaign he became communications commissioner, then vice minister of rites. In Kangxi 16 he became minister of works. With Inner Minister Kadai he announced bans among the outer Khorchin banners. On taking office the emperor warned him to purge the Works Ministry's chronic abuses. In Kangxi 19 failure to reform brought a five-rank demotion proposal; the emperor kept him on. Negligence over feast-hall vessels cost him the ministry but not his title.
18
二十二年，上以俄羅斯數犯邊，擾及索倫、飛牙喀諸部，命集兵黑龍江，將進討，遣瑪拉往索倫儲軍實。 尋疏言：「索倫總管博克所獲俄羅斯人及軍前招降者，皆迫於軍威，不宜久留索倫，應移之內地。 」詔允行。 復言：「雅克薩、尼布楚二城久為羅剎所據，臣密诇雅克薩惟耕種自給，尼布楚歲捕貂與喀爾喀貿易資養贍。 請飭喀爾喀車臣汗禁所部與尼布楚貿易，並飭黑龍江將軍水陸並進，示將攻取雅克薩，因刈其田禾，則俄羅斯將不戰自困。 」上然之，即以瑪拉所奏檄示喀爾喀。 二十四年，遣都統朋春等帥師往黑龍江議進兵，授瑪拉副都統銜，參贊軍務。 遣蒙古兵三十诇雅克薩城，生擒羅剎七人，得城中設備及乞援各部狀。 是年夏，朋春等攻羅剎克之，逐其人。 瑪拉在事有功。 二十五年，黑龍江佐領鄂色以耕牛多斃，農器損壞，奏請儲備，命瑪拉往黑龍江督理農務。 諭曰：「農事關軍餉，令嚴督合力播種。 」值歲豐，收穫甚稔。 二十七年，授護軍統領。
In Kangxi 22 Russia's border raids stirred Solon and Feiyaka; as troops gathered on the Amur, Ma La went to Solon to stock supplies. He urged moving inland Russians seized by Solon commander Boke or taken in battle—men held only by fear. The court agreed. He added: "Albazin and Nerchinsk are long Russian—Albazin farms for itself; Nerchinsk lives on sable trade with the Khalkha. Cut Nerchinsk trade via the Tushiyetu Khan, advance on Albazin by land and river, and mow their fields—Russia will collapse without battle." The emperor agreed and sent his memorial to the Khalkha. In Kangxi 24 Peng Chun went to the Amur to plan the advance; Ma La joined as acting deputy with staff rank. Thirty Mongols scouted Albazin, took seven Russians alive, and learned the garrison's state and pleas for help. That summer Peng Chun stormed Albazin and expelled the garrison. Ma La earned credit in the campaign. In Kangxi 25 assistant commandant Ese reported dead oxen and broken tools; Ma La was sent to oversee farming. He was told: "Farms feed the army—supervise planting closely." Harvest that year was abundant. In Kangxi 27 he became guards commander.
19
二十九年，噶爾丹侵掠喀爾喀，命瑪拉偕都統額赫納、前鋒統領碩鼐等率兵往討之，賜內廝馬以行。 未幾，噶爾丹掠烏珠穆沁，命裕親王福全等分統大軍出塞擊之，噶爾丹敗遁。 師旋，三十年，復來犯，至阿爾哈賚，無所掠而遁。 時土謝圖汗、車臣汗率所部來歸，上幸塞外撫輯，瑪拉扈從。 旋命偕都統瓦岱等率兵赴圖拉偵噶爾丹，抵克魯倫河，聞其遠竄，乃還。 授西安將軍。
In Kangxi 29, as Galdan raided the Khalkha, Ma La marched with Ehena and Shuonai and received an imperial horse. Soon Galdan struck Ujimchin; Prince Fuquan drove him beyond the passes in defeat. In Kangxi 30 he returned to Arhabsan, found no booty, and withdrew. As the Tushiyetu and Tsetsen khans submitted, the emperor toured the frontier and Ma La attended. He and Wadai were sent to scout Tula, reached the Kerulen, heard Galdan had fled, and turned back. He became Xi'an general.
20
三十二年，準噶爾和碩特部台吉巴圖爾額爾克濟農來降，上以其人未可信，命瑪拉徙入內地，毋令复逸。 瑪拉疏言：「巴圖爾額爾克濟農率所屬二千餘口，窮乏來歸，揆其情狀，當不復逸。 」遂遣官護送，併其子台吉雲木春來朝，優賚遣之。 未幾，瑪拉卒於官，賜祭葬，諡敏恪。
In Kangxi 32 Khoshut taiji Batur Erkejin of Dzungaria surrendered; distrusting him, the emperor told Ma La to resettle him inland. Ma La judged two thousand-odd destitute followers would not flee again. Escorts were sent; his son Yunmuchun attended court, was rewarded, and dismissed. He soon died in office with funeral honors and posthumous name Minke.
21
論曰：俄羅斯之為羅剎，譯言緩急異耳，非必東部別有是名也。 初遣兵诇敵，郎坦主其事； 取雅克薩城，朋春、薩布素迭為將，而郎坦與瑪拉實佐之。 尼布楚盟定，開市庫倫，是為我國與他國定約互市之始。 用兵當期必克，我苟草率，彼益猖狂，聖祖諭薩布素數言，得馭夷之要矣。
The commentary notes: "Luocha" for Russia is mere transliteration—not a separate eastern people. Lang Tan led the first reconnaissance; Peng Chun and Sabsu commanded the captures, with Lang Tan and Ma La as the chief aides. After Nerchinsk, Khuree trade opened—the first treaty market with a foreign power. War demands sure victory; rashness only emboldens foes—the emperor's words to Sabsu state the art of handling frontier powers.
