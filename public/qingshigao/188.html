In the fourth month, on bingxu day, he was transferred again as Left Vice Minister of the Board of Works. In the sixth month, on jiazi day, he was transferred with concurrent acting duties. In the eighth month, on guiwei day, he became Left Vice Minister of the Board of Works. In the fourth month, on bingxu day, he was transferred as Right Vice Minister of the Board of Works.
