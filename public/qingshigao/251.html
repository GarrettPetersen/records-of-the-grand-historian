圖海李之芳圖海，字麟洲，馬佳氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 父穆哈達，世居綏芬。 圖海自筆帖式歷國史院侍讀。 世祖嘗幸南苑，負寶從，顧其舉止，以為非常人。 擢內秘書院學士，授拜他喇布勒哈番，遷弘文院大學士、議政大臣。 順治十二年，加太子太保，攝刑部尚書事。 與大學士巴哈納等同訂律例。 侍衛阿拉那與公額爾克戴青兩家奴鬥於市，讞失實，坐欺罔，免死，削職。 世祖崩，遺命起用。 聖祖即位，授正黃旗滿洲都統。
Tu Hai and Li Zhifang. Tu Hai, whose style was Linzhou, belonged to the Majia clan and was a Manchu of the Plain Yellow Banner. His father Muha'da came from a family that had long lived in Suifen. Tu Hai worked his way up from copy clerk to reader at the Grand Secretariat of National History. When the Shizu Emperor once visited the Southern Park, Tu Hai attended carrying the imperial seal. The emperor took note of his bearing and judged him a man of uncommon ability. He was promoted to academician of the Inner Secretariat, granted the Defender of the Nation Baturu rank, and then made Grand Secretary of the Hall of Literary Glory and a deliberating minister of state. In Shunzhi 12 he received the additional title Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent and served concurrently as Minister of Justice. He worked with Grand Secretary Bahana and others to compile the revised legal code. When a bodyguard named Alana and the bond-servants of Duke Erke Daiqing fought in the market, Tu Hai's ruling did not square with the facts. Convicted of deceiving the court, he was spared execution but dismissed from office. On the Shizu Emperor's death, his final edict ordered Tu Hai brought back into service. After the Kangxi Emperor ascended the throne, Tu Hai was made banner general of the Manchu Plain Yellow Banner.
3
李自成餘眾郝搖旗、劉體純、李來亨嘯聚鄖、襄間。 康熙二年，命圖海為定西將軍，副靖西將軍都統穆里瑪，將禁旅，會湖廣、四川諸軍討之，屢破賊。 未幾，郝搖旗為副都統杜敏所擒，劉體純亦破滅，惟李來亨據茅麓山，恃險負固，圖海圍之，絕其外援。 來亨窮蹙，自焚死，其下以眾降。 執斬明新樂王及所署置官屬，俘三千餘以還。 六年，復為弘文院大學士，進一等阿達哈哈番。 頃之，以兼都統乞解機務，不許。 九年，改中和殿大學士，兼禮部尚書。
Remnants of Li Zicheng's forces—Hao Yaoqi, Liu Tichun, and Li Laiheng—gathered and held out between Yun and Xiang. In Kangxi 2 Tu Hai was appointed Pacifier of the West General, second to banner commander Murima, Pacifier of the West. He led the imperial guard, joined forces from Huguang and Sichuan, and repeatedly routed the rebels. Soon Hao Yaoqi was taken by Vice Banner Commander Du Min and Liu Tichun's force was wiped out. Only Li Laiheng remained on Maolu Mountain, holding the heights in a last stand. Tu Hai besieged him and severed his outside support. Cornered, Laiheng set himself on fire and died; his followers surrendered en masse. He captured and executed the Ming Prince of Xinle along with Li's appointed officials, then returned with more than three thousand prisoners. In Kangxi 6 he was again made Grand Secretary of the Hall of Literary Glory and promoted to first-class Ada Ha Banner rank. Soon afterward he asked to be relieved of ministerial duties on account of his concurrent banner command; the court refused. In Kangxi 9 he became Grand Secretary of the Hall of Central Harmony and concurrently Minister of Rites.
4
十二年，平南王尚可喜請老。 七月，吳三桂繼之，實探朝旨。 廷議移籓狀，莫洛、米思翰、明珠等皆主如所請，惟圖海持不可。 上意決，遂黜圖海議。 三桂既反，命攝戶部，理餉運。
In Kangxi 12 the Pacifier of the South, Prince Shang Kexi, petitioned to retire. In the seventh month Wu Sangui did the same—chiefly to sound out the court's intentions. When the court debated removing the princely fiefs, Moluo, Misihan, Mingzhu, and others all favored acceding to the requests—only Tu Hai opposed it. The emperor had already made up his mind, and Tu Hai's dissent was set aside. Once Sangui had risen in revolt, Tu Hai was put in charge of the Ministry of Revenue to manage grain and supply transport.
5
十四年，察哈爾布爾尼劫其父阿布柰以叛。 命信郡王鄂扎為撫遠大將軍，圖海副之，討布爾尼。 時禁旅多調發，圖海請籍八旗家奴驍健者率以行，在路騷掠，一不問。 至，下令曰：「察哈爾元裔，多珍寶，破之富且倍！ 」於是士卒奮勇，無不一當百。 戰於達祿，布爾尼設伏山谷，別以三千人來拒。 既戰，伏發，土默特兵挫。 圖海分兵迎擊，敵以四百騎繼進，力戰，覆其眾。 布爾尼乃悉眾出，用火攻，圖海令嚴陣待，連擊大破之，招撫人戶一千三百餘。 布爾尼以三十騎遁，科爾沁額駙沙津追斬之，察哈爾平。 師還，聖祖禦南苑大紅門，行郊勞禮。 敘功，進一等阿思哈尼哈番。
In Kangxi 14 the Chahar prince Bulwendi took his father Abunai hostage and rebelled. Trustful Prince Ezha was named Pacifying the Distant Grand General, with Tu Hai as deputy, to suppress Bulwendi. With most of the imperial guard already deployed, Tu Hai had sturdy banner bond-servants mustered to march with the army. They looted along the route, and he ignored every complaint. On reaching the front he proclaimed: "The Chahar are descendants of the Yuan and sit on great treasure—break them and you will be more than twice as rich!" The men took heart, and each fought as if he were worth a hundred. At Dalu, Bulwendi laid an ambush in the valleys and sent another three thousand men to block the advance. When battle was joined the ambush sprang, and the Tumed contingent was thrown into disorder. Tu Hai split off a detachment to counterattack; when four hundred enemy horsemen came up in support, his men fought hard and annihilated them. Bulwendi then committed his whole army and used fire attacks. Tu Hai held a tight formation, counterattacked again and again, and broke them completely, winning over more than thirteen hundred households. Bulwendi escaped with thirty riders; Khorchin imperial son-in-law Shajin ran him down and killed him, and the Chahar were pacified. On the army's return the Kangxi Emperor went to the Great Red Gate of the Southern Park to perform the suburban welcome for the victorious troops. His services were rewarded with promotion to first-class Asihani Banner rank.
6
陝西提督王輔臣以平涼叛應三桂，定西大將軍貝勒董額督諸軍攻之，久未下。 三桂遣王屏籓、吳之茂等犯秦、隴，欲與平涼合。 十五年，以圖海為撫遠大將軍，八旗每佐領出護軍二名，率以往。 臨發，上御太和殿賜敕印，命諸軍咸聽節制。 既至，明賞罰，申約束。 諸將請乘勢攻城，圖海宣言曰：「仁義之師，先招撫，後攻伐。 今奉天威討叛豎，無慮不克。 顧城中生靈數十萬，覆巢之下，殺戮必多。 當體聖主好生之德，俟其向化。 」城中聞者，莫不感泣，思自拔。 五月，奪虎山墩，虎山墩者，在平涼城北，高數十仞，賊守以精兵，通餉道。 圖海曰：「此平涼咽喉也。 」率兵仰攻，賊萬餘列火器以拒師。 圖海令兵更迭進，自巳至午，戰益力，遂奪而據之，發大砲攻城，城人洶懼。 圖海用幕客周昌策，招輔臣降。
Shaanxi Provincial Commander Wang Fuchen rebelled at Pingliang in league with Sangui. Pacifier of the West Grand General Prince Dong'e led the siege, yet the city held out for a long time. Sangui sent Wang Pingfan, Wu Zhimao, and others into Qin and Long, aiming to link up with the Pingliang garrison. In Kangxi 15 Tu Hai was appointed Pacifying the Distant Grand General. Each banner company supplied two guardsmen, and he marched west at their head. Before he set out, the emperor received him in the Hall of Supreme Harmony, gave him the command seal and edict, and placed every army under his orders. Once on the ground he made rewards and punishments unmistakable and tightened discipline throughout the command. When his generals urged an immediate assault, Tu Hai declared: "A humane army wins men over before it strikes." We march under Heaven's mandate to crush these rebels—victory is certain. Yet hundreds of thousands of lives are trapped inside; when the nest is torn apart, the killing will be immense. We must honor the emperor's love of life and give them time to submit of their own accord. Word spread inside the walls; men wept and began to think of breaking away from the rebels. In the fifth month he took Tiger Hill Redoubt north of Pingliang—a height of many tens of ren held by elite rebels and the lifeline of their grain road. Tu Hai said, "This is Pingliang's throat." He led his men up the slope; more than ten thousand defenders massed firearms to block them. Tu Hai sent his men forward in relays from mid-morning to noon, pressing harder until the redoubt fell. He then turned heavy guns on the city, and panic spread among its people. Tu Hai followed a plan devised by his secretary Zhou Chang to bring Fuchen over.
7
昌，字培公，荊門諸生。 好奇計。 佐振武將軍吳丹有勞，以七品官錄用。 圖海次潼關，以策乾之，客諸幕。 輔臣所署置總兵黃九疇、布政使龔榮遇皆昌鄉人，屢勸輔臣反正，以蠟丸告昌，昌白圖海。 圖海即令昌入城諭降，輔臣遣其將從昌出謁，圖海聞上，上許之。 乃假昌參議道，賚詔往撫。 輔臣使榮遇上軍民冊，子繼貞繳三桂所授敕印，顧猶觀望，復命昌偕兄子保定諭之，乃薙發降。 因令吳丹入城撫定。
Zhou Chang, style Peigong, was a licentiate from Jingmen. He was fond of bold stratagems. After serving Pacifier of Martial Valor Wu Dan with distinction, he was enrolled as a seventh-rank official. When Tu Hai camped at Tong Pass he took to Chang's plans and brought him onto his staff. Regional commander Huang Jiuchou and administration commissioner Gong Rongyu, whom Fuchen had appointed, were both from Chang's home district. They kept urging Fuchen to return to the throne, sent messages to Chang in wax pellets, and Chang reported this to Tu Hai. Tu Hai sent Chang into the city to negotiate surrender. Fuchen dispatched a general with him to pay respects outside the walls. Tu Hai reported to the throne, and the emperor consented. Chang was given the acting rank of participation commissioner and sent in with an imperial edict to win them over. Fuchen had Rongyu submit the civilian and military rolls; his son Jizhen handed over the seal and patent Wu Sangui had given him. Still Fuchen wavered, until Chang went in again with his nephew Baoding to persuade him; then he shaved his head and submitted. Tu Hai then sent Wu Dan into the city to restore order.
8
吳之茂聞平涼下，自秦州遁，遣將軍佛尼勒敗之於牡丹園，又敗之於西和縣北山。 將軍穆佔進攻王屏籓於樂門，敗賊於紅崖，復禮縣。 輔臣所署置巡撫陳彭，總兵週養民、王好問等相繼降。 秦地略定。 敘功，進三等公，世襲。
Hearing that Pingliang had fallen, Wu Zhimao fled from Qinzhou. General Fonile routed him at Mudan Garden and again on North Mountain in Xihe County. General Muzhan pressed Wang Pingfan at Lemen, broke the rebels at Red Cliff, and recovered Li County. Provincial governor Chen Peng and regional commanders Zhou Yangmin and Wang Haowen, whom Fuchen had appointed, surrendered in turn. The Qin region was largely brought under control. For his services he was made a third-rank duke with a hereditary title.
9
圖海疏請遣兵赴湖廣，會徵三桂，上命圖海親率精銳以行。 圖海疏陳陝西初定、反側未安狀，乃授穆佔征南將軍，率滿洲兵及平涼降卒往，圖海仍留鎮。 時平涼、慶陽雖下，漢中、興安猶為賊據。 圖海奏調綠旗兵，期明年正月檄提督孫思克赴秦州，趙良棟赴鳳翔，與張勇、王進寶會師進取，勇等謂須俟夏秋。 上慮克漢中、興安轉餉難，令守諸要隘，分兵赴荊州攻三桂。 十六年，圖海招撫韓城等縣偽官，又遣兵逼禮縣、益門，先後敗賊五盤山、喬家山、塘坊廟、芭蕉園、沙窩諸處，复塔什堡。 十七年，复疏請分兵下漢中、興安，上密諭止之。 將軍佛尼勒等又敗賊牛頭山香泉，四川總督周有德亦敗賊秦嶺，复潼關堡五寨。 慶陽賊袁本秀受三桂劄，謀亂。 圖海發慶陽、宜君、延安三營兵，會王進寶討平之，斬本秀衛遠溝。 頃之，入覲。 十八年，還鎮。
Tu Hai asked to send forces to Huguang to join the campaign against Sangui; the emperor ordered him to lead the elite corps in person. Tu Hai explained that Shaanxi had only just been secured and loyalties were still unsettled. Muzhan was made Pacifier of the South and sent south with Manchu troops and Pingliang defectors, while Tu Hai stayed behind to hold the province. Pingliang and Qingyang were in hand, but Hanzhong and Xing'an remained in rebel hands. Tu Hai asked for Green Standard reinforcements and planned to order Sun Sike to Qinzhou and Zhao Liangdong to Fengxiang in the first month of the new year, there to join Zhang Yong and Wang Jinbao for a joint advance. Yong and the others insisted they must wait until summer or autumn. The emperor worried that seizing Hanzhong and Xing'an would strain supply lines, so he ordered the key passes held and detachments sent to Jingzhou against Sangui. In Kangxi 16 Tu Hai induced the puppet officials of Hancheng and neighboring counties to submit, pressed Li County and Yimen, and in a series of actions routed rebel bands at Wupanshan, Qiaojiashan, Tangfang Temple, Bajiao Garden, Shawo, and elsewhere, retaking Tashibao. In Kangxi 17 he again asked to split forces and take Hanzhong and Xing'an; the emperor privately told him to hold off. General Fonile routed the rebels again at Niutoushan Xiangquan, while Sichuan Governor-General Zhou Youde beat them on the Qin Mountains and retook the five stockades of Tongguan Fort. At Qingyang the rebel Yuan Benxiu, having received orders from Sangui, plotted an uprising. Tu Hai sent the Qingyang, Yijun, and Yan'an garrisons, joined Wang Jinbao to crush the plot, and beheaded Benxiu at Weiyuangou. Before long he went to court for an audience. In Kangxi 18 he returned to his post.
10
湖南、廣西平。 上命亟攻寶雞，規取漢中、興安，定四川。 圖海乃厲師攻益門鎮，破之。 會賊毀偏橋，兵不得進，狀聞，詔嚴責。 乃決策期分四路：圖海親率將軍佛尼勒等趨興安，總兵官程福亮為後援，屯舊縣關； 將軍畢力克圖、提督孫思克等自略陽進，總兵官硃衣客為後援，駐西河； 將軍王進寶、總兵官費雅達自棧道進，總兵官高孟為後援，駐寶雞； 提督趙良棟自徽縣進。 十月，師次鎮安，分兵為二隊，進敗三桂將王遇隆，渡乾玉河，奪梁河關。 三桂將韓晉卿遁。 進寶亦復漢中。 良棟复徽縣、略陽。 畢力克圖復成縣，又復階州，遣參將康調元復文縣。 於是平利、紫陽、石泉、漢陰、洵陽、白河、竹山、竹溪、上津諸縣皆下。 興安既克，圖海統大軍之半屯鳳翔，尋移漢中，護諸軍餉。 會降將譚洪复叛，陝西總督哈佔溯江討之，詔圖海遙為聲援。
Hunan and Guangxi were pacified. The emperor ordered an immediate drive on Baoji, aiming to seize Hanzhong and Xing'an and secure Sichuan. Tu Hai tightened discipline and stormed Yimen Town, taking it. The rebels destroyed Pian Bridge and blocked the advance. When word reached Beijing, an edict rebuked him sharply. He then set a coordinated advance on four axes: Tu Hai would lead General Fonile and others toward Xing'an, with Regional Commander Cheng Fuliang in reserve at Jiuxian Pass; General Biliketu and Provincial Commander Sun Sike would advance from Lueyang, with Regional Commander Zhu Yike in reserve at Xihe; General Wang Jinbao and Regional Commander Feiyada would move up the plank road, with Regional Commander Gao Meng holding Baoji in reserve; Provincial Commander Zhao Liangdong would advance from Huixian. In the tenth month the army reached Zhen'an, split into two columns, routed Sangui's general Wang Yulong, crossed the Qianyu River, and took Lianghe Pass. Sangui's general Han Jinqing fled. Jinbao recovered Hanzhong as well. Liangdong retook Huixian and Lueyang. Biliketu retook Cheng County and Jiezhou, and sent Assistant Regional Commander Kang Tiaoyuan to recover Wen County. Pingli, Ziyang, Shiquan, Hanyin, Xunyang, Baihe, Zhushan, Zhuxi, Shangjin, and the other counties then fell in turn. After Xing'an fell, Tu Hai kept half the main force at Fengxiang, then shifted his headquarters to Hanzhong to supervise army supplies. When the surrendered general Tan Hong rose again, Shaanxi Governor-General Hazhan pursued him up the river; Tu Hai was ordered to support the operation from a distance.
11
二十年，以疾徵還。 卒，諡文襄。 太宗實錄成，贈少保兼太子太傅。 雍正初，追贈一等忠達公，配享太廟。 子諾敏，襲爵，歷刑、禮二部尚書，正黃旗蒙古都統。 諾敏子馬爾賽，自有傳。
In Kangxi 20 illness brought him a recall to the capital. He died and was posthumously titled Wenxiang. When the Veritable Records of Taizong were finished, he was posthumously made Junior Guardian and Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent. Early in the Yongzheng reign he was posthumously raised to first-rank Duke of Loyal Achievement and granted a place in the Imperial Ancestral Temple. His son Nuomin inherited the title, held the ministries of Justice and Rites in turn, and became banner general of the Mongol Plain Yellow Banner. Nuomin's son Ma'ersai is treated in a separate biography.
12
周昌初入城，自陳父明季死流寇，母孫剜目破面觸棺死，原捐軀表母烈。 及輔臣降，圖海以聞。 上命旌其母，遣官致祭，授昌布政使參政。 昌复參蔡毓榮軍事，事平，授山東登萊道，攝布政使，以與總兵互訐罷。 昌既罷，猶喜言兵。 噶爾丹擾邊，數上書當事陳利害。 後卒於家。
On his first entry into the city Zhou Chang told how his father had been killed by late-Ming bandits and how his mother Sun had gouged out her eyes, scarred her face, and thrown herself against the coffin to die with her husband. He asked to give his own life to honor her devotion. After Fuchen submitted, Tu Hai reported the matter to the throne. The emperor ordered honors for his mother, sent officials to perform the rites, and appointed Chang provincial administration participation commissioner. Chang again served on Cai Yurong's staff. After the war he was made Deng-Lai circuit intendant in Shandong and acting provincial administration commissioner, but was dismissed after feuding with the regional commander. Even after his dismissal Chang kept offering advice on military matters. When Galdan raided the frontier he wrote repeatedly to officials in charge, laying out the stakes. He later died at home.
13
李之芳，字鄴園，山東武定人。 順治四年進士，授金華府推官。 卓異，擢刑部主事。 累遷郎中，授廣西道御史。 疏請革錢糧陋規，禁州縣官迎送。 十七年，巡按山西。 聖祖即位，裁巡按，召回。 康熙元年，乞假歸。 二年，复授湖廣道御史。 五年，巡視浙江鹽政。 入掌河南道事。
Li Zhifang, style Yeyuan, came from Wuding in Shandong. He passed the jinshi examination in Shunzhi 4 and was appointed investigating censor of Jinhua Prefecture. Marked outstanding in evaluation, he was promoted to secretary in the Ministry of Justice. He rose through the ranks to director and was made censor of the Guangxi circuit. He memorialized to abolish corrupt tax-collection practices and forbid prefectural and county officials from lavish escorts. In Kangxi 17 he served as touring inspector of Shanxi. When the Kangxi Emperor came to the throne the touring-inspector posts were abolished and Li was recalled. In Kangxi 1 he took leave and returned home. In Kangxi 2 he was again made censor of the Huguang circuit. In Kangxi 5 he inspected Zhejiang's salt administration. He took charge of Henan circuit affairs.
14
大學士班布爾善坐鰲拜黨誅，之芳疏言：「昔大學士俱內直，諸司章奏，即日票擬。 自鰲拜輔政，大學士皆不入直，疏奏俱至次日看詳。 請復舊制，杜任意更改之弊。 」又疏言：「世祖時賞罰出至公，督撫不敢恣睢無忌。 十八年以後，督撫率多夤緣而得，有恃無恐。 勒索屬員，擾害百姓。 夫直省億萬之眾，皆世祖留遺之群黎，我皇上愛養之赤子，何堪此輩朘削？ 自與受同罪之法嚴，與者不承，則言者即涉虛，非特不敢糾督撫，且不敢糾司道守令。 有貪之利，無貪之害，又何憚而不怙惡自恣？ 今皇上親政，乞親裁，罷黜溺職督撫，以肅吏治。 」疏下部，尋甄別各省督撫，黜其尤者數人。 進秩視四品，擢左副都御史。 之芳數上封事，請嚴巡鹽考績，慎外官罰俸，皆關治體。 遷吏部侍郎。
After Grand Secretary Banbu'ershan was executed as a member of Oboi's faction, Zhifang memorialized: "In former times every grand secretary kept internal duty, and ministry memorials received draft rescripts the same day." Once Oboi dominated the regency, grand secretaries no longer kept internal duty, and memorials were not reviewed until the following day. Restore the old practice, he urged, to end the abuse of arbitrary tampering. He also wrote: "Under the Shizu Emperor rewards and punishments were utterly fair, and governors-general and governors did not dare run wild." After Kangxi 18 most governors-general and governors won their posts through connections and acted without fear. They squeezed their subordinates and preyed on the people. The tens of millions in the provinces are the people the Shizu Emperor bequeathed and the children our emperor nurtures—how can they endure being stripped by such men? With the law punishing briber and bribed alike, if the recipient denies guilt the accuser is treated as slanderous. Men no longer dare impeach governors-general and governors—nor even circuit intendants and magistrates. When corruption pays and honesty costs, why should they shrink from clinging to evil and doing as they pleased? Now that Your Majesty rules in person, I beg you to judge for yourself, dismiss negligent governors-general and governors, and restore official discipline. The memorial went to the ministries; soon the court screened provincial governors and dismissed several of the worst offenders. His rank was raised to equivalent fourth grade and he was made Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. Zhifang repeatedly submitted sealed memorials urging stricter evaluation of salt inspectors and caution in fining local officials—measures that touched the foundations of good government. He was transferred to Vice Minister of Personnel.
15
十二年，以兵部侍郎總督浙江軍務。 會吳三桂反，十三年，奏請復標兵原額，督習槍砲。 疏甫上，耿精忠亦叛，遣其將曾養性、白顯忠、馬九玉數道闚浙，浙大震。 之芳檄諸將扼仙霞關，調總兵李榮率副將王廷梅、牟大寅、陳世凱、鮑虎等分道禦寇。 時上命都統賴塔率師入浙，五月，偕賴塔率滿洲兵千、綠旗兵二千、鄉勇五百，進駐衢州。 眾皆謂會城重地，不宜輕委。 之芳曰：「不然。 衢踞上游，無衢，是無浙也。 今日之事，義無反顧。 」顯忠自常山陷開化、壽昌、淳安，養性自處州犯義烏、浦江、東陽、湯溪，沿河阻餉道。 溫州鎮總兵祖弘勳叛，召寇陷平陽，再進陷黃岩，集悍卒數万窺衢州。
In Kangxi 12, as Vice Minister of War, he was appointed to direct Zhejiang's military affairs. When Wu Sangui rebelled, in Kangxi 13 he asked to restore the Green Standard troops to full strength and supervised training in firearms and cannon. Hardly had the memorial gone up when Geng Jingzhong rebelled too, sending Zeng Yangxing, Bai Xianzhong, and Ma Jiuyu against Zhejiang by several routes. The province was thrown into alarm. Zhifang ordered his generals to hold Xianxia Pass and called up Regional Commander Li Rong with deputies Wang Tingmei, Mou Dayin, Chen Shikai, Bao Hu, and others to meet the invaders on several fronts. The emperor had ordered Banner Commander Laita into Zhejiang. In the fifth month Zhifang joined him with one thousand Manchu troops, two thousand Green Standard soldiers, and five hundred militia, and marched into Quzhou. Everyone said the provincial capital was too important to leave lightly defended. Zhifang said, "Not so." Quzhou commands the upper river—lose Quzhou and you lose Zhejiang. Today's fight brooks no retreat. Xianzhong swept from Changshan into Kaihua, Shouchang, and Chun'an; Yangxing advanced from Chuzhou against Yiwu, Pujiang, Dongyang, and Tangxi, cutting the river supply line. Wenzhou garrison commander Zu Hongxun rebelled, called in rebels to seize Pingyang, pushed on to Huangyan, and massed tens of thousands of hardened troops against Quzhou.
16
七月，之芳與賴塔閱兵水亭門，率總兵官李榮、副都統瑚圖等薄賊壘，軍坑西。 之芳手執刀督陣，或請少避，之芳曰：「三軍司命在吾，退即為賊乘。 今日勝敗，即吾死生矣！ 」守備程龍怯戰，斬以徇。 麾眾越壕拔柵，敗之。 遣陳世凱乘勝复義烏、湯​​溪，鮑虎复壽昌、淳安，牟大寅破常山，王廷梅敗賊於金華石梁、大溝源，李榮亦復東陽，复敗賊於金華壽溪，馘賊將，毀寨十八。 參將洪起元復嵊縣。 詔嘉之芳調度有方。
In the seventh month Zhifang and Laita reviewed the troops at Water Pavilion Gate, then led Li Rong, Hutu, and others against the rebel camp and encamped west of Junkeng. Zhifang took a blade in hand to direct the line. When aides urged him to step back he said, "The army's fate rests on me—retreat and the enemy will overrun us." Victory or defeat today is my life or death! Garrison commander Cheng Long had hung back in the fight; Zhifang beheaded him as an example to the ranks. He led his men over the ditch, tore down the palisade, and routed the enemy. Chen Shikai pressed the victory to retake Yiwu and Tangxi; Bao Hu retook Shouchang and Chun'an; Mou Dayin broke Changshan; Wang Tingmei routed the enemy at Jinhua Shiliang and Dagouyuan; Li Rong recovered Dongyang and won again at Jinhua Shouxi, beheading a rebel general and destroying eighteen camps. Assistant Regional Commander Hong Qiyuan retook Sheng County. An edict commended Zhifang's skillful command.
17
十月，賊將桑明等五萬眾由常山逼衢州西溝溪，倚山為營，覬聯南路賊巢。 之芳與賴塔議，出不意，遣廷梅與參領禪布夜趨溝溪，分隊進攻，又大破之，賊棄營遁。
In the tenth month rebel generals led by Sang Ming—fifty thousand strong—advanced from Changshan on Xigouxi west of Quzhou, entrenched along the hills, and tried to link up with rebel strongholds to the south. Zhifang and Laita planned a surprise: Wang Tingmei and company commander Chanbu marched on Gouxi by night, attacked in columns, broke the enemy again, and the rebels abandoned camp and fled.
18
十四年，康親王杰書破曾養性金華，复處州； 貝子傅拉塔亦復黃岩，進圍溫州。 惟九玉踞江山、常山、開化，連寨數十，與之芳相持。 五月，乘大雨河溢，由南塘搗賊前嶺，陣斬七百餘級。 十五年，遣將自遂安連破賊寨，遂復開化。
In Kangxi 14 Prince Kang Jieshu defeated Zeng Yangxing at Jinhua and retook Chuzhou; Prince Fu'lata also retook Huangyan and moved to besiege Wenzhou. Only Ma Jiuyu still held Jiangshan, Changshan, and Kaihua behind dozens of linked camps, deadlocked with Zhifang. In the fifth month, using heavy rains and flooded rivers as cover, he struck from Nantang at the rebels' forward ridge and killed more than seven hundred in battle. In Kangxi 15 he sent generals from Suian to smash rebel camps one after another and retook Kaihua.
19
會鄭錦入漳、泉，耿繼祚方攻建昌潰營遁。 上知閩中有變，命王撤溫州之圍取福建，之芳乃建議直搗仙霞關，曰：「進取之路，不在溫、處而在衢。 雖九玉死守河西難猝破，然其南江山，西則常山，皆間道可襲。 我兵一進，使彼首尾受敵，即河西之壘不能獨完。 」王至衢州，從之芳議。 遂進兵大溪灘，复江山，九玉走，欲別取道奪仙霞。 諸將受之芳密檄，急據關夾擊，其將金應虎等窮蹙降。
When Zheng Jing invaded Zhang and Quan, Geng Jizuo had been attacking Jianchang; his force collapsed and he fled. When word came of trouble in Fujian, the emperor ordered the prince to abandon the siege of Wenzhou and march on Fujian. Zhifang urged a direct strike at Xianxia Pass: "The road forward lies not through Wenzhou or Chuzhou but through Quzhou." Jiuyu may hold the west bank stubbornly, but Jiangshan lies to his south and Changshan to his west—both are paths we can strike along. Once our troops move, he will be caught front and rear, and even his west-bank stronghold cannot hold alone. When the prince reached Quzhou he adopted Zhifang's plan. The army advanced to Daxitan and retook Jiangshan. Jiuyu fled and tried to seize Xianxia by another route. On Zhifang's secret orders the generals seized the pass and attacked from both sides; Jin Yinghu and other rebel commanders, cornered, surrendered.
20
王師下福建，臨行，之芳啟曰：「王但飭諸軍勿虜掠，即長驅入，兵可不血刃也。 」未幾精忠降，溫、處賊皆潰散。 精忠所署置總兵馬鵬、汪文生、陳山，將軍程鳳等猶踞玉山、鉛山、弋陽、德興，之芳請會剿。 時吳三桂兵寇吉安、袁州，江西兵不能東，乃獨遣兵复玉山，文生遁； 自白沙關趨德興，擒鵬； 遣游擊郭守金等復鉛山、興安、弋陽、貴溪諸縣。 上嘉之芳勦賊鄰省有功，加兵部尚書銜。
As the imperial army prepared to enter Fujian, Zhifang advised the prince: "Order your men not to loot, march straight in, and you may take the province without drawing a sword." Soon Jingzhong surrendered and the rebel forces in Wenzhou and Chuzhou melted away. Jingzhong's appointees Ma Peng, Wang Wensheng, Chen Shan, and General Cheng Feng still held Yushan, Qianshan, Yiyang, and Dexing; Zhifang requested a joint campaign against them. Wu Sangui's forces were threatening Ji'an and Yuanzhou, so Jiangxi troops could not march east. Zhifang sent his own force to retake Yushan, and Wensheng fled; pressed from Baisha Pass into Dexing and captured Peng; and sent Brigadier Guo Shoujin and others to retake Qianshan, Xing'an, Yiyang, Guixi, and the other counties. The emperor praised Zhifang's success against rebels in a neighboring province and granted him the nominal rank of Minister of War.
21
十六年，遣參將蔣懋勳等敗賊玉山椒岩，山降。 先是文生、鳳皆乞降，而鳳病死，其妻王玉貞籍所屬六萬八千餘人就撫，而精忠將林爾瞻猶擁眾石壟。 之芳令懋勳等扼要隘，自以數十騎入寨，往撫慰之，爾瞻乃降。 十七年，擊賊子午口，克八仙、老鼠諸洞，賊寨悉平。 鄭錦寇瀕海，遣將嚴守禦，敗之於廟嶺湖，又敗之於溫州。 錦將詹天樞詣世凱降。 十八年，檄定海總兵牟大寅斬錦將童耀等孝順洋，奪獲船隻、器械以還。
In Kangxi 16 he sent Assistant Regional Commander Jiang Maoxun and others to rout the rebels at Yushan Jiaoyan, and Chen Shan submitted. Wensheng and Feng had already offered to surrender; Feng died of illness, and his wife Wang Yuzhen registered more than sixty-eight thousand followers for pacification. Yet Jingzhong's general Lin Erzhan still held out at Shilong with a large force. Zhifang had Maoxun seal the key passes, then rode into the camp with only a few dozen men to reassure them—and Erzhan surrendered. In Kangxi 17 he struck the rebels at Ziwukou, took the Baxian and Laoshu cave strongholds, and cleared every rebel camp. When Zheng Jing raided the coast Zhifang posted defenders, beat him at Miaoling Lake, and beat him again off Wenzhou. Zheng's general Zhan Tianshu came to Chen Shikai and surrendered. In Kangxi 18 he ordered Dinghai commander Mou Dayin to destroy Zheng's general Tong Yao and others at Xiaoshunyang, capturing their ships and arms.
22
之芳練世故，沉幾善謀。 康親王師將行，問之芳：「所策固萬全乎？ 」之芳曰：「軍已發，猶豫則士氣沮。 」乃詣王曰：「虜在吾目中久，明日捷書至矣！ 」前軍捷書果至，傑書大喜，以為神。 在杭州，與將軍圖喇約為兄弟。 精忠既叛，語圖喇勿縱兵暴民。 有滿兵犯法，之芳縛詣圖喇，以軍法治之，一軍肅然。 浙亂平，疏請蠲被兵州縣額賦，安輯流亡，甚有威惠。 所拔偏裨，皆累功至方鎮，而之芳以督臣不敘。 久之，追論大溪灘破賊功，授拖沙喇哈番，準襲一次。
Zhifang knew the ways of the world, kept his own counsel, and planned with care. Before Prince Kang's army marched, he asked Zhifang, "Is your plan truly foolproof?" Zhifang replied, "The army is already on the march—hesitation will kill its spirit." He then told the prince, "I have had these rebels in my sights for some time—tomorrow the victory report will arrive!" The victory report from the vanguard did arrive the next day; Jieshu was delighted and took it for prophecy. At Hangzhou he swore brotherhood with General Tula. When Jingzhong rebelled he urged Tula not to let his soldiers terrorize the populace. When a Manchu soldier broke the law Zhifang bound him and brought him before Tula for military punishment—and the whole army fell into line. After the Zhejiang rebellion ended he asked to remit taxes in war-ravaged counties and resettle refugees, earning wide respect and gratitude. The officers he promoted all rose on merit to high frontier commands, yet Zhifang as governor never entered his own name for reward. Long afterward the court reviewed his victory at Daxitan and granted him Tushala Banner rank, inheritable once.
23
入為兵部尚書，調吏部。 二十六年，授文華殿大學士。 二十七年，御史郭琇疏劾大學士明珠，謂內閣票擬，皆聽明珠指揮，上既罷明珠，並命之芳休致。 三十三年，卒於家，諡文襄。
He entered the capital as Minister of War and was then moved to the Ministry of Personnel. In Kangxi 26 he was made Grand Secretary of the Hall of Literary Splendor. In Kangxi 27 Censor Guo Xiu impeached Grand Secretary Mingzhu, charging that every Grand Secretariat draft followed Mingzhu's orders. The emperor dismissed Mingzhu and ordered Zhifang to retire as well. In Kangxi 33 he died at home and was posthumously titled Wenxiang.
24
之芳既卒，聖祖思其功，嘗諭群臣曰：「人能效命，即為勇士。 耿精忠叛，時之芳為總督，雖不諳騎射，執刀立船首，率眾突前破敵。 彼時同出征者，還京皆稱其勇。 今承平久，善射，能約束士卒，尚不乏人。 若屢經戰陣者，甚難得也！ 」世宗命立賢良祠，諭曰：「德若湯斌、功若之芳者，祀之。 」乾隆間，錄勳臣後，命予恩騎尉，世襲。
After Zhifang's death the Kangxi Emperor remembered his service and once told the ministers, "A man who gives his life for duty is a true warrior." When Geng Jingzhong rebelled, Zhifang was governor. Though no horseman or archer, he stood at the prow with a blade in hand and led a charge that broke the enemy. Every comrade on that campaign praised his courage when they returned to the capital. In these long years of peace we still have plenty of good archers who can keep their men in order. Men who have seen battle again and again are very hard to find! The Yongzheng Emperor ordered the Hall of Worthies built with the instruction: "Enshrine men of Tang Bin's virtue and Zhifang's achievement." Under Qianlong, when the court registered descendants of meritorious ministers, his line was granted the Enlisted Cavalry Commandant rank as an inheritable title.
25
論曰：圖海始阻撤籓之議，及其鷹揚西土，綏靖秦隴，卒收底川之績。 川軍入滇，遂竟全功。 之芳力扼三衢，敵雖東略，終不能得志仙霞。 下閩之功，與有勞焉。 雖曰遭時盤錯，抑亦聖祖馭材之效哉？ 並踐綸輔，易名曰襄。 嗚呼，偉矣！
The historian writes: Tu Hai first opposed removing the princely fiefs; then he took the field in the west, pacified Qin and Long, and at last won the credit for finishing the war on the rivers. Once the Sichuan army entered Yunnan, the great enterprise was complete. Zhifang held the three Qu prefectures firm; though the enemy pushed east, they never took Xianxia Pass. He shared in the merit of the conquest of Fujian. They lived in troubled times, no doubt—but was that not also proof of the Kangxi Emperor's gift for using men? Both rose to the highest ministerial rank and share the posthumous name Xiang. Alas—how great they were!
