1
唐努烏梁海，在烏裏雅蘇台之北，東南土謝圖汗部，南賽音諾顏部，西阿勒泰烏梁海，西南扎薩克圖汗部，北俄羅斯。 有總管五：曰唐努，曰薩拉吉克，曰托錦，曰庫布蘇庫勒諾爾，曰奇木奇克河。
The Tannu Uriankhai lay north of Uliassutai, with the Tsetsen Khan Banner to the southeast, the Sayn Noyan Banner to the south, the Altai Uriankhai to the west, the Zasaghtu Khan Banner to the southwest, and Russia to the north. The region was divided among five superintendents: Tannu, Sarajik, Tojin, Kubsugul Nor, and Kimchik River.
2
康熙五十四年，扎薩克圖汗部和托輝特輔國公博貝隨大軍赴推河防準噶爾策妄阿拉布坦，言：「準噶爾不靖，恃烏梁海障之。 乞往招，若抗即以兵取。 扎薩克台吉濟納彌達阿裏雅及根敦羅卜藏克兵俱習戰，請與同往。 」上韙其議，從之。 九月，烏梁海頭目和羅爾邁率屬降。 先是和羅爾邁居吹河，嘗以越界射獵為博貝縛獻，上宥其罪，諭還巢。 至是將遣子瑚洛處納請降。 博貝至，因遷其遊牧赴特斯。 冬，和羅爾邁遁，博貝追至呼爾罕什巴爾，執之。 五十九年，博貝擒烏梁海逃眾，晉貝勒。 時從征西將軍祁裏德軍。 六十年六月，議政王大臣議覆祁裏德，新收烏梁海二千五百三十名，應送至巴顏諾爾克地方居住，令車臣汗等旗分派兵三百名，並派台吉協同駐紮防守。 雍正二年，諭曰：「朕詢貝勒博貝，管轄烏梁海何以資生。 據奏在將軍祁裏德處借餉一萬八千餘兩，買牲分給，各得產業，今勝於昔。 所有借項，自以貝勒俸逐年扣抵。 朕思烏梁海俱朕之百姓，豈有朕之百姓而借餉於朕之理？ 所借銀兩，不必扣還。 諭祁裏德知之。 」三年，烏梁海和羅爾邁復遁，由阿哩克竄準噶爾界，博貝遣子額璘沁由托濟邀擒，而自赴克木克木齊克緝叛黨，誅之。
In Kangxi 54, Assistant Prince Bobai of the Zasaghtu Khan Banner and the Khotoi Khotogo Tribe marched with the main force to the Tui River to check the Dzungar leader Cebdan Araltan. He reported that the Dzungars were still restless and were shielded by the Uriankhai. He asked permission to go and win them over, and to seize them by force if they refused. The Zasaghtu taiji Jina Midariya and the soldiers under Gendun Lobzang were all seasoned fighters, and he asked that they accompany the mission. The emperor approved the plan and granted his request. In the ninth month the Uriankhai chief Khorolmai submitted with his people. Earlier Khorolmai had lived on the Chui River. Bobai had once seized him for hunting across the border and sent him up as a captive, but the throne pardoned him and told him to return to his own grounds. Now he was preparing to send his son Kholochun to offer submission. When Bobai arrived, he relocated their herds to Tes. That winter Khorolmai fled again. Bobai overtook him at Khurkhan Shibar and took him prisoner. In the fifty-ninth year Bobai rounded up fugitive Uriankhai and was raised to the rank of beile. He was then serving in the army of the western expedition commander Qilide. In the sixth month of the sixtieth year the Council of Princes and Ministers replied to Qilide's memorial: the 2,530 Uriankhai just brought in should be settled at Bayannuur, with three hundred men drawn from the Chengen Khan Banner and other banners, and taiji officers sent to garrison the area jointly. In Yongzheng 2 the throne asked how the Uriankhai under Beile Bobai's jurisdiction supported themselves. His memorial reported that he had borrowed more than eighteen thousand taels of provisions from General Qilide, bought livestock, and distributed herds so that each household had a livelihood; they were now better off than before. The debt was to be repaid gradually from his beile stipend. The emperor reflected that every Uriankhai was his subject; how could subjects be borrowing provisions from their own sovereign? The borrowed silver was not to be repaid. Qilide was instructed accordingly. In the third year Khorolmai fled once more and slipped through Ari into Dzungar territory. Bobai sent his son Erlingjin to cut him off at Toji and take him, while he himself went to Kemkemchik to round up rebel bands and put them to death.
3
初額魯特與喀爾喀構兵時，錯處科布多、烏蘭固木。 噶爾丹既滅，喀爾喀西境直抵阿勒泰，自唐努山陰之克木克木齊克至博木等處，皆博貝及來歸之額魯特貝淩旺布所屬烏梁海遊牧。 四年，策旺阿拉布坦言克木克木齊克舊隸準噶爾，乞還，上不許，慮伺間略烏梁海，詔博貝率所部兵千，隨前鋒統領定壽駐唐努山陽特斯地方防護之。 尋諭理籓院曰：「朕詳思克木克木齊克烏梁海皆博貝所屬，和羅爾邁既已就擒，交博貝撫恤，居之公所。 但念此等人向在喀爾喀邊外林木中射獵為生，與準噶爾所屬烏梁海接壤，又與俄羅斯連界。 宜令博貝等同大臣前往曉諭，令自為預備，以防不虞。 」三月，命大臣一員帶布帛茶葉賞克木克木齊克地方烏梁海，並令揀老成服眾之人作為首領。
When the Oirats and Khalkha first went to war, the Uriankhai had been stationed in the wrong places around Kobdo and Uran Ulaan. After Galdan's defeat the Khalkha western frontier ran straight to the Altai. From Kemkemchik on the northern face of the Tannu range to Bomu and beyond, the pastures belonged to Uriankhai under Bobai and the returned Oirat beile Lingwangbu. In the fourth year Cewang Araltan claimed that Kemkemchik had once belonged to the Dzungars and asked for its return. The emperor refused, fearing a chance to prey on the Uriankhai. Bobai was ordered to lead a thousand of his men with the vanguard commander Ding Shou to garrison Tes on the southern slope of the Tannu Mountains. Soon the Lifan Yuan was told that all Kemkemchik Uriankhai belonged to Bobai. With Khorolmai in custody, they should be placed under Bobai's care at the official station. Yet these people had long lived by hunting in the woods beyond the Khalkha frontier, pressed against both Dzungar Uriankhai and the Russian border. Bobai and the ministers should go and warn them to prepare on their own against any surprise. In the third month a minister was sent with cloth, tea, and gifts for the Kemkemchik Uriankhai, and told to choose respected elders to head each group.
4
五年，額駙策淩等與俄羅斯訂約，自恰克圖、鄂爾輝圖兩間為界，所立之鄂博，迤西至肯哲馮達霍呢音嶺、克木克木齊克之博木、沙弼納嶺。 循此山梁，由正中分中劃界，其兩邊各取五貂之烏梁海，仍令照舊各歸其主，彼此各徵一貂之烏梁海。 自定界之日，將各取一貂之處停止。
In the fifth year the imperial son-in-law Tsering and others made a treaty with Russia. The boundary ran between Kiakhta and Erkhii Tu, with cairns set westward along the Kenje Fenda Khoniin ridge, Bomu at Kemkemchik, and Shabina ridge. Along the ridge a line was drawn down the middle. Each side took five sable-paying Uriankhai bands, who were still to remain under their former lords, while each side also levied one additional sable-paying band from the other. From the day the border was fixed, the practice of each side taking one sable-tributing band was ended.
5
乾隆十六年，以和托輝特扎薩克貝勒青袞咱卜縱所屬人私出汛界與準噶爾回眾貿易，致潛居烏梁海，奪貝勒，詔額璘沁襲其爵，定烏梁海出入汛界例。 二十一年，青袞咱卜脅烏梁海叛，大兵至，皆棄去。 二十五年，鑄唐努烏梁海總管印給之。 嘉慶二年，烏裏雅蘇台參贊大臣額樂春以需索烏梁海奪職治罪。 道光三年，定禁烏梁海與商民貿易例，以山西民人私向烏梁海買取羊只涉訟。 二十四年六月，烏裏雅蘇台將軍桂輪劾總管垂敦紮布需索無厭，奪職。 咸豐年，奏唐努烏梁海界址。
In Qianlong 16 the Khotoi Khotogo zasaghtu beile Chingunjab was stripped of his rank for letting his people slip past the patrol line to trade with Dzungar Muslims and harbor Uriankhai in secret. Erlingjin was ordered to succeed him, and rules governing Uriankhai movement across the patrol border were laid down. In the twenty-first year Chingunjab forced the Uriankhai into rebellion, but when imperial troops arrived his followers deserted him. In the twenty-fifth year official seals were cast for the Tannu Uriankhai superintendents. In Jiaqing 2 the Uliassutai assistant minister Erlekun was dismissed and punished for extorting the Uriankhai. In Daoguang 3 trade between Uriankhai and private merchants was banned after Shanxi traders buying sheep on the sly led to lawsuits. In the sixth month of the twenty-fourth year Uliassutai General Gui Lun impeached Superintendent Choidunjab for endless exactions and removed him from office. During the Xianfeng reign the boundaries of Tannu Uriankhai were reported to the throne.
6
十年，與俄國定界約，是部之沙賓達巴哈實為西疆劃界之第一地段。 同治三年十一月，烏裏雅蘇台將軍明誼等奏：「唐努烏梁海遊牧內，俄使前開議單，載唐努鄂拉達巴哈即系唐努山嶺，自沙賓達巴哈界牌起，先往西，後往南。 亦據該使呈繪圖志，有順薩彥山嶺至奎屯鄂博所有界限地名。 我國舊存圖內雖無其地名，然據該使所指方向，續經庫倫辦事大臣文盛送雍正五年已定交界圖志，名目雖殊，界限大致相似。 唐努烏梁海遊牧雖有被俄人包去之嫌，與西二盟遊牧無礙。 明年立界時，俟與麟興、車林敦多布等妥商辦理。 」四年八月，麟興等奏：「據委員岳嵩武稟報，與唐努烏梁海總管凡齊爾馳赴博果素克大壩履勘起，沿站按圖詳查，行至唐努鄂拉達巴哈，核與俄國所畫唐努鄂拉達巴哈邊界相符。 除薩彥山因無路徑不能履勘，其唐努鄂拉達巴哈及邊境應分之珠嚕淖爾、塔斯啟勒山、哈喇塔蘇爾山、德布色克哈山數處，擇擬立界處所，繪會勘圖志呈閱。 」時俄立界使臣以事不能至。 九月，明誼等以軍務緊急，請緩約俄使立界。 六年，俄人遂越界至總管邁達爾遊牧內烏克果勒地方建屋種地。 總理各國事務衙門照會俄使，始由庫倫俄官行文令送之回國。 是年，廷旨促麟興等建立西疆毗連俄境界牌鄂博。 六月，專命榮全迅與俄官會立烏裏雅蘇台邊卡界牌鄂博。 八年五月，榮全與俄使穆魯木策夫至是部西南之賽留格木山嶺會立牌博，於是月二十六日起行，順賽留格木嶺至是部西南邊境盡處之博果蘇克壩，立第一界牌鄂博，科城立牌博於南，俄國立牌博於西。 由此向東北約八十里，名塔斯啟勒山，於山頂立第二牌博。 又向東北約九十里，至珠嚕淖爾，俄使言只就珠嚕淖爾迤北數十里唐努山之察布齊雅壩止，建立鄂博，由此直向西北，統至沙賓達巴哈，路既便捷，尤易行走。 榮全以俄使所指之路俱系是部遊牧內地，若照俄使所議，不惟與原圖大不相符，且將是部遊牧包去大半，向俄使反復開導，仍如原圖，於珠嚕淖爾東南之哈爾根山立第三牌博。 順淖爾北岸約二十餘里，至唐努山南察布齊雅壩，立第四牌博。 沿唐努山南，向西過莫多圖河、紮勒都倫河、烏爾圖河、察罕紮克蘇圖河，順哈喇塔蘇爾海山，至沙克魯河，轉向東北約二百五十餘里，至庫色爾壩，系是部西方邊界，立第五牌博。 向西北九十餘里，至唐努鄂拉達巴哈末處，過哈喇河偏西山下楚拉察水流之處，立第六牌博。 向北又東，順薩彥山過瑪納瑚河、蒙納克河、浩拉什河，由喀喇淖爾至蘇爾大壩，約一百五十里，立第七牌博。 向北又東約三百六十餘里，山脈連貫，直至沙賓達巴哈，於舊牌博之東山頂上立第八牌博。 照原圖至賽留格木山博果蘇克壩上，紅線以左為中國地，紅線以右為俄國地。 至六月二十二日竣事，而是部阿勒泰河、阿穆哈河區域皆入於俄。
In the tenth year a boundary treaty was made with Russia. Shabindaba in this region marked the first stretch of the western frontier to be demarcated. In the eleventh month of Tongzhi 3 Uliassutai General Mingyi and others reported that within Tannu Uriankhai pastures the Russian envoy's earlier negotiating paper identified Tannu Uladabaha as the Tannu ridge, running from the Shabindaba marker first west and then south. The envoy's maps likewise listed boundary points along the Sayin ridge as far as Kuitun Oboo. These names did not appear on older Chinese maps, but following the route the envoy indicated, Kobdo Commissioner Wensheng later sent maps of the border fixed in Yongzheng 5. The labels differed, yet the line was much the same. Some Tannu Uriankhai pastures might be enclosed by Russian claims, but the grazing lands of the western two leagues would not be affected. When the boundary was set the following year, the matter would be settled in consultation with Lin Xing, Charindundob, and the others. In the eighth month of the fourth year Lin Xing reported that Commissioner Yue Songwu, after riding to meet Superintendent Fanchir at Bogusik Dam, had surveyed the line station by station along the map and found Tannu Uladabaha matched the boundary Russia had drawn. Sayin Mountain could not be walked because there was no trail. For Tannu Uladabaha and the disputed sites at Zhulu Nor, Taskele Mountain, Khaltasur Mountain, Debsikha Mountain, and elsewhere, proposed marker locations were chosen and joint survey maps submitted. The Russian boundary commissioner could not come at that time. In the ninth month Mingyi and his colleagues, citing urgent military affairs, asked to postpone the boundary negotiations with Russia. In the sixth year Russians crossed the line into Ukugol within Superintendent Maidar's pastures and built houses and planted fields. The Zongli Yamen protested to the Russian minister, and only then did the Russian official at Kobdo order the intruders sent home. That year the court ordered Lin Xing and others to erect boundary markers and cairns along the western frontier adjoining Russia. In the sixth month Rongquan was specially ordered to hurry to Uliassutai and set up border pickets, markers, and cairns with Russian officials. In the fifth month of the eighth year Rongquan and the Russian envoy Murunov met on the Sairiugem ridge in the southwest of the region to set markers. They began on the twenty-sixth, followed the ridge to Bogusuk Dam at the southwestern tip, and raised the first marker, with Qing markers to the south and Russian markers to the west. About eighty li northeast lay Taskele Mountain, where a second marker was placed on the summit. Another ninety li northeast brought them to Zhulu Nor. The Russian envoy proposed markers only from a point north of the lake, at Chabu Qiya Dam on the Tannu range, then straight northwest to Shabindaba—a shorter and easier route. Rongquan replied that every route the Russians named lay inside the department's own pastures. Accepting their line would not only diverge sharply from the old map but would cut off most of the grazing lands. After repeated argument he held to the original map and set the third marker on Khargen Mountain southeast of Zhulu Nor. Following the north shore of the lake for some twenty li, they reached Chabu Qiya Dam on the southern face of the Tannu range and raised the fourth marker. They traced the southern slope of the Tannu range west across the Modutu, Zhalduun, Urtu, and Chahan Zakusu rivers, followed Khartasurhai Mountain to the Shakuru River, turned northeast for about 250 li to Kuseer Dam on the department's western edge, and set the fifth marker. Ninety-odd li northwest brought them to the end of Tannu Uladabaha, where the Chulacha stream runs below the western slope of the Khara River valley; there they set the sixth marker. Turning north and then east along the Sayin range, they crossed the Mana, Monak, and Holash rivers, went from Khara Nor to Sur Dam for about 150 li, and erected the seventh marker. Another 360 li north and east along the unbroken ridge brought them to Shabindaba, where the eighth marker was placed on the hill east of the old cairn. According to the original map as far as Bogusuk Dam on the Sairiugem range, land to the left of the red line was Chinese and land to the right was Russian. The work was finished on the twenty-second day of the sixth month, yet the Altai River and Amukha River districts of the region had all passed to Russia.
7
光緒五年，烏裏雅蘇台將軍以奇木齊克河總管報俄商在唐努烏梁海屬建蓋行棧數處，及春季以來，有俄人或三五十人或八九十人不等，在奇木齊克河北一帶中唐努山內刨挖金砂，例應禁止，咨總理各國事務衙門照會俄署使凱陽德轉飭邊官查禁。 七年五月，烏裏雅蘇台將軍以俄人在薩爾魯克地方居住，紮立木棚十處，附近挖過金砂大小凡一百餘處，照會俄駐庫領事迅飭邊界官嚴禁。
In Guangxu 5 the Uliassutai general relayed a report from the Kimchik River superintendent that Russian merchants had opened several trading posts in Tannu Uriankhai territory and that since spring bands of thirty to ninety Russians had been panning gold inside the Tannu range north of the Kimchik River. The practice was illegal, and the Zongli Yamen was asked to notify Minister Kayangde so border officials could stop it. In the fifth month of the seventh year the Uliassutai general reported Russians living at Sarluuk, ten wooden huts, and more than a hundred gold pits nearby. He asked the Russian consul at Kobdo to order border officials to enforce the ban.

Lianshun was told to inspect conditions and report back. Lianshun soon replied that Kimchik River lay more than a thousand li from the Tannu superintendent, with Uriankhai under the Sayn Noyan Oirat prince Dakdan Dorji in between. When disputes arose, papers could not get through, and nothing reached Uliassutai except by relay through the Tannu superintendent. After decades of looking to the court for favor, granting a seal at once would let them hold their own frontier line and serve as a buffer for the empire. In the twenty-sixth year Lianshun and others were ordered to prepare frontier defenses. The Boxer rising had begun, and Chinese and foreigners alike were unsettled. Lianshun instructed the Tannu superintendent Gunbudorji, the Sarajik superintendent Balejinima, the Tojin superintendent Lingkui, the Kubsugul Nor superintendent Keshikjiergal, and the Kimchik River superintendent Haidubu to raise troops and drill militia without delay. Gunbudorji and the others had their forces ready within days. He also arranged supplies, reinforced key posts, and told them to treat Russian merchants well and avoid provoking border clashes. In the twelfth month of the twenty-eighth year Lianshun again asked for rewards. Keshikjiergal received a second-rank cap button and Haidubu a second-rank peacock feather. That year Lianshun wrote that the Uriankhai had been under imperial civilization for nearly two hundred years and were scarcely different from the Khalkha Mongols. Chinese traders still obeyed the old rules and did not slip across the border to trade in secret. Russian traders in Uriankhai, by contrast, were beyond count. They built houses and lived there year-round, buying up antler velvet, fox, wolf, otter, lynx, sable, and squirrel pelts in enormous quantities. When Uriankhai came to Uliassutai to present tribute furs, they often had nothing left to exchange. The only remedy was flexibility: merchants at Uliassutai who had formerly traded in Uriankhai might register with officials and go, still receiving six-month passes from the general's office as regulations provided, while checkpoint troops were ordered to search carefully for contraband. This was approved.

In the eighth year Russia proposed remarking the Kobdo-Tarbagatai border and sought to take the Kaba River region. Allowing them to resurvey and alter the line would do real harm. In the ninth year Erqing'e and Assistant Minister Sheng Tai went ahead to the frontier, surveyed the strategic passes, and identified the principal mountains and rivers. Abandoning the Kaba River and Kuitun Mountain would have left Uriankhai and Kazakhs homeless. After more than ten days of dispute with the Russians, the line was shifted to the Alakabek River west of the Kaba River, recovering more than 130 li of land, and new boundary markers were set. Tarbagatai had garrisoned the borrowed land and could not withdraw without opening the way for a Russian advance south. The court agreed. In the sixth month of the eighteenth year Shakduerzhab, Erqing'e, and Kuifu reported that the Kaba River loan could not yet be returned: after two Tarbagatai demarcations the Mongols and Kazakhs had too little land, and they asked for a three-year extension. The Uriankhai were in distress. Tarbagatai Kazakhs would supply livestock, refugee households would be resettled, and troops would garrison the area. This was approved. Kobdo later pressed repeatedly for the return of the Kaba River, Tarbagatai resisted, and the matter was never settled.

The land supported both farming and herding and held mines and salt. The department had seven assistant commandants. The vice commander and the left- and right-wing expectant officials each doubled as superintendent of one banner. After the new demarcation eight border passes ran from Alakabek in the right wing southward to Aktas in the north, then northeast through Kezinaas, Sas, Khujirtublak, Usong Khujirtu, east through Hubusu to Luogaibu, and northeast to Hodong Nor in the left wing. Notable mountains included Xijitu, Gailatu, Homuda, and Khara Wenchang. Notable rivers included the Chahan Xilu, Sagesai, Sakbudo, Qinggeli, and Irtysh. Altai Nuur Uriankhai lay northwest of Kobdo, with Tannu Uriankhai to the east, Altai Uriankhai to the south, and Russia to the west and north.

初屬準噶爾。 乾隆二十二年九月，賽音諾顏扎薩克貝勒車木楚克劄布招撫阿爾泰山烏梁海。 有特勒伯克扎爾納克者，阿勒泰淖爾之烏梁海宰桑，攜屬至。 詔車木楚克紮布定貢賞例，宣示德意。 十二月，授阿勒泰淖爾烏梁海宰桑特勒伯克等為總管。 二十三年秋，烏梁海總管阿拉善、恩克等叛，車木楚克紮布剿阿拉善等，就擒。 恩兄竄哈屯河，冬，擒之。 尋定是部為二旗，各設總管一，歲貢貉皮如例，隸科布多參贊大臣。 道光中，查邊之政漸弛，俄人始築城於是部之吹河，我查邊界鄂博者往往不至其地。
They too had originally belonged to the Dzungars. In the ninth month of Qianlong 22 Sayn Noyan zasaghtu beile Chemchukzhab won over the Altai Uriankhai. The Altai Nuur zaisang Teleberk Zarnak arrived with his people. Chemchukzhab was ordered to set tribute and reward rules and proclaim the emperor's favor. In the twelfth month Teleberk and other Altai Nuur zaisang were appointed superintendents. In the autumn of the twenty-third year superintendents Alashan and Enke rebelled. Chemchukzhab defeated them and took Alashan prisoner. Enke's elder brother fled to the Khoto River and was captured that winter. The region was soon organized into two banners, each with a superintendent, annual raccoon-dog pelt tribute, under the Kobdo assistant minister. During the Daoguang reign border inspections grew lax. Russians built a town on the Chui River in this region, while Chinese inspectors often failed to reach the boundary cairns.
25
咸豐十年，定西疆界約，俄國畫界清單遂將是部包去。 同治六年七月，科布多參贊大臣廣鳳等奏俄雅什達喇城衙門給阿勒泰淖爾兩旗總管文，言阿勒泰淖爾、綽羅什拜、巴什庫斯、吹河均系俄羅斯遊牧。 如有人言系中國遊牧，拿送俄城。 又俄人來綽羅什拜地方伐木，已飭總管察罕等善言開導，靜候兩國分界大臣將疆界議定換約，立界後，再按照所分界限遵行，此時不可伐木蓋房，致滋事端。 時俄國官兵執去我查閱哈屯河扎薩克紮那紮布及台吉差官、蒙古員兵等，阻我查邊道路，稱是部遊牧為俄國地面，不許中國人往來。 十月，阿勒泰淖爾總管莽泰等報俄官取莽泰旗下一百四十餘人及總管察罕旗下二百四十餘人手印。 九月，明誼、錫霖、博勒果索與俄分界大臣照議單勘分西界，是部地遂非清有。 初議遷是部誠心內附者於卡內，而總管莽泰等言兩旗人丁祈全入卡內住牧。 廣鳳等諭以「所被俄國分去地面舊住人丁，隨地歸為俄國，務令安居故土，各守舊業，立界後斷不致仍前擾害」。 隨令莽岱等出卡回牧，並內徙之議亦輟。
The Xianfeng 10 western frontier treaty placed this department within Russia on the Russian boundary list. In the seventh month of Tongzhi 6 Kobdo Assistant Minister Guangfeng reported that the Russian office at Yashidara had told the Altai Nuur superintendents that Altai Nuur, Choluoshibai, Bashkus, and the Chui River were Russian pasture. Anyone who claimed they were Chinese pastures was to be sent to the Russian authorities. Russians were also cutting timber at Choluoshibai. Superintendents Chahan and others were told to reason with them and wait until the boundary treaty was signed; until then they must not cut timber or build houses. Russian troops seized Inspector Zanazhab of Khoto River, taiji officers, and Mongol soldiers on the patrol route, claiming the pastures were Russian and barring Chinese passage. In the tenth month Superintendent Mangtai reported that Russian officials had taken more than 140 men from his banner and collected fingerprints from more than 240 men under Superintendent Chahan. In the ninth month Mingyi, Xilin, and Boleiguosuo demarcated the western border with Russian commissioners according to the negotiating list, and this department passed out of Qing control. At first the court planned to move those who had sincerely submitted inside the border, but Mangtai and other superintendents said both banners wished to enter the pass entirely. Guangfeng told them that people on land now assigned to Russia must stay on their old grounds, keep their occupations, and would not be harassed once the boundary was fixed. Mangdai and others were then ordered out of the pass to their pastures, and the plan to move them inward was dropped.
