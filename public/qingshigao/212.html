交聘之典，春秋爲盛。 南北史本紀書交聘頗詳。 其時中土分裂，與列國之敵體相交，無以異也。 宋與遼金歲賀正旦、賀生辰外，有泛使，今謂之專使。 然皆事畢即行，不常駐，《金史》有盟聘表。 清有中夏，沿元明制，視海內外莫與爲對。 凡俄、英之來聘者，國史皆書曰來貢。 洎道光庚子訂約，始與敵體相等。 咸豐庚申之役，肇衅非一，而遣使駐京未允實行者，亦一大端。 自是而後，有約各國率遣使駐京。 同治中，志剛、孫家穀之出，是爲中國遣專使之始。 光緒建元，郭嵩燾、陳蘭彬諸人分使英、美，是爲中國遣駐使之始。 其時以使俄者兼德、奧，使英者兼法、義、比，使美者兼日斯巴尼亞、秘魯，而日本無附近之國，則特置使。 甲午以後，增置漸多，迄于宣統，俄、英、法、德、和、比、義、奧、日本皆特置使，日斯巴尼亞則改以法使兼，秘魯、墨西哥、古巴則以美使兼。 韓國置使旋廢。 有約之國，惟葡萄牙、瑞典、那威、丹馬諸國無駐使，有事則以就近駐使任之。 國際交涉，大至和戰之重，細至節文之末，爲使者罔弗與聞，關國家休戚者固至重也。 作交聘表。
The rituals of diplomatic exchange reached their fullest expression in the Spring and Autumn era. The reign annals of the Northern and Southern Histories treat diplomatic exchanges at length. In that age the Central Plain was fractured; treating with other powers as peer states differed little from what would follow. Beyond the Song's yearly New Year and birthday missions to Liao and Jin, there were general envoys—what we now call special envoys. Yet they all left when their business was finished and were not permanently resident; the History of Jin includes a table of alliances and missions. Once the Qing held the Central Realm, following Yuan and Ming precedent, it acknowledged no peer at home or abroad. Envoys from Russia, Britain, and the like were invariably entered in the dynastic records as coming to present tribute. Only after the treaties of the Daoguang gengzi year (1840) were foreign powers recognized as coequal states. The Xianfeng gengshen war had more than one provocation; the refusal to allow—and implement—resident ministers in Beijing was itself a major grievance. Thereafter treaty powers routinely posted ministers to the capital. During Tongzhi, the missions of Zhigang and Sun Jiagu marked China's first dispatch of special envoys abroad. At the opening of the Guangxu reign, Guo Songtao, Chen Lanbin, and others were assigned to Britain and the United States—the start of permanently resident Chinese missions. Initially the minister to Russia doubled for Germany and Austria; the minister to Britain for France, Italy, and Belgium; and the minister to the United States for Spain and Peru—while Japan, with no neighboring power to share a mission, received its own minister. After 1894 postings multiplied; by the Xuantong era Russia, Britain, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, Italy, Austria, and Japan each had dedicated ministers, while Spain fell under the French legation and Peru, Mexico, and Cuba under the American. A mission to Korea was established only to be withdrawn shortly afterward. Of the treaty powers, only Portugal, Sweden, Norway, and Denmark lacked resident ministers; matters were referred to whichever legation lay nearest. International affairs range from war and peace to the finest points of protocol, and envoys are party to all of it—work that touches the nation's fortunes is plainly vital.
