2
李長庚子廷鈺胡振聲王得祿邱良功陳步雲
Li Changgeng, his son Tingyu, Hu Zhensheng, Wang Delu, Qiu Lianggong, and Chen Buyun
3
許松年黃標林國良許廷桂
Xu Songnian, Huang Biao, Lin Guoliang, and Xu Tinggui
4
李長庚，字西岩，福建同安人。 乾隆三十六年武進士，授藍翎侍衛。 出為浙江衢州營都司，累遷樂清協副將。 五十二年，署福建海壇鎮總兵。 鄰海有盜，誤指所轄界，坐褫職。 罄家財募鄉勇，捕獲巨盜，起用，補海壇游擊，遷銅山參將。 自乾隆季年，安南內亂，招瀕海亡命劫內洋，以濟餉為患，粵東土盜鳳尾、水澳兩幫附之，遂益肆擾。 五十九年，夷艇始犯福建三澎，長庚擊走之。
Li Changgeng, whose style was Xiyan, came from Tong'an in Fujian. In Qianlong 36 he took his degree as a military licentiate and was appointed a Blue Bordered Bodyguard. He was posted as battalion commander of the Quzhou Garrison in Zhejiang and rose in due course to deputy commander of the Yueqing Brigade. In year 52 he acted as commander of the Haitan Garrison in Fujian. When pirates struck a neighboring stretch of coast, he was wrongly blamed for their operating within his jurisdiction and was stripped of his post. He poured his family fortune into raising local militia, captured a major pirate leader, was reinstated, filled the post of guerrilla colonel at Haitan, and was promoted to brigade general at Tongshan. In the late Qianlong years civil war in Annam drew coastal outlaws into raids on the inner seas to provision its armies, and the Guangdong bands Fengwei and Shui'ao joined them, so that piracy grew far worse. In year 59 foreign rebel junks first struck the three Pescadores off Fujian, and Changgeng beat them back.
5
嘉慶二年，遷澎湖協副將，擢浙江定海鎮總兵。 三年，迭擊洋匪於衢港及普陀。 四年，鳳尾幫引夷艇入溫州洋，敗之，賜花翎。 五年夏，夷艇合水澳、鳳尾百餘艘萃於浙洋，逼台州。 巡撫阮元奏以長庚總統三鎮水師擊之，會師海門。 賊泊松門山下相持，颶風大作，覆溺幾盡，其泊岸及附敗舟者皆就俘，獲安南偽侯倫貴利等四總兵，磔之，以敕印擲還其國。 是年，擢福建水師提督，尋調浙江。 安南烏艚船百餘號，總兵十二人，分前中後三隊，所獲四總兵，其後隊也。
In Jiaqing 2 he became deputy commander of the Penghu Brigade and was promoted to commander of the Dinghai Garrison in Zhejiang. In year 3 he fought sea bandits again and again at Qu Port and Putuo. In year 4 the Fengwei band brought foreign rebel junks into Wenzhou waters; he defeated them and was awarded the peacock feather. That summer more than a hundred foreign rebel craft, together with the Shui'ao and Fengwei bands, massed in Zhejiang waters and threatened Taizhou. Governor Ruan Yuan asked that Changgeng be given overall command of the three garrisons' fleets; the squadrons rendezvoused at Haimen. The pirates lay at anchor below Songmen Mountain in a stalemate until a typhoon struck and nearly wiped them out. Every man who made shore or clung to a wreck was taken. Four rebel "marquises" of Annam, including Lun Guili, were captured and executed by dismemberment, and their imperial seals were sent back to their country. That year he was made naval commander-in-chief of Fujian and soon after was transferred to Zhejiang. The Annamese force had numbered more than a hundred black war-junks and twelve brigade generals in three squadrons; the four generals taken had belonged to the rear squadron.
6
未幾，安南新阮內附，受封守約束，艇匪無所巢穴。 其在閩者，皆為漳盜蔡牽所並，有艇百餘，粵盜硃濆亦得數十艘。 牽，同安人，奸猾善用眾，既得夷艇，凡水澳、鳳尾諸黨悉歸之，遂猖獗。 阮元與長庚議夷艇高大，水師戰艦不能制，乃集捐十餘萬金付長庚，赴閩造大艦三十，名曰霆船，鑄大砲四百餘配之。 連敗牽等於海上，軍威大振。
Before long the new Nguyen court in Annam submitted to the Qing, accepted investiture, and kept its people in check, so the rebel fleets lost their havens. Those who remained in Fujian were absorbed by the Zhangzhou pirate Cai Qian, who commanded more than a hundred junks; the Guangdong pirate Zhu Fen took several dozen for his own. Cai Qian came from Tong'an. Cunning and adept at leading large forces, he won the foreign rebel junks and drew every Shui'ao and Fengwei band to his banner, after which his raids became relentless. Ruan Yuan and Changgeng agreed that the foreign rebel junks were too tall for the regular war fleet to overcome. More than a hundred thousand taels were raised and entrusted to Changgeng, who went to Fujian to build thirty large men-of-war called Thunder Ships and fit them with more than four hundred heavy guns. He defeated Cai Qian again and again at sea, and the navy's prestige soared.
7
八年，牽竄定海，進香普陀山，長庚掩至，牽僅以身免，窮追至閩洋，賊船糧盡帆壞，偽乞降於總督玉德，遣興泉永道慶徠赴三沙招撫，玉德遽檄浙師收港，牽得以其間修船揚帆去。 浙師追擊於三沙及溫州，毀其船六。 牽畏霆船，賄閩商造大艇，高於霆船，出洋以被劫報，牽得之，渡橫洋，劫台灣米以餉硃濆，遂與之合。
In year 8 Cai Qian slipped into Dinghai and went to offer incense at Mount Putuo. Changgeng surprised him, and Qian barely escaped alive. Changgeng chased him into Fujian waters until the pirate fleet was starving and its sails in tatters. Qian then pretended to surrender to Governor Yude, who sent Intendant Qing Lai to Sansha to negotiate. Yude abruptly ordered the Zhejiang fleet back to harbor, and Qian used the respite to refit his ships and escape. The Zhejiang fleet pursued him at Sansha and Wenzhou and destroyed six of his ships. Fearing the Thunder Ships, Qian bribed Fujian merchants to build junks even larger, sent them to sea under false reports of capture, took possession of them, crossed the open ocean, raided Taiwan for rice to supply Zhu Fen, and finally joined Zhu's band.
8
九年夏，連宗八十餘入閩，戕總兵胡振聲，詔治閩將不援罪，長庚總統兩省水師。 秋，牽、濆共犯浙，長庚合諸鎮兵擊之於定海北洋，衝賊為二，自當牽，急擊，逐至盡山。 牽以大艇得遁，委敗硃濆，濆怒，於是復分。 十年夏，調福建提督。 牽聞長庚至，遂竄浙，追敗之青龍港，又敗之於台州斗米洋。 復調浙江提督。
That summer more than eighty members of the Lian clan entered Fujian and killed Brigade General Hu Zhensheng. The court punished Fujian officers who had failed to relieve him and placed Changgeng in overall command of the fleets of both provinces. That autumn Cai Qian and Zhu Fen raided Zhejiang together. Changgeng united the garrison fleets and met them in the northern sea off Dinghai, splitting the pirate force in two. He took Qian on himself, pressed hard, and chased him as far as Jinshan. Qian escaped in his great junk and left Zhu Fen to bear the defeat. Zhu Fen was furious, and the two bands parted ways once more. In the summer of year 10 he was made naval commander-in-chief of Fujian. Hearing that Changgeng had come, Cai Qian fled into Zhejiang. Changgeng pursued him, defeated him at Qinglong Harbor, and beat him again at Doumiyang off Taizhou. He was transferred back to naval commander-in-chief of Zhejiang.
9
十一年正月，牽合百餘艘犯台灣，結土匪萬餘攻府城，自號鎮海王，沉舟鹿耳門阻援兵。 長庚至，不得入，諜知南汕、北汕、大港門可通小舟，遣總兵許松年、副將王得祿繞道入，攻洲仔尾，連敗之。 二月，松年登洲仔尾，焚其藔，牽反救，長庚遣兵出南汕，與松年夾擊，大敗之。 牽無去路，困守北汕。 會風潮驟漲，沉舟漂起，乃奪鹿耳門逸去，詔奪花翎、頂戴。 四月，蔡牽、硃濆同犯福寧外洋，擊敗之，追至台州斗米洋，擒其黨李按等。
In the first month of year 11 Cai Qian led more than a hundred ships against Taiwan, rallied more than ten thousand local bandits to besiege the prefectural seat, called himself King Who Pacifies the Sea, and sank ships in Lu'ermen to block reinforcements. Changgeng arrived but could not force an entrance. Scouts reported that small boats could pass through Nanshan, Beishan, and Dagangmen. He sent Brigade General Xu Songnian and Deputy Commander Wang Delu around by those routes to strike Zhouzaiwei and beat the pirates again and again. In the second month Xu Songnian stormed Zhouzaiwei and burned the pirate camps. Cai Qian turned back to relieve them, but Changgeng sent men out through Nanshan to join Xu in a pincer and routed the rebels. With no escape left, Cai Qian was trapped at Beishan. Then wind and tide rose sharply, the scuttled ships floated free, and he broke out through Lu'ermen. The court stripped Changgeng of his peacock feather and rank insignia. In the fourth month Cai Qian and Zhu Fen raided together off Funing. Changgeng defeated them, chased them to Doumiyang off Taizhou, and captured followers including Li An.
10
長庚疏言：「蔡逆未能殲擒者，實由兵船不得力，接濟未斷絕所致。 臣所乘之船，較各鎮為最大，及逼近牽船，尚低五六尺。 曾與三鎮總兵原預支養廉，捐造大船十五號，而督臣以造船需數月之久，借帑四五萬之多，不肯具奏。 且海賊無兩年不修之船，亦無一年不壞之槓料。 桅柁折則船為虛器，風篷爛則寸步難行。 乃逆賊在鹿耳門竄出，僅餘船三十，篷朽硝缺； 一回閩地，裝篷燂洗，煥然一新，糧藥充足，賊何日可滅？ 」詔逮治玉德，以阿林保代。 既至福建，諸文武吏以未協剿、未斷岸姦接濟、懼得罪​​，交譖長庚。 阿林保密劾其逗留，章三上，詔密詢浙江巡撫清安泰。 清安泰疏言：「長庚熟海島形勢、風雲沙線，每戰自持柁，老於操舟者不及。 兩年在軍，過門不入。 以捐造船械，傾其家貲。 所俘獲盡以賞功，士爭效死。 八月中戰漁山，圍攻蔡逆，火器瓦石雨下，身受多創，將士傷百四十人，鏖戰不退。 賊中語：'不畏千萬兵，只畏李長庚。 '實水師諸將之冠。 」且備陳海戰之難，非兩省合力不能成功狀。 時同戰諸鎮，亦交章言長庚實非逗留。 仁宗震怒，切責阿林保，謂：「朕若輕信其言，豈不自失良將？ 嗣後勦賊專倚長庚，儻阿林保從中掣肘，玉德即前車之鑑！ 」並飭造大同安梭船三十，未成以前，先僱商船備剿。 長庚聞之，益感奮。 是年秋，擊賊於漁山，受傷，事聞，復還翎頂。
Changgeng wrote in a memorial: "The rebel Cai has not been destroyed chiefly because our warships are too weak and shore supplies to the pirates have never been cut off. Even the ship I command—the largest in the fleet—is still five or six feet lower than Cai Qian's when we close to board. I once joined the three garrison commanders in advancing our stipends to fund fifteen large ships, but the governor refused to memorialize the plan, saying construction would take months and a treasury loan of forty or fifty thousand taels was too costly. Pirates, moreover, never let a ship go two years without repair or keep spars and timbers a full year without renewal. Break the mast or rudder and a ship is useless; let the sails rot and it cannot stir. Yet when the rebel broke out of Lu'ermen he had only thirty ships, with rotten sails and scarce powder; once back in Fujian he re-rigged, caulked, and refitted until his fleet looked new again and his grain and powder were full—how then can the pirates ever be destroyed?" An edict ordered Governor Yude arrested and replaced by Alinbao. Once in Fujian, civil and military officers who feared blame for poor cooperation and for letting shore suppliers feed the pirates joined in slandering Changgeng. Alinbao secretly accused him of stalling; three memorials went up, and the throne privately asked Zhejiang Governor Qing Antai for the truth. Qing Antai replied: "Changgeng knows every island, wind pattern, and shoal. In every battle he takes the helm himself, and men who have sailed all their lives cannot match him. For two years on campaign he passed his own gate without going in. He spent his family fortune building ships and arms for the fleet. Every prize he took went straight to reward merit, and his men vied to fight to the death. In the eighth month at Yushan he besieged the rebel Cai under a rain of shot and stones. He was badly wounded, a hundred and forty of his men were hurt, yet they fought on without yielding. Among the pirates they said, 'We do not fear millions of soldiers—we only fear Li Changgeng. ' He is truly the foremost commander of the fleet. He also explained at length how hard naval warfare was and that neither province could succeed alone. The garrison commanders who had fought with him likewise wrote that Changgeng had not been stalling. Emperor Renzong was furious and sharply rebuked Alinbao: "Had I believed him lightly, I would have lost a fine general through my own fault. Hereafter the pirate campaign will rest solely on Changgeng. If Alinbao hampers him from within, let Yude's fate be your warning!" He also ordered thirty Datong'an sloop warships built and, until they were ready, hired merchant ships for the campaign. Changgeng was stirred to still greater zeal when he heard this. That autumn he fought the pirates at Yushan and was wounded. When the court learned of it, his peacock feather and insignia were restored.
11
十二年春，擊敗牽於粵洋大星嶼。 十一月，又擊敗於閩洋浮鷹山。 十二月，遂偕福建提督張見升追牽入澳，窮其所向，至黑水洋。 牽僅存三艇，皆百戰之寇，以死拒。 長庚自以火攻船掛其艇尾，欲躍登，忽砲中喉，移時而殞。 時戰艦數十倍於賊，見升庸懦，遙見總統船亂，遽退，牽乃遁入安南外洋。 上震悼，褒血卩，初擬俟寇平錫以伯爵，乃追封三等壯烈伯，諡忠毅，於原籍建專祠。
In the spring of year 12 he defeated Cai Qian off Daxing Island in Guangdong waters. In the eleventh month he beat him again off Fuying Mountain in Fujian waters. In the twelfth month he and Fujian Commander Zhang Jiansheng chased Cai Qian into the open ocean and followed him all the way to the Black Water Sea. Cai Qian had only three junks left, manned by veterans who fought to the death. Changgeng himself brought a fire ship alongside the stern of Qian's junk to board, when a cannon ball struck his throat. He died shortly after. The government fleet outnumbered the pirates many times over, but Zhang Jiansheng was timid. Seeing the flagship in disorder from a distance, he withdrew at once, and Cai Qian escaped toward the open sea off Annam. The Emperor mourned him deeply and granted posthumous honors. He had been slated for a countship once the pirates were destroyed; instead he was posthumously made Baron Zhuanglie of the third rank, with the temple name Zhongyi, and a shrine was built in his home district.
12
長庚治軍嚴，信賞必罰，自偏裨下至隊長水手，耳目心志如一，人人皆可用。 與阮元同心整厲水師，數建功，為玉德所忌。 及阿林保之至閩也，置酒款長庚，謂曰：「大海捕魚，何時入網？ 海外事無左證，公但斬一酋，以牽首報，我飛章告捷，以餘賊歸善後辦理。 公受上賞，我亦邀次功，孰與窮年冒風濤僥倖萬一哉？ 」長庚謝曰：「吾何能為此？ 久視海船如廬舍，誓與賊同死，不與同生！ 」阿林保不懌。 既屢劾不得逞，則飛檄趣戰。 長庚緘所落齒寄其妻，志以身殉國。 既歿，詔部將王得祿、邱良功嗣任，勉以同心敵愾​​，為長庚雪仇。 二人遵其部勒，卒滅蔡牽，竟全功焉。
Changgeng ran a strict command, rewarding faithfully and punishing surely. From his deputies down to squad leaders and sailors, every man moved as one will, and each was fit for duty. He and Ruan Yuan worked together to rebuild the navy, won repeated victories, and earned Yude's jealousy. When Alinbao reached Fujian he entertained Changgeng with wine and said, "Fishing the open sea—who knows when the net will fill? At sea there are no witnesses. Behead one leader, call it Cai Qian's head, and I will rush a victory report to court. We can tidy up the rest later. You take the top honors and I share the credit—surely that beats spending years in storms for a one-in-ten-thousand chance?" Changgeng refused: "How could I do such a thing? I have long made a ship my home. I swear to die with these pirates rather than let them live!" Alinbao took offense. When his repeated accusations failed, he sent urgent orders demanding battle. Changgeng sent his wife the teeth he had lost in battle, resolved to die for the throne. After his death the court ordered his lieutenants Wang Delu and Qiu Lianggong to take up his command, urging them to stand together against the enemy and avenge Changgeng. They followed his battle plans, destroyed Cai Qian in the end, and brought the campaign to full success.
13
長庚無子，養同姓子廷鈺為嗣，襲伯爵，授二等侍衛。 道光中，出為南昌副將，累擢浙江提督。 因病不能巡洋，奪職家居。 咸豐初，治本籍團練，迭克廈門、金島、仙遊，授福建提督。 尋以誤報軍情解任，仍會辦團練。 十一年，卒，孫經寶襲爵。
Changgeng had no son of his own and adopted a kinsman, Tingyu, as heir. Tingyu inherited the barony and was appointed a Second-Rank Bodyguard. Under Daoguang he served as deputy commander at Nanchang and rose in due course to naval commander-in-chief of Zhejiang. Illness kept him from patrolling the coast, his post was revoked, and he retired to his home. Early in Xianfeng he raised militia in his home district, recaptured Xiamen, Jindao, and Xianyou in succession, and was made naval commander-in-chief of Fujian. He was soon removed for misreporting the military situation but continued to help organize the militia. In year 11 he died, and his grandson Jingbao inherited the title.
14
胡振聲，亦同安人，提督貴子。 起行伍，累擢至溫州鎮總兵。 從長庚大破夷艇於台州松門洋，自是屢從長庚擊賊海上。 嘉慶九年六月，率二十六艘運舟材赴福建，至浮鷹洋，遇賊，與總兵孫大剛夾攻，殲賊甚眾，而舟為砲焚，閩師不能救，遂被害。 優恤，諡武壯，予騎都尉兼雲騎尉世職。
Hu Zhensheng, also from Tong'an, was the son of a commander-in-chief's ennobled family. He rose through the ranks to commander of the Wenzhou Garrison. He fought with Changgeng in the great victory over foreign rebel junks at Songmenyang off Taizhou and thereafter joined him in many sea campaigns. In the sixth month of Jiaqing 9 he led twenty-six ships carrying ship timber to Fujian. At Fuyingyang they met pirates; he and Brigade General Sun Dagang attacked from both sides and destroyed many, but his vessel was set afire by cannon shot. The Fujian fleet could not reach him, and he was killed. The court granted generous posthumous honors, gave him the temple name Wuzhuang, and awarded hereditary ranks as Cavalry Commandant and Cloud Cavalry Commandant.
15
王得祿，字玉峰，福建嘉義人。 林爽文倡亂，陷縣城。 得祿家素豐，捐貲募鄉勇，助官軍復之，授把總。 明年，賊复圍城，從總兵柴大紀固守。 及圍解，率鄉勇搜捕大坪頂等處餘匪，焚瑯嶠賊巢，賊渠莊大田就擒。 台灣平，賜花翎、五品頂戴，遷千總。 嘉慶元年，巡洋至獺窟，遇賊，得祿先登，擒吳興信等。 歷年出洋捕海盜，號勇敢，累擢金門營游擊。 七年，從李長庚擊蔡牽於東滬洋，擒賊目徐業等百餘人，又擒呂送於崇武洋，被獎敘。 九年，從總兵羅仁太擊賊於虎頭山洋面，獲船械甚多。 十年，擊蔡牽於虎井洋，敗之，署澎湖協副將。 九月，遇牽於水澳，焚其舟，擒殲硃列等百餘人。 十一年春，牽入台灣，圍府城。 李長庚令得祿與許松年駕小舟自安平港入偵之，帆檣彌望，夜縱火焚賊舟，遂入屯柴頭港。 明日，賊自洲仔尾攻府城北門，得祿率兵躡其後，大呼以前，賊驚卻。 城內軍出夾攻，大敗之，乘勝至洲仔尾，破其營，賊乃遁。 五月，牽复竄鹿耳門，得祿首先沖擊，獲船十，沈船十一。 敘功，加總兵銜。 尋擢福寧鎮總兵。
Wang Delu, whose style was Yufeng, came from Jiayi in Fujian. When Lin Shuangwen rebelled, the county seat fell. Delu's family was wealthy. He donated funds, raised local militia, helped the government recover the city, and was made a platoon leader. The next year the rebels besieged the city again. He served under Brigade General Chai Daji in a stubborn defense. After the siege was raised he led militia against remnant rebels at Dapingding and elsewhere, burned the nest at Langjiao, and the bandit chief Zhuang Datian was taken. When Taiwan was pacified he received the peacock feather and fifth-rank insignia and was promoted to company commander. In Jiaqing 1, while on coastal patrol he reached Takou, met pirates, boarded first, and captured Wu Xingxin and others. Year after year he put to sea against pirates, earning a reputation for courage, and rose through repeated promotions until he was acting commander of the Kinmen battalion. In Jiaqing 7 he fought under Li Changgeng against Cai Qian in the Donghu Sea, taking more than a hundred pirates including the chieftain Xu Ye, and later captured Lü Song in the Chongwu Sea. His merit was duly recorded. In Jiaqing 9 he joined Brigade General Luo Rentai in fighting pirates off Hutoushan and captured a large quantity of ships and arms. In Jiaqing 10 he defeated Cai Qian at the Hujing Sea and was made acting deputy commander of the Penghu garrison. That September he cornered Qian at Shui'ao, burned his fleet, and killed or captured more than a hundred of his men, including Zhu Lie. In the spring of Jiaqing 11, Qian invaded Taiwan and laid siege to the prefectural seat. Li Changgeng sent Delu and Xu Songnian in small boats from Anping Harbor to reconnoiter. The horizon was thick with sails and masts. That night they set the pirate ships ablaze and then moved inland to camp at Chaitou Harbor. The next day the rebels struck the city's north gate from Zhouzaiwei. Delu came up from behind at a run, shouting as he charged, and the startled pirates fell back. The garrison sallied forth to attack from both sides and routed them. The victors pressed on to Zhouzaiwei, destroyed the rebel camp, and the pirates fled. In the fifth month Qian slipped back to Luerhmen. Delu led the assault, capturing ten ships and sinking eleven. His deeds were entered in the merit rolls and he received brigade-general rank. He was soon promoted to brigade general of the Funing garrison.
16
十二年，調南澳鎮。 七月，敗硃濆於雞籠洋，獲船十四。 十一月，又敗其黨於古雷洋，射殪賊目硃金，擒張祈，被獎敘。 未幾，李長庚戰歿，命得祿與邱良功繼任軍事。 十三年，擢浙江提督。 既而調福建，邱良功代之。 時阮元再任浙江巡撫，張師誠為福建巡撫，兩省合力，得祿與良功同心滅賊。 十四年八月，同擊蔡牽於定海漁山，敗之。 牽東南走，追至黑水洋，合擊累日，良功以浙舟駢列賊舟東，得祿率閩舟列浙舟東，戰酣，良功舟傷暫退，得祿舟進，附牽舟，諸賊黨隔不得援。 牽鉛丸盡，以番銀代，得祿額腕皆傷，擲火焚牽舟尾樓，复衝斷其柁。 牽知不免，舉砲自裂其舟沉於海。 詔以牽肆逆十有四年，渠魁就殲，厥功甚偉，錫封得祿二等子爵，賜雙眼花翎。 餘黨千二百人，後皆降，海盜遂息。
In Jiaqing 12 he was transferred to command the Nan'ao garrison. In the seventh month he routed Zhu Fen in the Jilong Sea and took fourteen ships. That November he defeated Zhu Fen's faction again in the Gulei Sea, shot down the pirate chief Zhu Jin, captured Zhang Qi, and received an official commendation. Not long after, Li Changgeng fell in battle, and Delu and Qiu Lianggong were ordered to succeed him in the campaign. In Jiaqing 13 he was promoted to provincial military commander of Zhejiang. He was soon transferred to Fujian, with Qiu Lianggong taking his former post. Ruan Yuan had returned as governor of Zhejiang and Zhang Shicheng governed Fujian. With the two provinces acting together, Delu and Lianggong joined forces in a shared determination to wipe out the pirates. In the eighth month of Jiaqing 14 they jointly attacked Cai Qian off Yushan near Dinghai and put him to flight. Qian fled southeast; they pursued him to the Black Water Sea and fought jointly for days. Lianggong lined the Zhejiang fleet abreast to the east of Qian's ships, while Delu deployed Fujian vessels east of the Zhejiang line. In the heat of battle Lianggong's ship was crippled and he pulled back briefly. Delu pressed forward, locked alongside Qian's vessel, and cut off the pirate lieutenants from reaching him. Qian ran out of lead shot and fired foreign silver coins instead. Delu took wounds to his brow and wrist, threw fire onto the stern tower of Qian's ship, and rammed again, snapping the rudder. Seeing no way out, Qian turned his cannon on his own vessel and blew it apart, sending it to the bottom. An imperial edict noted that Qian had rebelled for fourteen years and that at last the chief was destroyed—a signal achievement. Delu was enfeoffed as a second-class viscount and honored with the double-eyed peacock plume. The twelve hundred survivors eventually surrendered, and piracy on the coast subsided.
17
得祿為福建提督歷十載，屢疏陳緝捕事宜，改定水師船制，皆如議行。 二十五年，調浙江提督。 道光元年，乞病歸。 十二年，台灣張丙作亂，得祿率家屬擒賊目張紅頭等，加太子少保。 十八年，台匪沈和肆掠，輸糧助守，晉太子太保。 二十一年，英吉利犯廈門，命駐守澎湖。 次年，卒，贈伯爵，諡果毅。 次子朝綸襲子爵，官戶部員外郎。
As Fujian's provincial commander for ten years, Delu repeatedly memorialized on anti-piracy measures and reforms to the naval fleet, and his proposals were adopted. In Jiaqing 25 he was transferred back to provincial military commander of Zhejiang. In Daoguang 1 he retired on grounds of illness. In Daoguang 12, when Zhang Bing rebelled in Taiwan, Delu and his household captured the rebel chief Zhang Hongtou and others. He was raised to Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. In Daoguang 18, when the Taiwan bandit Shen He went on a rampage, Delu sent grain to support the defense and was promoted to Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent. In Daoguang 21, when the English attacked Xiamen, he was ordered to hold Penghu. He died the following year. He was posthumously made an earl and given the posthumous name Guoyi, "Resolute and Decisive." His second son Chaolun inherited the viscountcy and held office as an assistant secretary in the Ministry of Revenue.
18
邱良功，福建同安人。 起行伍，屢以獲盜功，洊擢閩安協副將。 嘉慶十年，偕許松年會剿蔡牽，追至小琉球，見台灣師船二為賊圍，赴援，松年舉旗招之，未至。 以違調遣被劾，褫職逮訊。 得白，復原官，署台灣副將。 十一年春，從李長庚擊蔡牽，破洲仔尾賊巢，牽乘間逸，奪頂戴。 五月，破牽於鹿耳門，賜花翎。 十二年，硃濆犯淡水，偕王得祿追至雞籠洋，連敗之，擒殲甚眾，被優敘。 十三年，擢浙江定海鎮總兵。 十四年，擢浙江提督。 偕王得祿合擊蔡牽於漁山外洋，乘上風逼之，夜半浪急，不得進。 明日，复要截環攻，牽且戰且走，傍午逾黑水洋，見綠水。 良功恐日暮賊遁，大呼突進，以己舟逼牽舟，兩篷相結。 賊以椗衝船，陷入死鬥。 良功腓被矛傷，毀賊椗，得脫出。 閩師繼之，牽遂裂舟自沉。 論功，錫封三等男爵，次於王得祿。 或為之不平，良功曰：「海疆肅清，已為快事，名位軒輊何足計？ 」二十二年，入覲，卒於途，賜卹，諡剛勇。 子聯恩襲男爵，官直隸河間協副將。
Qiu Lianggong came from Tong'an in Fujian. He came up through the ranks, and a string of captures earned him steady promotion until he was deputy commander of the Min'an garrison. In Jiaqing 10 he joined Xu Songnian in the campaign against Cai Qian and pursued the pirates to Little Ryukyu. Spotting two Taiwan government ships under siege, he sailed to help—but when Songnian signaled him by flag, he failed to rendezvous. He was impeached for disobeying orders, dismissed from office, and taken into custody for questioning. Exonerated, he was restored to his former rank and made acting deputy commander of Taiwan. In the spring of Jiaqing 11 he fought under Li Changgeng against Cai Qian and destroyed the nest at Zhouzaiwei—but Qian escaped in the confusion, and Lianggong lost his rank insignia as punishment. In the fifth month he routed Qian at Luerhmen and was awarded the peacock feather. In Jiaqing 12, when Zhu Fen struck Tamsui, he and Wang Delu pursued him to the Jilong Sea, defeated him in successive engagements, killed or captured a great number, and earned special commendation. In Jiaqing 13 he was promoted to brigade general of the Dinghai garrison in Zhejiang. In Jiaqing 14 he was made provincial military commander of Zhejiang. With Wang Delu he attacked Cai Qian in the open sea off Yushan, riding the wind to press him—but at midnight the seas turned rough and they could not close. The next day they tried again to block and surround him. Qian fought his way eastward, and by noon they had passed the Black Water Sea into open blue water. Fearing Qian would escape at nightfall, Lianggong shouted and dashed forward, driving his ship against Qian's until their sails became entangled. The pirates grappled with anchors and rammed his ship, and the two crews were locked in a fight to the death. A spear pierced Lianggong's calf, but he smashed the pirates' grappling gear and broke free. The Fujian fleet pressed the attack, and Qian scuttled his own vessel. For his service he was enfeoffed as a third-class baron—one rank below Wang Delu. Some felt the award slighted him, but Lianggong said, "The seas are cleared—that is joy enough. What do petty differences in rank matter? " In Jiaqing 22 he set out for court but died on the road; the throne granted condolence funds and the posthumous title Gangyong, Staunch and Brave. His son Lian'en inherited the baronetcy and held the post of deputy brigade commander of the Hejian Brigade in Zhili.
19
陳步雲，浙江瑞安人。 入伍隸水師，數獲盜，以勇力稱，授溫州營把總。 從良功追蔡牽，步雲以四十人駕舟徑逼牽艦鏖鬥，舟小不相當，見兩提督至，亟投火罐焚賊艦，以長戟鉤舷，率數卒躍登，短兵相搏，殲牽妻及其黨。 賊艦已壞，牽猶持利刃踞柁樓，顧欲取之。 良功隔船疾呼，船與水平，速去，放長繩水中援之起，而牽船沒矣。 步雲身被十數創，兩提督皆臨慰視。 事聞，賜獎武銀牌，擢千總。 累遷閩安副將。 總督孫爾準欲裁減師船，步雲言李提督所造船高大堅緻，其利遠勝同安夾板、快駒諸船、裁之緝匪無具，有事不能制敵，議乃寢。 爾準薦其才可勝專閫，入覲，宣宗曰：「汝即隨邱、王兩提督攻沉蔡牽之陳步雲耶？ 」詢戰功甚悉。 遂擢定海鎮總兵，歷瓊州、福寧、金門、海壇諸鎮。 道光十九年，以傷發，乞解職。 三十年，卒。
Chen Buyun came from Ruian in Zhejiang. He enlisted in the navy, captured pirates again and again, and won a name for raw courage before he was made platoon leader of the Wenzhou Battalion. When Lianggong pursued Cai Qian, Buyun put forty men in a boat and rowed straight at the pirate flagship. His craft was far too small for a fair fight, but as the two admirals came up he hurled fire pots onto the enemy deck, hooked the rail with a long halberd, and led a handful of men over the side. In close combat they killed Qian's wife and her band. The pirate ship was already breaking up, yet Qian still crouched on the helm tower with a naked blade, turning as though he meant to cut Buyun down. Lianggong shouted from the next boat: "Trim her to the waterline and get clear!" He dropped a long rope into the sea and hauled Buyun up just as Qian's vessel went under. Buyun had taken more than a dozen wounds, and both admirals came aboard to comfort him and see to his injuries. When word reached the throne, he received a silver commendation plaque for valor and was promoted to company commander. He rose in due course to deputy commander of the Min'an Brigade. When Governor-General Sun Erzhun proposed trimming the fleet, Buyun argued that the ships Admiral Li had built were tall, stout, and finely made—far superior to Tong'an double-planked craft and Kuaiju runners. Cut them and the navy would lack the tools to hunt pirates and could not hold an enemy in a crisis. The plan was dropped. Erzhun recommended him as fit for independent command. At court the Daoguang Emperor asked, "Are you Chen Buyun—the man who followed Admirals Qiu and Wang and helped sink Cai Qian? " The emperor questioned him closely about the fight. He was then made commander of the Dinghai Garrison and went on to hold the garrisons at Qiongzhou, Funing, Jinmen, and Haitan. In Daoguang 19 his old wounds reopened and he asked to be relieved of duty. In year 30 he died.
20
許松年，字蓉俊，浙江瑞安人。 以武舉效力水師，從李長庚積功至提標參將。 嘉慶十年，護理金門鎮總兵。 擊蔡牽於小琉球； 又擊硃濆、烏石二於宮仔洋，從李長庚追敗之於閩、粵交界甲子洋。 又迭擊牽於青龍港、斗米洋。 十一年，偕王得祿敗牽於台灣洲仔尾，跐海水而登，焚溺無算。 是年夏，李長庚攻牽於鹿耳門，松年扼張坑、返埕洋面，獲賊船一，沉船三，又於水澳擒蔡三來等。 李長庚論水師將材，舉松年可獨當一面，總督阿林保以疏聞。 十二年，從長庚擊蔡牽於大星嶼、浮鷹洋，松年躍入賊船獲之，被優敘。 十三年，硃濆潛匿東涌外洋，命松年躡剿，遂移師入粵。 追至長山尾，了見賊船四十餘，知其最巨者為濆所乘，並力圍攻，濆受砲傷，未幾斃。 詔嘉松年奮勇，克殲渠魁，賜花翎，予雲騎尉世職。 粵匪張保仔竄閩洋金門、廈門，松年遣漁船誘之，以舟師圍擊，獲船七，沉船六，被優敘。 十五年，傷發回籍，尋丁母憂。 十九年，授甘肅西寧鎮總兵，歷延綏、漳州、天津、碣石諸鎮。 道光元年，擢廣東陸路提督，調福建水師提督。 六年，台灣械鬥，松年方閱兵，彈壓解散，總督孫爾準與之不協，尋以治理輕縱，被議褫職，留台效力。 乞病歸，卒於家。 子錫麟，襲世職。
Xu Songnian, whose style was Rongjun, came from Ruian in Zhejiang. After taking his degree as a military licentiate he entered the navy; serving under Li Changgeng he rose on merit to brigade general on the admiral's staff. In Jiaqing 10 he served as acting commander of the Jinmen Garrison. He fought Cai Qian off Lesser Ryukyu; He also engaged Zhu Fen and Wushi II in Gongzai Bay and, with Li Changgeng in pursuit, routed them at Jiazi Bay where Fujian meets Guangdong. He clashed with Qian again and again at Qinglong Harbor and Doumi Bay. In year 11 he and Wang Delu beat Qian at Zhouziwei in Taiwan, wading through the surf to land; the pirates burned and drowned were beyond counting. That summer Li Changgeng struck Qian at Lu'ermen while Songnian held the waters off Zhangkeng and Fancheng, taking one pirate vessel and sinking three; at Shui'ao he also captured Cai Sanlai and his men. Appraising the navy's officers, Li Changgeng named Songnian as a man who could hold a sector on his own; Governor-General A Linbao passed the recommendation up by memorial. In year 12 he followed Changgeng against Cai Qian at Daxing Isle and Fuying Bay; Songnian boarded an enemy vessel and took it, earning a special commendation. In year 13 Zhu Fen hid in the open waters off Dongyong; Songnian was sent to hunt him down and the fleet crossed into Guangdong. At Changshanwei they sighted more than forty pirate junks and closed on the largest, which Fen himself commanded. Under combined fire Fen was hit by cannon; he died soon after. The throne praised Songnian's daring in destroying the chief bandit, granted him the peacock feather, and gave him the hereditary rank of Cloud Cavalry Commander. When the Guangdong outlaw Zhang Baozai slipped into Fujian waters off Jinmen and Xiamen, Songnian used fishing boats as bait and closed with the fleet, taking seven junks and sinking six; he was again singled out for reward. In year 15 his old wounds reopened and he went home; not long after he entered mourning for his mother. In year 19 he was made commander of the Xining Garrison in Gansu and later held the garrisons at Yansui, Zhangzhou, Tianjin, and Jieshi. In Daoguang 1 he was promoted to land forces admiral of Guangdong and transferred to naval admiral of Fujian. In year 6 faction fighting broke out in Taiwan while Songnian was inspecting troops; he suppressed the mobs and broke them up. Governor-General Sun Erzhun took against him, and soon he was impeached for lax rule, stripped of rank, and kept on the island to serve. He pleaded illness, went home, and died there. His son Xilin inherited the hereditary rank.
21
黃標，字殿豪，廣東潮州人。 由行伍拔補千總，擢守備。 乾隆五十五年，艇匪肆掠，總督福康安議練水師，募奇才異能者領之。 標技勇過人，生長海壖，習知水道險易，能久伏水底，視物歷歷可數，特被識拔。 以捕獲龍門洋盜及狗頭山匪功，擢都司，署游擊。
Huang Biao, whose style was Dianhao, came from Chaozhou in Guangdong. Raised from the ranks, he was made company commander and then battalion commander. In Qianlong 55 boat pirates were ravaging the coast; Governor-General Fuk'anggan proposed rebuilding the navy and recruiting exceptional men to command it. Biao's skill and nerve were extraordinary. Raised on the tidal flats, he knew every shoal and channel; he could stay under water for long stretches and see clearly enough to count what lay below. For this he was singled out. For capturing pirates in Longmen Bay and bandits on Gou Tou Mountain he was promoted to battalion director and given acting rank as guerrilla colonel.
22
嘉慶元年，剿匪於南澎外洋，獲李超勝等三十餘名。 仁宗素知其名，詔嘉緝捕勤能，擢參將。 二年，俘洋盜胡三勝等，复擊斃安南匪首，盡獲其眾，被優敘。 三年，遷澄海副將。 未幾，擢廣東左翼鎮總兵，命總統巡洋水師，責以肅清海盜。 四年，剿匪大放雞山及雙魚桅、夾門外洋，殲獲甚眾，賜花翎，命繪像以進。 尋以盜劫鹽艘被劾，詔原之。 六年，复擊賊於南澎外洋，獲田亞猛等。 七年，偕提督孫全謀剿博羅會匪，連破羊矢坑、羅溪營要隘，搗其巢。 事平優敘，並被珍賚。 自將水師，飲食寢處與士卒共，先後獲匪六百餘名，粵海倚為保障。 八年，偕孫全謀出海捕賊，賊遁廣州灣。 標議合兵守隘，俟賊糧盡可盡殲。 全謀慮持久有風濤患，乃分兵，賊得突圍逸出。 標歎曰：「此機一失，海警未已！ 」憤懣成疾。 尋坐師久無功，吏議奪職留任。 未幾，卒。
In Jiaqing 1 he hunted bandits in the outer waters off Nanpeng and took more than thirty men, including Li Chaosheng. The Jiaqing Emperor already knew his name; an edict praised his tireless work in pursuit and capture, and he was made brigade general. In year 2 he took sea pirates including Hu Sansheng, then killed an Annamese bandit chief and captured his whole band; he received a special commendation. In year 3 he was transferred to deputy commander of the Chenghai Brigade. Soon afterward he was made commander of the Guangdong Left Wing Garrison, put in overall charge of the patrol fleet, and ordered to clear the seas of pirates. In year 4 he crushed bandits off Dafangji Mountain and in the waters beyond Shuangyumao and Jiamen, killing and capturing a great host; he received the peacock feather and was ordered to sit for a portrait to be sent to court. He was soon impeached after pirates robbed a salt convoy, but the throne pardoned him. In year 6 he fought bandits again in the outer waters off Nanpeng and captured Tian Yameng and his men. In year 7 he joined Commander Sun Quanmou against the Boluo secret-society rebels, broke the passes at Yangshikeng and Luoxi Camp in succession, and destroyed their stronghold. After the campaign he was richly rewarded for merit and received imperial gifts. He commanded the fleet in person, eating and sleeping with his men. In time he took more than six hundred bandits, and Guangdong waters looked to him as their shield. In year 8 he and Sun Quanmou put to sea after the pirates, who fled into Guangzhou Bay. Biao urged a joint blockade of the passes until the pirates starved and could be wiped out. Quanmou feared a long siege would expose the fleet to storms, split his forces, and the pirates broke out and escaped. Biao sighed, "Miss this chance and the sea will never be quiet! Resentment and grief brought on illness. Soon the long campaign without victory led the civil officials to recommend removing his rank while leaving him in post. He died shortly after.
23
自安南夷艇散後，餘黨留粵者分五幫：曰林阿發、曰總兵保、曰郭學顯、曰烏石二、曰鄭乙。 提督錢夢虎、孫全謀皆庸材，不能辦賊。 標歿後，益無良將，惟林國良、許廷桂以死事聞。
After the Annamese rebel fleets broke up, the remnants in Guangdong split into five bands: Lin Afa, Zongbing Bao, Guo Xuexian, Wushi II, and Zheng Yi. Commanders Qian Menghu and Sun Quanmou were mediocre officers who could not suppress the pirates. After Biao died there were even fewer able commanders; only Lin Guoliang and Xu Tinggui were remembered for dying in battle.
24
國良，福建海澄人。 世襲騎都尉，授廣東碣石鎮標游擊，累遷海澄副將，繼標為左翼鎮總兵。 十三年，追剿烏石二於丫洲洋，擊沉數艘，賊艦續至益多。 國良以傷殞，優恤，諡果壯。
Lin Guoliang came from Haicheng in Fujian. He inherited the rank of Cavalry Commandant, served as guerrilla colonel of the Jieshi standard in Guangdong, rose to deputy commander at Haicheng, and succeeded Biao as commander of the Left Wing Garrison. In year 13 he pursued Wushi II to Yazhou Bay, sank several junks, but pirate ships kept arriving in greater numbers. Lin Guoliang died of his wounds. The court granted generous posthumous honors and the temple name Guozhuang.
25
廷桂，廣東歸善人。 由行伍擢千總。 乾隆中，從征台灣，累遷海門營參將。 國良歿，護理左翼鎮總兵。 十四年，擊殲匪首總兵保於外洋，圍其餘黨。 張保仔率大隊來援。 眾寡不敵，廷桂死之。 賜卹，予雲騎尉世職。
Xu Tinggui came from Guishan in Guangdong. He rose from the ranks to company commander. Under Qianlong he fought in Taiwan and rose in due course to brigade general of the Haimen Garrison. When Lin Guoliang died he acted as commander of the Left Wing Garrison. In year 14 he destroyed the bandit chief Zongbing Bao on the open sea and besieged the rest of his followers. Zhang Baozi came with a large relief force. Outnumbered, Xu Tinggui was killed. The court granted condolence honors and the hereditary rank of Cloud Cavalry Commandant.
26
洎蔡牽既滅，惟粵匪存，於是百齡為兩廣總督，乃斷接濟，整軍紀，越一年，剿撫以次定。 東南海氛始靖。
Once Cai Qian was gone only Guangdong bandits remained. Governor-General Bailin cut off their supplies, tightened discipline, and within a year subdued them by force and persuasion in turn. At last the southeast coast was calm.
27
論曰：東南海寇之擾，始末十有餘年。 惟浙師李長庚一人能辦賊，以閩帥牽掣而阻成功，然長庚忠誠勇略聞於海內，上結主知，廟算既孚，乃專倚畀。 洎閩、浙合力，賊勢浸衰，不幸長庚中殞，而王得祿、邱良功等以部將承其遺志，卒殲渠魁。 粵將惟黃標可用，而未盡其才。 百齡乘閩、浙殄賊之後，剿撫兼施，遂如摧枯拉朽。 要之海戰惟恃船堅砲利，與斷接濟而已，循之則勝，違之則敗。 得失之林，故無幸哉！
The commentators observe: The southeast sea pirates plagued the coast for more than ten years. Only Li Changgeng of the Zhejiang fleet could truly fight the pirates, though Fujian commanders hampered him. His loyalty and courage were known throughout the realm; he won the emperor's trust, and once court strategy was set the throne relied on him alone. When Fujian and Zhejiang joined forces the pirates weakened. Changgeng fell in battle, but Wang Delu, Qiu Lianggong, and other subordinates carried on his purpose and finally destroyed the rebel leader. Among Guangdong commanders only Huang Biao was truly capable, yet his talent was never fully used. Bailin, after Fujian and Zhejiang had destroyed the main pirate forces, combined suppression with pacification and swept the rest away like dry rot. In short, naval war depends on strong ships, heavy guns, and cutting off supplies. Follow that path and you win; ignore it and you lose. Success and failure follow such a clear pattern that luck has little to do with it.
