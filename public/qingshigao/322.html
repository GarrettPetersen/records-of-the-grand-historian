=竇光鼐=竇光鼐，字元調，山東諸城人。 乾隆七年進士，選庶吉士，散館授編修。 大考四等，罰俸。 高宗夙知光鼐，居數月，擢左中允。 累遷內閣學士。 二十年，授左副都御史。 督浙江學政。 上南巡，臨海縣訓導章知鄴將獻詩，光鼐以詩拙阻之。 知鄴欲訐光鼐，光鼐以聞。 上召知鄴試以詩，詩甚拙，且言原從軍。 上斥其妄，命奪職戍闢展。 後數年，上欲赦知鄴還，而知鄴妄為悖逆語，欲以陷光鼐，上乃誅之。
Dou Guangnai, styled Yuandiao, came from Zhucheng in Shandong. He passed the jinshi examination in 1742, entered the Hanlin Academy as a probationer academician, and on completing his term was made a compiler. He scored fourth class in the palace examination and was fined a portion of his salary. The Qianlong Emperor had known him for some time; a few months later he was promoted to Left Vice Director of Studies. He rose through successive promotions to Grand Secretary of the Imperial Cabinet. In 1755 he was appointed Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. He served as educational commissioner for Zhejiang. During an imperial southern tour, Zhang Zhiye, an instructor from Linhai County, prepared to present a poem, but Guangnai stopped him because the verse was poor. Zhiye tried to denounce Guangnai; Guangnai reported the matter to the Emperor. The Emperor summoned Zhiye for a poetic trial; his poem was wretched, and he admitted he had once been a soldier. The Emperor denounced his effrontery, stripped him of his post, and sentenced him to banishment at Bizhan. Years later the Emperor meant to pardon and recall him, but Zhiye uttered seditious words meant to frame Guangnai, and the Emperor had him put to death instead.
2
光鼐學政任滿，還京師。 秋讞，光鼐以廣西囚陳父悔守田禾殺賊，不宜入情實； 貴州囚羅阿扛逞兇殺人，不宜入緩決：持異議，簽商刑部，語忿激。 刑部遽以聞，上命大學士來保、史貽直，協辦大學士梁詩正覆覈，請如刑部議，且言光鼐先已畫題，何得又請改擬。 上詰光鼐，光鼐言：「兩案異議，本屬簽商，並非固執。 因會議時言詞過激，刑部遽將簽出未定之稿先行密奏。 臣未能降心抑氣，與刑部婉言，咎實難辭，請交部嚴加議處。 」上以「會讞大典，光鼐意氣自用，甚至紛呶謾罵而不自知。 設將來預議者尤而效之，於國憲朝章不可為訓」。 命下部嚴議，當左遷，仍命留任。 光鼐疏言：「事主殺竊盜，律止杖徒。 近來各省多以竊盜拒捕而被殺，比罪人不拒捕而擅殺，皆以鬥論，寬竊盜而嚴事主，非禁暴之意。 應請遵本律。 」議行。
When his tenure as educational commissioner ended, he returned to Beijing. At the autumn assizes, Guangnai argued that Chen Fuhui, a Guangxi convict who killed a thief while defending his crops, should not be classified as intentional murder; and that Luo Akang, a Guizhou murderer who had killed in violent arrogance, should not be entered for reprieve. He lodged objections and signed the file for discussion with the Ministry of Punishments, speaking with heated indignation. The Ministry of Punishments reported the matter at once. The Emperor ordered Grand Secretaries Laibao and Shi Yizhi, with Associate Grand Secretary Liang Shizheng, to re-examine the cases. They recommended following the ministry's decision and noted that Guangnai had already signed the memorial—why was he asking now to revise the draft? The Emperor interrogated Guangnai, who replied: "My objections in both cases were meant for countersigned consultation, not stubborn insistence. Because I spoke too sharply in conference, the Ministry of Punishments submitted the unsigned draft to Your Majesty in a secret memorial before consultation was finished. I failed to check my temper and negotiate courteously with the ministry. The fault is plainly mine; I beg that my case be referred to the ministry for strict discipline." The Emperor said: "At a formal judicial conference Guangnai indulges his temper to the point of shouting abuse without even noticing it. If future participants take this as precedent, it will undermine the authority of law and court discipline—this cannot be tolerated." He ordered the ministry to deliberate strict punishment, which warranted demotion, but still directed that Guangnai keep his post. Guangnai memorialized: "When a homeowner kills a burglar, the code provides only beating or imprisonment. Yet lately many provinces classify such killings as fighting, treating them like unpremeditated homicide during brawling—indulgent toward thieves and harsh toward householders, which defeats the purpose of restraining lawlessness. The original statute should be applied." The recommendation was approved and implemented.
3
二十七年，上以光鼐迂拙，不勝副都御史，命署內閣學士。 授順天府府尹。 坐屬縣蝗不以時捕，左遷四品京堂，仍留任。 旋赴三河、懷柔督捕蝗，疏言：「近京州縣多旗地，嗣後捕蝗，民為旗地佃，當一體撥夫應用。 」上從所請，以諭直隸總督楊廷璋。 廷璋言自方觀承始設護田夫，旗、民均役。 上復以詰光鼐，召還京師，令從軍機大臣入見。 問：「民為旗地佃，不肯撥夫應用，屬何人莊業？ 」光鼐不能對，請徵東北二路同知及三河、順義知縣質證。 退又疏請罷護田夫，別定派夫捕蝗事例。 上以光鼐所見迂鄙紕繆，下部議，奪職。
In 1762 the Emperor judged Guangnai too rigid and tactless for a vice censor-in-chief post and reassigned him as acting grand secretary of the cabinet. He was then appointed prefect of Shuntian Prefecture. When subordinate counties failed to control locusts on schedule, he was demoted to a fourth-rank capital post but allowed to remain in office. He soon went to Sanhe and Huairou to supervise locust control and reported: "Because many counties near the capital include banner estates, tenants farming banner land should be mustered for locust duty on equal terms with other farmers. The Emperor approved and relayed the order to Zhili Governor Yang Tingzhang. Yang replied that field-guard laborers had been instituted under Fang Guancheng, with bannermen and civilians serving alike. The Emperor questioned Guangnai again, summoned him back to Beijing, and required him to audience alongside the Grand Council. He asked whose estate managers were responsible when tenant farmers on banner land refused to supply laborers. Guangnai could not answer and asked that the subprefects of the northeast circuits and the magistrates of Sanhe and Shunyi be summoned to testify. On withdrawing he memorialized again to abolish field-guard laborers and establish separate rules for locust labor levies. The Emperor found his proposals pedantic and mistaken, referred him to the ministries for judgment, and stripped him of his post.
4
居數月，諭光鼐但拘鈍無能，無大過，左授通政司副使。 再遷宗人府府丞。 复督浙江學政，擢吏部侍郎。 浙江州縣倉庫多虧缺，上命察覈。 光鼐疏言：「前總督陳輝祖、巡撫王亶望貪墨敗露，總督富勒渾未嚴察。 臣聞嘉興、海鹽、平陽諸縣虧數皆逾十萬，當察覈分別定擬。 」上嘉其持正，命尚書曹文埴、侍郎姜晟往會巡撫伊齡阿及光鼐察覈。
Months later the Emperor ruled that Guangnai was merely dull and inept rather than guilty of grave misconduct, and demoted him to vice commissioner of the Court of Transmission. He was soon transferred again, to vice director of the Imperial Clan Court. He was reappointed educational commissioner for Zhejiang and promoted to vice minister of Personnel. Many Zhejiang prefectural and county granaries showed shortfalls; the Emperor ordered an audit. Guangnai reported: "After Governors Chen Huizu and Wang Danwang were exposed for corruption, Governor-General Fulehun failed to investigate rigorously. I have heard that shortfalls in Jiaxing, Haiyan, Pingyang, and other counties each exceed one hundred thousand taels; they should be audited and penalties set case by case." The Emperor commended his integrity and sent Ministers Cao Wenchi and Jiang Sheng to join Governor-General Yiling'a and Guangnai in conducting the audit.

旋疏劾永嘉知縣席世維借諸生穀輸倉； 平陽知縣黃梅假彌虧苛斂，且於母死日演劇； 仙居知縣徐延翰斃臨海諸生馬寘於獄； 並及布政使盛住上年詣京師，攜貲過豐，召物議； 總督富勒渾經嘉興，供應浩煩，餽閽役數至千百。 上命大學士阿桂如浙江按治。 阿桂疏言盛住詣京師，附攜應解參價銀三萬九千馀，非私貲； 平陽知縣黃梅母九十生日演劇，即以其夕死； 仙居諸生馬寘誣寺僧博，復與鬥毆，因下獄死。 光鼐語皆不仇。 光鼐再疏論梅事，言阿桂遣屬吏詣平陽諮訪，未得實，躬赴平陽覆察。 伊齡阿再疏劾光鼐赴平陽刑迫求佐證諸狀，上責光鼐乖張瞀亂，命奪職，逮下刑部。 光鼐尋奏：「親赴平陽，士民呈梅派捐單票，田一畝捐大錢五十； 又勒捐富戶數至千百貫； 每歲採買倉穀不予值。 梅在縣八年，所侵穀值及捐錢不下二十萬。 母死不欲發喪，特令演劇。 」上以光鼐呈單票有據，時阿桂已​​還京師，令復如浙江秉公按治，並命江蘇巡撫閔鶚元會讞，以光鼐質證。 阿桂、鶚元疏言梅婪索事實，論如律。 上以光鼐所奏非妄，命署光祿寺卿，阿桂、文埴、晟、伊齡阿皆下部議。 旋擢光鼐宗人府府丞。 遷禮部侍郎。 复督浙江學政。 再遷左都御史。
He soon impeached Xi Shiwei, magistrate of Yongjia, for borrowing grain from licentiates to fill the granary; Huang Mei, magistrate of Pingyang, for concealing deficits, extorting levies, and staging an opera on the day his mother died; Xu Yanhan, magistrate of Xianju, for beating to death the licentiate Ma Zhi of Linhai in prison; and Provincial Administration Commissioner Sheng Zhu, whose unusually large baggage on his trip to the capital the year before had drawn public suspicion; and Governor-General Fulehun, whose passage through Jiaxing had involved lavish hospitality and gifts to gate servants numbering in the hundreds and thousands. The Emperor sent Grand Secretary Agui to Zhejiang to investigate and adjudicate. Agui reported that Sheng Zhu had carried thirty-nine thousand-odd taels of silver due for ginseng tribute payment, not private goods; that Huang Mei's mother had turned ninety on the day of the opera and died that same evening; and that Ma Zhi had falsely accused a temple monk of gambling, fought with him, and died in prison after being detained. None of Guangnai's charges matched the facts. Guangnai memorialized again on Huang Mei's case, saying Agui's subordinates had inquired in Pingyang without establishing the facts, so he went there himself to reinvestigate. Yiling'a impeached him again, charging that in Pingyang he had used torture to extract corroborating testimony. The Emperor denounced Guangnai as reckless and muddled, stripped him of office, and handed him over to the Ministry of Punishments. Guangnai soon submitted: "When I went to Pingyang in person, local gentry and commoners presented Huang Mei's levy receipts, charging fifty large cash per mu; he also extorted donations from wealthy households amounting to hundreds and thousands of strings; each year he purchased granary grain without paying fair value. In eight years as magistrate, the grain he misappropriated and the levies he extorted totaled no less than two hundred thousand taels. When his mother died he delayed the funeral and staged the opera instead. The Emperor found Guangnai's receipts convincing. Agui had already returned to Beijing, so the Emperor sent Guangnai back to Zhejiang to investigate impartially and ordered Jiangsu Governor Min E'yuan to join the assize and take Guangnai's testimony. Agui and Min reported that Huang Mei's extortion was established and sentenced him according to law. The Emperor ruled that Guangnai's charges were not false and appointed him acting minister of the Court of Imperial Entertainments, while Agui, Cao Wenchi, Jiang Sheng, and Yiling'a were all referred to the ministries for disciplinary review. Guangnai was soon promoted to vice director of the Imperial Clan Court. He was transferred to vice minister of Rites. He was again appointed educational commissioner for Zhejiang. He was promoted again, to Left Censor-in-Chief.

六十年，充會試正考官，榜發，首歸安王以铻，次王以銜，兄弟聯名高第。 大學士和珅素嫉光鼐，言於上，謂光鼐迭為浙江學政，事有私。 上命解任聽部議，及廷試，和珅為讀卷官，以銜復以第一人及第，事乃解。 命予四品銜休致。 卒。
In 1795 he served as chief metropolitan examiner. When the results were published, Wang Yiyue of Gui'an ranked first and Wang Yixian second—brothers who had placed at the top together. Grand Secretary Heshen, who had long resented Guangnai, told the Emperor that because Guangnai had repeatedly served as Zhejiang educational commissioner, the affair involved favoritism. The Emperor ordered Guangnai dismissed pending ministry review. At the palace examination Heshen served as reader of papers, and Yixian again placed first, after which the matter was dropped. He was granted fourth-rank insignia and retired. He died.
7
=李漱芳=李漱芳，字藝圃，四川渠縣人。 乾隆二十二年進士，授吏部主事。 再遷郎中。 三十三年，授河南道監察御史。 巡視中城，尚書福隆安家奴藍大恃勢縱恣，挾無賴酗酒，橫行市肆間。 漱芳捕治，論奏，高宗深嘉之，命戍藍大，以福隆安下吏議。 尋擢工科給事中。 三十九年，壽張民王倫為亂。 漱芳疏陳奸民聚眾滋事，為飢寒所迫； 又言近畿亦有流民扶老攜幼，遷徙逃亡，有司監盧溝橋，阻不使北行。 給事中範宜賓亦以為言，請增設粥廠。 上命侍郎高樸、袁守侗率宜賓、漱芳往盧溝橋及近畿諸城鎮省視，初無流民。 倫亂定，俘其徒檻致京師廷鞫，命漱芳旁視，無言為飢寒迫者。 問歲事，對秋收尚及半。 上責漱芳妄言，代奸民解說，心術不可問，不宜复居言路，為世道人心害，宥罪，降禮部主事。 四十三年，禮部請以漱芳升授員外郎。 故事，郎中、員外郎員缺，選應升授者，擬正、陪上請。 至是，獨以漱芳請。 上不懌，責尚書永貴擅專邀譽，涉明季黨援朋比之習，奪其職。 漱芳久之乃遷員外郎。 卒。
Li Shufang, styled Yipu, came from Qu County in Sichuan. He passed the jinshi examination in 1757 and was appointed a principal clerk in the Ministry of Personnel. He was promoted again, to director. In 1768 he was appointed censor of the Henan circuit. While inspecting the central city, he found Lan Da, a bondservant of Minister Fulong'an, abusing his master's power, drinking with ruffians, and terrorizing the markets. Shufang arrested and prosecuted him. The Qianlong Emperor greatly commended the action, sentenced Lan Da to banishment, and referred Fulong'an to the ministries for review. He was soon promoted to supervising secretary of the Board of Works. In 1774 the Shouzhang commoner Wang Lun led a rebellion. Shufang reported that the rebels had gathered because they were driven by hunger and cold; and that refugees near the capital were fleeing with the elderly and children while officials at Lugou Bridge were blocking them from traveling north. Supervising Secretary Fan Yibin made similar claims and asked that additional soup kitchens be established. The Emperor sent Vice Ministers Gao Pu and Yuan Shoutong, with Fan and Shufang, to inspect Lugou Bridge and nearby towns. They found no refugees at first. After the rebellion was suppressed, captives were brought to Beijing in cangues for trial. Shufang was ordered to observe. None claimed they had been driven by hunger or cold. Asked about the harvest, they replied that more than half the autumn crop remained. The Emperor rebuked Shufang for fabricating reports and excusing rebels. His motives were suspect; he should not hold a remonstrance post again lest he harm public morals. His life was spared, but he was demoted to principal clerk in the Ministry of Rites. In 1778 the Ministry of Rites nominated Shufang for promotion to vice director. By precedent, when director and vice director posts fell vacant, candidates for promotion were nominated in principal and alternate pairs for imperial approval. On this occasion, however, only Shufang was nominated. The Emperor was displeased and rebuked Minister Yonggui for seeking credit on his own, accusing him of reviving late-Ming habits of factional patronage. Yonggui was stripped of office. Only much later was Shufang promoted to vice director. He died.

=範宜賓=範宜賓，漢軍鑲黃旗人，大學士文程後也。 以廕生官戶部郎中，歷御史給事中，累遷太常寺少卿。 出為安徽布政使，與巡撫胡文伯不相能，兩江總督高晉以聞。 上召宜賓還，授左副都御史。 宜賓奏言屬縣蝗見，屢請捕治，文伯執不可。 上為黜文伯，而宜賓亦以捕蝗不力下吏議，當左遷。 上以宜賓舊為御史尚黽勉，命仍為御史。 宜賓疏言籓臬有所陳奏，輒呈稿督撫，當禁飭。 上以整飭吏治，要在朝廷綱紀肅清，自無扶同蒙蔽之事，不在設法峻防，置其議不行。 及與漱芳同被譴，上以宜賓漢軍世僕，乃敢妄言幹譽，特重其罰，奪職，戍新疆。
Fan Yibin, a Han bannerman of the Bordered Yellow Banner, was a descendant of Grand Secretary Wencheng. Entering office by inheritance privilege, he became a director in the Ministry of Revenue, served as censor and supervising secretary, and rose to vice minister of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices. He was posted as Anhui provincial administration commissioner but clashed with Governor Hu Wenbo, prompting Liangjiang Governor-General Gao Jin to report the conflict. The Emperor recalled Fan and appointed him Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. Fan reported locust infestations in subordinate counties and repeatedly urged control measures, but Hu Wenbo refused. The Emperor dismissed Hu Wenbo, but Fan was also referred to the ministries for failing to control the locusts effectively and was slated for demotion. Seeing that Fan had once served as a censor and still showed diligence, the Emperor kept him in that post. Fan memorialized that provincial and circuit officials habitually submitted draft memorials to their governors first and urged that the practice be forbidden. The Emperor replied that sound governance depended on clear court discipline, not on suspicion and harsh preventive rules, and rejected the proposal. When he was punished along with Shufang, the Emperor held that as a hereditary Han banner bondservant Fan had dared speak falsely for credit and imposed a heavier sentence: dismissal and banishment to Xinjiang.

=曹錫寶=曹錫寶，字鴻書，一字劍亭，江南上海人。 乾隆初，以舉人考授內閣中書，充軍機處章京。 資深當擢侍讀，錫寶辭。 大學士傅恆知其欲以甲科進，乃不為請遷。 二十二年，成進士，改庶吉士。 以母憂歸，病瘍，數年乃愈。 三十一年，散館，改刑部主事。 再遷郎中。 授山東糧道。 衛千總寧廷言子惠以索逋殺千總張繼渠，錫寶下部議。 上巡山東，召見，命來京以部屬用。 以大學士阿桂奏，令入四庫全書館自效。 書成，以國子監司業升用。
Cao Xibao, styled Hongshu and also known as Jianting, came from Shanghai in Jiangnan. Early in the Qianlong reign he qualified through examination as a provincial graduate, became a secretariat drafter, and served as a Grand Council clerk. When seniority made him eligible for promotion to reader, Xibao declined. Grand Secretary Fu Heng knew he hoped to advance through the palace examination and did not recommend him for promotion. In 1757 he passed the jinshi examination and entered the Hanlin Academy. He went home to mourn his mother, fell ill with ulcers, and took several years to recover. In 1766 he completed his Hanlin term and was appointed principal clerk in the Ministry of Punishments. He was promoted again, to director. He was appointed grain intendant of Shandong. When guard company commander Ning Tingyan's son Hui killed fellow commander Zhang Jiqu while collecting a debt, Xibao referred the case to the ministries for judgment. During an imperial tour of Shandong he was summoned for audience and ordered to the capital for appointment within the ministries. On Grand Secretary Agui's recommendation he was assigned to the Siku Quanshu compilation project. When the compilation was finished he was promoted to vice director of the Imperial Academy.
10
居三年，上以錫寶補司業無期，特授陝西道監察御史。 時協辦大學士和珅執政，其奴劉全恃勢營私，衣服、車馬、居室皆踰制。 錫寶將論劾，侍郎南匯吳省欽與錫寶同鄉里，聞其事，和珅方從上熱河行在，馳以告和珅，令全毀其室，衣服、車馬有踰制，皆匿無跡。 錫寶疏至，上詰和珅。 和珅言平時戒約嚴，或扈從日久漸生事，乞嚴察重懲。 乃命留京辦事王大臣召錫寶問狀，又令步軍統領遣官從錫寶至全家察視，無跡，錫寶自承冒昧。 上召錫寶詣行在面詰，錫寶奏全倚勢營私，未有實跡，第為和珅「杜漸防微」，乃有此奏。 复諭軍機大臣、大學士梁國治等覆詢，錫寶又承「杜漸防微」語失當，請治罪。 下部議，當左遷。 上手詔略言：「平時用人行政，不肯存逆詐億不信之見。 若委用臣工不能推誠佈公，而猜疑防範，據一時無根之談，遽入人以罪，使天下重足而立、側目而視，斷無此政體。 錫寶未察虛實，以書生拘迂之見，託為正言陳奏。 姑寬其罰，改革職留任。 」五十七年，卒。
After three years, with no timely vacancy for the vice directorship, the Emperor specially appointed him censor of the Shaanxi circuit. Associate Grand Secretary Heshen was then in power. His bondservant Liu Quan abused that influence for private gain, with clothing, carriages, and residence all exceeding prescribed limits. Xibao prepared to impeach him. Vice Minister Wu Shengqin of Nanhui, a fellow townsman, learned of it and, with Heshen accompanying the Emperor at Rehe, rushed word to him. Heshen ordered Liu Quan to demolish his house and hide every item of clothing, carriage, or horse that exceeded regulations. When Xibao's memorial arrived, the Emperor interrogated Heshen. Later, with no reader vacancy available, he was made principal clerk in the Ministry of Rites instead.

壯圖以母老乞歸。 嘉慶四年，仁宗親政，召詣京師。 壯圖仍以母老乞歸，上賜其母大緞兩端，加壯圖給事中銜，賜奏事摺匣，命得上章言事。 壯圖未行，复上疏請清覈各省陋規，明定科條，上以為不可行。 既歸，疏請拔真才，儲實用，大要謂：「保舉未定處分，當下吏部嚴立科條； 科埸或通關節，當將房考落卷送主司搜閱。 其尤要者，謂六部滿洲司員案，文義多未曉暢，當嚴督令習經書通文理； 鄉會試加廣名額，司員先侭科甲挑補。 」下軍機大臣議，奏謂惟房考落卷送主司搜閱，事近可行，補入科場條例。
Zhuangtu asked to retire to care for his elderly mother. In 1799, when the Jiaqing Emperor took direct rule, Zhuangtu was summoned to Beijing. Zhuangtu again pleaded his elderly mother; the Emperor gave his mother two bolts of fine satin, granted Zhuangtu the rank of supervising secretary, and presented him with a memorial case so he could continue to report to the throne. Before leaving, Zhuangtu again memorialized to abolish illicit provincial fees and define clear rules; the Emperor rejected the proposal. After returning home he memorialized on recruiting talent, arguing in essence: "Rules for recommendation and punishment should be set by the Ministry of Personnel; When exam fraud is suspected, failed papers from room examiners should be forwarded to the chief examiner for review. Most urgently, Manchu clerks in the Six Ministries often lack literary competence and should be required to master the classics; Expand examination quotas and fill clerkships preferentially with degree-holders. The Grand Council considered the proposals and approved only forwarding failed examination papers to chief examiners, which was incorporated into examination regulations.
21
雲南巡撫初彭齡乞養歸，壯圖疏請留，上不允。 別疏復申前議，謂滿洲子弟十五六歲前專責習經書通文理，再習騎射繙譯。 上謂：「壯圖以前嘗駁飭之事復行瀆陳，更張本朝成法。 下雲南巡撫伊桑阿傳旨申飭。 」八年，疏言：「天下萬幾，皆皇上獨理。 內外諸臣不過浮沉旅進旅退之中，無能匡扶弼亮。 請於內之卿貳、翰詹、科道，外之籓、臬、道、府，慎選二十人，輪直內廷。 每日奏章諭旨，盡心檢校，有疏忽偏倚之處，許就近詳辨可否。 」上責：「壯圖言皆迂闊紕謬，斷不可行。 若如所奏，直於軍機大臣外復設內軍機，成何政體？ 」因及雲南布政使陳孝升、道員薩榮安方以冒銷軍需被罪，令巡撫那彥寶詰壯圖，何無一言奏及。 壯圖言以不得孝升等確據，未敢入告，仍請議處，上命寬之。 十三年，卒。
When Yunnan Governor Chu Pengling asked to retire to care for his parents, Zhuangtu urged that he be retained; the Emperor refused. In another memorial he renewed his earlier proposal that Manchu youths should study the classics before age fifteen or sixteen, then horsemanship, archery, and translation. The Emperor said: "Zhuangtu is harping again on proposals already rejected and trying to overturn established dynastic practice. The rebuke was relayed to Yunnan Governor Yisang'a for transmission to Zhuangtu. In 1803 he memorialized: "The myriad affairs of empire fall entirely on Your Majesty alone. Court officials drift through office unable to support or advise Your Majesty. Select twenty capable officials from among vice ministers, Hanlin scholars, censors, and provincial administrators to serve rotating duty in the inner court. Each day they should review memorials and edicts and discuss on the spot any errors or partiality they find. The Emperor retorted: "Zhuangtu's proposals are impractical and absurd—they cannot be adopted. As he proposes it, you would be creating a second Grand Council inside the palace—what kind of government would that be? He also asked why Zhuangtu had said nothing about Yunnan officials Chen Xiaosheng and Sa Rong'an, who had just been convicted of falsifying military accounts, and ordered Governor Nayenbao to question him. Zhuangtu replied that lacking solid evidence on Chen and the others he had not reported them, but still asked to be punished; the Emperor pardoned him. In 1808 he died.

=【論】=論曰：高宗中年後，遇有言事者，遣大臣按治，輒命其參與。 光鼐既將坐譴，卒得自白，阿桂之賢也。 灃劾國泰發庫藏掩覆，論者謂劉墉密與灃商榷，蓋亦有力焉。 漱芳、錫寶、壯圖皆不能實其言，大臣怙寵亂政，民迫於飢寒，卒成禍亂。 嗚呼，古昔聖王兢兢，重畏民碞，良有以也！
Commentary: After Qianlong's mid-reign, when an official spoke out on a matter the Emperor would send ministers to investigate and usually require the memorialist to join them. Dou Guangnai nearly faced punishment but was finally cleared—credit Agui's integrity. Feng's exposure of Guotai's treasury fraud owed something, commentators say, to Liu Yong's quiet backing. Li Shufang, Cao Xibao, and Yin Zhuangtu could not prove their charges; favored ministers corrupted government while the people suffered hunger and cold, bringing ruin in the end. Alas—the caution of ancient sage-kings toward popular anger was well founded indeed!
