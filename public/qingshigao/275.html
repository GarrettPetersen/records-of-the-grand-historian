格爾古德金世德趙士麟郭世隆傅臘塔馬如龍
Ge Ergu De, Jin Shide, Zhao Shilin, Guo Shilong, Fu Lata, and Ma Rulong
3
格爾古德，字宜亭，鈕祜祿氏，滿洲鑲藍旗人。 自筆帖式授內院副理事官。 康熙三年，從定西將軍圖海平湖廣茅麓山李自成餘部。 師還，遷弘文院侍讀，進翰林院侍讀學士，充日講起居注官。 十三年，從安親王岳樂討吳三桂。 三桂將林興珠降，上策請分水師，泊君山，斷常德道； 泊香爐夾扁山，斷長沙、衡州道：則三桂將坐困。 安親王令格爾古德馳奏，並以興珠語聞，上密諭駐岳州諸將議行。 師還，擢詹事，遷內閣學士。
Ge Ergu De, whose courtesy name was Yiting, belonged to the Niohuru clan and served as a Manchu bannerman of the Bordered Blue Banner. He rose from clerk of the brush to become associate director of the Inner Court. In Kangxi year 3 he marched with General Tulai, Fixing the West, to crush Li Zicheng's remaining followers at Maolu Mountain in Huguang. After the campaign he was made a reader in the Hall of Promoting Literature, then promoted to Hanlin reader-in-waiting and appointed daily lecturer and recorder of the emperor's movements. In year 13 he joined Prince An, Yuele, in the campaign against Wu Sangui. After Wu Sangui's general Lin Xingzhu defected, Ge proposed splitting the fleet—one squadron to hold Jun Mountain and sever the road to Changde; another to station at Xianglu Jia Bianshan and block the routes to Changsha and Hengzhou—so that Wu's commanders would be hemmed in without moving. Prince An sent Ge Ergu De to court with a rush memorial and Lin Xingzhu's testimony; the emperor privately told the generals at Yuezhou to discuss the plan and put it into effect. After the troops came home he was made Grand Mentor of the Heir Apparent and then Grand Secretary in the Grand Secretariat.
4
二十一年，授直隸巡撫。 上諭之曰：「金世德、於成龍為巡撫有聲，爾承其後，得名甚難。 若急於求名，或致僨事，爾其懍諸！ 直隸旗下莊頭與民雜處，倚恃聲勢，每為民害。 爾其嚴察懲創，即皇莊亦毋寬宥。 」八旗圈地屬於王公大臣者，輒置莊，設莊頭，主徵租，遂以病民，上深知之，故以諭格爾古德。 格爾古德尋疏言：「自鬻投旗之人，或作姦犯科，冀逃法網； 或遊手好閒，規避差徭。 本主聽其仍居本籍，放債牟利，則諱旗而稱民； 窩逃構訟，遇官長訪聞，又舍民而稱旗。 詐害良善，官不敢問。 應責成本主，止留農戶在莊，餘俱收回服役。 有徇縱者議處。 」下所司飭禁，並諭戶部：「凡鬻身之人，先經犯罪，投旗冀倖免者，與知情之本主，並從重治罪。 」時大學士明珠所屬佐領下人戶指圈民間塚地，民訴於戶部，事下巡撫，令宛平縣察勘。 知縣王養濂言無礙民塚，格爾古德疏劾圈佔塚地屬實，養濂罣吏議。 詔嗣後有如此者，嚴懲不貸。 自康熙初，鼇拜柄政，總督硃昌祚等以圈地獲罪，由是無敢訟言其失者。 至奸民竄入旗下，尋仇傾陷，狡桀莫能製。 獨格爾古德承上指，執法嚴懲，時稱為「鐵面」。
In year 21 he was appointed governor of Zhili. The emperor told him: "Jin Shide and Yu Chenglong made their names as governors of this province. You come after them, and earning a reputation will be no easy thing. If you chase fame too eagerly you may wreck the work—bear that in mind!" In Zhili, banner estate managers live among ordinary subjects and, leaning on their influence, frequently prey on the people. Investigate rigorously and punish offenders—even imperial estates must receive no leniency. " On estates carved from banner allotments for princes and senior ministers, managers were installed to collect rent at will, to the people's hurt. The emperor understood this abuse perfectly, which is why he gave Ge Ergu De these orders. Ge Ergu De soon submitted a memorial: "People who sell themselves into the banners sometimes commit crimes first, hoping to slip past the law; others are loafers scheming to dodge corvée and tax duties. Their masters let them stay in their home districts to lend at usury; then they hide their banner ties and pass as civilians; they shelter fugitives and stir up suits, and when magistrates look into matters they drop the civilian mask and invoke banner privilege. They swindle honest people, and officials dare not press the case. The sponsoring master should bear responsibility; only farming households may remain on the estate, and all others should be recalled to banner service. Anyone who shows favoritism should face disciplinary proceedings. " The memorial was sent to the relevant offices with orders to enforce the ban, and the Board of Revenue was told: "Anyone who sold himself into the banners after committing a crime, hoping for immunity, together with a master who knew of it, shall be punished with added severity. " About then, dependents of an assistant banner captain under Grand Secretary Mingzhu seized and fenced in civilian grave plots. The people appealed to the Board of Revenue; the case went to the governor, who ordered Wanping County to survey the ground. Magistrate Wang Yanglian reported that no civilian graves had been harmed. Ge Ergu De impeached him, proving the seizure of grave land was real, and Yanglian was referred for disciplinary action. An edict declared that anyone who did the like hereafter would be punished severely, without mercy. Since early Kangxi, when Oboi dominated the government, governors-general such as Zhu Changzuo had been punished for protesting land enclosures, and afterward no one dared sue over such wrongs. Wicked men who slipped under the banners pursued vendettas and framed innocents with cunning no one could curb. Only Ge Ergu De, acting on the emperor's intent, enforced the law with relentless severity and was known in his day as "Iron Face."
5
二十三年，上幸五台山，格爾古德迎駕，詢地方賢吏，以靈壽知縣陸隴其對。 尋疏薦井陘道李基和、盧龍知縣衛立鼎與隴其廉能，下部擢用。 頃之，以疾乞休，優詔慰留。 會詔廷臣公舉清廉官，首以格爾古德列奏。 上念其羸疾，遣御醫診視。 未幾，卒，賜卹加等，諡文清。
In year 23 the emperor traveled to Wutai Mountain. Ge Ergu De met the procession, and when asked which local officials were worthy, he named Lu Longqi, magistrate of Lingshou. He soon memorialized recommending Li Jihe of Jingxing Circuit, Wei Liding of Lulong, and Longqi for integrity and competence, and orders went out for their promotion. Before long he asked to retire on grounds of illness; a gracious edict comforted him and urged him to stay. When an edict called on the court to nominate incorrupt officials jointly, Ge Ergu De's name headed the list. Mindful of his frail health, the emperor sent an imperial physician to attend him. He died soon after. The court granted enhanced funeral honors and the posthumous title Wénqīng, "Cultured and Pure."
6
格爾古德清介，布衣蔬食，卻餽遺，纖毫不以自污。 上嘗責漕運總督碩幹居官無狀，碩幹言：「臣為眾所忌，故未能致聲譽。 」上曰：「格爾古德為巡撫，沒後人猶思慕稱頌。 居官苟善，豈有不致聲譽者？ 」為上所重如此。 祀直隸名宦。
Ge Ergu De was austere and upright—plain dress, simple fare, refusing gifts—unwilling to let the slightest stain touch him. The emperor once rebuked Grain Transport Director-General Shuogan for poor conduct in office. Shuogan replied, "I am widely envied, Your Majesty, and so could not win a reputation. " The emperor said, "Ge Ergu De was governor of Zhili, and after his death people still remember and praise him. If you govern well, how could you fail to earn a reputation? " Such was the esteem the emperor held for him. He was enshrined in the hall of famous officials of Zhili.
7
金世德，字孟求，漢軍正黃旗人，兵部侍郎維城子。 淹貫經史，精國書。 以廕生授內院博士，累擢左副都御史。 康熙七年，授直隸巡撫。 是時尚循明製，直隸不置兩司，世德請設守道理錢穀，巡道理刑名，如外省布政、按察二司。 由是始有專司。 畿北諸郡，旗、民雜處，易於容姦，請立屯長以治之。 唐縣等三十七州縣，田一千六百餘頃，河流沙擁，民不能耕。 歲輸銀二萬有奇、穀豆三百九十石，歷年責原戶納賦為民害，世德為奏請除額。 地震通州等九州縣，復請賑恤，並蠲免錢糧。 皆如所請行。 師南征，供億繁急。 世德單騎行營中，躬料芻糗，軍無橫索，吏無侵漁，市肆晏然。 十九年，卒，諡清惠。
Jin Shide, courtesy name Mengqiu, was a Han bannerman of the Plain Yellow Banner and the son of Vice Minister of War Weicheng. He was deeply versed in the classics and histories and expert in Manchu script. Through hereditary privilege he entered the Inner Court as a doctor and rose step by step to Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. In Kangxi year 7 he was appointed governor of Zhili. The province still followed the Ming arrangement without the usual dual commissions. Shide asked that the prefect handle grain and revenue and the intendant handle criminal matters, as in other provinces' administration and surveillance commissions. Dedicated offices for these duties were established from that time. In the districts north of the capital, bannermen and civilians lived side by side, which made crime easy to hide; he asked that village headmen be appointed to keep order. In thirty-seven prefectures and counties including Tang, more than 1,600 qing of farmland lay buried under river sand, leaving farmers unable to till the soil. The land still owed more than twenty thousand taels of silver and 390 shi of grain and beans yearly, and for years the original households had been forced to pay—a burden Shide asked the throne to lift entirely. Earthquakes struck nine prefectures and counties including Tongzhou; he again sought relief funds and tax exemptions. The court approved every request. When the southern campaign began, supply demands grew urgent and heavy. Shide rode alone through the camps, personally checking fodder and rations. Troops made no arbitrary levies, clerks extorted nothing, and the markets stayed calm. He died in year 19 and received the posthumous title Qīnghuì, "Pure and Kind."
8
趙士麟，字麟伯，雲南河陽人。 康熙三年進士，授貴州平遠推官。 改直隸容城知縣，緝盜衛民，創正學書院，與諸生講學。 行取，授吏部主事。 歷郎中，擢光祿寺少卿，三遷至左副都御史。 疏請台灣改郡縣比內地，設總兵鎮守，省沿海之戍卒，詔報可。
Zhao Shilin, whose courtesy name was Linbo, came from Heyang in Yunnan. He passed the jinshi examination in Kangxi year 3 and was appointed investigating censor at Pingyuan in Guizhou. Transferred to magistrate of Rongcheng in Zhili, he hunted down bandits to protect the people, founded the Zhengxue Academy, and lectured the students there. Selected for service in the capital, he was appointed a principal clerk in the Ministry of Personnel. He rose through bureau director to vice minister of the Court of Imperial Entertainments and, after three further promotions, to Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. He memorialized to reorganize Taiwan into prefectures and counties on the interior model, post a regional commander for defense, and cut coastal garrison troops. The throne approved.
9
二十三年，授浙江巡撫。 杭州民貸於駐防旗兵，名為「印子錢」，取息重，至鬻妻孥賣田舍； 不償，則閧於官。 營兵馬化龍毆官，成大獄。 士麟移會將軍掣繳券約，捐資代償。 將軍令減子歸母，母复減十之六。 事遂解，民大稱頌。 詔裁浙江總督，總督駐衢州，督標兵三千被汰，乏食譁掠，民罷市。 士麟仍濟以餉，因奏設副將一，定額兵八百餘，留撥各營缺額。 眾乃定。 浙中豪右衙蠹，驕悍不法，為民害。 士麟廉得其狀，悉置之法，強暴斂跡。 省城河道久淤，督役疏濬，半載訖工，民以為便。 复繕城隍，修學校，親蒞書院，與諸生講論經史及濂、洛、關、閩之學，士風大振。 禁革規費，積弊一清。 二十五年，移撫江蘇。 浙人懷之，繪圖以志去思，並於西湖敬一書院肖像祀之。 尋召為兵部督捕侍郎，調吏部，皆能舉其職。 三十七年，卒。 祀浙江名宦。
In year 23 he was appointed governor of Zhejiang. Hangzhou residents borrowed from garrison banner troops in loans called "seal-money," at crushing interest, until families sold wives, children, homes, and fields; and when they could not repay, they stormed the magistrate's office. A garrison soldier named Ma Hualong assaulted an official, and the affair ballooned into a major case. Shilin wrote the regional general to seize and cancel the loan contracts and contributed his own funds to settle the debts for the people. The general ordered principal and interest cut; the principal was then reduced by another sixty percent. The crisis ended, and the people praised him widely. An edict abolished the Zhejiang governor-generalship. The former governor-general remained at Quzhou, and three thousand of his personal troops were disbanded. Short of rations, they rioted and looted, and townspeople shut their shops. Shilin still fed them from provincial funds and memorialized to create one deputy regional command with a fixed quota of more than eight hundred men, drawing on vacancies across the camps. Order was restored. In Zhejiang, powerful families and yamen parasites were arrogant, violent, and lawless—a constant plague on the people. Shilin investigated thoroughly, punished every offender by law, and the bullies vanished from sight. The capital's canals had silted up for years. He supervised dredging, finished in half a year, and the people welcomed the relief. He repaired the walls and moat, restored the schools, and lectured in person at the academy on the classics and on the traditions of Zhou Dunyi, the Cheng brothers, Zhang Zai, and the Fujian school. Scholarly morale revived sharply. He banned customary surcharges, and entrenched abuses were swept away. In year 25 he was transferred to governor of Jiangsu. The people of Zhejiang missed him, commissioned paintings to remember his departure, and enshrined his portrait for worship at the Jingyi Academy beside West Lake. He was soon summoned as vice minister of war in charge of bandit suppression, then moved to the Ministry of Personnel, and discharged both offices capably. He died in year 37. He was enshrined in the hall of famous officials of Zhejiang.
10
士麟潛心正學，以硃子為歸。 躬行實踐，施於政事，士愨民恬，所至皆有聲績。
Shilin devoted himself to orthodox learning and took Zhu Xi as his standard. He lived what he taught and applied it in office; scholars grew steady and the people calm, and wherever he served he left a celebrated record.
11
郭世隆，字昌伯，漢軍鑲紅旗人。 父洪臣，原籍汾州。 順治二年，英親王阿濟格下九江，洪臣隨明將左夢庚來降，入旗，授佐領，分轄降眾。 累官湖廣道州總兵。 康熙四年，世隆襲管佐領，授禮部員外郎，改御史。 二十七年，盛京福陵守兵訴其兄冤死，命世隆往按，得誣良刑偪自縊狀，原審侍郎阿禮瑚等坐失實奪官。 頃之，超擢內閣學士。 聖祖謁孝陵，經通州，山西禮縣民訴知縣萬世緯及知府紀元婪索狀，命世隆會督撫按治。 世緯坐貪婪、科派、杖斃無罪人，元坐受賕薦世緯卓異，皆論死。
Guo Shilong, whose courtesy name was Changbo, was a Han bannerman of the Bordered Red Banner. His father Hongchen was originally from Fenzhou. In Shunzhi year 2, Prince Ying, Ajige, marched on Jiujiang. Hongchen came over with the Ming general Zuo Menggeng, entered the banners as an assistant banner captain, and took charge of the surrendered troops. He rose through the ranks to regional commander of Daozhou in Huguang. In Kangxi year 4, Shilong inherited his father's assistant banner captaincy, was appointed a vice director in the Ministry of Rites, and then became a censor. In year 27, guards at the Fuling Mausoleum in Mukden claimed an elder brother had been wrongfully killed. Shilong was sent to investigate and found an innocent man tortured into suicide. Vice Minister Alihu and the original reviewers were punished for false findings and stripped of office. Soon afterward he was promoted out of turn to grand secretary in the Grand Secretariat. The emperor visited the Xiaoling Mausoleum and passed through Tongzhou. People of Lixian in Shanxi accused Magistrate Wan Shiwei and Prefect Ji Yuan of extortion. Shilong was ordered to join the governor-general and governor in the investigation. Shiwei was found guilty of greed, illegal levies, and beating an innocent man to death. Yuan was convicted of taking bribes and recommending Shiwei as an outstanding official. Both were sentenced to death.
12
二十九年，代於成龍為直隸巡撫。 先是，罷任安溪知縣孫鏞告福建巡撫張仲舉、布政使張永茂侵蝕庫帑，遣郎中吳爾泰會總督勘訊，至即拘訊知府六人，連引州縣官數十人。 上聞疑之，命世隆往按，發仲舉與前布政使張汧竄改賦冊、侵隱已徵額銀捏作民欠，又汧遷湖廣巡撫虧福建庫帑三十餘萬，仲舉前任湖南布政亦虧帑，相約互抵； 嗣仲舉聞汧以贓敗，而福建庫尚未完，飭屬代為彌縫，左證悉合。 仲舉、永茂俱論罪如律。
In year 29 he succeeded Yu Chenglong as governor of Zhili. Earlier, dismissed Anxi magistrate Sun Yong had accused Fujian Governor Zhang Zhongju and Administration Commissioner Zhang Yongmao of embezzling provincial funds. Director Wu Ertai was sent with the governor-general to investigate; on arrival he immediately detained six prefects and dragged in dozens of county and subprefectural officials. The emperor grew suspicious and sent Shilong to investigate. He found Zhongju and former administration commissioner Zhang Qian had altered tax registers, hid collected revenues, and invented civilian arrears. Zhang Qian, now governor of Huguang, had left Fujian's treasury short by more than 300,000 taels; Zhongju had done the same in Hunan. The two had agreed to cover for each other. Later, when Zhongju learned Qian had fallen on corruption charges while Fujian's books were still short, he ordered subordinates to paper over the gap. Every piece of evidence lined up. Zhongju and Yongmao were both sentenced under the law.
13
世隆之任，帝諭曰：「於成龍居官甚善，繼之不易，爾當勤慎任事。 」順天、保定、真定、永平諸府旱，世隆奉命履勘，疏言：「被災者七十四州縣，請蠲本年及來年額賦。 霸、文安等十四州縣災尤重，請治賑。 」迭疏籌積貯，並以奉天歲豐，請飭山海關暫聽民間轉糶，仍限肩挑馱負，不得以大車裝載，皆如所請。 又疏言：「真定地當衝要，所屬贊皇縣，西有大峪曰子午套，素為盜藪，請移紫荊關副將駐真定； 調馬、步兵二千分防霸州。 」子牙河決，淹沒田畝，請修築大城等縣堤岸，並濬王家口、黑龍港諸支流堙塞者，皆報可。
When Shilong took office, the emperor told him: "Yu Chenglong governed this province admirably. Following him will not be easy. Be diligent and careful in your work. " Drought hit Shuntian, Baoding, Zhending, and Yongping. Shilong surveyed the damage on orders from the throne and memorialized: "Seventy-four prefectures and counties were stricken; I ask that this year's and next year's tax quotas be remitted. Fourteen prefectures and counties including Ba and Wen'an were hit hardest; I ask for organized famine relief. " He sent repeated memorials to build grain reserves and, noting Fengtian's good harvest, asked that Shanhaiguan temporarily allow private grain sales, limited to shoulder loads and pack animals but not large carts. The court approved every request. He also memorialized: "Zhending sits on a vital route. West of its Zanhuang County lies the great ravine called Ziwu Tao, long a bandit haunt. I ask that the deputy commander at Zijing Pass be moved to Zhending; and that two thousand cavalry and infantry be redeployed to garrison Bazhou in detachments." " The Ziya River burst its banks and flooded farmland. He asked for dikes in Dacheng and other counties and dredging of blocked tributaries such as Wangjiakou and Heilonggang. The court approved all of it.
14
三十四年，擢閩浙總督。 歲歉，率閉糴居奇。 世隆疏請蠲賦，並發帑二十萬，乞糴江、浙，海運平糶，詔俞之。 先是浙省奏請鼓鑄，官吏射利，請減其分數。 由是私鑄者眾，每錢不及七八分，壅滯不行。 三十八年，上南巡，世隆迎駕，至杭州，民擁輿赴訴。 乃停官爐，發帑收毀私錢，錢得流布。 上聞，為褒美。 鄞縣沿海田，被水沖決一千七十餘畝，請永免額賦。
In year 34 he was promoted to governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. In lean years, merchants routinely hoarded grain and waited for prices to spike. Shilong memorialized for tax relief, drew 200,000 taels from the treasury, and asked to buy grain in Jiangsu and Zhejiang for sale at fair prices by sea. The throne approved. Earlier, Zhejiang had asked permission to mint coin. Officials chasing profit had the alloy content reduced. Private counterfeiting spread, each coin worth less than seven or eight fen, and money stopped circulating. In year 38 the emperor toured the south. Shilong met the procession, and at Hangzhou crowds surrounded his sedan chair to plead their case. He shut the government mint, used treasury funds to buy up and destroy private coin, and currency began to circulate again. When the emperor heard, he praised Shilong warmly. More than 1,070 mu of coastal farmland in Yin County had been washed away; he asked for permanent exemption of the tax quota.
15
四十一年，調兩廣總督。 廣東海疆二千餘裡，守汛遼闊，盜賊出沒無常。 世隆疏定營製，增設兵船巡哨，迭擊敗海盜，沉其舟四十五。 疏報擒海陽巨盜蔡玉也等五人。 上遣刑部侍郎常綬往勘，因議世隆平時禁賊不嚴，盜發，朦朧掩飾，坐奪官。
In year 41 he was transferred to governor-general of Guangdong and Guangxi. Guangdong's coast stretched more than two thousand li, with garrisons far apart and pirates appearing and vanishing unpredictably. Shilong reorganized the coastal camps, added patrol warships, repeatedly defeated pirates, and sank forty-five of their boats. He reported the capture of the great Haiyang pirate Cai Yuye and four accomplices. The emperor sent Vice Minister of Justice Chang Shou to investigate. The finding was that Shilong had not enforced bandit suppression in peacetime and had glossed over outbreaks when they occurred. He was stripped of office.
16
四十六年，起湖廣總督、疏陳防守紅苗，請沿邊安設塘汛，禁內地民與苗往來，並勿與為婚姻。 未幾，召為刑部尚書。 五十年，以山西流匪陳四等潛入湖廣，鳩黨劫掠，世隆前任總督坐失察，奪官。 五十二年，萬壽，復原品。 居三年，卒。 直隸、福建、浙江、兩廣、湖廣皆祀名宦。
In year 46 he was recalled as governor-general of Huguang and memorialized on defending the Red Miao, asking for border guard posts, a ban on trade between interior subjects and the Miao, and a ban on intermarriage. Soon afterward he was summoned to serve as minister of justice. In year 50, Shanxi bandits led by Chen Si slipped into Huguang, gathered followers, and raided the countryside. Shilong, as the former governor-general, was punished for failure to detect the threat and stripped of office. In year 52, on the emperor's birthday celebration, his original rank was restored. Three years later he died. He was enshrined in the halls of famous officials in Zhili, Fujian, Zhejiang, the Two Guangs, and Huguang.
17
傅臘塔，伊爾根覺羅氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人。 自筆帖式授內閣中書，遷侍讀。 康熙十九年，授山東道御史，有聲台中。 二十五年，出為陝西布政使。 二十六年，擢左副都御史，遷工部侍郎。 二十七年，偕侍郎多奇往雲南按提督萬正色與總兵王珍互訐事。 讞實，正色、珍俱論罪有差。 調吏部，授兩江總督。 陛辭，上諭曰：「爾當潔己奉公，督兩江無如於成龍者，爾效之可矣！ 」傅臘塔至官，清弊政，斥貪墨，讞獄尤明慎。 贛縣民訴知縣劉瀚芳私徵銀米十餘萬，並蠹役不法。 傅臘塔因劾布政使多弘安、按察使吳延貴、贛南道鍾有德於吏役婪贓不速勘，復從輕擬，曲為庇護，弘安、延貴、有德並坐罷。
Fu Lata, of the Irgen Gioro clan, was a Manchu bannerman of the Bordered Yellow Banner. He rose from clerk of the brush to secretary in the Grand Secretariat and then to reader. In Kangxi year 19 he was appointed censor of the Shandong circuit and made a name for himself in the Censorate. In year 25 he was sent out as administration commissioner of Shaanxi. In year 26 he was promoted to Left Vice Censor-in-Chief and then to vice minister of works. In year 27 he went to Yunnan with Vice Minister Duoqi to investigate mutual accusations between Regional Commander Wan Zhengse and Regional Commander Wang Zhen. The charges were substantiated, and both men were sentenced, though to different penalties. After a stint in the Ministry of Personnel, he was appointed governor-general of the Two Jiangs. At his farewell audience the emperor told him: "Keep yourself pure and serve the public faithfully. Among governors of the Two Jiangs, none has matched Yu Chenglong. Take him as your model!" " Once in office, Fu Lata swept away abusive practices, expelled the corrupt, and was especially clear and careful in deciding cases. People of Gan County accused Magistrate Liu Hanfang of privately levying more than 100,000 taels' worth of silver and grain, with corrupt clerks acting outside the law. Fu Lata impeached Administration Commissioner Duo Hong'an, Surveillance Commissioner Wu Yangu, and Gannan Circuit Intendant Zhong Youde for failing to investigate clerks' corruption promptly, then recommending light sentences and shielding the guilty. All three were dismissed.
18
二十八年，上南巡，閱運河，命傅臘塔會河道總督王新命勘儀真河閘。 疏言：「閘外為北新洲，北新洲外又有漲沙平鋪江中。 應疏北新洲支河，直通四閘。 糧艘循漲沙尾入新河口，可以通行。 」別疏言：「江寧廛稅累民，內輸房稅，外輸廊鈔，更外輸棚租，請予蠲免。 」皆如所請。 二十九年，淮、徐飢，發常平倉穀賑恤，災民賴焉。 蘆洲丈量，例委佐貳，民苦需索。 傅臘塔定五年一行，悉以印官理其事。 歷年逋賦，量為帶徵，由是積困頓甦。 是年，監臨江南鄉試，疏稱士子應試者萬有餘人，請廣科舉額，下部議，增廣額四十名。 疏劾大學士徐元文、原任尚書徐乾學縱子弟招權罔利，巡撫洪之傑徇私袒庇。 詔毋深究，予元文休致。 沭陽民周廷鑑叩閽訟降調侍郎胡簡敬居鄉不法，並及之傑瞻徇狀，命傅臘塔按治，得實，簡敬及其子弟並治罪，之傑奪官。
In year 28 the emperor toured south, inspected the Grand Canal, and ordered Fu Lata to join Canal Director-General Wang Xinming in surveying the Yizhen river sluice. He memorialized: "Beyond the sluice lies Beixinzhou, and beyond that shifting sand lies flat across the river channel. The Beixinzhou branch should be dredged to connect directly with the four sluice gates. Grain barges could follow the edge of the sandbar into the new river mouth and pass through safely. " In a separate memorial he wrote: "Jiangning's market taxes crush the people—shop tax within, gallery dues without, and shed rent on top. I ask that all be remitted. " The court approved every request. In year 29 famine struck Huai and Xu. He released grain from the ever-normal granaries for relief, and the stricken population depended on it. Land surveys on Luzhou were routinely handed to deputy officials, and the people suffered endless extortion. Fu Lata set surveys for once every five years and required sealed officials to handle them directly. Years of tax arrears were collected in measured installments, and long-standing hardship eased. That year, while supervising the Jiangnan provincial examinations, he reported more than ten thousand candidates and asked to expand the quota. After deliberation, forty additional places were granted. He impeached Grand Secretary Xu Yuanwen and former Minister Xu Qianxue for letting their sons and brothers trade on their power for profit, and Governor Hong Zhijie for shielding them out of favoritism. An edict ordered no further investigation and allowed Yuanwen to retire. Zhou Tingjian of Shuyang appealed at the palace gate against demoted Vice Minister Hu Jianjing for misconduct at home and accused Hong Zhijie of covering for him. Fu Lata investigated, confirmed the charges, punished Jianjing and his sons, and stripped Zhijie of office.
19
三十二年，廣東巡撫江有良與巡鹽太常少卿沙拜互訐。 傅臘塔往按，有良、沙拜並坐受賕，奪官。 三十三年，疏言：「淮、揚所屬多版荒，巡撫宋犖曾請緩徵，格於部議。 臣履畝詳勘，鹽城、高郵等州縣因遇水災，業戶逃亡者眾。 今田有涸出之名，人無耕種之實，小民積困。 熟田額糧尚多懸欠，何能代償盈萬之荒賦？ 請恩賜蠲除，庶逃戶懷歸，安居樂業。 」疏入，下部議，不許，上特命免徵。 旋卒於官。 上聞，諭廷臣曰：「傅臘塔和而不流，不畏權勢，愛惜軍民。 兩江總督居官善者，於成龍而後，惟傅臘塔。 」遣太僕寺卿楊舒赴江寧致祭，贈太子太保，諡清端，予騎都尉世職。 士民懷之，為建祠江寧。 四十四年，上南巡，經雨花台，賜祠額曰「兩江遺愛」。 雍正中，入祀賢良祠。
In year 32, Guangdong Governor Jiang Youliang and salt-inspecting Vice Minister of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices Shabei filed mutual accusations. Fu Lata investigated on site. Both Youliang and Shabei were convicted of taking bribes and dismissed. In year 33 he memorialized: "Many registered fields under Huai and Yang are wasteland. Governor Song Luo once asked for deferred collection, but the ministries refused. I have surveyed the fields in person. In Yancheng, Gaoyou, and other counties, floods have driven many landowners to flee. The fields exist on paper as reclaimed land, but no one actually farms them, and ordinary people remain trapped in hardship. Taxes on cultivated land are still in arrears. How can farmers also pay tens of thousands in levies on wasteland? I ask that these levies be remitted by imperial grace, so fugitive households may return home and live in peace. " The ministries deliberated and refused. The emperor personally ordered the levies remitted. He died in office soon afterward. When the emperor heard, he told the court: "Fu Lata was conciliatory but never unprincipled, unafraid of power, and devoted to soldiers and civilians alike. Among governors-general of the Two Jiangs who governed well, after Yu Chenglong there was only Fu Lata." " He sent Minister of the Imperial Stud Yang Shu to Jiangning to perform the rites, posthumously granted him Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, the title Qīngduān, "Pure and Upright," and a hereditary commandant of cavalry rank. Scholars and commoners cherished his memory and built a shrine to him in Jiangning. In year 44, on a southern tour, the emperor passed Yuhuatai and bestowed on the shrine the plaque "Enduring Grace of the Two Jiangs." In the Yongzheng reign he was enshrined in the Shrine of Worthy Officials.
20
馬如龍，字見五，陝西綏德州人。 康熙十一年舉人。 十四年，陝西提督王輔臣據寧羌叛，其黨硃龍寇綏德，陷之。 如龍糾鄉勇倚山立寨，寇至，屢擊卻之。 輔臣誘以偽劄，斬其使。 會平逆將軍畢力克圖兵至，如龍渡河迎，呈偽劄，並陳賊虛實，因率所部為前鋒，克綏德。 畢力克圖以聞，即便宜令攝州事。 總督哈佔亦疏言如龍倡義拒賊狀，請優敘。
Ma Rulong, whose courtesy name was Jianwu, came from Suide Prefecture in Shaanxi. He passed the provincial examination in Kangxi year 11. In year 14, Shaanxi Regional Commander Wang Fuchen rebelled and seized Ningqiang. His follower Zhu Long raided Suide and captured it. Rulong rallied local militia, built a mountain stockade, and repeatedly beat back raiding bands. Fuchen tried to win him over with a forged commission. Rulong beheaded the envoy. When Pacify-Rebellion General Biliketu arrived, Rulong crossed the river to meet him, handed over the forged commission, and briefed him on the rebels' real strength. He then led his men as vanguard and retook Suide. Biliketu reported the feat and, acting on discretionary authority, put Rulong in charge of the prefecture. Governor-General Hazhan also memorialized on Rulong's leadership in resisting the rebels and asked for special recognition.
21
十六年，授直隸灤州知州。 州民猾而多盜，如龍鋤暴安良，豪右斂跡。 州有民殺人而埋其屍，四十年矣； 如龍宿逆旅，得白骨，問之，曰：「此屋十易主矣。 」縶最初一人至，鉤其情得實，置諸法。 昌平有殺人獄不得其主名，使如龍按之。 閱狀，則民父子殺於僧寺，並及僧五，而民居旁二姓皆與民有連，問之，謝不知。 使蹟之，二人相與語曰：「孰謂馬公察，易欺耳。 」執訊之，乃服。 自是民頌如龍能折獄。 十九年，以察出民間隱地，敘勞，入為戶部員外郎，歷刑部郎中，榷浙江北新關稅務。
In year 16 he was appointed prefect of Luanzhou in Zhili. The people were cunning and banditry common. Rulong suppressed the violent and protected the law-abiding, and local bullies vanished from sight. A murder had been committed in the prefecture and the body buried forty years earlier; Rulong stayed at a roadside inn, found human bones, and asked about them. He was told, "This house has had ten owners. " He arrested the very first owner, drew out a confession, and punished him by law. In Changping a murder case had gone unsolved; Rulong was sent to investigate. The file showed a father and son killed in a Buddhist temple along with five monks. Two neighboring clans were tied to the victims by marriage, but when questioned they claimed ignorance. He had them watched. The two muttered to each other, "Who says Prefect Ma is sharp? He's easy to fool. " He arrested and interrogated them, and they confessed. From then on the people praised Rulong for his skill in deciding cases. In year 19 his merit was recorded for uncovering concealed private land. He entered the capital as a vice director in the Ministry of Revenue, became a bureau director in the Ministry of Justice, and supervised customs at Beixin Pass in Zhejiang.
22
二十四年，遷杭州知府。 杭州民貸於旗營，息重不能償，質及子女。 如龍請於將軍，覈子母，以公使錢代償。 杭州民咸頌如龍。 二十八年，上南巡，聞其治行，超擢按察使。 平反庶獄，多所全活。 海賊楊士玉竄跡島嶼，勾土賊胡茂等剽掠商船，如龍設策擒之，盡殲其首從，巡撫張鵬翮以聞。 二十九年，遷布政使，屬吏有歲餽，悉禁絕之。 是年，紹興大水，庫儲絀，無可救濟。 如龍檄十一郡合輸米二萬餘石，按戶賑給，告屬吏曰：「是逾於歲餽多矣，」
In year 24 he was made prefect of Hangzhou. Hangzhou residents borrowed from the banner garrison at crushing interest. Unable to repay, they pledged even wives and children. Rulong asked the regional general to audit principal and interest and used public funds to settle the debts for the people. The people of Hangzhou praised him everywhere. In year 28 the emperor toured the south, heard of his record in office, and promoted him out of turn to surveillance commissioner. He overturned wrongful convictions and saved many lives. The pirate Yang Shiyu hid on offshore islands and joined local bandits such as Hu Mao in raiding merchant ships. Rulong devised a plan to capture them and wiped out the ringleaders and their followers. Governor Zhang Penghe reported the success. In year 29 he became administration commissioner and banned the yearly gifts his subordinates had been accustomed to offer. That year Shaoxing was hit by severe floods. Provincial stores were empty and there was nothing for relief. Rulong ordered eleven prefectures to contribute more than 20,000 shi of grain, distributed relief household by household, and told his subordinates, "That is far more than your yearly gifts combined,"
23
三十一年，授江西巡撫。 整飭常平倉，春以羨米出貸，秋收還倉。 飭州縣廣積儲，備兇荒。 仿白鹿洞遺法，建書院以教士。 嚴溺女之禁。 疏請罷追轉漕腳耗。 三十八年，入覲，賜御書「老成清望」榜。 時淮、揚薦飢，如龍以江西連歲豐稔，率僚屬捐米十萬賑之。 以老病累疏乞休，詔輒慰留。 四十年，卒，賜祭葬。
In year 31 he was appointed governor of Jiangxi. He reorganized the ever-normal granaries so surplus grain was lent out in spring and returned after the autumn harvest. He ordered prefectures and counties to build up grain reserves against famine. Following the Bailudong model, he founded an academy to educate scholars. He strictly enforced the ban on drowning infant girls. He memorialized to end the pursuit of transport surcharges on transferred grain tribute. In year 38 he attended court and received an imperial inscription: the plaque "Veteran Integrity and Clear Repute." Huai and Yang were stricken by successive famine. Because Jiangxi had enjoyed good harvests for years, Rulong led his staff in donating 100,000 shi of grain for relief. Citing age and illness, he repeatedly asked to retire, and each time the emperor comforted him and urged him to stay. He died in year 40, and the court granted him state sacrificial rites and funeral honors.
24
論曰：守成世為大臣者，以仁心行仁政，培養元氣，其先務也。 兵革初息，瘡痍未復，格爾古德等任封疆之重，拊循安輯，與民休息，政績卓卓在耳目。 廷褒老成，野留遺愛，有以哉！
The commentary says: In an age devoted to preserving the dynasty's legacy, great ministers should govern with humane hearts and humane policies, nurturing the realm's vital energies—that must come first. When the wars had only just ended and the land was still scarred, Ge Ergu De and his peers bore heavy frontier responsibilities, soothed and settled the people, let them recover, and left achievements plain for all to see. The court praised their seasoned integrity, and the countryside kept their memory with affection—small wonder!
