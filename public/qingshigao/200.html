咸豐元年辛亥五月甲辰暫署。乙巳陝甘總督。閏八月辛亥召。署。十月癸卯卒。補。五月乙巳遷。閩浙總督。九月乙丑病假。兼署閩浙總督。 First year of Xianfeng (xinhai) On the jiachen day of the fifth month he served temporarily; on yiyi he became Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu; on the xinhai day of the intercalary eighth month he was summoned to court and continued in an acting capacity; on the guimao day of the tenth month he died, and the post was then filled. On the yisi day of the fifth month he was transferred to Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang; on the yichou day of the ninth month he took medical leave while also serving concurrently as acting Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang.

咸豐二年壬子七月壬申召。兼署。十二月辛巳，遷。署四川總督。七月己酉假。兼署。十月壬辰免。閩浙總督。九月己酉革。署。十二月辛丑革，逮。署湖廣總督。七月壬申赴湘會剿。兩廣總督。七月壬辰遷。雲貴總督。六月戊戌降。任。署。十二月辛巳遷。河東河道總督。 Second year of Xianfeng (renzi) On the renshen day of the seventh month he was summoned to court and also served in a concurrent acting capacity; on the xinsi day of the twelfth month he was transferred to acting Governor-General of Sichuan. On the jiyou day of the seventh month he took leave while also serving concurrently in an acting capacity; on the renchen day of the tenth month he was relieved and became Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang. On the jiyou day of the ninth month he was dismissed and served in an acting capacity; on the xinchou day of the twelfth month he was again dismissed and arrested, then served as acting Governor-General of Huguang. On the renshen day of the seventh month he went to Hunan to join the joint suppression campaign and became Governor-General of Guangdong and Guangxi. On the renchen day of the seventh month he was transferred to Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou. On the wuxu day of the sixth month he was demoted, then took office in an acting capacity; on the xinsi day of the twelfth month he was transferred to Governor-General of the Hedong River Course.

咸豐三年癸丑九月丙午革。直隸總督。正月壬申被劾。署。二月壬辰江寧失守，死之。兩江總督。署。五月庚申差。署陝甘總督。八月己卯遷。四川總督。二月己亥，兼署。六月辛巳兼署。八月己卯調。閩浙總督。仍署。降調。八月己卯湖廣總督。五月壬子兼署雲貴總督。三月辛未革。任。護。十二月甲午遷。漕運總督。兼署。三月辛未遷。河東河道總督。 Third year of Xianfeng (guichou) On the bingwu day of the ninth month he was dismissed and became Governor-General of Zhili. On the renshen day of the first month he was impeached and served in an acting capacity; on the renchen day of the second month Jiangning was lost and he died in its defense, having been Governor-General of Liangjiang in an acting capacity. On the gengshen day of the fifth month he was dispatched on assignment to serve as acting Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu. On the jimao day of the eighth month he was transferred to Governor-General of Sichuan. On the jihai day of the second month he also served concurrently in an acting capacity; on the xinsi day of the sixth month he again served concurrently as acting governor-general; on the jimao day of the eighth month he was transferred to Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang while still serving in an acting capacity. He was demoted and transferred; on the jimao day of the eighth month he became Governor-General of Huguang. On the renzi day of the fifth month he also served concurrently as acting Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou. On the xinwei day of the third month he was dismissed, then took office and officiated; on the jiawu day of the twelfth month he was transferred to Governor-General of Grain Transport while also serving concurrently in an acting capacity. On the xinwei day of the third month he was transferred to Governor-General of the Hedong River Course.

咸豐四年甲寅九月丁亥撤。四川總督。兼署。正月戊午差。閩浙總督。二月辛巳陣亡。任。六月癸未革。湖廣總督。十一月戊子卒。雲貴總督。 Fourth year of Xianfeng (jiayin) On the dinghai day of the ninth month he was withdrawn from office as Governor-General of Sichuan while also serving concurrently in an acting capacity. On the wuwu day of the first month he was dispatched on assignment as Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang. On the xinsi day of the second month he fell in battle; he then took office, and on the guiwei day of the sixth month he was dismissed as Governor-General of Huguang. On the wuzi day of the eleventh month he died while serving as Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou.

咸豐五年乙卯四月己未革。湖廣總督。五月庚寅卒。河東河道總督。署。 Fifth year of Xianfeng (yimao) On the jiwei day of the fourth month he was dismissed as Governor-General of Huguang. On the gengyin day of the fifth month he died while serving as acting Governor-General of the Hedong River Course.

咸豐六年丙辰十二月己酉遷。署直隸總督。九月壬午病免。陝甘總督。護。八月戊子召。四川總督。署。正月庚申卒。江南河道總督。兼署。 Sixth year of Xianfeng (bingchen) On the jiyou day of the twelfth month he was transferred to acting Governor-General of Zhili. On the renwu day of the ninth month he was relieved due to illness as Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu and officiated in that capacity. On the wuzi day of the eighth month he was summoned to court as acting Governor-General of Sichuan. On the gengshen day of the first month he died while serving as Governor-General of the Jiangnan River Course and also holding a concurrent acting appointment.

咸豐七年丁巳四月癸巳病免。兩江總督。署。六月乙亥遷。四川總督。署。十二月己未革。兩廣總督。署。六月乙亥自盡。雲貴總督。署。 Seventh year of Xianfeng (dingsi) On the guisi day of the fourth month he was relieved due to illness as acting Governor-General of Liangjiang. On the yihai day of the sixth month he was transferred to acting Governor-General of Sichuan. On the jiwei day of the twelfth month he was dismissed as acting Governor-General of Guangdong and Guangxi. On the yihai day of the sixth month he committed suicide while serving as acting Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou.

咸豐八年戊午六月己未革。直隸總督。署。六月戊辰假。署閩浙總督。十一月己亥病免。雲貴總督。 Eighth year of Xianfeng (wuwu) On the jiwei day of the sixth month he was dismissed as acting Governor-General of Zhili. On the wuchen day of the sixth month he took leave while serving as acting Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang. On the jihai day of the eleventh month he was relieved due to illness as Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou.

咸豐九年己未二月壬戌卒。直隸總督。署。十一月丁亥陛見。署陝甘總督。四月壬寅遷。四川總督。署。十月庚子四川總督。四月壬戌病免。閩浙總督。四月壬寅遷。任。署。九月戊寅病免。兩廣總督。四月己未病免。漕運總督。兼署。十月乙卯署。三月己酉卒。河東河道總督。署。 Ninth year of Xianfeng (jiwei) On the renxu day of the second month he died while serving as acting Governor-General of Zhili. On the dinghai day of the eleventh month he had an audience at court while serving as acting Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu. On the renyin day of the fourth month he was transferred to acting Governor-General of Sichuan; on the gengzi day of the tenth month he became Governor-General of Sichuan. On the renxu day of the fourth month he was relieved due to illness as Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang. On the renyin day of the fourth month he was transferred, took office, and served in an acting capacity; on the wuyin day of the ninth month he was relieved due to illness as Governor-General of Guangdong and Guangxi. On the jiwei day of the fourth month he was relieved due to illness as Governor-General of Grain Transport while also serving concurrently in an acting capacity; on the yimao day of the tenth month he again served as acting governor-general. On the jiyou day of the third month he died while serving as acting Governor-General of the Hedong River Course.

咸豐十年庚申，六月庚辰，裁江南河道總督。四月癸未，兩江總督。署。癸巳殉。暫署。八月己卯入援。署陝甘總督。九月辛亥署。十月回任。六月丁亥革。兼署。七月丁未卒。署四川總督。十月庚辰病免。雲貴總督。兼署。庚辰，暫留。閏三月癸亥病免。署漕運總督。五月己亥革。兼署。 In the tenth year of Xianfeng (gengshen), on the gengchen day of the sixth month, the office of Governor-General of the Jiangnan River Course was abolished. On the guiwei day of the fourth month he became acting Governor-General of Liangjiang; on the guisi day he died for the state, after which another officer temporarily acted in the post. On the jimao day of the eighth month he entered the theater to provide relief and served as acting Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu; on the xinhai day of the ninth month he continued in an acting capacity; in the tenth month he returned to his regular post. On the dinghai day of the sixth month he was dismissed while also serving in a concurrent acting capacity; on the dingwei day of the seventh month he died, and another officer became acting Governor-General of Sichuan. On the gengchen day of the tenth month he was relieved due to illness as Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou while also holding a concurrent acting appointment; on that same gengchen day he was temporarily retained in office. On the guihai day of the intercalary third month he was relieved due to illness while serving as acting Governor-General of Grain Transport. On the jihai day of the fifth month he was dismissed while also serving in a concurrent acting capacity.

咸豐十一年辛酉正月丙午病免。直隸總督。七月戊申召。雲貴總督。十一月壬寅革。署。十一月庚戌來京。署漕運總督。 Eleventh year of Xianfeng (xinyou) On the bingwu day of the first month he was relieved due to illness as Governor-General of Zhili. On the wushen day of the seventh month he was summoned to court as Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou; on the renyin day of the eleventh month he was dismissed and served in an acting capacity. On the gengxu day of the eleventh month he came to the capital while serving as acting Governor-General of Grain Transport.

He was dismissed; on the bingshen day of the first month he became Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu; on the wuxu day he died while serving in an acting capacity; on the gengzi day of the seventh month another officer died in office after taking the post and officiating. 同治二年癸亥三月甲子遷。閩浙總督。五月丙寅罷。兩廣總督。殉。四月丁酉雲貴總督。 Second year of Tongzhi (guihai) On the jiazi day of the third month he was transferred to Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang. 同治三年甲子五月乙巳病免。陝甘總督。署。護。七月庚戌遷。河東河道總督。 Third year of Tongzhi (jiazi) On the yisi day of the fifth month he was relieved due to illness as acting Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu and officiated in that capacity. 同治四年乙丑赴山東督師。署兩江總督。假。八月甲辰署四川總督。尋回任。二月丙子罷。署兩廣總督。署。二月丙子遷。漕運總督。四月丁卯留。四月己巳遷。河東河道總督。 Fourth year of Tongzhi (yichou) He went to Shandong to direct the army while serving as acting Governor-General of Liangjiang. He took leave of absence; on the jiachen day of the eighth month he served as acting Governor-General of Sichuan and soon returned to his regular post. On the bingzi day of the second month he was removed from office while serving as acting Governor-General of Guangdong and Guangxi in an acting capacity. On the bingzi day of the second month he was transferred to Governor-General of Grain Transport; on the dingmao day of the fourth month he was retained in office. On the jisi day of the fourth month he was transferred to Governor-General of the Hedong River Course.

同治五年丙寅十一月丙辰剿匪。回兩江總督。八月癸卯罷。陝甘總督。署。八月癸卯遷。閩浙總督。署。十一月丁巳免。署湖廣總督。八月癸卯遷。漕運總督。八月癸卯遷。河東河道總督。 It was the fifth year of Tongzhi, cyclical year bingyin On the bingchen day of the eleventh month he suppressed bandits and returned to his post as Governor-General of Liangjiang. On the dingyou day of the twelfth month he died while serving as acting Governor-General of Sichuan. On the bingyin day of the first month he was removed from office as acting Governor-General of Huguang; on the gengzi day of the twelfth month he was transferred and again served in an acting capacity. On the guichou day of the second month he died while officiating as Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou.

同治七年戊辰七月乙未罷。直隸總督。七月乙未遷。兩江總督。七月乙未遷。閩浙總督。二月癸丑罷。雲貴總督。 It was the seventh year of Tongzhi, cyclical year wuchen On the yiwei day of the seventh month he was removed from office as Governor-General of Zhili. On the bingchen day of the seventh month he had an audience at court while another officer officiated in his place. In the ninth month he was relieved due to illness while serving as acting Governor-General of Guangdong and Guangxi and also holding a concurrent acting appointment.

宣統三年辛亥十二月病免。署。十月民軍入南寧，去職。十二月，署兩江總督。十月民軍據福州，死之。八月民軍據武昌，棄城走。甲寅革。甲子，湖廣總督。九月遷。湖廣總督。署。皆未任。署湖廣總督。三月調。署四川總督。護。十月辛丑民軍據成都，死之。九月民軍據廣州，去職。九月甲戌民軍據雲南，去職。三月病免。東三省總督。 It was the third year of Xuantong, cyclical year xinhai In the twelfth month he was relieved due to illness while serving in an acting capacity. In the tenth month Republican forces entered Nanning and he left office; in the twelfth month another officer served as acting Governor-General of Liangjiang. In the tenth month Republican forces seized Fuzhou and he died there. In the eighth month Republican forces seized Wuchang and he abandoned the city and fled; on the jiayin day he was dismissed; on the jiazi day he was appointed Governor-General of Huguang; in the ninth month he was transferred to Governor-General of Huguang in an acting capacity, but none of the appointees took office, and another officer served as acting Governor-General of Huguang. In the third month he was transferred to acting Governor-General of Sichuan and officiated in that capacity; on the xinchou day of the tenth month Republican forces seized Chengdu and he died there. In the ninth month Republican forces seized Guangzhou and he left office. On the jiaxu day of the ninth month Republican forces seized Yunnan and he left office. In the third month he was relieved due to illness as Governor-General of the Three Eastern Provinces.
