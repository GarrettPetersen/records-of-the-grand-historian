2
刑法一
Penal Law, Part One
3
中國自書契以來，以禮教治天下。 勞之來之而政出焉，匡之直之而刑生焉。 政也，刑也，凡皆以維持禮教於勿替。 故尚書曰：「明於五刑，以弼五教。 」又曰：「士制百姓於刑之中，以教祗德。 」古先哲王，其制刑之精義如此。 周衰禮廢，典籍散失。 魏李悝著法經六篇，流衍至於漢初，蕭何加為九章，歷代頗有增損分合。 至唐永徽律出，始集其成。 雖沿宋迄元、明而面目一變，然科條所布，於扶翼世教之意，未嘗不兢兢焉。 君子上下數千年間，觀其教化之昏明，與夫刑罰之中不中，而盛衰治亂之故，綦可睹矣。
From the time of written contracts onward, China has governed the realm through ritual and moral instruction. By nurturing and summoning the people, government takes shape; by guiding and rectifying them, penal law comes into being. Both government and penal law exist solely to preserve ritual teaching undiminished. Hence the Book of Documents states: 'Be clear in the five punishments, to support the five teachings.' It also says: 'The scholar-official sets the common people within the bounds of punishments, to teach reverent virtue.' Such was the refined meaning with which the ancient sage kings devised penal law. With the decline of the Zhou, ritual collapsed and the canonical texts were dispersed and lost. Li Kui of Wei wrote the six chapters of the Classic of Law; the tradition flowed on to early Han, when Xiao He added to it to form the Nine Chapters, and successive dynasties made many additions, cuts, splits, and consolidations. Not until the Tang Yonghui Code emerged was the body of law first gathered into a finished whole. Though from Song through Yuan and Ming its face changed altogether, the articles issued never ceased to show scrupulous concern for bolstering the moral teaching of the age. Across several millennia, a thoughtful observer can plainly read the reasons for prosperity and decline, order and disorder, in whether moral instruction was enlightened or benighted and whether punishments were measured or excessive.
4
有清起自遼左，不三四十年混一區宇。 聖祖沖年踐阼，與天下休養，六十餘稔，寬恤之詔，歲不絕書。 高宗運際昌明，一代法制，多所裁定。 仁宗以降，事多因循，未遑改作。 綜其終始，列朝刑政，雖不盡清明，然如明代之廠衛、廷杖，專意戮辱士大夫，無有也。 治獄者雖不盡仁恕，然如漢、唐之張湯、趙禹、周興、來俊臣輩，深文慘刻，無有也。 德宗末葉，庚子拳匪之變，創巨痛深，朝野上下，爭言變法，於是新律萌芽。 迨宣統遜位，而中國數千年相傳之刑典俱廢。 是故論有清一代之刑法，亦古今絕續之交也。 爰備志之，俾後有考焉。
The Qing rose from the Liao frontier; in little more than thirty or forty years they united the empire. The Sacred Ancestor came to the throne as a youth and rested the realm; for more than sixty years, edicts of clemency and relief never ceased from the annals. Under the High Ancestor, when the age was flourishing, much of the dynasty's legal order was definitively settled. From Emperor Renzong onward, affairs were largely left to precedent, and there was no opportunity for systematic reform. Viewed from beginning to end, penal policy under successive reigns was not wholly clear, yet nothing comparable to the Ming's Brocade Guard and court bastinado, aimed solely at shaming and destroying the gentry, ever appeared. Judicial officers were not always merciful, yet nothing like the Han and Tang exemplars Zhang Tang, Zhao Yu, Zhou Xing, and Lai Junchen—who deepened the letter of the law with pitiless harshness—ever arose. In the late Guangxu reign, the Boxer catastrophe of 1900 inflicted wounds vast and pain profound; above and below the court all pressed for reform, and the new penal code began to germinate. By the time Xuantong abdicated, China's penal codes, transmitted across millennia, had all been cast aside. Thus to treat Qing penal law as a whole is also to stand at the break between antiquity and the present in legal tradition. Hence it is recorded here in detail, that later ages may have grounds for study.
5
清太祖嗣服之初，始定國政，禁悖亂，戢盜賊，法制以立。 太宗繼武，於天聰七年，遣國舅阿什達爾漢等往外籓蒙古諸國宣布欽定法令，時所謂「盛京定例」是也。 嗣復陸續著有治罪條文，然皆因時立制，不盡垂諸久遠。
When the Qing Taizu first assumed rule, he established state policy, forbade sedition, suppressed brigandage, and thereby laid the foundations of law. Taizong carried on the military founding; in Tiancong 7 he sent the imperial uncle Ashdahan and others to the outer Mongol domains to proclaim the imperially ratified statutes—the so-called 'Statutes Fixed at Shengjing.' Later, provisions on punishment were drafted one after another, but all were expedients for the time and were not fully intended to endure.
6
世祖順治元年，攝政睿親王入關定亂，六月，即令問刑衙門准依明律治罪。 八月，刑科給事中孫襄陳刑法四事，一曰定刑書：「刑之有律，猶物之有規矩準繩也。 今法司所遵及故明律令，科條繁簡，情法輕重，當稽往憲，合時宜，斟酌損益，刊定成書，布告中外，俾知畫一遵守，庶奸慝不形，風俗移易。 」疏上，攝政王諭令法司會同廷臣詳繹明律，參酌時宜，集議允當，以便裁定成書，頒行天下。 十月，世祖入京，即皇帝位。 刑部左侍郎黨崇雅奏，在外官吏，乘茲新制未定，不無憑臆舞文之弊。 並乞暫用明律，候國制畫一，永垂令甲。 得旨：「在外仍照明律行，如有恣意輕重等弊，指參重處。 」二年，命修律官參稽滿、漢條例，分輕重等差，從刑科都給事中李士焜請也。
In Shunzhi 1, the Regent Prince Dorgon entered the passes to quell disorder; in the sixth month he immediately ordered the judicial offices to punish offenses according to the Ming code. In the eighth month, supervising secretary Sun Xiang of the Penal Section memorialized on four points of penal law; the first was to fix the code of punishments: 'For punishments to have laws is like things to have compass and square.' 'What the judicial offices now follow, together with the old Ming statutes, differ in the density of articles and in how circumstance and penalty weigh light or heavy; past codes should be consulted, present needs weighed, additions and subtractions made, a definitive text published and proclaimed at home and abroad, so that all may know one uniform rule to obey—then wickedness will not show itself and customs will shift.' When the memorial was submitted, the regent ordered the judicial offices together with court ministers to expound the Ming code in detail, consult present needs, confer until agreement, and promptly complete a definitive text for promulgation throughout the realm. In the tenth month Shizu entered the capital and took the imperial throne. Left vice minister of Punishments Dang Chongya memorialized that local officials, while the new order was unsettled, were not free of twisting the law to suit whim. He also asked that the Ming code be used provisionally until the national code was unified and could serve as permanent statute. The rescript read: 'Outside the capital, continue to apply the Ming code; if there are abuses such as arbitrary lightness or severity, report them for heavy punishment.' In year 2 he ordered code revisers to collate Manchu and Han regulations, distinguishing grades of severity, at the request of chief supervising secretary of the Penal Section Li Shikun.
7
三年五月，大清律成，世祖御制序文曰：「朕惟太祖、太宗創業東方，民淳法簡，大辟之外，惟有鞭笞。 朕仰荷天休，撫臨中夏，人民既眾，情偽多端。 每遇奏讞，輕重出入，頗煩擬議。 律例未定，有司無所稟承。 爰敕法司官廣集廷議，詳譯明律，參以國制，增損劑量，期於平允。 書成奏進，朕再三覆閱，仍命內院諸臣校訂妥確，乃允刊布，名曰大清律集解附例。 爾內外有司官吏，敬此成憲，勿得任意低昂，務使百官萬民，畏名義而重犯法，冀幾刑措之風，以昭我祖宗好生之德。 子孫臣民，其世世守之。 」十三年，復頒滿文大清律。
In the fifth month of year 3 the Great Qing Code was completed; Shizu wrote a preface stating: 'We reflect that Taizu and Taizong founded the enterprise in the east; the people were plain and the law spare—beyond capital crimes there were only flogging and beating.' 'We have received Heaven's grace and rule the central realm; the people are many and truth and artifice manifold.' 'Whenever cases come for judgment, lightness and severity diverge widely and deliberation is often vexing.' 'With statutes unsettled, the officials have no authority to which they may appeal.' 'Therefore We have charged judicial officers broadly to gather court deliberation, expound the Ming code in detail, consult our national institutions, add, subtract, and balance, aiming at fairness.' 'When the book was completed and presented, We reviewed it repeatedly, still ordered inner-court ministers to collate it until sound, then permitted publication under the title Collected Exegesis of the Great Qing Code with Appended Precedents.' 'You officials within and without, revere this established code; do not arbitrarily raise or lower penalties, so that officials and the myriad people may fear good name and respect the law, hoping thereby for the age when punishments lie unused, to display our ancestors' virtue of cherishing life. Descendants and subjects, guard it generation after generation.' Descendants and subjects, guard it generation after generation. In year 13 the Great Qing Code in Manchu was again promulgated.
8
康熙九年，聖祖命大學士管理刑部尚書事對喀納等將律文復行校正。 十八年，特諭刑部定律之外，所有條例，應去應存，著九卿、詹事、科道會同詳加酌定，確議具奏。 嗣經九卿等遵旨會同更改條例，別自為書，名為現行則例。 二十八年，台臣盛符升以律例須歸一貫，乞重加考定，以垂法守。 特交九卿議，准將現行則例附入大清律條。 隨命大學士圖納、張玉書等為總裁。 諸臣以律文昉自唐律，辭簡義賅，易致舛訛，於每篇正文後增用總注，疏解律義。 次第酌定名例四十六條，三十四年，先行繕呈。 三十六年，發回刑部，命將奏聞後更改之處補入。 至四十六年六月，輯進四十二本，留覽未發。
In Kangxi 9 the Sacred Ancestor ordered grand secretary Duokena, who was managing the Ministry of Punishments, and others again to correct the text of the code. In year 18 he specially decreed that beyond the code itself, all supplementary articles should be kept or struck as appropriate, and ordered the Nine Ministers, Academicians, and Censorate jointly to deliberate in detail and report definitively. Thereafter the Nine Ministers and others, obeying the decree, jointly revised the supplementary articles into a separate book called the Current Precedents in Force. In year 28 censor Sheng Fusheng, holding that statutes and precedents must form one consistent body, begged renewed examination and fixing so that the law might be handed down for observance. The matter was specially referred to the Nine Ministers; they approved attaching the Current Precedents in Force to the articles of the Great Qing Code. Grand secretaries Tuna, Zhang Yushu, and others were at once appointed chief revisers. The ministers noted that the code text derived from the Tang code—concise in wording, compressed in meaning—and easily led to error; therefore after the main text of each section they added general notes explicating the statutes. The General Principles in forty-six articles were settled in sequence; in year 34 they were first copied and presented. In year 36 they were returned to the Ministry of Punishments with orders to insert the changes reported after submission. By the sixth month of year 46, forty-two volumes were compiled and presented, retained for imperial perusal and not yet issued.
9
雍正元年，巡視東城御史湯之旭奏：「律例最關緊要，今六部見行則例，或有從重改輕，從輕擬重，有先行而今停，事同而法異者，未經畫一。 乞簡諳練律例大臣，專掌律例館總裁，將康熙六十一年以前之例並大清會典，逐條互訂，庶免參差。 」世宗允之，命大學士硃軾等為總裁，諭令於應增應減之處，再行詳加分晰，作速修完。 三年書成，五年頒布。 蓋明律以名例居首，其次則分隸於六部，合計三十門，都凡四百六十條。 順治初，釐定律書，將公式門之信牌移入職制，漏泄軍情移入軍政，於公式門刪漏用鈔印，於倉庫門刪鈔法，於詐偽門刪偽造寶鈔。 後又於名例增入邊遠充軍一條。 雍正三年之律，其刪除者：名例律之吏卒犯死罪、殺害軍人、在京犯罪軍民共三條，職制門選用軍職、官吏給由二條，婚姻門之蒙古、色目人婚姻一條，宮衛門之懸帶關防牌面一條。 其併入者：名例之邊遠充軍並於充軍地方，公式門之毀棄制書印信並二條為一，課程門之鹽法並十二條為一，宮衛門之衝突儀仗並三條為一，郵驛門之遞送公文並三條為一。 其改易者：名例之軍官軍人免發遣更為犯罪免發遣，軍官有犯更為軍籍有犯； 儀制門之收藏禁書及私習天文生節為收藏禁書。 其增入者：名例之天文生有犯充軍地方二條。 總計名例律四十六條。 吏律：曰職制十四條，曰公式十四條。 戶律：曰戶役十五條，曰田宅十一條，曰婚姻十七條，曰倉庫二十三條，曰課程八條，曰市廛五條。 禮律：曰祭祀六條，曰儀制二十條。 兵律：曰宮衛十六條，曰軍政二十一條，曰關津七條，曰廄牧十一條，曰郵驛十六條。 刑律：曰賊盜二十八條，曰人命二十條，曰鬥毆二十二條，曰罵詈八條，曰訴訟十二條，曰受贓十一條，曰詐偽十一條，曰犯奸十條，曰雜犯十一條，曰捕亡八條，曰斷獄二十九條。 工律：曰營造九條，曰河防四條。 蓋仍明律三十門，而總為四百三十六條。 律首六贓圖、五刑圖、獄具圖、喪服圖，大都沿明之舊。 納贖諸例圖、徒限內老疾收贖圖、誣輕為重收贖圖，銀數皆從現制。 其律文及律注，頗有增損改易。 律後總注，則康熙年間所創造。 律末並附比引律三十條。 此其大較也。 自時厥後，雖屢經纂修，然僅續增附律之條例，而律文未之或改。 惟乾隆五年，館修奏准芟除總注，並補入過失殺傷收贖一圖而已。
In Yongzheng 1, censor Tang Zhixu memorialized: 'Statutes and precedents are of the utmost importance; the currently enforced precedents of the Six Ministries sometimes change severity from heavy to light or light to heavy, or what was once in force is now suspended, or the case is the same while the law differs—none has been unified.' 'We beg that a minister well versed in statutes and precedents be appointed to head the Code and Precedent Office as chief reviser, and that precedents before Kangxi 61 together with the Great Qing Institutes be collated article by article, so that discrepancies may be avoided.' The Shizong approved and appointed grand secretary Zhu Shi and others as chief revisers, instructing them wherever addition or reduction was needed to analyze in detail again and complete the work swiftly. The book was completed in year 3 and promulgated in year 5. The Ming code placed General Principles first; next it divided sub-statutes under the Six Ministries—thirty sections in all, four hundred sixty articles. In early Shunzhi, when the code was revised, the credentials section of Formulas was moved into Official Institutions, leaking military intelligence into Military Administration; from Formulas the improper use of paper notes was deleted, from Granaries paper currency law, from Fraud counterfeit treasure notes. Later one article on exile to remote frontiers was added to General Principles. In the Yongzheng 3 code, deletions included: from General Principles, clerks and runners committing capital crimes, killing military personnel, and military and civilian crimes in the capital—three articles; from Official Institutions, selecting military offices and officials issuing credentials—two; from Marriage, Mongol and semu marriages—one; from Palace Guard, hanging pass tablets bearing seals—one. Merged: from General Principles, remote exile into places of exile; from Formulas, destroying imperial documents and seals—two into one; from Revenue Duties, salt law—twelve into one; from Palace Guard, rushing imperial escorts—three into one; from Postal Stations, forwarding documents—three into one. Altered: in General Principles, 'military officers and soldiers exempt from exile' became 'criminals exempt from exile,' and 'military officers who commit offenses' became 'offenses by those on the military register'; in Rites, sections on collecting forbidden books and privately studying astronomy were condensed into collecting forbidden books. Added: in General Principles, two articles on astronomers who offend and places of exile. In all, General Principles contain forty-six articles. Officials Code: Official Institutions fourteen articles, Formulas fourteen articles. Household Code: Household Service fifteen, Fields and Dwellings eleven, Marriage seventeen, Granaries twenty-three, Revenue Duties eight, Markets five. Rites Code: Sacrifices six, Ceremonial Regulations twenty. Military Code: Palace Guard sixteen, Military Administration twenty-one, Passes and Barriers seven, Stables and Herds eleven, Postal Stations sixteen. Penal Code: Robbery and Theft twenty-eight, Homicide twenty, Assault and Battery twenty-two, Abuse eight, Litigation twelve, Accepting Bribes eleven, Fraud eleven, Adultery ten, Miscellaneous Offenses eleven, Apprehension of Fugitives eight, Trial and Sentencing twenty-nine. Works Code: Construction nine, River Control four. It still followed the Ming code's thirty sections, but totaled four hundred thirty-six articles. At the head of the code, the six diagrams of bribery grades, five punishments, instruments of punishment, and mourning garments largely followed Ming practice. Diagrams for commutation by payment, for the aged and infirm within penal servitude limits, and for falsely charging a light offense as heavy—all silver amounts followed current regulations. The code text and its annotations had many additions, deletions, and alterations. The general notes after the code were a Kangxi-era innovation. At the end of the code, thirty articles of analogous citation were also appended. Such was the general outline. From that time on, although compilation and revision occurred repeatedly, only supplementary precedent articles were added; the code text itself was never changed. Only in Qianlong 5 did the revising office memorialize approval to remove the general notes and add one diagram on commutation for negligent killing and wounding—that alone.
10
例文自康熙初年僅存三百二十一條，末年增一百一十五條。 雍正三年，分別訂定，曰原例，累朝舊例凡三百二十一條； 曰增例，康熙間現行例凡二百九十條； 曰欽定例，上諭及臣工條奏凡二百有四條，總計八百十有五條。 其立法之善者，如犯罪存留養親，推及孀婦獨子； 若毆兄致死，並得准其承祀，恤孤嫠且教孝也。 犯死罪非常赦所不原，察有祖父子孫陣亡，准其優免一次，勸忠也。 枉法贓有祿人八十兩，無祿人及不枉法贓有祿人一百二十兩，俱實絞，嚴貪墨之誅也。 衙蠹索詐，驗贓加等治罪，懲胥役所以保良懦也。 強盜分別法無可貸、情有可原，殲渠魁、赦脅從之義也。 復仇以國法得伸與否為斷，杜兇殘之路也。 凡此諸端，或隱合古義，或矯正前失，皆良法也。 而要皆定制於康、雍時。
Precedent texts from early Kangxi retained only 321 articles; by late Kangxi, 115 were added. In Yongzheng 3 they were separately fixed: Original Precedents, accumulated old precedents of successive reigns—321 articles in all; Added Precedents, current precedents of the Kangxi period—290 articles in all; Imperially Fixed Precedents, imperial edicts and ministerial memorials—204 articles; 815 in all. Among sound legislation: such as allowing those who commit crimes to remain to support parents, extended to widows with only sons; if one beat an elder brother to death, permission was still granted to carry on the sacrifices—cherishing orphans and widows while teaching filial piety. For capital crimes not normally pardoned, if investigation showed grandfather, father, or son had died in battle array, one favorable exemption was granted—encouraging loyalty. For bent-law bribes, salaried officials at 80 taels; unsalaried officials and non-bent-law bribes for salaried officials at 120 taels—all truly strangled, strictly punishing corruption. Yamen parasites extorting by fraud, verified bribes punished by added grades—punishing clerks to protect the weak. For robbery, distinguishing where the law allows no pardon and where circumstance permits mercy—exterminating ringleaders while pardoning the coerced. Vengeance was decided by whether national law could be fulfilled—blocking the path of savage cruelty. In all these points, some tacitly accorded with ancient meaning, some corrected prior errors—all sound law. Yet all were customarily fixed in the Kangxi and Yongzheng periods.
11
又國初以來，凡纂修律例，類必欽命二三大臣為總裁，特開專館。 維時各部院則例陸續成書，苟與刑律相涉，館員俱一一釐正，故鮮乖牾。 自乾隆元年，刑部奏准三年修例一次。 十一年，內閣等衙門議改五年一修。 由是刑部專司其事，不復簡派總裁，律例館亦遂附屬於刑曹，與他部往往不相關會。 高宗臨御六十年，性矜明察，每閱讞牘，必求其情罪曲當，以萬變不齊之情，欲御以萬變不齊之例。 故乾隆一朝纂修八九次，刪原例、增例諸名目，而改變舊例及因案增設者為獨多。
Moreover, from the founding of the state, whenever statutes and precedents were compiled, two or three great ministers were invariably appointed chief revisers and a special office opened. As the various ministries' precedents were completed, if they touched penal law the office members corrected them one by one, so few contradictions arose. From Qianlong 1, the Ministry of Punishments memorialized approval to revise precedents once every three years. In year 11 the inner court and other yamens deliberated changing this to revision once every five years. Thereafter the Ministry of Punishments alone handled the matter, no longer appointing chief revisers; the Code and Precedent Office was also attached to the penal ministry, often without consultation with other ministries. The High Ancestor reigned sixty years; by nature he was strict and keenly observant; whenever he reviewed case records he sought whether circumstance and guilt were properly matched, wishing to govern endlessly varied circumstances with endlessly varied precedents. Hence under Qianlong compilation and revision occurred eight or nine times; labels such as Original and Added Precedents were dropped, while changes to old precedents and case-driven additions were especially numerous.
12
嘉慶以降，按期開館，沿道光、咸豐以迄同治，而條例乃增至一千八百九十有二。 蓋清代定例，一如宋時之編敕，有例不用律，律既多成虛文，而例遂愈滋繁碎。 其間前後牴觸，或律外加重，或因例破律，或一事設一例，或一省一地方專一例，甚且因此例而生彼例，不惟與他部則例參差，即一例分載各門者，亦不無歧異。 展轉糾紛，易滋高下。 雍正十三年，世宗遺詔有曰：「國家刑罰禁令之設，所以詰奸除暴，懲貪黜邪，以端風俗，以肅官方者也。 然寬嚴之用，又必因乎其時。 從前朕見人情淺薄，官吏營私，相習成風，罔知省改，不得不懲治整理，以戒將來。 今人心共知警惕矣，凡各衙門條例，有前嚴而改寬者，此乃從前部臣定議未協，朕與廷臣悉心酌核而後更定，自可垂諸永久。 若前寬而改嚴者，此乃整飭人心風俗之計，原欲暫行於一時，俟諸弊革除，仍可酌復舊章，此朕本意也。 向後遇事斟酌，如有應從舊例者，仍照舊例行。 」惜後世議法諸臣，未盡明世輕世重之故，每屆修例，第將歷奉諭旨及議准臣工條奏節次編入，從未統合全書，逐條釐正。 穆宗號稱中興，母后柄政，削平發、捻、回疆之亂，百端待理，尚於同治九年纂修一次。 德宗幼沖繼統，未遑興作。 兼之時勢多故，章程叢積，刑部既憚其繁猥，不敢議修，群臣亦未有言及者，因循久之。
From Jiaqing onward offices opened on schedule; through Daoguang, Xianfeng, and Tongzhi, precedents rose to 1,892. Qing fixed precedents resembled the Song practice of compiling edicts—precedents displaced the code; as the code largely became dead letter, precedents grew ever more fragmented. Meanwhile contradictions arose back and forth, penalties beyond the code, precedents overriding the code, one affair one precedent, one province or locality its own precedent—even one precedent spawning another; they diverged from other ministries' rules, and even one precedent split across sections could disagree. As disputes multiplied, severity easily varied. In Yongzheng 13, the Shizong's testamentary edict stated: 'The state's penal prohibitions and bans exist to check villainy, remove violence, punish greed, and expel evil, to rectify custom and make officials solemn.' Yet the use of leniency and severity must also follow the times. Formerly, seeing shallow hearts and officials pursuing private gain as ingrained habit, ignorant of reform, We had no choice but to punish and rectify to warn the future. Now all hearts know caution; wherever yamen precedents were strict before and changed to lenient, this was because former ministry deliberation was not harmonious and We with court ministers deliberated carefully before changing—this may endure permanently. Where they were lenient before and changed to strict, this was to rectify hearts and custom, originally intended only for a time; when abuses were removed the old statutes could still be restored—this was Our original intent. Hereafter when affairs are weighed, if the old precedent should apply, still follow the old precedent. Alas later legislators did not fully understand severity in one age and leniency in another; each revision only compiled edicts and memorials in sequence, never unifying the whole book and correcting article by article. The Muzong reign was called restoration; the empress dowager held power; the Taiping, Nian, and border rebellions were suppressed; a hundred matters awaited order—yet in Tongzhi 9 compilation was undertaken once. Dezong succeeded as a youth and had no leisure for new work. Moreover times were troubled and regulations piled up; the Ministry of Punishments feared the mass and dared not propose revision; no minister spoke of it—procrastination lasted long.
13
逮光緒二十六年，聯軍入京，兩宮西狩。 憂時之士，咸謂非取法歐、美，不足以圖強。 於是條陳時事者，頗稍稍議及刑律。 二十八年，直隸總督袁世凱、兩江總督劉坤一、湖廣總督張之洞，會保刑部左侍郎沈家本、出使美國大臣伍廷芳修訂法律，兼取中西。 旨如所請，並諭將一切現行律例，按照通商交涉情形，參酌各國法律，妥為擬議，務期中外通行，有裨治理。 自此而議律者，乃群措意於領事裁判權。
By Guangxu 26 the allied armies entered the capital and the two palaces fled west. Men anxious for the times all said that without taking Europe and America as model, strength could not be attained. Thus memorialists on current affairs began little by little to discuss penal law. In year 28, Zhili governor-general Yuan Shikai, Liangjiang governor-general Liu Kunyi, and Huguang governor-general Zhang Zhidong jointly recommended left vice minister of Punishments Shen Jiaben and minister plenipotentiary to the United States Wu Tingfang to revise the laws, drawing on China and the West. The decree approved as requested and instructed that all current statutes and precedents be drafted with reference to treaty and commercial circumstances, consulting foreign laws, so that Chinese and foreigners might both follow them and governance be aided. From this, those who deliberated on law turned their minds to consular jurisdiction.
14
是年刑部亦奏請開館修例。 三十一年，先將例內今昔情形不同，及例文無關引用，或兩例重複，或舊例停止者，奏准刪除三百四十四條。 三十三年，更命侍郎俞廉三與沈家本俱充修訂法律大臣。 沈家本等乃徵集館員，分科纂輯，並延聘東西各國之博士律師，藉備顧問。 其前數年編纂未竣之舊律，亦特設編案處，歸併分修。 十二月，遵旨議定滿、漢通行刑律，又刪並舊例四十九條。 宣統元年，全書纂成繕進，諭交憲政編查館核議。 二年，覆奏訂定，名為現行刑律。
That year the Ministry of Punishments also memorialized to open an office to revise precedents. In year 31, precedents whose circumstances differed from the past, or were irrelevant, or duplicated, or were suspended, were first memorialized for deletion—344 articles. In year 33, vice minister Yu Liansan was also ordered to serve with Shen Jiaben as revising law ministers. Shen Jiaben and others gathered office members to compile by section and engaged East and West doctors of law as consultants. The old code not yet finished in prior years also had a special drafting office for consolidated division of labor. In the twelfth month, obeying the decree, the universally applied Manchu and Han penal code was deliberated and fixed, and 49 old precedents were again deleted and merged. In Xuantong 1 the whole book was compiled and submitted; it was ordered referred to the Constitutional Compilation Office for deliberation. In year 2 it was reported back as fixed, named the Current Penal Code.
15
時官制改變，立憲詔下，東西洋學說朋興。 律雖仍舊分三十門，而芟削六部之目。 其因時事推移及新章遞嬗而刪者，如名例之犯罪免發遣、軍籍有犯、流囚家屬、流犯在道會赦、天文生有犯、工樂戶及婦人犯罪、充軍地方，職制之大臣專擅選官、文官不許封公侯、官員赴任過限、無故不朝參公座、奸黨，公式之照刷文卷、磨勘卷宗、封掌印信，戶役之丁夫差遣不平、隱蔽差役、逃避差役，田宅之任所置買田宅，婚姻之同姓為婚、良賤為婚姻，課程之監臨勢要中鹽、阻壞鹽法、私礬、舶商匿貨，禮制之朝見留難，宮衛之內府工作人匠替役，軍政之邊境申索軍需、公侯私役官軍、夜禁，關津之私越冒度關津、詐冒給路引、遞送逃軍妻女出城、私出外境及違禁下海、私役弓兵，廄牧之公使人等索借馬匹，郵驛之占宿驛舍上房，賊盜之起除刺字，鬥毆之良賤相毆，訴訟之軍民約會、詞訟誣告、充軍及遷徙，受贓之私受公侯財物，犯奸之良賤相奸，雜犯之搬做雜劇，捕亡之徒流人逃，斷獄之徒囚不應役，營造之有司官吏不住公廨是也。 其緣政體及刑制遷變而改者，如名例之化外人有犯改為蒙古及入國籍人有犯，徒流遷徙地方改為五徒三流二遣地方，婚姻之娶樂人為妻妾改娶娼妓為妻，人命之殺子孫及奴婢圖賴人節去「及奴婢」字，鬥毆之奴婢毆家長改為僱工人毆家長，罵詈之奴婢罵家長改為僱工人罵家長，犯奸之奴婢奸家長妻改為僱工人奸家長妻是也。 綜計全律仍存三百八十有九條，而比引律則刪存及半，依類散入各門，不列比附之目。 舊例除刪並外，合續纂之新例，統一千六十六條。 其督捕則例一書，順治朝命臣工纂進，原為旗下逃奴而設。 康熙十五年重加酌定，乾隆以後續有增入，計條文一百一十，亦經分別去留，附入刑律，而全書悉廢。 律首仍載服制全圖，以重禮教。 是年冬頒行焉。 若蒙古治罪各條，載諸理籓院則例，及西寧番子治罪條例，別行諸岷、洮等處者，以其習俗既殊，刑制亦異，未敢輕議更張。
At that time the official system changed, the constitutional edict was issued, and Eastern and Western learning flourished. Although the code still followed the old thirty sections, the Six Ministries headings were removed. Deleted as times shifted included: from General Principles, criminals exempt from exile, military register offenses, families of exiles, exiles meeting amnesty on the road, offending astronomers, artisans and musicians and women offenders, places of exile; from Official Institutions, ministers monopolizing selection, civil officials enfeoffing as duke or marquis, exceeding post limits, absenting joint audience, factions; from Formulas, brushing copies, examining dossiers, sealing seals; from Household Service, unfair corvée dispatch, concealing corvée, evading corvée; from Fields and Dwellings, purchasing property at one's post; from Marriage, same-surname marriage, free-debased marriage; from Revenue Duties, powerful salt mediation, obstructing salt law, private alum, concealing merchant goods; from Ceremonial Regulations, detaining court audience; from Palace Guard, inner-palace artisan substitution; from Military Administration, border military demands, dukes employing troops, night curfew; from Passes and Barriers, illegal crossing, false permits, forwarding escaped soldiers' families, forbidden sea travel, private archers; from Stables and Herds, demanding horses; from Postal Stations, occupying relay upper rooms; from Robbery and Theft, removing tattoo marks; from Assault, free-debased mutual beating; from Litigation, military-civilian appointments, false accusation, exile; from Bribes, receiving from dukes; from Adultery, free-debased adultery; from Miscellaneous, performing plays; from Apprehension, escaped laborers; from Trial, prisoners not laboring; from Construction, officials not residing in public yamen—and so on. Altered as polity and penal system shifted: from General Principles, 'foreigners who offend' became 'Mongols and persons who acquire nationality who offend,' exile places became places for five penal servitudes, three exiles, and two banishments; from Marriage, taking musicians as wives became taking prostitutes as wives; from Homicide, killing children and slaves to frame others—'and slaves' was cut; from Assault, slaves beating masters became hired workers beating masters; from Abuse, slaves cursing masters became hired workers cursing masters; from Adultery, slaves with master's wife became hired workers with master's wife—and so on. In all, the whole code retained 389 articles; analogous citation statutes were cut to half, distributed by category without a separate analogy section. Apart from deletions and mergers, together with newly compiled precedents, there were 1,066 in all. The Supervising Apprehension Precedents were ordered compiled in Shunzhi; they were originally for escaped banner bondservants. In Kangxi 15 it was again weighed and fixed; after Qianlong additions continued, totaling 110 articles, also separately retained or removed and attached to the penal code, while the whole book was abolished. At the head of the code the full mourning-garment diagram was still carried, to honor ritual teaching. It was promulgated that winter. Articles on punishing Mongols in the Court of Colonial Affairs Institutes, and Xining Tibetan punishment regulations enforced separately in Min, Tao, and elsewhere—because customs and penal systems differed, no light change was ventured.
16
新律則光緒三十二年法律館撰上刑民訴訟律，酌取英、美陪審制度。 各督撫多議其窒礙，遂寢。 三十三年，復先後奏上新刑律草案，總則十七章：曰法例，曰不論罪，曰未遂罪，曰累犯罪，曰俱發罪，曰共犯罪，曰刑名，曰宥恕減輕，曰自首減免，曰酌量減輕，曰加減例，曰緩刑，曰暫釋，曰恩赦，曰時效，曰時期計算，曰文例。 分則三十六章：曰關於帝室之罪，曰關於內亂之罪，曰關於國交之罪，曰關於外患之罪，曰關於漏泄機務之罪，曰關於瀆職之罪，曰關於妨害公務之罪，曰關於選舉之罪，曰關於騷擾之罪，曰關於逮捕監禁者脫逃之罪，曰關於藏匿罪人及湮滅證據之罪，曰關於偽證及誣告之罪，曰關於放火決水及水利之罪，曰關於危險物之罪，曰關於往來通信之罪，曰關於秩序之罪，曰關於偽造貨幣之罪，曰關於偽造文書及印文之罪，曰關於偽造度量衡之罪，曰關於祀典及墳墓之罪，曰關於鴉片煙之罪，曰關於賭博彩票之罪，曰關於奸非及重婚之罪，曰關於飲料水之罪，曰關於衛生之罪，曰關於殺傷之罪，曰關於墮胎之罪，曰關於遺棄之罪，曰關於逮捕監禁之罪，曰關於略誘及和誘之罪，曰關於安全信用名譽及秘密之罪，曰關於竊盜及強盜之罪，曰關於詐欺取財之罪，曰關於侵佔之罪，曰關於贓物之罪，曰關於毀棄損壞之罪。 兩編合共三百八十七條，經憲政編查館奏交部院及疆臣覈議，簽駁者夥。
The new code: in Guangxu 32 the Law Office drafted criminal and civil procedure laws, drawing on British and American jury systems. Most governor-generals found it obstructive and the matter was shelved. In year 33 they again memorialized the draft new penal code; General Part seventeen chapters: Legal Examples, Crimes Not Punishable Regardless, Attempted Crimes, Accumulated Crimes, Concurrent Crimes, Joint Crimes, Names of Punishments, Pardon and Mitigation, Self-Accusation and Mitigation, Discretionary Mitigation, Examples of Increase and Decrease, Suspended Sentence, Temporary Release, Amnesty, Limitation Period, Calculation of Time Periods, Textual Examples. Special Part thirty-six chapters: Crimes Concerning the Imperial House, Internal Disorder, Foreign Relations, External Threat, Leaking State Secrets, Malfeasance, Obstructing Official Business, Elections, Disturbance, Escape from Arrest and Imprisonment, Concealing Criminals and Destroying Evidence, False Testimony and False Accusation, Arson, Flood, and Water Works, Dangerous Substances, Communications, Public Order, Counterfeiting Currency, Forging Documents and Seals, Forging Weights and Measures, Sacrifices and Graves, Opium, Gambling and Lotteries, Adultery and Bigamy, Drinking Water, Public Health, Killing and Wounding, Abortion, Abandonment, Arrest and Imprisonment, Abduction and Enticement, Safety, Credit, Reputation, and Secrecy, Theft and Robbery, Fraud to Obtain Property, Embezzlement, Stolen Goods, Destruction and Damage. The two parts totaled 387 articles; referred to ministries and border officials for verification, objections were many.
17
宣統元年，沈家本等彙集各說，復奏進修正草案。 時江蘇提學使勞乃宣上書憲政編查館論之曰：「法律大臣會同法部奏進修改刑律，義關倫常諸條，未依舊律修入。 但於附則稱中國宗教遵孔，以綱常禮教為重。 如律中十惡親屬容隱，干名犯義，存留養親，及親屬相奸、相盜、相毆，發冢犯奸各條，未便蔑棄。 中國人有犯以上各罪，應仍依舊律，別輯單行法，以昭懲創。 竊維修訂新律，本為籌備立憲，統一法權。 凡中國人及在中國居住之外國人，皆應服從同一法律。 是此法律，本當以治中國人為主。 今乃依舊律別輯中國人單行法，是視此新刑律專為外國人設矣。 本末倒置，莫此為甚。 草案案語謂修訂刑律，所以收回領事裁判權。 刑律內有一二條為外國人所不遵奉，即無收回裁判權之實。 故所修刑律，專以摹仿外國為事。 此說實不盡然。 泰西各國，凡外國人居其國中，無不服從其國法律，不得執本國無此律以相爭，亦不得恃本國有此律以相抗。 今中國修訂刑律，乃謂為收回領事裁判權，必盡舍固有之禮教風俗，一一摹仿外國。 則同乎此國者，彼國有違言，同乎彼國者，此國又相反，是必窮之道也。 總之一國之律，必與各國之律相同，然後乃能令國內居住之外國人遵奉，萬萬無此理，亦萬萬無此事。 以此為收回領事裁判權之策，是終古無收回之望也。 且夫國之有刑，所以弼教。 一國之民有不遵禮教者，以刑齊之。 所謂禮防未然，刑禁已然，相輔而行，不可缺一者也。 故各省簽駁草案，每以維持風化立論，而案語乃指為渾道德法律為一。 其論無夫奸曰：『國家立法，期於令行禁止。 有法而不能行，轉使民玩法而肆無忌憚。 和姦之事，幾于禁之無可禁，誅之不勝誅，即刑章具在，亦祗具文。 必教育普及，家庭嚴正，輿論之力盛，廉恥之心生，然後淫靡之風可少衰。 』又曰：『防遏此等醜行，不在法律而在教化。 即列為專條，亦無實際。 』其立論在離法律與道德教化而二之，視法律為全無關於道德教化，故一意摹仿外國，而於舊律義關倫常諸條棄之如遺，焉用此法為乎？ 」謂宜將舊律有關禮教倫紀各節，逐一修入正文，並擬補干名犯義、犯罪存留養親、親屬相奸相毆、無夫奸、子孫違犯教令各條。 法律館爭之。 明年資政院開，憲政編查館奏交院議，將總則通過。 時勞乃宣充議員，與同院內閣學士陳寶琛等，於無夫奸及違犯教令二條尤力持不少怠，而分則遂未議決。 餘如民律、商律、刑事訴訟律、民事訴訟律、國籍法俱編纂告竣，未經核議。 惟法院編制法、違警律、禁煙條例均經宣統二年頒布，與現行刑律僅行之一年，而遜位之詔下矣。
In Xuantong 1, Shen Jiaben and others gathered opinions and again memorialized the revised draft. At that time Jiangsu provincial education commissioner Lao Naixuan memorialized the Constitutional Compilation Office: 'The law ministers together with the Ministry of Law memorialized the revised penal code; articles concerning human relations and constant principles were not revised into the old code.' 'But the supplementary provisions say China's religion honors Confucius and regards the constants of human relations and ritual teaching as weighty.' 'Such as the ten abominations, relatives concealing one another, offenses against elders' names, remaining to support parents, relatives committing adultery, theft, or beating one another, grave robbery and adultery—each cannot lightly be cast aside.' 'When Chinese commit the above crimes they should still follow the old code; a separate law should be compiled to display punishment.' 'We consider that revising the new code was originally to prepare constitutional government and unify legal authority.' 'All Chinese and foreigners residing in China should obey the same law.' 'This law should originally govern Chinese as its main purpose.' 'Now to compile a separate Chinese law according to the old code is to regard this new penal code as solely for foreigners.' 'Root and branch inverted—nothing exceeds this.' 'The draft's case language says revising the penal code recovers consular jurisdiction.' 'If one or two articles are not observed by foreigners, there is no reality of recovering jurisdiction.' 'Therefore the revised penal code specializes in imitating foreign countries.' 'This statement is not wholly so.' 'In all Western countries, foreigners residing there must obey that country's law; they may not argue their own country has no such law, nor resist on the ground their own country has such a law.' 'Now China revises the penal code yet says it recovers consular jurisdiction and must wholly abandon inherent ritual teaching and custom, imitating foreign countries in every point.' 'Then what agrees with this country, that country objects; what agrees with that country, this country opposes—a dead end.' 'In sum a country's laws must be identical with every country's laws before foreigners within can obey—no such principle, and no such thing will happen.' 'To take this as policy for recovering consular jurisdiction is to have no hope of recovery ever.' 'Moreover a state has punishments to assist teaching.' 'When a country's people do not observe ritual teaching, punishments align them.' 'Ritual guards what has not yet arisen, punishments forbid what has arisen—they assist each other; neither can be lacking.' 'Therefore provinces annotating the draft often argued from maintaining custom; yet the case language called this confusing morality and law.' 'Its argument on adultery without a husband said: "The state legislates expecting orders enforced and prohibitions observed.' 'When law cannot be enforced, it makes the people treat law as a game and act without dread.' 'Consensual adultery is nearly impossible to forbid, impossible to punish in excess; even when penal articles exist they are only empty text.' 'Only when education is universal, families strict, public opinion strong, and shame born can debauchery somewhat decline.' '" Again: "To check such conduct lies not in law but in teaching.' 'Even as a special article there is no practical effect.' '" Its argument separated law from moral teaching, viewing law as wholly unrelated to moral teaching; therefore it imitated foreign countries and cast aside old code articles on human relations—of what use is such a law?' He proposed that old-code sections on ritual teaching and human relations be revised into the main text, and supplementing offenses against elders' names, remaining to support parents, relatives committing adultery or beating one another, adultery without a husband, and children violating parental orders. The Law Office disputed this. The next year the Consultative Assembly opened; the Constitutional Compilation Office referred it to the Assembly, and the General Part passed. Lao Naixuan served as a member; with Chen Baochen and others, on adultery without a husband and violating parental orders they held firm, while the Special Part was not deliberated to conclusion. Civil code, commercial code, criminal and civil procedure laws, and nationality law were all compiled but not verified. Only the Court Organization Law, Police Offenses Law, and Anti-Opium Regulations were promulgated in Xuantong 2; with the Current Penal Code they ran only one year before the abdication edict.
