1
索額圖明珠-{余國柱}-佛倫
Songgotu, Mingzhu, Yu Guozhu, and Fu Lun
2
索額圖，赫舍里氏，滿洲正黃旗人，索尼第二子。 初授侍衛，自三等洊升一等。 康熙七年，授吏部侍郎。 八年五月，自請解任效力左右，復為一等侍衛。 及鼇拜獲罪，大學士班布爾善坐黨誅，授索額圖國史院大學士，兼佐領。 九年，改保和殿大學士。 十一年，世祖實錄成，加太子太傅。 十五年，大學士熊賜履票本有誤，改寫草簽，既又毀去。 索額圖與大學士巴泰、杜立德等疏劾，賜履坐罷歸。 十八年，京察，侍講學士顧八代隨徵稱職，翰林院以「政勤才長」注考，索額圖改注「浮躁」，竟坐降調。 語詳顧八代傳。
Songgotu of the Heseri clan was a Manchu of the Plain Yellow Banner and the second son of Sonin. He was first appointed an imperial guard and rose in succession from third to first rank. In Kangxi year seven he was appointed vice minister of the Board of Personnel. In the fifth month of year eight he asked to resign his office and serve at the emperor's side, and was again made a first-rank guard. When Oboi was convicted, Grand Secretary Ban Buerhan was executed as his partisan, and Songgotu was appointed grand secretary of the Historiography Institute while also serving as company commander. In year nine he was transferred to grand secretary of the Hall of Preserving Harmony. In year eleven, when the Veritable Record of the Shizu Emperor was completed, he received the additional title of grand tutor of the heir apparent. In year fifteen Grand Secretary Xiong Cilv's draft memorial contained an error; he rewrote it on a scratch note and then destroyed it. Songgotu joined Grand Secretaries Batai and Du Lide and others in memorializing against him, and Xiong Cilv was dismissed and sent home. At the metropolitan review in year eighteen, Lecturer Gu Badai was rated by the campaign staff as having performed competently, and the Hanlin Academy entered 'diligent in office and gifted in talent' in his review; Songgotu changed the entry to 'flighty,' and Gu was ultimately demoted on that account. The affair is told in full in Gu Badai's biography.
3
索額圖權勢日盛。 會地震，左都御史魏象樞入對，陳索額圖怙權貪縱狀，請嚴譴。 上曰：「修省當自朕始！ 」翌日，召索額圖及諸大臣諭曰：「茲遘地震，朕反躬修省。 爾等亦宜洗滌肺腸，公忠自矢。 自任用後，諸臣家計頗皆饒裕，乃朋比徇私，益加貪黷。 若事情發覺，國法具在，決不爾貸！ 」是時索額圖、明珠同柄朝政，互植私黨，貪侈傾朝右，故諭及之。 上並書「節制謹度」榜賜焉。
Songgotu's power grew day by day. When an earthquake struck, Left Censor-in-Chief Wei Xiangsu came before the throne and laid out Songgotu's abuse of power, greed, and license, asking for stern punishment. The emperor said, "Reform must begin with me! " The next day he summoned Songgotu and the other ministers and said, "Now that an earthquake has struck, I am examining myself. You too must cleanse your hearts and pledge yourselves to the public good with single-minded loyalty. Since you took office your households have all grown quite prosperous, yet you band together for private ends and grow ever greedier in your posts. If your deeds are exposed, the laws of the state are in force, and I will not spare you! " At that time Songgotu and Mingzhu together held the reins of government, each cultivating private factions; their greed and extravagance dominated the court, which is why he spoke of it. The emperor also wrote the words "restrain desires and be prudent in conduct" on a placard and bestowed it on them.
4
十九年八月，以病乞解任，上優旨獎其「勤敏練達，用兵以來，贊畫機宜」，改命為內大臣。 尋授議政大臣。 先是索額圖兄噶布拉，以冊諡孝誠仁皇后推恩所生，封一等公； 弟心裕，襲索尼初封一等伯； 法保，襲索尼加封一等公。 二十三年三月，以心裕等嬾惰驕縱，責索額圖弗能教，奪內大臣、議政大臣、太子太傅，但任佐領，並奪法保一等公。 二十五年，授領侍衛內大臣。
In the eighth month of year nineteen he asked to resign on grounds of illness; the emperor with a gracious edict praised his diligence, acuteness, and mastery, and his planning of strategy since the campaigns began, and appointed him an inner minister instead. Soon after he was appointed a deliberative minister. Earlier Songgotu's elder brother Gabula, through the grace extended when Empress Xiaochengren was given her posthumous title, had been enfeoffed duke of the first rank; his younger brother Xinyu had inherited Sonin's original barony of the first rank; and Fabao had inherited Sonin's augmented ducal rank of the first class. In the third month of year twenty-three, because Xinyu and the others were lazy, arrogant, and indulgent, the emperor blamed Songgotu for failing to teach them, stripped him of his posts as inner minister, deliberative minister, and grand tutor of the heir apparent, leaving him only as company commander, and also stripped Fabao of his ducal title. In year twenty-five he was appointed senior guard of the imperial bodyguard.
5
時俄羅斯屢侵黑龍江邊境，據雅克薩，其眾去復來，上發兵圍之。 察罕汗謝罪，使費耀多囉等來議界。 二十八年，上命索額圖與都統佟國綱往議。 索額圖奏謂：「尼布楚、雅克薩兩地當歸我。 」上曰：「尼布楚歸我，則俄羅斯貿易無所棲止，可以額爾固納河為界。 」索額圖等與議，費耀多囉果執尼布楚、雅克薩為請。 索額圖等力斥之，仍宣上意，以額爾固納河及格爾必齊河為界，立碑而還。
At that time Russia repeatedly raided the Heilongjiang frontier and held Albazin; its forces would withdraw and then return, and the emperor sent troops to besiege them. The Tsar apologized and sent Feodosii and others to negotiate the boundary. In year twenty-eight the emperor ordered Songgotu and Commander-in-Chief Tong Guowang to go negotiate. Songgotu memorialized, "Nerchinsk and Albazin should both be returned to us. " The emperor said, "If Nerchinsk is returned to us, Russia will have nowhere to lodge its trade; the Argun River may serve as the boundary. " Songgotu and his party negotiated, and Feodosii indeed insisted on Nerchinsk and Albazin. Songgotu and the others firmly rejected this, proclaimed the emperor's intent, fixed the boundary at the Argun and Gorbi rivers, erected a boundary stele, and returned.
6
二十九年，上以裕親王福全為大將軍，擊噶爾丹，命索額圖將盛京、吉林、科爾沁兵會於巴林，敗噶爾丹於烏闌布通。 以不窮追，鐫四級。 三十五年，從上親征，率八旗前鋒、察哈爾四旗及漢軍綠旗兵前行，並命督火器營。 大將軍費揚古自西路抵圖拉。 上駐克魯倫河，噶爾丹遁走。 費揚古截擊之於昭莫多，大敗其眾。 三十六年，上還幸寧夏，命索額圖督水驛，會噶爾丹死。 敘功，复前所鐫級。 四十年九月，以老乞休，心裕代為領侍衛內大臣。
In year twenty-nine the emperor appointed Prince Yu Fuquan commander-in-chief to attack Galdan and ordered Songgotu to lead troops from Shengjing, Jilin, and the Khorchin to rendezvous at Bailing, where Galdan was defeated at Ulan Butung. For failing to pursue the enemy to the end, four ranks were struck from his grade. In year thirty-five he accompanied the emperor on the personal campaign, led the Eight Banners vanguard, the four Chahar banners, and the Han Green Standard troops in the advance, and was also ordered to supervise the firearms brigade. Commander-in-Chief Fiyanggu reached Tula by the western route. The emperor encamped on the Kerulen River while Galdan fled. Fiyanggu intercepted him at Jao Modo and routed his army. In year thirty-six, as the emperor returned by way of Ningxia, he ordered Songgotu to supervise the water courier stations; soon afterward Galdan died. When merit was reckoned, the ranks previously struck from him were restored. In the ninth month of year forty he asked to retire on account of age, and Xinyu succeeded him as senior guard of the imperial bodyguard.
7
索額圖事皇太子謹，皇太子漸失上意。 四十一年，上閱河至德州，皇太子有疾，召索額圖自京師至德州侍疾。 居月馀，皇太子疾愈，還京師。 是歲，心裕以虐斃家人奪官。 四十二年五月，上命執索額圖，交宗人府拘禁，諭曰：「爾為大學士，以貪惡革退，後復起用，罔知愧悔。 爾家人訐爾，留內三年，朕意欲寬爾。 爾乃怙過不悛，結黨妄行，議論國事。 皇太子在德州，爾乘馬至中門始下，即此爾已應死。 爾所行事，任舉一端，無不當誅。 ​​朕念爾原系大臣，心有不忍，姑貸爾死。 」又命執索額圖諸子交心裕、法保拘禁，諭：「若別生事端，心裕、法保當族誅！ 」諸臣黨附索額圖者，麻爾圖、額庫禮、溫代、邵甘、佟寶並命嚴錮，阿米達以老貸之。 又命諸臣同祖子孫在部院者，皆奪官。 江潢以家有索額圖私書，下刑部論死。 仍諭滿洲人與偶有來往者，漢官與交結者，皆貸不問。 尋索額圖死於幽所。
Songgotu served the crown prince attentively, yet the crown prince gradually lost the emperor's favor. In year forty-one, while the emperor was inspecting the Grand Canal and reached Dezhou, the crown prince fell ill, and Songgotu was summoned from the capital to Dezhou to nurse him. After slightly more than a month the crown prince recovered and returned to the capital. That year Xinyu was stripped of office for cruelly beating a household member to death. In the fifth month of year forty-two the emperor ordered Songgotu arrested and imprisoned in the Imperial Clan Court, saying, "You were a grand secretary, dismissed for greed and wickedness, then employed again, yet you know no shame or remorse. Your household members accused you; you were kept within the palace for three years, and I meant to treat you leniently. Yet you cling to your faults and will not reform, form a faction and act recklessly, and discuss affairs of state. When the crown prince was at Dezhou, you rode your horse to the middle gate before dismounting; on that count alone you deserved death. Of all you have done, take any single instance—none would fail to merit execution. Remembering that you were once a high minister, my heart cannot bear it, and for the present I spare your life. " He also ordered Songgotu's sons arrested and placed in the custody of Xinyu and Fabao, saying, "If any further trouble arises, Xinyu and Fabao shall suffer clan extermination! " The officials who had joined Songgotu's faction—Ma'ertu, Ekuli, Wendai, Shao Gan, and Tong Bao—were all ordered strictly confined; Amida was spared because of his age. He also ordered that all collateral descendants of these men serving in the ministries and courts should be stripped of office. Jiang Huang, because he kept private letters from Songgotu at home, was sent to the Ministry of Punishments and sentenced to death. He further announced that Manchus who had had only occasional contact with him, and Han officials who had associated with him, would all be pardoned and not questioned. Before long Songgotu died in confinement.
8
後數年，皇太子以狂疾廢，上宣諭罪狀，謂：「索額圖助允礽潛謀大事，朕知其情，將索額圖處死。 今允礽欲為索額圖報仇，令朕戒慎不寧。 」並按誅索額圖二子格爾芬、阿爾吉善。 他日，上謂廷臣曰：「昔索額圖懷私，倡議皇太子服禦俱用黃色，一切儀制幾與朕相似。 驕縱之漸，實由於此。 索額圖誠本朝第一罪人也！ 」
Several years later the crown prince was deposed for violent madness; the emperor proclaimed his crimes, saying, "Songgotu helped Yinreng secretly plot great affairs; I knew the circumstances and had Songgotu put to death. Now Yinreng wishes to avenge Songgotu and keeps me from living in vigilance and peace. " He also had Songgotu's two sons Ge'erfen and A'erjishan executed according to the case. On another day the emperor told the court ministers, "In former times Songgotu, acting from private interest, proposed that the crown prince's mourning dress and daily utensils all use yellow, and that all ritual arrangements nearly resemble my own. The gradual growth of pride and indulgence in truth stemmed from this. Songgotu was truly this dynasty's foremost criminal! "
9
明珠，字端範，納喇氏，滿洲正黃旗人，葉赫貝勒金台石孫。 父尼雅哈，當太祖滅葉赫，來降，授佐領。 明珠自侍衛授鑾儀衛治儀正，遷內務府郎中。 康熙三年，擢總管。 五年，授弘文院學士。 七年，命閱淮、揚河工，議復興化白駒場舊閘，鑿黃河北岸引河。 旋授刑部尚書。 改都察院左都御史，充經筵講官。 十一年，遷兵部尚書。 十二年，上幸南苑，閱八旗甲兵於晾鷹台。 明珠先布條教使練習之，及期，軍容整肅，上嘉其能，因著為令。
Mingzhu, styled Duanfan, of the Nara clan, was a Manchu of the Plain Yellow Banner and the grandson of the Yehe beile Jintaishi. His father Niyaha, when Taizu destroyed Yehe, surrendered and was appointed company commander. Mingzhu was transferred from guard to director of ceremonial regulations in the Imperial Insignia Guard, then promoted to director in the Imperial Household. In Kangxi year three he was promoted to superintendent. In year five he was appointed academician of the Hongwen Academy. In year seven he was ordered to inspect the Huai and Yangtze river works and proposed restoring the old sluice at Xinghua's Baijuchang and cutting a diversion canal on the north bank of the Yellow River. Soon after he was appointed minister of punishments. He was transferred to left censor-in-chief in the Censorate and served as lecturer at the classic lecture. In year eleven he was promoted to minister of war. In year twelve, when the emperor visited the Southern Park and reviewed the armored troops of the Eight Banners on the Hawk-Drying Terrace, Mingzhu had first laid down regulations for them to drill; when the day came the troops were in tight order, the emperor praised his ability, and made the practice a standing rule.
10
康熙初，南疆大定，留重兵鎮之：吳三桂雲南，尚可喜廣東，耿精忠福建。 十馀年，漸跋扈，三桂尤驕縱。 可喜亦憂之，疏請撤籓，歸老海城。 精忠、三桂繼請。 上召諸大臣詢方略，戶部尚書米思翰、刑部尚書莫洛等主撤，明珠和之。 諸大臣皆默然。 上曰：「三桂等蓄謀久，不早除之，將養癰成患。 今日撤亦反，不撤亦反，不若先發。 」因下詔許之。 三桂遂反，精忠及可喜子之信皆叛應之。 時爭咎建議者，索額圖請誅之。 上曰：「此出自朕意，他人何罪？ 」明珠由是稱上旨。 十四年，調吏部尚書。 十六年，授武英殿大學士，屢充實錄、方略、一統志、明史諸書總裁，累加太子太師。 迨三叛既平，上諭廷臣以前議撤籓，惟明珠等能稱旨，且曰：「當時有請誅建議者，朕若從之，皆含冤泉壤矣！ 」
At the beginning of the Kangxi reign, after the southern frontier was largely pacified, heavy garrisons were left in place: Wu Sangui in Yunnan, Shang Kexi in Guangdong, and Geng Jingzhong in Fujian. After more than ten years they gradually grew overbearing, and Sangui was especially arrogant. Kexi too was troubled and memorialized asking to surrender his princedom and retire to Haicheng. Jingzhong and Sangui followed with similar requests. The emperor summoned the ministers to ask their counsel; Minister of Revenue Misihan, Minister of Punishments Moluo, and others favored withdrawal, and Mingzhu agreed. All the other ministers remained silent. The emperor said, "Sangui and the others have long nurtured their plot; if we do not remove them early, we will nurse an abscess until it becomes a grave affliction. If we withdraw them now they will rebel; if we do not withdraw them they will rebel too—we might as well strike first. " Thereupon an edict granting permission was issued. Sangui thereupon rebelled, and Jingzhong and Kexi's son Zhixin all rose in revolt in support. At the time many blamed those who had proposed the policy; Songgotu asked that they be put to death. The emperor said, "This sprang from my own intent—what crime have the others? " From this Mingzhu was said to have gauged the emperor's mind. In year fourteen he was transferred to minister of personnel. In year sixteen he was appointed grand secretary of the Hall of Military Eminence, repeatedly served as chief compiler of the Veritable Records, military chronicles, the Unified Gazetteer, and the History of Ming, and was repeatedly given the additional title of grand tutor of the heir apparent. When the three rebellions were finally suppressed, the emperor told the court ministers that in the earlier debate on withdrawing the princedoms only Mingzhu and others had grasped his intent, and said, "At the time some asked that those who had proposed it be put to death—had I followed them, all would now lie wronged beneath the earth! "
11
明珠既擅政，簠簋不飭，貨賄山積。 佛倫、餘國柱其黨也，援引致高位。 靳輔督南河，主築堤束水，下游不濬自通。 於成龍等議濬下游，與異議。 輔興屯田，議者謂不便於民，多不右輔，明珠獨是其議。 蔡毓榮、張汧皆明珠所薦引者也，迨得罪按治，恐累舉者，傅輕比，上諭斥，始定。 與索額圖互植黨相傾軋。 索額圖生而貴盛，性倨肆，有不附己者顯斥之，於朝士獨親李光地。 明珠則務謙和，輕財好施，以招來新進，異己者以陰謀陷之，與徐乾學等相結。 索額圖善事皇太子，而明珠反之，朝士有侍皇太子者，皆陰斥去。 薦湯斌傅皇太子，即以傾斌。 會天久不雨，光地所薦講官德格勒明易，上命筮，得夬，因陳小人居鼎鉉，天屯其膏，語斥明珠。 事具德格勒傳。
Once Mingzhu monopolized government, his conduct was unrestrained and bribes piled up like mountains. Fu Lun and Yu Guozhu were his partisans; through his patronage they rose to high office. Jin Fu supervised the southern waterways and advocated building dikes to confine the current so the lower reaches would clear without dredging. Yu Chenglong and others advocated dredging the lower reaches, differing from him. Fu promoted field colonies; critics said it was hard on the people, and many did not support him—only Mingzhu approved his plan. Cai Yurong and Zhang Han were both men Mingzhu had recommended; when they were later convicted and punished, the court at first imposed light sentences for fear of implicating their patrons, but the emperor rebuked this and only then were the penalties fixed. He and Songgotu each built factions and undermined one another. Born to wealth and eminence, Songgotu was arrogant by nature; he openly cast out those who would not follow him, and among court officials he favored Li Guangdi alone. Mingzhu, by contrast, cultivated humility, was free with money and generous, and won over rising officials; those who opposed him he ruined by secret plots, and he allied himself with Xu Qianxue and others. Songgotu cultivated the crown prince's favor, while Mingzhu did the opposite; court officials who attended the crown prince were all secretly driven away. He recommended Tang Bin to tutor the crown prince, then used that very appointment to bring Bin down. When drought lingered, Degeli, the lecturer on the Changes whom Li Guangdi had recommended, was ordered to divine and obtained Guai; he declared that petty men held the highest offices and heaven hoarded its bounty—words aimed at Mingzhu. The affair is told in full in Degeli's biography.
12
二十七年，御史郭琇疏劾：「明珠、國柱背公營私，閣中票擬皆出明珠指麾，輕重任意。 國柱承其風旨，即有舛錯，同官莫敢駁正。 聖明時有詰責，漫無省改。 凡奉諭旨或稱善，明珠則曰'由我力荐'； 或稱不善，明珠則曰'上意不喜，我從容挽救'； 且任意附益，市恩立威，因而要結群心，挾取貨賄。 日奏事畢，出中左門，滿、漢部院諸臣拱立以待，密語移時，上意罔不宣露。 部院事稍有關係者，必請命而行。 明珠廣結黨羽，滿洲則佛倫、格斯特及其族侄富拉塔、錫珠等，凡會議會推，力為把持； 漢人則國柱為之囊橐，督撫籓臬員缺，國柱等展轉徵賄，必滿欲而後止。 康熙二十三年學道報滿應升者，率往論價，缺皆預定。 靳輔與明珠交結，初議開下河，以為當任輔，欣然欲行。 及上欲別任，則以於成龍方沐上眷，舉以應命，而成龍官止按察使，題奏權仍屬輔，此時未有阻撓意也。 及輔張大其事，與成龍議不合，乃始一力阻撓。 明珠自知罪戾，對人柔顏甘語，百計款曲，而陰行鷙害，意毒謀險。 最忌者言官，惟恐發其奸狀，考選科道，輒與訂約，章奏必使先聞。 當佛倫為左都御史，見御史李興謙屢疏稱旨，吳震方頗有彈劾，即令借事排陷。 明珠智術足以彌縫罪惡，又有國柱奸謀附和，負恩亂政。 伏冀立加嚴譴。 」
In year twenty-seven Censor Guo Xiu memorialized accusing Mingzhu and Guozhu of betraying the public for private gain: "All draft endorsements in the Grand Secretariat issued from Mingzhu's direction, light or heavy as he pleased. Guozhu followed his lead; even when there were errors, his colleagues dared not correct them. Though the emperor at times rebuked them, they showed no reflection or reform. Whenever an imperial rescript was received or someone was praised, Mingzhu would say, "That was through my strong recommendation"; or when someone was criticized, Mingzhu would say, "The emperor disliked it; I calmly rescued the matter"; and he arbitrarily embellished matters, traded favors to build prestige, bound men's hearts, and extorted bribes. Each day when audience ended he left through the Central Left Gate; Manchu and Han officials of the ministries and courts stood bowed awaiting him, and after long whispered talk nothing of the emperor's intent failed to leak out. On any ministry or court matter that bore the slightest relation to him, they had to ask his leave before acting. Mingzhu built a wide faction: among Manchus, Fu Lun, Gest, and his clansmen's nephews Fulata and Xizhu—for every conference and joint recommendation he strove to control the outcome; among Han officials Guozhu served as his purse; for governor-general, governor, and provincial commissioner posts, Guozhu and others passed around demands for bribes and would not stop until they were fully satisfied. In Kangxi year twenty-three, when provincial education commissioners finished their terms and were due for promotion, they generally went to bargain over price, and all posts were prearranged. Jin Fu was allied with Mingzhu; when the lower river works were first proposed, he thought the task should fall to him and was eager to undertake it. When the emperor wished to appoint someone else, he nominated Yu Chenglong, who then enjoyed imperial favor, to answer the command; yet Chenglong's rank was only surveillance commissioner, and memorial authority still belonged to Fu—at that point he had not yet meant to obstruct. When Fu magnified the affair and clashed with Chenglong, only then did he set himself to obstruct with all his strength. Mingzhu knew his own guilt; toward others he wore a gentle face and sweet words and used every device to placate them, yet secretly he acted with fierce harm—his intent poisonous and his schemes treacherous. He most feared censorial officials, lest they expose his crimes; when censors were selected, he invariably made them agree that memorials must reach him first. When Fu Lun was left censor-in-chief, seeing that Censor Li Xingqian's memorials pleased the throne while Wu Zhenfang often impeached others, he ordered them brought down on pretext. Mingzhu's craft was sufficient to cover his crimes, and with Guozhu's wicked schemes echoing his own, he betrayed imperial favor and threw government into disorder. I humbly pray that stern punishment be imposed at once. "
13
疏入，上諭吏部曰：「國家建官分職，必矢志精白，大法小廉。 今在廷諸臣，自大學士以下，惟知互相結引，徇私傾陷。 凡遇會議，一二倡率於前，眾附和於後，一意詭隨。 廷議如此，國是何憑？ 至於緊要員缺，特令會同推舉，原期得人，亦欲令被舉者警心滌慮，恐致累及舉者，而貪黷匪類，往往敗露。 此皆植黨納賄所致。 朕不忍加罪大臣，且用兵時有曾著勞績者，免其發覺。 罷明珠大學士，交領侍衛內大臣酌用。 」未幾，授內大臣。 後從上徵噶爾丹，督西路軍餉，敘功復原級。
When the memorial entered, the emperor instructed the Board of Personnel, "In establishing offices and dividing duties, the state requires men to devote themselves to purity, uphold the great law, and keep themselves clean in small matters. Now the ministers at court, from the grand secretaries downward, know only how to draw one another into factions and pursue private ends to undermine rivals. Whenever they meet in conference, one or two lead at the front and the multitude echo behind, all bent on perverse compliance. When court deliberation is thus, upon what can the state rely? As for important posts, the court specially orders joint recommendation, originally hoping to obtain the right men and also wishing those recommended to guard their hearts and cleanse their thoughts, fearing lest they bring down their recommenders—yet greedy and corrupt villains are often exposed. All this stems from building factions and accepting bribes. I cannot bear to punish high ministers, and since some rendered merit in wartime, I spare them exposure. Dismiss Mingzhu from the grand secretaryship and refer him to the senior guard of the imperial bodyguard for appropriate employment. " Before long he was appointed inner minister. Later he followed the emperor in the campaign against Galdan, supervised military provisions on the western route, and when merit was tallied his original rank was restored.
14
明珠自罷政後，雖權勢未替，然為內大臣者二十年，竟不復柄用。 四十七年，卒。 子性德、揆敘自有傳。
From the time Mingzhu left office, though his influence did not wholly fade, he served as inner minister for twenty years and was never again entrusted with the reins of government. In year forty-seven he died. His sons Xingde and Kuixu have their own biographies.
15
-{余}-國柱，字兩石，湖廣大冶人。 順治九年進士，授兗州推官。 遷行人司行人，轉戶部主事。 康熙十五年，考授戶科給事中。 時方用兵，國柱屢疏言籌餉事，語多精覈。 二十年，擢左副都御史。 旋授江寧巡撫，請設機制寬大緞疋。 得旨：「非常用之物，何為勞費？ 」當明珠用事，國柱務罔利以迎合之，及內轉左都御史，遷戶部尚書，湯斌繼國柱撫江蘇； 國柱索斌獻明珠金，斌不能應，由是傾之。 二十六年，授武英殿大學士，益與明珠結，一時稱為「-{余}-秦檜」。 會上謁陵，中途召-{于}-成龍入對，成龍盡發明珠、國柱等貪私。 上歸詢高士奇，士奇亦以狀聞。 及郭琇疏論劾，言者蜂起，國柱門人陳世安亦具疏糾之，頗中要害，國柱遂奪官。 既出都，於江寧治第宅，營生計，復為給事中何金蘭所劾，命逐之回籍。 卒於家。
Yu Guozhu, styled Liangshi, was a native of Daye in Huguang. A jinshi of Shunzhi year nine, he was appointed investigating censor of Yanzhou. He was transferred to clerk in the Courier Service, then promoted to director in the Board of Revenue. In Kangxi year fifteen he passed examination and was appointed supervising censor of the Revenue Section. War was then underway; Guozhu repeatedly memorialized on raising provisions, his language often precise and penetrating. In year twenty he was promoted to vice censor-in-chief of the left. Soon after he was appointed governor of Jiangning and requested the establishment of looms to weave broad satin bolts. He received the rescript, "These are not items in regular use—why labor and spend on them? " While Mingzhu held power, Guozhu strove for illicit profit to please him; when he was transferred within the capital to left censor-in-chief and promoted to minister of revenue, Tang Bin succeeded Guozhu as governor of Jiangsu; Guozhu demanded that Bin present gold to Mingzhu; Bin could not comply, and on this account Guozhu brought him down. In year twenty-six he was appointed grand secretary of the Hall of Military Eminence, bound himself still closer to Mingzhu, and for a time was called "Yu Qin Hui." When the emperor was visiting the imperial tombs, he summoned Yu Chenglong en route for audience; Chenglong fully exposed the greed and private conduct of Mingzhu, Guozhu, and others. On his return the emperor questioned Gao Shiqi, who also reported the facts. When Guo Xiu's memorial impeaching them appeared, accusers swarmed forth; Guozhu's disciple Chen Shi'an also submitted a memorial accusing him, striking several vital points, and Guozhu was thereupon stripped of office. After leaving the capital he built a mansion at Jiangning and managed his livelihood; he was again impeached by Supervising Censor He Jinlan and ordered expelled to his native place. He died at home.
16
佛倫，舒穆祿氏，滿洲正白旗人。 自筆帖式累遷內閣學士。 吳三桂既死，其孫世璠猶據滇、黔，命佛倫總理糧餉，通鎮遠運道，旋兼理四川糧餉。 事平，遷刑部侍郎。 尋遷左都御史，擢工部尚書，轉刑、戶兩部。 先是下河工程，靳輔與按察使於成龍議不​​協，命佛倫偕侍郎熊一瀟等勘議。 佛倫受明珠指，議如輔言，為總漕慕天顏所劾。 御史陸祖修亦劾佛倫袒輔，且言：「九卿會議時，尚書科爾坤等阿佛倫意，尚書張玉書、左都御史徐乾學言興屯所佔民田應還之民，科爾坤置不聞。 他九卿或不得見隻字。 」上怒，下部嚴議。 及郭琇劾明珠，指佛倫為明珠黨，因解佛倫任。 召輔等廷對，佛倫乃奏停屯田，並汰前所設官。 部議奪佛倫官，上命留佐領。 旋授內務府總管。
Fu Lun of the Sumuru clan was a Manchu of the Plain White Banner. From clerk he rose in succession to academician of the Grand Secretariat. After Wu Sangui died, his grandson Shifan still held Yunnan and Guizhou; Fu Lun was ordered to oversee provisions, open the transport route through Zhenyuan, and soon also supervised Sichuan provisions. When the affair was settled, he was transferred to vice minister of punishments. Soon after he was transferred to left censor-in-chief, promoted to minister of works, and then transferred to the ministries of punishments and revenue. Earlier, on the lower river works, Jin Fu and Surveillance Commissioner Yu Chenglong could not agree; Fu Lun was ordered, together with Vice Minister Xiong Yixiao and others, to inspect and deliberate. Fu Lun followed Mingzhu's direction and decided as Fu advocated; he was impeached by Director-General of Grain Transport Mu Tianyan. Censor Lu Zuxiu also impeached Fu Lun for favoring Fu, saying, "At the conference of the Nine Ministers, Minister Ke'erkun and others flattered Fu Lun's intent; Ministers Zhang Yushu and Left Censor-in-Chief Xu Qianxue said the civilian fields seized for field colonies should be returned to the people, but Ke'erkun paid no heed. The other ministers sometimes did not even see a single character of the documents. " The emperor was angered and referred the matter to the ministries for strict deliberation. When Guo Xiu impeached Mingzhu and named Fu Lun as a member of Mingzhu's faction, Fu Lun was dismissed from office. Fu and others were summoned for court audience; Fu Lun then memorialized to halt field colonies and abolish the officials previously established for them. The ministries deliberated stripping Fu Lun of office; the emperor ordered that he retain his company commandership. Soon after he was appointed superintendent of the Imperial Household.
17
出為山東巡撫，疏請均賦役，令紳民一體應役，詔嘉其實心任事。 初，濰縣知縣硃敦厚以贓私為巡撫錢鎯所發，乞徐乾學請於鎯，獲免，且內擢主事。 至是事發，下佛倫鞫實，乾學坐奪官。 佛倫又劾琇知吳江縣時，嘗侵公帑，其父景昌故名爾標，乃明御史黃宗昌奴，坐賊黨誅，琇改父名冒封典，當追奪。 乾學故附明珠，後相失，或傳琇疏乾學實主之，故佛倫以是報。 尋擢川陝總督，入為禮部尚書。 三十八年，授文淵閣大學士。 三十九年，琇入覲，訟父受誣。 上詰佛倫，自承不實，當奪官，援赦得免。 未幾，以原品休致。 旋卒。
He went out as governor of Shandong and memorialized requesting equalization of tax and corvée levies, ordering gentry and commoners alike to perform corvée; an edict praised his earnest discharge of duty. Earlier, Wei County magistrate Zhu Dunhou was exposed for corruption by Governor Qian E; he begged Xu Qianxue to intercede with Qian, was spared, and was even promoted within the capital to director. When the affair came to light, Fu Lun was ordered to investigate the facts; Qianxue was stripped of office on the charge. Fu Lun also impeached Xiu for, when he was magistrate of Wujiang, having embezzled public funds; his father Jingchang was formerly named Erbiao, a slave of the Ming censor Huang Zongchang who was executed as a rebel partisan; Xiu changed his father's name to obtain enfeoffment honors by fraud, which should be revoked. Qianxue had formerly attached himself to Mingzhu but later fell out with him; some said Xiu's memorial was in truth Qianxue's work, and so Fu Lun repaid him in this way. Soon after he was promoted to governor-general of Shaanxi and Sichuan, then entered the capital as minister of rites. In year thirty-eight he was appointed grand secretary of the Hall of Literary Depth. In year thirty-nine Xiu came to audience and sued over his father's having been slandered. The emperor questioned Fu Lun; he confessed that the charges were untrue and that he deserved to be stripped of office, but invoked an amnesty and was spared. Before long he retired at his original rank. Soon after he died.
18
論曰：康熙中，滿洲大臣以權位相尚者，惟索額圖、明珠，一時氣勢熏灼，然不能終保令名，卒以貪侈敗。 索額圖以附皇太子得罪，禍延於後嗣。 明珠與索額圖競權，不附皇太子，雖被彈事罷相，聖祖猶念其贊撤籓，力全之，以視索額圖，豈不幸哉？ 若國柱、佛倫，則權門之疏附矣。
The commentator says: In the Kangxi reign, among Manchu ministers who vied in power and position, only Songgotu and Mingzhu—for a time their momentum scorched all around—yet they could not preserve a good name to the end and at last fell through greed and extravagance. Songgotu offended through attaching himself to the crown prince, and calamity extended to his descendants. Mingzhu competed with Songgotu for power and did not attach himself to the crown prince; though impeached and dismissed from the chancellorship, the Kangxi Emperor still remembered his support for withdrawing the princedoms and strove to preserve him—compared with Songgotu, was he not more fortunate? As for Guozhu and Fu Lun, they were but distant adherents of the power clique.
