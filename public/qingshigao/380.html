陳若霖戴三錫孫爾準程祖洛馬濟勝裕泰賀長齡
Chen Ruolin, Dai Sanxi, Sun Erzhun, Cheng Zuluo, Ma Jisheng, Yu Tai, and He Zhangling
3
陳若霖，字宗覲，福建閩縣人。 乾隆五十二年進士，選庶吉士，散館授刑部主事，累遷郎中。 束鹿縣民王洪中為人聚毆，訟不得直，自經死。 若霖鞫得其實，被議敘。 秩滿當外用，仍留部。 數從大臣赴各省讞獄，以寬恕稱。 嘉慶十三年，出為四川鹽茶道，擢山東按察使。 調廣東，署布政使，以佐總督百齡平海盜，賜花翎。 調湖北，復調四川，就遷布政使。 二十年，擢雲南巡撫。 水尾土州目黃金珠結內地奸民，殺副州目李文政，掠其家，鞫實，置於法。
Chen Ruolin, whose style name was Zongqin, came from Min County in Fujian. He passed the metropolitan examination in 1787, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and after completing his probation was posted as a secretary in the Ministry of Justice, rising eventually to director. When Wang Hongzhong, a man of Shulu County, was set upon and beaten by a crowd, he could obtain no redress in court and hanged himself in despair. Ruolin investigated the case, established what had happened, and was commended in the official merit review. Although his term entitled him to an outside appointment, he was kept on at the ministry. He often accompanied senior ministers on provincial criminal reviews and earned a reputation for clemency. In 1808 he left the capital as intendant of the Sichuan Salt and Tea Circuit and was soon promoted to surveillance commissioner of Shandong. Transferred to Guangdong as acting administration commissioner, he aided Governor-General Bai Ling in suppressing piracy and was awarded the peacock feather. After postings in Hubei and then Sichuan again, he was promoted on the spot to administration commissioner. In 1815 he was appointed governor of Yunnan. Huang Jinzhu, the native chieftain of Shuiwei, colluded with mainland criminals to murder Vice-Chieftain Li Wenzheng and loot his family; once the case was proved, Huang was executed according to law.
4
歷廣東、河南、浙江巡撫。 浙省南北新關科罰無度，限以半正額為止，卹商而課裕。 修蕭山新廟堤，建盤頭以御潮。 次年，新林塘圮，親往勘，疏言：「新林塘舊為險工，今距海日遠，塘以外為灶地，外復為牧地，中有馬塘，足為新林屏蔽，宜補築以遏潮汐。 疏通灶地各溝洫，引入牧地之莫家等灣以排泄之，即以灶地之土培護新林堤基。 西築橫塘以御江水。 責令灶牧各戶及蕭山、山陰、會稽三縣，分別修築。 」又奏修會稽、上虞等縣塘堤，並如議行。 二十四年，擢湖廣總督。 湖南鳳凰等廳屯丁額多為官佔，失業者眾，悉清釐發還徵租。 官入苗寨多婪索，或冒名詐財，嚴禁之。 又以屯地磽瘠租額重，為奏減苗租二萬餘石，免逋賦七萬餘石，苗民感之。
He went on to govern Guangdong, Henan, and Zhejiang in turn. At Zhejiang's northern and southern new customs houses, penalties had been exacted without restraint; he capped them at half the statutory rate, easing the burden on merchants while keeping revenue healthy. He restored the Xinmiao dike at Xiaoshan and built a headwork to break the tidal surge. The following year the Xinlin seawall failed; he inspected the site himself and reported: "Xinlin was once a critical floodwork, but the shoreline has receded. Beyond the wall lie salt flats, then pasture, with Matang in between—enough to shield Xinlin. The wall should be rebuilt to hold back the tide." He proposed clearing the salt-flat ditches and channeling runoff into bays such as Mojia on the pasture side, then using the excavated soil to buttress the Xinlin dike. On the west, a cross-dike should be built to hold back the river. Salt producers, herders, and the counties of Xiaoshan, Shanyin, and Kuaiji should each be assigned their share of the work. " He also petitioned to repair the seawalls of Kuaiji, Shangyu, and neighboring counties; the plan was approved and carried out. In 1819 he was appointed governor-general of Huguang. At Fenghuang and other Hunan garrisons, official encroachment had stripped many military colonists of their allotments; he investigated thoroughly, restored the quotas, and resumed rent collection. Officials entering Miao villages had grown notorious for extortion and impersonation scams; he banned such abuses outright. Finding garrison lands barren yet heavily taxed, he secured reductions of more than twenty thousand piculs in Miao rent and remission of over seventy thousand piculs in arrears, winning deep gratitude among the Miao.
5
道光二年，調四川。 中江覃萬典、犍為道士蕭來修等假神惑眾，捕誅首犯，不坐株連。 九姓長官司不諳吏治，奏請考試，獄訟別由瀘州及州判兼理。 四年，召授工部尚書，調刑部，兼管順天府尹事。 文安縣地形如釜底，自道光初堤防衝決，積水不能耕種，議請急行修築。 七年，命勘湖北京山黃家陵堤工，疏言：「下游災民籥請修治潰堤，上游居民謂口門下游乃襄河故道，復請廢之。 河流經行二百餘年，捨此不由，而別尋二百年以前故道，其說殊謬。 潛江、天門、漢川俱屬下游，而天門、漢川尤當衝要，何忍委之巨浸？ 惟有開通江流，堵合口門，因勢利導。 胡家灣沙洲當下游之衝，以四十餘丈之地束全江之水，下壅上潰，理有必然。 今洲已衝潰，乘勢挑濬新灘，展寬水道，使江流無衝突之患，然後增築京山、鍾祥口門堤壩，再於潰口築石壩二，以護堤攻沙，庶可經久。 」報可。 十二年，乞休歸，卒於途，賜卹。
In 1822 he was transferred to Sichuan. Tan Wandian of Zhongjiang, the Qianwei Daoist Xiao Laixiu, and others had posed as divinities to mislead crowds; Ruolin seized and executed the leaders without punishing their followers by association. The Nine-Surname chieftains were ill-equipped for civil governance; he petitioned for qualifying examinations and for Luzhou and its subprefectural judge to handle their legal cases. In 1824 he was recalled to the capital as minister of works, moved to the Ministry of Justice, and given concurrent charge of the Shuntian metropolitan magistracy. Wen'an lies in a natural basin; since the Daoguang reign began, breached dikes had left fields waterlogged and untilled, and he urged immediate reconstruction. In 1827 he was sent to survey the Huangjialing dike at Jingshan in northern Huguang and reported: "Downstream flood victims pleaded to repair the breach, while upstream residents insisted the channel below the gap was the old Xiang River bed and demanded it be left open." The river has run this way for over two centuries; to abandon the present channel and reopen a two-hundred-year-old bed is absurd. Qianjiang, Tianmen, and Hanchuan all lie downstream, with Tianmen and Hanchuan at the most vulnerable points—how could we condemn them to wholesale flooding? The answer is to clear the main channel, seal the breach, and guide the current along its natural course. The Hujiawan sandbar blocks the lower reach; squeezing the full river through barely forty zhang inevitably dams the flow below and forces breaches upstream. Now that the bar has washed away, he urged dredging the new shoal and widening the channel to end the choke point, then raising the Jingshan and Zhongxiang breach dikes and building two stone dams at the gap to shield the embankments and scour away silt—a durable fix. " The court approved his plan. In 1832 he asked to retire and died on the journey home; the court granted funeral honors.
6
戴三錫，順天大興人，原籍江蘇丹徒。 乾隆五十八年進士，授山西臨縣知縣。 連丁父母憂，嘉慶六年，服闋，發四川，補南充。 歷馬邊、瓘邊兩廳通判，署資州、眉州、卭州，並有政聲。 卭州民黃子賢以治病為名，倡立鴻鈞教，捕治之。 事聞，仁宗命送部引見，擢茂州直隸州知州。 歷寧遠知府、建昌道、四川按察使。 道光二年，遷江寧布政使，迴避本籍，仍調四川。 三年，署總督，五年，實授，兼署成都將軍。
Dai Sanxi was registered in Daxing, Shuntian, though his family hailed from Dantu in Jiangsu. After passing the metropolitan examination in 1793, he was appointed magistrate of Lin County in Shanxi. He mourned both parents in turn; when his mourning ended in 1801 he was sent to Sichuan and posted to Nanchong. He served as subprefect on the Mabian and Fanbian frontier circuits and as acting prefect of Zizhou, Meizhou, and Qiongzhou, earning praise for sound administration wherever he went. When Huang Zixian of Qiongzhou used medical practice as a cover to found the Hongjun sect, Sanxi seized and punished him. The Jiaqing Emperor, hearing of the case, ordered Sanxi to the capital for an audience and promoted him to magistrate of Maozhou. He rose through the posts of Ningyuan prefect, Jianchang circuit intendant, and Sichuan surveillance commissioner. In 1822 he was named Jiangning administration commissioner, but to avoid serving in his home province was sent back to Sichuan. In 1823 he served as acting governor-general; in 1825 he received the full appointment and also acted as regional commander at Chengdu.
7
三錫自牧令洊陟封疆，二十餘年，未離蜀地。 盡心民事，興復通省書院，增設義學三千餘所。 四川舊有義田，積儲備賑，穀多則變價添置良田。 三錫以歲久將膏腴多成官產，留穀太多，又虞霉變虧挪，差定三千至萬石為額。 溢額者出糶，價存司庫，以備兇歲賑恤之用。 又以蜀地惟成都附近俱平疇沃野，餘多山谷磽瘠，遇水沖塞，膏腴轉為砂石，因地制宜，多設渠堰，以資捍衛宣洩。 新都奸民楊守一倡立邪教，造妖書惑眾，擒誅之。 越巂生番劫奪商旅，掠漢民婦女，捕駔黠者數十人置之法，救出被掠男婦，給貲安撫。 屢被詔褒獎。 九年，因年老召來京，署工部侍郎。 尋致仕，未幾，卒。 詔嘉其「宣力有年，官聲素好」，贈尚書銜，依贈銜賜卹。
Sanxi had climbed from county magistrate to frontier governor over more than twenty years without ever leaving Sichuan. He threw himself into civil administration, restored the provincial academy, and founded more than three thousand charity schools across the province. Sichuan's charity estates had long stockpiled grain for famine relief, selling surplus grain to buy more fertile land when stores grew large. Sanxi found that over time the best charity lands had been absorbed as official property, stores had grown excessive, and grain risked spoilage and theft; he capped reserves at three thousand to ten thousand piculs. Any surplus was sold and the proceeds held in the provincial treasury for famine relief. Outside the Chengdu plain, Sichuan is mostly rugged and poor; flash floods could turn fertile soil to gravel, so he built canals and weirs suited to each locality for irrigation and flood control. When the Xindu agitator Yang Shouyi founded a heterodox cult and circulated seditious tracts, Sanxi captured and executed him. Yuexi tribesmen had been robbing travelers and abducting Han women; he arrested dozens of the ringleaders, freed the captives, and compensated them for their losses. The throne repeatedly commended him by edict. In 1829, on account of his age, he was recalled to the capital as acting vice minister of works. He soon retired from office and died shortly afterward. An edict praised his "years of devoted service and consistently fine reputation," posthumously granted him ministerial rank, and awarded funeral honors accordingly.
8
孫爾準，字平叔，江蘇金匱人，廣西巡撫永清子。 嘉慶十年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 十九年，出為福建汀州知府。 寧化民斂錢集會，大吏將治以叛逆。 爾準訊無他狀，論誅首要，鮮所株連。 歷鹽法道、江西按察使，調福建，就遷布政使。 道光元年，調廣東布政使，擢安徽巡撫。 河南邪匪邢名章等糾眾竄潁州，檄按察使惠顯率兵馳剿，格殺名章，殲其餘黨。 蠲緩被災各屬，災甚者賑恤之。 先是有言賑務積弊，毋得以銀折錢，爾準疏其弗便，仍循舊章。
Sun Erzhun, style name Pingshu, came from Jingui in Jiangsu and was the son of Yongqing, former governor of Guangxi. He passed the metropolitan examination in 1805, entered the Hanlin Academy, and was appointed a compiler. In 1814 he was posted as prefect of Tingzhou in Fujian. When Ninghua commoners pooled money for a public gathering, superiors were ready to prosecute them for treason. Erzhun found no evidence of sedition, executed only the ringleaders, and spared most participants. He served as salt-law intendant and Jiangxi surveillance commissioner, returned to Fujian, and was promoted on the spot to administration commissioner. In 1821 he became Guangdong administration commissioner and was soon appointed governor of Anhui. When Henan cultists led by Xing Mingzhang rallied a mob and fled into Yingzhou, Erzhun ordered Surveillance Commissioner Huixian to pursue them, killed Mingzhang in battle, and wiped out his followers. He remitted or deferred taxes in stricken districts and provided direct relief where the damage was worst. When reformers urged that relief funds no longer be paid in silver converted to cash, Erzhun argued the change would do more harm than good and kept the established practice.
9
三年，調福建巡撫。 延、建各屬山徑叢錯，多盜劫，以萬金為緝捕費，連獲賊首置之法，盜風衰息。 巡閱台灣，疏言：「台灣南北袤延千餘裡，初抵鹿耳門，可行舟楫。 嗣增設鹿仔港，而淺狹多沙，內山溪水赴海，別開港在嘉彰間，曰五條港，頗利商船。 又噶瑪蘭山峻路險，負戴難行，其地有烏石港、加禮遠港，可通五六百石小舟，皆宜設為正口。 」
In 1823 he was appointed governor of Fujian. In the rugged Yan and Jian districts, banditry was rampant; he allocated ten thousand taels for pursuit, captured successive ringleaders, and broke the back of the outlaw gangs. While inspecting Taiwan he reported: "The island stretches over a thousand li from north to south; ships once entered through Luermen." Luzai Harbor was later added, but it proved shallow and silty; as mountain streams reached the sea, a new port opened between Jia and Zhang—Wutiao Harbor—better suited to merchant shipping. Gamlan, with its steep trails unsuited to overland transport, has Wushi and Jialiyuan harbors that can take boats of five or six hundred piculs; both should be made official ports. "
10
五年，擢閩浙總督。 奏請噶瑪蘭收入版藉，設官治理。 彰化匪徒械鬥焚劫，旁近蜂起，全台震動，檄水師提督許松年剿捕，副將邵永福等趨艋舺，阻其北竄； 總兵陳化成以兵渡鹿仔，防其入海。 爾準親駐廈門，遣副將佟樞等分往彰化、淡水，搜山圍捕，诇知賊黨煽誘日眾，移陸路提督馬濟勝守廈門，自渡海駐彰化督剿，賊首李通遁，捕得伏誅。 令各莊舉首事，緝餘匪，閩人捕閩人，粵人捕粵人，以免誣累。
In 1825 he was appointed governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. He petitioned to bring Gamlan under regular administration and appoint officials to govern it. When Zhanghua brawlers burned and looted and unrest spread across the island, he ordered Naval Commander Xu Songnian to suppress them while Brigadier Shao Yongfu rushed to Mengjia to block a northern breakout; Major General Chen Huacheng crossed at Luzai with troops to keep them from fleeing by sea. Erzhun took post at Xiamen and sent Brigadier Tong Shu and others into Zhanghua and Tamsui to sweep the hills; learning that rebel ranks were swelling daily, he left Land Route Commander Ma Jisheng at Xiamen, crossed to Zhanghua to direct operations himself, and eventually captured and executed the fugitive leader Li Tong. He had each village elect leaders to hunt down stragglers—Fujianese arresting Fujianese and Cantonese arresting Cantonese—to prevent wrongful accusations across community lines.
11
台人有與生番貿易遂娶番婦者，俗名「番割」，其魁黃斗乃等久踞三灣，潛出為盜。 當亂起時，誘生番出山助鬥，遣參將黃其漢等分路偵擊。 番竄後山，士卒攀藤躡葛而登，擒黃斗乃等二十一人，斬以徇。 爾準疏陳匪徒起事，由於造謠焚掠，非叛逆，當以強盜論； 淡水以北分黨報復，當以械鬥論； 焚殺有據者始坐闢，餘俱末減。 其脅從旋解散者，多所保全。 又奏台灣北路至艋舺幾五百里，僅有守備一員，巡防難週。 調南路游擊一員駐竹塹，並於大甲、銅鑼灣、鬥換坪諸處添駐營汛，改建淡水土城。 頭道溪為生番出入總路，亦建土城，以屯丁駐守。 事平，加太子少保。 七年，入覲，宣宗嘉其治台灣匪亂悉合機宜，迅速蕆功，賜其子慧翼官主事。
Taiwanese who traded with aboriginal tribes and married tribal women were known as "fan'ge"; their leader Huang Dounai had long dominated Sanwan and raided from the hills. When the uprising began they incited aboriginal allies to join the fighting; Erzhun sent Colonel Huang Qihan and others along separate routes to hunt them down. The tribes fled into the back hills; soldiers climbed vine by vine, seized Huang Dounai and twenty-one others, and executed them as an example. Erzhun argued that the unrest stemmed from rumor, arson, and looting—not treason—and should be prosecuted as ordinary brigandage; north of Tamsui, where factions settled scores by force, cases should be treated as armed brawls; only those proved to have burned and killed faced sedition charges; all others received lighter sentences. Many who had been coerced and soon dispersed were spared. He also noted that the northern route from Taiwan to Mengjia ran nearly five hundred li with only one garrison commander—too thin to patrol effectively. He shifted a southern-route brigade to Zhubei, added posts at Dajia, Tongluowan, and Douhuanping, and rebuilt Tamsui's earthen fortifications. At Toudao Creek, the main aboriginal thoroughfare, he built another earthen fort manned by military colonists. When order was restored he was made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. In 1827 he presented himself at court; the Daoguang Emperor praised his Taiwan campaign as timely and decisive and granted his son Huiyi a directorship in the civil service.
12
木蘭陂者，創自宋熙寧間，溉民田四十萬畝，築石堤千一百餘丈以御海潮，歲久傾壞，爾準道經莆田，親勘修復。 工蕆，以宋長樂室女錢創陂實功首，建祠列入祀典。 爾準治閩最久，諳悉其風土人情，吏民皆相習，政從寬大，閩人安之。 九年，坐失察家僕收賄，鐫二級留任。 十一年，以病乞休。 逾年，卒，贈太子太師，賜子慧惇進士，慧翼員外郎，諡文靖，祀福建名宦及鄉賢祠。
The Mulan Barrage, built under the Song emperor Shenzong, had irrigated four hundred thousand mu and been shielded by an eleven-hundred-zhang stone seawall until it fell into ruin; passing through Putian, Erzhun surveyed and restored it himself. When the work was finished, he built a shrine to the Song-era Changle woman surnamed Qian who had first built the barrage and enrolled her in the official register of sacrifices. Having governed Fujian longer than any other post, Erzhun knew its customs intimately; officials and commoners alike were used to him, his rule was lenient, and the Fujianese were content. In 1829 he was demoted two ranks but kept in office for failing to prevent his servants from taking bribes. In 1831 he retired on grounds of illness. He died the following year and was posthumously made Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent; his son Huidun received metropolitan graduate honors, Huiyi was made vice director, his posthumous name was Wenjing, and he was enshrined among Fujian's eminent officials and local worthies.
13
程祖洛，安徽歙縣人。 嘉慶四年進士，授刑部主事，洊遷郎中。 諳練刑名，為仁宗所知。 京察記名道府，久未外簡，以截取銓授甘肅平涼知府。 部臣請留，詔斥規避邊遠，撤銷記名，留部永不外用。 久之，擢內閣學士。 尋授江西按察使，遷湖南布政使，調山東。
Cheng Zuluo came from She County in Anhui. He passed the metropolitan examinations in 1799, was posted as a secretary in the Ministry of Justice, and rose by successive promotions to director. Expert in criminal law and procedure, he came to the Jiaqing Emperor's attention. Listed in the metropolitan review for promotion to circuit-level posts, he went years without a provincial appointment until an exceptional selection made him prefect of Pingliang in Gansu. When ministry officials asked to keep him in Beijing, the throne rebuked him for avoiding remote service, struck his name from the promotion list, and ordered that he remain at the capital for life without provincial appointment. Eventually he was made a Grand Secretariat Bachelor. He was soon made surveillance commissioner of Jiangxi, then financial commissioner of Hunan, and was transferred to Shandong.
14

In 1822 he was made governor of Shaanxi and then transferred to Henan. The rebel Zhu Mazi, a sectarian outlaw, fled from Xincai into Anhui's Fuyang; Cheng caught him and had him executed. For counties bordering Zhili, Shandong, Anhui, and Hubei, where banditry was chronic, he set aside fifty thousand taels from the treasury to invest for interest as a standing fund for police operations. When the Zhang River broke through at Fanmafang in Anyang and shifted northward, the court sent Grand Secretary Dai Junyuan to inspect the breach jointly with local officials. Zuluo toured the river personally and jointly memorialized: "Since 1794, when the Zhang shifted south to join the Huan, it has repeatedly blocked and backed up the Wei, causing floods. Now that the channels have separated, they must not be allowed to merge again. He proposed building earthen dams at the places nearest the Huan above and below Fanmafang and along the breached southern bank, so the two rivers would flow separately and reduce flooding. " By the spring of 1824 the floods had subsided and the terrain was visible again. North of Tianshi, floodwater had been cut off from the ditch and could not be returned to the main channel. He then dug a drainage channel from the low ground at Longjiazhuang upstream, opened a diversion at Majiawa in Neihuang, and reinforced the great dike at Tianjiaying to drive the current southward. After that, the chronic problem of the Zhang and Wei merging was ended. The Heng River at Yucheng, Huimin Ditch, the Baqing River at Xiayi, and Yongcheng's flood-cut channel had long been eastern Henan's main drainage routes; repeated Yellow River silting had left the riverside counties flooded year after year, and he had them all dredged. At first Henan and Anhui had applied the severest penalties to Nian bandits, but the ministry later moved to lighten some provisions. Zuluo memorialized: "Outlaws who form Nian bands and incite raids can muster a crowd at a shout—their conspiracy begins the moment they organize. The new law's distinction based on premeditation creates too many obstacles; I ask that the old statute be restored. He also addressed the articles governing resistance to arrest and punishment of those who made arrests. He further argued: "When a captured thief reveals an older case, officials should not be penalized for earlier failures of supervision. Follow the Jiaqing edicts on this point, so that officials need not look over their shoulders." The throne approved all his proposals.

七年，丁母憂，服闋，署工部侍郎。 尋署湖南巡撫，調江蘇。 十二年，擢閩浙總督。 命查辦浙江鹽務，嚴定裁汰浮費章程，下部議行。 台灣奸民張丙、陳辦等倡亂，命將軍瑚松額督兵進剿，祖洛專治後路軍需。 十三年，提督馬濟勝破賊，張丙等就擒，赴台灣籌辦善後事宜，劾戰守不力之都司週進龍等，褫黜有差。 改營製，增防守。 優敘，賜花翎。 疏陳福建吏治，略曰：「安民必先懲蠹，不可以回護瞻顧而曲縱奸惡。 閩省吏治無子惠之政，而務寬大之名，始因官之庸劣，釀成頑梗之風，今又因民之譸張，遂有疲難之勢。 官曰民刁，民曰吏虐，互相傳播，漸失其真。 官不執法，幕不守法，因而愚民犯法，書役弄法，棍徒玩法。 必先懲不執法之官，然後能治犯法、弄法、玩法之人。 」於是連劾官吏不職者，略無假貸，吏治始肅。 已革縣丞秦師韓京控提督馬濟勝矇奏邀功，並訐祖洛偏袒欺矇，命侍郎趙盛奎偕學攻張鱗按鞫，白其誣，師韓遣戍新疆。 十五年，疏陳閩洋形勢，以漳州之南澳、銅山為籓籬，泉之廈門、金門為門戶，興化之海壇為右翼，閩安為省會咽喉，福寧之銅山為後戶。 巡緝守禦，全資寨城砲台。 就最要者四十四處，由官民捐貲修築。 十六年，丁父憂去官，服闋，引疾不出。 二十八年，卒，宣宗甚惜之，贈太子太保，諡簡敬。
In 1827 he mourned his mother; after the mourning period he served as acting vice minister of Works. He was soon acting governor of Hunan, then transferred to Jiangsu. In 1832 he was made governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. He was ordered to investigate Zhejiang's salt monopoly, drew up strict rules to cut wasteful spending, and had them sent to the ministry for approval. When Zhang Bing, Chen Ban, and other ruffians raised a rebellion in Taiwan, General Husonge was sent to command the campaign while Zuluo handled logistics. In 1833, after Provincial Commander Ma Jisheng defeated the rebels and captured Zhang Bing, Zuluo went to Taiwan to handle reconstruction, impeached negligent commanders including Zhou Jinlong, and demoted them as appropriate. He reformed the garrison system and strengthened coastal defense. He received an exceptional merit citation and was awarded the peacock feather. In a memorial on Fujian governance he wrote in essence: "To pacify the people you must first punish corruption; you cannot wink at wickedness out of favoritism or fear. Fujian administration has no real concern for the people yet prides itself on leniency; mediocre officials first bred obstinacy among the populace, and now popular exaggeration has made governance nearly unmanageable. Officials call the people difficult; the people call the officials cruel—each side repeats the charge until no one can tell truth from rumor. When magistrates will not enforce the law and their secretaries ignore it, commoners break the law in ignorance, yamen clerks twist it, and thugs exploit it. Punish officials who will not enforce the law first, and only then can you deal with those who break, twist, or abuse it." He then impeached a series of incompetent officials without leniency, and administration began to improve. Dismissed assistant magistrate Qin Shihan appealed to Beijing accusing Ma Jisheng of inflating his reports and Zuluo of covering for him; Vice Minister Zhao Shengkui and Hanlin examiner Zhang Lin were sent to investigate, found the charges false, and banished Shihan to Xinjiang. In 1835 he memorialized on Fujian's coastal defenses: Nan'ao and Tongshan off Zhangzhou as the outer barrier, Xiamen and Kinmen as the gates, Haitan off Xinghua as the right flank, Min'an as the capital's throat, and Funing's Tongshan as the rear guard. Coastal patrol and defense depended wholly on stockades and gun batteries. At the forty-four most critical sites, officials and local donors funded repairs. In 1836 he left office to mourn his father; after mourning he pleaded illness and declined further service. He died in 1848; the Daoguang Emperor mourned the loss, posthumously made him Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, and gave him the posthumous name Jianjing.

馬濟勝，山東菏澤人。 以武生入伍，從剿川、陝教匪，積功累擢江蘇撫標參將。 嘉慶十八年，會剿山東教匪，擢河北鎮總兵。 道光初，擢浙江提督，調福建陸路提督。 張丙等倡亂嘉義，台灣鎮總兵劉廷斌困守孤城。 濟勝率兵二千渡海赴援，戰於嘉義城下，大破賊，追至蘋港尾，擒斬甚眾； 進屯鹽水港，分兵搜剿，張丙及其悍黨先後就擒。 時命將軍瑚松額督師猶未至，詔褒成功迅速，賜雙眼花翎。 餘匪萬餘復來犯，俟其怠，擊之大潰，擒頭目賴滿等，追剿盡毀其巢，賊遂平。 宣宗深嘉其謀勇，錫封二等男爵。 又以馭兵安靖，御書「忠勇廉明」四字賜之。 召入覲，年逾七旬，猶壯健，溫詔褒獎，晉二等子爵，在御前侍衛上行走。 十六年，卒於官，贈太子太保，諡昭武，四子皆予官。
Ma Jisheng came from Heze in Shandong. Entering service as a military licentiate, he fought the sectarian rebels in Sichuan and Shaanxi and rose by accumulated merit to colonel in the Jiangsu provincial army. In 1813 he helped suppress the Shandong sectarians and was made commander of the Hebei garrison. Early in the Daoguang reign he was made provincial commander of Zhejiang, then transferred to the Fujian land forces. When Zhang Bing and his followers rebelled at Jiayi, Taiwan Garrison Commander Liu Tingbin was besieged in the city. Jisheng led two thousand men across the strait, routed the rebels below Jiayi, pursued them to Pinggangwei, and killed or captured a great many; advanced to Yanshui Harbor, sent columns to hunt the remainder, and captured Zhang Bing and his chief lieutenants. General Husonge, the designated supreme commander, had not yet arrived; the throne praised Jisheng's speed and awarded him the double-eyed peacock feather. When more than ten thousand holdouts attacked again, he waited until they tired, smashed them in one blow, captured leaders including Lai Man, destroyed their bases in pursuit, and the rebellion was ended. The Daoguang Emperor greatly admired his skill and courage and ennobled him as a second-class baron. For governing his troops with discipline and calm, the emperor wrote him the four characters "Loyal, Brave, Incorrupt, and Clear." Summoned to court at over seventy, still vigorous, he received a warm commendation, was raised to second-class viscount, and was appointed to serve in the Imperial Bodyguard. He died in office in 1836, was posthumously made Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent with the posthumous name Zhaowu, and all four sons received official appointments.

Yu Tai was a Manchu of the Plain Red Banner. A graduate of the Imperial Academy, he passed the examination for Grand Secretariat secretary and was later made reader-in-waiting. Late in the Jiaqing reign he was sent out as intendant of the Cheng-Mian-Long-Mao circuit in Sichuan, then served as surveillance commissioner in Sichuan, Hunan, and Anhui and as financial commissioner in Hunan, Shaanxi, and Anhui. In 1831 he became vice minister of justice at Mukden, moved to the Ministry of Works, and concurrently oversaw the Fengtian prefecture. He surveyed wasteland in the Khorchin banners and memorialized to ban unauthorized cultivation. In 1833 he was recalled to be vice minister of Justice, then sent out as governor of Guizhou. In 1836 rebels rose in Juzhou and Liping; he captured their leader Xu Yugui and others and executed them.

He was transferred to governor of Hunan. When the Zhenbian garrison mutinied, he impeached Commander Xiang Zunhua and Chen-Yuan intendant Chang Qing for negligence and removed them. He wrote: "The Miao frontier colonies that Intendant Fu Nai established in the Jiaqing era, combining farming with garrison duty, were an excellent border policy. Long peace has bred many abuses. The Zhenbian mutiny over borrowed pay has made the Miao watchful. Harsh Miao officials and corrupt colony headmen make future trouble likely. In 1840 he was made governor-general of Huguang. In 1841 the rebel Zhong Renjie rose at Chongyang in Hubei, seized the county seat, and took Tongcheng. Yu Tai hurried to Xianning and ordered Surveillance Commissioner Guo Xiongfei and Commander Yugu to advance against them. Chongyang lies deep in the mountains; the rebels blocked the passes and built fortified stockades. Government forces sent elite troops around their rear while the rebels split off to raid Qibi, were beaten back repeatedly, and held the western ridge of Chongyang as a last stand. Provincial Commander Liu Yunxiao defeated them repeatedly at Shipanshan and Heiqiao, destroyed their strongholds, and captured Renjie and his lieutenants Chen Baoming and Wang Dunzu. Tongcheng was soon retaken and the rebels' families were captured. When order was restored he was made Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent and awarded the double-eyed peacock feather. As British forces advanced up the Yangtze from the sea, the court ordered the recruitment and training of militia for river combat. Yu Tai built six large junks and four fast boats on the Guangdong model, assigned a hundred Hankou sailors to each, and drilled them every ten days. He reduced the old patrol fleet to save funds. When Jingzhou garrison troops repeatedly left camp to cause trouble, he asked that local officials arrest them and report to the general for discipline. Miao raids from Qianzhou were suppressed by force and conciliation until the groups dispersed.

Although Yuanfa was falsely reported dead, his men fled into the mountains, linked up with outlaws along the Guizhou-Guangdong border, and the rebellion spread. Feng Dexin was arrested; Yu Tai alone directed the campaign, coordinating with Guizhou and Guangdong forces to win several victories. In the spring of 1850 government forces swept the mountains, killing and capturing many rebels. The rebels fled to Caoxitang in Yongfu, were surrounded on all sides, and grew desperate. 長齡治黔九載，振興文教，貴陽、銅仁、安順、石阡四府，普安、八寨、郎岱、松桃四廳，黃平、普定，天柱、永從、甕安、清平、興義、普安諸州縣，皆建書院義學； 省會書院分上內外三舍，親試考覈，刊刻經籍，頒行州縣。
During nine years governing Guizhou, Zhangling revived education, founding academies and charity schools in Guiyang, Tongren, Anshun, Shiqian, and many departments and counties across the province; The provincial academy was divided into three halls; he examined students personally, printed classics, and distributed them province-wide.

二十五年，擢雲貴總督，兼署雲南巡撫。 漢、回連歲互鬥，永昌回變敗退後，复圖攻城，城回謀內應，迤西道羅天池悉捕誅之。 長齡親往督剿，擊走叛回，以肅清入告。 二十六年，回眾藉口善良不別，复叛，自請議處，撤銷獎敘，赴大理、永昌督剿。 匪尋竄散，請免投誠張富罪，軍犯王芝異團練出力，亦請釋回。 詔斥其庸懦，降補河南布政使。 二十七年，乞病歸。 滇回复擾雲州，多屬永昌遺孽，且得羅天池濫殺狀，追論長齡，褫職。 逾年，卒。
In 1845 he was made governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou and also acted as Yunnan governor. Han and Hui communities had fought for years; after the Yongchang Muslim rising was beaten back, rebels again plotted to storm the city with inside collaborators until Intendant Luo Tianchi arrested and executed the conspirators. Zhangling went in person to direct operations, drove off the rebels, and reported the region pacified. In 1846 the Muslims rebelled again, claiming innocents had been lumped with guilty; Zhangling asked to be punished, gave up his honors, and went to Dali and Yongchang to command the campaign. The rebels soon scattered; he asked clemency for the surrendered Zhang Fu and for the release of Wang Zhiyi, a military convict who had helped the militia. The throne rebuked his timidity and demoted him to Henan administration commissioner. In 1847 he retired on grounds of illness. When Yunnan Muslims raided Yunzhou—mostly Yongchang holdouts—and Luo Tianchi's mass killings came to light, Zhangling was stripped of office in a formal review. He died the following year.

論曰：陳若霖、戴三錫盡心民事，而三錫久任蜀疆，治效較多。 孫爾準、程祖洛先後治閩有聲，寬嚴殊途，其相濟之道乎？ 裕泰兩殄楚寇，勳施爛然。 賀長齡儒而不武，不足以奠岩疆也。
The historians remark: Chen Ruolin and Dai Sanxi both devoted themselves to civil administration, but Sanxi's long tenure in Sichuan produced the greater record. Sun Erzhun and Cheng Zuluo both governed Fujian with distinction—one lenient, one strict—were their different methods complementary? Yu Tai twice crushed rebellions in Huguang, his achievements conspicuous. He Zhangling was a scholar rather than a soldier, ill-suited to hold a troubled frontier.
