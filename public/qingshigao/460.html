2
左寶貴弟寶賢等永山鄧世昌劉步蟾林泰曾等戴宗騫
Zuo Baogui; his younger brothers Bao Xian and others; Yong Shan; Deng Shichang; Liu Buchan; Lin Taiceng and others; Dai Zongqian
3
左寶貴，字冠廷，山東費人。 咸豐初，隸江南軍。 嘗令當前敵，陣既接，旗兵中砲，殪，寶貴持其幟衝鋒入，大捷，繇是知名。 獲苗沛霖，克金陵，頻有功。 後以游擊從僧格林沁討捻，積勳至副將。 光緒初，尚書崇實巡視奉天、吉林，奏自隨。 既至，斬高希珍於土門，誅宋三好於石砬子。 邊外東北廟溝金宮四構黨圖大舉，复捕治之，餘燼悉平，賜號鏗色巴圖魯，晉記名提督。 授高州鎮總兵，仍留奉天。 平朝陽教匪，賞黃馬褂、雙眼花翎，駐瀋陽。
Zuo Baogui, whose style name was Guanting, was a native of Fei in Shandong. In the early Xianfeng era he joined the Jiangnan Army. On one occasion he was ordered to take the front rank against the enemy. When the lines met, the standard-bearer was killed by artillery fire. Baogui seized the flag and led a charge, winning a decisive victory and making his name known from that day on. He captured Miao Peilin, helped take Nanjing, and won distinction again and again. He later served as a guerrilla officer under Sengge Rinchen in campaigns against the Nian rebels, rising through accumulated merit to deputy commander. Early in the Guangxu reign, Minister Chongshi toured Fengtian and Jilin and had Baogui accompany him at his own request. Once they arrived, he beheaded Gao Xizhen at Tumen and executed Song Sanhao at Shi'azi. In the borderlands northeast of the passes, conspirators at Miaogou, Jingong, and Sigou planned a major uprising; he suppressed them as well until every last remnant was extinguished. He received the honorific title Kense Batür and was entered on the rolls for promotion to grand coordinator. He was appointed commander of the Gaozhou garrison while continuing to serve in Fengtian. He put down the sectarian rebels in Chaoyang, was awarded the yellow riding jacket and double-eyed peacock feathers, and was garrisoned at Shenyang.
4
二十年，朝鮮亂起，日本進兵。 朝議既決戰，衛汝貴、馬玉昆、丰紳阿各率所部往御之，寶貴自奉天來會，是為四大軍。 慮海道梗，乃繞道自遼東行，渡鴨綠江入平壤。 是時葉志超虛飾戰胜狀，電李鴻章入告，遂拜總統諸軍命。 於是汝貴、玉昆軍南門外大同江，志超部將江自康軍北門外小山，寶貴任城守。 未止舍，日軍猝至，寶貴與丰紳阿擊卻之。 敵退龍岡，分道來攻，又敗之。 志超乃聚全軍為嬰城計。
In 1894 unrest erupted in Korea and Japan marched troops in. After the court resolved on war, Wei Rugui, Ma Yukun, and Fengshen'a each marched their forces to meet the invaders. Baogui came down from Fengtian to join them, forming what were called the four great armies. Fearing the sea lanes would be cut, they marched overland through Liaodong, crossed the Yalu, and entered Pyongyang. At that time Ye Zhichao had inflated reports of victory and wired Li Hongzhang; on that basis he was appointed supreme commander of the allied forces. Wei Rugui and Ma Yukun held the southern approaches along the Taedong River, Ye Zhichao's lieutenant Jiang Zikang held the hill north of the city, and Baogui was assigned to hold the walls. They had barely encamped when Japanese troops struck; Baogui and Fengshen'a beat them off. The enemy fell back to Longgang and attacked again along several routes, only to be defeated once more. Zhichao then concentrated the whole army on a purely defensive, hold-the-city strategy.
5
時寶貴扼玄武門，日軍大隊至。 志超將潰圍北歸，寶貴不從，以兵守志超勿令逸。 寶貴狃於捕馬賊之功，頗輕敵。 日軍轝砲散置山巔，諜者以告，若弗聞。 登城指麾，中砲踣，猶能言，及城下，始殞。 其部將負屍開城走，遇日軍，又棄之，於是諸軍皆潰。 事聞，贈太子少保，諡忠壯，予騎都尉兼一云騎尉世職，子國楫襲。
Baogui was holding the Xuanwu Gate when a major Japanese column arrived. Zhichao intended to break out northward, but Baogui refused and stationed men to keep him from slipping away. Success against mounted bandits had made Baogui overconfident, and he tended to underestimate the foe. Japanese field guns were deployed on the heights; scouts reported it, yet he paid no heed. He climbed the wall to command the fight, was hit by shellfire and fell; he could still speak, but died only after being carried below the wall. His officers bore his body out through the opened gates, met Japanese troops, and abandoned the corpse; the armies then broke and fled. On report of his death he was posthumously made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent, honored with the posthumous name Zhongzhuang, granted a hereditary Commandant of Cavalry plus one Yunqiwei, and his son Guoji succeeded to the rank.
6
弟寶賢、寶清先後於直隸、奉天剿匪陣亡。
His younger brothers Bao Xian and Bao Qing were killed in turn fighting bandits in Zhili and Fengtian.
7
永山，袁氏，漢軍正白旗人，黑龍江駐防，吉林將軍富明阿子，黑龍江將軍壽山弟。 以廕授侍衛，歸東三省練軍。 中日戰起，從將軍依克唐阿軍，率黑龍江騎旅駐摩天嶺。 永山臨敵輒深入，為士卒先。 與日軍戰數有功，連殲其將。 既克龍灣，乘勝渡草河，規鳳凰，依克唐阿策襲其城，檄永山為軍鋒，偕壽山分率馬步隊深入攻之。 抵一面山，距城八里，張左右翼，各據一坡以待。 永山為右翼，尤得地勢。 敵作散隊，伍伍什什冒死前，復以大隊橫沖我左翼。 左翼潰，右翼亦不支，乃相繼退。 永山獨為殿，遇伏，連受槍傷，洞胸踣，復強起督戰，大呼殺賊而逝。 事聞，諡壯愍，予建祠奉天。
Yong Shan, of the Yuan clan, belonged to the Plain White Banner of the Hanjun and was stationed in Heilongjiang; he was the son of Jilin General Fuming'a and younger brother of Heilongjiang General Shoushan. He entered service by hereditary appointment as an imperial bodyguard and joined the training army of the Three Eastern Provinces. When war with Japan began he served under General Yiketang'a, commanding the Heilongjiang cavalry brigade at Motian Ridge. In battle Yong Shan always pushed far ahead, leading from the front. He fought the Japanese repeatedly with success and killed several of their commanders in succession. After Longwan fell he crossed the Cao River in pursuit and set his sights on Fengcheng; Yiketang'a devised a surprise on the city and named Yong Shan vanguard, while he and Shoushan led horse and foot in a deep strike. They reached Yimian Hill eight li from the city, formed left and right wings, and each wing held a hill to await attack. Yong Shan held the right wing, which enjoyed the better terrain. The Japanese came on in open skirmish lines, small groups pressing forward at all costs, then threw a large force in a frontal rush against our left. The left wing broke; the right could not stand, and both wings fell back one after another. Yong Shan alone covered the retreat, walked into an ambush, and took repeated gunfire; a shot through the chest dropped him, yet he rose again to command, crying to slay the foe until he expired. On report of his death he received the posthumous name Zhuangmin and a shrine was ordered erected in Fengtian.
8
鄧世昌，字正卿，廣東番禺人。 少有幹略，嘗從西人習布算術。 既長，入水師學堂，精測量、駕駛。 光緒初，管海東雲艦，徼循海口。 日本窺台灣，扼澎湖、基隆諸隘，補千總，調管振威艦。 以捕海盜，遷守備。 李鴻章治海軍，高其能，調北洋。 從丁汝昌赴英購鐵艦，益詳練海戰術。 八年，朝鮮內亂，復從汝昌泊仁川，為吳長慶陸軍後距。 事寧，遷游擊，賜號勃勇巴圖魯。 管揚威快艦，往來天津、朝鮮； 冬寒冰沍，巡視台、廈海防。 尋充經遠、致遠、靖遠、濟遠四船營務處，兼致遠管帶。
Deng Shichang, whose style name was Zhengqing, was a native of Panyu in Guangdong. As a youth he showed talent and initiative and had studied Western mathematics with foreigners. When he came of age he entered the naval academy and mastered surveying and ship handling. Early in the Guangxu reign he commanded the patrol vessel Hai Dongyun on coastal duty. When Japan cast its eye on Taiwan and seized Penghu and Keelung, he was promoted to company commander and given the Zhenwei. He was promoted to garrison commander for capturing pirates. Li Hongzhang, building the navy, prized his ability and moved him to the Beiyang Fleet. He accompanied Ding Ruchang to England to buy ironclads and grew thoroughly versed in naval warfare. In 1882, when Korea was torn by internal strife, he again followed Ruchang to Inchon as naval cover for Wu Changqing's army ashore. After order was restored he was made guerrilla officer and granted the title Boyong Batür. He took command of the fast cruiser Yangwei and shuttled between Tianjin and Korea; in winter, when the seas froze, he inspected the coastal defenses of Taiwan and Xiamen. He was soon made depot officer for the Jingyuan, Zhiyuan, Jingyuan, and Jiyuan and given command of the Zhiyuan as well.
9
十四年，台灣生番畔，以副將從汝昌往討。 戰埤南，毀其碉寨，擢總兵。 時定海軍經制，借補中軍副將，而以汝昌為提督，其左右翼總兵則閩人林泰曾、劉步蟾也。 汝昌故不習海戰，威令不行。 獨世昌以粵人任管駕，非時不登岸，閩人咸嫉之。
In 1888, when Taiwan's indigenous peoples rose, he went as deputy commander under Ruchang to subdue them. At Beinan he destroyed their fortifications and was promoted to commander. When the navy's permanent establishment was set, he was lent in as central squadron deputy commander; Ruchang became admiral, with the Fujian natives Lin Taiceng and Liu Buchan as wing commanders. Ruchang had never mastered naval combat and could not enforce discipline. Shichang alone, a Cantonese in charge of sailing, scarcely left his ship except when he must, and the Fujian officers resented him.
10
二十年夏，日侵朝，絕海道。 鴻章令濟遠、廣乙兩船赴牙山，遇日艦，先擊，廣乙受殊傷； 轟濟遠，都司沈壽昌，守備楊建章、黃承勳中砲死。 濟遠逃，日艦追之，管帶方柏謙豎白幟，追益亟，有水手發砲擊之，折日艦了樓，柏謙虛張胜狀，退塞威海東西兩口。 世昌憤欲進兵，汝昌尼其行，不果。 已而日艦集大連灣，窺金州，我國海軍乃大發，泊鴨綠江大東溝，以鐵艦十當敵艦十有二。 汝昌乘定遠居中，列諸船左右張兩翼。 日艦魚貫進，據上風，汝昌令轟擊，距遠不能中。 日艦小，運棹靈，倏分倏合，彈雨坌集，定遠被震，大纛僕。 世昌見帥旗沒，慮軍心搖，亟取致遠纛豎之。 戰良久，定遠擊沉其西京丸，我之超勇毀焉。
In the summer of 1894 Japan invaded Korea and severed the sea lanes. Li Hongzhang sent the Jiyuan and Guangyi to Asan; they met Japanese ships and fired first; the Guangyi was badly mauled; the Jiyuan was shelled; Battalion Commander Shen Shouchang and garrison commanders Yang Jianzhang and Huang Chengxun were killed by gunfire. The Jiyuan fled with Japanese ships in pursuit; her commander Fang Boyian ran up a white flag, yet when pursuit intensified a sailor fired and damaged a Japanese superstructure; Boyian reported a false victory and withdrew to seal Weihai's eastern and western entrances. Shichang burned to attack, but Ruchang held him back and the chance was lost. Soon Japanese squadrons massed at Dalian Bay and reconnoitered Jinzhou; the Chinese fleet sailed out in strength and anchored at Dadonggou on the Yalu with ten ironclads against twelve Japanese ships. Ruchang took the Dingyuan at the center and drew the fleet up in wings to either side. Japanese ships came on in column with the weather gauge; Ruchang ordered fire, but at long range the shots fell short. Japanese vessels were smaller and handier; they divided and re-formed at will; shot poured in; the Dingyuan shuddered and her mainmast banner fell. Seeing the admiral's flag down, Shichang feared morale would crack and at once hoisted the Zhiyuan's banner in its place. After hours of combat the Dingyuan sank the Japanese Saikyō Maru, but our Chaoyong was lost.
11
世昌乘致遠，最猛鷙，與日艦吉野浪速相當，吉野，日艦之中堅也。 戰既酣，致遠彈將罄，世昌誓死敵。 將士知大勢敗，陣稍亂，世昌大呼曰：「今日有死而已！ 然雖死而海軍聲威弗替，是即所以報國也！ 」眾乃定。 世昌遂鼓輪怒駛，欲猛觸吉野與同盡，中其魚雷，鍋船裂沉。 世昌身環氣圈不沒，汝昌及他將見之，令馳救。 拒弗上，縮臂出圈，死之。 其副游擊陳金揆同殉，全船二百五十人無逃者。 經遠管帶總兵林永昇、超勇管帶參將黃建寅、揚威管帶參將林履中並殞於陣。
Shichang fought from the Zhiyuan, the most aggressive ship in the fleet, pitted against the Japanese Yoshino and Naniwa; the Yoshino was the core of the enemy line. When the fight was hottest the Zhiyuan's shells were almost gone; Shichang vowed to die with the foe. The men knew the day was lost and the line wavered; Shichang roared: "Today we have only death! Yet if we die, let the navy's honor stand unbroken—that is how we serve the country!" The crew steadied at that. Shichang ordered full ahead to ram the Yoshino and die with her; a torpedo hit him, the boiler ruptured, and the ship went down. An air pocket kept Shichang afloat; Ruchang and other officers saw it and sent a boat. He refused to come aboard, slipped free of the bubble, and drowned. His second-in-command Chen Jinkui died beside him; not one of the ship's 250 men survived. Commander Lin Yongsheng of the Jingyuan, Staff Captain Huang Jianyin of the Chaoyong, and Staff Captain Lin Lüzhong of the Yangwei all died in action.
12
事聞，世昌諡壯節，餘皆優恤。 世昌既死，諸船或沉或逃，遂不復成軍。 世昌臨戰以忠義相激勵，死狀尤烈，世與左寶貴並稱雙忠云。 永昇等，忠義有傳。
On report of the battle Shichang received the posthumous name Zhuangjie; the others were all handsomely compensated. After Shichang fell the ships sank or ran; the fleet never fought as a unit again. In battle Shichang had stirred his men with talk of loyalty; his death was especially heroic; posterity paired him with Zuo Baogui as the "twin martyrs." Yongsheng and the rest have separate accounts of their loyalty.
13
劉步蟾，侯官人。 幼穎異，肄業福建船政學堂，卒業試第一。 隸建威船，徼循南北洋資實練。 同治十一年，會考閩、廣駕駛生，复冠其曹。 自是巡歷海岸河港，所蒞輒用​​西法測量。 台灣地勢、番部風土尤諳習，為圖說甚晰。 光緒改元，赴歐學槍砲、水雷諸技，還留福建，敘守備。 以丁寶楨、李鴻章論薦，擢游擊，會辦北洋操防。 十一年，赴德國購定遠艦。 維時海軍初立，借才異地，西人實為管帶，步蟾副之。 已而西人去，頗能舉其職。 十四年，以參將赴歐領四快船歸，遷副將，賜號強勇巴圖魯，擢右翼總兵。
Liu Buchan was a native of Houguan. As a boy he was unusually gifted; he entered the Fujian Naval Academy and graduated at the head of his class. He was assigned to the Jianwei and cruised the northern and southern seas for hands-on experience. In 1872 he took the joint examination of Fujian and Guangdong navigation cadets and again topped the list. Thereafter on every survey of coast, river, and port he applied Western surveying methods. He knew Taiwan's geography and aboriginal regions especially well and produced exceptionally clear illustrated reports. At the start of the Guangxu reign he studied artillery and mines in Europe; returning, he stayed in Fujian with the rank of garrison commander. Recommended by Ding Baozhen and Li Hongzhang, he was raised to guerrilla officer and assigned to Beiyang training and coastal defense. In 1885 he went to Germany to take delivery of the Dingyuan. The navy was new and relied on foreign expertise; Westerners nominally commanded while Buchan was second. Once the foreigners departed he performed his duties capably. In 1888, as staff captain, he fetched four fast cruisers from Europe; he was made deputy commander, granted Qiangyong Batür, and appointed right-wing commander.
14
二十年，中日戰起，海軍浮泊大東溝。 日艦至，督攝諸藝士御之，鏖戰三時許，沉敵艦三艘，運送銘軍八營，得以乘間登岸。 論功，晉記名提督，易其勇號曰格洪額。 明年，戰威海，中彈死。 步蟾通西學，海軍規制多出其手。 顧喜引用鄉人，視統帥丁汝昌蔑如也，時論責其不能和衷，致僨事。 然華人明海戰術，步蟾為最先，雖敗挫，殺敵甚眾。 上嘉其忠烈，詔優恤。
In 1894 war with Japan began; the fleet lay at Dadonggou. When Japanese ships appeared he directed the gun crews; after some three hours of bitter fighting three enemy vessels were sunk, and eight battalions of the Ming army were landed in the lull. For this service he was entered for grand coordinator and his honorific was changed to Gehong'e. The following year, in the battle for Weihai, he was killed by gunfire. Buchan mastered Western naval science; much of the fleet's organization was his work. Yet he favored fellow provincials and treated Admiral Ding Ruchang with contempt; critics blamed his failure to cooperate for the fleet's ruin. Among Chinese versed in naval warfare, however, Buchan stood first; though beaten, his guns inflicted heavy losses. The emperor honored his loyalty and ordered special compensation.
15
其左翼總兵林泰曾，亦籍侯官，同為船政學堂卒業生。 管鎮遠，戰大東溝，發砲敏捷，士卒用命，撲救火彈甚力，機營砲位無少損，賜號霍春助巴圖魯。 駛還威海，艦觸礁受傷，憤恨蹈海死。 副將左翼中營游擊楊用霖、廣東大鵬協右營守備黃祖蓮並殉焉。 優恤各如製。 祖蓮等，忠義有傳。
Left-wing commander Lin Taiceng was likewise a Houguan native and a Naval Academy graduate alongside Buchan. Commanding the Zhenyuan at Dadonggou, he fought with quick gunnery, disciplined crews, and tireless firefighting; his engine-room batteries were untouched, and he received the title Huochunzhu Batür. Sailing back to Weihai his ship hit a reef; in shame and fury he drowned himself. Deputy Commander Yang Yonglin and garrison commander Huang Zulian of Dapeng perished with him. Each was compensated according to statute. Huang Zulian and the rest have separate accounts of their loyalty.
16
戴宗騫，字孝侯，安徽壽州人。 少以廩生治鄉團，捻酋苗沛霖數陷州，宗騫潛結各圩寨以攜貳其黨。 同治初，謁李鴻章，上平捻十策，深器之，遂留參戎幕，積勳至知縣。 十一年，治南運河堤工。 時畿輔興水利，計臣慮餉詘，議裁兵。 宗騫上書，略謂：「津沽為九河故道，漳、衛交匯，水菑衍溢。 宜闢減河洩其勢，澌枝河分其漲，俾淮、練軍治之，則兵農合一，事半而功倍。 」鴻章以其議上聞，遂命董其役，成稻田六萬餘畝。 箸海上屯田志紀其事。
Dai Zongqian, whose style name was Xiaohou, was a native of Shouzhou in Anhui. As a young stipend scholar he raised local militia; when Nian leader Miao Peilin repeatedly seized Shouzhou, Zongqian secretly rallied the stockaded villages to turn Miao's men against one another. Early in the Tongzhi era he visited Li Hongzhang with ten plans to crush the Nian; Hongzhang prized him, kept him on staff, and promoted him to magistrate through accumulated service. In 1872 he oversaw diking on the South Grand Canal. As Zhili pushed waterworks, budget officials feared short funds and proposed troop reductions. Zongqian memorialized in substance: "The Tianjin area lies in the old bed of the Nine Rivers; where the Zhang and Wei converge, floods spread without check. Relief rivers should be cut to bleed off pressure and branch channels to split the rise, with Huai and Lian troops doing the work—soldiers and farmers together, half the effort for twice the gain." Hongzhang sent the plan up and put Zongqian in charge; more than sixty thousand mu of rice land were brought under cultivation. He recorded the project in his Haishang Tuntian Zhi (Gazetteer of Coastal Colonization).
17
光緒六年，中俄失和，吳大澂被命佐吉林邊務，奏宗騫自隨。 大澂兼攝屯政，宗騫為治道路，築砲台，設江防，徙直、東流民，假予產業，分部護之。 塞外灌莽千里，馬賊為民患，宗騫曰：「此屯政蠹也！ 」率將士步馳八九百里，獲渠率王林等駢誅之。 又以緣邊荒墾，戶籍殘耗，客民渙居不相顧，因令屯聚一處，略仿內地保甲，杜絕姦宄。 復設製造局、採金廠，行之期年，商民輻湊。 大澂上其績狀，遷知府。
In 1880, when relations with Russia soured, Wu Dacheng was assigned to Jilin border duty and asked that Zongqian accompany him. Dacheng also oversaw colonization; Zongqian built roads and forts, organized river defense, resettled migrants from Zhili and the northeast, lent them land, and stationed guards by district. A thousand li of wilderness bred mounted bandits; Zongqian declared: "This is the plague of frontier settlement! " He marched eight or nine hundred li, seized ringleader Wang Lin and his gang, and executed them in a batch. With frontier registers thin and settlers scattered, he gathered them into compact settlements on the interior baojia model to suppress crime. He founded arsenals and gold works; within a year trade and population surged. Dacheng reported his record and he was promoted to prefect.
18
八年，徙防洋、蒲河兩海口。 遭母憂歸，鴻章疏留，宗騫請終制，弗許。 時興海軍，練水師，闢軍港，檄防威海。 十三年，詣軍所，壁金線頂山，分鞏軍駐南岸，綏軍駐北岸。 明年，建兩岸海台各三，南曰趙北嘴、鹿角嘴、龍廟嘴，北曰北山嘴、黃泥崖、祭祀台。 後路分築陸台四，南岸口較闊，更建日島地阱砲台，屹然為東防重鎮。 十七年，校閱海軍禮成，論功晉道員。 威海地瘠，士氣衰，更斥貲立義塾，延名師，至是始聞謳誦聲。
In 1882 he was shifted to guard the Yang and Pu river mouths. On his mother's death he went home to mourn; Hongzhang asked the court to keep him in service; Zongqian pleaded to observe full mourning but was refused. As the navy was built, sailors trained, and a naval base opened, he was ordered to Weihai's defense. In 1887 he took command, fortified Jinxian Peak, and posted the Gong army south and the Sui army north. The following year he built three shore batteries on each side—south: Zhaobeizui, Lujiaozui, Longmiaozui; north: Beishanzui, Huangniya, Jisitai. Four rear land forts followed; where the southern channel was wider he added Rizhao Island's pit battery, making Weihai the eastern stronghold. After the 1891 naval review he was promoted to circuit intendant for merit. Weihai was poor and spiritless; he funded a charity school and hired noted teachers until recitation was heard in the streets.
19
二十年夏，日艦來攻，率師御之，傷其艦四艘，再至再敗之。 既而旅順、大連相繼淪沒，威海勢益孤，電請北洋、山東益師，久弗應。 其冬，連失文登、寧海。 時宗騫守北岸，分統劉超佩守南岸，宗騫與約，寇至互相應。 歲除，大風雪，戰橋頭集，綏軍大困，銳身救之出。
In the summer of 1894 Japanese ships attacked; he met them twice, damaging four vessels and beating them off each time. Then Lüshun and Dalian fell; Weihai stood alone; he wired Beiyang and Shandong for troops but waited in vain. That winter Wendeng and Ninghai fell one after another. Zongqian held the north bank while Liu Chaopei held the south; they pledged mutual support when attacked. On New Year's Eve, in blizzard and battle at Qiaotouji, the Sui army was trapped and he charged in to pull them out.
20
逾歲，日軍至，輒敗去，折而南。 宗騫往援，而超佩蹌踉遁，三台拱手讓敵，反訴巡撫李秉衡，誣宗騫背約。 宗騫抗辯，願復三台贖罪。 乃募敢死士奪還二台，唯龍廟嘴未復。 日軍倏大集，二台仍不守，宗騫退歸，登祭祀台。 所部卒譁變，宗騫佯弗省，行數武，槍齊發，材官追斬一人，眾散走。 宗騫既登，乃無一從者。 夜宿藥庫，丁汝昌詣籌戰守策，宗騫曰：「綏、鞏軍已西去，孤台危棘，恐資敵。 」汝昌令毀台，強掖之下。 宗騫念南北各有地阱台，此其勢尚可為，乃詣劉公島就副將張德山。 德山無戰守志，宗騫飲金死，威海師遂熸。 鴻章以死事聞，詔優恤。 復以秉衡請，贈太常寺卿。
When the new year came Japanese troops appeared, were beaten back, then swung south. Zongqian hurried to help, but Chaopei fled; three forts were handed over intact; Chaopei then told Governor Li Bingheng that Zongqian had broken their pact. Zongqian rebutted the charge and vowed to recover the three forts as penance. He raised suicide squads and retook two forts; only Longmiaozui remained in enemy hands. The Japanese concentrated in strength; the two forts fell again; Zongqian fell back to Jisitai. His men mutinied; he feigned ignorance; a few steps on, rifles crashed; a sergeant cut down one ringleader and the rest fled. On the rampart he stood utterly alone. That night in the magazine Ding Ruchang sought plans; Zongqian said: "The Sui and Gong forces have marched west; these lone forts are doomed and will only arm the foe. " Ruchang ordered the works blown and had him dragged down by force. Remembering pit batteries on both shores, he crossed to Liugong Island to seek Deputy Commander Zhang Deshan. Deshan would neither fight nor hold; Zongqian took poison; Weihai's land defense was finished. Hongzhang reported him killed in duty and the court ordered special compensation. At Bingheng's request he was posthumously made Vice Minister of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices.
21
論曰：中東之戰，陸軍皆遁，寶貴獨死平壤； 海軍皆降，世昌獨死東溝。 中外傳其壯節，並稱「雙忠」。 及日兵入奉，永山獨死鳳城，敵遂長驅進矣。 旅、大既失，威海勢孤，步蟾、宗騫皆先後誓死。 士氣如此，豈遂不可一戰？ 此主兵者之責。 五人雖敗，猶有榮焉！
The historian remarks: In the Sino-Japanese War every land army ran, yet Baogui alone died at Pyongyang; every naval unit surrendered, yet Shichang alone died at Dadonggou. At home and abroad their deaths were hailed as heroic; together they were called the "twin martyrs." When the Japanese entered Fengtian, Yong Shan alone fell at Fengcheng—and the enemy marched on unopposed. With Lüshun and Dalian gone, Weihai was cut off; Buchan and Zongqian each pledged his life. Morale like this—was there truly no fight left in them? The fault lay with those who held the armies' command. These five men were beaten, yet their honor stands!
