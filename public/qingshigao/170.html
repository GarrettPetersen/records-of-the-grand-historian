Third-rank Earl Who Assists DiligenceOrtai (Manchu Bordered Blue Banner). In the twelfth month of the fifth year of Yongzheng military merit made him first-rank Commandant of Chariots; a longevity amnesty in the first month made him third-rank baron; in the second month of the tenth year merit pacifying the Miao won first-rank earl; in the seventh month of the thirteenth year, when the various Miao rebelled, he was demoted to third-rank baron, but in the eighth month was additionally granted first-rank Commandant of Chariots and combined as first-rank viscount. Third-rank Earl Who Proclaims DiligenceZhang Tingyu (native of Tongcheng, Jiangnan).
