奉天將軍寧古塔將軍黑龍江將軍右衛將軍 Fengtian General Ningguta General Heilongjiang General General of the Right Guard

康熙元年壬寅，設鎭守遼東等處將軍、鎭守寧古塔等處將軍。自鎭守盛京昂邦將軍爲鎭守遼東等處將軍。順治十六年以昂邦將軍駐防，是年陞鎭守寧古塔等處將軍。 In Kangxi 1 (1662), year renyin, the posts of General for Garrisoning Liaodong and Other Places and General for Garrisoning Ningguta and Other Places were created. The Shengjing garrison Angbang General was redesignated General for Garrisoning Liaodong and Other Places. He had been stationed as Angbang General since Shunzhi 16; in that same year he was promoted to General for Garrisoning Ningguta and Other Places.

康熙二年癸卯 Kangxi 2 (1663), year guimao

康熙三年甲辰 Kangxi 3 (1664), year jiachen

康熙四年乙巳五月辛巳，鎭守遼東等處將軍。六月己未改鎭守奉天等處將軍。 Kangxi 4 (1665), year yisi In the fifth month, on day xinsi, he was made General for Garrisoning Liaodong and Other Places; in the sixth month, on day jiwei, the title was changed to General for Garrisoning Fengtian and Other Places.

康熙五年丙午 Kangxi 5 (1666), year bingwu

康熙六年丁未 Kangxi 6 (1667), year dingwei

康熙七年戊申九月庚子爲奉天將軍。 Kangxi 7 (1668), year wushen In the ninth month, on day gengzi, he was appointed Fengtian General.

康熙八年己酉 Kangxi 8 (1669), year jiyou

康熙九年庚戌六月己酉爲奉天將軍。 Kangxi 9 (1670), year gengxu In the sixth month, on day jiyou, he was appointed Fengtian General.

康熙十年辛亥 Kangxi 10 (1671), year xinhai

康熙十一年壬子 Kangxi 11 (1672), year renzi

康熙十二年癸丑卒，予祭葬。十二月乙丑以正黃旗副都統爲奉天將軍。 Kangxi 12 (1673), year guichou He died and was granted state funeral rites and burial honors. In the twelfth month, on day yichou, a vice commander-in-chief of the Plain Yellow Banner was appointed Fengtian General.

康熙十三年甲寅 Kangxi 13 (1674), year jiayin

康熙十四年乙卯 Kangxi 14 (1675), year yimao

康熙十五年丙辰 Kangxi 15 (1676), year bingchen

康熙十六年丁巳 Kangxi 16 (1677), year dingsi

康熙十七年戊午七月甲寅解任。爲奉天將軍。 Kangxi 17 (1678), year wuwu In the seventh month, on day jiayin, he was relieved of his post; another officer was appointed Fengtian General.

康熙十八年己未 Kangxi 18 (1679), year jiwei

康熙十九年庚申 Kangxi 19 (1680), year gengshen

康熙二十年辛酉 Kangxi 20 (1681), year xinyou

康熙二十一年壬戌 Kangxi 21 (1682), year renxu

康熙二十二年癸亥，九月設黑龍江將軍。正月辛巳爲奉天將軍。罷。八月庚子，爲寧古塔將軍。十月癸亥爲黑龍江將軍。 Kangxi 22 (1683), year guihai: in the ninth month the post of Heilongjiang General was created. In the first month, on day xinsi, he was appointed Fengtian General. He was dismissed. In the eighth month, on day gengzi, he was appointed Ningguta General. In the tenth month, on day guihai, he was appointed Heilongjiang General.

康熙二十三年甲子 Kangxi 23 (1684), year jiazi

康熙二十四年乙丑十一月辛未遷。貝勒十二月庚寅爲奉天將軍。 Kangxi 24 (1685), year yichou In the eleventh month, on day xinwei, he was transferred. In the twelfth month, on day gengyin, a beile was appointed Fengtian General.

康熙二十五年丙寅 Kangxi 25 (1686), year bingyin

康熙二十六年丁卯 Kangxi 26 (1687), year dingmao

康熙二十七年戊辰九月卒。十月庚戌爲奉天將軍。 Kangxi 27 (1688), year wuchen He died in the ninth month. In the tenth month, on day gengxu, another officer was appointed Fengtian General.

康熙二十八年己巳五月壬戌革。乙亥，爲寧古塔將軍。 Kangxi 28 (1689), year jisi In the fifth month, on day renxu, he was dismissed; on day yihai he was appointed Ningguta General.

康熙二十九年庚午移駐墨爾根。 Kangxi 29 (1690), year gengwu His headquarters were moved to Mergen.

康熙三十年辛未 Kangxi 30 (1691), year xinwei

康熙三十一年壬申 Kangxi 31 (1692), year renshen

康熙三十二年癸酉爲安北將軍，駐歸化城。 Kangxi 32 (1693), year guiyou He was appointed General Pacifying the North and stationed at Guihuacheng.

康熙三十三年甲戌 Kangxi 33 (1694), year jiaxu

康熙三十四年乙亥十月授右衛將軍，仍兼管歸化城將軍事。 Kangxi 34 (1695), year yihai In the tenth month he was appointed General of the Right Guard while still concurrently overseeing the duties of the Guihuacheng general.

康熙三十五年丙子六月癸丑革。七月己巳爲寧古塔將軍。 Kangxi 35 (1696), year bingzi In the sixth month, on day guichou, he was dismissed; in the seventh month, on day jisi, he was appointed Ningguta General.

康熙三十六年丁丑 Kangxi 36 (1697), year dingchou

康熙三十七年戊寅 Kangxi 37 (1698), year wuyin

康熙三十八年己卯四月戊午革。四月壬午爲奉天將軍。移駐齊齊哈爾。 Kangxi 38 (1699), year jimao In the fourth month, on day wuwu, he was dismissed; on day renwu he was appointed Fengtian General. His headquarters were moved to Qiqihar.

康熙三十九年庚辰 Kangxi 39 (1700), year gengchen

康熙四十年辛巳二月乙丑遷。爲寧古塔將軍。二月己未革。爲黑龍江將軍。 Kangxi 40 (1701), year xinsi In the second month, on day yichou, he was transferred and appointed Ningguta General. In the second month, on day jiwei, he was dismissed and another officer was appointed Heilongjiang General.

康熙四十一年壬午 Kangxi 41 (1702), year renwu

康熙四十二年癸未七月丁巳休致。八月丁亥爲黑龍江將軍。 Kangxi 42 (1703), year guiwei In the seventh month, on day dingsi, he retired; in the eighth month, on day dinghai, another officer was appointed Heilongjiang General.

康熙四十三年甲申 Kangxi 43 (1704), year jiashen

康熙四十四年乙酉 Kangxi 44 (1705), year yiyou

康熙四十五年丙戌 Kangxi 45 (1706), year bingxu

康熙四十六年丁亥 Kangxi 46 (1707), year dinghai

康熙四十七年戊子免。正月癸酉爲奉天將軍。九月庚寅遷。十一月丙申爲黑龍江將軍。 Kangxi 47 (1708), year wuzi He was dismissed. In the first month, on day guiyou, another officer was appointed Fengtian General. In the ninth month, on day gengyin, he was transferred; in the eleventh month, on day bingshen, he was appointed Heilongjiang General.

康熙四十八年己丑二月己酉遷。二月戊午署奉天將軍。二月己酉遷。二月己酉爲寧古塔將軍。二月乙巳革。二月己酉爲黑龍江將軍。 Kangxi 48 (1709), year jichou In the second month, on day jiyou, he was transferred; on day wuwu he was appointed acting Fengtian General. In the second month, on day jiyou, he was transferred and appointed Ningguta General. In the second month, on day yisi, he was dismissed; on day jiyou another officer was appointed Heilongjiang General.

康熙四十九年庚寅 Kangxi 49 (1710), year gengyin

康熙五十年辛卯 Kangxi 50 (1711), year xinmao

康熙五十一年壬辰十月丁巳遷。十月丁巳署奉天將軍。 Kangxi 51 (1712), year renchen In the tenth month, on day dingsi, he was transferred and appointed acting Fengtian General.

康熙五十二年癸巳 Kangxi 52 (1713), year guisi

康熙五十三年甲午 Kangxi 53 (1714), year jiawu

康熙五十四年乙未卒。三月辛丑署寧古塔將軍。卒。五月丙午，署黑龍江將軍。 Kangxi 54 (1715), year yiwei He died. In the third month, on day xinchou, an acting Ningguta General was appointed. He died. In the fifth month, on day bingwu, an acting Heilongjiang General was appointed.

康熙五十五年丙申十月戊子爲黑龍江將軍。 Kangxi 55 (1716), year bingshen In the tenth month, on day wuzi, he was appointed Heilongjiang General.

康熙五十六年丁酉 Kangxi 56 (1717), year dingyou

康熙五十七年戊戌七月署黑龍江將軍。正月乙亥乞休。三月爲右衛將軍。 Kangxi 57 (1718), year wuxu In the seventh month he was appointed acting Heilongjiang General. In the first month, on day yihai, he asked to retire; in the third month he was appointed General of the Right Guard.

康熙五十八年己亥 Kangxi 58 (1719), year jihai

康熙五十九年庚子署寧古塔將軍。二月甲子黑龍江將軍。 Kangxi 59 (1720), year gengzi Appointed acting Ningguta General. In the second month, on day jiazi, he was appointed Heilongjiang General.

康熙六十年辛丑 Kangxi 60 (1721), year xinchou

康熙六十一年壬寅 Kangxi 61 (1722), year renyin

奉天將軍寧古塔將軍黑龍江將軍右衛將軍烏里雅蘇台將軍青海辦事大臣駐藏辦事大臣 Fengtian General Ningguta General Heilongjiang General General of the Right Guard General at Uliastai Qinghai Affairs Commissioner Resident Commissioner in Tibet

雍正元年癸卯 Yongzheng 1, year guimao

雍正二年甲辰十二月癸未解任。爲奉天將軍。二月丁未召。寧古塔將軍。六月戊子降。爲右衛將軍。 Yongzheng 2, year jiachen In the twelfth month, on day guiwei, he was relieved of his post; another officer was appointed Fengtian General. In the second month, on day dingwei, he was summoned to serve as Ningguta General. In the sixth month, on day wuzi, he was demoted and appointed General of the Right Guard.

雍正三年乙巳十一月遷。奉天將軍。辦理青海事務。 Yongzheng 3, year yisi In the eleventh month he was transferred and appointed Fengtian General. He was assigned to manage Qinghai affairs.

雍正四年丙午二月召。爲黑龍江將軍。二月遷。爲右衛將軍。 Yongzheng 4, year bingwu In the second month he was summoned and appointed Heilongjiang General. In the second month he was transferred and appointed General of the Right Guard.

雍正五年丁未二月卒。閏三月爲奉天將軍。二月召。十二月爲黑龍江將軍。五月丁巳赴藏辦事。 正月丁巳赴藏辦事。 Yongzheng 5, year dingwei He died in the second month; in the intercalary third month another officer was appointed Fengtian General. In the second month he was summoned; in the twelfth month he was appointed Heilongjiang General. On day dingsi in the fifth month he went to Tibet on official business; on day dingsi in the first month he went to Tibet on official business.

雍正六年戊申九月癸亥免。署。 Yongzheng 6, year wushen In the ninth month, on day guihai, he was dismissed; an acting appointment followed.

雍正七年己酉二月遷。爲奉天將軍。 Yongzheng 7, year jiyou In the second month he was transferred and appointed Fengtian General.

雍正八年庚戌正月降。爲奉天將軍。正月遷。爲黑龍江將軍。回京。駐藏辦事。 Yongzheng 8, year gengxu In the first month he was demoted and appointed Fengtian General. In the first month he was transferred and appointed Heilongjiang General. He returned to the capital and was assigned to manage Tibetan affairs.

雍正九年辛亥正月遷。爲寧古塔將軍。六月召回京。駐藏辦事。 Yongzheng 9, year xinhai In the first month he was transferred and appointed Ningguta General. In the sixth month he was recalled to the capital and assigned to manage Tibetan affairs.

雍正十年壬子九月赴軍營。署。九月遷。爲寧古塔將軍。爲定邊左副將軍。 Yongzheng 10, year renzi In the ninth month he went to the army camp in an acting capacity. In the ninth month he was transferred and appointed Ningguta General. He was appointed Left Deputy General for Pacifying the Frontier.

雍正十一年癸丑 Yongzheng 11, year guichou

雍正十二年甲寅八月總理青海番人事務。召回京。八月赴藏辦事。尋卒。赴藏辦事。 Yongzheng 12, year jiayin In the eighth month he was placed in overall charge of Tibetan affairs in Qinghai. He was recalled to the capital, and in the eighth month went to Tibet on official business; he soon died. He had gone to Tibet on official business.

雍正十三年乙卯遷。爲奉天將軍。遷。爲黑龍江將軍。免。十二月右衛將軍。 Yongzheng 13, year yimao He was transferred and appointed Fengtian General. He was transferred and appointed Heilongjiang General. He was dismissed; in the twelfth month another officer was appointed General of the Right Guard.
