Yongzheng 12, year jiayin那蘇圖.杜賚.塔爾岱.申穆德.策凌.In the eighth month, 德齡 was placed in overall charge of Tibetan affairs in Qinghai.青保 and 苗壽 were recalled to the capital. In the eighth month, 阿爾珣 went to Tibet on official business and soon died. 那蘇泰 went to Tibet on official business.
Yongzheng 13, year yimaoNasutu was transferred. Jueluo Baixiu was appointed Fengtian General.杜賚.Ta'erdai was transferred. Nasutu was appointed Heilongjiang General.申穆德 was removed from office. In the twelfth month, 岱林布: Right Guard General.策凌.德齡.那蘇泰.
