1
=譚泰=譚泰，舒穆祿氏，滿洲正黃旗人，揚古利從弟也。 初授牛錄額真。 天聰八年，擢巴牙喇章京，與固山額真圖爾格分統左右翼兵，略錦州。 還從太宗伐明，自上方堡毀邊牆以入，敗明兵，克保安州。 擢巴牙喇纛章京，令關白諸事。 九年，揚古利賜第，侍衛宗室濟馬護欲得其舊居，揚古利不可。 濟馬護囑譚泰入奏，譚泰匿不以聞，濟馬護訴於上，上責譚泰曰：「爾為朕耳目，凡事當無隱。 濟馬護乃朕叔父之子，其言尚不能達，民間勞苦嗟怨，何由得使朕知？ 爾恃宗族強盛，欺陵愚弱，朕所深惡！ 」下刑部質訊，奪官。 尋复授本旗固山額真。
Tan Tai, of the Sumuru clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain Yellow Banner and was Yangguli's younger paternal cousin. He was first appointed niru commander. In the eighth year of Tiancong (1634), he was raised to vanguard officer and, together with banner commander Thurgai, led the left and right wings in operations against Jinzhou. On the return march he accompanied Taizong (Hong Taiji) against the Ming, breaching the border wall at Shangfang Fort to enter, routing Ming forces and taking Bao'an Prefecture. He was advanced to chief vanguard standard-bearer and charged with reporting everything directly to the emperor. In the ninth year (1635), when Yangguli received an imperial mansion, Jimahu of the guard corps tried to take over his old quarters, but Yangguli refused. Jimahu asked Tan Tai to bring the matter before the throne, but Tan Tai kept it secret. When Jimahu appealed directly to the emperor, the latter rebuked Tan Tai: "You are my eyes and ears; nothing should be hidden from me." "Jimahu is my uncle's own son, yet his words cannot reach me — how am I to learn of the people's toil and complaints?" "You lean on your clan's strength to oppress the weak — that is what I abhor above all!" He was handed over to the Board of Punishments for investigation and dismissed from his post. Soon afterward he was restored as banner commander of his own banner.
2
崇德元年，從武英郡王阿濟格等伐明，克延慶等十二城。 進圍定興，先登有功。 復與固山額真阿山等設伏，敗明遵化三屯營守兵，盡殲之。 師還，宴勞。 復從上伐朝鮮，朝鮮王棄城走，譚泰率師入其城，盡收其輜重。 從上逐朝鮮王至南漢城，受降而還。 四年，從睿親王多爾袞等伐明，與固山額真葉臣自太平寨破青山口，與明兵十三戰，皆捷。 輔國將軍鞏阿岱，濟馬護兄也，譚泰與相詬於禁門，坐罰。
In the first year of Chongde (1636) he joined Prince Ying Ajige's expedition against the Ming and captured twelve cities, Yanqing among them. During the siege of Dingxing he led the assault over the walls with distinction. He again joined banner commander Ashan in an ambush that routed and wiped out the Ming defenders at Santun Camp near Zunhua. On the army's return a celebratory feast was held in their honor. He again followed the emperor into Korea; when the Korean king fled his capital, Tan Tai entered the city and seized all military stores. He pursued the Korean king to Namhansung, witnessed his surrender, and marched home. In the fourth year (1639) he joined Prince Rui Dorgon's Ming campaign; with banner commander Yečen he broke through Qingshan Pass from Taiping Stockade and won all thirteen engagements with Ming forces. Assistant Prince Gong Adažai, Jimahu's elder brother, quarreled with Tan Tai at the palace gate, and Tan Tai was penalized.
3
六年，從圍錦州，譚泰將四百人自小凌河直抵海濱，絕明兵歸路。 與明總督洪承疇兵戰，大敗之。 授世職二等參將。 七年，從輔國公篇古等攻薊州，擊敗明總兵白騰蛟、白廣恩等，俘馘為諸軍最。 八年，命率銳卒與固山額真準塔更番戍錦州。 順治元年，從入關，逐破李自成於慶都。 復將巴牙喇兵躡擊，至真定，大破之，敘功授一等公。
In the sixth year (1641), during the siege of Jinzhou, he took four hundred men down the Xiao Ling River to the coast and cut off the Ming army's escape route. He engaged Grand Secretary Hong Chengchou's army and won a crushing victory. He received a hereditary rank equivalent to second-class deputy commander. In the seventh year he joined Fuguo Gong Panggu's attack on Jizhou, defeated regional commanders Bai Tengjiao and Bai Guang'en, and took more prisoners and heads than any other unit. In the eighth year he and banner commander Zhuntai were ordered to rotate crack troops in the Jinzhou garrison. In Shunzhi 1 (1644) he crossed into China proper and routed Li Zicheng at Qingdu. He led vanguard troops in pursuit to Zhending, broke Li's forces again, and was rewarded with a first-class dukedom for his service.
4
睿親王攝政，譚泰與巴牙喇纛章京圖賴、啟心郎索尼並見信任。 固山額真何洛會誣肅親王豪格怨譚泰等不附己，訐之睿親王，王謂譚泰忠，益信任之。 大學士希福忤譚泰，希福欲易賜第，譚泰不可，希福誚之，益怒。 其弟譚布以希福述睿親王自言過誤告譚泰，譚泰訐之法司，希福坐黜。 二年，英親王阿濟格坐奏軍事不實得罪，命譚泰與鰲拜等集眾宣其罪。 譚泰匿諭旨不以示眾，索尼發其罪，降世職昂邦章京，奪官。 譚泰怨索尼，訐索尼於內庫牧馬鼓琴及禁門橋下捕魚，索尼亦坐黜。 譚泰復起為本旗固山額真。
Under Prince Rui's regency Tan Tai shared the regent's confidence with chief vanguard Tülai and Kesin (adviser) Sony. Banner commander Heluohui falsely told Prince Rui that Prince Su Hooge resented Tan Tai and others for refusing to join him; the regent pronounced Tan Tai loyal and relied on him even more. Grand Secretary Xifu crossed Tan Tai; when Xifu sought to swap his imperial residence Tan Tai refused, Xifu taunted him, and Tan Tai's rage only grew. Tan Tai's brother Tan Bu told him that Xifu had repeated Prince Rui's own confessions of fault; Tan Tai denounced this to the judicial authorities and Xifu was dismissed. In the second year (1645) Prince Ying Ajige was punished for misreporting military affairs; Tan Tai and Oboi were ordered to assemble the troops and announce his crimes. Tan Tai hid the edict instead of reading it aloud; Sony exposed him, and he was demoted to hereditary Angbang janggin and stripped of rank. Tan Tai retaliated by denouncing Sony for keeping horses and playing music in the inner storehouse and fishing under the palace bridge; Sony was dismissed too. Tan Tai was soon restored as banner commander of his own banner.
5
初，師下江南，譚泰自西安逐捕流寇，慮不與平江南功，使謂圖賴曰：「我軍道迂險，後至。 今南京未下，請留待我軍。 」圖賴書告索尼，使啟睿親王，或發觀之，懼譚泰得罪，毀其書勿使達索尼。 圖賴師還，詰索尼，發其事，王鞫齎書者，得狀。 譚泰又坐與婦翁固山額真阿山遣巫者治病。 下廷臣議罪，論死，下獄，王使視之，並餽食焉。 譚泰曰：「王若拯我，我殺身報王！ 」乃出之獄。 五年，復原官。
Earlier, as forces moved south, Tan Tai was chasing bandits from Xi'an and feared missing credit for pacifying the south. He sent Tülai a message: "Our route is long and treacherous; we will arrive late." "Nanjing has not fallen yet — please hold until we arrive." Tülai wrote to Sony to inform the regent; someone intercepted the letter, fearing Tan Tai's ruin, destroyed it before it reached Sony. When Tülai returned he confronted Sony; the affair came to light, and the regent interrogated the messenger and established the facts. Tan Tai was further implicated for having Ashan, his father-in-law and a banner commander, employ a shaman to treat illness. The court deliberated and sentenced him to death; in prison the regent sent attendants—and even food. Tan Tai said, "If Your Highness saves me, I will repay you with my life!" He was then released. In the fifth year (1648) his former offices were restored.
6
金聲桓叛江西，授譚泰征南大將軍，率師討之。 聲桓以步騎七萬人抗我師，譚泰督諸軍與戰，次九江，大敗聲桓兵，獲其舟以濟師。 攻南昌，為長圍困之，數月，麾將士以雲梯登，聲桓中二矢，投水死； 又破其將王得仁。 南康、瑞州、臨江、袁州並下。 當聲桓叛時，李成棟以廣東應之，南昌圍急，成棟赴援。 譚泰師將至贛州，聞成棟入信豐，譚泰遣諸將乘勝襲擊，成棟兵潰，溺水死，克信豐。 別將徇撫州、建昌。 江西悉平。 師還，授一等精奇尼哈番。
When Jin Shenghuan rebelled in Jiangxi, Tan Tai was made southern expedition commander-general and marched to suppress him. Shenghuan met him with seventy thousand foot and horse; at Jiujiang Tan Tai routed his army and seized his boats to ferry the Qing forces. He laid siege to Nanchang for months, then ordered assault ladders; Shenghuan took two arrows and drowned himself; and defeated his general Wang Deren. Nankang, Ruizhou, Linjiang, and Yuanzhou fell in turn. Li Chengdong rose in Guangdong to support the rebellion; as Nanchang tightened, he marched to relieve it. Near Ganzhou Tan Tai learned Chengdong was at Xinfeng; he sent generals in pursuit, Chengdong's army broke, he drowned, and Xinfeng was taken. Other columns pacified Fuzhou and Jianchang. Jiangxi was fully pacified. On his return he was made a first-class baron.
7
七年，睿親王薨，上命吏、刑、工三部增設滿洲尚書各一，授譚泰吏部尚書。 八年，世祖親政，追論睿親王罪狀，大學士剛林、祁充格皆坐誅，罪不及譚泰。 時圖賴已卒，索尼方罪廢，譚泰毀圖賴墓室，洩舊忿。 五月，御史張煊劾大學士陳名夏等，下王大臣會鞫。 譚泰袒名夏，讞上，命未下，譚泰前奏，言煊劾皆虛，且所舉諸事皆在赦前，煊以外轉嫌，誣名夏等死罪，當反坐，煊遂見法。
In the seventh year (1650), after Prince Rui's death, the emperor added a Manchu minister to each of the Civil, Punishments, and Works boards and appointed Tan Tai Minister of Civil Office. In the eighth year (1651) the Shunzhi emperor assumed personal rule; Prince Rui was posthumously condemned and Grand Secretaries Ganglin and Qi Chongge were executed, but Tan Tai was not implicated. Tülai was already dead and Sony was under punishment; Tan Tai desecrated Tülai's tomb to settle an old grudge. In the fifth month Censor Zhang Huang impeached Grand Secretary Chen Mingxia and others; the case went to a tribunal of princes and ministers. Tan Tai shielded Chen Mingxia; before sentence was pronounced he argued that Zhang Huang's charges were false and referred to matters already amnestied; alleging capital crimes on that basis, he said Huang should suffer the penalty he had sought to impose — and Huang was executed.
8
譚泰愈縱恣，岳爾多其妻弟也，襲一等精奇尼哈番，為奪其族人法喀應襲一等阿思哈尼哈番合併為三等侯； 佟圖賴其女弟之夫也，時金礪駐防杭州，妄稱員缺，以佟圖賴擬補。 上自譚泰袒陳名夏構張煊，心厭惡之。 是歲八月，下詔責其專橫，命執付獄，集廷臣議罪。 鰲拜复訐譚泰阿附睿親王及營私擅政諸狀，讞皆實。 王大臣議誅譚泰及其子孫，上命誅譚泰，籍其家，子孫貸連坐。
Growing bolder, Tan Tai arranged for his brother-in-law Yue'erduo, who held a first-class barony, to seize clansman Faka's first-class viscounty and merge the two into a third-class marquisate; Totoi was married to his sister; when Jin Li was stationed at Hangzhou he falsely declared a vacancy and proposed Totoi to fill it. Ever since Tan Tai had protected Chen Mingxia and destroyed Zhang Huang, the emperor had grown to loathe him. In the eighth month an edict condemned his arrogance; he was arrested and the court assembled to judge him. Oboi further denounced Tan Tai's favoritism toward Prince Rui and private abuses; every charge was upheld. The tribunal recommended death for Tan Tai and his line; the emperor executed Tan Tai and seized his property but spared his descendants collective punishment.
9
=何洛會=何洛會，失其氏，滿洲鑲白旗人。 父阿吉賴，事太祖，從征戰，官牛錄額真。 卒，何洛會嗣，兼巴牙喇甲喇章京。 天聰八年，從伐明，略錦州。 九年，詔免諸功臣徭役，何洛會與焉。 崇德五年，授正黃旗蒙古固山額真。 從睿親王多爾袞伐明，圍錦州。 調滿洲固山額真。 七年，錦州既下，追論圍錦州時何洛會匿鄂羅塞臣破陣功，當奪官，上宥之。
Heluohui — his clan name is unrecorded; he was a Manchu of the Bordered White Banner. His father Ajilai served Taizu, fought in campaigns, and held the rank of company commander. On his death Heluohui succeeded him and also served as vanguard company officer. In Tiancong 8 he joined the Ming campaign and operated against Jinzhou. In the ninth year meritorious ministers were exempted from corvée; Heluohui was included. In Chongde 5 he became commander of the Mongol Plain Yellow Banner. He joined Prince Rui Dorgon's Ming expedition and besieged Jinzhou. He was transferred to command a Manchu banner. In the seventh year, after Jinzhou fell, Heluohui was found to have suppressed Orosencen's credit for breaking the enemy line; though liable to dismissal, the emperor pardoned him.
10
何洛會隸肅親王豪格，頗見任使。 世祖即位，睿親王攝政，與肅親王有隙。 何洛會訐肅親王與兩黃旗大臣揚善、俄莫克圖、伊成格、羅碩將謀亂，肅親王坐削爵，揚善等皆棄市。 賞何洛會告姦，籍俄莫克圖、伊成格家畀之，授世職二等甲喇章京。 尋從睿親王入關，擊李自成，逐至慶都。 還，睿親王令奉表迎世祖，擢內大臣，留守盛京。 阿哈尼堪將左翼，碩詹將右翼，並於熊耀城、錦州、寧遠、鳳凰城、興京、義州、新城、牛莊、岫岩城各置城守官，皆統於何洛會。
Heluohui served under Prince Su Hooge and enjoyed considerable favor. After the Shunzhi emperor's accession Prince Rui regented and clashed with Prince Su. Heluohui accused Prince Su and Yellow Banner ministers Yangshan, Emektu, Icengge, and Luoshuo of plotting rebellion; Prince Su lost his title and the ministers were executed. Rewarded for the denunciation, he received the confiscated estates of Emektu and Icengge and a second-class company commander's hereditary rank. He soon followed Prince Rui south, fought Li Zicheng, and pursued him to Qingdu. On the return march Prince Rui sent him to welcome the young emperor; he was made inner grand minister and left to guard the old capital. Ahanikan led the left wing and Suozhan the right; garrison commanders were posted at Xiongyao, Jinzhou, Ningyuan, Fenghuang, Xingjing, Yizhou, Xincheng, Niuzhuang, and Xiuyan — all under Heluohui.
11
順治二年，敘功，進世職一等。 旋命率師駐防西安，道河南，討定西平土寇劉洪起等。 是歲十二月，授定西大將軍，命自陝西徇四川。 時自成將劉體純等犯商州，叛將賀珍與其黨孫守法、胡向宸等分據漢中、興安。 三年，珍以七萬人犯西安，何洛會督兵迎戰，珍敗走，复逐破之，並破體純商州。
In Shunzhi 2 his service was recognized with promotion to a first-class hereditary rank. He was soon ordered to garrison Xi'an, marching through Henan to suppress bandits such as Liu Hongqi in Dingxi ping. In the twelfth month he was made western pacification commander-general with orders to enter Sichuan from Shaanxi. Li Zicheng's general Liu Tichun was raiding Shangzhou; the rebel He Zhen and allies Sun Shoufa and Hu Xiangchen held Hanzhong and Xing'an. In the third year He Zhen attacked Xi'an with seventy thousand men; Heluohui defeated and pursued him and also broke Liu Tichun at Shangzhou.
12
肅親王從入關，破李自成，复爵。 至是，上命為靖遠大將軍，下四川，召何洛會還京師。 四年，命率師駐防宣府，仍授正黃旗滿洲固山額真。 五年，調鑲白旗。 命佐譚泰定江西，擊破金聲桓、王得仁、李成棟，事具譚泰傳。 師還，賜所獲金銀珠玉，進世職三等精奇尼哈番。
Prince Su had followed the armies south, defeated Li Zicheng, and regained his title. The emperor now made Prince Su distant-pacification commander-general for Sichuan and recalled Heluohui to the capital. In the fourth year he was posted to Xuanfu and again given the Plain Yellow Manchu banner command. In the fifth year he transferred to the Bordered White Banner. He assisted Tan Tai in pacifying Jiangxi, defeating Jin Shenghuan, Wang Deren, and Li Chengdong — as recounted in Tan Tai's biography. On return he received captured gold, silver, and jewels and was raised to third-class baron.
13
肅親王師還，貝子屯齊等訐王諸悖妄狀，何洛會復從而證之，遂坐奪爵，以幽系終。 睿親王取肅親王福金，召肅親王諸子入府校射，何洛會詈之曰：「見此鬼魅，不覺心悸！ 」尚書譚泰聞其語。 及睿親王薨，世祖親政，何洛會語貝子錫翰曰：「兩黃旗大臣與我相惡，我嘗訐告肅親王，今豈肯容我？ 」八年二月，蘇克薩哈等訐睿親王將率兩白旗移駐永平，且私具上服禦，及薨用斂，何洛會、羅什、博爾惠等皆知狀。 時羅什、博爾惠已先誅，執何洛會，下王大臣會鞫。 譚泰、錫翰各以何洛會語告，又追論誣告肅親王罪，與其兄胡錫並磔死，籍其家。
After Prince Su's return, Beile Tunqi impeached him for many offenses; Heluohui testified again, and the prince lost his title and died under house arrest. Prince Rui took Prince Su's consort and summoned Su's sons to shoot in his mansion; Heluohui cursed them: "These demons make my heart quail!" Minister Tan Tai heard what he said. After Prince Rui's death and the emperor's personal rule, Heluohui told Beile Xihan: "The Yellow Banner ministers hate me — I denounced Prince Su once; will they spare me now?" In the second month of the eighth year Suksaha charged that Prince Rui had planned to move the White Banners to Yongping, secretly prepared imperial regalia, and was buried in it; Heluohui, Rosi, Bo'erhui, and others knew of this. Rosi and Bo'erhui were already dead; Heluohui was arrested and tried by the princes and ministers. Tan Tai and Xihan repeated his confession; for the false charge against Prince Su he and his brother Huxi were dismembered and their property seized.
14
=錫圖庫=錫圖庫，烏扎拉氏，滿洲正白旗人，世居烏拉。 兄福蘭，當太祖時來歸，授世職備禦。 卒，錫圖庫嗣，授牛錄額真，兼巴牙喇甲喇章京。 天聰四年，師克永平，錫圖庫與甲喇額真圖魯什等率兵循徼，得邏卒二、馬十七。 五年，诇敵大凌河，得二人以還。 上伐明，圍大凌河城，敗錦州援兵，錫圖庫皆有功。 六年，復從伐明，略宣府、大同邊外，多所斬獲。 八年，复略蒙古錫爾哈、錫伯圖諸地，斬七十餘級、俘百餘戶及馬駝，賚以所獲，進世職一等甲喇章京。 九年，偕噶布什賢噶喇依昂邦勞薩等略明邊，入長城，攻代、朔諸州，多所斬獲。
Xitu Ku, of the Ujala clan, was a Plain White Banner Manchu whose family came from Ula. His elder brother Fulan submitted in Taizu's reign and received a hereditary guard's rank. When his brother died, Xitu Ku inherited the family rank and was made niru commander while also holding the office of bayara jalan janggin. In the fourth year of Tiancong (1630), when the army took Yongping, Xitu Ku and jalan commander Tursi led patrols and brought back two Ming defectors and seventeen horses. The following year he reconnoitered enemy positions at Dalinghe and returned with two captives. During the campaigns against the Ming—the siege of Dalinghe and the rout of Jinzhou's relief forces—he distinguished himself throughout. In the sixth year (1632) he again joined a Ming expedition, harrying the lands beyond Xuanfu and Datong and returning with heavy loot and many slain. In the eighth year (1634) he raided the Mongol districts of Xierha and Xibotu, killing over seventy and taking more than a hundred households with horses and camels; for this he received a share of the spoils and was promoted to first-rank hereditary jalan janggin. In the ninth year (1635) he joined Gabsihiyan officers Laosa and others in a border raid, broke through the Great Wall, stormed Dai and Shuo, and won extensive kills and booty.
15
崇德元年，睿親王多爾袞率師伐明，攻寧遠，錫圖庫以二十人前驅，至中後所及山海關外诇敵，屢得邏卒，並獲其馬，又於前屯衛設伏敗敵。 喀木尼堪部葉類等盜科爾沁諸部馬叛走，錫圖庫率巴牙喇壯達八人詣寧古塔，與梅勒額真吳巴海督兵追之。 行數十日，及於溫鐸，招降不從，葉類潛遁，盡殲其黨九十四人，俘婦女八十餘，得馬五十六； 复逐捕葉類，入山，射之殪。 師還，上遣大臣出迎五里，宴勞，進世職一等梅勒章京。
In the first year of Chongde (1636), when Prince Rui Dorgon marched against the Ming and besieged Ningyuan, Xitu Ku rode ahead with twenty men, reconnoitered from Zhonghousuo to beyond Shanhaiguan, repeatedly picked off Ming scouts and their mounts, and laid an ambush at Qiantunwei that broke an enemy column. When Yelei of the Kamnikhan clan stole Khorchin horses and bolted, Xitu Ku took eight bayara lieutenants to Ningguta and, with meiren ejen Ubai, led the pursuit. After weeks on the trail they caught up at Wende; Yelei refused parley and slipped away, but his band of ninety-four was wiped out, more than eighty women were taken, and fifty-six horses seized; He then tracked Yelei into the hills and brought him down with an arrow. On their return the emperor sent ministers five li out to receive them, feasted the column, and raised Xitu Ku to first-rank hereditary meiren janggin.
16
五年，命偕巴牙喇纛章京濟什哈率師並徵蒙古敖漢、柰曼、烏喇特諸部兵伐索倫部，敗敵於甘河，擒部長博木博郭爾，籍千餘戶，得馬數百。 師還，賜宴北驛館，進世職三等昂邦章京。 旋授本旗梅勒額真。 七年，從貝勒阿巴泰伐明，自薊州越明都，下山東。 師還，以先出邊，部議當奪官，命寬之，白金百。 八年，擢巴牙喇纛章京。
In the fifth year (1640) he and chief vanguard Jisika commanded a force that mustered Aohan, Naiman, and Urad auxiliaries against the Solon; at the Gan River they routed the foe, seized chieftain Bombogor, enrolled over a thousand households, and captured hundreds of horses. On their return they were feasted at the North Post Station and he was promoted to third-rank hereditary angbang janggin. He was then named meiren ejen of his banner. In the seventh year (1642) he marched with Prince Abatai against the Ming, cutting south from Jizhou through the capital district into Shandong. On the march home he was charged with crossing the border prematurely; although the board recommended dismissal, the throne pardoned him with a fine of one hundred taels. In the eighth year (1643) he was made chief bayara janggin.
17
順治元年，從睿親王多爾袞伐明，敗李自成將唐通於一片石，遂入山海關，屢戰皆勝； 敗自成遊騎於三河，追擊至安肅。 旋從固山額真葉臣等取太原，戰於汾州、於絳州，屢破敵。 二年，進二等精奇尼哈番。 時自成猶據陝西，師自潼關、綏德南北兩路入，錫圖庫率師與北路軍會，敗賊延安。 自成走入湖廣，鍚圖庫移兵從之，自安陸至於荊門，屢擊敗自成兵。
In the first year of Shunzhi (1644) he followed Prince Rui Dorgon south, routed Li Zicheng's general Tang Tong at Yipianshi, passed through Shanhaiguan, and won every engagement thereafter; He smashed Zicheng's scouting cavalry at Sanhe and chased the fugitives to Ansu. He then joined banner commander Yečen in the capture of Taiyuan and repeatedly routed the enemy at Fenzhou and Jiangzhou. In the second year (1645) he was raised to second-class jingqi nihafan. While Zicheng still held Shaanxi, imperial columns entered by the Tongguan and Suide corridors; Xitu Ku brought his detachment to the northern force and crushed the rebels at Yan'an. When Zicheng fled into Huguang, Xitu Ku turned south in pursuit, harrying his columns from Anlu to Jingmen.
18
三年，復從肅親王豪格下四川，討張獻忠。 五年，進世職一等。 復從鄭親王濟爾哈朗下湖南。 六年，師次長沙，錫圖庫從左翼巴牙喇纛章京努三率兵前驅，攻湘潭。 努三軍北門，錫圖庫軍西門，遂克之。 進徇永興，斬明將尹舉智、杜貞明等。 再進定寶慶，取全州，破明將焦璉。 又移兵克永安關，取道州。 師還，賜白金三百。
In the third year (1646) he marched under Prince Su Hooge into Sichuan against Zhang Xianzhong. In the fifth year (1648) he gained a step in hereditary rank. He then campaigned in Hunan under Prince Zheng Jirhalang. In the sixth year (1649), as the army paused at Changsha, he rode with left-wing chief vanguard Nusan ahead of the column to assault Xiangtan. Nusan stormed the north gate while Xitu Ku struck the west, and the city fell. Pressing on to Yongxing, he killed the Ming commanders Yin Juzhi and Du Zhenming, among others. He next subdued Baoqing, seized Quanzhou, and broke the Ming general Jiao Lian. Shifting west he took Yong'an Pass and Daozhou. On the army's return he received a reward of three hundred taels of silver.
19
七年，睿親王多爾袞薨。 八年春，吳拜、羅什、博爾輝等訐英親王阿濟格將謀亂，鞫實，錫圖庫坐與謀，誅死，籍其家。
In the seventh year (1650) Prince Rui Dorgon died. In the spring of the eighth year (1651) Wubai, Rosi, Bo Erhui, and others accused Prince Ying Ajige of plotting treason; Xitu Ku was found an accomplice, put to death, and his estate forfeited.
20
=博爾輝=博爾輝，他塔喇氏，滿洲正白旗人。 初以巴牙喇壯達從征棟奎部，有俘馘。 天聰三年，從太宗伐明，自龍井關入攻遵化。 明總兵趙率教自山海關赴援，與戰，博爾輝斬其副將，明兵驚潰。 五年，擢巴牙喇甲喇章京，兼戶部參政。 復從伐明，與明兵遇寧遠，擊殺前隊七人。 八年，復從伐明，攻大同，明兵三千自龍門迎戰，博爾輝與噶布什賢章京錫特庫、牛錄額真星訥等奮擊破之。 九年，命偕承政馬福塔齎敕諭朝鮮國王。 師出邊招察哈爾部眾，自歸化經明邊東還，博爾輝殿。 明兵二百三十追擊我師，博爾輝以二十人擊卻之，斬十人，俘一人，得馬三。 明兵從我師，有垂為所獲者，博爾輝救之得脫。 崇德元年，敘功，授世職牛錄章京。 三年，裁參政，專任巴牙喇甲喇章京。
Bo Erhui, of the Tatara clan, was a Manchu of the Plain White Banner. He first served as bayara lieutenant in a campaign against the Dongkui and won captives and heads. In the third year of Tiancong (1629) he joined Hong Taiji's Ming expedition, entered through Longjing Pass, and assaulted Zunhua. When Ming commander Zhao Shuaijiao marched from Shanhaiguan to relieve the city, Bo Erhui slew his deputy and sent the Ming force fleeing in disorder. In the fifth year (1631) he was made bayara jalan janggin and also sat on the Board of Revenue. On another Ming campaign he met enemy troops at Ningyuan and killed seven in the forward line. In the eighth year (1634), during the assault on Datong, he and Gabsihiyan commander Sitku and niru captain Singne shattered three thousand Ming troops advancing from Longmen. In the ninth year (1635) he was sent with cheng'e Mafuta to deliver an edict to the Korean king. On a border mission to summon Chahar clans, he commanded the rearguard on the return march east from Guihua along the Ming frontier. When 230 Ming soldiers pursued the column, Bo Erhui turned with twenty men, killed ten, took one prisoner, and seized three horses. As Ming skirmishers pressed the rearguard he rescued men about to be taken and brought them off safely. In the first year of Chongde (1636) his service was rewarded with a hereditary niru janggin rank. In the third year (1638) he left the revenue board to serve full time as bayara jalan janggin.
21
順治元年，兼任刑部理事官。 從入關，擊李自成，敘功，進世職二等甲喇章京。 旋署巴牙喇纛章京。 從順承郡王勒克德渾下湖廣，師至武昌。 時自成將馬進忠、王進才既降复叛，據岳州，​​令博爾輝率師討之，次臨湘，擊敗其兵。 進攻岳州，進忠、進才走長沙，逐擊敗之，其將黑運昌以舟師降。 師還，優賚。 五年，真除巴牙喇纛章京，列議政大臣，進世職二等阿思哈尼哈番。
In the first year of Shunzhi (1644) he also held an executive post on the Board of Punishments. After entering the passes he fought Li Zicheng and was raised to second-rank hereditary jalan janggin. He was soon made acting chief bayara janggin. He marched into Huguang with Prince Shuncheng Lekdehun and the army halted at Wuchang. When the former rebel generals Ma Jinzhong and Wang Jincai, who had submitted and rebelled again, held Yuezhou, Bo Erhui was sent against them and routed their force at Linxiang. He pressed the attack on Yuezhou; when Jinzhong and Jincai fled to Changsha he hunted them down, and their admiral Hei Yunchang surrendered his river flotilla. On the army's return he received lavish rewards. In the fifth year (1648) he was confirmed chief bayara janggin, given a seat among the regency council, and promoted to second-class ashanihafan.
22
睿親王攝政，諸王多與忤。 鄭親王濟爾哈朗降郡王，旋复爵。 初以端重親王博洛、敬謹親王尼堪佐理事，亦以專擅降爵。 博爾輝及諸大臣羅什、額克親、吳拜、蘇拜皆謹事睿親王，從王獵喀喇城。 王薨，喪還。 英親王阿濟格為睿親王同母兄，欲繼王柄政，博爾輝等與阿爾津共發其罪，英親王奪爵幽禁，賞諸告者，博爾輝進世職二等精奇尼哈番。 博爾輝等傳睿親王遺言，复理事二王親王爵，以告兩黃旗大臣。 居月餘，命未下，博爾輝有疾，穆爾泰往視之，博爾輝以為言。 穆爾泰告額爾德赫，額爾德赫告敬謹郡王尼堪，遂與端重郡王博洛訴於鄭親王。 八年正月，复二王爵。 越八日，執博爾輝等下獄，坐博爾輝、羅什動搖國事，蠱惑人心，論死，籍其家。 額克親削宗室籍，及吳拜、蘇拜皆奪官為民。 議上，得旨：「朕每聞刑人，殊不忍。 二人罪當誅，姑宥死何如？ 」王大臣復以初議上，乃誅死。
Under Prince Rui's regency many princes fell out with him. Prince Zheng Jirhalang was demoted to commandery prince, then soon restored. Princes Boluo and Niken, who had initially helped govern, were likewise demoted for acting on their own authority. Bo Erhui, Rosi, Ekqin, Wubai, and Subai all curried favor with the regent and joined his hunt at Kala Castle. When the Prince died the funeral cortège returned to the capital. Prince Ying Ajige—Dorgon's elder brother—sought to seize the regency; Bo Erhui and A'erjin denounced him; Ajige was disgraced and imprisoned, the accusers were rewarded, and Bo Erhui rose to second-class jingqi nihafan. Bo Erhui and his allies claimed Dorgon's dying wish to restore Boluo and Niken as princes and told the Yellow Banner ministers. More than a month passed without an edict; when Bo Erhui fell ill and Mu'ertai called on him, he confided his grievance. Mu'ertai told E'erdehe, who told Prince Niken; Niken and Prince Boluo then brought the complaint to Prince Zheng. In the first month of the eighth year (1651) Boluo and Niken were restored to princely rank. Eight days later Bo Erhui and his circle were arrested; Bo Erhui and Rosi were condemned for subverting the state and poisoning public opinion, sentenced to death, and their estates seized. Ekqin was expunged from the Aisin Gioro rolls; Wubai and Subai were cashiered to common status. When the sentence went up for review, the emperor replied: "I am always loath to hear of executions. These two deserve death—might we spare them for now? " The princes and ministers resubmitted the original verdict, and the two were put to death.
23
=冷僧機=冷僧機，納喇氏，滿洲正黃旗人，葉赫部長金台石之族也。 葉赫亡，來歸，隸正藍旗，屬貝勒莽古爾泰。 天聰元年，敖漢部長索諾木來歸，尚公主為額駙，以冷僧機隸焉。 莽古爾泰既卒，九年，冷僧機詣法司言莽古爾泰及貝勒德格類與公主及索諾木結黨，設誓謀不軌。 冷僧機與甲喇額真屯布祿、巴克什愛巴禮並下法司，鞫實，冷僧機以自首免罪，屯布祿、​​愛巴禮皆坐誅，籍其家以★K2冷僧機，改隸正黃旗，授世職三等梅勒章京。
Leng Sengji, of the Nara clan, was a Plain Yellow Banner Manchu and a kinsman of Yehe chieftain Gintaisi. After Yehe fell he submitted, entered the Plain Blue Banner, and served under Prince Mang'ertai. In the first year of Tiancong (1627) Aohan chief Sonom submitted, married an imperial princess, and Leng Sengji was transferred to his household. After Mang'ertai's death, in the ninth year (1635) Leng Sengji went to the judicial authorities and accused Mang'ertai and Prince Degesi of forming a clique with the princess and Sonom and swearing treasonous oaths. Leng Sengji, jalan commander Tunbulu, and secretary Aibali were all examined; Leng Sengji was spared for confessing first, while Tunbulu and Aibali were executed and their households confiscated and given to Leng Sengji; he was transferred to the Plain Yellow Banner as third-rank hereditary meiren janggin.
24
崇德二年，固山額真都類坐事下兵部待鞫，兵部參政穆爾泰令諸在系者避都類。 或以告冷僧機，聞於上，穆爾泰及同官皆坐降罰，授冷僧機一等侍衛。 七年，祖大壽來歸，上幸牧馬所，命內大臣侍衛與大壽等校射，中的者有所賜，冷僧機得駝一。 世祖即位，授內大臣。 順治二年，進二等阿思哈尼哈番兼拖沙喇哈番。 譚泰訐索尼，引冷僧機為證，謝未聞，坐徇庇，當削世職籍沒，上貰之。 旋進世職三等精奇尼哈番。
In the second year of Chongde (1637), when banner commander Dulei was detained at the War Board pending trial, board councillor Mu'ertai ordered other prisoners to keep away from him. When Leng Sengji reported the favoritism, Mu'ertai and his colleagues were demoted and Leng Sengji was made a first-class guard. In the seventh year (1642), when Zu Dashou surrendered, the emperor held an archery contest at the horse farm; Leng Sengji hit the mark and won a camel. At the Shunzhi emperor's accession he became an inner grand minister. In the second year of Shunzhi (1645) he rose to second-class ashanihafan with tuwalarahafan. When Tan Tai accused Sony and Leng Sengji was called as witness, Leng Sengji claimed ignorance; though shielding Tan Tai should have cost him his rank and property, the emperor pardoned him. He was soon promoted to third-class jingqi nihafan.
25
七年，睿親王有疾，怨上未臨視，冷僧機及貝子錫翰等奏請上臨視，睿親王坐以擅請降世職，恩詔復故，進一等伯。 睿親王薨，以豫親王多鐸子多爾博為後，襲爵。 冷僧機言於上曰：「昔太宗登遐，兩黃旗大臣誓立肅親王。 睿親王定策奉上紹統，多爾博宜特見優遇。 」又舉侍衛羅什，羅什上為冷僧機乞恩。 八年，鄭親王濟爾哈朗等劾羅什蠱惑諸王，坐誅，辭連冷僧機。 上因命諸大臣詰誓立肅親王事，冷僧機窮，諸大臣兼發阿諛睿親王諸罪，論斬籍沒，命寬之。 九年，追論冷僧機與貝子鞏阿岱、錫翰，內大臣西訥布庫等迎合睿親王，亂國政，下王大臣鞫實，與鞏阿岱、錫翰、西訥布庫等並誅，籍其家。
In the seventh year (1650), when the ailing Prince Rui resented the emperor's failure to visit, Leng Sengji and Beile Xihan petitioned for an imperial visit; Leng Sengji was demoted for the improper petition, but an amnesty restored his rank and raised him to first-class marquis. After Prince Rui's death Dorbo, son of Prince Yu Dodo, was adopted as his heir and inherited the title. Leng Sengji told the emperor: "When Hong Taiji died the Yellow Banner ministers swore to raise Prince Su Hooge. Prince Rui chose you for the throne; Dorbo deserves special favor." He also recommended guard Rosi, who petitioned the throne on Leng Sengji's behalf. In the eighth year (1651) Prince Zheng Jirhalang charged Rosi with swaying the princes; Rosi was executed, implicating Leng Sengji. The emperor had ministers interrogate him about the oath to enthrone Prince Su; cornered, he faced further charges of fawning on Prince Rui; though condemned to death with forfeiture, the emperor spared him. In the ninth year (1652) he was tried again with Beile Gong'adai, Xihan, and inner minister Xinabuku for colluding with Prince Rui to corrupt government; all were executed and their estates seized.
26
=【論】=論曰：定金聲桓、王得仁之亂，譚泰專將，何洛會為之佐。 錫圖庫、博爾輝亦久從征戰有勞。 睿親王既薨，諸阿附者乃互相傾，何洛會之獄，譚泰證之； 錫圖庫之誅，博爾輝等發之：轉相排軋，同就誅夷。 若冷僧機者，專事告訐，其及也亦宜矣。
Discussion: In quelling Jin Shenghuan and Wang Deren's revolt, Tan Tai commanded alone with Heluohui as his deputy. Xitu Ku and Bo Erhui had likewise served long in the field with distinction. After Prince Rui's death his former clients turned on one another: Tan Tai testified in Heluohui's trial; and Bo Erhui's faction denounced Xitu Ku—each faction informing on the other until all were destroyed. Leng Sengji, who lived by accusation, richly deserved the same fate.
