=吳振棫=吳振棫，字仲雲，浙江錢塘人。 嘉慶十九年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 道光二年，出為雲南大理知府，歷山東登州、沂州、濟南，安徽鳳陽知府； 山東登萊青道； 貴州糧儲道； 貴州按察使； 山西、四川布政使。 咸豐二年，擢雲南巡撫。 尋甸、東川回匪蠢動，粵匪由廣西闌入開化、廣南境，偕總督吳文鎔先後遣將擊平之。 四年，調陝西巡撫，未行，署雲貴總督。 貴州興義、普安匪起，檄安義鎮總兵金剛保等剿之。 遵義亦被匪圍，合滇、黔兵力，迭戰獲勝，擒匪首楊鳳先於石阡葛莊司。 五年秋，始抵陝西任。 匪首陳通明受粵匪指揮，於潼關糾眾謀響應，以計擒之，並獲其黨張順、羅吉祥等置諸法，被詔嘉獎。 鹽課攤歸地丁，數倍於昔，奏請改行招販，先課后鹽，民便之。 未幾，擢四川總督。
Wu Zhenyu, whose style was Zhongyun, came from Qiantang in Zhejiang. He became a jinshi in Jiaqing 19, entered the Hanlin as a bachelor, and received appointment as reviser. In Daoguang 2 he was posted as prefect of Dali in Yunnan, then held successive prefectures in Shandong—Dengzhou, Yizhou, and Jinan—and Fengyang in Anhui; the Shandong Deng-Lai-Qing circuit intendant; the Guizhou grain and storage intendant; the Guizhou provincial judicial commissioner; and provincial administration commissioner in Shanxi and Sichuan. In Xianfeng 2 he was elevated to governor of Yunnan. When Hui rebels rose in Xundian and Dongchuan and Cantonese rebels from Guangxi crossed into Kaihua and Guangnan, he and Governor-General Wu Wenrong dispatched troops in turn until both threats were suppressed. In year 4 he was shifted to Shaanxi governor but had not yet departed when he was ordered to act as Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. Rebels broke out in Guizhou's Xingyi and Pu'an, and he ordered Anyi garrison commander Jin Gangbao and others against them. Zunyi too came under siege; pooling Yunnan and Guizhou troops he won successive engagements and took the rebel chief Yang Fengxian at Gezhuang in Shiqian. Not until autumn of year 5 did he arrive in Shaanxi to assume his post. Rebel chief Chen Tongming, directed by Cantonese rebels, mustered men at Tong Pass to coordinate an uprising; Wu captured him by ruse, seized accomplices Zhang Shun and Luo Jixiang, and had them executed—earning an imperial commendation. With the salt tax merged into the land tax at several times the old rate, he petitioned to revert to licensed merchants and tax-before-salt, which eased the burden on the people. Soon after he was promoted to governor-general of Sichuan.
2
七年，調雲貴總督。 雲南漢、回積仇，自中原兵事亟，協餉不至，回亂愈恣。 團練跋扈，動相殺掠，省城戒嚴。 前任總督恆春不能制，夫婦同縊，巡撫舒興阿亦以病求去，惟布政使桑春榮困守危城。 文宗知振棫熟悉滇省情形，故以代之。 命選川兵三千，攜餉五萬馳往，調前山東巡撫張亮基幫辦軍務以副之。 振或至，先駐宣威，進次​​曲靖。 疏言：「先剿後撫，勢順而易，不待智者而知。 兵盛餉足，必應如是。 前督臣林則徐剿永昌回匪，兵、練萬餘，本省有餉可籌； 彌渡獲勝，匪旋受撫，其地祗迤西一隅中之一隅。 此次匪遍三迤，情形迥不相同，非數千之兵、十數万之餉所能蕆事。 如率意迳行，徒損國威，於事無補。 臣初到滇，於漢、回兩無嫌怨，惟憑藉兵威，結以恩信，有所申訴，處以公平。 省城為根本重地，省回解散，此外漸次籌辦，其負嵎抗拒者，仍當力剿。 匪勢漸孤，較易得手。 否則不自量度而急乘之，更無轉圜地步，禍更烈矣。 現在兵無可調，餉無可籌，宵旰焦勞，事非一省。 臣為雲南一省計，並當為天下全局計，豈容再有貽誤，致令徵調無休？ 故未言剿先言撫，有萬不得已之苦衷，雖成敗利鈍難以逆料，捨此亦別無良策也。 」
In year 7 he was transferred to the Yunnan-Guizhou governor-generalship. Deep Han-Hui animosity in Yunnan worsened as central-plain wars drained resources—subsidies stopped coming and Hui rebellion spread. Local militia ran wild, raiding one another until the capital was put on strict guard. His predecessor Hengchun failed to restore order and he and his wife hanged themselves; Governor Shuxing'a pleaded illness to leave; only Sang Chunrong, as administration commissioner, held the besieged city. Emperor Wenzong knew Zhenyu understood Yunnan and sent him to replace Hengchun. He was to take three thousand Sichuan soldiers and fifty thousand taels in silver, while former Shandong governor Zhang Liangji was assigned to help manage military affairs. Zhenyu reached Yunnan, halted first at Xuanwei, then moved forward to Qujing. He memorialized: 'Suppress first, pacify after—that is the easy, natural course, obvious to any thoughtful observer. Only where troops are plentiful and funds secure can that policy succeed. Former governor Lin Zexu had crushed Yongchang Hui rebels with over ten thousand troops and militia, with provincial revenue to sustain them; victory at Midu led quickly to submission, and the trouble lay in but a single corner of the western frontier. Now rebels held all three western circuits—a far graver case that thousands of men and hundreds of thousands in silver could not settle. To rush ahead heedlessly would only humiliate the state and achieve nothing. On arriving I owed neither Han nor Hui a grudge; I would rely on disciplined force, win trust through fairness, and judge every grievance impartially. The capital is the root: disband provincial Hui forces there, then work outward; die-hards must still be fought. Isolated rebels would be easier to defeat. But overreach without means to retreat, and disaster would only deepen. Today neither troops nor money can be found; the throne labors over crises nationwide, not Yunnan alone. I must weigh Yunnan against the empire—how can we afford another misstep that would trigger endless mobilizations? Hence I speak of pacification before suppression—not from choice but necessity; the outcome is uncertain, yet no better course exists.' '
3
又奏：「在籍侍郎黃琮、御史竇墉、總兵周鳳岐奉命團練，設總局於省城。 周鳳岐意見不合，引嫌不肯與聞。 黃琮、竇墉聯銜出示，專主痛剿，民間紛紛集練，回眾疑忌日深。 地方官苦心解散，漢民往往閧堂塞署，逼官殺回。 故團練在他省為要務，在滇省竟為大患。 黃琮等每言省團可得六十萬人，無虞寇警。 回匪初至城外，不及千人，團丁招之不來，來即奔潰。 近日省練一萬餘人，月需餉數万，經費不敷。 練頭自行管帶，不盡官派。 回眾有求撫之意，梗議者忽用練往剿，妄殺邀功，致可撫者終不能撫。 黃琮、竇墉系特派人員，非臣力所能製，請旨定奪。 臣已諮桑春榮嚴覈守城之練，裁汰冗濫，以節糜費。 練歸官統，如不奉調派，自行出隊，即按軍法從事，庶一事權而免掣肘。 」疏入，詔褫黃琮、竇墉職，許回民悔悟自新，其負固不服者，痛加剿辦。 漢民借團練為名肆行殺掠者，以軍法從事。 於是振棫遣漢、回委員赴省城曉諭漢、回，解釋猜嫌，分畫所居街道，撥抵難民遺產，議定章程，遣散歸業。 先後剿平霑益回匪，殲咸寧土匪李廣沅。 八年四月，撫局粗定，入駐省城，偕張亮基籌辦迤西剿撫事宜。 臨安回匪攻府城，遣兵擊走之，又敗之於阿迷州，解河西縣之圍。
He added: 'Retired Grand Secretary Huang Cong, Censor Dou Yong, and Regional Commander Zhou Fengqi were charged with militia training and set up a headquarters in the capital. Zhou Fengqi, disagreeing, withdrew on grounds of conflict. Huang and Dou issued joint notices demanding harsh suppression; civilians rushed to drill while Hui distrust deepened daily. Officials struggled to disband units, yet Han mobs stormed government offices demanding executions of Hui. Thus what was vital elsewhere became Yunnan's gravest plague. They claimed six hundred thousand provincial militiamen could hold off any foe. When fewer than a thousand Hui rebels first appeared outside the walls, summoned militia either failed to show or broke and ran. Over ten thousand capital militia now cost tens of thousands monthly—funds cannot cover it. Captains lead private bands beyond full official control. Hui willing to submit were attacked by militia seeking glory, so pacification never took hold. Huang and Dou were imperial appointees beyond my authority—I beg the throne to decide.' I have instructed Sang Chunrong to audit capital garrison militia, cut waste, and save funds. Militia must answer to official command; unauthorized sorties will face military justice so authority stays unified.' The throne stripped Huang and Dou, allowed repentant Hui to reform, and ordered harsh action against die-hards. Han who used militia as cover for murder and looting faced military law. Zhenyu then sent Han and Hui commissioners to the capital to explain grievances, demarcate quarters, settle refugee estates, set rules, and send people home. He suppressed Zhanyi Hui rebels and destroyed Xianning bandit Li Guangyuan. By the fourth month of year 8 pacification was roughly in place; he entered the capital with Zhang Liangji to manage western campaigns. Lin'an Hui rebels besieged the prefectural seat; his troops drove them off, defeated them at Ami, and lifted the siege of Hexi.
4
是年冬，以病乞罷，因子春傑官雁平道，就養山西。 同治元年，命會同巡撫英桂防河，尋命赴陝西會辦軍務。 十年，卒，詔依例賜卹。
That winter he resigned for illness and retired to Shanxi where his son Chunjie served on the Yanping circuit. In Tongzhi 1 he joined Governor Yinggui on river defense, then was sent to Shaanxi for joint military affairs. He died in year 10; the court granted the usual mourning stipend.
5
=張亮基=張亮基，字石卿，江蘇銅山人。 道光十四年舉人，入貲為內閣中書。 從大學士王鼎赴河南治河，督築西壩。 工竣，賜花翎，擢侍讀。 二十六年，出為雲南臨安知府，總督林則徐曾與共事河工，知其才，密薦可大用，調署永昌。 邊夷滋擾，亮基用土弁左大雄擒匪首，事乃定。 超擢雲南按察使，就遷布政使。 三十年，擢雲南巡撫，兼署雲貴總督。 粵匪漸熾，嘗密疏論軍事，文宗韙之。
Zhang Liangji, style Shiqing, was from Tongshan in Jiangsu. A provincial graduate in Daoguang 14, he bought appointment as Grand Secretariat secretary. He accompanied Grand Secretary Wang Ding to Henan for river works and oversaw the western embankment. On completion he received the peacock feather and promotion to reader. In year 26 he became Lin'an prefect in Yunnan; Lin Zexu, who knew his ability from river work, secretly recommended him and had him act at Yongchang. When border tribes stirred, Liangji had native officer Zuo Daxiong seize the rebel chief and restore calm. He was fast-tracked to judicial commissioner and then administration commissioner of Yunnan. In year 30 he became Yunnan governor and acting Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. As Cantonese rebellion intensified he sent secret military memorials that Emperor Wenzong endorsed.
6
咸豐二年，調湖南巡撫，在途聞賊圍長沙，疏請駐守常德。 詔趣進解省城之圍，至則梯城而入，屢出隊與城外援軍夾擊，賊解圍去。 破岳州，入湖北，漢陽、武昌相繼陷，湖廣總督徐廣縉以罪罷，命亮基代之，規進剿。 亮基疏言宜防賊回竄，意在專顧湖南，詔趣速進。 三年春，賊棄武漢東下，亮基抵湖北籌辦收復撫卹事宜。 通城、崇陽、嘉魚、廣濟土匪起，平之。 賊自下游分竄江西，亮基督師扼道士洑、黃石港，分兵赴援。 秋，賊之分竄河南者，由羅山入湖北黃安、麻城境，水陸夾擊，殲之。
Transferred to Hunan in Xianfeng 2, he learned en route that rebels besieged Changsha and asked to hold Changde. Ordered to relieve the capital, he scaled the walls, sallied repeatedly with outside relief, and broke the siege. Rebels took Yuezhou and swept into Hubei; Hanyang and Wuchang fell; Xu Guangjin was dismissed and Liangji ordered to replace him for a counteroffensive. Liangji urged guarding against a southern return—really to keep focus on Hunan—until the throne ordered him forward. In spring of year 3 rebels abandoned Wuhan for the east; Liangji reached Hubei to organize recovery and relief. He suppressed local bandits in Tongcheng, Chongyang, Jiayu, and Guangji. When downstream rebels split toward Jiangxi, Liangji held Daoshifu and Huangshigang and sent detachments to assist. That autumn Henan-bound raiders entered Huang'an and Macheng via Luoshan; he destroyed them in combined land-and-water attacks.
7
調山東巡撫，未行，江西賊由九江來犯，令道員徐豐玉御之於田家鎮，戰失利，豐玉陣亡，亮基坐降四級留任。 時粵匪李開芳等犯畿輔，踞靜海。 亮基至山東，奉命扼德州，防其南逸。 南路賊欲由淮、徐窺伺北犯為應援，令按察使厲恩官率兵駐宿遷之北以防之。 四年，賊入山東境，亮基馳扼濟寧，杜其北竄。 尋陷鄆城，擾範縣、壽張、東平，繞出賊前截擊，敗之於臨清黑家莊。 既奏捷，幫辦軍務大臣勝保劾其取巧冒功，詔斥亮基欺罔，並追論初赴湖南不急趨長沙，及去湖北時但求自全，居心狡詐，職，遣戍軍台。 逾年，給事中毛鴻賓言臨清之役，勝保妄劾，御史宗稷辰亦言亮基能任事，未盡其用，乃釋回，發東河差遣，尋命往安徽隨辦軍務。
Transferred to Shandong, he was still en route when Jiujiang rebels struck; Intendant Xu Fengyu died at Tianjiazhen in defeat and Liangji was demoted four ranks but kept his post. Cantonese rebels led by Li Kaifang meanwhile invaded the capital approaches and held Jinghai. Reaching Shandong, he was told to hold Dezhou against a southern breakout. Expecting a Huai-Xu probe to support the northern drive, he posted Li En'guan north of Suqian. In year 4 rebels entered Shandong; he rushed to Jining to block a northern escape. They seized Yuncheng and raided Fan, Shouzhang, and Dongping; he cut ahead and routed them at Heijiazhuang near Linqing. After his victory report, Sheng Bao accused him of fraud; the throne called Liangji deceitful, cited his slow march to Changsha and self-preserving exit from Hubei, stripped his rank, and exiled him to the garrison posts. A year later Mao Hongbin showed Sheng Bao's charge at Linqing was false; Censor Zong Jichen argued Liangji was underused; he was recalled, sent to Eastern River duty, then Anhui military affairs.
8
七年，予五品頂戴，命赴雲南幫辦剿匪事宜。 雲南迴匪方熾，團練橫行省會，總督吳振棫初至，駐曲靖，裁抑練勇，招撫回眾。 霑益回最悍，集眾犯宣威，亮基督按察使徐之銘等率兵擊走之。 八年春，又敗之於袁家屯，殲賊甚眾，餘黨就撫，詔嘉之，授雲南巡撫。 既而振棫乞罷，擢雲貴總督，亮基薦徐之銘代為巡撫。 臨安回匪攻城，擾及阿迷，剿平之。 九年，省回就撫後，踞碧雞關，劫奪近郊，分剿乃散。 又剿平彝、安寧、緬寧、楚雄諸匪，武定、羅次、富民、祿豐、祿勸諸州縣先後克復。 然回、練互相猜忌，亂機時起。
In year 7 he received fifth-rank cap rank and orders to Yunnan to help suppress rebels. Yunnan Hui rebellion raged and capital militia ruled the streets; Wu Zhenyu, newly arrived at Qujing, was curbing militia and wooing Hui submission. The fiercest Zhanyi Hui gathered to strike Xuanwei; Liangji had Xu Zhiming and others repulse them. In spring of year 8 he routed them at Yuanjiatun, killed many, and accepted the rest; the court praised him and made him Yunnan governor. When Zhenyu resigned, Wu became governor-general and Liangji recommended Xu Zhiming as governor. Lin'an Hui besieged the city and threatened Ami; he suppressed them. In year 9 pacified capital Hui seized Biji Pass and raided suburbs until dispersed by divided attacks. He also cleared rebels in Yi, Anning, Mianning, and Chuxiong and recovered Wuding, Luoci, Fumin, Lufeng, and Luquan. Still, Hui and militia distrusted each other and unrest flickered on.
9
徐之銘既為巡撫，貪縱險狠，與亮基陰不相能，時構煽其間。 十年秋，回人掌教馬德新、徐元吉，武生馬現，率各屬回民來省乞撫，住城外江右館，亮基約之銘同詣撫諭。 之銘陰嗾已散練丁擁至督署阻撓，諭之不可，殺通海知縣雷焱於門，遂逼殺招撫委員紳士馬椿齡、孫鈞。 亮基為所脅持，不敢入告，以病乞罷，命劉源灝代之。 源灝久不至，亮基迳去。 十一年，至湖北，乃疏陳滇事，劾之銘不法。 會布政使鄧爾恆升任陝西巡撫，去滇，之銘嗾匪戕於路。 於是罷源灝，以潘鐸署總督，命亮基赴滇查辦，督師剿匪。 亮基疏請發部照募損充餉，募勇千人然後行，與潘鐸先後至四川，欲資其餉力、兵力。 四川兵事未定，無以濟之。 林自清者，亮基之舊部，方署雲南提督，與之銘及馬如龍等皆不協，回人仇之。 聞亮基在四川，擅率所部號萬人入川求效用，阻之不聽。 詔亮基撫諭解散，而之銘嗾馬如龍等聲言拒亮基不使入境，相持久之。 同治元年，潘鐸先抵任，請暫留之銘以畢撫局，遂改命亮基以總督銜署貴州巡撫。 未幾，之銘复陰嗾回眾​​為變，鐸被戕，而雲南之亂愈亟矣。
Governor Xu Zhiming proved greedy, brutal, and secretly hostile, constantly stirring trouble against Liangji. In autumn of year 10 Ma Dexin, Xu Yuanji, and Ma Xian led district Hui to beg pacification, lodging at the Jiangyou hostel outside the walls; Liangji asked Xu Zhiming to join him in parley. Xu secretly rallied disbanded militia to block the governor's yamen; when persuasion failed they killed Tonghai magistrate Lei Yan at the gate and forced the murder of pacification commissioners Ma Chunling and Sun Jun. Coerced, Liangji dared not report upward; he pleaded illness and Liu Yuanhao was named to succeed. Yuanhao delayed; Liangji departed without waiting. In year 11, reaching Hubei, he memorialized on Yunnan and impeached Xu's crimes. As Administration Commissioner Deng Erheng left for Shaanxi governor, Xu incited rebels to murder him on the road. Yuanhao was removed, Pan Duo named acting governor-general, and Liangji ordered to Yunnan to investigate and command suppression. Liangji asked for ministry licenses to raise funds by sale of office, to recruit a thousand men before marching; he and Pan Duo reached Sichuan hoping for money and troops. Sichuan itself was still at war and could offer no help. Liangji's former subordinate Lin Ziqing, acting Yunnan commander, clashed with Xu Zhiming and Ma Rulong and was hated by the Hui. Learning Liangji was in Sichuan, Lin marched ten thousand men into the province on his own authority; orders to stop him failed. The throne told Liangji to disband them, while Xu incited Ma Rulong to bar Liangji from Yunnan, prolonging the impasse. In Tongzhi 1 Pan Duo arrived and asked to keep Xu Zhiming to finish pacification; Liangji was instead named acting Guizhou governor with governor-general rank. Soon Xu again stirred Hui rebellion; Pan Duo was murdered and Yunnan collapsed further.
10
二年，亮基至貴州，黃號、白號、苗、教諸匪並熾，上下游遍地皆賊。 亮基令總兵沈宏富等攻遵義螺螄堰，破之，殲餘匪於上稽場。 令總兵劉義方等剿思南教匪，复普安、安南，又連破苗匪於桐梓鼎城及水城廳馬龍胯，擒匪首何潤科等於黔西，降萬人。 三年，尚大坪匪犯省城，督沈宏富等戰於郊，殲賊千計，復修文。 總兵林自清、趙德昌克龍里，又復興義，解清鎮之圍，收復定番、廣順、長寨諸城，破龍泉、湄潭黃、白號匪老巢，克滇西衛城。 四年，克黔西石阡、永寧、荔波，貴州地瘠財匱，飢軍索餉，時虞譁噪。 亮基撫馭防剿，僅得粗安，而所部諸將多驕蹇，輿論不協，為侍讀學士景其濬論劾。 亮基乃劾總兵林自清、劉有勳，副將池有連等劫掠扣餉，不聽調度，請嚴治。 詔布政使嚴樹森察奏，亮基復具疏自陳，言樹森規避貴州，安坐鄰省不親至，於是亮基、樹森並褫職。
In year 2 he reached Guizhou to find Yellow and White Banner rebels, Miao, and sectarians raging along every reach of the river. He had Shen Hongfu break the Luosi weir at Zunyi and wipe out survivors at Shangji. Liu Yifang crushed Sinan sectarians, retook Pu'an and Annan, defeated Miao at Tongzi and Shuicheng, captured He Runke in western Guizhou, and accepted ten thousand surrenders. In year 3 Shangdaping rebels threatened the capital; suburban fighting killed thousands and Xiuwen was recovered. Lin Ziqing and Zhao Dechang took Longli, relieved Xingyi and Qingzhen, recovered Dingfan, Guangshun, and Changzhai, destroyed Longquan-Meitan sect lairs, and seized western Yunnan's Weicheng. In year 4 he took Shiqian, Yongning, and Libo while starving troops in impoverished Guizhou clamored for pay. Liangji barely kept order while his generals grew insubordinate; Reader Jing Qijun impeached him. He counter-impeached Lin Ziqing, Liu Youxun, and Chi Youlian for looting, withholding pay, and disobedience. Yan Shusen was ordered to investigate; Liangji accused Yan of avoiding Guizhou from a neighboring province—both men were dismissed.
11
十年，卒。 湖南巡撫王文韶、貴州巡撫曾璧光先後請復原銜，各建專祠。 光緒三十四年，湖南、貴州京官合詞臚陳功德在民，追諡惠肅。
He died in year 10. Wang Wenshao of Hunan and Zeng Biguang of Guizhou later secured restoration of his rank and local shrines. In Guangxu 34 capital officials from Hunan and Guizhou petitioned for his merits; he received the posthumous name Huisu.
12
=毛鴻賓=毛鴻賓，字翊雲，山東歷城人。 道光十八年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 遷御史、給事中，數上封事論軍務。 咸豐三年，以尚書孫瑞珍薦，命回籍治團練。 四年，劾幫辦軍務大臣勝保罪狀，請嚴旨查辦。 五年，授湖北荊宜施道，調安襄鄖荊道，歷安徽按察使、江蘇布政使。
Mao Hongbin, style Yiyun, came from Licheng in Shandong. He became a jinshi in Daoguang 18, entered the Hanlin, and was appointed reviser. He rose to censor and supervising secretary and repeatedly memorialized on military affairs. In Xianfeng 3, on Sun Ruizhen's recommendation, he was sent home to organize militia. In year 4 he impeached Sheng Bao and demanded a stern imperial inquiry. In year 5 he took Hubei and Anxiang circuit posts, then Anhui judicial commissioner and Jiangsu administration commissioner.
13
十一年，署湖南巡撫，尋實授。 疏言：「湖南地居僻遠，向非富強，自前撫臣張亮基、駱秉章等於吏治民風實力講求，用能削平寇盜，屹為上游重鎮，用人之效，有明徵矣。 臣以為名將不過收戰陣之功，得賢督撫，斯能造封疆之福。 如左宗棠識略過人，其才力不在曾國籓、胡林翼之下，今但使之帶勇，殊不足以盡其長，倘畀以封疆重任，必能保境安民，兼顧大局。 前任雲貴總督張亮基，果決有為，雲南壤接邊陲，餉糈不給，漢、回仇釁相尋，即令經營盡善，亦僅有益一隅，似不若任以要地，俾展所長。 但使東南日有轉機，則云、貴遊氛無難迅掃，此輕重之機宜審者也。 」時湘軍所至有功，各省多往召募，鴻賓疏陳招勇流弊，請慎選將領以收實效，並被嘉納。
In year 11 he acted as Hunan governor, then received full appointment. He wrote: 'Hunan was never naturally strong; under Zhang Liangji and Luo Bingzhang it became an upstream bastion through real attention to governance—proof that good appointments matter. Famous generals win battles; worthy governors secure whole frontiers. Zuo Zongtang rivals Zeng Guofan and Hu Linyi; leading troops alone wastes his gifts—a frontier post would let him guard the realm and serve the larger strategy. Former governor-general Zhang Liangji is decisive, yet Yunnan's border troubles and Han-Hui strife limit him to local effect; a key inland post would better use his talents. Once the southeast stabilizes, Yunnan and Guizhou will follow—that is how priorities should be weighed.' As Hunan armies succeeded, provinces rushed to recruit; Hongbin warned of recruitment abuses and urged choosing capable commanders—the court approved.
14
石達開竄湖南，鴻賓遣知府席寶田、副將周達武、總兵趙福元分路進擊，解會同、黔陽之圍。 同治元年，進復來鳳，貴州提督田興恕兼署巡撫，軍報不實，信用左右，鴻賓疏劾之。 遣兵越境剿貴州竄匪，复天柱縣城。 又剿銅仁張家寨，匪首蕭文魁率眾降，克大小青兩堡。 江藍廳同知椿齡指團紳為土匪，鴻賓廉知椿齡有酷刑逼借事，劾罷之。 椿齡京控，訐鴻賓借貸不遂，鴻賓自請查辦，下總督官文鞫訊，得白。
When Shi Dakai entered Hunan, Hongbin sent Xi Baotian, Zhou Dawu, and Zhao Fuyuan by separate routes to lift sieges at Huitong and Qianyang. In Tongzhi 1 he retook Laifeng and impeached Guizhou commander Tian Xingyu, acting governor, for false reports and favoritism. He crossed into Guizhou to chase raiders and recovered Tiancheng. At Tongren's Zhang Family Stockade, Xiao Wenkui surrendered and both Qing forts fell. Jianglan subprefect Tongling called militia leaders bandits; Hongbin found he had tortured men to extort loans and removed him. Tongling appealed in Beijing that Hongbin had denied him a loan; Hongbin asked for inquiry and was exonerated by Guan Wen.
15
擢兩廣總督，英德土匪起，令按察使張運蘭剿平之。 偕巡撫郭嵩燾奏定變通緝捕章程，獲大盜者予優擢，允之。
Promoted to Guangdong-Guangxi governor-general, he had Zhang Yunlan suppress Yingde bandits. With Governor Guo Songtao he secured approval for flexible arrest rules rewarding capture of major bandits with promotion.
16
三年，江南既复，浙、贛餘氛未靖。 鴻賓疏言：「江西南路之防猶有未備，閩、粵交界均無防兵，慮賊上竄，以粵東為尾閭。 江西當四沖之地，宜合數省兵力，乘大勝餘威，聚而殲之。 已諮曾國籓調撥勁旅，繞越寧郡、石城一帶，扼賊南竄之路，臣派一軍於閩、粵交界會同進剿。 並請敕曾國籓嚴守南贛，俾毋竄越。 」
In year 3, though Jiangnan was recovered, Zhejiang and Jiangxi still smoldered. He warned: 'Southern Jiangxi remains weakly held; Fujian and Guangdong borders lack troops; rebels may drive north with Guangdong as their rear. Jiangxi is a crossroads—several provinces should combine forces while victory is fresh and destroy them together. He had asked Zeng Guofan for elite troops to block escape routes through Ning and Shicheng while he sent a column to the Fujian-Guangdong border. He also asked the throne to order Zeng to seal southern Ganzhou against breakout.' '
17
四年，坐前在湖南，道員胡鏞請諮引見，繳回咨文，委署道缺，降一級調用，回籍。 七年，卒。 宣統初，山東巡撫袁樹勳疏陳鴻賓功績，復原官，祀鄉賢祠。
In year 4 he was demoted one rank and sent home for mishandling Hunan intendant Hu Yong's audience request years earlier. He died in year 7. Early in Xuantong, Shandong governor Yuan Shuxun restored his rank and enshrined him among local worthies.
18
=張凱嵩=張凱嵩，字云卿，湖北江夏人。 道光二十五年進士，廣西即用知縣，歷宣化、懷集、臨桂知縣。 李星沅、勞崇光並薦其能，咸豐五年，擢慶遠知府。 剿平土匪王得勝等，擢左江道，調署右江道。 慶遠失守，革職留任。 八年，偕按察使蔣益澧破賊，克慶遠，復原官，署按察使，尋實授，遷布政使。 同治元年，巡撫劉長佑赴潯州籌剿撫，留凱嵩經畫後路。 荔浦張皋友陷陽朔，遣兵敗賊於鷓鴣岩，復其城，就擢巡撫。 諸匪中黃鼎鳳、張皋友最猖獗，分陷貴縣、陽朔、麕集大鹿灘、馬瀨，檄總兵李明惠、提督江忠義先剿馬瀨，進規貴縣，破之於桂嶺，殲擒賊首張皋友、陳土養。 二年，檄布政使劉坤一攻黃鼎鳳於登龍橋。 賊走覃塘，進圍之。 信都賊陳金剛等來援，道員蔣澤春逆擊敗之，進克容縣，坤一克覃塘。 三年，克天平寨，擒黃鼎鳳。 貴縣平，加頭品頂戴。
Zhang Kaisong, style Yunqing, was from Jiangxia in Hubei. A jinshi in Daoguang 25, he was posted directly as Guangxi magistrate at Xuanhua, Huaiji, and Lingui. Recommended by Li Xingyuan and Luo Chongguang, he became Qingyuan prefect in Xianfeng 5. After suppressing Wang Desheng and other bandits he rose to Zuojiang intendant and acted at Youjiang. When Qingyuan fell he was stripped of rank but kept on duty. In year 8 he and Jiang Yili retook Qingyuan, regained his rank, became judicial then administration commissioner. In Tongzhi 1 Governor Liu Changyou went to Xunzhou for campaigns while Kaisong managed the rear. When Zhang Gaoyou seized Yangshuo, Kaisong routed him at Zhegu Rock, recovered the city, and was promoted governor. Huang Dingfeng and Zhang Gaoyou were the worst, seizing Guixian, Yangshuo, Dalu Beach, and Mala; Li Minghui and Jiang Zhongyi crushed them at Guiling and captured Zhang and Chen Tuyang. In year 2 he sent Liu Kunyi against Huang Dingfeng at Denglong Bridge. Rebels fled to Qintang and he pressed the siege. Chen Jingang of Xindu marched to relieve them; Jiang Zechun defeated him, took Rong County, and Liu Kunyi took Qintang. In year 3 Tianping Stockade fell and Huang Dingfeng was captured. With Guixian pacified he received the first-rank official cap.
19
疏陳左右江積匪未清，議三路進兵，以劉坤一統七營留防潯州，易元泰統十一營由賓州、遷江達思恩，李士恩統水陸八營由橫州達南寧，節節進剿。 四年，坤一攻克大廟、江口、平菼，斬賊首梁安邦，南寧河道始通。 元泰剿上林，平之。 坤一擢江西巡撫去，以同知劉培一代領其軍，將親赴南寧督戰，會偽康王汪海洋竄粵，將入廣西，詔凱嵩駐防潯州。 五年，凱嵩至南寧，進攻山澤，督諸軍穴地轟城，奪山入，擒偽平章蘇仲熙等。 孫仁廣單騎走旺隴，追斬之。 山澤為賊所踞十餘年，至此悉平。
He proposed a three-pronged campaign: Liu Kunyi holding Xunzhou, Yi Yuantai marching from Bin and Qianjiang toward Si'en, and Li Shien from Heng toward Nanning. In year 4 Liu Kunyi took Damiao, Jiangkou, and Ping'an, killed Liang Anbang, and opened the Nanning waterways. Yi Yuantai pacified Shanglin. Liu Kunyi left for Jiangxi governor, Liu Peiyi took his troops, and Kaisong was ordered to hold Xunzhou when Wang Haiyang threatened Guangxi from Guangdong. In year 5 at Nanning he tunneled under Shanzhe, stormed the heights, and captured the rebel minister Su Zhongxi. Sun Renguang fled alone toward Wanglong and was run down and killed. Shanzhe, held by rebels over ten years, was finally cleared.
20
六年，擢雲貴總督。 自潘鐸被戕，滇事益紛。 行至巴東，稱病，三疏請罷，坐規避，褫職。 光緒六年，以五品京堂起用，授通政使參議，遷內閣侍讀學士，署順天府尹，授貴州巡撫。 十年，調雲南。 請於省城設開採五金總局，以興礦利，偕內閣學士周德潤勘越南界務。 十二年，卒於官。 廣西巡撫李秉衡疏陳凱嵩政績，請建專祠，廣西京官論其不當，罷之。 子仲炘，光緒三年進士，由翰林御史官至通政司參議，敢言有聲。
In year 6 he became Yunnan-Guizhou governor-general. After Pan Duo's murder Yunnan grew only more chaotic. At Badong he pleaded illness three times, was judged to be evading duty, and dismissed. In Guangxu 6 he reentered service as transmission vice commissioner, Hanlin reader, acting Shuntian prefect, then Guizhou governor. In year 10 he was transferred to Yunnan. He proposed a capital bureau for mining metals and joined Zhou Derun in surveying the Vietnam border. He died in office in year 12. Li Bingheng sought a shrine for Kaisong, but Guangxi capital officials objected and the proposal was dropped. His son Zhongxin, a Guangxu 3 jinshi, rose from Hanlin censor to transmission vice commissioner with a reputation for outspokenness.
21
=【論】=論曰：雲南地居邊遠，回、漢積仇，中原多故之秋，幾為王靈所不及。 吳振棫兼籌剿撫，實體中朝措置之難。 張亮基才足有為，誤用徐之銘，受其排擠，遂至不可收拾。 自潘鐸被戕之後，無人敢任其艱危。 毛鴻賓疏言內地寇平，邊方自靖，誠為確論。 張凱嵩因規避黜，後仍以舊勞起用，朝廷固鑑其情已。
The commentators note: remote Yunnan, with Han-Hui vendettas amid central chaos, nearly slipped beyond imperial control. Wu Zhenyu's combined pacification and suppression reflected how hard the court's task was. Zhang Liangji was capable but erred in trusting Xu Zhiming, whose intrigues ruined what he had built. After Pan Duo's death none would face Yunnan's peril. Mao Hongbin was right that pacifying the interior would calm the frontier. Zhang Kaisong was dismissed for evasion yet later restored—the court had weighed his case fairly.
