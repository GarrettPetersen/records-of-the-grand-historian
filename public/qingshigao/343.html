1
書麟，字紱齋，高佳氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人，大學士高晉子。 初授鑾儀衛整儀尉，累遷冠軍使，擢西安副都統。 乾隆三十八年，大軍徵金川，命為領隊大臣，從參贊大臣豐升額，力戰輒先登，克堅碉數十，功最。 金川平，加等議敘，圖形紫光閣。 授廣西巡撫，以父憂去。 起，署兵部侍郎。
Shu Lin, whose courtesy name was Fuzhai, belonged to the Gaojia clan and the Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner; he was the son of Grand Secretary Gao Jin. He began as an orderly officer in the Ceremonial Guard, rose through successive posts to Champion Messenger, and was then promoted to vice commander at Xi'an. In Qianlong 38, when the imperial forces marched against Jinchuan, he was named expedition leader under Commissioner Feng Sheng'e; in hard fighting he repeatedly led the assault, took dozens of fortified stockades, and won the highest distinction. After Jinchuan was pacified, his service was rewarded with graded honors, and his likeness was enshrined in the Hall of Purple Splendor. He was made governor of Guangxi but resigned when his father died. Recalled from mourning, he served as acting vice minister of war.
2
四十九年，出為安徽巡撫，歲旱，請留漕糧五萬石、關稅銀三十五萬兩賑之。 阜陽有荒地六千餘頃，疏請寬限清釐，民間交易用官弓丈量，以杜欺隱，期於漸復舊額。 帝以書麟盡心民瘼，予優敘。 黃、運兩河漫溢，帝因兩江總督李世傑未諳河工，命書麟佐之。 與世傑及河督李奉翰議，漫口有四，惟司家莊、湯家莊兩處分溜，急興工堵築； 又奏：「桃源境內河流因順黃壩生有淤灘，水勢紆折不暢。 於玉皇閣下挑引河，俾黃流東注會清，以資宣洩。 」
In the forty-ninth year he became governor of Anhui; when drought struck that year, he asked to retain fifty thousand piculs of transport grain and 350,000 taels of customs revenue for famine relief. More than six thousand qing of wasteland lay in Fuyang; he petitioned for more time to clear and register it, required private land deals to be measured with the official survey bow to curb fraud, and aimed to restore the former tax quota over time. The emperor rewarded him with special commendation for his devoted attention to the people's distress. When the Yellow and Grand Canal rivers burst their banks, the emperor had Shu Lin assist Governor-General Li Shijie of the Two Jiangs, who was unfamiliar with hydraulic engineering. He conferred with Li Shijie and River Commissioner Li Fenghan: of four breach points, only Sijiazhuang and Tangjiazhuang were diverting flow, and they urgently launched dike-building works there. He also reported: "In Taoyuan the river had silted up below the Shunhuang Dam, so the current meandered sluggishly. A cut should be made below the Jade Emperor Pavilion to send the Yellow River east into the Qing River and ease the flood. "
3
五十二年，擢兩江總督。 書麟素行清謹，出巡屬邑，輕騎減從，民不擾累，特詔嘉之。 和珅柄政，書麟與之忤。 未幾，有高郵巡檢陳倚道揭報書吏假印重徵事，遣重臣鞫實，坐書麟瞻徇，下部嚴議； 又失察句容書吏侵用錢糧，褫職，遣戍伊犁。 尋起為山西巡撫。 內閣學士尹壯圖論州縣虧空由於派累，疆臣中惟李世傑、書麟獨善其身，和珅尤忌之，命壯圖赴各省清查倉庫，自山西始，壯圖因獲譴。 五十六年，仍授兩江總督。 兩淮鹽政巴寧阿交結商人，坐書麟徇庇，复奪職，予三等侍衛，赴新疆效力。
In the fifty-second year he was raised to governor-general of the Two Jiangs. Shu Lin was known for probity and restraint; on inspection tours he traveled lightly with a small escort so the people were not burdened, and the throne issued a special commendation. After Heshen came to dominate the government, Shu Lin fell out with him. Soon afterward a Gaoyou inspector named Chen Yidao exposed clerks who had used forged seals to collect excess taxes; the court sent a senior official to investigate, found Shu Lin guilty of favoritism, and referred the case for severe punishment. He was further faulted for failing to detect Jurong clerks who had misappropriated tax grain, stripped of rank, and exiled to Yili. He was soon recalled and made governor of Shanxi. Academician Yin Zhuangtu of the Grand Secretariat argued that local deficits came from cumulative exactions; of the frontier governors only Li Shijie and Shu Lin had kept their hands clean, which Heshen especially resented. Heshen sent Zhuangtu to audit provincial granaries, starting in Shanxi, and Zhuangtu was punished in the affair. In the fifty-sixth year he was again made governor-general of the Two Jiangs. When Lianghuai salt controller Baning'a was found in league with merchants, Shu Lin was again dismissed for covering for him, given the rank of third-class bodyguard, and sent to Xinjiang on penal service.
4
嘉慶四年，和珅敗，召授吏部尚書，兼正紅旗漢軍都統，加太子少保。 尋協辦大學士，授閩浙總督。 弟廣興，以首發和珅姦擢官，既得官，多所彈擊，書麟不善所為，嘗於帝前言之。 至是，廣興以掌四川軍需獲咎，書麟請嚴治，且自引罪，詔宥之。 調雲貴，鞫前督富綱，得其貪婪狀，論如律； 又按問雲南巡撫江蘭諱災，得實，褫江蘭職。 時倮夷不靖，疏陳江蘭所奏不實，辦理草率，帝嘉其公正。 遂親赴黃草壩督兵分路進剿，擒賊首李文明等，遣降倮入箐招諭，曉以利害，夷眾五十二寨悔罪輸誠； 以土司苛派擾夷，立牌申禁：優詔褒賚，加太子太保。
In Jiaqing 4, after Heshen's downfall, he was recalled as minister of personnel, made commander of the Plain Red Chinese Martial Banner, and given the title junior guardian of the heir apparent. He was soon made associate grand secretary and governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. His younger brother Guangxing had won promotion for being first to denounce Heshen; once in office Guangxing impeached many officials, and Shu Lin disapproved—he once told the emperor so. When Guangxing was punished for mismanaging Sichuan military supplies, Shu Lin asked for strict punishment and took blame upon himself, but the throne pardoned him. Transferred to Yunnan-Guizhou, he tried former governor Fu Gang, proved his corruption, and punished him by law. He also investigated Yunnan governor Jiang Lan for concealing disasters, proved the charge, and stripped him of office. With the Luo tribes in unrest, he reported that Jiang Lan's accounts were false and his handling slipshod, and the emperor praised his impartiality. He went in person to Huangcaoba to direct a multi-pronged campaign, captured leaders such as Li Wenming, sent surrendered Luo into the ravines to preach terms, and won the submission of fifty-two Yi stockades. When native chiefs' exactions were harassing the Yi, he posted public bans; the throne rewarded him with a gracious edict and made him senior guardian of the heir apparent.
5
五年，調湖廣，督師剿襄陽青、藍、黃三號教匪。 會長齡等已敗賊瓦房口，書麟以東川、保豐為糧運要路，親往截剿。 帝念其年逾七旬，奔馳山谷間，賊情詭詐，戒毋冒險輕試。 六年，由竹山、房縣進剿徐天德，擒斬甚眾。 疏言：「勦賊之法，以固民心、培民氣為要。 撫輯得宜，賊即是民； 任其失所，民即是賊。 」帝俞之。 川匪苟文明等由陝西平利越老林圖竄房縣，偕長齡、明亮進擊，遇賊獅子崖，大敗之； 復分兵伏佘家溝、高尖山，天德等來襲，卻之。 疏請於襄陽添設提督，移協鎮於鄖陽、竹山二處。 天德等屯聚茅倫山，令孫清元等分隊破之。 因病乞解職，遣侍衛率御醫馳視。 未幾，卒於軍，帝深惜之，贈太子太傅，封一等男爵，以子吉郎阿嗣，諡文勤。 尋以倭什布治餉遲誤，詔斥書麟知而不舉，念其清廉公正，治軍成勞，奠醊恩禮仍有加焉。
In the fifth year he was transferred to Huguang to command the campaign against the Green, Blue, and Yellow sect rebels at Xiangyang. After Changling and others had routed the rebels at Wafangkou, Shu Lin saw Dongchuan and Baofeng as vital supply lines and went in person to cut them off. Mindful that he was past seventy, racing through mountain valleys against cunning rebels, the emperor warned him not to take reckless chances. In the sixth year he advanced from Zhushan and Fangxian against Xu Tiande and killed or captured a great many rebels. He wrote: "The key to suppressing rebels is to secure the people's loyalty and restore their morale. Treat people well and rebels become ordinary subjects again; leave them homeless and subjects become rebels. The emperor approved. Sichuan rebels led by Gou Wenming slipped from Pingli in Shaanxi through the old forest paths toward Fangxian; Shu Lin joined Changling and Mingliang, met them at Lion Cliff, and routed them. He then set ambushes at Shejiagou and Gaojianshan and beat back an attack by Tiande and his men. He asked to establish a provincial commander at Xiangyang and to shift deputy garrisons to Yunyang and Zhushan. When Tiande and his followers held Maolun Mountain, he sent Sun Qingyuan and others in separate columns to defeat them. Illness forced him to request relief; the court dispatched a bodyguard with an imperial physician to attend him. He soon died in the field; the emperor mourned him deeply, posthumously made him grand tutor of the heir apparent and first-class baron, with his son Jilang'a as heir, and gave him the posthumous name Wenqin. When Wo-shi-bu's delays in supply were exposed, an edict faulted Shu Lin for failing to report what he knew; yet remembering his integrity and his success in the field, the court still enhanced the funeral honors.
6
弟廣厚，乾隆四十三年進士。 由工部主事歷御史，出為江西吉南贛寧道，遷甘肅按察使。 嘉慶初，偕總兵吉蘭泰擊教匪張映祥、楊天柱於鞏昌、秦州，進蹙諸白水江，殲焉。 遷江西布政使，調甘肅。 賊出沒於岷州、禮縣間，廣厚督兵由岷州遮羊鋪遏其衝，保完善之地，境內乂安。 調廣東，坐與總督那彥成遊宴，解職，予三等侍衛，為庫車辦事大臣，調哈喇沙爾。 官至安徽、湖南巡撫。 卒。
His younger brother Guanghou became a jinshi in Qianlong 43. He rose from a clerk in the Ministry of Works to censor, then served as intendant of the Jianan-Ganning circuit in Jiangxi before becoming Gansu surveillance commissioner. Early in Jiaqing he joined Regional Commander Jilantai to fight sect rebels Zhang Yingxiang and Yang Tianzhu at Gongchang and Qinzhou, drove them to the Baishui River, and destroyed them. He became Jiangxi provincial treasurer and was then transferred back to Gansu. When rebels ranged between Minzhou and Lixian, Guanghou led troops from Minzhou to block them at Zheyangpu, secured the settled districts, and restored peace within the province. Sent to Guangdong, he was dismissed for banqueting with Governor-General Nayancheng, given third-class bodyguard rank, appointed frontier commissioner at Kuche, then transferred to Karashahr. He eventually served as governor of Anhui and Hunan. He died.
7
覺羅吉慶，隸正白旗。 父萬福，騎都尉，官江寧將軍，兼散秩大臣。 吉慶由官學生補內閣中書，遷侍讀，歷御史。 乾隆五十年，嗣世職。 擢鑲白旗蒙古副都統，累遷兵部侍郎。 命赴山東、湖南、湖北、河南讞獄，均稱旨，調戶部。
Jiqing of the Aisin Gioro clan belonged to the Plain White Banner. His father Wanfu was a commandant of cavalry who served as Jiangning general and also held extraordinary ministerial rank. Jiqing entered service from the Imperial School as a Grand Secretariat secretary, became a reader, and served as censor. In Qianlong 50 he inherited his family's hereditary post. He was promoted to vice commander of the Mongol Bordered White Banner and eventually became vice minister of war. Sent to try capital cases in Shandong, Hunan, Hubei, and Henan, he pleased the throne in each province and was transferred to the Ministry of Revenue.
8
五十六年，出為山東巡撫，歲祲，截留漕米三十萬石，撥豫、東軍船運米賑饑。 調浙江，閩海漁船赴浙洋剽掠，吉慶於島嶴編保甲，禁米出洋，嚴緝代賣盜贓； 兼署提督，獲海盜陳言等，及臨海邪匪李鶴皋，置之法。 鹽政岳謙執拗病民，劾罷之，遂兼鹽政。
In the fifty-sixth year he became governor of Shandong; when famine struck, he retained three hundred thousand piculs of transport grain and used Henan and Shandong military grain boats to deliver relief. As governor of Zhejiang he faced Fujian fishing boats raiding Zhejiang waters; he organized local militia on the islands, banned grain exports overseas, and cracked down on fencing stolen goods. Acting as provincial military commander, he captured pirates including Chen Yan and the Linhai rebel Li Hegao and executed them. When salt controller Yue Qian's stubborn policies were harming the people, he impeached and removed him and took over the salt administration himself.
9
嘉慶元年，擢兩廣總督，劾水師提督路超吉不勝任，貶超吉秩。 二年，廣西西隆亞稿寨苗匪句結貴州仲苗，竄踞八渡，率提督彭承堯進剿，克其要隘。 黔苗潛渡百樂窺泗城，令副將德昌等分路攻撲，毀苗砦十有九； 進攻亞稿，至戛雄遇賊，大敗之。 永豐、百樂等苗目渡江降，給酒食，令回寨招撫。 亞稿山路陡峻，選精卒由間道潛襲，克其巢，斬首千級，以功加太子太保，賜雙眼花翎。 亞稿之捷，投誠者十餘寨，惟附近那地、小河、廣平、蒙裡等寨猶恃險抗拒，會雲南兵至，會剿，盡克之。 賊首龍登連父子乞降，粵境悉平。 六年，命協辦大學士、總督如故。
In Jiaqing 1 he became governor-general of the Two Guangs, impeached naval commander Lu Chaoji for incompetence, and demoted him. In the second year Miao rebels of Yagao stockade in Guangxi's Xilong joined Guizhou Zhong Miao and seized Badu; he led Provincial Commander Peng Chengyao against them and took their strong points. When Guizhou Miao slipped across at Baile toward Sicheng, he sent Deputy General Dechang and others on separate routes and destroyed nineteen stockades. Pressing the attack on Yagao, he met the rebels at Gaxiong and routed them. Miao chiefs from Yongfeng, Baile, and elsewhere crossed the river to surrender; he fed them and sent them back to their stockades to win others over. Yagao's mountain paths were steep; he sent picked troops along a hidden route, stormed the rebel nest, and took a thousand heads; for this he was made senior guardian of the heir apparent and given the double-eyed peacock feather. After the Yagao victory more than ten stockades submitted, but Nadu, Xiaohe, Guangping, Mengli, and neighboring stockades still held out; Yunnan troops arrived, joined the campaign, and took them all. Rebel leaders Long Denglian and his son surrendered, and Guangdong was fully pacified. In the sixth year he was made associate grand secretary while remaining governor-general.
10
吉慶居官廉而察吏疏，博羅縣重犯越獄，司府徇隱； 又通省贓罰銀按縣派徵，為臬司漏規。 事並上聞，詔斥其因循。 陳爛屐四者，於博羅山中糾眾為添弟會，知府伊秉綬請發兵往捕，吉慶為提督孫全謀所蔽，未許。 七年，陳爛屐四果剽掠作亂，擾及數縣，遣師擒斬之。 餘黨曾鬼六复勾結永安諸賊相繼起，吉慶馳往剿捕，請調江西兵二千為助。 詔斥其張皇，始疑之。 尋敗賊於義容墟，曾清浩率眾四千餘人繳械降。 全謀擒賊渠薛文勝，暨匪眾四百餘，悉誅之。 事聞，帝以吉慶奏報前後不符，措置失當，罷協辦大學士，留總督任，命那彥成往按。
Jiqing was honest in office but lax toward his subordinates; when a serious criminal escaped jail in Boluo, prefectural officials covered it up. Fines levied province-wide by county were also being skimmed as secret fees by the provincial judge. When both matters reached the throne, an edict rebuked his slackness. Chen Lanji the Fourth rallied a Tiandi Society band in the Boluo hills; Prefect Yi Bingshou asked for troops, but Jiqing, swayed by Provincial Commander Sun Quanmou, refused. In the seventh year Chen Lanji the Fourth did raid and rebel, troubling several counties; Jiqing sent troops who captured and killed him. Remnant leader Zeng Guiliu then joined Yong'an rebels in a fresh uprising; Jiqing rushed to suppress them and asked for two thousand troops from Jiangxi. An edict rebuked his alarmism, and the court began to doubt him. He soon routed the rebels at Yirong market, and Zeng Qinghao surrendered with more than four thousand men. Quanmou captured rebel leader Xue Wensheng and more than four hundred followers and executed them all. When reports reached the throne, the emperor found Jiqing's accounts inconsistent and his handling inept, stripped him of associate grand secretary rank but kept him as governor, and sent Nayancheng to investigate.
11
吉慶復奏永安降匪多，請留兵防範，詔斥《頁》結局，解任聽勘。 巡撫瑚圖禮素與有隙，既奉密諭诇察，遂疏劾其疲輭不職，那彥成猶未至，獨鞫之，據高坐，設囚具，隸卒故加訶辱。 吉慶恚曰：「某雖不肖，曾備位政府，不可受辱傷國體！ 」因自戕。 帝聞，命那彥成陳狀，尋以吉慶素廉潔，治匪有功，無故輕生，詔免追論。
Jiqing then reported many surrendered rebels in Yong'an and asked to keep troops on guard; the throne rebuked his slipshod wrap-up, dismissed him, and ordered him to await trial. Governor Hutuli, who had long been his enemy, received a secret order to investigate and impeached him for incompetence. Before Nayancheng arrived, Hutuli tried him alone from a high seat with shackles ready, while clerks deliberately heaped abuse on him. Jiqing said in fury: "However unworthy I am, I once served in the central government and will not endure insults that dishonor the state! " He then took his own life. On hearing this, the emperor ordered Nayancheng to report the facts; mindful of Jiqing's integrity and success against bandits, and that he had died without due cause, the throne waived further prosecution.
12
子壽喜，仍襲世職，坐事黜，以弟常喜嗣。
His son Shouxi inherited the family post but was dismissed for misconduct; his younger brother Changxi succeeded instead.
13
覺羅長麟，字牧庵，隸正藍旗。 乾隆四十年進士，授刑部主事。 貌奇偉，明敏有口辯，居曹有聲。 歷郎中，出為福建興泉永道，累遷江蘇布政使。 五十一年，召授刑部侍郎。
Zhanglin of the Aisin Gioro clan, courtesy name Mu'an, belonged to the Plain Blue Banner. He became a jinshi in Qianlong 40 and was appointed a principal clerk in the Ministry of Punishments. Tall and striking in appearance, sharp and articulate, he quickly won a reputation in his bureau. He rose to director, served as intendant of Fujian's Xingquan-Yong circuit, and was promoted to Jiangsu provincial treasurer. In the fifty-first year he was recalled to the capital as vice minister of punishments.
14
五十二年，授山東巡撫，責所屬濬河道，修四十一州縣城工； 捕鉅野、汶上劇盜田玉堂等，置之法：詔嘉獎。 劾萊州知府徐大榕治平度州民羅有良獄，誤擬，大榕訴於京，刑部尚書胡季堂等往鞫，不直長麟。 帝以防河有勞，特寬之。 復以審擬濱州舉人薛對元罪失實，褫職，留修城工。 未幾，授江蘇巡撫。 嘗私行市井間訪察民隱，擒治強暴，禁革奢俗，清漕政，斥貪吏，為時所稱。
In the fifty-second year he became governor of Shandong, ordered his subordinates to dredge rivers, and repaired city walls in forty-one prefectures and counties. He captured major bandits such as Tian Yutang in Juye and Wenshang and executed them; the throne commended him. He impeached Laizhou prefect Xu Darong for a wrongful sentence in the case of Luo Youliang of Pingdu; Darong appealed to Beijing, Minister Hu Jitang investigated, and ruled against Zhanglin. The emperor spared him on account of his work on river control. He was again stripped of office when his judgment in the case of Binzhou licentiate Xue Duiyuan proved unfounded, but was kept on to supervise city repairs. He was soon appointed governor of Jiangsu. He sometimes toured the markets incognito to learn the people's grievances, punished bullies, banned wasteful customs, reformed grain transport, and expelled corrupt officials, winning wide praise.
15
五十七年，調山西。 入覲時，有市人董二誣告逆匪王倫潛匿山西某家，和申於宮門前言，務坐以逆黨。 長麟至官，訪悉某實董仇家，​​故傾陷，慨然曰：「吾發垂白，奈何滅人族以媚權相？ 」終反坐董二，和珅大忤。
In the fifty-seventh year he was transferred to Shanxi. During an imperial audience, a townsman named Dong Er falsely claimed that rebel Wang Lun was hiding in a Shanxi household; Heshen pressed the case at the palace gate and demanded a treason conviction. On taking office he found the accused family was Dong Er's personal enemy and had been framed; he declared: "My hair is already white—how can I destroy a whole clan to please a powerful minister? " He instead convicted Dong Er, to Heshen's great displeasure.
16
調浙江，擢兩廣總督，加太子少保。 整頓水師，擒獲海盜。 六十年，調署閩浙。 會將軍魁倫劾總督伍拉納、巡撫浦霖貪縱，並閩省庫藏虧絀事，命長麟按治，未得實，詔切責，乃奏婪索納賄狀。 伍拉納故和珅姻戚，帝疑長麟瞻徇，並斥其平日沽名取巧，奪職，予副都統銜，赴葉爾羌辦事。 尋授庫爾喀拉烏蘇領隊大臣，調喀什噶爾參贊大臣。 奏減回子王公年班進京行李，以卹驛站。 罷回民土貢。 有邊警，請調兵堵剿，詔以張皇斥之。
Transferred to Zhejiang, he became governor-general of the Two Guangs and junior guardian of the heir apparent. He reorganized the navy and captured pirates. In the sixtieth year he was made acting governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. When General Kuilun impeached Governors-General Wulana and Pulai for corruption and Fujian treasury deficits, Zhanglin was sent to investigate; failing at first, he was rebuked, then reported extortion and bribery. Wulana was related to Heshen by marriage; the emperor suspected Zhanglin of favoritism, rebuked his reputation-seeking, stripped his rank, and sent him to Yarkand. He was soon made expedition leader at Kurkara Usu, then Kashgar commissioner. He asked to reduce the baggage allowances for Muslim princes traveling to Beijing, to spare the courier stations. He abolished local tribute levies on Hui commoners. When border trouble arose he asked for troops; an edict rebuked his alarmism.
17
嘉慶四年，授雲貴總督，調閩浙。 五年，調陝甘。 時教匪未靖，勸民築堡團練，令川、陝、豫、楚交界處，一體仿行，募精壯難民入伍。 督師敗伍金柱於唐家河，又擊於傅家鎮。 將軍富成來援，戰歿。 复偕固原提督慶成擊賊於沔陽乾溝河。 六年，迭敗高天德、馬學禮於鐵爐川、舊州鋪、綱廠、武關，擒襄陽賊首馬應祥，詔嘉獎。 尋以副將蕭福祿搜捕汧陽悄悄會匪，濫殺邀功，仁宗疑之，诇察得實，斥長麟徇庇，停​​其議敘。 又以傅家鎮之戰，漫無籌措，致富成陣亡。 七年，召回京，降署吏部侍郎，遷禮部尚書，兼都統。 復命督兩廣，以母老留京。
In Jiaqing 4 he became governor-general of Yunnan-Guizhou, then Fujian-Zhejiang. In the fifth year he was transferred to Shaanxi-Gansu. With sect rebels still active, he urged stockades and militia, extended the policy to the Sichuan-Shaanxi-Henan-Hubei borderlands, and enrolled able refugees. He defeated Wu Jinzhu at Tangjia River and again at Fujia Town. General Fucheng came to reinforce and was killed in battle. He again joined Guyuan commander Qingcheng to attack rebels at Qiangou River in Mianyang. In the sixth year he repeatedly defeated Gao Tiande and Ma Xueli, captured Xiangyang rebel leader Ma Yingxiang, and won imperial praise. When Deputy General Xiao Fulu massacred Qianyang secret-society suspects to claim credit, the emperor investigated, found Zhanglin had shielded him, and suspended his commendation. He was also faulted for the battle at Fujia Town, where his lack of planning led to Fucheng's death. In the seventh year he was recalled, demoted to acting vice minister of personnel, made minister of rites, and given a concurrent banner command. Ordered again to the Two Guangs, he stayed in the capital because his mother was elderly.
18
八年，授兵部尚書，調刑部，兼管戶部三庫。 十年，兼翰林院掌院學士，尋協辦大學士。 十三年，命偕尚書戴衢亨察視南河。 長麟至清江浦，聞安徽諸生包世臣習河事，親訪之，同視海口，實不高仰，用其說罷改道之議。 與衢亨通籌河工，具得要領，帝嘉之。 复偕衢亨清查兩淮鹽務，責鹽政每年雜費悉報部覈銷，以息浮議。
In the eighth year he became minister of war, then punishments, and concurrently managed the three revenue treasuries. In the tenth year he became Hanlin chancellor and soon associate grand secretary. In the thirteenth year he and Minister Dai Yuheng were ordered to inspect the Southern River works. At Qingjiangpu he sought out Anhui scholar Bao Shichen, an expert on rivers; they inspected the estuary, found Bao's analysis sound, and dropped the plan to reroute the channel. He and Yuheng planned the river works together to general approval. He and Yuheng also audited Lianghuai salt finances and required full reporting of expenses to quiet rumors.
19
十五年，以目眚久在告，特詔解職。 逾年，卒，諡文敏。
In the fifteenth year prolonged eye trouble led to a special edict releasing him from office. He died the following year with the posthumous name Wenmin.
20
費淳，字筠浦，浙江錢塘人。 乾隆二十八年進士，授刑部主事。 歷郎中，充軍機章京。 出為江蘇常州知府，父憂去。 服闋，補山西太原，擢冀寧道。 累遷雲南布政使，有惠政。 以母老乞終養，喪除，起故官。 六十年，擢安徽巡撫，調江蘇。 嘉慶二年，疏言：「淮、徐、揚三府屬被水窪地，責州縣勸植蘆葦，以收地利。 應納錢糧，即照蘆課改折徵輸。 」詔議行。 調福建，復還江蘇。 四年，擢兩江總督。
Fei Chun, courtesy name Yunpu, was from Qiantang in Zhejiang. He became a jinshi in Qianlong 28 and was appointed a principal clerk in the Ministry of Punishments. He rose to director and served as a Grand Council clerk. He became prefect of Changzhou in Jiangsu but left office when his father died. After mourning he served at Taiyuan and was promoted to Jining circuit intendant. Promoted to Yunnan provincial treasurer, he earned a reputation for benevolent rule. He retired to care for his elderly mother; after her death he returned to office. In the sixtieth year he became governor of Anhui, then Jiangsu. In Jiaqing 2 he proposed that flooded lowlands in the Huai, Xu, and Yang region be planted with reeds. Tax grain due should be assessed and collected as reed duties instead. The throne approved the plan. He served in Fujian, then returned to Jiangsu. In the fourth year he became governor-general of the Two Jiangs.
21
淳歷官廉謹，為帝所重，兩淮鹽政徵瑞與淳為姻家，免其迴避。 時南河比歲漫溢，淳以江督事繁，自陳未諳河務，乞免兼管，允之。 命淳與總河詳議河務工程，應行分辦事具聞，帝密詢漕督蔣兆奎等優劣，諭曰：「安民首在任賢，除弊必先去貪。 汝操守雖優，察吏過寬。 去一貪吏，萬姓蒙福； 進一賢臣，一方受惠。 其悉心訪聞，慎勿迎合朕意，顛倒是非。 」淳具以實聞。 有匿名訐告常州知府胡觀瀾者，下淳按治，疏糾觀瀾與江陰知縣楊世綬勒派累民，得實，請嚴譴。 詔斥不先劾，以平日廉潔，覆奏無徇隱，寬之。 尋劾鹽巡道彭翼蒙奢侈糜費，褫翼蒙職。 复劾漕運總督富綱私受衛弁餽銀，時富綱已調雲貴總督，命吉慶嚴鞫，置諸法。 漕運旗丁苦累，屢議加徵調劑，偕漕督鐵保疏陳：「原徵隨漕項下有款可撥，以裨運丁； 又旗丁月米，令州縣改給折色，應領運費，責糧道放給，以免層層剝削。 」如所請行。
Fei Chun was valued for probity; Lianghuai salt controller Zheng Rui, his in-law, was exempted from recusal rules. With the Southern River flooding yearly, he asked to be relieved of river duties as governor-general, and the request was granted. Ordered to plan river works with the director-general, he was also secretly asked about transport officials; the emperor said: "To secure the people, appoint the worthy; to end abuses, remove the greedy. Your integrity is excellent, but you are too lenient toward officials. Remove one corrupt official and the people benefit; promote one worthy minister and a whole region gains. Investigate carefully and do not tell me only what I wish to hear. Fei Chun reported faithfully. When Changzhou prefect Hu Guanlan was anonymously accused, Fei Chun proved he and Jiangyin magistrate Yang Shishou had extorted the people and asked for severe punishment. Rebuked for not acting sooner, he was spared because of his usual integrity. He soon impeached salt patrol commissioner Peng Yimeng for extravagance and dismissed him. He also impeached transport director Fu Gang for taking bribes; Fu Gang was sent to Jiqing for trial and punishment. Banner transport laborers were overburdened; he and transport director Tie Bao reported that existing transport funds could aid them; monthly grain rations should be paid in silver by local officials, and transport fees issued by the grain intendant to stop skimming. The plan was adopted.
22
五年，邵家壩河工合龍，加太子少保。 六年，以足疾乞歸醫治，允之，命毋解職。 尋稱足疾已瘳，若遵旨回籍，轉涉欺矇，詔嘉其得大臣體，賜內府藥餌。 七年，宿州土匪王潮名糾眾戕官，檄鎮將剿捕。 事定，請於宿之南平集設撫民同知，裁寧國府同知，移駐其地，並調設營汛，從之。 八年，召授兵部尚書。 時河決河南衡家樓，橫溢張秋以南，由鹽河入海，有妨漕運，命淳往勘治，於張秋西岸加寬裹頭，東岸加高長堤，以防溜勢北掣，南口趁汶水北注之勢，引歸河身； 北口自大溜迤北，分導餘流，以資挽運：並仿南河刷沙法，制混江龍鐵篦船以疏淤。 明年，糧運過張秋無阻，降詔褒賚。 調吏部尚書、協辦大學士。 十一年，偕尚書長麟按問直隸籓司書吏侵冒錢糧​​獄，鞫實，論如律。
In the fifth year, when the Shaojiaba river junction was completed, he was made junior guardian of the heir apparent. In the sixth year he asked to go home to treat a foot ailment; leave was granted without removing him from office. When he said he had recovered but could not go home without seeming to deceive the throne, the emperor praised his integrity and sent palace medicines. In the seventh year Suzhou bandit Wang Chaoming killed officials; Fei Chun ordered troops to suppress him. Afterward he proposed a new subprefect at Nanpingji and garrison adjustments; the court agreed. In the eighth year he was recalled as minister of war. When the Yellow River broke at Hengjialou and flooded toward Zhangqiu, he was sent to repair the works, widening the west bank and heightening the east dike, and using the Wen River to restore the main channel; at the north mouth surplus flow was diverted to aid transport, and sand-flushing boats were built on the southern river model. The next year grain transport passed Zhangqiu unimpeded, and he was commended. He became minister of personnel and associate grand secretary. In the eleventh year he and Zhanglin tried Zhili clerks who had embezzled tax funds and punished them by law.
23
十二年，拜體仁閣大學士，管理工部，兼管戶部三庫。 十四年，以庫銀被竊，鐫秩留任。 已，复坐失察工部書吏冒領三庫銀，詔切責，削宮銜，左遷侍郎，調兵部。 逾年，复授工部尚書。 十六年，卒，復大學士，諡文恪，祀雲南名宦。
In the twelfth year he became grand secretary of the Hall of Entirety, managed the Ministry of Works, and the three revenue treasuries. In the fourteenth year treasury silver was stolen and his rank was reduced, though he kept his post. Soon he was rebuked again for failing to detect clerks fraud in the treasuries, stripped of palace rank, and demoted to vice minister of war. A year later he was again made minister of works. He died in the sixteenth year, was posthumously restored to grand secretary, given the name Wenkai, and enshrined among Yunnan's famous officials.
24
百齡，字菊溪，張氏，漢軍正黃旗人。 乾隆三十七年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 掌院阿桂重之，曰：「公輔器也！ 」督山西學政，改御史，歷奉天、順天府丞。 百齡負才自守，不干進，邅回閒職十餘年。
Bai Ling, courtesy name Juxi, of the Zhang clan, belonged to the Chinese Martial Plain Yellow Banner. He became a jinshi in Qianlong 37, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and was made an editor. Hanlin chancellor A Gui prized him, saying: "He is grand-secretary material! " He supervised Shanxi education, became a censor, and served as vice prefect of Fengtian and Shuntian. Talented and self-contained, he sought no promotion and lingered in minor posts for more than ten years.
25
仁宗親政後，始加拔擢。 嘉慶五年，出為湖南按察使，調浙江，歷貴州、雲南布政使。 八年，擢廣西巡撫。 武緣縣有冤獄，諸生黃萬鏐等為知縣孫廷標誣擬大辟，百齡下車，劾廷標逮問，帝嘉之，賜花翎； 洎定讞，特加太子少保。 十年，調廣東。 南海、番禺兩縣蠹役私設班館，羈留無辜，為民害，重懲之； 劾罷縱容之知縣王軾、趙興武，嚴申禁令：詔予優敘。 尋擢湖廣總督。 兩湖多盜，下令擒捕，行以便宜，江、湖晏然。 未幾，王軾訐百齡在粵用非刑斃命，逼勒供應，臨行用運夫二千餘名。 總督那彥成疏劾，並及到湖北後，截留廣東會奏批摺。 命吳熊光等按鞫，議褫職遣戍，帝原之，命效力實錄館。 尋予六品頂戴，赴福建治糧餉，事竣，授汀漳龍道。 擢湖南按察使，調江蘇，以病歸。 病痊，授鴻臚寺卿，歷山東按察使，就擢巡撫。
Only after the Renzong Emperor took power was he promoted. In Jiaqing 5 he became Hunan surveillance commissioner, then Zhejiang intendant, then treasurer of Guizhou and Yunnan. In the eighth year he became governor of Guangxi. At Wuyuan, magistrate Sun Tingbiao had falsely condemned licentiates including Huang Wanlin to death; Bai Ling impeached and arrested him, winning praise and the peacock feather; when the case was decided, he was specially made junior guardian of the heir apparent. In the tenth year he was transferred to Guangdong. In Nanhai and Panyu corrupt clerks ran private detention halls; he punished them severely; impeached indulgent magistrates Wang Shi and Zhao Xingwu, and strictly enforced the ban; the throne commended him. He was soon made governor-general of Huguang. Huguang had many bandits; he ordered their capture with discretionary powers, and the region grew calm. Soon Wang Shi accused him of illegal torture and death in Guangdong, coerced supplies, and using more than two thousand transport laborers on departure. Governor-General Nayancheng impeached him and cited his retention of a joint Guangdong memorial after reaching Hubei. Wu Xiongguang and others recommended dismissal and exile; the emperor pardoned him and assigned him to the Veritable Records Office. He was soon given sixth rank, sent to Fujian for grain supplies, then made intendant of Tingzhang-Long circuit. He became Hunan surveillance commissioner, then Jiangsu, then retired ill. Recovered, he became chamberlain of the Court of Imperial Stud, Shandong surveillance commissioner, then governor.
26
十四年，擢兩廣總督。 粵洋久不靖，巨寇張保挾眾數万，勢甚張。 百齡至，撤沿海商船，改鹽運由陸，禁銷贓、接濟水米諸弊。 籌餉練水師，懲貪去懦，水師提督孫全謀失機，劾逮治罪。 每一檄下，耳目震新。 巡哨週嚴，遇盜輒擊之沉海，群魁奪氣，始有投誠意。 張保妻鄭尤黠悍，遣硃爾賡額、溫承志往諭以利害，遂勸保降，要製府親臨乃聽命。 百齡曰：「粵人苦盜久矣！ 不坦懷待之，海氛何由息？ 」遂單舸出虎門，從者十數人，保率艦數百，轟砲如雷，環船跪迓，立撫其眾，許奏乞貸死。 旬日解散二萬餘人，繳砲船四百餘號，復令誘烏石二至雷州斬之，釋其餘黨，粵洋肅清。 帝愈嘉異之，复太子少保，賜雙眼花翎，予輕車都尉世職。
In the fourteenth year he became governor-general of the Two Guangs. Guangdong waters had long been lawless; the pirate Zhang Bao commanded tens of thousands and grew formidable. On taking office he pulled merchant ships off the coast, moved salt transport inland, and banned fencing and supply to pirates. He funded and trained the navy, punished corruption and cowardice, and impeached naval commander Sun Quanmou for missing his chance. Every order he issued shook officials into alertness. Patrols tightened; pirates were sunk on sight; the leaders lost nerve and began to talk of surrender. Zhang Bao's wife Zheng was fierce and clever; Bai Ling sent Zhu Ergeng'e and Wen Chengzhi to reason with her, and she urged surrender only if the governor came in person. Bai Ling said: "Guangdong has suffered pirates long enough! If we do not meet them openly and honestly, how will the seas ever be calm? He sailed alone through Humen with a dozen men; Bao brought hundreds of warships, guns thundering, and knelt from surrounding boats; Bai Ling pacified the men and promised to seek mercy for them. Within ten days he disbanded twenty thousand men, collected four hundred gunboats, lured Wu Shier to Leizhou for execution, freed the rest, and cleared Guangdong waters. The emperor praised him further, restored his rank as junior guardian of the heir apparent, gave him the double-eyed peacock feather, and granted a hereditary commandant of light chariots.
27
十六年，再乞病，回京，授刑部尚書，改左都御史，兼都統。 未幾，授兩江總督。 時河決王家營，上游綿拐山、李家樓並漫溢，論者謂河患在雲梯關海口不暢，多主改由馬港新河入海。 百齡親勘下游，疏言：「海口無高仰形跡，亦無攔門沙堤。 其受病在上年挑河二段內積淤三千餘丈。 又親至馬港口以下，見淤沙挑費更鉅，入海路窄。 二者相較，仍以修濬正河為便。 並請加挑灶工尾以下河身，兩岸接築新堤，於七套增建減水壩，修復王營減壩，重建磨盤埽。 」詔如議。 百齡年逾六旬始生子，值帝萬壽日，聞之，賜名扎拉芬以示寵異，勉其盡心治河。 次年春，諸工先後竣，漕運渡黃較早，迭加優賚，賜其子六品廕生。 洪湖連年水漲，五壩壞其四，詔責急修。 百齡以禮壩之決，由於河督陳鳳翔急開遲閉，以致棘手，奏劾之。 鳳翔被嚴譴，訴道廳請開禮壩時，百齡同批允； 又訐淮揚道硃爾賡額為百齡所倚，司葦蕩營有弊。 言官吳雲、馬履泰並論其舉劾失當，命松筠、初彭齡往按。 帝意方鄉用，議上，專坐硃爾賡額罪，以塞眾謗。 十八年，命協辦大學士，總督如故。
In the sixteenth year he returned ill to the capital as minister of punishments, then left censor-in-chief, with a concurrent banner command. He was soon made governor-general of the Two Jiangs. When the river broke at Wangjiaying and flooded upstream, many argued the trouble was a blocked estuary at Yunti Pass and favored a new outlet at Magang. Surveying the lower river, he reported: "The estuary shows no high barrier or blocking sand dike. The problem is more than three thousand zhang of silt in last year's dredged sections. Below Magang he found dredging costlier still and the sea outlet too narrow. He concluded that repairing the main channel was still preferable. He proposed further dredging, new dikes, a flood dam at Qitao, repairs at Wangying, and rebuilding the Mopan sao. The throne approved. Past sixty, he had a son; on the emperor's birthday the boy was named Zhala Fen and Bai Ling was urged to finish the river works. Next spring the works finished, transport crossed early, and his son received sixth-rank yinsheng privilege. Hong Lake floods had broken four of five dams; an edict ordered urgent repair. He blamed the Li Dam breach on commissioner Chen Fengxiang opening late and closing early, and impeached him. Chen appealed that Bai Ling had approved opening the Li Dam with the circuit offices; he also accused Huai-Yang commissioner Zhu Ergeng'e, Bai Ling's favorite, of abuses in the reed camp. Censors Wu Yun and Ma Lvtai criticized his personnel decisions; Songyun and Chu Pengling were sent to investigate. The emperor still favored him; Zhu Ergeng'e alone was punished to quiet critics. In the eighteenth year he became associate grand secretary while remaining governor-general.
28
十九年，初彭齡奉命赴江蘇同查虧帑，議不合。 彭齡為所掣，恚甚，遂劾百齡受鹽場稅關餽遺，按之未得實，彭齡坐誣被譴。 會鹽運使廖寅捕逆犯劉第五，部鞫為偽。 百齡亦坐失入，褫宮銜，罷協辦大學士。 江南莠民散佈逆詞，連及百齡，嚴詔責捕。 二十年，獲首、從方榮陞等百五十人，並抵法，复宮銜，封三等男爵，兼署安徽巡撫。 是年冬，病甚，命松筠往代，卒於江寧。 帝聞，悼惜，詔复協辦大學士，遣侍衛賜奠，許柩入城治喪。 將遣皇子奠醊，既而以江北災民未能撫卹，停其奠醊，仍賜祭葬如例，諡文敏。 子扎拉芬，襲男爵。
In the nineteenth year Chu Pengling came to Jiangsu to audit treasury deficits with him and they clashed. Chu, thwarted, impeached Bai Ling for salt and customs bribes; the charge failed and Chu was punished for slander. Meanwhile salt commissioner Liao Yin arrested rebel Liu Diwu, but the ministry found the case false. Bai Ling was faulted for the false arrest, stripped of palace rank, and removed as associate grand secretary. Seditious tracts in Jiangnan implicated him; a stern edict ordered arrests. In the twentieth year 150 rebels including Fang Rongsheng were executed; his rank was restored, he was made third-class baron, and acted as Anhui governor. That winter, gravely ill, he was replaced by Songyun and died at Jiangning. The emperor mourned him, restored his associate grand secretary rank, sent libations, and allowed his coffin into the city. A prince's sacrifice was planned but canceled because north Jiang refugees were unrestored; standard funeral honors remained, with posthumous name Wenmin. His son Zhala Fen inherited the barony.
29
伯麟，字玉亭，瑚錫哈哩氏，滿洲正黃旗人。 由繙譯舉人授兵部筆​​帖式，擢右春坊右贊善，累遷內閣學士。 乾隆五十七年，授盛京兵部侍郎，尋授山西巡撫。
Bo Lin, courtesy name Yuting, of the Husihali clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain Yellow Banner. From translator licentiate to war ministry clerk, he rose to right palace tutor and grand secretariat academician. In Qianlong 57 he became Mukden vice minister of war, then governor of Shanxi.
30
嘉慶九年，擢雲貴總督。 十年，緬甸與暹羅屬夷戛於臘構釁，求助於孟連土司刀派功，往援遇害，失其印。 伯麟以刀派功禍由自取，惟責暹羅繳所得印。 十一年，緬甸請預期納貢。 伯麟知其與暹羅構兵，為求助地，卻之。 後緬甸為戛於臘所敗，果來乞援，伯麟拒勿應，戛於臘旋亦敗走。 緬兵次車里土司界，嚴兵守邊，移檄訓戒，緬兵遂退。 迤南江外倮匪入邊劫掠，遣普洱鎮總兵那林泰剿平之。 十三年，緬甸四大萬頭目來請十三板納地，伯麟責其冒昧，諭以十三板納為九龍江土司所轄，俱屬內地，毋生覬覦，詔嘉其得體。 十四年，入覲，賜花翎。
In Jiaqing 9 he became governor-general of Yunnan-Guizhou. In the tenth year Burma and Siamese Gayaunla clashed; Menglian chief Dao Paigong died aiding them and lost his seal. Bo Lin blamed Dao Paigong and demanded only that Siam return the seal. In the eleventh year Burma asked to pay tribute early. Knowing Burma fought Siam for aid, he refused. When defeated Burma begged for help, Bo Lin refused; Gayaunla was soon beaten off. Burmese troops neared Cheli; he reinforced the border, sent a warning dispatch, and they withdrew. Luo raiders crossed the frontier; Pu'er commander Na Lintai suppressed them. In the thirteenth year Burmese chiefs sought thirteen-ban land; Bo Lin rebuked them and affirmed it as inner territory under Jiulong River; the throne praised his tact. In the fourteenth year he attended court and received the peacock feather.
31
十七年，騰越邊外野寨頭目拉幹出擾，遣兵擒之。 緬寧、騰越要隘舊設土練一千六百名，久廢，規復其制，給曠土耕種。 僧銅金從倮夷李文明為亂，已悔罪投誠，更姓名為張輔國，充南興土目； 至是復勾結倮眾侵擾，伯麟赴緬寧督土司會剿。 十八年正月，進逼南興，破其巢，輔國就戮，邊境肅清。 增設騰越鎮馬鹿塘、大壩二汛。
In the seventeenth year border chief Lagan raided; troops captured him. He restored 1,600 abandoned native militia at Tengyue and Mianning and granted land for farming. Monk Tongjin had followed Li Wenming, repented, renamed himself Zhang Fuguo, and became Nanxing native officer; he then rallied Luo raiders again; Bo Lin led native offices against him from Mianning. In the eighteenth year, first month, he stormed Nanxing, executed Fuguo, and pacified the border. He added garrisons at Malutang and Daba under Tengyue command.
32
二十二年，臨安邊外夷人高羅衣自稱窩泥王，偽署官職，糾眾萬餘，攻殺土目龍定國，擾瓦渣、溪處兩土司境，渡江窺伺內地，伯麟親往剿平之。 議定善後條規，使各土司綏靖夷民，以安反側。 敘功，加太子少保。 尋命協辦大學士，仍留總督任。 二十三年，羅衣從侄高老五竄藤條江外復為亂，擾及郡城。 督師剿擒之，餘黨悉殲。 增設臨安江內東、西兩路要隘塘汛官兵，以江外煙瘴最盛，降夷就撫，裁撤留防兵練。 二十五年，召授兵部尚書，兼都統。 复疏陳滇、黔邊務六事，如議行。
In the twenty-second year Yi leader Gao Luoyi beyond Lin'an styled himself king, mustered ten thousand men, killed native officers, and threatened inner territory; Bo Lin suppressed him in person. He enacted regulations so native offices could pacify the Yi and secure wavering groups. For merit he was made junior guardian of the heir apparent. He was soon made associate grand secretary while remaining governor-general. In the twenty-third year Gao Laowu rebelled again beyond Tengtiao River and threatened the prefectural seat. He crushed and captured him and destroyed the remnants. He garrisoned Lin'an's east and west routes and, as surrendered Yi were pacified beyond the malarial river, reduced retained militia. In the twenty-fifth year he was recalled as minister of war with a concurrent banner command. He memorialized six border policies for Yunnan-Guizhou, all adopted.
33
道光元年，拜體仁閣大學士，管理兵部。 尋以年老休致，仍充實錄館總裁。 三年，萬壽節，與十五老臣宴。 逾年，卒，諡文慎。
In Daoguang 1 he became grand secretary of the Hall of Entirety and managed the war ministry. He soon retired for age but remained veritable records chief compiler. In the third year he joined the fifteen elders at the longevity feast. He died the following year with posthumous name Wenshen.
34
伯麟任邊圻凡十六年，廉潔愛民，士林尤感戴之。 還朝後，以旗人生計為憂，疏陳調劑事宜，深中利弊。 論者謂有名臣風。
For sixteen years on the frontier Bo Lin was honest and beloved; scholars especially revered him. Back in the capital he memorialized on banner livelihood with keen insight into real interests. Commentators called him a minister of renown.
35
論曰：仁宗倚畀疆臣，膺重寄者，多參揆席。 書麟、吉慶並勤勞軍事，而盡瘁辱身，有幸不幸焉。 長麟、費淳先後治吳，一嚴一寬，才德互有優絀。 百齡號能臣之冠，機牙鋒銳，凌轢一時，晚節乃招物議。 如伯麟之安邊坐鎮，遺愛不湮，識量豈易及哉？
The critique says: Emperor Renzong trusted frontier governors, and many who bore heavy responsibility joined the Grand Secretariat. Shu Lin and Jiqing both toiled in war yet died in disgrace—each knew fortune and misfortune. Zhanglin and Fei Chun governed Jiangsu in turn, one strict and one mild, each with distinct gifts. Bai Ling was hailed the foremost capable minister—clever and domineering—yet his late years drew criticism. Bo Lin's steady frontier rule and lasting goodwill—who easily matched his judgment?
