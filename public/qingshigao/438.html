閻敬銘張之萬鹿傳霖林紹年
Yan Jingming, Zhang Zhiwan, Lu Chuanlin, and Lin Shaonian
3
閻敬銘，字丹初，陝西朝邑人。 道光二十五年進士，選庶吉士，散館改戶部主事。 咸豐九年，湖北巡撫胡林翼奏調赴鄂，總司糧台營務。 累遷郎中，擢四品京堂。 林翼請病，复疏薦敬銘才，授湖北按察使。 同治元年，嚴樹森繼為巡撫，亦推敬銘湖北賢能第一，署布政使。 以丁本生父憂歸，命治喪畢赴軍，未行，詔署山東鹽運使，擢署巡撫，疏乞終制，不許。 時山東教匪入新泰，捻、幅各匪犯鄒、曲阜，降眾竄陽穀、聊城。 敬銘既受任，檄總兵保德等進剿，而自督軍規淄川，克之。 已革參將宋景詩引降眾屯東昌，复叛，飭按察使丁寶楨討之。 景詩竄莘，敬銘檄軍防運河，令之曰：「使一匪潛渡者，殺無赦！ 」而自移軍博平。 已而保德、寶楨連敗賊唐邑馬橋，克王家海，別軍克甘官屯，賊遁開州。 事平，再請終制，仍不許。 三年，服除，實授。
Yan Jingming, whose courtesy name was Danchu, came from Chaoyi in Shaanxi. He passed the jinshi examination in 1845, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and after completing his term there was appointed a secretary in the Ministry of Revenue. In 1859, Hubei governor Hu Linyi had him transferred to the province and placed in overall charge of provisions and military logistics. He rose step by step to bureau director and was then promoted to a fourth-rank post in the capital. When Hu Linyi took sick leave, he again memorialized praising Jingming's ability, and Jingming was appointed judicial commissioner of Hubei. In 1862, when Yan Shusen became governor, he likewise ranked Jingming first among Hubei's able officials and appointed him acting provincial treasurer. He went home to mourn his birth father and was told to return to the army once the funeral was over, but before he could leave he was ordered to serve as acting Shandong salt transport commissioner and then acting governor. He asked to be allowed to complete the full mourning period, but the request was denied. At the time, religious rebels in Shandong had entered Xintai, Nian and Fu bandits were striking Zou and Qufu, and surrendered troops were scattering through Yanggu and Liaocheng. As soon as Jingming took office, he ordered regional commander Baode and others to press the campaign while he personally led troops to besiege Zichuan and took the city. The dismissed brigade-general Song Jingshi, who had brought surrendered troops to garrison Dongchang, rebelled again, and Jingming ordered judicial commissioner Ding Baozhen to suppress him. Jingshi fled toward Shen, and Jingming ordered troops to guard the Grand Canal with the command: "If even one rebel crosses in secret, execute without mercy! " He then shifted his own headquarters to Boping. Soon afterward Baode and Baozhen won a series of victories over the rebels at Tangyi and Maqiao, took Wangjiahai, and another column captured Ganguantun, driving the rebels toward Kaizhou. After order was restored, he again asked to complete his mourning obligations, but permission was still refused. In 1864, when his mourning period ended, he received a regular appointment to the post.
4
奏言抽調綠營兵練騎隊，朝旨允行，令即遣散募勇。 敬銘言：「東省變故頻仍，亂甫定，降眾未必革心。 綠營廢弛已久，驟裁勇易啟戎心。 臣不敢為節嗇帑項浮詞遺後患。 」又言：「兵之能強，端恃將領。 將領之材，亦資汲引。 如胡林翼、曾國籓、左宗棠倡率鄉里，楚將之名遂著。 前者僧格林沁奏稱不宜專用南勇，啟輕視朝廷之漸。 老成謀國，瞻言百里。 自古名將，北人為多。 臣北人也，恥不知兵。 以在軍久，見諸軍之成敗利鈍，必求其所以然之故。 深知不求將而言兵，有兵與無兵等。 今北方雖所在募勇，皆烏合耳。 為將者貪婪欺飾，不知尊君親上為何事，使握兵符，民變兵譁，後患滋大。 故欲強兵必先儲將。 北人之智勇兼備者，推多隆阿。 請飭多隆阿募北方將士，教之戰陣，擇其忠勇者，補授提、鎮、參、遊，俾綠營均成勁旅，何必更募勇丁？ 」時捻患熾，台臣議行團練。 敬銘言：「斂鄉里之財以為餉，集耕種之民以為兵，於事有害無益，不如力行堅壁清野之法。 」事遂寢。
He memorialized proposing to draw Green Standard troops for cavalry training. The court approved the plan and ordered him to disband the recruited braves at once. Jingming replied: "Shandong has been shaken by one crisis after another. The rebellion has only just been put down, and the surrendered troops may not yet be loyal at heart. The Green Standard forces have been neglected for years, and to disband the braves all at once could easily provoke mutiny. I dare not offer empty talk about saving money and leave trouble for those who come after me. " He went on: "An army becomes strong only through its commanders. Commanders, too, must be discovered and brought forward. Men such as Hu Linyi, Zeng Guofan, and Zuo Zongtang rallied their home regions, and the reputation of Hunan commanders spread far and wide. Earlier Sengge Rinchen had memorialized that the court should not rely exclusively on southern braves, warning that this would encourage a gradual contempt for the central government. That was the far-sighted counsel of a seasoned statesman. Throughout history, the most celebrated generals have usually come from the north. I am a northerner myself, and I am ashamed not to understand warfare. Having spent many years with the armies, I have watched their victories and defeats and always asked why things turned out as they did. I know well that to talk about armies without first finding commanders is no different from having no army at all. Today, although braves are being recruited all across the north, they are nothing more than a mob. Many commanders are greedy and deceitful and have no idea what loyalty to the throne means. Put military authority in their hands and you invite popular revolt and mutiny, with ever greater trouble to follow. If you want a strong army, you must first build up your commanders. Among northerners who combine wisdom and courage, Duo Long'a stands foremost. I ask that Duo Long'a be ordered to recruit northern officers and soldiers, train them in warfare, and promote the loyal and brave among them to posts from regional commander down to battalion commander, so that the Green Standard forces may become crack troops. Why recruit more braves at all? " At the time the Nian rebellion was raging, and censorial officials proposed organizing local militia. Jingming objected: "To squeeze the countryside for funds and turn farmers into soldiers does more harm than good. It would be better to enforce the policy of strengthening defenses and clearing the countryside. " The proposal was dropped.
5
四年，僧格林沁戰歿曹州，賊勢張，益趨張秋南，將犯省城。 敬銘督師東昌，還軍御之，增設砲劃防河，賊折而東。 移軍兗州，賊竄豐、沛。 乃檄總兵楊飛熊間道趨滕，防賊還竄。 賊果入湖濱，以飛熊扼運河，不得逞，竄徐州。 明年，賊入鉅野，游擊王心安失利。 敬銘方臥疾，強起視師東平。 兗沂曹濟道文彬督團勇擊賊，賊引去。 敬銘赴濟寧，會曾國籓商定分扼黃、運之議。 賊復大股趨鉅野、金鄉，分擾運西。 遣知府王成謙等要擊，而自督軍巡河，露宿四晝夜，賊連敗，始西遁。 有張積中者，結寨肥城黃崖，集眾自保，以不受撫，夷之。 六年，移疾歸，居久之，以工部侍郎召，不起。
In 1865, Sengge Rinchen was killed in battle at Caozhou. The rebels grew bolder and pushed farther south toward Zhangqiu, threatening the provincial capital. Jingming directed operations from Dongchang, brought his troops back to meet the threat, strengthened artillery defenses along the river, and the rebels turned east. He shifted his headquarters to Yanzhou while the rebels fled toward Feng and Pei. He then ordered regional commander Yang Feixiong to take a roundabout route to Teng to block any rebel attempt to double back. The rebels did enter the lake district, but Feixiong held the Grand Canal and blocked their advance, forcing them toward Xuzhou. The following year the rebels entered Juye, and battalion commander Wang Xin'an was defeated. Jingming was confined to his bed with illness but dragged himself up to take command at Dongping. Circuit intendant Wen Bin of the Yan-Yi-Cao-Ji circuit led local militia against the rebels and drove them off. Jingming went to Jining and met with Zeng Guofan to work out a plan for dividing control of the Yellow River and the Grand Canal. The rebels again advanced in force toward Juye and Jinxiang and sent detachments to raid the west bank of the canal. He sent prefect Wang Chengqian and others to intercept them while he personally patrolled the riverbank, sleeping in the open for four days and nights. The rebels suffered defeat after defeat and finally fled west. A man named Zhang Jizhong had fortified a stockade at Huangya in Feicheng and gathered followers for self-defense. Because he refused to submit, Jingming destroyed the stronghold and killed him. In 1867 he resigned on grounds of illness and went home. After a long retirement he was summoned as vice minister of Works but declined the appointment.
6
光緒三年，山西大饑，奉命察視賑務。 奏劾侵帑知州段鼎耀，置之法。 請裁減山、陝諸省差徭，並追彈尚書恩承、童華前奉使四川過境擾累狀，均下吏議。 八年，起戶部尚書，甫視事，以廣東布政使姚覲元、荊宜施道董俊漢賄結前任司員骫法，咸劾罷之。 兼署兵部。 疏陳興辦新疆屯田。 明年，充軍機大臣、總理衙門行走，晉協辦大學士。 十一年，授東閣大學士，仍筦戶部，賜黃馬褂。 自陳衰老，辭軍機大臣。 時上意將修圓明園，而敬銘論治以節用為本，會廷議錢法，失太后旨，因革職留任。 十三年，復職，遂乞休，章四上，乃得請。 十八年，卒，贈太子少保，諡文介。
In 1877, when Shanxi was struck by severe famine, he was ordered to inspect relief work. He memorialized against prefect Duan Dingyao for embezzling relief funds, and Duan was punished according to law. He asked that corvée levies be cut in Shanxi, Shaanxi, and other provinces, and also pursued charges against ministers En Cheng and Tong Hua for abuses committed on an earlier mission through Sichuan. All these matters were referred for official review. In 1882 he was recalled as minister of Revenue. Soon after taking office he impeached and had dismissed Guangdong provincial treasurer Yao Jingyuan and Jing-Yi-Shi circuit intendant Dong Junhan, who had bribed former ministry clerks and broken the law. He also served concurrently as acting minister of War. He memorialized proposing the establishment of military colonies in Xinjiang. The following year he was appointed to the Grand Council and the Zongli Yamen and promoted to associate grand secretary. In 1885 he was appointed Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion while continuing to head the Ministry of Revenue, and was granted the yellow riding jacket. He pleaded old age and resigned from the Grand Council. The court then intended to restore the Old Summer Palace, but Jingming always argued that government should rest on frugality. During a court debate on currency reform he fell out of favor with the empress dowager and was stripped of rank while kept in his post. In 1887 his rank was restored, but he then asked to retire. Only after submitting four memorials was his request granted. He died in 1892 and was posthumously granted the title Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent, with the posthumous name Wenjie.
7
敬銘質樸，以潔廉自矯厲，雖貴，望之若老儒。 善理財，在鄂治軍需，足食足兵，佐平大難。 及長戶部，精校財賦，立科條，令出期必行。 初直樞廷，太后頗信仗之，終以戇直早退雲。
Jingming was plain and unassuming, held himself to strict standards of integrity, and even at the height of his career looked like a humble old scholar. He was skilled at finance. In Hubei he managed military supplies so well that the armies were fed and manned, helping to end the great rebellion. As head of the Ministry of Revenue he scrutinized revenues and expenditures, drew up regulations, and saw that every order issued on schedule was enforced. When he first entered the Grand Council the empress dowager trusted him greatly, but in the end his blunt integrity brought about an early retirement.
8
張之萬，字子青，直隸南皮人。 道光二十七年，以一甲一名進士授修撰。 咸豐二年，出督河南學政。 粵賊破歸德，近偪開封，之萬條上防剿事宜，多允行。 俄，召還，授鍾郡王讀。 由侍讀累遷內閣學士。 同治元年，擢禮部侍郎，兼署工部。 嘗被詔偕太常寺卿許彭壽等匯輯前代帝王及垂簾事蹟可法戒者上之，錫名治平寶鑑。 會河南州縣以苛派擅殺為御史劉毓楠奏劾，命之萬往按，得實，巡撫鄭元善以下降黜有差，即以之萬署巡撫事。 疏陳軍興財匱，請仿湖北變通漕折，言：「汴漕一石舊折銀四兩，今請令州縣留辦公費七錢，實解司庫三兩三錢，以二兩購米實倉，餘一兩充汴餉，其三錢為通省公費。 」允行。
Zhang Zhiwan, whose courtesy name was Ziqing, came from Nanpi in Zhili. In 1847, as the top-ranked jinshi graduate of the first class, he was appointed a Hanlin compiler. In 1852 he was appointed educational commissioner of Henan. When Cantonese rebels took Guide and threatened Kaifeng, Zhiwan submitted detailed proposals for defense and suppression, most of which were approved. Soon afterward he was recalled to the capital and appointed tutor to Prince Zhong. He rose from reader in the Hanlin Academy to Hanlin academician. In 1862 he was promoted to vice minister of Rites and concurrently served as acting minister of Works. He was once ordered, together with Court of Imperial Sacrifices director Xu Pengshou and others, to compile precedents from earlier dynasties on emperors and regencies that could serve as models or warnings. The work was granted the title Precious Mirror of Good Governance. When censor Liu Yunan impeached Henan officials for harsh levies and unauthorized executions, Zhiwan was sent to investigate. He confirmed the charges, and governor Zheng Yuanshan and others were demoted or dismissed. Zhiwan was then appointed acting governor. He memorialized that wartime finances were exhausted and asked to adapt Hubei's flexible grain-transport conversion. He proposed: "Grain transport to Bian had formerly been commuted at four taels of silver per shi. I ask that prefectures and counties retain seven qian for office expenses and remit three taels three qian to the provincial treasury. Two taels should be used to buy rice for the granaries, one tael for Bian army pay, and the remaining three qian for province-wide public expenses. " The proposal was approved.
9
捻酋陳大喜犯南陽，之萬親赴汝州督師。 大喜竄阜陽，勾結皖捻，一由岳城趨楊莊偪雷堰，一入張岡，總兵張曜馳擊破之。 團練大臣毛昶熙諸軍相繼至，連戰皆捷，斬逆酋張鳳舞，汝南肅清。 之萬駐軍許州，既分遣諸將設防，自引軍還省； 而亳捻乘虛襲許，陷兩寨，坐降二級留任。 西捻張總愚竄鄧州，藍大順走西坪，謀與合。 張曜既敗總愚重陽店，乘勝襲西坪，大順亦敗走。 之萬復進汝州。 三年，移屯南陽，賊犯開封，還軍擊走之。 四年，遷河道總督。 僧格林沁戰歿曹州，督兵大臣皆獲咎。 之萬亦革職留任，以助防省城功，給二品頂戴。 五年，移督漕運。 捻入徐州，之萬以里下河為財賦所出，嚴防清、淮及六塘河諸要地。 六年，淮軍獲賴文光於揚州，東捻平。 捷聞，賜之萬花翎、頭品頂戴。 七年，會剿西捻，總愚溺死，東南大定。 之萬疏陳江北善後事宜。 九年，調江蘇巡撫。 遷浙閩總督，以母老乞養歸。
When Nian chieftain Chen Daxi attacked Nanyang, Zhiwan went in person to Ruzhou to take command. Daxi fled to Fuyang and joined forces with Anhui Nian rebels. One column advanced from Yuecheng toward Yangzhuang to threaten Leiyan, while another entered Zhanggang. Regional commander Zhang Yao rushed to attack and defeated them. Militia commissioner Mao Changxi and other forces arrived in succession, winning every engagement. Rebel chieftain Zhang Fengwu was killed, and Runan was pacified. Zhiwan stationed his army at Xuzhou. After posting generals to defensive positions, he led his main force back to the provincial capital; but Bozhou Nian rebels seized the opportunity to strike Xuzhou, capturing two stockades. Zhiwan was demoted two ranks but kept in office. Western Nian leader Zhang Zongyu fled to Dengzhou while Lan Dashun retreated to Xiping, planning to join forces. Zhang Yao had already defeated Zongyu at Chongyangdian and, pressing his advantage, attacked Xiping, where Dashun was also routed. Zhiwan again advanced to Ruzhou. In 1864 he moved his headquarters to Nanyang. When rebels threatened Kaifeng, he brought his troops back and drove them off. In 1865 he was appointed director-general of waterways. When Sengge Rinchen was killed at Caozhou, the senior commanders were all held accountable. Zhiwan was also stripped of rank but kept in his post. For helping defend the provincial capital he was granted a second-rank official button. In 1866 he was transferred to supervise the grain transport system. When the Nian entered Xuzhou, Zhiwan tightened defenses along the Qing, Huai, and Liutang rivers, knowing that the Lixia River region was the empire's fiscal heartland. In 1867 the Huai army captured Lai Wenguang at Yangzhou, and the eastern Nian rebellion was ended. When news of the victory arrived, Zhiwan was granted a peacock feather and a first-rank official button. In 1868 he joined the campaign against the western Nian. Zongyu drowned, and the southeast was finally pacified. Zhiwan memorialized on postwar reconstruction in the region north of the Yangzi. In 1870 he was transferred to governor of Jiangsu. He was appointed governor-general of Zhejiang and Fujian but asked to retire to care for his aged mother and returned home.
10
光緒八年，起兵部尚書，調刑部。 十年，入軍機，兼署吏部，充上書房總師傅、協辦大學士。 十五年，授體仁閣大學士，轉東閣。 賜雙眼花翎、紫韁。 二十年，免直軍機。
In 1882 he was recalled as minister of War and then transferred to the Ministry of Punishments. In 1884 he entered the Grand Council, concurrently served as acting minister of Personnel, and was appointed chief tutor in the Upper Study and associate grand secretary. In 1889 he was appointed Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion and then transferred to the Eastern Pavilion. He was granted the double-eyed peacock feather and a purple bridle. In 1894 he was relieved of duty in the Grand Council.
11
之萬入直凡十年，領樞密者為禮親王世鐸，治尚安靜，故得無事。 及日韓事棘，之萬乃先罷退。 又二年，以病致仕。 卒，年八十七，贈太傅，諡文達。
Zhiwan served on the Grand Council for ten years in all. Prince Li Shixie led the council and favored a quiet approach, so affairs passed without major disturbance. When tensions over Korea and Japan grew acute, Zhiwan was the first to leave the council. Two years later he retired because of illness. He died at eighty-seven and was posthumously granted the title Grand Tutor, with the posthumous name Wenda.
12
鹿傳霖，字滋軒，直隸定興人。 父丕宗，官都勻知府，死寇難，諡壯節，傳霖其第五子也。 當丕宗守都勻時，叛苗麕聚城下，傳霖方率健卒迎餉，聞警，馳還助城守，相持十閱月，援絕城陷。 傳霖投總督告父死狀，大兵攻复都勻，奉父母遺骸歸葬，時年甫二十，由是知名。 以舉人從欽差大臣勝保徵捻，授同知。 同治元年，成進士，選庶吉士，散館改廣西知縣。 以督剿柳、雒土匪功，賜孔雀翎，擢桂林知府。 光緒四年，調廉州。 時李揚才將叛擾越南，急捕之，立散其黨。 旋升惠潮嘉道。 擢福建按察使，調四川，遷布政使。 九年，授河南巡撫，清釐州縣納糧積弊，歲增三十餘萬。 十一年，調陝西，引疾歸。 十五年，再出撫陝。 值黃河西囓，將與洛通。 傳霖增築石壩三十餘座，得無患。 中日構釁，遣兵入衛，命兼攝西安將軍。 二十一年，擢四川總督。 蜀故多盜，特立一軍捕治之。 夔、萬大饑，發上游積穀，又採湖北糧米平糶。
Lu Chuanlin, whose courtesy name was Zixuan, came from Dingxing in Zhili. His father Pizong had served as prefect of Duyun and died fighting bandits, receiving the posthumous name Zhuangjie. Chuanlin was his fifth son. While Pizong was defending Duyun, rebel Miao besieged the city. Chuanlin was leading picked troops to fetch supplies when he heard the alarm, galloped back to help defend the walls, and held out for ten months until supplies were exhausted and the city fell. Chuanlin went to the governor-general to report his father's death. After the main army recovered Duyun, he brought his parents' remains home for burial. He was only twenty, and from that time his name became known. As a provincial graduate he followed imperial commissioner Sengbao in the campaign against the Nian and was appointed subprefect. In 1862 he passed the jinshi examination, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and after completing his term there was appointed a county magistrate in Guangxi. For merit in suppressing bandits in Liu and Luo, he was granted a peacock feather and promoted to prefect of Guilin. In 1878 he was transferred to Lianzhou. At that time Li Yangcai was plotting rebellion and trouble in Vietnam. Chuanlin arrested him quickly and immediately broke up his faction. He was soon promoted to intendant of Huizhou, Chaozhou, and Jiaying. He was promoted to judicial commissioner of Fujian, transferred to Sichuan, and then appointed provincial treasurer. In 1883 he was appointed governor of Henan, cleared away long-standing abuses in tax collection, and increased annual receipts by more than three hundred thousand taels. In 1885 he was transferred to Shaanxi and retired because of illness. In 1889 he returned to office as governor of Shaanxi. At that time the Yellow River was eroding westward and threatened to join the Luo River. Chuanlin built more than thirty stone dams and averted the disaster. When the Sino-Japanese War broke out, he sent troops to guard the capital and was ordered concurrently to act as regional commander at Xi'an. In 1895 he was promoted to governor-general of Sichuan. Sichuan had long been troubled by bandits, and he established a special force to hunt them down. When Kuizhou and Wanzhou suffered severe famine, he released stored grain from upriver and also brought rice from Hubei to sell at fair prices.
13
是時英、俄交窺西藏，藏番恃俄援，梗英畫界。 英嗾廓爾喀與藏構兵，而瞻對土民苦藏官苛虐，思內附。 傳霖以瞻對為蜀門戶，瞻不化服，無以威藏番； 藏番不聽命，則界無時定。 而英之忌俄者益急圖藏，藏亡瞻必隨亡，行且​​及於蜀。 會硃窩、章谷土司爭襲事起，傳霖檄知府羅以禮、知縣穆秉文往諭，以提督周萬順統防邊各軍進駐打箭爐。 瞻酋仔仲則忠札霸以兵侵章谷，抗我軍。 傳霖乘機進發，迭克諸要害。 各土司讋服，率兵聽調。 渡雅龍江抵瞻巢，斬馘過當，盡收三瞻地，乃請歸流改漢，條陳善後之策，疏十數上。 會成都將軍恭壽、駐藏辦事大臣文海交章言其不便，達賴复疏訴於朝，廷議中變，傳霖解職去。
At that time both Britain and Russia were watching Tibet. Tibetan monks relied on Russian support and obstructed British efforts to demarcate the border. Britain incited Nepal to fight Tibet, while the people of Zhandui, oppressed by harsh Tibetan rule, wished to submit to the Qing. Chuanlin argued that Zhandui was the gateway to Sichuan: unless Zhandui submitted, there would be no way to intimidate the Tibetans; and unless the Tibetans obeyed, the border could never be settled. The British, fearing Russia, were all the more eager to seize Tibet. If Tibet were lost, Zhandui would follow, and Sichuan would soon be threatened. When disputes over succession arose among the Zhujiao and Zhanggu chieftains, Chuanlin sent prefect Luo Yili and magistrate Mu Bingwen to instruct them and ordered regional commander Zhou Wanshun to lead frontier troops to garrison Dajianlu. The Zhandui chieftain Zai Zhongze Zhongba invaded Zhanggu with troops and resisted the Qing army. Chuanlin seized the opportunity to advance and captured one strategic point after another. The native chieftains submitted in fear and brought their troops under his command. He crossed the Yalong River to the Zhandui stronghold, killed many, and recovered all three Zhandui districts. He then memorialized to place them under direct administration and assimilate the population, submitting more than a dozen memorials on postwar policy. But Chengdu regional commander Gong Shou and Tibet resident commissioner Wen Hai jointly memorialized against the plan, and the Dalai Lama also appealed to the court. Court opinion shifted, and Chuanlin was removed from office.
14
二十四年，召授廣東巡撫，旋移江蘇，攝兩江總督。 二十六年，拳匪亂作，傳霖募三營入衛，奔及乘輿於大同。 至太原，授兩廣總督。 旋命入直軍機，從幸長安。 擢左都御史，遷禮部尚書，兼署工部。 明年，回蹕，兼督辦政務大臣。 凡疏陳加賦括財、損民以益上者，傳霖率擯勿用； 而務汰冗費，去中飽，並奏罷不急之工：均報可。 有詔自后宮內供需皆取給內務府，戶部專掌軍國大計，實傳霖發之也。 三十年，轉吏部。 三十二年，新官制成，乃退直，專治部事。 尋仍入直，解部務，以尚書協辦大學士。 命查辦歸化城墾務大臣貽穀，論遣戍，參劾不職者數十人。
In 1898 he was summoned and appointed governor of Guangdong, soon transferred to Jiangsu, and acted as governor-general of the two Jiangs. In 1900, when the Boxer movement erupted, Chuanlin recruited three battalions to guard the court and caught up with the imperial train at Datong. At Taiyuan he was appointed governor-general of Guangdong and Guangxi. He was soon ordered into the Grand Council and followed the court to Chang'an. He was promoted to left censor-in-chief, appointed minister of Rites, and concurrently served as acting minister of Works. The following year, when the court returned to Beijing, he was also placed in charge of administrative reform. Whenever memorials proposed raising taxes, seizing wealth, and harming the people to enrich the throne, Chuanlin generally rejected them; he worked to cut redundant spending, eliminate graft, and memorialized to halt unnecessary construction projects, all of which were approved. An edict then required that all inner-palace supplies come from the Imperial Household Department while the Ministry of Revenue handled only state finances—a change that in fact originated with Chuanlin. In 1904 he was transferred to the Ministry of Personnel. In 1906, when the new official system was introduced, he left the Grand Council and devoted himself solely to ministry affairs. Soon he re-entered the Grand Council, gave up his ministry duties, and served as associate grand secretary while retaining his ministerial rank. He was ordered to investigate Guyi, commissioner for land reclamation at Guihua, found him guilty and sentenced him to banishment, and impeached several dozen incompetent officials.
15
宣統嗣立，與攝政醇親王同受遺詔，加太子少保，晉太子太保。 歷拜體仁閣、東閣大學士，兼經筵講官。 二年春，疾作，章四上，皆溫諭慰留。 七月，卒，年七十五，贈太保，諡文端。
When the Xuantong emperor succeeded, he and Prince Chun the regent received the late emperor's testament together. He was granted Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent and then promoted to Grand Guardian. In succession he was appointed Grand Secretary of the Tiren Pavilion and Eastern Pavilion and also served as lecturer at the imperial lectures. In the second spring of the Xuantong reign he fell ill and submitted four memorials requesting leave, each answered with warm edicts urging him to stay. In the seventh month he died at seventy-five, was posthumously granted Grand Tutor, and received the posthumous name Wenduan.
16
傳霖起外吏，知民疾苦。 所至廉約率下，尤惡貪吏，雖貴勢不稍貰。 其在軍機，凡事不苟同，喜扶持善類。 晚病重聽，屢乞休不獲，居恆鬱鬱雲。
Chuanlin rose from local office and understood the hardships of the people. Wherever he served he was frugal and strict with subordinates, and he especially hated corrupt officials, sparing no one however powerful. In the Grand Council he never agreed casually and liked to support men of worth. In his later years illness left him hard of hearing. He repeatedly asked to retire without success and was often depressed.
17
林紹年，字贊虞，福建閩縣人。 同治十三年進士，以編修歷充鄉會試同考官。 光緒十四年，改御史。 時議修頤和園，先是疆吏籌​​設海軍經費，輸存北洋，及園工興，陰移其費以助工，號為「進獻」。 紹年極陳：「生民疲敝，當以儉化天下，使督撫愛養百姓。 若誅求進獻，未足以言忠。 請即下詔停輸，還所進奉。 」得旨嚴飭。 會以憂去，服除，補山西監察御史。 疏嚴門禁，杜宦寺交通之漸。 十九年，陝西考官丁維禔夤緣內監得試差，复疏論之。
Lin Shaonian, whose courtesy name was Zanyu, came from Min County in Fujian. In 1874 he passed the jinshi examination and, as a Hanlin compiler, successively served as associate examiner in provincial and metropolitan examinations. In 1888 he was transferred to the censorate. At that time the court was planning repairs to the Summer Palace. Frontier officials had earlier raised funds for a navy and deposited them with the Beiyang fleet, but when palace construction began those funds were quietly diverted to the project under the name of "tribute offerings." Shaonian protested forcefully: "The people are exhausted. The ruler should lead the empire in frugality and require governors to cherish and nurture the people. Extorting tribute in the name of loyalty is not loyalty at all. I ask that an edict immediately halt such deliveries and return what has already been presented." " The court issued a stern edict rebuking the practice. He soon left office for mourning. When mourning ended he was appointed censor of Shanxi. He memorialized tightening palace security and blocking the gradual encroachment of eunuchs on politics. In 1893 Shaanxi examiner Ding Weiti had obtained his appointment through connections with palace eunuchs, and Shaonian again memorialized against it.
18
俄，授雲南昭通府知府。 邊瘠難治，土目祿爾泰橫暴，睚眥殺人，莫敢訴，猝捕戮之，眾懾而定。 期年劾罷文武吏不職者五人。 調攝雲南府，甫受事，安寧州盜劫貨戕人於塗，州牧以總督崧蕃怒緝捕不力，妄系平民二十餘。 紹年覆按，疑其枉，謁總督廷爭，卒獲正犯，出二十餘人者於死。 崧蕃愧謝，密疏薦紹年可大用。 擢迤南道，未之任，擢貴州按察使。 二十六年，遷雲南布政使，就擢巡撫，兼署雲貴總督。 廣西遊匪侵滇邊，遣將擊卻之。 招撫八達河村民之陷匪者，以斷賊接濟，益大出兵合剿。 滇境既清，乃以全力赴援廣西，而蒙自土匪乘間復發，連陷臨安、石屏。 紹年會商總督丁振鐸，檄按察使劉春霖扼通海，廣南軍躡其後，不兩月事平。 疏言督撫同城任事非便，自請裁缺，從之。 移撫貴州，而湖北、廣東兩巡撫旋亦議裁。 印江團首呂志禮、楊鑫不相能，積十餘載，相殘殺。 紹年至，以兵脅之降，仍擁眾不散，遂案誅之。
Before long he was appointed prefect of Zhaotong in Yunnan. The border was poor and hard to govern. Native official Lu Ertai was violent and killed people over trifles, yet no one dared complain. Shaonian suddenly captured and executed him, and the region was awed into submission. Within a year he impeached and dismissed five incompetent civil and military officials. He was transferred to act as prefect of Yunnan. As soon as he took office, bandits at Anning robbed and killed travelers on the road. The prefect, fearing Governor Songshan's anger over weak pursuit, falsely imprisoned more than twenty civilians. Shaonian reinvestigated, suspected they had been wronged, argued the case before the governor-general, finally obtained the real culprits, and saved more than twenty innocent people from death. Songshan apologized and secretly memorialized recommending Shaonian for high office. He was promoted to intendant of Yinan but did not take up the post and was then elevated to judicial commissioner of Guizhou. In 1900 he was moved to provincial treasurer of Yunnan, then promoted to governor and concurrently acting governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou. Guangxi roaming bandits invaded the Yunnan border, and he sent generals to drive them back. He pacified villagers along the Bada River who had joined the bandits in order to cut off rebel supplies, then sent larger forces for joint suppression. Once the Yunnan border was cleared he devoted his full strength to aiding Guangxi, but Mengzi bandits seized the opportunity to rise again and captured Lin'an and Shiping in succession. Shaonian consulted Governor-General Ding Zhentuo, ordered judicial commissioner Liu Chunlin to hold Tonghai while Guangnan troops pursued from behind, and within two months order was restored. He memorialized that having governor-general and governor in the same city was awkward for administration and asked that his own post be abolished. The request was granted. He was transferred to governor of Guizhou, and soon the governorships of Hubei and Guangdong were also slated for abolition. Yinjiang militia leaders Lü Zhili and Yang Xin had feuded for more than ten years and killed one another. When Shaonian arrived he compelled them to submit by force of arms, but they still held their followers and would not disperse, so he prosecuted and executed them.
19
紹年默察大勢，非立憲不足以救亡，請預定政體以係人心，不報。 三十一年，移廣西。 明年，內召，以侍郎充軍機大臣，兼署郵傳部尚書，授度支部侍郎。 時黑龍江新設行省，驟擢道員段芝貴為巡撫。 紹年言芝貴望輕，不稱邊帥任。 御史趙啟霖劾芝貴，因及慶親王奕劻子載振納賄漁色事，命大臣按驗所劾，稱無左證，褫啟霖職，而芝貴亦由是罷。 紹年言御史風聞言事，啟霖無罪，爭之不得，遂稱疾。
Shaonian quietly observed the general trend and believed that without constitutional government the state could not be saved. He asked that the political system be settled in advance to steady popular sentiment, but received no response. In 1905 he was transferred to Guangxi. The following year he was summoned to the capital, appointed to the Grand Council as a vice minister, concurrently acting minister of Posts and Communications, and granted the post of vice minister of the Revenue Board. At that time Heilongjiang had just been made a province, and circuit intendant Duan Zhigui was abruptly promoted to governor. Shaonian said Zhigui's standing was too slight and he was unfit for a frontier command. Censor Zhao Qilin impeached Zhigui and also implicated Prince Qing Yikuang's son Zaizhen in bribery and debauchery. Ministers were ordered to investigate, declared there was no evidence, stripped Zhao of office, and Zhigui was dismissed as a result. Shaonian argued that censors speak on rumor and that Zhao was innocent. When he could not prevail he claimed illness.
20
出為河南巡撫。 以州縣吏罄貲遠宦，人地不習，無益於杜弊。 請援漢、唐故事，免避本籍。 部議自縣丞以下，如所請行。 益飭吏治，得朝貴請託書輒焚之。 兩疏糾彈百餘人。 調倉場侍郎。
He went out as governor of Henan. Because prefectural and county officials exhausted their resources to take distant appointments and were unfamiliar with local conditions, this did nothing to stop abuses. He requested following Han and Tang precedent to exempt officials from the rule avoiding service in their home provinces. The ministry deliberated and, from assistant magistrate downward, approved as requested. He further tightened official discipline and burned recommendation letters from court nobles whenever they arrived. In two memorials he impeached more than a hundred officials. He was transferred to vice minister of the Grain Granary.
21
宣統元年，徙民政部侍郎。 時奕劻握政柄，陝西巡撫恩壽與有連，總督升允劾其贓私，不報。 俄，解升允職。 紹年召對論其事，以為賞罰不當，則是非不明。 退复具疏言之，不省。 二年，充經筵講官，署學部侍郎，改弼德院顧問大臣。 以病請告。 卒，年六十八，諡文直。
In 1909 he was transferred to vice minister of Civil Affairs. At the time Prince Qing Yikuang held real power. Shaanxi governor En Shou was connected with him, and governor-general Sheng Yun impeached En for corruption, but the court took no action. Soon afterward Sheng Yun was dismissed from office. Shaonian was summoned to discuss the affair and argued that when rewards and punishments are misapplied, right and wrong cannot be distinguished. After leaving the audience he submitted a detailed memorial on the matter, but the court paid no attention. In 1910 he served as lecturer at the imperial lectures, acted as vice minister of Education, and was appointed advisory minister of the Bide Academy. He requested sick leave. He died at sixty-eight and received the posthumous name Wenzhi.
22
論曰：同、光以後，世稱軍機權重，然特領班王大臣主其事耳。 次者僅乃得參機務。 光、宣之際，政既失馭，權乃益紛，雖當國無以為治焉。 敬銘質樸，之萬練達，傳霖廉約，紹年勁直，其任封疆、治軍旅多有績，而立朝不復有所建樹。 敬銘初欲得君專國政，為勢所限，終不能行其志，世尤惜之。
The historian comments: After the Tongzhi and Guangxu reigns, people spoke of the Grand Council as all-powerful, but in fact only the princely minister who led the council truly directed its affairs. Those below him could only take part in deliberations. By the late Guangxu and Xuantong reigns the government had already lost control, power grew ever more fragmented, and even those nominally in charge could no longer govern effectively. Jingming was plain, Zhiwan experienced, Chuanlin frugal, and Shaonian firm and upright. In frontier posts and military affairs they often achieved much, yet at court they accomplished little of lasting note. Jingming had once hoped to win the emperor's trust and direct state policy himself, but circumstances prevented him from carrying out his aims, and later generations especially regretted it.
