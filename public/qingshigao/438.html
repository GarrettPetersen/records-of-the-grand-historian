閻敬銘張之萬鹿傳霖林紹年
Yan Jingming, Zhang Zhiwan, Lu Chuanlin, and Lin Shaonian
3
閻敬銘，字丹初，陝西朝邑人。 道光二十五年進士，選庶吉士，散館改戶部主事。 咸豐九年，湖北巡撫胡林翼奏調赴鄂，總司糧台營務。 累遷郎中，擢四品京堂。 林翼請病，复疏薦敬銘才，授湖北按察使。 同治元年，嚴樹森繼為巡撫，亦推敬銘湖北賢能第一，署布政使。 以丁本生父憂歸，命治喪畢赴軍，未行，詔署山東鹽運使，擢署巡撫，疏乞終制，不許。 時山東教匪入新泰，捻、幅各匪犯鄒、曲阜，降眾竄陽穀、聊城。 敬銘既受任，檄總兵保德等進剿，而自督軍規淄川，克之。 已革參將宋景詩引降眾屯東昌，复叛，飭按察使丁寶楨討之。 景詩竄莘，敬銘檄軍防運河，令之曰：「使一匪潛渡者，殺無赦！ 」而自移軍博平。 已而保德、寶楨連敗賊唐邑馬橋，克王家海，別軍克甘官屯，賊遁開州。 事平，再請終制，仍不許。 三年，服除，實授。
Yan Jingming, whose style was Danchu, came from Chaoyi in Shaanxi. He earned his jinshi degree in 1845, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and on completing his academy term was appointed a principal secretary in the Ministry of Revenue. In 1859, Hubei governor Hu Linyi had him transferred to the province to oversee grain supply and military logistics. He rose through the ranks to director and was then promoted to a fourth-rank capital post. When Hu Linyi went on sick leave, he again recommended Yan's abilities in a memorial, and Yan was appointed Hubei provincial judicial commissioner. In 1862, when Yan Shusen became governor, he likewise rated Yan Jingming the foremost talent in Hubei and named him acting provincial treasurer. He went home to observe mourning for his father and was told to rejoin the army once the mourning period ended; before he could leave, he was ordered to serve as acting Shandong salt transport commissioner and then acting governor. He asked to be allowed to finish mourning, but the court refused. At that time religious rebels in Shandong entered Xintai, Nian and Fu bandits raided Zou and Qufu, and surrendered troops scattered into Yanggu and Liaocheng. As soon as Yan took office, he ordered Brigade-General Baode and others to press the attack while he personally led troops to besiege Zichuan and took the city. The dismissed vice commander Song Jingshi had led surrendered troops to camp at Dongchang and rebelled again; Yan ordered provincial judicial commissioner Ding Baozhen to suppress him. Song fled to Shen county; Yan ordered troops to guard the Grand Canal and declared, "If even one bandit crosses in secret, execute without mercy! " He then shifted his own forces to Boping. Soon Baode and Ding Baozhen won successive victories over the rebels at Tangyi and Maqiao, took Wangjiahai, and another column captured Ganguantun, driving the rebels toward Kaizhou. After order was restored, he again asked to complete mourning, but was again refused. In 1864, when his mourning ended, he received a regular appointment.
4
奏言抽調綠營兵練騎隊，朝旨允行，令即遣散募勇。 敬銘言：「東省變故頻仍，亂甫定，降眾未必革心。 綠營廢弛已久，驟裁勇易啟戎心。 臣不敢為節嗇帑項浮詞遺後患。 」又言：「兵之能強，端恃將領。 將領之材，亦資汲引。 如胡林翼、曾國籓、左宗棠倡率鄉里，楚將之名遂著。 前者僧格林沁奏稱不宜專用南勇，啟輕視朝廷之漸。 老成謀國，瞻言百里。 自古名將，北人為多。 臣北人也，恥不知兵。 以在軍久，見諸軍之成敗利鈍，必求其所以然之故。 深知不求將而言兵，有兵與無兵等。 今北方雖所在募勇，皆烏合耳。 為將者貪婪欺飾，不知尊君親上為何事，使握兵符，民變兵譁，後患滋大。 故欲強兵必先儲將。 北人之智勇兼備者，推多隆阿。 請飭多隆阿募北方將士，教之戰陣，擇其忠勇者，補授提、鎮、參、遊，俾綠營均成勁旅，何必更募勇丁？ 」時捻患熾，台臣議行團練。 敬銘言：「斂鄉里之財以為餉，集耕種之民以為兵，於事有害無益，不如力行堅壁清野之法。 」事遂寢。
He memorialized that Green Standard troops should be drawn off to train cavalry; the court approved and ordered him to disband the recruited braves at once. Yan replied, "Shandong has been shaken by repeated crises; the rebellion has only just ended, and the surrendered men may not yet be loyal. The Green Standard forces have long been neglected; to dismiss the braves all at once could easily provoke mutiny. I dare not offer empty talk of saving money and leave trouble for those who come after. " He went on, "An army's strength depends entirely on its commanders. Commanders' talent, too, depends on being discovered and promoted. Men such as Hu Linyi, Zeng Guofan, and Zuo Zongtang rallied their home regions, and the fame of Hunan generals spread accordingly. Earlier Sengge Rinchen had memorialized that the court should not rely solely on southern braves, lest contempt for the throne take root. That was the far-sighted counsel of a seasoned statesman. Throughout history, famous generals have mostly come from the north. I am a northerner myself, and I am ashamed not to understand warfare. Having long served with the armies, I have watched their victories and defeats and always sought the reasons behind them. I know well that to discuss armies without first securing commanders is no different from having no army at all. Today, although braves are recruited everywhere in the north, they are only rabble. Their commanders are greedy and deceitful and do not know what it means to honor the throne; put arms in their hands and you invite popular revolt and mutiny, with worse trouble to follow. To build a strong army, one must first cultivate commanders. Among northerners who combine wisdom and courage, Duolong'a stands foremost. I ask that Duolong'a be ordered to recruit northern officers and soldiers, train them in warfare, and promote the loyal and brave among them to provincial, brigade, regimental, and battalion commands, so that the Green Standard armies become elite forces. Why recruit more braves? " At that time the Nian rebellion was raging, and censorial officials proposed organizing local militia. Yan objected: "To levy local wealth for pay and turn farmers into soldiers does more harm than good. It is better to enforce the policy of strengthening walls and clearing the countryside. " The proposal was dropped.
5
四年，僧格林沁戰歿曹州，賊勢張，益趨張秋南，將犯省城。 敬銘督師東昌，還軍御之，增設砲劃防河，賊折而東。 移軍兗州，賊竄豐、沛。 乃檄總兵楊飛熊間道趨滕，防賊還竄。 賊果入湖濱，以飛熊扼運河，不得逞，竄徐州。 明年，賊入鉅野，游擊王心安失利。 敬銘方臥疾，強起視師東平。 兗沂曹濟道文彬督團勇擊賊，賊引去。 敬銘赴濟寧，會曾國籓商定分扼黃、運之議。 賊復大股趨鉅野、金鄉，分擾運西。 遣知府王成謙等要擊，而自督軍巡河，露宿四晝夜，賊連敗，始西遁。 有張積中者，結寨肥城黃崖，集眾自保，以不受撫，夷之。 六年，移疾歸，居久之，以工部侍郎召，不起。
In 1865, Sengge Rinchen was killed in battle at Caozhou; the rebels grew bolder and pushed south toward Zhangqiu, threatening the provincial capital. Yan commanded at Dongchang, brought his army back to meet them, strengthened artillery defenses along the river, and the rebels turned east. He shifted his army to Yanzhou while the rebels fled toward Feng and Pei. He then ordered Brigade-General Yang Feixiong by a side route to Teng to block any rebel attempt to double back. The rebels did enter the lake district, but Yang held the Grand Canal and blocked their advance, forcing them toward Xuzhou. The following year the rebels entered Juye, and Battalion Commander Wang Xin'an was defeated. Yan was ill in bed but roused himself to take command at Dongping. Circuit Intendant Wen Bin of Yan-Yi-Cao-Ji led militia against the rebels and drove them off. Yan went to Jining and met Zeng Guofan to agree on how to divide control of the Yellow River and the Grand Canal. The rebels again advanced in force toward Juye and Jinxiang and raided the west bank of the canal in separate columns. He sent Prefect Wang Chengqian and others to intercept them while he personally patrolled the river, sleeping in the open for four days and nights until the rebels were beaten repeatedly and fled west. A man named Zhang Jizhong had fortified Huangya in Feicheng and gathered followers for self-defense; because he refused to submit, Yan destroyed the stronghold. In 1867 he resigned on grounds of illness and went home; long afterward he was summoned as vice minister of Works but declined.
6
光緒三年，山西大饑，奉命察視賑務。 奏劾侵帑知州段鼎耀，置之法。 請裁減山、陝諸省差徭，並追彈尚書恩承、童華前奉使四川過境擾累狀，均下吏議。 八年，起戶部尚書，甫視事，以廣東布政使姚覲元、荊宜施道董俊漢賄結前任司員骫法，咸劾罷之。 兼署兵部。 疏陳興辦新疆屯田。 明年，充軍機大臣、總理衙門行走，晉協辦大學士。 十一年，授東閣大學士，仍筦戶部，賜黃馬褂。 自陳衰老，辭軍機大臣。 時上意將修圓明園，而敬銘論治以節用為本，會廷議錢法，失太后旨，因革職留任。 十三年，復職，遂乞休，章四上，乃得請。 十八年，卒，贈太子少保，諡文介。
In 1877, when Shanxi suffered a great famine, he was ordered to inspect relief work. He memorialized against Prefect Duan Dingyao for embezzling relief funds and had him punished. He asked to cut corvée levies in Shanxi, Shaanxi, and other provinces, and also pursued charges against Ministers En Cheng and Tong Hua for harassing the regions they passed through on earlier missions to Sichuan; all cases were referred for judicial review. In 1882 he was recalled as minister of Revenue, and as soon as he took office he impeached and dismissed Guangdong provincial treasurer Yao Jingyuan and Jing-Yi-Shi Circuit Intendant Dong Junhan for bribing former ministry officials and breaking the law. He also served concurrently as acting minister of War. He memorialized on establishing garrison farms in Xinjiang. The following year he became a Grand Councilor and attendant at the Zongli Yamen and was promoted to associate grand secretary. In 1885 he was appointed Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion while continuing to head the Ministry of Revenue, and was granted a yellow riding jacket. He pleaded old age and resigned from the Grand Council. The court then intended to restore the Old Summer Palace, while Yan's governing principle was frugality; when the court debated currency reform he offended the empress dowager and was stripped of rank but kept in office. In 1887 his rank was restored, but he then asked to retire; only after four memorials was he allowed to leave office. He died in 1892 and was posthumously made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent with the posthumous name Wenjie.
7
敬銘質樸，以潔廉自矯厲，雖貴，望之若老儒。 善理財，在鄂治軍需，足食足兵，佐平大難。 及長戶部，精校財賦，立科條，令出期必行。 初直樞廷，太后頗信仗之，終以戇直早退雲。
Yan was plain and unadorned and held himself to strict integrity; even at the height of rank he looked like an elderly scholar. He was skilled at finance; in Hubei he managed military supplies so that armies were fed and equipped, helping to end the great rebellion. As head of the Ministry of Revenue he audited finances carefully, set regulations, and saw that every order issued on schedule was carried out. When he first entered the Grand Council the empress dowager trusted him greatly, but in the end his blunt integrity brought an early retirement.
8
張之萬，字子青，直隸南皮人。 道光二十七年，以一甲一名進士授修撰。 咸豐二年，出督河南學政。 粵賊破歸德，近偪開封，之萬條上防剿事宜，多允行。 俄，召還，授鍾郡王讀。 由侍讀累遷內閣學士。 同治元年，擢禮部侍郎，兼署工部。 嘗被詔偕太常寺卿許彭壽等匯輯前代帝王及垂簾事蹟可法戒者上之，錫名治平寶鑑。 會河南州縣以苛派擅殺為御史劉毓楠奏劾，命之萬往按，得實，巡撫鄭元善以下降黜有差，即以之萬署巡撫事。 疏陳軍興財匱，請仿湖北變通漕折，言：「汴漕一石舊折銀四兩，今請令州縣留辦公費七錢，實解司庫三兩三錢，以二兩購米實倉，餘一兩充汴餉，其三錢為通省公費。 」允行。
Zhang Zhiwan, whose style was Ziqing, came from Nanpi in Zhili. In 1847, as top graduate of the jinshi examination, he was appointed Hanlin compiler. In 1852 he was sent out as educational commissioner of Henan. When Taiping rebels took Guide and threatened Kaifeng, Zhang submitted detailed plans for defense and suppression, most of which the court approved. Soon he was recalled to serve as tutor to Prince Zhong. He rose from reader in waiting to Grand Secretariat academician. In 1862 he was promoted to vice minister of Rites and concurrently served as acting minister of Works. He was once ordered, together with Taichang Temple Director Xu Pengshou and others, to compile precedents from past emperors and regencies that could serve as models or warnings; the work was titled Precious Mirror of Good Governance. When Censor Liu Yunan impeached Henan officials for harsh levies and unauthorized executions, Zhang was sent to investigate, confirmed the charges, and had Governor Zheng Yuanshan and others demoted or dismissed; Zhang was then named acting governor. He memorialized that wartime finances were exhausted and asked to follow Hubei's flexible grain-tax conversion, proposing: "Bian grain transport formerly converted one shi to four taels of silver; now let prefectures and counties retain seven qian for office expenses, remit three taels three qian to the provincial treasury, use two taels to buy rice for the granary, devote one tael to Bian pay, and use the three qian as general provincial expenses. " The proposal was approved.
9
捻酋陳大喜犯南陽，之萬親赴汝州督師。 大喜竄阜陽，勾結皖捻，一由岳城趨楊莊偪雷堰，一入張岡，總兵張曜馳擊破之。 團練大臣毛昶熙諸軍相繼至，連戰皆捷，斬逆酋張鳳舞，汝南肅清。 之萬駐軍許州，既分遣諸將設防，自引軍還省； 而亳捻乘虛襲許，陷兩寨，坐降二級留任。 西捻張總愚竄鄧州，藍大順走西坪，謀與合。 張曜既敗總愚重陽店，乘勝襲西坪，大順亦敗走。 之萬復進汝州。 三年，移屯南陽，賊犯開封，還軍擊走之。 四年，遷河道總督。 僧格林沁戰歿曹州，督兵大臣皆獲咎。 之萬亦革職留任，以助防省城功，給二品頂戴。 五年，移督漕運。 捻入徐州，之萬以里下河為財賦所出，嚴防清、淮及六塘河諸要地。 六年，淮軍獲賴文光於揚州，東捻平。 捷聞，賜之萬花翎、頭品頂戴。 七年，會剿西捻，總愚溺死，東南大定。 之萬疏陳江北善後事宜。 九年，調江蘇巡撫。 遷浙閩總督，以母老乞養歸。
When Nian chieftain Chen Daxi attacked Nanyang, Zhang went in person to Ruzhou to take command. Chen fled to Fuyang and joined forces with Anhui Nian rebels; one column advanced from Yuecheng toward Yangzhuang to threaten Leiyan, another entered Zhanggang; Brigade-General Zhang Yao rushed to attack and defeated them. Militia Commissioner Mao Changxi and other forces arrived in succession and won every battle; rebel chieftain Zhang Fengwu was beheaded and Runan was pacified. Zhang stationed troops at Xuzhou, dispatched generals to set up defenses, and then led the main force back to the provincial capital; but Bozhou Nian rebels seized the chance to raid Xuzhou and took two stockades, for which Zhang was demoted two ranks but kept in office. Western Nian leader Zhang Zongyu fled to Dengzhou while Lan Dashun retreated to Xiping, intending to join forces. Zhang Yao had already defeated Zhang Zongyu at Chongyangdian, then pressed the attack to Xiping and routed Lan Dashun as well. Zhang again advanced to Ruzhou. In 1864 he shifted camp to Nanyang; when rebels threatened Kaifeng he brought his army back and drove them off. In 1865 he was transferred to director-general of waterways. When Sengge Rinchen was killed at Caozhou, every supervising commander was blamed. Zhang too was stripped of rank but kept in office; for helping defend the provincial capital he was given a second-rank cap button. In 1866 he was transferred to supervise grain transport. When the Nian entered Xuzhou, Zhang, knowing the Lixiahe region was the province's fiscal heartland, strictly defended key points on the Qing and Huai rivers and the Liutang River. In 1867 the Huai Army captured Lai Wenguang at Yangzhou and the eastern Nian rebellion ended. On news of the victory he was granted a peacock feather and a first-rank cap button. In 1868 he joined the campaign against the western Nian; Zhang Zongyu drowned and the southeast was finally pacified. Zhang memorialized on postwar arrangements north of the Yangtze. In 1870 he was transferred to governor of Jiangsu. He was promoted to governor-general of Zhejiang and Fujian, but asked to retire and return home because his mother was elderly.
10
光緒八年，起兵部尚書，調刑部。 十年，入軍機，兼署吏部，充上書房總師傅、協辦大學士。 十五年，授體仁閣大學士，轉東閣。 賜雙眼花翎、紫韁。 二十年，免直軍機。
In 1882 he was recalled as minister of War and then transferred to the Ministry of Punishments. In 1884 he entered the Grand Council, concurrently served as acting minister of Personnel, and was appointed chief tutor of the Upper Study Hall and associate grand secretary. In 1889 he was appointed Grand Secretary of the Hall of Embodied Benevolence and then transferred to the Eastern Pavilion. He was granted double-eyed peacock feathers and a purple bridle. In 1894 he was relieved of duty in the Grand Council.
11
之萬入直凡十年，領樞密者為禮親王世鐸，治尚安靜，故得無事。 及日韓事棘，之萬乃先罷退。 又二年，以病致仕。 卒，年八十七，贈太傅，諡文達。
Zhang served in the Grand Council for ten years altogether; Prince Li Shiduo led the council and favored a quiet approach to governance, so affairs remained largely untroubled. When the Japan-Korea crisis grew acute, Zhang was the first to step down. Two years later he retired on grounds of illness. He died at the age of eighty-seven and was posthumously made Grand Tutor with the posthumous name Wenda.
12
鹿傳霖，字滋軒，直隸定興人。 父丕宗，官都勻知府，死寇難，諡壯節，傳霖其第五子也。 當丕宗守都勻時，叛苗麕聚城下，傳霖方率健卒迎餉，聞警，馳還助城守，相持十閱月，援絕城陷。 傳霖投總督告父死狀，大兵攻复都勻，奉父母遺骸歸葬，時年甫二十，由是知名。 以舉人從欽差大臣勝保徵捻，授同知。 同治元年，成進士，選庶吉士，散館改廣西知縣。 以督剿柳、雒土匪功，賜孔雀翎，擢桂林知府。 光緒四年，調廉州。 時李揚才將叛擾越南，急捕之，立散其黨。 旋升惠潮嘉道。 擢福建按察使，調四川，遷布政使。 九年，授河南巡撫，清釐州縣納糧積弊，歲增三十餘萬。 十一年，調陝西，引疾歸。 十五年，再出撫陝。 值黃河西囓，將與洛通。 傳霖增築石壩三十餘座，得無患。 中日構釁，遣兵入衛，命兼攝西安將軍。 二十一年，擢四川總督。 蜀故多盜，特立一軍捕治之。 夔、萬大饑，發上游積穀，又採湖北糧米平糶。
Lu Chuanlin, whose style was Zixuan, came from Dingxing in Zhili. His father Pizong had served as prefect of Duyun and died fighting bandits, receiving the posthumous name Zhuangjie; Chuanlin was his fifth son. While Pizong was defending Duyun, rebel Miao gathered outside the walls; Chuanlin was leading picked troops to meet a supply convoy, but on hearing the alarm he galloped back to help defend the city. The siege lasted ten months until supplies were cut off and the city fell. Chuanlin reported his father's death to the governor; imperial forces recaptured Duyun, and he brought his parents' remains home for burial. He was only twenty, and his fame dates from that episode. As a provincial graduate he followed Imperial Commissioner Sheng Bao in the campaign against the Nian and was appointed subprefect. In 1862 he earned his jinshi degree, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and on completing his academy term was appointed a county magistrate in Guangxi. For supervising the suppression of bandits in Liu and Luo, he was granted a peacock feather and promoted to prefect of Guilin. In 1878 he was transferred to Lianzhou. At that time Li Yangcai was plotting rebellion and raids into Vietnam; Lu arrested him at once and broke up his following. He was soon promoted to circuit intendant of Huizhou, Chaozhou, and Jieyang. He was promoted to Fujian provincial judicial commissioner, transferred to Sichuan, and then made provincial treasurer. In 1883 he was appointed governor of Henan, cleared long-standing abuses in local grain payments, and increased annual revenue by more than three hundred thousand taels. In 1885 he was transferred to Shaanxi, then retired on grounds of illness. In 1889 he returned to govern Shaanxi. When the Yellow River was eroding westward and threatened to join the Luo, Lu built more than thirty stone dams and averted disaster. When war broke out with Japan he sent troops to guard the court and was ordered to serve concurrently as general at Xi'an. In 1895 he was promoted to governor-general of Sichuan. Sichuan had long been plagued by bandits; he formed a special force to hunt them down. When Kui and Wan suffered famine, he released stored grain from upstream and brought in Hubei rice to sell at fair prices.
13
是時英、俄交窺西藏，藏番恃俄援，梗英畫界。 英嗾廓爾喀與藏構兵，而瞻對土民苦藏官苛虐，思內附。 傳霖以瞻對為蜀門戶，瞻不化服，無以威藏番； 藏番不聽命，則界無時定。 而英之忌俄者益急圖藏，藏亡瞻必隨亡，行且​​及於蜀。 會硃窩、章谷土司爭襲事起，傳霖檄知府羅以禮、知縣穆秉文往諭，以提督周萬順統防邊各軍進駐打箭爐。 瞻酋仔仲則忠札霸以兵侵章谷，抗我軍。 傳霖乘機進發，迭克諸要害。 各土司讋服，率兵聽調。 渡雅龍江抵瞻巢，斬馘過當，盡收三瞻地，乃請歸流改漢，條陳善後之策，疏十數上。 會成都將軍恭壽、駐藏辦事大臣文海交章言其不便，達賴复疏訴於朝，廷議中變，傳霖解職去。
Britain and Russia were both watching Tibet; Tibetan tribes relied on Russian support and blocked British boundary demarcation. Britain incited the Gurkhas to fight Tibet, while the people of Drayi, suffering harsh rule by Tibetan officials, wished to come under Chinese administration. Lu held that Drayi was the gateway to Sichuan; unless Drayi submitted, there was no way to intimidate the Tibetan tribes; and if the Tibetan tribes would not obey, the border could never be settled. British opponents of Russia were pressing ever harder on Tibet; if Tibet lost Drayi it would soon lose everything, and Sichuan would be next. When the Zhukuo and Zhanggu chiefs disputed succession, Lu ordered Prefect Luo Yili and Magistrate Mu Bingwen to mediate and had Provincial Commander Zhou Wanshun lead border troops to Dartsendo. The Drayi chieftain Zizhong Zezhong Zhaba invaded Zhanggu with troops and resisted the imperial forces. Lu seized the opportunity to advance and took key positions one after another. The native chiefs submitted and brought their troops under his command. He crossed the Yalong River to the Drayi stronghold, executed many captives, recovered all three Drayi districts, and then memorialized repeatedly to place the region under regular administration and sinicize it, setting forth postwar policies in more than ten memorials. Chengdu General Gongshou and Tibetan Resident Commissioner Wen Hai jointly memorialized that the policy was unwise, and the Dalai Lama appealed to the court; the court reversed course, and Lu was dismissed.
14
二十四年，召授廣東巡撫，旋移江蘇，攝兩江總督。 二十六年，拳匪亂作，傳霖募三營入衛，奔及乘輿於大同。 至太原，授兩廣總督。 旋命入直軍機，從幸長安。 擢左都御史，遷禮部尚書，兼署工部。 明年，回蹕，兼督辦政務大臣。 凡疏陳加賦括財、損民以益上者，傳霖率擯勿用； 而務汰冗費，去中飽，並奏罷不急之工：均報可。 有詔自后宮內供需皆取給內務府，戶部專掌軍國大計，實傳霖發之也。 三十年，轉吏部。 三十二年，新官制成，乃退直，專治部事。 尋仍入直，解部務，以尚書協辦大學士。 命查辦歸化城墾務大臣貽穀，論遣戍，參劾不職者數十人。
In 1898 he was summoned as governor of Guangdong, soon transferred to Jiangsu, and served as acting governor-general of Liangjiang. In 1900, when the Boxer uprising broke out, Lu recruited three battalions to guard the court and caught up with the imperial procession at Datong. At Taiyuan he was appointed governor-general of Guangdong and Guangxi. He was soon ordered into the Grand Council and accompanied the court to Chang'an. He was promoted to left censor-in-chief, made minister of Rites, and concurrently served as acting minister of Works. The following year, when the court returned to Beijing, he also supervised administrative reform. He generally rejected memorials proposing tax increases, wealth seizures, or policies that harmed the people to enrich the throne; but pressed to cut waste, eliminate graft, and stop nonessential projects, all of which the court approved. An edict followed that inner palace supplies should come from the Imperial Household Department while the Ministry of Revenue would handle state and military finance—a reform Lu had initiated. In 1904 he was transferred to the Ministry of Personnel. In 1906, when the new official system took effect, he left the Grand Council to focus on ministry affairs. Soon he reentered the Grand Council, gave up ministry duties, and served as minister and associate grand secretary. He was ordered to investigate Guihua reclamation commissioner Yigu, who was sentenced to exile, and impeached dozens of incompetent officials.
15
宣統嗣立，與攝政醇親王同受遺詔，加太子少保，晉太子太保。 歷拜體仁閣、東閣大學士，兼經筵講官。 二年春，疾作，章四上，皆溫諭慰留。 七月，卒，年七十五，贈太保，諡文端。
When Xuantong succeeded, he and Regent Prince Chun received the late emperor's deathbed testament together; he was made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent and then Grand Guardian. He served in turn as Grand Secretary of the Hall of Embodied Benevolence and the Eastern Pavilion and also lectured at the classics colloquium. In the spring of 1910 he fell ill and submitted four retirement memorials; each time the court warmly urged him to remain. He died in the seventh month at the age of seventy-five and was posthumously made Grand Guardian with the posthumous name Wenduan.
16
傳霖起外吏，知民疾苦。 所至廉約率下，尤惡貪吏，雖貴勢不稍貰。 其在軍機，凡事不苟同，喜扶持善類。 晚病重聽，屢乞休不獲，居恆鬱鬱雲。
Lu rose from local officialdom and knew the people's hardships. Wherever he served he was frugal and restrained his subordinates, hated corrupt officials above all, and would not spare even the powerful. In the Grand Council he never agreed casually and liked to support worthy men. In his later years illness left him hard of hearing; he repeatedly asked to retire without success and lived in gloom.
17
林紹年，字贊虞，福建閩縣人。 同治十三年進士，以編修歷充鄉會試同考官。 光緒十四年，改御史。 時議修頤和園，先是疆吏籌​​設海軍經費，輸存北洋，及園工興，陰移其費以助工，號為「進獻」。 紹年極陳：「生民疲敝，當以儉化天下，使督撫愛養百姓。 若誅求進獻，未足以言忠。 請即下詔停輸，還所進奉。 」得旨嚴飭。 會以憂去，服除，補山西監察御史。 疏嚴門禁，杜宦寺交通之漸。 十九年，陝西考官丁維禔夤緣內監得試差，复疏論之。
Lin Shaonian, whose style was Zanyu, came from Min county in Fujian. He earned his jinshi degree in 1874 and as a Hanlin compiler served repeatedly as associate examiner for provincial and metropolitan examinations. In 1888 he was made a censor. The court was discussing restoration of the Summer Palace; frontier governors had earlier raised naval funds and deposited them with the Beiyang fleet, but when palace construction began they secretly diverted the money and called it "tribute offerings." Lin protested forcefully: "The people are exhausted; the court should lead the realm by example in thrift and require governors to care for the people. Extorting such offerings is not loyalty. I ask that the court immediately order the payments stopped and the offerings returned. " The court issued a stern rebuke. He then went on mourning leave, and on returning was appointed surveillance censor in Shanxi. He memorialized to tighten palace security and block eunuchs from forming outside connections. In 1893, when Shaanxi examiner Ding Weiti obtained his assignment through eunuch connections, Lin memorialized against the practice again.
18
俄，授雲南昭通府知府。 邊瘠難治，土目祿爾泰橫暴，睚眥殺人，莫敢訴，猝捕戮之，眾懾而定。 期年劾罷文武吏不職者五人。 調攝雲南府，甫受事，安寧州盜劫貨戕人於塗，州牧以總督崧蕃怒緝捕不力，妄系平民二十餘。 紹年覆按，疑其枉，謁總督廷爭，卒獲正犯，出二十餘人者於死。 崧蕃愧謝，密疏薦紹年可大用。 擢迤南道，未之任，擢貴州按察使。 二十六年，遷雲南布政使，就擢巡撫，兼署雲貴總督。 廣西遊匪侵滇邊，遣將擊卻之。 招撫八達河村民之陷匪者，以斷賊接濟，益大出兵合剿。 滇境既清，乃以全力赴援廣西，而蒙自土匪乘間復發，連陷臨安、石屏。 紹年會商總督丁振鐸，檄按察使劉春霖扼通海，廣南軍躡其後，不兩月事平。 疏言督撫同城任事非便，自請裁缺，從之。 移撫貴州，而湖北、廣東兩巡撫旋亦議裁。 印江團首呂志禮、楊鑫不相能，積十餘載，相殘殺。 紹年至，以兵脅之降，仍擁眾不散，遂案誅之。
Soon he was appointed prefect of Zhaotong in Yunnan. The border was poor and hard to govern; native chieftain Lu Ertai was violent and killed over trifles with impunity. Lin arrested and executed him at once, and order was restored. Within a year he impeached and dismissed five incompetent civil and military officials. Transferred to act as prefect of Yunnan, he had barely taken office when bandits in Anning robbed and killed travelers on the road; the local prefect, fearing Governor Songfan's anger over poor policing, wrongly detained more than twenty civilians. Lin reinvestigated, suspected a miscarriage of justice, went to the governor and argued the case in court, eventually found the real culprits, and saved more than twenty innocent men from execution. Songfan apologized and secretly recommended Lin for higher office. He was promoted to circuit intendant of Yinan but did not take the post and was made Guizhou provincial judicial commissioner instead. In 1900 he was transferred to Yunnan provincial treasurer, then promoted to governor and concurrently served as acting governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou. When Guangxi roaming bandits raided the Yunnan border, he sent generals to drive them back. He pacified villagers along the Badu River who had joined the bandits, cutting off rebel supplies, and then sent larger forces for a joint campaign. Once Yunnan was pacified he sent full aid to Guangxi, but Mengzi bandits seized the chance to rise again and took Lin'an and Shiping in succession. Lin consulted Governor Ding Zhenduo, ordered Provincial Judicial Commissioner Liu Chunlin to hold Tonghai while Guangnan troops pursued from behind, and within two months the rebellion was suppressed. He memorialized that having governor and governor-general in the same city was awkward, asked to abolish the redundant post, and the court agreed. He was transferred to Guizhou, and soon the governorships of Hubei and Guangdong were also slated for abolition. Yinjiang militia leaders Lü Zhili and Yang Xin had been feuding for more than ten years and killing each other. When Lin arrived he threatened them with troops to submit; they still kept their forces together, so he prosecuted and executed them.
19
紹年默察大勢，非立憲不足以救亡，請預定政體以係人心，不報。 三十一年，移廣西。 明年，內召，以侍郎充軍機大臣，兼署郵傳部尚書，授度支部侍郎。 時黑龍江新設行省，驟擢道員段芝貴為巡撫。 紹年言芝貴望輕，不稱邊帥任。 御史趙啟霖劾芝貴，因及慶親王奕劻子載振納賄漁色事，命大臣按驗所劾，稱無左證，褫啟霖職，而芝貴亦由是罷。 紹年言御史風聞言事，啟霖無罪，爭之不得，遂稱疾。
Lin saw clearly that only constitutional government could save the dynasty and asked that the political system be fixed in advance to steady public opinion, but received no reply. In 1905 he was transferred to Guangxi. The following year he was summoned to the capital, served as vice minister on the Grand Council, concurrently headed the Ministry of Posts and Communications, and was appointed vice minister of the Treasury Board. Heilongjiang had just been made a province, and Circuit Intendant Duan Zhigui was abruptly promoted to governor. Lin said Duan's reputation was too slight for a frontier command. Censor Zhao Qilin impeached Duan and also accused Prince Qing Yikuang's son Zaizhen of bribery and debauchery; grandees were ordered to investigate, found no proof, and stripped Zhao of office, but Duan was dismissed as well. Lin argued that censors were meant to report on hearsay and that Zhao was innocent; when he failed to prevail he claimed illness.
20
出為河南巡撫。 以州縣吏罄貲遠宦，人地不習，無益於杜弊。 請援漢、唐故事，免避本籍。 部議自縣丞以下，如所請行。 益飭吏治，得朝貴請託書輒焚之。 兩疏糾彈百餘人。 調倉場侍郎。
He was sent out as governor of Henan. Because county officials often exhausted their fortunes to serve far from home and knew neither the people nor the locality, which did nothing to stop corruption, he asked to follow Han and Tang precedent and exempt lower officials from the rule barring service in one's home province. The ministry approved his request for all posts from assistant magistrate downward. He tightened official discipline further and burned any letters of recommendation from court nobles. In two memorials he impeached more than a hundred officials. He was transferred to vice minister of the granary depots.
21
宣統元年，徙民政部侍郎。 時奕劻握政柄，陝西巡撫恩壽與有連，總督升允劾其贓私，不報。 俄，解升允職。 紹年召對論其事，以為賞罰不當，則是非不明。 退复具疏言之，不省。 二年，充經筵講官，署學部侍郎，改弼德院顧問大臣。 以病請告。 卒，年六十八，諡文直。
In 1909 he was transferred to vice minister of Civil Affairs. Prince Qing Yikuang then held power; Shaanxi Governor Enshou was connected with him; Governor-General Sheng Yun impeached Enshou for corruption, but the court took no action. Soon Sheng Yun was dismissed instead. Lin was summoned to discuss the matter and argued that when rewards and punishments were misapplied, right and wrong became impossible to discern. After leaving audience he memorialized again on the point, but the court paid no attention. In 1910 he served as classics colloquium lecturer, acted as vice minister of Education, and was made advisory grandee of the Bide Academy. He then requested sick leave. He died at the age of sixty-eight and was given the posthumous name Wenzhi.
22
論曰：同、光以後，世稱軍機權重，然特領班王大臣主其事耳。 次者僅乃得參機務。 光、宣之際，政既失馭，權乃益紛，雖當國無以為治焉。 敬銘質樸，之萬練達，傳霖廉約，紹年勁直，其任封疆、治軍旅多有績，而立朝不復有所建樹。 敬銘初欲得君專國政，為勢所限，終不能行其志，世尤惜之。
The historian comments: After the Tongzhi and Guangxu reigns, the Grand Council was said to wield great power, but in fact only the leading prince grandee truly directed its affairs. Those beneath him could only take part in deliberations. By the late Guangxu and Xuantong reigns, government had lost its grip, power grew ever more fragmented, and even those nominally in charge could no longer govern effectively. Yan was plain, Zhang experienced, Lu frugal, and Lin firm and upright; all achieved much on the frontier and in military affairs, but at court they accomplished little. Yan had once hoped to win the emperor's trust and direct state policy, but circumstances blocked him and he never realized his aims—a loss the age especially regretted.
