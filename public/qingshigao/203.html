道光二十一年辛丑閏三月丁卯遷。江蘇巡撫。十二月乙巳免。代。十二月戊子遷。山西巡撫。九月丙辰遷。署河南巡撫。五月壬午調。江西巡撫。五月壬午調。湖北巡撫。七月己卯免。八月庚子代。八月丁酉差。廣東巡撫。閏三月丁卯調。廣西巡撫。 The twenty-first year of the Daoguang reign (xinchou). On the dingmao day of the intercalary third month he was appointed Governor of Jiangsu; on the yisi day of the twelfth month he was dismissed and replaced. On the wuzi day of the twelfth month he was appointed Governor of Shanxi. On the bingchen day of the ninth month he was transferred to acting Governor of Henan. On the renwu day of the fifth month he was transferred to Governor of Jiangxi. On the renwu day of the fifth month he was transferred to Governor of Hubei; on the jimao day of the seventh month he was dismissed; on the gengzi day of the eighth month he was replaced. On the dingyou day of the eighth month he was assigned on special duty as Governor of Guangdong. On the dingmao day of the intercalary third month he was transferred to Governor of Guangxi.

道光二十二年壬寅九月己未革。江蘇巡撫。五月戊辰差。署。十二月己未山東巡撫。己亥調。代。三月丙子遷。陝西巡撫。署。九月乙亥遷。陝西巡撫。五月癸酉假。署浙江巡撫。十二月己亥調。廣東巡撫。 The twenty-second year of the Daoguang reign (renyin). On the jiwei day of the ninth month he was dismissed as Governor of Jiangsu. On the wuchen day of the fifth month he was on special assignment in an acting capacity; in the twelfth month, on jiwei, he was appointed Governor of Shandong; on jihai he was transferred and replaced. On the bingzi day of the third month he was appointed acting Governor of Shaanxi; on the yihai day of the ninth month he was transferred again as Governor of Shaanxi. On the guiyou day of the fifth month he was granted leave while serving as acting Governor of Zhejiang. On the jihai day of the twelfth month he was transferred to Governor of Guangdong.

道光二十三年癸卯十一月壬午調。安徽巡撫。十二月甲辰調。山東巡撫。五月戊辰遷。浙江巡撫。閏七月甲午調。代。十一月辛巳補。壬午調。任。十二月甲辰卒。代。五月戊辰調。湖南巡撫。閏七月甲午來京。雲南巡撫。 The twenty-third year of the Daoguang reign (guimao). On the renwu day of the eleventh month he was transferred to Governor of Anhui. On the jiachen day of the twelfth month he was transferred to Governor of Shandong. On the wuchen day of the fifth month he was appointed Governor of Zhejiang; in the intercalary seventh month, on jiawu, he was transferred and replaced; on the xinsi day of the eleventh month he filled the vacancy; on renwu he was transferred and took office; on the jiachen day of the twelfth month he died in office and was replaced. On the wuchen day of the fifth month he was transferred to Governor of Hunan. On the jiawu day of the intercalary seventh month he came to the capital as Governor of Yunnan.

道光二十四年甲辰十月癸未覲。護。十月甲午覲。兼署。 The twenty-fourth year of the Daoguang reign (jiachen). On the guiwei day of the tenth month he attended imperial audience while serving as caretaker. On the jiawu day of the tenth month he attended imperial audience while concurrently acting in another capacity.

道光二十五年乙巳正月庚午免。江蘇巡撫。署。八月辛丑調。山西巡撫。正月庚午調。陝西巡撫。二月乙卯調。代。署。二月乙卯免。福建巡撫。署。四月甲辰代。八月辛丑調。補。正月庚午調。廣東巡撫。四月甲辰調。雲南巡撫。四月壬子遷。代。八月辛丑調。補。四月癸卯遷。甲辰貴州巡撫。 The twenty-fifth year of the Daoguang reign (yisi). On the gengwu day of the first month he was dismissed as Governor of Jiangsu while serving in an acting capacity. On the xinchou day of the eighth month he was transferred to Governor of Shanxi. On the gengwu day of the first month he was transferred to Governor of Shaanxi; on the yimao day of the second month he was transferred and replaced while serving in an acting capacity. On the yimao day of the second month he was dismissed as acting Governor of Fujian; on the jiachen day of the fourth month he was replaced; on the xinchou day of the eighth month he was transferred and formally filled the post. On the gengwu day of the first month he was transferred to Governor of Guangdong. On the jiachen day of the fourth month he was transferred to Governor of Yunnan; on the renzi day of the fourth month he was transferred and replaced; on the xinchou day of the eighth month he was transferred and formally filled the post. On the guimao day of the fourth month he was transferred; on jiachen he was appointed Governor of Guizhou.

道光二十六年丙午八月乙亥遷。江蘇巡撫。署。九月戊申署。十二月丁卯免。山西巡撫。署。三月乙酉卒。陝西巡撫。署。十一月己酉假。護。十二月丙子調。福建巡撫。十月庚午覲。護浙江巡撫。十二月癸丑革。廣東巡撫。兼署。十月丙寅免。廣西巡撫。十二月丙子調。代。正月壬午免。雲南巡撫。八月乙亥調。雲南巡撫。九月丁未憂。代。十二月癸丑調。補。 The twenty-sixth year of the Daoguang reign (bingwu). On the yihai day of the eighth month he was appointed Governor of Jiangsu in an acting capacity; in the ninth month, on wushen, he continued to act in the post. On the dingmao day of the twelfth month he was dismissed as acting Governor of Shanxi. On the yiyou day of the third month he died in office as acting Governor of Shaanxi; on the jiyou day of the eleventh month he was granted leave while a caretaker was appointed. On the bingzi day of the twelfth month he was transferred to Governor of Fujian. On the gengwu day of the tenth month he attended imperial audience while serving as caretaker for the Governor of Zhejiang. On the guichou day of the twelfth month he was dismissed as Governor of Guangdong while concurrently acting in another capacity. On the bingyin day of the tenth month he was dismissed as Governor of Guangxi; on the bingzi day of the twelfth month he was transferred and replaced. On the renwu day of the first month he was dismissed as Governor of Yunnan; on the yihai day of the eighth month he was transferred again to Governor of Yunnan; on the dingwei day of the ninth month he took mourning leave and was replaced; on the guichou day of the twelfth month he was transferred and formally filled the post.

道光二十七年丁未十一月壬辰來京。山東巡撫。署。三月乙未調。陝西巡撫。十二月甲戌調。護廣東巡撫。 The twenty-seventh year of the Daoguang reign (dingwei). On the renchen day of the eleventh month he came to the capital as acting Governor of Shandong. On the yiwei day of the third month he was transferred to Governor of Shaanxi. On the jiaxu day of the twelfth month he was transferred and served as caretaker for the Governor of Guangdong.

道光二十八年戊申六月癸卯卒。山東巡撫。八月丁巳革。河南巡撫。署。九月甲戌遷。陝西巡撫。十二月丙寅革。署。代。六月丙辰憂。浙江巡撫。署。庚午代。六月庚午調。江西巡撫。署。九月癸巳覲。署貴州巡撫。 The twenty-eighth year of the Daoguang reign (wushen). On the guimao day of the sixth month he died in office as Governor of Shandong. On the dingsi day of the eighth month he was dismissed as acting Governor of Henan. On the jiaxu day of the ninth month he was appointed Governor of Shaanxi; on the bingyin day of the twelfth month he was dismissed while acting in the post and replaced. On the bingchen day of the sixth month he took mourning leave as acting Governor of Zhejiang; on gengwu he was replaced. On the gengwu day of the sixth month he was transferred to acting Governor of Jiangxi. On the guisi day of the ninth month he attended imperial audience while serving as acting Governor of Guizhou.

道光二十九年己酉四月壬寅遷。江蘇巡撫。署。九月己酉遷。山東巡撫。署。五月己未革逮。山西巡撫。署。八月丙戌遷。代。十一月甲辰調湖北。任。四月壬寅調。江西巡撫。閏四月癸酉調。湖北巡撫。十一月甲辰憂。代。閏四月癸酉憂。湖南巡撫。七月戊戌遷。代。七月己未遷。雲南巡撫。兼署。 The twenty-ninth year of the Daoguang reign (jiyou). On the renyin day of the fourth month he was appointed acting Governor of Jiangsu. On the jiyou day of the ninth month he was appointed acting Governor of Shandong. On the jiwei day of the fifth month he was dismissed and arrested as acting Governor of Shanxi; on the bingxu day of the eighth month he was transferred and replaced; on the jiachen day of the eleventh month he was transferred to Hubei and took office. On the renyin day of the fourth month he was transferred to Governor of Jiangxi. On the guiyou day of the intercalary fourth month he was transferred to Governor of Hubei; on the jiachen day of the eleventh month he took mourning leave and was replaced. On the guiyou day of the intercalary fourth month he took mourning leave as Governor of Hunan; on the wuxu day of the seventh month he was transferred and replaced. 道光三十年庚戌十一月丙午遷。浙江巡撫。八月壬午開缺。江西巡撫。暫署。十二月辛未革。代。二月辛巳來京。署。三月丙寅湖南巡撫。十月壬午革。暫署。十一月庚子卒。署廣西巡撫。暫署。八月癸亥卒。雲南巡撫。 The thirtieth year of the Daoguang reign (gengxu). On the bingwu day of the eleventh month he was appointed Governor of Zhejiang. On the renwu day of the eighth month the post of Governor of Jiangxi was declared vacant and he temporarily acted in it; on the xinwei day of the twelfth month he was dismissed and replaced. On the xinsi day of the second month he came to the capital in an acting capacity; on the bingyin day of the third month he was appointed Governor of Hunan. On the renwu day of the tenth month he was dismissed and temporarily acted in the post; on the gengzi day of the eleventh month he died in office while serving as acting Governor of Guangxi, with another officer temporarily acting in turn. On the guihai day of the eighth month he died in office as Governor of Yunnan.
