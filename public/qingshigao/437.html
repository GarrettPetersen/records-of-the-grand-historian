1
榮祿，字仲華，瓜爾佳氏，滿洲正白旗人。 祖喀什噶爾幫辦大臣塔斯哈，父總兵長壽，均見忠義傳。
Rong Lu, whose courtesy name was Zhonghua, belonged to the Guwalgiya clan and came from the Manchu Plain White Banner. His grandfather Tasgha, who served as assistant commissioner at Kashgar, and his father Changshou, a regional commander, both appear in the biographies of the loyal and righteous.
2
榮祿以廕生賞主事，隸工部，晉員外郎。 出為直隸候補道。 同治初，設神機營，賞五品京堂，充翼長，兼專操大臣。 再遷左翼總兵。 用大學士文祥薦，改工部侍郎，調戶部，兼總管內務府大臣。 穆宗崩，德宗嗣統。 榮祿言於恭親王，乃請頒詔，俟嗣皇帝有子，承繼穆宗。 其後始定以紹統者為嗣。 ，兼步軍統領。 遷左都御史，擢工部尚書。 慈禧皇太后嘗欲自選宮監，榮祿奏非祖制，忤旨。 會學士寶廷奏言滿大臣兼差多，乃解尚書及內務府差。 又以被劾納賄，降二級，旋開復，出為西安將軍。 二十年，祝嘏留京，再授步軍統領。 日本構釁，恭親王、慶親王督辦軍務，榮祿參其事。 和議成，疏薦溫處道袁世凱練新軍，是曰「新建陸軍」。 授兵部尚書、協辦大學士。 疏請益練新軍，而調甘肅提督董福祥軍入衛京師。
Through hereditary privilege Rong Lu received appointment as a principal clerk in the Board of Works and rose to vice director. He was then posted as an expectant intendant in Zhili. Early in the Tongzhi reign, when the Shenji firearms camp was established, he was granted fifth-rank Beijing service, appointed wing commander, and made concurrent specialist drill commissioner. He was promoted again to commander of the Left Wing. On Grand Secretary Wen Xiang's recommendation he was moved to vice minister of Works, then to Revenue, and concurrently appointed superintendent of the Imperial Household. After Emperor Muzong's death, Emperor Dezong ascended the throne. Rong Lu urged Prince Gong to seek an edict providing that when the heir emperor should have a son, that son would succeed Emperor Muzong. Only later was it settled that the adopted successor would stand as heir. , and he was also appointed commander of the Metropolitan Infantry Brigade. He was made Left Censor-in-Chief and then promoted to Minister of Works. When Empress Dowager Cixi once sought to select palace eunuchs herself, Rong Lu protested that this violated ancestral practice and drew her displeasure. When Academician Bao Ting reported that Manchu grand ministers held too many concurrent posts, Rong Lu was stripped of his posts as Minister of Works and superintendent of the Imperial Household. After further impeachment for bribery he was demoted two ranks, soon restored, and appointed military governor of Xi'an. In the twentieth year of Guangxu he stayed in the capital for imperial birthday celebrations and was again made commander of the Metropolitan Infantry Brigade. When Japan opened hostilities, Prince Gong and Prince Qing directed military affairs with Rong Lu among their staff. After peace he recommended Yuan Shikai, then intendant at Wenzhou, to train a new army—the force later known as the Newly Established Army. He was appointed Minister of War and associate grand secretary. He urged further expansion of the new army and the transfer of Gansu regional commander Dong Fuxiang's force to guard the capital.
3
二十四年，晉大學士，命為直隸總督。 是時上擢用主事康有為及知府譚嗣同等參預新政，議變法，斥舊臣，召直隸按察使袁世凱入覲，超授侍郎，統練兵。 榮祿不自安，御史楊崇伊奏請太后再垂簾，於是太后复臨朝訓政，召榮祿為軍機大臣，以世凱代之，命查拿康有為，斬譚嗣同等六人於市，以上有疾，詔徵醫； 復命榮祿管兵部，仍節制北洋海陸各軍。 榮祿乃奏設武衛軍，以聶士成駐蘆臺為前軍，董福祥駐薊州為後軍，宋慶駐山海關為左軍，世凱駐小站為右軍，而自募萬人為中軍，駐南苑。 時太后議廢帝，立端王載漪子溥俊為穆宗嗣，患外人為梗，用榮祿言，改稱「大阿哥」。
In the twenty-fourth year he became grand secretary and governor-general of Zhili. The emperor then elevated Clerk Kang Youwei, Prefect Tan Sitong, and others into the new-policy councils, debated sweeping reform, sidelined veteran ministers, summoned Zhili judicial commissioner Yuan Shikai for audience, and abruptly made him vice minister in charge of military training. Uneasy, Rong Lu watched as Censor Yang Chongyi asked the empress dowager to resume regency; she did, recalled Rong Lu to the Grand Council, put Yuan Shikai in his Zhili post, ordered Kang Youwei arrested, and had Tan Sitong and five others executed in the marketplace while an edict, citing the emperor's illness, called for physicians. Rong Lu was again placed over the Board of War while retaining command of all Beiyang land and sea forces. He then organized the Wuwei Army—Nie Shicheng at Lutai as the Front Army, Dong Fuxiang at Jizhou as the Rear, Song Qing at Shanhaiguan as the Left, Yuan Shikai at Xiaozhan as the Right, and ten thousand men he raised himself as the Central Army at the Southern Hunting Park. When the empress dowager planned to depose Guangxu and install Prince Duan's son Pujun as heir to Muzong, fearing foreign interference she followed Rong Lu's counsel and styled the boy merely "Great Heir Apparent."
4
二十六年，拳匪亂作，載漪等稱其術，太后信之，欲倚以排外人。 福祥率甘軍攻使館，月餘不下； 榮祿不能阻，載漪等益橫，京師大亂，駢戮忠諫大臣。 榮祿踉蹌入言，太后厲色斥之。 聯軍入京，兩宮西幸，駐蹕太原。 榮祿請赴行在，不許，命為留京辦事大臣。 已而詔詣西安，既至，寵禮有加，賞黃馬褂，賜雙眼花翎、紫韁。 隨扈還京，加太子太保，轉文華殿大學士。 二十九年卒，贈太傅，諡文忠，晉一等男爵。
In the twenty-sixth year the Boxer uprising began; Zaiyi and others extolled Boxer magic, the empress dowager trusted them, and hoped to use them against foreigners. Dong Fuxiang led the Gansu troops against the legation quarter yet failed to reduce it for more than a month. Rong Lu could not restrain them; Zaiyi and his faction grew bolder, the capital descended into chaos, and ministers who spoke loyal truth were cut down one after another. When Rong Lu staggered in to plead, the empress dowager glared and dismissed him. Allied forces entered Beijing; the two courts fled west and lodged at Taiyuan. Rong Lu asked to join the flight but was refused and left in Beijing as resident minister. He was soon summoned to Xi'an, where extraordinary honors followed: the yellow jacket, double-eyed peacock feather, and purple reins. Escorting the court home, he received Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent and became Grand Secretary of the Wenhua Hall. He died in the twenty-ninth year, was posthumously honored as Grand Tutor with the temple name Wenzhong, and advanced to first-class baron.
5
榮祿久直內廷，得太后信仗。 眷顧之隆，一時無比。 事無鉅細，常待一言決焉。
Rong Lu had long served inside the palace and held the empress dowager's confidence. No official of his time received such favor. Great or small, affairs often waited on his word alone.
6
王文韶，字夔石，浙江仁和人。 咸豐二年進士，銓戶部主事。 累遷郎中，出為湖北安襄鄖荊道。 左宗棠、李鴻章皆薦其才。 擢按察使，遷湖南布政使。 同治十年，署巡撫。 黔苗亂熾，桂東淪寇域。 文韶條上援黔、防境機宜，以兵事屬按察使席寶田，督其部將蘇元春、龔繼昌等進剿，斬首逆張秀眉烏鴉坡，黔境平。 文韶繪苗疆要塞圖，上之朝。 十一年，除真。 寧遠莠民倡亂，耒陽硃鴻英复妄稱明裔構眾，先後檄道員陳寶箴討平之。 光緒元年，遣總兵謝晉鈞平新化、衡、永土寇。 撫湘六年，內治稱靜謐焉。 入權兵部侍郎，直軍機。 會歲旱，各省籥災，中旨罪己。 文韶亦自陳無狀，詔革職留任。 旋除禮部侍郎，兼總理衙門行走。 八年，御史洪良品、鄧承修劾雲南軍需案，文韶坐失察，奪二級。 乞養歸，終母喪，還前除。
Wang Wenshao, courtesy name Kuishi, came from Renhe in Zhejiang. Having passed the jinshi examination in the second year of Xianfeng, he was appointed principal clerk in the Board of Revenue. He rose through director to intendant over Anxiang, Xiangyang, and Jingzhou in Hubei. Both Zuo Zongtang and Li Hongzhang commended his ability. He became judicial commissioner and then financial commissioner of Hunan. In the tenth year of Tongzhi he acted as governor. Miao uprisings in Guizhou burned hot while eastern Guangxi slipped under rebel control. Wenshao laid out plans to aid Guizhou and secure the frontier, entrusted operations to Judicial Commissioner Xi Baotian, and through generals Su Yuanchun and Gong Jichang beheaded the rebel Zhang Xiumei at Wuyapo, pacifying Guizhou. He mapped the Miao frontier fortresses and sent the chart to court. In the eleventh year he received full appointment as governor. Bad elements at Ningyuan rose in revolt and Zhu Hongying at Leiyang pretended Ming descent to gather mobs; Wenshao repeatedly ordered Intendant Chen Baozhen to crush them. In Guangxu's first year he sent Regional Commander Xie Jinjun to quell brigands in Xinhua, Hengzhou, and Yongzhou. During six years governing Hunan his civil administration won praise for calm order. He entered the capital as acting vice minister of War and joined the Grand Council. As drought spread and provinces reported famine, the throne issued a self-reproach edict. Wenshao confessed his shortcomings and was ordered dismissed yet kept at his duties. He was soon made vice minister of Rites and given concurrent service in the Zongli Yamen. In the eighth year Censors Hong Liangpin and Deng Chengxiu attacked the Yunnan military-supplies scandal; Wenshao lost two ranks for inadequate oversight. He sought leave to care for his mother, completed mourning, and returned to his former rank.
7
十五年，授雲貴總督。 武定會匪陷富民、祿勸，人心恟懼。 文韶斬獲叛將，三日而定。 無何，鎮邊夷亂起，檄迤南道劉春霖分道進攻，拓地三百里。 徙建廳城於猛朗，募勇屯墾。 改臨安猛丁歸流，移府經歷駐其地。 其餘寇亂及土族叛服不常，皆隨時殄滅。
In the fifteenth year he became governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou. When secret-society rebels at Wuding took Fumin and Luquan, fear swept the region. Wenshao executed the rebel chiefs and restored order within three days. Border tribes in Zhenbian soon rebelled; he sent Commissioner Liu Chunlin on divided columns, extending control three hundred li. He relocated the prefectural seat to Menglang and recruited militia for garrison farming. He brought Mengding in Lin'an under regular administration and stationed a prefectural aide on site. Other raids and shifting tribal loyalties he crushed whenever they appeared.
8
初，英、法並緬、越後，西南緣邊防務益棘。 文韶綏靖各路土司，令自為守。 會日韓啟釁，詔入都詢方略。 既至，奉幫辦北洋之命。 鴻章赴日議和，文韶權直隸總督、北洋大臣。 和議成，實授。 時關內外主客軍四百餘營，酌留湘、淮、豫三十營，餘悉散遣，士卒帖然。 建議籌修旅大砲台，謂：「旅順舊台密於防前，疏於防後，敵自大連灣入，遂失所芘； 大連舊台，專顧防海，未及防陸，敵自金州登岸，遂不能支。 今重整海防，必彌其罅隙。 」又請加意水師、武備各學堂，以儲將才，嫺武幹，俟財力稍足，徐圖擴充。 又陳河運漕糧積弊，請蘇漕統歸海運，他若勘吉林三姓金礦、磁州煤礦，踵鴻章後次第成之，而京漢鐵路亦興築於是時矣。 又奏設北洋大學堂、鐵路學堂、育才館、俄文館，造就甚眾。
After Britain and France absorbed Burma and Annam, defense along the southwestern frontier grew acute. Wenshao soothed the native chieftains and left them to defend their own domains. When war broke out between Japan and Korea, he was summoned to the capital for strategy. On reaching Beijing he was ordered to assist Beiyang affairs. While Li Hongzhang negotiated in Japan, Wenshao acted as Zhili governor-general and Beiyang minister. After peace he received full appointment. More than four hundred guest and home camps then garrisoned north China; he kept thirty Hunan, Huai, and Henan battalions, disbanded the rest, and the troops accepted the change without unrest. He urged rebuilding the Lüda fortifications, arguing that old Lüshun batteries were thick toward the sea but thin to the rear, so an enemy entering Dalian Bay left the port undefended. Dalian's old works guarded the sea alone; when the enemy landed at Jinzhou they collapsed. Rebuilding coastal defense, he said, required closing every gap. He also pressed for stronger naval and military academies to train officers and staff, to be enlarged as funds permitted. He exposed Grand Canal transport abuses and urged shifting Suzhou grain to sea carriage; gold mines in Jilin's Three Surnames and Cizhou coal, following Li Hongzhang's initiatives, came on line in turn, and the Beijing-Hankou railway was launched in these years. He founded Beiyang University, a railway school, a talent hall, and a Russian language school, training a large cohort of students.
9
二十四年，入贊軍機，以戶部尚書協辦大學士。 二十六年，拳匪仇教，文韶力言外釁不可啟，不見納。 宮車既出，三日，始追及懷來。 自聯軍犯京，事急，兩宮召軍機，惟文韶一人入見，諭必侍行。 至是立召對，泣慰之，遂隨扈，自晉入秦，晉體仁閣大學士。 明年，改外務部會辦大臣，旋賞黃馬褂。 署全權大臣，命先還京，佐辦中俄條約。 交還東三省及關外鐵路，事寧，賞雙眼花翎。 充政務處大臣，督辦路礦總局。 轉文淵閣，晉武英殿。 三十一年，免直軍機。 明年，稱疾乞休。
In the twenty-fourth year he joined the Grand Council as Minister of Revenue and associate grand secretary. In the twenty-sixth year, as Boxers turned on Christians, Wenshao warned repeatedly against provoking foreign powers and was ignored. The court had already fled; three days passed before he overtook it at Huailai. When allies threatened Beijing and the court summoned the Grand Council in crisis, only Wenshao was admitted and told he must travel with them. Called at once to audience, the sovereigns wept and comforted him; he escorted them from Shanxi into Shaanxi and was made Grand Secretary of the Tiren Hall. The following year he became co-minister of foreign affairs and soon received the yellow jacket. Made plenipotentiary, he was sent back to Beijing ahead of the court to help negotiate the Sino-Russian treaty. When the three eastern provinces and railways beyond the pass were restored and matters settled, he received the double-eyed peacock feather. He sat on the Political Affairs Council and directed the Railway and Mining Bureau. He moved from the Wenyuan Pavilion to Grand Secretary of the Wuying Hall. In the thirty-first year he left the Grand Council. The next year he cited illness and asked to retire.
10
文韶歷官中外，詳練吏職，究識大體，然更事久，明於趨避，亦往往被口語。 三十四年，鄉舉重逢，賜太子太保。 其冬，卒，年七十九，晉贈太保，諡文勤。
Wenshao served widely, mastered routine administration and broad policy, yet decades in office taught him caution and gossip often called him too prudent. In the thirty-fourth year he celebrated the jinshi cycle of his year and received Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. He died that winter at seventy-nine, posthumously honored as Grand Guardian with the temple name Wenqin.
11
張之洞，字香濤，直隸南皮人。 少有大略，務博覽為詞章，記誦絕人。 年十六，舉鄉試第一。 同治二年，成進士，廷對策不循常式，用一甲三名授編修。 六年，充浙江鄉試副考官，旋督湖北學政。 十二年，典試四川，就授學政。 所取士多俊才，遊其門者，皆私自喜得為學塗徑。 光緒初，擢司業，再遷洗馬。 之洞以文儒致清要，遇事敢為大言。 俄人議歸伊犁，與使俄大臣崇厚訂新約十八條。 之洞論奏其失，請斬崇厚，毀俄約。 疏上，乃褫崇厚職治罪，以侍郎曾紀澤為使俄大臣，議改約。 六年，授侍講，再遷庶子。 复論紀澤定約執成見，但論界務，不爭商務，並附陳設防、練兵之策。 疏凡七八上。 往者詞臣率雍容養望，自之洞喜言事，同時寶廷、陳寶琛、張佩綸輩崛起，糾彈時政，號為清流。 七年，由侍講學士擢閣學。 俄授山西巡撫。 當大祲後，首劾布政使葆亨、冀寧道王定安等黷貨，舉廉明吏五人，條上治晉要務，未及行，移督兩廣。
Zhang Zhidong, courtesy name Xiangtao, came from Nanpi in Zhili. As a youth he thought on a grand scale, devoured books and polished prose, and possessed a memory few could match. At sixteen he ranked first in the provincial examinations. In Tongzhi's second year he passed the jinshi; his palace examination essay broke convention and he was named compiler as third of the first class. In the sixth year he assisted the Zhejiang provincial exams, then became Hubei educational commissioner. In the twelfth year he chief-examined Sichuan and was appointed educational commissioner there. Most scholars he chose were brilliant men, and those who entered his circle privately rejoiced to have found a true path in learning. Early in Guangxu he rose to vice director of education and then groom of the heir apparent. A literary scholar who rose to eminent posts, Zhidong spoke boldly whenever affairs demanded. When Russia negotiated the return of Yili, Minister to Russia Chonghou signed a new eighteen-article treaty. Zhidong attacked the treaty in memorials, demanded Chonghou's execution, and urged abrogation of the Russian agreement. The throne stripped and punished Chonghou and sent Vice Minister Zeng Jize to Russia to renegotiate. In the sixth year he became court expositor and soon rose to sublector of the heir apparent. He again faulted Zeng Jize for clinging to preconceptions—haggling over borders while yielding commercial rights—and appended proposals for frontier defense and military training. He submitted seven or eight such memorials in all. Literary officials had usually cultivated reputation in comfort; Zhidong's appetite for policy debate helped Bao Ting, Chen Baochen, Zhang Peilun, and others rise as the "Pure Stream," impeaching the times. In the seventh year he rose from Hanlin expositor to Hanlin academician. He was soon appointed governor of Shanxi. After severe famine he impeached corrupt officials including Commissioner Baoheng and Intendant Wang Ding'an, named five upright men, and outlined essentials of Shanxi governance—then was transferred to the two Guang before his program took effect.
12
八年，法越事起，建議當速遣師赴援，示以戰意，乃可居間調解。 因薦唐炯、徐延旭、張曜材任將帥。 十年春，入覲。 四月，兩廣總督張樹聲解任專治軍，遂以之洞代。 當是時，雲貴總督岑毓英、廣西巡撫潘鼎新皆出督師，尚書彭玉麟治兵廣東。 越將劉永福者，故中國人，素驍勇，與法抗。 法攻越未能下，復分兵攻台灣，其後遂據基隆。 朝議和戰久不決，之洞至，言戰事氣自倍，以玉麟夙著威望，虛己聽從之。 奏請主事唐景崧募健卒出關，與永福相犄角。 朝旨因就加永福提督、景崧五品卿銜，炯、延旭亦皆已至巡撫，當前敵，被劾得罪去，並坐舉者。 之洞獨以籌餉械勞，免議。 廣西軍既敗於越，朝旨免鼎新，以提督蘇元春統其軍，而之洞復奏遣提督馮子材、總兵王孝祺等，皆宿將，於是滇、越兩軍合扼鎮南關，殊死戰，遂克諒山。 會法提督孤拔攻閩、浙，砲毀其坐船，孤拔殪，而我軍不知，法原停戰，廷議許焉。 授李鴻章全權大臣，定約，以北圻為界。 敘克諒山功，賞花翎。
When the Franco-Vietnamese crisis erupted in the eighth year, he urged rapid dispatch of relief forces to show readiness to fight, thereby strengthening China's hand in mediation. He recommended Tang Jiong, Xu Yanxu, and Zhang Yao for field command. In the spring of the tenth year he was called to audience. In the fourth month Zhang Shusheng stepped down to focus on the war and Zhidong succeeded him as governor-general. Governor-General Cen Yuying of Yunnan-Guizhou and Guangxi Governor Pan Dingxin commanded in the field while Minister Peng Yulin directed Guangdong forces. Vietnamese commander Liu Yongfu, originally Chinese and famed for valor, fought the French. Unable to subdue Vietnam, France sent another column against Taiwan and eventually held Keelung. With court opinion deadlocked, Zhidong's arrival doubled fighting spirit; deferring to Peng Yulin's prestige, he followed the elder general's lead. He asked that Clerk Tang Jingsong recruit tough fighters to cross the border and coordinate with Yongfu in a pincer. The court made Yongfu regional commander and gave Jingsong fifth-rank rank; Tang Jiong and Xu Yanxu, already on the front as governors, were impeached, cashiered, and their patrons punished alongside them. Only Zhidong, credited with supplying funds and arms, escaped punishment. When Guangxi troops fell in Vietnam, Pan Dingxin was removed and Su Yuanchun took over; Zhidong sent veterans Feng Zicai and Wang Xiaoqi until Yunnan and Guangxi forces jointly held Zhennanguan, fought to the death, and captured Lang Son. French Admiral Courbet struck Fujian and Zhejiang; Chinese guns sank his flagship and killed him, but our forces did not know it; France had already agreed to a truce, which the court accepted. Li Hongzhang negotiated the treaty setting the border at Tonkin. For the Lang Son victory he received the peacock feather.
13
之洞恥言和，則陰自圖強，設廣東水陸師學堂，創槍砲廠，開礦務局。 疏請大治水師，歲提專款購兵艦。 復立廣雅書院。 武備文事並舉。 十二年，兼署巡撫。 於兩粵邊防控制之宜，輒多更置。 著沿海險要圖說上之。 在粵六年，調補兩湖。
Ashamed of mere peace talk, Zhidong quietly pursued self-strengthening—naval and army schools, an arsenal, and a mining bureau in Guangdong. He urged a major naval buildup funded by annual appropriations for warships. He also established the Guangya Academy. He pursued arms and letters together. In the twelfth year he acted concurrently as governor. He frequently reorganized the two Guang frontier commands. He drew up an illustrated coastal defense manual and sent it to court. After six years in Guangdong he was moved to Huguang.
14
會海軍衙門奏請修京通鐵路，臺諫爭陳鐵路之害，請停辦。 翁同龢等請試修邊地，便用兵； 徐會灃請改修德州濟寧路，利漕運。 之洞議曰：「修路之利，以通土貨、厚民生為最大，徵兵、轉餉次之。 今宜自京外盧溝橋起，經河南以達湖北漢口鎮。 此幹路樞紐，中國大利所萃也。 河北路成，則三晉之轍接於井陘，關隴之驂交於洛口； 自河以南，則東引淮、吳，南通湘、蜀，萬里聲息，刻期可通。 其便利有數端：內處腹地，無慮引敵，利一； 原野廣漠，墳廬易避，利二； 廠盛站多，役夫賈客可舍舊圖新，利三； 以一路控八九省之衢，人貨輻輳，足裕餉源，利四； 近畿有事，淮、楚精兵崇朝可集，利五； 太原旺煤鐵，運行便則開採必多，利六； 海上用兵，漕運無梗，利七。 有此七利，分段分年成之。 北路責之直隸總督，南路責之湖廣總督，副以河南巡撫。 」得旨報可，遂有移楚之命。 大冶產鐵，江西萍鄉產煤，之洞乃奏開鍊鐵廠漢陽大別山下，資路用，兼設槍砲鋼藥專廠。 又以荊襄宜桑棉麻枲而饒皮革，設織布、紡紗、繅絲、制麻革諸局，佐之以堤工，通之以幣政。 由是湖北財賦稱饒，土木工作亦日興矣。
When the Naval Yamen proposed the Beijing-Tongzhou railway, censors protested the dangers of railways and demanded a halt. Weng Tonghe and others urged trial lines on the frontier for military use; Xu Huifeng favored a Dezhou-Jining line to aid Grand Canal transport. Zhidong argued: "Railways chiefly move native goods and enrich livelihoods; troop movement and grain transport rank second. The trunk line should run from Lugou Bridge outside Beijing through Henan to Hankou in Hubei. That trunk would become the hub where China's greatest economic gains converged. Once the Hebei segment opened, Shanxi traffic would reach Jingxing and Shaanxi convoys would meet at Luokou; south of the Yellow River it would link Huai and Wu to the east and Hunan and Sichuan to the south, carrying news across ten thousand li on schedule. He listed seven benefits: an interior route would not invite invasion; open country would spare graves; numerous stations would let laborers and merchants adapt; one line would command eight or nine provinces and swell revenue; trouble near the capital could summon Huai and Chu veterans within a morning; cheap transport would expand Taiyuan's coal and iron; war at sea would not choke grain supply. With these seven gains, he urged sectional construction year by year. Zhili would build the northern segment, Huguang the southern, with Henan assisting. The throne approved, and he was transferred to Huguang. Daye iron and Jiangxi Pingxiang coal led him to open an ironworks at Hanyang under Daye Hill for the railway, plus specialized gun, cannon, and powder plants. Jingxiang's silk, cotton, hemp, and leather supported weaving, spinning, silk-reeling, and leather factories, backed by dikes and currency reform. Hubei revenues grew rich and public works multiplied.
15
二十一年，中東事棘，代劉坤一督兩江，至則巡閱江防，購新出後膛砲，改築西式砲台，設專將專兵領之。 募德人教練，名曰「江南自強軍」。 採東西規制，廣立武備、農工商、鐵路、方言、軍醫諸學堂。 尋還任湖北。 時國威新挫，朝士日議變法，廢時文，改試策論。 之洞言：「廢時文，非廢五經、四書也，故文體必正，命題之意必嚴。 否則國家重教之旨不顯，必致不讀經文，背道忘本，非細故也。 今宜首場試史論及本朝政法，二場試時務，三場以經義終焉。 各隨場去留而層遞取之，庶少流弊。 」又言：「武科宜罷騎射、刀石，專試火器。 欲挽重文輕武之習，必使兵皆識字，勵行伍以科舉。 」二十四年，政變作，之洞先著勸學篇以見意，得免議。
In the twenty-first year, as the Sino-Japanese crisis sharpened, he replaced Liu Kunyi in the two Jiang, inspected river defenses, bought breech-loading guns, rebuilt Western batteries, and assigned dedicated commanders. German instructors trained the "Jiangnan Self-Strengthening Army." Following Eastern and Western models, he founded military, agricultural, industrial, commercial, railway, language, and army medical schools. He soon returned to Hubei. National prestige freshly wounded, court scholars debated reform—abolishing eight-legged essays for policy examinations. Zhidong warned: abolishing essay form did not mean abandoning the classics—style and themes must stay rigorous. Otherwise students would neglect the classics, abandon the Way, and forget their roots. He proposed first testing history and Qing institutions, second current affairs, third classical exposition. Elimination after each session would curb abuses. He also urged ending mounted archery and stone-lifting in military exams in favor of firearms. To reverse contempt for soldiers, troops must learn literacy and examination honors should reach the ranks. When the 1898 coup came, his earlier Exhortation to Learning showed his stance and spared him blame.
16
二十六年，京師拳亂，時坤一督兩江，鴻章督兩廣，袁世凱撫山東，要請之洞，同與外國領事定保護東南之約。 及聯軍內犯，兩宮西幸，而東南幸無事。 明年，和議成，兩宮回鑾。 論功，加太子少保。 以兵事粗定，乃與坤一合上變法三疏。 其論中國積弱不振之故，宜變通者十二事，宜採西法者十一事。 於是停捐納，去書吏，考差役，卹刑獄，籌八旗生計，裁屯衛，汰綠營，定礦律、商律、路律、交涉律，行銀圓，取印花稅，擴郵政。 其尤要者，則設學堂，停科舉，獎遊學。 皆次第行焉。
During the 1900 Boxer crisis, Kunyi, Hongzhang, and Yuan Shikai summoned Zhidong to pledge with foreign consuls to protect the southeast. When allies invaded and the court fled west, the southeast stayed calm. Peace followed the next year and the court returned. He received Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent for his service. With fighting subsiding, he and Kunyi submitted three reform memorials. They listed twelve causes of weakness and eleven Western reforms to adopt. Purchase of office ended, clerks and runners reformed, prisons eased, Banner livelihoods funded, garrisons cut, Green Standards thinned, new commercial and mining laws enacted, silver dollars and stamp tax introduced, and postal service expanded. Schools, abolition of the old examinations, and rewards for overseas study mattered most. These reforms rolled out step by step.
17
二十八年，充督辦商務大臣，再署兩江總督。 有道員私獻商人金二十萬為壽，請開礦海州，立劾罷之。 考鹽法利弊，設兵輪緝私，歲有贏課。 明年，入覲，充經濟特科閱卷大臣，釐定大學堂章程，畢，仍命還任。 陛辭奏對，請化除滿、漢畛域，以彰聖德，遏亂萌，上為動​​容。 旋裁巡撫，以之洞兼之。 三十二年，晉協辦大學士。 未幾，內召，擢體仁閣大學士，授軍機大臣，兼筦學部。 三十四年，督辦粵漢鐵路。
In the twenty-eighth year he supervised commerce and again acted in the two Jiang. When a circuit intendant offered a merchant two hundred thousand taels for birthday favors to open Haizhou mines, Zhidong impeached and removed him. Reviewing salt administration, he deployed armed patrols against smuggling and produced annual surpluses. The next year he entered audience, examined the special economic candidates, finalized university statutes, and returned to his post. At farewell audience he urged breaking Manchu-Han barriers to display imperial virtue and curb unrest; the throne was moved. When the Jiangsu governorship was abolished, Zhidong held it concurrently. In the thirty-second year he became associate grand secretary. Soon summoned to Beijing, he became Tiren Hall grand secretary, grand councillor, and head of the Ministry of Education. In the thirty-fourth year he supervised the Guang-Han Railway.
18
德宗暨慈禧皇太后相繼崩，醇親王載灃監國攝政。 之洞以顧命重臣晉太子太保。 逾年，親貴浸用事，通私謁。 議立海軍，之洞言海軍費絀可緩立，爭之不得。 移疾，遂卒，年七十三，朝野震悼。 贈太保，諡文襄。
Guangxu and Cixi died in succession; Prince Chun Zai Feng became regent. As a deathbed trusted minister, Zhidong received Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. Within a year imperial princes meddled in policy and accepted private petitions. Debating a new navy, Zhidong argued funds were insufficient; he lost the debate. He pleaded illness, died at seventy-three, and court and country mourned. He was posthumously honored as Grand Guardian with the temple name Wenxiang.
19
之洞短身巨髯，風儀峻整。 蒞官所至，必有興作。 務宏大，不問費多寡。 愛才好客，名流文士爭趨之。 任疆寄數十年，及卒，家不增一畝雲。
Short and bearded, Zhidong carried himself with stern dignity. Every post he held saw new construction. He sought grandeur without counting cost. He loved talent and entertained guests; leading scholars gathered at his door. After decades governing provinces, his family owned not one added mu of land.
20
瞿鴻禨，字子玖，湖南善化人。 同治十年進士，授編修。 光緒元年，大考一等，擢侍講學士。 久乃遷詹事，晉內閣學士。 先後典福建、廣西鄉試，督河南、浙江、四川學政。 所行皆本功令，律下尤嚴。
Qu Hongji, courtesy name Zijiu, came from Shanhua in Hunan. A Tongzhi tenth-year jinshi, he became a Hanlin compiler. In Guangxu's first year he topped the palace examination and became Hanlin expositor. Long afterward he became sublector and then Hanlin academician of the Grand Secretariat. He successively ran the Fujian and Guangxi provincial exams and served as educational commissioner in Henan, Zhejiang, and Sichuan. His administration strictly followed official regulations, with especially stern discipline.
21
朝鮮戰事起，我師出平壤。 鴻禨上四路進兵之策，請兼募沿海漁人蜑戶編為舟師，使敵備多力分，庶可制勝。 及和議成，鴻禨方自蜀還，復奏言秦中地形險要，請豫建陪都。 日本增兵遼東，鴻禨以敵情叵測，請敕劉坤一、王文韶簡練勁旅，不可專任淮軍。 適坤一奏劾山西將賀星明侵餉，革職，鴻禨言：「刑賞治天下之大柄，軍紀廢弛已久，宜嚴懲以儆其餘。 」又：「葉志超、龔照嶼等敗軍辱國，罪當死。 和約既定，勢不能與勾，宜籍其財產，或令巨款捐贖，然後貸其一死。 」皆不報。 旋遷禮部侍郎，出督江蘇學政。 請罷武科。
When war broke out in Korea, Chinese forces advanced on Pyongyang. Hongji proposed a four-route advance and recruiting coastal fishermen and boat people as naval auxiliaries to divide enemy strength. After peace, returning from Sichuan, he urged preparing a secondary capital in the strategic Qin region. When Japan reinforced Liaodong, Hongji—deeming enemy intentions uncertain—ordered Liu Kunyi and Wang Wenshao to train elite troops rather than rely solely on the Huai Army. When Kunyi impeached Shanxi commander He Xingming for embezzling pay, Hongji argued: "Reward and punishment govern the realm; discipline has collapsed and severe punishment must warn others. He added: "Ye Zhichao and Gong Zhaoyu disgraced the nation and deserve death. With peace concluded, execution is impractical; confiscate their property or accept huge ransoms, then spare their lives. " The throne did not respond. He soon became vice minister of Rites and supervised education in Jiangsu. He petitioned to abolish military examinations.
22
兩宮西狩，鴻禨差竣詣行在，道授左都御使，晉工部尚書，仍以西安陪都為言。 既至，命直軍機，兼充政務處大臣。 請以策論試士，開經濟特科，汰書吏，悉允行。 改總理各國事務衙門為外務部，班六部上，以鴻禨為尚書。 時方與各國議和，鴻禨治事明敏，諳究外交，承旨擬諭，語中竅要，頗當上意焉。 扈蹕回鑾，賞黃馬褂，加太子太保。
When the court fled west, Hongji joined the traveling court, was made Left Censor-in-Chief and Minister of Works en route, and again urged making Xi'an a secondary capital. On arrival he joined the Grand Council and the Political Affairs Council. He proposed policy-discourse examinations, a special economic exam, and cutting clerks—all approved. The Zongli Yamen became the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, ranked above the Six Ministries, with Hongji as its first minister. During negotiations with foreign powers, Hongji handled affairs with sharp clarity, mastered diplomacy, drafted edicts that hit essentials, and pleased the throne. Escorting the court home, he received the yellow jacket and Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent.
23
自新政議起，興學、通商、勸工諸政，有司多藉端巧取。 鴻禨請降旨禁革苛派，任民間自辦。 又請旨以戶部正雜諸款供地方正用，宮中歲費，遵先朝定例，量入為出，不便自戶部增撥。 裁汰內務府冗員，用節糜費。 充中日議約全權大臣。 是時中外咸以立憲為請，朝廷下詔豫備憲政始基，勗天下以忠君尊孔、尚公尚武尚實，用鴻禨言也。 三十二年，協辦大學士。 特旨派議改官制大臣，鴻禨以樞廷事冗辭。 旋命與大學士孫家鼐复核，頗有裁正焉。
As new policies spread, officials often used schools, commerce, and industry programs to levy extortionate fees. Hongji sought an edict banning harsh levies and letting people manage reforms themselves. He urged funding local expenses from regular Board of Revenue accounts and limiting palace spending to ancestral quotas without extra allocations. He cut redundant Imperial Household staff to curb waste. He served as plenipotentiary for the Sino-Japanese treaty negotiations. When China debated constitutionalism, the court issued an edict laying constitutional foundations, urging loyalty to the sovereign, reverence for Confucius, and public, martial, and practical virtues—following Hongji's counsel. In the thirty-second year he became associate grand secretary. A special edict named him to the governmental reform commission, but he declined citing Grand Council overload. He was then ordered to review the plan with Grand Secretary Sun Jianai and corrected many provisions.
24
鴻禨持躬清刻，以儒臣驟登政地，銳於任事。 素善岑春煊，春煊入朝，留長郵傳部。 密疏劾慶親王奕劻，奕劻惡春煊，遂及鴻禨。 會鴻禨因直言忤太后旨，侍講學士惲毓鼎劾以攬權恣縱，遂罷斥歸里。 辛亥，湘變起，流寓上海，旋卒。 後追諡文慎。
Austere and upright, Hongji rose suddenly from scholarship to power and threw himself into affairs. He favored Cen Chunxuan, who on entering court headed the Ministry of Posts and Communications. He secretly impeached Prince Qing Yikuang; Yikuang hated Cen Chunxuan and turned on Hongji as well. Frank speech offended the empress dowager; Expositor Yun Yiding impeached him for grasping power, and he was dismissed to his home. When the 1911 revolution erupted in Hunan, he took refuge in Shanghai and soon died. He was posthumously honored with the temple name Wenshen.
25
論曰：德宗親政，憤於外侮，思變法自強。 乃以輔導無人，戊戌黨禍，庚子匪亂，遂相繼而作。 太后再出垂簾，初堅復舊，繼勉圖新。 宣統改元，議行憲政。 政體既變，國本遂搖，而大勢不可問矣。 榮祿屢參大變，文韶久達世務。 鴻禨後起，參議立憲，終以失寵太后，不免放斥。 唯之洞一時稱賢，而監國攝政，親貴用事，欲挽救而未能，遂以憂死。 人之雲亡，邦國殄瘁，尚何言哉？
The commentary says: When Guangxu took personal rule, humiliated by foreign aggression, he sought reform and self-strengthening. Lacking trustworthy guidance, the 1898 purge and the 1900 Boxer catastrophe followed in turn. Cixi resumed regency—first bent on restoration, then pressing toward reform. Under the Xuantong reign, constitutional government was debated. Once institutions changed, the dynastic foundation shook and the empire's fate was beyond remedy. Rong Lu shaped repeated crises; Wenshao long mastered practical governance. Hongji rose later to debate constitutionalism yet lost Cixi's favor and was cast out. Only Zhidong was called the age's great minister, yet under regency princes ruled; he tried to save the state, failed, and died in grief. When such men are gone, the state withers—what more is there to say?
