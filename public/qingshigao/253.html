1
=莫洛=莫洛，伊爾根覺羅氏，滿洲正紅旗人，世居呼納赫魯。 祖溫察，太祖時來歸。 莫洛初授刑部理事官，累遷工部郎中。 康熙六年，擢左副都御史。 七年，出為山西陝西總督。 陝西飢，平涼、臨洮、鞏昌、西安、延安、鳳翔、漢中、興安諸府州多逋賦，有司令現戶均輸，民苦之，奏請蠲免。 迭疏清釐加派、火耗諸弊。 八年，輔政大臣鼇拜獲譴，法司以莫洛附鼇拜，請逮問，詔以能任事，貸勿治，仍留任。 九年，計典，仍以前罪奪職。 陝西民籥留，甘肅巡撫劉鬥同，提督張勇、柏永馥等疏言莫洛清正，在官有善政，乞留以慰民望。 上諭曰：「簡用督撫，原以綏輯地方，愛養百姓。 莫洛既能得民，其免處分，供職如故。 」俄擢刑部尚書。
Mo Luo, of the Irgen Gioro clan, was a Manchu bannerman of the Plain Red Banner whose family had long lived at Hunakehulu. His ancestor Wenchai came over to the Qing side in the reign of Taizu. Mo Luo began as a clerk in the Ministry of Punishments and rose through the ranks to become a director in the Ministry of Works. In 1667 he was promoted to Left Vice Censor-in-Chief. The following year he was appointed Governor-General of Shanxi and Shaanxi. When famine struck Shaanxi, many prefectures and departments—including Pingliang, Lintao, Gongchang, Xi'an, Yan'an, Fengxiang, Hanzhong, and Xing'an—were heavily in arrears on land tax. Some local officials had ordered current households to share the burden evenly, to the people's great distress. Mo Luo memorialized for tax remission. He sent repeated memorials to root out abuses such as extra surcharges and unauthorized meltage-and-wear fees. In 1669, after the disgraced regent Oboi fell, the judicial authorities charged Mo Luo with having sided with him and asked that he be arrested and tried. The emperor ruled that his proven ability warranted pardon; he was not prosecuted and kept his post. At the 1670 triennial review, however, he was again stripped of office on the earlier charge. The people of Shaanxi petitioned to keep him in office. Gansu Governor Liu Dou, together with Provincial Military Commander Zhang Yong, Bai Yongfu, and others, memorialized that Mo Luo was upright, had governed well, and should be retained to satisfy popular expectation. The emperor said, "Governors and governors-general are appointed to bring order to the provinces and care for the people. Since Mo Luo has won the people's trust, let his punishment be waived and let him continue in office as before. " Soon afterward he was promoted to Minister of Punishments.
2
十三年，吳三桂等奏請撤籓，上敕廷臣議，皆主勿徙，惟莫洛與米思翰、明珠議撤。 三桂反，四川提督鄭蛟麟等叛應之。 二月，命莫洛經略陝西，拜武英殿大學士，仍管兵部，賜以敕印，既至，策遣諸軍徵四川。 時蛟麟兵據廣元百丈關，莫洛遣都統馬一寶、將軍席卜臣赴漢中，副都統科爾寬赴廣元，擊賊。 十月，蛟麟將何德成犯寧羌，為官軍所敗，還奔四川，莫洛因遣提督王輔臣駐其地。 踰月，蛟麟將彭時亨复據七盤、百丈諸關，劫略陽糧艘，截陸運棧道。
In 1674, Wu Sangui and the other feudatory princes petitioned to surrender their domains. The emperor ordered the court to debate the matter. Most ministers opposed relocation, but Mo Luo, Misihan, and Mingzhu argued for abolishing the feudatories. When Sangui rose in rebellion, Sichuan Provincial Military Commander Zheng Jiaolin and others joined him. In the second month he was appointed to coordinate military affairs in Shaanxi, made a Grand Secretary of the Hall of Military Glory while retaining charge of the Ministry of War, and granted an imperial commission seal. On arrival he drew up plans and dispatched the armies against Sichuan. Jiaolin's forces then held Baizhang Pass at Guangyuan. Mo Luo sent Commandant Ma Yibao and General Xibuchen to Hanzhong and Vice Commandant Ke'erkuang to Guangyuan to attack the rebels. In the tenth month Jiaolin's general He Decheng attacked Ningqiang, was beaten by government forces, and fled back into Sichuan. Mo Luo then sent Provincial Military Commander Wang Fuchen to hold the area. A month later Jiaolin's general Peng Shiheng reoccupied Qipan, Baizhang, and other passes, raided grain convoys at Lueyang, and severed the overland supply route.
3
廣元軍缺餉兩月矣，總兵王懷忠所部潰散，而輔臣亦陰懷異志。 輔臣故與莫洛有郄，奉檄使隨徵，益怏怏，藉口戎備寡，莫洛益以騎兵二千，少之； 又以馬疲瘠不任用搖軍心，軍無鬥志。 十二月，莫洛至寧羌，兩營相去二里許。 先是，上命莫洛統綠營步旅下四川，嗣慮巴蜀道險，令貝勒洞鄂率滿洲騎兵兼程繼進。 軍未至，是月庚寅朔，癸巳，輔臣煽所部噪餉，襲劫莫洛。 莫洛督兵擊卻之。 甫定，輔臣复率悍黨至，砲矢雨坌，莫洛被創，卒於軍。 卹典久未行，二十二年，命予祭葬，諡忠愍，授世職拜他喇布勒哈番兼拖沙喇哈番。 子常安，襲。
The Guangyuan army had gone without pay for two months. Regional Commander Wang Huaizhong's troops had broken and scattered, and Fuchen was already secretly disloyal. Fuchen and Mo Luo had long been at odds. Ordered to join the campaign, he grew more resentful still. Claiming that his forces were too weak, he received two thousand additional cavalry from Mo Luo—yet still deemed them insufficient. He further complained that the horses were worn out and unfit for service, undermining morale until the army lost all fighting spirit. In the twelfth month Mo Luo reached Ningqiang, his camp and Fuchen's standing about two li apart. Earlier the emperor had ordered Mo Luo to lead Green Standard infantry into Sichuan. Fearing the treacherous Ba-Shu routes, he then ordered Prince Dong'e to follow with Manchu cavalry by forced marches. Before reinforcements arrived, on the guisi day of that month Fuchen incited his men to riot for back pay and attacked Mo Luo. Mo Luo led his troops and repulsed the assault. Hardly had order been restored when Fuchen returned at the head of his hard fighters. Cannon and musket fire poured down like rain. Mo Luo was wounded and died in camp. Funeral honors were long delayed. In 1683 he was granted state sacrifices and burial, given the posthumous title Zhongmin (Loyal and Commiserated), and his family received the hereditary ranks of baturalaburuhan and tosahalaburuhan. His son Chang'an inherited the rank.
4
=陳福=陳福，字箕演，陝西榆林人。 國初師定陝西，福以武舉應募，從寧夏總兵劉芳名剿寇。 敘功，授守備。 又從都統李國翰下四川，遷遵義游擊。 康熙初，從總督李國英討李自成遺黨郝搖旗、李來亨等。 敘功，加右都督銜，擢成都副將，遷重慶總兵。 十二年，入覲，授寧夏總兵。
Chen Fu, styled Jiyan, was a native of Yulin in Shaanxi. When the dynasty's armies pacified Shaanxi, Fu enrolled as a military licentiate and served under Ningxia Regional Commander Liu Fangming in suppressing bandits. For his service he was appointed garrison commander. He later followed Commandant Li Guohan into Sichuan and was transferred to roving-attacker at Zunyi. Early in the Kangxi reign he served under Governor-General Li Guoying against Li Zicheng's remnant followers Hao Yaoqi, Li Laiheng, and others. For his merit he received the brevet rank of Right Commissioner-in-Chief, was promoted to deputy regional commander at Chengdu, and later transferred to regional commander at Chongqing. In 1673 he presented himself at court and was appointed Regional Commander at Ningxia.
5
吳三桂反，鄭蛟麟以四川叛應之，遣使誘福。 福家留重慶，弟奇官守備，妻子亦在賊中，賊以是劫福。 福執其使，具疏入告，遣其弟諸生壽齎詣京師。 上嘉福忠，授拜他喇布勒哈番，並官壽主事。 輔臣據平涼，福上戰守方略。 十四年，擢陝西提督，進三等阿思哈尼哈番，又官奇參將。 進規花馬池，惠安、安定、定邊諸城堡，以次皆下。 上擢壽鴻臚寺少卿。 福率副將泰必圖乘勝薄固原，圍之匝月。 輔臣遣其將來援，城賊亦突出，泰必圖戰死。 福引兵還靈州，斬逃將賈從哲、張元經以徇。
When Wu Sangui rebelled, Zheng Jiaolin rose in Sichuan in support and sent envoys to win Fu over. Fu's family had remained in Chongqing. His younger brother Qi was a garrison commander, and his wife and children were in rebel hands. The rebels used them to pressure him. Fu arrested the envoy, memorialized the court in full, and sent his younger brother Shou, a licentiate, to deliver the report to Beijing. The emperor commended Fu's loyalty, granted him the rank of baturalaburuhan, and appointed Shou as a director in the ministries. When Fuchen held Pingliang, Fu submitted plans for offense and defense. In 1675 he was promoted to Provincial Military Commander of Shaanxi, advanced to third-rank ashanihanihan, and his brother Qi was made deputy regional commander. He advanced against Huamachi and the fortresses of Hui'an, Anding, Dingbian, and others, which fell one after another. The emperor promoted Shou to Vice Director of the Court of Imperial Entertainments. Fu led Deputy Regional Commander Taibitu in a victorious push against Guyuan and besieged the city for a full month. Fuchen sent reinforcements, and the garrison sallied out as well. Taibitu was killed in action. Fu withdrew to Lingzhou and executed the fleeing generals Jia Congzhe and Zhang Yuanjing as an example.
6
上命福佐貝勒洞鄂攻平涼。 福疏言固原有賊萬餘，若我兵徑趨平涼，慮賊斷我餉道，當先取固原，上韙之。 十二月，福督兵取固原。 天寒大雪，士卒苦遠役，且懲前敗，有戒心。 是月甲寅朔，乙亥，師次惠安，下令：「五鼓會食，集城下，後者斬。 」夜半時，參將熊虎等鼓譟入，刺福死。 上以趙良棟代，收虎及首謀把總劉德及營兵戕福者悉誅之，贈福三等公，以三等精奇尼哈番世襲，諡忠愍。 建祠寧夏。 擢奇天津總兵。 時福子世琳、世勳並陷賊，命以壽子世怡襲爵。
The emperor ordered Fu to assist Prince Dong'e in the attack on Pingliang. Fu memorialized that Guyuan held more than ten thousand rebels and that marching straight on Pingliang risked having supply lines cut. Guyuan must be taken first. The emperor agreed. In the twelfth month Fu led the assault on Guyuan. Bitter cold and heavy snow afflicted the troops on the long march. Chastened by the earlier defeat, they fought with caution. On the yihai day of that month the army halted at Hui'an. Fu ordered, "At the fifth watch take your meal and assemble below the walls. Latecomers will be executed. " At midnight Deputy Regional Commander Xiong Hu and others raised a mutiny, broke in, and stabbed Fu to death. The emperor replaced him with Zhao Liangdong, arrested Xiong Hu, the ringleader Platoon Commander Liu De, and all the mutineers who had killed Fu, and had them executed. Fu was posthumously made a third-rank duke with a hereditary third-rank jingqinihanihan and given the posthumous title Zhongmin. A memorial shrine was built for him at Ningxia. Qi was promoted to Regional Commander at Tianjin. Fu's sons Shilin and Shixun were then trapped in rebel territory, so the court ordered that Shou's son Shiyi should inherit the rank.
7
事定，壽棄官入四川求福妻子，得之遵義山中，將入都，上召世琳入見，問母子流離狀，深愍之，命襲爵，改籍寧夏。 旋授直隸三屯協副將。 累遷古北鎮總兵、鑾儀使。 世琳子益，益子大用，相繼襲爵。 益官至楚姚鎮總兵。 大用乾隆間官江南提督，所屬游擊楊天相，獲海盜，總督蘇凌阿讞以為誣，誅天相，大用亦被譴。 嘉慶初，予守備銜，休致。
After the rebellion ended, Shou resigned his post and went into Sichuan to find Fu's wife and children. He discovered them in the mountains near Zunyi. As they were about to enter the capital, the emperor summoned Shilin and asked about the family's ordeal. Deeply moved, he ordered Shilin to inherit the rank and registered the family at Ningxia. He was soon appointed Deputy Regional Commander of the Santun Brigade in Zhili. He rose through the ranks to Regional Commander at Gubei Pass and Director of Imperial Equipage. Shilin's son Yi and Yi's son Dayong inherited the rank in turn. Yi rose to Regional Commander at Chuyao. During the Qianlong reign Dayong served as Provincial Military Commander of Jiangnan. His subordinate Roving-Attacker Yang Tianxiang captured pirates, but Governor-General Su Ling'a ruled the case a fabrication, had Yang executed, and reprimanded Dayong as well. Early in the Jiaqing reign he received the brevet rank of garrison commander and retired.
8
=王之鼎=王之鼎，字公定，漢軍正紅旗人。 父世選，仕明為參將。 歸太宗，授三等昂邦章京。 從世祖入關，徵江南有功，進二等。 卒，之鼎襲，進一等精奇尼哈番，署參領。 從貝勒屯齊徵湖南，擊走李定國、孫可望。 授正紅旗漢軍副都統。 駐防貴州。 康熙元年，授福建中路總兵，討鄭錦，克廈門、金門、古浪諸島。 三年，敗錦將黃盛、林茂、裴德等，拔銅山衛，進三等伯。 八年，召還，仍授本旗副都統。 十年，授江南提督。
Wang Zhiding, styled Gongding, was a Chinese Bannerman of the Plain Red Banner. His father Shixuan had served the Ming as a deputy regional commander. He submitted to Taizong and was granted the third-rank title angbang janggin. He followed the Shizu Emperor through the passes into China and distinguished himself in the Jiangnan campaigns, advancing to second rank. On his death Zhiding inherited the rank, was advanced to first-rank jingqinihanihan, and served as acting company commander. He followed Prince Tunqi in the Hunan campaign and drove off Li Dingguo and Sun Kewang. He was appointed Vice Commander-in-Chief of the Chinese Bannermen of the Plain Red Banner. He was posted on garrison duty in Guizhou. In 1662 he was appointed Regional Commander of Fujian's Middle Route, campaigned against Zheng Jing, and captured Xiamen, Jinmen, Gulang, and other islands. In 1664 he defeated Jing's generals Huang Sheng, Lin Mao, and Pei De, captured Tongshan Guard, and was advanced to third-rank earl. In 1669 he was recalled and reappointed Vice Commander-in-Chief of his banner. In 1671 he was appointed Provincial Military Commander of Jiangnan.
9
十二年，授鎮海將軍，駐守京口。 吳三桂、耿精忠相繼反。 上命之鼎分兵防安慶，而以安南將軍華善帥師佐之。 之鼎調崇明沙船，江陰、瓜洲戰艦，扼津要，令綠旗水師駐黃浦操防，兼備水陸。 上命簡親王喇佈為揚威大將軍，駐江寧，之鼎贊軍事。 十七年，改福建水師提督，加定海將軍。 閩寇日蹙，而楚、蜀間軍事方亟，請移鎮要地自效。
In 1673 he was appointed Pacifier of the Sea General and stationed at Jingkou. Wu Sangui and Geng Jingzhong rose in rebellion one after another. The emperor ordered Zhiding to detach troops to defend Anqing while General Huashan of Pacifying the South led a force to assist him. Zhiding deployed Chongming sand boats and warships from Jiangyin and Guazhou to hold key crossings, stationed Green Standard naval forces at Huangpu for drill and defense, and prepared for both land and river warfare. The emperor appointed Prince Jian Labu Grand General of Awe-Inspiring Might, stationed at Jiangning, with Zhiding assisting in military affairs. In 1678 he was transferred to Provincial Military Commander of the Fujian Naval Forces with the additional title Pacifier of Dinghai. Rebels in Fujian were daily being cornered, but fighting in Huguang and Sichuan remained fierce. He asked to be transferred to a more critical post where he could serve.
10
十八年，調四川提督。 十九年，到官，會寇犯永寧，遂率總兵李芳述等討之，戰屢勝。 六月，勇略將軍趙良棟將進剿雲、貴，調芳述守敘州，之鼎留鎮永寧。 九月，吳世璠遣將尤廷玉、胡國柱攻永寧，圍之數匝。 時城中糧盡已兩閱月，之鼎猶率兵挑戰，士氣倍奮。 嗣為賊诇知，築長圍以守。 至是月甲子，寇穴城入，總兵費雅達，副將楊三虎，游擊週尚功，守備李逢春、魯明芝、席豹督兵巷戰，皆死。 之鼎解印付家人，令間道走成都，率總兵楊魁、何成德、王永世、傅汝友，游擊陳先鳳、陳田、劉應科等出禦賊，身受重創。 賊湧至，之鼎自度不能免，拔劍自刎，未殊，與魁等俱被執，傳送貴陽。 賊黨夏國相等百計誘降，之鼎厲聲叱曰：「死則死耳，肯向鼠輩乞活耶？ 」久之，賊知不可奪，遂遇害。 魁等皆不屈死。 事聞，贈之鼎太子少保，諡忠毅。 子毓賢，官至貴州布政使，毓秀襲爵。
In 1679 he was transferred to Provincial Military Commander of Sichuan. In 1680 he assumed office. When rebels attacked Yongning, he led Regional Commander Li Fangshu and others against them and won repeated victories. In the sixth month Valiant Strategy General Zhao Liangdong prepared to advance into Yunnan and Guizhou. Fangshu was transferred to hold Xuzhou while Zhiding remained to garrison Yongning. In the ninth month Wu Shifan sent Generals You Tingyu and Hu Guozhu to attack Yongning and surrounded the city in tight rings. Grain in the city had been exhausted for two months, yet Zhiding still led sorties against the enemy and morale remained high. When the rebels learned of the city's weakness through scouts, they built long siege lines and settled in to starve it out. On the jiazi day of that month the rebels tunneled into the city. Regional Commander Fei Yada, Deputy Regional Commander Yang Sanhu, Roving-Attacker Zhou Shanggong, and Garrison Commanders Li Fengchun, Lu Mingzhi, and Xi Bao led street fighting and all perished. Zhiding handed his seal to his household and ordered them to flee by a secret route to Chengdu. He led Regional Commanders Yang Kui, He Chengde, Wang Yongshi, and Fu Ruyou and Roving-Attackers Chen Xianfeng, Chen Tian, and Liu Yingke out to meet the enemy and was gravely wounded. As the rebels closed in, Zhiding judged that escape was impossible, drew his sword, and cut his throat. Before he died he was seized together with Yang Kui and the others and sent under escort to Guiyang. Rebel leaders such as Xia Guoxiang tried every means to win him over. Zhiding shouted, "Death is death—would I beg my life from such vermin? " In time the rebels saw he could not be swayed and put him to death. Yang Kui and the others likewise refused to submit and were killed. When word reached the court, Zhiding was posthumously made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent and given the posthumous title Zhongyi (Loyal and Resolute). His son Yuxian rose to Guizhou Provincial Administration Commissioner; Yuxiu inherited the rank.
11
=費雅達=費雅達，漢軍正白旗人。 自整儀尉累遷潼關副將。 王輔臣叛，廷議設漢中總兵討賊，以授費雅達，署都督僉事。 進取漢中，破賊彝門鎮，抵秦嶺，拔北木城，與王進寶會師奪武關。 敘功，加都督同知。 永寧之役，城陷身死，贈左都督、太子少傅，諡忠勇。 魁等皆予卹。
Fei Yada was a Chinese Bannerman of the Plain White Banner. He rose from Director of Ceremonial Guards to Deputy Regional Commander at Tong Pass. When Wang Fuchen rebelled, the court decided to create a Regional Commander post at Hanzhong to suppress the rebels and appointed Fei Yada, with acting rank of Regional Vice Commissioner. He advanced into Hanzhong, routed the rebels at Yimen Town, reached the Qin Mountains, captured Beimu Fortress, and joined Wang Jinbao to retake Wu Pass. For his merit he was promoted to Regional Vice Commander. At Yongning, when the city fell he died in battle. He was posthumously made Left Regional Commander and Junior Tutor of the Heir Apparent, with the posthumous title Zhongyong. Yang Kui and the others likewise received funeral honors.
12
從三虎等戰死者，又有千總蔣得福、趙鳴鳳、王英傑； 從之鼎死者，又有從軍廕生潘濟世：並卹如例。
Among those who fell with Yang Sanhu were Battalion Commanders Jiang Defu, Zhao Mingfeng, and Wang Yingjie. Among those who died with Zhiding was the military student Pan Jishi. All received funeral honors according to precedent.
13
=李興元=李興元，字若始，漢軍鑲黃旗人。 以拔貢授直隸沙河知縣，報最，遷祁州。 歷江西吉安、直隸永平知府，晉陝西隴右道。 康熙十一年，授雲南按察使。 其明年，詔敕有司審理平西籓下逃兵。 時平西勳莊棋布，管莊者殺人奪貨，滋為民患。 訟牒命、盜兩案，兵居半。 又勒平民為餘丁； 不從，則曰：「是我逃兵也。 」稱貸重息，人或絲毫負，亦以「逃兵」誣之，有司亡誰何。 興元素持風力，諗知劉昆強項，令為審事官。 有犯者論如法，部民德之，而大忤三桂意。
Li Xingyuan, styled Ruoshi, was a Chinese Bannerman of the Bordered Yellow Banner. As a selected tribute graduate he was appointed magistrate of Shahe in Zhili. Rated highest at evaluation, he was transferred to Qizhou. He served as prefect of Ji'an in Jiangxi and Yongping in Zhili, then was promoted to Longyou Circuit Intendant in Shaanxi. In 1672 he was appointed Provincial Surveillance Commissioner of Yunnan. The following year an edict ordered local officials to try deserters from Wu Sangui's Pacification of the West domain. Wu Sangui's merit estates were scattered across the province like chess pieces. Their managers killed and plundered, becoming a growing scourge on the people. On the dingyou day of that month he put on court robes, took the official seat, cursed the rebels, and cut his throat with his belt dagger.

疏略曰：「臣一介豎儒，叨沐皇上高厚深恩，歷擢今職。 嘗以潔己奉公，自矢夙夜，但媿才具庸劣，未效寸長。 茲值裁兵夏逢龍倡亂，劫奪撫臣敕印，分兵圍臣衙門，露刃逼脅。 臣幼讀詩書，粗知節義，雖斧鑕在前，豈肯喪恥偷生？ 臣母年七十有六，在臣任所； 臣長子旉，遠在原籍； 其餘二子尚未成童，煢煢孤嫠，死將安歸？ 因遣妻女奉母潛逃。 臣如微服匿影，或可倖免以圖後效。 伏念臣守土之官也，城存與存，城亡與亡，義所當然。 今勉盡一死，以報國恩。 所恨事起倉猝，既不能先事綢繆，默消反側； 復不能臨期捍禦，獨守孤城。 上辜三十載之皇恩，下棄七旬餘之老母，君親兩負，死有餘慚。 」上覽疏，深愍傷之，召廷臣展讀，聞者皆感泣。 下部議卹，部議援陳丹赤例，贈通政使，特旨贈工部侍郎。 次年上南巡，旉迎謁，手書「忠節」二字賜之，遂以為諡。 立祠武昌，書「丹心炳冊」扁以賜。
The memorial read in part: "Your servant is but a humble scholar who has bathed in the emperor's profound grace and risen to my present post. I have striven to keep myself pure in public service and to labor day and night, yet I am ashamed that my mediocre talents have rendered no real service. Now, as troops are being discharged, Xia Fenglong has risen in mutiny, seized the governor's seal, surrounded my yamen with armed men, and threatens me at blade's point. From youth I have read the classics and know something of integrity. Though execution awaits, how could I disgrace myself to cling to life? My mother is seventy-six and lives at my post; my eldest son Fu is far away in our native place; my other two sons are still children. Bereft widows and orphans—if I die, where shall they turn? I have therefore sent my wife and daughters to escort my mother to safety in secret. If I were to slip away in disguise, I might perhaps survive to serve again later. Yet I reflect that as an official charged with guarding this territory, I must live or die with the city. That is righteousness itself. I shall now do my utmost to die once, and thereby repay the state's grace. I regret only that the crisis came so suddenly: I could neither foresee it and quietly forestall the mutiny, nor at the last moment defend the city alone. I have failed the thirty years of imperial grace above and abandoned my mother of more than seventy below. I have failed both sovereign and parent. Even in death my shame is more than I can bear. " When the emperor read the memorial, he was deeply moved. He summoned the court to hear it read aloud, and all who listened wept. The ministries deliberated on funeral honors and proposed citing Chen Danchi's precedent for posthumous Commissioner of Transmission. A special edict raised the grant to Vice Minister of Works. The following year, during an imperial southern tour, Fu came forward to audience. The emperor wrote Loyal Integrity with his own brush and bestowed it as Yingliu's posthumous title. A shrine was built at Wuchang, and the emperor bestowed the plaque Loyal Heart Shines in the Records.
27
雍正八年，錄忠臣後，授其子旉鳳陽知府； 芳蔚州知州，尋改員外郎； 孫鳳毛內閣中書。 與映榴同時死者，都司宣德仁，贈副將。
In 1730, when the court enrolled descendants of loyal ministers, his son Fu was appointed Prefect of Fengyang; Fang was made Magistrate of Weizhou and later transferred to director in the ministries; and grandson Fengmao was made a Secretariat secretary. Xuan Deren, a brigade vice commander who died at the same time, was posthumously made Deputy Regional Commander.
28
=【論】=論曰：功令褒死事，倉卒遇變與艱難效死者同，所以獎忠義也。 莫洛與福，先事宜知有變，師行有進無退，雖死不撓。 之鼎效忠於孤城，興元抗節於大憝。 若啟泰、丹赤、映榴，皆能死其官者。 啟泰以其家殉，與馬雄鎮比烈； 映榴遺疏款款，則又范承謨蒙谷自序之亞也。
Commentary: The merit regulations honor those who die in service. Whether death comes in sudden crisis or through prolonged hardship, the reward is the same—so that loyalty and righteousness may be encouraged. Mo Luo and Chen Fu should have foreseen trouble, yet on campaign they advanced without retreat and did not yield even in death. Wang Zhiding proved loyal in a besieged city; Li Xingyuan held to his integrity against a mighty rebel. Chen Qitai, Chen Danchi, and Ye Yingliu all died at their posts. Qitai sacrificed his entire household, a fervor comparable to that of Ma Xiongzhen; and Yingliu's earnest final memorial ranks next only to Fan Chengmo's Menggu self-preface.
