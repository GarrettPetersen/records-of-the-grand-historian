4
求均數時差
Finding the Mean-Anomaly Equation of Time
5
以本日太陽引數宮度分，滿三十秒進一分用。 用後編日躔均數時差表，察其所對之數，得均數時差，記加減號。 引數有零分者，用中比例求之。
Take the day's solar anomaly in mansion degrees and minutes, carrying one minute for every full thirty seconds. Look up the day's value in the later compilation's solar mean-equation-of-time table, record the mean equation of time, and note whether to add or subtract. When the anomaly has leftover minutes, obtain the value by middle interpolation.
6
求升度時差
Finding the Right-Ascension Equation of Time
7
以本日太陽黃道實行宮度分，滿三十秒進一分用。 用後編日躔升度時差表，察其所對之數，得升度時差，記加減號。 實行有零分者，用中比例求之。
Take the day's solar true ecliptic longitude in mansion degrees and minutes, carrying one minute for every full thirty seconds. Look up the day's value in the later compilation's solar right-ascension equation table, record the right-ascension equation, and note whether to add or subtract. When the true longitude has leftover minutes, obtain the value by middle interpolation.
8
求時差總
Finding the Total Equation of Time
9
以均數時差與升度時差相加減，得時差總。 兩時差同為加或同為減者，則相加得時差總，加亦為加，減亦為減。 兩時差一為加一為減者，則相減得時差總，加數大為加，減數大為減。
Combine the mean and right-ascension equations of time by addition or subtraction to obtain the total equation of time. When both corrections share the same sign, add them for the total; the result keeps that sign. When one correction is added and the other subtracted, take their difference for the total; whichever magnitude is larger sets the sign.
10
求凌犯用時
Finding the Operative Time for Transit
11
置凌犯時刻，加減時差總，得凌犯用時。
Take the nominal transit instant, apply the total equation of time, and obtain the operative transit time.
12
求本時太陽黃道經度
Finding the Sun's Ecliptic Longitude at the Given Hour
13
以周日一千四百四十分為一率，本次日兩太陽實行相減帶秒減，足三十秒進一分用，有度化分。 為二率，凌犯時刻化分為三率，求得四率與本日太陽實行相加，得本時太陽黃道經度。
Use 1,440 parts of the sidereal day as the first ratio; subtract the two days' solar true longitudes with seconds included, carry one minute per thirty seconds, and convert degrees to minutes. That is the second ratio; convert the transit time to minutes as the third; the resulting fourth ratio, added to the day's solar true longitude, gives the sun's ecliptic longitude at the hour.
14
求本時春分距午時分
Finding the Equinox-to-Noon Interval at the Given Hour
15
以本時太陽黃道經度，滿三十分進一度用。 察黃平象限表內右邊所列春分距午時分與凌犯用時相加，內減十二時，不足減，加二十四時減之。 得本時春分距午時分。 滿二十四時去之。
Use the sun's ecliptic longitude at the hour, carrying one degree for every full thirty minutes. From the ecliptic-equator quadrant table take the equinox-to-noon value on the right, add the operative transit time, and subtract twelve hours; if that fails, add twenty-four hours first. This yields the equinox-to-noon interval at the given hour. Remove any excess above twenty-four hours.
16
求本時黃白大距
Finding the Moon's Ecliptic Latitude at the Given Hour
17
以周日一千四百四十分為一率，本次日兩黃白大距相減為二率，凌犯時刻化分為三率，求得四率。 加減本日黃白大距，本日黃白大距大相減，小相加。 得本時黃白大距。
Use the sidereal day's 1,440 parts as first ratio, the two days' lunar-latitude difference as second, and the transit time in minutes as third to obtain the fourth ratio. Apply it to the day's lunar latitude: subtract if the day's value is larger, add if it is smaller. This gives the moon's ecliptic latitude at the hour.
18
求本時月距正交
Finding the Moon's Distance from the Node at the Given Hour
19
以周日一千四百四十分為一率，本次日兩月距正交相減化秒為二率，凌犯時刻化分為三率，求得四率。 收作度分秒，與本日月距正交相加，得本時月距正交。
Use the sidereal day's 1,440 parts as first ratio, the nodal-distance difference in seconds as second, and the transit time in minutes as third to obtain the fourth ratio. Convert to degrees, minutes, and seconds, add to the day's nodal distance, and obtain the moon's nodal distance at the hour.
20
求太陰實緯
Finding the Moon's True Latitude
21
以半徑為一率，本時黃白大距正弦為二率，本時月距正交正弦為三率，如本時月距正交過三宮者，與六宮減，過六宮者減六宮； 過九宮者，與十二宮減，用其餘。 求得四率，為太陰實緯正弦，檢表得太陰實緯，記南北號。 本時月距正交初宮至五宮為北，六宮至十一宮為南。 如本時月距正交恰在初宮、六宮者，則無實緯。 恰在三宮、九宮者，則本時黃白大距即實緯度，三宮為北，九宮為南。
Use the radius as first ratio, the sine of lunar latitude as second, and the sine of nodal distance as third; if nodal distance passes the third mansion subtract from six, if it passes the sixth subtract six mansions; if it passes the ninth mansion, subtract from twelve and use the remainder. The fourth ratio is the sine of true latitude; look it up in tables for the true latitude and record north or south. Nodal distance from the 1st through 5th mansions is north; from the 6th through 11th is south. If nodal distance falls exactly on the 1st or 6th mansion, true latitude is zero. At the 3rd or 9th mansion the ecliptic latitude equals true latitude; the 3rd is north and the 9th south.
22
求黃平象限及限距地高
Finding the Ecliptic-Equator Quadrant and Limit Altitude
23
以本時春分距午時分，察黃平象限表內，取其與時分相近者所對之數錄之，得黃平象限。 隨看左邊之限距地高錄之，得限距地高。
Using the equinox-to-noon interval at the hour, find the nearest time entry in the ecliptic-equator quadrant table and record its corresponding quadrant value. Record the limit altitude from the left column as well.
24
求星經度
Finding the Star's Longitude
25
按所取之星，察儀象考成卷二十六表內所載本星之黃道經度，加入歲差，表以乾隆九年甲子為元，至道光十四年甲午，計九十年，應加歲差一度十六分三十秒，以後每年遞加歲差五十一秒。 得本年星經度。
For the chosen star consult Yixiang kaocheng, vol. 26, for its ecliptic longitude and add precession from the table epoch (Qianlong year 9, jiazi): over ninety years to Daoguang year 14, jiawu, add 1°16′30″, then 51″ per year thereafter. This gives the star's longitude for the current year.
26
如求五星經度，則以周日一千四百四十分為一率，凌犯時刻化分為二率，一日星實行為三率，以本次日兩實行相減，得一日星實行。 求得四率，為距時星實行。 與本日星經度相加減，順行加，退行減。 得本時星經度。
For the five planets use the sidereal day's 1,440 parts as first ratio, transit time in minutes as second, and one day's true motion as third—the daily motion being the difference of the two days' true motions. The fourth ratio is the planet's true motion over the interval. Add or subtract from the day's star longitude: add for direct motion, subtract for retrograde. This gives the star's longitude at the hour.
27
求星緯度
Finding the Star's Latitude
28
按所取之星，察儀象考成卷二十六表內所載本星之黃道緯度錄之，無歲差。 記南北號。
For the chosen star record its ecliptic latitude from Yixiang kaocheng, vol. 26; precession does not apply. Record whether it is north or south.
29
如求五星緯度，則以周日一千四百四十分為一率，凌犯時刻化分為二率，一日星緯較為三率，本次日兩緯度同為南或同為北者，則相減得星緯較。 一為南一為北者，則相加得星緯較。 求得四率。 與本日星緯度相加減，本日緯度大相減，本日緯度小相加。 若相加為三率者，所得四率必與本日緯度相減，仍依本日南北號。 如所得四率大於本日星緯，則以所得四率轉減本日星緯，其南北號應與次日同。 得本時星緯度，記南北號。
For the five planets use the sidereal day's 1,440 parts as first ratio, transit time in minutes as second, and one day's latitude change as third; if both days share the same hemisphere, subtract to get the daily latitude change. If one is south and one north, add them for the daily latitude change. Obtain the fourth ratio. Apply it to the day's star latitude: subtract if the day's value is larger, add if smaller. When addition formed the third ratio, subtract the fourth from the day's latitude while keeping the day's north-south sign. If the fourth ratio exceeds the day's latitude, subtract the day's value from it instead; the sign then follows the next day. This gives the star's latitude at the hour; record north or south.
30
求月距限
Finding the Moon's Distance from the Limit
31
以星經度與黃平象限相減，得月距限，記東西號。 星經度大為限東，小為限西。 如星經度與黃平象限一在三宮內，一在九宮外，應將三宮內者加十二宮減之。 所得月距限太陰實緯南在六十度內，實緯北在八十度內者，不必求地平限度。 如緯南過六十度，緯北過八十度，則求地平限度。
Subtract the ecliptic-equator quadrant from the star's longitude to get the moon's distance from the limit, and record east or west. Greater star longitude means limit east; lesser means limit west. If one value lies within the 3rd mansion and the other beyond the 9th, add twelve mansions to the inner one before subtracting. When the moon-distance-from-limit is within 60° for southern true latitude or 80° for northern, the horizon limit need not be calculated. If southern latitude exceeds 60° or northern exceeds 80°, calculate the horizon limit.
32
求距限差
Finding the Limit-Distance Correction
33
以限距地高及太陰實緯度分，察距限差表內縱橫所對之數錄之，得距限差，記加減號。 太陰實緯南減北加。
Cross-read the limit-distance table by limit altitude and true latitude to obtain the correction and note whether to add or subtract. Subtract for southern true latitude, add for northern.
34
求地平限度
Finding the Horizon Limit
35
置九十度，加減距限差，得地平限度。
Start from ninety degrees, apply the limit-distance correction, and obtain the horizon limit.
36
以地平限度內減最小視經差八度五十五分一十七秒，得視地平限度，如月距限大於視地平限度者，為月在地平下，即不必算。 因太陰距地最近，其視行隨時不同，故取最小視經差以定視限。 乃按最小限距地高，月在黃道極南，求得最小黃經高弧交角二十六度六分二十四秒。 以最小太陰地半徑差及最速月實行，求得最小距分三十七分八秒。 變赤道度得九度一十七分，求其相當最小黃道度為八度三十一分三十四秒。 再加最小東西差二十三分四十三秒，得最小視經差八度五十五分一十七秒。 然月在最高時，地半徑差最小，而其月實行必遲，則距分轉大。 今俱取其最小者，恐有遺漏耳。
Subtract the minimum apparent longitude difference of 8°55′17″ from the horizon limit for the apparent horizon limit; if the moon's distance from the limit exceeds it, the moon is below the horizon and the case is dropped. Because the moon is nearest Earth its apparent motion varies, so the minimum apparent longitude difference sets the apparent limit. At minimum limit altitude with the moon at the southern ecliptic pole, the minimum ecliptic-longitude altitude-arc angle is 26°6′24″. Minimum lunar parallax and fastest lunar true motion yield a minimum separation of 37′8″. In equatorial measure this is 9°17′, equivalent to a minimum ecliptic value of 8°31′34″. Adding the minimum east-west difference of 23′43″ gives the minimum apparent longitude difference of 8°55′17″. Yet at perigee parallax is smallest while true motion is slowest, so separation can be larger. Using minima throughout may therefore miss some cases.
37
求距極分邊
Finding the Polar-Side Distance
38
以半徑為一率，月距限餘弦為二率，限距地高正切為三率，求得四率，為距極分邊正切，檢表得距極分邊。
A proportion with radius, the cosine of moon-distance-from-limit, and the tangent of limit altitude yields the polar-side tangent, converted to degrees from tables.
39
求月距黃極
Finding the Moon's Distance from the Ecliptic Pole
40
置九十度，加減太陰實緯，南加北減。 得月距黃極。
Start from ninety degrees and apply true latitude: add for south, subtract for north. This gives the moon's distance from the ecliptic pole.
41
求距月分邊
Finding the Moon-Side Distance
42
以月距黃極內減距極分邊，得距月分邊。
Subtract the polar-side distance from the moon's ecliptic-pole distance to obtain the moon-side distance.
43
求黃經高弧交角
Finding the Ecliptic-Longitude Altitude-Arc Angle
44
以距月分邊正弦為一率，距極分邊正弦為二率，月距限正切為三率，求得四率，為黃經高弧交角正切，檢表得黃經高弧交角。 若月距限為初度，是太陰正當黃平象限，則黃經與高弧合，無黃經高弧交角。
A proportion with the sines of moon-side and polar-side distances and the tangent of moon-distance-from-limit yields the ecliptic-longitude altitude-arc tangent, converted to degrees from tables. If moon-distance-from-limit is zero the moon lies on the ecliptic-equator quadrant, longitude and altitude arc coincide, and no angle is needed.
45
求本次日月實引
Finding the Moon's True Anomaly for This and the Next Day
46
以本日月引數加減本日初均，得本日月實引，以次日月引數加減次日初均，得次日月實引。
Apply the day's first equation to its anomaly argument for the day's true anomaly, and likewise for the next day.
47
求本時月實引
Finding the Moon's True Anomaly at the Given Hour
48
以周日一千四百四十分為一率，凌犯時刻化分為二率，本次日兩實引相減帶秒減，足三十秒進一分用，度化分。 為三率，求得四率。 收為度分，與本日月實引相加，得本時月實引。
Use the sidereal day's 1,440 parts as first ratio and transit time in minutes as second; subtract the two days' true anomalies with seconds included, carry one minute per thirty seconds, and convert degrees to minutes. That is the third ratio; obtain the fourth. Convert to degrees and minutes, add to the day's true anomaly, and obtain the moon's true anomaly at the hour.
49
求本時本天心距地
Finding the Moon's Center Distance at the Given Hour
50
以周日一千四百四十分為一率，凌犯時刻化分為二率，本次日兩本天心距地數相減為三率，求得四率。 與本日本天心距地數相加減，本日本天心距地數大相減，小相加。 得本時本天心距地。
Use the sidereal day's 1,440 parts as first ratio, transit time in minutes as second, and the two days' center-distance difference as third to obtain the fourth ratio. Apply it to the day's center distance: subtract if the day's value is larger, add if smaller. This gives the moon's center distance at the hour.
51
求距地較
Finding the Distance Correction
52
以本時本天心距地內減距地小數，得距地較。
Subtract the mean-distance constant from the center distance at the hour to obtain the distance correction.
53
求月距天頂
Finding the Moon's Distance from the Zenith
54
以黃經高弧交角正弦為一率，限距地高正弦為二率，月距限正弦為三率，求得四率為月距天頂正弦，檢表得月距天頂。 若無黃經高弧交角，則以月距黃極內減限距地高即得。
A proportion with the sines of the ecliptic-longitude altitude-arc angle, limit altitude, and moon-distance-from-limit yields the zenith-distance sine, converted to degrees from tables. Without that angle, subtract limit altitude from the moon's ecliptic-pole distance.
55
求太陰地半徑差
Finding the Moon's Parallax
56
以本時月實引滿三十分，進一度用。 及本時本天心距地，察後編交食太陰地半徑差表內所對之數，即太陰地半徑差。 如本時本天心距地有遠近者，以距地較比例求之。
Use the moon's true anomaly at the hour, carrying one degree for every full thirty minutes. Together with the center distance at the hour, look up the later compilation's lunar-parallax table for the value. If the center distance differs from the mean, adjust by proportion using the distance correction.
57
求本時高下差
Finding the Altitude Difference at the Given Hour
58
以半徑為一率，月距天頂正弦為二率，太陰地半徑差為三率，若推凌犯五星，除土、木二星無地半徑差外，火、金、水三星皆有地半徑差。 乃看星引數，自十宮十五度至一宮十五度，為最高限。 自一宮十五度至四宮十五度，自七宮十五度至十宮十五度，為中距限。 自四宮十五度至七宮十五度，為最卑限。 以星引數所當之限，察其本星最大地半徑差，與太陰地半徑差相減，得星月地平高下差，為三率。 求得四率，即本時高下差。
Use the radius, sine of zenith distance, and lunar parallax as the three ratios; for planetary transits, Saturn and Jupiter lack parallax, but Mars, Venus, and Mercury have it. Inspect the planet's anomaly: from the 10th mansion at 15° to the 1st mansion at 15° is the apogee range. From the 1st mansion at 15° to the 4th at 15°, and from the 7th at 15° to the 10th at 15°, is the mean-distance range. From the 4th mansion at 15° to the 7th at 15° is the perigee range. For the planet's anomaly range, take its maximum parallax, subtract lunar parallax for the star-moon horizon altitude difference as the third ratio. The fourth ratio is the altitude difference at the hour.
59
求東西差
Finding the East-West Difference
60
以半徑為一率，黃經高弧交角正弦為二率，本時高下差為三率，求得四率，即東西差。 如無交角，則無東西差，高下差即南北差，凌犯用時即凌犯視時。
A proportion with radius, the sine of the ecliptic-longitude altitude-arc angle, and the altitude difference yields the east-west difference. Without the angle there is no east-west difference; altitude difference equals north-south difference, and operative time equals apparent time.
61
求南北差
Finding the North-South Difference
62
以半徑為一率，黃經高弧交角餘弦為二率，本時高下差為三率，求得四率，即南北差。
A proportion with radius, the cosine of the ecliptic-longitude altitude-arc angle, and the altitude difference yields the north-south difference.
63
求太陰視緯
Finding the Moon's Apparent Latitude
64
以太陰實緯與南北差相加減，得太陰視緯，記南北號。 緯南相加仍為南，緯北相減仍為北，如南北差大，則反減變北為南。
Combine true latitude with the north-south difference for apparent latitude and record north or south. Adding to southern latitude keeps south; subtracting from northern keeps north; a large north-south difference reverses the sign.
65
求太陰距星
Finding the Moon's Separation from the Star
66
以太陰視緯與星緯相加減，得太陰距星，記月在上下號。 如兩緯度同為北或同為南者則相減； 月緯大，北為在上，南為在下； 月緯小，北為在下，南為在上。 兩緯度一為南一為北者則相加。 月緯北為在上，月緯南為在下。 若兩緯度相同，減盡無餘，為月掩星，凡相距在一度以內者用； 過一度外者，為緯大，不用，即不必算。
Combine apparent and star latitudes for the moon-star separation and record whether the moon is above or below the star. If both latitudes share the same hemisphere, subtract; if the moon's latitude is larger, north means above and south below; if the moon's latitude is smaller, north means below and south above. If one is south and one north, add. Northern moon latitude means above; southern means below. If the latitudes match exactly, it is a lunar occultation, used when separation is within one degree; beyond one degree the latitude gap is too large and the case is dropped.
67
求太陰實行
Finding the Moon's True Motion
68
以本時月實引滿三十分，進一度用。 及本時本天心距地，察後編交食太陰實行表內所對之數，得太陰實行。 如本時本天心距地有遠近者，以距地較比例求之。
Use the moon's true anomaly at the hour, carrying one degree for every full thirty minutes. Together with the center distance at the hour, look up the later compilation's lunar true-motion table. If the center distance differs from the mean, adjust by proportion using the distance correction.
69
求距分
Finding the Separation in Arcminutes
70
以太陰實行為一率，東西差為二率，一小時化作三千六百秒為三率，求得四率，即距分，記加減號。 月距限東為減，月距限西為加。
Use lunar true motion, east-west difference, and one hour as 3,600 seconds in a proportion for the separation in arcminutes, and note whether to add or subtract. Subtract when the moon is east of the limit; add when west.
71
求凌犯視時
Finding the Apparent Transit Time
72
置凌犯用時，加減距分，得凌犯視時，如凌犯用時不足減距分，加二十四時減之，所得凌犯視時為在前一日； 如加滿二十四時去之，所得凌犯視時為在次日。 時刻在日出前日入後者用； 在日出後日入前者，即為在晝，不用。
Take the operative transit time and apply the separation; if subtraction fails, add twenty-four hours first—the apparent time then falls on the previous day; if addition exceeds twenty-four hours, remove the excess and the apparent time falls on the next day. Use only times before sunrise or after sunset; times between sunrise and sunset are daytime and are dropped.
73
如月在緯南，月距限過六十度，及月在緯北，月距限過七十度者，須用下法求之。
When the moon is south of the ecliptic with moon-distance-from-limit over 60°, or north with over 70°, use the method below.
74
求視時春分距午時分
Finding the Equinox-to-Noon Interval at Apparent Time
75
置本時春分距午，加減距分，得視時春分距午。 如本時春分距午不足減距分者，加二十四時減之； 若相加過二十四時者去之。
Take equinox-to-noon at the hour, apply the separation, and obtain the value at apparent time. If subtraction fails, add twenty-four hours first; if addition exceeds twenty-four hours, remove the excess.
76
求視時黃平象限
Finding the Ecliptic-Equator Quadrant at Apparent Time
77
以視時春分距午時分，察黃平象限表內，取其與時分相近者，所對之數錄之，即得視時黃平象限。
Using equinox-to-noon at apparent time, find the nearest entry in the ecliptic-equator quadrant table and record its value.
78
求視時月距限
Finding the Moon's Distance from the Limit at Apparent Time
79
置星經度，與視時黃平象限相減，得視時月距限，其度小於地平限度者用； 若大於地平限度者，為月在地平下，不用。
Subtract the apparent-time ecliptic-equator quadrant from the star's longitude for moon-distance-from-limit at apparent time; use when it is less than the horizon limit; if it exceeds the horizon limit, the moon is below the horizon and the case is dropped.
80
黃平象限表
Ecliptic-Equator Quadrant Table
81
黃平象限表，按京師北極高度三十九度五十五分，黃赤大距二十三度二十九分，依黃道經度，逐度推得春分距午時分、黃平象限宮度、限距地高度分，三段列之。 表名「春分距午」者，乃春分距午正赤道度所變之時分也。 「黃平象限」者，乃本時黃平象限之宮度也。 「限距地高」者，乃本時黃平象限距地平之高度也。 表自三宮初度列起者，因太陽黃道經度三宮初度為春分，即春分距午之初也。
The ecliptic-equator quadrant table, for Beijing at polar altitude 39°55′ and obliquity 23°29′, lists by ecliptic longitude the equinox-to-noon interval, quadrant mansion, and limit altitude in three columns. The column "equinox-to-noon" gives the time equivalent of the equatorial degree at equinox-to-noon. "Ecliptic-equator quadrant" is the quadrant's mansion degree at the hour. "Limit altitude" is how high the ecliptic-equator quadrant stands above the horizon at the hour. The table starts at the 3rd mansion's initial degree because that solar longitude marks the equinox, the beginning of equinox-to-noon.
82
用表之法，以本時太陽黃道經度之宮度，察其所對之春分距午時分，加凌犯用時，得數內減十二時，不足減者加二十四時減之，得本時春分距午時分。 依此時分，取其相近之春分距午時分所對之黃平象限宮度及限距地高度分，即得所求之黃平象限及限距地高也。 設本時太陽經度一宮十五度，凌犯用時十九時四十五分，求春分距午及黃平象限★限距地高，則察本表黃道經度一宮十五度所對之春分距午為二十一時九分五十四秒。 加凌犯用時十九時四十五分，內減十二時，餘過二十四時去之。 得四時五十四分五十四秒，為所求之春分距午時分。 乃以此時分察相近者，得四時五十四分五十一秒。 其所對之黃平象限為五宮十六度五十九分二十七秒，即所求之黃平象限宮度。 其所對之限距地高為七十二度四十九分五十八秒，即所求之限距地高也。 若黃道經度有零分者，滿三十分以上則進為一度，不用中比例，因逐度所差甚微故也。
To use the table, find equinox-to-noon for the sun's mansion degree, add operative transit time, subtract twelve hours, adding twenty-four first if needed, for equinox-to-noon at the hour. With that interval, take the nearest table entry's quadrant mansion and limit altitude. Suppose solar longitude is 1st mansion 15° and operative time 19h45m; for equinox-to-noon, quadrant, and limit altitude, the table at 1st mansion 15° gives equinox-to-noon 21h9m54s. Add 19h45m, subtract twelve hours, and remove any excess over twenty-four hours. This yields 4h54m54s as the equinox-to-noon interval sought. The nearest table entry is 4h54m51s. Its quadrant is 5th mansion 16°59′27″. Its limit altitude is 72°49′58″. Carry one degree when fractional minutes reach thirty; middle interpolation is omitted because per-degree differences are negligible.

距限差表
Limit-Distance Correction Table
85
距限差表，按限距地高度逐段列之，前列太陰實緯度分，中列黃道南北，自初度十分至五度十七分之距限差，緯南為減，緯北為加。
The limit-distance table lists by limit altitude with true latitude, ecliptic north/south, and corrections from 0°10′ to 5°17′; subtract for south latitude, add for north.
86
用表之法，以限距地高之度與太陰實緯度，察其縱橫相遇之數，即所求之距限差也。 設限距地高二十八度，太陰距黃道南四度二十分，求距限差，則察限距地高二十八度格內橫對太陰實緯四度二十分之距限差為八度十二分，即所求之距限差。 其緯在黃道南，是為減差也。 限距地高以逐度為率，若限距地高有三十分以上者，進作一度，不及三十分者去之。 太陰實緯以十分為率，若太陰實緯有零分者，五分以上進作十分，不足五分者去之。 俱不用中比例，因逐度分之數所差甚微故也。
Cross-read by limit altitude and true latitude for the correction sought. For limit altitude 28° and latitude 4°20′ south, the table gives correction 8°12′. Southern latitude means subtract the correction. Round limit altitude to whole degrees: carry up at thirty minutes, discard below. Round true latitude to tenths of a degree: carry at five minutes, discard below. Middle interpolation is omitted for both because stepwise differences are negligible.
