Lin Wencha, Zhao Deguang, and Zhang Wende
4
江忠義，字味根，湖南新寧人，忠源從弟。 咸豐二年，忠源率楚勇援長沙，忠義年十八，從軍，轉戰湖北、江西。 忠源殉難廬州，遂分將其軍。 五年，從提督和春复廬州，擢知縣。 七年，劉長佑援江西，攻臨江不利，時忠義在籍，巡撫駱秉章檄率新練勇千人往助之，至則破石達開於平墟。 臨江既克，擢知府，賞花翎。 八年，克崇仁，進攻新城，五戰皆捷，加道銜。 江西肅清，凱歸。
Jiang Zhongyi, whose style was Weigen, came from Xinning in Hunan and was a younger cousin of Jiang Zhongyuan. In 1852, Zhongyuan led Hunan militia to relieve Changsha. Zhongyi, then eighteen, enlisted and campaigned through Hubei and Jiangxi. After Zhongyuan fell at Luzhou, Zhongyi took over part of his force. In 1855 he followed Grand Secretary He Chun in retaking Luzhou and was promoted to magistrate. In 1857, Liu Changyou went to aid Jiangxi, but the assault on Linjiang faltered. Zhongyi was then at home on leave; Governor Luo Bingzhang ordered him to lead a thousand freshly trained militiamen to help. On arrival he routed Shi Dakai at Pingxu. Once Linjiang fell, he was promoted to prefect and awarded the peacock feather. In 1858 he took Chongren, pressed the attack on Xincheng, won five battles in a row, and received the rank of circuit intendant. With Jiangxi pacified, he returned home in triumph.
5
九年，石達開犯永州，忠義赴援，連戰破之，擢道員。 又敗賊新寧摩訶嶺，扼武岡。 賊圍寶慶，忠義進援，會諸軍迭戰解圍，賜號額爾德木巴圖魯。 十年，駐守綏靖，母病歸。 賊遂陷綏寧、城步，圍武岡，忠義聞警，分軍守新寧，自援武岡，破其眾。 新寧之賊走踞東安，一戰克之，加按察使銜。 又破賊於寧遠四廣橋。 十一年春，連破賊於全州白芒營、宜章栗源堡，還軍屯新寧，遣參將江忠朝扼全州，賊目餘成義斬其酋以降。 加二品頂戴，​​特擢署貴州巡撫。 石達開復自粵竄楚，眾號十萬，忠義以三千人扼會同，大破之。 賊糾湖北來鳳賊黨肆擾，擊走之，遂克來鳳。 達開走入四川。 十二月，丁母憂，請終制，詔允開署缺，仍在湖南勦賊。
In 1859, when Shi Dakai invaded Yongzhou, Zhongyi marched to its relief, defeated him in a series of battles, and was promoted to circuit commissioner. He again routed the rebels at Mohe Ridge in Xinning and sealed off Wugang. When rebels besieged Baoqing, Zhongyi marched to its relief. Together with the other armies he fought battle after battle until the siege was raised, and he was granted the title Erde Mubaturu. In 1860 he garrisoned Suijing, but returned home when his mother fell ill. The rebels then overran Suining and Chengbu and besieged Wugang. At the alarm Zhongyi detached a force to hold Xinning and led the main body to relieve Wugang, where he broke the enemy. The Xinning rebels fled to Dong'an; he seized the place in a single battle and received the rank of provincial surveillance commissioner. He again defeated the rebels at Siguang Bridge in Ningyuan. In the spring of 1861 he routed the rebels in turn at Baimang Camp in Quanzhou and Liyuan Fort in Yizhang, then withdrew to encamp at Xinning. He sent Brigade Commander Jiang Zhongchao to hold Quanzhou, and the rebel officer Yu Chengyi slew his leader and surrendered. He received the second-rank cap button and was specially promoted to acting governor of Guizhou. Shi Dakai again broke out of Guangdong into Hunan at the head of a force said to number one hundred thousand. Zhongyi with three thousand men blocked him at Huitong and won a crushing victory. The rebels rallied allies from Laifeng in Hubei to raid the region; he drove them off and then captured Laifeng. Dakai fled into Sichuan. In the twelfth month he entered mourning for his mother and asked to observe the full mourning term. The court allowed him to leave his acting post but ordered him to continue suppressing rebels in Hunan.
6
同治元年，移師援黔，克天柱，改授貴州提督。 調援廣西，克修仁，殪賊渠張高友。 皖南賊熾，曾國籓疏調援皖，廣西巡撫劉長佑請留不遣，命署廣西提督。 二年，江西、廣東皆調援，先後報可，忠義以廣東兵有餘糧，他將足辦賊； 江西餉絀兵單，賊數十萬，萬一不支，東南全局瓦解，乃奏請力援江西。 檄道員席寶田率前部先發，會剿陶家渡，自將攻湖口，逼賊營，屢出奇兵抄擊，斷文橋，攻太平關，賊酋黃文金受重創遁去，賜黃馬褂。 進援青陽，分三路進戰，破賊壘，圍解，太平、石埭、寧國諸城賊第出降，詔嘉獎，予優敘。 會疾作，返就醫南昌，未至，卒於吳城，年甫三十。 優詔悼惜，依總督例賜卹，贈尚書銜，諡誠恪，立功地建專祠。 光緒十一年，加贈太子少保。
In 1862 he moved his army to aid Guizhou, captured Tianzhu, and was appointed provincial military commissioner of Guizhou. Transferred to aid Guangxi, he took Xiuren and killed the rebel leader Zhang Gaoyou. As rebels flared in southern Anhui, Zeng Guofan asked that he be sent to aid Anhui, but Guangxi Governor Liu Changyou pleaded to keep him. He was ordered to serve as acting provincial military commissioner of Guangxi. In 1863 both Jiangxi and Guangdong requested his aid, and each request was approved in turn. Zhongyi reasoned that Guangdong's troops had ample supplies and other generals could handle the rebels there; whereas Jiangxi was short of pay and thinly manned, facing rebels said to number in the hundreds of thousands. If it gave way, the whole southeast would unravel. He therefore memorialized to throw his full strength into aiding Jiangxi. He ordered Circuit Intendant Xi Baotian to march the vanguard ahead and join the action at Taojiadu, while he himself attacked Hukou, pressed the rebel camps, and repeatedly sent flanking columns. He severed Wen Bridge, stormed Taiping Pass, and drove off the badly wounded rebel chief Huang Wenjin. He was awarded the yellow jacket. Marching to relieve Qingyang, he attacked in three columns, stormed the rebel stockades, and raised the siege. At Taiping, Shidi, Ningguo, and other towns the rebels surrendered in turn. The court praised him and granted preferential promotion. When illness struck he turned back to seek treatment at Nanchang, but died at Wucheng before he arrived. He was only thirty. An edict mourned him with special favor. Condolence followed the precedent for a governor-general. He was posthumously given ministerial rank, honored with the posthumous name Chengyi, and a special shrine was built where he had won his merit. In 1885 he was further posthumously made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent.
7
從弟忠珀，記名提督。 同治八年，剿貴州苗，攻克鎮遠、府衛二城，中砲亡，諡武愍。
His younger cousin Zhongpo held registered rank as provincial military commissioner. In 1869, while suppressing the Miao in Guizhou, he captured Zhenyuan and the two cities of Fuwei, was killed by cannon fire, and was honored with the posthumous name Wumin.
8
週寬世，字厚齋，湖南湘鄉人。 咸豐初，從湘軍，隸李續賓部下。 戰城陵磯、花園、半壁山，皆有功，擢千總。 從援江西，攻廣信，戰烏石山，寬世出左路突陣，為諸軍先，復其城，擢守備。 破賊義寧，擢都司。 回援武漢，戰通城，寬世馳斬馬賊三，生擒七，以游擊補用。 從攻武昌，六年，李續賓夜出偵賊，之雙鳳山，突戰，寬世潛繞山趾橫擊之，賊敗奔； 又戰鷹嘴，受砲傷，假歸。 累功擢參將，賜花翎。
Zhou Kuanshi, whose style was Houzhai, came from Xiangxiang in Hunan. Early in the Xianfeng reign he joined the Hunan Army under Li Xubin. He fought at Chenglingji, Huayuan, and Banbishan with distinction in each action and was promoted to battalion commander. He marched to aid Jiangxi, attacked Guangxin, and fought at Wushi Mountain. Kuanshi led the left wing in a charge and was first among the armies to break in, recovering the city. He was promoted to garrison commander. He defeated the rebels at Yining and was promoted to regional commander. Returning to relieve Wuhan, he fought at Tongcheng. Kuanshi rode out and cut down three mounted rebels and captured seven alive. He was appointed acting brigade commander. He joined the assault on Wuchang. In 1856 Li Xubin went out at night to scout the rebels and reached Shuangfeng Mountain, where battle was joined. Kuanshi secretly circled the foot of the mountain and struck across the flank, and the rebels broke and fled. He fought again at Eagle Beak, was wounded by cannon fire, and took leave to recuperate. For accumulated merit he was promoted to deputy commander and awarded the peacock feather.
9
既而羅澤南卒於軍，續賓代將，召寬世回營。 迭破賊於雙鳳山、魯家港、小龜山，克武漢，復大冶、興國，擢副將。 七年，從攻九江，破援賊於童司簰，毀其壘，賜號義勇巴圖魯。 破小池口賊屯，會克湖口，复彭澤。 賊由臨江犯興國，寬世率千六百人擊走之。 八年，回援湖北，戰麻城西南斗坡山。 賊設伏，以馬隊誘戰，寬世待其近，突擊之，遂破其伏軍，進克黃安，而麻城亦下。 大軍克九江，論功，以總兵記名。
When Luo Zemin died in the field, Xubin took command in his place and summoned Kuanshi back to camp. He defeated the rebels in turn at Shuangfeng Mountain, Lujia Harbor, and Little Tortoise Mountain, took Wuchang, recovered Daye and Xingguo, and was promoted to vice commander. In 1857 he joined the assault on Jiujiang, routed relief rebels at Tongsi Pai, destroyed their stockades, and was granted the title Yiyong Batulu. He stormed the rebel camp at Xiaochikou, helped take Hukou, and recovered Pengze. When rebels from Linjiang invaded Xingguo, Kuanshi with sixteen hundred men drove them off. In 1858 he returned to aid Hubei and fought at Doupo Mountain southwest of Macheng. The rebels laid an ambush and used cavalry to bait a fight. Kuanshi waited until they were close, then struck suddenly, broke the ambush, advanced to take Huang'an, and Macheng fell as well. When the main army took Jiujiang, his merit was assessed and he was registered for promotion to regional commander.
10
從李續賓進軍安徽，戰楓香鋪、小池驛，克太湖、潛山，搗舒城，寬世皆為軍鋒。 十月，進攻三河，續賓戰沒，寬世斂餘眾守二日，彈丸俱盡，夜率親卒突圍，受重傷。 是年冬，授湖南永州鎮總兵。 九年，石達開犯湖南，巡撫駱秉章令寬世募新軍二千援祁陽。 破賊長慶橋，又敗之長葉嶺。 進援寶慶，屯城東，連敗賊長衝口、五里牌。 李續宜援師至，會諸軍內外夾擊，賊解圍走。 回剿永州土匪，平之。 十一年，擢湖南提督。
He followed Li Xubin into Anhui, fought at Fengxiangpu and Xiaochiyi, took Taihu and Qianshan, and raided Shucheng. In each action Kuanshi led the vanguard. In the tenth month he advanced on Sanhe, where Xubin fell in battle. Kuanshi rallied the survivors and held for two days until ammunition ran out, then led his personal guard in a night breakout and was badly wounded. That winter he was appointed regional commander of the Yongzhou Garrison in Hunan. In 1859, when Shi Dakai invaded Hunan, Governor Luo Bingzhang ordered Kuanshi to raise two thousand fresh troops to relieve Qiyang. He defeated the rebels at Changqing Bridge and again at Changye Ridge. He marched to relieve Baoqing, encamped east of the city, and defeated the rebels in turn at Changchongkou and Wulipai. When Li Xuyi's relief force arrived, the armies inside and outside struck together in a pincer, and the rebels broke the siege and fled. He returned to suppress the bandits of Yongzhou and pacified them. In 1861 he was promoted to provincial military commissioner of Hunan.
11
同治元年，赴安徽助剿，駐守桐城。 二年，捻匪馬融和犯桐城，擊走之，移防六安。 皖北漸定，調守安慶。 三年，赴援江西，克東鄉。 四年，破霆軍叛勇，追賊入廣東，會諸軍殲賊於嘉應。 五年，回湖南提督任。 傷發，乞休。 光緒十三年，卒。
In 1862 he went to Anhui to assist in suppression and garrisoned Tongcheng. In 1863 the Nian bandit Ma Ronghe attacked Tongcheng. Kuanshi drove him off and shifted his garrison to Lu'an. As northern Anhui gradually settled, he was transferred to guard Anqing. In 1864 he went to aid Jiangxi and captured Dongxiang. In 1865 he defeated mutineers from the Thunder Army, pursued the rebels into Guangdong, and joined the other armies in destroying them at Jiaying. In 1866 he returned to his post as provincial military commissioner of Hunan. When his old wounds reopened he asked to retire. He died in 1887.
12
石清吉，字祥瑞，直隸沙河人。 道光二十一年武進士，官三等侍衛。 咸豐初，出為湖北鄖陽鎮守備，從剿黃陂、崇陽、應城，累擢參將。 克安陸、京山皆有功，以勇稱，所統曰飛虎軍。 尋隸將軍都興阿軍，常從多隆阿轉戰。 七年，援蘄州，拔太湖，攻安慶。 八年，由安慶退保宿松，大戰破賊。 九年，攻太湖。 十年，大戰小池，克太湖，功皆最。 十一年，安慶既下，會諸軍克桐城。
Shi Qingji, whose style was Xiangrui, came from Shahe in Zhili. A military jinshi of 1841, he served as a third-rank imperial bodyguard. Early in the Xianfeng reign he was posted as garrison commander of Yunyang Garrison in Hubei. He took part in suppressing Huangpi, Chongyang, and Yingcheng and was repeatedly promoted to deputy commander. He distinguished himself in taking Anlu and Jingshan, was famed for valor, and commanded a force known as the Flying Tiger Army. He was soon attached to the army of General Duxing'a and often campaigned under Duolong'a. In 1857 he relieved Qizhou, captured Taihu, and attacked Anqing. In 1858 he withdrew from Anqing to hold Susong and won a major victory over the rebels. In 1859 he attacked Taihu. In 1860 he fought a major battle at Xiaochi, captured Taihu, and ranked first in merit throughout. In 1861, after Anqing fell, he joined the other armies in taking Tongcheng.
13
同治元年，從攻廬州，清吉屯城西北，破賊壘，擒斬數千。 進毀賊柵，樹雲梯攻城，賊方死拒，而陳玉成兵敗遽去，遂由西門攻入，克廬州。 清吉累以戰功賜號幹勇巴圖魯，擢總兵，加提督銜。 多隆阿督師赴陝西，以清吉統五千人留守廬州。 二年，苗沛霖复叛，廬、壽、開土蜂匪起，清吉悉剿平之。 粵、捻諸匪合擾豫、楚之交，清吉赴援湖北，屯孝感、黃岡，拔難民近萬。
In 1862 he joined the assault on Luzhou. Qingji encamped northwest of the city, stormed rebel stockades, and captured or killed several thousand. He advanced to destroy the rebel palisades and set scaling ladders against the walls. The defenders were still fighting to the death when Chen Yucheng's army was routed and fled, and Qingji entered through the west gate and took Luzhou. For repeated battle merit Qingji was granted the title Ganyong Batulu, promoted to regional commander, and given the rank of provincial military commissioner. When Duolong'a led the army to Shaanxi, Qingji was left with five thousand men to hold Luzhou. In 1863 Miao Peilin rebelled again, and bandits swarmed through Luzhou, Shouzhou, and Kaicheng. Qingji suppressed them all. Guangdong and Nian bandits together raided the Henan-Hubei border. Qingji went to aid Hubei, encamped at Xiaogan and Huanggang, and rescued nearly ten thousand civilians.
14
三年九月，匪酋陳得才、馬融和合犯蘄水，圍副都統富森保於關口。 清吉率軍馳援，會大霧，賊馬步數万麕集。 清吉進至藥山，賊渡河抄後路，圍數重，截其四營為二。 自辰至午，血戰，被九創，殞於陣。 從戰歿者，副將江星南、谷明發，游擊曾佔彪、段會元。 事聞，詔視提督陣亡例賜卹，入祀京師昭忠祠，予騎都尉世職，諡威毅，建專祠。
In the ninth month of 1864 the bandit chiefs Chen Decai and Ma Ronghe together invaded Qishui and besieged Vice Commander-in-Chief Fusenbao at Guankou. Qingji hurried to their relief, but a heavy fog descended and tens of thousands of rebel horse and foot massed around him. Qingji advanced to Yaoshan. The rebels crossed the river to cut his rear, surrounded him in ring after ring, and split his four camps in two. From morning until noon he fought a bloody battle, received nine wounds, and fell on the field. Those who died with him in battle were Vice Commanders Jiang Xingnan and Gu Mingfa and Brigade Commanders Zeng Zhanbiao and Duan Huiyuan. When word reached the court, an edict granted condolence by the precedent for a provincial military commissioner killed in battle, enshrined him in the Capital Shrine of Loyalty, granted a hereditary rank of Cavalry Commandant, honored him with the posthumous name Weiyi, and built a special shrine.
15
餘際昌，湖北穀城人。 咸豐初入伍，剿匪積功至守備，署撫標右營游擊，為巡撫胡林翼所識拔。 七年，從戰黃梅、廣濟。 八年，陳玉成自太湖竄蘄州，際昌奉檄防皖、楚之交，敗賊南陽河，毀賊壘三十餘，擒賊目。 賊走英山，追躡之，復其城，擢游擊。 又破賊彌陀寺，晉參將。 李續賓軍覆三河，潛山、太湖复陷，際昌屯英山，遏潛、太之衝。 九年，進拔天堂。 賊大舉來爭，際昌敗諸王婆坳，追至雞冠嶺而還。 再敗賊槎水畈，斬馘千餘。 時大軍圍太湖急，陳玉成糾黨十餘萬相持小池驛。 十年正月，際昌偕金國琛由間道出高橫嶺，與諸軍夾擊，大破之，遂復太湖，乘勝會攻克潛山，擢副將，署湖北督標中軍副將。 陳玉成自六安回援安慶，霍山复陷。 際昌偕總兵成大吉擊破之，复霍山，加總兵銜。 十一年，陳玉成入霍山，自黑石渡撲樂兒嶺。 際昌軍潰，賊上竄黃州，革職留營。 尋從克黃州，率新募昌勝五營援河南。
Yu Jichang came from Gucheng in Hubei. Early in the Xianfeng reign he enlisted, rose through merit in bandit suppression to garrison commander, served as acting brigade commander of the governor's right battalion, and was recognized and promoted by Governor Hu Linyi. In 1857 he fought at Huangmei and Guangji. In 1858, when Chen Yucheng broke out of Taihu toward Qizhou, Jichang was ordered to guard the Anhui-Hubei border. He defeated the rebels at Nanyang River, destroyed more than thirty stockades, and captured a rebel officer. The rebels fled to Yingshan. He pursued them, recovered the city, and was promoted to brigade commander. He again defeated the rebels at Mituo Temple and was advanced to deputy commander. When Li Xubin's army was destroyed at Sanhe and Qianshan and Taihu fell again, Jichang encamped at Yingshan to block the route between Qianshan and Taihu. In 1859 he advanced and took Tiantang. The rebels came in force to contest the ground. Jichang defeated them at Wangpo'ao and pursued as far as Jiguan Ridge before returning. He again defeated the rebels at Chashuifan and killed or captured more than a thousand. At that time the main army was pressing hard on Taihu. Chen Yucheng rallied more than one hundred thousand followers and held them at bay at Xiaochiyi. In the first month of 1860, Jichang and Jin Guochen took a bypath to Gaoheng Ridge and joined the other armies in a pincer, winning a crushing victory. They recovered Taihu and, pressing the advantage, took Qianshan with the combined forces. Jichang was promoted to vice commander and appointed acting vice commander of the central army of the Hubei governor's banner. Chen Yucheng returned from Lu'an to relieve Anqing, and Huoshan fell again. Jichang and Regional Commander Cheng Daji defeated him, recovered Huoshan, and Jichang received the rank of regional commander. In 1861 Chen Yucheng entered Huoshan and, crossing from Heishi Ford, struck at Le'er Ridge. Jichang's army was routed and the rebels fled upriver to Huangzhou. He was dismissed from office but kept with the army. He soon followed in the capture of Huangzhou and led five newly raised battalions of the Changsheng Corps to aid Henan.
16
同治元年，屯陳留。 捻匪麕集杞縣，際昌馳擊，大破之，進拔焦、趙二寨，復原官，賜號偉勇巴圖魯。 十月，攻捻於汝寧，破平輿寨，生擒賊酋陳文，詔以總兵記名。 僧格林沁嘉其勇，令充翼長，從剿渦河，斬賊渠楊興太等。 二年春，追破陳大喜於阜陽吳老莊。 捻首張總愚竄侯集，際昌會張曜夜襲之，擒其黨獨角虎、週馬，授河北鎮總兵。 夏，逐賊楚、豫間，敗之麻城，躡至方家寨，中伏力戰，受三十餘創，死之。 贈提督，予騎都尉世職，諡威毅。
In 1862 he encamped at Chenliu. Nian bandits massed at Qixian. Jichang rode out and won a crushing victory, then advanced to take the stockades of Jiao and Zhao. He recovered his former office and was granted the title Weiyong Batulu. In the tenth month he attacked the Nian at Runing, stormed Pingyu Stockade, and captured the bandit chief Chen Wen alive. An edict registered him for promotion to regional commander. Senggelinqin praised his valor and made him a wing commander. He followed the campaign on the Wo River and beheaded the bandit leader Yang Xingtai and others. In the spring of 1863 he pursued and defeated Chen Daxi at Wulaozhuang in Fuyang. When the Nian chief Zhang Zongyu fled to Houji, Jichang joined Zhang Yao in a night assault and captured his followers One-Horned Tiger and Zhou Ma. He was appointed regional commander of the Hebei Garrison. That summer he pursued the rebels between Hubei and Henan, defeated them at Macheng, and followed to Fangjia Stockade. There he fell into an ambush, fought on despite more than thirty wounds, and died. He was posthumously made provincial military commissioner, granted a hereditary rank of Cavalry Commandant, and honored with the posthumous name Weiyi.
17
林文察，字子明，福建台灣人。 咸豐八年，從剿台灣淡水土匪，捐餉助軍，以游擊留福建補用。 十年，九壟山匪郭萬淙掠建寧、邵武間，汀州、龍嚴匪胡熊擾寧洋、永安。 文察隨軍進剿，擒其黨百餘人。 郭萬淙遁據邵武上山坊，文察合軍蹙之，降其眾，复破胡熊於東板土寨，擒之，擢參將，賜號固勇巴圖魯。 十一年，援浙，克江山，晉副將，晉號烏訥思齊巴圖魯。 汀州、連城相繼陷，文察回援，破賊金雞嶺，設伏，敗之江防，遂拔連城，乘勝克汀州，以總兵記名。 冬，杭州既陷，調援浙，文察領台勇二千人駐衢州。 同治元年，破處州賊屯，而遂昌陷，文察進軍逼之。 李世賢自江山來援，文察設伏大柘、大廟及石練山之前後，賊至，擊走之。 夜，賊來劫營，復為伏兵所敗，复遂昌，進克松陽。 會總兵秦如虎攻處州，賊棄城遁，並克縉雲，授福寧鎮總兵。 尋擢福建提督。
Lin Wencha, whose style was Ziming, came from Taiwan in Fujian. In 1858 he took part in suppressing bandits in Tamsui, Taiwan, contributed funds to aid the army, and was retained in Fujian for appointment as brigade commander. In 1860 the Jiulong Mountain bandit Guo Wancong raided between Jianning and Shaowu, while the Tingzhou and Longyan bandit Hu Xiong harassed Ningyang and Yong'an. Wencha followed the army in the suppression and captured more than a hundred of their followers. Guo Wancong fled to Shangshanfang in Shaowu. Wencha combined forces to corner him and accepted the surrender of his followers. He again defeated Hu Xiong at Dongban Earth Stockade and captured him. Wencha was promoted to deputy commander and granted the title Guyong Batulu. In 1861 he aided Zhejiang, captured Jiangshan, was advanced to vice commander, and received the advanced title Unesiqi Batulu. Tingzhou and Liancheng fell in succession. Wencha returned to relieve them, defeated the rebels at Golden Cock Ridge, laid an ambush and routed them at the river defense, then took Liancheng and pressed on to capture Tingzhou. He was registered for promotion to regional commander. That winter, after Hangzhou fell, he was transferred to aid Zhejiang. Wencha led two thousand Taiwan braves to garrison Quzhou. In 1862 he stormed the rebel camp at Chuzhou, but Suichang fell. Wencha advanced to press the town. Li Shixian came from Jiangshan to relieve the town. Wencha set ambushes around Dazhe, Damiao, and Shilianshan, and when the rebels arrived he drove them off. That night the rebels raided the camp and were again defeated by the ambush force. He recovered Suichang and advanced to take Songyang. Joining Regional Commander Qin Ruhu in attacking Chuzhou, he drove the rebels from the city and also took Jinyun. He was appointed regional commander of the Funing Garrison. He was soon promoted to provincial military commissioner of Fujian.
18
二年，台灣不靖，總督左宗棠令渡台號召舊部，統領諸軍。 文察分軍攻彰化及鬥六，克之。 諭降諸莊，賊渠戴萬生、林戇晟遁走。 三年，破樵溪口賊莊，斬其酋林傳，毀張厝莊、四塊厝賊巢，戴萬生、林戇晟並伏誅。
In 1863, when Taiwan was unsettled, Governor Zuo Zongtang ordered him to cross the strait, rally his old followers, and command all the armies. Wencha divided his army to attack Zhanghua and Douliu and captured both. He persuaded the villages to surrender while the rebel leaders Dai Wansheng and Lin Gangsheng fled. In 1864 he stormed the rebel village at Qiaoxikou, beheaded its chief Lin Chuan, and destroyed the rebel nests at Zhangcuozhuang and Sikuaicuo. Dai Wansheng and Lin Gangsheng were both executed.
19
粵匪李世賢、汪海洋合陷漳州，文察倉猝率二百人內渡，遇賊萬松關，歿於陣，贈太子少保，予騎都尉世職，諡剛愍。 本籍及漳州建專祠。
When Guangdong rebels Li Shixian and Wang Haiyang together took Zhangzhou, Wencha hastily led two hundred men across to the mainland, met the rebels at Wansong Pass, and fell in battle. He was posthumously made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent, granted a hereditary rank of Cavalry Commandant, and honored with the posthumous name Gangmin. Special shrines were built in his native place and at Zhangzhou.
20
子朝棟，光緒中，法兵犯台灣，陷基隆，朝棟率家兵助戰有功，捐鉅貲，賜四品京堂，有聲於時。
His son Chaodong, during the Guangxu reign, when French troops invaded Taiwan and took Keelung, led household troops to good effect in the fighting, contributed a large sum, was granted the rank of Fourth-Rank Beijing Attendant, and was widely known in his day.
21
趙德光，原姓張，貴州郎岱人。 從副將趙德昌轉戰雲南，德昌弟畜之，故冒姓趙氏。 拔補千總，擢都司。 咸豐十年，自領一軍，戰獨山，屢敗賊，擢游擊。 十一年，賊窺省城，德光擊走之。 又敗之羊場平寨，設伏於主戎山麓，殪賊無算，擢參將，賜號豪勇巴圖魯。 教匪踞玉華、尚大坪，以王卡為屏蔽。 德光率所部攻破楊義司、郭家莊、馬籠口賊營，斷其援，又破腰蘿溪、新砦岩要隘，進偪王卡。 德光先登，賊大潰，救出男婦數千人，擢副將。
Zhao Deguang, whose original surname was Zhang, came from Langdai in Guizhou. He followed Vice Commander Zhao Dechang in campaigns in Yunnan. Dechang raised him like a younger brother, and he therefore took the surname Zhao. He was selected and appointed battalion commander, then promoted to regional commander. In 1860 he commanded an army in his own right, fought at Dushan, repeatedly defeated the rebels, and was promoted to brigade commander. In 1861, when rebels probed the provincial capital, Deguang drove them off. He again defeated them at Yangchangping Stockade and laid an ambush at the foot of Zhurong Mountain, killing countless rebels. He was promoted to deputy commander and granted the title Haoyong Batulu. Teaching-bandits held Yuhua and Shangdaping, with Wangka as their shield. Deguang led his troops to storm the rebel camps at Yangyisi, Guojiazhuang, and Malongkou, sever their relief, break the passes at Yaoluoxi and Xinzhaiyan, and advance on Wangka. Deguang was first up the wall. The rebels broke in disorder, and several thousand men and women were rescued. He was promoted to vice commander.
22
同治二年，壩芒匪首潘明杰由龍里窺伺省城。 德光迎擊三江橋，賊敗走。 進攻甲秀閣賊巢，遂克龍里舊縣，補都勻協副將，以總兵記名。 三年，尚大坪匪撲省城，德光與布政使龔自閎等固守，賊尋退，加提督銜，署古州鎮總兵。 旋解清鎮圍，克龍里、廣順、定番、長寨，以提督記名。 四年，匪首何二久踞開州、尚大坪，擾近省州縣，無寧歲。 德光選精銳過清水江剿之。 賊糾集苗匪、教匪沿江以拒，乘間過江攻開州。 德光固守十餘日，殺賊八九百人，乘勝追擊，克沿江獅子坉、鎮江坉、三龍營賊屯。 進克濱江賊巢，斬馘二千餘，何二棄尚大坪而遁，被優敘，署安義鎮總兵。
In 1863 the Bama bandit chief Pan Mingjie from Longli watched for a chance to strike the provincial capital. Deguang met them at Sanjiang Bridge and drove them off. He advanced on the rebel nest at Jiaxiu Pavilion and took the old county seat of Longli. He was appointed vice commander of the Duyun Brigade and registered for promotion to regional commander. In 1864 bandits from Shangdaping rushed the provincial capital. Deguang, with Provincial Administration Commissioner Gong Zihong and others, held firm until the rebels withdrew. He received the rank of provincial military commissioner and was appointed acting regional commander of the Guzhou Garrison. He then lifted the siege of Qingzhen and took Longli, Guangshun, Dingfan, and Changzhai. He was registered for promotion to provincial military commissioner. In 1865 the bandit chief He Erjiu held Kaizhou and Shangdaping and harassed nearby prefectures and counties, leaving the region without a peaceful year. Deguang chose elite troops to cross the Qingshui River and suppress them. The rebels rallied Miao and teaching-bandits along the river to resist, then seized an opening to cross and attack Kaizhou. Deguang held firm for more than ten days, killed eight or nine hundred rebels, and pressed the pursuit. He took the rebel camps at Shizitun, Zhenjiangtun, and Sanlongying along the river. He advanced to take the riverside rebel nest and killed or captured more than two thousand. He Er abandoned Shangdaping and fled. Deguang received preferential promotion and was appointed acting regional commander of the Anyi Garrison.
23
五年，署貴州提督。 攻克永寧，解安順圍。 六年，援定番，乘雷雨破賊，斬賊首許八十等，平花山賊屯，拔底季賊巢，晉號博奇巴圖魯。 尋勦賊安平蘆荻哨，深入賊伏，中槍陣亡。 詔依提督陣亡賜卹，贈太子太保，予騎都尉兼一云騎尉世職，諡剛節，建專祠。 遺腹生子秉鈞，襲世職，復姓張氏。
In 1866 he served as acting provincial military commissioner of Guizhou. He took Yongning and lifted the siege of Anshun. In 1867 he aided Dingfan, used a thunderstorm to break the rebels, beheaded the rebel chief Xu Bashi and others, pacified the rebel camp at Huashan, and took the rebel nest at Diji. He advanced in title to Boqi Batulu. Soon after, while suppressing rebels at Anping Ludishao, he went deep into an ambush, was shot, and fell on the field. An edict granted condolence by the precedent for a provincial military commissioner killed in battle. He was posthumously made Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, granted a hereditary rank of Cavalry Commandant with one additional Cloud Cavalry Commandant, honored with the posthumous name Gangjie, and given a special shrine. A posthumous son, Bingjun, inherited the hereditary rank and restored the surname Zhang.
24
張文德，湖南鳳凰廳人。 幼育於文氏，從姓文，名龍德。 入行伍，隸鎮筸營。 咸豐初，從剿江寧、廬州，敘把總。 六年，從提督和春攻三河賊壘不下，文德請獨身持檄諭賊降，投誠者相繼至，遂克三河。 七年，從復鎮江，擢都司。 八年，從援福建，下浦城、松溪、政和、崇安，賜花翎。 九年，敘援浙江功，晉游擊。 十年，從張國樑解鎮江圍，援賊復至，文德扼水柵七晝夜，賊引去，擢副將。 自是從將軍巴棟阿、提督馮子材守鎮江。 十一年，補廣東羅定協副將。
Zhang Wende came from Fenghuang Subprefecture in Hunan. Raised in youth by the Wen clan, he took their surname and was named Longde. He entered the ranks and was attached to the Zhengan Garrison. Early in the Xianfeng reign he took part in suppressing Jiangning and Luzhou and was recorded for promotion to platoon commander. In 1856 he followed Grand Secretary He Chun in attacking the rebel stockades at Sanhe without success. Wende asked to go alone with a summons to induce surrender. Defectors came in one after another, and Sanhe was taken. In 1857 he took part in recovering Zhenjiang and was promoted to regional commander. In 1858 he joined the relief of Fujian, took Pucheng, Songxi, Zhenghe, and Chong'an, and was awarded the peacock feather. In 1859 his merit in aiding Zhejiang was recorded and he was advanced to brigade commander. In 1860 he followed Zhang Guoliang in lifting the siege of Zhenjiang. When relief rebels returned, Wende held the water barrier for seven days and nights until they withdrew. He was promoted to vice commander. From then on he followed General Badong'a and Provincial Military Commissioner Feng Zicai in defending Zhenjiang. In 1861 he was appointed vice commander of the Luoding Brigade in Guangdong.
25
同治元年，賊屢攻鎮江，皆擊退。 馮子材奏言：「文德力挫賊鋒，重圍疊解，實為特出之材。 」授貴州鎮遠鎮總兵，賜號翼勇巴圖魯。 文德以生父年七十無子，養父文氏有二子，陳請復姓，更名文德。 二年，連破賊牧馬口、薛村，克柏林村賊壘，加提督銜。 賊由東路來犯，文德禦諸駭溪、諫壁，腹中砲，腸出，裹創而戰，援軍至，賊乃退； 又破之博洛村，攻丹陽，毀賊壘，擒賊目。 三年，克白堍鎮及寶堰，賊黨紛紛來降。 會鮑超攻丹陽，招賊酋蔣鑑為內應，克其城，斬賊酋陳時永，擒賴桂芳，以提督記名。 江南平，予一品封典，命赴鎮遠鎮本任。
In 1862 the rebels repeatedly attacked Zhenjiang, and each time he drove them back. Feng Zicai memorialized: "Wende has repeatedly blunted the rebels' edge and lifted siege after siege. He is truly an outstanding talent." He was appointed regional commander of the Zhenyuan Garrison in Guizhou and granted the title Yiyong Batulu. Because his birth father at seventy had no sons while his adoptive Wen clan had two, Wende petitioned to restore his surname Zhang and took the name Wende. In 1863 he defeated the rebels in turn at Muma Pass and Xue Village, took the rebel stockade at Bolin Village, and received the rank of provincial military commissioner. When rebels came by the eastern route, Wende met them at Haixi and Jianbi. Shot in the abdomen so that his intestines protruded, he bound the wound and kept fighting until relief arrived and the rebels withdrew. He again defeated them at Boluo Village, attacked Danyang, destroyed rebel stockades, and captured a rebel officer. In 1864 he took Baiyan Town and Baoyan, and rebel bands surrendered in succession. Joining Bao Chao in attacking Danyang, he won the rebel chief Jiang Jian as an inside agent, took the city, beheaded the rebel chief Chen Shiyong, and captured Lai Guifang. He was registered for promotion to provincial military commissioner. When Jiangnan was pacified he received a first-rank patent of nobility and was ordered to take up his post at the Zhenyuan Garrison.
26
四年，總督勞崇光令募楚勇規荔波、獨山。 丁父憂，解職。 六年，署貴州提督。 七年，克開州，破鼎照山賊砦，克龍里、貴定，斬賊酋潘名桀，餘賊多降，被珍賚。 進攻平越，擒金大五，連克麻哈、都勻，賜黃馬褂，晉號達桑阿巴圖魯。 請假歸葬親，文德既去，賊复熾。 八年，回貴州，以糧匱軍潰，都勻复陷。 詔原之，免議，署古州鎮。 十年，授威寧鎮總兵，督軍剿古州苗。 由九甲、五台山、扁擔山及古州、丹江分路雕剿，年餘，苗渠先後伏誅。 十三年，全黔肅清，予雲騎尉世職。 光緒元年，加頭品頂帶，擢貴州提督，剿平黎平侗匪。 七年，卒，賜卹，貴陽建專祠。
In 1865 Governor Lao Chongguang ordered him to raise Hunan braves to recover Libo and Dushan. He entered mourning for his father and left office. In 1867 he served as acting provincial military commissioner of Guizhou. In 1868 he took Kaizhou, stormed the rebel fort at Dingzhao Mountain, and captured Longli and Guiding. He beheaded the rebel chief Pan Mingjie, most of the remaining rebels surrendered, and he received imperial rewards. He advanced on Pingyue, captured Jin Dawu, and took Ma'a and Duyun in succession. He was awarded the yellow jacket and advanced in title to Dasang'a Batulu. He asked leave to return home and bury his parent. Once Wende had gone, the rebels flared up again. In 1869 he returned to Guizhou, but supplies ran short, his army collapsed, and Duyun fell again. An edict pardoned him, exempted him from censure, and appointed him to act at the Guzhou Garrison. In 1871 he was appointed regional commander of the Weining Garrison and directed the suppression of the Miao at Guzhou. He divided his forces for pursuit from Jiujia, Wutai Mountain, Biandanshan, Guzhou, and Danjiang. After more than a year the Miao leaders were executed one after another. In 1874 all Guizhou was pacified and he was granted a hereditary rank of Cloud Cavalry Commandant. In 1875 he received the first-rank cap button, was promoted to provincial military commissioner of Guizhou, and pacified the Dong bandits of Liping. He died in 1881. Condolence was granted and a special shrine was built at Guiyang.
27
論曰：江忠源諸弟並從治軍，忠義最為傑出，將大用而早沒，時論惜之。 週寬世為李續賓所倚，無役不從，及自將亦未著奇績。 石清吉、餘際昌、陳大富、林文察、趙德光等，皆久歷行間，以死勤事。 張文德佐馮子材守鎮江，功最著，底定黔疆，與有勞焉。
The commentators say: Jiang Zhongyuan's younger brothers all followed him into military service. Zhongyi was the most outstanding, seemed poised for great responsibility, but died young — a loss widely regretted at the time. Zhou Kuanshi was a man Li Xubin relied on and followed into every campaign, yet even when he commanded in his own right he won no remarkable victories. Shi Qingji, Yu Jichang, Chen Dafu, Lin Wencha, Zhao Deguang, and others all served long in the field and died in the performance of duty. Zhang Wende aided Feng Zicai in defending Zhenjiang with the most distinguished merit, then helped pacify Guizhou — accomplishments in which he had no small share.
