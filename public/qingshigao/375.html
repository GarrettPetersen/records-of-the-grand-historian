1
白鎔，字小山，順天通州人。 嘉慶四年進士，選庶吉士，授編修，典福建鄉試。 十八年，大考二等，擢贊善。 督安徽學政，詔密詢地方利弊，疏言：「安徽錢糧，惟鳳陽、泗州遭湖、河之害，積逋較巨。 遇豐稔之年，循例帶徵舊額。 在小民以一年而輸數年之賦，雖樂歲不免拮据； 而官吏懼譴，規避多方，積重難返。 與其存徵之名，緻小民日受追呼，國計依然無補，何如核徵之實，使官吏從容措理，舊額尚可漸清。 請嗣後二屬錢糧，每年祗帶徵一年，儻遇歉收，再行遞緩，民力漸紓，催科者自顧考成，行之必有效。 」詔允行。
Bai Rong, whose courtesy name was Xiaoshan, came from Tongzhou in the Shuntian prefecture. He received his jinshi degree in the fourth year of Jiaqing, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, was made a compiler, and served as chief examiner for the Fujian provincial examination. In his eighteenth year of service he ranked second class in the palace examination and was promoted to tutor in the Eastern Palace. While serving as Anhui educational commissioner he was secretly instructed to report on local conditions; he memorialized: "Among Anhui's land-tax obligations, only Fengyang and Sizhou, battered by floods from lakes and rivers, carried unusually heavy arrears. In bumper years officials still followed precedent and collected outstanding quotas along with the current levy. Ordinary households were thus asked to pay several years' taxes in a single season, so that even a prosperous year left them strapped; while magistrates, dreading censure, devised endless dodges until the burden grew too entrenched to lift. Better to collect what was truly due than to preserve the fiction of arrears while commoners faced daily dunning with no gain to the treasury; if collectors worked from verified figures, old quotas might still be cleared over time. He asked that thereafter these two prefectures collect at most one year of arrears per annum, deferring further if the harvest failed, so that popular strength could recover and tax agents, mindful of their own evaluations, would enforce the rule—this, he said, would surely work. " The throne approved and ordered it carried out.
2
青陽有孝子曰徐守仁，幼孤，事母孝。 母沒，廬墓三年，鎔造廬贈賻，題請旌表。 訪求明臣左光斗遺裔，取列縣庠。 按試所至，集士人講學，以正人心厚風俗為本。 累遷少詹事。 道光元年，督廣東學政。 歷詹事、內閣學士。 七年，擢工部侍郎，調吏部。 九年，偕尚書松筠赴直隸按外委白勤被誣冤斃獄，護理總督屠之申以下降黜有差。 督江蘇學政。 尋偕侍郎寶興勘視南河垛料，舉實以聞。 十一年，擢左都御史，召還京，未至，命查勘江南災賑。 時尚書穆彰阿、硃士彥亦奉命勘湖、河汎溢狀，穆彰阿先回京，鎔遂偕士彥履勘沿河閘壩工程，與總督陶澍定議以工代賑。 赴安徽，週曆太平、寧國、池州、安慶、廬州各郡，先後疏劾飾災侵賑諸弊。 次年，回京，署翰林院掌院學士，典順天鄉試。 十三年，擢工部尚書，典武會試。 故事，武闈雙好不足額，始取單好。 是科雙好不盡取中，坐降大理寺卿。 十九年，乞病歸，卒於家，年七十四。
At Qingyang lived a filial son named Xu Shouren, who lost his father early yet served his mother with exemplary devotion. After her death he mourned at the grave for three years; Rong built a hut for him, gave funeral gifts, and petitioned for an imperial commendation. He searched out descendants of the Ming minister Zuo Guangdou and enrolled them in the district academy. Wherever he conducted examinations he gathered scholars for lectures, aiming above all to rectify hearts and strengthen local morals. He rose by stages to junior mentor of the heir apparent. In the first year of Daoguang he was appointed educational commissioner for Guangdong. He held the posts of mentor of the heir apparent and bachelor of the Grand Secretariat. In the seventh year he was made vice minister of works, then transferred to the Ministry of Personnel. In the ninth year he joined Minister Songyun in Zhili to review the case of sub-official Bai Qin, executed in prison on a false charge; Acting Governor Tu Zhichao and subordinates were demoted in varying degrees. He served as Jiangsu educational commissioner. He soon joined Vice Minister Bao Xing in inspecting stockpiles along the Southern Canal and reported what they found. In the eleventh year he was promoted to Left Censor-in-Chief and recalled to the capital, but before he arrived was sent to inspect disaster relief in Jiangnan. Ministers Mujangga and Zhu Shiyan were then surveying flood damage on lakes and rivers; Mujangga returned first, and Rong with Zhu Shiyan inspected river sluices and dams, agreeing with Governor Tao Shu to substitute public works for relief grain. In Anhui he toured Taiping, Ningguo, Chizhou, Anqing, Luzhou, and other prefectures, memorializing in turn officials who padded disaster reports or stole relief funds. The next year he returned to the capital, served as acting chancellor of the Hanlin Academy, and presided over the Shuntian provincial examination. In the thirteenth year he became Minister of Works and chief examiner for the military metropolitan examination. By custom, when candidates excelling in both archery and horsemanship did not fill the military quota, single-excellence candidates were admitted. That year not all double-excellence candidates were passed; he was demoted to chief of the Court of Judicial Review for the lapse. In the nineteenth year he retired on grounds of illness and died at home at seventy-four.
3
鎔事母孝，教子弟嚴。 宣宗嘗嘉其家法之善，以勉朝臣雲。
Rong was devoted to his mother and stern in raising his sons and nephews. Emperor Xuanzong once praised his household discipline as a model for the court.
4
孫桓，字建侯。 同治二年進士，授吏部主事。 累遷郎中。 掌選，清嚴慎密，吏不能欺，為時所稱。 光緒中，洊擢兵部侍郎，綜覈一如為司官時。 十七年，因病乞休，尋卒。
Sun Huan, courtesy name Jianhou. He took his jinshi degree in the second year of Tongzhi and was appointed a principal clerk in the Ministry of Personnel. He rose by stages to director. Overseeing appointments, he was lucid, strict, and meticulous; clerks could not fool him, and contemporaries praised his integrity. Under Guangxu he was repeatedly promoted to vice minister of war, reviewing cases with the same rigor he had shown as a bureau officer. In the seventeenth year he asked to retire for illness and soon died.
5
史致儼，字容莊，江蘇江都人。 家酷貧。 甫冠，為諸生，學政謝墉器其才，給膏火，居尊經閣讀書。 薦預召試，未與選。 嘉慶四年，成進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 督四川學政。 累遷右庶子。 二十一年，督河南學政。 自滑縣匪平，猶有伏莽，密詔偵察。 疏陳彰、衛二郡民間習邪教猶眾，州縣編查保甲，有名無實，撰敦俗篇，刊布以化導之。 商丘廩生陳忠錦以不濫保被毆，知府、經歷受賕，反加斥責，忿而自經。 疏劾，譴罪有差。
Shi Zhiyan, whose courtesy name was Rongzhuang, came from Jiangdu in Jiangsu. His family was desperately poor. Soon after his capping ceremony he became a licentiate; Commissioner Xie Yong admired his talent, gave him a stipend, and lodged him in the Zunjing Pavilion to study. He was recommended for the special palace examination but was not chosen. He received his jinshi in the fourth year of Jiaqing, entered the Hanlin Academy, and was made a compiler. He served as Sichuan educational commissioner. He rose to right vice director of the Secretariat. In the twenty-first year he was made Henan educational commissioner. Although the Huaxian rebels had been suppressed, covert troublemakers remained, and he received secret orders to investigate. He reported that heterodox sects still flourished in Zhang and Wei; local baojia registers were empty formalities; he wrote an essay on renewing customs and circulated it for moral instruction. Chen Zhongjin, a Shangqiu grain-student, was beaten for refusing false guarantees; the prefect and intendant's clerk took bribes and blamed him instead; he hanged himself in outrage. He impeached the officials involved, and they were punished in varying degrees.
6
道光元年，典湖北鄉試。 累遷內閣學士。 三年，擢刑部侍郎，調禮部。 五年，督福建學政。 奏分台灣舉人中額，增所屬四縣學額。 漳、泉諸郡習械鬥，諸生與者，屏不與試，悍風稍息。 九年，偕侍郎鍾昌赴山西鞫獄，平定知州故出人罪，鞫實，論兇犯如律，褫知州恆傑職。 調刑部，歷左都御史，遷禮部尚書。 兩典順天鄉試。 調工部，又調刑部。 勤於其職，竟日坐堂上閱案牘，揅析論難，視司員如弟子。 任刑部凡四年，京察，以刑名詳慎，被議敘。 十八年，乞解職。 尋卒，年七十九，贈太子太保，祀鄉賢及名宦祠。
In the first year of Daoguang he served as chief examiner for Hubei. He rose to grand secretariat bachelor. In the third year he became vice minister of punishments, then moved to the Ministry of Rites. In the fifth year he was appointed Fujian educational commissioner. He proposed allotting juren quotas separately for Taiwan and increasing academy seats in its four counties. Zhang and Quan were notorious for armed feuds; students involved were barred from examinations, and the violence gradually eased. In the ninth year he and Vice Minister Zhong Chang tried a case in Shanxi where the Pingding prefect had unlawfully freed criminals; the facts were proved, ringleaders were sentenced by law, and Prefect Heng Jie was dismissed. He moved to the Ministry of Punishments, became Left Censor-in-Chief, then Minister of Rites. He twice served as chief examiner for Shuntian. He was shifted to Works, then back to Punishments. Conscientious in office, he spent whole days in court reviewing dossiers, debating fine points of law, and treating junior officers like pupils. After four years in Punishments, the capital evaluation commended his careful handling of criminal cases. In the eighteenth year he asked to leave office. He soon died at seventy-nine, was posthumously made Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, and entered the local worthies' and eminent officials' shrines.
7
那清安，字竹汀，葉赫納喇氏，滿洲正白旗人。 嘉慶十年進士，授戶部主事，遷翰林院侍講。 累遷內閣學士。 二十四年，授禮部侍郎，歷刑部、工部。 道光元年，命赴直隸讞獄，擢左都御史，管光祿寺事，兼都統。 尋遷兵部尚書，調刑部。 四年，出為熱河都統，偕左都御史松筠等赴土默特讞獄，事竣，疏言：「蒙古惡習，常有移屍訛詐，為害滋甚。 蒙古律例，凡軍流徒犯，罪止折枷，情重法輕。 請嗣後遇有假捏人命詐財者，所擬軍流徒罪即行實發，不准折枷，以懲刁惡。 」下所司議行。 六年，召授左都御史。 逾年，復任熱河都統，召對，詢知其母年老，命仍還左都御史任。 十一年，复授兵部尚書，典順天鄉試及會試。 十四年，以疾乞解職，允之。 尋卒，贈太子太保，諡恭勤。
Na Qing'an, courtesy name Zhuting, of the Yehe Nara clan, was a Plain White Banner Manchu. He took his jinshi in the tenth year of Jiaqing, entered the Ministry of Revenue as a principal, and became a Hanlin expositor. He rose to grand secretariat bachelor. In the twenty-fourth year he became vice minister of rites and later held posts in Punishments and Works. In Daoguang's first year he was sent to try cases in Zhili, made Left Censor-in-Chief, oversaw the Court of Imperial Entertainments, and served concurrently as banner commander. He was soon made Minister of War, then transferred to Punishments. In the fourth year he became Rehe commander; with Left Censor-in-Chief Songyun he tried cases among the Tumed Mongols and then memorialized: "A vicious Mongol practice is to move corpses to frame others and extort money. Under Mongol law, military exile, banishment, and penal servitude are routinely commuted to cangue, so serious cases receive light penalties. He asked that hereafter anyone who staged a death to extort money serve the full sentence of exile or penal servitude without commutation, to curb such fraud. " The memorial was sent to the relevant offices for enactment. In the sixth year he was recalled and made Left Censor-in-Chief. A year later he resumed command at Rehe; at audience the emperor learned his mother was aged and sent him back to the Left Censor-in-Chief post instead. In the eleventh year he again became Minister of War and chief examiner for Shuntian and the metropolitan examinations. In the fourteenth year he retired for illness, and the request was granted. He soon died, was posthumously made Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, and received the posthumous name Gongqin.
8
那清安工為館體應制詩，時皆誦習。 因與穆彰阿同榜成進士，晚乃受宣宗知，迭秉文衡。 既卒，會兵部以慶廉送武會試有殘疾，為監試御史所劾。 先是那清安為監射大臣，曾以慶廉殘疾扣除，上追念其持正，予其子全慶加二級。 全慶，光緒初官大學士，自有傳。
Na Qing'an excelled at regulated court poetry in the academy manner, and his compositions were widely memorized. A jinshi of the same year as Mujangga, he won Emperor Xuanzong's favor only late in life and repeatedly oversaw civil examinations. After his death the Ministry of War was impeached for entering Qinglian in the military metropolitan examination despite a physical disability. When Na Qing'an had supervised the archery examination he had struck Qinglian from the rolls for disability; the emperor, remembering his firmness, granted his son Quanqing two additional ranks. Quanqing became a grand secretary in early Guangxu and has a separate biography.
9
昇寅，字賓旭，馬佳氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人。 拔貢，考授禮部七品小京官。 舉嘉慶五年鄉試。 累遷員外郎，改御史。 疏言學校為人才根本，請嚴課程，務實用，戒奢靡； 又疏陳防禁考試八旗生懷挾冒替諸弊：從之。 改右庶子，累遷副都御史。 二十一年，授盛京禮部侍郎，署盛京將軍。 調刑部，召為工部侍郎，又調刑部。 道光六年，出為熱河都統。 以蒙古各旗招內地遊民開採煤礦，往往生事械鬥，疏請諭禁，從之。 八年，命赴甘肅偕總督鄂山按寧夏將軍慶山、副都統噶普唐阿互劾事，罷慶山，即以昇寅代之。 歷成都、綏遠城將軍。 命鞫鄂爾多斯京控獄，奏言：「蒙古京控日繁，請自後各部落封禁地樹立界牌，以杜私墾； 蒙古阿勒巴圖禁止餽贈，以息爭端； 扎薩克王、貝勒等毋用內地書吏，以免教唆； 各旗協理台吉，會同盟長選舉，以昭慎重； 盟長會盟需用烏拉，應明定限制，以免浮索：庶積弊清而獄訟息。 」
Sheng Yin, courtesy name Binxu, of the Maja clan, was a Bordered Yellow Banner Manchu. Having graduated as a tribute student, he passed the selection examination and became a seventh-rank secretary in the Ministry of Rites. He passed the provincial examination in the fifth year of Jiaqing. He rose to vice director, then became a censor. He argued that schools are the foundation of talent and urged stricter curricula, practical study, and restraint of extravagance; he also proposed measures against cheating by Banner students in examinations, including hidden notes and impersonation, and the throne approved. He became right vice director of the Secretariat and rose to vice censor-in-chief. In the twenty-first year he was made Mukden vice minister of rites and acting Mukden commander. He moved to Punishments, was recalled as vice minister of works, then returned to Punishments. In the sixth year of Daoguang he was posted as Rehe commander. Mongol banners were hiring inland migrants to mine coal, often sparking armed fights; he memorialized for a ban, which was approved. In the eighth year he joined Governor Eshan in Gansu to settle the mutual charges between Ningxia Commander Qing Shan and Vice Commander Gaputang'a; Qing Shan was removed and Sheng Yin took his place. He commanded at Chengdu and Suiyuan. Trying an Ordos appeal at the capital, he reported: "Mongol appeals to Beijing grow daily; boundary markers should be set on each tribe's sealed pasture to stop illegal farming; Mongol commoners should be barred from giving gifts that breed lawsuits; princes and beile should not hire inland clerks who teach them to litigate; assistant banner princes should be chosen jointly by league chiefs and elders to ensure care. League chiefs' alliance meetings required ceremonial gifts; he urged fixed limits on such demands so old abuses and lawsuits might end. "
10
十一年，召授左都御史，兼都統。 十二年，署工部尚書。 京畿旱，疏請發米，設十廠煮粥以濟災民，從之。 十三年，偕侍郎鄂順安按西安將軍徐錕貪縱，得實，議褫職。 十四年，命閱兵山東、河南，就鞫桐柏知縣寧飛濱故出人罪，治如律。 命赴廣東、湖南按事，授禮部尚書，未至，卒於途。 優詔賜卹，稱其老成清介，贈太子太保，諡勤直。
In the eleventh year he was recalled as Left Censor-in-Chief and concurrent banner commander. In the twelfth year he served as acting Minister of Works. When drought struck the capital region he asked to release grain and open ten congee kitchens for the afflicted, and the request was approved. In the thirteenth year he and Vice Minister Eshun'an investigated Xi'an Commander Xu Kun for corruption and lax discipline, proved the charges, and recommended his removal. In the fourteenth year he inspected troops in Shandong and Henan and tried Tongbai Prefect Ning Feibin for unlawfully freeing criminals, sentencing him by law. Sent to investigate affairs in Guangdong and Hunan, he was appointed Minister of Rites but died en route before taking office. The throne granted special mourning favors, praising his seasoned integrity, posthumously made him Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent, and gave him the posthumous name Qinzhi.
11
子寶琳，直隸保定知府，濬定州洿澤，有治績； 寶珣，同治中，官兵部侍郎、山海關副都統。 孫紹祺，咸豐六年進士，由編修官至理籓院尚書； 紹諴，光緒中，山西布政使，從治鄭州河工，終駐藏大臣； 紹英，宣統初，度支部侍郎，內務府大臣。
His son Baolin, prefect of Baoding in Zhili, dredged Dingzhou's stagnant marshes with notable success; Baoxun under Tongzhi was vice minister of war and vice commander at Shanhaiguan. Grandson Shaoqi, a Xianfeng sixth-year jinshi, rose from compiler to minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs; Shaoxuan under Guangxu was Shanxi financial commissioner, helped on the Zhengzhou river project, and finished as Tibet resident commissioner; Shaoying in early Xuantong was vice minister of the Revenue Board and minister of the Imperial Household.
12
李宗昉，字芝齡，江蘇山陽人。 嘉慶七年一甲二名進士，授編修，典陝甘鄉試。 大考二等，擢贊善。 督貴州學政，累遷侍讀學士，督浙江學政。 歷詹事、內閣學士。 道光元年，授禮部侍郎。 次年，典會試，又典江西鄉試，留學政。 值大水，歲饑，與巡撫籌賑務，多所全活。 調戶部侍郎。 初，宗昉督學貴州時，巡撫議丈全省田為增賦計，民情惶駭，會檄學官徵集圖書，得御史包承祚奏疏，乾隆初，學政鄒一桂請丈田，而承祚奏駁之，極言黔中山多平地少，民每虛佔不毛之地，胥吏高下其手，以丈高下不可準之田，賦未必增，民受其害。 部議停止，宗昉持以示巡撫曰：「此事學臣嘗奏之，被駁。 今必解其所駁乃可。 」巡撫亦悟，事得寢。 至是，官戶部，署巡撫麟慶因復奏上其事，部援故事詳覆之，乃定議不行。 歷工部、吏部侍郎，兼管國子監、順天府尹事。 自七年至十年，典順天鄉試二，會試一，浙江鄉試一，得士稱盛。 擢左都御史、禮部尚書。 二十四年，以疾乞休。 二十六年，卒，依例賜卹。
Li Zongfang, courtesy name Zhiling, came from Shanyang in Jiangsu. He took second place in the first class of jinshi in Jiaqing's seventh year, became a compiler, and chief examiner for Shaanxi-Gansu. He ranked second class in the palace examination and was made tutor in the Eastern Palace. He was Guizhou educational commissioner, rose to Hanlin reader, then Zhejiang educational commissioner. He held the posts of mentor and grand secretariat bachelor. In Daoguang's first year he became vice minister of rites. The next year he ran the metropolitan and Jiangxi provincial examinations and stayed on as educational commissioner. When severe floods brought famine he worked with the governor on relief and saved many lives. He was moved to vice minister of revenue. Earlier, as Guizhou educational commissioner, he had faced a governor's plan to survey the whole province for higher taxes, which terrified the populace; collecting documents on order, he found Censor Bao Chengzuo's memorial from early Qianlong rebutting Commissioner Zou Yigui's survey proposal—arguing that Guizhou's mountains left little arable land, that people falsely claimed wasteland, that clerks manipulated figures, that uneven terrain could not be measured fairly, that revenue might not rise, and that the people would suffer. The ministry had halted the plan; Zongfang showed the governor the document, saying, "An educational commissioner once proposed this and was rejected. You must answer that rejection before reviving it." The governor took the point and the scheme was abandoned. Now in the Revenue ministry, Acting Governor Lin Qing revived the proposal; the ministry cited precedent in a full reply and finally blocked it. He was vice minister of works and personnel, concurrently overseeing the Directorate of Education and Shuntian prefecture. From the seventh through tenth years he twice ran Shuntian's provincial exam, once the metropolitan exam, and once Zhejiang's—each cohort acclaimed. He became Left Censor-in-Chief and Minister of Rites. In the twenty-fourth year he retired for illness. He died in the twenty-sixth year and received standard mourning grants.
13
姚元之，字伯昂，安徽桐城人。 嘉慶十年進士，選庶吉士，授編修，典陝甘鄉試。 入直南書房。 給事中花傑劾戴衢亨、英和援引，詔元之文字本佳，斥傑詆訐，尋亦罷元之入直。 十七年，大考一等，擢侍講。 復以武英殿刊刻聖訓有誤，仍降編修。 十九年，督河南學政，疏禁坊刻類典等書以杜剿襲； 又密陳河南與安徽、湖北交界地多捻匪，陳州、汝寧鹽運迥殊，土匪把持：並嘉納之。 累遷內閣學士。
Yao Yuanzhi, courtesy name Bo'ang, came from Tongcheng in Anhui. He took his jinshi in Jiaqing's tenth year, entered the Hanlin Academy, became a compiler, and examined Shaanxi-Gansu. He served in the Southern Study. Censor Hua Jie accused Dai Quheng and Yinghe of patronage; the throne praised Yuanzhi's prose and rebuked Jie for slander, yet soon removed Yuanzhi from the Southern Study as well. In the seventeenth year he ranked first class in the palace examination and became expositor. Errors in the Wuying Hall printing of the Sacred Instructions nonetheless cost him his rank and he was demoted to compiler. In the nineteenth year, as Henan educational commissioner, he banned commercial compendia and similar books to curb plagiarism; he also secretly reported heavy Nian bandit activity on Henan's borders with Anhui and Hubei, and ruffian control of Chen-Ru salt routes—the court welcomed both reports. He rose to grand secretariat bachelor.
14
道光十三年，授工部侍郎。 疏陳台灣營務積弊，窩娼聚賭，械鬥殺人，操演僱人替代，詔下閩督嚴察整頓。 調戶部，又調刑部。 迭典順天、江西鄉試。 督浙江學政，未滿，十八年，擢左都御史，召回京。 尋以南昌知府張寅為江西巡撫裕泰劾罷，元之為寅疏辯，臚陳政績，請查辦，詔斥冒昧，降二級調用。 二十一年，海防方亟，疏陳廣東形勢，豫籌戰守，下靖逆將軍奕山等採行。 授內閣學士。 二十三年，京察，以年衰休致。
In Daoguang's thirteenth year he became vice minister of works. He exposed Taiwan garrison abuses—brothels, gambling, armed killings, and hired stand-ins at drill—and the Fujian governor was ordered to crack down. He moved to Revenue, then to Punishments. He repeatedly served as chief examiner for Shuntian and Jiangxi. As Zhejiang educational commissioner, before his term ended he was made Left Censor-in-Chief in the eighteenth year and recalled. When Jiangxi Governor Yutai impeached Nanchang Prefect Zhang Yin, Yuanzhi defended Zhang, citing his record and asking for review; the throne called him presumptuous and demoted him two ranks. In the twenty-first year, with coastal defense urgent, he outlined Guangdong's strategic position and proposed defensive measures, which Yishan and others were ordered to implement. He was made grand secretariat bachelor. At the twenty-third year's capital evaluation he retired for old age.
15
元之學於族祖鼐，文章爾雅，書畫並工。 習於掌故，館閣推為祭酒。 愛士好事，穆彰阿素重之。 後以論洋務不合，乃被黜。 咸豐二年，卒。
Trained by his kinsman Yao Nai, he wrote refined prose and excelled at calligraphy and painting. Fluent in institutional precedent, the academies hailed him as their doyen. He championed scholars and good works; Mujangga had long esteemed him. Later his views on foreign affairs fell out of favor and he was pushed out. He died in the second year of Xianfeng.
16
何汝霖，字雨人，江蘇江寧人。 拔貢，考授工部七品小京官。 中式道光五年舉人，充軍機章京，累遷郎中。 歷內閣侍讀學士、大理寺少卿。 偕侍郎恩桂按事浙江，查勘南河料垛。 命在軍機大臣上行走，歷宗人府丞、副都御史。 二十二年，授兵部侍郎，調戶部。 偕大學士敬徵勘東河工程。 二十五年，擢兵部尚書。 值太后七旬萬壽，汝霖母丁年九十，五世同堂，賜御書扁額，尋以母憂歸。 江蘇大水，命在籍襄治賑務。 先是，總督陶澍於江寧立豐備倉以備荒，縣令虧挪穀價，大吏許以他款抵。 汝霖曰：「倉穀以備兇。 今荒像如此，汝霖不敢欺朝廷，當各為奏上。 」乃以給賑用。 服闋，命以一品頂戴署禮部侍郎，尋署戶部尚書，仍直軍機處，授禮部尚書。
He Rulin, courtesy name Yuren, came from Jiangning in Jiangsu. A tribute graduate, he passed selection and became a seventh-rank secretary in Works. He passed the provincial exam in Daoguang's fifth year, joined the Grand Council staff, and rose to director. He was a Grand Secretariat reader and vice president of the Court of Judicial Review. With Vice Minister En Gui he investigated Zhejiang affairs and inspected Southern Canal stockpiles. He was assigned to the Grand Council under the grand ministers and served as Imperial Clan vice director and vice censor-in-chief. In the twenty-second year he became vice minister of war, then moved to revenue. He joined Grand Secretary Jingzheng in surveying Eastern Canal works. In the twenty-fifth year he became Minister of War. At the empress dowager's seventieth birthday, Rulin's mother Lady Ding was ninety with five generations in one household; the emperor bestowed an inscribed plaque; he soon left office to mourn her. When Jiangsu flooded he was ordered home to help with relief. Governor Tao Shu had built Jiangning's Fengbei granary for famine years, but magistrates had embezzled grain funds and superiors let other accounts cover the shortfall. Rulin said, "Granary grain exists for famine. With distress this severe I cannot deceive the throne; each shortfall must be reported separately." " The grain was released for relief. After mourning he returned with first-rank insignia as acting vice minister of rites, soon acting Minister of Revenue, still on the Grand Council, then Minister of Rites.
17
汝霖久襄樞務，資勞己深，尚書陳孚恩由章京躋大臣，駸用事，厭汝霖居其前。 汝霖年逾七十，一日在直，觸火爐幾僕。 孚恩笑曰：「人當避爐，爐豈能避人？ 」汝霖知其諷己，咸豐二年，以足疾乞罷直，許之。 未幾，卒，諡恪慎，祀鄉賢。 子兆瀛，浙江鹽運使。
Long experienced in council work, Rulin outranked Minister Chen Fu'en, who had climbed from council clerk to power and resented standing below him. Past seventy, he once stumbled against a brazier while on council duty. Fu'en laughed, "People should dodge the stove—can the stove dodge people?" " Rulin took the jibe; in Xianfeng's second year he asked to leave council duty for foot trouble and was allowed. He soon died, received the posthumous name Kejin, and entered the local worthies' shrine. His son Zhaoying was Zhejiang salt transport commissioner.
18
季芝昌，字仙九，江蘇江陰人。 父麟，直隸鉅鹿知縣，居官慈惠。 嘉慶十八年，捕邪教，焚其籍，免株連數千人。 坐捕匪不力，戍伊犁。
Ji Zhichang, courtesy name Xianjiu, came from Jiangyin in Jiangsu. His father Lin, magistrate of Julu in Zhili, governed with kindness. In Jiaqing's eighteenth year he suppressed a heterodox sect, burned its registers, and spared thousands from collective punishment. Punished for failing to catch bandits, he was exiled to Yili.
19
芝昌年逾四十，成道光十二年一甲三名進士，授編修，散館第一。 未幾，大考第三，擢侍讀，督山東學政。 十九年，大考复第三，擢少詹事，晉詹事，典江西鄉試，督浙江學政。 母憂歸，服闋，擢內閣學士。 二十三年，授禮部侍郎，督安徽學政，調吏部，又調倉場。 二十八年，命偕定郡王載銓籌辦長蘆鹽務，清查天津倉庫，疏陳：「蘆鹽積累，各商憚於承運，懸岸至四十餘處。 請將河南二十四州縣仿淮南例改票鹽，先課後引。 直隸二十四州縣限半年招商招販，無商販即責成州縣領運，或由鹽政遴員官運。 支銷浮費及官役陋規，永遠裁汰。 每年應完帑利，灘及通綱額引，與正課一律徵收。 其協濟補欠充公等項加價名目，概行革除。 並於各引鹽加斤免課，每斤准其減價敵私。 」詔依議行。
Past forty, Zhichang took third place in the first class in Daoguang's twelfth year, became a compiler, and topped the Hanlin dispersal examination. Soon ranked third in the palace examination, he became reader and Shandong educational commissioner. In the nineteenth year he again placed third in the palace exam, rose to junior mentor then mentor, examined Jiangxi, and became Zhejiang educational commissioner. After mourning his mother he returned as grand secretariat bachelor. In the twenty-third year he became vice minister of rites and Anhui educational commissioner, then moved to Personnel and the grain transport post. In the twenty-eighth year he and Prince Ding Zaiquan reorganized Changlu salt and audited Tianjin granaries, reporting: "Changlu salt had piled up; merchants feared to move it, and stock sat at more than forty depots. He proposed that Henan's twenty-four prefectures and counties adopt Huainan's ticket-salt system, taxing before issuing transport permits. Zhili's twenty-four jurisdictions were given six months to attract merchants; failing that, prefectures must move salt themselves or the commissioner appoint official carriers. Extravagant charges and clerks' customary fees were to be abolished permanently. Annual treasury profits, beach levies, and general quota permits were to be collected with the main salt tax. Surcharges for mutual aid, deficit makeup, and public funds were abolished outright. Each transport permit would carry extra weight tax-free, with a per-catty price cut to undercut smugglers. " The throne approved the plan.
20
二十九年，偕大學士耆英赴浙江閱兵，並清查倉庫，籌辦鹽務。 途經東河、南河，查詢節浮費、裁冗員事宜，奏減東河正款二十萬兩，裁泉河通判、歸河通判，南河每年用款以三百萬兩為率，減省五六十萬兩，並揚運通判於江防廳，改為江運同知，裁丹陽縣丞、靈壁主簿、呂梁洪巡檢，從之。 耆英病留清江浦，芝昌獨赴浙江，疏陳變通鹽務章程七事：杭、嘉、紹三所引鹽，分別加斤，止令完交正課； 松所引鹽，酌裁科則； 虛懸口岸，選商接辦，並籌款收鹽； 緝私責成官商，由運司審覈； 緝獲私鹽，分別充賞，及補課作正配銷； 禁革引地陋規； 覈裁巡驗浮費。 尋查州縣倉庫，統計實虧之數，多至三百九十餘萬，請將虧數最多之員，革職，勒追； 不足，則由原任上司按成分賠，或由本省各官分成提補； 其有欠在胥吏者，尤嚴補追，毋任倖免：並從之。
In the twenty-ninth year he joined Grand Secretary Qiying in Zhejiang to review troops, audit granaries, and reform salt. En route on the Eastern and Southern canals he studied waste and redundant posts, cutting the Eastern Canal budget by two hundred thousand taels, abolishing two assistant prefects, capping the Southern Canal at three million taels with five or six hundred thousand saved, moving one assistant prefect to river defense as sub-prefect, and abolishing three minor posts—all approved. Qiying fell ill at Qingjiangpu; Zhichang went on alone to Zhejiang and proposed seven salt reforms: for Hang, Jia, and Shao salt, add weight separately and collect only the main tax; for Song depot salt, adjust the tax schedule; for idle transit ports, assign merchants, raise funds, and buy up stock; Anti-smuggling duty was assigned to officials and merchants under the transport commissioner's review; seized contraband salt would be rewarded by grade, and recovered tax could count toward regular quota sales; customary fees along transit routes were abolished; patrol and inspection surcharges were audited and reduced. He soon audited prefectural and county granaries, finding deficits as high as 3.9 million taels, and asked that the worst offenders be dismissed and forced to repay; shortfalls were to be recovered from former superiors by graded shares or from provincial officials in joint contribution; clerk debts were to be pursued especially hard with no leniency—all approved.
21
授山西巡撫，未一月，召署吏部侍郎，命在軍機大臣上行走。 尋授戶部侍郎。 三十年，擢左都御史。 咸豐元年，出為閩浙總督。 艇匪在浙洋劫掠山東兵船，被剿遁閩洋，遣水師截擊，賊眾畏罪投誠，分別安置。 二年，兼署福州將軍。 疏請停罷捐納舉人、附生之例； 又奏禁鹽商代銷官運，以杜取巧：並從之。 尋以疾乞休。
Made Shanxi governor, within a month he was recalled as acting vice minister of personnel and assigned to the Grand Council. He soon became vice minister of revenue. In the thirtieth year he was made Left Censor-in-Chief. In Xianfeng's first year he became governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. Boat pirates in Zhejiang waters raided a Shandong military ship; driven into Fujian waters, he sent the navy to intercept; the gang, fearing punishment, surrendered and was resettled in groups. In the second year he also acted as Fuzhou commander. He asked to end the sale of juren and supplemental licentiate degrees by contribution; and to forbid salt merchants from substituting for government transport to block loopholes—both approved. He soon retired for illness.
22
芝昌以文字受宣宗特達之知，嘗曰：「汝為文，行所無事，譬之於射，五矢無一失。 」及查辦長蘆、兩浙鹽務稱旨，遂驟進膺樞務。 甫數月，宣宗崩，文宗猶欲用之，畀以外任。 未一歲，謝職歸。 久之，卒於家，未予卹典。 光緒初，署閩浙總督文煜奏陳政績，追諡文敏。 子念詒，道光三十年進士，官編修。 孫邦楨，同治十二年進士，官至福建布政使。
Zhichang's prose won Emperor Xuanzong's special regard; the emperor once told him, "Your writing does only what is needed, like archery—five arrows, five hits." His handling of Changlu and the two Zhe salt systems pleased the throne, and he was suddenly thrust into Grand Council work. Within months Xuanzong died; Wenzong still meant to use him and sent him to a provincial post. Within a year he resigned and went home. He died at home years later without receiving standard mourning honors. In early Guangxu, Acting Governor-General Wen Yu cited his record and he received the posthumous name Wenmin. His son Nianyi, a Daoguang thirtieth-year jinshi, was a Hanlin compiler. Grandson Bangzhen, a Tongzhi twelfth-year jinshi, became Fujian financial commissioner.
23
論曰：承平，士大夫平進而致列卿，或以恪謹稱，或以文學顯，固不能盡有所建樹； 或餘澤延世，子孫復繼簪纓，若白鎔、那清安、升寅諸人是也。 季芝昌晚遭殊遇，已值宣宗倦勤之年，暫任兼圻，奉身而退，其見幾知止者耶？
Commentary: In peaceful times officials rose by routine to high office, some noted for diligence, some for letters—not all could leave a major mark; yet some families' favor lasted generations, sons and grandsons again wearing court insignia, as with Bai Rong, Na Qing'an, and Sheng Yin. Ji Zhichang's late favor came as Xuanzong wearied of rule; he briefly held a frontier post, then withdrew intact—did he not read the moment and know when to stop?
