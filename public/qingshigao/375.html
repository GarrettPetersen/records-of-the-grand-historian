1
白鎔，字小山，順天通州人。 嘉慶四年進士，選庶吉士，授編修，典福建鄉試。 十八年，大考二等，擢贊善。 督安徽學政，詔密詢地方利弊，疏言：「安徽錢糧，惟鳳陽、泗州遭湖、河之害，積逋較巨。 遇豐稔之年，循例帶徵舊額。 在小民以一年而輸數年之賦，雖樂歲不免拮据； 而官吏懼譴，規避多方，積重難返。 與其存徵之名，緻小民日受追呼，國計依然無補，何如核徵之實，使官吏從容措理，舊額尚可漸清。 請嗣後二屬錢糧，每年祗帶徵一年，儻遇歉收，再行遞緩，民力漸紓，催科者自顧考成，行之必有效。 」詔允行。
Bai Rong, whose courtesy name was Xiaoshan, came from Tongzhou in Shuntian. He passed the jinshi examination in Jiaqing 4, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, was appointed a compiler, and served as chief examiner for the Fujian provincial examination. In the eighteenth year he placed in the second class in the palace examination and was promoted to tutor in the Eastern Palace. While serving as Anhui educational commissioner, he received a secret order to report on local conditions; he memorialized: "Among Anhui's grain and land taxes, only Fengyang and Sizhou, having suffered from lakes and rivers, carry comparatively large accumulated arrears. In years of good harvest, by established practice they also collect the old quotas in the same levy. Common people must pay several years' taxes in one year, and even in a prosperous year they still feel the pinch; while officials, fearing reprimand, resort to every evasion, so the burden piles up and becomes hard to undo. Rather than keep up the pretense of full collection while the people face daily dunning with no gain to the treasury, it would be better to collect only what can truly be paid, let officials work at a manageable pace, and gradually clear the old quotas. He asked that thereafter for these two jurisdictions only one year's arrears be added to each annual levy; if harvest failed, further deferral could follow. Popular strength would ease, collectors would mind their performance reviews, and the policy would surely work. " The emperor approved and ordered it carried out.
2
青陽有孝子曰徐守仁，幼孤，事母孝。 母沒，廬墓三年，鎔造廬贈賻，題請旌表。 訪求明臣左光斗遺裔，取列縣庠。 按試所至，集士人講學，以正人心厚風俗為本。 累遷少詹事。 道光元年，督廣東學政。 歷詹事、內閣學士。 七年，擢工部侍郎，調吏部。 九年，偕尚書松筠赴直隸按外委白勤被誣冤斃獄，護理總督屠之申以下降黜有差。 督江蘇學政。 尋偕侍郎寶興勘視南河垛料，舉實以聞。 十一年，擢左都御史，召還京，未至，命查勘江南災賑。 時尚書穆彰阿、硃士彥亦奉命勘湖、河汎溢狀，穆彰阿先回京，鎔遂偕士彥履勘沿河閘壩工程，與總督陶澍定議以工代賑。 赴安徽，週曆太平、寧國、池州、安慶、廬州各郡，先後疏劾飾災侵賑諸弊。 次年，回京，署翰林院掌院學士，典順天鄉試。 十三年，擢工部尚書，典武會試。 故事，武闈雙好不足額，始取單好。 是科雙好不盡取中，坐降大理寺卿。 十九年，乞病歸，卒於家，年七十四。
At Qingyang there lived a filial son named Xu Shouren, who was orphaned young and served his mother with exemplary devotion. When his mother died he dwelt by her grave for three years; Rong built a mourning hut for him, gave funeral gifts, and memorialized for an official commendation. He searched out descendants of the Ming minister Zuo Guangdou and enrolled them in the county school. Wherever he held examinations he gathered scholars to lecture, making the rectification of hearts and the strengthening of custom his foundation. He rose through successive posts to junior tutor of the heir apparent. In Daoguang 1 he was appointed educational commissioner of Guangdong. He served in turn as grand tutor and as an academician of the Grand Secretariat. In the seventh year he was promoted vice minister of works and transferred to the Board of Personnel. In the ninth year he went with Minister Songyun to Zhili to investigate subordinate Bai Qin, who had been falsely accused and died unjustly in prison; Acting Governor-General Tu Zhisen and others were demoted in varying degrees. He served as Jiangsu educational commissioner. Soon afterward he joined Vice Minister Bao Xing in inspecting stockpiled materials on the southern Yellow River and reported what he found. In the eleventh year he was promoted left censor-in-chief and recalled to the capital; before he arrived he was ordered to inspect disaster relief in Jiangnan. Ministers Mujangga and Zhu Shiyan had also been ordered to survey lake and river flooding; Mujangga returned first, and Rong then with Shiyan inspected sluices and dams along the river and, with Governor-General Tao Zhu, adopted work-for-relief. He went to Anhui and toured Taiping, Ningguo, Chizhou, Anqing, Luzhou, and other prefectures, memorializing in turn to impeach the abuses of falsifying disasters and embezzling relief funds. The next year he returned to the capital, served as acting chancellor of the Hanlin Academy, and presided over the Shuntian provincial examination. In the thirteenth year he was promoted minister of works and presided over the military metropolitan examination. By established practice, when candidates with double excellence did not fill the quota, those with single excellence were admitted. In that session not all double-excellence candidates were admitted, and he was demoted to vice president of the Court of Appeals on that account. In the nineteenth year he asked leave on grounds of illness and went home; he died there at seventy-four.
3
鎔事母孝，教子弟嚴。 宣宗嘗嘉其家法之善，以勉朝臣雲。
Rong was filial toward his mother and strict in teaching his sons and nephews. The Daoguang Emperor once praised the excellence of his household discipline and held it up to encourage the court.
4
孫桓，字建侯。 同治二年進士，授吏部主事。 累遷郎中。 掌選，清嚴慎密，吏不能欺，為時所稱。 光緒中，洊擢兵部侍郎，綜覈一如為司官時。 十七年，因病乞休，尋卒。
Sun Huan, whose courtesy name was Jianhou. He passed the jinshi examination in Tongzhi 2 and was appointed a principal clerk in the Board of Personnel. He rose through successive posts to bureau director. In charge of appointments, he was austere and meticulous; clerks could not deceive him, and his contemporaries praised him. During the Guangxu reign he was promoted in turn to vice minister of war and oversaw affairs with the same rigor he had shown as a bureau officer. In the seventeenth year he asked to retire on grounds of illness and soon died.
5
史致儼，字容莊，江蘇江都人。 家酷貧。 甫冠，為諸生，學政謝墉器其才，給膏火，居尊經閣讀書。 薦預召試，未與選。 嘉慶四年，成進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 督四川學政。 累遷右庶子。 二十一年，督河南學政。 自滑縣匪平，猶有伏莽，密詔偵察。 疏陳彰、衛二郡民間習邪教猶眾，州縣編查保甲，有名無實，撰敦俗篇，刊布以化導之。 商丘廩生陳忠錦以不濫保被毆，知府、經歷受賕，反加斥責，忿而自經。 疏劾，譴罪有差。
Shi Zhiyan, whose courtesy name was Rongzhuang, came from Jiangdu in Jiangsu. His family was desperately poor. Shortly after coming of age he became a licentiate; Educational Commissioner Xie Yong valued his talent, supplied him with stipends, and lodged him in the Zunjing Pavilion to study. He was recommended for the special palace examination but was not chosen. In Jiaqing 4 he passed the jinshi, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, and was appointed a compiler. He served as Sichuan educational commissioner. He rose through successive posts to right vice director of the Hanlin Academy. In the twenty-first year he was appointed Henan educational commissioner. Although the bandits in Huaxian had been suppressed, hidden troublemakers remained, and he received a secret order to investigate. He memorialized that in Zhang and Wei prefectures heterodox sects were still widely practiced, that prefectures and counties kept baojia registers in name only, and he wrote an "Exhortation on Customs" and published it for moral guidance. Chen Zhongjin, a licentiate of Shangqiu, was beaten for refusing to sign false guarantees; the prefect and grain intendant had taken bribes and rebuked him instead, and he hanged himself in anger. He memorialized to impeach them, and punishments were assigned in varying degrees.
6
道光元年，典湖北鄉試。 累遷內閣學士。 三年，擢刑部侍郎，調禮部。 五年，督福建學政。 奏分台灣舉人中額，增所屬四縣學額。 漳、泉諸郡習械鬥，諸生與者，屏不與試，悍風稍息。 九年，偕侍郎鍾昌赴山西鞫獄，平定知州故出人罪，鞫實，論兇犯如律，褫知州恆傑職。 調刑部，歷左都御史，遷禮部尚書。 兩典順天鄉試。 調工部，又調刑部。 勤於其職，竟日坐堂上閱案牘，揅析論難，視司員如弟子。 任刑部凡四年，京察，以刑名詳慎，被議敘。 十八年，乞解職。 尋卒，年七十九，贈太子太保，祀鄉賢及名宦祠。
In Daoguang 1 he presided over the Hubei provincial examination. He rose through successive posts to academician of the Grand Secretariat. In the third year he was promoted vice minister of punishments and transferred to the Board of Rites. In the fifth year he was appointed Fujian educational commissioner. He memorialized to set aside a separate juren quota for Taiwan and to increase school quotas for its four subordinate counties. Zhangzhou, Quanzhou, and other prefectures were given to armed brawling; students who took part were barred from examinations, and the violent custom gradually subsided. In the ninth year he went with Vice Minister Zhong Chang to Shanxi to try cases; the magistrate of Pingding had improperly released persons guilty of homicide; the facts were established, the principal offenders were sentenced according to law, and Magistrate Heng Jie was dismissed from office. He was transferred to the Board of Punishments, served as left censor-in-chief, and was promoted minister of rites. He twice presided over the Shuntian provincial examination. He was transferred to the Board of Works and then again to the Board of Punishments. Diligent in office, he sat in court all day reviewing case papers, analyzing and debating points in detail, and treating bureau officers like students. He served at the Board of Punishments for four years in all; in the capital evaluation he was commended for careful attention to penal matters. In the eighteenth year he asked to leave office. He soon died at seventy-nine, was posthumously granted Grand Tutor of the Crown Prince, and was enshrined in the halls of local worthies and eminent officials.
7
那清安，字竹汀，葉赫納喇氏，滿洲正白旗人。 嘉慶十年進士，授戶部主事，遷翰林院侍講。 累遷內閣學士。 二十四年，授禮部侍郎，歷刑部、工部。 道光元年，命赴直隸讞獄，擢左都御史，管光祿寺事，兼都統。 尋遷兵部尚書，調刑部。 四年，出為熱河都統，偕左都御史松筠等赴土默特讞獄，事竣，疏言：「蒙古惡習，常有移屍訛詐，為害滋甚。 蒙古律例，凡軍流徒犯，罪止折枷，情重法輕。 請嗣後遇有假捏人命詐財者，所擬軍流徒罪即行實發，不准折枷，以懲刁惡。 」下所司議行。 六年，召授左都御史。 逾年，復任熱河都統，召對，詢知其母年老，命仍還左都御史任。 十一年，复授兵部尚書，典順天鄉試及會試。 十四年，以疾乞解職，允之。 尋卒，贈太子太保，諡恭勤。
Na Qing'an, whose courtesy name was Zhuting, of the Yehe Nara clan, was a Manchu of the Plain White Banner. He passed the jinshi examination in Jiaqing 10, was appointed a principal clerk in the Board of Revenue, and was promoted to Hanlin reader. He rose through successive posts to academician of the Grand Secretariat. In the twenty-fourth year he was appointed vice minister of rites and served in turn at the Boards of Punishments and Works. In Daoguang 1 he was ordered to Zhili to try cases, was promoted left censor-in-chief, supervised the Court of Imperial Entertainments, and concurrently served as grand marshal. Soon afterward he was promoted minister of war and transferred to the Board of Punishments. In the fourth year he was posted as Rehe military governor; with Left Censor-in-Chief Songyun and others he went to Tumet to try cases, and when the work was done he memorialized: "Among Mongol bad customs, moving corpses to extort and fabricate claims is common and the harm grows ever worse. Under Mongol law, offenders sentenced to military exile, penal servitude, or exile need only pay a commuted cangue fine; where the crime is grave the penalty is light. He asked that hereafter when anyone fabricated a death to extort money, sentences of military exile, penal servitude, or exile be carried out in full without commutation to cangue, to punish the crafty and wicked. " The proposal was referred to the relevant office and approved for implementation. In the sixth year he was recalled and appointed left censor-in-chief. The following year he was again appointed Rehe military governor; when summoned for audience the emperor learned his mother was elderly and ordered him back to his post as left censor-in-chief. In the eleventh year he was again appointed minister of war and presided over the Shuntian provincial and metropolitan examinations. In the fourteenth year he asked to leave office on grounds of illness, and his request was granted. He soon died, was posthumously granted Grand Tutor of the Crown Prince, and was given the posthumous name Gongqin (Respectful and Diligent).
8
那清安工為館體應制詩，時皆誦習。 因與穆彰阿同榜成進士，晚乃受宣宗知，迭秉文衡。 既卒，會兵部以慶廉送武會試有殘疾，為監試御史所劾。 先是那清安為監射大臣，曾以慶廉殘疾扣除，上追念其持正，予其子全慶加二級。 全慶，光緒初官大學士，自有傳。
Na Qing'an excelled at regulated verse in the court style for imperial occasions, and everyone recited his poems. Having passed the jinshi in the same year as Mujangga, he won the Daoguang Emperor's favor only late in life and repeatedly held literary selection posts. After his death, when the Board of War forwarded Qing Lian to the military metropolitan examination despite his disability, the supervising censor impeached the matter. Earlier, when Na Qing'an supervised the archery examination, he had disqualified Qing Lian for disability; the emperor recalled his uprightness and granted his son Quanqing an additional two ranks. Quanqing served as grand secretary in early Guangxu and has his own biography.
9
昇寅，字賓旭，馬佳氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人。 拔貢，考授禮部七品小京官。 舉嘉慶五年鄉試。 累遷員外郎，改御史。 疏言學校為人才根本，請嚴課程，務實用，戒奢靡； 又疏陳防禁考試八旗生懷挾冒替諸弊：從之。 改右庶子，累遷副都御史。 二十一年，授盛京禮部侍郎，署盛京將軍。 調刑部，召為工部侍郎，又調刑部。 道光六年，出為熱河都統。 以蒙古各旗招內地遊民開採煤礦，往往生事械鬥，疏請諭禁，從之。 八年，命赴甘肅偕總督鄂山按寧夏將軍慶山、副都統噶普唐阿互劾事，罷慶山，即以昇寅代之。 歷成都、綏遠城將軍。 命鞫鄂爾多斯京控獄，奏言：「蒙古京控日繁，請自後各部落封禁地樹立界牌，以杜私墾； 蒙古阿勒巴圖禁止餽贈，以息爭端； 扎薩克王、貝勒等毋用內地書吏，以免教唆； 各旗協理台吉，會同盟長選舉，以昭慎重； 盟長會盟需用烏拉，應明定限制，以免浮索：庶積弊清而獄訟息。 」
Sheng Yin, whose courtesy name was Binxu, of the Majia clan, was a Manchu of the Bordered Yellow Banner. Selected as a tribute scholar, he passed the examination and was appointed a seventh-rank clerk in the Board of Rites. He passed the provincial examination in Jiaqing 5. He rose through successive posts to bureau director and was then transferred to the censorate. He memorialized that schools are the foundation of talent and asked for strict curricula, practical training, and restraint of luxury; He also memorialized on preventing banner students from cheating in examinations by hidden slips, impersonation, and similar abuses: the court approved. He was transferred to right vice director of the Hanlin Academy and rose through successive posts to vice president of the censorate. In the twenty-first year he was appointed vice minister of rites at Mukden and acting Mukden military governor. He was transferred to the Board of Punishments, recalled as vice minister of works, and then transferred again to the Board of Punishments. In Daoguang 6 he was posted as Rehe military governor. Because Mongol banners recruited inland migrants to open coal mines and brawls often followed, he memorialized asking for a prohibition, and the court approved. In the eighth year he was ordered to Gansu with Governor-General Eshan to investigate the mutual impeachment of Ningxia General Qingshan and Vice President Gaputang'a; Qingshan was dismissed and Sheng Yin replaced him. He served in turn as military governor of Chengdu and of Suiyuancheng. Ordered to try cases from Ordos appealed to the capital, he memorialized: "Appeals from Mongols to the capital grow daily; hereafter boundary markers should be set up on each tribe's sealed lands to stop illicit cultivation; Mongol subjects should be forbidden to accept gifts, to quell disputes; jasak princes, beile, and others should not employ inland clerks, to avoid instigation; assistant taiji for each banner should be chosen jointly with league chiefs, to show due care; League chiefs require wula (tribute goods) for league assemblies; clear limits should be set to prevent excessive demands, so that accumulated abuses may be cleared and litigation cease. "
10
十一年，召授左都御史，兼都統。 十二年，署工部尚書。 京畿旱，疏請發米，設十廠煮粥以濟災民，從之。 十三年，偕侍郎鄂順安按西安將軍徐錕貪縱，得實，議褫職。 十四年，命閱兵山東、河南，就鞫桐柏知縣寧飛濱故出人罪，治如律。 命赴廣東、湖南按事，授禮部尚書，未至，卒於途。 優詔賜卹，稱其老成清介，贈太子太保，諡勤直。
In the eleventh year he was summoned and appointed Censor-in-Chief of the Left, concurrently serving as colonel general. In the twelfth year he served as acting Minister of Works. When the capital region suffered drought, he memorialized requesting the release of grain and the establishment of ten soup kitchens to feed disaster victims; the request was approved. In the thirteenth year, together with Vice President E Shun'an, he investigated Xi'an General Xu Kun for corruption and lax discipline; the charges were substantiated and dismissal from office was recommended. In the fourteenth year he was ordered to inspect troops in Shandong and Henan; en route he tried Tongbai Magistrate Ning Feibin on the spot for unlawfully releasing a prisoner and punished him according to law. He was ordered to Guangdong and Hunan to investigate affairs and was appointed Minister of Rites, but before he arrived he died on the journey. An imperial edict granted generous mourning honors, praising him as mature, upright, and incorruptible; he was posthumously granted the title Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent and given the posthumous name Qinzhí (Diligent and Upright).
11
子寶琳，直隸保定知府，濬定州洿澤，有治績； 寶珣，同治中，官兵部侍郎、山海關副都統。 孫紹祺，咸豐六年進士，由編修官至理籓院尚書； 紹諴，光緒中，山西布政使，從治鄭州河工，終駐藏大臣； 紹英，宣統初，度支部侍郎，內務府大臣。
His son Baolin served as prefect of Baoding in Zhili, dredged the foul marshes of Dingzhou, and won a reputation for good governance; Baoxun, during the Tongzhi reign, served as Vice President of the Board of War and as Vice President at Shanhaiguan. His grandson Shaoqi, a jinshi of Xianfeng 6, rose from compiler to Minister of the Court of Colonial Affairs; Shaoxuan, during the Guangxu reign, served as financial commissioner of Shanxi, assisted in managing the Zhengzhou river works, and ultimately became Resident Commissioner in Tibet; Shaoying, in the early Xuantong reign, served as Vice President of the Board of Revenue and as Minister of the Imperial Household.
12
李宗昉，字芝齡，江蘇山陽人。 嘉慶七年一甲二名進士，授編修，典陝甘鄉試。 大考二等，擢贊善。 督貴州學政，累遷侍讀學士，督浙江學政。 歷詹事、內閣學士。 道光元年，授禮部侍郎。 次年，典會試，又典江西鄉試，留學政。 值大水，歲饑，與巡撫籌賑務，多所全活。 調戶部侍郎。 初，宗昉督學貴州時，巡撫議丈全省田為增賦計，民情惶駭，會檄學官徵集圖書，得御史包承祚奏疏，乾隆初，學政鄒一桂請丈田，而承祚奏駁之，極言黔中山多平地少，民每虛佔不毛之地，胥吏高下其手，以丈高下不可準之田，賦未必增，民受其害。 部議停止，宗昉持以示巡撫曰：「此事學臣嘗奏之，被駁。 今必解其所駁乃可。 」巡撫亦悟，事得寢。 至是，官戶部，署巡撫麟慶因復奏上其事，部援故事詳覆之，乃定議不行。 歷工部、吏部侍郎，兼管國子監、順天府尹事。 自七年至十年，典順天鄉試二，會試一，浙江鄉試一，得士稱盛。 擢左都御史、禮部尚書。 二十四年，以疾乞休。 二十六年，卒，依例賜卹。
Li Zongfang, whose courtesy name was Zhiling, came from Shanyang in Jiangsu. In Jiaqing 7 he placed second in the first class of jinshi, was appointed a compiler, and presided over the Shaanxi-Gansu provincial examination. In the palace examination he placed in the second class and was promoted to tutor in the Eastern Palace. He served as educational commissioner of Guizhou, was promoted to Reader in the Hanlin Academy, and then served as educational commissioner of Zhejiang. He served in turn as Grand Tutor and as a Grand Secretary of the Grand Secretariat. In Daoguang 1 he was appointed Vice President of the Board of Rites. The following year he presided over the metropolitan examination and also over the Jiangxi provincial examination, and remained in office as educational commissioner. When severe floods and famine struck, he worked with the governor to organize relief and saved many lives. He was transferred to Vice President of the Board of Revenue. Earlier, while Zongfang was serving as educational commissioner of Guizhou, the governor proposed surveying all fields in the province to increase tax revenue, alarming the populace. Educational officials were ordered to collect books and documents, and Zongfang found a memorial by Censor Bao Chengzuo: in the early Qianlong reign, Educational Commissioner Zou Yigui had requested a land survey, but Chengzuo had memorialized against it, arguing forcefully that Guizhou has more mountains than flat land, that people often falsely claim barren ground, that clerks manipulate the figures, and that surveying fields of uneven quality would not necessarily increase revenue but would harm the people. The ministry deliberated and ordered the plan stopped. Zongfang took the document to the governor and said, "The educational commissioner once memorialized on this matter and was rejected. Before proceeding now, the grounds of that rejection must be addressed. " The governor understood as well, and the matter was dropped. By then, while serving in the Board of Revenue, Acting Governor Lin Qing again memorialized on the matter; the ministry cited precedent in a detailed reply and finally decided against it. He served as Vice President of the Boards of Works and Personnel, concurrently overseeing the Imperial Academy and the governorship of Shuntian Prefecture. From the seventh to the tenth year he presided twice over the Shuntian provincial examination, once over the metropolitan examination, and once over the Zhejiang provincial examination, and the scholars he selected were widely acclaimed. He was promoted to Censor-in-Chief of the Left and Minister of Rites. In the twenty-fourth year he requested retirement on grounds of illness. He died in the twenty-sixth year and was granted mourning honors according to precedent.
13
姚元之，字伯昂，安徽桐城人。 嘉慶十年進士，選庶吉士，授編修，典陝甘鄉試。 入直南書房。 給事中花傑劾戴衢亨、英和援引，詔元之文字本佳，斥傑詆訐，尋亦罷元之入直。 十七年，大考一等，擢侍講。 復以武英殿刊刻聖訓有誤，仍降編修。 十九年，督河南學政，疏禁坊刻類典等書以杜剿襲； 又密陳河南與安徽、湖北交界地多捻匪，陳州、汝寧鹽運迥殊，土匪把持：並嘉納之。 累遷內閣學士。
Yao Yuanzhi, whose courtesy name was Bo'ang, came from Tongcheng in Anhui. He passed the jinshi examination in Jiaqing 10, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, was appointed a compiler, and presided over the Shaanxi-Gansu provincial examination. He entered service in the Southern Library. Supervising Secretary Hua Jie impeached Dai Quheng and Ying He for favoritism in appointments; an edict declared that Yuanzhi's writing was originally excellent and rebuked Jie for slander, but soon Yuanzhi was also removed from service in the Southern Library. In the seventeenth year he placed in the first class in the palace examination and was promoted to Reader. Again, because errors were found in the Hall of Military Glory edition of the Sacred Instructions, he was demoted back to compiler. In the nineteenth year, as educational commissioner of Henan, he memorialized banning commercially printed imitation-classics and similar books to stop plagiarism; he also secretly reported that the borderlands between Henan and Anhui and Hubei harbored many Nian bandits, that salt transport in Chenzhou and Runing differed greatly, and that local bandits controlled the trade; all these proposals were commended and adopted. He was promoted to Grand Secretary of the Grand Secretariat.
14
道光十三年，授工部侍郎。 疏陳台灣營務積弊，窩娼聚賭，械鬥殺人，操演僱人替代，詔下閩督嚴察整頓。 調戶部，又調刑部。 迭典順天、江西鄉試。 督浙江學政，未滿，十八年，擢左都御史，召回京。 尋以南昌知府張寅為江西巡撫裕泰劾罷，元之為寅疏辯，臚陳政績，請查辦，詔斥冒昧，降二級調用。 二十一年，海防方亟，疏陳廣東形勢，豫籌戰守，下靖逆將軍奕山等採行。 授內閣學士。 二十三年，京察，以年衰休致。
In Daoguang 13 he was appointed Vice President of the Board of Works. He memorialized on accumulated abuses in Taiwan garrison affairs—harboring prostitutes and gambling dens, armed fights and killings, and hiring substitutes for drill—and an edict ordered the Fujian governor-general to investigate strictly and rectify the situation. He was transferred to the Board of Revenue and then to the Board of Punishments. He presided in turn over the Shuntian and Jiangxi provincial examinations. While serving as educational commissioner of Zhejiang, before his term was complete, in the eighteenth year he was promoted to Censor-in-Chief of the Left and recalled to the capital. Soon afterward, when Nanchang Prefect Zhang Yin was impeached and dismissed by Jiangxi Governor Yutai, Yuanzhi memorialized in Zhang's defense, setting forth his achievements in detail and requesting an investigation; an edict rebuked his presumption and demoted him two ranks with reassignment. In the twenty-first year, when coastal defense was urgently needed, he memorialized on Guangdong's strategic situation and proposed plans for offense and defense; the memorial was sent down to General for Pacifying Rebellion Yishan and others for implementation. He was appointed Grand Secretary of the Grand Secretariat. In the twenty-third year, in the capital personnel evaluation, he retired on grounds of advanced age.
15
元之學於族祖鼐，文章爾雅，書畫並工。 習於掌故，館閣推為祭酒。 愛士好事，穆彰阿素重之。 後以論洋務不合，乃被黜。 咸豐二年，卒。
Yuanzhi studied under his clan elder Nai; his prose was refined and elegant, and he was accomplished in both calligraphy and painting. Well versed in institutional precedents, he was regarded by the Hanlin and academy offices as their leading authority. He loved scholars and delighted in good works, and Muzhanga had long held him in high regard. Later, because his views on foreign affairs did not accord with prevailing opinion, he was dismissed. He died in Xianfeng 2.
16
何汝霖，字雨人，江蘇江寧人。 拔貢，考授工部七品小京官。 中式道光五年舉人，充軍機章京，累遷郎中。 歷內閣侍讀學士、大理寺少卿。 偕侍郎恩桂按事浙江，查勘南河料垛。 命在軍機大臣上行走，歷宗人府丞、副都御史。 二十二年，授兵部侍郎，調戶部。 偕大學士敬徵勘東河工程。 二十五年，擢兵部尚書。 值太后七旬萬壽，汝霖母丁年九十，五世同堂，賜御書扁額，尋以母憂歸。 江蘇大水，命在籍襄治賑務。 先是，總督陶澍於江寧立豐備倉以備荒，縣令虧挪穀價，大吏許以他款抵。 汝霖曰：「倉穀以備兇。 今荒像如此，汝霖不敢欺朝廷，當各為奏上。 」乃以給賑用。 服闋，命以一品頂戴署禮部侍郎，尋署戶部尚書，仍直軍機處，授禮部尚書。
He Rulin, whose courtesy name was Yuren, came from Jiangning in Jiangsu. As a selected tribute student, he passed the examination and was appointed a seventh-rank clerk in the Board of Works. He passed the provincial examination in Daoguang 5, served as a Grand Council clerk, and was promoted to department director. He served as Reader in the Hanlin Academy and as Vice Minister of the Court of Judicial Review. Together with Vice President En Gui, he investigated affairs in Zhejiang and inspected stored materials for the Southern River works. He was ordered to serve on the staff of the Grand Council ministers and served in turn as Vice Director of the Imperial Clan Court and as Vice Censor-in-Chief. In the twenty-second year he was appointed Vice President of the Board of War and then transferred to the Board of Revenue. Together with Grand Secretary Jingzheng, he inspected the Eastern River engineering works. In the twenty-fifth year he was promoted to Minister of War. On the occasion of the Empress Dowager's seventieth birthday, Rulin's mother, née Ding, was ninety sui and five generations lived under one roof; an imperial calligraphy plaque was bestowed, but soon afterward he returned home to mourn his mother. When Jiangsu suffered severe floods, he was ordered, while at home, to assist in managing relief work. Earlier, Governor-General Tao Shu had established the Fengbei Granary in Jiangning as a famine reserve, but the county magistrate embezzled and diverted grain funds, and senior officials allowed the shortfall to be offset with other funds. Rulin said, "Granary grain is kept against famine. Now that famine conditions are so severe, I dare not deceive the court; each of us should report separately to the throne. " The grain was then used for relief. When his mourning period ended, he was ordered to serve with first-rank hat ornament as acting Vice President of the Board of Rites, soon as acting Minister of the Board of Revenue, while continuing to serve at the Grand Council; he was then appointed Minister of Rites.
17
汝霖久襄樞務，資勞己深，尚書陳孚恩由章京躋大臣，駸用事，厭汝霖居其前。 汝霖年逾七十，一日在直，觸火爐幾僕。 孚恩笑曰：「人當避爐，爐豈能避人？ 」汝霖知其諷己，咸豐二年，以足疾乞罷直，許之。 未幾，卒，諡恪慎，祀鄉賢。 子兆瀛，浙江鹽運使。
Rulin had long assisted in core state affairs and his seniority and service were considerable; Minister Chen Fu'en, who had risen from Grand Council clerk to grand minister, gradually came to wield power and resented Rulin's precedence over him. Rulin was over seventy; one day while on duty at the council, he bumped into the brazier and nearly fell. Fu'en smiled and said, "A man should avoid the brazier; can a brazier avoid a man? " Rulin knew he was being mocked; in Xianfeng 2, on grounds of a foot ailment he requested to leave council duty, and permission was granted. Before long he died and was given the posthumous name Ke Shen (Respectful and Prudent); he was enshrined among local worthies. His son Zhaoying served as Salt Transport Commissioner of Zhejiang.
18
季芝昌，字仙九，江蘇江陰人。 父麟，直隸鉅鹿知縣，居官慈惠。 嘉慶十八年，捕邪教，焚其籍，免株連數千人。 坐捕匪不力，戍伊犁。
Ji Zhichang, whose courtesy name was Xianjiu, came from Jiangyin in Jiangsu. His father Lin served as magistrate of Julu in Zhili and was benevolent and gracious in office. In Jiaqing 18 he suppressed a heterodox sect, burned its registers, and spared several thousand people from guilt by association. Convicted of insufficient effort in suppressing bandits, he was banished to Ili.
19
芝昌年逾四十，成道光十二年一甲三名進士，授編修，散館第一。 未幾，大考第三，擢侍讀，督山東學政。 十九年，大考复第三，擢少詹事，晉詹事，典江西鄉試，督浙江學政。 母憂歸，服闋，擢內閣學士。 二十三年，授禮部侍郎，督安徽學政，調吏部，又調倉場。 二十八年，命偕定郡王載銓籌辦長蘆鹽務，清查天津倉庫，疏陳：「蘆鹽積累，各商憚於承運，懸岸至四十餘處。 請將河南二十四州縣仿淮南例改票鹽，先課後引。 直隸二十四州縣限半年招商招販，無商販即責成州縣領運，或由鹽政遴員官運。 支銷浮費及官役陋規，永遠裁汰。 每年應完帑利，灘及通綱額引，與正課一律徵收。 其協濟補欠充公等項加價名目，概行革除。 並於各引鹽加斤免課，每斤准其減價敵私。 」詔依議行。
Zhichang was over forty when he placed third in the first class of jinshi in Daoguang 12, was appointed a compiler, and ranked first in the Hanlin graduation evaluation. Before long he placed third in the palace examination, was promoted to Reader, and served as educational commissioner of Shandong. In the nineteenth year he again placed third in the palace examination, was promoted to Junior Grand Tutor and then Grand Tutor, presided over the Jiangxi provincial examination, and served as educational commissioner of Zhejiang. He returned home to mourn his mother, and when the mourning period ended he was promoted to Grand Secretary of the Grand Secretariat. In the twenty-third year he was appointed Vice President of the Board of Rites and educational commissioner of Anhui, then transferred to the Board of Personnel and afterward to the grain depot. In the twenty-eighth year he was ordered, together with Prince of Ding Zaiquan, to manage Changlu salt affairs and inspect Tianjin warehouses; he memorialized: "Changlu salt has accumulated, merchants are reluctant to undertake transport, and suspended distribution points number more than forty. He requested that twenty-four prefectures and counties in Henan follow the Huainan precedent and switch to the ticket-salt system, collecting tax first and issuing certificates afterward. In twenty-four prefectures and counties of Zhili, merchants and dealers should be recruited within half a year; if none come forward, prefectures and counties should be held responsible to lead transport, or the salt commissioner should select officials for official transport. Inflated expenses and illicit customary fees paid to officials and clerks should be permanently abolished. Each year the treasury profits due, along with beach and general quota salt certificates, should be collected uniformly with the regular tax. Surcharge items such as mutual aid, arrears makeup, and confiscation should all be abolished. Weight should also be added to each salt certificate exempt from tax, allowing each catty to be sold at a reduced price to compete with smuggled salt. " An edict ordered implementation as proposed.
20
二十九年，偕大學士耆英赴浙江閱兵，並清查倉庫，籌辦鹽務。 途經東河、南河，查詢節浮費、裁冗員事宜，奏減東河正款二十萬兩，裁泉河通判、歸河通判，南河每年用款以三百萬兩為率，減省五六十萬兩，並揚運通判於江防廳，改為江運同知，裁丹陽縣丞、靈壁主簿、呂梁洪巡檢，從之。 耆英病留清江浦，芝昌獨赴浙江，疏陳變通鹽務章程七事：杭、嘉、紹三所引鹽，分別加斤，止令完交正課； 松所引鹽，酌裁科則； 虛懸口岸，選商接辦，並籌款收鹽； 緝私責成官商，由運司審覈； 緝獲私鹽，分別充賞，及補課作正配銷； 禁革引地陋規； 覈裁巡驗浮費。 尋查州縣倉庫，統計實虧之數，多至三百九十餘萬，請將虧數最多之員，革職，勒追； 不足，則由原任上司按成分賠，或由本省各官分成提補； 其有欠在胥吏者，尤嚴補追，毋任倖免：並從之。
In the twenty-ninth year, together with Grand Secretary Qishan, he went to Zhejiang to inspect troops, inspect warehouses, and manage salt affairs. En route through the Eastern and Southern Rivers, he inquired into reducing inflated expenses and cutting redundant posts; he memorialized reducing the Eastern River regular appropriation by two hundred thousand taels and abolishing the Quanhe and Guihe assistant prefects; he proposed setting the Southern River annual expenditure at three million taels as the standard, with a reduction of five or six hundred thousand taels; he also proposed transferring the Yangyun assistant prefect to the River Defense Office as River Transport Sub-Prefect, and abolishing the Danyang Assistant Magistrate, Lingbi Registrar, and Lülianghong Inspector; all were approved. When Qishan fell ill and remained at Qingjiangpu, Zhichang went alone to Zhejiang and memorialized seven revised salt regulations: for salt certificates of the Hangzhou, Jiaxing, and Shaoxing offices, weight should be added separately and only the regular tax required; for salt certificates of the Songjiang office, tax schedules should be appropriately reduced; for nominally suspended port stations, merchants should be selected to take over operations and funds should be raised to purchase salt; responsibility for suppressing smuggling should rest on officials and merchants, subject to review by the salt transport commissioner; seized smuggled salt should be rewarded by grade, and back taxes should count toward regular quota sales; illicit customary fees in salt distribution districts should be abolished; inflated expenses for patrol and inspection should be verified and reduced. He soon inspected prefectural and county granaries and found actual deficits totaling more than 3,900,000 taels in many cases; he asked that officials with the largest shortfalls be dismissed and forced to make restitution; if that proved insufficient, former superiors should pay proportional shares, or officials throughout the province should contribute proportionally to make up the loss; where deficits lay with clerks, recovery should be especially strict and no one allowed to escape liability: all approved.
21
授山西巡撫，未一月，召署吏部侍郎，命在軍機大臣上行走。 尋授戶部侍郎。 三十年，擢左都御史。 咸豐元年，出為閩浙總督。 艇匪在浙洋劫掠山東兵船，被剿遁閩洋，遣水師截擊，賊眾畏罪投誠，分別安置。 二年，兼署福州將軍。 疏請停罷捐納舉人、附生之例； 又奏禁鹽商代銷官運，以杜取巧：並從之。 尋以疾乞休。
He was appointed governor of Shanxi; within a month he was recalled to serve as acting vice minister of personnel and ordered to serve on the Grand Council. Soon afterward he was appointed vice minister of revenue. In the thirtieth year he was promoted left censor-in-chief. In Xianfeng 1 he was posted as governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. Boat bandits in Zhejiang waters plundered Shandong military vessels; pursued, they fled into Fujian waters; he sent naval forces to intercept them, and the bandits, fearing punishment, surrendered and were settled in separate groups. In the second year he also served as acting military governor of Fuzhou. He memorialized asking to abolish the practice of purchasing juren and supplemental licentiate status by contribution; He also memorialized prohibiting salt merchants from selling government-transported salt on officials' behalf, to stop evasion: all approved. Soon afterward he asked to retire on grounds of illness.
22
芝昌以文字受宣宗特達之知，嘗曰：「汝為文，行所無事，譬之於射，五矢無一失。 」及查辦長蘆、兩浙鹽務稱旨，遂驟進膺樞務。 甫數月，宣宗崩，文宗猶欲用之，畀以外任。 未一歲，謝職歸。 久之，卒於家，未予卹典。 光緒初，署閩浙總督文煜奏陳政績，追諡文敏。 子念詒，道光三十年進士，官編修。 孫邦楨，同治十二年進士，官至福建布政使。
Zhichang won the Daoguang Emperor's special favor through his writing; the emperor once said, "When you write, you proceed as if nothing were amiss—like archery, five arrows without a miss. " When his investigation of Changlu and the two Zhejiang salt administrations pleased the emperor, he was suddenly advanced to Grand Council service. Only a few months later the Daoguang Emperor died; the Xianfeng Emperor still wished to employ him and gave him an outside appointment. Within less than a year he resigned and went home. Long afterward he died at home without receiving the usual condolence gifts by regulation. In early Guangxu, Acting Governor-General Wen Yu memorialized his achievements, and he was posthumously given the name Wenmin. His son Nianyi passed the jinshi examination in Daoguang 30 and served as a compiler. His grandson Bangzhen passed the jinshi examination in Tongzhi 12 and rose to Fujian provincial treasurer.
23
論曰：承平，士大夫平進而致列卿，或以恪謹稱，或以文學顯，固不能盡有所建樹； 或餘澤延世，子孫復繼簪纓，若白鎔、那清安、升寅諸人是也。 季芝昌晚遭殊遇，已值宣宗倦勤之年，暫任兼圻，奉身而退，其見幾知止者耶？
The commentator says: In peaceful times, scholar-officials who rose by routine to ministerial rank might be praised for dutiful caution or distinguished for literary talent, yet could not all achieve notable accomplishments; or their lingering favor might extend through generations, with sons and grandsons again inheriting official rank, as with Bai Rong, Na Qing'an, Sheng Yin, and the like. Ji Zhichang late in life met with extraordinary favor, already when the Daoguang Emperor had grown weary of governing; he briefly held concurrent frontier posts, then withdrew while preserving himself—was this not seeing the moment and knowing when to stop?
