慕天顏阿山噶禮
Mu Tianyan, Ashan, and Gali
3
慕天顏，字拱極，甘肅靜寧人。 順治十二年進士，授浙江錢塘知縣。 遷廣西南寧同知，再遷福建興化知府。 康熙九年，擢湖廣上荊南道。 總督劉兆麒疏言天顏習邊海諸事，請調福建興泉道。 尋擢江蘇布政使。 十二年，喪母。 總督麻勒吉、巡撫瑪祜疏言：「天顏廉明勤敏，清積年逋賦，釐剔挪移，事未竟，請令在官守制。 」十三年，入覲，疏言：「江南田地錢糧有隱佔、詭寄諸弊，臣飭州縣通計田額，均分里甲； 又因科則不等，立徵收截票之法，每戶實徵錢糧分十限，於開徵日給限票，依限完納截票。 逾限未截，按數追比，吏不能欺民。 」下部，著為令。
Mu Tianyan, whose courtesy name was Gongji, came from Jingning in Gansu. He took his jinshi degree in the twelfth year of Shunzhi and was appointed magistrate of Qiantang in Zhejiang. He was moved to subprefect of Nanning in Guangxi and later to prefect of Xinghua in Fujian. In the ninth year of Kangxi he was raised to intendant of Upper Jingnan Circuit in Huguang. Governor-General Liu Zhaoqi reported that Tianyan was experienced in coastal matters and asked that he be transferred to the Xing-Quan intendant post in Fujian. He was soon promoted to provincial treasurer of Jiangsu. In the twelfth year his mother died. Governor-General Melehei and Governor Mahu wrote: "Tianyan is upright, diligent, and quick to act. He has been clearing years of tax arrears and rooting out diversions, but the task is unfinished. We ask that he be allowed to remain in office while observing mourning." In the thirteenth year he came to court and reported: "Jiangnan land taxes suffer from hidden occupation and false registration. I have ordered the prefectures and counties to tally the full acreage and spread the burden evenly across li and jia;" Because tax rates varied, he also set up a stub-receipt system: each household's actual levy was split into ten installments; on the opening day of collection they received installment tickets and, when each installment was paid on time, they turned in the stub. If the deadline passed without surrender of the stub, the full amount was pursued; in this way clerks could not cheat the people. The memorial went to the ministry and was made a standing regulation.
4
十五年，擢江寧巡撫。 疏進錢糧交代冊，上嘉其清晰，命布政使交代當以此為式。 尋以節減驛站錢糧，加兵部侍郎。 師徵吳三桂，大將軍貝勒尚善請造船濟師，下天顏督造送岳州。 敘勞，加太子少保、兵部尚書，仍兼右副都御史。 時諸道兵應徵發，舳艫蔽江，夫役牽挽，動以千萬計。 天顏疏言：「縴夫募諸民間，夫給銀一錢。 民爭逃匿，計裡均派，先期拘集，飢寒踣頓。 及兵既到，計船給夫，兵與船戶橫索財物，鞭撻死傷。 臣擬軍赴前敵，仍給縴夫； 其凱旋還京，並各省調遣歸標官兵，每船應夫若干，以其直給船戶，令僱水手。 」上從之，命下直省，著為令。
In the fifteenth year he was made governor of Jiangning. He presented a land-tax handover ledger that the emperor praised for its clarity and ordered provincial treasurers to use as the standard for handovers. He was soon given the additional rank of vice minister of War for cutting courier-station levies. During the campaign against Wu Sangui, Grand Marshal Prince Shangshan asked for boats to transport troops, and Tianyan was ordered to build them and send them to Yuezhou. For merit he was made Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent and minister of War, while still serving as vice censor-in-chief on the right. Troops from every circuit were being called up; the river was thick with boats, and the haulers who towed them ran into the millions. Tianyan wrote: "Tow-line laborers are drafted from the populace, each man paid one cash." People fled and hid; quotas were split by li, men were rounded up ahead of time, and they fell from hunger and cold. Once the troops came, labor was counted by boat; soldiers and boatmen seized goods by force and flogged men to death or injury. I propose that when troops march to the front, tow-line labor should still be supplied; for troops returning in triumph to the capital and banner soldiers sent back from the provinces, each boat should have a set quota of men, the fee paid to the boat owner, who should hire sailors. The emperor agreed and ordered it sent to every province as a regulation.
5
江南水道交錯，天顏為布政使時，請於巡撫瑪祜，濬吳淞江、劉河淤道。 十九年，江南困霪雨，疏言：「附近吳淞江、劉河諸州縣水道通暢，旋溢旋消。 宜興、常熟、武進、江陰、金壇諸縣水無出路，或要口湮塞，致積雨成壑。 常熟白茆港為長洲、崑山、無錫諸水出海要道，武進孟瀆河為丹陽、宜興、金壇諸水歸江要道，請動帑疏濬。 」上從之。 於是濬白茆港四十三里達海，濬孟瀆河四十八里達江，皆建閘以時啟閉，費帑九萬有奇。 又嘗疏請減浮糧，除版荒、坍沒公佔田地，部議坍沒許豁除，版荒令覆勘。 二十年，疏請募民墾版荒，六年後起科。
Jiangnan's waterways interlace; while Tianyan was provincial treasurer he asked Governor Mahu to dredge the silted Wusong River and Liu River channels. In the nineteenth year Jiangnan suffered endless rain. He wrote: "Around the Wusong and Liu rivers the local waterways are open and flood and drain in turn." In Yixing, Changshu, Wujin, Jiangyin, and Jintan water had nowhere to go, or vital mouths were choked, so the rains became deep basins. Changshu's Baimao Harbor was the main sea outlet for Changzhou, Kunshan, and Wuxi; Wujin's Mengdu River was the main Yangtze outlet for Danyang, Yixing, and Jintan. He asked for treasury funds to dredge them. The emperor agreed. Baimao Harbor was dredged forty-three li to the sea and the Mengdu River forty-eight li to the Yangtze; sluice gates were built to open and close on schedule, costing more than ninety thousand taels. He also once asked to cut inflated grain quotas and strike wasteland, collapsed fields, and publicly occupied land from the rolls; the ministry allowed collapsed fields to be removed but ordered wasteland to be re-surveyed. In the twentieth year he asked to recruit people to open wasteland, with tax to begin after six years.
6
揚州知府高德貴虧帑數万，既劾罷，旋卒； 天顏疏銷草豆價，戶部覈減七千有奇，天顏檄追德貴家屬。 京口防禦高騰龍，德貴族也，與參領馬崇駿以天顏奏銷浮冒訐於將軍楊鳳翔，鳳翔格不行。 總督阿席熙劾崇駿、騰龍婪取，上遣郎中圖爾宸、鍾有德會天顏勘治。 崇駿、騰龍叩閽訟天顏奏銷浮冒，惡其訐告構罪狀，唆總督劾奏。 上命圖爾宸、鍾有德具獄，崇駿、騰龍婪取罪至死，天顏以草豆價戶部覈減諉罪德貴，當左遷。 得旨，如議。
Yangzhou prefect Gao Degui was short tens of thousands in the treasury; after impeachment and dismissal he soon died; Tianyan memorialized to cancel fodder and bean charges; the Board of Revenue cut more than seven thousand, and Tianyan ordered Gao Degui's family pursued. Jingkou defense commandant Gao Tenglong, Degui's kinsman, and adjutant Ma Chongjun accused Tianyan before General Yang Fengxiang of padding write-offs; Fengxiang blocked it. Governor-General A Xixi impeached Chongjun and Tenglong for greed; the emperor sent directors Tulichen and Zhong Youde to investigate with Tianyan. Chongjun and Tenglong appealed at the gate, accusing Tianyan of padded write-offs, resenting his charges, and urging the governor-general to impeach him. The emperor ordered a full report; Chongjun and Tenglong were sentenced to death for extortion, while Tianyan, for blaming Degui over fodder and bean prices the Board had cut, was to be demoted. The edict followed the recommendation.
7
天顏將去官，疏列成勞，且言：「夙夜冰兢精白，不意遭誣訐，蒙鑑宥不加嚴譴。 」上以天顏未聞有廉名，乃自言「冰兢精白」，非是，命嚴飭。 二十三年，起湖北巡撫，复諭之曰：「爾前為巡撫，未能潔己率屬。 今宜痛改前非，廉謹自持，以副任使。 」旋移貴州。
About to leave office, Tianyan listed his achievements and said: "I have been scrupulously pure day and night, yet was slandered and, through your grace, spared severe punishment." The emperor, who had never heard Tianyan praised for integrity, said calling himself "scrupulously pure" was improper and ordered a stern reprimand. In the twenty-third year he was made governor of Hubei again and told: "As governor before, you failed to keep yourself pure and lead your staff." Now reform your past faults, hold yourself with integrity and care, and meet the charge given you." He was soon moved to Guizhou.
8
二十六年，授漕運總督，疏言：「京口至瓜洲，漕船往來，風濤最險。 請仿民間渡生船，官設十船，導引護防。 」部議非例，不允。 上曰：「朕南巡見京口、瓜洲往來人眾，備船過渡，有益於民。 其如所請行。 」天顏疏陳江南、江西累年未完漕項銀米請恩貰，上命盡免康熙十七年以前積逋。 江南揚州、淮安所屬運河東瀕海諸州縣地卑下，謂之下河，頻歲被水。 上先用湯斌議，遣侍郎孫在豐疏濬下河。 河道總督靳輔議起翟家壩迄高家堰築重堤，束堤堰溢出之水北出清口，謂疏濬無益。 天顏仍主疏濬，並修築高家堰，與不協。 上遣尚書佛倫、熊一瀟，給事中達奇納、趙吉士會勘，佛倫等主用輔議，天顏、在豐議與輔異。 天顏密疏力爭，輔疏劾天顏與在豐有連，欲在豐建功，故堅阻上游築堤。 下部議，奪天顏職，而輔亦為御史郭琇、陸祖修，給事中劉楷交章劾罷。 初，輔請於仲家莊建閘，引駱馬湖水，別鑿中河，俾漕船避黃河之險，天顏亦議為無益。 上命學士開音布、侍衛馬武往視，還奏天顏令漕船毋入中河，上以責天顏，逮下獄。 天顏反覆申辨，副都御史噶爾圖舉天顏訴辭先後互異，坐奏事上書不以實論罪，上追錄天顏造舟濟師，特寬之。 三十五年，卒。
In the twenty-sixth year he became director-general of grain transport and wrote: "From Jingkou to Guazhou grain boats travel where wind and waves are most dangerous." He asked to follow civilian rescue ferries and have the government set up ten boats to guide and protect passage. The ministry said it was not precedent and refused. The emperor said: "On my southern tour I saw crowds crossing between Jingkou and Guazhou; boats for ferrying help the people." Let it be done as he asked. Tianyan reported that Jiangnan and Jiangxi had for years left grain transport quotas in silver and grain unpaid and asked grace; the emperor remitted all arrears before Kangxi 17. Along the Grand Canal east of the sea in Yangzhou and Huai'an the land lies low—the "Lower River"—and flooded year after year. The emperor first followed Tang Bin and sent Vice Minister Sun Zaifeng to dredge the Lower River. River director Jin Fu proposed heavy dikes from Zhujia Dam to Gaojia Weir to force overflow north to Qingkou, saying dredging was useless. Tianyan still urged dredging and building Gaojia Weir, at odds with Jin Fu. The emperor sent ministers Folun and Xiong Yixiao and supervising secretaries Daqina and Zhao Jishi to inspect; Folun favored Jin Fu, Tianyan and Zaifeng disagreed. Tianyan secretly argued hard; Jin Fu impeached him as allied with Zaifeng, wanting Zaifeng's credit, and blocking upstream dikes. The ministry stripped Tianyan of office; Jin Fu was also impeached out by censors Guo Xiu and Lu Zuxiu and supervising secretary Liu Kai. Earlier Jin Fu asked for a sluice at Zhongjiazhuang to draw Luoma Lake and cut a Middle River so grain boats could avoid the Yellow River; Tianyan also called it useless. The emperor sent Academician Kaiyinbu and guardsman Ma Wu to inspect; they reported Tianyan kept grain boats out of the Middle River. The emperor rebuked him and imprisoned him. Tianyan argued repeatedly; Vice Censor-in-Chief Galtu said his statements contradicted each other and convicted him of untruthful memorials. The emperor recalled his boat-building service and specially spared him. In the thirty-fifth year he died.
9
天顏歷官有惠績，嘗疏請有司虧帑雖逾限，於發遣前清償，仍貰其罪。 獄囚因逸犯株連，待質已三年者，於秋審時開釋； 獄囚無親屬饋食，月給米三斗：皆卹下之政。 在江南，興水利，蠲積逋，而請免縴夫，甦一時之困，江南民尤頌之。 獨劾嘉定知縣陸隴其不協於輿論，左都御史魏象樞疏言：「天顏劾隴其，稱其操守絕一塵，德有餘而才不足。 今之有司，惟操守為難； 既知之矣，何不留以長養百姓？ 請嚴飭諸督撫大破積習，勿使廉吏灰心，貪風日長。 」會詔舉清廉，象樞遂以隴其應，語具隴其傳。
In office Tianyan had benevolent record; he once asked that officials with treasury shortfalls who paid in full before exile still be pardoned though past deadline. Prisoners implicated by escaped felons who had waited three years for trial should be released at autumn assizes; prisoners without kin to feed them should get three dou of rice monthly—all policies to comfort the lowly. In Jiangnan he promoted waterworks, remitted arrears, and abolished tow-line labor, easing immediate hardship; Jiangnan people especially praised him. Only impeaching Jiading magistrate Lu Longqi clashed with opinion. Left censor-in-chief Wei Xiangshu wrote: "Tianyan impeached Longqi yet called his integrity spotless, his virtue ample but talent short." Among officials today, integrity alone is hard; since you know it, why not keep him to nurture the people? I ask all governors to break deep habits and not let upright men lose heart while greed grows." When an edict sought the incorrupt, Xiangshu nominated Longqi; see Longqi's biography.
10
阿山，伊拉哩氏，滿洲鑲藍旗人。 初自吏部筆帖式歷刑部主事、戶部員外部。 康熙十八年，授翰林院侍講，七遷至戶部侍郎。 三十年，命治賑西安、鳳翔二府，明年還京。 上聞流民有至襄陽者，以問阿山。 阿山言正月已得雪，民無流亡。 上曰：「正月雖雪，二、三月雨不時，麥收未可望。 流民至襄陽甚多，汝未之知耳。 」坐奉使不盡心，左授郎中。 三十三年，擢左副都御史。 三十五年，上親征噶爾丹，阿山從。 授阿密達為將軍，逐噶爾丹，阿山為參贊。 師還，授盛京禮部侍郎。 三十六年，授翰林院掌院學士。
Ashan, of the Irara clan, was a Manchu of the Bordered Blue Banner. He began as a clerk in Personnel, rose through vice director in Punishments to department director in Revenue. In Kangxi 18 he was made Hanlin expositor and after seven steps reached vice minister of Revenue. In the thirtieth year he was ordered to manage relief in Xi'an and Fengxiang and returned the next year. The emperor heard displaced people reached Xiangyang and asked Ashan. Ashan said snow had fallen in the first month and people had not fled. The emperor said: "Though it snowed in the first month, untimely rain in the second and third months means the wheat harvest cannot be hoped for." Many displaced people reached Xiangyang—you did not know." For not serving wholeheartedly he was demoted to department director. In the thirty-third year he was made vice censor-in-chief on the left. In the thirty-fifth year the emperor campaigned against Galdan in person and Ashan followed. Amida was made general to pursue Galdan, with Ashan as deputy. When the army returned he was vice minister of Rites at Mukden. In the thirty-sixth year he was chancellor of the Hanlin Academy.
11
三十九年，授江南江西總督。 安徽布政使張四教以憂去官，巡撫高永爵劾四教擅動庫帑，下阿山察奏。 阿山言四教動庫帑為公用，請免議，上復命具實狀以聞。 阿山乃言：「三十八年上南巡，四教發庫帑十一萬供辦，議令各官扣俸抵補。 各官皆自承，臣不敢隱。 」上責阿山徇情沽譽，命漕運總督桑額鞫四教，論如律。 阿山當奪職，上寬之，命留任。
In the thirty-ninth year he was governor-general of Jiangnan and Jiangxi. Anhui treasurer Zhang Sijiao left on mourning; Governor Gao Yongjue impeached unauthorized treasury use and Ashan was ordered to investigate. Ashan said Sijiao's use was public and asked exemption; the emperor again ordered the true facts. Ashan then said: "In the thirty-eighth year on the southern tour Sijiao spent 110,000 from the treasury for arrangements; officials were to repay from salary." Every official admitted it; I dared not conceal it." The emperor rebuked Ashan for favoritism and ordered grain transport director Sang'e to try Sijiao by law. Ashan should have been stripped; the emperor spared him and kept him in post.
12
四十三年，阿山劾江西巡撫張志棟大計不公，志棟及布政使李興祖、按察使劉廷璣、道員韓象起等皆奪職。 阿山又言大計志棟主之，請復興祖等官。 給事中許志進劾阿山恩威自擅，阿山疏辯，且詆志進為淮安漕標營卒子，素行不端，為志棟報復。 志進亦追論阿山庇張四教，並收屬吏賄賂，盜倉穀不問，貪婬惡跡，縱妾父生事。 疏並下部議，部議皆奪職。 上复寬阿山，命留任如故。 四十四年，疏劾江寧知府陳鵬年貪酷，並以妓樓改建講堂，凟聖諭，大不敬。 命會桑額及河道總督張鵬翮集讞，坐鵬年罪至斬，上特命來京，事具鵬年傳。
In the forty-third year Ashan impeached Jiangxi governor Zhang Zhidong for unfair grand evaluation; Zhidong, treasurer Li Xingzu, judge Liu Tingji, intendant Han Xiangqi, and others lost office. Ashan also said Zhidong ran the evaluation and asked Xingzu and others restored. Supervising secretary Xu Zhijin impeached Ashan for arrogating favor and punishment; Ashan defended and slandered Zhijin as son of a Huai'an grain-transport camp soldier, ill-conducted, retaliating for Zhidong. Zhijin also pursued Ashan for shielding Sijiao, taking subordinates' bribes, ignoring granary theft, lewd conduct, and letting his concubine's father make trouble. Both memorials went to the ministry; all were recommended stripped of office. The emperor again spared Ashan and kept him as before. In the forty-fourth year he impeached Jiangning prefect Chen Pengnian for greed and cruelty, and rebuilding a lecture hall on a brothel site, profaning the sage's teaching—grave disrespect. He ordered Sang'e and river director-general Zhang Penghe to try the case jointly; Pengnian was sentenced to death, but the emperor specially ordered him to the capital—the full account is in Pengnian's biography.
13
阿山與桑額、鵬翮議自泗州開河築堤，引淮水至黃家堰，入張福口，會出清口，是為溜淮套，疏請上臨視。 四十五年，授刑部尚書。 四十六年，上南巡，臨視溜淮套，諭曰：「阿山等奏溜淮套別開一河，分洩淮水，繪圖進呈。 朕策騎自清口至曹家廟，見地勢甚高，雖成河，不能直達清口，與所進圖不同。 且所立標竿多在民塚上，朕何忍發此無限枯骨耶？ 」命鵬翮罷其事。 下九卿議，阿山及桑額、鵬翮皆奪職； 上以阿山主其議，命但坐阿山，遂奪職。 五十一年，江蘇布政使宜思恭以虧帑坐譴，因列訴總督噶禮等頻向需索，阿山亦受節餽，下部議，上以阿山老，寬之。 五十二年，萬壽，復原品。 逾年，卒。
Ashan, Sang'e, and Penghe proposed a canal and dikes from Sizhou to draw Huai water to Huangjia Weir, through Zhangfukou to Qingkou—the Liuhuaitao scheme—and asked the emperor to inspect. In the forty-fifth year he was made minister of Punishments. In the forty-sixth year on the southern tour the emperor inspected Liuhuaitao and said: "Ashan and others said Liuhuaitao would open a separate river to divert the Huai and submitted drawings." I rode from Qingkou to Caojiamiao and saw the ground was very high; even with a river it could not reach Qingkou directly, unlike the plan submitted. Moreover the survey stakes stood mostly on commoners' graves—how could I bear to disturb these endless dry bones?" He ordered Penghe to drop the project. The Nine Ministers deliberated; Ashan, Sang'e, and Penghe were all to lose office; the emperor held Ashan chiefly responsible and stripped only him of office. In the fifty-first year Jiangsu treasurer Yi Sigong was punished for treasury shortfall and complained that Governor-General Gali and others had repeatedly demanded supplies; Ashan had also taken festival gifts. The ministry deliberated; the emperor spared him for age. In the fifty-second year, for the imperial birthday, his original rank was restored. The next year he died.
14
阿山故精察，上嘗問大學士李光地：「阿山在官何若？ 」光地奏：「臣嘗與同僚，廉幹，果於任事。 其失民心，獨劾陳鵬年一事耳。 」上頷之。
Ashan was sharp by nature; the emperor once asked Grand Secretary Li Guangdi: "How is Ashan in office?" Guangdi replied: "I once served with him; he is incorruptible, capable, and decisive in affairs." He lost popular support only over impeaching Chen Pengnian." The emperor nodded.
15
噶禮，棟鄂氏，滿洲正紅旗人，何和哩四世孫也。 自廕生授吏部主事，再遷郎中。 康熙三十五年，上親征噶爾丹，次克魯倫河。 噶禮從左都御史於成龍督運中路兵糧，首達行在，召對，當上意。 尋擢盛京戶部理事官。 歲餘三遷，授內閣學士。 三十八年，授山西巡撫。 噶禮當官勤敏能治事，然貪甚，縱吏虐民。 撫山西數年，山西民不能堪。 會潞安知府缺員，噶禮疏薦霍州知州李紹祖，紹祖使酒自刎，噶禮匿不以奏。 上聞之，下九卿議罪，擬奪噶禮職，上寬之。 御史劉若鼐疏論噶禮貪，得贓無慮數十萬，太原知府趙鳳詔為其腹心，專用酷刑以濟貪壑事。 下噶禮復奏，得辨釋。
Gali, of the Donggo clan, was a Plain Red Banner Manchu and fourth-generation descendant of Heheli. By yin privilege he was made a department director in Personnel and twice promoted to bureau director. In Kangxi 35 the emperor campaigned against Galdan in person and halted at the Kerulen River. Gali followed Left Censor-in-Chief Yu Chenglong supervising middle-route grain transport, reached camp first, was summoned, and pleased the emperor. Soon after he was director of the Mukden Board of Revenue. Within a year he rose three ranks and became a Grand Secretariat academician. In the thirty-eighth year he was governor of Shanxi. Gali was diligent and capable in office, yet very greedy and let clerks abuse the people. After several years in Shanxi the people could not endure him. When Lu'an prefect fell vacant, Gali recommended Huozhou magistrate Li Shaozu; Shaozu cut his throat while drunk, and Gali concealed it without reporting. When the emperor heard, the Nine Ministers recommended stripping Gali of office; the emperor spared him. Censor Liu Ruonai said Gali was greedy with bribes of several hundred thousand; Taiyuan prefect Zhao Fengzhao was his man and used cruel torture to fill his greed. Gali was ordered to reply and was exonerated.
16
平遙民郭明奇等以噶禮庇貪婪知縣王綬，走京師詣巡城御史袁橋列訴。 橋疏聞，並言「噶禮通省錢糧加火耗十之二，分補大同、臨汾等縣虧帑，餘並以入己，得四十餘萬； 指修解州祠宇，用巡撫印簿勒捐； 令家伶赴平陽、汾州、潞安三府迫富民饋遺； 又以訟得臨汾、介休富民亢時鼎、梁湄金； 縱汾州同知馬遴； 庇洪洞知縣杜連登，皆貪吏； 隱平定雹災」，凡七事。 上命噶禮復奏，山西學政鄒士聰代太原士民疏留噶禮。 御史蔡珍疏劾士璁「職在衡文，乃與巡撫朋比。 且袁橋疏得旨二日後，太原士民即具呈，顯為誣偽。 噶禮與士璁同城，委為不知，是昏憒也； 知而不阻，是幸恩也。 請並敕部議處」。 尋噶禮復奏，以明奇等屢坐事走京師誣告，並辨橋、珍所言皆無據。 下九卿察奏，明奇等下刑部治罪，橋、珍坐誣譴罷。
Pingyao commoners Guo Mingqi and others, because Gali shielded greedy magistrate Wang Shou, went to the capital and appealed to patrol censor Yuan Qiao. Qiao reported and said: "Gali added two-tenths melting fees to provincial land tax, covered Datong and Linfen shortfalls, kept the rest—over four hundred thousand; citing Jiezhou temple repair he used the governor's seal register to extort donations; he sent household actors to Pingyang, Fenzhou, and Lu'an to force gifts from the wealthy; through lawsuits he got gold from Linfen wealthy man Kang Shiding and Jiexiu's Liang Mei; he indulged Fenzhou vice prefect Ma Lin; he shielded Hongdong magistrate Du Liandeng—all greedy officials; he concealed Pingding's hail disaster"—seven charges in all. The emperor ordered Gali to reply; Shanxi education intendant Zou Shicong submitted for Taiyuan scholars and people to keep Gali. Censor Cai Zhen impeached Shicong: "His duty is examining texts, yet he bands with the governor." Two days after Yuan Qiao's memorial was approved, Taiyuan already had a petition—clearly false." Gali and Shicong share a city; claiming ignorance is stupidity; to know and not stop it is currying favor." I ask both be referred to the ministry for punishment." Soon Gali replied that Mingqi and others had been convicted and fled to slander, and rebutted Qiao and Zhen as groundless. The Nine Ministers reported; Mingqi went to Punishments, Qiao and Zhen were punished for false accusation and dismissed.
17
四十八年，遷戶部侍郎，旋擢江南江西總督。 噶禮至江南，益恣肆，累疏劾江蘇巡撫於準、布政使宜思恭、按察使焦映漢，皆坐罷。 知府陳鵬年初為總督阿山劾罷，上復命守蘇州； 及宜思恭罷，署布政使。 鵬年素伉直，忤噶禮。 噶禮續劾宜思恭虧帑，又論糧道賈樸建關開河皆有所侵蝕，遂及鵬年覈報不實，鵬年復坐罷。 噶禮复密疏鵬年虎丘詩怨望，上不為動。
In the forty-eighth year he became vice minister of Revenue, then soon governor-general of Jiangnan and Jiangxi. In Jiangnan Gali grew more unrestrained, repeatedly impeaching Jiangsu governor Yu Zhun, treasurer Yi Sigong, and judge Jiao Yinghan—all dismissed. Prefect Chen Pengnian had first been impeached out by Governor-General Ashan; the emperor again ordered him to hold Suzhou; when Yi Sigong was dismissed he acted as treasurer. Pengnian was upright by nature and clashed with Gali. Gali continued impeaching Yi Sigong for shortfall and said grain intendant Jia Pu's sluice and canal involved embezzlement, extending to Pengnian's false verification; Pengnian was dismissed again. Gali also secretly said Pengnian's Tiger Hill poem showed resentment; the emperor was unmoved.
18
巡撫張伯行有廉聲，至則又與噶禮忤。 五十年，伯行疏言本科江南鄉試取士不協輿論，正考官副都御史左必蕃亦檢舉同考官知縣王曰俞、方名所薦士有不通文字者。 上命尚書張鵬翮如揚州會噶禮及伯行察審。 鵬翮至，會讞，既得副考官編修趙晉及曰俞、名諸交通狀，伯行欲窮其獄。 噶禮盛怒，刑證人，遂罷讞。 伯行乃劾噶禮，謂輿論盛傳總督與監臨提調交通鬻舉人； 及事發，又傳總督索銀五十萬，許不竟其事：請敕解任就讞。 噶禮亦劾伯行，謂：「方會讞時，臣正鞫囚，伯行謂臣言不當，臣恐爭論失體，緘口結舌。 伯行遂陰謀誣陷，以鬻舉人得銀五十萬汙臣，臣不能與俱生。 」因及伯行專事著書，猜忌糊塗，不能清理案牘。 時方有戴名世之獄，又言：「南山集刻板在蘇州印行，伯行豈得不知？ 進士方苞以作序連坐，伯行夙與友，不肯捕治。 」並羅列伯行不職數事。
Governor Zhang Bohang had a reputation for integrity; on arrival he clashed with Gali again. In the fiftieth year Bohang said the Jiangnan provincial exam selections did not match opinion; chief examiner Vice Censor-in-Chief Zuo Bifan also reported associate examiners Wang Yuen and Fang Ming recommended illiterate candidates. The emperor ordered Minister Zhang Penghe to Yangzhou with Gali and Bohang to investigate. When Penghe arrived they tried the case; once Zhao Jin, Yuen, and Ming's bribery was found, Bohang wanted to pursue it fully. Gali was furious, tortured witnesses, and the trial was dropped. Bohang then impeached Gali, saying rumor held the governor and supervising examiner sold candidates; when it broke, rumor held the governor demanded five hundred thousand taels and promised not to finish: he asked Gali be relieved and tried. Gali also impeached Bohang: "During trial I was examining prisoners; Bohang said my words were improper. Fearing argument would lose decorum, I held my tongue." Bohang then plotted to slander me with selling candidates for five hundred thousand taels—I cannot live with him." He also said Bohang devoted himself to books, was suspicious and muddled, and could not clear case backlogs. During the Dai Mingshi case he also said: "Nanshan Ji blocks were printed in Suzhou—could Bohang not know?" Jinshi Fang Bao was implicated for a preface; Bohang was his friend and would not arrest him." He also listed several counts of Bohang's dereliction.
19
疏入，上並命解任，令鵬翮會漕運總督赫壽察奏。 獄具，晉、曰俞、名及所取士交通得賄，當科場舞弊律論罪； 噶禮劾伯行不能清理案牘事實，餘皆督撫會銜題諮舊事，苞為伯行逮送刑部，南山集刻板在江寧，皆免議； 伯行妄奏噶禮鬻舉人，當奪職。 上切責鵬翮、赫壽瞻徇，又命尚書穆和倫、張廷樞覆讞，仍如鵬翮等議。 上諭曰：「噶禮才有餘，治事敏練，而性喜生事，屢疏劾伯行。 朕以伯行操守為天下第一，手批不淮。 此議是非顛倒！ 」下九卿、詹事、科道察奏，复諭曰：「噶禮操守，朕不能信； 若無張伯行，江南必受其朘削且半矣。 即如陳鵬年稍有聲譽，噶禮欲害之，摘虎丘詩有悖謬語，朕閱其詩，初無他意。 又劾中軍副將李麟騎射皆劣。 麟比來迎駕，朕試以騎射，俱優。 若令噶禮與較，定不能及。 朕於是心疑噶禮矣。 互劾之案，遣大臣往讞，為噶禮所製。 爾等皆能體朕保全廉吏之心，使正人無所疑懼，則海宇蒙昇平之福矣。 九卿等議噶禮與伯行同任封疆，互劾失大臣禮，皆奪職； 上命留伯行任，噶禮如議奪職。
When the memorials entered, the emperor ordered both relieved and Penghe to join grain transport director He Shou in reporting. The case concluded: Jin, Yuen, Ming, and bribe-taking candidates were punished under examination fraud law; Gali's charge that Bohang could not clear backlogs stood; the rest were old joint memorial matters; Bao went to Punishments through Bohang, Nanshan Ji blocks were in Jiangning—all exempted; Bohang's false charge that Gali sold candidates merited stripping of office. The emperor sharply rebuked Penghe and He Shou for favoritism and ordered Ministers Mu Helun and Zhang Tinglu to retry; they still followed Penghe. The emperor said: "Gali has talent to spare and is skilled in affairs, yet loves trouble and repeatedly impeached Bohang." I hold Bohang's integrity the finest under heaven and personally rejected it in the margin." This turns right and wrong upside down!" The Nine Ministers, academicians, and censorate reported; he again said: "I cannot trust Gali's integrity; without Zhang Bohang, Jiangnan would have been drained by half." Even Chen Pengnian, with some reputation—Gali wanted to harm him over a Tiger Hill poem; when I read it there was no other intent." He also impeached deputy commander Li Lin for poor riding and archery." Lin had lately greeted me on tour; I tested riding and archery and both were excellent." If Gali were matched against him, he could not compare." From this I began to doubt Gali. The mutual impeachment case, sent to great ministers for trial, was manipulated by Gali. If you all embody my wish to preserve upright officials and let righteous men be without doubt or fear, the realm will enjoy great peace." The Nine Ministers said Gali and Bohang, sharing frontier duty, had mutually impeached against great-minister propriety—both stripped of office; the emperor kept Bohang in office; Gali was stripped as recommended.
20
五十三年，噶禮母叩閽，言噶禮與弟色勒奇、子幹都置毒食物中謀弒母，噶禮妻以別戶子幹泰為子，縱令糾眾毀屋。 下刑部鞫得實，擬噶禮當極刑，妻論絞，色勒奇、幹都皆斬，幹泰發黑龍江，家產沒入官。 上令噶禮自盡，妻從死，餘如部議。
In the fifty-third year Gali's mother appealed at the gate, saying Gali with brother Seleqi and son Gandu poisoned food to murder her; his wife took Gandu, another household's son, as her own and incited a crowd to destroy the house. Punishments tried and found truth; Gali was sentenced to death, his wife to strangulation, Seleqi and Gandu to decapitation, Gandu to Heilongjiang, property confiscated. The emperor ordered Gali to take his own life; his wife died with him; the rest followed the ministry.
21
論曰：廉吏往往不獲於上，豈長官皆不肖，抑其強項固有所不可堪歟？ 隴其之廉，天顏知之而不能容。 鵬年初扼於阿山，繼挫於噶禮，皆欲中以危法，抑又甚矣。 伯行與噶禮互劾，再讞不得直。 幸賴聖祖仁明，隴其復起，鵬年致大用，伯行亦終獲全。 二三正人詘而得申，人心風氣震盪洋溢，所被至遠。 噶禮不足以語此，蓋天顏、阿山亦弗能喻也。
The commentator says: Upright officials often fail with superiors—are all superiors unworthy, or is their stubborn integrity unbearable? Longqi's integrity Tianyan knew yet could not tolerate. Pengnian was first checked by Ashan, then thwarted by Gali; both sought capital traps—worse still. Bohang and Gali mutually impeached; twice justice was not reached. Fortunately the sage ancestor was benevolent and clear; Longqi rose again, Pengnian was greatly employed, and Bohang was finally preserved. When two or three upright men were humbled yet vindicated, sentiment and moral atmosphere surged far and wide. Gali is not fit to speak of this; Tianyan and Ashan likewise cannot be compared.
