齊蘇勒嵇曾筠子璜高斌從子高晉完顏偉顧琮白鍾山
Qi Sulei, Ji Cengyun, Ji Huang, Gao Bin, Gao Jin, Wan Yanwei, Gu Cong, and Bai Zhongshan
3
齊蘇勒，字篤之，納喇氏，滿洲正白旗人。 自官學選天文生為欽天監博士，遷靈臺郎。 擢內務府主事，授永定河分司。 康熙四十二年，聖祖南巡閱河，齊蘇勒扈蹕。 至淮安，上諭黃河險要處應下挑水埽壩，命往煙墩、九里岡、龍窩修築。 齊蘇勒於回鑾前畢工，上嘉之。 洊擢翰林院侍講、國子監祭酒，仍領永定河分司事。 河決武陟，奉命同副都御史牛鈕監修堤工。 疏言：「自沁河堤頭至滎澤大堤十八里，擇平衍處築遙堤。 使河水趨一道，專力刷深，不致旁溢。 」六十一年，世宗即位，擢山東按察使，兼理運河事。 命先往河南籌辦黃河堤工。 時河南巡撫楊宗義請於馬營口南舊有河形處濬引河。 齊蘇勒同河道總督陳鵬年疏言：「河不兩行，此洩則彼淤。 馬營口堤甫成，若開引河，慮旁洩侵堤。 」事乃寢。
Qi Sulei, whose courtesy name was Duzhi, belonged to the Nara clan and was a Manchu of the Plain White Banner. Chosen from the Imperial Academy as a student of astronomy, he was appointed doctor of the Directorate of Astronomy and later promoted to an officer of the Observatory. He rose to a directorship in the Imperial Household Department and was given charge of the Yongding River as sub-director. In Kangxi 42, when the Kangxi Emperor toured the south to inspect the waterways, Qi Sulei accompanied the imperial procession. At Huai'an the emperor directed that brush revetments and dams be set at the Yellow River's most dangerous stretches, and sent him to build works at Yandun, Jiuligang, and Longwo. Qi Sulei finished the projects before the court headed home, and the emperor commended him. He was promoted in turn to expositor of the Hanlin Academy and chancellor of the Imperial Academy, yet continued to direct the Yongding River office. After a breach at Wuzhi, he was ordered to supervise dike repairs alongside Vice Censor-in-chief Niu Niu. In a memorial he wrote: "From the head of the Qin River embankment to the great dike at Xingze—eighteen li in all—level ground should be chosen and a setback dike built." The current should be confined to one course so its full force could scour the bed deeper and keep the water from spilling sideways. " In the sixty-first year of Kangxi, when the Yongzheng Emperor came to the throne, Qi Sulei was made surveillance commissioner of Shandong and put in charge of the Grand Canal. He was sent ahead to Henan to organize Yellow River dike projects. The Henan governor Yang Zongyi then proposed opening a diversion where an old channel still showed south of Mayingkou. Qi Sulei and Director-general of Rivers Chen Pengnian replied: "The Yellow River cannot be made to run in two channels—release water in one place and the other course silts up." "The Mayingkou embankment was only just finished; cutting a diversion might let water leak alongside and eat away the dike." " The proposal was set aside.
4
雍正元年，授河道總督。 既上官，疏言：「治河之道，若瀕危而後圖之，則一丈之險頓成百丈，千金之費糜至萬金。 惟先時豫防，庶力省而功易就。 」又言：「各堤壩歲久多傾圮，弊在河員廢弛，冒銷帑金。 宜嚴立定章示懲勸。 」並允行。 乃週曆黃河、運河，凡堤形高卑闊狹，水勢淺深緩急，皆計裡測量。 總河私費，舊取給屬官，歲一萬三千餘金，及年節餽遺，行部供張，齊蘇勒裁革殆盡。 舉劾必當其能否，人皆懍懍奉法。
In Yongzheng 1 he was appointed Director-general of Rivers. Once in office he wrote: "River control fails when one waits until danger is imminent: a one-zhang risk becomes a hundred-zhang catastrophe, and a thousand-tael outlay balloons to ten thousand." "Only timely prevention saves labor and makes success easier." He added: "Dikes and dams along the line have crumbled after years of neglect; the fault lies with river officials who slacken duty and embezzle state funds." "Permanent rules with real penalties and rewards should be set." " The court approved everything and ordered it implemented. He toured the Yellow River and Grand Canal, measuring every dike's height, width, and profile and every reach's depth and current in li. Private expenses of the director-general—over 13,000 taels yearly from subordinates, holiday gifts, and tour provisions—Qi Sulei slashed nearly to nothing. Promotions and dismissals matched real ability, and his staff served the regulations in earnest.
5
陽武、祥符、商丘三縣界黃河，北岸有支流三，逼堤繞行五十餘裡； 南岸青佛寺有支流一，逼堤繞行四十餘裡。 齊蘇勒慮刷損大堤，令築壩堵禦，並接築子堤九千二百八十八丈，隔堤七百八十丈。 又以洪澤湖水弱，慮黃水倒灌，奏築清口兩岸大壩，中留水門，束高清水以抵黃流。 及淮水暢下，壩在波濤中，又慮壩為水蝕，遣員弁駐工，湖漲下埽防壩，黃漲則用混江龍、鐵篦子諸器，駕小舟往來疏濬，不使沙停，水患始緩。 詔豫籌山東諸湖蓄洩以利漕運，疏言：「兗州、濟寧境內，如南旺、馬蹋、蜀山、安山、馬場、昭陽、獨山、微山、稀山等湖，皆運道資以蓄洩，昔人謂之'水櫃'。 民乘涸佔種，湖身漸狹。 宜乘水落，除已墾熟田，丈量立界，禁侵越。 謹渟蓄：當運河盛漲，引水使與湖平，即築堰截堵； 如遇水淺，則引之從高下注諸湖。 或宜堤，或宜樹，或宜建閘啟閉，令諸州縣量事程功，則湖水深廣，漕艘無阻矣。 」
On the north bank where Yangwu, Xiangfu, and Shangqiu meet the river, three branch streams hugged the dike for more than fifty li; on the south bank at Qingfosi a single branch wound along the dike for more than forty li. Fearing scour on the main embankment, he had blocking dams built and added subsidiary and partition dikes totaling 9,288 and 780 zhang. With Hongze Lake low and Yellow River water threatening to back up, he proposed great dams at Qingkou with central gates to pin clear lake water against the muddy river. Once the Huai ran freely the dams stood in open water; he posted officers there—laying brush when the lake rose, using scoop dredgers and iron rakes from small boats when the Yellow rose—so silt would not lodge and the crisis eased. Ordered to plan how Shandong's lakes could store and release water for the grain fleet, he listed Nanwang, Mata, Shushan, and other lakes in Yanzhou and Jining—the "water cabinets" of old—on which the canal depended. People planted on exposed lake bed as waters fell, narrowing the basins. After drawdown, already cultivated fields should be surveyed, boundaries marked, and further encroachment barred. "For storage: when the canal floods, channel water until it matches the lakes, then dam the inflow; when water is low, divert it downhill into the lakes. Where dikes, trees, or sluice gates are needed, let each district plan accordingly, and the lakes will hold enough depth for the grain boats." "
6
二年，廣西巡撫李紱入對，上諭及淮、揚運河淤墊年久，水高於城，危險可慮。 紱請於運河西別濬新河，以其土築西堤； 而以舊河身作東堤，東岸當不至潰決。 上命與齊蘇勒商度，齊蘇勒奏言：「淮河上接洪澤，下通江口。 西岸臨白馬、寶應、界首諸湖，水勢汪洋無際。 若別挑新河，築西堤於湖水中，不惟糜費巨金，抑且大工難就。 」上是其言。 是秋颶風作，海潮騰踴丈餘。 黃河入海之路，二水沖激，曆三晝夜，而濱海堤岸屹然。 上嘉其修築堅固，賜孔雀翎，並予拜他喇布勒哈番世職。
In year 2, Guangxi governor Li Fu was received in audience; the emperor noted the Huai-Yang canal's decades of silt, with water standing above the towns—a grave risk. Li Fu proposed a new channel west of the canal, using the excavated earth for a western embankment; and the old bed as the eastern dike, which he said would keep the east bank from failing. The emperor told him to consult Qi Sulei, who replied: "The Huai joins Hongze above and the sea below." "Its west bank faces Baima, Baoying, Jieshou, and other lakes—an endless sheet of water." "Cutting a new channel and building a western dike out in the lakes would cost a fortune and might never be finished." " The emperor accepted his view. That autumn a hurricane drove the tide more than a zhang high. Where the Yellow River meets the sea the two waters hammered each other for three days and nights, yet the coastal dikes held. The emperor praised his solid work, gave him a peacock feather, and granted a hereditary Baitalabulehan rank.
7
三年，副總河嵇曾筠奏於祥符縣回回寨濬引河，事將竣，齊蘇勒奉命偕總督田文鏡察視。 齊蘇勒奏言：「濬引河必上口正對頂沖，而下口有建瓴之勢，乃能吸大溜入新河，藉其水力滌刷寬深。 今所濬引河，與現在水向不甚相對。 當移上三十餘丈，對沖迎溜。 复於對岸建挑水壩，挑溜順行，以對引河之口。 俟水漲時相機開放，庶河流東注，而南岸堤根可保無虞。 」上命內閣學士何國宗等以儀器測量，命齊蘇勒會勘。 齊蘇勒奏：「儀器測度地勢，於河工高下之宜甚有準則。 今洪澤湖滾水壩舊立門檻太高，不便於洩水。 請敕諸臣繞至湖口，用儀器測定，將門檻改低，庶宣防有賴。 」又奏言：「治河物料用葦、柳，而柳尤適宜。 今飭屬於空閒地種柳，沮洳地種葦。 應請凡種柳八千株、葦二頃者，予紀錄一次，著為例。 」均稱旨。 尋又奏言：「供應節禮，並已裁革。 河標四營舊有坐糧，歲千餘金，以之修造墩台，制換衣甲、器械； 鹽商陋規歲二千金，為出操驗兵賞功犒勞之用。 每年往來勘估，伏秋兩汛，出駐工次，車馬舟楫，日用所需，拮据實甚。 河庫道收額解錢糧，向有隨平餘銀五千餘，除道署日用工食，請恩准支銷。 」上允之。 四年，以堵築睢寧硃家口決口，加兵部尚書、太子太傅。 五年，疏言：「黃河鬥岸常患衝激，應改斜坡，俾水隨坡溜，坡上懸密柳抵之。 既久溜入中泓，柳枝霑泥，並成沙灘，則易險為平。 」從其請。 是年，齊蘇勒有疾，上遣醫往視。 尋入覲，命歲支養廉萬金。
In year 3, Vice Director-general Ji Cengyun reported dredging a diversion at Huihuizhai in Xiangfu; near completion Qi Sulei was sent with Governor Tian Wenjing to inspect. Qi Sulei wrote: "A diversion's upper mouth must meet the main current squarely and its lower mouth drop steeply, so the main flow is sucked in and scours the new bed wider and deeper." "The channel now cut does not line up with the live current." "Move the upper opening thirty-odd zhang upstream to take the rush head-on." "Build a deflection dam on the far bank to steer the stream into the new mouth." "Open it when floods allow so the river runs east and the south bank stays secure." " He ordered Academician He Guozong and others to survey with instruments and Qi Sulei to join them. Qi Sulei said instrument leveling gave reliable standards for river works. The old sill on Hongze's rolling dam was too high to drain the lake properly. He asked officials to measure at the lake mouth and lower the sill to improve flood control." He also urged reeds and willows for river works, especially willow. Subordinates were told to plant willows on dry ground and reeds in wetlands. He proposed merit records for eight thousand willows or two qing of reeds, as a standing rule." " All was approved. He soon added: "Holiday gifts and perquisites are already abolished." The four river camps once drew sitting grain of 1,000-odd taels yearly for blockhouses, armor, and arms; a salt merchants' levy of 2,000 taels paid drill troops and rewards. Annual inspections and flood-season camps, with horses, boats, and daily costs, left him genuinely pressed. Surplus scale-fee silver of 5,000-odd taels at the river treasury, beyond the intendant's routine costs, he asked leave to spend." " The emperor agreed. In year 4, for closing the breach at Zhujiakou in Suining, he was made Minister of War and Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent. In year 5 he proposed sloping steep Yellow River banks, with dense willows on the slope to break the current. "Over time the current centers, willows catch silt, sand bars form, and a hazard becomes a flat beach." " The court agreed. That year he fell ill and the emperor sent a doctor. Soon after he was received at court and granted 10,000 taels yearly as integrity-nurture funds.
8
六年，兩江總督範時繹、江蘇巡撫陳時夏濬吳淞江，上命齊蘇勒料理。 築壩陳家渡，松江知府周中鋐、千總陸章乘舟督工下埽，潮回壩陷，溺焉。 齊蘇勒往視察，下為土埂，中有停沙，因督令疏濬，壩工乃竟。 复偕曾筠會勘河南雷家寺支河，是秋事畢。 於是黃河自碭山至海口，運河自邳州至江口，縱橫綿亙三千餘裡，兩岸堤防崇廣若一，河工益完整。
In year 6, Fan Shiyi and Jiangsu governor Chen Shixia dredged the Wusong; the emperor put Qi Sulei in charge. At Chenjiadu, Prefect Zhou Zhongqian and Commander Lu Zhang oversaw brush work from boats; the tide turned, the dam failed, and both drowned. Qi Sulei found an earthen ridge below with trapped sand, ordered dredging, and the dam was finished. He and Cengyun surveyed the Leijiasi branch in Henan; by autumn that too was done. From Dangshan to the sea and Pizhou to the river mouth—3,000 li of embankments now uniform in height and breadth—the river system was more complete than ever.
9
七年春，疾甚，上復遣醫往視。 尋卒，賜銀三千兩為歸櫬資，進世職三等阿達哈哈番，賜祭葬，諡勤恪。 上又以靳輔、齊蘇勒實能為國宣勞，有功民社，命尹繼善等擇地，令有司春秋致祭。
In spring of year 7 his illness worsened and the emperor again sent a doctor. He died soon after; 3,000 taels were granted for his funeral, his hereditary rank raised to third-grade Adaha Khan, with rites and posthumous name Qinke. The emperor said Jin Fu and Qi Sulei had truly served the realm and ordered Yin Jishan to choose a site for seasonal sacrifice.
10
齊蘇勒久任河督，世宗深器之，嘗諭曰：「爾清勤不待言，而獨立不倚，從未聞夤緣結交，尤屬可嘉。 」又曰：「隆科多、年羹堯作威福，攬權勢。 隆科多於朕前謂爾操守難信，年羹堯前歲數詆爾不學無術，朕以此知爾獨立也。 」又曰：「齊蘇勒歷練老成，清慎勤三字均屬無媿。 」八年，京師賢良祠成，復命與靳輔同入祀。
Yongzheng greatly trusted Qi Sulei after his long tenure as river director, telling him: "Your diligence is plain, but that you stand alone and never court factions is especially admirable." He also said: "Longkodo and Nian Gengyao threw their weight about and hoarded power." "Longkodo told me your integrity was suspect; Nian Gengyao called you unlearned year after year—from that I knew you owed neither of them." "He also said Qi Sulei was seasoned and without fault on clarity, caution, and diligence." In year 8, when the Worthies Shrine was finished, he was enshrined with Jin Fu.
11
嵇曾筠，字松友，江南長洲人。 父永仁，諸生，從福建總督范承謨死事； 母楊守節，撫曾筠成立：事分見忠義、列女傳中。
Ji Cengyun, courtesy name Songyou, came from Changzhou in Jiangnan. His father Yongren, a licentiate, died with Governor-general Fan Chengmo of Fujian; his mother Yang remained chaste and raised him—told in the Loyalty and Chaste Women chapters.
12
曾筠，康熙四十五年進士，選庶吉士，授編修。 累遷侍講。 雍正元年，直南書房，兼上書房。 擢左僉都御史，署河南巡撫，即充鄉試考官。 遷兵部侍郎。 河決中牟劉家莊、十里店諸地。 詔往督築，逾數月，工竟。 二年春，奏言：「黃、沁並漲，漫溢銚期營、秦家廠、馬營口諸堤。 循流審視，窮致患之由。 見北岸長沙灘，逼水南趨，至倉頭口，繞廣武山根，逶迤屈曲而下。 官莊峪又有山嘴外伸，河流由西南直注東北，秦家廠諸地頂沖受險。 請於倉頭口對面橫灘開引河，俾水勢由西北而東南，毋令激射東北； 並培釘船幫大壩，更於上下增築減水壩，秦家廠諸地險勢可減。 」又與河督齊蘇勒會奏培兩岸堤，北起滎澤，至山東曹縣； 南亦起滎澤，至江南碭山：都計十二萬三千餘丈。 皆從之。
Cengyun passed the jinshi in Kangxi 45, entered the Hanlin as bachelor, and became a compiler. He rose to expositor. In Yongzheng 1 he served in the Southern and Upper Studies. He became Left Vice Censor-in-chief, acted as Henan governor, and served as provincial examiner. He was made Vice Minister of War. The river broke at Liujiazhuang, Shilidian, and other sites in Zhongmou. Ordered to supervise repairs, he finished in a few months. In spring of year 2 he reported the Yellow and Qin in flood, overtopping dikes at Yaoqiying, Qinjiachang, Mayingkou, and elsewhere. He traced the current and investigated the causes to the end. On the north bank a long sand bar drove the current south to Cangtoukou, where it wound around Guangwu Mountain in serpentine bends. At Guanzhuangyu a spur thrust out; the stream ran southwest to northeast, and Qinjiachang and nearby points took the full brunt. He proposed a diversion opposite Cangtoukou across the bar so the current would run northwest to southeast instead of slamming northeast; and strengthen the nailed-boat-side great dam, add overflow dams up- and downstream, and ease the hazard at Qinjiachang. " With Director-general Qi Sulei he also proposed strengthening both banks from Xingze north to Caoxian in Shandong; southward from Xingze to Dangshan in Jiangnan—123,000-odd zhang in all. All was approved.
13
授河南副總河，駐武陟。 疏言：「鄭州大堤石家橋迤東大溜南趨，應下埽簽椿，复於埽灣建磯嘴壩一。 中牟拉牌寨黃流逼射，應下埽護岸，建磯嘴挑水壩二。 穆家樓堤工坐衝，亦應下埽加鑲。 陽武北岸祥符珠水、牛趙二處堤工，近因中牟迤下，新長淤灘，大溜北趨成衝，應順埽加鑲。 」又言：「小丹河自辛句口至河內清化鎮水口二千餘裡。 昔人建閘開渠，定三日放水濟漕，一日塞口灌田。 日久閘夫賣水阻運，請嚴飭。 仍用官三民一之法，違治其罪。 」又言祥符南岸回回寨對面淤灘直出河心，致河勢南趨逼省城。 請於北岸舊河身濬引河，導水直行。 上諮齊蘇勒用曾筠議。 四年，奏衛河水盛，請於汲、湯陰、內黃、大名諸縣築草壩二十七。 又請培鄭州薛家集諸處埽壩。
He became Vice Director-general of Henan, based at Wuzhi. He wrote that east of Shijiaqiao on Zhengzhou's great dike the main current ran south and needed brush revetments, piles, and a stone spur at the bend. At Lapai in Zhongmou the Yellow River hammered the bank; he called for brush protection and two deflection spurs. The Mujialou works sat in the main rush and needed brush and reinforcement. North of Xiangfu at Zhushui and Niuzhao, new silt below Zhongmou had turned the main current north; brush should follow and reinforce the bank. "The Xiao Dan River runs 2,000-odd li from Xingoukou to Qinghua Town in Hanoi." "Ancients built sluices: three days' water for the grain fleet, one day blocked for fields." "Sluice men now sold water and blocked transport—he asked strict enforcement." "Keep the three-official, one-private rule and punish violators." He also said a silt bar at Huihuizhai on Xiangfu's south bank pushed the current south toward the provincial capital. He asked a north-bank diversion in the old channel to straighten the flow. The emperor consulted Qi Sulei and took Cengyun's plan. In year 4 he reported the Wei in flood and asked for twenty-seven grass dams in Ji, Tangyin, Neihuang, Daming, and elsewhere. He also sought stronger revetments at Xuejiaji in Zhengzhou.
14
五年，命兼管山東黃河堤工。 尋轉吏部侍郎，仍留副總河任。 六年，疏言：「儀封北岸因水勢衝急，雷家寺上首灘崖刷成支河。 請將舊堤加幫，接築土壩，跨斷支河，以防掣溜侵堤。 青龍岡水勢縈紆，將上灣淘作深兜，與下灣相對。 請乘勢開引河，導水東行。 」尋擢兵部尚書，調吏部，仍管副總河事。 奏請培蘭陽耿家寨北堤，下埽簽椿築壩。
In year 5 he was put in charge of Shandong Yellow River dikes as well. Soon he became Vice Minister of Personnel but kept the river post. In year 6 he reported that at Leijiasi on Yifeng's north bank swift water had scoured a branch channel. He proposed strengthening the old dike, linking earthen dams, and cutting off the branch before it undermined the main embankment. At Qinglonggang the current circled; the upper bend was scouring a deep pocket opposite the lower bend. He asked to open a diversion and steer the water east. " Soon he was Minister of War, then Personnel, while still managing river affairs. He proposed strengthening Lanyang's north dike at Gengjiazhai with brush, piles, and dams.
15
七年，授河南山東河道總督，疏請開荊隆口引河。 八年，署江南河道總督，疏言：「山水異漲，匯歸駱馬湖，溢運浮黃，河、湖合一。 請於山盱周橋以南開壩洩水，並啟高、寶諸堰，分水入江海。 高堰山盱石工察有椿腐石欹，順砌卑矮者，應築月壩，加高培實。 其年久傾圮者，全行改築。 興工之際，築壩攔水，留舊石工為障。 俟新基築定，再除舊石，仍留舊底二層，以御風浪。 」又奏：「禹王台壩工為江南下游保障。 沭水源長性猛，壩工受衝。 請於現有竹絡壩二十七丈外，依頂沖形勢，建石工六百餘丈。 接連岡阜，仍築土堤，並濬沭河口門，使循故道直趨入海。 」十年，奏揚州芒稻河閘商工草率，改歸官轄，並增設閘官。 十二月，加太子太保。 十一年四月，授文華殿大學士，兼吏部尚書，仍總督江南河道，予一品封典。 十二月，丁母憂，命在任守制。 曾筠奏懇回籍終制，溫詔許之。 以高斌暫署，仍諭曾筠本籍距淮安不遠，明歲工程，就近協同經理。 十二年四月，同高斌奏增築海口辛家蕩堤閘。 同副總河白鍾山奏修清江龍王閘，濬通鳳陽廠引河。 十三年，諭曾筠葬母事畢赴工。 高宗御極，命總理浙江海塘工程。
In year 7 he became Director-general of Henan and Shandong Rivers and asked to open Jinglongkou diversion. In year 8, acting Jiangnan director-general, he wrote that mountain floods poured into Luoma Lake, lifting the canal and mingling river with lake. He asked dams south of Zhouqiao on Shan(xi) and opening Gaobao and other weirs to send water to river and sea. Stone works on the Gaobao dike at Shanxi had rotten piles and leaning stones; low faces needed crescent dams raised and packed. Long-collapsed sections should be rebuilt entirely. During construction, coffer dams should hold water back, leaving old stone as a screen. Once the new base was firm, old stone could come out but two old layers should remain against wind and waves. He also said the Yuwangtai dam protected lower Jiangnan. The Shu River ran long and hard; the dam took the impact. Beyond twenty-seven zhang of bamboo-cage dam he asked six hundred-odd zhang of stone built to the head-on shape. Link hills with earthen dikes, dredge the Shu mouth, and restore its old course to the sea. In year 10 he reported slipshod merchant work on Yangzhou's Mangdao sluice and asked official control with new sluice officers. In the twelfth month he was made Grand Guardian of the Heir Apparent. In month 4 of year 11 he became Grand Secretary of the Hall of Literary Glory and Minister of Personnel, still river director-general, with first-rank enfeoffment. In month 12 his mother died; he was ordered to mourn in office. Cengyun begged to go home for full mourning and was warmly allowed. Gao Bin acted in his place; the court noted his home was near Huai'an and asked him to help with next year's works. In month 4 of year 12 he and Gao Bin proposed new estuary dikes and sluices at Xinjiadang. With Vice Director-general Bai Zhongshan he repaired Qingjiang's Dragon King sluice and dredged the Fengyang factory channel. In year 13 he was told to resume work after his mother's burial. When Qianlong acceded, he was put in charge of Zhejiang seawalls.
16
乾隆元年，兼浙江巡撫。 尋命改為總督，兼管鹽政。 曾筠條奏鹽政，請改商捕為官役，嚴緝私販，定緝私賞罰。 地方有搶鹽奸徒，官吏用盜案例參處。 又疏請於海寧築尖山壩，建魚鱗石塘七千四百餘丈。 入覲，加太子太傅。 二年，疏請築淳安淳河石磡。 三年，疏請修樂清濱海堤； 又疏請發省城義倉運溫、台諸縣平糶：並從之。 尋召入閣治事，以疾請回籍調治。 上令其子璜歸省，又遣醫診視。 卒，贈少保，賜祭葬，諡文敏，祀浙江賢良祠。 又命視靳輔、齊蘇勒例，一體祠祀。
In Qianlong 1 he also governed Zhejiang. Soon he became governor-general with the salt monopoly. He proposed replacing merchant salt catchers with official runners, tightening anti-smuggling, and fixing seizure rewards and penalties. Local salt gangs were to be punished under bandit law. He also asked for the Jianshan dam at Haining and 7,400-odd zhang of fish-scale stone seawall. At audience he was made Grand Tutor of the Heir Apparent. In year 2 he proposed the Chunhe stone weir at Chun'an. In year 3 he asked repairs on Yueqing's coastal dike; and to release provincial charity grain for relief sale in Wen and Tai—all approved. Soon he entered the Grand Council; ill, he asked to go home to recover. The emperor sent his son Huang home to visit and a physician to treat him. He died, was posthumously Junior Guardian with posthumous name Wenmin, and enshrined in Zhejiang's Worthies Shrine. He was also ordered worshipped like Jin Fu and Qi Sulei.
17
曾筠在官，視國事如家事。 知人善任，恭慎廉明，治河尤著績。 用引河殺險法，前後省庫帑甚鉅。 第三子璜，亦由治河有功，官大學士，繼其武。
In office Cengyun treated state business as his own household affairs. He knew talent, served with respect and integrity, and shone especially in river work. His diversion method to kill dangerous currents saved the treasury enormous sums. His third son Huang, also famed for river work, became Grand Secretary and followed his path.
18
璜，字尚佐。 幼讀禹貢，曰：「禹治水皆自下而上。 蓋下游宣通，水自順流而下。 」長老咸驚異。 雍正七年，賜舉人。 八年，成進士，選庶吉士，年裁二十。 授編修，再遷諭德。 乾隆元年，命直南書房。 三年，丁父憂，服闋，擢庶子。 兩歲四遷左僉都御史。 九年，奏：「督撫閱兵，祗就趨走應對定將弁能否。 請近省命大臣，邊省命將軍、副都統，簡閱行伍。 」是歲令大學士訥親閱河南、山東、江南三省行伍，璜此奏發之也。
Huang, courtesy name Shangzuo. As a boy reading the Yugong he said: "Yu controlled floods from downstream upward." "When the lower course is clear, water runs down by itself." The elders marveled. In Yongzheng 7 he was granted juren status. In year 8 he passed the jinshi, entered the Hanlin as bachelor, aged only twenty. He became compiler and rose twice to preceptor. In Qianlong 1 he entered the Southern Study. In year 3 his father died; after mourning he became sub-expositor. Within two years he rose four ranks to Left Vice Censor-in-chief. In year 9 he wrote that governors reviewing troops judged officers only by drill and answers; he asked ministers in nearby provinces and generals on the frontier to inspect troops properly." " That year Grand Secretary Neqin inspected Henan, Shandong, and Jiangnan—Huang's memorial had started it.
19
璜侍曾筠行河，習工事。 奏河工疏築諸事：請浚毛城鋪壩下引河，並於順河集諸地開河引溜，修築黃河岸，留新黃河、韓家堂諸地舊口，洩盛漲，議行。 授大理寺卿。 累遷戶部侍郎。 十八年十月，黃、淮並漲。 璜疏請濬銅山以下、清口以上河身，並仿明劉天和製平底方船，用鐵耙疏沙，修補高堰石工、歸仁堤閘，酌复江南境內減水閘壩。 尚書舒赫德等被命視河，奏請派熟諳工程大員董理堤防，因令璜偕工部侍郎德爾敏督修。 璜奏：「高堰工程有磚石之殊，年分有新舊之異。 今當修砌石工，堤外築攔水壩，並將舊有磚工盡改石工。 石較磚重，椿木應培增。 舊修石堤用石二進，石後用磚二進，磚與土不相融結，久經風浪，根空基圮，令於磚石後加築灰土三尺，以御沖刷。 」又奏：「串場河為諸水總匯。 請自石閘南更建閘二，並就舊河道疏濬，直達海口。 」十九年，奏：「高堰、高澖、龍門、古溝四處深塘兜灣，請修復草壩。 」皆從之。 是年堤工竟，議敘，轉吏部。 二十年，以母病，乞假歸。
Huang toured rivers with Cengyun and learned the engineering. He proposed dredging below Maochengpu, opening diversions at Shunheji, repairing Yellow River banks, and leaving old mouths at Xin Huanghe and Hanjiatang to spill floods—all approved. He was made president of the Court of Judicial Review. He rose to Vice Minister of Revenue. In month 10 of year 18 the Yellow and Huai both flooded. Huang asked dredging from Tongshan to Qingkou, flat-bottom boats with iron rakes after Liu Tianhe, Gaobao stone repairs, Guiren sluices, and Jiangnan overflow dams restored. Ministers including Shuhede inspected the river and asked skilled officials; Huang was sent with Vice Minister De Ermin to supervise. Huang wrote that Gaobao works mixed brick and stone of different ages. He proposed stone repairs, outer overflow dams, and replacing all brick with stone. Stone outweighed brick; piles must be deepened. Old dikes had two stone courses and two brick behind; brick and earth failed to bond; he added three chi of lime-earth behind brick and stone against scour. He also said the Chuanchang River gathered all waters. He asked two new sluices south of the stone gate and the old channel dredged straight to the sea. In year 19 he asked grass dams restored at Gaobao, Gaoxi, Longmen, and Gugou deep bends. " All was approved. That year the dikes were done; for merit he moved to Personnel. In year 20 his mother fell ill and he begged leave home.
20
二十二年春，上以璜母病癒，授南河副總河，並諭曰：「璜侍父曾筠久任河工，見聞所及，諳練非難。 母雖年近八十，常、淮帶水，侭可輕舟迎養，固無異在家侍奉也。 」四月，上南巡，臨視高堰、清口及徐州諸工。 以伏汛將至，近河諸地歲頻歉，貧民甚多，諭疏築諸工同時並舉，以工代賑。 因璜前奏請於昭關增滾壩、濬支河，南關舊壩改建滾水石壩，即命璜董其事。 璜奏：「運河東堤減水入下河，經劉莊、伍祐、新興諸場，分注鬥龍、新洋二港歸海。 但劉莊大團閘至新興石閘相距較遠，請於伍祐沿窪口、蔡家港各增建石閘，引水出新洋港。 並疏射陽湖港口，使之徑直。 濬串場河以西孔家溝、岡溝河、皮家河支流凡三。 此皆下河歸海之路也。 湖河諸水，歸海紆回，歸江迳直。 多一分入江，即少一分入海。 應挑河築壩，使湖河水勢相平，乃將各壩開放。 則湖水既減，可為容納來水地。 伏秋水盛，洩高郵湖引入運河，出車邏、南關二壩，則歸海水少，下河田廬可無慮矣。 」上諭曰：「璜此奏分別緩急，因勢利導，會全局而熟籌之。 改紆為直，移遠為近，濬淺為深，具有條理。 即令尹繼善、白鍾山等會璜次第興舉。 」十一月，高郵運河東堤新建石壩工成，奏請酌定水則，車邏、南關二壩過水至三尺五寸，開五里中閘； 至五尺，開新建石壩。 又奏：「車邏、南關壩脊高於高郵湖面二尺七寸。 芒稻閘為湖水歸江第一尾閭，請常年啟放，俾江、湖脈絡貫通。 」上深嘉之，從所請，並降旨命勒石閘畔。
In spring of year 22, with his mother recovered, he was made Vice Director-general of Southern Rivers; the emperor said his years with Cengyun had trained him well. "Though she is nearly eighty, the Chang and Huai routes allow light boats—caring for her need not differ from home." " In month 4 the emperor toured south and inspected Gaobao, Qingkou, and Xuzhou works. With the spring flood near and many poor along the river, he was told to run dredging and building together as work-for-relief. As Huang had asked for rolling dams at Zhaoguan, branch dredging, and a new stone rolling dam at the south sluice, he was put in charge. Huang wrote that canal overflow east of the dike passed Liuzhuang and other stations to Doulong and Xinyang harbors and the sea. He asked stone sluices at Wuyou and Caijiagang between Liuzhuang and Xinxing to send water to Xinyang Harbor. Sheyang Lake's mouth should also be dredged straight. West of the Chuanchang River he listed three tributaries—Kongjiagou, Gangou, and Pijia—to dredge. All were lower-river routes to the sea. Lake and river water reached the sea by winding paths but the Yangtze by straighter ones. Every fraction sent to the Yangtze was a fraction less to the sea. Canals and dams should be cut until lake and river stood level, then the dams could open. Lower lake levels would leave room for incoming water. "In autumn floods, Gaoyou Lake could spill into the canal at Cheba and Nanguan, sparing the lower river fields." The emperor praised Huang for ranking priorities, following terrain, and planning the whole system. "Turn winding to straight, distant to near, shallow to deep—well ordered." He told Yin Jishan, Bai Zhongshan, and others to implement it with Huang step by step. In month 11 the new Gaoyou east-bank stone dam was done; he set rules—at three chi five cun on Cheba and Nanguan, open the middle sluice five li away; at five chi, open the new stone dam. He noted Cheba and Nanguan crests stood two chi seven cun above Gaoyou Lake. Mangdao sluice, the lake's first outlet to the Yangtze, should stay open so river and lake stayed linked. The emperor approved, praised him deeply, and ordered an inscription at the sluice.
21
二十三年正月，擢工部尚書。 五月，上下江諸工皆竟。 九月，調禮部。 二十四年四月，請在籍終養。 二十五年，詣京師祝上壽。 歸至清江浦，奏言：「歸江之路，尚有應籌。 請於金灣壩下開引河，並濬董家溝。 又以廖家溝、石羊溝、董家溝三壩改低三尺，使與芒稻閘相準。 」上命交尹繼善等勘議。 二十九年，丁母憂。 三十二年，服闋，署禮部尚書，旋實授。 七月，授河東河道總督，奏：「楊橋大壩為河南第一要工，雖已堵閉，時輒滲漏。 而北岸河灘順直，既不能挑引河分溜，大壩迤東又遍地飛沙，不能建越堤。 請將壩身裹戧培厚，用資完固。 」璜每巡河，不避艱險，身先屬吏。 一夕聞虞城工險，馳往。 天甫曉，雨雹交下，下埽岌岌欲崩，從者失色，勸璜姑退。 璜立堤上叱曰：「埽去我與俱去！ 」雨雹息，堤卒無恙。
In month 1 of year 23 he became Minister of Works. In month 5 upper and lower Jiang works were finished. In month 9 he moved to Rites. In month 4 of year 24 he asked to finish mourning at home. In year 25 he went to court for the emperor's birthday. Back at Qingjiangpu he said the Yangtze return route still needed work. He asked a diversion below Jinwan Dam and dredging of Dongjiagou. He also asked Liaojiagou, Shiyangou, and Dongjiagou lowered three chi to match Mangdao. The emperor sent Yin Jishan and others to survey. In year 29 his mother died. In year 32, mourning done, he acted then held Minister of Rites. In month 7 he became Eastern Rivers director-general and reported Yangqiao dam, though closed, often leaked. The north bank was too straight for diversions; east of the dam flying sand forbade overflow dikes. He asked the dam body wrapped and thickened. On river tours Huang never shunned hardship and led from the front. One night hearing Yucheng was in peril he rode there at once. At dawn rain and hail struck; the revetment nearly failed; aides begged him to step back. Huang stood on the dike and cried: "If the revetment goes, I go with it!" The storm passed and the dike held.
22
三十三年九月，召授工部尚書，罷直南書房。 尋以在河督任未甄別佐雜，左遷左副都御史。 三十六年，遷工部侍郎。 三十八年，擢尚書，調兵部。 四十年，復調工部。 四十四年，調吏部，協辦大學士。 初，璜議挽黃河北流仍歸山東故道，入對嘗及之。 是歲河決青龍岡，大學士阿桂視工。 上以璜議諮阿桂及河督李奉翰，僉謂地北高南低，水性就下； 欲導河北注，揣時度勢，斷不能行。 上復命廷臣集議，仍謂黃河南徙已久，不可輕議改道，寢其事。
In month 9 of year 33 he was summoned as Minister of Works and left the Southern Study. Soon he was demoted to Left Vice Censor-in-chief for failing to classify river staff while director. In year 36 he became Vice Minister of Works. In year 38 he was promoted minister and moved to War. In year 40 he returned to Works. In year 44 he moved to Personnel and assisted as Grand Secretary. Earlier Huang had urged returning the Yellow River north to Shandong's old bed and said so at court. That year the river broke at Qinglonggang; Grand Secretary Agui inspected. The emperor asked Agui and Director-general Li Fenghan; all said the north was higher, the south lower—water runs downhill; forcing the Yellow River north, they said, could not be done. Court debate again held the river had been south too long to shift lightly, and the plan died.
23
四十七年，加太子太保，在上書房總師傅上行走。 並以璜年老，諭冬令日出後入朝，賜玄狐端罩。 五十年正月，與千叟宴，為漢大臣領班。 五十一年，以老乞休，賜詩慰留。 上幸避暑山莊，命留京辦事。 五十五年四月，以璜成進士逾六十年，重與恩榮宴。 璜年八十，與高宗同歲生，生日在六月，奏改萬壽節後。 上嘉其知禮，代定八月十九日，賜詩及聯榜、上方珍玩寵之。 五十六年，复賜肩輿入直。 五十九年七月，卒，年八十有四，命皇八子奠醊，贈太子太師，賜祭葬，諡文恭。
In year 47 he became Grand Guardian and chief tutor in the Upper Study. In winter he might enter after sunrise; he received a sable-lined court robe. In month 1 of year 50 he led Han ministers at the thousand-elder banquet. In year 51 he begged to retire; the emperor answered with a poem asking him to remain. When the emperor went to the Summer Resort he stayed in Beijing on duty. In month 4 of year 55, sixty years from his jinshi, he received the grace banquet again. Huang was eighty, born the same year as Qianlong; his birthday fell in the sixth month and he asked to celebrate after the Longevity Festival. The emperor praised his tact, set the date as the nineteenth of the eighth month, and gave poems and imperial gifts. In year 56 he again received a sedan chair for court. In month 7 of year 59 he died at eighty-four; the eighth prince offered libations; he was posthumously Grand Tutor with posthumous name Wengong.
24
子八，長承謙，進士，官至侍讀，先璜卒。 族子承恩，舉人，累官至河東河道總督。
He had eight sons; eldest Chengqian, a jinshi and reader, died before him. Clansman Chengen, a provincial graduate, became Eastern Rivers director-general.
25
高斌，字右文，高佳氏，滿洲鑲黃旗人，初隸內務府。 雍正元年，授內務府主事。 再遷郎中，管蘇州織造。 六年，授廣東布政使，調浙江、江蘇、河南諸省。 九年，遷河東副總河。 十年，調兩淮鹽政，兼署江寧織造。 十一年，署江南河道總督。 十二年，回鹽政任。 复署河道總督，培范公堤六萬四千餘丈。 十三年，回鹽政任。 旋授江南河道總督。
Gao Bin, courtesy name Youwen, of the Gaojia clan, was a Bordered Yellow Banner Manchu first under the Imperial Household Department. In Yongzheng 1 he became an Imperial Household director. He rose twice to bureau director and ran Suzhou weaving. In year 6 he was Guangdong treasurer, then served Zhejiang, Jiangsu, and Henan. In year 9 he became Vice Director-general of Eastern Rivers. In year 10 he took Liang-Huai salt and acted as Nanjing weaving superintendent. In year 11 he acted as Jiangnan river director-general. In year 12 he returned to salt. He again directed rivers and strengthened the Fan Gong dike over 64,000 zhang. In year 13 he returned to salt again. Soon he was Jiangnan river director-general.
26
乾隆元年，疏請河工搶修工段需用土方，令河兵挑運十之四​​，用民工十之六。 又請葦蕩營採柴均歸廠運。 又請各州縣河工外解各項悉歸河庫道。 河南永城、江南蕭縣頻年被河患，上命高斌會兩江總督趙弘恩、河南巡撫富德籌疏通之策。 高斌等奏：「黃河南岸碭山毛城鋪向有減水石壩一，蕭縣王家山有天然減水石閘一，睢寧縣峰山有減水閘四，建自康熙間，誠分黃導淮以水治水之善策。 年久淤淺，水發為患。 毛城鋪舊有洪溝、巴河二河，為減洩黃水故道。 閘下地勢，東北偏高，水向南行，漫入祝家口。 請俟水涸疏濬二河，並於二河上游開蔣溝河，築祝家口、潘家口二壩。 漳水南流，使盡入蔣溝、洪溝、巴河分流下注，永城、碭山諸縣當無水患。 王家山天然閘減水會入徐溪口，舊有引河，間有淤淺； 峰山減水四閘，歷年既久，引河亦有淤淺：均應疏濬。 」又奏：「淮揚運河自清口至瓜洲三百餘裡，其源為分洪澤湖水入天妃閘，建瓴而下，經淮安、寶應、高郵、揚州以達於江，惟借東西漕堤為障。 請於天妃、正越兩閘之下，相距百餘丈，各建草壩三。 壩下建正石閘二，越河石閘二。 又於所建二閘尾各建草壩三。 重重關鎖，層層收蓄，則水平溜緩，可禦洪澤湖異漲，亦可減運河水勢。 湖水三分入運，七分會黃。 山盱尾閭天然南北二壩，非洪澤湖異漲不可輕開，使清水全力禦黃； 而高、寶諸湖所受之水，循軌入口，不至泛溢下河。 則高、寶、興、鹽諸縣民田可免洪湖洩水之患。 」疏入，均議行。
In Qianlong 1 he set emergency earthwork at four-tenths river soldiers and six-tenths hired labor. Reed-marsh fuel cutting was to go through depot transport. All prefectural river surcharges were to pass through the river treasury intendant. Frequent floods hit Yongcheng and Xiaoxian; Gao Bin joined Zhao Hongen and Fude to plan relief. They cited Kangxi-era overflow works at Maochengpu, Wangjiashan, and Fengshan as the classic policy of splitting Yellow water to guide the Huai. After years they had silted and floods worsened. Maochengpu's Honggou and Bahe had been Yellow River spillways. Below the sluice the bed sloped northeast; water ran south into Zhujiakou. When water fell he asked both rivers dredged, Jianggou opened upstream, and dams at Zhujiakou and Panjiakou. South-flowing Zhang water should split through Jianggou, Honggou, and Bahe so Yongcheng and Dangshan would be safe. Wangjiashan's natural sluice fed Xuxikou; its diversion sometimes silted; Fengshan's four sluices and diversions also needed dredging. He described the 300-li Huai-Yang canal from Qingkou to Guazhou, fed by Hongze through Tianfei sluice, bounded only by grain dikes. Below Tianfei and Zhengyue he asked three grass dams each, a hundred-odd zhang apart. Below them he wanted two main and two bypass stone sluices. At each new sluice tail he asked three more grass dams. Layered locks would ease the current, hold Hongze floods, and calm the canal. Three-tenths of lake water would enter the canal, seven-tenths meet the Yellow. Shanxi's natural north and south tail dams should open only in Hongze floods so clear water held the Yellow; Gaobao and other lakes would feed in without flooding the lower river. Gaobao, Xing, and salt districts would escape Hongze spill damage. All was approved.

Censor Xia Zhifang warned Maochengpu diversion would endanger Gaobao and Huai-Yang. Gao Bin was told to survey with Ji Cengyun and Liu Yongcheng. Yan Sisheng and Wang Anguo asked estuary dredging; Gao Bin joined Hongen and Shao Ji to survey. In month 3 of year 2 Gao Bin asked audience. Zhao Hongen, promoted to Minister of Revenue, also went to Beijing. The emperor convened princes and ministers and summoned Xia Zhifang and others. Gao Bin said Jin Fu built Maochengpu overflow dam in Kangxi 17 to send water to Hongze and help clear water scour Yellow silt. For sixty years the channel and people had benefited. Present work only deepened the old channel below the dam—not opening the dam itself. Discharged water wound 600 li through Xuzhou, Xiaoxian, and other counties, stored in Yangmeng and five lakes. By the lake it was clear—no sand scourge, no fear the lake could not hold it. Xia Zhifang held firm; Censor Zhen Zhihuang said Huai-Yang's millions were terrified of opening Maochengpu. Zhong Heng's memorial said the same. The emperor took Gao Bin's side and rebuked Zhihuang, Heng, and Xia.

In year 4 opinion held Gao Bin's new mouth was safer; Qianlong sent Grand Secretary Ortai to verify. Ortai still backed the new transport mouth. In month 8 Gao Bin had audience and was told to survey Zhili rivers with Sun Jiagan and Gu Cong. In year 6 he said from Suyuan to Qinghe the Yellow ran fast and pressed the canal. He asked the canal's south thread dike serve as the Yellow's north setback dike. He also asked Guazhou's low mouth moved and a bypass cut to cut Huai inflow. He asked Zhenjiang south-bank brush changed to brick. All went to the ministries for implementation.

Excavated spoil would become north-bank slope embankments against north overflow. The south bank would be linked to check south current. The lower mouth sand bar would be dredged as needed. Upstream he asked rolling dams at Shuangying south and Hulindian and Xiaohuijiazhuang north; and dikes lowered below dam crest. Guojia's old south-bank grass dam should be rebuilt to match. In month 12 he assisted as Grand Secretary on the Grand Council.

十一年，御史楊開鼎劾南河河道總督白鍾山河決匿災不報，命高斌往江南會總督尹繼善按治，白鍾山坐奪官。 疏言：「淮、黃二瀆，每年伏秋水漲，以老壩口水志為準則。 乾隆七年最大，水志連底水一丈四尺七寸，當以此較量每年水勢。 各處閘壩開閉，應以就近石工水漲尺寸為度。 」運河水漲，又命高斌往勘。 疏陳培六塘河謝家莊、龍溝口諸處堤堰，濬中墩河、項家衝東門河； 又疏請豁免海州、沭陽​​、贛榆諸縣逋賦，及板浦、徐瀆、中正、莞瀆、臨洪、興莊諸場折價帶徵銀：並從之。 高斌嘗謂黃水宜合不宜分，清水宜蓄不宜洩，惟規度湖河水勢，視其縮盈以定蓄洩，方不至泛溢阻礙為民害。 諸所籌畫，皆可循守。 十二年三月，授文淵閣大學士。 四月，命往江南同河道總督週學健督理防汛。 五月，直隸水利工竟。
In year 11 Censor Yang Kaiding accused Bai Zhongshan of hiding a breach; Gao Bin investigated with Yin Jishan; Bai was dismissed. He said Huai and Yellow floods used the old dam gauge as standard. Qianlong 7's peak—one zhang four chi seven cun on the gauge—should be the yearly benchmark. Sluices should open and close by nearby stone flood marks. When the canal rose he was sent to survey again. He strengthened Liutang dikes and dredged Zhongdun and Xiangjiachong rivers; he exempted Haizhou and Shuyang taxes and eased salt-field levies—all approved. Gao Bin held Yellow water should stay united, clear water stored; only measured storage and release prevented harm. His plans were sound precedent. In month 3 of year 12 he became Grand Secretary of the Hall of Literary Deepness. In month 4 he went to Jiangnan with Zhou Xuejian on flood defense. In month 5 Zhili water works finished.

In intercalary month 5 he briefly ran Liangjiang. In month 8 the Xuyang Yangwu breach was open; Gao Bin asked to hurry there before the edict arrived. The emperor praised his zeal as befitting a great minister. In month 11 the breach closed; he surveyed Tianjin works with Wang Youdun. In year 17, aged seventy, he received an imperial poem.

On the year 22 southern tour the emperor recalled Gao Bin's river achievements. Maochengpu divided Yellow water; his Xuzhou gauge opened only at seven chi. Censor Wu Wei impeached his appointments; he was transferred east without deep blame. In year 9 he said Shandong flooded because upstream scattered and downstream blocked. Soon he acted as river director-general. In month 1 of year 3 Zhu Zao was appointed; Gu Cong assisted. Capital southwest waters converged on east and west ponds, silting and flooding. He asked scoop boats dredging mud under Sanjiao Pond and Qinghe intendants. Zao left; Gu Cong was river director-general again. 乾隆元年，奏：「河標兵駐濟寧，無倉儲，每稱貸貴糴。 請以生息銀二千七百有奇買穀四千石，設倉存貯，春借秋收。 」又奏：「豫東河防，水落時，當堵塞支河。 伏秋水漲，購料募夫，每慮不及。 請發河南、山東司庫銀分存鄭州及武陟、封丘、曹、單諸縣，永遠貯備。 」皆從之。 四年，疏言：「漳水舊自直隸入海，康熙四十五年，引漳入衛濟運，故道漸淤，全歸衛河，勢難容受。 嗣於德州哨馬營建滾水壩，開引河洩衛水，由鉤盤河達老黃河入海。 然漳、衛二水隨時淤塞，虛糜帑金。 漳水舊有正河、支河，應擇易浚者復其故道。 於館陶建閘，衛水大，聽漳入海以防漲； 衛水小，分漳入衛以濟運。 」奏入，命大學士鄂爾泰詳議，議在丘縣東和爾寨村承漳河北折之勢，接開十餘裡，至漳洞村入舊河； 因於新河東流入衛處建閘，以時啟閉，上從之。 時漕運總督補熙請造十丈大船，運河當以水深四尺為則。 白鍾山謂：「徬河無源之水，雨至而後泉旺，泉旺而後河盈。 上徬閉、下徬啟，則下徬倍深，上徬倍淺。 各徬相距遠近不均，水近者深，則遠者必淺。 以人役水，以水送舟，必不能均深四尺。 」侍郎趙殿最又請於館陶、臨清各立衛河水則，白鍾山謂：「尺寸不足，將衛輝民田渠徬盡閉，致妨灌溉，事既難行，尺寸既足，將官渠官徬盡閉，來源頓息。 下流已逝，運河之水亦立見消涸。 二者均屬非計。 」議並寢。

Gu Cong blamed Bai's measures; over 100,000 taels of his property was seized for compensation.
51
十五年，授永定河道。 十八年，河決張家路，命從尚書舒赫德往勘。 旋命以按察使銜協辦南河事。 十九年，复授河東河道總督。 二十年，署山東巡撫。 請罷孔氏世襲曲阜知縣，上命改授世襲六品官。 尋奏濟寧以南積水未消，請緩開汶河大壩，疏濬下游河道。 上命白鍾山往勘南河，文武各官聽調遣。
In year 15 he became Yongding River intendant. In year 18 Zhangjialu broke; he surveyed with Shuhede. Soon he assisted Southern Rivers as surveillance commissioner. In year 19 he was Eastern Rivers director-general again. In year 20 he acted as Shandong governor. He asked to end the Kong hereditary Qufu magistracy; the emperor made it a hereditary sixth-rank post. Standing flood south of Jining led him to delay opening the Wen great dam and dredge downstream. Qianlong sent Bai to survey Southern Rivers with full authority.
52
二十二年，調江南河道總督，疏言：「自河決張家路，沙停河淤，下流不暢，南高北洼。 迨孫家集復決，河底益高。 黃河受病，率由水勢側注北岸，沖刷溝槽。 惟有南北分籌，南宜疏，北宜築。 築則支河不致奪溜，疏則稍分有餘之水勢，庶徐州得以少安。 臣與河臣張師載商榷，以為南岸長灘較北岸更險，必於橫亙處濬引河，導溜歸中，岸堤益加高厚。 北岸無堤，漫水如梁家馬路、徐家莊等處支河數十道，及黃家莊、郭家堂等處漫槽矮灘，宜築土壩。 水平則收束以刷正河，水漲則平漫平消，不至衝槽奪溜。 並於孫家集培堤增壩，以為重障。 駱馬湖北受蒙陰山水，西受微山湖水，其尾閭在六塘河。 上游湖堤在在殘缺，亟應修補捍防。 」皆從之。
In year 22 he said since Zhangjialu the river had silted, south high north low. Sunjiaji's breach raised the bed further. Yellow River ills came from current slanting north and scouring gullies. South needed dredging, north building. Building blocked branch seizures; dredging divided force—Xuzhou might rest easier. He and Zhang Shizai said the south long beach was worse—dredge a cross-bar diversion and raise dikes. North bank overflows at Liangjia Malu, Xujiazhuang, Huangjiazhuang, and Guojiatang needed earthen dams. Level water should scour the main channel; floods should spread flat without seizing branches. Sunjiaji also needed stronger dikes and dams as a barrier. Luoma Lake took Mengyin and Weishan waters; its tail was the Liutang River. Upstream lake dikes were broken and needed urgent repair. All approved.
53
荊山橋工竟，議敘。 奏言：「寧夏上游河水陡漲，急報下游防範。 正陽關為淮水上下關鍵，應仿寧夏水報法，派員專司其事。 」又奏：「上江諸水皆歸安河以達洪澤湖。 安河間段淤淺，連年水患由此。 宜多募漁船，伐蘆撈泥，俾尾閭一通，上游皆有去路。 又歸仁堤下舊有涵洞，穿鮑家河以達安河，久經湮塞。 擬開浚分林子河一支，則安河進水之地亦有所分，患可漸減。 」報聞。 二十三年，加太子少保。 二十六年，卒，贈太子太保，賜祭葬，諡莊恪。
Jingshan Bridge work finished; merit was deliberated. Ningxia floods needed urgent downstream warning. Zhengyang Pass needed Ningxia-style flood reporting. Upper Jiang waters all reached Hongze through An River. Silted An River sections caused yearly floods. Hire fishing boats to dredge reeds and mud so upstream had outlets. Old Guiren culverts to Baojia and An rivers were long blocked. Dredging Fenlinzi branch would ease An River intake. Reported received. In year 23 he was Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent. In year 26 he died, posthumously Grand Guardian with posthumous name Zhuangke.
54
論曰：自靳輔治河、淮，繼其後者，疏濬修築，守成法惟謹。 世宗朝，齊蘇勒最著，嵇曾筠、高斌皆仍世繼業，與靳輔同祠河上，有功德於民，克應祭法。 完顏偉、顧琮、白鍾山隨事補苴，不負當官之責。 高斌任事二十年，疏毛家鋪引河，排眾議行之，民蒙其利。 奪淮之役，縛赴工次待決。 雷霆不測之威，赫矣哉！
The critique: after Jin Fu governed river and Huai, successors dredged and built by careful precedent. In Yongzheng's court Qi Sulei stood out; Ji Cengyun and Gao Bin continued the line and were enshrined with Jin Fu. Wan Yan Wei, Gu Cong, and Bai Zhongshan patched gaps and did their duty. Gao Bin served twenty years, opened Maojiapu diversion against opposition, and the people gained. When the Huai was seized he was bound at the works awaiting execution. How terrible and splendid the unpredictable thunder of imperial wrath!
