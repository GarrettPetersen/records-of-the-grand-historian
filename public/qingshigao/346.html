1
恆瑞，宗室，隸正白旗，吉林將軍薩喇善子。 乾隆中，授侍衛，赴西藏辦事，擢熱河都統，遷福州將軍。 五十二年，台灣林爽文作亂，命率駐防兵往剿，參贊軍務，偕總督常青赴南路。 鳳山賊勢方熾，高宗知常青、恆瑞不可恃，命福康安督師。 賊圍總兵柴大紀於諸羅，恆瑞駐軍鹽水港，逗留不進，詔解任。 福康安至，屢為疏陳戰績，帝益怒，斥其徇護，逮恆瑞論罪。 事平，減死戍伊犁。 尋予副都統銜，充伊犁參贊大臣。 歷定邊左副將軍、綏遠城將軍，調西安。
Heng Rui belonged to the imperial clan and was registered in the Plain White Banner; he was the son of Sala Shan, the general of Jilin. Under Qianlong he became an imperial guardsman, served on affairs in Tibet, rose to commander-in-chief at Rehe, and was later appointed general at Fuzhou. In 1787, when Lin Shuangwen rose in Taiwan, he was ordered to lead garrison forces against the rebels, appointed to assist in the campaign, and marched with Governor-General Changqing along the southern front. With rebel power blazing at Fengshan, the Qianlong Emperor concluded that Changqing and Heng Rui were not to be trusted, and put Fuk'anggan in overall command. When the rebels besieged Brigadier Chai Daji at Zhuluo, Heng Rui held his force at Yanshui Harbor and stalled; the court dismissed him from his post. After Fuk'anggan arrived, Heng Rui kept sending memorials trumpeting his victories; the emperor's anger only deepened, he was accused of covering for him, and Heng Rui was arrested to stand trial. Once the rebellion was over, his death sentence was reduced and he was sent into exile garrison service at Ili. Before long he received the rank of deputy lieutenant-general and served as assistant grand minister at Ili. He later served as Left Deputy General on the frontier, as general at Suiyuan, and was then transferred to Xi'an.
2
嘉慶元年，命率駐防兵三千，偕都統永保會剿湖北教匪。 三月，與總兵文圖破賊竹山。 永保至，合師由房縣進剿，文圖分剿三里坪、喇叭洞諸賊悉盡； 而恆瑞追賊至保康，未大創之。 賊首姚之富踞襄陽，勢甚熾，命恆瑞進剿。 五月，偕明亮進次呂堰，擊賊岳家溝、劉家集，擒斬二千餘。 賊圍棗陽，設伏王家岡，誘賊敗之； 又敗之於蔣家垱、曲家灣，棗陽圍解。 賊偽降，潛襲官軍後路； 急以後隊為前隊，擊退之。 賊走丫兒山，與張家垱賊相犄角，奮擊一晝夜，破賊營十餘，殲賊甚眾，被獎賚。 七月，破賊隨州龍門山，與永保會攻鍾祥賊巢，連破之鄧家河、黑沙河、雙溝。 賊乃分竄唐縣、呂堰，追至滹沱鎮，复竄倉台。 尋，之富渡滾河，圍景安於鄧州。 詔斥諸將玩誤，逮永保，責恆瑞戴罪立功。
In 1796 he was ordered to lead three thousand garrison troops and, with Commander-in-Chief Yongbao, to help suppress the White Lotus rebels in Hubei. In spring he and Brigadier Wentu routed the rebels at Zhushan. When Yongbao arrived, their united forces pushed from Fang County; Wentu meanwhile cleared out the rebel bands at Sanliping, Labadong, and elsewhere; while Heng Rui chased the rebels only to Baokang and failed to deal them a serious blow. Rebel chief Yao Zhifu held Xiangyang in great strength, and Heng Rui was ordered to move against him. In summer he joined Mingliang at Lüyan, struck the rebels at Yuejiagou and Liujiaji, and killed or captured more than two thousand men. When the rebels besieged Zaoyang, he laid an ambush at Wangjiagang, drew them in, and routed them; then defeated them again at Jiangjiadang and Qujiawan, lifting the siege of Zaoyang. The rebels pretended to surrender and then struck at the army's rear; he quickly swung his rear guard forward and beat them off. The rebels retreated to Ya'er Mountain and linked up with those at Zhangjiadang; after a full day and night of hard fighting he overran more than ten camps and killed a great many rebels, earning imperial rewards. In autumn he routed the rebels at Longmen Mountain in Suizhou, then with Yongbao stormed the rebel stronghold at Zhongxiang and took Dengjiahe, Heishahe, and Shuanggou in turn. The rebels then scattered toward Tang County and Lüyan; pursued to Hutuo Town, they slipped away again toward Cangtai. Soon afterward Zhifu crossed the Gun River and besieged Jing'an at Dengzhou. An edict rebuked the commanders for dallying; Yongbao was arrested, and Heng Rui was ordered to win back his honor on the battlefield.
3
二年正月，偕惠齡等剿襄陽賊，賊首劉起榮就擒； 又與慶成敗賊鄭家河​​，擒賊目李潮； 進剿泰山寺、龍鳳溝，擒賊目姚爽等：賜花翎。 於是賊分竄，由河南入陝，恆瑞追賊至山陽，遇王廷詔、李全等，擊走之。 五月，追賊陝南，與惠齡夾攻於黃龍垱，殲賊三千餘。 廷詔、全復與賊之富合趨紫陽，渡漢江，恆瑞坐縱賊，奪花翎。 賊遂分路入川，廷詔竄開縣、雲陽、萬縣，犯夔州，西與大寧賊響應，恆瑞追及，連敗之，乃竄太平。 八月，當陽逸匪掠白河、洵陽，命馳赴興安扼剿，偕慶成擊賊於張家灘，由牛氾嶺繞出賊前，奪賊營九。 廷詔等奔紫陽，與惠齡夾擊敗之。 恆瑞率師還漢中，敗賊西鄉，又敗之褒城黃沙鋪。 十一月，之富等西奔，將渡漢北竄。 偕慶成蹙諸半渡，賊西趨寧、沔。 師進，遇高均德於桑樹灣，乃議四面設伏，恆瑞令撒拉爾回兵假鄉勇旗幟誘之，自由山梁馳下，慶成等分路夾擊，俘斬甚眾。 捷聞，被優賚。 十二月，破王廷詔於保寧，進解營山圍。
In early 1797 he joined Huiling and others against the Xiangyang rebels, and rebel chief Liu Qirong was taken; he and Qing Cheng also routed the rebels at Zhengjiahe and captured the rebel officer Li Chao; then pressed the rebels at Taishan Temple and Longfenggou and captured officers such as Yao Shuang, for which he received the peacock feather. The rebels then scattered into Shaanxi from Henan; Heng Rui pursued them to Shanyang, met Wang Tingzhao, Li Quan, and their bands, and drove them back. In summer he chased the rebels through southern Shaanxi; at Huanglongdang he and Huiling caught them in a pincer and killed more than three thousand. Tingzhao and Quan rejoined Zhifu and made for Ziyang, crossing the Han; Heng Rui was blamed for letting them get away and stripped of his peacock feather. The rebels then split into Sichuan; Tingzhao swept through Kai, Yunyang, and Wan counties, struck at Kuizhou, and linked up with rebels in Daning to the west; Heng Rui overtook them, defeated them repeatedly, and drove them toward Taiping. In autumn fugitives from Dangyang raided Baihe and Xunyang; he was ordered to Xing'an to cut them off; with Qing Cheng he struck at Zhangjiatan, swung around Niufan Ridge ahead of the rebels, and took nine camps. Tingzhao and his band fled to Ziyang, where Huiling joined in a pincer and routed them. Heng Rui brought his force back to Hanzhong, beat the rebels at Xixiang, and again at Huangshapu in Bao. In winter Zhifu and his followers fled west, preparing to cross north of the Han and break away. With Qing Cheng he caught them mid-crossing; the rebels veered west toward Ning and Mian. Advancing, they met Gao Junde at Sangshuwan and laid a four-sided ambush; Heng Rui sent Salar Muslim troops disguised as local militia to draw the enemy in, then charged down the ridge while Qing Cheng and others struck from several directions; a great many were killed or captured. News of the victory brought him exceptional rewards. At year's end he routed Wang Tingzhao at Baoning and marched to relieve the siege of Yingshan.
4
三年，川匪羅其清犯順慶，偕慶成往援，因賊勢蔓延，請勒保、宜綿遣兵會剿。 賊竄蓬州，潛結冉文儔擾儀隴，恆瑞扼磨盤寨，與惠齡等合擊之，文儔敗走，陝匪龍紹週與合，敗之楊家寨。 六月，與德楞泰夾擊高均德於石人河，复偕惠齡攻老林場賊卡，進逼大神山，均德、文儔踞險死拒，分路進攻，賊奔箕山； 而徐天德、樊人傑為將軍富成追擊，窮蹙，亦入焉。 惠齡、德楞泰攻其前，恆瑞攻其後，盡破山寨，先後斬馘近萬。 其清，李全、王廷詔奔大鵬山，進圍，十一月，克之。 命赴陝與宜綿等會剿張漢潮。 未幾，李全、樊人傑竄西鄉。 帝以恆瑞未迎擊，嚴斥之。
In 1798 the Sichuan rebel Luo Qiqing struck at Shunqing; Heng Rui went with Qing Cheng to help, and as the rebels spread he asked Lebao and Yimian to send forces for a joint campaign. The rebels slipped into Pengzhou and secretly linked with Ran Wenchou to raid Yilong; Heng Rui held Mopanzhai and, with Huiling and others, routed Wenchou; when the Shaanxi rebel Long Shaozhou joined him, they were beaten at Yangjiazhai. In summer he and Delengtai caught Gao Junde at Shirenhe in a pincer; with Huiling he stormed the rebel posts at Laolinchang and pressed Dashen Mountain; Junde and Wenchou held the heights and fought desperately; attacking from several sides, they drove the rebels toward Jishan; while Xu Tiande and Fan Renjie, harried and cornered by General Fucheng, fled in as well. Huiling and Delengtai struck from the front while Heng Rui hit from behind; every stockade fell, and nearly ten thousand rebels were killed or taken in all. Qiqing, Li Quan, and Wang Tingzhao fled to Dapeng Mountain; the army invested it and, in winter, took it. He was ordered to Shaanxi to join Yimian and others against Zhang Hanchao. Soon Li Quan and Fan Renjie broke into Xixiang. The emperor sharply rebuked him for failing to intercept them.
5
四年，署陝甘總督，赴寧羌擊藍、白兩號賊。 張應祥等竄秦州、兩當，又擊走張漢潮、冉學勝股匪。 五月，解署任，剿白號賊於白馬關，地與川西龍安接壤，遣將冒雨掩擊，賊竄西和、禮縣； 令布政使廣厚、總兵吉蘭泰截剿，自趨賈家店、黑馬關抄擊藍號賊，敗之於老柏樹，复花翎。 賊竄川北，至秋，折回陝境，擊走之。 乃赴城固、洋縣，會明亮剿張漢潮，破之東西叉河，賊從馬埡道遁老林，要之於清水溝，复乘霧雨徐渡三渡水。 帝疑諸將縱賊，又以恆瑞前剿藍號賊垂盡，舍之回陝，下尚書那彥成察劾。 那彥成，恆瑞之婿也，覆陳回師出總督松筠意，得免罪。 尋明亮殲漢潮，恆瑞自五郎追擊，餘黨李得士等由大建溝入老林，趨秦嶺，與那彥成會剿冉學勝等，賊奔澇谷； 扼兩岔河，追擊於山陽東溝，敗之。
In 1799 he acted as governor-general of Shaanxi-Gansu and went to Ningqiang to fight the Blue and White rebel bands. Zhang Yingxiang and others slipped into Qinzhou and Liangdang; he also drove back the bands of Zhang Hanchao and Ran Xuesheng. In summer he left the acting governorship and fought the White-Banner rebels at Baimaguan on the border with Long'an in western Sichuan; he sent officers in a rainy night attack, and the rebels fled toward Xihe and Li; he set Provincial Treasurer Guanghou and Brigadier Jilantai to cut them off while he himself swept toward Jiajiadian and Heimaguan to hit the Blue-Banner rebels from behind, routed them at Laobaishu, and won back his peacock feather. The rebels fled into northern Sichuan; in autumn they doubled back into Shaanxi and were driven out. He then went to Chenggu and Yang, joined Mingliang against Zhang Hanchao, and routed him at Dongxichahe; the rebels fled along the Mayada trail into the deep woods; they blocked them at Qingshuigou and, using fog and rain as cover, crossed the Sandu River at leisure. The emperor suspected his commanders of letting the rebels slip away; he also questioned why Heng Rui, who had nearly destroyed the Blue-Banner rebels, had left them and returned to Shaanxi, and ordered Minister Nayancheng to investigate. Nayancheng was Heng Rui's son-in-law; he reported that the withdrawal had been ordered by Governor-General Songyun, and Heng Rui escaped punishment. Soon Mingliang destroyed Hanchao; Heng Rui pursued from Wulang; remnants led by Li Deshi entered the deep woods through Dajiangou and made for the Qinling; with Nayancheng he moved against Ran Xuesheng and others; the rebels fled to Laogu; he blocked them at Liangchahe, chased them to Donggou in Shanyang, and routed them.
6
五年，川匪二萬餘由略陽寇兩當、徽縣，恆瑞自褒城入棧，賊竄隴州、清水、秦安，偕那彥成追至汪家山，大敗之。 總兵凝德戰歿秦安，恆瑞赴援，复偕那彥成敗賊於龍泉溝、深都堡，總兵多爾濟、札普戰歿洵陽。 詔促恆瑞赴鎮安、五郎勦賊，三月，抵唐藏。 楊開甲、高均德方擾南星，留總兵觀祥駐守，自​​赴商州。 帝疑其趨避，累詔詰責，乃赴鎮安剿冉文勝等，敗之於大中溪。 會額勒登保破開甲於輝峪，恆瑞自龍駒寨抄截，開甲逸走，圍副將李天林於漫川關，馳援，斬賊目羅貴等，賊乃分路西竄。 敘功，予雲騎尉世職。 六月，率總兵德忠駐守太渠、唐藏。 時伍金柱、高天德、馬學禮犯西鄉，提督王文雄戰歿，乃進兵大石川，賊奔灘口，為楊遇春所破。
In 1800 more than twenty thousand Sichuan rebels from Lüeyang struck Liangdang and Hui; Heng Rui entered the plank roads from Bao; the rebels fled through Longzhou, Qingshui, and Qin'an; with Nayancheng he overtook them at Wangjiashan and crushed them. Brigadier Ningde fell at Qin'an; Heng Rui hurried to help; with Nayancheng he again beat the rebels at Longquangou and Shendubao; Brigadiers Duo'erji and Zhapu died fighting at Xunyang. An edict pressed him to move on Zhen'an and Wulang; in spring he reached Tangcang. Yang Kaijia and Gao Junde were raiding Nanxing; he left Brigadier Guanxiang to hold the line and went on to Shangzhou himself. The emperor suspected him of shying away from the fight and rebuked him repeatedly; he then went to Zhen'an against Ran Wensheng and others and routed them at Dazhongxi. Meanwhile Eledengbao beat Kaijia at Huiyu; Heng Rui intercepted from Longjuzhai; Kaijia slipped away but then besieged Deputy Commander Li Tianlin at Manchuanguan; Heng Rui raced to relieve him, killed officers such as Luo Gui, and the rebels scattered westward. For his service he received the hereditary rank of Cloud Cavalry Captain. In summer he and Brigadier Dezhong garrisoned Taiqu and Tangcang. When Wu Jinzhu, Gao Tiande, and Ma Xueli struck Xixiang, Provincial Commander Wang Wenxiong was killed; Heng Rui advanced to Dashichuan; the rebels fled to Tankou and were broken by Yang Yuchun.
7
恆瑞自教匪起，久在行間，以偏師數臨大敵，至是老病，久無顯功。 帝慮其不任戰，詢額勒登保，上其狀，命回鎮西安。 逾年卒。
Since the sectarian uprising Heng Rui had spent years in the field, repeatedly leading smaller forces against major rebel armies; by now he was old and sick and had long ceased to win striking victories. Fearing he could no longer bear the strain of campaigning, the emperor asked Eledengbao, who reported on his condition, and ordered him back to his post at Xi'an. He died the following year.
8
慶成，孫氏，漢軍正白旗人，提督思克曾孫，都統五福孫也。 由鑾儀衛整儀尉，累遷廣東督標副將。 乾隆五十三年，從總督孫士毅徵安南，屢擒敵有功，賜花翎、錫郎阿巴圖魯勇號。 內擢正白旗漢軍副都統、戶部侍郎、御前侍衛、正紅旗護軍統領。 五十七年，出為古北口提督。
Qing Cheng, of the Sun clan, belonged to the Han Plain White Banner; he was the great-grandson of Provincial Commander Sike and the grandson of Commander-in-Chief Wufu. He began in the Imperial Procession Guard and rose step by step to deputy commander of the Guangdong governor's brigade. In 1788 he followed Governor-General Sun Shiyi against Annam, took many prisoners in repeated successes, and received the peacock feather and the brave title Xilangabatulu. He was then promoted in the capital to deputy lieutenant-general of the Han Plain White Banner, vice minister of revenue, imperial bodyguard, and commander of the Plain Red Banner garrison. In 1792 he was appointed provincial commander at Gubeikou.
9
嘉慶元年，率兵赴南陽、襄陽剿教匪，偕恆瑞迭敗姚之富、劉之協於雙溝、張家集。 賊屯棗陽丫兒山，分踞張家垱，連營十餘裡，遮官軍，慶成先進，襲其寨，大破之，擒宋廷貴、陳正五，追敗餘匪於紅土山，擒黃玉貴，加太子少保。 之富竄鍾祥，合劉起榮、張富國等眾五六萬，偕永保等冒雨攻克之，晉太子太保。 賊遁雙溝，擾唐縣滹沱鎮。 慶成等以久戰兵疲，不能圍剿，詔嚴斥之。 賊竄棗陽太平鎮，四路合攻，斬數千級，慶成受矛傷，被優賚。 十一月，賊潛渡滾河北竄，與永保等並被嚴譴，盡奪宮銜、花翎、勇號，易惠齡為總統。 尋偕惠齡連破賊於王家城、梓山。 二年正月，大戰興隆集，斬二千餘級。 分路追賊，慶成射中賊首劉起榮，擒之，在諸將中戰最力。 高宗以慶成為五福孫，不次擢用； 自縱賊滾河，慮其少年自用，不能服眾，命惠齡察奏，至是詔免前罪。 二月，擊賊曾家店，胸中矛，裹創而戰。 賊敗竄河南境，分數路，慶成追李全，連破之確山五里川、盧氏火焰溝。 四月，李全、王廷詔合陷鄖西，馳復其城，賊不戰分遁。 未幾，之富竄渡漢江，降二品頂戴，​​暫留提督任。 襄匪竄開州，偕惠齡追敗之南天峒、火焰壩，复花翎。 賊趨大寧，與川匪合，慶成與川軍會剿。 九月，偕恆瑞截擊湖北迴竄之賊於洵陽，而李全、王廷詔沿漢東走，慶成登舟下漢以要其前； 惠齡、恆瑞從陸躡其後，至紫陽夾攻之，賊竄興安，慶成一晝夜追及，大破之司渡河。
In 1796 he led troops to Nanyang and Xiangyang against the rebels; with Heng Rui he repeatedly routed Yao Zhifu and Liu Zhixie at Shuanggou and Zhangjiaji. The rebels held Ya'er Mountain in Zaoyang and Zhangjiadang besides, their camps stretching more than ten li across the government line of march; Qing Cheng led the attack, stormed their stockade, won a crushing victory, took Song Tinggui and Chen Zhengwu, ran down the survivors at Hongtushan and captured Huang Yugui, and was made junior guardian of the heir apparent. Zhifu fled to Zhongxiang and gathered fifty or sixty thousand men under Liu Qirong, Zhang Fuguo, and others; with Yongbao and others Qing Cheng took the place in a rainy assault and was promoted to grand guardian of the heir apparent. The rebels slipped away to Shuanggou and raided Hutuo Town in Tang County. Qing Cheng and his fellows, their troops exhausted by long campaigning, failed to encircle the rebels; the court rebuked them sharply. The rebels fled to Taiping Town in Zaoyang; a four-pronged assault killed several thousand; Qing Cheng took a spear wound and received exceptional rewards. In winter the rebels slipped north across the Gun; Qing Cheng and Yongbao were sharply censured, stripped of all court ranks, the peacock feather, and his brave title, and Huiling replaced him as supreme commander. Soon he and Huiling routed the rebels in succession at Wangjiacheng and Zishan. In early 1797 a major battle at Xinglongji cost the rebels more than two thousand lives. In the pursuit by separate routes Qing Cheng shot rebel chief Liu Qirong and took him alive; among the commanders he fought the hardest. Because Qing Cheng was Wufu's grandson, the Qianlong Emperor had promoted him ahead of his years; after he let the rebels cross the Gun, the emperor feared the young man's self-will and inability to command respect, and ordered Huiling to investigate; now an edict pardoned his earlier faults. In spring he struck the rebels at Zengjiadian, took a spear in the chest, and fought on with his wounds bound up. The beaten rebels scattered into Henan along several routes; Qing Cheng chased Li Quan and routed him repeatedly at Wulichuan in Queshan and at Huoyangou in Lushi. In summer Li Quan and Wang Tingzhao together seized Yunxi; Qing Cheng raced to retake the city, and the rebels slipped away without a fight. Soon Zhifu broke across the Han; Qing Cheng was reduced to second-grade rank but kept temporarily as provincial commander. Xiangyang rebels fled into Kaizhou; with Huiling he ran them down at Nantiandong and Huoyanba and won back his peacock feather. The rebels made for Daning and joined the Sichuan rebels; Qing Cheng campaigned with the Sichuan forces. In autumn he and Heng Rui cut off at Xunyang the rebels doubling back from Hubei; as Li Quan and Wang Tingzhao moved down the east bank of the Han, Qing Cheng took boats downriver to block their path; Huiling and Heng Rui pressed them overland; at Ziyang they caught the rebels in a pincer; the rebels fled to Xing'an; Qing Cheng chased through a day and a night, overtook them, and crushed them at Sihedu.
10

In 1797 the northern Yongshui route was opened; twenty thousand troops were left to guard Guizhou and Huguang; Fu Zhina was made brigade commander at Zhenqian and shared command with the provincial commander. Since Subprefect Fu Nai had built forts and opened garrison farms on the Miao frontier, defenses tightened, but the Miao had not yet submitted and gathered to resist; senior officials blamed Fu Nai and were about to impeach him; Fu Zhina argued hard and stopped it. He diverted military supplies to help the work; the garrison farms later prospered and Miao unrest subsided. People praised Fu Nai's work and endlessly celebrated Fu Zhina as well. In 1800 black Miao from Shaijintang raided from Zhenqian; with Fu Nai he drove them back; he again struck Miao bands at Gouyan, burned their stockade, and the Miao, frightened, sued for peace. In 1803 the Yongshui Miao leader Long Liusheng rose in revolt and was captured. He acted as Hunan provincial commander, then was appointed Guizhou provincial commander; discipline was strict and he was acclaimed as a famous general. In 1810 he died in office.

亮祿，伊爾根覺羅氏，滿洲正紅旗人。 襲輕車都尉世職，授密雲協領。 嘉慶初，以參將發河南，署游擊。 三年，教匪窺河南，巡撫吳熊光駐防盧氏，兵多他調。 寶豐、郟縣賊起，掠汝州。 布政使馬慧裕不嫺軍事，亮祿曰：「兵貴神速。 今賊初起，烏合易滅，請兼程往剿。 」賊屯寶豐翟家集，東阻大溝，恃險不退，亮祿聲言京兵且至，樹八旗大纛，鞭馬腹，俾騰踔嘶號，聲震數里，賊懼； 夜吹角而進，躍馬逾壕，火其寨，一鼓殲之，擒其渠李岳等。 奏入，仁宗大悅，立擢副將。 累遷雲南開化鎮總兵。 七年，卒，帝甚惜之。
Liang Lu, of the Irgen Gioro clan, belonged to the Manchu Plain Red Banner. He inherited the hereditary rank of commandant of light chariots and was appointed company commander of the Miyun brigade. Early in the Jiaqing reign he was sent to Henan as a colonel and acted as brigade commander. In 1798 the rebels probed Henan; Governor Wu Xiongguang held Lushi, but most of his troops had been sent elsewhere. Rebels rose in Baofeng and Jia and raided Ruzhou. Provincial Treasurer Ma Huiyu knew little of war; Liang Lu said, "Speed is everything in war. They have only just risen, a mob that can easily be broken; let me go at once by forced marches to destroy them. " The rebels held Zhijiaji in Baofeng behind a great ditch to the east and would not budge; Liang Lu spread word that capital troops were coming, raised the Eight Banners' great standards, whipped his horses until they leapt and neighed, and the din carried for miles; the rebels were terrified; That night he sounded the horn, charged over the ditch, burned their camp, wiped them out in one assault, and captured leaders such as Li Yue. When the report reached court, the Jiaqing Emperor was delighted and immediately promoted him to deputy commander. He rose step by step to brigade commander at Kaihua in Yunnan. In 1802 he died, to the emperor's deep regret.

論曰：恆瑞、慶成戮力襄陽，剿匪最久，後皆獨當一面，功過不掩，故仁宗始終保全。 七十五孤軍苦戰，徒以失懽群帥，未奏顯功，論者惜之。 富志那獨平枝江、宜都一路，移鎮苗疆，與傅鼐和衷弭亂，有足稱焉。
The historian comments: Heng Rui and Qing Cheng fought side by side at Xiangyang longer than any others against the rebels; each later held an independent command; their merits and faults stood side by side, and so the Jiaqing Emperor protected them to the end. Qi Shi Wu fought bitterly with isolated forces, but because he lost favor with his fellow commanders he never won striking glory; historians regret it. Fu Zhina alone pacified the Zhijiang-Yidu corridor, then garrisoned the Miao frontier and, working in harmony with Fu Nai, quelled unrest there—achievements well worth praise.
