方積硃爾漢楊頀廖寅陳昌齊硃爾賡額查崇華方積，字有堂，安徽定遠人。 拔貢生。 以州判發四川，補閬中知縣，署梁山。 達州東鄉賊起，梁山當其衝，賊犯縣境，營白兔山守兵潰。 積以一百人據小山為疑兵，賊不敢進。 築砦二百餘所，令人自為守。 他縣流民依集者三十餘萬人，賊至無所掠食，屢出奇兵擊走之。 堅壁清野之法，蓋自梁山始。 既而萬縣寶靈寺賊起，越境剿平之，又助大兵殲伍文相於石壩山，卻林亮功於望牛埡，斃亮功弟廷相，賜花翎。 擢寧遠知府，仍留駐梁山，凡四年。 至嘉慶六年，諸路賊漸平，調夔州，繼劉清為建昌道。 涼山生番叛，率師討平之。 未幾，里塘正土司索諾木根登殺副土司，奪其印，副將德寧兵為所困。 積單騎往，密授舊頭目希拉工布方略，以其眾破之。 歷川北道、鹽茶道，擢按察使。 馬邊、峨眉嶺諸夷結梁山生番盜邊，積偕提督丰紳由馬邊三河口鑿山深入，克六拔夷巢，遂出赤夷間道，進攻嶺夷十二地。 浹旬之間，每戰皆捷。 曲曲烏助逆死拒，潛師出其後，殄之。 遷布政使。
This chapter treats Fang Ji, Zhu Erhan, Yang Hu, Liao Yin, Chen Changqi, Zhu Ergenge, and Cha Chonghua. Fang Ji, styled Youtang, came from Dingyuan in Anhui. He passed the tribute-student selection examination. Sent to Sichuan as a subprefectural judge, he received the Langzhong magistracy and acted as magistrate of Liangshan. Rebels rose in Dongxiang, Dazhou, and Liangshan stood directly in their path. When they crossed into the county, the garrison at White Rabbit Mountain broke and fled. With a hundred men posted on a small hill as a feint, Fang Ji kept the rebels from pressing forward. He fortified more than two hundred stockades and organized the people to garrison their own defenses. Some three hundred thousand refugees from neighboring counties rallied to him; the rebels found nothing left to loot, and he repeatedly routed them with surprise sorties. The strategy of scorched earth and fortified strongpoints appears to have begun at Liangshan. When rebels broke out at Baoling Temple in Wan County, he crossed into the district and put them down. He then helped the main force destroy Wu Wenxiang at Shiba Mountain, drove Lin Liangong back at Wangniuya, killed Lin's younger brother Tingsxiang, and received peacock feather insignia. Promoted to prefect of Ningyuan, he nevertheless stayed on at Liangshan for four years. By the sixth year of Jiaqing, rebel bands on the main routes were being subdued; he was moved to Kuizhou and succeeded Liu Qing as intendant of Jianchang. When the raw tribes of Liangshan rose in revolt, he led a campaign that brought them back under control. Not long afterward, Suonuomugendeng, chief native official of Litang, murdered his deputy, seized the official seal, and trapped Deputy General De Ning's force. Fang Ji rode in alone, secretly briefed the former headman Xilagongbu on his plan, and broke the usurper with Xilagongbu's men. After serving as intendant of Chuanbei and of the Salt-and-Tea Circuit, he was promoted to surveillance commissioner. When tribes around Mabian and Emeiling joined Liangshan raw tribes in border raids, Fang Ji and Regional Commander Feng Shen cut inward from Sanhekou at Mabian, took the Liuba Yi stronghold, slipped through a hidden route among the Chi Yi, and struck twelve Ling Yi settlements. In less than two weeks, he won every engagement. Ququwu backed the rebels and fought to the last; Fang Ji sent a hidden force around their rear and wiped them out. He was promoted to financial commissioner.

積官四川二十餘年，馳驅殆遍，山川風土，了然於胸，用兵輒獨當一面。 及任籓司，僚屬多故交，一無瞻徇。 清節自勵，​​尤為時稱。 卒於官，祀名宦。 硃爾漢，字麗江，順天大興人。 少為戶部吏。 乾隆中，官甘肅靖遠典史，母憂去官。 服闋待次，時平涼回酋田五作亂，爾漢與通判吳廷芳、知縣黃家駒守靖遠城，賊來攻。 靖遠回豪哈得城等期夜半為內應，爾漢得其情，令守者悉登城不得下，至哈得成家，陽科其穀餉軍，因拘之； 分遣人誘擒城下賊，賊之雜守者在城上已數十人，縣役鐵光保最為劇賊，猝擒之。 角聲起，扼城上賊無脫者，外賊覺，遂引去。 由是以知兵聞，擢隆德知縣。 徙底店砦降回，擢涇州直隸州知州。 擒教匪劉松，擢鞏昌知府。
Fang Ji spent more than twenty years in Sichuan, riding until he had seen nearly every corner of the province; he knew its terrain and people by heart, and in war he was repeatedly entrusted with an independent command. As provincial treasurer, he had many old friends among his staff, yet he never showed them partiality. He held himself to strict integrity, for which contemporaries especially praised him. He died in office and was entered in the temple of eminent local officials. Zhu Erhan, styled Lijiang, came from Daxing in Shuntian. As a young man he served as a clerk in the Ministry of Revenue. Under Qianlong he held the post of Jingyuan jail warden in Gansu, then resigned to observe mourning for his mother. After his mourning period ended and he was awaiting a new appointment, the Hui chieftain Tian Wu of Pingliang rebelled. Zhu Erhan, Subprefect Wu Tingfang, and Magistrate Huang Jiaju held Jingyuan when the rebels attacked. Hade Cheng, a leading Hui figure in Jingyuan, and others had plotted a midnight uprising from within. Zhu Erhan uncovered the plan, kept every defender on the walls, went to Hade Cheng's house under the pretense of requisitioning grain for the garrison, and seized him. He sent parties to lure and capture rebels below the walls. Dozens of infiltrators were already on the ramparts; Tie Guangbao, a county runner and one of the worst rebels, was taken in a sudden rush. At the signal horn, the men on the walls closed on the infiltrators and none escaped; the rebels outside realized the plot had failed and withdrew. This won him a reputation as a capable commander, and he was promoted to magistrate of Longde. After resettling surrendered Hui from Didian Stockade, he was promoted to magistrate of the directly administered prefecture of Jingzhou. He captured the sect rebel Liu Song and was raised to prefect of Gongchang.
4
嘉慶元年，教匪起，蔓延三省。 二年，四川賊尤熾，總統宜綿駐達州，檄爾漢參軍事。 是時王三槐踞方山坪，白岩山者，地險固，賊渠林亮功、樊人傑屯山上，與方山坪為聲援。 將軍舒亮、提督穆克登布屯山前之韓彭坳，爾漢兵三百、鄉勇三千屯山後之排亞口。 排亞口之上曰金鳳觀，曰草店，曰鴨坪，一日盡攻克之。 復進，有木柵當隘，不見賊，惟以犬守。 兵躍攀柵，賊自崖旁斫傷之，鳴鑼掣旗，左右賊大至，爾漢慮斷後路，退師。 先是與韓彭坳諸師為期，中道而止，賊得專力山後，故不克。 既而奉節賊千餘來援，敗之，擒賊渠邱廣福。 岩賊久困欲走，傾巢來犯，戰一晝夜不得路，仍退。 爾漢攻之三閱月，博戰被創，乃回鞏昌。
In the first year of Jiaqing, sect rebels rose and the rebellion spread across three provinces. In the second year, the rebellion burned hottest in Sichuan; Grand Coordinator Yimian, stationed at Dazhou, called Zhu Erhan to join the campaign. Wang Sanhuai held Fangshanping, while White Rock Mountain — rugged and easily defended — was held by rebel chiefs Lin Liangong and Fan Renjie in mutual support with Fangshanping. General Shuliang and Regional Commander Mukedengbu took up position at Hanpeng'ao on the mountain's front; Zhu Erhan, with three hundred regular troops and three thousand militia, blocked Paiyakou on the rear. Above Paiyakou lay Jinfengguan, Caodian, and Yaping; he took all three in one day. Pressing forward, he found a wooden barricade across the defile; no rebels were visible — only dogs on watch. His men scrambled up the palisade; rebels hacked at them from the cliffs, gongs rang and flags went up, and enemy forces swarmed in from both sides. Fearing encirclement, Zhu Erhan pulled back. He had arranged a joint attack with the Hanpeng'ao column, but halted midway; the rebels were therefore free to throw their full strength against the mountain's rear, and the assault failed. When more than a thousand rebels from Fengjie marched to relieve the garrison, he routed them and captured the rebel chief Qiu Guangfu. The mountain rebels, long trapped and desperate to break out, attacked in full strength; after a day and night of fighting with no escape, they fell back again. Zhu Erhan pressed the siege for three months, was wounded in heavy fighting, and then returned to Gongchang.
5
三年，運麥十萬石餉軍，行至成縣，賊渠高均德來奪，敗之於格樓壩，擒其黨李德勝。 四年，張漢潮犯秦州，爾漢赴成縣會剿。 鞏昌警至，馳還，賊已據城東鴛鴦河，夜掠賊卡而入，城守始固，以功擢鞏秦階道。 生番鐵布者，居西傾山中，眾十餘萬，乘教匪猖獗，時出盜內地。 爾漢以鐵布未叛亂，且地險，一構兵非數年不能平。 鐵布奉回教，乃召其阿渾諭之，於是來首者踵至。 一日書姓名一紙，曰：此鐵布黨也。 又出一圖，曰：盜巢及要隘盡於此。 分遣百餘人捕之，悉就擒，鐵布遂定。 六年，川、楚、陝賊漸蹙，餘賊多竄甘肅，率兵扼剿，凡數十戰皆捷。 八年，甘肅匪平，上功最，賜花翎。
In the third year, while hauling one hundred thousand shi of wheat to the army, he reached Cheng County; rebel chief Gao Junde tried to seize the grain, but Zhu Erhan defeated him at Gelouba and captured his lieutenant Li Desheng. In the fourth year, when Zhang Hanchao struck at Qinzhou, Zhu Erhan went to Cheng County to join the joint suppression. Word came that Gongchang was threatened, and he raced back. Rebels had already taken Yuanyang River east of the city; he slipped in by night through their outposts, stabilized the defense, and for this service was promoted intendant of the Gong-Qin-Jie Circuit. The raw tribesman Tiebu lived in the Western Qinling Mountains with a following of more than one hundred thousand; while sect rebels ran wild, his people often raided the interior. Zhu Erhan reasoned that Tiebu had not yet openly rebelled and that, in such rugged country, once war began it would take years to finish. Tiebu had embraced Islam; Zhu Erhan summoned the Muslim religious leader to counsel the people, and surrenders soon poured in one after another. One day he wrote out a list of names and said, "These are Tiebu's partisans." He then produced a map and said, "Every rebel nest and key pass is marked on this." He sent out more than a hundred men to arrest them; all were captured, and the Tiebu uprising was thus pacified. In the sixth year, as the rebels in Sichuan, Hubei, and Shaanxi were increasingly cornered, most of the survivors fled into Gansu. He led troops to intercept and suppress them, winning every one of several dozen engagements. In the eighth year, the rebels in Gansu were suppressed; his achievements were ranked first, and he was awarded the peacock feather.
6
爾漢有識斷，能得人死力，奴客悉以兵法部之。 自出仕即在行間，後遂與教匪相終始。 用兵有法，所用鄉勇侯達海，侍衛李榮華，武舉劉養鵬，千總鄒坤、桂攀桂皆操刺勇健善戰，故所至有功。 尋調廣東肇羅道，擢廣西按察使，署布政使。 十二年，卒於官。 楊頀，字邁功，江西金谿人。 乾隆四十九年進士，授刑部主事。 總辦秋審，執法平。 內監訟其弟妻，頀按律杖贖守夫墓。 和珅方總刑部，意有所徇，駁詰之，頀面爭。 和珅叱曰：「司員敢爾！ 」頀厲聲曰：「司員主稿，知為刑獄得其平耳！ 何叱為？ 」和珅不能奪。 及珅敗，擢員外郎。 仁宗召見，嘉其有守，命解餉四十萬兩赴四川濟軍。 川、陝大吏交章論薦，授陝西延榆綏道。 時三省清釐叛產，撫卹難民，事方殷，詔責疆吏慎選公正大員如《音蒦》及劉清者任其事。 頀週曆田野，綜覈不苟，民漸復業。 巡撫秦承恩檄府縣募民補伍，頀曰：「農工商賈各有其業，若預選送營，曠日失業，與抽丁何殊？ 」議乃寢。 調甘肅平慶涇固鹽法道。
Zhu Erhan was shrewd and resolute, could inspire men to fight to the death for him, and drilled all his retainers and servants according to military tactics. From the start of his career he served in the field, and he remained engaged with the White Lotus rebels throughout. He employed sound military methods, and the men he relied on—the militia leader Hou Dahai, bodyguard Li Ronghua, military licentiate Liu Yangpeng, and company commanders Zou Kun and Gui Pangui—were all expert with arms, brave, hardy, and skilled in combat, so he won success wherever he campaigned. He was soon transferred to the Zhaoluo Circuit in Guangdong, promoted to Guangxi Surveillance Commissioner, and appointed acting Provincial Administration Commissioner. In the twelfth year, he died in office. Yang Hu, courtesy name Maigong, was a native of Jinxi, Jiangxi. In the forty-ninth year of the Qianlong reign he passed the jinshi examination and was appointed a principal clerk in the Ministry of Punishments. As overseer of the autumn judicial review, he applied the law fairly. When a palace eunuch sued his brother's wife, Yang Hu, following the law, sentenced her to caning with redemption for failing to guard her husband's tomb. Heshen was then in charge of the Ministry of Punishments and wanted to show favor; he overturned the ruling and pressed him, and Yang Hu argued with him directly. Heshen rebuked him, saying, "How dare a clerk behave like this!" Yang Hu replied sharply, "As the drafting clerk, I know only that justice in criminal cases must be even!" "Why rebuke me?" Heshen could not prevail against him. After Heshen's downfall, he was promoted to vice director. Emperor Renzong received him in audience, commended his steadfastness, and ordered him to transport four hundred thousand taels of silver to Sichuan to supply the army. The senior officials of Sichuan and Shaanxi submitted joint memorials recommending him, and he was appointed Intendant of the Yan-Yu-Sui Circuit in Shaanxi. At that time the three provinces were clearing rebel estates and providing relief to war victims, and the task was urgent; an edict admonished frontier officials to select carefully upright senior officials such as Yang Hu and Liu Qing to handle it. Yang Hu toured the countryside, investigated matters thoroughly and without slackness, and the people gradually returned to their livelihoods. Governor Qin Chengen ordered the prefectures and counties to recruit civilians to fill military quotas; Yang Hu said, "Farmers, artisans, merchants, and traders each have their own trade; if they are chosen in advance and sent to camp, they lose their livelihoods for days on end—how is that different from conscription?" The plan was then dropped. He was transferred to serve as Salt Controller of the Ping-Qing-Jing-Gu Circuit in Gansu.
7
嘉慶九年，擢安徽按察使，捕六安州匪劉成巨置諸法。 十三年，遷江寧布政使。 淮、揚大水，乘舴艋歷災區訪問疾苦，渡湖幾覆，災黎感之。 尋以失察山陽知縣王仲漢冒賑，坐褫職。 詔頀查賑認真，平日實心辦事，留河工效力。 復起用，歷淮海道、浙江按察使、江蘇布政使。 二十二年，擢浙江巡撫。 未幾，坐臨海民毆差釀大獄，降四品京堂； 復不俟代去任，降禮部郎中。 引疾歸。 道光五年，重宴鹿鳴，加四品卿銜。 卒，年八十五。 廖寅，字亮工，四川鄰水人。 乾隆四十四年舉人。 家貧，不能常試禮部，十二年中，僅再至都。 以大挑知縣官河南，署葉縣。 時教匪方熾，葉當衝，寅撫民不擾。 民有從逆者，捕其魁乃定。 長子思芳有武略，省父至葉，任以守衛事。 詔捕教首劉之協，久不獲。 一日，思芳巡歷近郊，見二人縶馬坐樹下語，異之，歸戒門者伺狀。 俄二人入城飲肆中，有識之者，其一即之協。 寅趣思芳往與雜坐，出不意縛之，鞫得實，​​械至都伏法。 特擢江蘇鎮江知府。 濬丹陽九曲河，築徬，以時啟閉，民便之。 擢江西吉南贛道，兼筦關榷，正稅外無多取，吏胥奉法。 會南昌煽亂，捕首惡置法。 安遠复亂，單騎往諭，解散黨與，耆民等縛其魁以獻，事遂平。 歷署布政使、按察使。 嘉慶十六年，遷兩淮鹽運使。 卹灶丁，治私梟，鹽課漸增。 河北滑縣教匪起，總督百齡檄寅往徐州協守禦。 會捕逆匪劉第五，誤系同姓名者，坐失察降調，上念其擒劉之協功，許捐復原職。 以老病歸，遂卒。
In the ninth year of the Jiaqing reign he was promoted to Anhui Surveillance Commissioner and captured the Lu'an bandit Liu Chengju, bringing him to justice. In the thirteenth year he was transferred to Jiangning Provincial Administration Commissioner. When Huai and Yangzhou were struck by major floods, he traveled through the stricken areas in a small boat to inquire into the people's suffering; crossing a lake he nearly overturned, and the flood victims were deeply moved. Soon afterward he was stripped of his rank for failing to detect that Shanyang Magistrate Wang Zhonghan had misappropriated relief funds. An edict noted that Yang Hu had investigated relief conscientiously and had always worked earnestly, and he was retained to serve on river conservancy. Reinstated, he served in succession as Huai-Hai Circuit Intendant, Zhejiang Surveillance Commissioner, and Jiangsu Provincial Administration Commissioner. In the twenty-second year he was promoted to Governor of Zhejiang. Not long afterward, after Linhai residents beat government runners and a major case erupted, he was demoted to a fourth-rank capital official. Again, without waiting for his replacement, he left his post and was demoted to Director in the Ministry of Rites. He pleaded illness and retired home. In the fifth year of the Daoguang reign, at the re-gathering banquet for provincial graduates, he was granted the rank of fourth-rank ministerial official. He died at the age of eighty-five. Liao Yin, courtesy name Lianggong, was a native of Linshui, Sichuan. In the forty-fourth year of the Qianlong reign he passed the provincial examination. His family was poor, and he could not regularly travel to the capital for the metropolitan examination; in twelve years he went to the capital only twice. Through the great selection he was appointed a magistrate in Henan and served as acting magistrate of Ye County. At that time the White Lotus rebels were rampant; Ye County lay on the front line, and Liao Yin governed the people without harassing them. When locals joined the rebellion, he captured the ringleaders, and peace was restored. His eldest son Sifang had military ability; he came to Ye to visit his father and was put in charge of security. An imperial order called for the capture of the rebel leader Liu Zhixie, but he remained at large for a long time. One day Sifang patrolled the outskirts and saw two men tether their horses and sit talking under a tree; finding this suspicious, he returned and told the gatekeepers to watch for such men. Soon the two entered the city and drank at a tavern; someone recognized one of them—he was Liu Zhixie himself. Liao Yin urged Sifang to come and sit casually with Liu Zhixie; they then seized him by surprise, and after interrogation confirmed the facts, sent him in shackles to the capital, where he was executed. He was specially promoted to prefect of Zhenjiang, Jiangsu. He dredged the Jiuchu River at Danyang, built sluice embankments, and regulated their opening and closing by season, to the people's benefit. He was promoted to Jinan-Gan Circuit intendant in Jiangxi and concurrently managed customs levies; he collected nothing beyond the regular tax, and clerks and runners obeyed the law. When rebellion was stirred up in Nanchang, he arrested the ringleaders and punished them according to law. When Anyuan rebelled again, he rode there alone to reason with the rebels, dispersed the gang, and local elders bound their leader and handed him over; the disturbance was then pacified. He served in succession as acting provincial treasurer and judicial commissioner. In the sixteenth year of the Jiaqing reign, he was appointed Salt Transport Commissioner of Lianghuai. He looked after the salt-furnace workers, cracked down on illegal salt traders, and salt revenue gradually rose. When White Lotus rebels rose in Huaxian, Hebei, Governor-General Bailing ordered Liao Yin to Xuzhou to help with the defense. While trying to capture the rebel Liu Diwu, he mistakenly detained a namesake; he was demoted for negligence, but the emperor, recalling his service in capturing Liu Zhixie, allowed him to buy his way back to his former rank. He retired home because of old age and illness, and soon died.
8
思芳少時居鄉治團練，從軍數有功，官至江蘇候補道。 在葉手擒劉之協，名聞天下。 後以捕劉第五獲罪下獄，尋赦之。 陳昌齊，字賓臣，廣東海康人。 乾隆三十六年進士，選庶吉士，授編修，累遷中允。 大學士和珅欲羅致之，昌齊以非掌院，無晉謁禮，不往。 大考，左遷編修。 尋授御史，遷給事中。
In his youth Sifang organized local militia in his home district; he won repeated distinctions in military service and rose to acting circuit intendant of Jiangsu. At Ye County he personally captured Liu Zhixie, and his name became known throughout the empire. Later he was imprisoned for the Liu Diwu affair, but was soon pardoned. Chen Changqi, courtesy name Binchen, was a native of Haikang, Guangdong. In the thirty-sixth year of the Qianlong reign he passed the jinshi examination, entered the Hanlin Academy as a bachelor, was appointed reviser, and was successively promoted to middle attendant. Grand Secretary Heshen tried to win him over, but Changqi, saying he was not the academy director and had no duty to pay a formal call, refused to go. At the grand examination he was demoted to reviser. He was soon appointed censor and then promoted to supervising secretary.
9
昌齊生海邦，習洋盜情狀。 上疏論剿捕事，略曰：「洋匪上岸，率不過一二百人，陸居會匪助兇行劫。 沿海居民皆採捕為生，習拳勇，諳水勢，匪以利誘，往往從匪。 可以為盜，即可用以捕盜。 宜令地方官明示，有能出洋剿捕，或遇匪上岸，殲擒送官驗實者，船物一概充賞。 被誘從匪者，能擒盜連船投首，免罪。 則兵力所未及，丁壯亦必圖賞力捕。 仍令地方各官稽戶口，編保甲，以清其源。 於各埠訪拏濟匪糧物，各市鎮嚴緝代匪銷贓，俾絕水陸勾通之路。 庶幾洋面肅清，地方寧謐。 」
Changqi came from the coast and was familiar with the ways of sea pirates. He submitted a memorial on suppression and capture, saying in summary: "When sea bandits come ashore, they usually number no more than one or two hundred men, with local secret-society bandits on land helping them rob and kill. Coastal people live by fishing and hunting; they are trained in boxing and know the waters well, and bandits often lure them with profit into joining the gangs. Men who can serve as pirates can also be used to catch them. Local officials should announce clearly that anyone who goes to sea to hunt bandits, or who kills or captures bandits when they come ashore and delivers them to the authorities for verification, shall receive the ships and goods in full as reward. Those who were lured into following the bandits, if they capture pirates together with their ships and surrender, shall be pardoned. Then even where troops cannot reach, able-bodied men will surely exert themselves for the reward and capture bandits by force. Officials should also be ordered to check household registers and organize the baojia system, so as to cut off the source of the trouble. At every port they should hunt down those who supply bandits with food and goods; at every market town they should strictly arrest those who dispose of stolen goods for the bandits, so as to sever the links between land and sea. In this way the seas may be cleared and the region restored to peace. "
10
嘉慶九年，出為浙江溫處道。 時海寇蔡牽肆擾，昌齊修戰艦，簡軍伍，募人出海繪浙、閩海洋全圖，纖悉備具。 每牒報賊情及道里遠近稍有虛妄，必指斥之。 與提督李長庚深相結納，俾無掣肘，鞫海盜必詳盡得其情。 德楞泰奉命按閱閩、浙，議申海禁，謂不數月盜可盡斃。 昌齊曰：「環海居民耕而食者十之五，餘皆捕魚為業。 若禁其下海，數万漁戶無以為生，激變之咎誰任之？ 」德楞泰改容稱善。 在任五年，以鞫獄遲延，部議鐫級。 江南、福建大吏闢調，皆不往。 歸里，主雷陽粵秀講席。 修通志。 考據詳覈，著書終老焉。 硃爾賡額，原名友桂，字白泉，漢軍正紅旗人，裔出明代。 王父孝純，工詩古文，有異才，由四川知縣歷官至兩淮鹽運使。
In the ninth year of the Jiaqing reign he was appointed Wen-Chu Circuit intendant of Zhejiang. At that time the pirate Cai Qian was ravaging the coast; Changqi repaired warships, picked troops, and hired men to go to sea and draw complete charts of the Zhejiang and Fujian waters, down to the smallest detail. Whenever a dispatch reporting bandit movements or distances was even slightly inaccurate, he rebuked it. He formed a close alliance with Admiral Li Changgeng so that neither was hampered by the other, and when questioning pirates he always extracted the full truth. Delengtai was ordered to inspect Fujian and Zhejiang, proposed enforcing a sea ban, and said the pirates could all be wiped out within a few months. Changqi said: "Of the people along the coast, half live by farming and the rest live by fishing. If they are forbidden to put to sea, tens of thousands of fishing households will have no way to live — who will answer for the revolt that follows? " Delengtai's expression changed and he praised the point. After five years in office he was demoted one rank by the ministry for delays in trying cases. When senior officials in Jiangnan and Fujian solicited his transfer, he refused to go. He returned home and headed the lecture chair at the Leiyang Yuexiu Academy. He compiled the local gazetteer. His scholarship was meticulous, and he spent his old age writing books. Zhu Ergenge, original name Yougui, courtesy name Baiquan, was a Han Bannerman of the Plain Red Banner and a descendant of the Ming. His grandfather Xiaochun was accomplished in poetry and classical prose, possessed unusual talent, and rose from county magistrate in Sichuan to Salt Transport Commissioner of Lianghuai.
11
硃爾賡額納貲為兵部主事，充軍機章京，累遷郎中，出為江安糧道。 兩江總督蘇凌阿閽人為和珅舊奴，恣睢用事，廉得其狀，白而逐之。 從總督赴安徽察治劉之協逆黨，株連數百人，多所省釋。 署安徽布政使，引疾歸。 以母老乞改京秩，授戶部郎中。 和珅奴劉全之婿號檳榔蔣者，倚勢奪民產，訟於部，刑責不稍貸。 西賈利旗產，​​嗾言官疏陳，使得與漢民通售買，下部議，啖以重賄，卻之，持不可。 大學士硃珪管部，聞而重之。 故事，自告改京官，不外用。 珪薦其才守可大受，復出為廣東潮州知府。 海盜方張，硃濆尤黠悍，乃親歷海壖，治鄉團，調鎮兵千守沿海，斷內奸接濟。 濆糧絕，屢敗走台灣，潮盜膽落，因其窮蹙解散之。 盜魁黃茂高、許雲湘、王騰魁、楊勝廣、黃德東、關兆奎受撫，選其強幹者編入練勇。 會匪李崇玉踞惠、潮山谷中，時游弋海上，使降人招之自首，硃濆部眾亦有來投者。 會以母憂去，未竟其事，服闋，補雲南曲靖。
Zhu Ergenge bought appointment as a principal secretary in the Ministry of War, served as a Grand Council clerk, rose successively to department director, and was then appointed grain intendant of Jiang'an. Governor-General Su Ling'a of the Two Jiangs kept a favorite who had been an old slave of Heshen and abused his power; Zhu Ergenge investigated the matter, reported it, and had the man driven out. When he accompanied the governor-general to Anhui to investigate Liu Zhixie's rebel associates, although hundreds were implicated, he released many of them. After serving as acting provincial treasurer of Anhui, he pleaded illness and retired home. Citing his mother's advanced age, he petitioned for a capital post and was appointed a director in the Ministry of Revenue. Betel-nut Jiang, son-in-law of Liu Quan, a servant of Heshen, had used his connections to seize commoners' property. When the case came before the ministry, Zhu Ergenge showed no leniency in applying the law. Western merchants sought profit from banner lands and induced memorialists to propose opening trade with Han subjects. When the matter came to the ministry, they offered heavy bribes, which he refused, maintaining that the proposal could not be allowed. Grand Secretary Zhu Gui, who oversaw the ministry, heard of this and thought highly of him. Precedent held that officials who had voluntarily transferred to capital posts were not reassigned to provincial service. Zhu Gui recommended him as talented and upright enough for greater responsibility, and he was again posted as prefect of Chaozhou in Guangdong. With piracy on the rise and Zhu Fen proving especially cunning and fierce, he personally toured the coast, organized village militias, stationed a thousand garrison troops along the shore, and severed supply lines from collaborators within. When Zhu Fen's supplies ran out and he was repeatedly defeated and driven to Taiwan, the pirates of Chaozhou lost heart; Zhu Ergenge took advantage of their plight to break up their bands. The pirate leaders Huang Maogao, Xu Yunxiang, Wang Tengkui, Yang Shengguang, Huang Dedong, and Guan Zhaokui submitted to pacification; their strongest followers were selected and incorporated into militia units. The secret-society bandit Li Chongyu held the mountainous borderlands of Huizhou and Chaozhou and sometimes raided at sea. Zhu Ergenge sent former pirates to urge him to surrender, and some of Zhu Fen's followers also came over. He then left office to observe mourning for his mother before the work was finished; when the mourning period ended, he was assigned to Qujing in Yunnan.
12
嘉慶十四年，百齡為兩廣總督，疏請調硃爾賡額廣東，擢高廉道，署督糧道，剿匪事一以倚之。 勘海口砲台舊在山上，發砲輒從桅頂過，悉改建於山麓，屢碎盜艦，挫其鋒。 暫改運鹽由陸，撤紅單船入內港，以杜接濟。 戒並海郡縣嚴斷水米，如在潮州時。 匪勢漸蹙，用舊降人招郭學顯就撫。 未幾，鄭一妻與張保仔率眾逾萬泊虎門，要總督親至海口面議，文武噤莫敢決，硃爾賡額獨進曰：「保仔自知罪大，眾多無糧，拂其請，將死鬥。 請撤兵衛，單舟迳詣，諭以恩威，必可集事。 」先遣南海、番禺兩令往傳命，使熟籌而志堅。 翌晨，從百齡登舟，行四十里，見列艦數百，夾水如衢，舉砲迎，聲震城中。 請總督過舟，叱之曰：「保仔當泥首乞命，如仍驕肆遲疑，無死所矣！ 」迨晡，保仔登舟，請留三千人招西路賊烏石二，不聽則擒之以自贖，許之，給米千石慰遣。 保仔乃使餘眾登岸受撫，自起椗出洋。 群謂其所散皆罷弱，自留精銳，得米將不可製，笑應之曰：「此不必以口舌爭。 」至期，保仔果誘烏石二至高州，誅之。 海盜悉平，以功獲優敘，賜花翎。 尋調署南韶道。
In Jiaqing 14 (1809), Bailing became Governor-General of the Two Guangs and memorialized to have Zhu Ergenge transferred to Guangdong, promoting him to intendant of the Gaolian Circuit and acting grain commissioner, relying on him entirely for anti-piracy operations. He found that the Haikou batteries, built on the heights, sent shots sailing over enemy masts; he had them all relocated to the foothills, repeatedly smashing pirate vessels and blunting their offensive. Salt was temporarily moved overland, and licensed red-stamp shipping vessels were barred from inner harbors to cut off supplies to the pirates. He ordered coastal prefectures and counties to enforce the same strict embargo on food and freshwater that he had used in Chaozhou. As the rebels grew increasingly cornered, he employed former pirates to persuade Guo Xuexian to surrender. Soon Zheng Yi's widow and Zhang Baozai anchored more than ten thousand men at Humen and demanded that the governor-general come to Haikou in person. The civil and military officers were too frightened to decide. Zhu Ergenge alone stepped forward: "Baozai knows his crimes are grave and his men are starving. If we refuse, they will fight to the death. Withdraw the escort and go alone in a single boat to appeal to both mercy and force — that will surely settle it. " He first sent the magistrates of Nanhai and Panyu to deliver the message so they could prepare carefully and steel their resolve. The next morning he accompanied Bailing aboard ship. After forty li they saw hundreds of vessels massed on both sides of the channel like a street; the pirates fired salutes that shook the city. Bailing was invited aboard the pirates' ship, where Zhu Ergenge rebuked Zhang Baozai: "You should come begging for your life with dust on your head. If you remain arrogant and hesitate, you will have nowhere to die!" " By evening Zhang Baozai came aboard and asked to keep three thousand men to recruit the western pirate Wushi Er, offering to capture him if refused as proof of loyalty. This was granted, and a thousand shi of rice was given to provision and send them off. Zhang Baozai then sent the rest of his followers ashore to surrender while he himself weighed anchor and put to sea. Others said he had sent ashore only the weak while keeping his best men, and that the rice would make him impossible to control. Zhu Ergenge smiled and replied: "That is not worth arguing about. " When the time came, Zhang Baozai indeed lured Wushi Er to Gaozhou and killed him. With the pirates fully suppressed, he received distinguished commendation for his service and was awarded the peacock feather. He was soon transferred to serve as acting intendant of the Nanshao Circuit.
13
十六年，河決李家樓，特命百齡為兩江總督治河事，調硃爾賡額為江南鹽巡道。 至則佐百齡定計，接築洪澤湖口束清壩，逼溜刷深太平河，使水有所歸。 次年，李家樓決口合龍，新築格堤遏水與大堤平。 初，當事主守格堤​​，奉嚴旨，失守者從軍法。 至是見事危急，請改守大堤，聽河溜穿格堤而下，免旁洩之險。 又新築減壩受水攻，展側上游築斜壩挑水，數日壩根掛淤乃穩固。 所籌措工事悉合機宜。 葦蕩營久為弊藪，樵兵空額無人，營員領帑，臨時僱募，弁目專其利。 又為灘棍所持，盪料歸灘棍者十五六。 歸弁目者十二三，歸工用者十一二，歲僅得葦十數万束。 百齡檄硃爾賡額督治其事，乃請以盪地不產柴者給樵兵，人四十畝，給牛具籽種，建棚廠以居，盪始有兵。 濬溝渠便筏出入，採運始及遠，建衙署俾營員常年駐盪，民挾制偷竊者有禁，盪始有官。 受事之年，採足正額二百四十萬束。 於是灘棍之利盡失，廳員得料抵價，少所沾潤，皆不便之。 適有船兵中途改束，斤重不敷，八廳藉欲撼搖全局。 百齡悉其奸，偕河督察訊，硃爾賡額往勘定十七年新葦，每束箍口以二尺八寸為率，增舊三寸，估右營得葦八百萬束； 會署江寧布政使，未及估左營。 時河督陳鳳翔為百齡所劾，自訴於朝，命尚書松筠、侍郎初彭齡按訊，牽及葦蕩事。 廳員熒說，嗾驗尾幫，舟載餘葦九百束，據其重率，以衡已收三百萬束之數，斥為不足，遂被劾虛糜錢糧，苦累樵兵，遣戍伊犁。 時冤之。
In Jiaqing 16 (1811), the Yellow River broke at Lijialou. Bailing was specially appointed Governor-General of the Two Jiangs to handle river works, and Zhu Ergenge was transferred to salt patrol commissioner for Jiangnan. On arrival he helped Bailing plan construction, linking the Shuqing Dam at the Hongze Lake outlet to force the current to scour the Taiping River deeper so the water would have an outlet. The following year the Lijialou breach was closed, and the newly built containment dike held the water level even with the main embankment. At first officials were ordered under strict imperial command to hold the containment dike; anyone who lost it would face military punishment. Seeing the situation turn critical, he petitioned to shift defense to the main dike and allow the current to pass through the containment dike, avoiding the risk of a side breach. He also built new spillway dams to take the brunt of the water, extending upstream with slanted dikes to redirect the flow; after several days silt accumulated at the base and the works held firm. Every engineering measure he planned proved well suited to conditions. The reed marsh camps had long been riddled with corruption: authorized cutter posts sat empty while camp officers collected salaries, hired men on the spot, and noncommissioned officers kept the profits. Beach thugs also controlled the trade, taking fifteen or sixteen tenths of the marsh reed harvest. Sergeants took twelve or thirteen tenths, actual labor got eleven or twelve, and only a hundred thousand-odd bundles of reeds were harvested each year. Bailing ordered Zhu Ergenge to reform the system. He proposed granting non-productive marshland to cutters — forty mu each — with oxen, tools, and seed, and building barracks so the marshes finally had actual personnel. He dredged channels for raft traffic so harvesting could reach distant areas, built offices for year-round official presence, and curbed theft by locals — so the marshes finally had proper governance. In his first year in charge, the full quota of 2.4 million bundles was harvested. The beach thugs lost their profits; yamen staff who had skimmed from material payments found their perquisites cut — and all resented the change. Just then transport crews altered bundle sizes en route so weights fell short; officials from eight sub-offices seized the chance to try to overturn the whole reform. Bailing saw through the scheme and joined the river inspector in an inquiry. Zhu Ergenge went to set standards for the new reed harvest of year 17: bundle mouths at two chi eight cun, three cun larger than before; the right camp was estimated at eight million bundles. He was concurrently acting Jiangning financial commissioner and had not yet assessed the left camp. River governor Chen Fengxiang, whom Bailing had impeached, appealed to the throne. Songyun and Vice Minister Chu Pengling were sent to investigate, and the reed marsh affair was drawn into the case. Yamen staff spread rumors and instigated an inspection of the trailing convoy: a boat with nine hundred leftover bundles was weighed, and by that standard the three million bundles already collected were declared short. Zhu Ergenge was impeached for wasting public funds and burdening the cutters, and exiled to Yili. Many at the time considered this a miscarriage of justice.
14
硃爾賡額因百齡前劾鳳翔詞不盡實，獄無結正，原以身任，遂不辯。 在戍六年，放還，久之，卒。 查崇華，字九峰，安徽涇縣人。 少孤，遊福建傭書。 久之，福州將軍魁倫闢佐幕，甚見信任。 魁倫劾總督伍拉納、巡撫浦霖，即命署總督，治其獄。 閩地瘠苦，歷任大吏責供張無藝，所屬羅織大戶勒賄，民不堪命，至是貪酷之吏悉伏辜。 崇華名聞於時。 納貲為通判，留福建。
Zhu Ergenge felt that Bailing's earlier impeachment of Chen Fengxiang had not been fully truthful and the case remained unsettled; he had intended to accept responsibility himself and therefore did not defend himself. After six years in exile he was released; he died some time later. Cha Chonghua, styled Jiufeng, was a native of Jing County in Anhui. Orphaned as a boy, he went to Fujian and made a living copying books. In time Fujian Regional Commander Kuilun took him onto his staff, where he won deep trust. When Kuilun impeached Governor-General Wulana and Governor Pulín, he was at once appointed acting governor-general to try their cases. Fujian was poor and hard-pressed; successive governors had demanded lavish hospitality without limit, while subordinates framed wealthy families to extort bribes until the people could endure no more. Under Chonghua's prosecution, the most grasping officials were all punished. Chonghua's reputation spread at the time. He bought appointment as subprefect and stayed on in Fujian.
15
嘉慶十四年，海盜蔡牽平，以功賜花翎。 硃渥欲歸誠，未決，崇華隻身至海舶，諭以禍福，遂受降。 十七年，署台灣淡水同知。 高媽達妖言惑眾，捕獲，訊得劉林、祝現謀以次年閏八月望在京師舉事，四方起應之。 崇華牒請奏聞，大吏以其語不經，置之，僅以傳教罪誅高媽達。 至十八年九月十五日，果有林清、祝現之變，劉林者即林清別名也。 自高媽達伏法，福建匪黨巳解散，得無事。 尋以道員謁選，授河南南汝光道。 教匪鉅魁劉松久在逃，懸緝十餘載，偵知潛匿安徽宿州傳教，捕獲之。 母憂去官。
In Jiaqing 14, when the pirate Cai Qian was suppressed, he received the peacock feather for his service. Zhu Wo wished to surrender but hesitated; Chonghua went alone to his ship, explained the consequences, and won his submission. In the seventeenth year he served as acting subprefect of Tamsui in Taiwan. Gao Mada used sorcery to mislead the people. When captured and questioned, he revealed that Liu Lin and Zhu Xian planned an uprising in the capital on the full moon of the intercalary eighth month of the following year, with coordinated risings across the realm. Chonghua memorialized urging that the court be informed, but senior officials dismissed his report as incredible and executed Gao Mada only for illegal preaching. On the fifteenth day of the ninth month of the eighteenth year, the uprising of Lin Qing and Zhu Xian broke out exactly as foretold; Liu Lin was in fact Lin Qing's alias. After Gao Mada was executed, Fujian's rebel bands had already broken up, and the province remained at peace. Soon afterward, as a circuit intendant awaiting assignment, he was appointed to the Nanyu-Guang Circuit in Henan. The rebel chief Liu Song had been at large for more than a decade. Intelligence placed him secretly preaching in Suzhou, Anhui, and Chonghua captured him. He resigned to observe mourning for his mother.
16
道光二年，補陝西鳳邠道。 值大軍徵張格爾，調駐嘉峪關治軍需。 自川、楚軍興，將吏習於糜費，崇華一主覈實，以內地馬駝出關不耐寒苦，關外有台站應付，長僱徒糜芻秣，悉罷之，節帑甚鉅。 凡三署按察使，治獄明慎。 以老乞歸，卒。
In Daoguang 2 he was appointed to the Feng-Bin Circuit in Shaanxi. When the main force marched against Zhang Ge'er, he was posted at Jiayuguan to manage military supplies. Since the Sichuan-Hubei campaigns, officers had grown used to extravagance; Chonghua insisted on strict accounting. Horses and camels from the interior could not endure the cold beyond the pass, where relay stations should provide transport, so he abolished long-term hiring and wasteful fodder contracts, saving enormous sums from the treasury. He served three times as acting surveillance commissioner and handled cases with clarity and care. He pleaded old age, retired home, and died.
17
論曰：剿平教匪，不獨賴將帥戮力，一時守土之吏，與有勞焉。 最顯者為四川劉清，而方積亦倡行堅壁清野，保障一方，後復屢定番亂，蜀人與清並稱。 他如硃爾漢之保鞏昌，楊頀之清叛產、撫難民，廖寅之擒劉之協，皆卓有建樹。 陳昌齊、硃爾賡額於治海寇並具謀略，而硃爾賡額功尤顯矣。 查崇華預發林清逆謀，為疆臣所格； 及筦西征軍需，以撙節稱，故同著於篇。
The commentary observes: Suppressing the White Lotus rebels did not depend on generals alone; local officials of the day also bore their share of the burden. The most celebrated was Liu Qing of Sichuan, but Fang Ji also pioneered the scorched-earth and fortified-defense strategy that secured Liangshan, and later repeatedly pacified tribal uprisings; Sichuan people ranked him alongside Liu Qing. Others too made outstanding marks: Zhu Erhan's defense of Gongchang, Yang Hu's clearing of rebel estates and relief of war victims, and Liao Yin's capture of Liu Zhixie. Chen Changqi and Zhu Ergenge both showed strategic talent against sea pirates, though Zhu Ergenge's achievements were especially striking. Cha Chonghua had forewarned of Lin Qing's conspiracy but was overruled by provincial governors; when he later managed supplies for the western campaigns, he was praised for thrift, and is therefore included in this chapter.
