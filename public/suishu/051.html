1
長孫覽長孫覽，字休因，河南洛陽人也。 祖稚，魏太師、假黃鉞、上党文宣王。 父紹遠，周小宗伯、上党郡公。 覽性弘雅，有器量，略涉書記，尤曉鐘律。 魏大統中，起家東宮親信。 周明帝時，為大都督。 武帝在籓，與覽親善，及即位，彌加禮焉，超拜車騎大將軍。 每公卿上奏，必令省讀。 覽有口辯，聲氣雄壯，凡所宣傳，百僚屬目，帝每嘉歎之。 覽初名善，帝謂之曰：「朕以萬機，委卿先覽。 」遂賜名焉。 及誅宇文護，以功進封薛國公。 其後曆小司空。 從平齊，進位柱國，封第二子寬管國公。 宣帝時，進位上柱國、大司徒，俄曆同、涇二州刺史。 高祖為丞相，轉宜州刺史。
Zhangsun Lan, whose style name was Xiuyin, came from Luoyang in Henan. His grandfather Zhi had served as Grand Preceptor of Wei, held the ceremonial Yellow Battle-Ax, and been enfeoffed as Prince Wenxuan of Shangdang. His father Shaoyuan had been Zhou Minor Director of the Ancestral Temple and Duke of Shangdang. Lan was magnanimous and refined by nature, possessed breadth of character, had some training in clerical work, and was especially expert in pitch pipes and musical regulation. During the Great Concord era of Western Wei he began his career as a trusted attendant in the Eastern Palace. Under Emperor Ming of Zhou he served as Grand Commander. While still a prince, Emperor Wu had been on close terms with Lan; once he took the throne he honored him all the more and promoted him straightaway to Grand General of Chariots and Cavalry. Whenever senior officials submitted memorials, the emperor always had Lan review them first. Lan was a gifted speaker with a powerful, resonant voice; whenever he delivered the emperor's pronouncements the entire court watched him, and the emperor often expressed his admiration. Lan had originally been named Shan. The emperor told him, "I am entrusting the myriad affairs of state to you for prior review." " The emperor thereupon bestowed upon him the name Lan. After Yuwen Hu was executed, he was promoted for his service and enfeoffed as Duke of Xue. He later served as Junior Minister of Works. He took part in the conquest of Qi, was promoted to Pillar of State, and enfeoffed his second son Kuan as Duke of Guan. Under Emperor Xuan he was promoted to Senior Pillar of State and Grand Minister of Education, and soon afterward served in turn as inspector of Tong and Jing provinces. When Yang Jian was still Chancellor, Lan was transferred to serve as inspector of Yi province.
2
開皇二年，將有事於江南，征為東南道行軍元帥，統八總管出壽陽，水陸俱進。 師臨江，陳人大駭。 會陳宣帝卒，覽欲乘釁遂滅之，監軍高熲以禮不伐喪而還。 上常命覽與安德王雄、上柱國元諧、李充、左僕射高熲、右衛大將軍虞慶則、吳州總管賀若弼等同宴，上曰：「朕昔在周朝，備展誠節，但苦猜忌，每致寒心。 為臣若此，竟何情賴？ 朕之於公，義則君臣，恩猶父子。 朕當與公共用終吉，罪非謀逆，一無所問。 朕亦知公至誠，特付太子，宜數參見之，庶得漸相親愛。 柱臣素望，實屬於公，宜識朕意。 」其恩禮如此。 又為蜀王秀納覽女為妃。 其後以母憂去職。 歲餘，起令復位。 俄轉涇州刺史，所在並有政績。 卒官。 子洪嗣。 仕曆宋順臨三州刺史、司農少卿、北平太守。 從子熾熾字仲光，上党文宣王稚之曾孫也。 祖裕，魏太常卿、冀州刺史。 父兕，周開府儀同三司、熊絳二州刺史、平原侯。 熾性敏慧，美姿儀，頗涉群書，兼長武藝。 建德初，武帝尚道法，尤好玄言，求學兼經史、善於談論者，為通道館學士。 熾應其選，與英俊並遊，通涉彌博。 建德二年，授雍州倉城令，尋轉盩啡令。 頻宰二邑，考績連最，遷崤郡守。 入為禦正上士。 高祖作相，擢為丞相府功曹參軍，加大都督，封陽平縣子，邑二百戶。 遷稍伯下大夫。 其年王謙反，熾從信州總管王長述溯江而上。 以熾為前軍，破謙一鎮，定楚、合等五州，擒偽總管荊山西元振，以功拜儀同三司。 及高祖受禪，熾率官屬先入清宮，即日授內史舍人、上儀同三司。 尋以本官攝判東宮右庶子，出入兩宮，甚被委遇。 加以處事周密，高祖每稱美之。 授左領軍長史，持節，使于東南道三十六州，廢置州郡，巡省風俗。 還授太子僕，加諫議大夫，攝長安令。 與大興令梁毗俱為稱職。 然毗以嚴正聞，熾以寬平顯，為政不同，部內各化。 尋領右常平監，遷雍州贊治，改封饒良縣子。 遷鴻臚少卿。 後數歲，轉太常少卿，進位開府儀同三司。 複持節為河南道二十八州巡省大使，于路授吏部侍郎。 大業元年，遷大理卿，複為西南道大使，巡省風俗。 擢拜戶部尚書。 吐谷渾寇張掖，令熾率精騎五千擊走之，追至青海而還，以功授銀青光祿大夫。 六年，幸江都宮，留熾于東都居守，仍攝左候衛將軍事。 其年卒官，時年六十二。 諡曰靜。 子安世，通事謁者。 熾弟晟晟字季晟，性通敏，略涉書記，善彈工射，趫捷過人。 時周室尚武，貴遊子弟鹹以相矜，每共馳射，時輩皆出其下。 年十八，為司衛上士。 初未知名，人弗之識也，唯高祖一見，深嗟異焉，乃攜其手而謂人曰：「長孫郎武藝逸群，適與其言，又多奇略。 後之名將，非此子邪？ 」
In Kaihuang year 2, as the court prepared operations in the south, he was appointed campaign commander of the Southeast Circuit, led eight area commanders out from Shouyang, and advanced by land and water together. When the army reached the Yangzi, Chen was thrown into alarm. When Emperor Xuan of Chen died, Lan wanted to exploit the occasion and destroy Chen outright, but the army supervisor Gao Feng cited the rule against attacking a state in mourning and ordered the withdrawal. The emperor often had Lan dine with Prince Xiong of Ande, Senior Pillar of State Yuan Xie, Li Chong, Left Vice Director Gao Feng, Right Guards General Yu Qingze, and Wu province area commander He Ruobi, and said to them, "In the Zhou court I gave my full loyalty, yet I was plagued by suspicion and constantly chilled at heart." To serve as a minister under such conditions—what loyalty could one count on? Between us, in duty we are ruler and ministers, yet in affection we are like father and sons. I intend to share lasting good fortune with you; unless the offense is outright treason, I shall question nothing. I know your utmost loyalty and have especially entrusted you to the Crown Prince; you should visit him often so that you may gradually grow close. The longstanding esteem owed a pillar of the state truly belongs to you; understand what I mean. " His favor and ceremony toward them were of this order. He also had Lan's daughter marry Prince Xiu of Shu as his consort. He later left office to observe mourning for his mother. After more than a year he was recalled to his post. He was soon transferred to inspector of Jing province, and everywhere he served he left a record of good governance. He died in office. His son Hong succeeded him. In office he served in turn as inspector of Song, Shun, and Lin provinces, as Vice Minister of Agriculture, and as administrator of Beiping. His nephew's son Chi, style name Zhongguang, was a great-grandson of Prince Wenxuan of Shangdang, Zhi. His grandfather Yu had been Wei Director of the Ancestral Temple and inspector of Ji province. His father Si had been Zhou Opener of the Gate with ceremony equal to the Three Excellencies, inspector of Xiong and Jiang provinces, and Marquis of Pingyuan. Chi was quick-witted by nature, handsome in bearing, widely read, and also skilled in martial arts. At the start of the Jiande era Emperor Wu favored Daoist practice and especially loved abstruse discourse; he sought men learned in both classics and histories who were skilled debaters and appointed them scholars of the Hall of Universal Passage. Chi was chosen for the post, kept company with the court's brightest minds, and broadened his learning still further. In Jiande year 2 he was appointed magistrate of Cangcheng in Yong province, and soon afterward was transferred to magistrate of Dangyin. After governing two districts in succession with top performance ratings, he was promoted to administrator of Xiao commandery. He entered the capital and became senior attendant of the Imperial Rectifier. When Yang Jian was Chancellor, Chi was promoted to staff officer of merit records in the chancellor's office, given the additional title of Grand Commander, and enfeoffed as Viscount of Yangping with a fief of two hundred households. He was transferred to junior grand master of the Office of the Regional Inspector. That year Wang Qian rebelled; Chi followed Xin province area commander Wang Changshu up the Yangzi. Chi served as vanguard, broke one of Qian's fortified posts, pacified Chu, He, and three other provinces, captured the rebel area commander Yuan Zhen of western Jingshan, and was appointed Acting Three Excellencies for his merit. When Yang Jian accepted the throne, Chi led the officials into the palace first to secure it; that same day he was appointed attendant of the Palace Secretariat and Senior Acting Three Excellencies. Soon, while retaining his original post, he was made acting Right Vice President of the Eastern Palace; moving between both palaces, he enjoyed great trust and favor. Because he handled affairs with meticulous care, Emperor Gaozu often praised him. He was appointed chief clerk of the Left Army of the Palace Guards and, bearing the staff of authority, was sent as envoy to the thirty-six provinces of the Southeast Circuit to reorganize prefectures and commanderies and inspect local customs. On his return he was appointed master of the household of the heir apparent, given the additional title of grand master of remonstrance, and served as acting magistrate of Chang'an. He and Liang Pi, magistrate of Daxing, were both regarded as exemplary officials. Pi was known for severity and rectitude, while Chi was noted for leniency and fairness; though their styles differed, each district was transformed under its magistrate. He soon took charge of the Right Office of Regular Granaries, was transferred to assistant administrator of Yong province, and was re-enfeoffed as Viscount of Raoliang. He was transferred to vice director of the Court for Diplomatic Reception. Several years later he became vice director of the Ancestral Temple and was promoted to Opener of the Gate with ceremony equal to the Three Excellencies. Again bearing the staff of authority, he served as touring envoy to the twenty-eight provinces of the Henan Circuit and was appointed vice director of the Ministry of Personnel en route. In Daye year 1 he became director of the Court of Judicial Review and again served as envoy of the Southwest Circuit to inspect local customs. He was promoted to Minister of the Household Registers. When the Tuyuhun raided Zhangye, Chi was ordered to lead five thousand elite cavalry to drive them off; he pursued them to Qinghai and returned, and was granted Silver-Gleaming Grand Master of Splendid Happiness for his service. In year 6 the emperor visited the Jiangdu Palace; Chi was left at the Eastern Capital to hold the city and also acted as commander of the Left Guards. He died in office that year, at the age of sixty-two. He was given the posthumous title Jing. His son Anshi served as attendant for diplomatic affairs. Chi's younger brother Sheng, style name Jisheng, was quick-witted by nature, had some clerical training, was skilled at archery, and was exceptionally agile. The Zhou court then prized martial prowess, and young nobles vied with one another at riding and archery; in such contests Sheng outclassed all his peers. At eighteen he became senior attendant of the Palace Guards. At first he was unknown, but Emperor Gaozu, upon meeting him once, was deeply impressed; taking him by the hand he said to those present, "This Zhangsun lad's martial skill is beyond the common run; in conversation just now I found he has many extraordinary stratagems as well." Surely the famous generals of the future will be this young man? "
3
宣帝時，突厥攝圖請婚于周，以趙王招女妻之。 然周與攝圖各相誇競，妙選驍勇以充使者，因遣晟副汝南公宇文神慶送千金公主至其牙。 前後使人數十輩，攝圖多不禮，見晟而獨愛焉，每共遊獵，留之竟歲。 嘗有二雕，飛而爭肉，因以兩箭與晟曰：「請射取之。 」晟乃彎弓馳往，遇雕相攫，遂一發而雙貫焉。 攝圖喜，命諸子弟貴人皆相親友，冀昵近之，以學彈射。 其弟處羅侯號突利設，尤得眾心。 而為攝圖所忌，密托心腹，陰與晟盟。 晟與之遊獵，因察山川形勢，部眾強弱，皆盡知之。 時高祖作相，晟以狀白高祖。 高祖大喜，遷奉車都尉。
Under Emperor Xuan the Türk leader Shetu sought a marriage alliance with Zhou, and the daughter of Prince Zhao was given to him in marriage. Zhou and Shetu each sought to outshine the other, so the bravest men were chosen as envoys; Sheng was sent as deputy to Duke Yuwen Shenqing of Runan to escort the Princess of a Thousand Gold to the Türk royal camp. Dozens of envoys had come before and after, and Shetu had usually treated them discourteously, but he took a special liking to Sheng, hunted with him often, and kept him for a full year. Once two eagles were fighting in the air over a piece of meat; Shetu handed Sheng two arrows and said, "Shoot them down for me." " Sheng bent his bow and galloped toward them; as the eagles grappled with each other he loosed one arrow and transfixed them both. Shetu was delighted and ordered all his sons, younger kinsmen, and nobles to befriend Sheng, hoping to draw close to him and learn archery. His younger brother Chulühou, titled Tuli shad, was especially popular among the tribes. Shetu envied him, however; he secretly entrusted his closest followers and formed a covert alliance with Sheng. While hunting with him, Sheng surveyed the terrain and assessed the strength of each tribal faction until he knew them all thoroughly. When Yang Jian was Chancellor, Sheng reported what he had learned to him. Yang Jian was greatly pleased and promoted him to commandant of the imperial carriages.
4
至開皇元年，攝圖曰：「我周家親也，今隋公自立而不能制，複何面目見可賀敦乎」？ 因與高寶寧攻陷臨渝鎮，約諸面部落謀共南侵。 高祖新立，由是大懼，修築長城，發兵屯北境，命陰壽鎮幽州，虞慶則鎮并州，屯兵數萬人以為之備。 晟先知攝圖、玷厥、阿波、突利等叔侄兄弟各統強兵，俱號可汗，分居四面，內懷猜忌，外示和同，難以力征，易可離間，因上書曰：「臣聞喪亂之極，必致升平，是故上天啟其機，聖人成其務。 伏惟皇帝陛下當百王之末，膺千載之期，諸夏雖安，戎場尚梗，興師致討，未是其時，棄於度外，又複侵擾。 故宜密運籌策，漸以攘之，計失則百姓不甯，計得則萬代之福。 吉凶所系，伏願詳思。 臣於週末，忝充外使，匈奴倚伏，實所具知。 玷厥之於攝圖，兵強而位下，外名相屬，內隙已彰，鼓動其情，必將自戰。 又處羅侯者，攝圖之弟，奸多而勢弱，曲取於眾心，國人愛之，因為攝圖所忌，其心殊不自安，跡示彌縫，實懷疑懼。 又阿波首鼠，介在其間，頗畏攝圖，受其牽率，唯強是與，未有定心。 今宜遠交而近攻，離強而合弱，通使玷厥，說合阿波，則攝圖回兵，自防右地。 又引處羅，遣連奚、霫，則攝圖分眾，還備左方。 首尾猜嫌，腹心離阻，十數年後，承釁討之，必可一舉而空其國矣。 」上省表大悅，因召與語。 晟複口陳形勢，手畫山川，寫其虛實，皆如指掌。 上深嗟異，皆納用焉。 因遣太僕元暉出伊吾道，使詣玷厥，賜以狼頭纛，謬為欽敬，禮數甚優。 玷厥使來，引居攝圖使上。 反間既行，果相猜貳。 授晟車騎將軍，出黃龍道，齎幣賜奚、霫、契丹等，遣為嚮導，得至處羅侯所，深布心腹，誘令內附。
In Kaihuang year 1 Shetu said, "I am kin to the Zhou house; now the Duke of Sui has proclaimed himself and I cannot restrain him—how can I face Kehedun?" He then joined Gao Baoning in capturing Linyu post and conspired with the tribal chiefs for a joint invasion of the south. The newly enthroned emperor was deeply alarmed; he repaired the Great Wall, sent troops to garrison the northern frontier, placed Yin Shou at You province and Yu Qingze at Bing province, and stationed tens of thousands of men in readiness. Sheng had long known that Shetu, Dianjue, Apo, Tuli, and their kinsmen each commanded strong forces, all styled themselves khaghans, and held the four quarters; inwardly suspicious yet outwardly united, they could not easily be conquered by force but could readily be set against one another. He therefore submitted a memorial: "I have heard that the depths of chaos must give way to peace; Heaven opens the opportunity, and the sage completes the work." Your Majesty stands at the end of an age of kings and has received a mandate for a new era; though the central domains are at peace, the frontier remains troubled; to raise armies for punitive campaigns is not yet timely, yet if left unchecked the barbarians will raid again. Secret stratagems should therefore be deployed to wear them down gradually; if the plan fails the people will know no peace, but if it succeeds it will secure blessings for ages to come. Fortune and disaster hang upon this; I humbly beg Your Majesty to consider it carefully. At the end of Zhou I had the honor of serving as an envoy abroad and came to know the Xiongnu factions and their shifting fortunes in full detail. Dianjue's forces are strong but his rank is subordinate to Shetu's; though nominally subject to him, their rift is already clear—stir his resentment and they will surely fight each other. Chulühou, Shetu's younger brother, is cunning but weak in power; he curries favor with the tribes and is beloved by them, and Shetu envies him for it; though outwardly conciliatory, he is inwardly uneasy and afraid. Apo, meanwhile, wavers between them; he fears Shetu, follows his lead, and sides with whichever faction is stronger—his loyalty is unsettled. We should befriend the distant and strike at the near, divide the strong and unite the weak, send envoys to Dianjue and win Apo over—then Shetu will have to turn his armies back to defend his western domains. Further, draw in Chulühou and ally with the Xi and Mohe tribes—then Shetu will have to divide his forces and guard his eastern flank. Once suspicion divides head from tail and the center falls apart, a punitive campaign launched a dozen years hence will surely empty their state in a single stroke. " The emperor was greatly pleased when he read the memorial and summoned Sheng to discuss it. Sheng again described the situation orally and sketched the terrain with his hand, laying out each faction's strengths and weaknesses as clearly as lines on his palm. The emperor marveled and adopted every recommendation. He therefore sent Master of the Stud Yuan Hui by the Yiwu route as envoy to Dianjue, bestowing a wolf-head banner and treating him with elaborate courtesy while feigning deep respect. When Dianjue's envoy arrived, he was seated above Shetu's envoy. Once the stratagem of alienation took effect, the Türk factions indeed turned suspicious and divided against one another. Sheng was appointed Grand General of Chariots and Cavalry and sent out by the Yellow Dragon route with gifts for the Xi, Mohe, Khitan, and others; using them as guides he reached Chulühou's camp, won his confidence, and induced him to submit.
5
二年，攝圖四十萬騎自蘭州入，至於周盤，破達奚長儒軍，更欲南入。 玷厥不從，引兵而去。 時晟又說染幹詐告攝圖曰：「鐵勒等反，欲襲其牙。 」攝圖乃懼，回兵出塞。
In year 2 Shetu led four hundred thousand cavalry in from Lanzhou to Zhoupan, defeated Daxi Changru's army, and prepared to drive farther south. Dianjue refused to follow and withdrew with his troops. Sheng also persuaded Tuli to send Shetu a false report that the Tiele had rebelled and were about to attack his royal camp. " Shetu was alarmed and withdrew his army beyond the frontier.
6
後數月，突厥大入，發八道元帥分出拒之。 阿波至涼州，與竇榮定戰，賊帥累北。 時晟為偏將，使謂之曰：「攝圖每來，戰皆大勝。 阿波才入，便即致敗，此乃突厥之恥，豈不內愧於心乎？ 且攝圖之與阿波，兵勢本敵。 今攝圖日勝，為眾所崇，阿波不利，為國生辱。 攝圖必當因以罪歸於阿波，成其夙計，滅北牙矣。 願自量度，能禦之乎？ 」阿波使至，晟又謂之曰：「今達頭與隋連和，而攝圖不能制。 可汗何不依附天子，連結達頭，相合為強，此萬全之計。 豈若喪兵負罪，歸就攝圖，受其戮辱邪？ 」阿波納之，因留塞上，使人隨晟入朝。 時攝圖與衛王軍遇，戰于白道，敗走至磧。 聞阿波懷貳，乃掩北牙，盡獲其眾而殺其母。 阿波還無所歸，西奔玷厥，乞師十余萬，東擊攝圖，複得故地，收散卒數萬，與攝圖相攻。 阿波頻勝，其勢益張。 攝圖又遣使朝貢，公主自請改姓，乞為帝女，上許之。
Several months later the Türks invaded in force, and eight circuit campaign commanders were dispatched to meet them. Apo reached Liangzhou and fought Dou Rongding, and the enemy leader was repeatedly driven back. Sheng, then a subordinate general, sent a messenger to tell Apo, "Whenever Shetu campaigns, he wins great victories." You have only just arrived and already been defeated—this is a disgrace to the Türks. Can you not feel ashamed at heart? Moreover, Shetu and Apo were originally military equals. Now Shetu wins victory after victory and is honored by the tribes, while your setbacks bring shame upon the Türk state. Shetu will surely shift the blame onto you, fulfill his long-standing plan, and destroy the Northern Court. Consider for yourself—can you withstand him? " When Apo's envoy arrived, Sheng told him further, "Tardu has now allied with Sui, and Shetu cannot control him." Why not attach yourself to the Son of Heaven and join with Tardu to grow strong together? That would be a plan of complete security. Surely that is better than losing your army, bearing guilt, returning to Shetu, and suffering his slaughter and humiliation? " Apo accepted the advice, remained on the frontier, and sent an envoy to follow Sheng to court. Shetu then met the army of the Prince of Wei at Baidao, was defeated, and fled into the desert. Learning that Apo was disloyal, he raided the Northern Court, seized all his followers, and killed Apo's mother. Apo had nowhere to turn, fled west to Dianjue, and borrowed more than a hundred thousand troops; marching east against Shetu he recovered his old territory, rallied tens of thousands of scattered soldiers, and fought Shetu back and forth. Apo won repeatedly and his power grew steadily. Shetu again sent tribute envoys; the princess asked to change her surname and become the emperor's daughter, and the emperor agreed.
7
四年，遣晟副虞慶則使於攝圖，賜公主姓為楊氏，改封大義公主。 攝圖奉詔，不肯起拜，晟進曰：「突厥與隋俱是大國天子，可汗不起，安敢違意。 但可賀敦為帝女，則可汗是大隋女婿，奈何無禮，不敬婦公乎？ 」攝圖乃笑謂其達官曰：「須拜婦公，我從之耳。 」於是乃拜詔書。 使還稱旨，授儀同三司、左勳衛車騎將軍。
In year 4 Sheng accompanied Yu Qingze on a mission to Shetu; the princess was given the surname Yang and re-enfeoffed as Princess Dayi. Shetu received the decree but refused to rise and bow; Sheng stepped forward and said, "The Türks and Sui are both great powers with Sons of Heaven; if the khan does not rise, how dare I go against his wishes?" But Kehedun is the emperor's daughter, so the khan is Great Sui's son-in-law—how can he be discourteous and fail to respect his father-in-law? " Shetu laughed and told his high officials, "I must bow to my father-in-law—I shall do as he says." " He thereupon bowed to the edict. When the mission returned with a favorable report, Sheng was appointed Acting Three Excellencies and grand general of chariots and cavalry of the Left Merit Guard.
8
七年，攝圖死，遣晟持節拜其弟處羅侯為莫何可汗，以其子雍閭為葉護可汗。 處羅侯因晟奏曰：「阿波為天所滅，與五六千騎在山谷間，伏聽詔旨，當取之以獻。 」乃召文武議焉。 樂安西元諧曰：「請就彼梟首，以懲其惡。 」武陽公李充曰：「請生將入朝，顯戮以示百姓。 」上謂晟曰：「於卿何如？ 」晟對曰：「若突厥背誕，須齊之以刑。 今其昆弟自相夷滅，阿波之惡，非負國家，因其困窮，取而為戮，恐非招遠之道，不如兩存之。 」上曰：「善。 」八年，處羅侯死，遣晟往吊，仍齎陳國所獻寶器以賜雍閭。
In year 7 Shetu died; Sheng was sent with the staff of authority to invest his younger brother Chulühou as Mohé khaghan and his son Yunglu as yabghu khaghan. Through Sheng, Chulühou reported, "Heaven has destroyed Apo; he lies hidden in the valleys with five or six thousand horsemen awaiting your decree—I shall seize him and present him to you." " The emperor summoned his civil and military officials to deliberate. Yuan Xie of Xi in Le'an said, "Let his head be taken there and displayed to punish his crimes." " Duke Li Chong of Wuyang said, "Bring him alive to court and execute him publicly as an example to the people." " The emperor asked Sheng, "What do you think?" " Sheng replied, "If the Türks turn treacherous, they must be punished." Now their brothers are destroying one another; Apo's crimes are not against our state—if we seize him in his distress and execute him, I fear that is not how to win over distant peoples; it would be better to let both survive. " The emperor said, "Good." In year 8 Chulühou died; Sheng was sent to offer condolences and also brought the precious vessels Chen had presented to bestow upon Yunglu.
9
十三年，流人楊欽亡入突厥，詐言彭公劉昶共宇文氏女謀欲反隋，稱遣其來，密告主。 雍閭信之，乃不修職貢。 又遣晟出使，微觀察焉。 公主見晟，乃言辭不遜，又遣所私胡人安遂迦共欽計議，扇惑雍閭。 晟至京師，具以狀奏。 又遣晟往索欽，雍閭欲勿與，謬答曰：「檢校客內，無此色人。 」晟乃貨其達官，知欽所在，夜掩獲之，以示雍閭，因發公主私事，國人大恥。 雍閭執遂迦等，並以付晟。 上大喜，加授開府，仍遣入籓，蒞殺大義公主。 雍閭又表請婚，僉議將許之。 晟又奏曰：「臣觀雍閭，反覆無信，特共玷厥有隙，所以依倚國家。 縱與為婚，終當必叛。 今若得尚公主，承藉威靈，玷厥、染幹必又受其徵發。 強而更反，後恐難圖。 且染幹者，處羅侯之子也，素有誠款，於今兩代。 臣前與相見，亦乞通婚，不如許之，招令南徙，兵少力弱，易可撫馴，使敵雍閭，以為邊捍。 」上曰：「善。 」又遣慰喻染幹，許尚公主。
In year 13 the exile Yang Qin fled to the Türks and falsely claimed that Duke Liu Chang of Peng and a Yuwen clanswoman were plotting rebellion against Sui and had sent him to report secretly to the khaghan. Yunglu believed him and stopped sending tribute. The emperor again sent Sheng on a mission to observe the situation discreetly. When the princess saw Sheng she spoke insolently; she also sent her private Hu retainer Ansuijia to plot with Qin and stir up Yunglu. When Sheng reached the capital he reported the full situation to the throne. The emperor again sent Sheng to demand Qin; Yunglu wished to refuse and falsely replied, "I have checked among my guests and there is no such person." " Sheng bribed Yunglu's high officials, learned Qin's whereabouts, seized him in a night raid, and presented him to Yunglu; he thereby exposed the princess's private affairs, and the Türks were deeply shamed. Yunglu arrested Suijia and the others and handed them all over to Sheng. The emperor was greatly pleased, gave him the additional title of Opener of the Gate, and again sent him to the frontier to preside over the execution of Princess Dayi. Yunglu again petitioned for a marriage alliance, and the court was about to approve it. Sheng memorialized again, "I observe that Yunglu is fickle and untrustworthy; he relies on our state only because he is at odds with Dianjue." Even if we grant him a marriage alliance, he will surely rebel in the end. If he now marries an imperial princess and borrows her prestige, Dianjue and Tuli will surely again be subject to his military summons. Once strengthened he will turn against us again, and he may later prove hard to control. Moreover Tuli is Chulühou's son and has shown sincere loyalty for two generations now. When I met him before he too asked for a marriage alliance; we should grant it, summon him to move south—his forces are few and weak and easily managed—and set him against Yunglu as a frontier shield. " The emperor said, "Good." The emperor again sent envoys to reassure Tuli and promised him an imperial princess in marriage.
10
十七年，染幹遣五百騎隨晟來逆女，以宗女封安義公主以妻之。 晟說染幹率眾南徙，居度斤舊鎮。 雍閭疾之，亟來抄略。 染幹伺知動靜，輒遣奏聞，是以賊來每先有備。
In year 17 Tuli sent five hundred horsemen with Sheng to welcome the bride; an imperial clanswoman was enfeoffed as Princess Anyi and given to him in marriage. Sheng persuaded Tuli to lead his people south and settle at the old site of Dujin. Yunglu resented this and repeatedly raided him. Tuli kept watch on enemy movements and promptly reported to court, so whenever raiders came he was always prepared.
11
十九年，染幹因晟奏，雍閭作攻具，欲打大同城。 詔發六總管，並取漢王節度，分道出塞討之。 雍閭大懼，複共達頭同盟，合力掩襲染幹，大戰于長城下。 染幹敗績，殺其兄弟子侄，而部落亡散。 染幹與晟獨以五騎逼夜南走，至旦，行百餘裡，收得數百騎，乃相與謀曰：「今兵敗入朝，一降人耳，大隋天子豈禮我乎？ 玷厥雖來，本無冤隙，若往投之，必相存濟。 」晟知其懷貳，乃密遣從者入伏遠鎮，令速舉烽。 染幹見四烽俱發，問晟曰：「城上然烽何也？ 」晟紿之曰：「城高地迥，必遙見賊來。 我國家法，若賊少舉二烽，來多舉三烽，大逼舉四烽，使見賊多而又近耳。 」染幹大懼，謂其眾曰：「追兵已逼，且可投城。 」既入鎮，晟留其達官執室以領其眾，自將染幹馳驛入朝。 帝大喜，進授左勳衛驃騎將軍，持節護突厥。 晟遣降虜覘候雍閭，知其牙內屢有災變，夜見赤虹，光照數百里，天狗隕，雨血三日，流星墜其營內，有聲如雷。 每夜自驚，言隋師且至。 並遣奏知，仍請出討突厥。 都速等歸染幹，前後至者男女萬餘口，晟安置之。 由是突厥悅附。 尋以染幹為意利珍豆啟人可汗，賜射于武安殿。 選善射者十二人，分為兩朋。 啟人曰：「臣由長孫大使得見天子，今日賜射，願入其朋。 」許之。 給晟箭六侯，發皆入鹿，啟人之朋竟勝。 時有群飛，上曰：「公善彈，為我取之。 」十發俱中，並應丸而落。 是日百官獲賚，晟獨居多。 尋遣領五萬人，於朔州築大利城以處染幹。 安義公主死，持節送義城公主，複以妻之。 晟又奏：「染幹部落歸者既眾，雖在長城之內，猶被雍閭抄略，往來辛苦，不得甯居。 請徙五原，以河為固，于夏、勝兩州之間，東西至河，南北四百里，掘為橫塹，令處其內，任情放牧，免於抄略，人必自安。 」上並從之。
In year 19 Tuli reported through Sheng that Yunglu was building siege engines and intended to attack Datong. An edict mobilized six area commanders under the Prince of Han to advance beyond the frontier by separate routes and punish him. Yunglu was greatly alarmed, renewed his alliance with Tardu, and together launched a surprise attack on Tuli; they fought a great battle below the Great Wall. Tuli was defeated; his brothers, sons, and nephews were killed and his tribes scattered. Tuli and Sheng fled south through the night with only five horsemen; by dawn they had ridden more than a hundred li and rallied several hundred riders; they then said to one another, "Now that we are defeated, if we enter court we are merely surrendered men—will the Son of Heaven of Great Sui honor us?" Though Dianjue is our enemy, we have no private grievance with him; if we submit to him, he will surely shelter us. " Sheng knew he was wavering and secretly sent a follower into Fuyuan post to order the beacon fires lit at once. When Tuli saw all four beacons blaze, he asked Sheng, "Why are the beacon fires lit on the wall?" " Sheng deceived him, saying, "The post is high and commands a wide view—they must have seen the enemy coming from far off." By our law, two beacons mean few enemies, three mean many, and four mean a large force close at hand. " Tuli was greatly alarmed and told his followers, "The pursuers are close—we had better take refuge in the city." " Once inside the post, Sheng left Tuli's high official Zhishi to lead the tribesmen while he himself escorted Tuli by relay to court. The emperor was delighted, promoted him to general of agile cavalry of the Left Merit Guard, and sent him with the staff of authority to oversee the Türks. Sheng sent surrendered Türks to spy on Yunglu and learned that his camp was plagued by omens: a red rainbow at night lit the sky for hundreds of li; a comet fell; blood rained for three days; a meteor crashed in his camp with a thunderous roar. Each night he startled himself, saying that Sui troops were about to arrive. He reported all this to court and again requested permission to campaign against the Türks. Dusu and others submitted to Tuli; more than ten thousand men and women arrived in succession, and Sheng settled them. From this the Türks were pleased and submitted willingly. Soon Tuli was made Qaghan Qilibi of the Qiren, and the emperor granted an archery contest at Wuan Hall. Twelve skilled archers were chosen and divided into two teams. The Qaghan said, "Through Grand Master Zhangsun I was able to see the Son of Heaven; for today's archery contest I wish to join his team." " Permission was granted. Sheng was given six hou-grade arrows; every shot hit the deer target, and the Qaghan's team won. A flock of birds was flying overhead; the emperor said, "You are skilled with the sling—bring them down for me." " Ten shots all hit, and every bird fell at the strike of the pellet. That day the officials all received gifts, but Sheng alone received the largest share. He was soon sent to lead fifty thousand men to build Dali city at Shuozhou to settle Tuli. When Princess Anyi died, he escorted Princess Yicheng with the staff of authority and again gave her to Tuli in marriage. Sheng memorialized again, "Tuli's submitted tribes are already numerous; though they are within the Great Wall they are still raided by Yunglu and cannot live in peace." I request that they be moved to Wuyuan with the river as their defense; between Xia and Sheng provinces, from the river east and west and four hundred li north and south, a transverse trench should be dug so they may dwell within it and pasture freely, safe from raids—the people will surely settle in peace. " The emperor approved all of it.
12
二十年，都藍大亂，為其部下所殺。 晟因奏請曰：「今王師臨境，戰數有功，賊內攜離，其主被殺，乘此招誘，必並來降，請遣染幹部下分頭招慰。 」上許之，果盡來附。 達頭恐怖，又大集兵。 詔晟部領降人，為秦川行軍總管，取晉王廣節度出討。 達頭與王相抗，晟進策曰：「突厥飲泉，易可行毒。 」因取諸藥毒水上流，達頭人畜飲之多死，於是大驚曰：「天雨惡水，其亡我乎？ 」因夜遁。 晟追之，斬首千餘級，俘百餘口，六畜數千頭。 王大喜，引晟入內，同宴極歡。 有突厥達官來降，時亦預坐，說言突厥之內，大畏長孫總管，聞其弓聲，謂為霹靂，見其走馬，稱為閃電。 王笑曰：「將軍震怒，威行域外，遂與雷霆為比，一何壯哉！ 」師旋，授上開府儀同三司，複遣還大利城，安撫新附。
In year 20 Dudou fell into great disorder and was killed by his subordinates. Sheng memorialized, "Now that the imperial army is at the border with repeated victories, the enemy are divided within and their lord is dead—if we seize this moment to entice them, they will surely all submit; I beg that Tuli's subordinates be sent out separately to summon and reassure them." " The emperor agreed, and indeed they all submitted. Tardu was terrified and again gathered a great army. An edict ordered Sheng to command the surrendered Türks as campaign commander of the Qinchuan Circuit under Prince Guang of Jin and advance to attack. Tardu opposed the prince; Sheng proposed, "The Türks drink from springs—it would be easy to poison the water." " He poisoned the upstream waters with drugs; many of Tardu's men and livestock died from drinking, and Tardu cried in alarm, "Heaven has rained foul water—am I doomed?" " He fled in the night. Sheng pursued him, took more than a thousand heads, captured more than a hundred prisoners, and seized several thousand head of livestock. The prince was delighted, brought Sheng inside, and feasted with him in great joy. A surrendered Türk high official was also at the feast and said that among the Türks they greatly feared Area Commander Zhangsun—his bow was called thunder and his galloping horse lightning. The prince laughed and said, "The general's wrath shakes the realm and his authority extends beyond the borders—he is compared with thunder. How magnificent!" " When the army returned he was appointed senior opener of the gate with ceremony equal to the Three Excellencies and again sent back to Dali city to settle the newly submitted tribes.
13
仁壽元年，晟表奏曰：「臣夜登城樓，望見磧北有赤氣，長百餘裡，皆如雨足，下垂被地。 謹驗兵書，此名灑血，其下之國必且破亡。 欲滅匈奴，宜在今日。 」詔楊素為行軍元帥，晟為受降使者，送染幹北伐。 二年，軍次北河，值賊帥思力俟斤等領兵拒戰，晟與大將軍梁默擊走之，轉戰六十餘裡，賊眾多降。 晟又教染幹分遣使者，往北方鐵勒等部招攜取之。 三年，有鐵勒、思結、伏利具、渾、斛薩、阿拔、僕骨等十餘部，盡背達頭，請來降附。 達頭眾大潰，西奔吐谷渾。 晟送染幹安置於磧口。
In Renshou year 1 Sheng memorialized, "I climbed the city tower at night and saw north of the desert a red vapor more than a hundred li long, like rain shafts hanging down to the ground." According to the military texts this omen is called 'spilled blood'; the state beneath it will surely fall. If we wish to destroy the Xiongnu, the time is now. " An edict appointed Yang Su campaign commander, Sheng envoy for receiving surrenders, and sent Tuli on a northern campaign. In year 2 the army halted at North River; the rebel leaders Silijin and others led troops to resist; Sheng and Great General Liang Mo drove them off in fighting that ranged more than sixty li, and many enemy surrendered. Sheng also instructed Tuli to send envoys to the northern Tiele and other tribes to win them over. In year 3 more than ten tribes including Tiele, Sijie, Fuliju, Hun, Husai, Aba, and Pugu all abandoned Tardu and requested to submit. Tardu's forces collapsed and fled west to the Tuyuhun. Sheng escorted Tuli and settled him at Qikou.
14
事畢，入朝，遇高祖崩，匿喪未發。 煬帝引晟於大行前委以內衙宿衛，知門禁事，即日拜左領軍將軍。 遇楊諒作逆，敕以本官為相州刺史，發山東兵馬，與李雄等共經略之。 晟辭曰：「有男行布，今在逆地，忽蒙此任，情所不安。 」帝曰：「公著勤誠，朕之所悉。 今相州之地，本是齊都，人俗澆浮，易可搔擾。 儻生變動，賊勢即張，思所以鎮之，非公莫可。 公體國之深，終不可以兒害義，故用相委，公其勿辭。 」於是遣捉相州。 諒破，追還，轉武衛將軍。
When the affair was concluded he entered court just as Emperor Gaozu died; the mourning was concealed and not yet announced. Emperor Yang brought Sheng before the imperial bier and entrusted him with inner-palace guard duty and control of the gates; that same day he was appointed general of the Left Army of the Palace Guards. When Yang Liang rebelled, an edict appointed him inspector of Xiang province, mobilized Shandong forces, and sent him with Li Xiong and others to suppress the rebellion. Sheng declined, saying, "My son Xingbu is now in rebel territory; to receive this appointment suddenly is more than I can bear in conscience." " The emperor said, "Your loyal devotion is well known to me." Xiang province was once the Qi capital; the people are shallow and unstable and easily stirred to revolt. If trouble arises the rebels' power will swell at once; I can think of no one but you to hold the region down. Your devotion to the state runs too deep for a son to outweigh duty—therefore I entrust this to you; do not decline. " He thereupon dispatched him to take Xiang province. When Liang was defeated, Sheng was recalled and made general of the Martial Guards.
15
大業三年，煬帝幸榆林，欲出塞外，陳兵耀武，經突厥中，指於涿郡。 仍恐染幹驚懼，先遣晟往喻旨，稱述帝意。 染幹聽之，因召所部諸國，奚、霫、室韋等種落數十酋長咸萃。 晟以牙中草穢，欲令染乾親自除之，示諸部落，以明威重，乃指帳前草曰：「此根大香。 」染幹遽嗅之曰：「殊不香也。 」晟曰：「天子行幸所在，諸侯躬親灑掃，耘除禦路，以表至敬之心。 今牙中蕪穢，謂是留香草耳。 」染幹乃悟曰：「奴罪過。 奴之骨肉，皆天子賜也，得效筋力，豈敢有辭？ 特以邊人不知法耳，賴將軍恩澤而教導之。 將軍之惠，奴之幸也。 」遂拔所佩刀，親自芟草，其貴人及諸部爭放效之。 乃發榆林北境，至於其牙，又東達於薊，長三千里，廣百步，舉國就役而開禦道。 帝聞晟策，乃益嘉焉。 後除淮陽太守，未赴任，複為右驍衛將軍。
In Daye year 3 Emperor Yang visited Yulin, wished to march beyond the frontier, displayed his armies in martial splendor, passed through Türk territory, and aimed for Zhuo commandery. Fearing Tuli would be alarmed, he first sent Sheng ahead to explain the imperial intent. Tuli listened and summoned the tribes under his authority; chieftains of several dozen Xi, Mohe, Shiwei, and other tribes all gathered. Because the grass in the royal camp was overgrown, Sheng wished Tuli to clear it himself to display imperial authority to the tribes; he pointed to the grass before the tent and said, "This root is very fragrant." " Tuli hastily sniffed it and said, "It is not fragrant at all." " Sheng said, "Wherever the Son of Heaven travels, the feudal lords personally sweep and clear the imperial road to show their utmost reverence." Now the camp is overgrown—you must think this is fragrant grass left for the emperor." " Tuli then understood and said, "Your slave has sinned." Your slave's very flesh and bone were bestowed by the Son of Heaven; how dare I refuse to exert my strength? It is only that we frontier people do not know the law; I rely on the general's grace to instruct us. The general's kindness is your slave's good fortune. " He drew the knife at his belt and personally cut the grass; his nobles and the tribes all vied to follow his example. They then opened a road from the northern border of Yulin to the royal camp and eastward to Ji—three thousand li long and a hundred paces wide—with the whole nation mobilized to build the imperial highway. When the emperor heard of Sheng's stratagem, he praised him all the more. He was later made administrator of Huaiyang but did not take up the post and was again appointed general of the Right Agile Cavalry Guards.
16
五年，卒，時年五十八。 帝深悼惜之，賵贈甚厚。 後突厥圍雁門，帝歎曰：「向使長孫晟在，不令匈奴至此！ 」晟好奇計，務功名。 性至孝，居憂毀瘠，為朝士所稱。 貞觀中，追贈司空、上柱國、齊國公，諡曰獻。 少子無忌嗣。
In year 5 he died, at the age of fifty-eight. The emperor deeply mourned him and gave lavish funeral gifts. Later, when the Türks besieged Yanmen, the emperor sighed and said, "If Zhangsun Sheng were still alive, the Xiongnu would never have reached this far!" " Sheng delighted in unusual stratagems and pursued fame and achievement. By nature he was supremely filial; in mourning he wasted away, and the court praised him for it. In the Zhenguan era he was posthumously made Minister of Works, Senior Pillar of State, and Duke of Qi; his posthumous title was Xian. His youngest son Wuji succeeded him.
17
其長子行布，亦多謀略，有父風。 起家漢王諒庫真，甚見親狎。 後遇諒於并州起逆，率眾南拒官軍，乃留行布城守，遂與豆盧毓等閉門拒諒，城陷，遇害。 次子恆安，以兄功授鷹揚郎將。 史評史臣曰：長孫氏爰自代陰，來儀京洛，門傳鍾鼎，家誓山河。 漢代八王，無以方其茂績； 張氏七葉，不能譬此重光。 覽獨擅雄辨，熾早稱爽俊，俱司禮閣，並統師旅，且公且侯，文武不墜。 晟體資英武，兼包奇略，因機制變，懷彼戎夷。 傾巢盡落，屈膝稽顙，塞垣絕鳴鏑之旅，渭橋有單于之拜。 惠流邊朔，功光王府，保茲爵祿，不亦宜乎！
His eldest son Xingbu was also resourceful and bore his father's manner. He began his career as a kuzhen attendant to Prince Liang of Han and was greatly favored. When Liang rebelled at Bingzhou and marched south against the imperial army, he left Xingbu to defend the city; Xingbu closed the gates with Dou Luyu and others to resist Liang, but when the city fell he was killed. The second son Heng'an was appointed commandant of the Hawkish Guards on account of his elder brother's merit. The historian's appraisal: The historiographer writes, "The Zhangsun clan, from Dai and Yin of old, came to settle at Luoyang; their house bore the emblems of nobility and pledged itself to the realm." The eight kings of Han cannot match their abundant achievements; nor can the seven generations of the Zhang clan compare with this repeated glory. Lan excelled in eloquence; Chi was early acclaimed as brilliant; both served the ritual offices and commanded armies—dukes and marquises together, civil and martial achievement unbroken. Sheng was endowed with heroic martial prowess and extraordinary stratagems; adapting to opportunity he controlled the barbarian peoples. Their power was overturned and all submitted; they bent the knee; along the frontier the whistling arrows ceased, and at Wei Bridge the khan performed his obeisance. His kindness flowed to the northern frontier and his achievements illumined the royal house; to preserve such rank and emolument was only fitting!
