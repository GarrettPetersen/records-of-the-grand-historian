○韓擒虎弟僧壽洪
○ Han Qinhu; his brothers Sengshou and Hong
3
韓擒虎，字子通，河南東垣人也，後家新安。 父雄，以武烈知名，仕周，官至大將軍、洛虞等八州刺史。 擒虎少慷慨，以膽略見稱，容貌魁岸，有雄傑之表。 性又好書，經史百家皆略知大旨。 周太祖見而異之，令與諸子遊集。 後以軍功，拜都督、新安太守，稍遷儀同三司，襲爵新義郡公。 武帝伐齊，齊將獨孤永業守金墉城，擒虎說下之。 進平范陽，加上儀同，拜永州刺史。 陳人逼光州，擒虎以行軍總管擊破之。 又從宇文忻平合州。 高祖作相，遷和州刺史。 陳將甄慶、任蠻奴、蕭摩訶等共為聲援，頻寇江北，前後入界。 擒虎屢挫其鋒，陳人奪氣。
Han Qinhu, whose courtesy name was Zitong, came from Dongyuan in Henan and later made his home in Xin'an. His father Han Xiong won renown for martial prowess, served the Northern Zhou, and eventually reached the rank of Grand General while holding eight prefectures, among them Luo and Yu. As a young man Qinhu was generous and daring, celebrated for nerve and strategy, with a towering frame and the look of a born champion. He loved learning too and had a working grasp of the essentials of the classics, histories, and the hundred schools. When Zhou Taizu saw him he took him for remarkable and had him keep company with the imperial princes. Later, on account of battlefield merit, he was made area commander and Administrator of Xin'an, then gradually promoted to Yitong Third Rank and succeeded to the title Duke of Xinyi. When Emperor Wu campaigned against Qi, the Qi commander Dugu Yongye held Jinyong; Qinhu persuaded him to submit. He went on to pacify Fanyang, received an additional Yitong rank, and was appointed Administrator of Yongzhou. When Chen troops pressed Guangzhou, Qinhu, serving as campaigning commander, routed them. He also followed Yuwen Xin in bringing Hezhou to submission. When Gaozu took power as Chancellor, Qinhu was transferred to Administrator of Hezhou. Chen commanders Zhen Qing, Ren Mannu, Xiao Mohe, and others backed one another and raided the north bank of the Yangtze time and again. Qinhu repeatedly broke their momentum until the Chen lost their fighting spirit.
4
開皇初，高祖潛有吞併江南之志，以擒虎有文武才用，夙著聲名，於是拜為廬州總管，委以平陳之任，甚為敵人所憚。 及大舉伐陳，以擒虎為先鋒。 擒虎率五百人宵濟，襲採石，守者皆醉，擒虎遂取之。 進攻姑熟，半日而拔，次於新林。 江南父老素聞其威信，來謁軍門，晝夜不絕。 陳人大駭，其將樊巡、魯世真、田瑞等相繼降之。 晉王廣上狀，高祖聞而大悅，宴賜群臣。 晉王遣行軍總管杜彥與擒虎合軍，步騎二萬，陳叔寶遣領軍蔡徵守朱雀航，聞擒虎將至，眾懼而潰。 任蠻奴為賀若弼所敗，棄軍降於擒虎。 擒虎以精騎五百，直入硃雀門。 陳人欲戰，蠻奴捴之曰：「老夫尚降，諸君何事！ 」眾皆散走。 遂平金陵，執陳主叔寶。 時賀若弼亦有功。 乃下詔于晉王曰：「此二公者，深謀大略，東南逋寇，朕本委之，靜地恤民，悉如朕意。 九州不一，已數百年，以名臣之功，成太平之業，天下盛事，何用過此！ 聞以欣然，實深慶快。 平定江表，二人之力也。 」賜物萬段。 又下優詔於擒虎、弼曰：「申國威于萬里，宣朝化於一隅，使東南之民俱出湯火，數百年寇旬日廓清，專是公之功也。 高名塞于宇宙，盛業光於天壤，逖聽前古，罕聞其匹。 班師凱入，誠知非遠，相思之甚，寸陰若歲。 」及至京，弼與擒虎爭功於上前，弼曰：「臣在蔣山死戰，破其銳卒，擒其驍將，震揚威武，遂平陳國。 韓擒虎略不交陣，豈臣之比！ 」擒虎曰：「本奉明旨，令臣與弼同時合勢，以取偽都。 弼乃敢先期，逢賊遂戰，致令將士傷死甚多。 臣以輕騎五百，兵不血刃，直取金陵，降任蠻奴，執陳叔寶，據其府庫，傾其巢穴。 弼至夕，方扣北掖門，臣啟關而納之。 斯乃救罪不暇，安得與臣相比！ 」上曰：「二將俱合上勳。 」於是進位上柱國，賜物八千段。 有司劾擒虎放縱士卒，淫汙陳宮，坐此不加爵邑。
Early in the Kaihuang era Gaozu quietly aimed to absorb the south. Seeing that Qinhu combined civil and military gifts with a reputation already established, he appointed him Commander-in-Chief of Luzhou and entrusted him with the conquest of Chen—a man the enemy deeply dreaded. When the great assault on Chen was mounted, Qinhu was made vanguard. Qinhu led five hundred men across the river by night and struck Caishi; the garrison was drunk, and he seized the place. He pressed Gushu and captured it within half a day, then halted at Xinlin. The elders of Jiangnan, who had long known his authority and good faith, came to his camp day and night without pause. The Chen were thrown into panic; their officers Fan Xun, Lu Shizhen, Tian Rui, and others submitted to him one after another. The Prince of Jin, Yang Guang, sent a memorial; when Gaozu heard the news he was delighted and gave a feast to reward his ministers. The Prince of Jin sent the campaigning commander Du Yan to unite with Qinhu at the head of twenty thousand infantry and cavalry. Chen Shubao sent the Director of the Palace Guard Cai Zheng to hold the Zhuque ford; when the army heard Qinhu was approaching, fear broke their ranks and they fled. Ren Mannu, already beaten by He Ruobi, cast off his force and surrendered to Qinhu. Qinhu entered the Zhuque Gate at the head of five hundred elite horsemen. The Chen wanted to fight, but Mannu held them back, saying, "Even I, an old man, have submitted—what are you still fighting for!" The troops all broke and ran. He thereupon pacified Jinling and took the Chen ruler Shubao prisoner. He Ruobi had won merit as well. He then sent an edict to the Prince of Jin: "These two men possess deep strategy and grand design. The fugitive rebels of the southeast I originally entrusted to them; in quieting the realm and caring for the people they have acted wholly as I wished. For centuries the realm has not been united; through the service of great ministers a peaceful order is won—what triumph under heaven could exceed this!" To hear of it fills me with pleasure; my delight is profound. The pacification of Jiangbiao is the achievement of these two men. He bestowed gifts of ten thousand bolts of silk. Another gracious edict went to Qinhu and Bi: "You have proclaimed our might across ten thousand li and spread the court's civilizing influence in a distant quarter, lifting the people of the southeast out of fire and flood; centuries of marauding were swept away in days—this is your work alone. Your great names fill the cosmos; your splendid deeds light heaven and earth; searching the past, one seldom hears their like. Your victorious return cannot be far off; I miss you so keenly that every inch of shadow feels like a year. When they reached the capital, Bi and Qinhu disputed their merits before the throne. Bi said, "Your servant fought to the death on Mount Jiang, broke their elite troops, captured their fiercest commanders, displayed martial might, and thereby pacified Chen. Han Qinhu barely engaged the enemy in battle—how can he be compared with your servant!" Qinhu said, "I originally received the explicit order that your servant and Bi should combine our forces at the same moment to seize the false capital. Yet Bi dared to act early, met the enemy and gave battle, and caused a great many officers and men to be wounded or killed. Your servant, with five hundred light horsemen and without drawing blood from our blades, went straight to Jinling, received Ren Mannu's surrender, seized Chen Shubao, took their treasuries, and overturned their lair. Bi did not arrive until evening, when he knocked at the North Yeta Gate; your servant opened the gate and let him in. He scarcely had time to answer for his offenses—how could he be compared with your servant!" The emperor said, "Both generals alike deserve supreme merit." He was then promoted to Supreme Pillar of State and given eight thousand bolts of goods. The authorities impeached Qinhu for allowing his troops to run loose and defile the Chen palace; for this he received no added fief or estate.

先是，江東有謠歌曰：「黃斑青驄馬，發自壽陽涘。 來時冬氣末，去日春風始。 」皆不知所謂。 擒虎本名豹，平陳之際，又乘青驄馬，往反時節與歌相應，至是方悟。 其後突厥來朝，上謂之曰：「汝聞江南有陳國天子乎？ 」對曰：「聞之。 」上命左右引突厥詣擒虎前，曰：「此是執得陳國天子者。 」擒虎厲然顧之，突厥惶恐，不敢仰視，其有威容如此。 別封壽光縣公，食邑千戶。 以行軍總管屯金城，禦備胡寇，即拜涼州總管。 俄征還京，上宴之內殿，恩禮殊厚。 無何，其鄰母見擒虎門下儀衛甚盛，有同王者，母異而問之。 其中人曰：「我來迎王。 」忽然不見。 又有人疾篤，忽驚走至擒虎家曰：「我欲謁王。 」左右問曰：「何王也？ 」答曰：「閻羅王。 」擒虎子弟欲撻之，擒虎止之曰：「生為上柱國，死作閻羅王，斯亦足矣。 」因寢疾，數日竟卒，時年五十五。 子世諤嗣。
Earlier, in Jiangdong, there was a ballad that ran: "A piebald horse with yellow spots, setting out from the banks of Shouyang. Arriving as winter wanes, departing when the spring wind rises." No one knew what it meant. Qinhu's original name was Bao; during the pacification of Chen he again rode a blue piebald horse, and the seasons of his coming and going matched the song—only then did people understand. Later, when the Turks came to court, the emperor said to them, "Have you heard of the Son of Heaven of Chen in the south?" They answered, "We have." The emperor ordered attendants to lead the Turks before Qinhu and said, "This is the man who captured the Son of Heaven of Chen." Qinhu turned on them with a fierce look; the Turks were terrified and dared not raise their eyes—such was the force of his bearing. He was separately enfeoffed Duke of Shouguang with a fief of one thousand households. As campaigning commander he was posted at Jincheng to guard against northern raiders and was immediately appointed Commander-in-Chief of Liangzhou. Before long he was recalled to the capital; the emperor feasted him in the inner hall with exceptional kindness. Before long a neighbor woman saw at Qinhu's gate an escort as splendid as a king's; astonished, she asked what it meant. Someone in the party said, "I have come to welcome the king." Then they vanished. Another man, desperately ill, suddenly ran in alarm to Qinhu's house and said, "I wish to pay court to the king." His attendants asked, "Which king?" He answered, "King Yama." Qinhu's sons and nephews wanted to thrash him, but Qinhu stopped them, saying, "Alive I was a Supreme Pillar of State; dead I shall be King Yama—that is enough." He then fell ill and within a few days died, at the age of fifty-five. His son Shiyu succeeded him.
6
世諤倜儻驍捷，有父風。 楊玄感之作亂也，引世諤為將，每戰先登。 及玄感敗，為吏所拘。 時帝在高陽，送詣行所。 世諤曰令守者市酒殽以酣暢，揚言曰：「吾死在朝夕，不醉何為！ 」漸以酒進守者，守者狎之，遂飲令致醉。 世諤因得逃奔山賊，不知所終。
Shiyu was dashing, fierce, and quick, with his father's spirit. When Yang Xuangan rose in rebellion he took Shiyu as a commander; in every battle Shiyu led the assault. When Xuangan was defeated, Shiyu was seized by the authorities. The emperor was then at Gaoyang; Shiyu was sent to the traveling court. Shiyu had the guards buy wine and food for a hearty feast and cried aloud, "I shall die any day now—if I do not get drunk, what is the point!" Little by little he pressed wine on the guards; they grew familiar with him and drank until he had made them drunk. Shiyu thus escaped and fled to mountain bandits; nothing more is known of his fate.

僧壽字玄慶，擒虎母弟也，亦以勇烈知名。 周武帝時，為侍伯中旅下大夫。 高祖得政，從韋孝寬平尉迥，每戰有功，授大將軍，封昌樂公，邑千戶。 開皇初，拜安州刺史。 時擒虎為廬州總管，朝廷不欲同在淮南，轉為熊州刺史。 後轉蔚州刺史，進爵廣陵郡公。 尋以行軍總管擊突厥于雞頭山，破之。 後坐事免。 數歲，複拜蔚州刺史。 突厥甚憚之。 十七年，屯蘭州以備胡。 明年，遼東之役，領行軍總管，還，檢校靈州總管事。 從楊素擊突厥，破之，進位上柱國，改封江都郡公。 煬帝即位，又改封新蔡郡公。 自是之後，不復任用。 大業五年，從幸太原。 有京兆人達奚通妾王氏，能清歌，朝臣多相會觀之，僧壽亦豫焉，坐是除名。 尋令復位。 八年，卒于京師，時年六十五。 有子孝基。
Sengshou, whose courtesy name was Xuanqing, was Qinhu's younger uterine brother and was likewise famed for fierce valor. Under Emperor Wu of Zhou he served as a lower grand master in the Central Brigade of the Palace Guard. When Gaozu took power he followed Wei Xiaokuan in putting down Yu Chi Jiong; in every battle he distinguished himself and was made Grand General and enfeoffed Duke of Changle with a fief of one thousand households. At the beginning of Kaihuang he was appointed Administrator of Anzhou. At that time Qinhu was Commander-in-Chief of Luzhou; the court did not want both brothers in Huainan, so Sengshou was moved to Administrator of Xiongzhou. Later he was transferred to Administrator of Yu prefecture and advanced to Duke of Guangling. Soon, as campaigning commander, he struck the Turks at Mount Jitou and defeated them. Later he was dismissed for an offense. Several years later he was again appointed Administrator of Yu prefecture. The Turks greatly feared him. In the seventeenth year he was posted at Lanzhou to guard against northern peoples. The next year, in the Liaodong campaign, he served as campaigning commander; on his return he acted as inspector of the affairs of the Commander-in-Chief of Lingzhou. Following Yang Su he attacked the Turks and defeated them, was promoted to Supreme Pillar of State, and his fief was changed to Duke of Jiangdu. When Emperor Yang succeeded, his fief was again changed to Duke of Xincai. From then on he was no longer employed. In the fifth year of Daye he accompanied the emperor to Taiyuan. A concubine of Daxi Tong of Jingzhao named Wang could sing beautifully; many courtiers gathered to watch her, and Sengshou was among them; for this he was removed from office. Soon he was ordered restored to his post. In the eighth year he died in the capital, aged sixty-five. He had a son Xiaoji.
8
洪字叔明，擒虎季弟也。 少驍勇，善射，膂力過人。 仕周侍伯上士，後以軍功拜大都督。 高祖為丞相，從韋孝寬破尉迥於相州，加上開府，甘棠縣侯，邑八百戶。 高祖受禪，進爵為公。 尋授驃騎將軍。 開皇九年，平陳之役，授行軍總管。 及陳平，晉王廣大獵于蔣山，有猛獸在圍中，眾皆懼。 洪馳馬射之，應弦而倒。 陳氏諸將，列觀於側，莫不歎伏焉。 王大喜，賜縑百匹。 尋以功加柱國，拜蔣州刺史。 數歲，轉廉州刺史。 時突厥屢為邊患，朝廷以洪驍勇，檢校朔州總管事。 尋拜代州總管。 仁壽元年，突厥達頭可汗犯塞，洪率蔚州刺史劉隆、大將軍李藥王拒之。 遇虜于恆安，眾寡不敵，洪四面搏戰，身被重創，將士沮氣。 虜悉眾圍之，矢下如雨。 洪偽與虜和，圍少解。 洪率所領潰圍而出，死者大半，殺虜亦倍。 洪及藥王除名為民，隆竟坐死。 煬帝北巡，至恆安，見白骨被野，以問侍臣。 侍臣曰：「往者韓洪與虜戰處也。 」帝憫然傷之，收葬骸骨，命五郡沙門為設佛供，拜洪隴西太守。 未幾，硃崖民王萬昌作亂，詔洪擊平之。 以功加位金紫光祿大夫，領郡如故。 俄而萬昌弟仲通複叛，又詔洪討平之。 師未旋，遇疾而卒，時年六十三。
Hong, whose courtesy name was Shuming, was Qinhu's youngest brother. In youth he was fierce and brave, skilled at archery, with strength beyond ordinary men. He served Zhou as a senior gentleman of the Palace Guard and later, for military merit, was appointed Grand Commander. When Gaozu was Chancellor he followed Wei Xiaokuan in defeating Yu Chi Jiong at Xiangzhou, was given the rank of Upper Opening Master, and made Marquis of Gantang with a fief of eight hundred households. When Gaozu received the abdication, his rank was advanced to duke. Soon he was appointed General of the Swift Cavalry. In the ninth year of Kaihuang, during the conquest of Chen, he was appointed campaigning commander. After Chen fell, the Prince of Jin held a great hunt on Mount Jiang; when a fierce beast appeared inside the ring, everyone was afraid. Hong galloped up and shot it; at the twang of the string the beast fell. The Chen commanders watching from the side were all struck with wonder. The prince was delighted and gave him a hundred bolts of silk. Soon, for his merit, he was advanced to Pillar of State and appointed Administrator of Jiangzhou. Several years later he was transferred to Administrator of Lianzhou. The Turks were raiding the frontier again and again; because Hong was fierce and bold, the court made him acting inspector of the affairs of the Commander-in-Chief of Shuozhou. Soon he was appointed Commander-in-Chief of Daizhou. In the first year of Renshou, the Turk qaghan Tardu crossed the border; Hong led the Administrator of Yu Liu Long and the Grand General Li Yaowang to meet him. They met the enemy at Heng'an; badly outnumbered, Hong fought on every side, took heavy wounds himself, and his troops lost heart. The enemy closed in with their full strength; arrows fell like rain. Hong feigned a truce with the enemy, and the encirclement slackened. Hong broke out with his command; more than half his men died, yet they killed twice as many of the enemy. Hong and Li Yaowang were removed from office and reduced to commoners; Liu Long was executed. On a northern tour Emperor Yang came to Heng'an, saw white bones strewn across the countryside, and asked his attendants. They said, "This is where Han Hong once fought the barbarians." Moved to pity, he had the bones gathered and buried, ordered monks from five commanderies to perform Buddhist offerings, and appointed Hong Administrator of Longxi. Before long Wang Wanchang of Zhuyai rebelled; an edict ordered Hong to crush the rising. For his merit he was advanced to Grand Master of Splendid Happiness with Golden Seal and Purple Ribbon while retaining his prefecture. Soon Wanchang's younger brother Zhongtong rebelled again, and Hong was again ordered to put him down. Before his troops could return he fell ill and died, at the age of sixty-three.

○賀若弼
○ He Ruobi
10
賀若弼，字輔伯，河南洛陽人也。 父敦，以武烈知名，仕周為金州總管，宇文護忌而害之。 臨刑，呼弼謂之曰：「吾必欲平江南，然此心不果，汝當成吾志。 且吾以舌死，汝不可不思。 」因引錐刺弼舌出血，誡以慎口。 弼少慷慨有大志，驍勇便弓馬，解屬文，博涉書記，有重名於當世。 周齊王憲聞而敬之，引為記室。 未幾，封當亭縣公，遷小內史。 周武帝時，上柱國烏丸軌言於帝曰：「太子非帝王器，臣亦嘗與賀若弼論之。 」帝呼弼問之，弼知太子不可動搖，恐禍及己，詭對曰：「皇太子德業日新，未睹其闕。 」帝默然。 弼既退，軌讓其背己，弼曰：「君不密則失臣，臣不密則失身，所以不敢輕議也。 」及宣帝嗣位，軌竟見誅，弼乃獲免。 尋與韋孝寬伐陳，攻拔數十城，弼計居多。 拜夀州刺史，改封襄邑縣公。 高祖為丞相，尉迥作亂鄴城，恐弼為變，遣長孫平馳驛代之。
He Ruobi, whose courtesy name was Fubo, came from Luoyang in Henan. His father Dun won fame for martial prowess, served Zhou as Commander-in-Chief of Jinzhou, and was killed by Yuwen Hu out of jealousy. Facing execution he called Bi to him and said, "I was resolved to pacify the south, yet this wish went unfulfilled—you must complete it for me. And I die because of my tongue—do not forget that lesson." He then drove an awl through Bi's tongue until it bled, warning him to be careful in speech. In youth Bi was generous and ambitious, fierce and skilled with bow and horse, capable in literary composition, widely read in records, and celebrated in his day. Prince Xian of Qi heard of him, admired him, and took him on as recorder. Before long he was enfeoffed Duke of Dangting and promoted to Junior Palace Scribe. Under Emperor Wu of Zhou, the Supreme Pillar Wuyan Gui told the emperor, "The Crown Prince lacks the makings of a ruler; I have discussed this with He Ruobi as well." The emperor summoned Bi and questioned him; knowing the Crown Prince could not be moved and fearing harm to himself, Bi answered evasively, "The Crown Prince's virtue and accomplishments improve daily; I have seen no fault in him." The emperor said nothing. After Bi withdrew, Gui reproached him for betraying him; Bi said, "When the ruler is not discreet he loses his minister; when the minister is not discreet he loses his life—that is why I dared not speak lightly." When Emperor Xuan succeeded, Gui was indeed executed, and Bi thus escaped harm. Soon he followed Wei Xiaokuan in campaigning against Chen and took dozens of cities; Bi's stratagems led the way. He was appointed Administrator of Shouzhou and his fief was changed to Duke of Xiangyi. When Gaozu was Chancellor, Yu Chi Jiong rebelled at Ye; fearing Bi might turn traitor, he sent Zhangsun Ping by urgent relay to replace him.
11
高祖受禪，陰有並江南之志，訪可任者。 高熲曰：「朝臣之內，文武才幹，無若賀若弼者。 」高祖曰：「公得之矣。 」於是拜弼為吳州總管，委以平陳之事，弼忻然以為己任。 與壽州總管源雄並為重鎮。 弼遺雄詩曰：「交河驃騎幕，合浦伏波營，勿使騏驎上，無我二人名。」
When Gaozu took the throne he quietly aimed to absorb the south and asked whom he could entrust. Gao Jiong said, "Among the ministers at court, none matches He Ruobi in civil and military talent." Gaozu said, "You have found the man." He then appointed Bi Commander-in-Chief of Wuzhou and entrusted him with the conquest of Chen; Bi gladly made it his own mission. He and Yuan Xiong, Commander-in-Chief of Shouzhou, formed paired bastions of strength. Bi sent Xiong a poem: "The swift-cavalry camp at Jiaohe, the Fubo camp at Hepu—let no qilin charge heavenward without our two names upon it."
12
獻取陳十策，上稱善，賜以寶刀。 開皇九年，大舉伐陳，以弼為行軍總管。 將渡江，酹酒而咒曰：「弼親承廟略，遠振國威，伐罪吊民，除凶翦暴，上天長江，鑒其若此。 如便福善禍淫，大軍利涉； 如事有乖違，得葬江魚腹中，死且不恨。 」先是，弼請緣江防人每交代之際，必集曆陽。 於是大列旗幟，營幕被野。 陳人以為大兵至，悉發國中士馬。 既知防人交代，其眾複散。 後以為常，不復設備。 及此，弼以大軍濟江，陳人弗之覺也。 襲陳南徐州，拔之，執其刺史黃恪。 軍令嚴肅，秋毫不犯。 有軍士于民間沽酒者，弼立斬之。 進屯蔣山之白土岡，陳將魯達、周智安、任蠻奴、田瑞、樊毅、孔范、蕭摩訶等以勁兵拒戰。 田瑞先犯弼軍，弼擊走之。 魯達等相繼遞進，弼軍屢卻。 弼揣知其驕，士卒且惰，於是督厲將士，殊死戰，遂大破之。 麾下開府員明擒摩訶至，弼命左右牽斬之。 摩訶顏色自若，弼釋而禮之。 從北掖門而入。 時韓擒虎已執陳叔寶，弼至，呼叔寶視之。 叔寶惶懼流汗，股忄栗再拜。 弼謂之曰：「小國之君，當大國卿，拜，禮也。 入朝不失作歸命侯，無勞恐懼。 」既而弼恚恨不獲叔寶，功在韓擒虎之後，於是與擒虎相詢，挺刃而出。 上聞弼有功，大悅，下詔褒揚，語在《韓擒虎傳》。 晉王以弼先期決戰，違軍命，於是以弼屬吏。 上驛召之，及見，迎勞曰：」克定三吳，公之功也。 「命登禦坐，賜物八千段，加位上柱國，進爵宋國公，真食襄邑三千戶，加以寶劍、寶帶、金甕、金盤各一，並雉尾扇、曲蓋，雜彩二千段，女樂二部，又賜陳叔寶妹為妾。 拜右領軍大將軍，尋轉右武候大將軍。
He submitted ten stratagems for taking Chen; the emperor praised them and gave him a precious saber. In the ninth year of Kaihuang the great assault on Chen was mounted; Bi was made campaigning commander. As he was about to cross the Yangtze he poured a libation and swore, "Bi personally carries out the court's plan, displays our might afar, punishes the guilty and comforts the people, uproots cruelty and violence—Heaven and the Long River, bear witness!" If Heaven favors the good and punishes evil, the great army will cross safely; if things go otherwise, let me be buried in the bellies of the river fish—I shall die without regret." Earlier Bi had arranged that whenever the river guards changed shifts they must assemble at Liyang. There he arrayed banners on a vast scale until camp pavilions covered the countryside. The Chen believed a great army had arrived and mobilized every soldier and horse in the realm. When they learned it was only a guard rotation, the forces dispersed again. Afterward they took it as routine and no longer kept watch. This time Bi ferried the great army across the river, and the Chen never noticed. He struck Chen Southern Xuzhou, captured it, and seized its Administrator Huang Ke. His army orders were severe; not a hair of the people was touched. When a soldier bought wine from civilians, Bi had him beheaded on the spot. He advanced to Baitu Hill on Mount Jiang; the Chen commanders Lu Da, Zhou Zhi'an, Ren Mannu, Tian Rui, Fan Yi, Kong Fan, Xiao Mohe, and others met him with elite troops. Tian Rui attacked first; Bi beat him back. Lu Da and the others pressed forward in turn; Bi's army fell back again and again. Bi judged them arrogant and his own men slack; he then drove his troops to fight to the death and routed them utterly. His subordinate the Opening Master Yuan Ming brought Mohe in bonds; Bi ordered his men to drag him out for execution. Mohe's face remained calm; Bi released him and treated him with courtesy. He entered through the North Yeta Gate. By then Han Qinhu had already taken Chen Shubao prisoner; when Bi arrived he had Shubao brought out before him. Shubao was terrified, sweat streaming down him, his legs shaking as he bowed twice. Bi told him, "A ruler of a small state who meets a minister of a great power bows—that is proper ritual. When you go to court you will still be made Marquis Who Surrendered—there is no need for such fear." Then Bi, resentful that he had not taken Shubao himself and that his merit ranked behind Qinhu's, faced Qinhu and drew his blade. When the emperor heard of Bi's merit he was greatly pleased and issued an edict of praise—the text appears in the biography of Han Qinhu. The Prince of Jin, because Bi had fought ahead of schedule in violation of orders, handed him over to the legal officers. The emperor summoned him by urgent relay; when they met he welcomed and praised him, saying, "Pacifying the Three Wu is your achievement." He had him sit on the imperial couch, gave eight thousand bolts of goods, advanced him to Supreme Pillar of State, promoted him to Duke of Song with a true fief of three thousand households at Xiangyi, and also bestowed a precious saber, belt, golden jar, and golden platter, peacock-tail fans and a curved canopy, two thousand bolts of mixed silks, two troupes of female musicians, and Chen Shubao's younger sister as a concubine. He was appointed General of the Right Palace Guard and soon transferred to General of the Right Martial Guard.
13
弼時貴盛，位望隆重，其兄隆為武都郡公，弟東為萬榮郡公，並刺史、列將。 弼家珍玩不可勝計，婢妾曳綺羅者數百，時人榮之。 弼自謂功名出朝臣之右，每以宰相自許。 既而楊素為右僕射，弼仍為將軍，甚不平，形於言色，由是免官，弼怨望愈甚。 後數年，下弼獄，上謂之曰：「我以高熲、楊素為宰相，汝每倡言，雲此二人惟堪啖飯耳，是何意也？ 」弼曰：「熲，臣之故人，素，臣之舅子，臣並知其為人，誠有此語。 」公卿奏弼怨望，罪當死。 上惜其功，於是除名為民。 歲餘，複其爵位。 上亦忌之，不復任使，然每宴賜，遇之甚厚。 開皇十九年，上幸仁壽宮，宴王公，詔弼為五言詩，詞意憤怨，帝覽而容之。 嘗遇突厥入朝，上賜之射，突厥一發中的。 上曰：「非賀若弼無能當此。 」於是命弼。 弼再拜祝曰：「臣若赤誠奉國者，當一發破的。 如其不然，發不中也。 」既射，一發而中。 上大悅，顧謂突厥曰：「此人天賜我也！」
Bi was then at the height of power; his elder brother Long was Duke of Wudu and his younger brother Dong Duke of Wanrong—both prefects and field commanders. His house held treasures beyond counting; hundreds of concubines trailed silk and gauze—men of the age envied him. Bi believed his merit outshone the ministers and constantly spoke of himself as fit for the chancellorship. When Yang Su became Right Vice Director while Bi remained a mere general, he was deeply discontent, showed it openly, and was dismissed; his resentment only deepened. Years later Bi was thrown into prison; the emperor said to him, "I made Gao Jiong and Yang Su my chancellors, yet you kept saying those two were fit only to eat rice—what did you mean by that?" Bi answered, "Jiong is an old friend of mine and Su is my nephew by marriage; I know them both well—those words were truly spoken." The ministers memorialized that Bi harbored resentment and deserved death. The emperor valued his past service and therefore struck his name from the rolls and reduced him to a commoner. After more than a year his rank and title were restored. The emperor also grew wary and no longer gave him office, yet at every feast and gift he treated him generously. In the nineteenth year of Kaihuang the emperor visited Renshou Palace and feasted the princes and dukes; he ordered Bi to compose a pentasyllabic poem—its tone was bitter, yet the emperor read it and indulged him. Once when Turks came to court the emperor granted them a shooting match; a Turk hit the target on his first shot. The emperor said, "Only He Ruobi can answer this." He then summoned Bi. Bi bowed twice and prayed, "If I am loyal and devoted to the state, let this one shot strike home. If not, let it miss." He shot and hit with a single arrow. The emperor was delighted and told the Turks, "Heaven gave me this man!"
14
煬帝之在東宮，嘗謂弼曰：「楊素、韓擒虎、史萬歲三人，俱稱良將，優劣如何？ 」弼曰：「楊素是猛將，非謀將； 韓擒虎是鬥將，非領將； 史萬歲是騎將，非大將。 」太子曰：「然則大將誰也？ 」弼拜曰：「唯殿下所擇。 」弼意自許為大將。 及煬帝嗣位，尤被疏忌。 大業三年，從駕北巡，至榆林。 帝時為大帳，其下可坐數千人，召突厥啟民可汗饗之。 弼以為大侈，與高熲、宇文弼等私議得失，為人所奏，竟坐誅，時年六十四。 妻子為官奴婢，群從徙邊。
When Emperor Yang was crown prince he once asked Bi, "Yang Su, Han Qinhu, and Shi Wansui are all called fine generals—how do they compare?" Bi said, "Yang Su is a fierce fighter, not a strategist; Han Qinhu is a duelist, not a commander; Shi Wansui is a cavalry leader, not a supreme commander." The crown prince asked, "Then who is the great marshal?" Bi bowed and said, "Whomever Your Highness selects." In his heart Bi meant himself as the great marshal. When Emperor Yang took the throne, Bi was kept at an even greater distance and viewed with suspicion. In the third year of Daye he accompanied the emperor on the northern tour as far as Yulin. The emperor pitched a great tent that could seat thousands and summoned the Turk qaghan Qimin to a feast. Bi judged the display extravagantly grand and privately discussed its faults with Gao Jiong, Yuwen Bi, and others; someone denounced them and he was executed, aged sixty-four. His wife and children were made government slaves; his kinsmen were exiled to the borderlands.
15
子懷亮，慷慨有父風，以柱國世子拜儀同三司。 坐弼為奴，俄亦誅死。
His son Huailiang was generous and bold like his father; as heir of a Pillar of State he was appointed Yitong Third Rank. Because of Bi's disgrace he too was soon put to death.
16
史臣曰：夫天地未泰，聖哲啟其機； 疆埸尚梗，爪牙宣其力。 周之方、邵，漢室韓、彭，代有其人，非一時也。 自晉衰微，中原幅裂，區宇分隔，將三百年。 陳氏憑長江之地險，恃金陵之餘氣，以為天限南北，人莫能窺。 高祖爰應千齡，將一函夏。 賀若弼慷慨，申必取之長策，韓擒虎奮發，賈餘勇以爭先，勢甚疾雷，鋒逾駭電。 隋氏自此一戎，威加四海。 稽諸天道，或時有廢興，考之人謀，實二臣之力。 其俶儻英略，賀若居多，武毅威雄，韓擒虎稱重。 方于晉之王、杜，勳庸綽有餘地。 然賀若功成名立，矜伐不已，竟顛殞於非命，亦不密以失身。 若念父臨終之言，必不及於斯禍矣。 韓擒虎累世將家，威聲動俗，敵國既破，名遂身全，幸也。 廣陵、甘棠，鹹有武藝，驍雄膽略，並為當時所推，赳赳干城，難兄難弟矣。
The historiographer writes: When heaven and earth are not yet at peace, sages open the way forward; when the frontiers still bar the way, warriors display their strength. Fang and Shao of Zhou, Han Xin and Peng Yue of Han—every age produces such men; it is never a matter of one moment alone. From Jin's decline the central plains were torn asunder; the realm lay divided for nearly three hundred years. The Chen trusted the Yangtze's natural barrier and Jinling's lingering strength, believing heaven itself divided north from south so that none could cross. Gaozu then answered the summons of the age and set out to unite the realm within the four seas. He Ruobi, bold and generous, laid out the strategy that had to prevail; Han Qinhu threw himself forward and spent his last strength to race ahead—momentum like thunder, blades swifter than lightning. With this single campaign the house of Sui made its might felt across the four seas. Measured against heaven's way, fortune rises and falls in its seasons; measured by human counsel, it was truly the work of these two ministers. In daring strategy and heroic design He Ruo predominates; in martial sternness and commanding presence Han Qinhu carries the greater weight. Even beside Wang Jun and Du Yu of Jin, their achievements still stand taller. Yet He Ruo, once merit and fame were won, kept boasting without end and finally died before his time—having failed, too, to guard his tongue and preserve himself. Had he remembered his father's dying words, he would never have come to this ruin. Han Qinhu came from generations of commanders, his renown shaking the age; once the enemy state fell, he kept both fame and life—fortunate indeed. Guangling and Gantang both possessed martial skill; fierce courage and daring strategy alike won the esteem of the age—stalwart shields of the state, brothers matched in glory.
