王奐
Wang Huan
2
王奐字彥孫，琅邪臨沂人也。 祖僧朗，宋左光祿、儀同。 父粹，黃門郎。 奐出繼從祖中書令球，故字彥孫。
Wang Huan, courtesy name Yansun, came from Linyi in Langya. His grandfather Wang Senglang had served the Song as Left Household Grandee with the privilege of Three Offices. His father Wang Cui had been a Yellow Gate Gentleman. Huan was adopted by his father's cousin, Secretariat Director Wang Qiu, which is why his courtesy name was Yansun.
3
解褐著作佐郎，太子舍人，安陸王冠軍主簿，太子洗馬，本州別駕，中書郎，桂陽王司空諮議，黃門郎。 元徽元年，爲晉熙王征虜長史、江夏內史，遷侍中，領步兵校尉。 復出爲晉熙王鎮西長史，加冠軍將軍、江夏武昌太守。 徵祠部尚書，轉掌吏部。
He entered service as an assistant in the Masters of Writing, then served successively as crown prince attendant, chief clerk on Prince Anlu's staff, crown prince groom, native commandery aide, secretariat gentleman, adviser to Prince Guiyang's ministry of works, and yellow gate gentleman. In 473 he was made chief administrator on Prince Jinxi's staff and interior governor of Jiangxia, then promoted to palace attendant while also holding the post of colonel of footsoldiers. He went out again as chief administrator on Prince Jinxi's western staff, with the added rank of champion general and concurrent governorship of Jiangxia and Wuchang. He was recalled to be minister of rites and soon took charge of the Ministry of Personnel.
4
昇明初，遷冠軍將軍、丹陽尹。 初，王晏父普曜爲沈攸之長史，常慮攸之舉事，不得還。 時奐爲吏部，轉普曜爲內職，晏深德之。 及晏仕世祖府，奐從弟蘊反，世祖謂晏曰：「王奐宋家外戚，王蘊親同逆黨，旣其羣從，豈能無異意。 我欲具以啓聞。 」晏叩頭曰：「王奐脩謹，保無異志。 晏父母在都，請以爲質。 」世祖乃止。
Early in the Shengming era he was made champion general and intendant of Danyang. Earlier, Wang Yan's father Puyao had served as Shen Youzhi's chief administrator and lived in constant fear that Youzhi would rebel and he would never get home. When Huan headed the Ministry of Personnel, he moved Puyao to a court post inside the capital, for which Yan was profoundly grateful. When Yan joined the heir apparent's staff, Huan's cousin Wang Yun rose in revolt. The heir apparent told Yan, "Wang Huan is tied to the Song by marriage, and Wang Yun stands with the rebels. Men of the same clan—how can they be free of divided loyalties?" I mean to lay the whole matter before the throne. " Yan kowtowed and said, "Wang Huan is scrupulous and careful—I swear he has no treacherous designs." My parents are in the capital—let them stand as hostages for him." " The heir apparent dropped the matter.
5
出爲吳興太守，秩中二千石，將軍如故。 尋進號征虜將軍。 ，進號左將軍。 明年，遷太常，領鄱陽王師，仍轉侍中，祕書監，領驍騎將軍。 又遷征虜將軍、臨川王鎮西長史、領南蠻校尉、南郡內史。 奐一歲三遷，上表固讓南蠻曰：「今天地初辟，萬物載新，荊蠻來威，巴濮不擾。 但使邊民樂業，有司脩務，本府舊州，日就殷阜。 臣昔遊西土，較見盈虛，兼日者戎燼之後，痍毀難復。 雖復緝以善政，未及來蘇。 今復割撤大府，制置偏校，崇望不足以助強，語實安能以相弊？ 且資力旣分，職司增廣，衆勞務倍，文案滋煩。 非獨臣見其難，竊以爲國計非允。 」見許。 於是罷南蠻校尉官。 進號前將軍。
He went out to govern Wuxing at the middle two-thousand-dan rank, retaining his general's title. Soon afterward his title was raised to expelling barbarians general. In 479 he was promoted to left general. The following year he became grand minister of ceremonies and tutor to Prince Poyang, then palace attendant, director of the secretariat, and valiant cavalry general. He was moved again to expelling barbarians general, chief administrator on Prince Linchuan's western staff, with the added posts of colonel of the southern barbarians and interior governor of Nan commandery. Huan received three promotions within a single year. He memorialized the throne, firmly declining the southern barbarians command, writing: "Heaven and earth have only just been renewed; all things are fresh again. The tribes of Jing have submitted, and Ba and Pu are quiet." Let the frontier people prosper in their work and the officials do theirs, and this old seat of government will grow richer day by day. I have traveled in the west before and seen firsthand how lean or full those regions are; and since the recent wars, the devastation has been so deep that recovery is still far off. Even the best governance has not yet brought those lands back to life. To split off part of the great headquarters and set up a subsidiary command would add prestige without real strength, and in practice can only weaken what is already there. Resources would be divided, offices multiplied, public labor doubled, and paperwork endlessly increased. I am not alone in seeing the difficulty; I believe the policy itself is unsound for the realm. " The emperor approved his request. The post of colonel of the southern barbarians was then abolished. His title was raised to vanguard general.
6
世祖卽位，徵右僕射。 仍轉使持節監湘州軍事、前將軍、湘州刺史。 ，徙爲散騎常侍、江州刺史。 初省江州軍府。 四年，遷右僕射，本州中正。 奐無學術，以事幹見處。 遷尚書僕射，中正如故。 校籍郎王植屬吏部郎孔琇之以校籍令史俞公喜求進署，矯稱奐意，植坐免官。
When Emperor Wu ascended the throne, Huan was recalled as right vice director of the secretariat. He was soon made inspector of Xiang with discretionary authority, holding the rank of vanguard general. In 484 he was moved to cavalier attendant-in-ordinary and inspector of Jiang province. It was the first reduction of the Jiang province military staff. In the fourth year of Yongming he became right vice director of the secretariat and director of selection for his native commandery. Huan was no scholar; he rose through executive talent. He was promoted to vice director of the masters of writing while keeping his selection post. Registrar Wang Zhi, working under personnel officer Kong Xiuzhi, had clerk Yu Gongxi lobby for promotion in Huan's name without authorization; Zhi was stripped of his post.
7
六年，遷散騎常侍，領軍將軍。 奐欲請車駕幸府。 上晚信佛法，御膳不宰牲。 使王晏謂奐曰：「吾前去年爲斷殺事，不復幸詣大臣已判，無容欻爾也。 」王儉卒，上用奐爲尚書令，以問王晏。 晏位遇已重，與奐不能相推，答上曰：「柳世隆有重望，恐不宜在奐後。 」乃轉爲左僕射，加給事中，出爲使持節、散騎常侍、都督雍梁南北秦四州郢州之竟陵司州之隨郡軍事、鎮北將軍、雍州刺史。 上謂王晏曰：「奐於釋氏，實自專至。 其在鎮或以此妨務，卿相見言次及之，勿道吾意也。 」上以行北諸戍士卒多襤縷，送褶三千具，令奐分賦之。
In the sixth year he became cavalier attendant-in-ordinary and general of the guards. Huan wanted to invite the emperor to visit his household. The emperor had lately taken up Buddhism and would not have animals killed for his table. He sent Wang Yan to tell Huan, "Since I swore off slaughter last year I no longer visit ministers at home—that is settled, and there is no changing it on a whim." " When Wang Jian died, the emperor meant to make Huan director of the masters of writing and asked Wang Yan's view. Yan was already too powerful to yield to Huan, and told the emperor, "Liu Shilong's standing is too great—he should not rank below Huan." " Huan was made left vice director with the added title of attendant within the yellow gates, then sent out to Yong as inspector with discretionary authority, commanding the armies of Yong, Liang, and northern and southern Qin, with Jingling and Suixiang, under the title pacifier of the north. The emperor told Wang Yan, "Huan's devotion to Buddhism is absolute." That may interfere with his duties in the province—bring it up casually when you see him, but do not say I sent you." " Learning that the northern garrisons were in rags, the emperor sent three thousand pairs of trousers for Huan to distribute.
8
十一年，奐輙殺寧蠻長史劉興祖，上大怒，使御史中丞孔稚珪奏其事曰：
In the eleventh year Huan killed Pacifier of the Man chief administrator Liu Xingzu on his own authority. The emperor was furious and ordered imperial censor Kong Zhigui to present the case:
9
:雍州刺史王奐啓錄小府長史劉興祖，虛稱「興祖扇動山蠻，規生逆謀，誑言誹謗，言辭不遜」。 敕使送興祖下都，奐慮所啓欺妄，於獄打殺興祖，詐啓稱自經死。 止今體傷楗蒼，事暴聞聽。
Wang Huan, inspector of Yong, reported and seized his minor headquarters chief Liu Xingzu, falsely charging that Xingzu was stirring up the mountain tribes, plotting rebellion, spreading slander, and speaking insolently. The throne ordered Xingzu sent to the capital. Fearful that his charges would be exposed, Huan had him beaten to death in prison and reported a suicide. The body's wounds are now plain for all to see, and the matter has come openly before the court.
10
:攝興祖門生劉倪到臺辨問，列「興祖與奐共事，不能相和。 自去年朱公恩領軍征蠻失利，興祖啓聞，以啓呈奐，奐因此便相嫌恨。 若云興祖有罪，便應事在民間； 民間恬然，都無事迹。 去十年九月十八日，奐使仗身三十人來，稱敕錄興祖付獄。 安定郡蠻先在郡贓私，興祖旣知其取與，卽牒啓，奐不問。 興祖後執錄，奐仍令蠻領仗身於獄守視。 興祖未死之前，於獄以物畫漆柈子中出密報家，道無罪，令啓乞出都一辨，萬死無恨」。 又云：「奐駐興祖嚴禁信使，欲作方便，殺以除口舌。 」又云：「奐意乃可。 奐第三息彪隨奐在州，凡事是非皆干豫，扇構密除興祖。 」又云：「興祖家餉糜，中下藥，食兩口便覺，回乞獄子，食者皆大利。 興祖大呌道『糜中有藥』。 近獄之家，無人不聞。 」又云：「奐治著興祖日急，判無濟理。 十一月二十一日，奐使獄吏來報興祖家，道興祖於獄自經死。 尸出，家人共洗浴之，見興祖頸下有傷，肩胛烏，陰下破碎，實非興祖自經死。 家人及門義共見，非是一人。 」重攝檢雍州都留田文喜，列與倪符同狀。
Xingzu's student Liu Ni was brought to the capital for questioning and testified that Xingzu and Huan had served together but never got along. Last year Zhu Gong'en's campaign against the Man tribes failed; Xingzu reported it and showed Huan the memorial, after which Huan turned against him. If Xingzu were truly guilty, the matter should have shown among the people; yet the countryside was quiet and no evidence existed. On the eighteenth day of the ninth month, ten years ago, Huan sent thirty armed retainers claiming an imperial order to seize Xingzu and imprison him. A tribesman of Anding had been taking bribes in the prefecture; Xingzu learned of it and filed a report, but Huan ignored it. When Xingzu was arrested, Huan even put that tribesman in charge of the guards watching him in prison. Before he died, Xingzu scratched a secret message inside a lacquered tray smuggled to his family, declaring his innocence and begging them to petition for a hearing in the capital—even death would be worth it." He also said that Huan held Xingzu under strict guard, blocking all messengers, seeking a chance to kill him and silence him. " He added that Huan's intentions were clear. Huan's third son Biao was with him in the province, meddling in every decision and secretly plotting Xingzu's removal. " His family sent porridge laced with poison; after two bites Xingzu was ill, and every prisoner who ate it was violently purged." Xingzu shouted, "The porridge is poisoned!" Everyone near the prison heard it. " He said Huan's pressure on Xingzu grew daily and there was no hope of saving him." On the twenty-first of the eleventh month Huan's jailers told Xingzu's family he had hanged himself. When the body was released the family washed it and found wounds on the neck, bruised shoulders, and shattered lower body—clearly not a hanging. Family and retainers alike witnessed it—many people, not one. " Tian Wenxi of the Yong headquarters was questioned again and corroborated Ni's account in full.
11
:興祖在獄，嗛苦望下，旣蒙降旨，欣願始遂，豈容於此，方復自經？ 敕以十九日至，興祖以二十一日死，推理檢迹，灼然矯假。 尋敕使送下，奐輙拒詔，所謗諸條，悉出奐意。 毀故丞相若陳顯達，誹訕朝事，莫此之深。 彪私隨父之鎮，敢亂王法，罪竝合窮戮。
Xingzu had suffered in prison, longing for the capital; once the order came to send him down his hope was fulfilled—how could he then kill himself? The decree was due on the nineteenth; Xingzu died on the twenty-first—the timeline alone proves the fraud. When the imperial escort was sent, Huan defied the order on his own authority; every charge came from his own mind. He defamed the late chancellor Chen Xianda and reviled the court—nothing went deeper. Biao had followed his father to the province and dared violate the law—his crimes warrant the fullest punishment.
12
上遣中書舍人呂文顯、直閤將軍曹道剛領齋仗五百人收奐。 敕鎮西司馬曹虎從江陵步道會襄陽。
The emperor sent palace gentleman Lü Wenxian and direct gate general Cao Daogang with five hundred guards to arrest Huan. Cao Hu, major of the western command, was ordered to march from Jiangling overland to join them at Xiangyang.
13
奐子彪素凶剽，奐不能制。 女婿殷叡懼禍，謂奐曰：「曹、呂今來，旣不見真敕，恐爲姦變，政宜錄取，馳啓聞耳。 」奐納之。 彪輙令率州內得千餘人，開鎮庫，取仗，配衣甲，出南堂陳兵，閉門拒守。 奐門生鄭羽叩頭啓奐，乞出城迎臺使。 奐曰：「我不作賊，欲先遣啓自申。 政恐曹、呂輩小人相陵藉，故且閉門自守耳。 」彪遂出與虎軍戰，其黨范虎領二百人降臺軍，彪敗走歸。 土人起義攻州西門，彪登門拒戰，却之。 奐司馬黃瑤起、寧蠻長史裴叔業於城內起兵攻奐。 奐聞兵入，還內禮佛，未及起，軍人遂斬之。 年五十九。 執彪及弟爽、弼、殷叡，皆伏誅。
Huan's son Biao had always been brutal, and Huan could not restrain him. His son-in-law Yin Rui, fearing disaster, urged Huan: "Cao and Lü are here without showing a genuine decree—they may be plotting treachery. Arrest them and report to the throne at once." " Huan agreed. Biao on his own authority rallied more than a thousand men in the province, opened the arsenal, armed them, drew them up in the southern hall, and shut the gates to resist. Huan's student Zheng Yu kowtowed and begged permission to go out and receive the imperial envoys. Huan said, "I am no rebel—I mean to send a memorial explaining myself first." I only fear men like Cao and Lü will abuse us, so for now we hold the gates." " Biao went out to fight Hu's troops; his follower Fan Hu defected with two hundred men, and Biao was beaten back inside. Local militia rose and attacked the western gate; Biao fought them off from the wall. Huan's major Huang Yaoqi and Pacifier of the Man chief Pei Shuye rose inside the city against him. Hearing soldiers enter, Huan went inside to pray before the Buddha; before he could rise, they cut him down. He was fifty-nine. Biao, his brothers Shuang and Bi, and Yin Rui were seized and executed.
14
詔曰：「逆賊王奐，險詖之性，自少及長。 外飾廉勤，內懷凶慝，貽戾郷伍，取棄衣冠。 拔其文筆之用，擢以顯任，出牧樊阿，政刑弛亂。 第三息彪矯弄威權，父子均勢。 故寧蠻長史劉興祖忠於奉國，每事匡執，奐忿其異己，誣以訕謗，肆怒囚錄，然後奏聞。 朕察奐愚詐，詔送興祖還都，乃懼姦謀發露，潛加殺害。 欺罔旣彰，中使辯覈，遂授兵登陴，逆捍王命。 天威電掃，義夫咸奮，曾未浹辰，罪人斯獲，方隅克殄，漢南肅清。 自非犯官兼預同逆謀，爲一時所驅逼者，悉無所問。」
An edict declared: "The traitor Wang Huan was treacherous and perverse from youth to old age." He wore the mask of probity while nursing malice within, offending neighbors and forfeiting the respect of the gentry. His clerical skill won him high office; sent to govern Fan'e, he let law and administration fall into disorder. His third son Biao usurped authority until father and son shared equal power. The late Pacifier of the Man chief Liu Xingzu had served the state faithfully and corrected Huan at every turn; Huan, resenting his independence, jailed him on false charges of slander and only then reported to the throne. Seeing through Huan's deceit, We ordered Xingzu sent to the capital; fearing exposure, he had him killed in secret. When the fraud was exposed and the imperial envoy investigated, he armed his men, manned the walls, and defied the throne. Imperial wrath struck like lightning; loyal men rose as one; within a day the culprit was seized, the rebellion crushed, and the lands south of the Han restored to order. Except officials who shared in the plot, all who had been swept along in the moment would go unpunished."
15
奐長子太子中庶子融，融弟司徒從事中郎琛，於都棄市。 餘孫皆原宥。
Huan's eldest son Rong, crown prince household aide, and his brother Chen, secretariat attendant, were executed in public at the capital. The remaining grandsons were all spared.

=殷叡字文子，陳郡人。 晉太常融七世孫也。 宋元嘉末，祖元素坐染太初事誅。 叡遺腹亦當從戮，外曾祖王僧朗啓孝武救之，得免。 叡解文義，有口才，司徒褚淵甚重之，謂之曰：「諸殷自荊州以來，無出卿右者。 」叡歛容答曰：「殷族衰悴，誠不如昔，若此旨爲虛，故不足降； 此旨爲實，彌不可聞。 」奐爲雍州，啓叡爲府長史。
Yin Rui, courtesy name Wenzi, was from Chen commandery. He was seven generations removed from Jin grand minister of ceremonies Yin Rong. Late in the Song Yuanjia era his grandfather Yuansu was executed for complicity in the Taichu plot. Rui, born after his father's death, was also marked for execution; his great-great-grandfather Wang Senglang pleaded with Emperor Xiaowu and saved his life. Rui was literate and articulate; Minister of Works Chu Yuan held him in high regard and said, "Since the Yins came from Jing province, none has matched you." " Rui composed himself and replied, "The Yin clan has declined and is no longer what it was; if you speak in flattery, it is not worth answering;" if you speak in truth, it is all the more unbearable to hear." " When Huan governed Yong he appointed Rui chief administrator of his staff.
19
叡族父恒，字昭度，與叡同承融後。 宋司空景仁孫也。 恒及父道矜，竝有古風，以是見蚩於世，其事非一。 恒，宋泰始初，爲度支尚書，坐屬父疾及身疾多，爲有司所奏。 明帝詔曰：「殷道矜有生便病，比更無橫病。 恒因愚習惰，久妨清敘。 左遷散騎常侍，領校尉。 」恒歷官清顯，至金紫光祿大夫。 建武中，卒。 奐弟女爲長沙王晃妃，世祖詔曰：「奐自陷逆節，長沙王妃男女竝長，且奐又出繼，前代或當有准，可特不離絕。 」奐從弟繢。
Rui's kinsman Heng, courtesy name Zhaodu, shared descent from Yin Rong's line with Rui. He was grandson of the Song minister of works Yin Jingren. Heng and his father Daoqin both cultivated old-fashioned ways and were widely ridiculed for it—there were many such stories. At the start of the Song Taishi era Heng was minister of revenue but was reported for excessive leave due to his father's illness and his own frequent sickness. Emperor Ming decreed, "Yin Daoqin has been sick since birth; lately he has had no sudden illness." Heng's foolish habits and laziness have long blocked his promotion. He was demoted to cavalier attendant-in-ordinary and colonel of the palace guards. " Heng served in distinguished posts up to household grandee with golden bell and purple ribbon. He died during the Jianwu era. The daughter of Huan's brother Hui was consort to Prince Changsha Huang. The emperor decreed, "Though Huan has rebelled, the princess and her grown children remain, and Huan was adopted out—precedent allows the tie not to be broken." " Huan's cousin Wang Hui.

=繢字叔素，宋車騎將軍景文子也。 弱冠，爲祕書郎，太子舍人，轉中書舍人。 景文以此授超階，令繢經年乃受。 景文封江安侯，繢襲其本爵，爲始平縣五等男。 遷祕書丞，司徒右長史。 元徽末，除寧朔將軍、建平王征北長史、南東海太守，黃門郎，寧朔將軍、東陽太守。 世祖爲撫軍，吏部尚書張岱選繢爲長史，呈選牒。 太祖笑謂岱曰：「此可謂素望。 」遷散騎常侍，驍騎將軍。
Hui, courtesy name Shusu, was the son of Song cavalry general Wang Jingwen. At twenty he was a secretariat gentleman and crown prince attendant, then palace gentleman of the secretariat. Jingwen had granted him a leap in rank but made Hui wait a year before accepting it. Jingwen was made Marquis of Jiang'an; Hui inherited his original title as fifth-grade baron of Shiping. He became secretariat assistant and right chief administrator on the ministry of works staff. Late in Yuanhui he was made pacifying the north general, chief administrator on Prince Jianping's northern staff, governor of southern Donghai, yellow gate gentleman, and governor of Dongyang. When the heir apparent held the pacifying general post, personnel minister Zhang Dai nominated Hui as chief administrator and submitted the appointment papers. The founding emperor smiled and told Dai, "That is what one calls an established reputation." " He was made cavalier attendant-in-ordinary and valiant cavalry general.
23
出補義興太守。 輙錄郡吏陳伯喜付陽羨獄，欲殺之，縣令孔逭不知何罪，不受繢教，爲有司所奏，繢坐白衣領職。 遷太子中庶子，領驍騎，轉長兼侍中。 世祖出射雉，繢信佛法，稱疾不從駕。 轉左民尚書，以母老乞解職，改授寧朔將軍、大司馬長史、淮陵太守。 出爲宣城太守，秩中二千石。 ，遷輔國將軍、太傅長史，不拜。 仍爲冠軍將軍、豫章內史。 進號征虜。 又坐事免官。 除冠軍將軍，司徒左長史，散騎常侍，隨王師。 除征虜將軍，驃騎長史，遷散騎常侍，太常。 ，卒。 年五十三。 諡靖子。
He went out to serve as governor of Yixing. He arrested commandery clerk Chen Boxi and sent him to Yangxian prison to be killed; county magistrate Kong Huan, not knowing the charge, refused the order; reported to the authorities, Hui was reduced to serving in plain clothes while keeping his post. He became crown prince household aide with valiant cavalry command, then chief concurrent palace attendant. When the emperor went pheasant hunting, Hui, a devout Buddhist, pleaded illness and stayed behind. He became minister of the left for the people, then resigned because his mother was elderly and was reassigned as pacifying the north general, grand marshal chief administrator, and governor of Huailing. He went out to govern Xuancheng at the middle two-thousand-dan rank. In 494 he was offered supporter of the state general and grand tutor chief administrator but declined. He then served as champion general and interior governor of Yuzhang. His title was raised to expelling barbarians general. He was again dismissed for an offense. He was appointed champion general, left chief administrator of the ministry of works, cavalier attendant-in-ordinary, and tutor to Prince Sui. He was made expelling barbarians general and swift cavalry chief administrator, then cavalier attendant-in-ordinary and grand minister of ceremonies. In 499 he died. He was fifty-three. He was posthumously titled Jingzi.
24
繢女適安陸王子敬，世祖寵子。 ，納妃，脩外舅姑之敬。 世祖遣文惠太子相隨往繢家置酒設樂，公卿皆冠冕而至，當世榮之。
Hui's daughter married Prince Anlu Zijing, the emperor's favorite son. In 485 she was installed as consort and showed full respect to her husband's parents by marriage. The emperor sent Crown Prince Wenhuì with him to Hui's home for a feast with music; the highest officials all came in full court dress—a glory of the age.
25
張沖
Zhang Chong
26
張沖字思約，吳郡吳人。 父柬，通直郎。 沖出繼從伯侍中景胤，小名查，父邵，小名梨。 宋文帝戲景胤曰：「查何如梨。 」景胤答曰：「梨是百果之宗，查何敢及。」
Zhang Chong, courtesy name Siyue, was from Wu in Wu commandery. His father Zhang Jian was a direct communication gentleman. Chong was adopted by his father's cousin, attendant-in-ordinary Zhang Jingyin; his childhood name was Cha; his father Shao's was Li. Emperor Wen of Song teased Jingyin, "How does Cha compare with Li?" " Jingyin replied, "The pear is lord of all fruits—how could Cha compare?"
27
沖亦少有至性，辟州主簿，隨從叔永爲將帥，除綏遠將軍、盱眙太守。 永征彭城，遇寒雪，軍人足脛凍斷者十七八，沖足指皆墮。 除尚書駕部郎，桂陽王征南中兵，振威將軍。 歷驃騎太尉南中郎參軍，不拜。 遷征西從事中郎，通直郎，武陵王北中郎直兵參軍，長水校尉，除寧朔將軍，本官如故。 遷左軍將軍，加寧朔將軍，輔國將軍。 沖少從戎事，朝廷以幹力相待，故歷處軍校之官。 出爲馬頭太守，徙盱眙太守，輔國將軍如故。 ，遷西陽王冠軍司馬。 八年，爲假節、監青冀二州刺史事，將軍如故。 沖父初卒，遺命曰：「祭我必以郷土所產，無用牲物。 」沖在鎮，四時還吳園中取果菜，流涕薦焉。 仍轉刺史。
Chong too was known from youth for deep filial feeling; summoned as provincial chief clerk, he followed his cousin Yong on campaign and was made pacifying the distant general and governor of Xuyi. On Yong's Pengcheng campaign they met blizzards; seven or eight soldiers in ten lost feet to frostbite, and Chong lost all his toes. He was made master of writing in the imperial transport section, central army officer on Prince Guiyang's southern staff, and inspiring awe general. He was offered a post as military aide on the swift cavalry grand marshal's staff but declined. He became western expedition attendant, direct communication gentleman, direct military aide on Prince Wuling's northern staff, colonel of the long river, and pacifying the north general while keeping his other posts. He was made left army general with the added ranks of pacifying the north and supporter of the state. Chong had soldiered since youth; the court valued his executive ability and kept him in military posts. He governed Matou, then Xuyi, retaining his supporter of the state rank. In 488 he became champion major on Prince Xiyang's staff. In the eighth year he held provisional authority over Qing and Ji provinces, retaining his general's rank. When his father died he left instructions: "Sacrifice to me only with produce of our homeland—no slaughtered animals." " While stationed away, each season he returned to the family gardens in Wu for fruit and vegetables and offered them with tears. He was then made provincial inspector.
28
鬱林卽位，進號冠軍將軍。 明帝卽位，以晉壽太守王洪範代沖。 除黃門郎，加征虜將軍。 ，虜寇淮泗，假沖節，都督青冀二州北討諸軍事，本官如故。 虜并兵攻司州，詔青徐出軍分其兵勢。 沖遣軍主桑係祖由渣口攻拔虜建陵、驛馬、厚丘三城，多所殺獲。 又與洪範遣軍主崔季延襲虜紀城，據之。 沖又遣軍主杜僧護攻拔虜虎坑、馮時、卽丘三城，驅生口輜重還，至溘溝，虜救兵至，緣道要擊，僧護力戰，大破之。
When Emperor Yulin ascended, Chong was promoted to champion general. When Emperor Ming took power, Jinshou governor Wang Hongfan replaced him. He was made yellow gate gentleman with the added rank of expelling barbarians general. In 495 the enemy raided the Huai and Si region; Chong was given provisional authority to command the northern campaign in Qing and Ji. The enemy massed against Si province; the court ordered armies from Qing and Xu to divide their strength. Chong sent commander Sang Xizu through Zhakou to capture the enemy cities of Jianling, Yima, and Houqiu with heavy slaughter and booty. With Hongfan he sent commander Cui Jiyan to seize and hold the enemy city of Jicheng. Chong also sent Du Senghu to take Hukeng, Fengshi, and Jiqiu, driving captives and baggage homeward; at Keigou enemy reinforcements ambushed the road, but Senghu fought them off in a great victory.
29
其年，遷廬陵王北中郎司馬、加冠軍將軍。 未拜，豐城公遙昌爲豫州，上慮寇難未已，徙沖爲征虜長史、南梁郡太守。 ，除江夏王前軍長史。 東昏卽位，出爲建安王征虜長史、輔國將軍、江夏內史，行郢州府州事。 ，遷持節、督豫州軍事、豫州刺史，代裴叔業。 竟不行。 明年，遷督南兖兖徐青冀五州、輔國將軍、南兖州刺史，持節如故。 會司州刺史申希祖卒，以沖爲督司州軍事、冠軍將軍、司州刺史。 裴叔業以壽春降虜，又遷沖爲督南兖兖徐青冀五州、南兖州刺史，持節、將軍如故。 竝未拜。 崔慧景事平，徵建安王寶夤還都，以沖爲督郢司二州、郢州刺史，持節、將軍如故。 一歲之中，頻授四州，至此受任。 其冬，進征虜將軍。 封定襄侯，食邑千戶。
That year he became northern gentlemen's major on Prince Luling's staff with the rank of champion general. Before he took up the post, Duke of Fengcheng Yaochang held Yu province; fearing continued border trouble, the emperor moved Chong to expelling barbarians chief administrator and governor of Nanliang. In 498 he was made chief administrator on Prince Jiangxia's vanguard staff. When Emperor Donghun ascended, Chong went out as expelling barbarians chief administrator on Prince Jian'an's staff, supporter of the state general, interior governor of Jiangxia, and acting administrator of Jing province. In 499 he was made inspector of Yu with discretionary authority, replacing Pei Shuye. He never actually took up the post. The following year he was made commander of southern Yan, Yan, Xu, Qing, and Ji, supporter of the state general, and inspector of southern Yan. When Si inspector Shen Xizu died, Chong was appointed to command Si's armies as champion general and inspector. After Pei Shuye surrendered Shouchun to the enemy, Chong was again assigned the five provinces and southern Yan, retaining his staff and rank. He accepted none of these appointments. After Cui Huijing's rebellion was crushed, Prince Jian'an was recalled and Chong was made commander of Jing and Si and inspector of Jing. Four provinces had been offered him within a single year; only now did he accept command. That winter he was promoted to expelling barbarians general. He was enfeoffed as Marquis of Dingxiang with a fief of one thousand households.
30
梁王義師起，東昏遣驍騎將軍薛元嗣、制局監暨榮伯領兵及糧運百四十餘船送沖，使拒西師。 元嗣等懲劉山陽之敗，疑沖不敢進，停住夏口浦。 聞義師將至，元嗣、榮伯相率入郢城。 時竟陵太守房僧寄被代還至郢，東昏敕僧寄留守魯山，除驍騎將軍。 僧寄謂沖曰：「臣雖未荷朝廷深恩，實蒙先帝厚澤。 蔭其樹者不折其枝，實欲微立塵效。 」沖深相許諾，共結盟誓。 乃分部拒守。 遣軍主孫樂祖數千人助僧寄據魯山岸立城壘。
When the Prince of Liang's army rose, Emperor Donghun sent Xue Yuansi and Ji Rongbo with troops and over 140 grain ships to reinforce Chong against the western forces. Mindful of Liu Shanyang's defeat, Yuansi and his men doubted Chong would advance and halted at Xiakou. Hearing the righteous army was near, Yuansi and Rongbo withdrew into Jing city together. Jingling governor Fang Sengji, relieved of office, was returning to Jing; Emperor Donghun ordered him to hold Lushan and made him valiant cavalry general. Sengji told Chong, "I have not earned the present court's favor, but I owe the late emperor a great debt." One who enjoys the shade does not break the branches—I only wish to render some small service. " Chong gave his solemn pledge, and they bound themselves by oath. They then divided their forces to hold the defenses. He sent commander Sun Lezu with several thousand men to help Sengji fortify Lushan on the riverbank.
31
明年二月，梁王出沔口，圍魯山城。 遣軍主曹景宗等過江攻郢城，未及盡濟，沖遣中兵參軍陳光靜等開門出擊，爲義師所破，光靜戰死，沖固守不出。 景宗於是據石橋浦，連軍相續，下至加湖。 東昏遣軍主巴西梓潼二郡太守吳子陽、光子衿、李文釗、陳虎牙等十三軍援郢，至加湖不得進，乃築城舉烽，城內亦舉火應之。 而內外各自保，不能相救。
The following second month the Prince of Liang advanced from Hankou and besieged Lushan. Cao Jingzong and others crossed the river against Jing city; before the crossing was complete, Chong sent Chen Guangjing out through the gates in a sortie; the righteous army broke them, Guangjing was killed, and Chong held the city without emerging. Jingzong then occupied Shiqiao Ford and extended his lines down to Jia Lake. Emperor Donghun sent Wu Ziyang of Baxi and Zitong, Guang Zijin, Li Wenzhao, Chen Huya, and thirteen armies to relieve Jing; halted at Jia Lake, they built a fort and lit beacon fires, answered by signal fires from within the city. But the forces inside and out each held their own ground and could not help one another.
32
沖病死，元嗣、榮伯與沖子孜及長史江夏內史程茂固守。 東昏詔贈沖散騎常侍、護軍將軍。 假元嗣子陽節。
Chong died of illness; Yuansi, Rongbo, his son Zi, and chief administrator Cheng Mao continued the defense. Emperor Donghun posthumously honored Chong as cavalier attendant-in-ordinary and protector of the army. Yuansi was granted provisional authority under Ziyang's commission.
33
江水暴長，加湖城淹漬，義師乘高艦攻之，子陽等大敗散。 魯山城乏糧，軍人於磯頭捕細魚供食，密治輕船，將奔夏口。 梁王命偏軍斷其取路，防備越逸。 房僧寄病死，孫樂祖窘，以城降。
The Yangtze rose in flood and drowned the fort at Jia Lake; the righteous army attacked from high ships and routed Ziyang's forces. Lushan ran out of grain; soldiers caught small fish at the stony shore and secretly built light boats to escape to Xiakou. The Prince of Liang sent detachments to block their escape and prevent a breakout. Fang Sengji died; Sun Lezu, cornered, surrendered the city.
34
郢城被圍二百餘日，士庶病死者七八百家。 魯山旣敗，程茂及元嗣等議降，使孜爲書與梁王。 沖故吏青州治中房長瑜謂孜曰：「前使君忠貫昊天，操逾松竹。 郎君但當端坐畫一，以荷析薪。 若天運不與，幅巾待命，以下從使君。 今若隨諸人之計，非唯郢州士女失高山之望，亦恐彼所不取也。 」魯山陷後二日，元嗣等以郢城降。
Jing was besieged for over two hundred days; seven or eight hundred households among the populace died of disease. After Lushan fell, Cheng Mao and Yuansi debated surrender and had Zi write to the Prince of Liang. Chong's former officer Fang Changyu, director of selection for Qing, told Zi, "Your father's loyalty reached heaven; his integrity surpassed pine and bamboo." You need only hold firm to one course and bear the family burden. If fortune fails, wait in plain dress to follow your father below. If you follow the others now, you will disappoint all Jing—and they may not accept you either." " Two days after Lushan fell, Yuansi surrendered Jing.
35
東昏以程茂爲督郢司二州、輔國將軍、郢州刺史，元嗣爲督雍梁南北秦四州郢州之竟陵司州之隨郡、冠軍將軍、雍州刺史，竝持節。 時郢魯二城已降，死者相積，竟無叛散。 時以沖及房僧寄比臧洪之被圍也。 贈僧寄益州刺史。
Emperor Donghun appointed Cheng Mao to command Jing and Si and made Yuansi commander of Yong and the four Qin provinces with Jingling and Suixiang—both with full authority. Though Jing and Lushan had fallen with the dead piled high, none had defected or fled. Chong and Fang Sengji were then compared to Zang Hong besieged at his post. Sengji was posthumously made inspector of Yi province.
36
時新蔡太守席謙，永明中爲中書郎王融所薦。 父恭穆，鎮西司馬，爲魚復侯所害。 至是謙鎮盆城，聞義師東下，曰：「我家世忠貞，殞死不二。 」爲陳伯之所殺。
Xincai governor Xi Qian had been recommended in the Yongming era by secretariat gentleman Wang Rong. His father Gongmu, major of the western command, was killed by the Marquis of Yufu. Now holding Pencheng, he heard the righteous army was marching east and said, "My family has been loyal for generations—we die without wavering." " Chen Bozhi killed him.
37
【史論】
Historian's Appraisal
38
史臣曰：石碏棄子，弘滅親之戒； 鮑永晚降，知事新之節。 王奐誠在靡貳，迹允嚴科； 張沖未達天心，守迷義運。 致危之理異，爲亡之事一也。
The historian writes: Shi Que abandoned his son, teaching the warning against destroying one's kin; Bao Yong submitted late, yet knew the duty of serving the new order. Wang Huan was loyal without duplicity, his conduct meeting the harsh standard; Zhang Chong failed to read heaven's intent and clung blindly to a doomed cause. The paths to ruin differed, but the end was the same.
39
贊曰：王居北牧，子未克家。 終成干紀，覆此冑華。 張壘窮守，死如亂麻。 爲悟旣晚，辯見方賒。
The encomium reads: Wang ruled the north; his son could not keep the house. Rebellion followed at last, and this house's glory was overthrown. Zhang's fortress held to the end; the dead lay tangled like hemp. Understanding came too late; clear sight was still far off.
