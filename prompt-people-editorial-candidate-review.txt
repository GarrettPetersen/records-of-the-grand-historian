# Independent Post-Repair Candidate Review

You are reviewing the consequences of already independently approved translation
repairs. The host has not changed the chapter or canonical extraction. It gives
you a sealed dossier listing the name-like candidates that appear only after the
approved English replacements are replayed.

For every listed candidate, make exactly one source-backed decision in the
provided `decisionSeed`:

- add one `candidateDispositions` item when it is not a person; use only the
  supplied reason vocabulary and explain the immediate Chinese/English context
  in `note`; or
- add one `candidatePeople` item when it is a reference to an existing local
  person; use that person's full local ID, an allowed mention kind, and a
  source-based `note`.

Do not create a new person, omit a candidate, alter fingerprints, alter the
chapter, alter extraction data, or decide from English capitalization alone.
Chinese text controls the classification. A title, place, polity, era, book,
office, collective, or deity is not a person merely because it is capitalized.
If the supplied people do not support a safe link, reject the earlier repair in
the editorial workflow rather than inventing an identity.
