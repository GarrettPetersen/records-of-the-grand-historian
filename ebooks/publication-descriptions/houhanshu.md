Fan Ye (范曄), the author of the Book of Later Han (Hou Hanshu, 後漢書), was born in Shunyang during the Eastern Jin dynasty. The Book of Song says his mother gave birth to him in a privy, where he hit his head on a brick, earning him the childhood name, “Brick” (Zhuan, 磚).

The brick doesn't seem to have done any lasting damage, as he proved himself to be a bright child. He loved learning and reading, voraciously consuming classic texts and histories. He was skilled at writing and music. As a young adult, he worked on the staff of Liu Yu (the future emperor Wu of Liu Song). He had a series of jobs working on the staffs of various generals, even reluctantly joining a northern campaign as a logistics officer.

As an official in the Liu Song court, Fan Ye made some dubious friendships and rivalries.
