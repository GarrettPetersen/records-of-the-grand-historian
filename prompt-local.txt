LITERAL: When Emperor Xiaowen was in Dai, he had three sons by his former queen. After Empress Dowager Dou gained his favor, the former queen died, and the three sons also died one after another. Therefore Emperor Xiaojing was able to be established.
IDIOMATIC: While Emperor Wen was still in Dai, he had three sons by his former consort. After Lady Dou won his favor, that consort died, and the three sons died in turn as well. This was how Emperor Jing came to be named heir.
