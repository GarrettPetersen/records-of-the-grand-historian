## Witness checks

- [Jinshu 8, oldid 2644502](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2644502):
  verified the death and Shi Shi succession in the fourth-month paragraph
  under Yonghe year five; Shi Zun's deposition follows under month five.
- [Jinshu 107, oldid 2556247](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2556247):
  verified Yonghe five for Shi Hu's imperial accession, subsequent death in
  the narrative, and the final explicit `太和六年` alongside a total of
  fifteen reign-years. The former narrative sequence favors the 349 reading,
  but the latter phrase is genuinely present in this digital witness. No
  conclusion about its manuscript provenance or emendation follows from this.
- [Zizhi Tongjian 98, oldid 1502617](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=1502617):
  verified the death and Shi Shi succession on a `己巳` day after fourth-month
  heading within Yonghe year five, before month five. Its editor's Western
  year heading is not itself an original event inscription. The later
  compilation is corroboration, not a demonstrated independent contemporary
  witness. No Western day conversion was checked or is asserted.
- [Taiwan Ministry of Education reign-title index](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/appendix.jsp?ID=23&la=0&page=2&powerMode=0&ver=4):
  verified Eastern Jin Xian'kang 335–342, Yonghe 345–356, Taihe 366–371.
  Under the stated inclusive era-year convention, 345 + 4 = 349 and
  366 + 5 = 371; 349 − 335 + 1 = 15, whereas 371 − 335 + 1 = 37. The
  arithmetic is valid as a conventional year-label comparison, not an exact
  elapsed duration or a day-level calendar conversion.

## Remaining limitations

The dossier's cautious conclusion is supported: the passages and inclusive
arithmetic favor 349, but they do not explain why the Jinshu 107 digital text
has `太和六年`, demonstrate distinct textual traditions, or justify silently
discarding it. Nor does the Jinshu 025 source's sequencing alone establish
the year the seal returned. Whether the schema and evidence can represent the
conflict in an approvable form is a separate reviewer/workflow decision. Leave
the retained blocker and all other outstanding research holds intact, and
require a fresh complete independent audit of any eventual repaired inputs.
