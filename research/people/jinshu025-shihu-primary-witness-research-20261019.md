   son Shi Shi succeeded him. Its more specific cyclical day is a separate
   claim and is not here converted to a modern day. This eleventh-century
   compilation may use earlier transmitted records: it is corroborating
   textual evidence, not an independently witnessed contemporary event.
4. Taiwan Ministry of Education's [reign-title index](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/appendix.jsp?ID=23&la=0&page=2&powerMode=0&ver=4)

   the usual numbered-era-year convention, 永和五年 corresponds to the
   conventional year AD 349 and 太和六年 to AD 371. The index lists
   year spans, not a day-level proleptic calendar conversion.

## Assessment and remaining gate

The explicit annals sequence and the Tongjian's later compiled chronology
strengthen the case for 349. Furthermore, the Jinshu 107 claim of fifteen
years from 咸康元年 is arithmetically consistent with counting 335–349
inclusively, not 335–371. This is an inference from the quoted text and the
index, not proof of why 太和六年 occurs or which manuscript reading is
original. Jinshu 107's own contradictory label is real in the pinned witness;
the dossier does not establish independent manuscript traditions, a licensed
emendation, or that the two texts have independent source origins.
