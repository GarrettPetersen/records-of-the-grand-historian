## Exact proposed replacement fields

### s0014

Source: `及長，不好學，惟與左右嬉戲，不能尊敬保傅。` Growing older is
not necessarily adulthood; 左右 are attendants, not necessarily pages.

- literal: `When he grew older, he disliked study and only played with his attendants; he failed to respect his guardians and tutors.`
- idiomatic: `As he grew older, he neglected his studies, played with his attendants, and showed no respect for his guardians and tutors.`

### s0015

Source: `賈后素忌太子有令譽，因此密敕黃門閹宦媚諛于太子曰：「殿下誠可及壯時極意所欲，何為恆自拘束？」` The idiomatic
omits the empress's resentment and its causal role; the literal retains them.

- idiomatic: `Empress Jia had long resented the crown prince's good reputation, so she secretly instructed the Yellow Gate eunuchs to flatter him: "Your Highness should indulge your desires while you are young. Why always restrain yourself?"`

### s0016

Source: `每見喜怒之際，輒歎曰：「殿下不知用威刑，天下豈得畏服！」`
The preceding eunuchs are the contextual speakers; the existing singular
`she` incorrectly suggests the empress. The subject is implicit in this unit,
so the attribution and quotation continuity deserve separate review.

- literal: `Whenever they saw signs of his pleasure or anger, they would sigh, "Your Highness does not know how to use authority and punishment. How can the realm fear and obey you?"`
- idiomatic: `Whenever they saw him pleased or angry, the eunuchs would sigh, "Your Highness does not know how to use authority and punishment. How can the realm fear and obey you?"`

### s0017

Source: `太子所幸蔣美人生男，又言宜隆其賞賜，多為皇孫造玩弄之器，太子從之。` The prior idiomatic's stipends are not specified; preserve
both enhanced rewards and the grandson's many playthings. The precise recipient
of 賞賜 is left implicit, as in the source.

- idiomatic: `When the crown prince's favored Lady Jiang bore a son, they also urged lavish rewards and the making of many toys for the imperial grandson. The crown prince followed their advice.`

### s0018

Source: `於是慢弛益彰，或廢朝侍，恆在後園遊戲。` Restore the growing
arrogance and laxity and distinguish occasional missed attendance from the
habitual garden games.

- idiomatic: `His arrogance and laxity became increasingly evident; he sometimes neglected court attendance and was constantly playing in the rear garden.`

### s0019 — revised wording, still subject to contextual review

Source: `愛埤車小馬，令左右馳騎，斷其鞅勒，使墮地為樂。` The previous
crosscheck properly held the earlier draft because it made the prince himself
cut the straps. The cutting subject is omitted; the proposed passive avoids
assigning that action to a particular person. 鞅勒 is rendered broadly as
harness and reins, not the existing exact cheek-piece labels. Verify the
specific harness terms and relationship between carts and riding; do not
invent a cart wreck.

- literal: `He loved low carts and ponies and ordered his attendants to ride at speed; their harness and reins were cut, making them fall to the ground for his amusement.`
- idiomatic: `He delighted in low carts and ponies and had his attendants ride fast; their harness and reins were cut so that they fell, to his amusement.`

### s0020

Source: `或有犯忤者，手自捶擊之。` The victims are not explicitly limited
to servants.

- idiomatic: `If someone offended him, he beat that person with his own hands.`

### s0021

Source: `性拘小忌，不許繕壁修牆，正瓦動屋。` Restore 動屋 and avoid
introducing builders as an agent. `house` is a broad rendering; the precise
distinction between the two wall terms may merit further stylistic review.

- literal: `Bound by petty taboos, he would not allow walls to be mended, roof tiles straightened, or the house disturbed.`
- idiomatic: `Preoccupied with petty taboos, he would not allow walls to be repaired, roof tiles straightened, or the building disturbed.`

### s0022

Source: `而于宮中為市，使人屠酤，手揣斤兩，輕重不差。` 使人 makes
others the butchers and liquor sellers, not the prince; he estimates weight
by hand, not the weight of coins or an exact ounce.

- literal: `Yet he set up a market in the palace, had people butcher and sell liquor, and estimated weights with his own hand without error.`
- idiomatic: `Yet he set up a market in the palace, had others butcher and sell liquor, and could estimate an object's weight by hand without error.`

### s0023

Source: `其母本屠家女也，故太子好之。` Her family background is given;
the source does not say her own trade was butchery.

- idiomatic: `His mother was originally a butcher's daughter, which was why the crown prince enjoyed this.`

### s0024 — lexical research hold; agency-only proposal, not a final translation

Source: `又令西園賣葵菜、藍子、雞、面之屬，而收其利。` The independent
commodity review confirms a displayed 籃子/藍子 and 面/麵 witness problem;
neither the reading nor flour-versus-noodles is settled. Do not accept the
following as resolving it or silently amend the Chinese. It retains the
existing provisional `indigo seed` and `flour` solely so that the separate
agency defect can be reviewed. Both fields remain held pending edition-image
and lexical research and reconciliation with Jinshu 056. 令西園賣 is caused
sale by the Western Garden, not personal peddling.

- idiomatic, **held**: `He also had the Western Garden sell mallow, indigo seed, chickens, flour, and similar goods, and collected the profits.`

The existing literal already represents the instruction to the Western Garden;
its `indigo seed` and `flour` remain on the same lexical hold. Avoid treating
either proposed wording as an edition-grounded resolution.

### s0025

Source: `東宮舊制，月請錢五十萬，備於眾用，太子恆探取二月，以供嬖寵。`
The prior idiomatic omits the allowance and general-purpose setting. `two
months' funds in advance` follows the earlier reviewed interpretation of
探取二月; compare that interpretation and the unchanged literal independently.

- idiomatic: `Under the Eastern Palace's established rules, five hundred thousand cash were requested each month for general expenses, but the crown prince regularly drew two months' funds in advance to support his favorites.`
