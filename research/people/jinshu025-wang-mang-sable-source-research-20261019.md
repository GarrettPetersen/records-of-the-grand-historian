## Witnesses and possible bounded chronology

1. [Hanshu 98, fixed Wikisource revision 2180641](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2180641&title=%E6%BC%A2%E6%9B%B8%2F%E5%8D%B7098),
   the Empress Yuan biography, narrates Wang Mang's change from Han black
   sable to yellow, followed by the dowager's instruction that her own staff
   retain black sable. Its paragraph frames events following Wang Mang's
   usurpation; the next paragraph records the dowager's death in 建國五年,
   second month, 癸丑. Her response supplies a narrative reason to place the
   change before her death, not merely within Wang Mang's reign. This is an
   inference from the biography's sequence; it is not an explicitly dated
   decree or a contemporaneous independent witness.
2. [Taiping yulan 688, fixed Wikisource revision 2588009](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2588009&title=%E5%A4%AA%E5%B9%B3%E5%BE%A1%E8%A6%BD%2F0688)
   attributes a closely matching black-to-yellow change and dowager response
   to the Hanshu. It is a later quotation of that tradition, **not** an
   independent corroborating witness or an exact date.
3. [Hanshu 99 middle, fixed Wikisource revision 5978173](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=5978173&title=%E6%BC%A2%E6%9B%B8%2F%E5%8D%B7099%E4%B8%AD)
   starts 始建國元年 with the removal of the Han designation. The Taiwan
   Ministry of Education's [reign-title index](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/appendix.jsp?ID=43&ver=0)
   lists 始建國 as AD 9–13. Thus its fifth year is the conventional AD 13
   year label. The source's second-month death is a terminus for an act before
   the death if the sequence is accepted, not a date for the sable change.

## What remains for the independent reviewer

The Hanshu offers a potentially tighter, source-attributed terminus than the
entire Xin reign: the dowager's response is reported before her death in the
conventional year 13. The same paragraph frames the matter after usurpation,
but do not infer a Gregorian lower-bound year merely from 始建國元年: the
precise accession and calendar boundary need separate review. Nor does the
biography date the change to 12 just because the later Zizhi Tongjian places
the retrospective passage among year-four material. Check the conventional
year versus proleptic-Gregorian representation and whether this source and
sequence support the proposed schema and confidence level. The supplied index
does not itself convert the cyclical day or prove an exact Western day.
