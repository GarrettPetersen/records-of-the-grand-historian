## External comparison, not independent historical witnesses

The [annotated Japanese rendering](https://readingnotesofjinshu.com/translation/biographies/vol-53)
was consulted on 2026-10-19 UTC. It interprets `天教` as the emperor, flags the
`成國` and `會同一日見賜` readings as uncertain, and renders s0092's `差`
as an adverse outcome. Its reading is a scholarly lead, not proof, and no
wording was copied into the proposal. The
[Shidianguji transcription](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/LS0005/chapter/LS0005_791)
and a [Chinese rendering](https://www.beiyeshu.cn/%E6%99%8B%E4%B9%A6/%E5%88%97%E4%BC%A0/23-%E6%84%8D%E6%80%80%E5%A4%AA%E5%AD%90%E5%88%97%E4%BC%A0)
were opened but not independently collated as textual witnesses. Do not use
different digital punctuation as authority to change the Chinese source.
