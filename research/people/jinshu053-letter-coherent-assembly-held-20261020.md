## Exact holds and review instructions

- **s0078:** `父子之情，實相憐湣` does not justify claiming the son died or
  confidently selecting either reciprocal or one-directional pity. The neutral
  rendering needs language review, not a claim of grammatical certainty.
- **s0081–s0082, s0088, s0092, s0097:** `天教`/`天` may be an imperial
  reference; leave it unresolved rather than stating as fact either a
  supernatural actor or an actual imperial command. Do not create a person
  named Tianjiao. The inscription `題言東宮發` in s0082 has competing
  parses, including sender versus opening instructions; `短函` and `疏` are
  also unresolved. Do not assert the provenance or recipient of the item.
- **s0084:** `入見國家` is preserved as entering to see the sovereign;
  whether an interview occurred is a nuance for review, and there is no basis
  for an invented brief meeting. The following passive does not identify an
  agent. `中宮` here may be quarters rather than the empress herself.
- **s0087:** `不得王是成國耳` must be resolved as a whole; the available
  annotated Japanese interpretation explicitly records uncertainty and differs
  from a Chinese paraphrase. Neither the prior “Cheng principality” assertion
  nor an ellipsis can be silently published as if established.
- **s0088:** `與` does not on its own establish that the empress ordered or
  forced a prior drink. This reported utterance is distinct from the actual
  consumption reported in s0089.
- **s0091–s0093:** `持酒可喜`, the disputed outcome of `道文差`, and
  `會同一日見賜` require separate philological decisions. A banquet and a
  successful or failed recovery cannot simply be inferred. s0094–s0095 remain
  within the reply; the reference of `殿下` is not settled here.
- **s0096–s0098:** Chen Wu is expressly the messenger, not necessarily the
  original speaker. Her reported speech runs through s0097; `惡物` is broader
  than a proven poison. `遂可` remains unresolved, and the request to finish
  the remainder in the Eastern Palace is not evidence that it was accepted.
- **s0101–s0104:** Do not call the document itself an edict or identify the
  first unnamed maid with Chengfu. `又` need not prove a second maid. `研墨`
  must be assessed as a possible action versus an object; `青` does not prove
  a modern precise color. Do not invent plural runners or paper placement in
  the case. s0105 `不容` may indicate no permission or no opportunity.
- **s0106:** The sentence ends the outer letter. `見誣` and `見明` are the
  writer's assertions, not objective findings. The separate speech beginning
  at s0108 must not be accidentally included in the letter.
