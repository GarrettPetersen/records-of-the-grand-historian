## Source and overreach checks

- **s0074–s0080:** The draft follows the opening and broadly preserves the
  contrast between the prince's claims and the narrator's introduction. At
  s0078 the neutral `father and son ... a matter of deep pity` retains the
  unresolved direction of 相憐湣; it does not state a death. The requests in
  s0079–s0080 remain appropriately unspecific as to the nature of the honor
  and the means of seeking blessings. The assembly quotes previously reviewed
  s0075–s0077, but this check does not reapprove or apply them.
- **s0081–s0089:** The enclosed placeholders are necessary holds, not English
  to publish. In particular the s0082 item and inscription, the full s0087
  statement and the temporal, subject and causative reading in s0088 cannot
  be settled just by this quote map. The draft's neutral `visit` at s0081 does
  not specify whom the attendants visit. In s0084 `enter to see` retains
  the ambiguity of whether an audience occurred; the next passive does not
  invent who sent him. The draft's `inner palace` is a defensible spatial
  reading of 中宮 here, but its exact referent needs contextual review.
- **s0090–s0099:** The unresolved construction, referent and recovery in
  s0091–s0093 must be decided without assuming a banquet or a successful
  recovery. The reply and relayed message remain correctly nested through
  s0095 and s0097. `harmful` for 惡物 is broader than a poison finding. In
  s0098 the bracketed 遂可飲二升 and s0099 `another sheng` are interdependent:
  do not release the latter as independently established if the former's
  force and timing remain unresolved. The request to finish the remainder at
  the Eastern Palace does not establish its acceptance.
- **s0100–s0106:** s0100 preserves the speaker's claim of lost awareness.
  s0101 attributes the instruction to an edict, not the document's authorship
  to the emperor. s0102 leaves the sheets' location unstated. In s0104 the
  draft's definite `the young maid Chengfu` might encourage a reader to infer
  that she is the first maid; use an English formulation that leaves same vs
  different maid open, without asserting either. 研墨 remains a live
  object-versus-action question. In s0105 the draft `I was not allowed` is
  stronger than the assembly's own acknowledged alternative that haste left
  him no opportunity: hold that exact phrase pending review, or use a neutral
  formulation preserving both. s0106 presents innocence and hoped-for public
  recognition as the prince's assertions, not independently established fact.
