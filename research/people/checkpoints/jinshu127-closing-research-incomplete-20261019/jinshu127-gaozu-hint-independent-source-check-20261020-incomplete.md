## Source check and recommendation

The chapter's s0232 describes Gaozu's sacrifice at Lord Xinling's tomb and s0173 recalls his encounter with Kuai Tong; neither supplies Western-year endpoints. [Shiji 77, Wei Gongzi biography](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=%E5%8F%B2%E8%A8%98/%E5%8D%B7077&oldid=367917) states Gaozu made repeated offerings when passing Daliang **after** becoming emperor, and separately records the five tomb-guard households in his twelfth year following the campaign against Qing Bu. The repeated offerings are his activity; later telling of them is not. The biography does not establish that any particular offering occurred in 206 or 195 BC, or continuously throughout that interval. [Shiji 8, Gaozu annals](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=%E5%8F%B2%E8%A8%98/%E5%8D%B7008&oldid=2440965) places his death in the fourth month within the year-twelve account. These primary narratives do not themselves give the Western endpoints. A modern historical conversion must be separately sourced and checked; the prior research note cites a chronology for his Han kingship in 206 BC and death in 195 BC, but that conventional combined kingship-and-imperial span is not an accession-to-imperial-rule date or his lifetime, and does not date either cited Jinshu action to its endpoints.

For this chapter-local candidate, recommend **withdrawing the unqualified `BC 206-195` hint to `[]`** and preserving the undated specific sacrifice attestation. The narrower source-supported statement is that Gaozu performed the remembered sacrifice after attaining imperial status, not that it occupied all years of the unqualified interval. This is not a claim that he lacked activity between those years or that the conventional kingship/death chronology is false. The local attestation is genuinely undated in Jinshu; external evidence qualifies its relative setting, but the current chapter extraction does not include a separately cited, mapped chronology establishing exact hint endpoints. If a later reviewer wants a qualified historical-regnal hint instead, first establish its primary-source anchors, reliable Western-year mapping, precise meaning and a repository-supported provenance path; do not label 206 as the start of his imperial reign or bind the interval to the sacrifice. A later reception event, if dated, must remain separate and use only the approved `retrospective` vocabulary.

This is a proposed repair, not an edit to the retained candidate; it requires the normal sealed-packet workflow and independent review on the exact final fingerprints. It does not close the separate s0173, full-chapter editorial, chronology, identity or publication gates.
