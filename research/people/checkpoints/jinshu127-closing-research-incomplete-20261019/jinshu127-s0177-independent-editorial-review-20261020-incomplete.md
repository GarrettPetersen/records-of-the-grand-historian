The chapter witness [Jinshu 127](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/晉書/卷127)
reads `四年，僭即皇帝位於南郊，大赦，改元為建平，設行廟于宮南，遣使奉策告成焉。`
It supports the stated acts but does not itself specify what accomplishment
was reported or the detailed contents of the 策. `Document` preserves 策 more
conservatively than a specific accession proclamation; it should not be read as
proof of the document's full contents. `At the southern suburb` removes the
unwarranted explicit altar in the prior idiomatic text while retaining 南郊.

`僭` is evaluative: the two revisions attribute the label of usurpation to the
history rather than presenting its legitimist judgment as objective fact.
`Temporary ancestral temple` is preferable to an apparently physically traveling
building. [Tongdian, imperial ancestral temples](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=554543)
explains royal temples in ancestral terms; the [Jinshu Wen Qiao biography](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/晉書/卷067)
uses 行廟 for a ritual structure at which ancestral spirits are addressed. These
parallels contextualize the proposed reading without proving the exact dedication
of Murong De's particular temple.

The proposed replacements omit `焉`. I revise both to add `there`, taking the
immediately preceding 行廟 as its plausible locative referent. The syntax does
not definitively establish what accomplishment is reported, or exclude every
alternative antecedent; the added locative remains an explicitly documented
interpretation, not proof that the temple's construction was the achievement.
In particular, the two fields do not claim the document was itself the report,
that its content was an accession proclamation, or to whom the report was made.
