## s0177: 告成

Staging, retained draft, and [Wikisource 127](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/晉書/卷127) contain `設行廟于宮南，遣使奉策告成焉`, following De's assumption of imperial status, amnesty and change of era. The phrase carries an act of reporting or announcing accomplishment (`告成`), with a 策 conveyed by emissaries; the current idiomatic `sent envoys bearing the accession proclamation` omits the reporting act and specifies the document more narrowly than the sentence itself requires. The literal `sent envoys with the document to report completion` preserves more but does not represent the locative `焉` or its probable ritual context. Both should be reviewed together.

The immediate antecedent is the 行廟 south of the palace, making a report there a plausible reading; it is not proof, solely from grammar, of a particular document's complete contents or whether `成` refers narrowly to completion of the temple, assumption of imperial status, or the completed ritual. Compare the contemporaneously styled ritual expression `遣使以克定之功，策告祜廟` in [Quan Jin Wen, volume 6](https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=546474&if=gb), which explicitly identifies the accomplishment and the temple; it supports the possibility of a ceremonial report, not a definite reconstruction of this sentence. [Cefu Yuangui's 奉先 section](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/SK1959/chapter/1l95gkvr4bxvj) repeats the same account in an ancestral-worship context; it is a later compilation, not independent contemporaneous eyewitness evidence.

Conservative working wording, requiring proposal-level independent editorial decision and date review before application:

- Literal: `In the fourth year, he illegitimately assumed the imperial throne in the southern suburb, proclaimed a general amnesty, changed the era name to Jianping, established a temporary ancestral temple south of the palace, and sent envoys bearing a written document to report the accomplishment there.`
- Idiomatic: `In the fourth year, he claimed the imperial throne at the southern suburb, issued a general amnesty, adopted the Jianping era name, established a temporary ancestral temple south of the palace, and sent envoys with a written report of the accomplishment.`

`There` is interpretative; if subsequent review rejects its antecedent, omit it. The wording avoids asserting the event reported or the audience more precisely than the sentence warrants. `僭` reflects the history's delegitimizing perspective, which should not be silently presented as an objective judgment; the working literal flags that voice, while idiomatic `claimed` is less explicit. `Temporary` is a contextual rendering of 行廟 and does not assert that the structure itself moved. This review does not independently determine the Western year, nor approve any date or complete any chapter.
