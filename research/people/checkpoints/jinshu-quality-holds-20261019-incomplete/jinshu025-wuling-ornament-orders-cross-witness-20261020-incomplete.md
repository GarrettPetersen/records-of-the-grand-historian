## Source, current translation, and distinct claim predicates

The retained source's s0427 reads `胡廣曰：「昔趙武靈王為胡服，以金貂飾首。`
Its literal English has “gold sable,” and its idiomatic English has “gilded
sable hats.” In the retained three-date-holds candidate, zero-based claim 135
is an unresolved attestation of Wuling's Hu-dress reform, while claim 136 is
an unresolved event-participation claim whose action combines adopting Hu
dress and “gilded sable headgear.” Both cite s0427. The following s0428
attributes the later transfer of the ruler's cap to Qin; it does not itself
date Wuling's original act.

The [*Hou Hanshu*, “Carriages and Dress,” lower treatise](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/CADAL01026176/chapter/1lfti2qpw46xw)
also attributes to Hu Guang a retrospective explanation: Wuling modeled Hu
dress, used a gold ornament on the head, and placed a sable tail in front.
It separately mentions Qin's subsequent reuse. This transmitted parallel
provides a reason for an independent bilingual editorial reviewer to examine
whether “gilded sable” incorrectly combines two distinct materials. It is not
proof that the wording was an eyewitness description, that every later
headpiece had identical construction, or that s0427 literally specifies a
particular hat construction. A cautious candidate English rendering for
review is “adorned his head with gold and sable”; no translation or extraction
change is authorized here. The associated extraction action must be reviewed
with both English fields if any source correction is accepted, and any
downstream approval must be reconciled against the changed fingerprints.

## Different dated order and the limits of dating the ornament

The previously reviewed fixed [*Shiji* 43 revision](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=%E5%8F%B2%E8%A8%98%2F%E5%8D%B7043&oldid=7903603)
places Wuling's adoption and initial Hu-dress order in his nineteenth-year
entry, without identifying the s0427 gold-and-sable ornament's date. The
existing research also cites a scholar who conventionally associates the
nineteenth year with BC 307; that remains a historical year label, not a
verified proleptic-Gregorian point or a demonstrated year-envelope.

The [*Jinben Zhushu Jinian Shuzheng*, lower volume](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E4%BB%8A%E6%9C%AC%E7%AB%B9%E6%9B%B8%E7%B4%80%E5%B9%B4%E7%96%8F%E8%AD%89/%E5%8D%B7%E4%B8%8B)
presents a thirteenth-year passage with `貂服` and quotes a different
transmitted passage attributed to the *Bamboo Annals* via *Shuijing zhu*,
where Wei King Xiang's seventeenth year orders specified persons to wear
`貉服`. These are not interchangeable text witnesses; the wording and
year-number of the edited entry and the quotation differ. Neither explicitly
states that Wuling's own head was adorned with gold and sable in that year.

Yohei Kakinuma, “赵武灵王改革新探,” translated by Wang Bo, *Journal of
Handan College* 25.4 (2015), [p. 10, note 1, and p. 5 abstract](https://yohey.w.waseda.jp/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/shizhao201512.pdf),
discusses the quotation's apparent chronological tension with *Shiji*: it
identifies Wei Xiang 17 as Wuling 24, conventionally BC 302, versus Wuling
19, conventionally BC 307. The author suggests as a possibility that an
earlier order aimed at an upper stratum was followed by another order
extending dress requirements. That reconciliation is a scholarly inference,
not a dated source assertion that the two are the same order, an established
date of the ornament, or a calculation of Western calendar endpoints.

Consequently: do not date the gold-and-sable component of claim 136 simply
by importing BC 307 (or BC 302), and do not treat the later quotation as an
independent contemporary witness to the ornament. Any narrower temporal
repair for claim 135 still needs independent confirmation of event ownership,
the chosen date convention and the representation of uncertain year bounds.
Claim 136 remains unresolved unless its combined action is appropriately
resolved through the approved editorial/schema workflow and independently
reviewed evidence. Do not use a temporal-only repair to alter its predicate.
All other date holds, fresh independent full re-audit, identity and publication
