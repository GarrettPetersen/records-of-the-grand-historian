## Decisions

- **s0181, both fields: HOLD exact wording.** `簡…及…二百人為太學生` supports selection of 200 in total, with `及` introducing a further category; the old idiomatic omits both the selection and the second category. `子弟` supports young male family members rather than officeholders themselves. The translation `second-rank scholar households` is an uncertain institutional interpretation of `二品士門`, and the source alone does not establish the precise technical boundaries of that group or how to render it. The proposed wording is an improvement in structure but is not approved as a final exact translation until independent institutional evidence resolves this term. `學官`/`太學生` support the educational context; the exact institutional labels deserve the same care.
- **s0182, both fields: support the specific replacement.** `恭己南面而朝諸侯` describes the ruler facing south and receiving the lords at court; the old “to court the lords” reverses this relationship in English. The proposed “conduct myself respectfully, face south, and receive the lords at court” is supported in context. This is approval of the exact proposed fields as a focused correction, not an endorsement of every inherited phrase, the following joke, or the complete chapter.
- **s0194, both fields: HOLD exact wording.** In s0192–s0196, Hong requests office/income for his father, Zhang Hua objects, and De defends filial loyalty and appoints the father. Thus an honored parent is contextually plausible for `親尊`, but the line itself does not identify the parent, and the antecedent should not be treated as settled merely from that plausible reading. `散所輕之財，招所重之死` contrasts wealth lightly valued with the highly valued willingness/service involving death; “service at the risk of a life, which I value greatly” risks assigning the valuation to *life* rather than to the death-related service. The intended offer of service may involve someone ready to die, but the exact expression and reference require a further source-backed decision. Do not adopt the proposed fields as final or imply an actual death from this line. Later s0197 separately reports Hong's death by bandits; it does not retroactively remove the ambiguity of the pledge in s0191 or wording here.
- **s0272, literal: support as provisional paired-field correction.** `初，德迎其兄子超于長安，及是而至` distinguishes an earlier 迎 action from Chao's later arrival. “Had welcomed … from Chang'an” is liable to suggest that the earlier action was a completed reception. The proposal “had sought to bring … from Chang'an; at this point Chao arrived” aligns with the existing idiomatic and preserves that sequence without explicitly specifying a messenger or route. `迎` can involve going to meet or fetching as well as welcoming; “sought to bring” is an interpretive rendering, not proof of a particular method or unsuccessful attempt. Accept this targeted change subject to that qualification, not as a settled philological account of the intervening action.
- **s0275, literal: support the specific paired-field correction.** `子為父後者人爵二級` gives each qualifying son two grades of rank; it does not explicitly say that each already held a rank to be increased. The proposed grant avoids the unsupported assumption, retains the qualification and matches the idiomatic. This finding does not decide details of a particular rank system beyond what the phrase states.
