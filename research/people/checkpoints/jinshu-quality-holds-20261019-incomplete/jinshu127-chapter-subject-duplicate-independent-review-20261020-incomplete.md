## Main subject: seven local records

The source strongly supports p001, p020, p045, p066, p078, p114 and p154 as the same named individual, Murong De 慕容德. This is not merely matching labels: the chapter is a continuous biography whose initial title and opening (s0002–s0003) give `慕容德` and identify him as the youngest son of Murong Huang 皝. Key source anchors for each local record are:

- p001: s0002–s0003 explicitly name and introduce 慕容德. At s0037 寶 appoints 德.
- p020: immediately following that appointment, s0038 has 德 dispatch Murong Qing to resist an attack on Ye; this continues the subject of s0037.
- p045: s0082–s0083 narrate Murong Bao summoning Murong Zhong, who had originally urged 德 to take a supreme title. This follows the narrative of De's establishment of rulership, not a newly introduced homonym.
- p066: s0111 德 personally campaigns; the retrospective s0112–s0114 says Li Bian hoped 德 would lead the army out and then notes 德 did not. The local reference is continuous with the same ruler's earlier actions.
- p078: s0139–s0142 report successive actions by 德, culminating in s0141 where 德 sends Murong Zhong and s0142 where 德 advances to Langya. The record also cites the same chapter subject's s0177 enthronement.
- p114: s0197 德 hears of Hong's death; the next s0198 begins `明年，德如齊城`, explicitly continuing him.
- p154: s0242 德 hears and laughs; after the intervening Huan Xuan context, s0244 lists those who fled `於德`; s0245 identifies `德中書侍郎韓范`. Later s0261 has 德 speak. In s0275–s0279 this same narrative subject names Chao crown prince, dies, is buried, and receives `偽諡獻武皇帝`. Thus p154's posthumous-name claim belongs to the same chapter subject introduced as p001; preserve the source's framing of the title as illegitimate.

The conclusion concerns identity, not accuracy of every date, role, family edge, translation, or claim carried by these records. In particular any consolidation must avoid creating unsupported combined date assertions and must pass the existing cross-chapter identity and source-fingerprint gates.
