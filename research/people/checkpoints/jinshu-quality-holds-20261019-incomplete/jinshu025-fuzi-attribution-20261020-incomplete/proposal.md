## Editorial question, not an applied correction

The literal may more faithfully begin `The Fu Zi says`. One possible idiomatic rendering is `The Fu Zi, a work by Fu Xuan, says: ...`; the explanatory attribution is an addition supported by 047, not words in 025, so its use and styling require independent editorial adjudication. This would preserve a genuine English occurrence of the author's name for a backlink. Alternatively, render only `The Fu Zi says` and address whether the site has an appropriate work-to-author citation/link feature; do not falsely tag a book title as a personal name just to satisfy backlink coverage. If either translation changes, regenerate its source fingerprint, unit digest and candidate packet, then reconcile surfaces, dispositions and downstream approvals through the normal owner-controlled workflow. An isolated source wording change cannot reuse the existing sealed audit.
