## Narrow exact-field proposals for another reviewer

**s0214, idiomatic.** Chinese: `段宏等聞宮中有變，勒兵屯四門。` The source says Duan Hong and others heard of a palace disturbance, mustered/commanded troops and stationed them at the four gates; it does not explicitly say they sealed the gates. The existing literal says troops camped there and does not require the same repair.

- Before: `Duan Hong and the rest heard the alarm and sealed the four gates with troops.`
- Proposed: `Duan Hong and the others heard of the palace disturbance and stationed their troops at the four gates.`

The proposal preserves the immediate location and troop deployment; whether a fuller military nuance of 勒 needs expression remains open to editorial review.

**s0215, idiomatic.** Chinese: `德入宮，誅赤眉等，達懼而奔魏。` The current English renders `赤眉等` as Chimei's “faction”, which adds a specific organizational characterization, and omits 達's fear. A narrower rendering preserves both the execution of Chimei and others and the explicit fear:

- Before: `Murong De re-entered the palace, executed Hou Chimei's faction, and Murong Da fled to Wei.`
- Proposed: `Murong De entered the palace and executed Hou Chimei and the others; Murong Da fled in fear to Wei.`

The names are supported by s0212–s0213's immediate context, which names Hou Chimei and Murong Da. The existing literal already includes “Chimei and the rest” and fear. “Entered” avoids asserting anything beyond 入 here, although the prior sequence describes an escape from the palace.

**s0223, both fields.** Chinese: `深宜審量虛實，大校成敗，養兵厲甲，廣農積糧，進為雪恥討寇之資，退為山河萬全之固。` Both existing fields translate `萬全` as “ten-thousand-li”, inventing a distance absent from the source; 萬全 expresses complete security, not ten thousand li. The proposal changes only the final phrase; the inherited wording elsewhere is not endorsed as polished or fully resolved.

- Before (both fields): `It is deeply fitting to weigh void and substance, broadly gauge victory and defeat, nurture troops and harden armor, widen farming and pile grain, advance as capital to wash shame and punish bandits, retreat as a ten-thousand-li bulwark of mountains and rivers.`
- Proposed (both fields): `It is deeply fitting to weigh void and substance, broadly gauge victory and defeat, nurture troops and harden armor, widen farming and pile grain, advance as capital to wash shame and punish bandits, retreat behind the fully secure defenses of mountains and rivers.`

The proposed final clause is contextual English, not a claim that a literal withdrawal actually occurred. Independently consider whether the forward/backward contrast is better rendered as resources for offensive action and security in defense before accepting either exact full field.

Do not silently infer that an easy morning produces year-long unrest.
