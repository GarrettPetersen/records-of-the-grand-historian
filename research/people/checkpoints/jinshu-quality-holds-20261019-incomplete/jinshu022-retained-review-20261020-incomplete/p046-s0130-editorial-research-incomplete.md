## Finding

Review 4 identifies the unsupported Son of Heaven reading in s0130 and asks
for removal of claims 295–296 and the AD 266 active hint. The apparent problem
extends beyond those two dated rows. The source has `時邁其猶，昊天子之`;
both English translations instead turn `昊天子之` into a Son of Heaven who
upholds a rite. The lyric echoes the *Book of Odes*' *Shi Mai*, whose
`昊天其子之` is explained in a transmitted primary commentary as Heaven
treating the ruler as its son, rather than as a separate Son of Heaven being
the actor: [collected commentaries on *Shi Mai*](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/mid-page/7601263589027348530).
The parallel [Song shu music treatise](https://www.wenlinshe.com/tw/chapter/zhongguolishi/song-shu/zhi-di-shi-le-er.html)
also transmits the Jin hymn's `昊天子之` wording. This is contextual evidence,
not license to silently replace Jinshu's wording with the *Odes* wording.

The compact candidate `p046` is a historical `Son of Heaven` whose only
surfaces are Chinese `天子` and English `Son of Heaven` in s0130. All six
claims assigned to p046 and the person record's name and ruler role ultimately
depend on that same mistaken segmentation: sex, historicity, title, polity,
attestation and a ritual event. Merely deleting the two dated claims and
clearing the hint would leave the false title/person, remaining claims, and
both misleading translations in place. It would also retain a Chinese surface
whose characters cross the intended subject–verb boundary.

An independent editorial reviewer should check the reading and the complete
Jin hymn context, propose faithful literal and idiomatic translations of the
whole unit (including the interpretation of `其猶`), and review removal of
the false p046 person and its dependent surfaces and claims through the normal
false-person/editorial workflow. Check potential candidate-disposition and
downstream identity effects; do not mechanically remove a person without that
review. Other hymn mentions, including p047 and p048, are not covered here.
