## Source context and identity

The complete s0231–s0235 section is a single stair hymn headed
`祠潁川府君登歌`: s0232 says `于邈先後，實司於天`, and s0233
`顯矣皇祖，帝祉肇臻`. The next heading begins at s0236. Thus the
extracted notes saying that the earlier hymn title is outside the chunk obscure
the actual context. The heading indicates a named-by-title commemorated
ancestor, but the passage does not explicitly say that 先後 and 皇祖 are two
different people, or that either is a person different from the Yingchuan lord
p055. Their parallel presentation strongly motivates reviewing them as
possible references to the hymn dedicatee, not creating two additional
separate individuals by default. It does not make an identity merge automatic.

Jinshu 019 s0510 lists retrospective sacrifices to the Western General,
Yuzhang, Yingchuan and Jingzhao lords together with the Xuan, Jing and Wen
emperors: the same distinctive ceremonial series appears at Jinshu 022
s0221, s0226, s0231, s0236, s0240, s0247 and s0254. Jinshu 001 s0010–s0013
traces the family sequence and names `潁川太守儁` (courtesy 元異) as son of
the Yuzhang prefect and father of the Jingzhao governor. This is useful
cross-chapter evidence for identifying the hymn's Yingchuan dedicatee, but
neither the chain nor the heading expressly attributes each poetic epithet to
the same individual. Have an independent identity reviewer assess the link,
variant 儁/雋 if used, and the stanza as a whole. Do not infer dates from the
genealogy. The heading's commemorative person itself is not thereby disposable.
