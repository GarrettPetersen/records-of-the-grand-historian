## Primary evidence and limits

1. The local unit reads `時邁其猶，昊天子之`. [Jinshu 22, music treatise](https://skqs.dazhishi.com/show_gryoprjada.html) reproduces that text, in the song for sacrifice to Heaven and Earth and the five suburbs. The immediately adjacent evening-sacrifice song begins `皇矣有晉，時邁其德`; this parallels `時邁其` with an abstract noun, not a calendar phrase. The [Song shu 20 parallel](https://www.arteducation.com.tw/guwen/bookv_6727.html) also preserves `時邁其猶，昊天子之` and `於猶繹思` and attributes the songs to Fu Xuan. These are parallel witnesses, not independent commentaries.
2. The *Shijing* hymn [*Shi Mai*, with Mao commentary and Zheng Xuan's subcommentary](https://wk.100ke.info/zh-hant/%E6%AF%9B%E8%A9%A9%E6%AD%A3%E7%BE%A9/%E5%8D%B7%E5%8D%81%E4%B9%9D) reads `時邁其邦，昊天其子之`; Mao glosses `邁，行`, and Zheng explicitly construes Heaven as treating the ruler as its child (`天其子愛之`). The allusion supports the subject–verb reading of `昊天 / 子 / 之` in the Jin hymn. It does not by itself prove that Jin's abstract `其猶` means the same thing as *Shi Mai*'s `其邦` or settle the idiom `時邁` here.
3. In another *Shijing* passage, *Chang Wu*, Mao glosses `猶，謀也`, evidence that `猶` can mean a plan or counsel; see [a digitized historical *Mao shi* commentary](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/48/NAJDA-273-0199_%E6%AF%9B%E8%A9%A9%E8%A8%BB%E7%96%8F_%E5%B7%BB17%E4%B8%8B-18.pdf). That semantic possibility and the parallel `時邁其德` make ‘its plan’ plausible, but neither supplies a commentary on this exact Jin clause. `邁` might mean proceeding, carrying out, or advancing in this context; the agency and nuance of `時` remain uncertain. Do not infer a definite calendar or translate as ‘prudent awe’ merely from the modern senses of 猶.
4. Search-index OCR from [Keio's historical book image](https://iiif.lib.keio.ac.jp/KAN/8-19-12-2/pdf/8-19-12-2.pdf) and [Berkeley's historical book image](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/record/73665/files/000052370732901613_1C039.pdf) reports `時邁其猷`; the latter also reports `於循繹思`. Neither scan was independently inspectable here. Their OCR may be erroneous, and the witnesses' edition identities and independence remain unverified. Treat them as leads for image-based collation, not proof of textual variants; do not emend the local source.

No direct traditional commentary on the Jin hymn's exact `時邁其猶` or `於猶繹思` was located in this search. In particular, the latter cannot safely be used to assert that 猶 in s0130 means ‘caution’: the OCR lead suggests a possible different reading there, but does not establish it. Keep the full-unit translation held until an editor accepts a clearly qualified interpretation or a direct commentary/verified textual collation resolves it.

## Suggested editorial direction, not an approved correction

The secure second half is ‘August Heaven treats it as its child’, with `it` referring to Jin/the dynasty in the song's context. A source-backed tentative complete candidate, **only if the editorial reviewer accepts the above first-clause inference**, would be:

- Literal: ‘It advances its plan; August Heaven treats it as a child.’
- Idiomatic: ‘Jin carries its plans forward, and August Heaven cherishes it as its own child.’

Both use ‘plan(s)’ for 猶 and ‘advances/carries forward’ for 時邁 as contextually plausible, not demonstrably certain; the literal's initial subject and the idiomatic's explicit Jin are contextual inferences. Neither claims ‘seasons’ or adds a separate ruler upholding a rhythm. Omit ‘plans’ if a subsequent reviewer prefers to retain a hold over the interpretive uncertainty.
