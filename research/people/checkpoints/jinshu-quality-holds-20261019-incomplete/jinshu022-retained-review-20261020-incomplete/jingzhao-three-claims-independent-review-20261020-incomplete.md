sacrificial hymn for the Lord of Jingzhao; s0237 `于惟曾皇，顯顯令德`

chapter. That context supports ancestor-praise but does not settle whether
先後 is a distinct female queen mother, whether 皇祖 is a separate individual
from the dedicatee, or whether the extracted precise roles and confidence are
correct. Do not normalize their rows merely to satisfy a diagnostic; they need
complete person and candidate-accounting review. No mergers or deletions are
endorsed here. The current English `queen mother` and `she` in s0232, and
person p056's asserted female sex and occupation “minister for Heaven,” are
not established by the reported heading alone and require independent editorial
and extraction review.

This does not approve the proposed row as a whole or the chapter's other
