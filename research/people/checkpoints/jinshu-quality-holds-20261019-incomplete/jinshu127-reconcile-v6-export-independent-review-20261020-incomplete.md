If reconciliation finds no unscanned item, the ordinary `--reconcile-current` path continues and writes extraction and generated packet instead of writing only the requested review export. ## Coverage and handoff limitations

The packet enumerator considers exact **preferred English** strings only in units already evidenced by a person's mention or claim. It does not independently enumerate all alternative names, Chinese surfaces, unevidenced units, all scanner ambiguities, or a name span already covered by another person's mention. It therefore cannot establish chapter-wide coverage or correctness from three questions. The packet includes local person IDs and the immediate unit's zh/en/literal but not the persons' identifying records, neighboring source context or all unresolved candidate and reconciliation changes. A reviewer needs separately sealed, fingerprint-bound context and source-backed identity evidence, and must not infer that the three items exhaust the work. Subsequent candidate accounting, reviewer independence, source/extraction validation, date audit, identity resolution and publication gates remain mandatory.
