This is not a production approval, date-audit approval, or authorization to publish. The original research validator patch remains unchanged: SHA-256 `566766ef54b540ecbd56f429028ffa0de7b6ce8768ebca03dc3c387c96548a2a`. The added self-test amendment is a separate patch, SHA-256 `67a26b500b08d070d7b7658a8493fa361eed31cd64fa323148f9c02208c8346f`; `git apply --check` succeeds against the staging-script fixture after the original patch.

The original staging in-file self-test treats both a source-only death and an explicitly unresolved source-dated death as invalid without a Western active-date hint. Applying the original research patch alone therefore causes that self-test to fail. The separate amendment preserves the source-only rejection and tests that a source-dated death with an explicit substantive unresolved reason and no Western chronology can be captured as a hold, both expanded and compact. This capture is not an independent date approval. The validator self-test passes in the isolated fixture with both patches.

The 053 packet rejects a fabricated complete report while the unresolved hold remains. Mutating its source causes a synthetic previously current report to become stale and ebook/site readiness to reject it. No real historical date approval was fabricated or recorded.

Important pre-existing limitation: both the unpatched staging validator and the research-patched validator accept a synthetic person with an undated-only attestation and a death claim containing an unresolved date context *and* a separate concrete end date. This baseline exception means these checks do not establish that all mixed concrete-death shapes are rejected; it requires independent investigation and must not be represented as fixed by this patch. The focused checks also show that the validator tests for an independent attestation's existence, not semantic entailment. The independent date-audit and publication gates remain mandatory.
