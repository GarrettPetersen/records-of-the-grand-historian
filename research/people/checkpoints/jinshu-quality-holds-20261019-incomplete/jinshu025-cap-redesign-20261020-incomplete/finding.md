## Finding

Jinshu 025 s0444 attributes to Fuzi the statement that Emperor Ming of Wei,
because the cap resembled the tongtian and far-roaming caps, `改令卑下`.
The chapter's own literal and idiomatic English render this as having it
"made lower." But retained candidate claim-162 describes Cao Rui ordering the
cap "reserved for lower ranks" and claim-163 says the same with a causal
clause. Restricting who may wear it is a different assertion from lowering
the cap's physical form, and the source unit does not state that reservation.
Review-3's event descriptions correctly describe the cap as made lower, but
its chronology checks do not constitute an approval of the incorrect candidate
event wording.

The [Sui shu, juan 12](https://skqs.dazhishi.com/show_qmdkvxngyv.html)
quotes Fuzi describing Wei Mingdi changing the cap's shape, removing the
rolled tube, and adding an element resembling a mountain peak. The
[Tongdian, rites 17](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=555188) describes
the same material redesign, including lowering it relative to the other caps.
These later textual witnesses support the physical-form interpretation;
their accounts differ in detail, so do not import their additional details
into the Jinshu assertion without independent editorial review. Neither
witness cited here provides a numbered year for this particular act. The
review-3 research hold remains unresolved; this note does not justify a
calendar endpoint or an undated reclassification.
