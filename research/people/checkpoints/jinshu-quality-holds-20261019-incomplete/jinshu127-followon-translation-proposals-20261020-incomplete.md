## Exact replacement proposals

### s0181 literal and idiomatic — provisional, institution term held

Source witness: `建立學官，簡公卿已下子弟及二品士門二百人為太學生。`
This is one selection of 200, not 200 per category; `及` preserves a distinct
second category. The current idiomatic omits the category and the act of
selection; the literal collapses `及` into “down to”. The phrase
`second-rank scholar households` is deliberately close to the source but is
not yet a demonstrated technical definition of 二品士門. Obtain independent
institutional evidence before final publication.

- literal before: `He established schools, selecting two hundred sons of dukes and ministers down to second rank gentry households as academy students.`
- literal after: `He established educational offices and selected two hundred young men from the families of dukes and ministers and those below them, as well as from second-rank scholar households, as students of the imperial academy.`
- idiomatic before: `He founded an academy with two hundred students from elite families.`
- idiomatic after: `He established educational offices and selected two hundred young men from the families of dukes and ministers and those below them, as well as from second-rank scholar households, as students of the imperial academy.`

### s0182 literal and idiomatic — provisional exact clause replacement

Source witness: `後因宴其群臣，酒酣，笑而言曰：「朕雖寡薄，恭己南面而朝諸侯，在上不驕，夕惕於位，可方自古何等主也？」`
The lords attend the south-facing ruler at court, not the ruler “court[ing]”
them. Both existing fields have the same text and both proposed fields are:

- before: `Later at a banquet for his ministers, when the wine was warm he laughed and said, 'Though I am meager, I reverently face south to court the lords; not proud on high, anxious at my post by night; with whom among the ancients may I be compared?'`
- after: `Later at a banquet for his ministers, when the wine was warm he laughed and said, 'Though I am meager, I conduct myself respectfully, face south, and receive the lords at court; not proud on high, anxious at my post by night; with whom among the ancients may I be compared?'`

This targeted replacement does not certify the remaining inherited phrasing.

### s0194 literal and idiomatic — provisional, assess contextual antecedent

Source witness: `德曰：「吾方散所輕之財，招所重之死，況為親尊而可吝乎！`
Context s0192 says Du Hong requests support for his father; s0193 objects and
s0195 explicitly contrasts the ruler's family with the envoy's father. The
proposal keeps “an honored parent” rather than introducing an explicit person
not named in s0194. Both existing fields have the same text and both proposed

- before: `De said, 'I am now scattering the wealth I lightly esteem to recruit the deaths I heavily value; how could I be stingy for a revered kinsman?`
- after: `De said, 'I am giving away wealth, which I value lightly, to seek service at the risk of a life, which I value greatly; how could I begrudge it for an honored parent?`

The Chinese does not literally say “a life”; it is a contextual paraphrase
of 所重之死. Independently evaluate both this paraphrase and the reference
of 親尊 before acceptance; do not assert the envoy's death as an outcome.

### s0272 literal — paired-field repair

Source witness: `初，德迎其兄子超于長安，及是而至。` The later arrival

- before: `Earlier, De had welcomed his brother's son Chao from Chang'an; at this point he arrived.`

proposal as independently proving a summons or the precise method.
