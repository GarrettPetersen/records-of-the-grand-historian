## Decisions

**s0020 idiomatic: support the correction with a nuance.** The pinned source
`蒙遜引水灌城，城潰，執太守呂純以歸` has Mengxun divert/lead water to
inundate the city, its `城潰`, capture of governor Lü Chun, and return with
him. It does not say that Mengxun broke dikes. The proposed “diverted water
to flood the city; the city fell, and he returned with its governor Lü Chun
as a prisoner” removes the invented dikes and preserves these participants.
`城潰` can be rendered as the city being breached or falling in the siege;
“fell” is contextually plausible but does not explicitly represent the water
breaching/giving way aspect. If the host wants a closer proposal for separate
review, use: `Mengxun diverted water to flood the city; the city was breached, and he captured its governor Lü Chun and brought him back.`
This does not assert an enemy surrender, extra cause, or outcome beyond the
source. The existing literal has no dike claim and does not require the same
targeted correction; this is not a general endorsement of its prose.

**s0023 idiomatic: support the exact proposal.** The source
`呂弘去張掖，將東走，業議欲擊之` attributes the consideration of attack
to Ye, not to a council. `When Lü Hong left Zhangye and was about to flee east, Duan Ye considered attacking him.` preserves Lü Hong's impending
eastward flight, Ye as the person weighing attack, and Hong as object.
The following s0024–s0027 contains Mengxun's objection and Ye's reply and
unsuccessful pursuit; it does not supply a council as decision-maker. The
existing literal already attributes the deliberation to Ye. This review does
not certify all surrounding translation or historical dating.
