## Specific findings

1. **s0268, reversal of an adversarial relation, both English fields.** `昔郤克仇齊，子胥怨楚` is translated “Formerly Xi Ke avenged Qi, Wu Zixu resented Chu.” `仇齊` places Qi as the object of Xi Ke's hostility or vengeance; “avenged Qi” instead makes Qi the party vindicated. Render, for example, “Xi Ke took vengeance on Qi,” retaining the parallel with Zixu's resentment of Chu. Candidate p157 is unrelated to this finding; p158 is Xi Ke.
2. **s0255, opposite sense of conquest, both English fields.** `可以西並強秦，北抗大魏` is rendered “we could west merge with strong Qin and north resist Great Wei.” `西並強秦` is an ambition to annex/conquer strong Qin in the west, not an agreement to merge with it. Independent editorial review should determine natural wording in context.
3. **s0267, material omission in idiomatic field.** The Chinese specifies `步兵三十七萬，車一萬七千乘，鐵騎五萬三千`, then describes the extent, flags, and reverberating gongs and drums. The idiomatic “He staged a review west of the city with hundreds of thousands on parade” omits each specific reported figure (370,000 infantry; 17,000 vehicles; 53,000 armored cavalry) and the descriptive detail. The literal field retains the numbers; the idiomatic version should not erase them or silently assert their historical accuracy.
4. **s0272, material relationship omission in idiomatic field.** `德迎其兄子超于長安` identifies Chao as De's elder brother's son. The literal field gives “his brother's son,” but the idiomatic “Murong Chao, whom he had summoned from Chang'an” omits the relationship. Preserve the nephew relationship without claiming a named father not established in this sentence.
5. **s0275, material omission in idiomatic field.** The Chinese concludes `子為父後者人爵二級`; the literal field records a two-grade rank grant to sons succeeding their fathers, whereas the idiomatic field stops after the amnesty. Preserve this provision, subject to editorial review of its exact scope and phrasing.
6. **p157, false alias and mention boundary.** Candidate p157's second `personal` name claim has English `Formerly Shaokang`, and its s0262 English `personal-name` surface also spans `Formerly Shaokang`. `昔` means “formerly” and is not part of 少康's name. The candidate already separately records the correct `Shaokang` personal name. Review a targeted deletion of the false name claim and narrow the English hyperlink surface to `Shaokang`; preserve the historical adverb in the translation.
7. **p163, unsupported temporal attribution needs independent date repair.** p163 is explicitly an inferred intermediary, the unnamed father of Chao and De's elder brother, derived from s0272 `其兄子超`. His attestation claim cites that unit with `sourceDate.text` `義熙前後` and an AD 403–405 western interval; the person hint also says AD 405. Neither `義熙前後` nor a statement that the unnamed brother was alive in 403–405 occurs in s0272. The arrival of Chao is not evidence of his father's contemporary life. A later reference to a father and a lifespan/activity interval must not be conflated: inspect the chapter-wide evidence and the date-audit schema, remove or replace unsupported source-date/interval claims and hints only through a reviewed repair, and do not infer death or other dates. This observation does not assert that a properly labeled retrospective attestation would necessarily be impermissible.

## Exact English evidence and suggested minimal corrections

- **s0255** source: `跨地數千，眾逾十萬，可以西並強秦，北抗大魏。` Literal and idiomatic are both exactly: “Spanning several thousand li, hosts exceeding a hundred thousand, we could west merge with strong Qin and north resist Great Wei.” Minimal proposed replacement for both: “Spanning several thousand li with a host exceeding a hundred thousand, we could annex strong Qin to the west and resist Great Wei to the north.”
- **s0267** source: `於是講武於城西，步兵三十七萬，車一萬七千乘，鐵騎五萬三千，周亙山澤，旌旗彌漫，鉦鼓之聲，振動天地。` Literal: “Thereupon he drilled troops west of the city: three hundred seventy thousand foot, seventeen thousand carts, fifty-three thousand armored horse, filling mountains and marshes, banners covering heaven, the clash of gongs and drums shaking heaven and earth.” Idiomatic: “He staged a review west of the city with hundreds of thousands on parade.” Proposed idiomatic: “He reviewed his forces west of the city: 370,000 infantry, 17,000 chariots, and 53,000 armored cavalry. They stretched across the mountains and marshes, their banners filled the landscape, and the sound of gongs and drums shook heaven and earth.” These are the text's reported numbers, not independent verified troop counts; `車` may be rendered “vehicles” if the reviewer prefers not to specify military chariots.
- **s0268** source: `德登高望之，顧謂劉軌、高雅之曰：「昔郤克仇齊，子胥怨楚，終能暢其剛烈，名流千載。` Literal and idiomatic are both exactly: “De climbed high and looked over them, and said to Liu Gui and Gao Yazhi, 'Formerly Xi Ke avenged Qi, Wu Zixu resented Chu, and in the end they could vent their fierce integrity and their names flow a thousand years.” Minimal replacement of `avenged Qi` in both fields: `took vengeance on Qi`; retain the surrounding sentence for a separate broader stylistic review. The complete current English text in both fields is: “Formerly Shaokang with one brigade's host restored Xia and matched Heaven; how much more I occupy the land of the three Qi, rely on the hosts of five provinces, teach them with armies, train them with ritual yielding; above and below know righteousness, each man thinks to rouse himself; we repair armor and await a crack—this has been long.” The false `Formerly Shaokang` alias and English surface are extraction errors, not a request to remove “Formerly” from the translation.
