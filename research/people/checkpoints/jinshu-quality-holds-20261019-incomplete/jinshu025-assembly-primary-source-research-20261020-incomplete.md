## Additional primary witness, and its limits

[逸周書, 王會](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E9%80%B8%E5%91%A8%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7%E4%B8%83#%E7%8E%8B%E6%9C%83)
opens `成周之會，墠上張赤弈陰羽` and describes the ruler, regalia and
assembled guests. It places `殷公` at the left below the hall and `曹叔`
in a list at the inner platform. It also records `宗馬十二` among the
offerings. The overlap is a concrete textual parallel to investigate, not proof
of an independent eyewitness, of direct quotation, of exact identity, or that
the named participants literally bowed. Its body does not give a numbered
year for this gathering; the online edition intersperses commentary in angle
brackets, which must not be mistaken for the ancient main text. The same
edition's commentary identifies the gathering with a completed royal city;
that is an interpretation, not a calendar year in the primary account.

The later *逸周書管箋*, [volume 17](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/NGJ892412000002900153217/chapter/1lqqx3km88yeh),
reports a commentator's interpretation that, according to the Bamboo Annals,
the Chengzhou gathering was in Cheng's seventh year. It also discusses tension
with the dating of the enfeoffment of Tang Shu. This is evidence of a historical
interpretation, **not** confirmation that the particular assembly in Jinshu
025 was dated in an independently verified annals entry. I did not verify the
commentator's underlying Bamboo Annals wording in a stable primary edition,
and a regnal-year identification alone would not settle its conversion to an
absolute Western year. Do not promote that interpretation to a fact or an
endpoint without examining its original witness, textual history, event
identity, and competing chronologies.

## Consequence for the retained work

These sources offer useful leads but do not establish a defensible Western
year, starting or ending year, or exclusive reign interval for claim-10–15.
Do not remove the research hold or populate p004–p006's active hints on this
evidence alone. The role and submission/bowing assertions should also be
examined in a separate source/editorial review: *王會* describes the relevant
titles as positioned at the gathering but does not by itself attest the
Jinshu phrase's precise `低首` interpretation. This note does not decide that
issue, change any source, or approve any identity merge.

For further work, the existing owner can consult a stable, edition-specific

different possible readings and report uncertainty. Any proposed change still
requires the existing workflow's validation, independent complete re-audit,
and the editorial, identity, and publication gates.
