## Blockers

1. **Reviewer identity whitespace bypass.** Review replay checks `review.reviewerId.trim()` for nonemptiness but compares the untrimmed value to the untrimmed excluded IDs. A review packet excluding `author` accepts `reviewerId: "author "` and adds the reviewed person span. Normalize identifiers before both binding and comparison, or reject noncanonical strings. An exclusion list supplied by the caller also does not establish that all relevant authors are excluded: derive or validate it against trusted editorial/reconciliation provenance. String IDs alone do not prove independence.
2. **Partial scanner overlap hides unscanned complete name.** `reviewUnscannedSurfaces` skips a preferred-name occurrence whenever any scanner candidate overlaps it, not when it accounts for the entire occurrence. With source `段宏至。` / `Duan Hong arrived.`, preferred person `Duan Hong`, only candidate `Hong`, and an existing unchanged erroneous not-person disposition for that partial candidate, reconciliation returned no unscanned review request, no unresolved candidates/spans, and no mentions. The prior erroneous disposition is an explicit premise: this does not demonstrate that the patch newly corrupts a correct earlier decision or that the synthetic case exists in the corpus. It does show that the stated unscanned-occurrence coverage invariant fails and that a partial disposition may mask an omitted full-person link. Test complete-span coverage and real scanner/validator fixtures; do not assume every overlapping candidate fully accounts for an entire preferred name.
