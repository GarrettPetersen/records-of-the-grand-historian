## Findings

1. **Lü Meng: direction supported; refine the citation.** S0011 reports
   Chui's comparison `非復吳下阿蒙也`, not a contemporary activity by Lü
   Meng in Chui's time; it contains no AD 200–219 endpoints. The cited
   [Sanguozhi 54 transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E4%B8%89%E5%9C%8B%E5%BF%97/%E5%8D%B754)
   contains `非復吳下阿蒙` in Lü Meng's biography, in an annotation quoting
   *Jiangbiao zhuan* (`江表傳`), with Lu Su addressing Meng after study.
   Identify this as the quoted annotation rather than implying those precise
   words are necessarily the unannotated biography. It supports the allusion,
   not both endpoints of the retained activity interval.
2. **Wu Chen and Xia Ji: direction supported; distinguish two passages and
   compilation from the underlying text.** S0016 explicitly recalls Wu Chen's
   advice and the king setting Xia Ji aside, with no BC date. The cited
   [Ming compilation *Chunqiu Zuozhuan shushi*, juan 19](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B%E5%B7%A6%E5%82%B3%E5%B1%AC%E4%BA%8B_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%8D%B719)
   reproduces the Chu attack and the king's remonstrance under `成公二年`,
   then separately gives `七年` material including Wu Chen's opposition to
   Zifan and later departure. The appearance of a later-year heading does not
   date the earlier king's episode to BC 584. Do not treat this reorganized
   compilation's heading as an independently verified event-date conversion;
   it is not a direct photographic manuscript witness. The proposal properly
   stops short of claiming that BC 584 is historically impossible.
3. **Vocabulary and candidate boundary.** The proposed
   `event-participation` / `retrospective-reference` is the documented
   reception classification; the narrower `receptionType`, if supplied, must
   be `retrospective`, never `posthumous`. The event role or action must remain
   source-supported and specific to each person. Replacing these old living
   attestations and reconciling hints must be a complete reviewed change set;
   an undated retrospective event does not establish the subjects' dates of
   life or independently prove their death. Empty hints/research holds and
   candidate validation require the actual final structure; no candidate was
   provided for this review. A genuine unresolved chronology hold blocks date
   approval even where structural validation succeeds.
