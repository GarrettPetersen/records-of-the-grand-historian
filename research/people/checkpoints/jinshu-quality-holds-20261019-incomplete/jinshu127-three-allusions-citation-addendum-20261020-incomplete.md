attributed there to Lu Su, rather than necessarily the unannotated biography.

The linked [*Chunqiu Zuozhuan shushi*, juan 19](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B%E5%B7%A6%E5%82%B3%E5%B1%AC%E4%BA%8B_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%8D%B719)
is a Ming rearrangement: it reproduces the Chu attack and King Zhuang's
remonstrance under `成公二年`, followed separately by later `七年` material.
The later heading alone does not date the earlier episode to BC 584. The
compilation is not an independently verified Western-year conversion or a
direct photographic manuscript witness.
