## Primary-text checks

1. The [Book of Jin 53 transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7053)
   independently reproduces the source's childhood/affection sentence and the
   palace-fire episode: `太子時年五歲`. The age is part of this historical
   account; no year is named in the anecdote. A reported age alone is not an
   absolute date or a reason to change the chapter's translation.
2. The [Zizhi Tongjian 82 transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%B3%87%E6%B2%BB%E9%80%9A%E9%91%91/%E5%8D%B7082)
   likewise says `遹年五歲` in a passage introduced by `初`, looking back
   from the narrated appointment of Yu as Prince of Guangling. The surrounding
   annal's placement does not date this retrospective fire to the appointment
   year. These later histories may share prior material; they should not be
   counted as independent eyewitness confirmation.
3. A digital transcription of the [collected fragments attributed to Wang
   Yin's Book of Jin, volume 7](https://skqs.dazhishi.com/show_axyqxgaqdq.html)
   presents the same fire episode with `六七歲時`, i.e. six or seven, not
   five. It is a later reconstruction of an earlier work with editorial
   additions in the displayed text, not a verified manuscript edition or
   independently demonstrated historical event. It establishes a material
   source-attribution question to investigate, not a reason to overwrite the
   received chapter's five-sui account or invent a birth/fire year.
4. The [Book of Jin 3 imperial annal](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7003)
   records several separately dated fires in Taikang 10, then the emperor's
   death in Taixi 1. Neither cited annal entry explicitly equates one of those
   fires with the five-sui anecdote. Their proximity to the Guangling
   appointment is therefore **not** a license to assign the anecdote to a
   particular 289 fire. His presence in the anecdote may support an upper
   bound conditional on the identification and historical chronology, but a
   reviewed cross-source citation and calendar conversion would be needed;
   it would not prove every year of a full-reign activity range.
