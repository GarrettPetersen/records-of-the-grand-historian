## Blockers and compatibility

1. **Historical provenance gate has no supported recovery path for actual retained input.** On encountering an unscanned occurrence, v5 requires nonempty `extraction.run.agentId`, every chunk's `agentId`, and nonempty trusted editor IDs for applied repairs, before it can even emit the packet. The real Jinshu 127 corrected research extraction SHA-256 `ba7e5437d01c7065015f6f73e2be26d45390b97a8fcd85f454ac2c56355cf4f6` records a null overall extraction agent ID and null agent IDs for all seven Grok Bot chunks. Its applied review history SHA-256 `093ceb5a45d1be3500d1449ebdfbd4d304081049fd11e7257a50d1d93b26917e` also contains a historical reviewer with a null `agentId`. These are existing recorded fields, not evidence that the actual workers were identical or independent. The gate correctly refuses to invent identities, but the patch supplies no fingerprint-bound, independently reviewed provenance recovery mechanism for this retained work; it conflicts with the project's requirement to preserve and resume historical work. This is conditional on encountering an unscanned item, not a claim that every 127 reconciliation immediately errors.
2. **Derived editor exclusions are not proven complete.** The CLI calls `validateAppliedEditorialDecisions` and then treats the IDs in the supplied decision document as trusted editorial provenance. That validator checks that each included historical decision is represented among applied repairs, but does not require the converse: that every applied repair is covered by history. As a deliberate *synthetic tampering test*, I took the real 127 extraction with 36 applied repairs and supplied only the last two of its seven history records. The two records contain six decisions and list `/root/chapter127_repairs` and `/root/chapter127_source`; `validateAppliedEditorialDecisions` nevertheless accepted the shortened document. Under v5 the exclusions derived from this shortened document would omit earlier editors. This proves a validation/integrity gap, **not** that the actual complete history lacks coverage or has been tampered with. Require a sealed and complete provenance/approval set and reverse coverage for applied repairs before relying on it as an exclusion set; do not fabricate missing IDs or weaken current gates.

Unchanged caveats include v4's limited source-context exploration and the absence of a tested complete host-owned CLI review export/restart/replay with real fingerprinted artifacts. Keep the patch isolated, address the blockers, then run fresh independent adversarial, compatibility and end-to-end checks. This review is not an approval of the chapter or the general reconciler.
