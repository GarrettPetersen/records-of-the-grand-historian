The local s0090 reads `廆懼，首服。`; naming Hui follows its explicit
subject. The [ordinary Wikisource transcription of chapter 108](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7108)

same chapter](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8_(%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC)/%E5%8D%B7108)
reads `燾懼首服`; its linked permanent revision is [oldid 616370](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8_(%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC)/%E5%8D%B7108&oldid=616370).
The immediate narrative describes Hui threatening Cui Tao and then sending Tao
back to Cui Bi. Tao is consequently a plausible intended subject, but this is
an inference from context, not proof of an original reading. Neither digital
transcription establishes manuscript ancestry or priority, and no manuscript
was inspected. The note accurately describes the alternate as a digital
transcription without asserting an edition's or manuscript's authority.

Taiwan's Ministry of Education [dictionary entry for 首服](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=130255&la=0&powerMode=0)
gives a verbal sense of self-reporting and admitting guilt. “Confessed” is a
defensible concise rendering, while “groveled” adds a physical action the
entry does not establish. The entry does not settle the subject here; the
translation does not specify an offense.
