## Findings

1. **r0001, s0037 — supported; represented.** `以都督專總南夏` concerns
   the jurisdiction of the commander, not the season summer. Candidate literal
   reads “oversee the southern Xia domains”; idiomatic “southern affairs”
   conveys the intended administrative scope more generally. The six provinces
   and preceding appointments are present. This does not independently establish
   every nuance of 南夏's geopolitical extent.
2. **r0002, s0007 — supported; represented.** `及垂嗣位，改范陽王，稍遷魏尹，加散騎常侍`
   occurs in De's biography; the subject of his change of title and promotion is
   De, not Chui. The candidate supplies `[De]` and “Yin of Wei” in literal;
   idiomatic also names De and the Wei capital-region office. The parenthetical
   explanation is interpretive, not a separate source quotation.
3. **r0003, s0014 — supported; represented.** `後遇垂敗，徙于長安，苻堅以為張掖太守，數歲免歸`
   says Chui was defeated and De was dismissed and returned after several
   years. Candidate idiomatic removes the added claim of a disgraced return;
   literal also preserves dismissal and return. The source does not itself
   specify the cause of the dismissal.
4. **r0004, s0056 — supported; represented.** `章司馬丁建率眾來降` names
   丁建, not 丁通. Candidate idiomatic identifies Ding Jian as Tuoba Zhang's
   chief clerk and preserves his surrender; “defected” is a contextual rendering.
5. **r0005, s0056 — supported; represented.** The same `丁建` is Ding Jian
   in candidate literal as well. This is a separate field correction, not a
   second individual or event. A separate extraction/identity audit must check
   that related name claims did not preserve the original confusion.
6. **r0006, s0065 — supported; represented.** `尋而寶以德為丞相，領冀州牧，承制南夏`
   again uses 南夏 in an administrative, not seasonal, setting. Candidate
   literal has “delegated authority over the southern Xia domains”; idiomatic
   has “empowering him to govern the south.”
7. **r0007, s0105 — supported with a rationale correction; represented.**
   `德遣其中軍慕容法、撫軍慕容和等距之，王師敗績` supplies both named
   commanders, “and others” (等), resistance (距), and the defeat of 王師.
   Context s0104 explicitly introduces a Jin army, so the candidate's “Jin
   army” is supported contextually. The proposal's statement that the name
   after 撫軍 was corrupted in this packet is **not** supported by this
   witness: it explicitly reads 慕容和. The candidate retains the correct
   names, collective, and action; do not repeat that corruption claim as fact.
8. **r0008, s0131 — revision needed to the old rationale; name represented.**
   The retained text reads `避閭渾昔負國恩`, while the candidate has “Pilü
   Hun.” A separately edited witness of this same biography has
   `辟閭渾昔負國恩` in the same speech, followed in the same narrative by
   `遣使喻齊郡太守辟閭渾` and the account of Hun's flight and death:
   [Jinshu 127, Shidianguji](https://www.shidianguji.com/en/book/LS0005/chapter/LS0005_1507).
   This supports associating the two written forms in this narrative, but the
   old assertion that 避閭 is simply a *corruption* is stronger than the
   evidence presented here: retain it as the current witness's spelling and
   the other spelling as a witnessed variant, not a proven emendation. The
   candidate's name reading remains; whether both precise written forms and
   corresponding extraction claims are faithfully preserved requires an
   identity/claim audit.
9. **r0009, s0177 — revision needed; the candidate has superseded the old
   wording.** `四年，僭即皇帝位於南郊…設行廟于宮南，遣使奉策告成焉`
   supports the accession, hostile/usurpation framing, temple, envoy, and
   reporting of completion. The old “envoys bearing the accession proclamation”
   does not explicitly render 告成 and construes 策 more narrowly than this
   sentence establishes. Both candidate fields instead say a document was
   carried “to report the accomplishment there” and explicitly describe the
   history's usurpation framing. The idiomatic retains “Long'an 4 (AD 400),”
   but 四年 by itself does not name the era: corroborating that contextual
   dating and its exact downstream claims remains for the independent date
   audit. This focused review is not that audit.
10. **r0010, s0243 — supported; represented.** `時桓玄將行篡逆，誅不附己者`
    uses 將 with 行 as “was about to,” not a separately introduced general.
    Candidate literal makes Huan Xuan the actor and avoids the invented
    general. The idiomatic “purged dissenters” is a compressed interpretation
    of killing those not attached to him; it should not be used to invent a
    separate group, role, or named person.
11. **r0011, s0271 — supported; represented.** `德以慕容鎮為前鋒，慕容鐘為大都督，配以步卒二萬，騎五千，克期將發，而德寢疾，於是罷兵`
    names the vanguard and supreme commander, 20,000 infantry, 5,000 cavalry,
    a fixed departure date and illness before the force was called off.
    Candidate idiomatic preserves these details; literal also names them.
    “Halted the army” should be understood as the force/campaign being called
    off, not an independently attested duration or exact stopping location.
