# Jinshu 127: focused translation findings, s0205–s0213 (incomplete)

Research-only proposals; independently reviewed in the companion
`jinshu127-s0205-s0213-independent-source-review-20261020-incomplete.md`, but
not applied or approved as a whole chapter. Source is the
retained reconciled candidate `jinshu127-s0177-two-dates-reconciled-20261020-incomplete/jinshu/127.json`,
SHA-256 `cf2d789947d99dbd56ed75c96eb7e097d85e2699cf73e96268cf2fd22b90c264`.
The latest p141 chronology proposal does not change this source; its extraction
SHA-256 is `5f5e8325fd88a44c682cfa3ae475aad26df0841f0b39878b6e478691cf5b3fc8`.

1. **s0205, both fields:** `遂以謨從至漢城陽景王廟` says that he took
   Yan Mo along to the temple of the Han Prince Jing of Chengyang. Both
   translations instead say “the temple of Prince Jing of Chengyang at
   Hancheng,” treating 漢 as part of a place name. Proposed minimal correction:
   replace that phrase in each field with “the temple of Han Prince Jing of
   Chengyang.” The remainder of the long sentence is not approved here.
2. **s0210, idiomatic:** `立冶于商山，置鹽官于烏常澤，以廣軍國之用`
   says he set up smelting at Mount Shang and appointed/established salt
   officials at Wuchang Marsh to expand resources for military and state use.
   The current “opened ironworks … and salt monopolies … to swell the army's
   coffers” adds iron and monopolies and omits the state. Suggested revision:
   “He established smelting works on Mount Shang and a salt office at Wuchang
   Marsh to increase resources for the army and the state.” The literal field
   preserves these distinctions; it is not otherwise approved here.
3. **s0213, both fields:** `中黃門遜進扶德逾城，隱於進舍` names the
   palace attendant Xun Jin and says De hid at Jin's residence. The literal
   field misreads `進舍` as “a forward lodging”; the idiomatic field omits both
   Xun Jin's name and the location. Suggested revision, subject to style and
   role review: “Palace attendant Xun Jin helped De over the wall, and De hid
   at Jin's residence.” This avoids assigning the act of hiding to Xun Jin.

These are targeted proposals only. The separate reviewer supports the findings
and recommends the conservative wording above; no editorial decision has been
sealed. Other sentences and downstream date and identity evidence remain
unchecked.
