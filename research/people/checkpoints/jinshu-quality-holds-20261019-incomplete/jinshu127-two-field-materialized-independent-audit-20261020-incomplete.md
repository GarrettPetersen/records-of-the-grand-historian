## Reconciliation defect and correction

**The original held extraction must not be published.** In s0213, `中黃門遜進扶德逾城，隱於進舍。` refers to palace attendant Xun Jin and his residence; the second English `Jin` is not the polity. The unsafe reconciler added disposition `jinshu:127:cand_b79e43b7adcb8ce4` as `not-person / polity / Jin` while preserving a p125 English `Jin` span but removing its candidate reference. Its zero unresolved counts and validator pass did not detect this source error.

The corrected compact extraction differs from that held compact extraction **only** by removal of the `cand_b79e43b7adcb8ce4` / `Jin` disposition entry. Upon independent expansion and strict validation, p125's existing second `Jin` span at code points 64–67 again references that candidate, and no disposition remains for it. Both its initial `Xun Jin` and its Chinese `遜進` span remain. This correction is supported by the source, not by a blanket inference that every `Jin` is a person.
