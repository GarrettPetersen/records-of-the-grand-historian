## Source and proposed scope

At s0011, Chui says the addressee is no longer `吳下阿蒙`. This is a
comparison to Lü Meng, not an account of Lü Meng's own activity at the time
of Chui's speech. [Sanguozhi 54](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E4%B8%89%E5%9C%8B%E5%BF%97/%E5%8D%B754)
attributes the matching phrase to Lu Su on observing Lü Meng's progress in
learning. This supports the allusion's identification, but neither the
Jinshu sentence nor the quoted passage establishes the present claim's
AD 200–219 interval. Proposed: remove original p009 `claim-78` as a living
attestation and preserve the comparison as a separately classified, undated
`event-participation` / `retrospective-reference` for p009, evidenced by
`s0011`; do not infer a date for Chui's speech or Lü Meng's activity here.
The existing p009 hint `AD 200-219` has no cited endpoint evidence in this
proposal and requires independent research or a clearly recorded research
hold, not automatic approval. This does not prove those years historically
false.

At s0016, Murong De recalls that Chu's King Zhuang accepted Wu Chen's advice
and did not take Xia Ji: `納巫臣之諫而棄夏姬`. The sentence supplies no
Western year. The [Zuozhuan account](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B%E5%B7%A6%E5%82%B3%E5%B1%AC%E4%BA%8B_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%8D%B719)
reports the advice in the Chu attack on Chen narrative and later events; its
later chapter heading is not by itself evidence that the earlier event took
place in the currently stored BC 584. Proposed: remove original p012
`claim-81` and p013 `claim-82` as living attestations at BC 584. Preserve the
recalled example as one undated `retrospective-reference` apiece, with p012
described as the adviser and p013 as the person referred to, each tied to
`s0016`. The p012 and p013 activity hints `BC 584` both require separately
sourced endpoint research or explicit research holds. This neither disproves
the exact year nor equates the date of the later speech with the earlier
event.
