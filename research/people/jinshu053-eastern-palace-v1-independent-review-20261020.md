## Source assessment

The pinned source says `元康元年，出就東宮，又詔曰：「遹尚幼蒙，今出東宮，惟當賴師傅群賢之訓。`
The current v1 wording correctly changes the former `leaves/left the Eastern
Palace` to movement **to** that palace; in context `出就東宮` introduces the
same move as the quoted `今出東宮`. An independent parallel in
[Tongdian juan 30](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E9%80%9A%E5%85%B8/%E5%8D%B7030)
says `始之東宮` and `出止東宮`, supporting going to or taking up residence
there. Its different wording should not be silently substituted into the source.
The `Yu` named in the edict is the crown prince and the source opens a quotation
here. The candidate correctly leaves that quotation open until s0204.

The remainder is a narrower hold: the candidate reads `師傅群賢` as tutors
**and other** worthy men. A [study of Western Jin crown-prince tutors](https://repository.kulib.kyoto-u.ac.jp/dspace/bitstream/2433/240644/1/rek16_03.pdf)
construes the expression as worthy men serving as tutors (`師傅の群賢`). That
is evidence of a plausible alternative syntactic reading, not proof that the
draft's conjunction is impossible. Do not treat the extra distinct `other`
group as conclusively established. Consider the conservative `the instruction
of his worthy tutors`, subject to a separate source/translation review; do not
change the date or source text based solely on this short review. The noun
`men` is also more specific than `群賢` itself, though the six immediately
preceding tutors are male in context.

The following unchanged s0204 closes the quotation, but its `相長益` remains
on hold: English `help one another improve` may suggest mutual improvement among
companions rather than their benefiting the prince. It requires review with
s0205, not automatic approval merely because the quotation punctuation joins.
