## p009, Lü Meng 呂蒙

[Sanguozhi 55, Gan Ning biography](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E4%B8%89%E5%9C%8B%E5%BF%97/%E5%8D%B755#%E7%94%98%E5%AF%A7)
explicitly includes 呂蒙 among the officers accompanying Sun Quan at Hefei in
Jian'an year 20. Its linked Western year is AD 215. This supplies an activity
attestation in 215, not evidence that his activity began in 200 or ended in
219. A reviewer must verify identity against the allusion before adopting it.

[Sanguozhi 54, Lü Meng biography](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E4%B8%89%E5%9C%8B%E5%BF%97/%E5%8D%B754#%E5%91%82%E8%92%99)
reports that he died aged 42 after the capture of Guan Yu, but does not give a
Western year or explicit death-year heading in that biography.
[Zizhi tongjian 68](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%B3%87%E6%B2%BB%E9%80%9A%E9%91%91/%E5%8D%B7068)
places his death after its twelfth-month account of Guan Yu's capture in the
Jian'an 24 narrative. It is a later historical synthesis, not a direct
contemporary witness. Do not turn the regnal year into a firm AD 219 death:
the twelfth lunar month crosses a Western calendar boundary, and the passage
does not establish a Western day of Lü's death. The age also does not justify
an exact Western birth year without determining the date and age convention.
Keep both endpoints unresolved unless separately checked.

## p012, Wu Chen 巫臣

The [Zuozhuan, Duke Cheng year 8](https://ctext.org/chun-qiu-zuo-zhuan/cheng-gong-ba-nian)
records the Jin ruler sending Shengong Wuchen to Wu; see also the readily
visible transcription in the [Siku-labelled *Chunqiu Zuozhuan shushi*, juan
19](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B%E5%B7%A6%E5%82%B3%E5%B1%AC%E4%BA%8B_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%8D%B719),
which explicitly headings the embassy `八年秋`. This compilation is a later
arrangement of the earlier narrative, not an independent original witness.
For the conventional Western conversion, [Yang Zhaogui's article](https://cup.cuhk.edu.hk/chinesepress/journal/ICS58/ICS58_41-64.pdf),
p. 42, equates Cheng year 8 to 583 BC. These together support a proposed
activity attestation at **BC 583**, independently of the allusion's speech;
they do not establish birth, death, or an activity interval endpoint. Verify
whether the date-packet schema permits recording externally sourced chronology
and linking its evidence without falsely attributing the embassy to s0016.

## p013, Xia Ji 夏姬

The [Zuozhuan, Duke Cheng year 2](https://ctext.org/chun-qiu-zuo-zhuan/cheng-gong-er-nian)
recounts her situation, including Wu Chen's remonstrance, then later his
journey with her. The [Siku-labelled rearrangement](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B%E5%B7%A6%E5%82%B3%E5%B1%AC%E4%BA%8B_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%8D%B719)
preserves the same narrative under the Cheng 2 heading; the account explicitly
looks back to King Zhuang and events before the Battle of Bi before moving
forward to the subsequent journey. [This scholarly article](https://31653860.s21i.faiusr.com/61/8/ABUIABA9GAAgsoTcuAYooO3lmwc.pdf),
pp. 5–6 of the PDF, calls the section Cheng 2 (589 BC); that identifies the
chronicle section, not a demonstration that all the events it recounts
occurred in 589. The narrative places the journey after the Battle of An, but
further source and calendar work is needed to decide whether an exact activity
year is justified. It does not substantiate the old **BC 584** hint. Leave her
activity date on research hold rather than infer it from Wu Chen's 583 embassy.
