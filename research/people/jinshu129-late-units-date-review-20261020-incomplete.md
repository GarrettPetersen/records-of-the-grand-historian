1. **An accession is not an observation by Mengxun.** `claim-968` states that
   p117 witnessed the Song accession in AD 420, and `claim-928` treats this
   accession as his dated attestation. In s0255, “義熙八年僭立，後八年而宋氏受禪”
   gives a chronological comparison: eight years after Mengxun's accession, the
   Song house received the abdication. It does not say he witnessed, attended,
   learned of, or participated in the accession. Keep the comparative
   chronology distinct from his personal activity; review the hint and claim
   against all other evidence, without inferring a replacement date. Full-unit
   Chinese SHA-256: `d8c00225eb754b3b5198e937d4c40c8966047f5e3a37947115251bb90914c67c`.
   A separate reviewer confirmed the packet IDs and text and noted that the
   same passage also gives his later death: it does not rule out his having
   lived through the accession. The concern is unsupported personal
   observation or participation, not a claim of non-contemporaneity; the word
   “witnessed” may itself be read more than one way.

2. **A detention is not a five-year activity record for the detainer.**
   `claim-817` makes p097 Qifu Chipan's attestation an AD 416–421 interval,
   citing s0202: “在熾磐所五年，暉又為之固請，乃得還姑臧。” In context, s0201
   says Chipan took Kui Ren captive, and s0202 says Ren spent five years in
   Chipan's custody before Duan Hui pleaded again and Ren returned. The passage
   does not specify Western-year endpoints or show Chipan personally active
   throughout that interval. Distinguish the captivity duration and Ren's
   return from a dated activity interval for Chipan; neither propose a new
   Western date nor assume that every possible interval means continuous
   activity without checking the schema. Full-unit Chinese SHA-256: s0201
   `3410ced530ed86875e5cab8a8895e4f2a1f03cf13476132cb91179f9fd87d7ec`;
   s0202 `0800182ea5610b0e2efd4afaf3395c808a5eac9c0929150f511279b6a276e69e`.

3. **Same embassy apparently assigned two years.** p103 Zhu Lingshi's
   `claim-843` assigns his sending of envoys to AD 416 from s0183, “晉益州刺史硃齡石遣使來聘”;
   `claim-844` assigns the sending “last winter” to AD 415 from s0188,
   “去冬益州刺史硃齡石遣使詣臣”. The latter appears in Mengxun's response
   memorial (s0184–s0192) and naturally refers back to the prior embassy.
   These two send-event dates need reconciliation, including whether the first
   is instead intended as the date of arrival, and independent evidence for the
   memorial's year and Chinese-calendar boundary. The cited text alone does not
   establish either Western year. Full-unit Chinese SHA-256: s0183
   `adf08997240a0827a662db41ff3691e3cca26cc8e4f19aa75bb1a39555a033fa`;
   s0188 `35c5549420f71ec362b331d2d3fce0fcbdf21b4481cfa033362034f7968d7df0`.

4. **Earlier episodes are historical allusions, not contemporary appearances.**
   In s0225, Juqu Chengdu invokes “高祖有彭城之敗” in advice to Mengxun.
   `claim-1013` and `claim-1014` place the Han founder's Pengcheng defeat in
   BC 205, while p125 has a BC 205 activity hint. The sentence establishes a
   later quotation of an earlier event but supplies no Western year; independently
   source the original event/conversion and distinguish it from the later
   reception. Similarly, s0259's historian invokes “陳、吳之事”; p135/p136
   `claim-1068`, `claim-1070`, `claim-1072`, and `claim-1074` assign the earlier
   rebellion to BC 209, but that sentence itself gives no numeric date and is a
   later retrospective allusion. Retain any justified historical event and its
   independently checked date separately from the reception; do not treat the
   historian's allusion date as the subjects' living activity. Full-unit Chinese
   SHA-256: s0225

5. **A reign label does not itself describe its ruler's activity.** p131's
   `claim-1054` treats “安帝隆安五年” in s0255 as an AD 401 attestation. In the

   provincial governor, not a described act or appearance of Emperor An.
   Separately check the reign conversion and any other source for the emperor's
   own activity; the label alone is insufficient personal event evidence. The
   s0255 hash is recorded in finding 1.
