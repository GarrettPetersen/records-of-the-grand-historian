## Source and claim comparison

The chapter names Murong Huang (`p001`) and his father Murong Hui (`p002`).
In s0006, `廆為遼東公，立為世子` states that Hui was Duke of Liaodong and
Huang was established as heir; this sentence contains neither `建武初` nor
an explicit year. The following s0007 begins `建武初` and describes Huang's
appointments, enfeoffment and campaigns. It does not explicitly date Hui's
ducal title or Huang's appointment as heir in s0006.

Compact extraction claims 61 (p002, ducal title), 62 (p001, heir status) and
136 (p002, attestation) each cite only s0006 yet contain `sourceDate.text`
`建武初` and a precise Western year AD 317. The s0006 source-date attribution
is therefore incorrect as a quotation or direct temporal context. It is not
proof that Hui was not active in 317, nor by itself proof that Huang did not
become heir in 317. Claim 130 (p001 attestation) cites s0006 **and** s0007,
and so its AD 317 question differs from the s0006-only claims. Claims 63–66
are based on s0007, where `建武初` is genuinely present; the Western conversion
to one exact year needs its own dated source or calendar justification and
cannot be rejected merely because the claim 61 citation is faulty.

The report marks claims 61, 62 and 136 supported, citing s0006 and using the
same generic explanatory text as other checks, without a specific source for
the date conversion. Its 181 `personChecks` all use exactly the same evidence
array, s0002 `慕容皝`, including for p002 Hui and p003 Yuwen Qidegui. The
report has no external references and records no findings. These facts
demonstrate inadequate recorded support for the cited checks, not that all
593 item verdicts or all 181 people's dates are false.

## Chronological caution

Pinned Jinshu 108 s0058 says Emperor Min appointed Hui duke of Changli and
Liaodong during Jianxing; s0096 later describes promotion to *Liaodong
commandery* duke. [Taiping Yulan 121](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh/%E5%A4%AA%E5%B9%B3%E5%BE%A1%E8%A6%BD/0121),
quoting Cui Hong, attributes appointment to that latter rank to Taixing year 4
(conventionally 321). These are potentially distinct appointments, and the
earlier account's offer/acceptance and text traditions require study. Do not
replace the AD 317 assertions mechanically with 321 or assume which title
Jinshu 109 s0006 means without evidence. The order of the undated s0006 and
the dated following s0007 should not itself be treated as an exact date.
