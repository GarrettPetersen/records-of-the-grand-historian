## Evidence

- Local 053 s0024 reads `葵菜、藍子、雞、面`, rendered as “mallow,
  indigo seed, chickens, flour” literally and “greens, dye seed, chicken,
  and flour” idiomatically. Its paired passage in 056 s0167 has the same
  four-item Chinese reading, but both English fields render 面 as noodles.
- [Wikisource's Jinshu 053 transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7053)
  also has 藍子. [Shidianguji's Jinshu 053 transcription](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/LS0005/chapter/LS0005_791)
  instead has 籃子 and 麵. These digital presentations are not proven to be
  independent witnesses, nor have I inspected page scans or established
  either's exemplar.
- [Yuding pianzi leibian, Siku transcription, under 葵菜](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E5%BE%A1%E5%AE%9A%E9%A7%A2%E5%AD%97%E9%A1%9E%E7%B7%A8_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%85%A8%E8%A6%BD7)
  quotes Jinshu's crown-prince account with 籃子 and 麵. This is a later
  transmitted quotation, not proof of the Tang archetype or independent
  ancestry from the Shidianguji reading.
- More importantly, [Chajing's Siku transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%8C%B6%E7%B6%93_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%85%A8%E8%A6%BD)
  quotes the *Jiang family biography* for the same memorial with 藍子 in a
  differently ordered list. [Taiping yulan's Siku transcription](https://wk.100ke.info/wiki/%E5%A4%AA%E5%B9%B3%E5%BE%A1%E8%A6%BD_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%85%A8%E8%A6%BD29)
  likewise attributes 藍子 to the Jiang biography, again with a differently
  ordered list. These are later transmitted quotations of that work, not
  inspected original Jin manuscripts or automatically independent readings.
  Their different lists and transmission mean they cannot automatically be
  substituted for either Jinshu passage.

## Consequence and remaining review

The *Chajing* and *Taiping yulan* quotations materially caution against
silently replacing the local 藍 with 籃 on the strength of other digital
Jinshu transcriptions. Conversely the later 籃 reading is real evidence that
cannot be erased by calling “indigo seed” certain. The cited texts do not by
themselves establish what commodity 藍子 means, the reading of an authoritative
Jinshu edition, or whether 面 is flour or noodles. Punctuation and 麵
normalization in a modern digital text are not decisive about the historical
object. A source editor should inspect edition scans and lexical evidence,
record the variant and uncertainty, and make an explicit wording decision for
both 053 s0024 and the parallel 056 s0167. No source, translation, extraction,
review state, queue claim, or canonical data was changed by this note. The
chapter-wide editorial, independent date, identity, and publication gates all
remain open.
