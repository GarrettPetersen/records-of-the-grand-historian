## Independent source check

I inspected the Chinese units in the local source files. Jinshu 025 s0427
attributes to Hu Guang the statement that Zhao Wuling made Hu dress and adorned
his head with gold and sable; that unit does not give a year. Shiji 043 s0436
places the assembly in spring, first month, of the nineteenth year. The king
expresses the intention to adopt Hu dress in s0442 and discusses instruction in
Hu dress and mounted archery in s0451. Following that discussion, s0462 says
`於是遂胡服矣`. In s0510–s0512 the uncle is given the dress, wears it to
court the next day, and the Hu-dress order is first issued. The next explicit
year heading, twentieth year, appears at s0532.

This supports the note's description of the narrative and its distinction
between the first-month assembly and later events; the first month is not
expressly attached to the adoption or order. Placement within a historical
regnal-year narrative is not by itself proof of an exact proleptic Gregorian
day or Western calendar year. The Shiji adoption passage does not mention the
specific gold-and-sable headgear, so transferring its chronology to that detail
would need additional evidence. The note appropriately leaves that detail and
the combined claim unresolved rather than authorizing such a transfer.
