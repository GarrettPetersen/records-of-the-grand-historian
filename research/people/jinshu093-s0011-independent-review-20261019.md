**Required correction:** the cited *Book of Han* biography and commendation of
Shi Dan are chapter **82**, not 83. The correct primary source is
[*Hanshu* 82](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/漢書/卷082), concluding `贊`:
the historian praises `丹之輔道副主，掩惡揚美`. Chapter 83 instead begins
with Xue Xuan. This error affects the citation, not the broader observation that
“scandal” is not present in Jinshu's quoted Chinese.

**Verified:** all four pinned exact-byte SHA-256 hashes match the staged Git
objects. Staged s0011 and the [Book of Jin 93](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/晉書/卷093)
transcription agree on the quoted passage. The extraction's p001 has
`樊靡卿` as a `personal-name`, p002 is labeled as his father, and claim-6 makes
p001 a biological son of p002 with `explicit` certainty. The evidence in
[*Later Han* 32](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/後漢書/卷32) reads `樊宏字靡卿`
and `父重，字君雲`, and describes `樊重子父` as ethical local notables. It
also names Hong's son 鯈 and praises him. The proposed interpretation of the
Jin passage as Hong and his father has substantial support but the draft is
right not to treat the unnamed pair's referent as unambiguous from s0011 alone.
Later Han 32 indeed says Zhong's tomb was visited in Jianwu year 18 and Hong
died in year 27; neither statement proves an exact BC 6 birth year or that
s0011's retrospective assessment occurred within those dates.

The staged claim IDs 3, 8, 11, 16 and 20 have the intervals described by the

[*Shiji* 49](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/史記/卷049) identifies the empress's
brothers 竇長君 and 竇廣國 and praises both. This supports examining a two-man
reading, but independently validate which person or people the particular Jin
reference covers and whether the extraction requires a separately linked local

defensible research hypothesis, not an already approved translation. The
draft's paired neutral Fan phrasing avoids prematurely settling the relationship;
it too still needs independent editorial review in context. Do not import
cross-book names into local evidence as if they occur in Jin s0011, and do not
equate a prose research note with a schema-valid provenance record.
