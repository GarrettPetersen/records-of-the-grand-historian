description from a conclusively demonstrated translation error and requires
an independent classical-Chinese editorial decision. It does not establish
whether `卑下` indicates physical height, comparative status, or another
precise reading. No exact year is established. A reign span can sometimes
justify a carefully evidenced event bound under the date-audit rules; it is
not by itself a precise year or continuous activity. Keep the yearless hold
and avoid inventing restrictions or changes to the source while the precise
question remains open. This review accepts the cited note as limited research
only, and authorizes neither changing candidate claims nor closing the audit.
