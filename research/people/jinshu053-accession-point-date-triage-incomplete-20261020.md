## Pre-accession contexts

The packet's `claim-129` assigns p003 an AD 290 attestation from s0002,
where the introduction identifies the prince's mother, `母曰謝才人`, but
states no calendar year. `claim-130` assigns p005 AD 290 for s0012, where
Emperor Wu tells Fu Zhi that the boy will bring prosperity. `claim-131`
assigns p006 AD 290 for s0013, where the emperor likens the boy to Emperor
Xuan. Neither unit identifies the event as happening in 290; the emperor is
the source and the named predecessor is the subject of a comparison, not
automatically an actor living in the year of the comparison.

Similarly, `claim-132` through `claim-135` assign p007–p010 AD 290 from
the appointments at s0200. In context, s0199 describes the prince's earlier
enfeoffment as Prince of Guangling; s0201 *then* records Emperor Hui's
accession and the elevation to crown prince. The chronology and conversion of
the accession may supply a defensible outer bound, but order alone does not
establish the appointments all occurred in the accession year. Establish
each endpoint through a pinned witness before retaining any point year.

## Post-accession contexts

`claim-136` through `claim-141` assign p011–p016 AD 290 with source text
`惠帝即位後` and evidence s0202, the appointment of tutors following the
preceding accession in s0201. `後` establishes sequence, not by itself the
Western year of all six appointments; check source context and appropriate
chronology witnesses before replacing any point with a bound or interval.

These are targeted checks, not a finding that the actual events did not occur
in AD 290. Cross-check all cited hints and events, distinguish retrospective
reference from living activity where applicable, and keep unanchored dates
unresolved instead of guessing. An independent reviewer and a fresh full audit
of the final source and extraction remain required. No canonical data, report,
queue, lease, lock or retained artifact was modified.
