# Independent review: Jinshu 025 Wuling Hu-dress chronology

**Scope:** narrow review of the separate research note
`jinshu025-wuling-hufu-research-20261019.md`; not a complete date audit,
repair, production approval, ownership claim, or release authorization.
I independently read the cited fixed Shiji revision through the Wikisource
API, the journal PDF, and the retained candidate and blocker.

The note's main distinction is supported. Shiji 43's Wuling nineteenth-year
entry begins with a spring first-month meeting and later narrates the king's
adoption of Hu dress (`遂胡服矣`), persuasion of his uncle, and an initial
Hu-dress order (`始出胡服令`); the twentieth-year heading follows. It does
not specify that the Jinshu's gold-and-sable decoration occurred during that
year or give a converted Western day. The entry's opening first-month heading
should not automatically be attached to every subsequent action in the long
narrative. Shiji is a transmitted literary witness, not an independent
eyewitness verification of the fourth-century BC events.

Ye Guoliang's cited PDF does print `趙武靈王十九年（前 ３０７）` in the
relevant sentence about a riding-and-archery order and Hu dress. It appears on
printed page 90, the twelfth PDF page (zero-based page index 11); the citation
is correct. This is a published scholarly placement, not an explicit Western
year in Shiji, a mathematical proof eliminating alternative reign chronologies,
or evidence of the headgear's date. The article states the placement in passing;
the cited passage does not provide a detailed derivation of the conversion.
