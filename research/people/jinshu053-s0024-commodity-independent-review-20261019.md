## Source checks

- [Wikisource, Jinshu 053](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7053)
  reads 藍子 and 面 in the corresponding passage. [Shidianguji, Jinshu 053](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/LS0005/chapter/LS0005_791)
  instead reads 籃子 and 麵. [Yuding pianzi leibian, Siku transcription, 葵菜 entry](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E5%BE%A1%E5%AE%9A%E9%A7%A2%E5%AD%97%E9%A1%9E%E7%B7%A8_%28%E5%9B%9B%E5%BA%AB%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8%E6%9C%AC%29/%E5%85%A8%E8%A6%BD7)
  attributes the latter pair to the Jinshu prince biography. These observations
  establish displayed readings only, not independent transmission or the
  underlying Jinshu edition.
- The originally cited full-text aggregate links for Chajing and Taiping Yulan
  did not expose the target passages in my web reader. Use these directly
  addressable alternatives for rechecking: [Chajing, section seven](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%8C%B6%E7%B6%93/%E4%B8%83%E4%B9%8B%E4%BA%8B)
  attributes a differently ordered 藍子 list to the Jiang family biography;
  [Taiping Yulan, Sibu Congkan page 151 transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3ASibu_Congkan_Sanbian353-%E6%9D%8E%E6%98%89-%E5%A4%AA%E5%B9%B3%E5%BE%A1%E8%A6%BD-136-120.djvu/151)
  likewise displays a differently ordered 藍子 list attributed to the Jiang
  biography. The latter page is explicitly marked **unproofread**; I inspected
  its displayed transcription, not the underlying page image. These alternative
  links are not the Siku witnesses named in the original note, and one must not
  silently relabel them as such. They do not establish textual independence.
- Another [Taiping Yulan digital transcription, 葵 entry](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/SBCK435/chapter/1kgozy3g5tdsq)
  attributes a list including 藍子 and 麵 to Jinshu, but contains apparent OCR
  errors in the surrounding transcription. Treat it as a locator, not a clean
  independent witness or decisive edition.

## Disposition

The note's cautious conclusion is supported: retain a research hold; do not
silently emend 藍 to 籃 or claim that either seed or basket is proved, and do
not decide flour versus noodles merely from normalization. To close it, inspect
identified Jinshu edition images and useful lexical evidence, assess the
transmission, choose and document a defensible translation consistently across
the parallels, and independently review the final wording and agency. Source or
extraction amendments require fresh fingerprint-bound downstream validation and
review. This limited review is not chapter completion or date-audit approval.
