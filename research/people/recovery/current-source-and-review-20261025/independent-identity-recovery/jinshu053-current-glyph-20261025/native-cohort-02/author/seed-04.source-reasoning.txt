merge jinshu:053:p070, sanguozhi:040:p031
jinshu:053:s0192 cites 遂使冤逾楚建，酷甚戾園. sanguozhi:040:s0042 places 楚建 beside 申生、衛伋、禦寇 and says 當嗣立之正. Both are retrospective exempla of a wronged rightful heir, with the same specific Chu designation 楚建. Their explicit historical allusion, rather than shared romanization, supports one referent.
