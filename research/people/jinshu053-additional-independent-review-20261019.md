proposed renderings acceptable in meaning for: **s0133, s0136–s0138, s0142,
s0167–s0169, s0172–s0176, s0178–s0185 and s0187**. These improve on

## Rows needing revision or a preserved hold

* **s0131:** the proposed literal sensibly avoids treating `策` as a name,
  and 兼 means holding an office concurrently; the idiomatic `acting`
  blurs that. Prefer `concurrently Director of Works` in both fields. The
  exact segmentation and identity of `衛尉伊策` still merit a source-backed
  check before assigning a confident personal identity to `Yi`; the text
  alone does not establish a two-character name `Yi Ce`. Preserve the
  separately identified uncertainty rather than inventing a surname.
* **s0132:** the literal preserves `淮陵` as Huailing and `奄有` as holding
  it; idiomatic `a great fief in Huailing` may read as a single precisely
  located place. [Jinshu 053](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/晉書/卷053)
  also describes his prior enfeoffment as Prince of Guangling, so the
  relationship of these labels requires contextual historical review. Keep
  the idiomatic under HOLD; do not equate the places or assert residence.
* **s0134:** both versions depend on the interpretation of `祗爾德行，以從保傅`;
  keep the author's HOLD. Neither the staged `I charged you` nor the proposed
  `cultivated virtue under` should be treated as an established solution.
* **s0135:** literal is supported. The idiomatic recasts the calamity as a
  plot that `led to` the death, weakening the emperor's explicit first-person
  responsibility `致爾`. Replace it with, for example, `Yet I was blind to
  the wicked plot and brought you to an untimely death; the tragedies of Shen
  Sheng and Xiao Ji have returned in our own day.` Keep the allusion, and
  do not imply an exact method of killing.
* **s0139:** retain HOLD. [Wikisource](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/晉書/卷053)
  and [Shidianguji](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/SK0712/chapter/1kfjj3kgstp2r)
  read `祠以太牢`, whereas the pinned file and [Chinese Classics](https://www.chinese-classics.org/read/history/jin-shu/jin-shu-053)
  read `祠乙太牢`. This suggests an `乙`/`以` transcription issue, but
  multiple web copies need not represent independent editions. Do not render
  `yi tai-lao` as a ritual name or silently emend the sealed source. A
  reviewer may propose a separate 乙→以 source restoration with a verified
  base-edition witness; if accepted, `offer a tai-lao sacrifice` is a
  defensible reading, subject to ritual review. The current `ox sacrifice`
  specifies only one animal without support in the sentence.
* **s0140:** preserve HOLD for `魂而有靈，尚獲爾心`. `may it still know
  your heart` does not resolve whose heart is meant and does not adequately
  explain `獲`; do not present it as settled.
* **s0141:** mourning-weight correction is important: `斬衰` is unhemmed,
  while `齊衰` is hemmed; [Liji Zhengyi](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/禮記正義/40)
  supports the distinction. Still HOLD the exact sense of `具吉凶之制`
  and what was prepared or provided; `rites` can suppress a distinction
  between prescribed ceremonial arrangements and their objects. Avoid
  `Minister Director` as an exact office unless independently justified.
* **s0143:** HOLD `臨軒`, `洗馬`, and `殯` as the author recommends. An
  Eastern Palace office does not mean a horse-grooming job; however, whether
  the last term is best rendered bier, coffin, or lying-in-state requires
  context. Do not introduce a precise modern institutional title unsupported
  by the source. Preserve the opening of the funeral address through s0166.
* **s0170–s0171:** the English restores the missing Bin and avoids invented
  birth order, but approval is conditional on source restoration or an
  explicitly documented emendation policy. Both missing `虨` characters
  are found in [Wikisource](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/晉書/卷053) and
  [Shidianguji](https://www.shidianguji.com/mid-page/7463962545133617190);
  the next biography names him and his courtesy name. The omissions in both
  places are also shared by [Chinese Classics](https://www.chinese-classics.org/read/history/jin-shu/jin-shu-053),
  so it is not an independent correction witness. The digital witnesses'
  print/manuscript independence is unproved. Keep restoration separate from
  bilingual decisions, and pin exact character, edition provenance and source
  fingerprints rather than inventing a clean text.
* **s0177:** continue HOLD on `還妃王氏以母之` and the institutional title
  `太孫太妃`. `restore Consort Wang` could imply restoring an office rather
  than bringing her back; `act as his mother` must not assert biological
  maternity. Render `Wang` without the unsupported full name until the
  correct context and source-supported identity are independently reviewed.
* **s0186:** continue HOLD. `詒厥` can allude to planning for descendants;
  the proposed `his descendants` may imply a particular antecedent. Do not
  turn this alone into a detailed succession plan. Check allusion and syntax.
* **s0188:** continue HOLD. Both renderings properly avoid saying the prince
  was enthroned emperor; that corrects the staged idiomatic. But the
  idiomatic drops the source's four/three distinction and specific animal
  images, while `three audiences` and `four teachings` require contextual
  verification. `豹姿未變` and `鳳德已衰` need a defensible metaphorical
  reading without treating moral promise as established biography. Do not
  accept as a complete translation simply because the summary is improved.
