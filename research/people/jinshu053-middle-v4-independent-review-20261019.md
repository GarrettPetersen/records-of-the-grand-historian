## Withdrawn and uncovered units

- **s0104:** I retract my earlier categorical claim that 研墨 here *must*
  mean “ground ink.” [Academia Sinica's character comparison](https://chardb.iis.sinica.edu.tw/meancompare/784f/7814)
  lists 研 as a form of 硯, and the series `持筆研墨黃紙來` naturally permits
  pen, inkstone, ink and yellow paper as things brought. The source does not
  securely assert the action of grinding. Existing literal omits ink, and
  existing idiomatic omits inkstone, ink and making him write. Suggested
  **new, independently reviewable** both-field wording: `Another young maid,
  Chengfu, brought a brush, inkstone, ink, and yellow paper and made me write.`
  The proposal is not present in v4 and is not self-approved here.
- **s0069–s0070:** the author correctly leaves difficult rhymes unresolved.
  Existing English cannot be deemed acceptable solely because no v4 row exists.
- **s0087:** author correctly flags `不得王是成國耳`. One modern online
  translation [reads it as not receiving a kingly title but obtaining a
  fief](https://dudianji.com/book/晉書/卷五十三·列傳第二十三); that is a
  tentative interpretation, not decisive textual authority or grounds to
  preserve the current invented “Cheng principality.” Seek edition/commentary.
- **s0126:** author correctly flags the prior “repent posthumously” as
  unsupported. The source depicts Jia saying she had privately hoped Yu would
  take the lesson to heart, reconsider filial duty and prostrate himself,
  enabling restoration of his rank/name; exact subjects and sequence require
  independent checking before proposing a definitive replacement.
- **s0072, s0074, s0110, s0116, s0117, s0125, s0129:** no v4 proposed
  changes, not a completeness finding; s0110/s0116/s0129 close the speeches
  mentioned above and must be included when reconstructing quotations.
