## Assessment

**The death is explicit, but its exact Western year and an upper bound are not
established by these sources.** The retained repair's `onOrBefore: AD 319` is
not supported by the grouping alone, and its reason incorrectly treats
`太興初` as synonymous with the first year of Taixing and the two headings as
competing. No revised candidate is offered here: the owner must use the
approved repair workflow and a fresh independent audit.

1. In [Jinshu 108](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7108),
   Murong Hui's biography places the three-state attack `太興初` (early in the
   Taixing reign period); it then narrates Cui Bi's defeat and Gao Zhan's
   relocation to Jicheng. `初` is not the character `元` and does not mean
   specifically Taixing 1. Both the account and its later passage about Gao
   Zhan's death give no explicit date for that death. The latter places the
   death after his submission and a sequence of refusals and Song Gai's plot;
   it does not specify how long those events took.
2. Local Jinshu 6, s0208–s0209, places Murong's attack on Liaodong and Cui
   Bi's flight immediately after a twelfth-month heading in Taixing 2. The
   passage is also quoted in [Hamada Kōsaku's source collection](https://jkcf.or.jp/cms/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/1-01j.pdf),
   PDF p. 54 (printed p. 57). The
   Western designation AD 319 for Taixing 2 is also given by [Zizhi tongjian
   91](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%B3%87%E6%B2%BB%E9%80%9A%E9%91%91/%E5%8D%B7091).
   These support a relative sequence: Zhan died after Cui Bi's defeat, dated by
   the annal to the twelfth month of Taixing 2. That year designation is not
   by itself an exact Western-date conversion of the twelfth lunar month, and
   does not date every later episode in the biography.
3. *Tongjian* 91 presents Cui Bi's attack, defeat, Zhan's transfer and death
   in a narrative group after a twelfth-month heading and before the heading
   for Taixing 3. It also groups a subsequent Goguryeo attack and Murong
   counterattack before the Taixing 3 heading. **Jinshu 108 explicitly places
   that Goguryeo attack `明年` (the following year).** This demonstrates that
   the two accounts' section boundaries cannot safely be interpreted as
   exhaustive event-year boundaries. It does not prove that the two texts are
   independent witnesses or that the death occurred in Taixing 3.
4. The next *Tongjian* heading dates Pei Yi's arrival in Jiankang to Taixing 3
   month 3. Although the compiler places the death narrative before the
   separate report of Pei's dispatch, the source does not state that Zhan's
   death preceded that departure or arrival. Narrative adjacency therefore
   does not establish that as an upper bound either.

The conservative evidence is an explicitly undated death *after* Cui Bi's
defeat in the Taixing 2 account; an exact AD 319 or 320 death would require
additional explicit chronological evidence and reliable calendar conversion.
At most, a reviewer may consider a one-sided Western lower bound using the
conventional AD 319 identification of Taixing 2, after verifying the schema's
source/precision requirements and supporting conversion. Do not claim AD 320
is the upper bound without separate evidence. Do not substitute an empty
unresolved reason or falsify a citation to pass the independent date gate.
