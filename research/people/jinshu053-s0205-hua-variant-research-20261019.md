Together, the matching office, father–son relationship, son's name, and Eastern
Palace role strongly support the historical identification **Hua Yi and Hua
Heng**, and support considering 廙 for the chapter-053 source. They do not
prove that 暠 is absent from all valid editions, why the discrepancy arose, or
that the variants are independent witnesses. No edition scan has been directly
checked for the character. An apparent name should not be silently changed or
reported as an established alias of Hua Yi without a reviewed textual decision.

## Pinned extraction implications

The pinned extraction has `p024` named `Hua Gao` / `華暠`, role minister
director, with source mention anchored to s0205; `p025` is `Hua Heng` / `華恆`
and described as “Son of Hua Gao; companion to crown prince,” with p024 in
its relationships. The Chinese individual-name mention for p025 is the given
name `恆`, while the full name is supplied from context. Thus the present
extraction faithfully reflects the currently pinned Chinese but risks exposing a
historically misleading father and propagating it into search, person pages,
relationships and identity decisions. A decision must consider both persons,
their contextual mentions and existing cross-chapter identity records—not just
replace a string or mechanically merge records.

## Proposed safe action

1. Keep this as a source/editorial hold. Locate and visually verify a named
   printed edition or scan and its relevant apparatus; compare the chapter 044
   relationship and the two chapter 053 readings. Preserve both cited readings
   and edition provenance. A reviewer may adopt 廙 with a transparent textual
   note if the project’s source policy and independent review support it, or
   retain 暠 with explicit documented uncertainty if they do not.
2. If adopted, propose exact changed Chinese and both English fields together,
   independently review the text and source attribution, and preserve the old
   witness reading; do not fabricate an unqualified `華暠` alias. If retained,
   qualify the historical identification and avoid publishing the father/son
   relationship as settled without the variant evidence.
3. Host coordinates the normal extraction update and reviews the `p024`/`p025`
   identity and relationships against any existing person records, mention
   anchoring, and source-sensitive stale editorial, chronology and identity
   approvals. Run normal validators and fresh independent gates as required.
   Never infer a merge purely from matching names or reuse invalidated approvals.
