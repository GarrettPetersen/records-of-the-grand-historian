## Separate s0053 editorial packet: dissent pending revision

The proposal correctly identifies lost content in the current idiomatic translation and moves away from making an unsupported local surname into `Lady Jiang`. However, both suggested English versions present 茹毛飲血 as a simple future act of eating raw flesh and drinking blood; they omit its oath function. The primary historical commentary on the parallel passage, Hu Sanxing's note to [Zizhi Tongjian 83](https://ancient-china-books.github.io/zizhitongjian/OEBPS/Text/6227.html), explicitly glosses this phrase as making an oath. An idiomatic translation should communicate the oath, with the underlying physical imagery preserved in the literal field or in a suitably explicit English rendering. Do not simply apply either suggested version unchanged.

That same commentary identifies the Jiang of the forged statement with Bin's mother Jiang Baolin, and interprets 內主 as the proposed empress. This is stronger, attributed evidence for an identity comparison and context for translating the designation, but it is not an explicit identity statement in the local Jinshu s0053 passage and does not by itself authorize a silent merge or erase the fact that the statement was forged. The proposed neutral `ruler of the inner court` is defensible as a cautious reading but should be evaluated against that commentary; it must not be mistaken for proof the person actually held the position. Do not insert Jiang Jun's identity into the quotation itself as an unqualified fact.
