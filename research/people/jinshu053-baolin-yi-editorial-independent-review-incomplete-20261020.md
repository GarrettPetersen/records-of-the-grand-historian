1. **s0065 literal — REVISE.** 澹以兵仗送太子妃王氏、三皇孫于金墉城，考竟
   謝淑妃及太子保林蔣俊 specifies an armed escort and interrogation ending in
   death. `Dan sent armed guards` implies an extra dispatch, and the proposed
   passive makes the agency of 考竟 less clear than the compact Chinese. A
   conservative version is: `Dan had Crown Princess Wang and the three imperial
   grandsons escorted under armed guard to Jinyong City; Consort Xie and Jiang
   Jun, a baolin of the crown prince, were interrogated to death.` It preserves
   the explicit three grandsons and does not claim Dan personally walked with
   them or independently resolve the responsibility for the interrogation.
2. **s0065 idiomatic — REVISE.** Use the same guarded escort and death wording,
   while retaining the explanatory rank: `Dan had Crown Princess Wang and the
   three imperial grandsons escorted under armed guard to Jinyong City; Consort
   Xie and Jiang Jun, one of the crown prince's consorts with the rank of
   baolin, were interrogated to death.` The prior `executed` omits 考's
   interrogation; the prior `Wang consort and grandsons` drops the number.
   `Consort Xie` does not pretend to settle a more specific English equivalent
   for 淑妃. This judgment does not identify her as the crown prince's mother.
3. **s0131 literal — REVISE.** The proposal correctly distinguishes the
   restoration patent 冊復 and reads 策 as the act of proclamation, rather
   than assigning the official the two-character name `Yi Ce`. 持節 need not
   be narrowed to a `tally`; the insignia denotes authorized credentials.
   Recommend: `When Deposed Commoner Jia died, they executed Liu Zhen, Sun Lü,
   Cheng Ju, and others, and restored the crown prince by patent, saying: 'The
   emperor sends an envoy bearing his credentials, Yi, the Commandant of the
   Guard, concurrently serving as Director of Works, to issue a patent to the
   spirit of the late crown prince, saying: Alas!` The grammatical parse of 伊
   as the envoy's name is plausible in context, but the local wording does not
   itself establish a full name or a cross-chapter identification. The final
   quotation continues into s0132; retain the chapter's quotation conventions.
4. **s0132 literal — ACCEPT.** The proposed direct address preserves 爾 and
   the extraordinary favor and land granted in 大啟土宇，奄有淮陵, and
   `Huailing` represents 淮陵 rather than `Hailing`. Do not turn the location
   into a separately named individual. The passage addresses the deceased
   crown prince inside the imperial patent begun in s0131.
5. **s0132 idiomatic — ACCEPT.** The proposed direct speech restores the young
   prince's intelligence, the former emperor's favor, and the bestowed domain;
   the prior one-line summary suppressed these and obscured the speaker. `He`
   in the preceding literal proposal is the late emperor; idiomatic version's
   colon expresses the relation without inventing a new speaker.
6. **s0172 literal — ACCEPT.** `In the fourth month, he was posthumously
   enfeoffed as Prince of Nanyang` preserves 追 and the immediate prior subject.
   The pronoun avoids silently repairing the lacunose Chinese name in s0171.
7. **s0172 idiomatic — ACCEPT.** `In the fourth month, he was posthumously made
   Prince of Nanyang` restores the omission of 追 and the implied subject. The
   fourth-month wording does not prove a Western-calendar date and is not a date
   audit. Independently review s0171's `Eldest` and the source lacuna.
