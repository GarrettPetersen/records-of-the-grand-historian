`a5b100cbfd54d44f1dbc224b36651c3bba65bcc4fe6a2ef6eab66b1aefb4f232`, its
physical neighbors, and both existing English fields. It does not approve a

| Unit | Narrow independent assessment |

| s0014 | The proposed literal is sound. Idiomatic “neglected his studies” loses 不好學's dislike of learning; prefer “disliked studying.” The existing literal's “when grown” and idiomatic “adult” are overly definite, and “pages” is narrower than 左右. |
| s0015 | Causal link and eunuch-directed flattery should be restored in idiomatic; the existing literal already retains them. “Resented” is plausible but 忌 can also denote fear or jealousy; consider “resented or feared” only if house style permits that ambiguity, rather than assert a specific emotion. The quotation opens and closes within this unit. |
| s0016 | Context after s0015 supports the eunuchs as speakers, not the existing “she.” Both proposed versions preserve the utterance and its separate quotation. “Authority and punishment” is a defensible rendering of 威刑 without prescribing punishment type. |
| s0017 | The idiomatic should retain generous rewards and many toys; existing literal has both. The source does not explicitly identify the recipient of the rewards. Lady Jiang is a usable reference but 美人 is a formal rank; check house style for preserving that rank rather than treating it as her personal name. |
| s0018 | Proposed idiomatic restores increasing arrogance/laxity, occasional missed court attendance and habitual garden play; existing literal already conveys them. |
| s0019 | Both proposed passives avoid assigning an omitted actor to 斷 and avoid the existing imagined cart wreck. The technical word “reins” is not certain: [Yu Pian](https://www.wenlinshe.com/tw/chapter/skqs_jingbu/yu-pian/juan-26.html) glosses 勒 as a horse's bit/bridle and 鞅 as a neck strap. Prefer broadly “their tack was cut” or verify “harness and bridle”; retain causal fall for amusement and do not invent who cut it. The relationship between the small carts and the act of riding warrants care. |
| s0020 | Proposed idiomatic accurately avoids limiting the victims to servants; existing literal is already consistent. |
| s0021 | Proposed texts restore 動屋 omitted from both existing fields. “Disturb the house/building” is safely broad but awkward; “make alterations to the house” may better convey 動屋. Separately confirm distinctions between 壁 and 牆 before claiming precise wall/fence terminology. |
| s0022 | Both proposed fields correctly assign butchering/selling liquor to others, and hand-estimation of weight to the prince; neither exact ounces nor coins are stated. Existing literal invents an exact ounce and idiomatic reverses the agent. |
| s0023 | The existing literal and proposed idiomatic “butcher's daughter” may imply the father personally had that trade. 屠家女 only establishes a daughter of a butcher's family; prefer “a daughter of a butcher's family.” The existing idiomatic also mistakenly gives her a trade. |
| s0024 | Hold both fields for edition and lexical research. Source-as-presented leaves 藍子/籃子 and 面/麵 in question; neither “indigo seed” nor “flour” should be treated as resolved. Agency is causative, but 西園 can denote the garden or its personnel; “had [goods] sold in the Western Garden” avoids making a place literally sell. The existing literal's “pocketing” may also add a pejorative inference not explicit in 收其利. |
| s0025 | Proposed idiomatic restores the monthly 500,000 cash and general expenses. “In advance” for 探取 is supported by Hu Sanxing's gloss quoted in the [Ministry of Education dictionary](https://pedia.cloud.edu.tw/Entry/Detail?title=%E6%8E%A2%E5%8F%96); do not infer an exact lead time beyond the two-month funds described. Existing literal “precedent allotted” is slightly more definite than 月請 (“requested monthly”) and “drew two months ahead” may misleadingly suggest a specific temporal lead rather than amount; review both fields. |
