## Source and chronology

- Staged Jinshu 129 s0004 and the current [Wikisource transcription of Jinshu
  129](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7129)
  agree on the relevant passage: Liang Xi and Lü Guang each considered Juqu
  Mengxun remarkable and feared him. No date is expressed there. The wording
  does not require their assessments to have occurred at the same time or
  establish either endpoint of an interval.
- Staged Jinshu 122 s0086 describes Peng Ji seizing Liang Xi; s0087 describes
  Lü Guang entering Guzang. The [current Jinshu 122 transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7122)
  supports the sequence, though that portion alone does not independently give
  a Western year. The source later has a spelling variant 彭齊 for the person
  referred to as 彭濟 in s0086; this note does not resolve that identity or
  textual variation.
- The [current Zizhi Tongjian 106 transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%B3%87%E6%B2%BB%E9%80%9A%E9%91%91/%E5%8D%B7106)
  places the sequence after a ninth-month heading, then before the next
  tenth-month heading, within 太元十年; it expressly says 彭濟 captured 梁熙
  and 呂光 killed him. This is a later compilation and may share source
  traditions with Jinshu; its positioning is evidence of the compiler's
  chronology, not an independent eyewitness or an exact death day.
- The separate [Academia Sinica published chronology](https://www.litphil.sinica.edu.tw/newsletter/123/123-95-150.pdf)
  labels 太元十年 as 385 and 十一年 as 386. This independently confirms the
  conventional year-number correspondence, but it does **not**, on its own,
  show that every day of 太元十年 fell in Western 385. A [secondary calendar
  table](https://sangokushi-biyori.com/reference-gregorian-calendar-385)
  places the ninth month inside 385 and shows that the regnal year extends
  into 386 on the proleptic Gregorian scale. It attributes its calculations to
  Academia Sinica; its exact boundaries were not independently calculated or
  checked against a scholarly calendar table here. Do not silently equate
  lunar September with Western September, and do not assign an exact death
  date. The chronology supports treating a claim that Liang was continuously
  active into 386 as suspect, conditional on accepting the compilation and
  calendar attribution; it does not establish his appraisal's start date.
- The later Longfei 2/Shenxi 1 correspondence in s0014 does not date s0004.
  This review has not independently established its Western conversion.

The handoff's exact old Wikisource revision URLs returned an access error in
this review; the accessible current transcriptions, not those old revisions,
were compared. No physical edition or independent manuscript was inspected.

## Implications and boundaries

The note correctly separates the three single-claim intervals from the three
person-level activity hints. The evidence reviewed here establishes no precise
new interval for p001, p002, or p003, and does not establish all source
evidence relevant to their lifetime activity. Liang's death does not date Lü's
separate assessment. Whether a particular schema interval is intended as
continuous activity or as a broad possible-event bound needs inspection before
any proposed replacement. No date was repaired, no audit report changed, and
the chapter remains research-blocked; a fresh independent whole-chapter audit
and all other completion gates remain required.
