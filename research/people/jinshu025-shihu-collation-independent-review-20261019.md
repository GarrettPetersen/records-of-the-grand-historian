## Finding in the original draft

The draft attributes its linked 352 memorial chronology to the *Zizhi
Tongjian*. The linked [modern compilation](https://jinsung.chronicle.wiki/d/16_09)
indeed puts the memorial in its `352-2` section, after the fifth-month
campaign. However, the [actual Tongjian volume 99, revision 1502618](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=%E8%B3%87%E6%B2%BB%E9%80%9A%E9%91%91/%E5%8D%B7099&oldid=1502618)
places the parallel Wang Zhuo attack, memorial, and appointment under Yonghe
9, fifth month, conventionally 353, not Yonghe 8. Its short summary does not
quote `季龍自斃`; that wording occurs in the [Jinshu 86 account](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E6%99%89%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7086).
Treat the compilation's 352 chronology as its editorial placement, not the
Tongjian's; preserve the 352/353 disagreement and avoid claiming this evidence
fixes the memorial's year. Both transmitted sequences are before the
compilation's and Tongjian's accounts of Zhang Chonghua's death, but do not
constitute independent eyewitnesses or establish the death year precisely.

## Corroborated source checks and limitations

- Direct retrieval of [Jinshu 107 collation note 3](https://skqs.dazhishi.com/show_gldrnrpkdz.html)

  subsection explicitly corrects the main text's `太和六年` to `永和五年`
  and cites *Taiping Yulan* 120. They are not the same numeral; preserve both.
  The note's claim that the original was already in error remains its
  inference, not independent proof of manuscript history.
