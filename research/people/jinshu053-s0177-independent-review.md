The source reads `還妃王氏以母之，稱太孫太妃。` In the preceding sentence
the ongoing edict appoints Zang imperial grandson-heir, and the next sentence
transfers the crown prince's staff to that heir. This supports the first
reviewer's proposed literal wording: “Let Consort Wang be brought back to stand
as his mother, and let her be styled Taifei of the Grandson-Heir.” The
proposed idiomatic sentence, “Bring Consort Wang back to stand as his mother,
with the title Taifei of the Grandson-Heir,” conveys the same directive. The
antecedent of “his” is Zang in the immediately preceding sentence. Neither
rendering asserts biological motherhood, physical destination, a completed
act, or a marriage to Zang. `太孫太妃` remains transliterated rather than
claiming an exact technical English equivalent. No extra quotation mark should
be placed inside the still-open multi-sentence quotation.

parallels do not establish textual independence.
