# Bounded chapter completion: measured process proposal

The needed change is chapter-local identity certification, not faster exhaustive
same-name comparisons. Independently review each chapter person and its retained
canonical identity; preserve unresolved cross-chapter equivalence explicitly.
Source/editorial/date, concrete false-merge, coverage, family, artifact, CI and live
release checks remain. Do not make confident identity claims to satisfy a timer.

The tested prototype builds one persistent dossier per accepted canonical person,
uses the existing source-backed Chinese/name rules through an inverted index, and
emits one case per canonical person in a chapter. Candidate/context reads have hard
8-dossier/16-key/8-posting maximums. Overflow is explicit reviewed uncertainty, never
proof of separation. New merges/separations still require normal independent native
resolution application. Retained merged membership is protected and independently
checked against a primary identity anchor, including an outside source when present.

## One-hour operational target

| Budget | Work |
| --- | --- |
|10minutes|Current source/extraction/editorial/date evidence; reuse unchanged approvals.|
|20minutes|Independent bounded per-person identity review; explicit uncertainty.|
|15minutes|Native scoped qualification, generated pages/backlinks/search/sitemap/ebook contribution.|
|15minutes|Required CI, deployment and live content verification.|

Time budgets are targets, not approval deadlines. An actual source contradiction
or unsupported canonical membership stays held. Cross-chapter ambiguity can finish
as independently reviewed uncertainty without blocking the source-backed chapter.
The first successful timed nonempty live release is the acceptance test.
