    愍懷太子Crown Prince Minhuai
    湣懷太子遹，字熙祖，惠帝長子，母曰謝才人。Crown Prince Minhuai, Yu, courtesy name Xizu, was Emperor Hui's eldest son; his mother was Talented Lady Xie.
    嘗與諸皇子共戲殿上，惠帝來朝，執諸皇子手，次至太子，帝曰：「是汝兒也。」Once he was playing in the palace hall with the imperial princes; when Emperor Hui came to audience he took each prince's hand in turn; when he reached the crown prince, Emperor Wu said, 'This is your son.'
    太子時年五歲，牽帝裾入暗中。The crown prince was then five years old; he tugged the emperor's robe into the shadows.
    帝問其故，太子曰：「暮夜倉卒，宜備非常，不宜令照見人君也。」The emperor asked why; the crown prince said, 'Suddenly at night one should guard against the unexpected; one must not let firelight reveal the ruler's face.'
    嘗對群臣稱太子似宣帝，於是令譽流於天下。He once declared before the assembled ministers that the crown prince resembled Emperor Xuan; thereafter praise of him spread through the realm.
    時望氣者言廣陵有天子氣，故封為廣陵王，邑五萬戶。Astrologers said Guangling bore an emperor's aura, so Yu was made Prince of Guangling with fifty thousand households.
    惠帝即位，立為皇太子。When Hui Di took the throne, Yu was made crown prince.
    元康元年，出就東宮，又詔曰：「遹尚幼蒙，今出東宮，惟當賴師傅群賢之訓。In Yuankang year one he moved to the Eastern Palace, and an edict declared: "Yu is still young and inexperienced; now that he has moved to the Eastern Palace he must rely on his tutors and worthy mentors for instruction.
    於是使太保衛瓘息庭、司空泰息略、太子太傅楊濟息毖、太子少師裴楷息憲、太子少傅張華息禕、尚書令華暠息恆與太子游處，以相輔導焉。Thereupon Grand Guardian Wei Guan's son Ting, Director of Works Tai's son Lue, Grand Mentor to the Crown Prince Yang Ji's son Bi, Junior Tutor to the Crown Prince Pei Kai's son Xian, Junior Mentor to the Crown Prince Zhang Hua's son Yi, and Minister Director Hua Gao's son Heng were assigned to keep company with the crown prince and assist in his guidance.
    賈后素忌太子有令譽，因此密敕黃門閹宦媚諛于太子曰：「殿下誠可及壯時極意所欲，何為恆自拘束？」Empress Jia had long envied the crown prince's fine reputation and therefore secretly ordered Yellow Gate eunuchs to flatter him: 'Your Highness may indeed indulge every whim while still young—why bind yourself so tightly?'
    每見喜怒之際，輒歎曰：「殿下不知用威刑，天下豈得畏服！」Whenever they saw him pleased or angry, they would sigh: "Your Highness does not know how to use authority and punishments. How could the realm fear and obey you?"
    太子所幸蔣美人生男，又言宜隆其賞賜，多為皇孫造玩弄之器，太子從之。When Lady Jiang, whom the crown prince favored, bore a son, they also said rewards should be lavish and many toys fashioned for the imperial grandson; the crown prince obeyed.
    其母本屠家女也，故太子好之。His mother was originally a butcher’s daughter, which is why the crown prince enjoyed this.
    東宮舊制，月請錢五十萬，備於眾用，太子恆探取二月，以供嬖寵。The Eastern Palace normally received five hundred thousand cash a month for all its expenses; he habitually drew two months in advance to provide for his favorites.
    洗馬江統陳五事以諫之，太子不納，語在《統傳》中。Groom of the Household Jiang Tong presented five admonitions; the crown prince rejected them—the account appears in Jiang Tong's biography.
    舍人杜錫以太子非賈后所生，而後性凶暴，深以為憂，每盡忠規勸太子修德進善，遠於讒謗。Gentleman-of-the-Household Du Xi, because the crown prince was not Empress Jia's son and the empress was savage by nature, deeply worried; he repeatedly urged the crown prince to cultivate virtue and do good, standing far from slander.
    太子怒，使人以針著錫常所坐氈中而剌之。Angered, Yu had someone put needles into the felt mat where Du Xi usually sat, so that they would prick him.
    太子性剛，知賈謐恃後之貴，不能假借之。The crown prince's temper was stiff; knowing Jia Mi relied on the empress's power, he would not indulge him.
    太子不能從。Yu ignored him.
    初，賈后母郭槐欲以韓壽女為太子妃，太子亦欲婚韓氏以自固。Earlier, Empress Jia's mother, Guo Huai, had wanted Han Shou's daughter to become crown princess. The crown prince also hoped that marrying into the Han family would strengthen his position.
    而壽妻賈午及後皆不聽，而為太子聘王衍小女惠風。But Han Shou's wife Jia Wu and the empress both refused, and instead betrothed the crown prince to Wang Yan's younger daughter Huifeng.
    太子聞衍長女美，而賈后為謐聘之，心不能平，頗以為言。The crown prince heard Wang's elder daughter was beautiful while the empress betrothed her to Jia Mi; his heart could not rest easy and he spoke of it often.
    謐嘗與太子圍棋，爭道，成都王穎見而訶謐，謐意愈不平，因此譖太子于後曰：「太子廣買田業，多畜私財以結小人者，為賈氏故也。Once while playing go with the crown prince Mi quarreled over a move; Ying, Prince of Chengdu, saw and rebuked Mi; Mi grew more resentful and slandered the crown prince to the empress: "The crown prince buys fields wholesale, hoards private wealth, and courts petty men—all because of the Jia clan.
    後納其言，又宣揚太子之短，布諸遠近。The empress accepted his words and further broadcast the crown prince's faults throughout near and far.
    于時朝野咸知賈后有害太子意。Then court and countryside knew the empress meant to harm the crown prince.
    中護軍趙俊請太子廢後，太子不聽。Central Guard Zhao Jun asked the crown prince to depose the empress first; the crown prince refused.
    十二月，賈后將廢太子，詐稱上不和，呼太子入朝。In the twelfth month, when Empress Jia would depose the crown prince, she falsely claimed the emperor was unwell and summoned the crown prince to court.
    使黃門侍郎潘岳作書草，若禱神之文，有如太子素意，因醉而書之，令小婢承福以紙筆及書草使太子書之。She had Gentleman of the Yellow Gate Pan Yue draft a prayer text mimicking the Crown Prince's usual intent; because he was drunk she made him copy it, having the young maid Chengfu bring paper, brush, and draft.
    太子醉迷不覺，遂依而寫之，其字半不成。Too drunk and confused to realize what he was doing, the crown prince copied it, but half the characters were incomplete.
    帝幸式乾殿，召公卿入，使黃門令董猛乙太子書及青紙詔曰：「遹書如此，今賜死。」The emperor visited Shi-Qian Hall, summoned dukes and ministers, and had Yellow Gate Chief Dong Meng display the crown prince's script with an edict on blue paper: "Yu has written thus—now sentence him to death."
    遍示諸公王，莫有言者，惟張華、裴頠證明太子。It was shown all around to the lords and princes; none spoke, except Zhang Hua and Pei Wei, who verified the crown prince's innocence.
    後懼事變，乃表免太子為庶人，詔許之。The empress feared events might shift and therefore memorialized to degrade the crown prince to commoner status; an edict approved.
    於是使尚書和郁持節，解結為副，及大將軍梁王肜、鎮東將軍淮南王允、前將軍東武公澹、趙王倫、太保何劭詣東宮，廢太子為庶人。Thereupon Minister He Yu was dispatched with insignia, with Xie Jie as deputy, together with General-in-Chief Sima Rong, Prince of Liang; Eastern Garrison General Sima Yun, Prince of Huainan; Forward General Sima Dan, Duke of Dongwu; Sima Lun, Prince of Zhao; and Grand Guardian He Shao. They went to the Eastern Palace to depose the crown prince as a commoner.
    是日太子游玄圃，聞有使者至，改服出崇賢門，再拜受詔，步出承華門，乘粗犢車。That day the crown prince roamed the Mystic Garden; hearing envoys arrived he changed clothes, exited Chongxian Gate, twice bowed receiving the edict, walked out Chenghua Gate, and rode a rough ox-cart.
    澹以兵仗送太子妃王氏、三皇孫于金墉城，考竟謝淑妃及太子保林蔣俊。Dan escorted Crown Princess Wang and the three imperial grandsons to Jinyong under armed guard, and interrogated Consort Xie and the crown prince’s Lady Baolin Jiang Jun to death.
    明年正月，賈后又使黃門自首，欲與太子為逆。The next first month Empress Jia again had a Yellow Gate confess plotting rebellion with the crown prince.
    又遣澹以千兵防送太子，更幽于許昌宮之別坊，令治書御史劉振持節守之。She further sent Dan with a thousand troops to escort the crown prince, moving him to a separate compound of Xuchang palace under Supervising Secretary Liu Zhen with insignia.
    又曰：「南風起兮吹白沙，遙望魯國郁嵯峨，千歲髑髏生齒牙。」Another went: 'South wind rises blowing white sand; far off Lu kingdom gloomy and tall; thousand-year skulls sprout teeth.'
    沙門，太子小字也。'Shamen' was the crown prince's childhood byname.
    初，太子之廢也，妃父王衍表請離婚。When the crown prince was first deposed, his father-in-law Wang Yan memorialized requesting divorce.
    太子至許，遺妃書曰：「鄙雖頑愚，心念為善，欲盡忠孝之節，無有惡逆之心。After reaching Xu he sent his consort a letter: "Though I am coarse and foolish, at heart I wished to do good and fulfill loyalty and filial piety—I harbored no rebellious evil.
    自為太子以來，敕見禁檢，不得見母。Since becoming crown prince, edicts confined me—I could not see my mother.
    去年十二月，道文疾病困篤，父子之情，實相憐湣。Last twelfth month Daowen became gravely ill; the affection between father and son made us deeply pity one another.
    '中宮遙呼陳舞：'昨天教與太子酒棗。The empress called to Chen Wu from a distance: "Yesterday His Majesty ordered wine and jujubes for the crown prince."
    太子既廢非其罪，眾情憤怨。The crown prince had been deposed through no fault of his own, and people were indignant and resentful.
    右衛督司馬雅，宗室之疏屬也，與常從督許超並有寵于太子，二人深傷之，說趙王倫謀臣孫秀曰：「國無適嗣，社稷將危，大臣之禍必起。Right Guard Commandant Sima Ya, a distant kinsman of the imperial house, and Attendant Commandant Xu Chao, both favorites of the crown prince, grieved deeply; they told Sun Xiu, stratagemist to Lun, Prince of Zhao: "The state has no true heir—altar and grain will be imperiled; great ministers' disaster is sure to rise.
    而公奉事中宮，與賈后親密，太子之廢，皆云豫知，一旦事起，禍必及矣。You serve the empress and are close to her. Everyone says you knew in advance of the prince’s deposition. If trouble breaks out, disaster will surely reach you too.
    計既定，而秀說倫曰：「太子為人剛猛，若得志之日，必肆其情性矣。With the plan set, Xiu said to Lun, "The crown prince is by nature rigid and fierce; if he gains his will he will certainly give free rein to his temper.
    今雖欲建大功于太子，太子雖將含忍宿忿，必不能加賞於公，當謂公逼百姓之望，翻覆以免罪耳。Even if you now seek to perform a great service for the crown prince, he may suppress his old resentment, but he will certainly not reward you. He will think that you changed sides only under pressure from the people's wishes, hoping to escape punishment.
    不若遷延卻期，賈后必害太子，然後廢賈后，為太子報仇，猶足以為功，乃可以得志。」Better delay: Empress Jia is sure to harm the crown prince; then depose Jia, avenge the crown prince—merit enough to win your will."
    秀因使反間，言殿中人欲廢賈后，迎太子。Sun Xiu then had disinformation circulated, claiming that men in the palace wished to depose Empress Jia and bring the crown prince back.
    三月，矯詔使黃門孫慮齋至許昌以害太子。In the third month, she forged an edict and sent the Yellow Gate attendant Sun Lü to carry the pills to Xuchang and kill the crown prince.
    初，太子恐見鴆，恆自煮食於前。Earlier, fearing that he would be poisoned, the crown prince always cooked his own food in front of himself.
    慮以告劉振，振乃徙太子于小坊中，絕不與食，宮中猶於牆壁上過食與太子。Lü reported this to Liu Zhen, who moved the crown prince to a small compound and denied him food. Palace personnel still passed food to him over the walls.
    慮乃逼太子以藥，太子不肯服，因如廁，慮以藥杵椎殺之，太子大呼，聲聞於外。Sun Lü then tried to force the medicine on the crown prince, but he refused to take it. When the crown prince went to the privy, Lü beat him to death with a medicine pestle. The crown prince cried out loudly, and his cries could be heard outside.
    將以庶人禮葬之，賈后表曰：「遹不幸喪亡，傷其迷悖，又早短折，悲痛之懷，不能自己。They proposed burying him by commoner rites; Empress Jia memorialized: "Yu has unfortunately died—I mourn his confusion and error and early cutting-off; grief overwhelms me.
    遹雖罪在莫大，猶王者子孫，便以匹庶送終，情實憐湣，特乞天恩，賜以王禮。Though Yu's guilt was utmost he remains an imperial grandson—to dispatch him as a mere commoner is truly pitiable—may Heaven grant kingly burial.
    詔以廣陵王禮葬之。An edict buried him with rites due the Prince of Guangling.
    及賈庶人死，乃誅劉振、孫慮、程據等，冊復太子曰：「皇帝使使持節、兼司空、衛尉伊策故皇太子之靈曰：嗚呼！When Deposed Commoner Jia died, they executed Liu Zhen, Sun Lü, Cheng Ju, and others, and posthumously restored the crown prince; the edict read: "The emperor dispatches the credential-bearer, concurrent Director of Works, Commandant of the Guard Yi to announce by patent to the spirit of the late crown prince: Alas!
    朕奉遵遺旨，越建爾儲副，以光顯我祖宗。I followed his last wishes and established you as heir, to glorify our ancestors.
    今追復皇太子喪禮，反葬京畿，祠乙太牢。Now I restore the funeral rites due the crown prince, return your burial to the capital region, and offer a full tailao sacrifice of ox, sheep, and pig.
    帝為太子服長子斬衰，群臣齊衰，使尚書和郁率東宮官屬具吉凶之制，迎太子喪于許昌。The emperor wore unhemmed mourning for the crown prince as for an eldest son; the ministers wore hemmed mourning. He sent Minister He Yu to lead the Eastern Palace officials in preparing the prescribed auspicious and mourning rites and bringing the crown prince's bier back from Xuchang.
    又為哀策曰：「皇帝臨軒，使洗馬劉務告于皇太子之殯曰：咨爾遹！They further composed a condolence text: "The emperor facing the gallery sends Groom of the Household Liu Wu to the crown prince's lying-in-state: Ah, Yu!
    是用建爾儲副，永統皇基。Therefore I established you as heir, to perpetuate the imperial foundations forever.
    諡曰湣懷。Canonized Crown Prince Minhuai.
    太子三子：虨、臧、尚，並與父同幽金墉。The crown prince had three sons—Bin, Zang, and Shang—all imprisoned with their father at Jinyong.
    己巳，詔曰：「咎征數發，奸回作變，遹既逼廢，非命而沒。On jisi day an edict stated: "Ill omens pile; crafty factions stir; Yu was driven from office and died unnaturally.
    太子官屬即轉為太孫官屬。Eastern Palace staff transferred to grand-heir posts.
    五月，倫與太孫俱之東宮，太孫自西掖門出，車服侍從皆湣懷之舊也。In the fifth month Lun and the grand heir went together to the Eastern Palace; the grand heir exited the Western Side Gate—carriages, dress, and attendants all followed Minhuai's old train.
    史臣曰：湣懷挺岐嶷之姿，表夙成之質。The historiographers say: Minhuai was endowed with exceptional wisdom and displayed precocious gifts.
    及於繼明宸極，守器春坊，四教不勤，三朝或闕，豹姿未變，鳳德已衰，信惑奸邪，疏斥正士，好屠酤之賤役，耽苑囿之佚遊，可謂靡不有初，鮮克有終者也。When the new ruler ascended the imperial throne and the prince guarded the succession in the Eastern Palace, he neglected the four teachings and sometimes omitted the three daily greetings. He trusted wicked deceivers and drove away upright men, enjoyed the lowly work of butchering and selling wine, and indulged in idle outings in the parks. Truly one may say: "All have a beginning, but few carry through to the end."
    贊曰：湣懷聰穎，諒惟天挺。The encomium says: Minhuai was intelligent and perceptive, truly endowed by Heaven.
    震宮肇建，儲德不恢。When he first entered the heir’s palace, his princely virtue failed to grow.
