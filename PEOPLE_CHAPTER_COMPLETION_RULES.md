# Finish the supported chapter record

This November 15 instruction makes `PEOPLE_CHAPTER_OWNER_PROCESS.md` concrete.
The work is to capture every individual and mention, read the relevant source
context, record supported details, and conservatively associate person records.
One persistent owner performs the chapter pass and repairs; one independent reviewer
checks the result. Do not schedule separate extraction, chronology-research,
editorial and identity-author waves for the same chapter.

## What closes a question

- A fact the text does not supply stays unknown. Use the native supported undated,
  uncertain or retrospective representation where appropriate. Do not research a
  lifetime, office, biography or precise year merely to fill an optional field.
- A possible match to another person can remain explicitly uncertain and separate.
  The native dossier review permits an approved retained assignment with
  `unresolvedIdentity: true`. This approves the source-backed record and its
  conservative membership, not a positive identification. A bounded or truncated
  search is never proof of unique identity. No exhaustive negative comparisons or
  recursive completion of external chapters.
- Missing people/mentions, wrong event subjects, invented facts, contradictory
  attribution and false merges are blocking defects. Unknown information and
  insufficient evidence for an optional cross-chapter match are different from
  these contradictions; explain which applies instead of using a blanket hold.
- A Western year actually asserted still needs valid evidence. Retain a supported
  regnal date without inventing a conversion. Do not erase existing supported years
  or clear a saved research hold to force completion; route exact evidence and any
  proposed correction to the same independent reviewer. Reusable calendar witnesses
  are host-supplied evidence, not another full model audit.
- A source-form name can remain a source-form name with explicit uncertainty.
  Do not silently emend it to a familiar historical person or require proof of an
  identification that the published record does not claim. Unsupported romanization
  or a contradictory name still needs a reviewed correction or honest representation.

These rules do not automatically resolve existing holds. Preserve the original
finding, record the precise proposed source-based treatment and have the same
independent reviewer assess it under the unchanged native contract.

## Scope the translation work

Read all source units and both English fields during the same chapter pass. Repair
definite omissions, mistranslations and attribution/name errors when found, including
repetitions and the parallel English field. Do not demand a separate speculative
literary polish or external biography project before completing accurate tagging.
Known fixable English defects cannot be deferred or hidden as uncertainty.

## Keep model work substantive and host work mechanical

The host assembles native packets, canonical candidate plans, source locators, exact
byte/document hashes, counts and report wrappers BEFORE requesting a review. The
agent records source-backed decisions, witnesses and uncertainty through bounded
tools. Do not ask it to invent native IDs, calculate hashes, rewrite all historical
repair rows or manufacture a perfect monolithic report.

Editorial preparation retains unchanged accepted records verbatim and adds a pending
review only for new or changed corrections. A single review uses native schema3;
multiple records use schema4. Archive prior raw files and failed reports. Pending
templates grant no approval; changing a template cannot retag an old approval.
Partially written semantic reports require same-reviewer reconciliation, not deletion.

Return all real defects together. The original owner patches them; the same reviewer
checks those changes and affected dependencies. Reuse unchanged valid evidence only
through native validation. Preserve max-three-round/non-improvement breakers. A host
packet/applier defect requires one shared code fix, not another full content review.

## Scheduling and completion

Use `PEOPLE_REMOTE_CONCURRENCY.md` to process separate runnable chapters in parallel,
favoring GrokBot when suitable and adding capable Cursor cloud agents. Drain partial
work first. One host performs canonical writes/builds; workers share the checkout
and use isolated artifacts, never copies of the repository or corpus.

A draft, passing extraction validator, source-curation receipt or prepared identity
packet is not completion. Release the independently reviewed source-backed record,
person pages, chapter backlinks and ebook eligibility through normal CI/publication
gates. Report the actual remaining blocker and published nonempty chapter count.
