Historical rules: date the named person's OWN event, not a descendant's action,
later quotation, republication, or commemoration. Read the Chinese; the English
and previous model assertions are not independent evidence. Verify conversions
against a primary passage and a reliable calendar reference, including the ruler,
polity, reign and repeated era names. Preserve competing readings. Never infer
twins from 同生 in a maternal list. Preserve age reckoning; do not automatically
convert traditional inclusive age into a precise birth year. Conventional regnal
year labels need not equal the Gregorian year of every month/day within them.
There is no year zero. Use BC/AD. Keep birth/death separate from activity.
Preserve later reception as its own event, not living activity: use event kind
posthumous-commemoration, posthumous-reference, or retrospective-reference, or
receptionType: posthumous/retrospective on a narrower honor/work claim. Record
the reception's own date, source and subject role. Retrospective does not imply
dead; require evidence before calling it posthumous. A later-written biography
can still attest an earlier life event. Do not use reception dates as life dates
or active hints, and do not discard the later reference when repairing them.
For verified month/day events, normalize Western years/bounds to proleptic
Gregorian and state that convention in the conversion evidence. Check the
converter's calendar setting: Academia Sinica defaults to Julian before 1582,
which can change the Western year near New Year. Retain the Chinese date and
identify Julian witnesses explicitly. Do not apply a fixed offset across
centuries or pretend a reign-year-only label specifies a Gregorian event date.

Use westernBounds with before/onOrBefore/after/onOrAfter positive BC/AD year
objects for genuine one-sided knowledge. An event after an accession in AD 479
can still occur in AD 479: onOrAfter 479, not after 479. Never invent a second
endpoint or turn a bound into an exact year. Preserve relative event sequence.

unresolvedReason, not a guessed year. Research blocks approval; report it honestly.
